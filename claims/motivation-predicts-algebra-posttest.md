## Evidence

### Barbieri 2016

Barbieri, C. & Booth, J.L. (2016). Support for struggling students in algebra: Contributions of incorrect worked examples. Learning and Individual Differences, 48, 36–44. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Support+for+struggling+students+in+algebra%22+Barbieri+Booth+incorrect+worked+examples. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression model (R² = .454, F = 13.92**) predicting post-test scores from pre-test, SES, URM status, Time 1 competence expectancy (β = .40**), Time 1 belongingness (β = .19*), and condition dummies in the same classroom experiment.

> "The model reveals no main effect of condition on learning, but CE1 and BL1 predicted post-test scores."
