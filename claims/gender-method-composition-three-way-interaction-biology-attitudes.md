# Gender, teaching method, and gender-group composition show a statistically significant three-way interaction on undergraduate biology students’ attitudes

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In nonmajor biology classes, the effect of teaching method (CL vs TL) on attitudes depended on the interaction of student gender and gender-group composition (single vs mixed). [→ Almasri Firas 2021](#almasri-firas-2021)

## Evidence

### Almasri Firas 2021

Almasri Firas, Hewapathirana Gertrude I., Ghaddar Fatme, Lee Nick, Ibrahim Bashar. (2021). Measuring attitudes towards biology major and non-major: Effect of students’ gender, group composition, and learning environment. PLoS ONE 16(5): e0251453. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251453

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Three-way ANOVA in the Results section on nonmajor biology students’ attitude scores, testing gender × teaching method × gender composition. The article reports F (1, 2367) = 139.848, p<0.001, "η2 = 0.056, R2 = 0.451".

> "Hypothesis 1 and Hypothesis 2 were tested and there was a statistically significant three-way interaction between gender, teaching method, and gender composition 4W,2W+2M,4M; (F (1, 2367) = 139.848, p<0.001 η2 = 0.056, R2 = 0.451)."
