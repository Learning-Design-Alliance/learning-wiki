q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual analysis within Dialogical Self Theory: because the synthetic position is frictionless and always available, "consulting it is easier than convening the inner conversation", so the work of surveying and integrating voices migrates outward. This is a theoretical argument with no data.

"When habitually enlisted, the synthetic position tends to absorb the integrative authorship the theory assigns to the meta-position and the promoter position, a process we term synthetic promotion, producing a condition we name authorial suspension."

Related Claims¶

  • Installing the synthetic position as promoter can raise self-narrative coherence while lowering authorship of it (authorship-coherence dissociation) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Repeated reflective prompts may habituate, so scaffolding can replace rather than strengthen the user's own metacognitive habits — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Outcome turns on the structural location the synthetic position occupies, not the volume of use; population-level studies indexing use alone should record a washed-out average effect — related
  • People may absorb bias from AI and carry it beyond their interactions with the algorithm — related
  • Authorial suspension is marked by a fluency-ownership gap, deferral of commitment despite exhausted deliberation, and an overview unavailable on withdrawal
