Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

RM-ANOVA of accuracy normalised to control across four conditions in 49 stressed teachers; "a signi ﬁcant main effect of condition" with ηp2 = 0.44. Bonferroni-corrected t-tests showed SpeNeut accuracy (M = −3.71) significantly lower than GenNeg, GenNeut, and SpeNeg.
