### Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto 2025

Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto, Panúncio-Pinto Maria Paula, Bollela Valdes Roberto, de Almeida Troncon Luiz Ernesto. (2025). Faculty development for undergraduate student programmatic assessment: a Brazilian multi-centered longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07337-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-assessment of knowledge on a seven-point scale (adapted from Bhanji et al.) completed retrospectively before and after workshops by 297 participants (82.7%), analysed with Wilcoxon matched pairs signed rank tests. The article reports significant increases for all topics, with prior knowledge lowest for fundamental concepts of programmatic assessment.

> "Perceived levels of knowledge after the workshops were significantly higher than those before for all topics evaluated, for the participants of both the Centralized (p = 0.0001) and the various Decentralized workshop (p levels lower than 0.001 for all topics)."
