Descriptive analysis of national survey data on beginning-teacher preparation experiences by race/ethnicity (2020–21). The report states "Only about half of beginning teachers of color" had taken any teaching classes prior to teaching, versus 59% of beginning White teachers.

"Only about half of beginning teachers of color—including just 37% of Black and 50% of Latino/a beginning teachers—had taken any teaching classes prior to teaching, compared to 59% of beginning White teachers."
