### Alsamadani 2017

Hashem Ahmed Alsamadani. (2017). The Effect of Talking Story Books on Saudi Young EFL Learners’ Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n5p204

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

MANCOVA between-subjects result for the four-week intervention (experimental n=40, control n=39). The article reports "F (2, 76) = 47.51, p = 0.00, partial eta squared = 0.56", stating 56% of variance in retelling is explained by the talking story books; post means were 22.1 (experimental) versus 14.8 (control).
