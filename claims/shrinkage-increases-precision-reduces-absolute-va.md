# Empirical Bayes shrinkage increases the precision of value-added estimates and reduces their absolute value for teachers of hard-to-predict students

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Herrmann 2013

Herrmann, M., Walsh, E., Isenberg, E., & Resch, A. (2013). Shrinkage of Value-Added Estimates and Characteristics of Students with Hard-to-Predict Achievement Levels. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/shrinkage-of-valueadded-estimates-and-characteristics-of-students-with-hardtopredict-achievement-levels

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The paper's analysis of empirical Bayes shrinkage applied to teacher value-added estimates finds that shrinkage "increases their estimates’ precision and reduces the absolute value of their value-added estimates" for teachers of hard-to-predict students. No effect size is printed in the available text.
