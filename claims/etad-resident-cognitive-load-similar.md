## Evidence

### Braund H 2024

Braund H, Hall AK, Caners K, Walker M, Dagnone D, Sherbino J, Sibbald M, Wang B, Howes D, Day AG, Wu W, Szulewski A. (2024). Evaluating the Value of Eye-Tracking Augmented Debriefing in Medical Simulation—A Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000825

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT, residents rated total, intrinsic, and extraneous cognitive load on 1–9 single-item scales after each debrief; arm averages were compared by linear regression controlling for site. The adjusted difference of "0.2, 95% CI, −0.2 to 0.6, P = 0.38" was not significant.

> "Averaging the items across stations 1 and 2, we find that the CL was similar between arms (eye tracking minus control mean adjusted difference controlling for site = 0.2, 95% CI, −0.2 to 0.6, P = 0.38)."
