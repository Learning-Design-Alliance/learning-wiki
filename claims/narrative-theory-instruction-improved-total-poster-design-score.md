## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with narrative theory scored significantly higher on total poster design score than control students taught by the case method (M 84.87 vs 81.20, F(1, 88) = 7.89, p = .009). [→ Chao-Ming Yang & Tzu-Fan Hsu 2017](#chao-ming-yang-tzu-fan-hsu-2017)

## Evidence

### Chao-Ming Yang & Tzu-Fan Hsu 2017

Chao-Ming Yang & Tzu-Fan Hsu. (2017). New Perspective on Visual Communication Design Education: An Empirical Study of Applying Narrative Theory to Graphic Design Courses. International Journal of Higher Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v6n2p188

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA of posttest scores in a matched-group teaching experiment: 30 freshmen split into an experimental group receiving narrative theory instruction and a control group receiving case-method instruction over 4 weeks. The experimental group's total score was significantly higher, "a significance level of .009 (p < .01)". No effect size is printed.

> "for the total score, a significance level of .009 (p < .01) was reached between the groups with F(1, 88) = 7.89 (Mexperimental group = 84.87 > Mcontrol group = 81.20"
