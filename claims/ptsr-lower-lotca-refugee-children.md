### Belhan Çelik 2025

Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary screening analysis of 82 Syrian refugee children aged 13–16 (M = 14.32, SD = 0.61), comparing 34 children with PTSR (mean CPTS-RI 30.58) to 34 without (mean 6.82) using a non-parametric Mann–Whitney U test on LOTCA scores.

> "As shown in Table 1, children with PTSR scored significantly lower in all LOTCA subdomains except for orientation."
