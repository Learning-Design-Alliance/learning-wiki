q2 · i? · associational · r2

Re-analysis of the ERS state assessment data excluding IL and OH districts. The revised estimate of ".007 SDs" in the remaining 24 states leads the authors to conclude there was essentially no ELA recovery in those states.

"After excluding Illinois and Ohio from the full ERS sample, the ERS data indicate an average ELA recovery in the remaining 24 states of .007 SDs (much lower than .019 reported when IL and OH are included)."
