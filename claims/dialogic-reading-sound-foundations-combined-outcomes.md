## Evidence

### WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading 2007

WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education, Revised February 8, 2007. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

RCT with 167 at-risk low-income four-year-olds from four Head Start centers, comparing Dialogic Reading plus adapted Sound Foundations to regular Head Start services over 30 weeks. The review reports a significant difference "favoring the intervention group on the Print concepts factor", confirmed by WWC.

> "Whitehurst, Epstein, et al. (1994) reported a statistically significant difference favoring the intervention group on the Print concepts factor."

### WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading 2007 (2)

WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education, Revised February 8, 2007. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Same Whitehurst, Epstein, et al. (1994) Head Start RCT; the review reports improvement indices of "+24 percentile points" for print knowledge and +20 for early reading/writing, versus +6 for oral language (not significant) and +1 for phonological processing. No standardized effect sizes are printed for these findings.

> "The improvement index for print knowledge is +24 percentile points for the one print knowl-
