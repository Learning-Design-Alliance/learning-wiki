Evidence¶

Stephen Lipscomb 2023¶

Stephen Lipscomb, Ijun Lai, Duncan Chaplin, Alma Vigil, Hena Matthias. (2023). Staff Attrition from Pennsylvania Public Schools During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Mathematica. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational analysis relating elementary teachers' remote learning conditions to attrition in Pennsylvania public schools during the pandemic. The brief reports no association with increased attrition; no effect size or test statistic is printed, so the finding is reported as a non-association, not tested equivalence.

"Remote learning was not associated with increased attrition among elementary school teachers."
