## Evidence

### John F. Arnold and Leesa A. Sidhu 2014

John F. Arnold and Leesa A. Sidhu. (2014). A Statistical Analysis of the Performance of First Year Engineering Students at UNSW Canberra and the Impact of the State Where They Undertook Year 12 Study. UNSW Canberra. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.07400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.07400)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' consideration of mean ATAR scores across jurisdictions, reported in the conclusion section, rules out lower entering ability on ATAR as the explanation, since Queensland means were "either not statistically significant or significantly higher".

> "the differences between the mean ATAR scores of Queensland students joining UNSW Canberra are either not statistically significant or significantly higher than students from these other jurisdictions."
