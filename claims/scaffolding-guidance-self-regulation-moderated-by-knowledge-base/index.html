Evidence¶

Olga Chernikova 2020¶

Olga Chernikova, Nicole Heitzmann, Maximilian Christian Fink, Venance Timothy, Tina Seidel, Frank Fischer, DFG Research group COSIMA. (2020). Facilitating Diagnostic Competences in Higher Education—a Meta-Analysis in Medical and Teacher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09492-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analytic moderator analysis comparing scaffolding types positioned on a self-regulation continuum against learners' coded professional knowledge base (low vs. high). The abstract reports the direction of the interaction; no separate effect size for this moderator is printed in the supplied text.

"Scaffolding types providing high levels of guidance are more effective for less advanced learners, whereas scaffolding types relying on high levels of self-regulation are more effective for advanced learners."
