Evidence¶

Gulfer Capar 2015¶

Gulfer Capar, Kamuran Tarim. (2015). Efficacy of the Cooperative Learning Method on Mathematics Achievement and Attitude: A Meta-Analysis Research. Educational Sciences Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.2.2098

q3 · i?

Publication bias analysis in the meta-analysis comparing 23 published comparisons (refereed journal articles and reports) with 13 unpublished studies (MA or Ph.D. theses). The article reports "no significant differences were seen" between published and unpublished effect sizes.

"the average effect size for the published studies was d+ = 0.44, whereas the average effect size for the unpublished studies was d+ = 0.88. In terms of the effect among the grades, no significant differences were seen between the effect sizes of the published and unpublished studies (QB = 3.97; p = 0.70)."
