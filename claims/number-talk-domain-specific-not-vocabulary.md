## Evidence

### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2011

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Levine Susan C. (2011). Some types of parent number talk count more than others: Relations between parents' input and children's cardinal-number knowledge. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2011.01050.x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null secondary analysis in the Results section (N=43 for PPVT). Vocabulary was correlated with SES (r(41)=.58, p<.001) and parents' overall word tokens (r(41)=.52, p<.001), but number talk added nothing, supporting domain specificity. No effect sizes printed.

> "in a partial correlation controlling for parents' SES and overall non-number talk, we found no significant correlations between children's vocabulary knowledge and any of the 4 types of parent number talk (all p's>.35)"
