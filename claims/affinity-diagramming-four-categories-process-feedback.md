Qualitative analysis of open-ended survey answers from 12 anonymized participants (P1–P12), analyzed in Spanish by three researchers over about 5 h using affinity diagramming. The article reports "four main categories"; no effect size applies.

> "The affinity diagramming process resulted in four main categories: course feedback methodology, understanding and improving the medical procedure, intervention improvements, and self-awareness, each with several subcategories."
