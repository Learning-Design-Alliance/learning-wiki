Evidence¶

James Soland 2021¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th grade: The role of social-emotional learning trajectories. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12463

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The article's framing of prior research in its introduction: it reports that studies indicate a successful transition depends on both academic preparation and SEL skills for independence. No effect size is printed; this is background literature as the article attributes it.

"Studies also indicate that a successful transition is a function not only of how prepared the students are academically but also whether they have the social-emotional learning (SEL) skills to succeed in a more independent secondary school environment."
