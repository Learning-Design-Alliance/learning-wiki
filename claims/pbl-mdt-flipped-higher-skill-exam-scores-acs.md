BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08150-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Skills assessment within the same randomized two-group clerkship study (n = 50 per group). The results text states "the skill exam scores of the experimental group (78.46 ± 4.97) were also higher than those of the control group (75.30 ± 4.29)"; Table 2 prints t = 3.403, P = 0.001. Inter-rater reliability for the skill examination was Cohen's kappa 0.89. No effect size is reported.
