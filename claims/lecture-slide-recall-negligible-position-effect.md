`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational memory test of the 'Fifty Years of Punch' lecture: thirty attendees wrote recognizable descriptions of each slide eight days later, and frequencies per slide were tabulated (Table I, grouped in Table II). The author concludes there are "other causes at work which produce fluctuations much greater than any effect due merely to position".
