Novice providers' success in performing lumbar puncture: a randomized controlled phantom study between a conventional spinal needle and a novel bioimpedance needle. Same randomized controlled phantom trial of 60 LP-naive medical students, 30 per group. The Results section reports first-attempt success of "5 (16.7%)" conventional versus "15 (50%)" bioimpedance cases, analysed by the independent samples proportion procedure.

> "The procedures were successful at the first attempt in 5 (16.7%) and 15 (50%) cases (p = 0.006), respectively."
