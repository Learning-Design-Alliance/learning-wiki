## Evidence

### Brouwers Melissa C 2011

Brouwers Melissa C, Makarski Julie, Durocher Lisa D, Levinson Anthony J. (2011). E-learning interventions are comparable to user's manual in a randomized trial of training strategies for the AGREE II. Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-81

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the trial's headline null result from the Discussion, restating the Results: 87 participants were randomized 1:1:1 and 60 completed (20 per condition), each appraising one of ten test PGs. No effect sizes are printed for the condition contrasts.

> "In contrast to our hypotheses, participants randomized to the training condition that included the Tutorial + Practice Exercise did not demonstrate superior performance with the AGREE II, greater satisfaction with the training experience, higher levels of self-efficacy, or more positive attitudes toward the tool than did participants randomized to the other two conditions."
