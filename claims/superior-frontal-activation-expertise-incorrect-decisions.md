## Evidence

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary ROI extraction (insula, anterior cingulate, middle and superior frontal regions) in the same fMRI study; this condition-specific contrast reached significance without a printed effect size.

> "During incorrect decisions, activity in bilateral superior frontal region was lower for High Expertise pilots (0.51±0.50) compared to Moderate Expertise pilots (2.26±1.43) (p = .0174)."

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014 (2)

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null result across secondary ROIs for all decisions, with one near-threshold region; descriptive means show lower activity in High Expertise pilots (0.61±0.50) than Moderate (1.29±0.88).

> "Although there were no statistically significant expertise-related differences in these secondary ROIs, the bilateral middle frontal region approached significance (p = .051)."
