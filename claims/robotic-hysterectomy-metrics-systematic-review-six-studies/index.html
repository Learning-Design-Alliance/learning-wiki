The review's own PRISMA-guided search of OVID MEDLINE and EMBASE in May 2023 identified six eligible studies, reporting that "The initial search yielded 531 articles, of which 3 were included."

Arcamo K 2025 (2)¶

Arcamo K, Murugappan S, Larkins K, Mohan H, Costello A, Pendlebury A, McNally O, McBain R. (2025). Determining the metrics of competence in robotic hysterectomy: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02471-6

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis concludes that "the lack of standardized and validated metrics specific to robotic hysterectomy" persists, and that established tools cannot reliably differentiate skill level.

"This review highlights the lack of standardized and validated metrics specific to robotic hysterectomy, as well as the inability of established assessment tools to differentiate between robotic surgical skill level."
