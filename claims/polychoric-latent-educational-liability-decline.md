## Evidence

### Van Hootegem 2023

Van Hootegem, A., Røgeberg, O., Bratsberg, B., & Lyngstad, T. H. (2023). Correlation between cognitive ability and educational attainment weakens over birth cohorts. Scientific Reports, 13, 17747. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-44605-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Polychoric-correlation variant treating both stanine scores and binned attainment as discretized latent continuous constructs, with latent thresholds varying across cohorts. The series is "consistently higher" than the Pearson series but shows "the same pattern with a long term secular decline".

> "While the correlation starts slightly under 0.75 for the cohort born in 1950, it declines steadily to just above 0.65 in more recent cohorts."
