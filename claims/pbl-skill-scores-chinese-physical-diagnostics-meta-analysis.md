## Evidence

### Wang 2016

Wang, J., Xu, Y., Liu, X., Xiong, W., Xie, J., & Zhao, J. (2016). Assessing the effectiveness of problem-based learning in physical diagnostics education in China: a meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5093758/

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 10 studies with usable skill-score data (1599 medical students; PBL group = 805, control group = 794) found "a significant difference in skill scores (SMD = 1.46, 95%CI [0.89, 2.02], P < 0.001) in favor of PBL". Heterogeneity was high (I2 = 96%).

> "The analytical results showed a significant difference in skill scores (SMD = 1.46, 95%CI [0.89, 2.02], P < 0.001) in favor of PBL, compared with the traditional teaching (Fig. 4)."
