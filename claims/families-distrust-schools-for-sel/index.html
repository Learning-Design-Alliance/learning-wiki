Duchesneau 2020¶

Duchesneau, N. (2020). Social, Emotional, and Academic Development Through an Equity Lens (#ReEnvisionSEL). The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/social-emotional-and-academic-development-through-an-equity-lens/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus group study found participants argued school systems and many educators are "too biased, fail to incorporate student and family voice, and are systemically racist," making them unprepared to implement pull-out programs or explicit social-emotional curricula reflecting students' needs.

"when asked whether they trust the education system to teach students of color the social-emotional factors they valued, MOST PARTICIPANTS RESPONDED WITH A RESOUNDING "NO.""
