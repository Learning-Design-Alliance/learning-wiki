### Wolfram 2023

Wolfram, T. & Morris, D. (2023). Conventional twin studies overestimate the environmental differences between families relevant to educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 8, 24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00173-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative re-analysis presented in Table 2, compiling sibling and DZ twin correlations for educational attainment from registers and twin studies in Finland, Germany, Norway, Sweden, the UK, and the USA. DZ twin correlations were "invariably higher" than sibling correlations.
