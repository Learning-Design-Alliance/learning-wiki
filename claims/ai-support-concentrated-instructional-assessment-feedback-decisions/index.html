Frequency analysis of inductively coded decision types across the review's 27 included empirical studies. The review reports that "instructional decisions were the most frequently coded category," with curriculum (4, 14.8%) and learning environment management (3, 11.1%) least coded; percentages refer to the proportion of the 27 included studies and categories were not mutually exclusive.

"instructional decisions were the most frequently coded category across the 27 reviewed studies (23 studies, 85.2%), followed by assessment (14, 51.9%) and feedback decisions (11, 40.7%)."
