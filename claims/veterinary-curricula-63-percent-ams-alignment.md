Ade-Yusuf Oyinlola Rodhiat et al. (2026). From classrooms to practice: does pre-service training for one health professions provide readiness for antimicrobial stewardship? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09843-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Curriculum-mapping analysis of two veterinary pre-service curricula (UniLorin, EISMV) against AMS-relevant indicators derived from the WOAH Day-1 competencies. The article reports overall alignment of 63%, with UniLorin at 77% and EISMV at 48%. No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, AMS alignment with WOAH Day-one competency recommendations was 63% (Table 5). Veterinary pre-service curricula showed consistently higher alignment on clinical competencies, but lower alignment on broader public health competencies. By universities, UniLorin recorded higher alignment (77%) than EISMV (48%)"
