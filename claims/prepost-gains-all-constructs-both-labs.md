## Evidence

### Markert 2026

Markert, E., Verostek, M., Dew, M., Chen, X., Lory-Moran, M., Padavan, S., Walia, H., Wittich, P., and Holmes, N.G. (2026). CURE-like, not cure-all: Varying broad relevance in experimentation labs produces similar student outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.00464)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pre/post statistics (Fig. 3, Table V) from surveys in both semesters and both lab conditions. Scores increased for every construct; "the largest effect sizes consistently occur for self-efficacy (d >0.8)" and the smallest for recognition (0.1< d <0.3) and belonging (0.2< d <0.5). The article prints ranges, not exact effect-size values, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Using both listwise deletion and multiple imputation, the largest effect sizes consistently occur for self-efficacy (d >0.8) and the smallest for recognition (0.1< d <0.3) and belonging (0.2< d <0.5)."
