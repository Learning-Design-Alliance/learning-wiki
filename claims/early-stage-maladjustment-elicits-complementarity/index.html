Evidence¶

Dietzel 1974¶

Dietzel, C. S.; Abeles, Norman. (1974). Client-Therapist Complementarity and Therapeutic Outcome. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED091646 doi:10.1037/h0076731

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis relating each client's maladjustment index (distance of the early-session behavioral coordinate from the Circumplex grid center) to early-stage therapist complementarity. The study reports a "rank correlation coefficient r' = +.51 (p < .02)", supporting the prediction that severely maladjusted clients elicit more complementarity.

"rank correlation coefficient r' = +.51 (p < .02) was obtained.This significant correlation supported Hypothesis V which proposed that more severely maladjqsted clients would elicit higher levels of complementarity from their therapists."
