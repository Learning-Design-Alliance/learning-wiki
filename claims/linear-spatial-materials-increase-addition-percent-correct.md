## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schiffman J 2018

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in the same randomized kindergarten experiment (n = 29) found a "time by condition interaction, F(1,26) = 4.615, p = .05, ηp2 = .14". Percent correct rose from 33% to 55% in the linear-spatial condition (Hedges' g = .74) but only from 24% to 28% in the irregular condition (g = .15).
