# First-Conscientiousness-decile students have worse ARCP outcomes in year 2 of training but not in year 1

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` First-decile candidates had a higher average ARCP score (more outcomes other than 1) in year 2 of training (p=0.019, n=517) but not in year 1. [→ Sawdon Marina 2020](#sawdon-marina-2020)

## Evidence

### Sawdon Marina 2020

Sawdon Marina, McLachlan JC. (2020). '10% of your medical students will cause 90% of your problems': a prospective correlational study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

T-test comparing first-CI-decile candidates' ARCP scores with other candidates found a significant difference in year 2 of training (p=0.019, n=517) but no significant difference in year 1. Only p-values are printed for this contrast.

> "First decile candidates had a higher average score (indicating more outcomes other than 1), as shown by t-test in year 2 of training (p=0.019, n=517), but not in year 1."

### Sawdon Marina 2020 (2)

Sawdon Marina, McLachlan JC. (2020). '10% of your medical students will cause 90% of your problems': a prospective correlational study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Odds ratio analysis of first-CI-decile students failing to achieve the optimum ARCP outcome, reported as OR=1.6126 (CI: 1.1400 to 2.2809, p=0.0069, n=618). The OR is a printed effect-size-like statistic but its magnitude is modest.

> "The OR that students in the first decile of the CI score failed to achieve the optimum ARCP outcome was 1.6126 (CI: 1.1400 to 2.2809, p=0.0069, n=618)."
