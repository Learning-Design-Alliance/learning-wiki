Survey descriptive results on access to structured guidance among 102 Swedish ED clinicians. "About 42% reported having access to clinic‐level practices/informal protocols" while a strong majority (84%) wanted national guidelines.

> "About 42% reported having access to clinic‐level practices/informal protocols for maladaptive exercise management, while 34% had no such clinic‐level practices/informal protocols and the remaining 24% did not know if there were any."
