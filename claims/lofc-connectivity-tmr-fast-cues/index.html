Evidence¶

Kawasoe 2026¶

Kawasoe, R., Matsumura, K., Shinohara, T., Arima, K., Takeo, Y., Ikeda, T. & Sugata, H. (2026). Wakeful targeted memory reactivation during short rest periods modulates early motor learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00407-9

q2 · i? · causal · r2

EEG source-level lagged-coherence connectivity analysis of rest-interval data comparing the TMRfast and TMRregular groups, using a liberal exploratory FDR threshold (q = 0.3). The article cautions the findings should be interpreted with caution and confirmed with stringent thresholds.

"The TMRfast group showed significantly stronger FC in the alpha band between the left lOFC and right pars orbitalis (t = 4.32, p < 0.05, FDR-corrected) (Fig. 5), as well as with the left rostral middle frontal gyrus (RMFG) (t = 4.46, p < 0.05, FDR-corrected)"
