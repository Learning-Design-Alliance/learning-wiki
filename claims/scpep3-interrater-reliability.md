`q2 i?` Polychoric correlation coefficients between two independent raters averaged 0.64–0.93 across the ten Performance subtests, described as moderate to very large correlations. [→ Yu Lu 2019](#yu-lu-2019)

## Evidence

### Yu Lu 2019

Yu Lu, Zhu Xiaoqin, Shek Daniel T. L., Zou Xiao-Bing, Deng Hong-Zhu, Au Yeung Peter W. H. (2019). Validation of the Simplified Chinese Psychoeducational Profile Third Edition in Mainland China. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 49(4), 1599-1612. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3827-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability analysis on a subsample of 59 children with ASD (ages 2–6) rated on the same day by two experienced researchers. The article reports coefficients "ranged from 0.64 to 0.93"; seven items with biased coefficients were excluded from the aggregates.

> "The mean and median polychoric correlation coefficients for each Performance subtest ranged from 0.64 to 0.93, suggesting moderate to very large correlations (Hopkins and Stanley 1981)."
