### Adrie A. Koehler 2023

Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mixed-methods study of 54 female EFL students at an Iranian language institute; the experimental group listened to podcasts and the control group to radio programs, then both transcribed, summarized, and discussed them. The review reports the podcast group "achieved significantly higher scores than the participants from the control group, t(52) = 3.45; p = .001"; students also held positive perceptions of educational podcast use.

> "the participants in the experimental group achieved significantly higher scores than the participants from the control group, t(52) = 3.45; p = .001, suggesting that the podcast had a greater effect on EFL students' self-regulation"
