### Hörsch 2026

Hörsch, H., Schumacher, J., & Wolff, F. (2026). Long-Term Effects of an Intervention Targeting Dimensional and Temporal Comparisons on Students' Math and English Academic Self-Concepts: A Waiting-List Replication and Extension Study. Within the same models, "the math self-concept change scores correlated positively with the change scores of the temporal comparison-related belief directly" after the intervention in both groups, with printed correlations r = .30 (Group A) and r = .28 (Group B), supporting Hypothesis 5; correlations at follow-ups were also positive.
