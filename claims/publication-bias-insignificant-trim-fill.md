## Evidence

### Erdem Cahit 2026

Erdem Cahit, Kaya Metin, Polat Mustafa, Eğmir Eray. (2026). Thinking and feeling matter: a second-order meta-analysis of student characteristics across developmental domains and their effect on academic outcomes. BMC Psychology, 14, 919. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05035-x

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication-bias analysis of the 71-effect-size data set: Egger's regression test non-significant; trim-and-fill added four studies (5.6% of effect sizes), adjusting the mean from r = 0.26 to r = 0.25, a difference of about 0.008, interpreted as insignificant publication bias.

> "the Egger’s regression test results (t = 0.95; p = 0.34) also show the absence of publication bias. Duvall and Tweedie Trim and Fill test results required adding four studies to the left of the mean effect size."
