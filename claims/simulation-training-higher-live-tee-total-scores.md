## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Residents trained on the 3D simulator scored significantly higher on the 110-point live TEE assessment (88.60 ± 15.60 vs 64.70 ± 16.70, p < 0.001). [→ Ou Xinxu 2026](#ou-xinxu-2026)

### Ou Xinxu 2026

(2026). 3D models and simulations training improves TEE performance on pediatric patients with simple valvular congenital heart disease compared to traditional apprenticeship training: a clinical randomized controlled trial.
