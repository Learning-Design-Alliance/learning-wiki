A student response in class 3 of the qualitative corpus illustrates learning through error in the safe simulation environment. The participant described applying classroom knowledge in a "safer, controlled manner that allowed me to test things and receive feedback without posing risks".

> "I was able to apply classroom knowledge to a real-world scenario, but in a safer, controlled manner that allowed me to test things and receive feedback without posing risks to the patients or ourselves (e17)"
