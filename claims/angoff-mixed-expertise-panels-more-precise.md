## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2016

Shulruf Boaz, Wilkinson Tim, Weller Jennifer, Jones Philip, Poole Phillippa. (2016). Insights into the Angoff method: results from a simulation study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0656-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the Fig. 3 simulation results: because experts tend to be more stringent, homogeneous panels are biased, so "a mixed-expertise panel" gives the most precise cut-score. No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "but it also suggests that a panel which has only experts or only non-experts would yield a cut-score which is less precise than a cut-score yielded by a mixed-expertise panel (Fig. 3), particularly given the already-documented association between stringency and expertise"
