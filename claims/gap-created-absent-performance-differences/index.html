Evidence¶

Autin 2019¶

Autin, Frédérique; Batruch, Anatolia; Butera, Fabrizio. (2019). The function of selection of assessment leads evaluators to artificially create the social class achievement gap. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000307

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The experiments assessed a single test supposedly produced by a high- or low-SES student, so any difference in evaluated mistakes reflects evaluator bias rather than actual performance. The article frames this as creating the gap "even in the absence of performance differences"; no effect size is printed.

"We hypothesized that even in the absence of performance differences, assessment’s function of selection (i.e., compare, rank and track students) leads evaluators to create a SES achievement gap."
