# Anesthesia residents' self-reported self-confidence for managing OR circulation, CVC, and pain-complication emergencies rose from pre-test to post-test after PBL training, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-PBL self-confidence (combined strongly agree/agree ratings) rose in all four topic categories: airway 35% to 72%, circulation 21% to 80%, CVC 49% to 86%, pain management 17% to 80% (all P<0.001). [→ Komasawa Nobuyasu 2018](#komasawa-nobuyasu-2018)

## Evidence

### Komasawa Nobuyasu 2018

Komasawa Nobuyasu, Berg Benjamin W., Minami Toshiaki. (2018). Problem-based learning for anesthesia resident operating room crisis management training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0207594

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey of 35 anesthesia residents across three ALS-Op PBL courses, chi-square comparison of 5-point self-confidence ratings before and immediately after training. The article reports combined strongly agree/agree ratings "increased from pre-course ratings in all categories" with the printed percentages and P values.

> "35% pre- to 72% post- (P<0.001) in airway, 21% pre- to 80% post- (P<0.001) in circulation, 49% pre- to 86% post- (P<0.001) in CVC, and 17% pre- to 80% post- (P<0.001) in pain management related complications."
