What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: The Expert Mathematician, Middle School Mathematics, Revised October 14, 2006. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/513

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The WWC's intervention rating for the math achievement domain, based on one study meeting evidence standards. Appendix A4 states the program "did not meet the criteria for positive effects because it had only one study" and that the one study "showed a substantively important positive effect."
