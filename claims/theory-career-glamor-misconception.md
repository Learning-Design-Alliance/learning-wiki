### Mike Verostek 2024

Mike Verostek, Molly Griston, Jesús Botello, and Benjamin Zwickl. (2024). Beyond The Big Bang Theory: Revealing the everyday research lives of theoretical physics faculty. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1119/5.0145050](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0145050)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of 11 theoretical physics faculty from five colleges and universities; thematic analysis of misconceptions. One faculty member stated students have "a false impression that every day you're going to make a breakthrough."

> "Even some people who have worked with me, who are actually very smart and very good at it, they have a false impression that every day you're going to make a breakthrough. The expectations are not realistic."
