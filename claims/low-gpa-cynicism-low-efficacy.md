Epidemiology of Burnout and Its Association with Academic Performance Among Medical Students at Hail University, Saudi Arabia. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate analysis of the cross-sectional survey (N = 218) shows GPA gradients for cynicism (80.0% versus 45.8%; P = 0.009) and low professional efficacy (57.1% versus 22.9%; P = 0.001), as reported in Results and Table 1.

> "The highest rate of cynicism was observed among students whose GPAs ranged between 2.01–2.50, while the lowest rate was observed among those whose GPAs ranged between 3.51–4.00 (80.0% versus 45.8%; P = 0.009). The highest rate of low professional efficacy was observed among students whose GPAs were ≤2.0 while the lowest rate was observed among those whose GPAs ranged between 3.51–4.00 (57.1% versus 22.9%; P = 0.001)."
