Evidence¶

Matthew Inglis 2008¶

Matthew Inglis, Juan Pablo Mejia-Ramos. (2008). The Effect of Authority on the Persuasiveness of Mathematical Arguments. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370000802584513

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 3 with 214 research-active mathematicians used reworded instructions asking how persuaded they were that the claim is true given only the argument; both authority effects replicated with no effect size printed.

"Participants in the named condition found the heuristic argument to be more persuasive than those in the anonymous condition, t(212.0)=2.75, p=0.007, with the magnitude of the difference in means similar to that found in the previous experiments. A similar result was found in the visual argument, t(208)=2.37, p=0.019"
