### Sohlin Amalie Middelboe 2026

Sohlin Amalie Middelboe, Hoffmann Ida Madeline, Poulsen Anja, Gjærde Line Klingen, Chang Todd P., Lee Joy Yeonjoo, Overbeck Gritt, Lund Stine, Sørensen Jette Led, Kjærgaard Jesper. (2026). European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-06898-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised pilot trial: 12 interprofessional doctor-nurse dyads trained in four immersive VR scenarios, video-recorded managing a mannequin-based paediatric emergency at baseline and 1-month follow-up, rated by two blinded raters. The article reports a significant improvement in teamwork measured with CTS® in both groups.

> "We found a significant improvement from baseline to follow-up in teamwork measured with CTS® (facilitator-guided mean 1.2, CI 0.5 to 1.9, p = 0.005; self-guided mean 1.4, CI 0.8 to 2.0, p < 0.001)"
