## Evidence

### Fodor Gergely H. 2025

Fodor Gergely H., Tolnai József, Rárosi Ferenc, Nagy Attila, Peták Ferenc. (2025). Artificial intelligence-based chatbots improve the efficiency of course orientation among medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08146-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 75 first-year ESL medical students assigned to a custom-trained ChatGPT chatbot (n = 35) or the e-learning syllabus (n = 40) on a six-item course-requirements test. The AI group's "median 5.0 [IQR 4.8 − 6.0]" exceeded the Traditional group's 3.5, P < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.29 (95% CI 0.79–1.78).

> "The AI group scored significantly higher (median 5.0 [IQR 4.8 − 6.0], out of 6) than the Traditional group (3.5 [2.8 − 4.3], P < 0.001)."
