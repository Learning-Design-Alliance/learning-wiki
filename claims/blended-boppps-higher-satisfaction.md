Impacts of blended learning with BOPPPS model on Chinese medical undergraduate students: a comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis of 44 studies. `q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 6 studies with 532 students pooling questionnaire-based satisfaction as an odds ratio. The pooled estimate was "OR = 1.381"; no standardized effect size was printed for this outcome.

> "The mixed BOPPPS teaching mode was related to high satisfaction (OR = 1.381; 95% CI = 1.261–1.512; I2 = 68.5%; P = 0.007)"
