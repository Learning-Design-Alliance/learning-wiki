Perception finding from interviews with central office administrators and school principals in the implementation study. Leadership support at three levels was seen as key, with escalated collaboration depending on "explicit support from these leaders."

> "Leaders—at the city, central office, and school levels—are perceived to play a key role in promoting collaboration. Escalated conversation and action around cross-sector collaboration will depend on explicit support from these leaders."
