## Evidence

### Arendale 2014

David R. Arendale. (2014). Understanding the Integrated Learning Course Model: Academic Transition to Tertiary Education. International Journal of Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v3n4p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental fall 2002 study of eight TRIO SSS students in the IL course linked to introductory psychology, compared with a matched-pairs control (mean ACT 14.5) and a TRIO control (mean ACT 13.7). The article reports "F(2, 29)=6.53, p<.01" on final total points; no effect size is printed.

> "The overall ANOV A revealed F(2, 29)=6.53, p<.01 and the Scheffe contrasts showed the [IL course] students differed significantly from the TRIO controls (p<.01)"
