### Madsgaard 2022

Madsgaard, A., Smith‐Strøm, H., Hunskår, I., & Røykenes, K. (2022). A rollercoaster of emotions: An integrative review of emotions and its impact on health professional students' learning in simulation‐based education. Nursing Open, 9, 108–121. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1100

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing DeMaria et al. (2010), a quantitative intervention study with 25 medical students and a control group: the intervention group, which "experienced greater anxiety than the control group on both anxiety score and heart rate", achieved higher practical competency examination results.

> "The intervention group achieved higher practical competency examination than the control group."
