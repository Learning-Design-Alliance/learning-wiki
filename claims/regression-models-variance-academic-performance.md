## Evidence

### Hou Yujiao 2020

Hou Yujiao, Mei Guang, Liu Yutong, Xu Weisheng. (2020). Physical Fitness with Regular Lifestyle Is Positively Related to Academic Performance among Chinese Medical and Dental Students. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/5602395

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six multiple linear regression models were developed with basic course grades, professional course grades, and fitness test scores as dependent variables. All models were statistically significant at p < 0.001 with R² values ranging from 0.24 to 0.58.
