### Axt Steffen 2018

Axt Steffen, Storz Pirmin, Ehrenberg Carolin, Falch Claudius, Immenroth Marc, Kirschniak Andreas, Muller Sven. (2018). Evaluation of self-educational training methods to learn laparoscopic skills - a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 18:85. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1193-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial, medians with interquartile ranges show the primer group completed knots faster than the video-only group; the trial reports the difference as "significant different" with p = 0.001. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Especially median knotting time per completed knot was significant different between Group B and Group A (308 s [100–1221] versus 394 s [138–1397] respectively, p = 0.001)."
