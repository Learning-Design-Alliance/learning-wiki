### Kaldaras 2023

Kaldaras, L., & Wieman, C. (2023). Introducing an Instructional Model for Teaching Blended Math-Science Sensemaking in Undergraduate STEM Courses Using Computer Simulations. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post level assignment in an algebra-based introductory physics course (73 students, paper-pencil lab activity). Most students (68) started at level 1 "Pattern" sub-level; upon completion most transitioned to level 3 (conceptual). The article reports "a clear progression of all 73 students from the lowest, level 1 to the highest, level 3".

> "Overall, there is a clear progression of all 73 students from the lowest, level 1 to the highest, level 3 of the cognitive model."
