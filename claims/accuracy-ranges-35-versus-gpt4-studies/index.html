Evidence¶

Jin Hye Kyung 2024¶

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Descriptive synthesis of the 23 included studies (17 medicine, 3 pharmacy, 2 nursing, 1 dentistry) published 2022-2024 across multiple countries. The review reports "varying accuracy levels, ranging from 36 to 77% for ChatGPT-3.5 and 64.4–100% for GPT-4".
