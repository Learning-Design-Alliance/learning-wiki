## Evidence

### So Wi-Young 2012

So Wi-Young. (2012). Association between physical activity and academic performance in Korean adolescent students. BMC Public Health 12, 258. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-12-258

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted multivariate logistic regression on KYRBWS-V data for 35,454 girls comparing moderate PA frequencies with no moderate PA; once, 4 times, and 5+ times weekly were not statistically significant (e.g., 5+ times OR = 0.904, p = 0.111).

> "those who participated in moderate PA twice a week (OR = 1.113, 95% CI = 1.008–1.229, p = 0.035) and thrice a week (OR = 1.306, 95% CI = 1.161–1.468, p < 0.001)"
