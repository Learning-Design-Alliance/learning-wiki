### Kim J 2021

Kim J, Shin EJ, Han KH, Park S, Youn JH, Jin G, Lee JY. (2021). Efficacy of Smart Speaker–Based Metamemory Training in Older Adults: Case-Control Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/20177

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Case-control cohort analysis (MMT n=29) reports digit span backward improvement at "P<.001; t=4.52; Cohen d=0.84"; the model II interaction remained significant (η²=0.10, P=.02, Table 3). Control group change was not significant (P=.69).
