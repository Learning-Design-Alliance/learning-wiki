Descriptive trend analysis of high school out-mobility components in Chapter 1 (Figures 4A and 4C). The report states the school-year dropout rate fell from "7.1 percent" in 1995 to "3.2 percent by 2006," and the summer dropout rate fell from 5.8 to 2.8 percent.

"In 1995, 7.1 percent of high school students dropped out at some point during the school year. This number dropped to 3.2 percent by 2006, a decrease of more than 50 percent."
