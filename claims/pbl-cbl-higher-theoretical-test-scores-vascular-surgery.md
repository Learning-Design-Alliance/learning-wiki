## Evidence

### Zhang Mao 2024

Zhang Mao, Hu Wei. (2024). Application of PBL combined with CBL teaching method in clinical teaching of vascular surgery. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative analysis at the end of the rotation between a PBL+CBL group (n = 40) and a traditional teaching group (n = 40), stratified randomized sampling with comparable baseline scores (p > 0.05). Table 2 reports "85.50 ± 1.485" vs 75.75 ± 1.195, t = 5.115, p < 0.0001; no effect size is printed.

> "In terms of knowledge mastery, students in the PBL combined with CBL teaching group had significantly higher theoretical test scores than those in the traditional teaching group"
