## Evidence

### Shuang Li 2026

Shuang Li, Haiyang Xin, Yimeng Sun, Qiannan Niu, Lingyun Huang, Gaowei Chen, Yibing Zhang, Ching Sing Chai. (2026). MIRACLE: Multi-Agent Intelligent Regulation to Advance Collaborative Learning Environment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.22318/cscl2026.255772](https://doi.org/10.22318/cscl2026.255772)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of artifact quality for 18 group artifacts assessed by two raters with a three-dimensional rubric (ICC = .89), after Shapiro-Wilk showed non-normality. "experimental groups (Mdn = 7.50, M = 7.61, SD = 0.99) produced significantly higher-quality artifacts than control groups", r = .77.

> "Results revealed that experimental groups (Mdn = 7.50, M = 7.61, SD = 0.99) produced significantly higher-quality artifacts than control groups (Mdn = 5.50, M = 4.78, SD = 1.48), z = -3.27, p < .001, r = .77, representing a large effect size."
