Ataman Karacop 2017¶

Ataman Karacop, Emine Hatun Diken. (2017). The Effects of Jigsaw Technique Based on Cooperative Learning on Prospective Science Teachers' Science Process Skill. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.6. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133003

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive document analysis of open-ended SOS responses from the 25 experimental-group student teachers (Table 4); the most frequent comment (f=9) was boredom with the high number of experiments, and f=5 for repeated experiments from previous courses; lack of time was also cited f=5.

"We got bored of the high number of the experiments, and this affected our learning badly. 9 While conducting the experiments, the professors should have helped us more. 1 We got bored as we conducted some of the experiments in the previous laboratory courses. 5"
