### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2014

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, Julia Gouvea, and Edward F. Redish. (2014). A Vision of Interdisciplinary Education: Students' Reasoning about "High-Energy Bonds" and ATP. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010115](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010115)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theoretical argument comparing reconciliation frameworks in the physics education research literature. The authors argue that the elicit-confront-resolve strategy is "problematic when the conflicting ideas to be resolved are those from different disciplines," and present Elby pairs as an alternative grounded in the resources framework.

> "This replacement is problematic when the conflicting ideas to be resolved are those from different disciplines, and neither is more “correct” than the other."
