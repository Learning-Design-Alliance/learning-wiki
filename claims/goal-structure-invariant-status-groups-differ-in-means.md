## Evidence

### Daumiller 2019

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group confirmatory factor analyses across the three status groups (221 full professors, 370 postdoc staff, 427 staff without a PhD) showed the more parsimonious models did not fit worse, "indicating complete measurement invariance across the status groups".

> "The –2ΔLL difference tests were not statistically significant, and ΔCFI and ΔRMSEA were under the commonly suggested cut-off values of ΔCFI = .02 and ΔRMSEA = .015 (Chen, 2007), indicating complete measurement invariance across the status groups."

### Daumiller 2019 (2)

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate analysis of variance on latent goal means "revealed signif- icant differences between the three groups of instruc- tors" (η² = .06); full professors reported fewer work avoidance and appearance goals, staff without a PhD stronger work avoidance and appearance avoidance goals and fewer learning approach goals.

> "a MANOVA with all 10 goal classes as dependent varia- bles and group membership as a factor revealed signif- icant differences between the three groups of instruc- tors (Wilks = .889; multivariate F(20,1934) = 5.874; p < .001; 2 = .06)."
