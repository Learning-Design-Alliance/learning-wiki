Evidence¶

Pontifex 2016¶

Pontifex, M. B., Gwizdala, K. L., Parks, A. C., Pfeiffer, K. A. & Fenn, K. M. (2016). The Association between Physical Activity During the Day and Long-Term Memory Stability. Scientific Reports 6, 38148. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep38148

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression in the same 92-participant paired-associate study, with VO2max percentile entered beyond descriptive factors. Greater fitness predicted "a greater proportion of items maintained" (β = 0.225, p = 0.032). Only a standardized β is printed; no effect size.

"Hierarchical regression analysis indicated that individuals with greater-fitness exhibited more stable long-term memory as indexed by a greater proportion of items maintained between session 1 and session 2 (β = 0.225, t (88) = 2.2, p = 0.032), even after controlling for Age and Sex"
