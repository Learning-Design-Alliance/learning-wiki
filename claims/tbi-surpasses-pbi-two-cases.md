## Evidence

### Ha 2017

Do Thi Ha. (2017). The Impact of Different Instructions on Vietnamese EFL Students' Acquisition of Formulaic Sequences. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparison of the PBI (N=17) and TBI (N=15) groups on Form and Meaning Recall of Repeating chunks in the Delayed Test. The article notes this goes against Eyckmans, Boers and Lindstromberg (2016) on L1 translation treatment.

> "Surprisingly, TBI members tend to surpass PBI when it comes to chunk production (W=73, p=0.04022) for Repeating ones."

### Ha 2017 (2)

Do Thi Ha. (2017). The Impact of Different Instructions on Vietnamese EFL Students' Acquisition of Formulaic Sequences. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney test on Form Recognition of Non-repeating items in the Immediate Test among the three groups; the TBI group (N=15) was predominant. The article attributes the TBI advantage to the involvement load hypothesis.

> "Howe ver, concerning Non-repea ting words, such ability of TBI group is predominant (W=64, p=0.0267)."
