## Evidence

### Hall Matthew M. 2019

Hall Matthew M., Lewis Jason J., Joseph Joshua W., Ketterer Andrew R., Rosen Carlo L., Dubosh Nicole M. (2019). Standardized Video Interview Scores Correlate Poorly with Faculty and Patient Ratings. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2019.11.44054

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of a prospectively collected clerkship dataset; 36 of 43 EM applicants provided self-reported SVI scores. Spearman correlation with faculty professionalism ratings was rho = 0.09 (p=0.13), a negligible printed correlation effect size (r = 0.09) the article reports as no correlation.

> "SVI scores did not correlate with CAT scores (rho = 0.11, p = 0.06), nor with faculty evaluation of professionalism (rho = 0.09, p=0.13) or patient care/communication (rho = 0.12, p = 0.04)."

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`
