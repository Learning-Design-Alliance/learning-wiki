## Evidence

### DaisyQuest 2006

DaisyQuest. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: DaisyQuest. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Randomized comparison in Barker and Torgesen (1995) against math-oriented software control, with effects confirmed "for two of the ﬁve phonological awareness measures" (Undersea Challenge and Production Test of Segmenting). Three additional positive phonological awareness effects and three phonics effects were substantively important but not statistically significant per WWC criteria.

> "When the DaisyQuest group was compared with the math- oriented software, the study authors found, and the WWC con- ﬁrmed, statistically signiﬁcant effects favoring the DaisyQuest group for two of the ﬁve phonological awareness measures"
