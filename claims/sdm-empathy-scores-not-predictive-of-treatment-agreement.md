Induru Vikranth, Deffendall Catherine, Theobald Ceceila, Green Jennifer, Ridinger Heather. (2025). Assessing shared decision-making: a case study of third-year medical student standardized patient encounters. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.676f.d093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Univariate logistic regression on the 120 matched rubrics assessed whether SDM or empathy scores predicted agreement. "neither performance on the SDM nor empathy subscale was predictive of treatment disagreement"; disagreement was slightly more frequent with lower faculty-assessed empathy (OR = 1.23, 0.93–1.62, p = .152), not significant.
