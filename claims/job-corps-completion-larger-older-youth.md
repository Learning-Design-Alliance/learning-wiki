# Job Corps' effect on completing school is larger for older youth (18–24) than for younger youth (16–17)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Among youth without a credential at baseline, the effect on earning a diploma or GED was statistically significant in both age subgroups, with a larger effect for ages 18–24 (0.40, +15) than ages 16–17 (0.26, +10). [→ Job Corps 2008](#job-corps-2008)

## Evidence

### Job Corps 2008

Job Corps. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Job Corps. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://jobcorps.dol.gov/about.htm

Subgroup analysis (Appendix A4.1) of the randomized controlled trial: for ages 18–24 (n = 4,082) the difference was 15.6 percentage points, effect size 0.40; for ages 16–17 (n = 4,515) the difference was 10.6 points, effect size 0.26; both statistically significant.
