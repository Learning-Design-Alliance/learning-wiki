# Harmonic centrality and local clustering coefficient show no relation with working memory capacity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No statistically significant relation was found between working memory capacity and harmonic centrality (p = 0.168) or the local clustering coefficient (p = 0.175); equivalence was not tested. [→ Catrysse 2025](#catrysse-2025)

## Evidence

### Catrysse 2025

Catrysse, L., van Daal, T., Jarodzka, H., Kaakinen, J. K., Donche, V., & Gijbels, D. (2025). How Eye Read: A Social Network Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10000-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null findings from the mixed-effects models in the 31-reader study: the printed WMC effects were non-significant for harmonic centrality (p = 0.168) and for the local clustering coefficient (p = 0.175), with marginal R² of 0.026 and 0.009 respectively.

> "There is no relation between working memory capacity and harmonic centrality."
