### FASTalk program description and evidence summary 2019

FASTalk program description and evidence summary. (2019). Family Engagement Lab. https://www.familyengagementlab.org/fastalk

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A quasi-experimental study matched 330 Oakland Unified School District kindergarten students in participating schools with students in non-participating schools. The article reports that "FASTalk students demonstrated 1.1 months of additional literacy gains when compared to the matched comparison group." No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "An additional qualifying quasi-experimental study of 330 Oakland Unified School District (OUSD) kindergarten students matched students in schools who participated in FASTalk with students in the schools who did not participate in FASTalk."
