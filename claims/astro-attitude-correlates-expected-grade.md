## Evidence

### Adam S. Trotter 2019

Adam S. Trotter, Daniel E. Reichart, Aaron P. LaCluyzé & Rachel Freed. (2019). Factors Contributing to Attitudinal Gains in Introductory Astronomy Courses. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.32374/rtsre.2019.014](https://doi.org/10.32374/rtsre.2019.014)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of 749 end-of-semester survey responses collected across 4 semesters at 10 institutions. In simultaneous multiple linear regression on Attitude Index, Grade Index was positively correlated; Table 4 prints coefficient 0.16 with p-value 1.6E-12. The finding is "Positively correlated" as the article states. Causality could not be established.

> "Grade Index (Q17): what grade students expected to receive in the course at the time they took the survey. -1 = F, 0 = C, +1 = A. Positively correlated."
