### Denis Pasco 2025

Denis Pasco, Erik Gustafsson, Lucas Burget, Thibault Asselborn, Pierre Dillenbourg, Consortium Lamue, Marie Mazerolle. (2025). Rapid in-class assessment of handwriting using a digital tablet: Multidimensional analysis of gender and socioeconomic status. Computers & Education: X Reality, 232, 105311. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105311

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Component-level mixed-model analysis found both gender and socioeconomic index significantly predicted kinematic scores, with the article reporting "B =-4.889" for the gender contrast.

> "Boys demonstrated significantly lower kinematic scores compared to girls (B =-4.889, 95 % CI [-7.094, -2.685], t(1355) =-4.347, p < .001), and a higher socioeconomic index was associated with improved kinematic scores"
