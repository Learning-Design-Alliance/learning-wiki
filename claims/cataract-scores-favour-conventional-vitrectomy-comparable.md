## Evidence

### Eckardt F 2026

Eckardt F, Gerhardt M, Priglinger S. (2026). Teaching ophthalmic microsurgical skills: conventional microscopy versus heads-up visualization. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10269-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover EyeSi study in 32 novices; paired comparison of cataract module total scores between systems. Table 2 reports a total-score Cohen's d of -0.42, favouring the conventional microscope.

> "Focusing on the cataract module, the subjects achieved significantly higher scores using the conventional microscopic than with the 3D-heads-up display (p = 0.023)."

### Eckardt F 2026 (2)

Eckardt F, Gerhardt M, Priglinger S. (2026). Teaching ophthalmic microsurgical skills: conventional microscopy versus heads-up visualization. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10269-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same crossover study, vitrectomy module; paired comparison found no significant score difference between the two visualization systems. No effect size is printed for this null finding.

> "Across the vitrectomy module there was no significant difference between conventional microscopic visualization and the 3D- heads-up display (p = 0.126)."
