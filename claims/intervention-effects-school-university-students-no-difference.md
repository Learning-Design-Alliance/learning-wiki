# Intervention effects on relative metacomprehension accuracy did not differ between school and university students

## Evidence

### Anja Prinz & Stefanie Golke & Jörg Wittwer 2020

Anja Prinz & Stefanie Golke & Jörg Wittwer. (2020). To What Extent Do Situation-Model-Approach Interventions Improve Relative Metacomprehension Accuracy? Meta-Analytic Insights. Educational Psychology Review (2020) 32:917–949. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09558-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Exploratory moderator analysis reported in a footnote, checking whether including school and university students affected the average effect; the difference test was non-significant.

> "the average effects for school students, g = 0.39, 95% CI [0.15, 0.63], k = 8, and university students, g = 0.50, 95% CI [0.33, 0.66], k = 18, did not significantly differ from each other, Q(1) = 0.52, p = .473, R2 < .01"
