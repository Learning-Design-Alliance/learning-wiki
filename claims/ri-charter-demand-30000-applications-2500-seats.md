Margaret E. Raymond, PhD; Marzena Sasnal, PhD. (2025). The Impact of Rhode Island's Charter School Performance Review System on the Education Landscape. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/rhode-island-2025/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the interview-based policy review, officials reported surging demand for charter seats: for the 2024–2025 school year, 30,000 applications were submitted for only 2,500 available seats, leaving the needs of many families unmet. These are descriptive figures; no effect size is reported.

> "As the Rhode Island charter sector has become recognized for its high standards and quality instruction, demand for charter school seats has surged. For example, for the 2024–2025 school year, 30,000 applications were submitted for only 2,500 available seats."
