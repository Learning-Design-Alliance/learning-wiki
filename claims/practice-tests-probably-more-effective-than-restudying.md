The effects of teaching strategies on learning to think critically in primary and secondary schools: an overview of systematic reviews. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.158087.1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The overview authors prepared plain language summaries for 37 teaching strategies judged most relevant to teaching critical thinking. For practice tests, they concluded that they are probably more effective than restudying for both retention of information and transfer of knowledge or skills.
