## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Practice was significantly more difficult in the Redesigned condition, consistent with the theory of change that practice prioritizes harder KCs. [→ Qianru Lyu 2026](#qianru-lyu-2026)
`q3 i?` Redesigned-condition students received more practice opportunities overall and allocated relatively more practice to new KCs, though new KCs received fewer opportunities than established KCs. [→ Qianru Lyu 2026 (2)](#qianru-lyu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Qianru Lyu 2026

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Manipulation check extending the AFM with a condition effect, testing the prediction that the Redesigned Tutor reallocates practice toward hidden, split KCs and raises overall difficulty; the article reports a significant difficulty increase with β = −0.10, p = .020.

> "Practice was significantly more difficult in the Redesigned condition (β=−0.10,p=.020)."

### Qianru Lyu 2026 (2)

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory mixed-effects analysis of log-data practice opportunities in the crossover study. New KCs received fewer opportunities than established KCs (β = −0.74, p < .001), but "the Redesigned condition allocated relatively more practice to them" (β = 0.23, p = .041).

> "A linear mixed-effects model of practice opportunities showed that students in the Redesigned conditionreceivedmoreopportunitiesoverall(β= 0.30,p < .001)."
