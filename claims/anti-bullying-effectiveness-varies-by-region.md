## Evidence

### Hannah Gaffney 2019

Hannah Gaffney, David P. Farrington, & Maria M. Ttofi. (2019). Examining the Effectiveness of School-Bullying Intervention Programs Globally: a Meta-analysis. International Journal of Bullying Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42380-019-0007-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Regional moderator analysis (Table 3) of weighted mean effect sizes across seven geographical regions for the 100 evaluations.

> "In regard to school-bullying perpetration outcomes, evaluations conducted in North America were the most effective, followed by Scandinavian studies, and then European studies."

### Hannah Gaffney 2019 (2)

Hannah Gaffney, David P. Farrington, & Maria M. Ttofi. (2019). Examining the Effectiveness of School-Bullying Intervention Programs Globally: a Meta-analysis. International Journal of Bullying Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42380-019-0007-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Regional comparison for victimization outcomes; the authors report that for perpetration, the North American effect size "was not significantly different from the weighted mean for European and Scandinavian studies."

> "When weighted mean effect sizes were estimated for European and Scandinavian countries collectively, they were significantly more effective in reducing bullying victimization outcomes than North American studies."
