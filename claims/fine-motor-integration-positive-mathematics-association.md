## Evidence

### Macdonald Kirstin 2018

Macdonald Kirstin, Milne Nikki, Orr Robin, Pope Rodney. (2018). Relationships between Motor Proficiency and Academic Performance in Mathematics and Reading in School-Aged Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15081603

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of 16 observational studies of fine motor integration and mathematics. Reported correlations included "moderate associations (r = 0.417−0.59)" in seven studies and a strong association (r = 0.612−0.673) in two studies; the one non-significant study was the only one with high school participants.

> "When taking into consideration all studies that examined associations between fine motor integration (also referred to as visual motor integration or copying skills) and mathematics performance variables (Table S1), 15 of the 16 studies found significant positive associations, with 12 of these significant associations reported by studies with fair to good methodological quality"
