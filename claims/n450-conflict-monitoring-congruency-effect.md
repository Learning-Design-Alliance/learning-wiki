## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The N450 amplitude was significantly greater (more negative) for incongruent than congruent conditions, with no significant main effect of self-referential information. [→ Li 2024](#li-2024)

## Evidence

### Li 2024

Li, Z., Wang, J., Chen, Y., Li, Q., Yin, S. & Chen, A. (2024). Attenuated conflict self-referential information facilitating conflict resolution. npj Science of Learning 9, 47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00256-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Stimuli-locked N450 (350–480 ms, centro-parietal electrodes) ANOVA from the EEG Stroop experiment. The congruency main effect was significant (F(1,32) = 7.46, p = 0.010, ηp² = 0.19); the interaction with self-reference was not significant (p = 0.059) and the self-reference main effect was not significant (F < 1).

> "The main effect of congruency was signi ﬁcant, F(1, 32) = 7.46, p = 0.010, ηp 2 = 0.19, indicating that the N450 amplitude evoked by incongruent condition (3.94, SE = 0.60) was signiﬁcantly greater (more negative) than that evoked by congruent condition (4.68, SE = 0.62)"
