Heatmap analysis of a verbal preference learner (PID=9) vs a visual preference learner (PID=0), defined by post-survey Q3/Q4 choices. The authors explicitly hedge the pattern, stating "the observed patterns indicate that personalization could enhance learning outcomes"; no statistic is printed.
