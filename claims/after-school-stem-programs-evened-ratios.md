`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The article reports, citing Jones (2017), that US high school science and math class gender ratios evened out from the 1990s onward, attributing this partly to intervention programs offering after-school STEM programs and scholarships. The article states this shows the gender gap is not due to innate abilities.

> "In the US in the 1990s boys outnumbered girls in science and math classes in high school, but the numbers have evened out and in some cases girls outnumber the boys (Jones 2017). According to this study intervention programs like AAUW, the Girl Scouts, Girls Inc., Tech Bridge and Girls Who Code, that oﬀered after school STEM programs and scholarships for girls were a big part of why this change occurred."
