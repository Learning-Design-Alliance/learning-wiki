### Coristine A 2025

Virtual Patients Using Large Language Models: Scalable, Contextualized Simulation of Clinician-Patient Dialogue With Feedback. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68486

Paired mixed-model ANOVA comparisons of initial ratings across matched conversations differing only in GPT model. Final-rating subgroup differences ranged from 0.17 to 0.71 but did not always reach statistical significance.

> "Ratings were significantly higher for GPT-4.0-Turbo versus GPT-3.5-Turbo (difference: dialogue overall 0.92, 95% CI 0.64-1.19; experience overall 0.58, 95% CI 0.21-0.96; feedback average 1.33, 95% CI 0.80-1.87)."
