# Most RSF-supported Cohort 1 schools increased the percentage of students meeting or exceeding ISAT standards from year 1 to year 2

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All but two Cohort 1 schools gained in the percentage of students meeting or exceeding reading standards, with gains ranging from 6% to 16%; the cohort average rose from 58% to 66% while the districtwide change was 2%. [→ Young 2008](#young-2008)

## Evidence

### Young 2008

Young, V. M., Humphrey, D. C., Wang, H., Bosetti, K. R., Cassidy, L., Wechsler, M. E., Rivera, E., Murray, S., & Schanzenbach, D. W. (2008). Renaissance Schools Fund-Supported Schools: Early Outcomes, Challenges, and Opportunities. SRI International. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/renaissance-schools-fund-supported-schools-early-outcomes-challenges-and-opportunities

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive longitudinal analysis of ISAT performance for Cohort 1 students with prior test data, 2005-06 to 2006-07. Reading passing rates rose "from 58% to 66% among Cohort 1 schools"; in mathematics, rates rose from 64% to 73% versus 64% to 69% districtwide, with gains of 3% to 31% and one school declining 2%.

> "The average percentage of students meeting or exceeding standards in reading increased from 58% to 66% among Cohort 1 schools, compared with a change of 2%, from 59% to 61% districtwide, without controlling for differences in background characteristics."
