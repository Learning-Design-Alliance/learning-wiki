## Evidence

### Shortlidge Erin E. 2018

Shortlidge Erin E., Eddy Sarah L. (2018). The trade-off between graduate student research and teaching: A myth? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0199576

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey regression analysis of 338 life science PhD students (best-fit linear regression: research training adequacy index ~ training in EBT index). The article reports "a slightly positive relationship (β = 0.15 ± 0.042, t = 3.64, p = 0.0003)", contradicting the hypothesized trade-off.

> "Increased training in EBT practices did not reduce students’ confidence that their research training has adequately prepared them to be a researcher (Fig 1). Instead, there was a slightly positive relationship (β = 0.15 ± 0.042, t = 3.64, p = 0.0003)."
