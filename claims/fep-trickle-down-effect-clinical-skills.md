### Thapa R. 2023

Thapa R., Nikolli K., McMahon D., Blakemore S., Tamang S., Bhatta S., Gautam P., Shrestha R., Rajbhandari R. (2023). Novel on-site follow-up and enhancement program (FEP) improves knowledge, clinical skills and enabling environment of skilled birth attendants in Nepal. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0285653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 4 comparison in the Results section of Cohort B2017 non-FEP SBAs (n = 93) with Cohort C2017 non-FEP SBAs working at FEP sites (n = 22). Knowledge (86.59 vs 80.31, p = 0.01) and enabling environment (83.29 vs 57.74, p<0.001) also favored the prior-FEP site group.

> "There is an observed difference in clinical skills with SBAs working at a prior FEP facility having skills scores of 81% compared to 41% for those not working at a prior FEP site (p<0.001)."
