## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2013

Ayush Gupta, Andrew Elby, Luke D. Conlin. (2013). How substance-based ontologies for gravity can be productive: A case study. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Close qualitative analysis of a videotaped small-group discussion among teachers (Lynn, Mira, Daniel, Lisa) during a two-week 2009 summer professional development workshop, from Episode 1. The authors state that "the incorrect and conflicting ontological metaphors for gravity become the source for generation of a sophisticated argument."

> "In the case of Lynn and her group, the incorrect and conflicting ontological metaphors for gravity become the source for generation of a sophisticated argument for why objects of different masses fall with the same acceleration."
