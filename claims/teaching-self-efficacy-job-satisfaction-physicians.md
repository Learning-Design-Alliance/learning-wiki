### Kiver Benjamin 2025

Kiver Benjamin, Berberat Pascal O., Gartmeier Martin. (2025). The relation between teaching-related self-efficacy and general job-related well-being – a cross-sectional study among young physicians. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001777

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of physicians in didactics training at TUM University Hospital; Pearson correlation showed a significant positive association with job satisfaction (r=0.138; p=0.020; N=283), i.e. physicians "correlated positively with their job satisfaction". The study was "a conveniance sample with 315 participants", exploratory with hypotheses formulated after data collection.
