## Evidence

### Singh 2013

Singh, C., and Marshman, E. (2013). Analogous Patterns of Student Reasoning Difficulties in Introductory Physics and Upper-Level Quantum Mechanics. 2013 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.inv.010

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Written test of the same 39 physics graduate students after at least one semester of graduate-level QM. The multiple-choice question presented Andy's and Caroline's conflicting statements about measurement; "52%" agreed incorrectly, and interviews suggest the measurement misconception was so strong that students ignored basic linear algebra violations.

> "Table 1 shows that for question 3, 52% of the graduate students incorrectly agreed either with Andy or Caroline or both."
