## Evidence

### Wu Yanyan 2026

Wu Yanyan, Liu Fang, Zhang Hui. (2026). Application of case-based learning combined with three-dimensional image reconstruction technology in clinical teaching of liver ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09839-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this single-center RCT, 60 residents were randomized 1:1 after a pre-course test; the 30 experimental residents rated all five satisfaction domains higher than the 30 controls, with every item reaching p < 0.001 on independent t-tests. The article reports no effect size for this outcome.

> "The survey results showed that the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group in all five domains: “self-assessment of mastery of anatomical structures”, “self-assessment of ultrasound image comprehension”, “interest in learning”, “classroom interaction”, and “satisfaction with teaching” (all p < 0.001, independent t-test, Table 2; Fig. 3)."
