Evidence¶

Soheila Shafiee Varzaneh & Roya Baharlooie 2015¶

Soheila Shafiee Varzaneh & Roya Baharlooie. (2015). The Effect of Virtual vs. Traditional Classroom Instruction on Creative Thinking of Iranian High School EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n5p177

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Comparison of low-proficiency subgroups (n = 30 per condition) in the same quasi-experiment; the low VLI posttest creativity mean was 91.10 (SD = 9.35) versus 52.65 (SD = 3.85) for low TCI, described as the "major difference" across proficiency levels.

"The major difference was related to the performance of low VLI in pre- and post-test, which indicates that the application of VLI had a real positive effect on this group."
