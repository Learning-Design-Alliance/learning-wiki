## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariable multilevel ordinal logistic regression linking 2008 World Bank GDP per capita to each doctor's nationality and qualification country (table 6): "OR 1.22 (1.19 to 1.25)" for absolute GDP difference. In multivariable models the effect diminished but remained significant (OR 1.06, p=0.001).

> "the most potent predictor in this regard was the absolute difference in GDP between country of nationality and place of qualification. This indicated that the odds of a less satisfactory (vs more satisfactory) outcome increased by approximately 22% for every US$10 000 per capita difference between the two countries, regardless of the direction of the disparity."
