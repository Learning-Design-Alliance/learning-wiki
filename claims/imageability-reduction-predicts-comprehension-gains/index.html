Siegelman 2022¶

Siegelman, N., Rueckl, J. G., van den Bunt, M., Frijters, J. C., Zevin, J. D., Lovett, M. W., Seidenberg, M. S., Pugh, K. R., & Morris, R. D. (2022). Stepwise regression on Passage Comprehension gains in the RD intervention sample; model fit improvement was marginal (F(4, 116) = 2.35, p = .058), yet "greater residualized gains in Passage Comprehension were significantly associated with a greater reduction in reliance on imageability".

"Examining the individual predictors showed that greater residualized gains in Passage Comprehension were significantly associated with a greater reduction in reliance on imageability over the course of the intervention"
