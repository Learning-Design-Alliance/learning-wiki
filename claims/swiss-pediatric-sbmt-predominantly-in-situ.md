## Evidence

### Stocker M 2017

Stocker M, Laine K, Ulmer F. (2017). Use of simulation-based medical training in Swiss pediatric hospitals: a national survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0940-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results across 24 educational units at 20 hospitals offering SBMT. The article reports "22 out of 24 units" conducting in-situ training and 15 of 24 using high-technology mannequins; 6 of 24 also used a designated simulation center.

> "More than 90% of the educational units offering SBMT (22 out of 24 units) were conducting in-situ training and 62.5% (15 out of 24) were using high-technology mannequins."
