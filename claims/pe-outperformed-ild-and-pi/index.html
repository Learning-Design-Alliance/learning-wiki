Krišťák 2014¶

Krišťák, Ľ., Němec, M., Danihelová, Z. (2014). Interactive Methods of Teaching Physics at Technical Universities. Informatics in Education, 13(1), 51–71. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2014.03

Comparison within the authors' multi-year experiment: PI was tested in one experimental group in 2012 and its results were worse than P&E's; the authors attribute P&E's advantage to the use of students' worksheets. A separate sentence reports ILD tested in 2011 seminars with P&E proven better.

"In 2012 the method Peer Instructions was also tested in one experimental group, nevertheless, achieved results were worse than in the case of P&E method (a great advantage of P&E method when compared to PI method is the use of students’ worksheets)."
