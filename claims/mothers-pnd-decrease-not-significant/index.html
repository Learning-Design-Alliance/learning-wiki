Evidence¶

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut 2016¶

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut. (2016). The Whole is Greater than the Sum of the Parts: The Effects of an Antenatal Orientation Interviews Training for Prospective Parents Postnatal Depression Levels. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.23. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1112927

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test for mothers in the experimental group (n=6); decrease observed but not statistically significant, z=1.90, p>.05, no effect size printed.

"although there was a decrease in the scores of the mothers in the experimental group after the orientation interviews, this decrease was not statistically significant ( z=1.90, p>.05)"

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut 2016 (2)¶

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut. (2016). The Whole is Greater than the Sum of the Parts: The Effects of an Antenatal Orientation Interviews Training for Prospective Parents Postnatal Depression Levels. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.23. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1112927

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test for mothers in the control group (n=7); no significant pre-post difference, z=.26, p>.05.

"Similarly, there was no significant difference between the pre-test and post-test scores of the mothers in the control group ( z=.26, p> .05)."
