`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a large-scale observational log analysis in which Jirschitzka et al. sampled more than 70,000 edits from 398 German Wikipedia articles on alternative medicine and nutrition and trained a supervised classifier to label edits as pro alternative, pro conventional, or neutral. The reported pattern is a "typical confirmation bias" in editing choices. No effect size is printed.

> "The studies showed a typical confirmation bias (Jonas et al. 2001): Wikipedia contributors primarily edited those articles that were consistent with their own view on the topic (see Fig. 1)."
