## Evidence

### Serçe Hüseyin 2026

Serçe Hüseyin, Aydoğmuş Mevlüt, Şentürk Cihad. (2026). Psychological predictors of professional readiness: modeling the effects of preservice teachers' 21st-century skills on self-efficacy and professional attitudes. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05039-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM with maximum likelihood estimation on self-report data from 401 preservice teachers at two Turkish universities; the structural path from 21st-century skills to teacher self-efficacy was significant, "the predictive effect of twenty-first century skills on teacher self-efficacy is 0.58" (β = 0.58, a large effect) with R2 = 0.34.

> "the predictive effect of twenty-first century skills on teacher self-efficacy is 0.58 (95% CI [0.48; 0.74], p < 0.001). It is observed that twenty-first century skills explain 34% of the variance in teacher self-efficacy (R2 = 0.34)."
