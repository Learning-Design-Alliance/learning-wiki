## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2020

Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Perceived irrelevance and mastery vs. performance achievement goals: Two mindset variables within attitudinal experiences of life science majors in introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Within-major CLASS analysis (Table 4): biology gains d = 0.58 (p < 0.05); health science gains d = 0.39 (p = 0.14). Both majors showed significant post-test differences (biology d = 0.79, p < 0.01; health d = 0.52, p < 0.05).

> "As for normalized gains, biology majors who perceive relevance show statistically more expert-like gains than students who do not, while health science majors do not demonstrate this statistical significance and only show a moderate effect size."
