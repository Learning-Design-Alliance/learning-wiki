### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Effect of integrated case-based and problem-based learning on clinical thinking skills of assistant general practitioner trainees: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06634-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RCT comparing CBL-PBL (n = 35) and LBL groups on the CTSES's three dimensions after the course; Table 2 prints post-course between-group p values of 0.000 for each domain. For example, critical thinking rose from 8.333 to 18.333 in CBL-PBL versus 8.359 to 13.660 in LBL. No standardised effect size is printed.
