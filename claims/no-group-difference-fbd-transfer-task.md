## Evidence

### Cetron 2026

Cetron, J.S., Hillis, M.E., Diamond, S.G., May, V.V. & Kraemer, D.J.M. (2026). Neural patterns reflect conceptual grasp of novice students following first class learning in physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00394-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioral results from the fMRI near-transfer task: each of 50 participants made 12 binary judgments per run across 8 runs, judging whether free-body diagrams correctly labeled forces. Grand means were 63.21% (lab) and 68.12% (textbook). The printed t-test is the only statistic; no effect size is reported.

> "A two-sample t-test revealed that there was no significant difference in how well the lab and textbook groups performed on the FBD task (t = -1.57, df = 45.63, p = 0.12)."
