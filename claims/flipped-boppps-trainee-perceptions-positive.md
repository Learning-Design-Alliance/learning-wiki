### Luo Bin 2025

Luo Bin, Zhou Fangyao, Zheng Jiabin, Hu Weixian, Lv Zejian, Yang Zifeng, Feng Xingyu. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom combined with BOPPPS model in standardized residency training for gastrointestinal surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07855-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-rotation 10-question survey completed by the 33 experimental-group trainees, scored from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree), on perceptions of the new teaching model.

> "A remarkable 97.0% of the trainees believed that the new teaching model was scientific (90.9% Strongly Agree, 6.1% Agree, and 3.0% Neutral). Furthermore, all trainees agreed that the new teaching model was worthy of promotion (93.9% Strongly Agree, 6.1% Agree)."
