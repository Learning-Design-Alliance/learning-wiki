## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Children using BKT-adaptive task selection scored higher on the paper post-test (69% vs 54%) than children receiving random tasks. [→ Yannier 2024](#yannier-2024)

## Evidence

### Yannier 2024

Yannier, N., Hudson, S. E., Chang, H., & Koedinger, K. R. (2024). AI Adaptivity in a Mixed-Reality System Improves Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 34, 1541–1558. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00388-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled experiment with 36 children (18 pairs) at a science center; ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test as covariate found "69% for the adaptive condition and 54% for the randomized condition", d = 0.66.

> "The overall results indicated that the average scores on the full post-tests (prediction and explanation items) was 69% for the adaptive condition and 54% for the randomized condition, F(1,34) = 4.02, p = 0.05, d = 0.66 (Cohen's d)."
