## Evidence

### Alaee 2023

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview analysis of mentor-mentee pairs in the remote REU study; the authors report that with limited tools and proximity "it was harder to form this good mentor-mentee relationship." Cases included email-only contact, time-zone conflicts preventing meetings, and a mentee-mentor mismatch over expected independence and time commitment.

> "However, due to a lack of tools and lack of physical proximity in a re-mote lab setting, it was harder to form this good mentor-mentee relationship."
