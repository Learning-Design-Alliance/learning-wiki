The EVALUATE Group 2019¶

The EVALUATE Group. (2019). Evaluating the impact of virtual exchange on initial teacher education: a European policy experiment. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2019.29.9782490057337

q2 · i? · causal · r1

An eight-item pre-test and nine-item post-test questionnaire (post-test α=.90) was developed to measure foreign language competence. The report states these were "not standardised tests, but situated and pragmatic attempts" to capture limited language gains.

"Foreign language competence development is inevitably contextual. Accordingly, these were not standardised tests, but situated and pragmatic attempts to capture the kind of limited language gains learners might make in the course of a short-term intervention."
