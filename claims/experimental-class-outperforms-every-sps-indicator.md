## Evidence

### Sahhyar & Nst 2017

Sahhyar & Nst, F. H. (2017). The Effect of Scientific Inquiry Learning Model Based on Conceptual Change on Physics Cognitive Competence and Science Process Skill (SPS) of Students at Senior High School. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5), 120-126. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133077

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

All eleven SPS indicators were higher in the experimental class: observing 63.80% vs 54.17%, inferring 59.38% vs 47.22%, questioning 65.36% vs 56.67%, interpreting 61.20% vs 41.67%, classifying 66.93% vs 51.67%, predicting 62.50% vs 52.22%, communicating 62.24% vs 51.94%, making hypothesis 55.73% vs 44.17%, planning 66.15% vs 42.50%, applying concept or principle 64.32% vs 58.33%, generalizing 61.20% vs 50.83%. The study reports "the average percentage of each indicator in experimental class was higher than in control class."
