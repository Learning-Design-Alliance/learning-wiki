Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation matrix row (Table 2, n = 208) from the cross-sectional survey. Self-efficacy correlates positively with establishing harmonious relationships (r = 0.362), listening sensitively (r = 0.341), recognizing patient problems (r = 0.285), and verifying feelings (r = 0.432), all significant at the 0.01 level.
