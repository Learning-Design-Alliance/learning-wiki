# In reflection conditions, learners' current hypotheses rose from the start to the end of reflection phases; diagnostic accuracy at the end of the virtual patient encounter was higher than the current hypothesis at its start

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the reflection conditions, participants' current hypotheses rose from the start to the end of reflection phases (t(73) = 2.73, p = .004). [→ Fink Maximilian C. 2021](#fink-maximilian-c-2021)
`q3 i?` Exploratively, diagnostic accuracy at the end of the virtual patient encounter was significantly higher than the current hypothesis at its start in the reflection conditions (t(79) = 7.91, p < .001). [→ Fink Maximilian C. 2021 (2)](#fink-maximilian-c-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C. 2021

Fink Maximilian C., Heitzmann Nicole, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Learning to diagnose accurately through virtual patients: do reflection phases have an added benefit? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02937-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test across both reflection conditions combined in the learning phase: "participants improved their current hypothesis from the start to the end of reflection phases (t(73) = 2.73, p = .004)". Categorically, not changing the hypothesis (90.1%) predominated over improvement (7.0%).

> "A paired samples t-test showed that participants improved their current hypothesis from the start to the end of reflection phases (t(73) = 2.73, p = .004)."

### Fink Maximilian C. 2021 (2)

Fink Maximilian C., Heitzmann Nicole, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Learning to diagnose accurately through virtual patients: do reflection phases have an added benefit? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02937-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Explorative paired t-test in the reflection conditions: "diagnostic accuracy at the end of the virtual patient encounter was significantly higher than their current hypothesis at the start" (t(79) = 7.91, p < .001). Categorical analysis showed not changing (66.6%) more frequent than improvement (29.7%).

> "an explorative paired samples t-test of the reflection conditions showed that the participants' diagnostic accuracy at the end of the virtual patient encounter was significantly higher than their current hypothesis at the start of the virtual patient encounter (t(79) = 7.91, p < .001)."
