Laura Gabler 2024¶

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on Rasch-scaled word problem scores (N = 115) showed a significant time × treatment interaction: "F(222.00, 2) = 3.45, p = 0.034, ηp2 = 0.03".

"A repeated measures ANOVA (Table 5) showed a significant interaction effect of measurement time and treatment group with a small effect size (F(222.00, 2) = 3.45, p = 0.034, ηp2 = 0.03)."
