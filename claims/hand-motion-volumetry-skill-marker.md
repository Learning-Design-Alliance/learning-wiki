Descriptive/exploratory analysis of automatically recorded hand motion volumetry in the prospective cohort. The article notes "Lower volumetry is associated with shorter, more controlled movements, indicating better ergonomics" but was not incorporated into LC-CUSUM criteria given lack of benchmarks.

> "The hand motion volumetry parameter is recorded by the Versius system software. It represents the average movement of the controls in centimetres and reflects the fluidity and efficiency of motion during surgery through clutch usage. Lower volumetry is associated with shorter, more controlled movements, indicating better ergonomics and potentially correlating with higher technical skills"
