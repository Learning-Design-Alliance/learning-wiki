Descriptive adoption measure from the RCT experiment group (n=3,581): "only a small number of students (14.2%)" used the interface before the midterm, which the authors compare to Rogers' estimated 13.5%-16% of people willing to experiment with new technology.

> "we found only a small number of students (14.2%) who were provided access to our course GPT interface, and emailed to alert them to this opportunity, chose to use our GPT interface."
