## Evidence

### Niederhauser A 2019

Niederhauser A, Züllig S, Marschall J, Schweiger A, John G, Kuster SP, Schwappach DLB. (2019). Change in staff perspectives on indwelling urinary catheter use after implementation of an intervention bundle in seven Swiss acute care hospitals: results of a before/after survey study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028740

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Repeated cross-sectional survey (n=1568 at T0, 1521 at T1) using a 13-item 7-point scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.79/0.80). Table 3 prints an overall effect size of 0.31; the largest subgroup effect was 0.52 for staff with managerial function.

> "The mean scale score for perception of current practices and culture increased significantly within the overall sample and within all subgroups between the two time points (p<0.001) (table 3)."
