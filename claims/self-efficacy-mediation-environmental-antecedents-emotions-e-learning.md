`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM (robust maximum likelihood, N = 17019) testing CVT mediation; the model fit well (CFI = 0.932, RMSEA = 0.045) and "e-learning self-efficacy mediated the relation of environmental antecedents with positive emotions, partially for class organization (indirect effect: 0.17, p < .001)".
