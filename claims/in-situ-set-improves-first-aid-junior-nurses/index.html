In situ simulated emergency training added to routine training raises self-rated first aid ability in junior nurses

In a randomised trial of 82 junior nurses, in situ simulated emergency training held 2-3 times a month on top of once-monthly routine stratified training raised scores on a self-rated first aid questionnaire more than routine training alone; the extra training sessions are not separated from the simulation itself
