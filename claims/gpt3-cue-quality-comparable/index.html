Evidence¶

Abdelghani 2023¶

Abdelghani, R., Wang, Y.-H., Yuan, X., Wang, T., Lucas, P., Sauzéon, H., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2023). GPT-3-driven pedagogical agents for training children's curious question-asking skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://hal.science/hal-03890447v1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Human annotation study of GPT-3-generated cues (semantic relatedness on a 5-Likert scale; divergence level) compared to hand-crafted cues. Offensiveness annotation showed 100% scored as not at all offensive (m=5, SD=0); relevance annotation showed 100% relevant.

"The one-way ANOVA test failed to exclude similarities between at least two groups (F(2,74)=0.41, p-value = 0.66), with a power test of 0.98 indicating that we only have 2% chance to mistakenly conclude that none exists"
