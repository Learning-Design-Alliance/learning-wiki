Eugenio Tufino, Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Creating a customisable Socratic AI physics tutor. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Illustrative use case 2: a rectangular-loop electromagnetic induction problem. The Gem adapts to the student's algebra-based formulation of Faraday's Law, guides derivation of E=B·L·v, corrects a decimal error, and supports right-hand-rule reasoning toward the clockwise current direction (0.18 A magnitude).

> "The full interaction demonstrates how the the student's algebra-based knowledge by confirming their 'role-engineered' Gem manages a complex, two-part problem involving both procedural calculations and abstract spatial reasoning."
