Cowan Nelson 2014¶

Cowan Nelson. (2014). Working Memory Underpins Cognitive Development, Learning, and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4207727/

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Cowan et al. (2010): "when the size of the array to be remembered was large (3 more-relevant and 3 less-relevant items, rather than 2 of each) then young children were no longer able to allocate more attention to the more-relevant items," with the attentional resource apparently deducted from that available for optimal allocation.
