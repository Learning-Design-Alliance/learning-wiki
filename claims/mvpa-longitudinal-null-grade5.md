# Grade 4 school-day MVPA does not predict Grade 5 Fall course grades in residualized longitudinal analyses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Longitudinal analyses found no significant associations between Grade 4 mean daily school-day MVPA and Grade 5 Fall course grades in math, reading, spelling, or writing. [→ Elish Paul N. 2022](#elish-paul-n-2022)

## Evidence

### Elish Paul N. 2022

Elish Paul N., Bryan Cassandra S., Boedeker Peter J., Calvert Hannah G., Kay Christi M., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). The longitudinal association between objectively-measured school-day physical activity and academic achievement in US elementary school students. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01328-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Residualized change score analyses predicting Grade 5 Fall (T3) grades from Grade 4 mean MVPA while controlling for Grade 4 grades and covariates. The article reports "the conditional associations were again negative yet nonsignificant for all outcomes"; no coefficient reached the Bonferroni-adjusted p-critical of 0.00271 (e.g., writing p = 0.018).

> "Findings indicated that the conditional associations were again negative yet nonsignificant for all outcomes"
