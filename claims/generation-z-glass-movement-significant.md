### Szabó DI 2024

> "Glass Movement (GM) showed interesting results, Generation Z performed a surprisingly low mean (1.47 ± 1.5) compared to the others (Children: 4.05 ± 3.8, Adults: 5.2 ± 3.6), making this difference significant (p = 0.019)"
