## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2017

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Schatschneider Christopher. (2017). Expanding the developmental models of writing: A direct and indirect effects model of developmental writing (DIEW). Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000129

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the final complete mediation SEM (Figure 6) with 193 Grade 1 children, working memory predicted vocabulary and grammatical knowledge (γs = .40 & .40, ps < .001), spelling (γ = .39), and handwriting fluency (γ = .23). The article reports 'working memory was no longer directly related to writing once all the language and cognitive skills were accounted for.'

> "It was directly related to foundational oral language skills (vocabulary and grammatical knowledge), higher-order cognitive skills (inference and theory of mind), and transcription skills (spelling and handwriting fluency)."
