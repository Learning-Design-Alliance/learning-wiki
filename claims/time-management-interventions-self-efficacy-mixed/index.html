Evidence¶

Christopher A. Wolters 2025¶

Christopher A. Wolters, Anna C. Brady, Hyun Ji Lee. (2025). Time Management and Achievement Motivation: A Review of What We Know and Directions for Where to Go. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10032-4

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Terry and Doolittle's study of 64 students using a web-based goal-setting and monitoring tool over 2 weeks, finding gains only in time management strategy use. In contrast, Abdul Kader and Eissa's matched-groups training study (60 fifth-grade boys) reported improved time management and self-efficacy.

"Pretest–posttest comparisons showed that students' daily use of the tool increased only their reported use of time management strategies but not their self-efficacy for SRL nor their self-efficacy for addressing challenges."
