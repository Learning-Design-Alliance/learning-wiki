Evidence¶

Pascal Riachi 2026¶

Pascal Riachi, Sofie Kamber, Stella Brogna, Andrew Gloster, Rafael Wampfler. (2026). Toward Accessible Psychotherapy Training Using AI-Driven Interactive Patient Avatars. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17786

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mixed-methods expert evaluation with two female psychologists (ages 25–30), one licensed with 4–7 years of experience and one in training with 1–3 years, each using the system for approximately 90 minutes. Participants reported that the feedback mechanism "increased awareness of therapeutic language and intervention choices". No effect sizes were reported.

"Both participants valued the immediate, turn-by-turn ACT feedback, reporting that the visual ACT score and retry/continue mechanism increased awareness of therapeutic language and intervention choices."
