Bifactor item response theory analysis of placement test item responses (N = 1,098 test takers), examining item loadings against thresholds of 0.6 for overall and 0.3 for domain measurement. The report states "61 percent (46 items) demonstrated strong contributions to measuring ELA performance," while few domain items did.

> "Of the 75 total items on the test, 61 percent (46 items) demonstrated strong contributions to measuring ELA performance. However, few items made strong contributions to measuring performance on each of the three ELA test domains: vocabulary, language use, and sentence meaning."
