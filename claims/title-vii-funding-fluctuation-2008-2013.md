### RMC Research Corporation 2015

RMC Research Corporation. (2015). Underuse of Title VII funding for Indian education in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah. Phoenix, AZ: West Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559751

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of USASpending.gov new-award data by fiscal year (Exhibits 1–3). Utah funding peaked in 2010 and stabilized around $1.3 million in 2012 and 2013; Nevada peaked in 2012 and dropped about 4 percent from 2012 to 2013.

> "LEAs in Utah experienced the greatest fluctuations in Title VII funding of new awards, which increased 38 percent from 2008 to 2009, another 29 percent in 2010, then dropped 20 percent in 2011, and increased 9 percent in 2012."
