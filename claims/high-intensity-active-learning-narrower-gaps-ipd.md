# Only high-intensity active learning substantially narrows achievement gaps; low-intensity active learning yields much smaller gap reductions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exam-score gaps are −0.48 SDs with low-intensity active learning versus −0.36 SDs with high-intensity active learning (22% vs. 42% reduction relative to lecturing). [→ Theobald Elli J. et al 2020](#theobald-elli-j-et-al-2020)
`q3 i?` Passing-rate gaps are −9.6% with low-intensity versus −2.0% with high-intensity active learning (16% increase vs. 76% reduction relative to lecturing). [→ Theobald Elli J. et al 2020 (2)](#theobald-elli-j-et-al-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Theobald Elli J. et al 2020

Theobald Elli J. et al. (2020). Active learning narrows achievement gaps for underrepresented students in undergraduate science, technology, engineering, and math. Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1916903117

`q3 · i?`

Moderator analysis using a three-way Trt × MGS × intensity interaction in the hierarchical Bayesian models, where intensity is the reported proportion of class time spent on in-class activities. The article reports "a 22% and 42% reduction, respectively, in the achievement gap"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "students from MGS vs. non-MGS average a difference of −0.48 (95% CI: −0.60 to −0.37) with low-intensity active learning, but only −0.36 (95% CI: −0.45 to −0.27) with high-intensity active learning"

### Theobald Elli J. et al 2020 (2)

Theobald Elli J. et al. (2020). Active learning narrows achievement gaps for underrepresented students in undergraduate science, technology, engineering, and math. Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1916903117

`q3 · i?`

The same moderator analysis applied to passing-rate data from 26 studies. The article reports "a 16% increase and a 76% reduction, respectively, in the achievement gap relative to passive learning"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "differences in passing rates for students from MGS vs. non-MGS are −9.6% (95% CI: −11.0% to −8.2%) with low-intensity active learning, but only −2.0% (95% CI: −3.3 to 0.63%) with high-intensity active learning"
