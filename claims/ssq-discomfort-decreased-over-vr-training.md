## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Cooper Natalia 2021

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulation Sickness Questionnaire ratings collected at baseline, after trial 1 and after trial 5 for the two VR groups (14 each). The article reports a significant main effect of time with no main effect of group, and states that performing the task in VR did not increase SSQ scores relative to baseline.

> "A mixed ANOVA with group (NC, ATV) as a between-subjects factor and time (baseline, trial 1, trial 5) as a within-subject factor showed a significant main effect of time (F(2,52) = 7.02, p = 0.002), but no main effect of group."
