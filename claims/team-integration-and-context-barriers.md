Referral section finding. In the UK and Germany a gap related to suboptimal use of the diabetic team in referral to other members such as specialist diabetic nurse, podiatrist and dietician; in Canada, suboptimal integration between healthcare professionals and lack of knowledge of other professionals' contributions were observed.

> "Suboptimal integration of the diabetic team during the referral process was also reported in the United Kingdom, Canada and Germany."

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Context Issues section finding from EIMSED-NA. Bureaucracy was also named as a barrier in all four countries, with high documentation burden perceived as problematic and physicians performing administrative tasks for which they were not formally trained.

> "Additionally, third party payers were identified as context-related issues in Austria, Germany and the United Kingdom [EIMSED-NA]. Limitations set by health insurance bodies/cost carriers on the prescribing of newer (and in most cases, more expensive) drugs are perceived as a barrier to optimal treatment by physicians [EIMSED-NA]."
