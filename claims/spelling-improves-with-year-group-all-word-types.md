## Evidence

### Niolaki 2022

Niolaki, G., Vousden, J., Terzopoulos, A., Shepherd, D.-L., Debney, L., & Masterson, J. (2022). Spelling predictors; investigating the role of phonological ability and rapid naming in a large cross-sectional British study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 641 children in Yr R to Yr 6 using mixed-effects models with nested school/classroom structure; year group significantly predicted spelling for all three word types, with printed F statistics and r2 values of 0.98, .96 and 0.92.

> "Experience with spelling significantly predicted irregular, regular and pseudoword spelling scores, (F (1, 23.54) = 592.27, F (1, 21.77) = 260.65 and F (1, 21.01) = 123.42, respectively)"
