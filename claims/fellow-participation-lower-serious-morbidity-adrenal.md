`q2 i1` In a cohort of 3694 adrenalectomy patients from the ACS NSQIP database, fellow participation was associated with significantly lower odds of serious morbidity (odds ratio = 0.31; 95% CI, 0.17–0.57) and overall morbidity (odds ratio = 0.51; 95% CI, 0.32–0.82), with no significant association with 30-day mortality. ## Evidence

### Gimm Oliver 2019

Gimm Oliver, Barczyński Marcin, Mihai Radu, Raffaelli Marco. (2019). Training in endocrine surgery. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-019-01828-4

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r?`

The review reports this retrospective cohort study of 3694 adrenalectomies from the ACS NSQIP database, in which 2315 (63%) involved a resident and 647 (17%) a fellow. Printed odds ratios are small-to-moderate protective associations; the review attributes the finding to the cited study.

> "Fellow participation was associated with significantly lower odds of overall (odds ratio = 0.51; 95% CI, 0.32–0.82) and serious (odds ratio = 0.31; 95% CI, 0.17–0.57) morbidity. There was no significant association between trainee participation and 30-day mortality."
