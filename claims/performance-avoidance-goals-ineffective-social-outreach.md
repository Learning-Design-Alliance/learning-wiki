## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with higher performance avoidance goals reach out to more peers (out-degree, positive association) but are reached out to less by peers (in-degree, negative association). [→ Hemi 2025](#hemi-2025)

## Evidence

### Hemi 2025

Hemi, A., Madjar, N., Rich, Y., & Daumiller, M. (2025). Relationships between students' achievement goals and social positioning in the classroom. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1047–1062. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12762

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multilevel SNA analysis (472 students); H4 was only partially supported — in-degree coefficient −2.09* and out-degree coefficient 2.40* in the results table. The quote's asymmetry ("their peers reached out to them less than less performance avoidant classmates") is the core finding; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Namely, students with higher levels of performance avoidance goals tended to reach out to more peers with whom they frequently spent time but at the same time, their peers reached out to them less than less performance avoidant classmates."
