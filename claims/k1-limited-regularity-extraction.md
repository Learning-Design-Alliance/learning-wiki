### Duan 2025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-sample t-tests against the 50% chance level on recognition accuracy by age group. All groups exceeded chance overall (Cohen's ds > 0.52); the article reports K1 children could merely extract positional regularities at high and moderate levels, with their low-consistency t-test not significant (p = 0.140).
