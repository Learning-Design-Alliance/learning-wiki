## Evidence

### Borgonovi Francesca 2025

Borgonovi Francesca, Andrieu Elodie. (2025). The Relationship Between Internet Use, Achievement, and Persistence in Digital Tasks. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.12503

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Observational GLMM/MIRT analysis of PISA 2018 reading item responses (Model 1a) among 128,433 students, using percentile item position to index declines in accuracy over the 2-h assessment. The article prints "(OR = 0.32; B = −1.14; p ≤ 0.01)".

> "when test items are placed at the end of the test students have odds of giving a correct answer that are about 68% lower than when they are placed at the start of the test (OR = 0.32; B = −1.14; p ≤ 0.01)."
