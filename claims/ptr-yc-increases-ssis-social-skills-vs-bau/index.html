Evidence¶

Glen Dunlap 2018¶

Glen Dunlap, Phillip Strain, Janice K. Lee, Jaclyn Joseph, and Nancy Leech. (2018). A Randomized Controlled Evaluation of Prevent-Teach-Reinforce for Young Children. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417724874

q4 · i2 · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA in the same classroom-level RCT on the SSIS social skills subscale found a significant time-by-group interaction, "F(1, 158) = 10.52, p = .001", ηp² = .062; posttest means were 66.11 (PTR-YC) vs 55.37 (BAU), with similar pretest levels.

"Results for the mixed ANOVA specific to SSIS social skills revealed a statistically interaction effect between time and group, F(1, 158) = 10.52, p = .001. The effect size was small for the interaction analyses (ηp 2 = .062)."
