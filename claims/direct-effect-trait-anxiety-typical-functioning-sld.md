## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis in the typical-functioning SLD group (n = 54). The article reports "significant direct effects of trait anxiety on academic performance (β = − 0.28, t = − 3.14, p < 0.01)" and a bootstrap confidence interval including zero for the indirect effect; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The mediation analyses, as Fig. 1b, revealed significant direct effects of trait anxiety on academic performance (β = − 0.28, t = − 3.14, p < 0.01) but not the indirect effect through test anxiety (β = − 0.08, BootLLCI = − 0.2272, BootULCI = 0.0364)."
