Correlational synthesis in Tables 2–3 of the meta-analytic database; text reading correlations reaching r = .98 with maze contrast with maze/sentence-verification to reading-comprehension relations of "from .40 to .66".

> "Notable are .80 between the maze task and text reading efficiency, .90 between sentence verification and text reading efficiency, .93 between the maze task and text reading rate, and .98 between the maze task and text reading accuracy."
