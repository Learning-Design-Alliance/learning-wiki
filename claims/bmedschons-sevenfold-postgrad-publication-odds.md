## Subclaims
`q3 i?` BMedSc(Hons) graduates were more than seven times as likely to have at least one peer-reviewed journal publication post-graduation compared with matched controls (77.7% vs 37.2%). [→ Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2025](#al-busaidi-ibrahim-s-2025)

## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2025

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Ju William, Alamri Yassar, Wilkinson Tim J. (2025). Association of completing an intercalated year-long research degree with post-graduation academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07133-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Matched-cohort study comparing 94 BMedSc(Hons) graduates with 94 controls matched on gender and graduation year, publications identified via MEDLINE and Google Scholar. "Over two-thirds of BMedSc(Hons) graduates" had at least one post-graduation publication, OR 7.3, p < 0.001.

> "Over two-thirds of BMedSc(Hons) graduates (n = 73, 77.7%) had published at least one publication post-graduation compared with 35 (37.2%) controls (odds ratio [OR] 7.3, 95% confidence interval [CI] 3.13–17.21, p < 0.001"
