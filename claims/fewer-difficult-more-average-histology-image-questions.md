## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Item analysis of histology image-based questions in the A11HDT module showed significantly more questions rated 'average' (p < 0.01) and significantly fewer rated 'difficult' (p < 0.001) after the intervention. [→ Then 2023](#then-2023)

## Evidence

### Then 2023

Then, S.-M., Kokolski, M., Mbaki, Y., Merrick, D., & Anderson, S. (2023). An international collaborative approach to learning histology using a virtual microscope. Anatomia, Histologia, Embryologia, 52, 21-30. https://doi.org/10.1111/ahe.12888

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ExamSys difficulty index and discrimination analysis of histology image-based questions in the A11HDT module, comparing before (n=2 cohorts, 2012/13 and 2013/14) and after (n=5 cohorts, 2014/15–2018/19) using two-way ANOVA with Sidak's multiple comparisons test. Significantly more questions were "'average'(p < 0.01)" and significantly fewer "'difficult'(p < 0.001)".

> "This analysis indicated that students found more questions ‘easy', significantly more questions ‘average'(p < 0.01) and significantly fewer questions ‘difficult'(p < 0.001) after the IH project was introduced."
