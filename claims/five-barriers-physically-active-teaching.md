## Evidence

### Rupp Robert 2025

Rupp Robert, Wallmann-Sperlich Birgit, Bucksch Jens. (2025). Strategies and barriers to implementing physically active teaching in universities from the perspective of lecturers: a qualitative study. BMC Public Health 25, Article 855. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22075-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Structured content analysis of 16 semi-structured interviews. The article names the five sub-themes: "Uncertainty in implementation", "Lack of space: too small rooms / too many students", "Lack of time", "Students' unwillingness to move", and "Organisational social norms: Teaching culture based on sitting / Student expectations".

> "Five sub-themes were identified as barriers to the implementation of physically active teaching."

### Rupp Robert 2025 (2)

Rupp Robert, Wallmann-Sperlich Birgit, Bucksch Jens. (2025). Strategies and barriers to implementing physically active teaching in universities from the perspective of lecturers: a qualitative study. BMC Public Health 25, Article 855. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22075-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding on organisational social norms: lecturers noted concerns about being seen as "weird" or feeling uncomfortable prevented stronger integration of physically activating methods, as sitting was simply the norm in university teaching.

> "Half (n = 8) of the lecturers reported a teaching culture that did not support regular stimuli of physical activity."
