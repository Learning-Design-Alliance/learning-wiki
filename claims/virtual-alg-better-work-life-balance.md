Descriptive questionnaire result from Classes 2023 and 2024 (Table 1, question 12): 71.1% disagreed that in-person ALG gave better work-life balance. Qualitative responses cited flexibility, reduced commute time, and reduced stress as reasons.

> "the students responded that they had a better work-life balance (71.1%) with the virtual learning environment and did not face greater academic challenges (59.5%) when they participated virtually."
