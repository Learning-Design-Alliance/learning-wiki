Ehrlich 2020¶

Ehrlich, S.B., Connors, M.C., Stein, A.G., Francis, J., Easton, J.Q., Kabourek, S.E., & Farrar, I.C. (2020). Closer to home: More equitable pre-k access and enrollment in Chicago. q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive pre-post comparison of distance to the closest school with any pre-k across student groups (Figures 8-9). Most groups, including Black students, were no more or less likely to live close to a school with pre-k after the shift; the number of any-pre-k classrooms near home increased only for students in mostly-Latinx neighborhoods and decreased slightly for other groups, possibly due to the 2013-14 closing of 49 elementary schools.

"White and highest-income students lived statistically significantly farther from a CPS school with pre-k in the three years after the policy shift (2013–14 through 2015–16) than in the three years before."
