# Nonmajor biology men show significantly higher positive attitudes in CL than TL classes, with a larger effect in mixed-gender classes

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Nonmajor men’s attitudes toward biology were significantly more positive in CL than TL classes in both mixed-gender (d = 1.8417) and single-gender (d = 1.2460) classes. [→ Almasri Firas 2021](#almasri-firas-2021)
`q3 i3` Men’s positive attitude advantage in CL mixed-gender groups (partial η2 = 0.284) exceeded their advantage in CL single-gender groups (partial η2 = 0.153). [→ Almasri Firas 2021 (2)](#almasri-firas-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Almasri Firas 2021

Almasri Firas, Hewapathirana Gertrude I., Ghaddar Fatme, Lee Nick, Ibrahim Bashar. (2021). Measuring attitudes towards biology major and non-major: Effect of students’ gender, group composition, and learning environment. PLoS ONE 16(5): e0251453. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251453

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparisons in the Results section for nonmajor men, CL versus TL, in mixed- and single-gender classes, with printed means and Cohen’s d of 1.8417 and 1.2460, both p<0.001.

> "Results indicated that nonmajor biology men have significantly higher positive attitude toward biology in CL classes compared to TL classes in both mix-gender (M_CL μ = 3.75 SD = 0.55 versus M_TL μ = 2.69 SD = 0.6) p<0.001 effect size Cohen’s d = 1.8417 and single-gender classes (M_CL μ = 3.28 SD = 0.59 versus M_TL μ = 2.54 SD = 0.60) p<0.001 effect size Cohen’s d = 1.2460."

### Almasri Firas 2021 (2)

Almasri Firas, Hewapathirana Gertrude I., Ghaddar Fatme, Lee Nick, Ibrahim Bashar. (2021). Measuring attitudes towards biology major and non-major: Effect of students’ gender, group composition, and learning environment. PLoS ONE 16(5): e0251453. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251453

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA comparing men’s attitudes across CL group compositions in the Results section: mixed-gender groups partial η2 = 0.284 versus single-gender groups partial η2 = 0.153, with Cohen’s d = 0.2769 between them.

> "Men showed significantly higher positive attitude in CL mixed-gender groups (2M+2W), (F (1, 1265) = 502.49, p <0.001 partial η2 = 0.284) compared to men in CL single-gender groups effects (F (1, 1102) = 199.724, P < 0.001 partial η2 = 0.153) with an effect size Cohen’s d = 0.2769."
