### Nishiyama Masahiro 2023

Nishiyama Masahiro, Kyono Yuki, Yamaguchi Hiroshi, Kawamura Aoi, Oikawa Shizuka, Tokumoto Shoichi, Tomioka Kazumi, Nozu Kandai, Nagase Hiroaki. (2023). Association of early bedtime at 3 years of age with higher academic performance and better non-cognitive skills in elementary school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression in the adjusted kindness model (n = 3036; 433 children, 10.6%, reported low kindness). The 22:00 group (OR 1.76) and ≥23:00 group (OR 2.15) had higher odds of low kindness; the 21:00 adjusted OR of 1.46 (95% CI 0.96–2.21) included 1.

> "children who went to bed later had lesser kindness (22:00 vs. 18:00–20:00, OR: 1.76, 95% CI 1.15–2.68; and ≥ 23:00 vs. 18:00–20:00, OR: 2.15, 95% CI 1.23–3.76)."
