q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's own synthesis of the three RT studies (Tsou 2011; Young & Rasinski 2009, 2018) concludes there is "no evidence demonstrating unique, reliable, or EL-specific gains" from RT alone, leaving a mismatch between widespread practice and the evidence. This is the reviewers' narrative judgment, not a pooled estimate.

"To our knowledge, there is no evidence demonstrating unique, reliable, or EL-specific gains in prosody, fluency, or comprehension attributable to readers' theater alone"
