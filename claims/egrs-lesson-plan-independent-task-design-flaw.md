## Evidence

### Brahm Fleisch and Kerryn Dixon 2019

Brahm Fleisch and Kerryn Dixon. (2019). Identifying mechanisms of change in the Early Grade Reading Study in South Africa. South African Journal of Education, Volume 39, Number 3, August 2019. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v39n3a1696

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' analysis of case-study data against the EGRS daily lesson plans. The article states that "independent tasks designed for learners ... remain the same for the whole year", that learners' need for more challenging work is not factored in, and that disruptions in class increase.

> "One of the design flaws of the EGRS lesson plans is that independent tasks designed for learners take place while the teacher is doing group guided reading , remain the same for the whole year."
