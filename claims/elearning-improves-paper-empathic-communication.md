## Evidence

### Shinohara Eriko 2022

Shinohara Eriko, Ohashi Yukiko, Hada Ayako, Usui Yuriko. (2022). Effects of 1-day e-learning education on perinatal psychological support skills among midwives and perinatal healthcare workers in Japan: a randomised controlled study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-022-00832-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the paper-and-pencil multiple-choice empathic communication test in the same randomised trial (N = 115); post-test means were 5.48 (SD 0.81) intervention versus 4.42 (SD 0.76) control, reported as p < .001 in Table 2.

> "For the secondary outcome, the scores of the ECS-P showed the main effects of the group (F(1) = 32.364, p < 0.0001) and timing (F(1) = 32.364, p < 0.001). Their interactive effect was also significant (F(1,1) = 40.269, p < 0.001)."
