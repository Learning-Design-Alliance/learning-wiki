## Evidence

### Kaldaras 2023

Kaldaras, L., & Wieman, C. (2023). Introducing an Instructional Model for Teaching Blended Math-Science Sensemaking in Undergraduate STEM Courses Using Computer Simulations. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of physics students who did not provide a formula before the activity: 5 of 6 figured out the correct formula from simulation-based quantitative pattern tasks without the MC list. The authors interpret that "engaging in blended MSS at level 2 (quantitative) "Pattern" seems to be critical in helping students transition to level 3".

> "Rather, they were able to figure out the correct formula upon completing the tasks focused on using the simulation to identify the quantitative patterns, which is reflective of engaging in blended MSS at level 2 (quantitative) "Pattern" level. Therefore, engaging in blended MSS at level 2 (quantitative) "Pattern" seems to be critical in helping students transition to level 3."
