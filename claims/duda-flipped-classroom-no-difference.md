## Evidence

### Walter Setti 2025

Walter Setti, Rebecca Tarello, Erica Volta, Lucia Ferlino, Monica Gori, Gualtiero Volpe. (2025). DUDA: a digital didactic learning unit based on educational escape rooms and multisensory learning activities for primary school children during COVID-19 lockdown. Education Tech Research Dev 73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10407-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of classroom-questionnaire scores between the flipped group (17 children, 7 of whom did not return the classroom questionnaire) and the classroom-only group, using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. The article reports "zval = 1.25, p = .22" for Factor 1 and "zval = .42, p = .66" for Factor 2; no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "We did not find any significant difference between the two groups for both Factor 1 (Wilcoxon signed-rank test: zval = 1.25, p = .22) and Factor 2 (Wilcoxon signed-rank test: zval = .42, p = .66)."
