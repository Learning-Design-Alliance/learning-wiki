Evidence¶

Swanson 2010¶

Swanson, E. A., & Vaughn, S. (2010). An Observation Study of Reading Instruction Provided to Elementary Students With Learning Disabilities in the Resource Room. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational results across 10 classrooms: PA totaled 60 minutes (2.8% of instruction), and "Students were engaged in blending or segmenting phonemes for 20% of PA instructional time (or <1% of the total reading instructional time)"; 40% of PA instruction was rated low average or weak.
