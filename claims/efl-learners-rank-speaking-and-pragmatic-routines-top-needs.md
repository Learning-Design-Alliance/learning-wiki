## Evidence

### Üstünbaş 2017

Üstünbaş, Ü. (2017). Language Learners' Feelings about Pragmatics in Action. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 11(2), 155-168. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/novitasroyal

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire administered to the 134 participants, who ranked language skills from highest to lowest need for improvement. Speaking ranked first; "64 out of 134 participants consider pragmatic routines as the sub-skill having the utmost significance."

> "it was found that EFL learners see their primary area of need for improvement as speaking (N=72), followed by vocabulary knowledge (N=42), grammar (N=13), listening (N=5), and writing (N=1)."
