The article reports research finding that underrepresented minoritized faculty representation on admissions committees was associated with increased matriculated student body diversity, and that faculty diversity shapes a welcoming organizational culture. The association is correlational; no effect size is printed.

> "Research suggests that increased faculty diversity is fundamental for shaping an organizational culture that is welcoming to underrepresented minoritized students and that greater representation of underrepresented minoritized faculty on admissions committees was associated with increased diversity of the matriculated student body."
