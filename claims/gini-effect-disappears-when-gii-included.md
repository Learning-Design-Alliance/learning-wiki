# In stepwise country-level regressions, GII is the only significant predictor of achievement and the Gini coefficient's association disappears

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Once GII is added, the Gini coefficient is no longer significant while GII remains the only significant predictor in all three cycles. [→ Li Zhang 2024](#li-zhang-2024)

## Evidence

### Li Zhang 2024

Li Zhang, Baolige Chao, Yan Gao, Wenjing Wang, Yingzi Yuan, Chuangsheng Chen & Ziqiang Xin. (2024). Gender Inequality is negatively associated with academic achievement for both boys and girls. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00261-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Country-level stepwise regressions controlling GDP per capita show that adding GII renders the Gini coefficient nonsignificant while GII "became the only signiﬁcant predictor" with standardized coefficients β = −0.77, β = −0.79, and β = −0.69 across the 2012, 2015, and 2018 cycles.

> "In Model 2, the GII became the only signiﬁcant predictor for the 2012 PISA,β = −0.77, p < 0.001; the 2015 PISA, β = −0.79, p < 0.001; and the 2018 PISA, β = −0.69, p < 0.001."
