## Evidence

### Lye 2014

Lye, S. Y., Wee, L. K., Kwek, Y. C., Abas, S., & Tay, L. Y. (2014). Design, customization and implementation of energy simulation with 5E model in elementary classroom. Educational Technology & Society, 17(3), 121-137. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1408.7040](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1408.7040)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-features perception survey (5-point Likert) with 27 respondents: colour scheme mean 4.41 (SD 0.97), bar graphs mean 4.33 (SD 1.04), speed indication mean 4.30 (SD 1.03), Cronbach's Alpha 0.932. Supported by focus group comments such as 'The indication of height (blue line) helps me to see the relationship between GPE and height'.
