`q2 i?` Skill gaps between children of different socioeconomic status appear early and then persist in parallel during school years, and are highly correlated with family background. [→ Cunha 2007](#cunha-2007)

## Evidence

### Cunha 2007

Flavio Cunha and James Heckman. (2007). The Technology of Skill Formation. NBER Working Paper No. 12840. http://www.nber.org/papers/w12840

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper summarizes evidence from CHLM's survey of child development data: graphs of cognitive and noncognitive skills by age show "early divergence and then parallelism" across socioeconomic groups. Levels of child skills are highly correlated with parental education and maternal ability.

> "CHLM present many graphs of child cognitive and noncognitive skills by age showing early divergence and then parallelism during school-going years across children with parents of different socioeconomic status."
