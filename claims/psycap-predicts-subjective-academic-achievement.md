## Evidence

### Hu Yousong 2025

Hu Yousong, Weng Yuqian, Ning Ping, Han Li, Zhang Youxue. (2025). The relationship between psychological capital and subjective academic achievement among middle school students: the chain mediating role of adolescent internalizing problems and life attitude. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03495-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1,019 Chengdu middle school students analyzed with PROCESS Model 6 (5000 bootstrap samples), controlling for age, gender, PHE, HHI, and prior academic achievement. The direct path was significant: "psychological capital is positively associated with subjective academic achievement (β = 0.18, t = 4.85, p < 0.001)". The direct effect value was 0.180 (59.80% of total effect).

> "within the chain mediation model, psychological capital is positively associated with subjective academic achievement (β = 0.18, t = 4.85, p < 0.001)"
