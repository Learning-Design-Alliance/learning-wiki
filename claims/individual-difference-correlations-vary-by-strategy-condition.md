# Relationships between individual differences and final test performance were nonsignificant in retrieval practice conditions but significant in the rereading condition

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Correlations of both working memory and self-efficacy with final test performance were positive and significant only in the rereading condition and not significant in any retrieval condition. [→ Redifer 2026](#redifer-2026)

## Evidence

### Redifer 2026

Redifer, J. L., Myers, S. J., Bae, C. L., Naas, A., & Scott, A. (2026). Testing the testing effect with advanced materials while accounting for individual differences. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09758-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Exploratory condition-wise correlations (ANCOVA interaction tests were not possible because the condition effect was non-significant). In the reread condition, correlations were significant for working memory, "r(46) = 0.29, p =.04", and self-efficacy, r(48) = 0.33, p =.02; all retrieval-condition correlations were nonsignificant.

> "For all other conditions, the correlations between working memory and test performance and between self-efficacy and test performance were not significant, r's = − 0.23–0.25, p's > 0.05"
