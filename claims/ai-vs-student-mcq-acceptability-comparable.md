Al-Najafi Dheyaa, Krause Katherine D., Wang Yundi, Zuo Qi Kang, Koblanski Maya E, Leong Cameron J., Schmidt Emma, Faran Muhammad, Verma Vanay, Vyas Ravi, Campbell Matthew, Hwang Jaehyun, Deng Jiawen, Palepu Anita. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-exam survey in the ITT population (N=127 AI-gen, N=131 Student-gen) compared nine acceptability domains on 10-point Likert scales using Welch t-tests with Bonferroni correction; absolute mean differences ranged from 0.09 to 0.57 points. The largest effect was for identifying knowledge gaps (d = 0.31, adjusted p = 0.123), favoring the student-generated exam.

> "After correction, no acceptability domain reached statistical significance (all Bonferroni-adjusted p ≥ 0.12), and effect sizes were uniformly small (all Cohen's |d| ≤ 0.31)"
