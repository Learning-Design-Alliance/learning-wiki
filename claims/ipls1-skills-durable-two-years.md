# The IPLS 1 advantage on diffusion-specific code elements is durable: no significant difference appears among capstone students who took IPLS 1 concurrently, one year before, or two or more years before the task.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of three IPLS 1 timing subgroups (concurrent, one year before, two or more years before) within the same capstone task data; the article reports no significant difference among subgroups, with "durable for at least two years" as its suggested interpretation. The authors print only test statistics, not effect sizes.

> "For the IPLS 1-specific code elements, there is no statistically significant difference between the performance of these three subgroups (Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.94), suggesting that the difference observed between the IPLS 1 and non-IPLS 1 groups is durable for at least two years."
