### Dolmans 1994

Dolmans, D., Schmidt, H. & Gijselaers, W. (1994). The relationship between student-generated learning issues and self-study in problem-based learning. Instructional Science: An International Journal of Learning and Cognition, 22(4), 251–267. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00891779

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational comparison at the University of Limburg between the percentage of faculty objectives identified per problem and Topic Checklist scores of actual self-study. The article reports "a low proportion of variance of TOC scores could be predicted" from the learning-issue measures.

> "Comparisons between the procedures revealed that a low proportion of variance of TOC scores could be predicted from the percentage of faculty objectives identified for each problem and the direct match between learning issues and TOC scores."
