## Evidence

### Dovis Sebastiaan 2015

Dovis Sebastiaan, Van der Oord Saskia, Wiers Reinout W., Prins Pier J. M. (2015). Improving Executive Functioning in Children with ADHD: Training Multiple Executive Functions within the Context of a Computer Game. A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0121651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Follow-up contrasts of the 2x3 (full-active vs placebo) repeated measures MANOVA in this three-arm RCT of 89 children with ADHD. The contrasts indicate CBTT forward and backward performance "improved more in the full-active condition than in the placebo condition", with p-values .002-.020 and medium-to-large effect sizes.

> "compared to pre-test performance, post-test- and/or follow-up performance on the Stoptask, the STROOP and the CBTT forward and backward improved more in the full-active condition than in the placebo condition (p-values ranged from. 002 to. 020; effect sizes ranged from medium to large; see Table 3 and Fig 3A–3D)"

### Dovis Sebastiaan 2015 (2)

Dovis Sebastiaan, Van der Oord Saskia, Wiers Reinout W., Prins Pier J. M. (2015). Improving Executive Functioning in Children with ADHD: Training Multiple Executive Functions within the Context of a Computer Game. A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0121651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus 3x3 repeated measures MANOVA simple contrasts in the RCT: "a significant Treatment condition x Time interaction for the CBTT-fwd (p = .002)" between pre- and post-test, with non-significant trends for the Stoptask and CBTT-bkw.

> "Between the pre- and post-test there was a significant Treatment condition x Time interaction for the CBTT-fwd (p = .002), and a non-significant trend for the Stoptask (p = .037) and the CBTT-bkw (p = .039; see Table 2)"
