Alexander Mayer 2014¶

Alexander Mayer, MDRC. (2014). Performance-Based Scholarships: Replication at Six Sites Using Randomized Controlled Trials. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562816

q4 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trials across six sites measuring registration from transcript and Clearinghouse data; the abstract states that in the remainder of sites "there are no statistically significant impacts on registration in the second semester."

"Have mixed impacts on the proportion of students who registered for college in the second semester of the program . In two states, there are small impacts on second -semester registration (4.6 percentage points in Arizona and 3.8 percentage points in California)."
