## Evidence

### Sulistyorini Dewie 2025

Sulistyorini Dewie, Huq K A T M Ehsanul, Babaita Abdulfatai Olamilekan, Aivey Sadia A, Huiying Gao, Kazawa Kana, Fukushima Yasuko, Kako Mayumi, Moriyama Michiko. (2025). Effectiveness of education programme to increase competency of health cadres in Indonesia: a cluster non-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095428

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects model with endline competency as outcome, PHC as random intercept, fitted by maximum likelihood. The abstract-reported coefficients match table 4: "groups (β (95% CI) 2.49 (0.57 to 4.41), p=0.012)" among the significant positive predictors.

> "Mixed-effect model indicated that groups (β (95% CI) 2.49 (0.57 to 4.41), p=0.012), baseline knowledge (β(95% CI) 0.73 (0.54 to 0.92), p<0.001) and midline health assessment skills (β (95% CI) 0.54 (0.25 to 0.82), p<0.001) were significant positive predictors"

### Sulistyorini Dewie 2025 (2)

Sulistyorini Dewie, Huq K A T M Ehsanul, Babaita Abdulfatai Olamilekan, Aivey Sadia A, Huiying Gao, Kazawa Kana, Fukushima Yasuko, Kako Mayumi, Moriyama Michiko. (2025). Effectiveness of education programme to increase competency of health cadres in Indonesia: a cluster non-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095428

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same mixed-effects model: age carried a negative coefficient (β −0.20, 95% CI −0.30 to −0.10, p<0.001). The model's marginal and conditional R² were both 0.52 and the intraclass correlation coefficient for PHC was 0.

> "while age was negatively associated with competency (β (95% CI) −0.20 (−0.30 to −0.10), p<0.001))."
