Regular rhythmic primes improve sentence repetition in children with developmental language disorder. npj Science of Learning 8:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00170-1

In the cumulative link mixed model analysis, "The prime x group interaction" did not significantly improve model fit (χ2 (1) = 0.39, p = 0.53), so the regular-versus-irregular prime benefit was not statistically distinguishable between the DLD and TD groups.

"The prime x group interaction, χ2 (1) = 0.39, p = 0.53, AIC = 1175.73 did not improve the model with prime and group as fixed effects (AIC = 1174.12)"
