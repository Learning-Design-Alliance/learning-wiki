## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chicago research shows disadvantaged schools that develop strong organizational supports are just as likely to show learning improvements and hold on to their teaching staff as advantaged schools. [→ Elaine Allensworth 2012](#elaine-allensworth-2012)

## Evidence

### Elaine Allensworth 2012

Elaine Allensworth. (2012). Want to Improve Teaching? Create Collaborative, Supportive Schools. American Educator, Fall 2012. http://www.annenberginstitute.org/VUE

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The article reports this finding from Chicago research (citing Bryk et al. and the teacher mobility study) as evidence that organizational supports, not student composition, determine improvement and retention outcomes.

> "schools serving highly disadvantaged students that do manage to develop strong organizational supports for teaching are just as likely to show learning improvements and to hold on to their teaching staff as are schools serving more advantaged student populations."
