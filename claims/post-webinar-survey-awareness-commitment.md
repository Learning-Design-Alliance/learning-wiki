### Shepherd V 2025

Shepherd V, Svobodova M, Ivins N, Russell AM, Volkmer A, Jayes M, Woolfall K, Clout M, Munnery K, Treweek S. (2025). Addressing the knowledge gap: development of stakeholder-informed training to improve the inclusion of adults with impaired capacity to consent in trials. Trials. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-025-09182-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Evaluation survey distributed by email to attendees of the December 2024 launch webinar (160 registered attendees; 29 responded). Attendees rated the webinar very good (76%, n = 22) or good (24%, n = 7) and reported planned actions including completing and distributing the training.

> "The post-webinar survey indicated an increased awareness amongst attendees and a commitment to changing practice; however, the proportion of attendees who completed the survey was relatively low, although comparable with previous events run by the research centre, and may be subject to response bias"
