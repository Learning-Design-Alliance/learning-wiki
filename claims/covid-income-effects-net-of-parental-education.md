`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Methodological specification in the regression design: because parental education and household income are likely strongly correlated, the authors "run analyses where we control for the interaction between parental education and the treatment-dummy" alongside the income interaction. The same is done for gender and migration background.

> "In order to isolate the additional impact of COVID-19 along (for example) household income, we therefore run analyses where we control for the interaction between parental education and the treatment-dummy in addition to the interaction with household income."
