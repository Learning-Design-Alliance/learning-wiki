Numerical light-deflection visualization (section 3) of a virtual journey toward a ten-solar-mass black hole (Schwarzschild radius 30 km), with photon paths computed by solving the geodesic equation numerically. At 45 km above the horizon "the black disc covers exactly half the sky".

> "45 km abo ve the event horizon ( r = 1.5rs) it records the image of ﬁgure 8(c), in which the black disc covers exactly half the sky."
