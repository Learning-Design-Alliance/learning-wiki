Evidence¶

National Charter School Resource Center 2020¶

National Charter School Resource Center, lead authors Aimee Evan, Laura Groth, and Hannah Sullivan. (2020). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools: Part 1 The Role and Perspective of Charter School Authorizers. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED609911

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authorizer interview findings on operational capacity: authorizers frequently named disconnect from community needs, evident in enrollment decline, as a distress sign; one authorizer tracked projected against actual enrollment as a predictor of financial concerns.

"Authorizers also noted that declining enrollment can be the first quantitative indicator of other factors such as poor school culture or weak leadership that are more difficult to detect in normal reporting functions."
