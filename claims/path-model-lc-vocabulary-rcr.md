# In a double mediation path model, RCR is associated with listening comprehension, which is associated with vocabulary, with the direct vocabulary-to-RCR effect fully mediated

## Evidence

### Lefèvre 2024

Lefèvre, E., Law, J. M., Prado, J., Anders, R., & Cavalli, E. (2024). Reading Comprehension Resiliency in Adolescents with and without Dyslexia relates to Vocabulary, Listening Comprehension and Socioeconomic Status. https://osf.io/fzyeh/?view_only=84fc51248a0d48ba9a505835e525efdc

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (lavaan) on the merged sample of 95 adolescents testing the hypothesized double mediation model; model fit was excellent (X² = 0.02, p = .89; CFI = 1.00). The printed paths D and B were significant as quoted.

> "RCR was associated with listening comprehension (D: β = 0.23, SE = 0.10, Z = 2.25, p = .024) which was itself associated with vocabulary (B: β = 0.27, SE = 0.10, Z = 2.68, p = .007)."

### Lefèvre 2024 (2)

Lefèvre, E., Law, J. M., Prado, J., Anders, R., & Cavalli, E. (2024). Reading Comprehension Resiliency in Adolescents with and without Dyslexia relates to Vocabulary, Listening Comprehension and Socioeconomic Status. https://osf.io/fzyeh/?view_only=84fc51248a0d48ba9a505835e525efdc

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same path analysis: the vocabulary-to-RCR direct path E' was fully mediated, and the specific indirect path B*D through listening comprehension did not reach significance (p = .08). Bootstrapped standard errors (n = 3000) replicated the initial results.

> "The direct effect of vocabulary on RCR was fully mediated (E’: β = 0.19, SE = 0. 10, Z = 1.87, p = .06) by listening comprehension but the indirect effect, taking into account only vocabulary and listening comprehension, was not significant (B*D: β = 0.06, SE = 0.04, Z = 1.72, p = .08)."
