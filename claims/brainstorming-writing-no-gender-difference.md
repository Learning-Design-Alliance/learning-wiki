## Evidence

### Amoush 2015

Amoush, K. H. (2015). The Impact of Employing Brainstorming Strategy on Improving Writing Performance of English Major Students at Balqa Applied University in Jordan. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(35). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086359

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing male (n=20, M=47.38, SD 2.96) and female (n=20, M=47.76, SD 2.56) students within the experimental group on post-test essay scores. The difference "was not significant", so males and females were equal in writing performance.

> "the mean of the females was a  little bit higher than that of the males’ .this difference between the post- test means scores was not significant  as t value was -0.41 with degree of freedom (38)"
