`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Discriminative validity analysis (Results): participants grouped by OSATS GRS skill level and compared with Kruskal-Wallis testing; both versions "could differentiate between three different levels (novices, intermediates, experts) of experience (p < 0.05)".

> "In general, both unweighted and weighted A-OSATS could differentiate between three different levels (novices, intermediates, experts) of experience (p < 0.05)."
