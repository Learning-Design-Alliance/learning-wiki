## Evidence

### Pettit RK 2015

Pettit RK, McCoy L, Kinney M, Schwartz FN. (2015). Student perceptions of gamified audience response system interactions in large group lectures and via lecture capture technology. BMC Medical Education 15:92. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0373-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of grouped Likert responses between the 60% of respondents who typically experienced TP presentations in person and the 40% via Echo360 lecture capture, using Chi Square/Fisher's Exact tests. The article reports "No Likert scale questions showed a significant difference in proportion of positive and negative responses between the two groups of students"; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "No Likert scale questions showed a significant difference in proportion of positive and negative responses between the two groups of students."

### Pettit RK 2015 (2)

Pettit RK, McCoy L, Kinney M, Schwartz FN. (2015). Student perceptions of gamified audience response system interactions in large group lectures and via lecture capture technology. BMC Medical Education 15:92. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0373-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Open-coding of 42 responses to the prompt on learning value of viewing TP presentations on Echo360. Themes included Self-pace, Pause, Review/Repeat/Practice/Helpful, and Engagement; most responses were positive, but "Eight students indicated that viewing the TP presentations on Echo was not as engaging as participating in class," citing missing competition drive and atmosphere.

> "Eight students indicated that viewing the TP presentations on Echo was not as engaging as participating in class"
