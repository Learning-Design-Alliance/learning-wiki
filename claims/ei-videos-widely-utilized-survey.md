## Evidence

### Michael Courtney 2011

Michael Courtney, Tom Slusher, and Amy Courtney. (2011). EI Videos. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1204.2462](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1204.2462)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous survey of 32 cadets attending an evening EI session late in the Fall 2010 semester, reported with Figure 4. The survey found "Only 2/32 (6%) of cadets had not watched any EI videos", and 19/32 reported watching 10 or fewer of the over 50 available videos; 22% reported watching more than 20.

> "Only 2/32 (6%) of cadets had not watched any EI videos, showing that  the videos were widely utilized among cadets attending evening EI."
