## Evidence

### Gambarı and Yusuf 2015

Amosa Isiaka Gambarı, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf. (2015). Effectiveness of Computer-Assisted Stad Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Problem Solving, Achievement and Retention. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085945

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of the delayed retention test (administered four weeks after the post-test) for the same 84 students. The article reports "an F (1, 81) = 3.870, p = 0.053" and treats it as significant, with mean retention gains of 39.71 (STAD) versus 40.13 (ICI).

> "Table 5 shows that the main effect of treatment group (computer -assisted STAD) and ICI groups produced an F (1, 81) = 3.870, p = 0.053. This result was significant at the .05 alpha level."

### Gambarı and Yusuf 2015 (2)

Amosa Isiaka Gambarı, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf. (2015). Effectiveness of Computer-Assisted Stad Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Problem Solving, Achievement and Retention. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085945

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The Discussion section contradicts the Results section's characterization of the retention test, stating "no significant difference in retention" between the STAD and ICI groups. The p = 0.053 value is marginal either way.

> "The results of the analyses related to hypothesis three indicated no significant difference in retention of students taught physics using computer -assisted STAD cooperative learning and those taught using ICI."
