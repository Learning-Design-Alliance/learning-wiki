## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Survey responses deviated significantly from an even distribution across the five Q1 options in 2022–2023 (χ²=50.71) and 2023–2024 (χ²=43.43), which the author reads as rejecting H01 of no impact on learning; the test compares answer frequencies with an even spread and does not measure learning. ### Hongbin Song 2025

Hongbin Song. (2025). Evaluation of a Virtual Laboratory Platform in General Education on Quantum Information Science. General Education Division, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.23813](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.23813)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square test of multiple-choice Q1 responses against an even distribution across five options, with 52 samples in 2022–2023 and 14 in 2023–2024. Both chi-square values exceed the critical value 9.488 (α = 0.05, 4 df), which the author treats as rejecting H01 (no impact on learning); the test shows only that answers were not evenly spread, with no pre-test or comparison group. No effect size is printed.
