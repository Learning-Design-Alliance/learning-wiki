## Evidence

### Adileh 2012

Adileh, M. (2012). Teaching music as a university elective course through e-learning. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2012(1), 71-79. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000246

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison on a 40-item four-point Likert attitude scale (reliability 0.92) administered to the same 179 students. Table 4 reports blended M = 130.535 vs FTF M = 124.505, t(177) = 3.003, p = .003; no effect size is printed.

> "there was a significant difference in attitudes towards music learning between the FTF and blended groups after the instruction took place (p<.01).The experimental (blended) group's mean score on the Attitude Scale was higher than the control (FTF) group's mean score."
