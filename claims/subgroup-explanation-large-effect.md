## Evidence

### Cappetta 2013

Cappetta, R.W., and Zollman, A. (2013). Agents of Change in Promoting Reflective Abstraction: A Quasi-Experimental Study on Limits in College Calculus. REDIMAT – Journal of Research in Mathematics Education, 2(3), 343-357. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2013.35

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-sided t-test on the explanation measure for the twelve-student three-tiered comparison subgroup (six per section), re-scored on the five-point extended response rubric. Means were 44.33 (experimental) versus 35.50 (traditional).

> "A significant difference was identified for the explanation measure, t (10) = 1.98, p = .04.  Cohen's d = 1.14 indicating a large effect."
