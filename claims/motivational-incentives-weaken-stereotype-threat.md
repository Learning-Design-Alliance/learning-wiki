## Evidence

### Shewach 2019

Shewach, O. R., Sackett, P. R., & Quint, S. (2019). Stereotype Threat Effects in Settings With Features Likely Versus Unlikely in Operational Test Settings: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Applied Psychology, 104(12), 1514–1534. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/apl0000420

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator comparison of lab studies offering differential incentives (money, extra credit, gifts, or applicant primes) against studies without them. The incentive-present effect is d = -.14 and the no-incentive effect d = -.41; the contrast is reported as "t(146) ⫽ 1.85, p ⫽ .03" with no standardized effect size for the difference.

> "When any form of motivational incentive was present, the magnitude of stereotype threat was d ⫽ ⫺.14 (k ⫽ 11, N ⫽ 697, SD␦ ⫽ .45), which was significantly weaker than d ⫽ ⫺.41 (k ⫽ 137, N ⫽ 6,690, SD␦ ⫽ .49) when no motivational incentives were present, t(146) ⫽ 1.85, p ⫽ .03."
