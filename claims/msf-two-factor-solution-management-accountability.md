# Exploratory factor analysis of the colleague and coworker questionnaires yields a two-factor solution (clinical management with leadership; accountability) explaining at least 75% of variance with Cronbach's α above 0.9 and high generalizability coefficients

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2021 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the medical colleague and coworker questionnaires, a two-factor structure (clinical management with leadership and accountability) explained at least 75% of total variance, with Cronbach's α above 0.90 and generalizability coefficients (Ep2) of 0.98, 0.94, 0.97 and 0.92. [→ Carenzo L 2021](#carenzo-l-2021)

## Evidence

### Carenzo L 2021

Carenzo L, Cena T, Carfagna F, Rondi V, Ingrassia PL, Cecconi M, Violato C, Della Corte F, Vaschetto R. (2021). Assessing anaesthesiology and intensive care specialty physicians: An Italian language multisource feedback system. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250404

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis (principal axis, Promax rotation, loadings ≥0.32) of colleague and coworker responses. "The Cronbach α coefficients for the factors for the medical colleague and non-medical colleague instruments were high, all above 0.90", with Ep2 of 0.98/0.94 (colleague) and 0.97/0.92 (coworker).

> "Factor analyses resulted in a two-factor solution: clinical management with leadership and accountability accounting for at least 75% of the total variance for the medical colleague and coworker's survey with high internal consistency reliability (Cronbach's α > 0.9)."
