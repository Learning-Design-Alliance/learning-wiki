## Evidence

### Gamboa Oscar Andrés 2018

Gamboa Oscar Andrés, Agudelo Sergio Iván, Maldonado María Jose, Leguizamón Diana C., Cala Sandra M. (2018). Evaluation of two strategies for debriefing simulation in the development of skills for neonatal resuscitation: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3831-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple blind randomized clinical trial in which 24 professionals (12 per arm) were randomized to oral or video-assisted debriefing after three standardized simulated scenarios, with a blinded reviewer scoring a checklist. The difference coefficient was "− 3.6% (95% CI − 13.77% to 6.47%)"; no significant difference between strategies.

> "The two debriefing strategies increased the technical and behavioral neonatal resuscitation skills of the participants, without one being superior to the other. The coefficient of the difference in the compliance percentage between the two types of debriefing was − 3.6% (95% CI − 13.77% to 6.47%)."
