# Both novice and intermediate microsurgeons improved completion and idle times when repeating task segments

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Completion times decreased from segment 1 to 2 and from segment 3 to 4 for both groups, suggesting a learning effect from repetition. [→ Gholami Soheil 2024](#gholami-soheil-2024)

## Evidence

### Gholami Soheil 2024

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of completion-time medians across repeated segments within one session: novices improved from 39.92(29.11) to 26.02(12.88) s and intermediates from 19.11(15.29) to 11.21(12.81) s between S.1 and S.2; the article reports these as descriptive observations without a significance test.

> "we observe improvements from the first segment to the second one and from the third segment to the fourth one, indicating enhanced performance in repetition of the same segment"
