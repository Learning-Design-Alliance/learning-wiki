The review reports, citing Godat et al. (2007), a pilot study using survey, video analysis, interaction coding and focus groups in a virtual corporation crisis environment; students also reported learning decision-making (57%), risk-taking (64%) and problem-solving (62%) skills.

> "An overall mean value of 62% for small groups compared to 59% for the MGB210 GP (large group) of students agreeing that VR increased their understanding in solving crisis-type problems"
