## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 17 sessions, 319 questionnaires were completed by 20 students (85.3% response rate), with means near 1.2 on the 6-point scale for most items; preparedness (M=1.89) and anxiety (M=2.64) were rated relatively weaker. [→ Huhn D 2015](#huhn-d-2015)

## Evidence

### Huhn D 2015

Huhn D, Eckart W, Karimian-Jazi K, Amr A, Herzog W, Nikendei C. (2015). Voluntary peer-led exam preparation course for international first year students: Tutees' perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0391-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive quantitative evaluation using 14-item 6-point Likert questionnaires after every session. Table 2 reports e.g. atmosphere M=1.2 (SD=.52), tutor support M=1.14, better prepared M=1.89 (SD=1.00), less scared M=2.64 (SD=1.48); no inferential tests were reported.

> "A total of 319 evaluation questionnaires were completed over the 17 sessions by 20 students, corresponding to a response rate of 85.3 %."
