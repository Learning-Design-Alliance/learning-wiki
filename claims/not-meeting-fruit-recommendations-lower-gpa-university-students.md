## Evidence

### Ong C.K.Y. 2021

Ong C.K.Y., Hutchesson M.J., Patterson A.J., Whatnall M.C. (2021). Is There an Association between Health Risk Behaviours and Academic Achievement among University Students? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168314

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted linear regression in a cross-sectional survey of 1543 Australian university students, controlling for socio-demographic and student characteristics. The article reports that "lower GPA remained significantly associated with not meeting fruit recommendations (β = −0.203, p ≤ 0.001)". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "lower GPA remained significantly associated with not meeting fruit recommendations (β = −0.203, p ≤ 0.001), consuming > 1 cup of soft drink per week (β = −0.307, p ≤ 0.001)"
