# Using cartoons in social studies lessons significantly increases grade 4 students' awareness of water issues compared with traditional teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Omani grade 4 students taught a water unit with cartoons scored significantly higher on a water-awareness post-test than students taught with traditional methods. [→ Al-Rabaani 2017](#al-rabaani-2017)

## Evidence

### Al-Rabaani 2017

Al-Rabaani, A. H., & Al-AAmri, I. H. (2017). The Effect of Using Cartoons on Developing Omani Grade 4 Students’ Awareness of Water Issues and their Attitudes towards Using them in Teaching Social Studies. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 8(1), 35-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141986

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with 33 experimental and 32 control grade 4 students at one school in Al-Dakiliah province; post-test t-test showed a significant difference in favor of the cartoon group, with means of 14.59 versus 9.43 out of 20.

> "The results showed statistically significant differences between experimental and control groups in favor of the experimental group. The mean of the experimental group as (14.59) from a total of (20) which means that the awareness level reached 72.9% compared to the control group, whose mean was (9.43)"
