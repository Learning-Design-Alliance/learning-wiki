`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Causal analysis of the nationally representative TIMSS 2019 Irish eighth-grade sample using a multivariate Bayesian Causal Forests model with sampling weights and five plausible values. The article reports the average treatment effect of daily mathematics homework versus the baseline of up to one or two times per week, calling it "an approximately0.1 standard deviation increase in mathematics achievement".

> "Looking at the “Every Day” section for mathematics in Table 2, the ATE and 95% credible interval is 7.36 and (1.81, 15.60)."
