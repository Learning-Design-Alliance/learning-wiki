## Evidence

### Cavanagh A. J. 2018

Cavanagh A. J., Chen X., Bathgate M., Frederick J., Hanauer D. I., Graham M. J. (2018). Trust, Growth Mindset, and Student Commitment to Active Learning in a College Science Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-06-0107

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVAs in the N = 245 survey comparing naturally occurring gender groups: men scored higher than women on overall commitment, F(1, 239) = 4.56, p = 0.01, and on persuasion and commitment subscales; differences in trust, mindset, engagement, exposure, identification, and grade were nonsignificant.
