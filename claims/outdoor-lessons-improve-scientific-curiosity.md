## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kan Lin Ting & Nyet Moi Siew 2014

Kan Lin Ting & Nyet Moi Siew. (2014). Effects of Outdoor School Ground Lessons on Students' Science Process Skills and Scientific Curiosity. Journal of Education and Learning, 3(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v3n4p96

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on post-test Children's Scientific Curiosity Scale scores in the quasi-experiment (eco-hunt n = 63, control n = 56). Pre-test means did not differ significantly (t(117) = 1.930, p = .056).

> "there was a significant difference in post-test mean scores between “eco-hunt” group and control group (t(117) = 5.394, p < .001) in scientific curiosity"

### Kan Lin Ting & Nyet Moi Siew 2014 (2)

Kan Lin Ting & Nyet Moi Siew. (2014). Effects of Outdoor School Ground Lessons on Students' Science Process Skills and Scientific Curiosity. Journal of Education and Learning, 3(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v3n4p96

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests within groups on scientific curiosity: eco-hunt t(62) = -2.641, p = .010; control t(55) = -2.822, p = .007. The article reports the mean changes of 5.159 and -5.625 descriptively; no effect size is printed.

> "an increase mean score of 5.159 in “eco-hunt” group was found, on the contrary, a decrease mean score of 5.625 was observed in control group"
