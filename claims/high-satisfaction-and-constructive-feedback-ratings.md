End-of-course questionnaire (n = 173 of 182) with 14 Likert-type quantitative items analyzed descriptively. The printed percentages are descriptive response distributions; no inferential test or effect size is reported for these items.

> "At the end of the course, the majority of the students were satisfied (88.5%) and felt that their expectations of the course had been fulfilled (85.0%) (see Table 2).
