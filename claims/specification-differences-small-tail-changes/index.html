Comparison of value-added estimates across model specifications using urban school district data. The paper reports the specifications "produce substantially different value-added estimates" but "small changes in the tails of value-added distribution."

"While these different specifications produce substantially different value-added estimates, they produce small changes in the tails of value-added distribution"
