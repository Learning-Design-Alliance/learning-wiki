## Evidence

### Wang Song 2019

Wang Song, Zhao Yajun, Li Jingguang, Wang Xu, Luo Kui, Gong Qiyong. (2019). Brain structure links trait conscientiousness to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48704-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational and hierarchical regression analysis in the same 148-student sample, with academic performance measured by Chinese National University Entrance Exam (CNUEE) scores collected from school databases. The printed correlation was r = 0.30, an effect of medium size by the correlation code table.

> "First, we verified the positive correlation of trait conscientiousness with academic performance (r = 0.30, p < 0.001). Further regression analysis revealed that trait conscientiousness explained additional variance in academic performance after adjusting for gender, age and total GMV (△R2 = 7.4%, β = 0.28, p < 0.001)."
