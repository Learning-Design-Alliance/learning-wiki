## Evidence

### Kelley 2015

Kelley, E. S., Goldstein, H., Spencer, T. D., & Sherman, A. (2015). Effects of automated Tier 2 storybook intervention on vocabulary and comprehension of preschool children with limited oral language skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 31, 47-61. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized control group design with 18 children (n = 9 per group) from public prekindergarten programs serving families with low income; ANOVA on unit test gain scores used "Bonferroni adjusted alpha levels of 0.016". The treatment group showed "greater gains than comparison participants" on all three units.

> "participants in the treatment group had greater gains than comparison participants for each of the three units [F(1, 17) = 24.33, 8.53, 30.86 for Units 1, 2, 3 respectively, p ≤ 0.01]"

### Kelley 2015 (2)

Kelley, E. S., Goldstein, H., Spencer, T. D., & Sherman, A. (2015). Effects of automated Tier 2 storybook intervention on vocabulary and comprehension of preschool children with limited oral language skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 31, 47-61. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.12.004

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Cohen's d computed from gain scores in the same randomized group design (n = 9 per group) were large for every unit: "Unit 1, d = 2.33; Unit 2, d = 1.37; Unit 3, d = 2.62."

> "Effect sizes, Cohen's d, calculated using the gain scores for each unit test were large: Unit 1, d = 2.33; Unit 2, d = 1.37; Unit 3, d = 2.62."
