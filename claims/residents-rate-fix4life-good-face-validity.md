## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2026

Rahimi A. Masie, van Emden Michael, Hardon Sem F., Simons Maarten, Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim, Daams Freek. (2026). Assessing the realism and face validity of Fix For Life: an embalmed human cadaver model for high-fidelity laparoscopic training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12550-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face validation survey of 30 junior surgical residents after a hands-on laparoscopy course on Fix4Life cadavers, using a five-point Likert form. The appendectomy scored "higher than 4, equaling to a rating of “Good”" on all points (e.g., laparoscopic view 4.30, SD 0.53).

> "The residents scored the laparoscopic appendectomy higher than 4, equaling to a rating of “Good” for all assessment points: laparoscopic view (4.30 Mean, SD 0.53), organ color (4.27 Mean, SD 0.74)"
