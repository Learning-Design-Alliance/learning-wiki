## Evidence

### Shen L 2024

Shen L, Xu D, Zhang X. (2024). What factors influencing surgical nurses' competence in implementing person-centered care in the perioperative period?: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000040035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based questionnaire survey of 437 surgical nurses from 4 tertiary hospitals in Hefei, Anhui, China, collected June to September 2023; stepwise multiple regression with dummy-coded predictors. The article reports "All 4 contributing factors accounted for 59.2% of the variation" (adjusted R2 = 0.592).

> "The regression model for surgical nurses' PCC competence was significant (F = 158.930, P < .001) indicating that PCC competence was influenced by empathy (β = 0.502, P < .001), job burnout (β = −0.288, P < .001), humanistic nursing training (β = 0.167, P < .001), and personnel agency (β = 0.083, P < .001). All 4 contributing factors accounted for 59.2% of the variation."
