## Evidence

### Lee Young Hwan 2018

Lee Young Hwan, Kim Kyong-Jee. (2018). Enhancement of student perceptions of learner-centeredness and community of inquiry in flipped classrooms. BMC Medical Education, 18, 242. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1347-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired t-tests on the 34-item Revised Community of Inquiry instrument with 55 students (n = 55). Total post-course means were 3.73 (teaching), 3.70 (social), and 3.80 (cognitive), each with t(p) <.001 in Table 2; no effect sizes printed.

> "The participants’ sense of community of inquiry increased significantly over time in all three presences."
