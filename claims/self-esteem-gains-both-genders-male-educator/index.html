Evidence¶

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013¶

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Gender analysis within the PC cluster (PCfemale n = 86, PCmale n = 97) using difference variables because of baseline differences. The discussion reports small effect sizes for girls and moderate effect sizes for boys, with boys showing a probably artificial ceiling effect.

"Girls showed lower self-esteem scores in the pre-test , however, pedagogical significance and effect sizes could be achieved for both genders."

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013 (2)¶

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Because of group size, gender effects could only be analyzed within the PC cluster; a male educator conducted the interventions in all classes, so no educator-gender contrast was manipulated.

"Our third hypothesis was rejected, the educator’s gender showing no effect."
