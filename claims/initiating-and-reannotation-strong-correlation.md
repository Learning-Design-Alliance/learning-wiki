## Evidence

### Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang 2026

Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang. (2026). From individual annotations to shared attention: How re-annotation supports collaborative knowledge building in CSCL. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09486-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-level Kendall's tau correlation analysis (16 groups) aggregating annotation frequencies, reported under RQ2 with a printed correlation of τ = 0.95, p < 0.05. The authors describe it as indicating a "persistent engagement pattern across annotation behaviors"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the results demonstrate a strong positive correlation between initiat- ing annotations and re-annotations (τ = 0.95, p  < 0.05), indicating that students who ranked higher in initiating annotations also tended to rank higher in re-annotations."
