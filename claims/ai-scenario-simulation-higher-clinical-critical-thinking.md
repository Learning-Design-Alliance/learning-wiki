## Evidence

### Zheng Koulong 2024

Zheng Koulong, Shen Zhiyu, Chen Zanhao, Che Chang, Zhu Huixia. (2024). Application of AI-empowered scenario-based simulation teaching mode in cardiovascular disease education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05977-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment of 34 vs 32 undergraduates using a 100-point clinical critical thinking scale adapted from Robert Ennis's framework, covering "logical reasoning, central argument, argumentation evidence and organizational structure". Table 2 reports 92.941 ± 2.131 vs 89.312 ± 2.533, t = 6.311, P < 0.001. No effect size printed.

> "Furthermore, the clinical critical thinking proficiency of the experimental group surpassed that of the control group, as indicated by statistical significance (p < 0.001) (Table 2)."
