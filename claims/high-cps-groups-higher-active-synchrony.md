## Evidence

### Cukurova 2017

Cukurova, M., Luckin, R., Millán, E., & Mavrikis, M. (2017). The NISPI framework: Analysing collaborative problem-solving from students' physical interactions. Preprint submitted to Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.08.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of video-coded group behaviour from twelve university groups and three high school groups during open-ended TALKOO activities. The article reports "the highest three percentage values of active synchrony" for the high CPS groups versus much lower values for low CPS groups; no effect size is printed.

> "The three high CPS university groups have the highest three percentage values of active synchrony. On the other hand, those groups which were identified as low CPS by human observers appear to present much lower percentages of active synchrony."
