## Evidence

### Hedman Leif Rune 2020

Hedman Leif Rune, Felländer-Tsai Li. (2020). Simulation-based skills training in non-performing orthopedic surgeons: skills acquisition, motivation, and flow during the COVID-19 pandemic. Acta Orthopaedica. https://doi.org/10.1080/17453674.2020.1781413

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pilot experiment, part of a previously published investigation, in which 30 surgical novices trained on manipulative diathermy in the MIST-VR simulator for about 1 hour to a pre-set criterion; the authors report that flow state "was correlated with the best performance (data not shown)". No correlation coefficients are printed.

> "However, flow state scores for the short and long version of the flow scales were positively correlated, and flow state assessed by the short flow state scale was correlated with the best performance (data not shown)."
