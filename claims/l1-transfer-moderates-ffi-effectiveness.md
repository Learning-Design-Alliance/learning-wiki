## Evidence

### Schenck 2024

Schenck, A. (2024). Learning How to Use Input-Based and Output-Based Form-Focused Instruction: A Meta-Analytic Comparison of Persian EFL Learners. Journal of Second Language Acquisition and Teaching, 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1457419

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Cross-study comparison within the meta-analysis: verb tenses and the conditional (L1-similar) favored input, while passive voice, causative, relative clause, and so/such clauses favored output. The article frames this as a suggestion positive transfer may occur.

> "Experimental studies that examined features similar to the L1 had higher values for input-based instruction."
