## Evidence

### Wang Lijing 2025

Wang Lijing, Sun Jiancui, Wang Lijuan, Zheng Danheng, Zhou Yulan. (2025). Fundamental motor skills, executive functions, and academic achievement of Chinese schoolchildren: Testing a mediating model. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0336470

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis within the SEM models (Table 6, N = 733), testing Age*FMS, Gender*FMS, Age*EF, and Gender*EF interaction terms on each path. Only the Age*EF interaction on the mathematics path was significant; the authors state the older the children, the weaker the influence of FMS on math scores via this path.

> "In the mathematics model, it is only found that age significantly and negatively moderates the path between EF and mathematics (β = −0.627, 95% CI [−0.698, −0.332], p < 0.001)."
