## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2019

Suzanne White Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Andrew Boudreaux, Trevor Smith, Charlotte Zimmermann. (2019). A Conceptual Blend Analysis of Physics Quantitative Literacy Reasoning Inventory Items. Physics Education Research Conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1911.01552](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1911.01552)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview case analysis of one student (Jessie) on a PIQL proportional reasoning item about the steepness of a slide, analyzed via CBT. Jessie never reconciled that the hypotenuse is appropriate only if height is held constant, and used "a common heuristic of a physics classroom."

> "Jessie activates the generic space shown in Fig. 3, but it is unstable; they aren't creating an independent quantity. Jessie shows weakness in the projection process between the blend and each of the two input spaces."
