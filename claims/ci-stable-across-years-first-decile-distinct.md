## Evidence

### Sawdon Marina 2020

Sawdon Marina, McLachlan JC. (2020). '10% of your medical students will cause 90% of your problems': a prospective correlational study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation of combined year-1 and year-2 CI scores across 3 cohorts found the CI "is stable between years 1 and 2" (p=0.001, R=0.54); r=0.54 is printed. The authors then used the average of both years.

> "we observed that the CI is stable between years 1 and 2; analysis using a Pearson’s correlation test of the combined CI scores for 3 cohorts of students showed a high degree of correlation (p=0.001, with R=0.54)"

### Sawdon Marina 2020 (2)

Sawdon Marina, McLachlan JC. (2020). '10% of your medical students will cause 90% of your problems': a prospective correlational study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA across CI deciles (F(9, 848)=935.66, p<0.001) with post-hoc t-test showing "the first decile differs from all other deciles (p<0.001, n=858)", reported with figure 1. This motivated treating the first decile separately in later analyses.

> "One-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) indicates that the deciles do not all belong to the same group (F(9, 848)=935.66, p<0.001), and a post-hoc t-test reveals that the first decile differs from all other deciles (p<0.001, n=858)."
