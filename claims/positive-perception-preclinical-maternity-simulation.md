## Evidence

### ALFozan et al. 2015

Haya ALFozan, Yousria El Sayed, Farida Habib. (2015). Designing, Implementing and Evaluating Preclinical Simulation Lab for Maternity Nursing Course. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.12. https://www.iiste.org/journals/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-rotation questionnaire study of 64 maternity nursing students using Jeffries' (2005) 20-item five-point Likert perception scale across five categories. Students agreed there was "enough information provided at the beginning of simulation"; fidelity items drew the least agreement, with only 75% agreeing the scenario resembled a real-life situation.

> "Participants expressed positive perception toward the simulation-based sessions. Regarding objective and information 92.2% of the students agreed that there was enough information provided at the beginning of simulation (M = 4.08, S D = 0.7o)"
