## Evidence

### Lei Y 2025

Lei Y, Zhang Z, Zhou F, Xia T, Xu N, Sun Z. (2025). Virtual reality force feedback spine surgery simulator training for pedicle screw placement: assessing the impact of seniority and spinal region. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07695-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline freehand sawbone placement across four spinal regions in the 64-fellow study. The authors attribute the lumbar advantage to junior surgeons having "fewer opportunities to perform pedicle screw placement" in the other regions.

> "pre-training lumbar pedicle screw placement accuracy (75.94% ±13.07%) was higher than that of atlantoaxial (58.13% ±15.59%), subaxial cervical (63.13%±15.37%), and thoracic vertebrae (59.38%±18.52%), with significant differences observed (p = 0.002 for L vs. A, p = 0.025 for L vs. LC, p = 0.004 for L vs. T)."
