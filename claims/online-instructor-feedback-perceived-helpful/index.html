Evidence¶

Ebadi 2018¶

Ebadi, S., & Asakereh, A. (2018). Using Voice Thread to Enhance Speaking Accuracy: A Case Study of Iranian EFL Learners. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1198886

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Diary data from 5 of 9 experimental participants, triangulated with interviews; one participant wrote that "the comments I received from the instructor helped me to correct my grammar", while several reported insufficient peer comments.

"In their diaries, they all unanimously referred to the facilitative effects of online feedback they received from their instructor."
