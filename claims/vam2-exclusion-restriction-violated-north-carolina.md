## Evidence

### Rothstein 2008

Rothstein, Jesse. (2008). Teacher Quality in Educational Production: Tracking, Decay, and Student Achievement. NBER Working Paper No. 14442. http://www.nber.org/papers/w14442

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Falsification of VAM2 in North Carolina data (Table 4): controlling for 4th grade math and reading scores, the printed residual correlations are effect sizes of r = -0.21 in math and r = -0.19 in reading — residuals are "strongly correlated between 4th and 5th grades" — violating the exclusion restriction in both subjects.

> "The VAM2 residuals, like the VAM1 residuals, are strongly correlated between 4th and 5th grades, -0.21 in math and -0.19 in reading."
