Barrier and training-history results from the cross-sectional survey (N = 420). The study reports "the lack of supplies reported by 70% of the participants followed by insufficient training (69.3%)" and that only 121 of 420 students (28.8%) had previously received formal training in infection control practices.

> "The most frequently reported barrier is the lack of supplies reported by 70% of the participants followed by insufficient training (69.3%) and lack of supervision (55.5%)"
