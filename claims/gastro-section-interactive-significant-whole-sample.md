### Löffler Sabine 2011

Löffler Sabine, Feja Christine, Widmann Jan, Claus Ilona, von Lindeman Katharina, Eisnach Kristina. (2011). Interactive versus Reproductive Learning, a Comparison of Medical School Graduates with Participants of a Postgraduate CPD Session. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000769](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000769)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-analysis applying the univariate trifactorial variance analysis only to post-test questions on the gastrointestinal tract. The article reports the interactive vs reproductive difference was significant for the entire sample, not only weak-prior-knowledge subjects, with printed medians of 15 vs 14 (vocational) and 16 vs 15 (postgraduate).

> "A significant effect for the intervention group (p=0.006) and the training group (p=0.042) can be seen. The intervention groups differ significantly, as already shown for the entire dataset, from the control group (p<0.0005)."
