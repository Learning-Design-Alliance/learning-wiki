`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of multi-select statements from the group simulation questionnaires (Table 3); because statements were non-ordinal, responses were analyzed by frequency of selection. "78% of the students felt that they had learned a new theory"; no test statistics were reported.

> "78% of the students felt that they had learned a new theory and were exposed to new directions of thought. In terms of applying learning, more than 56% of the interns felt that they would know how to use, infer and apply different tools acquired in the simulation in the future (Table 3)."
