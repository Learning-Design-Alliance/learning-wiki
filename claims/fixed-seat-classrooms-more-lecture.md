## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Classroom type has a medium effect on lecture time, with those teaching in traditional fixed-seat classrooms reporting higher proportions of class time spent in lecture. [→ Apkarian 2021](#apkarian-2021)

## Evidence

### Apkarian 2021

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Welch t-test comparing self-reported lecture percentage between instructors in group-work-designed classrooms and traditional fixed-seat lecture halls in the national survey. The article reports a "medium" effect, g = 0.63, and notes some difference may reflect active-learning-oriented instructors requesting such rooms.

> "The amount of lecture used by instructors in different classroom types is different, t(3612.2) = 18.98, p < 0.001, g = 0.63 (medium)."
