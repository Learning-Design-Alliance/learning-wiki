## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2018

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Jacob T. Stanley, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2018). Characterizing lab instructors' self-reported learning goals to inform development of an experimental modeling skills assessment. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020118)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview study of 35 lab instructors (19 optics, 16 electronics); semistructured interviews coded with an a priori scheme derived from the Modeling Framework. Results show "every subtask was identified as important by multiple optics and electronics instructors" and no instructor named zero subtasks.

> "As shown in Fig. 2, every subtask was identified as important by multiple optics and electronics instructors. Every instructor said at least one subtask was important"
