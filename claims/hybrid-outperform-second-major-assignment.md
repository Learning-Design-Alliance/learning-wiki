### Aly 2013

Aly, I. (2013). Performance in an online introductory course in a hybrid classroom setting. Canadian Journal of Higher Education, 43(2), 85-99. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1013589

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on the second major assignment showed hybrid students "performed significantly better than those in the online section", with a reported F-Value of 8.755 at the 0.003 significance level.
