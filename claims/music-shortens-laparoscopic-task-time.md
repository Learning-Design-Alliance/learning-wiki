## Evidence

### Han Ying 2022

Han Ying, Zheng Bin, Zhao Linyong, Hu Jiankun, Zhang Chao, Xiao Ran, Wang Chunyan, Pu Dan. (2022). Impact of background music on the performance of laparoscopy teams. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03503-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way within-subject ANOVA across the three acoustic conditions in the same 57 teams. Object transportation was fastest under music, with the printed means "65.9 ± 17.8 s" (music), 74.9 ± 19.9 s (neutral), and 90.4 ± 24.1 s (noisy), p < 0.001; post-hoc music vs. noisy was significant.

> "The time to perform a task under the pleasant and smooth music (65.9 ± 17.8 s) was shorter than the time to perform tasks in a neutral (74.9 ± 19.9 s) and a noisy environment (90.4 ± 24.1 s; p < 0.001)."
