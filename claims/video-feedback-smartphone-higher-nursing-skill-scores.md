## Evidence

### Xiaoxian Yang 2019

Xiaoxian Yang, Ri-Hua Xie, Si Chen, Wei Yu, Yan Liao, Daniel Krewski, Shi Wu Wen. (2019). Using Video Feedback Through Smartphone Instant Messaging in Fundamental Nursing Skills Teaching: Observational Study. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/15386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of 195 second-year nursing students (65 video feedback, 130 control) in 6 classes at Wuxi Taihu University; groups compared on final examination and 6 skill assignments. Bed making adjusted mean difference 8.82 (95% CI 6.67, 10.97); final examination 1.30 (95% CI 0.44, 2.16).

> "Scores on the final examination, bed making, aseptic procedure, vital signs measurement, and oxygen therapy were significantly higher in the video feedback group than in the control group."
