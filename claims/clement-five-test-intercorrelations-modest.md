## Evidence

### Geyer 1921

Geyer, D. L. (1921). The Reliability of Rankings by Group Intelligence Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeduapsy

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports, citing J. A. Clement's study of 49 Northwestern University students, ten pairwise Pearson correlations (e.g., Army-Thurstone 0.60, Pressey-Indiana 0.22). The review attributes the finding to Clement's published study.

> "It is here seen that the Indiana Mental Survey test correlates with none of the others by as much as 0.40, and that the Pressey Cross Out test has no correlations as high as 0.45."
