## Evidence

### Peura 2026

Peura, P., Koponen, T., Aro, T., Viholainen, H., & Aro, M. (2026). Dynamics of change in math self-efficacy, sources of self-efficacy, and math skills. Journal of Educational Psychology, 118(2), 209-223. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001006

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Bolck–Croon–Hagenaars analyses with Wald tests and Cohen's d compared gender composition across the four self-efficacy trajectories. The Low Stable trajectory had 67.1% girls versus 45.0% and 45.1% in the high-initial trajectories, with d = 0.90 and d = 0.86.

> "The proportion of girls in the Low Stable SE trajectory (67.1% girls) was higher than in the profiles characterized by high initial SE trajectories (High Stable SE, 45.0% girls, d = 0.90; Decreasing SE, 45.1% girls, d = 0.86)."
