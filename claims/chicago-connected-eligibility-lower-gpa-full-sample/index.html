Schachner 2025¶

Schachner, J.N., Marwell, N.P., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J.A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2025). How did Chicago Connected broadband access eligibility and participation affect remote learning engagement and achievement during the COVID-19 pandemic? Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1153

q2 · i? · associational · r3

OLS models predicting fall 2020 GPA as a function of eligibility in the full analytic sample of 77,056 students with controls. The estimate was "a slightly lower fall 2020 GPA among the full analytic sample" of -0.05 GPA points, uniform across pre-pandemic performance levels.

"Program eligibility was associated with a slightly lower fall 2020 GPA among the full analytic sample, but the magnitude (-0.05 GPA points) did not vary by students' pre-pandemic academic performance."
