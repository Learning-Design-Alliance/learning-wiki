# All-on-site training in adult nursing and home health nursing was associated with increased subjective attainment of many nursing skills during the pandemic

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All-on-site adult nursing showed increased subjective attainment for seven of 21 skills (regression coefficients 0.655–1.265, p < 0.05). [→ Oriyama Sanae 2025](#oriyama-sanae-2025)
`q3 i?` All-on-site home health nursing showed increased attainment for four of 21 skills (regression coefficients 0.838–1.290, p < 0.05). [→ Oriyama Sanae 2025 (2)](#oriyama-sanae-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Oriyama Sanae 2025

Oriyama Sanae. (2025). Influence of the COVID-19 pandemic on nursing skills experience and subjective attainment among final-year nursing students in Japan: a questionnaire-based multicenter cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07832-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression on the during-pandemic sample (n=303) with subjective attainment of each skill as response variable and age, gender, and clinical practice experience as explanatory variables. "The most closely related area to on-site attainment was adult nursing" with seven skills significant.

> "The most closely related area to on-site attainment was adult nursing, with seven skills—"Management of indwelling bladder catheter", "Transportation by stretcher", "Washing patient’s hair on the bed", Hot footbath", "Monitoring the patient’s bathing", "Intravenous infusion management", and "Administration of oxygen therapy"—showing increased subjective attainment (regression coefficients: 0.655–1.265, p < 0.05)"

### Oriyama Sanae 2025 (2)

Oriyama Sanae. (2025). Influence of the COVID-19 pandemic on nursing skills experience and subjective attainment among final-year nursing students in Japan: a questionnaire-based multicenter cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07832-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same logistic regression analysis of the pandemic cohort (n=303): all-on-site home health nursing showed "increased attainment for four of the 21 skills learned entirely on-site" with printed coefficients and p < 0.05.

> "home health attainment showed increased attainment for four of the 21 skills learned entirely on-site—"Assistance with urinal on the bed", "Change hospital gowns for patients without infusion lines", "Intravenous infusion management", and "Making a cold compress" (regression coefficients: 0.838–1.290, p < 0.05)"
