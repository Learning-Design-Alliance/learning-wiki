q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of stated intentions against enacted AI-coded behaviors in the 98-student classroom study. The article reports r=.33, p<.01 for both contrasts, the only significant intention-enactment alignment found.

"students whoselected“justgivemethefinal answers” showed higher executive REQUEST rates (46.4%vs.19.2%,r=.33, p < .01) and higher answer-seeking content (39.9%vs.16.0%,r=.33,p < .01)"

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same correlational intent-enactment analysis, reporting null associations for learning-oriented intentions with no printed effect sizes beyond the correlation bounds; the article concludes students could not reliably translate learning-oriented goals into aligned strategies.

"Common learning-oriented intentions – concept explanations, verification, step- by-step support – did not significantly predict their enacted codes (all|r|< .18, p > .10)"
