## Evidence

### Hassan Khosravi 2019

Hassan Khosravi, Kirsty Kitto, Joseph Jay Williams. (2019). RiPPLE: A Crowdsourced Adaptive Platform for Recommendation of Learning Activities. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.63.12](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.63.12)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pilot in an on-campus relational-databases course; propensity score matching on GPA, age, residency status, and program level matched 198 RiPPLE users to 198 non-users. Matched midterm scores favored RiPPLE users, "d = 0.54, p < .001".

> "Students' exam scores after matching revealed the same pattern, where the students in the experimental group had a statistically significantly better performance (µ = 74% ± 15%) than the students in the control group (µ = 64% ± 21%, d = 0.54, p < .001)."

### Hassan Khosravi 2019 (2)

Hassan Khosravi, Kirsty Kitto, Joseph Jay Williams. (2019). RiPPLE: A Crowdsourced Adaptive Platform for Recommendation of Learning Activities. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.63.12](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.63.12)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of all 215 RiPPLE-group versus 238 non-RiPPLE-group students on the week-7 midterm before matching, using a t-test. The unmatched comparison showed "d = 0.44, p < .001" favoring RiPPLE users.

> "The average exam scores before matching for the students in the experimental group (µ = 73% ± 16%) were significantly higher than those of the students in the control group (µ = 65% ± 20%, d = 0.44, p < .001)."
