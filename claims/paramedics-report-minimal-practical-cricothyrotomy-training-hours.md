`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on recalled training hours among the 70 respondents with five or more years of experience in the 86-paramedic cohort. The authors report "Half of subjects with five or more years of experience had less than one hour of experience (39/70, 56%, 95% CI [44–68%]) per year of practical cricothyrotomy training." Recall-based responses are subject to recall bias, as the article acknowledges.
