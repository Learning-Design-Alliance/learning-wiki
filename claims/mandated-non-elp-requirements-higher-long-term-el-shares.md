Descriptive analysis of the percentage of ELs not proficient within 5 years of first enrollment (a proxy for long-term EL status), shown in Figure 3. The brief reports over one-third of ELs in mandated non-ELP states have not reclassified within 5 years, with "an 8 percentage point difference" versus single-ELP states.

> "Generally, states that mandate non-ELP requirements have higher shares of long-term ELs compared to states that rely exclusively on ELP assessments or those that incorporate non-ELP criteria optionally."
