## Evidence

### Wickliffe C. Abraham 2019

Wickliffe C. Abraham, Owen D. Jones and David L. Glanzman. (2019). Is plasticity of synapses the mechanism of long-term memory storage? npj Science of Learning 4:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0048-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review attributing this optogenetic experiment to Ryan et al. The review reports memory induction succeeded despite protein synthesis inhibition that "eliminated conditioning-induced hippocampal LTP" at potentiated perforant path-dentate gyrus synapses, which the review says supports cell-intrinsic storage.

> "the long-term memory for contextual fear conditioning in mice can be induced by optogenetic stimulation of hippocampal neurons that were active during behavioural training, even though the mice had received post-training injections of a protein synthesis inhibitor, a treatment that caused retrograde amnesia and eliminated conditioning-induced hippocampal LTP"
