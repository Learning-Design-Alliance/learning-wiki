### Putwain 2018

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., Pekrun, R., Becker, S., & Symes, W. (2018). Expectancy of Success, Attainment Value, Engagement, and Achievement: A Moderated Mediation Analysis. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/key-stage-2-tests-2016-mathematics-test-materials

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM path from T4 engagement to T5 National Curriculum Test achievement in the 586-pupil longitudinal sample. The article reports "T5 achievement was predicted by T4 engagement (β = .59, p < .001)"; no separate effect size is printed.

> "T5 achievement was predicted by T4 engagement (β = .59, p < .001) over and above the variance accounted for by T1 achievement (β = .43, p < .001) and T2 engagement (β = .36, p < .001)."
