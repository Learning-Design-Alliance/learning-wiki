## Evidence

### Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2021

Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). Development and Validation of a conceptual survey instrument to evaluate introductory physics students' understanding of thermodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Open-ended bonus questions on a midterm exam in a first-semester algebra-based course before instruction on the laws: of 207 students, 134 (65%) responded. The article reports "only 5% made a statement that includes heat transfer as part of the conservation law" and 15% claimed heat is conserved.

> "for the ﬁrst law question, while about half of the students stated that energy is conserved, e.g.,“Energy cannot be created or destroyed” (52%), only 5% made a statement that includes heat transfer as part of the conservation law."
