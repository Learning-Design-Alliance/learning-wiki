Two-factor variance analysis (Table 4) of tennis-ball throwing results from the 2015-2016 experiment in 30 Aktobe schools. The article reports the method factor's impact made 47.5% and the share of non-regulated factors 52.5%.

"Significant difference in indicators between the gradations of factor "A" (F a1/a2 = 6,68 > F0,05 = 2,04) demonstrates that the gaming method is more favorable for the development of speed-strength qualities than the traditional one."
