## Evidence

### Dolan 2026

Dolan, S., L. Nowell, N. Moules, A. Kaba, and M. Brockway. 2026. “Investigating Healthcare Educators' Interprofessional Socialisation Following an Interprofessional Simulation Facilitator Training Programme: A Mixed Methods Study.” Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 6: 6726–6749. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70267

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Matched pairs t-test analysis with bootstrapping of individual survey items from the same programme evaluation survey. Changes in scores from pre- to post-test were found to be significant in eight out of 11 survey items; for example Item 1 improved from 5.25 to 6.06 (Cohen's d = 0.88).

> "Changes in scores from pre- to post-test were found to be significant in eight out of 11 survey items. Correlation matrices and Kruskal–Wallis H-tests of demographic variables and IS scores (pre, post and change) revealed no significant relationships between co-variates (Data S2)."
