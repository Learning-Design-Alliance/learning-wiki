### Jorge Fábrega 2026

Jorge Fábrega. (2026). Same Performance, Different Process: Epistemic Ownership in AI-Mediated Education. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02731

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Qualitative reconstruction of two within-semester, within-assignment pairs with identical or closely matched scores (Tables 5–7). Pair 2 matched both students on "the same normalized assignment score of 0.8727" and six turns, yet Conversation A showed D+I+E and Conversation B showed Direction only. The article states these comparisons do not establish causal equivalence.

> "Both students worked on F24 Assignment 3, received the same normalized assignment score of 0.8727, and interacted with the AI for six student turns."
