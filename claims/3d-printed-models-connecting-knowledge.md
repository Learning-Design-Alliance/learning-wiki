### Lisk Kristina 2024

Lisk Kristina, Cheung Jeffrey JH. (2024). A case study: exploring the impact of 3D printed models on cognitive integration during clinical skills training. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.78564

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of observations and focus group data. Field notes recorded varied use sequences: one pair palpated immediately during instructor explanation, one positioned the scapula beside their partner, and one reviewed the model for about 30 seconds before attempting techniques. Students said the models helped them "map" their learning.

> "The 3D printed models provided an additional modality that helped learners connect knowledge across different sources of information and different knowledge types."
