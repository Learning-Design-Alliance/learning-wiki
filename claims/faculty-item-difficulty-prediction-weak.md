## Evidence

### Kibble JD 2011

Kibble JD, Johnson T. (2011). Are faculty predictions or item taxonomies useful for estimating the outcome of multiple-choice examinations? Adv Physiol Educ 35: 396–401. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00062.2011

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of 272 examination items from six multiple-choice exams in an undergraduate human physiology course, comparing faculty-assigned difficulty annotations with item P values. The article reports a "statistically significant, but relatively low, correlation" and that faculty members were correct only 48% of the time when estimating difficulty.

> "Overall analysis showed a statistically significant, but relatively low, correlation between the intended item difficulty and actual student scores (ρ = −0.19, P < 0.01)"
