## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The distance between item estimates for adjacent complexity levels always exceeds four logits, so the 95% confidence intervals around adjacent groups do not overlap. [→ Dawson-Tunik 2005](#dawson-tunik-2005)
`q2 i?` The authors interpret these statistically significant gaps as indicating that complexity levels are distinct forms of reasoning. [→ Dawson-Tunik 2005](#dawson-tunik-2005)

## Evidence

### Dawson-Tunik 2005

Dawson-Tunik, T. L., Commons, M., Wilson, M., & Fischer, K. W. (2005). The shape of development. European Journal of Developmental Psychology, 2(2), 163–195. https://doi.org/10.1080/17405620544000011

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item analysis of the Rasch rating scale solution on the 747-interview sample; the mean standard error for item estimates was .22 logits, so differences over .88 logits are significant at p = .05. "there is no overlap in 95% conﬁdence intervals around groups of item level estimates."

> "The distance between estimates for adjacent complexity levels is always greater than four logits—thus there is no overlap in 95% conﬁdence intervals around groups of item level estimates."
