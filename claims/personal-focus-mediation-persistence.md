# Personal focus in students' essays partially mediated the UVI x confidence effect on persistence, whereas engagement did not mediate direct persistence effects

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The UVI increased personal focus (z = 28.09, b = 0.77, p < .001), and personal focus partially mediated the UVI x confidence effects on enrollment (z = 2.42, p = .02) and on biomedical major through enrollment (z = 2.39, p = .02). [→ Hecht 2019](#hecht-2019)
`q3 i?` Engagement (essay length) did not mediate the direct effects of the UVI on persistence (ps > .13). [→ Hecht 2019 (2)](#hecht-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Hecht 2019

Hecht, C. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2019). Promoting Persistence in the Biological and Medical Sciences: An Expectancy-Value Approach to Intervention. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000356

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Moderated mediation models on the randomized trial data tested linguistic mediators. Personal focus (first- and second-person pronoun proportion via LIWC) showed a significant personal focus x confidence interaction on enrollment (z = 2.43, b = 0.33, p = .02) and partially mediated the persistence effects.

> "Indeed, personal focus partially mediated the UVI x confidence effects on enrollment (z = 2.42, p = .02) and the UVI x confidence effect on biomedical major, through enrollment (z = 2.39, p = .02)."

### Hecht 2019 (2)

Hecht, C. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2019). Promoting Persistence in the Biological and Medical Sciences: An Expectancy-Value Approach to Intervention. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000356

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The same moderated mediation models tested engagement, measured as average word count across the three writing assignments, as a mediator of direct UVI effects on persistence. The article reports no significant mediation (ps > .13).

> "Engagement did not mediate the direct effects on persistence (ps > .13)."
