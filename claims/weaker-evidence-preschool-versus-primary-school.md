`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis comparing evidence across age groups: six preschool studies versus 19 primary school studies. The discussion reports "fewer studies conducted in preschool-aged children compared to older children and the effects were smaller". One community-setting study reported a small effect.

> "However, there were fewer studies conducted in preschool-aged children compared to older children and the effects were smaller, therefore less evidence of effective experiential learning approaches was found for this age group."
