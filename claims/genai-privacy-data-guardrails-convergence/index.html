q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Document analysis of IT and security unit guidance across the 50-institution corpus. The convergence section reports that "IT and security units commonly warn users not to enter sensitive, confidential, proprietary, or regulated data into consumer AI tools," citing examples such as data-classification rules at Michigan and UT Austin.
