# SES moderated change in emotional/motivational regulation, with lower-SES children increasing and higher-SES children slightly decreasing

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` An SES × emotional/motivational regulation interaction (η²p = .06) showed low-SES children increased while higher-SES children displayed a slight decrease between pre- and post-test. [→ Zachariou 2023](#zachariou-2023)

## Evidence
