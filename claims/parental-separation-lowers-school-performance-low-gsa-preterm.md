## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among preterm children with low GSA scores (20th percentile) whose parents separated between 3 and 5 years after birth, school performance at age five decreased by 3.7 points (standard error = 1.54, p = 0.01). [→ Nusinovici Simon 2018](#nusinovici-simon-2018)

## Evidence

### Nusinovici Simon 2018

Nusinovici Simon, Olliac Bertrand, Flamant Cyril, Müller Jean-Baptiste, Olivier Marion, Rouger Valérie, Gascoin Géraldine, Basset Hélène, Bouvard Charlotte, Rozé Jean-Christophe, Hanf Matthieu. (2018). Impact of parental separation or divorce on school performance in preterm children: A population-based study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population-based matched cohort analysis of 3,308 preterm infants from the LIFT cohort, using exact matching and weighted quantile regressions of teacher-rated GSA scores. The article reports "the decrease in performance amounted to 3.7 points (standard error = 1.54, p = 0.01)" for children in the lowest GSA quantile.

> "Parental separations were associated with a lower school performance. This lower performance only occurred for children with a low GSA score (corresponding to the 20th percentile) whose parents had undergone separation when the child was between 3 and 5 years of age. For these children, the decrease in performance amounted to 3.7 points (standard error = 1.54, p = 0.01)"
