Post-task Intrinsic Motivation Inventory comparison across the four experimental groups in the ESL writing study found no significant differences on Interest/Enjoyment, Perceived Competence, Effort/Importance, or Pressure/Tension.

"No significant between-group differences were observed for Interest/Enjoyment (𝐹= 1.087,𝑝= 0.358,𝜂2 = 0.029), Perceived Competence (𝐹= 0.453,𝑝= 0.716,𝜂 2 = 0.012), Effort/Importance (𝐹= 1.152,𝑝= 0.332,𝜂 2 = 0.030), or Pressure/Tension (𝐹= 0.546,𝑝= 0.652,𝜂 2 = 0.015)."
