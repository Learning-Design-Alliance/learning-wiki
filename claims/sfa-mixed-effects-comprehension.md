## Evidence

### Success for All® 2017

Success for All®. (2017). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/672

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC comprehension synthesis covered eight studies including 9,733 students in 143 schools. Madden et al. (1993) showed a significant positive effect for low-quartile second graders; Ross et al. (1998) showed a negative and substantively important effect size; six studies were indeterminate.

> "In the eight studies that reported findings, the estimated impact of the intervention on outcomes in the comprehension domain was positive and statistically significant for one study, negative and substantively important for one study, and indeterminate for six studies."
