## Evidence

### Gehrke-Beck Sabine 2025

Gehrke-Beck Sabine, Holzhausen Ylva. (2025). Entrustment of student supervision to GP trainees - a qualitative interview study of factors influencing entrustment decisions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07870-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on context factors. GPs withheld students from stressed trainees during rushes; a GP called unstructured after-hours teaching his "absolute negative example"; a trainee said "there has to be explicit space for it"; availability of students, teachers and suitable patients also shaped delegation.

> "In the GP practice setting, workload and availability of protected teaching time are cited as factors that influence entrustment."
