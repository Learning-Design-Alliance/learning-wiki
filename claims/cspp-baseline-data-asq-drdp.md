## Evidence

### Burr 2023

Burr, E., Burho, J., & Perez, A. (2023). Pre-Referral Processes in California State Preschool Programs: How Practitioners Decide to Refer Multilingual Children for Special Education Evaluation. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655866

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of CSPP coordinators. The DRDP, a formative assessment using classroom observations, is administered twice per year for every child per CSPP requirements; families also complete the ASQ and ASQ:SE-2 screeners.

> "Across the board, interviewees described how educators regularly access baseline data from the ASQ and DRDP. Educators might use these data to determine whether a child could benefit from additional instructional support."
