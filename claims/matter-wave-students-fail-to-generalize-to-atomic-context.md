# Students taught delocalized wave descriptions in one context do not generalize to contexts where interpretation is not addressed

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students from the Matter-Wave course overwhelmingly preferred a wave-packet description of electrons in the double-slit experiment but did not generalize this to atomic electrons, with a majority still agreeing that atomic electrons exist as localized particles. [→ Baily 2010](#baily-2010)

## Evidence

### Baily 2010

Baily, C., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2010). Interpretation in Quantum Physics as Hidden Curriculum. AIP Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515251](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-instruction survey data from Instructor B's Matter-Wave course. Students were explicitly taught that electrons propagate as delocalized waves through both slits, but "these students did not seem to generalize this notion" to the atomic electron context.

> "these students did not seem to generalize this notion of particles as delocalized waves in the double-slit experiment to the context of atoms, with a majority still agreeing that atomic electrons exist as localized particles"
