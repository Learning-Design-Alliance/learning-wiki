## Evidence

### Anan Schütt 2024

Anan Schütt, Tobias Huber, Jauwairia Nasir, Cristina Conati, Elisabeth André. (2024). Does Difficulty even Matter? Investigating Difficulty Adjustment and Practice Behavior in an Open-Ended Learning Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636876](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636876)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-condition comparison in the online user study (86 participants after filtering) using Kruskal-Wallis H-test on normalized learning gain. The article reports "no significant difference for learning gain" with H(2) = 1.31, p = 0.52, and an estimated η² of -0.008.

> "Here we observe that there is no significant difference for learning gain (KW H-test, H(2) = 1.31, p = 0.52, 𝜂2 = -0.008)"

### Anan Schütt 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same between-condition analysis for the subjective measures: flow via ANOVA and perceived difficulty via Kruskal-Wallis H-test. The article reports no significant difference for either measure, with the printed statistics quoted above.
