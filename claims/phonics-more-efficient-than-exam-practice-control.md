## Evidence

### Robert Alexander Graham Coates 2017

Robert Alexander Graham Coates, Judith Gorham, and Richard Nicholas. (2017). The Efficacy of Phonics-Based Instruction of English as a Second Language in an Italian High School: A Randomised Controlled Trial. Gist Education and Learning Research Journal, No. 15, pp. 29-67. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1692-5777

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-factor ANOVA comparing cohort improvements across orthography, pronunciation and interview. The article notes the control cohort, emphasising exam practice, actually had more exam practise, which was not reflected in their final scores."all areas improved more in the trial"

> "According to the ANOV A tests, all areas improved more in the trial (overall results for orthography ANOV A F value 4.21, pronunciation, 20.93 and interview, 5.59) than the control, and were statistically significant for the long vowel and digraph categories."
