# Students found the GoCPS application platform logistically easy to use, but applications to schools with additional requirements caused difficulties

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Almost all students who interacted with the GoCPS platform felt the application process for schools without additional requirements was easy to understand and complete. [→ Andria Shyjka 2021](#andria-shyjka-2021)
`q2 i?` Applications to schools or programs with extra requirements (essays, teacher recommendations, selective enrollment testing) caused confusion and, for some students, incomplete applications at the deadline. [→ Andria Shyjka 2021 (2)](#andria-shyjka-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Andria Shyjka 2021

Andria Shyjka. (2021). Student Experience with the High School Choice Process in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago Consortium Research Brief, July 2021. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-experience-with-the-high-school-choice-process-in-Chicago-Public-Schools

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Application Process section finding from the focus groups: students appreciated navigating the platform from a computer or phone, most had counselor or family support, and three students handled the whole process independently.

> "Almost all the students we spoke to interacted directly with the GoCPS platform and felt the application process for schools without additional application requirements was easy to understand and complete."

### Andria Shyjka 2021 (2)

Andria Shyjka. (2021). Student Experience with the High School Choice Process in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago Consortium Research Brief, July 2021. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-experience-with-the-high-school-choice-process-in-Chicago-Public-Schools

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same study: students noted confusion about differing requirements for essays, teacher recommendations, and selective enrollment testing; one student was still missing a teacher recommendation signature at the deadline.

> "For several students applying to these schools, the more demanding application process was challenging and required additional school or parental support or resulted in an incomplete application at the deadline."
