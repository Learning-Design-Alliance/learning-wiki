## Evidence

### Sakaki 2025

Sakaki, M., Murayama, K., Frenzel, A. C., Goetz, T., Marsh, H. W., Lichtenfeld, S., Wünsch, M., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Parents' academic expectations and aspirations predict students' achievement emotions. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000959

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Orthogonal-transformation dynamic panel models on the PALMA waves included over-aspiration (the within-person difference between aspiration and expectation) plus the expectation–aspiration mean; students whose parents held over-aspirations reported 'reduced levels of enjoyment and pride, as well as higher levels of anger, anxiety, shame, and hopelessness'.

> "In addition, when parents' held over-aspirations, students reported reduced levels of enjoyment and pride, as well as higher levels of anger, anxiety, shame, and hopelessness."

### Sakaki 2025 (2)

Sakaki, M., Murayama, K., Frenzel, A. C., Goetz, T., Marsh, H. W., Lichtenfeld, S., Wünsch, M., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Parents' academic expectations and aspirations predict students' achievement emotions. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000959

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness check adding same-year end-of-year math grade as a covariate to the over-aspiration models; over-aspiration remained 'significantly predictive of anxiety and pride'.

> "In our subsequent analysis, over-aspiration was significantly predictive of anxiety and pride even when the school grade from the same year was used as a covariate (Table S14)."
