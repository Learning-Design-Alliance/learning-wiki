Evidence¶

Anna Keune 2022¶

Anna Keune (2022). Material syntonicity: Examining computational performance and its materiality through weaving and sewing crafts. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2022.2100704

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Video assemblage analysis of the fabric manipulation unit. Thirteen of sixteen students implemented personal variations of the twisted square; stitches and knots distorted the fabric matrix into 3D shapes, and feeling then sewing anticipated knots and stitches produced "speculative 3D modeling in physical space," as in Aki's windmill pattern.

"Analyzing student-fabric intra-actions over time highlighted computation in fabric manipulation as speculative 3D modeling in physical space. All but three students (n = 13) implemented personal variations of the twisted square, such as creating larger squares, changing the positional arrangement of squares, or sewing new shapes."
