## Evidence

### Felten Renaud 2025

Felten Renaud, Bigaut Kevin, Wirth Thomas, Kremer Laurent, Gauer Lucas, Arnold Cécile, Ollivier Irène, Godet Julien, Scherlinger Marc, Dubois Maxime, Sebbag Eden, De Sèze Jerome, Gottenberg Jacques-Eric. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Patient-reported secondary outcome on 0–10 Likert scales in the randomized trial: perception of procedure duration significantly better in the simulation group (p = 0.043); feeling-at-ease median 10.0 vs 8.0 was a tendency (p = 0.16). No effect size printed.

> "The overall median score for the patient’s perception of the procedure duration was 6.0 (IQR 4.5–8.5), with a significantly better score for the simulation group scoring of 6.0 (IQR 5.0-9.3) compared to the control group scoring 5.0 (IQR 4.0–6.0) (p = 0.043)."
