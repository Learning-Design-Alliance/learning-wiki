# The four cognitive measures form four distinct but significantly correlated latent factors whose relations are stable across the two age groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The four-factor cognitive model fit well and fit better than one-, two-, and three-factor alternatives. [→ Christopher 2012](#christopher-2012)
`q3 i?` Cognitive factor relations did not vary across age groups (Δχ2(15) = 17.75, p = .28). [→ Christopher 2012 (2)](#christopher-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Christopher 2012

Christopher, M. E., Miyake, A., Keenan, J. M., Pennington, B., DeFries, J. C., Wadsworth, S. J., Willcutt, E., & Olson, R. K. (2012). Predicting Word Reading and Comprehension with Executive Function and Speed Measures Across Development: A Latent Variable Analysis. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3360115/

`q3 · i?`

Confirmatory factor analysis of working memory, inhibition, processing speed, and naming speed measures. The article reports "The high CFI value (.99), low RMSEA value (.02), and nonsignificant χ2 ([83] = 94.24, p = .18)" and that the four-factor model had the best fit among alternatives.

> "The high CFI value (.99), low RMSEA value (.02), and nonsignificant χ2 ([83] = 94.24, p = .18) show that the four-factor model fit the data well."

### Christopher 2012 (2)

Christopher, M. E., Miyake, A., Keenan, J. M., Pennington, B., DeFries, J. C., Wadsworth, S. J., Willcutt, E., & Olson, R. K. (2012). Predicting Word Reading and Comprehension with Executive Function and Speed Measures Across Development: A Latent Variable Analysis. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3360115/

`q3 · i?`

Multi-group invariance test of the cognitive CFA across ages 8 to 10 and 11 to 16. The article reports the constrained model "was not significant, Δχ2(15) = 17.75, p = .28," supporting stable cognitive factor relations across ages.

> "The chi-square difference between the baseline model and the model with all parameters constrained equal was not significant, Δχ2(15) = 17.75, p = .28."
