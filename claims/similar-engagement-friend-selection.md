## Evidence

### Huiyoung Shin 2022

Huiyoung Shin. (2022). Social contagion of academic behavior: Comparing social networks of close friends and admired peers. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal SAOM analysis of classroom friend networks across two waves (N = 542) found a significant similar-engagement selection effect, with the article reporting "OR = 1.30, p < .05" for initiating friendships with academically similar peers.

> "Regarding relationship selection, adolescents were more likely to initiate friendships with peers who had similar levels of academic engagement (OR = 1.30, p < .05)."
