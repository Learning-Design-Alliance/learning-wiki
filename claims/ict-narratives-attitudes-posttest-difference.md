## Evidence

### Fatma Taşkin Ekici 2015

Fatma Taşkin Ekici, Sultan Pekmezci. (2015). Using ICT-Supported Narratives in Teaching Science and their Effects on Middle School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077655

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of post-test attitudes towards science (control n=21, experimental n=23) on the 30-item Benli (2010) scale; Table 4 prints post-test mean ranks of 14.36 versus 29.93, z=-4.020, p=.000. The article reports the effect size "abs(r) = .32", i.e. r = .32, a small-to-medium effect by its cited convention.

> "The distributions of attitude scores between experimental and control group were found to differ significantly, z = -2.140, p = .032, abs(r) = .32"

### Fatma Taşkin Ekici 2015 (2)

Fatma Taşkin Ekici, Sultan Pekmezci. (2015). Using ICT-Supported Narratives in Teaching Science and their Effects on Middle School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077655

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of pre-test attitudes between the same groups; mean ranks were 19.19 (control) versus 25.52 (experimental), with the article reporting the effect size "abs(r) = .25", i.e. r = .25, and no significant difference, establishing baseline equivalence on this measure.

> "the two distributions of ratings were not found to differ significantly, z = -1.635, p > .05, abs(r) = .25"
