## Evidence

### Murray 2020

Murray, D.; Boothby, C.; Zhao, H.; Minik, V.; Bérubé, N.; Larivière, V.; Sugimoto, C. R. (2020). Exploring the personal and professional factors associated with student evaluations of tenure-track faculty. PLoS ONE 15(6): e0233515. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233515

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Observational regression on the matched dataset; accent mention was treated as a proxy for non-native English speakers who may encounter bias and stereotyping in student evaluations.

> "Faculty who were mentioned as having an accent in a comment left on their RMP2018 profile were associated with 0.17 point lower ratings of overall quality than those for whom an accent was not mentioned (β = −0.17, 95% CI = [−0.20, −0.14])."

### Murray 2020 (2)

Murray, D.; Boothby, C.; Zhao, H.; Minik, V.; Bérubé, N.; Larivière, V.; Sugimoto, C. R. (2020). Exploring the personal and professional factors associated with student evaluations of tenure-track faculty. PLoS ONE 15(6): e0233515. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233515

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same regression; TA mention served as the authors' self-described "highly flawed" proxy for teaching large courses, consistent with past findings of a small negative class-size effect.

> "Faculty for whom a comment on RMP2018 mentioned a teaching assistant were associated with 0.18 point lower ratings of overall quality (β = −0.18, 95% CI = [−0.22, −0.15])."
