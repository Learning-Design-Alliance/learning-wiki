### Jianhao Shen 2024

Jianhao Shen, Ye Yuan, Srbuhi Mirzoyan, Ming Zhang, Chenguang Wang. (2024). MEASURING VISION-LANGUAGE STEM SKILLS OF NEURAL MODELS. Published as a conference paper at ICLR 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.17205](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.17205)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmark evaluation of zero-shot foundation models (CLIP, GPT-3.5-Turbo) on the STEM test sets under the IXL SmartScore exam simulation. The models score "averaging 54.7%" below elementary students, and GPT-3.5-Turbo reaches 49.0% average accuracy versus the human accuracy reference of 83.0 from seven university students.

> "While these models are able to advance the model performance compared to the near random-chance performance of previous neural models, they still drop the performance by averaging 54.7% compared to that of average elementary students. For example, the models are only capable of understanding 2.5% third-grade skills."
