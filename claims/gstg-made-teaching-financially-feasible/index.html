Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2024¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Findings from interviews and focus groups with grant recipients conducted for this report. One recipient recalled having only $5 in his bank account and stated he would not have continued his teaching career without the grant.

"Grant recipients reported that the scholarship made the teaching profession a more financially feasible option and that they otherwise would not have been able to pursue teaching."

Carver-Thomas 2024 (2)¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Self-reported intentions from the report's grant recipient interviews; the report presents these as stated plans rather than measured retention outcomes.

"Additionally, several grant recipients noted that they planned to remain teaching in a high-need school for longer to meet their service requirements."
