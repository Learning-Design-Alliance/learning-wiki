## Evidence

### Robles M. J. 2019

Robles M. J., Miralles Ramón, Esperanza Ascension, Riera Mercedes. (2019). Different ways to present clinical cases in a classroom: video projection versus live representation of a simulated clinical scene with actors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1494-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 131 physiotherapy and medicine students in a delirium seminar; paired comparisons within each presentation-format group. The article reports "a large Effect Size (ES > 0.80)" for both groups, with printed effect sizes of 1.97 and 1.98 on theoretical knowledge.

> "Students in both groups (live scene and video) significantly improved in all questionnaire scores after the seminar (p = 0.001) with a large Effect Size (ES > 0.80)."
