# Growth-score differences between CBE and COLO are small, exceeding 0.2 only in Reading Grades K–1 (favoring CBE) and Mathematics Grade 8 (favoring COLO)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Hedges' g effect sizes for winter-minus-fall RIT growth between engines exceed 0.2 in absolute value only for Reading Grades K and 1 (CBE higher) and Mathematics Grade 8 (COLO higher), all small by Cohen's guidelines. [→ Bo 2020](#bo-2020)

## Evidence

### Bo 2020

Bo, E., & Meyer, P. (2020). Comparability of MAP Growth tests administered through different technology and psychometric infrastructure: An engine evaluation study based on empirical data. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/comparability-of-map-growth-tests-administered-through-different-technology-and-psychometric-infrastructure-an-engine-evaluation-study-based-on-empirical-data/

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive effect-size analysis of Fall 2019 and Winter 2020 MAP Growth RIT records from 171,093 Nebraska students in 803 schools, comparing matched CBE and COLO school groups. Growth scores were computed as winter minus fall RIT; the article reports "the Altair effects are small according to Cohen’s guidelines" in the three grades noted.

> "As shown in the last column, only Grades K–1 have the absolute effect size values above 0.2 in Reading. In Mathematics, only Grade 8 has the absolute effect size value above 0.2. In all three cases (i.e., Reading Grade K, Reading Grade 1, and Mathematics Grade 8), the Altair effects are small according to Cohen’s guidelines."
