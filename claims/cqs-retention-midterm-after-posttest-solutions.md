## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with quantum measurement of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up analysis in in-person class 2 (N = 28, Table IV) of midterm performance about two weeks after the post-test, after post-test solutions were posted on the course learning management system, showing "correctness was nearly 100% for each" of six matched questions.

> "We find that students performed very well on similar questions that were asked two weeks later on the midterm examination of the second in-person class. The post-test questions that had very similar analogues on the midterm exam were 2a, 2b, 2c, 3, 4a, and 4b. On these questions, correctness was nearly 100% for each"
