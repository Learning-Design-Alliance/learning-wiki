# Both metacognitive awareness dimensions correlate positively with deep approach and organized studying and negatively with an unreflective approach

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Knowledge about cognition and regulation of cognition each correlate positively with deep approach (r = .527 and r = .552) and organized studying (r = .434 and r = .478), and negatively with unreflective approach (r = -.541 and r = -.223). [→ Tuononen 2023](#tuononen-2023)

## Evidence

### Tuononen 2023

Tuononen, T., Hyytinen, H., Räisänen, M., Hailikari, T., & Parpala, A. (2023). Metacognitive awareness in relation to university students' learning profiles. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 37–54. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09314-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson's correlation analysis (Table 2) in the survey of 462 students, correlations significant at the 0.001 level: knowledge about cognition with deep approach r = .527, regulation of cognition with deep approach r = .552, knowledge about cognition with unreflective approach r = -.541, and with organized studying r = .434 and r = .478.
