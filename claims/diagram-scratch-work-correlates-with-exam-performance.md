## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2015

Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Impact of guided reflection with peers on the development of effective problem solving strategies and physics learning. [doi:10.1119/1.4947159](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4947159)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis over all students in both recitation groups on the 40-question multiple-choice final exam, where no partial credit was given for showing work. The correlation is reported as significant with no coefficient printed; the correlation was somewhat higher for the PR group.

> "There was a significant positive correlation between how often students did scr atch work or drew diagrams and how well they performed on the final exam regardless of whether they were in the traditional group or the PR group"
