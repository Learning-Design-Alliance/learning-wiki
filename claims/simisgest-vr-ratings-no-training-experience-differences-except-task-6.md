### Alvarez-Lopez F 2020

Alvarez-Lopez F, Maina MF, Saigí-Rubió F. (2020). Use of a Low-Cost Portable 3D Virtual Reality Gesture-Mediated Simulator for Training and Learning Basic Psychomotor Skills in Minimally Invasive Surgery: Development and Content Validity Study. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert medians were compared across training levels (residents, practicing surgeons, other) and experience levels using the Kruskal-Wallis test with significance set at <0.05; the article reports no significant differences across these groupings.

> "Tables 4 and 5 show that there were no significant differences in the different ratings when the level of training (Table 4) or experience (Table 5) was considered."

### Alvarez-Lopez F 2020 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In the task-level analysis by training level (Table 4), Task 6 showed a significant difference (P=.02), with lower scores among less-trained participants; all other task comparisons were non-significant.

> "For Task 6 (Table 4), a lower score was assigned by individuals with lower levels of training (P=.02)."
