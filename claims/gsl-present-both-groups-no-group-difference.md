## Evidence

### Olivo 2024

Olivo, G., Persson, J. & Hedenius, M. (2024). Exploring brain plasticity in developmental dyslexia through implicit sequence learning. npj Science of Learning, 9:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00250-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioral analysis of RT change on the SRT task across the two sessions in 17 DD and 18 TD children; GSL was defined as the sequence-independent RT decrease normalized by average RT. Only p values are printed, no effect size.

> "GSL was observed across both groups in the form of a signiﬁcant reduction in reaction times from day 1 to day 2 (p < 0.001), but there were no group differences in GSL (p = 0.12)."
