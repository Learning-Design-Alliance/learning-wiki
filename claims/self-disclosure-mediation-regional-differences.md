### Huinan Hu 2026

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Region-specific bootstrapped indirect effects (Table 3) show the self-disclosure mediation pattern holds in HK but not the US; Table 3 additionally prints a significant positive US indirect path via maladaptive coping to bullying (β = 0.02, p < 0.05).

> "The mediating effects of both adaptive coping and maladaptive coping between self-disclosure and prosocial behaviors were significant in HK adolescents but not significant in US adolescents (AC: βHK = 0.25, p < 0.001; βUS = 0.02, p > 0.05; MC: βHK = 0.02, p < 0.05; βUS = -0.01, p > 0.05)."
