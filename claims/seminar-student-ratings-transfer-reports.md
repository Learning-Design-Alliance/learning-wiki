### Simon Cullen 2018

Simon Cullen, Judith Fan, Eva van der Brugge and Adam Elga (2018). Improving analytical reasoning and argument understanding: a quasi-experimental field study of argument visualization. npj Science of Learning 3:21. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0038-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report from anonymous end-of-semester surveys across seminar iterations, which also reported an overall course rating of 4.9/5 and a fourfold increase in philosophy-major intention in the seminar group.

> "Students strongly agreed that the seminar improved their ability to construct and evaluate written arguments (4.9/5, SD = 0.36), to read and understand academic articles (4.9/5, SD = 0.36), and that their new skills would help them in other coursework (4.2/5, SD = 0.75)"
