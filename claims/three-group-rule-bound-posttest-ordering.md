# On the rule-bound posttest, focused outperformed both unfocused and control groups, and unfocused outperformed control

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-way ANOVA on rule-bound posttest accuracy showed significant differences among focused, unfocused, and control groups (F = 102.25, p = .00), with post hocs showing focused > unfocused > control. [→ Alavinia 2018](#alavinia-2018)

## Evidence

### Alavinia 2018

Alavinia, P., Shafaei, A., & Salimi, A. (2018). The Effects of Focused/Unfocused Audio-Appended Reading Tasks on Intermediate Female EFL Learners' Written Accuracy. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 203-220. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11214a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA (Table 13: F = 102.25, Sig. 0.00) with LSD post hoc comparisons (Table 14: focused-unfocused 0.36*, focused-control 0.44*, unfocused-control 0.07*) on rule-bound posttest accuracy, n = 15 per group.

> "the learners of focused rule-bound group produced more accurate language than the learners of unfocused and control rule -bound groups on rendering task post -test. Also, it must be noted that the participants of unfocused rule -bound group outperformed the participants of the control rule-bound group"
