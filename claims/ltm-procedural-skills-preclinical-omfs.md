## Evidence

### Lin Yue 2025

Lin Yue, Wen Zhe, Li Yuqi, Wei Xiaosong, Zhang Jiafen, Yan Tinglin, Bao Zhexuan. (2025). Video-enhanced training for entrustable professional activities in preclinical oral surgery: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08368-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 36 fifth-year students in a preclinical transitional course were randomly assigned to live-video or traditional teaching; an ANCOVA controlling pre-PTC scores found the "procedural skills assessment scores... showed a significant difference" between the named groups.

> "The experimental group also demonstrated superior procedural skill test scores (89.49 ± 0.73 vs. 79.48 ± 0.73, p < 0.001)"
