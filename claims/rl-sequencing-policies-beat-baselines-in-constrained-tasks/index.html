Reinforcement-learning sequencing policies beat baseline sequencing in about half of controlled studies, mostly in constrained tasks and against weak baselines

A review of 41 controlled studies found reinforcement-learning-induced instructional sequencing policies significantly outperformed all baselines in 21, with success concentrated in paired-associate, concept-learning and activity-type sequencing and none for sequencing interdependent content.
