### Eugenio Tufino 2026

Eugenio Tufino, Paola Damiani. (2026). Embodied inquiry with AI as facilitator: an exploratory case study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21349

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the Pair C session, the Gem supplied unmeasured masses after student pressure; students then reported their own values (355 g and 341 g) within the AI-generated record. The authors note all figures are as reported by students in AI-generated text.

> "With Pair C it gave way: pressed by the students to “just tell us” which can weighed more, the Gem produced mass values for the two cans (384 g and 349 g) that no one had measured, effectively inventing an observation"
