# Calibration accuracy improvements on French did not transfer to German and Math courses without the intervention, arguing against a maturation explanation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Calibration accuracy on German and Math exams did not change statistically detectably from pre-test to post-test, and intervention group did not impact this. [→ Nederhand 2020](#nederhand-2020)

## Evidence

### Nederhand 2020

Nederhand, M. L., Tabbers, H. K., Jongerling, J., & Rikers, R. M. J. P. (2020). Reflection on exam grades to improve calibration of secondary school students: a longitudinal study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09233-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test/post-test comparison in two non-intervention courses (German and Math) administered to all intervention groups at the beginning and end of the intervention period, analyzed with two-level models. The article reports no statistically detectable change, "all t’s<2".

> "Results showed that calibration accuracy on the German and Math exams did not change statistically detectable from pre-test to post-test (pretest: MGer =1 . 1 5 ,SDGer = 0.77; MMath = 1.25, SDMath = 0.96; post-test: MGer =1 . 0 3 ,SDGer = 0.80; MMath =1 . 1 9 , SDMath = 0.89), all t’s<2 ."
