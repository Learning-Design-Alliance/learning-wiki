## Evidence

### Chaumont Corentin 2024

Chaumont Corentin, Morgat Charles, Ollitrault Pierre, Brejoux Celia, Extramiana Fabrice, Milliez Paul, Savoure Arnaud, Al Hamoud Raphaël, Eltchaninoff Hélène, Anselme Frederic. (2024). How to improve medical students’ ECG interpretation skills ? Multicenter survey and results of a comparative study evaluating a new educational approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05929-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional electronic survey of 401 fourth- to sixth-year students at three French medical schools using 1–5 Likert scales. The survey found "a median of 2, IQR 1–3 (p < 0.0001)" for emergency-situation confidence, significantly lower than routine confidence.

> "students reported an overall confidence level of 3 (IQR 2–3) in ECG interpretation during routine medical situations. Confidence in emergency situations was significantly lower as compared to routine medical situations with a median of 2, IQR 1–3 (p < 0.0001)."

### Chaumont Corentin 2024 (2)

Chaumont Corentin, Morgat Charles, Ollitrault Pierre, Brejoux Celia, Extramiana Fabrice, Milliez Paul, Savoure Arnaud, Al Hamoud Raphaël, Eltchaninoff Hélène, Anselme Frederic. (2024). How to improve medical students’ ECG interpretation skills ? Multicenter survey and results of a comparative study evaluating a new educational approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05929-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same survey of 401 students; satisfaction with ECG teaching was rated "a median rating of 2, IQR 1–3", and 386/401 (96.3%) advocated more extensive ECG training.

> "Satisfaction with ECG teaching during the early years of medical training was generally low with a median rating of 2, IQR 1–3."
