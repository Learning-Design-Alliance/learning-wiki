Survey finding on prerequisites: "thirty institutions still making this requirement" of general psychology. Quoted professors (Colvin, Kohl, Henmon, Chase) express mixed views, and the author argues that with the trend of functionalism or behaviorism, prerequisites in biology are more essential than general psychology.
