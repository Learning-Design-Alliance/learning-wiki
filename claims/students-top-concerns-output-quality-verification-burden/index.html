Kudina 2025¶

Kudina, O. (2025). Using AI in engineering education: a balancing act, driven by clear purpose. Draft version, to appear in The Routledge Handbook of the Philosophy of Engineering, 2nd ed. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16626 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.16626

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Questionnaire analysis of problematic aspects, summarized in Table 3 with counts: quality of output (53), verification burden (34), ethical concerns (19), impairing learning (18). Students reported that LLMs "produce incorrect, fabricated, or misleading information with confidence" and that checking is time-consuming.

"The summary of problematic aspects of students’ use of LLMs in education, as presented in Table 3, identifies questioning the quality of output and the need for constant evaluation as the top two concerns."
