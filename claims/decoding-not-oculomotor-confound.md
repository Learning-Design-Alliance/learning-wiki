## Evidence

### Guoyang Liu 2025

Guoyang Liu, Yueyuan Zheng, Michelle Hei Lam Tsang, Yazhou Zhao & Janet H. Hsiao. (2025). Understanding the role of eye movement pattern and consistency during face recognition through EEG decoding. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00316-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Additional control analyses (Supplementary Notes 1–5) addressed potential ocular-artifact confounds: decoding from raw eye movement data and fixation-based recurrent neural network classification "yielded chance-level performance", approximated vEOG/hEOG failed to decode, and no significant old/new differences appeared in average fixations per trial or saccade length.

> "neither raw eye movement data nor eye ﬁxation data contained sufﬁcient discriminative information for old/new face recognition."
