## Evidence

### Zou Ya 2026

Zou Ya, Xie Wenzhao, Huang Hui, Liu Dan, Zhou Qing, Yi Qifeng, Tong Xiaoliang, Mao Ping. (2026). The effects of case-based, problem-based, and team-based learning on nursing students’ problem-solving, self-directed learning, and communication skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08602-3

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by teaching method for problem-solving abilities, reported in Table 4. All three named methods show significant pooled effects, with varying heterogeneity ("I2 = 95%", "0%", "97%").

> "CBL (SMD = 1.36, 95% CI: 0.19–2.52, I2 = 95%, P = 0.02), PBL (SMD = 0.56, 95% CI: 0.35–0.76, I2 = 0%, P<0.001), and TBL (SMD = 1.52, 95% CI: 0.21–2.83, I2 = 97%, P = 0.02) all significantly improved nursing students’ problem-solving abilities"
