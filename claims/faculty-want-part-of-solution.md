Category IV finding from qualitative interviews with five physics faculty. Faculty "recognize that research has some good things to offer them" yet "feel a need to be part of the solution themselves."

> "They recognize that research has some good things to offer them and that researchers have expertise in teaching and learning that could be valuable. Yet, they feel a need to be part of the solution themselves."
