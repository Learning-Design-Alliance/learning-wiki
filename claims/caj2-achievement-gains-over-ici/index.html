Gambari 2016¶

Gambari, I. A. & Yusuf, M. O. (2016). Effects of Computer-Assisted Jigsaw II Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Achievement and Retention. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(4), 352-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117586

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on post-test scores of the quasi-experimental study (n = 80, experimental n = 42, control n = 38) with pre-test as covariate. The article reports "an F (1, 77) = 19.072, p = 0.000" for the treatment main effect, a statistically significant difference favoring Jigsaw II.
