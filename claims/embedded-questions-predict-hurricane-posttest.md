Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

User-log data recorded students' multiple-choice attempts on embedded questions, scored 4 points for a first-try correct answer down to 0 after more than 4 tries. In the HLM the embedded questions coefficient was γ60 = 5.552, p < .001 (Table 3); the association is correlational, not causal.

> "Higher scores on the embedded questions in Hurricane! also significantly predicted posttest scores on the Hurricane! Concept Knowledge posttest (Table 3)."
