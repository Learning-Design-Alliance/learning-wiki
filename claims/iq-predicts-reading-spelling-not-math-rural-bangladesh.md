Factors associated with school achievement of children aged 8–10 years in rural Bangladesh: Findings from a post hoc analysis of a community-based study. PLoS ONE 16(7): e0254693. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254693

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted regression using the culturally adapted WASI-II full-scale IQ in the 372-child cohort. The findings report IQ coefficients for reading and spelling but no significant math association, which the authors call "surprising" and possibly related to the education system or the non-standardized test.
