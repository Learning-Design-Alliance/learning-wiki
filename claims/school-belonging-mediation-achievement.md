# School belonging mediated the relations of parental involvement and individual factors with academic achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` School belonging showed significant mediating effects linking parental involvement and children's individual factors to academic achievement. [→ Seol 2013](#seol-2013)
`q2 i?` The double-mediation indirect effect of parental involvement through individual factors and belonging on achievement was significant, with a standardized indirect coefficient of .21. [→ Seol 2013 (2)](#seol-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Seol 2013

Seol, K. O., & Jung, S. W. (2013). School Belonging as a Mediator of the Relationship Between Individual and Parental Factors and Academic Achievement of Elementary School Students. The Korean Journal of School Psychology, 10(1), 41-58. https://koreapsych.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Path analysis (AMOS) of data from 250 fifth graders testing the model in Figure 1, which showed good fit. The article reports that school belonging "모두 유의미한 매개효과를 갖는 것으로 드러" for both parental and individual factors.

> "부모의 긍정적 학습관여와 아동 개인 요인이
학업 성적을 설명하는데 아동의 학교소속감이
모두 유의미한 매개효과를 갖는 것으로 드러
났다."

### Seol 2013 (2)

Seol, K. O., & Jung, S. W. (2013). School Belonging as a Mediator of the Relationship Between Individual and Parental Factors and Academic Achievement of Elementary School Students. The Korean Journal of School Psychology, 10(1), 41-58. https://koreapsych.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Bootstrapping test of the double-mediation indirect effect in the same 250-child path model. The article reports the "효과 표준화 계수 .21은 p<.001수준에서 유의" result; Sobel tests additionally showed full mediation for parental involvement and partial mediation for individual factors.

> "부스트래핑을 이용해 부모의 긍정적 학습관여
->개인요인->소속감->학업성취의 이중매개모
형의 간접효과의 유의도를 알아본 결과 간접
효과 표준화 계수 .21은 p<.001수준에서 유의
했다."
