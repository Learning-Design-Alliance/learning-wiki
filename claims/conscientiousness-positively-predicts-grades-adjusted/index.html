Evidence¶

Nicolas Hübner 2022¶

Nicolas Hübner, Marion Spengler, Benjamin Nagengast, Lex Borghans, Trudie Schils, and Ulrich Trautwein. (2022). When Academic Achievement (Also) Reflects Personality: Using the Personality-Achievement Saturation Hypothesis (PASH) to Explain Differential Associations Between Achievement Measures and Personality Traits. American Psychological Association. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000571

q3 · i2 · associational · r3

Meta-analytically aggregated result from three upper-secondary studies (TOSCA 2002, TOSCA LAU, TOSCA 2006), multivariate multiple regression Model II with covariates. The article reports a "positive association between conscientiousness and course grades (β = .24, p < .001)" in mathematics.

"we found a positive association between conscientiousness and course grades (β = .24, p < .001) after controlling for the other personality traits and covariates in the model. Students who scored higher on conscientiousness had statistically significantly higher grades."

q3 · i1 · associational · r3

Aggregated adjusted regression results for English across the three studies; the article prints "a statistically s ignificant positive association between conscientiousness and course grades (β = .14, p < .001)" in English.

"The results for English revealed a statistically s ignificant positive association between conscientiousness and course grades (β = .14, p < .001; see Table 5). In line with our hypothesis, we also found a positive regression coefficient for openness (β = .16, p < .001)."
