# An online EBM course integrated with clinical clerkships significantly improved question development, sources of evidence, and search strategies scores among medical students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An online EBM course with six didactic modules run concurrently with clinical clerkships significantly improved pre-post scores for question development (3.73 to 4.13), sources of evidence (3.96 to 4.53), and search strategies (5.07 to 5.86), all P<0.001. [→ Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016](#kyriakoulis-konstantinos-2016)

## Evidence

### Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos, Patelarou Athina, Laliotis Aggelos, Wan Andrew C, Matalliotakis Michail, Tsiou Chrysoula, Patelarou Evridiki. (2016). Educational strategies for teaching evidence-based practice to undergraduate health students: systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5066070/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post evaluation of an online EBM course (six online didactic modules and sessions) run concurrently with clinical clerkships for 3rd-year medical students (n=139) in the US. Table 2 reports "Question development: 3.73 (1.27) vs. 4.13 (1.39), P<0.001" plus significant gains in sources of evidence and search strategies.
