## Evidence

### Richard Océane 2023

Richard Océane, Jollant Fabrice, Billon Grégoire, Attoe Chris, Vodovar Dominique, Piot Marie-Aude. (2023). Simulation training in suicide risk assessment and intervention: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2199469

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 12 RCTs (n = 2,000) assessing attitudes in the review. The pooled effect versus active comparators was SMD = 0.49 (95% CI 0.21–0.77), a printed standardized mean difference; versus inactive comparators no significant difference was found at immediate post-test, but at 2–4 months versus inactive comparators SMD = 0.65 (95% CI 0.43–0.87).

> "At immediate post-test to one-month follow-up, a significant large effect size was found comparing simulation training to an active comparator (five studies [42,43,51–53:] Standardized Mean Difference (SMD) = 0.49, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.21–0.77) with moderate heterogeneity (I [2] = 46%)."
