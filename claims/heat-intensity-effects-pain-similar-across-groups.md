### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

fMRI experiment in healthy volunteers (n=40) during a pain reversal-learning task; voxelwise whole-brain analyses of heat-evoked activation controlling for group. The article reports robust intensity-related changes "did not differ between groups".

> "We observed robust intensity-related changes within pain modulatory regions, including bilateral insula, striatum, dorsal anterior cingulate, thalamus, and other regions that did not differ between groups"
