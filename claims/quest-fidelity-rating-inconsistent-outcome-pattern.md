### Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary 2015

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary. (2015). Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581287

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

On-treatment analysis of 931 observations using lesson-observation fidelity ratings (0–3 scale). Table 3.10 shows Low rating coef. 0.38*** (s.e. 0.06), Medium 0.17 (s.e. 0.07), High -0.05 (s.e. 0.07), against a not-implemented reference category.

> "The results suggest that schools with a low fidelity rating had improved outcomes relative to those who had not implemented the programme successfully at all (Table 3.10). However, this finding was not consistent across the two higher fidelity groups (Medium and High)."
