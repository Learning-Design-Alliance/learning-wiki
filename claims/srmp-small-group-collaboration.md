### Or Graur 2018

Or Graur. (2018). The Harvard Science Research Mentoring Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.08078](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.08078)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The director's qualitative observation from directing the program, based on his experience grouping students and assigning them to mentors. No systematic comparison of group sizes is reported.

> "I have found that this usually works well in groups of 2–3; larger groups tend to develop power dynamics between the students in which one or two students lead the work and the others are sidelined."
