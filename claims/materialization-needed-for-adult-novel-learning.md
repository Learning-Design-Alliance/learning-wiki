## Evidence

### Arievitch 2005

Arievitch, I.M., & Haenen, J.P.P. (2005). Connecting Sociocultural Theory and Educational Practice: Galperin's Approach. Educational Psychologist, 40(3), 155-165. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep4003_2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article's review of studies (citing Ausubel, 1968; Salmina, 1988; Talyzina, 1981) countering the assumption that material action forms are necessary only for young children. It reports that "a lot of studies have shown that even adults... often need at least partial materialization."

> "a lot of studies have shown that even adults, when they are learning anything substantially new for them, often need at least partial materialization with the help of something like an orienting chart"
