Virtual reality simulation for chemotherapy drug spill management training in pharmacy education: A cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09864-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study of 37 pharmacy interns at a Taiwanese tertiary medical center; within-cohort Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed "self-perceived scores increased significantly across all four items in both cohorts (all p < 0.001)". No effect sizes were printed.

> "After the first session, self-perceived scores increased significantly across all four items in both cohorts (all p < 0.001)."

### Chang Yen-Lin 2026 (2)

Virtual reality simulation for chemotherapy drug spill management training in pharmacy education: A cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09864-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

First-phase changes in Cohort 1 (n = 17) after traditional instructor-led instruction, from Table 2: "The median change score was + 2 for each item (Q1–Q4)" with all p < 0.001 by the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Only p-values and medians were printed; no effect size.

> "The median change score was + 2 for each item (Q1–Q4), and all four within-cohort changes were statistically significant (all p < 0.001)."

### Chang Yen-Lin 2026 (3)
