Evidence¶

Abdelghani 2026¶

Abdelghani, R., Kaiser, P., Murayama, K. (2026). Changes in Help-Seeking Strategies Predict unaided Performance during AI-based Mathematical Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28472

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Turn-level mixed-effects models using normalized within-session position, estimated on the corpus of 880 substantive turns from 112 students. The authors report a population slope of -.40, p < .001, for the shift toward closure-oriented requests.

"HS shifted away from conceptual and procedural assistance and toward answer- and verification-seeking as the interaction progressed (population slope=−.40,p < .001)."

Abdelghani 2026 (2)¶

Abdelghani, R., Kaiser, P., Murayama, K. (2026). Changes in Help-Seeking Strategies Predict unaided Performance during AI-based Mathematical Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28472

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Same mixed-effects analysis of within-session position for SRL functions. The authors report a modest positive population slope of .26, p < .001, toward explicit regulation, contrasting with the help-seeking direction.

"In contrast, SRL functions shifted modestly away from request-dominated interaction toward greater explicit planning, monitoring, or evaluation (population slope=.26,p < .001)."
