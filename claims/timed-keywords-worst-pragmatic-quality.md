## Evidence

### Fiona Draxler 2023

Fiona Draxler, Henrike Weingärtner, Maximiliane Windl, Albrecht Schmidt, and Lewis L. Chuang. (2023). Useful but Distracting: Keyword Highlights and Time-Synchronization in Captions for Language Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Pairwise comparisons from the repeated-measures ANOVA in the same 49-participant within-subject study show Timed Keywords "performs significantly worse than Standard Captions" (d = 1.96), Keyword Highlights (d = 1.77), and Timed Keyword Highlights (d = 1.54) on pragmatic quality.

> "Timed Keywords performs significantly worse than Standard Captions (𝑡 = 10.29, 𝑝 < 0.001,𝑑 = 1.96), Keyword Highlights (𝑡 = 9.30,𝑝 < 0.001,𝑑 = 1.77), and Timed Keyword Highlights (𝑡 = 8.10, 𝑝 < 0.001,𝑑 = 1.54)."
