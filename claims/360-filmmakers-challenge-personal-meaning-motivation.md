### 360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program 2017

360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bf98d40e-980c-4f54-9985-46bf5675d2fa

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey and interview documentation found students found personal meaning and pride in their media; 94% of educators reported the project "had personal importance for their students," 72% of students found it more interesting than other school projects, and 83% of educators reported typically disengaged students became focused. Student reflections connected motivation to personal meaning rather than grades.

> "94% of educators reported that the 360 Filmmakers Challenge had personal importance for their students."
