### Hamerski 2022

Hamerski, P. C., McPadden, D., Caballero, M. D., & Irving, P. W. (2022). Students' perspectives on computational challenges in physics class. Physical Review Physics Education Research 18, 020109. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.18.020109

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative case study of one high school physics class with a computation-integrated curriculum, drawing mainly on student interviews. The authors summarize their central finding: computation is "a double-edged sword" whose benefits depend on student familiarity.

> "we found that computation is a double-edged sword: It can make science learning more authentic for students who are familiar with it, but it can also generate frustration and an aversion towards physics for students who are not."
