## Evidence

### Jo Tondeur 2023

Jo Tondeur, Sarah Howard, Manon Van Zanten, Pierre Gorissen, Irma Van der Neut, Dana Uerz, Marijke Kral. (2023). The HeDiCom framework: Higher Education teachers' digital competencies for the future. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10193-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Systematic review conducted between December 2020 and February 2021 using Web of Science with four inclusion criteria; initially 122 empirical articles were identified, narrowed to 36 relevant articles, and full-text readings identified the 21 articles synthesized for the review stage.

> "Full-text readings of these articles further identified 21 articles that were specifically addressing digital competencies of teachers in Higher Education."

### Jo Tondeur 2023 (2)

Jo Tondeur, Sarah Howard, Manon Van Zanten, Pierre Gorissen, Irma Van der Neut, Dana Uerz, Marijke Kral. (2023). The HeDiCom framework: Higher Education teachers' digital competencies for the future. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10193-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert meetings comprised 11 Dutch-speaking senior academics across three groups meeting online twice over two months; the resulting first version was then validated through online sessions with 34 educational professionals and teachers, refining main- and sub-dimensions iteratively.

> "The resulting first version of the framework was then validated through online sessions with 34 educational professionals and teachers."
