## Evidence

### Kvintová Jana 2025

Kvintová Jana, Lacková Lucia, Liu Hongyang, Váchová Lucie, Potmesil Milon, Růžičková Veronika, Kopecký Kamil, Regec Vojtech. (2025). Resilience displays similar associative patterns with academic achievement regardless of the personality and mental health profile of future teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03697-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the 793-student survey, k-means clustering on TIPI traits and PHQ-4 symptoms produced three profiles (introverted low-symptom, high depressive/anxious with reduced emotional stability, extroverted low-symptom); Z tests and Steiger's Z tests compared regression models across clusters and found no significant differences.

> "Tests for differences in model efficiency and structure revealed no significant differences across clusters for any academic achievement subscale (Supplementary Tables S8, S9). This consistency underscores the universal applicability of resilience across diverse personality traits and mental health profiles."
