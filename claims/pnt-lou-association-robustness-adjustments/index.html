q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Six-factor robustness model adding GenAI guidance clarity (GGC), fitting well (χ2(396) = 625.61, CFI = 0.982). The article reports 'PNT remained positively associated with LOU, β = 0.478', attenuated from 0.568, and explained variance in LOU increased from 32.2 to 36.9%.

"PNT remained positively associated with LOU, β = 0.478, p < 0.001, while GGC was independently associated with LOU, β = 0.243, p < 0.001, and ACS, β = 0.196, p < 0.001."
