Jie Cao 2026¶

Jie Cao, Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Christian Schunn, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). Assessing the Impact and Underlying Pathways of Sequenced AI Feedback on Student Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07469

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bootstrap mediation model (N=199); the behavioral mediator sub_3 negatively predicted post-test (B = −0.33, SE = 0.10, p = .001), which the authors read as trial-and-error rather than constructive iteration.

"Conversely, there was a significant neg- ative indirect effect through the average number of tasks with >=3 sub- missions (Ind=−0.19,SE= 0.09,p=.03,95%CI[−0.374,−0.037])."

Jie Cao 2026 (2)¶

Jie Cao, Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Christian Schunn, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). Assessing the Impact and Underlying Pathways of Sequenced AI Feedback on Student Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07469

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same mediation model; mental effort did not significantly predict post-test (B = −0.03, p = .77), so the cognitive pathway was neutral despite layered feedback raising mental effort.

"The indirect effect through mental effort wasnotsignificant(Ind=−0.02,SE= 0.06,p=.80,95%CI[−0.167,0.099]), indicating that self-reported mental effort did not translate into a per- formance advantage or disadvantage in this context."
