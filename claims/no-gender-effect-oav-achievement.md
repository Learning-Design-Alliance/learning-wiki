ANOVA on the achievement test of the same 40-student randomized experiment tested group, gender and their interaction; the overall model was not significant (F = 1.91, p > .05) and no effect size was printed.

> "There is a significant difference in terms of group (F = 4.86, p < .05), but there is no significant difference in terms of gender (F = .00, p > .05), and the interaction between group and gender is not statistically significant (F = .09, p > .05)."
