### Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022

Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton. (2022). The Meta-Analytic Rain Cloud Plot: A New Approach to Visualizing Clearinghouse Data. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2022.2031366

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

RQ4 comparison of mean scores (Figure 7; Table 9): practitioner means 4.488 (FP), 4.855 (BP), 4.494 (RFP), 5.341 (MARC) versus researcher means 5.378, 4.955, 5.116, 5.807. The article reports "researchers score higher than practitioners on all four visualization types."

> "As the figure indicates, researchers score higher than practitioners on all four visualization types, although researchers have the largest gains over practitioners on FPs and RFPs."

### Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022 (2)

Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton. (2022). The Meta-Analytic Rain Cloud Plot: A New Approach to Visualizing Clearinghouse Data. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2022.2031366

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

t-tests of practitioner versus researcher scores by visualization (Table 9): FP difference -0.890, t = -3.474, p = 0.000; RFP difference -0.622, t = -2.450, p = 0.008; BP difference -0.100, p = 0.317; MARC difference -0.466, p = 0.019.

> "The differences between practitioners and researchers are significant at the pre-determined a¼ 0:05=4¼ 0:0125 level for both FP and RFP, which can also be seen in Figure 7 ."
