Evidence¶

Toraman 2016¶

Toraman, C., & Demir, E. (2016). The effect of constructivism on attitudes towards lessons: A meta–analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 62, 115-142. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.62.8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis by educational level (Table 3): primary (33 studies, ES = 0,727), middle school (4 studies, ES = 0,203, p = 0,780), undergraduate (6 studies, ES = 1,550). The between-group test was non-significant, so level is not a significant differentiating factor.

"On examination of Table 3, we are able to ascertain that heterogenity does not exist between attitudinal studies when the moderating factor of educational level (Q (b) (2) = 0,840, p>.05) is taken into consideration. Differences in levels of education do not represent an important factor in the meta–analysis process."
