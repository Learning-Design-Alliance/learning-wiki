# Construction, manufacturing, and wholesale trade led to good jobs for low-income students at sub-baccalaureate levels, but no industry pathway was promising for students with only a high school degree

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Table 3 descriptive analysis of industries (minimum 40 observations) by sub-baccalaureate degree level; utilities reached 97% for some-college students, and construction, manufacturing, and wholesale trade were promising across certificate, associate, and some-college levels.

> "While almost all industry pathways were promising for students with a bachelor’s degree or higher, there were no promising industry pathways for those who had just a high school diploma."
