# In the effort self-talk condition, competence beliefs were unrelated to performance change, unlike in the ability and no self-talk conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the effort self-talk condition, children's competence beliefs were unrelated to their task performance (t = 0.251, p = .802, b = .013). [→ Thomaes 2020](#thomaes-2020)
`q3 i?` In the ability and no self-talk control conditions, more negative competence beliefs were associated with worsened overtime performance (t = 3.569, p = .001, b = .189; t = 4.514, p < .001, b = .214). [→ Thomaes 2020 (2)](#thomaes-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Thomaes 2020

Thomaes, Tjaarda, Brummelman, and Sedikides. (2020). Effort Self‐Talk Benefits the Mathematics Performance of Children With Negative Competence Beliefs. Child Development, 91(6), 2211–2220. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13347

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Condition-wise regression of competence beliefs on performance change in the effort self-talk condition (N = 69): beliefs were "unrelated to their task performance, t = 0.251, p = .802, b = .013".

> "In the effort self‐talk condition, however, children’s competence beliefs were unrelated to their task performance, t = 0.251, p = .802, b = .013."

### Thomaes 2020 (2)

Thomaes, Tjaarda, Brummelman, and Sedikides. (2020). Effort Self‐Talk Benefits the Mathematics Performance of Children With Negative Competence Beliefs. Child Development, 91(6), 2211–2220. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13347

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Condition-wise regressions in the comparison conditions of the same experiment: "more negative competence beliefs were associated with worsened overtime performance" in both the ability condition (t = 3.569) and the control condition (t = 4.514).

> "In the ability and the no self‐talk control conditions, more negative competence beliefs were associated with worsened overtime performance, t = 3.569, p = .001, b = .189 and t = 4.514, p < .001, b = .214, respectively."
