## Evidence

### Samson 2022

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of four iron-only supplementation intervention studies reporting school performance outcomes; all four demonstrated positive effects on various measures of school performance.

> "Four studies investigated the effects of iron supplementation on school performance [16,52,54,55]. All studies demonstrated a positive effect of iron treatment on the various measures of school performance."

### Samson 2022 (2)

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review reports the Soemantri et al. supplementation trial stratified by baseline anemia status: significant score improvement among anemic iron-treated children, no significant differences among non-anemic cases.

> "among the anemic cases, the adjusted mean difference in scores of iron-treated children (mean difference = 3.64) was significantly different from the placebo-treated children (mean difference = −0.67). Conversely, among the non-anemic cases, there were no significant differences in changes between iron (mean difference = −0.29) and placebo (mean difference = 0.28) treated children"
