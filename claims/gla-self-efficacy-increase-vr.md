# Both GLA groups showed a large, significant increase in pipetting self-efficacy, with no difference between conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Self-efficacy increased significantly over time in both groups (re-experiencing d = 0.94; self-explaining d = 1.04), with no condition difference or interaction. [→ Valdemar Stenberdt 2025](#valdemar-stenberdt-2025)

## Evidence

### Valdemar Stenberdt 2025

Valdemar Stenberdt, Ban Mouid Shiwalia, Guido Makransky. (2025). Re-Experiencing vs. Self-Explaining: Comparing Generative Learning Activities in VR. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10096-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Self-efficacy analysis on 159 students in the VR pipetting experiment using a mixed ANOVA with Time and Condition. Both groups improved significantly (p < 0.001), effects persisted after Bonferroni correction; "There were no differences in the two groups’ self-efficacy" and no Condition × Time interaction. Supports H3a but not H3b.

> "An ANOV A showed a large, significant increase in students’ self-efficacy over time, F(1, 157)  = 114.774, p < 0.001, η p2 = 0.422."
