`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same cluster-randomized trial, time-by-condition analysis of fall-to-spring change. The article reports "marginally significant gains on NWF-WRC (t = 1.81, df = 14, p = .0919)" and no significant differences on NWF-CLS or ORF.
