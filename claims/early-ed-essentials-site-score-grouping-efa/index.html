Ehrlich 2018¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis (principal axis factoring with oblique rotation) of site-level measure scores across the 81 validation sites, extracting four to six factors. The authors compared loadings from the most appropriate EFA to their theoretically hypothesized classification, leading to final adjustments presented in the results section.

"We ran three rounds of EFA on the teacher and parent survey measures across the 81 sites, varying the number of factors being extracted (from four to six) to see how this changed "grouping" of the measures."

q2 · i? · design · r2

Score construction following HLM creation of site-level measure scores: each modeled site-level measure score was standardized, then measures grouped under an essential were averaged so "each measure score, therefore, contributed equally to the essential it was categorized under." Essential scores were standardized again for interpretation of final analytic models.

"We then averaged the set of measures under each essential support to create site -level essential scores."
