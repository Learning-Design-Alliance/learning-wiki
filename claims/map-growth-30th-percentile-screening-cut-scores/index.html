Evidence¶

He 2021¶

He, W., & Meyer, P. (2021). MAP Growth universal screening benchmarks: Establishing MAP Growth as an effective universal screener. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Classification accuracy analyses in the primary sample of students from five states (Spring 2018) compared 31 candidate MAP Growth cut scores against 11 criterion cut scores per subject, grade, and term. The study reports the 30th-percentile candidates "performed the best" and recommends them as universal screening cut scores.

"the candidate MAP Growth cut scores corresponding to the 30th percentile rank based on the national norms performed the best for identifying students in need of intensive intervention, given that the bottom 10% of the students on the criterion measure are assumed as students actually in need of intensive intervention"
