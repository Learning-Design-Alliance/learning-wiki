# In the prior 2025 evaluation, Air Tutors showed positive short-term benchmark gains at sufficient dosage but negative short-term statewide effects in the full sample

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 2025 evaluation found short-term impacts varied by outcome measure: negative significant statewide effects in the full sample, but positive significant benchmark gains for 24-hour dosage students in both districts. [→ Fong 2026](#fong-2026)

## Evidence

### Fong 2026

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2026). An Evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: A 1-Year Follow-Up on Math Achievement. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681370

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The brief reports, citing its own 2025 evaluation (Fong et al., 2025), that in the 24-hour dosage analysis there were "positive and statistically significant gains on district benchmark assessments in both districts". No effect sizes for those short-term gains are printed in this brief.

> "For the 24-hour dosage analysis, there were no statistically significant impacts on statewide math assessments, but there were positive and statistically significant gains on district benchmark assessments in both districts, highlighting the program’s potential to improve math achievement when students received sufficient tutoring dosage."
