## Evidence

### Ziyan Xu 2022

Ziyan Xu, Xinqiao Gao, Jun Wei, Huiqin Liu, & Yu Zhang. (2022). Adolescent user behaviors on short video application, cognitive functioning and academic performance. https://www.statista.com/statistics/1095186/tiktok-us-users-age/

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r?`

Bootstrap mediation testing (5,000 replications) within the two-group SEM. The article reports working memory and verbal ability as significant mediators for the younger cohort, while "No other pathways were found significant for the elder cohort".

> "For the younger cohort, the total indirect effects of daily usage and recommender reliance on academic performance are significantly negative (β=-0.19, 95% CI [-0.27, -0.12] and β=-0.09, 95% CI [-0.15, -0.03] respectively)"
