## Evidence

### Gong Qiong 2024

Gong Qiong, Tao Ting. (2024). The relationship between short video usage and academic achievement among elementary school students: The mediating effect of attention and the moderating effect of parental short video usage. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309899

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis (SPSS PROCESS Model 7) on the 1,052-student sample tested the manifest moderator. The interaction of short video usage × parental usage duration was significant (β = -0.092, t = -5.293, p < 0.001), and the moderated mediation effect was -0.019.

> "Further interpretation of the interaction term indicated that parental short video usage duration moderated the relationship between short video usage and attention (β = -0.092, t = -5.293, p < 0.001). It was a positive moderation, meaning that longer parental short video usage duration enhances the negative impact of children's short video usage on their attention."
