q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Network meta-analysis of theoretical knowledge exam scores from 50 studies, reported via league matrix of 10 teaching designs with 45 comparisons; mean differences with 95% CIs versus LBL are as printed, and PACS-CBL's evidence was rated low quality while 3D-PBL was high quality.

"Compared to LBL, PACS-CBL (MD 13.53, 95% CI 11.83 to 15.23) had the greatest improvement in theoretical exam scores, followed by 3D-PBL (MD 9.44, 95% CI 8.69 to 10.19) and 3D-CBL (MD 7.83, 95% CI 6.99 to 8.66)."
