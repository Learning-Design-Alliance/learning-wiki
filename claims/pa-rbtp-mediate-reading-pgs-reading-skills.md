## Evidence

### Remon 2025

Remon, A., Mascheretti, S., Voronin, I., Feng, B., Ouellet-Morin, I., Brendgen, M., Vitaro, F., Robaey, P., Andlauer, T. F. M., Boivin, M. & Dionne, G. (2025). Mediational effects of reading-related intermediate phenotypes from polygenic scores to reading skills. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00346-x

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation mediation modeling with 5000 bootstrap replicates in the twin sample (n = 328); the model accounted for 28.4% and 27.0% of variance in Decoding and Comprehension. Specific indirect effects via PA were 0.051 (decoding) and 0.036 (comprehension); via RBTP 0.034 (decoding only). The article calls these "small, although significant, indirect paths" in the Discussion.

> "we found a signiﬁcant total indirect effect from Reading-PGS to Decoding (β = 0.090, SE = 0.031; 95% CI = 0.028/0.150) and to Comprehension (β = 0.079, SE = 0.029; 95% CI = 0.019/0.140). Within these total indirect effects, three speciﬁc indirect effects were signiﬁcant, involving PA and RBTP as mediators"
