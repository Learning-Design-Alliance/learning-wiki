## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2025

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Barton, H. D., Rowell, S. F., Chalker, D. L., & Fink, A. (2025). Students Don't Learn the Way They Think They Do in a Large, Active-Learning Genetics Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-10-0251

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way within-subjects ANOVA on course pretest/posttest percent correct for 444 consenting students with complete pretest and posttest data. "a significant main effect for time of test" with partial η² = 0.492; "students scored significantly higher on the posttest than the pre-test".

> "The results indicated a significant main effect for time of test (F(1,444) = 430.74, p < 0.001, partial η2 =  0.492). Unsurprisingly, post-hoc testing using Tukey's HSD indicated that students scored significantly higher on the posttest than the pre-test (t = 20.8, p < 0.001)"

### Walck-Shannon 2025 (2)

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Barton, H. D., Rowell, S. F., Chalker, D. L., & Fink, A. (2025). Students Don't Learn the Way They Think They Do in a Large, Active-Learning Genetics Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-10-0251

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Two-way within-subjects ANOVA on pretest/posttest percent correct for 444 students. "there was also a significant interaction between time of test and activity type" (partial η² = 0.017); gains were 24.5% for prediction, 21.5% for tell-then-practice, and 18.3% for worked examples.

> "there was also a significant interaction between time of test and activity type (F(2, 888) = 7.70, p < 0.001, partial η2 =  0.017). Questions aligned with prediction activities showed a significantly larger increase in score from pretest to posttest compared with questions aligned with worked example activities (t = 7.31, p < 0.001) and tell-then-practice activities (t = 8.44, p < 0.001)"

### Walck-Shannon 2025 (3)

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Barton, H. D., Rowell, S. F., Chalker, D. L., & Fink, A. (2025). Students Don't Learn the Way They Think They Do in a Large, Active-Learning Genetics Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-10-0251

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same ANOVA's activity-type main effect (partial η² = 0.059). "students scored significantly higher overall on questions aligned to prediction activities than questions aligned to tell-then-practice" and worked example activities; worked example vs. tell-then-practice showed no significant difference (t = 0.279, p = 0.958).

> "there was a significant main effect for activity type (F(2, 888) = 27.80, p < 0.001, partial η2 =  0.059). Post-hoc Tukey's HSD testing showed that students scored significantly higher overall on questions aligned to prediction activities than questions aligned to tell-then-practice (t = 8.447, p < 0.001) and worked example (t = 5.950, p < 0.001) activities"
