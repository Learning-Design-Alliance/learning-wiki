## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann and John R. Thompson 2006

Michael C. Wittmann and John R. Thompson. (2006). Integrated approaches in physics education: A graduate level course in physics, pedagogy, and education research. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608240](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608240)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous take-home exam data from the electric circuits unit's question-intent analysis task. Example responses quoted in the article include a biology GS noting the current question "gives insight into whether or not the students truly consider the battery as a constant current source" and a physics GS distinguishing holistic versus piece-wise analysis.

> "In both examples, we find that students after instruction are able to carefully interpret student reasoning in a way that is useful for interpreting curriculum materials and facilitation of student learning."
