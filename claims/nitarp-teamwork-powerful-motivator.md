L. M. Rebull, D. A. French, W. Laurence, T. Roberts, G. K. Squires, M. T. Fitzgerald, and V. Gorjian. (2018). Major Outcomes of an Authentic Astronomy Research Experience Professional Development Program: An Analysis of 8 Years of Data from NITARP. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of open-ended survey responses; the paper states the importance of working in a team "was not at all understood prior to this analysis" and "emerged, powerfully and obviously, upon reading all the survey answers."

> "Even if they approach the project initially thinking that they will work on their own within the team, most teachers find rather rapidly that the teamwork itself is a powerful motivator [20]."
