## Evidence

### Qi Hong 2026

Qi Hong, Ban Jingjing, Wang Nana, Guo Huimin, Yang Yang, Tang Shan. (2026). Development and evaluation of a literature seminar course based on flipped classroom and team-based learning in the graduate nursing curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08823-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dimension-level paired t-tests in the 124-student study support this: thesis writing rose from 1.74 ± 0.89 to 2.51 ± 0.80 (t = -7.371), problem identification from 1.76 ± 0.93 to 2.56 ± 0.98 (t = -6.718), data processing from 1.77 ± 0.89 to 2.50 ± 0.83 (t = -6.456), all p < 0.001.

> "Notably, the most pronounced improvements were observed in thesis writing, problem identification, and data processing abilities."
