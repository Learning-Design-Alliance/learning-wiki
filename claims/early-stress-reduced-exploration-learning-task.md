Harms Madeline B., Shannon-Bowen Katherine E., Hanson Jamie L., Pollak Seth D. (2018). Instrumental Learning and Cognitive Flexibility Processes are Impaired in Children Exposed to Early Life Stress. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12596

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral analysis of the acquisition condition found "stress-exposed adolescents did not follow the optimal strategy of initially responding to images indiscriminately" to explore and gather information, consistent with reduced exploration when incentives are at stake.

> "Unlike controls, stress-exposed adolescents did not follow the optimal strategy of initially responding to images indiscriminately at the beginning of each acquisition run to explore and gather information."
