Nyamboga TO 2026¶

Nyamboga TO (2026) From digital transformation to intelligent classrooms: artificial intelligence, adaptive leadership, and service delivery in global higher education—a systematic literature review. Front. Educ. 11:1899369. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1899369

q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis of 155 studies (2019–2024) retrieved via PRISMA procedures; the review reports this as a dominant pattern, with high certainty for AI adoption patterns in its GRADE-adapted assessment. The synthesis states that "AI significantly enhances teaching, learning, and administrative efficiency when supported by adequate infrastructure."
