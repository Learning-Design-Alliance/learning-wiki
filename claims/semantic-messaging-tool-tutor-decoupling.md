### Ritter 2016

Ritter, S. (2016). Plug-in Tutor Agents: Still Pluggin'. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 405–415. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0062-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The author describes the key architectural feature of semantic messaging that decoupled tool and tutor, enabling independent evolution of each component.

> "A key aspect of the decoupling of tool and tutor was that the communication between the two happen at the Bsemantic^ level. Semantic-level communication allowed changes in both tool and tutor without having to modify both components together."
