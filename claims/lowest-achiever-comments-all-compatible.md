`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comment-categorization analysis of written comments for the lowest achievers in the 11 TBL classes. Of 1,313 comments, 1,207 were negative and reasonable, and "All the negative comments were compatible with the criteria for evaluation"; passive participation was the largest category.

> "Among the 1,313 comments for the lowest achievers, 1,207 comments were negative and reasonable, while the other 106 comments were not specific and understandable. All the negative comments were compatible with the criteria for evaluation (Table 3)."
