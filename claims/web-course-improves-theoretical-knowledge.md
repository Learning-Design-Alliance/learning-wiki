## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Svensson Leif, Claesson Andreas, Herlitz Johan, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). The effect of a national web course “Help-Brain-Heart” as a supplemental learning tool before CPR training: a cluster randomised trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-017-0439-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of 1232 students in a cluster randomised trial. At 6 months means were 2.8 vs 3.2 for stroke symptoms (P < 0.001), 2.6 vs 2.9 for AMI symptoms (P = 0.008), and 3.2 vs 3.4 for lifestyle factors (P < 0.001).

> "The intervention group scored significantly better in terms of total score for the survey questions regarding stroke, AMI and lifestyle factors, both directly after training and at the 6 months follow-up (Table 3)."
