Position-paper argument (type e) in Section 1.1 on opportunities for learning: the authors assert, without reporting new data, that LLMs can assist "the development of reading and writing skills" and critical thinking for elementary students, among uses across primary to professional levels.

> "For elementary school students, large language models can assist in the development of reading and writing skills (e.g., by suggesting syntactic and grammatical corrections), as well as in the development of writing style and critical thinking skills."
