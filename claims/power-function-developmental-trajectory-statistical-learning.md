## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Duan 2025

Duan, R., Sun, Q., & Tong, X. (2025). Non-linear development in statistical learning of visual orthographic regularities. npj Science of Learning 10, 6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00298-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model fitting in MATLAB of accuracy rates across nine age groups. The power function explained 70.42%, 59.71%, and 18.00% of variance for high, moderate, and low consistency; the article reports the linear model's AICc and BICc were all higher, indicating the power function performs better.

> "the model can account for more than 18.00% variance of the accuracy
rates across all age groups (R2: 70.42% for the high-consistency level, 59.71%
for the moderate-consistency level, and 18.00% for the low-consistency
level)"

### Duan 2025 (2)

Duan, R., Sun, Q., & Tong, X. (2025). Non-linear development in statistical learning of visual orthographic regularities. npj Science of Learning 10, 6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00298-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square and confidence-interval evaluation of fitted power-function exponents. The value of zero fell outside the 95% CIs for high and moderate levels, indicating significant developmental differences from K1 to adults for those levels, but not for low consistency.

> "the 95% CIs ofns were [0.038 0.155], [0.015 0.101], and
[−0.023 0.073], for high, moderate, and low consistency levels, respectively."
