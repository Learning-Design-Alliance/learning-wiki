## Evidence

### Creupelandt Hanne 2019

Creupelandt Hanne, Anthierens Sibyl, Habraken Hilde, Sirdifield Coral, Siriwardena Aloysius Niroshan, Christiaens Thierry. (2019). A tailored e-learning gives long-term changes in determinants of GPs’ benzodiazepines prescribing: a pretest-posttest study with self-report assessments. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2019.1663591

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest-posttest self-report study (n = 244), analyzed with the McNemar Bowker test: "When starting the module, only 18.4% reported success, while several months after completing the e-module, 56.9% reported successfully minimizing BZD prescribing".

> "When starting the module, only 18.4% reported success, while several months after completing the e-module, 56.9% reported successfully minimizing BZD prescribing: this number more than tripled."

### Creupelandt Hanne 2019 (2)

Creupelandt Hanne, Anthierens Sibyl, Habraken Hilde, Sirdifield Coral, Siriwardena Aloysius Niroshan, Christiaens Thierry. (2019). A tailored e-learning gives long-term changes in determinants of GPs’ benzodiazepines prescribing: a pretest-posttest study with self-report assessments. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2019.1663591

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intention outcome in the same 244-GP pretest-posttest cohort: "the number of participants intending to prescribe fewer BZDs almost tripled" on completing the module, reported as a significant positive effect on intentions and efforts to change.

> "Firstly, the intervention appeared to have a significant and positive effect on participants’ intentions and efforts to change their prescribing practices: on completing the module the number of participants intending to prescribe fewer BZDs almost tripled."
