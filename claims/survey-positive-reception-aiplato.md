Post-semester survey of ten five-point Likert statements (n = 87), quantified with the Top 2 Box Percentage method. Responses "revealed a distinctly positive reception of aiPlato"; the most agreeable item concerned frequent use of Evaluate My Work for step-by-step feedback, and the most disagreeable was that AI Tutor Chat was helpful when stuck.
