## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2023

Rahimi A. Masie, Hardon Sem F., Willuth E., Lang F., Haney Caelan M., Felinska Eleni A., Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Müller-Stich Beat P., Horeman Tim, Nickel F., Daams Freek. (2023). Force-based assessment of tissue handling skills in simulation training for robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-09905-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression analyzing the effect of robotic surgery experience on outcome measurements in the 60-participant study; the article reports "clear regression of the total task time" and trial-specific p-values for force parameters, with no coefficients or effect sizes printed.

> "The regression analysis indicate clear regression of the total task time (p ≤ 0.05) and partial regression for the maximum impulse (Trial 1 p ≤ 0.001, Trial 2 p = 0.050 and Trial 6 p = 0.007), maximum force (Trial 1 p = 0.029), force volume (Trial 1 p = 0.006 and Trial 2 p = 0.029) and mean non-zero force (Trial 1 p = 0.023)"
