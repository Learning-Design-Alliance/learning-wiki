# Interest profiles combine homogeneous and heterogeneous patterns across the six dimensions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The identified interest profiles consist of both homogeneous patterns (similar scores on all dimensions) and heterogeneous patterns characterized by interactions across dimensions. [→ Akkerman 2020](#akkerman-2020)

## Evidence

### Akkerman 2020

Akkerman, D. M., Vulperhorst, J. P., & Akkerman, S. F. (2020). A developmental extension to the multidimensional structure of interests. Journal of Educational Psychology, 112(1), 183-203. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000361

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Descriptive structural finding from the latent profile analyses of the 204 students' interest reports: the six profiles at each time point include both homogeneous and heterogeneous dimensional patterns, which were then examined longitudinally. No effect size is printed.

> "These profiles, consisting of both homogeneous (i.e., similar scores on all dimensions) and heterogeneous (i.e., characterized by interactions across dimensions) patterns of scores across the dimensions, were then investigated longitudinally to evaluate them within developmental theory."
