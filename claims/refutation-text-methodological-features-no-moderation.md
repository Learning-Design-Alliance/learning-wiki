## Evidence

### Schroeder 2022

Noah L. Schroeder & Aurelia C. Kucera. (2022). Refutation Text Facilitates Learning: a Meta-Analysis of Between-Subjects Experiments. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis comparing refutation text against expository/scientific text, mixed control conditions, non-specified text, and problem-solving conditions across the 44 comparisons. All coded comparison conditions showed significant positive effects favoring refutation text.

> "Importantly, this moderator analysis showed that refutation text was significantly more effective than all comparison conditions, and these positive effects did not vary significantly depending on the comparison condition (Qb(3) = 2.56, p = 0.47)."

### Schroeder 2022 (2)

Noah L. Schroeder & Aurelia C. Kucera. (2022). Refutation Text Facilitates Learning: a Meta-Analysis of Between-Subjects Experiments. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Age moderator analysis across the 44 comparisons, coded as primary, middle, secondary, post-secondary, mixed adult, and mixed ages. Most comparisons used post-secondary samples; prior knowledge (Q(2) = 2.18, p = 0.34), assignment type (Qb(2) = 4.33, p = 0.11), test format (Qb(5) = 3.76, p = 0.58), and location (Qb(2) = 0.63, p = 0.73) also did not significantly moderate.

> "We found that the effects of refutation text were robust and consistent regardless of the age of the learners (Qb(5) = 6.60, p = 0.25). While most of the comparisons in our sample used post-secondary samples, the results revealed that refutation text was associated with positive effects across all other age groups examined."
