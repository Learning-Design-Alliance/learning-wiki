Evidence¶

Truman L. Kelley; Ernest C. Witham 1918¶

Truman L. Kelley; Ernest C. Witham. (1918). Communications and Discussions. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

An analytical statement by Kelley, offered without data, enumerating the causes of overlapping between grade distributions. It argues the three-factor attribution: "unreliability of tests", "unimportance of function tested", and "failure to properly classify".

"Three factors make for overlapping: (a) unreliability of tests; (b) unimportance of function tested; and (c) failure to properly classify, and only in so far as the first two are known not to be the causes can improper classification be established."
