## Evidence

### Joke Voogt 2015

Joke Voogt, Therese Laferrière, Alain Breuleux, Rebecca C. Itow, Daniel T. Hickey, Susan McKenney (2015). Collaborative design as a form of professional development. Instr Sci (2015) 43:259-282. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-014-9340-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-research study (Anto 2013) in a remote Ethiopian university where facilitators and novice teachers collaboratively designed communicative English lessons; the article reports the novice-versus-facilitator finding descriptively, with no test statistics or effect sizes.

> "in the Ethiopian study, the parallel involvement of facilitators in regular teaching and training activities, and shortage of time were considered factors hindering teacher learning in the first cycle. The findings showed that novice teachers performed as well as facilitators."
