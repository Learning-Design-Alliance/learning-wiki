## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gallo Cordoba 2022

Gallo Cordoba, B., Leckie, G., and Browne, W.J. (2022). Decomposing Ethnic Achievement Gaps across Multiple Levels of Analysis and for Multiple Ethnic Groups. Sociological Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1177/00811750221099503

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

District-extended decomposition applied to the same 2011 SABER 11 census data. Under Approach 1 the school and district segregation components are each 0.19 SD of the 0.46 SD gap; the article prints no standardised effect size for these components, so no magnitude code is assigned.

> "Under Approach 1, the 0.46 SD overall gap is decomposed into the 0.07 SD within-school gap ˆβW , the 0.19 SD effect of school segregation ˆωSˆβCS and the 0.19 SD effect of district segregation ˆωDˆβCD."

### Gallo Cordoba 2022 (2)

Gallo Cordoba, B., Leckie, G., and Browne, W.J. (2022). Decomposing Ethnic Achievement Gaps across Multiple Levels of Analysis and for Multiple Ethnic Groups. Sociological Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1177/00811750221099503

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Approach 2 decomposition of the same census data: the between-district gap component is 0.29 SD, and the components account for "the 5.7%, 31.3% and 63% of the overall gap". No standardised effect size is printed for this component.

> "the 0.29 SD that can be attributed to the between-district gap componentˆωDˆβ BD. These elements account for the 5.7%, 31.3% and 63% of the overall gap, respectively."
