`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Proof-of-concept demonstration reported in Materials and Methods and Results: courses for fictional patients Mario (AF, warfarin) and Anne (lumbar degenerative disc, NSAID plus exercises) were generated by the planning and execution module, with interaction sequences shown in Tables 2 and 3 and Figure 6. This is a design validation, not a patient learning outcome study.

> "we validate that the constructs of our framework are appropriate for representing content and deriving personalized courses for two use cases: anticoagulation treatment of an atrial fibrillation patient and lower back pain management to treat a lumbar degenerative disc condition."
