# Age, gender, and presence of diseases significantly affect K-DTVP-2 score, while only age significantly affects VMI-3R score

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Multiple regression identified age, gender, and presence of diseases as significant predictors of K-DTVP-2 score. [→ Kim E 2014](#kim-e-2014)
`q2 i?` Multiple regression identified only age as a significant predictor of VMI-3R score. [→ Kim E 2014 (2)](#kim-e-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Kim E 2014

Kim E, Park Y-K, Byun Y-H, Park M-S, Kim H. (2014). Influence of aging on visual perception and visual motor integration in Korean adults. Journal of Exercise Rehabilitation 10(4):245-250. https://doi.org/10.12965/jer.140147

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on K-DTVP-2 score (n=139) with gender, age, BMI, diseases, and exercise as predictors; the model explained 62.7% of the total variance (F=44.710, P<0.001), with scores decreased with increasing age (B=-0.512).

> "The significant affecting factor on K-DTVP-2 score were (t=-9.130, P<0.001), gender (t=3.029, P=0.003), and the presence of diseases (t=-2.504, P=0.013)."

### Kim E 2014 (2)

Kim E, Park Y-K, Byun Y-H, Park M-S, Kim H. (2014). Influence of aging on visual perception and visual motor integration in Korean adults. Journal of Exercise Rehabilitation 10(4):245-250. https://doi.org/10.12965/jer.140147

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on VMI-3R score (n=192) with the same five predictors; the VMI-3R score decreased with increasing age (B=-0.224) and the model explained 34.4% of the total variance (F=19.510, P<0.001).

> "The significant affecting factor on VMI-3R score was only age (t=-6.300, P<0.001)."
