Manikin-based two-minute CPR cycle assessment of 80 students immediately after AHA BLS training, measuring ventilation success and tidal volume with QCPR manikins. The study reports "44.3% of ventilations delivered were of adequate tidal volume" and "2.2" unsuccessful ventilations per cycle.
