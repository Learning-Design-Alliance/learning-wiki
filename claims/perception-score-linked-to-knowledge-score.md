Saiboon Ismail M., Zahari Fareena, Isa Hisham M., Sabardin Dazlin M., Robertson Colin E. (2021). E-Learning in Teaching Emergency Disaster Response Among Undergraduate Medical Students in Malaysia. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.628178

Observational association within the same study: participants grouped by self-gain perception score (strongly agree n = 136, mean 14.25; agree n = 30, mean 11.77; disagree n = 2, mean 7.5). The ANOVA found "a significant difference between the PS groups' knowledge score (p < 0.001)". No effect size printed; association, not causation.
