### van Bonn Sara M. 2024

van Bonn Sara M., Grajek Jan S., Rettschlag Stefanie, Schraven Sebastian P., Mlynski Robert. (2024). Interactive electronic visualization formats in student teaching. Hno. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00106-024-01436-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective single-center evaluation of the electronic ward round in winter semester 2020/2021; 43 students completed the voluntary evaluation form. On the 5-point Likert item, "13,95 %" fully agreed and "48,84 %" agreed with the knowledge-gain statement; the discussion reports 62,79 % overall agreement. No effect size is printed.
