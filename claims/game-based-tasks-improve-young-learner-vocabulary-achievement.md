### Mehregan 2014

Masoumeh Mehregan. (2014). Game-Based Tasks for Foreign Language Instruction: Perspectives on Young Learners' Vocabulary Acquisition. The IAFOR Journal of Language Learning. https://iafor.org/journal

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 40 Iranian young learners (20 per group) homogenized at elementary level via the KET reading section; independent samples T-test on post-test scores after 20 treatment sessions. The article reports a statistically significant group difference with "t (38) = -5.52, p < 0.05"; no effect size is printed.
