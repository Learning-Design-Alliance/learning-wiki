Yu Caixia, Guo Wanwei, Yu Linna, Huang Chongming, Zhao Guoxiang. (2026). Development of core competency indicators for emergency medicine residents based on the competency model: a Delphi and analytic hierarchy process study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09876-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analytic hierarchy process weighting of first-level indicators from the second Delphi round (Table 4). The article states the explicit competencies accounted for 47% of total weight while "the implicit differentiating competencies (self-concept, trait, and motivation) that lie below the iceberg accounted for 53% of the total weight."

> "skill had the highest single weight at 0.405, followed by trait at 0.268, self-concept at 0.163, motivation at 0.100, and knowledge at the lowest weight of 0.065."
