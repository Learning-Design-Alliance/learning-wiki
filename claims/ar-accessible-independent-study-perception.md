Thematic analysis of short-answer responses from 10 educators. The study reports "Positive feedback was received from nine educators" who appreciated AR as "an accessible learning tool for independent study". No effect size is printed.

> "Positive feedback was received from nine educators and one educator concluded that AR currently does not present any perceived benefits for learning anatomy where other digital methods cannot suffice.
