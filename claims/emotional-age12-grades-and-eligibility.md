## Evidence

### Agnafors S 2021

Agnafors S, Barmark M, Sydsjö G. (2021). Mental health and academic performance: a study on selection and causation effects from childhood to early adulthood. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-020-01934-5

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regressions (Models 2 and 3) in the SESBiC cohort; for incomplete grades OR = 2.35 (1.07–5.12, p 0.032, N 901) and for non-eligibility OR = 1.95 (1.06–3.59, p 0.031, N 760), showing "an association was found between internalizing problems at age 12 and incomplete grades from compulsory school".

> "Likewise, an association was found between internalizing problems at age 12 and incomplete grades from compulsory school after controlling for the above-mentioned variables (Table 2)."
