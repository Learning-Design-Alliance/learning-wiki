# E-M scoring leaves residual score distortion, suggesting some disengaged test responses are nonrapid rather than rapid guesses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The inability of E-M scoring to remove all score distortion suggests additional nonrapid item responses reflect less-than-full engagement by some test takers. [→ Steven Wise 2020](#steven-wise-2020)

## Evidence

### Steven Wise 2020

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Using retest data to evaluate and improve effort-moderated scoring. Journal of Educational Measurement. https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12275

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Third reported finding from the retest-data evaluation: the authors interpret residual distortion as evidence of "nonrapid item responses that reflect less‐than‐full engagement by some test takers." This is the authors' interpretation of the pattern.

> "Finally, the inability of E‐M scoring to account for all of the score distortion suggests the additional presence of nonrapid item responses that reflect less‐than‐full engagement by some test takers."
