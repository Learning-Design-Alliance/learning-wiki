## Evidence

### Maheswari and Ramakrishnan 2015

I. Uma Maheswari, N. Ramakrishnan. (2015). EFFECTIVENESS OF CAI PACKAGE ON ACHIEVEMENT IN PHYSICS OF IX STANDARD STUDENTS. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, Vol. 10 No. 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097442

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup t-test on posttest CTWPE scores for the 40 boys (20 experimental, 20 control). The article reports the "'t' value ... is 3.170" with significance at 0.01 and 0.05 for df 38, and experimental mean 77 exceeding control mean 64.

> "the 't' value between the experimental and control groups' boys in their mean achievement on Criterion Test Work, Power and Energy is 3.170. It is significant at 0.01 and 0.05 level for the df 38. The mean of Experimental group (77) is higher than the mean of control group (64)."
