## Evidence

### Postiglione A and De Angelis I 2021

Postiglione A and De Angelis I. (2021). Students' understanding of gravity using the rubber sheet analogy: an Italian experience. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post item on how planets move around the Sun (Figure 6). Before, 52% answered correctly; after, "the percentage of correct answers rises to 83%"; about 80% remained correct at four months. Descriptive percentages only.

> "Right after the activity the percentage of correct answers rises to 83%, while all the other wrong answers reduce."

### Postiglione A and De Angelis I 2021 (2)

Postiglione A and De Angelis I. (2021). Students' understanding of gravity using the rubber sheet analogy: an Italian experience. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item on which planets orbit faster around the Sun (Figure 7), probing Kepler's third law. The quote prints the pre, post and four-month percentages of correct responses; no inferential statistic is reported.

> "If before the activity the 70% already know how to correctly respond, this percentage rises to 91% after the activity, and to 83% four months after (see the Appendix)."

### Postiglione A and De Angelis I 2021 (3)

Postiglione A and De Angelis I. (2021). Students' understanding of gravity using the rubber sheet analogy: an Italian experience. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item on how space probes travel (Figure 8). Before the activity 55% answered incorrectly; after, "the majority of students (79%) have understood what is gravity assist", with 76% correct at four months.

> "After the activity the majority of students (79%) have understood what is gravity assist, a trend that is conﬁrmed after four months (76%)."
