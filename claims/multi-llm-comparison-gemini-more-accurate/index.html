From Dataset 1: group G4 compared outputs of three LLMs (ChatGPT-3.5, Gemini-1.5 Flash, Perplexity), though prompts were not identical across models. Students judged that "Gemini produced more accurate results overall" but it could not make finer distinctions such as between direct and indirect anaphora.

"In their analysis, the students observed that Gemini produced more accurate results overall, even if it still struggled with certain aspects of the text."
