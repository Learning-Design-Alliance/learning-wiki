## Subclaims
`q2 i?` MANOVA showed significant inter-country variability for the pharmaceutical care (p = 0.045) and organisation and management (p = 0.002) clusters, and no significant variability for the pharmaceutical public health (p = 0.094) and professional and personal (p = 0.2) clusters. [→ Udoh Arit 2018](#udoh-arit-2018)

## Evidence

### Udoh Arit 2018

Udoh Arit, Bruno Andreia, Bates Ian. (2018). A survey of pharmacists’ perception of foundation level competencies in African countries. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0280-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA via Pillai's trace on 426 replies from Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa and Ghana evaluating relevance weighting per country per competency, with Bonferroni-corrected post hoc analysis; variability was significant in two clusters and absent in the other two.

> "Specific inter-country variability was observed in the ‘pharmaceutical care’ (Pillia’s trace V = 0.083, F = 1.624, df = 18, p = 0.045), and ‘organisation and management’ (Pillia’s trace V = 0.136, F = 2.279, df = 18, p = 0.002) clusters."
