Gripshover 2022¶

Gripshover, S., Londerée, A., Ahuvia, I., Shyjka, A., Kroshinsky, F., Ryan, N., Farrington, C., & Paunesku, D. (2022). Learning Conditions Are an Actionable, Early Indicator of Math Learning. PERTS & University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publication/learning-conditions-are-an-actionable-early-indicator-of-math-learning

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of survey and grade data from over 4,000 U.S. 8th–12th graders collected October–March of 2019–20. The report states students in the “Strongly Agree” range were “more than two times as likely to earn a B or better” than those in the “Strongly Disagree” range; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Students who average in the “Strongly Agree” range across all learning conditions—indicating strongly positive learning conditions—are more than two times as likely to earn a B or better in math that term compared with students in the “Strongly Disagree” range."
