## Evidence

### R. Garcia 2007

R. Garcia, A. Zozulya, and J. Stickney. (2007). MATLAB codes for teaching quantum physics: Part 1. https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1622 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0704.1622](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0704.1622)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors report, from their teaching experience at WPI, this common student error, which they illustrate with the incorrect identity ⟨p||Ψ⟩|x⟩ = |Ψ⟩⟨p|x⟩. The claim rests on the authors' instructional observation, not a controlled study. They note "we learn much about student thinking from the answers given by our best students."

> "we ﬁnd a common error when studying 1D quantum mechanics is a student treating Ψ(x) and |Ψ⟩ interchangeably, ignoring the fact that the ﬁrst is a scalar but the ket corresponds to a column vector"
