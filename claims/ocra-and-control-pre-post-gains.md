# Both traditional teaching and the OCRA approach significantly improved pre-test to post-test achievement, with a larger gain in the OCRA group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group improved significantly from pre-test (27.86) to post-test (62.24), a total increase of 34.38 marks. [→ Shariman and Talib 2017](#shariman-and-talib-2017)
`q2 i?` The control group also improved significantly from pre-test (22.62) to post-test (40.86), a total increase of 18.24 marks, though the article calls the achievement not outstanding. [→ Shariman and Talib 2017 (2)](#shariman-and-talib-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Shariman and Talib 2017

Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman and Othman Talib. (2017). OCRA, A Mobile Learning Prototype for Understanding Chemistry Concepts. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group t-test for the 46-student experimental group taught with the OCRA prototype; pre-test mean 27.86 (SD 7.00), post-test mean 62.24 (SD 12.22), t value 15.83, reported significant at p< .05.

> "Table 2 demonstrates there is a significant difference (p< .05) between the scores of the pre-test and  post-test for the experimental group which is a total increase of 34.38 marks."

### Shariman and Talib 2017 (2)

Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman and Othman Talib. (2017). OCRA, A Mobile Learning Prototype for Understanding Chemistry Concepts. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group t-test for the 46-student control group taught with traditional methods; pre-test mean 22.62 (SD 7.79), post-test mean 40.86 (SD 10.90), t value -9.77, reported significant at p<.05.

> "Table 1 demonstrates there is a significant   difference (p<.05)  between the scores of the pre-test and  post-test for the control group which is a total increase of 18.24 marks."
