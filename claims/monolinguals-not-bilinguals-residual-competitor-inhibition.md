## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011

Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica. (2011). Bilingualism influences inhibitory control in auditory comprehension. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3582323/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Negative-priming experiment with 30 monolinguals and 30 bilinguals; probes appeared in locations previously occupied by competitor or control pictures. "monolinguals identified priming probes in positions previously occupied by competitor pictures (M = 505.5, SE = 14.3) slower than priming probes in positions previously occupied by control pictures (M = 485.5, SE = 12.9)"; bilinguals did not differ, p > .5. Trial type by group interaction F(1, 58) = 10.7, p < .01.

> "Follow-up t-tests revealed that monolinguals identified priming probes in positions previously occupied by competitor pictures (M = 505.5, SE = 14.3) slower than priming probes in positions previously occupied by control pictures (M = 485.5, SE = 12.9), t(29) = 4.3, p < .001"
