Evidence¶

Wang 2013¶

Wang, J., Lin, C.-F. C., Yu, W.-C. W., & Wu, E. (2013). Meaningful engagement in Facebook learning environments: Merging social and academic lives. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 14(1), Article 26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006268

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of Facebook postings in the two course groups (134 members). The article reports 297 wall postings and 2,057 comments monthly on average, with each comment obtaining 5.38 likes on average, and higher use during examination periods.

"The most commonly used feature was the comment feature (87% of all content), which allows active participation in group discussion among the students and between students and instructors."

Wang 2013 (2)¶

Wang, J., Lin, C.-F. C., Yu, W.-C. W., & Wu, E. (2013). Meaningful engagement in Facebook learning environments: Merging social and academic lives. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 14(1), Article 26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006268

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Kolmogorov-Smirnov comparison of daily Facebook usage by gender in the course sample. Females averaged 122 minutes daily versus 103 minutes for males; the sample was 81% female.

"The analysis (z= 4.250; p<.05) also showed a significant difference in the time spent on Facebook across genders: the female respondents spent more time on the site daily compared with the male respondents."
