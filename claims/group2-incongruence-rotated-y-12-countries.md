## Evidence

### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group 2 of the TIMSS 2019 invariance analysis (including Ireland, Israel, Japan, Kazakhstan, Norway, Qatar, South Korea, United States), united on polynomial family 36 with equality constraints on interactive-quadratic effects and the cubic effect of autonomy. The model "contributed to explain 7.8% of the variance".

> "The resulting model contributed to explain 7.8% of the variance (R2 = .072), which represented a 30.9% increase relative to the additive model (R2= .055)."
