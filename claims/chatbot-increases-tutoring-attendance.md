# Chatbot outreach increased supplemental instruction tutoring attendance by about two percentage points, mediating a small share of the grade effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across courses, treated students were about two percentage points more likely to attend supplemental instruction tutoring, with a natural indirect effect on final grades of about 0.32 grade points (17.8% of the total effect). [→ Meyer 2026](#meyer-2026)

## Evidence

### Meyer 2026

Meyer, Katharine, Lindsay C. Page, Catherine Mata, Eric N. Smith, B. Tyler Walsh, C. Lindsey Fifield, Michelle Tyson, Amy Eremionkhale, Michael Evans, Shelby Frost, and Eye Eoun Jung. (2026). Let's Chat: Leveraging Chatbot Outreach for Improved Course Performance. (EdWorkingPaper: 22-564). https://edworkingpapers.com/ai22-564

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ITT analysis of tutoring attendance from the pooled RCT sample, with formal mediation analysis. The natural indirect effect of tutoring on final numeric grades was about 0.32 grade points (p=0.041; 17.8% of the total effect) and 0.004 percentage points on earning a B or higher (p=0.046, 9.6%).

> "across courses, students assigned to treatment are about two percentage points more likely to attend SI (Table 7). These effect sizes are large in relative terms compared to low baseline rates of SI attendance – 7% of students in Government and 2.4% of students in Microeconomics."
