## Evidence

### Brame CJ 2015

Brame CJ, Biel R. (2015). Test-Enhanced Learning: The Potential for Testing to Promote Greater Learning in Undergraduate Science Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-11-0208

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Smith and Karpicke (2014), laboratory experiments in which undergraduates read four ~500-word texts and received one of several retrieval-practice formats or none. The review states "there was no significant difference in the benefits conferred by the different types of retrieval practice," though one experiment found a slight hybrid advantage for verbatim short-answer tests.

> "Retrieval practice with multiple-choice, free-response, and hybrid formats improved students’ performance on a final, delayed test taken 1 wk later when compared with a no-retrieval control."
