Nouira Hajer 2024¶

Nouira Hajer, Jaoued Oussama, Ouanes Islem, Jrad Maissa, Chtioui Soumaya, Gharbi Rim, Fekih Hassen Mohamed, Ben Sik Ali Habiba, Elatrous Souheil. (2024). q3 · i? · review · r2

Kienbacher CL et al.'s randomized controlled trial, reported by the review, comparing CPR quality across no-PPE control, FFP2 with valve, and FFP2 without valve conditions; descriptive means only, equivalence was not statistically tested in the quoted text.

"This randomised controlled trial has revealed that PPE, including masks with and without valves, does not impair CPR quality (mean compression depth was 56 mm (SD=3.7) using no additional PPE (control), 56 mm (SD=3.6) using a FFP2 mask with a valve and 56 mm (SD=3.6) using a FFP2 mask (without a valve)."
