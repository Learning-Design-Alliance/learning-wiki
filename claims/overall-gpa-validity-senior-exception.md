## Evidence

### Bacon 2006

Donald R. Bacon and Beth Bean. (2006). GPA in Research Studies: An Invaluable but Neglected Opportunity. Journal of Marketing Education, Vol. 28 No. 1, April 2006 35-42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0273475305284638

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Predictive-validity analysis in Table 1 of the undergraduate sample: correlations of various end-of-3rd-year GPA metrics with 4th-year course grades. The senior exception is a stated scope limit; the article concludes limited GPAs are more valid "unless the research context is limited to 4th-year students".

> "The 3rd-year marketing-only GPA, with a modest reliability of only .67, correlates .67 with 4th- year marketing course grades, while overall 3rd-year GPA, with a high reliability of .93, correlates .55 with 4th- year mar- keting course grades."
