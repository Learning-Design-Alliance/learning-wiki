`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First-attempt comparison in the randomized controlled trial of 43 medical students; the excellent level was defined as overall performance of 90% or above, and "71.2% versus 23.7%; p<0.001" favored the feedback device.

> "A higher proportion of students achieved "excellent" level with the use of a feedback-equipped device (71.2% versus 23.7%; p<0.001)."
