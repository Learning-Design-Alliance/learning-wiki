`q2 i?` Posttest scores were significantly better than pretest scores on all questions for both 11 undergraduates and 19 graduate students after working through the QuILT. [→ Christof Keebaugh 2017](#christof-keebaugh-2017)

## Evidence

### Christof Keebaugh 2017

Christof Keebaugh, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Developing and evaluating an interactive tutorial on degenerate perturbation theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary evaluation: 11 upper-level undergraduates worked through the tutorial in class plus homework; 19 first-year graduate students did it as weekly homework. Table I reports e.g. undergraduate question 3 rising from 2.6 to 100 (g = 1.00) and graduate question 3 from 11.7 to 86.0 (g = 0.84).

> "The posttest scores are signiﬁcantly better than the pretest scores on all of these questions for both groups."
