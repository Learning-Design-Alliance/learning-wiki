## Evidence

### Allan Pau 2016

Allan Pau, Chen Yu Sui, Lee Verna Kar Mun, Sow Chew Fei, Alwis Ranjit De. (2016). What does the multiple mini interview have to offer over the panel interview? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29874

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

t-test comparison of mean MMI scores by number of stations at which the applicant was flagged as a concern, among 131 applicants. Eleven (8.4%) were flagged at two or more stations; the study reports mean MMI scores of "73.5 (95% CI 71.9–75.1)" versus "61.7 (95% CI 55.1–68.4, p=0.001)".

> "The mean MMI score for those assessed as a concern at one station or none was statistically significantly higher at 73.5 (95% CI 71.9–75.1) compared with those assessed as a concern at two stations or more at 61.7 (95% CI 55.1–68.4, p=0.001)"
