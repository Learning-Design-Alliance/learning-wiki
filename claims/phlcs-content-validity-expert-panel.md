## Evidence

### Chang C-C 2025

Chang C-C, Tseng P-L, Liu C-C, Ming J-L, Fan S-H, Tung C-Y. (2025). Development and validation of the Physician’s Health Literacy Competence Scale: A step towards effective doctor–patient communication. Medicine 104(9): e41643. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041643

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity index assessment by six experts in healthcare decision-making, health literacy, and medical education plus a physician specializing in medical ethics. The article reports CVI of 0.78 for one dimension and above 0.80 for the others, with all items retained after minor wording revisions.

> "Results indicated that the “Concepts and Assessment” dimension had a content validity index of 0.78, while all other dimensions scored above 0.80. Based on these findings, all items were retained, though minor revisions were made to certain items’ wording following expert feedback."
