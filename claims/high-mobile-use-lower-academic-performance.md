## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 501 secondary students grouped by CERM scores; two-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc. The main effect of mobile-use group was significant for "Spanish (p = 0.006), mathematics (p = 0.036) and physical education (p = 0.036)". No effect size reported for this comparison.

> "Without the assumption of the gender effect, significant differences were described for Spanish (p = 0.006), mathematics (p = 0.036) and physical education (p = 0.036), with higher values in the LMP and MMP than in the HMP group"
