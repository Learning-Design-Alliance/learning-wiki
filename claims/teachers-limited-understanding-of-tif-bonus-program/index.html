Evidence¶

Hanley Chiang 2017¶

Hanley Chiang, Cecilia Speroni, Mariesa Herrmann, Kristin Hallgren, Paul Burkander, Alison Wellington. (2017). Evaluation of the Teacher Incentive Fund: Final Report on Implementation and Impacts of Pay-for-Performance Across Four Years. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The report's key findings describe teachers' limited understanding of the bonus program, reporting that "about 40 percent of teachers who were eligible to receive a bonus knew they could" and that perceived maximum bonuses were roughly 40 percent of the actual maximum. No effect size is printed.

"about 40 percent of teachers who were eligible to receive a bonus knew they could, and teachers typically thought the maximum bonus they could earn was about 40 percent of the actual maximum."
