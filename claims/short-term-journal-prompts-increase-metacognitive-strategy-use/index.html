Evidence¶

Nadja M. Gentner 2024¶

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Repeated-measures ANOVA on self-reported metacognitive strategy use (t1–t4) in the standardized part of the learning journals for the whole sample. "The results indicate an increase in metacognitive strategy use over time" with ηp² = 0.30.

"There was again a main effect of time, F(2.49,181.95) = 31.35, p < .001, ηp² = 0.30. The results indicate an increase in metacognitive strategy use over time"
