Evidence¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré 2015¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré, Meredith Welch, and Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). Rising Tide: Do College Grad Rate Gains Benefit All Students? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566656

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Peer-institution comparison from the College Results Online database (Table 3): both institutions have similar median SAT/ACT scores, URM percentages, and enrollments above 15,000. NC State "bump[ed] up graduation rates by 12 points" while Auburn's URM rates declined.

"NC State drastically improved its success for underrepresented students, bumping up graduation rates by 12 points and cutting the gap between them and white students by almost half. Meanwhile, graduation rates for underrepresented students at Auburn declined, and the gap grew to more than 20 points."
