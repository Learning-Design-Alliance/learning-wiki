GLME modeling with number of sessions showed the best model fit for global competence scores. The model predicted "each additional session reduces the failure score (30 – actual score) by 2%" and increased chances of scoring above 28 points by approximately 3%.

> "The model predicts that each additional session reduces the failure score (30 – actual score) by 2% (95% CI, 1%–3%), P < 0.001, and 1 additional session is associated with approximately 3% (95% CI, 2%–5%), P < 0.001, increase"
