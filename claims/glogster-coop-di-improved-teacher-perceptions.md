## Evidence

### Awada and Faour 2016

Ghada M. Awada and Kawthar H. Faour. (2016). Effect of Glogster and Cooperative Learning Differentiated Instruction on Teachers' Perceptions. Teaching English with Technology, 18(2), 93-114. http://www.tewtjournal.org

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-method study with 18 teachers of grade 8 EFL learners in 8 Beirut public schools; pre-treatment interviews were compared with a post-treatment Google Drive survey, indicating a "significant positive change in the perceptions of both science and English teachers". No effect size is printed.

> "The comparison between the data elicited from the conducted survey that was filled out after the implementation of Glogster and cooperative learning differentiated instruction and interview that was conducted prior to the implementation of the treatment indicated significant positive change in the perceptions of both science and English teachers."
