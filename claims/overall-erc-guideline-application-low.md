## Evidence

### Fischer H 2014

Fischer H, Bachmann K, Strunk G, Neuhold S, Zapletal B, Maurer C, Fast A, Stumpf D, Greif R. (2014). Translation of ERC resuscitation guidelines into clinical practice by emergency physicians. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-22-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive findings from the Austria-wide unannounced questionnaire survey (N = 836) show low guideline application: the article reports "less than half of the ERC-ALS providers did not apply therapeutic hypothermia" and an overall intraosseous-access rate of n = 66 (7.9%).

> "Even less than half of the ERC-ALS providers did not apply therapeutic hypothermia in spite of the topic being covered extensively during the ERC-ALS course."
