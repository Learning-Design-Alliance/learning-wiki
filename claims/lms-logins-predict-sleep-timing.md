## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis comparing population-derived LMS login parameters (averaged over five semesters) with student-averaged actigraphy parameters (n = 181) across eight class-start-time categories. The article reports a correlation of r = 0.98 (printed as "r(6) = 0.98, 95% CI = 0.91 to 1.00") for login onset versus sleep offset, with r.m.s.e. = 7 min.

> "However, the LMS login onset closely approximated the sleep offset and these measures were highly correlated (r(6) = 0.98, 95% CI = 0.91 to 1.00, P < 0.001, r.m.s.e. = 7 min)."
