Xiu RL 2026¶

Xiu RL, Aguilar SJ, Macías AJ, Shikanova A, Al-Sulaiti R, Junjunia M and Jebril ST (2026) K-12 in-service teachers’ beliefs about generative AI in classrooms: insights from the United States, India, Qatar, Colombia, and the Philippines. q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple regression (Model 1) on survey data from 1,405 K-12 teachers in five countries, N = 1,204 analyzed. "AI Readiness was the dominant predictor" with B = .59 and the model explained "50% of the variance in Positive AI Impact Beliefs". A sensitivity analysis with a two-item AIR composite yielded B = .15, p < .001.

"The first regression model explained 50% of the variance in Positive AI Impact Beliefs (R2 = .50; p < .001; Table 5), a notably large effect for a cross-national survey study. AI Readiness was the dominant predictor [B = .59, 95% CI (.52, .66), p < .001]"
