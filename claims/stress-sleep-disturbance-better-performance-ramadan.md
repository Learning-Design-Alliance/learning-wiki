## Evidence

### Attash Heba M. 2025

Attash Heba M., Dawood Omar T., Aladul Mohammed I., Essa Narmin S. (2025). Association between ramadan fasting, sleep disturbances, fatigue, and academic performance among Iraqi university students: A cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-24482-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (same 922-student survey; omnibus χ²(14) = 317.09, p < 0.01). The article reports these associations may reflect "increased academic effort rather than hindrances, possibly due to high-achievers' commitment" — an author interpretation, not a tested result.

> "stress or anxiety during fasting (aOR = 1.68, 95% CI [1.14–2.48], p < 0.01) and disrupted sleep schedules (aOR = 2.54, 95% CI [1.48–4.36], p < 0.01) emerged as factors positively associated with high academic performance"
