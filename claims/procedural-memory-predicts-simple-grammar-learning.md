## Evidence

### Antoniou Mark 2016

Antoniou Mark, Ettlinger Marc, Wong Patrick C. M. (2016). Complexity, Training Paradigm Design, and the Contribution of Memory Subsystems to Grammar Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158812

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on the Experiment 1 baseline group (n = 36) examined procedural, declarative and working memory as predictors of simple-rule generalization. The result that "for the simple grammar the only significant predictor was procedural memory" was corroborated by the same pattern in the Experiment 2 no-feedback group (β = .424, p = .035).

> "for the simple grammar the only significant predictor was procedural memory, B = .437, β = .531, R2 = .282, adjusted R2 = .261, p = .001"
