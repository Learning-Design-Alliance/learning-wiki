## Evidence

### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA in a 2×3 factorial experiment (N=55) shows pre-academic performance main effects on achievement in all three modules, e.g. "F = 12.593, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.215" for module 1, with high performers scoring higher.

> "pre-academic performance significantly influenced achievement scores across all three modules (F = 12.593, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.215; F = 5.774, P = 0.020, ηp2 = 0.112; F = 11.914, P = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.206), with high pre-academic performance students scoring higher"

### Li Yawen 2026 (2)

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA results for two of three modules show significant pre-academic performance effects on NASA-TLX subjective load, e.g. "F = 21.692, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.320", with high performers reporting lower load.

> "In the modules “Determining Burn Depth” and “Fluid Therapy for Burns”, pre-academic performance significantly affected subjective cognitive load (F = 21.692, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.320; F = 13.450, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.226), with high pre-academic performing students reporting lower cognitive load"
