## Evidence

### Li Feiyan 2026

Li Feiyan, Zhao Haojing, Huang Qiqiao, Zhang Chunhui, Chen Geyu. (2026). Development and validation of a competency-based evaluation framework for clinical medical trainees in the era of artificial intelligence: a mixed-methods study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08779-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of the 72-item instrument administered via a digital testing platform to 276 valid respondents (residents, postgraduates, educators). Cronbach's α = 0.928, KMO = 0.884, and Bartlett's test of sphericity χ2 = 421.35, df = 28, p < 0.001, 'confirming that the correlation matrix was appropriate for further structural exploration.'

> "Reliability analysis demonstrated excellent internal consistency, with a Cronbach's α of 0.928, exceeding the threshold widely accepted as evidence of strong reliability in educational measurement."
