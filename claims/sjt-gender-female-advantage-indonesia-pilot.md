### Soemantri Diantha 2022

Soemantri Diantha, Findyartini Ardi, Yolanda Sophie, Morley Emma, Patterson Fiona. (2022). Evaluation of Situational Judgment Tests in student selection in Indonesia and the impact on diversity issues. BMC Medical Education, 22:239. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03247-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results-section analysis of pilot SJT scores from two applicant cohorts, using independent t-tests for gender. The article reports that significant differences were found between male and female candidates in 2017 and 2018 (p = 0.000), with females performing better than males; no effect size is printed in this sentence.

> "Regarding gender, significant differences were found between male and female candidates in 2017 and 2018 (p = 0.000) with females performing better than males."
