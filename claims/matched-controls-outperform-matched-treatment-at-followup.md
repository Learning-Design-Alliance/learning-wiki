## Evidence

### Bailey 2015

Bailey, D. H., Nguyen, T., Jenkins, J. M., Domina, T., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2015). Fadeout in an Early Mathematics Intervention: Same Old Schools or Underlying Skills? SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched-sample analysis comparing treatment children to a control subset with similar post-test REMA scores. The article reports the matched control group "significantly outperformed the matched treatment group at the follow-up assessment (p < .001)", a pattern consistent with the underlying skills model.

> "In the matched sample, children in both groups performed identically at the post-test assessment, and the matched control group significantly outperformed the matched treatment group at the follow-up assessment (p < .001; Figure 3)."
