### Heskin L. 2021

Heskin L., Simms C., Traynor O., Galvin R. (2021). Designing a synthetic simulator to teach open surgical skills for limb exploration in trauma: a qualitative study exploring the experiences and perspectives of educators and surgical trainees. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01417-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In Theme 1 of the interview study, participants named apprenticeship-model preference, low fidelity and high expense as barriers; access, logistics and trainer time in the operating room were also cited.

> "They highlighted reasons for lack of buy-in or resistance to simulation may be the belief that the Halstead apprenticeship model is better and some may feel that simulation is simply not like the real thing, that the fidelity is low and the expense is high."
