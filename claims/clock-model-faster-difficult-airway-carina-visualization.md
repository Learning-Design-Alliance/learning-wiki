## Evidence

### Zhou Z 2024

Zhou Z, Zhang K, Zhao X, Hu Y, He Y, Wan L, Yao W. (2024). Evaluation of a 12-hole clock model for improving bronchoscopic skills in simulated normal and difficult airways among anesthesia residents: A randomized controlled study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000038510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 43 anesthesia residents randomized to clock-model (n = 21) or manikin (n = 22) training; posttraining difficult-airway test medians favored the intervention group, "shorter in the intervention group than in the control group" (P = .0425). No effect size printed.

> "However, in difficult airways, the time to visualization of the carina was shorter in the intervention group than in the control group (24.0 [16.0, 32.0] s vs 27.0 [21.0, 35.5] s, P = .0425)."
