Evidence¶

Fancsali 2016¶

Fancsali, C., Abe, Y., & Pyatigorsky, M. (2016). Developing Content-Area Literacy: The Reading Apprenticeship Improving Secondary Education (RAISE) project: A Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566986

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Year-2 teacher survey impacts from the 42-school cluster RCT (Exhibit 3, N = 206 teachers): fostering student independence ES = 0.51, students practicing metacognitive inquiry ES = 0.46, students practicing comprehension strategies ES = 0.62, student collaboration ES = 0.47, and confidence in literacy instruction ES = 0.41, all significant.

"RAISE had statistically significant impacts on teachers' use of core Reading Apprenticeship practices and on their confidence in delivering literacy instruction with effect sizes ranging from 0.41 to 0.62."

Related Claims¶

  • By the end of year 2, RAISE significantly improved science students' literacy assessment scores (ES = 0.32), with a non-significant meaningful effect in ELA (0.22) and no effect in history — related
  • RAISE produced significant positive impacts on student metacognitive conversations (ES = 0.21) and integration of content and literacy activity (ES = 0.18), with other student mediating outcomes positive but not significant — related
  • RAISE's impact on the full literacy-assessment sample was positive but not statistically significant (ES = 0.11), which the authors attribute to possibly insufficient sample size — related
