## Evidence

### Jiang N 2024

Jiang N, Zhang Y, Liang S, Lyu X, Chen S, Huang X, Pan H. (2024). Effectiveness of Virtual Simulations Versus Mannequins and Real Persons in Medical and Nursing Education: Meta-Analysis and Trial Sequential Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/56195

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 8 RCTs assessing knowledge, conducted per Cochrane methodology. The pooled SMD=0.08 (95% CI –0.30 to 0.47) was not significant; the "inner wedge" area had been reached, indicating strong evidence the insignificant result would persist. Leave-one-out analysis was consistent.

> "the pooled effect size did not reflect a significant difference between VSs and mannequins or RPs on the knowledge outcome (SMD=0.08; 95% CI –0.30 to 0.47; I2=67%; P=.002)"
