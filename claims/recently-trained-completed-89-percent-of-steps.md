### Ansquer Raihei 2019

Ansquer Raihei, Mesnier Thomas, Farampour Farnam, Oriot Denis, Ghazali Daniel Aiham. (2019). Long-term retention assessment after simulation-based-training of pediatric procedural skills among adult emergency physicians: a multicenter observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1793-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of the percentage of expected procedural steps completed by each group (Fig. 2 with accompanying text). BVM ventilation and pulse checking were the most frequently performed steps cohort-wide (93 and 79%); gastric tube insertion was least performed (29%). Percentages are descriptive; no effect size printed.

> "Overall, the G1 participants performed 89% of the procedural steps that were expected in the scenario. In contrast, the G4 and C participants performed less than 50% of them."
