## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Class-averaged normalized LSCI gains did not differ statistically between three required-textbook classes and one optional-textbook class. [→ Rudolph 2013](#rudolph-2013)

## Evidence

### Rudolph 2013

Rudolph, A. L. (2013). Do You Always Need a Textbook to Teach Astro 101?. Astronomy Education Review. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The LSCI was administered pre- and post-instruction; class-averaged normalized gains were <g> = 0.47, 0.34, 0.34 (N = 33, 28, 36) for required-textbook classes versus <g> = 0.38 (N = 75) for the optional-textbook class. Demographics of the classes showed no statistical difference before and after the change.

> "A 2-sample t-test assuming equal variance with α = 0.01 comparing these two groups (required versus optional text) showed no statistical difference between these groups (two-tailed p = 0.693)."
