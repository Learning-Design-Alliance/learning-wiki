# CSC coaching significantly reduced teachers' need for change in behavior management practices (BMS discrepancy scores, d = .68)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` BMS Rating Scale total discrepancy scores were significantly smaller at postintervention than baseline, a medium effect. [→ Dudek et al. 2019](#dudek-et-al-2019)

## Evidence

### Dudek et al. 2019

Christopher M. Dudek, MEd, Linda A. Reddy, PhD, Adam Lekwa, PhD, Anh N. Hua, PhD, and Gregory A. Fabiano, PhD. (2019). Improving Universal Classroom Practices Through Teacher Formative Assessment and Coaching. Assessment for Effective Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508418772919

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t test on BMS Rating Scale total discrepancy scores for the 32 waitlist teachers. The article reports "t (30) = 3.84, p < .001, d = .68", characterized as a medium effect.

> "Similarly for BMS discrepancy scores, teachers had significantly smaller discrepancy scores at postintervention assessment (M = 17.48, SD = 12.98) than at baseline assessment (M = 26.19, SD = 14.36), t (30) = 3.84, p < .001, d = .68."
