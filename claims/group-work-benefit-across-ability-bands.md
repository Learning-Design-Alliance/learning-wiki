## Evidence

### Anastasia Sofroniou and Konstantinos Poutos 2016

Anastasia Sofroniou and Konstantinos Poutos. (2016). Investigating the Effectiveness of Group Work in Mathematics. Educ. Sci. 2016, 6, 30. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030030

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis reported with Figure 6, clustering students by overall exam performance in 20% intervals to assess group work effectiveness for different academic strength levels; the article notes the trend was similar throughout the categories.

> "The results illustrate the beneficial effect of group work for almost all student categories. For example, students achieving a final result between 60% and 80% in their exam presented an average mark of 70.2% in the integration section when working in groups. In the Control class this percentage corresponded to 64%."
