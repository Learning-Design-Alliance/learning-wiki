## Evidence

### Chang 2022

Chang, M., Brainerd, C.J. (2022). Association and dissociation between judgments of learning and memory: A Meta-analysis of the font size effect. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09287-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analyses (univariate and multivariate meta-regressions) identified four boundary conditions. The article states larger fonts "only reliably increased both JOLs and memory accuracy" under these four conditions; no per-moderator effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "larger font sizes only reliably increased both JOLs and memory accuracy (a) when font sizes ranged from intermediate to very large, (b) when study materials were unrelated word lists, (c) when JOLs were solicited immediately after encoding, and (d) when study time was relatively brief."
