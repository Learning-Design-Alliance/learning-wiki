Independent-samples t-test comparing the 255 poor- and 60 good-sleep-quality students (Table 3); the same analysis found GPA did not differ (p = 0.316), and no GPA equivalence test was reported. Screen time also did not differ (p = 0.565).

> "In contrast, the most recent exam grade was significantly higher in students with good sleep quality (75.3 ± 14.0) compared to those with poor sleep quality (71.1 ± 14.5; p = 0.042)."
