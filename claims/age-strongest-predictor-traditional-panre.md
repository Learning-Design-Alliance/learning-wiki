## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bivariate analysis across age groups in the 80,553-PA cohort, reported in Results and Figure 1: selection "monotonically increased with age (P < .001; Figure 1)". The regression printed "aOR 5.36; P < .001" for PAs 65 and older versus younger than 35; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The likelihood of selecting the traditional PANRE, however, monotonically increased with age (P < .001; Figure 1). The majority of PAs age 65 years and older (64.5%) chose the traditional PANRE."
