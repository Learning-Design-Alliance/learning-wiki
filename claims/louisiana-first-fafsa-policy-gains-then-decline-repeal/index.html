EdTrust's analysis of Louisiana public high school FAFSA rates shows the first-year gains under the 2018 Board of Education requirement: "an 11.3% increase in submissions and a 9.7% increase in completions." Descriptive rates only.

"FAFSA submission and completion rates peaked in the first year of implementation for the class of 2018, with an 11.3% increase in submissions and a 9.7% increase in completions."
