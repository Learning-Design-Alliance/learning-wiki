## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Time-sampled coding of learners' comments in collaborative and observational phases, classified along elicitation, externalisation, and consensus building categories (Cohen's κ=.72 between raters).

> "Regarding consensus building, scripted learners were more involved in negotiation processes than were learners in the control condition, t(15.81)=4.63, p<.001, d=1.63."

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018 (2)

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same coded-comment analysis; externalisations also showed a significant difference, t(15.96)=2.66, p=.017, d=0.97, with scripted learners sharing more thoughts with peers.

> "Regarding elicitations, learners who were supported with the script asked significantly more questions than did learners in the control condition, t(16)=2.47, p=.025, d=1.30."
