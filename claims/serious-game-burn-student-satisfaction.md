`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Student satisfaction ANCOVA from the randomized trial (Table 4): adjusted difference "favored the intervention group by 2.02 points 95% CI : 1.40 to 2.63" with printed effect size η² = 0.391, corrected for multiple comparisons.

> "Post intervention student satisfaction increased in both groups but were significantly higher in the intervention group (7.51 ± 1.54) compared to the control group (5.48 ± 0.95). The ANCOVA, revealed a statistically significant difference (P-value < 0.001). The adjusted mean difference favored the intervention group by 2.02 points 95% CI : 1.40 to 2.63, representing an effect size of 0.391"
