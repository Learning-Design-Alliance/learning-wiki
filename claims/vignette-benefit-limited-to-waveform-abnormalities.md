## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2021

Viljoen Charle André, Millar Rob Scott, Manning Kathryn, Hoevelmann Julian, Burch Vanessa Celeste. (2021). Clinically contextualised ECG interpretation: the impact of prior clinical exposure and case vignettes on ECG diagnostic accuracy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02854-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analyses of the within-participant test comparisons, split by arrhythmias versus waveform abnormalities. Junior students "were not influenced by case vignettes when interpreting arrhythmias", while "all groups showed an increase in diagnostic accuracy" for waveform abnormalities; Table 2 shows per-condition p-values (e.g. RVH junior p < 0.001).

> "As shown in Fig. 4B, subgroup analyses showed that junior students were not influenced by case vignettes when interpreting arrhythmias. Although statistically significant, senior students and residents showed an increase of only 3 percentage units in their scores of the arrhythmia section of the tests, when they were provided with case vignettes. All groups showed an increase in diagnostic accuracy when they were provided with a case vignette for ECGs with abnormal waveforms (Fig. 4C)."
