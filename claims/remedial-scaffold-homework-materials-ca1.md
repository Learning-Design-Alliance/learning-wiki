David James Woo, Deliang Wang, Kai Guo. (2026). AI Slop or AI-enhancement? Student Perceptions of AI-generated Media for an English for Academic Purposes Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of material-usage preferences against CA1 scores in the mixed-methods study. The authors interpret that struggling students "turned to homework feedback materials to catch up, rather than the materials causing low scores"; adoption was independent, without instructor guidance.

> "These students relied heavily on “Homework reports and videos” and showed a strong negative correlation with CA1 scores (-0.478) (see Figure 6). The implication is that these students who struggled in CA1 turned to homework feedback materials to catch up, rather than the materials causing low scores."
