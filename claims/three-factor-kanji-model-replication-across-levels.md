## Evidence

### Otsuka 2020

Otsuka, S., & Murai, T. (2020). The multidimensionality of Japanese kanji abilities. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-59852-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Replicability CFAs were run on seven additional datasets from Levels 2, Pre-2, 3 and 4 in 2006 and 2016, with the article reporting that "the CFI, TLI, and SRMR values with these data indicated a preferred fit for the three-dimensional model" and composite reliabilities of 0.86 to 1.02 for writing accuracy.

> "the RMSEA estimated with the Level 2 data of the exam implemented at non-public sites at the later date in 2006 (0.43) and at both dates in 2016 (0.040, 0.054, respectively) and the Level 3 data at public sites in 2016 (0.049) were significantly low (all p close > 0.05), and acceptable in all other cases"
