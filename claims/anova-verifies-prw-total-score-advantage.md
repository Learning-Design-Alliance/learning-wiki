# A one-way ANOVA on the same data verified the t-test finding that the experimental group outperformed the control group on total marks

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` A separate one-way ANOVA on total marks confirmed the experimental group performed significantly better than the control group. [→ Wickramaarachchi 2014](#wickramaarachchi-2014)

`q2 i?` First-year ESL undergraduates who engaged in prw tasks before reading performed significantly better on total inferential comprehension scores than students who performed tasks without a pre-reading component. [→ Wickramaarachchi 2014](#wickramaarachchi-2014)

## Evidence

### Wickramaarachchi 2014

Wickramaarachchi, T.I. (2014). Writing-Reading Relationships: Effectiveness of Writing Activities as Pre-Reading Tasks to Enhance L2 Inferential Reading Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(5). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.5p.213

`q2 · i?`

One-way ANOVA verification analysis on the same total-mark data (Table 3 reports Between Groups Sum of Squares 123.245, df 1, F 9.42, Sig. .004). The article states the test "verified the findings of the Independent two samples t-test". No effect size is printed.

> "Based on the findings of the independent samples t-test and the one-way ANOVA test, in considering the total marks of all the students, the students of the experimental group have performed significantly better than the students from the control group."
