Systematic content review of 35 studies on emotional design in pictorial representations, coded within and between studies. The review reports "a substantial heterogeneity in emotional design implementations, especially in terms of visual sophistication and anthropomorphic elements."

Related Claims¶

  • Anthropomorphism is the most common emotional design feature, implemented in 23 of 35 reviewed articles (66%) — related
  • Visual sophistication of emotional design materials varies substantially and may act as a boundary condition for emotional design effects — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • A conceptual framework paper reports, citing a meta-analysis (Roesler et al., 2021), that anthropomorphism benefits human-robot interaction with effects varying across task contexts and design implementations — related
  • Among human-cell studies, only 5 of 14 found the expected positive effect of high emotional design on retention and 5 on transfer; only 5 of 9 studies measuring emotions found the expected effect on positive emotions — related
  • Multimedia design was a frequently reported facilitator, and one study found e-modules with more sophisticated visual and interactive features led to stronger learning outcomes — related
