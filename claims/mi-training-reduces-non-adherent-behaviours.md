## Evidence

### Svensson C 2020

Svensson C, Wickström H, Emanuelson U, Bard AM, Reyher KK, Forsberg L. (2020). Training in motivational interviewing improves cattle veterinarians' communication skills for herd health management. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1136/vr.105646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive pre/post comparison of MITI-coded role-play recordings from 31 veterinarians in the 6-month MI training programme. The article reports 22 (71 per cent) veterinarians showed high numbers of MI-non-adherent Behaviours before training whereas none did after; the proportion improving was highest for Persuade, Giving Information and MI-non-adherent Behaviours.

> "22 (71 per cent) veterinarians demonstrated high numbers of MI-non-adherent Behaviours (more than 8 statements per 20 minutes) before training whereas none reached this level after training."
