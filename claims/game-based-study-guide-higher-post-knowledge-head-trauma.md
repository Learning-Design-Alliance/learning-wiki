## Evidence

### Bashiri Bonab Esmaeil 2025

Bashiri Bonab Esmaeil, Ghafouri Raziyeh, Moosavi Soolmaz, Nasiri Malihe. (2025). Effectiveness of a digital game based study guide on head trauma assessment on knowledge and practical skills of nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08122-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental two-group comparison of 93 eighth-semester nursing students; independent samples t-test on post-intervention knowledge. The intervention group mean was 5.59 versus 4.86 in the control group, with "t =−3.23 df = 91 p = 0.002" and printed effect size d = 0.68 (reported as 0/68, 95% CI 0.26–1.10).
