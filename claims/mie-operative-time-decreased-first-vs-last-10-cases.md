`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of initial 10 versus final 10 cases (Fig. 4) in the retrospective cohort; the article reports significant decreases in operative time for both surgeons, each at p < 0.001.

> "The OT of the trainer decreased from 436,4(± 34,77) to 331,4(± 72,00) (p < 0.001), while the OT of the trainee decreased from 504,4 (± 45,20) to 369,3(± 78,20) (p < 0.001)."
