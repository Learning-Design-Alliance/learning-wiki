## Evidence

### Kiyani Amber 2025

Kiyani Amber, Hanif Fariha, Muhammad Muhammad, Iqbal Sana, Zaib Nadia, Bashir Ulfat, Ali Kamran. (2025). Benchmarking ChatGPT-generated multiple-choice questions against faculty-authored items in dental education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-28492-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects formative assessment of 547 undergraduate dental students across four institutions; a paired samples t-test compared mean item scores of ChatGPT-developed and human-developed tests, reporting "a statistically significant difference between the two; t(545) = 19.22, p < 0.001".

> "Items developed by ChatGPT had a lower average score (Mean = 9.01) compared to those by Human experts (Mean = 11.6), suggesting the AI-developed items were relatively more difficult (Table 3)."
