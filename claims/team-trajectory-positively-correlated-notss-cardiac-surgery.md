## Evidence

### Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD 2024

Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD, Dias Roger D. MD PhD, Kennedy-Metz Lauren R. PhD, Varni Giovanna PhD, Gombolay Matthew PhD, Yule Steven PhD, Salas Eduardo PhD, Zenati Marco A. MD. (2024). Deep Learning Analysis of Surgical Video Recordings to Assess Nontechnical Skills. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.22520

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of OpenPose-derived motion features from 30 cardiac surgery procedures at a tertiary academic US hospital against team-level NOTSS ratings by 3 trained raters during the separation-from-bypass phase. The correlation "trajectory: r = 0.51; P = .005" was significant at the printed P < .05 threshold.

> "The average trajectory and acceleration of team displacement showed a positive correlation with overall NOTSS scores (trajectory: r = 0.51; P = .005; acceleration: r = 0.48; P = .008)."
