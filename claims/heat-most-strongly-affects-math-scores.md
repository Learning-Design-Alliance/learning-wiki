`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Large-scale observational analysis combining daily temperature data with MAP Growth scores for grades 3–8 across six states, using roughly three million test events from fall 2022, 2023 and 2024. The brief reports "higher temperatures most strongly affect math scores". No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "the analysis finds that higher temperatures most strongly affect math scores, with students in high-poverty schools facing the greatest impact."
