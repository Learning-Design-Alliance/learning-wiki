Conceptual argument in the introduction, attributing the cognitive debt construct to Bozkurt et al. (2026) and the ghost student phenomenon to the same source. The article offers no empirical data for this claim; it is a theoretical argument motivating the framework.

"When generative AI substitutes for student thinking rather than supporting it, learners risk accumulating what Bozkurt et al. ( 2026 ) describe as “cognitive debt,” a condition in which the productive struggle essential to deep learning is quietly outsourced to automated systems."
