## Evidence

### Schmidt Thorsten 2020

Schmidt Thorsten, Gazou Anastasia, Rieß Angelika, Rieß Olaf, Grundmann-Hauser Kathrin, Falb Ruth, Schadeck Malou, Heinrich Tilman, Abeditashi Mahkameh, Schmidt Jana, Mau-Holzmann Ulrike A., Schnabel Kai P. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary subgroup analysis of the anonymous questionnaire (n = 114) comparing male and female students on technical difficulties and conformity in answering. The authors observed no major differences between young and older students on the evaluated items.

> "While only 7% of male students (1.45 ± 0.1 on a 6-point Likert scale) reported technical difficulties when using eduVote, 24% of female students had technical difficulties (2.11 ± 0.15) (p = 0.017). Interestingly, while 36% (4.07 ± 0.1) of male students reported to typically join the majority when a teacher asks a question, only 15% of female students (4.62 ± 0.15) do so (p = 0.019)."
