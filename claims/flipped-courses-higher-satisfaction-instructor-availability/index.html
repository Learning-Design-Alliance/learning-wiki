Evidence¶

Gross 2015¶

Gross, B., Hoffman, M., Marinari, M., DeSimone, K., & Burke, P. (2015). Flipped @ SBU: Student satisfaction and the college classroom. Educational Research Quarterly, 39(2), 37-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1166718

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Survey comparison using six items from the University Student Satisfaction and Time Management Questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha = .886). The authors found "modest but consistent evidence that flipped courses were superior" across all six items.

"Here, we again found modest but consistent evidence that flipped courses were superior, scoring slightly higher than traditional lecture-based classes on all six items, with a significantly higher score found for the belief that the course instructor was more available in a flipped class."
