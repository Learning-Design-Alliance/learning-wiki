Evidence¶

Jingjit 2015¶

Mathukorn Jingjit. (2015). The Effects of Multimedia Learning on Thai Primary Pupils' Achievement in Size and Depth of Vocabulary Knowledge. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.32. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083500

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with time-series design; 42 third-grade pupils at Municipal School 2, Hadyai, took picture vocabulary tests (size) weekly. The article reports a statistically significant difference across T1, T2, and T3 with "p-value = 0.000"; no effect size is printed.

"The test of difference means provides the evidence that pupils' achievement derived from T1, T2, and T3 is significant different (p-value = 0.000), leading to conclude that the multimedia programme positivel y impact on the improvement of pupils' achievement in size of vocabulary knowledge."
