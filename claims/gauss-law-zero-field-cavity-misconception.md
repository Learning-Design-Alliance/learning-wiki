### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Problem Solving and Learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06352](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06352)

Interview data from the case study: all students identified Gauss's law as the correct principle but none could solve the sphere-with-cavity problem without help. The article reports "the same incorrect answer: the electric ﬁeld is zero everywhere inside the cavity", attributing it to ignoring the asymmetric charge distribution.

> "Except for one student who came up with a different incorrect answer, the rest of thestudents came up with the same incorrect answer: the electric ﬁeld is zero everywhere inside the cavity."
