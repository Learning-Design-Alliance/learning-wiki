## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-by-item comparison of Transformed vs Traditional courses from the matched first-year E-CLASS data set, tested with Mann-Whitney U and Holm-Bonferroni correction. The article reports "16 of the 30 E-CLASS items" favor Transformed courses post-instruction; no effect size is printed for this count.

> "16 of the 30 E-CLASS items show Transformed courses scoring signiﬁcantly better at the end of the course (Mann-Whitney U [21] and Holm-Bonferroni [23] corrected p < 0.05)."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Same item-wise analysis of the matched E-CLASS data set; effect sizes are printed per item, with moderate differences (d > 0.3) on Item 14 and Item 23 and "small for all other items ( d < 0.3)".

> "The magnitude of the diﬀerence between post-instruction scores was moderate (Cohen's d > 0.3) for only two of these items (Items 14 – “When doing an experiment, I usually think up my own ques- tions to investigate” and item 23 – “When I am doing an experiment, I try to make predictions to see if my results are reasonable”) and small for all other items ( d < 0.3)."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (3)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "Students in Traditional classes did not have signiﬁcantly higher post-instruction means on any items."
