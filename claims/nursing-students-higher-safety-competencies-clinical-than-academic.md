## Evidence

### Kohanová Dominika 2026

Kohanová Dominika, Gurková Elena, Palese Alvisa, Bartoníčková Daniela, Mazalová Lenka, Özsaban Aysel, Bayram Aysun, Basaran‐Acil Seher, Ispir Demir Öznur, Chiappinotto Stefania. (2026). Patient Safety Competencies, Clinical Learning Environment and Unfinished Care From the Perspective of Nursing Students: A Multinational Study. Journal of Clinical Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jocn.70379

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1442 nursing students in four countries using paired-samples t-tests on H-PEPSS domain scores. The study found "Statistically significant differences were observed for five of the six domains" with Cohen's d values "ranged from negligible to small (|d| = 0.02–0.27)".

> "Statistically significant differences were observed for five of the six domains, whereas no difference was identified for ‘Patient safety culture’ (p = 0.445). However, effect size analysis indicated that the magnitude of these differences was small."
