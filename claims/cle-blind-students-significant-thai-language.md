## Evidence

### Rattanavich 2015

Rattanavich, S. (2015). Concentrated Language Encounter Approach in Practice for Global Teaching of Literacy: Lighthouse Strategy Implementation. International Education Studies, 8(7), 21. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v8n7p21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study with a control group pretest and post-test design at the Bangkok School for the Blind, using a pragmatic test of Thai language use, observation with reliability of 0.80 to 1.00, and t-tests and Chi-Square via SPSS. The article reports CLE students "performed significantly better in Thai language development" at the .01 level; no effect size is printed.

> "The findings revealed that (with the exception of one small group in kindergarten) the CLE students performed significantly better in Thai language development than those taught through traditional instruction at a .01 level of confidence."
