Item-level analysis of the 36-item FSS administered after each of the two HFS courses to the same 43 practitioners. The article reports these items as significantly higher at M2 (p-values printed per item in Table 2, e.g. Pi4 P = 0.038); no effect size is printed.

> "higher Pi4 (it was really clear to me how my performance was going), Pi7 (I was not concerned about what others might be thinking of me), Pi13 (I was aware of how well I was performing)"
