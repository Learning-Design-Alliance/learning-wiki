## Evidence

### Kubo Taro 2022

Kubo Taro, Takayama Tatsuya, Fujisaki Akira, Nakamura Shigeru, Teratani Takumi, Sata Naohiro, Kitayama Joji, Nakai Hideo, Iwami Daiki, Fujimura Tetsuya. (2022). Novel high-quality and reality biomaterial as a kidney surgery simulation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263179

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tensiometer towing tests on the four kidney model types at 10 mm and 15 mm markings; at 10 mm the NVP-fixed kidney (347 ± 38.45 cN) was closest to fresh (383.25 ± 144.89 cN). The article concludes the NVP-fixed kidney had "the most suitable towing value close to the fresh kidney".

> "When the marking was 10 mm, the tension during traction was 383.25 ± 144.89 cN, 139.75 ± 49.26 cN, 149.25 ± 55.76 cN, and 347 ± 38.45 cN for the fresh, freeze-thaw, FA-fixed, and NVP-fixed kidney, respectively."
