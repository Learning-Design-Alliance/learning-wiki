Evidence¶

Oluwadare 2015¶

Oluwadare, F. A. (2015). ICT Use in Preschool Science Education: A Case Study of Some Private Nursery Schools in Ekiti State. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(31). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083397

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test comparing pre- and post-test EMSAT scores within the experimental (ICT) group of 132 children. Table 2 prints t = -29.030, df = 131, mean difference -9.530, significance 0.000; no effect size is printed.

"The mean grade for the pre-test of the study was 25.80 (SD= 4.30) compared to 35.30 (SD= 2.41) of the post-test. At α = .05 and df = 131, the critical value of the t ratio was less than 0.001"
