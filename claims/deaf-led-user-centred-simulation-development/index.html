Evidence¶

Terry Julia 2025¶

Terry Julia, Wilks Rachel, Davies Joanne. (2025). Simulated learning interventions to improve communication and practice with deaf and hard of hearing patients: a systematic review and qualitative synthesis. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10452-5

q3 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative synthesis of intervention development across the six studies. Two studies were Deaf-led/user-centred; two included one team member with lived experience of deafness but "lacked detail about roles"; two were developed solely by faculty staff. Anderson and Blok "began projects by collecting data from Deaf communities in focus groups and forums".

"All studies detailed how they developed and designed the Deaf patient simulation, with two studies described as Deaf-led/user-centred showing involvement from initial project stages where there was consultation and data gathering with Deaf communities"
