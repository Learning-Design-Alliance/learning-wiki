### Aiken 2013

Aiken, J. M. (2013). Transforming High School Physics with Modeling and Computation. Georgia State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.3725](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.3725)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An informal survey at the start of the year queried students' at-home access to computing devices and prior exposure to programming (N=36 in Table 7). The thesis also reports that "86% of the students had little to no experience with programming," with the second most common programming response being a two-week QBASIC activity in seventh grade.

> "Most of the students (91%) reported that they were either "comfortable" with the computer or "very comfortable" with the computer."
