## Evidence

### Krieglstein 2025

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Wesenberg, L., Rey, G. D., & Schneider, S. (2025). The Distorting Influence of Primacy Effects on Reporting Cognitive Load in Learning Materials of Varying Complexity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09980-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVAs on during-learning GCL ratings. Increasing condition: F(1.82, 94.66) = 43.55, ηp² = 0.46 (Huynh–Feldt correction, ε = 0.88); descriptives confirmed a decrease, so hypothesis 3a was not confirmed. Decreasing condition: F(2, 92) = 44.63, ηp² = 0.49; descriptives confirmed an increase, disconfirming 3b.

> "a repeated-measures ANOVA including the three GCL assessments during learning was significant, F(1.82, 94.66) = 43.55, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.46."
