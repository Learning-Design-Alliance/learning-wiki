# Students reported that continued interaction with a cGPT improved its adherence to instructions

## Subclaims

### Lo S 2026

Lo S, Lea T, Mavromoustakos A, Joseph B, Yii M, Exintaris B, Karunaratne N, Pearson DL, Ritchie T, Yuriev E and Pyun J (2026) Co-creating custom GPTs: an autoethnographic study of undergraduate students as partners in generative AI innovation. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Inductive thematic analysis of summer-scholarship SPAR logs. Students anticipated outcomes of language choices better with continued engagement, reporting that "the more times the cGPT is used, the more consistently it may adhere to the instructions". The article presents this as a student interpretation, not a tested result.
