Descriptive platform statistics reported in this policy report: more than 5,000 submissions from educators in approximately 550 districts, over 3,400 unique users, and "about 55 percent" of electronic-portfolio submitters awarded. No inferential test is reported; figures are descriptive counts.

"As of late July 2017, there have been more than 5,000 submissions on the platform from educators in approximately 550 school districts across the nation. Over 3,400 unique users have applied for at least one micro-credential, and about 55 percent of those who submitted their required “electronic portfolio” of evidence have been awarded a micro-credential."
