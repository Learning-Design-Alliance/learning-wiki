## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe 2015

Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe. (2015). The impact of social integration on student persistence in introductory Modeling Instruction courses. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate logistic regression on students in a Fall 2014 mechanics Modeling Instruction course (73 enrolled), predicting persistence in any second-semester physics from centrality measures; Table I reports estimates including total degree 0.20 (p = 0.016), indegree 0.37 (p = 0.018) and closeness 113.36 (p = 0.037). The article reports "statistically signiﬁcant positivecorrelationswithadegree, indegreeandcloseness for persistence in physics."
