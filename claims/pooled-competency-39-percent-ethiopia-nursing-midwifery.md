`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of nine institutional-based cross-sectional studies (2706 participants) using random-effects models in STATA version 17; the pooled proportion was "39.02% (95% CI: 29.95–48.09)" with I2 = 96.14%. The article notes the count discrepancy between '10 studies' here and nine included studies elsewhere.

> "Among the 10 studies, the pooled proportion of clinical practice competency among midwifery and nursing students was 39.02% (95% CI: 29.95–48.09). There was significant heterogeneity between the studies, with p = 0.00, τ 2 = 183.62, I 2 = 96.14% and H 2 = 25.92"
