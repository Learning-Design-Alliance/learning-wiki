Repeated measures analysis of the five dimensions in the same 21-student cohort. The article reports significant gains in all five dimensions, with teaching content improving from 17.14 ± 1.98 to 23.86 ± 1.80 and logical structure from 16.71 ± 1.15 to 23.52 ± 1.50.

> "Notably, the largest improvements were observed in teaching content and logical structure, suggesting that the training was particularly effective in enhancing knowledge accuracy and instructional clarity."
