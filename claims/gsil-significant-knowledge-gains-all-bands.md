## Evidence

### Jong et al. 2017

Morris Siu-Yung Jong, Tom Chan, Vincent Tam and Ming-Tak Hue. (2017). Gamifying Outdoor Social Inquiry Learning with Context-Aware Technology. International Conference Educational Technologies 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579295

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental study compared the gamified SIL (GSIL) approach with the conventional SIL approach in terms of students' knowledge acquisition, involving "a total of 373 Grade-10 students from top, middle, and bottom academic-banding schools" (128 high-, 127 moderate-, 118 low-achieving). Effect sizes were 0.30, 0.62 and 1.04 respectively.

> "Results indicated that, compared to the conventional approach, GSIL had different degrees of significant positive effects on the high, moderate, and low academic-achieving students (Cohen's d effect sizes: 0.30, 0.62, and 1.04)."
