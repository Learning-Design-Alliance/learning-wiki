## Evidence

### Kristin Kolloff 2025

Kristin Kolloff, Ebru Ger, Claudia M. Roebers. (2025). The role of metacognitive experience: Can feedback training affect kindergarteners' monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09422-4

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of within-person Gamma correlations between choice latency and recognition correctness in 214 kindergarteners confirmed cue validity (OR = 1.20, p < 0.01). Children responded faster on correct than incorrect answers at both measurement points.

> "Results revealed that retrieval fluency as a cue was available for the children, both pretest and posttest (γ = 0.18, SE = 0.07, OR = 1.20, z = 2.76, p < 0.01; see Appendix Figure D1)."
