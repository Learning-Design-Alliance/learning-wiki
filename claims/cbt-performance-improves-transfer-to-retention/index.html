Evidence¶

Stanton 1990¶

Stanton, N. A. and Stammers, R. B. (1990). A comparison of structured and unstructured navigation through a CBT package. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ414518

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the same 40-participant laboratory experiment, ANOVA showed significant within-group improvement from the transfer task to the retention task one week later in both the unstructured condition (F1.38 = 11.258, P < 0.01) and the structured condition (F1.38 = 6.468, P < 0.05).

"Both groups performance improved significantly from the transfer task to the retention task (Unstructured condition F1.38 = 11.258, P < 0.01; Structured condition F1.38 = 6.468, P < 0.05)."
