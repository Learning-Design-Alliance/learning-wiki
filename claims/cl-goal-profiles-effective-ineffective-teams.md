`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Content analysis of stimulated-recall group interviews, with goal statements categorized and profile congruence computed (Lemos's coefficients). The article reports "a moderate congruity" (C = 0.55) and "no conﬁgurative similarity" (ES = 0.09) between the two profiles.

> "The goal proﬁles of students in effective and ineffective CL teams showed a moderate congruity (coefﬁcient C between the two proﬁles ¼ 0.55) and no conﬁgurative similarity (coefﬁcient ES between the two proﬁles ¼ 0.09)."
