## Evidence

### Jayson M. Nissen 2016

Jayson M. Nissen, Robert M. Talbot, Amreen Nasim Thompson, and Ben Van Dusen. (2016). A comparison of normalized gain and Cohen's d for analyzing gains on concept inventories. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.09180](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.09180)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of the three normalized gain calculation methods across courses in the LASSO dataset found correlations of 0.99 between g and c, and 0.93 between gI and each of the other two (all p < 0.001). The authors concluded the measures were similar enough to use only course-average g in further analyses.

> "Correlations between all three measures were all large and statistically signiﬁcant, as shown in Table 1. The scatter plots for these three measures are shown in Figure 1. These results indicated that all three measures were very similar."
