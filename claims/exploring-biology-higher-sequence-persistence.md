## Evidence

### Wienhold Caroline J.; Branchaw Janet 2018

Wienhold Caroline J.; Branchaw Janet. (2018). Exploring Biology: A Vision and Change Disciplinary First-Year Seminar Improves Academic Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Institutional data from the matched-pairs data set (n = 698) tracking enrollment and completion of the two-semester introductory biology sequence for majors. "completed the full two-semester sequence by the end of their second year (93.2 vs. 88.2%)" with χ2, p = 0.036.

> "a greater percentage of Exploring Biology students continued on to enroll in the second semester of Introductory Biology (88.3 vs. 82.5%, χ2, p = 0.032), and a greater percentage of Exploring Biology students completed the full two-semester sequence by the end of their second year (93.2 vs. 88.2%, χ2, p = 0.036)."
