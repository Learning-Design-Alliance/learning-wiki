Evidence¶

Buchan 2020¶

Buchan, L., Hejmadi, M., Abrahams, L. & Hurst, L. D. (2020). A RCT for assessment of active human-centred learning finds teacher-centric non-human teaching of evolution optimal. npj Science of Learning 5:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00078-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multivariate model exploring gender, age, ability and SoW across both tranches, accounting for "7.10 –8.30% of the total variance" in student score. Higher teacher-judged ability predicted greater gains; age was not significant and gender effects did not replicate.

"Results indicate that age was not a signi ﬁcant variable but students with higher science ability (as judged by their teachers) improved more. The results also showed weak and non-replicable gender effects which supports the conclusion of the National Pupil Database22."

Related Claims¶

  • Serially taught lesson combinations produce replicable positive interaction effects, with the two most effective schemes showing positive interaction terms — related
  • The mutualism model's coupling structure held equally across sexes and age did not directly predict change scores — related
