## Evidence

### Foorman 2017

Foorman, B., Herrera, S., Dombek, J., Schatschneider, C., & Petscher, Y. (2017). The relative effectiveness of two approaches to early literacy intervention in grades K–2 (REL 2017–251). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive pre-post percentile-rank comparison from table 1 of the two-cohort cluster-randomized trial; the report states "the average difference between baseline and outcome percentile ranks on FRA reading measures was 13–25 percentile points across grades", with parallel language gains of 6–25 points, and cautions the gains may reflect regression to the mean or normative growth.

> "The average difference between baseline and outcome percentile ranks on FRA reading measures was 13–25 percentile points across grades."

### Foorman 2017 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive finding for FRA Sentence Comprehension in grade 1, an average difference of 35–39 percentile points; "students in schools in both intervention groups began just below the 30th percentile and ended the year above the 60th percentile", and the report notes the norms are kindergarten-based.
