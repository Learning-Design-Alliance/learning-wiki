# Double-blind experimental design can be successfully implemented in CALL-based pedagogical intervention research

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The study demonstrates that randomization and blinding can be automated through a system API so that neither instructors nor students know participant assignment to groups, a rare arrangement in pedagogical intervention research. [→ Evgeny Chukharev-Hudilainen and Tatiana A. Klepikova 2016](#evgeny-chukharev-hudilainen-and-tatiana-a-klepikova-2016)

## Evidence

### Evgeny Chukharev-Hudilainen and Tatiana A. Klepikova 2016

Evgeny Chukharev-Hudilainen and Tatiana A. Klepikova. (2016). The effectiveness of computer-based spaced repetition in foreign language vocabulary instruction: a double-blind study. calico journal vol 33.3. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i3.26055

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Methodological contribution demonstrated within the study itself: a program assigned participants to 224 control and experimental groups via the system API using a quasi-random number generator, and the key was withheld from researchers until the study was completed. This is a design demonstration, not an effect-size finding.

> "we also demonstrated the feasibility of randomized controlled double-blind trials in CALL research, wherein both the instructors and the students are unaware of the participant assignment to groups—a rare case in pedagogical intervention research"
