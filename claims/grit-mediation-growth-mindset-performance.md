Bootstrap mediation analysis (5000 iterations) in the Mplus SEM of 398 female art majors; the grit-mediated indirect effect was significant. The article reports "Ind1 (GM -> GT -> AP) at 0.07 (p < .01)" with a 95% CI excluding zero.

> "Ind1 (GM -> GT -> AP) at 0.07 (p < .01) and Ind2 (GM -> LA -> AP) at 0.09 (p < .001). BOOTSTRAP analysis (5000 iterations) indicated that the 95% confidence intervals of Ind1 and Ind2 did not include 0, supporting H2 and H3."
