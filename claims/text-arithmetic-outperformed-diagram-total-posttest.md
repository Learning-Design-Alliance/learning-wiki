# In inquiry learning of combinatorics, learners given a Text + Arithmetic representation outperformed learners given tree diagrams on total post-test scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants in the Text + Arithmetic condition outperformed participants in the Diagram condition and the Diagram + Arithmetic condition on total post-test scores. [→ Bas Kolloffel 2009](#bas-kolloffel-2009)

## Evidence

### Bas Kolloffel 2009

Bas Kolloffel, Tessa H. S. Eysink, Ton de Jong, Pascal Wilhelm. (2009). The effects of representational format on learning combinatorics from an interactive computer simulation. Instr Sci 37:503–517. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9056-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment with 123 pupils in a between-subjects pre-test post-test design across five representational-format conditions. The post-hoc LSD analysis showed "participants in the Text + Arithmetic condition outperformed participants in the Diagram condition"; overall format effect was F(4,119) = 2.57, P<.05.

> "A post-hoc least signiﬁcant difference (LSD) analysis revealed that participants in the Text + Arithmetic condition outperformed participants in the Diagram condition ( P\ .01), and participants in the Diagram + Arithmetic condition (P\ .05)."
