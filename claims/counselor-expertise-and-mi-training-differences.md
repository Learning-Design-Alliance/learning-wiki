## Evidence

### Kuipers L 2024

Kuipers L, Hoekstra T, SCI Physical Activity Counseling Panel, Hoekstra F. (2024). Professionals' knowledge, skills and confidence on using the best practices for spinal cord injury physical activity counseling in Canada and the Netherlands. The Journal of Spinal Cord Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/10790268.2024.2391595

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Demographic comparison of the enrolled survey sample (Canada n = 45, Netherlands n = 41) reported in the Results. Therapeutic recreation therapist was more common in Canada (42.2%, p < 0.005) and PA/lifestyle counseling in the Netherlands (48.8%).

> "The majority of Canadian respondents worked as recreation therapist (42%) and/or PA/lifestyle counselor (31%). The majority of Dutch respondents worked as PA/lifestyle counselor (49%) and/or physiotherapist (27%)."

### Kuipers L 2024 (2)

Kuipers L, Hoekstra T, SCI Physical Activity Counseling Panel, Hoekstra F. (2024). Professionals' knowledge, skills and confidence on using the best practices for spinal cord injury physical activity counseling in Canada and the Netherlands. The Journal of Spinal Cord Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/10790268.2024.2391595

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results comparison of formal training received, printed in Table 1 with p = 0.02 for Motivational Interviewing training. Canadians more often had other behavior change or counseling training (68.9% vs 41.5%, p = 0.01).

> "More Dutch respondents had received formal training in Motivational Interviewing (MI) compared to Canadians (Canada 51%; Netherlands 76%)."
