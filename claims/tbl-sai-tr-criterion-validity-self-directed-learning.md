Criterion validity correlation analysis between TBL-SAI-TR and the Turkish Self-Directed Learning Scale. The article prints "r = 0.778 (positive high)" for the totals, plus r = 0.536 (accountability), r = 0.679 (preference), and r = 0.719 (satisfaction), all with p < 0.05.
