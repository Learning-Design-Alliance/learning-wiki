## Evidence

### Servais Elliot L. 2025

Servais Elliot L., Rashidi Laila, Porwal Priyanshi, Garibaldi Mark, Hung Andrew J. (2025). Novel force feedback technology improves suturing in robotic-assisted surgery: a pre-clinical study. Surgical Endoscopy 39(2):1217-1226. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11472-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Error counts across seven pre-identified error types were recorded in real time by the lab coordinator in the same randomized study. Errors were "significantly reduced when force feedback was utilized (0.44 vs 0.88 errors; p = 0.012)". No effect size was printed.

> "The average number of errors for all the exercises combined was significantly reduced when force feedback was utilized (0.44 vs 0.88 errors; p = 0.012; Fig. 4A)."

### Servais Elliot L. 2025 (2)

Servais Elliot L., Rashidi Laila, Porwal Priyanshi, Garibaldi Mark, Hung Andrew J. (2025). Novel force feedback technology improves suturing in robotic-assisted surgery: a pre-clinical study. Surgical Endoscopy 39(2):1217-1226. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11472-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Error-type analyses with Bonferroni corrections in the same randomized study. "suture break (p = 0.001) and suture fray (p = 0.0001) were significantly lower in the FFB group". No effect size was printed.

> "Out of the seven error types that were recorded, the counts of suture break (p = 0.001) and suture fray (p = 0.0001) were significantly lower in the FFB group as compared to the non-FFB group"
