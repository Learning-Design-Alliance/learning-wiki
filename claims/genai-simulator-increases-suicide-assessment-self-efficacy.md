## Evidence

### Elyoseph Zohar 2026

Elyoseph Zohar, Levi-Belz Yossi, Levkovich Inbar, Haber Yuval, Gramaglia Carla Maria, Castroman Jorge López, Cecile Hanon, Olie Emilie. (2026). The effectiveness of multilingual AI-based simulator for suicide risk assessment training in improving self-efficacy among young psychiatrists: a pilot study across twenty languages. BMC Psychiatry, 26, 98. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-025-07737-9

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test in a before-after pilot study of 27 psychiatrists using the SEE THE PAIN simulator for a 20-minute virtual-patient risk assessment. The article reports "Hedge's g = 0.62" for the self-efficacy increase; normality of difference scores was met (Shapiro-Wilk p = .069).

> "the results showed a significant increase in self-efficacy scores from pre-intervention (M = 6.7, SD = 1.4) to post-intervention (M = 7.6, SD = 1.2), t(26) = -3.307, p = 0. 01, Hedge's g = 0.62"
