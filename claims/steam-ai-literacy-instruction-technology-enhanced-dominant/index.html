Analysis of instructional modalities across the reviewed studies (RQ1, Fig. 5). The review reports the printed proportions and suggests unplugged methods "remain valuable for younger learners and introductory contexts", but most research emphasizes tool-supported experiences.

"82.1% of studies relied on technology-enhanced activities, incorporating digital tools, platforms, or programming environments. In contrast, only 17.9% of the studies employed unplugged approaches, such as card-based simulations or role-playing tasks"
