## Evidence

### Chen Kuo-Su 2021

Chen Kuo-Su, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Huang Min-Ping, Chen Chih-Ken, Hung Ming-Jui. (2021). Academic outcome and moderator of flipped classroom learning program “Teaching on the Run”. Biomedical Journal, 44(5), 636–643. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bj.2020.05.009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GEE moderator analysis across eight models on the 115-participant TOTR data; the printed interaction was β = 4.451 (1.15, 7.75), p = 0.008 for class participation × T3 vs. T2. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "GEE model showed that class participation is a significant moderator influencing the score change from mid-test to post-test."
