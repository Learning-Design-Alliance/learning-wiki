Snipes 2016¶

Snipes, J., & Tran, L. (2016). Early indicators and academic mindsets in the Clark County School District. San Francisco, CA: REL West @ WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED573499

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of individual 8th grade indicators in table 1 (sample size = 22,355 as printed). The report states the Fs indicator "captured 65 percent" and low GPA "identified 55 percent" of students who fell off track, with false-positive rates of 10 to 20 percent for academic indicators.

"The true-positive rate for the credit indicator was approximately twice as high as the true-positive rate for the attendance indicator (64 versus 32 percent)."
