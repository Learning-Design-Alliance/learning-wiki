The article reports the integrated model of academic dishonesty of Murdock and Anderman (2006), which frames cheating around three motivational questions. It states "extrinsic goals... are more strongly associated with cheating"; no effect size is printed and the model is reported second-hand.

"Their model suggests that extrinsic goals, which focus on external rewards rather than the intrinsic enjoyment of learning, are more strongly associated with cheating."
