Lin, L.-F. (2017). Impacts of the Problem-based Learning Pedagogy on English Learners’ Reading Comprehension, Strategy Use, and Active Learning Attitudes. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2320

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative categorization of the 26 PBL participants' self-reports across 10 weeks, producing eight task types with a totaled frequency of 74. After-class discussion ranked highest (23 participants); searching for data ranked second (20 participants). No effect size applies.

> "The task ranked with the highest percentage indicated that there were 23 participants describing that they joined the discussion after class. Among the 26 participants, up to 89% of the PBL participants extended their learning after class."
