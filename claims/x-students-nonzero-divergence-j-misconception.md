`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Open-response CURrENT Q3 results at Institution X (N=11). The article reports that "10/11 X-students incorrectly thought ∇⋅J would be non-zero inside the wire", with at least half attributing it to converging field lines; ∇⋅J = 0 inside the wire by charge conservation.

> "However, 10/11 X-students incorrectly thought ∇⋅J would be non-zero inside the wire (the remaining student left this question blank), because the magnitude of J is increasing to the right."
