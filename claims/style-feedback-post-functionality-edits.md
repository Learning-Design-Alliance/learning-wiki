`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Among nudged real-time-access students who viewed feedback and made a style-based or combined edit, 79% directly incorporated the style insights shown in their feedback (Figure 7 context). The percentage is descriptive; no test statistic is printed.

> "Now considering only the students who viewed style feedback and made a style based edit or a combined style and functionality edit, we found that 79% of those students directly incorporated the style insights shown in their feedback."
