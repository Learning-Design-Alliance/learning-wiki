## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2014

Andrew J. Mason. (2014). Potential relationship of chosen major to problem solving attitudes and course performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.049](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational categorization of 69 students' end-of-semester free-response essays across two semesters, tabulated by major in Table I (e.g., Bio: 15 Framework, 8 Performance, 10 Vague). The article reports the distribution "appear[s] to contradict the assumption" of major-based differences.

> "The results appear to contradict the assumption that biology and health science majors would be less framework-oriented than would be computer science or chemistry majors."
