The editorial summarises Zamecnik et al. (2024)'s study of the perception of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams, building on Festinger's two-dimensional concept of cohesion (task and social); no effect size or statistics are printed here.

"The study puts into relation learning team members' interactions with a survey-based measure of task cohesion and finds relations between the strength of perceived task cohesion and team performance."
