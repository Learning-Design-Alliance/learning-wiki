## Evidence

### Wilcox 2013

Wilcox, B. R., Caballero, M. D., Rehn, D. A., & Pollock, S. J. (2013). ACER: An Analytic Framework for Students' Use of Mathematics in Upper-Division Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exam analysis of the Execution component: 60% of 172 solutions contained Execution elements, reduced mainly by one class (N=55) asked only to set up the integral. Of solutions with Execution elements, "51% of 53" made mathematical errors; roughly half of those made only slight errors.

> "Overall, half the students' exams containing elements of Execution (51% of 103, N=53) made various math- ematical errors while solving integrals or simpliﬁng their expression algebraically (elements CE2 and CE3)."
