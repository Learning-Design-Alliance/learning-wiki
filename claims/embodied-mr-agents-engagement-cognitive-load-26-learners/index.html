The review reports, citing Nguyen et al., a Design Science Research study in which 26 adult learners participated in embodied agentic AI-supported mixed-reality learning experiences aimed at active, self-regulated learning and human-AI shared regulation. The study "reported higher engagement and lower cognitive load"; no effect size is printed.
