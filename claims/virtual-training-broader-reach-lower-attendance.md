Authors' experiential report from the Training Events section comparing virtual and in-person event formats after the 2020 pivot to virtual platforms. The article states virtual events have "a broader reach of participant attendance that is much higher compared to in-person events". No comparative statistics are printed.

> "Virtual events have a broader reach of participant attendance that is much higher compared to in-person events and enable a considerably more equitable service to the community."

Attendance observation from the same experiential account of virtual training events. The authors report that "the actual attendance is typically only 50% of those who have registered", attributing this to the pervasive culture of multi-tasking within HEP and distraction by other professional duties.

> "Due to this issue, although initial registrations for these events are very high, the actual attendance is typically only 50% of those who have registered."
