Parker 2025¶

Parker, M. J., & Zavala-Cerna, M. G. (2025). What Don't You Understand? Using Large Language Models to Identify and Characterize Student Misconceptions About Challenging Topics. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00294 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.00294

IRT validation using the py-irt package on results from 765 Immunology students across three cohorts. "The Spearman correlation was .99, p < 10-5" between IRT-based and CTT-based quiz difficulty rankings, supporting the simpler CTT approach.
