## Evidence

Pre/post quiz question in the three-course in-class evaluation; Question 1 states Bob's photo-detector does not click. The article reports 41% incorrect on the pretest versus 'only 6% had this difficulty' on the posttest.

> "On the pretest, 41% of students incorrectly claimed that Bob will know the polarization of the photon se nt by Alice and a "bit" of the key can be generated even when a photon is not detected by Bob's detector but on the posttest only 6% had this difficulty."
