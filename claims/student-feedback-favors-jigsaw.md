`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported structured feedback from all 150 students using a pre-validated Likert questionnaire; besides the headline ratings, 94% endorsed implementing JT regularly and 86% reported continuous motivation during sessions.

> "93% of students rated JT as good (45%) and excellent (48%). Moreover, 89% of students expressed a preference for JT over conventional tutorials."
