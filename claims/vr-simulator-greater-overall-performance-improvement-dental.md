`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized case-control study of 55 third-year dental students found that "The improvement in overall performance was significantly greater in the intervention group than the control group (P < 0.05)". The intervention group used a VR simulator plus conventional practice, while the control group used conventional practice alone.
