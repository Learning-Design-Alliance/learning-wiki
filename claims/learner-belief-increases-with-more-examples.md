`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects analysis of bet allocations from 140 Mechanical Turk learners who saw teachers' examples sequentially and rated each of four options. The article reports the printed time coefficient and t statistic; no standardized effect size.

> "Overall, learners tended to assign a higher probability to the correct answer as they received more examples (Fig. 4B; main effect of time: β=0.03, t=4.751, P< 0.001)."
