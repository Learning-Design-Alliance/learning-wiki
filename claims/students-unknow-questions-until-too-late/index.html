Evidence¶

Nodine 2012¶

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the focus groups, a current student described the problem of not knowing what to ask: "If you ask the right questions, if you know which questions to ask, you can get a lot of information back. I don't know what questions to ask." Participants reported enrolling in courses that did not count toward their major and learning too late about financial aid credit limits.
