Multivariable regression analysis of risk factors for conversion among the 486 RPDs in the E-MIPS registry. The article also reports an odds ratio of 0.86 (95% CI: 0.753–0.973) per 1 mm increase in pancreatic duct diameter, and ASA class III more common among converted cases (P=0.019).

> "Patients with a soft/normal pancreatic texture had a significantly higher risk of conversion compared with those with a firm pancreas (OR: 3.24, 95% CI: 1.78–5.92)."
