`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The article reports, citing Korpershoek and colleagues (2016), a meta-analysis of 54 interventions examining classroom management programs on primary school students' academic, behavioral, social-emotional and motivational outcomes, reporting "a small, statistically significant effect (g = 0.08 to 0.39) on most outcomes". This is second-hand attribution from the literature review.

> "This meta-analysis suggested that primary school classroom management programs had a small, statistically significant effect (g = 0.08 to 0.39) on most outcomes, with stronger effects emerging for interventions focused on social-emotional development and teacher development."
