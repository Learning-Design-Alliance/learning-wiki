## Evidence

### Fatma Betül Güreş 2025

Fatma Betül Güreş, Tanya Nazaretsky, Bahar Radmehr, Martina Rau, and Tanja Käser. (2025). How Instructional Sequence and Personalized Support Impact Diagnostic Strategy Learning. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98465-5_53](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98465-5_53)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-groups experiment (PS-I n = 43, I-PS n = 37) in PharmaSim with pharmacy technician apprentices; Mixed Linear Models on post-test scores showed the I-PS group's C2 performance dropped relative to its other clients, "while PS-I maintained their performance."

> "These findings suggest that the I-PS group had difficulty applying or transferring what they learned to a more challenging scenario (Client C2), while PS-I maintained their performance."
