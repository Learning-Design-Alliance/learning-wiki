`q2 i2` The pooled effect size across ten reconfigured teaching strategy subtopics is ES = 0.497, which the authors characterize as a medium effect. [→ Guirguis 2017](#guirguis-2017)

## Evidence

### Guirguis 2017

Guirguis, R., & Pankowski, J. (2017). Potential Effects of Teaching Strategies on Students’ Academic Performance under a Trump Administration. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 5(4). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i4.2263

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

A re-analysis of studies drawn from Hattie (2009), conducted in STATA with forest, scree, and funnel plots. The pooled random-effects estimate was "ES = .0497", described as "evidence of a medium effect".

> "The findings of this meta -analysis illustrate that the subtopics outlined for the new topic of Strategies for Teacher Preparation offer an overall effect size of ES = .0497, which based on the work of Hattie is evidence of a medium effect."
