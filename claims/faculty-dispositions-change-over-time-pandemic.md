The preface summarizes two worldwide surveys of higher education faculty administered at two time periods during the pandemic, reporting that "These patterns were also found to change over time" regarding dispositions and affordance use. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The findings reveal different patterns of dispositions and different uses of technological affordances to foster online learning. These patterns were also found to change over time, highlighting different conditions possibly enabling or hindering the development of competencies for online teaching and learning."
