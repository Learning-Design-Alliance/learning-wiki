Evidence¶

Olaniyan 2015¶

Olaniyan, Ademola Olatide, Omosewo, Esther O., & Nwankwo, Levi I. (2015). Effect of polya problem-solving model on senior secondary school students' performance in current electricity. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 3(1), 97-104. https://ojcs.siue.edu/ojs/index.php/ejsme

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental, non-randomized non-equivalent pre-test post-test control group study with 120 S.S.2 physics students in two purposively selected schools; ANCOVA of post-test scores gave a significant treatment effect, "P value (.022)". Post-test means were 33.40 (Polya) versus 12.30 (lecture).

"The result in table 2 showed the P value (.022) is less than P alpha level of 0.050, (P < 0.050). This suggests that the Hypothesis HO1 was rejected. There was significant difference in the performance of students exposed to Polya, Problem-Solving Models and Lecture method in Performance Test on Current Electricity."
