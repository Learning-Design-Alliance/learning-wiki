## Evidence

### Saredi Giovanni 2015

Saredi Giovanni, Pirola Giacomo Maria, Pacchetti Andrea, Lovisolo Jon Alexander, Borroni Giacomo, Sembenini Federico, Marconi Alberto Mario. (2015). Evaluation of the learning curve for thulium laser enucleation of the prostate with the aid of a simulator tool but without tutoring: comparison of two surgeons with different levels of endoscopic experience. BMC Urology 15. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-015-0045-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of outcomes between surgeon A (over 25 years endoscopic experience, 48 operations) and surgeon B (about 15 years, 52 operations) in the 100-patient series; both practiced on the CyberSim simulator before operating on humans.

> "The absence of noticeable differences in the perioperative data and urodynamic results for the patients treated by the two surgeons shows that this technique is feasible even without direct tutoring."
