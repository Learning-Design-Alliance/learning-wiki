Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative interview findings on disadvantages: character limits, plain-text-only formatting, and criticism of one-way communication; one student suggested "a two-way channel of communication between instructor and students".

> "A few students mentioned the limited capacity of the SMS in term of the limited number of characters that can be sent in one SMS message. In addition, couple of students complained about the lack of formatting options in the SMS."
