## Evidence

### Katz 2013

Jennifer Katz. (2013). The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Engaging students in inclusive education. Canadian Journal of Education, 36(1). https://journals.sfu.ca/cje/index.php/cje

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA of observed interactive behavior (Table 10): overall F(9,68) = 34.893, partial η = .511; interact with adult η = .121, interact with peer η = .242, no interaction η = .553, all p < .001. Table 3 shows treatment peer interaction rising from 10.95 to 25.52 per 60 minutes while control stayed near 8.89.

> "Students in treatment classrooms implementing the Three Block Model of UDL were observed to more frequently interact with adults and peers and reported higher levels of student autonomy, while students in control classrooms spent more time not interacting."
