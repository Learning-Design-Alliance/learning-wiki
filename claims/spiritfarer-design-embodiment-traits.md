# Four game design traits of Spiritfarer were consistently reported as positively influencing players' affect and gaming experience

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants consistently identified cozy vibes, diverse character designs, tasks without fail states, and subtle universal narrative design as positively influencing their affect and experience. [→ Noah Glaser 2024](#noah-glaser-2024)

## Evidence

### Noah Glaser 2024

Noah Glaser, Lucas Jensen, Tina Riedy, Maggie Center, Jim Shifflett, Joseph Griffin. (2024). Unlocking the Everdoor: analyzing the serious game Spiritfarer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10357-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of LED responses found these four design traits repeatedly raised by participants; the article reports that "Throughout the LED responses a number of game design traits were consistently brought up by participants as having a positive influence on their affect and gaming experience."

> "These game design traits are (a) the cozy vibes of the gaming experience, (b) the attributes of character designs including their diversity and representation, (c) the developer's decision to have tasks without fail states and removal of traditional gameplay decisions, and (d) the subtle but universal narrative design."
