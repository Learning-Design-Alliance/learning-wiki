Bruzgulis 2019¶

Bruzgulis, A., Hart, H., & Young, C. (2019). The challenges facing CPS administrators: Roadblocks to school improvement. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/challenges-facing-cps-administrators

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Latent class analysis of all 2017 roadblock item responses across administrators; the brief reports three distinct response groups, with the Considerable group rating most items extremely concerning and financial items rated most highly in all three groups.

"When we looked across administrators' responses for the degree to which they considered proposed issues to be roadblocks, we found respondents fell into three similarly sized groups: Limited, Moderate, and Considerable Roadblocks groups"
