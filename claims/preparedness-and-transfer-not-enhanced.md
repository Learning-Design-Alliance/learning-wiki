## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was only a small increase in preparedness for CB-EMS operations (3.0 ± 0.7 vs 3.1 ± 0.9), and participants ranked transferability of training content lower after simulation than before (3.2 ± 0.7 vs 2.9 ± 0.4). [→ Ruebsam Marie-Luise 2023](#ruebsam-marie-luise-2023)

## Evidence

### Ruebsam Marie-Luise 2023

Ruebsam Marie-Luise, Orsson Dorota, Metelmann Bibiana, Orsson Jakub, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Metelmann Camilla. (2023). Cross-border simulation training for German and Polish emergency medical teams is feasible: conception and evaluation of a bilingual simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04823-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive pre-post survey results; the discussion states "scores indicate that training did not enhance perceived preparedness of participants for CB-EMS operations." Participants assumed daily CB-EMS operations would not be as challenging as during simulation (3.1 ± 1.1 vs 2.3 ± 1.2). No effect size printed.

> "Before simulation, the participants ranked transferability of training content higher than afterwards (3.2 ± 0.7 vs 2.9 ± 0.4)."
