Spearman rank-order correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional survey (n = 397). The article reports "ρ = 0.683, p < 0.001" for the total scales and notes the weakest cross-scale correlation was ρ = 0.128.

> "The strongest correlation was observed between NEBPPR Total and PCC Total (ρ = 0.683, p < 0.001), followed by Benefit‐Not Harming and PCC Total (ρ = 0.668, p < 0.001)."
