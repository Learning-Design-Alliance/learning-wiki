## Evidence

### Umoren RA 2025

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Manikin-based analysis using the NeoNatalie Live neonatal manikin, which provides high-fidelity feedback on ventilation pressures and volumes; ventilation time was stable across the two assessments. No effect size printed.

> "Analysis of the data collected using the neonatal manikin showed stable performance immediately after the course and at 6 months after the course, with no significant difference in median (IQR) percentage of ventilation time (83% [57%-100%] vs 84% [68%-100%]; P = .27)."

### Umoren RA 2025 (2)

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Among 47 participants assessed with the manikin at both time points, appropriate ventilation rate counts were lower at 6 months but not significantly (P = .06); with a 40-60 breaths per minute range the difference was P = .54. No effect size printed.

> "The number of participants providing appropriate ventilation rates (30-50 breaths per minute) at the assessment immediately after the course was 23 (49%) vs 14 (30%) at the 6-month follow-up (P = .06) among 47 participants assessed using the manikin at both time points."
