## Evidence

### Adrian Madsen 2014

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2014). How physics instruction impacts students' beliefs about learning physics: A meta-analysis of 24 studies. https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6522

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 24 published CLASS/MPEX studies synthesizing pre-to-post shifts in percent expert-like responses across US and Canadian undergraduate physics courses. The review reports that "in typical physics classes, students' beliefs deteriorate or at best stay the same."

> "We report that in typical physics classes, students' beliefs deteriorate or at best stay the same. There are a few types of interventions, including an explicit focus on model-building and/or developing expert-like beliefs that lead to significant improvements in beliefs."
