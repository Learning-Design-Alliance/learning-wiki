## Evidence

### Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise 2015

Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise. (2015). An Online Tutor for Astronomy: The GEAS Self-Review Library. [doi:10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990](https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental comparison of two course versions taught by the same instructor, one on-campus in Fall 2010 and one by distance learning from Fall 2011 to Spring 2014, with 88% of the two-hour final identical across cohorts. Classroom mean was 76.6 ± 1.5 and remote 79.6 ± 1.7; the article reports a two-sample t-test it calls inconclusive, so no effect size is asserted.

> "Distance learners performed slightly better, with a mean value two pooled standard deviations of the mean above that for the in-class students. (A two-sample t-test yields an inconclusive less than 9% probability that the two scores were drawn from the same parent sample.)"
