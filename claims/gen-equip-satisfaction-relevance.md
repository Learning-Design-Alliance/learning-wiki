## Evidence

### Jackson L 2018

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 2 cross-sectional online feedback survey (22 questions, Survey Monkey) with 81 respondents from 14 countries. The article prints the relevance percentages above; 82.5% would recommend the resources. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size.

> "Of the respondents, 73.8% reported the module was highly or very relevant to their own clinical practice, while 78.8% believed it was highly or very relevant to primary care practice generally and 76.3% reported the resources matched well to the primary care curriculum."

### Jackson L 2018 (2)

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 3 workshop surveys (N = 44 paper, 72.1% response; N = 35 online, 57.4%) and phase 4 interviews (21 interviews to saturation) triangulated the satisfaction findings qualitatively. Respondents reported returning to modules repeatedly in practice.

> "These results were confirmed by workshop attendees and interviewed learners, who reported that the Gen-Equip resources were useful, interactive, easy to access/use, and applicable to practice."
