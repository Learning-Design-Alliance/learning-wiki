## Evidence

### Emily M. Smith 2019

Emily M. Smith, Martin M. Stein, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Surprise! Shifting students away from model-verifying frames in physics labs. arXiv preprint arXiv:1807.04710. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Video analysis of two students, Ali and Ben, during the second week of the first lab of an honors introductory mechanics course. The authors report the pair's pre-lab conversation stating "the purpose of the lab is to prove that the period is not affected by the angle," identifying a shared model-verifying frame.

> "As they are setting up the pendulum prior to the lab session starting, they discuss that this week they will be comparing different angles and that " the purpose of the lab is to prove that the period is not affected by the angle.""
