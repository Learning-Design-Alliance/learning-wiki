`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlational analysis of the total sample (N = 106) at pre-test and post-test (Table 2). The authors report the positive associations "with large effect sizes" for expressiveness and preciseness, and a negative association for emotionality that reaches a large magnitude at post-test; no standardised effect-size value is printed in the quoted sentence.

> "there is a positive association of 2 of the communication styles (Expressiveness and Preciseness) with respect to self-efficacy that is maintained in both measures (pre and post), with large effect sizes"
