## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Erdem Onan 2024

Erdem Onan, Felicitas Biwer, Roman Abel, Wisnu Wiradhany & Anique de Bruin. (2024). Optimizing self-organized study orders: combining refutations and metacognitive prompts improves the use of interleaved practice. npj Science of Learning 9:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00245-7

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered mixed-subjects experiment (2×3 mixed ANOVA on switch proportions across pre-, post-intervention and delayed-transfer tasks). The simple main effect of Time in the intervention condition was significant, "F(1.64, 73.63) = 40.38, p < 0.001, η2 g = 0.266"; the control-condition increase did not reach significance (p = 0.156).

> "The results revealed that participants in the interven tion condition signiﬁcantly increased their use of interleaved practice, F(1.64, 73.63) = 40.38, p < 0.001, η2 g = 0.266."

### Erdem Onan 2024 (2)

Erdem Onan, Felicitas Biwer, Roman Abel, Wisnu Wiradhany & Anique de Bruin. (2024). Optimizing self-organized study orders: combining refutations and metacognitive prompts improves the use of interleaved practice. npj Science of Learning 9:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00245-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`
