`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses from the same 52 students, coded by seven raters with proportion agreement > 0.7 for all questions. Table V reports Unprepared Pre 9 / Post 2 (difference -7) and Confidence Pre 10 / Post 19 (difference 9); 10 of 52 respondents expressed post-survey confidence they lacked pre-survey.

> "students less often mentioned feelings of intimidation and unpreparedness after the lesson, while mentioning more often that papers feel important/worthwhile and they are confident in their abilities."
