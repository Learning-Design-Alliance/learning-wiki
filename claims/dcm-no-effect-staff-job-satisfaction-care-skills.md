## Evidence

### Schaap FD 2019

Schaap FD, Finnema EJ, Stewart RE, Dijkstra GJ, Reijneveld SA. (2019). Effects of Dementia Care Mapping on job satisfaction and caring skills of staff caring for older people with intellectual disabilities: A quasi‐experimental study. Journal of Applied Research in Intellectual Disabilities. [doi:10.1111/jar.12615](https://doi.org/10.1111/jar.12615)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi‐experimental study in 23 group homes (127 intervention, 94 control staff at baseline; N = 227 analysed) using multilevel mixed‐effect intention‐to‐treat models. The article reports "no differences in change regarding any of the primary outcomes" with printed effect sizes from −0.18 to −0.66; equivalence was not tested.

> "Between groups, we found no differences in change regarding any of the primary outcomes (MWSS‐HC, P‐CAT and SCIDS), between T0 and T1, and between T0 and T2. Effect sizes varied from −0.18 to −0.47 for T0‐T1 and from −0.30 to −0.66 for T0 to T2."
