## Evidence

### Zhang Yan 2025

Zhang Yan, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Yan Yingbin. (2025). Case-based virtual reality simulation for penetrating facial wound suture skill training in medical students: design and pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08151-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of the five training results and final test across 26 students (Table 1, Fig. 3) showed item scores and total score rose across practices, with the article stating "students' scores reached a plateau after 2 to 4 times of the practice (P < 0.05, Table 1)"; no effect size printed.

> "With the increased number of practices, the performance gradually improved, particularly for “History and Physical Examination” and the total scores. Furthermore, students’ scores reached a plateau after 2 to 4 times of the practice (P < 0.05, Table 1)."
