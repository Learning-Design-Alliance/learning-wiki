# Most students value school CPR training and report increased confidence and sufficient knowledge after training, with no difference between web-course and control groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 98% of students in both groups said it is important to learn CPR in school, and large majorities reported increased confidence and sufficient knowledge for compressions and ventilation, with no significant between-group differences. [→ Nord Anette 2017](#nord-anette-2017)

## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Svensson Leif, Claesson Andreas, Herlitz Johan, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). The effect of a national web course “Help-Brain-Heart” as a supplemental learning tool before CPR training: a cluster randomised trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-017-0439-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported questionnaire directly after training in a cluster randomised trial. Students also reported sufficient knowledge to do chest compressions (82% vs 81%) and rescue breaths (78% vs 75%), all not significant between groups.

> "Most of the students (98% in both groups; NS) indicated that it is important to learn CPR in school and they felt more confident about acting compared with before the training (82% of the intervention group and 88% of the control group; NS)."
