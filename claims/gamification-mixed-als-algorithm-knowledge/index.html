q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this finding from a cluster randomised controlled trial of gamification in ALS training for clinical nurses (Kim et al.). Scores were "similar post‐test scores for basic content" but lower for ALS-algorithm knowledge in the gamification group; no effect size is printed.

"In a cluster randomised trial, Kim et al. [44] reported similar post‐test scores for basic content but lower scores for knowledge related to ALS algorithms in the gamification group."
