Evidence¶

Anders Bouwer 2010¶

Anders Bouwer, Bert Bredeweg. (2010). Graphical means for inspecting qualitative models of system behaviour. Instr Sci 38:173–208. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9083-4

q2 · i? · design · r2

Treatment analysis of answers to 27 on-screen questions across two scenarios (10 on a one-population scenario, 17 on the three-population Cerrado Succession Hypothesis scenario); not all participants completed all questions but all reached question 22. Mean accuracy was "M = 94.3%, SD = 8.6%".

"The percentage of correct answers was generally high ( M = 94.3%, SD = 8.6%) and did not differ between scenarios 1 and 2."
