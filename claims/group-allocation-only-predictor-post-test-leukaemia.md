## Evidence

### Morgulis Yuri 2012

Morgulis Yuri, Kumar Rakesh K, Lindeman Robert, Velan Gary M. (2012). Impact on learning of an e-learning module on leukaemia: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 12:36. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-36

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise linear regression on trial data using IBM SPSS Statistics version 19 found "group allocation was the only significant predictor of performance in the post-test"; neither WAM nor time spent studying was predictive in the model.

> "In a stepwise multiple regression analysis, group allocation was the only significant predictor of performance in the post-test (R = 0.516, R 2 = 0.266, P = 0.001), i.e. 26.6 % of the variance in post-test scores was accounted for by group membership."
