## Evidence

### Wei 2026

Wei, Y., and Perkins, M. (2026). Generative AI and student collaboration: a scoping review of group work processes, outcomes, and risks. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00217-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's thematic synthesis of 18 studies reports AI positioned as an active participant — a "facilitator, collaborator, and cognitive scaffold" — including agents as conversation facilitators or mock-students (Kiesler et al. 2025), a dynamic ZPD scaffold (Baskara 2024), and a creative partner (Creely and Blannin 2025). The review reports these as recurring patterns, not tested effects.

> "GenAI reconfigures knowledge development by shifting it from an individual internal process to a more distributed and interactive one, in which learners engage with AI as a facilitator, collaborator, and cognitive scaffold."
