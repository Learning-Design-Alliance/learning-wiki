## Evidence

### Sajja 2026

Sajja, R., Sermet, Y., Fodale, B., & Demir, I. (2026). Evaluating AI-Powered Learning Assistants in Engineering Higher Education: Student Engagement, Ethical Challenges, and Policy Implications. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

System-generated usage logs across the semester (Table 1); students averaged 13.12 total AI interactions (SD = 11.81), mostly chatbot (Mean = 11.56). Logs did not capture external AI tool use.

> "chatbot messages were by far the most frequently used feature within the Educational AI Hub, with 555 total interactions and usage by 46 out of 48 students who engaged with the tool at least once (96%)."

### Sajja 2026 (2)

Sajja, R., Sermet, Y., Fodale, B., & Demir, I. (2026). Evaluating AI-Powered Learning Assistants in Engineering Higher Education: Student Engagement, Ethical Challenges, and Policy Implications. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Manual coding plus keyword-based classification of all chatbot messages (Table 4): Software Help 234 (40.4%), Conceptual/Theory Help 176 (30.4%), Assignment/Class Help 113 (19.5%), Miscellaneous 38 (6.6%), Technical Issues 18 (3.1%).

> "the majority of student inquiries (40.4%) fell under the Software Help category."
