### Shadnaz Asgari 2021

Shadnaz Asgari, Jelena Trajkovic, Mehran Rahmani, Wenlu Zhang, Roger C. Lo, and Antonella Sciortino. (2021). An Observational Study of Engineering Online Education During the COVID-19 Pandemic. IEEE Access. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0250041](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250041)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Student survey (627 respondents, Qualtrics, July 27-August 12) on challenges during Spring 2020 online instruction; Fig. 3 summarizes prevalence. The article also reports "more than 50% of our students did not have access to a private or even quiet space" and 55% lacked motivation, attributed in comments to pandemic uncertainty and loss of peer interaction.

> "About 70% of students indicated difficulty in maintaining their focus or experiencing Zoom fatigue after attending multiple online sessions. 55% of students felt social disconnection from their classmates/peers, while 64% did not feel engaged during the online classes."
