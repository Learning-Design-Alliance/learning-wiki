## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lefor Alan Kawarai 2020

Lefor Alan Kawarai, Harada Kanako, Kawahira Hiroshi, Mitsuishi Mamoru. (2020). The effect of simulator fidelity on procedure skill training: a literature review. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5ea6.ae73

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Across the five reviewed studies that included an HF group, an LF group, and a didactic or control group, the review reports "either LF or HF had better outcomes than a control group with no intervention or a group that received didactic training only."

> "These five studies found similar outcomes with LF and HF simulators and that either LF or HF had better outcomes than a control group with no intervention or a group that received didactic training only."
