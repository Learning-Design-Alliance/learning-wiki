### López-Jiménez 2021

López-Jiménez, J. J., Fernández-Alemán, J. L., García-Berná, J. A., López González, L., González Sequeros, O., Nicolás Ros, J., Carrillo de Gea, J. M., Idri, A., & Toval, A. (2021). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nine-question five-point Likert questionnaire completed by 87, 71 and 38 students in the three cohorts; final assessments were 4.28 (SIDRA), 4.61 (R-G-SIDRA) and 4.47 (RB-G-SIDRA); 96–100% would use the system in more subjects.

> "The use of the three SIDRA systems was positively evaluated by the students, with median 4 or 5 in all of the questions for the three systems, confirming hypothesis H6."
