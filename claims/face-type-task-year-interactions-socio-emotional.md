## Evidence

### Mayer Sveta 2025

Mayer Sveta, Dumontheil Iroise, Wilkinson Hannah R., Porayska-Pomsta Kaska, Farran Emily K., Tolmie Andrew K., Mareschal Denis, the UnLocke Team. (2025). SEE+ computerized classroom-based training enhances 7- to 10-year-olds' socio-emotional cognition through observation and inference. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0330934

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-ups of the mixed ANOVA interactions showed "children were more accurate for real faces than cartoon faces" in both tasks, with the difference larger for ToM (ηp2 = .144, a large effect) than ER (ηp2 = .008); Year 5 children (M = 4.414) were more accurate than Year 3 (M = 3.943), and the real–cartoon difference was larger in Year 5 (Mdiff = 0.505) than Year 3 (Mdiff = 0.334).

> "while children were more accurate for real faces than cartoon faces in both ER and ToM tasks (p's < .001), the difference was larger for ToM (Mdiff = 0.688, ηp2 = .144) than ER (Mdiff = 0.151, ηp2 = .008)"
