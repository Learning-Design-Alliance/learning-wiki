### Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert 2026

Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert. (2026). Adapting Public Exams for Introductory Physics: A Mixed-Methods Investigation of Student Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic coding of open-ended written responses collected after each of three exams from consenting students in the NMSU physics course. The authors found "a significant number of student comments (39)" referring to Public Exams directing students to core concepts, and positive comments outnumbered negative ones on all three exams.

> "Overall, students reported they liked the exam format and identified positive impacts such as anxiety reduction."
