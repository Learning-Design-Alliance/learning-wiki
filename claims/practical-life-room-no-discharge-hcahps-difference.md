## Evidence

### Barbosa Luciana Gardin 2020

Barbosa Luciana Gardin, Frazão Cinthya da Silva. (2020). Impact of demonstration in a realistic simulation environment as a postoperative education in patients’ experience. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2020AO4831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized controlled trial, analyzing the discharge-specific HCAHPS subscore covering only the questions related to preparation for patient discharge. The discharge HCAHPS was not normally distributed (p<0.001), so non-parametric tests were used; no effect size was printed.

> "The evaluation of the general and discharge HCAHPS scores showed similar distribution between the Intervention and Control Groups (p=0.496 and p=0.400, respectively)."
