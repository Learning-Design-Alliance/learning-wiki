## Evidence

### Ajredini 2013

Ajredini, F., Izairi, N., Zajkov, O. (2013). Real Experiments versus PhET Simulations for Understanding Electrostatic Charging. European J of Physics Education, 5(1). https://ejpe.phywe.de

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest results for situations e) and f), which investigated understanding of electrostatic induction. The authors had expected the Sim group to benefit from the Balloons and Static Electricity visualization, but "students in Real group achieved better result than the ones in the Sim group".

> "Only 11 % of the students from the Real group, 7 % from the Sim group and 3 % from the control group gave correct answers, while the percentages of the incorrect answers are 20 %, 41% and 47 % for the Real group, Sim group and control group, respectively."
