## Evidence

### Perruchet 1998

Perruchet, P., & Vinter, A. (1998). PARSER: A Model for Word Segmentation. Journal of Memory and Language, 39, 246-263. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.1998.2576

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation (Study 2) of the Saffran et al. (1997) attention-disturbing conditions, with syllables processed in blocks of five percepts and random skips simulating attentional shifts. Observed means were 21.1 (adults) and 21.3 (children) where chance is 18; PARSER scored "a comparable score (21.34)".

> "PARSER provided a comparable score (21.34) while actually processing only 3% of the training material."
