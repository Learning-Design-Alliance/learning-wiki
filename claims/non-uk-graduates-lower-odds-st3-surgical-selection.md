`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cross-sectional analysis of 682 general surgery applicants in the 2024 UK ST3 national selection, using multivariable models informed by a directed acyclic graph. The article reports international medical graduates had "79% lower odds of receiving offers compared with UK/RoI graduates", with an adjusted odds ratio of aOR=0.21 (95% CI 0.14 to 0.33).
