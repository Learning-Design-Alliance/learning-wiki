# In regression models, the best and mean of member performances significantly affect team outcomes, whereas the standard deviation has a weak effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Each additional point in the best individual performance predicts a 1-point increase in team performance after the first collaboration (Model 1, p < 0.0001). [→ Hyewon Jang 2026](#hyewon-jang-2026)
`q3 i?` Each 1-point increase in mean individual performance predicts a 1.29-point increase in team performance (Model 2, p < 0.0001). [→ Hyewon Jang 2026 (2)](#hyewon-jang-2026-2)
`q3 i?` The effect of the standard deviation of member performances is less significant than the effects of the mean or best, and weaker still when controlling for the mean (Model 4). [→ Hyewon Jang 2026 (3)](#hyewon-jang-2026-3)

## Evidence

### Hyewon Jang 2026

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression (Model 1) on 44 team scores predicting team correctness after the first collaboration from the best member score; Table II reports a standardized coefficient of 1.00 with p < 0.0001 and R2 = 0.48.

> "With each additional point in the best individual performance of a team, we can expect the team performance after the first collaboration to increase by an average of 1 point according to Model 1."

### Hyewon Jang 2026 (2)

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression (Model 2) on the same 44 team scores; Table II reports a standardized coefficient of 1.29 for the mean of member scores with p < 0.0001 and R2 = 0.35.

> "For each increase of 1 point in the mean individual performance of a team, the predicted team performance after the first collaboration increases by 1.29 points according to Model 2."

### Hyewon Jang 2026 (3)

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression Models 3 and 4 on 44 team scores: the standard deviation coefficient was 1.09 (p < 0.05) uncontrolled and 0.74 (p < 0.1) when controlling for the mean, weaker than the best or mean effects.

> "However, the effect of the standard deviation of member performances is less significant than either the effect of the mean or the effect of the best of member performances. When we control for the mean of member performances using Model 4, the effect is weaker."
