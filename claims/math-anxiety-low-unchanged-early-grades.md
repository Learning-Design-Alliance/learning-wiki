`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate latent growth curve modeling of anxiety measured with the AEQ-ES at four time points in the same 232-student Norwegian sample. The article reports levels "remained low and did not change significantly", though individual differences in initial levels and slopes were significant.

> "whereas the levels of MA remained low and did not change significantly during the three years (see, Figure 1). However, significant individual differences in both the initial levels and the slopes were observed in all achievement emotions"
