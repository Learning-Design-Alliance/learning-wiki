## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants who completed the pre-and-post course quiz had knowledge scores that rose from baseline to post-course in one group measured before and after, with no comparison group. [→ Davies E. Bethan 2018](#davies-e-bethan-2018)

## Evidence

### Davies E. Bethan 2018

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-arm pre-post change on the 20-item true/false quiz embedded in the eLearning platform, among the n = 11 intervention participants who completed it; "rising by over three points to 14.72 (± 2.14)".

> "Participants' mean knowledge scores at baseline were 11.45 (± 1.21) out of possible maximum score of 20, rising by over three points to 14.72 (± 2.14) at post-course (Z = − 2.94, p = .003, n = 11)."
