Pearson correlation of age with reading scores in the metacognitive group (N=30); Table 14 prints r = -.427, significant at the 0.05 level (p = .019). The article describes "a significant reversed relationship".

"Furthermore, between two variables there is a significant reversed relationship. Besides, increasing learners' age leads to a reduction in reading scores in metacognitive group."
