## Evidence

### Zheng Koulong 2024

Zheng Koulong, Shen Zhiyu, Chen Zanhao, Che Chang, Zhu Huixia. (2024). Application of AI-empowered scenario-based simulation teaching mode in cardiovascular disease education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05977-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment comparing 34 experimental-group and 32 control-group medical undergraduates. Table 2 reports theoretical exam means of 82.91 ± 5.054 versus 72.16 ± 7.595, with "t 6.811" and "P-value < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

> "students in the experimental group had an average score of 83.26 on the theoretical final exam, whereas students in the control group had an average score of 79.56. The scores of the control group were significantly lower than those of the experimental group (p < 0.05)."
