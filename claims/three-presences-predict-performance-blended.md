## Evidence

### Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua 2025

Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua. (2025). Mediating role of online academic emotions between online presence and learning performance in blended learning environments. Scientific Reports 15, 29875. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-15947-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational results (Table 1) from the same 971-student survey; learning performance measured via communication ability, self-regulated learning, perseverance, and innovative thinking. All three presence dimensions were "significantly and positively correlated (p < 0.01) with learning performance". No standardised effect size is printed for this statement.

> "In addition, teaching presence, social presence, and cognitive presence were all significantly and positively correlated (p < 0.01) with learning performance. Thus, Hypotheses H2a, H2b, and H2c were valid."
