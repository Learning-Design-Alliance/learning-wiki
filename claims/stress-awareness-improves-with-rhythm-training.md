# Stress awareness improved to a greater extent in the rhythm-trained groups in readers with and without difficulties

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-Palma 2026

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Non-parametric longitudinal analyses; Table 1 prints simple-effect sizes of Cliff's delta = .51 (PAR) and Cliff's delta = .42 (RM) for children without difficulties, and Table 2 prints Cliff's delta = .53 and Cliff's delta = .56 for children with difficulties, both interactions significant at p < .01.

> "In the case of the measure of linguistic rhythm (stress awareness), there was a significant interaction, and the effect size was larger in the two groups in which rhythm was trained."
