# The no-thin-pillar rule was diagnostically informative in three of four cases and the binding constraint in one

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Across four retrospective capstone cases, the no-thin-pillar rule surfaced cyber-physical and data-driven-decision gaps hidden behind strong analytics profiles in three cases and blocked certification in one. [→ Smith 2026](#smith-2026)

A Conceptual Framework for Enhancing Workforce Readiness for Smart Manufacturing in the AI Era. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11540 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.11540](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.11540)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-case synthesis of four retrospective case studies scored by the CDI instructional team from archived capstone artifacts. The rule flagged at-floor P2/P3 pillars "behind otherwise strong P1 profiles" without blocking certification in Cases 1 and 2.

> "The no-thin-pillar rule helped flag gaps in Cases 1 and 2, surfacing P2 or P3 pillars sitting at the floor behind otherwise strong P1 profiles; the rule was not the binding gate for any student in these two cases, but it identified the pillars in most need of remediation"
