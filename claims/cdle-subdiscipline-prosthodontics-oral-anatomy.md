Sub-discipline analysis of the dentistry questions (ten sub-disciplines) in the 600-question CDLE benchmark, with chi-square/Fisher's exact tests and Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons. The article reports "Gemini 2.5 Pro achieved the highest accuracy at 100%, significantly exceeding GPT-4o (68.97%) (p = 0.019)" in oral anatomy, and GPT-5 Thinking, DeepSeek-R1, and Gemini 2.5 Pro all outperformed GPT-4o in prosthodontics.
