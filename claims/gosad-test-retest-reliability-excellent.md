## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test-retest analysis in which the same three raters re-scored six randomly selected debriefings (~10% of the original 57) two months later. The article reports "ICC of 0.976" and notes this exceeds the original OSAD's reported 0.89. No effect size printed.

> "The test-retest reliability of the total average scores of the 10% G-OSAD ratings reassessed after 2 months showed ICC of 0.976 (95% confidence interval, 0.837–0.997; p = 0.001)."
