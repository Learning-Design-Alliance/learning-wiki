## Evidence

### BookNook: Evidence Rating Strong 2023

BookNook: Evidence Rating Strong. (2023). https://www.booknook.org

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized study in six Rocketship public charter schools in Northern California; cohorts of first through fourth grade students randomly assigned within grades. The final sample included 1,777 students (n=959 treatment, 818 control), and the treatment group showed significantly more growth than the control group on the NWEA MAP, with a printed effect size of ES = +0.05.

> "After 12 weeks, students in cohorts assigned to receive BookNook demonstrated significantly more growth than the control group on the NWEA MAP (ES = +0.05), qualifying BookNook for a “Strong” rating."
