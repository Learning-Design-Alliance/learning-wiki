### Yan Xinjian 2024

Enhancing cosmetic suturing skill acquisition in surgical residents through spaced learning training: a randomized controlled trial. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2363940

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three-month follow-up test of the randomized controlled trial; residents had no further cosmetic suturing practice during the interval. "the spaced training group had significantly higher test total scores, suture quality scores, suture technique scores, and suturing time scores" on all four measures; no effect sizes printed.

> "the spaced training group had significantly higher test total scores, suture quality scores, suture technique scores, and suturing time scores than the concentrated training group (75.60 ± 4.78 vs. 60.68 ± 10.40, p = 0.0001; 32.26 ± 4.01 vs. 26.23 ± 4.16, p = 0.0019; 28.68 ± 2.63 vs. 22.18 ± 3.94, p = 0.0001; 14.67 ± 1.15 vs. 12.27 ± 6.07, p = 0.0460)"
