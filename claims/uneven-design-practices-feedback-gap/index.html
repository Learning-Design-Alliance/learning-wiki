Gibson 2025¶

Gibson, D., Azukas, M. E., & Knezek, G. (2025). Practitioner Beliefs and Behaviors in AI-Enhanced Education: DOT Framework Survey Evidence.

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Respondents (n = 72) rated four design-thinking-aligned behaviors on a three-point scale (1 = Never, 3 = Always). Experimenting with prompts was most frequent (M = 2.65); needs identification (M = 2.53) and outlining goals (M = 2.43) were moderate.

"Collecting feedback and iterating on AI-generated outputs was least frequent (M = 2.32, SD = 0.77), a finding suggesting that the feedback loop central to both open systems theory and design thinking's testing stage remains the least-developed dimension of current practice."
