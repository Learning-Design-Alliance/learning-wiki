## Evidence

### Conlon Peter 2012

Conlon Peter, Hecker Kent, Sabatini Susan. (2012). What should we be selecting for? A systematic approach for determining which personal characteristics to assess for during admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-105

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA comparing practice owners (n = 465) and non-owners (n = 468) from the paired-comparison survey (Wilk's Lambda = .93, F(11, 921) = 5.40, p < .001). Non-owners scored communication and empathy higher; owners scored personal management skills and sound judgment higher, with printed effect sizes d = 0.20 to 0.32.

> "The non-owners scored communication skills (F(1,931) =24.39, p < .001, d = 0.32) and empathy (F (1,931) =9.09, p = .003, d =0.20) higher than the owners."
