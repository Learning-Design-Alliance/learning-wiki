### Zheng Hui 2026

Zheng Hui, Ouyang Yun, Zeng Yifei, He Xinyu, Song Liping, Yu Xiangyuan. (2026). Systematic evaluation of the teaching effectiveness of flipped classroom mode in undergraduate preventive medicine courses in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896815

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 8 studies; substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 91.8%, p < 0.001) led to a random-effects model. Heterogeneity for problem-solving ability was substantially reduced (I2 = 0%, p = 0.558) in subgroups with experimental group sample size > 100. The article prints no standardized effect size for this finding.
