## Evidence

### Arora Samridhi 2025

Arora Samridhi, Shingne Ranjana, Ravi Sathyanarayan Kelegere, Jha Indira. (2025). Comparison of Self-directed Learning and Didactic Lecture in Teaching Clinical Anatomy to 1st Year MBBS Students. Annals of African Medicine. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_104_25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention MCQ administered 4 weeks after the sessions in the crossover study of 124 first-year MBBS students: "Batch A was 6.01 ± 0.71, and Batch B was 6.61 ± 0.73", reported significant at P = 0.0009; no effect size printed.

> "Mean scores of the retention test conducted 4 weeks after the didactic lecture. Batch A was 6.01 ± 0.71, and Batch B was 6.61 ± 0.73. P = 0.0009 by Student’s paired test"

### Arora Samridhi 2025 (2)

Arora Samridhi, Shingne Ranjana, Ravi Sathyanarayan Kelegere, Jha Indira. (2025). Comparison of Self-directed Learning and Didactic Lecture in Teaching Clinical Anatomy to 1st Year MBBS Students. Annals of African Medicine. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_104_25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second retention comparison in the same 4-week follow-up: "Didactic lecture 6.31 ± 0.779 and SDL 6.99 ± 0.68", reported significant at P = 0.0001; no effect size printed.

> "Comparing mean scores of the retention test conducted 4 weeks after the SDL session. Didactic lecture 6.31 ± 0.779 and SDL 6.99 ± 0.68 Student’s paired t-test, P = 0.0001"
