The review reports, citing Barteit et al., a systematic review of head-mounted devices including 27 studies with 956 participants, mostly medical students (N=573, 59.9%) and residents (N=289, 30.2%), finding results at least non-inferior to conventional teaching.

> "Moreover, Barteit et al. included 27 studies comprising 956 participants (24). The participants represented all types of healthcare professionals, particularly medical students (N=573, 59.9%) and residents (N=289, 30.2%). Most of these studies showed that AR and VR achieved results that were at least non-inferior to conventional teaching and training."
