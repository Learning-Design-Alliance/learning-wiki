Individual case analysis in Appendix B of Subject 12, a resident in the intervention group, across four sessions. The authors report the subject took longer to scan CXRs in Session 2 (the first after feedback), with heterogeneity rising then returning to average, and total time ending "less than 25%" below baseline, close to an expert.

> "The misdiagnosis rate for the subject for the final session was 4 out of 18, which is a 75. 00% improvement from the baseline."
