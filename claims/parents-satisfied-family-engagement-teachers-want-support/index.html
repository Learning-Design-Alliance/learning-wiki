Morgan 2024 (2)¶

Morgan, I., Gillispie, C., & Waller, A. (2024). Supporting Young Learners Through Family Engagement: Engaging First. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658182

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same Survey 2, teacher sample of approximately 300 first and second grade teachers. Teachers also reported wanting more professional development (66%) and access to best practices (59%).

"Only 58% of teachers said their school carves out specific time for family engagement, and 73% were concerned that there is not enough time in the day to engage with families."
