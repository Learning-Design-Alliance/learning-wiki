### Özüdoğru and Özüdoğru 2017

Melike Özüdoğru, Fatma Özüdoğru. (2017). The Effect of Situated Learning on Students' Vocational English Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11): 2037-2044. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051120

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test within the control group (N = 58), which received traditional instruction via course book reading, translation, and fill-in-the-blank vocabulary exercises. Mean difference -8.30; the article reports the gain as significant with t(58) = -4.43, p <.05.

> "students’ post -test scores were significantly higher than their pre -test scores, t (58) = -4.43, p <.05. Overall results indicated that there was an improvement in the control group between the pre -test and post -test scores."
