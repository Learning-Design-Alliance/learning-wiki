## Evidence

### Smyth Wendy 2013

Smyth Wendy, Fielding Elaine, Beattie Elizabeth, Gardner Anne, Moyle Wendy, Franklin Sara, Hines Sonia, MacAndrew Margaret. (2013). A survey-based study of knowledge of Alzheimer's disease among health care staff. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2318-13-2

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation between an 11-point self-rated knowledge scale (mean 5.2, SD 2.23) and ADKS score in the survey (n = 325 for the self-rating; Table 1 reports r = .367). The printed correlation r = .37 is a small-to-moderate effect.

> "Respondents who rated themselves as having more knowledge of dementia tended to score significantly higher on the ADKS (r = .37, p <.001)."
