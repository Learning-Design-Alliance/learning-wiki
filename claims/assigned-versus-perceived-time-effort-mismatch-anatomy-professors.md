## Evidence

### Hall TR 2025

Hall TR, Mosley CF, Vakil JB, Lambert CK, Savelli DM, Balta JY. (2025). Who is teaching anatomy? An examination of the demographic characteristics, academic backgrounds, and professional responsibilities of US anatomy teachers. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing assigned and perceived percent time effort among full, associate, and assistant professors (assigned means: 58% teaching, 16% research, 10% service, 10% administration). Only test statistics and r² values were printed; no standardized effect size was reported.

> "Results indicated that significant differences existed between actual and perceived administration (Z = −5.37, p < 0.01, r 2 = 0.07), research (Z = 5.76, p < 0.01, r 2 = 0.08), teaching (Z = 2.09, p < 0.04, r 2 = 0.01), continuing education (Z = −2.85, p < 0.01, r 2 = 0.02) and mentoring (Z = −6.68, p < 0.01, r 2 = 0.11) percent time efforts."
