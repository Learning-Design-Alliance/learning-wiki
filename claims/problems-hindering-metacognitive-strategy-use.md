# Lack of knowledge of cognition, regulation of cognition, and intrinsic motivation hinder learners' metacognitive strategy use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Interviews with four successful and four less successful participants revealed that most lacked knowledge of what reading strategies are and how to apply them, most did not use self-regulation strategies, and most were extrinsically rather than intrinsically motivated to read in English. [→ Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh 2011](#thao-m-t-nguyen-and-lap-q-trinh-2011)

## Evidence

### Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh 2011

Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh. (2011). Learners' Metacognitive Strategy Use and Reading Comprehension: Insights from a Vietnamese Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 1, January - March 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071083

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of interviews with eight purposively selected participants; five of eight lacked knowledge of cognition, six of eight did not use self-regulation strategies, and six of eight were more extrinsically than intrinsically motivated.

> "Data from the interviews illustrated that five out of eight participants were not fully aware of what these strategies are. They did not know what these strategies are, and they did not know how, when and why to apply these strategies when reading."
