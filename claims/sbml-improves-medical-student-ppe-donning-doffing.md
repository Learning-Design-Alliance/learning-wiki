## Evidence

### Miller Danielle T. MD 2022

Miller Danielle T. MD, MEd; Pokrajac Nicholas MD; Ngo Jessica MD; Gallegos Moises MD, MPH; Dixon William MD, MSEd; Roszczynialski Kelly N. MD; Ng Kristen MD; Taleghani Nounou MD, PhD; Gisondi Michael A. MD (2022). Simulation-Based Mastery Learning Improves the Performance of Donning and Doffing of Personal Protective Equipment by Medical Students. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2022.2.54748

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-test/post-test study of 155 medical students at a university teaching hospital; after baseline testing only 153 entered SBML and 151 reached mastery ("98.7% of students (151/153) achieved mastery"). Paired t-test showed improvement from "a mean baseline testing score of 12.55/21" to 21/21.

> "Performance improved from a mean baseline testing score of 12.55/21 (standard deviation [SD] = 2.86), to a post-test mean score of 21/21(SD = 0), t(150) = 36.3, P <0.001)"
