`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review contrasts classroom pilots of Cognitive Tutor and LISTEN in the developing world, where students navigated software fairly quickly, with Gitau, Marsden, and Donner (2010)'s finding that mobile-only Internet users in South Africa required tutorials and user groups to reach web applications.

> "Basic ICT skills by learners were not a major factor in classroom settings but posed major hurdles for individual ICT use."
