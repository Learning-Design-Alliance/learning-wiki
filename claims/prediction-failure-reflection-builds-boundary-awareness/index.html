Evidence¶

Li Li 2025¶

Li Li, Yu Cao. (2025). Awareness of Technological Isomorphism: Integrating AI into Elementary Mathematics Teaching on Data and Prediction, A Case Study of the Compound Line Graph. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09598

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative discourse analysis of Segment 3 (Reflection on Prediction Failures, 5 minutes), using a secondary dataset in which the flask lid was opened at the 90-minute mark. Students initially estimated about 67°C from trend; the actual value was 55°C, and S2 exclaimed "Because it didn't know the lid was opened!"

"The AI predicted 67°C, but the actual temperature was 55°C! The prediction error increased significantly by 12°C! Why did the model produce such a large deviation here?"

Li Li 2025 (2)¶

Li Li, Yu Cao. (2025). Awareness of Technological Isomorphism: Integrating AI into Elementary Mathematics Teaching on Data and Prediction, A Case Study of the Compound Line Graph. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09598

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The authors' interpretation of the same Segment 3 discourse: students recognized that human reasoning accesses situational context unavailable to the model, which remains confined to numerical inputs. Subsequent articulations by S5 and S6 used the boundaries of structural correspondence to explain AI's limits.

"Classroom observations in Segment 3 suggest that S2's statement marked a pivotal shift wherein Technological Isomorphism Awareness transitioned from basic recognition to boundary awareness."
