# Win-stay/lose-shift associations are not privileged: consistency sometimes favours win-shift, and reaction times show no outcome-by-action interaction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In win maximization, participants were significantly less consistent deploying win-stay than win-shift (r = 0.708 vs [abs] r = 0.874; z = -2.935, p = 0.003), with no significant difference in loss maximization conditions. [→ Yajing Zhang 2023](#yajing-zhang-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of outcome-action associations within Experiment 1: "participants were signi ﬁcantly less consistent in their ability to deploy win-stay behaviour (during repetition) than win-shift behaviour (during alternation)" (r = 0.708 vs [abs] r = 0.874; z = -2.935, p = 0.003).
