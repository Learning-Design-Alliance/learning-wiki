## Evidence

### Almutairi et al. 2020

Faisal M. Almutairi, Abdullah A. Almodaires & Alaa J. Zeyab. (2020). Effectiveness of Flipped Learning: Improving Pre-Service Teachers' Prowess in Producing Videos. International Education Studies, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v13n7p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test within the experimental group (N=37) in the quasi-experiment found a significant pre-test/post-test difference (Z=5.153, p=.00001<.05); the authors conclude "a significant difference does exist". No effect size was printed.

> "there is a significant difference between the pre-test and post-test scores of the pre-service teachers in the experimental group (Z=5.153, p=.00001<.05)"

### Almutairi et al. 2020 (2)

Faisal M. Almutairi, Abdullah A. Almodaires & Alaa J. Zeyab. (2020). Effectiveness of Flipped Learning: Improving Pre-Service Teachers' Prowess in Producing Videos. International Education Studies, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v13n7p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test within the control group in the same quasi-experiment found a significant pre-test/post-test difference (Z=-5.2093, p=.00001<.05). The authors add that the control group's gains did not reach the levels demonstrated by the experimental group, an untested assertion. No effect size was printed.

> "there is a significant difference between the pre-test and post-test scores of the pre-service teachers in the control group (Z=-5.2093, p=.00001<.05)"
