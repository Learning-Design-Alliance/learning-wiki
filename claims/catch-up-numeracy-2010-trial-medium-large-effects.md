### National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER) 2014

National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER), evaluation team led by Simon Rutt. (2014). Catch Up® Numeracy: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The report describes a 2010 trial with 246 pupils across eleven local authorities (Dowker and Sigley, 2010), tested before and after with the Basic Number Screening Test. It prints effect sizes of 0.3 and 0.4 standard deviations, medium to large, but schools rather than randomisation assigned pupils to groups, which the report notes may have introduced bias of unknown direction.

> "Post-test results suggest the intervention had medium to large effects: 0.3 standard deviations versus matched-time controls, and 0.4 standard d eviations versus pure controls. These results were found to be highly statistically significant."
