Appendix A1 of the Cave et al. (1993) randomized controlled trial reports baseline balance checks among the 1,941 respondents (85% of JOBSTART and 83% of control youth responded to the 48-month survey) on gender, ethnicity, family structure, employment history, and public assistance receipt.

"They found no statistically significant differences between the research groups on these characteristics."
