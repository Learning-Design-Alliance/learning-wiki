# MI-based strategy improves five intelligence types while logical-mathematical intelligence decreases, partly due to instrument bias

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experimental-group students improved primarily in intrapersonal, interpersonal, kinesthetic, visual-spatial and musical intelligences, while logical-mathematical intelligence tended to remain or decrease. [→ Winarti 2019](#winarti-2019)
`q2 i?` The logical-mathematical decrease may reflect the instrument: 5 of its 7 items concern numbers and calculation rather than logic, so scientific-logic gains were not captured. [→ Winarti 2019 (2)](#winarti-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Winarti 2019

Winarti, A., Yuanita, L., & Nur, M. (2019). The effectiveness of multiple intelligences based teaching strategy in enhancing the multiple intelligences and Science Process Skills of junior high school students. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 9(2), 122-135. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.404

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of MI sub-scores before and after the 12-week quasi-experiment; the experimental group improved in five named intelligence types while control-group MI scores did not improve. No effect sizes are printed for the sub-scores.

> "In the experimental group, students' MI scores improved primarily in five types of intelligences, namely intrapersonal, interpersonal, kinesthetic, visual spatial, and musical intelligences. Linguistic intelligence was slightly increased, while the mathematical-logic intelligence tends to remain."

### Winarti 2019 (2)

Winarti, A., Yuanita, L., & Nur, M. (2019). The effectiveness of multiple intelligences based teaching strategy in enhancing the multiple intelligences and Science Process Skills of junior high school students. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 9(2), 122-135. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.404

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the logical-mathematical decrease: the MI instrument's 7 items for that intelligence are weighted toward calculation, so development of scientific logic was not captured. This is an author explanation, not a tested result.

> "From the 7 items, 5 items are related to numbers, calculation, and interest in mathematics, and only two items relate to the ability of logic."
