# Robot-assisted training with simulated social interaction improves autistic children's on-focus durational expansion and post-focus f0 compression

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Robot-assisted SSI training improved on-focus expansion in duration and produced post-focus compression of mean f0 in autistic children's speech. [→ Chen 2026](#chen-2026)
`q3 i?` After robot-assisted training, post-focus words showed significantly lower f0 than broad focus words with a larger difference than before training. [→ Chen 2026 (2)](#chen-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Authors' summary of pre/post LMM post-hoc comparisons for the robot-assisted SSI training group in the randomized study; on-focus f0 and intensity expansion did not significantly improve. No effect size printed.

> "the robot speech training may improve significantly in f0 in the post-focus position, on-focus expansion in using the acoustic cue of duration and it leads to some improvement in the use of intensity."

### Chen 2026 (2)

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre/post comparison in the robot-assisted SSI group showing post-focus f0 compression strengthened after training, described descriptively with no test statistic or effect size printed.

> "post-focus words have significantly lower f0 than broad focus words in both pre- and post-training sessions, but the difference in f0 between post-focus and broad focus words is larger in post-sessions."
