## Evidence

### Wu Zhiwu 2021

Wu Zhiwu, Li Mingming, Zhu Fangzhi, Lei Lei, Cheng Ran, Hu Tao. (2021). The effects of oral health education regarding periodontal health on non-dental undergraduates in southwestern China—exploring the feasibility of an e-learning course for oral health promotion. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported frequency of six oral care methods surveyed before and after the courses (Fig. 3). The article reports increases in flossing, interproximal brushing, gargling and tongue cleaning (P < 0.001) for both groups, and separately that "the e-learning course group tended to use floss and interproximal brushes more frequently than those who received the traditional course (P < 0.001)".
