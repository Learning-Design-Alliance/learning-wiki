## Evidence

### Gwynne 2024

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., Young, C., & Hart, H. (2024). Student experiences through the COVID-19 pandemic: Attendance, online learning platform use, and annual survey reports. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/district-data/metrics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis relating district attendance data and Google Meets participation to 2020-21 course grades for students in grades 4-12 enrolled in regular district schools. The report found these relationships were "as large or larger during the remote/hybrid year as in pre-pandemic years."

> "We found that the relationships between attendance (measured as official attendance, or time in synchronous instruction) and course grades were as large or larger during the remote/hybrid year as in pre-pandemic years."
