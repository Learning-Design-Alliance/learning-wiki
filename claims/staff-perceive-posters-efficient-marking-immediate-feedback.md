## Evidence

### Ross Andrew 2019

Ross Andrew, Dlungwane Thembelihle, Van Wyk Jacqueline. (2019). Using poster presentation to assess large classes: a case study of a first-year undergraduate module at a South African university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1863-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Staff Likert questionnaire (15 items) with 10 of 12 assessors responding in the same case study; 100% agreed marking was more efficient than written assignments, and 100% agreed posters were "an effective way to provide feedback to all students". All marking was completed in a 4-hour period.
