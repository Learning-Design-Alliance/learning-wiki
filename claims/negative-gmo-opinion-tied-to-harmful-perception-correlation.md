## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2019

Chrispeels H. E., Chapman J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2019). Peer Teaching Increases Knowledge and Changes Perceptions about Genetically Modified Crops in Non–Science Major Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional correlation on pre-SLP responses (n = 37), Fisher's exact test with Bonferroni-corrected post hoc comparison; correlation of disagreement with harmful/suspect perception at p = 0.0002.

> "there was a significant correlation between students who disagreed with the use of GMOs and those who perceived them as harmful or suspect before the SLP (Fisher's exact test followed by post hoc pairwise comparison with Bonferroni correction; p = 0.0002)"
