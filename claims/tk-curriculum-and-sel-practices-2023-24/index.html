q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statewide LEA survey reported in the TK Instruction and Assessment section and Table 5. The report notes about one fifth (20%) of LEAs used kindergarten curriculum in TK classrooms, down from 26%.

"In the 2023–24 school year, LEAs reported using a variety of curricula in their TK programs, with most LEAs (72%) using more than one curriculum type. About half of LEAs used a social-emotional curriculum (49%) and/or a PreK literacy-specific curriculum (46%)"

Wang 2025 (2)¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statewide LEA survey reported in the TK Instruction and Assessment section and Table 7. The report states the percentage of LEAs reporting each listed social-emotional learning strategy increased from 2022–23 to 2023–24.

"LEAs used a variety of strategies in 2023–24 to create such learning environments, most commonly through promoting play-based learning (79%) and designing developmentally appropriate learning environments (70%)."
