## Evidence

### Rotgans 2011

Rotgans, J. I., & Schmidt, H. G. (2011). Cognitive engagement in the problem-based learning classroom. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 16(4), 465–479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9272-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of the five repeated measurements in the 208-student sample, testing a sequential causal model. The article reports the model fitted well (Chi-square/df = 2.06, p = .10, CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = .07) and "81% of the variance in the last situational cognitive engagement measure could be explained"; no standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Overall, 81% of the variance in the last situational cognitive engagement measure could be explained by the preceding ones."
