## Evidence

### Mascheroni J 2024

Mascheroni J, Stockburger M, Patwala A, Mont L, Rao A, Retzlaff H, Garweg C, Gallagher AG, Verbelen T. (2024). Surgical skill simulation training to proficiency reduces procedural errors among novice cardiac device implanters: a randomized study. Europace. https://doi.org/10.1093/europace/euae229

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

χ2 test of independence in the randomized study (n = 30) relating training group to benchmark demonstration: "χ2 (1, n = 30) = 8.57, P = 0.003", with 73% versus 20% of trainees reaching the benchmark.

> "In the objectively assessed video-recorded implant performance, 11/15 (73%) trainees in the PBP group and 3/15 (20%) in the SIM group demonstrated the skills proficiency benchmark. The relationship between training group and benchmark demonstration was significant, χ2 (1, n = 30) = 8.57, P = 0.003."
