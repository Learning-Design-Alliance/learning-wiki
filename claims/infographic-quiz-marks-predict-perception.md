# Regression shows a significant relationship between quiz marks and students' perception in both colleges, though far stronger in the IT College (R-square 0.919) than the Business College (R-square 0.226)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alqudah 2019

Alqudah, D., Bidin, A., & Md Hussin, M. A. H. (2019). The Impact of Educational Infographic on Students’ Interaction and Perception in Jordanian Higher Education: Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 669-688. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12443a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simple regression with mean quiz marks (MM) predicting mean perception responses (MP), run separately per college. The IT model was significant at 0.00 with F = 204.273 and R-square change 0.919; Table 7 shows the Business model significant at 0.03 with F = 10.541 and R-square change 0.226. The article's text repeats the 0.919 value for the Business College, which conflicts with its own Table 7 value of 0.226.

> "the significance level is 0.00, which is less than 0.05 when F value is 204.273. Thus, H0 can be rejected, which means that significant effects exist when using infographic in instructional lectures on receiving and perception knowledge by students."
