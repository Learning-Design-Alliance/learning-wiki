### Lohela Terhi Johanna 2016

Lohela Terhi Johanna, Nesbitt Robin Clark, Manu Alexander, Vesel Linda, Okyere Eunice, Kirkwood Betty, Gabrysch Sabine. (2016). Competence of health workers in emergency obstetric care: an assessment using clinical vignettes in Brong Ahafo region, Ghana. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional facility assessment comparing median vignette scores across hospitals, health centres, maternity homes and clinics with Kruskal-Wallis tests (n=64 respondents). The abstract states "Competence varied significantly by facility type", with hospitals highest and clinics lowest; the Results section reports p<0.001 for this association.

> "Competence varied significantly by facility type: hospital respondents, who were mainly doctors and midwives, achieved highest scores (70% correct) and clinic respondents scored lowest (45% correct)."
