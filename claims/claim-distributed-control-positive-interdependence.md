Qualitative analysis of artifacts, field notes and video interviews from a six-session enrichment program with 3rd and 4th graders (14 students analyzed). Across all groups the authors report the distributed infrastructure produced "positive interdependence" with "coordinated, non -redundant contributions towards a common goal".
