## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Workshop participants identified lack of teacher competence (from minimal training or continuing professional development) and lack of confidence (stemming from worries around classroom management) as central barriers to implementing PAL. [→ Daly-Smith A. 2019](#daly-smith-a-2019)

## Evidence

### Daly-Smith A. 2019

Daly-Smith A., Quarmby T., Archbold V.S.J., Routen A.C., Morris J.L., Gammon C., Bartholomew J.B., Resaland G.K., Llewellyn B., Allman R., Dorling H. (2019). Implementing physically active learning: Future directions for research, policy, and practice. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2019.05.007

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from inductive analysis of workshop group discussions; participants linked confidence to worries around classroom management and to feeling comfortable with a more chaotic classroom. The article states participants "identified a lack of teacher confidence as a central barrier to implementation".

> "Alongside a lack of awareness about how PAL might be implemented, where and when to use it, and how it might be sustained throughout a period of time, participants also identified a lack of teacher confidence as a central barrier to implementation"
