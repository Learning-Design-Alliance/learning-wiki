## Evidence

### Kumar Manish 2025

Kumar Manish, Mittal Mahima, Chavan Seema Shankarsingh, Krishnan S Vimal, Gilbert John H.V., Sanatombi Devi Elsa. (2025). Interprofessional simulation training in acute pediatrics using rapid cycle deliberate practice (ip-star). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08302-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired Likert survey (n = 72, Wilcoxon signed rank test) administered before and after the intervention; Table 3 shows all ten confidence items improving to a post-intervention median of 4, each with p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.
