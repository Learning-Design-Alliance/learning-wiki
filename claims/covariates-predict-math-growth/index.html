Evidence¶

Prast 2018¶

Prast, E. J., Van de Weijer-Bergsma, E., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Van Luit, J. E. H. (2018). Differentiated instruction in primary mathematics: Effects of teacher professional development on student achievement. Learning and Instruction, 54, 22-34. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.009

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Latent growth curve Model 1 (N = 4751, Year 1) with covariates predicting intercept and slope of CMT competence scores. All covariates had significant positive effects on the intercept, largest for grade level; in Year 2 the covariates explained 88% of intercept and 22% of slope variance.

"Taken together, grade level, nonverbal intelligence, visual-spatial working memory and verbal working memory explained 88% of the variance of the intercept and 16% of the variance of the slope of mathematics achievement."
