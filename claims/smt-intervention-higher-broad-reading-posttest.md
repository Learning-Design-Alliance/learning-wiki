## Evidence

### Taub and Lazarus 2012

Gordon E. Taub, Philip J. Lazarus. (2012). The Effects Of Training In Timing And Rhythm On Reading Achievement. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, Volume 5, Number 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073174

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pre-test/post-test control group study of 280 high school students; one-way ANCOVA with three pre-test covariates found the group main effect on post-test Broad Reading, "F (1, 244) = 7.86, p  = .005", with a small effect "η 2 = .031", accounting for 3.1% of variance.

> "These data indicate that the experimental group’s post-test scores were statistically significantly higher than the control group’s post -test scores on the Broad Reading dependent variable, F (1, 244) = 7.86, p  = .005. The treatment had a small effect on Broad Reading post-test scores (η 2 = .031)"
