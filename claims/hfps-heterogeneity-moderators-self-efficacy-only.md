## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Subgroup analyses found scenario (ANOVA Q-test 11.43, p=0.003), manikin brand (Q-test 10.59, p=0.001) and control intervention (Q-test 13.37, p=0.010) were sources of heterogeneity for self-efficacy, but not for the remaining outcomes. [→ La Cerra Carmen 2019](#la-cerra-carmen-2019)

## Evidence

### La Cerra Carmen 2019

La Cerra Carmen, Dante Angelo, Caponnetto Valeria, Franconi Ilaria, Gaxhja Elona, Petrucci Cristina, Alfes Celeste M, Lancia Loreto. (2019). Effects of high-fidelity simulation based on life-threatening clinical condition scenarios on learning outcomes of undergraduate and postgraduate nursing students: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open 9(2):e025306. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025306

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup moderator analyses across the meta-analysed outcomes, using scenario, manikin brand and control intervention as moderators. Overall heterogeneity was significant for all outcomes ("I2 from 70.09% to 89.85%").

> "The scenario (analysis of variance [ANOVA] Q-test 11.43, p=0.003), manikin brand (ANOVA Q-test 10.59, p=0.001), and control intervention (ANOVA Q-test 13.37, p=0.010) appeared to be the source of heterogeneity for self-efficacy."
