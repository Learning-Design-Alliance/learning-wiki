## Evidence

### Pomerantz et al. 2007

Eva M. Pomerantz, Elizabeth A. Moorman and Scott D. Litwack. (2007). The How, Whom, and Why of Parents' Involvement in Children's Academic Lives: More Is Not Always Better. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430305567

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this to Mueller and Dweck's laboratory experiment in which an unknown adult gave elementary school children process-focused (effort) versus person-focused (ability) praise; children receiving process-focused praise 'persisted to a greater extent' and performed better after failure. No effect size is printed.

> "Children given process-focused praise also persisted to a greater extent, expressed more positive affect, and performed better in the face of failure."
