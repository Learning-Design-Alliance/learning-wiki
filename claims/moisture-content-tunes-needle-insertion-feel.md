## Evidence

### Yamada Toshiyuki 2017

Yamada Toshiyuki, Osako Motohiko, Uchimuro Tomoya, Yoon Ryogen, Morikawa Toshiaki, Sugimoto Maki, Suda Hisao, Shimizu Hideyuki. (2017). Three-Dimensional Printing of Life-Like Models for Simulation and Training of Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery. Innovations (Philadelphia, Pa.). https://doi.org/10.1097/IMI.0000000000000423

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A mechanical/physical measurement of dried versus pre-drying weight of three porcine hearts to determine moisture content, used to tune the model material; the tactile similarity of needle insertion is the authors' qualitative judgment, with no test statistic.

> "In addition, the moisture content of the porcine heart was 76%, which we used in the model. At this percentage, the feeling of needle insertion was most similar to the feeling experienced during an actual surgery."
