## Evidence

### W. H. Winch 1910

W. H. Winch. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy 'transfer'? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Winch+accuracy+school+children

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The author's interpretive conclusion (General Conclusions section) drawn from the discrepancy between the high correlations he measured and the absent or irregular transfer effects. He notes the point accords with correlational theory but states it because educationists may over-infer from high correlation.

> "Two quantities may be highly correlated hut the ratios of their growth may not be; nor may we be able to produce increase in the one by producing increase in the other."
