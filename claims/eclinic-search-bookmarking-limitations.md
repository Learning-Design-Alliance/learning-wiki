### Al-Jasmi Fatma 2010

Al-Jasmi Fatma, Moldovan Laura, Clarke Joe TR. (2010). Hunter disease eClinic: interactive, computer-assisted, problem-based approach to independent learning about a rare genetic disease. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-72

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' own stated limitation in the Discussion. They note the learner "must rely on the table of contents to locate a topic of interest" and that bookmarking could not be implemented owing to the technical limitations of the Flash software; hyperlinks to web-based search engines such as PubMed partially compensate.

> "One of the limitations of the eClinic program is the lack of an index or an internal search engine enabling the learner to undertake searches for specific topics covered in the eBook or the eClinic."
