## Evidence

### James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann 2023

James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann. (2023). A Testing Load: Investigating Test Mode Effects on Test Score, Cognitive Load and Scratch Paper Use with Secondary School Students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09781-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the perceived-difficulty hierarchical linear model, "Students rated paper questions as more difficult (M = 4.17, 95% CI = 3.46, 4.88) compared to computer questions (M = 3.97, 95% CI = 3.26, 4.68)"; the mode main effect was significant (F(1,760) = 11.66, p < 0.001) but the interaction was not (p = 0.052).
