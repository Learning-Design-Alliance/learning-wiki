## Evidence

### Lahme 2025

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Weekly-survey analysis of self-reported time spent (winsorized to 5–300 min, quartiles considered) across nine Exp- and three Pro-tasks; the article reports "no significant difference", p= 0.43, r= 0.25. Median times ranged from 20 min (Exp 2) to 90 min (Pro 1).

> "Mann-Whitney-U test revealed no significant difference regarding
the average time spent on task between the nine Exp-tasks and three Pro-tasks (U = 9.00, Z= 0.85, p= 0.43, r= 0.25)."
