The article reports, citing Razzaq and Heffernan (2006), that uncontrolled hint use risks reducing learning and autonomy, and notes that when to offer hints should be carefully evaluated because unmotivated learners may prioritize speed over reasoning.

> "However, a continuous and uncontrolled use of hints could reduce considerably both the students' learning and their autonomous solving capacity, as Razzaq and Heffernan (2006) pointed out."
