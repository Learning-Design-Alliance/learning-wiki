`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

By comparing the top-10 lists from 2018 and 2026 for each role, the authors identified competency areas that did not change their listing. For Clinical Nursing these included "nursing documentation, ethics and legal issues, data protection and privacy, and eHealth and electronic health records."

> "These competency areas formed a set of invariants that established the core of health informatics for nurses over the years (Supplementary Appendix F, available in the online version only)."
