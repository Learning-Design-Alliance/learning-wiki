# Student age significantly moderates the BFLPE, which is strongest in high school (β = −0.32) and weakest in primary school (β = −0.21)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The BFLPE occurs at all ages but is strongest among high school students and weakest among primary school students. [→ Fang J 2018](#fang-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Fang J 2018

Fang J, Huang X, Zhang M, Huang F, Li Z and Yuan Q (2018) The Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect on Academic Self-Concept: A Meta-Analysis. Front. Psychol. 9:1569. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01569

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Mixed-effects moderator analysis across the 56 effect sizes; the main effect of student age was significant (Z = −17.56, p < 0.001; Q = 7.86, df = 3, p < 0.05), with β = −0.32 for high school and β = −0.21 for primary school. The article concludes the BFLPE "occurs at primary school age and reaches peak value during high school."

> "students in high school indicate the strongest eﬀect ( βhighschool = − 0.32), while middle school and college students show a moderate eﬀect ( βmiddleschool = − 0.28, βcollege = −0.23)"
