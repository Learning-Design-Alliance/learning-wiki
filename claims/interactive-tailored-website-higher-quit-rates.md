### Lehto Tuomas MSc 2011

Lehto Tuomas MSc, Oinas-Kukkonen Harri PhD. (2011). Persuasive Features in Web-Based Alcohol and Smoking Interventions: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3222186/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

RCT among 2523 recruited smokeless tobacco users comparing an interactive, tailored Web-based intervention (ChewFree.com enhanced) versus a more linear, text-based website. The review reports "Participants in the enhanced condition quit at significantly higher rates (vs basic condition). Abstinence was 40.6% in the enhanced condition vs 21.2% in the basic condition (P < .001)."

> "Participants in the enhanced condition quit at significantly higher rates (vs basic condition). Abstinence was 40.6% in the enhanced condition vs 21.2% in the basic condition (P < .001). Using intent-to-treat analysis, quit rates were 12.6% vs 7.9% (P < .001)"
