Evidence¶

Damsgaard 2023¶

Damsgaard, L., Nielsen, A.-M. V., Topor, M. K., Hansen, R. A., Jensen, S. K., Markers, R. L., Gejl, A. K., Malling, A. S. B., & Wienecke, J. (2023). Embodied Learning Activities Focusing on Letter-Sound Knowledge Increase Spelling Performance in 1st Grade Children with Low and High Reading Ability. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09791-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of the RCT comparing low performers (below median baseline word reading; n=26 total) in MOVE (n=12) versus CON (n=14) using linear mixed models found significantly higher improvement for low performers in MOVE on letter-sound measures.

"Between-group analyses revealed a significantly higher improvement for low performers in MOVE compared to low performers in CON from T1 to T2 in all letter-sounds (p < 0.0001, mean = 4.1, 95% CI (2.6, 5.6), d = 2.2), conditional letter-sounds (p < 0.0001, mean = 3.4, 95% CI (2.2, 4.5), d = 2.2)"

Damsgaard 2023 (2)¶

Damsgaard, L., Nielsen, A.-M. V., Topor, M. K., Hansen, R. A., Jensen, S. K., Markers, R. L., Gejl, A. K., Malling, A. S. B., & Wienecke, J. (2023). Embodied Learning Activities Focusing on Letter-Sound Knowledge Increase Spelling Performance in 1st Grade Children with Low and High Reading Ability. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09791-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

The same subgroup analysis showed a significant spelling transfer effect for low performers in MOVE versus low performers in CON, with d = 0.8 reported for the spelling transfer effect.

"and for spelling performance (transfer effect) (p = 0.037, mean = 1.8, 95% CI (0.1, 3.5) d = 0.8) (Fig. 4 )."
