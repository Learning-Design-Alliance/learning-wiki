## Evidence

### Ifenthaler 2010

Ifenthaler, D. (2010). Bridging the Gap between Expert-Novice Differences: The Model-Based Feedback Approach. Journal of Research on Technology in Education, 43(2), 103-117. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2010.10782564

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest comparison of written texts and concept maps from the same participants found low similarity on most of the seven HIMATT measures, with exceptions for graphical, structural, and gamma matching.

> "Interestingly, written text and concept maps seem to represent different structure and content, because we found only low similarities between the seven HIMATT measures."
