## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The large-spacing condition (lag of 15 other-species exemplars) outperformed the small-spacing condition (lag of 3), d = 0.752, with juxtaposition held constant. [→ Monica S. Birnbaum 2013](#monica-s-birnbaum-2013)

## Evidence

### Monica S. Birnbaum 2013

Monica S. Birnbaum, Nate Kornell, Elizabeth Ligon Bjork, & Robert A. Bjork. (2013). Why interleaving enhances inductive learning: The roles of discrimination and retrieval. Memory & Cognition, 41, 392–402. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-012-0272-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 53 UCLA undergraduates studying 16 butterfly species, all interleaved, varying only lag (3 vs. 15 intervening exemplars) while holding juxtaposition, time on task, and time until test constant. "t(51) 0 2.74, p 0 .008", effect size d = 0.752, favored large spacing.

> "the participants in the large-spacing condition performed significantly better ( M 0 .42, SD 0 .198) than those in the small-spacing condition ( M 0 .29, SD 0 .125), t(51) 0 2.74, p 0 .008, d 0 0.752"
