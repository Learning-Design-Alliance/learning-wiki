## Evidence

### Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay 2025

Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay. (2025). Addressing sarcopenia management in primary care in Türkiye: gaps in the field and training needs of family physicians. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1660782

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of survey responses: educated participants more often chose "Syndrome" (44.9% vs 32.8% of uneducated), χ² = 16.772, p = 0.001. Only the test statistic and p-value are printed; no effect size.

> "Participants’ responses to the question “How to define sarcopenia?” differed significantly depending on whether they had received prior education on sarcopenia (p = 0.001)."

### Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay 2025 (2)

Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay. (2025). Addressing sarcopenia management in primary care in Türkiye: gaps in the field and training needs of family physicians. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1660782

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis (Table 7): responses on the most reliable measure of muscle function differed by acquaintance with a diagnosed case, χ² = 12.935, p = 0.012. No effect size is printed.

> "A significant difference was identified between participants’ responses to the most reliable measure of muscle function in sarcopenia diagnosis and having a relative or acquaintance diagnosed with sarcopenia (p = 0.012)."
