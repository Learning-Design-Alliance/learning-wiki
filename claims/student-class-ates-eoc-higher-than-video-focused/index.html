Walter L. Leite 2021¶

Multilevel Mixture Modeling with Propensity Score Weights for Quasi-Experimental Evaluation of Virtual Learning Environments. Structural Equation Modeling: A Multidisciplinary Journal, 28:6, 964-982. https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2021.1919895

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Planned contrasts of mean intercepts from a multilevel mixture regression of Algebra I EOC scores with inverse propensity weights at the student level, using class C1 as reference because "watching instructional videos is the most passive use of the VLE". No effect sizes were printed for these contrasts.

"The student-level ATE estimates show that students in the “Video and Practice Focused” class (i.e., C2) and the “Review Focused” class (i.e., C4) obtained higher Algebra 1 EOC scores than students in the “Instructional Video Focused” class in all classroom latent classes."
