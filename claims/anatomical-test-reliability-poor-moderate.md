## Evidence

### Kooloos Jan G.M. 2020

Kooloos Jan G.M., Bergman Esther M., Scheffers Marieke A.G.P., Schepens-Franke Annelieke N., Vorstenbosch Marc A.T.M. (2020). The Effect of Passive and Active Education Methods Applied in Repetition Activities on the Retention of Anatomical Knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1924

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of the five 40-item multiple-choice tests used in the study (Table 2). The authors state the moderate alphas "would not be acceptable for a summative assessment" and that about 100 questions per test would be the standard, but time constraints limited tests to 40 items.

> "The pre‐course test showed a particularly low reliability (Cronbach's alpha −0,022; see Table 2), indicating a highly random measure. Considering the tests consisted of only 40 items, the other alphas were reasonably satisfactory."
