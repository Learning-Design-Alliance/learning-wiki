## Evidence

### Ricci A 2025

Ricci A, Minearo I, Hielscher A. (2025). Focused anatomy workshops for clerkships and the USMLE Step 1 examination. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2536

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous Qualtrics interest survey of first-year medical students; "40 responded (33% response rate)", and most respondents indicated they would (48%) or may (48%) attend monthly sessions.

> "The interest survey was sent to 122 first‐year medical students in the Class of 2027 at LCOM, of which 40 responded (33% response rate)."

### Ricci A 2025 (2)

Ricci A, Minearo I, Hielscher A. (2025). Focused anatomy workshops for clerkships and the USMLE Step 1 examination. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2536

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey preference results: "a workshop style was the highest‐rated option (19 students, 48%)" and "MCQ was the highest‐rated option (37 students, 93%)"; 75% preferred monthly frequency.

> "Regarding the structure of the sessions (Figure 1C), a workshop style was the highest‐rated option (19 students, 48%), followed by a problem‐based format (15 students, 38%). Regarding the preferred format of questions (Figure 1D), MCQ was the highest‐rated option (37 students, 93%)."
