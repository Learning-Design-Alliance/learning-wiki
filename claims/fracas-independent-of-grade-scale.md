## Evidence

### Cassandra A. Paul and David J. Webb 2022

Cassandra A. Paul and David J. Webb. (2022). Percent Grade Scale Amplifies Racial/Ethnic Inequities in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020103)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison across the 73-class database finds instructors gave A-grades 40.7% ± 0.5% of the time under CLASP4 versus 45.0% ± 0.6% under CLASP10, a difference with Cohen's d = 0.17, a small effect. The HLM fit to equation 1 finds the grade-scale terms statistically insignificant (P = 0.72 and P = 0.41).

> "Overall, instructors using CLASP4 grading gave A’s on exam problems/questions 40.7% ± 0.5% of the time while under CLASP10 grading the percentage of A-grades was 45.0% ± 0.6%."
