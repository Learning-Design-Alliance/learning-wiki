`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's Data Collection and Analysis section states that planned meta‐analytic, heterogeneity, subgroup, sensitivity, and publication bias synthesis methods were abandoned because only one study met the inclusion criteria; single‐study effect sizes with 95% confidence intervals were reported instead.

> "However, these synthesis methods were not used given that only one study met the inclusion criteria for the review. Instead, effect sizes (and their corresponding 95% confidence intervals) were reported for all eligible outcomes reported in the study."
