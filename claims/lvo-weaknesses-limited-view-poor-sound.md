## Evidence

### Pickering 2011

Pickering, L. E., & Walsh, E. J. (2011). Using Videoconferencing Technology to Enhance Classroom Observation Methodology for the Instruction of Preservice Early Childhood Professionals. Journal of Digital Learning in Teacher Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ918901

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test results from Wave 2 (all 40 students rating both methods). Table 3 shows ability to see everything: OSO M = 3.74 vs LVO M = 2.84, t = 4.08, p = .000; sound quality hindered observation: OSO M = 2.38 vs LVO M = 2.95, t = -2.47, p = .018.

> "However, students did indicate that it was more difficult to see and hear during the LVOs compared to the OSOs."
