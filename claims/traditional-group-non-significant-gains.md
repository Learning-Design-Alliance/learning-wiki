### Bedre Duygu Ozden 2026

Bedre Duygu Ozden, Govsa Figen, Yagdi Tahir, Duygu Ibrahim, Ozer Mehmet Asim, Gokmen Gokhan. (2026). From anatomy to surgical safety: scenario-based learning for internal carotid artery patterns in undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10076-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-post comparison in the traditional instruction group (n = 60). The article reports "only slight and statistically non-significant gains in all areas", with anatomical understanding moving from 3.05 ± 0.78 to 3.28 ± 0.72 (p = 0.10).

> "By contrast, the traditional group, which received traditional instruction, showed only slight and statistically non-significant gains in all areas (e.g., anatomical understanding: 3.05 ± 0.78 to 3.28 ± 0.72, p = 0.10; clinical relevance: 3.05 ± 0.71 to 3.23 ± 0.63, p = 0.13)."
