### Neupane Harish Chandra 2020

Neupane Harish Chandra, Shrestha Niki, Adhikari Shital, Angadi Siddeshwar, Shrestha Bishow Kumar, Gauli Basanta. (2020). Knowledge of Health Care Professionals and Medical Students Regarding Covid-19 in a Tertiary Care Hospital in Nepal. JNMA: Journal of the Nepal Medical Association. https://doi.org/10.31729/jnma.4995

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Domain-level result from the 181-respondent survey: the management protocol domain contained the lowest-scoring items, including the case vignette on severity assessment answered correctly by 91 (50.3%) and aerosol-generation prevention by 74 (40.9%).

> "However, the respondents scored the lowest in the domain related to the management of COVID-19. This could be ascribed to questions which were tad bit technical in this domain and also because confirmed cases of COVID-19 have been few and far between in Chitwan, so far."
