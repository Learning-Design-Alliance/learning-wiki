Descriptive survey results from 927 students grouped by year of study. The article reports that "students preferred deductive grammar instruction over inductive grammar instruction."

> "the results for Item 19 (“A teacher should teach  English by explaining the gram mar rules.” (I)) and Item 24 (“It is better to discover grammatical rules as a student than to have them prese nted by the teacher.” (D)) show that students preferred deductive grammar instruction over inductive grammar instruction."
