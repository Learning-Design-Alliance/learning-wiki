## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2019

Lynn S. Fuchs, Douglas Fuchs, and Jennifer K. Gilbert. (2019). Does the Severity of Students' Pre-Intervention Math Deficits Affect Responsiveness to Generally Effective First-Grade Intervention? Exceptional Children, 85(2), 147–162. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918782628

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Main-effects model on square-root-transformed calculations scores from the same RCT; the intervention-versus-control contrast was significant (ES = 0.53) while the practice-variant contrast was not after false-discovery-rate adjustment.

> "a significant effect for Contrast 1, β2 = 0.66, SE = 0.09, p < .001, ES = 0.53 (both intervention conditions vs. control), but not Contrast 2"
