McKenzie 2025¶

McKenzie, R. & Allensworth, E. (2025). Variability in the Grades Teachers Give: Teacher Grade Value-Add, Mismatch, and Long-Term Effects. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312251345094

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Regressions of residualized ninth-grade outcomes (core GPA, failures, credits, attendance) on teacher grade effects, pooling four subjects (Table 4). The article reports total-effect spillover of 0.127 GPA points versus an expected 0.070, and that persistent effects show "stronger relationships with broad ninth-grade outcomes than mismatch or total grade effects."

"Students who have a ninth-grade teacher with persistent effects one standard deviation above the mean have ninth-grade GPAs that are 0.156 points higher than average."
