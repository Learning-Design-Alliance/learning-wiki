Evidence¶

Spatariu 2010¶

Spatariu, A., & Winsor, D. L. (2010). A Qualitative Perspective: Pre-Service Teachers Discuss Educational Technology Online. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102777

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Directed content analysis of the support-of-position category across treatment groups. The article reports "examples for support (71%)" and "examples against (9%)"; five of the treatment groups presented the same rank order, with treatment Group 3 the outlier.

"The distribution was as follows: examples for support (71%), no examples for support (4%), examples against (9%), and no examples against (6%)"

Spatariu 2010 (2)¶

Spatariu, A., & Winsor, D. L. (2010). A Qualitative Perspective: Pre-Service Teachers Discuss Educational Technology Online. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102777

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative comparison finding that control groups were "more mixed on the dilemma" and floundered in this category by comparison, with discussions driven primarily by emotional responses rather than justified support.

"the control groups, who were more mixed on the dilemma with regard to being for or against educational technology. In addition, the control groups demonstrated more limitations to their discussions by having far less support for their positions"
