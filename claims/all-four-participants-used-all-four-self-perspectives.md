### Nilsen 2016

Nilsen, A. P. (2016). Navigating windows into past human minds: A framework of shifting selves in historical perspective taking. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 25, 372-410. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2016.1160830

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative think-aloud analysis of four participants' transcripts reading Salem and Józefów documents. The article reports that "All four participants used all four self perspectives, often making rapid shifts among them," and that shifts varied, "with 14 as the maximum number of switches for one document."
