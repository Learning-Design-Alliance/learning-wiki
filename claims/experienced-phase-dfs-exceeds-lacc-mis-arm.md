## Evidence

### Baeten IGT 2021

Baeten IGT, Hoogendam JP, Schreuder HWR, Jürgenliemk-Schulz IM, Verheijen RHM, Zweemer RP, Gerestein CG. (2021). The influence of learning curve of robot-assisted laparoscopy on oncological outcomes in early-stage cervical cancer: an observational cohort study. BJOG 2021;128:563–571. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.16399

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-study comparison reported in the article's discussion, contrasting this cohort's experienced phase with the LACC trial's MIS arm (4.5-year DFS of 86%). The authors note the LACC centres' learning curves are unknown and its proficiency assurance was limited to ten laparoscopic surgeries and two procedural videos.

> "When regarding the 4.5-year DFS, the outcome parameter provided in the Laparoscopic Approach in Cervical Cancer (LACC) trial, we found that 93% of the patients treated during the experienced phase (group 2) were free of recurrence at 4.5 years of follow up."
