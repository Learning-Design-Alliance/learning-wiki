## Evidence

### Maria Entezari & Mohammad Javdan 2016

Maria Entezari & Mohammad Javdan. (2016). Active Learning and Flipped Classroom, Hand in Hand Approach to Improve Students Learning in Human Anatomy and Physiology. International Journal of Higher Education, Vol. 5, No. 4. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n4p222

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparison of correct answers by question difficulty (knowledge, comprehension/application, analysis/synthesis) between treatment (n=32) and control (n=34) groups, analyzed across three exams. All three levels favored the treatment, with "6.28± 0.18 vs 5.38± 0.19 p<0.001" at medium and "6.14± 0.19 vs 5.09± 0.2 p<0.001" at difficult level.

> "The mean of given correct answers for easy, medium, and hard levels in the treatment group vs the control group were [Mean ± SE; (6.79± 1. 3 vs 6.19± 0.18 p<0.05; 6.28± 0.18 vs 5.38± 0.19 p<0.001; 6.14± 0.19 vs 5.09± 0.2 p<0.001)] respectively ."
