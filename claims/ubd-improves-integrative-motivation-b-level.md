## Evidence

### Yurtseven 2015

Yurtseven, N., & Altun, S. (2015). The Investigation of the Impact of Understanding by Design (UbD) on EFL Teaching: Students' Foreign Language Learning Motivation and Views. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i3.1204

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of 115 B-level treatment versus 113 control students; ANCOVA assumptions were met for integrative motivation. Treatment post-test mean was 73.55 versus 72.10 for controls, with printed effect size d = .54, a moderate level.

> "it can be seen that there is a significant difference in integrative motivation levels of treatment and control groups at p < 0.05 level (p = .00 < .05). This means that the integrative motivation is significant in favor of the treatment group."
