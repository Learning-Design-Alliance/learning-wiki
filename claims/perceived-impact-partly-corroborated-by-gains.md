## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceived lack of impact was corroborated for students citing already knowing how to learn or limited applicability, but not for those citing lack of interest or lack of appeal, who used the tool above average with substantial gains. [→ Eelco Braad 2022](#eelco-braad-2022)

## Evidence

### Eelco Braad 2022

Eelco Braad, Nick Degens, Wolmet Barendregt, Wijnand IJsselsteijn. (2022). Improving metacognition through self-explication in a digital self-regulated learning tool. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10156-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis of intervention-group remarks (Tables 7–8) cross-checked against relative MAI gains and tool use. Four reasons for perceived lack of impact and seven ways of perceived impact were identified; perceived impact was generally corroborated by substantial gains and above-average use.

> "However, the perception was not corroborated for the group of students who cited a lack of interest, motivation, or relevance, nor for the group of students who found the tool not sufficiently appealing. Both groups used the tool above average and had substantial metacognitive gains."
