`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 'Knowledge' theme across both intervention groups in the nested qualitative study. Participants appreciated the adoption of clinical tools (e.g., SBST), better understanding of cases at risk of chronicity, and, in EEI, how and why to communicate with patients.

> "While many physiotherapists (n = 143) in both interventions mentioned that the modules did not provide a lot of new information, they commonly reported that the modules served as a very useful and necessary knowledge refresher."
