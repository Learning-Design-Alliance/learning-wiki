## Evidence

### Judith Schoonenboom 2008

Judith Schoonenboom. (2008). The effect of a script and a structured interface in grounding discussions. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9042-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA with contrasts on 10 groups, 42 students in the European Virtual Seminar; input scored as number of the seven input questions answered and length in words. The article reports "more inputs than students in the non-scripted condition ( p=0.000, Cohen 's d=1.8)"; scripted students also provided longer inputs (p=0.000, d=1.0), and script+interface longer than script-only (p=0.005, d=1.3).

> "The results show that students in both scripted conditions provided more inputs than students in the non-scripted condition ( p=0.000, Cohen 's d=1.8)."

### Judith Schoonenboom 2008 (2)

Judith Schoonenboom. (2008). The effect of a script and a structured interface in grounding discussions. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9042-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Contrast between the two scripted conditions on input length in words (unit of analysis: group discussion, N=19). The article reports the script+interface condition "provided longer inputs than students in the script- only condition ( p=0.005, Cohen 's d=1.3)".

> "students in the script+interface condition provided longer inputs than students in the script- only condition ( p=0.005, Cohen 's d=1.3)."
