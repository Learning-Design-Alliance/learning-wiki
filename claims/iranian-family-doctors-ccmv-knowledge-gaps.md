## Evidence

### Hosseinzadeh Adli Ahmad 2018

Hosseinzadeh Adli Ahmad, Karami Chiman, Baghban Rahimi Sanaz, Mirarab Azam, Tabarraei Alijan. (2018). What family doctors know about congenital CMV: a regional survey in Iran. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-018-0470-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 450 randomly distributed single-page questionnaires among Iranian medical doctors, with 198 respondents analyzed using SPSS Chi-Square tests. The authors report that "The knowledge of general practitioners about cCMV was less than usual."

> "The knowledge of general practitioners about cCMV was less than usual. The field of expertise had a profound effect in this survey, but age and gender did not."
