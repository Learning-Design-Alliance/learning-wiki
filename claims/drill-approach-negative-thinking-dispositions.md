## Evidence

### Norazlinda Mohd Darby & Abdullah Mat Rashid 2017

Norazlinda Mohd Darby & Abdullah Mat Rashid. (2017). Critical Thinking Disposition: The Effects of Infusion Approach in Engineering Drawing. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n3p305

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theoretical argument and authors' interpretation, not a tested result: the article asserts that drill and memorization environments hinder higher-order thinking and cultivate negative dispositions. The conclusion adds that control students exhibited indifference, intolerance, close-mindedness and disinclination towards reasoning.

> "Higher-order thinking is hindered if the learning environment is only filled with activities such as answering drill questions and memorizing facts. The students not only practice lower-order thinking but also negative thinking dispositions such as negligence, indifference and bias."
