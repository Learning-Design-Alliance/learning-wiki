Analytical instructional derivation in the quantitative-understanding section: the authors combine equipartition of energy with the minimum slope read from the measured excitation curve, (Eex/pex)minimum = (58.05 ± 0.15) m/s, and "yields a transition temperature of Tλ = 2.162 K ± 0.012 K". No human data; this is a worked estimate for students.

> "which combined with the mass of a helium atom of 6.646 x 10−27 kg yields a transition temperature of Tλ = 2.162 K ± 0.012 K, in good agreement with the actual transition temperature of 2.17 K."
