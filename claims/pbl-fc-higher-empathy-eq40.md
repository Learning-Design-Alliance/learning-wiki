## Evidence

### Yang Yu 2026

Yang Yu, Xue Jiarui, Hu Lingfei, Fang Kai, Wei Chaobing, Wu Changfan, Jin Huixiang. (2026). A combined PBL and flipped classroom intervention enhances self-directed learning ability and empathy in ophthalmology undergraduates. Scientific Reports 16, 11696. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47584-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

EQ-40 empathy scale administered one week before and after class in the quasi-experimental two-group study. The abstract prints post-class means of "46.91 ± 5.56 vs. 41.89 ± 5.61"; both groups also improved within-group (both P < 0.001). Results are illustrated in Fig. 2.

> "Before the intervention, empathy scores were not significantly different between the two groups (P = 0.429). After the intervention, the score of the PBL-FC group was greatly higher than that of the traditional group (P < 0.001)."
