## Evidence

### Rickard 1997

Three-parameter power functions with an asymptote were fit separately to each participant's overall RT and SD data in Experiment 1; the printed means are RT M = -.505 and SD M = -0.435, with a binomial sign test at p < .01. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Sixteen of the 18 participants showed steeper rate estimates for the RT (M = -.505) than for the SD (M = -0.435), a difference that is strongly reliable by a binomial sign test (p \< .01), disconflrming this prediction of the theory"
