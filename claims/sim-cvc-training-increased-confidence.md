# Participation in the simulation-based CVC training session significantly increased learner confidence in CVC placement

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learner self-reported confidence with CVC placement increased significantly after training (median 1.5 to 4.0 on a 5-point Likert scale). [→ Good Ryan J. MD 2022](#good-ryan-j-md-2022)

## Evidence

### Good Ryan J. MD 2022

Good Ryan J. MD, Mashburn Danielle MPAS, PA-C, Jekich Erika MS, PA-C, Miller Kristen MS, Leroue Matthew K. MD, Woods Jason MD, Czaja Angela S. MD, MSc, PhD. (2022). Simulation-Based Training for Ultrasound-Guided Central Venous Catheter Placement in Pediatric Patients. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11276

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post confidence ratings on a Likert scale (1 = not confident at all, 5 = extremely confident) collected from a voluntary convenience sample of learners. Median rose from 1.5 to 4.0 (p < .001, n = 16). No effect size was printed.

> "Learner confidence with CVC placement also increased significantly after participation, from a median confidence level of 1.5 before (IQR: 1.0-2.0) to a median confidence level of 4.0 after (IQR: 3.0-4.0, p < .001 by Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test, n = 16)"
