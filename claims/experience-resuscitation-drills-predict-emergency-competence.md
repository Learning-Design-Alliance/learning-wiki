## Evidence

### Bian Xiao Jie 2026

Bian Xiao Jie, Gao Su Yu, Zhang Shi Qing, Xue Fang, Liu Feng Lian and Chen Shu Fang. (2026). Perceived organizational support and self-efficacy as mediating factors between job burnout and emergency response competence among new nurses: a study based on emergency departments and intensive care units. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1775808

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple linear regression on the 321-nurse survey (R2 = 0.552, adjusted R2 = 0.540, F = 21.49, P < 0.001). Resuscitation experience had β = 9.051 (P < 0.001) and drill participation β = 4.103 (P = 0.002); no standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "The results indicated that length of employment, experience in rescuing critically ill patients, participation in emergency drills for public health incidents, job burnout, perceived organizational support, and self-efficacy were significantly related to emergency response competence among new nurses in emergency and critical care departments (P < 0.05)."
