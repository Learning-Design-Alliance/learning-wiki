### Kubo Taro 2022

Kubo Taro, Takayama Tatsuya, Fujisaki Akira, Nakamura Shigeru, Teratani Takumi, Sata Naohiro, Kitayama Joji, Nakai Hideo, Iwami Daiki, Fujimura Tetsuya. (2022). Novel high-quality and reality biomaterial as a kidney surgery simulation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263179

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Suturing (ligation) test outcome from the strength evaluation of hemisphere-deficient renal parenchyma at 10 mm and 15 mm diameters. The FA-fixed kidney failed by disintegration; the article reports its ligation tension values were far below fresh kidney.

> "In particular, it was confirmed that the tissue of the FA-fixed kidney was disintegrated rather than torn when the suture was pulled."
