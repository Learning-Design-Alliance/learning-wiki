Evidence¶

Parveen and Batool 2012¶

Qaisara Parveen, Sadia Batool. (2012). Effect of Cooperative Learning on Achievement of Students in General Science at Secondary Level. International Education Studies, Vol. 5, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n2p154

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline comparison of the two 18-student groups on the 30-item pretest; means were 11.89 (experimental) and 11.78 (control) with "no significant difference (t=1.00)".

"Both the groups were found to be almost equal on their pretest performance with no significant difference (t=1.00) in terms of mean and spread of scores."

Parveen and Batool 2012 (2)¶

Qaisara Parveen, Sadia Batool. (2012). Effect of Cooperative Learning on Achievement of Students in General Science at Secondary Level. International Education Studies, Vol. 5, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n2p154

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline comparison on annual examination marks (53.0 experimental vs 50.3 control); the article reports the t-value (0.6) was not significant at .05 level.

"Though the subjects were matched on the General Scie nce marks obtained by them in the annual examination, however, they were equivalent in their previous perfo rmance in General Science because t-value (0.6) was not significant at .05 level."
