Balci 2016¶

Balci, C. & Yenice, N. (2016). Effects of the scientific argumentation based learning process on teaching the unit of cell division and inheritance to eighth grade students. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 2(1), 67-84. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105345

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on the Attitudes towards Science and Technology Course Scale (Tekeli, 2009; four dimensions) controlling for pretest scores showed significant differences favoring the experiment group on positive attitude and interest dimensions only; negative attitude (F (1,74)= .642, p> .05) and importance (F (1,74)= .335, p> .05) were not significant.

"Table 7 shows that when the pre-test scores are controlled, there appear significant differences between the post-test scores of the groups for two dimensions: positive attitude (F (1,74)= 18.913, p< .05) and interest (F (1,74)= 11.643, p< .05)."
