# SCT performance was not correlated with multiple-choice factual knowledge performance (r = 0.11)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 1 causal `r2`, 1 associational `r1` · `q3` · `i1` small
> **Currency** · newest study 2017 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims

`q3 i1` SCT performance showed a weak, non-significant-appearing correlation of r = 0.11 with MCQ performance on factual visceral surgery knowledge. [→ Schubach F 2017](#schubach-f-2017)

`q3 i?` SCT and multiple-choice test results were uncorrelated, with an extremely low coefficient of determination (R2 = 0.009), which the authors interpret as testing a construct independent of factual knowledge. [→ Goos M 2016](#goos-m-2016)

## Evidence

### Schubach F 2017

Schubach F, Goos M, Fabry G, Vach W, Boeker M. (2017). Virtual patients in the acquisition of clinical reasoning skills: does presentation mode matter? A quasi-randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1004-2

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis reported in the SCT results section relating script concordance test scores to the end-of-semester multiple-choice test; the article prints "r = 0.11" and characterizes the correlation as absent. No p-value is printed for this correlation.

> "SCT performance was not correlated to MCQ performance (r = 0.11)."

### Goos M 2016

Goos M, Schubach F, Seifert G, Boeker M. (2016). Validation of undergraduate medical student script concordance test (SCT) scores on the clinical assessment of the acute abdomen. BMC Surgery 16:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-016-0173-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the same validation study, reported in Results: SCT and multiple-choice results were uncorrelated, with a coefficient of determination that was "extremely low (R2 = 0.009)".

> "There was no correlation between the SCT and the multiple choice test results. The coefficient of determination was extremely low (R2 = 0.009), which indicates that a construct independent of factual knowledge was tested for."
