## Evidence

### Kramer 2024

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the Cog-ED hierarchical model, participants' average autonomy over days positively predicted indifference points, "β= 0.56, 95% CI = [0.05, 1.09]", a wide interval that excluded zero.

> "Next, we found a positive between-person effect of experienced feelings of autonomy (β= 0.56, 95% CI = [0.05, 1.09])."

### Kramer 2024 (2)

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the self-report model, average autonomy positively predicted academic motivation, "β= 0.60, 95% CI = [0.27, 0.92]"; no between-person effects of competence or relatedness were found.

> "Next, we found a positive between-person effect of experienced feelings of autonomy (β= 0.60, 95% CI = [0.27, 0.92])."
