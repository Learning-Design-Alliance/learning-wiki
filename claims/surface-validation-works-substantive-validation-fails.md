The article reports Vicente and Matute's (2023) experiment: participants exposed to biased AI recommendations reproduced those biases in later judgments even after assistance was removed, with "80.7% of participants detected errors" yet deferred anyway, treating the system as an authority. No effect size is printed.

> "Critically, 80.7% of participants detected errors in the AI recommendations, demonstrating functional surface validation. But they continued following erroneous advice, suggesting that detecting a problem and overriding an algorithmic recommendation require different cognitive capacities"
