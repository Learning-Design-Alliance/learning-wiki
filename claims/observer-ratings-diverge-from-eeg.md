# Observer ratings diverged from neural measures: behavioral ratings showed no teacher-initiated versus student-initiated difference and rated group work as more inattentive than independent work

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In contrast to EEG, observer ratings showed no significant difference between teacher-initiated and student-initiated activities (r = 0.11), and rated inattentiveness higher during group work (22%) than independent work (6%), r = 0.34. [→ Jennie K. Grammer 2021](#jennie-k-grammer-2021)

## Evidence

### Jennie K. Grammer 2021

Jennie K. Grammer, Keye Xu and Agatha Lenartowicz. (2021). Effects of context on the neural correlates of attention in a college classroom. npj Science of Learning 6:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00094-8

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Video-based behavioral coding of inattentive intervals by five trained coders (ICC = 0.97), analyzed with the same planned contrasts. "In contrast to neural measures" no teacher- vs student-initiated difference emerged; group work was rated more inattentive, r = 0.34.

> "In contrast to neural measures, there were no observable differences in teacher-initiated relative to student-initiated activities ( b = −0.02, t(41) = −0.71, p = 0.48, r = 0.11), and attention was rated as lower during the group ( MGroup = 22%) relative to independent work (MIndependent = 6%, b = 0.08, t(41) = 2.28, p = 0.03, r = 0.34)"
