### Bataineh 2017

Bataineh, R. F., & Salah, N. A. (2017). The Effectiveness of Drama-Based Instruction in Jordanian EFL Students' Writing Performance. TESOL International Journal, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1247797

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test analysis of the five components (Table 4, n=40 per group) shows higher experimental means on every aspect: thesis statement (t = 2.74, Sig. 0.007), unity (t = 2.40, Sig. 0.019), completeness and coherence (t = 2.53, Sig. 0.013), wording and cohesion (t = 2.80, Sig. 0.006), organization (t = 5.55, Sig. 0.000).
