## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gick 1983

Gick, M. L., & Holyoak, K. J. (1983). Schema Induction and Analogical Transfer. Cognitive Psychology, 15, 1-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(83)90002-6)90002-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the diagrams-only condition (n = 15), only 7% produced the convergence solution without a hint, versus 31% combined across analog conditions; after the hint the diagrams-only total reached 67%, not significantly less than 78%, G2(1) < 1.

> "Combining the two analog conditions, 31% of their subjects produced the convergence solution without the hint, as compared to only one of the 15 diagrams-only subjects (7%)."
