Results of station 3 (dose calibrator use) in the same quasi-experimental cohort comparison. "SBT students achieved a mean score of 6.133, while CBT students scored 4.133"; the difference was significant and survived Bonferroni correction.

> "SBT students achieved a mean score of 6.133, while CBT students scored 4.133. An independent-samples t-test with Welch's correction showed a statistically significant difference between groups (t = 4.90, df = 40, p < 0.001), which remained statistically significant after Bonferroni correction."
