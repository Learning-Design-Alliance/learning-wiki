Evidence¶

Muhammad 2016¶

Muhammad, A. U., Bala, D., & Ladu, K. M. (2016). Effectiveness of Demonstration and Lecture Methods in Learning Concept in Economics among Secondary School Students in Borno State, Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(12). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099610

q2 · i? · causal · r1

T-tests comparing post-test scores of male and female students (13 each per school) taught with the lecture method showed no significant difference. The article attributes any observed difference to sampling errors.

"Thus, there were no statistically significant diffe rences between the academic performance of male and female student s exposed to lecture method in learning economics concepts in both schools."
