## Evidence

### Dircks Monika 2017

Dircks Monika, Mayr Andreas, Freidank Annette, Kornhuber Johannes, Dörje Frank, Friedland Kristina. (2017). Advances in clinical pharmacy education in Germany: a quasi-experimental single-blinded study to evaluate a patient-centred clinical pharmacy course in psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1092-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary regression analysis of the OSCE data examining prior pharmacy-technician vocational training as a covariate; the article reports no detected influence and does not show the detailed results. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "Four students in the intervention group and 5 students in the control group completed vocational training to be a pharmacy technician before they entered university education. No influence of this prior knowledge was detected via statistical regression analysis (results not shown)."
