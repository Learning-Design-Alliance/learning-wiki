## Evidence

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i2`

Usage-logging analysis (RQ2) for the goal-reminder condition comparing intervention to baseline: average daily scrolling declined by 42%, "t(14) = 2.39, p = 0.03, d = 0.62".

> "Thus, usage logging showed that average daily scrolling de- clined by 42% in C goal (comparing intervention to baseline, t(14) = 2.39, p = 0.03, d = 0.62), and by 73% in Cno-feed (t(13) = 4.15, p = 0.001, d = 1.11)."

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020 (2)

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory per-item PAUM survey comparison (RQ2) for the goal-reminder condition: passive newsfeed browsing decreased (r = 0.57) while active browsing did not (r = 0.05); the authors note these p-values are uncorrected and exploratory.

> "in Cgoal, participants reported less passive, but not active, browsing of the newsfeed during the intervention block compared to baseline (Passive browsing: p = 0.03, r = 0.57, Active browsing: p = 1, r = 0.05, Wilcoxon signed rank test)"
