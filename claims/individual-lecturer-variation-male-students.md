# A marginally significant lecturer main effect reflected male students' greater variation in ratings of individual lecturers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A 2(student sex) x 4(lecturer) ANOVA found a marginally significant main effect of lecturer, with male students showing more variation in their evaluations than female students. [→ Graves 2017](#graves-2017)

## Evidence

### Graves 2017

Graves, A.L., Hoshino-Browne, E., & Lui, K.P.H. (2017). Swimming against the Tide: Gender Bias in the Physics Classroom. Journal of Women and Minorities in Science and Engineering 23(1), 15-36. [doi:10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584](https://doi.org/10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory ANOVA treating the four individual actors as levels, in the same video-lecture experiment. Male students showed more variation, F(1, 113) = 4.86, p = .027, rating one male lecturer (M = 4.20) above one female lecturer (M = 3.32), though that comparison was not significant at the lowered critical alpha of .01.

> "a main effect of lecturer was marginally significant, F(1, 113) = 2.55, p = .06. We examined the extent to which this marginally significant effect of lecturer was consistent across female and male students' ratings"
