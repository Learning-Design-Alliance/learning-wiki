Evidence¶

de Vos 2018¶

de Vos, J. F., Schriefers, H., Nivard, M. G., & Lemhöfer, K. (2018). A Meta-Analysis and Meta-Regression of Incidental Second Language Word Learning from Spoken Input. Language Learning. https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12296

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression contrast between task/+interaction and task/–interaction treatments across the 32 studies. The article concludes "participants learned more words when the learning task that they engaged in involved interaction with a conversational partner than when it did not".

"One contrast was signiﬁcant, namely task/–interaction versus task/ +interaction, β = 0.63, 95% CI [0.26, 1.00], SE = 0.19, z = 3.37, p < .001"
