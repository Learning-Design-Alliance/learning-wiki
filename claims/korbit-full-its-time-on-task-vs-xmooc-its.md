# Full Korbit ITS increased time spent learning relative to an xMOOC-like reduced ITS in a randomized A/B test

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an A/B testing experiment with 612 participants, students were randomly assigned to the Full ITS (80%) or the xMOOC ITS (20%). [→ Serban 2020](#serban-2020)
`q2 i?` Full ITS students spent more time on the system than xMOOC ITS students (39.86 vs 22.98 min, a 73.46% increase). [→ Serban 2020 (2)](#serban-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Serban 2020

Serban, I.V., Gupta, V., Kochmar, E., Vu, D.D., Belfer, R., Pineau, J., Courville, A., Charlin, L., Bengio, Y. (2020). A Large-Scale, Open-Domain, Mixed-Interface Dialogue-Based ITS for STEM. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_70](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_70)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized A/B testing experiment with 612 participants run from October to December 2019; students 'were randomly assigned to either the Full ITS (80%) or xMOOC ITS (20%)', and the system was kept fixed during the period to limit confounding factors.

> "The experiment was conducted between October 7th and December 22nd, 2019, in an A/B testing setup with n=612 participants. Students who enrolled online were randomly assigned to either the Full ITS (80%) or xMOOC ITS (20%)."

### Serban 2020 (2)

Serban, I.V., Gupta, V., Kochmar, E., Vu, D.D., Belfer, R., Pineau, J., Courville, A., Charlin, L., Bengio, Y. (2020). A Large-Scale, Open-Domain, Mixed-Interface Dialogue-Based ITS for STEM. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_70](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_70)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results in Table 1 of the same A/B test: 'The average time spent in the Full ITS was 39.86 min compared to 22.98 min in the xMOOC ITS', which the authors call a staggering 73.46% increase in time spent.

> "The average time spent in the Full ITS was 39.86 min compared to 22.98 min in the xMOOC ITS . As such, the Full ITS yields a staggering 73.46% increase in time spent."
