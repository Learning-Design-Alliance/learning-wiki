## Evidence

### Talan 2020

Talan, T. & Batdi, V. (2020). Evaluating the Flipped Classroom Model through the Multi-Complementary Approach. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 21(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1269613

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication bias analysis using Duval and Tweedie's trim-and-fill method, alongside inclusion of unpublished theses and a fail-safe calculation. The authors report the adjusted point value "may be determined as 0.21619", and that the fail-safe value of 5725.2 was extremely high, so bias was judged not to threaten the significant effect size.

> "Based on this method, the point estimation in REM for joint workings and the 95% confidence interval is 0.46212. The estimated point value may be determined as 0.21619, using the "trim and fill" property."
