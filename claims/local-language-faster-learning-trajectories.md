`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects models on 1,326 learners with week as fixed effect and random slopes by language, fitted separately for practice assessments and weekly tests. "the week-to-week increase (slope) for practice tasks is greater for learners who chose Leb Lango"; weekly tests showed the same faster-improvement pattern (slopes 0.111 vs 0.045 and 0.035).
