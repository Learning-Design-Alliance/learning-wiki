Qualitative content analysis with an inductive approach applied to verbatim statements extracted from all 48 included studies; the review reports that "All studies identified facilitating features" of the learning approach or strategies. Findings are descriptive, not effect-size based.

"All studies identified facilitating features of the learning approach (online versus blended) or strategies (including multi-media, clinical experience or practice, and interaction with peers or educators)."
