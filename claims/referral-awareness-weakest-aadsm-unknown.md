### Agrawal A 2026

Assessment of the knowledge and awareness among general medical practitioners toward dental intervention in diagnosing and treating obstructive sleep apnea: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08867-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Referral-domain results from the 125-practitioner survey (Table 5 and Fig. 1): "91.2% stated that they lack knowledge about dental treatment options." Only 18 of 125 (14.4%) were aware of the AADSM Mastery Program, with 82.4% not aware.

> "The majority of the respondents (73.6%) admitted that medical-dental collaboration is significant to OSA care; 91.2% stated that they lack knowledge about dental treatment options, which means that they have limited access to dental sleep medicine training pathways."
