Kyle Dunbar 2025¶

Kyle Dunbar, Katie Wilczak, Kelly Mills, Nick Mireles, and Alison Shell. (2025). Portraits in Progress: Emerging Future-Ready Skills Assessment Strategies. Digital Promise and Pearson. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/0a2c7a9e-634b-4ec1-ba5d-3c8bfa19e696

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Insight from interviews and convening. One district found a measure defined skills differently than they did, so results did not represent student ability on the district's meaning of the skill; teacher scoring also varied even with the same rubric, and self-reported survey data may lack validity.

"Districts that have Portrait of a Graduate assessments in place expressed a need for guidance, systems, and processes to support the technical quality of the assessment tools they create."

Kyle Dunbar 2025 (2)¶

Kyle Dunbar, Katie Wilczak, Kelly Mills, Nick Mireles, and Alison Shell. (2025). Portraits in Progress: Emerging Future-Ready Skills Assessment Strategies. Digital Promise and Pearson. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/0a2c7a9e-634b-4ec1-ba5d-3c8bfa19e696

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Cross-district finding on rubric comparability. The report frames this as a tension: districts value unique and localized skill constructs, measures, and tools, yet also desire comparability and validity, which the report says can be overcome but requires additional research and development.

"These locally-designed rubrics are all slightly different from one another, which limits the comparability of scores across districts, making it difficult for districts to get a sense of how their students are performing compared to students elsewhere in other states or districts."
