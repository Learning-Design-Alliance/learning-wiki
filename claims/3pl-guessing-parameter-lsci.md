## Evidence

### Colin S. Wallace 2017

Colin S. Wallace, Timothy G. Chambers, Edward E. Prather. (2017). An item response theory evaluation of the Light and Spectroscopy Concept Inventory national data set. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-comparison analysis of post-instruction responses of all 3205 students, comparing 2PL and 3PL fits via RMSEA, which cannot decrease merely by adding a parameter. The lower 3PL RMSEA led the authors to abandon the 2PL model; some items had guessing parameters as high as 0.44.

> "The 2PL model's RMSEA is 0.07 while the 3PL model's RMSEA is 0.06. This suggests that guessing was a signiﬁcant factor in many students' responses on many items."
