## Evidence

### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Study 2 narrative ANOVA on POMAS error types: main effect with η² = 0.151; students produced more morphological errors per word (M = 0.07, SD = 0.06) than orthographic (M = 0.03) and phonological (M = 0.02) errors. Error-type × grade interaction was marginal (F(6, 156) = 2.04, p = .063).

> "A significant main effect of the spelling error type was also obtained [F(2, 156) = 58.68, p < .001, η2 = 0.151]."
