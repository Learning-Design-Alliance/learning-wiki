Diamond, K.E., Justice, L.M., Siegler, R.S., & Snyder, P.A. (2013). Synthesis of IES Research on Early Intervention and Early Childhood Education. (NCSER 2013-3001). Washington, DC: National Center for Special Education Research, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED544212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The synthesis reports, citing Perlman and Fantuzzo, a correlational study of all second-grade children in one large urban district in which 12 percent had documented abuse or maltreatment and 8 percent had been homeless. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Study findings showed that children who were homeless when they were younger than 3 years of age were especially vulnerable to exhibiting lower levels of academic achievement and poor social skills in the early primary grades."
