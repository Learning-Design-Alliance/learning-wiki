### Anjali Singh 2024

Anjali Singh, Christopher Brooks, Xu Wang, Warren Li, Juho Kim, Deepti Wilson. (2024). Bridging Learnersourcing and AI: Exploring the Dynamics of Student-AI Collaborative Feedback Generation. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636853](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636853)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A generalized linear mixed effects model analyzed the binary Phrasing outcome. The article reports that "condition had a significant impact on the log-odds of phrasing" and that the interaction between condition and course performance was significant, though post-hoc comparisons at the mean value of course performance were not significant (p=0.1017).

> "the model suggested that condition had a significant impact on the log-odds of phrasing ( ˆ𝛽 = 8.09, SE = 3.39, p < 0.05) and that the interaction between condition and students' course performance was significant ( ˆ𝛽 = -5.36, SE = 2.20, p < 0.05)"
