## Evidence

### Ourania Miliou 2024

Ourania Miliou, Maria Adamou, Aekaterini Mavri, Andri Ioannou. (2024). An exploratory case study of the use of a digital self-assessment tool of 21st-century skills in makerspace contexts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10314-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 finding from teacher interviews. Teachers used the educator's dashboard to see who was progressing and who was falling behind and adjusted teaching accordingly; one teacher said the tool helps in changing or improving the lesson.

> "The teachers further argued that tool forced them to follow a more goal-oriented teaching approach directly targeting the development of 21st-century skills during the making activities."

### Ourania Miliou 2024 (2)

Ourania Miliou, Maria Adamou, Aekaterini Mavri, Andri Ioannou. (2024). An exploratory case study of the use of a digital self-assessment tool of 21st-century skills in makerspace contexts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10314-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 negative finding from teacher interviews. A teacher noted time issues in an 80-minute lesson, needing to leave time for challenges so students read them carefully rather than rushing.

> "the tool was perceived to increase the workload of the teachers in course planning and to interrupt their overall activity, especially for those who used the parallel approach"
