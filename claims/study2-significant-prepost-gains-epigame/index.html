Evidence¶

Martinez-Garza 2017¶

Martinez-Garza, M. M., & Clark, D. B. (2017). Investigating Epistemic Stances in Game Play with Data Mining. International Journal of Gaming and Computer-Mediated Simulations, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.4018/IJGCMS.2017070101

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Study 2, a classroom deployment in which 123 7th graders played EPIGAME for five consecutive 45-minute class periods; 104 provided both pre- and post-tests on a 21-item conceptual test. The matched-pairs t-test found "a statistically significant increase in test performance" with d = 1.62.

"A matched-pairs t-test showed a statistically significant increase in test performance (t = 11.702, df = 103, p < 0.0001) (Figure 4). The value of Cohen's d suggests a large effect size (d = 1.62)."
