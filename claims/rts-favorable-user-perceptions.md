## Evidence

### Siu Yikchi 2025

Siu Yikchi, Bai Hefei, Yoon Jung-Min, Ye Hongqiang, Liu Yunsong, Zhou Yongsheng. (2025). Enhancing Preclinical Training for Removable Partial Dentures Through Participatory 3D Simulation: Development and Usability Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71743

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Questionnaire study of the 13 training-group students plus faculty using 10 Likert items (1-5). Table 2 shows mean ratings from 4.6 to 5.0, with 100% agreement that the program is easy to use and allows learning about surveying 3D casts. No effect size is printed.

> "Users indicated a greater preference for the RTS over traditional training methods and expressed great satisfaction with the responsive features. Faculty members also had positive views, reporting that the system significantly reduced preparation time for teaching and improved teaching effectiveness."
