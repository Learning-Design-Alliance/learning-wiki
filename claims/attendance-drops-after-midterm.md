Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attendance data were collected through the classroom-activity answer papers across weeks of the General Physics 1 (Spring 2010) and General Physics 2 (Fall 2010) courses. The report notes the difficulty of interpreting fluctuations because "students may have their private motivations when to attend or skip a class".

> "The trend seems to indicate that students find it easier to skip classes after the midterm exam when the semester end is approaching."
