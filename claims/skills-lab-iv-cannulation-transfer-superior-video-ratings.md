# Skills laboratory training of IV cannulation yields better video-assessed clinical performance than bedside teaching in first-year medical students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students trained in a skills laboratory scored significantly better on IPPI global ratings by blinded video assessors than students who received bedside teaching (median 3.1 vs 3.6, p=0.015). [→ Lund Frederike 2012](#lund-frederike-2012)
`q3 i?` The skills laboratory group completed significantly more single procedural steps correctly on the binary checklist (64% vs 53%, p=0.004). [→ Lund Frederike 2012 (2)](#lund-frederike-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Lund Frederike 2012

Lund Frederike, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Krautter Markus, Möltner Andreas, Werner Anne, Weyrich Peter, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2012). Effectiveness of IV Cannulation Skills Laboratory Training and Its Transfer into Clinical Practice: A Randomized, Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0032831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 81 first-year medical students (IG n=41, CG n=40) were video-recorded performing IV cannulation on volunteer student patients; two blinded assessors rated performance with the IPPI. The intervention group's median IPPI rating was significantly better, "median scores: IG: 3.1; CG: 3.6; p = 0.015".

> "IG scored significantly better on IPPI [41] ratings (median scores: IG: 3.1; CG: 3.6; p = 0.015) as shown in Table 2."

### Lund Frederike 2012 (2)

Lund Frederike, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Krautter Markus, Möltner Andreas, Werner Anne, Weyrich Peter, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2012). Effectiveness of IV Cannulation Skills Laboratory Training and Its Transfer into Clinical Practice: A Randomized, Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0032831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the same video-rating analysis, blinded assessors scored the 25-item binary checklist; the skills laboratory group demonstrated a significantly higher percentage of correct procedural steps, "IG: 64% SD(14) for binary checklist items; CG: 53% SD(18)".

> "Students of the IG completed significantly more single steps of the procedure correctly (IG: 64% SD(14) for binary checklist items; CG: 53% SD(18); 95% CI 10.25% to 11.75%; p = 0.004)"
