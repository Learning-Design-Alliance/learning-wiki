## Evidence

### Simonsmeier 2022

Simonsmeier, B. A., Flaig, M., Deiglmayr, A., Schalk, L., & Schneider, M. (2022). Domain-specific prior knowledge and learning: A meta-analysis. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2021.1939700

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic integration (random-effects, robust variance estimation) of 7,772 effect sizes from 476 studies correlating prior knowledge with posttest knowledge. The printed effect size rP+ = .531 (95% CI [.509, .552]) was strong by Cohen's standards, but I² = 94.16 and the prediction interval ranged −.067 to .848.

> "The average correlation was rPþ ¼ .531 (95% CI [.509, .552], see Table 2). The correlation was statistically significantly greater than zero and strong according to the standards set by Cohen (1992)."
