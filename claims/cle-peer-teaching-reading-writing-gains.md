### Rattanavich 2017

Saowalak Rattanavich. (2017). The Effects of an Experimental Training Program for Teachers of Vocational English Using Concentrated Language Encounter Instructional Processes and Reciprocal Peer Teaching Strategies. English Language Teaching, 10(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n12p58

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of variance with repeated measures of students' reading and writing scores at pre-test and two post-tests in Class A (Teacher 1), with Holm-Bonferroni multiple comparisons at the 0.05 level; reading yielded "F= 169.817, p-value < 0.01, Partial η² = 0.854". Writing in Class A gave F = 34.659, partial η² = 0.544; all classes were significant.
