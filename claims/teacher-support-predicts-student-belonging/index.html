In the Belonging and Collaboration in Practice section, the primer attributes this finding to Allen et al. (2018), a meta-analysis on fostering school belonging listed in its references, stating that "Teacher support is one of the strongest predictors of student belonging." No effect size is printed in the primer.

"Teacher support is one of the strongest predictors of student belonging and creating a classroom culture where students can feel safe in taking intellectual risks is a necessary foundation (Allen et al., 2018)."
