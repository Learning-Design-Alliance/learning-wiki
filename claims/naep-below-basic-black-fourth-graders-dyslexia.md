### Laura Cassidy 2023

Laura Cassidy, Kayla Reggio, Bennett A. Shaywitz and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Prevalence of undiagnosed dyslexia in African-American primary school children. npj Science of Learning 8:52. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00204-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports repeated NAEP waves (the Nation's Report Card, reading and math scores of several thousand fourth and eighth graders every two years); this comparison is drawn from those reported data, not a new experiment.

> "For more than a decade NAEP data have consistently demonstrated that more than half of black fourth graders perform below the NAEP Basic level in reading compared to about 20% of white students"
