## Evidence

### Shams El-Adawy 2023

Shams El-Adawy, Cydney Alexis, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2023). Emerging STEM education researchers' positioning and perception of discipline-based education research. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.04401](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.04401)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based category from the phenomenographic analysis. Improvers span three sub-categories: classroom practice, departmental teaching practice, and student-centered service improvers. One participant reported that lack of formal training "seems like a requirement to share those ideas with those communities."

> "Participants in the Improvers category see the figured world of DB ER as an opportunity to improve practice locally in an evidence-based way."
