The review reports, citing Sun et al., a three-level meta-analysis of GenAI and critical thinking. The review states the "positive mean association (g = 0.544)" coexists with a prediction interval containing both harm and benefit, with heterogeneity study characteristics largely fail to explain.

> "A recent three-level meta-analysis of GenAI and critical thinking makes the pattern precise: a positive mean association (g = 0.544) is accompanied by a 95% prediction interval running from − 0.588 to 1.677 and by substantial heterogeneity that measured study characteristics largely fail to explain"
