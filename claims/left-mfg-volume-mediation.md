## Evidence

### Ma 2025

Ma, L., Wang, Y., Wang, J., et al. (2025). Reciprocal Predictions Between Reading Achievement and Cognitive Flexibility Development in Children and the Mediating Roles of the Left Middle Frontal Gyrus. Human Brain Mapping. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.70309

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis in the longitudinal cohort tested left MFG gray matter volume as mediator of both cross-lagged paths; both indirect effects were significant with 95% confidence intervals excluding zero, as printed in Figure 4.

> "(A) Reading achievement promoted the gray matter volume of the left MFG to predict cognitive flexibility development (indirect effect, 95% CI = [0.0072, 0.0699]). (B) Cognitive flexibility promoted the gray matter volume of the left MFG to predict reading achievement development (indirect effect, 95% CI = [0.0033, 0.0541])."
