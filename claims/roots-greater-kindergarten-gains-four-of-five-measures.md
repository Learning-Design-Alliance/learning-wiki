# The ROOTS intervention produced greater fall-to-spring kindergarten gains than control instruction on four of five outcome measures for at-risk students

## Evidence

### Ben Clarke 2016

Ben Clarke, Christian Doabler, Keith Smolkowski, Evangeline Kurtz Nelson, Hank Fien, Scott K. Baker & Derek Kosty (2016). Testing the Immediate and Long-Term Efficacy of a Tier 2 Kindergarten Mathematics Intervention. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1116034

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized block trial with 290 at-risk kindergartners in 37 classrooms; students nested in 58 ROOTS groups were combined and compared to no-treatment controls using a partially nested time × condition mixed model. The article reports "greater gains than control students" on four measures; Hedges's g was .58 (ASPENS), .28 (oral counting), .32 (TEMA), and .75 (RAENS).

> "Students in the ROOTS condition made greater gains than control students on the ASPENS ( t D 5.20, df D 136, p < .0001), oral counting ( t D 2.14, df D 132, p D .0333), TEMA standard scores ( t D 3.35, df D 142, p D .0010), and RAENS ( t D 6.84, df D 162, p < .0001)."
