### Alvarez-Lopez F 2020

Alvarez-Lopez F, Maina MF, Saigí-Rubió F. (2020). Use of a Low-Cost Portable 3D Virtual Reality Gesture-Mediated Simulator for Training and Learning Basic Psychomotor Skills in Minimally Invasive Surgery: Development and Content Validity Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/17491

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity was assessed via a literature review of the validated MIST-VR and an expert panel answering 10 questions on training capacity and 6 on tasks, scored on a 5-point Likert scale. Almost all respondents (n=29/30, 97%) rated the simulator as enabling learning of basic psychomotor skills with scores of 4 and 5.

> "The sources of content-related validity evidence showed that this study’s simulator is a reliable training tool and that the exercises enable learning of the basic psychomotor skills required in MIS (Likert score: 4.28-4.67)."
