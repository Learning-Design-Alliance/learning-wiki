### Cui Liping 2024

Cui Liping, Dong Yuting, Zhang Shan, Ma Wenxia, Li Min. (2024). Construction and application of procedural pathways combined with information management in nursing staff skills training system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05593-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Monthly Newcastle Nursing Satisfaction Scale surveys of 40% of patients cared for by trained nurses, comparing the 2021 experimental group with the 2020 control group; Table 5 reports t values from 3.25 (month 1) to 12.96 (month 7), all significant.

> "there were statistically significant differences in monthly scores between the experimental group and the control group (P < 0.05), and the scores of the experimental group were higher than those of the control group"
