Subgroup analysis of the same A/B test on the Korbi platform, restricted to students scoring for beginner variants. The article reports "a 19% relative reduction inultimate failure" and "a30% relative reduction in theskip rate" versus control, suggesting beginners are more engaged with beginner variants. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Isolating the students who score for beginner variants only, we see a 19% relative reduction inultimate failure when comparing theexpected to the control group, which demonstrates a bigger impact for beginners."
