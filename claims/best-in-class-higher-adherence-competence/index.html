Evidence¶

Maureen A. Conroy 2019¶

Maureen A. Conroy, Kevin S. Sutherland, James Algina, Crystal Ladwig, Brittany Werch, Jose Martinez, Gabriel Jessee, Maria Gyure. (2019). Outcomes of the BEST in CLASS Intervention on Teachers' Use of Effective Practices, Self-Efficacy, and Classroom Quality. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2018-0003.V48-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive fidelity results from 756 BiCACS observations across both conditions (BEST in CLASS n = 389; BAU n = 367) at pre- and posttest. BAU posttest adherence was 2.43 (SD = 1.61) and BEST in CLASS competence was 5.51 (SD = 0.33) versus 4.30 (SD = 0.42) in BAU; no inferential test is printed for these contrasts.

"For teachers in the BEST in CLASS condition, mean adherence was 4.53 (SD = 0.98, range = 3.72–5.99) at posttest."
