De Clercq, Mikaël, Helen Watt, & Paul Richardson. (2021). Profiles of Teachers’ Striving and Wellbeing: Evolution and relations with context factors, retention and professional engagement. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000702

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal transition analysis of the same 414-teacher sample; the article reports that "Most movers from maladaptive types underwent a recovery transition to a more adaptive profile by mid-career". No effect size is printed for this finding.
