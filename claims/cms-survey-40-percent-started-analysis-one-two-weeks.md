## Evidence

### S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini 2011

S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini. (2011). An outlook of the user support model to educate the users community at the CMS Experiment. Proceedings of the DPF-2011 Conference, Providence, RI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.0355](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.0355)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A CMS computing project survey of user computing and analysis needs, including User Support questions; about 120 participants took part, and users found the documentation "very valuable as shown in Figures 1 and 2". No effect sizes are reported.

> "About 120 participants took part in it. The survey indicated that the users find SWGuide and WorkBook as very valuable as shown in Figures 1 and 2."

### S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini 2011 (2)

S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini. (2011). An outlook of the user support model to educate the users community at the CMS Experiment. Proceedings of the DPF-2011 Conference, Providence, RI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.0355](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.0355)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same survey reported onboarding time: "around 40% users said that it took them 1 -2 weeks to get started with their analysis", which the authors call a very big success given the complexity of the tools. The figure is a self-reported percentage, not an effect size.

> "Getting help from these resources, around 40% users said that it took them 1 -2 weeks to get started with their analysis."
