## Evidence

### Celentano C 2026

Celentano C, Matarrelli B, Rosati M, Mercaldi M, Meloni C, Molino C, Padelli A, Conti F, Prefumo F, Freeze R. (2026). Evaluating the effect of two different training interventions on improving vaginal twin birth rates and provider confidence and knowledge levels: A pre‐ and post‐intervention study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70947

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of two different interventions across two hospitals: Pescara used quarterly staff-wide training (6.4% to 31.5%) while Florence used dedicated skilled teams (16.2% to 56.4%). The authors interpret the higher Florence rate as possibly reflecting faster implementation with teams.

> "Our results suggest that dedicated teams may be able to support a more rapid implementation of vaginal twin birth, as evidenced by the higher vaginal birth rates in Florence."
