## Evidence

### da Silva Gisele Cabral 2025

da Silva Gisele Cabral, Campanharo Cássia Regina Vancini, Lopes Maria Carolina Barbosa Teixeira, Okuno Meiry Fernanda Pinto, Miura Carla Roberta Monteiro, Batista Ruth Ester Assayag. (2025). Clinical simulation and deliberate practice in rapid-cycle deliberate practice: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0632

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of Educational Practices Questionnaire responses from 134 students; SWSP collaboration mean was 4.64 versus 4.36 for RCDP (p=0.004), the only EPQ factor favoring SWSP.

> "On the collaboration factor, the SWSP strategy received higher scores, meaning participants had more opportunities to work with peers during the simulated practice and complete the work together (p=0.004)."
