`q2 i?` After treatment, the WebQuest experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on the Science Achievement Test (p<.05). [→ Ahmet Unal 2016](#ahmet-unal-2016)

## Evidence

### Ahmet Unal 2016

Ahmet Unal, Melek Altıparmak Karakuş. (2016). Interacting Science through Web Quests. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(7). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040712

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with control group; 49 7th graders (24 experimental, 25 control) in a public elementary school in Mugla, Turkey. Post-test means favored the WebQuest group and "the difference was found to be significant (p<.05)"; no effect size printed.

> "The mean score of the experimental group (X=24,17) was calculated to be higher than the mean score of the control group (X=16,80). In order to see if this difference is significant or not,  t-test was performed and the difference was found to be significant (p<.05)."
