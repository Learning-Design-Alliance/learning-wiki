## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, Michael C. Wittmann, Eleanor C. Sayre, Brian W. Frank. (2009). Students’ Responses To Different Representations Of A Vector Addition Question. 2009 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266745

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fisher's exact tests across the grid and no-grid question pairs in the UMaine algebra-based class found no significant grid effect, a result the authors call "surprising, given the previous results of Nguyen and Meltzer."

> "Our second hypothesis—that use of the components method is affected by the presence of coordinate grids—was not confirmed in either the drawn or written explanations [Fig. 2, Fig. 3]."
