Analysis of critical incidents coded by thinking-in-levels across the three PFO stages (Graphs 2 and 3). The article reports that in the Play stage "students mostly approached the game system at the micro-level" and that macro-level reasoning increased in open modding.

"In the Open modding stage, the abstraction incidents significantly increased (Graph 3) as well as the macro-level reasoning. In that stage, students approached the simulation game as one system with choices, consequences and rules bound together"
