Evidence¶

Gage 2024¶

Gage, N. A., Salomonson, K., Ballew, T., Clavenna-Deane, B., & Grasley-Boy, N. (2024). The Impact of Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) on Student Attendance and Behavior. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655758

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Multilevel logistic regression on 942 students in 111 homerooms with a dichotomous outcome (any behavior incident), controlling for prior-year incidents and demographics. The report states students in treatment schools "were significantly less likely to have a behavior incident" (Treatment log-OR = -1.15, p = 0.004; OR = 0.32, converted g = -0.63).

"Again, we found that there was a significant treatment effect, which suggests that students in treatment schools were significantly less likely to have a behavior incident"
