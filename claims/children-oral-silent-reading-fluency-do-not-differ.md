# For children in Grades 3–5, with and without dyslexia, oral and silent text reading fluency do not differ

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Madelon van den Boer 2022

Madelon van den Boer, Loes Bazen, Elise de Bree. (2022). The Same yet Different: Oral and Silent Reading in Children and Adolescents with Dyslexia. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-022-09856-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (2 mode × 2 group) with 40 children with dyslexia and 18 typical readers in Grades 3–5. The main effect of reading mode was not significant (F(1,55) = 1.98, p = 0.165, ηp2 = 0.035), nor the interaction (F(1,55) = 1.19, p = 0.280, ηp2 = 0.021), showing "neither for the children with dyslexia, nor for the typical readers" did fluency differ.

> "fluency of oral and silent reading did not differ from each other, neither for the children with dyslexia, nor for the typical readers"
