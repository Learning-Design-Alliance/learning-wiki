# The IV approach to mediator analysis in clustered education RCTs has very little statistical power

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The instrumental variable approach, which can adjust for potential omitted variable and simultaneity biases, has very little statistical power for mediator analyses. [→ Schochet 2009](#schochet-2009)

## Evidence

### Schochet 2009

Schochet, Peter Z. (2009). Do Typical RCTs of Education Interventions Have Sufficient Statistical Power for Linking Impacts on Teacher Practice and Student Achievement Outcomes? (NCEE 2009-4065). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-typical-rcts-of-education-interventions-have-sufficient-statistical-power-for-linking-impacts-on-teacher-practice-and-student-achievement-outcomes

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulated power analysis comparing the IV estimator's non-centrality parameter to the OLS case. The report explains that "the variance of the IV estimator is likely to be considerably larger than the variance of the comparable OLS estimator" because treatment status explains little mediator variance.

> "The IV approach, which can adjust for potential omitted variable and simultaneity biases, has very little statistical power for mediator analyses."
