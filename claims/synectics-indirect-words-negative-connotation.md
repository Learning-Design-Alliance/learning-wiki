### Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT 2017

Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT. (2017). THE EFFECTIVENESS OF SYNECTICS INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL ON FOREIGN LANGUAGE VOCABULARY TEACHING. International Journal of Languages’ Education and Teaching, Volume 5, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.18298/ijlet.1753

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Categorization of indirectly learned words as negative, positive or neutral by English subject experts. In the second experimental group, 10 words were neutral, 22 negative and 15 positive; the article reports negative words had the highest percentage in both groups.

> "Seventeen of the words students learned indirectly in the teaching process in the first experimental group have neutral connotation; 22 of them have negative connotations, and 11 of them have  positive connotations."
