## Evidence

### Dechamps 2025

Dechamps, T., & Skulmowski, A. (2025). The Effective Design of Tasks Involving Learning by Drawing: Current Trends and Methodological Progress in Research on Drawing to Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10026-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that in Schwamborn et al. (2010), participants read, drew, highlighted, or mentally imagined main ideas from a science text on the chemistry of laundering, and "significant positive effects of drawing on all three tests" (transfer, retention, drawing) were found.

> "The results showed significant positive effects of drawing on all three tests (Schwamborn et al., 2010)."

### Dechamps 2025 (2)

Dechamps, T., & Skulmowski, A. (2025). The Effective Design of Tasks Involving Learning by Drawing: Current Trends and Methodological Progress in Research on Drawing to Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10026-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that in Schmeck et al. (2014) Experiment 1, participants read a text on the biology of influenza or additionally drew pictures, and "a significant increase of knowledge in the drawing group for all tests" was observed relative to the control group.

> "The results of Experiment 1 showed a significant increase of knowledge in the drawing group for all tests compared to the control group."
