### Zhang Yan 2025

Zhang Yan, Lu Yihong, Yuan Huiyun, Meng Chao, Mu Yifei, Qian Xiaolei, Konge Lars, Bian Zhengqian. (2025). Evaluation of healthcare simulation centers: development of the “STRIVE” tool using the Delphi method. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08332-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Delphi consensus study of 30 invited heads of healthcare simulation centers in mainland China, selected across regions, hospital types and ranks. Twenty-four experts completed round one, "with a response rate of 80%", and the final model contained 24 items across six categories.
