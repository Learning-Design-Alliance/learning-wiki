Evidence¶

Koç and Koç 2017¶

Didem Koban Koç & Serdar Engin Koç. (2017). On the Role of Media Input in the Learning of Formulaic Sequences by EFL Learners. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 93–110. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1275

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on the posttest of 20 experimental and 20 control participants after the 3-week treatment. The article reports the experimental group's higher means but states "this difference was not significant"; equivalence was not tested.

"Table 1 shows that the learning rate for the experimental group (M = 13.75, SD = 4.55) was higher than that of the control group (M = 11.80, SD = 5.99). However, this difference was not significant."
