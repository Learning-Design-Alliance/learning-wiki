Evidence¶

Moore 2018¶

James Christopher Moore. (2018). Efficacy of Multimedia Learning Modules as Preparation for Lecture-Based Tutorials in Electromagnetism. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010023

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Comparison of common closed-book free-response exams across four semesters of the same course. For electrostatics the non-MLM group averaged (67 ± 2)% versus (75 ± 2)% for the MLM group; for electromagnetism (76 ± 3)% versus (85 ± 2)%, a "significantly greater exam average" at p < 1%.

"The MLM group had a signiﬁcantly greater exam average for both exams compared to the non-MLM group valid at the p < 1% level."
