## Evidence

### Stephen Gorard 2015

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2015). Fresh Start Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581126

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group analyses of the same school-led RCT using the same effect-size calculation for sub-groups such as FSM pupils, EAL pupils, boys and those with identified special needs. The summary table prints an effect size of +0.24 for the FSM group (104 pupils).

> "The impact evaluation also suggests positive progress results for FSM -eligible pupils, and for all pupil sub -groups regardless of age, sex, first language, ethnicity, or special education need. However, as the numbers of pupils in the sub -groups are smaller, these findings are less secure than the overall finding."
