### Yıldız Çelik Hilal 2025

Yıldız Çelik Hilal, Uğur Esra. (2025). Turkish validity and reliability study of the simulation self-report cognitive load measurement tool 2.0. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07504-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal consistency analysis of the 101 students' responses after a respiratory-distress simulation scenario found a total Cronbach's alpha of 0.822, with subdimension values '0.757, 0.572, and 0.848, respectively' for intrinsic, extraneous, and germane load.

> "The total Cronbach’s alpha for the adapted tool was found to be 0.822, indicating good internal consistency. The Cronbach’s alpha values for the intrinsic, extraneous, and germane cognitive load subdimensions were 0.757, 0.572, and 0.848, respectively (Table 4)."
