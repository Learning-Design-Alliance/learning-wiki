## Evidence

### Wiznia Daniel 2012

Wiznia Daniel, Korom Robert, Marzuk Peter, Safdieh Joseph, Grafstein Bernice. (2012). PBL 2.0: enhancing problem-based learning through increased student participation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v17i0.17375

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report survey percentages from the post-course PBL 2.0 evaluation on a five-point Likert scale, grouped into Agree/Neutral/Disagree for analysis. The article prints "62% of students spent more time preparing all of the learning objectives"; no effect size is reported.

> "Regarding student time invested in preparing for learning objectives, 62% of students spent more time preparing all of the learning objectives compared to their previous PBL experiences."

### Wiznia Daniel 2012 (2)

Wiznia Daniel, Korom Robert, Marzuk Peter, Safdieh Joseph, Grafstein Bernice. (2012). PBL 2.0: enhancing problem-based learning through increased student participation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v17i0.17375

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same survey, separate item on time spent preparing the student's own assigned objective. Percentages are descriptive self-reports; the article prints no statistical test or effect size for this item.

> "47% of students spent the same amount of time preparing their assigned objective as compared to last year, 30% spent ‘less’ or ‘much less’ time on their own objectives, whereas 22% spent ‘more’ or ‘much more.’"
