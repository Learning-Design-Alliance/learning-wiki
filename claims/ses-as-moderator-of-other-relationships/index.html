Evidence¶

Tan 2024¶

Tan, C. Y. (2024). Socioeconomic Status and Student Learning: Insights from an Umbrella Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09929-3

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Among 26 meta-analyses in the umbrella review, seven investigated SES as a moderator of other variable–outcome associations (e.g., Kuncel et al. 2010 on test scores and grades; Tucker-Drob & Bates 2016 on SES × genes interactions), rather than direct SES–learning associations.

"seven meta-analyses did not examine the SES-student learning outcomes association but investigated moderating effects of SES in associations between other variables and students’ learning outcomes."
