Meyer 2022¶

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

A design assertion in the assessment-design section of the white paper, not an empirical comparison study. The article states the adaptive format's precision advantage and that "A fixed-form test could only obtain precision to a similar extent with a longer and more time-consuming test." No data or effect size is reported.

"The adaptive nature of the assessment produces better measurement precision throughout the range of the scale than a traditional fixed-form assessment of the same length. A fixed-form test could only obtain precision to a similar extent with a longer and more time-consuming test."
