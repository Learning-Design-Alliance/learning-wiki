Robinson 2026¶

Robinson, Carly D., David Gormley, Ana Trindade Ribeiro, and Susanna Loeb. (2026). Access is Not Enough: Human Support Improves Engagement with AI Tutoring. EdWorkingPaper No. 26-1451, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai26-1451

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Site-level subgroup analysis (Table A8) of the two RCTs, ranging from a treatment effect of -1.359 minutes at one site to 9.114 minutes at another. The article cautions that subgroup estimates should be interpreted cautiously given the small sample size.

"Treatment effects also varied across sites (Table A8). Several sites showed little or no effect, while others experienced meaningful increases in platform usage, suggesting that local implementation conditions may influence the extent to which human support increases student usage."
