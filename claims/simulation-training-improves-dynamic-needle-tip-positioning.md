## Evidence

### Oh Eun Jung 2020

Oh Eun Jung, Lee Jong-Hwan, Kwon Eun Jin, Min Jeong Jin. (2020). Simulation-based training using a vessel phantom effectively improved first attempt success and dynamic needle-tip positioning ability for ultrasound-guided radial artery cannulation in real patients: An assessor-blinded randomized controlled study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234567

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial's real-patient phase, dynamic needle-tip positioning ability was assessed from video by a blinded assessor using a 17-item checklist; "68.2% vs. 7.5%" favored the simulation group. Visualization at least once: 100% vs 70.0% (P < 0.0001).

> "The proportion of residents who were able to dynamically position the needle-tip during cannulation was significantly higher in the simulation group compared to the control group (68.2% vs. 7.5%, P < 0.0001, respectively)."
