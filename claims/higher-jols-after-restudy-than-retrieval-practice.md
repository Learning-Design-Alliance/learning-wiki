# Students gave higher judgments of learning after restudy than after retrieval practice in Experiment 2 but not in Experiment 1

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Experiment 2 found significantly higher JOLs after restudy than after retrieval practice (t(122) = 5.25, p < .001, d = .33). [→ Luotong Hui 2021](#luotong-hui-2021)
`q3 i?` Experiment 1 found no difference in JOLs between the two strategies (t(62) = .78, p = .44). [→ Luotong Hui 2021 (2)](#luotong-hui-2021-2)
