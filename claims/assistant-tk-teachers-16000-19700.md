## Evidence

### Melnick 2022

Melnick, H., García, E., & Leung-Gagné, M. (2022). Building a well-qualified transitional kindergarten workforce in California: Needs and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/826.674

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Projection from the report's staffing-configuration assumption of one lead and one assistant teacher per class, with class sizes of up to 24 (2022–23) and up to 20 (2023–24 onward) at 90% enrollment efficiency. The report projects total need but not additional need, because no baseline assistant data exist.

> "California will need at least 16,000 to 19,700 assistant TK teachers by 2025–26, assuming each class will have an assistant teacher to meet child–adult ratios of 12:1 in 2022–23 and 10:1 in 2023–24."
