## Evidence

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire analysis of team properties at each of the three hackathon events (Fig 4); all teams reported team goal clarity values ≧ 4.00 and relatively higher goal clarity than other team measures. No effect size was printed.

> "We observed a significant rise in the team goal clarity between the first and second hackathon event and relative stability by the third hackathon event (see Fig 4)."

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022 (2)

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire analysis across the three events; all teams reported team process values ≧ 4.00, and the third-event drop remained ≧ 4.00. The authors link the drop to course progression and parallel course load. No effect size was printed.

> "We observed a rise in the team process between the first and second hackathon event and a drop at the third hackathon event (see Fig 4)."

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022 (3)

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire analysis of team participation across the three events; participation perception dropped between the first and second events but remained ≧ 4.00 before rising again. No effect size was printed.

> "But, the third hackathon event saw a rise at the third hackathon event to the same level as the first hackathon (see Fig 4)."
