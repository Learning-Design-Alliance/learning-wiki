Evidence¶

Neitzel 2025¶

Neitzel, A.J., & Storey, N. (2025). Air Reading: A randomized evaluation of a virtual tutoring model in Louisiana schools. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://education.jhu.edu/crre

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Dosage analysis in the Louisiana RCT replaced the treatment indicator with attendance measures; high dosage was defined as 56 or more sessions. On average treatment students "attended 54.2 sessions (SD = 14.5), and 56% reached the high-dosage threshold of 56 or more sessions". The article reports "no significant relationship between level of dosage of tutoring session and achievement".

"Both high- and low-dosage groups outperformed controls, but neither estimate alone reached statistical significance when compared to control."
