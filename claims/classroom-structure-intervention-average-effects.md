# Classroom structure interventions produce significant positive average effects on achievement and engagement and reduce disengagement, with a non-significant effect on competence beliefs

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Patall 2023

Patall, E. A., Yates, N., Lee, J., Chen, S., Bhat, P., Lee, K., Beretvas, S. N., Guan, S., Cohen, M., Zhai, F., Brecht-Heitz, S., DeLorenzo, D., & Gonzales, K. (2023). A meta-analysis of teachers’ provision of structure in the classroom and students’ academic competence beliefs, engagement, and achievement. Educational Psychologist. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED631356

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A meta-analysis of intervention studies with control groups pooled 71 samples from 46 structure intervention studies, reporting a "positive statistically significant average effect (g) on achievement (0.33), engagement (0.46), and disengagement (-0.34)". The competence beliefs effect (g = 0.26) was not significant; these g values fall below d = 0.4 (and the achievement and disengagement values below d = 0.4 on the standard thresholds), so impact is coded as 1.

> "A meta-analysis of 71 samples from 46 structure intervention studies revealed a positive statistically significant average effect (g) on achievement (0.33), engagement (0.46), and disengagement (-0.34), but a statistically non-significant effect for competence beliefs (0.26)."
