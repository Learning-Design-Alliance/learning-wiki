### E. Tufino 2025

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rubric-based assessment of group-submitted Jupyter Notebook exercises in the two 3-hour introduction sessions (S1 and S2), scored on four ability levels from missing to near mastery. The article reports "high scores prevalent" without printing percentages or effect sizes.

> "The high scores prevalent in these sessions indicate an effective grasp of Python programming essentials early in the course, particularly in skills related to 'Implement'."
