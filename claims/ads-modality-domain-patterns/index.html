q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis: generative AI and chatbot-based virtual patients "more commonly associated with enhancements in affective outcomes", with variable, predominantly subjective cognitive effects; one study showed comparable diagnostic accuracy to real-patient simulation.

"Generative AI and conversational agents (chatbot-based virtual patients) were more commonly associated with enhancements in affective outcomes including motivation, perceived competence, and satisfaction, with variable and predominantly subjective effects on cognitive skill development [7, 30–33, 36]."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Affective outcomes (satisfaction, confidence, engagement) are consistently positive across ADS modalities but rest predominantly on subjective, short-term measures — related
  • Cognitive outcomes of AI-driven simulation are the most variable across modalities and designs, with generative AI gains often perceived rather than objectively validated — related
  • AI-driven simulation is associated with a statistically significant improvement in overall learning outcomes compared to traditional training in dental education — related
  • GenAI/LLM modalities provide the strongest evidence for cognitive and communication outcomes, while chatbots show weak independent effects — reports the opposite
  • Several included studies found haptic VR training equivalent rather than superior to conventional dental training — reports the opposite
  • Review syntheses: VR/AR most often improved academic achievement and motivation, but sometimes performed no better than conventional technologies — related
  • Immersion principle: people do not necessarily learn better in 3D immersive virtual reality than with a corresponding 2D desktop presentation (d = -0.10 across 9 tests) — related
  • AR/VR training shows equal or superior outcomes versus conventional training in dental and OMFS education — related
  • Haptic simulator trials more frequently show superior accuracy outcomes when force-feedback and structured guidance are included — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Software-based platforms generally improve examination scores, diagnostic accuracy, and decision-making performance — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The greatest impact of virtual or blended laboratories is in the cognitive domain and the least in the psychomotor domain — related
  • Feedback is necessary for performance improvement on virtual colonoscopy simulators; without feedback novices do not improve — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Video cases significantly improved cognitive, affective, and overall learning outcomes compared to paper cases in dental education — related
