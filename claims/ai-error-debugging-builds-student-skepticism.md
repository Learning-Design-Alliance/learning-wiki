`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Authors' observational reflection from a single cohort of two high-school students and early undergraduates in a ten-week project, not a controlled comparison. The article reports this developmental arc and suggests working with imperfect AI outputs "may itself be a valuable pedagogical intervention."

> "Early in the project, students accepted LLM outputs with little scrutiny; by the end, they had developed skepticism and debugging skills that allowed them to identify errors independently."
