Evidence¶

Anja Prinz 2021¶

Anja Prinz, Stefanie Golke, Jörg Wittwer. (2021). Counteracting detrimental effects of misconceptions on learning and metacomprehension accuracy: The utility of refutation texts and think sheets. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09535-8

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Contrast analysis (weights: think-sheet +2, refutation-text −1, control −1) on response-confidence accuracy (gamma correlations) in the randomized experiment, with 14 participants excluded for performance-score invariance: "F(1, 75) = 4.43, p = .039, η2 = .06 (medium effect)".
