Offerdahl E. G., McConnell M., Boyer J. (2018). Can I Have Your Recipe? Using a Fidelity of Implementation (FOI) Framework to Identify the Key Ingredients of Formative Assessment for Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-02-0029

`q2 · i2` · `theoretical · r2`

The review attributes this to the Kluger and DeNisi (1996) meta-analysis of feedback interventions, citing it as evidence that "there are potentially productive and unproductive adaptations of feedback." The article reports the printed effect size d = 0.41 and that more than 38% of studies measured a negative effect.

> "In a meta-analysis of feedback interventions, Kluger and DeNisi (1996) detected an average positive effect (d = 0.41) of feedback on student learning, yet in more than 38% of the studies, a negative effect was measured."
