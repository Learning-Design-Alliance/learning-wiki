## Evidence

### Iordanou 2010

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher-Irwin comparisons restricted to participants with rebuttal opportunity, on the social topic at final assessment: "38 of 39" experimental versus "2 of 8" control (p = .018); on the science topic 33 of 37 versus 0 of 3 (p \< .001). No effect size is printed.

> "At the final assessment, however, the proportion of experimental condition participants who made a rebuttal—38 of 39—was significantly higher than the corresponding proportion of the control condition participants—2 of 8 (a ¼ .018, Fisher-Irwin test)."

### Iordanou 2010 (2)

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fisher-Irwin comparison across the two experimental conditions on the science topic at final assessment among participants with rebuttal opportunity: "20 of 20" SCI versus "13 of 17" SOC (p = .036). No effect size is printed.

> "Although all of the SCI participants who had an opportunity to make a rebuttal did so—20 of 20—not all of the SOC participants who had an opportunity to make a Rebuttal did so—13 of 17 (a ¼ .036, Fisher-Irwin test)."
