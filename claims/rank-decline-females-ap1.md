## Evidence

### Turnbull 2019

Turnbull, S. M., Vanholsbeeck, F., Locke, K., & O'Neale, D. R. J. (2019). Bourdieu, networks, and movements: Using the concepts of habitus, field and capital to understand a network analysis of gender differences in undergraduate physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0222357](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0222357)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests compared percentile-rank change from high school to AP1 by gender; the study reports "Difference in Position=−5.71" for low achievers and "Difference in Position=−8.57" for middle achievers, with no standardized effect size printed.

> "On average, low achieving female students went down around 6 ranks compared to their male counterparts (Difference in Position=−5.71, CI:−8.57 -−2.86), while middle achieving female students went down around 9 ranks relative to their male counterparts (Difference in Position=−8.57, CI:−12.86 -−2.86). There was no significant gender difference in rank change for top achievers."
