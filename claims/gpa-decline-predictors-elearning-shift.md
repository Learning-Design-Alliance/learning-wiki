### Alghamdi Amal A. 2024

Alghamdi Amal A., Alyousif Ghada F., AlQarni Amani M., Amer Fatma H., Alfadhel Taghreed O., Almutairi Rawan N., Almutairi Shatha M., Almutairi Anwar D., Hakami Nouf A., Al Ghamdi Kholoud. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression models (Table 6) estimating relative risk of GPA fluctuation in the 1371-student national survey, adjusted for gender and academic year. The article reports "adjusted OR = 3.25, 95% CI = 2.14–4.96" for senior students and further adjusted ORs for GPA B and shy personality.

> "The odds of the decline in GPA was greater amongst the senior students (adjusted OR = 3.25, 95% CI = 2.14–4.96), the participants with a GPA of B (adjusted OR = 2.08, 95% CI = 1.42–3.05), and the participants with a shy personality (adjusted OR = 1.50, 95% CI = 1.03–2.18)."
