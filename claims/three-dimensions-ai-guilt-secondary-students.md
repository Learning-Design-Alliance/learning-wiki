## Evidence

### Chan 2024

Chan, C. K. Y. (2024). Exploring the Factors of “AI Guilt” Among Students – Are You Guilty of Using AI in Your Homework?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.10777](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.10777)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses from 97 Hong Kong secondary students (37 female, 57 male, mean age 16.5), with coding "reviewed by multiple researchers to ensure reliability and validity". Thematic analysis "revealing key dimensions of factors that lead to AI guilt" as the three named themes.

> "These initial codes were then grouped into broader themes through thematic analysis, revealing key dimensions of factors that lead to AI guilt such as (1). Perceived laziness and authenticity (2). Fear of judgment, and (3). Concerns about identity and self-efficacy."
