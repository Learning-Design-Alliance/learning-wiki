## Evidence

### E. Michael Nussbaum 2015

E. Michael Nussbaum, Marissa C. Owens, Gale M. Sinatra, Abeera P. Rehmat, Jacqueline R. Cordova, Sajjad Ahmad, Fred C. Harris, Jr., Sergiu M. Dascalu. (2015). Losing the Lake: Simulations to Promote Gains in Student Knowledge and Interest about Climate Change. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijese.2015.277a

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the 8-item interest survey (internal consistency .81 pre, .86 post) in the randomized trial; the increase was driven by the first two items (Las Vegas water sources, p = .002; home conservation, p < .001). Printed effect size: "d = .23".

> "The Group x Time effect was significant ( F(1, 110) = 3.98, p < .05).  In the experimental group, the means increased by one point (from 22.25 to 23.25, p = .015, d = .23) but did not change in the control group (23.01 at pretest, 22.9 at posttest, p = .69)."
