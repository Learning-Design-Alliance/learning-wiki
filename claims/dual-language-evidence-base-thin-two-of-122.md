# The evidence base on dual language programs is thin: only two of 122 identified studies met WWC standards

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 122 studies identified in a February 2020 literature search, two met WWC standards, so effects of dual language programs implemented in most settings are unknown. [→ review-1](#review-1)

## Evidence

### review-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, December). Dual language programs. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/726

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The WWC's systematic review using the version 4.1 English language arts protocol identified 122 studies; 43 did not meet standards, 71 were ineligible (typically lacking a comparison group), and 6 were out of scope. The report concludes effects in most settings are unknown and more rigorous research is needed.

> "The WWC identified 122 studies that investigated the effectiveness of dual language programs from a literature search in the Education Resources Information Center (ERIC) and other databases in February 2020. Of these 122 studies, two meet WWC standards"
