Evidence¶

Griffith 2025¶

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2025). Funding student needs: A review of state funding policies for English learners and students from low-income backgrounds. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/471.440

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's synthesis of five state adequacy studies conducted between 2016 and 2023 found recommended "weights for EL students ranged from 0.15 (district of Columbia) to 0.40 (Michigan and Nevada)" and at-risk weights from 0.30 to 0.81, converted to 2024 dollars.

"The recommended weights for EL students ranged from 0.15 (district of Columbia) to 0.40 (Michigan and Nevada), and for low-income/at-risk students ranged from 0.30 (Michigan) to 0.81 (delaware). We multiplied these weights by each study’s recommended base amount and then adjusted these amounts for inflation,44 showing that in 2024 dollars, the funding recommendations ranged from $1,548 (delaware) to $4,976 (Nevada) for EL students and from $3,389 (Michigan) to $8,357 (delaware) for at-risk students."
