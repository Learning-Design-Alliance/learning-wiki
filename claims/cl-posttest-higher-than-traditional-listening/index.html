Evidence¶

Min Han 2015¶

Min Han. (2015). An Empirical Study on the Application of Cooperative Learning to English Listening Classes. English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p177

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-samples t-test on posttest scores of two intact classes (CG N=53, mean 59.09; EG N=52, mean 64.54) after a semester in which the experiment class received CL for two hours weekly. The article reports "the significant level is 0.000". No effect size is printed.

"In the posttest, the mean score in the experiment class is much higher than that of control class. As the significant level is 0.000, that is p<0.01, we can say in this research, CL is very effective and brings significant results to students’ listening competence"
