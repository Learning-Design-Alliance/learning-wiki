Evidence¶

Linda Froese 2022¶

Linda Froese, Julian Roelle. (2022). Expert example standards but not idea unit standards help learners accurately evaluate the quality of self-generated examples. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09293-z

q3 · i3 · causal · r3

In the 2×2 factorial experiment (N = 131 university students), all learners generated examples for eight declarative concepts and rated them no/partial/full credit. The article reports "a mean bias of 22.9%" significantly greater than zero, d = 1.20, and overall absolute accuracy of 32.4%.

"Overall, they were overconfident with a mean bias of 22.9% (SD = 19.1%), which was significantly greater than zero, t(130) = 13.75, p < .001, d = 1.20."
