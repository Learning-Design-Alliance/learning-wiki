## Evidence

### Baños Raúl 2020

Baños Raúl, Fuentesal Julio, Conte Luis, Ortiz-Camacho María del Mar, Zamarripa Jorge. (2020). Satisfaction, Enjoyment and Boredom with Physical Education as Mediator between Autonomy Support and Academic Performance in Physical Education. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17238898

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling in the cross-sectional sample of 748 Mexican third-grade secondary students (374 girls, 374 boys) found "autonomy support does not directly predict PE performance"; the direct path was non-significant and no effect size was printed for it.

> "autonomy support does not directly predict PE performance, but rather that it is necessary that students feel satisfied with PE for there to be a positive forecast of the student’s academic performance."
