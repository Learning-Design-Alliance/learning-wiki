## Evidence

### Sidiq Mohammad et al 2025

Sidiq Mohammad et al. (2025). Screen time exposure and academic performance, anxiety, and behavioral problems among school children. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19409

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey of 537 parents; total screen time across all digital displays was 21.45 ± 8.54 h per week. The authors note this is well beyond the WHO general recommended limit of 1 h a day.

> "This study revealed that children are engaged in excessive screen time, as reported by parents, exceeding 3 h per day, among relatively young children aged 4–14 years. These figures are three times greater than the recommended 1–2 h per day"
