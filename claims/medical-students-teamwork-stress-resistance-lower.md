## Evidence

### Harendza Sigrid 2019

Harendza Sigrid, Soll Henning, Prediger Sarah, Kadmon Martina, Berberat Pascal O., Oubaid Viktor. (2019). Assessing core competences of medical students with a test for flight school applicants. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1438-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same GAP-test comparison of 67 medical students versus 117 selected flight school applicants rated on behaviour checklists; t-tests showed significantly lower student ratings for teamwork and stress resistance with the printed effect sizes d = .77 and d = .70.

> "they reached significantly lower ratings for the competence domains teamwork (TW), p < .001 (d = .77), stress resistance (SR), p < .001 (d = .70), and awareness (AW), p < .001 (d = 1.31)"
