The Relation Between Text Reading and Reading Comprehension Varies as a Function of Developmental Phase, Orthographic Depth, and Measurement Characteristics: Evidence From a Meta-Analysis. `q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderation analysis by grade and orthographic depth across the meta-analytic corpus, with r = .69 (shallow) and r = .73 (deep) in primary grades, and shallow-orthography correlations falling to .42 and .30 in middle and high school versus .59 and .54 in deep orthographies.

> "the relation was similarly strong regardless of orthographic depth in primary grades (r = .69 in shallow orthographies & r = .73 in deep orthographies), but in later phases of development in middle and high school, the relation was substantially weaker in shallow orthographies than in deep orthographies"
