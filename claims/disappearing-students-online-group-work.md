## Evidence

### DePriter 2012

DePriter, T. (2012). An Investigation of Teaching Strategy in the Distance Learning Mathematics Classroom. Journal of Educators Online, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020037

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The researcher's observational account of the intervention reports that "disappearing" students, most notably in the constructivist-based group, complicated group formation because projects began during the second week of the term.

> "It is not uncommon in the online Math 009 course for students to withdraw during the first few weeks of class. This made establishing and maintaining groups difficult"
