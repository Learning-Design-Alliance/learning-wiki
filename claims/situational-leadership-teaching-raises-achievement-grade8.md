## Evidence

### Shaukat Ali Raza and Asma Sikandar 2018

Shaukat Ali Raza and Asma Sikandar. (2018). Impact of Leadership Style of Teacher on the Performance of Students: An Application of Hersey and Blanchard Situational Model. Bulletin of Education and Research, 40(3), 73-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1209826

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest experiment: 40 experimental students taught General Science chapters 5-7 with readiness-matched styles for 10 weeks; 40 controls taught traditionally. Independent samples t-test gave t = -3.64, p = 0.00 at posttest; the article reports "a significant difference in mean scores". No effect size is printed.

> "Whereas, a significant difference in mean scores of control group (31.58) and experimental group (37.25) is found i.e. the achievement scores of experimental group are better than those of contr ol group."
