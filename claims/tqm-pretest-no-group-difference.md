# Experimental and control groups did not differ significantly in TQM perceptions before the training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-test perception scores of the experimental and control groups were not statistically significant different (Z = -.214, p = .831), indicating similar baseline perceptions. [→ Altunay 2016](#altunay-2016)

## Evidence

### Altunay 2016

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test on pre-test scores in the pretest-posttest control-group design (n = 25 per group as printed in Table 1); mean ranks were 25.06 (experimental) and 25.94 (control), Z = -.214, p = .831. Equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant result.

> "Mann -Whitney U test was found that it was not statistically significant between pre -test scores about the perceptions of experimental and control groups."
