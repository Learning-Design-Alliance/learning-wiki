## Evidence

### Sirin 2005

Sirin, S. R. (2005). Socioeconomic Status and Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analytic Review of Research. Review of Educational Research, 75(3), 417-453. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543075003417

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of school location across 26 studies categorized as suburban (k = 9), urban (k = 13), and rural (k = 4); Qb(2, 26) = 11.62, p < .005. The printed effect sizes were mean correlations of r = .28 (suburban), r = .23 (urban), and r = .17 (rural); only the suburban-rural pairwise comparison was significant at p < .001.

> "the average ES for the suburban schools was the largest (.28), and the average ES for the rural schools was the smallest (.17). The average ES for urban schools was also .23."
