## Evidence

### Caballero 2015

Caballero, M. D., Wilcox, B. R., Pollock, S. J., & Doughty, L. (2015). Unpacking Students' Use of Mathematics in Upper-division Physics: Where do we go from here? arXiv. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/36/6/065004](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/36/6/065004)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of post-ACER studies of Taylor series and Dirac delta functions, which examined exam solutions and coordinated them with think-aloud interviews coded using ACER components. The review reports "the overwhelming majority of the students ( >80%) used the appropriate tool" when prompted, but half or more struggled without prompting. No effect size is printed.

> "the overwhelming majority of the students ( >80%) used the appropriate tool. However, when explicit prompting was removed in think-aloud interviews, both studies found that half or more of the students struggled to activate the necessary mathematical tool"
