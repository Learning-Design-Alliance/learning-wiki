## Evidence

### Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Cronbach’s Alpha of Domain‑Specific Knowledge Tests Before and After Learning: A Meta‑Analysis of Published Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09982-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Cluster-robust random-effects meta-analysis of 285 effect sizes from 55 samples of knowledge tests reporting Alphas before and after learning. The estimated average Alpha was 0.85 with 90% CI [0.82, 0.87], described as against Hypothesis 1 which predicted 0.70–0.75.

> "the overall estimate from the cluster-robust random-effects meta-analysis was Alpha = 0.85, with a 90% confidence interval (CI) of [0.82, 0.87]"

### Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025 (2)

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Cronbach’s Alpha of Domain‑Specific Knowledge Tests Before and After Learning: A Meta‑Analysis of Published Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09982-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Derived from the meta-analytic Alpha using the Spearman–Brown formula. The expected item intercorrelation was "rexp = 0.22, 90% CI [0.18; 0.25]", in line with Hypothesis 2 predicting rexp < 0.30, though the broad PI indicates substantial heterogeneity.

> "This estimated intercorrelation was rexp = 0.22, 90% CI [0.18; 0.25], with a 90% prediction interval (PI) of 90% PI [0.03, 0.57]"
