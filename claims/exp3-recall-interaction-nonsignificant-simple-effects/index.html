Evidence¶

Michael Batashvili 2022¶

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Experiment 3 (48 participants) used three facts as reminders and an emphasized-title interference story. Simple effects showed significant differences only between the reminder condition and all other conditions, but "after applying the Bonferroni correction, none of the compar isons fell below the adjusted alpha".

"However, there was a signi ﬁcant interaction between reminder and interference effects on recall accuracy, F(1,47) = 5.06, p = 0.03, η² = 0.03, BF 10 = 0.15 (see Fig. 4)"

Michael Batashvili 2022 (2)¶

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 3 (48 participants) yes/no recognition accuracy ANOVA: no significant reminder, interference, or interaction effects on recognition accuracy. Equivalence was not tested.

"there was no signi ﬁcant main effect of reminder, F(1,47) = 0.02, p = 0.88, η² = <0.001, BF 10 = 0.15, or of interference factors on recognition accuracy, F(1,47) = 1.53, p = 0.22, η² = 0.01, BF10 = 0.38"
