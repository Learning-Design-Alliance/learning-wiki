## Evidence

### Lee Jia Jia 2026

Lee Jia Jia, Yeap Li Ling, Mohammed Ali Haider, Yap Chuan Sheng, Hassan Bassam Abdul Rasool, Dujaili Juman Abdulelah, Saw Pui San, Blebil Ali Qais. (2026). Experiential learning to advocacy: A peer-led approach to safe medication disposal in pharmacy education. PLOS One 21(4): e0343961. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0343961

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest–posttest study of 43 Malaysian undergraduate pharmacy students who completed a validated 10-item questionnaire before and after a peer-led workshop; the Wilcoxon signed-rank test gave Z = −4.827, p < 0.001, with a large effect size (r = 0.74).

> "Overall, participants' mean knowledge score of safe medication disposal significantly increased from 6.86 to 8.44, indicating a meaningful enhancement in understanding proper medication disposal"
