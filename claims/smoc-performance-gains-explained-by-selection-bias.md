## Evidence

### Robinson 2021

Robinson, D. H. (2021). A Complete SMOCkery: Daily Online Testing Did Not Boost College Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09588-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reanalysis of major-composition data for 994 students (2008) and 941 (2011) in the introductory psychology course, computing a major-weighted average GPA for each group. The weighted difference of 0.11 matched the reported GPA advantages, so "the student performance increases can be fully explained by selection bias".

> "This “major” effect size for the online testing group over the traditional group (3.29 − 3.18 = 0.11) is almost identical to the reported advantages reported by Pennebaker et al. ( 2013) for both the concurrent semester (3.07 − 2.96 = 0.11) and the subsequent semester (3.10 − 2.98 = 0.12)."
