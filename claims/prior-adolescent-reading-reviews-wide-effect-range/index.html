Julia J. Yi 2026¶

Julia J. Yi, A. Angelique Aitken, Emily Sharp, Adiba Nusrat, Micheal Sandbank. (2026). What Works for Teaching Word Reading to Adolescents? A Meta-Analytic Investigation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10124-9

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The review's own synthesis of ten prior reviews (Table 1, 2003-2023), which it reports showed positive effects "stretching from 0 – 1.31" but conceptualized literacy outcomes too broadly to isolate word-reading approach effects.

"Previous meta-analyses and systematic reviews examining adolescents and reading interventions have generally reported statistically significant, positive mean effects of the interventions on word reading, though with a wide range of observed effects stretching from 0 – 1.31"
