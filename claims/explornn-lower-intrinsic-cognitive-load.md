## Evidence

### Alex Bäuerle 2022

Alex Bäuerle, Patrick Albus, Raphael Stork, Tina Seufert, and Timo Ropinski. (2022). exploRNN: Teaching Recurrent Neural Networks through Visual Exploration. [doi:10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on intrinsic cognitive load in the between-subjects study (37 participants) found a significant difference (F(1,34)=3.85, p=.029, η²p=.099). The authors note perceived difficulty was 12.71% lower for exploRNN even though the text content was identical.

> "Contrary to our expectations, we found a significant difference between text and exploRNN group for ICL (F (1, 34) = 3 .85, p = .029, η2p = .099)."
