## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After removing questions with point biserials below 0.30, physical questions had a significantly higher point biserial value than VR questions (p = 0.0053), with both above the 0.30 quality threshold. [→ Zhang Y 2026](#zhang-y-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Sun J, Marshall K, Mitchell JP, Laprade J, Nederveen JP, Helli PB, Rebalka IA. (2026). A preliminary comparison of bellringer performance across three visual modalities for the assessment of anatomy knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70145

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on point biserial values from the mock bellringer exam after excluding six questions with point biserials <0.30. Physical questions averaged 0.6342 ± 0.0217 versus VR 0.5427 ± 0.0222, a "significantly higher (p‐value = 0.0053)" difference.

> "a significantly higher (p‐value = 0.0053) point biserial value for physical questions (point biserial = 0.6342 ± 0.0217) was detected compared to VR (point biserial = 0.5427 ± 0.0222)"
