## Evidence

### Mark Feng Teng 2024

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA across four annual MCK measurements in the longitudinal sample. The time main effect was significant: "F = 3356.249, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.938", showing MCK scores increased cumulatively from grade 3 to grade 6.

> "The main effect of time was significant, F = 3356.249, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.938, indicating that the MCK scores increased with cumulative time."

### Mark Feng Teng 2024 (2)

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

The omnibus time-by-ethnic-group interaction on MCK in the same ANOVA was significant: "F = 353.394, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.615", indicating divergent MCK growth trajectories for the two groups across the four measurements.

> "The interaction of time*ethnic group was significant, F = 353.394, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.615, indicating that the Han and Yao students had different development trends in the four measurements."
