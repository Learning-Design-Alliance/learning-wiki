# Pre-post knowledge scores rose for all staff groups during the project period, but univariate change-score comparisons against non-pilot areas were null for physicians

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average quiz scores rose pre to post (clinical 65 to 82, public health 65 to 71, primary care 79 to 85), yet the univariate change scores of clinical and public health physicians in pilot areas were not statistically different from non-pilot areas. [→ Wang Zi-Yue 2021](#wang-zi-yue-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pre-post comparison in the quasi-experiment survey: clinical quiz rose from 65 to 82 points, public health quiz from 65 to 71, and primary care quiz from 79 to 85, all pre-post differences statistically significant. These gains are not attributed to E-learning until the DID and regression models.

> "the average score of the clinical quiz raised from 65 to 82 points (P < 0.001), the average score of the public health quiz raised from 65 to 71 points (P = 0.009), and the average score of the primary care quiz raised from 79 to 85 points (P < 0.001)."

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021 (2)

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate change-score comparison in the quasi-experiment: pilot clinical physicians gained +17 points versus +18 in non-pilot areas (P = 0.714); a similar null was observed for public health physicians (P = 0.772).

> "the change score among the clinical physicians in the pilot area (+ 17 points) was not statistically different from the physicians in the non-pilot area (+ 18 points, P = 0.714)."
