`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 2 presents matched pre- and post-intervention open reflections of the 11 students (S1–S11) on VR’s usefulness in the medical curriculum, showing moves such as “I have no idea” to “Should be used more”; the abstract states all participants recommended curricular integration.

> "While initial perceptions were also positive in general, some participants indicated hesitation and scepticism during the pre-intervention survey. A clear shift can be observed in the post-intervention results, with pronounced improvement in some instances"
