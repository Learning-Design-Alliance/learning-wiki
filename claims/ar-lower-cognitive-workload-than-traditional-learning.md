## Evidence

### Zingg L 2025

Zingg L, Häusler M, Hein J, Jecklin S, Kottner S, Gascho D, Cavalcanti N, Voinov P, Götschi T, Carrillo F, Lagler F, Fürnstahl P, Farshad M. (2025). Using Hologram-Based Augmented Reality in Anatomy Learning: The TEACHANATOMY Randomized Trial. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006012

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial using adapted NASA Task Load Index (6 questions, score range 1–10, lower scores indicating lower cognitive workload). Internal consistency was good (Cronbach's alpha = .763). AR group median 3.2 [IQR = 3.0–3.7] vs TL 4.4 [IQR = 3.8–5.0], P < .001; no numeric effect size printed.

> "The results from the NASA Task Load Index revealed that learning with AR was associated with a significant decrease in overall workload score, with a median of 3.2 [IQR = 3.0–3.7] points in the AR group compared to a median of 4.4 [IQR = 3.8–5.0] points in the TL group (P < .001; see Supplemental Digital Appendixes 8 and 9 at http://links.lww.com/ACADMED/B685)."
