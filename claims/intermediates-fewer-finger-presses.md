## Evidence

### Gholami Soheil 2024

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

A novel tactile metric, number of presses (MNoP), counted pinching actions via Finger-TPS force sensors on thumb, index, and middle fingers; significant t-tests included S.1 left hand t=6.29, p<.001 and S.2 right hand t=4.90, p<.001.

> "The differences were statistically significant in the first segment for both hands, in the second segment for the right hand, and in the third segment for the right hand as well"
