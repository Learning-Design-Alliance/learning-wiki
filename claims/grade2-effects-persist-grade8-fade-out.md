## Evidence

### Rosholm 2025

Rosholm, M., Tonnesen, P. B., Rasmussen, K., Overgaard, S., Færch, J. V., Malm, S. G. & Harder, J. (2025). A tailored small group instruction intervention in mathematics benefits low achievers. npj Science of Learning 10, 18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00310-9

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Medium-term outcomes from national grade-3 math tests (16–18 months post-intervention) for second graders and grade-9 school-leaving exams (~14 months) for eighth graders. The printed medium-term effect size 0.32 in grade 2 corresponds to a small-to-medium effect; the grade-8 effect of 0.1 was not statistically significant.

> "in grade 2, the overall treatment effect is reduced by 31% to 0.32 with some variation across treatment arms, while in grade 8, it is reduced to 0.1 and is no longer statistically signiﬁcant."
