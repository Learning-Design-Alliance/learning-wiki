# Stimulation, suction, proper PPV rate, and time to completion of key neonatal resuscitation tasks did not show a statistically significant change from weeks 4 to 8 in one uncontrolled pre-post study during simulation training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant change between weeks 4 and 8 in adequate stimulation, suction, or delivery of PPV at the proper rate. [→ Vail Brennan 2018](#vail-brennan-2018)
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant change in median time to completion of key NR tasks between weeks 4 and 8. [→ Vail Brennan 2018 (2)](#vail-brennan-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Vail Brennan 2018

Vail Brennan, Morgan Melissa C., Spindler Hilary, Christmas Amelia, Cohen Susanna R., Walker Dilys M. (2018). The power of practice: simulation training improving the quality of neonatal resuscitation skills in Bihar, India. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1254-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of 113 matched simulation pairs using McNemar's Test; stimulation changed from 35.5% to 24.3% (p = 0.08), suction 62.2% to 70.3% (p = 0.25), PPV rate 36.8% to 49.1% (p = 0.08); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no statistically significant change between weeks 4 and 8 in adequate stimulation, suction, or delivery of PPV with the proper rate (Table 2)."

### Vail Brennan 2018 (2)

Vail Brennan, Morgan Melissa C., Spindler Hilary, Christmas Amelia, Cohen Susanna R., Walker Dilys M. (2018). The power of practice: simulation training improving the quality of neonatal resuscitation skills in Bihar, India. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1254-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test on time-to-task medians across 113 matched pairs; e.g., median time to initiation of PPV changed from 83 to 84 seconds (p = 0.90); equivalence was not tested.

> "Additionally, there was no statistically significant change in median time to completion of key NR tasks (Table 3)."
