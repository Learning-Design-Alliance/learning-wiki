### Katia Tödt 2026

Katia Tödt, Corinne Büching, and Bastian Granas. (2026). Digital Education and Innovation: Objectives and Conceptual Classification of the Volume. In Digital Education and Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-26816-7_1

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' conceptual argument in the section adapting the knowledge triangle. They list as expanded innovation content pedagogical practices, changing teacher roles, hybrid formats, flipped classroom models and collaborative development of digital materials, noting legal, ethical and financial framework conditions as particular challenges in public education.

> "Digital education innovations must therefore always be understood as an interplay of technological, pedagogical, organisational, and governance-related dimensions."
