### McDaniel Mark A. 2022

McDaniel Mark A., Cahill Michael J., Frey Regina F., Limeri Lisa B., Lemons Paula P. (2022). Learning Introductory Biology: Students' Concept-Building Approaches Predict Transfer on Biology Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-12-0335

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Second mixed-effects model adding RAPM (centered at 5.59) to the ACT model for the same exam data. The article reports the approach-by-question-type interaction "remained a large-size effect in the presence of the ACT (and RAPM) covariate," confirming the transfer advantage is not explained by general ability.

> "the interaction of concept-building approach with question type remained robust, F(1, 53) = 8.71, MSE = 0.004, p = 0.005, ηp2 = 0.14, showing a pattern nearly identical to that described earlier for simple contrasts"
