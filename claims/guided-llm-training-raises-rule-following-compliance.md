# Prompt-literacy guidance produced higher transcript-coded rule-following compliance than unrestricted LLM access, driven by process-over-answer and stepwise-hint behaviors

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Guided-LLM students showed higher overall rule-family compliance than Unrestricted-LLM students (d = 0.81, p = .025). [→ Mohammad Amanlou 2026](#mohammad-amanlou-2026)
`q2 i?` The contrast was driven mainly by process-over-answer (100% vs 22%) and stepwise-hint seeking (41% vs 7%), while concept tutoring was common in both groups. [→ Mohammad Amanlou 2026](#mohammad-amanlou-2026)
