### Mohammad Amanlou 2026

Mohammad Amanlou, Yasaman Amou-Jafari, Fereshte Bagheri, Fatemeh Boloukazari, Mehrad Liviyan, Elahe Khodaverdi Nadrabadi, Shahab Sherafat, Behnam Bahrak. (2026). `q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check comparing the two LLM-access groups across Practices 2–4, with three raters coding 67 student-practice transcripts against six rule families (Fleiss' κ = .896). Table 2 reports "Overall compliance, assigned" at "2.43 / 6" vs "3.21 / 6", "d = 0.81; p = .025"; rule-level R1 was 22% vs 100% and R3 7% vs 41%. Assignment to the four-week quasi-experimental conditions was not randomized, and the authors state that their findings are associational rather than causal.
