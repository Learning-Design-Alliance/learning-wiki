# Commissions scores do not differ significantly between fitness clusters in either gender

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The d2 commissions (error) measure showed no significant difference between low- and high-fitness clusters for boys or girls. [→ Reigal RE 2020](#reigal-re-2020)

## Evidence

### Reigal RE 2020

Reigal RE, Moral-Campillo L, Juárez-Ruiz de Mier R, Morillo-Baro JP, Morales-Sánchez V, Pastrana JL and Hernández-Mendo A (2020) Physical Fitness Level Is Related to Attention and Concentration in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

U-Mann Whitney tests comparing d2 commissions scores between fitness clusters (non-normal variable) in the full clustered sample. The article reports the tests "were not signiﬁcant"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Diﬀerences for the commissions (C) measure of the D2 attention test were calculated with the U-Mann Whitney test but they were not signiﬁcant."
