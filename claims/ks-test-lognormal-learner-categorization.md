The algorithm (Table 2) applies a KS test at the 5% significance level to viewing time lengths of learners exceeding successively higher viewed-video counts, labelling those whose times follow a lognormal distribution as lognormal-rounders; "Table 1 showed the number of all-rounders is very small for each course".

> "Table 1 showed the number of all-rounders is very small for each course. However, even the learners, who decided to complete a course, might not view all videos."
