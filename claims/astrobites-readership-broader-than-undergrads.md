Readership statistics from Google Analytics (2014–2019) and regular readership surveys reported in Section 2.4. The site reports "nearly 46,000 pageviews each month from over 15,000 users in 2018", with just over 50% of readers from the United States. Surveys found the bulk of readers are graduate students (30–40%), researchers (10–30%), and astronomy enthusiasts (15–30%).
