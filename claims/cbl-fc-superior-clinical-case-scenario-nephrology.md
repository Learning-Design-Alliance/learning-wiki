## Evidence

### Hu Bibi 2024

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled study assigned 46 students to CBL/FC and 46 to LBT during nephrology rotation; post-intervention clinical case scenario scores favored CBL/FC, with the CBL/FC group scoring "80.39 ± 15.05 vs. 71.43 ± 15.61, P = 0.01". No effect size is printed.
