# Internal medicine residents' self-reported confidence in understanding ultrasound principles rose from pre-test to post-test after a simulation-based ultrasound training module, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the training module, mean confidence in understanding the use of ultrasound during invasive procedures increased from 2.63 to 4.00 on a 5-point Likert scale (conservative p < .0001). [→ Keddis Mira T 2011](#keddis-mira-t-2011)

## Evidence

### Keddis Mira T 2011

Keddis Mira T, Cullen Michael W, Reed Darcy A, Halvorsen Andrew J, McDonald Furman S, Takahashi Paul Y, Bhagra Anjali. (2011). Effectiveness of an Ultrasound Training Module for Internal Medicine Residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-75

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey of first-year internal medicine interns at one residency program after a simulation-based ultrasound module during orientation; 55 of 76 interns completed the post-survey. Mean confidence rose to "4.00 (0.47)" with a conservative upper-bound paired t-test p < .0001 reported in Table 1.

> "Following the intervention, 55 (72%) interns again rated their confidence in understanding the use of ultrasound, and the mean (SD) increased to 4.00 (0.47)."
