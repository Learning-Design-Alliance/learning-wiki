Multigroup multilevel mediation model for the teacher confidence and enthusiasm dimension in the TRIAD RCT: treatment effect "0.25 points higher (p = .001)"; indirect effect significant for African American students (b = 0.06, p = .03) but not non-African American students (b = 0.04, p = .10).
