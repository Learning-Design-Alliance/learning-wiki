## Evidence

### Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser 2019

Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser. (2019). Lighting the wick in the candle of learning: generating a prediction stimulates curiosity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0056-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral analysis of the same preregistered within-subjects experiment (29 participants): memory performance as percentage of items recalled exactly, compared for high- versus low-curiosity trials using participant-specific trichotomization. A within-subjects ANOVA revealed no curiosity-by-condition interaction (F(1, 28) = 0.70, p = 0.411).

> "high-curiosity facts ( M = 61.95 and SD = 17.63) were better remembered than low-curiosity facts (M = 58.21, SD = 16.64), t(28) = 1.76, p = 0.044, and d = 0.327"
