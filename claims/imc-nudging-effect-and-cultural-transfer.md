`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' conclusion drawn from the two 2023 surveys; the article states "an indication in our data" of a nudging effect on participants open to a scientific career, not a tested causal estimate.

> "We found an indication in our data that International Particle Physics Masterclasses do have a nudging effect on participants who do not exclude the possibility of becoming a scientist or even particle physicist."

### Júlia Kekeláková and Boris Tomášik 2023 (2)

Júlia Kekeláková and Boris Tomášik. (2023). On the long-term impact of non-formal learning in particle physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.15906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.15906)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the conclusions section, framed as the impact on the majority of former participants who do not pursue scientific or R&D careers.

> "IMC then provide a kind of cultural transfer in which the (good) practices followed in particle physics are exported to other fields and professions."
