## Evidence

### Sirmandi and Sardareh 2016

Effat Heidari Sirmandi, Sedigheh Abbasnasab Sardareh. (2016). The Effect of BBC World Clips With and Without Subtitles on Intermediate EFL Learners' Vocabulary Development. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116325

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same three-group experiment, the unsubtitled group improved from a pretest mean of 18.5 to a posttest mean of 34.9 while the control reached 30.0; the article reports the no-subtitle hypothesis "Is rejected". No effect size is printed.

> "Regarding the presented information on pre and posttest of the unsubtitled group in Table 1 and 2, it revealed that the second research hypothesis "Watching BBC World clips without English subtitles have no significant impact on Intermediate Iranian EFL learners' vocabulary development." Is rejected."
