Generative AI in medical education: feasibility and educational value of LLM-generated clinical cases with MCQs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08085-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In-class Likert questionnaire with 20 students (50% male, 50% female); 35% had prior LLM experience. Table 4 shows, e.g., 7(35%) strongly agree and 12(60%) agree for enriched learning resources. Findings were reported as frequencies and percentages with no inferential test.

> "Analysis of student feedback revealed strong endorsement of the learning approach's benefits (see Table 4). 95% of them agreed that LLMs enriched clinical case-related learning resources and supported personalized learning needs."
