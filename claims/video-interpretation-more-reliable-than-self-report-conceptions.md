## Evidence

### Henna Vilppu 2019

Henna Vilppu, Ilona Södervik, Liisa Postareff, Mari Murtonen. (2019). The effect of short online pedagogical training on university teachers’ interpretations of teaching–learning situations. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09496-z

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theoretical methodological argument, not a tested comparison: the article lists self-report risks including “socially desirable responding”, self-deception and validity problems with Likert items, and cites prior video-assessment research showing beliefs shape what participants observe.

> "Thus, we claim that a method where a researcher analyses a respondent’s interpretation of a situation can provide a more reliable view of the respondent’s conceptions than just relying on self-reporting."
