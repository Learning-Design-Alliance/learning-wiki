## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Making JOLs with word stems after Lists 1–4 improved interim free recall of criterial List 5 relative to restudy (d = 0.51). [→ Veit Kubik 2022](#veit-kubik-2022)

## Evidence

### Veit Kubik 2022

Veit Kubik, Kenneth Koslowski, Torsten Schubert, Alp Aslan. (2022). Metacognitive judgments can potentiate new learning: The role of covert retrieval. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:1057–1077. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09307-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Controlled lab experiment in a multiple-list paradigm with N = 161 undergraduates randomly assigned to four practice-type groups; planned contrast on interim List-5 free recall found word-stem JOLs "superior to restudy, t(78) = 2.24, p = .014, d = 0.51".

> "Planned contrast analyses revealed that making JOLs with word stems was superior to restudy, t(78) = 2.24, p = .014, d = 0.51, 95% CI [0.13, ∞] (one-tailed)"
