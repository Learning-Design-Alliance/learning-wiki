## Evidence

### Ramírez-Vélez R 2015

Ramírez-Vélez R, Correa-Bautista JE, Muñoz-Rodríguez DI, Ramírez L, González-Ruíz K, Domínguez-Sánchez MA, Durán-Palomino D, Girabent-Farrés M, Flórez-López ME, Bagur-Calafat MC. (2015). Evidence-based practice: beliefs, attitudes, knowledge, and skills among Colombian physical therapists. Colombia Médica. [doi:10.25100/cm.v46i1.1654](https://doi.org/10.25100/cm.v46i1.1654)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1,064 Colombian physical therapists using a validated 50-item EBP questionnaire. In the attitudes and beliefs results, "Seventy-one percent of those surveyed strongly agreed" that EBP was necessary, with 27.4% agreeing; 64.1% strongly agreed EBP improved quality of patient care.

> "Seventy-one percent of those surveyed strongly agreed and 27.4% agreed that using EBP was necessary when practicing physiotherapy."
