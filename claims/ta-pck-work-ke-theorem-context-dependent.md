## Evidence

### Liam Doyle 2026

Liam Doyle, Alexandru Maries, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Exploring pedagogical content knowledge of physics teaching assistants using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1103/fgqc-dfbz](https://doi.org/10.1103/fgqc-dfbz)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Question-level PCK analysis of the three work-kinetic energy theorem items (Q8, Q24, Q25) in Section III B. The article reports scores of 57%, 90%, and 36% respectively and notes consistency with prior FCI and TUG-K studies.

> "TAs' PCK score on Q25 was one of the lowest on the survey and their score on Q24 was the highest, suggesting that TAs' ability to identify alternate conceptions related to the work-kinetic energy theorem is context dependent, a finding that is consistent with previous papers on PCK using the FCI [39] and the TUG-K [38]."
