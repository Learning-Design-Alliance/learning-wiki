## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students described calibrating their confidence against peers during GRAT discussions, which could either positively or negatively impact their self-confidence. [→ N. Mitchell 2025](#n-mitchell-2025)

## Evidence

### N. Mitchell 2025

N. Mitchell, M. Rashid, J. Foulds, and K. Forbes. (2025). Thinking About the Why: A Qualitative Study on Students' Perspectives of Paediatric Team‐Based Learning Discussions. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70161

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive qualitative focus-group analysis of 26 third-year medical students after a KFQ-based TBL session; the theme analysis found calibration with peers "could either positively or negatively impact their self-confidence", with external validation raising confidence and discrepant answers causing self-doubt.

> "Calibration refers to students seeing how confident they feel compared to their peers, the knowledge and experience they may have with respect to their peers and reflecting on how far off they feel from normative performance."
