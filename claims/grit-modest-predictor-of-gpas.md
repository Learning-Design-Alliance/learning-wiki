## Evidence

### Duckworth 2019

Duckworth, A. L., Quirk, A., Gallop, R., Hoyle, R. H., Kelly, D. R., & Matthews, M. D. (2019). Cognitive and noncognitive predictors of success. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1910510116

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same OLS regression models as the ability findings, comparing grit's coefficients with those of cognitive and physical ability. All three grit coefficients were statistically significant but "smaller" than the ability predictors' coefficients.

> "By comparison, grit predicted a smaller proportion of variance in military (β = 0.10, P < 0.001, 99% CI: 0.08 to 0.13), academic (β = 0.07, P < 0.001, 99% CI: 0.04 to 0.09), and physical (β = 0.06, P < 0.001, 99% CI: 0.04 to 0.08) GPAs."
