Lehesvuori 2026¶

Lehesvuori, S., Järvinen, M., Lämsä, J., Heilala, V., Hämäläinen, R. (2026). Exploring interconnections between communicative activities, situational stress reactions and cognitive engagement in undergraduate mathematics lesson. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09786-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Micro-level moment-by-moment analysis of Examples 1 and 2 combining transcript coding and second-by-second ASV values; the article reports all cases showed "at least 50 percent increase" in ASV in close temporal context of the critical incident.
