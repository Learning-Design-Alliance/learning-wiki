## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2010

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2010). Refined Characterization of Student Perspectives on Quantum Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from analysis of 19 post-instruction interviews. The authors underscore "the need to distinguish between the personal and the public perspectives" of modern physics students, paralleling McCaskey et al.'s double-answered Force Concept Inventory study.

> "Students are most likely to vacillate in their responses when what makes intuitive sense to them is not in agreement with what they consider to be a correct response."
