## Evidence

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on the 9-item Tzafilkou et al.-adapted motivation scale in the randomized two-group experiment. The interactive group's extrinsic motivation (M = 5.52) exceeded the traditional group's (M = 4.08); the study reports "t(48) = 3.85, p = .001, d = 1.09", a 35.29% increase attributed to competition, instant rewards, and social feedback.

> "Extrinsic motivation  was significantly higher in the interactive group ( M = 5.52) than in the traditional group ( M = 4.08), t(48) = 3.85, p = .001, d = 1.09 , indicating a 35.29% increase"

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026 (2)

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same motivation scale, combined index. The interactive group scored M = 5.12 versus the traditional group's 3.36; the study reports "t(48) = 3.75, p = .001, d = 1.06", a 52.38% increase the authors link to autonomy and variety in activities.

> "General motivation , a combined index of motivational drivers, also favoured  the interactive group ( M = 5.12 ) over the traditional group ( M = 3.36 ), t(48) = 3.75, p = .001, d = 1.06"

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026 (3)

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Same motivation scale, intrinsic subscale: no significant difference between interactive (M = 4.48) and traditional (M = 4.20) groups, "p = .590". The appendix prints Cohen's d = 0.15, a negligible effect; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "intrinsic motivation  — the internal desire to learn for personal satisfaction — showed no significant difference (M = 4.48 vs. 4.20, p = .590)"
