## Evidence

### Abdekhoda M 2016

Abdekhoda M, Dehnad A, Yousefi M. (2016). Effectiveness of training intervention to improve medical student's information literacy skills. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2016.44

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of 90 postgraduate students in a cross-sectional study using a 5-point Likert questionnaire; paired t-test showed a significant attitude improvement, with means rising from "2.9 (0.8) before intervention" to "3.9 (0.7) after intervention."

> "There was a significant improvement (p=0.00), in student's attitude. The mean (standard deviation [SD]) was 2.9 (0.8) before intervention versus the mean (SD) 3.9 (0.7) after intervention."
