Dallavis 2021¶

Dallavis, J., Kuhfeld, M., Tarasawa, B., & Ponisciak, S. (2021). Achievement growth in K-8 Catholic schools using NWEA data. Journal of Catholic Education, 24(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.15365/joce.2402012021

The national K-8 MAP Growth sector comparison reports that sector differences in achievement growth were moderated by "age and subject," with early grades showing sector gaps in growth and middle school math showing comparable growth. No statistics are printed in the available text.

"Additionally, these patterns varied by age and subject."
