# IBO theoretical items steadily increased their number of depictive representations (0.24 per item in 1993 to 2.00 in 2017), and representations became the central meaning-carrying element with few text-graphic redundancies

## Evidence

### Opitz Sebastian 2020

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive representation coding across IBO cohorts shows theory items rose from "0.24 (1993) to 2.00 (2017)" depictive representations per item; descriptive trend, no test statistic reported for this figure.

> "While practical exams used a relatively constant number of representations per item over the years (average: 1.12), the increased emphasis of IBO theoretical items on students' work with data (see Figures 3 and 4) is reflected in a steady increase in the average number of depictive representations per item: 0.24 (1993) to 2.00 (2017)."

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (2)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

ANOVA of semanticity codes shows a sharp increase in decorative elements at IBO 2017 (F(5, 618) = 87.27, p < 0.001, η² = 0.41), where representations added context such as pictures of researchers.

> "This is manifested in the sharp increase of decorative elements at IBO 2017 (F(5, 618) = 87.27, p < 0.001, η² = 0.41)."

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (3)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semanticity coding (Slough and McTigue's levels) shows most representations add new information to the text, causing very few redundancies; representations are "the central meaning–carrying element" of theoretical items.

> "The results underline a general feature of IBO items: The representations (especially in recent IBOs) are typically the central meaning–carrying element of the item, especially in theoretical tasks."
