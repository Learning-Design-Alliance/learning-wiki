Evidence¶

Chen et al. 2018¶

Xiaokan Chen, Hongmei Li, Min Gui. (2018). Instructional Effects of Syntactic Parsing on Chinese College Students’ EFL Reading Rates. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 6, No. 11. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i11.3470

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Semi-structured interview conducted in Chinese with 10 experimental-group participants selected by criteria sampling based on reading-rate gains (5 above-average gainers, 3 near average, 2 marginal). Responses were audio recorded, transcribed, and thematically coded.

"On the whole, all 10 students expressed positive attitudes towards the syntactic parsing ability training method."

Chen et al. 2018 (2)¶

Xiaokan Chen, Hongmei Li, Min Gui. (2018). Instructional Effects of Syntactic Parsing on Chinese College Students’ EFL Reading Rates. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 6, No. 11. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i11.3470

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of interview responses on perceived benefits. Three of 10 participants reported they could not recall and use the skill with difficult texts after class, suggesting the ability "may have not been automatized".

"Nine out of 10 students reported increased reading rates through the training. In addition, 8 out of 10 students responded that they were more efficient and faster in dealing with long complex sentences."
