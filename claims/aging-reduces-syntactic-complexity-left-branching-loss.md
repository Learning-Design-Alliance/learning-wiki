### Kemper 1989

Kemper, S., Kynette, D., Rash, S., O'Brien, K., & Sprott, R. (1989). Life-span changes to adults' language: Effects of memory and genre. Applied Psycholinguistics, 10, 49-66. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0142716400008419

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of two age groups (young 18-28, elderly 60-92) across three language samples: an oral questionnaire, an oral statement, and a written statement. The article reports "an overall decrement in the complexity of adults' oral and written statements" tied to left-branching clause loss.
