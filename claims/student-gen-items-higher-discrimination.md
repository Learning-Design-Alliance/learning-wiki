## Evidence

### Al-Najafi Dheyaa 2026

Al-Najafi Dheyaa, Krause Katherine D., Wang Yundi, Zuo Qi Kang, Koblanski Maya E, Leong Cameron J., Schmidt Emma, Faran Muhammad, Verma Vanay, Vyas Ravi, Campbell Matthew, Hwang Jaehyun, Deng Jiawen, Palepu Anita. (2026). Psychometric performance and student perceptions of AI- versus student-generated multiple-choice questions: a single-center randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09671-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Item-level discrimination index comparison in the ITT population using the extreme groups method, tested with the Mann-Whitney U test; bootstrap 95% CIs partially overlapped (Student-gen 0.22–0.27; AI-gen 0.16–0.22). The printed effect size is rrb = 0.310.

> "Student-gen MCQs demonstrated significantly higher item-level discrimination than AI-gen MCQs (mean DI: 0.25 ± 0.13 versus 0.19 ± 0.15; Mann-Whitney U test: W = 8214, p = 0.0001), corresponding to a small effect size (rrb = 0.310)."

### Al-Najafi Dheyaa 2026 (2)

Al-Najafi Dheyaa, Krause Katherine D., Wang Yundi, Zuo Qi Kang, Koblanski Maya E, Leong Cameron J., Schmidt Emma, Faran Muhammad, Verma Vanay, Vyas Ravi, Campbell Matthew, Hwang Jaehyun, Deng Jiawen, Palepu Anita. (2026). Psychometric performance and student perceptions of AI- versus student-generated multiple-choice questions: a single-center randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09671-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Items were categorized against published DI thresholds (poor ≤ 0.20; acceptable 0.21–0.24; good 0.25–0.34; excellent ≥ 0.35) in the ITT population. The article attributes the low discrimination in both arms to "score compression expected in an open-resource formative setting".

> "A majority of AI-gen items fell in the poor-discrimination category (62.5%), compared with a plurality of Student-gen items (42.9%); correspondingly, nearly half of Student-gen items reached the good-to-excellent range (47.3%) versus approximately one-third of AI-gen items (33.9%)"
