## Evidence

### Onivehu and Ohawuiro 2018

Adams Ogirima Onivehu, Onyiyeche Emilia Ohawuiro. (2018). Effect of PowerPoint Presentation on Students' Cognitive Achievement in Geography. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, VII(1), 46-60. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120184

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test comparison of 21 male and 24 female students within the 45-student PowerPoint group. The article reports "no significant difference between mean scores of the male and female respondents" (t(43)=0.82, p>.005); no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "the mean score of male respondents was 9.70 and standard deviation of 3.83, while the mean score of female respondents was 10.02 and standard deviation of 3.62. These results show that there was no significant difference between mean scores of the male and female respondents in the experimental group (t(43)=0.82, p>.005)."
