# Traditional-uniform schools showed higher frequencies of frequent bullying and discrimination responses than sports-uniform schools in contingency tables

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Answer-frequency distributions for bullying and discrimination differed significantly by uniform type, with lower frequencies in the usually and always categories in sports-uniform schools. [→ Cristi-Montero 2022](#cristi-montero-2022)

## Evidence

### Cristi-Montero 2022

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests on contingency tables of bullying (Table 2) and discrimination (Table 3) response categories in the cross-sectional sample. The article reports significant differences in answer frequency (χ2 = 10.7, p = 0.012; χ2 = 10.3, p < 0.001) with lower usually/always frequencies in SU schools. No effect size printed.

> "Significant differences in answer frequency according to bullying and discrimination feelings (χ2 = 10.7; p = 0.012 and χ2 = 10.3; p < 0.001, respectively). Lower answer frequency is observed in “usually” and “always” categories in schools wearing SUs, both bullying and discrimination."
