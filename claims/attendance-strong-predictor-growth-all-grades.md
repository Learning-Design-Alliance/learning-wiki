# Attendance rate was a strong positive predictor of both math and reading growth across all grades

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Attendance rate significantly predicted growth in both math and reading across all grades (p<.01), with, for example, a one percent attendance increase associated with a 0.23-point math scale-score increase in kindergarten. [→ Bahar 2025](#bahar-2025)
