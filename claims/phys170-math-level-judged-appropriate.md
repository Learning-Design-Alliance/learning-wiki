## Evidence

### S. G. J. Mochrie 2015

S. G. J. Mochrie. (2015). Vision and change in introductory physics for the life sciences. [doi:10.1119/1.4947003](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4947003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Annual anonymous end-of-semester surveys are used to ascertain students' opinions about the course. For the calculus level, it is "too high" for 19%, "just right" for 66%, and "too low" for 15%; in view of these responses the authors judge the mathematics used is appropriate. Only response fractions are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "Concerning the level of calculus speciﬁcally, it is "too high" for 19%, "just right" for 66%, and "too low" for 15% (Table III)."
