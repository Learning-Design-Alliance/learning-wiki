## Evidence

### Al-Mugheed K 2025

Al-Mugheed K, Al-Oweidat I, Alzoubi MM, Alnaeem MM, Alshehri AS, Hendy A, Baghdadi NA, Farghaly Abdelaliem SM. (2025). Effectiveness of a high-fidelity simulation in terms of cardiac auscultation knowledge and skills among nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000044970

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 2 paired-sample t-test results: intervention knowledge rose from 9.4 ± 1.6 to 12.6 ± 2.4 (P = .001) while control rose from 8.6 ± 2.2 to 9.4 ± 3.1 with paired P = .11. Skills and overall paired tests followed the same pattern (.001 vs .09 and .06). The article states "In contrast, students in the traditional teaching group did not demonstrate statistically significant improvements in mean knowledge scores."
