Addressing the knowledge gap: development of stakeholder-informed training to improve the inclusion of adults with impaired capacity to consent in trials. `q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Initial evaluation via an optional feedback survey built into the Moodle LMS; only 6 of 80+ registered users had completed it at time of writing. The change in mean perceived knowledge score from 7.2 to 8.8 is descriptive; no statistical test is reported.

> "Scores from those completing the survey to date (n = 6) indicate an increase in their perceived knowledge about how to design and conduct trials involving adults lacking capacity to consent before and after completing the CONSULT e-learning out of 10, with a change in mean score from 7.2 to 8.8"
