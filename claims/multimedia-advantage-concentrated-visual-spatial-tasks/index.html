Evidence¶

Marina Milovanoviü 2013¶

Marina Milovanoviü, Jasmina Obradoviü, Aleksandar Milajiü. (2013). APPLICATION OF INTERACTIVE MULTIMEDIA TOOLS IN TEACHING MATHEMATICS – EXAMPLES OF LESSONS FROM GEOMETRY. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008863

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Task-level analysis of Tables 1 and 2 (25 students per group per faculty). Test 1 task 4: Architecture 17.8 vs 13.2 (t = –4.265, p = 0.000); CCM 17.76 vs 13.6 (t = –3.918, p = 0.000). The authors attribute this to needing to "see given bodies and intersections by using movements in three-dimensional space".

"students from both multimedia groups were remarkably more successful in solving problems which demand visual comprehension (tasks 4 and 5), while the average scores in tasks 1, 2 and 3 were practically the same on the both groups"
