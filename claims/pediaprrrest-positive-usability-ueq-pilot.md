## Evidence

### Corazza F 2020

Corazza F, Snijders D, Arpone M, Stritoni V, Martinolli F, Daverio M, Losi MG, Soldi L, Tesauri F, Da Dalt L, Bressan S. (2020). Development and Usability of a Novel Interactive Tablet App (PediAppRREST) to Support the Management of Pediatric Cardiac Arrest: Pilot High-Fidelity Simulation-Based Study. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/19070

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Primary-outcome usability evaluation in a single-center nonrandomized controlled simulation pilot with 48 pediatric residents; teams in the intervention group (n=11 teams) used the app during a nonshockable PCA scenario. The authors report the mean UEQ scale scores "were substantially above the 0.8 cutoff"; per-scale means range from 1.568 (dependability) to 2.205 (novelty), with no effect size reported.

> "The PediAppRREST app attractiveness, perspicuity, efficiency, dependability, stimulation, and novelty were on average evaluated positively; mean UEQ scale scores were substantially above the 0.8 cutoff."
