Partial correlation check of the assumed hierarchy computed between nonadjacent levels, reported in the preliminary analyses section. The near-vanishing Analysis-Synthesis relationship in eleven of sixteen replications supports the Y-shaped structure over the assumed hierarchy.

"When the g-factor and Knowledge to Application levels composing the stem of the Y are partialed out, there is a near vanishing of the relationship between Analysis and Synthesis for eleven of the sixteen replications."
