Evidence¶

Roul 2014¶

Sushanta Kumar Roul. (2014). Language Development of the Preschool Children: The Effects of an Audio-Visual Intervention Program in Delhi. International Journal of Instruction, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085265

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group pre-test/post-test comparison of the 50-child experimental group on the Language Development Test total. The article reports means of 51.94 and 100.5 and a t-ratio of 14.41, significant at 0.01, and interprets this as a positive effect of the program.

"It can be seen that the mean pre-test and post-test scores of experimental group language development total is found to be 51.94 and 100.5 respectively with S.D. 14.91 and 18.26. The t-ratio between the two groups comes out to be 14.41. It is significant at 0.01 level of significance."
