# SRO removal was not significantly related to changes in overall discipline infractions or in suspensions at either the student or school level

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Changes in overall discipline infractions were not significantly related to SRO removal at the student or school level. [→ Arneson 2024](#arneson-2024)
`q2 i?` Suspension trends (in-school and out-of-school) for partial and full removal schools were not significantly different from schools that retained both SROs. [→ Arneson 2024 (2)](#arneson-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Arneson 2024

Arneson, A., Hinze-Pifer, R., Franklin, K., & Johnson, D.W. (2024). Removing police officers from Chicago schools: Trends and outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/removing-police-officers-from-Chicago-schools

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null difference-in-difference result from the CPS administrative data analysis of overall discipline infractions; the districtwide increase did not differ significantly by SRO removal status. Equivalence was not tested.

> "While discipline infractions increased districtwide in 2022–23 compared to 2018–19, our analyses did not detect a relationship between these changes at the student ( Table C.1 in Appendix C ) nor school (Table C.2 ) levels and the removal of SROs."

### Arneson 2024 (2)

Arneson, A., Hinze-Pifer, R., Franklin, K., & Johnson, D.W. (2024). Removing police officers from Chicago schools: Trends and outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/removing-police-officers-from-Chicago-schools

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null difference-in-difference result for suspensions, inclusive of in-school and out-of-school suspension, examined at both school and student levels in the same CPS analysis. No effect size was printed.

> "The trends for the partial and full removal categories were not significantly different than for schools that retained both SROs ( Tables C5 and C6 )."
