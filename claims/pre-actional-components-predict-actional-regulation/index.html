Evidence¶

Mathias Mejeh 2026¶

Mathias Mejeh, Yvonne M. Fromm (2026). Fostering self-regulated learning through adaptive learning technology: A differentiated perspective on the role of feedback. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102394

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Longitudinal multilevel analysis (HLM) of weekly self-reports from 194 university students over eight weeks of homework in an ALT-supported stochastics course, testing the pre-actional to actional transition. The model found planning, task value, self-efficacy, goal orientation, and joy "significantly positively associated with regulation" and anger and anxiety negatively associated.

"the linear mixed-effects model revealed that planning, task value, self-efficacy, goal orientation, and joy were significantly positively associated with regulation. Conversely, anger and anxiety showed significant negative associations with regulation."
