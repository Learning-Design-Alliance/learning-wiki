### Weggelaar-Jansen AM 2018

Weggelaar-Jansen AM, van Wijngaarden J. (2018). Transferring skills in quality collaboratives focused on improving patient logistics. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3051-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of AA and PR project team members rating skill availability on a five-point scale. The results state "the skills related to change management were most lacking" while analytical skills were more available; change management availability means ranged from 3.2 to 3.8.

> "For both QICs, respondents felt that the skills related to change management were most lacking. In contrast, analytical skills were seen as more available in the team."
