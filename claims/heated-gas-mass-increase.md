### L. B. Okun 2008

L. B. Okun. (2008). The theory of relativity and the Pythagorean theorem. https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.2379 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0809.2379](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0809.2379)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in section 9.3: heating raises the kinetic energy of every particle while total momentum stays zero, so "the mass of the gas as a whole increases" and each particle's mass is unchanged. The author notes the contrary statement appears in the literature.

> "As a result, the mass of the gas as a whole increases, while the mass of each individual particle remai ns unchanged."
