## Evidence

### Pöyhönen 2024

Pöyhönen, N., Ojantausta, O., Kaunonen, M., Vehviläinen-Julkunen, K., & Ikonen, R. (2024). Developing an Instrument to Measure Public Health Nurses’ Competence Related to Breastfeeding Beyond 12 Months. Journal of Human Lactation. https://doi.org/10.1177/08903344241254343

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric pilot analysis of the LBCS completed by 197 public health nurses (attitude analyses n = 177). Table 3 reports Cronbach's alpha of 0.80 for the overall instrument, 0.89 for the attitudes dimension, and 0.56 for the knowledge and skills dimension; the abstract reports α = 0.796 overall.

> "The internal consistency of the entire instrument and subscales was at a good level, except for the knowledge and skills dimension, and met the requirements set for a new instrument."

### Pöyhönen 2024 (2)

Pöyhönen, N., Ojantausta, O., Kaunonen, M., Vehviläinen-Julkunen, K., & Ikonen, R. (2024). Developing an Instrument to Measure Public Health Nurses’ Competence Related to Breastfeeding Beyond 12 Months. Journal of Human Lactation. https://doi.org/10.1177/08903344241254343

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' discussion of the pilot psychometric results. The knowledge and skills dimension's Cronbach's alpha was 0.56 (Table 3), below the recommended > 0.70 for a new instrument, and Rasch person reliability was 0.411.

> "Although the internal consistency of the entire instrument was at a good level, Cronbach’s alpha and Rasch analysis did not support the internal consistency of the knowledge and skills dimension alone."
