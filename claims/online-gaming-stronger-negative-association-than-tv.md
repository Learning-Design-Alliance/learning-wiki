Heterogeneity analysis using regression coefficient Z-tests comparing screen exposure types in the 175-student CEPS subsample. The article reports gaming effect sizes on mathematics (β = −0.068), English (β = −0.053), and total scores (β = −0.155), all P < 0.01.

> "Heterogeneity Z-test showed that the negative effect of online gaming time on academic performance was significantly stronger than that of TV watching time (Z = 4.217, P < 0.001)."
