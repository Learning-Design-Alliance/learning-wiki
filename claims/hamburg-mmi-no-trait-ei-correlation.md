## Evidence

### Knorr M 2018

Knorr M, Schwibbe A, Ehrhardt M, Lackamp J, Zimmermann S, Hampe W. (2018). Validity evidence for the Hamburg multiple mini-interview. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1208-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Study 1: online survey three months after admission of both admitted and rejected MMI candidates from the 2014 Hamburg cohort (n = 69 MMI participants per correlation table). Spearman rank correlation between MMI score and TEIQue-SF was "ρ = .07, p > .05", non-significant; the printed correlation carries no standardised effect-size interpretation for a detected effect.

> "There were no significant correlations between MMI results and the TEIQue-SF (ρ = .07, p > .05) or the STEM (ρ = .05, p > .05)."
