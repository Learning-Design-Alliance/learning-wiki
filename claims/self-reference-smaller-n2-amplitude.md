## Evidence

### Li 2024

Li, Z., Wang, J., Chen, Y., Li, Q., Yin, S. & Chen, A. (2024). Attenuated conflict self-referential information facilitating conflict resolution. npj Science of Learning 9, 47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00256-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Stimuli-locked N2 (200–250 ms, left central electrodes) ANOVA from the same 33-participant EEG Stroop experiment. The main effect of self-referential information was significant (F(1,32) = 7.16, p = 0.012, ηp² = 0.18); the congruency interaction was not significant (p = 0.252). The authors interpret the smaller N2 as preliminary identification of self-referential information consuming fewer top-down cognitive resources.

> "The main effect of self-referential information was signi ﬁcant, F(1, 32) = 7.16, p = 0.012, ηp 2 = 0.18, indicating that the N2 amplitude evoked by non-self-reference condition (0.76, SE = 0.57) was signiﬁcantly greater (more negative) than that evoked by self-reference condition (1.28, SE = 0.58)"
