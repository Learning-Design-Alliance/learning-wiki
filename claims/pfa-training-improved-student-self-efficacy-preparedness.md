## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mixed-methods study (Bahadır Yılmaz 2025, Turkey) of a face-to-face PFA training program based on the WHO guide for field workers, adapted and expanded over six 90-minute interactive sessions, in 62 final-year undergraduate nursing students (32 intervention, 30 control). The review reports "p < 0.001" and "p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Significant improvements in triage, incident management skills, knowledge, and confidence (p < 0.001), as well as in DRSES and preparedness compared with controls (p < 0.05), with enhanced role clarity and psychosocial response capacities."
