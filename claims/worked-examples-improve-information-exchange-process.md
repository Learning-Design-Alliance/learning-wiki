## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rexhäuser 2026

Rexhäuser, D., Radkowitsch, A., Richters, C., Glogger-Frey, I., Vogel-Blaschka, D., Meier, J., & Abele, S. (2026). Effects of specificity of self-explanation prompts for worked examples on computer-supported learning of collaborative diagnostic reasoning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09468-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA with time point as within-subjects factor and example condition as between-subjects factor in the pre-post experiment; the worked example group improved more than the problem-solving group on the "exchange of relevant information" process score, supporting H1.2. No effect size printed in the text provided.

> "As expected, the worked example group demonstrated a stronger improvement in the exchange of relevant information, as indicator for the quality of the CDR process, than the problem-solving group from pretest to posttest (significant interaction between time point and condition)."

### Rexhäuser 2026 (2)

Rexhäuser, D., Radkowitsch, A., Richters, C., Glogger-Frey, I., Vogel-Blaschka, D., Meier, J., & Abele, S. (2026). Effects of specificity of self-explanation prompts for worked examples on computer-supported learning of collaborative diagnostic reasoning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09468-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory RM-ANOVAs at the level of the four CDR process steps; dyads in the worked example condition exchanged more information in the initial-situation step, F (1,75) = 12.191, p <.001. The article also descriptively compared exchange of individual pieces of relevant information between groups.

> "Our exploratory analyses demonstrated that this interaction effect is owing to dyads in the worked example condition exchanging more information about the process steps of the initial situation, F (1,75) = 12.191, p <.001"
