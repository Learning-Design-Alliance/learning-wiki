Evidence¶

Vasilopoulos 2023¶

Vasilopoulos, F., Jeffrey, H., Wu, Y., & Dumontheil, I. (2023). Multi-Level Meta-Analysis of Physical Activity Interventions During Childhood: Effects of Physical Activity on Cognition and Academic Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09760-2

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level meta-analysis of creativity outcomes from five studies (14 effect sizes, 345 participants) in Table 4, showing a summary Hedges' g of "0.70" with 95% CI 0.20–1.20 and p < 0.01. Included interventions implemented dance and enriched activities; GRADE rating is low certainty.

Related Claims¶

  • Light and moderate physical activity and sedentary behavior show no significant relationship with any creativity component or overall creativity — related
  • Executive functions in childhood benefit from moderate-to-vigorous intensity and aerobic physical activity interventions — related
  • Highly creative physical activity interventions produce a large summary effect on creativity measures in children — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
