## Evidence

### Lena S. Geiger 2024

Lena S. Geiger, Torsten Wüstenberg, Zhenxiang Zang, Mirjam Melzer, Stephanie H. Witt, Marcella Rietschel, Markus M. Nöthen, Stefan Herms, Franziska Degenhardt, Andreas Meyer-Lindenberg & Carolin Moessnang. (2024). Longitudinal markers of cognitive procedural learning in fronto-striatal circuits and putative effects of a BDNF plasticity-related variant. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00282-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-level curve fitting of error rates across sessions s1-s5 of a modified Sternberg task in 23 healthy adults (11 Val, 12 Met carriers), comparing linear and exponential models via likelihood ratio tests. The authors report "asuperiority of the exponential model for all conditions" with strongest evidence in the practiced condition.

> "The comparison of models within genotype groups using likelihood ratio tests revealed asuperiority of the exponential model for all conditions, with strongest evidence in thepracticed condition in the Met group (see Table 2)."
