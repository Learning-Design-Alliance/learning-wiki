### Furin Molly MD 2016

Furin Molly MD, MS, Kohn Melissa MD, MS, Overberger Ryan DO, MS, Jaslow David MD, MPH. (2016). Out-of-Hospital Surgical Airway Management: Does Scope of Practice Equal Actual Practice? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.3.28729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional self-administered 22-question survey of 86 actively practicing ground paramedics at four Pennsylvania EMS agencies, analyzed with descriptive statistics. The survey found "Only 20% of subjects had attempted out-of-hospital surgical airway management (17/86; 95% CI [11–28%])" and that 76% of attempters had over ten years' experience. Of the 17 attempts, five were needle cricothyrotomies, ten used commercial devices, and two were open cricothyrotomies.

> "Only 20% of subjects had attempted out-of-hospital surgical airway management (17/86; 95% CI [11–28%]). Of these paramedics, 76% have greater than 10 years' experience in the field (13/17; 95% CI [53–90%])."
