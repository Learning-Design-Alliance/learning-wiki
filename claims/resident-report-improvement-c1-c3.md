### Atsukawa Natsuko 2025

Atsukawa Natsuko, Tatekawa Hiroyuki, Oura Tatsushi, Matsushita Shu, Horiuchi Daisuke, Takita Hirotaka, Mitsuyama Yasuhito, Omori Ayako, Shimono Taro, Miki Yukio, Ueda Daiju. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational evaluation of 900 reports (450 first term, 450 last term; 225 CT and 225 MRI each) from nine residents, scored by GPT-4o on six criteria. Mean revised rates fell from 61% to 46% (C1), 29% to 20% (C2), and 44% to 32% (C3); "revealed significant improvement in C1 (P < 0.001), C2 (P = 0.023), and C3 (P = 0.004)".

> "revealed significant improvement in C1 (P < 0.001), C2 (P = 0.023), and C3 (P = 0.004), while C4 (P = 0.05), C5 (P = 0.14), and C6 (P = 0.91) did not show significant changes"
