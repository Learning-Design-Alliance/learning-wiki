`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis (N = 186) found the Budget and Resources construct significant overall (p = .020), with librarians rating themselves lowest (M = 1.99) versus teachers (M = 2.48); "a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.019)". No effect size was printed.

> "post-hoc testing via pair-wise analysis identified a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.019) on the construct of Budget and Resources, but did not show a statistically significant differ -ence between the administrator and teacher roles (p =.757)"
