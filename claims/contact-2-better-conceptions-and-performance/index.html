Evidence¶

Biemans 1996¶

Biemans, H.J.A., Simons, P.RJ. (1996). Contact-2: a computer-assisted instructional strategy for promoting conceptual change. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00120487

q2 · i? · causal · r?

An experimental comparison in which 74 fifth- and sixth-graders were assigned to three instructional conditions (original CONTACT, revised CONTACT-2, control NO ACTIVATION) in a mixed between-within-subjects design with two between-subjects factors. The abstract reports "Students from the CONTACT-2 condition constructed better conceptions and achieved higher learning performance scores than students from the other two conditions", with no effect size printed.
