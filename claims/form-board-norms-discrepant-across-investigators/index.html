Wallin 1915¶

Wallin, J. E. W. (1915). Age Norms of Psycho-Motor Capacity. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://search.worldcat.org/search?q=Age+Norms+of+Psycho-Motor+Capacity

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of the author's norms with Goddard's and Sylvester's published norms (see Graph I). The author reports average differences of 4.4 sec and 5.6 sec and states that "wholly discrepant diagnoses will be made" depending on the norms used.

"The average difference between my norms and <3oddard's amounts to 4.4 sec. for ages 5 to 12, and between my norms and Sylvester's, 5.G sec. for ages 4 to 12. It is evident that wholly discrepant diagnoses will be made according as the individual is rated by Goddard's, Sylvester's or my norms."
