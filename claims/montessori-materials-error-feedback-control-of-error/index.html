Marshall 2026¶

Marshall, C., Livingston, L., & Van Herwegen, J. (2026). Mapping the Montessori Mathematics Curriculum to Dehaene's Four Pillars of Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00436-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The authors' illustrative mapping: the Spindle Box's fixed count of "exactly 55 spindles" lets children notice surplus or shortage themselves; the empty compartment for 0 draws attention to zero having no quantity.

"For example, the Spindle Box (see figure 1) has exactly 55 spindles. If children reach the end and there are not enough spindles, or some are left over, they notice this error, Attention is piqued, and repetition leads to correction."
