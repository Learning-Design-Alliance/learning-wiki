Secondary GPA analysis in the same observational study comparing quintile subgroups designated at 14 days versus 30 days; only the 30-day designation yielded a significant difference.

> "As secondary analysis, we calculated GPAs for the initially designated Ir﻿regular (3.41 ± 0.33) an﻿d Regular (3.60 ± 0.32) groups; the difference in these subgroups (n = 12 each) was not significant (p = 0.16). When Irregular and Regular groups were designated using the full 30-day sleep record, rather than 14 days, there was a significant difference in GPA (p < 0.02) between Irregular (3.42 ± 0.34) and Regular (3.72 ± 0.24) groups."
