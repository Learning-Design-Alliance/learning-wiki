# Healthcare educators' attitudes toward interprofessional socialisation increased significantly after an interprofessional simulation facilitator training programme

## Evidence

### Dolan 2026

Dolan, S., L. Nowell, N. Moules, A. Kaba, and M. Brockway. 2026. “Investigating Healthcare Educators' Interprofessional Socialisation Following an Interprofessional Simulation Facilitator Training Programme: A Mixed Methods Study.” Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 6: 6726–6749. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70267

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Secondary analysis of provincial simulation programme evaluation pre- and post-surveys from the PIAT day of SimSkills-Novice, analysed with a paired t-test with bootstrapping. The paired t-test indicated a significant increase of 3.53 points (t(84) = 6.80, p < 0.001; 95% CI [2.50, 4.56]) with a moderate effect size (d = 0.74, 95% CI [0.50, 0.98]).

> "The paired t-test indicated a significant increase of 3.53 points (t(84) = 6.80, p < 0.001; 95% CI [2.50, 4.56]) between mean pre- and post-test IS scores (n = 85) with a moderate effect size (d = 0.74, 95% CI [0.50, 0.98])"
