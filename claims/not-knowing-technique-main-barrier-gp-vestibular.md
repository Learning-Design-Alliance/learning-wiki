### van Vugt 2017

van Vugt, V. A., Diaz Nerio, P. M., van der Wouden, J. C., van der Horst, H. E., & Maarsingh, O. R. (2017). Use of canalith repositioning manoeuvres and vestibular rehabilitation: a GP survey. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care, 35(1), 19–26. [doi:10.1080/02813432.2017.1288683](https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2017.1288683)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis (Table 4) of reasons given by non-users among 426 responding GPs. The article reports "that they did not know how to perform the technique" as the top reason for all three treatments. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The most important reason for GPs not to use the Epley manoeuvre, Brandt-Daroff exercises and VR was that they did not know how to perform the technique (49.5, 89.6 and 92.4%)."
