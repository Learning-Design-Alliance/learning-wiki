## Evidence

### van der Sande 2023

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression on immediate post-tests: total duration coefficient b = 0.004 (SE = 0.002), significant for affirming motivations; b = 0.001 (SE = 0.001), not significant for comprehension. Number of sessions was not related to either outcome.

> "The effect of total duration was significant for affirming motivations but not for reading comprehension. Effects on affirming motivations were larger if the total duration of the intervention was longer."

### van der Sande 2023 (2)

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression on immediate post-tests: percentage of boys coefficient b = 0.023 (SE = 0.011), significant for affirming motivations; b = -0.008 (SE = 0.010), not significant for comprehension.

> "Effects on affirming reading motivations were larger if the share of boys in the sample was higher."
