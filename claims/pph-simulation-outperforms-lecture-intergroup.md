# The intervention group receiving lecture plus clinical simulation showed greater cognitive-performance improvement (67.8%) than the control group receiving lecture plus routine training (57.4%), a statistically significant intergroup difference

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intervention group (didactic lecture plus clinical simulation scenario) improved cognitive performance by 67.8% versus 57.4% in the control group (didactic lecture plus routine institutional training), with a statistically significant intergroup difference (p < 0.00). ## Evidence

### Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025

Coelho Tatiane da Silva, Fonseca Luciana Mara Monti, Cardoso Maria Vera Lúcia Moreira Leitão, Aquino Priscila de Souza, da Costa Camila Chaves, Maciel Nathanael de Souza, Damasceno Ana Kelve de Castro. (2025). Clinical simulation for nurses' knowledge on postpartum hemorrhage: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0214

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group comparison in the RCT (18 IG, 19 CG nurses). The intervention group showed "an improvement in cognitive performance of 67.8%" after lecture plus simulation.

> "For the IG, who were exposed to the didactic lecture and the clinical simulation scenario, an improvement in cognitive performance of 67.8% was observed."

### Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025 (2)

Coelho Tatiane da Silva, Fonseca Luciana Mara Monti, Cardoso Maria Vera Lúcia Moreira Leitão, Aquino Priscila de Souza, da Costa Camila Chaves, Maciel Nathanael de Souza, Damasceno Ana Kelve de Castro. (2025). Clinical simulation for nurses' knowledge on postpartum hemorrhage: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0214

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intergroup analysis (Wilcoxon) comparing control and intervention group scores before and after the intervention found a statistically significant difference (p < 0.00), which the authors read as simulation outperforming the traditional method.

> "In addition to the intergroup comparative analysis before and after the intervention, a statistically significant difference was observed (p < 0.00), rejecting the null hypothesis and indicating that clinical simulation has a significant effect on the knowledge of nursing professionals compared to the traditional method."

### Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025 (3)

Coelho Tatiane da Silva, Fonseca Luciana Mara Monti, Cardoso Maria Vera Lúcia Moreira Leitão, Aquino Priscila de Souza, da Costa Camila Chaves, Maciel Nathanael de Souza, Damasceno Ana Kelve de Castro. (2025). Clinical simulation for nurses' knowledge on postpartum hemorrhage: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0214

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control-group result in the same RCT: nurses receiving lecture plus routine institutional training showed a 57.4% improvement in cognitive performance, smaller than the intervention group's 67.8%.

> "The results of the nurses in the CG, who were exposed to the didactic lecture and the training typically offered by the institution, showed a 57.4% improvement in cognitive performance regarding knowledge of PPH management before and after the intervention."
