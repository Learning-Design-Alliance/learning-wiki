## Evidence

### Eglington 2022

Eglington, L.G., Pavlik, P.I. (2022). How to Optimize Student Learning Using Student Models That Adapt Rapidly to Individual Differences. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00296-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing student-level absolute error values across models within each of the six datasets. The article reports reduction "in all six of the datasets we evaluated" with ts > 6.57, ps < .0001; no effect size printed.

> "Adding PEV to PFAP (i.e., EPFA) or student -level proportion al correctness to R KCPFA reduced absolute value of error relative to RKCPFA or PFAP in all six of the datasets we evaluated, ts > 6.57, ps < .0001."
