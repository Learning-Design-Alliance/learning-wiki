## Evidence

### Noerholk LM 2022

Noerholk LM, Morcke AM, Kulasegaram K, Nørgaard LN, Harmsen L, Andreasen LA, Pedersen NG, Johnsson V, Vamadevan A, Tolsgaard MG. (2022). Does group size matter during collaborative skills learning? A randomised study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14791

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Minute-by-minute ICAP coding of recorded training sessions. Category 0 + 1 time rose from 16.4% in dyads to 29.5% in tetrads (F(3,90) = 13.75, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31); Tukey tests showed tetrads differed from all other groups and triads differed from dyads.

> "Time spent in the non-learning and passive learning activities (category 0 + 1) increased with group size (mean per cent [SD] singles: 20.7 [5.2], dyads: 16.4 [8.3], triads: 23.7 [8.2], tetrads: 29.5 [7.2]; F(3,90) = 13.75, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31)"
