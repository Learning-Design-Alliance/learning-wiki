### Nizamani 2026

Nizamani, S. B., Lee, Y., Donekal Chandrashekar, N., Ellis, M., & Ramakrishnan, N. (2026). Understanding Student Use of Large Language Models Across Computer Science Subfields. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01158 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of testing as a verification strategy among LLM users across the seven assignments. The article reports "Algorithms had the highest testing rate (87.8%), while HCI had the lowest (27.5%)" with a significant chi-square (χ 2 = 133.03, df = 6, p < 0.001); no effect size is printed.

> "Algorithms had the highest testing rate (87.8%), while HCI had the lowest (27.5%). Networking (61.0%), Software Engineering (65.6%), and Web Development (57.6%) showed moderate testing usage, whereas Machine Learning (30.6%) and HCI (27.5%) showed lower reliance on testing. This variation was statistically significant (χ 2 = 133.03, df = 6, p < 0.001)."

### Nizamani 2026 (2)

Nizamani, S. B., Lee, Y., Donekal Chandrashekar, N., Ellis, M., & Ramakrishnan, N. (2026). Understanding Student Use of Large Language Models Across Computer Science Subfields. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01158. [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of web search as a verification strategy among LLM users across the seven assignments. The article reports usage "ranging from 32.5% in Databases to 56.0% in HCI", with higher use in more conceptual or open-ended tasks; χ 2 = 22.79, df = 6, p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Web search usage also varied across assignments, ranging from 32.5% in Databases to 56.0% in HCI. Higher web search usage was observed in HCI (56.0%), Web Development (49.4%), and Machine Learning (47.1%), indicating greater reliance on external information sources in more conceptual or open-ended tasks. This variation was also statistically significant (χ 2 = 22.79, df = 6, p < 0.001)."
