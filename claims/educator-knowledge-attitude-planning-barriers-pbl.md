## Evidence

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding under the nurse-educator theme. Participants said many educators criticize PBL as time-consuming or inappropriate for nursing education and are unsure how to evaluate student learning in PBL settings, stifling creativity.

> "We lack a thorough understanding of PBL's concepts and advantages because we have had limited exposure to PBL during our education."

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025 (2)

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: educators sometimes jump into PBL without comprehending prerequisite procedures and factors, and inadequate planning also means needed materials or resources are not on hand, making implementation harder and slower.

> "Without enough planning, we struggle to match the problem scenarios with the learning objectives, which causes our students' learning experiences to be fragmented and unproductive."
