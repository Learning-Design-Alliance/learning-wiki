## Evidence

### Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer 2023

Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer. (2023). Strategies for remediating the impact of math anxiety on high school math performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00188-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM across second-semester quarters with grades as outcome and fixed factors of math anxiety, group, and quarter: "we again observe a significant interaction between math anxiety and intervention group" (χ2(1) = 6.74, p = 0.010), with no significant three-way interaction (χ2(1) = 0.03, p = 0.86); the article states these results "confirm that the effects observed in 3rd quarter extend to the 4th quarter".

> "Notably, for math grades, we again observe a signiﬁcant interaction between math anxiety and intervention group, χ2(1) = 6.74, p = 0.010, as previously discussed (Fig. 2). There was not a signiﬁcant interaction between intervention and quarter for math grades, χ2(1) = 3.75, p = 0.053. Finally, for math grades, there was no signiﬁcant three-way-interaction between math anxiety, intervention group, and quarter, χ2(1) = 0.03, p = 0.86."
