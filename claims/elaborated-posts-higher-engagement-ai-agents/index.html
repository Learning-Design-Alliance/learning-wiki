Kruskal-Wallis tests across short (<500), medium (500–2K), and long (>2K) post categories in the 28,683-post corpus showed "significant differences across length categories for both upvotes (H= 2,841.2,p < .001) and comments (H= 1,247.8,p < .001)"; long posts averaged 15.8 upvotes versus 3.0 for short posts.
