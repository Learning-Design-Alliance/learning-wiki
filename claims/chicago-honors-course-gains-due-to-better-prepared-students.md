## Evidence

### Shazia Rafiullah Miller 2002

Shazia Rafiullah Miller, Elaine M. Allensworth, Julie Reed Kochanek. (2002). Student Performance: Course Taking, Test Scores, and Outcomes — The State of Chicago Public High Schools: 1993 to 2000. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-performance-course-taking-test-scores-and-outcomes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Short-term cohort trend analysis of first-year course-taking among high school eligible Chicago students, adjusted for student characteristics including eighth-grade ITBS scores. The report found "only a small increase" after adjustment and concluded most growth resulted from incoming demographics, particularly higher ITBS scores.

> "Once we adjust for changes in student composition however, we see only a small increase in the percent of students passing an honors course, from 8.4 percent in 1993–94 to 9.6 in 1999–00 (see Figure 13)."
