# URM students who participated in PLTL showed a significant decrease in DFW rates, from nearly 40% to about 15%, in introductory biology

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among URM students in introductory biology, participation in PLTL was associated with a significant decrease in DFW rates from nearly 40% to about 15%. [→ Snyder JJ 2016](#snyder-jj-2016)

## Evidence

### Snyder JJ 2016

Snyder JJ, Sloane JD, Dunk RDP, Wiles JR. (2016). Peer-Led Team Learning Helps Minority Students Succeed. PLoS Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002398

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational chi-square analysis of course outcomes in a two-semester introductory biology sequence at Syracuse University, comparing URM students who participated in PLTL with those who did not. The article reports "a dramatic and significant decrease" in DFWs with χ² = 9.016, p = 0.003; no effect size is printed.

> "there was a dramatic and significant decrease—from nearly 40% down to about 15%—in the number of students earning Ds, Fs, or Withdrawing from the course (DFWs) among URMs who participated in PLTL (x 2 = 9.016, n = 90, df = 1, p = 0.003)"
