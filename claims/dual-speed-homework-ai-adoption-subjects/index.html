Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal 2026¶

Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal. (2026). Navigating AI in STEM: what secondary students actually do with generative AI-driven tools. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00637-8

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Question D1 analysis (Fig. 5) of subject-specific homework AI use in the n = 416 sample. Printed counts: natural sciences n = 204 never (peaking in years 3 and 4 at n = 67 and n = 56), mathematics n = 158 never, computer science n = 54 very often, economics n = 155 in categories 4 and 5.

"A Kruskal-Wallis H test confirms that the frequency of these tools’ integration is significantly subject-dependent (H = 88.83, p < .001), reflecting a dual-speed adoption pattern where applied disciplines accommodate AI as a productivity tool while procedural disciplines maintain traditional boundaries."
