Liane Kaufmann, Michael von Aster, Silke M. Göbel, Josef Marksteiner, and Elise Klein. (2020). Developmental Dyscalculia in Adults: Current Issues and Open Questions for Future Research. Lernen und Lernstörungen, 9(2), 126–137. https://doi.org/10.1024/2235-0977/a000294

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting prevalence and persistence figures from prior studies. The review states DD affects "5 – 7 % of the general population" and that affected children do not grow out of it, with arithmetic difficulties persisting into adulthood if untreated.

> "The prevalence of DD is rather high, affecting 5 – 7 % of the general population (Gross-Tsur, Manor & Shalev, 1996; Rubisten & Henik, 2009; Schulz et al., 2018). Notably, affected children do not ‘grow out’ of DD. Rather, arithmetic difficulties tend to persist into adulthood if untreated"
