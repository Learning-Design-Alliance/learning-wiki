## Evidence

### Jenny Mirjam Annina 2018

Jenny Mirjam Annina, Keller Niklas, Gigerenzer Gerd. (2018). Assessing minimal medical statistical literacy using the Quick Risk Test: a prospective observational study in Germany. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020847

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational administration of the Quick Risk Test at the start of a CME workshop to 16 senior educators, who received no training by the authors; the article reports "only 81% could identify the correct definition of sensitivity, and only 63% the correct definition of specificity."

> "Senior educators answered on average three-quarters of the questions correctly (median=75%). Among the senior educators, the median percentage correct across all 10 questions was 75% (IQR=62.5%–81.2%)."
