## Evidence

### Benjamin P. Koester 2016

Benjamin P. Koester, Galina Grom, and Timothy A. McKay. (2016). Patterns of Gendered Performance Difference in Introductory STEM Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of the lab courses associated with the same STEM subjects as the gateway lectures. The article reports lab grade anomalies averaging 0.12 letter grades (a small bonus, ranging -0.16 to 0.35) and GPDs with "a mean of 0.02, ranging from -0.1 to 0.06"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "More strikingly, their gendered performance diﬀerences are both small and various, with a mean of 0.02, ranging from -0.1 to 0.06."
