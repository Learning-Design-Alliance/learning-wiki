Ruiz-Martín Héctor, Blanco Fernando, Ferrero Marta. (2024). Which learning techniques supported by cognitive research do students use at secondary school? `q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Between-subjects t-tests comparing students answering whether they had been taught how to learn, within the n = 2,414 analytic sample. The article reports “Cohen’s d = 0.258” for spaced practice and d = −0.345 for grades.

> "students who reported having been taught how to study were more likely to apply spaced practice than those who did not (Cohen’s d = 0.258; corrected P-value < 0.001). However, their average grades were also lower (d = − 0.345; corrected P-value < 0.001)."
