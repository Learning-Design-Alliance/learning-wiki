## Evidence

### Meredydd Williams 2019

Meredydd Williams, Jason R C Nurse, Sadie Creese. (2019). Smartwatch Games: Encouraging Privacy-Protective Behaviour in a Longitudinal Study. [doi:10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-gameplay evaluation questionnaire (Table IX) completed by all 10 participants; on strong agreement that the app was educational, "80% of control participants were in strong agreement" versus 20% of treatment. The authors attribute this to the narrower privacy content and harder tasks.

> "Whereas 80% of control participants were in strong agreement, this was matched by 20% of the treatment group."
