### Collins Jack Charles 2017

Collins Jack Charles, Schneider Carl Richard, Naughtin Clare Louise, Wilson Frances, de Almeida Neto Abilio Cesar, Moles Rebekah Jane. (2017). Mystery shopping and coaching as a form of audit and feedback to improve community pharmacy management of non-prescription medicine requests: an intervention study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019462

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of questioning scores from the same 521 mystery shopping visits, scored with the WHAT-STOP-GO-based datasheet (maximum 18 points, converted to percentages). "questioning scores were found to improve over time" (rs=0.204; P<0.001); overall median questioning score was 44% (IQR=22%–75%).
