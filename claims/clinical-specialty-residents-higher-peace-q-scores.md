## Evidence

### Silva João Paulo Aureliano 2025

Silva João Paulo Aureliano, Soares Aleida Nazareth, Pagano Adriana Silvina, Savoi Cristiana Guimarães Paes, Silva Alexandre Ernesto, Moura Alexandre Sampaio. (2025). Translation, cross-cultural adaptation, and validation of the Palliative Care Knowledge Questionnaire for PEACE (PEACE-Q) in Brazilian Portuguese. Einstein. https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1243

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison among the 63 test-retest residents, classified as clinical, surgical, or mixed based on frequency of operating room involvement, and compared with Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney tests. The article reports "[p=0.013]" with the medians quoted; no effect size is printed.

> "The overall performance of residents in clinical specialties was higher than that of those in surgical or mixed specialties (median scores [IQR]: 27.0 [25.0-28.0], 26.0 [23.0-28.0], and 23.5 [21.5-25.5], respectively; [p=0.013])."
