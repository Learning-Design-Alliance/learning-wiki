Bo Yuan and Jiazi Hu. (2025). Bridging MOOCs, Smart Teaching, and AI-Assisted Learning: A Unified Quantitative Model. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.14266](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.14266)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Simulation of a single class (N=100, d=10) under the unified model with equal per-component budgets. "the class mean mastery increases from 0.498" across four successive states, showing cumulative progression through the three stages.

> "the class mean mastery increases from 0.498 initially to 0.666 after MOOC learning, 0.775 after ST, and 0.851 after AI-assisted learning"

### Bo Yuan and Jiazi Hu 2025 (2)

Bo Yuan and Jiazi Hu. (2025). Bridging MOOCs, Smart Teaching, and AI-Assisted Learning: A Unified Quantitative Model. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.14266](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.14266)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Robustness analysis repeating the simulation for 100 independently generated classes (Table I). "The class mean mastery increases consistently" with small standard deviations and narrow 95% confidence intervals, indicating stable progression.

> "The class mean mastery increases consistently across the four states, from 0.485 at the initial state to 0.655 after MOOC learning, 0.767 after ST, and 0.844 after AI-assisted learning"
