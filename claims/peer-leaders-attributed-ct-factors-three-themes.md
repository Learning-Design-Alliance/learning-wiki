Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative coding of the open-ended critical thinking questionnaire given to peer leaders at post-test. "interacting with others, utilizing different approaches to learning, and problem solving" were the recurring perceived influences; one participant also cited having to elicit discussion in the PLTL group.

> "three common themes emerged from the participants’ responses: interacting with others, utilizing different approaches to learning, and problem solving."
