## Evidence

### Lu Chen 2025

Lu Chen, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Wu Chengcheng, Yang Jin. (2025). Effectiveness of video-based peer feedback in self-training for skill acquisition among surgical residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08029-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 33 first-year surgical residents (VPF n = 17, VEF n = 16) with OSCE pre- and post-training scores; within-group improvements on wound dressing change, catheterization, and simple interrupted suture each reached "significant improvement compared to their pre-training levels (P < 0.05)" (Table 2 within-group P values all < 0.01).

> "However, post-training scores for all tasks in both groups demonstrated significant improvement compared to their pre-training levels (P < 0.05)."
