### Joan Étude Arrow 2023

Joan Étude Arrow, Sara E. Marsh, Josephine C. Meyer. (2023). A Holistic Approach to Quantum Ethics Education. [doi:10.1109/qce57702.2023.20332](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce57702.2023.20332)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anecdotal evaluation of QEP's debut March 2023 workshop at Perimeter Institute for PSI Master's students, using an anonymous end-of-class Mentimeter poll. The authors state the data was "purely anecdotal" but responses "reﬂected thoughtf ul engagement with the material" and named takeaways such as evaluating hype and identifying minority voices.

> "we collected anonymous data on student response s to a Mentimeter poll at the end of class. While the data collecte d was purely anecdotal, student responses reﬂected thoughtf ul engagement with the material and identiﬁed tangible take- aways they can carry over into their work"
