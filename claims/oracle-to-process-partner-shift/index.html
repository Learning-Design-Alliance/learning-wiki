shift-1¶

Weihao Qu, Ling Zheng, Chris Buzaid, and Daniel Crawford. 2026. LearnAI: Just-in-Time AI Co-Creation Across Disciplines at a University. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19164 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.19164

q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with five purposively sampled Layer 2 clients (all at least four months post-first-session) and two of four tutors. Interviewees described the shift in their own words, e.g. a CS professor who came to feel AI is "more like a collaborator." The article notes the shift claim rests primarily on interview accounts, tutor accounts, artifacts, and boundary cases.

"Before sessions, several clients framed AI in passive, oracle-like terms. After sessions, they described AI as a collaborative tool requiring human direction."
