## Evidence

### Mikkelsen N 2025

Mikkelsen N, Larsen EB, Arnfred SM. (2025). Authentic Versus Simulated Patient Videos: Effect on Mental Status Examination—An Educational Randomized Controlled Trial. Academic Psychiatry 49(3):222–230. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-024-02111-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student feedback analysis (simple ANOVAs) on self-reported hours spent on the e-modules in the cluster-RCT; the Full group's mean was over 50% higher. Self-reported time is susceptible to bias, as the article notes; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean time spent by the Limited group was 3.3 h (SD 2.1), while the Full group spent 5.1 h (SD 3.9) (p = 0.001)"
