National Academies of Sciences 2018¶

National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. (2018). How People Learn II: Learners, Contexts, and Cultures. Consensus Study Report. https://www.nationalacademies.org/publications/24783 doi:10.17226/24783

q1 · i? · review · r?

The project's statement of task, as printed on the activity page, defines the study as an update and extension of the 2000 report, covering "learning from birth through adulthood in both formal and informal settings." No empirical findings are reported on this page.

"An ad hoc committee will conduct a study and prepare a report that will update and extend the NRC report How People Learn (NRC, 2000) by reviewing and synthesizing research that has emerged across the various disciplines that focus on the study of learning from birth through adulthood in both formal and informal settings."
