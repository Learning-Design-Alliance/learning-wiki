## Evidence

### Rautiainen 2026

Rautiainen, J., Aro, T., Aro, M., Tolvanen, A., & Koponen, T. (2026). Hierarchical Nature of Arithmetic Skills: Similarities and Differences Between Arithmetic Operations. Journal of Educational Psychology, Early online. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001066

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baseline latent SEM model fitted to the longitudinal sample of 207 children, with predictors measured in fall of second grade and multiplication fluency in spring of third grade. The article reports that "numerical abilities strongly predicted, and serial retrieval fluency moderately predicted third grade multiplication skill"; the model explained R2 = .35 (R2 = .42 before residualization).

> "In the baseline observations, of the three latent structures, numerical abilities strongly predicted, and serial retrieval fluency moderately predicted third grade multiplication skill. Working memory appeared not to have significant associations with multiplication fluency on the baseline model."
