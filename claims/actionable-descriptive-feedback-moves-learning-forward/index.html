Evidence¶

Barbara Jones 2024¶

Barbara Jones, Molly Faulkner-Bond, and Jennifer Blitz. (2024). Accelerate English Learner Success: Students Take Ownership Over Their Learning With Formative Assessment. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673602

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The brief asserts, citing Black and Wiliam (1998), that actionable and descriptive feedback moves student learning forward; it offers no study design or effect size of its own for this assertion. The feedback should relate to the intended learning as expressed in the success criteria and also honor individual students' sensemaking.

"Overall, actionable and descriptive feedback to students is a powerful response that research has shown to move student learning forward."

Barbara Jones 2024 (2)¶

Barbara Jones, Molly Faulkner-Bond, and Jennifer Blitz. (2024). Accelerate English Learner Success: Students Take Ownership Over Their Learning With Formative Assessment. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673602

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Table 1 of the brief contrasts effective and less-effective feedback qualities, including that effective feedback "Provides hints or clues but lets the student make the decisions about their learning" while less-effective feedback tells students their next steps and is of a too-large grain size. This is a descriptive framework, not a tested comparison.
