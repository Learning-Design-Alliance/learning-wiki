## Subclaims
`q3 i0` A two-way repeated measures ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of modality on illustration scores, with 2D and prosection superior to VR, and no modality-by-order interaction. [→ Cheung H. C. Kristy 2026](#cheung-h-c-kristy-2026)

## Evidence

### Cheung H. C. Kristy 2026

Cheung H. C. Kristy, Zhong Lily Shengjia, Wall Shelley L., Lisk Kristina. (2026). Exploring the impact of virtual reality anatomy training on preparing biomedical illustrators for drawing anatomical structures. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70150

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA (within-subject modality, between-subject group) on 51 illustrations scored by two experts from 17 participants; mean scores were 2D 16.47, VR 14.24, prosection 16.35. The "main effect of modalities was found" with η 2 G = 0.16 and no interaction.

> "A main effect of modalities was found, F(1.58, 23.76) = 7.33, p = 0.006, η 2 G = 0.16, with no interactions between modalities and their order of use."
