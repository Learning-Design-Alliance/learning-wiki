Six-year completion tracking of CPS graduating classes of 2008–2014 (Figures 9–11). For the class of 2014: 51.3% of immediate four-year enrollees earned bachelor's degrees; 7.6% of immediate two-year enrollees earned bachelor's degrees plus 19.3% associate/certificate; only 2.4% of delayed/non-enrollees earned bachelor's degrees.

> "Among high school graduates who immediately enrolled in a two-year college, completion rates have remained relatively steady since 2008."
