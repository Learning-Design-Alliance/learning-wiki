## Evidence

### Dallı Öznur Erbay 2025

Dallı Öznur Erbay (2025). Spiritual Care Practices and Competence Among Critical Care Nurses in Türkiye: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. Journal of Religion and Health, 64(5), 4074–4087. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02353-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling with maximum likelihood estimation on the 323-nurse cross-sectional survey data, with the three SCCS subdimensions as independent variables and NSCTS as the dependent variable. All three paths "significantly predicted NSCTS scores"; the strongest standardized effect was β = 0.393 for PSCPC (p = 0.001).

> "Path analysis revealed that AISC (β = 0.171, p = 0.006), PSCPC (β = 0.393, p = 0.001), and ATPSC (β = 0.232, p = 0.026) significantly predicted NSCTS scores (Table 4)."

### Dallı Öznur Erbay 2025 (2)

Dallı Öznur Erbay (2025). Spiritual Care Practices and Competence Among Critical Care Nurses in Türkiye: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. Journal of Religion and Health, 64(5), 4074–4087. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02353-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM analysis of the 323-nurse cross-sectional survey. The article reports the three competence subdimensions "collectively explained 54.5% of the variance in spiritual care practices (R2 = 0.545)"; no standardized effect size beyond R2 is printed for this omnibus result.

> "The independent variables collectively explained 54.5% of the variance in spiritual care practices (R2 = 0.545)."
