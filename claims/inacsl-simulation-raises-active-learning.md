## Evidence

### Tu Lewei 2026

Tu Lewei, Wu Huijie, Xia Xinyi, Zhu Xuehua, Mei Binbin, Zhang Yan, Xu Mengyan, Yang Yeqin. (2026). Effects of standards of best practice in simulation-based training among midwifery students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08081-y

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Comparison on the active learning dimension (10 items) of the Educational Practices in Simulation Scale in the same quasi-experimental historical-control study of 129 midwifery students. The intervention group scored "significantly superior" on active learning, with a printed mean difference of 0.130 and Cohen's d = -0.490 (Table 5), P = 0.006.

> "The intervention group achieved a significantly superior active learning score on the Educational Practices in Simulation Scale (4.18 ± 0.23) than the control group (4.05 ± 0.29), with a mean difference of 0.130 (95% CI: −0.222 to −0.374, P = 0.006)."
