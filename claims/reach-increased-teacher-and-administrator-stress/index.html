Sporte 2016¶

Sporte, S.E., & Jiang, J.Y. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year 3 Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Summary section reporting Year 3 survey results from the MVMS teacher survey and Consortium Administrator Survey on self-reported stress and anxiety attributable to the evaluation process.

"Slightly fewer than four out of every five teachers agreed REACH has increased their levels of stress and anxiety and more than two out of three of administrators agreed that REACH had increased their own levels of stress."

Sporte 2016 (2)¶

Sporte, S.E., & Jiang, J.Y. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year 3 Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Year 3 survey findings (Figure 7) on perceived cost-benefit of the evaluation process for teachers and administrators in CPS.

"over half of teachers and over 40 percent of administrators agreed or strongly agreed the evaluation process takes more effort than the results are worth"

Jiang 2014¶

Jiang, J. (2014). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year Two Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-evaluation-practice-year-2-teacher-and-administrator-perceptions-reach

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Teacher and principal survey responses in the year-two REACH brief. "The large majority of teachers (79 percent)" reported increased stress and anxiety, with principals concurring at a similar proportion; no effect size is printed.

"The large majority of teachers (79 percent) reported that the evaluation process had increased their levels of stress and anxiety. A similar proportion of principals agreed teachers felt more stress as a result of the new system"
