# Group cohesion and interactivity parameters (clustering coefficient, density, average degree) correlate positively with performance, while group size correlates negatively

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Group-level network parameters measuring cohesion and interactivity (average clustering coefficient, density, average degree, number of interactions) were consistently moderate to strongly and positively correlated with grades, and group size was negatively correlated (overall r = -0.57). [→ Saqr M 2018](#saqr-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Saqr M 2018

Saqr M, Fors U, Nouri J. (2018). Using social network analysis to understand online Problem-Based Learning and predict performance. PLoS ONE 13(9): e0203590. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203590

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

RQ1 Pearson correlation tests of group network parameters against grades (Table 3) in 20 BPBL groups across four courses (215 students). The article reports cohesion and interactivity measures "consistently moderate to strongly correlated with performance" and group size negatively correlated with overall r = -0.57, p < 0.01.

> "Average group clustering coefficient (which measures group cohesion) as well as density (which measures group interactivity), followed by the measures of quantity of interactions (average degree, number of interactions and number of S-S interactions), were consistently moderate to strongly correlated with performance consistently in individual courses and in relation to the overall results."
