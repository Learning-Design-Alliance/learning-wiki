## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2020

Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Suzanne White Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Charlotte Zimmerman, Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). Toward a valid instrument for measuring physics quantitative literacy. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.smith_t](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.smith_t)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

CFA on v2.1 and v2.2 with a unidimensional model shows adequate fit; MMA with LANS sparsification on the final two versions found no discernible substructure, corroborating EFA and CFA conclusions of unidimensionality.

> "Speciﬁcally, the CFI and TLI were above 0.93 for both versions under CFA using a unidimensional model. Additionally, the standardized root mean square of the residuals was below 0.04, and the root mean square of the error of approximation was below 0.04"
