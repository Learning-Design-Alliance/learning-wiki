# Student-student interaction does not directly affect active learning but works indirectly through emotional engagement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The direct path from student-student interaction to active learning was not supported (β = 0.1179, not significant). [→ Molinillo 2018](#molinillo-2018)
`q2 i?` Student-student interaction positively affects emotional engagement (β = 0.2737, p < 0.001), which mediates its influence on active learning. [→ Molinillo 2018 (2)](#molinillo-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Molinillo 2018

Molinillo, S., Aguilar-Illescas, R., Anaya-Sánchez, R., & Vallespín-Arán, M. (2018). Exploring the impacts of interactions, social presence and emotional engagement on active collaborative learning in a social web-based environment. Computers & Education, 123, 41-52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.04.012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM hypothesis test; the direct student-student interaction path to active learning was rejected ("Stud. - stud. interaction -> active learning 0.1179", t = 1.9459, not significant).

> "H2. Stud. - stud. interaction -> active learning 0.1179 1.9459 --- No"

### Molinillo 2018 (2)

Molinillo, S., Aguilar-Illescas, R., Anaya-Sánchez, R., & Vallespín-Arán, M. (2018). Exploring the impacts of interactions, social presence and emotional engagement on active collaborative learning in a social web-based environment. Computers & Education, 123, 41-52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.04.012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`
