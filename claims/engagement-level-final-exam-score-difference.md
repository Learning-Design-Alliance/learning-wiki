# Higher aiPlato engagement is associated with higher final exam scores in one-way ANOVA and Tukey post hoc comparisons

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean final exam scores differed across low, medium, and high aiPlato engagement groups (statistically significant omnibus one-way ANOVA). [→ Atharva Dange 2026](#atharva-dange-2026)
`q2 i?` Post hoc Tukey HSD tests revealed a statistically significant difference between the low- and high-engagement groups on final exam scores. [→ Atharva Dange 2026 (2)](#atharva-dange-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Atharva Dange 2026

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

One-way ANOVA on final exam scores with students binned into low, medium, and high engagement by average attempt rate across the four aiPlato extra-credit assignments. The article reports the omnibus test as significant, "indicating that at least one engagement group differed in mean final exam score."

> "The ANOVA yielded a statistically significant result (p <0.05), indicating that at least one engagement group differed in mean final exam score."

### Atharva Dange 2026 (2)

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Post hoc Tukey HSD tests compared all possible pairs of engagement-group means on the cumulative final exam. The low-versus-high contrast was significant, "indicating that higher levels of engagement with aiPlato were associated with higher final exam performance."

> "Post hoc analysis revealed a statistically significant difference between the low- and high-engagement groups, indicating that higher levels of engagement with aiPlato were associated with higher final exam performance."
