`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cost and processing-time analysis for 1,000 lesson completions based on OpenAI API pricing as of Sept. 1, 2024 and token counts (Table 6, Figure 6). The article contrasts this with human grading at 15 seconds per response, 16.7 hours, and $500 at $30 per hour for a skilled human grader.

> "Using GPT-4o (few-shot), assessing the performance of 1,000 tutors completing the lesson would cost $8.85 and take 3.5 hours."
