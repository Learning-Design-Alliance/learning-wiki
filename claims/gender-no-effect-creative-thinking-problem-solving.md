## Evidence

### Robabeh Puran 2017

Robabeh Puran, Mohamad Hasan Behzadi, Ahmad Shahvarani, Farhad Hosseinzadeh Lotfi. (2017). The Effects of Training and Other Factors on Problem Solving in Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(3): 448-460. https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.3.448

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test (Hypothesis 1) on creative thinking scores of 450 second-grade high school students pre-tested in Kashan; Levene's test p = 0.914 accepted variance equality, t = 0.204, df = 449, p = 0.838, mean difference 0.2645. The article reports "the mean difference ... was insignificant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The p-value (0.838) of the t-test for means equity was greater than 0.05, thus the mean difference between the creative thinking scores of female and male students was insignificant."
