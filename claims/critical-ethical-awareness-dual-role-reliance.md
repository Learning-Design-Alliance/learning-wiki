# Critical-ethical awareness plays a dual role: it negatively predicts general GenAI reliance but positively predicts shortcut reliance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher critical-ethical awareness is associated with lower general GenAI reliance (β = -.11). [→ Amzalag M 2026](#amzalag-m-2026)
`q2 i?` Higher critical-ethical awareness is associated with greater use of GenAI as a cognitive shortcut (β = .15), in contrast to its negative association with general reliance. [→ Amzalag M 2026 (2)](#amzalag-m-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Amzalag M 2026

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression predicting general GenAI reliance (N = 817; R2 = .411). Use frequency added ΔR2 = .217 and perceived usefulness was the strongest positive predictor (β = .47). The article reports critical-ethical awareness "negatively predicted reliance (β = -.11, p < .001)".

> "GenAI critical-ethical awareness negatively predicted reliance (β = -.11, p < .001), indicating that learners with higher critical awareness tend to rely less heavily on GenAI tools."

### Amzalag M 2026 (2)

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression predicting GenAI shortcut reliance (N = 819; R2 = .078), with literacy dimensions adding ΔR2 = .031. The article notes this positive coefficient stands "in contrast to GenAI reliance".

> "in contrast to GenAI reliance, GenAI critical-ethical awareness positively predicted the use of GenAI as a cognitive shortcut in the final model (β = .15, p < .001)."
