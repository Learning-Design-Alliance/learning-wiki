## Evidence

### Sidiq Mohammad et al 2025

Sidiq Mohammad et al. (2025). Screen time exposure and academic performance, anxiety, and behavioral problems among school children. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19409

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the 537-child cross-sectional survey using the Spence Children Anxiety Scale (SCAS-Child). The printed effect size is "r = 0.526, p < 0.001" for the SCAS total. Mean SCAS total was 46.1 ± 14.7 with panic agoraphobia the highest subscale (9.1 ± 3.3).

> "Screen time was also positively correlated with the SCAS total score for anxiety (r = 0.526, p < 0.001) and all its subscales."
