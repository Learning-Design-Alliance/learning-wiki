Evidence¶

Björn B. de Koning 2017¶

Björn B. de Koning, Lisanne T. Bos, Stephanie I. Wassenburg & Menno van der Schoot. (2017). Effects of a Reading Strategy Training Aimed at Improving Mental Simulation in Primary School Children. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 869–889. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9380-4

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Reading motivation was measured with a one-item 4-point Likert scale before and after training. The ANOVA showed "a significant training group × time interaction" with F(1,94) = 15.46, p < 0.001, ηp² = 0.14; the training group's time effect was significant (F(1,45) = 36.04, ηp² = 0.43) but the control group's was not (F(1,47) = 0.02, p = 0.89).

"An ANOV A (with the above factors) on the motivation data confirms this in a significant training group × time interaction ( F(1,94) = 15.46, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.14)"
