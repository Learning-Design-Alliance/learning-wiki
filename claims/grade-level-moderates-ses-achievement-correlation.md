## Evidence

### Sirin 2005

Sirin, S. R. (2005). Socioeconomic Status and Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analytic Review of Research. Review of Educational Research, 75(3), 417-453. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543075003417

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis over 71 correlations (64 student-level plus 7 longitudinal); Qb(3, 70) = 162.23, p < .001. The printed effect sizes were mean correlations of r = .19, r = .27, r = .31, and r = .26 across grade levels; all pairwise comparisons significant at p < .001 except elementary vs. high school.

> "the mean ES was .19 for the kindergarten students, .27 for the elementary school students, .31 for middle school students, and .26 for high school students."
