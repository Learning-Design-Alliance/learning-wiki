Serhat Aydın 2016¶

Serhat Aydın. (2016). Using Excel in Teacher Education for Sustainability. Journal of Teacher Education for Sustainability, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1515/jtes-2016-0017

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pretest t-tests for independent samples compared Rasch mean SATS-36 scores of the randomly assigned control and experiment groups before instruction; the article reports no significant difference on the six factors. Equivalence was not tested beyond these comparisons.

"The pretest scores show that there are no significant difference between control and experiment groups in terms of the six factors of attitudes towards mathematics."
