## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a structured training curriculum, scores for each of the three laparoscopic tasks and the total score rose significantly among all study participants in a single-group pre-post study (P < .001). ## Evidence

### Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD 2016

Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD. (2016). Students' Interest in Surgery Affects Laparoscopic Practicing Performance. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2016.00039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post study of 36 clerks and 14 interns at TMU-Wan-Fang Medical Center using a laparoscopic trainer box. The article reports "significant improvement in each task and in the total score", with total scores rising from 199 (192) to 589 (315), P < .001.

> "All medical students demonstrated significant improvement in each task and in the total score (sum of scores of all 3 tasks; Table 1). The total BM and PTM scores were 199 (192) and 589 (315), respectively (P < .001)."
