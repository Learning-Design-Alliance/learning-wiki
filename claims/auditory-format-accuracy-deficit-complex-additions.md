# Complex additions are more accurate in visual than auditory format, with the auditory deficit shrinking with age

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The format effect on CRs was modulated by age-group: accuracy improved with age for auditory but not visual additions. [→ Van Rinsveld A 2015](#van-rinsveld-a-2015)

## Evidence

### Van Rinsveld A 2015

Van Rinsveld A, Brunner M, Landerl K, Schiltz C and Ugen S (2015) The relation between language and arithmetic in bilinguals: insights from different stages of language acquisition. Front. Psychol. 6:265. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00265

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The format × age-group interaction on complex-addition CRs (η² = 0.153) showed accuracy gains with age specifically for auditory-presented additions; this interaction remained significant after standardization.

> "the format eﬀect was modulated by age-group [format × age-group interaction: F(4, 185) = 8. 365; p < 0. 001; η 2 = 0. 153]. Decomposition of this interaction showed that participants became more accurate with age for auditory presente d additions"
