`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Robbins and Kegley (2010), a study of 51 students in a Principles of Management course or a Creative Inquiry free elective course, with data collected using the Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking pre- and post-testing. "Results revealed a significant increase in the participants' creative self-efficacy" in the Creative Inquiry course.
