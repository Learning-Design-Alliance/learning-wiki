Sebring 2006¶

Sebring, P. B., Allensworth, E., Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., & Luppescu, S. (2006). The Essential Supports for School Improvement. Research Report, Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/essential-supports-school-improvement

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Community-context analysis of Chicago elementary schools' 1990-1996 achievement trends, contrasting improvement and stagnation rates across community racial composition and income types. The report also found disproportionately more improving schools among predominantly Latino, racially diverse, and racially integrated schools.
