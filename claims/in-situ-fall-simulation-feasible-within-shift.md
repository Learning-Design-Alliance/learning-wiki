## Evidence

### Acosta DJ 2021

Acosta DJ, Rinfret A, Plant J, Hsu AT. (2021). Using Patient Simulation to Promote Best Practices in Fall Prevention and Postfall Assessment in Nursing Homes. Journal of Nursing Care Quality. https://doi.org/10.1097/NCQ.0000000000000599

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Duration data from 16 of 27 completed simulations (59.3%) conducted July–December 2019 across day, evening, and night shifts. The article reports "16.3 ± 3.7 minutes"; three additional simulations were excluded because of resident emergencies.

> "The average length of time of the simulation exercise, including both the simulation and the debrief discussion, was 16.3 ± 3.7 minutes and ranged between 10.0 and 23.3 minutes."
