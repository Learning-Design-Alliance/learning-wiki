# Skilled EFL writers are aware that coherence and continuity are required elements of text and deliberately fulfill coherence requirements when composing and revising.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe 2001

Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe. (2001). Investigating the Metacognitive Awareness and Strategies of English-Majored University Student Writers. ERIC Document ED465281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED465281

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview transcription analysis in the declarative-knowledge section of the nine-writer study. Subjects described paragraphs as related rather than independent and systematic arrangement around a main idea; the article adds they could not clearly define coherence, which Johns acknowledges is hard to teach and learn.

> "The analysis of transcriptions reveals that all the subjects in this study were aware of the important role coherence and continuity play in writing."
