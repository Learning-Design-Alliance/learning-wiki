### Rogers Kaliisa 2023

Rogers Kaliisa, Kamila Misiejuk, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Mohammad Khalil, Mohammed Saqr. (2023). Have Learning Analytics Dashboards Lived Up to the Hype? A Systematic Review of Impact on Students' Achievement, Motivation, Participation and Attitude. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://laka.ub.uni-siegen.de/index.php/laka/article/view/23

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review-level critique of studies reporting medium achievement effects via user/non-user comparisons. The review states such designs "primarily compared the activity levels of the dashboard i.e., users vs. non-users", which are neither randomised nor a true intervention-control contrast.

> "their designs primarily compared the activity levels of the dashboard i.e., users vs. non-users, which is neither randomised nor equal to any form of intervention vs. control"
