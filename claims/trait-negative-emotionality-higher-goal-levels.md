Correlational analysis in Study 1 between trait questionnaires (CESD, TAI, BFI:NE) and the baseline goal parameter of the best-fitting model. The article reports small positive correlations (r = .12, r = .13, r = .11, ps < 0.05) and links this pattern to prior work on depression and high average goal levels.

> "We also found that those who reported higher levels of depression, anxiety, and negative emotionality were more likely to set higher levels of goals (rs = .12, .13, .11, ps < 0.05)."
