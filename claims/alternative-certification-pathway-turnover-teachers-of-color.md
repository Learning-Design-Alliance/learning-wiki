## Evidence

### Carver-Thomas 2018

Carver-Thomas, D. (2018). Diversifying the teaching profession: How to recruit and retain teachers of color. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/diversifying-teaching-profession

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive analysis reported in the retention and preparation sections, citing Title II Higher Education Act state data showing candidates of color were "44% more likely to enroll in an alternative certification program in 2014–15 than in 2008–09."

> "In 2013, a quarter of all new teachers of color entered teaching through an alternative certification pathway. That is double the share of White teachers who entered through an alternative certification pathway."

### Carver-Thomas 2018 (2)

Carver-Thomas, D. (2018). Diversifying the teaching profession: How to recruit and retain teachers of color. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/diversifying-teaching-profession

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this controlled finding in the retention section, alongside evidence that teachers with the least comprehensive preparation are two to three times more likely to leave than the most prepared candidates.

> "Recent evidence also shows that alternatively certified teachers are 25% more likely to turn over than their traditionally certified counterparts, even after controlling for key student, teacher, and school characteristics."
