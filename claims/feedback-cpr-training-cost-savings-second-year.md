A simulated cost analysis for training 500 students per year, based on the study organization's 2021 costs converted from Brazilian Reals to US Dollars; the feedback model "decreases to $18 per student" from year two.

> "The initial investment in the first year is comparable for both models, with traditional training costing $27 per student and the feedback approach costing $26 per student. However, from the second year onward, the cost of the feedback model decreases to $18 per student."
