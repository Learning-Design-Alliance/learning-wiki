## Evidence

### Duffy Barbara 2023

Duffy Barbara, Tully Roisin, Stanton Alice V. (2023). An online case-based teaching and assessment program on clinical history-taking skills and reasoning using simulated patients in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03950-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Service evaluation of an online case-based program for 275 year-3 medical students, comparing attendance across three rotation groups with 2, 5 and 7 weeks of prior clinical attachment. Group means were 75%, 73% and 60% of tutorials; the comparison "higher attendance rates for Groups 1 and 2 students compared with Group 3 students" was significant at p < 0.001.

> "Analysis by group demonstrated higher attendance rates for Groups 1 and 2 students compared with Group 3 students (p < 0.001) (Table 3 and Fig. 2)."
