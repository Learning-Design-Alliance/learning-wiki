## Evidence

### Rusch Peter 2017

Rusch Peter, Kimmig Rainer, Lecuru Fabrice, Persson Jan, Ponce Jordi, Degueldre Michel, Verheijen René. (2017). The Society of European Robotic Gynaecological Surgery (SERGS) Pilot Curriculum for robot assisted gynecological surgery. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-017-4612-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the pilot program's implementation experience, comparing it with the ERUS programme; the article states the ERUS programme "clearly has shown that more systematic and stepwise training is more efficient and results in the shortest period to gain sufficient proficiency."

> "Open feedback together with a less structured surgical training, as opposed to systematic structured assessment and modular training in index procedures, resulted in a longer than envisaged training period to gain sufficient competence."
