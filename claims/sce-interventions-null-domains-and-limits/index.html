Evidence¶

Xining Wang 2023¶

Xining Wang, Gareth W. Young, Adéla Plechatá, Conor Mc Guckin, Guido Makransky. (2023). Utilizing virtual reality to assist social competence education and social support for children from under-represented backgrounds. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104815

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 1 mixed ANOVA results: the ER subscale time effect was p = .347 and NSS p = .071, so "Domains of nonverbal sending skills, and emotion regulation did not improve significantly"; the authors note the sample had no emotion-regulation difficulties.

"Domains of nonverbal sending skills, and emotion regulation did not improve significantly from pre-to post-test."

Xining Wang 2023 (2)¶

Xining Wang, Gareth W. Young, Adéla Plechatá, Conor Mc Guckin, Guido Makransky. (2023). Utilizing virtual reality to assist social competence education and social support for children from under-represented backgrounds. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104815

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 2 YSSQ results (50 children; Bonferroni α = .017): utilization of social resources time effect p = .275; objective social support rose numerically in all three groups, p = .033, significant only before correction, which the authors describe as "close to having a positive influence". The authors state existing family and school support is unlikely to change without environmental change, and short-term SCE teaching cannot greatly raise perceived social support for left-behind youth.

"Nevertheless, children's utilization of social resources and objective social support were not improved by any of the pedagogies in Study 2."
