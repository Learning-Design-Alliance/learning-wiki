`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of focus group discussions (n = 25) with Malaysian receiver participants; one receiver stated "I'm not so confident until we have completed the entire process". Barriers named include management prioritisation, finances and technical support. No effect sizes reported.

> "However, the confidence level in cascading the knowledge increased after receivers had gone through the whole developmental process of RLOs and organised several internal workshops within their own institutions."
