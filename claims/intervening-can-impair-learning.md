## Evidence

### Nilsson Maria Skyvell 2010

Nilsson Maria Skyvell, Pennbrant Sandra, Pilhammar Ewa, Wenestam Claes-Göran. (2010). Pedagogical strategies used in clinical medical education: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the discussion of observed intervening episodes: students whose actions were interrupted had to stand aside as observers, and the authors observed that "the students could thus experience a lack of feedback resulting in a lack of explanation and diminished understanding". Some students felt excluded and their knowledge undervalued.

> "However, by using this strategy, the action planned by the students was often interrupted. Consequently, this strategy could also impair the students' learning process which could result in a deterioration of the relationship between teacher and student."
