## Evidence

### Anil Kamat 2022

Anil Kamat, Basiel Makled, Jack Norfleet, Steven D. Schwaitzberg, Xavier Intes, Suvranu De, Anirban Dutta. (2022). Directed information flow during laparoscopic surgical skill acquisition dissociated skill level and medical simulation technology. npj Science of Learning 7:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00138-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way MANOVA on the coefficient of variation of Granger-causal connectivity across five repeated FLS trials. The article reports the significant interaction for the interhemispheric LPMC-to-RPMC connection with "partial η2 = 0.290"; Figure 3c shows CoV increased in VR for novices and decreased for experts.

> "Two-way MANOVA found a statistically signi ﬁcant effect of the interaction between the skill level and the simulator technology on the CoV of the interregional directed functional connectivity from LPMC to RPMC ( F (1, 21) = 8.561, p = 0.008; partial η2 = 0.290)"
