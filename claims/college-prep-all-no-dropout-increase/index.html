q3 · i? · causal · r2

From the discussion of the cohort evaluation: graduation rates did not decline when students began high school in college-preparatory rather than remedial courses, and very low ability students were only slightly more likely to graduate.

"Requiring all students to take college-prep courses rather than accumulating credits through remedial coursework did not aggravate an already high dropout rate."
