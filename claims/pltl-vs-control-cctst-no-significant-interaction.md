# PLTL peer leaders show no significant overall critical thinking difference from a matched non-PLTL control group, though controls trended toward negative gains

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Snyder Julia J. 2015

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way RM ANOVA comparing 37 PLTL leaders with 17 non-PLTL controls on CCTST total scores. "no significant interaction was observed" despite a small leader gain (+0.30) versus a control decline (−1.47); the printed partial eta squared = 0.045 was non-significant at p = 0.124.

> "A two-way repeated measures ANOVA was conducted to compare total pre-test and post-test scores of the experimental and control groups, and no significant interaction was observed for critical thinking skills of peer leaders participating in the PLTL training and leadership experience, F (1, 52) = 2.440, p = 0.124, power = 0.335, partial eta squared = 0.045."

### Snyder Julia J. 2015 (2)

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison from the same quasi-experimental study: controls' mean post-test fell to 16.35, "indicating an average negative gain of 1.47". The authors report greater than 50% control-group attrition and suggest some controls may not have taken the post-test with sufficient effort.

> "The mean post-test score of similar students without the PLTL training and leadership experience was 16.35 (n = 17), indicating an average negative gain of 1.47."
