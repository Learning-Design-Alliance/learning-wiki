ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Propensity score matched comparison of 353 coached and 353 uncoached students from ELS:02 data. The WWC reports SAT Math at effect size 0.13 (+5, p .08) and SAT verbal at effect size 0.08 (+3, p .31), domain average effect size 0.10, +4, not statistically significant; effect sizes were computed from the study's ordinary least squares regression coefficient.
