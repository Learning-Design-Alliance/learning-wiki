## Evidence

### Bansal Sandeep 2025

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA subscale analysis in the same 51-student longitudinal cohort; the article reports a medium effect size (ηp² = 0.07) for Willingness to Sacrifice and "a decline in the willingness to make personal sacrifices by the end of Phase 2 of clinical training as compared to basic science training in Phase 1."

> "The Bonferroni adjusted post-hoc analysis for subscale of Willingness to Sacrifice revealed a statistically significant difference in mean score between the second and fourth measurement points (p = 0.02), suggesting a decline in the willingness to make personal sacrifices by the end of Phase 2 of clinical training as compared to basic science training in Phase 1."

### Bansal Sandeep 2025 (2)

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same repeated-measures ANOVA analysis; the Readiness to Start subscale showed no statistically significant effect of time (p = 0.09, small effect size ηp² = 0.04 reported), and no post-hoc analysis was completed for this subscale. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Repeated-measures ANOVA, however, found the effect of time on the strength of motivation to have no statistical significance within the subscale of Readiness to Start"
