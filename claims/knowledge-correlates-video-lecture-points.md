## Evidence

### Dankbaar Mary E. W. 2017

Dankbaar Mary E. W., Richters Olivier, Kalkman Cor J., Prins Gerrie, ten Cate Olle T. J., van Merrienboer Jeroen J. G., Schuit Stephanie C. E. (2017). Comparative effectiveness of a serious game and an e-module to support patient safety knowledge and awareness. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0836-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of log-data from the 18 game-group students with available logs, linking game activity measures to knowledge test scores. The authors note this may be a spurious relationship given the game group's longer total learning time.

> "There was a significant association between knowledge scores and game-time (r = 0.56, p = 0.04), in particular between knowledge and video-lecture points (r = 0.68, p = 0.008)."
