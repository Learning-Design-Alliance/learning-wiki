Evidence¶

McCarthy 2020¶

McCarthy, E., Morgan, C. & Sussman, J. (2020). Growing strong learners in Galt Joint Union Elementary School District. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613373

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive statistics and multiple regression on the 2018-2019 prior-year cohort (n = 141 with complete DRDP data). The article reports ELs were expected to grow less than Non-ELs in all domains, statistically significant for only PD, with tests appearing underpowered.

"the difference between ELs' and Non-ELs' mean ATL-REG scores was 13 points in fall 2018 and 35 points in spring 2019"
