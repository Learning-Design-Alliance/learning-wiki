## Evidence

### Triepels CPR 2019

Triepels CPR, Smeets CFA, Notten KJB, Kruitwagen RFPM, Futterer JJ, Vergeldt TFM, Van Kuijk SMJ. (2019). Does three‐dimensional anatomy improve student understanding? Three‐Dimensional Methods for Learning Anatomy. Clinical Anatomy 32:25–33. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23405

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Khot et al. (2013), an RCT in which the traditional group studying six photographed views of a plastic model scored 40% versus 41% for the virtual reality group, described as almost equal.

> "Only one study that explored the educational effectiveness of using virtual reality found it offered no advantage over static presentations of 2D anatomical depictions (Khot et al., 2013)."
