`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Model fit evaluation in the SEM analysis of 165 nurses: the χ2 test was significant (χ2 = 147.71, df = 72, p < 0.001), but CFI = 0.96, TLI = 0.94, and RMSEA = 0.07 indicated "a reasonable fit to the data"; the model explained 38% of competency variance.

> "Overall, the hypothesized model explained 38% of the variance in nurses’ patient safety competency, providing moderate support for the model (see Fig. 2)."
