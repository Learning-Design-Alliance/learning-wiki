Lerch S.P., Huwendiek S., Nendaz M., Klöppel S., Pinilla S. (2025). Perceptions of Ad Hoc Entrustment, the Need for Supervision, and Coping Strategies in Clinical Residents: A Qualitative Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006019

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

All 12 residents offered improvement suggestions in the interviews, spanning curriculum and supervision, teaching content, and context factors. One resident proposed teaching "skills are taught one after the other, not, for example, three skills at the same time," and another stressed supervisors' specialist qualification as prerequisite for good supervision.

> "Residents mentioned increasing attending physician staffing to enhance the quality of their learning process."
