## Evidence

### Suppan M 2021

Suppan M, Stuby L, Carrera E, Cottet P, Koka A, Assal F, Savoldelli GL, Suppan L. (2021). Asynchronous Distance Learning of the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale During the COVID-19 Pandemic (E-Learning vs Video): Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/23594

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized, data analyst–blinded web-based trial at the University of Geneva (April–June 2020) with 75 completers (video n=34, e-learning n=41). The e-learning group averaged "38 correct answers, 95% CI 37-39" versus 35 for the video group, P<.001. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Participants who followed the e-learning module performed better than those who followed the video (38 correct answers, 95% CI 37-39, vs 35 correct answers, 95% CI 34-36, P<.001)."
