## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2023

Meagan Sundstrom, Rebeckah K. Fussell, Anna McLean Phillips, Mark Akubo, Scott E. Allen, David Hammer, Rachel E. Scherr, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Instructing nontraditional physics labs: Toward responsiveness to student epistemic framing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interaction analysis of Group A (three students) in the mechanism testing activity. The TA cued the High Agency-Falsification frame, but the group shifted to the Low Agency-Falsification frame, reacting subjectively to incoming data.

> "This interaction shifts the group from expecting a conﬁrmatory result to expecting a falsifying result, but does not shift the group from low to high epistemic agency (the groups shifts to the Low Agency-Falsiﬁcation frame)."
