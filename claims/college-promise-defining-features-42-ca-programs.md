## Evidence

### Rauner 2018

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Definitional statement in the report's introduction, attributed to Perna & Leigh (2018) and Miller-Adams (2015), establishing the "two defining features of College Promise programs" used to bound the scan.

> "most researchers agree on two defining features of College Promise programs: 1) financial support that encourages students to attend postsecondary institutions and 2) eligibility criteria based on where students live or attend school"

### Rauner 2018 (2)

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Findings section reporting database characteristics of the 42 programs active in fall 2017; all met the scan's inclusion criteria of place-based eligibility, direct financial support, and no cap on eligible students.

> "All 42 of the state's programs determine eligible students based on where students live or attend school, offer direct financial support for students, and serve all students who meet the program's eligibility criteria."
