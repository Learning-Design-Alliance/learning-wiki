Eunjin Seo, Margarett Clapper, Cameron A. Hecht, Robert Crosnoe, David S. Yeager. (2025). Peer Mindset Culture as a Developmental Context for Belonging. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10082-8

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the social disconnection pathway. The review argues fixed mindsets lead students to interpret struggle as low ability, making help-seeking feel risky and offering help feel futile, and links fixed mindset cultures with performance goal orientations and a zero-sum mentality.

> "We propose that fixed mindset peer cultures may undermine belonging by discouraging both the giving and receiving of help (Muenks & Yan, 2024; Seo et al., 2025b), narrowing students' opportunities to feel seen, supported, and connected."
