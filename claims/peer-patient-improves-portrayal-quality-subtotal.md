## Evidence

### Pritchard Shane A. 2020

Pritchard Shane A., Keating Jennifer L., Nestel Debra, Blackstock Felicity C. (2020). Physiotherapy students can be educated to portray realistic patient roles in simulation: a pragmatic observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02382-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory paired analysis of the complete data set (n = 23) testing the quality-of-performance sub-total of the portrayal instrument. The subset of 16 videos also showed a significant difference (2.06 [0.20–3.91], p = 0.03).

> "The pre and post-intervention mean sub-total scores for quality were 15.4 (4.0) and 18.5 (3.7) respectively. A significant difference was observed in favour of the post-intervention mean score (mean difference 3.1, 95%CI [0.64–5.49], p = 0.02)."
