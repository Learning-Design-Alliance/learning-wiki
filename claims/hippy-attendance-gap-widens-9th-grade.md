### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of attendance data from independent samples t-tests showed that although both groups' attendance declined across grades, the difference between HIPPY and comparison children was largest in 9th grade, with a 6-percentage-point gap (94% vs. 88%).

> "In fact, the largest gap in school attendance rates appears in the 9th grade (94% attendance rate for HIPPY children, 88% for comparison group)."
