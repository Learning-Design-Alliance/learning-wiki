Malhotra 2026¶

Malhotra, M., & Chhabra, I. (2026). Ensuring academic integrity through automated online exam proctoring a decade long systematic review. Discover Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-026-01224-3

Comparative synthesis in the review's literature-analysis section contrasts CNN and RNN strengths and limits: "RNN-based models perform better in temporal analysis" while CNNs need extensive training data. The review also reports CNNs enhanced with gaze estimation modules showed improved anomaly-detection accuracy.

"while CNNs have high visual accuracy, they demand extensive training data, which may not generalize across demographic settings. RNN-based models perform better in temporal analysis but are prone to vanishing gradients and limited scalability."
