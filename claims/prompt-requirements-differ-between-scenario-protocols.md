## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures analysis of prompts per scenario-protocol in the trained group (N = 60). The main effect was significant with printed effect size partial η² = .74; follow-up contrasts showed "The most prompts were required in scenario C (p ≤ .001) compared to the other 5 scenario-protocols" (mean 17.75).

> "A significant main effect for scenario-protocols was found F (5,55) = 30.86, p < .001, partial η² = .74, indicating that, as expected (2b) the mean number of prompts provided differed between scenario-protocols."
