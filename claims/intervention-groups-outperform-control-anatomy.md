### Löffler Sabine 2011

Löffler Sabine, Feja Christine, Widmann Jan, Claus Ilona, von Lindeman Katharina, Eisnach Kristina. (2011). Interactive versus Reproductive Learning, a Comparison of Medical School Graduates with Participants of a Postgraduate CPD Session. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000769](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000769)

Secondary ANOVA analysis comparing intervention participants with a control group that took both tests but no intervention, in the same quasi-experimental design. The article reports intervention participants attained 23.3 vs 19.8 points, p<0.0005.

> "There was a significant influence by the intervention (yes/no) (p<0.0005). On average, participants in the intervention group attained 23.3, the control group 19.8 points in the post-test"
