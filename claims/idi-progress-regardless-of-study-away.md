### Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley 2024

Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley. (2024). Domestic Study Away in Higher Education: A Systematic Literature Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241279296

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Rust (2015), a study using Bennett's DMIS and the Intercultural Development Inventory; progress was attributed to intentional course design rather than the study away component. No effect size is printed.

> "This study found that students enrolled in a course that was intentionally designed to develop intercultural awareness and knowledge, regardless of whether study away was a component of the course, showed progress along the intercultural development continuum."
