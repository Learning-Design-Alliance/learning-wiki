# Combined virtual-jaw training produces higher supragingival scaling operation process scores than jaw-model or virtual-reality training alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Operation process scores, graded double-blinded by three professional dentists, were significantly higher in V-J and J-V groups than in V and J groups. [→ Zhang Jie 2021](#zhang-jie-2021)

## Evidence

### Zhang Jie 2021

Zhang Jie, Xing Jiawei, Zheng Min, Sheng Jie, Zhang Kailiang, Zhang Baoping. (2021). Effectiveness of virtual simulation and jaw model for undergraduate periodontal teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03064-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Double-blinded operation assessment of the randomized experiment, scored by 3 dentists using the 100-point standard sheet (Table 1). Combined groups scored "V-J (72.00 ± 5.92)" and "J-V (70.00 ± 3.05)" versus V 61.67 ± 7.85 and J 60.67 ± 2.58.

> "Group V-J (72.00 ± 5.92) and Group J-V (70.00 ± 3.05) showed a relatively better performance on the operation process compared with the other two groups (V: 61.67 ± 7.85; J: 60.67 ± 2.58; P < 0.05, Fig. 5A)"
