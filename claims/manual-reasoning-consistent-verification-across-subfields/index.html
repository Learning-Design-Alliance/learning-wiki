Evidence¶

Nizamani 2026¶

Nizamani, S. B., Lee, Y., Donekal Chandrashekar, N., Ellis, M., & Ramakrishnan, N. (2026). Understanding Student Use of Large Language Models Across Computer Science Subfields. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01158 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of verification strategies among students who reported active LLM use (assistant, peer, or expert role) across the seven assignments. The article reports manual reasoning "ranging from 70.7% to 82.4% across assignments"; a chi-square test showed no significant difference (χ2 = 5.73, df = 6, p = 0.455); equivalence was not tested.

"manual reasoning was the most consistently used verification method, ranging from 70.7% to 82.4% across assignments. HCI showed the highest manual reasoning rate (82.4%), while Networking had the lowest (70.7%)."
