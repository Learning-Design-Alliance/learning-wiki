Winarno et al. 2017¶

Winarno, S., Muthu, K. S., & Ling, L. S. (2017). Direct Problem-Based Learning (DPBL): A Framework for Integrating Direct Instruction and Problem-Based Learning Approach. International Education Studies, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p119

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Lecturer evaluation of DPBL activities by the five lecturers involved in the study, reported only as aggregate descriptive ratings in Table 4: 4.80 (SD .45) that DPBL is possible to be used, 4.60 (SD .55) that it is effective to improve creative thinking and teamwork skills, summary 4.70 (SD .50). Individual ratings are not reported, and the conclusion says "nearly all lecturers agreed", not all. These are the lecturers' judgements, not a measure of creative thinking or teamwork; no inferential test or effect size is printed.
