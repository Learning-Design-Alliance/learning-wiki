# GAIQ produces more compact data in which respondents themselves categorize features, shifting analysis work from researcher to respondent

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the GAIQ, respondents relate their own feature names to a pre-defined feature list and state preferences, whereas in interview data the referent of an instructor's statements can be ambiguous. [→ Edit Yerushalmi 2011](#edit-yerushalmi-2011)

## Evidence

### Edit Yerushalmi 2011

Edit Yerushalmi, Charles Henderson, William Mamudi, Chandralekha Singh, Shih-Yin Lin. (2011). The Group Administered Interactive Questionnaire: An Alternative to Individual Interviews. PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3680003](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' comparison of sample data from an interview (Figure 3) and the GAIQ (Figure 4). In the interview excerpt they note "The intent of the interviewee can be masked in the conversation", while in the GAIQ the TA himself linked his feature "approach/execution" to feature 3 from the list.

> "Thus, GAIQ shifts some of the analysis work from the researcher to the respondent as the respondents themselves differentiate between the features, preferences and reasons and categorize the features."
