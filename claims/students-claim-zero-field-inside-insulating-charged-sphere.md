## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student understanding of Symmetry and Gauss' law. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07725](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07725)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written and interview evidence from a multiple-choice item on the field inside a hollow insulating sphere with six discrete charge blobs versus uniform charge; 50% of 168 students answered item 2 correctly per Table 1. The most common difficulty "was assuming that the electric ﬁeld inside the sphere in experiment (I) is also zero"; some students said "this had to do with electrical shielding".
