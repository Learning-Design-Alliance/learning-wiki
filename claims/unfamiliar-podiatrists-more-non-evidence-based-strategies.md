`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Odds-ratio comparison of management-strategy responses between familiar and unfamiliar respondents, reported with 95% confidence intervals and p values. The article reports "More podiatrists unfamiliar with DCD identified management strategies not currently supported by the evidence", with those familiar 76, 43 and 59% less likely to identify the three interventions respectively.

> "More podiatrists unfamiliar with DCD identified management strategies not currently supported by the evidence; increasing flexibility (OR = 0.24 95% CI = 0.15 to 0.39, p < 0.001), footwear advice (OR = 0.56, 95% CI = 0.34 to 0.93, p = 0.03), and foot orthoses (OR = 0.41, 95% CI = 0.26 to 0.68, p = 0.001)"
