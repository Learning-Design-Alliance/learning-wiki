## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5000 subsamples) in the high-functioning SLD group (n = 51), controlling for age and gender. The article reports "a significant indirect effect through test anxiety (β = − 0.07, BootLLCI = − 0.1834, BootULCI = − 0.0017)" with a nonsignificant direct effect; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The mediation analyses, as Fig. 1c, revealed nonsignificant direct effects of trait anxiety on academic performance (β = 0.12, t = 1.65, p > 0.05) but a significant indirect effect through test anxiety (β = − 0.07, BootLLCI = − 0.1834, BootULCI = − 0.0017)."
