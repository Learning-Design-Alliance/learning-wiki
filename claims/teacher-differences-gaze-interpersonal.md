Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09538-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

The per-teacher ANOVA in the case study found that for Joanne the effect of eye contact on teacher agency was opposite, with printed effect size η² = 0.029; her agency was significantly higher during student gazes at teacher face than during student-started eye contacts.

> "With Joanne, the effect of eye contact on teacher agency was opposite ( F(3, 476) = 4.70, p =. 0 0 3 ,η2 = 0.029)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

### Haataja 2021 (3)

Haataja, E., Salonen, V., Laine, A., Toivanen, M., & Hannula, M. S. (2021). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The per-teacher analysis found Fred's results differed from the others: "the studentstarted eye contacts were frequent during low teacher communion", which the authors interpreted as the reciprocal nature of scaffolding interaction with sometimes unproductive student initiatives.

> "In this gaze category, Fred 's results differed from the others. In his lesson, the studentstarted eye contacts were frequent during low teacher communion."
