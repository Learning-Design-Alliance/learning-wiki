## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Children with bad academic performance had lower monocular and binocular near visual acuity and a longer near point of convergence than children with good academic performance (all p < 0.001). [→ Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina 2020](#alvarez-peregrina-cristina-2020)

## Evidence

### Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina 2020

Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina, Sánchez-Tena Miguel Ángel, Andreu-Vázquez Cristina, Villa-Collar Cesar. (2020). Visual Health and Academic Performance in School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Optometric screening comparing 1006 children with bad and 9212 with good academic performance. Table 4 reports, e.g., monocular VA (LE) mean 0.84 ± 0.20 (bad) versus 0.91 ± 0.16 (good), and NPC 6.47 ± 6.08 cm versus 5.40 ± 5.49 cm, all p < 0.001.

> "Results from visual screening show that visual acuity in distance vision is lower in children with bad academic performance, and that they also have a longer near point of convergence."
