## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model covariate effects from the within-group cohort in Bettermarks. "More repetitions and more total assignments led to lower absolute error rates", both negatively associated with absolute error rate.

> "More repetitions and more total assignments led to lower absolute error rates (repetitions: b = -2.49e-02; t = -32.59; p < .001; assignments: b = -1.83e-03; t = -12.63; p < .001)."

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021 (2)

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate effects from Analysis 1b on the same within-group cohort. Both repetitions and assignments were negatively associated with relative error rate.

> "Students who repeated more problem sets yielded a lower relative error rate than students who engaged in less repetitions (b = -4.21e-2; t = -61.47; p < .001). Students who computed more assignments yielded a lower relative error rate (b = -1.56e-3; t = -11.24; p < .001)."
