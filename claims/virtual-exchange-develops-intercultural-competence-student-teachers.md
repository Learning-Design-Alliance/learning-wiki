## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Student teachers who participated in virtual exchange showed statistically significant development of intercultural communicative competence between pre-test and post-test. [→ The EVALUATE Group 2019](#the-evaluate-group-2019)

## Evidence

### The EVALUATE Group 2019

The EVALUATE Group. (2019). Evaluating the impact of virtual exchange on initial teacher education: a European policy experiment. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2019.29.9782490057337

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental pre-/post-test design with control group across 25 virtual exchanges (n=1,018 invited; 57% completed both tests) measured intercultural competence using the Portalla and Chen (2010) 20-item instrument. The report states pre-post intercultural communicative competence correlations indicated "a rho=.705, p<.01".

> "Pre- and post-test Cronbach alphas indicated reliable constructs, and pre-post intercultural communicative competence correlations indicated a rho=.705, p<.01."
