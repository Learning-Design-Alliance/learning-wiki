## Evidence

### Lanqin Zheng 2016

Lanqin Zheng. (2016). The effectiveness of self-regulated learning scaffolds on academic performance in computer-based learning environments: a meta-analysis. Asia Pacific Education Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12564-016-9426-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis of how scaffolds facilitate SRL: 26 of 29 studies supported the whole SRL process (2349 subjects), with the remainder supporting monitoring, adaptation, or reflection specifically. The between-group difference was not statistically significant.

> "Supporting the whole process of SRL was associated with the highest effect size (ES = 0.469) and supporting reﬂection with the lowest effect size (ES =- 0.105). No statistically signiﬁcant dif- ference was found among the three categories ( Q = 4.561, df = 3, p = 0.207)"
