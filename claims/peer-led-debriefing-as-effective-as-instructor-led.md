## Evidence

### Jaffrelot M 2024

Jaffrelot M, Boet S, Floch Y, Garg N, Dubois D, Laparra V, Touffet L, Bould MD. (2024). Learning with our peers: peer-led versus instructor-led debriefing for simulated crises, a randomized controlled trial. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.23317

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled repeated-measures trial of 61 undergraduate medical students randomized to instructor-led, peer-debriefer, or peer-debriefee debriefing. "No significant differences in performance were found" among modalities on the OGRS; equivalence was not statistically tested.

> "No significant differences in performance were found, after adjusting for the year of medical school training, among debriefing modalities (P = 0.147) or between the immediate post-test and retention test (P = 0.358)."
