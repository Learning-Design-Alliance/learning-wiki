`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched VCR analysis by NCES locale (Figure 8) for brick-and-mortar charters only, with online schools excluded; urban reading advantage of 30 days of learning, other contrasts not statistically significant.

> "Charter students in urban New Mexico post stronger learning gains in reading relative to their TPS VCRs, representing 30 extra days of learning. Urban charter school students do not perform differently from their peers in TPS in math. Students in suburban, town, and rural charter schools in New Mexico are on par with their respective TPS VCRs in growth in both reading and math."
