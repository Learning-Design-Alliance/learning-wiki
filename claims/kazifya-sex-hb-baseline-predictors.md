## Evidence

### Minja 2025

Minja, E. G., Mrimi, E. C., Mponzi, W. P., Beckmann, J., Finda, M. F., Okumu, F. O., Long, K. Z., Lang, C., Utzinger, J., & Gerber, M. (2025). Effect of a School-Based Physical Activity and Multi-Micronutrient Supplementation Intervention on Cognitive Function and Academic Achievement Among Schoolchildren in Tanzania: Secondary Outcome from the KaziAfya Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 22(9), 1335. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22091335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed-effects regression from the KaziAfya trial (n = 559): hemoglobin and baseline accuracy significantly predicted post-intervention Flanker accuracy. No effect size was printed.

> "Hb level was positively associated with accuracy (p < 0.001) and baseline accuracy for congruent stimuli positively predicted post-intervention accuracy for congruent stimuli (p < 0.001)."

### Minja 2025 (2)

Minja, E. G., Mrimi, E. C., Mponzi, W. P., Beckmann, J., Finda, M. F., Okumu, F. O., Long, K. Z., Lang, C., Utzinger, J., & Gerber, M. (2025). Effect of a School-Based Physical Activity and Multi-Micronutrient Supplementation Intervention on Cognitive Function and Academic Achievement Among Schoolchildren in Tanzania: Secondary Outcome from the KaziAfya Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 22(9), 1335. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22091335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed-effects regression from the KaziAfya trial: sex significantly predicted Flanker reaction time in both congruent and incongruent conditions, with boys faster; sex also predicted accuracy (p = 0.01), suggesting lower accuracy among boys.

> "However, sex turned out to be a significant predictor in both conditions, with boys responding significantly faster than girls (p < 0.001)."
