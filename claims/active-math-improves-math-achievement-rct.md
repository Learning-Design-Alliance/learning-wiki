## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Over a 9-month cluster-randomized trial, children receiving active math improved their math score by 1.2 points more than controls (p = 0.002), a relative Cohen's d of 0.38. [→ Have Mona 2018](#have-mona-2018)

## Evidence

### Have Mona 2018

Have Mona, Nielsen Jacob Have, Ernst Martin Thomsen, Gejl Anne Kaer, Fredens Kjeld, Grøntved Anders, Kristensen Peter Lund. (2018). Classroom-based physical activity improves children's math achievement – A randomized controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208787

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized controlled trial in 12 Danish schools with 505 children (mean age 7.2 ± 0.3 years); mixed effects regression models adjusted for gender, allocation group and baseline values, schools as random effects. "the intervention group improved math score by 1.2 (95% CI 0.4; 1.9) points more than the control"; relative Cohen's d = 0.38, absolute d = 0.91 and 1.48 for control and intervention.

> "the intervention group improved math score by 1.2 (95% CI 0.4; 1.9) points more than the control (p = 0.002). Expressed in percentage terms, the control group improved math score by 17.5% and the intervention group by 24.7%"
