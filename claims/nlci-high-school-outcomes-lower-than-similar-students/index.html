Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou 2020¶

Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou. (2020). Supporting Neighborhood Schools from Pre-K through High School: Successes and Challenges of the North Lawndale Cluster Initiative. UChicago Consortium Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-neighborhood-schools-from-pre-k-through-high-school

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Cohort comparison of Collins Academy High School students with students of similar background and eighth-grade achievement at other CPS high schools, covering ninth-grade and college outcomes. The report notes some college-persistence analyses included as few as 12 students; no effect size printed.

"In most of the NLCI years, students at the NLCI high school had lower ninth-grade attendance, ninth-grade GPAs, college enrollment rates, and college persistence rates than students with similar backgrounds at other CPS schools."
