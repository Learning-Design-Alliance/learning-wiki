## Evidence

### Bielka Kateryna 2024

Bielka Kateryna, Kuchyn Iurii, Fomina Hanna, Khomenko Olena, Kyselova Iryna, Frank Michael. (2024). Difficult airway simulation-based training for anaesthesiologists: efficacy and skills retention within six months. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-024-02423-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of CI/CICV simulation rounds: Group 1 (n=24) initial, Group 2 (n=24) post-training, Group 3 (n=19) at 6 months, analysed by Fisher's exact test with OR. "the number of anaesthesiologists, who attempted more than 3 laryngoscopies decreased" with the reported ORs. In the CI scenario specifically, >3 attempts fell from 15/24 to 9/24 and 5/19 (OR 5.27 and 4.6).

> "the number of anaesthesiologists, who attempted more than 3 laryngoscopies decreased (OR = 7 [1.8–26.8], p = 0.006 right after training and OR = 3.9 [1.06–14.4], p = 0.035 6 month later)"
