## Evidence

### Amina Cviko 2012

Amina Cviko, Susan McKenney, Joke Voogt. (2012). Teachers enacting a technology-rich curriculum for emergent literacy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9208-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis of integration scores over the eight activities found a significant proportion of variance explained by time worked within PictoPal for three teachers; for the fourth teacher no significant correlation was found between time and extent of integration.

### Amina Cviko 2012 (2)

Amina Cviko, Susan McKenney, Joke Voogt. (2012). Teachers enacting a technology-rich curriculum for emergent literacy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9208-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression of pupil engagement on successive on-computer activities in the four classrooms. The article reports that engagement "increases over time", indicating junior kindergarteners' skills to work within and enjoy PictoPal grow during implementation.

> "The successive on-computer activities explained a signiﬁcant proportion of variance in pupils engagement scores for classes 1a, 1b, 2a and 2b, respectively R2 = .72, F (1, 6) = 15.17, p< .05; R2 = .49, F (1, 6) = 5.85, p< .05; R2 = .83, F (1, 6) = 29.96, p< .05 and R2 = .91, F (1, 6) = 64.00, p< .05."
