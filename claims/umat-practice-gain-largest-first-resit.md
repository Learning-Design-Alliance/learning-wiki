## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The predominant increment in total UMAT percentile occurred between first attempt and first re-sit, with a further significant increase at the second re-sit but no further increment subsequently; UMAT-3 showed no gain beyond the first re-sit. [→ Puddey Ian B 2014](#puddey-ian-b-2014)

## Evidence

### Puddey Ian B 2014

Puddey Ian B, Mercer Annette, Andrich David, Styles Irene. (2014). Practice effects in medical school entrance testing with the undergraduate medicine and health sciences admission test (UMAT). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-48

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated measures analysis of variance across the 2000–2012 cohort comparing percentile scores by number of sittings. "RANOVA indicated a further significant increase in percentile score at the second re-sit (P < 0.001, Bonferroni correction)"; for UMAT-3 there was "only an increase in relative percentile between the first attempt and the first re-sit with no further increment at any subsequent re-sit."

> "The predominant increment for total UMAT occurred between the first attempt and the first re-sit. RANOVA indicated a further significant increase in percentile score at the second re-sit (P < 0.001, Bonferroni correction) but no further increment with subsequent re-sits."
