Fan Y 2026¶

Fan Y, Li X and Zhang R (2026). Family-school autonomy support for children's responsible use of generative artificial intelligence: a self-determination theory synthesis and developmental research agenda. Front. Psychol. 17:1969217. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969217

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Wang et al. (2026), that "clear rules strengthened both the protective pattern for reflection and the risk pattern for unreflective use" in relation to academic impostor syndrome. The review presents this as evidence that policy clarity interacts with use style rather than uniformly reducing risk.

"Perceived policy clarity amplified the contrasting associations of reflective and unreflective use with impostor syndrome: clear rules strengthened both the protective pattern for reflection and the risk pattern for unreflective use"
