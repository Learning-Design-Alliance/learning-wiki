Jacob Hartog 2021¶

Jacob Hartog, Lily Fesler, Brittany Tabora. (2021). Characteristics and Performance of High School Equivalency Exam Takers in New Jersey. Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/rel/regions/midatlantic

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational comparison across the three New Jersey HSE exams among the same grade 8 cohort. The study reports that "GED takers had received higher grade 8 standardized test scores" and differed in race/ethnicity and lunch eligibility relative to HiSET and TASC takers.

"GED takers had received higher grade 8 standardized test scores, were more likely to identify as White, and were less likely to have been eligible for the national school lunch program in grade 8 than HiSET and TASC takers."

  • Under the new thresholds, two-thirds of GED takers and more than half of HiSET and TASC takers passed on the first attempt, and three-quarters of all takers ever passed each exam — related
  • After New Jersey reduced the passing thresholds for two exams, first-attempt passing rates were similar across the three HSE exams — related
