q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CTC bilingual authorization data 1990–91 to 2015–16 (Figure 10) and institution program counts (Figure 9). Proposition 58, passed November 2016, removes restrictions on bilingual education, likely increasing demand for bilingual teachers; 14% of surveyed districts reported bilingual teacher shortages.

"At its peak in 1994–95, California granted over 1,800 bilingual authorizations (see Figure 10). Even after the passage of Proposition 227, California issued over 1,200 bilingual authorizations a year between 2003–04 and 2009–10. Since 2010, new bilingual authorizations have declined steadily, with fewer than 700 teachers authorized in 2015–16."
