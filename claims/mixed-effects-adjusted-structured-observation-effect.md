## Evidence

### Zhu Xiaocheng 2026

Zhu Xiaocheng, Tang Anliu, Chin Munle, Chang Huan. (2026). Developing purposeful observation during early clinical exposure in anesthesiology: a cluster-randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10042-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model with teaching group as fixed effect and clerkship group as random effect; the adjusted model included age, sex, academic track, prior operating room exposure, and baseline interest in anesthesiology as covariates, yielding "β = 5.89" (95% CI 5.47 to 6.31, P < 0.001).

> "Unadjusted model	β = 5.81	5.41 to 6.22	< 0.001
