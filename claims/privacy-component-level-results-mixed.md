## Evidence

### Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen 2016

Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen, Faraji Mona. (2016). Comparison of effect between group discussion and educational booklet on Iranian nursing students’ attitude and practice toward patient privacy. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.29

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Component-level analysis (Table 3) of attitude and practice across physical, informational, psychological, and social privacy domains using Mann–Whitney U-tests. The article reports "the difference was not statistically significant in the domain of social privacy" for attitude, and practice differences significant in physical (P=0.005) and social (P=0.001) domains only.

> "after the intervention, the mean attitude scores in all components were higher in the discussion group (P<0.05), nonetheless, the difference was not statistically significant in the domain of social privacy"
