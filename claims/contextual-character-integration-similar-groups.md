Questionnaire items Q14 (Table 3) and Q15 (Table 4) asked both groups about integration of social/historical context and of original characters. The experimental group had higher "Maybe" rates (23.81% and 33.33%), which the authors read as a more critical or nuanced evaluation.

> "In the control group, exactly half of the participants (50%) affirmed that the text successfully incorporates these contextual elements, suggesting a recognition of the text's ability to resonate with the original narrative's setting and background."
