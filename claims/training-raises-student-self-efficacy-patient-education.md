## Evidence

### Grbavac V 2024

Grbavac V, Naletilić M, Šimić J. (2024). Impact of short-term patient education intervention on the teaching skills of physical therapy students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06360-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Self-efficacy was measured with a 21-item patient education assessment completed before and after the experiment. The overall self-efficacy result showed d = 1.033 with p < .001; the LSD post hoc test indicated intervention-group scores were significantly higher after the intervention.

> "The intervention group did not perform differently from the control group on self-efficacy items before the experiment (T1), and their average results are shown in Table 4 (p > .05). However, they significantly outperformed the control group after the experiment (T2, p < .001)."
