Evidence¶

Kaplan 2015¶

Kaplan, A., Ozturk, M., & Ocal, M. F. (2015). Relieving of misconceptions of derivative concept with derive. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 1(1), 64-74. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105126

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Between-group comparison of post-test results for the experiment group (Derive) and control group (traditional instruction), each reported with 19 students in the results tables. The experiment group's understanding level was higher for all attainments except the third, where both groups reached 63% understanding.

"When comparing the post -test results, the “understanding” level of experiment group is higher than that of control group for all attainments except the third attainment. In general, there was a decrease in percentages of “misconception” category."
