Claims

A computational model of learning reproduced the direction of two tutor A/B experiments without human training data, but not students' absolute performance

Simulated learners trained only on the tutors' task structure showed the same main effects as students in a blocked-versus-interleaved fractions experiment and a constrained-versus-unconstrained problem-design experiment, while over-predicting early errors where students had prior knowledge.
