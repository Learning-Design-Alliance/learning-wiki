Grobler 2012¶

Grobler, B., Bisschoff, T., & Beeka, A. (2012). Changing perceptions of teachers regarding the importance and competence of their principals as leaders. South African Journal of Education, 32(1), 40-55. https://journals.co.za/handle/10520/EJC132899

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Factor analytic procedures on both pre- and post-test data yielded the same seven factors with "all reliability coefficients were above 0.7" and 74.05% of variance explained, supporting the instrument's internal consistency.

"As all reliability coefficients were above 0.7 and 74.05% of variance could be explained by these factors, it was decided that these seven factors could be used in the pre- and post-test analysis"
