## Evidence

### Amanda Buddemeyer 2022

Amanda Buddemeyer, Xiaoyi Tian, Erin Walker. (2022). Dominance as an Indicator of Rapport and Learning in Human-Agent Communication. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.02361](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.02361)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Transcript coding analysis of 14 students' conversations with the Emma robot, where the control score is the percentage of a participant's turns coded one-up. The authors report a "mean control score of 0.6577" versus an aggregate robot control score of 0.0713 over 855 turns.

> "The 14 students have a mean control score of 0.6577 and a median control score of 0.6707. This is supportive of our hypothesis that students would tend to assert control when interacting with Emma given Emma's low power positioning as a teachable agent."
