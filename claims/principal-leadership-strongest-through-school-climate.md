## Evidence

### Sebastian 2013

Sebastian, J., & Allensworth, E. (2013). How Do Secondary Principals Influence Teaching and Learning? Principal's Research Review, 8(4). https://www.nassp.org/prr

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of pathways in the Bryk et al. (2010) model using administrative and survey data from Chicago high schools in 2006–07, with leadership and organization measured from 3,529 teachers in 99 high schools. The authors report the climate pathway was "by far the strongest mechanism" linking leadership to instruction and achievement.

> "Of all the school processes, high school leaders had the largest influence on school achievement through school culture and climate."
