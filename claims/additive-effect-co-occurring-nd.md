`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Additional analyses on the 9018-student sample examining the number of neurodevelopmental disorders per individual, adjusted for background characteristics. The article reports a stepwise increase in odds of lower marks with each additional diagnosis, e.g. "first language: OR = 3.07, 95% CI = 2.87–3.29, p < .001"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Each additional diagnostic category increased the likelihood of obtaining lower marks in language subjects (first language: OR = 3.07, 95% CI = 2.87–3.29, p < .001; foreign language: OR = 2.61, 95% CI = 2.45–2.78, p < .001), mathematics (OR = 2.39, 95% CI = 2.25–2.54, p < .001), arts (OR = 1.93, 95% CI = 1.81–2.06, p < .001), and PE (OR = 1.70, 95% CI = 1.60–1.81, p < .001)."
