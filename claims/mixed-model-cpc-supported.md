## Evidence

### Knox S 2015

Knox S, Cullen W, Dunne CP. (2015). A national study of Continuous Professional Competence (CPC) amongst pre-hospital practitioners. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-1197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey question on preferred CPC model. "the majority of EMT (88 %) and Paramedics/AP (77 %) respondents favoured" the mixed model; 84% of EMTs and 77% of Paramedics/APs also supported minimum standards including evidence of patient care report completion and CPG compliance.

> "Overall, the majority of EMT (88 %) and Paramedics/AP (77 %) respondents favoured the introduction of CPC by the Regulator using a ‘mixed’ model approach of combining ‘mandatory’ and ‘voluntary’ activities"
