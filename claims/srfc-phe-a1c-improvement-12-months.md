## Evidence

### Gorrindo P 2014

Gorrindo P, Peltz A, Ladner TR, Reddy I, Miller BM, Miller RF, Fowler MJ. (2014). Medical Students as Health Educators at a Student-Run Free Clinic: Improving the Clinical Outcomes of Diabetic Patients. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000164

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective EMR review of 45 diabetic patients who established care at Shade Tree Clinic between 2008 and 2011, comparing A1c at initial presentation (T-0) and 12 ± 3 months later (T-1) with a related-samples Wilcoxon Signed Rank test. The mean change was 1.7 (95% CI 1.1-2.5), "improving from a mean of 9.6 at T-0 to a mean of 7.9 at T-1."

> "Mean hemoglobin A1c values improved significantly from 9.6 to 7.9, after a mean of 12.5 ± 1.5 months (P < .0001)."
