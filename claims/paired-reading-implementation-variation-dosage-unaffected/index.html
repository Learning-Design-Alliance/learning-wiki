Evidence¶

Lloyd et al. 2015¶

Cheryl Lloyd, Triin Edovald, Zsolt Kiss, Stephen Morris, Amy Skipp and Hashim Ahmed (NatCen Social Research). (2015). Paired Reading Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581127

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative process evaluation based on in-depth interviews with 8 staff members across 8 schools, including senior leaders and Year 7 and Year 9 teachers. The report concludes these "appear to be natural variations between the settings" unlikely to have affected dosage.

"There was some variation in the intervention group schools in terms of the programme set-up and delivery. There was also a varying level of support provided to pupils within the intervention by the teachers involved, based mainly on the reading ability of the pupils."
