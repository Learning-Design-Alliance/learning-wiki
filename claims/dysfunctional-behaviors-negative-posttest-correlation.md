# A higher proportion of dysfunctional behaviors during micro-failures is significantly negatively correlated with posttest scores, while the proportion of functional behaviors shows a non-significant positive correlation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The proportion of Dysfunctional behaviors correlated significantly negatively with dyadic posttest scores (Kendall's τ = −0.31, P < 0.05). [→ Rachel Lam 2019](#rachel-lam-2019)
`q2 i?` The proportion of Functional behaviors showed a non-significant positive correlation with posttest scores (Kendall's τ = 0.24, P = 0.09); equivalence was not tested. [→ Rachel Lam 2019 (2)](#rachel-lam-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Rachel Lam 2019

Rachel Lam. (2019). What students do when encountering failure in collaborative tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0045-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-tailed Kendall's tau correlations computed at the dyad level (20 dyads), collapsing across conditions, on proportions of Functional and Dysfunctional behaviors within micro-failure episodes. The negative "Kendall's τ = −0.31, P < 0.05" for Dysfunctional behaviors reached significance.

> "There was a non-significant positive correlation between Functional behaviors and posttest scores, Kendall's τ = 0.24, P = 0.09, and a significant negative correlation between Dysfunctional behaviors and posttest scores, Kendall's τ = −0.31, P < 0.05."

### Rachel Lam 2019 (2)

Rachel Lam. (2019). What students do when encountering failure in collaborative tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0045-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same dyad-level correlational analysis of behavior proportions; the Functional-behavior correlation was positive but non-significant ("Kendall's τ = 0.24, P = 0.09"), so no effect size conclusion is drawn beyond the printed τ.

> "There was a non-significant positive correlation between Functional behaviors and posttest scores, Kendall's τ = 0.24, P = 0.09, and a significant negative correlation between Dysfunctional behaviors and posttest scores, Kendall's τ = −0.31, P < 0.05."
