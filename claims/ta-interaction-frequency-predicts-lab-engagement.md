## Evidence

### Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll 2013

Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll (2013). Interactions between teaching assistants and students boost engagement in physics labs. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across the 16 observed lab sections of Physics 100, relating TA behaviour measures to engagement snapshots. The article reports interactions were "a positive and signiﬁcant predictor of student engagement" with printed effect size r(14) = 0.52, p = 0.03, r2 = 0.27.

> "the frequency of interactions between TAs and students was a positive and signiﬁcant predictor of student engagement: r(14) = 0.52, p = 0.03."
