## Evidence

### Kelli Shar 2020

Kelli Shar, Rosemary S. Russ, James T. Laverty. (2020). Student Epistemological Framing on Paper-Based Assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of think-aloud interview data from 18 students solving constructed-response physics exam problems found "54 students started in the Algorithmic Physics frame" out of 67 initial instances. Only three students shifted frames across all problems; on the Gravitron problem, no student shifted their initial frame despite struggling.

> "Looking across the 67 total initial frame instances, 54 students started in the Algorithmic Physics frame, 11 students started in the Conceptual Physics frame, one student started in an Algorithmic Math frame, and one student started in a Conceptual Math frame."
