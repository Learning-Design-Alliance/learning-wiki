## Evidence

### Wilson 2024

Wilson, J., Zhang, F., Palermo, C., Cordero, T. C., Myers, M. C., Eacker, H., Potter, A., & Coles, J. (2024). Predictors of middle school students’ perceptions of automated writing evaluation. Computers & Education, 211, 104985. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104985

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression results showing demographic predictors of perceptions. LEP, FRL, and grade were significant usability predictors at both timepoints. FRL predicted usefulness only in spring. No demographics predicted desirability.

> "the following variables were significant predictors of usability ratings both prior to students' exposure to MI Write (fall) and following treatment (spring): district fixed effects, teacher caring, LEP status, FRL status, grade level, liking writing beliefs, recursive process beliefs, and writing proficiency."
