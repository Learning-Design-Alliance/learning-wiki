Per-lesson multiple regressions on worksheet fill-in-blank scores. Lesson 1: F(1, 21) = 6.49, p < .05, R2 = 0.24; Lesson 2: F(1, 30) = 33.08, p < .01, R2 = 0.52, both significant. Lesson 3: F(1, 25) = 0.01, p = .94; Lesson 4: F(2, 25) = 1.28, p = .30, both non-significant.
