Adherence coding against the Trull and Ebner-Priemer (2020) reporting guidelines (Table 2): justify sample 40 (15.6%), justify sample size 12 (4.7%), explain rationale for sampling design 20 (7.8%), rationale for sampling and scheduling 36 (14.0%). The review concludes justifications were "rarely provided".
