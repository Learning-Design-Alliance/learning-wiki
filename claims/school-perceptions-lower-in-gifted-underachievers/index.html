Review synthesis of perceptions-of-school findings (§4.3.3), mostly using SAAS-R scales. The review reports underachievers scored significantly lower on attitudes towards school and teachers (Abu-Hamour & Al-Hmouz, 2013) and lower on environmental perceptions (Ritchotte et al., 2014), but "Figg et al. (2012) found no significant differences between groups on the same measures."
