### Panczyk M 2017

Panczyk M, Juszczyk G, Zarzeka A, Samoliński Ł, Belowska J, Cieślak I, Gotlib J. (2017). Evidence-based selection process to the Master of Public Health program at Medical University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1007-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 605 students. "For the graduates who earned the Bachelor's title outside the MUW, significantly lower first-year GPA were noted (βstand. = −0.241)"; the effect was slightly stronger for cumulative GPA (βstand. = −0.287, P < 0.0001).
