## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video analysis of 39 matched first and last simulation videos (94 nurses, 33 primary health centers, median 62 days apart), compared with generalized estimating equations adjusted for time in days. The article reports "increased from 1 to 2 (p = 0.03)" for history questions and "increased from 2 to 3 (p = 0.03)" for management steps.
