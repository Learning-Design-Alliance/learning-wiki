Chen et al. 2015¶

Chin-Chih Chen, Jill V. Hamm, & Thomas W. Farmer. (2015). Exceptionality and Peer Victimization Involvement in Late Childhood: Subtypes, Stability, and Social Marginalization. Remedial and Special Education, 36(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED588091

Descriptive stability percentages from the same longitudinal sample: well over half of students with disabilities were stably involved, nearly 40% of nondisabled students, and only a quarter of academically gifted students; "more than 40% of academically gifted students moved in and out of peer victimization involvement".
