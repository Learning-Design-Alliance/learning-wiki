Observational analysis of state staffing databases for the fall 2016 cohort of 1,188 Arizona, 473 Nevada, and 740 Utah principals. The report states "30 percent of principals in Utah remained in the same school compared with 43 percent in Nevada and 36 percent in Arizona."

> "From fall 2016 to fall 2020, 30 percent of principals in Utah remained in the same school compared with 43 percent in Nevada and 36 percent in Arizona (figure 1)."
