## Evidence

### K. Dunnett 2020

K. Dunnett, M. K. Kristiansson, G. Eklund, H. Öström, A. Rydh, and F. Hellberg. (2020). Transforming physics laboratory work from 'cookbook' type to genuine inquiry. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.12831](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.12831)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Following a recommendation from Imperial College, the developers recruited "a pair of second year students who had taken the thermodynamics course the previous year" to complete the new laboratory. The trial "found several weaknesses in the documentation" and gave teaching assistants delivery practice.

> "It conﬁrmed that the material created to support experi- mental work overall was appropriate and ac- cessible, and found several weaknesses in the documentation of the experiment."
