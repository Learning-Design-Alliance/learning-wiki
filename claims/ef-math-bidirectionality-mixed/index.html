Making the Executive 'Function' for the Foundations of Mathematics: the Need for Explicit Theories of Change for Early Interventions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09824-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Coolen et al. (2021), that "only EF surviving as a predictor of growth" once baseline mathematics was controlled, against studies reporting bidirectionality (e.g. Cameron et al. 2019; Schmitt et al. 2017).

"However, there is also some indication of asymmetries in early in childhood, with at least one study reporting concurrent and longitudinal relationships between EF and foundational mathematics skills in 4-year olds, but only EF surviving as a predictor of growth for these skills once baseline levels of mathematics skills were taken into account (Coolen et al., 2021)."
