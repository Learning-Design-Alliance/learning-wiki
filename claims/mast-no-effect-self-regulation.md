# MAST shows no effect on self-regulation in four-to-five-year-old children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The 12-week MAST programme produced no effect on self-regulation, an ability the article describes as underlying both language and movement skills. [→ Cunningham 2025](#cunningham-2025)

## Evidence

### Cunningham 2025

Cunningham, Anna J.; Baikousi, Violeta; Eyre, Emma; Duncan, Michael; Crotti, Matteo; Martins, Ricardo; Wood, Clare. (2025). A movement and story-telling intervention improves language and fundamental movement skills and is feasible for delivery by teachers in the first year of school. LEARNING AND INSTRUCTION. https://hdl.handle.net/10446/299265

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomised controlled trial of 214 children across nine primary schools; self-regulation was assessed at pre- and post-test alongside language and movement. The article reports "not effect on self-regulation"; no effect size is printed for this null finding.

> "There was a significant effect of MAST on language (d = 0.2) and fundamental movement skills (d = 0.65), but not effect on self-regulation."
