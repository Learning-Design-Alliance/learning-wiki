## Evidence

### Hall TR 2025

Hall TR, Mosley CF, Vakil JB, Lambert CK, Savelli DM, Balta JY. (2025). Who is teaching anatomy? An examination of the demographic characteristics, academic backgrounds, and professional responsibilities of US anatomy teachers. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis tests across full, associate, and assistant professors with Dunn's post hoc tests and Bonferroni correction; the article prints epsilon-squared values of 0.02 to 0.06 for these omnibus tests. Assistant professors had less assigned and perceived administration time but more teaching time than associate and full professors.

> "Significant differences existed in assigned administration (H(2) = 24.18, p < 0.01, ε 2 = 0.06), service (H(2) = 6.60, p = 0.04, ε 2 = 0.02), and teaching (H(2) = 20.18, p < 0.01, ε 2 = 0.05) percent time effort."
