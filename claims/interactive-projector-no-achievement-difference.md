## Evidence

### Li-Ying Liu 2015

Li-Ying Liu, Meng-Tzu Cheng. (2015). Interactive Projector as an Interactive Teaching Tool in the Classroom: Evaluating Teaching Efficiency and Interactivity. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057376

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental two-group posttest-only study of 126 7th-grade Taiwanese students; ANCOVA on the 31-item knowledge assessment with first midterm exam scores as covariate found no significant difference between groups. Means were nearly identical: "Mean=19.20, SD=6.72" versus "Mean=19.22, SD=7.28".

> "It is found that there is no significant difference in student knowledge acquisition between general group (Mean=19.20, SD=6.72) and interactive group (Mean=19.22, SD=7.28)."
