Evidence¶

López 2026¶

López, P., Fidalgo, R., Álvarez-Moreno, C., Díaz-Tejedor, R., van den Bergh, H., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2026). Compositional Analysis of Writing Instructional Practices Performed with Digital Tools: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10153-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Separate three-level meta-regressions with cluster-robust inference on the 42-study corpus tested each contextual variable; none was significant. Duration and intensity were also non-significant: "Duration, F(1, 33) = 0.508, p = .4810; Intensity, F(1, 22) = 0.254, p = .6195."

"None showed a statistically significant omnibus moderation effect: Language context, F(3, 38) = 0.245, p = .8645; Educational level, F(2, 39) = 2.128, p = .1327; Research design, F(2, 39) = 0.623, p = .5415."
