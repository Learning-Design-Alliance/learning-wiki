## Evidence

### Froelich 2012

Froelich, J., Breuer, D., Doepfner, M., & Amonn, F. (2012). Effects of a teacher training programme on symptoms of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. International Journal of Special Education, 27(3), 76-87. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001061

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 42 identified students assigned non-randomly to intervention (25 students, 8 teachers) or control (17 students, 8 teachers). Multilevel analysis with baseline covariate found "an intervention effect on ADHD symptoms, but not on ODD-symptoms".

> "the between-group analysis using baseline scores as covariates to control for group differences at baseline revealed an intervention effect on ADHD symptoms, but not on ODD-symptoms"
