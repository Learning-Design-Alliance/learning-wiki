# Sensitive and critical periods exist in child development: some skills are more readily acquired at certain stages, and later remediation is less effective

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some skills or traits are more readily acquired at certain stages of childhood, e.g., second languages learned before age 12 are spoken without an accent, and IQ scores become stable by around age 10. [→ Cunha 2007](#cunha-2007)
`q2 i?` Later remediation of disadvantage is less effective: Romanian orphans rescued later from deprivation had lower cognitive performance at age 6, and adult remediation programs have low economic returns. [→ Cunha 2007 (2)](#cunha-2007-2)

## Evidence

### Cunha 2007

Flavio Cunha and James Heckman. (2007). The Technology of Skill Formation. NBER Working Paper No. 12840. http://www.nber.org/papers/w12840

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reviews evidence for critical and sensitive periods, citing Newport on accent-free second language acquisition before age 12 and Pinker on syntax. IQ scores become stable by age 10, suggesting a sensitive period below age 10; noncognitive skills remain malleable into the early 20s.

> "For example, on average, if a second language is learned before age 12, the child speaks it without an accent (Elissa L. Newport, 1990). If syntax and grammar are not acquired early on, they appear to be very difficult to learn later on in life (Steven Pinker, 1994)."

### Cunha 2007 (2)

Flavio Cunha and James Heckman. (2007). The Technology of Skill Formation. NBER Working Paper No. 12840. http://www.nber.org/papers/w12840

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper cites the O'Connor et al. (2000) adoption study of Romanian orphans: later rescue from orphanage deprivation predicted lower age-6 cognitive performance. Public job training and GED programs show low economic returns and meager effects for most persons.

> "A study by Thomas G. O'Connor et al. (2000) of adopted Romanian infants reared in severely deprived orphanage environments before their adoption supports this claim. The later the Romanian orphan was rescued from the social and emotional isolation of the orphanage, the lower was his or her cognitive performance at age 6."
