## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Posttest comparisons showed no significant group differences for affection (p = 0.738), interest (p = 0.558), or importance of physics in daily life (p = 0.214). [→ Guzel 2016](#guzel-2016)

## Evidence

### Guzel 2016

Hatice Guzel. (2016). The effect of brightness of lamps teaching based on the 5E model on students' academic achievement and attitudes. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2915

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-tests on the 30-item Science Attitude Scale's three subfactors, each compared pre and post between the experimental (n=29) and control (n=33) groups; all posttest comparisons were non-significant. No effect sizes are printed.

> "As seen in Table 6, there is no s ignificant difference between groups (p =0.738 >0.05). This result shows that there are no changes in the attitudes of the students in terms of “affection” subfactor."
