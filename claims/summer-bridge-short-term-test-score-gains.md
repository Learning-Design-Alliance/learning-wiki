Melissa Roderick, Mimi Engel, Jenny Nagaoka, with Brian A. Jacob, Sophie Degener, Alex Orfei, Susan Stone, Jen Bacon. (2003). Ending Social Promotion: Results From Summer Bridge. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-results-summer-bridge

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report's evaluation of Chicago's mandatory Summer Bridge program (1997-2000) analyzed achievement of all participating students. It reports that sixth and eighth graders showed substantial gains, and in all three grades the rate of increase was above their school-year rate.

> "Sixth and eighth graders had substantial test-score gains. In all three grades, the rate at which Summer Bridge students increased their test scores was above their rate during the regular school year."
