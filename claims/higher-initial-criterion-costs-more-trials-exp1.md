## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rawson 2011

Rawson, K. A., & Dunlosky, J. (2011). Optimizing Schedules of Retrieval Practice for Durable and Efficient Learning: How Much Is Enough? Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 140(3), 283–302. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023956

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 1 efficiency analysis of total RMF trials per item combined across Sessions 1 and 2 for the four initial-criterion groups. The one-way ANOVA was significant with ηp2 = .16, consistent with higher criteria requiring more RMF trials during Session 1.

> "For total trials per item, a one-way ANOVA revealed a significant effect of initial learning criterion, F(3, 102) ⫽ 6.67, MSE ⫽ 3.50, p ⬍ .001, ␩p2 ⫽ .16."
