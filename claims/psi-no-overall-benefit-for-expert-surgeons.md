## Evidence

### Jones Gareth G. 2018

Jones Gareth G., Logishetty K., Clarke S., Collins R., Jaere M., Harris S., Cobb J. P. (2018). Do patient-specific instruments (PSI) for UKA allow non-expert surgeons to achieve the same saw cut accuracy as expert surgeons? Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-018-3031-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group comparison for the 4 expert surgeons in the sawbone experiment; Table 2 reports expert compound error 7.7° (2.3°) conventional vs 7.3° (4.1°) PSI, p = 0.849, with a significant coronal improvement (p = 0.037) but no sagittal or axial benefit.

> "For the expert group, PSI only reduced error in the coronal plane. There was no difference between the trainees and experts using PSI (Table 1)."
