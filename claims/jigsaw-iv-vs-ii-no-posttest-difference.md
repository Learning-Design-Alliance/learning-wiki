## Evidence

### Turkmen 2015

Turkmen, H. & Buyukaltay, D. (2015). Which one is better? Jigsaw II versus Jigsaw IV on the subject of the building blocks of matter and atom. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 1(2), 88-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105340

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-group t-test comparing post-test scores of the 24-student Jigsaw IV group (mean 7.12, SD 2.36) and 24-student Jigsaw II group (mean 7.95, SD 1.92); t = -1.340, p = 0.187. The article concludes "The Jigsaw IV technique and Jigsaw II technique ... have shown equally success".

> "A statistically significant difference at level of 0.05 was not obtained in the independent group t -test performed for the achievement post -tests of the control and experiment groups."
