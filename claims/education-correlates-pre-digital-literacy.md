## Evidence

### Martínez-Alcalá CI 2018

Martínez-Alcalá CI, Rosales-Lagarde A, Alonso-Lavernia MÁ, Ramírez-Salvador JÁ, Jiménez-Rodríguez B, Cepeda-Rebollar RM, López-Noguerola JS, Bautista-Díaz ML and Agis-Juárez RA (2018) Digital Inclusion in Older Adults: A Comparison Between Face-to-Face and Blended Digital Literacy Workshops. Front. ICT 5:21. https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00021

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the Results section relating years of education to pre SDLE scores for each group; r = 0.53 (FFG) and r = 0.34 (BLG, described as marginal). Age and education were inversely but non-significantly correlated in both groups.

> "Nevertheless, a positive correlation between years of education and the pre SDLE scores was found for the FFG and a marginal one for the BLG ( r = 0.53, p < 0.01; r = 0.34, p < 0.05, respectively)."

### Martínez-Alcalá CI 2018 (2)

Martínez-Alcalá CI, Rosales-Lagarde A, Alonso-Lavernia MÁ, Ramírez-Salvador JÁ, Jiménez-Rodríguez B, Cepeda-Rebollar RM, López-Noguerola JS, Bautista-Díaz ML and Agis-Juárez RA (2018) Digital Inclusion in Older Adults: A Comparison Between Face-to-Face and Blended Digital Literacy Workshops. Front. ICT 5:21. https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00021

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlations between age and pre SDLE scores were non-significant in both groups; no effect size beyond the printed r values is reported, and equivalence was not tested.

> "Neither between age and the pre FFG SDLE scores, nor between age and the pre BLG SDLE scores ( r = − 0.17, p > 0.10; r = − 0.01, p > 0.10, respectively)."
