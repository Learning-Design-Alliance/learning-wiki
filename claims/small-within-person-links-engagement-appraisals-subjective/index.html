Evidence¶

Breitwieser 2026¶

Breitwieser, J., Brod, G., & Malmberg, L.-E. (2026). Testing a three-phase model of the relationship between motivational appraisals, observable engagement, and subjective engagement: Insights from combining mobile learning data and daily diaries. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001009

q2 · i0 · associational · r1

Pooled within-person correlations from 3,149 data points of 189 participants, computed on person-centred deviation scores. Engagement correlated with expectancy "(r = .05, p < .05)" and value, not cost; satisfaction r = .07 and task delay r = -.07 were also significant but rather small.

"At the within-person level, vocabulary engagement was positively correlated with expectancy (r = .05, p < .05) and value (r = .05, p < .05), but not with cost (r = -.01, n.s.)."
