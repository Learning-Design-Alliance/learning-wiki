# Whether student GenAI use constitutes academic misconduct depends fundamentally on the prior question of the university's purpose

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The question of whether students' use of GenAI in higher education constitutes academic misconduct depends on what the purpose of higher education is taken to be; characterisations of integrity and misconduct are secondary to it. [→ Tristan B. Taylor & Travis LaCroix 2026](#tristan-b-taylor-travis-lacroix-2026)
`q1 i?` If a university continues to fulfil its function regardless of students' AI use, then calling GenAI use misconduct is a category mistake; if GenAI use does constitute misconduct, institutional promotion of GenAI in pedagogy would be self-defeating. [→ Tristan B. Taylor & Travis LaCroix 2026](#tristan-b-taylor-travis-lacroix-2026)
