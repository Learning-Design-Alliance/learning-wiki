Fontes Carlos Edmundo Rodrigues, Castilio Rafael Fernandez, Moreno Félix Plaza, Aboboreira Mateus de Amorim, de Lima Maria Luíza Bernardo, Mansano Yves Henrique Ramos, Bodgan Claudio, Cavazana Willian Cesar, Massago Miyoko, Gurgel Sanderland José Tavares, de Andrade Luciano. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-group post-intervention usability study of 35 critical-care graduate healthcare professionals performing simulated pediatric bladder catheterization with Vuzix M400 glasses and remote telementoring. Descriptive analysis showed medians of "3.5 to 5 on a 0–7 scale" across the seven-item questionnaire.

> "Median responses ranged from 3.5 to 5 on a 0–7 scale. Items related to procedural facilitation, image quality, and understanding showed higher median values (up to 5), while items related to comfort and efficiency presented median values of 3.5, corresponding to neutral perceptions."
