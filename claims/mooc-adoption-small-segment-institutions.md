### I. Elaine Allen and Jeff Seaman with Russell Poulin and Terri Taylor Straut 2016

I. Elaine Allen and Jeff Seaman with Russell Poulin and Terri Taylor Straut. (2016). Online Report Card - Tracking Online Education in the United States. Babson Survey Research Group. https://onlinelearningconsortium.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

National survey of institutions on MOOC activity, 2015 administration. The report states many institutions (27.8%) remain undecided while the single largest group (58.7%) say they have no plans for a MOOC.

> "The percent of higher education institutions that currently have a MOOC increased from 2.6% in 2012 to 5.0% in 2013, to 8.0%  in 2014, and now stands at 11.3%."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey item asked of all institutions, with and without MOOCs, on belief in MOOC sustainability. The report suggests remaining institutions may be deciding against MOOCs because of sustainability doubts.

> "The number of institutions saying that they believed MOOCs to be sustainable fell from 28.3% in 2012 to only 16.3% in 2014."
