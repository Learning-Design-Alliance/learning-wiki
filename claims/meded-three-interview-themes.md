Semi-structured interviews conducted on Microsoft Teams, transcribed verbatim and analysed inductively in NVivo 14 following Braun and Clarke's stages of thematic analysis; themes are summarised in Fig. 3.

> "A total of 13 participants were interviewed, including 5 who were both student-attendees and student-teachers. Three themes emerged: academic value, highlighting knowledge and skills gained through MedED; career prospects, focusing on long-term professional benefits; and sense of community and wellbeing"
