# On a high-difficulty energy problem, GPT-4o scored the written arguments of students who answered the multiple-choice question correctly significantly higher than those who answered incorrectly (Cohen's d = 0.87)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` LLM-generated argument scores on Quiz 08 were significantly higher for students who answered the MC question correctly (2.14) than incorrectly (1.12), with Cohen's d = 0.87. [→ Winter Allen 2025](#winter-allen-2025)

## Evidence

### Winter Allen 2025

Winter Allen, Anand Shanker, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Students' Perceptions to a Large Language Model's Generated Feedback and Scores of Argumentation Essays. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.allen](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.allen)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Table I of this exploratory classroom study reports average LLM scores for Quiz 08, where students (N=730) solved a high difficulty problem with an MC average of about 47%. Correct answerers averaged 2.14 versus 1.12 for incorrect, with p << 0.001 and "Cohen's d" of 0.87 from a t-test.

> "Quiz 08 1.69 ± 1.29 2.14 ± 1.24 1.12 ± 1.11 << 0.001 0.87"
