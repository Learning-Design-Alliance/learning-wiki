## Evidence

### Chen Yiwen 2025

Chen Yiwen, Song Jiali, Li Mengying, He Huijuan, Wang Xiangrong, Zhou Siyu, Wang Ling. (2025). Integrating jigsaw teaching into self-regulated learning instruction: an instructional design to improve nursing students’ self-regulated learning. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1437265

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent and paired t-tests on the 17-item Learning Engagement Scale in the two-class quasi-experiment. The article reports the total and dedication scores "significantly higher than those of the traditional teaching method (p < 0.05)", with printed Cohen's d = 0.477 (total) and 0.565 (dedication); concentration was not significant (p = 0.157).

> "However, after the intervention, the total learning engagement and dedication dimension scores of nursing students in the intervention group were significantly higher than those of the traditional teaching method (p < 0.05)."
