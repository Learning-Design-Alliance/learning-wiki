# Children's own polygenic index for educational attainment is negatively associated with their depressive traits at age 8

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` A one-S.D. change in the child's education PGI corresponded to a 0.04 S.D. decrease in depressive traits. [→ Hughes 2024](#hughes-2024)

## Evidence

### Hughes 2024

Hughes, A. M., Torvik, F. A., van Bergen, E., Hannigan, L. J., Corfield, E. C., Andreassen, O. A., Ystrom, E., Ask, H., Davey Smith, G., Davies, N. M. & Havdahl, A. (2024). Parental education and children’s depression, anxiety, and ADHD traits, a within-family study in MoBa. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00260-8

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Within-family MR in MoBa trios adjusting for parental PGIs. The child's education PGI negatively associated with depressive traits: "a decrease of 0.04 S.D." per PGI S.D. (p = 0.001), about 0.1 point on the 26-point Short Mood and Feelings Questionnaire. The authors describe the effect as small.

> "Theeffect was small: a one-S.D. change in the PGI corresponded to a decrease of 0.04 S.D. (CI = 0.02,−0.02,p = 0.001) in depressive traits, or around 0.1 point on a 26-point scale (Fig.3)."
