Evidence¶

Arnold 2025¶

Arnold, J., Hayes, S., & Zagata, E. (2025). Leading inclusive, coherent, and effective assessment systems: Closing opportunity and outcome gaps for students with disabilities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673562

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The brief cites federal civil rights data showing discipline disproportionality: students with disabilities were "16 percent of the total national enrollment population in 2018" yet 25–28 percent of suspensions and expulsions, and were also overrepresented among restraint and seclusion cases.

"Although students with disabilities represented 16 percent of the total national enrollment population in 2018, they made up 25 percent of students who received an in-school suspension, 28 percent of students who received an out-of-school suspension, and 25 percent of students who were expelled (ED, 2021)."
