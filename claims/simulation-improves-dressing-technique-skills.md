`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wound-care domain of the same before-and-after quasi-experiment (Table 3): mechanical cleaning of intact periwound skin 27(54.0) to 50(100.0), D = 0.87; hand hygiene before dressing 28(56.0) to 48(96.0), p<0.001, D = 0.91; aseptic/sterile technique p=0.007, D = 0.55.

> "After the intervention, significant frequencies of adjustments were observed, with emphasis on items related to cleaning the incision, wound margins and periwound skin in unidirectional movements."
