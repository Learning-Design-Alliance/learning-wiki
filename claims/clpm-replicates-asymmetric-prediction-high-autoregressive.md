## Evidence

### Bailey et al. 2020

Drew H. Bailey, Yoonkyung Oh, George Farkas, Paul Morgan, Marianne Hillemeier. (2020). Reciprocal Effects of Reading and Mathematics? Beyond the Cross-Lagged Panel Model. Developmental Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/dev0000902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

CLPM fitted to the same ECLS-K:2011 K-3 sample (N=9,612). The model shows "higher cross-lagged paths from math to reading than from reading to math" and autoregressive paths of .74 to .81, matching prior CLPM research.

> "In the CLPM, we replicate previous findings of asymmetric prediction and high autoregressive paths (Duncan et al., 2007), with higher cross-lagged paths from math to reading than from reading to math, and very high autoregressive paths ranging from .74 to .81."
