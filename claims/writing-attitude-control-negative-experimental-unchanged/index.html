Evidence¶

Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018¶

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent samples t-test on the Turkish I Writing Expression Course Attitude Scale (alpha .90/.95 in this study) for the control group (n=48). The article concludes the writing attitude changed negatively.

"the control group writing attitude pre-test mean score was 3.78, and the posttest mean score was 3.52. It was observed that there was a significant difference between these two scores [t(47)=2.529;p<.05]"

Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018 (2)¶

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent samples t-test on the same attitude scale for the Facebook experimental group (n=48). The article reports no significant difference and concludes writing attitudes did not change.

"the experimental group writing attitude pre -test mean score was 3.60, and the posttest mean score was 3.63. It was observed that there was no significant difference between these two scores [t(47)=-.427;p>.05]"
