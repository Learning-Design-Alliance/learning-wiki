# One case-study student who engaged in self-regulated learning improved from 18-24% below the class average on early tests to 6% below by the fourth test, with an FCI normalized gain of 0.70 versus 0.34 for students with similar scientific reasoning scores.

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Isaac, an at-risk student who described engaging in self-regulated learning, improved his in-class test performance and showed a much higher FCI normalized gain (0.70 vs 0.34) than students with similar scientific reasoning scores. [→ Jeffrey A. Phillips 2016](#jeffrey-a-phillips-2016)

## Evidence

### Jeffrey A. Phillips 2016

Jeffrey A. Phillips. (2016). Student self-assessment and reflection in a learner controlled environment. arXiv:1608.00313v1 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.055](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.055)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Single-student case study from the Results, Case Study subsection. Isaac entered at risk (GPA 0.3 below class average, CTSR 63%), scored 18-24% below the class average on the first three tests, and 6% below on the fourth. The causal attribution is the author's interpretation.

> "His FCI normalized gain was much higher than students with similar scientiﬁc reasoning scores (0.70 vs 0.34) [10]. Perhaps because of his self-regulated learning, he exceeded expectations."
