Obafemi 2015¶

Obafemi, D. T. A. (2015). Bridging Gender Gap in the Physics Classroom: The Instructional Method Perspective. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079043

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A 4x2 ANCOVA of students' application of Light waves classified by instructional methods and gender, using pretest scores as a covariate. The article reports "Gender is significant" with F = 4.052 exceeding the critical value of 4.00; no effect size is printed.
