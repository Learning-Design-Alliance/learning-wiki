### Nelskamp Arne 2021

Nelskamp Arne, Schnurr Benedikt, Germanyuk Alexandra, Sterz Jasmina, Lorenz Jonas, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Seifert Lukas B. (2021). Comparison of ‘Mental training’ and physical practice in the mediation of a structured facial examination: a quasi randomized, blinded and controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02603-0

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation offered in the Discussion to reconcile heterogeneous findings in the surgical education literature, where some studies of laparoscopic knot-tying and suturing found no significant effect of Mental Training. This is a hypothesis, not a tested result in this study.

> "This difference in complexity might explain why ‘Mental Training’ is especially suitable for more complex motor skills. Future studies have to investigate for what degree of complexity and which motor skills in particular mental training is superior compared to physical practice"
