## Evidence

### Sabrina Ludwig 2026

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

T-tests on cognitive load measures collected via scenario-embedded experience sampling and post-scenario questionnaires. The PS-DI group showed "significantly higher IICL than DI-PS" with d = .53; retrospective intrinsic load did not differ (p = .59).

> "The PS-DI rated the task during the PS scenario itself as more difficult and, hence, showed significantly higher IICL than DI-PS. A moderate effect size for IICL can be reported (d = .53)."
