The abstract cites prior published analyses of the non-longitudinal PRIMR dataset, attributing the findings to those studies: "Impacts ranged from .3 to .5 standard deviations." The cited studies were not read for this extraction.

> "Previous research using the non-longitudinal dataset from t he PRIMR study shows statistically significant impacts on English and Kiswahili outcomes (Piper, Zuilkowski & Mugenda, 2014); for the poor (Piper, Jepkemei & Kibukho, 2014); and particularly in schools provided additional instructional support by TAC tutors (Piper & Zuilkowski, 2014). Impacts ranged from .3 to .5 standard deviations (Piper & Mugenda, 2014)."
