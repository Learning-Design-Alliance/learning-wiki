# Both astronomy self-efficacy scales show high internal consistency and additivity, with Cronbach's alpha of 0.896 for APSE and 0.942 for ISE in the combined cohorts

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The eight-item APSE scale reached Cronbach's alpha 0.896 and the five-item ISE scale 0.942 for both cohorts combined, with nonsignificant additivity tests and Tukey indices near 1. [→ Rachel Freed 2022](#rachel-freed-2022)

## Evidence

### Rachel Freed 2022

Rachel Freed, David McKinnon, Michael Fitzgerald, and Christina M. Norris. (2022). Development and validation of an astronomy self-efficacy instrument for understanding and doing. Physical Review Physics Education Research 18, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.18.010117

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis in SPSS v26 on the combined cohorts (N = 301 per Table IV) applying criteria of alpha above 0.7 and a Tukey additivity index close to 1. The same table shows the ISE scale "comprising five items for both cohorts possess a Cronbach's α ¼ 0.942" with a nonsignificant additivity test.

> "Table IV shows that for the APSE potential scale for both cohorts in which eight items are employed, Cronbach's α is 0.896 with a nonsignificant test of additivity [ Fð1; 300Þ¼ 0.012, p ¼ 0.913] and a Tukey's estimate of power to achieve additivity ¼ 0.985."

### Rachel Freed 2022 (2)

Rachel Freed, David McKinnon, Michael Fitzgerald, and Christina M. Norris. (2022). Development and validation of an astronomy self-efficacy instrument for understanding and doing. Physical Review Physics Education Research 18, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.18.010117

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analyses computed separately for cohort A (N = 243, robotic-telescope lab course) and cohort B (N = 58, nonlab course); the article reports both scales behaved acceptably in each cohort separately.

> "Moreover, Table IV also shows that the potential scales for the separate cohorts of students A and B who had, respectively, used and not used robotic telescopes, also behave well in terms of reliability and additivity."
