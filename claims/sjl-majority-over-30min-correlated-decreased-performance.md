## Evidence

### Smarr 2018

Smarr, B. L., & Schirmer, A. E. (2018). 3.4 million real-world learning management system logins reveal the majority of students experience social jet lag correlated with decreased performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-23044-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of two years of LMS login events for 14,894 students at Northeastern Illinois University. The abstract reports that "a majority of students experience more than 30 minutes of SJL on average, with greater amplitude correlating strongly with a significant decrease in academic performance". No standardized effect size is printed for the overall correlation.

> "revealed a majority of students experience more than 30 minutes of SJL on average, with greater amplitude correlating strongly with a significant decrease in academic performance, especially in people with later apparent chronotypes"
