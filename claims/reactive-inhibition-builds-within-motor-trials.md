## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Within-trial RT slowing, evidence of reactive inhibition, was statistically significant in all four trial-length/break-length groups, with effect sizes from d = −0.51 to d = −1.32. [→ Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard 2022](#mohan-w-gupta-and-timothy-c-rickard-2022)

## Evidence

### Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard 2022

Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard. (2022). Dissipation of reactive inhibition is sufficient to explain post-rest improvements in motor sequence learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00140-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the experiment, 30 s trials were split into three 10 s bins and first-versus-third-bin mean RTs compared with paired-samples t-tests; RI was significant in both 30 s trial groups ("d = −0.56" and d = −0.51). The 10 s trial groups also showed RI (d = −1.32 and d = −0.98).

> "A paired-samples t-test, averaged over all practice trials, yielded evidence of RI in both the 30 s break group, t(36) = −3.42, p = 0.0016, d = −0.56, and the 10 s break group, t(40) = −3.26, p = 0.0023, d = −0.51."
