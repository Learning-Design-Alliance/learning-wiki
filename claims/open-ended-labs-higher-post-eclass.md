## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Aggregate comparison of matched pre/post E-CLASS scores across N = 4915 students in 147 course instances; post means 17.5 (open-ended, N = 1149) vs 14.4 (guided, N = 3766), "p ≪ 0.01", d = 0.4.

> "Table III shows that students in courses using open-ended activities scored significantly higher than students in courses using only traditional guided labs both pre- and postinstruction ( p ≪ 0.01)."
