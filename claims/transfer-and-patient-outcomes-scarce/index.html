q3 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis using Kirkpatrick's framework across 104 studies; the review reports only 2 studies at level 3 (clinical intubation performance, telesupervised ultrasound scans) and 1 at level 4 (postoperative visual acuity after cataract surgery training).

"Only 2 studies involved physicians' behaviors (Kirkpatrick level 3, 2 points): one on intubation performance during clinical anesthesia29 and another on telesupervised ultrasound scans.31 A single study reported health care outcomes (Kirkpatrick level 4, 3 points) of patients' postoperative visual acuity following cataract surgery training32"
