## Evidence

### Stephen B. Gilbert 2015

Stephen B. Gilbert, Stephen B. Blessing, Enruo Guo. (2015). Authoring Effective Embedded Tutors: An Overview of the Extensible Problem Specific Tutor (xPST) System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:428–454. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0045-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison study with sixteen participants classified as programmers or non-programmers, each building three tutors in statistics and geometry with either xPST or CTAT. The quote reports no quality differences by programming background.

> "no differences were observed between the tutors produced by the programmer and non-programmers, with all but 2 tutors (one xPST tutor and one CTA T tutor, both done by non-programmers) being judged at the highest caliber."

### Stephen B. Gilbert 2015 (2)

Stephen B. Gilbert, Stephen B. Blessing, Enruo Guo. (2015). Authoring Effective Embedded Tutors: An Overview of the Extensible Problem Specific Tutor (xPST) System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:428–454. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0045-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Timing data from the same sixteen-author comparison. The quote reports CTAT's early time advantage disappearing by the third problem.

> "the CTA T authors spent less time constructing their tutors in the beginning, but by the third tutor problem there were no significant time differences, with all but one participant spending no more than 25 min per problem, regardless of tool."
