# Children born preterm score significantly lower than term-born peers on aggregate standardized reading measures

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Preterm children show significant deficits in aggregate reading measures relative to term-born peers (MD, −7.98; 95% CI, −13.05 to −2.91). [→ McBryde M 2020](#mcbryde-m-2020)

## Evidence

### McBryde M 2020

McBryde M, Fitzallen GC, Liley HG, Taylor HG, Bora S. (2020). Academic Outcomes of School-Aged Children Born Preterm: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2020.2027

`q3 · i?`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 33 cohort/cross-sectional studies pooling mean differences on standardized tests normed to a mean (SD) of 100 (15). The pooled reading difference was "MD, −7.98; 95% CI, −13.05 to −2.91" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 92%).

> "The meta-analysis found preterm children are at risk of significant academic difficulties in aggregate measures of reading (MD, −7.98; 95% CI, −13.05 to −2.91; I2 = 92%)"
