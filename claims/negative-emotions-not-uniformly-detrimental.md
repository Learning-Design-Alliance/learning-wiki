### Reinhard Pekrun 2024

Reinhard Pekrun. (2024). Overcoming Fragmentation in Motivation Science: Why, When, and How Should We Integrate Theories? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09846-5

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the commentary's integration-versus-differentiation section, drawing on the cognitive-motivational model of emotion effects within control-value theory. The author argues that "Simply characterizing emotions like anxiety, confusion, or shame as detrimental to engagement and achievement may not be sufficient."

> "anxiety can deplete students’ working memory resources and undermine their interest and intrinsic motivation, but can boost extrinsic motivation to invest effort in order to avoid failure. Furthermore, anxiety can promote detail-oriented, analytic thinking which can be helpful on some types of tasks."
