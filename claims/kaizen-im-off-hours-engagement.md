Nevin Christa R, Westfall Andrew O, Rodriguez J Martin, Dempsey Donald M, Cherrington Andrea, Roy Brita, Patel Mukesh, Willig James H. (2014). Gamification as a tool for enhancing graduate medical education. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2013-132486

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Software-log analysis of Kaizen-IM use during the 2012–2013 season: participants answered 16,427 questions, with round participation of 92, 90 and 55 players and question completion rates of 71, 69 and 66 per cent in rounds 1–3. The logs show "28.8% of questions were done on Saturdays and Sundays while 53.1% were completed between 17:00 and 08:00".
