Núñez-Regueiro 2024¶

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

The study's summary substantive conclusion from the cubic RSA of TIMSS 2019 data: SDT predictions were "close to universally true (93% of students)", with nonlinear and interactive processes qualifying generalization to extreme motivational states.

"these findings demonstrate that predictions based on SDT are close to universally true (93% of students), although they may not generalize well to extreme states of autonomous or controlled motivation (nonlinear and interactive processes)."
