### Bolatli 2026 (2)

Bolatli, G., S. Birisci, and Z. Bolatli. 2026. An Alternative Approach in Anatomy Education: Design of a Learning Environment Based on Artificial Intelligence‐Supported Virtual Manipulatives and Investigation of Its Effectiveness. Clinical Anatomy 39, no. 1: 30–44. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Students reported ChatGPT used "terminology not typically associated with anatomical" (S21), gave different answers to rephrased questions (S19), and could not answer turuncus cerebri (S22); S23 wanted more comprehensive manipulative content.

> "The majority of negative opinions regarding AI were technical, particularly, focusing on the feedback generated by ChatGPT."
