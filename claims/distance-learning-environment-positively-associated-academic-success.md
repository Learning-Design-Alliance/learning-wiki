## Evidence

### Ghasempour Saeed 2023

Ghasempour Saeed, Esmaeeli Maede, Abbasi Ali, Hosseinzadeh Ali, Ebrahimi Hossein. (2023). Relationship between academic success, distance education learning environments, and its related factors among medical sciences students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04856-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 208 medical sciences students using Pearson correlation. Table 2 reports r = 0.306 between academic success and the distance education learning environment, significant at the 0.01 level; the "positive and significant correlation" spans all components except student perception.

> "Pearson’s correlation coefficient results found a positive and significant correlation between academic success, distance education learning environment, and all its principal components except student perception (P < 0.05)."

### Ghasempour Saeed 2023 (2)

Ghasempour Saeed, Esmaeeli Maede, Abbasi Ali, Hosseinzadeh Ali, Ebrahimi Hossein. (2023). Relationship between academic success, distance education learning environments, and its related factors among medical sciences students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04856-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Enter method) on the 208-student survey; the model explained 18.17% of variance in academic success. The printed unstandardized coefficient is 0.233 (SE 0.054, t 4.33, P < 0.001); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "This model showed that for each unit of increase in distance education learning environment, students’ mean academic success score increases by 0.233 units (P < 0.001)."
