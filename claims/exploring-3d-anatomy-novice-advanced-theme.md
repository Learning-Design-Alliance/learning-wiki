## Evidence

### Zhang J 2026

Zhang J, Sachdeva M, McCaig C, Gordon E, Shapiro L, Keenan ID. (2026). Exploring 3D anatomy: A free online course supporting novice learner inquiry and spatial conceptualization. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70288

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological focus group study with eight undergraduate medical students (Focus Group 1, n = 6, July 2024; Focus Group 2, n = 2, November 2024) at a UK medical school, analyzed using double-coding and interpretative phenomenological analysis. Participants described the course as "most valuable for novice students" and suggested an induction-type group activity early in the program, while advanced participants requested "more clinical application" such as laparoscopic-perspective content.

> "Participants provided their views of the relative value of Exploring 3D Anatomy to distinct target audiences, describing how they felt Exploring 3D Anatomy would be most valuable for novice students, who are in the early stages of their anatomy studies."
