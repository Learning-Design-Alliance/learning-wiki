### Ong Kelly 2023

Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same construct validity study, participants were asked to identify and sample the hyper-echoic 'abnormal' region on ultrasound, with a maximum of 3 attempts. The study reports "none of the novice participants identified the target lesion (0%)" versus all experts (p = 0.01); no effect size was printed. Experts averaged 69.7 s to identify the lesion.
