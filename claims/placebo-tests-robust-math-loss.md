`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Robustness analysis re-estimating the main regression with a departure dummy for every PISA cycle, with 95% confidence intervals (Fig. 2). Specifications with and without controls, quadratic trends, dropped years, and survey versus senate weights produced nearly identical results.

> "Figure 2 shows that although estimates for different years vary, representing small variation in results of different PISA rounds, only the departure in 2022 is substantial."
