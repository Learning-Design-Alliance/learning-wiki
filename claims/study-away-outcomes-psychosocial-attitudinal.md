Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley. (2024). Domestic Study Away in Higher Education: A Systematic Literature Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241279296

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review classified 144 outcomes in 49 publications using Mayhew et al.'s (2016) eight categories. It reports "Psychosocial Change was the most common outcome category, representing 34% of the outcomes, and in 57% of the publications", with Attitudes/Values selected for almost 30% of outcomes and found in 65% of publications.
