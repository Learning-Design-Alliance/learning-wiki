### B. P. Johnson 2023

B. P. Johnson, I. Iturrate, R. Y. Fakhreddine, M. Bönstrup, E. R. Buch, E. M. Robertson and L. G. Cohen. (2023). Generalization of procedural motor sequence learning after a single practice trial. npj Science of Learning 8:45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00194-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 (n = 537) randomly assigned one trial of skill A followed by four skill B variants sharing parsing rule, transition, ordinal+transition structure, or identity. "higher in the SAME A and PARSING RULE than in the TRANSITION group (both p < 0.001; FWE-corrected)".

> "Performance at the onset of skill B was signi ﬁcantly different between groups (Kruskal-Wallis test: X2 (3, 533) = 25.305, p < 0.001; Fig. 3c), higher in the SAME A and PARSING RULE than in the TRANSITION group (both p < 0.001; FWE-corrected)."

### B. P. Johnson 2023 (2)
