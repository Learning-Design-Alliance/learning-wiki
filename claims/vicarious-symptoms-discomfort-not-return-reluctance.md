### Rybak Alexis 2025

Rybak Alexis, Decavele Maxens, Taytard Jessica, Allaire Manon, Sabourdin Nada, Serresse Laure, Nathan Nadia, Tamzali Yanis, Renaud Marie-Christine, Carrié Alain, Foy Jean-Philippe, Monsel Antoine, Bottemanne Hugo. (2025). When simulation becomes physical: vicarious symptoms in standardized patients during osces. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07950-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of discomfort reports between SPs with and without VSs on the exam day in the cohort. The article reports "8/18 [31%] versus 22/198 [11%], p = 0.01"; no effect size is printed.

> "Participants who experienced VSs during the OSCE day reported significantly more frequently discomfort during the OSCE role-playing (8/18 [31%] versus 22/198 [11%], p = 0.01)."

### Rybak Alexis 2025 (2)

Rybak Alexis, Decavele Maxens, Taytard Jessica, Allaire Manon, Sabourdin Nada, Serresse Laure, Nathan Nadia, Tamzali Yanis, Renaud Marie-Christine, Carrié Alain, Foy Jean-Philippe, Monsel Antoine, Bottemanne Hugo. (2025). When simulation becomes physical: vicarious symptoms in standardized patients during osces. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07950-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of willingness to return: 43/224 (19%) did not imagine participating the following year just after the OSCE, and 45/200 (22%) after one week. The article reports no correlation with VSs; equivalence was not tested.

> "We found no correlation between VSs and volunteering for participating in the following year."
