## Evidence

### E. Tufino 2025

E. Tufino, S. Oss, and M. Alemani. (2025). Using Jupyter Notebooks to foster computational skills and professional practice in an introductory physics lab course. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012022](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012022)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rubric-scored review exercises (S3) assigned at the beginning of the second semester after a three-month interruption, testing retention of computational skills. The article also notes "the presence of some groups scoring 1 (inadequate) underscores the variability in skill retention"; no effect size is printed.

> "The analysis shows that about 83% of the groups scored 2 or 3 in both 'Implement' and 'Communicate,' showing that students' mastery of the skills imparted in the previous semester lasted over the break."
