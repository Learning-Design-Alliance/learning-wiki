`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of the spiritual education courses factor across included cross-sectional studies (Table 2: random effects model, OR 2.68, 95% CI 2.28–3.14, p < 0.01). The quoted sentence reports the direction without a standardised effect size.

> "A meta‐analysis of whether the nurses had received spiritual education courses revealed that nurses who had received spiritual education scored higher in spiritual care ability."
