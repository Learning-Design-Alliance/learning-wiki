## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2020

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Hermione and the Secretary: How gendered task division in introductory physics labs can disrupt equitable learning. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab7831](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab7831)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic observation finding across the twelve observed lab sections. The authors report that "the symmetry breaking and 'phase separation' into different roles generally does not seem to occur in same-gender groups," and that same-gender partners tend to adopt similar types and amounts of work. No effect size is reported.

> "Our observations suggest that unlike in mixed-gender groups, the symmetry breaking and "phase separation" into different roles generally does not seem to occur in same-gender groups."
