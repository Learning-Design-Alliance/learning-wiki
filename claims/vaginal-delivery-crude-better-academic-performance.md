`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate analysis in a descriptive cross-sectional study of 481 pupils in Ogbomoso, Nigeria, using weighted academic scores from school records. The article reports a "statistically significant bivariate association" with vaginal delivery performing better; no effect size is printed.

> "Mode of delivery also showed a statistically significant bivariate association, with vaginally delivered pupils performing better (p = 0.041)."
