# A path model shows HIV status relates to worse educational outcomes through cascades of cognitive, mental-health and physical-health difficulties

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an exploratory path model with good fit, HIV status was associated with cognitive difficulties (β = 0.114), worse physical health (β = 0.142) and direct effects on educational delay and missing school for clinics. [→ Toska Elona 2019](#toska-elona-2019)
`q2 i?` Cognitive difficulties and physical health issues in turn were associated with worse mental health, absenteeism and educational delay, forming cascades to poorer educational experiences. [→ Toska Elona 2019 (2)](#toska-elona-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Toska Elona 2019

Toska Elona, Cluver Lucie, Orkin Mark, Bains Anurita, Sherr Lorraine, Berezin McKenzie, Gulaid Laurie. (2019). Screening and supporting through schools: educational experiences and needs of adolescents living with HIV in a South African cohort. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-019-6580-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory structural path model (STATA15) on the cohort, retaining only significant pathways with unstandardised β weights; all constructs were observed variables. The cognitive-difficulties branch shows HIV status leading through cognition to mental health, absenteeism and delay.

> "Adolescents living with HIV experienced higher levels of cognitive difficulties (β = 0.114, p < 0.001), which in turn were associated with worse mental health (β = 0.223, p < 0.001), higher rates of absenteeism (β = 0.084, p < 0.001) and educational delay (β = 0.284, p < 0.001)."

### Toska Elona 2019 (2)

Toska Elona, Cluver Lucie, Orkin Mark, Bains Anurita, Sherr Lorraine, Berezin McKenzie, Gulaid Laurie. (2019). Screening and supporting through schools: educational experiences and needs of adolescents living with HIV in a South African cohort. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-019-6580-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The physical-health branch of the same path model shows worse physical health exacerbating cognitive difficulties, mental health, absenteeism and educational delay; the final model "had good fit for all pre-selected criteria (RMSEA = 0.026, CFI = 0.981 and TLI = 0.949, χ2/df = 2.01)".

> "In a separate branch of the model, adolescents living with HIV experienced worse physical health issues (β = 0.142, p < 0.001), which exacerbated cognitive difficulties (β = 0.198, p < 0.001), mental health (β = 0.168, p < 0.001), absenteeism (β = 0.064, p < 0.001) and educational delay (β = 0.107, p < 0.001)."
