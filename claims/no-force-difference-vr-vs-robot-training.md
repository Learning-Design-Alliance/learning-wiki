## Evidence

### von Bechtolsheim Felix 2024

von Bechtolsheim Felix, Franz Andreas, Schmidt Sofia, Schneider Alfred, La Rosée Felicitas, Radulova-Mauersberger Olga, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Hümpel Anja, Bodenstedt Sebastian, Speidel Stefanie, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian. (2024). The development of tissue handling skills is sufficient and comparable after training in virtual reality or on a surgical robotic system: a prospective randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10842-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled trial of 87 robotic novices randomized to FRS training on a DaVinci VR simulator (n=43) or a real DaVinci robotic system (n=44), with force measured via a force-measuring device. The article reports that "the force application was not different between groups" at Midterm and Final tests; no effect size is printed.

> "In the Midterm and Final test, the force application was not different between groups. Both groups displayed sufficient learning curves with significant improvement of force application."
