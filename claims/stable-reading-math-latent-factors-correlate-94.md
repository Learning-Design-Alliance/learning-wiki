## Evidence

### Bailey et al. 2020

Drew H. Bailey, Yoonkyung Oh, George Farkas, Paul Morgan, Marianne Hillemeier. (2020). Reciprocal Effects of Reading and Mathematics? Beyond the Cross-Lagged Panel Model. Developmental Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/dev0000902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

RI-CLPM variance decomposition of ECLS-K:2011 K-3 reading and math scores (N=9,612). The stable-factor correlation is "near unity (φ = .94)"; the article reports this as evidence that unchanging individual and environmental factors affecting both domains substantially overlap.

> "The correlation between stable math and reading factors is near unity (φ = .94), consistent with the interpretation that the empirically stable individual and environmental factors affecting children's math and reading achievement similarly throughout this developmental period substantially overlap."
