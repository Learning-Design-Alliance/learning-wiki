## Evidence

### Alba López-Moraga 2024

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Reinstatement test 24 h after an unsignaled 0.4 mA foot shock in Experiment 2. The article reports the group difference on lever-press suppression with the printed effect size ηp2 = 0.175 (Table 3), which is large (i = 3); freezing, rearing, and avoidance showed no significant group or day effects.

> "In Experiment 2, we observed a signiﬁcant difference in the suppression of lever pressing between Avoiders and their Yoked counterparts ( Q(1, 13.94) = 5.58, p = 0.042), with Yoked animals showingmore suppression than Avoiders in the reinstatement test."

### Alba López-Moraga 2024 (2)

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reinstatement test in Experiment 3, which used a doubled 0.8 mA reinstatement shock and only two extinction sessions. Contrary to expectation, fear measures decreased rather than increased; no significant group differences were observed.

> "Against our expectations, we even saw a signiﬁcant decrease in freezing ( Q(1, 11.33) = 6.72, p = 0.024) and in suppression of lever pressing ( F(1, 21) = 5.31, p = 0.031) from the last extinction session to the reinstatement test."
