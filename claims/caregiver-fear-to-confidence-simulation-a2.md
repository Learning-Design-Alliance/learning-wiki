`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive-exploratory qualitative study A2 (level VI) with 15 caregivers of children with special health needs, using skills training with low-, medium- and high-fidelity pediatric simulators. The review reports the emotional trajectory qualitatively, with no effect size.

> "Initially, feelings of fear and anxiety were triggered, especially in high-fidelity simulation. However, after training, caregivers felt relieved, self-confident and satisfied with their performance"
