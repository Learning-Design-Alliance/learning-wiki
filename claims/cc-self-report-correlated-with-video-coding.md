## Evidence

### Vig 2014

Vig, R., Taylor, M. W., Star, J. R., & Chao, T. (2014). Assessing implementation fidelity: Challenges as seen through the lens of two experimental studies. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2014/annualmeeting/abstracts/view.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Contrasting Cases project, approximately 100 8th and 9th grade Algebra I teachers were randomly assigned to treatment or control; treatment teachers were videotaped monthly and completed an instructional practices survey twice per year plus a teacher log. The researchers report a significant positive correlation between self-report and video coding measures.

> "treatment teachers' self-reported instructional practices, as measured by their survey and log responses, were significantly and positively correlated with scores from our coding of their videos"
