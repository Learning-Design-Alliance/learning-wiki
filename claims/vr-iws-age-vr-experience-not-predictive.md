## Evidence

### Herrera 2024

Herrera, G., Vera, L., Pérez-Fuster, P., López-Fernández, A., López, Á., Savaş-Taşkesen, Ü., & Newbutt, N. (2024). Multisite usability and safety trial of an immersive virtual reality implementation of a work organization system for autistic learners: implications for technology design. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10422-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null result from the same linear regression analysis of first-session adapted ASQ factor scores (N = 127 tasks). The article reports age, previous VR experience and task type "did not result sufficiently significant to be included in the final model"; no effect sizes are printed for these predictors.

> "Other variables such as the age of the participants, having previous experience using VR or the type of task conducted did not result sufficiently significant to be included in the final model."
