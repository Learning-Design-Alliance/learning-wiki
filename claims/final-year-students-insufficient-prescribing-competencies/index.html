Evidence¶

Brinkman 2018¶

Brinkman, D. J., Tichelaar, J., Graaf, S., Otten, R. H. J., Richir, M. C., and van Agtmael, M. A. (2018). Do final‐year medical students have sufficient prescribing competencies? A systematic literature review. British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.13491

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of 47 articles evaluating prescribing competencies of final-year medical students, involving 10,339 students in total. The review concludes students showed "a general lack of preparedness, self‐confidence, knowledge and skills" in the named CPT topics.

"Studies showed that students had a general lack of preparedness, self‐confidence, knowledge and skills, specifically regarding general and antimicrobial prescribing and pharmacovigilance."
