Evidence¶

Hoda Harati 2026¶

Hoda Harati, Gretchen McAllister, Yingying Liu. (2026). Cultivating inclusive mindsets exploring faculty's and students' perspectives on using online simulations for inclusive pedagogy in teacher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10662-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Semi-structured interviews with four teacher educators who used the simulation in literacy courses, thematically analyzed. TEs described it as mediating discussions on difficult topics beyond what traditional lecture achieved.

"This simulation was a great tool for me as a teacher educator because it mediated discussions on difficult topics. It allowed students to interact and reflect in ways that a traditional lecture or discussion could not achieve, especially when dealing with DEI topics."

Hoda Harati 2026 (2)¶

Hoda Harati, Gretchen McAllister, Yingying Liu. (2026). Cultivating inclusive mindsets exploring faculty's and students' perspectives on using online simulations for inclusive pedagogy in teacher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10662-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

From the TE interviews (theme: Simulation Effectiveness): challenges included accessibility features going unnoticed, careful planning for curriculum integration, and decision options that "felt too binary."

"The simulation's format could be limiting, especially when the decisions felt too binary. Students wished for more nuanced options or opportunities to incorporate multiple approaches."
