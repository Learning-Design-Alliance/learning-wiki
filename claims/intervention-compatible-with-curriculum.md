### Quintas-Hijós Alejandro 2020

Quintas-Hijós Alejandro, Peñarrubia-Lozano Carlos, Bustamante Juan Carlos. (2020). Analysis of the applicability and utility of a gamified didactics with exergames at primary schools: Qualitative findings from a natural experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231269

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Teacher interviews in the four-school natural experiment. Teachers welcomed the novelty and considered the intervention applicable because it is compatible with the school curriculum and PE contents, describing exergame and gamification as technical elements to develop the curriculum.

> "Teachers considered the intervention applicable as they thought it was compatible with conventional didactic methods: “I believe that virtue lies in mixing things. Not just one thing, not all exergames…. precisely what we have done."
