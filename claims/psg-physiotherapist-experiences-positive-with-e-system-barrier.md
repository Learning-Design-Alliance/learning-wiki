## Evidence

### Stevens A 2017

Stevens A, Köke A, van der Weijden T, Beurskens A. (2017). Ready for goal setting? Process evaluation of a patient-specific goal-setting method in physiotherapy. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-017-2557-9

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Conventional content analysis of 38 written reflection reports, inductively coded by two researchers into four themes including opinion of the PSG, awareness of patient-centred communication, perceived patient participation, and the electronic patient system.

> "The majority of the physiotherapists perceived increased patient participation, in terms of patients being actively involved and enjoying the discussion about their treatment and goals."
