## Evidence

### Gahwaji 2016

Gahwaji, N. (2016). The Effects Of Two Different Instructional Programmes On Literacy Skills Of Kindergarten Children. Journal of International Education Research, 12(1). https://journals.cluteonline.com/index.php/JIER

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 41 children (TIP group n=21, games group n=20) in a Jeddah kindergarten; covariance analysis of variance neutralised pre-test impact. Post-test means were TIP M=24.95 (SD=2.25) versus games M=28.40 (SD=1.64), described as "in favour of the group that learnt using educational games".

> "These study results have shown that there exists statistically significant difference (significance α = 0.05) on literacy skill assessment dimension between the two experimental groups. This difference was in favour of the group that learnt using educational games."
