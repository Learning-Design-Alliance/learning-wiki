Evidence¶

Daniele Verí 2026¶

Daniele Verí. (2026). Beyond AI Literacy: A Structured Review and Exploratory Meta-Analysis of Measures for Competent Generative-AI Use. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15624 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.15624

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Sensitivity analysis adding a composite mean of 12 dependent cross-factor correlations from a teacher study (N = 288) to the three direct effects. The composite measures a different comparison than total-score correlations; its interval also includes zero.

"Adding the Zhang cross-factor composite yields four effects and a combined reportedN = 3,053: r = .079, Hartung-Knapp 95% CI [-.025, .181], normal-theory CI [.021, .136], and model-based prediction interval [-.070, .224]."
