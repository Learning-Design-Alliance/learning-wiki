### K Anniesmitha 2026

K Anniesmitha, Rao K G Mohandas, Aithal P Ashwini, Bandekar Nikita N., Mandal Tatiyana, George Bincy M. (2026). Effectiveness of technology-enhanced learning in neuroanatomy education among health professionals - a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09764-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Randomised study of 31 medical and biomedical students comparing an AR app (GreyMapp-AR, n = 15) with brain cross-section study (n = 16). The cross-section group "improved their test scores much more (P = 0.035)"; the review codes this as decreased learning with AR.

> "Compared with the students with GreyMapp-AR, the students who worked with cross-sections (n = 16) improved their test scores much more (P = 0.035) (n = 15)."
