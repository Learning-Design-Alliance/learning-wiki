## Evidence

### Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman 2021

Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman. (2021). The Role of Direct Strategy Instruction and Indirect Activation of Self-Regulated Learning—Evidence from Classroom Observation Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09534-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Classroom observation study of 69 primary school teachers' strategy instruction in language arts and math classrooms. The review reports that "teachers ' suggestion of strategy use occurred only in 2% of the observed intervals", with metacognitive strategy instruction even smaller since not all coded strategies were metacognitive.

> "The observations yielded that teachers ' suggestion of strategy use occurred only in 2% of the observed intervals."

### Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman 2021 (2)

Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman. (2021). The Role of Direct Strategy Instruction and Indirect Activation of Self-Regulated Learning—Evidence from Classroom Observation Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09534-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observation study of type and frequency of strategy instruction adapted from Moely et al.'s instrument. The review reports that "Teachers spent only 9% of their instruction on strategies", with teachers most frequently recommending learning aids, metacognitive activity, and elaboration strategies.

> "Teachers spent only 9% of their instruction on strategies."

### Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman 2021 (3)

Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman. (2021). The Role of Direct Strategy Instruction and Indirect Activation of Self-Regulated Learning—Evidence from Classroom Observation Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09534-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Classroom observation study in senior secondary schools coded for traditional, activating, or process-oriented teaching features. The review reports that "Teachers spent only 5% of their teaching time on process-oriented teaching", while more than 40% of teaching time activated students to work independently without explicit instruction in how to manage that autonomy.

> "Teachers spent only 5% of their teaching time on process-oriented teaching."
