Evidence¶

Jeffers 2019¶

Jeffers, J. M., & Poling, S. (2019). The development and implementation of a 12-month simulation-based learning curriculum for pediatric emergency medicine fellows utilizing debriefing with good judgment and rapid cycle deliberate practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1417-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

End-of-curriculum anonymous online survey with 7 of 8 participants responding (88% response rate). Learners reported "high satisfaction (4.4/5, SD = 0.5)" and educational value 4.9/5 (SD = 0.38); these are self-reported descriptive ratings with no inferential test.

"Overall, the learners found self-reported high satisfaction (4.4/5, SD = 0.5) and educational value (4.9/5, SD = 0.38) in the curriculum with an 88% response rate (7/8 participants)"
