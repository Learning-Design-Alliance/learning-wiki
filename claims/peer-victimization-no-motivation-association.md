## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, J., Kaufman, T., Mastrotheodoros, S., & Branje, S. (2025). Longitudinal associations between academic motivation and school-related stressors in adolescents transitioning to secondary school. Learning and Instruction, 98, 102144. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102144

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the six bivariate latent growth curve models in the four-wave study of 290 Dutch adolescents; Table 7 shows non-significant intercept and slope covariances for peer victimization with both motivation types. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no statistically significant associations between the growth factors of peer victimization and those of either type of academic motivation."
