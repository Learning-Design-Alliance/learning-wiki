## Evidence

### Kim Min-Kyeong 2024

Kim Min-Kyeong, Kim Hae Won. (2024). Impact of a change from A–F grading to honors/pass/fail grading on academic performance at Yonsei University College of Medicine in Korea: a cross-sectional serial mediation analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.20

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional cohort comparison using the Dundee Ready Education Environment Measure (Cronbach's α 0.864). Table 1 reports DREEM totals of 110.81±22.46 (A–F) versus 121.72±24.68 (honors/pass/fail), t=-3.569, P<0.001, supporting "perceived the learning environment more positively".

> "Regarding learning-related characteristics, the honors/pass/fail grading class perceived the learning environment more positively and scored significantly higher on intrinsic motivation."
