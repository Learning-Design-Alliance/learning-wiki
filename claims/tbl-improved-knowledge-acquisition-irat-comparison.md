### Jabeen Kauser 2026

Jabeen Kauser, Rehman Javeria, Fatima Syeda Sadia, Ahmed Imran, Ghias Kulsoom. (2026). Does medical students’ perception of team-based learning have an impact on achieving planned learning outcomes?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09185-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Whole-cohort quantitative analysis of 96 Year 2 medical students in the Immunity and Infectious Diseases module, comparing iRAT, tRAT and end-of-session test scores using independent t-tests. The study reports "The tRAT scores and end-of-session test scores were significantly higher than the iRAT scores" with p < 0.0001; Table 1 lists iRAT mean 5.95, tRAT mean 7.02, end-of-session test mean 6.66.
