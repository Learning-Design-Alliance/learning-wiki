q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's theory-mapping of 84 studies found that "a considerable number of studies (N = 33, 39%) did not clearly men- tion the underlying SRL theoretical framework for their study". Among the 52 that did, Zimmerman's three-phase model led (N = 23), followed by Winne and Hadwin's four-phase COPES model (N = 9), Co- and Socially Shared Regulation of Learning (N = 4), and Pintrich's model (N = 3).
