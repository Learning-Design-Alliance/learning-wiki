Pickering 2011¶

Pickering, L. E., & Walsh, E. J. (2011). Using Videoconferencing Technology to Enhance Classroom Observation Methodology for the Instruction of Preservice Early Childhood Professionals. Journal of Digital Learning in Teacher Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ918901

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Single-sample t-tests (test value 3 = Neutral) on LVO-only ratings from all 40 students in Wave 3 (Section 1 n = 24; Section 2 n = 16). Table 4 shows, e.g., no travel required beneficial M = 4.95, t = 55.88, p = .000; ability to see everything M = 2.90, t = -.75, p = .457.

"Patterns of responses in this wave of data (see Table 4) continued to reinforce the idea that LVOs were beneficial for students, as all but two of the items were statistically significant in the expected direction."
