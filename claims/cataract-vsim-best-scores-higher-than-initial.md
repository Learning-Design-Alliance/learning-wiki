## Evidence

### Xiang Xiaoli 2026

Xiang Xiaoli, Zhu Xiaoyu, Ji Zijia, Lu Min. (2026). Application of a virtual simulation teaching model in enhancing the clinical understanding of cataract surgery among undergraduate medical students: a single-arm pre-post interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09667-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the single-arm pre-post study of 31 fourth-year clinical medicine students, the Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed "the best scores were significantly higher than the initial scores (Z=-2.936, P = 0.003)". Medians were 80.00 initially versus 89.00 at best.

> "Descriptive statistics showed that the median initial score was 80.00 (IQR: 67.00–91.00), and the median best score was 89.00 (IQR: 79.00–99.00). The Wilcoxon signed-rank test revealed that the best scores were significantly higher than the initial scores (Z=-2.936, P = 0.003)."
