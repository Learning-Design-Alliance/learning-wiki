`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline survey data from Table 4 (attitudes toward gender identity/masculinity, Question 13): control teachers averaged 26.97 versus 25.98 and 25.88 in the training groups, with clustered p-values of 0.02 and 0.01. Items such as educated women make unruly wives (p = 0.00 for both contrasts) and when you beat girls, you raise obedient wives (p = 0.01, 0.05) drove the difference.
