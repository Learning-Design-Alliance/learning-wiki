Comparison of the 333-student survey grouped by self-assessed knowledge adequacy (Yes n=34; No n=262; I don't know n=37). Only the contaminated-tooth question showed a significant difference (Table 6); the article reports no significant differences for the other questions.

> "The correct response rate among students who answered “Yes” (22 out of 34; 64.7%) was significantly higher than those who answered “No” (114 out of 262; 43.5%) or “I don’t know” (10 out of 37; 27%) (p = 0.006)."
