Seeskin, A., Nagaoka, J., & Mahaffie, S. (2018). Hidden risk: Changes in GPA across the transition to high school. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/hidden-risk-changes-gpa-across-transition-high-school

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive school-level analysis of GPA change distributions across CPS high schools (Figure 7). "Of the 64 CPS high schools with at least 20 Black students in their 2016–17 freshman cohort, 17 schools saw their Black students lose more than 0.5 points" while 14 improved; Black and Latino students in selective enrollment schools tended to see especially large declines.

> "Of the 64 CPS high schools with at least 20 Black students in their 2016–17 freshman cohort, 17 schools saw their Black students lose more than 0.5 points in core GPA on average between the eighth and ninth grades, while 14 saw their Black students improve their GPAs, on average"
