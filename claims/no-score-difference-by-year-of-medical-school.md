Secondary analysis in the randomized trial found no year-of-study effect: "not significantly different in the 3 groups based on the year of study ... (P=.52)". No effect size was printed.

> "The global score was not significantly different in the 3 groups based on the year of study: 50.3 (SD 11.4) points in fourth year, 51.3 (SD 10.9) points in fifth year, and 53.5 (SD 10.5) points in sixth year (P=.52)."
