## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the concentration-versus-score analysis, 53.3% of FCI questions were answered without any mental model, 34.4% with predominance of two erroneous models, and only 13.3% with a mix of a correct and an incorrect model. [→ Paco Talero 2012](#paco-talero-2012)

Paco Talero, Orlando Organista y Luis Barbosa. (2012). Aplicación del FCI a estudiantes de ingeniería de Bogotá: una interpretación de los resultados mediante un modelo aleatorio de dos niveles. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.7277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.7277)

Concentration-versus-score analysis of FCI responses (Fig. 1) from the field work with engineering students in Bogotá.
