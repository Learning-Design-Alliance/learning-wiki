# The copying effect on described ability was driven by a mixed-strategy subgroup, while pure observational outcome learners showed no description-sensitive learning rates

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the observational outcome learner subgroup, learning rates did not differ by demonstrator description. [→ Selbing 2025](#selbing-2025)
`q2 i?` For the mixed strategy learner subgroup, copying rates differed significantly by description, with a mean difference of 0.19 favoring copying of Best demonstrators. [→ Selbing 2025 (2)](#selbing-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Selbing 2025

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of fitted parameters in the best model for the 17 participants classified as pure observational outcome learners. Learning rates did not differ by description, t(16.00) = −0.7, p = 0.49.

> "An analysis of the best model for the subgroup of pure observational outcome learners showed no systematic difference in observational learning rates as a function of demonstrator description (Mean = −0.09, t(16.00) =−0.7, p = 0.49)."

### Selbing 2025 (2)

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of fitted parameters in the best model for the 19 participants classified as mixed strategy learners. Copying rates differed by description, t(18) = 2.57, p = 0.019, mean difference 0.19; neither separate rate differed from zero (minimum p = 0.15).

> "An analysis of the best model for the subgroup of mixed strategy learners showed a signiﬁcant difference in copying rates as a function of demon- strator description (t (18) = 2.57,p = 0.019), with a mean difference of 0.19"
