Gregor Benz, Sarah Taha, Andreas Vorholzer. (2026). Data evaluation processes of different-sized datasets: an eye-tracking with concurrent think-aloud study. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.31045](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.31045)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Integrated analysis of eye-tracking, think-aloud, and written justifications in the N = 20 study reveals "a multi-stage selection process from perception to verbalization to written argumentation", with average proportions of 60.0%, 41.7%, and 38.0%. Only descriptive percentages are printed.

> "although eye-tracking fixations showed broad visual attention (e.g., on average 60.0 % of all fixations were force-related), only a subset of this information is verbalized (on average 41.7 %) and further reduced in written justifications (on average 38.0 %)."
