### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)

### Griffin Pitts 2026 (2)

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exam-score quartile analysis (602 students) found "perceptions of pro- gramming difficulty (χ 2(3) = 12.177, p = .007)" differed across quartiles; Q1 (M = 4.090) and Q2 (M = 4.109) reported the greatest perceived difficulty and Q3 the lowest (M = 3.830).

> "Kruskal–Wallis tests indicated significant differences across quartiles for self- efficacy (χ 2(3) = 10.662, p = .014) and perceptions of pro- gramming difficulty (χ 2(3) = 12.177, p = .007)"
