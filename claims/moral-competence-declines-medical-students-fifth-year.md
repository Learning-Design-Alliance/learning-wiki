# Moral competence diverges by fifth year: psychology students score higher than medical students, with a significant field-by-year interaction

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zielina M 2025

Zielina M, Pinkasová T, Dostál D, Ivanová K, Macháček M, Matějek J, Řebíková B, Kuchař J, Škoda J, Pospíšilová K, Volejníčková RM, Doležal A. (2025). Assessing moral competence in medical and psychology students: effects on anxiety and test duration in online versus paper-based testing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08237-w

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional mixed-effects regression on MCT C-scores (n = 717). First-year medical and psychology students did not differ, with "virtually no differences between the fields" (d = 0.05, t(6.58) = 0.22, p = 0.829).

> "in the first year, there were virtually no differences between the fields (d = 0.05, small effect; t(6.58) = 0.22, p = 0.829)"

### Zielina M 2025 (2)

Zielina M, Pinkasová T, Dostál D, Ivanová K, Macháček M, Matějek J, Řebíková B, Kuchař J, Škoda J, Pospíšilová K, Volejníčková RM, Doležal A. (2025). Assessing moral competence in medical and psychology students: effects on anxiety and test duration in online versus paper-based testing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08237-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects regression on MCT C-scores reporting the Field × Year interaction and year-specific contrasts. The interaction was significant, t(708.68) = 2.23, p = 0.026, and "fifth year: Psych > Med by d = 0.56", a medium effect.

> "Key tests. Field × Year interaction: t(708.68) = 2.23, p = 0.026 (significant). First year: d = 0.05 (ns); fifth year: Psych > Med by d = 0.56."
