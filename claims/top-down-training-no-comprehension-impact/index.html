Evidence¶

Oliver and Young 2016¶

Rhonda Oliver, Shahreen Young. (2016). Improving reading fluency and comprehension in adult ESL learners using bottom-up and top-down vocabulary training. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 6(1), 111-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2016.6.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 2 posttest comprehension (scored out of 4) in the 20-participant classroom quasi-experiment. The article reports that contextual training "had no impact on understanding"; the printed test gave p = .069 for the comprehension comparison. Equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"It appears from these results that contextual training is linked to improved reading speed; however, it had no impact on understanding."
