### Uchswas Paul 2026 (2)

Uchswas Paul, Shail Shah, Sri Vaishnavi Mylavarapu, M. Parvez Rashid, and Edward Gehringer. (2026). Optimizing Peer Grading: A Systematic Literature Review of Reviewer Assignment Strategies and Quantity of Reviewers. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-15743-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-15743-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of engagement and workload findings (RQ3). Mi et al. and Fang et al. recommended a cap of five reviews per student; Lee et al. reported engagement remained high when five peer reviews were required; Vista et al. recommended four reviews as a workload–accuracy balance; Chan et al. found three reviews could foster meaningful participation with trust and credibility mechanisms in place.

> "Many studies indicated that requiring more than five reviews per student could lead to disengagement or rushed, low-effort grading that could impact learning gains."
