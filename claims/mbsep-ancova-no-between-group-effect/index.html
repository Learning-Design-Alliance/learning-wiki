Evidence¶

Bati 2015¶

Bati, K. & Kaptan, F. (2015). The Effect of Modeling Based Science Education on Critical Thinking. Educational Policy Analysis and Strategic Research, 10(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1127622

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on post-test CCT-X scores of the experimental (n=60) and control (n=54) groups with pre-test scores as covariate, in the four-group quasi-experimental design. Corrected means were 36.48 (experimental) and 35.02 (control); the article prints π 2 = 0,029 and concludes the program "did not bring about a significant difference".

"Table 12 shows that there was not a significant difference between groups' post -test mean scores when they were adjusted for pre -test scores (F 1.111 = 3.332, p = .071 , π 2 = 0,029)."
