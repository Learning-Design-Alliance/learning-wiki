## Evidence

### Jackson Mallory A. 2026

Jackson Mallory A., Liu Hongjiao, Moon Sungmin, Doherty Jennifer H., Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2026). Use of a specific set of learner-centered evidence-based teaching practices correlates with higher exam performance across seven STEM departments. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327269

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory PCA at the course level (146 courses, 10 aggregated PORTAAL practices). The article reports the first two PCs explained 61.2% of total variance, with PC1 indexing overall intensity and PC2 contrasting the learner-centered set against instruction-centered practices (instructor explaining/answering, working alone, alternative answers, high Bloom's questions).

> "The group that was positively correlated with PC2 consisted of the following four practices: volunteers giving/explaining answers, randomly called students giving/explaining answers, the instructor prompting logic, and the instructor giving positive feedback."
