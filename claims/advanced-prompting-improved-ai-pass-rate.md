## Evidence

### Ivory 2026

Ivory, M., Finnerty, S., Dunn, K., Philpot, R., & Warmelink, L. (2026). The Integrity of Psychology Assessments in the AI Age: A Critical Examination. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09729-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the same case study, essay assessments were re-prompted at escalating levels: basic prompting produced one passing essay, while intermediate prompting (component-by-component requests, 5–20 minutes) produced passing outputs for all five essays.

> "Out of the five assessments that were referred to as 'essays', only one passed at the basic level (a first-year cognitive psychology topic), but all passed at the intermediate level."
