## Evidence

### Ericsson 1993

K. Anders Ericsson and Walter Kintsch. (1993). Long-Term Working Memory. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Long-Term+Working+Memory

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Chase and Simon (1973), that chess experts' superior memory for representative positions depended on familiar meaningful configurations: "the experts' advantage disappeared when chess boards of randomly arranged chess pieces were used as stimuli." The review notes this pattern was replicated across chess, bridge, go, music, electronics, programming, dance, basketball, field hockey, and figure skating.

> "Chase and Simon found that the experts' advantage disappeared when chess boards of randomly arranged chess pieces were used as stimuli in the memory tasks."
