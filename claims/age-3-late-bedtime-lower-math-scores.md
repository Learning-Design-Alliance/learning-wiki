## Evidence

### Nishiyama Masahiro 2023

Nishiyama Masahiro, Kyono Yuki, Yamaguchi Hiroshi, Kawamura Aoi, Oikawa Shizuka, Tokumoto Shoichi, Tomioka Kazumi, Nozu Kandai, Nagase Hiroaki. (2023). Association of early bedtime at 3 years of age with higher academic performance and better non-cognitive skills in elementary school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort regression for math scores (adjusted n = 3268): the ≥23:00 bedtime estimate was −5.28 (p = 0.004), while 21:00 (p = 0.097) and 22:00 (p = 0.137) were not significant after adjustment. Unadjusted contrasts were larger, with "8.51 points lower for bedtime at ≥ 23:00".

> "Multiple regression analysis adjusted for the above variables revealed that the math score was lower for children whose bedtime was at ≥ 23:00 than for children whose bedtime was at 18:00–20:00."
