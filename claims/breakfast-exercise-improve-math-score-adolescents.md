# Combined breakfast and exercise improves mathematics test score over a morning in adolescents compared with fasting and sedentary behavior

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The increase in mathematics test score over the morning was greater in the breakfast-and-exercise group than the fasted-sedentary group (15.2% vs 6.7% improvement, p = 0.014). [→ Kawabata Masato 2021](#kawabata-masato-2021)

## Evidence

### Kawabata Masato 2021

Kawabata Masato, Lee Kerry, Choo Hui-Cheng, Burns Stephen F. (2021). Breakfast and Exercise Improve Academic and Cognitive Performance in Adolescents. Nutrients 13(4). https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13041278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized four-group parallel trial in 82 adolescents using linear mixed modelling with B-E as reference. The B-E group improved 15.2% on correct answers versus 6.7% in F-S (p = 0.014); B-E did not differ from B-S or F-E.

> "The increase was greater in the B-E than F-S group (β11 = −3.30, p = 0.014) but B-E did not differ from the B-S (β12 = −1.68, p = 0.253) or F-E (β13 = −2.81, p = 0.078) groups."
