Kahn P, Carrigan M, Wyman I, Liu R, Smith P, Andonegui AR, Murtagh L and Song F (2026) Still emerging: understanding Generative AI use in Higher Education. Front. Educ. 11:1885253. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885253

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Validated Performance Expectancy scales on both surveys (Table 1). The student distribution was skewed to the right (p-values <0.05 on a D'Agostino test) with a mean of 5; the staff scale mean was 3.9 (SD 1.6), so the article concludes suitable affordances for easing workloads were "not straightforwardly in view for many staff respondents."

> "While a direct comparison between the responses of staff and students is not appropriate given the different items that were used across the two surveys, the skew indicates a positive response on the part of respondents on this dimension of the student survey. The mean of 5 on this scale for the student responses would suggest that specific use cases were typically in view for the student respondents."
