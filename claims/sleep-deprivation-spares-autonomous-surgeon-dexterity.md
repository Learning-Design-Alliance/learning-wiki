## Evidence

### Mohtashami Fariba MD 2014

Mohtashami Fariba MD, Thiele Allison MD, PhD, Karreman Erwin PhD, Thiel John MD. (2014). Comparing Technical Dexterity of Sleep-Deprived Versus Intoxicated Surgeons. JSLS : Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00142

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Competency ratings of nine staff gynecologists' video clips (Table 2): rested vs postcall competent percentages were 62 vs 57 (task 1), 48 vs 52 (task 2), 37 vs 35 (task 3). The study tested surgeons postcall with <3 hours of sleep in 24 hours.

> "there was no difference between the number of times the performance was rated “competent” for rested compared with postcall for all 3 tasks. This suggests that participants performed equally well in both states."
