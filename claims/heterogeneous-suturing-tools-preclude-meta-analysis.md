### Morris Marie C. 2012

Morris Marie C., Gallagher Tom K., Ridgway Paul F. (2012). Tools used to assess medical students competence in procedural skills at the end of a primary medical degree: a systematic review. Medical Education Online. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3427596/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of the three suturing tools (Liddell, Wang, O'Connor), which share themes of safe handling of instruments, suture insertion, knot tying and suture removal. The review states "the content of the three tools could be merged".

> "The ultimate heterogeneity of these tools preclude a meta analysis study, however, the content of the three tools could be merged and used in a panel of competence markers for surgical skills ability."
