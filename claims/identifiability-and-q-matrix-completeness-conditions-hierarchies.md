Statement of the article's analytical objective, from the abstract. The article addresses two linked problems: identifiability of the latent space under attribute hierarchies, and completeness of the Q-matrix, which the article notes "may not at all be straightforward" to construct when attributes are hierarchical. No empirical data are reported.
