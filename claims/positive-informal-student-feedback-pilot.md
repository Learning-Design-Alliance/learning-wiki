## Evidence

### Katy Chubb 2017

Katy Chubb, Rosie Hood, Thomas Wilson, Jonathan Holdship, and Sarah Hutton. (2017). Discovering New Variable Stars at Key Stage 3. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa649f](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa649f)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal student feedback collected during the closing session of the pilot, reported as positive with quoted pupil comments including "I feel like a real astronomer". The authors interpret this as implying the club is a promising way to show young students how rewarding research can be. No quantitative measures.

> "Feedback from the students involved was taken informally during the closing session, in addition to the aforementioned teachers observations throughout the project. It was found to be positive, with comments such as "I never knew a complex subject like astronomy would fascinate me greatly, I will now deﬁnitely consider it!""
