### Lehmann Ronny 2019

Lehmann Ronny, Seitz Anke, Meyburg Jochen, Hoppe Bettina, Hoffmann Georg Friedrich, Tönshoff Burkhard, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Pediatric in-hospital emergencies: real life experiences, previous training and the need for training among physicians and nurses. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4051-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of free-text survey responses from 53 physicians (92 responses) and 75 nurses (117 responses) on experienced emergencies; respiratory failure, resuscitation, seizure, shock/sepsis and arrhythmia led both lists. The article states "Resuscitation situations were judged the most precarious for both professions."

> "Both physicians and nurses reported the same types of emergencies as most frequently experienced. This is comparable to the pediatric emergency prevalence reported by others [23, 24]. Resuscitation situations were judged the most precarious for both professions."
