The impact of preparatory activities on medical school selection outcomes: a cross-sectional survey of applicants to the university of Adelaide medical school in 2007. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-159

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from logistic regression on the 359 oral assessment participants in the 2007 Adelaide survey. The most common difficulties were inability to judge performance (66%), cost of attending (36%), and difficulty structuring answers (26%); no difficulty reached significance after Bonferroni correction. Equivalence was not tested.

> "None of the difficulties identified with the oral assessment interview significantly decreased the likelihood of being offered a place in the program (Table 4)."
