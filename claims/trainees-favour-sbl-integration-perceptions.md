Perception items of the cross-sectional survey of 90 trainees, reported as frequencies. Majorities believed SBL "could reduce procedure-related risks", favoured "combining simulation learning with traditional teaching", and agreed SBL "should be mandatory during pre-doctoral education".

> "Sixty-five participants (72.2% of the sample) believed SBL could reduce procedure-related risks, and 73 trainees (81.1%) considered that combining simulation learning with traditional teaching could improve post-graduate training and education, Fig. 2. Seventy-four trainees (82.2%) agreed that SBL should be mandatory during pre-doctoral education."
