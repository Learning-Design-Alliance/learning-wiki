## Evidence

### Gao Xiaoxing 2026

Gao Xiaoxing, Huang Xiaoming, Hu Rongrong, Zhang Li, Liu Huiting, Zhang Bingqing, Wei Chong, Qiu Wei, Zhang Mengyu, Sun Xuefeng. (2026). Evaluating Large Language Model–Based Automated Scoring in a Voice-Based Virtual Standardized Patient Platform for Medical Students: Cross-Sectional Agreement Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95578

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional observational study of 92 fourth-year medical students each completing one 15-minute voice-based VSP encounter, scored by one of 10 blinded faculty raters and by DeepSeek-V3 with a calibrated prompt. The primary mixed-effects model showed the AI total score was positively associated with the human total score (β=0.37, 95% CI 0.26‐0.48; P<.001), with "Spearman ρ=0.50, ICC(2,1)=0.51, and MAE=3.11 points."

> "Agreement was moderate: Spearman ρ=0.50, ICC(2,1)=0.51, and MAE=3.11 points."
