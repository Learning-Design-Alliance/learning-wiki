# Opportunities for peer collaboration largely disappeared when STEM courses went remote, with few instructors sustaining collaborative activities online

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q1`–`q2`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Few STEM instructors provided examples of collaborative activities during remote learning, even when such activities were part of the course before COVID; none of 28 interviewed instructors described out-of-class collaborative assignments producing a joint product. [→ Means 2020](#means-2020)
`q2 i?` On the national survey, 33% of students reported their STEM instructor had them work on group projects after COVID. [→ Means 2020 (2)](#means-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Means 2020

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Instructor interviews across the 28 case study courses found collaborative pre-COVID activities (e.g., iClicker pair work) often replaced with individual online quizzes; one mathematics instructor sustained group work via Zoom breakout groups. The report states none described out-of-class joint-product work.

> "Few STEM instructors provided examples of including collaborative activities during remote learning, even when such activities were a part of the course before the coronavirus."

### Means 2020 (2)

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National survey of 620 STEM students asked about post-COVID course practices; "33%" reported working on group projects after COVID, contrasting with the case study instructors' reports.

> "On the national survey, 33% of students reported that their STEM course instructor had them work on group projects after COVID."
