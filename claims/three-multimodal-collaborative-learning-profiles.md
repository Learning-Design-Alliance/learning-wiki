# Among 32 dyads in an open-ended collaborative tabletop task, three behavioral clusters split into two learning (gainer) profiles and one non-learning profile despite similar task performance

## Evidence

### Jauwairia Nasir 2021

Jauwairia Nasir, Aditi Kothiyal, Barbara Bruno, Pierre Dillenbourg. (2021). Many are the ways to learn identifying multi-modal behavioral profiles of collaborative learning in constructivist activities. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:485–523. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09358-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cluster analysis of 48 teams reduced to 32 (children aged 9 to 12) in the JUSThink robot-mediated activity; Table 3 reports mean Absolute_LG of 0.678* (Expressive Explorers) and 0.616 (Calm Tinkerers) versus 0.383* (Silent Wanderers) with near-identical Last_Error (0.461/0.393/0.393), showing "the performance (Last_Error) in the task is very similar for each group".

> "Approach B gives 3 behavioral clusters with the first two exhibiting high learning and the third lower learning; hence, with respect to learning, the groups can be named as type 1 gainers, type 2 gainers, and non-gainers, respectively. Note that the performance (Last_Error) in the task is very similar for each group."
