M. Tuveri, E. Murgia, and V. Fanti (2025). Analogical models to introduce high school students to modern physics: an inquiry-based activity on Rutherford's gold foil experiment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of group worksheets and classroom discussion from the hands-on phase, in which seven groups each performed between 5 and 10 marble launches against a hidden obstacle built by a peer group. Examples include Group 2 inferring a straight obstacle and Group 4 inferring multiple smaller elements.

> "All groups successfully constructed a functional black box using simple materials and engaged in authentic experimental reasoning."
