## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Perceived usefulness as a learning material, for knowledge acquisition, and for skill improvement was positively and significantly correlated with learning motivation, confidence in practice, and class satisfaction (r = 0.728 to 0.885). [→ Lee Nam-Ju 2016](#lee-nam-ju-2016)

## Evidence

### Lee Nam-Ju 2016

Lee Nam-Ju, Chae Sun-Mi, Kim Haejin, Lee Ji-Hye, Min Hyojin Jennifer, Park Da-Eun. (2016). Mobile-Based Video Learning Outcomes in Clinical Nursing Skill Education: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Computers, Informatics, Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1097/CIN.0000000000000183

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Intervention-group survey (n = 36): mean perceived usefulness 7.67/10 as a learning material, 7.39 for knowledge, 7.00 for skill improvement. All usefulness correlations were significant (r = 0.728 to 0.885), with the strongest being usefulness-for-skill-improvement with confidence ("r = 0.882, P < .001") and satisfaction (r = 0.885).

> "Students' perceived usefulness of the video clip as a learning material, for knowledge acquisition, or for skill improvement was positively correlated with learning motivation (r = 0.775, P < .001; r = 0.728, P < .001; r = 0.759, P < .001, respectively), confidence in practice (r = 0.762, P < .001; r = 0.759, P < .001; r = 0.882, P < .001, respectively), and class satisfaction (r = 0.752, P < .001; r = 0.788, P < .001; r = 0.885, P < .001, respectively)."
