# Productive failure students significantly outperformed lecture-practice and facilitated complex problem solving students on well-structured and higher-order application post-test problems

## Evidence

### Kapur 2011

Kapur, M. (2011). A further study of productive failure in mathematical problem solving: unpacking the design components. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9144-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A quasi-experimental classroom study in which 109 seventh-grade mathematics students taught by the same teacher in a Singapore school were assigned to one of three learning designs. The article reports that PF students "significantly outperformed their counterparts in the other two conditions" on both problem types on the post-test; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Despite seemingly failing in their collective and individual problem-solving efforts, PF students significantly outperformed their counterparts in the other two conditions on both the well-structured and higher-order application problems on the post-test"
