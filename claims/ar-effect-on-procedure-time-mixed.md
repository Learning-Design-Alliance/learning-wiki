## Evidence

### El Ashry M 2026

El Ashry M, El Ashry A, Khalique H, Abdalle Y, Yeung T. (2026). Augmented Reality in Surgical Training: Systematic Review of Its Impact on Technical Performance in Surgical Trainees. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71572

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Crossover RCT (n=60) of iSurgeon telestration; the review reports the 29.8% training-time reduction (1163 vs 1658 seconds, P<.001).

> "Wild et al [7] reported the most pronounced efficiency gain: total laparoscopic training time was reduced by 29.8% with AR telestration (mean 1163 [SD 275] vs mean 1658 [SD 375] seconds; P<.001)."

### El Ashry M 2026 (2)

El Ashry M, El Ashry A, Khalique H, Abdalle Y, Yeung T. (2026). Augmented Reality in Surgical Training: Systematic Review of Its Impact on Technical Performance in Surgical Trainees. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71572

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports no significant operative-time difference for complex procedures (P=.09) and suggests AR's efficiency benefits are most detectable in discrete, structured tasks; equivalence was not tested.

> "For more complex procedures, no significant differences in total operative time were observed (mean 79.6 [SD 25.7] vs mean 84.5 [SD 33.2] minutes; P=.09), suggesting that AR's efficiency benefits are most readily detectable in discrete, structured tasks (Table 3)."
