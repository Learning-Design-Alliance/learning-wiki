Subih Maha, Rababa Majdi, Aryan Fatima Sabri, Alnaeem Mohammad, AlRahahleh Mohammad H, Al niarat Tahany Fareed, Saleh Zyad T., Alsulami Ghaida Shujayyi, Almagharbeh Wesam T., Elshatarat Rami A. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07074-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 176 nurses, 15-item counseling knowledge subscale. Item-level results as printed: 58.0% correctly advised skipping a missed dose, 52.3% chose at least once every four weeks for INR testing, 56.8% correctly advised against warfarin in pregnancy.

> "Overall, participants exhibited a mean score of 8.07 (SD = 2.31) out of 15, indicating a moderate level of knowledge regarding patient counseling on warfarin management"
