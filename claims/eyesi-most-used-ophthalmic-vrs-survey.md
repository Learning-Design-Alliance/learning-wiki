International cross-sectional survey of 170 respondents from 26 countries on VRS use in ophthalmic surgical education. The authors report simulator access patterns: "The EyeSi VR Magic (Haag-Streit, Heidelberg, Germany) was the most frequently used (n = 136, 80%" with HelpMeSee second (n = 82, 48%).

> "The EyeSi VR Magic (Haag-Streit, Heidelberg, Germany) was the most frequently used (n = 136, 80%, defined as group 1), followed by HMS (HMS, [HMS foundation, Jersey City, New Jersey, United States]) (n = 82, 48%, group 2)."
