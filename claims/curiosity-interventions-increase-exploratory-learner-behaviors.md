# Curiosity-oriented linguistic interventions via CURIOBOT consistently improve all learner-side exploratory dimensions relative to baseline LLM tutoring across model families

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all three model families, CURIOBOT consistently improves all learner-side dimensions (L1–L4) relative to Baseline, with all differences significant at p<0.005; largest gains in exploratory questioning (+21–32%), curiosity (+26–35%), agency (+30–34%), and productive struggle (+44–47%). ## Evidence

### Ganganath 2026

Ganganath, G., Bolonghege, P., Lyu, Q., Varakantham, P., & Kandappu, T. (2026). Curiosity as Linguistic Intervention: Using LLM Tutoring Dialogues to Influence Exploratory Learning Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22349

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled 3×3×3 factorial study (§5.1) with 45 participants and 270 tutoring conversations comparing CURIOBOT vs Baseline across Claude, Gemini, and GPT. The article reports gains in "exploratory questioning (L1: +21–32%)" and productive struggle (L2: +44–47%); only p-values printed, no effect sizes.
