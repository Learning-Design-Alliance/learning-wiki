## Evidence

### King 2025

King, R. B., & Wang, F. (2025). The rich get richer: Socioeconomic advantage amplifies the role of growth mindsets in learning. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 792–810. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12755

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 interaction tests using log-transformed 2018 GDP per capita as country affluence in the three-level PISA 2018 analysis. The article reports significant positive moderation for all three outcomes and states "strong support for the rich get richer hypothesis" at this level.

> "Country affluence significantly moderated the relationship of growth mindsets with academic achievement (b = 5.17, SE = 1.85, p < .01, effect size = .13), intrinsic motivation (b = .06, SE = .01, p < .001, effect size = .12) and academic engagement (b = .04, SE = .01, p < .001, effect size = .06). Hence, at the country level, there was strong support for the rich get richer hypothesis."
