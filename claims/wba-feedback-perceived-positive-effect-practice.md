Systematic review synthesis of 15 prospective, non-comparative descriptive or observational studies of WBA feedback in postgraduate doctors. The review concludes there is "good evidence that if well implemented, feedback from workplace based assessments, particularly multisource feedback, leads to a perceived positive effect on practice."
