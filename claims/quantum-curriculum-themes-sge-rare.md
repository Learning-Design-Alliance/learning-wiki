Emergent coding of topics in syllabi from 50 institutions with complete quantum curricula, grouped into themes using Griffiths' and McIntyre textbooks' tables of contents. The article reports the Schrödinger equation at 98%, 3D quantum mechanics at 94%, and the SGE in only 28% of curricula.
