## Evidence

### Gossmann 2021

Gossmann, K., Eilers, R., Rosner, R., & Barke, A. (2021). How do licensed psychotherapists in Germany feel about treating patients with posttraumatic stress symptoms? – an experimental study based on case vignettes. European Journal of Psychotraumatology, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2021.1995265

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Second block of the hierarchical regression on the vignette-survey data added self-rated fears (VAS B) and perceived external barriers (VAS C); both significantly lowered readiness, with standardized betas of −.442 and −.120.

> "fears/doubts (β = −.442, t(514) = −11.30, p < .001) and perceived external barriers (β = −.120 t(514) = −3.14, p = .002) markedly lowered the readiness to treat the vignette patient"

### Gossmann 2021 (2)

Gossmann, K., Eilers, R., Rosner, R., & Barke, A. (2021). How do licensed psychotherapists in Germany feel about treating patients with posttraumatic stress symptoms? – an experimental study based on case vignettes. European Journal of Psychotraumatology, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2021.1995265

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the same regression's second block, perceived external barriers such as reimbursement and administrative demands significantly reduced readiness, standardized β = −.120, p = .002.

> "perceived external barriers (β = −.120 t(514) = −3.14, p = .002) markedly lowered the readiness to treat the vignette patient"
