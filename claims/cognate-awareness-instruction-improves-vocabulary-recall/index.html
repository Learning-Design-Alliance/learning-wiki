Evidence¶

Kasap 2018¶

Kasap, S. (2018). The role of cognate English–Kurdish words in teaching English. African Educational Research Journal, 6(4): 213-217. https://doi.org/10.30918/AERJ.64.18.046

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment in a Van high school: 32 Kurdish-Turkish bilinguals received cognate-structure instruction before a delayed post-test. Table 4 prints study-group pre-test M = 20.28, post-test M = 33.16, t = -16.425, df = 31, Sig. .000; the author reports "a meaningful difference between pre-test and post-test results".

"In Table 4, we can clearly see the similarity between orthographic similarities between Kurdish words and English words (such as Run – revan; Novelty - Nuyîtî; Warm -Germ; Right - Rast etc.) naturally recollecting English words through Kurdish is much easier than learning them through Turkish"
