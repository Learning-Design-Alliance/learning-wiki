Olive Ross, Meagan Sundstrom and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Predictive Modeling for High Impact Active Learning Classrooms. arXiv. [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-9955835/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9955835/v1)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model predictions from simulated COPUS data spanning the parameter space of the 69-class dataset, shown in Fig. 2 heatmaps. The discussion adds that classes relying on group clicker questions as the only form of active learning have "consistently low effect sizes, comparable to the effect sizes seen in fully lecture classes."

> "Classes that use no group worksheets are predicted to have consistently small effect sizes, even when other active learning strategies (e.g., group clicker questions) are used"
