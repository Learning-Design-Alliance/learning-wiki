# Traditional curriculum-based physical training is more favourable than the game-like method for developing stamina in ninth-grade students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average long-distance running times were significantly lower (better) under the traditional method than the game-like method (F a1/a2 = 5,85 > F0,05 = 2,04). [→ Тulegen A. Botagariyeva 2016](#ulegen-a-botagariyeva-2016)

## Evidence

### Тulegen A. Botagariyeva 2016

Тulegen A. Botagariyeva, Svetlana S. Kubiyeva, Venera E. Baizakova, Nurolla Mambetov, Yerkin K. Tulegenov, Alpysbay S. Aralbayev and Dulat U. Kairgozhin. (2016). Studying the Effectiveness of Physical Education in the Secondary School (by the Example of Kazakhstan). International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(10), 3575-3594. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-factor variance analysis of the 2015-2016 pedagogical experiment in 30 randomly sampled Aktobe schools (15 traditional, 15 game-like). The article reports the traditional method's running means were "significantly lower than during the use of the gaming method"; factor A impact was 34.5%, factor B 15.5%.

> "The average indicators of the long-distance running (2000 m., in minutes) given the traditional training method were significantly lower than during the use of the gaming method (F a1/a2 = 5,85 > F0,05 = 2,04)."
