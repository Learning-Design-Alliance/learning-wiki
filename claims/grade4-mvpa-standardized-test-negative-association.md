# Grade 4 full-year mean school-day MVPA is negatively associated with Grade 4 standardized test scores, with negligible magnitude

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Grade 4 mean daily school-day MVPA had significant negative associations with Grade 4 math (β = −0.206) and ELA (β = −0.283) standardized test scores, but not Lexile after correction. [→ Elish Paul N. 2022](#elish-paul-n-2022)

## Evidence

### Elish Paul N. 2022

Elish Paul N., Bryan Cassandra S., Boedeker Peter J., Calvert Hannah G., Kay Christi M., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). The longitudinal association between objectively-measured school-day physical activity and academic achievement in US elementary school students. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01328-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel models of Grade 4 mean (T1 and T2) MVPA predicting Georgia Milestones scores, adjusted for covariates and prior achievement. The article reports "standardized test scores from T2" with βMath = −0.206 and βELA = −0.283 (p < .0001); standardized effect sizes were −0.032 and −0.043, described as about 0.03–0.04 standard deviations.
