## Evidence

### Şengör K 2025

Şengör K, Alptekin HM, Kara Ö, Görücü R, Ayoğlu T, Akyüz N. (2025). Assessment tool for surgical hand washing skills: a scale development study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03192-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity evaluation by 10 experts in surgical nursing, educational measurement, and nursing education, each rating items on a 4-point appropriateness scale using the Davis technique. The article reports the mean CVI was "calculated as 0.976" and every item exceeded 0.80, which the authors interpret as sufficient content validity.

> "The average CVI for the 13-item ATSHWS was calculated as 0.976. It was determined that the CVI for each item of the ATSHWS exceeded 0.80."
