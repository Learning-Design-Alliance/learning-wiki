The multivariate linear regression model in the retrospective cohort reported "the R2 value was 41.8 %", meaning 41.8% of GPA variance was predicted by gender, college, program, graduation year, HSGA, APT and ACT combined.

> "Additionally, the R2 value was 41.8 %. It indicates that 41.8 % of the variance in GPA scores can predict from the variables gender, type of college, type of program, year of graduation, the HSGA, APT and ACT tests scores."
