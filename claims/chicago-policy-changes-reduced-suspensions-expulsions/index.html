“...and they cared”: How to create better 2020¶

“...and they cared”: How to create better, safer learning environments for girls of color. (2020). National Women’s Law Center & The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED607322

q2 · i? · review · r1

Ed Trust calculations of Chicago Public Schools suspension data show "In 2012, Chicago suspended 1 in 3 Black girls. In 2018, that fell to 1 in 12." The guide attributes the change to intentional policy decisions; no effect size is printed.

“...and they cared”: How to create better 2020 (2)¶

“...and they cared”: How to create better, safer learning environments for girls of color. (2020). National Women’s Law Center & The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED607322

q2 · i? · design · r2

The guide’s Chicago policy timeline reports, since 2011–2012, a "67% decrease in suspensions" and an "82% decrease in expulsions" following changes to suspension policy, the code of conduct, and the Suspension and Expulsion Reduction Plan. No effect size is printed.

"Since 2011-2012: 67% decrease in suspensions 82% decrease in expulsions"
