> "There was not only a statistically significant but only a moderate correlation between state and trait scores, with r²=0.32 (figure 2C), suggesting that while students' usual anxiety predisposition partly explained situational OSCE-related anxiety, the immediate exam context might have contributed to elevated state anxiety."
