## Evidence

### Heckmann Markus B. 2025

Heckmann Markus B., Mages Christine, Finke Daniel, Reich Christoph, Haney Ailís Ceara, Riedel Caroline, Bugaj Till Johannes, Frey Norbert, Rahm Ann-Kathrin. (2025). Effects of mentoring on self-reflection and competence in Final year medical students’ internal medicine rotation. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331057

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate and multivariate negative binomial regression analyses of form completion and competence in the randomized study. Trends "did not reach statistical significance and were not sustained in multivariate models"; the authors attribute this to limited sample size and short intervention duration.

> "Although univariate analyses showed trends toward significance for controlled regulation (OR = 1.16, p = 0.0699) and autonomous regulation (OR = 1.22, p = 0.0580) with competence, these did not reach statistical significance and were not sustained in multivariate models."
