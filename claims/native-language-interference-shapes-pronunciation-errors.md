Narrative review section on factors influencing pronunciation mastery. The digest reports, as a research consensus, that "the learner's first language influences the pronunciation of the target language"; no empirical study details or effect sizes are printed.

> "Most researchers agree that the learner's first language influences the pronunciation of the target language and is a significant factor in accounting for foreign accents. So-called interference or negative transfer from the first language is likely to cause errors in aspiration, intonation, and rhythm in the target language."
