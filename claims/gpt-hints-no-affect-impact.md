## Evidence

### Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker 2023

Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker. (2023). Large Language Models (GPT) for automating feedback on programming assignments. Proceedings of the 31st International Conference on Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2023.950](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2023.950)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction on 1,304 affect survey responses, randomly prompted after roughly one in three submissions; both conditions "predominantly reported the focused state".

> "No significant differences between conditions were observed for the reported frequency of any of the affective states (Table 1)."

### Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker 2023 (2)

Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker. (2023). Large Language Models (GPT) for automating feedback on programming assignments. Proceedings of the 31st International Conference on Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2023.950](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2023.950)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-state test from Table 1 for frustration, one of the six self-reported options; no significant difference between the randomized conditions after alpha adjustment.

> "Frustration did not significantly differ between the control (Mdn=0) and experimental (Mdn=0) groups (W=611, adjusted α=0.02, p=0.653, Mann-Whitney U test)."
