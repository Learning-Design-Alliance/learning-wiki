# Merging different technical understandings of Minecraft items into shared nomenclature enabled parallel division of building work (Extract 2)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When group members used different names for the same building block (cobblestone), reaching a shared understanding of the Minecraft items inventory let them distribute the work and build different parts of the building in parallel. [→ Mørch 2024](#mrch-2024)

## Evidence

### Mørch 2024

Mørch, A. I., Andersen, R., Eie, S., & Mifsud, L. (2024). Emergent group understanding: Investigating intersubjectivity in sociotechnical interdependencies. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09432-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interaction analysis (Extract 2, reconstruction phase) of group 7, where Emil, Jacob and Nora discussed floor building blocks. The quote shows the merged understanding of "Minecraft nomenclature" after a discussion of alternative names for one block; outcome stated analytically by the authors, no effect size.

> "we can assume that they had reached a shared understanding of cobblestone as the name for this object and that intersubjective learning of the Minecraft building block inventory had been achieved. The three students' different technical understandings (of whom two were more gamer oriented or technical than the third) merged into a common understanding, adopting Minecraft nomenclature. This understanding allowed them to distribute the work and build different parts of the building in parallel."
