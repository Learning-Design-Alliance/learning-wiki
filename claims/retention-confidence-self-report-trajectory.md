Ad hoc Likert-scale self-report collected at follow-up in the pilot randomized trial across both groups; at 3 months 49% (18/37) reported forgetting at least one step, attributing this primarily to a lack of regular practice.

> "At 6 months, confidence in skill retention improved markedly: 90% (28/31) rated their confidence at 4 or 5, and only 3 participants reported having forgotten any step, suggesting sustained confidence in perceived skill retention over time."
