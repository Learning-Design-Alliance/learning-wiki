Xiaoyu Hou 2026¶

Xiaoyu Hou, Bo Xiao, Hexu Liu and Shane Mueller. (2026). The Role of Instructional Guidance in Generative AI-Assisted Learning: Empirical Evidence from Construction Engineering Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05509 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.05509

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Overall post-intervention test (multiple-choice plus open-ended, max 18) in the same randomized experiment (n = 95). The prompted group's total of 18.86 "significantly exceeding both the NA (15.35) and Unprompted (15.73) groups (F = 6.82, p = 0.0017)".

"The Prompted group achieves the highest mean performance (18.86), significantly exceeding both the NA (15.35) and Unprompted (15.73) groups (F = 6.82, p = 0.0017)."
