### Dechamps 2025

Dechamps, T., & Skulmowski, A. (2025). The Effective Design of Tasks Involving Learning by Drawing: Current Trends and Methodological Progress in Research on Drawing to Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10026-2

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that the Cromley et al. (2020) meta-analysis found drawing "capable of increasing factual, inferential, as well as transfer knowledge," and that its largest effect was on factual knowledge, contradicting the expectation that drawing mainly produces higher-level knowledge.

> "Interestingly, learning by drawing had the largest effect on factual knowledge in that meta-analysis, contrary to the interpretation of Van Meter and Garner (2005)."
