## Evidence

### Zhang Wang 2025

Zhang Wang, Li Zonghuan, Li Pengcheng, Liu Changhuan, Wang Zheng, Liu Yuping, Zhu Lekai, Shi Yulong, Fang Xue, Wang Xinghuan, Xie Zhe, Wang Xin. (2025). MOOC-virtual simulation integration enhances surgical clinical skills: a quasi-experimental study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2579396

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of blended (n = 60) vs. traditional (n = 79) groups on a four-station Structured Clinical Skills Assessment scored by two blinded examiners (κ = 0.89). The blended group scored "94.26 ± 3.24 vs. 89.76 ± 5.09" with d = 1.05.

> "The experimental group achieved significantly higher Structured Clinical Skills Assessment Scores than the control group (94.26 ± 3.24 vs. 89.76 ± 5.09; t = 6.352, p < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 1.05)"
