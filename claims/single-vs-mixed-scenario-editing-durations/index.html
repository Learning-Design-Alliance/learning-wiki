Semi-Markov model fitting for Group 1 vs. Group 3 (Table 8); the article reports β12 =−.73, β21 =−1.02, and β24 =−.50 significantly below 0 after Bonferroni correction, while “Duration times on T and P mostly did not show statistically signiﬁcant differences.”

"In particular, β12 =−.73,β21 =−1.02, andβ24 =−.50 are signiﬁcantly smaller statistically than 0, indicating that Group 1 spent a longer time in E before the transition to J and in J before the transition to T."
