`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity-matched subgroup analysis of emergency operations (unscheduled patients with ongoing refractory cardiac compromise); the emergency subgroup comprised 20 matched pairs. The article reports similar operative characteristics, complication rates, length of stay and long-term survival (Figure 3).

> "These findings suggest that, with appropriate selection and oversight, senior residents can safely perform emergency cardiac surgery with outcomes equivalent to those achieved by experienced consultants."
