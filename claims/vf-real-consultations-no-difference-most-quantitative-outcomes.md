## Evidence

### Dohms M. C. 2020

Dohms M. C., Collares C. F., Tibério I. C. (2020). Video-based feedback using real consultations for a formative assessment in communication skills. BMC Medical Education 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1955-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of 54 medical residents pre/post with a control group across seven instruments. Table 1 reports non-significant Time*Group effects for CARE (p = 0.300), CCOG-based (p = 0.336), SPIKES-based (p = 0.758) and resident PPC (p = 0.418); "The quantitative results did not reveal significant differences in most questionnaires".
