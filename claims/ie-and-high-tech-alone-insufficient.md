### Richard R. Hake 2002

Richard R. Hake. (2002). Lessons From the Physics-Education Reform Effort. Conservation Ecology. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.5751/es-00286-050228](https://doi.org/10.5751/es-00286-050228)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports, citing Cummings et al. (1999), a study of the Rensselaer standard physics Studio Course with group work and computer use. Gains were "close to those characteristic of T courses"; the suggested cause was that activities were predominantly traditional activities adapted to the studio environment.

> "Their measurement of <g>’s using the FCI and the Force Motion Concept Evaluation (Thornton & Sokoloff 1998) yielded values close to those characteristic of T courses (Hake 1998a,b,c)."
