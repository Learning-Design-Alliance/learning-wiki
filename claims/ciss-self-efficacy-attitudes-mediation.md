`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis (generalized linear regression models) of the 3-month condom-use effect in the randomized trial, using the Self-efficacy for Negotiating Condom-use Scale, Sexual Risk Scale, and DASS-21. Quote prints the mediated proportions and p-values as reported.

> "Improved self-efficacy accounted for 43% of the total effect (P = 0.02), whereas favorable changes in attitudes towards condom use accounted for 22% (P = 0.16). Improvements on negative mood states did not mediate the intervention effect; only 2% of the overall effect was explained by changes in depressive mood states (P = 0.71)"
