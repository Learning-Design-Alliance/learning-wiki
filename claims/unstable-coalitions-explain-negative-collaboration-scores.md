## Evidence

### Taraman 2016

Taraman, S., Hassan, Y., Shawky, D., & Badawi, A. (2016). Employing Game theory and Multilevel Analysis to Predict the Factors that Affect Collaborative Learning Outcomes: An Empirical Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1610.05075](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1610.05075)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

CGT analysis using teammates' opinions as payoffs, assuming individual benefit outside collaboration is proportional to topic knowledge and coalition gains proportional to members' average score. The article reports instability justified by the tendency to form sub-coalitions and leave the grand coalition, which affected group grades.

> "This resulted in non-additive and unstable coalitions except for some dyads where grand coalitions have greater score than what each individual thinks s/he knows about the topic."
