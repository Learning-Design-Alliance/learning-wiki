Narrative review statement, citing Bandura (1997) and Zeidner (1998), on anticipatory affect in the motivational planning and activation phase. Test anxiety is identified as the most common and most researched form of academic anxiety.

> "Students who anticipate being anxious on tests and worry about doing poorly even before they begin the test can set in motion a downward spiral of maladaptive cognitions, emotions, and behaviors that lead them to do poorly on the exam"
