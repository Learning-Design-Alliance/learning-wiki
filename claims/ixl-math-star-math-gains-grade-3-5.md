# IXL Math raises Grade 3-5 mathematics achievement on the Renaissance Star Math after one semester (effect size +0.13)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a cluster RCT in Grades 3-5, IXL Math students outgained control students on the EOY Renaissance Star Math by 10 points (effect size = +0.13), a statistically significant positive impact after one semester. [→ IXL Math 2023](#ixl-math-2023)

## Evidence

### IXL Math 2023

IXL Math. (2023). IXL Math. https://www.ixl.com/help-center/article/1850087/what_are_the_technical_requirements_for_ixl

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Cluster randomized-control trial in Grades 3-5 in four elementary schools in Holland, Michigan, spring 2023; teachers (classes) were randomly assigned within grades to IXL Math (n=11) or business as usual (n=10), with 545 students (263 treatment, 282 control) analyzed. The page reports "effect size = +0.13" on the EOY Renaissance Star Math.

> "Results of quantitative analyses showed that IXL Math had a statistically significant positive impact on student mathematics achievement after one semester, with IXL Math students outgaining control students on the EOY Renaissance Star Math by 10 points (effect size = +0.13)."
