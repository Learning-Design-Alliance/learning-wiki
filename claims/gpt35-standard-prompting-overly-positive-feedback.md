## Evidence

### Jieun Han 2024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An experiment using gpt-3.5-turbo configured as an English writing teacher generated rubric-based essay feedback, which 21 English education experts rated on 7-point Likert scales for tone and helpfulness. Experts rated positiveness 5.93, directness 3.72, and helpfulness 3.41 out of 7.

> "The experts rate the feedback’s positiveness at 5.93 and directness at 3.72. This result indicates gpt-3.5-turbo’s inherent tendency to generate positive feedback."
