## Evidence

### Mengesha Assefa Kebad 2024

Mengesha Assefa Kebad, Ayele Habtamu Semagne, Misker Melshew Fenta, Beyna Alemante Tafese. (2024). Assessing the effectiveness of flipped classroom teaching–learning method among undergraduate medical students at gondar university, college of medicine and health sciences: an interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06105-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-test on a validated 5-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire in the same quasi-experiment. The flipped group's mean was "4.2 (± 0.7 SD)" versus 3.9 (± 0.5 SD); statistically significant, no effect size printed.

> "Moreover, the mean satisfaction score for the Flipped Classroom Group was 4.2 (± 0.7 SD) compared to 3.9 (± 0.5 SD) for the Traditional Lecture-based Group. The t-value was 2.34 with 98 degrees of freedom, and the p-value was 0.021."
