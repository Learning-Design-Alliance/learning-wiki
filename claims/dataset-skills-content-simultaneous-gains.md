## Evidence

### Schwortz 2020

Schwortz, A. C., & Burrows, A. C. (2020). Authentic Science Experiences with STEM Datasets: Post-secondary Results and Potential Gender Influences. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of matched scores by question type in the same matched sample: content questions showed "gains of 0.412 or an effect size of 1.01", exceeding skills-question gains of 0.349 (effect size 0.73), both described as large.

> "There was a large improvement on the content questions with gains of 0.412 or an effect size of 1.01. Skills questions also had a large gain, though a lesser one than of the content questions, of 0.349 or effect size of 0.73."
