## Evidence

### Ilić IM 2023

Ilić IM, Ilić MD. (2023). The relationship between the burnout syndrome and academic success of medical students: a cross-sectional study. Archives of Industrial Hygiene and Toxicology. https://doi.org/10.2478/aiht-2023-74-3719

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression within the GPA >8 subgroup of the 760-student cross-sectional survey, adjusted for sociodemographic and lifestyle covariates. Sedative use was reported by 7.9% of higher-GPA versus 2.6% of lower-GPA students; the article reports "adjusted OR=6.44; 95 % CI=1.80–22.99; P=0.004".

> "and use of sedatives among students with GPA >8 (adjusted OR=6.44; 95 % CI=1.80–22.99; P=0.004)"
