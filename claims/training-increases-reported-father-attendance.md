## Evidence

### Burn M. 2019

Burn M., Tully L. A., Jiang Y., Piotrowska P. J., Collins D. A. J., Sargeant K., Hawes D., Moul C., Lenroot R. K., Frick P. J., Anderson V., Kimonis E. R., Dadds M. R. (2019). Evaluating Practitioner Training to Improve Competencies and Organizational Practices for Engaging Fathers in Parenting Interventions. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-018-0836-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 (face-to-face training) Wilcoxon signed rank comparison of practitioner-reported father attendance at pre-training versus 2-month follow-up; the article reports "a significant change in scores" with z = − 4.63, p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "Analysis of practitioner reports of frequency of father attendance rates found that there was a significant change in scores from pre-training (never 4.7%, rarely 29.0%, sometimes 47.7%, often 16.6%, always, 2.1%) to follow-up (never 3.0%, rarely 15.0%, sometimes 52.0%, often 24.0%, always 6.0%), with Wilcoxon Signed Rank test z = − 4.63, p < 0.001."

### Burn M. 2019 (2)

Burn M., Tully L. A., Jiang Y., Piotrowska P. J., Collins D. A. J., Sargeant K., Hawes D., Moul C., Lenroot R. K., Frick P. J., Anderson V., Kimonis E. R., Dadds M. R. (2019). Evaluating Practitioner Training to Improve Competencies and Organizational Practices for Engaging Fathers in Parenting Interventions. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-018-0836-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 2 (online training) Wilcoxon signed rank comparison of practitioner-reported father attendance from pre-training to 2-month follow-up, showing "improvements in practitioner-reported ratings of father attendance" with z = − 2.66, p = 0.008. No effect size is printed.

> "Analysis of practitioner reports of frequency of father attendance found there were significant changes in scores from pre-training (never 1.9%, rarely 33.0%, sometimes 43.3%, often 15.7%, always 6.1%) to follow-up (never 3.8%, rarely 17.9%, sometimes 46.2%, often 28.3%, always 3.8%), with Wilcoxon Signed Rank test z = − 2.66, p = 0.008, indicating improvements in practitioner-reported ratings of father attendance from pre-training to follow-up."
