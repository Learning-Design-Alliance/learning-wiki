SCL measured while participants approached flowers was analyzed with a 2 × 4 × 2 × 3 ANOVA in the VR study. A significant group by reinforcement rate interaction (F(2, 459) = 6.43, ηp² = 0.03) and a small group main effect (ηp² = 0.01) were found; "learners, compared to non-learners, had signi ﬁcantly lower SCL across the task".
