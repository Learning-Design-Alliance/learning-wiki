Jacob 2026¶

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., Schneider, H., & Peters Hinton, V. (2026). Institutional conditions for integrating emerging technologies in higher education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/294

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus group finding: virtually every participant stressed equity, but campuses lack hardware, connectivity, and licensing capacity; pilots funded by grants struggled to scale, and faculty abandoned tools that were not centrally provided because only some students could afford subscriptions.

"The tension between intention and reality surfaced repeatedly. “Equity comes into the conversation whenever we’re talking about choosing a technology at an enterprise level,” one IT leader noted."
