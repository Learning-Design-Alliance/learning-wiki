Zongran Wang, Liu Mingzhuo & A.Y.M. Atiquil Islam. (2026). Reimagining teacher-AI co-design in learning task design: trends and perspectives. HUMANITIES AND SOCIAL SCIENCES COMMUNICATIONS. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-06981-y

Thematic synthesis of 28 empirical studies (2015-2025) on teacher-AI co-design of learning tasks. The review's thematic coding found "GenAI tools are predominantly employed to support lesson planning, prompt generation, and creative ideation" with traditional AI used for analytics and feedback design.
