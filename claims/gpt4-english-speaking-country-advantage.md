## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In subgroup 1 meta-analysis, GPT-4's pooled accuracy was 86% (95% CI 82-89) for English-speaking countries versus 80% (95% CI 76-83) for non-English-speaking countries in original languages, a statistically significant difference (P=.02); GPT-3.5 showed 57% versus 58% with no significant difference (P=.72).
