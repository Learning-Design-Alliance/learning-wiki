## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2019

Tiffin Paul A, Paton Lewis W. (2019). Does 'online confidence' predict application success and later academic performance in medical school? A UK-based national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034437

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Univariable and adjusted logistic models predicting self-reported ethnicity in the full confidence-rating sample. The printed univariable odds ratio was OR = 0.18 (0.15 to 0.22, p<0.001); the article concludes the inter-ethnic group differences for confidence bias "appear to be an artefact of performance on the related cognitive test", while the confidence judgement relationship remained significant after adjustment (OR 0.74, 0.64 to 0.87, p<0.001).

> "Self-reported ethnicity ('white' vs 'black') can be predicted from confidence bias score (OR 0.18, 0.15 to 0.22, p<0.001). However, once the influence of performance on the decision analysis subtest is controlled for, this relationship with 'confidence bias' score disappears (OR 1.04, 0.84 to 1.29, p=0.7)."
