### Wyman 1921

Wyman, J. B., & Wendle, M. (1921). What is Reading Ability? The Journal of Educational Psychology, 518-531. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=%22What+is+Reading+Ability%22

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grade VIII reading study reliability analysis: composition (two written topics) yielded the lowest reliability coefficient in the battery. The general conclusion adds that "the average score on from 15 to 20 compositions must be taken" if compositions measure ability. Reliability coefficients are printed, but no standardised effect size, so no magnitude is coded.

> "If compositions are to be used, by applying Brown's formula it can be seen that in order to have a relia- bility coefficient comparable to the Terman (0.85) it would be necessary to give 17 compositions"
