### Wood Thomas Charles 2022

Wood Thomas Charles, Maqsood Sundas, Saunders Alex, Sancha William, Nanavaty Mayank A., Wearne Michael, Rajak Saul. (2022). Non-technical skills simulation-based training model for managing intraoperative posterior capsule rupture during cataract surgery. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-022-01962-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Questionnaire data from 13 of 17 participants (four removed for incomplete responses) collected via QualtricsXM after the training intervention and repeat simulation. The article reports percentages of respondents agreeing on training need, NTS importance, enjoyment, realism and educational benefit; no effect sizes are printed.

> "100% of responding surgeons (n = 13) were in agreement that further training is required in order to prepare ophthalmic surgery trainees for managing intraoperative complications."
