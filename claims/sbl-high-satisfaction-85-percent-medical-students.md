## Evidence

### Agha Sajida 2015

Agha Sajida, Alhamrani Asma Y., Khan Muhammad A. (2015). Satisfaction of medical students with simulation based learning. Saudi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.15537/smj.2015.6.11501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 115 third- and fourth-year medical students at KSAU-HS using a 40-item self-developed 5-point Likert questionnaire (alpha 0.73). The study found "The overall satisfaction scores with SBL were high (85%)"; no effect size was printed.

> "The overall satisfaction scores with SBL were high (85%), as most of them considered it a useful addition to learning, and felt the need for more training sessions with simulators (71%)."
