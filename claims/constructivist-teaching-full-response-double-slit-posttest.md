## Evidence

### Kocakulah and Kural 2010

Mustafa Sabri Kocakulah, Mehmet Kural. (2010). Investigation of conceptual change about double-slit interference in secondary school physics. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ908941

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest of the conceptual understanding test in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control-group study with 41 eleventh-grade students. The article reports that "the full response was provided by about 38% of the experiment and 10% of the control group students"; no effect size or significance test is printed for this contrast.

> "Nevertheless, the full response was provided by about 38% of the experiment and 10% of the control group students and this is where the difference is first revealed."
