University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-preventable-failure

Districtwide trend analysis of publicly reported CPS rates shows the on-track rate "increased from 58 to 89 percent" over the decade after the indicator became a reform focus. No effect size is printed.
