q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of five non-randomized studies (727 participants) assessing professional English proficiency, defined as translating specialized literature and mastering discipline-specific terminology. Substantial heterogeneity was observed (I² = 99%, T² = 0.12, p < 0.001).

"A random effects model revealed a RD of 0.59, with a 95% CI ranging from 0.29 to 0.90. These findings indicate a statistically significant improvement in professional English proficiency following the implementation of bilingual instruction in immunology"
