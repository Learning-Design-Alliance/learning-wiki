## Evidence

### Bruno Sauce 2021

Bruno Sauce, John Wiedenhoeft, Nicholas Judd and Torkel Klingberg. (2021). Change by challenge: A common genetic basis behind childhood cognitive development and cognitive training. npj Science of Learning 6:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00096-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A general linear model ('baseline WM model') with baseline WM as outcome and sample, baseline age, gender and cogPGS as predictors, run on the combined 344-participant sample. The article reports "β = 0.10, p = 0.03" and "1.0% of variance explained".

> "Using the baseline WM model, we found that cogPGS signiﬁcantly explained the baseline variation in WM ( β = 0.10, p = 0.03). That translates into 1.0% of variance explained by cogPGS after accounting for the effect of gender and age differences."
