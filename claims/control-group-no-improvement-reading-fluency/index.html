Evidence¶

Ayed H. Ziadat & Mohammad Soud A. AL-Awan 2018¶

Ayed H. Ziadat & Mohammad Soud A. AL-Awan. (2018). The Effectiveness of Neurological Impress Method on Reading Fluency of Students with Learning Disabilities in Amman, Jordan. International Education Studies, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p165

Authors' statement in the Results and Discussion section about the no-treatment control group across the sixteen-week period. The control group's printed means were 1.79 (pre) and 1.84 (post); no statistical test of this within-group change is printed.
