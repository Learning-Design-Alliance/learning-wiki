q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theme Analysis of narratives in the teacher–student relationship subcategory identified sexist attitudes of (male) teachers; one participant reported body-critical comments that “created great remorse” and another described a teacher with complaints for inappropriate sexist behaviors.

"My PE teacher increased the rejection I felt toward my body. He made comments like: “you can't waste your body this way”, or … “you're going to lose all your potential”."

Related Claims¶

  • In compulsory secondary education PE, anger and fear predominated, driven mainly by physical fitness testing, grading pressures and “exposed body” experiences — related
  • Group formation via the captain technique, soccer dominance in free play, content repetition and public anthropometric exposure generated fear, anger, sadness and disgust, especially among girls — related
  • Participants' emotional trajectories shifted progressively from predominantly positive emotions in primary PE to more negative, intense and frequent emotions in secondary PE — related
  • Joy was the predominant emotion in compulsory primary education PE, linked to diverse content, cooperative games, absence of competitiveness and cathartic release — related
  • Sex instruction should work by countering motives with motives, using fear, disgust, self-respect and desire for the regard of others against sexual carelessness — related
