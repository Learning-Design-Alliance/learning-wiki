### Schlosser‐Hupf 2025

Schlosser‐Hupf, Sophie, Staudacher, Jonas, Wagner, Verena, Unger, Mira, Sousa, Paula, Donning, Inga Marie, Müller‐Schilling, Martina, Heinrich, Henriette, and the GENIUS Study Group. (2025). Harmonising Gastroenterology Training: An Analysis of Gastroenterology Training Curricula of the United European Gastroenterology Member Societies. United European Gastroenterology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1002/ueg2.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of assessment and examination structures across the 42 national curricula. The study reports that "Structured assessment tools were inconsistently employed", with DOPS in 9.5% (4/42), Mini‐CEX in 2.4%, EPAs in 7.1% (3/42), logbooks in 42.9% (18/42), and formal knowledge examinations in 71.4% (30/42).

> "Structured assessment tools were inconsistently employed: DOPS was included in 9.5% (4/42) of the curricula, while Mini‐Clinical Evaluation Exercises (Mini‐CEX) were used in only one curriculum (2.4%). The majority of curricula did not specify how competencies should be assessed."
