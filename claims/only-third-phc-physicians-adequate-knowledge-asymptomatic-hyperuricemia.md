Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 201 PHC physicians in Jeddah's Ministry of Health centers. Adequate knowledge was defined as >20/40 correct answers; the reported result was that mean knowledge scores (18.1) was generally low, with "only 32.8% of participants having adequate knowledge about AH". No effect size is printed.
