Moganadas SR 2026¶

Moganadas SR, Marín-González F, Nun SH and Gan CL (2026) Generative AI as a didactic-pedagogical mediator: rethinking human roles and pedagogical design in higher education. Front. Educ. 11:1856839. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1856839

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

This is the article's own statement of its second contribution, derived from a focused integrative interdisciplinary synthesis rather than empirical measurement. The synthesis draws on literature including Chi and Wylie's active, constructive, and interactive engagement framework. No effect size is reported; the support is conceptual argument.

"Second, it strengthens the theorization of learner agency by showing that prompting, questioning, verification, comparison, revision, and disciplinary judgment are central to knowledge construction in human-AI interaction."
