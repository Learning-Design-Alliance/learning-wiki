## Evidence

### Dorsett 2014

Dorsett, R., Rienzo, C., Rolfe, H., Burns, H., Robertson, B.-A., Thorpe, B., & Wall, K. (2014). Mind the Gap: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581160

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation finding from the randomised controlled trial: because randomisation assigned eligibility for the animation course rather than attendance, and "72% of pupils eligible for the animation course did not attend any sessions", the estimates are of the effect of eligibility, not participation.

> "Participation in the course was very low: 72% of pupils eligible for the animation course did not attend any sessions. The results must therefore be regarded as estimating the effect of eligibility for the animation course (alone or in combination with teacher training, as appropriate) rather than participation in it."
