## Evidence

### Egüz 2019

Egüz, Ş., Öztürk, C. & Kesten, A. (2019). Technology-supported global education: A mixed method study. Pegem Eğitim ve Öğretim Dergisi, 9(4), 1209-1244. http://dx.doi.org/10.14527/pegegog.2019.040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-factor repeated-measures ANOVA for the 44-student experimental group; printed means pre-test 54.55, post-test 82.55, retention 74.18; Bonferroni post-hoc showed differences 2>1 and 3>1; no effect size printed.

> "there was a statistically significant difference between the pre -test, post-test and retention test scores of the experimental group students [ F (2.86)=48.35, p<.05]"

### Egüz 2019 (2)

Egüz, Ş., Öztürk, C. & Kesten, A. (2019). Technology-supported global education: A mixed method study. Pegem Eğitim ve Öğretim Dergisi, 9(4), 1209-1244. http://dx.doi.org/10.14527/pegegog.2019.040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-factor repeated-measures ANOVA for the control group; printed means pre-test 50.54, post-test 60.68, retention 39.20; Bonferroni post-hoc showed 2>1, 3<1 and 2>3, i.e. retention dropped below pre-test; no effect size printed.

> "there is a statistically significant difference between the pre -test, post-test and retention test scores of the  control group. [F (2.80)=20.20, p<.05]"
