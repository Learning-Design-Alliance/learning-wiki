### Gómez‐Torres 2026

Gómez‐Torres, P., S. Galarreta‐Aperte, I. Blázquez‐Ornat, I. Antón‐Solanas, and E. Ramón‐Arbués. (2026). Nursing Students' Perceptions of a Clinical Simulation Experience in Community Care: Strengths and Limitations. Nursing Open 13, no. 9: e70807. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70807

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of reflective narratives from 103 students, coded in ATLAS.ti by two authors with a third resolving differences. Students described scenarios as "realistic and educationally valuable" for applying theory, reasoning and communication.

> "Students described the scenarios as realistic and educationally valuable because they supported the application of theoretical knowledge, clinical reasoning, communication practice and collaborative learning in a safe environment."
