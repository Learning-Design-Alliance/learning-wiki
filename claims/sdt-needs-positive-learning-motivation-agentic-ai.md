## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dhanya Pramod and Kanchan Pranay Patil 2026

Dhanya Pramod and Kanchan Pranay Patil. (2026). Agentic AI in educational environments and its association with social and motivational pathways to learning performance. Discover Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-026-02168-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Cross-sectional perception-based survey of 398 business-school students in India analyzed with PLS-SEM; the study reports that "perceived relatedness (β = 0.189, 95% CI: 0.109–0.272) had a positive relationship with learning motivation" alongside the autonomy and competence paths.

> "Perceived autonomy (β = 0.504, 95% CI: 0.442–0.560), perceived competence (β = 0.425, 95% CI: 0.358–0.491), and perceived relatedness (β = 0.189, 95% CI: 0.109–0.272) had a positive relationship with learning motivation."
