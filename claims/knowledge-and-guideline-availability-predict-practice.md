`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression from the same 320-worker survey predicting adequate practice. The analysis found "Good knowledge was a key independent predictor of better practice (AOR = 2.1; 95% CI: 1.3–3.5)" and on-site guidelines raised odds of adequate practice (AOR = 1.9; 95% CI: 1.2–3.1).

> "Good knowledge was a key independent predictor of better practice (AOR = 2.1; 95% CI: 1.3–3.5). Furthermore, the physical availability of clinical guidelines at the facility significantly increased the odds of adequate practice (AOR = 1.9; 95% CI: 1.2–3.1)"
