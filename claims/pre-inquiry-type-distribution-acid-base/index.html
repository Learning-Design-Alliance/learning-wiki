Evidence¶

Kang and Noh 2017¶

Houn Tae Kang, Suk Goo Noh. (2017). The Effect on Elementary Science Education Based on Student's Pre-inquiry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050908

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive frequency analysis of the 52 pre-inquiries collected by questionnaire from the experimental class before the 'Acid and Base' unit, classified using Good's question types. "29 information inquiries (55.7%)" formed the largest category.

"there were 10 explanation inquiries (19.2%), 29 information inquiries (55.7%), 7 procedural inquiries (13.4%), 2 confirmation inquiries (3.8%), 4 non-task curiosity inquiries (7.9%)."

Kang and Noh 2017 (2)¶

Houn Tae Kang, Suk Goo Noh. (2017). The Effect on Elementary Science Education Based on Student's Pre-inquiry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050908

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Selection procedure: seven fellow teachers and one science education specialist chose 24 of the 52 pre-inquiries as meaningful for the unit. The article notes non-task curiosity and procedural questions "are few in number, but they are not of low quality".

"Among the 52 pre-inquiries presented by the students, 7 fellow teachers and 1 science education specialist were selected and 24 meaningful pre-inquiries related to the ‘Acid and Base’ unit and applicable to the class were selected."
