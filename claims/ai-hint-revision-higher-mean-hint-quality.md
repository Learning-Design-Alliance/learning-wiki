## Evidence

### Anjali Singh 2024

Anjali Singh, Christopher Brooks, Xu Wang, Warren Li, Juho Kim, Deepti Wilson. (2024). Bridging Learnersourcing and AI: Exploring the Dynamics of Student-AI Collaborative Feedback Generation. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636853](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636853)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled experiment with 72 Master's students in a data visualization course compared hint quality across two conditions using a mixed-model crossover design. The article reports that students in the AI-hint-revision condition "wrote hints with higher mean values of Full Accuracy, Specificity, and Utility in each reflection". Not all of these results were statistically significant.

> "Compared to the students in the Hint-writing condition, students in the AI-hint-revision condition wrote hints with higher mean values of Full Accuracy, Specificity, and Utility in each reflection, and higher mean value of Phrasing in reflection-2"
