## Evidence

### Dicke 2026

Dicke, T., Ferber, K., Devine, E., Collie, R., Granziera, H., Parker, P., Guo, J., Marsh, H., et al. (2026). Association of Teacher Well-Being With Student Motivation and Achievement: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10216-6

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Three-level meta-analysis of 186 articles and 811 effect sizes from 355,637 teachers, pooling correlations between teacher well-being indicators and student outcomes. The review reports "small to moderate associations between student outcomes and teacher well-being (r = .18)". Effect sizes were transformed to Fisher's z and back-transformed to r.

> "Based on 186 articles, 811 effect sizes, and data from 355,637 teachers, we investigate these relationships through the lens of the Job Demands–Resources Model, Transactional Stress Model, Self-Determination Theory, and the PERMA model. Using three-level meta-analytic models, we found small to moderate associations between student outcomes and teacher well-being (r = .18)"
