Mustafa Karadağ, Resul Ağirtaş, Atilla Pulur. (2018). Effects of Peer Education on Attitudes of Secondary School Students Towards the Course of Physical Education and Sports. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i4a.3171

> "there is significant difference (p <.05) in the opinion item 1 “I have no interest in anything related to Physical Education and Sport s”. While the students expressed, at the pretest, their opinion about this opinion item at the level of “I totally do not agree”"
