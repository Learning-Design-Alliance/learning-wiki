Kelley 1916¶

Kelley, Truman Lee. (1916). A constructive ability test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VII, No. 1. https://www.google.com/search?q=Kelley+1916+A+Constructive+Ability+Test+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Applied examination of 250 delinquent boys using the test and grading scale. The article reports their average merit was 51.3 and that they "showed nearly as great backwardness in such other mental tests as Binet and completion tests", offered as evidence the test is highly correlated with mentality.

"These boys, of average age 15.20, showed an average accomplishment equal to that of the normal 9.5 year old. The average merit of the structures built by these boys is 51.3, ap-proximately represented by Plate IX."
