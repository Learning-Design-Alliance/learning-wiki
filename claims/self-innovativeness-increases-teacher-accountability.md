## Evidence

### Vidergor Hava E 2023

Vidergor Hava E. (2023). The effect of teachers' self- innovativeness on accountability, distance learning self-efficacy, and teaching practices. Computers & Education, 199, 104777. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM path analysis of questionnaire data from 200 Israeli elementary and secondary teachers found the largest direct effect of self-innovativeness on accountability (0.479); Table 4 reports the direct effect as .483, t = 7.751, p = .000, described as "a high effect size on Teachers' Accountability (0.483)".

> "Results of the path analysis indicated an influence of Teachers' Self-Innovativeness on Teachers' Accountability (0.479), a lower influence on Teachers' DL Self-Efficacy (0.221), and Teachers' DL Teaching Practices (0.196)."
