## Evidence

### First Step to Success: WWC Intervention Report 2012

First Step to Success: WWC Intervention Report. (2012). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/179

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two RCTs (46 kindergarteners in Oregon; about 198 students in grades 1–3 in New Mexico). Domain-average effect size was 0.76 (+28 improvement index); Walker et al. (1998) reported effect sizes such as "0.97" for academic engaged time and 1.17 for ESP Adaptive Behavior, all favoring the intervention group.

> "Walker et al. (1998) found, and the WWC confirmed, four positive and statistically significant differences between treatment and comparison groups on academic engaged time, the Child Behavior Checklist–Teacher Report Forms (CBCL-TRF) Aggression Subscale, the Early Screening Project (ESP) Adaptive Behavior Subscale, and the ESP Maladaptive Behavior Subscale."
