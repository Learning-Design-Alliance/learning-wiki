`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test results for the same quasi-experiment: the endline-to-post-training comparison yielded p = 0.061 for the intervention group and p = 0.095 for the comparison group, neither reaching the .05 threshold; means are reported descriptively. No effect size is printed.

> "The trend of means from baseline, endline and post-training measures for the 15 intervention group project were (70.4, 102.1 & 119.8), while the control teams means were (42.9, 54.6 & 58.7) respectively."
