The review reports Agarwal et al.'s cross-sectional study comparing Chat-GPT 3.5, Bard, and Bing on reasoning-based physiology MCQs referenced to the Indian NMC 11-module curriculum. The review reports validity and difficulty differences between the named tools without reproducing the printed p values.

> "Chat-GPT's generated MCQs were significantly more valid than the other AI tools examined in the study. However, the difficulty level was lower compared to Bard and Bing"
