This is a meta-analytic finding cited within the review's ITS synthesis section. The review notes it was published over five years prior, calling for updated effectiveness research.

> "Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper (2014) found that ITS had a moderate effect of students' learning, and that ITS were less effective that human tutoring, but ITS outperformed all other instruction methods (such as traditional classroom instruction, reading printed or digital text, or homework assignments)."
