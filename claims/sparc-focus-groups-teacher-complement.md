`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses and 4 focus groups (3-6 students each) conducted after gameplay in the pilots. Students described SPARC as "a novel learning experience that they found to be a good complement to teacher instruction" and reported high engagement.

> "In focus groups, students wanted more interactive activities like SPARC, but also valued the role that teachers played in providing the foundational information."
