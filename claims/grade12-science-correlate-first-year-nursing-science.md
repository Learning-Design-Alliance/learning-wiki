# Grade 12 physical science, life science and mathematics grades each correlate positively and significantly with first-year nursing science module grades

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Spearman correlations between grade 12 physical science, life science and mathematics grades and first-year science module grades are positive and significant (rs = 0.522, 0.478, 0.464 respectively, p < 0.01). [→ Mthimunye 2017](#mthimunye-2017)

## Evidence

### Mthimunye 2017

Mthimunye, K.D.T. & Daniels, F.M., 2017, ‘Performance in grade 12 mathematics and science predicts student nurses’ performance in first year science modules at a university in the Western Cape’, Curationis 40(1), a1776. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v40i1.1776

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of grade 12 subject grades against first-year science module grades for 226 nursing students, reported in the Results section. Printed correlation coefficients (effect sizes): rs = 0.522 (physical science), rs = 0.478 (life science) and rs = 0.464 (mathematics), all "significant positive correlation" at p < 0.01.

> "The results of the Spearman correlation (rs) analysis (Table 2) indicate a significant positive correlation between the following variables: physical science and first year science modules (0.522, p < 0.01); life science and first year science modules (0.478, p < 0.01); and mathematics and first year science modules (0.464, p < 0.01)."
