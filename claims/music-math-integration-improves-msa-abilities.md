## Subclaims
`q2 i3` After a five-week music-math integrated intervention, students in both grade levels showed statistically significant improvements in all three MSA areas (modeling, strategy, application) with p values less than 0.001 and Cohen's d > 1.66. [→ An et al. 2013](#an-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### An et al. 2013

Song An, Mary Margaret Capraro, Daniel A. Tillman. (2013). Elementary Teachers Integrate Music Activities into Regular Mathematics Lessons: Effects on Students' Mathematical Abilities. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/0js732gf

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with multiple pretests and posttests (10 MSA observations) in one first-grade class (n=21) and one third-grade class (n=25) across five weekly music-integrated lessons. The article reports "p values on all three MSA areas less than 0.001" and "Cohen's d > 1.66" for both grades.

> "In general, the ability levels of the first and third grade students in all three mathematical areas as assessed by MSA showed statistically-significant improvements after the intervention (see Table 4 & 5) with p values on all three MSA areas less than 0.001. Large effect sizes were found in both first grade and third grade students before and after three MSA areas with Cohen's d > 1.66."
