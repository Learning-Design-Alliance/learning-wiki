### MacKenzie R K 2016

MacKenzie R K, Cleland J A, Ayansina D, Nicholson S. (2016). Does the UKCAT predict performance on exit from medical school? A national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-011313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate multilevel results for the national cohort, with UKCAT scores ranging from 1630 to 3380 (a spread of 1750 points). The article reports this corresponds to a 10.5-point UKFPO difference between highest and lowest UKCAT scores.

> "An increase of 333, 500, 333 and 167 in the total UKCAT cognitive score increases the average SJT, EPM decile, EPM total and UKFPO scores by one mark, respectively."
