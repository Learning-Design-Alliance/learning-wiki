## Evidence

### Dahlkemper 2023

Dahlkemper, M. N., Lahme, S. Z., & Klein, P. (2023). How do physics students evaluate artificial intelligence responses on comprehension questions? A study on the perceived scientific accuracy and linguistic quality. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010142](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010142)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analyses for question 3 (fluid dynamics) comparing ChatGPT response R3.4 with the sample solution R3.6. The article reports linguistic quality "does not diﬀer between R3.4 and R3.6" (p = 0.28) but significantly higher scientific accuracy for the expert solution (p = 0.01).

> "the perception of linguistic quality does not diﬀer between R3.4 and R3.6 [T(92)=1.09, p=0.28]; however, the expert solution has signiﬁcantly higher rat- ings regarding the scientiﬁc accuracy than the ChatGPT response R3.4 [T(92)=2.82, p=0.01]."
