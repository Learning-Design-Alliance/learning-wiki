`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Concept-test frequency analysis (Table 3). Pre-test, 6 students in each group showed complete understanding and misconceptions converged on "It is the name used for mausoleums" and "It is the name referring to the remnants of the old"; post-test complete-understanding counts rose to 18 (experimental) and 12 (control).

> "In the post-test, on the other hand, 18 students from the experimental group and 12 from the control group provided complete understanding responses to the question about the concept, noting "It is the name referring to the naturally occurring entities"."
