Evidence¶

Ravesh and Tabrizi 2017¶

Mahnaz Mahmoudi Ravesh, Hossein Heidari Tabrizi. (2017). The Effect of Teaching Interlanguage Pragmatics on Interpretation Ability of Iranian Translation Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 8(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.3p.44

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t test on pre-test checklist scores of both groups (means 20.13 treatment, 19.53 control) showed no significant difference; the article concludes participants did not have a reasonable performance on the pre-test. No effect size is printed.

"According to Table 5, the value Sig. = 0.483 (p > 0.05) indicates that, in the pre-test, the groups' performances were not different because no significant difference was seen."
