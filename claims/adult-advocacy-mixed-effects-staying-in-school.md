`q3 i?` Of three studies including adult advocates as a key component, two interventions showed positive or potentially positive effects on staying in or progressing in school, while a third showed no discernible effect. [→ Dynarski 2008](#dynarski-2008)

The panel's review of five experimental studies of four dropout interventions with adult advocacy components. It found 'a fairly wide variation in observed effects' and that no study assessed the discrete effect of advocacy, warranting only a moderate rating. No effect sizes are printed.
