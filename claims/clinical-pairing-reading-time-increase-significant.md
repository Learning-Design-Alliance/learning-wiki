`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Author-reported curricular evaluation of a paired clinical-reading schedule, with satisfaction and reading time self-reported before and after the intervention. The article reports residents "spent significantly more time reading after the intervention"; both measured outcomes were self-reported and no effect size is printed.
