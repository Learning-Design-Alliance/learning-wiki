Pairwise comparisons in the ECLS-K:2011 ANOVA showed the gender pattern. The article reports "Females earned higher scores than males across income levels and ComDis status for reading," with the reverse for math and science.

> "Females earned higher scores than males across income levels and ComDis status for reading. For math and science, the reverse was true, males earned higher scores than females across income levels and ComDis status, with the exception of the middle-income group for mathematics, for which there was no significant gender difference."
