## Evidence

### Ashleigh Leary 2019

Ashleigh Leary, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). The difficulties associated with integrating computation into undergraduate physics. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence on the instructor-time force: one interviewee prioritized "getting tenure" and was unsure how integrating computation would fit, illustrating how existing obligations crowd out learning and integrating new techniques.

> "[I have other goals] right now too...Chief of which is you know getting tenure. So while being an agent of change to include computation more broadly could be something that enhances my tenure proﬁle. I'm not quite sure how I would do that right now."
