Evidence¶

Goetz 2006¶

Goetz, T., Frenzel, A. C., Pekrun, R., Hall, N. C., & Lüdtke, O. (2006). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation models including midterm grades as manifest achievement variables alongside latent domain-specific emotion factors. The article states "positive relations were found between positive emotions" and negative emotions showed negative relations, indicating high convergent and discriminant validity of the domain-specific emotion factors.

"Overall, positive relations were found between positive emotions (enjoyment, pride) and achievement, and negative relations were found between negative emotions (anxiety, anger, boredom) and achievement."
