## Evidence

### van Sassen C 2025

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on feedback specificity in the 62-supervisor vignette experiment: valence had a large effect (η² = 0.76); vignette outcome did not affect specificity (F1,61 = 0.70, P = .41), and the interaction was nonsignificant.

> "the valence of the feedback had a significant effect on the specificity of the idea units (F1,61 = 191.36, P < .001, η2 = 0.76): negative feedback contained a statistically significant higher proportion of specific idea units than positive feedback (mean [SE], 0.88 [0.02] vs 0.44 [0.03]; P < .001)."
