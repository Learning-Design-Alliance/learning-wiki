`q1 i?` For this assessment, LDF is judged superior because its assumptions are probably not violated, it handles nonuniform DIF, and its advantages are operational here. [→ Holweger 1998](#holweger-1998)

## Evidence

### Holweger 1998

Holweger, Nancy; Weston, Timothy. (1998). Differential Item Functioning: An Applied Comparison of the Item Characteristic Curve Method with the Logistic Discriminant Function Method. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED422362

`q1 · i?`

Authors' evaluative conclusion (type e), not a tested result: the IRT advantages of model specificity and sample independence "are not fully realized in the assessment used in this study," while LDF's simultaneous calculation, significance test, and ease of use are operational.

> "Clearly, in the case of this assessment instrument, the LDF method is superior to the ICC method for the calculation of DIF."
