## Evidence

### Blanchard E 2022

Blanchard E, Booker D, Peterson DT, Carter T. (2022). Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice: Application to Adult Advanced Life Support. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11269

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey of 21 participants from the eight offerings of the RCDP ACLS curriculum at a single institution, requiring agreement rankings on experience quality. The survey found "95% of respondents strongly agreed that this activity would improve their performance in a clinical setting." No effect size was reported.

> "Ninety-five percent of respondents strongly agreed that this activity would improve their performance in a clinical setting, 86% strongly agreed that the educational objectives had been met, and 100% agreed that the feedback they received was valuable."
