Barbieri 2016¶

Barbieri, C. & Booth, J.L. (2016). Support for struggling students in algebra: Contributions of incorrect worked examples. Learning and Individual Differences, 48, 36–44. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Support+for+struggling+students+in+algebra%22+Barbieri+Booth+incorrect+worked+examples. doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2016.04.001

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Classroom experiment: 125 middle-school students randomly assigned within classroom to problem-solving (n=41), correct examples (n=44), or incorrect examples (n=40) worksheets over a systems-of-equations unit. The 3x2 RMANOVA showed Condition F(2,122)=1.040, p=.357; all students improved from pre- to post-test.

"participants demonstrated improvement from pre- to post-test4 but this did not vary by condition providing no supporting evidence for Hypothesis 1."
