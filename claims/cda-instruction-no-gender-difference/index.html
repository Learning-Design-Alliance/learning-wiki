Evidence¶

Javadi 2019¶

Javadi, J., & Mohammadi, M. (2019). The Effect of Critical Discourse-Based Instruction on Iranian English Major Students’ Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 33-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201136

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent t-test on pre-to-post gains of male and female students in the experimental groups; the printed table gives t = 0.528, P = 0.600, and the article reports males gained 07.73 and females 07.23, a null finding with no effect size reported.

"Since the probability level is significantly higher than 0.05, there is no significant difference between the two groups. In a nutshell, it cannot be said that critical discourse-based instruction varies between genders."
