### Pears 2016

Pears, K. C., Kim, H. K., Fisher, P., & Yoerger, K. (2016). Increasing pre-kindergarten early literacy skills in children with developmental disabilities and delays. Journal of School Psychology, 57, 15-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2016.05.004

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of preschool service records (scheduled service hours coded into categories) against literacy measures in the study sample. The article reports no significant associations except a negative one with emergent writing ratings; no effect size is printed.

> "Interestingly, neither hours of individual ECSE nor hours of ECSE consultation and training services were significantly associated with literacy with the exception of a significant negative association between individual ECSE services and teacher ratings of emergent writing."
