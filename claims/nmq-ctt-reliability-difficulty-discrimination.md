CTT analysis of 429 pre-instruction NMQ attempts, computing difficulty, discrimination (Kelley 27% method) and KR-20. The two low-discrimination items (NMQ32 FTR and FTR NMQ36) were answered correctly by almost all students.

> "For the full version of the NMQ, the KR-20 reliability was calculated to be 0 .94, which indicates a high level of internal consistency."
