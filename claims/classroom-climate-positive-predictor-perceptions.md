## Evidence

### Wilson 2024

Wilson, J., Zhang, F., Palermo, C., Cordero, T. C., Myers, M. C., Eacker, H., Potter, A., & Coles, J. (2024). Predictors of middle school students’ perceptions of automated writing evaluation. Computers & Education, 211, 104985. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104985

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression results showing classroom climate block contributions. Teacher caring predicted usability and usefulness in fall, while classroom belonging predicted usefulness in spring. Climate was a consistent but moderate predictor.

> "Controlling for districts, students reporting more optimal classroom climate also reported higher usability, usefulness, and desirability for MI Write."
