## Evidence

### Wang Yuzhi 2024

Wang Yuzhi, Wilder Samantha, Hijazi Mahmoud, Myles Marquisha D., Mirza Mahin, Van Til Monica, Maatman Thomas, Ghani Khurshid R., Lane Brian R., Rogers Craig G. (2024). Surgeon Skill and Perioperative Outcomes in Robot-Assisted Partial Nephrectomy. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.21696

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational quality improvement analysis of 10 surgeons' registry outcomes, risk-adjusted via patient-level mixed-effects logistic regression with patients nested within surgeons. Each 1-point higher overall SPaN score was associated with "a 6.8% lower rate (95% CI, −8.3% to −5.2%) of prolonged hospital LOS" and lower EBL, PSM, ED visit, and readmission rates (P < .001 for all).

> "1 point higher in overall score was significantly associated with a 6.8% lower rate (95% CI, −8.3% to −5.2%) of prolonged hospital LOS, a 2.6% lower rate (95% CI, −3.0% to −2.1%) of extensive EBL"
