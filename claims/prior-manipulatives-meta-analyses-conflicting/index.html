The review reports, citing Parham (1983) and Sutawidjaja (1987), conflicting pooled effects; it also reports Domino (2010) found an overall effect size of 0.50 for physical manipulatives in grades K-6 from 1989 to 2010. These are second-hand figures from prior syntheses.

"Parham (1983) obtained a mean effect size of 1.03 for the effect of manipulative use on student achievement compared to nonuse in grades 1-6, whereas Sutawidjaja’s (1987) meta-analysis focused on primary grades students found an overall mean effect size of zero"
