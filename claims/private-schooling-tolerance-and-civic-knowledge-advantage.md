## Evidence

### Shakeel 2024

Shakeel, M. D., Wolf, P. J., Johnson, A. H., Harris, M. A., & Morris, S. R. (2024). The Public Purposes of Private Education: a Civic Outcomes Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09874-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

RVE subgroup results from the meta-analysis (Table 2): political tolerance pooled ES 0.120 (SE 0.028, 81 effects, 18 databases), significant at the 99% CI. The article states the evidence is "especially strong" for political tolerance.

> "Private school attendance is significantly correlated with higher levels of political tolerance (0.120 SD, 99% CI), political participation (0.180 SD, 90% CI), civic engagement (0.161 SD, 99% CI), civic knowledge/skills (0.061 SD, 95% CI), political knowledge (0.160 SD, 99% CI), and social capital (0.088 SD, 90% CI)."

### Shakeel 2024 (2)

Shakeel, M. D., Wolf, P. J., Johnson, A. H., Harris, M. A., & Morris, S. R. (2024). The Public Purposes of Private Education: a Civic Outcomes Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09874-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

RVE confirmatory analysis (Table 2): the civic knowledge and skills category pools to 0.121 SD (SE 0.036, 57 effects, 14 databases), significant at the 99% CI. The article states the evidence is "especially strong" for political knowledge and skills.

> "positive for political knowledge and skills (0.121 SD, 99% CI)"
