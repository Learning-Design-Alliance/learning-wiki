Evidence¶

E. B. Skaggs 1918¶

E. B. Skaggs. (1918). The Correlation of General Intelligence Tests and School Standing. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · associational · r1

The author's theoretical argument, illustrated from four years of limited use of prominent mental tests, that tests have decided limitations as predictors. The article also cites Miss F. Mateer's finding that "The intelligence ratios are subject to change."

"An individual may be extremely lazy, a shirker, and yet be "very superior" in general intelligence. He may have no "stick-to-it-iveness," no ideals, and yet stand high intellectually. The emotional life of the individual is little touched by the tests."
