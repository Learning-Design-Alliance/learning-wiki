Evidence¶

Campoli 2022¶

Campoli, A. K., & Darling-Hammond, L. (with Podolsky, A., & Levin, S.). (2022). Principal learning opportunities and school outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/438.376

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Logistic regression of teacher retention on professional development factors (Table 3) in the full professional development sample, controlling for teacher, school, and district characteristics. Printed odds ratios range from 0.99 (managing change) to 1.04 (meeting needs of diverse learners), with no significance markers.

"The overall extent of principals' professional development, as well as all the components of professional development, also appear to be positively related to teacher retention; that is, the odds that a teacher will still be teaching in the same school the following year. However, none of the relationships reaches a level of statistical significance."
