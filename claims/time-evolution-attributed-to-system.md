### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Predicative analysis of the fifth and sixth tasks. The authors offer two sustainable hypotheses for the two students (Layne and Sean), tracing each back to their characterization of the State of a Physical System.

> "Two students shared the idea that Time Evolution is attached to Physical Systems themselves (e.1.T.). This can be seen in two different ways: 1) Linguistic economy, since saying “Time Evolution of the State of a Physical System” is longer than doing the same with “Time Evolution of the Physical System”"

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017 (2)

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same analysis; the authors state Mike was the only student evidencing comprehension of this feature, quoting his answer that the equation of state as a function of time depends on the initial state.

> "An interesting conception evidenced in this study is the one emphasizing the role of initial conditions in the determination of the State, both in Classical and Quantum Mechanics (e.3.T.)."
