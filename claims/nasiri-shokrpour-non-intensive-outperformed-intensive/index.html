Narrative review reporting a comparison study by Nasiri and Shokrpour (2012) of 170 freshmen at Shiraz University of Medical Sciences, comparing an intensive reading course (10 hours/week) with a non-intensive course (4 hours/week, 68 hours total). The review reports "the students in the non-intensive group indicated significantly better performance than those in the intensive group".
