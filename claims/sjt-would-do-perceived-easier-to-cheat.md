## Evidence

### De Leng Wendy E. 2018

De Leng Wendy E., Stegers-Jager Karen M., Born Marise Ph., Themmen Axel P. N. (2018). Influence of response instructions and response format on applicant perceptions of a situational judgement test for medical school selection. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1390-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA in the randomised survey experiment (N = 372) found the instructions main effect on ease of cheating, F(1, 362) = 4.53, p = .034, η2 = .01. The article states should-do instructions are "also perceived as more difficult to fake than would-do instructions".

> "response instructions had a significant main effect on the ease-of-cheating item (F(1, 362) = 4.53, p = .034, η2 = .01) with the would-do instructions (M = 5.33, SD = 1.79) judged as easier to cheat than the should-do instructions (M = 4.92, SD = 1.86)."
