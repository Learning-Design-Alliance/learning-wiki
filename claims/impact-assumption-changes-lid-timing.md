## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Simulating the same setup with impact calculated via conservation of the sum of angular momenta, energy, or linear momenta yields lid-vertical times of t=0.50s, t=0.47s and t=0.34s respectively; the linear-momentum result is unphysical. [→ U Schröter 2009](#u-schroter-2009)
