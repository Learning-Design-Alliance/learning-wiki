## Evidence

### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in two modules shows significant modality effects on knowledge acquisition efficiency, e.g. "F = 5.843, P = 0.005, ηp2 = 0.203", with the text-graphic composite modality yielding the highest efficiency.

> "material modality significantly impacted knowledge acquisition efficiency (F = 4.105, P = 0.023, ηp2 = 0.151; F = 5.843, P = 0.005, ηp2 = 0.203), with the text-graphic composite modality yielding the highest efficiency"
