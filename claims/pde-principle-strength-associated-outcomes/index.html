A retrospective review by Engle (2012) systematically reviewed 23 studies of PDE using "rough synoptic estimates" of each study's embodiment of the four principles (strong, moderate, weak, or mixed) and evidence of three outcomes. The review reports that "studies where the four principles were most strongly embodied were also associated with the strongest PDE outcomes." No effect size is printed.
