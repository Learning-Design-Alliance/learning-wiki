### Meaney Peter Andrew 2024

Meaney Peter Andrew, Hokororo Adolfine, Ndosi Hanston, Dahlen Alex, Jacob Theopista, Mwanga Joseph R, Kalabamu Florence Salvatory, Joyce Christine Lynn, Mediratta Rishi, Rozenfeld Boris, Berg Marc, Smith Zachary Haines, Chami Neema, Mkopi Namala, Mwanga Castory, Diocles Enock, Agweyu Ambrose. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Efficacy outcome from the 6-month observational PACE study, assessed as change in conscious competence from baseline knowledge probes. The authors report 76% of the conscious competence improvement was due to reduction in unconscious incompetence, and they call the initial efficacy rate of 41% consistent with prior studies.

> "For providers who completed initial learning (n=110), average conscious competence increased 42±1 percentage points, while average unconscious incompetence decreased 31±1 percentage points."
