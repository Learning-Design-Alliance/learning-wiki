### Bellini Raffaella 2024

Bellini Raffaella, Rossettini Giacomo, Letafatkar Amir, Dell’Isola Andrea, Battista Simone. (2024). Physiotherapy students’ academic performance and satisfaction after following a chest physiotherapy course in hybrid mode: a case-control study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-76051-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theoretical knowledge was assessed at the end of the seminar with a ten-question multiple-choice quiz on the Moodle platform. The median difference of 2 had "a median difference of 2 [95% CI -0.24; 4.24]", a CI crossing zero, so the article reports no conclusive difference.

> "At the multiple-choice quiz, the remote group presented a median of 14 [12–16] and the face-to-face group of 16 [14.5–18.], with a median difference of 2 [95% CI -0.24; 4.24] in favour of the face-to-face one and an effect size of 0.61 [95%CI -0.07;1.29]."
