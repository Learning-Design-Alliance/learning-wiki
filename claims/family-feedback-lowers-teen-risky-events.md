## Evidence

### Emre Sezgin 2019

Emre Sezgin, Simon Lin. (2019). Technology-Based Interventions, Assessments, and Solutions for Safe Driving Training for Adolescents: Rapid Review. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/11942

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the Farah et al longitudinal study, in which in-vehicle data recorders were placed in cars for 12 months of teens' first driving year; the review attributes the finding that "family feedback with or without guidance on parental involvement" lowered event rates.

> "Drivers in the groups who received family feedback with or without guidance on parental involvement had lower event occurrence rates than did the control (no-feedback) group during the solo-driving phase."
