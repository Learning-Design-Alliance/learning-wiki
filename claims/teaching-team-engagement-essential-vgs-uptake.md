## Evidence

### Verkuyl M. 2021

Verkuyl M., Lapum J.L., St-Amant O., Hughes M., Romaniuk D. (2021). Curricular uptake of virtual gaming simulation in nursing education. Nurse Education in Practice. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nepr.2021.102967

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' experiential account of embedding a VGS in a year one health assessment course with over 550 students across three sites; they state that "engagement of the teaching team is essential" and that early engagement made instructors champions of uptake. No effect size is reported.

> "We found engagement of the teaching team is essential when using VGS in a course. In addition, when using VGS, it is important to follow the process of prebrief, enactment, debrief and evaluation."
