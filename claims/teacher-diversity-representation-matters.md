## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Focus group participants noted the lack of representation of people of color in their teachers and argued that seeing themselves in classrooms supports belonging, relationships, and trust. [→ Duchesneau 2020](#duchesneau-2020)

## Evidence

### Duchesneau 2020

Duchesneau, N. (2020). Social, Emotional, and Academic Development Through an Equity Lens (#ReEnvisionSEL). The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/social-emotional-and-academic-development-through-an-equity-lens/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group participants noted lack of teacher representation alongside curriculum representation. One Black father said "Hire more Black teachers." A Black mother observed an all-boys school that was 90% Black with all White teachers where "they didn't get [the boys]."

> "When students see themselves in their classrooms, they are more likely to feel they belong and build relationships with teachers and build trust in schools."
