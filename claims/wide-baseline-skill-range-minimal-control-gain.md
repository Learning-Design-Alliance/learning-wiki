Thomaschewski M, Vonthein R, Keck T, Laubert T, Benecke C. (2025). Laparoscopic simulation training improves operating room performance of surgical residents: a multicenter randomized trial (NOVICE). International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline evaluation during the first cholecystectomy (FAS, n = 18) showed a wide skill spread; the discussion notes the control group's "mean improvement of 1.9 points which was not significantly different and less than 10% of the scale range."

> "The laparoscopic performance ranged from a very low score of 7 to a high score of 19 according to GOALS assessment[23]. From the literature, it is well known that surgical residents are differently talented in MIS at the beginning of training[35], which could be confirmed in the current study."
