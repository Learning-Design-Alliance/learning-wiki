Evidence¶

Debra Kamps 2015¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Consumer satisfaction questionnaires from 66 of 86 CW-FIT teachers using a 4-point Likert-type scale, plus student surveys in which 89% of 1,055 students said they liked the intervention.

"Teachers (n = 66) expressed overall satisfaction with the CW-FIT intervention. Mean ratings (1 = high acceptability, 4 = low) across items averaged 1.4 to 1.88 (very true to mostly true)."

Debra Kamps 2015 (2)¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up informal inquiries at eight schools in the spring of the final project year, completed 1 to 3 years after participation. Maintenance was measured by "self-report and not based on follow-up data".

"Twenty of 45 intervention teachers (44%) reported still using the intervention. This was self-report and not based on follow-up data."
