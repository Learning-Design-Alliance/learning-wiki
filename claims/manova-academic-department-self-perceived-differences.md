## Evidence

### Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp 2026

Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp, Arslan Sümeyye, Fidan Özlem. (2026). The relationship between professional competence and medical error tendency among final-year nursing students. Work (Reading, Mass.). https://doi.org/10.1177/10519815251409136

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way MANOVA (Table 3) in the 125-student survey; Tukey HSD showed students with high academic achievement (A1 to A3) had significantly higher scores in both measures than lower-achieving students (p < 0.001).

> "a significant difference in core competency, and medical error tendency scores based on academic achievement (Wilks' Lambda (λ) = 0.750; F = 9.366; p < 0.001; Partial η2=0.134)"

### Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp 2026 (2)

Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp, Arslan Sümeyye, Fidan Özlem. (2026). The relationship between professional competence and medical error tendency among final-year nursing students. Work (Reading, Mass.). https://doi.org/10.1177/10519815251409136

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same MANOVA (Table 3): Surgical Unit students (M = 5.87, SD = 0.75) scored lower on competence than Intensive Care Unit students (M = 6.44, SD = 0.54), and lower on METSN than Intensive Care (M = 4.65) and specialized clinics (M = 4.62).

> "Significant differences were found in core competency and medical error tendency scores based on the clinical practice department (λ=0.872, F = 2.826, p = 0.011, Partial η2=0.066)"

### Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp 2026 (3)

Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp, Arslan Sümeyye, Fidan Özlem. (2026). The relationship between professional competence and medical error tendency among final-year nursing students. Work (Reading, Mass.). https://doi.org/10.1177/10519815251409136

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same MANOVA (Table 3): students who perceived themselves as competent (λ = 0.843; F = 5.378; p < 0.001; Partial η² = 0.082) and those confident in nursing skills had significantly higher scores on both measures; Tukey showed a > c > b for confidence.

> "rated their nursing skills as competent (Wilks' Lambda (λ) = 0.615; F = 16.676; p < 0.001; Partial η2=0.216) had significantly higher scores in both core competency and medical error tendency"
