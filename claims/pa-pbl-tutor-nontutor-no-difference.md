### Xie Jingtao 2026

Xie Jingtao, Chen Ziyan, Lin Duhuang, Zhuang Guansheng, Zheng Ziyuan, Su Yun, Xin Gang. (2026). Peer-assisted learning combined with problem-based learning enhances clinical reasoning and knowledge application among medical students. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of final examination scores between PA-PBL tutors (n = 13) and non-tutors (n = 121) within the experimental group. The article reports "no statistically significant differences between the two subgroups in total score or in any of the individual question formats"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "In the experimental group, there were no statistically significant differences between the two subgroups in total score or in any of the individual question formats (P > 0.05, Table 4)"
