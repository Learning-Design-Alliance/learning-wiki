### Trezek and Mayer 2019

Beverly Trezek and Connie Mayer. (2019). Reading and Deafness: State of the Evidence and Implications for Research and Practice. Educ. Sci. 2019, 9, 216. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci9030216

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of 28 correlational studies of reading comprehension and phonics with deaf and hard-of-hearing students, including studies of languages other than English. The review reports "17 studies reported signiﬁcant correlations with reading comprehension" for PA/PS skills; no effect sizes are printed.

> "In considering the results for PA /PS skills across all investigations, 17 studies reported signiﬁcant correlations with reading comprehension. Another ﬁve studies included in the review reported signiﬁcant correlations between OA and reading comprehension."
