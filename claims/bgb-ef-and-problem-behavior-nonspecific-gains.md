## Evidence

### Dovis Sebastiaan 2015

Dovis Sebastiaan, Van der Oord Saskia, Wiers Reinout W., Prins Pier J. M. (2015). Improving Executive Functioning in Children with ADHD: Training Multiple Executive Functions within the Context of a Computer Game. A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0121651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Repeated measures MANOVA on the eight BRIEF scales and four SPSRQ-C scales in the RCT showed a main effect of Time (F(30,318) = 4.91, p < .001) and no Treatment x Time interaction; parents reported "a significant improvement (with large effect sizes) on almost all scales of the BRIEF".

> "after training, parents reported a significant improvement (with large effect sizes) on almost all scales of the BRIEF (EF behavior; only improvement on the Organization of Materials scale was no longer significant after Bonferroni correction)"

### Dovis Sebastiaan 2015 (2)

Dovis Sebastiaan, Van der Oord Saskia, Wiers Reinout W., Prins Pier J. M. (2015). Improving Executive Functioning in Children with ADHD: Training Multiple Executive Functions within the Context of a Computer Game. A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0121651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Repeated measures MANOVA on ODD/CD scales, PedsQL Psychosocial Health, and HSQ severity in the RCT showed a main effect of Time (F(14,334) = 5.15, p < .001) and no Treatment x Time interaction; parents reported "a significant improvement on all general problem behavior indices", while children themselves reported no significant difference in their Psychosocial Health Summary Score.

> "after training, parents reported a significant improvement on all general problem behavior indices (effect sizes ranged from medium to large), and teachers reported a significant improvement on the ODD scale of the DBDRS (medium effect size)"
