Methodological characteristics analysis (RQ4, Table 6) across the 38 reviewed studies. The review reports "The majority of studies (22, 58%) involved sample sizes of fewer than 45 participants" and notes small sample sizes are common, possibly reflecting practical constraints of IVR research.

### Lee 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Domain and equipment analysis in RQ4 (Table 6). The review also reports that "A significant number of studies (15 studies, 39%) did not specify the software used," and that HTC VIVE HMDs with built-in eye trackers were the most common systems, suggesting reliance on default vendor configurations.

> "29 studies (91%) were conducted in professional domains such as medicine, healthcare, or police training."
