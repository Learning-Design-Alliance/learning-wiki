## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In schools with higher levels of bushfire impact, the slope of change from Year 3 to Year 5 NAPLAN scores is significantly reduced for reading and numeracy. [→ Gibbs 2019](#gibbs-2019)

## Evidence

### Gibbs 2019

Gibbs, L., Nursey, J., Cook, J., Ireton, G., Alkemade, N., Roberts, M., Gallagher, H. C., Bryant, R., Block, K., Molyneaux, R., & Forbes, D. (2019). Delayed Disaster Impacts on Academic Performance of Primary School Children. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13200

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel random-slope analysis of 24,642 students' NAPLAN scores, controlling for demographics, tested whether bushfire affectedness predicted the Year 3 to Year 5 slope. The article reports "a significant negative relationship at Level 2 between the slope and affected level" for reading and numeracy.

> "Conversely, the predictive relationship between Year 3 and Year 5 NAPLAN scores is affected by level of bushfire impact for the reading and numeracy domains."
