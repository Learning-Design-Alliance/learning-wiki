Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis. (2019). Better beware: comparing metacognition for phishing and legitimate emails. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09197-5

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' forward-looking recommendation in the Implications for interventions section, grounded in the observed disparity in metacognitive performance between legitimate and phishing emails. They also propose within-subject analysis of which phishing features make messages deceptive.

> "Future studies developing anti-phishing interventions should investigate both performance and metacognition metrics to understand how users are shifting their decision-making strategies. If successful, an intervention might make the calibration curve for phishing messages look more like the curve for legitimate emails."
