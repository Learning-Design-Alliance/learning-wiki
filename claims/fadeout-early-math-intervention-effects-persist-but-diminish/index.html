Evidence¶

Bailey 2015¶

Bailey, D. H., Nguyen, T., Jenkins, J. M., Domina, T., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2015). Fadeout in an Early Mathematics Intervention: Same Old Schools or Underlying Skills? SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562173

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of the TRIAD evaluation of the Building Blocks curriculum in 42 low-resource schools, with 361 control and 418 treatment children assessed with the REMA at post-test and kindergarten follow-up. The article reports the treatment effect "faded out but remained statistically significant at the follow-up assessment (p < .05)".

"In the full sample, children in the treatment group outperformed children in the control group at the post-test assessment (p < .001). This effect faded out but remained statistically significant at the follow-up assessment (p < .05; Figure 2)."
