q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Exploratory inverse-variance random-effects meta-analysis of 7 studies (248 intervention vs. 251 control participants) pooling related but non-identical score-based endpoints. The printed effect size is SMD 0.90 (95% CI 0.18 to 1.62, I² = 92.5%, P = 0.015); GRADE-informed certainty was very low for this synthesis.

"The pooled analysis suggested that LLM-based teaching was associated with higher post-course score-based outcomes than non-AI control teaching, with a pooled effect size of SMD 0.90 (95% CI 0.18 to 1.62; I² = 92.5%; P = 0.015)."
