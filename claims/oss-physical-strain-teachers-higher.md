## Evidence

### Herrmann-Werner Anne 2019

Herrmann-Werner Anne, Loda Teresa, Wiesner Lisa M, Erschens Rebecca Sarah, Junne Florian, Zipfel Stephan. (2019). Is an obesity simulation suit in an undergraduate medical communication class a valuable teaching tool? A cross-sectional proof of concept study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-029738

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison via Kruskal-Wallis/Mann-Whitney U tests in the same questionnaire survey. Teachers rated SP physical strain "M=4.83±1.23", significantly higher than SPs (M=3.92±1.08) and students (M=3.82±1.56); χ2=12.3; df=2; p<0.01.

> "the results showed a significant difference in physical strain for the SPs (χ2=12.3; df=2; p<0.01). The teachers (M=4.83±1.23) regarded the physical strain for SPs significantly higher compared to the SPs (M=3.92±1.08) and the students (M=3.82±1.56)."
