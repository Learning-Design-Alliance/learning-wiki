`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group Bonferroni post hoc comparison of OSCE scores at the one-month retention test in the randomized controlled trial. Table 3 shows the 2–3 mean difference of -0.13 (0.51) with intervention means of 28.30 (3.69) immediately after and 28.43 (3.09) one month after.

> "It should be noted that the level of clinical skill performance in the intervention group one month after the intervention was not significantly different from that immediately after the intervention (P = 1.00)"
