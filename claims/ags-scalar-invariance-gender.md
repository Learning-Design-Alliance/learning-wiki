### Lin Rongmao 2024

Lin Rongmao, Chen Yanping, Shen Yilin, Hu Ting, Huang Ying, Yang Yishan, Yu Xueting, Ding Jinliang. (2024). Academic grit scale for Chinese middle- and upper-grade primary school students: testing its factor structure and measurement invariance. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01622-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group CFA of the AGS one-factor model across 935 boys and 959 girls. Although S-B χ2 increased significantly at the metric and scalar steps, ΔCFI values (0.001 and 0.002) were below the 0.005 criterion, "showing that scalar invariance was supported for both boys and girls".

> "In this case, S-B χ2 significantly increased (p = .01), but the change in CFI (0.002) was less than 0.005, showing that scalar invariance was supported for both boys and girls."
