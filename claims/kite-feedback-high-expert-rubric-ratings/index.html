q2 · i? · design · r2

Expert evaluation of 44 interaction triples (Round 1 answer, KITE feedback, Round 2 answer) using a three-dimension rubric adapted from prior work. The results section reports "93.18% Yes judgments on each dimension".

"KITE receives consistently high ratings for scaffolding, guidance, coherence, and tone, with 93.18% Yes judgments on each dimension."

q2 · i? · design · r2

Same expert rubric evaluation; the mistake remediation dimension was not applicable in 29.55% of cases. The article reports "63.63% Yes judgments" for mistake remediation.

"Mistake remediation receives 63.63% Yes judgments, but 29.55% of cases are marked N/A because the simulated student's initial response was already correct and no error identification was required."
