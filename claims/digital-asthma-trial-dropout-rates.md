## Evidence

### Morrison D 2014

Morrison D, Wyke S, Agur K, Cameron EJ, Docking RI, MacKenzie AM, McConnachie A, Raghuvir V, Thomson NC, Mair FS. (2014). Digital Asthma Self-Management Interventions: A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3958674/

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Metareview audit of retention: dropout data were available for 11 of 19 RCTs with a 4.5–23% range; the authors note this is no worse than nondigital asthma self-management interventions (0–54%, median 15% in a cited Cochrane review).

> "Dropout rates were available for 11 of the 19 RCTs and ranged from 4.5% to 23%, but again, the reporting of these numbers for 3 of these RCTs was conflicting (Krishna, Jan, Guendelman)."
