## Evidence

### Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy 2022

Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy, Ziwen Han, Hammad Shaikh, Qi Yin Zheng, Lisa-Angelique Lim, Anna Rafferty, Andrew Petersen, and Joseph Jay Williams. 2022. How can Email Interventions Increase Students’ Completion of Online Homework? A Case Study Using A/B Comparisons. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22), March 21–25, 2022, Online, USA. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506874

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Panel data regression on weeks 5–8 submission timestamps. The article reports no "statistically significant impact on start time" (coefficient = 1.105; z = 0.86; p > .10) or end time (coefficient = -0.118; z = -0.11; p > .10). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "receiving email reminders do not result in a statistically significant impact on start time (coefficient = 1.105; z = 0.86; p > .10; panel regression with time and participant effects) or on end time (coefficient = -0.118; z = -0.11; p > .10; panel regression with time and participant effects)."
