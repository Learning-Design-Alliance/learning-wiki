Thomas J. Kane 2021¶

Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, vol. 40, issue 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/is-college-remediation-a-barrier-or-a-boost-evidence-from-the-tennessee-sails-program

The article's headline conclusion, drawn from the SAILS and co-requisite analyses: both alternatives "produced modest gains for students and taxpayers," yet remediation requirements are judged not to be a primary driver of low degree completion. No effect size is printed for this overall conclusion.

"Although both alternatives to pre‐requisite remediation produced modest gains for students and taxpayers, remediation requirements are not a primary driver of low degree completion rates."
