## Evidence

### Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L 2010

Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L. (2010). Normative data on the sleep habits of Australian children and adolescents. SLEEP. https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.10.1381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA of wake times in the cohort found significant day type × age (P < 0.0001) and day type × sex (P = 0.004) interactions; the article reports wake times "82 (76-88) min later on non-school days" and girls waking 15 min later than boys on non-school days.

> "While wake up times were strikingly similar across sex and age groups on school days, with almost all participants rising close to 07:00, on non-school days wake times increased at the rate of 10 (8-12) min/ year of age on non-school days."
