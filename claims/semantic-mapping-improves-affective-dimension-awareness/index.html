Nilforoushan 2012¶

Somayeh Nilforoushan. (2012). The Effect of Teaching Vocabulary through Semantic Mapping on EFL Learners' Awareness of the Affective Dimensions of Deep Vocabulary Knowledge. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p164

q3 · i? · causal · r2

MANOVA on the awareness test scores of 26 experimental and 24 control participants (after outlier removal) showed a significant multivariate group effect; the Group effect in Table 6 reports Pillai's Trace of .852 with F=129.87, p=.000, "Partial Eta Squared" .852.

"The results of the MANOV A are reported in Table 6. which shows a signficicant effect of the independent variable (teaching through semantic mapping) on both depe ndent variables by virtue of the F value of 864.70, degree of freedom 2 and p<0.05"

Nilforoushan 2012 (2)¶

Somayeh Nilforoushan. (2012). The Effect of Teaching Vocabulary through Semantic Mapping on EFL Learners' Awareness of the Affective Dimensions of Deep Vocabulary Knowledge. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p164

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Between-subjects tests (Table 7) on the same 26 experimental and 24 control participants showed significant group effects on both dependent variables, with partial eta squared of .507 for potency and .808 for evaluation.

"the independent variable of "group" had a significant effect on the dependent variable of "potency": F(1,46)=47.266 p<0.05. it is also significant for the dependent variable of evaluation: F (1,46) =193.083, p< 0.05"
