### Keddis Mira T 2011

Keddis Mira T, Cullen Michael W, Reed Darcy A, Halvorsen Andrew J, McDonald Furman S, Takahashi Paul Y, Bhagra Anjali. (2011). Effectiveness of an Ultrasound Training Module for Internal Medicine Residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-75

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their single-program pre/post survey; they attribute the pattern to practice format: "residents were asked to identify these structures on resident volunteers, thus allowing for a real-life image with the ultrasound."

> "We found the largest improvement in identifying the internal jugular vein, which has clinically relevant ramification when residents are attempting central line placement in critically ill patients, and in identifying the thyroid gland."
