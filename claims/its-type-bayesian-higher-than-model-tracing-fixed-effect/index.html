Under a random-effects model, model tracing, Bayesian network modeling, and other types of ITS produced statistically significant effect sizes while constraint-based modeling and expectation and misconception tailoring did not; the higher mean for Bayesian network modeling was significantly greater than model tracing and constraint-based modeling only under a fixed-effect model. → Wenting Ma 2014
