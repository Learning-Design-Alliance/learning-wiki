## Evidence

### Yang Lin 2020

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Arterial puncture test after training in the retrospective cohort. First-attempt success favoured ST (p=0.033); puncture time was "9.56±5.24 vs 12.15±6.87 min" (p=0.002) and patient comfort NRS "5.49±1.72 vs 4.71±1.57" (p=0.023). No effect sizes printed.

> "the success rate of the procedure was higher in the ST group than in the CT group (78.72% vs 58.33%, p=0.033)"

### Yang Lin 2020 (2)

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Complication analysis in the same puncture test: bruising, haematoma, infection and pseudoaneurysm rates showed no significant difference between groups (p>0.05); equivalence was not tested. Total puncture success rate was also similar (p>0.05).

> "there was no significant difference in the incidence of complications between the ST and CT groups (17.02% vs 18.75%, p>0.05)"
