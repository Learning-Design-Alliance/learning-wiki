## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yamada Masaaki 2019

Yamada Masaaki, Sekine Michikazu, Tatsuse Takashi, Asaka Yukiko. (2019). Association between lifestyle, parental smoke, socioeconomic status, and academic performance in Japanese elementary school children: the Super Diet Education Project. Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12199-019-0776-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in the cross-sectional survey of 1663 children found "low SES (OR 1.48; 95% CI, 1.03–2.14)" significantly associated with low academic performance; SES was measured by subjective family affluence.

> "low SES (OR 1.48; 95% CI, 1.03–2.14) had significant associations with low AP"

### Yamada Masaaki 2019 (2)

Yamada Masaaki, Sekine Michikazu, Tatsuse Takashi, Asaka Yukiko. (2019). Association between lifestyle, parental smoke, socioeconomic status, and academic performance in Japanese elementary school children: the Super Diet Education Project. Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12199-019-0776-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Grade-stratified logistic regression showed SES significant or marginal in senior grades but not significant in junior grades. The authors note this supports the Nippon Foundation report that academic differences due to SES widened in children aged 10 or older.

> "Analyses stratified by grade showed stronger associations between academic performance and socioeconomic status in senior (OR = 1.62 for middle, OR = 1.52 for low in grades 4 to 6) than in junior children (OR = 1.15 for middle, OR = 1.38 for low in grades 1 to 3)"
