## Evidence

### Nicole Shechtman 2019

Nicole Shechtman, Jeremy Roschelle, Mingyu Feng, Corinne Singleton. (2019). An Efficacy Study of a Digital Core Curriculum for Grade 5 Mathematics. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850482

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory RQ4 descriptive analysis of RM-CC5 system use metrics for 881 of 940 treatment students, aggregated at the school level (Table 3). Students met about 36.7 objectives (school SD 5.2), worked an estimated 14.5 problems per day, and spent an estimated 9.8 minutes per day in Smarter Solving. The article concludes "all the RM-CC5 schools were compliant with the program model."

> "Across all treatment schools, students were logged into RM-CC5 for an overall estimated average of 56.7 minutes per day, with a school-level SD of about 7.7 minutes."
