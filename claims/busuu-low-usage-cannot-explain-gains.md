Post-busuu survey of the research group on usage behaviour. The article reports "the average study frequency was once per week" with 10–15 minute sessions, totalling about 120 minutes, and states this low value cannot explain the group difference.

> "the average study frequency was once per week and the average study length was between 10 and 15 minutes. It means for the whole research only 120 minutes, 2 hours on average, which is a very low value. This low value cannot explain the significant difference between the two groups."
