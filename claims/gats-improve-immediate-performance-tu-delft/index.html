delft-immediate¶

Yuri Noviello, Naaz Sibia, Anastasiia Birillo, Thomas Overklift Vaupel Klein, Michael Liut, and Gosia Migut. 2026. AI-Generated Traces for Novice Programmers: Learning Effects and Learner Differences in a Multi-Institutional Study. Proceedings of the 31st ACM Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3803400.3809346

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experiment in a 9-week Java CS1 course at TU Delft (pooled N=151; While-loop N=138), analyzed with linear mixed models controlling for topic and prior experience; Table 1 row 21 reports Δ=5.420, p=0.003, padj=0.017, and the While-loop row reports Δ=8.227, p=0.003, padj=0.0159. Other topics showed positive but non-significant differences that failed correction.
