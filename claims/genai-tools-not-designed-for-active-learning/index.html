Evidence¶

Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch 2025¶

Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch. (2025). Learning against the machine: the double edged sword of (Gen)AI in STEM education. International Journal of STEM Education 12:66. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00588-6

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary reports, citing Gupta et al. (2025), that GenAI training regimes yield neither robust domain expertise nor pedagogy-aligned tutoring capabilities; the reviewed study's design is not described in the article. The authors conclude current GenAI models cannot provide the affordances of active learning.

"Empirical evidence indicated that current training regimes of GenAI neither yield robust domain expertise nor pedagogy-aligned capabilities as expert tutors (Gupta et al., 2025)."
