Post-intervention qualitative theme analysis of Penryn College journals and interviews. The article reports participants "did not cause them to feel judged" and felt held as "experts of their own lives", a difference not reported at Mthombo High School.

> "Participants from Penryn College, unlike those from Mthombo High School, according to the following examples of response, also stated that the intervention programme helped them express their emotions; gave them the opportunity to express themselves honestly ; did not cause them to feel judged; and held them in the position of  experts of their own lives"
