## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant interaction effect was found between any health behaviour and trait mindfulness in predicting academic performance in the simultaneous regression model (Model 2, N = 540). [→ Heller Sebastian 2024](#heller-sebastian-2024)

## Evidence

### Heller Sebastian 2024

Heller Sebastian, Reichel Jennifer L., Mülder Lina M., Schäfer Markus, Schwab Lisa, Werner Antonia M., Letzel Stephan, Rigotti Thomas, Dietz Pavel. (2024). The association between health behaviours and academic performance moderated by trait mindfulness amongst university students: an observational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1340235

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression Model 2 (N = 540) including covariates, all health behaviours, trait mindfulness, and their interaction terms; "no significant interaction effect between any health behaviour and trait mindfulness" was found. The model was statistically significant overall (F(21, 540) = 1.721, p = 0.024).

> "There was no significant interaction effect between any health behaviour and trait mindfulness, and no significant association of any health behaviour or trait mindfulness with academic performance (see Table 3)."
