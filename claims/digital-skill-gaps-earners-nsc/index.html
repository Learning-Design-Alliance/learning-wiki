The report cites National Skills Coalition data: "13 percent of all current American earners lack any digital skills," with stark racial gaps — half of Black and 57 percent of Latino earners have limited or no digital skills versus nine percent of white earners. NSC data also show earners with no digital skills concentrated in lower earnings quintiles.

"the National Skills Coalition (NSC) reports that 13 percent of all current American earners lack any digital skills. Eighteen percent have limited skills and only a third are at the advanced level. The numbers are far worse for people of color: Half of Black and 57 percent of Latino earners have limited or no digital skills."
