## Evidence

### Hasegawa Takumi 2026

Hasegawa Takumi, Etoh Tsuyoshi, Nakano Tomoki, Aoyama Yoshimasa, Matsunobu Yusuke, Fujishima Reo, Fujita Shunsuke, Shitomi Yuki, Akagi Tomonori, Ninomiya Shigeo, Ueda Yoshitake, Tokuyasu Tatsushi, Inomata Masafumi. (2026). Impact of artificial intelligence intervention on awareness of anatomical landmarks in laparoscopic gastrectomy: An exploratory study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-13043-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-institution study of 12 surgeons in three expertise groups annotating 96 infrapyloric images before and after gAI overlay; the trainers' change rate was significantly lower than both trainee groups ("p < 0.001 for both"). No effect size printed for this comparison.

> "In the infrapyloric area, recognition changes occurred in 50/96 images (52, 95 CI 42.2–61.8%) in the JT group, 36/96 images (38, 95 CI 28.5–47.5%) in the ST group, and 9/96 images (9, 95 CI 5.0–16.9%) in the T group, with the T group exhibiting significantly lower rates than the JT group and ST groups (p < 0.001 for both)."
