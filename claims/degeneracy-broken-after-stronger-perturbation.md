# Students do not realize that some degeneracy is broken after the stronger perturbation is treated in step 1, which would allow the weaker perturbation to be diagonal in each degenerate subspace of H0strong

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students did not realize that after step 1 the degeneracy of H0strong is less than that of H0 (for n = 2, reduced from an 8-fold degeneracy to three separate two-fold degeneracies), making H′weak diagonal in the degenerate subspaces possible. [→ Emily Marshman 2020](#emily-marshman-2020)

## Evidence

### Emily Marshman 2020

Emily Marshman, Christof Keebaugh, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Student difﬁculties with ﬁnding the corrections to the energy spectrum of the hydrogen atom for the strong and weak ﬁeld Zeeman effects using degenerate perturbation theory. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.07144](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.07144)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview data: students argued that neither coupled nor uncoupled representations form a good basis in the limiting cases because neither diagonalizes both H′Z and H′fs. For n = 2 the article notes "the degeneracy in the new unperturbed Hamiltonian ˆH 0Z = ˆH 0 + ˆH ′Z is reduced to three separate two-fold degeneracies".

> "They did not realize that in general, after taking into account the stronger perturbation in step 1, the dimension of some of the de- generate subspaces is reduced."
