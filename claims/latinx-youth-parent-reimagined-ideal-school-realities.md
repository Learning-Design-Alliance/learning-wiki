### Lorenzo-Blanco 2025

Lorenzo-Blanco, Elma I., Henriquez, Kimberly L., Romero, Michelle Y. Martin, Stein, Gabriela Livas, & Isaza, Alejandra Garcia. (2025). “So no one feels left out:” Latinx youths’ and parents’ re-imagined school realities in the context of cultural stressors. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000956

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Reflexive thematic analysis of focus groups with 45 youth (Mage = 15.26 years, SD = .79) and 28 parents (Mage = 42.36, SD = 8.51) in six high schools, analyzed “each with three accompanying subthemes.” Participants reimagined schools where students feel “welcomed, supported, included, and valued.”

> "Guided by reflexive thematic analysis, we constructed two themes: 1) inclusive, welcoming, and safe schools and 2) schools without cultural stressors and filled with supportive allies; each with three accompanying subthemes."
