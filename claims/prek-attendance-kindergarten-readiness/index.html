High-Quality Early Child Education is Associated With 2023¶

High-Quality Early Child Education is Associated With... (2023). Regional Educational Laboratory West, WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/west

q2 · i? · review · r?

The summary reports, citing the Phillips et al. (2017) consensus statement on pre-kindergarten effects, greater kindergarten readiness among pre-k attendees at year end. No effect sizes are printed.

"Children who attend pre-kindergarten programs are more ready for kindergarten at the end of their pre-kindergarten year than children who do not attend pre-kindergarten."
