## Evidence

### Dahl-Leonard 2024

Dahl-Leonard, K., Hall, C., & Peacott, D. (2024). A meta-analysis of technology-delivered literacy instruction for elementary students. Education Tech Research Dev (2024) 72:1507–1538. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10354-0

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication bias analyses (funnel plot, Egger's regression, trim-and-fill) within the meta-analysis of 53 studies. The article reports "b = 0.37, df = 51, p = .51" for Egger's test and a trim-and-fill adjusted mean of g = 0.29; no standardized effect size is printed for the bias tests themselves.

> "An Egger's regression test indicated no evidence of funnel plot asymmetry (b = 0.37, df = 51, p = .51). Results of the trim and fill analysis indicated that five studies were missing from the right side of the funnel plot. Including these missing studies in the random-effects model would increase the mean effect size from g = 0.24 to g = 0.29."
