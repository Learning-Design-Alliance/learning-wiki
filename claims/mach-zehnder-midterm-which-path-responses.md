## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2014

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2014). Ontological Flexibility and the Learning of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Midterm exam question during the first implementation of the modern physics curriculum at University of Colorado Boulder, asking students to explain in which of two beam-splitter experiments (X or Y) photons would exhibit wave-like behavior. One student wrote the photon can take either path and be represented as a wave in Experiment Y.

> "Nearly 40% of students focused on the lack of which-path information, and 33% said the availability of two paths for the photon was key to predicting wave-like behavior in Experiment Y."
