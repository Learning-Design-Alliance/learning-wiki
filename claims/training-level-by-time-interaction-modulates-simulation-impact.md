### Li Congqing 2026

Li Congqing, Song Fei, Xu Youjiang, Zhang Liwei, Wang Wenyan. (2026). Efficacy of laparoscopic simulator training in standardized obstetrics and gynecology residents: a prospective study demonstrating skill convergence. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-46957-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA (Time Weeks 1-4 and Task Module Modules 1-4 within-subjects; Training Level between-subjects) revealed significant interaction effects for all four modules (e.g., Module 2: F(6, 81) = 71.268, P < 0.001). The article reports that "simulation’s impact is modulated by prior clinical experience".

> "The significant interaction effect between training level and time confirms that simulation’s impact is modulated by prior clinical experience."
