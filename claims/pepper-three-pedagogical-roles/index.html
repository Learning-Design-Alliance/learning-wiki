Thematic categorical analysis of the 13 included studies, reported in the review's results on technological implementation and the robot's role. The review counts the three role configurations and cites Donnermann et al. (2022) as the higher-education tutoring example.

"In six studies (46.2%), Pepper functioned as a tutor or teaching assistant, performing tasks such as guidance, support or structured feedback, as described in the study by Donnermann et al. ( 2022) in higher education."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Reported benefits of Pepper centre on motivation and engagement, with social-skill and academic-performance gains reported less frequently — related
  • Empirical research on the Pepper robot in formal education is recent, concentrated in primary education, and dominated by qualitative designs with small samples — related
  • In university physics education, AI has been applied for tutoring and explanations, formative feedback and scaffolding, collaborative problem solving, simulation and modeling, instructional design, and AI literacy development — related
  • Role metaphors positioned GenAI as helper, learning partner, and friend/foe, with a high proportion combining friend and foe — related
  • Students' main AI challenges are content reliability and depth, and most expect AI to serve as tutor, creative partner, and critical thinking partner rather than mere content generator — related
  • In second-language teaching for pupils with special educational needs, Pepper was associated with higher participation than digital tablets — related
  • Inclusion of learners with special educational needs is typically implicit rather than explicitly designed even in advanced agentic AI systems — related
  • AI most often serves as an assistant or draft generator (33.3% of studies) in teacher-AI design collaboration, while co-designer or partner roles appear in only 13.3% — related
  • Pedagogical agents as coaches and tutors are most useful with novice learners, while virtual role-players benefit learners across expertise levels — related
  • Adult learners view AI as a collaborative learning agent for solving complex problems, sharing knowledge, and honing higher-order thinking — related
  • Robot-assisted language learning is a rapidly developing field with increasing implementation of robots as language tutors — related
