Case examination of several interviewed students who considered leaving their PhD programs, culminating in the article's model of characteristic risk pathways. The typology derives from interview narratives rather than attrition records.

> "Lastly, we identify two characteristic types of students at a higher risk of leaving their programs: students who enter graduate school with broad interests and struggle to find a group, and students who join a research group early based on research interest alone and subsequently encounter issues with a negative mentoring relationship."
