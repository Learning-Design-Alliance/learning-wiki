Christina A. Bauer, Aashna Poddar, Eddie Brummelman, & Andrei Cimpian. (2025). The Brilliance–Belonging Model: How Cultural Beliefs About Intellectual Ability Undermine Educational Equity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10034-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Bian et al. (2017) and Jaxon et al. (2019), that gender-brilliance stereotypes emerge between ages 5 and 7 in US samples, with similar patterns among 7-year-olds in South Korea and Japan. Children as young as 6 also perceive low-SES children as less smart.

> "6- and 7-year-old girls, but not 5-year-old girls, were less likely than same-age boys to view their own gender as “really, really smart”"
