### Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu. (2016). Cognitive Issues in Learning Advanced Physics: An Example from Quantum Mechanics. arXiv:1602.05619. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05619](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05619)

> "Similarly, alternative conceptions about conceptual aspects of quantum mechanics can lead to students making mathe- matical errors that they would otherwise not make in a linear algebra course [9]."
