The effects of teaching strategies on learning to think critically in primary and secondary schools: an overview of systematic reviews. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.158087.1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

This overview of systematic reviews mapped characteristics of 326 systematic reviews of teaching strategies. The authors report that 99% of the reviews did not consider adverse effects, 93% did not assess the risk of bias for included studies, and 100% did not assess the credibility of subgroup effects.

> "Important limitations of the reviews included not considering adverse effects (99% of the reviews), not assessing the risk of bias for included studies (93% of the reviews), and not assessing the credibility of subgroup effects (100% of the reviews)."
