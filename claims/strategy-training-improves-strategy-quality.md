## Evidence

### Agor 2014

John T. Agor. (2014). Language-Learner Strategy Instruction and English Achievement: Voices from Ghana. English Language Teaching, 7(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n6p175

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of video-taped post-intervention task transcripts. The author contrasts an experimental student's contextually informed guess with the control group's context-free guesses, and notes experimental speakers deployed fillers while control speakers paused considerably before deploying a strategy, betraying themselves.

> "In terms of quality, we observe that the four strategies exhibited by the experimental class are of higher quality than the two guesses made by the control class."
