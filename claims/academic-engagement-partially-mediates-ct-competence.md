### Su Weilin 2026

Su Weilin, Chen Chen. (2026). The mediating role of academic engagement and the moderating role of academic self-efficacy in the relationship between critical thinking and academic competence: evidence from Chinese college students. BMC Psychology, 14, 700. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04446-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation testing following Hayes and Preacher's two-step framework in the 363-student three-wave data. After adding engagement, engagement predicts competence (β = 0.250, p < 0.001) and critical thinking retains a significant direct effect (β = 0.147, p < 0.05), indicating "academic engagement partially mediates the pathway".

> "the results of Model 7 in Table 3 reveal that academic engagement positively predict academic competence (β = 0.250, p < 0.001), and critical thinking still has a significant effect on academic competence (β = 0.147, p < 0.05). These results therefore indicate that academic engagement partially mediates the pathway from critical thinking to academic competence among Chinese college students"
