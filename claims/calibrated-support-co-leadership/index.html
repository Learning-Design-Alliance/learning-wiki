Cross-project qualitative finding from staff reflections on Collaborative Innovation projects. The analysis found "Deeper co-leadership was more evident" when scaffolding enabled progressive partner responsibility; no effect sizes are reported.

"Deeper co-leadership was more evident when staﬀ support enabled partners to progressively take on responsibility for inquiry, facilitation, interpretation, and decision-making."

Contrast finding from the same reflection analysis: when staff assumed too much responsibility for facilitation, data activities, or project management, partners had fewer opportunities to practice skills and take up shared ownership.

"Where staﬀ carried more of the process, the work could continue, but shared ownership and co-leadership were less evident."
