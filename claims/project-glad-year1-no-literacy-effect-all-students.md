## Subclaims
`q3 i0` In Year 1 of a cluster randomized trial, treatment effects on Gates-MacGinitie reading comprehension (g = 0.07) and vocabulary (g = 0.06) were small and not statistically significant. [→ Deussen et al. 2014](#deussen-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Deussen et al. 2014

Theresa Deussen, Elizabeth Autio, Angela Roccograndi, Makoto Hanita. (2014). The Impact of Project GLAD on Students' Literacy and Science Learning: Year 1 Results from a Cluster-Randomized Trial of Sheltered Instruction. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Two-level HLM analysis of a cluster randomized trial in 30 Idaho schools (42 treatment, 50 control classrooms; analytic sample n=2,253), with GM pretests as covariates. The article reports "only small effect sizes and no statistically significant treatment effect" for reading comprehension and vocabulary.

> "With only small effect sizes and no statistically significant treatment effect, we found no evidence that Project GLAD improved students' reading comprehension and vocabulary in the first year of implementation."
