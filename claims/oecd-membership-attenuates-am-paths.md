## Evidence

### Xiong Yuan 2026

Xiong Yuan, Wang Mingjun. (2026). Psychological resilience as a selective perceptual amplifier in post-crisis learning: a dual-pathway model of academic motivation, perceived school support, and cognitive engagement – a multi-country study based on PISA 2022. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04665-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Design-corrected regression with OECD × predictor interaction terms on PISA 2022 data. OECD membership moderated AM→PSS (β = -0.048, t = -9.55, p < .001) and AM→CES (β = -0.057, t = -8.26, p < .001). The PSS→CES path was not significantly moderated (β = 0.002, p = 0.765).

> "The negative coefficients indicate that the direct effects of AM on both PSS and CES are weaker in OECD countries compared to non-OECD countries."
