### Lee Juncheol 2018

Lee Juncheol, Song Yeongtak, Oh Jaehoon, Chee Youngjoon, Ahn Chiwon, Shin Hyungoo, Kang Hyunggoo, Lim Tae Ho. (2018). Smartwatch feedback device for high-quality chest compressions by a single rescuer during infant cardiac arrest: a randomized, controlled simulation study. European Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MEJ.0000000000000537

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preliminary pilot with six volunteers randomly divided into two groups of three, compressing an infant mannequin for 2 min; the article reports "99 ± 1.7% for the feedback group and 72 ± 25.5% for the control group". These descriptive means informed the sample-size calculation.

> "The %CD values were 99 ± 1.7% for the feedback group and 72 ± 25.5% for the control group."
