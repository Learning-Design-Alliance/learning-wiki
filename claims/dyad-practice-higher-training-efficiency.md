## Evidence

### Tolsgaard MG 2015

Tolsgaard MG, Madsen ME, Ringsted C, Oxlund BS, Oldenburg A, Sorensen JL, Ottesen B, Tabor A. (2015). The effect of dyad versus individual simulation‐based ultrasound training on skills transfer. Medical Education 49: 286–295. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U-test comparison of efficiency (final-attempt simulator score divided by total attempted modules) between the randomised groups, reported in Results and plotted in Fig. 3. The article reports "5.88 ± 1.13 points/attempt" versus "2.79 ± 0.92 points/attempt (p < 0.01)". No effect size is printed.

> "However, the dyad group demonstrated significantly higher training efficiency with a mean ± SD of 5.88 ± 1.13 points/attempt compared with the single‐practice group, which achieved a mean ± SD of 2.79 ± 0.92 points/attempt (p < 0.01) (Fig. 3)."
