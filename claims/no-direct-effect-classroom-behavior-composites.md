## Evidence

### Brock et al. 2018

Laura L. Brock, William M. Murrah, Elizabeth A. Cottone, Andrew J. Mashburn, and David W. Grissmer. (2018). An after-school intervention targeting executive function and visuospatial skills also improves classroom behavior. International Journal of Behavioral Development, 42(5), 474–484. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025417738057

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intent-to-treat regressions (N = 87) controlling for school, age, sex, grade, and pre-test scores tested direct effects on teacher-rated composites. The article reports no support for direct effects and a trend for problem behaviors with "b ¼ –.14, p ¼ .06"; no effect size is printed.

> "We do not find support for direct effects for learning-related and problem behavior composites, although a trend is noted for problem behaviors ( b ¼ –.14, p ¼ .06)."
