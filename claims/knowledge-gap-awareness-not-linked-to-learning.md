# PF students reported more awareness of knowledge gaps than example observers, but awareness of knowledge gaps did not correlate with conceptual knowledge acquisition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Condition significantly affected self-reported awareness of knowledge gaps (F(2, 170) = 11.57, p < 0.001), with PF students reporting more awareness than both VF conditions. [→ Christian Hartmann 2021](#christian-hartmann-2021)
`q3 i0` Awareness of knowledge gaps did not correlate with conceptual knowledge post-test scores overall or within any condition. [→ Christian Hartmann 2021 (2)](#christian-hartmann-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Christian Hartmann 2021

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the seven-item self-report scale showed a significant main effect of condition, F(2, 170) = 11.57, p < 0.001; Bonferroni post-hoc tests showed "PF students reported significantly more awareness of knowledge gaps" than both VF conditions. No standardised effect size was printed for the post-hoc contrasts.

> "PF students reported significantly more awareness of knowledge gaps (M = 2.88, SD = 1.07) than did students in both the VF-process (M = 2.06, SD = 1.00, p < 0.001) and the VF-outcome condition (M = 2.15, SD = 0.91, p < 0.001)"

### Christian Hartmann 2021 (2)

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis found "no significant correlation between students’ awareness of knowledge gaps and their performance on the conceptual knowledge post-test", overall r(173) = −0.09, p = 0.223, nor within conditions (PF r = −0.12, VF-process r = −0.08, VF-outcome r = −0.06).

> "there was no significant correlation between students’ awareness of knowledge gaps and their performance on the conceptual knowledge post-test, either overall r(173) = −0.09, p = 0.223"
