### Natarajan Jansirani 2022

Natarajan Jansirani, Joseph Mickael A., Al Shibli Zainab S., Al Hajji Safa S., Al Hanawi Diana K., Al Kharusi Arwa N., Al Maqbali Intisar M. (2022). Skill competency evaluated by two evaluators using a standardised OSCE checklist (inter-rater reliability 0.9). The article reports no significant difference between groups (χ2 [1,55] = 0.262; P = 0.609).

> "The majority of students (n = 35, 63.64%) displayed good performance scores and none were in the poor performance category. There was no significant difference in the skill performance of both groups (χ2 [1,55] = 0.262; P = 0.609)"
