## Evidence

### Solberg Runar Barstad 2021

Solberg Runar Barstad, Steene-Johannessen Jostein, Anderssen Sigmund Alfred, Ekelund Ulf, Säfvenbom Reidar, Haugen Tommy, Berntsen Sveinung, Åvitsland Andreas, Lerum Øystein, Resaland Geir Kåre, Kolle Elin. (2021). Effects of a school-based physical activity intervention on academic performance in 14-year old adolescents: a cluster randomized controlled trial – the School in Motion study. BMC Public Health 21, 871. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-10901-x

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Cluster RCT of 30 Norwegian secondary schools (2084 consenting students, 76% of eligible), linear mixed models with school as random effect, standardized national numeracy tests at baseline and follow-up. The article reports "1.7 (95% CI: 0.9 to 2.5; Cohen’s d = 0.12)" points in favour of PAL students versus controls.

> "The mean difference in change in numeracy was 1.7 (95% CI: 0.9 to 2.5; Cohen’s d = 0.12) and 2.0 (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.7; Cohen’s d = 0.23) points in favour of students in the PAL and DWBH intervention, respectively."

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Same cluster RCT, same mixed-model analysis of national numeracy tests. The article reports "2.0 (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.7; Cohen’s d = 0.23)" points in favour of DWBH students versus controls, a small effect by the article's own classification.

> "The mean difference in change in numeracy was 1.7 (95% CI: 0.9 to 2.5; Cohen’s d = 0.12) and 2.0 (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.7; Cohen’s d = 0.23) points in favour of students in the PAL and DWBH intervention, respectively."
