### Tseng Pei-Ling 2026

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Q-factor analysis; Factor III (n = 6 per Table 1) was labeled the Interpretive Model – Shared Decision-Making type, aligning with the mutual participation model and Emanuel and Emanuel's interpretive model. Participants wanted plain language instead of jargon and physicians who understand their needs and actual condition.

> "Participants associated with this factor highly value their relationship with physicians, actively cooperating and seeking to build rapport (+4)."
