# Practice question format (VSAQ vs MCQ) shows no significant main effect on final retention test performance in a real-life health science course

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The 2×2 repeated measures ANOVA showed no significant main effect of practice test question format on final test score (F(1,44) = 3.23, p = 0.08, η2 = 0.02). [→ van Wijk EV 2024](#van-wijk-ev-2024)

## Evidence

### van Wijk EV 2024

van Wijk EV, de Jonge M, van Blankenstein FM, Janse RJ, Langers AMJ. (2024). The battle of question formats: a comparative study of retrieval practice using very short answer questions and multiple choice questions. BMC Medical Education, 24:1547. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06538-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized within-subjects experiment in the 2024 SELF course at Leiden University Medical Center with 45 students practicing with both VSAQs and MCQs without feedback. The ANOVA found "no significant main effect of question format in practice tests on the final test score" (η2 = 0.02).

> "The 2 × 2 (question format practice test × question format final test) repeated measures ANOVA showed no significant main effect of question format in practice tests on the final test score (F(1,44) = 3.23, p = 0.08, η2 = 0.02)."
