## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Worth 2015

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r?`

On-treatment analysis linking per-pupil game usage logs (median 16 games, interquartile range 20) to numeracy outcomes. The report states the association was significant with "an effect size of 0.19 (0.10– 0.27) per 20 games played"; pupils playing more than 50 games were top-coded at 50.

> "The number of games played by pupils in the numeracy intervention was significantly associated with higher performance on the numeracy test. The effect size was 0.19 (0.10– 0.27) per 20 games played"

### Worth 2015 (2)

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

On-treatment analysis for the literacy group (median 10 games per pupil, interquartile range 15). The report also states that for both interventions there was no significant association between more hours spent teaching the programme units in class and more impact; no effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

> "There was no significant association between more games played and a greater impact of the literacy intervention."
