Authors' interpretation of the between-subject results across the four topics in the pedagogical experiment. The pattern held in both the full sample and the fine-art-mark-5 subgroup, with Powers largest and Perimeter smallest both times.

> "We found the greatest difference in the fine art effect on mathematics between the control group and the experimental group in the topic Powers, and the smallest difference in Perimeter. The latter is a topic that is already visual by nature, which could explain the smaller difference in the mathematics test results."
