## Evidence

### Gosavi A 2025

Gosavi A, Kanneganti A, Khoo ET, Singh K, Shen L, Rauff M, Liu C, Choolani M. (2025). Virtual reality simulation training for childbirth: A cluster randomized crossover study. Int J Gynecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70053

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t-test of pre-simulation quiz scores in the cluster randomized crossover trial (n = 50 group A, 59 group B as printed in Table 2). No participant had prior NVD experience. No effect size was printed.

> "There was no significant difference in baseline knowledge between group A (i.e., mannequin simulation first) and group B (i.e., VR simulation first) with pre‐simulation mean percentage correct scores of 38.9% and 38.2%, respectively (P = 0.848) (Table 2)."
