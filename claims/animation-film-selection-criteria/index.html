Vereș 2020¶

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

Authors' account of comparing three YouTube animated films (ESA's Paxi, The Children's World, CuriostyLand) before selecting one for the experimental lesson. This is the authors' stated rationale, not a tested result.

"We chose to use the movie Paxi - The Solar Sy stem because we wanted to have a soundtrack, the explanations to be offered by an animated character, the viewing time not to exceed 5- 6 minutes, the information to be adapted to the children’ s age and the animation to give students the m ovement of the planets, an aspect that could not be represented by the illustrative materials in 2D format."
