## Evidence

### Wallin 1921

Wallin, J. E. W. (1921). Intelligence Irregularity as Measured by Scattering in the Binet Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-scale comparison of average scattering across the same clinic populations, with the author's stated caveats about unequal numbers of tests, base-determination methods, and extent of testing across scales. The author reports being "struck by the unusually low bases which subjects frequently obtain in this scale."

> "we find that the scattering is considerably greater in the Stanford scale, the average excess amounting to 0.24 year as compared with the 1911 scale, and 0.61 year as compared with the 1908."
