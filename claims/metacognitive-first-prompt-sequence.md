## Evidence

### Nückles 2020

Nückles, M., Roelle, J., Glogger-Frey, I., Waldeyer, J., & Renkl, A. (2020). The Self-Regulation-View in Writing-to-Learn: Using Journal Writing to Optimize Cognitive Load in Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09541-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Two experiments with tenth-graders (social pressure content) manipulating prompt sequence: cognitive-first versus metacognitive-first. "the learners in the metacognitive-first group outperformed their counterparts" on process quality and conceptual knowledge.

> "In both experiments, the learners in the metacognitive-first group outperformed their counterparts regarding the quality of the executed organization and metacognitive processes as well as in a conceptual knowledge test"
