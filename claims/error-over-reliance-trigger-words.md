## Evidence

### Putt O 2022

Putt O, Westacott R, Sam AH, Gurnell M, Brown CA. (2022). Using very short answer errors to guide teaching. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.13458

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 1 reports incorrect responses to Example 4 among the 1417 students: gout at 28.6% of incorrect answers, rheumatoid arthritis at 20.4%, and fracture at 12.3%, with 39.5% answering incorrectly. The article states "These errors resulted from candidates incorrectly focusing on trigger words and instigating erroneous pattern recognition."
