## Evidence

### Marianthi Grizioti 2025

Marianthi Grizioti. (2025). Computational thinking beyond coding: exploring student computational practices while playing and modifying a socio-scientific simulation game with integrated computational tools. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10477-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of 16 middle school students' interactions over a 7-week afterschool STEM club intervention, coding 480 quotations into 6 themes. The article reports that "students implemented these CT practices in different forms" including visual abstraction, which "has not been described in any known CT Framework so far."

> "students implemented these CT practices in different forms, depending on their interaction with the tool (e.g. whether they were designing or playing, modifying the data or the rules). These forms were not part of the initial coding schema"
