# Head Start impacts on children's PPVT receptive vocabulary scores are larger in communities with lower availability of non-Head Start child care options

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children randomized in center groups with lower levels of non-Head Start child care availability showed significantly larger Head Start impacts on gains in PPVT scores than children in center groups with richer options (b=-2.20, SE=.91, p<.05). [→ Morris 2014](#morris-2014)

## Evidence

### Morris 2014

Morris, P. A., Connors, M. C., McCoy, D. C., Gomez, C. J., Yoshikawa, H., & Aber, J. L. (2014). Do Head Start impacts vary by neighborhood context? SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562798

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized evaluation (Head Start Impact Study) with multi-level models including random slopes, regressing spring PPVT on fall scores, center-group fixed effects, covariates, and treatment-by-neighborhood interactions. The article reports "a significant effect of child care availability on the treatment impact" with the printed coefficient.

> "Initial results demonstrate a significant effect of child care availability on the treatment impact on children's PPVT scores, (b=-2.20, SE=.91, p<.05), where children who were randomized in center groups in which there were lower levels of non-Head Start child care availability in the community showed significantly larger Head Start impacts on gains in PPVT scores"
