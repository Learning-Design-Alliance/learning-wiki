Pretest given to 65 students in Spring-1996 after traditional lecture instruction on superposition, with follow-up interviews. The article reports that "most students do not demonstrate a functional un- derstanding of superposition" and that 40% showed no superposition unless peaks overlapped.

> "From explanations given by students on the pretest and in follow-up interviews, we find that most students do not demonstrate a functional un- derstanding of superposition (only 5% sketched Fig- ure 4(a))."
