# Years of social media exposure by grade 8 are negatively associated with Italian and mathematics competence scores in descriptive regressions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Each additional year of exposure to social media by grade 8 is associated with lower standardized competence scores (B = −0.05 in Italian, −0.04 in mathematics), an association the authors describe as purely descriptive. [→ Gui Marco 2026](#gui-marco-2026)

## Evidence

### Gui Marco 2026

Gui Marco, Respi Chiara, Abbiati Giovanni, Paladini Cristiana, Ercolanoni Sofia, Milzani Francesca, Pirola Tiziana, Vezzoli Giovanni. (2026). Longitudinal effect of early social media use on standardized learning outcomes during school career. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02522-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive regression of grade-8 INVALSI competence scores on years of social media exposure in the full sample of 5,227 students; the association persists when other technology exposure is added as regressors (B = −0.06 Italian, −0.04 mathematics). The authors state this association "is purely descriptive in nature"; no standardized effect size beyond the regression coefficients is printed.

> "The robust association between the number of years of exposure to social media (measured at grade 8) and standardized competence scores outcomes at the same grade level in Italian and mathematics reveals a negative association (beta coefficient (B) = −0.05 s.d. in Italian competences for every additional year of exposure (95% confidence interval (CI) −0.07, −0.03); degrees of freedom (df) = 10 and −0.04 in mathematics (95% CI −0.05, −0.02); df = 10)."
