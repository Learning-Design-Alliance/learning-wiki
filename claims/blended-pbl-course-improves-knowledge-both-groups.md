## Evidence

### Back David A 2014

Back David A, Haberstroh Nicole, Antolic Andrea, Sostmann Kai, Schmidmaier Gerhard, Hoff Eike. (2014). Blended learning approach improves teaching in a problem-based learning environment in orthopedics - a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental within/between-group comparison of 20-item multiple-choice written tests in the 'Upper Extremities and Spine' block course, with 44 students (29 users, 15 non-users) tested before, directly after, and three months after the course; the article reports a "significant improvement from pre- to post-tests for both groups".

> "Users as well as non-users had significantly better results in both post-tests (p < 0.001), with slightly better results for NESTOR users in both post-tests."
