# With the alerting & guiding dashboard, teachers' workload dropped most on the performance and frustration subscales, while cognitive load, temporal demand and effort differences were non-significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The performance subscale (feeling accomplished) showed a large drop in the alerting & guiding vs no dashboard condition (Cohen's d = 1.2, p = 0.003). [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023](#reet-kasepalu-2023)
`q2 i3` The frustration subscale showed a large drop in the alerting & guiding vs no dashboard condition (Cohen's d = 1, p = 0.005). [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023](#reet-kasepalu-2023)
`q2 i2` Teachers felt more accomplished in the alerting & guiding than the mirroring-only condition (Cohen's d = 0.8, p = 0.04), a medium effect. [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023](#reet-kasepalu-2023)
`q2 i?` Differences in cognitive load, temporal demand and effort subscales were non-significant though means showed a similar pattern. [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023](#reet-kasepalu-2023)

## Evidence

### Reet Kasepalu 2023

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same quasi-experiment, RTLX subscale analyses showed "performance (how accomplished and successful the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1.2, p = 0.003)" and frustration (Cohen's d = 1, p = 0.005) had the sharpest drops; mirroring-vs-guiding performance contrast showed a medium effect 0.8, p = 0.04; other subscales were non-significant.

> "These two subscales were performance (how accomplished and successful the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1.2, p = 0.003) and level of frustration (how discouraged, stressed and annoyed the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1, p = 0.005)."
