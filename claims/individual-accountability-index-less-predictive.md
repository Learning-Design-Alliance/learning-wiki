## Evidence

### Cukurova 2017

Cukurova, M., Luckin, R., Millán, E., & Mavrikis, M. (2017). The NISPI framework: Analysing collaborative problem-solving from students' physical interactions. Preprint submitted to Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.08.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of coded group interactions (Table 3 percentages). High CPS groups showed values such as 42.86% and 53.46%, but "their individual accountability value measured in calculations are quite high" for two non-high groups, so the index does not align cleanly with observer ratings. No effect size is printed.

> "for groups Univ. A and Univ. G, even though were not considered as a high CPS group by human observers, their individual accountability value measured in calculations are quite high with the respective values of 64.71% and 47.83%."
