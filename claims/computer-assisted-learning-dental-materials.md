## Evidence

### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2023

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Tan Wen-Wu, Tan Hung-Jia, Khoo Chia-Wee, Afrashtehfar Kelvin I. (2023). Innovative Pedagogical Strategies in Health Professions Education: Active Learning in Dental Materials Science. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20032041

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Non-randomized controlled study (Deshpande et al., 60 second-year undergraduate students per Table 2) adding video and live demonstrations plus laboratory tours to conventional lectures; the experimental group showed greater understanding by test scores, with no effect size printed. Virtual Patient and digital lecture studies reported acceptance and feedback only.

> "Deshpande, S. et al. [25] demonstrated that students in the experimental group, following video and live demonstrations of relevant laboratory dental material procedures in addition to conventional lectures and guided laboratory tours, revealed a greater understanding of related topics than the control group according to their test scores."

### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2023 (2)

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Tan Wen-Wu, Tan Hung-Jia, Khoo Chia-Wee, Afrashtehfar Kelvin I. (2023). Innovative Pedagogical Strategies in Health Professions Education: Active Learning in Dental Materials Science. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20032041

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Historically controlled non-randomized study (Barbour et al., 142 second-year undergraduate students per Table 2) of an eVoting audience-response system in an undergraduate dental materials science course; no significant exam impact, equivalence not tested, no effect size printed.

> "It was discovered that the eVoting system proved overwhelmingly accepted among students, but such an innovative pedagogical strategy was found to have no significant impact on the examination results."
