## Evidence

### Peter A. Bennett 2025

Peter A. Bennett. (2025). Online “Advanced Labs” in Physics. [doi:10.1119/5.0242200](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0242200)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor's retrospective comparison of ten years of in-person and ten years of online Advanced Lab delivery at ASU, reported descriptively with no test statistics. The author reports that "the average performance of the online cohort was comparable to that of the in-person cohort".

> "Within those groups, the average performance of the online cohort was comparable to that of the in-person cohort. The online cohort, however, had a broader distribution, with some students unable to pass the course in their first attempt"
