Across interviews with twelve pre-service teachers (six per group), the article reports that PBL participants cited "more self -confidence" and "intrinsic motivation" while LBCC participants cited "to feel inadequacy" and "to feel ineffectiveness," reflecting contrasting motivational profiles.
