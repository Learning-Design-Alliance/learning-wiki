## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis supports treating phubbing, cybergossip, and media multitasking as indicators of one latent factor, 'habitual digital behaviors', with invariance across gender and academic-year groups. [→ Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022](#ana-cebollero-salinas-2022)

## Evidence

### Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022

Ana Cebollero-Salinas, Jacobo Cano-Escoriaza, Santos Orejudo. (2022). Are socio-emotional e-competencies protective factors against habitual digital behaviors (media multitasking, phubbing, cybergossip) in Spanish secondary students?. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104464

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CFA within the SEM analysis of questionnaire data from n = 776 secondary students (55.0% girls, mean age 13.83). The authors report that "these three variables can be regarded as belonging to the same factor", with invariant fit across gender (RMSEA = 0.000) and academic-year groups (RMSEA = 0.006).

> "Data confirm that these three variables can be regarded as belonging to the same factor, which we denote as “habitual digital behaviors”."
