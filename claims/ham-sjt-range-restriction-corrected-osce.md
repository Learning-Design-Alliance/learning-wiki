Mielke, I., Breil, S. M., Hissbach, J., Ehrhardt, M., & Knorr, M. (2025). Predicting undergraduate OSCE performance using traditional and construct-driven situational judgment tests at admission. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 30(3), 777–794. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10379-3

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the high-stakes sample (nHS = 160) with a Thorndike's Case 2 correction using the unrestricted standard deviation from admitted and non-admitted applicants. The raw correlation (r = .14, p = .083) was insignificant; corrected it reached "rвАЙ=.20" (r = .20).

> "However, when controlling for the range restriction on the HAM-SJT due to its relevance in one admission quota, the relation between the HAM-SJT and OSCE Interaction performance increased to rвАЙ=.20, which was comparable to the moderate effect found for the communal scale of the CD-SJT."
