`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified subgroup analysis by trainee type in the same randomized trial (table 2). Fellows: 90.5% vs 85.9% (OR 1.57, P=0.39); SRNAs: 81.3% vs 72.7% (OR 1.82, P=0.34); these subgroup results were not statistically significant.

> "Residents had a first attempt success of 93% (132/142) in the treatment group versus 81.4% (140/172) in the control group (odds ratio 3.18 (95% CI 1.62 to 6.24), P=0.001)."
