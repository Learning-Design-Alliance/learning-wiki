# Working memory measures taken at age 7–8 predict mathematics performance at age 9–10 two years later, over and above earlier mathematics performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a longitudinal SEM, paths from spatial–sequential and verbal working memory factors measured at T1 to mathematics at T2 two years later were statistically significant, controlling for mathematics at T1. [→ Allen 2020](#allen-2020)

## Evidence

### Allen 2020

Allen, K., Giofrè, D., Higgins, S., & Adams, J. (2020). Using working memory performance to predict mathematics performance 2 years on. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-020-01382-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal SEM (N = 159, FIML estimation) with three latent working memory factors at T1 predicting mathematics at T2, with an autoregressive path from mathematics at T1. The article reports the T2 paths from "sequential and verbal working memory, were statistically significant" while the simultaneous path was not; no effect size value is printed.

> "As for mathematics at T2, the path from mathematics at T1 as well as paths from sequential and verbal working memory, were statistically significant albeit with a small effect size, whilst the path from simultaneous working memory was not."
