# Physical activity is positively associated with academic performance, but the article could not demonstrate an association for team sports specifically

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Weekly frequency, weekly hours, and individual physical activity correlate positively with academic performance (tau 0.057–0.116), but the association for team physical activity was not demonstrated (tau = 0.024). [→ Sánchez-Hernando 2021](#sanchez-hernando-2021)

## Evidence

### Sánchez-Hernando 2021

Sánchez-Hernando, B.; Antón-Solanas, I.; Juárez-Vela, R.; Gea-Caballero, V.; Carboneres-Tafaner, M.I.; Ferrer-Gracia, E.; Gállego-Diéguez, J.; Santolalla-Arnedo, I.; Gasch-Gallén, Á. (2021). Healthy Lifestyle and Academic Performance in Middle School Students from the Region of Aragón (Spain). Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 18(16): 8624. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168624

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey; Table 3 reports Kendall tau = 0.057 (weekly frequency), tau = 0.073 (hours), tau = 0.024 (team) and tau = 0.116 (individual), all p < 0.001; the largest printed effect size is tau = 0.116 for individual activity. The authors state "we were unable to demonstrate an association between practicing team sports and academic performance".

> "Statistically significant and positive correlations were observed between frequency of weekly physical activity, hours of weekly physical activity, team and individual physical activity, and academic performance (Table 3). Further, a statistically significant association was found between academic performance and physical activity in terms of weekly frequency, hours per week, and individual activity."
