## Evidence

### Andrew Low and Yasemin Kalender 2024

Andrew Low and Yasemin Kalender. (2024). Predictors and Socio-Demographic Disparities in STEM Degree Outcomes: A UK Longitudinal Study using Hierarchical Logistic Regression. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.05853](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.05853)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical logistic regression of 8,965 STEM graduates (2013/14–2021/22) with random intercepts for STEM Subject. The best model shows "Black students have 0.39 (95% CI: 0.26–0.59) times the odds" of a first versus White students; the AME for Black students is -0.16 (95% CI: -0.22, -0.10) and for Asian students -0.09 (95% CI: -0.13, -0.06).

> "Black students have 0.39 (95% CI: 0.26–0.59) times the odds of achieving a first-class degree compared to White students. Asian students have 0.61 (95% CI: 0.49–0.75) times the odds"
