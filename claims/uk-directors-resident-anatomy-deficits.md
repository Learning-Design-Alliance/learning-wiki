Narrative review reporting a UK survey of surgical program directors: 24% judged new residents' anatomy knowledge seriously lacking and 67% in need of a refresher, while 52% saw less knowledge than a decade prior. The review also cites a 7-fold increase in anatomical-error claims to the Medical Defence Union between 1995 and 2000.
