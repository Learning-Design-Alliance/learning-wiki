`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of confidence and accuracy per case in the 252-participant survey. Case 7 had the lowest accuracy (34.92% correct) despite a mean confidence rating of 6.33, described as "a high rate of error coincides with unjustified confidence".

> "The findings show that confidence is not a reliable indicator of competency in diagnosis for certain types of lesions (especially case 7 and, to a lesser extent, case 4)."
