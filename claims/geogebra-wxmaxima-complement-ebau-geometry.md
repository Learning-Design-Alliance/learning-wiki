# For an EBAU geometry exercise, the optimal solution combines Geogebra for the visual relative-position part and WxMaxima for the calculation part

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Working an EBAU geometry exercise three ways, the author finds the manual solution tedious and lacking visualization, WxMaxima simplifies calculation but lacks visualization, and Geogebra is visual but its distance result is approximate. [→ José Manuel Fernández-Barroso 2024](#jose-manuel-fernandez-barroso-2024)
`q1 i?` The author concludes the optimal solution mixes the two programs: Geogebra for the first part, WxMaxima for the second. [→ José Manuel Fernández-Barroso 2024 (2)](#jose-manuel-fernandez-barroso-2024-2)
