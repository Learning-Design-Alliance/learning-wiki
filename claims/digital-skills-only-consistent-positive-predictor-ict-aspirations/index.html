Evidence¶

Popa 2022¶

Popa, D. M., Oprea, S.-V., & Bâra, A. (2022). Learning after COVID-19 and the ICT career aspirations: Are students entering the AI era with weaker skills? Department of Economic Informatics and Cybernetics, Bucharest University of Economic Studies. simona.oprea@csie.ase.ro. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27391 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.27391

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Country-level multivariate linear regression of ΔICT on standardized autonomy, digital skills and teacher support effects from PISA 2022. The article reports the printed coefficients and calls digital skills "the only consistently positive predictor".

"The standardized coefficients reveal that teacher support has the strongest (negative) association (β = -0.324), followed by autonomy (β = -0.192), whereas digital skills have a weaker positive effect (β = 0.072)."
