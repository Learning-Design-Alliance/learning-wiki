## Evidence

### Sattler David N. 2015

Sattler David N., McKnight Patrick E., Naney Linda, Mathis Randy. (2015). Grant Peer Review: Improving Inter-Rater Reliability with Training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0130450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A Poisson regression in the same trial compared self-reported criteria-reading time by experience level: experienced reviewers averaged "M = 6.0 minutes" versus 4.2 for novices, z = 3.22, p = 0.001.

> "Additionally, experienced reviewers (M = 6.0 minutes, SD = 5.6) spent more time reading the criteria than the novice reviewers (M = 4.2 minutes, SD = 4.0; Poisson regression, z = 3.22, p = 0.001)."
