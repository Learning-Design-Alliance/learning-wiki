## Evidence

### Timonen Tomi 2022

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intraclass correlation analysis comparing distances measured by five novices and five experts in VR versus PACS on five cadaver temporal bones. The article reports ICC values of at least 0.783 across groups and distance types, described as "A strong correlation"; no standardized effect size beyond the ICCs is printed.

> "A strong correlation between VR and cross-sectional viewing was found for the measurements of the fiducial (ICCnovices ≥ 0.906, ICCexperts ≥ 0.916) and the anatomical distances (ICCnovices ≥ 0.783, ICCexperts ≥ 0.900) in both groups."

### Timonen Tomi 2022 (2)

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Inter-rater reliability across participants in both methods; mean ICCs ranged from 0.848 to 0.999, with VR inter-rater ICCs exceeding PACS in each group and distance type. Intra-rater reliability could not be tested since participants measured each temporal bone only once per method.

> "High inter-rater reliability (mean ICC > 0.848) was found in both cross-sectional viewing and VR methods."
