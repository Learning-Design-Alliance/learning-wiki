## Evidence

### Arcand KK 2024

Arcand KK, Schonhut-Stasik JS, Kane SG, Sturdevant G, Russo M, Watzke M, Hsu B and Smith LF (2024). A Universe of Sound: processing NASA data into sonifications to explore participant response. Front. Commun. 9:1288896. https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1288896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online survey of cleaned sample of 3,184 responses comparing BLV and sighted participants' Likert ratings after each of three sonifications, analyzed with two-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests. The BLV group "rated learning more, particularly for Cassiopeia A" with p = 0.02407; no effect size is printed.

> "For each soniﬁcation, the BLV group rated learning more, particularly for Cassiopeia A, where the low p-value ( p = 0.02407) suggests a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the responses of the BLV and sighted groups."
