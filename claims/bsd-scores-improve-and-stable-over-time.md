## Evidence

### Maurer and Keim 2018

Trent W. Maurer, Cassidy Keim. (2018). Teaching about prejudice with a Bogardus Social Distance Scale activity: Replication and extension. IJ-SoTL, Vol. 12, No. 1, Art. 7. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2018.120107

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures MANOVA on 240 students in an introductory family science course across three time points; the within-subjects Time effect remained significant after Huynh-Feldt correction, F(1.79, 419.32) = 46.344, p < .001, with printed effect size partial eta-squared = 0.17, demonstrating a decline in BSDS scores over time.
