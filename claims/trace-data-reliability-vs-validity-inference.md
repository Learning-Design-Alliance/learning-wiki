de Barba, P. G., Oliveira, E. A., & English, N. (2025). Development and validation of a learning analytics rubric for self-regulated learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10521-x

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's literature review notes that the relationship between observable trace data and underlying SRL constructs is not always straightforward, requiring thoughtful integration of theory and empirical evidence. A systematic review by Du et al. (2023) found previous research either relies only on trace data or considers other data sources for triangulation.

> "While learning analytics can reliably (i.e., consistently) record certain behaviors, such as logins to an LMS, it cannot necessarily validate inferences about what learners know or are motivated to learn (Song et al., 2025; Winne, 2020)."
