## Evidence

### DeVore 2013

DeVore, S., Gauthier, A., Levy, J., & Singh, C. (2013). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. 2013 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud interviews with six graduate students found the low-pass filter's effect is overlooked: "interviewed students had great difficulty with the fact that the frequencies that will make it into the output signal" can be estimated using the time constant.

> "For example, interviewed students had great difficulty with the fact that the frequencies that will make it into the output signal can be estimated by making use of the time constant, τ."
