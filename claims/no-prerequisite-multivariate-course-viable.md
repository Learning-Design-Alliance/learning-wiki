## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with a background course (or both) performed comparably or better than students without on the examinations, except midterm 2 for the linear-algebra-only group, suggesting the material was made accessible without prerequisites. [→ Amy Wagaman 2013](#amy-wagaman-2013)

## Evidence

### Amy Wagaman 2013

Amy Wagaman, Amherst College. (2013). Meeting Student Needs for Multivariate Data Analysis: A Case Study in Teaching a Multivariate Data Analysis Course with No Pre-requisites. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.7141](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.7141)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison of converted percent exam scores across four background groups in a single 19-student course offering; the author notes "the small group sizes and observational nature of this evaluation" and concludes there is not a large gap between students with and without background courses.

> "Based on these comparisons, students with a background course (or both) performed comparably or better than their counterparts on the examinations, except midterm 2 for group 2. However, there is not a large gap in performance between students with and without background courses."
