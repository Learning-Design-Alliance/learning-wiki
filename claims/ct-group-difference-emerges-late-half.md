## Evidence

### Pu Dan 2019

Pu Dan, Ni Juhua, Song Demao, Zhang Weiguang, Wang Yuedan, Wu Liling, Wang Xian, Wang Yun. (2019). Influence of critical thinking disposition on the learning efficiency of problem-based learning in undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1418-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test on Late-Half tutorial averages (Strong-CT n = 28, Weak-CT n = 30). Table 5 prints Late-Half PBL scores of 89.83 ± 2.65 versus 87.54 ± 2.21, P = 0.001, with all five dimensions also significant.

> "However, in the Late-Half PBL tutorials, students with strong CT dispositions had higher PBL scores than students with weak CT dispositions"

### Pu Dan 2019 (2)

Pu Dan, Ni Juhua, Song Demao, Zhang Weiguang, Wang Yuedan, Wu Liling, Wang Xian, Wang Yun. (2019). Influence of critical thinking disposition on the learning efficiency of problem-based learning in undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1418-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test on Early-Half tutorial averages in the same extreme-group comparison. Table 5 prints an Early-Half PBL score difference of 88.96 ± 2.50 versus 88.05 ± 2.63, P = 0.186; no dimension reached significance.

> "It was observed that the average scores of the PBL and the five dimensions in the two groups were not different in the Early-Half PBL tutorials."
