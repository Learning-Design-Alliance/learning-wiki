## Evidence

### Engerer Cosima 2016

Engerer Cosima, Berberat Pascal O., Dinkel Andreas, Rudolph Baerbel, Sattel Heribert, Wuensch Alexander. (2016). Integrating 360° behavior-orientated feedback in communication skills training for medical undergraduates: concept, acceptance and students’ self-ratings of communication competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0792-0

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre-post self-assessment on 10-cm visual analogue scales completed before and after the CST by 34 students; t-test for dependent variables with effect sizes by Glass's Δ. The article reports "a pre-post effect size of ES = 0.74 on a global rating".

> "Overall, student self-ratings of communication competence significantly improved in nine of the ten communication items assessed by VAS and showed a pre-post effect size of ES = 0.74 on a global rating."

### Engerer Cosima 2016 (2)

Engerer Cosima, Berberat Pascal O., Dinkel Andreas, Rudolph Baerbel, Sattel Heribert, Wuensch Alexander. (2016). Integrating 360° behavior-orientated feedback in communication skills training for medical undergraduates: concept, acceptance and students’ self-ratings of communication competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0792-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Results section analysis of the general-theme VAS items for the same 34-student pre-post design. The article reports "ES = 2.87 and ES = 1.82" for theoretical knowledge and application of knowledge, and notes the high theoretical-knowledge effect seemed surprising given students' low evaluation of the theoretical part.

> "Especially, in the items ‘theoretical knowledge’ and ‘application of knowledge’, significant improvement was noted by the students (both p < 0.000) with ES = 2.87 and ES = 1.82."
