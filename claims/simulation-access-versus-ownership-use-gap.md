# Programs that only have access to a simulation resource use it less frequently than programs that have it

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A series of chi-square analyses showed that programs reporting 'having access to' a given simulation resource use it less frequently than programs reporting 'having' it; the gap is widest for advanced manikins (91% available, 71% used). [→ McKenna K. D. 2015](#mckenna-k-d-2015)

## Evidence

### McKenna K. D. 2015

McKenna K. D., Carhart E., Bercher D., Spain A., Todaro J., Freel J. (2015). Simulation Use in Paramedic Education Research (SUPER): A Descriptive Study. Prehospital Emergency Care. [doi:10.3109/10903127.2014.995845](https://doi.org/10.3109/10903127.2014.995845)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analyses comparing use by availability status across the survey sample. The advanced-manikin gap is reported as wider than for other manikin types, with the printed percentages 91% available and 71% used.

> "Advanced manikins were available to 91% of programs (76% have and 15% have access to), but only 71% of these programs indicated that they use them. This is a wider gap than in all other manikin types reported."
