### Dai 2026 (2)

Dai (2026). Rethinking AI-assisted writing instruction: feedback literacy scripts, calibration training, and student writing development. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1829268

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics across the four groups (Table 1) show the APCA group's lowest mean absolute error and overconfidence rate; the regular AI, FRAC, and combined groups showed higher error and overconfidence rates. Values are descriptive.

> "The APCA group had the lowest mean absolute error (M = 2.25, SD = 1.87) and the lowest overconfidence rate (5.0%)."
