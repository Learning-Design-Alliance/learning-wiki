## Evidence

### Pilakouta 2011

Pilakouta, M. (2011). Statistical uncertainty in educational experiment on the attenuation of gamma radiation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview-based observation of students in the TEI Piraeus physics laboratory who faced problems during data acquisition. The author records that "More than 80% of the students fail to calculate correctly the statistical uncertainty", using the net counts alone instead of the combined uncertainty of source and background counts.

> "More than 80% of the students fail to calculate correctly the statistical uncertainty (absolute and relative) because they underestimate or ignore the background radiation or they do not feel enough confident to use the combined uncertainty."
