# After processing the same 540 syllables as 8-month-old infants, PARSER had extracted a high proportion of the test words and none of the boundary-crossing nonwords

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Perruchet 1998

Perruchet, P., & Vinter, A. (1998). PARSER: A Model for Word Segmentation. Journal of Memory and Language, 39, 246-263. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.1998.2576

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation (Study 3) of the Saffran et al. (1996a) infant experiments, run with the forgetting rate increased from 0.05 to 0.08. The article states these findings "allow only indirect conclusions" because translating a weight into listening duration is arbitrary.

> "Over the 100 simulations, 52 had the two words involved in the test in PS (with a weight greater than 1), and 93 had at least one of the two words after processing 540 syllables."
