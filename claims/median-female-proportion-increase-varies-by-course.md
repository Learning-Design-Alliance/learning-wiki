## Evidence

### Eamonn Corrigan 2023

Eamonn Corrigan, Martin Williams, and Mary A. Wells. (2023). High School Enrolment Choices — Understanding the STEM Gender Gap. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted least squares regressions of median female proportion versus time per course (Table I), on eleven years of Ontario school-level data. Grade 10 Science and 11U Functions showed no statistically significant change; 12U Biology had the largest estimate (+0.594) and grade 12 mathematics courses estimates around 0.15.

> "Of the remaining STEM courses, all show an increase in the median proportion of female students. The effect size, however, varies widely between STEM courses."
