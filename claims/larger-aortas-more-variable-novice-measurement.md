# Measurement variability between novices and assessors was greater for aortas of 2.5 cm or larger than for smaller aortas

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At the mid infrarenal level, the absolute mean difference of novice measurements was significantly greater in aortae measured 2.5 cm or greater (0.68 ± 0.48) than in aortae less than 2.5 cm (0.22 ± 0.26; P = .001), an effect also observed at distal infrarenal and maximal coronal levels. ## Evidence

### Nguyen Anh TV 2013

Nguyen Anh TV, Hill Geraldine B, Versteeg Matthew PT, Thomson Ian A, van Rij Andre M. (2013). Novices may be trained to screen for abdominal aortic aneurysms using ultrasound. Cardiovascular Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-7120-11-42

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of measurement variation by aortic size in the 215-participant screening study, reported in the Factors affecting performance section. The article reports the mid-infrarenal contrast with P = .001 and notes the same pattern at distal infrarenal (P = .0251) and maximal coronal (P = .0067) levels; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "the absolute mean difference of novice measurements was significantly greater in aortae measured 2.5 cm or greater (0.68 ± 0.48) compared with those taken in aortae with diameters less than 2.5 cm (0.22 ± 0.26; P = .001)"
