## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In think-aloud interviews, inflated explanations were much more common for BSF figures while individualized explanations were used more often for ICB figures; all eight students gave inflated explanations for at least 4 of 5 BSF figures. [→ Finby B 2021](#finby-b-2021)

## Evidence

### Finby B 2021

Finby B, Heyer LJ, Malcolm Campbell A. (2021). Data‐rich textbook figures promote core competencies: Comparison of two textbooks. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21488

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Think-aloud interviews with eight incoming first-year Biology majors (of twelve scheduled) engaging with five paired figures on antibody response, endosymbiosis, hemoglobin, neurons, and carbon fixation, coded inductively in AtlasTI. The coded summary showed "Inflated explanations were much more common for BSF figures than for ICB figures."

> "Inflated explanations were much more common for BSF figures than for ICB figures. Conversely, students used individualized explanations for ICB figures more often than they did for BSF figures."
