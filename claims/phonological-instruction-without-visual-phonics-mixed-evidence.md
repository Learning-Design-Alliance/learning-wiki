### Trezek and Mayer 2019

Beverly Trezek and Connie Mayer. (2019). Reading and Deafness: State of the Evidence and Implications for Research and Practice. Educ. Sci. 2019, 9, 216. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci9030216

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

From the same standards-based examination of 30 intervention studies using CEC evidence-based practice procedures. The review reports the mixed evidence classification was assigned because "mixed results were obtained in one investigation."

> "Two of the studies, both using single-subject design [54,55], comprised the phonological instruction without Visual Phonics category. Due to the number of studies and the fact that mixed results were obtained in one investigation, the CEC [50] mixed evidence classification was assigned to this practice."
