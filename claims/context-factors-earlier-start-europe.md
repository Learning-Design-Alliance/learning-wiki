## Evidence

### Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021

Albina Zavgorodniaia, Raj Shrestha, Juho Leinonen, Arto Hellas, John Edwards. (2021). Morning or Evening? An Examination of Circadian Rhythms of CS1 Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-context comparison of median keystroke timestamps (Table II, Fig. 3). The authors propose two contextual differences: the US allowed late submission for reduced points while European late work was not accepted, and European courses used tens of small weekly sub-projects versus two US sub-projects.

> "in every chronotype, students in the European context work on their projects at least 25 hours earlier, on average, than students in the US context, and European morning students work fully 50 hours earlier on average."
