# SDTM improved history-taking performance versus traditional training (d = 0.59)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` SDTM residents scored significantly higher on the standardized history-taking test than controls (10.19 vs 9.44 of 15, d = 0.59). [→ Yang Liu 2022](#yang-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison on a 15-point history-taking checklist with a standardized patient: SDTM 10.19 ± 1.348 vs control 9.44 ± 1.188, T = 2.665, P = 0.009, "a medium effect size (d = 0.59)".

> "There was a significant difference in history-taking performance scores between the two groups (P <0.001) with a medium effect size (d = 0.59)."
