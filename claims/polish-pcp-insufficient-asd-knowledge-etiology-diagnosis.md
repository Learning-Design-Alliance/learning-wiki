### Domarecki Patryk 2025

Domarecki Patryk, Plata-Nazar Katarzyna, Sohl Kristin. (2025). An Assessment of the Knowledge of Autism Spectrum Disorder Among Polish Primary Care Physicians. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61040761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 166 Polish primary care physicians using a 20-item knowledge questionnaire scored by category. The cohort's overall correct rate was "50.8% of correct answers", with etiology at 37.9% and diagnosis at 42.7%; support knowledge reached 70.1%.

> "In total, the whole cohort of respondents provided 50.8% of correct answers for the questionnaire. The lowest results (37.9% and 42.7%, respectively) refer to the knowledge of ASD etiology and diagnostic process."
