### Tanya Chichekian 2026

Tanya Chichekian, Maria Cutumisu, Annie Savard and Yi-Mei Zhang. (2026). Profiling Pre-service Teachers' Computational Thinking: The Role of Metacognition and Coding Experience. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 31–41. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9077

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A Kruskal-Wallis test on the single-item 10-point perceived difficulty measure (L_Diff) compared profiles after the Programming Lamps task. The article reports "H = 12.34, p < .01" with Proficient learners at M = 2.50 versus Novice at M = 4.10; no effect size is printed.

> "Finally, the  Kruskal-Wallis test confirmed differences in L_Diff , H = 12.34, p < .01, with Proficient learners reporting lower perceived difficulty (M = 2.50, SD = 1.20) than Novice learners (M = 4.10, SD = 1.50)."
