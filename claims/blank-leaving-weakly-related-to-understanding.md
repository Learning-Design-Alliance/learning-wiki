## Evidence

### Cassandra Paul 2018

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, Mary K. Chessey, and James Lucas. (2018). Pondering zeros: analysis of a decade of blanks and missed quizzes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis plotting unweighted average grade for non-blank answers against fraction blank (Fig. 3, 10-point-scale courses); a slight negative correlation exists but the article concludes blank-leaving is "a poor predictor of general understanding". Students leaving 10–15% blank spanned all letter grades A through F.

> "an R2 = 0 .13 suggests that the variable “Fraction of Answers Left Blank” is a poor predictor of general understanding of the material."

### Cassandra Paul 2018 (2)

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, Mary K. Chessey, and James Lucas. (2018). Pondering zeros: analysis of a decade of blanks and missed quizzes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sub-analysis restricted to students earning B (85% or 3.0) or better on all attempted problems (Fig. 4); blank-leaving is less common overall but the between-group differences seen in the full sample persist.

> "While there is less blank-leaving behavior overall, we still ﬁnd simi- lar differences between the student groups that we saw in Fig. 1 which included all students."
