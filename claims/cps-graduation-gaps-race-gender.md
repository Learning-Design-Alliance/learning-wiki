Descriptive disaggregation of the 2018–19 CPS ninth-grade cohort's four-year graduation rates by race/ethnicity and gender. Rates ranged from 75.6% for Black young men to 95.9% for Asian/Pacific Islander young women, against an all-students rate of 84.0%.
