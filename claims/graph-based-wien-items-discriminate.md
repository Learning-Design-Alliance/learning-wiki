### Colin S. Wallace 2017

Colin S. Wallace, Timothy G. Chambers, Edward E. Prather. (2017). An item response theory evaluation of the Light and Spectroscopy Concept Inventory national data set. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT item-parameter analysis of the reduced LSCI (Tables II and III): the five highest-discrimination items (12, 24, 26, 9, 6) all come from the Wien/luminosity-area-temperature group and all require graph interpretation, while word-based items (3, 16, 20) have lower discrimination. No effect size is printed.

> "This demonstrates that graph-based items assessing Astro 101 students' understandings of Wien's law and/or the luminosity-area-temperature relationship are especially eﬀective at discriminating between high- and low-ability students."
