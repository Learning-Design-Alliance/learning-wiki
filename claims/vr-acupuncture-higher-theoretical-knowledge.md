## Evidence

### Wu Lingyi 2026

Wu Lingyi, Chellappa Vigneshkumar, Xie Qi, Mao Xuan, Luximon Yan, Yu Riji. (2026). Immersive virtual reality for acupuncture knowledge and practical skills: a comparative educational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1922688

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the stratified randomized posttest comparison of 60 third-year TCM undergraduates (n = 30 per group), the VR group outperformed controls on the 12-item theory test, with the authors reporting a "large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.90, 95% CI [0.36, 1.43])".

> "students in the experimental group achieved a mean score of 10.08 (SD = 0.99), whereas the control group obtained a mean score of 9.04 (SD = 1.31). This between-group difference was statistically significant (t(58) = 3.484, p < 0.001) and corresponded to a large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.90, 95% CI [0.36, 1.43])"
