## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The total number of schemas invoked rose significantly (paired t-test p = 0.004, Cohen's d = 1.34), while schema distribution stayed about the same. [→ Tyler Garcia 2024](#tyler-garcia-2024)

## Evidence

### Tyler Garcia 2024

Tyler Garcia, Bill Bridges, Caitlin Solis, Wyatt Jones, and James T. Laverty, Caleb Linville, Scott Tanona, Jonathan Herington. (2024). The Effect of Value-Focused Discussions on Scientists' Ethical Decision Making. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.06292](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.06292)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on total schemas invoked per dilemma across pre- and post-interviews in the fellowship study; the increase in count of distinct reasonings was significant while the percentage breakdown of schemas showed no significant change.

> "The paired t-test for the total number of schemas invoked gives us a p-value of 0.004 and a Cohen's d of 1.34 (large effect size). The difference between the the total post-interview schemas invoked and total pre-interview schemas invoked are statistically significant."
