## Evidence

### Kyungmee Lee 2018

Kyungmee Lee. (2018). Everyone already has their community beyond the screen: reconceptualizing online learning and expanding boundaries. Educational Technology Research and Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9613-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of seventeen student-teachers in a 12-week online course on educational applications of technologies designed using the double-layered CoP model. The other half's professional communities "achieved much in the areas of membership development and knowledge sharing, but often failed to established shared practice".

> "More than a half of the course participants (10 out of 17) had successful external CoP experiences including collaborative practice."

### Kyungmee Lee 2018 (2)

Kyungmee Lee. (2018). Everyone already has their community beyond the screen: reconceptualizing online learning and expanding boundaries. Educational Technology Research and Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9613-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Brief follow-up email sent a few months after the 12-week double-layered CoP course ended. Jane reported her school CoP continued because she found it "extremely helpful to collaborate with other members on certain tasks".

> "Nine students responded that they were continuing to participate in their external professional CoPs."
