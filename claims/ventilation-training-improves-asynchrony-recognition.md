## Evidence

### Liu Shengjun 2025

Liu Shengjun, Zhao Zhangyi, Chen Xiangyu, Chi Yi, Yuan Siyi, Cai Fuhong, Song Zhangwei, Ma Yue, He Huaiwu, Su Longxiang, Long Yun. (2025). Evaluation of health care providers’ ability to identify patient-ventilator triggering asynchrony in intensive care unit: a translational observational study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06638-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the 109 qualified professionals comparing those with at least three theoretical class units (≥6 hours) of training against nontrained professionals. The article reports the trained-versus-nontrained accuracy contrasts and p values quoted here.

> "the recognition accuracy of trained professionals was greater than that of nontrained professionals for ineffective triggers [76.7% vs. 59.2% (p = 0.009)], auto-triggers [26.7% vs. 12.2% (p = 0.014)] and reverse triggers [30.8% vs. 12.2% (p = 0.002)]"

### Liu Shengjun 2025 (2)

Liu Shengjun, Zhao Zhangyi, Chen Xiangyu, Chi Yi, Yuan Siyi, Cai Fuhong, Song Zhangwei, Ma Yue, He Huaiwu, Su Longxiang, Long Yun. (2025). Evaluation of health care providers’ ability to identify patient-ventilator triggering asynchrony in intensive care unit: a translational observational study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06638-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary multiple logistic regression (forward mode) on identifying ≥4 of 8 asynchronies, entered with title, ICU working years, ventilation experience, training and publications. The article reports OR 3.37 (95% CI 1.30–8.75) for training.

> "An odds ratio of 3.37 (95% confidence interval (CI) 1.30–8.75) was obtained, which indicated that trained healthcare providers who had successfully completed at least 3 theoretical class units, including modes and settings of mechanical ventilation, patient ventilator synchrony, and waveform analysis, had 4-fold increased odds of identifying ≥ 4 patient-ventilator asynchrony correctly"
