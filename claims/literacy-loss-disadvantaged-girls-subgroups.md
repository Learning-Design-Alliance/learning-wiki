`q3 i?` The pandemic's negative effect on girls' reading ability was largest among the poorest wealth quintile (3.1–3.8%), rural girls (~1.8%), and OBC caste girls (1.5–1.6%); no significant effect was seen in the richest wealth quintile. [→ Nandi 2023](#nandi-2023)

## Evidence

### Nandi 2023

Nandi, A., Haberland, N., Kozak, M., & Ngô, T. D. (2023). The gendered effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on adolescent literacy and schooling outcomes in India. npj Science of Learning, 8, 42. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00193-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup PSM/IPW analysis of NFHS-5 girls' data by wealth quintile, residence, and caste. The effect "was largest among girls in the poorest wealth quintile (quintile 1)" at 3.1–3.8%, with no significant effect in quintile 5. Rural effect (~1.8%) was about twice the urban effect; OBC girls had the highest caste-group reduction.

> "the estimated effect was largest among girls in the poorest wealth quintile (quintile 1) (3.1% –3.8% reduction in reading ability), and it ranged from 0.9% –2.1% in wealth quintiles 2, 3, and 4. No statistically signi ﬁcant effect was seen in the richest wealth quintile (quintile 5)."
