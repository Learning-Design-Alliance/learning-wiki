### Henrik Örnebring 2007

Henrik Örnebring. (2007). Alternate reality gaming and convergence culture: The case of Alias. International Journal of Cultural Studies 10(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1367877907083079

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative archival case study reconstructing the two ABC-produced Alias ARGs from archive sites and player forums. The analysis finds the ties to the original fictional universe were decidedly 'one-way', with the rich interplay between texts described by Jenkins for the Matrix universe absent here.

> "the Season 1 and Season 2 Alias ARGs did not provide any insights into the backstory that could not also be gleaned from watching the show, and neither did they provide much of an opportunity for fans and participants to interact with the full fictional universe of Alias"
