### Abdulghani HM 2023

Abdulghani HM, Marwa K, Alghamdi NA, Almasoud RN, Faraj AT, Alshuraimi AF, Mohamed KM, Alnafisah OS, Ahmad T, Ahmed MZ, Khalil MS. (2023). Prevalence of the medical student syndrome among health professions students and its effects on their academic performance. Medicine 2023;102:43(e35594). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figures 1 and 2 report SHAI main-section and negative-consequences classifications by academic year in the cross-sectional sample; third-year students (44.3% of participants) showed the highest anxiety and hypochondria levels. No effect size is printed.

> "In Figure 1, third-year students reported more anxiety and hypochondria than other-year students in the main section. Similarly, third-year students have higher levels of anxiety and hypochondriac problems than other students in other years (Fig. 2)."

### Abdulghani HM 2023 (2)

Abdulghani HM, Marwa K, Alghamdi NA, Almasoud RN, Faraj AT, Alshuraimi AF, Mohamed KM, Alnafisah OS, Ahmad T, Ahmed MZ, Khalil MS. (2023). Prevalence of the medical student syndrome among health professions students and its effects on their academic performance. Medicine 2023;102:43(e35594). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 2 chi-square test of SHAI main section by academic year; the year association did not reach significance (P = .09), so the third-year pattern is descriptive rather than a confirmed year effect.

> "First and third year students have more hypochondriac (1st year = 33.3%; 3rd year = 10.5%) problems reported in the current study compared to other years (χ2 = 13.6; P = .09)."
