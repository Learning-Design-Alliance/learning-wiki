## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2018

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2018). Admission testing for higher education: A multi-cohort study on the validity of high-fidelity curriculum-sampling tests. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198746

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regressions of enrollment on standardized admission test scores controlling for the discouraging phone call, in each cohort of Study 1. The printed odds ratio effect size eB = 1.33 (2013) is small, consistent with impact 1; the curriculum-sampling score was the only significant predictor in 2013 and 2014, reflecting "self-chosen enrollment".

> "With each standard deviation unit increase in the test score, the odds of enrollment increased with eB = 1.33, eB = 2.04, and eB = 1.55 in 2013, 2014, and 2015, respectively."
