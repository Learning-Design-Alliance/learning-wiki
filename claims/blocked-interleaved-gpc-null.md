`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

In McMurray et al.'s (2019) study, first-grade students practiced GPCs either blocked (two new GPCs per block) or interleaved (six GPCs in parallel); the review reports no clear test-performance difference. The article notes even the blocked condition trained multiple GPCs simultaneously, unlike typical classroom practice.

> "No clear difference in test performance emerged between the two learning conditions."
