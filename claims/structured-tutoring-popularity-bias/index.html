Systematic review vote-counting analysis comparing element frequency in effective versus ineffective interventions. The review reports structured tutoring was "used in 15 effective interventions" while "25% of the studies using structured tutoring did not elicit statistically significant improvements."

"Interestingly, structured tutoring was the most common practice element, being used in 15 effective interventions. However, 25% of the studies using structured tutoring did not elicit statistically significant improvements."
