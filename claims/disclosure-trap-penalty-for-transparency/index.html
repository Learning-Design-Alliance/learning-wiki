The article reports, citing Gonsalves, research into student attitudes showing "students fear that declaring AI use, even if permitted, will lead to lower marks". The authors argue that in the absence of clear disclosure protocols, grader bias may penalize transparency, driving AI use underground.

"students fear that declaring AI use, even if permitted, will lead to lower marks, regardless of institutional policy [9]."
