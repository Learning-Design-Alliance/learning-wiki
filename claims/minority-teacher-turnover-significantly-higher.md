### Ingersoll 2017

Ingersoll, R., May, H., & Collins, G. (2017). Minority teacher recruitment, employment, and retention: 1987 to 2013. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/minority-teacher-recruitment

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal analysis of Teacher Follow-Up Survey migration and attrition data across seven cycles (Table 4). The report found minority turnover significantly higher in five of seven cycles, and "minority turnover was, respectively, 18%, 24%, and 25% higher than nonminority teacher turnover" in the three most recent cycles.

> "In the 2004–05, 2008–09, and 2012–13 school years, minority turnover was, respectively, 18%, 24%, and 25% higher than nonminority teacher turnover."
