## Evidence

### Ebadi and Pourzandi 2015

Saman Ebadi, Mahsa Pourzandi. (2015). The Effects of Explicit and Implicit Pragmatic Instruction on the Development of Compliments and Compliment Responses. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 4, October - December. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+Effects+of+Explicit+and+Implicit+Pragmatic+Instruction+on+the+Development+of+Compliments+and+Compliment+Responses

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison between the implicit instruction group and the control group in this quasi-experimental study. The article reports the printed percentages 79%, 21% and 83% descriptively; no significance test or effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "All participants in control group used just three strategies in both parts of the questionnaire. In other words, 79% were able to use three strategies and the other 21% had irrelevant Cs or CRs. This is while 83% of language learners in implicit instruction group managed to use 8 strategies."
