### Ortlieb 2013

Ortlieb, E. (2013). Using Anticipatory Reading Guides to Improve Elementary Students' Comprehension. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2), 145-162. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085407

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparing scores across the third-grade team's seven classes, the two treatment classes gained 7% overall versus 2% for Teachers A and B and no gain for Teacher C. On a social studies test, "Teacher A and B had a mastery rate of 50% and Teacher C experienced 80%, where the research group had a mastery of 79%" despite serving the lowest guided-reading-level students.

> "Teacher A and B experienced increases of 2% where teacher C did not have an increase. The two classes involved in the experimental treatment group experienced an overall increase of 7%."
