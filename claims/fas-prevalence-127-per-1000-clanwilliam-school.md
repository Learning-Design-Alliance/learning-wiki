## Evidence

### Lubbe Melissa 2017

Lubbe Melissa, van Walbeek Corné, Vellios Nicole. (2017). The Prevalence of Fetal Alcohol Syndrome and Its Impact on a Child’s Classroom Performance: A Case Study of a Rural South African School. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph14080896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Case study at a quintile 1 farm school near Clanwilliam; a local physician diagnosed FAS in three rounds (screening, examination, hospital-record review) among 166 learners, finding "21 learners or 12.7% of the full sample". No effect size is printed for prevalence.

> "the final round of reviewing hospital records resulted in a final diagnosis of FAS in 21 learners or 12.7% of the full sample (11 boys and 10 girls)"
