## Evidence

### Vecchione Michele 2021

Vecchione Michele, Vacca Mariacarolina. (2021). An Italian adaptation of the Child-Adolescent Perfectionism Scale: Testing measurement invariance across grade levels and exploring associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255814

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on school grades in both samples, with CAPS-14 scales at step 1 and personality traits at step 2. The article reports "SOP-Striving predicted higher grades in both groups" and that its effect "remained statistically significant" after the traits were controlled; the full model accounted for 7% and 10% of grade variance.

> "At the first step, SOP-Striving predicted higher grades in both groups. SOP-Critical and SSP, by contrast, predicted grades negatively among middle and high school students, respectively."
