Meta‐regression and subgroup analyses across the included studies found no significant moderator effects: synchronicity (QM = 0.10, df = 1, p = 0.8), repeat testing (QM = 0.8, df = 1, p = 0.4), study design (QM = 0.01, df = 1, p = 0.9), student level (QM = 0.1, df = 1, p = 0.8), and allocated e‐learning time (QM = 0.005, df = 1, p = 0.9).

"Moderator analysis indicated that knowledge gain was not significantly influenced by student characteristics (learner level), intervention characteristics (synchronicity, duration, and presence of repeat testing), or study characteristics (study design)."
