### Park Sunghae 2026

Park Sunghae, Oh Namkee, Rhu Jinsoo, Choi Gyu-Seong, Kim Jongman. (2026). Comparison of learning curve difference of laparoscopist according to controller type of flexible scope during laparoscopic liver resection. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12909-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression of operation time over chronological case order plotted for major liver resections (Fig. 3A–C), stratified by controller type. This is a plotted/regression trend in a retrospective cohort, not a randomized comparison; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, these findings suggest that, for major resections, operative efficiency improved over time in both groups when the joystick-controlled system was used, whereas beginner-assisted cases did not show comparable improvement with the conventional controller type."
