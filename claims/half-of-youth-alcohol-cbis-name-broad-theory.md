## Evidence

### Tebb Kathleen P. 2016

Tebb Kathleen P., Erenrich Rebecca K., Jasik Carolyn Bradner, Berna Mark S., Lester James C., Ozer Elizabeth M. (2016). Use of theory in computer-based interventions to reduce alcohol use among adolescents and young adults: a systematic review. BMC Public Health 16, 517. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-016-3183-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of CBIs for youth alcohol use, searched in PsychINFO, PubMed and Google Scholar between November and December 2014; 600 unique articles screened to 100 articles covering 42 unique CBIs. The review reports that "22 CBIs (52 %) explicitly named one or more theoretical frameworks" and lists the primary theories named.

> "In examining the use of theory, 22 CBIs (52 %) explicitly named one or more theoretical frameworks. Primary theories mentioned were social cognitive theory, transtheoretical model, theory of planned behavior and reasoned action, and health belief model."
