`q4 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation with Fisher's z transformation in the RCT; the GM group correlation was "very strong (r = 0.77; p = 0.005; 95% CI [0.32–0.94]), explaining about 60% of the variance in cadaver performance (R2 = 60%)".

> "In the CM group, the correlation between the last training procedure and cadaver performance was moderate yet not significant (r = 0.45; p = 0.16; 95% CI [− 0.20 to 0.83])."
