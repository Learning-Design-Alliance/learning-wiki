## Evidence

### Nevin Christa R 2014

Nevin Christa R, Westfall Andrew O, Rodriguez J Martin, Dempsey Donald M, Cherrington Andrea, Roy Brita, Patel Mukesh, Willig James H. (2014). Gamification as a tool for enhancing graduate medical education. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2013-132486

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of daily question counts and user counts the day before versus the day of each weekly notification email, using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests after histogram-based normality checks failed. Results are shown in figure 2 and described as statistically significant increases.

> "Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed statistically significant increases in total questions answered for rounds 1 and 2 and number of participants for rounds 1 and 3, when comparing totals for days before and days when weekly ‘status of competition’ emails were sent."
