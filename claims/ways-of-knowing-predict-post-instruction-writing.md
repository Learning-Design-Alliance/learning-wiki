## Evidence

### Ammar 2016

Ammar, Abdullah Mahmoud Ismial. (2016). Reflective Blogfolios in the Language Classroom: Impact on EFL Tertiary Students' Argumentative Writing Skills and Ways of Knowing. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(5). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.5p.247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis with ways of knowing as predictor and argumentative essay writing as dependent variable, run separately on pre- and post-assessment data from the 51 participants. Post R² = .084 is small; R² is variance explained, not a standardized effect size.

> "ways of knowing were not able to predict argumentative writing performance in the pre -assessment [F=2.763, P=.103, R 2 =.053]. Contrariwise, post -assessment results show ways of knowing as strong predictors of students' argumentation skills [F = 4.508, P = .039, R2 = .084]."
