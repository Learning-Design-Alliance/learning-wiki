### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standard two-step mediation analysis (Baron & Kenny; Bühner & Ziegler) regressing follow-up self-regulation outcomes on condition and post-test mindfulness. For ability to stop ruminating, the indirect effect through mindful acceptance accounted for 38% of the variance (adjusted r², F(2,25) = 9.55).

> "Of the eight qualifying outcome variables, six were fully mediated by changes in one or both mindfulness scales at post -test (table 5). Table 5 shows a summary of all mediation models."
