# Instruction in language, phonological awareness, and decoding improved performance in the taught domain, while interventions teaching print knowledge or early writing exclusively did not

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (4 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Teaching language produced a significant weighted effect size of 0.19 on language outcomes among 74 interventions. [→ Sarah Herrera 2021](#sarah-herrera-2021)
`q3 i1` Teaching phonological awareness produced a significant weighted effect size of 0.32 on phonological awareness outcomes among 36 interventions. [→ Sarah Herrera 2021 (2)](#sarah-herrera-2021-2)
`q3 i1` Teaching print knowledge produced a significant weighted effect size of 0.23 on print knowledge outcomes among 38 interventions. [→ Sarah Herrera 2021 (3)](#sarah-herrera-2021-3)

## Evidence

### Sarah Herrera 2021

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Weighted synthesis across 74 interventions teaching language in high-quality impact studies. The review reports a statistically significant weighted effect size of 0.19 on language outcomes, "equivalent to an 8 percentile point increase in language performance."

> "Teaching language resulted in a statistically significant weighted effect size of 0.19 on language outcomes, among the 74 interventions evaluated in studies that included at least one effect size estimate"

### Sarah Herrera 2021 (2)

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Weighted synthesis across 36 interventions teaching phonological awareness. The review reports a significant weighted effect size of 0.32, "equivalent to a 13 percentile point increase in performance," and significantly larger than for interventions not teaching phonological awareness.

> "Teaching phonological awareness resulted in a significant weighted effect size of 0.32 on phonological awareness outcomes, among the 36 interventions evaluated in studies that included at least one effect size estimate"

### Sarah Herrera 2021 (3)

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Weighted synthesis across 38 interventions teaching print knowledge. The review reports a significant weighted effect size of 0.23 on print knowledge outcomes, a 9 percentile point increase, versus 0.10 for interventions not teaching print knowledge.

> "Interventions that taught print knowledge resulted in a significant weighted effect size of 0.23 on print knowledge outcomes, among the 38 interventions evaluated in studies that included at least one effect size estimate"

### Sarah Herrera 2021 (4)

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of high-quality impact studies across the 112 interventions with at least one effect size estimate. The review reports that for print knowledge and early writing, "interventions that taught either domain exclusively did not improve performance in the taught domain."

> "For the print knowledge and early writing domains, interventions that taught either domain exclusively did not improve performance in the taught domain."
