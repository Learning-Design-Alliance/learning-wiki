## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After full standard practice, weekly 40-second reactivations produced a smaller performance decline at 30-day retention than no reactivations. [→ Gilad Schrift 2022](#gilad-schrift-2022)

## Evidence

### Gilad Schrift 2022

Gilad Schrift, Dror Dotan and Nitzan Censor. (2022). Brief memory reactivations induce learning in the numeric domain. npj Science of Learning 7:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00136-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2, a 2x2 repeated-measures ANOVA on recall score with Session (Post-training retest, Retention) and Group (Reactivations, Control); the Reactivations group's decline (−12.99 ± 3.48 S.E.) was smaller than the Control group's (−30.03 ± 5.95 S.E.). No effect size is printed.

> "a signi ﬁcant Session x Group interaction (F1,35 = 6.26, P = 0.017) indicated that the decline in performance in the Reactivationsgroup (mean post-training retest – retention score change= −12.99 ± 3.48 S.E., t(18)= −3.72, P = 0.002, paired t-test) was smaller than in the Control group (mean score change= −30.03 ± 5.95 S.E., t(17)= −5.05, P = 0.0001, paired t-test)"
