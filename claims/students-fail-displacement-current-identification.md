## Evidence

### Álvaro Suárez 2024

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C. Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2024). Learning difficulties among students when applying Ampère-Maxwell's law and its implications for teaching. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of written answers to question 2 on a discharging pair of spheres, where a displacement current should reduce circulation below Ampère's prediction. "Category A (12,3%) includes the students who understand the phenomenon properly"; some in A still assigned the displacement current the wrong sign.

> "Category A (12,3%) includes the students who understand the phenomenon properly, identifying that the value of the magnetic field circulation differs from what was predicted by Ampère's law."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Written-test category analysis for question 2: "In category C (43,1%), we group together the answers from students who consider the conduction current to be constant", split into rote application of Ampère's law (C1, 27,7%) and ad-hoc explanations (C2, 15,4%).

> "In category C (43,1%), we group together the answers from students who consider the conduction current to be constant."
