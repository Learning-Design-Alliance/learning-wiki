Exploratory Pearson correlations among post-intervention measures in the 60-student trial (Table 4) show r = 0.68 (PSS–DMC) to r = 0.85 (CD-RISC–DMC; HRV–HRR). Increases in SDNN and RMSSD and a lower LF/HF ratio were associated with better resilience and decision-making (Figure 2).

> "All correlations are positive and significant at p < 0.01 or p < 0.001."
