### Emily Marshman 2018

Emily Marshman, Zeynep Y. Kalender, Christian Schunn, Timothy Nokes-Malach, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). A longitudinal analysis of students' motivational characteristics in introductory physics courses: Gender differences. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0185](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0185)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Change-score analysis from the beginning of Physics 1 to the beginning of Physics 2 in calculus-based courses; the MANOVA on overall motivation change was not significant, but the univariate fascination test was. No effect size is printed for this change, so no magnitude is reported.

> "We did find that there was a significant difference in the change scores of males and females in regards to fascination in calculus-based Physics 1 courses (result of ANOVA gives  F(1, 280 )=5.688, p=0.018)."
