### Zhang Yongshuo 2026

Zhang Yongshuo, Li Wenbo, Zhang Chuchen, Wang Yawei, Feng Shuo. (2026). Reforming hospital management education with the P-MASE pedagogy: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08948-8

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Skills assessment in the RCT (Table 5): simulated departmental satisfaction in the budgeting/resource-allocation task was 82.5 ± 3.4% vs. 74.3 ± 5.1% (P < 0.001), interpreted as trade-off reasoning under constraints.

> "in the standardized budgeting/resource-allocation simulation task, the experimental group demonstrated higher performance, reflected by higher simulated departmental satisfaction (82.5 ± 3.4% vs. 74.3 ± 5.1%, P < 0.001)"

### Zhang Yongshuo 2026 (3)
