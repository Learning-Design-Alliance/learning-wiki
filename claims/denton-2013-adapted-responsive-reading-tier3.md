## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, C. R., Vaughn, S., & McClelland, A. M. (2017). Intensive Reading Interventions for Inadequate Responders in Grades K–3: A Synthesis. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 191–210. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714446

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Study in Table 6 of the synthesis: grade 2 students (some repeating grade 1) who responded inadequately to Tier 2 received an adapted Responsive Reading and Read Naturally Tier 3 intervention in groups of 2–3 for 45 min, 4–5×/week, 24–26 weeks, with "data-based decision making to adjust instruction"; all printed effects favored treatment.

> "Treatment > Control: WJ-III Basic Reading: d = .56; WJ-III Letter-Word ID: d = .44; WJ-III Word Attack: d = .65; TOWRE Word Reading Efficiency: d = .42"
