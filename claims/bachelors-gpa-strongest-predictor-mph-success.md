## Evidence

### Panczyk M 2017

Panczyk M, Juszczyk G, Zarzeka A, Samoliński Ł, Belowska J, Cieślak I, Gotlib J. (2017). Evidence-based selection process to the Master of Public Health program at Medical University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1007-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective predictive analysis of recruitment data and learning results of 605 students (2008–2012) using multiple linear regression. The Bachelor's studies GPA "had the greatest influence on the first-year GPA (βstand. = 0.367)" and was the strongest predictor in the cumulative GPA model (βstand. = 0.447).

> "The value of Bachelor's studies GPA constituting one of the admission criteria had the greatest influence on the first-year GPA (βstand. = 0.367)."
