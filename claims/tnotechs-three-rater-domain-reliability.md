## Evidence

### van Maarseveen O. E. C. 2020

van Maarseveen O. E. C., Ham W. H. W., Huijsmans R. L. N., Dolmans R. G. F., Leenen L. P. H. (2020). Reliability of the assessment of non-technical skills by using video-recorded trauma resuscitations. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-020-01401-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability analysis of the same 18-video, three-rater fully crossed design. The highest average aICC was Cooperation and resource management (0.95, 95% CI 0.89–0.98) and the lowest was Leadership (0.76, 95% CI 0.49–0.90); "the calculated ICC values were between 0.95 and 0.76".

> "When reliability was calculated for the mean of three assessors, the overall score and each domain were ‘excellent’, as the calculated ICC values were between 0.95 and 0.76."

### van Maarseveen O. E. C. 2020 (2)

van Maarseveen O. E. C., Ham W. H. W., Huijsmans R. L. N., Dolmans R. G. F., Leenen L. P. H. (2020). Reliability of the assessment of non-technical skills by using video-recorded trauma resuscitations. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-020-01401-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Single-rater reliability from the same study was lower than three-rater reliability: Leadership (aICC = 0.52) and Situation awareness and coping with stress (aICC = 0.59) were rated fair, while Communication and interaction and Assessment and decision making were good (aICC = 0.73).

> "The scoring domains ‘’Leadership’ (aICC = 0.52) and ‘Situation awareness and coping with stress’ (aICC = 0.59) had a only a ‘fair’ single assessor reliability."
