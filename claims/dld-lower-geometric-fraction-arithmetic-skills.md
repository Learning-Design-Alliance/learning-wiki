## Evidence

### Kleemans 2025

Kleemans, T., Vissers, C. T. W. M., & Segers, E. (2025). Linguistic precursors of sixth-grade geometric and fraction skills in children with and without Developmental Language Disorder. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102019. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102019

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

MANCOVA comparing children with DLD (n = 46) and TD peers (n = 122) with age, fathers' SES, and sex as covariates. DLD mean geometric skills was 15.63 vs. 39.95 for TD, F(1,163) = 107.89, p < .001, ηp2 = .40.

> "Geometric skills (Grade 6) 15.63 7.98 39.95 14.04 107.89 <.001 .40"

### Kleemans 2025 (2)

Kleemans, T., Vissers, C. T. W. M., & Segers, E. (2025). Linguistic precursors of sixth-grade geometric and fraction skills in children with and without Developmental Language Disorder. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102019. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102019

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same MANCOVA design: DLD children scored 4.15 on fraction skills versus 19.18 for TD, F(1,163) = 68.44, p < .001, ηp2 = .30. The article notes these effect sizes were more than double those of arithmetic skills.

> "Fraction skills (Grade 6) 4.15 5.21 19.18 11.30 68.44 <.001 .30"
