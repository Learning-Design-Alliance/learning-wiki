## Evidence

### Schachner 2024

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The report's review states no specific dosage is established and that coaching "appears more sensitive to the quality of coaching rather than the dosage." It reports studied dosages ranging from 3 to 32 sessions and sessions from a few minutes to 5 hours.

> "The research literature does not conclusively suggest that there is a specific dosage (i.e., total number of hours, frequency, or length of sessions) necessary for effective coaching, but rather that coaching appears more sensitive to the quality of coaching rather than the dosage."

### Schachner 2024 (2)

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The review reports that for "more comprehensive coaching," a common theme is greater effectiveness when coaching is "continuous over a sustained duration," noting early-learning coaching is implemented over an average of 5 to 7 months.

> "However, for more comprehensive coaching, a common theme in the literature is that coaching is more effective when it is continuous over a sustained duration."
