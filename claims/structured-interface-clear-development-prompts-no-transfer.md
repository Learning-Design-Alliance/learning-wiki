## Evidence

### Brender 2025

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Uittenhove, K., Mondada, F., Bumbacher, E. (2025). Structured Prompts, Better Outcomes? Exploring the Effects of a Structured Interface with ChatGPT in a Graduate Robotics Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square tests comparing joint distributions of prompt types and attributes between conditions across 291 session-2 and 411 session-3 prompts. The authors state the effect was short-lived: once the interface was removed, use of Clear Development prompts did not differ from the comparison group.

> "students in the intervention group had a higher proportion of clear development prompts (prompts coded as Development and Clarity), χ2(3)=8.57, p = .036, in session 2, when using the structured interface, but there was no significant difference in session 3"
