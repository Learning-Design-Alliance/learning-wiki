Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 554 Spanish health professionals conducted April–July 2023 with a 27-question instrument. In the results, "only 69% and 56.5% of the respondents could identify" the critical inhalation steps for pMDI and DPI, meaning 31% and 43.5% could not.

> "Regarding the technical knowledge of the inhalers (Table 2), only 69% and 56.5% of the respondents could identify, respectively, which were the critical steps for correct inhalation of the pressurized cartridge (pMDI) and the multidose dry powder inhaler (DPI)."
