## Subclaims
`q3 i2` With goals experimentally induced, the goals × partner competence interaction on model preference was significant (β = .28, p < .03, partial η² = .08); performance-approach goals predicted more preference for the confirmation model than performance-avoidance goals when the partner was more competent (β = .39, p < .03, partial η² = .07) but not when equal (β = −.17, p = .28). ## Evidence

### Sommet 2015

Sommet, N., Darnon, C., & Butera, F. (2015). To Confirm or to Conform? Performance Goals as a Regulator of Conflict With More-Competent Others. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 580–598. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037240

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 73 French vocational school students (3 outliers dropped) in which performance goals were manipulated via validated instructions and partner competence via grade level. Outcome was preference for a confirmation over a compliance model; the interaction reached partial η² = .08 (medium effect), providing convergent validity beyond self-report.
