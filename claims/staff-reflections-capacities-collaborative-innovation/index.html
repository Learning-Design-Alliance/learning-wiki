Latia P. White 2026¶

Latia P. White, Ed. D. (2026). Beyond Participation: The Capacities That Shape Engagement in Collaborative Innovation. Digital Promise, Center for Learner Pathway Innovations. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/e933ec0e-f153-4046-842b-61b696b00ba2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of structured reflections from 10 researchers and 11 program designers and implementers, coded within the dimensions of skills, knowledge, and dispositions. The team "developed preliminary codes within the three dimensions named in the research question" and identified recurring patterns. No effect sizes are reported.

"To understand what supports meaningful engagement in Collaborative Innovation, we gathered structured reflections from 10 researchers and 11 program designers and implementers within the Center for Learner Pathway Innovations."
