### Shengyao Ye 2024

Shengyao Ye, Xuefen Lin, Jenatabadi Hashem Salarzadeh, Samsudin Nadia, Chunchun Ke, Ishak Zahari. (2024). Emotional intelligence impact on academic achievement and psychological well-being among university students: the mediating role of positive psychological characteristics. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01886-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling of survey data from 518 Chinese university students (323 undergraduate, 189 postgraduate) found "a substantial positive link with self-efficacy, motivation, and resilience" in both groups; Table 3 reports direct estimates such as EI→Self-efficacy 0.417*** (undergraduate) and 0.467*** (postgraduate).

> "Emotional intelligence has been found to have a substantial positive link with self-efficacy, motivation, and resilience among both undergraduate and postgraduate students."
