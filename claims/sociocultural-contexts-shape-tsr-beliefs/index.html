Robinson 2022¶

Robinson, C. D. (2022). A Framework for Motivating Teacher-Student Relationships. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09706-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Document analysis of the 2015 Every Student Succeeds Act as an illustration of policy context; the article also cites Valli and Buese's finding that NCLB demands demoted teachers' relationships with students. Presented as illustrative, not a tested effect.

"The 449-page document refers to the term "standard" 244 times, "assessment" 380 times, and the term "relationship" in the social context 6 times (ESSA, 2015)."

Robinson 2022 (2)¶

Robinson, C. D. (2022). A Framework for Motivating Teacher-Student Relationships. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09706-0

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument that stereotypes and a predominantly White teaching force may lead teachers to hold biases affecting the value and self-efficacy of TSRs with stigmatized students, potentially contributing to documented racial match effects on student outcomes. No new data reported.

"they may have lower relational efficacy with students who do not share their ethnic-racial backgrounds"
