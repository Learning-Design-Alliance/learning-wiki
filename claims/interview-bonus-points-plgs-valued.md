## Evidence

### Korner 2025

Korner, M., & Likos, C. N. (2025). On the design of a profession-oriented course on Theoretical Mechanics for physics education students. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative finding from N = 8 semi-structured student interviews (4 per year) covering peer activities, perceived relevance of contexts, and differences to other lectures; students valued the structured, voluntary, feedback-provided engagement.

> "The possibility of completing the bonus exercises (bonus points) was reported most of all and it was highly appreciated, followed by the implementation of PLGs."

### Korner 2025 (2)

Korner, M., & Likos, C. N. (2025). On the design of a profession-oriented course on Theoretical Mechanics for physics education students. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

From the same N = 8 interviews: the profession-oriented linking, delivered through the lecturer's high-school teaching expertise, shaped students' perception of the course's relevance; some interviewees also reported the workload was high relative to the credits.

> "it was appreciated that there have been at several points cross-references to the students' future teaching at schools, as one of the lecturers (MK) contributed her expertise from high-school teaching."
