## Evidence

### Oudman 2024

Oudman, S., van de Pol, J., Janssen, E., & van Gog, T. (2024). Primary school students' awareness of their monitoring and regulation judgment accuracy. Learning and Instruction, 92, 101907. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101907

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-level interaction analysis in the same multilevel models testing whether students' mean task performance (averaged across two days, grand mean centered) moderated the accuracy-SOJ-m relationship. The interaction was non-significant for both multiplication and division tasks, so higher-performing students were no more aware of their monitoring accuracy than lower-performing peers.

> "For both the multiplication and division task, the interaction effect was not significant (see M2 in Table 2), indicating that students' mean task performance had no impact on their monitoring accuracy awareness before self-scoring."
