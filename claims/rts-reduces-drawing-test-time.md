## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Siu Yikchi 2025

Siu Yikchi, Bai Hefei, Yoon Jung-Min, Ye Hongqiang, Liu Yunsong, Zhou Yongsheng. (2025). Enhancing Preclinical Training for Removable Partial Dentures Through Participatory 3D Simulation: Development and Usability Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71743

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Test-time analysis of the same 26-student randomized comparison. Drawing test time was significantly shorter for the RTS group (P<.001); theoretical test time showed no significant difference (P=.14 per the Abstract). No effect size is printed for the time difference.

> "the mean completion times were 23.2 (SD 2.2) minutes in the training group and 24.9 (SD 2.8) minutes in the control group for the theoretical test; they were 12.6 (SD 1.9) minutes and 17.7 (SD 3) minutes for the drawing test, respectively, with a significant difference in the latter test only (P<.001)."
