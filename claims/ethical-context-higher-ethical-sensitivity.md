## Evidence

### Mary Jo Billiot 2012

Mary Jo Billiot, David Daniel, Sid Glandon, TerryAnn Glandon. (2012). Educational Context: Preparing Accounting Students To Identify Ethical Dilemmas. American Journal Of Business Education – May/June 2012 Volume 5, Number 3. https://clutejournals.com/index.php/AJBE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA with between-subjects factors on 172 accounting majors measured after each of four scenario phases; the article reports "significant differences by group (p = 0.011)" with lower agreement means indicating greater sensitivity.

> "The ethical context group ( mean = 4.06) was significantly more sensitive than the accounting context group (mean = 4.46, p = 0.077) and the comparison group (mean = 4.54, p = 0.004) over the four phases."
