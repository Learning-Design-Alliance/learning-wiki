## Evidence

### Chen 2024

Chen, Y., Iyer, P., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Reflection on Purpose Changes Students' Academic Interests: A Scalable Intervention in an Online Course Catalog. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression using the LIWC22 causation index in the randomized field experiment: odds were "2.70 times greater" for new users and 1.86 times greater for existing users in the self-interest condition; the self-transcendent condition showed odds ratios of 2.29 and 1.69. Coded i = 2 based on the printed odds ratios.

> "In the self-interest purpose condition, the odds were 2.70 times greater for new users to write their search queries in a causal way and the odds for existing users were 1.86 times greater (logOR = 0.99, z = 4.67, p < 0.01; logOR = 0.62, z = 2.78, p < 0.01)"
