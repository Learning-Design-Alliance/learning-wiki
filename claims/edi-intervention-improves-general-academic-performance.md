## Evidence

### Cantero María-José 2020

Cantero María-José, Bañuls Raquel, Viguer Paz. (2020). Effectiveness of an Emotional Intelligence Intervention and Its Impact on Academic Performance in Spanish Pre-Adolescent Elementary Students: Results from the EDI Program. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207621

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA on the full sample (n = 182) with group as between-subject and time (T1–T4) as within-subject factors. The article reports "a significant principal effect of the time-group interaction on overall performance" with ηp2 = 0.12.

> "Univariate analyses showed a significant principal effect of the time-group interaction on overall performance, F (3, 540) = 23.44, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.12, mathematics performance F (3, 540) = 6.26, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.03, and language performance, F (3, 540) = 15.92, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.08."

### Cantero María-José 2020 (2)

Cantero María-José, Bañuls Raquel, Viguer Paz. (2020). Effectiveness of an Emotional Intelligence Intervention and Its Impact on Academic Performance in Spanish Pre-Adolescent Elementary Students: Results from the EDI Program. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207621

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis of the intervention group's general academic performance (grades 0–10). The article reports "a significant increase in scores from T1 to T2" with d = 0.51 and from T1 to T4 with d = 0.49.

> "In the intervention group, there was a significant increase in scores from T1 to T2 (mean difference = 0.57, SE = 0.04, p < 0.001, d = 0.51, 95% CI [0.47, 0.67]) and from T1 to T4 (mean difference = 0.59, SE = 0.09, p < 0.001, d = 0.49, 95% CI [0.36, 0.82])."
