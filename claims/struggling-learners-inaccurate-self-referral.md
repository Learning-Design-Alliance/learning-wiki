`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The guidelines report, as evidence for intervening proactively rather than relying on learner initiative, that "only about 7% of struggling learners accurately self-referred to one guidance program", with most participants being high achievers with chronic performance anxiety.

### Chou Calvin L. 2019 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The guidelines report that academically weaker students and those suffering burnout tend to avoid seeking assistance, so programs must be willing to compel participation; "Students who accept remediation demonstrate longer-term improvement in test-taking than those who decline".

> "the program must have the capacity, support, and willingness to compel struggling learners into remediation [6]. Students who accept remediation demonstrate longer-term improvement in test-taking than those who decline [135]."
