Descriptive analysis of RAEQ scale means in Study 1 (350 German university students). The article reports that "Reappraisal and competence development wereconsistentlymorecommon" and that some strategies differed across emotions, e.g., social support seeking was more common for regulating enjoyment and boredom than anxiety.
