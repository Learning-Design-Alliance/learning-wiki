## Evidence

### Zwilling 2019

Zwilling, C. E., Daugherty, A. M., Hillman, C. H., Kramer, A. F., Cohen, N. J. & Barbey, A. K. (2019). Enhanced decision-making through multimodal training. npj Science of Learning 4:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0049-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the 16-week RCT (n = 160), ANCOVA models with active control as reference tested post-intervention A-DMC facets controlling for pre-intervention performance. The fitness group showed "belief assessment ( p = 0.0007, Cohen's d = 0.73" and "Information Integration (p = 0.012, Cohen 's d = 0.47". HICRT and value assessment was null (p > 0.05).

> "The uni-modal HICRT intervention improved belief assessment ( p = 0.0007, Cohen's d = 0.73,d f = (4,132)) and Information Integration (p = 0.012, Cohen 's d = 0.47,d f = (4,132))."
