## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Converting raw compression metrics into dichotomous quality indices substantially inflates measured variability, so large changes are needed to be confident of real change. [→ Almeida Debora 2020](#almeida-debora-2020)

## Evidence

### Almeida Debora 2020

Almeida Debora, Clark Carol, Jones Michael, McConnell Phillip, Williams Jonathan. (2020). Consistency and variability in human performance during simulate infant CPR: a reliability study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quality-index reliability analysis using the same 27-participant dataset; Table 4 reports between-day ICCs of .52 (rate QI), .81 (depth QI), .77 (leaning QI) and .68 (duty cycle QI), with MDC of 101% of the mean for rate QI and 96% for duty cycle QI, the study reporting "greater variability when measuring reliability of performance using QI".

> "The percentage MDC was moderate for compression depth QI (21%), but considerably higher for the other QIs, demonstrating greater variability when measuring reliability of performance using QI."
