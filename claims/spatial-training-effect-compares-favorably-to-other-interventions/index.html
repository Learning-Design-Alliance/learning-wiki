Katie A. Gilligan-Lee 2022¶

Katie A. Gilligan-Lee, Zachary C. K. Hawes and Kelly S. Mix. (2022). Spatial thinking as the missing piece in mathematics curricula. npj Science of Learning 7:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00128-9

The commentary contextualizes the meta-analytic effect of g = 0.28 against benchmark effect sizes, stating it "compares favorably to the average effects" of randomized (0.16), non-randomized (0.23), and mathematics-specific (0.06) educational interventions.

"with an overall effect size of 0.28 (Hedges 's g) for spatial training on mathematics compared to controls 9. This finding demonstrates that spatial training improves not only spatial but also mathematics outcomes, with an effect size that compares favorably to the average effects of other randomized (0.16) and non-randomized (0.23) educational interventions 10, and mathematics-interventions more specifically (0.06) 11."
