# Tutors found explaining difficult EBM concepts and practical skills via the online platform challenging and saw a need for more advanced technology

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Tutors reported that explaining critical appraisal and searching techniques through emails and online discussions, without diagrams, demonstrations or immediate responses, was the main challenge of the fully online format. [→ Rohwer Anke 2013](#rohwer-anke-2013)
`q2 i?` Tutors across the board felt there was room to improve the online environment with new technologies such as chat rooms, blogs, video clips, screencasts and social media. [→ Rohwer Anke 2013 (2)](#rohwer-anke-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Rohwer Anke 2013

Rohwer Anke, Young Taryn, van Schalkwyk Susan. (2013). Effective or just practical? An evaluation of an online postgraduate module on evidence-based medicine (EBM). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-77

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Individual semi-structured interviews with module tutors, recorded, transcribed and manually coded for themes on benefits, barriers and challenges. Tutors judged some critical appraisal aspects "sometimes better explained in a contact session" than online.

> "The main challenge that emerged for the tutors was explaining difficult concepts like critical appraisal and practical skills like searching techniques via emails and online discussions, without the ability of drawing diagrams, demonstrations and immediate responses to questions."

### Rohwer Anke 2013 (2)

Rohwer Anke, Young Taryn, van Schalkwyk Susan. (2013). Effective or just practical? An evaluation of an online postgraduate module on evidence-based medicine (EBM). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-77

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same tutor interview study; tutors named chat rooms, blogs, video- and multimedia clips, screencasts, RSS feeds and social media accounts as tools that could better connect them with students.

> "Indeed, tutors across the board felt that there was room to improve the online environment with new technologies that could support learning."
