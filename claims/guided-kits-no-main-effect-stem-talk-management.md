## Evidence

### Karen L. Bierman 2025

Karen L. Bierman, Lynn S. Liben, Meg Small, Jennifer Connell, Brenda Heinrichs, Jessica Menold, Scarlett Rae Miller, Morgan Mannweiler. (2025). Guided activity kits impact parents’ scaffolding of child STEM play. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102082

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized intervention in which 75 parent-child dyads (children Mage = 4.82 years) received five activity kits over 10 weeks, assigned to guided (n = 50) or basic (n = 25) kits. The article reports that "No main effect of intervention appeared on parent STEM talk or directive management." No effect size is printed for this null finding.
