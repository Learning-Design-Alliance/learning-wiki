## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2013

Ilic Dragan, Hart William, Fiddes Patrick, Misso Marie, Villanueva Elmer. (2013). Adopting a blended learning approach to teaching evidence based medicine: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled trial course assessment data: BL (n = 36) versus DID (n = 35) on criterion-based tasks graded out of 100% by EBM tutors using a rubric. Task 2 means were 97.43 (BL) versus 96.00 (DID), p = 0.01; Task 1 showed "no significant difference" (p = 0.14). No effect size printed.

> "students allocated to the BL group scored a significantly higher grade on the second course assessment task when compared to the DID (p = 0.01)"
