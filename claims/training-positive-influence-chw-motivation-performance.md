`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a Pakistani 8-day TBA training where intervention-arm TBAs "showed better performance than untrained TBAs", while in India trained TBAs did not follow lifesaving practices despite training. No effect sizes are printed.

> "An 8-day training course for TBAs in Pakistan found that TBAs in the intervention arm showed better performance than untrained TBAs (Miller et al. 2012). Saravanan et al. (2011) found that despite training of TBAs, lifesaving practices for mothers and babies were not followed in India."
