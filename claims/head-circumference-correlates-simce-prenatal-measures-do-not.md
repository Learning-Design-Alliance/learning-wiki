## Evidence

### Ivanovic Daniza M. et al 2019

Ivanovic Daniza M. et al. (2019). A multifactorial approach of nutritional, intellectual, brain development, cardiovascular risk, socio-economic, demographic and educational variables affecting the scholastic achievement in Chilean students: An eight- year follow-up study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0212279

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis (Table 1) in the 2010 5ESG and 1HSG samples; head circumference-for-age Z-score correlations reached r = 0.330 with mathematics in 1HSG (p = .0001), higher for mathematics than language.

> "Postnatal nutritional background markers height-for-age Z-score and head circumference-for-age Z-score, correlated positively and significantly with the 2009 SIMCE outcomes in both grades and tests"

### Ivanovic Daniza M. et al 2019 (2)

Ivanovic Daniza M. et al. (2019). A multifactorial approach of nutritional, intellectual, brain development, cardiovascular risk, socio-economic, demographic and educational variables affecting the scholastic achievement in Chilean students: An eight- year follow-up study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0212279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlation analysis (Table 1); the breastfeeding correlation was negative, which the authors explain by longer breastfeeding duration tending to occur in lower-SES families whose achievement is lower.

> "Prenatal nutritional background and early anthropometric measurements such as birth weight and birth length did not correlate with 2009 SIMCE outcomes; the exception was the duration of breastfeeding in MSA of 2010 5ESG (p = .0364)"
