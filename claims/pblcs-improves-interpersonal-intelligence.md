## Evidence

### Talib and Bini Kailani 2014

Ahmad Talib, Ismail Bin Kailani. (2014). Problem Based Learning in Cooperative Situation (PBLCS) and Its Impact on Development of Personal Intelligence. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 3(4). http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 103 PBLCS and 103 traditional-class students; one-way MANOVA univariate test for KIER gave F = 198.686, sig. 0.000 (Table 6). Descriptive means were 9.10 (treated) versus 0.61 (controlled), so "the mean score development of KIER students at treated class is higher".

> "The development level of students' interpersonal intelligence, using problem-based learning in a cooperative situation model was better than students using traditional learning strategies, with the significance 0.000 <α = 0.05."
