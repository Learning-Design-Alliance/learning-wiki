## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post self-report on a 0–10 Likert scale in the randomized teaching intervention; Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis showed ratings were "significantly lower after the teaching intervention" (6.42 ± 1.74 vs. 4.87 ± 1.17, p = < 0.001). No effect size was printed.

> "Learners rated the perceived difficulty of laparoscopy significantly lower after the teaching intervention on a 0–10 Likert scale (6.42 ± 1.74 vs. 4.87 ± 1.17, p =  < 0.001)."

### Kölbel Benny 2024 (2)

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post career-aspiration ratings on a 0–10 Likert scale in the same intervention rose from 4.55 ± 2.72 to 4.87 ± 2.90, a statistically significant change (p = 0.02) with no printed effect size. The authors call the improvement slight.

> "The participants rated the likelihood of pursuing a career in general surgery significantly more after the teaching intervention on a 0–10 Likert scale (4.55 ± 2.72 vs. 4.87 ± 2.90, p = 0.02)."
