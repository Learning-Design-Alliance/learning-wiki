Evidence¶

King 1930¶

King, Irving. (1930). Measurements of the Physical Growth of Two Children. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · associational · r1

This is a theoretical argument from the article's opening discussion, not a tested result. The author reasons that growth inferences from one-time group measurements may be misleading because "the great individual differences" within an age group may mask true growth curves. The article offers no data for this in the argument itself.

"It is possible that all the supposed facts thus brought to light, except those of the most general character, may be quite spurious, on accouut of the great individual differences which may exist in the subjects."
