# No significant improvement was observed for voltage problems on parallel or mixed circuits, and students had difficulty differentiating electric potential from voltage

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students had difficulties solving similar voltage problems for parallel or mixed circuits correctly, and the authors do not observe a significant improvement following the teaching intervention for these circuit types. [→ Bodensiek 2018](#bodensiek-2018)

## Evidence

### Bodensiek 2018

Bodensiek, O., Sonntag, D., Glawe, I., & Müller, R. (2018). 3D-Printable Height Models for DC Circuits. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1805.02522](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1805.02522)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null result from the same exploratory pre-post case study (N=59): the article reports that for parallel or mixed circuits "we do not observe a significant improvement following the teaching intervention"; no effect size is printed.

> "Contrary to these promising results, students had difficulties to solve similar problems for parallel or mixed circuits correctly. In these cases we do not observe a significant improvement following the teaching intervention."

### Bodensiek 2018 (2)

Bodensiek, O., Sonntag, D., Glawe, I., & Müller, R. (2018). 3D-Printable Height Models for DC Circuits. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1805.02522](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1805.02522)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`
