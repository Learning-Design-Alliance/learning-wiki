## Evidence

### Koc Akran 2018

Koc Akran, S., & Uzum, B. (2018). The effect of the layered curriculum on the 6th grade students' learning styles in Science lesson. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(3), 141-152. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.3.141

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive qualitative analysis of diaries and semi-structured interviews with experimental-group students; the article reports students "mostly chose activities that they thought would reinforce the topic" and that layered curriculum applications "motivate students towards lessons and raise more awareness of responsibility". Self-reported data only; no effect sizes.
