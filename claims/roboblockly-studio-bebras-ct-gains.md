## Evidence

### Leyi Li 2026

Leyi Li, Chenyu Du, Jiafei Sun, Erick Purwanto, and Qing Zhang. (2026). RoboBlockly Studio: Conversational Block Programming with Embodied Robot Feedback for Computational Thinking. Designing Interactive Systems Conference (DIS '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3800645.3813071

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the counterbalanced within-subject study (N=32 high school students, no prior programming experience), a 15-minute four-problem Bebras pre-test and matched post-test showed a significant gain: "1.28 out of 4 tasks correctly in the pre-test" versus 2.88 in the post-test, t(31)=9.53, p<.001, dz = 1.69; Wilcoxon confirmed (W = 430, p < .001).

> "On average, participants solved 1.28 out of 4 tasks correctly in the pre-test (SD = 1.02), compared to 2.88 in the post-test (SD = 0.83). A paired-sample t-test confirmed that this difference was statistically significant, 𝑡( 31)= 9.53, 𝑝<. 001, with a large within-subject effect size (𝑑𝑧 = 1.69)."
