## Evidence

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional component analyses (Figure 3) regressing cognitive outcomes on quartiles of each of the nine aMED components separately, with Bonferroni-corrected significance. The article reports "significant protective associations were observed for vegetable intake in both AREDS and AREDS2"; no effect sizes are printed for these components.

> "In addition, significant protective associations were observed for vegetable intake in both AREDS and AREDS2, and for nut intake and moderate alcohol intake in AREDS2."
