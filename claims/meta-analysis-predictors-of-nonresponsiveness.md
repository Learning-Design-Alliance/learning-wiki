The article reports, citing Nelson, Benner, and Gonzalez (2003), a meta-analysis extending the authors' earlier review with seven additional studies of nonresponders. The review attributes the printed Zr values for seven learner characteristics to that meta-analysis.

> "Second, they used meta-analytic tech- niques to explore the relative im- portance of individual learner charac- teristics to treatment effectiveness. Noteworthy predictors were rapid naming (Zr = 0.51), problem behavior (Zr= 0.46), phonological awareness (Zr = 0.42), alphabetic understanding (Zr = 0.35), memory (Zr = 0.31), IQ (Zr = 0.26), and demographics (Zr = 0.07)."
