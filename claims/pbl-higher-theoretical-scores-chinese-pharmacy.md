## Evidence

### Zhou Jiyin 2016

Zhou Jiyin, Zhou Shiwen, Huang Chunji, Xu Rufu, Zhang Zuo, Zeng Shengya, Qian Guisheng. (2016). Effectiveness of problem-based learning in Chinese pharmacy education: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0546-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 16 randomized controlled trials (17 outcome reports) in Chinese pharmacy education, pooled with a random-effects model in RevMan 5.3. The pooled effect "showed a significant difference in theoretical scores" favoring PBL over traditional lectures, with SMD = 1.17 and 95% CI [0.77, 1.57].

> "The pooled effect size showed a significant difference in theoretical scores (SMD = 1.17, 95 % CI [0.77, 1.57], P < 0.00001) in favor of PBL, compared with traditional lectures (Fig. 2)."
