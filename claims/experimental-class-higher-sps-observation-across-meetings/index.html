Evidence¶

Sahhyar & Nst 2017¶

Sahhyar & Nst, F. H. (2017). The Effect of Scientific Inquiry Learning Model Based on Conceptual Change on Physics Cognitive Competence and Science Process Skill (SPS) of Students at Senior High School. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5), 120-126. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133077

q3 · i? · causal · r2

SPS was observed at each of three meetings. Experimental class: Meeting I 40% (competent), Meeting II 63% (good), Meeting III 85% (excellent), average 63% (good). Control class: Meeting I 34% (fail), Meeting II 48% (competent), Meeting III 69% (good), average 50% (competent). The study reports "the average percentage of SPS assessment was higher in experimental class with good category than in control class with competent category."

"Table 11 showed the average percentage of SPS assessment was higher in experimental class with good category than in control class with competent category."
