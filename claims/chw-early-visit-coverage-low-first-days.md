### Tomlinson M 2025

Tomlinson M, Rotheram-Borus MJ, Stansert Katzen L, Gertsch W, le Roux I, Dippenaar E, le Roux K. (2025). Relationship of perinatal outcomes to the competence and quantity of contact with community health workers. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.15.04094

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive visit-timing analysis among intervention-group mothers, with counts and percentages printed for each window. The authors attribute the low early coverage to travel distance and state "there is still significant room for improvement".

> "Among new mothers, 32 (7%) were visited in the first two days of returning from the hospital, 117 (26%) were visited in the first week, 257 (57%) were visited within their first month, 360 (80%) were visited within the first three months, and 405 (90%) were visited in the first six months of life."
