## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of MAT validity for creativity and potential ratings (Table 3); creativity ratings were made by faculty or work supervisors, with 60% of studies evaluating student creativity. Printed correlation effect sizes: ρ = .36 for creativity, ρ = .37 and ρ = .49 for potential ratings, all with SDρ = 0.

> "The true score correlation for predicting these ratings was a moderate .36 (k ⫽ 6, N ⫽ 1,104, SDρ ⫽ 0). The MAT is a strong predictor of the counseling potential ratings subsample (ρ ⫽ .49, k ⫽ 6, N ⫽ 192) and a moderate predictor for the overall potential ratings (ρ ⫽ .37, k ⫽ 11, N ⫽ 494) analysis."
