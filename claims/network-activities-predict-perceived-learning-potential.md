Multilevel regression analyses of ego-network data from 1227 participants (6135 contacts) tested whether network activities predicted perceived learning potential.

> "Multilevel regression analyses were conducted respectively to measure the impact of individual and network-characteristics on network activities and to see whether network activities predicted perceived learning potential."
