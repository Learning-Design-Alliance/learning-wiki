The review reports participants' opinion data collected in two included studies (Larkins et al. 2022; Ghazi et al. 2023): "Between the Hugo RAS and the dV-trainer, 50% reported that the skills were ‘absolutely transferable’". Ghazi participants gave the SP platform higher SURG-TLX and difficulty scores.
