## Evidence

### Pagerols M 2022

Pagerols M, Prat R, Rivas C, Español-Martín G, Puigbó J, Pagespetit È, Haro JM, Ramos-Quiroga JA, Casas M, Bosch R. (2022). The impact of psychopathology on academic performance in school-age children and adolescents. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-08242-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixed-effects ordinal logistic regression across Tables 2–4: model 0 crude analyses showed higher scores on all CBCL syndrome scales significantly associated with lower grades (p < 0.001); after mutual adjustment, "somatic complaints, social problems, and aggressive behavior were no longer related to educational outcomes".

> "when we additionally included all the CBCL scales in the model to account for comorbid psychopathology, somatic complaints, social problems, and aggressive behavior were no longer related to educational outcomes"
