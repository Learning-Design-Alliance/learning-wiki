## Evidence

### Holt Emily A. 2015

Holt Emily A., Young Craig, Keetch Jared, Larsen Skylar, Mollner Brayden. (2015). The Greatest Learning Return on Your Pedagogical Investment: Alignment, Assessment or In-Class Instruction? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0137446

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across the 15 observed sections. RTOP scores correlated with assessment Bloom scores (Pearson r = 0.63) and with per-section student participation (Pearson r = 0.64); both correlations are printed but their significance is not reported.

> "RTOP scores were linearly correlated with assessment Bloom scores (Pearson r = 0.63) and the number of students in each section that participated in our study (Pearson r = 0.64)."
