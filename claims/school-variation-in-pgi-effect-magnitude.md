# Between-school variation in EA-PGI effects is substantial: the average EA-PGI effect is 0.22 with slope SD 0.034, and variance explained by EA-PGI ranges from ~2% in the weakest-effect schools to ~8% in the strongest

> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2022 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The mean within-family EA-PGI effect on achievement is 0.22 with between-school slope SD 0.034; in the weakest 2.5% of schools the effect is <15% of an SD, in the strongest 2.5% it is >29% of an SD (~8% versus ~2% variance explained). [→ Rosa Cheesman 2022](#rosa-cheesman-2022)

## Evidence

### Rosa Cheesman 2022

Rosa Cheesman, Nicolai T. Borgen, Torkild H. Lyngstad, Espen M. Eilertsen, Ziada Ayorech, Fartein A. Torvik, Ole A. Andreassen, Henrik D. Zachrisson and Eivind Ystrom. (2022). A population-wide gene-environment interaction study on how genes, schools, and residential areas shape achievement. npj Science of Learning 7:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00145-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 3a results visualised in Fig. 2 show school-specific EA-PGI slopes with "standard deviation of slopes = 0.034"; the weakest 2.5% of schools show effects below 15% of an SD and the strongest above 29% (roughly 8% versus 2% of achievement variance).

> "the mean effect of the EA-PGI on achievement is 0.22 but there is variation around this average slope between schools (standard deviation of slopes = 0.034). In the 2.5% of schools with the weakest effects (red lines), the effect of students' EA-PGI is <15% of an SD"
