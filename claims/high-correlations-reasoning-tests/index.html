W. H. Winch 1911¶

W. H. Winch. (1911). Mental Adaptation During the School Day as Measured by Arithmetical Reasoning.—Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mental+Adaptation+During+the+School+Day+as+Measured+by+Arithmetical+Reasoning

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Third series (infants): product-moment correlations computed from individual marks on the orally administered problem sets, marked one mark per rational step. The article also prints test-retest coefficients of +.78 and +.78 (preliminaries) and +.84 and +.79 (finals). These are correlations, not standardised effect sizes for a comparison.

"The total marks of the Group A series correlated with those of the Group B series to the extent of + .99 in the prelimina-ries and to the extent of + .88 in the finals."
