## Evidence

### Gaspard 2021

Gaspard, H., Parrisius, C., Piesch, H., Kleinhansl, M., Wille, E., Nagengast, B., Trautwein, U., & Hulleman, C. S. (2021). The potential of relevance interventions for scaling up: A cluster-randomized trial testing the effectiveness of a relevance intervention in math classrooms. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000663

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r3`

The article reports, citing its own first cluster-randomized trial with 82 ninth-grade classrooms in which five doctoral candidates delivered the intervention, that "stronger effects in the quotations condition (d = 0.30 at posttest, d = 0.26 at follow-up)" were found compared with the text condition.

> "both intervention conditions showed positive effects on students’ utility value six weeks and five months after the intervention, with stronger effects in the quotations condition (d = 0.30 at posttest, d = 0.26 at follow-up) compared with the text condition (d = 0.14 at posttest, d = 0.16 at follow-up)."
