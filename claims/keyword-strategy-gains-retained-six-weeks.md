### Aracı 2023

Aracı, N., Melekoğlu, M. A., & Çetin, M. E. (2023). Impact of a Mnemonic Strategy on Learning Science Concepts for Middle School Students With Specific Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 21(1), 69-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1386167

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way repeated-measures ANOVA on the experimental group's (n = 13) pretest, posttest, and six-week follow-up scores. Posttest (x̄ = 7.61) and follow-up (x̄ = 7.00) means did not differ significantly, indicating retention of gains.

> "Table 5 shows a significant difference between the pretest–posttest and pretest–follow-up test measurements (F (2,24) = 108.576, p < .05). No significant difference was found between the posttest and the follow-up test."
