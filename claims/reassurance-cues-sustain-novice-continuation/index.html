q2 · i? · review · r2

Qualitative synthesis of reassurance excerpts in the review's results. In Tambaum (2017), pumping made up 42 of 208 observed scaffolding behaviours; echoing and agreeing question-answers let novices retain independence while course-correcting without confrontational negative feedback.

"Providing reassurance is a key component of scaffolding, as novices completing a task by themselves are often faced with the dilemma ‘have I chosen the right action?’."
