## Evidence

### Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael 2026

Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael, Eilers Jantje, Herrmann Hans Joachim, Neurath Markus Friedrich, Kohl Matthias, Zopf Yurdagül, Burgard Leonie Cordelia. (2026). In-Person vs. Virtual: A Comparative Study of Teaching Methods in Nutritional Medicine. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18050821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire data from the randomized trial (OLM n = 39, IPL n = 38) on 0–100% agreement scales. Table 2 shows exam-preparation adequacy means of 0.79 (OLM) vs. 0.45 (IPL) and 82.1% vs. 48.6% openness to repeating the format; the authors call the questionnaire data descriptive and hypothesis-generating.

> "A pronounced difference in means was observed for the adequacy of exam preparation, with higher ratings in the OLM group compared to the IPL group. Consistent with this, a greater proportion of students in the OLM group indicated openness to taking the same format again for exam preparation."
