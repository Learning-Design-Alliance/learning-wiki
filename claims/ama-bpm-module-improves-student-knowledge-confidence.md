## Evidence

### Patel Mandeep K. 2026

Patel Mandeep K., Timko‐Swaim Lynn, Conklin Perrilynn, Lu Wei‐Hsin. (2026). Curriculum Integration of an Interactive Blood Pressure Measurement Module to Improve Healthcare Students' Knowledge, Confidence, and Skills. The Journal of Clinical Hypertension. https://doi.org/10.1111/jch.70234

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-method pre–post study of 136 first-year medical and 70 first-year PA students at Stony Brook University; pre/post surveys and OSCEs measured change. The abstract reports statistically significant improvements in knowledge and confidence, with greater post-training "confidence in their technique and comfort in performing BPM on patients".

> "Both medical and PA student cohorts demonstrated statistically significant improvements in BPM knowledge and self‐reported confidence. Post‐training, students reported greater confidence in their technique and comfort in performing BPM on patients."
