Evidence¶

Danni Chen 2024¶

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Item-level BLMM on overnight evaluation change: the delta-theta by feedback interaction was significant (higher vs. lower, mediandiff = 0.05, 95% HDI [0.01, 0.08]); cue-elicited delta-theta power "predicted the post-TMR over-night evaluation updating for cued snacks," significantly in the higher (median = 0.04) but not lower condition. The uncued subject-level model showed no prediction.

"cue-elicited delta-theta power predicted the post-TMR over-night evaluation updating for cued snacks as a function of feedback"
