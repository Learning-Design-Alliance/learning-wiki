## Evidence

### Schmalstieg 2025

Schmalstieg, F., Schmellenkamp, M., Schwerter, J., & Zeume, T. (2025). Difficulty Generating Factors for Context-free Language Construction Assignments. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3702652.3744201](https://doi.org/10.1145/3702652.3744201)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fixed-effects panel regression on CFG tasks from Task groups 4–6. Multiplicities increased number of attempts (coefficient 2.78, SE 0.56, p<0.001), time spent (5.39 min, SE 1.02, p<0.001), reduced first-attempt success (−0.09, SE 0.05, p<0.05), and increased perceived interpretation difficulty (0.20 ×SD, SE 0.08, p<0.05).

> "Table 4 shows that multiplicities substantially impaired perfor- mance and increased both interpretation and construction difficulty These effects were robust in Groups 4 and 5 but absent in Group 6"
