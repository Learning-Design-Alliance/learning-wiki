Janit 2011¶

Janit, Adrian; Hammock, Georgina; and Richardson, Deborah. (2011). The Power of Fiction: Reading Stories in Abnormal Psychology. International Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2011.050116

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on Narrative Questionnaire scores across the four Experiment 2 conditions showed "transportation scores varied as a f unction of t ext genre, F (3, 82) = 5. 40, p =.002, η2 = .17". Means were 60 (textbook-plus-story), 54.71 (story), 50.83 (typed), and 51.96 (photocopied).

"A one- way ANOVA conducted on NQ total scores revealed that transportation scores varied as a f unction of t ext genre, F (3, 82) = 5. 40, p =.002, η2 = .17."
