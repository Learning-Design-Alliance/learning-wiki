Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study of 33 children with hearing impairment in two schools for the Deaf in Enugu State, all exposed to auditory training. The article reports that "all the c hildren with hearing impairment exposed to reading comprehension through the use of auditory training achieved a measure of reading."
