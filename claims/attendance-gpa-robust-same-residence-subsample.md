## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robustness analysis on sixth-grade students who progressed from a district K-5 school to a 6-8 school while residing at the same address, treating new middle-school distance as exogenous variation. No effect size is printed for this subsample.

> "That is, the value-added models have reduced the size of the coefficients from the baseline regressions, and yet, with the introduction of an instrumental variables design, the coefficients of days present remain positive and significant and are larger than under ordinary least squares regressions."
