### Carenzo L 2021

Carenzo L, Cena T, Carfagna F, Rondi V, Ingrassia PL, Cecconi M, Violato C, Della Corte F, Vaschetto R. (2021). Assessing anaesthesiology and intensive care specialty physicians: An Italian language multisource feedback system. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250404

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level response analysis of the 543 coworker questionnaires, using the article's 15% cut-off for flagging items needing reassessment or cancellation. "Ability to take a leadership role when circumstances required (22.06%)" was the most difficult item.

> "Three items in the non-medical coworker questionnaires resulted in >15% of unable to assess answers. The three items were: ability in delivering effective handovers (15.14%), ability in providing honest and constructive feedback (15.33%) and ability to take a leadership role when circumstances required (22.06%)."
