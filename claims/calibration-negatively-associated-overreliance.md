## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among AI-assisted participants, awareness calibration was negatively associated with acceptance of incorrect AI advice and with attribution bias; the authors do not interpret this causally. [→ Ren S 2026](#ren-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Ren S 2026

Ren S (2026) College students’ metacognitive awareness of generative-AI reliance: an experimental study of decision confidence and attribution bias. Front. Psychol. 17:1926110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1926110

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis (Table 4) among participants in the two AI-assisted conditions. The article reports that "Awareness calibration was negatively associated with acceptance of incorrect AI advice and attribution bias"; because variables were measured within the same decision episode, the correlations are not interpreted causally. No standardized coefficients appear in the quoted text.
