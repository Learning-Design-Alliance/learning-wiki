## Evidence

### Anusitviwat C 2025

Anusitviwat C, Suwannaphisit S, Bvonpanttarananon J, Tangtrakulwanich B. (2025). Comparing ChatGPT and DeepSeek for Assessment of Multiple-Choice Questions in Orthopedic Medical Education: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/75607

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of 209 orthopedic MCQs from 2023-2024 summative assessments, each entered into GPT-4o and DeepSeek-V3. The article reports "significantly higher total accuracy of 80.4% (168/209) compared with 74.2% (155/209)" with χ2 testing at P=.04; no effect size was printed.

> "Overall, ChatGPT achieved a significantly higher total accuracy of 80.4% (168/209) compared with 74.2% (155/209) for DeepSeek (P=.04)."
