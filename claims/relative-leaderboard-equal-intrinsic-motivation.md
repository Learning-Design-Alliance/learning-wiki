# On a relative leaderboard, intrinsic motivation is equal across top, middle, and bottom ranked students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Top-, middle-, and bottom-ranked learners on a relative leaderboard demonstrated an equal level of intrinsic motivation at all three measurement stages, supporting H1b. [→ Bai 2021](#bai-2021)

## Evidence

### Bai 2021

Bai, Shurui, Khe Foon Hew, Michael Sailer, and Chengyuan Jia. 2021. "From top to bottom: how positions on different types of leaderboard may affect fully online student learning performance, intrinsic motivation, and course engagement." Computers & Education 173: 104297. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104297.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 26 postgraduate students in a fully online course using a relative leaderboard showing five neighbours; IMI interest/enjoyment measured at pre-intervention, mid-term, and post-intervention. No significant variations were found among the three position levels at any stage.

> "we found no significant variations among the students at the three position levels throughout the intervention (pre-intervention: H = 2.86, p = .24; mid-term: F = 0.81, p = .46; post-intervention: F = 0.53, p = .59."
