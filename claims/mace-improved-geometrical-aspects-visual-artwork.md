## Evidence

### Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019

Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen. (2019). Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of pre- and posttest Visual Arts Assignment scores of students in three conditions (MACE lessons + PD, MACE lessons only, regular textbook geometry). Class-level differences in improvement on the VAA aspects were explained by the MACE lesson series: "space, t(94) = 3.66, p < 0.01; space suggestion, t(94) = 2.14, p < 0.05; composition, t(94) = 4.88, p < 0.001".

> "These differences between classes could be explained by the MACE lesson series (space, t(94) = 3.66, p < 0.01; space suggestion, t(94) = 2.14, p < 0.05; composition, t(94) = 4.88, p < 0.001)."
