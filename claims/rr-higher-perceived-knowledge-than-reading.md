## Evidence

### Robin Schmucker 2024

Robin Schmucker, Meng Xia, Amos Azaria, and Tom Mitchell. (2024). Ruffle&Riley: Insights from Designing and Evaluating a Large Language Model-Based Conversational Tutoring System. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Evaluation 2 learning-experience survey (7-point Likert, collected after post-test) compared R&R and reading via one-sided t-tests. The study reports significantly higher perceived knowledge for R&R; ratings for engagement, remembering, and task difficulty favored R&R but were not significant, and no effect sizes were printed.

> "the one-sided t-test unveiled that R&R users rated their pre- and post-activity knowledge (i.e., perceived knowledge) significantly higher than participants in the reading conditions."
