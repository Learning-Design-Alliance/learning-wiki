## Evidence

### Robinson 2021

Robinson, D. H. (2021). A Complete SMOCkery: Daily Online Testing Did Not Boost College Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09588-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Examination of the differential-attrition threat comparing drop counts across the 2008 and 2011 course offerings. The author reasons that if many dropouts were lower middle class, "any analysis would show a reduction in the performance differences between the low and high middle-class students".

> "in 2008 when the psychology course was less rigorous with no daily quizzes, only 32 students dropped the course. Comparatively, in 2011 when the rigor was increased, almost twice as many students (58) dropped. Students from lower SES families unfortunately tend to drop courses at higher rates than do their richer counterparts."
