## Evidence

### Lowrie 2010

Lowrie, Tom and Diezmann, Carmel. (2010). Solving graphics tasks: Gender differences in middle-school students. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2009.11.005

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus repeated measures MANCOVA on pooled GLIM decoding scores with gender as between-subjects factor, the six graphical languages as within-subjects factor, and Raven's test as covariate. The analysis "revealed statistically significant differences between the performance of boys and girls across the six graphical languages" (partial η2 = .15) and a strong covariate effect (partial η2 = .50).

> "A repeated measures multivariate analysis of covariance with gender as be tween subjects factor , the six graphical languages as within subjects f actor and score on Raven’s test as co variate revealed statistically significant differences between the performance of boys and girls across the six graphical languages, F (6, 309) = 9.37, p  < .001, partial η2 = .15, with a significant effect for the covariate, F (6, 309) = 50.47, p  < .001, partial η2 = .50."
