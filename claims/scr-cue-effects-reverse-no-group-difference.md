## Evidence

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory physiological analysis of heat-evoked skin conductance during medium heat trials; the article reports effects were significant in frequentist but not Bayesian models and that only 13 Instructed Group participants had usable SCR variation, so results are taken with caution.

> "Heat-evoked SCRs were influenced by predictive cues on medium trials and reversed as contingencies changed, but the magnitude of these differences did not differ by Group"
