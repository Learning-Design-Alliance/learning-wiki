## Evidence

### Castillo-Gualda 2023

Castillo-Gualda, R., Moraleda, A., & Brackett, M. A. (2023). Preventive Initiatives to Promote Psychological Adjustment Among Primary Students: Findings of RULER Approach in Spanish Public Schools. International Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2), pp. 206-232. https://doi.org/10.17583/ijep.10970

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA comparing 207 students from one RULER-trained experimental school (n = 131) and one waiting-list control school (n = 76). The article reports "lower values in the students whose teachers attended the SEL training". No pretest was used.

> "statistically significant differences were found (small effect size, ηp2 < .03) in several dimensions: Clinical maladjustment (F = 5.80, p = .017), Emotional symptoms index (F = 5.78, p = .017), and Clinical scale (F = 4.98, p = .027)"
