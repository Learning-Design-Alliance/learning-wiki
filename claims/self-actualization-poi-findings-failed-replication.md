## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Foulds reported significant correlations between POI subscales and rated performance on empathy, genuineness, and a composite score, but replication attempts did not confirm these results. [→ Rowe 1975](#rowe-1975)

## Evidence

### Rowe 1975

Rowe, Wayne; Murphy, Harry B.; De Csipkes, Robert A. (1975). The Relationship of Counselor Characteristics and Counseling Effectiveness. Review of Educational Research, 45(2). https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543045002231

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Foulds's POI studies with 30 practicum students rated by trained judges, and notes the claimed personality correlates "could not, however, be confirmed"; a replication correlational matrix yielded one significant figure attributed to chance.

> "It was concluded that particular personality correlates of the ability to perform effectively in counseling had been identified. These results could not, however, be confirmed (Rowe & Winborn, 1973)."
