## Evidence

### Surman Craig B. H. 2013

Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4009378/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc analysis of the same controlled family-study sample defined extreme DESR as exceeding the 95th percentile of control DESR scores. The study reports "Scores of 9 and above exceeded this level, which occurred in 54.9 % of subjects with ADHD and 3.3 % of subjects without ADHD."

> "We defined an extreme DESR total score as the score that exceeded the 95th percentile of DESR scores among controls. Scores of 9 and above exceeded this level, which occurred in 54.9 % of subjects with ADHD and 3.3 % of subjects without ADHD."
