# On a control-aligned portal test taken immediately after instruction, GPA-based groups showed no significant difference from the control group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference between the experimental groups and the control group on the EPA post-instruction test. [→ Celal Boyraz 2017](#celal-boyraz-2017)

## Evidence

### Celal Boyraz 2017

Celal Boyraz, Gökhan Serin. (2017). Science Instruction through the Game and Physical Activities Course: An Interdisciplinary Teaching Practice. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051119

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the EPA test, built from the control group's web portal end-of-unit tests to guard against researcher bias, administered to all groups after instruction; no significant group difference was found and no effect size was printed. Equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no statistically significant difference between the experimental groups that received science instruction integrated with the GPA course, and the control group, F(3,78)=1.828, p>.05."
