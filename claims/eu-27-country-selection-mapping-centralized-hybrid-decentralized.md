## Evidence

### Hagelsteen K 2021

Hagelsteen K, Pedersen H, Bergenfelz A, Mathieu C. (2021). Different approaches to selection of surgical trainees in the European Union. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02779-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Documentary mapping of selection processes across 27 EU countries using grey literature, PubMed searches, and correspondence with UEMS Section of Surgery delegates (seven delegates responded). The article reports "Twelve countries have decentralized selection processes" and 14 centralized, with Slovakia excluded for lack of English information.

> "Twelve countries have decentralized selection processes with recruitment, evaluation and selection decision-making by local universities and medical schools or hospitals. Two countries have hybrid systems and 14 countries have national centralized systems."
