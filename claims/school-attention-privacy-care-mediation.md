## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Vanderhoven 2015

Vanderhoven, E., Schellens, T., Vanderlinde, R., & Valcke, M. (2015). Developing educational materials about risks on social network sites: A design-based research approach. Ghent University. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9415-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey of 638 randomly selected pupils aged 14-19 measuring privacy care, an unsafe behavior-index, and school attention, analysed with regression and bootstrap mediation analysis (SPSS).

> "results also showed that school education has a positive influence on privacy care, and that, by raising privacy care, it also has an indirect positive influence on the safety of pupils’ behavior (confirmed by a bootstrapping method"
