Interpretation of the blocking-factor analysis: Calc 1 could have the largest blocking factor or block nothing at all depending on the school. The article cites ACM curriculum recommendations that Pre-Calculus be the prerequisite for Discrete Structures and there be no math prerequisite for CS 1.

"Our data supports the stance that Calculus 1 need not be a prerequisite for lower-level CS courses."
