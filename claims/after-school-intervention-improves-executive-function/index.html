Evidence¶

Brock et al. 2018¶

Laura L. Brock, William M. Murrah, Elizabeth A. Cottone, Andrew J. Mashburn, and David W. Grissmer. (2018). An after-school intervention targeting executive function and visuospatial skills also improves classroom behavior. International Journal of Behavioral Development, 42(5), 474–484. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025417738057

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Intent-to-treat regression analyses of the randomized trial (N = 87) controlling for school, age, sex, grade, and pre-test scores found the intervention improved EF, with the article reporting "b ¼ .28, p < .001". No standardized effect size is printed for this coefficient.

"As hypothesized, the intervention improves directly assessed EF ( b ¼ .28, p < .001) and VS ( b ¼ .26, p < .001) skills."
