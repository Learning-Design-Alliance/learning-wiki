# Students reported significantly higher perception of instructor Caring on the MUSIC Inventory in Fall 2025 after DURA course updates

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a comparison of MUSIC survey results between Fall 2024 and Fall 2025, students in the DURA-updated semester reported significantly higher perception of instructor Caring (p < .001), while other MUSIC scales were consistent. [→ Margaret Ellis 2026](#margaret-ellis-2026)

### Margaret Ellis 2026

Margaret Ellis, Nikitha Donekal Chandrashekar, Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Mohammed Farghally, Jake O'Brien, and Naren Ramakrishnan. (2026). Demystify, Use, Reflect, Assess (DURA): An Experience Report on LLM Integration in CS2. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30908

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of MUSIC Inventory responses between Fall 2025 (n = 235 of 442) and Fall 2024 (n = 329 of 597) on a 1-6 Likert scale. The authors report "significantly higher perception of instructor Caring (p < .001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Student MUSIC perceptions were quite consistent but students reported significantly higher perception of instructor Caring (p < .001) during the semester we made these DURA course updates."
