## Evidence

### Kontro 2020

Kontro, I.; Génois, M. (2020). Combining surveys and sensors to explore student behaviour. [doi:10.3390/educsci10030068](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10030068)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 91 students with both CLASS and MBT scores (Table 4). Participation metrics correlate near zero with MBT (0.063, 0.059, 0.014) and CLASS (0.106, -0.041, -0.190); the printed effect size r = -0.190 (% SC vs CLASS) is the largest participation-metric correlation and falls in the small range. The "low to moderate (0.405) correlation between the overall CLASS score and the MBT score" is the exception, which this claim excludes.

> "The correlations are very low for non-dependent variables except for a low to moderate (0.405) correlation between the overall CLASS score and the MBT score."
