Likert-scale confidence ratings by test objective (Table 5) showed aberrant duct confidence of 4.14-5.52 out of 10 for PGY1-5, the lowest of all objectives; the abstract states "PGY1-5 residents were least confident identifying aberrant ducts, followed by BDI."
