Evidence¶

Huang et al. 2018¶

Simin Huang, Peng Xu, Lie Feng, Chunting Lu & Jing Yang. (2018). An Effective Teaching Method to Enhance History-Taking Skills for Chinese Medical Students. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p144

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Feedback questionnaire given to observation-group students only (n=25) after the final examination, consisting of 6 dichotomous yes/no questions about perceptions and satisfaction. Reported percentages include 96% (24/25) for improved history-taking skills and 92% (23/25) for increased confidence; 84% (21/25) reported increased motivation and 80% (20/25) increased medical-record writing ability.

"Specifically, 96% (24/25) of the students stated that the activity improved their history-taking skills, and 92% (23/25) of the students stated that this activity increased their confidence in communicating with real patients."
