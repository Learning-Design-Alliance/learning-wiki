## Evidence

### Scherff Aline D. 2025

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2025). EYE-ECG2: Addressing medical student feedback in an RCT with eye-tracking videos featuring cued retrospective reporting and modified learning sequences for ECG interpretation skills training. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001771

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the EYE-ECG2 RCT (N=94), paired t-tests across all participants showed pre-post gains of "BS=3.98%±9.82%", RS=9.89%±11.20%, and CS=9.70%±9.20%, all statistically significant. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "Paired t-tests show the training session significantly and moderately improved participant ECG interpretation skills (ascending lines in figure 4 (Fig. 4); pre-post gain BS=3.98%±9.82%, t(93)=3.93, p<.02*10-2; RS=9.89%±11.20%, t(93)=8.56, p<.03*10-11; CS=9.70%±9.20%, t(93)=10.22, p<.03*10-14)."
