## Evidence

### Cowan 2014

Cowan, R., & Powell, D. (2014). The Contributions of Domain-General and Numerical Factors to Third-Grade Arithmetic Skills and Mathematical Learning Disability. Journal of Educational Psychology, 106(1), 214-229. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0034097

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of deficit incidence (scores more than 1SD below the mean) in MLD (n=29), LA (n=42), and TA (n=187) groups, with persistent subsets excluding low CPM scores. Most children in difficulty groups had more than one type of deficit.

> "In the single point groups, 86% (25/29) MLD and 55% (23/42) LA children had multiple deficits. This pattern was repeated in the persistent subset: 91% (10/11) of the MLD group and 50% (7/14) of the LA group had combinations of deficits."
