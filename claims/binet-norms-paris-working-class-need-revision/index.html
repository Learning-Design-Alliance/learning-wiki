q1 · i? · design · r1

Huey's caution, attributed partly to Binet himself, about the generalizability of the norms. American institutions using the scale want to know whether the French age norms hold for American children of the same ages.

"the Binet norms have been worked out for children of the working classes of Paris and vicinity. They may need some revision when applied to other social classes and to other regions, even in France."
