## Evidence

### Isazadeh 2016

Isazadeh, P., Makui, S. M. Z., & Ansarian, L. (2016). Effect of Instructional vs. Authentic Video Materials on Introvert and Extrovert Iranian EFL Learners' Vocabulary Learning. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.4n.4p.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing the two authentic-video groups (combined) with the control group on the researcher-made posttest. The authentic-video groups "outperformed the control group", t(48) = 3.553, p = .001; no effect size is printed.

> "the experimental group (combination of group 1 and 2) ( M = 20.44, SD = 3.097) outperformed the control group ( M = 17.40, SD = 2.778), t (48) = 3.553, p = .001"
