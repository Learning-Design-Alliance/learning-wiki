## Evidence

### Yurdagül Gülistan 2025

Yurdagül Gülistan (2025). Investigation of Nurse's Pressure Ulcer Management Self‐Efficacy Status and Influencing Factors: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal 22, no. 7: e70714. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70714

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis (Table 4) of demographic and professional variables on PUM-SES in the 436-nurse study; the abstract reports "Sociodemographic variables explained only 3.9% of the total variance" and the clinic coefficient β = −0.159, p = 0.001 on total score.

> "while the unit worked had a significant and negative effect on all sub‐dimensions and the total score (β = −0.159, p = 0.001)."

### Yurdagül Gülistan 2025 (2)

Yurdagül Gülistan (2025). Investigation of Nurse's Pressure Ulcer Management Self‐Efficacy Status and Influencing Factors: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal 22, no. 7: e70714. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70714

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the Results section examining the effect of nurses' PU-related training on scale scores; the article reports no effect. This contrasts with the significant bivariate training comparisons, so the training finding is mixed.

> "it was observed that nurses' PU‐related training had no effect on the PUM‐SES sub‐dimensions and total score."
