## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exemplary student essay responses coordinated conservation of energy, kinetic and potential energy, and multiple representations to reconcile that breaking the ATP O-P bond requires energy while hydrolysis releases energy. [→ Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013](#benjamin-w-dreyfus-2013)

## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Julia Gouvea, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Chemical energy in an introductory physics course for the life sciences. [doi:10.1119/1.4870391](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4870391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of written exam responses to the ATP reconciliation essay (Fig. 7). Jasper linked bond energy and gravitational energy through analogy and coordinated the PE graph with molecule diagrams; Anya invoked conservation of energy. The authors note not all students made these connections and Anya's response was incomplete.

> "Both Jasper and Anya are able to leverage “physics” concepts about energy (e.g. conservation of energy, kinetic and potential energy) to explain this biochemical scenario."
