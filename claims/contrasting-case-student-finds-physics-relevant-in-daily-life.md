### Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle 2019

Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle. (2019). An uncommon case of relevance through everyday experiences. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.nair](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.nair)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Single-student case study built from three semi-structured video-recorded interviews across two semesters in an IPLS studio course. The initial survey anchor shows Sam was "not intrinsically interested in physics from the start", yet later interviews document everyday physics connections. No effect size is reported.

> "In the initial survey Sam reports —on a scale of 1-to-10— maximum anxiety (10/10) about the physics course, low level of preparation (3/10), and low sense of capability (4/10)."
