## Evidence

### le Roux 2021

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Type III Sums of Squares ANOVAs comparing the Botswana (n = 139), Namibian (n = 402) and South African (n = 904) samples on all study variables. The article reports effect sizes "ranging from η2 p = .01 to η2 p = .05", described as small, with pairwise Holm-adjusted comparisons.

> "Overall, with eﬀect sizes ranging from η2 p = .01 to η2 p = .05 the magnitude of these diﬀerences is small. The largest eﬀect of country was shown for social media use ( η2 p =.05, 90% CI [.03,. 07])."
