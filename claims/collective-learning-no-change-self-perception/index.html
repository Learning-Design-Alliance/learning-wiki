Evidence¶

Bautista Alcaine 2024¶

Bautista Alcaine, P., Vicente Sánchez, E., Orejudo Hernández, S., & Cano Escoriaza, J. (2024). Training pre-service teachers to deal with cyberbullying: Collective intelligence as a mode of learning. Computers & Education, 220, 105123. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105123

q3 · i? · causal · r1

In the same repeated measures mixed-model analysis of 180 participants, Question 2 (self-perception of capacity to act) showed no significant change across phases; the mean slightly decreased from 7.022 in phase 1 to 6.900 in phase 7.

"in the case of question 2 (p2), no statistically significant differences are found between the means of the different phases (p2, F6,1074 = 1.17, p = 0.319), so we cannot infer changes over time in this item"
