## Subclaims
`q1 i?` If perseverance is less than the time needed to master a task, it reduces the degree of learning; a child needing two hours, allowed one hour, who perseveres only thirty minutes learns 25 per cent under an assumed linear function. [→ Carroll 1962](#carroll-1962)

## Evidence

John B. Carroll. (1962). A Model Of School Learning. Harvard University. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Model+Of+School+Learning%22+Carroll

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Illustrative theoretical statement in the Perseverance section. Perseverance-in-learning is measured only as time actively engaged, and is a function of motivation and emotional variables; poor instruction may reduce perseverance beyond the time it wastes.

> "if a child needs two hours to learn something, is allowed one hour, but will persevere only thirty minutes, the degree of learning is only 25 per cent."
