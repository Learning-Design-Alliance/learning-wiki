# Nursing students rated the ESNPNSD positively on all six UEQ dimensions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After using the system, students' UEQ ratings were positive (above the 0.8 threshold) across attractiveness, dependability, efficiency, novelty, perspicuity, and stimulation, with total sample means of 1.75, 1.46, 1.58, 1.59, 1.74, and 1.23 respectively. [→ Abedi Ali 2025](#abedi-ali-2025)

## Evidence

### Abedi Ali 2025

Abedi Ali, Shamsabadi Ahmadreza, Shoraka Hamid Reza, Hosseini Mohammad, Khorashadizadeh Fatemeh, Rostami Rohalla, Namazinia Mohammad. (2025). Design and assessment of a web-based educational electronic system for non-practical nursing skills development among nursing students. BMC Medical Education, 25, 453. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07024-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional UEQ evaluation with 72 nursing students from three cohorts (27, 24, and 21) across two semesters, plus professors and health informatics experts. Table 2 reports total means of 1.75 (attractiveness), 1.46 (dependability), 1.58 (efficiency), 1.59 (novelty), 1.74 (perspicuity), and 1.23 (stimulation); the article reports "positive average scores across all key dimensions" with no effect sizes.

> "The evaluation of the system using the User Experience Questionnaire (UEQ) revealed positive average scores across all key dimensions, including attractiveness, dependability, efficiency, novelty, perspicuity, and stimulation."
