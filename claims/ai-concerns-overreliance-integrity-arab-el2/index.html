Evidence¶

Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh 2026¶

Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh. (2026). AI tools in Arab University English classrooms: Looking back and forward. Preprint, Ibb University, Yemen. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0138-4793

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's cross-study synthesis reports recurring concerns about generative AI tools, citing Jasim (2025), Alshammri (2024), and Shormani & Alfahad (2025). For example, Jasim (2025) found overreliance on AI weakened critical engagement among 30 Iraqi second-year learners.

"ChatGPT and other AI generative tools raised issues related to accuracy, overreliance, academic integrity, and reduced critical engagement (Jasim, 2025; Alshammri, 2024 ; Shormani & Alfahad, 2025 )."
