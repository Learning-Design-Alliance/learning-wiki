Aitken 2026¶

Aitken, A. A., Graham, S., Cao, Y., & Nusrat, A. (2026). The Impact of Writing Instruction for English Language Learners in the United States: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10197-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Separate moderator models tested study quality indicators (design, fidelity, pretest equivalence, reliability, floor/ceiling, training, N-of-1, teacher effects, attrition), participant characteristics (grade level, language status), and intervention characteristics (treatment type, dosage, control condition, outcome type). The article reports "no significant variation".
