### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i0` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlational analysis of learning-management-system-tracked course website visits during the 12-week semester against end-of-semester TOSLS scores and final grade; the article reports "no relationship" for both outcomes, with correlations of r = 0.035 and r = 0.011.

> "Correlative analyses showed that the frequency of accessing the course website had no relationship with students' practical SL skills at the end of the semester (r = 0.035; P = 0.177) or final grade in the course (r = 0.011; P = 0.445)."
