Kalyuga 2026¶

Kalyuga, S. (2026). Instructional Implications of Evolutionary Perspective on Human Cognitive Architecture as an Intelligent Natural Information Processing System. Educational Psychology Review, 38, 33. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10135-6

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the goal-driven approach section: the article attributes the worked example effect's reliable multi-study demonstration to the schema-acquisition goal only, and argues that for motivational or emotional goals "other instructional means could be more effective." No new data are reported.

"this effect had been originally hypothesized by cognitive load theory and then reliably demonstrated in multiple studies in various task domains when applied only to attaining the basic instructional goal of acquiring organized domain specific knowledge structures (schemas)."
