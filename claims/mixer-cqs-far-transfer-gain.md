## Evidence

### Hall 2014

Hall, L., Tazzyman, S., Hume, C., Endrass, B., Lim, M.-Y., Hofstede, G., Paiva, A., Andre, E., Kappas, A., & Aylett, R. (2014). Learning to Overcome Cultural Conflict through Engaging with Intelligent Agents in Synthetic Cultures. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0031-y

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post (T1/T2) far-transfer analysis of the CQS behavioural subscale in the school evaluation (N=144; complete T1/T2 CQS data n=122), using repeated measures ANOVA with condition as between factor. The quote reports "F (1, 120)=16.02, p<.001 , ηp2=.19"; MIXER means rose from 14.39 to 18.74 while control fell from 10.13 to 8.13.

> "A significant interaction effect between time and condition was found, F (1, 120)=16.02, p<.001 , ηp2=.19."
