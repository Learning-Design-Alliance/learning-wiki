## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2022

Yafeng Pan, Suzanne Dikker, Yi Zhu, Cuirong Yang, Yi Hu and Pavel Goldstein. (2022). Instructor-learner body coupling reflects instruction and learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00131-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model on overall BtBC with Instructional Approach and continuous Instructor Motion as fixed factors and Dyad as random intercept. The model "generated a signi ﬁcant interaction effect", with the positive association confined to the scaffolding condition; replacing instructor motion with learner motion produced no significant effects (βs < 0.05, ps > 0.06).

> "This new model generated a signi ﬁcant interaction effect ( β = 0.04, SE = 0.02, t = 2.09, p = 0.046, R2 β = 0.13), indicating that in the scaffolding condition instructor motion was positively associated with BtBC"
