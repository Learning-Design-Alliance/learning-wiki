`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-case discussion of virtual fieldwork: the authors state VR replication in Geography "loses the embodied presence" of complex natural environments and team-based problem-solving; an interpretive claim with no measured outcomes.

> "Virtual reality (VR) can be used to replicate field work in the physical sciences, but with Geography, it loses the embodied presence of being in a complex natural environment and team-based, problem-solving."

### Louise Starkey 2023 (2)

Louise Starkey, Anne Yates, Mairead de Roiste, Karsten Lundqvist, Adreanne Ormond, John Randal, Allan Sylvester. (2023). Each discipline is different: teacher capabilities for future-focussed digitally infused undergraduate programmes. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10196-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The Geography case study's teacher-capabilities section states that Geography teachers, primarily in Physical Geography and GIS, need capabilities in "discipline spe- cific applications such as GIS, Remote Sensing software, and UAVs"; a stated need, not a tested result.
