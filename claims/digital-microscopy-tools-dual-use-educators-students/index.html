Nava E 2026¶

Nava E, Jalote‐Parmar A, Våpenstad C, Valla M. (2026). Blended and digital approaches in histology and pathology teaching: A scoping review. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70169

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis states that "Thirty studies described digital learning tools with digital microscopy"; Table 3 categorizes web-based tools with digital microscopy integrated in 30 articles, the largest tool category identified.

"Thirty studies described digital learning tools with digital microscopy, which were used by educators for teaching and by students for independent studies."

Nava E 2026 (2)¶

Nava E, Jalote‐Parmar A, Våpenstad C, Valla M. (2026). Blended and digital approaches in histology and pathology teaching: A scoping review. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70169

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports that digital microscopy made microscopy more accessible, allowing students to practice anytime and anywhere, and that digitized whole slide images can be viewed, annotated, and shared using digital microscopy software.

"Digital microscopy has made microscopy more accessible to students, 13 allowing them to practice anytime and anywhere."
