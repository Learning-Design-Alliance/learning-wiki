Evidence¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Paired-samples t-test on the general (non-domain) AQT in the longitudinal course study reports a "large and significant decrease" in originality, Cohen’s d = 1.273, from M = 2.72 to M = 1.99.

"Conversely to DS-AQT, AQT originality showed a large and significant decrease from Time 1 (M = 2.72, SD = 0.50) to Time 2 (M = 1.99, SD = 0.61), t(64) = -10.266, p < .001, Cohen’s d = 1.273."

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026 (2)¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Paired-samples t-test of general AQT complexity across the semester finds no significant change, t(64) = 0.385, p = .702, d = 0.047; equivalence was not tested.

"Finally, there was no significant change in AQT complexity between Time 1 (M = 3.47, SD = 0.40) and Time 2 (M = 3.49, SD = 0.38), t(64) = 0.385, p = .702, Cohen’s d = 0.047."
