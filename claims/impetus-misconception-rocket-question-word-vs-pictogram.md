## Evidence

### M. Risch 2010

M. Risch. (2010). Investigations about Science Misconceptions. ArXiv.org. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire results for rocket question part B among 941 students: the article reports "even more students fell into the impetus thinking" compared with the preceding pictogram part; 55.2% chose curved upward without turn in part A (Table IV) and 47.9% answered speed increases steadily in part B.

> "In this part of the rocket question, asked in words rather than pictograms visualizing the problem as in the preceding part, even more students fell into the impetus thinking (constant force causes constant motion), confirming theories of „prevailing misconceptions“"
