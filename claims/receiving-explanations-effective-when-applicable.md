## Evidence

### Janssen 2010

Janssen, J., Kirschner, F., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Paas, F. (2010). Making the Black Box of Collaborative Learning Transparent: Combining Process-Oriented and Cognitive Load Approaches. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9131-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Webb and colleagues, that "elaborate explanations were only effective when the receiver was able to apply the explanation in a related task." It also notes receiving non-elaborated explanations or none is negatively correlated with learning. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Studies by Webb and Farivar ( 1999), Webb et al .( 1995), and Webb and Mastergeorge ( 2003), for example, showed that elaborate explanations were only effective when the receiver was able to apply the explanation in a related task."
