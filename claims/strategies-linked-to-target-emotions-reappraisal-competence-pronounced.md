### Stockinger 2025

Stockinger, K., Dresel, M., Marsh, H. W., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Strategies for regulating achievement emotions: Conceptualization and relations with university students' emotions, well-being, and health. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102089

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent correlation models in Study 1 (350 German university students) relating the six strategies to enjoyment, anxiety, and boredom. The article reports "small to large effects" and that for enjoyment, relations with reappraisal and competence development were positive; the authors interpret cross-sectional links cautiously given possible reciprocal causation.

> "Most strategies were significantly related to their target emotion (small to large effects; Table 2). Linkages with reappraisal and competence development were most pronounced for all three emotions."
