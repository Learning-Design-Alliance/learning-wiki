Herring 1918¶

Herring, J. P. (1918). Measurement of some abilities in scientific thinking. The Journal of Educational Psychology, IX(10), 535-558. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075736

q2 · i? · design · r2

Tryout of the second edition with grade and high school pupils; the article reports the printed counts 140, 141, seven discarded, thirty-three retained, and separately that "About 1800 grade pupils took the test in May, 1917." No effect size is printed.

"In March, 1917, the forty exercises of the second edition were submitted to 140 grade school pupils and to 141 high school pupils. So far as could be seen most of the exercises were good ones. Seven were discarded, leaving three for each of the eleven abilities,—thirty-three in all."
