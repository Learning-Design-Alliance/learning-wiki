# Early left anterior negativity (ELAN, 80–150 ms) shows L1-L2 difference in degree, not in kind: L1 elicits enhanced negativity, and the morphological priming effect on ELAN is present only in Chinese

## Evidence

### Fei Gao 2023

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Three-way repeated-measures ANOVA on the 80–150 ms ERP window from 30 bilinguals showed a large language effect on ELAN (partial η2 = 0.588), with L1 Chinese at −1.2 ± 0.23 μV versus L2 English at −0.52 ± 0.21 μV.

> "There was a signi ﬁcant main effect of language, F(1,29) = 41.362, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.588, such that L1 Chinese (−1.2 ± 0.23 μV) elicited enhanced negativity than L2 English (−0.52 ± 0.21 μV)."

### Fei Gao 2023 (2)

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up of the marginal three-way region × language × priming interaction (F(1,29) = 3.849, p = 0.059, partial η2 = 0.117) showed the morphological priming effect on ELAN in Chinese only; the article interprets early structural sensitivity in L1 and L2 as difference in degree, yet not in kind.

> "Chinese morphological priming was associated with signiﬁcant greater negativity in the anterior regions (−3.8 ± 0.51 μV) than Chinese semantic priming ( −3.14 ± 0.44 μV) (p = 0.03), while this morphological priming effect on ELAN was absent in English tasks ( p = 0.61)."
