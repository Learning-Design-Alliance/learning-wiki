## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Besser Ilan 2026

Besser Ilan, N., Shavit, H., Zohar, N., & Jaffe-Dax, S. (2026). Enhanced word learning and neural synchrony during children's storytelling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00417-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled within-subject experiment with 24 first-grade-age children, each learning one stimulus set via scaffolding storytelling and one via passive listening. The repeated measures ANOVA showed a significant strategy effect on question type (partial η2 = 0.20), with "Scaffolding showing a significant advantage in word learning (object names)" (Cohen's d = 0.4).

> "the learning strategy had a significant effect on question type scores [F(1, 23) = 5.8, p < 0.05,  partial η2 = 0.20, repeated measures ANOVA], with Scaffolding showing a significant advantage in word learning (object names) compared to the Passive strategy [2.6 ± 1.2 vs. 1.9 ± 1.2, t(23) = 1.8, p < 0.05, Cohen’s d = 0.4"
