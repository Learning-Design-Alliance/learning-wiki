# Interactive elements (worksheets and computer exercises) helped students better understand the lecture's content, with the worksheet-supported exam question answered about two-thirds correctly versus usually well under half on similar questions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students rated that the interactive units, especially worksheets and computer exercises, helped them understand the lecture contents, and lecturer observations showed motivated group work. [→ Gregor Feldbauer und Marcel Steffen 2017](#gregor-feldbauer-und-marcel-steffen-2017)
`q2 i?` On the exam question prepared by the worksheets, students scored on average about two thirds of the possible points, while on similar questions the average usually dropped to clearly under half. [→ Gregor Feldbauer und Marcel Steffen 2017 (2)](#gregor-feldbauer-und-marcel-steffen-2017-2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire data at evaluation points F1 (N = 6) and F2 (N = 7) in a Classroom Action Research study of about eight regularly attending master's students. Students reported the lecture contents became more understandable during the interactive units, and the units explicitly supported this increase in understanding, confirming H1.1.
