Narrative account of the educational barrier landscape in the introduction, citing prior literature. The article states that "General education teachers are not at all trained to support students with visual impairments" and that TVIs often lack science expertise and knowledge of modern assistive technologies.

> "General education teachers are not at all trained to support students with visual impairments. Thus, support from Teachers for the Visually Impaired (TVIs) plays a crucial role, yet even this specialized assistance is often inadequate for addressing the specific needs of science education"

### L. Petitdemange and S. Nashed 2025 (2)

L. Petitdemange and S. Nashed. (2025). ENABLING BLIND AND VISUALLY IMPAIRED INDIVIDUALS TO PURSUE CAREERS IN SCIENCE. SF2A 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.17620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.17620)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Narrative review of barriers with citations to prior studies. The article reports limited availability of adapted materials, production delays, missing tactile diagrams for geometric and spatial concepts, and practical work "frequently omitted from the curriculum", limiting experiential learning.

> "The challenges faced by BVI students are partly attributable to the limited availability of adapted materials, such as large print and Braille-transcribed textbooks, as well as delays in their production, all of which significantly hinder the learning process"
