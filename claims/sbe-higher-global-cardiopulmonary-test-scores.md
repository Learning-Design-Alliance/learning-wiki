## Evidence

### Meyers Lori 2020

Meyers Lori, Mahoney Bryan, Schaffernocker Troy, Way David, Winfield Scott, Uribe Alberto, Mavarez-Martinez Ana, Palettas Marilly, Lipps Jonathan. (2020). The effect of supplemental high Fidelity simulation training in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02322-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized two-group observational pilot at one medical school: 89 volunteer simulation students versus 99 lecture-only students on the end-of-block test. The simulation group's "median score of 106" exceeded the lecture group's 99 (p < 0.001).

> "The SBE group had a median score of 106 [IQR: 97, 110] and the LBE group scored 99 [IQR: 89, 105] (p < 0.001)."
