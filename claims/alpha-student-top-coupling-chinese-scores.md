## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Students with better Chinese final exam outcomes showed stronger alpha-band inter-brain coupling to the top-performing students during Chinese sessions (r = 0.433). [→ Jingjing Chen 2023](#jingjing-chen-2023)

## Evidence

### Jingjing Chen 2023

Jingjing Chen, Penghao Qian, Xinqiao Gao, Baosong Li, Yu Zhang and Dan Zhang. (2023). Inter-brain coupling reflects disciplinary differences in real-world classroom learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00162-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of classroom EEG from 30 students during Chinese sessions. Alpha-band student-top coupling (to 4 top students) correlated with Chinese final exam scores: "r = 0.433, Pearson's p = 0.017"; permutation p = 0.005 against 5000 shuffled versions.

> "The students with better learning outcomes in Chinese were found to have stronger inter-brain couplings to the top- performing students ( r = 0.433, Pearson 's p = 0.017, n = 30, top students' number = 4)."
