Bates 2025¶

Bates, M., Cashdollar, S., Mitchell, E., Nagaoka, J., & Clinton, C. (2025). Promising pathways: College and industry routes to good jobs for Illinois youth from low-income families. https://dpi.uillinois.edu/applied-research/iwerc/current-projects/hs-to-work-portfolio/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Descriptive analysis of HS2C data on five Illinois cohorts (2008-2012) of FAFSA-completing students employed in stable Illinois jobs, shown in Figure 1 pictograms by parental earnings quintile; "the likelihood of being in a good job increased steadily by parental earnings quintile."

"Only 52% of students who grew up in the lowest economic quintile ended up in a good job, while 76% of those from the highest economic quintile ended up in a good job."
