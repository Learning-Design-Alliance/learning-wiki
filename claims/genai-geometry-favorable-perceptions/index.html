Davor I 2026¶

Davor I (2026) Generative AI-supported instruction as a cognitive scaffold: effects on senior high school students' geometric reasoning and proof construction. Front. Educ. 11:1869961. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1869961

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive statistics (means and SDs) on the perception subscale, assessed only among experimental-group students. The lowest-rated item was "Generative AI helps me explain my answers better in geometry" (M = 3.81, SD = 1.180) and the highest was feedback helping correct mistakes (M = 4.23, SD = .812); the article reports an overall perception mean of 4.10.
