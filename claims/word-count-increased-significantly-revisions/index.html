Evidence¶

Gulley 2012¶

Gulley, B. (2012). Feedback on developmental writing students' first drafts. Journal of Developmental Education, 36(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035676

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Exploratory mixed design ANCOVA on Editor-generated word counts; unlike the other ANCOVas, "all three of the multivariate tests were significant at the .05 level", and average word count was higher across all groups in the second draft.

"The within subjects comparison for first draft word count and revised draft word count indicated statistically significant results with F = 23.776, p < .000. This comparison had a large effect size of .998."
