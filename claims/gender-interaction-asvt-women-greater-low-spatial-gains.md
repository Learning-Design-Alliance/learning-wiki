## Evidence

### Galada 2025

Galada, Aditi and Baytar, Fatma. (2025). Design and evaluation of a problem-based learning VR module for apparel fit correction training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Main-effects regression model predicting ASVT post-test score from pre-test score and gender in the 40-participant study. The article reports that "Gender was found to be a significant predictor of the ASVT post-test score even when controlling for the ASVT pre-test score"; no effect size was printed.

> "Gender was found to be a significant predictor of the ASVT post-test score even when controlling for the ASVT pre-test score."

### Galada 2025 (2)

Galada, Aditi and Baytar, Fatma. (2025). Design and evaluation of a problem-based learning VR module for apparel fit correction training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311587

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second regression model adding the gender × pre-test interaction for ASVT post-test scores. The interaction "was found to be statistically significant as well," and "When having low apparel spatial visualization skills, women saw a higher increase in skill as compared to men"; no effect size was printed.

> "In the model with the interaction term, the interaction term was found to be statistically significant as well. When having low apparel spatial visualization skills, women saw a higher increase in skill as compared to men."
