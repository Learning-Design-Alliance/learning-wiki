Nelson-Barber 2015¶

Nelson-Barber, S., & Trumbull, E. (2015). The Common Core Initiative, Education Outcomes, and American Indian/Alaska Native Students: Observations and Recommendations [a report from the Center on Standards & Assessment Implementation (CSAI) at WestEd]. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED570362

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report cites Faircloth & Tippeconnic (2010) for "high dropout rates of up to 50 percent among AI/AN youth" and Guillory & Wolverton (2008) for inadequate college support. These are descriptive statistics reported in the review, with no effect size.

"At present, high dropout rates of up to 50 percent among AI/AN youth (Faircloth & Tippeconnic, 2010) and inadequate support to persevere at the college level (Guillory & Wolverton, 2008) threaten successful outcomes for AI/AN students."

Nelson-Barber 2015 (2)¶

Nelson-Barber, S., & Trumbull, E. (2015). The Common Core Initiative, Education Outcomes, and American Indian/Alaska Native Students: Observations and Recommendations [a report from the Center on Standards & Assessment Implementation (CSAI) at WestEd]. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED570362

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report, citing Lopez, Heilig, & Schram (2013) and Bosse et al. (2011), states that despite rising college attendance, AI/AN students' "rates of matriculation and retention in college are still very low compared to their White peers."

"Although more AI/AN students are going to college than in the past (Lopez, Heilig, & Schram, 2013), their rates of matriculation and retention in college are still very low compared to their White peers"
