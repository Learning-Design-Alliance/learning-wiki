## Evidence

### Simard 2023

Simard, L., Bouchard, J., Lavallière, M., & Chevrette, T. (2023). Promoting physical activity and academic achievement through physically active learning: Qualitative perspectives of co-design and implementation processes. PLoS ONE, 18(11), e0294422. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294422

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of student focus groups and teacher interviews in the implementation evaluation. Students reported "increased PA outside the classroom and a reduction in screen time at home", attributed by the article to enthusiasm from active classroom sessions.

> "Students reported "enjoying being active in class," and surprisingly, this enthusiasm translated to reported, "increased PA outside the classroom and a reduction in screen time at home.""

### Simard 2023 (2)

Simard, L., Bouchard, J., Lavallière, M., & Chevrette, T. (2023). Promoting physical activity and academic achievement through physically active learning: Qualitative perspectives of co-design and implementation processes. PLoS ONE, 18(11), e0294422. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294422

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Teacher observation finding reported in the perceived outcomes section of the qualitative analysis: despite increased PA, neither teacher group "observed any changes in the children's physical fitness", while several students reported perceived endurance improvements.

> "Despite the increase in PA due to the PAL program, neither the HEPTs nor the 4GTs observed any changes in the children's physical fitness."
