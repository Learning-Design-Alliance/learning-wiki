Clinical-interview case study of two students, Alex and Pat, in an introductory physics class for engineers (Kuo et al. 2013); both could use v = v0 + at satisfactorily, but Pat's reasoning was modeled as connecting the base+change symbolic form to relationships between velocity and acceleration, and "the mathematical formulation of equation (7) is stably connected to the conceptual schema of base+change."
