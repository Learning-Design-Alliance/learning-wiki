## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Bootstrap mediation showed significant indirect effects of the logical style (β = 0.07) and creative style (β = 0.22) on engagement via self-efficacy, while the indirect paths for humorous and caring styles were non-significant. [→ Zhou Wei 2025](#zhou-wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhou Wei 2025

Zhou Wei, Liu Qiong. (2025). Effect of university teachers’ teaching style on EFL learners’ classroom engagement: the mediating role of self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03069-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis with 5,000 iterations found the two cognitive-oriented indirect paths significant with 95% CIs excluding zero; for humorous and caring styles the 95% CI included zero, so those mediation paths were rejected.

> "the logical teaching style → self-efficacy → classroom engagement (β = 0.07, p < 0.001), the creative teaching style→ self-efficacy → classroom engagement (β = 0.22, p < 0.05). Thus, H4 was partially supported."
