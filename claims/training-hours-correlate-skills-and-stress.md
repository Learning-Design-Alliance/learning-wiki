### Jiang Juan 2025

Jiang Juan, Deng Pengbo, Li Ruijia, Yang Qian, Hu Chengping, Li Yuanyuan. (2025). The effect of research training on research skills, academic performance, and stress level among medical undergraduates in China: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07809-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 3) among the 95 trained undergraduates: weekly training hours correlated positively with all three research skills (r = 0.243, 0.235, 0.363); the GPA correlation (r = 0.118, P = 0.308) was not significant.

> "weekly hours of research training were positively correlated with scientific literature reading ability (r = 0.243, P = 0.018), research data analysis ability (r = 0.235, P = 0.022), and scientific paper writing ability (r = 0.363, P < 0.001) but were not significantly correlated with GPA (r = 0.118, P = 0.308)."
