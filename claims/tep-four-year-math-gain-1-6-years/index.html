Joshua Furgeson 2014¶

Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/fr-the-equity-project-charter-school-impacts-on-student-achievement-final-report

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Evaluation of TEP's first four years comparing TEP students to similar students in comparable New York City public schools, using benchmarks for average annual learning gains. The report found "an additional 1.6 years of school in math" by the end of the 2012-2013 school year. No standardized effect size is printed.

"students who attended TEP for four years had test score gains equal to an additional 1.6 years of school in math"

Joshua Ferguson 2014¶

Joshua Ferguson, Moira McCullough, Brian Gill. (2014). Mathematica's Evaluation of The Equity Project Charter School: High Salaries for Teachers, Positive Impacts on Student Achievement (In Focus Brief). Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/if-mathematicas-evaluation-of-the-equity-project-charter-school-high-salaries-for-teachers-positive

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Evaluation brief reporting TEP impacts through the 2012-2013 school year, benchmarked against average annual learning gains for similar students in comparable New York City public schools; the math benchmark equals "an additional 1.6 years of school in math".

"students who attended TEP for four years had test score gains equal to an additional 1.6 years of school in math, an additional 0.4 years of school in English language arts, and an additional 0.6 years of school in science"
