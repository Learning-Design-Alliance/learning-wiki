### Richardson H. 2023

Richardson H., Saxe R., Bedny M. (2023). Neural correlates of theory of mind reasoning in congenitally blind children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2023.101285

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of false-belief items from the non-visual ToM task in 12 blind and 21 sighted children. The article reports "blind and sighted children did not differ in performance"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "When looking at the false-belief items separately, blind and sighted children did not differ in performance (group effect, controlling for age: b=−0.10, t=−1.5, CI=[−0.23,0.04], p=.15; Supplementary Figure 3)."
