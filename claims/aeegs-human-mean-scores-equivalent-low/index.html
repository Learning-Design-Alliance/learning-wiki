q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of score distributions from the correlational study of 1,008 students' scripts. The article reports the means were "almost equivalent and low when compared to the obtainable marks of 20"; these are descriptive statistics with no significance test reported for the mean difference.

"the mean of AEEGS and Human scores were 5.94 and 5.97, respectively, which is almost equivalent and low when compared to the obtainable marks of 20, with the AEEGS given slightly higher scores than human expert raters."
