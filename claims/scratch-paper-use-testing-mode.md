## Evidence

### James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann 2023

James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann. (2023). A Testing Load: Investigating Test Mode Effects on Test Score, Cognitive Load and Scratch Paper Use with Secondary School Students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09781-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The scratch-paper hierarchical linear model found "Students used scratch paper more during PBT (M = 1.91, 95% CI = 0.30, 3.52) compared to CBT (M = 1.26, 95% CI = −0.35, 2.87)"; the article adds that use "decreased as question difficulty increased" during CBT, with a significant mode main effect (F(1,767) = 26.61, p < 0.001).
