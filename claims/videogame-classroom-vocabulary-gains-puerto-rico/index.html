Claims

Sixth-grade students in a Puerto Rican public school who played My English Coach in class showed significant pre-post growth in English receptive vocabulary, but so did a same-school control class that did not play it, while an external control group did not

In one small purposive-sample study, sixth graders who played My English Coach and a same-school control class without the game both gained significantly in receptive vocabulary from pre- to post-test (the authors suggest a Hawthorne effect); an external control group did not.
