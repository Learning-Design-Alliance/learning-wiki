Evidence¶

Gambari et al. 2015¶

Amosa Isiaka Gambari, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf, David Akpa Thomas. (2015). Effects of Computer-Assisted STAD, LTM and ICI Cooperative Learning Strategies on Nigerian Secondary School Students' Achievement, Gender and Motivation in Physics. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, Volume 3, Issue 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085925

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA of posttest Physics Achievement Test scores for 90 SSII students in three schools randomly assigned to STAD, LTM or ICI, with pretest as covariate. The article reports "an F (1, 90) = 52.084, p = 0.000" for the treatment main effect.

"Table 2 revealed that an F (1, 90) = 52.084, p = 0.000 for the main effect (treatment) was significant; this indicates that the method of instruction produced a significant effect on the posttest achievement scores"

Gambari et al. 2015 (2)¶

Amosa Isiaka Gambari, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf, David Akpa Thomas. (2015). Effects of Computer-Assisted STAD, LTM and ICI Cooperative Learning Strategies on Nigerian Secondary School Students' Achievement, Gender and Motivation in Physics. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, Volume 3, Issue 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085925

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Scheffe post-hoc analysis of group mean scores in the same ANCOVA study. The article also reports a significant difference between LTM (X = 75.47) and ICI (69.53), and between STAD and LTM in Table 3.

"Significant difference was also established in the post-test mean scores of students exposed to STAD (X = 82.40) and those exposed to ICI (X = 69.53)."
