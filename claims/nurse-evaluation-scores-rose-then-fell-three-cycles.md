## Evidence

### Alameddine M 2015

Alameddine M, Mufarrij A, Saliba M, Mourad Y, Jabbour R, Hitti E. (2015). Nurses' evaluation of physicians' non-clinical performance in emergency departments: advantages, disadvantages and lessons learned. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0733-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparing mean scores across three evaluation cycles (May 2012, January 2013, December 2013) in a cross-sectional survey of ED nurses at one Lebanese academic medical center. The article reports "statistically significant only for leadership skills (P = 0.005) and overall evaluation (P = 0.001)"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "the scores in the second cycle were higher than that in the first cycle on the three dimensions of non-clinical performance (communication, professionalism and leadership skills), as well as on the overall evaluation. Yet, the difference was statistically significant only for leadership skills (P = 0.005) and overall evaluation (P = 0.001)."
