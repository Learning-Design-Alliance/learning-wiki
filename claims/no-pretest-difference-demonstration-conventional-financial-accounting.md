## Evidence

### Inuwa 2018

Inuwa, U., Abdullah, Z., & Hassan, H. (2018). A Mixed-Method Study of the Effect of the Demonstration Method on Students' Achievement in Financial Accounting. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 577-592. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191665

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on pre-test FAAT scores of the pre-test-post-test-control group experiment with 60 students per group. The article reports "t (118) = -.168, p = .867", supporting the null of equal initial achievement; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "before exposing students to the treatment , there was no statistically significant difference between the mean achievement score of demonstration method students (M = 24.05, SD = 4.031) and conventional approach students (M = 24.18, SD = 4.645), t (118) = -.168, p = .867"
