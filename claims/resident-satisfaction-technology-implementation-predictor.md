## Evidence

### He Lidan 2026

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Structured five-point Likert satisfaction survey of all 28 residents, analyzed with one-sample t-tests against the neutral midpoint of 3. All domains were positive with "Cohen's d ranging from 1.99 to 3.43"; teaching method innovation rated highest at 4.75 ± 0.51.

> "Results indicated statistically significant positive evaluations across all satisfaction domains (p < 0.001 for all items), with large effect sizes (Cohen's d ranging from 1.99 to 3.43) .Teaching method innovation received the highest satisfaction rating (4.75 ± 0.51, 95% CI [4.55–4.95]), followed by practice platform construction (4.68 ± 0.54, 95% CI [4.47–4.89])"

### He Lidan 2026 (2)

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Multiple regression on the same satisfaction survey data. The article reports "the strongest predictor of overall training satisfaction (β = 0.61, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.37)" for technological implementation; a principal component analysis had first yielded two satisfaction factors.

> "Multiple regression analysis indicated that satisfaction with technological implementation was the strongest predictor of overall training satisfaction (β = 0.61, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.37)."
