q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis linking eighth grade ITBS reading and math scores to ninth grade attendance. The brief reports 60 percent of students two or more years below grade level missed two or more weeks of school by ninth grade's end.

"Over three-quarters of the stu- dents who had reading and math test scores two or more years below grade level were moderate to extreme truants in at least one major subject by the end of ninth grade"

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same ITBS-linked attendance analysis showing truancy among top students is almost twice as high when class cutting is factored in. The brief attributes this, citing a 1986 Chicago Panel study, to a "culture of cutting" in schools with weak norms.

"Forty-two percent of the top Chicago students, those with achievement skills that place them on or above grade level for national norms, can be considered moderate to extreme truants in at least one major subject by the end of ninth grade."
