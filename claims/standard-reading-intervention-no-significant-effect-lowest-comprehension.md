## Evidence

### Al Otaiba 2018

Al Otaiba, S., Petscher, Y., Wanzek, J., Lan, P., & Rivas, B. (2018). I'm not throwing away my shot: What Alexander Hamilton can tell us about standard reading interventions. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 33(3), 156–167. https://doi.org/10.1111/ldrp.12179

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized control trial reanalysis of 194 fourth graders (n = 97 per condition) scoring at or below the 15th percentile on GMRT comprehension, comparing Passport to Literacy (average 94 daily 30-min small-group sessions) with business-as-usual. The article reports "statistically significant results were not observed" across all seven multilevel models (p-values .153–.893).

> "Despite our hypothesis that we would find significant differences favoring the treatment group, statistically significant results were not observed."
