Evidence¶

Mustafa Sat 2026¶

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Post-program transfer survey (N = 31), means 5.68–6.71. The article reports "71.0% strongly agreed that they could design similar activities using wearable e-textile technologies" and 80.6% agreement on applying learning at school; a follow-up report described a school science fair implementation.

"Specifically, 71.0% strongly agreed that they could design similar activities using wearable e-textile technologies."

Mustafa Sat 2026 (2)¶

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Same transfer survey (N = 31). The art-dimension item (M = 5.68, SD = 1.25) was lowest: "Only 32.3% of participants strongly agreed that they could effectively incorporate the artistic dimension into their STEAM-focused e-textile designs."

"Only 32.3% of participants strongly agreed that they could effectively incorporate the artistic dimension into their STEAM-focused e-textile designs, whereas 12.9% were neutral."
