# GenAI was less fruitful in the definition stage of design, where students reflected it did not support critical thinking and risked shallow learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reflected that GenAI did not help them think critically about their approach and choices, and that using it for interview scripts meant learning less. [→ Hauke Sandhaus 2024](#hauke-sandhaus-2024)

## Evidence

### Hauke Sandhaus 2024

Hauke Sandhaus, Maria Teresa Parreira, and Wendy Ju. (2024). Student Reflections on Self-Initiated GenAI Use in HCI Education. LLMs as Research Tools Workshop at CHI conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the interviews' challenges section (§3.3.2), students reflected on classes where they used GenAI for interview question and insight generation; one student said "Now you're learning less in HCI class" when scripts are dumped into ChatGPT.

> "“Now you’re learning less in HCI class. ... To code up an interview script, if you don’t have ChatGPT you need to read over the script. ... Everyone in the class, they just dump the script into ChatGPT and try to generate a result. ” (I5)"
