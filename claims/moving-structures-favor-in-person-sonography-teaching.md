# Structures characterized by movement in the dynamic sonography image favor in-person instruction: the in-person group scored better on the sixth sweep and on two bonus-point sweeps

`q3 i1` The control group achieved a statistically significantly better result on the sixth sweep (posterior neck) with p = 0.009 and Cohen-d = 0.369. [→ Everad F. 2024](#everad-f-2024)

### Everad F. 2024

Everad F., Seifert L., Mansour N., Hofauer B., Knopf A., Offergeld C. (2024). Sonographie als Gegenstand virtuellen Wissenstransfers zum praktischen Kompetenzerwerb. HNO. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00106-024-01476-1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Controlled comparison across the six sonography sweeps: only in the sixth sweep (posterior neck) did the KG achieve "ein statistisch signifikant besseres Ergebnis" with p = 0.009 and an effect size of Cohen-d = 0.369, a small effect; other sweeps showed only minor performance differences.

> "Nur im 6. Sweep (hinterer Halsabschnitt) erreichte die KG ein statistisch signifikant besseres Ergebnis (p = 0,009, Cohen-d = 0,369)."
