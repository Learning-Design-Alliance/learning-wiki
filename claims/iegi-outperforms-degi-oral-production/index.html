Evidence¶

Đặng 2012¶

Đặng, T. T. Đ., & Nguyễn, H. T. (2012). Direct versus Indirect Explicit Methods of Enhancing EFL Students' English Grammatical Competence: A Concept Checking-based Consciousness-raising Tasks Model. English Language Teaching, 6(1), 112. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n1p112

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on oral test scores (EG M=5.93, SD=1.08; CG M=5.39, SD=.783; t=2.75, df=92), administered a week after treatment. The article reports significance with no effect size printed.

"In the OT, the mean of Group 1 (EG) was 5.93, higher than that of Group 2 (CG) which was at 5.39. This difference resulted in a t score with a significant value of .007; i.e., less than the .05 alpha levels set for this project."
