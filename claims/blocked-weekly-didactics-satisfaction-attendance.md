## Evidence

### Wood D. Brian 2020

Wood D. Brian, Jordan Jaime, Cooney Rob, Goldflam Katja, Bright Leah, Gottlieb Michael. (2020). Conference Didactic Planning and Structure: An Evidence-based Guide to Best Practices from the Council of Emergency Medicine Residency Directors. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.5.46762

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of studies of blocked weekly conference formats in EM and other specialties, citing studies 10-13. The review reports "higher learner satisfaction, improved attendance, and fewer interruptions" for the blocked format; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Residents appreciate having protected educational time and, compared to shorter daily formats, the blocked weekly didactic structure has demonstrated higher learner satisfaction, improved attendance, and fewer interruptions."

### Wood D. Brian 2020 (2)

Wood D. Brian, Jordan Jaime, Cooney Rob, Goldflam Katja, Bright Leah, Gottlieb Michael. (2020). Conference Didactic Planning and Structure: An Evidence-based Guide to Best Practices from the Council of Emergency Medicine Residency Directors. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.5.46762

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The same review section reports that objective outcomes such as standardized test and board scores showed no demonstrated differences across conference formats, citing studies 10-14; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "While learners perceive improved learning with this format, studies have failed to demonstrate differences in objective outcomes such as scores on standardized tests or board examinations."
