# No significant interactions emerged between text medium and any other variable (test time, complexity, engagement)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The ANOVA found no significant interactions at any level between medium and the other variables, so medium effects did not depend on test time, complexity, or engagement. [→ Annette Kujawski Taylor 2011](#annette-kujawski-taylor-2011)

## Evidence

### Annette Kujawski Taylor 2011

Annette Kujawski Taylor. (2011). Students Learn Equally Well From Digital as From Paperbound Texts. Teaching of Psychology, 38(4), 278-281. https://doi.org/10.1177/0098628311421330

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus 2×2×2×2 mixed ANOVA (test time within; medium, complexity, engagement between) on quiz accuracy from the randomized experiment. No interaction reached significance at any level, including medium × test time and medium × complexity.

> "Furthermore, there were no significant interactions, at any levels, between any of the variables. Thus, neither the immediate nor the delayed testing interval produced any difference in comprehension between the digital and the paperbound texts."
