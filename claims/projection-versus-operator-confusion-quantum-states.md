## Evidence

### Marshman 2024

Marshman, E., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2024). Using multiple representations to improve student understanding of quantum states. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of written responses (Table IV) from 85 undergraduates plus think-aloud interviews with 23 students. The authors relate the difficulty to a reasoning primitive they call "operator measures observable", i.e., acting with an operator corresponds to measurement.

> "Table IV shows that 40% of the undergraduate students claimed , e.g.,  that writing a momentum eigenstate with eigenvalue 𝑝′ in the position representation involves the momentum operator."
