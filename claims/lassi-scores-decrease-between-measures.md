## Evidence

### Zhou Yuanyuan 2016

Zhou Yuanyuan, Graham Lori, West Courtney. (2016). The relationship between study strategies and academic performance. Int J Med Educ 7:324-332. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.57dc.fe0f

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing first and second measurements in the cohort. The SDLRS also significantly decreased (Cohen's d(104) = .42, p = .000). After the drop, "the LASSI subscales' scores were more closely associated with academic performance".

> "Results indicated that for the LASSI, eight subscales significantly decreased. They were: Attitude (Cohen's d(147) = .37, p < .001), Concentration (Cohen's d(147) = .38, p < .001), Information Processing (Cohen's d(147) = .32, p < 0.001), Motivation (Cohen's d(147) = .75, p < .001)"
