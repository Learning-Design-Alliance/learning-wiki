`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Bialystok et al. (2005)'s finding that bilingual children learning alphabetic second languages like Hebrew or Spanish with English first "performed better in PA tasks than monolingual and bilingual children who spoke English and Chinese". The review infers bilingual children may also benefit in writing acquisition.

> "Bialystok et al. (2005)determined that children who grow up bilingually have a general advantage in reading acquisition. Children learning two alphabetic languages especially beneﬁt while learning to read."
