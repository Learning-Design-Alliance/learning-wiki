## Evidence

### Kreiter Clarence D 2013

Kreiter Clarence D. (2013). A measurement perspective on affirmative action in U.S. medical education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.20531

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports well-established mean differences on cognitive ability and educational achievement tests, with printed effect sizes of 1.0 SD for African-Americans and 0.70 SD for Hispanics relative to the majority mean, quoting that "African-Americans score approximately 1.0 standard deviation (SD) below the mean attained by Whites". Sackett and Wilk's analysis shows only 0.01 of the lower-scoring group is selected at a 0.10 selection rate with a 1 SD differential.

> "African-Americans score approximately 1.0 standard deviation (SD) below the mean attained by Whites, and that Hispanics fall about 0.70 SD below the majority mean"
