## Evidence

### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Robustness analysis reported in SI Appendix, section 3, classifying students into four groups by parents' occupations and analyzing the lowest category with cost, benefit, and high-school fixed-effect controls; the study found students from the lowest social class were "3 percentage points less likely to always wait."

> "we found that students whose parents belonged to the lowest social class were 3 percentage points less likely to always wait (SE = 0.003, P < 0.001) after taking the costs and benefits of waiting and high school fixed effects into account."
