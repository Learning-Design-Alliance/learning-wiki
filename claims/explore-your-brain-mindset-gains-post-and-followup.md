# The growth mindset intervention increased students' growth mindset endorsement directly after the intervention (d = .38) and at 1-year follow-up (d = .25) versus an active control.

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Immediately post-intervention, intervention students reported 2.33 points more growth mindset endorsement than controls, a small-medium effect (d = .38). [→ Janssen 2025](#janssen-2025)

## Evidence

### Janssen 2025

Janssen, T. W. P., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Explore your brain: A randomized controlled trial into the effectiveness of a growth mindset intervention with psychosocial and psychophysiological components. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 280–302. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12572

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Manipulation check in a cluster RCT with 439 students in 20 classes, analyzed with linear mixed models one week after the 4×50-min intervention. The Time × Condition interaction was significant, F(1, 450.89) = 11.98, p < .001, and the article prints the effect size d = .38, calling it "a small‐medium effect size (d = .38)".

> "The positive slope, β = 2.33, 95% CI [1.01, 3.65], indicates that the growth mindset intervention resulted in 2.33 points more growth mindset endorsement from pre (T0) to direct‐post (T1) than the control condition. This is a small‐medium effect size (d = .38)."

### Janssen 2025 (2)

Janssen, T. W. P., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Explore your brain: A randomized controlled trial into the effectiveness of a growth mindset intervention with psychosocial and psychophysiological components. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 280–302. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12572

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

One-year follow-up in the same LMM analysis of the cluster RCT; the Time × Condition interaction remained significant (p = .049). The article prints the effect size d = .25, calling it "a small effect size (d = .25)". Intention-to-treat, missing data handled via ML estimation.

> "The positive slope, β = 1.62, 95% CI [.01, 3.25], indicates that the growth mindset intervention resulted in 1.62 points more growth mindset endorsement from pre (T0) to 1‐year follow‐up (T2) than the control condition. This is a small effect size (d = .25), which is approximately 2/3 of the effect size directly after the interventions (manipulation check)."
