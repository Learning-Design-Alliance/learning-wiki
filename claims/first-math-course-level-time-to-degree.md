## Evidence

### Sarah Garner 2012

Sarah Garner, Michael J. Tremmel, Sarah J. Schmidt, John P. Wisniewski, Eric Agol. (2012). Evaluation of a College Freshman Diversity Research Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of the student term major database (Figure 6), plotting mean quarters to graduate versus first math class for all, STEM, Phys/Astr, and Pre-MAP graduates. The authors attribute the trend at least in part to a delayed start to major course work for which these math courses are a pre- or co-requisite.

> "There is a strong trend for all STEM majors, mimicked by the Physics and Astronomy majors, indicating that the lower the level of the first math course, the longer it will take students to graduate."
