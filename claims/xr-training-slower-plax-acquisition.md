## Evidence

### Mulder Tessa A. 2026

Mulder Tessa A., Jacobs Robin C., Zuurmond Manon S., Qaddoumi Adel, Regeer Madelien V., Dekker Friedo W., Bauer Martijn P., Srámek Alexandr, Langers Alexandra M.J., Hierck Beerend P. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT, total test time did not differ significantly, but "the time to visualize the PLAX" was "longer in the XR group: 177 s versus 128 s, p-value 0.039".

> "There was no significant difference in the time taken to complete the test, however the time to visualize the PLAX in order to measure the EPSS (time till optimal image) was longer in the XR group: 177 s versus 128 s, p-value 0.039."
