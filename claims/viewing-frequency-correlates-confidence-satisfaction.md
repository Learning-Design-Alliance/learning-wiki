## Evidence

### Lee Nam-Ju 2016

Lee Nam-Ju, Chae Sun-Mi, Kim Haejin, Lee Ji-Hye, Min Hyojin Jennifer, Park Da-Eun. (2016). Mobile-Based Video Learning Outcomes in Clinical Nursing Skill Education: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Computers, Informatics, Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1097/CIN.0000000000000183

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlational analysis of intervention-group experience data (mean 3.89 viewings, SD 2.04, in 1 week). Viewing frequency "was significantly and positively correlated with confidence in practice (r = 0.352, P = .035) and class satisfaction (r = 0.453, P = .005)"; device type was unrelated to outcomes.

> "The type of device was not associated with any of the outcome variables, but the number of viewings was significantly and positively correlated with confidence in practice (r = 0.352, P = .035) and class satisfaction (r = 0.453, P = .005)."
