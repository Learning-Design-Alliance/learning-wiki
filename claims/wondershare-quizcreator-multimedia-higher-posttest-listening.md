## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Posttest listening scores of the experimental group taught with interactive multimedia materials were significantly higher than those of the control group taught with audio materials (t-cal 5.583 > t-table 2.000, df 70). [→ Andi Tenri Ampa 2015](#andi-tenri-ampa-2015)

## Evidence

### Andi Tenri Ampa 2015

Andi Tenri Ampa. (2015). The Implementation of Interactive Multimedia Learning Materials in Teaching Listening Skills. English Language Teaching, 8(12). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n12p56

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group study: 36 students per group, experimental taught with Wondershare Quizcreator multimedia, control with audio materials. Posttest means were "43.47<58.25"; the t-test gave t-cal 5.583 at df 70, so H1 was accepted.
