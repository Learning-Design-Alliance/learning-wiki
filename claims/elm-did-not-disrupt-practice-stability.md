### Doabler 2018

Doabler, C. T., Nelson, N. J., Kennedy, P. C., Stoolmiller, M., Fien, H., Clarke, B., Gearin, B., Smolkowski, K., & Baker, S. K. (2018). Investigating the Longitudinal Effects of a Core Mathematics Program on Evidence-Based Teaching Practices in Mathematics. Learning Disability Quarterly, 41(3), 144–158. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948718756040

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nested chi-square model-fit comparison between the unconstrained baseline and complete covariance constraints models for each rate variable, with parametric bootstrap p values reported in Table 3. Overall latent longitudinal correlations were .34, .72, .53, and .83 for demonstrations, feedback, group responses, and individual responses.

> "the complete covariance constraints model is the preferred model means that the ELM intervention did not significantly disrupt the Year 1 to Year 2 stability of evidence-based teaching practices."
