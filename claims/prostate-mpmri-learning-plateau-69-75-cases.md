`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective educational study of 14 trainees interpreting 200 prostate mpMRI cases; learning curves for PI-RADSv2.1 (≥3 vs <3) agreement were modeled with GEE and segmented regression. Both groups improved rapidly in the first 50 cases (urology +29.94%, radiology +23.77%), then "no significant further improvement was observed" beyond the inflection points.

> "Segmented regression identified inflection points at 69 cases (46.0, 76.7) for urology trainees and 75 cases (64.0, 81.0) for radiology trainees. Beyond these points, no significant further improvement was observed"
