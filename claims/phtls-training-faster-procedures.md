## Evidence

### Häske David 2018

Häske David, Beckers Stefan K., Hofmann Marzellus, Lefering Rolf, Preiser Christine, Gliwitzky Bernhard, Grützner Paul Alfred, Stöckle Ulrich, Münzberg Matthias. (2018). Performance Assessment of Emergency Teams and Communication in Trauma Care (PERFECT checklist)—Explorative analysis, development and validation of the PERFECT checklist: Part of the prospective longitudinal mixed-methods EPPTC trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202795

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Explorative video analysis of randomly selected videos at each measuring point of the EPPTC trial, reporting mean times with 95% confidence intervals of procedures in the primary assessment. The printed values are descriptive means with no test statistic or effect size.

> "On average, the procedures were carried out at minute 2:29, 95%CI 1:54–3:03 at t0, at minute 1:11, 95%C 0:53–1:30 at t1 and at minute 1:14, 95%CI 0:56–1:31 at t2."
