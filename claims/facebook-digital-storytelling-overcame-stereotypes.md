`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative study of 25 American and approximately 40 South African undergraduates using a private Facebook group for digital storytelling. The review reports the activity "allowed students from both countries to overcome the stereotypes they had about each other" and provided a space to post opinions and include emotion in stories.

> "The use of Facebook for digital storytelling allowed students from both countries to overcome the stereotypes they had about each other as they recognized similarities between them, created an opportunity for friendships"
