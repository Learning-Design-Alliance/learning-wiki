## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Students’ views about the nature of experimental physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of matched pre/post E-CLASS responses from FY students (N = 5609) shows the overall favorable fraction fell from 0.679 to 0.655; the article reports the shift was "decreased signiﬁcantly" with d = −0.15, driven by small negative shifts on 17 items.

> "This fraction favorable decreased signiﬁcantly over the course of one semester (or quarter) of instruction ( p ≪ 0.01, d = −0.15)."
