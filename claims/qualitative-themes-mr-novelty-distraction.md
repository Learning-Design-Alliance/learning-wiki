## Evidence

### Wang Chris 2020

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis (NVivo 11) of open questionnaire responses from the MR group identified immersion, novelty, and visualisations "helpful for learning spatial knowledge", alongside distraction, overwhelm, and unhelpfulness for nominal learning (Table 3).

> "The participants in the MR group found the learning tool to be immersive which helped their engagement. They also found the device to be novel and exciting to use. They felt that the holographic visualizations and animations were helpful for learning spatial knowledge."
