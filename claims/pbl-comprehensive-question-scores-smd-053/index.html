Evidence¶

Xu Wei 2021¶

Xu Wei, Ye Tingjie, Wang Xiaoling, Hayashi. Shogo. (2021). The effectiveness of the problem-based learning in medical cell biology education: A systematic meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027402

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of the 3 included studies that reported comprehensive question scores; the printed effect size SMD = 0.53 (95% CI 0.29, 0.78) is a medium effect, which the authors read as PBL students' ability to "analyze and solve comprehensive questions was stronger than that from LBL curriculum".

"Three studies revealed the ability of student to solve comprehensive questions in PBL and LBL group. The pooled SMDs with 95% CIs of comprehensive question score were calculated to be 0.53 (0.29, 0.78) (Fig. 3). This finding proved that the ability of student from PBL curriculum to analyze and solve comprehensive questions was stronger than that from LBL curriculum."
