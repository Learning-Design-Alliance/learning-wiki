# Exploratory search behaviour in visual and semantic tasks is not correlated across children once ADHD score is controlled

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A linear regression between composite exploratory indices for visual and semantic search, controlling for ADHD score, was not significant. [→ Van den Driessche 2019](#van-den-driessche-2019)

## Evidence

### Van den Driessche 2019

Van den Driessche, C., Chevrier, F., Cleeremans, A. & Sackur, J. (2019). Lower Attentional Skills predict increased exploratory foraging patterns. Scientific Reports 9:10948. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-46761-0

`q3 · i?`

Secondary analysis summing significant quantiles per child into visual and semantic exploration indices; the regression controlling for ADHD score was not significant (p > 0.3). Equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "The linear regression between the two indices controlled for the ADHD score was not significant (p > 0.3)."
