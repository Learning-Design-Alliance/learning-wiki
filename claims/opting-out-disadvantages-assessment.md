## Evidence

### Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman 2024

Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman. (2024). Opting Out as an Untapped Resource in Instructional Design: Review and Implications. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09879-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Harden et al. (1976), two factors discouraging opt-out options in tests: opting out "may obscure implicit knowledge" and differing interpretations "might lead to varying utilization rates, potentially impacting students' relative success".

> "First, opting out may obscure implicit knowledge that could aid in the process of elimination and accurate guessing (see also Higham, 2007; Powell et al., 2005). Second, differences in individual interpretations of opting out might lead to varying utilization rates, potentially impacting students' relative success."
