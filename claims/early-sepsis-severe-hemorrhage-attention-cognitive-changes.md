## Evidence

### do Carmo André Luis Santos 2022

do Carmo André Luis Santos, Fredo Fernanda Wagner, Bruck Isac, de Lima Joseli do Rocio Maito, Janke Rebecca Nóbrega Ribas Gusso Harder, Fogaça Thais da Glória Messias, Glaser Jacqueline Andrea, Riechi Tatiana Izabele Jaworski de Sá, Antoniuk Sergio Antonio. (2022). Neurological, cognitive and learning evaluation of students who were born preterm. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2022/40/2020252

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort comparison of children with and without early neonatal sepsis on the parent-rated Vanderbilt scale; only p-values and percentages printed, no effect sizes. No differences appeared on neuropsychological and psychoeducational tests.

> "The PIs with early sepsis had higher frequency of change in the Vanderbilt Assessment test for lack of attention (36.5 vs 11.8%; p=0.041) and hyperactivity (42.3 vs 12.0%; p=0.042)."

### do Carmo André Luis Santos 2022 (2)

do Carmo André Luis Santos, Fredo Fernanda Wagner, Bruck Isac, de Lima Joseli do Rocio Maito, Janke Rebecca Nóbrega Ribas Gusso Harder, Fogaça Thais da Glória Messias, Glaser Jacqueline Andrea, Riechi Tatiana Izabele Jaworski de Sá, Antoniuk Sergio Antonio. (2022). Neurological, cognitive and learning evaluation of students who were born preterm. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2022/40/2020252

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis within children with periventricular/intraventricular hemorrhage comparing severe (grades III and IV) versus milder hemorrhage on the Piaget operational stage assessment; HPIV presence alone showed no difference in evaluation results.

> "However, among those who had HPIV, the ones with grades III and IV hemorrhage presented higher levels of change in the Piaget operational stage (83.3 vs 35.3%; p=0.013)."
