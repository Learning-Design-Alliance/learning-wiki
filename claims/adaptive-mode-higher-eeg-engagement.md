# The EEG-adaptive exercise condition produced significantly higher cognitive engagement during training than the unrestricted chatbot condition (p = .018), and was the only condition above the 0.5 baseline

### Alexandre Clin Deffarges 2026

Alexandre Clin Deffarges, Nataliya Kosmyna, and Pattie Maes. (2026). Socrates went Nuclear: Comparing Interaction Strategies for AI systems in a Learning Context using Brain Sensing. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.00584. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00584

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on per-participant mean E_norm during training was marginally significant (F(2,47)=2.908, p=.064); Welch's t-tests showed Adaptive (mean 0.547) higher than Full (0.389), with no significant difference for Socratic vs. Adaptive (p=.251) or Full vs. Socratic (p=.244). A complementary LMM on 22,775 EEG epochs confirmed the pattern (Adaptive-Full p=.013).
