# Adaptive students request significantly fewer on-demand hints and spend longer on a step before requesting help than Control students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Maniktala 2020

Maniktala, M., Cody, C., Isvik, A., Lytle, N., Chi, M., & Barnes, T. (2020). Extending the Hint Factory for the Assistance Dilemma: A Novel, Data-driven Help-Need Predictor for Proactive Problem-solving Help. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 4. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hint-usage analysis in the controlled Deep Thought study. Adaptive students received 28 proactive hints (22.8% of steps) and 6 on-demand hints on average, versus 14 on-demand hints for Control; the test showed Adaptive students "requested significantly fewer on-demand hints during training". No effect size is printed.

> "A Mann Whitney U test shows that students in the Adaptive condition requested signiﬁcantly fewer on-demand hints during training than those in the Control condition (U = 908.5, z = 3.46, p< .01)."

### Maniktala 2020 (2)

Maniktala, M., Cody, C., Isvik, A., Lytle, N., Chi, M., & Barnes, T. (2020). Extending the Hint Factory for the Assistance Dilemma: A Novel, Data-driven Help-Need Predictor for Proactive Problem-solving Help. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 4. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Same study's hint-timing analysis: Adaptive students requested hints after a median of 44 seconds (M = 70s, SD = 125s) versus a median of 21 seconds (M = 29s, SD = 21s) for Control, suggesting Adaptive students "may engage longer on their own" before seeking help. No effect size is printed.

> "A Mann Whitney U test on the time spent in a step before requesting help shows a signiﬁcant difference between the two conditions ( U = 600, z = 1.97, p = .02)."
