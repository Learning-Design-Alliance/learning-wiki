## Evidence

### Lepper 2005

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of GPAs in the second school district (n = 216). The quote reports the extrinsic correlation with GPA was "significant but, in this case, negative" with r = -.23; five of six subscale-by-subject correlations were negative.

> "the correlation between extrinsic motivation and overall GPA was also significant but, in this case, negative (r ⫽ ⫺.23, p ⬍ .01)."

### Lepper 2005 (2)

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of California Achievement Test scores in the first school district subset (n = 136). The quote reports scores "negatively correlated with extrinsic motivation (r ⫽ ⫺.32, p ⬍ .001)", an effect size of r = -.32; all six subscale correlations were negative.

> "standardized test scores proved both positively correlated with intrinsic motivation (r ⫽ .27, p ⬍ .01) and negatively correlated with extrinsic motivation (r ⫽ ⫺.32, p ⬍ .001)."
