## Evidence

### Kipen Eva 2019

Kipen Eva, Flynn Eleanor, Woodward-Kron Robyn. (2019). Self-regulated learning lens on trainee perceptions of the mini-CEX: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-026796

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from the perceived-outcome theme of the thematic analysis of twelve trainee interviews. One participant described how the consultant's words "makes me feel better or worse about myself as a clinician".

> "According to the participants, feedback was reportedly critically important in self-reflection, assisting with calibrating self-evaluation and self-reaction."

### Kipen Eva 2019 (2)

Kipen Eva, Flynn Eleanor, Woodward-Kron Robyn. (2019). Self-regulated learning lens on trainee perceptions of the mini-CEX: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-026796

`q2 · i?`

Subtheme of feedback within the perceived-outcome theme. The article contrasts this with feedback perceived as unhelpful, which was "brief, generic and delivered by supervisors in the workflow, without much forethought or time".

> "Feedback that was perceived as meaningful was delivered in a private space with an engaged supervisor in an unhurried manner."
