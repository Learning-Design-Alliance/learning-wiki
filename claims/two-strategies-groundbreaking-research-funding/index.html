The review reports, citing Heinze (2008), a study of nine groundbreaking-research programs that identified two funding strategies: five programs emphasizing applicant track record and leadership, and four emphasizing originality and intellectual merit of the proposed research. The reviewed study's design is not described further in this report.

"A study of nine programs, intended to encourage groundbreaking research, identified two strategies that funders used that had distinct goals and criteria elements (Heinze 2008). Five of nine programs emphasized the applicant's track record and leadership qualifications"
