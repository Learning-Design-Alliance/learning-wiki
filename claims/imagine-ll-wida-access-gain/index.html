Imagine Language & Literacy 2023¶

Imagine Language & Literacy. (2023). Evidence rating: promising. https://www.evidenceforessa.org/programs/imagine-language-literacy

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

The third study used 2022-2023 data to compare ELL students in grades 1-5 who used the program with similar students supported through district-provided resources; WIDA ACCESS outcomes covered 2,762 students, with a printed "effect size = +0.14" favoring users.

"Literacy proficiency outcomes were assessed for 2,762 students in grades 1-5 with the WIDA ACCESS assessment and 1,514 students in grades 4 and 5 with the Florida Assessment of Student Thinking (FAST)."
