## Evidence

### Shadaan 2013

Shadaan, P., & Leong, K. E. (2013). Effectiveness of Using Geogebra on Students' Understanding in Learning Circles. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 1(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086434

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of a survey questionnaire administered to the experimental group after treatment; 93% reported learning a lot, 82% were excited and engaged, while the article notes "some students reported they were not so confident when using the GeoGebra software" (54% agreed they felt confident).

> "The majority of students, about 93% of them mentioned that they learnt a lot using GeoGebra and benefited much through the teacher-students interactions when using GeoGebra, while 82% of the students mentioned that they were excited about using GeoGebra software"
