### Edward F. Redish 2006

Edward F. Redish. (2006). Problem Solving and the Use of Math in Physics Courses. Invited talk, World View on Physics Education in 2005, Delhi. Originally published 2005. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608268](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608268)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author's interpretive analysis of traditional physics instruction across the curriculum, from introductory courses through the major. He adds that exams often require only one-step recognition, giving cues so students need not recognize deep structures, and that instructors tend to pander with simple problems.

> "We tend to let them do the mathematical manipulations in the process step, and we rarely ask them to interpret their results and even less often ask them to evaluate whether the initial model is adequate."
