The WWC systematic review's credit accumulation and persistence domain rests on this single RCT conducted in Boston (MA) and Fulton County (GA), rated as "a statistically significant positive effect" with a small extent of evidence (1,397 students). The WWC confirmed the difference; no effect size is printed in this section's text.

> "Castleman et al. (2014) reported, and the WWC confirmed, a positive and statistically significant difference between the summer counseling group and the comparison group on the percentage of students who remained enrolled in college after the spring of their freshman year and after the fall of their sophomore year."
