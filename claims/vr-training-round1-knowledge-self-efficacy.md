## Evidence

### Lin Pei-Chao 2024

Lin Pei-Chao, Wung Shu-Fen, Lin Pei-Chen, Lin Yi-Ching, Lin Cheng-Yu, Huang Hsiao-Ling. (2024). Virtual reality-based simulation learning on geriatric oral health care for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Oral Health 24, Article 627. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04249-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

GEE longitudinal analysis of a pilot parallel-group randomized controlled trial with 50 nursing students (25 per group), controlling for gender, academic year, VR game experience, and oral health learning experience. The article reports knowledge "effect size: 1.15" and self-efficacy "effect size: 0.82".

> "Compared to the participants in the control group, those in the experimental group had significantly greater improvements in geriatric oral health care knowledge [β: 2.56, 95% CI: 1.05, 3.03; p < 0.001; effect size: 1.15], and self-efficacy [β: 5.04, 95% CI: 1.70, 8.38; p = 0.003; effect size: 0.82] after the first round of training."
