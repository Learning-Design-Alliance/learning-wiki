# After four weeks, CMRL residents scored higher than MBL residents in comprehensive analysis ability and prescribing medical orders ability

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CMRL group scores in comprehensive analysis ability after teaching were higher than MBL group scores (88.27 ± 10.95% vs. 76.02 ± 18.58%, p = 0.001). [→ Li Min 2025](#li-min-2025)
`q3 i?` CMRL group scores in prescribing medical orders after teaching were higher than MBL group scores (90.03 ± 9.63% vs. 85.17 ± 10.17%, p = 0.041). [→ Li Min 2025](#li-min-2025)

## Evidence

### Li Min 2025

Li Min, Liu Tao, Wen Yiqiong, Zhang Min, Zhu Wenye, Wang Xuming, Hou Yunjiao, Li Lin, Zu Anju, Sun Shibo. (2025). Combination of mentor-based and resident-based learning may benefit to improving the ability of clinical practice in resident training. BMC Medical Education 25:197. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06798-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 72 second-year residents (36 per group) in standardized resident training, four weeks of teaching. The between-group comparison after teaching showed "ability of comprehensive analysis 88.27 ± 10.95% vs. 76.02 ± 18.58%, p = 0.001" and prescribing medical orders p = 0.041; no effect sizes were printed.

> "scores of CMRL group in trainees' ability of comprehensive analysis and prescribing medical orders after teaching were higher than those of the MBL group (ability of comprehensive analysis 88.27 ± 10.95% vs. 76.02 ± 18.58%, p = 0.001; ability of prescribing medical orders 90.03 ± 9.63% vs. 85.17 ± 10.17%, p = 0.041; respectively)"
