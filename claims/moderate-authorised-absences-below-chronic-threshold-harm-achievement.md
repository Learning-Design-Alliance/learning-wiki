## Evidence

### Dräger 2024

Dräger, J., Klein, M., & Sosu, E. M. (2024). Trajectories of school absences across compulsory schooling and their impact on children's academic achievement: An analysis based on linked longitudinal survey and school administrative data. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306716

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

RWR-adjusted regression across the four continuous outcomes (Attainment 8, English, Math). Printed effect size: "(-0.13 to -0.21 SD)" for CMAA — a small deficit of 0.13 to 0.21 standard deviations versus CLA; the discussion notes CMAA pupils never breached the 10% chronic-absence threshold yet missed about 80 schooldays more over 11 years.

> "‘Moderately Increasing Unauthorised Absences’ (-0.61 to -0.70 SD) and ‘Consistently Moderate Authorised Absences’ (-0.13 to -0.21 SD) also negatively affected achievement compared to ‘Consistently Low Absences’."
