## Evidence

### Ramadanov N 2026

Ramadanov N, Heinz M, Hable R, Becker R, Banke IJ. (2026). Surgeon experience in hip arthroscopy improves operative efficiency and reduces conversion to total hip arthroplasty: A meta‐analysis. Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70431

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of seven studies reporting operative time, comparing early versus late surgeon experience phases in hip arthroscopy. The pooled estimate showed "a statistically significant reduction in operative time of –31.77 min", with high heterogeneity (I² = 92%).

> "The pooled estimate under the random‐effects model showed a statistically significant reduction in operative time of –31.77 min (95% confidence interval [CI] –51.40 to –12.14)."
