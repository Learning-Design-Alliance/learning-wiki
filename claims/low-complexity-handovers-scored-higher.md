## Evidence

### Moore Malcolm 2017

Moore Malcolm, Roberts Chris, Newbury Jonathan, Crossley Jim. (2017). Am I getting an accurate picture: a tool to assess clinical handover in remote settings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1067-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of checklist sub-total scores by rated case complexity among the 55 assessed calls (24 low, 24 medium, 2 high, 5 not rated). "Low complexity cases" scored more highly than medium complexity cases, p = 0.003.

> "Low complexity cases - sub-total mean 14.60 (2.83)) were scored more highly than medium complexity cases - 12.66 (2.86) (p = 0.003)."
