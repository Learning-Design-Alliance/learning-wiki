### Nicholas Mattei 2014

Nicholas Mattei, Thomas Dodson, Joshua T. Guerin, Judy Goldsmith, Joan M. Mazur. (2014). Lessons Learned from Development of a Software Tool to Support Academic Advising. [doi:10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659](https://doi.org/10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Domain-expert survey of 10 advisors (faculty, departmental, and college staff advisors). In contrast, 7 of 10 rated case-based analogy to past student performance as the least important aspect of advising.

> "Nearly all advisors, across all categories, saw requirements as the most important priority when recommending courses to students. This criteria was rated as the ﬁrst priority for 9 of 10 advisors surveyed."

### Nicholas Mattei 2014 (2)

Nicholas Mattei, Thomas Dodson, Joshua T. Guerin, Judy Goldsmith, Joan M. Mazur. (2014). Lessons Learned from Development of a Software Tool to Support Academic Advising. [doi:10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659](https://doi.org/10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same advisor survey. Advisors worried students would skip person-to-person advising, a concern the authors note was not supported by the student survey results.

> "7 of the 10 advisors said they would rarely or never use our system or recommend our system to students."
