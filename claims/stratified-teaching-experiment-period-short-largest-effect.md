## Evidence

### Shi 2020

Shi, H., Cheung, E.S.T., & Cheung, A.C.K. (2020) The impact of stratified teaching on the academic performance of Chinese middle school students: A meta-analysis. Science Insights Education Frontiers, 7(1):735-760. https://doi.org/10.15354/sief.20.re012

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Experiment-period moderator analysis (Table 5) of the included studies: 3 short, 17 medium, and 6 long datasets, combined test Z = 10.933, P = 0.000, between-group QBET = 1.944, P = 0.378 (no significant difference).

> "In terms of a single period, the effect size of the short period SMD = 0.82, reaching a significant level; compared to the medium and long periods, the stratified teaching in the short experimental period has the most apparent effect on students' academic performance."
