## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median DKAS scores rose from 34.5 at baseline to 45 post-UDMOOC, a statistically significant increase after adjusting for general education and dementia exposure. [→ Eccleston 2019](#eccleston-2019)

## Evidence

### Eccleston 2019

Eccleston, C., Doherty, K., Bindoff, A., Robinson, A., Vickers, J. & McInerney, F. (2019). Building dementia knowledge globally through the Understanding Dementia Massive Open Online Course (MOOC). npj Science of Learning 4:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0042-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of 4894 DKAS respondents from the 2016–2017 UDMOOCs using multilevel models with random intercepts. Median baseline score was "34.5 (IQR: 27 –41)"; post-course median was 45, with F(1,4886) = 4245.6, p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "The median score post-UDMOOC was 45 (IQR: 41 –48), which was a significant increase on baseline scores ( F(1,4886) = 4245.6, p < 0.001 after adjusting for general education and dementia exposure)."
