`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric (CDM) analysis of assessment grades from a cohort of 178 learners across 21 mapped assessments in an Australian ITE program produced attainment probabilities per standard; the article reports the printed averages, e.g. "Standard 1 was 0.86 ( SD = 0.33)" and "Standard 4 was 0.50 (SD = 0.42)". No effect size is printed.

> "The average level of attainment for Standard 1 was 0.86 ( SD = 0.33), Standard 2 was 0.59 ( SD = 0.43), Standard 3 was 0.68 (SD = 0.40), and Standard 4 was 0.50 (SD = 0.42)."
