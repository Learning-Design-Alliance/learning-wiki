## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, self-rated feelings of preparedness rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in anesthesiology (p < 0.001), urology (p = 0.013) and taking history (p = 0.035), while cardiology, pulmonology, surgery and neurology did not change significantly.
