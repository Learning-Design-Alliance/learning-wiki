### Hart 2020

Hart, H., Young, C., Chen, A., Zou, A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2020). Supporting school improvement: Early findings from reexamination of the 5Essentials survey. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-school-improvement

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

First-phase validation analyses of administrative and survey data from 2011–18 in 535 elementary and 207 high schools, with each of the 22 measures examined separately against improvement in student outcomes. The report concludes: "Overall, the results validate the survey measures as predictors of school improvement."

> "Overall, the results validate the survey measures as predictors of school improvement."

### Hart 2020 (2)

Hart, H., Young, C., Chen, A., Zou, A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2020). Supporting school improvement: Early findings from reexamination of the 5Essentials survey. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-school-improvement

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Results overview across the elementary and high school outcomes. The report states "all measures were not associated with all outcomes" while "nearly all outcomes were positively related to at least one-half" the measures; college enrollment was the exception, related to less than half the measures when considering growth.

> "Notably, all measures were not associated with all outcomes. But nearly all outcomes were positively related to at least one-half the 5Essentials Survey measures."
