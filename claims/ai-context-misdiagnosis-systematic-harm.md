`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is an illustrative hypothetical example constructed by the report authors, not an empirical study; it traces how context-blind AI decisions (e.g., misreading English vocabulary as weak math) could compound when scaled. No effect size is reported.

> "Although this example is only meant to be illustrative, it shows how a misunderstanding of context, amplified through the replication of a decision-making pattern across tens of thousands of students, could systematically harm a student group."
