### Cassandra Paul 2018

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, Mary K. Chessey, and James Lucas. (2018). Pondering zeros: analysis of a decade of blanks and missed quizzes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.09711)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same decade-long observational analysis of UC Davis introductory physics grade databases; first-generation defined as students for whom neither parent had earned a bachelor's degree or higher. The article reports the blank fraction is "about 1.3 times the average over non-ﬁrst-generation" (Fig. 1).

> "The average fraction of blank answers left by students who are the ﬁrst college generation in their family is about 1.3 times the average over non-ﬁrst-generation."
