## Evidence

### Bjarne Skjødt Worm 2013

Bjarne Skjødt Worm. (2013). Learning from Simple Ebooks, Online Cases or Classroom Teaching When Acquiring Complex Knowledge. A Randomized Controlled Trial in Respiratory Physiology and Pulmonology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Login counts measured within Moodle for the two e-learning groups (n = 21 each): eCase averaged 2.8 logins (95% CI 2.4–3.1) versus 1.6 (95% CI 1.3–1.9) for eBook, Mann-Whitney U-test p < 0.001.

> "Group 1b had significantly (p<0.001) more logins (2.8) at the web-application as did group 1a (1.6) (table 4)."
