Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc. (2017). Validation of the 5-item doctor-patient communication competency instrument for medical students (DPCC-MS) using two years of assessment data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1026-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Convergent validity analysis correlating DPCC-MS scores with admission MMI scores for the 242 students who did their MMI in 2009 or 2010, with a two-to-three-year time lag expected. The printed effect size is r = .22: "The Pearson correlation coefficient between DPCC-MS mean scores and MMI scores was .22 (p = .001)".
