## Evidence

### Miri Kheizaran 2024

Miri Kheizaran, Yaghoubi Ali, Kholousi Sadaf, Yousofzadeh Mahdi, Zanganeh Alireza, Gharayi Mehdi, Namazinia Mohammad. (2024). Comparative study on the impact of ‘Infographic versus video feedback’ on enhancing students’ clinical skills in basic life support. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05763-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison of 38 infographic-group and 38 video-feedback-group nursing students; post-training knowledge measured with a 20-item researcher-created BLS questionnaire. The infographic group scored "17.0 ± 1.0" versus 16.5 ± 0.9, p = 0.032; no effect size is printed.

> "Post-training, the infographic group showed a statistically significantly higher CPR knowledge score (17.0 ± 1.0) compared to the video feedback group (16.5 ± 0.9, p = 0.032)."
