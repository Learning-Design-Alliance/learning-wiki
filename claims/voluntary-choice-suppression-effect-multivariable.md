## Evidence

### Yilmaz Merve 2026

Yilmaz Merve, Korkmaz Fatos. (2026). Newly Graduated Nurses' Professional Competency and Caring Perception: A Cross‐Sectional Correlational Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70614

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression analysis among 249 newly graduated nurses. Willingness to choose the nursing profession had OR = 1.44 (95% CI 0.87–2.39, p = 0.15) in the multivariable model, not retaining statistical significance after adjustment for other predictors.

> "The change in the direction and loss of statistical significance observed for voluntary choice of the profession in the multivariable model likely reflects shared variance and suppression effects among correlated predictors."
