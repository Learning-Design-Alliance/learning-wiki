## Evidence

### McEwan D. 2017

McEwan D., Ruissen G.R., Eys M.A., Zumbo B.D., Beauchamp M.R. (2017). The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training on Teamwork Behaviors and Team Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Controlled Interventions. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169604

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 39 interventions from 33 controlled studies assessing teamwork as the outcome. The pooled effect was "significant, medium-to-large" (d = 0.683, 95% CI 0.43–0.94); after excluding two outlier studies the effect was d = 0.550 (95% CI 0.39–0.71). Fail-safe N was 3598 and Egger's test was non-significant (p = 0.78).

> "The results revealed that teamwork interventions had a significant, medium-to-large effect on teamwork, d (SE) = 0.683 (0.13), 95% CI = 0.43–0.94, Z = 5.23, p < 0.001; Q (df) = 660.7 (38), I2 = 94.2."
