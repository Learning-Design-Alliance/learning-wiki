`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A conceptual argument advanced in the article's analysis: internal collaboration scripts, the learners' prior knowledge structures about collaborating, shape how external scripts play out in interaction. The article offers this as a conceptual claim rather than an empirical test.

> "In addition, research on collaboration scripts needs to consider the learners' internal collaboration scripts as a further determinant of collaboration behavior."
