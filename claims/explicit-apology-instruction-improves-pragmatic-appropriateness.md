## Evidence

### Rajabi et al. 2015

Shima Rajabi, Akbar Azizifar, & Habib Gowhary. (2015). Investigating the of Explicit Instruction of Apology Speech Act on Pragmatic Development of Iranian EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.53

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests comparing pre- and post-test DCT scores of the 73-participant pre-posttest experimental/control design. Experimental groups at intermediate (t=-8.074, p=.000) and advanced (t=-5.805, p=.000) levels improved significantly; "significant differences are observable at p=.000".

> "significant differences are observable at p=.000 which show that experimental groups at both levels showed significant differences at the post test in comparison to the pre-tests"

### Rajabi et al. 2015 (2)

Shima Rajabi, Akbar Azizifar, & Habib Gowhary. (2015). Investigating the of Explicit Instruction of Apology Speech Act on Pragmatic Development of Iranian EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.53

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing post-test DCT scores between experimental and control groups at both levels (intermediate t=7.544, p=.000; advanced t=8.66, p=.000), showing "significant differences in performing apology speech act between experimental and control groups at both level".

> "The results showed that there were significant differences in performing apology speech act between experimental and control groups at both level because the obtained p values are lower than the accepted level at .05"
