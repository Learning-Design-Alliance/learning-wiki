### Zhorabekova A 2026

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Re-estimation of all four models on the held-out confirmatory half (n = 201), independent of the exploratory factor analysis. The article reports general-factor loadings of .64–.89, ECV = .815, and maximum HTMT = .97 in this independent half.

> "The pattern held: the one-factor model fitted poorly [χ2(65) = 355.6; CFI = .879; RMSEA = .150, 90% CI [.135, .165]; SRMR = .054], whereas the bifactor model fitted best"
