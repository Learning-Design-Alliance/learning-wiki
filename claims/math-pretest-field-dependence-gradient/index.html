Evidence¶

Mery Luz Vega; Christian Hederich M 2015¶

Mery Luz Vega; Christian Hederich M. (2015). The Impact of a Cooperative Learning Program on the Academic Achievement in Mathematics and Language in Fourth Grade Students and its Relation to Cognitive Style. New Approaches in Educational Research 4(2), 84-90. https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.2015.7.124

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pretest ANOVA on math scores by cognitive style subgroup in the 76-student sample (EFT mean 9.5, SD 6.3). The authors say this reflects Hederich's slant that the educational system favors field independent individuals.

"The ANOVA test showed significant differences linked to style groups (F=5,06; p=0,009). Specifically, the post -hoc analysis found a significant difference between field independents (FI) and field dependents (FD) in the DSH test of Tukey (p=0,006)."
