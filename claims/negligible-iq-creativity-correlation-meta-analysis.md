## Evidence

### Kim 2005

Kim, K. H. (2005). Can Only Intelligent People Be Creative? A Meta-Analysis. The Journal of Secondary Gifted Education, XVI(2/3), 57-66. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Can+Only+Intelligent+People+Be+Creative

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Quantitative meta-analysis using Schwarzer's Meta 5.3 with Fisher's z transformation and a random-effects model, synthesizing 447 correlation coefficients from 21 studies (45,880 participants). The weighted mean was "r = .174 (95% CI = .165 – .183)"; the unweighted mean was .137. The author interprets this as a negligible relationship.

> "The mean value of r was .137 (95% CI = .128 – .146), or .174 (95% CI = .165 – .183) after weighting by sample size. Thus, the mean correlation coefficient was judged to be small and statistically significant."
