### Svavarsdóttir MH 2015

Svavarsdóttir MH, Sigurðardóttir ÁK, Steinsbekk A. (2015). How to become an expert educator: a qualitative study on the view of health professionals with experience in patient education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0370-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the same qualitative interview study of 19 cardiac-care health professionals. Participants said novices "may not be capable of individualizing the educational session" despite good theoretical knowledge. Descriptive finding; no effect size reported.

> "It was recognized that some novices have good theoretical knowledge and disseminate a wealth of good information, but they may not be capable of individualizing the educational session or selecting the most relevant information for the patient."
