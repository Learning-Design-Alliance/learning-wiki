## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across the four fitness components, aerobic fitness showed the strongest association with exam performance, followed by muscular endurance, muscular strength, and flexibility. [→ Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018](#hsieh-shu-shih-2018)

## Evidence

### Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018

Hsieh Shu-Shih, Tsai Jia-Ren, Chang Shao-Hsi, Cheng Chih-Fu, Sung Yao-Ting, Hung Tsung-Min (2018). The Relations between 3-year Changes in Physical Fitness and Academic Performance in Nationally Representative Sample of Junior High School Students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-34370-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-component comparison from the adjusted ANOVAs in the nationwide sample of 382,259 students. The article reports "the relationship was strongest for aerobic fitness and weakest for muscular strength and flexibility", with H-H versus N-N score gaps of 23, 20, 15, and 15 points respectively.

> "Results indicated that while all components of fitness were positively associated with exam performance, the relationship was strongest for aerobic fitness and weakest for muscular strength and flexibility."
