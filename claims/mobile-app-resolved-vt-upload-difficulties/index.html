Ebadi 2018¶

Ebadi, S., & Asakereh, A. (2018). Using Voice Thread to Enhance Speaking Accuracy: A Case Study of Iranian EFL Learners. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1198886

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative diary analysis from 5 experimental participants; the authors conclude that using a mobile version of a web-based application can be more efficient, citing Stockwell (2012) on the portability of phones.
