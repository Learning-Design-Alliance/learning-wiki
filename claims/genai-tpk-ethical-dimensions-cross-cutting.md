### Ó Ceallaigh 2026

Ó Ceallaigh, T. J. & Murphy, S. (2026). Teaching the teachers: A systematic review of genAI-specific technological pedagogical knowledge (TPK) in teacher education. Computers and Education Open, 10, 100367. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100367

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Thematic cross-case synthesis of 17 peer-reviewed studies (2022–2025) on teacher educators' engagement with GenAI, conducted per PRISMA with a two-phase coding model. The authors report that ethical concerns "emerged consistently across studies" and were coded as cross-cutting analytical features.

> "Ethical concerns, such as bias, transparency, privacy, authorship, and accountability, emerged consistently across studies and were therefore coded as cross-cutting analytical features rather than isolated topical issues."
