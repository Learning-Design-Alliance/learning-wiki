Comparison of major complication rates before (n = 855) and after (n = 254) the proficiency cutoff; the article reports complications "occurred less frequently after proficiency was attained (19.6% vs 14.6%; P = 0.012)".

> "Evaluation of major morbidity revealed a cutoff for proficiency at 63 RDP procedures, and such complications occurred less frequently after proficiency was attained (19.6% vs 14.6%; P = 0.012; Fig. 2B)."
