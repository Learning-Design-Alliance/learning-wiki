q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical claim in this commentary: the time-saving promise, attributed to Watters (2021), dates to the very beginning of personalised learning yet 'remains always on the point of being realised'; no empirical support is cited for technologies reducing workload.

"There is little evidence that 'personalised learning' technologies have ever done anything of the kind. So what faith can anyone still have in it?"
