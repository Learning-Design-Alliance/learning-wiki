`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of standardized coefficients and confidence intervals across the six bivariate latent growth curve models in the four-wave study of 290 Dutch adolescents, used by the authors to compare which stressor was most strongly associated with each motivation type.

> "A comparison of confidence intervals further confirmed that parental academic pressure had the strongest association with controlled academic motivation, while perceived negative relationships with teachers had the strongest association with autonomous academic motivation."
