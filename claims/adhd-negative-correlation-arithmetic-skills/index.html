Descriptive correlational analysis of the full sample (n = 536) reported in the behavioral results section (Table 2), described as in line with previous meta-analytic research. The article reports "a small negative correlation between ADHD status and all skills" without printing a correlation coefficient for this contrast.

"In line with previous research 36, there was also a small negative correlation between ADHD status and all skills (with the exception of vocabulary)."
