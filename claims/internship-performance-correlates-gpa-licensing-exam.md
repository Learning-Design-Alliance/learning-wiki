### Yun Je-Yeon 2024

Yun Je-Yeon, Ryu Hyunjin, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Hyun Bae, Choi Seung, Park Wan Beom, Bae Eun Jung, Yim Jae-Joon, Myung Sun Jung. (2024). Subtyping of Performance Trajectory During Medical School, Medical Internship, and the First Year of Residency in Training Physicians: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of 888 interns who completed an SNUH internship 2015–2019, with monthly director-scored performance (Cronbach's α 0.915–0.960). The article reports "a significant positive correlation with the medical school GPA (r = 0.194, P < 0.001)" and the licensing written score.

> "the medical internship performance score showed a significant positive correlation with the medical school GPA (r = 0.194, P < 0.001) and score for the written part of the medical licensing examination (r = 0.125, P < 0.001)"
