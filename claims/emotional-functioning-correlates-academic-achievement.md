# Among university students, lower depression and loneliness scores and higher social satisfaction scores are associated with higher academic achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Lower scores for depression and loneliness, and higher scores on all dimensions of social satisfaction, were associated with higher achievement scores (e.g., r = -0.26 with loneliness, r = 0.28 with overall social satisfaction). [→ Bahmani Dena Sadeghi 2018](#bahmani-dena-sadeghi-2018)

## Evidence

### Bahmani Dena Sadeghi 2018

Bahmani Dena Sadeghi, Faraji Parviz, Faraji Robab, Lang Undine E., Holsboer-Trachsler Edith, Brand Serge. (2018). Is emotional functioning related to academic achievement among university students? Results from a cross-sectional Iranian sample. Brazilian Journal of Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1590/1516-4446-2017-2434

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 275 undergraduate medical and nursing students (mean age 21.24 years; 66.1% females) at Ardabil University of Medical Sciences; Pearson correlations showed "higher achievement scores" with lower depression and loneliness and higher social satisfaction (Table 1: r = -0.26 loneliness, r = -0.18 depression, r = 0.28 overall social satisfaction).

> "Lower scores for depression and loneliness, as well as higher scores on all dimensions of social satisfaction, were associated with higher achievement scores."
