## Evidence

### Guan 2026

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis using the provincial AI industry competitiveness index with high vs. low classification, adjusted for the same structural school-level covariates. Regional AI development exerted an independent contextual influence on student AI literacy.

> "Students in high AI-development regions demonstrated higher overall AI literacy levels (𝛽=0.037,𝑆𝐸= 0.007,𝑡(𝑑𝑓= 9,349) = 5.022,𝑝 <0.001), indicating a contextual advantage."

### Guan 2026 (2)

[doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Province-level fixed-effects robustness model. In the high/low classification model the readiness × regional AI development interaction was also non-significant (β = −0.009, p = 0.194), and simple slopes did not differ significantly between high- and low-AI regions.

### Guan 2026 (3)

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Province-level robustness checks across heterogeneous regional environments. Regional AI industry development showed an independent positive association (β = 0.010, p < 0.01), while the readiness interaction remained non-significant, indicating regional context is not a boundary condition for the readiness effect.

> "school AI readiness remained a positive and statistically significant predictor of student AI literacy (𝛽= 0.014,𝑆𝐸= 0.004,𝑡(𝑑𝑓= 940.198) = 3.847,𝑝 <0.001) after controlling for contextual covariates."
