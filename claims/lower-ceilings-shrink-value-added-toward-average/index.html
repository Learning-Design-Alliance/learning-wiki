Alexandra Resch 2014¶

Alexandra Resch, Eric Isenberg. (2014). How Do Test Scores at the Floor and Ceiling Affect Value-Added Estimates? Working Paper 33. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/how-do-test-scores-at-the-floor-and-ceiling-affect-value-added-estimates

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Analysis of data from a large urban district shows a monotonic pattern: "the lower the ceiling, the more the value-added estimates tend to shrink toward the average teacher," attributed to imprecise information in ceiling scores.

"the lower the ceiling, the more the value-added estimates tend to shrink toward the average teacher, because test scores at the ceiling contain imprecise information about students' achievement"
