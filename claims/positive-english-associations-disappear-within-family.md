### Demange Perline A. 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational regressions in the total MoBa sample showed positive associations with English scores (e.g. ß = 0.028 for postnatal alcohol problematic use), but the children-of-siblings analysis showed these do not persist, suggesting confounding rather than causal effects.

> "In any case, both associations disappear in the within-family analysis, suggesting that neither reflects a causal effect of parental symptoms on children’s English scores."
