## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2018

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2018). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: Achieve3000®. https://whatworks.ed.gov

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

One-year quasi-experiment in Bayonne, NJ with 156 students (80 intervention, 76 comparison in four classrooms); WWC calculations showed intervention mean 866.06 vs comparison 817.57, effect size 0.29, improvement index +11, original p < .01 but WWC-computed p = .41 after clustering correction.

> "The authors used the Scholastic Reading Inventory (SRI) assessment for students in grade 5. The outcome was reviewed in the comprehension domain."
