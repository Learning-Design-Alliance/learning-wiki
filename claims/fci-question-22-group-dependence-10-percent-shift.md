## Evidence

### Stewart et al. 2007

John Stewart, Heather Griffin, and Gay Stewart. (2007). Context sensitivity in the force concept inventory. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 3, 010102. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.3.010102

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analysis of the single largest per-question shift. The authors attribute the large shift primarily to removal of question 22 from its group, with question 21 providing additional contextual information, rather than to the figure removal also applied. No effect size is printed.
