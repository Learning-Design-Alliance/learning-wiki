> "rated their knowledge regarding women's nutritional needs at various stage of life as excellent (n = 3; 1.2%); very good (n = 42; 16.3%); good (n = 30; 11.6%); adequate (n = 81; 31.4%); poor (n = 85; 32.9%); and very poor (n = 17; 6.6%)."
