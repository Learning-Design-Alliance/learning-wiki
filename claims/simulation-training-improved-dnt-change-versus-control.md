## Evidence

### Svobodová Veronika 2023

Svobodová Veronika, Maršálková Hana, Volevach Ekaterina, Mikulík Robert. (2023). Simulation-based team training improves door-to-needle time for intravenous thrombolysis. BMJ Open Quality 12(1):e002107. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2022-002107

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nationwide cohort comparison of 17,134 thrombolysed patients from 41 certified Czech stroke centres, using prospectively collected SITS registry data. The behavioural-change (Kirkpatrick level 3) analysis found "improved their DNT in 2018 as compared with 2015 by 30 min" in trained centres versus 20 min in untrained centres (p=0.01).

> "Stroke centres that passed simulation training improved their DNT in 2018 as compared with 2015 by 30 min as compared with 20 min in stroke centres that did not pass simulation training in a simulation centre (p=0.01)."
