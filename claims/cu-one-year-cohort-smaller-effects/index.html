Stephen Gorard 2017¶

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See, Emma Smith, Patrick White. (2017). Children's University: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581159

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Additional findings from the RCT's secondary analysis of the one-year Year 6 volunteer cohort. The report states the smaller effects "suggest that children may benefit from longer involvement in CU".

"Children receiving CU for one year (those in Year 6) made a small gain in maths, but less progress in reading, compared to children in control schools. The smaller size of these effects compared to the results for the Year 5 cohort (that received CU for two years) suggests that children may benefit from longer involvement in CU."
