## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, Megan, Soland, James, Pitts, Christine, & Burchinal, Margaret. (2019). Trends in Children's Academic Skills at School Entry: 2010 to 2017. EdWorkingPaper No. 19-137, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. http://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai19-137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unconditional three-level HLM fit separately by subject to quantify within- and between-district variation in 2010 scores and in score trends. The article reports "less of the variance in the linear slope across time is between districts," with trend ICCs of .35 (math) and .39 (reading).

> "The district ICC for scores in 2010 is .47 in reading and .48 in math. That is to say, 47% of variation in reading scores in 2010 is between districts while 53% is between schools within districts."
