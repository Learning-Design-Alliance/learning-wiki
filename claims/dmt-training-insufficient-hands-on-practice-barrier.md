## Evidence

### Mariño Rodrigo 2025

Mariño Rodrigo, Dziaruddin Nabihah, Kadir Kathreena, Sim Christina P. C., Quah Bernadette, Chompu‐inwai Papimon, Sirimaharaj Varisara. (2025). Dental and Maxillofacial Trauma Skills Content in the Southeast Asian Dental Curricula: A Mixed Methods Study. Dental Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1111/edt.13048

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 2 semi-structured interviews with eight academics from the four participating countries, analysed thematically. Interviewees "highlighted a disproportionate focus on theoretical contents over practical exposure," which they said limits students' ability to gain practical experience and affects proficiency in addressing trauma cases clinically.

> "In our dental course, the training for maxillofacial trauma (DMT) is mostly theoretical, with lectures, case discussions and seminars, but without enough hands-on practice."
