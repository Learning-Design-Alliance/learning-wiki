`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary outcome comparison in the same prospective cohort study of 33 proficient and 47 expert participants. Expert mean exercise times were less than proficient times on all five laparoscopic exercises, e.g. L-4 178 (±71) vs 402 (±156) seconds, supporting the principal secondary construct-validity comparison.

> "For those in the expert group, exercise times were less than for the proficient participants: L-1, 138 (±30) vs 199 (±50); L-2, 164 (±51) vs 245 (±51); L-3, 182 (±62) vs 294 (±104); L-4, 178 (±71) vs 402 (±156); and L-5, 239 (±76) vs 386 (±135)."
