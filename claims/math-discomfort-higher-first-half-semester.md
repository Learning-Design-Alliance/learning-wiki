## Evidence

### Giaco Corsiglia 2020

Giaco Corsiglia, Tyler Garcia, Benjamin P. Schermerhorn, Gina Passante, Homeyra Sadaghiani, and Steven Pollock. (2020). Characterizing and monitoring student discomfort in upper-division quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.corsiglia](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.corsiglia)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of weekly survey explanations at University A found math discomfort concentrated in the first half of the semester. Interviewees at University B echoed this, pointing to less familiarity with linear algebra and matrix manipulation than with integration and differential equations at the start of the course.

> "We also found that more students reported discomfort with the mathematics in the first half of the semester, despite the fact that the mathematics required in the second half tends to be more computationally intensive."
