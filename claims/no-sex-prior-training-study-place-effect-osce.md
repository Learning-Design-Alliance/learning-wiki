## Evidence

### Störmann S 2016

Störmann S, Stankiewicz M, Raes P, Berchtold C, Kosanke Y, Illes G, Loose P, Angstwurm MW. (2016). How well do final year undergraduate medical students master practical clinical skills? GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001057](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001057)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sub-group comparisons of total OSCE score among the 214 participants: the female-versus-male contrast was not significant (p=0.069); the article also reports no significant differences for studying at LMU Munich from the beginning (p=0.349) or prior healthcare training (p=0.363).

> "Female participants had a tendency towards slightly higher scores; however, this difference was not significant (73.7%±10.1% versus 71.1%±9.9%; p=0.069)."
