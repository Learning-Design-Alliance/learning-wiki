# Robot-assisted digital storytelling improves hospitalized children's therapeutic communication about intravenous injections more than video-based health education

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ching-Yi Chang 2023

Ching-Yi Chang, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Ya-Lien Chou, Zi-Yin Xu, & Hsiu-Ju Jen. (2023). Effects of robot-assisted digital storytelling on hospitalized children's communication during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10209-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled experiment in a regional hospital children's ward: experimental group (n = 21) used robot-assisted digital storytelling, control group (n = 26) received video-based health education. The experimental group "obtained higher scores on therapeutic communication than the control group" (mean = 3.24 vs 2.65).

> "There was a significant difference in therapeutic communication between the two groups (t = 2.09, p < .05), with a medium effect size (d = 0.64)."
