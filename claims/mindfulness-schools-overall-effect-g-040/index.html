Evidence¶

Charlotte Zenner 2014¶

Charlotte Zenner, Solveig Herrnleben-Kurz and Harald Walach. (2014). Mindfulness-based interventions in schools—a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00603

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Quantitative synthesis (inverse variance random-effects model) of 24 studies of school-based mindfulness interventions, 1348 students instructed in mindfulness and 876 controls. The review reports "Overall effect sizes were Hedge' s g = 0.40 between groups and g = 0.41 within groups ( p < 0.0001)" — a small-to-medium pooled effect.
