## Evidence

### Berger Laurenz 2025

Berger Laurenz, Civilla Lorenzo, Dodier Philippe, Rössler Karl, Moscato Francesco. (2025). Smartwatch-based wrist tremor assessment in neurosurgical simulator training. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-08244-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Small quasi-experimental cohort study of nine neurosurgical residents performing simulated aneurysm clipping on two days, with three training cohorts formed by stratified randomization. Post hoc comparisons showed the Video + AR group had "reductions of 56% and 69%" in full-bandwidth total and peak power; no effect size was printed.

> "Post hoc analyses revealed that the total and peak power of the full tremor bandwidth were significantly reduced in the Video + AR group from Day 0 to Day 14 (p = 0.0186 and p = 0.0021, respectively), with reductions of 56% and 69%, respectively."
