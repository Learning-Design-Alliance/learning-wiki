School-level application of the Difference method: each school's Difference comes from the median mental and educational indices of all its children, which decreases the spread of difference values. No effect size is printed.

> "In all, 56 different schools were tested and the middle 50 per cent show differences from plus 3 to minus 3. For schools or classes a difference of medians above or below 3 becomes significant."
