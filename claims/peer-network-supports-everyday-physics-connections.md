### Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle 2019

Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle. (2019). An uncommon case of relevance through everyday experiences. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.nair](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.nair)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview evidence from the case study: conversations with an engineering friend about weightlifting as levers and pulleys, bike-gear forces, a biking-direction momentum debate, and falling icicles all prompted Sam's everyday physics reasoning. Descriptive interview data, no effect size.

> "I actually would credit it to my friend, he's an engineer... because he sees physics in everyday life more than I do. And so when he'll bring it up, it'll make the wheels in my head turn."
