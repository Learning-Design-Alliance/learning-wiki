## Evidence

### Yanjia Li 2026

Yanjia Li, Kelcy Kexin Han, Tianrui Hu, Yi-Fan Cao, Huamin Qu, Sicheng Song. (2026). SupplyNet: Supporting Visual Exploratory Learning in Supply Chain via Contextual Multi-Agent Simulation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24694 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.24694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.24694)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of user-study participants' behavioral patterns. Beginners (P4, P8-10, P14) preferred visual encodings and the Timeline Tree Map; advanced participants (P3, P12) preferred the Analysis Console. Qualitative findings; no effect sizes.

> "While participants universally valued the learning support inte- grated within the system, our study revealed that the most effec- tive forms of support differed significantly based on the user’s level of expertise."
