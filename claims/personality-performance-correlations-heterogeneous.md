## Evidence

### Poropat 2009

Poropat, A. E. (2009). A Meta-Analysis of the Five-Factor Model of Personality and Academic Performance. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014996

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Initial random-effects meta-analysis (Hunter & Schmidt method) computing Cochran's Q and I2 for each FFM dimension and intelligence, with credibility values and Q calculated on correlations corrected for internal reliability. The author reports that "most of the variation between samples is systematic".

> "The significant Q statistics for correlations with each of the FFM factors, as well as intelligence, indicate the presence of heterogeneity among the correlations, while the values for I2 show that most of the variation between samples is systematic."
