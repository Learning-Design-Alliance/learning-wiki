## Evidence

### Park 2025

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quantile-regression moderation analysis (sample likely too small to be well-powered for interactions, per the authors): baseline WJ-IV Science interacted with condition on willingness-to-pay; the main effect of WJ-IV Science was b = 0.40, p = .001. No standardized effect size printed.

> "We found a significant interaction between condition and baseline science knowledge on willingness-to-pay (Figure 3A, WJ-IV Science x Condition: b = -0.40, 95% CI [-0.70, -0.10], t(97) = -2.65, p = .009"
