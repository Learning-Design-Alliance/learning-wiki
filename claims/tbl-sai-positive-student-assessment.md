## Evidence

### Ngoc Lan Duong Thi 2025

Ngoc Lan Duong Thi, Quynh Anh Nguyen Ngoc, Hoang Thi Phuong Thao. (2025). A pre – post quasi-experimental study of team-based learning effectiveness for Vietnamese nursing students. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323656

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course administration of the 33-item TBL-SAI (Cronbach's alpha 0.941) to 186 nursing students; results reported descriptively in the results section as means ± SD with no inferential test or effect size.

> "The average student assessment score on TBL (TBL-SAI) is 124.83 ± 10.068, of which, the average score on accountability is 31.19 ± 2.975, the average score for enjoyment of lectures or studying in class is 57.10 ± 5.279, and the average score for student satisfaction is 36.54 ± 3.815."
