Mejia-Domenzain, P., Laini, E., Neshaei, S. P., Wambsganss, T., and Käser, T. (2023). Visualizing Self-Regulated Learner Profiles in Dashboards: Design Insights from Teachers. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-36336-8_96](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-36336-8_96)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Clustering analysis of clickstream log data from 292 students in an undergraduate mathematics flipped-classroom course. The pipeline integrated per-dimension patterns into five profiles; "the best-performing profile had higher Control, lower intensity Effort, constant intensity (Consistency), and up-to-date Proactivity".

> "We applied the pipeline to log data collected from 292 students (29% identifying as female and 71% as male) of an undergraduate mathematics FC course [4]. After the first clustering step, we obtained different patterns for the five di- mensions"
