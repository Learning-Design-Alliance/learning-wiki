A comparative predictive validity study commissioned by Massachusetts examined whether PARCC and MCAS high school exam scores predict college grades, finding "similar to the validity of scores on the MCAS." No effect size is printed.

"The validity of scores on PARCC assessments in predicting college grades is similar to the validity of scores on the MCAS."
