# The problem posing learning model did not significantly raise students' learning achievement compared with control instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the quasi-experimental study, the learning achievement of students taught with the problem posing model was not significantly higher than that of control students at post-test. [→ Christidamayani 2020](#christidamayani-2020)

## Evidence

### Christidamayani 2020

Christidamayani, A. P., & Kristanto, Y. D. (2020). The Effects of Problem Posing Learning Model on Students' Learning Achievement and Motivation. Indonesian Journal on Learning and Advanced Education, 2(2), 100-108. https://doi.org/10.23917/ijolae.v2i2.9981

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-tailed Mann-Whitney U test compared post-test achievement of the problem posing experimental group and the control group in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports the model "seems to have a positive effect on the student learning achievement, but the effect is not significant"; hypothesis 1 was not supported.

> "Therefore, although the problem posing learning model seems to have a positive effect on the student learning achievement, but the effect is not significant."
