`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis on a selected subset of video-recorded assessments in the pilot RCT, using intraclass correlation coefficients with the article's stated thresholds (0.75-0.9 good, >0.9 excellent). The article states high reliability strengthens the validity of the results.

> "There was excellent intra-rater reliability for assessor A (n = 10) (ICC [1,1] = 0.98; P < 0.001) and very good intra-rater reliability for assessor B (n = 10) (ICC [1,1] = 0.79; P = 0.034). Inter-rater reliability showed equally significant results: Assessor A vs B (n = 10) (ICC [2,1] = 0.92, P < 0.001)."
