## Evidence

### Zheng 2026

Zheng, Y., Lo, T. W., Lau, E. Y., Eskes, G., Hui, L. L., & Hsiao, J. H. (2026). The Role of Eye Movement Consistency in Aging-Related Decline in Face Recognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00437-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analysis of 301 middle-to-older adults performing a face recognition task with eye tracking. The article reports higher overall entropy and C3 entropy with age; only the C3 entropy association (r(286) = .142, p = .019) survived controlling aging-related cognitive abilities, so subsequent analysis focused on C3 entropy.

> "it was associated with lower eye movement consistency, as reflected in higher overall entropy (r(297) = .1 66, p = .00 4, padjusted = .008) and C3 entropy (r(297) = .17 0, p = .00 3, p adjusted = .0 06, Figure 1C)"
