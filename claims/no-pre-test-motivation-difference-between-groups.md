## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An independent-samples t-test on pre-test motivation scores found no statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups (t = -.011, Sig. .99). [→ Salimi and Larsari 2015](#salimi-and-larsari-2015)

## Evidence

### Salimi and Larsari 2015

Asghar Salimi, Vahid Nowrozi Larsari. (2015). On the Comparative Impact of Self-assessment and Teacher-assessment on Iranian EFL Learners' Academic Motivation. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.74

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on pre-test AMTB scores of both 30-learner groups, with Levene's test (Sig. .04) leading the authors to use the equal-variances-not-assumed row; the groups were equivalent before treatment. No effect size is printed.

> "the level of significance of the t-test (-.011) for the equality of means is .99  that is higher than the level of significance .05, indicating that the differences between the mean scores of both experimental and control groups in the pre-test are not statistically significant."
