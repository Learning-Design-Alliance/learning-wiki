Assessment embedded in the Exploring GANs activity, in which students explored up to four web-based GAN tools for 10 minutes and identified each tool's generator and discriminator. "Of the 58 responses, 65% of student responses were able to correctly identify what the generator was trying to generate"; percentages are descriptive.

> "Of the 58 responses, 65% of student responses were able to correctly identify what the generator was trying to generate, and 61% of student responses were able to correctly identify the dataset that the discriminator was trained on."
