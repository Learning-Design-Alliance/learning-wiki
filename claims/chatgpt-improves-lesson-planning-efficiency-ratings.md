## Evidence

### Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire survey of the 13 teachers using a 5-point Likert scale on ChatGPT's impact on teaching efficiency. The article reports descriptive averages: "the reduction of lesson planning time (average score: 4.2)", content generation 3.8, topic selection 3.7; these are descriptive ratings, not tested effects.

> "the accelerated speed of topic selection (average score: 3.7), the rapid generation of high-quality instructional content (average score: 3.8), and the reduction of lesson planning time (average score: 4.2)."
