### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA examining effects of Time, Group, and their interaction on examination scores. The "significant Time × Group interaction" with partial η² = 0.12 indicates the experimental group retained knowledge better over time while the control group's scores declined after the immediate post-class assessment.

> "A repeated-measures ANOVA revealed a significant Time × Group interaction (F(2, 104) = 7.11, P = 0.001, partial η² = 0.12). Post-hoc comparisons showed that while both groups improved from Test 1 to Test 2, the control group's scores declined at the final examination (85.56) to near their Test 1 level (84.74), whereas the experimental group maintained higher scores (94.82)."
