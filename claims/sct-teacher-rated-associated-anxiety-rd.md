# Teacher-reported SCT is positively associated with teacher-reported anxiety in children with reading disorder, cross-sectionally and longitudinally

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an adjusted cross-sectional linear regression among children with reading disorder, teacher-reported SCT was positively associated with teacher-reported anxiety (p = .001). [→ Hossain Bushra 2022](#hossain-bushra-2022)
`q2 i?` In adjusted longitudinal mixed-effects models, a one-point increase in teacher-reported SCT corresponded to a .15-point increase in teacher-reported anxiety within an individual over time (p < .001). [→ Hossain Bushra 2022 (2)](#hossain-bushra-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Hossain Bushra 2022

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional adjusted linear regression at baseline in a cohort of 147 children with RD recruited from three specialized schools. Teacher-reported SCT remained significantly associated with teacher-reported anxiety, "after controlling for age and sex of the participant, time at school, and the interaction term with ADHD-IN (p = .001)."

> "This positive association remained statistically significant after controlling for age and sex of the participant, time at school, and the interaction term with ADHD-IN (p = .001)."

### Hossain Bushra 2022 (2)

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted longitudinal linear mixed-effects models (16 models in total, with subject and school as random effects) tracking within-individual change over up to five timepoints. The article reports that "every one-point increase in the SCT scale score was significantly associated with a .15-point increase in the anxiety scale score within an individual."

> "teacher-reported SCT remained significantly and positively associated with teacher-reported anxiety, whereby every one-point increase in the SCT scale score was significantly associated with a .15-point increase in the anxiety scale score within an individual"
