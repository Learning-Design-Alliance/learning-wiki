## Evidence

### Traxler 2015

Traxler, A. L., Cid, X. C., Blue, J., Barthelemy, R. (2015). Enriching gender in PER: A binary past and a complex future. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports Docktor and Heller's analysis of FCI scores for more than 5000 students, of whom 20% are women, taken over a 10-year period. The quoted gaps and gains are as printed; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "They found a consistent gap averaging over 15% in pretest scores, and another consistent gap of greater than 13% in post-test scores. Both men and women made gains representing approximately six questions on the FCI during the term"
