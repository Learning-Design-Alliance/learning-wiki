# Student talk time correlates positively with reading literacy achievement (r = 0.18)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Individual talk time showed a modest positive partial correlation with reading literacy test success (r = 0.18, p < 0.01). [→ Sedova 2023](#sedova-2023)

## Evidence

### Sedova 2023

Sedova, K., & Sedlacek, M. (2023). How vocal and silent forms of participation in combination relate to student achievement. Instructional Science, 51, 343–361. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09609-1

`q2 · i1`

Pearson (partial) correlation analysis among key variables in the sample of 639 ninth-graders; talk time averaged approximately 12 s per lesson. The article characterizes the association as not strong but not marginal, since achievement is multifactorial.

> "The second strongest relationship was identified between student achievement and student talk time (r = 0.18; p < 0.01). Although these are not strong associations, they cannot be marginalized, because learning outcomes are undoubtedly a multifactor phenomenon."
