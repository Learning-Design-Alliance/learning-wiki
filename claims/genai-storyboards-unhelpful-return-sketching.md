Hauke Sandhaus, Maria Teresa Parreira, and Wendy Ju. (2024). Student Reflections on Self-Initiated GenAI Use in HCI Education. LLMs as Research Tools Workshop at CHI conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Student reflections in §3.3.2 on storyboard generation: GenAI use consumed time attempting to generate desired outcomes rather than quick iteration, and one student described how hand sketching prompts thinking: "Like, why am I placing it here?"

> "However, they found that the storyboards generated by GenAI were unhelpful, prompting a return to traditional sketching with pen and paper."
