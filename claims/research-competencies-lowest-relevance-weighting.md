### Udoh Arit 2018

Udoh Arit, Bruno Andreia, Bates Ian. (2018). A survey of pharmacists’ perception of foundation level competencies in African countries. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0280-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Overall rating pattern from the cross-sectional survey of 469 African pharmacists rating all 100 GbCF v1 behaviours; the article reports care and public health clusters "received higher weighting on relevance" than research-related competencies.

> "In general, the competencies within the ‘pharmaceutical care’ and ‘pharmaceutical public health’ clusters received higher weighting on relevance compared to the research-related competencies which had the lowest."
