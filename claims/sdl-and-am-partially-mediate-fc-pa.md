### Yang Hechong 2026

Yang Hechong, Cheng Shuaichao, Cheng Guangxin. (2026). The impact of the flipped classroom model on college students' physical exercise behavior: a chain mediation effect of self-directed learning ability and exercise motivation. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1804884

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Structural equation modeling with bias-corrected bootstrap (5,000 resamples) tested the chained mediation pathway in the quasi-experimental sample. Indirect effects via SDL ability alone (0.07) and AM alone (0.06) were both significant, with SDL playing the primary mediating role.

> "The indirect effect via SDL ability alone was 0.07 (95% CI [0.03, 0.13]), via AM alone was 0.06 (95% CI [0.02, 0.11]), and the chained indirect effect via SDL ability then AM was 0.04 (95% CI [0.01, 0.08]). All bootstrap CIs excluded zero, indicating statistically significant partial mediation."
