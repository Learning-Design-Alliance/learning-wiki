Ibrahim 2017¶

Ibrahim, H. G. (2017). Utilization of Innovations and Techniques of Educational Technology in Delivering of Educational Practicum and its Impact on Increasing Academic Achievement among Pre-Service Teachers. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(8), 185-194. https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1142431.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-post comparison within the experimental group at the remembering level, reported in Table (7). The article reports the calculated T value as 82.22, greater than the tabulated value, with the post-application mean higher than the pre-application mean.

"This means that there are statistically significant differences between the score of the experimental group in the pre and post application of the achievement test at the level of remembering, in favor of the post-application, thus the first experimental hypothesis of the current study is rejected."

Pre-post comparison within the experimental group at the application level, reported in Table (9). The article reports the calculated T value as 15.50, with the post-application mean (44.6) greater than the pre-application mean (1.75).

"This means that there are statistically significant differences between the mean scores of students in the pre-and-post application at the level of application, in favor of the post-application. Thus, the third hypothesis of the current study is rejected."
