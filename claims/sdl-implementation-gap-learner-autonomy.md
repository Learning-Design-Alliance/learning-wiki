### Wilkes S. 2026

Wilkes S., Maggio L. A., Martin P. C., Melton J., Zheng B. (2026). Self-Directed Learning in Health Professions Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2128

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic characterization of SDL implementation across 125 included studies, coding teacher role, learner resource selection, and learner self-assessment. The authors describe this as "a persistent gap between SDL as theorized and SDL as implemented."

> "Although many interventions were labeled as SDL, only 21% of studies involved learners in resource selection and 25% in assessment, both of which are core components of Knowles' definition."
