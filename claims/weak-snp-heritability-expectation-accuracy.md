### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Genome-wide complex trait analysis (GCTA-GREML) of teacher expectation accuracy among unrelated ALSPAC participants of European ancestry, controlling for the first 20 principal components. The article states it "found weak evidence for SNP heritability of teacher expectation accuracy at both ages".

> "SNP heritability was estimated at 6.5% (95% CI − 6.7%, 19.6%) for Key Stage 2 and 14% for Key Stage 3 (95% CI − 4.6%, 33.4%) after adjustment for principal components of ancestry."
