# Tenacity and self-composure scores increase with age in university students in both studies

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Pearson correlations between age and tenacity (r = .235 Study 1; r = .227 Study 2) and self-composure (r = .239 Study 1; r = .227 Study 2) were significant at p < .001, indicating an age-related trend. [→ Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020](#kannangara-chathurika-sewwandi-2020)
`q3 i2` Study 2 replicated the age-related trend with correlations of r = .227 for both factors at p < .001. [→ Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020 (2)](#kannangara-chathurika-sewwandi-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020

Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi, Allen Rosie Elizabeth, Carson Jerome Francis, Khan Samia Zahraa Noor, Waugh Gill, Kandadi Kondal Reddy. (2020). Onwards and upwards: The development, piloting and validation of a new measure of academic tenacity- The Bolton Uni-Stride Scale (BUSS). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235157

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlational analysis in Study 1 (n=1117) relating age to BUSS factor scores. Printed effect sizes r = .235 (tenacity) and r = .239 (self-composure) at p < .001, which the authors describe as "a linear relationship between age and tenacity (.235) and self-composure (.239) score".

> "In addition, Pearson’s correlational analysis identified a linear relationship between age and tenacity (.235) and self-composure (.239) score at p < .001. Such that, findings reflected an age-related trend, as the mean tenacity and self-composure scores increased with age."

### Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020 (2)

Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi, Allen Rosie Elizabeth, Carson Jerome Francis, Khan Samia Zahraa Noor, Waugh Gill, Kandadi Kondal Reddy. (2020). Onwards and upwards: The development, piloting and validation of a new measure of academic tenacity- The Bolton Uni-Stride Scale (BUSS). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235157

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlational analysis in Study 2 (n=340) replicating the Study 1 age trend. Printed effect size r = .227 for both tenacity and self-composure at p < .001, which the authors report as "a linear relationship between age and tenacity (.227) and self-composure (.227) score".

> "Likewise, Pearson’s correlational analysis identified a linear relationship between age and tenacity (.227) and self-composure (.227) score at p < .001. These findings concur with that of study 1, suggesting an age-related trend as the mean tenacity and self-composure scores increased with age."
