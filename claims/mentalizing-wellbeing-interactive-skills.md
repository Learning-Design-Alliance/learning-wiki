### Støve 2026

Støve, L. L., Dalgaard, N. T., Mohr, J. W., Hansen, J. E., Wendelboe, K. I., Hestbaek, E., Vieira, C. C., Væver, M. S., & Smith-Nielsen, J. (2026). `q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-analyses: mentalizing (k = 4, N = 301, r = .166, p = .013) and mental well-being (k = 7, N = 1253, r = .084, p = .001). Under RVE, "the association between mentalizing abilities and interactive skills became non-significant, whereas the link with mental well-being remained significant."

> "The meta-analyses revealed significant but small, positive correlations between child-care professionals' mentalizing abilities, i.e., the ability to understand and interpret children's emotions, intentions, and perspectives (Fonagy & Allison, 2012), mental well-being and caregiver interactive skills (see Table 5)."
