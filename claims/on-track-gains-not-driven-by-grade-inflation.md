## Evidence

### Roderick 2014

Roderick, M., Kelley-Kemple, T., Johnson, D.W., and Beechum, N.O. (2014). Preventable Failure: Improvements in Long-Term Outcomes when High Schools Focused on the Ninth Grade Year. UChicago CCSR Research Summary. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/preventable-failure-improvements-long-term-outcomes-when-high-schools-focused-ninth

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grade-distribution analysis in the 17 secondary-mover schools. The summary reports Ds/Fs "declined significantly, from 33 to 22 percent" and Bs-or-better rose from 28 to 37 percent, with a 10-point rise in Bs-or-better and 12-point rise in students with no Fs.

> "The portion of students with D's or F's declined significantly, from 33 to 22 percent, while the percentage of students with B's or better increased from 28 to 37 percent. Thus, there is little evidence, on average, that the increase in on-track rates in these schools was driven by simply focusing on turning F's into D's"
