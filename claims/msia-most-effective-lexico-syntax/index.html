Evidence¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA of lexico-syntax subtest scores in the 120-student quasi-experiment. The article reports "the calculated f value which is 16.282 was significant"; no effect size is named in the reporting text.

"Table 9 shows that the calculated f value which is 16.282 was significant at p>0.05.The hypothesis therefore rejected, this result indicates that the instructional approaches had significant effect on the lexico-syntax achievement of the underachieving students."

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012 (2)¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Duncan test on lexico-syntax means (Table 10): MSIA 8.67 (A); MSIA&MCIA 7.00, CIA 6.67, MCIA 6.30 (B), 30 cases per group.

"The MSIA is rated ‘A’ which indicates that MSIA is the most eff ective of the four approaches in the lexico-syntax achievement of the students. This is follo wed by the combination of MSIA+MCIA, MCIA and CIA, which all fall within the same grouping of ‘B’."
