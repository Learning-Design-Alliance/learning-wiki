## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jairo Navarrete 2024

Jairo Navarrete, Valentina Giaconi, Gonzalo Contador, Mariano Vazquez. (2024). Another reason why normalized gain should continue to be used to analyze concept inventories (and estimate learning rates). arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.07730](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.07730)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical development in Sections IV-V derives that F* statistically depends on X* through its definition, inducing the correlation; the article concludes observing it "does not imply that the ngain transformation (estimating a learning rate) is biased".

> "Furthermore, even when the LR-Model assumed independence between the pretest ( X) and the learning rate ( F ), these error contributions induced a spurious negative correlation between the noisy versions of the pretest ( X ∗) and the learning rate ( F ∗)."
