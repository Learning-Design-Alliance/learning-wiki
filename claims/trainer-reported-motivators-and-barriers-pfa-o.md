## Evidence

### Saguin Emeric 2024

Saguin Emeric, Salomé Julia, Favodon Benoît, Lahutte Bertrand, Gignoux-Froment Frédérique. (2024). Validation of a didactic model evaluating the usability, usefulness and acceptability of psychological first aid teaching through simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06491-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative semantic-category analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses from 42 participants in the cross-sectional survey. The article reports "40.48% (n = 17) mentioned the value of financial incentives" and "35.71% (n = 15)" partner choice; percentages are descriptive counts, no effect size printed.

> "Among the most common categories, 40.48% (n = 17) mentioned the value of financial incentives (compensation for trainers), 35.71% (n = 15) stressed the importance of being able to choose their partner for training in order to coordinate with them beforehand, 19.05% (n = 8) highlighted the importance of institutional recognition for conducting introductory training (expression of satisfaction, medals, etc.)."

### Saguin Emeric 2024 (2)

Saguin Emeric, Salomé Julia, Favodon Benoît, Lahutte Bertrand, Gignoux-Froment Frédérique. (2024). Validation of a didactic model evaluating the usability, usefulness and acceptability of psychological first aid teaching through simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06491-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative analysis of open-ended responses from 39 participants on factors limiting dissemination. The article reports "lack of time or scheduling conflicts (n = 20, 51.28%)" as the most frequent barrier, followed by lack of trainee awareness at 35.90%; descriptive frequencies only.

> "The following ideas were found in decreasing order of frequency: (1) lack of time or scheduling conflicts (n = 20, 51.28%), (2) lack of prior knowledge among potential trainees about the training courses and how they are carried out (n = 14, 35.90%), (3) lack of trainers (n = 11, 28.21%)."
