Jiang 2016¶

Jiang, J. Y., & Sporte, S. E. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Differences in Observation and Value-Added Scores by Teacher, Student, and School Characteristics. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562786

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 2013-14 REACH scores by teacher race/ethnicity, adjusted for the schools teachers work in. The report attributes the observation-score gap largely to school-level poverty, as minority teachers are overrepresented in the highest-poverty schools.

"There were no significant differences by teacher race/ethnicity on either reading or math value-added scores."
