## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, the proportion of accurate blood loss estimates (within 5% of actual volume) rose from 7% to 24% after use of a pictorial guide, following pre-guide overestimation of more than 25%. [→ Mahoney B 2021](#mahoney-b-2021)

## Evidence

### Mahoney B 2021

Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Homcha and colleagues evaluated a pictorial guide for blood loss assessment during simulated cesarean delivery, comparing estimates before and after guide use. The review reports the guide "revealed an increase from 7% to 24% of accurate estimation"; no effect size is printed.

> "Prior to use of the pictorial guide, they observed a more than 25% overestimation of blood loss, whereas use of the guide revealed an increase from 7% to 24% of accurate estimation defined as an estimate within 5% of the actual volume lost."
