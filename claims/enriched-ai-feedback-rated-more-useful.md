# Students receiving emotionally enriched AI feedback rate the feedback as more useful than students receiving neutral feedback, in both moderation and creation tasks

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the moderation task, the experimental group rated AI feedback significantly higher than the control group (p <0.001, r = 0.289). [→ Omar Alsaiari 2025](#omar-alsaiari-2025)

## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2025

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of 1-5 star helpfulness ratings of AI feedback in the moderation task within the RiPPLE RCT: control Mrank = 139.79 (N=91) versus experimental Mrank = 190.74 (N=103), "statistically significant (p <0.001)" with r = 0.289.

> "the experimental group gave a higher Mrank = 190.74, (N=103). This difference was statistically significant (p <0.001) and had a moderate effect size (r = 0.289)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2025 (2)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of feedback helpfulness ratings in the creation task of the same RCT: control Mrank = 43.89 (N=54) versus experimental Mrank = 54.43 (N=42), "statistically significant (p = 0.040)", r = 0.206.

> "The control group’s Mrank = 43.89 (N=54), compared to the experimental group’s higher Mrank = 54.43 (N=42). This result was also statistically significant (p = 0.040) and demonstrated a small to medium effect size (r = 0.206)."
