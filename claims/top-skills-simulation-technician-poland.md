Nationwide survey of simulation center employees rating technician skills; the article reports the percentages of respondents valuing each skill and confirms the hierarchy with mean scores such as "Coping when things go off-plan (M = 4.75)".

> "The most valued skills were the ability to cope with technical or organizational failures (77%), operate equipment (77%), and use computers effectively (75%). In contrast, acting ability and equipment repair were most often rated as low-importance skills."
