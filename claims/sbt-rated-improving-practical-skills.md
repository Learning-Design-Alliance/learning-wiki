## Evidence

### Pillong Lukas 2025

Pillong Lukas, Sprengart Franziska Marietta, Recker Florian, et al. (2025). Reflecting realities: gauging the pulse of simulator-based training in medical minds—resonance of simulator-based ultrasound training in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07198-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likert-scale (1–7) ratings of SBT benefits in the 343-participant survey, shown as rain- and violin plots (Fig. 3). "median scores between 4.79 (third-year students) and 6.49 (PJ students) for practical skills"; theory-transfer medians ranged 5.86–6.19. These are descriptive medians, not tested effect sizes.

> "all groups reported an improvement in practical skills and the transfer of theoretical knowledge into practice, with median scores between 4.79 (third-year students) and 6.49 (PJ students) for practical skills (Fig. 3b) and between 5.86 (third-year students) and 6.19 (physicians) for transferring theory into practice (Fig. 3c)."
