Evidence¶

Jeanne Wanzek 2019¶

Jeanne Wanzek, Greg Roberts, Sharon Vaughn, Elizabeth Swanson, and Katherine Sargent. (2019). Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Latent moderated structural equation modeling of the randomized control trial (1,487 students, 85 classes randomly assigned at class level) tested moderation of PACT's effect by class pretest ASK content knowledge. The interaction was ".17 (p = .11)" with a corresponding effect size of .02, and moderation at values other than the mean was also not significant.

"However, content knowledge at pretest does not predict treat- ment's effect. The interaction effect (γ04z.jxj) is .17 (p = .11), which indicates that treatment's effect on social studies content knowledge does not vary across different levels of pre- treatment content knowledge"
