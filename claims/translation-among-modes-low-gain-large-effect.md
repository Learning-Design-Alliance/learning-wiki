### Sinaga 2015

Pre/post-test results on translation among different modes of concept representation, measured with 15 short essay questions (reliability 0.88). Effect sizes were d = 0.87 (waves and optics) and d = 0.85 (electromagnetism), while normalized gains were rated low by Hake's criteria.
