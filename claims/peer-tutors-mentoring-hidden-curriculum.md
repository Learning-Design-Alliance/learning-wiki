## Evidence

### Tamachi Shameena 2018

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview account from a student in the IPA sample; she reported tutors’ mentoring went beyond session content to “how to answer, how to behave”, insider guidance on being a clinical medical student.

> "They don’t ask like what’s going to come up and things like that but just how to answer, how to behave."
