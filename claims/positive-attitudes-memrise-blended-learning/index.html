Evidence¶

Ebadi and Ghuchi 2018¶

Saman Ebadi, Khadijeh Davood Ghuchi. (2018). Investigating the Effects of Blended Learning Approach on Vocabulary Enhancement from EFL Learners' Perspectives. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 8 No. 2, April - June 2018. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1185411

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative open-thematic coding of semi-structured interviews with 5 experimental-group students selected for high pre- and post-test scores, each lasting about 20 minutes. Participants praised "flexibility and convenient timing" and "systematic and personalized learning"; one noted the method "constituted active learning".

"The findings revealed that the participants identified several main benefits to the blended learning environment, including its flexibility and convenient timing, its systematic and personalized learning, as well as some suggestions with regard to the usefulness and the use of various sources in blended learning."
