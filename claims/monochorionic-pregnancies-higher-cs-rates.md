### Celentano C 2026

Celentano C, Matarrelli B, Rosati M, Mercaldi M, Meloni C, Molino C, Padelli A, Conti F, Prefumo F, Freeze R. (2026). Evaluating the effect of two different training interventions on improving vaginal twin birth rates and provider confidence and knowledge levels: A pre‐ and post‐intervention study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70947

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective analysis of clinical characteristics and CS rates across 278 twin pregnancies at two hospitals. Monochorionic pregnancies were the only characteristic significantly associated with higher CS rates, with P values of <0.001 overall, <0.001 in Pescara, and 0.038 in Florence.

> "no statistically significant differences were found for any characteristics except for a slightly higher incidence of monochorionic pregnancies and slightly higher CS rates for those pregnancies (P < 0.001, P < 0.001, and P = 0.038, overall, Pescara, and Florence, respectively)."
