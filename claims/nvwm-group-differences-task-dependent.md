# Deaf children's working memory disadvantage is task-dependent: no group differences on Spatial Span Forward or narrative measures

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There were no group differences among native signers, non-native signers, and hearing children on the Spatial Span Forward task, nor on BSL Narrative content or structure scores. [→ Marshall C 2015](#marshall-c-2015)

## Evidence

### Marshall C 2015

Marshall C, Jones A, Denmark T, Mason K, Atkinson J, Botting N and Morgan G (2015) Deaf children's non-verbal working memory is impacted by their language experience. Front. Psychol. 6:527. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00527

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVAs controlling for age and WASI score across the three groups found no significant group effect on the Spatial Span Forward task. Narrative content and structure also showed no group differences (content F(2, 53) = 0.542, p = 0.585; structure F(2, 53) = 0.174, p = 0.841).

> "There were no group diﬀerences for the Spatial Span Forward task, F(2, 54) = 2.474, p = 0.094."
