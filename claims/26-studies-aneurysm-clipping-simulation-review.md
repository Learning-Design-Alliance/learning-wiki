`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines searched MEDLINE, Embase, PubMed, Cochrane, and PsycInfo through 21 September 2022. The abstract reports the screening yield: "Of the 2068 articles screened, 26 studies met the inclusion criteria" across four simulation categories.

> "Of the 2068 articles screened, 26 studies met the inclusion criteria. The chosen reports used a wide range of simulation approaches including ex vivo methods (n = 6); virtual reality (VR) platforms (n = 11); and static (n = 6) and dynamic (n = 3) 3D-printed aneurysm models (n = 6)."
