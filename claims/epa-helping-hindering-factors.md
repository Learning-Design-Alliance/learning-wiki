`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Axial coding of interviews identified factors related to workplace self-regulated learning (learning goal, (self-)study, debriefing session, motivation, patients/case mix, supervisor), each with helping and hindering EPA interactions summarised in Table 2. Qualitative; no effect size printed.

> "For each factor, we identified influences that either help or hinder learning. Helping influences are those that led to trainees gaining insights and enhancing their competency development."
