## Evidence

### Donatini G. 2020

Donatini G., Bakkar S., Leclere F. M., Dib W., Suaud S., Oriot D., Breque C., Richer J. P., Faure J. P., Danion J. (2020). SimLife model: introducing a new teaching device in endocrine surgery simulation. Updates in Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-020-00871-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 32 training sessions at the ABS Lab (24 adrenalectomies, 4 thyroid lobectomies with neck dissection), scored with a scale validated by six national experts following the Downing method and rated by two external observers. The article reports learners "scored above average in all procedures evaluated"; per-procedure score ranges are given in Table 1.

> "Learners' performance scored above average in all procedures evaluated. Satisfaction scores for the specified four items ranged between 8.43 (SD 0.87) and 8.89 (SD 0.96)."
