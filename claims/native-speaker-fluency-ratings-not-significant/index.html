Evidence¶

Michael McGuire & Jenifer Larson-Hall 2017¶

Michael McGuire & Jenifer Larson-Hall. (2017). Teaching Formulaic Sequences in the Classroom: Effects on Spoken Fluency. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 1–25. http://dx.doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1271

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Sixteen native-speaker undergraduate judges rated pre- and posttest excerpts on a 1–7 scale after a 20-minute normative training session; interrater reliability was Cronbach's alpha 0.89. The between-group test was "not statistical at the α = .05 level" despite d = 1.0; within-group paired tests were also nonstatistical.

"A t-test examining fluency rating differences between the groups (assuming unequal variances) was not statistical at the α = .05 level (t 13.6 = -2.1, p = .051, 95%CI: -2.4, .0097, d = 1.0), but with an effect size of d = 1.0, which is a medium-size effect"
