Twig Science — Evidence Rating: Promising 2025¶

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

A 2024–25 quasi-experimental study in one rural Iowa school with nearly 400 students used matched comparison groups, "controlling for baseline achievement and demographic variables," and found greater benchmark gains for program users (effect size = +0.15).

"students in Grades 6–8 who used Twig Science showed significantly greater gains on the Imagine Galileo science benchmark assessment than their peers who did not use the program (effect size = +0.15)"
