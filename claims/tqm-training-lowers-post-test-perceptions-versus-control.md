# TQM training lowers teachers' post-test perceptions of TQM applicability in their schools relative to an untrained control group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After TQM training, the experimental group's post-test perception scores were significantly lower than the control group's (Mann-Whitney U test, Z = -3.45, p = .001). [→ Altunay 2016](#altunay-2016)

## Evidence

### Altunay 2016

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control-group experiment with 25 experimental and 25 control participants (teachers and principals). The Mann-Whitney U test on post-test scores was statistically significant, with mean ranks of 18.40 (experimental) versus 32.60 (control), Z = -3.45, p = .001.

> "Mann -Whitney U test was found that it was statistically significant between post- test scores about the perceptions of experimental and control groups. The result of this post-test showed that the level of perception of the experimental group was less than the control group."
