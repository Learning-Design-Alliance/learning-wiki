## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across pre- and post-surveys, the highest proportion of advocacy acts reported by six novice ESL teachers took place at the school level, contradicting the expectation that classroom acts would dominate. [→ Garrone-Shufran 2022](#garrone-shufran-2022)

## Evidence

### Garrone-Shufran 2022

Garrone-Shufran, S. (2022). "I Think You Have to Be a Fighter": Novice ESL Teachers' Descriptions of Advocacy for Emergent Bilinguals. Teacher Education Quarterly, Summer 2022. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1361502

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive case study of six novice ESL teachers; deductive coding of pre- and post-survey reports of advocacy actions by site. The article reports "the highest proportion of reported advocacy acts took place at the school level" (Table 2).

> "Athanases and deOliveira's (2008) suggestion that classroom acts of advocacy might be more prevalent in the practice of novice teachers was not clearly discovered in this research study, as the highest proportion of reported advocacy acts took place at the school level."
