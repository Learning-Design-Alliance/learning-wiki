Linus W. Kline 1909¶

Linus W. Kline, Ph.D. (1909). Some Experimental Evidence in Regard to Formal Discipline. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Some+experimental+evidence+in+regard+to+formal+discipline%22+Kline

Table 2 groups subjects by days of practice (1 to 20); improvement in the practice task rose with distribution, and the article notes negative transfer values were larger the more practice was spread out, confirming the distribution principle of Ebbinghaus and Jost.

"In passing from 1 day to 20 days, the percentage of increase of efficiency rises from 193.6 to 273.5, and, with a few exceptions, to be expected from the small number of cases in some groups"
