Evidence¶

Rosenzweig 2021¶

Rosenzweig, E. Q., Harackiewicz, J. M., Hecht, C. A., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., Asher, M. W., & Hyde, J. S. (2021). College Students’ Reasons for Leaving Biomedical Fields: Disenchantment with Biomedicine or Attraction to Other Fields? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000456

q3 · i? · qualitative · r3

Consensus coding of all 192 leaver responses into four themes (confidence/ability, value/interest, interpersonal factors, costs/barriers; k = .77). Among the 37 attraction-group students, 35 discussed interest in or value for new fields.

"Most students leaving biomedical fields explained their choice in relation to interest or value, referencing in particular concepts related to interest and fit."
