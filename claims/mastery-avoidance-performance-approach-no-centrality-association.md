## Evidence

### Hemi 2025

Hemi, A., Madjar, N., Rich, Y., & Daumiller, M. (2025). Relationships between students' achievement goals and social positioning in the classroom. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1047–1062. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12762

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same multilevel modeling analysis of 472 students, the two undirected hypotheses (H2 mastery avoidance, H3 performance approach) were tested in the same four models; both were not supported. The article reports "no significant associations" with any centrality metric and prints no effect size.

> "The second and third hypotheses were not supported; no significant associations were found between mastery avoidance goals or performance approach goals and any of the centrality metrics measured in this study."
