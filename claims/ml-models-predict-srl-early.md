## Evidence

### Sabourin 2013

Sabourin, J. L., Shores, L. R., Mott, B. W., & Lester, J. C. (2013). Understanding and Predicting Student Self-Regulated Learning Strategies in Game-Based Learning Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 23, 94–114. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0004-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

10-fold cross-validated machine learning classifiers (Naïve Bayes, Decision Tree, SVM, Logistic Regression, Neural Network) trained on 49 features from four time points. "All of the learned models were able to offer a predictive accuracy statistically significantly better than a most-frequent class baseline".

> "All of the learned models were able to offer a predictive accuracy statistically significantly better than a most-frequent class baseline (at p<0.01). Due to the fact that the classes were identified using an even tertiary split, the most frequent class (Medium) model has a predictive accuracy of 33.5 %."
