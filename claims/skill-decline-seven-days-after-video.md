# Skill scores decline within seven days after video-based acquisition, while self-study after an initial low score raises performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Seven days after video-based acquisition, the video-first group scored lower than the group that had meanwhile watched the video (12.5 vs. 15.9, p < 0.001), and within-group scores decreased for the video-first group and increased for the self-study-first group. ## Evidence

### Ott Thomas 2024

Ott Thomas, Demare Tim, Möhrke Julia, Silber Saskia, Schwab Johannes, Reuter Lukas, Westhphal Ruben, Schmidtmann Irene, Dietz Sven-Oliver, Pirlich Nina, Ziebart Alexander, Engelhard Kristin. (2024). Does an instructional video as a stand-alone tool promote the acquisition of practical clinical skills? A randomised simulation research trial of skills acquisition and short-term retention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 secondary endpoint at seven-day retention: of 78 participants, 59 returned; blinded scoring on the 0–20 checklist. The Vid-Self group "tended to yield lower scores than Self-Vid" (12.5 vs. 15.9, p < 0.001).

> "In Study 1, at T2 (after seven days), Vid-Self tended to yield lower scores than Self-Vid (mean 12.5, SD 3.6 vs. mean 15.9, SD 2.2, p < 0.001)"

### Ott Thomas 2024 (2)

Ott Thomas, Demare Tim, Möhrke Julia, Silber Saskia, Schwab Johannes, Reuter Lukas, Westhphal Ruben, Schmidtmann Irene, Dietz Sven-Oliver, Pirlich Nina, Ziebart Alexander, Engelhard Kristin. (2024). Does an instructional video as a stand-alone tool promote the acquisition of practical clinical skills? A randomised simulation research trial of skills acquisition and short-term retention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired within-group comparisons in Study 1 between T1 and T2 (exploratory, no multiple-testing correction). Scores "tended to decrease for Vid-Self" and increase for Self-Vid, both p < 0.001.

> "From T1 to T2, the scores tended to decrease for Vid-Self (T1: mean 14.8, SD 3.5; T2: mean 12.5, SD 3.6, p < 0.001) and increase for Self-Vid (T1: mean 7.7, SD 2.6, p < 0.001; T2: mean: 15.9, SD 2.2, p < 0.001)"
