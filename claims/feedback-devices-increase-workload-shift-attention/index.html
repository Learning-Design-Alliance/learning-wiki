q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis citing Wagner et al. (2022), which investigated the effect of feedback devices on visual attention and pediatric resuscitation quality; the review states the workload increase was small and did not interfere with resuscitation quality.

"Moreover, feedback improved team coordination and reduced compression gaps, although it slightly increased workload and caused some visual attention to shift from the patient to the device [41]."
