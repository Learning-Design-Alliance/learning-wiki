## Evidence

### Zeng Qin 2023

Zeng Qin, Wang Kai, Liu Wei-xin, Zeng Jiu-zhi, Li Xing-lan, Zhang Qing-feng, Ren Shang-qing, Xu Wen-ming. (2023). Efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in advanced life support training: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04654-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 21 RCTs measuring skill performance at course conclusion. The pooled estimate was "SMD = 0.63; 95% CI: 0.21–1.04, P = 0.003, I2 = 92%"; the article's sentence states no significant difference between the HF and control groups while printing a significant P value, an internal inconsistency. Subgroup and sensitivity analyses attributed heterogeneity to different training settings and outcome measures.

> "A total of twenty-one RCTs [18–20, 22–31, 33, 36–42] measured skill performance at the course conclusion. No significant difference was observed between the HF and control groups (SMD = 0.63; 95% CI: 0.21–1.04, P = 0.003, I2 = 92%)."
