## Evidence

### Feng Xinrui 2024

Feng Xinrui, Wu Weiru, Bi Qinghua. (2024). Reform of teaching and practice of the integrated teaching method BOPPPS-PBL in the course “clinical haematological test technique”. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05765-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled trial comparing 19 control-grade students (2015–2016) with 51 BOPPPS-PBL students (2017–2019); the article reports the BOPPPS-PBL group "achieved higher summary evaluation scores than the control group (p < 0.01)". Table 5 shows control averages of 76.00 and 79.96 versus 81.08, 83.30 and 85.41.

> "Post-teaching reform, the BOPPPS-PBL group achieved higher summary evaluation scores than the control group (p < 0.01), with a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05)."
