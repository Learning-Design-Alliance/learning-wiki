Evidence¶

Eddy et al. 2021¶

Laura D. Eddy, Arthur D. Anastopoulos, Melissa R. Dvorsky, Paul J. Silvia, Jeffrey D. Labban & Joshua M. Langberg (2021). An RCT of a CBT Intervention for Emerging Adults with ADHD Attending College: Functional Outcomes. Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2020.1867989

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Multi-site RCT of 250 college students with ADHD randomized to immediate ACCESS or delayed treatment; multi-group latent growth curve models on the LASSI-2 Time Management scale showed a medium between-condition effect ("d = .48 [.23, .73]").

"The immediate ACCESS (b = 4.23, SE = .56, p < .001) and the DTC (b = 1.52, SE = .45, p = .001) conditions both increased in time management significantly over time, but the rate of change was significantly higher in the ACCESS group, Wald (1) = 14.16, p < .001, d = .48 [.23, .73]."
