## Evidence

### Law Marcus 2015

Law Marcus, Rapoport Mark J., Seitz Dallas, Davidson Marla, Madan Robert, Wiens Andrew. (2015). Evaluation of a National Online Educational Program in Geriatric Psychiatry. Academic Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-015-0377-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective post-then-pre web survey of the 2012–2013 OSG; 29 of 50 participants (58%) responded. "79 % (n = 23) of survey respondents reported improved efficacy beliefs" on a five-point Likert domain (Cronbach's α = 0.70), analyzed with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. No effect size printed.

> "A significant positive effect was observed in each of two domains: 79 % (n = 23) of survey respondents reported improved efficacy beliefs (z = −3.69, p < .001)"
