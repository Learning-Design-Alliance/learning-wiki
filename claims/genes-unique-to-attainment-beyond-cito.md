## Evidence

### Antonie Knigge 2022

Antonie Knigge, Ineke Maas, Kim Stienstra, Eveline L. de Zeeuw and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2022). Delayed tracking and inequality of opportunity: Gene-environment interactions in educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 7:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00122-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Cholesky decomposition for all tracking types together (N = 8847). The article states 57% of the genetic impact on attainment is due to genes that also influence performance and the remaining 43% to genes that influence only attainment, indicating attainment is partly the result of "genetic characteristics that are not captured by the performance test".

> "This indicates that educational attainment is for a considerable part the result of genetic characteristics that are not captured by the performance test at the end of primary school."
