Evidence¶

Aktas 2016¶

Aktas, S., & Aydin, A. (2016). The effect of the smart board usage in science and technology lessons. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 64, 125-138. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.64.7

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Retention test with the same 25-item achievement test applied four weeks after the post-test to both intact classes (experimental N=37, control N=38). The article reports the permanence scores "were found to be significantly different between the two groups" (t=2.555, p=.013).

"The average permanence test score of the experimental group was X̅ = 18.81. The average permanence test score of the control group was X̅ = 16.26. The permanence test achievement scores were found to be significantly different between the two groups (t=2.555, p <.05)."
