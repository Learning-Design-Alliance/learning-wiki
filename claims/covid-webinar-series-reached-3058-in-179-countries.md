Observational participation analysis of registration and attendance data for the COVID-19 Vaccination: Building Global Capacity webinar series. It reports "3058 individuals from 179 countries" participated in at least one webinar, plus 6893 YouTube recording views; the series reached at least 93% of WHO Member States and most participants were from LMICs.

> "From 9 February 2021 until 15 June 2021, 3058 individuals from 179 countries participated in at least one webinar with similar numbers of health workers and public health professionals (Table 1); there were 6893 additional views of recordings of the webinars through YouTube."
