## Evidence

### Baron Tal 2020

Baron Tal, Grossman Robert I., Abramson Steven B., Pusic Martin V., Rivera Rafael, Triola Marc M., Yanai Itai. (2020). Signatures of medical student applicants and academic success. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0227108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results section comparison of USMLE Step 1 means across the four signatures in the same cohort, each tested against a randomly selected group of the same size; significance reported at P < 0.01 with printed 95% CI differences, no standardized effect size.

> "For the USMLE Step 1 scores, the improvers and the statics significantly underperformed (236.2 and 236.0, respectively vs 239.4; 95% CI difference [-5.47, -0.95] and [-5.74, -1.1], respectively; P < 0.01, for both), while the solids and risers performed significantly better (241.8 and 243.0, respectively vs 239.4; 95% CI difference [0.39, 4.46] and [1.36, 6], respectively; P < 0.01, for both)."
