## Evidence

### Langkilde Fredrik 2024

Langkilde Fredrik, Masaba Patrick, Edenbrandt Lars, Gren Magnus, Halil Airin, Hellström Mikael, Larsson Måns, Naeem Ameer Ali, Wallström Jonas, Maier Stephan E., Jäderling Fredrik. (2024). Manual prostate MRI segmentation by readers with different experience: a study of the learning progress. European Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-023-10515-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational segmentation study of 100 bi-parametric prostate MRI scans from the G2 trial, with two inexperienced and two experienced residents segmenting against one expert radiologist's reference. The study reports "200/200 (100%) accurate segmentations" for inexperienced and "197/200 (98%)" for experienced residents using DSC > 0.8.

> "The inexperienced residents had a total of 200/200 (100%) accurate segmentations and the experienced residents 197/200 (98%) accurate segmentations."
