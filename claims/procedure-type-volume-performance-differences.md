Meliagros Pete MD, Garber Adam MD, Ambrosio Matthew MS, Kang Le PhD, Forrest Rebecca MD. (2026). Mind the Gap: Rethinking Procedural Skill Assessment in Medical Residency. Southern Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.14423/SMJ.0000000000001992

Retrospective observational comparison of lumbar puncture and paracentesis evaluations; the Table shows mean attempts before first completed checklist of 1.33 (LP) versus 1.73 (paracentesis, P=0.016), and mean GRS score of 3.44 (LP) versus 3.91 (paracentesis, P<0.001).

> "In total, 81% of resident operators achieved a GRS score ≥4 for paracentesis versus 55% for lumbar puncture (P<0.001)."
