## Evidence

### van den Berg V. 2019

van den Berg V., Singh A.S., Komen A., Hazelebach C., van Hilvoorde I., Chinapaw M.J.M. (2019). Integrating Juggling with Math Lessons: A Randomized Controlled Trial Assessing Effects of Physically Active Learning on Maths Performance and Enjoyment in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 16(14): 2452. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16142452

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of the cluster-randomized trial, with group as fixed factor and class and school as random intercepts, adjusted for pretest multiplication performance, age, gender, motor skill level and academic math performance, found "no significant intervention effect on multiplication memorization performance".

> "We found no significant intervention effect on multiplication memorization performance (see Table 4)."

### van den Berg V. 2019 (2)

van den Berg V., Singh A.S., Komen A., Hazelebach C., van Hilvoorde I., Chinapaw M.J.M. (2019). Integrating Juggling with Math Lessons: A Randomized Controlled Trial Assessing Effects of Physically Active Learning on Maths Performance and Enjoyment in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 16(14): 2452. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16142452

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 4 of the trial reports post-intervention multiplication performance of 25.9 (SD 4.8) in the intervention group and 23.3 (SD 7.1) in the control group, with an adjusted intervention effect of "0.4 [−0.4; 1.2]", an unstandardized coefficient whose interval spans zero.

> "Multiplication performance 25.9 (4.8) 23.3 (7.1) 0.4 [−0.4; 1.2]"
