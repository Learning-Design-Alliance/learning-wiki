## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2024

Kim Young-Suk Grace. (2024). Writing Fluency: Its Relations With Language, Cognitive, and Transcription Skills, and Writing Quality Using Longitudinal Data From Kindergarten to Grade 2. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(4). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000841

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling with longitudinal data from 261 English-speaking children followed from kindergarten to Grade 2; writing fluency was a latent variable. The model fit was good and Grade 2 writing fluency was "moderately and independently related to writing quality (.40, p < .001)".

> "Grade 2 writing fluency was moderately and independently related to writing quality (.40, p < .001) after accounting for all the other variables in the model."
