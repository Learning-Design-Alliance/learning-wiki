Abeysuriya V., Gamage J., Jayawardana W. A. D., Abeysuriya V. (2026). Enhancing adult learning through problem-based learning and knowledge scaffolding: a case study from postgraduate immunology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09488-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Structured feedback survey of the PBL–scaffolding intervention in the Immunity to Infectious Diseases module; 29 of 30 forms returned. Students reported that "small-group activities encouraged idea sharing (87%)"; percentages are descriptive Likert summaries with no effect size.

> "The majority reported that small-group activities encouraged idea sharing (87%), class-wide integration boosted confidence (83%), and the exercise enhanced problem-solving skills (80%)."
