## Evidence

### Beyaztaş 2017

Beyaztaş, D. İ., Kaptı, S. B., & Hymer, B. (2017). The Relationship between Student Teachers' Perception of Intelligence and Their Goal Orientation. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9): 1519-1528. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050909

`q2 · i?`

Pearson Product Moment Correlation analysis of the 1409-student survey (Table 10, ** p <.01). The article reports a "low -level positive correlation" for entity–performance goal and incremental–learning goal pairs; correlations are reported by level, with no effect-size coefficients printed in the text.
