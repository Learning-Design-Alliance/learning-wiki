## Evidence

### Wu Yali 2020

Wu Yali, Gong Mingfu, Zhang Dong, Zhang Chun. (2020). Educational impact of the mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise in resident standardization training: a comparative study between resident and professional degree postgraduate trainees. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060520920052

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null retrospective comparison of mini-CEX process times across the 154 trainees. Observation and feedback "was not significantly different from that of the RE group", with printed means of 19.25 (1.56) and 7.65 (0.33) minutes for PDPG versus 20.16 (3.83) and 8.08 (1.72) minutes for RE; equivalence was not tested.

> "The mean (SD) observation and feedback time of the PDPG group was 19.25 (1.56) minutes and 7.65 (0.33) minutes, respectively, which was not significantly different from that of the RE group, 20.16 (3.83) minutes and 8.08 (1.72) minutes, respectively."
