# Turkish TIMSS 2015 students found open-ended mathematics items more difficult than multiple-choice items at both grade levels

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At eighth grade, difficulty indices of open-ended items were closer to zero than multiple-choice items at all cognitive levels, a significant difference. [→ İlhan et al. 2020](#ilhan-et-al-2020)
`q3 i?` At fourth grade, the difference in difficulty indices between types was also statistically significant. [→ İlhan et al. 2020 (2)](#ilhan-et-al-2020-2)

## Evidence

### İlhan et al. 2020

Mustafa İlhan, Nagihan Boztunç Öztürk, Melek Gülşah Şahin. (2020). The Effect of the Item’s Type and Cognitive Level on Its Difficulty Index: The Sample of TIMSS 2015. Participatory Educational Research, 7(2), 47-59. http://dx.doi.org/10.17275/per.20.19.7.2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis of TIMSS 2015 Turkey eighth-grade mathematics item statistics (215 items). An independent samples t-test compared difficulty indices by item type; mean p̄ was .45 for multiple choice and .28 for open-ended, "t(213)=8.50, p<.001".

> "when the differences between difficulty indices of multiple choice and open-ended items were scrutinised through independent samples t-test, the result was found to be significant [𝑝̅ values for multiple choice and open ended items are .45 and .28 respectively, t(213)=8.50, p<.001]"

### İlhan et al. 2020 (2)

Mustafa İlhan, Nagihan Boztunç Öztürk, Melek Gülşah Şahin. (2020). The Effect of the Item’s Type and Cognitive Level on Its Difficulty Index: The Sample of TIMSS 2015. Participatory Educational Research, 7(2), 47-59. http://dx.doi.org/10.17275/per.20.19.7.2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fourth-grade TIMSS 2015 Turkey item analysis (178 items). Both the type contrast (p̄ .51 vs .40, t(176)=4.29, p<.001) and the cognitive-level contrast were statistically significant in the printed tests.

> "the differences between the difficulty indices of the items in terms of both cognitive levels [𝑝̅ values of the items at the level of knowing, applying and reasoning are .53, .42 and .36 respectively; F(2-175)=12.79, p<.001] and types [𝑝̅ values for multiple choice and open ended items are .51 and .40 respectively, t(176)=4.29, p<.001] are statistically significant"
