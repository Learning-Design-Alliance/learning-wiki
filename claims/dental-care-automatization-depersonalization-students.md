Qualitative thematic analysis of focus-group transcripts (theme 1, dental care automatization); a participant stated: "We become so automatic that we don’t even think about what we can improve..." (S11). The analysis attributes the mechanistic approach to insufficient reflection opportunities.

> "Task automatization depersonalizes the work, making students feel like parts of a machine, unable to identify or suggest improvements. Furthermore, there is social pressure to be grateful for the circumstances."
