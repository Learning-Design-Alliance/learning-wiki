## Evidence

### Tan Nigel CK 2011

Tan Nigel CK, Kandiah Nagaendran, Chan Yiong Huak, Umapathi Thirugnanam, Lee Sze Haur, Tan Kevin. (2011). A controlled study of team-based learning for undergraduate clinical neurology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-91

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported engagement measured anonymously with a modified validated tool on a five-point Likert scale in the TBL group (n = 49). Median scores were 4-5 across statements; 93.9% agreed TBL was more enjoyable than conventional teaching.

> "Measures of engagement were high in the TBL group (table 2); most students reported active participation in themselves and their peers. The majority (81.6%) preferred TBL to conventional tutorials."
