`q3 i?` Self-reported e-learning usage time (122.5 vs 98.3 min, P = 0.314) and satisfaction scores (5.9 vs 5.9, P = 0.971) showed no significant difference between groups. [→ Jean S 2026](#jean-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Jean S 2026

Jean S, Thill J, Burey J, Besnier E, Lambert M, Lefevre-Scelles A, Abily J, Gastaldi G, Allard E, Hanouz J-L, Veber B, Dureuil B, Thill C, Compère V. (2026). Pedagogical impact of different levels of e-learning teaching during anesthesia residency: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09225-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcomes in the randomized trial (n = 44 surveyed): no significant difference in self-reported working time or satisfaction on a 0–10 scale. The authors note working time is "self-reported and therefore subject to various biases".

> "The reporting time of e-learning usage (Int-EL group, 122.5 ± 91.9 min vs. Pass-EL group, 98.3 ± 66.4 min, P = 0.314) and the mean resident satisfaction score (Int-EL group, 5.9 ± 2.5 vs. Pass-EL group, 5.9 ± 2.2, P = 0.971) were no different between the two groups"
