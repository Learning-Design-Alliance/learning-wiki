## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group comparisons of pre- and post-procedural STAI-S scores. High-anxiety medians fell from 54.00 to 48.00 (mandibular, Z = −4.526, p < 0.001) and 53.00 to 48.00 (maxillary, Z = −3.109, p = 0.002); low-anxiety groups showed no significant change.

> "participants in the high anxiety group exhibited a significant reduction in state anxiety following both the mandibular and maxillary procedures (p < 0.001 and p = 0.002, respectively)."
