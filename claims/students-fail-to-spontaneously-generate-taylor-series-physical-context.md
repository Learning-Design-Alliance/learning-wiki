### Trevor I. Smith 2011

Trevor I. Smith, John R. Thompson, Donald B. Mountcastle. (2011). Student Understanding of Taylor Series Expansions in Statistical Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Teaching interviews with four students in Year 1, in which students completed portions of the Boltzmann Factor tutorial while thinking aloud. The article reports only one student succeeded spontaneously, and other portions of his interview suggest his success was more a result of memorizing the textbook derivation.

> "During the four teaching interviews, only one student (Joel[12]) succeeded in spontaneously generating a Taylor series expansion of reservoir entropy as a function of energy (as in Eq. 4)"
