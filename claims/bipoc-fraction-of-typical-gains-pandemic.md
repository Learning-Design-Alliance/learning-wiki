# Black, AIAN, and Latinx students made 55-68% of typical math gains and 73-86% of typical reading gains during the pandemic, while Asian American students made 88-100%

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All BIPOC groups made learning gains from fall 2019 to fall 2020, but Black, AIAN, and Latinx students made only 55-66% of their pre-pandemic math gains and at least 70% of their historical reading gains, whereas Asian American students made 88% of pre-pandemic math gains and gained equivalently in reading. Cohort comparison of fall-to-fall MAP Growth gains: the pandemic cohort (fall 2019-fall 2020) was compared with a same-race/ethnicity pre-pandemic cohort (fall 2018-fall 2019), expressed as a percentage of historical gains (Figure 3). "all BIPOC student groups made achievement gains from fall 2019 to fall 2020," but at reduced rates; in reading all groups made at least 70% of historical gains.

> "First, all BIPOC student groups made achievement gains from fall 2019 to fall 2020. Most students made between 60-70% of the pre-pandemic math gains for their group on average, and Asian American students made 88% of the pre-pandemic math gains."
