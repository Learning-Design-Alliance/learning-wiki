Yan Z 2026¶

Yan Z, Fang Z and Zou W (2026). The association between generative AI use and university students' critical thinking: a moderated mediation model of GenAI feedback literacy and reflective thinking. Front. Psychol. 17:1939286. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1939286

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

Conditional indirect effects from 5,000 bootstrap resamples (N = 421). Index = a × b₃ = 0.030; at low reflective thinking the indirect effect was not significant (β = 0.046, CI [−0.007, 0.100]); at high reflective thinking it "was the strongest of the three levels (β = 0.106, 95% CI [0.047, 0.179])".

"The index is therefore signifi- cantly positive, supporting moderated mediation: as reflective think- ing rises, the indirect effect of GenAI use on critical thinking through GenAI feedback literacy strengthens. H6 is supported."
