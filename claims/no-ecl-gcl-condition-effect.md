Rexigel, E., Qerimi, L., Bley, J., Malone, S., Küchemann, S., & Kuhn, J. (2026). Learning quantum properties with informationally redundant external representations: An eye-tracking study. arXiv. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/b18l-zn9q](https://doi.org/10.1103/b18l-zn9q)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regressions on the Klepsch et al. cognitive-load scales in the four-condition experiment found no significant overall effects: "no signiﬁcant overall eﬀect could be identiﬁed for the ECL" and none for GCL. No effect sizes are printed.

> "In contrast, no signiﬁcant overall eﬀect could be identiﬁed for the ECL (F (4, 108) = 1.165, p = .33, R 2 = 0.041, R 2ad j = 0.006) and the GCL (F (4, 108) = 1 .547, p = .19, R 2 = 0 .054, R2ad j = 0 .019)."
