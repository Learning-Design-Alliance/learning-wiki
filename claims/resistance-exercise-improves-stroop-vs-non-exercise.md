## Evidence

### Harveson Andrew T. 2019

Harveson Andrew T., Hannon James C., Brusseau Timothy A., Podlog Leslie, Papadopoulos Charilaos, Hall Morgan S., Celeste EvaRose. (2019). Acute Exercise and Academic Achievement in Middle School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16193527

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized crossover study, cognition was measured with the Victoria version Stroop Test (Dot, Word, Color tasks) administered 5–20 min post-exercise. The repeated measures ANOVA found "significant differences in mean scores between resistance exercise and non-exercise" on all three Stroop elements, with η2 of 0.116, 0.138, and 0.109.

> "Secondary analysis using repeated measures ANOVA revealed significant differences in mean scores between resistance exercise and non-exercise in the Stroop Dot test (F1,62 = 8.14, p = 0.006, η2 = 0.116), Stroop Word test (F1,62 = 9.90, p = 0.003, η2 = 0.138), and Stroop Color test (F1,62 = 7.57, p = 0.008, η2 = 0.109)."
