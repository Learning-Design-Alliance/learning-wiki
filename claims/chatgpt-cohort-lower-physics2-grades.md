Comparison of grade averages per grading period (Table I) and per evaluation (Table II) between the 2022B control cohort and the 2023A ChatGPT cohort; the control cohort "consistently outperforms" the ChatGPT cohort in every period and in the overall aggregate.

> "A comparison of the averages in columns 2 and 3 reveals that the performance of the first cohort (Physics 2 2022B) consistently outperforms that of the subsequent cohort (Physics 2 2023A) across all grading periods and in the overall aggregate."

### Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez 2023 (2)

Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez. (2023). ChatGPT in the Classroom: Boon or Bane for Physics Students' Academic Performance?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-sample t-test on final grades of the two cohorts: equal-variance p = 0.00518 and Welch-corrected p = 0.00613, both below 0.05, "providing statistical evidence that the means of the two populations are different".

> "For homogeneous variances, the probability is 0.00518, which is less than 0.05, providing statistical evidence that the means of the two populations are different."
