## Evidence

### Roschelle 2020

Roschelle, J. & Hodkowski, N.M. (2020). Using Research on Homework to Improve Remote and Hybrid Learning [White Paper]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/107

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review claim citing Elawar & Corno (1985) on teachers' written feedback; the paper adds the error-practice risk of delayed feedback. No effect size printed.

> "Research clearly shows that when students receive timely feedback on homework, they learn more (Elawar & Corno, 1985). Conversely, when students get little or very delayed feedback on how well they are doing, homework can't possibly help them to learn because they might be practicing errors."

### Roschelle 2020 (2)

Roschelle, J. & Hodkowski, N.M. (2020). Using Research on Homework to Improve Remote and Hybrid Learning [White Paper]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/107

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors describe their own rigorous study of the ASSISTments platform, which scored and reported homework so teachers could spend more time on harder problems; "students to learn much more" with no effect size printed here.

> "In a rigorous study (Roschelle et al., 2016), we found that using computer-collected information can also help teachers to adapt, and helps students to learn much more."
