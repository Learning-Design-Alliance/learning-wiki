## Evidence

### Janssen 2010

Janssen, J., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Kanselaar, G. (2010). Task-related and social regulation during online collaborative learning. Metacognition and Learning. [doi:10.1007/s11409-010-9061-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-010-9061-5)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multiple regression on 101 groups' factor scores; the social activities factor (greetings, social support, social resistance, shared understanding, positive group-process evaluations) significantly and negatively predicted group performance, β=−.483, p=.03.

> "Additionally, a significantly negative effect of performing social activities on group performance was found ( β=−.483, p=.03). This indicates that the more groups are engaged in social activities, the worse they perform."
