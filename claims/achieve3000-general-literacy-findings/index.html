Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2018¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2018). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: Achieve3000®. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q3 · i? · review · r3

WWC review of two studies including 32,110 students: Borman et al.'s quasi-experiment showed a statistically significant positive CST-ELA effect (domain effect size 0.04, improvement index +2), and Hill and Lenard showed a significant positive LevelSet effect in spring 2015.

"Hill and Lenard (2016) reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant positive effect of Achieve3000® for students in grades 2–5 on the LevelSet Lexile score in spring 2015."
