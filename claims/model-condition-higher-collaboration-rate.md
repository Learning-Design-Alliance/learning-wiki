### Sally P.W. Wu 2019

Sally P.W. Wu, Jainaba Corr, Martina A. Rau. (2019). How instructors frame students' interactions with educational technologies can enhance or reduce learning with multiple representations. Computers & Education 128 (2019) 199–213. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analysis of collaboration rate, measured as the proportion of problems students completed within five minutes of their partners, in the model and draw conditions. The article reports t(177) = 1.758, p = .081, with M = 79.7% (model) versus M = 71.6% (draw).

> "We found a marginal effect of condition on collaboration rate, t(177) = 1.758, p = .081, such that partners in the model condition collaborated at a higher rate (M= 79.7%,SD = 28.0%) than partners in the draw condition (M = 71.6%, SD = 31.3%)."
