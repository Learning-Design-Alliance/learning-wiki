Nagendrababu Venkateshbabu et al. (2025). A Multi-National Questionnaire-Based Analysis of Dental Students’ Knowledge of the Management of Deep Caries and the Exposed Pulp. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.100844

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired-scenario McNemar analysis of the ten-school survey. The article prints P < .001 for the asymptomatic age contrast and, separately, no difference when symptoms were present (undergraduates, P = .12; postgraduates, P = 1.000).

> "there was a significant difference between treatment plans for younger (Case 1) and older patients (Case 3) in the absence of symptoms (P < .001), with more respondents electing to remove all the caries in older patients compared with younger patients."
