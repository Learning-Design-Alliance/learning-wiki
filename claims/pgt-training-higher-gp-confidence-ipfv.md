## Evidence

### Crossley Caroline 2025

Crossley Caroline, Greville Heath, Pelkowitz Daniel, Gee Amanda, Pelkowitz Lindi, Thompson Sandra C. (2025). Knowledge and practices of regional and rural general practitioners in the identification and management of intimate partner and family violence: a mixed methods study in Western Australia. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-025-02754-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey subgroup comparison of the 25 respondents disaggregated by postgraduate training experience (72% had some PGT). The trained group was "more likely to feel confident" across risk assessment, identification and safety planning; percentages are descriptive with no effect size or significance test printed.

> "GPs who had attended at least one PGT session on IPV/FV were more likely to feel confident in assessing the risk to a patient who discloses IPV/FV (14% of those without PGT; 71% of those with PGT), in identifying signs and symptoms associated with IPV/FV (14% no PGT; 65% with PGT) and in helping a patient to create a safety plan (14% no PGT; 53% with PGT)."
