# Time constraints from complex simulation logistics act on debriefing and may jeopardise its learning potential

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the laissez-faire settings, the logistics of rotating large numbers of students produced a need to complete debriefing within approximately 15 min, and the authors argue such time constraints might act negatively on learning in both patterns. [→ Nyström S 2016](#nystrom-s-2016)

## Evidence

### Nyström S 2016

Nyström S, Dahlberg J, Edelbring S, Hult H, Abrandt Dahlgren M. (2016). Debriefing practices in interprofessional simulation with students: a sociomaterial perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0666-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational finding from the video-recorded debriefings at site 1, where student rotation between stations constrained session length. The authors treat time as an actor related to debriefing processes and outcomes; no effect size is reported.

> "The logistics act as a constraint on the debriefing, producing a need for the debriefing session to be completed within approximately 15 min. The time constraint is shown in the instructor's sayings, as repeated comments on the need to proceed or stop the debriefing."
