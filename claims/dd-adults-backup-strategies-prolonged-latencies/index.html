Evidence¶

Liane Kaufmann 2020¶

Liane Kaufmann, Michael von Aster, Silke M. Göbel, Josef Marksteiner, and Elise Klein. (2020). Developmental Dyscalculia in Adults: Current Issues and Open Questions for Future Research. Lernen und Lernstörungen, 9(2), 126–137. https://doi.org/10.1024/2235-0977/a000294

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review reports Cappelletti and Price (2014) findings that DD adults' "longer response latencies ... seem to be number-specific": slower on numerical magnitude judgements but equally fast on non-numerical semantic categorization. A case study (Kaufmann et al., 2011a) documented finger usage on single-digit problems with remarkably longer reaction times.

"compared with controls, adults with DD needed longer to solve tasks requiring numerical magnitude judgements, but were as fast as controls in tasks involving non-numerical semantic categorizations"
