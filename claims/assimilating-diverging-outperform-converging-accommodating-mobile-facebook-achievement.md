## Evidence

### Chen 2015

Chen, Y. (2015). Linking Learning Styles and Learning on Mobile Facebook. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 16(2). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/1973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A quasi-experimental study of 134 university students in an Electronic Commerce class in Taiwan used mobile Facebook as a learning management system for nine weeks. ANCOVA controlling for pre-test scores found the Assimilating group's "mean scores of the Assimilating group (M=83.76, s=4.07)" and Diverging group significantly exceeded Converging and Accommodating groups.

> "The results showed that the mean scores of the Assimilating group (M=83.76, s=4.07) and the Diverging group (M=81.87, s=5.35) were significantly higher than the mean scores for the Converging group (M=69.92, s=5.01) and the Accomodating group (M=66.83, s=6.89)."
