## Evidence

### Tia Achil 2025

Tia Achil, Konan Amoin G., Hauser Jonas, Ndri Kouassi Y., Ciclet Olivier, Esso Lasme E., Nindjin Charlemagne. (2025). A Cross-Sectional Study of the Relationship Between Dietary Micronutrient Intake, Cognition and Academic Performance Among School-Aged Children in Taabo, Côte d’Ivoire. Nutrients 17(22):3602. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17223602

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conclusion of the cross-sectional study of 252 children: nutrient intakes significantly associated with cognition showed "no association ... with academic performance". The article reports no effect size for this null finding, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "While thiamin, riboflavin, folate, iron, zinc and the nutrient blend showed significant associations with cognition, no association was found with academic performance."
