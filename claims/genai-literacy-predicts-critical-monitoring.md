## Evidence

### Amzalag M 2026

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Hierarchical multiple regression on survey data (N = 818 for this model) predicting GenAI critical monitoring; the final model explained 25.0% of variance (R2 = .250), with literacy dimensions adding ΔR2 = .160. The article reports "the strongest positive predictor of monitoring behavior (β = .41, p < .001)".

> "GenAI critical-ethical awareness emerged as the strongest positive predictor of monitoring behavior (β = .41, p < .001), followed by operational literacy (β = .09, p = .031)."
