# A data-driven redesign of four MathTutor units produced no significant difference in pre-to-post learning gains compared with the Original Tutor

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized within-subjects crossover study, learning gains (post minus pre) did not differ significantly between students using the Redesigned Tutor and the Original Tutor, nor by unit or their interaction. [→ Qianru Lyu 2026](#qianru-lyu-2026)

## Evidence

### Qianru Lyu 2026

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered randomized within-subjects crossover classroom study with 123 students from 8 classes; linear mixed-effects model of pre–post test performance with random intercepts for student, school, and content unit (final N = 95). The article reports "Learning gains did not differ significantly by condition". Both conditions showed comparable pre-to-post gains (Original d = 0.57; Redesigned d = 0.64).

> "Learning gains did not differ significantly by condition (t(279) =−0.30,p=.760), nor by unit or their interaction (ps > .20)."
