### Gautam Yadav 2024

Gautam Yadav, Paulo F. Carvalho, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2024. Beyond Repetition: The Role of Varied Questioning and Feedback in Knowledge Generalization. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3664688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GLMM results on quiz-adjusted opportunity counts, with fixed effects for unique, same-day repeated, and different-day repeated questions. Neither repeat coefficient was significant, so "the benefits of repetition for long-term retention are less clear".

> "For same-day repeated opportunities, the analysis showed a marginally positive effect (p = .064), suggesting that immediate repetition may enhance short-term learning. In contrast, different-day repeated opportunities exhibited a non-significant negative trend (p = .314)."
