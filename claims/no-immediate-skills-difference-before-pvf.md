## Evidence

### Mohsenzadeh Habibeh 2025

Mohsenzadeh Habibeh, Farsi Zahra, Afaghi Effat. (2025). The effects of personalized video feedback of basic cardiac life support on knowledge and skills levels in undergraduate nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06935-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion-section statement of the trial's immediate post-test result (t = -1.043, p = 0.303 in Table 3): "no statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups in terms of BCLS skills mean scores immediately after training," which the authors call expected since PVF had not yet been provided.

> "The findings correspondingly showed no statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups in terms of BCLS skills mean scores immediately after training, while one week and one month later, there was a statistically significant difference."
