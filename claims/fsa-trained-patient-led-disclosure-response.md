Thematic analysis of the same disclosure question: FSA-trained participants 'would spend time engaging and supporting the patient themselves' before referral, often adopting a patient-led approach including seeking patient consent before referrals or reports.

> "Participants who had received FSA Training (26.63% of responses) commonly indicated that, before referring to social work or external service providers, they would spend time engaging and supporting the patient themselves."
