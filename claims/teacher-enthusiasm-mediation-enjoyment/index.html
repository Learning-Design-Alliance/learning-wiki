Evidence¶

Frenzel 2009¶

Frenzel, A. C., Goetz, T., Lüdtke, O., Pekrun, R., & Sutton, R. E. (2009). Emotional Transmission in the Classroom: Exploring the Relationship Between Teacher and Student Enjoyment. Journal of Educational Psychology, 101(3), 705–716. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014695

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Step 3 ML-SEM mediation model (Figure 1) in the same 71-classroom longitudinal field study; the article reports the mediated indirect effect was statistically significant (p < .05) via Mplus IND command, and 89.4% of between-classes variance of student enjoyment was explained.

"Consistent with our mediation hypothesis, teacher enjoyment significantly predicted student-perceived enthusiasm during teaching (/H9252/H11005.42, p /H11021.01), which in turn significantly predicted student enjoyment (/H9252/H11005.37, p /H11021.01). The direct path from teacher enjoyment to student enjoyment was no longer statistically significant ( /H9252/H11005.22, p /H11005.08), further supporting the mediation assumption."
