Rachel Ivie, Susan White, and Raymond Y. Chu. (2018). Three years later: gender differences in the advisor's impact on career choices in astronomy and astrophysics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.11836](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.11836)

From the implications section of the LSAGS analysis. The article reports that "gender alone does not directly impact whether one is working in astronomy, gender does affect persistence indirectly," with gender directly related to relocating for a spouse or partner and to advisor encouragement, and indirectly related to changing advisors and limiting career options.

> "While gender alone does not directly impact whether one is working in astronomy, gender does affect persistence indirectly."
