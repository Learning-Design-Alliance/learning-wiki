## Evidence

### Bock Anna 2021

Bock Anna, Kniha Kristian, Goloborodko Evgeny, Lemos Martin, Rittich Anne Barbara, Möhlhenrich Stephan Christian, Rafai Nicole, Hölzle Frank, Modabber Ali. (2021). Effectiveness of face-to-face, blended and e-learning in teaching the application of local anaesthesia: a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02569-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retrospective pre/post self-assessment collected at the end of the seminar; "all three groups confirmed a theoretical knowledge gain and an improvement of their practical skills after the seminar, which was statistically significant". After the seminar students felt fairly well prepared (mean score = 3.97, SD = 2.03).

> "Regarding the self-assessment, all three groups confirmed a theoretical knowledge gain and an improvement of their practical skills after the seminar, which was statistically significant (Fig. 5). There were no statistical differences in the self-assessments between the groups."
