### Ma Xiang 2025

Ma Xiang, Pan Wei, Yu Xiao-ning. (2025). Evaluating AI-generated examination papers in periodontology: a comparative study with human-designed counterparts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07706-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-type difficulty analysis (classical test theory) within the randomized trial, using the article's thresholds (low: ≥0.7; moderate: 0.4–0.7). AI X-type questions at difficulty 0.77 were classified easy; human X-type questions at 0.62 were moderate.

> "AI-generated X-type questions (difficulty = 0.77 [95% CI: 0.74–0.80]) were classified as "easy," while human-designed X-type questions (difficulty = 0.62 [95% CI: 0.58–0.66]) fell into the "moderate" category"
