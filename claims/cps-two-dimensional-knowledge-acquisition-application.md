# CPS is empirically best modeled as two distinct processes, knowledge acquisition and knowledge application, in high school students

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A two-dimensional CPS model (knowledge acquisition and knowledge application) fits significantly better than a one-dimensional model, with the two dimensions substantially but not perfectly correlated (latent r = .74). [→ Greiff 2013](#greiff-2013)

## Evidence

### Greiff 2013

Greiff, S., Wüstenberg, S., Molnár, G., Fischer, A., Funke, J., & Csapó, B. (2013, February 18). Complex Problem Solving in Educational Contexts—Something Beyond g: Concept, Assessment, Measurement Invariance, and Construct Validity. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031856

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Confirmatory factor analyses within SEM on MicroDYN data from 855 Hungarian students in Grades 5–11 tested a two-dimensional versus one-dimensional CPS measurement model. The two dimensions were "significantly correlated on a latent level" at r = .74, and a chi-square difference test showed the two-dimensional model fit significantly better than the one-dimensional model.

> "Within the two-dimensional model, the measures of knowledge acquisition and application were significantly correlated on a latent level ( r /H11005.74, p /H11021.001; manifest correlation: r /H11005.52, p /H11022.001)."
