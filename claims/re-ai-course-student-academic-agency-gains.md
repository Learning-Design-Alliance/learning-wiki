### Valentina Kuskova 2026

Valentina Kuskova, Sonia Howell, Brianna Stines, and Brianna Conaghan. (2026). A Human-Centered Approach to Ethical AI Education in Underresourced Secondary Schools. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.26004](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.26004)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course student survey (122 responses; 68.2% response rate) from the inaugural course implementation, analyzed descriptively. Students rated academic agency at "mean= 4.37" on a five-point scale, with open-ended responses attributing gains to assignment rigor and teaching fellow feedback.

> "Students reported strong gains in academic agency and preparedness for college-level work (mean= 4.37), including increased confidence handling college coursework, clearer understanding of academic expectations, greater willingness to seek help, and improved planning and time management."
