Zhipeng Yin, Sribala Vidyadhari Chinta, Zichong Wang, Matthew Gonzalez, Wenbin Zhang. (2024). FairAIED: Navigating Fairness, Bias, and Ethics in Educational AI Applications. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.18745](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.18745)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A survey case study of AI-driven Early Warning Systems in U.S. K-12 districts reports this group-level over-identification, quoting that models "may unintentionally over-identify certain student groups". The article attributes it to objective functions prioritizing predictive accuracy; no effect size is printed.

> "models trained to minimize prediction errors overall may unintentionally over-identify certain student groups (such as African American and Hispanic students) as “at-risk,” due to historical biases embedded within disciplinary and attendance data"
