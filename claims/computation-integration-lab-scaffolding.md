Qualitative analysis of Python code in 14 teams' written reports addressing RQ3 on computational thinking, with an external Python-specialist researcher reviewing the CT analysis. The authors conclude "integration of computation-based activities into laboratory tasks with appropriate scaffolding" is needed. No effect size is reported.
