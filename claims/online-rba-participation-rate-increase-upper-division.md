## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2020

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2020). Student behavior and test security in online conceptual assessment. arXiv:1912.05698v1 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of RBA administration across six upper-division courses at five institutions, all offering course credit and reminders. The study reports "an overall participation rate of 94%", and notes historical participation rates for two courses were never higher than 80%.

> "Overall, the courses enrolled 217 students of which 207 responded to the RBAs for an overall participation rate of 94%."
