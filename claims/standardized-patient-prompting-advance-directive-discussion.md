### Lassen Christoph L. 2024

Lassen Christoph L., Jaschinsky Fabian, Stamouli Elena, Lindenberg Nicole, Wiese Christoph H. R. (2024). Anesthesiological Preoperative Interview with a Palliative Care Patient: A Simulation-Based Experiment Using Standardized Patients. Medicina (Kaunas). https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina60101577

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-group simulation experiment with 16 anesthesiologists per group: in group 2 the standardized patient raised four issues, including the advance directive, if unaddressed, and "the advance directive was discussed in a total of 20 cases (63%)".

> "Because, in group 2, the existence of an advance directive was addressed directly by the patients, the advance directive was discussed in a total of 20 cases (63%)."
