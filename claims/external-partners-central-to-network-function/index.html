Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project. (1998). https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Findings from the 1997 Principal Survey of implementation-network schools. The survey found "Nearly two-thirds of the principals reported that their schools were invited to participate in networks by their external partners," while only 17 percent were invited by another school and 19 percent formed their own networks.

"Nearly two-thirds of the principals reported that their schools were invited to participate in networks by their external partners (see Table 8)."
