### Briesch 2019

Briesch, A.M., Daniels, B. & Beneville, M. (2019). Unpacking the Term “Self-Management”: Understanding Intervention Applications Within the School-Based Literature. J Behav Educ 28, 54–77. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10864-018-9303-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of the single-case literature on self-management interventions to support student behavior, covering 56 studies. The review reports that "Prompted self-monitoring was the most frequently used configuration ( N = 23)", with self-monitoring plus reinforcement (N = 6) and prompted self-monitoring plus graphing (N = 5) also common.

> "Prompted self-monitoring was the most frequently used configuration ( N = 23); however, the use of self-monitoring plus reinforcement based on both accuracy and performance ( N = 6) and prompted self-monitoring plus graphing ( N = 5) were also common."
