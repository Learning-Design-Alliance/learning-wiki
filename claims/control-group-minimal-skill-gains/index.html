Final diagnostics of the control group (185 people) in the pedagogical experiment, assessed with qualimetric methods. The article reports gains "only in the skills of self -analysis (by 3%) and self -organizing (by 1%)"; these are descriptive percentages with no test statistic.

"the pupilsd of the control group have improved their results only in the skills of self -analysis (by 3%) and self -organizing (by 1%). The skills of self -goal-setting, self-planning, self-cntrol, and self-correction were on the same level. No one of the pupils has achieved the 3d or the 4th level."
