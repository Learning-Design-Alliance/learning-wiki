## Evidence

### Suzuki Reina 2022

Suzuki Reina, Kanai Mio, Oya Kazumasa, Harada Yohei, Horie Ryohei, Sekiguchi Hiroshi. (2022). A prospective randomized study to compare standard versus intensive training strategies on long-term improvement in critical care ultrasonography proficiency. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03780-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective CCUS examination survey in the medical intensive care unit (July 2012–June 2014); 400 of 580 examinations were documented by fellows. "Patient care plan changed in 227 cases (57%) after CCUS examinations", with varying proportions across examination types. Impact on management was self-reported via survey.

> "Patient care plan changed in 227 cases (57%) after CCUS examinations—95 (49%) among the single cardiac, 35 (70%) among single thoracic, and 21 (64%) among single abdominal."
