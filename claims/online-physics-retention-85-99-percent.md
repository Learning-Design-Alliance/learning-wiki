## Evidence

### Kalani Hettiarachchilage 2023

Kalani Hettiarachchilage, Neel Haldolaarachchige. (2023). Effective Model with Personalized Online Teaching and Learning Science in the Era of ChatGPT. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of registration and completion data across four restructured online introductory physics courses at CSI (CUNY) with approximately 800 total registered students. The authors report retention percentages per course and state these are "well above the normal retention levels for the in-person class model." No comparison group or statistical test is reported.

> "Student retention average is 99% for PHY156 and PHY206, 90% for PHY116, and 85% for PHY114. These averages are well above the normal retention levels for the in-person class model."
