`q3 i?` Post-training Mini-CEX scores were significantly higher in the integrated-teaching group (27.2 ± 0.4) than in the traditional-teaching group (24.1 ± 0.3). [→ Chen Xiaolin 2026](#chen-xiaolin-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen Xiaolin 2026

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial, the Mini-CEX (a 20-minute evaluation of comprehensive clinical performance using real cases) showed post-training means of "27.2 ± 0.4 vs. 24.1 ± 0.3, p < 0.00"; pre-training Mini-CEX scores did not differ (p = 0.94).

> "after the training, participants in the experimental group achieved significantly higher scores compared to those in the control group (90.7 ± 1.4 vs. 82.4 ± 0.9, p < 0.00; 27.2 ± 0.4 vs. 24.1 ± 0.3, p < 0.00)"
