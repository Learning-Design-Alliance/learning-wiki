`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Five-point Likert questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.84 for the task-efficiency subsection) completed by 29 students in the AI-enhanced flipped classroom group. The article reports students found AI "particularly valuable in saving time on collecting basic information (mean 3.79, agreement percentage 75.86%)". A separate knowledge-support table reports clarity in explaining complex mechanisms at mean 3.93 and accuracy of information lowest at 3.34.
