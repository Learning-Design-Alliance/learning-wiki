Exploratory question-level Fisher's exact test in the same field experiment. The pre-learning attempt of the same question showed no significant difference (64% vs. 62%; odds ratio:0.56; Fisher'sp:0.47). Diary analysis attributed the gap to "more limited exposure to realistic and diverse examples of nutrition labels."
