## Evidence

### Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi 2012

Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi. (2012). The Impact of Task-based Approach on Vocabulary Learning in ESP Courses. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p118

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on the 25 experimental-group students' teacher-made technical vocabulary scores before and after thirteen weeks of task-based instruction. The article reports the mean increased from 18.52 (SD 5.347) to 24.00 (SD 2.693) and the paired t of -6.326 was significant at p < .05.

> "the t-value with its 24 degrees of freedom (df) was -6.326. In fact, the observed (6.326) was more than t-critical (2.064), at 0.05 level of significance (p<.05). As a result, a significant difference was detected between learners’ mean scores in the pre-test and the post-test within the experimental group."
