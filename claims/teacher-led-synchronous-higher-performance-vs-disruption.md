# Countries with Accentuated Teacher-Led Synchronous Instruction showed better PISA 2022 performance than countries with Accentuated Instructional Disruption across all domains, with modest effects

## Evidence

### Giang Pham 2026

Giang Pham, Franziska Maria Locher, Dimitra Kolovou, Eliane Arnold. (2026). COVID-19: A cross-national analysis with PISA data. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102366. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102366

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Double-selection lasso regression at country level (N = 47) contrasting Cluster 2 vs Cluster 3 as treatment, controlling for pre-pandemic performance, ESCS, GDP and school-closure measures. The article reports "approximately 7 PISA points in mathematics (p = .04)" and 10 points in reading (p = .02); the science difference (p = .08) was not significant.

> "countries characterised by Accentuated Teacher-Led Synchronous Instruction (Cluster 2) demonstrated better student performance in 2022 across all domains compared with countries with Accentuated Instructional Disruption (Cluster 3). However, the size of these effects was modest: approximately 7 PISA points in mathematics (p = .04), 10 points in reading (p = .02), and 5.8 points in science (p = .08)."
