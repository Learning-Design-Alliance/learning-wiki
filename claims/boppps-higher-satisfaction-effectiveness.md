## Evidence

### Cheng Wei-Qin 2025

Cheng Wei-Qin, He Ling, Cai Jin-Hua, Fan Xiao. (2025). Hybrid BOPPPS model in radiology education: a case study of pediatric undergraduates. BMC Medical Education, 25. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07279-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of 418 students on a five-point Likert scale. Teaching satisfaction was 4.39 ± 0.67 (BOPPPS) vs 3.79 ± 0.75 (LBL), t = 8.551, p < 0.001; overall teaching effectiveness 4.23 ± 0.70 vs 3.75 ± 0.69, t = 7.047, p < 0.001.

> "Overall, the BOPPPS group showed significantly higher levels of satisfaction and effectiveness in the course compared to the LBL group (both p < 0.01)."
