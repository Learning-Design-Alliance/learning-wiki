# In a regression of post-course CLASS scores, only pre-course attitudes significantly predict post-course attitudes; gender, ethnicity, and their interaction are not significant

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Fitting Post ~ Pre + Gender + EthRep + Gender × EthRep to 264 students, only the Pre coefficient is significant (βPre = 0.57, 95% CI = (0.46, 0.67), p < 0.01); the full model has R² = 0.32, and neither gender, ethnic representation, nor their interaction significantly predicts post-course expert-like beliefs. ## Evidence

Linear regression on the matched UMI sample (N=264) with an interaction term for gender and ethnic representation. The article reports "only the coeﬃcient for Pre is signiﬁcant" with βPre = 0.57 and R2 = 0.32, indicating substantial variance remains unexplained.
