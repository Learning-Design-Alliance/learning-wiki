## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Combined Q3-Q4 problem-solving scores differed significantly in favor of the experimental group (M: 27.82; SD: 11.26 vs M: 17.16; SD: 9.05), t(74)=4.546, p<0.05. [→ Çakiroğlu 2013](#cakiroglu-2013)

## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2013

Çakiroğlu, Ü. (2013). Using a hybrid approach to facilitate learning introductory programming. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1), 161-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008878

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Domain-level analysis of the ProKT posttest combining Q3 and Q4; independent t-test favored the experimental group. The article prints "t(74)=4.546" with p below 0.05 and no effect size. Q3 alone was significant (t(74)=3.594, p: 0.001) and Q4 significant by Mann Whitney U (U=343.500, p: 0.000).

> "Statistically, a significant difference exists in the “problem solving” domain of programming knowledge between groups in favor of the EG, which are t(74)=4.546, (p:0.000 <0. 05)."

### Çakiroğlu 2013 (2)

Çakiroğlu, Ü. (2013). Using a hybrid approach to facilitate learning introductory programming. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1), 161-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008878

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Domain-level analysis combining Q1 and Q2 on the ProKT posttest; the t-test found no significant difference between the groups, though descriptive means favored EG. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.

> "Posttest results for (Q1 and Q2), did not show significant difference in the mean scores between groups for “conceptual understanding”; the statistical result was t(74)=1.507 and p>0.05)."
