## Evidence

### Falade Joshua 2025

Falade Joshua, Adebayo Ezekiel Taiwo, Eegunranti Benjamin Adekunle, Fatusi Adesegun Olayiwola. (2025). Connecting Emotional Intelligence with Academic Performance and Family Dynamics among Fresh Undergraduates in a Health Sciences University: Preliminary Findings. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences 35(1). https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v35i1.7

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the 560-respondent cross-sectional census identified parental marital status as an independent predictor; the table prints OR = 13.466, 95% CI 4.752–38.157, p < 0.001, versus non-married as reference. Chi-square showed 95.9% vs 72.6% above-average EI.

> "The odds of having above-average EI were significantly higher among respondents with married parents (OR = 13.446, p < 0.001)."
