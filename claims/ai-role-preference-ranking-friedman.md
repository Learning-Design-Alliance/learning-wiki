### Wangda Zhu 2025

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Friedman test on final preference rankings from the within-subjects counterbalanced experiment (N = 26); the Tutor ranked best (M= 2.35) and the Struggling Student worst (M= 4.42). Only a test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported here.

> "The test revealed a significant effect of AI role on student rankings,χ2(4, N= 26) = 28.83,p < .001, indicating that students demonstrated distinct preferences for different AI pedagogical approaches."
