### Eiring Lisa 2026

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tutor assessments from the quasi-experimental study. Tutors observed that "the depth of information and the structure of the answers had improved considerably" after the second training round.

> "The tutors also noted an improvement after the second round, particularly in terms of the quality of the students’ presentations: the depth of information and the structure of the answers had improved considerably."

### Eiring Lisa 2026 (2)

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standardized tutor evaluations of students' answer structure using a six-point scale. The proportion of ratings in the best categories (1 and 2) increased by 17.76% between the first and second training rounds.

> "In T1 (n = 171), 56.73% of the ratings fell into categories 1 and 2, whereas in T2 (n = 160), this proportion of ratings in categories 1 and 2 increased by 17.76%."
