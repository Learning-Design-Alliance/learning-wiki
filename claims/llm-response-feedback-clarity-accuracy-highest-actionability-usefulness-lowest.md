### Mendonça PC 2026

Mendonça PC, Quintal F, Figueiredo M and Mendonça F (2026) Perceived usefulness and intention to use large language model-generated feedback across three educational levels: a user-centred study in programming. Front. Educ. 11:1934069. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934069

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional rating study of 144 students across three educational levels who rated 893 response-level LLM feedback instances on five-point Likert items. Descriptive results show "Clarity (M = 4.43) and accuracy (M = 4.40) had the highest means"; favourable ratings (mean ≥4) ranged from 72.2% for actionability to 83.3% for clarity.

> "Clarity (M = 4.43) and accuracy (M = 4.40) had the highest means, and actionability (M = 4.24) and usefulness (M = 4.24) had the lowest."
