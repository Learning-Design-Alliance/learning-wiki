Evidence¶

Starkey-Perret 2017¶

Starkey-Perret, R., Belan, S., Lê Ngo, T. P., & Rialland, G. (2017). The effect of form-focussed pre-task activities on accuracy in L2 production in an ESP course in French higher education. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 171-195). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.751

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Action-research pilot comparing written productions of B1 students who self-selected into completing pre-task FonF micro-tasks with those who did not, over three macro-tasks in a 12-week semester. The article reports "no significant gain in accuracy" for the pre-task group; intergroup differences fell within standard deviations.

"The present study did not show that there was any significant gain in accuracy for the group of students who completed the pre-tasks."
