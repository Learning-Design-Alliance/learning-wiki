## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, H. and Zhang, W. (2026). Empowerment over enforcement: unpacking the psychological drivers of AI-assisted deep revision in EFL writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1871022. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871022

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same PLS-SEM structural model (N = 327), Perceived Competence was also a significant positive predictor of Deep Revision Engagement (β = 0.218, t = 2.964, p = 0.003). f² was 0.251 for IM but "weak" (0.042) for PC and PS.

> "Both Intrinsic Motivation ( β = 0.448, t = 6.765, p < 0.001) and Psychological Safety (β = 0.181, t = 2.633, p = 0.008) positively predict Deep Revision Engagement, as their respective corrected bias 95% CI did not include zero"
