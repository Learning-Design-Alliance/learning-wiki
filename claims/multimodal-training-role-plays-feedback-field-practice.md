## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of six focus group discussions with the ten CBR workers at three time points. Workers valued "immediate feedback" from role-play assessments; views on whether five weeks of training was long enough were split roughly half and half.

> "Whilst no teaching methods were considered redundant, role-plays had particular support from CBR workers. Getting immediate feedback was highly valued as a way to identify problem areas and ultimately improve skills."
