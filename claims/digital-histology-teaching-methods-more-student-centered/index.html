q2 · i? · review · r2

The scoping review's synthesis of 98 included articles on digital and blended histology and pathology education reports that "teaching methods have become more accessible, interactive, and student‐centered than traditional instruction." No effect sizes are printed for this synthesis-level statement.

"The literature indicated that teaching methods have become more accessible, interactive, and student‐centered than traditional instruction."
