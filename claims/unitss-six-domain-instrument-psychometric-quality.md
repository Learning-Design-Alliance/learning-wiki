## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exploratory factor analysis of the UNITSS with 472 teachers supported six domains (creating a positive learning climate, instruction/design, differentiation, student engagement, assessment, professional development) with Cronbach's alpha ranging from .76 to .83. ## Evidence

### Douwes‐van Ark 2026

Douwes‐van Ark, I. M. E., Fokkens‐Bruinsma, M., Deinum, J. F., & Korpershoek, H. (2026). University teachers' self‐efficacy beliefs. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70088

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis with oblimin rotation (N = 472); five misfitting items (loadings below .30) were deleted, and instructional strategies and education design items merged into a single instruction/design domain after a separate differentiation domain emerged.

> "To assess the internal consistency, Cronbach's alpha was calculated and ranged from .76 to .83 for the adapted domains. The psychometric quality of the UNITSS was found to be sufficient to answer our research questions."
