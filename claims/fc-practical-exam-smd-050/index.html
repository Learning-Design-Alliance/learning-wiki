Evidence¶

Zheng Hui 2026¶

Zheng Hui, Ouyang Yun, Zeng Yifei, He Xinyu, Song Liping, Yu Xiangyuan. (2026). Systematic evaluation of the teaching effectiveness of flipped classroom mode in undergraduate preventive medicine courses in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896815

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 7 studies of practical skills assessment in preventive medicine courses; moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 71.1%) led to a random-effects model with a printed effect size of SMD = 0.50, i.e. "The pooled SMD was 0.50 (95% CI: 0.30–0.70)", statistically significant. Sample-size subgroup analysis reduced heterogeneity to I2 = 0% for groups >100.

"For practical skills assessment, a total of 7 studies (7–9, 20, 21, 27) were included. Heterogeneity across these studies was moderate (I2 = 71.1%, p = 0.002), and a random-effects model was used. The pooled SMD was 0.50 (95% CI: 0.30–0.70)"
