Survey descriptive analysis of graduate coursework in gross anatomy, histology, neuroanatomy, and embryology: "respondents completed graduate coursework in 2.3 (±1.6)" subdisciplines on average; 32% completed all four; 19% (121 of 654) completed none.

> "On average, respondents completed graduate coursework in 2.3 (±1.6) of the traditional anatomical sciences subdisciplines. Only 32% (210 of 654) of respondents completed graduate coursework in all four traditional anatomical sciences subdisciplines."
