## Evidence

### Aadithreya Varman 2024

Aadithreya Varman, Varman N V Arulmozhi, Balakumar Dinesh. (2024). Assessing the surgical competency of novice surgeons by using a three-dimensional heads-up display microscope. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/IJO.IJO_2677_23

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective within-surgeon comparison in a private practice setting: each of 20 novice surgeons performed 10 surgeries per system (200 data points per group), scored with the ICO-OSCAR: phaco rubric. Mean scores were "60.19 (11.41) for TM surgeries and 62.99 (11.11) for 3D surgeries" (P = 0.013).

> "The mean surgical competency scores were 60.19 (11.41) for TM surgeries and 62.99 (11.11) for 3D surgeries. 3D surgeries had significantly higher surgical competency scores than TM surgeries (P = 0.013)."
