## Evidence

### Seidi Masoumeh 2024

Seidi Masoumeh, Ramezani-Aliakbari Fatemeh, Doosti-Irani Amin. (2024). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom method using clinical scenarios and Educational Technology versus Subject-Based Lectures in a gastrointestinal physiology course for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05863-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with two groups of 30 medical students each; intervention group received flipped classroom with clinical scenarios, control received subject-based lectures. Table 2 reports intervention post-test mean 17.55 (SD 3.11) versus control 9.4 (SD 3.04), both pre-post comparisons p < 0.001, described as "a significant increase in the average post-test scores".

> "The findings in Table 2 indicate a significant increase in the average post-test scores compared to the pre-test scores in both the intervention and control groups in the theoretical test."
