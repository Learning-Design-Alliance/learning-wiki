# Information-relay centralities and eigenvector centrality are positively correlated with final, clinical and MCQ performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All information-relay centrality measures and eigenvector centrality correlated positively with final performance (eigenvector τb (33) = 0.45, p < 0.001), and eigenvector centrality also correlated with clinical grades (0.38) and MCQ grades (0.37). [→ Saqr Mohammed 2018](#saqr-mohammed-2018)

## Evidence

### Saqr Mohammed 2018

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kendall's Tau-b correlations between network parameters and three outcome measures (final, clinical exam, MCQ grades) for 35 students. The study reports eigenvector centrality correlations of "0.45, p < 0.001" (final), "0.38" (clinical) and "0.37" (MCQ), all significant.

> "All centrality scores measuring the role in information relay were positively correlated with final performance as well as Eigen centrality (τb (33) = 0.45, p < 0.001); Eigenvector centrality was also positively correlated with clinical grades (τb (33) = 0.38, p < 0.001), and MCQ grades (τb (33) = 0.37, p < 0.001)"
