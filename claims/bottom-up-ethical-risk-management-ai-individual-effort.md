`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' integration of the risk-management theme. Faculty reported guidance practices (usage reporting criteria, awareness raising, scenario-based content, changed tasks, detection tools, human control) and students reported verifying AI outputs via multiple sources and original sources; the article notes these efforts fall short.
