Mental health mediates the association between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic performance in European schoolchildren. Jornal de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2024.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional linear regression in the same multi-country sample (valid sample 466 for academic performance models), CRF measured by the multistage 20-m shuttle run test and academic performance by teacher grades across GPA, natural sciences, native language, maths, physical education and arts. The study reports "β ranging from 0.249 to 0.337, p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size beyond these regression coefficients is printed, so impact is coded i?
