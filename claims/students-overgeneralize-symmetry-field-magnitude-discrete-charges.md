Analysis of written responses and think-aloud interviews on a multiple-choice item contrasting a sphere with six discrete charge blobs versus uniform charge distribution. On item 1, 57% of 168 students answered correctly per Table 1; students claimed the ﬁeld magnitude must work out to be "the same at all the three points shown".
