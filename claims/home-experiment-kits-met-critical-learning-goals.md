## Evidence

### Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier 2021

Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier (2021). Meeting Laboratory Course Learning Goals Remotely via Custom Home Experiment Kits. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0021600](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0021600)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-semester attitude survey (Likert and free response) in the remote Summer 2020 course, with all but one student responding; students reported they "perceive a benefit from working with the kits" beyond simulations or instructor-provided data.

> "Students do seem to perceive a benefit from working with the kits that they do not feel they would have gotten from only working with simulations or instructor-provided data sets."

### Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier 2021 (2)

Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier (2021). Meeting Laboratory Course Learning Goals Remotely via Custom Home Experiment Kits. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0021600](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0021600)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretive statement in the remote implementation section: at-home experiments replicating the onsite labs' objectives addressed course learning goals LG-1 through LG-10 "at least as well as we had been doing in the onsite labs".

> "we were able to address course learning goals LG -1 through LG -10, as outlined in Section II , at least as well as we had been doing in the onsite labs"
