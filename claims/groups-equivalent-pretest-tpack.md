## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Levene's statistic tests showed no significant pre-intervention difference between groups on any of the four ICT knowledge dimensions. [→ Arwa Ahmed Abdo Qasem 2016](#arwa-ahmed-abdo-qasem-2016)

## Evidence

### Arwa Ahmed Abdo Qasem 2016

Arwa Ahmed Abdo Qasem, Gandla Viswanathappa. (2016). Blended Learning Approach to Develop the Teachers' TPACK. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(3), 264-276. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2013.09.248

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest equivalence check on the 60 matched and randomly assigned teachers: Levene statistics for TK, TPK, TCK and TPACK all yielded p>0.05 (e.g. TK p=0.249), indicating "the groups were equivalent in their ICT knowledge scores".

> "An examination of Levene's Statistic results in Table 2 reveals that there is no significant difference between the groups regarding their ICT knowledge scores (p>0.05)."
