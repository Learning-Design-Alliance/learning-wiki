# Added interpretive support did not significantly affect the evolution of student teachers' educational beliefs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Dyads that received additional support for using sources, forming arguments, or both showed no significant difference in belief change compared to dyads without it. [→ Voet 2018](#voet-2018)

## Evidence

### Voet 2018

Voet, M. & De Wever, B. (2018). Effects of immersion in inquiry-based learning on student teachers' educational beliefs. Instructional Science, 46(3), 383-403. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9439-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student teacher dyads were randomly divided over four conditions receiving additional support for using sources, forming arguments, or both. Analyses reported in the appendix showed this extra support layer had no significant impact on belief change, so a parsimonious model without conditions was used. No effect size printed.

> "analyses indicate that this extra layer of support that was added to the macro -script (see section 5.2. Design principles of the IBL activity) had no significant impact on the evolution of student teachers’ educational beliefs"
