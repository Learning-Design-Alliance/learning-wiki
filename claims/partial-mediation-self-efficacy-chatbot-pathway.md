Serial mediation model with bootstrapped confidence intervals (5,000 resamples). The article reports an indirect effect of 0.69 via chatbot usage and −0.24 via proof-review usage.

> "Bootstrapped indirect effects confirmed a partial mediation pathway from self-efficacy to Midterm 3 via chatbot usage (indirect = 0.69, 95% CI[0.14, 1.56]), whereas the proof-review pathway was not significant (indirect = −0.24, 95% CI[−0.72, 0.12])."
