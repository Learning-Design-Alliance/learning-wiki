## Evidence

### Chartash David 2026

Chartash David, Bievre Nicolas, Marušić Ana, Ota Yuma, Asada Yoshikazu, Kubo Saori, Kanno Takeshi, Yagi Machiko Saeki, Matsuyama Yasushi. (2026). Evaluation of a Problem-Based Learning Program’s Effect on Artificial Intelligence Ethics Among Japanese Medical Students: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84535

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cooccurrence network analysis of 118 students' patient-explanation essays; only 264 shared cooccurrence pairs existed, against 1030 unique pairs at pretest and 989 at posttest, indicating a major shift in conceptual relationships rather than discourse volume.

> "The patient explanation network’s Jaccard coefficient was 0.1156, indicating very low similarity between the 2 time points."

### Chartash David 2026 (2)

Chartash David, Bievre Nicolas, Marušić Ana, Ota Yuma, Asada Yoshikazu, Kubo Saori, Kanno Takeshi, Yagi Machiko Saeki, Matsuyama Yasushi. (2026). Evaluation of a Problem-Based Learning Program’s Effect on Artificial Intelligence Ethics Among Japanese Medical Students: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84535

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative structural analysis of the physician-precaution cooccurrence network (Jaccard 0.1213; 191 shared pairs, 631 unique at pretest, 753 at posttest), showing students explored a wider range of new professional and ethical concerns.

> "The pretest network comprised 3 separate, unconnected clusters (Figure 3), which transformed into a single, integrated network at posttest, where “AI” and “information” acted as hubs (Figure 4)."
