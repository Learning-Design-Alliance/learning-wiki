Self-reported practice section of the same cross-sectional survey (n = 356), scored binary per item with satisfactory defined as an overall score of at least 75%. The article reports "a satisfactory level of practice regarding fluid monitoring for burn patients (94.89%)"; the rule-of-nines item was lowest at 85.7%.

> "Participants reported a satisfactory level of practice regarding fluid monitoring for burn patients (94.89%). Most students indicated that they recorded their signature and date after fluid administration (n = 349, 98%). In contrast, a lower proportion reported assessing patients using the rule of nines (n = 305, 85.7%)"
