# Adults with ADHD report deficient emotional self-regulation symptoms far more frequently than adults without ADHD

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adults with ADHD scored significantly higher on the DESR scale than non-ADHD adults, endorsing every DESR item more frequently. [→ Surman Craig B. H. 2013](#surman-craig-b-h-2013)

## Evidence

### Surman Craig B. H. 2013

Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4009378/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Controlled observational comparison of 206 adults with ADHD and 123 adults without ADHD from a family study, using eight self-report Barkley Current Behavior Scale items. The study reports "significantly higher in ADHD subjects than in non-ADHD subjects" with means 11.5 ± 5.48 vs. 2.68 ± 2.23 and z = 23.51, p<0.001.

> "The mean total DESR scale score was significantly higher in ADHD subjects than in non-ADHD subjects (11.5 ± 5.48 vs. 2.68 ± 2.23; z = 23.51, p<0.001). Subjects with ADHD reported significantly more frequent occurrences of every item of DESR than non-ADHD subjects (p<0.001 for each item)."
