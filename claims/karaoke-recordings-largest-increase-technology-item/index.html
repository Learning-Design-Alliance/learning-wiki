Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı 2017¶

Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı. (2017). Effect of pop music on students’ attitudes to music lessons. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3321

q2 · i? · causal · r2

In the experimental group's posttest data, item 14 rose from a pretest mean of 1.91 to a posttest mean of 4.18 (t(168) = 21.78, p = 0.00 per Table 3). The article states "The use of karaoke recording for song learning in the experimental group explains this increase in the mean points."

"When the mean points are investigated, the highest increase was observed for item 14 ( = 1.91, = 4.18). The proposition in this item is related to the level of use of technological facilities in music lessons."
