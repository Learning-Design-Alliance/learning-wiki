Ana Saavedra · Daniel L. Schwartz. (2026). Improving the transfer of inquiry strategies by using short instructional videos. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10648-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Fine-grained sequence analysis of Tuglet clickstream data (Fig. 8), restricted to students who started with the default strategy; the printed percentages are 53% switching and 70% maintaining, larger than any other treatment.

"For the Expanded treatment, 53% of the students who started with the default strategy switched to the base-unit strategy for their next setup (between sequence 1 and 2), and 70% of these students continued to use the base-unit strategy for their third setup. These are larger percentages than any of the other treatments."
