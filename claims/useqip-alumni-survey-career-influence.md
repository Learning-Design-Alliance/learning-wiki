### John M. Donohue 2026

John M. Donohue, Michael J. Grabowecky, George Nichols, Martin Laforest, Lino Eugene, Fiona Thompson, Peter Sprenger, Kevin Resch, David G. Cory. (2026). USEQIP: Outcomes and experiences from 17 years of undergraduate summer schools in experimental quantum information science. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.25124](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.25124)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey sent in early 2026 to all 198 alumni from 2018-2025, receiving 36 responses. The authors report "25/36 (69%) felt USEQIP influenced their career a great deal"; excluding 2021 virtual-program alumni, all 26 respondents reported moderate (5/26) or great (21/26) influence, which the authors attribute to in-person interactions and peer networking.

> "In total, 25/36 (69%) felt USEQIP influenced their career a great deal, with only 4/36 saying a little or not at all."
