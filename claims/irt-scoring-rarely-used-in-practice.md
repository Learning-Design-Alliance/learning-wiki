## Evidence

### Conejo 2016

Conejo, R., Guzmán, E., & Trella, M. (2016). The SIETTE Automatic Assessment Environment. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 270–292. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0078-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the Siette usage database covering tests created between 2002 and 2014 at the University of Málaga. The article reports the distribution of assessment criteria across tests, finding that "IRT was used in only 18 tests" compared to 642 tests using percentage correct and 581 using item scoring. No inferential statistics are reported.

> "Most of current tests in Siette use the percentage of correct answers as the assessment criterion (642 tests). The application of this assessment criterion is followed by the alternative of item scoring (581 tests). In contrast, IRT was used in only 18 tests."
