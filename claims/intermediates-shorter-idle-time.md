## Evidence

### Gholami Soheil 2024

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Idle time (MTidle), the duration instruments are held motionless, was compared between six novices and six intermediates across segments; Wilcoxon tests were significant for most segment-instrument pairs (e.g., S.3 both U=3.57, p<.001).

> "Intermediate participants exhibited shorter idle times when manipulating the needle holder, the tweezers, or both, compared to their novice counterparts"

### Gholami Soheil 2024 (2)

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same idle-time analysis reports a null for segment 1 with tweezers and both instruments; the article prints no effect size for this finding, so the null does not establish equivalence.

> "it is worth noting that the statistical significance was not evident in the first segment when the tweezers and both instruments were considered"
