# Frontal addressing in an instructional video enhances parasocial interaction and retention performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Frontal addressing (eye contact) led to enhanced PSI and enhanced retention performance in a 2×2 experiment with university students. [→ Maik Beege 2019](#maik-beege-2019)

## Evidence

### Maik Beege 2019

Maik Beege. (2019). Parasocial Learning – The Impacts of Social Entities in Learning with Educational Videos. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.05.001

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Experiment with 88 university students watching a statistics lecture filmed from a frontal or 40° lateral angle; frontal addressing enhanced PSI and retention. "Frontal addressing led to an enhanced PSI" with ηp2=.09 and ηp2=.14 for retention.

> "Frontal addressing led to an enhanced PSI, F(1,87)=7.44, p=.008, ηp2=.09, and an enhanced retention performance, F(1,87)=13.17, p<.001, ηp2=.14."
