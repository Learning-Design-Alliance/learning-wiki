Herrmann 2019¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In a randomized trial of 100 elementary schools with half assigned to the program, the study reports that "principals did not increase the number of times they observed teachers" despite faithful implementation. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"Although the program was implemented as planned, principals did not increase the number of times they observed teachers."
