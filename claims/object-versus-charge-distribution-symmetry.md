## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student understanding of symmetry and Gauss’s law of electricity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07376](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07376)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Findings from interviews and written responses on Problems 14–16, in which six point charges were distributed equidistantly on an insulating sphere so the charge distribution lacks spherical symmetry; the most common difficulty on Problem 14 was assuming equal field magnitude at three points.

> "Interviews and written responses suggest that many students incorrectly believed that a spherical symmetry exists in this case for exploiting Gauss’s law to calculate the electric ﬁeld readily."
