## Evidence

### La Cerra Carmen 2019

La Cerra Carmen, Dante Angelo, Caponnetto Valeria, Franconi Ilaria, Gaxhja Elona, Petrucci Cristina, Alfes Celeste M, Lancia Loreto. (2019). Effects of high-fidelity simulation based on life-threatening clinical condition scenarios on learning outcomes of undergraduate and postgraduate nursing students: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open 9(2):e025306. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025306

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effect meta-analysis of self-efficacy self-rating instruments (e.g., Resuscitation Self-Efficacy Scale). The pooled effect was negligible and non-significant: "d=0.05, 95% CI [−0.45 to 0.55], Z-test=0.20, p=0.840".

> "No significant differences were detected between HFPS and control groups for the satisfaction (d=0.38, 95% CI [−0.01 to 0.77], Z-test=1.90, p=0.053), self-confidence (d=0.21, 95% CI [−0.02 to 0.43], Z-test=1.75, p=0.072) and self-efficacy (d=0.05, 95% CI [−0.45 to 0.55], Z-test=0.20, p=0.840)"
