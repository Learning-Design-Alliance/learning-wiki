# Regression shows a significant positive relationship between quiz marks and students' interaction in both colleges, with quiz marks explaining 75.6% (IT) and 70.3% (Business) of interaction variance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the IT College, mean quiz marks significantly predict mean interaction responses (F = 55.867, sig. 0.00, R-square change 0.756). [→ Alqudah 2019](#alqudah-2019)

## Evidence

### Alqudah 2019

Alqudah, D., Bidin, A., & Md Hussin, M. A. H. (2019). The Impact of Educational Infographic on Students’ Interaction and Perception in Jordanian Higher Education: Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 669-688. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12443a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simple regression analysis with mean quiz marks (MM) as independent variable and mean questionnaire interaction responses (MI) as dependent variable, conducted separately for each college's experimental group. The IT model reached significance at 0.00 with F = 55.867 and an R-square change of 0.756; the Business model reached significance at 0.00 with F = 85.195 and an R-square change of 0.703. R-square is variance explained, not a standardised effect size.

> "the level of significance is 0.00, which is less than 0.05 when the value of F is 55.867. Therefore, H0 can be rejected. Thus, using infographics by instructional lecture has a significant impact on the interaction of students."
