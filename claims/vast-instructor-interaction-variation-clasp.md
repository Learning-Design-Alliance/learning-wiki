## Evidence

### Emily A. West 2012

Emily A. West, Cassandra A. Paul, David Webb, and Wendell H. Potter. (2012). Variation of Instructor-Student Interactions in an Introductory Interactive Physics Course. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Systematic observational results from RIOT-coded discussion/lab sections of a reformed introductory physics course, reporting that instructor-student interaction variation occurs 'in the relative amount of small group (SG) versus whole class discussion (WCD) time' and in interaction types within each mode.

> "This variation occurs in the relative amount of small group (SG) versus whole class discussion (WCD) time, the type of interactions that occur during SG time, and the type of interactions that occur during WCD time."
