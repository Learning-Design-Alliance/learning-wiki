In the multicenter survey, paired-samples t-tests contrasted operational AI usage (e.g., writing effective prompts) against critical AI awareness (biases, deepfakes); the printed means and p-value support the overestimation pattern.

"the results revealed a statistically significant difference (𝑝= 0.015), with students reporting higher confidence in their critical evaluation skills (𝑀= 4.28) than in their operational skills (𝑀= 4.15)."
