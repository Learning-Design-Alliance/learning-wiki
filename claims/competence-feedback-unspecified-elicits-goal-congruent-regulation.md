# With explicit competence feedback, performance-approach and performance-avoidance goals predict competitive and protective regulation respectively when partner competence is higher or unspecified, but not equal (Study 3)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims

`q3 i1` Performance-approach goals were more positively associated with competitive regulation when partner competence was higher or non-specified than when equal (interaction β = .14, p < .05, partial η² = .014, a small effect). [→ Sommet 2015](#sommet-2015)
`q3 i1` Performance-avoidance goals were more positively associated with protective regulation when partner competence was higher (β = .27, p < .03) or non-specified (β = .24, p = .05) than when equal (interaction β = .14, p < .04, partial η² = .015, a small effect). [→ Sommet 2015 (2)](#sommet-2015-2)

`q3 i1` The contrast of interest (higher/unspecified vs. equal competence) interacted with performance-approach goals on competitive regulation. [→ Sommet 2015](#sommet-2015-2)

`q3 i1` The same contrast interacted with performance-avoidance goals on protective regulation. [→ Sommet 2015 (2)](#sommet-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Sommet 2015

Sommet, N., Darnon, C., & Butera, F. (2015). To Confirm or to Conform? Performance Goals as a Regulator of Conflict With More-Competent Others. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 580–598. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037240

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Internet study with 289 university students who received bogus competence scores (equal 65/100, superior 80/100, or no feedback) before disagreeing with a fictitious partner about the etiology of bipolar disorder. The contrast of interest (higher + unspecified vs. equal) interacted with performance-approach goals as hypothesized, partial η² = .014 (small effect).

> "More important, in line with our first hypothesis, the interaction between our contrast of interest and performance-approach goals was significant, /H9252/H11005.14, F(1, 277) /H110054.09, p /H11021.05, /H9257p2 /H11005.014"
