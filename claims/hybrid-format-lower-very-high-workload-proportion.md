Results-section analysis of workload categories across the three 2023 EDAIC formats. Table 2 shows 18 (8%) of examiners at TLX ≥ 80 overall, with only 2 (3%) in the hybrid group versus 13 (12%) online. The article reports the hybrid proportion as "significantly lower" (p = 0.0484).

> "Seventy six percent of examiners expressed high workload (≥50), with no significant difference across examination type, however in Hybrid examination the proportion of examiners who expressed very high workload was significantly lower comparing to other examination types (p = 0.0484)."
