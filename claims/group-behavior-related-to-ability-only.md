# Behavior in the group was related to ability but not to gender, ethnic background, or group composition characteristics

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who solved problems aloud during group work had higher ability than students in the other behavior categories, who did not differ in ability; behavior was unrelated to gender, ethnic background, or group characteristics. [→ Webb 1993](#webb-1993)

## Evidence

### Webb 1993

Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of variance relating pretest ability to the five behavior categories among the 53 students. The article reports the F test as printed and states that students in the latter four behavior categories did not differ in ability, and that behavior was not related to any group profile variable.

> "students who solved the problems aloud during group work had higher ability than students who indicated a need for help, copied other students' work, or did not participate, F(4, 48) = 4.13, p < .006"
