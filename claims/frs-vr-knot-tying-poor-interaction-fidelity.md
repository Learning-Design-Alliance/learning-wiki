## Evidence

### Deravi Noosha D. 2026

Deravi Noosha D., Behrens Nathan, Warnock Brielle, Hunt Maya L., Stefanidis Dimitrios, Holmstrom Amy, Maxey Katie Stanton, Tarras Samantha, Ritter E. Matthew. (2026). Training benchmarks for the Fundamentals of Robotic Surgery virtual reality tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12957-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert repetitions on the SimNow platform showed four Knot Tying repetitions scored zero due to critical error from tower avulsion. The authors attribute this to "poor interaction fidelity as the VR suture does not exhibit the same behavioral properties as physical suture."

> "Knot Tying was the lowest-scoring task and demonstrated the greatest variability in performance among experts (Table 2)."
