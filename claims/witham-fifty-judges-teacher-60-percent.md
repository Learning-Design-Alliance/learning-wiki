### Ernest C. Witham 1913

Ernest C. Witham. (1913). School and Teacher Measurement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Witham+school+and+teacher+measurement

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A judgment-survey in which the author polled fifty prominent school men for proportions of 100 across six school factors. The printed averages are: the teacher 60%, course of study 10%, sanitation and hygiene 10%, outside influences 10%, building 5%, equipment 5%.

> "fifty prominent school men including superintendents, college presidents, college professors of education, teachers and normal and high school principals, from Maine to California, were asked to give the proportions of 100 they would assign to these six factors."
