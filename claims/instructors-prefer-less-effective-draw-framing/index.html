Evidence¶

Sally P.W. Wu 2019¶

Sally P.W. Wu, Jainaba Corr, Martina A. Rau. (2019). How instructors frame students' interactions with educational technologies can enhance or reduce learning with multiple representations. Computers & Education 128 (2019) 199–213. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.012

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with four TAs, two from each experimental condition. The article reports that all TAs recommended students draw on paper and that instructors favored the drawing-focused practice despite its lower measured effectiveness.

"In contrast to our results, instructors seemed to favor the practice of generating intermediate drawings on paper: the framing practice that was less effective than the control condition."
