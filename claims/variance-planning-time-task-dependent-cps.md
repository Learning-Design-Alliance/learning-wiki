## Evidence

### Eichmann 2019

Eichmann, B., Goldhammer, F., Greiff, S., Pucite, L., & Naumann, J. (2019). The role of planning in complex problem solving. Computers & Education, 128, 1-12. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.08.004

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

GLMM analysis of log data from N = 1346 German students on 21 interactive PISA 2012 CPS tasks found no significant fixed effect for the variance indicator (estimate = -1.85, p = 0.155) but a significant random effect with correlation r = -0.39 with task easiness, indicating "in di ﬃcult tasks a high variation of time is bene ﬁcial while in easy tasks a low variation is bene ﬁcial."

> "The cor- relation between task easiness and the e ﬀect of the indicator was r = −0.39. The random e ﬀect indicates that in di ﬃcult tasks a high variation of time is bene ﬁcial while in easy tasks a low variation is bene ﬁcial."
