Twyford 1955¶

Twyford, L., abstractor. (1955). Audio-Visual Communication Review research abstracts: training device effectiveness studies. Audio-Visual Communication Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Audio-Visual+Communication+Review

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Experimental groups at a military setting received instruction on the hull trainer or the mockup instead of practicing starting and stopping the M47 tank; effectiveness was measured by a performance test plus a paper-and-pencil test, with groups equated for intelligence and one instructor for all groups.

"For teaching starting and stopping procedures the inexpensive mockup was found to be a better training aid than the tank hull trainer. Actual participation was not found to be required."
