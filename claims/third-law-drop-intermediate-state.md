### Camp 2010 (2)

Camp, P. J. (2010). U-shaped Development of Newtonian Concepts: Implications for Pedagogical Design and Research Practice. Spelman College. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.1647](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.1647)
