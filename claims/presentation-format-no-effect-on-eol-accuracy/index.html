Evidence¶

Jemstedt 2017¶

Jemstedt, A., Kubik, V., & Jönsson, F. U. (2017). What moderates the accuracy of ease of learning judgments? Metacognition and Learning, 12, 337–355. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-017-9172-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 3 mixed-design study with 64 psychology students compared sequential and simultaneous EOL judgment conditions; the hypothesis that simultaneous presentation would improve accuracy was not supported, F(1, 56) = .48, p = .491. Absolute accuracy also did not differ by format.

"There was no main effect of presentation format, that is, EOL accuracy was the same independent of whether judgments were made with items presented sequentially (M = .63,SD = .18) or simultaneously (M = .60,SD =. 2 8 ) , F(1, 56) = .48,p = .491, mean difference = .03, 95% CI = [−.05, .10],d = .12."
