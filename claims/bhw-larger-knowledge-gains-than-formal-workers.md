## Evidence

### Ku Grace Marie V. 2014

Ku Grace Marie V., Kegels Guy. (2014). Integrating chronic care with primary care activities: enriching healthcare staff knowledge and skills and improving glycemic control of a cohort of people with diabetes through the First Line Diabetes Care Project in the Philippines. Global Health Action. https://doi.org/10.3402/gha.v7.25286

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparison of knowledge change between the 87 barangay health workers and 23 formal healthcare workers (health officer, nurses, midwives) among the 110 tested; the article reports the BHW improvement "was more marked" with the printed averages and p=0.013.

> "The improvement in knowledge was more marked among the BHW, whose post-test knowledge ratings increased by an average of 25.0%, as compared to the formal healthcare workers whose post-test knowledge ratings increased by an average of 12.5% (p=0.013)"
