## Evidence

### Monroe Katherine S 2016

Monroe Katherine S. (2016). The relationship between assessment methods and self-directed learning readiness in medical education. International Journal of Medical Education, 7:75-80. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.56bd.b282

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of five evaluation metrics (GPA, OSCE, USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, clerkship evaluations) against four SDLRS subscales in 77 fourth-year medical students at one US medical school. The author reports that "no combination of students’ grades and/or test scores overwhelmingly predicted their aptitude for self-directed learning."

> "Given individual differences among participants for the variables considered, no combination of students’ grades and/or test scores overwhelmingly predicted their aptitude for self-directed learning."
