## Evidence

### Major ME 2020

Major ME, Ramaekers SPJ, Engelbert RHH, Van der Schaaf M. (2020). Preparing undergraduate students for clinical work in a complex environment: evaluation of an e-learning module on physiotherapy in the intensive care unit. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02035-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of data from 14 students (three focus groups) and 5 experts (semi-structured interviews) in a mixed methods proof of concept study. The authors report that learning objectives with regards to "recognition, interpretation, understanding and simple application were achieved". No effect size is printed.

> "This study confirms that an e-learning module is a feasible and valuable teaching method to prepare international undergraduates for intensive care unit physiotherapy (ICU-PT), as learning objectives with regards to recognition, interpretation, understanding and simple application were achieved."
