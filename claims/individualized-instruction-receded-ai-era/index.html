The review's Research Question 3 synthesis, contrasting teacher-designed individualized instruction with AI-assisted personalized learning that relies on learner models, algorithmic recommendations, and data-driven adaptation; the review notes emerging hybrid models combining teacher expertise and system-generated recommendations.

"Only one study used individualized instruction as its primary term, indicating that it has largely receded in recent AI-focused literature; where it appeared, it referred to teacher-designed variation in content and pacing based on professional judgment rather than automated analytics."
