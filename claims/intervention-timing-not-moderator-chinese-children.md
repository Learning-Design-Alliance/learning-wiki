### Yijun Ruan 2024

Yijun Ruan, Urs Maurer, Catherine McBride. (2024). Effectiveness of Reading Interventions on Literacy Skills for Chinese Children with and Without Dyslexia: a Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09915-9

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression on intervention timing as moderator, with children younger than age 9 (preschool to grade 3) classified as young and grade 4 to 6 as old. Both age groups showed significant effects, described as comparable.

> "In addition, reading intervention improved literacy skills of older children (g = 0.90) and younger children (g = 0.63) compa-rably."
