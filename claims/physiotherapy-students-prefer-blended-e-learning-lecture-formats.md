## Evidence

### Gardner P 2016

Gardner P, Slater H, Jordan JE, Fary RE, Chua J, Briggs AM. (2016). Physiotherapy students’ perspectives of online e-learning for interdisciplinary management of chronic health conditions: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0593-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive grounded-theory analysis of three focus groups with 23 physiotherapy students (78% female, mean age 23 years). Key theme 1 reports that "blended delivery modes were considered important" and that students cited flexibility and comprehensive information as e-learning advantages.

> "While students perceived online e-learning as acceptable, blended delivery modes were considered important for the chronic disease management curriculum."
