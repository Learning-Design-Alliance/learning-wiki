Evidence¶

Jinxi Wen 2025¶

Jinxi Wen, Wenjing Lyu, Jin Liu & Li Yang. (2025). Commensality and academic performance: measuring peer acceptance among college students. npj Science of Learning 10:69. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00357-8

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

Subsample OLS regressions (Table 4, N = 2241 members vs 244 non-members first semester). For members, third-category β = 0.087, p < 0.001; for non-members, no first-semester significance and only roommate commensality in semester two (β = 0.197, p = 0.048).

"the short-term link between peer relationships to academic performance varies signiﬁcantly between party members or lea- gue members and non-members"

Jinxi Wen 2025 (2)¶

Jinxi Wen, Wenjing Lyu, Jin Liu & Li Yang. (2025). Commensality and academic performance: measuring peer acceptance among college students. npj Science of Learning 10:69. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00357-8

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

Subsample OLS regressions by class-leader experience (Table 5, N = 1132 non-leaders vs 1353 leaders). Sustained: leaders' roommate commensality "(β = 0.086, p = 0.008)" and diversity index "(β = 0.136, p < 0.001)"; non-leaders only third-category commensality significant (β = 0.126, p = 0.003).

"former class leaders continue to show greater links to peer rela- tionships"

Jinxi Wen 2025 (3)¶

Jinxi Wen, Wenjing Lyu, Jin Liu & Li Yang. (2025). Commensality and academic performance: measuring peer acceptance among college students. npj Science of Learning 10:69. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00357-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Subsample regressions for Han (N = 2183) versus minority (N = 302) students (Table 7 and accompanying text). Among Han students, third-category commensality β = 0.074 (p < 0.01) first semester and β = 0.142 (p < 0.001) sustained; among minorities these were not significant.

"For ethnic minority students, ho wever, the association with peer accep- tance is more pronounced in the number of failed subjects rather than for average scores or rankings"
