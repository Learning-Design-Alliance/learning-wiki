## Evidence

### Borchers 2025

Borchers, C., Fleischer, H., Schanze, S., Scheiter, K., & Aleven, V. (2025). High scaffolding of an unfamiliar strategy improves conceptual learning but reduces enjoyment compared to low scaffolding and strategy freedom. Computers & Education, 236, 105364. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105364

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A crossover experiment with 61 German undergraduates in remedial chemistry preparatory courses compared learning gains from two tutoring systems. ANOVA on conceptual pre/post scores showed significantly larger gains with StoichTutor than ORCCA, "suggesting that students improved more with StoichTutor (Cohen's d = 0.42) than ORCCA".

> "For conceptual learning gain, ANOVA revealed significant differences in gains between students using ORCCA and StoichTutor based on conceptual pre- and post-tests, F(1, 59) = 6.47, p = .014, suggesting that students improved more with StoichTutor (Cohen's d = 0.42) than ORCCA (Cohen's d = 0.10; see Table 3)."
