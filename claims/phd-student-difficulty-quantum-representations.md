## Evidence

### Marshman 2024

Marshman, E., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2024). Using multiple representations to improve student understanding of quantum states. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pretest data from 97 first-year Ph.D. students after traditional instruction. The article reports a dichotomy: 79% wrote ⟨p|p′⟩ = δ(p−p′) on Q5, "but only 54% correctly answered Q3", indicating many recall expressions without knowing what they mean physically.

> "on Q6, 65% of the Ph.D. students correctly wrote that ⟨𝑥|𝑝′⟩ = 𝑒𝑖𝑝′𝑥 ℏ⁄ (as compared to only 13% of the undergraduate students). But only 55% correctly answered Q2"
