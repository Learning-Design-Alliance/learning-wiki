## Evidence

### Lisa F. Smith 2013

Lisa F. Smith, Kimberly K. Arcand, Jeffrey K. Smith, Randall K. Smith, Jay Bookbinder, Megan Watzke. (2013). Examining Perceptions Of Astronomy Images Across Mobile Platforms. [doi:10.22323/2.13020201](https://doi.org/10.22323/2.13020201)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same online survey (2183 respondents), the item asking which label conveys the information best about the image favoured the question and answer format 66.1% to 33.9%, "at p <.001". The fun fact format was chosen only for Saturn. No effect size is reported.

> "the question and answer label format (66.1%) again was preferred significantly more than the fun fact label (33.9%), at p <.001."
