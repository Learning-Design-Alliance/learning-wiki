## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A significant negative interaction between fall GM scores and A2i treatment (p = .015) indicated larger impacts for students who began the year as weaker readers. [→ Connor 2022](#connor-2022)

## Evidence

### Connor 2022

Connor, Carol McDonald; May, Henry; Sparapani, Nicole; Hwang, Jin Kyoung; Adams, Ashley; Wood, Taffeta S.; Siegal, Sarah; Wolfe, Cassidy; Day, Stephanie. (2022). Bringing Assessment-to-Instruction (A2i) Technology to Scale: Exploring the Process from Development to Implementation. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000758

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM impact models for grades 2–3 that added fall pretest scores and a baseline-literacy by treatment interaction; the interaction was significant (p = .015) with no effect size printed.

> "For GM, the main effect of A2i was not significant (p = .36), but there was a significant negative coefficient of the interaction with fall GM scores (p = .015). This suggests that the impact of A2i was greater for students with lower initial GM scores."
