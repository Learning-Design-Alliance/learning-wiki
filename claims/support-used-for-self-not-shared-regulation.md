# Students reported using the collaboration support mainly for self-regulation of their own contribution rather than for socially shared regulation of the group's participation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across GAT and GAT + Prompt conditions, students' reported use of the support was higher for individual self-regulation than for group-level shared regulation, and the conditions did not differ significantly in either use. [→ Strauß 2021](#strauß-2021)

## Evidence
