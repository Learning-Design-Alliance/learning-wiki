## Evidence

### Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021

Puthiaparampil Thomas, Rahman Mizanur. (2021). How important is distractor efficiency for grading Best Answer Questions? BMC Medical Education, 21:29. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02463-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive psychometrics of the 7 BAQ tests of 50 items each (Table 1), from final professional examinations 2013-14 to 2018-19 with student numbers 24–122 per test. The authors report these were "comparable with that of other studies".

> "The mean DIFi ranged from 0.53 to 0.69, mean DISi from 0.12 to 0.22, items with 100% DE from 2–16%, items with 50% and above DE from 50–68% and the mean FDs/item from 1.48 to 2.16."
