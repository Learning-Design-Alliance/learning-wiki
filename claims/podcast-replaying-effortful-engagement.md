## Evidence

### Sydney Enock Msonde 2017

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interviews with three students per section, selected to cover low, medium and high pre-test performance, conducted at the end of each unit. The article reports the 67% figure descriptively for interviewed students' perceptions of podcast replaying; no inferential test is reported.

> "The majority (67%) of interviewed stu-dents perceived effortful engagement for repeatedly replaying of podcasts material enhanced their learning; others perceived the social interaction to be a new experience; and a few students felt that carrying out reﬂection was a new learning experience"
