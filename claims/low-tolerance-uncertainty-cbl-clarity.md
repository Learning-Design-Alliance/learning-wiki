### Bruen Catherine 2025

Bruen Catherine, Illing Jan, Daly Ronan, Meagher Frances, Delany Caroline, Offiah Gozie, Doherty Sally, Stuart Ellen, Crehan Martina, Kelly Helen. (2025). Medical student experiences of Case-Based Learning (CBL) at a multicultural medical school. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06585-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Learning point from the SGFS data: students requested the facilitator's answer sheet after sessions and facilitator-led wrap-ups; the authors link the lower uncertainty tolerance to Hofstede's cultural dimensions for students from non-Western cultures.

> "Some students who are at the early stages of becoming an active learner may have a lower tolerance of uncertainty and need clarity at the end of the case either from a facilitator who is perceived as credible (i.e., clinician) or additional information is provided to reinforce the key learning points."
