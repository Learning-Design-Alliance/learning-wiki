## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported GPA was negatively associated with academic, family, friend, practical and emotional needs, but not significantly with financial needs. [→ Tindle 2022](#tindle-2022)

## Evidence

### Tindle 2022

Tindle, R., Castillo, P., Doring, N., Grant, L., & Willis, R. (2022). Developing and validating a university needs instrument to measure the psychosocial needs of university students. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1550–1570. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12515

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational analysis in the 511-student sample: GPA correlated negatively with five of the six UNI factors (Table 3 reports r values from −.14 to −.29, all significant), while the financial needs correlation (−.08) was not significant. The authors interpret this as needs possibly contributing to reduced academic performance.

> "Students' GPA was negatively associated with their psychosocial needs, except for their financial needs."
