## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2013

Beth Thacker, Hani Dulli, Dave Pattillo, and Keith West. (2013). Lessons from a Large-Scale Assessment: Results from Conceptual Inventories. Physics Department, Texas Tech University. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post FCI assessment of algebra-based large lecture sections comparing T labs with RT labs plus recitations under PER-informed lecture; the article reports a gain of "0.36 +/- 0.02", a "64% increase". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "When T labs are replaced by RT labs plus recitations, the gain increases to 0.36 +/- 0.02, a 64% increase. With both RT labs and PERL instruction, normalized gain is in the interactive engagement region on a "Hake" plot."
