# In one case-study school, a principal's lack of Framework knowledge and inability to rate practice led teachers to perceive the new evaluation as subjective, undermining buy-in

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` At Walton Elementary, teachers perceived the pilot evaluation as subjective because the principal lacked the instructional knowledge to use the Framework well, and many disengaged from the process. [→ Sartain 2011](#sartain-2011)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study of Walton Elementary School across two pilot years, drawing on principal and teacher interviews. The case concludes that "the principal's lack of knowledge of the Framework and inability to rate teaching practice led teachers to believe the pilot evaluation was subjective," and many teachers disengaged as a result.
