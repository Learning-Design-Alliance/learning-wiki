## Evidence

### Nagle 2016

Nagle, K., Pratt-Williams, J., Schmidt, R., Swantek, C., Lyulchenko, M., & McGhee, R. (2016). Evaluation of the Rural Math Excel Partnership Project: Final Report. SRI International. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/projects/other/i3_ruralmath.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The impact study used a quasi-experimental matched comparison design at the LEA level, comparing SOL scores of treatment and comparison students before and after the intervention. The evaluators report no impact on achievement, possibly because of content misalignment between RMEP and SOL exams, sample dilution, short implementation duration, and small sample size.

> "SRI evaluators found that the RMEP Project had no impact on students' achievement or attitudes."
