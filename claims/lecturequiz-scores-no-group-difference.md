Mixed ANOVA on LLM-evaluated LectureQuiz scores (six questions after each weekly lecture): no significant Group effect (F(1,26)=2.64, p=0.11), Week effect (F(2,52)=1.59, p=0.21), or Group × Week interaction (F(2,52)=0.55, p=0.58); no effect size printed.
