### Berg Helen 2020

Berg Helen, Steinsbekk Aslak. (2020). Is individual practice in an immersive and interactive virtual reality application non-inferior to practicing with traditional equipment in learning systematic clinical observation? A randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02030-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis of individual observations in the practical test from the randomized controlled trial. "students in VR did better" on respiratory frequency (97.3% vs 89.3%, p = 0.006); other observations (airways, saturation, blood pressure, pulse, disability, temperature, skin) showed no significant differences.

> "The outcome which showed a difference was the reporting of Respiratory frequency where students in VR did better (97.3% correct in VR vs 89.3% in TP, absolute difference 8% points, 95% CI 2.3 to 13.8)"
