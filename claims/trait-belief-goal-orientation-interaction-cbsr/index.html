Evidence¶

Compagnoni 2019¶

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

ANCOVA interaction term (N = 147, controlled for EF, gender, age); the profile diagram (Fig. 2) shows mastery-oriented children with malleable trait beliefs tended toward higher CBSR. The effect is marginal (p = .11) and should be treated as suggestive.

"However, the interaction term of trait beliefs and goal orientation on CBSR showed a small effect size (F(1, 132) = 2.61, p =. 1 1 , p a r t i a lη 2 = .019, f = .14), indicating that children with a mastery orientation tended to show higher CBSR when they adopted a view of traits as malleable"
