Evidence¶

Awofala 2014¶

Awofala, A. O. A. (2014). Examining Personalisation of Instruction, Attitudes toward and Achievement in Mathematics Word Problems among Nigerian Senior Secondary School Students. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology, 2(4), 273-288. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1059026

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-post non-equivalent control group study of 350 senior secondary year one students (experimental n=183, control n=167) in intact classes. The MANOVA on post-treatment ATMWPI subscale scores was significant, F(7, 342) = 12.46, p = .000, with "η 2 p=.20"; the author notes this multivariate partial eta squared of 0.20 was described as small in magnitude, with 80% of variance unexplained.

"The F ratio for MANOVA showed that the post -treatment mean scores were statistically significant at the 0.05 level, F(7, 342) = 12.46, p = .000, η 2 p=.20."
