## Evidence

### Bernardini 2004

Bernardini, P., & Schlyter, S. (2004). Growing syntactic structure and code-mixing in the weaker language: The Ivy Hypothesis. Bilingualism: Language and Cognition, 7(1), 49-69. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1366728904001270

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors relate documented differences in input quantity (daycare language, parental language use, contact with grandparents) to the measured syntactic asymmetry between languages. They state "The amount of input in the two languages is clearly related to language dominance."

> "The amount of input in the two languages is clearly related to language dominance: the reduced input in French for Paul, L´eo and Alex, in Swedish for Alice, and in Italian for Lukas can be related to their syntactic development in their Weaker Language, according to the measures presented below."
