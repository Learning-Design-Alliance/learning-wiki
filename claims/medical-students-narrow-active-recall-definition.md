### Heng W 2025

Heng W, Ho MH, Mah XH, Lim JJ, Syamsury NBHA, Haagensen E, Ong ELC, Hubbard P. (2025). Exploring pre‐clinical medical students' perception of and participation in active learning: A mixed‐methods transnational study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15611

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online questionnaire of 266 pre-clinical students across UK and Malaysia campuses. In the definition section, "94.7% total of participants agreed" that active learning is important; word-tree analysis (Figures 1–2) showed definitions centred on 'active recall'.

> "When asked if ‘Active learning is important for medical students’ 94.7% total of participants agreed (Figure 3a)."
