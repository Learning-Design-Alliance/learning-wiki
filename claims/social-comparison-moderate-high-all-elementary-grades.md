## Evidence

### Schneider 2020

Rebecca Schneider, Jörn R. Sparfeldt. (2020). Academic competence and affect self-concepts in elementary school students: Social and dimensional comparisons. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-019-09532-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional GI/E path analyses of N = 858 German second-to-fourth graders using reported grades and SDQ I self-concept factors. The study found "path coefficients on corresponding competence self-concepts were of large size for mathematics" with the printed β ranges; no standardized effect size beyond the path coefficients is printed.

> "Concerning social comparison processes, path coefficients on corresponding competence self-concepts were of large size for mathematics (.57 ≤ β ≤ .74) and of medium size for German (.31 ≤ β ≤ .43)."

### Schneider 2020 (2)

Rebecca Schneider, Jörn R. Sparfeldt. (2020). Academic competence and affect self-concepts in elementary school students: Social and dimensional comparisons. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-019-09532-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary GI/E analyses with n = 349 first graders using teacher-reported grade equivalents. The study reports "the path coefficient on the corresponding competence self-concept was of medium size for mathematics (β = .45)" and high for German, indicating social comparisons already in first grade.

> "Concerning social comparison processes, the path coefficient on the corresponding competence self-concept was of medium size for mathematics (β = .45) and of high magnitude for German (β = .52)."
