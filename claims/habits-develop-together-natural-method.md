Later studies of learning, including the typewriting work, showed that in natural learning "the lower-order habits are perfected in and through the formation of the higher," refuting the logical method of perfecting elements first. The article cites Bryan and Harter's telegraphy findings as supporting evidence.

> "the lower-order habits are perfected in and through the formation of the higher even as the further development of the higher is dependent upon the perfection of the lower"
