`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Final results of a multisite randomized experiment of the Talent Transfer Initiative, which offered a $20,000 incentive to high-performing teachers to move to low-performing schools. The report states the intervention "had positive effects on student test scores in math and reading in elementary schools."

> "The intervention had positive effects on student test scores in math and reading in elementary schools—the equivalent of a 4 to 10 percentile point increase."
