# Attendance at SI-PASS meetings correlates positively with final exam performance within the experimental group

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among SI-PASS participants, the number of meetings attended correlated positively with exam performance (r = .28). [→ Puttaert 2025](#puttaert-2025)

## Evidence

### Puttaert 2025

Puttaert, N., Dachet, D., Baye, A., Quertemont, E., Leduc, L., Nyssen, A.-S., & Verpoorten, D. (2025). Effects of SI-PASS on a high-risk course – A randomized controlled trial. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102042. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102042

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the experimental group relating number of SI-PASS meetings attended to final exam grades. The regression "exhibits a positive correlation (r(90) = .28, p = .007)", a printed effect size of r = .28, shown in Fig. 1 as a trend where higher engagement tends toward better performance.

> "The linear regression calculated on participation in SI-PASS meetings exhibits a positive correlation (r(90) = .28, p = .007) between students' attendance at SI-PASS meetings and their exam performance (Fig. 1)."
