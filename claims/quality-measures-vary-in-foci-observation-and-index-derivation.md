Descriptive review statement about the most widely used scales — CLASS, ECERS-3 (and predecessors), ITERS and SSTEW — which "share the aim of identifying high-quality provision" yet differ in foci, observation approach and index derivation.

> "yet vary widely in foci (e.g. the balance of structural and process quality dimen- sions they consider; curricular or interactional aspects of quality included), approach to observing quality (e.g. sustained observation over half a day or more; frequent but brief observation intervals; self-reported recollections) and their approaches for deriving quality indices therefrom"
