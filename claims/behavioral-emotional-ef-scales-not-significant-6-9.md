### Quílez-Robres A. 2021

Quílez-Robres A., Moyano N., Cortés-Pascual A. (2021). Task Monitoring and Working Memory as Executive Components Predictive of General and Specific Academic Achievements in 6–9-Year-Old Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18136681

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null finding from the correlation and regression analyses of the 133-child sample; Table 1 prints non-significant correlations for inhibition (r = −0.154), self-monitoring (r = −0.080), flexibility (r = −0.117), and emotional control (r = −0.026) with general achievement. No effect size is printed for the null tests.

> "The scales that comprise the index of behavioral regulation (inhibition and self-monitoring) and emotional regulation (flexibility and emotional control) have not been found to be relevant in explaining academic achievement in students aged 6–9."
