### Hegerius A 2020

Hegerius A, Caduff-Janosa P, Savage R, Ellenius J. (2020). E-Learning in Pharmacovigilance: An Evaluation of Microlearning-Based Modules Developed by Uppsala Monitoring Centre. Drug Safety. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40264-020-00981-w

Survey results from 550 learners for modules 1-4 and 223 learners for module 5 showed that "97.2% and 94.9% of the learners, respectively, selected either 'excellent' or 'good'."

> "When asked to rate modules 1–4 and module 5, 97.2% and 94.9% of the learners, respectively, selected either 'excellent' or 'good'."
