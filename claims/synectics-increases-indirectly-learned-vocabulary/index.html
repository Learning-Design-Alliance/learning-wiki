Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT 2017¶

Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT. (2017). THE EFFECTIVENESS OF SYNECTICS INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL ON FOREIGN LANGUAGE VOCABULARY TEACHING. International Journal of Languages’ Education and Teaching, Volume 5, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.18298/ijlet.1753

Counts of indirectly learned words collected via the third part of the achievement test in each of the four groups. A second evidence basis is the posttest mean percentage of 88.28 vs 66.38 (t=9.69, p<.00, d=2.08); the article states experimental students "learned about five times more words".

"the number of learned words indirectly during the process is 50 in the first experimental group, 47 in the second experimental group, 1 3 in the first control group and 11 in the second control group."
