## Evidence

### Catherine P. Bradshaw 2012

Catherine P. Bradshaw, Tracy E. Waasdorp, Philip J. Leaf. (2012). Effects of School-Wide Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports on Child Behavior Problems. Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2012-0243

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Group randomized controlled effectiveness trial in 37 elementary schools; 3-level HLM on teacher TOCA-C ratings at 5 time points over 4 years. Children in SWPBIS schools had "lower levels of aggressive and disruptive behaviors compared with those in the control schools" (ES = 0.12).
