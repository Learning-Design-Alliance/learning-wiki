### Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh 2005

Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview task comparing two wheels with different rim masses and an attached putty piece: per the interview record, "About 20% of the students claimed that nothing would be diﬀerent in the two cases", while successive hints enabled "about 80% of them through the correct reasoning". No clear difference in assistance required emerged between introductory and advanced students.

> "About 20% of the students claimed that nothing would be diﬀerent in the two cases. However, a few hints were enough to drive about 80% of them through the correct reasoning."
