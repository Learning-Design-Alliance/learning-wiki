### Robertson Eleanor 2015

Robertson Eleanor, Morgan Lauren, New Steve, Pickering Sharon, Hadi Mohammed, Collins Gary, Rivero Arias Oliver, Griffin Damian, McCulloch Peter. (2015). Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical outcome analysis using hospital episode statistics for all patients operated on by the involved consultants in the six months before and after the intervention, with logistic and linear regression adjusted for age and gender. The article reports "Complications and LOS both improved in the intervention group but worsened in the control group" with no significant changes (complication rate p = 0.08; LOS p = 0.095; readmission p = 0.33).

> "Complications and LOS both improved in the intervention group but worsened in the control group after the intervention, whilst readmissions went up in both groups. None of the clinical outcome changes achieved statistical significance."
