# Mathematical models (Gompertz for accuracy, Michaelis-Menten for time) significantly correlated both cases practiced and practice time with exam diagnostic accuracy (p<0.001), estimating 34 cases and about 112 minutes for 75% accuracy

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Fitted Gompertz and Michaelis-Menten functions correlated number of cases practiced and time spent practicing with exam diagnostic accuracy at p<0.001 for all coefficients. [→ Waechter Jason 2019](#waechter-jason-2019)
`q2 i?` The models estimate an average student needs 34 practice cases and about 112 minutes of practice to score 75% on the rhythm strip exam. [→ Waechter Jason 2019](#waechter-jason-2019)

## Evidence

### Waechter Jason 2019

Waechter Jason, Reading David, Lee Chel Hee, Walker Mathieu. (2019). Quantifying the medical student learning curve for ECG rhythm strip interpretation using deliberate practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc40. [doi:10.3205/zma001248](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001248)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Curve fitting on exam data from the observational cohort (Calgary exam data only; McGill excluded as contaminated). Gompertz (accuracy) and Michaelis-Menten (time) fits gave "p values<0.001 for all coefficients in the models" by AIC goodness-of-fit assessment.

> "We generated mathematical models that accurately correlated both the number of cases practiced and time spent practicing, with diagnostic accuracy on an exam (p<0.001)."
