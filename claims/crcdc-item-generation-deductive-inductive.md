## Evidence

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Deductive item generation from a systematic review searched across PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus, Web of Science, ProQuest, and Google Scholar. Findings as printed: 130 records identified, 28 retained for full-text review, 21 included, generating 10 preliminary items.

> "Of the 130 records initially identified, 28 articles were retained for full-text review after removal of duplicates and abstract screening. Ultimately, 21 studies met the inclusion criteria and were included in the final analysis. Content analysis of these studies resulted in the generation of 10 preliminary items."

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026 (2)

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Inductive item generation from conventional content analysis (Graneheim and Lundman) of semi-structured Skype interviews with 15 purposively sampled ICU nurses. Data saturation was operationally defined and occurred after the 15th interview.

> "Through constant comparison and iterative review, subcategories were further integrated into overarching categories. This analytic process resulted in the generation of 20 items, 7 subcategories, and 3 principal categories: Analytical–Evaluative Clinical Reflection (AECR), Person-Centered Ethical Reflection (PCER), and Risk-Oriented Preventive Reflection (ROPR)."
