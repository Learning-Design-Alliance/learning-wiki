Qualitative analysis of survey responses; concerns included that ChatGPT's training data ends in September 2021, potential bias, student misconceptions, and severe ramifications of incorrect lab-safety information.

### Gyeong-Geon Lee 2024 (2)

Gyeong-Geon Lee, and Xiaoming Zhai. (2024). Using ChatGPT for Science Learning: A Study on Pre-service Teachers' Lesson Planning. [doi:10.1109/tlt.2024.3401457](https://doi.org/10.1109/tlt.2024.3401457)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The article reports that almost every teacher raising over-dependence proposed remedies: diversifying information sources (teacher most cited, 3/11) and structuring learning processes around classroom interactions (11/29), such as pre-use digital literacy education, structured group discussions, and post-use reflection.

> "the pre -service teachers were also worried that students might be overly dependent on ChatGPT, given its feasibility and high accessibility (13/29)."
