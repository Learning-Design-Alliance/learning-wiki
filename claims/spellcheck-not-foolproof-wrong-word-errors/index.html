Evidence¶

Steven C. Pan 2021¶

Steven C. Pan, Timothy C. Rickard, Robert A. Bjork. (2021). Does Spelling Still Matter— and If So, How Should It Be Taught? Perspectives from Contemporary and Historical Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09611-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Lunsford and Lunsford's (2008) corpus analysis of student papers: overall error rate "had not substantially changed (i.e., 2.45 errors per 100 words in 2008, versus 2.26 and 2.11 errors per 100 words in 1988 and 1917"), with correctly spelled but incorrect words (e.g., homonyms) now the most common error at nearly 14% of mistakes.

"a follow-up study by Lunsford and Lunsford ( 2008) found that the overall rate of writing errors (of all types) had not substantially changed (i.e., 2.45 errors per 100 words in 2008"
