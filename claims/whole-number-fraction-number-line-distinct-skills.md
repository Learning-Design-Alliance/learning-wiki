## Evidence

### Namkung 2016

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary correlational analysis of the two number line measures in the study's sample of 139 at-risk fourth graders, reported before path modeling. The printed correlation r(137) = .16 was not significant, and the authors interpret this as indicating distinct estimation skills; equivalence was not tested.

> "The correlation between whole-number and fraction number line estimation measure was low and not significant, r(137) = .16, p = .070, suggesting that the two represent different estimation skills."
