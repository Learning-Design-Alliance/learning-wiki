## Evidence

### Alexander Small 2017

Alexander Small. (2017). Range restriction, admissions criteria, and correlation studies of standardized tests. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.02895](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.02895)

`q1 · i3` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation result from Table 1: the printed effect size r = 0.709 for the product with performance in the full pool exceeds r = 0.502 for GRE and r = 0.496 for experience, because the simulated performance function explicitly depends on the product of the two variables. The article cautions real performance may not be predictable from anything so simple.

> "if we examine correlations with the product of GRE score and research experience in Table 1, we see that this product has a stronger correlation with subsequent performance than any other variable."
