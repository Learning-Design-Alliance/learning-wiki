## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Videotaped-lesson fidelity ratings showed statistically significant and fairly substantial differences favoring treatment teachers on the total fidelity score (ES = 1.31) and on generating an estimate prior to solving (ES = 1.76). [→ Jitendra 2017](#jitendra-2017)

## Evidence

### Jitendra 2017

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Karl, S. R., Im, S-H., & Slater, S. C. (2017). Investigating a Tier 1 intervention focused on proportional reasoning: A follow-up study. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 83(4), 340-358. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402917691017

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity analysis of one videotaped lesson per classroom, rated 0-3 by two of four coders with inter-rater reliability averaging 0.94. Item 4 (generates an estimate prior to solving) also differed significantly (t(18) = 3.92, p <.001, ES = 1.76), with treatment teachers at M = 1.91 versus control M = 0.22.

> "Results indicated statistically significant and fairly substantial differences between the treatment (M = 14.45, SD = 4.13) and control groups (M = 9.00, SD = 4.18) on the total fidelity score (t(18) = 2.92, p = .009, ES = 1.31)"
