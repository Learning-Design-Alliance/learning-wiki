## Evidence

### Li Ting 2024

Li Ting, Song Ruohong, Zhong Wenjie, Liao Wenao, Hu Jiang, Liu Xilin, Wang Fei. (2024). Use of problem-based learning in orthopaedics education: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05244-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 11 RCTs (n=1259), fixed effects model, no significant heterogeneity (I2 =32%). Pooled unstandardized mean difference MD=5.69 (95% CI: 5.11~6.26, P<0.00001) favouring PBL; no standardized effect size was printed for this outcome.

> "A total of 11 [13, 15, 24, 32, 36, 44, 52, 54, 57–59] studies (n=1259) reported total scores. There was no significant heterogeneity (P=0.14, I2 =32%); therefore, a fixed effects model was used. The PBL group had higher total PBL scores than the traditional teaching group (MD=5.69, 95% CI: 5.11~6.26, P<0.00001; Fig. 6)."
