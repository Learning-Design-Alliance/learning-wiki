## Evidence

### Zhang J and Yin Y 2026

Zhang J and Yin Y (2026). A study on AI-enabled course development, AI proficiency, and first-year students' academic and psychological adjustment. Front. Psychol. 17:1909803. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1909803

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation (PROCESS Model 4, 5,000 resamples) in the 551-student cross-sectional survey: path a = 0.46, path b = 0.27, direct effect c′ = 0.29 (all p < 0.001). The article reports "The indirect effect was 0.124 (Boot SE = 0.026, 95% CI [0.076, 0.181]), which does not include zero" and a mediated proportion of 30.0%.
