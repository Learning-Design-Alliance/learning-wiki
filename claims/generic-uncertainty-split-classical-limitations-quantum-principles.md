## Evidence

### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Matthew Dew, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Context affects student thinking about sources of uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey of 150 upper-level students from five institutions; open-response generic questions analyzed qualitatively for proportions of students coded as mentioning limitations. "69% of students mentioned at least one limitations source of uncertainty" for the classical question versus 19% for the quantum question.

> "In response to the classical question, 69% of students mentioned at least one limitations source of uncertainty"

### Emily M. Stump 2023 (2)

Emily M. Stump, Matthew Dew, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Context affects student thinking about sources of uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same survey, generic quantum question responses coded with the same scheme. "most students mentioned at least one principles source of uncertainty in response to the quantum question (73%)" while very few (5%) mentioned principles for the classical question.

> "most students mentioned at least one principles source of uncertainty in response to the quantum question (73%)"
