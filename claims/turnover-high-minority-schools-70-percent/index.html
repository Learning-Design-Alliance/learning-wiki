Carver-Thomas 2017¶

Carver-Thomas, D. & Darling-Hammond, L. (2017). Teacher turnover: Why it matters and what we can do about it. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-turnover

SASS/TFS analysis comparing top-quartile (more than 55% students of color) and bottom-quartile (fewer than 10%) schools; the report states turnover is "about 70% greater" in high-minority schools, with math/science 90% and alternatively certified teachers 150% higher.
