## Evidence

### Nelson SW 2018

Nelson SW, Germann CA, MacVane CZ, Bloch RB, Fallon TS, Strout TD. (2018). Intern as Patient: A Patient Experience Simulation to Cultivate Empathy in Emergency Medicine Residents. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.11.35198

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group themes formulated four months into intern year about what residents learned and will apply to practice. Participants discussed "setting expectations on ED process for patients and family members" and overestimating time waits; one resident reported now apologizing for long waits. Qualitative, no effect sizes.

> "Mentioned often was the importance of good communication content. Participants discussed setting expectations on ED process for patients and family members."
