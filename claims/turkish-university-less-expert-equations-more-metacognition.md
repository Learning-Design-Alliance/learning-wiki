## Evidence

### Nuri Balta 2016

Nuri Balta, Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying Turkish high school and university students' attitudes and approaches to physics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level normalized-score analysis by school type from the 528-student Turkish survey. The article reports university students' most unfavorable items concern feeling stuck, equations applied to specific situations, and symbolic problem solving, while their most favorable were items 21, 22 and 29 about applying principles across contexts and checking unreasonable answers; on item 16 only university students had a positive normalized score (56% favorable).

> "on items 1, 6, 11, 12, 30 and 31 the normalized scores of the university students are more unfavorable (negative) than high school students in all three types of high schools."
