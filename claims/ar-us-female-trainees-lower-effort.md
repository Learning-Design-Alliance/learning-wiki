## Evidence

### Liao Shu-Chen 2024

Liao Shu-Chen, Shao Shih-Chieh, Gao Shi-Ying, Lai Edward Chia-Cheng. (2024). Augmented reality visualization for ultrasound-guided interventions: a pilot randomized crossover trial to assess trainee performance and cognitive load. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05998-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis by sex in the crossover trial. Female trainees' effort scores were significantly lower with AR-US, with a printed mean difference of -3.07 (95% CI -6.10 to -0.03, p = 0.05).

> "Among female trainees, the NASA-TLX scores for effort were lower for AR-US, compared to S-US [10.6 (4.44) vs. 13.67 (3.64), mean difference: -3.07 (95% CI: -6.10 to -0.03), p = 0.05] (Fig. 5C)."
