`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group Mann-Whitney U analysis of IPPI technical components (preparation, technical performance, asepsis) comparing the continuous-video control group and segmented-interactive treatment group. Table 9 reports Mann-Whitney U = 1508.500 and Z = −2.499; no effect size was printed.

> "The p-value is 0.012, p < 0.05. Therefore, the null hypothesis was rejected. There are significant differences in technical components between the control group and the treatment group"
