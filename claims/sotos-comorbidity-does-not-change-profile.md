## Evidence

### Butti 2025

Butti, N., Urgesi, C., Decio, A., Pezzani, L., Milani, D., and Montirosso, R. (2025). A Further Characterisation of the Neuropsychological Profile, Social Perception, and Academic Skills in Sotos Syndrome. Journal of Intellectual Disability Research, 69: 664–674. https://doi.org/10.1111/jir.13250

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory mixed-model ANOVA with ASD/ADHD comorbidity as between-subjects factor and domain as within-subject variable in the Sotos syndrome sample. Both the main effect and the interaction were non-significant; the printed partial eta-squared values are reported but no negligible-effect interpretation is asserted.

> "the exploratory analysis indicated that neither its main effect (F 1,21  = 1.39, p = 0.252, n 2 p  = 0.06) nor the interaction with domain (F 5,105  = 0.88, p = 0.498, n 2 p  = 0.04) were significant."
