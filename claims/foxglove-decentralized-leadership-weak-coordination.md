# Foxglove Elementary distributes leadership across staff roles but has few mechanisms to coordinate the work

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r1` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` At Foxglove Elementary, leadership is almost entirely decentralized, with tasks delegated to assistant principals, coaches, counselors, and teachers, yet few mechanisms coordinate work within and across groups. [→ Elaine Allensworth 2019](#elaine-allensworth-2019)

## Evidence

### Elaine Allensworth 2019

Elaine Allensworth. (2019). Elementary School Case Studies. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/how-do-principals-influence-student-achievement

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative case study of Foxglove Elementary based on principal and teacher interviews. The case reports principal Delaney delegating discipline, finances, lesson-plan checking, data analysis, attendance supports, and hiring across staff, noting "few mechanisms in place to coordinate this work".

> "Although leadership at Foxglove is distributed and staff members have ownership over various pieces of the work, there are few mechanisms in place to coordinate this work within and across groups and individuals."
