## Evidence

### Rohmer 2022

Rohmer, O., Doignon‐Camus, N., Audusseau, J., Trautmann, S., Chaillou, A.‐C., & Popa‐Roch, M. (2022). Removing the academic framing in student evaluations improves achievement in children with dyslexia: The mediating role of self‐judgement of competence. Dyslexia, 28(3), 309–324. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1713

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

SEM analysis of the 183-student sample assessed the effect of dyslexia on the four School Social Judgement Scale dimensions. The article reports "self‐judgement of competence was significantly lower in students with dyslexia" (β = −.71, p < .01), with non-significant effects for warmth, assertiveness, and effort.

> "This effect was significantly negative for competence (β = −.71, p < .01), indicating that self‐judgement of competence was significantly lower in students with dyslexia than in students without dyslexia, but was not significant for warmth (β = −.28, p = .25)"
