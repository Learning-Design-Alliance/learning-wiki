### Noah Leibnitz and Yangqiuting Li 2026 (2)

Noah Leibnitz and Yangqiuting Li. (2026). Investigating students’ gender expression and its relation to sense of belonging in introductory physics courses. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Same interview study: "a majority (20/26) of interviewees felt that masculinity and femininity were not mutually exclusive." Relatedly, most interviewees (15/26) expressed an understanding of gender fluidity, e.g., variation by day or situation.

> "Although some students acknowledged that they often thought of masculine and feminine traits as opposites, a majority (20/26) of interviewees felt that masculinity and femininity were not mutually exclusive."
