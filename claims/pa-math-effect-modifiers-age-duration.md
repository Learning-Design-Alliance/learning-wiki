# Intervention effect sizes on mathematics are negatively associated with participants' age and intervention duration, while intervention type does not explain heterogeneity

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Meta-regression found participants' age (β = −0.051, p = 0.045) and intervention duration (β = −0.003, p = 0.002) negatively associated with effect sizes; type of intervention did not influence effect sizes. [→ Sneck S. 2019](#sneck-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Sneck S. 2019

Sneck S., Viholainen H., Syväoja H., Kankaapää A., Hakonen H., Poikkeus A.-M., Tammelin T. (2019). Effects of school-based physical activity on mathematics performance in children: a systematic review. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-019-0866-6

`q3 · i?`

Meta-regression of study-level variables across the 11 studies in the meta-analysis, conducted because heterogeneity was large. The review reports age (β = −0.051, p = 0.045) and duration (β = −0.003, p = 0.002) as significant moderators.

> "In the moderator analysis, the age of participants (β = − 0.051, p = 0.045) and the duration of intervention (β = − 0.003, p = 0.002) were found to explain heterogeneity. The type of intervention was not found to influence ESs."
