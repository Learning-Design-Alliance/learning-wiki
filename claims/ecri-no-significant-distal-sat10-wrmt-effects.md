### Fien 2015

Fien, H., Smith, J. L. M., Smolkowski, K., Baker, S. K., Nelson, N. J., & Chaparro, E. (2015). An Examination of the Efficacy of a Multitiered Intervention on Early Reading Outcomes for First Grade Students at-Risk for Reading Difficulties. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 48, 602-621. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601958

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time-by-condition multilevel analysis of fall-to-spring gains on standardized distal measures in the cluster-randomized trial. The article reports no significant findings for the three named measures and a "marginally significant gain, however, for WRMT-BS (t = 2.00, df = 14, p = .0651)".

> "The analyses did not result in statistically significant findings for SAT10, WRMT-BS, or WRMT-TR. We found a marginally significant gain, however, for WRMT-BS (t = 2.00, df = 14, p = .0651)."
