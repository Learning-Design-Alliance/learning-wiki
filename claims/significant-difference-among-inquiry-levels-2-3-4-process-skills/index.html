Evidence¶

Hardianti 2017¶

Hardianti, T. & Kuswanto, H. (2017). Difference among Levels of Inquiry: Process Skills Improvement at Senior High School in Indonesia. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 119-130. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_8.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent control group study; three SMA classes (N = 25, 24, 28) treated with ILL-3, ILL-4, and ILL-2 respectively. One-way ANOVA on gain scores showed "significant difference in effectiveness among ILL-2, ILL -3, dan ILL -4"; Table 3 reports F = 15.837, Sig. 0.000.

"The analysis of the ANOVA test used indicated that there was significant difference in effectiveness among ILL-2, ILL -3, dan ILL -4 ( with Sig <0. 05) in improving process skills."
