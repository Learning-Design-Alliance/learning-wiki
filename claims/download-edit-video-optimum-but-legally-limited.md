### Micolich 2008

Micolich, A. (2008). The latent potential of YouTube – Will it become the 21st Century Lecturer's Film Archive? CAL-laborate, 2008. https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3441 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0808.3441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0808.3441)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

In 'Downloading and editing the video', the author lists the advantages of a local file: "Improved reliability", editing and cropping to avoid class distraction, optimal display size in PowerPoint, and portability to non-internet lecture theatres.

> "A video file stored on the hard-drive of the lecture theatre computer is guaranteed to play without the stutters and extended download breaks typical of streaming video."

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Legal discussion in 'Legal issues revisited' for the third route, assuming an original non-infringing work. The author notes permission shifts the web site's role to helping educators locate content and identify its owner.

> "The same cannot be said for the third possibility, which would not only violate the terms of use for just about any online video web site, but it would also likely violate copyright unless the permission of the copyright owner was given to crop or edit the file and thereby create a derivative work, as well as to reproduce and publically display the resulting video."
