Evidence¶

Nishimura 2014¶

Nishimura, T. (2014). Effective Professional Development of Teachers: A Guide to Actualizing Inclusive Schooling. International Journal of Whole Schooling, 10(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016781

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A quasi-experimental pre-post comparison group study of eight teachers in an eight-week IPD program measured Reliable Change Index (RCI) scores on the STATIC instrument. The article reports "a strong effect was noted for 1 participant, a moderate effect for 1 participant, and no effect for 4 participants," with two participants showing negative effects.

"Individual pre-post total scores were calculated on the STATIC instrument for each participant in the intervention group demonstrated a strong effect was noted for 1 participant, a moderate effect for 1 participant, and no effect for 4 participants."
