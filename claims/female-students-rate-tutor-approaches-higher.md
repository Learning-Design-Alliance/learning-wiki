## Evidence

### Barnawi Ahlam 2024

Barnawi Ahlam, Sonbol Ahmed M., Al-Shawwa Lana, Abulaban Alwalla, Asiri Khalil, Bagasi Abdulaziz, Alafari Reem, Alamoudi Aliaa Amr. (2024). Employing students' evaluations and tutors' perceptions to evaluate a faculty development program on problem-based learning at the Faculty of Medicine, King Abdulaziz University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05662-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-tests comparing male (n = 176) and female (n = 108) students' ratings in the questionnaire phase. The article concludes "tutors' learning approaches tended to be viewed more favorably by this group"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "the results reveal significant associations among the scores for the constructive/active learning (female: 3.56 ± 0.9 vs. male: 3.28 ± 1.0, p = 0.019), self-directed learning (3.79 ± 0.9 vs. 3.33 ± 1.0, p < 0.001), contextual learning (3.55 ± 0.9 vs. 3.23 ± 0.9, p = 0.005), and collaborative learning (3.28 ± 1.1 vs. 2.99 ± 1.1, p = 0.031) approaches."
