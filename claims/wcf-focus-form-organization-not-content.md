### Mostafavi Marzieh 2025

Mostafavi Marzieh, Shokrpour Nasrin, Afraz Shahram. (2025). Beliefs, practices, and awareness of written corrective feedback among EFL teachers: a mixed-methods study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03861-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Corpus analysis of the 1500 collected essays coded feedback focus per Lee's categories. The article reports a "predominant focus on form" and organization, with only five teachers addressing content; figures are descriptive with no effect size.

> "The analysis revealed a predominant focus on form (all but four teachers, with 28 focusing solely on it) and organization (70 teachers except for four exclusively). 60% of teachers addressed both form and organization."
