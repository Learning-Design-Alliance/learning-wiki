Ergen 2017¶

Ergen, B., & Kanadli, S. (2017). The effect of self-regulated learning strategies on academic achievement: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 55-74. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.4

Moderator analysis across Science (k=4), Mathematics (k=8), Social Sciences (k=6), and Language (k=3) studies; Qb = 4.182, p = 0.242. Mathematics showed the largest subgroup effect size (1.098), described as large, versus moderate effects elsewhere.
