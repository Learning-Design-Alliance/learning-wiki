## Evidence

### Sykes EA 2021

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this two-arm RCT, 20 undergraduate novices randomized to RHAP (n = 10) or square knot (n = 10) repaired porcine enterotomies. Deep-cavity RHAP repairs were "significantly faster than repairs performed by the square knot group" (16 [SD 2] v. 21 [SD 1] min, p = 0.04); no effect size was printed.

> "Repairs performed by the RHAP knot group in simulated deep body cavities were significantly faster than repairs performed by the square knot group (16 [SD 2] v. 21 [SD 1] min, p = 0.04)"
