Ba Ruikai, Feng Xinran, Yuan Lixian, Tong Juan, Ban Jinghao, Wu Meiling, Han Bing, Xu Xinyue, Qiu Xinyu, Guo Hao, Xuan Kun. (2026). A novel extended flipped classroom model helps dental undergraduates grow into dentists. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08525-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of open-ended comments from the 2022 and 2023 cohorts: two trained coders performed inductive open coding with inter-coder reliability Cohen's κ = 0.78 (95% CI 0.70–0.86; raw agreement 87%). The 2023 word cloud shifted toward pedagogy and skills tokens such as cases, practice, and thinking.

> "Three cross-cutting themes emerged— (1) case-based, stepwise practice (~ 42%); (2) clarity & demonstration (~ 36%); and (3) assessment calibration & depth (~ 28%). These themes triangulate with the 2023 quantitative gains."
