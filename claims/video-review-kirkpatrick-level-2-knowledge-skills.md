## Evidence

### Robbrecht M 2025

Robbrecht M, Van Winckel M, Mulder A, Embo M. (2025). What is the learning effect of video review in postgraduate medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06515-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports on Goldberg et al.'s RCT of GPs in training using a psychiatric screening questionnaire: "Significant differences in favour of video review were observed when comparing pre- and post-test scores between the index and control groups." Contrast with Isreb et al., where only two of 10 video reviews added one point on a 10-point scale, and Mazer et al., who found no questionnaire differences.
