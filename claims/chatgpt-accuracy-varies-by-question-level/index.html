Evidence¶

Ali Kürşat Erümit 2025¶

Ali Kürşat Erümit, Rabia Özdemir Sarıalioğlu. (2025). Artificial intelligence in science and chemistry education: a systematic review. Discover Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-00622-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Clark, an examination of ChatGPT's answers to general chemistry exam questions, in which the tool "showed high success in open-ended and skill-free questions". Second-hand report of a single study; no effect size.

"the artificial intelligence tool showed high success in open-ended and skill-free questions and could correctly define concepts in closed-format questions despite the chemical symbols in the question content"

Ali Kürşat Erümit 2025 (2)¶

Ali Kürşat Erümit, Rabia Özdemir Sarıalioğlu. (2025). Artificial intelligence in science and chemistry education: a systematic review. Discover Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-00622-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Tassoti (study A1 with 23 chemistry teacher candidates), that "ChatGPT provides understandable answers for middle and high school level questions, but its accuracy rate is low at the university level". Second-hand report; no effect size.

"ChatGPT provides understandable answers for middle and high school level questions, but its accuracy rate is low at the university level"
