## Evidence

### Stallasch 2024

Stallasch, S. E., Lüdtke, O., Artelt, C., Hedges, L. V., & Brunner, M. (2024). Single- and Multilevel Perspectives on Covariate Selection in Randomized Intervention Studies on Student Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09898-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Multilevel meta-analytic results for the total student population in elementary school (grades 1-4) from the German samples, showing sociodemographic covariates (gender, migration background, socioeconomic status) as the stronger group-level predictors.

> "Notably, with first–fourth graders, SC outweighed IP at both L2 and L3 ( Pooled R2 L2/uni007C.varSC = 0.35 ; Pooled R2 L3/uni007C.varSC = 0.52 )."
