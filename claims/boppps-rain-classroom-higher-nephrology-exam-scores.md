Comparison of the BOPPPS model combined with rain classroom teaching and traditional method on nephrology education for fifth-year undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08714-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative analysis over one academic year: 36 fifth-year students per group, allocated by rotation week (odd weeks control, even weeks experimental), same instructors and curriculum. The experimental mean was "82.27 ± 8.64" versus "77.52 ± 9.82", p = 0.032; no effect size was reported.

> "Students in the experimental group taught via the BOPPPS-Rain Classroom model achieved higher final examination scores on average (82.27 ± 8.64) than those in the traditional lecture group (77.52 ± 9.82), and this difference reached statistical significance (p = 0.032)."
