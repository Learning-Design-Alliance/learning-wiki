`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of average Pearson correlations between candidate predictor variables (SAT, ACT, FCI, CSEM, and prerequisite grades) and combined midterm-plus-final exam scores, averaged over the five semesters studied. Grades in prerequisite courses show "the largest correlations".

> "As Table II shows, grades in pre-requisite courses have the largest correlations."

F. J. Kontur and N. B. Terry. (2013). The benefits of completing homework for students with different aptitudes in an introductory physics course. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1305.2213](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1305.2213)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Table II row for the combined prerequisite-grade variable: an average Pearson correlation of 0.72 with a standard deviation of 0.04 across the five semesters. This combined GPA was then used to measure student aptitude in the study.
