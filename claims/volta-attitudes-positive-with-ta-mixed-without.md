# Student attitudes toward VOLTA were positive when it supplemented TA help but mixed when it replaced TA assistance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All 7 surveyed Cohort 2 respondents showed a positive attitude toward VOLTA, while in Cohort 3 the majority of mean attitude scores fell below 3 with decreased TA involvement. [→ Saleheen 2018](#saleheen-2018)

## Evidence

### Saleheen 2018

Saleheen, F., Wang, Z., Picone, J., Butz, B. P., and Won, C.-H. (2018). Efficacy of a Virtual Teaching Assistant in an Open Laboratory Environment for Electric Circuits. Advances in Engineering Education. http://www.temple.edu/csnap/volta.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Likert-scale usability surveys of the experimental groups: 16 respondents in Cohort 1, 7 of 10 in Cohort 2, and 6 of 8 in Cohort 3. The article reports "all 7 respondents showed a positive attitude towards VOL TA" in Cohort 2, but in Cohort 3 only 3 of 6 respondents indicated a positive attitude.

> "These results suggest that the majority of students did not appreciate VOL TA v3 when there was decreased TA involvement, though this is a very small sample size."
