## Evidence

### Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016

Yeh Ting-Kuang, Huang Hsiu-Mei, Chan Wing P, Chang Chun-Yen. (2016). Effects of congruence between preferred and perceived learning environments in nursing education in Taiwan: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open 6(5): e009925. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009925

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preinstruction survey of the 124 first-year nursing students using the Pr-LEI preference instrument, plotted on the four-quadrant (PBL, LBL) diagram; "most of the students preferred a PBL-LBL hybrid learning environment", with a few preferring fully PBL or fully LBL environments.

> "in the preinstruction survey, most of the students preferred a PBL-LBL hybrid learning environment (most of the mean scores being in the upper-right quadrant at (X, Y)=(3.96, 4.04))"
