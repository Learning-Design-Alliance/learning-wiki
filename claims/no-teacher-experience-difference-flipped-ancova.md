## Evidence

### Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown 2018

Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown. (2018). Flipped learning in an EFL environment: Does the teacher's experience affect learning outcomes? The EUROCALL Review, Volume 26, No. 1. https://www.eurocall-languages.org/the-eurocall-review

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Because the NFE group scored higher at pre-test, the authors ran an ANCOVA with pre-test scores adjusted to 8.74. Adjusted post-test means were 15.91 (NFE) and 15.40 (FE); "F(1, 36) = .087, p = .77".

> "The ANCOVA result showed that at the post- test stage there was no statistically significant difference, F(1, 36) = .087, p = .77, between the NFE group with an adjusted post -test score of 15.91 and the FE group with an adjusted post -test score of 15.40."
