### Burns Ryan D. 2019

Burns Ryan D., Bai Yang, Fu You, Pfledderer Christopher D., Brusseau Timothy A. (2019). Parent Engagement and Support, Physical Activity, and Academic Performance (PESPAAP): A Proposed Theoretical Model. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16234698

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports a cross-sectional survey of 2,669 sixth grade students, citing Mahuro and Hungi, finding "a unit increase in parental academic participation associated with a 6%–15% improvement in numeracy scores" and a 6%-12% literacy improvement; these are percentages, not standardized effect sizes.

> "using a cross-sectional survey and a sample of 2,669 sixth grade students, it was found that a unit increase in parental academic participation associated with a 6%–15% improvement in numeracy scores and a 6%–12% improvement in student literacy scores"
