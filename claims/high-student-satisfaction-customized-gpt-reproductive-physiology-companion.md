Implementation and evaluation of customized artificial intelligence personal tutor (chat GPT physiology companion) for medical students in the reproduction module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09088-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of 47 third-year medical students using a 20-item Likert questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha = 0.917) on overall satisfaction with the GPT companion. The article reports "83% of students recommend the customized GPT tool to other students (mean of 4.40 and a median of 5)" and 80.9% would recommend it for future courses.

> "83% of students recommend the customized GPT tool to other students (mean of 4.40 and a median of 5), reflecting high satisfaction. Similarly, 80.9% expressed a willingness to recommend the companion for use in future courses."
