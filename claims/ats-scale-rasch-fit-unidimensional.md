## Evidence

### Koparan 2014

Koparan, T., & Güven, B. (2014). The Effect on the 8th Grade Students' Attitude towards Statistics of Project Based Learning. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(2), 73-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Rasch rating-scale analysis (Winsteps 3.72) of 70 students' responses to the 20-item ATS. The article reports person separation 2.00, reliability .80, item reliability .84, and "CRONBACH ALPHA (KR-20) ... = .85", indicating items measured a single unidimensional construct.

> "All items fit the expectations of the Rasch model. In other words, all items had zstd infit and/or outfit statistics between -2 and 2 (Table 1)."
