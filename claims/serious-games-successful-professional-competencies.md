### Bijl 2024

Bijl, A., Veldkamp, B. P., Wools, S., & de Klerk, S. (2024). Serious games in high-stakes assessment contexts: a systematic literature review into the game design principles for valid game-based performance assessment. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2041–2064. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10362-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 56 studies (published 2006–2020) on serious games for professional competencies. The review reports the proportion of studies per competency type meeting its success criteria, quoting that "84%, 83%, and 100% reported research results showing the SG was successful". Success was defined as significant improvement or a significant relationship with a validated measure.

> "Divided over the type of professional competency evaluated, 84%, 83%, and 100% reported research results showing the SG was successful for cognitive skills, knowledge, and motor skills respectively"
