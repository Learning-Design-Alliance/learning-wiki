### Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin 1996

Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin. (1996). One Hundred Years of Forgetting: A Quantitative Description of Retention. Psychological Review. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.103.4.734

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A quantitative literature synthesis assembling 210 published retention data sets meeting objective inclusion criteria and fitting each to 105 two-parameter functions, sorting over 22,000 correlations for patterns. The article reports "The best fits were to the logarithmic function, the power function, the exponential in the square root of time, and the hyperbola in the square root of time."

> "A sample of 210 published data sets were assembled that (a) plotted amount remembered versus time, (b) had 5 or more points, and ( c j were smooth enough to fit at least 1 of the functions tested with a correlation coefficient of .90 or greater. Each was fit to 105 different 2-parameter functions."
