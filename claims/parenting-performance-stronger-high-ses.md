## Evidence

### Ali Nayab 2023

Ali Nayab, Ullah Asad, Khan Abdul Majid, Khan Yunas, Ali Sajid, Khan Aisha, Bakhtawar, Khan Asad, Din Maaz Ud, Ullah Rahat, Khan Umar Niaz, Aziz Tariq, Ahmad Mushtaq. (2023). Academic performance of children in relation to gender, parenting styles, and socioeconomic status: What attributes are important. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kendall's Tau-c analysis controlling for SES in the 448-student cross-sectional sample. The association was positive and significant for both "respondents from high socioeconomic status" and "respondents from low socioeconomic status", with Tc = 0.689 versus 0.218 respectively; the overall-table value was Tc = 0.487. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The influence of parenting style on the academic performance of children in the context of respondents’ socioeconomic status was positive (Tc = 0.689) and highly significant (P = 0.000) among the above-mentioned variables for respondents from high socioeconomic status. The association of the above-said variables was also positive (Tc = 0.218) and significant (P = 0.009) for respondents from low socioeconomic status."
