## Evidence

### Liu YJ 2026

Liu YJ, Ma HZ, Luo HY, Xiong YX, Qu FW, Xie JP, Guo Y. (2026). From traditional classroom to AI-enhanced flipped classroom: a three-year pedagogical evolution for international students in pharmacology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09608-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Critical-reflection items of the post-course questionnaire (n=29 respondents). Students rated AI lowest on information accuracy (mean 3.34) and visualisation suggestions (3.14), and 51.72% named content requiring substantial modification as the main challenge.

> "A notable 75.86% ( n = 22) of students simultaneously selected the three roles of personal tutor, creative partner, and criti cal thinking partner, reflecting a strong expectation for AI to evolve beyond basic information retrieval"
