### Thacker 2025

Thacker, B., Wang, J., Zhang, Y., Tran, Q. B., Vemula, D. S., & Kushimo, T. (2025). From Research to Resources: Assessing Student Understanding and Skills in Quantum Computing. NSF grant DUE 2235464. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-case synthesis of rubric-coded and qualitatively analyzed interviews in both cohorts. The article also reports most students "demonstrated their ability to apply single-qubit and two-qubit gates correctly, except rotation gates" and favored Dirac representation.

> "Common difficulties across both classes were matrix representation of qubits, use of rotation gates, and a quantitative (based on recalling and analyzing the circuit) understanding of teleportation."
