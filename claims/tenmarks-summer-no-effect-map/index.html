Evidence¶

Christina Luke 2016¶

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/26

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Kettle Moraine matched-comparison analysis: 139 pilot participants versus a demographically similar control group, with a linear model controlling for baseline score, grade, gender, race, and free/reduced-price lunch status. No effect was detected; no effect size is printed.

"We found no effect of TenMarks on NWEA MAP Math scores pre and post compared to a control group who did not use the program. Because of limited student engagement, results should be treated with caution."
