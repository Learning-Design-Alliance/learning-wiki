Related Claims¶

  • Immersive VR-haptic and ITS modalities are more often associated with objective psychomotor and cognitive gains, while generative AI platforms are more often associated with affective outcomes — related
  • GenAI/LLM modalities provide the strongest evidence for cognitive and communication outcomes, while chatbots show weak independent effects — related
  • Controlled GenAI virtual-patient and communication studies reported short-term skill gains, but positive findings were not universal — reports the opposite
  • Affective outcomes (satisfaction, confidence, engagement) are consistently positive across ADS modalities but rest predominantly on subjective, short-term measures — related
  • Evidence on AI-driven simulation in dental education shows moderate-to-high heterogeneity and limited certainty — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • AI-driven simulation is associated with a statistically significant improvement in overall learning outcomes compared to traditional training in dental education — related
