## Evidence

### Asmare Biachew 2018

Asmare Biachew, Taddele Mekuanint, Berihun Sileshi, Wagnew Fasil. (2018). Nutritional status and correlation with academic performance among primary school children, northwest Ethiopia. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3909-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 436 primary school children in Debre Markos Town using ENA for SMART software to compute weight-for-age, height-for-age and weight-for-height Z-scores. The study found "The percentage of children having any kind of under nutrition (stunting, wasting and underweight) was 56.2%" with the printed CIs for each indicator. No effect size is printed for prevalence.

> "The overall prevalence of stunting, underweight and wasting were 27.5% (95% CI 23.2–31.9%), 20.4% (95% CI 16.5–24.3%) and 8.7% (95% CI 6.2–11.5%) respectively. The percentage of children having any kind of under nutrition (stunting, wasting and underweight) was 56.2%."
