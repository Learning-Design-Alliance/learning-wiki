### Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students' Difficulties with Equations involving Circuit Elements. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same three multiple-choice questions were administered to 42 first-year physics graduate students enrolled in a semester-long TA training course two years in a row. Table 3 prints graduate correct responses of 93% (resistance), 86% (capacitance) and 76% (inductance).

> "Table 3 shows that while the graduate students perform significantly better than the introductory students, they have similar difficulties . Also, the inductor question is relatively difficult even for them."
