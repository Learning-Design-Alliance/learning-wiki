# Decision making was the one critical thinking dimension that did not significantly improve under ARDESOS v.2, unlike problem solving

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Decision making showed only a small, marginally significant mean increase (.367, p=.018 in the mean contrast; p=.063 pre-post) and the authors treat it as not reaching significance. [→ Saiz et al. 2015](#saiz-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Saiz et al. 2015

Carlos Saiz, Silvia F. Rivas and Sonia Olivares. (2015). Collaborative learning supported by rubrics improves critical thinking. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v15i1.12905

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 144-student quasi-experiment, the decision-making factor's pre-post difference was not significant (p=.063, d=.16) and its contrast with the v.1 mean was only significant at 5% (p=.018). The article calls this dimension modest and in some cases non-significant.

> "The decision-making variable evolved in a similar fashion to the others, although the analyses revealed a small increase (.367) in the mean of v.2 (CI 95%: .02-.71)"
