## Evidence

### Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011

Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert. (2011). The Racial/Ethnic Composition of Elementary Schools and Young Children's Academic and Socioemotional Functioning. American Educational Research Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4554342/

Effect-size comparison within the same ECLS-K regression models, benchmarking composition coefficients against parent education, a robust predictor. "the parent education coefficient ranged from .09 to .10."

> "Specifically, for reading and mathematics achievement, respectively, standardized coefficients were an absolute value of .05 and .04 for school diversity and .05 and .06 for proportion same-racial/ethnic peers, whereas the parent education coefficient ranged from .09 to .10."
