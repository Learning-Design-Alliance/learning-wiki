Impact of a Structured Training Program on Medical Student Confidence and Behavior During Their First Radial Arterial Puncture: Comparative Study. Mann-Whitney U comparisons of VAS questionnaire ratings between group 1 (n=20 with complete data) and group 2 (n=43); all six perception measures were nonsignificant (e.g., confidence P=.80, satisfaction P=.09). "No statistically significant differences were observed between group 1 and group 2."

> "No statistically significant differences were observed between group 1 and group 2 in terms of global anticipatory apprehension, apprehension about causing pain, apprehension about procedural failure, satisfaction with the procedure, apprehension about repeating the procedure, or confidence in repeating the procedure"
