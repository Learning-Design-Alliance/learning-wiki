Evidence¶

Wartenberg 2023¶

Wartenberg, G., Aldrup, K., Grund, S., & Klusmann, U. (2023). Satisfied and High Performing? A Meta-Analysis and Systematic Review of the Correlates of Teachers' Job Satisfaction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09831-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup and meta-regression analyses comparing teacher self-reports with external reports (student ratings, observations, school records). For general interaction, self-reports yielded r = 0.25 versus r = 0.14 for external reports; correlations remained positive and significant in either case.

"the effect sizes were significantly larger when they were based on self-reports (k = 7, r = 0.25, CIr = [0.21, 0.29]) as compared with reports by students or external observers (k = 7, r = 0.14, CIr = [0.08, 0.19])"
