## Evidence

### Ketcheson et al. 2017

Leah Ketcheson, Janet Hauck and Dale Ulrich. (2017). The effects of an early motor skill intervention on motor skills, levels of physical activity, and socialization in young children with autism spectrum disorder: A pilot study. Autism 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361316650611

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA of the pilot intervention; the gross quotient is the composite of locomotor and object control subtests. The article reports "partial η2 = 0.53" for the between-group difference. Gains were significant baseline to time 2, non-significant time 2 to 3.

> "and gross quotient (F(1, 14) = 15.61 p < 0.01, partial η2 = 0.53)"
