Bin Bakheet 2026¶

Bin Bakheet, T., Alamri, H., & Alshaya, F. (2026). Developing deep learning in science through an adaptive AI-based STEM instructional program: evidence from sixth-grade classrooms. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00630-1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive log analysis of the experimental group (N = 15) over eight weeks, aggregating per-student values into group means with min–max ranges. Table 3 reports, for example, mastery achieved by end of unit in the 88–92% range and 2–4 remediation cycles per student; these are descriptive summaries with no effect sizes.

"These indicators capture learner interaction patterns, mastery progression, remediation cycles, and adaptive redirection mechanisms embedded within the instructional design. All indicators were computed using system-generated LMS log data for the experimental group (N = 15)."
