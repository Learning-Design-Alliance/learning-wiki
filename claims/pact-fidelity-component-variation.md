### Jeanne Wanzek 2019

Jeanne Wanzek, Greg Roberts, Sharon Vaughn, Elizabeth Swanson, and Katherine Sargent. (2019). Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational fidelity analysis: one treatment and one comparison class per teacher were audio recorded, and blind coders rated each PACT component on a 1-to-4 Likert-type scale against a gold standard. Teachers typically struggled with "extending student thinking, facilitating discussion and note-taking, and making connections to essential words."

> "The knowledge acquisition and team-based learning knowledge application portions of the instruction were the areas in which teachers had the most difficulty, with more than 50% of the observations in these two areas receiving a rating of 2 (mid-low implementation; some elements of the compo- nent implemented)."
