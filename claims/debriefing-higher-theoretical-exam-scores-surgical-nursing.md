## Evidence

### Cao Xi 2026

Cao Xi, Wang Min, Huang Ye, Hu Qiu-Min, Tang Rong. (2026). Research on the application of debriefing in simulation-based education of surgical nursing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09133-7

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-and-after study of 340 third-year nursing students. Paired t-test showed a significant increase in theoretical exam scores from control to debriefing phase, with "The magnitude of this difference was large (Cohen's ds = 1.09)".

> "Theoretical exam scores were significantly higher in the experimental phase compared to the control phase (P < 0.001). The mean score was 8.11 points higher (95% CI: 7.32 to 8.90), representing a 12.32% difference relative to the control phase. The magnitude of this difference was large (Cohen's ds = 1.09)."
