### Meyers AB 2026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postsimulation SET-M survey of 15 SRNAs. The article reports "94% (N = 15)" strong agreement for confidence in providing patient safety interventions and communicating with the team, with one SRNA selecting somewhat agree.
