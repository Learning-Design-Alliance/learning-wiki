## Evidence

### Veloo 2016

Veloo, A., Md-Ali, R., & Chairany, S. (2016). Using Cooperative Teams-Game-Tournament in 11 Religious School to Improve Mathematics Understanding and Communication. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 13(2), 97-123. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134733

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed between-within subjects ANOVA on mathematics communication scores across pre-test, post-test 1 and post-test 2 in the same 64-student randomized control group study. The article reports a significant interaction with Wilks Lambda = 0.89, F(2,61) = 3.87, p < .05, partial eta squared = 0.11.

> "There was significant interaction between group type and time periods, (Wilks Lambda = 0.89, F (2,61) = 3.87, p < .05, partial eta squared (λ)= 0.11)."

### Veloo 2016 (2)

Veloo, A., Md-Ali, R., & Chairany, S. (2016). Using Cooperative Teams-Game-Tournament in 11 Religious School to Improve Mathematics Understanding and Communication. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 13(2), 97-123. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134733

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-subject effect for mathematics communication from Table 5 of the same study. The article reports the group effect as significant with F(1,62) = 4.81, p = .03, partial eta squared = 0.07, described as moderately supporting the overall findings.

> "The main effect for experimental group and control group was significant, F (1, 62) = 4.81, p < .05, partial eta squared ( λ) = 0.07), suggesting a difference in the effectiveness of the cooperative TGT teaching approaches on mathematics communication."

### Veloo 2016 (3)

Veloo, A., Md-Ali, R., & Chairany, S. (2016). Using Cooperative Teams-Game-Tournament in 11 Religious School to Improve Mathematics Understanding and Communication. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 13(2), 97-123. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134733

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive means from Table 6 for post-test 1 mathematics communication: experimental group M = 32.22 (SD = 6.47) versus control group M = 26.69 (SD = 9.16), a printed difference of 5.53 attributed to the TGT effect.

> "Figure 3 shows that the post 1 mathematics communication score for the experimental group (32.22) is higher than the control group (26.69)."
