Holman 2024¶

Holman, Kenneth. (2024). Exploring Fraction Comprehension and Interest in Elementary Education Through AI-Powered Personalized Learning. https://stars.library.ucf.edu/etd2024/32

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated Measures ANOVA in the Mathbot quasi-experiment measured situational interest with a six-item Situational Interest Survey alongside the Fraction Comparison Assessment. The article reports that "changes in situational interest were not statistically significant." No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

"Repeated Measures ANOVA revealed modest improvements in fraction comprehension for students using Mathbot compared to traditional instruction, though changes in situational interest were not statistically significant."
