# Retention was higher in majority-White schools in Arizona and Nevada but the converse held in Utah

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Makkonen 2021

Makkonen, R., & Jaquet, K. (2021). Principal retention patterns in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah (REL 2022–129). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory West. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/principal-retention-patterns-arizona-nevada-and-utah

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four-year retention analysis by school racial composition (figure 5) from state staffing data. Rates in schools where fewer than half of students were White ranged from 34 percent in Arizona to 44 percent in Nevada, with state-specific directions of difference.

> "In both Arizona and Nevada these rates were 4–5 percentage points lower than those in schools where more than half of students were White. In Utah the four-year retention rate was approximately 8 percentage points higher in schools where fewer than 50 percent of students were White."
