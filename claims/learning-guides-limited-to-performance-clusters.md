## Evidence

### Roselló Atanet Iván 2025

Roselló Atanet Iván, Tomova Mihaela, Sieg Miriam, Sehy Victoria, Mäder Patrick, März Maren. (2025). Generating learning guides for medical education with LLMs and statistical analysis of test results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06978-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cluster analysis of 2,932 PT46 participations using the Sieg et al. clustering procedure. The authors conclude "it only makes sense to generate learning guides for test takers in clusters 0, 1 and 3" given the data gaps in clusters 2 and 4.

> "Table 3 shows that the mean number of omitted responses was 6.28, 6.39 and 7.28 for clusters 0, 1 and 3, versus 93.1 and 114.41 for clusters 2 and 4."
