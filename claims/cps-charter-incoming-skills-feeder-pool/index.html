Evidence¶

Gwynne 2017¶

Gwynne, J.A., & Moore, P.T. (2017). Chicago's charter high schools: Organizational features, enrollment, school transfers, and student performance. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://cps.edu/NewSchools/Pages/RFP2017.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive feeder-pool comparison in the Executive Summary comparing charter enrollees' eighth-grade test scores, grades, attendance, and study habits to peers from the same elementary schools and neighborhoods, countering the high-performing-selection concern. Test scores were "similar to or lower than" feeder peers.

"Nearly all charter high schools enrolled students with higher eighth-grade attendance than their feeder pool, and about one-third of charter high schools enrolled students whose eighth-grade GPAs were higher than their feeder pools."
