## Evidence

### Lundervold 2013

Lundervold, D. A., Ament, P. A., Holt, P. S., & Hunt, L. S. (2013). Comparison of Younger and Older Adults' Acceptability of Treatment for Generalized Anxiety Disorder Co-Occurring with Parkinson's Disease. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, 8(2). https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1555-7855

`q3 · i0`

The same mixed factorial ANOVA tested age cohort as the between-subjects factor. The article reports "no difference between age groups in treatment type ratings" with F(1,13) = .009, p = .924, η2 = .000, and low observed power (.051). The authors interpret this as older adults being just as psychologically minded as younger adults.
