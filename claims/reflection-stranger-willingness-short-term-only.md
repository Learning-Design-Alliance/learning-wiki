## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire measure directly after training in the cluster randomised trial found "a significant difference between group T and group RT", with "a more positive attitude in group RT" (35% vs 32% would do both, table 3, p=0.006); the difference was not significant at 6 months.

> "If a stranger suffered OHCA, there was a significant difference between group T and group RT in how the students would act, with a more positive attitude in group RT (table 3) but there was no significant difference between groups O and T (27% and 32% would do both compressions and ventilations)."
