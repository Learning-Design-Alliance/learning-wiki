## Subclaims
`q2 i?` AI role significantly affected task confidence, with the Struggling Student role eliciting significantly lower confidence than all four other roles. [→ Wangda Zhu 2025](#wangda-zhu-2025)
`q2 i?` AI role significantly affected perceived helpfulness and ease of interaction, with the Struggling Student rated significantly lower than all other roles on both measures. [→ Wangda Zhu 2025 (2)](#wangda-zhu-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wangda Zhu 2025

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman test across the five roles on post-task task-confidence ratings (N = 26); the Struggling Student scored lowest (M= 1.88) and Wilcoxon post-hocs confirmed it differed from all four competent roles. Only test statistics are printed.

> "The Friedman test revealed a significant effect of AI role on task confidence,χ 2(4, N= 26) = 24.85,p < .001."

### Wangda Zhu 2025 (2)

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman tests on perceived helpfulness and ease of interaction across five roles (N = 26); the Struggling Student was rated significantly less helpful and harder to interact with than all four other roles, with no differences among the competent roles. Only test statistics are printed.

> "AI role significantly affected perceived helpfulness,χ 2(4, N= 26) = 32.63,p < .001."
