## Evidence

### Kim Sunmi 2022

Kim Sunmi, Jeong Seok Hee, Kim Hee Sun, Jeong Young Ju. (2022). Academic Success of Online Learning in Undergraduate Nursing Education Programs in the COVID-19 Pandemic Era. Journal of Professional Nursing, 38, 6-16. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2021.10.005

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on survey data from 200 nursing students; in the third step cyber-class flow entered with self-directed learning and obstacles. The article reports "cyber-class flow (β = 0.65, p < 0.001) was identified as a significant predictor of satisfaction with online classes."

> "In addition, cyber-class flow (β = 0.65, p < 0.001) was identified as a significant predictor of satisfaction with online classes. The overall explanatory power of the final model in the third step of the hierarchical regression analysis was 48.9% (F = 24.81, p < 0.001)"
