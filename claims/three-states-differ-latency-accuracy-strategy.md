## Evidence

### Tenison 2015

Tenison, C., & Anderson, J. R. (2015). Modeling the distinct phases of skill acquisition. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000204

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics for trials assigned to their most probable state (Table 3): mean latency fell from 7.98 s (State 1) to 2.95 s (State 2) to 1.41 s (State 3), accuracy rose from 84.0% to 98.1%, and retrieval reports rose from 17% to 95%.

> "The concurrent reports show that State 1 is dominated by procedural computation (either calculation or partial), whereas, problems labeled as State 2 and 3 are generally reported as solved by retrieving the answer."
