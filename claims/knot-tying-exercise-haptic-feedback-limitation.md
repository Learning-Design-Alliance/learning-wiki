## Evidence

### Cannata G 2025

Cannata G, Leone N, Salzano A, Rebecchi F, Morino M (2025). Training in the use of basic functions of the daVinci Xi® robot: a comparative study of residents’ skills. Updates in Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-025-02150-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Qualitative and descriptive findings from the console phase of the 30-resident study, reported in the Discussion. The authors state the difficulty aligns with the previously identified limitation of robotic surgery: the lack of tactile and haptic feedback. No effect size is printed.

> "In Group B, differing levels of surgical experience likely contributed to 9 participants (60%) not completing the knot tying on their first attempt. The most common difficulty reported by both groups for this exercise was thread or needle breakage due to excessive pulling"
