q3 · i? · associational · r2

Zero-order correlations in the same sample showed "teacher-observed emotion regulation was correlated with both academic outcomes (GPA: r = 0.33, p < 0.001 and academic outcomes: r = 0.38, p < 0.001)" compared with r = 0.28 for ERA on both measures.

"Similar to performance-measured ERA, teacher-observed emotion regulation was correlated with both academic outcomes (GPA: r = 0.33, p < 0.001 and academic outcomes: r = 0.38, p < 0.001) and both interpersonal outcomes (understanding others: r = 0.19, p = 0.005 and leadership potential: r = 0.20, p = 0.003)."
