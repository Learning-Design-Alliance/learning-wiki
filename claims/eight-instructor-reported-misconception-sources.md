## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Instructors identified eight sources of misconceptions: four pertaining to teaching methods (traditional introductions, blurring observation and interpretation, prolonged disconnect from physical systems, jargon), three to educational materials (popular science media, ambiguous textbooks, ambiguous figures), and one to students (inappropriate use of problem-solving tools).
