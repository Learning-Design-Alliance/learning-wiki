### Francois St-Hilaire 2021

Francois St-Hilaire, Nathan Burns, Robert Belfer, Muhammad Shayan, Ariella Smofsky, Dung Do Vu, Antoine Frau, Joseph Potochny, Farid Faraji, Vincent Pavero, Neroli Ko, Ansona Onyi Ching, Sabina Elkins, Anush Stepanyan, Adela Matajova, Laurent Charlin, Yoshua Bengio, Iulian Vlad Serban, and Ekaterina Kochmar. (2021). A Comparative Study of Learning Outcomes for Online Learning Platforms. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.07763](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.07763)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Observational correlation analysis of Platform B interaction logs from the comparative study. The article reports first-try correct rate correlating with learning gains (r=0.44) and post-quiz results (r=0.46), and exercises completed with post-quiz score (r=0.28).

> "the rate of correct answers on the ﬁrst try positively correlates with both learning gains ( r=0.44) and post-quiz results ( r=0.46), and the number of exercises completed positively correlates with the post-quiz score ( r=0.28)."

### Francois St-Hilaire 2021 (2)

Francois St-Hilaire, Nathan Burns, Robert Belfer, Muhammad Shayan, Ariella Smofsky, Dung Do Vu, Antoine Frau, Joseph Potochny, Farid Faraji, Vincent Pavero, Neroli Ko, Ansona Onyi Ching, Sabina Elkins, Anush Stepanyan, Adela Matajova, Laurent Charlin, Yoshua Bengio, Iulian Vlad Serban, and Ekaterina Kochmar. (2021). A Comparative Study of Learning Outcomes for Online Learning Platforms. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.07763](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.07763)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same log-based correlation analysis on Platform B: time on exercises correlated positively with first-try correct rate (r=0.34) and negatively with average attempts to a correct answer (r=−0.34).

> "there is a positive correlation between the time spent on exercises and the rate at which participants provided correct answers on the ﬁrst try (r=0.34). At the same time, the time spent on exercises and the average number of attempts participants needed to get a correct answer are negatively correlated ( r=−0.34)."
