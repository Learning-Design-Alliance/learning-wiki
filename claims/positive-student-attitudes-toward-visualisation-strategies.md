## Evidence

### Nahari 2016

Nahari, A. A., & Alfadda, H. A. (2016). From Memorising to Visualising: The Effect of Using Visualisation Strategies to Improve Students' Spelling Skills. English Language Teaching, 9(6), 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 15-item, 5-point Likert-scale questionnaire adapted from Mesmeh (2012) was administered to the 21 experimental-group students. Most respondents reported that visualisation strategies were enjoyable, easy to use, and preferable to traditional methods.

> "In total, 90.48% of the participants agreed that visual strategies are interesting. The second most favourable response (85.71%) was to the question regarding visual strategies being simple and easy to use."
