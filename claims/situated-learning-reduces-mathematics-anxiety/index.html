Evidence¶

Sharma 2016¶

Sharma, Y. (2016). Alleviating mathematics anxiety of elementary school students: A situated perspective. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(2), 509-517. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1110268

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Non-equivalent control group quasi-experiment with 99 seventh graders (49 experimental, 50 control) in two purposively selected schools; 2×2 ANCOVA on mathematics anxiety scores with pre-mathematics anxiety as covariate. The article prints F (1, 94) = 49.853, p<0.01, and the printed effect size eta for treatment was .35, which the article calls medium to large. Adjusted means were 68.98 (situated) vs 78.21 (traditional).

"From Table 2, it can be seen that F (1, 94) = 49.853, p<0.01 is significant. Eta for treatment was .35, which, according to Cohen (1988) is medium to large effect."
