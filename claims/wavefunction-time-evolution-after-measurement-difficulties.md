### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students Understanding of Quantum Measurement Part 1: Investigation of Difficulties. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05720](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05720)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Question on consecutive position measurements of an electron in the ground state of a 1D infinite square well, given to 15 students; "Only two out of ﬁfteen students me ntioned the correct time evolution."

> "These students claimed that the second position measurement will yield the same val ue as the ﬁrst one unless there was an “outside disturbance”. Only two out of ﬁfteen students me ntioned the correct time evolution of the quantum mechanical system after the position measure ment."
