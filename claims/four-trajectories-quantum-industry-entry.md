`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of a corpus of research interviews with quantum industry professionals; the analysis subset focused on 36 employee interviews across 21 companies, coded collaboratively with inter-rater reliability rounds reaching 82% pre-discussion agreement. The article reports "four trajectories" of preparation.

> "Using thematic analysis, we identify four trajectories: (1) continuity of research practice from academia to industry, (2) reframing of prior expertise for quantum applications, (3) incremental engagement through various professional opportunities, and (4) network-enabled entry."
