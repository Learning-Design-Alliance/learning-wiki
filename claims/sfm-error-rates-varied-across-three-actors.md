## Evidence

### Abshire Martha A. 2020

Abshire Martha A., Li Xintong, Basyal Pragyashree Sharma, Teply Melissa L., Singh Arun L., Hayes Margaret M., Turnbull Alison E. (2020). Actor feedback and rigorous monitoring: Essential quality assurance tools for testing behavioral interventions with simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233538

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis of quality assurance data from the SCIP randomized trial: a two-person QA team independently reviewed video recordings and transcripts of simulations performed by three standardized family members and compared error rates using the Kruskal-Wallis rank sum test. The article reports "a statistically significant difference between the total error rates of the three SFMs (H(2) = 8.22, p = 0.02)" and a similarly significant difference in major error rates.

> "There was a statistically significant difference between the total error rates of the three SFMs (H(2) = 8.22, p = 0.02), and the rates of major errors committed (H(2) = 8.33, p = 0.02)"
