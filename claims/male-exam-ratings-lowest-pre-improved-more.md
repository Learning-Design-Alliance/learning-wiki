## Evidence

### McBain Lynn 2016

McBain Lynn, Pullon Susan, Garrett Sue, Hoare Kath. (2016). Genital examination training: assessing the effectiveness of an integrated female and male teaching programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0822-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre/post self-assessment questionnaire analysis (62 matched pairs; no comparison group, and the multimodal programme combined videos, model practice, teaching associates and supervised examinations) with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; Table 2 shows male exam pre medians of 1–2 rising to 3–4 post (p <.0001). The article states skill, confidence and comfort regarding male examinations "were rated particularly low on the pre-teaching programme self-assessment".

> "Following the teaching programme skill, confidence and comfort had improved for both female and male exams, but improved more for male exams."
