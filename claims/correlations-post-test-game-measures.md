## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Accuracy in the game was positively correlated with post-test scores (r = .51), and motivation, response time and reading time also correlated positively with post-test. [→ Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay 2015](#nergiz-ercil-cagiltay-2015)

## Evidence

### Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay 2015

Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay, Erol Ozcelik, Nese Sahin Ozcelik. (2015). The effect of competition on learning in games. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.001

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 2) among the study measures; post-test correlated with reading time (r = .17), response time (r = .32), accuracy (r = .51) and motivation (r = .25); motivation and reading time were not significantly correlated (r = -.07).

> "Total reading time of explanations, motivation, total response time of answering to questions and accuracy in the game were positively correlated with post-test scores."
