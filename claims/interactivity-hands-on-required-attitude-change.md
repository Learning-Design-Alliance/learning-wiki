# Multimedia materials alone are insufficient: attitude changes required interactivity and hands-on use

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The group using lower-interactivity CD presentations (ESB) showed no significant attitude change, indicating that presenting multimedia demonstrations without hands-on student work is not enough. [→ Záhorec 2010](#zahorec-2010)

## Evidence

### Záhorec 2010

Záhorec, J., Hašková, A., & Munk, M. (2010). Impact of Electronic Teaching Materials on Process of Education – Results of an Experiment. Informatics in Education, 9(2), 261–281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064255

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two multimedia groups: ESB used a CD material with "a lower level of interactivity and with a lower possibility of hands-on activities", while ESA used flash animations and interactive simulations with hands-on access at school or home.

> "this was the case of the experimental group ESB and multimedia teaching material Physics Teaching Supporting Teaching Presentation used in it. To elicite the changes, the students must also have the possibility to work with these materials, to have hands-on experience"
