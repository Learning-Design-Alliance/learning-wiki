## Evidence

### Santesson Ia 2024

Santesson Ia, Schell Carl Otto, Bjurling-Sjöberg Petronella. (2024). Capability to identify and manage critical conditions: effects of an interprofessional training intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05567-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal cohort Likert-scale self-confidence ratings across three data collections. The article reports significant increases for identifying signs, initial treatment, and communication, all with p < 0.001, sustained at six months.

> "the level of self-confidence increased from before the training until the week after, with a total proportion that agreed (totally or partly) about self-confidence to identify signs of critical conditions increasing from 78.4% before to 97.7%, p < 0.001, initial treatment from 68.2% to 94.3%, p < 0.001, and communication from 78.4% to 96.6%, p < 0.001"

### Santesson Ia 2024 (2)

Santesson Ia, Schell Carl Otto, Bjurling-Sjöberg Petronella. (2024). Capability to identify and manage critical conditions: effects of an interprofessional training intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05567-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional survey comparing trained versus untrained staff on four self-confidence items. The article reports trained staff agreed to a higher degree on all items but "significant differences were found only regarding self-confidence of initial treatment (p.002)"; ANs differed significantly on all items.

> "although significant differences were found only regarding self-confidence of initial treatment (p.002)"
