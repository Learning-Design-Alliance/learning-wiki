Evidence¶

Russell Scheinberg 2026¶

Russell Scheinberg, Ameeta Agrawal, Tetyana Sydorenko, Kalab Kahsay, Nina Vyatkina, Griet Boone. (2026). What Changes When the Interlocutor Is an AI? Interactional Fluency and Linguistic Uptake in L2 Spoken Dialogue. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22225

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Paired comparison of 78 learners' human-human vs. AI dialogues in a counterbalanced within-participant design. Human dialogues were longer (d=−0.44) with nearly three times as many turns (d=−1.35); AI turns were longer (d=0.39) with more "words per turn (M= 18.0 vs. 12.1, d= 0.51, p < .001)".

"Human-human dialogues were significantly longer (M= 764 s vs. 608 s,d=−0.44,p < .001) and contained nearly three times as many turns (M= 66.5 vs. 22.2,d=−1.35,p < .001)."
