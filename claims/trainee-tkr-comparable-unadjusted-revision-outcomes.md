## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In unadjusted Kaplan–Meier analysis of NJR data, the cumulative probability of failure of trainee-performed TKRs follows a very similar trend to consultant-performed TKRs, with confidence intervals overlapping at all follow-up intervals except 3 years, and the upper confidence interval for trainees below the ODEP A* threshold at all intervals. ## Evidence

### Fowler Timothy J. 2025

Fowler Timothy J., Howells Nicholas R., Blom Ashley W., Sayers Adrian, Whitehouse Michael R. (2025). Association between surgeon training grade and the risk of revision following total knee replacement: An analysis of National Joint Registry data. PLoS Med 22(8): e1004685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1004685

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Unadjusted Kaplan–Meier analysis of 953,081 primary TKRs in the NJR (96,544 trainee-performed), displayed in Fig 2A and Table 2. At 15 years, consultant % Failure was 4.79 (95% CI [4.67, 4.92]) versus trainee (overall) 4.75 (95% CI [4.43, 5.10]); the "confidence intervals for consultant- and trainee-performed TKRs overlap" at all intervals except 3 years.

> "There is a subtle divergence in the probability of failure between 1 and 4 years, with separation between the confidence intervals noted at the 3-year interval. However, the confidence intervals for consultant- and trainee-performed TKRs overlap at all other intervals of follow-up (Table 2)."
