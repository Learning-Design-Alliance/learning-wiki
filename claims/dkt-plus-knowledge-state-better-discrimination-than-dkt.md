## Evidence

### Yeung 2018

Yeung, C.-K., Lin, Z., Yang, K., & Yeung, D.-Y. (2018). Incorporating Features Learned by an Enhanced Deep Knowledge Tracing Model for STEM/Non-STEM Job Prediction. arXiv preprint arXiv:1806.03256. https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03256

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent sample t-tests over each mathematical skill, visualized as a heatmap in Figure 3 (Section 5.2.2). The authors report that "the knowledge state extracted from the DKT+ has a better discrimination power between the STEM and the non-STEM classes" for the majority of skills.

> "For majority of the skills, the independent sample t-tests show that the knowledge state extracted from the DKT+ has a better discrimination power between the STEM and the non-STEM classes, as their magnitudes of t-score are higher than those in the DKT."
