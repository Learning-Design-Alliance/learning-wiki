## Evidence

### Xie Lichun 2025

Xie Lichun, Mo Yongkang, Ma Jiejia, Xiao Yubin, Wu Xiaojia, Ma Xilun, Sun Danmiao, Ke Dongping, Mei Wei, Huang Ruibin. (2025). Rapid-cycle deliberate practice with an ex vivo porcine model of intussusception improves radiology residents' competence in air enema reduction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08339-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 24 radiology residents (n = 12 per group) trained on an ex vivo porcine intussusception model; blinded post-training assessment showed the RCDP group "outperformed the TTM group in both theoretical and practical assessment scores" with the printed means and P values. No effect size for the between-group contrast beyond those printed in Table 3.

> "Between-group comparison, the RCDP group outperformed the TTM group in both theoretical and practical assessment scores (88.67 ± 5.88 vs. 82.41 ± 7.31, P = 0.031 and 89.17 ± 4.64 vs. 80.92 ± 6.65, P = 0.048, respectively)."
