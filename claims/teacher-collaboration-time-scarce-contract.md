### BetsAnn Smith 1998

BetsAnn Smith. (1998). It's About Time: Opportunities to Learn in Chicago's Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/its-about-time-opportunities-learn

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Drawing on teacher interviews and contract analysis, the report finds the 8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m. contract leaves no regular weekly block for collaborative work; some schools use flex-time early releases of 90-120 minute work blocks, which some parents and Local School Councils have voted to end.

> "In all cases, time for teacher learning and development is scarce and fragmented; there is no chunk of time set aside in the basic contract and school schedule for teachers to work to- gether on a weekly basis."
