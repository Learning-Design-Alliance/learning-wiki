Cross-sectional survey of 36 directors rating the importance of 31 obesity-related competencies on a four-point Likert scale. "'Perform effectively in an interprofessional team'" and "'discuss obesity in a non-judgmental manner'" led; details for all competencies are in Fig. 2.

> "The competencies most frequently reported as ‘very important’ were ‘Perform effectively in an interprofessional team’ and ‘discuss obesity in a non-judgmental manner’. These were considered ‘very important’ by 86% and 81% of respondents, respectively."
