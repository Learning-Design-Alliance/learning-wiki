## Evidence

### Moire K. M. Prescott 2024

Moire K. M. Prescott, Laura Madson, Sandra M. Way, Kelly N. Sanderson. (2024). Prevalence of a growth mindset among introductory astronomy students. arXiv:2407.06147 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of postsurvey responses versus final grade percentage for survey completers, by demographic group (Table X). Attitude measures showed somewhat stronger correlations (competence ρ = 0.4; mindset temperature ρ = 0.4).

> "Overall, mindset beliefs scores are only weakly correlated with final grades ( ρ = 0.2), with even weaker correlations suggested for male versus female and White versus Hispanic students."
