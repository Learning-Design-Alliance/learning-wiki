## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, P., Yu, G., Xu, R., Cao, S., Lin, M., Liu, Y., & Wu, Y. (2026). Factors associated with medication safety competence among clinical nurses in China: A latent profile analysis. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000749

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA across the four latent profiles (N=1,228) on six MSCS dimensions; Table 2 reports F values from 1750.276 to 6192.598, all p<.001. Dimension means rose monotonically from Profile 1 to Profile 4 (e.g., patient-centered medication management 3.26 to 4.96).

> "the mean scores of medication safety competence dimensions significantly differed among the four profiles (all p<.05). Profile 1 (n=112, 9%) had the lowest scores of medication safety competence."
