q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's RQ2 synthesis reports that when mistakes happen, models "can provide alternative solutions instantly, allowing continuous iteration and refinement", which the authors say accelerates learning cycles and enhances problem-solving efficiency. No effect sizes are printed.

"In STEM programs, LLMs assist students in testing, debugging, and improving technical setups or prototypes during project-based or inquiry-driven activities"
