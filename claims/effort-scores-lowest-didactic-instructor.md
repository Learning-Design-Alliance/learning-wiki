### Stieha Vicki 2024

Stieha Vicki, Earl Brittnee, Hagens Harrisen, Haynes Meagan, Ulappa Amy, Bond Laura, Oxford Julia Thom. (2024). An exploration of the relationship between active learning and student motivation in STEM: a mixed methods study. Advances in Physiology Education. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00247.2022

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA with post-hoc Tukey tests across seven instructors found significant effort differences (F = 2.977, P = 0.007 as printed); "nearly all instructors (INSTs A/C/D/E) differed significantly from INST F", whose upper-division didactic course had the lowest effort mean (3.01, Table 5).

> "Comparing the means for the effort scores, nearly all instructors (INSTs A/C/D/E) differed significantly from INST F. The effort scores for INST F, an upper division course, were lowest."
