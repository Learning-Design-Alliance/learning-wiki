## Evidence

### Nye 2004

Nye, B., Konstantopoulos, S., & Hedges, L. V. (2004). How Large Are Teacher Effects? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 26(3), 237-257. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=How+Large+Are+Teacher+Effects

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Variance decomposition from the HLM analysis of the randomized STAR data, comparing teacher and school variance components by grade. The authors add that for students with equal previous achievement scores, teacher effects are much larger than school effects. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In reading, the between teacher variance component is over twice as large as between-school variance component at grade 2 and over three times as large at grade 3."
