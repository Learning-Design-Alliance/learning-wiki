`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme from student interviews mirroring the faculty structure. Students cited academic integrity violations and lack of oversight as user-related risks, and "the production of false information", algorithmic manipulation, privacy and data security, algorithmic bias, and intellectual property violations as algorithm-related risks.

> "Students also expressed ethical risks in a similar manner, based on users and algorithms"
