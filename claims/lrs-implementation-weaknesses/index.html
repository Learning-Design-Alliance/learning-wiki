Evidence¶

Wiggins et al. 2017¶

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation findings from case study interviews, focus groups, and observations. The report also notes criticism that "the small size of the handset made it difficult to type properly". These usability concerns qualify the generally positive perceptions.

"Some teachers and pupils felt that the inability of the system to let children 'show their working' was a weakness. There was also a concern that incorrectly programmed or over-specified answers meant that pupils occasionally received negative feedback when in fact their answer was wholly or partially correct."
