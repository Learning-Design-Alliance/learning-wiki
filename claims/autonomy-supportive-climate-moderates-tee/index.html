Related Claims¶

  • Students labeled with low teacher expectations reported weaker later perceived competence in low autonomy-supportive classrooms (a correlational moderation) — related
  • Teachers' expectations and perceived autonomy-supportive climate are uncorrelated, making climate an unlikely consequence of expectations — related
