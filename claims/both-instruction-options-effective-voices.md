## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within-group gains from pretest to delayed posttest were significant and large on the written form and meaning/use test (eta squared .40 in COG and .50 in STAN) and on the oral test (etas squared .49 and .55). [→ Bielak et al. 2013](#bielak-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### Bielak et al. 2013

Jakub Bielak, Mirosųaw Pawlak, Anna Mystkowska-Wiertelak. (2013). Teaching the English active and passive voice with the help of cognitive grammar: An empirical study. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching 3 (4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135829

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t tests on the written form and meaning/use test in the quasi-experimental study. Pretest-to-delayed-posttest gains were significant for both groups, with printed eta squared values of .40 (COG) and .50 (STAN).

> "all of these differences reached statistical significance and their magnitudes were very large (all etas squared were much higher than .14, ranging between .40 and .82)"
