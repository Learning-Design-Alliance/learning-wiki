## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Wacht Oren, Ben Shlomo Izhar, Gitler Asaf, Gidron Yori. (2025). Does heart rate variability predict and improve performance in pediatric CPR?—a simulation study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-025-01209-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 3 min SPB (inhale 4 s, hold 1 s, exhale 5 s) versus an educational video in 57 paramedic students and medics. The article reports "no differences were found between groups" on recall (d = 0.06), compression rate, depth, and threat/challenge appraisal (chi-square tests, all p > 0.05).

> "No difference was found between the groups on information recall score (t(55) = 0.23, p = 0.62; Cohen's d = 0.06). We also calculated the chi-square for cognitive appraisal and chest compression rate and depth, as well as scores as categorical outcomes, and again, no differences were found between groups"
