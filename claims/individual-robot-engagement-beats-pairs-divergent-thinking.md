`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of Mazzoni et al. (2020), a quasi-experimental pre-post study with 171 primary school students interacting with an educational robot. The review reports the individual-versus-pair contrast in divergent-thinking gains and suggests possible causes such as conflicts or cognitive overload in unstructured peer interactions. No effect size is printed.

> "Mazzoni et al. (2020) observed that children who engaged individually with an educational robot showed greater gains in divergent thinking than those who worked in pairs"
