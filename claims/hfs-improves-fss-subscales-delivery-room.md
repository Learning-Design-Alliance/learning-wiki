# In a cohort of 43 practitioners, three Flow State Scale subscales — merging of action and awareness, unambiguous feedback, and loss of self-consciousness — had significantly higher self-reported scores at the second than the first high-fidelity simulation course in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Strozzi Mariachiara Martina 2021

Strozzi Mariachiara Martina, Varrica Alessandro, Colivicchi Micaela, Pelazzo Claudia, Negri Rossana, Galante Anna, Ianniello Patrizia, Sterpone Rossella, Nannini Priscilla, Leo Daniela, Mannarino Francesca, Striani Manuel, Montani Stefania, Gazzolo Diego. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational pretest-posttest study of 43 practitioners attending two HFS delivery-room courses; Short Flow State Scale administered at the end of each model. The article reports "higher (P < 0.05, for all) SFSS values at M2 than M1" for the three named subscales; no effect size is printed.

> "Conversely, higher (P < 0.05, for all) SFSS values at M2 than M1 were found regarding SFSS2 (merging of action and awareness), SFSS4 (unambiguous feed-back), and SFSS7 (loss of self-consciousness)."
