## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable multilevel logistic regression on Oriel recruitment data (table 5). Interview score OR 6.78 (6.47 to 7.10) in the multivariable model; univariable interview OR 8.41 (8.10 to 8.73). The article reports shortlisting scores "become non-significant" once interview scores are controlled.

> "Another noteworthy observation is that once the odds are conditioned on the relative interview scores the shortlisting scores become non-significant predictors of ’appointability'."
