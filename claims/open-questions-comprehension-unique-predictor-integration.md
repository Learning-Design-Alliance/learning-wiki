## Evidence

### Kullberg 2023

Kullberg, N., Kiili, C., Bråten, I., González-Ibáñez, R., & Leppänen, P. H. T. (2023). Sixth graders' selection and integration when writing from multiple online texts. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09613-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the hierarchical regression, open-question comprehension (β = .257 in step 1, p = .007) was a unique positive predictor while the cloze test was not (β = .043, p = .662). The authors suggest open questions required skills more closely related to composing from multiple texts.

> "It is noteworthy that reading comprehension assessed with open questions was a unique positive predictor of integration performance, whereas reading comprehension measured with the cloze test was not."
