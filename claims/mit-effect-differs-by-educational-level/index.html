Evidence¶

Baş 2016¶

Baş, G. (2016). The effect of multiple intelligences theory-based education on academic achievement: A meta-analytic review. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 1833–1864. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.6.0015

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis by educational level across 52 elementary, 21 high-school and 2 university studies, random effects model after heterogeneity QB(2) = 7.317, p < .001. The article interprets MIT-based instruction as having "a larger effect size in high schools than in elementary schools or universities".

"the largest effect size was concluded to belong to the high-school level with d = 1.516 (95% CI = 1.115 – 1.917), while the smallest effect size belonged to the elementary-school level with d = 0.925 (95% CI = 0.766 – 1.084)"
