Evidence¶

Walkington 2013¶

Walkington, C. A. (2013). Using adaptive learning technologies to personalize instruction to student interests: The impact of relevant contexts on performance and learning outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology, 105(4), 932–945. doi:10.1037/a0031882

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Gaming detector (Baker & deCarvalho, 2008) run on Unit 6 log data. Student's t-test compared gaming estimates between conditions. Experimental students gamed less with "Cohen's d = 0.35", a small effect. No significant gaming differences in Unit 10 (p = 0.737).
