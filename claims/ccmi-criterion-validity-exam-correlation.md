### Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Assessing Student Learning in Middle-Division Classical Mechanics/Math Methods. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.008)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

Criterion validity analysis at CU comparing CCMI post-test scores to exam performance. Each class took three exams (two hour exams and a final), whose averages were z-scored to allow comparisons across instructors; the article reports r = 0.71 and that the linear model accounts for "50% of the variance in exam scores associated with CCMI scores." Similarly high correlations were observed on the CUE.

> "CCMI post-test scores were strongly correlated with these z-scored exam averages (r = 0.71, p < 0.05); a linear model can thus account for 50% of the variance in exam scores associated with CCMI scores."
