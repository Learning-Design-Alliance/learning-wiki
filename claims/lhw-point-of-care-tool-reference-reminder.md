Qualitative interview study of trained lay health workers in Zomba, Malawi. One participant stated: "the tool guides and reminds us in our work"; others said pictorials on the chart helped clients with low literacy grasp information.

> "While both components were highly valued, the point-of-care tool was noted to be particularly important, functioning both as a reference during patient interactions and as a general reminder/training refresher."
