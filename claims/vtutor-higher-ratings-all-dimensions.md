# VTutor received significantly higher ratings than the SadTalker talking head on all five evaluation dimensions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` VTutor was rated significantly higher than SadTalker on general preference, sync accuracy, naturalness, emotional expression, and visual coherence (p < .01). [→ Eason Chen 2025](#eason-chen-2025)
`q2 i3` For General Preference, VTutor averaged 5.00 (SD 1.75) versus SadTalker's 3.62 (SD 1.51), t = 4.2218, p = 0.0001, Cohen's d = 0.8444; for Sync Accuracy, 4.58 (SD 1.82) versus 3.66 (SD 1.49), d = 0.5528. [→ Eason Chen 2025 (2)](#eason-chen-2025-2)

Independent-samples t-tests compared the two agents' ratings on 1–7 scales in the 50-participant online evaluation; the article reports a "significant advantage across all evaluation dimensions ( p < .01)" for VTutor.
