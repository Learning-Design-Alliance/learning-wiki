## Evidence

### Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017

Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org/learning-studios

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analyses across the 60 pilot sites relating implementation scope to student outcomes. All agency indicators were at least marginally significant, with choosing to learn something new and giving feedback to peers showing the strongest relationships with project exposure; correlations are reported without coefficients.

> "For engagement and persistence indicators, every indicator had positive and significant correlations with the number of Learning Studio projects implemented in the classroom."

### Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017 (2)

Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org/learning-studios

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative grouping of sites into emergent and established contexts in an early analysis using the full set of quantitative and qualitative responses. Emergent sites focused on developing technology skills and executing assignments; established sites had student-driven learning and design thinking already in place.

> "While the outcomes at “established” sites were generally stronger, the Learning Studio was one of many causal contributors; in “emergent” sites, Learning Studios served a larger role as a catalyst for new ways of engaging students in 21st century competencies."
