`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral results section of a within-subject study in which 48 high school students (ages 14-18) completed two counterbalanced quizzes with rotating AI modes. The authors report that "behavioral tests yielded highly significant differences between the three AI interaction modes," with Friedman tests significant for all three measures.

> "Compared to the EEG results, behavioral tests yielded highly significant differences between the three AI interaction modes."
