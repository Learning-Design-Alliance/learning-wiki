### Songlin Xu 2023

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Limitation analysis in the Discussion of the N=311 experiment: video-level analyses of the five AI-topic course videos did not always reach significance, though group differences persisted in per-video means; the authors attribute this to insufficient sample sizes for individual video analyses.

> "While the overall analysis found significant accuracy differences between groups, results were not always significant when analyzing videos individually. However, group differences remained present in mean values per video."
