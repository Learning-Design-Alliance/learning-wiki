### Abreu M 2026

Abreu M, Suárez A, Stari C and Martí A C. (2026). Exploring the potential of ChatGPT for feedback and evaluation in experimental physics. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae8110](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae8110)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the API-based evaluation's technical precision and conceptual depth dimension. The model detected some genuine notation and statistical errors but its conceptual interpretation of physical results was often inconsistent, and "GPT flagged non-existent errors or formulas that were correctly expressed by students".

> "In several instances, GPT flagged non-existent errors or formulas that were correctly expressed by students, revealing an irregular and often inaccurate performance in assessing technical and conceptual aspects."
