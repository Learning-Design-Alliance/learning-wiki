## Evidence

### Breuer Sonja 2023

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderation analyses A by CE type: "true–false items with a high guessing probability of 50%" (k = 5; d av = −1.51, p = 0.001) showed a larger gap than single-choice items.

> "When true–false items with a high guessing probability of 50% ( k = 5; d av = −1.51, p = 0.001) were used rather than single-choice 1 out of 4 or 5 options items with a guessing probability of 20–25%"

### Breuer Sonja 2023 (2)

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Aggregated moderation analysis "revealed no significant effects of the CE format type overall"; equivalence not tested.

> "Further analyses with the aggregated CE format categories ( single-choice versus multiple-choice ) as the moderator revealed no significant effects of the CE format type overall or for any of the individual cognitive ability categories"
