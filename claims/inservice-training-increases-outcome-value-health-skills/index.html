Evidence¶

Fahlman M 2011¶

Fahlman M, McCaughtry N, Martin J, Shen B. (2011). School health educators' self-efficacy, intentions to teach, and implementation of nutrition education increases after in-service training. Am J Health Educ. 2011;42(3):181-190. https://doi.org/10.1080/19325037.2011.10599191

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the 59-teacher sample for the single-item outcome-value measure (ranked 1-6). Conflict resolution rose from 3.4 to 4.7 and personal health skills from 2.0 to 4.4 in the intervention group; other ranked skills (computer, math, reading, writing) showed no significant change.

"Teachers who participated in the in-service had a significant increase in the importance of conflict resolution skills (F = 6.24, P = 0.018) and personal health skills (F = 5.91, P = 0.026). They also were higher in these measures than the control group at post-test."
