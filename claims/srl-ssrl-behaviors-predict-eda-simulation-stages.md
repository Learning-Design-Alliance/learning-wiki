`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The commentary summarizes Moreno et al.'s (2025) empirical study in which "Sixty-two medical residents participated in simulations coded for behavioral and regulatory strategies, with EDA data collected via wearable sensors." Supervised machine learning (Lasso regression) predicted average EDA peaks per segment across the three phases of the simulation task.

> "Results showed that different patterns of SRL and SSRL behaviors were predictive of EDA at distinct stages of the simulation, highlighting how learners' regulatory needs shift over time."
