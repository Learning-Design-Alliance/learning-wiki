## Evidence

### Josje Lodder 2021

Josje Lodder, Bastiaan Heeren, Johan Jeuring, Wendy Neijenhuis. (2021). Generation and Use of Hints and Feedback in a Hilbert-Style Axiomatic Proof Tutor. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 31:99-133. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00222-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of homework from 65 students in the course Logic and Computer Science across academic years 2014-2015 and 2015-2016. The authors report that "22 had to redo their homework exercise on axiomatic proofs" versus 5 out of 65 for semantic tableaux; no effect size is printed.

> "From these students, 22 had to redo their homework exercise on axiomatic proofs. This is significantly higher than, for example, the number of students in the same group who had to redo the exercise on semantic tableaux: 5 out of 65."
