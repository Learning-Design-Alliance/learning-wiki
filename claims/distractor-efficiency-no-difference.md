`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level distractor efficiency was compared in the ITT population with a Wilcoxon rank-sum test; a functional distractor was selected by at least 5% of examinees. Bootstrap 95% CIs for mean DE overlapped (Student-gen 34.3%–45.6%; AI-gen 27.7%–37.7%), which the article reads as supporting comparable distractor plausibility.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in DE between protocols (mean DE: 39.8% ± 30.4% for Student-gen versus 32.6% ± 27.2% for AI-gen; Wilcoxon rank-sum test, W = 7182, p = 0.070; Fig. 6)."
