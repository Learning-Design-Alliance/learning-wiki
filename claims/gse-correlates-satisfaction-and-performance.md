## Evidence

### Innab Adnan 2026

Innab Adnan, Hakamy Essa, Abdulgafor Nedaa, AlOtaibi Nora Ghalib. (2026). The influence of self-efficacy and satisfaction with e-learning on academic performance among nursing students: a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09236-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson's product-moment correlation among 163 nursing students. All three pairwise correlations significant at p<.001: GSE-satisfaction r=.579, GSE-performance r=.511, satisfaction-performance r=.539.

> "General self-efficacy was significantly associated with satisfaction with e-learning (r = .579, p < .001) and academic performance (r = .511, p < .001), while satisfaction with e-learning was also significantly correlated with academic performance (r = .539, p < .001)."
