## Evidence

### Wolbers 2018

Wolbers, K., Dostal, H., Graham, S., Branum-Martin, L., Kilpatrick, J., & Saulsburry, R. (2018). Strategic and Interactive Writing Instruction: An Efficacy Study in Grades 3-5. Journal of Educational and Developmental Psychology, 8(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jedp.v8n1p99

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Researchers rated videotaped lessons with the SIWI Observation and Fidelity Instrument (53 items). The article reports fidelity "did not differ by genre but did differ by subcategories on the SIWI-OFI," with interactive instruction highest.

> "Teachers' percentages of fidelity to SIWI ranged from 60.4%-89% with an average of 75%."

### Wolbers 2018 (2)

Wolbers, K., Dostal, H., Graham, S., Branum-Martin, L., Kilpatrick, J., & Saulsburry, R. (2018). Strategic and Interactive Writing Instruction: An Efficacy Study in Grades 3-5. Journal of Educational and Developmental Psychology, 8(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jedp.v8n1p99

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article cites a previous study of the 3-year SIWI professional development program showing steady fidelity increases, concluding that "teachers need ongoing, longitudinal PD with supported practice."

> "Teachers performed at 74% fidelity on average after 1 year of SIWI PD, 85% after 2 years, and 94% after 3 years (Wolbers, Dostal, Skerrit & Stephenson, 2016)."
