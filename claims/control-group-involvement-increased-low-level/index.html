Akyol 2018¶

Wilcoxon Signed Rank test on the 26 control children: pre-test mean 1.97, post-test 2.42, z=-3.319, p=0.001 as printed in Table 4. The discussion states control involvement "increased from extremely low leve l to low level" while the experiment group reached medium-high.
