## Evidence

### Leopold D.R. 2024

Leopold D.R., Kim H., Carlson K.W., Rowe M.A., Groff B.R., Major M.P., Willcutt E.G., Cutting L.E., Banich M.T. (2024). Stimulus shapes strategy: Effects of stimulus characteristics and individual differences in academic achievement on the neural mechanisms engaged during the N-back task. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2024.101372

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Whole-brain fMRI contrast of stimulus type across both N-back conditions (0-back and 2-back > fixation) in 126 youth, controlling for age and gender. The study reports nonsense shapes "were associated with greater activation across occipital and parahippocampal cortex, lateral and posterior hippocampus". No effect size printed.

> "The nonsense shape stimuli, however, were associated with greater activation across occipital and parahippocampal cortex, lateral and posterior hippocampus (Fig. 4B), right inferior frontal cortex, bilateral anterior operculum/insular cortex,"
