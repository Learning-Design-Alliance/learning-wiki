### Feinberg JL 2017

Feinberg JL, Russell D, Mola A, Bowles KH, Lipman TH. (2017). Developing an Adapted Cardiac Rehabilitation Training for Home Care Clinicians. Journal of Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation and Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1097/HCR.0000000000000228

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative focus group analysis: 19 clinicians (RNs = 11; PTs/OTs = 8) in four 30-minute groups, coded with the constant comparative method. Some clinicians said they "do not know what cardiac rehab is", and therapists wanted "specific protocols of increasing [activity] load".

> "Clinicians identified gaps in care transitions between the hospital and home care setting. Clinicians often lacked information about the patient's hospitalization and prior functioning."
