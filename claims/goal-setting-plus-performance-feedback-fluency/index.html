Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

The review's vote-counting synthesis for the fluency domain, where four studies of goal setting added to performance feedback during oral reading showed a consistent positive direction of effect. No effect magnitudes are reported because the review used effect-direction vote counting rather than meta-analysis.
