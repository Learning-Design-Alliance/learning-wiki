## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among W/HU students, DTI-measured self-assessed clinical reasoning increased significantly from the 5th to the 8th semester with a large effect size, and from the 5th to the 6th semester with a medium effect size, while the 6th-to-8th semester difference was not significant. ## Evidence

### Gehlhar Kirsten 2014

Gehlhar Kirsten, Klimke-Jung Kathrin, Stosch Christoph, Fischer Martin R. (2014). Do different medical curricula influence self-assessed clinical thinking of students? GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 31(2), Doc23. [doi:10.3205/zma000915](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000915)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quasi-experimental cross-sectional survey of model-curriculum students at Witten/Herdecke University in the 5th, 6th and 8th semesters, compared with the Mann-Whitney test. The article reports "a distinct corresponding semester-related increase" with significance and effect-size wording but prints no numeric d value in the text.

> "Among students at W/HU (see figure 3 (Fig. 3)), a distinct corresponding semester-related increase in self-assessed clinical reasoning was evidenced using DTI; the increase from the 5th to the 8th semester is significant and has a large effect size."
