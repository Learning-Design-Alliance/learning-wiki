## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Liang 2011

Liang, L. A. (2011). Scaffolding Middle School Students' Comprehension and Response to Short Stories. RMLE Online, 34(8). ISSN 1940-4476. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ925248

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest procedural knowledge assessments scored by trained scorers with rubrics (inter-rater reliability 92%–98%). Both groups scored near zero on pretests; "the cognitive-oriented group's means improved for the cognitive-oriented items but not for the reader-response items" and vice versa.

> "A significant main effect for treatment was noted for the cognitive-oriented section of the posttests, F (1, 158) = 490.00, p < .001 with an effect size of 0.76, and for the reader-response section on the posttests, F (1, 158) = 711.36, p < .001, with an effect size of 0.82."
