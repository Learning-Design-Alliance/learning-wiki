## Evidence

### Vignoles et al. 2015

Anna Vignoles, John Jerrim, Richard Cowan. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Primary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. http://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/library/maths-mastery-primary

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome analysis of the same clustered RCT: a second OLS regression including a pre-test-by-treatment interaction (equation 2), estimated on the 4,176-pupil analysis sample with school-clustered standard errors. The interaction coefficient was 0.003 overall (SE 0.009).

> "The interaction effect is not significantly different from zero, with the 95% confidence interval ranging from -0.01 to +0.02. Thus there is little evidence that the effect of Mathematics Mastery differs between children with different levels of prior achievement."
