Megan Kuhfeld 2019¶

Megan Kuhfeld. (2019). Rethinking summer slide: The more you gain, the more you lose. KappanOnline. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/rethinking-summer-slide-the-more-you-gain-the-more-you-lose/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Large-scale observational analysis of seasonal score patterns on NWEA MAP Growth assessments for 3.4 million students. The article reports that "summer slide is common, but not inevitable." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Megan Kuhfeld draws on data from the 3.4 million students who took the NWEA MAP Growth assessments to find that summer slide is common, but not inevitable."
