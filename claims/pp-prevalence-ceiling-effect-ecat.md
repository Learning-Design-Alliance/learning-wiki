Chi-squared analysis of reported pain at any time point across E-CAT groups in the prospective cohort. The authors attribute the discrepancy with intensity findings to a clinical "ceiling effect": most patients across all groups experienced at least mild transient pain after molar RCT.

> "Although PP prevalence trended upward with higher E-CAT scores (from 83.0% in the low group to 94.7% in the high group), this did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.067)"
