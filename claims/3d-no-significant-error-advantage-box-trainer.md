## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference in error count between 3D and 2D groups for Task 1 or Task 2. [→ Kunert W 2020](#kunert-w-2020)

## Evidence

### Kunert W 2020

Kunert W, Storz P, Dietz N, Axt S, Falch C, Kirschniak A, Wilhelm P. (2020). Learning curves, potential and speed in training of laparoscopic skills: a randomised comparative study in a box trainer. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07768-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparison of error counts across repetitions in the same 40-novice box-trainer study. The article reports "no significant difference in error count" with median error reductions of 1 (Task 1) and 0 (Task 2); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in error count (Task 1: median error reduction with 3D = 1; Task 2: median error reduction with 3D = 0)."
