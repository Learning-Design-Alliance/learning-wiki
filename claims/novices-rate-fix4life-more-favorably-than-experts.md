## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2026

Rahimi A. Masie, van Emden Michael, Hardon Sem F., Simons Maarten, Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim, Daams Freek. (2026). Assessing the realism and face validity of Fix For Life: an embalmed human cadaver model for high-fidelity laparoscopic training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12550-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of 30 novices versus 10 experts on face validation ratings. "There was a significant difference in lifelike manipulation of organs (p < 0.001)"; cholecystectomy tissue manipulation (p = 0.036) and TEP tissue color (p = 0.014) also differed.

> "When comparing the novices’ and experts’ assessment of the laparoscopic appendectomy, there was a significant difference in lifelike manipulation of organs (p < 0.001) and comparability with reality (p = 0.046)."
