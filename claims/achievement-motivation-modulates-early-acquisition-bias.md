## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants' achievement motivation modulated the difference in conditioned response to goal-relevant vs. goal-irrelevant stimuli during early acquisition (η2p = 0.069). [→ Yoann Stussi 2019](#yoann-stussi-2019)

## Evidence

### Yoann Stussi 2019

Yoann Stussi, Aude Ferrero, Gilles Pourtois and David Sander. (2019). Achievement motivation modulates Pavlovian aversive conditioning to goal-relevant stimuli. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0043-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures GLM contrast on early-acquisition conditioned responses in 72 participants, testing the a priori hypothesis about achievement motivation modulation. The article reports the interaction "modulated by participants ’ achievement motivation" with printed effect size η2p = 0.069.

> "the difference between the CR to goal-relevant stimuli and the CR to goal-irrelevant stimuli was modulated by participants ’ achievement motivation during early acquisition, F(1, 70) = 5.15, p = 0.026, η2p = 0.069, 90% CI [0.004, 0.177], with high level of achievement motivation resulting in a greater difference in CR acquisition readiness"
