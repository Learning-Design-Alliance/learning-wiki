Questionnaire study of everyday conceptions of holography in 166 students of a Berlin comprehensive school in grades 8-10; only "slightly more than 12 percent claim to have already seen a real hologram". No effect sizes reported.

> "In a questionnaire 166 students of a Berlin comprehensive school ( Gesamtschule) were asked to impart their everyday conceptions of holography. This study was conduced in the 8 th and 10th grades. Of the questioned students, allegedly slightly more than 12 percent claim to have already seen a real hologram."
