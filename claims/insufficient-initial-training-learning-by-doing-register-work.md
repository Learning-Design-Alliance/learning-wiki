## Evidence

### Bjørngaard Hilde 2022

Bjørngaard Hilde, Koksvik Hege Svean, Jakobsen Bente, Grønning Kjersti. (2022). Nurses experience increased clinical and organisational competence by working with a medical quality register, RevNatus – a qualitative study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-022-08595-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interviews, nurses described varied initial training: uncertainty about entering variables, measuring disease activity, and setting damage-index values. Some said their "knowledge improved quickly by ‘learning and doing’". No effect size is applicable to this qualitative finding.

> "Some said that their ‘basic’ knowledge about registers was insufficient when they started to register data in RevNatus, but their knowledge improved quickly by ‘learning and doing’."
