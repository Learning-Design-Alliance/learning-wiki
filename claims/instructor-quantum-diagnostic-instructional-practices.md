## Evidence

### Shayan Majidy 2024

Shayan Majidy. (2024). Addressing misconceptions in university physics: A review and experiences from quantum physics educators. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the 12-instructor interview study: several instructors described quizzes containing trap answers based on common misunderstandings, with clickers or polling apps revealing response distributions; the article states these are "the intended benefits of the activity, rather than outcomes independently assessed in this study."

> "One instructor, for example, presents measurement scenarios to examine whether students believe a conscious observer is required for wave-function collapse."
