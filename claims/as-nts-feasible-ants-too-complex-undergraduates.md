## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi P 2019

Moll-Khosrawi P, Kamphausen A, Hampe W, Schulte-Uentrop L, Zimmermann S, Kubitz JC. (2019). Anaesthesiology students’ Non-Technical skills: development and evaluation of a behavioural marker system for students (AS-NTS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Step 4 interviews with eight first-year anaesthesiology residents who used both AS-NTS and ANTS in simulation training including video taping; no further dimension had to be added, and the eight trainees "decided to continue their postgraduate training curriculum using AS-NTS, rather than ANTS".

> "they confirmed the feasibility of AS-NTS and concluded that in undergraduates, as well as in the first 2 years of residency in anaesthesiology, the ANTS system is too complex."

### Moll-Khosrawi P 2019 (2)

Moll-Khosrawi P, Kamphausen A, Hampe W, Schulte-Uentrop L, Zimmermann S, Kubitz JC. (2019). Anaesthesiology students’ Non-Technical skills: development and evaluation of a behavioural marker system for students (AS-NTS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Evaluation questionnaire study of 21 anaesthetists with at least three uses of AS-NTS in undergraduate teaching; the tool was confirmed "feasible and practical" and raters additionally rated the importance of each dimension.

> "The results from an additional evaluation questionnaire, completed by 21 anaesthetits, who had used the rating tool at least three times in undergraduate medical education, confirmed that the AS-NTS was feasible and practical."
