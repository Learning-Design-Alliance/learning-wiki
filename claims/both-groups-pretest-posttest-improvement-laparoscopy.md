## Evidence

### Sloth Sigurd Beier 2021

Sloth Sigurd Beier, Jensen Rune Dall, Seyer-Hansen Mikkel, Christensen Mette Krogh, De Win Gunter. (2021). Remote training in laparoscopy: a randomized trial comparing home-based self-regulated training to centralized instructor-regulated training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08429-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of LASTT task times in the randomized trial showed significant pretest-to-posttest improvement in both arms; the printed estimated decreases in mean task time are quoted. No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "For hand–eye coordination, the estimated decrease in mean task time was 33 (95% CI 18–48; p < 0.001) and 46 (95% CI 31–62; p < 0.001) seconds in the CIRT and HSRT group, respectively. For bimanual coordination, the estimated decrease in mean task time was 24 (95% CI 15–34; p < 0.001) and 24 (95% CI 14–34; p < 0.001) seconds"
