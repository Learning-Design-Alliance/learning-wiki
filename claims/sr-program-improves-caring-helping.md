## Evidence

### Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017

Romualdas K. Malinauskas, Dalius R. Juodsnukis. (2017). Education of Social Responsibility among Sports Schools Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.289

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group post-program comparison in the Results section of the educational experiment (26 experimental vs 26 control students). The article reports a significant difference in caring and helping; no effect size is printed.

> "After experiment significant differences were found between experimental and control groups in ―respect‖ (t (50) = 2.31; p = .02) and in ―caring and helping‖ ( t (50) = 2.00; p = .04)."
