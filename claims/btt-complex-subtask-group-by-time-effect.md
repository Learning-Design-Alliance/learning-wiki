## Evidence

### Hehl 2026

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed model on complex-subtask progress-test scores in the 22-participant pilot. Group comparisons showed differences for the last two progress tests (PT5: p = 0.0014; PT6: p = 0.0024), and "each training group showed signiﬁcant improvements for each pair of subsequent progress tests".

> "the time spent on the BTT (β = 0.0617, SE = 0.0043, df = 110, t = 14.485, p < 0.0001) and the interaction GROUP×BTT time (β = 0.0259, SE = 0.0060, df = 110, t = 4.309, p < 0.0001) had a signiﬁcant effect on task performance of complex task variants"
