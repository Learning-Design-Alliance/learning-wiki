## Evidence

### Heng W 2025

Heng W, Ho MH, Mah XH, Lim JJ, Syamsury NBHA, Haagensen E, Ong ELC, Hubbard P. (2025). Exploring pre‐clinical medical students' perception of and participation in active learning: A mixed‐methods transnational study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15611

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of five focus groups (25 students, 18 NUMed, 7 UK). "inadequate time and exhaustion emerge as significant factors impeding students' adoption of active learning methods"; students also described safety with familiar peers enabling participation.

> "The focus groups highlighted a number of factors that influenced students' engagement in active learning. For instance, inadequate time and exhaustion emerge as significant factors impeding students' adoption of active learning methods."
