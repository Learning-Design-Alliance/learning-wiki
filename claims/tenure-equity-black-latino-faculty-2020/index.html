Evidence¶

Jinann Bitar 2022¶

Jinann Bitar, Gabriel Montague, Lauren Ilano. (2022). Faculty Diversity and Student Success Go Hand in Hand, So Why Are University Faculties So White? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626440

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS 2020 tenure data for 542 public institutions, comparing the percentage of tenured Black faculty to overall tenured faculty per campus. The analysis found "44.5% of institutions had an A grade" for Black faculty tenure equity. No effect size is printed.

"44.5% of institutions had an A grade for their equitable hiring of Black faculty, and 15.9% of institutions had an F grade for tenure equity for Black faculty. Notably, 23 of the institutions (4.2%) in our sample had no Black faculty"
