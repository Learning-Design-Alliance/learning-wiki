### Thomas J. Bing and Edward F. Redish 2007

Thomas J. Bing and Edward F. Redish. (2007). Symbolic Manipulators Affect Mathematical Mindsets. https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1187 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.1187](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.1187)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Roadblock analysis of one honors student (S1) in a six-student homework group on Problem 5.6 in Griffiths; the authors identify five roadblocks in a seven-minute stretch, and note the "very small effort investment" required to try another integral in Mathematica.

> "It costs S1 a very small effort investment to try evaluating this slightly different integral."
