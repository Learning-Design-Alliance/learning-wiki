### Wang Sufen 2026

Wang Sufen, Shen Jun, Qiang Banghong, Xu Honghai, Huang Xiaomei, Wang Lizheng. (2026). An integrated teaching model combining PBL and medical humanities education for pathology laboratory teaching: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09701-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teaching satisfaction was assessed with a single-item question rated as very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, or dissatisfied, and compared using the χ² test. The "significantly" higher satisfaction in the experimental group (χ² = 15.356, P = 0.002) included 55.4% very satisfied versus 20.3% in the control group.

> "overall teaching satisfaction differed significantly between the experimental and control groups (χ² = 15.356, P = 0.002). Students in the experimental group reported higher overall teaching satisfaction than those in the control group"
