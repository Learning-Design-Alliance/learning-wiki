q2 · i? · design · r2

Open-ended sentence task in the same 12-participant questionnaire: for the sentence mela–mangiare–fatto, "Only one par0cipant correctly interpreted the full sentence" while the other 11 recognized at least 2 of 3 signs.

"Most par0cipants interpreted the sequence as “Ho bevuto e mangiato” (I drank and ate), likely due to the confusion between the signs for “apple” and “water”. Only one par0cipant correctly interpreted the full sentence."
