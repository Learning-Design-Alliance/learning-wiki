Content analysis of coded Positive Feedback tags across the four cohorts found similar values in cohorts I, II and IV with a statistically significant increase in cohort III; the interpretation as crisis-period support is the authors' own and no effect size is printed.

> "the annotated PF tags were similar in cohorts I, II, and IV, with a statistically significant increase in cohort III. This peak may reflect reinforcement in the forms of sympathy and socioemotional support during a time of crisis."
