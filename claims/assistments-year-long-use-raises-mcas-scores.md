# Seventh graders using ASSISTments significantly outperformed comparison students on year-end MCAS scores after adjusting for prior-year scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` After ANCOVA adjustment for 6th-grade pre-test scores, treatment students' adjusted post-test mean exceeded the comparison group's by 3.3 percentage points, an effect size of d = .23. [→ Koedinger 2010](#koedinger-2010)

## Evidence

### Koedinger 2010

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 1,240 seventh graders in three treatment schools and one comparison school; a 2 × 2 ANCOVA with pre-test covariate showed a main effect for condition, F(1, 1235) = 12.3, p < .001. The adjusted difference of 3.3% corresponds to "a boost of 7 months of schooling" and d = .23.

> "the adjusted post-test means for all (total) students using ASSISTment (M = 51.7, SE = .45) is higher than the adjusted means for the comparison group (M = 48.4, SE = .82) with a difference of 3.3%"
