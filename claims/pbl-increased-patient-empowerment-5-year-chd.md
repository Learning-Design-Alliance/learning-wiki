## Evidence

### Andreae Christina 2023

Andreae Christina, Tingström Pia, Nilsson Staffan, Jaarsma Tiny, Karlsson Nadine, Kärner Köhler Anita. (2023). Does problem-based learning improve patient empowerment and cardiac risk factors in patients with coronary heart disease in a Swedish primary care setting? A long-term prospective, randomised, parallel single randomised trial (COR-PRIM). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2022-065230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified mixed-model analysis of the randomised COR-PRIM trial (n=72 PBL, n=71 control at 5-year follow-up), adjusted for age, sex, education and marital status. The PBL group's empowerment rose from baseline M 36.54 to 39.39 at 5 years; the article reports "it did not, however, change significantly over time in the control group".

> "SWE-CES increased significantly at time 3 (M=38.34±5.76, p=0.023) and time 5 (M=39.39±5.23, p<0.001) (table 2) compared with the baseline"
