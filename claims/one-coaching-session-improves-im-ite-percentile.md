## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Residents who met with a trained coach at least once had significantly greater IM-ITE percentile improvement (p = 0.022, Cohen's d = 0.691) than residents who did not participate in coaching. [→ Burtson Kathryn M. MD 2025](#burtson-kathryn-m-md-2025)

## Evidence

### Burtson Kathryn M. MD 2025

Burtson Kathryn M. MD, Wilson Kelsey R. DO, Kiger Michelle E. MD, Jung Eulho PhD, Hartzell Joshua D. MD, Meyer Holly PhD. (2025). Academic Coaching to Promote Self-Directed Learning in Graduate Medical Education. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-025-09424-7

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Controlled pre-test post-test comparison of 2022-2023 IM-ITE changes among 51 PGY1-PGY2 residents with paired data; coached (n=18) vs not coached (n=33) via independent-samples t-test. Percentile improvement "p = 0.022, ES = 0.691"; cohorts were not randomized.

> "Residents who met with a trained coach at least once had a significant improvement in performance on their IM-ITE percentile score compared to residents who did not partake in the coaching program, p = 0.022, ES = 0.691"
