## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among non-RCT participants, those who opted for small group tutorials had significantly higher overall test grades and sub-scores than those attending interactive seminars. [→ de Jong Zuzana 2010](#de-jong-zuzana-2010)

## Evidence

### de Jong Zuzana 2010

de Jong Zuzana, van Nies Jessica AB, Peters Sonja WM, Vink Sylvia, Dekker Friedo W, Scherpbier Albert. (2010). Interactive seminars or small group tutorials in preclinical medical education: results of a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-79

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational comparison of the 105 students free to choose format; 60 small-group choosers vs 30 seminar choosers sat the test. Overall grade mean 6.8 (SD 1.2) vs 6.2 (SD 1.3), mean Δ 0.60, P = 0.034; open orthopaedics sub-score P = 0.034.

> "The non-RCT participants who had chosen to attend small groups had significantly higher overall test grades and sub-scores compared to the non-RCT participants attending the interactive seminars."
