Why Teach Quantum In Your Own Time: The Values of Grassroots Organizations Involved in Quantum Technologies Education and Outreach. Originally published 2024. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey and interview analysis of grassroots quantum organizations reporting goals (N=20 organizations responded across survey and interviews). The results section reports that most organizations are dedicated to "quantum education" and reach learners at all levels; education and creating opportunities were the most frequent mentioned goals.

> "Most of the grassroots organizations (n=15) are dedicated to quantum education and aim to reach STEM and non-STEM students at all levels, academics, professionals, and the general public."
