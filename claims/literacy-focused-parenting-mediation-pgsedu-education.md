`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal SEM testing hypothesis 2 in MoBa trios; mediation estimated with 1,000 bootstraps. The literacy-focused model explained 5% of variance in educational performance; the positive-parenting mediation model explained 2%.

> "Maternal literacy‐focused parenting at 5 years was significantly associated with children's educational performance (Figure 3; β = 0.17, 95% CI [0.16, 0.19], p < .001) and significantly mediated the effect of children's PGSedu on their educational performance (a*b: β = 0.01, 95% CI [1 × 10−3, 0.01], p = .023)"
