`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the standardized Delaram et al. (2006) questionnaire, completed by students on a 5-point Likert scale, evaluated perceptions of educational objectives, instructor performance, interactions, environment, and monitoring. The total evaluation score was 98.96 out of 165 points.

> "The results indicated that the students assigned the highest scores to the dimensions of instructor performance and monitoring and evaluation, respectively. In contrast, the educational environment dimension received the lowest score in this assessment."
