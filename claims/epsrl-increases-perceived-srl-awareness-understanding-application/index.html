Evidence¶

Khiat 2022¶

Khiat, H., & Vogel, S. (2022). A self-regulated learning management system: Enhancing performance, motivation and reflection in learning. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 19(2), 43-59. https://doi.org/10.53761/1.19.2.4

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Within-group pre-post comparison of the 76 intervention students' perception of awareness of self-regulated learning, using Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks. The test showed "Z = -4.788, p = 0.000, dCohen = 1.314", a statistically and practically significant increase with effect size dCohen = 1.314.

"A Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test showed Z = -4.788, p = 0.000, dCohen = 1.314 and the mean rank before and after the use of ePSRL Management System are 25.64 and 40.12 respectively."

Khiat 2022 (2)¶

Khiat, H., & Vogel, S. (2022). A self-regulated learning management system: Enhancing performance, motivation and reflection in learning. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 19(2), 43-59. https://doi.org/10.53761/1.19.2.4

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks pre-post comparison of the same 76 intervention students' perceived understanding of self-regulated learning strategies, showing "Z = -4.181, p = 0.000, dCohen = 1.093" with mean ranks rising from 26.22 to 44.52 and effect size dCohen = 1.093.

"A Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test showed Z = -4.181, p = 0.000, dCohen = 1.093 and the mean rank before and after the use of ePSRL Management System are 26.22 and 44.52 respectively."

Khiat 2022 (3)¶

Khiat, H., & Vogel, S. (2022). A self-regulated learning management system: Enhancing performance, motivation and reflection in learning. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 19(2), 43-59. https://doi.org/10.53761/1.19.2.4

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks pre-post comparison of perceived likelihood of applying self-regulated learning strategies among the 76 intervention students, showing "Z = -2.832, p = 0.005, dCohen = 0.687", both statistically and practically significant with effect size dCohen = 0.687.

"A Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test showed Z = -2.832, p = 0.005, dCohen = 0.687 and the mean rank before and after the use of ePSRL Management System are 34.48 and 39.04 respectively."
