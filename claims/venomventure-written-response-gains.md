## Evidence

### Thanukos Anastasia 2026

Thanukos Anastasia, MacDonald Teresa, Quimby Claire, White Lisa D. (2026). VENOMventure, an Immersive Escape-Style Game, Teaches Families the Foundations of Phylogenetics. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0172

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Written answers to "What kinds of information do you think these diagrams show?" were coded 0–1 for referencing relationships among ancestors and descendants. Scores improved significantly (r = 0.291), though medians were 0.75 at both timepoints.

> "A related-samples Wilcoxon signed-rank test found a significant difference of small effect size between pre- (Mdn = 0.75, IQR = 0–0.75) and post-test scores (Mdn = 0.75, IQR = 0.75–0.75) in the direction consistent with learning (N = 325, Z = −5.248, p<0.001, r = 0.291)"
