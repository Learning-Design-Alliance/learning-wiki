## Evidence

### Caris MG 2018

Caris MG, Sikkens JJ, Kusurkar RA, van Agtmael MA. (2018). E-learning on antibiotic prescribing—the role of autonomous motivation in participation: a prospective cohort study. Journal of Antimicrobial Chemotherapy 73(8). https://doi.org/10.1093/jac/dky169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational multicentre cohort study in two academic and two teaching hospitals; logistic regression related RAM to e-learning participation, with a crude OR of "2.1 (95% CI 1.3–3.4)" per 10-point RAM increase. Residency in an academic hospital was a significant confounder, not an effect modifier.

> "Logistic regression analysis showed a significant association between RAM and participation in e-learning, with a crude OR of 2.1 (95% CI 1.3–3.4)."

### Caris MG 2018 (2)

Caris MG, Sikkens JJ, Kusurkar RA, van Agtmael MA. (2018). E-learning on antibiotic prescribing—the role of autonomous motivation in participation: a prospective cohort study. Journal of Antimicrobial Chemotherapy 73(8). https://doi.org/10.1093/jac/dky169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective cohort of 86 residents (74% female, mean age 30 years); overall participation was 58% (n = 50). Rates contrasted residents with "negative RAM (i.e. more controlled motivation)" against those with positive RAM. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size beyond odds ratios is printed for this contrast.

> "Participation was 41% in residents with negative RAM (i.e. more controlled motivation) and 62% in residents with positive RAM (i.e. more autonomous motivation)."
