### Wang Zhizhuo 2025

Wang Zhizhuo, Wu Peiyun, Shi Shaoyun, Zhang Weiwei, Lin Cheng. (2025). Experiences and perceptions of students in occupational therapy regarding the use of desktop virtual environments-based simulation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07495-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant account from the reflective-observation theme of the interview study, describing device-based access: "Once the Internet is connected to my device, such as iPad, I can access to the system", including stopping and resuming sessions at will.

> "Once the Internet is connected to my device, such as iPad, I can access to the system. It is much easier to operate and control the system. If I have to handle other businesses, I can stop it and resume when available."
