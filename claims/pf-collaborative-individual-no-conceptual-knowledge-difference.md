# Collaborative and individual problem solving in traditional PF do not differ in conceptual knowledge after instruction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Tenth-grade students in the collaborative PF condition did not outperform individually working students on the conceptual knowledge post-test; the total effect of condition was not statistically significant. [→ Charleen Brand 2023](#charleen-brand-2023)

## Evidence

### Charleen Brand 2023

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 137 tenth-grade students (PF COLL n = 87 analysed, of 105 initially assigned to 35 groups of three; PF IND n = 50) in the traditional variance PF setting. The mediation analysis found "both conditions did not differ in conceptual knowledge acquisition" (total effect c = 0.57, p = 0.241).

> "the total effect of the conditions on conceptual knowledge was not statistically significant, c = 0.57, SE = 0.48, t = 1.17, p = 0.241, p’ = 1.00, 95% CI [− 0.39, 1.53]. That is, both conditions did not differ in conceptual knowledge acquisition"
