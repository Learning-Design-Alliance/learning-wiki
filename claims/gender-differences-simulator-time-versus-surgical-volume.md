Gender comparison within the survey sample using t tests. "Males performed significantly more cataract surgeries in the OR compared to females" (p = 0.03) while females trained longer (p = 0.004); both genders rated VRS impact similarly (p = 0.9).

> "Males performed significantly more cataract surgeries in the OR compared to females (1809 +/- 4228 vs. 672 +/- 2063; p = 0.03). Females, on the other hand, spent longer training time in mean, even when adjusting to the simulator type (77.6 +/- 450.9 h for females vs. 36.5 +/- 43.3 h for males; p = 0.004)."
