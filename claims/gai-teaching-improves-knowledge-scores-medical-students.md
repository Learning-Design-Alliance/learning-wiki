## Evidence

### Ma Xingming 2026

Ma Xingming, Liu Xianting, Sun Haojie. (2026). The effectiveness of GAI-assisted teaching methods in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1813108

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 57 studies (2,902 control, 2,723 GAI-assisted participants) reported in the knowledge examination scores section. The pooled effect showed "a significant improvement in knowledge scores in the GAI-assisted teaching group" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 94%).

> "Compared with the control group, the pooled effect of the studies (SMD = 0.95, 95% CI: 0.72-1.18, p < 0.00001) showed a significant improvement in knowledge scores in the GAI-assisted teaching group."
