An interpretive editorial statement about the state of American education, tracing the vogue of the term motivation to the doctrine of interest and crediting it with refocusing teacher attention on "the learning process itself" and pupil attitude.

> "This emphasis on motivation has the advantage of forcing the attention of the teacher from the text book ,the course of study or the class lesson plans to the learning process itself and to the attitude of the pupil toward school tasks."
