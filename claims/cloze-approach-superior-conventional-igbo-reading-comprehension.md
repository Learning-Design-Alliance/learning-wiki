### Nneka Justina Eze 2015

Nneka Justina Eze. (2015). Effects of Cloze Instructional Approach on Senior Secondary School Students' Achievement in Igbo Language Reading Comprehension. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079076

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent control group study of two intact Senior Secondary Class 1 classes (47 cloze, 42 control), analyzed with ANCOVA at 95% confidence level. The cloze group's adjusted mean was 55.96 versus 41.64 for the conventional group, and the article reports a statistically significant difference in favour of the cloze instructional approach.

> "Since the calculated value ( 90.214) is greater than the critical value (3,92) at the alpha  level of 0.05 the researcher rejects the null hypo thesis and concludes that there is significant difference in t he mean reading comprehension scores of students ta ught Igbo Language reading using cloze instructional approach and those taught Igbo reading using the conventional method."
