## Evidence

### Boelens 2018

Boelens, R., Voet, M., & De Wever, B. (2018). The design of blended learning in response to student diversity in higher education: Instructors' views and use of differentiated instruction in blended learning. Computers & Education, 120, 197–212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.02.009

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-center comparison from the interview matrix analysis; center B instructors reported more strategies on average (M=4.17) than center A (M=2.86), and most center B instructors held a transformation profile.

> "the average number of reported strategies in center B (M=4.17, SD=1.47) was higher than that in center A (M=2.86, SD=1.75)."

### Boelens 2018 (2)

Boelens, R., Voet, M., & De Wever, B. (2018). The design of blended learning in response to student diversity in higher education: Instructors' views and use of differentiated instruction in blended learning. Computers & Education, 120, 197–212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.02.009

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Within-center A analysis by trajectory; "a trend toward a more deliberate design" appeared for instructors with over one year in the e-learning trajectory, while others held disregard or adaptation profiles.

> "There appears to be a trend toward a more deliberate design, described here as a transformation profile, for instructors working already more than one year  in the e -learning trajectory, while instructors working in the modular or non-modular trajectory often held a disregard or adaptation profile."
