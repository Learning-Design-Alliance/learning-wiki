Kristen Hengtgen and Hector Biaggi 2024¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

EdTrust analysis of DESE/NCES data comparing the SY 2021-22 public high school population by race with AP test takers by race; the brief names lack of preparation support and AP exam fees as reasons for the underrepresentation.

"Black and Latino students make up almost one-third of all public-school students, but they represent only 18% of AP test takers, often because students lack sufficient support to feel prepared to pass the test, or because AP exam fees can be a financial barrier."
