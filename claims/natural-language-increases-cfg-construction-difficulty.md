## Evidence

### Schmalstieg 2025

Schmalstieg, F., Schmellenkamp, M., Schwerter, J., & Zeume, T. (2025). Difficulty Generating Factors for Context-free Language Construction Assignments. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3702652.3744201](https://doi.org/10.1145/3702652.3744201)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fixed-effects panel regression on linked participant data from an interactive learning system study in an introductory theoretical computer science course. The natural language variation increased number of attempts (coefficient 1.83, SE 0.49, p<0.001), time spent (4.75 min, SE 1.15, p<0.001), and reduced first-attempt success probability (−0.10, SE 0.05, p<0.05).

> "In the Task groups 4–6, natural language formulations reduced performance compared to set notation: students made more attempts, took longer, and were less likely to succeed on their first try"
