Related Claims¶

  • Traditional unstructured/semi-structured admission interviews have low-to-moderate reliability (weighted average ra = .42), which limits their maximum attainable validity to rx'y' = .65
  • Interview validity tends to be higher for non-academic outcomes than for academic outcomes
  • Mechanical/formula-based selection decisions outperform holistic/clinical (human-judgement) decisions, with an average effect size advantage of +.11 SD across 161 studies
  • Oral exam scores correlate only modestly with written exams and OSCEs (r = 0.10–0.74), suggesting oral exams assess distinct skills
  • LoU Interview ratings correlated .65 with readers' ratings of written ethnographic protocols, which the author read as support for the interview's validity tempered by second-hand information — related
