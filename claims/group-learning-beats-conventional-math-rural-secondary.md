## Evidence

### Hossain 2012

Hossain, M.A., & Tarmizi, R.A. (2012). Gender-related effects of group learning on mathematics achievement among the rural secondary students. Egitim Arastirmalari-Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 47, 1-22. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057355

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental equivalent pre-post test control group design with 84 experimental and 84 control students over 15 weeks; independent-sample t-test on MAT post-test scores. The article reports the post-test difference was "significant [t (166) = 34.25, p < 0.05] in favor of the experimental group"; pre-test means were equivalent (25.26 vs 25.38).

> "Table 4 shows the experimental group scored post -test mean of 47.35 (SD = 2.64) which is higher than the control group with post -test mean score of 34.97 (SD = 1.99). The difference between these two post-test means was significant [t (166) = 34.25, p < 0.05] in favor of the experimental group"
