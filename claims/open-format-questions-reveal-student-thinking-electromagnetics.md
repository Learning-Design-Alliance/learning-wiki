## Evidence

### Frank V. Kowalski and Susan E. Kowalski 2014

Frank V. Kowalski and Susan E. Kowalski. (2014). Embedded Formative Assessment in the Undergraduate Engineering Classroom. [doi:10.1109/tale.2014.7062618](https://doi.org/10.1109/tale.2014.7062618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In-class open-format question in an intermediate electromagnetics course for third-year engineering physics majors; sixty students constructed digital-ink responses on Tablet PCs submitted via InkSurvey. The authors report "students’ lack of fluency in calculus" and ignored dot products as surprises revealed by the sketches.

> "One surprise from the graphical submissions is students’ lack of fluency in calculus, as shown by their inability to express the infinitesimal charge."
