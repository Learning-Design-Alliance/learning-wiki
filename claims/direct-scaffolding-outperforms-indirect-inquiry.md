## Evidence

### Wu 2016

Wu, H.-L., Weng, H.-L., & She, H.-C. (2016). Effects of scaffolds and scientific reasoning ability on web-based scientific inquiry. International Journal of Contemporary Educational Research, 3(1), 12-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1216018

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

2x2 MANOVA on online inquiry performance across four inquiry steps. Direct scaffolding group outperformed indirect on making hypotheses (mean difference = 1.13) and drawing conclusions (mean difference = 1.42). "partial η 2 = 0.15".

> "MANOVA results showed significant differences between the two scaffolding groups on the combined online scientific inquiry competencies, F (4, 70) = 3.13, p < 0.05, Pillai's trace = 0.15, partial η 2 = 0.15."
