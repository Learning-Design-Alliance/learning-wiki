Evidence¶

Patricia A Alexander 2016¶

Patricia A Alexander. (2016). Relational thinking and relational reasoning: harnessing the power of patterning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.4

q2 · i? · review · r2

The article's synthesis of its own training studies with children and adults and of Bayesian network findings: the trained component processes also underlie the other three forms, supporting broader trainability. No effect size is printed.

"there is now empirical evidence that the component processes of analogical reasoning (i.e., encode, infer, map, and apply 48) that my colleagues and I employed in the training of this form of relational reasoning 72 also underlie anomalous, antithetical, and antinomous reasoning."
