## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For non-professional participants, knowledge gain from the VR environment was significantly higher than from 2D drawings (p = 0.001 method 1; p = 0.0006 method 2). [→ M.A. Kraus 2022](#ma-kraus-2022)

## Evidence

### M.A. Kraus 2022

M.A. Kraus, R. Rust, M. Rietschel, D. Hall. (2022). Improved Perception of AEC Construction Details via Immersive Teaching in Virtual Reality. Preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.10617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.10617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment with 41 participants randomly split into a VR test group and a 2D-drawing control group, analysed with Welch's t-tests on two knowledge-gain measures. The article reports the differences are "especially signiﬁcant for non-professionals" with the printed p-values.

> "The diﬀerences are especially signiﬁcant for non-professionals, withp-values of 0.001 (method 1) and 0.0006 (method 2)."
