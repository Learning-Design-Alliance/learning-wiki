## Evidence

### Farahman Farrokhi 2012

Farahman Farrokhi, Mohammad Hassan Chehrazad. (2012). The Effects of Planned Focus on Form on Iranian EFL Learners' Oral Accuracy. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n1p70

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the control group's performance across the six story-retelling sessions in the quasi-experiment; the control group's session-to-session scores showed no significant improvement, supporting the claim that meaning-focused activity alone did not raise accuracy.

> "That the control group, which received no feedback, did not significantly improve their scores on the post tests is also significant in showing that purely meaning focused activities without any kind of form focused treatment do not lead the learners to improve their oral accuracy"
