Evidence¶

Guntur 2024¶

Guntur, M., & Purnomo, Y. W. (2024). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Regulated Learning Interventions Studies on Learning Outcomes in Online and Blended Environments. Online Learning Journal, 28(3). https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v28i3.4025

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Publication-bias assessment of the 15 included studies using a funnel plot and Egger's Regression Intercept test, analyzed in JASP. The authors report the funnel plot alone was inconclusive, so the Egger test was used; the resulting data "does not show bias".

"Egger's regression intercept test yielded a value of 0.691 for the intercept. These findings suggest that publication bias is unlikely to account for the observed consistency in impact size across studies."
