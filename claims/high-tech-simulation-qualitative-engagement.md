Content analysis of structured written reflection forms completed by twenty-five students after the simulations. Feedback was gathered via open-ended questions to maintain consistency across participants; no quantitative statistics are reported.

> "Participants in the high-tech group highlighted the value of hands-on engagement with advanced technologies, including VR, 3D printing, and EMG. In contrast, those in the low-tech group expressed mixed feelings, reporting less perceived impact on their functional skills"
