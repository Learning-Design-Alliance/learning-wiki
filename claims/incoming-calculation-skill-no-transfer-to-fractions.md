## Evidence

### Namkung 2016

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the study's structural equation model in 139 at-risk fourth graders, reported in the Discussion: incoming calculation skill (fall WRAT-4) significantly predicted whole-number calculations but not fraction calculations. The quoted interpretation is the authors' own; no effect size is printed for this null path.

> "However, it is surprising that incoming calculation skill did not predict fraction calculations given the hierarchical nature of calculations. This finding suggests that fraction calculations may be distinct from whole-number calculations and that fluency with whole-number calculations may not automatically transfer to fraction calculations."
