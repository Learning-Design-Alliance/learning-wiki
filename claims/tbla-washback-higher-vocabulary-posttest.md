## Evidence

### Hamzeh 2016

Alireza Hamzeh. (2016). Investigating the Washback Effects of Task-based Instruction on the Iranian EFL Learners' Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 9(12). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n12p16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-treatment-posttest study of 30 Iranian EFL learners (EG N=15, CG N=15) taught vocabulary for 12 ninety-minute sessions. The article reports a significant independent-samples t-test: "a significant difference in how EG outperformed on the test compared to their counterparts in the control group."
