Drawing on its findings about which students fare worst under sorting, the report states that "students with the weakest skills relative to their peers need close monitoring and support." The release offers no data beyond this recommendation-level statement.

"Students with the weakest skills relative to their peers need close monitoring and support, regardless of whether they have low- or high-achievement relative to a national comparison."
