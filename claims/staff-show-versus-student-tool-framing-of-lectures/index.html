Evidence¶

MacKay 2018¶

MacKay, J. R. D. (2018). Show and ‘Tool’: How lecture recording transforms staff and student perspectives on lectures in higher education. University of Edinburgh. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.05.019

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Grounded theory analysis of 13 unstructured staff interviews and free-text responses from 159 first-year student survey respondents. The analysis found a demarcation: staff summarised the lecture as 'it's to inspire them, it's to enthuse them', while 'not a single student within the survey discussed the ‘entertainment’ value of a lecture'.

"By contrast, not a single student within the survey discussed the ‘entertainment’ value of a lecture. Within the survey, students focussed on lectures as mainly a means to an end, and that end was often framed as examination."
