## Evidence

### Jin et al. 2026

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 LMMs with Bonferroni-corrected EMM comparisons (43 Japanese-speaking students; Blocked-effort trials blocked elaboration after an opt-in choice); completing elaboration added a benefit only to Sense Score, as "Chosen-effort Trials received significantly higher Sense Scores than Blocked-effort Trials".

> "Chosen-effort Trials received significantly higher Sense Scores than Blocked-effort Trials, whereas Chosen-effort and Blocked-effort Trials did not differ significantly in Interest or Like Scores."

### Jin et al. 2026 (2)

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Experiment 2 pairwise EMM comparisons: willingness to engage alone (Blocked-effort) raised all three evaluations relative to Low-effort trials, indicating engagement without executed effort still boosted evaluations.

> "Compared with Low-effort Trials, both Chosen-effort and Blocked-effort Trials received significantly higher ratings across Sense, Interest, and Like Scores."

### Jin et al. 2026 (3)

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 processing-time LMM showed Trial Type differences between all three trial types; the positive EMM differences place Chosen-effort above Blocked-effort above Low-effort in processing time.

> "The EMM difference was 44.825 s between Chosen-effort and Blocked-effort Trials, compared with 5.419 s between Blocked-effort and Low-effort Trials."
