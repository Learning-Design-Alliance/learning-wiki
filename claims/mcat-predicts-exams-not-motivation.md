## Evidence

### Bansal Sandeep 2025

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same longitudinal cohort; the article reports MCAT–examination effect sizes of rs = 0.45 (p < 0.001), rs = 0.36, and rs = 0.28, indicating "students who performed well on MCAT also did well in medical school assessments." MCAT and strength of motivation showed no significant relationship.

> "A positive and significant correlation was observed between MCAT scores and performance on examination sets 2 (rs = 0.45, p < 0.001), 3 (rs = 0.36, p = 0.01), and 4 (rs =0.28, p = 0.05), which indicates that students who performed well on MCAT also did well in medical school assessments."
