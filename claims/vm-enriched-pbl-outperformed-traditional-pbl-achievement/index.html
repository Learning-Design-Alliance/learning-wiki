Evidence¶

Çakıroğlu 2014¶

Çakıroğlu, Ü. (2014). Enriching project-based learning environments with virtual manipulatives: A comparative study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 55, 201-222. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2014.55.12

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test nonequivalent control group study with one experimental group (N=30) taught with VM-enriched ePBL and one comparison group (N=30) taught with traditional PBL. The independent t-test on post-test Achievement Test scores showed a significant difference favoring the EG, "t (58)= 2.88; p= 0.005".

"According to the independent t -test results, a statistically significant difference between the mean scores of post -tests of EG (M= 60.20; SD= 13.88) and CG (M= 50.60; SD= 11.78) students was found (t (58)= 2.88; p= 0.005)"

Çakıroğlu 2014 (2)¶

Çakıroğlu, Ü. (2014). Enriching project-based learning environments with virtual manipulatives: A comparative study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 55, 201-222. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2014.55.12

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The same quasi-experimental study's pre-test analysis found no significant difference between groups (EG M=40.4, SD=10.41; CG M=42.43, SD=11.37), which the author says "reflects the similar backgrounds of the students in the EG and in the CG before the intervention".

"The independent t -test results on pre -test scores (t(58)= -0.830; p= 0.410) show that there was no significant difference among the mean scores of the groups."
