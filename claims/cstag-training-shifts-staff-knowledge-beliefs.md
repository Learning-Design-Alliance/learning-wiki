### DePaoli 2025

DePaoli, J. L., & Loewe, S. B. (2025). Keeping schools safe? The research on behavioral threat assessments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/808.437

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report's synthesis of CSTAG implementation studies finds that staff self-reported knowledge and perspectives changed positively after standard 1-day trainings: staff "could more accurately assess a threat" and showed decreased support for zero-tolerance policies. No effect sizes are printed.

> "After school staff participated in their standard 1-day trainings, they could more accurately assess a threat, had decreased support for zero-tolerance policies, and had a better awareness of the goals of threat assessment"
