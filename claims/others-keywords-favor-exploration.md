Pablo Flores Romero, Kin Nok Nicholas Fung, Guang Rong & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Structured human-LLM interaction design reveals exploration and exploitation dynamics in higher education content generation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00332-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Keyword-source analysis across 25 structured GPT interactions. "138 out of 204, 68%" of used guide keywords were others' keywords; all participants included others' keywords, and all but five included at least one of their own.

> "most of the guide's keywords used by participants corresponded to other's keywords, not personally provided by the participant, instead of their own (138 out of 204, 68%). All participants included others' keywords in their prompts."
