`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likert-scale evaluation of ultrasound simulator training in the 343-participant survey (Fig. 6). "all respondent groups highly valued pathology training for clinical practice" with averages 6.31–6.54, while sufficiency-of-simulator-alone ratings were 2.17–2.69. Descriptive means only; no test statistic printed for these items.

> "all respondent groups highly valued pathology training for clinical practice (Fig. 6b), with average values from 6.31 (physicians) to 6.54 (PJ students). However, the view that training on the ultrasound simulator alone is sufficient to learn examination techniques (Fig. 6c) did not find significant support in any group (average values 2.17 to 2.69)."
