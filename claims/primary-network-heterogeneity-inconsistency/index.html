Tian Qi 2026¶

Tian Qi, Liu Yaowen, Wang Ziyu, Qin Gang. (2026). Comparative effects of physical education teaching models on motor skill development in school-aged children and adolescents: a systematic review and network meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1905122

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Design-by-treatment inconsistency assessment of the under-18 primary NMA. Restriction to under-18 school-PE studies "reduced heterogeneity and aligned the analysis with the stated population, but it did not establish transitivity or eliminate inconsistency."

"The 13-study under-18 primary network retained substantial heterogeneity (tau-squared = 0.1531; I-squared = 65.67%). The between-design component of the design-by-treatment decomposition was statistically significant (Q = 12.91, df = 2, p = 0.0016)."

Tian Qi, Liu Yaowen, Wang Ziyu, Qin Gang. (2026). Comparative effects of physical education teaching models on motor skill development in school-aged children and adolescents: a systematic review and network meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1905122

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Local node-splitting analysis (Table 7) of the primary network. "These linked discrepancies arose within the same sparse loop and should not be read as four independent failures," reinforcing that P-scores are conditional rather than definitive rankings.

"In the 13-study under-18 primary network, four of eight local comparisons were statistically significant (p = 0.039): Nonlinear-Control, Linear-Nonlinear, Linear-TGfU, and TGfU-Traditional."
