`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Interpretive conclusion of the scoping review's narrative synthesis, which used no pooled statistics. The review notes studies "used heterogeneous instruments and many relied on subjective or self-perceived outcomes," and that satisfaction alone does not demonstrate psychomotor competence or safe clinical transfer.

> "favorable perception should be interpreted as learner acceptance, not as proof of competence or clinical transfer"
