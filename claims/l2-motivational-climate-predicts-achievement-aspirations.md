## Evidence

### David W. Putwain 2026

David W. Putwain, Andrea Mallaburn, Hanna Gaspard. (2026). Student perceptions of teacher messages about the value of science in the classroom. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102365. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Class-level (L2) paths from the same ML-SEMs of 1591 students in 62 science classes, predicting T4 class-average achievement and aspirations from T3 class-average beliefs with autoregressive controls. The article reports large effect sizes in prose; no standardized coefficient for these paths appears in the quoted text.

> "classes with higher average utility value (i.e., the L2 portion of the model) showed higher achievement beyond prior levels, with a large effect size; classes with higher average expectancy and utility value, and lower effort cost, showed higher aspirations, beyond prior levels, with large effect sizes."
