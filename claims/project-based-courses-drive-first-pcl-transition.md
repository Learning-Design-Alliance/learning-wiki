Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). Physics computational literacy: Programming, modeling, and collaboration at the journeyman level. Physical Review Physics Education Research 22, 010103. https://doi.org/10.1103/6fny-yzyb

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview-based results section on the novice-to-journeyman transition. Students reported a consensus that introductory courses gave a foundation but that project-based courses from the third semester onward provided skills more applicable to research; the analysis states "the social elements of these project-based courses were crucial". One student called AST2000 a turning point.

> "First, the social elements of these project-based courses were crucial, as they provided students with opportunities to work with and learn from their peers"
