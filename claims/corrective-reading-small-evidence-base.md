# The evidence base for Corrective Reading in grades K–3 is small: one of 25 reviewed studies met WWC standards, and no studies addressed general reading achievement

## Subclaims

The WWC's review of the evidence base reports that 24 studies failed evidence screens (e.g., inappropriate grade samples, no comparison group, or combined interventions), leaving a single RCT with 79 third-grade students; Appendix A5 rates extent of evidence as small in each domain.

> "Twenty-five studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of Corrective Reading. One study (Torgesen et al., 2006) was a randomized controlled trial that met WWC evidence standards. The remaining 24 studies did not meet evidence screens."
