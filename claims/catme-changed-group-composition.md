Navlekar Anisha S., Theobald Elli J., Griffith Ken, Limeri Lisa B. (2025). Strategically creating maximally heterogeneous lab groups did not improve group performance in an introductory biology lab class. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323799

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check comparing group structures (Table 2) across formation strategies in both semesters confirmed "using CATME created groups that were noticeably different in composition from when groups were assigned at random". Descriptive group counts only.

> "In both semesters, the sections where groups were created to be maximally heterogeneous had fewer homogenous groups and more groups that were mixed and with isolated URM students or isolated women."
