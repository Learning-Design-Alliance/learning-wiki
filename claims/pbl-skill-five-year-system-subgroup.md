## Evidence

### Wang 2016

Wang, J., Xu, Y., Liu, X., Xiong, W., Xie, J., & Zhao, J. (2016). Assessing the effectiveness of problem-based learning in physical diagnostics education in China: a meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5093758/

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Stratified random-effects analysis pooling 5 studies whose target population were all five-year system students (I2 = 92%) found "a statistically significant difference was also observed in skill scores (SMD = 1.08, 95%CI [0.47, 1.69], P = 0.0006) in favor of PBL".
