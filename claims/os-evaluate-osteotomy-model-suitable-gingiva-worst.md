### Schaffrath K 2025

Schaffrath K, Ooms M, Bock A, Katz MS, Hölzle F, Modabber A. (2025). Improvement of surgical skills in students using a newly developed 3D printed osteotomy model of a partially retained wisdom tooth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08394-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of the model by five oral surgeons using a 10-point Likert questionnaire on structure, realistic feeling and exercise quality. The article reports "the worst results were obtained for the gingiva"; Table 1 gives gingiva median 3 (5) versus osteotomy tooth 9 (2) and 10 (1).

> "Oral surgeons evaluated the gingiva, neighbored tooth, osteotomy tooth, bone, and fixation in terms of structure and realistic feeling. The best results were obtained for teeth and bone, while the worst results were obtained for the gingiva (Table 1)."
