## Evidence

### Ene 2019

Ene, E., & Webb, R. C. (2019). A tale of two test setups: The effect of randomizing a popular conceptual survey in electricity and magnetism. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1908.09221](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1908.09221)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of BEMA responses from 3480 students at TAMU administered on WebAssign, where the WebAssign team randomized the order of the answer-options for nine BEMA questions. The article reports this null finding for valid times-in-testing; equivalence was not tested.

> "Our study did not detect any significant effect of the option randomization alone on the BEMA scores, for valid times-in-testing."
