## Evidence

### Dreyer Tom 2025

Dreyer Tom, Papadopoulos Symeon, Wiesner Rudolf, Karay Yassin. (2025). Classroom teaching versus online teaching in physiology practical course – does this lead to different examination results? GMS Journal for Medical Education, 42(1), Doc8. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2025-42/zma001732.shtml

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis across the 198 physiology course students relating each student's number of face-to-face attendances (mean 8.6, SD 1.7) to total exam points. The article reports "a value of r=0.236" with p<.001, described as a small positive correlation.

> "The Spearman correlation between the number of FtF participants and the test score achieved is significant at the 0.01 level (2-sided) (p<.001). The Spearman's correlation test achieves a value of r=0.236."
