## Evidence

### Lehto Tuomas MSc 2011

Lehto Tuomas MSc, Oinas-Kukkonen Harri PhD. (2011). Persuasive Features in Web-Based Alcohol and Smoking Interventions: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3222186/

`q3 · i1` · `review · r2`

RCT among 3971 smokers in the UK and Republic of Ireland who purchased a nicotine patch and connected to a website, comparing a Web-based tailored behavioral smoking cessation program (CQ Plan) to a nontailored condition. The review reports "Continuous abstinence rates at 6 weeks were 29.0% in the tailored condition vs 23.9% in the nontailored condition (OR = 1.30, P < .001)" and at 12 weeks "22.8% versus 18.1%, respectively (OR = 1.34, P < .001)".
