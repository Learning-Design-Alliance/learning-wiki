## Evidence

### Wodarz M.N. 2025

Wodarz M.N., Komarova N.L., Ma T. (2025). The functional form of the association between K-12 student performance and household income in U.S. school districts. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0329296

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

State-by-state BIC model comparison of district-level performance versus income data from 42 states, reported in Results. The article states the linear function was best "in about half of the states" and the saturating model in the other half; the Fig 4(b) caption adds that "the nonlinear model was selected in 90 out of 168 cases."

> "When comparing functions f1(x) and f2(x) with each other, it turned out that in about half of the states, the linear function (Eq 2) is the best model by the BIC, while the nonlinear model with saturation (Eq 1) is chosen to be the best in the other half of the states"
