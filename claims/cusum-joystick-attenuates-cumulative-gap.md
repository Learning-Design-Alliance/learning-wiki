`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

CUSUM analysis of operation time over case sequence using expert-based target times (Fig. 5A–I), across the overall cohort and subgroups. The authors describe CUSUM as descriptive and sensitive to the selected target value; no effect size printed.

> "Overall, the CUSUM analysis supported the findings of the linear regression analysis and further demonstrated that joystick-controlled scope use was associated with attenuation of the cumulative operation time gap between expert and beginner scopists, particularly when compared with the conventional controller."
