### O.T.A. Potters 2023

O.T.A. Potters, T.J.P. van Schijndel, S. Jak, J. Voogt. (2023). Two decades of research on children's creativity development during primary education in relation to task characteristics. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100532

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic synthesis within the systematic review of 52 studies, analyzing tasks by response domain. The review reports weaker patterns "when tasks with a combination of figural and verbal responses were used". No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Less pronounced developmental patterns were found for the originality subdimension (open-ended fragmenting taxonomy quadrant), and for general creativity and some subdimensions when tasks with a combination of figural and verbal responses were used."
