Gregorio Orlando 2026¶

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-experiment Likert (1-5) questionnaires in the n=36 study showed AIfred "scored higher than ChatGPT in perceived learning support (4.5/5 vs. 3.4/5;p < .001)"; innovation and satisfaction also favored AIfred. Only p-values and means are printed.

Gregorio Orlando 2026 (2)¶

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Self-reported cognitive demand "trended higher with AIfred (3.4/5 vs. 2.9/5;p=.03)", consistent with observed engagement; perceived productivity did not differ (4.1/5 vs. 4.0/5, p=.61). No effect sizes are printed.
