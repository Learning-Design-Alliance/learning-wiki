## Evidence

### Meng Jing 2025

Meng Jing, Wang Li, Jin Danni, Li Zihan, Tao Qingqing, Ying Lan. (2025). Situational simulation teaching can effectively enhance the clinical thinking ability of residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08248-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

EPA assessment by two blinded, standardized instructors within 2 weeks post-course in the same RCT (Table 7). For example, treatment decision-making scored 4.49 ± 0.63 versus 3.50 ± 0.76 (t = 7.779, P < 0.001); compliance rates ranged from 89.47% to 100% in the intervention group. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The intervention group showed significantly higher EPAs scores and compliance rates in the dimensions of history-taking, physical examination, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment decisions, recognition and management of critical illness, doctor-patient communication, teamwork, humanistic care, and self-directed learning ability compared to the control group, with statistically significant differences (P < 0.001)"
