## Subclaims
`q2 i?` UCAT scores for cardiac (p = 0.01) and lung (p = 0.001) applications improved most consistently; first trimester ultrasound showed the least improvement (p = 0.078, not significant). [→ Buhumaid R 2025](#buhumaid-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Buhumaid R 2025

Buhumaid R, Kilian P, Zidan MA, Salman H, AlSharhan R, Khansaheb H, Zary N. (2025). From novice to proficient: a longitudinal study of POCUS skill development through a hybrid certification program in the United Arab Emirates. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07138-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA per application on UCAT scores (Table 4) from nine participants. Cardiac and lung showed the most consistent improvement; first trimester's change did not reach significance.

> "the repeated measures of the UCAT scores for the first trimester ultrasound application showed the least improvement (p = 0.078). This aligns with the findings on confidence and highlights a potential area for curriculum enhancement."
