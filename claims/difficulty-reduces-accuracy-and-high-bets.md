## Evidence

### Vo 2014

Vo, V. Y., Li, R., Kornell, N., Pouget, A., & Cantlon, J. F. (2014). Young Children Bet On Their Numerical Skills: Metacognition in the Numerical Domain. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614538458

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Figure 3 and the accompanying text report that as discrimination difficulty increased, both accuracy and the proportion of high bets decreased across conditions, with main effects of difficulty on accuracy (F(2, 42) = 274.51, p < .001) and on risk (F(2, 42) = 30.30, p < .001).

> "Accuracy and the proportion of high bets decreased with increasing difficulty for both conditions (main effect of difficulty across conditions: accuracy, F(2, 42) = 274.51, p < .001;risk, F(2, 42) = 30.30, p < .001)."
