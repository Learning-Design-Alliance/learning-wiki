# In Experiment 1, the total number of concepts used in pathophysiological case explanations shows an inverted U-shaped relation with level of medical expertise

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The total number of concepts in case explanations first increases and then decreases across expertise levels, replicating the encapsulation hypothesis. [→ Boshuizen 1995](#boshuizen-1995)

## Evidence

### Boshuizen 1995

Boshuizen, H. P. A., Schmidt, H. G., Custers, E. J. F. M., & van de Wiel, M. W. (1995). Knowledge Development and Restructuring in the Domain of Medicine: The Role of Theory and Practice. Learning and Instruction. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/learning-and-instruction

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 4-level (second-, fourth-, sixth-year students, internists) experiment with two internal-medicine cases and three reading-time conditions; explanations schematised as semantic networks. The article reports the inverted U-shaped relationship as "(F(3. 84)=8.61. p=O.OOOl)".

> "Like in the original study, the total number of concepts had an inverted U-shaped relationship with level of expertise (F(3. 84)=8.61. p=O.OOOl)."
