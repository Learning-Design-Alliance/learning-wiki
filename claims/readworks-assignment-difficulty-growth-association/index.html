Tare 2019¶

Tare, M. & Shell, A. R. (2019). Designing for learner variability: Examining the impact of research-based edtech in the classroom. Washington, DC: Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/82839c44-adb1-400c-b422-9bfbbca65985

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of ReadWorks assignment logs in a rural southern school district's elementary schools, fall 2018. The difference between student and assignment Lexiles was estimated with a mixed-effect linear model with random intercepts by student and grade (t = 4.7, p < .01; 95% CI of 215-535 points). Students completed about six assignments (median) over five months.

"students tended to be assigned articles that have a higher difficulty (based on Lexile level) than the students' STAR Lexile level, such that on average, the Lexile level of assignments was around 375 points higher than the Lexile level of the student"

Tare 2019 (2)¶

Tare, M. & Shell, A. R. (2019). Designing for learner variability: Examining the impact of research-based edtech in the classroom. Washington, DC: Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/82839c44-adb1-400c-b422-9bfbbca65985

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Mixed-effects linear model of STAR Lexile growth including fixed effects of free lunch status and beginning Lexile and random teacher effects; significant relationships for number of assignments (t = 2.8, df = 123.8, p < .01), difficulty (t = 3.7, df = 149.8, p < .001), and multiple choice accuracy (t = 7.3, df = 47.6, p < .001). Students beginning at Lexile BR200 or lower were excluded, for a final N of 1,099 students.

"greater STAR score growth was positively related to completing more assignments on ReadWorks, completing harder assignments on ReadWorks, and having a higher accuracy on ReadWorks multiple choice comprehension questions"
