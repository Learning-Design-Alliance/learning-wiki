Patiño Hernández JF 2026¶

Patiño Hernández JF, Chiappe A and Ortega González E (2026). From digital competence to AI-responsive pedagogy: a scoping review of technology-supported teacher education. Front. Educ. 11:1949296. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1949296

q2 · i? · review · r2

Descriptive frequency analysis of the review's RQ1/RQ2 subset (N = 155) in the Results section. The review reports that competence frameworks were the most frequent strategy, with the quote showing "n = 72; 46.5%". The authors caution frequencies "should not be interpreted as prevalence estimates for the wider teacher education literature".

"Eight primary categories were identified: frameworks and models for digital competence and ICT integration (n = 72; 46.5%), digital modalities and courses (n = 20; 12.9%), specific digital resources and environments (n = 18; 11.6%)"
