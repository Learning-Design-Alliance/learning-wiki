DTI evidence shows protracted development of the uncinate fasciculus and cingulum bundle into adulthood, age-invariant fornix integrity between age 5 and young adulthood, and an association between uncinate fractional anisotropy and better recall in 9- to 15-year-olds. → Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A 2012
