## Evidence

### Arthur I. Gates 1921

Arthur I. Gates. (1921). An Experimental and Statistical Study of Reading and Reading tests (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0064253

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the conclusions on rate tests, the article reports mean grade correlations with the rate criteria: Brown 0.82, Monroe 0.87, Directions 0.79, Courtis 0.76, and concludes "mechanically controls comprehension" tests are best, assuming criterion validity.

> "That a rate test which provides no control of compre- hension (Courtis) gives less reliable results than one which provides a partial control by requiring a repro- duction following the reading (Brown)."
