## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Christophe Casseau 2023

Christophe Casseau, Jean-Rémy Falleri, Thomas Degueule and Xavier Blanc. (2023). MOON: Assisting Students in Completing Educational Notebook Scenarios. [doi:10.1109/vl-hcc57772.2023.00026](https://doi.org/10.1109/vl-hcc57772.2023.00026)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correctness was graded on the French [0-20] scale for the 21 notebooks of the controlled experiment. The article reports "the experimental group has a mean of 14.3 with a standard deviation of 3.6, while the control group has a mean of 12.4 with a standard deviation of 4.4", with a small effect size favoring MOON.

> "For Correctness, the Mann-Whitney U test yields a p-value p = 0.29 and an effect size rbc = −0.292929. Thus, we cannot reject the null hypothesis that the grades are the same between the groups."
