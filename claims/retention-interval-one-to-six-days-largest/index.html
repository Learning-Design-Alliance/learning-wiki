Evidence¶

Adesope 2017¶

Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis of retention interval (Table 4): <1 day k = 117 (g = 0.56), 1–6 days k = 58 (g = 0.82), 7–42 days k = 52 (g = 0.69), mixed k = 35 (g = 0.51). Post hoc analysis confirmed 1–6 days significantly exceeded less than 1 day.

"Results also show that the highest weighted effect size was obtained with a time lag of 1 to 6 days (g = 0.82; k = 58). The between-levels difference was statistically significant, QΒ(4) = 69.07, p < .001."
