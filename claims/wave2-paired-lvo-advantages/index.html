Evidence¶

Pickering 2011¶

Pickering, L. E., & Walsh, E. J. (2011). Using Videoconferencing Technology to Enhance Classroom Observation Methodology for the Instruction of Preservice Early Childhood Professionals. Journal of Digital Learning in Teacher Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ918901

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-tests in Wave 2, where all 40 students (Section 1 n = 17; Section 2 n = 23) completed two OSOs and two LVOs and rated both methods. Table 3 shows, e.g., distracting to children: OSO M = 3.67 vs LVO M = 2.81, t = 6.96, p = .000.

"Students also reported that the LVO methodology was less distracting for the children and more convenient in terms of both time and travel."
