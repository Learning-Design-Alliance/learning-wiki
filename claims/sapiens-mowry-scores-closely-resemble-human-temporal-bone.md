## Evidence

### Iannella G 2025

Iannella G, Pace A, Greco A, De Virgilio A, Bellizzi MG, Croce E, Lechien JR, Maniaci A, Cocuzza S, Gioacchini FM, Re M, Collettini A, Gatti L, Perrone T, Simon F, Gargula S, Magliulo G. (2025). The SAPIENS 3D-printed temporal bone model: a real tool for advanced otologic surgery education. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology 282(6):3051–3063. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-024-09199-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire evaluation by ENT residents after dissecting both SAPIENS models. The Mowry questionnaire uses a 4-point Likert scale with a maximum score of 61, where a total above 40 indicates a model closely resembling human temporal bone. Residents rated the models "53.2 for the transparent TB model and 55.4 for the opaque TB model". Notable sub-scores included sigmoid sinus (4/4 and 3.8/4) and facial nerve (4/4 and 3.8/4).

> "The average total score on the Mowry questionnaire was 53.2 for the transparent TB model and 55.4 for the opaque TB model. These scores indicate that the 3D-printed models closely resemble the human TB in terms of anatomy and dissection."
