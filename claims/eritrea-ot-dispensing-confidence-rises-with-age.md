## Evidence

### Gyawali Rajendra 2019

Gyawali Rajendra, Bhayal Bharat Kumar. (2019). Practice scope and job confidence of two-year trained optometry technicians in Eritrea. BMC Medical Education, 19, 303. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1738-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression from the 2017 survey of 70 OTs relating age, sex, work Zoba and job duration to confidence on each job skill. The quote prints the age odds ratios for dispensing skills; the article notes p < 0.05 for all and no other significant associations.

> "The confidence of the OTs in different dispensing skills increased significantly with increasing age (single vision dispensing: odds ratio (OR) = 1.4, 95% confidence interval (CI):1.1, 1.9; complex single vision dispending: OR = 1.3, 95% CI:1.1, 1.7"
