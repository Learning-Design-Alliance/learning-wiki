q3 · i? · review · r3

Results table entry for Torbeyns et al. (2017), an RCT in which the intervention group used bike desks 4 class hours/week for 22 weeks. Energy expenditure with bike desk access was "128.5 ± 34.7 kcal.h−1 vs. 94.6 ± 16.7 kcal.h−1 (p< 0.001)", reported as +36%. No standardized effect size is printed.

"EE: IG EE access bike desks (+36%) > EE normal hours IG bike desks vs. EE IG normal hours: 128.5 ± 34.7 kcal.h−1 vs. 94.6 ± 16.7 kcal.h−1 (p< 0.001)"

  • Studying on a stationary bike (PAL) has no significant effect on university students' final course grades compared to a standard desk — related
  • Upright active desks reduce classroom sitting time and increase standing time in primary-school children — related
