Amber Laxton, Mike Berlin, Patti Constantakis, Ph.D. (2017). Accelerating Change: A Guide to the Adult Learning Ed-Tech Market. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d14be2b4-b279-4e5a-8e24-81eec9e16509

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The Market Overview section reports spending figures: "$145 per K-12 student versus $50 per adult learner on technology," noting the U.S. spends $10 billion yearly on adult learning services with only $200 million on digital materials. The guide itself cautions this is not an apples-to-apples comparison.

> "Based on the number of students served in each area, the U.S. conservatively spends approximately $145 per K-12 student versus $50 per adult learner on technology. If every low-skilled adult was enrolled in an adult education program, then technology spending would drop to $5 per learner."
