q2 · i? · associational · r1

Secondhand report of an ETS study of Praxis I results (2005–2009): "considerable diferences in pass rates between white and African American candidates" across reading, writing, and math, as listed descriptively; no standardized effect size is printed.

"between 2005 and 2009, there were considerable diferences in pass rates between white and African American candidates: 82 percent compared to 41 percent for reading; 80 percent compared to 44 percent for writing; and 78 percent compared to 37 percent for math"
