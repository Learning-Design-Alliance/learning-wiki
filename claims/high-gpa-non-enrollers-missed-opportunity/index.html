Nagaoka 2021¶

Nagaoka, J., Lee, J.S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2021). Navigating the maze: Understanding CPS graduates' paths through college. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/navigating-the-maze

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of non-enrollers among the approximately 63,000 tracked CPS graduates. The study reports "Ten percent" of non-enrollers had "an average high school GPA above 3.0" and characterizes this as a missed opportunity.

"Ten percent of students who did not enroll in college within six years had an average high school GPA above 3.0—a missed opportunity for high-achieving students."
