## Evidence

### Snoder 2017

Snoder, P. (2017). Improving English Learners’ Productive Collocation Knowledge: The Effects of Involvement Load, Spacing, and Intentionality. TESL Canada Journal, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1277

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects comparison with 45 participants (25 spaced, 20 intensive); means 7.72 vs 5.85. Quote fragment: "not statistically significant". Equivalence was not tested.

> "The result of the t-test was not statistically significant: t (43): 1.911, p = 0.063. This result indicates that, in this study, spacing the participants’ three exposures to the target collocations was not significantly more effective in promoting learning gains than when the target collocations were exposed to them intensively."
