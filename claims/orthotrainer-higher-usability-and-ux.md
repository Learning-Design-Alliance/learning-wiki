## Evidence

### Lang Norbert Alexander 2026

Lang Norbert Alexander, Bell Laura, Backes Lorena, Lang Franziska Alina, Wolf Michael, Mücke Katharina, Kruse Teresa, Becker Kathrin, Knaup Isabel, Lemos Martin. (2026). Utilising a web-based e-learning platform for orthodontic case analysis training: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09968-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

UEQ analysis across all dimensions showed significant differences favouring the digital condition, with no significant main effect of time across any dimension.

> "Pragmatic, hedonic and overall quality were always rated higher for the digital compared to the analogue application (pragmatic quality: β = 2.64, 95% CI [2.04, 3.25], p < 0.001; hedonic quality: β = 2.65, 95% CI [1.92, 3.39], p < 0.001; overall quality: β = 2.65, 95% CI [2.05, 3.25], p < 0.001)."
