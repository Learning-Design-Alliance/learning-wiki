Syllabus-based analysis of the complete quantum curricula of 56 institutions (129 syllabi), counting quantum-related lecture minutes. The article reports mean 63.5 hours, SD 28.1, median 60.3, mode 53.8, minimum 12, maximum 121.7 hours.

> "The mean of the 56 institutions is 63.5 hours with a standard deviation of 28.1 hours. The median was 60.3 hours, and the mode was 53.8 hours. The minimum was 12 hours, while the maximum was 121.7 hours."
