## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Under the information-before (IB) timing condition, pairs discussing online (OD) outperformed pairs discussing in person (PD) on knowledge construction. [→ Noroozi 2012](#noroozi-2012)

## Evidence

### Noroozi 2012

Noroozi, O., Busstra, M. C., Mulder, M., Biemans, H. J. A., Tobi, H., Geelen, A., van't Veer, P., Chizari, M. (2012). Online discussion compensates for suboptimal timing of supportive information presentation in a digitally supported learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev (2012) 60:193-221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9217-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reported contrast from the sequential experiment's second phase: within the IB timing condition, students randomly assigned to online discussion using the Drewlite chat tool showed better knowledge construction on test 2 than pairs discussing face-to-face in front of a shared computer. The detailed post-hoc statistics appear in the portion of the Results beyond the supplied text.

> "When supportive information had been presented before each learning task cluster, online discussion led to better results than personal discussion."
