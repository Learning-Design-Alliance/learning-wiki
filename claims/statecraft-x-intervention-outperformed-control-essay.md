# Students in the Statecraft X intervention class outperformed control class students on all four essay criteria in a summative writing task

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On all four essay criteria (multiple viewpoints, proposed solutions, disposition, persuasiveness), intervention students scored significantly higher than control students, with t-tests rejecting equality of means for each criterion. [→ Chee 2013](#chee-2013)

## Evidence

### Chee 2013

Chee, Y.S., Mehrotra, S. and Liu, Q. (2013). Effective Game Based Citizenship Education in the Age of new Media. The Electronic Journal of e-Learning, Vol 11 Issue 1, pp16-28. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1012864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of a 42-student intervention class against a 40-student control class taught by traditional instruction, assessed on a common 40-minute summative essay. The article reports t-tests rejecting equality of means on each criterion; Table 1 prints t values of 6.83, 5.48, 5.22, and 5.17 with p < .001.

> "Table 1 shows that, on all four essay criteria, students from the intervention group outperformed those in the control group. Their mean rubric scores, coded from 1 (lowest) to 4 (highest) based on the level of attainment on each criterion, were higher than those of the control students."
