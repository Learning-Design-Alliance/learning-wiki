### Rasikawati 2019

Ira Rasikawati (2019). Corpus-Based Data-Driven Learning to Augment L2 Students’ Vocabulary Repertoire. International Dialogues on Education, 6(2), 83-98. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1245249

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this single comparison study of 46 first-year students in Macedonia, in which the experimental group used an online concordancer. The review attributes the finding that these students "learned more collocations" and scored better on the verb-adverb collocation test; no effect size is printed.

> "Daskalovska (2015) compared the effectiveness of corpus -based activities and traditional activities for learning collocations among 46 first -year students in a university in the Republic of Macedonia. The study found that students in the experimental group, who used the online concordancer learned more collocations and showed better results on the test that measured their knowledge of verb-adverb collocations."
