Thematic finding from reflective essays and focus group interviews in the case study. Participants documented classroom events and turned to ChatGPT for clarification; the article reports the ability to return and adjust prompts enabled exploring perspectives before decisions, mentioned by four participants.

"Several participants reported that their interactions with ChatGPT followed a pattern of inquiry-based learning."
