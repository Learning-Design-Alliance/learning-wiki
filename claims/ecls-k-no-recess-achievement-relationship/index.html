Evidence¶

Howie Erin K. 2023¶

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional ECLS-K analysis (Yesil Dagli, 2012) of 3,951 kindergartners, 79% with daily recess, found no relationship between recess frequency or duration separately and reading scores, though combinations of frequency and duration differed in reading scores. No effect size printed.

"No relationship between recess frequency and recess duration separately with reading scores. Different combinations of recess frequency and duration resulted in higher reading scores."

Howie Erin K. 2023 (2)¶

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

q2 · i? · review · r3

Longitudinal ECLS-K analysis (Dills, 2011) from kindergarten through 5th grade, with average recess falling from 133.4 to 89.4 minutes per week, found no effect of recess on reading or math scores. No effect size printed.

"No effect of recess on reading or math scores."
