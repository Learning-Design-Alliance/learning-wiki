# Chronic HPA axis activation via CRH and glucocorticoids is proposed to partially mediate the neural effects of early life stress

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Animal experiments show blocking CRH receptors improved memory performance and prevented dendritic atrophy in the hippocampus, and restoring glucocorticoid receptor mRNA deficits or blocking glucocorticoid receptors reversed anxiety, social, and freezing behavior changes. `q2 i?` In humans, epigenetic changes in glucocorticoids are associated with hippocampal changes, psychopathology symptoms, and altered learning, and abnormal HPA responsivity is often observed after varied early life stress experiences, varying with age.
