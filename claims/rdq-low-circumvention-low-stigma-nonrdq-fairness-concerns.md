### Krauthausen M 2026

Krauthausen M, Wingender L, Deyerl V, Hagen PE, Leutritz T, Simmenroth A. (2026). Rural admission quota in Germany: students’ attitudes, challenges and the need for support programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08776-w

Descriptive Likert results among RDQ students: buying out (M = 2.34), legal action (M = 2.17), and planning to work in a different field after obligations (M = 2.18) on a 1–5 scale; avoiding disclosure of quota admission was low (M = 1.17).

> "Intentions to circumvent or withdraw from the contractual rural service requirements were generally low."
