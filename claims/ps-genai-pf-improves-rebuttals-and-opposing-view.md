## Evidence

### Chang 2026

Chang, Y., Liu, Q., Lu, Y. & Miao, E. (2026). Leveraging generative AI to facilitate peer feedback in collaborative argumentation learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00586-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H test of structural-complexity gains across the three conditions in the same 12-group study. The rebuttal data and warrant element differed significantly ("H = 8.41, p = 0.015"), with pairwise PS-GenAI PF > PF, p = 0.016; no standardised effect size is printed in the pairwise sentence.

> "there was a significant difference between the scores of the three groups on the rebuttal data and warrant (H = 8.41, p = 0.015 < 0.05, ε2 = 0.14), with the PS-GenAI PF group (Mean = 0.49, SD = 0.80) and the GenAI-PF group (Mean = 0.49, SD = 1.38) scored higher than the PF group"

### Chang 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`
