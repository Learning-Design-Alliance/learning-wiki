## Evidence

### F. Rogosch 2026

F. Rogosch, A. Schrader. (2026). AI-Generated Interactive Fiction for Educational Use: A Pilot Study of Perceived Comprehensibility, Coherence, and Engagement. Proceedings of EDULEARN26 Conference. https://doi.org/10.21125/edulearn.2026.1075

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory correlation analysis in the pilot (N = 22) linking questionnaire sub-dimensions to gameplay telemetry. Correlations were "all small-to-moderate (|ρ| ≤ 0.38)" and none significant; the strongest was engagement with extra clicks (ρ = -0.38, p = 0.08).

> "Spearman rank correlations between each sub -dimension and the gameplay metrics (duration, extra clicks, first-try-correct rate, total wrong clicks) were all small-to-moderate (|ρ| ≤ 0.38) and none reached the uncorrected p < 0.05 threshold."
