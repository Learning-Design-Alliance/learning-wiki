## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2018

Shulruf Boaz, Damodaran Arvin, Jones Phil, Kennedy Sean, Mangos George, O'Sullivan Anthony J., Rhee Joel, Taylor Silas, Velan Gary, Harris Peter. (2018). Enhancing the defensibility of examiners' marks in high stake OSCEs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1112-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors report discussions with student representatives after the 2016 OSCE, plus a convenience-sample survey of experienced examiners from different disciplines who reported the Borderline grade to be clear and preferred to the previous grading system; these are the authors' own accounts of acceptability, not controlled comparisons.

> "after the examination the general response by students was that the Borderline grade "felt fairer", resulted in a clear grade and took station difficulty into account."
