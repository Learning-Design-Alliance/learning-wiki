## Evidence

### Burkholder 2022

Burkholder, E. W., Sake, M., & Zhang, J. (2022). Preparation for future active learning. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.09021](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.09021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison across two course iterations at a large public university. In the second iteration, weekly surveys showed almost no comments about students having to teach themselves: "only one student of 54 mentioned this on one of the weekly surveys." No effect size is reported.

> "Interestingly, we saw almost no comments about students having to teach themselves ideas – only one student of 54 mentioned this on one of the weekly surveys."
