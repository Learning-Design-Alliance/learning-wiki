## Evidence

### Dombek 2017

Dombek, J., Crowe, E. C., Spencer, M., Tighe, E. L., Coffinger, S., Zargar, E., Wood, T., Petscher, Y., & Connor, C. M. (2017). Acquiring Science and Social Studies Knowledge in Kindergarten Through Fourth Grade: Conceptualization, Design, Implementation, and Efficacy Testing of Content-Area Literacy Instruction (CALI). Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(3), 301-320. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000128

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RCT with 418 K-4 students randomly assigned within classrooms to CALI or business-as-usual, analyzed with HLM controlling for pre-test unit scores and grade level. Found "significant treatment effects of CALI" with g = 2.27 for social studies and g = 2.10 for science.

> "We found significant treatment effects of CALI for both Social Studies (g = 2.27) and Science (g = 2.10). Students randomly assigned to CALI achieved significantly higher social studies and science post-unit scores on the proximal measures."

### Dombek 2017 (2)

Dombek, J., Crowe, E. C., Spencer, M., Tighe, E. L., Coffinger, S., Zargar, E., Wood, T., Petscher, Y., & Connor, C. M. (2017). Acquiring Science and Social Studies Knowledge in Kindergarten Through Fourth Grade: Conceptualization, Design, Implementation, and Efficacy Testing of Content-Area Literacy Instruction (CALI). Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(3), 301-320. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000128

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same RCT and HLM analysis; science post-unit scores on proximal measures significantly favored CALI with g = 2.10, measured with researcher-developed pre-post unit assessments including counterfactual items.

> "We found significant treatment effects of CALI for both Social Studies (g = 2.27) and Science (g = 2.10). Students randomly assigned to CALI achieved significantly higher social studies and science post-unit scores on the proximal measures."
