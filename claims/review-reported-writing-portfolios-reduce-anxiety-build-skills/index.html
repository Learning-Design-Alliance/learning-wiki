q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, on the perception of preservice teachers, that Saavedra-Jeldres and Campos-Espinoza (2019) found participants valued portfolio writing as an effective strategy to improve writing and reflection skills in EFL. This is a second-hand report within the review; its design is not described here.
