### Herath H. M. M. T. B. 2018

Herath H. M. M. T. B., Keragala B. S. D. P., Udeshika W. A. E., Samarawickrama S. S. M., Pahalagamage S. P., Kulatunga Aruna, Rodrigo Chaturaka. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Practice and attitude items from the cross-sectional questionnaire of 123 NHSL non-specialist doctors covering the preceding 12 months: "Only 22 (17.9%) and 17 (13.8%) doctors had discussed" risk reduction and self-examination with patients.

> "Only 22 (17.9%) and 17 (13.8%) doctors had discussed regarding skin cancer/melanoma risk reduction and self examination of skin with patients (respectively). A majority (90, 73.2%) stated that they were not confident in educating a patient regarding skin (self) examination."
