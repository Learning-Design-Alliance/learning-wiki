Basarkod 2023¶

Fixed effects from the three-level multilevel models with gender coded female = 1, male = 2; the pattern appeared in both zero-order correlations and models accounting for achievement, SES, and age.

"There was a positive effect of gender on math enjoyment (β = .110) and a negative effect on reading enjoyment (β = -.323). That is, girls had lower math enjoyment, but higher reading enjoyment, compared to boys."
