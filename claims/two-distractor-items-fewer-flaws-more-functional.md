## Evidence

### Sunsundegui P 2026

Sunsundegui P, Llorente-Ortega M, Lucena F, Fernández-Ros N, Solas M, Alcaide AB, Landecho MF, Quiroga J, Iñarrairaegui M, Herrero JI. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized exam comparison, flaws were assessed with the 19-item Tarrant et al. checklist; low-functioning distractors were those selected by less than 5% of students. Both contrasts reached statistical significance as printed.

> "Items with two distractors demonstrated a lower frequency of item-writing flaws (39.8% versus 54%; p = 0.045), and a higher proportion of questions without low-functioning distractors (38.8% versus 18%, p = 0.01)."
