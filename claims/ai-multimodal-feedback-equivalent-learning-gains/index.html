Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA in the RQ1.1 learning evaluation, with post-test score as dependent variable, condition as between-group factor, and pre-test as covariate; homogeneity of regression slopes was satisfied (p = 0.24). The "effect of condition on post-test scores was not significant" with partial η² = 0.0047.

"After controlling for pre-test performance, the effect of condition on post-test scores was not significant (𝐹=0.91,𝑝=0.34, partial𝜂 2 =0.0047)."

Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2026 (2)¶

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-tests within each condition in the RQ1.1 analysis of the randomized experiment (197 learners). The AI group improved significantly with Cohen's d = 0.7623; the baseline group's paired test gave d = 0.5376 (t = −3.4841, p = 0.0006).

"The AI Multimodal Feedback group also demonstrated significant improvement from pre- to post-test (𝑡=− 5.6273, 𝑝<0.001***,𝐶𝑜ℎ𝑒𝑛′𝑠𝑑=0.7623)."
