## Evidence

### Hübner 2023

Hübner, N., Wagner, W., Zitzmann, S., & Nagengast, B. (2023). How Strong Is the Evidence for a Causal Reciprocal Effect? Contrasting Traditional and New Methods to Investigate the Reciprocal Effects Model of Self-Concept and Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09724-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The review reports, citing Möller et al. (2020), a meta-analysis on dimensional comparisons that found "coefficients between achievement and self-concept in similar subjects but substan - tial negative path coefficients in dissimilar subjects". No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

> "coefficients between achievement and self-concept in similar subjects but substan - tial negative path coefficients in dissimilar subjects."

### Fabian Wolff 2026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports, citing Möller et al. (2020), a meta-analysis of the classic I/E model including "N = 212,121 participants from k = 118 datasets" that found "strong positive within-domain effects and small to moderate negative between-domain effects."
