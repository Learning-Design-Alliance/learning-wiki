### M Tuveri 2024

M Tuveri, A Steri and D Fadda. (2024). Using storytelling to foster the teaching and learning of gravitational waves physics at high-school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive motivation results (Fig. 21, Table 3) from the 76-answer questionnaire: students rated motivation to study gravitational physics related contents in the future relatively high, while career-pursuit items scored lower. The authors attribute the divergence to physics as a school subject differing from astronomy/astrophysics interest.

> "Finally, concerning motivation, even if students seemed to be not very motivated in pursuing a career in physics (as also noted before in the case of science communication items), they want to learn more about gravitational waves physics."
