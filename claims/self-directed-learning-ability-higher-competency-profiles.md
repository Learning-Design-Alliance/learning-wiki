`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable multinomial logistic regression with the low-level profile as reference, adjusted for age, marital status, monthly income, professional title and years of nursing experience; the article reports "OR 1.119, 95% CI 1.077 to 1.163" for moderate-level and "OR 1.142, 95% CI 1.078 to 1.210" for high-level membership, both p<0.001 in table 4.

> "After adjustment for demographic and occupational variables, self-directed learning ability was positively associated with membership of the moderate-level profile (OR 1.119, 95% CI 1.077 to 1.163) and high-level profile (OR 1.142, 95% CI 1.078 to 1.210) rather than the low-level profile."
