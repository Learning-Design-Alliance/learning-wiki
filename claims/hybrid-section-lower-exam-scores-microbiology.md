## Evidence

### Adams A. E. M. 2015

Adams A. E. M., Randall S., Traustadóttir T. (2015). A Tale of Two Sections: An Experiment to Compare the Effectiveness of a Hybrid versus a Traditional Lecture Format in Introductory Microbiology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of two sections of a 16-wk introductory microbiology course taught by one instructor (n = 202 hybrid, 122 traditional). Table 2 reports e.g. Exam 1 means of 74.4 ± 15.6 (hybrid) vs. 84.6 ± 11.5 (traditional), p < 0.001; the article reports students in the hybrid section "achieved significantly lower scores than those in the traditional section for all exams".

> "Analysis of exam scores from the three midterm exams and the final exam indicated that students in the hybrid section achieved significantly lower scores than those in the traditional section for all exams (p < 0.05; Table 2 and Figure 3)."
