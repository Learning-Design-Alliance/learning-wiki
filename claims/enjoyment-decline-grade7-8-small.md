> "Students' enjoyment of mathematics classes generally decreased from Grade 7 to Grade 8. This decrease was statistically significant (repeated measures t test, t[1, 541] /H110052.64, p /H11021.01), but it was very small in terms of effect size (Cohen's d /H11005.06)."
