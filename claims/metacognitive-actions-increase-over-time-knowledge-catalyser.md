## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2010

The editorial reports, citing Pifarre and Cobos, an observational study of the Knowledge Catalyser forum in a normal course, analyzing student postings coded as metacognitive actions (planning, clarifying, monitoring). The reported increase is over time with no statistics printed.

> "The use by students of these actions to help direct the work of the group and its members increased over time, indicating an increase in the employment of metacognitive skills using the tools designed into the collaboration technology."
