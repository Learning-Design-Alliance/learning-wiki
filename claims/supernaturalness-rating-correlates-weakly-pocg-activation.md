## Evidence

### Xiaodong Xu 2025

Xiaodong Xu, Cheng Jia, Kang Chen & Lijuan Chen. (2025). Unveiling the neural mechanisms of supernatural fiction comprehension using fNIRS. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00327-0

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis on trial-level data from the 61-participant fNIRS reading experiment, linking online supernaturalness ratings to the SR-minus-SF activation difference at left poCG (CH3). The printed effect size is very small: "r = 0.058".

> "The correlation analyses revealed a signiﬁcant positive correlation between the supernaturalness ratings and the activation difference of SR and SF in the Left poCG (CH3) ( r = 0.058, t(2560) = 2.93, praw = 0.003, pFDR = 0.028; See Fig. 2a)."
