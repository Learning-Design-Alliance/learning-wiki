Exploratory logistic regression (McFadden's pseudo-R² = 0.091, AIC = 118.32) predicting quantifiable learning gains across the 95 studies, reported in Results §4.2. The model is explicitly labeled exploratory.

"learning context (formal vs. informal) was a significant positive predictor (OR = 3.17, 95% CI [1.00, 10.08], 𝑝=. 049). Immersion (any vs. none) showed a suggestive positive association (OR = 3.00, 95% CI [0.83, 10.86], 𝑝=. 094)."
