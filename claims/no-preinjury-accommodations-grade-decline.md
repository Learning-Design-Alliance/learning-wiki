# Children with no pre-injury academic accommodations showed significant negative grade changes after mTBI regardless of post-injury accommodation status

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among children with no accommodations before injury, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction showed significantly more negative grade changes after injury whether or not accommodations were used after (p < .05 and p = .034). [→ Kramer A 2025](#kramer-a-2025)
`q2 i?` Children who used accommodations before injury did not show significant grade changes after injury irrespective of post-injury accommodation use (p > .05). [→ Kramer A 2025 (2)](#kramer-a-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Kramer A 2025

Kramer A, Foley J, Hansen C, Teramoto M. (2025). Parent‐reported academic outcomes after a mild traumatic brain injury in the pediatric population. J Sch Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.13502

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction (Table 3) in the 285-parent survey; among children with no pre-injury accommodations who used them after, 21 reported negative versus 1 positive grade change. No effect size printed.

> "children with no accommodations used before injury showed significantly more negative grade changes after injury, regardless of whether or not accommodations were used after injury (p < .05)"

### Kramer A 2025 (2)

Kramer A, Foley J, Hansen C, Teramoto M. (2025). Parent‐reported academic outcomes after a mild traumatic brain injury in the pediatric population. J Sch Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.13502

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis (Table 3); the authors suggest these children may have had a working structure in place or more resources directed to their support. No effect size printed.

> "children who used accommodations before injury did not show significant grade changes after injury, irrespective of the use of accommodations after injury (p > .05)"
