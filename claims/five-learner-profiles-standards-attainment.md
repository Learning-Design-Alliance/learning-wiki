# Hierarchical clustering of CDM attainment probabilities identifies five learner groups with distinct patterns of professional standards attainment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Clustering the attainment probabilities of 178 pre-service teachers yielded five groups — Well-rounded (71), Knowledgeable and strategic (23), Student-centred (34), Novice (23), and Engaging and effective (27) — each with a distinct pattern across the four standards. A hierarchical agglomerative clustering of CDM attainment probabilities for the 178-learner cohort, with cluster count chosen via dendrogram and silhouette analysis (printed widths "0.67, 0.57, 0.65, 0.52, and 0.30"). Table 4 reports per-cluster average attainments, e.g. the Well-rounded group at 0.99 (0.058) on Standard 1.

> "The dendrogram ( Figure 1) and the silhouette analysis (silhouette widths were 0.67, 0.57, 0.65, 0.52, and 0.30, respectively) suggested five groups of learners with distinct patterns of standard attainment ."
