# Experts show longer fixation durations only in ELN videos, with no overall expertise main effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average fixation duration did not differ by expertise overall, but experts had longer fixation durations in ELN videos than in other video types, unlike the other groups. [→ Bertram Raymond 2013](#bertram-raymond-2013)

## Evidence

### Bertram Raymond 2013

Bertram Raymond, Helle Laura, Kaakinen Johanna K., Svedström Erkki. (2013). The Effect of Expertise on Eye Movement Behaviour in Medical Image Perception. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear mixed-effects modelling of 31,950 fixations with participants and videos as crossed random effects: no Expertise main effect, but a significant Expertise × Video interaction reflecting longer expert fixation durations in ELN videos only.

> "However, there was no significant main effect for Expertise, even though experts had numerically shorter average fixation durations than non-experts (E: 356 ms; SE: 372 ms; N: 360 ms). Importantly though, there was a significant interaction between Expertise and Video (p<.05), see Table A in Appendix S1."
