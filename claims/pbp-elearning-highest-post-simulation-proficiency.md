## Evidence

### McCarthy Nora 2023

McCarthy Nora, Neville Karen, Pope Andrew, Barry Lee, Livingstone Vicki. (2023). Effectiveness of a proficiency-based progression e-learning approach to training in communication in the context of clinically deteriorating patients: a multi-arm randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open 13(8):e072488. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072488

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multi-arm randomised double-blind controlled trial; primary analysis of 114 final-year medical students randomised to HSE, PBP or self-directed arms, assessed pre and post by two blinded independent raters in a standardised low-fidelity simulation. Fisher's exact test gave p<0.001; "Proficiency was highest in the (PBP) group (81.1%, 30/37)".

> "Post-intervention, the proficiency in each group as demonstrated in the low-fidelity simulation environment improved and there was a statistically significant difference in proficiency between the three groups (p<0.001). Proficiency was highest in the (PBP) group (81.1%, 30/37)"
