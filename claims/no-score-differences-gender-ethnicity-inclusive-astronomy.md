`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of average cumulative course scores for the 20 students who responded to the demographics survey, comparing male (N = 10, 84.3%) vs female (N = 10, 85.7%) and non-URM (N = 16, 84.8%) vs URM (N = 4, 85.8%) groups. The article reports "no diﬀerences in average scores across gender" and culture/ethnicity; equivalence was not formally tested.
