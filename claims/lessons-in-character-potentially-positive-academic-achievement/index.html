Lessons in Character 2006¶

Lessons in Character. (2006). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/270

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC recalculation of the Dietsch, Bayha, & Zheng (2005) randomized controlled trial of 372 fourth-grade students in 21 classrooms. The WWC found "0.46 Statistically significant" for mathematics grades and an attendance effect size of 0.48, also statistically significant; reading (ES 0.31) was not significant.
