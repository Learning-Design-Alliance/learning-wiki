# No coded study characteristics moderated the emotion–political learning associations in Bayesian regularized meta-analysis

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Bayesian regularized moderator analyses including sample size, publication status, discipline, country, sampling, measurement type, state vs. trait, object focus, discrete emotion, and learning category identified no relevant moderators of the emotion–learning associations. ## Evidence

### Elisabeth Graf 2024

Elisabeth Graf, Johanna L. Donath, Elouise Botes, Martin Voracek, Thomas Goetz. (2024). The Associations Between Discrete Emotions and Political Learning: A Cross-Disciplinary Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09893-y

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Exploratory Bayesian regularized meta-analysis (BRMA with horseshoe priors, three-level mixed effects models) of coded study descriptors as moderators; the article reports it "could not identify any relevant moderator from the included variables". An additional cognitive-versus-behavioral learning-outcome dummy also showed no significant effects.

> "As shown in Table  H1, we could not identify any relevant moderator from the included variables."
