# Student model sophistication increased significantly across successive iterations during the KBM unit

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed statistically significant improvement between each pair of successive model iterations (1–2, 2–3, and 3–4). [→ Dina Soliman 2026](#dina-soliman-2026)

## Evidence

### Dina Soliman 2026

Dina Soliman, Xueqi Feng, Marlene Scardamalia, Carol Chan. (2026). Knowledge building–modeling: Grade 5 students creating models, using discourse analytics, and advancing systems thinking. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09493-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

A paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on 153 student models coded with a five-level rubric (kappa = 0.85 for 20% double-coded). All three comparisons between successive iterations reached significance; no effect size is printed.

> "Results show a statistically significant improvement in models between iterations 1 and 2 (z = −3.649, p < 0.001), iterations 2 and 3 (z = −2.829, p < 0.01), and iterations 3 and 4 (z = −2.309, p < 0.05). These findings suggest that the KBM design helped students create more refined models which represent systems interrelationships."
