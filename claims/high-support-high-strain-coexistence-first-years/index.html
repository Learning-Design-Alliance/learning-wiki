Evidence¶

Zhang J and Yin Y 2026¶

Zhang J and Yin Y (2026). A study on AI-enabled course development, AI proficiency, and first-year students' academic and psychological adjustment. Front. Psychol. 17:1909803. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1909803

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive statistics from the 551-student cross-sectional survey show a coexistence of high support and high strain: 83.48% believed AI improves learning efficiency and 84.03% would proactively use AI tools, while "The endorsement rates were 69.51% for anxiety/tension, 68.78% for irritability, 67.88% for difficulty relaxing and stress, 67.70% for low self-worth, 67.52% for loneliness and lack of support, and 66.97% for additional pressure from AI platform tasks."
