## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cognitive load mediated the relationship between Tool-use and conceptual understanding (indirect effect ab = − 1.027), although there was no total effect of Tool-use. [→ Ruf 2023](#ruf-2023)

## Evidence

### Ruf 2023

Ruf, A., Zahn, C., Roos, A.-L., & Opwis, K. (2023). How do enhanced videos support generative learning and conceptual understanding in individuals and groups? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10275-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression-based mediation (PROCESS) with cognitive load as mediator between Tool-use and combined conceptual understanding; the a path (β = 0.740, p = 0.003) and b path (β = − 1.388, p < 0.001) were significant, but the c path was not (β = − 0.083, p > 0.10).

> "the relationship between Tool-use and "conceptual understanding" was found to be mediated by cognitive load, ab = − 1.027, 95% CI[-1.738, -0.391]."
