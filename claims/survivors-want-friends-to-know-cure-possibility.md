## Evidence

### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 473 Japanese adult cancer survivors, mostly early stage (311/473, 65.8%), recruited from independent research company panels. Survivors ranked "the possibility of a cure as a result of early detection and treatment" first (193/473, 40.8%).

> "The 5 most important types of information that the cancer survivors wanted their friends to know included: “the possibility of a cure as a result of early detection and treatment” (193/473, 40.8%), “types of cancer treatment” (154/473, 32.6%)"
