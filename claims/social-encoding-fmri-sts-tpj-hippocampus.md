## Evidence

### Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a follow-up fMRI study by Jeong et al. examining encoding under translation versus simulated social interaction, with L1 text and L1 videos as baselines controlling visual information. Compared with translation learning, the simulated video condition activated bilateral posterior STS and right IPL and showed the delayed-test correlations.

> "this condition also resulted in significant positive correlations between performance scores at delayed post-test and neural activities in the right TPJ, hippocampus, and motor areas"
