## Evidence

### Zhou Huiqin 2025

Zhou Huiqin, Huang Liyuan, Du Yuanzhuo, Lio Jonathan, Luo Bin, Xiao Xingyuan, Yang Kun. (2025). Group competition mechanism in surgical robot training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08080-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 60 robotic-naive undergraduates assigned to independent training (Group A) or team-based competition (Group B) on a dV-Trainer. The Results section reports "Group B scored significantly higher than Group A" on MB (P < 0.05) with fitted curves yA = 115.77ln(x) + 534.51 and yB = 174.41ln(x) + 604.74.

> "Group B scored significantly higher than Group A from the baseline test to the end of the study (P < 0.05). The logarithmic simulation of the scoring curve for each group follows: yA = 115.77ln(x) + 534.51 (R2 = 0.909); yB = 174.41ln(x) + 604.74 (R2 = 0.891)."
