Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan, Nabag Wisal Omer Mohamed. (2023). Structured viva validity, reliability, and acceptability as an assessment tool in health professions education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04524-6

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of reliability data from the 24 included studies. The review reports alpha values of 0.80 and 0.75 for structured viva and notes reliability coefficients of "(0.7 to 0.8) for the use of a structured rating procedure for viva compared to 0.3 to 0.4 for an unstructured viva exam". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Cronbach alpha of structured viva was α = 0.80 compared to α = 0.50 to the conventional viva as described by Madhukumar et al. [1], it was α = 0.75 as described by Anastaki et al. (27)"
