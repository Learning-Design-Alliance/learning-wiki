# Training significantly improved self-efficacy in situation awareness and in communication and teamwork, but not task management

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Post-training situation awareness and communication/teamwork self-efficacy differed significantly between groups (P < 0.05), while task management did not. [→ Mohammadi Masoumeh 2025](#mohammadi-masoumeh-2025)
`q3 i?` Within the intervention group, paired t-tests showed situation awareness and communication/teamwork self-efficacy significantly higher after training, but task management was not significantly different between the two times. [→ Mohammadi Masoumeh 2025 (2)](#mohammadi-masoumeh-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Mohammadi Masoumeh 2025

Mohammadi Masoumeh, Omid Athar, Tarrahi Mohammad Javad, Ghadami Ahmad. (2025). The impact of intraoperative non-technical skills training on scrub practitioners' self-efficacy: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07042-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group component comparisons in the same RCT (n = 27 analyzed per group per Table 4). The article reports "statistically significant differences (P < 0.05)" for situation awareness and communication and teamwork only; no effect sizes printed.

> "However, after the training, self-efficacy in the two skills of situation awareness and communication and teamwork showed statistically significant differences (P < 0.05)."

### Mohammadi Masoumeh 2025 (2)

Mohammadi Masoumeh, Omid Athar, Tarrahi Mohammad Javad, Ghadami Ahmad. (2025). The impact of intraoperative non-technical skills training on scrub practitioners' self-efficacy: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07042-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired t-test analysis in the RCT. Intervention-group means rose for situation awareness (9.29 to 10.14) and communication and teamwork (9.25 to 10.03), but "task management were not significantly different between the two times". No effect sizes printed.

> "The paired t-test results showed that in the intervention group, the mean level of both situation awareness and communication and teamwork was significantly higher after the intervention than before the intervention (P < 0.05); the mean levels of self-efficacy in task management were not significantly different between the two times in the groups (P > 0.05)."
