## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Final-year clerkship students from a traditional discipline-based programme (M = 63.8%) and a competency-based programme (M = 62.8%) did not differ significantly in the proportion of relevant diagnostic tests selected across six dyspnoea cases. [→ Czeskleba A. 2020](#czeskleba-a-2020)

## Evidence

### Czeskleba A. 2020

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Computer-based ASCLIRE assessment of 60 final-year clerkship students (30 TC, 30 CC) at one faculty; relevant test selection scored against specialist standard. TC averaged "63.8% of relevant diagnostic tests" vs CC 62.8%, not significant for any case.

> "Overall, the TC students selected, on average, 63.8% of relevant diagnostic tests. The CC students did not show a significantly different result (62.8%) for any of the six cases (numeric details in appendix 1)."
