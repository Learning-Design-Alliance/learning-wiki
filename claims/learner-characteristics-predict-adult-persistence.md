### Mary Beth Calhoon 2013

Mary Beth Calhoon, Hollis S. Scarborough, Brett Miller. (2013). Interventions for struggling adolescent and adult readers: instructional, learner, and situational differences. Read Writ 26:489–494. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-013-9442-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports, from Greenberg, Wise, Frijters, and Morris's profiling of completers (100 h of classes), mid-program withdrawers, and early withdrawers, that these learner characteristics "all signiﬁcantly predicted persistence". No effect sizes are printed.

> "specific learner characteristics (English language status, age, some reading related skills, avoidance of reading, previous adult education experience), class assignment, and whether the learner received Women, Infants, and Children benefits all signiﬁcantly predicted persistence."
