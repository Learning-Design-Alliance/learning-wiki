## Evidence

### Paddock Michael T. DO 2015

Paddock Michael T. DO, MS; Bailitz John MD; Horowitz Russ MD; Khishfe Basem MD; Cosby Karen MD; Sergel Michelle J. MD. (2015). Disaster Response Team FAST Skills Training with a Portable Ultrasound Simulator Compared to Traditional Training: Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.1.23720

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary endpoint of the randomized trial: 74-item validated multiple-choice US/FAST knowledge test administered pre- and post-course to all 36 participants. The article reports p<0.001 in each group; no effect size is printed.

> "Within each training group, there was a statistically significant improvement in the mean pre- and post-course knowledge test scores (p<0.001 in each group)."

### Paddock Michael T. DO 2015 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
