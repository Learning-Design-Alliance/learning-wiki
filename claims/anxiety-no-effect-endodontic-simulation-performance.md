## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational cross-sectional simulation study of 93 third-year dental students performing root canal treatment on extracted molars, stratified by preoperative STAI-S (low <44 vs high ≥45). Mann-Whitney U comparisons found "no statistically significant differences in RCT scores or total procedure duration"; mandibular medians were 4.0 vs 3.0 (p = 0.293) and durations 110 vs 115 min (p = 0.643).

> "There were no statistically significant differences in RCT scores or total procedure duration between low- and high-anxiety groups in either mandibular or maxillary endodontic procedures (p > 0.05 for all comparisons)."
