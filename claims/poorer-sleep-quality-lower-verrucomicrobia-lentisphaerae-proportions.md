A preliminary examination of gut microbiota, sleep, and cognitive flexibility in healthy older adults. Sleep Medicine, 38, 104-107. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7433257/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlations between PSQI scores and gut microbial phylum proportions in 37 healthy older adults. Table 1 shows Verrucomicrobia rs=-.52 (p<.01) and Lentisphaerae rs=-.37 (p<.05) with PSQI, supporting that "Poorer sleep quality was also associated with lower proportions of the phyla Verrucomicrobia and Lentisphaerae."
