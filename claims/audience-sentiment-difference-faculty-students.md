`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Sentiment analysis across target audiences of the 214 documents: administrators had the highest mean (0.99675), faculty 0.976451, students 0.910272, researchers 0.8989. The Kruskal–Wallis statistic was 22.7987 (p = 0.0004), and Dunn's test showed "significant divergence between documents intended for faculty versus students". Other pairwise comparisons showed no significant differences.

> "Post-hoc analysis with Dunn's test revealed significant divergence between documents intended for faculty versus students (p = 0.000460)."
