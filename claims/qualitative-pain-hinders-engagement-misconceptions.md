`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic framework analysis of 8 focus group discussions and 12 in-depth interviews with adolescent girls in four purposively-selected intervention-arm schools, conducted half after intervention delivery and half at endline. Findings as printed, including one verbatim fragment: "pain hindered their ability to focus and engage in learning activities." No effect size reported.

> "In qualitative interviews, participants reported that pain hindered their ability to focus and engage in learning activities, contributing to absenteeism and reduced class participation. Other students reported attending classes but that their menstrual pain led to decreased motivation, reduced focus, and poorer engagement in class lessons."
