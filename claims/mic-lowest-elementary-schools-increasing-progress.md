### Perry 2019

Perry, R., & Huang, K. (2019). Spotlight on student achievement: Analyses of statewide assessment data in Math in Common districts. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 3 analysis of MiC elementary schools grouped into four 2015 baseline achievement levels shows level 1 schools' beyond-prediction percentage climbing across years, an 11-percentage-point change the authors interpret as progress deepening with implementation.

> "47 percent of lower-performing schools scored beyond prediction in 2018, while only 40 percent of these schools did so in 2017 and 36 percent did so in 2016."
