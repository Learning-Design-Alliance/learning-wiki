# Novice learners subjectively prefer the endoscopic technique for learning basic ear-surgery skills

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants not previously trained in middle ear surgery, mainly medical students and residents, showed a subjective preference for the endoscopic technique on a five-point Likert questionnaire. [→ Anschuetz Lukas 2019](#anschuetz-lukas-2019)
`q2 i?` Preference for the endoscope increased with decreasing experience, as shown by questionnaire item Q3. [→ Anschuetz Lukas 2019 (2)](#anschuetz-lukas-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subjective feedback collected at the end of session 2 with a five-point Likert-scale questionnaire, after a think-aloud process with 6 participants ensured question understanding. The article reports a preference for the endoscopic technique mainly among novices.

> "In general, we observed a preference of the endoscopic technique mainly in medical students and residents, who were not previously trained in middle ear surgery."

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019 (2)

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire item Q3 from the same five-point Likert feedback survey summarized per education level in Figure 3. The article states preference for the endoscope increases as experience decreases; no effect size is printed.

> "This becomes especially evident in Q3 showing an increasing preference for the endoscope with decreasing experience."
