## Evidence

### Piñeros-Alvarez JL 2026

Piñeros-Alvarez JL, Portuguez-Jaramillo NE, Urbano-Cano AL. (2026). Effect of Real-time Feedback on the Quality of Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation for Medical Students in Colombia. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50647

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized study's 30-compressions/two-ventilations test, the feedback group's total compression target was "73% (5–83)" vs "0.5% (0–19)" in controls.

> "the total compression target was significantly higher in the intervention group compared to the control group; intervention group 73% (5–83) vs control group 0.5% (0–19); P < .05"

### Piñeros-Alvarez JL 2026 (2)

Piñeros-Alvarez JL, Portuguez-Jaramillo NE, Urbano-Cano AL. (2026). Effect of Real-time Feedback on the Quality of Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation for Medical Students in Colombia. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50647

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 30:2 test, median depth was "5.5 cm (5–5.8)" with feedback vs "6 cm (5.9–6.1)" without; compression rate showed no significant difference (P = .544).

> "The median compression depth demonstrated better control in the intervention group: 5.5 cm (5–5.8) vs control group 6 cm (5.9–6.1); P < .05"
