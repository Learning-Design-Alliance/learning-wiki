## Evidence

### Hibbert Emily J 2013

Hibbert Emily J, Lambert Tim, Carter John N, Learoyd Diana L, Twigg Stephen, Clarke Stephen. (2013). A randomized controlled pilot trial comparing the impact of access to clinical endocrinology video demonstrations with access to usual revision resources on medical student performance of clinical endocrinology skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the randomized pilot trial's thyroid examination station, performance standard was uniformly high and "There was no difference in the standard of performance" on examiner global judgment or any of the 25 criteria. The null finding is reported as no detected difference, not equivalence.

> "Ninety percent (n = 9/10) of students who had viewed the video were judged globally as having performed satisfactorily compared with 100% (n = 12/12) of those who had not viewed the video (p = 0.926)."
