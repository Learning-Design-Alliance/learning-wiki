## Evidence

### Bindman 2015

Bindman, S. W., Pomerantz, E. M., & Roisman, G. I. (2015). Do Children's Executive Functions Account for Associations Between Early Autonomy-Supportive Parenting and Achievement Through High School? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000017

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM analysis of NICHD SECCYD observational parenting data (1,306 dyads) with covariates for temperament, Bayley MDI, maternal education, income-to-needs, gender, and ethnicity; the simultaneous model showed "Mothers’ early autonomy support and cognitive stimulation both predicted enhanced executive functions at 54 months."
