## Evidence

### Hilton 2022

Hilton, C. B., Goldwater, M. B., Hancock, D., Clemson, M., Huang, A., & Denyer, G. (2022). Scalable Science Education via Online Cooperative Questioning. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0249

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Marginal structural models for the step 4 cooperative activity (Tables 3 and 4, Figure 2): metabolism MCQ coefficient 7.825 (p < 0.001), SAQ coefficient 9.684 (p < 0.001); the combined score reflects authoring quality plus meaningful peer feedback comments.

> "For deeper levels of engagement in the cooperative question-authoring task, there was a predicted 7.8% increase for each rise in level of engagement for the multiple-choice exam mark, and a 9.7% increase in the mark on the short-answer section of the exam."
