# A WestEd research bulletin reports a curriculum revision that replaced a decorative photograph in a math problem with a problem-relevant sketch, on the developers' view that such photographs distract students

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The bulletin reports a developer's view, with no new data, that pictures not directly related to the math of a problem, such as an amusement park photograph, distract students, while sketches closely representing the problem's details better help students acquire the math content. `q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' interpretation of cognitive science research as applied in the bumper car floor plan revision, where a photograph was replaced with a sketch of three possible floor plans including one that does not meet the requirements. No new empirical data are reported for this specific change.

> "Schneider explains that pictures like the one of an amusement park are not just filling space unnecessarily but actually distract students because such pictures are not directly related to the math of the problem."
