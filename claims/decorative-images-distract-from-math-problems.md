# Decorative photographs unrelated to the mathematics of a problem distract students, and replacing them with problem-relevant sketches strengthens visual-verbal connection

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Pictures not directly related to the math of a problem, such as an amusement park photograph, distract students, while sketches closely representing the problem's details better help students acquire the math content. The authors' interpretation of cognitive science research as applied in the bumper car floor plan revision, where a photograph was replaced with a sketch of three possible floor plans including one that does not meet the requirements. No new empirical data are reported for this specific change.
