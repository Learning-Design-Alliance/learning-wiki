# CLT-aligned e-learning modules improved cognitive load profiles and OSCE performance in simulation-based medical training (review-attributed to Gutierrez et al., 2023)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The review reports, citing Gutierrez et al. (2023), that randomly assigned postgraduate trainees using CLT-informed preparatory modules showed lower extraneous load, higher germane load, and significantly better OSCE performance. [→ Wei 2025](#wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Wei 2025

Wei, X., Roschelle, J., Pautz Stephenson, S. & Wortman, A. (2025). Researching digital learning platforms: Foundations, methods, and policy recommendations. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/257

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this to a pilot randomized study by Gutierrez et al. (2023) in a simulation-based medical training program, where first-year postgraduate trainees were randomly assigned to weekly online preparatory modules 'explicitly informed by CLT to reduce extraneous load.' No effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that the group using the modules experienced lower extraneous cognitive load and higher germane load during instruction and also demonstrated significantly better performance on an objective structured clinical examination (OSCE)."
