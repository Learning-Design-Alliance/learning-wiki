## Evidence

### Correa-Burrows P 2016

Correa-Burrows P, Burrows R, Blanco E, Reyes M, Gahagan S. (2016). Nutritional quality of diet and academic performance in Chilean students. Bulletin of the World Health Organization. https://doi.org/10.2471/BLT.15.161315

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate results from the fully adjusted logistic models (Model 4) in the cohort (n = 395): e.g. vocational high school OR 0.24 (0.14–0.39) and adult high school OR 0.17 (0.06–0.52) for language tests versus academic high school.

> "Regarding the covariates’ influence, the type of secondary education remained tied to academic performance in the mandatory tests after adjustments, whereas sex remained associated with having a high school GPA (≥ 75th percentile). Maternal education and body weight were no longer significant in models with full adjustment."
