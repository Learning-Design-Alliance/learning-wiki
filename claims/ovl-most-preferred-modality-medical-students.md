### Rafey IA 2026

Rafey IA, Abdullah A, Saeed Z, Khan UU, Mohyuddin W. (2026). Comparative analysis of university lectures, textbooks, and online video lectures: preferences, engagement, and impact on academic performance among medical students of Nowshera Medical College. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09921-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 355 undergraduate medical students at Nowshera Medical College using stratified random sampling. The chi-square goodness-of-fit showed "a significant deviation from equal preference" across the three modalities, with OVL dominant.

> "OVL was the most preferred learning modality (71.7%), followed by TB (26.9%) and UL (1.4%) among a total of 355 students (Fig. 1). Chi-square goodness-of-fit indicated a significant deviation from equal preference (χ² (2) = 267.90, p < 0.001)."
