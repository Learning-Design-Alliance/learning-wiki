## Evidence

### Datu 2025

Datu, J. A. D., Sun, X. H., Liu, S., Pang, F., & Watt, H. (2025). Are gritty children more engaged in math? Triarchic model of grit, math self-efficacy beliefs, and math engagement in Chinese children in Macau. School Psychology International, 46(6), 582–602. https://doi.org/10.1177/01430343251386902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM indirect-effect analysis in the 794-student Macau sample. Table 4 and Figure 1 print the only significant indirect effect: "ab= .123, p= .041" from perseverance via math self-efficacy to behavioral engagement. The article also reports perseverance positively predicted behavioral engagement directly.

> "Indirect effect analyses showed perseverance was linked to greater behavioral math engagement, through higher math self-efficacy beliefs, which partly confirmed Hypothesis 4 (Table 4)."
