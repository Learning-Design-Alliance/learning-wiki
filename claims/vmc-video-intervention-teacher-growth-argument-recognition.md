## Evidence

### Maher 2014

Maher, C. A., Palius, M. F., Maher, J. A., Hmelo-Silver, C. E., & Sigley, R. (2014). Teachers Can Learn to Attend to Students' Reasoning Using Videos as a Tool. Issues in Teacher Education, 23(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1045688

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 177 pre- and in-service teachers (127 experimental, 50 comparison) scored blindly on a video-based pre/post assessment. Logistic regression found each experimental subgroup exceeded comparison: "the growth rate of each of the experimental subgroups exceeded that of the comparison group".

> "The analysis indicated that the data provided evidence that the growth rate of each of the experimental subgroups exceeded that of the comparison group at a significance level that varied from 0.05 to less than 0.0001."
