## Evidence

### Kuo 2015

Kuo, E., & Wieman, C. E. (2015). Seeking instructional specificity: an example from analogical instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test comparison using four CSEM items on equipotential lines and electric field, administered in the first week before the intervention. No significant difference in prior knowledge; CC students scored slightly higher. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "On the pre-test questions, there was no difference in mean score (out of 4 possible points: mGR = 1.30, sdGR = 1.08, mCC = 1.45, sdCC = 1.09), t(229) = 1.06, p = .29."
