## Evidence

### Alghadier Mshari 2026

Alghadier Mshari, Badcock Nicholas. (2026). Sleep, movement, and marks: exploring the relationship between sleep quality, physical activity, and academic performance in male university students. PeerJ 14:e21154. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21154

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples t test comparing GPA between good (PSQI ≤ 5) and poor (PSQI > 5) sleepers in the same 130-student cross-sectional sample; Cohen's d = 0.93 indicates a large difference.

> "good sleepers presented a greater GPA (3.28 ± 0.45) than poor sleepers did (2.87 ± 0.43); this difference was significant according to an independent-samples t test (t(86) = 4.98, p < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 0.93)."
