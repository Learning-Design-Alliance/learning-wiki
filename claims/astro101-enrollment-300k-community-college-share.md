The chapter's enrollment section synthesizes AIP and NCES statistics, estimating "at least 300,000 students taking introductory astronomy annually" and a 33 to 42 percent community-college share, building on Fraknoi's earlier estimates of 100,000 to 125,000 at two-year colleges.

> "Thus there are at least 300,000 students taking introductory astronomy annually in the United States. This is an astounding 10% of incoming freshman every year (Snyder et al., 2018) and, as near as we can tell, 33 to 42 percent of them are taking that introductory astronomy course (hereafter referred to as "Astro 101") at a community college."
