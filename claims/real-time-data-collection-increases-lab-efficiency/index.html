Ethnographic observation of the momentum labs in both years by a supporting faculty member. The duration contrast is a qualitative field-note observation, not a controlled timing measurement; students engaged in the same type of activities in both years.

"Also the ethnographer indicated in her notes that the lab lasted only an hour, whereas during Fall 2004, the lab lasted a full two hours. This indicates that the technology increased the efficiency of the class significantly even though the students were engaged in the same type of activities."
