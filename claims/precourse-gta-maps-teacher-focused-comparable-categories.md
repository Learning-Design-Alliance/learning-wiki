## Evidence

### Star W. Lee 2019

Star W. Lee. (2019). The Impact of a Pedagogy Course on the Teaching Beliefs of Inexperienced Graduate Teaching Assistants. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-07-0137

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of eight precourse group concept maps from inexperienced biology GTAs enrolled in Biology 301, coded to TBI categories with 93% interrater agreement. Percentages of traditional, instructive, and transitional terms were "32.63% traditional, 45.44% instructive, and 21.93% transitional"; no responsive or reform-based terms appeared.

> "The terms on the precourse maps were categorized as follows: 32.63% traditional, 45.44% instructive, and 21.93% transitional (Figure 2). There were no terms that were categorized as responsive or reform based. There were no significant differences between the TBI categories in the precourse maps."
