## Evidence

### Kvintová Jana 2025

Kvintová Jana, Lacková Lucia, Liu Hongyang, Váchová Lucie, Potmesil Milon, Růžičková Veronika, Kopecký Kamil, Regec Vojtech. (2025). Resilience displays similar associative patterns with academic achievement regardless of the personality and mental health profile of future teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03697-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cluster-wise regressions on the AAQ coping subscale in the 793-student survey. All three cluster models were significant with adjusted R² of 0.06–0.15; Control was significant in Cluster 1 (β = 0.09, p = 0.025) and PCHST was strongest in Clusters 2 and 3.

> "Significant overall models were observed for coping with study demands in all clusters (Cluster 1: F [5, 289] = 5.06, p < 0.001, adj. R² = 0.06; Cluster 2: F[5, 209] = 8.35, p < 0.001, adj. R² = 0.15; Cluster 3: F[5, 277] = 7.31, p < 0.001, adj. R² = 0.10). Control (CNT) emerged as a significant predictor in Cluster 1 (β = 0.09, p = 0.025)"
