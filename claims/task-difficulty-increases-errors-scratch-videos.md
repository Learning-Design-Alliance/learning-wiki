### Veronica Pimenova 2026

Veronica Pimenova, Chris Lee, Baramee Bhakdibhumi, Simon Chu, and Andrew Begel. (2026). Leveling the Playing Field: Temporal Video Segmentation for Individuals with ADHD in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24612

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Poisson GLMM analysis of error counts from the within-subjects study (N=162 observations, 27 participants). The article reports "significantly more errors on Medium tasks" and Hard tasks relative to Easy; the Medium–Hard contrast did not reach significance (p=.472).

> "Using Easy tasks as the baseline, participants committed significantly more errors on Medium tasks (𝛽= 1.54,𝑝<. 001) and Hard tasks (𝛽= 1.85,𝑝<. 001)."
