## Evidence

### Roye-Green Karen J. 2025

Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis comparing nursing (Y4) and medical (Y4–Y5) students in late clinical training to minimize curriculum-structure differences. Medical students more often identified "the correct definition of sepsis (Q11)" (69.0% vs. 47.3%, p = 0.013), while several other group differences were no longer significant.

> "medical students were more likely to identify the correct definition of sepsis (Q11) in both the general and subgroup analysis but this difference achieved statistical significance in the subgroup analysis (69.0% vs. 47.3%, p = 0.013)"
