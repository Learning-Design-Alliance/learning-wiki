`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The scoping review's conclusion, based on synthesis of thirteen included studies, states that "surgical simulators are highly effective and valuable for residents to practice technical maneuvers" such as septal dissection and osteotomies. This is the review's own synthesis-level judgment rather than a single experimental effect size.

> "Beyond intra-operating teaching and modeling, surgical simulators are highly effective and valuable for residents to practice technical maneuvers; however, future efforts leveraging artificial intelligence and software technologies can help further improve rhinoplasty education."
