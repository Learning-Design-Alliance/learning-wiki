## Evidence

### Pulfrey 2011

Pulfrey, C., Buchs, C., & Butera, F. (2011). Why grades engender performance-avoidance goals: The mediating role of autonomous motivation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(3), 683–700. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 115-student field experiment, the mixed-effects analysis of performance-approach goals produced an ICC of 0% and showed the grade contrast "had no effect on pretask performance-approach goals"; means were 3.03 (graded) versus 3.06 (nongraded). Experiment 2 replicated this null (β = −0.04, ns).

> "Stage 2 analyses showed that our fixed-effect contrast, the grade contrast, had no effect on pretask performance-approach goals"
