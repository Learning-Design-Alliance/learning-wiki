Subgroup analysis by study sample size within the random-effects meta-analysis; the strata pooled at "42.16%" and "37.53%" respectively, both with very high heterogeneity. The authors state sample size significantly affects the reported proportion.

> "The pooled proportion for studies with sample sizes of 102–250 was 42.16% (95% CI: 24.87–59.45, I 2 = 93.41%, p = 0.00), while for studies with sample sizes of 251–414, the pooled proportion was 37.53% (95% CI: 26.05–49, I 2 = 97.18%, p = 0.00)"
