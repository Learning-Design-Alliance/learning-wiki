### Bello Ajediran I 2014

Bello Ajediran I, Ofori Eric K, Alabi Oluwasegun J, Adjei David N. (2014). Assessment of the level of agreement in the interpretation of plain radiographs of lumbar spondylosis among clinical physiotherapists in Ghana. BMC Medical Imaging. [doi:10.1186/1471-2342-14-13](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2342-14-13)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact test analysis of 41 physiotherapists' agreement levels (low/moderate/high) against demographic and clinical variables. The article reports association with "academic qualification (0.006)" and "sex (p = 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The levels of agreement were significantly associated with academic qualification (0.006) and the sex (p = 0.001) of the physiotherapists."
