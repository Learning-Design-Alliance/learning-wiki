## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fei Gao 2023

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Three-way repeated-measures ANOVA on the 300–500 ms window from 30 bilinguals found a region × laterality × priming interaction (partial η2 = 0.256); semantic priming (−2.33 ± 0.44 μV) exceeded morphological priming (−1.61 ± 0.53 μV) at the midline fronto-central electrode. The LAN difference wave showed no significant language effect or language-related interaction.

> "Meanwhile, there was a signi ﬁcant interaction effect between region, laterality, and priming type, F(2,58) = 9.99, p = 0.002, partial η2 = 0.256. Simple main effect results showed that semantic priming ( −2.33 ± 0.44 μV) involved greater negativity in the midline electrode of fronto-central cortex, as compared to morphological priming ( −1.61 ± 0.53 μV) (p = 0.013)."
