Evidence¶

Shaban Eman E. 2025¶

Shaban Eman E., Elmelliti Hussam, Shaban Amira, Shaban Ahmed, Zaki Hany A. (2025). Impact of early clinical exposure on medical students’ competency development: a systematic review of evidence from Asian and African countries. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08103-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review with narrative synthesis of 15 studies of early clinical exposure (ECE) in Asian and African medical schools. Three of the included studies (one quasi-experimental, two non-randomised interventional) reported students' perceptions of ECE's effect on communication skills; averaged across them, "42.16% strongly agreed and 46.48% agreed" that ECE improved their communication skills. These are self-reported perceptions, not measured skill, and no effect size is printed.

"The average data from these studies showed that the majority of the students agreed that ECE improved their communication skills (42.16% strongly agreed and 46.48% agreed)"
