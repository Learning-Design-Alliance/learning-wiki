q3 · i? · causal · r3

Study characteristics table for Bettinger & Baker (2014). Institutions provided student lists and InsideTrack© randomly assigned students; in seven 50/50 lotteries a coin flip decided treatment, and rebalancing occurred before the coin flip. In 10 lotteries students were reassigned after randomization, compromising random assignment.

"The study was conducted with 17 different cohorts in eight participating universities during the 2003-2004 and 2007-2008 school years."
