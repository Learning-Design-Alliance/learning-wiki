## Evidence

### Melikoğlu Seçil Erden 2025

Melikoğlu Seçil Erden, Demircan Burcu, Yorulmaz Yasemin Kıyak. (2025). The effect of video-assisted teaching on nursing students'practice skills and anxiety levels in Range of Motion (ROM) exercises: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07714-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 36 video-group and 36 control-group students were evaluated on a full-size human model by blinded observers using the ROM Exercise Practice Skill Checklist one week after training. The video group scored "87.13 ± 18.10 versus 57.44 ± 16.52 in the control group, a statistically significant difference (p = 0.000)". A post hoc analysis reported an effect size of 1.7 and test power of 99% for ROM skill performance.

> "The skill scores of the students in the video group were 87.13 ± 18.10 versus 57.44 ± 16.52 in the control group, a statistically significant difference (p = 0.000)."
