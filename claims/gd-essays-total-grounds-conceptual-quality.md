`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate analyses of covariance on essay-quality measures from the group products of the 39-group study; the article reports GD groups "produced signiﬁcantly better essays in terms of total grounds quality, total con-ceptual quality, and average conceptual quality."

### Jeroen Janssen 2008 (2)

Jeroen Janssen, Gijsbert Erkens, Paul A. Kirschner, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Effects of representational guidance during computer-supported collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9078-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANCOVA results from the same essay analysis; two of the five essay measures showed no significant condition difference, so the essay-quality effect is partial rather than uniform.

> "The univariate analyses of covariance shown in Table 7 indicate that GD groups did not cover more topics in their essays than did TD groups, nor were their essays better in terms of average grounds quality."
