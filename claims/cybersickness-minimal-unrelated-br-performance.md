### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Sun J, Marshall K, Mitchell JP, Laprade J, Nederveen JP, Helli PB, Rebalka IA. (2026). A preliminary comparison of bellringer performance across three visual modalities for the assessment of anatomy knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70145

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-exam Simulation Sickness Questionnaire administered after the mock bellringer exam; 89% (124/140) of participants scored below 10, and only 2% (3/140) reached the concerning range. The article reports "No significant relationships were observed between total SSQ scores and overall BR success or VR question success."
