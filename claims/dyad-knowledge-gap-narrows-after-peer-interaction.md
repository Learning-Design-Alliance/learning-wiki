## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The knowledge level gap between dyad members was significantly smaller after peer interaction than before it. [→ Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019](#diego-pablo-de-la-hera-2019)

## Evidence

### Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019

Diego Pablo de la Hera, Mariano Sigman and Cecilia Ines Calero. (2019). Social interaction and conceptual change pave the way away from children 's misconceptions about the Earth. npj Science of Learning 4:12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0051-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test over 15 dyads of second graders comparing knowledge level gap between Interviews 1 and 2. The gap "was narrowed after peer interaction", t(14) = 2.75, p = 0.016, d = −0.61, 95% CI (−1.35, −0.12).

> "On average, the knowledge level gap between both members of a dyad ( ΔML) was narrowed after peer interaction, as found with a paired-samples t test, t(14) = 2.75, p = 0.016, d = −0.61, 95% CI (−1.35, −0.12)"
