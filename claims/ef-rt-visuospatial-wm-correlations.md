# Mean RT in HF blocks is negatively correlated with visuospatial WM and mixed-block RT positively with intelligence

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Mean RT in both the incongruent and mixed blocks of HF was negatively correlated with visuospatial WM (r = −0.29 and r = −0.21), even after controlling for accuracy in each block. [→ Ger Ebru 2023](#ger-ebru-2023)

## Evidence

### Ger Ebru 2023

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i2`

Exploratory correlational analyses in the N = 147 sample: mean RT in the incongruent and mixed HF blocks correlated negatively with visuospatial WM (Mole), "r = −0.29, −0.21", holding after controlling for accuracy. Non-parametric correlations yielded the same pattern.

> "The mean RT in both the incongruent and mixed block of HF was negatively correlated with visuospatial WM (r = −0.29, −0.21, p < .0001, .01, respectively), even after controlling for accuracy in each block"
