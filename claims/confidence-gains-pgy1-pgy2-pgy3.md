### Wilkoff MH 2025

Wilkoff MH, Seltzer ES, Piniella NR, Seepersaud H, Loanzon P, Kurtz S, Salonia J, Jodorkovsky D. (2025). Acute Upper Gastrointestinal Bleeding: A Hands-On Simulation Case for Internal Medicine Residents Improves Knowledge and Confidence. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11541

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post confidence ratings on a 4-point Likert scale (1 = not confident to 4 = extremely confident) in the simulation cohort. The article reports significant increases for PGY1 and PGY2 in all categories; no effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant increase in the level of confidence pre- and postsimulation for PGY 1 and PGY 2 residents in all categories (p < .05; Table 2)."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same pre/post Likert-based confidence analysis, PGY3 subgroup. Significant increases were limited to the three named categories; no effect sizes are printed.

> "PGY 3 residents had a significant increase in confidence in managing acute variceal bleeds (p = .03), peptic ulcer bleeding (p = .002), and outpatient EV (p = .003; Table 2)."
