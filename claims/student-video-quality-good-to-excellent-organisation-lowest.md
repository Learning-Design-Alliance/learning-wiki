## Evidence

### Si Qingzong 2026

Si Qingzong, Li Siqi, Cai Lulu, Huang Qian, Huang Chupeng, Zhang Xinyue, An Xiaoli. (2026). Experiential teaching centered on science popularisation videos: a randomised controlled trial on the impact on learning outcomes and core competencies of dental preventive medicine students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09910-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quality assessment of only the five finalised videos (of nine draft teams) by three instructors using a structured rubric with ICC = 0.81. Content organisation and logical coherence (3.53 ± 0.51) received "the lowest and most inconsistent scores".

> "After being evaluated by three teachers using a single-blind scoring method, the overall quality score for the videos was 3.92 ± 0.19, with scores ranging from 3.83 to 4.35, corresponding to the “good” to “excellent” range on the validated 5-point rubric."
