DaisyQuest for Preschool Children 2006¶

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the Lonigan et al. (2003) randomized controlled trial, the word blending outcome showed a mean difference of 1.35, effect size 0.33, marked "ns" (not statistically significant). Syllable/phoneme blending had an effect size of 0.01 and multiple-choice blending 0.26, both ns. Equivalence was not tested.
