## Evidence

### Benden 2026

Benden, D. K., & Lauermann, F. (2026). The Interplay of Personal and Situational Factors in Situated Expectancy-Value Theory: Cross-Classified Analyses of Students' Situation-Specific Motivations in the Math Domain. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001032

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplemental predictive-validity analyses using Model 3 with course performance as an additional student-level outcome, controlling for gender, high-school math performance, advanced coursework, and participation mode; grades were available for 850 students, standardized within course.

> "students' test performance significantly positively predicted their end-of-term exam performance in math-intensive STEM courses (bs = 0.32 to 0.34, ps < .001, across the four models; see Table 6). Students' test-specific motivational beliefs did not have significant incremental predictive effects (b = −0.09 to 0.08, ps ≥ .116)."
