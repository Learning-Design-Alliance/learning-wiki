## Evidence

Zinski Anne, Blackwell Kristina T.C. Panizzi Woodley, Belue F. Mike, Brooks William S. (2017). Is lecture dead? A preliminary study of medical students' evaluation of teaching methods in the preclinical curriculum. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.59b9.5f40

Chi-square tests compared the proportions of MS1 (N=55) and MS2 (N=65) students ranking each method in their top 5. Lecture showed the largest cohort gap; the patient presentation contrast (63.1% vs 45.5%, χ2(1, N=120)=3.75, p=0.05) is reported in the same Results section.

> "94.5% of MS1 students included lecture in their top 5 preferred methods, and significantly fewer (66.2%) MS2 students did likewise (χ2(1, N=120) =16.33, p<0.0001)."
