Narrative synthesis: generative AI and chatbot-based virtual patients "more commonly associated with enhancements in affective outcomes", with variable, predominantly subjective cognitive effects; one study showed comparable diagnostic accuracy to real-patient simulation.

> "Generative AI and conversational agents (chatbot-based virtual patients) were more commonly associated with enhancements in affective outcomes including motivation, perceived competence, and satisfaction, with variable and predominantly subjective effects on cognitive skill development [7, 30–33, 36]."
