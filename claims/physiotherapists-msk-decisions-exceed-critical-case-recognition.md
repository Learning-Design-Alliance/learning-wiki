### Nákity K 2025

Nákity K, Kasza BB, Tatár BB, Szűcs M, Kis D, Barzó P, Domján A. (2025). Assessment of differential diagnostic skills of physiotherapists related to the cervical spine - approaches to improving effectiveness: observational, cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 25:1065. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07682-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 128 Hungarian physiotherapists scoring eight cervical case vignettes (CS 1–8) as intervention versus advice/referral. The Results section reports "61.7% respondents made appropriate treatment decisions in both MSK cases" against 22.7% (n = 29) for the CM cases; no effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Seventy-nine (61.7%) respondents made appropriate treatment decisions in both MSK cases (CS 4, CS 6), and 22.7% of the respondents (n = 29) made appropriate treatment decisions in the CM cases (CS 1, CS 2, CS 3, CS 5, CS 7, and CS 8)."
