### Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian 2015

Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian. (2015). Efficiency of Using a Web-Based Approach to Teach Reading Strategies to Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p30

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test on a paper-based TOEFL-style test (three readings, five multiple-choice questions each, 50 min) administered before and after the Web-based program to the same 30 participants. No effect size is printed.

> "we can conclude that the difference between the participants' pretest scores (M = 9.86, SD = 2.47) and their posttest scores ( M = 10.83, SD = 2.74) t (29) = -2.09 in the paper-based condition is significant."
