## Evidence

### Ann-Kathrin Krause 2025

Ann-Kathrin Krause, Jasmin Breitwieser, Garvin Brod. (2025). Understanding Learning Strategy Use Through the Lens of Habit. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10087-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the same 64-student proof-of-concept survey, reported with Fig. 4. Means and SDs are descriptive only; the article notes highlighting and rereading showed ceiling effects and concept mapping a bimodal distribution. No inferential test is attached to these descriptives.

> "Highlighting showed the highest habit strength (M = 6.21, SD = 1.00), followed by rereading (M = 5.75, SD = 1.45). In contrast, the more effective strategies exhibited lower values: self-testing had a mean of 4.04 ( SD = 1.66), and concept mapping showed a mean of 3.06 ( SD = 1.51, see Fig. 4)."
