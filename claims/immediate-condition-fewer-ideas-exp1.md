`q3 i1` In Experiment 1, participants in the immediate condition generated fewer ideas than participants in the conscious thought and unconscious thought conditions. [→ Ritter 2012](#ritter-2012)

## Evidence

### Ritter 2012

Ritter, S.M.; van Baaren, R.B.; Dijksterhuis, A.J. (2012). Creativity: The role of unconscious processes in idea generation and idea selection. Thinking Skills and Creativity, 7, 21-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2011.12.002

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 ANOVA on fluency across the three thought conditions (immediate n = 37, conscious n = 37, unconscious n = 38) yielded a main effect with the printed effect size /DC12 = .09 (the article's notation for eta-squared, a small effect); immediate participants generated fewer ideas than conscious thinkers, t(72) = 3.06, p < .01, and unconscious thinkers, t(73) = 2.13, p < .05.

> "An ANOVA on the number of ideas revealed a main effect of thought condition, F(2,109) = 5.43, p < .01, /DC12 = .09."
