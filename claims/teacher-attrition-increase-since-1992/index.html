Trend analysis of NCES Schools and Staffing Survey data shows attrition rose from 5.1% in 1992 to 8.4% in 2005; the report states the 3% increase "amounts to about 90,000 additional teachers needing to be hired across the U.S. each year."

"5.1% of public school teachers left the workforce in 1992, while 8.4% left in 2005. Attrition rates have continued to hover around 8% since then"
