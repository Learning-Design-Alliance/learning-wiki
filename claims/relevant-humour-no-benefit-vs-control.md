## Evidence

### Bender 2026

Bender, L., Renkl, A., & Endres, T. (2026). Punchline with(out) purpose: Integrating research on instructional humour and seductive details. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reported across both studies. Bayesian ANCOVAs comparing relevant humour to control yielded anecdotal evidence for recall (Study 1 BF01 = 2.74; Study 2 BF01 = 2.29) and moderate evidence for transfer (Study 1 BF01 = 3.59; Study 2 BF01 = 3.70) favouring the null hypothesis.

> "the relevant-humour group did not clearly outperform the control group in either of the studies."
