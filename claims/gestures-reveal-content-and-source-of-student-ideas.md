### Rachel E. Scherr 2007

Rachel E. Scherr. (2007). Gesture analysis for physics education researchers. Accepted to Physical Review Special Topics – Physics Education Research, May 2007. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Detailed video analysis of a minute-and-a-half episode of three students and a TA drawing velocity vectors on a projectile trajectory. The author reports the half-parabola gesture appeared "about forty seconds before" the TA's components intervention, so the idea was Jenny's own construction.

> "Since the gesture first occurred before the TA intervention, it was surely not a result of that intervention – and perhaps the intervention was not as helpful as one might have thought, at least to Jenny."
