# In one uncontrolled pre-test/post-test study, after implementation of the standardized TBL framework in a 6-week course, students' self-reported perception of the TBL process rose significantly from pre-test to post-test on all items except pre-class preparation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a one-group pretest-posttest survey of OMS II students, self-reported ratings of all TBL Process items rose significantly from pre-test to post-test except the item on consistently reviewing pre-class preparation materials (p = 0.07), with e.g. immediate feedback rising from 3.06 (1.02) to 3.99 (0.87). ## Evidence

### Andrews-Dickert Rebecca 2024

Andrews-Dickert Rebecca, Nagaraj Ranjini, Zhan Lilian, Knittig Laura, Zhao Yuan. (2024). Improving learning experience through implementing standardized team-based learning process in undergraduate medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06025-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest survey; pre-survey n = 53 of 105 (50.4%), post-survey n = 72 of 104 (69.2%) OMS II students, compared with unpaired t-tests. Table 2 reports significant increases on all TBL Process items except "I consistently reviewed the pre-class preparation materials prior to TBL sessions" (3.36 to 3.76, p = 0.07); immediate feedback rose 3.06 (1.02) to 3.99 (0.87), p < 0.0001.

> "In TBL Process, significant increase in student perception was found in all items except for pre-class preparation."
