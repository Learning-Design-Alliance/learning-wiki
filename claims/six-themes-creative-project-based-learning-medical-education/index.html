Evidence¶

Yi Tan Yong 2025¶

Yi Tan Yong, Shreyans Panwar, Vallabhajosyula Ranganath. (2025). Learning by making – student-made models and creative projects for medical education: systematic review with qualitative synthesis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06716-8

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review with qualitative content and thematic analysis of 17 studies published 2010–2022. The review reports that "six key themes were derived from the 20 codes that were identified across the studies." No effect sizes reported; impact null.

"Following thematic analysis, six key themes were derived from the 20 codes that were identified across the studies. These themes were (1) enhanced learning, (2) collaborative learning, (3) deep learning, (4) student engagement, (5) student disengagement, and (6) faculty engagement."
