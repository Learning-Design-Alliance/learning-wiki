## Evidence

### Andrea López-Incera and Wolfgang Dür 2019

Andrea López-Incera and Wolfgang Dür. (2019). Entangle me! A game to demonstrate the principles of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1119/1.5086275](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5086275)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Opinion survey of n = 73 high-school juniors after playing the game; Table I prints e.g. 64% fully agreeing "I have enjoyed the game" and 53% fully agreeing it was useful, while 60% fully disagreed that instructions were complicated.

> "The survey results presented in Table I show a good acceptance by the students. In addition, when asked for difficulties they had during the game, some of the scientists reported that they were too far from the particles so they could not hit them enough times"
