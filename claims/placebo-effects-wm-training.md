## Evidence

### Gobet Fernand 2022

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-analytic finding from the authors' WM-training meta-analyses: far-transfer placebo effects attributable to passive or weak control conditions, with the printed magnitude range of 0.15–0.20 SMD.

> "However, we did find that placebos always occur in WM training when it comes to far transfer (Sala & Gobet, 2020b). These placebos are around 0.15 to 0.20 standardized mean difference at best and often affected by publication bias."

### Gobet Fernand 2022 (2)

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' video-game meta-analysis found no placebo effects, indicating the placebo pattern is specific to WM training rather than universal across cognitive-training regimens.

> "For example, they are not present in either action- or nonaction-video-game training (Sala et al., 2018)."
