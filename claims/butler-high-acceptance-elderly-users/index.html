Evidence¶

Etchemendy 2011¶

Etchemendy, E.; Baños, RM.; Botella, C.; Castilla López, DV.; Alcañiz Raya, ML.; Rasal, P.; Farfallini, L. (2011). An e-health platform for the elderly population: The butler system. Computers and Education. 56(1):275-279. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.07.022

q2 · i? · design · r2

A study of seventeen elderly users (from 58 to 79 years old) who "completed four sessions with the system" and could choose which technological resources they used in each session. The abstract reports "they obtained high levels of satisfaction, low levels of subjective difficulty and would recommend Butler to other users"; no effect sizes or statistics are printed.
