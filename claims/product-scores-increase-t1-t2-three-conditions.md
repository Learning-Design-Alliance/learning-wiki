# Product scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 2 in three of four conditions, but the content condition's increase was only close to significant

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Product scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 2 for control (9.06, χ2=9.572, p=.002), request (6.82, χ2=5.587, p=.018), and combination (8.70, χ2=8.270, p<.004) conditions; the content condition increased 5.40, close to significant (χ2=3.186, p=.074).
