## Evidence

### Aimilios Hadjiliasi 2024

Aimilios Hadjiliasi, Louis Nisiotis, Irene Polycarpou. (2024). A Comparative Assessment of Technology Acceptance and Learning Outcomes in Computer-based versus VR-based Pedagogical Agents. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct64951.2024.00148](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct64951.2024.00148)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-experience UTAUT questionnaire (EE, PE, BITU, ATUT items on a 7-point Likert scale) administered to both groups of 46; after Cronbach's alpha reliability checks and Shapiro-Wilk normality violations, medians and IQRs were compared with the Mann-Whitney U test, which found differences "not signiﬁcant".

> "The test rev ealed that even though there are some minor differences between th e two technologies, those are not signiﬁcant, denoting that thei r differ- ences are likely due to random chance and not due to true effec t."
