## Evidence

### Lei Y 2025

Lei Y, Zhang Z, Zhou F, Xia T, Xu N, Sun Z. (2025). Virtual reality force feedback spine surgery simulator training for pedicle screw placement: assessing the impact of seniority and spinal region. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07695-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-centered cross-sectional pre-post study of 64 fellows from three tertiary hospitals, in PGY groups A (18), B (30), and C (16), trained 50 min on VRFF-SSTS. Accuracy "increased to 88.33%±6.18%" for Group A, 89.60%±7.09% for B, 90.00%±6.33% for C (all p < 0.001).

> "Post-training, there was a significant enhancement in screw placement accuracy across all groups compared to pre-training (p < 0.001). The accuracy for Group A increased to 88.33%±6.18%, for Group B to 89.60%±7.09%, and for Group C to 90.00%±6.33%. No significant differences were observed between seniority levels post-training."
