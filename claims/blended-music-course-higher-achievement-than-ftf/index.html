Evidence¶

Adileh 2012¶

Adileh, M. (2012). Teaching music as a university elective course through e-learning. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2012(1), 71-79. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000246

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-test comparison in a pre-test/post-test control group design with 179 university students (93 FTF, 86 blended) purposefully assigned for equivalency. The blended group's mean of 61.49 exceeded the FTF group's 51.16, with t(177) = 6.913 reported in Table 2; the article reports "significant difference in course achievement" without an effect size.

"there was significant difference in course achievement between the FTF and blended groups (p<.01). The experimental (blended) group's mean score on the achievement test was higher than the control (FTF) group's mean score."
