## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fourth-year dental students exposed to the Erietta simulation scored significantly higher on a seven-question knowledge test than controls (mean 5.40 vs 3.61, t101=6.40, P<.001). [→ Papadopoulos L 2013](#papadopoulos-l-2013)

## Evidence

### Papadopoulos L 2013

Papadopoulos L, Pentzou A-E, Louloudiadis K, Tsiatsos T-K. (2013). Design and Evaluation of a Simulation for Pediatric Dentistry in Virtual Worlds. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 103 fourth-year dental students: simulation group (n=52) mean 5.40 (SD 1.40) vs control (n=51) mean 3.61 (SD 1.44) on a seven-question knowledge questionnaire; "A t test for independent samples showed a statistically significant difference between the groups". No effect size printed.

> "A t test for independent samples showed a statistically significant difference between the groups, indicating a gain in knowledge for the students who were exposed to the simulation: t 101=6.40, P<.001"
