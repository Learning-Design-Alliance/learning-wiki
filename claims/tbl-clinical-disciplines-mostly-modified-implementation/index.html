Evidence¶

Sterpu Irene 2024¶

Sterpu Irene, Herling Lotta, Nordquist Jonas, Rotgans Jerome, Acharya Ganesh. (2024). Team-based learning (TBL) in clinical disciplines for undergraduate medical students—a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04975-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

Scoping review of 49 peer-reviewed studies on TBL in clinical disciplines; the review's descriptive synthesis reports that "one or more TBL steps were missing" in 44 of 49 studies, with only four describing full-concept TBL.

"Most of the studies (n = 44, 90%) described the implementation of a modified version of TBL in which one or more TBL steps were missing, and one study had undefined protocol for the implementation."
