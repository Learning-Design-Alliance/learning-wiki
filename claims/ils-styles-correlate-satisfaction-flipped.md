## Evidence

### Baherimoghadam Tahereh 2025

Baherimoghadam Tahereh, Naseri Navid, Tabesh Amir, Hamedani Shahram, Mousavi Seyedeh Fatemeh. (2025). The influence of learning styles on the efficacy of flipped classroom in dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07916-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Spearman correlations between ILS dimensions and satisfaction in the flipped group (Table 6): Active r = 0.777, p = 0.046; Intuitive r = 0.713, p = 0.006; Sequential r = 0.573, p = 0.017; reflective, sensing, visual, verbal and global correlations were not significant.

> "in the flipped classroom, active processing in the dimension of information process had a significant relationship with the level of satisfaction in training. In addition, in the dimension of information perception, a significant relationship was observed between the intuitive perception of information and the level of satisfaction."
