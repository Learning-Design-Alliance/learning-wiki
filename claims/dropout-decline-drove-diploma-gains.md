Analysis of student outcomes at age 19 across cohorts (Figure 3, Table 1). The share earning a regular diploma rose from 43 percent (1998 cohort) to 62 percent (2014 cohort); dropout counts fell from over 10,000 to just over 3,000 students.

> "The percentage of students in each cohort recorded as dropping out of high school by age 19 has declined dramatically over the last 15 years, falling from 35 to 11 percent of students."
