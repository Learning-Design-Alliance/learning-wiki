## Evidence

### McCoy C. Eric MD 2019

McCoy C. Eric MD, MPH; Rahman Asif MD; Rendon Juan C. MD; Anderson Craig L. PhD, MPH; Langdorf Mark I. MD, MPHE; Lotfipour Shahram MD, MPH; Chakravarthy Bharath MD, MPH. (2019). Randomized Controlled Trial of Simulation vs. Standard Training for Teaching Medical Students High-quality Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2018.11.39040

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized parallel-arm trial of 70 fourth-year medical students randomized to high-fidelity simulation (SIM) or standard low-fidelity manikin (STD) training; primary outcome measured in a simulated cardiac arrest scenario. The trial found "the mean compression depth was 4.57 cm" for SIM versus 3.89 cm for STD, p=0.02. No effect size was printed.

> "the mean compression depth was 4.57 cm (95% CI [4.30 – 4.82]) for the SIM group and 3.89 cm (95% CI [3.50 – 4.27]) for the standard (STD) group, p=0.02"
