`q3 i?` After a 1-week intervention, the ChatGPT group scored significantly higher than the video-only control group on the desktop VR operational test (mean 73.12, SD 10.06 vs mean 65.54, SD 12.48; P<.001). [→ Qiao J 2025](#qiao-j-2025)

## Evidence

### Qiao J 2025

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 187 dental undergraduates randomly assigned by sealed envelope to a ChatGPT-3.5 group or a video-only control group for a 1-week skill acquisition period. The t test for operation performance revealed "a statistically significant difference (t176.241=4.569, P<.001)"; Table 1 reports means of 73.12 (SD 10.06) versus 65.54 (SD 12.48).

> "However, the t test for operation performance revealed a statistically significant difference (t176.241=4.569, P<.001), indicating that the learning modes exerted an impact on operation learning."
