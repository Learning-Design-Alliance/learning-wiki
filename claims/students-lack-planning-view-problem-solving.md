## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Problem Solving and Learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06352](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06352)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-questionnaire administered at the start of the interview session to students in all three groups before any aid tool was used. The article reports the majority disagreement on planning and separately that "Half of the students also agreed with the statement “Physics problem solving is all about matching given quantities in the problem to the formula in the book"".

> "a majority of the students (regardless of the group to which t hey belonged) disagreed with the statement “When confronted with a physics problem, I ﬁrst spend a reasonable amount of time planning how to solve the problem before actually solving it"."
