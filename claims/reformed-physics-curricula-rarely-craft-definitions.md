Angela Little and Leslie Atkins Elliott. (2018). On the Importance of Engaging Students in Crafting Definitions. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/1.5051144](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5051144)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors assert, without presenting data, that extended definition crafting is rare even in reformed introductory physics curricula, and that Physics by Inquiry engages teachers in operationally defining concepts but is used almost entirely in teacher professional development rather than with introductory students.

> "Even in many reformed introductory physics curricula such as Tutorials in Introductory Physics14 or Modeling Instruction15,16 it is rare that students spend extended time crafting definitions for themselves."
