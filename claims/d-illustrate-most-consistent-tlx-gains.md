## Evidence

### Si Chen 2026

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

TLX post-hoc analysis in the 16-participant within-subject study (six clip pairs for D-Illustrate); Table 2 shows e.g. learning satisfaction rising from 7.75 (2.23) to 8.90 (2.12). The article states D-Illustrate "had the most consistent impact" among the four design ideas.

> "For D-Illustrate, post-hoc analysis revealed significant reductions in mental demand (EMM = –0.71, t(30.3) = -3.07, p < .05), physical demand (EMM = –0.70, t(33.3) = -3.04, p < .05), and temporal pressure (EMM = –0.68, t(36.3) = -3.14, p < .05), along with increased learning satisfaction (EMM = 1.15, t(32.8) = -4.64, p < .001)."
