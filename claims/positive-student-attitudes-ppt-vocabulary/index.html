Nam and Trinh 2012¶

Ta Thanh Nam & Lap Q. Trinh. (2012). PowerPoint as a potential tool to learners' vocabulary retention: Empirical evidences from a Vietnamese secondary education setting. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 4, October - December. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070209

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of a 24-item Likert questionnaire (four clusters: cognitive, interest, classroom interaction, difficulties) administered after the study; the questionnaire's "reliability coefficient of the questionnaire is high (α = .81)". Means are descriptive; no inferential test is reported.

"The mean score of the questionnaire is high (M = 3.42, SD = 0.40) in comparison with the scale 3 in the five-point scale. This result reveals that students had positive attitudes towards the use of PPT in teaching vocabulary."
