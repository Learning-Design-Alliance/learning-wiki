Observational analysis of video access data from the Superstar Learning platform for the FCCL group; descriptive statistics only, no effect size. The authors read this as "tangible evidence" of adoption of the pre-class segment.

> "On average, each student viewed 2.8 (SD=0.4) out of the 3 videos assigned per week. Furthermore, the average viewing proportion for each video was 78.5% (SD=12.3%), indicating that students consistently watched the majority of the assigned content"
