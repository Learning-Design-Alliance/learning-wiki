### Gilmore Camilla K. 2010

Gilmore Camilla K., McCarthy Shannon E., Spelke Elizabeth S. (2010). Non-symbolic arithmetic abilities and achievement in the first year of formal schooling in mathematics. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.02.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three hierarchical regression analyses were performed on Experiment 2 data. In model 1, both non-symbolic addition (R2 change = .033, p = .036) and symbolic number knowledge (R2 change = .084, p < .001) contributed to school mathematics achievement after verbal IQ and literacy; in model 2 non-symbolic addition strongly predicted number symbol mastery (R2 change = .041, p = .017).

> "In this analysis, non-symbolic numerical performance no longer contributed significantly to children’s school mathematics achievement (Table 3, model 3)."
