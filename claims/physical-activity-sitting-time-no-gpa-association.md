## Evidence

### Ong C.K.Y. 2021

Ong C.K.Y., Hutchesson M.J., Patterson A.J., Whatnall M.C. (2021). Is There an Association between Health Risk Behaviours and Academic Achievement among University Students? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168314

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted linear regression in the cross-sectional survey (n = 1543). Table 3 reports adjusted β = −0.010 (p = 0.877) for physical activity and β = −0.011 (p = 0.851) for sitting time; equivalence was not tested.

> "Interestingly, meeting national physical activity recommendations and having an average sitting time of more than 8 h/day was not significantly associated with GPA."
