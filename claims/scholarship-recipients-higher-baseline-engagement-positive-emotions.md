`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline single-factor analysis of demographic variables in the 65-student cohort found a significant scholarship effect on engagement and positive emotions; only test statistics and means are printed, no effect size, so no magnitude is coded. Scholarship group n = 5.

> "The learning engagement (93.33 ± 11.93) and positive emotion scores (87.33 ± 3.51) of the scholarship group were significantly higher than those of the non-scholarship group. However, there was no significant difference between the two groups in terms of total academic emotion scores (p > 0.05)"
