Hemmeter et al. 2015¶

Mary Louise Hemmeter, Jessica K. Hardy, Alana G. Schnitz, Jessie Morris Adams, and Kiersten A. Kinder. (2015). Effects of Training and Coaching With Performance Feedback on Teachers' Use of Pyramid Model Practices. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121415594924

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Maintenance data were intermittent checklist observations after intervention ended on each goal. Reminders were brief statements to continue using practices, with no coaching; with these reminders both teachers maintained their use of targeted practices.

"Kendra maintained all practices at criterion levels, but Bianca and Susan both needed reminders to continue using practices they had already acquired."
