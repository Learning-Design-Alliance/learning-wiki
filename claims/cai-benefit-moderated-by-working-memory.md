`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the same 509-student field study. The article reports that a one-standard deviation gain in WM capacity resulted in a supplementary CAI benefit of 0.57 points regardless of students' prior knowledge, SES, and ASC, an approximate improvement from the 68th to the 78th percentile.

> "Fig 5 (right panel) shows the significant Instruction Method * WM capacity interaction effect (β = .57, p = .03, 95% CI [.06; 1.12]) found in Model 4, Table 1."
