## Evidence

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of 504 children with ADHD testing the inattention → PS → WM pathway. The article reports full mediation by PS; the printed indirect effect is an unstandardized coefficient with a bootstrap CI, so no standardized effect size is available.

> "The direct effect between inattention symptom severity and WM was not significant, but the indirect effect through PS was significant. This indicates that the relationship between inattention symptom severity and WM performance is fully mediated through PS"
