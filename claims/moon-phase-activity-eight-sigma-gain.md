## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the night laboratories were introduced, the fraction of students answering the moon-phase question correctly rose from 28% (2006 posttest) to 82% (2007 posttest), an eight-sigma difference attributed to the 'Light and Shadow in the Solar System' activity. ### Ian C. Jacobi 2008

Ian C. Jacobi, Heidi Jo Newberg, Darren Broder, Rose A. Finn, Anthony J. Milano, Lee A. Newberg, Allan T. Weatherwax, and Douglas C. B. Whittet. (2008). Effect of Night Laboratories on Learning Objectives for a Non-Major Astronomy Class. https://www.rpi.edu/dept/phys/observatory/labs.html [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0809.3817](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0809.3817)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest/posttest comparison of the Fall 2006 (no laboratories, 82 students) and Fall 2007 (with laboratories, 68 students) cohorts on the same assessment. The article reports the gain "in 2007, 82% of the class was able to answer this question correctly" and attributes it to the cloudy activity introduced in 2007.

> "In 2006, only 28% of the class had the right answer on the post test, but in 2007, 82% of the class was able to answer this question correctly."
