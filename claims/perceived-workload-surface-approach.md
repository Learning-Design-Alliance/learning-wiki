## Evidence

### Baeten 2010

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review synthesis of correlational studies on perceived workload and approaches to learning. The review reports the relationship is "signiﬁcantly negative with a deep approach to learning" across multiple cited studies, with one study finding no significant relationship.

> "Besides this study, many researchers have showed that an excessive perceived or inappropriate workload is positively associated at a signiﬁcant level with a surface approach"

### Baeten 2010 (2)

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a study focusing on information load rather than general perceived workload, reaching results analogous to the workload findings. Kember's explanation is that students resort to short cuts, i.e. the surface approach, to cope with excessive perceived demands.

> "students who perceived an overload of information scored higher on the surface and lower on the deep approach"
