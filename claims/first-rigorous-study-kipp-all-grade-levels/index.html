The summary frames the evaluation as "the first high-quality study to rigorously examine the impacts of the network of KIPP public charter schools at all elementary and secondary grade levels," concluding impacts are strongest at elementary and middle school levels.

"In the first high-quality study to rigorously examine the impacts of the network of KIPP public charter schools at all elementary and secondary grade levels, Mathematica found that KIPP schools have positive impacts on student achievement, particularly at the elementary and middle school levels."
