Narrative characterization of the 25 included studies by educational stage in the discussion section. The review states "In all of them, the effectiveness of AI has been shown" and notes a tendency toward more studies at advanced educational stages.

"Thus, there are three studies in primary education, eleven contextualised studies in secondary education, one in vocational studies and ten studies in university. In all of them, the effectiveness of AI has been shown."
