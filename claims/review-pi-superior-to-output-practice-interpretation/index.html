Narrative review synthesis in Section 2.4 attributing the conclusion to five named prior studies; the review reports, citing those authors, that PI outperformed output practice, particularly on interpretation tasks.

Hossein Hashemnezhad & Sanaz Khalili Zangalani. (2012). The Effects of Processing Instruction and Traditional Instruction on Iranian EFL Learners' Writing Ability. English Language Teaching, 5(11). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n11p125

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Narrative review attributing this finding to Allen (2000); the review also reports VanPatten and Wong's (2004) replication found different results from Allen's but similar results to VanPatten and Cadierno (1993), so production findings are inconsistent across studies.

"Allen (2000) investigated the relative effect of PI and TI on the acquisiti on of the French ca usative and found that PI was as effective as TI enabling learners to interpret the French causa tive and that TI is more effective in enabling learners to produce the French causative."
