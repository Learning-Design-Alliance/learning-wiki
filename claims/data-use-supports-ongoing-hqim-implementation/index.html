Hannan 2026 (2)¶

Hannan, M., Rainier, S., & Perry, R. (2026). Beyond curriculum adoption: What district leaders are saying about using Illustrative Mathematics. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681318

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Spotlight interview: Willow's data-based learning cycle paired teacher action plans with principal building-level plans, forming the basis for classroom visits and collaborative "hall chats" after visits.

"Teachers used a common protocol to examine district dashboard data, diving into the prior year’s achievement test results; considering 3- to 4-year trends; and later reviewing results for particular standards, questions, and student subgroups."
