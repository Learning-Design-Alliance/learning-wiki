## Evidence

### van Dorst R. W. J. J. 2026

van Dorst R. W. J. J., Rinkes I. H. M. Borel, Gilg S., Molenaar I. Q., Hagendoorn J., Sparrelid E. (2026). Reaching proficiency in robotic liver surgery in two high-volume European HPB centers. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03372-y

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Binary logistic regression on the bi-institutional cohort, adjusted for IWATE difficulty score and console surgeons. The model shows "a decrease in odds of complications with center experience" with OR=0.988 (95%CI [0.976-0.999], p=.034); the printed per-case odds ratio is very close to 1.

> "Binary logistic regression showed significant correlation between chronological case number and risk of the occurrence of early complications (OR=0.988, 95%CI [0.976-0.999], p = .034), indicating a decrease in odds of complications with center experience, independent of individual surgeon differences (p = .979)."
