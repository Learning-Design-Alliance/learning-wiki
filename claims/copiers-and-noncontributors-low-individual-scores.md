## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Webb 1993

Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavior coding of tape-recorded group work for 53 students; six students (11%) were coded as copying. Table 2 shows their mean individual test score was .13 versus a group score of .94. The article states their low individual scores "show that they did not use the group's resources to learn how to solve the problems for themselves."

> "A few students (n = 6 or 11%) clearly copied other students' work without trying to understand it"
