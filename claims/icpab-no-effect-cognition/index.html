Evidence¶

Peiris D. L. I. H. K. 2022¶

Peiris D. L. I. H. K., Duan Yanping, Vandelanotte Corneel, Liang Wei, Yang Min, Baker Julien Steven. (2022). Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19159479

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of four intervention samples from three RCTs for inhibition. The pooled SMD = −0.64 was not significant (p = 0.30) with I2 = 97%; after sensitivity analysis the estimate was SMD = −1.48 (p = 0.0006) favoring control, still with I2 = 83%.

"With a significant level of considerable heterogeneity (I2 = 97%, p < 0.00001), it was found that the IcPABs do not have significant effects on the inhibitory performance (SMD = −0.64, 95% CI [−1.85 to 0.56], p = 0.30) among the participants"

Peiris D. L. I. H. K. 2022 (2)¶

Peiris D. L. I. H. K., Duan Yanping, Vandelanotte Corneel, Liang Wei, Yang Min, Baker Julien Steven. (2022). Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19159479

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of three samples (n = 585) from two studies for attention performance. The pooled SMD = 0.31 was not significant (p = 0.67) with extreme heterogeneity (I2 = 98%); updating (two samples, SMD = −0.07, p = 0.65) was also null.

"Three intervention samples from two studies showed positive impacts of IcPAB on children’s attention performance [9,11] with small to large pooled effects (SMD = 0.31, 95% CI [−1.15 to 1.77], p = 0.67; I2 = 98%, p < 0.00001"
