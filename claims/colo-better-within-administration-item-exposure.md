## Evidence

### Hu 2021

Hu, A., Chien, M., & Meyer, P. (2021). Comparability of MAP Growth tests administered through different technology and psychometric infrastructure: A simulation study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/comparability-of-map-growth-tests-administered-through-different-technology-and-psychometric-infrastructure-a-simulation-study/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item exposure rates within an administration were computed for each item in the pool (Tables 4.7–4.15) for both engines. The report attributes the difference to the randomesque procedure "implemented on COLO but not on the CBE."

> "Within the same administration, more items in the item bank were administered and less items were overexposed by COLO than by the CBE. This is likely due to the randomesque procedure implemented on COLO but not on the CBE to control item exposure."
