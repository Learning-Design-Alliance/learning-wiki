### Malhotra 2026

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Jagendra Singh et al., an eye-gaze classification system using Haar Cascade and CNN that "achieved an accuracy of more than 98%". The review also reports 95% accuracy for Gaze View Tracking (Li et al.) and for an OCSVM-based browser-plugin system (Dilini et al.).

> "The eyeball localization is then classified using the Haar Cascade classifier, which was trained and tested using CNN. The model achieved an accuracy of more than 98%."
