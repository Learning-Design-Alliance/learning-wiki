`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "when the use of strategyRead and write simultaneously(S2) was compared with the use of strategyWrite intensively, read selectively(S3), students who used the latter one scored higher ( 3.14 points), and the difference was statistically significant (p= 0.045with the large effect size)"
