Evidence¶

Tanya Chichekian 2026¶

Tanya Chichekian, Maria Cutumisu, Annie Savard and Yi-Mei Zhang. (2026). Profiling Pre-service Teachers' Computational Thinking: The Role of Metacognition and Coding Experience. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 31–41. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9077

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of survey and behavioural variables among 128 pre-service teachers (Table 1). The article reports coding experience correlating with digital literacy at "r = .22, p < .05", coding comfort at r = .29, and perceived difficulty at r = –.22; the cognitive-load interpretation is the authors'.

"Prior coding experience (CodExp) positively correlated with digital literacy (CT_DL, r = .22, p < .05) and coding comfort (CT_CD, r = .29, p < .01), but negatively correlated with perceived task difficulty (L_Diff, r = –.22, p < .05), suggesting coding experience enhances CT skills and reduces task -related cognitive load."
