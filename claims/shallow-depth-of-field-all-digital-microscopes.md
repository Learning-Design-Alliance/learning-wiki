### Awad L 2024

Awad L, Abdi Z, Langridge BJ, Karoshi A, Butler PEM. (2024). A Comparison of Commercially Available Digital Microscopes for Their Use in Bench-Model Simulation of Microsurgery. Journal of Reconstructive Microsurgery. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0044-1787980

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the questionnaire study, depth-of-field mean rank differences versus the LTM were 85.24 (PDM), 75.56 (KDM), 61.79 (PLDM), and 74.11 (smartphone), all significant at p < 0.0001; "Depth of field was found to be poorer in all digital microscopes." Trainees commented the shallow depth was detrimental to surgical practice.
