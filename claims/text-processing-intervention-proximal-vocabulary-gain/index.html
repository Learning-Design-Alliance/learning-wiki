Amy E. Barth 2016¶

Amy E. Barth, Sharon Vaughn, Philip Capin, Eunsoo Cho, Stephanie Stillman-Spisak, Leticia Martinez, and Heather Kincaid. (2016). Effects of a Text-processing Comprehension Intervention on Struggling Middle School Readers. Top Lang Disord. 2016; 36(4): 368–389. https://doi.org/10.1097/TLD.0000000000000101

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

One-way ANCOVA on the researcher-developed vocabulary matching measure with pretest as covariate, in a randomized trial with 83 treatment and 51 comparison students. The article reports "treatment students performed better than the control students" with F (1, 131) = 7.00, p = .009, ηp2= .051.
