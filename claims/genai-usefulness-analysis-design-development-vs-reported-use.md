## Evidence

### Luo 2025

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of perceived usefulness ratings for ID tasks grouped by ADDIE phase (N = 70). Analysis, design and development tasks "were generally regarded as having the highest perceived utility than tasks in the implementation and evaluation phase."

> "Overall, tasks in the analysis, design and development phase (i.e. "Write performance objectives," "Select or modify instructional content", "Create design document") were generally regarded as having the highest perceived utility than tasks in the implementation and evaluation phase (i.e., "Monitor the implementation", "Conduct client reviews")."

### Luo 2025 (2)

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of perceived usefulness for communication, research, and management task items (N = 70). Communication (M = 2.03) and research tasks were "perceived as highly valuable compared to management (M = 3.26)."

> "Communication tasks (M = 2.03) and research-related tasks (M = 2.56 for "Application of research and theory" and M = 2.47 for "Data collection and analysis") were perceived as highly valuable compared to management (M = 3.26)."

### Luo 2025 (3)

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of self-reported GenAI use for specific ID tasks (N = 70). The article reports IDs had not yet adopted or only infrequently employed these tools in most areas, and "rarely used them in the implementation and evaluation phase."

> "IDs most frequently used these tools to "write performance objectives" (M = 2.30) and to "select or modify instructional content" (M = 2.27). They rarely used them in the implementation and evaluation phase."
