## Evidence

### Linder Ifat 2025

Linder Ifat, Lukach Zafit, Pergament Keren, Steckler Rafi, Tepper Sigal. (2025). The effect of simulation training on the counseling skills of nutritional science students in dietetic internships. BMC Medical Education 25:1114. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07674-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire analysis of the personal simulation experience among 20 interns, with answers re-coded to a 1–5 scale for comparability with actors and tutors. Reported means are "AVG = 4.65, SD = 0.54" and "AVG = 4.49, SD = 0.69", with no significance test.

> "An examination of the interns' experience with the personal simulation revealed that the simulation scenarios were very relevant (AVG = 4.65, SD = 0.54) and that the interns benefited from the simulation consultation (AVG = 4.49, SD = 0.69)."
