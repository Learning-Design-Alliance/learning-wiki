### Zheng 2026

Zheng, Y., Lo, T. W., Lau, E. Y., Eskes, G., Hui, L. L., & Hsiao, J. H. (2026). The Role of Eye Movement Consistency in Aging-Related Decline in Face Recognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00437-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Partial correlation analyses in the 301-participant face recognition study. The article reports the age–d′ (r(286) = -.149) and age–false alarm (r(286) = .153) correlations remained significant after controlling aging-related cognitive abilities, while the age–RT correlation became insignificant (r(286) = .049).

> "older age was associated with poorer recognition d′ (r(297) = -.227, p < .001, padjusted < .001, Figure 1A), higher false alarm rate (r(297) = .237, p < .001, padjusted < .001, Figure 1B), and longer RT (r(297) = .259, p < .001"
