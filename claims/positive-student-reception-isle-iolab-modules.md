## Evidence

### E. Tufino 2025

E. Tufino, P. Onorato and S. Oss. (2025). Exploring active learning in physics with ISLE-based modules in high school. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-module Likert-scale attitude survey of all classrooms (100 students) in the pilot implementation. The article reports 61% high ratings, 33% neutral and 6% lower ratings, indicating "a largely positive reception with some room for improvement". No effect size is printed.

> "In response to the question ”2) The activities proposed were fun and interesting”, out of 100 students (the students of all classrooms), 61 rated them highly (61%), 33 were neutral (33%) and 6 gave lower ratings (6%)."

### E. Tufino 2025 (2)

E. Tufino, P. Onorato and S. Oss. (2025). Exploring active learning in physics with ISLE-based modules in high school. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same post-module survey item on technical confidence with sensor devices. Percentages are descriptive only; no statistical test or effect size is reported for this item.

> "Regarding the capability to use iOLab or similar sensor devices for experiments, from 100 students, 43 (43%) felt confident ('4' or '5' rating), 29 (29%) were neutral, and 19 (19%) expressed lower confidence ('1' or '2')."
