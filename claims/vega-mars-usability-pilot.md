### Shin Sujin 2026

Shin Sujin, Choi Jaehwa, Hong Eunmin, Lee Miji, Go Jinseon, Yu Subin, Lee Minjae. (2026). Development of vignette-based evaluation with generative artificial intelligence (VEGA) for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09685-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-usage MARS survey of 12 undergraduate nursing students after one week of VEGA use; descriptive statistics only. The article reports "The overall mean score was 3.62 ± 1.04, with item-level means ranging from 2.58 to 4.42." Domain means: information 4.25 ± 0.87, functionality 3.60 ± 0.82, aesthetics 3.53 ± 0.97, subjective quality 3.44 ± 1.21, engagement 3.40 ± 1.09.
