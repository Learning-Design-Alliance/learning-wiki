## Evidence

### Hutton John S 2018

Hutton John S, Huang Guixia, Phelan Kieran J, DeWitt Thomas, Ittenbach Richard F. (2018). Shared reading quality assessment by parental report: preliminary validation of the DialogPR. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1298-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the DialogPR in the combined cross-sectional sample of 49 mother-child dyads from two MRI-based studies. The article reports "internal consistency was acceptable to good at rCo-α = 0.82" with SEM = 2.0; no effect size is printed for this reliability finding.

> "For reliability, internal consistency was acceptable to good at rCo-α = 0.82. The DialogPR’s standard error of measurement, a measure of reproducibility of test scores, was estimated at SEM = 2.0."
