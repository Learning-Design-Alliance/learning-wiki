Sarac 2017¶

Sarac, H., & Tarhan, D. (2017). Effect of multimedia assisted 7e learning model applications on academic achievement and retention in students. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 299-311. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.3.299

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

One-way repeated-measures ANOVA on experiment-1's pre-test, post-test and retention scores (n=23). F(1.5-33.6)=69.353, p<.05, eta-squared .76; control-1's parallel ANOVA gave F(2-44)=22.783, p<.05, eta-squared .51. Bonferroni comparisons favored post-test and retention test over pre-test.

"there is a statistically significant difference between the test -1 group pre-test and the post-test and retention test averages according to the result of the one -way ANOVA, (F (1.5-33.6) = 69.353, p < .05). Also, the effect size between the average is .76."
