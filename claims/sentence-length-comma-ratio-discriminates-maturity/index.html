Evidence¶

C.E. Holdich 2002¶

C.E. Holdich, R.G. Holdich and P.W.H. Chung. (2002). Assessing aspects of children's written grammar: automating the process. Computers and Education, Vol 39, pp37-50. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/2.5/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Computer analysis of the 12 SATs stories: Level 2c-4c stories scored 11.2 to infinity, Level 4a-5b stories around 1, and Level 5a/5+ stories below 1. The authors state smaller numbers indicate 'a more frequent and secure use of commas'.

"The method of dividing the mean sentence lengt h by the number of commas was particularly useful for discriminating between the middle and higher levels, although again, deviations from expected scores indicated either a re lative strength, or weakness in punctuation for a particular writer."
