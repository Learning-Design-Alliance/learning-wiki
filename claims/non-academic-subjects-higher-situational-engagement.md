## Evidence

### Pöysä 2018

Pöysä, S., Vasalampi, K., Muotka, J., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2018). Variation in situation-specific engagement among lower secondary school students. Learning and Instruction, 53, 64-73. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.07.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired-comparison contrasts within the two-level multivariate model of lesson-to-lesson ratings found "home economics lessons or physical education lessons" differed from academic subjects on all three engagement factors, corroborating Shernoff et al.'s earlier experience-sampling findings.

> "Students rated their behavioral/cognitive engagement and emotional engagement higher and disaffection lower when attending home economics lessons or physical education lessons than when attending lessons of academic subjects."
