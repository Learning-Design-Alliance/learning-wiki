`q3 i?` Expert raters scored teams significantly better directly after PHTLS training (mean 69±7) than before (mean 31±8), and one year later (mean 66±13) scores remained better than before the course though slightly lower than immediately after. [→ Häske David 2018](#haske-david-2018)

## Evidence

### Häske David 2018

Häske David, Beckers Stefan K., Hofmann Marzellus, Lefering Rolf, Preiser Christine, Gliwitzky Bernhard, Grützner Paul Alfred, Stöckle Ulrich, Münzberg Matthias. (2018). Performance Assessment of Emergency Teams and Communication in Trauma Care (PERFECT checklist)—Explorative analysis, development and validation of the PERFECT checklist: Part of the prospective longitudinal mixed-methods EPPTC trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202795

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation review of 36 videos from three measuring points (t0, t1, t2) of a two-day PHTLS course, rated by six blinded experienced instructors using the newly developed checklist. Descriptive means show the improvement; the article reports the t1 difference as significant without printing an effect size or test statistic.

> "All experts rated the recorded scenarios at t0 with the lowest sum score (mean 31 ± 8), with a significantly better performance of the teams at t1 (mean 69 ± 7). At t2 the performance was still better (mean 66 ± 13) than at t0, but slightly lower than at t1."
