### Phuong Bui 2020

Phuong Bui, Gabriela Rodríguez-Aflecht, Boglárka Brezovszky, Minna M. Hannula-Sormunen, Samuli Laato, Erno Lehtinen. (2020). Understanding students' game experiences throughout the developmental process of the number navigation game. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09755-8

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with six fifth-grade students (three boys, three girls) after playing the NNG 4 prototype in 2016, analyzed with Braun and Clarke's thematic analysis in Nvivo. The theme of new extrinsic elements was "the biggest interests for participants".

> "All students showed excitement in having "their own island" (also "the village" or "city page") where they could construct buildings and purchase ships using the materials and coins."
