## Evidence

### Brouwers Melissa C 2011

Brouwers Melissa C, Makarski Julie, Durocher Lisa D, Levinson Anthony J. (2011). E-learning interventions are comparable to user's manual in a randomized trial of training strategies for the AGREE II. Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-81

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported time-on-task was compared across the three conditions by ANOVA (Table 5); for example, reading and rating the test PG averaged 70.50 minutes in the Tutorial arm and 75.55 in the control, p = 0.63. No effect sizes are printed.

> "There were no significant differences as a function of training condition in the time spent by participants reviewing either the PDF version of the AGREE II or in the time taken to complete the test PG (p > 0.05 for all comparisons)."

### Brouwers Melissa C 2011 (2)

Brouwers Melissa C, Makarski Julie, Durocher Lisa D, Levinson Anthony J. (2011). E-learning interventions are comparable to user's manual in a randomized trial of training strategies for the AGREE II. Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-81

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Discussion, the authors compare this study's pass rate with the initial pass rate in the separate project that developed the pass-fail algorithm, whose participants were trainees early in their post-graduate career with less experience in health methods or PGs.

> "In contrast to pass rates of 86% reported in this study, the initial pass rates for those participants was 73%, suggesting the training may be better suited for novice users."
