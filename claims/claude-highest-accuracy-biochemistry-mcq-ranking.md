## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was significant variation in correct responses among the four chatbots, with Claude best, GPT-4 and Gemini above the student average, and Copilot lowest and below the student average. [→ Bolgova O 2025](#bolgova-o-2025)

## Evidence

### Bolgova O 2025

Bolgova O, Shypilova I, Mavrych V. (2025). Large Language Models in Biochemistry Education: Comparative Evaluation of Performance. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67244

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Per-chatbot accuracy results from the 5-attempt testing of each chatbot on 200 biochemistry MCQs in August 2024, reported in the Results overview and Figure 1. Means and SDs are descriptive; the article reports "Copilot showed the lowest result (mean 64%, SD 0%)" and no between-chatbot effect size.

> "The best result was recorded for Claude (92.5%, SD 0%), followed by GPT-4 (mean 85.1%, SD 1%) and Gemini (mean 78.5%, SD 0%), which were better than the students’ average. Copilot showed the lowest result (mean 64%, SD 0%; Figure 1)."
