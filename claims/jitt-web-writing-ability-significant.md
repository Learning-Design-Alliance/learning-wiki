## Evidence

### Rewadee Chantoem & Saowalak Rattanavich 2016

Rewadee Chantoem & Saowalak Rattanavich. (2016). Just-In-Time Teaching Techniques through Web Technologies for Vocational Students' Reading and Writing Abilities. English Language Teaching, 9(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n1p65

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized true control-group pretest-posttest experiment with 40 Thai vocational students in two classes of 20, taught over ten weeks. Between-groups t-test showed "a significant difference in writing ability at the .01 level" favoring the JiTT group (post-test means 14.9 vs 12.7).

> "there was a significant difference in writing ability at the .01 level between the experimental and control groups ( t2 = 2.836**, p-value = .007 )"
