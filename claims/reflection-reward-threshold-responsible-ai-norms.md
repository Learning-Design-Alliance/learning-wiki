`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation results shown in Figure 1: stationary frequencies of four AI-use strategies computed from a small-mutation Markov chain over homogeneous states (N = 100, β = 0.1). The article reports that "a sharp transition occurs around a critical threshold of r ≈ 1.5". No effect size is printed.

> "As the reflection reward increases, a sharp transition occurs around a critical threshold of r ≈ 1.5. Beyond this point, responsible AI use coupled with meaningful reflection rapidly becomes the dominant strategy, while opportunistic AI use correspondingly collapses."
