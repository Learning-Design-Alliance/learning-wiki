Quasi-experimental cohort comparison of 186 first-year dental medicine students (TC n=93, EC n=93) using identical 40-item multiple-choice theory examinations. Observed means show "Mean theory scores were higher in the EC than in the TC" in both committees.

> "Mean theory scores were higher in the EC than in the TC in both Committee 3 (53.48 ± 18.85 vs. 46.25 ± 20.00) and Committee 4 (52.38 ± 22.67 vs. 39.66 ± 18.42)"
