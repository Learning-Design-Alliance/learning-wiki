## Evidence

### Qi Liu 2025

Qi Liu, Siyu Zhu, Can Liu, Yanmiao Yang, Chunmei Lan, Xinwei Song, Benjamin Becker, Keith M. Kendrick, Xinqi Zhou & Weihua Zhao. (2025). Neural signals gradually shift towards inferior frontal gyrus and long-term effects following facial imitation-based social learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00372-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioral analysis of 80 male participants' auto-decoded imitation performance (FaceReader) across 12 analyzed sessions of a 16-session FISL course; a two-way ANOVA found significant main effects of repetition and valence, with the quoted means showing the "greater area under the curve (AUC) for imitative learning performance of positive emotion".

> "with greater area under the curve (AUC) for imitative learning perfor- mance of positive emotion (mean ± SD = 2.77 ± 1.25) compared to negative ones (mean ± SD = 0.98 ± 0.37)"

### Qi Liu 2025 (2)
