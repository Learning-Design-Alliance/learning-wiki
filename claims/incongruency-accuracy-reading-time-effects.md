# Both text incongruency and knowledge inaccuracy produce increased reading times on target sentences

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Incongruent targets were read longer than congruent targets (d = .26) and false targets were read longer than true targets (d = .26). [→ Moort 2022](#moort-2022)
`q3 i0` An accuracy spill-over effect was observed, with longer reading times on spill-over sentences following false than true targets (d = .13), a negligible effect size. [→ Moort 2022 (2)](#moort-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Moort 2022

Moort, M. L. van, Koornneef, A. W., Wilderjans, T. F., & Broek, P. W. van den. (2022). Validation processes and reading purpose: Is validation against knowledge and prior text influenced by reading goal? Journal of Educational Psychology, 114(7), 1533-1552. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000769

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression on target-sentence reading times in the self-paced reading experiment showed "inconsistency effects of congruency" and "accuracy," both with d = .26, indicating that both text-internal incongruency and inaccuracy with background knowledge increased reading times.

> "we observed inconsistency effects of congruency (b = .08, SE = .01, t = 7.62, p , .001, d = .26) and accuracy (b = .12, SE = .01, t = 11.08, p , .001, d = .26), with longer reading times for incongruent (M = 2707 ms, SE = 27) than congruent (M = 2493 ms, SE = 25) targets and longer reading times for false (M = 2765 ms, SE = 28) than true targets (M = 2438 ms, SE = 24)"

### Moort 2022 (2)

Moort, M. L. van, Koornneef, A. W., Wilderjans, T. F., & Broek, P. W. van den. (2022). Validation processes and reading purpose: Is validation against knowledge and prior text influenced by reading goal? Journal of Educational Psychology, 114(7), 1533-1552. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000769

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

On spill-over sentences (sentence 9), mixed-effects regression showed "spill-over effects of accuracy" with d = .13, a negligible effect size, indicating that the processing disruption from knowledge inaccuracies extended into the next sentence after the target.

> "we observed spill-over effects of accuracy ( b = .06, SE = .01, t = 5.88, p , .001, d = .13), with longer reading times on spill-over sentences following"
