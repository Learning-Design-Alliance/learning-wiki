## Evidence

### Touran Ahour & Saeideh Entezari Maleki 2014

Touran Ahour & Saeideh Entezari Maleki. (2014). The Effect of Metadiscourse Instruction on Iranian EFL Learners' Speaking Ability. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p69

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test on the control group's (n = 17) PET speaking pretest and posttest scores, reported in Results section 3.4.4. Table 9 prints a paired-differences mean of -2.059; no effect size is printed, and equivalence was not tested.

> "As indicated in Tabl e 9, the sig. (2-tailed) was .068. It exceeded 0.05 level of significance. Therefore, no significant difference was proved between the pretest and posttest in the control group."
