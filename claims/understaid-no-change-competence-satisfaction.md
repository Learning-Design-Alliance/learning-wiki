### Núñez-Naveira L. 2016

UnderstAID, an ICT Platform to Help Informal Caregivers of People with Dementia: A Pilot Randomized Controlled Study. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2016/5726465

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "No change was observed in their feelings about the competence and satisfaction as caregivers, but this is coherent with the fact previously commented about their years of experience with the care of the relative with dementia."
