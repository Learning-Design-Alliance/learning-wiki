`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of mean Accuracy scores assigned by each of the four evaluators (Table 2). Evaluator 2's mean was 70.7 (16.4) versus 78–79.4 for the others; standard deviations remained "relatively consistent across all evaluators at 19% of mean Accuracy score."

> "there is a systematic difference in mean Accuracy score for Evaluator 2, one of the two electrophysiologists, whose scores tend to be lower, e.g., more critical, compared to the mean scores of the other three evaluators (ANOVA F = 7.20, p < 0.001)."
