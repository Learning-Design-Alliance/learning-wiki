# PF and DI groups showed no significant difference in climate declarative knowledge gains

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A two-way ANCOVA found no significant main effect of treatment group or teacher experience, and no significant interaction, on climate declarative knowledge, consistent with the non-directional hypothesis. [→ Jacobson 2017](#jacobson-2017)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2017

Jacobson, Michael, Markauskaite, Lina, Portolese, Alisha, Kapur, Manu, Lai, Polly, & Roberts, Gareth (2017). Designs for learning about climate change as a complex system. Learning and Instruction, 52, pp. 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA on climate declarative knowledge adjusting for pre-test scores, in the quasi-experiment with 110 Year Nine students. No significant effects were found; the article reports both groups improved but the improvement was not statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "For climate declarative knowledge, a two-way ANCOVA revealed that there were no significant main effects for either treatment group (F(1,105) = 1.652, p >.1, = .015) or level of teacher experience (F(1,105) = .006, p >.1, < .001)."
