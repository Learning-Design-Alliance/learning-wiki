## Evidence

### Harrison Neil Malcolm 2022

Harrison Neil Malcolm, Dennis Ashley. (2022). Developing an integrated national simulation-based educational programme for Scottish junior doctors through structured, multistep action research cycles. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-059229

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Step 5 of the process: the AHP numerically combined the graduate questionnaire usefulness scores and the clinical educators' significance scores, converted to proportions of 1, with equal weighting (figure 2).

> "It was felt that both criteria represented equally important factors influencing the focus of the intervention and therefore each were given a 50% influence on the combined score."
