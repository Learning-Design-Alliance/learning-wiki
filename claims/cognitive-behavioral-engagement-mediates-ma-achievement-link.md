## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For all three math anxiety dimensions, the indirect effect on math achievement through cognitive-behavioral engagement was significant, while emotional and social engagement did not mediate the association. [→ Quintero et al. 2022](#quintero-et-al-2022)

## Evidence

### Quintero et al. 2022

Michaela Quintero, Leslie Hasty, Tianyu Li, Seowon Song and Zhe Wang. (2022). A multidimensional examination of math anxiety and engagement on math achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12482

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three SEM mediation models (test, learning, and application MA) with 5,000-sample bias-corrected bootstrap confidence intervals. Figure 1 reports the test MA indirect effect via cognitive-behavioral engagement as β = -.24 [-.61, -.04]; Figures 2 and 3 report β = -.23 and β = -.22 for learning and application MA respectively.

> "The results showed that all three dimensions of MA indirectly predicted math achievement, which was mediated by cognitive-behavioral engagement. Neither emotional nor social engagement mediated the association between MA and math achievement."
