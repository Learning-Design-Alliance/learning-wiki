The report's framing statement describes the coverage limits of traditional annual state assessment growth measures as the motivation for examining alternatives. It states these measures "usually covers only reading and math and only in grades 4–8."

"But the typical measure of student growth—progress on state assessments from one school year to the next—usually covers only reading and math and only in grades 4–8."
