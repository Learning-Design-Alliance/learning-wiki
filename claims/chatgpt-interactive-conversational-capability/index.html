Evidence¶

Baidoo-Anu 2023¶

Baidoo-Anu, D., & Owusu Ansah, L. (2023). Education in the Era of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI): Understanding the Potential Benefits of ChatGPT in Promoting Teaching and Learning. Journal of AI, 7(1), 52-62. https://doi.org/10.61969/jai.1337500

q1 · i? · review · r1

The authors' exploratory demonstration: they prompted ChatGPT to explain itself, describe how it works, and discuss COVID-19's impact on pedagogy, concluding it "is very interactional" and produces convincing creative content. No systematic evaluation was conducted.

"the outcome clearly shows that ChatGPT is very interactional and capable of not only holding a realistic human -like conversation on a wide range of topics, but also it produces a convincing creative content."
