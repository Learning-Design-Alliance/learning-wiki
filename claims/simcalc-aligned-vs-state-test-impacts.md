The chapter reports the Scaling Up SimCalc randomized controlled trial (Roschelle, Shechtman, et al., 2010) as an example that proximal, curriculum-aligned measures show stronger impacts than distal accountability measures. No effect size is printed in the chapter.

> "researchers found significant positive impacts for assessment items related to the conceptual skills emphasized in the SimCalc mathematical inquiry environment, but no significant difference in students’ performance on items chosen from the relevant state test."
