Inductive thematic analysis of 200 coded responses to reflection questions Ref1 and Ref2 from students in the CS1 course. Themes included Easier/Simpler/Faster, Fun/Interesting, and "Supports Analyzing & Solving Problems"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The most frequently reported advantage was that it was Easier/Simpler/Faster, that is, using natural language made writing and modifying their code easier than traditional code writing."
