## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Contrary to the facilitators' initial worry, each breakout group developed a unique approach to integrating tools into a case-based session. [→ Spicer J. O. 2021](#spicer-j-o-2021)

## Evidence

### Spicer J. O. 2021

Spicer J. O., Nguyen T. T., Arnold M. W., Anderson T., Khalife R. (2021). A Faculty Development Workshop for Planning and Implementing Interactive Virtual Case-Based Teaching. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11126

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observation from the 15-minute small-group breakout sessions in which groups of four to six participants planned virtual interactive case-based sessions using the Breakout Session Worksheet.

> "Although we were initially worried that participants would all develop the same teaching plan in the breakout sessions, we found that each group developed a unique approach and provided different perspectives on the optimal tools for each portion of the case."
