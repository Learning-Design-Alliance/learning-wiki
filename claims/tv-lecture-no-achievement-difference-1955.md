Evans, Richard I.; Roney, H. Burr; and McAdams, Walter J. (1955). An Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Instruction and Audience Reaction to Programming an Educational Television Station. Journal of Applied Psychology, 39, 277-79. [doi:10.1037/h0042123](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0042123)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison at the University of Houston: in the psychology course 17 subjects took a TV-lecture only (correspondence) section, 96 a traditional campus lecture section, and 30 TV-lectures plus two weekly campus discussion periods; the biology course had matched traditional and TV-plus-discussion sections. The article reports "no significant differences betwee~ the sections" on standardized achievement tests; equivalence was not tested.
