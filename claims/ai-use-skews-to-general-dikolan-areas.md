## Evidence

### Nikolai Maurer 2026

Nikolai Maurer, Mathea Brückner, Christoph Thyssen, Sebastian Becker-Genschow, Johannes Huwer. (2026). Science educators' AI literacy and AI usage in teaching: Implications for post-qualification programs. Computers and Education Open 10. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100376

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of subject-level AI use across the DiKoLAN areas (nsubject = 115 data sets), distinguishing classroom use from lesson preparation and follow-up. The printed means show "M = 15 mentions" for subject-specific versus "M = 28.8 mentions" for general areas; ISE was most used and DAQ least.

> "Overall, AI is used much less frequently on average in the more subject-specific areas (SIM, DAP, DAQ; M = 15 mentions) than in the more general areas (COM, PRE, ISE, DOC, AFA; M = 28.8 mentions)."
