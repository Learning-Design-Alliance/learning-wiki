Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training satisfaction questionnaire in the 26-participant sample. "The mean score for the appropriateness of the online format was 9.12 (SD 1.14)"; 69.2% were very satisfied, 80.8% found the duration about right, and 92.3% found the content informative and clear.

> "The mean score for the appropriateness of the online format was 9.12 (SD 1.14), reflecting a high degree of appropriateness. The majority of participants (n = 17; 65.4%) completed the STP in one sitting, while 9 (34.6%) needed multiple sessions."
