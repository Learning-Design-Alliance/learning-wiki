Thematic analysis of 364 GAI interaction logs collected during the six-week experiment. Students used GAI "for knowledge acquisition (n = 220, 60.440%)" plus visualization and analytical support, suggesting GAI served as an external source of evidence and representational support for resolving disagreement.

> "Students most frequently used GAI for knowledge acquisition (n = 220, 60.440%), particularly to obtain factual information (n = 85) and basic conceptual explanations (n = 75). They also used GAI for visualization requests (n = 90, 24.725%) and analytical support (n = 30, 8.242%)"
