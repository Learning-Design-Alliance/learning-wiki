## Evidence

### Curtis Sally 2020

Curtis Sally, Smith Daniel. (2020). A comparison of undergraduate outcomes for students from gateway courses and standard entry medicine courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1918-y

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of EPM Normal Deviate scores at graduation across 3760 SEMED and 555 gateway cases from the UKMED cohort. Gateway students scored lower, "Cohen’s d = 0.692".

> "The mean EPM Normal Deviate score for SEMED students was −.0350 (SD = 1.121, N = 3760); for gateway students it was − 0.883 (SD = 1.591, N = 555) Cohen’s d = 0.692."

### Curtis Sally 2020 (2)

Curtis Sally, Smith Daniel. (2020). A comparison of undergraduate outcomes for students from gateway courses and standard entry medicine courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1918-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple regression (Model 2) predicting EPM deviate score from course type plus A-level and UCAT entry scores, N = 3350. The authors note "course type is still significant but only accounts for 1.6% of the variance", down from 6.4% in Model 1 (sr2 = 0.064). No effect size is printed for the regression finding.

> "EPM deviate model 2 (R2 adj = 0.094. N = 3350, P < 0.001) shows that course type is still significant but only accounts for 1.6% of the variance once the measures of attainment and aptitude on entry are included."
