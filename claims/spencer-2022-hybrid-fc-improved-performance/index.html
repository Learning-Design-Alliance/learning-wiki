Evidence¶

Yan Shen 2025¶

Yan Shen, Dan Spencer, Jennifer Tagsold, Hyunsong Kim. (2025). Integrating cognition, self-regulation, motivation, and metacognition: a framework of post-pandemic flipped classroom design. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10485-y

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Review's account of Spencer et al.'s (2022) blended/hybrid FC implementation in a college-level calculus course, which included an FC Orientation module, online lessons and assessments, in-class small-group problem solving, and independent after-class problems. The review reports performances and perceptions "improved significantly from the traditional lecture format"; no effect size is printed.
