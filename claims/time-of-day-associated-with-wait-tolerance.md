## Evidence

### Rogan A 2026

Rogan A, Watt E, Murphy S, Wheeler E, Woods L, Galwankar S, Peckler B. (2026). Worth the Wait? Comparison of Emergency Department Patients' Waiting Room Tolerance for Real Patient Care vs Training/Simulation Scenarios. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.48916

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis of 827 responses from the mixed-effects model. Responses between "midnight and 12 pm" were associated with selecting 0–20 minute waits; the paper separately reports that 6 pm–midnight respondents more often selected 101–120 minutes.

> "Those participants submitting a response between midnight and 12 pm were significantly more likely to select an appropriate wait time of 0–20 minutes compared to those submitting a response between 12 pm–6 pm, and between 6.pm to midnight."
