Evidence¶

Cheng Dazhi 2022¶

Cheng Dazhi, Shi Kaihui, Wang Naiyi, Miao Xinyang, Zhou Xinlin. (2022). Examining the Differential Role of General and Specific Processing Speed in Predicting Mathematical Achievement in Junior High School. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10010001

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression analysis of 212 junior high students tested at the start of Grade 7, controlling for age, gender, nonverbal matrix reasoning, spatial abilities, and visual attention. The article reports that these specific speed measures "accounted for 5.6%, 7.2%, and 5.1% of the variances in mathematics" with Bonferroni-corrected significance.

"Word semantic, simple subtraction, and complex subtraction accounted for 5.6%, 7.2%, and 5.1% of the variances in mathematics."
