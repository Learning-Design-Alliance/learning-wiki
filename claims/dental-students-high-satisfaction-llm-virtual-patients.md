Cross-sectional feasibility study of 41 second-year dental students in 8 groups completing postsession questionnaires via Microsoft Forms. Most Satisfaction items received a median of 5.00 (IQR 4.00-5.00) and Self-Confidence items a median of 4.00 (IQR 4.00-5.00), with Cronbach α=0.94 and α=0.95.

> "the median item scores on the Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning questionnaire were toward the upper end of the 1 to 5 scale. In the student satisfaction subscale (Figure 3), most items received a median score of 5.00 (IQR 4.00-5.00; the highest possible score)"
