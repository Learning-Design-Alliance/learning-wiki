## Evidence

### Ravishankar Chatta Subramaniam 2025

Ravishankar Chatta Subramaniam, Nikhil Borse, Winter Allen, Amogh Sirnoorkar, Jason W. Morphew, Carina M. Rebello, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Applying a STEM Ways of Thinking Framework for Student-generated Engineering Design-based Physics Problems. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010122](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010122)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of transcripts and reports from 14 teams addressing RQ2 (design, science, mathematics, and metacognitive thinking), coded via a two-stage scheme with team-based consensus coding. The authors identify these four areas as needing structured support. No effect size is reported.

> "structured support for assessing design limitations, engaging in a feasibility study, adopting a systematic approach to applying physics and mathematics in their iterations, and engaging in metacognitive reflection"
