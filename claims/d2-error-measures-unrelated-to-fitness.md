### Reigal RE 2020

Reigal RE, Moral-Campillo L, Juárez-Ruiz de Mier R, Morillo-Baro JP, Morales-Sánchez V, Pastrana JL and Hernández-Mendo A (2020) Physical Fitness Level Is Related to Attention and Concentration in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the full sample (Table 2) found the accuracy/consistency measures of the d2 test unrelated to fitness: "the omissions, commissions and index of variation (D2 test) were not related to any of the physical fitness variables." No effect sizes are printed for these null relationships.
