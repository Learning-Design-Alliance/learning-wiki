Moderation analysis using Hayes (2017) process macro model 58 on the survey data. Incorporating technostress raised R² in EXP from 0.59 to 0.65 and in EP from 0.43 to 0.51; H6a–H6d were all supported.

"Technostress★CGB→EXP=-0.14 (significant at 0.05 level), Technostress★SIB→EXP=-0.10 (significant at 0.05 level), Technostress★PIB→EXP =-0.12 (significant at 0.05 level), and Technostress★AB→EXP =-0.13 (significant at 0.05 level) all have negative directions."
