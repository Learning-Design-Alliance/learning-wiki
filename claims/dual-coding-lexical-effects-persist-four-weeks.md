# The advantage of dual coding elucidation for correct lexical usage persists over a four-week delay

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The pictorial group significantly outperformed the verbal group on both the immediate posttest (d = .50) and the four-week delayed posttest (d = .40), despite a significant coding-by-time interaction reflecting score decline. [→ Lee 2017](#lee-2017)

## Evidence

### Lee 2017

Lee, Sunjung. (2017). Raising EFL learners' awareness of L2 lexical errors and correct usage: A dual coding approach. English Teaching, 72(2), 29-50. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.72.2.201706.29

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up mean comparisons after a significant coding × time interaction, F(1,132) = 4.80, p = .030, ηp² = .04, showed the pictorial advantage held at both intervals; delayed means fell to 7.48 (pictorial) and 5.74 (verbal) of 25, with "t(133) = 2.33, p = .021, d = .40".

> "On the immediate test, there was a significant mean difference between the pictorial and verbal group, t(133) = 2.90, p = .004, d = .50. On the delayed test, there was a  significant mean difference between the pictorial and verbal group, t(133) = 2.33, p = .021, d = .40."
