# Gamified exergaming shows no intrinsic-motivation harm: intrinsic motivation and amotivation changed adversely only in the control group, and external regulation fell only in the experimental group

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Quintas 2020

Quintas, A., Bustamante, J.-C., Pradas, F., & Castellar, C. (2020). Psychological effects of gamified didactics with exergames in Physical Education at primary schools: results from a natural experiment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103874

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Simple ANOVA follow-up on intrinsic motivation (PLOC scale) within each treatment group of the natural experiment; the control group declined significantly with η p2=.033 (a small effect) while the experimental group did not change significantly.

> "Performing a more specific analysis in each group using simple ANOVA (Post-pre) showed that the control group displayed less  intrinsic motivation with time (F(1)=4.22, p=.042, η p2=.033). The experimental group did not significantly d isplay intrinsic motivation (F(1)=.54, p=.461, η p2=.003)."

### Quintas 2020 (2)

Quintas, A., Bustamante, J.-C., Pradas, F., & Castellar, C. (2020). Psychological effects of gamified didactics with exergames in Physical Education at primary schools: results from a natural experiment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103874

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Simple ANOVA follow-up on external regulation; the hypothesised increase in external regulation under gamification (H2) was not supported because it decreased significantly in the experimental group with ηp2=.032 (a small effect).

> "external regulation significantly decreased in the experimental group with time (F(1)=6.21, p=.014, ηp2=.032), and it increased in the control group, but not significantly (F(1)=.375, p=.541, η p2=.003)."

### Quintas 2020 (3)

Quintas, A., Bustamante, J.-C., Pradas, F., & Castellar, C. (2020). Psychological effects of gamified didactics with exergames in Physical Education at primary schools: results from a natural experiment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103874

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Simple ANOVA follow-up on amotivation within each group; the article reports amotivation rose significantly in the control group with ηp2=.025 (a small effect) but not in the experimental group, so H3 was not supported as an interaction effect.

> "Specifically, the control group displayed increased amotivation with time (F(1)=8.32, p=.004, ηp2=.025), but the experimental group did not (F(1)=2.76, p=.014, η p2=.014)."
