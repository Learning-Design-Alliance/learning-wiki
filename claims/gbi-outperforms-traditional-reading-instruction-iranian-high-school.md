## Evidence

### Rezvan Adelnia 2016

Rezvan Adelnia, Hadi Salehi. (2016). Improving Iranian High School Students' Reading Comprehension Using the Tenets of Genre Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.4p.187

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 40 experimental and 40 control participants after 10 sessions of instruction on the same 10 descriptive passages. The independent-samples t-test in Table 6 reports t = 2.248, df = 78, Sig. = .027, mean difference 7.07500. The article reports "a significant difference between the performance of the control and experimental groups in reading posttest".

> "According to Table 5, in the posttest, the mean score of the participants in the experimental group is 67.50 and in the control group is 60.42, showing a mean difference of 7.08 which seems to be significant."
