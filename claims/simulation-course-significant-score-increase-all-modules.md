## Evidence

### Siaulys Monica Maria 2019

Siaulys Monica Maria, da Cunha Lissandra Borba, Torloni Maria Regina, Kondo Mario Macoto. (2019). Obstetric emergency simulation training course: experience of a private-public partnership in Brazil. Reproductive Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12978-019-0689-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-experimental before-and-after study of 340 professionals who completed both tests, comparing pre- and post-course MCQ scores with Wilcoxon and Friedman tests; the article reports "a significant increase in post-course test scores in all four modules" with percent score change as the measure. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant increase in post-course test scores in all four modules (p < 0.001). On average, scores increased 55% in the hypertension and 65–69% in the hemorrhage, sepsis and resuscitation modules."
