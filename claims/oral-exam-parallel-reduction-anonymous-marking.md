Hinton Daniel P., Higson Helen. (2017). A large-scale examination of the effectiveness of anonymous marking in reducing group performance differences in higher education assessment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182711

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' observation comparing regression results across assessment modes in the archival dataset. They offer three possible explanations: improved teaching quality, heightened assessor awareness of unconscious bias, and students' perception of procedural justice.
