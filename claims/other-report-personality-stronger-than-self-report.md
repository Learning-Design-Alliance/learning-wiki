# Other-reports of teacher personality are more strongly associated with teacher outcomes than self-reports

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For every Big Five domain, the other-reported subgroup shows a larger pooled correlation with outcomes than the self-reported subgroup (e.g., extraversion: other-reported r = 0.37 vs self-reported r = 0.09). [→ Lisa E. Kim 2019](#lisa-e-kim-2019)

## Evidence

### Lisa E. Kim 2019

Lisa E. Kim, Verena Jörg & Robert M. Klassen. (2019). A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Teacher Personality on Teacher Effectiveness and Burnout. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9458-2

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Source-of-report moderator analysis in Table 2 of the meta-analysis: for extraversion, other-reported personality pools k = 7 effect sizes at r = 0.37 (95% CI 0.19–0.53), versus a self-reported pooled r of 0.09 (95% CI 0.02–0.15). The same direction holds for all five domains.
