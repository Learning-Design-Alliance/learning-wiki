## Evidence

### Kurup Chanchal 2026

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Facilitators analysis (n = 53 codes) found managerial/workplace support coded 30 times (57%) and IQN community/agency support 14 times (26%); managers linked valuing staff and utilising skills to retention.

> "Managers emphasised that strong support systems are central to successful integration."

### Kurup Chanchal 2026 (2)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Skill transfer strategies were coded 9 times (17% of n = 53); managers recommended free CPD courses in employment packages and fair career-planning discussions to develop IQNs' areas of interest.

> "Participants highlighted targeted strategies such as continuous professional development, mentorship, and career planning as essential to maximising IQNs' contributions."
