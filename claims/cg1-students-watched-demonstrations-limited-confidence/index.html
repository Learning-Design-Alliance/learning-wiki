Evidence¶

Zeynep Tatli & Alipasa Ayas 2012¶

Zeynep Tatli & Alipasa Ayas. (2012). Virtual Chemistry Laboratory: Effect of Constructivist Learning Environment. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 13(1). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of semi-structured interviews with CG-I students (Table 5). The article reports that because the teacher acted as a guide during experimenting they did not develop self-confidence to perform experiments (50%) and had limited chance to relate experiments with daily life (50%).

"CG-I students have indicated that their teacher has done most of the time the experiment as a demonstration (%62.5), sometime they had chance to do the experiment by themselves (25%)."
