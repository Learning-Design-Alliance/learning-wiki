q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Spearman correlation analysis of session-level logs from a 60-minute in-person lab study in which 55 CS1 students completed the rainfall problem in Clover. The analysis found students with high tab accept rates "were far more likely to fail attention checks (𝜌= 0.73,𝑝< 0.001)", an effect size of rho = 0.73, and had lower average dwell time (rho = -0.47).

"Furthermore, students whoTAR were far more likely to fail attention checks (𝜌= 0.73,𝑝< 0.001). TARwas also strongly negatively correlated withADT, (𝜌=− 0.47)"
