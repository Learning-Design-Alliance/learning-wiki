### Sang-Yoon Chang 2022

Sang-Yoon Chang, Kay Yoon, Simeon Wuthier, and Kelei Zhang. (2022). Capture the Flag for Team Construction in Cybersecurity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

A qualitative analysis of the two student teams' written assignment submissions in the Virtual Teams course. The teams stated the initial role assignment was "an accurate representation of their team members’ expertise"; no quantitative effect size is reported.

> "In their qualitative reflections on the CTF exercise and the team discussion for the course assignment, both teams indicated that the initial role assignment based on the CTF performance was an accurate representation of their team members’ expertise. One team made no adjustment to their initial role assignment, while the other team made only one role swap between two members."
