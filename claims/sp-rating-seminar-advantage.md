## Evidence

### Lauerer Elias 2021

Lauerer Elias, Tiedemann Elena, Polak Thomas, Simmenroth Anne. (2021). Can smoking cessation be taught online? A prospective study comparing e-learning and role-playing in medical education. International Journal of Medical Education, 12, 12-21. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5ff9.bccc

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Subjective global rating by standardised patients on a five-level Likert scale in the same OSCE. The authors state the SP rating is an important parameter to improve the quality and reliability of the OSCE assessment process.

> "The SPs rated counselling and motivational skills significantly better in the attendance-course group (M=4.7) than in the web-based group (M=4.2), t(27.836)=2.0, p=.028, d= 0.67."
