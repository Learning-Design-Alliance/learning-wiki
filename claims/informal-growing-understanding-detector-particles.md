### I. Andrews 2018

I. Andrews, K. Nikolopoulos. (2018). Introducing particle physics concepts through visual art. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aad276](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aad276)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal evaluation through one-to-one discussions during the pilot workshops with KS4 students. The authors report that "Students have shown growing understanding regarding detector operation, the families of particles, and the properties of the force carriers"; a word cloud of responses to "Describe one thing that you learnt today" showed quark appearing prominently. No test of learning is reported; the study's quantitative measures are student feedback (4 in 5 reported more interest, 35% said they were more likely to study physics at university, and 75% found physics more accessible).

> "During the workshop learning was evaluated informally through one-to-one discussions. Students have shown growing understanding regarding detector operation, the families of particles, and the properties of the force carriers."
