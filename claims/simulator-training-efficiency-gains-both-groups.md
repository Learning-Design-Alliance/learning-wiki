Impact of educational level on medical students’ ability to learn abdominal point-of-care ultrasonography: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09057-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group comparison of the simulator's basic-skills hand–eye coordination exercises; "Both groups showed a significant improvement between the first and second hand–eye coordination exercises", with paired t-tests significant at P < 0.01.

> "Both groups showed a significant improvement between the first and second hand–eye coordination exercises, as evidenced by reductions in total exercise time, average time across different stages, and the number of times assistance was required (paired t-tests, P < 0.01)."
