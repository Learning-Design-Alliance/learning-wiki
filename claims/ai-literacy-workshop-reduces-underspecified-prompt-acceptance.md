# A two-hour AI literacy workshop made middle-school students less likely to accept underspecified suggested prompts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intervention students accepted suggested prompts less often than controls overall (OR = 0.47, p = .022), with the difference concentrated on underspecified prompts (51.5% vs. 66.7%). [→ Olivier Clerc 2026](#olivier-clerc-2026)

## Evidence

### Olivier Clerc 2026

Teaching Students to Question the Machine: Short-Term Effects of an AI Literacy Workshop on Middle-School Students’ Regulation of LLM Interaction. ALIT4ALL 2026: 2nd International Workshop on AI Literacy Education For All. https://osf.io/fyu7c/overview

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Logistic GLM with cluster-robust standard errors in the quasi-experimental study (N = 116, grades 8–9) predicting prompt acceptance from condition, specification, and confidence. The follow-up contrast showed "intervention students accepted these prompts less often than controls (51.5% vs. 66.7%; OR = 0.47,𝑝=.022 )". No effect size beyond the OR was printed.

> "intervention students accepted these prompts less often than controls (51.5% vs. 66.7%; OR = 0.47,𝑝=.022 ), whereas groups did not differ reliably for well-specified prompts (74.4% vs. 79.2%;𝑝=.385)."
