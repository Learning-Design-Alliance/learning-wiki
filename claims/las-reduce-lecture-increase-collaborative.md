## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2020

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen. (2020). Associations between learning assistants, passing introductory physics, and equity: A quantitative critical race theory investigation. Physical Review Physics Education Research 16, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.16.010117

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

An online survey with open-ended questions completed by five of the nine LA-using faculty asked whether LAs changed how they taught. Responses were "unanimous" that LAs allowed reduced lecturing and increased collaborative activities; details varied by instructor.

> "The responses were unanimous in stating that in the semesters they had LAs, the LAs allowed them to reduce the time spent lecturing and increase use of collaborative learning activities."
