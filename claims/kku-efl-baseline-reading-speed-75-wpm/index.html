Evidence¶

Kana'an et al. 2014¶

Basim Hamdan Ibrahim Kana'an, Salahud Din Abdul Rab & Ahlullah Siddiqui. (2014). The Effect of Expansion of Vision Span on Reading Speed: A Case Study of EFL Major Students at King Khalid University. English Language Teaching, 7(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p57

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline descriptive measurement of the sample's reading speed, reported in the Results section and the conclusions, where the authors state the average speed of KKU EFL undergraduates "is 75 wpm with approximately 70% comprehension". Descriptive figures only; no inferential test.

"Jordan (1997, p. 17) considers a reading speed of less than 200 wpm as slow, about 250 wpm as average and above 300 wpm as fast. In the context of EFL students of King Khalid University, however, the reading speed is only 75 wpm."
