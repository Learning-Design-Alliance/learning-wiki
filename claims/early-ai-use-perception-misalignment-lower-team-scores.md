## Evidence

### Laura Graf 2026

Laura Graf, Ramona Beinstingel, Stephan Krusche, Oleksandra Poquet. (2026). Students' Perception Accuracy of Partners' AI Use and its Relation to Collaboration Performance. Preprint, Technical University of Munich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23237

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Observational three-wave survey study of 103 student pairs in an introductory programming course; bivariate association between team-level misalignment at t1 and the instructor-assigned project grade (M = 95.9, SD = 5.1). The article prints an effect size for this finding: "the Spearman correlation ρ = −.201 (p = .03)". Robust regression confirmed the association controlling for prior performance.

> "Teams whose members had higher misalignment in their AI use perceptions at the beginning of the project tended to receive lower scores, as captured by the Spearman correlation ρ = −.201 (p = .03) between early perception misalignment (|d| at t1) and the project score."
