## Evidence

### Gehrke-Beck Sabine 2025

Gehrke-Beck Sabine, Holzhausen Ylva. (2025). Entrustment of student supervision to GP trainees - a qualitative interview study of factors influencing entrustment decisions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07870-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on task characteristics. A GP found it "a little easier with those who are in their final year or who come to us for a block placement", and longer tests like the Mini Mental or DemTect were delegated because trainees "often have more time for longer things".

> "Entrustment also depended to some extent on the characteristics of the student, whom the trainee should supervise."
