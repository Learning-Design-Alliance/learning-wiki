Making sense of quantum teleportation: An intervention study on students' conceptions using a diagrammatic approach. arXiv:2511.21443 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2025. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of exam and group-work data showed a student 'conflates the mathematical rule of how boxes are allowed to move across wires in QPic' with physical movement of the state box; similar confusions appeared as students traced wires with their fingers.

> "Here, the student is drawing upon concepts that had been introduced throughout the intervention, but conflates the mathematical rule of how boxes are allowed to move across wires in QPic with a physical movement of the state box in 'reality.'"
