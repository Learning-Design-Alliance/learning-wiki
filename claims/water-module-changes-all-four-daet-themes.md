# First-year students in the integrative 'Water Module' course showed changes in all four Draw-an-Engineer-Test themes, unlike both control groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 'Water Module' treatment group was the only group of students who showed a change in all four themes (image, engineering process, humanities and social science integration, and types of knowledge) between pre- and post-test drawings. [→ Singer 2015](#singer-2015)

## Evidence

### Singer 2015

Singer, K. P., Foutz, T., Navarro, M., & Thompson, S. (2015). Investigating The Extent That An Integrative Learning Module Broadens The Perception Of First-Year Students About The Engineering Profession. American Journal of Engineering Education, 6(2), 99-110. https://www.cluteinstitute.com/ojs/index.php/AJEE/article/view/9603

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of pre/post Draw-an-Engineer-Test drawings from eight consenting students in the treatment group, coded by a Ph.D. candidate and reviewed by the research team for triangulation. The article reports that "Five of the eight students in this group changed their perception of the Image of an engineer" and that the group was the only one to change on all four themes.

> "The “Water Module” group was the only set of students who showed a change in all four themes (Table 1). Five of the eight students in this group changed their perception of the Image of an engineer shifting away from a “skilled” technical worker depiction toward a professional and business oriented person."
