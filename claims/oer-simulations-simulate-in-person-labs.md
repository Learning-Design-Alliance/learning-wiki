## Evidence

### Haldolaarachchige 2021

Haldolaarachchige, N., & Hettiarachchilage, K. (2021). A Set of Virtual Experiments of Fluids, Waves, Thermodynamics, Optics, and Modern Physics for Virtual Teaching of Introductory Physics. Preprint. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2101.00993](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2101.00993)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors state the design basis of the manual in the abstract: "All the virtual experiments were based on open education  resource (OER) type simulations." Each experiment section then names a specific OER simulation, such as thephysicsaviary, ophysics, olabs, PhET, and a Pearson calorimetry simulator, with linked lesson videos for data collection and Excel analysis.

> "All the virtual experiments were based on open education  resource (OER) type simulations. Virtual experiments were designed to simulate in -person physical lab experiments."
