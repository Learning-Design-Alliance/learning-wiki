Evidence¶

Şen 2017¶

Şen, C., & Güler, G. (2017). Effect of Strategy Teaching For the Solution of Ratio Problems on Students' Proportional Reasoning Skills. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 5(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1142503

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of which strategies experimental and control students used on PRT questions after the intervention (Table 8). Unit ratio was used 46.8% of the time in the experimental group and 30.2% in the control group; the authors note it "is an intuitive strategy".

"It was determined that students more frequently used the equivalent fractions creation strategy in the questions included in the comparison-elongation problem type. In the answers given to part-part-whole and related groups problem types, it was seen that unit ratio and cross-multiplication strategies were more frequently used."
