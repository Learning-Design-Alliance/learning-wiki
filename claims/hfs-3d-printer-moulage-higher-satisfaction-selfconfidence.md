## Evidence

### Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin 2026

Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin. (2026). The Effect of 3D Printer and Moulage Use on Improving Stoma Care Knowledge and Skills in Nursing Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.71068

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison on the Satisfaction with Current Learning subscale (Cronbach's alpha 0.941). "the satisfaction score of those in the experimental group regarding current learning is higher than those in the control group" (p = 0.019, r = 0.269).

> "A statistically significant difference was found between the students' satisfaction scores regarding current learning according to the groups. (p = 0.019) This difference is due to the mean ranks of the current satisfaction scores according to the groups. It is seen that the satisfaction score of those in the experimental group regarding current learning is higher than those in the control group."

### Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin 2026 (2)

Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin. (2026). The Effect of 3D Printer and Moulage Use on Improving Stoma Care Knowledge and Skills in Nursing Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.71068

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison on the Self-Confidence in Learning subscale (Cronbach's alpha 0.909). "The self‐confidence scores in learning were higher in the experimental group than in the control group" (p = 0.001, r = 0.378).

> "There is a statistically significant difference between the participants' self‐confidence scores in learning according to the groups. (p < 0.001) This difference is due to the mean ranks of the current satisfaction scores according to the groups. The self‐confidence scores in learning were higher in the experimental group than in the control group."
