Analysis of MCQ and distractor use in a large first year Health Faculty Foundation Program: assessing the effects of changing from five to four options. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pooled 4 × 2 mixed model ANOVA of DF and DI across all courses, with course and offering as main effects. The analysis found "there was no significant difference in DI" (p = .26) or DF (p = .58) for the main effect of offering; the course × offering interaction was also not significant (DI p = .533; DF p = .10).
