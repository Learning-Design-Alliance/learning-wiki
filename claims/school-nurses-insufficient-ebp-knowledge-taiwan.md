## Evidence

### Hsieh Pei-Lin 2018

Hsieh Pei-Lin, Chen Sue-Hsien, Chang Li-Chun. (2018). School Nurses' Perceptions, Knowledge, and Related Factors Associated with Evidence-Based Practice in Taiwan. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15091845

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 1200 elementary school nurses in Taiwan (45% response rate). Descriptive results show "The mean score in the knowledge section was 2.88 (SD = 1.08)", with 94.7% answering the concept item correctly but only 10.7% the meta-analysis item and 17.3% the evidence-levels item.

> "The mean score in the knowledge section was 2.88 (SD = 1.08), indicating that school nurses had insufficient EBP knowledge. The item with the highest score (94.7%) was "The concept of EBP means helping nursing staff identify problems and develop problem-solving abilities,""
