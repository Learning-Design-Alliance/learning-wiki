### Hautz SC 2015

Hautz SC, Hautz WE, Keller N, Feufel MA, Spies C. (2015). The scholar role in the National Competence Based Catalogues of Learning Objectives for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) compared to other international frameworks. GMS German Medical Science. [doi:10.3205/000224](https://doi.org/10.3205/000224)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the framework comparison, the authors report lifelong learning "described in the NKLM on a level much less abstract", contrasting general international statements with NKLM objectives on recognising and judging one's own state of development and taking appropriate measures.

> "The ability for lifelong learning is described in the NKLM on a level much less abstract then in the other included frameworks."
