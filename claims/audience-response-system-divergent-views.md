`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the combined faculty and resident focus groups on audience response systems. A faculty member said the system "makes it safe to respond"; a resident questioned its engagement value, saying "I don't know how dynamic it is". Perceptual data only; no learning outcome was measured.

> "I guess it's better than having no participation. I mean, it's some interaction. I don't know how dynamic it is"
