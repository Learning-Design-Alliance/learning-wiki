## Evidence

### Wolfram 2023

Wolfram, T. & Morris, D. (2023). Conventional twin studies overestimate the environmental differences between families relevant to educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 8, 24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00173-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model fitting across six NTFD baseline models (three phenotypic assortment, three social homogamy) on TwinLife twin, parent, and sibling data; the ASTE model had the lowest AIC and BIC. Results report "A = 51% (46 –56%), S = 10% (0.1 –18%), T = 16% (8 –26%), and E = 23% (21–26%)".

> "Our best ﬁtting model overall, reporting both the lowest Akaike's Information Criterion and lowest Bayesian Information Criterion 43,44, was the PA ASTE model where A = 51% (46 –56%), S = 10% (0.1 –18%), T = 16% (8 –26%), and E = 23% (21–26%)."
