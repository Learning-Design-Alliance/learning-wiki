Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the cluster randomized controlled trial, the teacher-led strategy showed no statistically significant improvement in any outcome index after a single OHE session, unlike dentist-led and peer-led strategies.

> "The differences between baseline and evaluation I scores of all indices related to the oral health knowledge, behavior and oral hygiene status of adolescents in case of TL strategy were statistically insignificant."
