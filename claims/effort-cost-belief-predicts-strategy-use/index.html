Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this correlational finding from research by Hui and colleagues (2022), attributing it to that study: learners limited effort regulation toward desirable difficulties because they experienced the costs of effort as too high.

"learners' effort ratings directly predicted their use of the desirable difficulty retrieval practice, and perceived learning did not appear to play a role."
