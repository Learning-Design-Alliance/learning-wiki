## Evidence

### van Leest 2021

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School‐level mediation models showed a direct SES effect for both recommendation types, indicating that schools with similar performance but different populations provided different track recommendations.

> "a direct effect of SES (path c') on track recommendations at school level was reported for both recommendations (test‐based recommendations: b  = .26, p < .001; judgement‐based recommendations: b  = .25, p < .001)"
