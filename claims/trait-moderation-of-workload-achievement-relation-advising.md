`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Case Study #1 synthesizes a trait-aware advising pattern from course-planning research using registrar enrollment transactions, workload estimates, opt-in trait short forms, and "preregistered linear mixed-effects models with student random intercepts and trait×workload interactions"; the article reports the three-way interaction as significant without printing a test statistic.
