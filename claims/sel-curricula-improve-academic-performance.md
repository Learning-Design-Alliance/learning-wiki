### Sheppard 2015

Sheppard, S. M. (2015). Supporting Research-Based Personalization for Reading Success. Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The paper's SEL section reports, citing prior studies, that SEL curricula strengthen students' SEL skills with downstream academic benefits. The paper adds that students with strong SEL skills often have improved attitudes, encounter less emotional distress, and display fewer conduct problems. No effect sizes are printed.
