## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At T1 the SCA-trained group scored significantly higher than the ACR group on the ACS scenario (13,25±2,62 vs 10,30±3,51, p=0,03). [→ Ghazali Hanen 2022](#ghazali-hanen-2022)

## Evidence

### Ghazali Hanen 2022

Ghazali Hanen, Ouaness Islem, Zaouch Khedija, Hamed Rym, Ines Chermiti, Manai Hela. (2022). Skill retention following simulation learning in acute coronary syndrome versus cardiac arrest. La Tunisie Médicale. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9703463/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison at one month on the ACS scenario among the 22 participants re-evaluated: the trained group remained "significativement supérieure" (p=0,03).

> "La moyenne générale du groupe SCA ST+ formé sur simulateur était significativement supérieure à celle du groupe ACR : 13,25± 2,62 vs. 10,30 ± 3,51, p=0,03 respectivement."

### Ghazali Hanen 2022 (2)

Ghazali Hanen, Ouaness Islem, Zaouch Khedija, Hamed Rym, Ines Chermiti, Manai Hela. (2022). Skill retention following simulation learning in acute coronary syndrome versus cardiac arrest. La Tunisie Médicale. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9703463/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison at one month on the cardiac-arrest scenario: the trained group's mean remained numerically higher but "sans différence significative" (p=0,602), showing loss of the immediate advantage.

> "La moyenne générale du groupe ACR formé sur simulateur était supérieure à celle du groupe SCA ST+ mais sans différence significative : 13,96 ± 2,41 vs. 13,33± 2,94; p=0,602 respectivement."
