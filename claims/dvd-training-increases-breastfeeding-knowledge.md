## Evidence

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial in three Zhejiang hospitals; 96 intervention and 95 control clinicians completed pre- and post-DVD knowledge assessments (mini-CUBA, range 0–12). The intervention group rose from "M = 5.39" to "M = 7.74" while the control group did not change significantly.

> "The total knowledge score significantly increased post-DVD for IG (pre-DVD: M = 5.39, SD = 2.03; post-DVD: M = 7.74, SD = 1.71; t (95) = − 10.95, p < 0.01), but no significant change in total knowledge score for CG between pre- and post-DVD"
