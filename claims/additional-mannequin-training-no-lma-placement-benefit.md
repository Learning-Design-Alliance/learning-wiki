## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intervention students receiving approximately 20 minutes of additional mannequin training were no more likely than controls to receive an overall placement rating of > 3/5 on their first patient (37% vs 48%, X2 = 0.81, p = 0.37). [→ Laiou Elpiniki 2011](#laiou-elpiniki-2011)

## Evidence

### Laiou Elpiniki 2011

Laiou Elpiniki, Clutton-Brock Thomas H, Lilford Richard J, Taylor Celia A. (2011). The effects of laryngeal mask airway passage simulation training on the acquisition of undergraduate clinical skills: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-57

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial in which 78 completing Year 4 medical students were assessed by blinded clinical instructors on their first real-patient LMA placement; the primary outcome was the instructors' 5-point overall success rating. The trial reports "no difference in the percentage of participants achieving a rating of > 3".

> "There was no difference in the percentage of participants achieving a rating of > 3 between the intervention and control groups (37% Vs. 48%, X2 = 0.81, p = 0.37)."
