## Evidence

### Brender 2025

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Giang, C., Mathex, L., Käser, T., & Mondada, F. (2025). The handwriting programming language for primary school: aligning computer science education with established teaching practices. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10503-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student perception survey administered during the last lesson, adapted from the technology acceptance model survey of Teo (2009), covering usefulness, ease of use, attitude/interest, complexity, self-efficacy and behavioural intention. The abstract reports that "students perceived HPL as positively as Scratch"; the article states the pre- and post-test scores and questionnaire responses were compared between groups through a Kruskal-Wallis non-parametric one-way ANOVA.
