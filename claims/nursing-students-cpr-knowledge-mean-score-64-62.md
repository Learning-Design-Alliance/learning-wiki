### Vural Mutlu 2017

Vural Mutlu, Koşar Mustafa Feridun, Kerimoğlu Orhan, Kızkapan Fatih, Kahyaoğlu Serdar, Tuğrul Sevil, İşleyen Hasan Burak. (2017). Cardiopulmonary resuscitation knowledge among nursing students: a questionnaire study. Anatolian Journal of Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.14744/AnatolJCardiol.2016.7156

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of randomly selected probationer nursing students in Turkey, scored against 2010 AHA guidelines. The mean was "64.62±17.84 out of 100 points", with 47.5% good, 18.3% excellent, and 34.2% poor.

> "The mean score was 25.2±6.96 for 39 questions. It was estimated as 64.62±17.84 out of 100 points. Total scores are converted into percentages and divided into pre-fixed grades as follows: >80 as excellent (18.3%), 60–80 good (47.5%), and <60 poor (34.2%)."
