## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Undergraduates with K6 scores of 13–24 in the first semester had a significantly higher incident risk of poor academic performance than those scoring 0–4, with an adjusted HR of 1.62 (1.18–2.23). [→ Chu 2023](#chu-2023)

## Evidence

### Chu 2023

Chu, T., Liu, X., Takayanagi, S., Matsushita, T., & Kishimoto, H. (2023). Association between mental health and academic performance among university undergraduates: The interacting role of lifestyle behaviors. International Journal of Methods in Psychiatric Research, 32(1), e1938. https://doi.org/10.1002/mpr.1938

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population-based prospective Cox proportional hazards analysis of 1823 Japanese undergraduates followed 4 years, adjusting for sex, major field of study, and lifestyle behavior risk scores; the adjusted "HR (95%CI) for poor academic performance was 1.62 (1.18–2.23)".

> "the incident risk of poor academic performance in the 13–24‐point K6 group was also significantly higher than that in the 0–4‐point K6 group, and the HR (95%CI) for poor academic performance was 1.62 (1.18–2.23)"
