# Overall U.S. teacher turnover is about 16% annually, with about 8% leaving the profession and about 8% moving schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About 8% of teachers leave the profession each year and about 8% shift schools, for an overall turnover rate of about 16%. [→ Carver-Thomas 2017](#carver-thomas-2017)

## Evidence

### Carver-Thomas 2017

Carver-Thomas, D. & Darling-Hammond, L. (2017). Teacher turnover: Why it matters and what we can do about it. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-turnover

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the nationally representative Schools and Staffing Survey (2011–12) and Teacher Follow-Up Survey (2012–13), reporting that "about 8% shift schools" and attrition is about 8%, yielding roughly 16% total turnover.

> "In addition to the 8% of teachers who leave the profession each year, about 8% shift schools. Thus, the overall turnover rate is currently about 16%."
