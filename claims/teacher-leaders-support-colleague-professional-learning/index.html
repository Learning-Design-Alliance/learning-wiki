Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/preparing-teacher-leaders-to-lead-professional-learning-insights-from-the-national-writing-project

q1 · i? · design · r1

The brief's opening statement of the teacher-leader role, offered as the authors' framing rather than a tested result. It asserts that teacher leaders "can draw on their own teaching experiences to coach teachers" and are well-positioned to improve colleagues' instruction; no data or effect size is reported.

"Teacher leaders can play a valuable role in supporting the professional learning of their colleagues. They can draw on their own teaching experiences to coach teachers, and they are well-positioned as school leaders to improve the instruction of their colleagues."
