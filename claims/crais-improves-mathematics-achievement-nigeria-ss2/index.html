Sabainah Oyebola Akinoso 2015¶

Sabainah Oyebola Akinoso. (2015). Teaching Mathematics in a Volatile, Uncertain, Complex and Ambiguous (VUCA) World: The Use of Concrete – Representational – Abstract Instructional Strategy. JISTE Vol. 19, No. 1, 2015. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177074

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pretest-posttest, control group, quasi-experimental study of 191 SS II students in four public schools in Ibadan, analysed by ANCOVA. The CRAIS group's adjusted posttest mean was 21.32 versus 19.07 for the control, and "treatment has significant effect on students’ achievement in mathematics".

"From Table 2, treatment has significant effect on students’ achievement in mathematics (F (1, 190) = 3.31; p< .05). Hypothesis 1 is, therefore, not rejected."
