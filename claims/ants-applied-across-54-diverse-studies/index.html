Kang Jiamin 2024¶

Kang Jiamin, Hu Jiale, Yan Chunji, Xing Xueyan, Tu Shumin, Zhou Fang. (2024). Development and applications of the Anaesthetists’ Non-Technical Skills behavioural marker system: a systematic review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-075019

Systematic review using a citation search strategy in Scopus and Web of Science (2002–May 2022), appraised with Joanna Briggs Institute checklists and synthesised by framework analysis. The review reports "54 studies were identified" with varied methods and settings.

"54 studies were identified. The ANTS system was applied across a wide variety of study objectives, settings and units of analysis. The methods used in these studies varied and included quantitative (n=42), mixed (n=8) and qualitative (n=4) approaches."
