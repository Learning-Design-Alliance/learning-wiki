## Evidence

### Iordanou 2010

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Three-way repeated-measures ANOVA on the proportion of Clarify (exposition) utterances in the coded dialogues. The printed effect size is "partial g2 ¼ .21" (partial η² = .21): "a Time x Condition significant interaction, F(2, 53) ¼ 6.96, p ¼ .002". Per-topic interactions were also significant (social partial η² = .17, science partial η² = .13).
