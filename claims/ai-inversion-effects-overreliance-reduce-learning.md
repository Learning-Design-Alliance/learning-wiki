## Evidence

### Bauer 2025

Bauer, E., Greiff, S., Graesser, A. C., Scheiter, K., & Sailer, M. (2025). Looking Beyond the Hype: Understanding the Effects of AI on Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10020-8

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports Fan et al. (2024), which compared support through ChatGPT, a human expert chat, writing analytics tools, and no support during a writing task. The ChatGPT group showed better supported-phase performance but "no differences in the posttest knowledge gain or knowledge transfer".

> "while the ChatGPT group, compared to the other groups, showed improved task performance during the supported intervention phase, there were no differences in the posttest knowledge gain or knowledge transfer."
