Two-level piecewise growth model analysis of five IRT-equated CBM administrations for 307 fifth and sixth graders (191 intervention, 116 comparison) in inclusive classrooms. The article reports "The effect size comparing intervention and comparison groups using Cohen's (1988) d was .43" for the Condition slope in phase 1 (p = .012).
