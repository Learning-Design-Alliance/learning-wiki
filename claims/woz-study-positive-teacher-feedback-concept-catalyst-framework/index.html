q1 · i? · design · r1

The article attributes this to prior work: a Wizard-of-Oz study in which the framework was introduced to K-12 engineering teachers. Teachers "reported positive feedback on the efficiency, quality, and diversity of content they wrote." No sample size, measures, or statistics are printed in this article.

"The tool's design is based on prior work which introduced the framework to K-12 Engineering teachers in a Wizard-of-Oz study in which teachers reported positive feedback on the efficiency, quality, and diversity of content they wrote, explaining how it helped them more deeply engage with the EDP."
