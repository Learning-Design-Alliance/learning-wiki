## Evidence

### Franziska Greinert 2023

Franziska Greinert, Rainer Müller, Philipp Bitzenbauer, Malte S. Ubben, Kim-Alessandro Weber. (2023). The Future Quantum Workforce: Competences, Requirements and Forecasts. arXiv:2208.08249. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Main 2 percentage-allocation question with N = 67 responses, clustered into >30, 20-30 and <20 groups (Tab. III). Mean allocations were 34% computing, 24% communication, 22% sensing, 18% simulation. "If there should be a stronger focus on one QT area, it should be on quantum computing".

> "For the curriculum distribution, at least 60% of the participants preferred to assign between 20% and 30% of the eﬀorts on quantum sensing, communication and simulation."
