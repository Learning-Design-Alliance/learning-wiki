Hoachlander 2020¶

Hoachlander, G. (2020). Building a system of college and career pathways in New Mexico. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/new-mexico-education

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's evidence review states that research has found CTE course-taking has "little or no effect (positive or negative) on academic achievement in high school." No effect size is printed for this general finding.

"In general, research has found that CTE course-taking has little or no effect (positive or negative) on academic achievement in high school."

Hoachlander 2020 (2)¶

Hoachlander, G. (2020). Building a system of college and career pathways in New Mexico. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/new-mexico-education

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review describes an experimental evaluation in which CTE teachers worked with math teachers to create math-enhanced lessons; after one year, experimental students "performed equally on technical skills and significantly better than control students on two standardized tests of math ability." No effect size is printed.

"One notable exception is an evaluation of an experiment in which CTE teachers worked closely with math teachers to create "math-enhanced" lessons for CTE instruction and curriculum. After 1 year of these lessons, students in the experimental classrooms performed equally on technical skills and significantly better than control students on two standardized tests of math ability."
