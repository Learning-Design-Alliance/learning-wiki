### Zarei Soraya 2022

Zarei Soraya, Mohammadi Shahriar. (2022). Challenges of higher education related to e-learning in developing countries during COVID-19 spread: a review of the perspectives of students, instructors, policymakers, and ICT experts. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Sangster et al. and Diab and Elgahsh, that connectivity problems made online courses inaccessible for students in developing countries, with the review noting "a poor internet connection" as the cause. Rural and underprivileged students were especially affected.

> "online courses might be completely or partially inaccessible to the students in developing countries due to a poor internet connection and also in many cases students may stay logged out"
