## Evidence

### Safa A. Elgamal 2011

Safa A. Elgamal, MBBCh, PhD, Eric A. Roy, PhD, Michael T. Sharratt, PhD. (2011). Age and Verbal Fluency: The Mediating Effect of Speed of Processing. Canadian Geriatrics Journal, Volume 14, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.5770/cgj.v14i3.17

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 130 healthy adults in three age groups (62 young, 30 middle-aged, 38 older) compared on neuropsychological measures using one-way ANOVA with Fisher's LSD post hoc tests. The abstract reports "category fluency as measured by animal category ( p < .001)" and processing speed differences favouring the young group, with older adults higher on the NART (p < .05).

> "The young group performed better than the older group on category fluency as measured by animal category ( p < .001) and on processing speed composite score (p < .001). However, the older group performed better than the young group on the National Adult Reading Test ( p < .05)"
