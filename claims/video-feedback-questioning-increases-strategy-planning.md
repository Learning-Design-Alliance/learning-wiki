### García-González Luis 2013

García-González Luis, Moreno M. Perla, Moreno Alberto, Gil Alexander, del Villar Fernando. (2013). Effectiveness of a Video-Feedback and Questioning Programme to Develop Cognitive Expertise in Sport. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0082270

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Strategy-planning interviews (Planning Interview question) from the same 11-player quasi-experiment, analysed with rank-based MANOVA. Concept Structure post-test showed "significant differences between experimental and control groups in number of linkages (p <.05) and triple concepts (p <.01)"; sophistication also significant.

> "Follow-up univariate analysis of Concept Structure variables in post-test (Table 6) showed significant differences between experimental and control groups in number of linkages (p <.05) and triple concepts (p <.01) with higher values in the experimental group"
