## Evidence

### Nugraini 2013

Nugraini, S. H., Choo, K. A., Hin, H. S., & Hoon, T. S. (2013). Impact of e-AV Biology Website for Learning About Renewable Energy. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1015407

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' interpretation of the overall quasi-experimental results across 256 students, comparing the magnitude of change in attitude and interest dimensions against the knowledge dimension. They attribute the stronger attitudinal effect to students' reinforced acceptance of the teaching media.

> "The results of the change of Attitude and Interest are more obvious compared to the change of Knowledge. This could be due to a more reinforced acceptance of students toward teaching media based on Interest, which subsequently brings an impact on their Attitude as well."
