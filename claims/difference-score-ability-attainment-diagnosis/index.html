Evidence¶

Pintner 1920¶

Pintner, R., and Marshall, H. (1920). A Combined Mental-Educational Survey. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Combined+Mental-Educational+Survey+Pintner+Marshall

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The authors describe their new rating from the standardization of both tests: the Difference is derived by subtracting the mental from the educational index, and they call it "the most important contribution that the science of mental measurement can offer to the educator".

"The new rating that we have introduced here is what we have called the Difference, i. e., the difference of a child's standing in the educational and mental tests, or the difference between his native capacity and his actual accomplishment."

Pintner 1920 (2)¶

Pintner, R., and Marshall, H. (1920). A Combined Mental-Educational Survey. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Combined+Mental-Educational+Survey+Pintner+Marshall

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A frequency distribution of Differences of 4303 cases was made and divided into a five-fold grouping; the middle band of minus 8 to plus 8 covers 53.5 per cent of cases. The article does not report an effect size for this distribution.

"In other words, Differences ranging from minus 8 through zero to plus 8 are found in about 50 per cent of the cases and are, therefore, not significant."
