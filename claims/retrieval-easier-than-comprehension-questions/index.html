q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Preliminary analysis of Study Part 3 responses pooling both conditions. The retrieval-versus-comprehension contrast was "significant, with a large effect size (Mann–WhitneyU= 2982.5,Z= 6.332,p <0.001,r= 0.578)"; single- and multiple-insight subtypes differed only marginally (r = 0.18).
