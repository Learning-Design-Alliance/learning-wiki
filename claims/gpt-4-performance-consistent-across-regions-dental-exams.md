## Evidence

### Liu Mingxin 2025

Liu Mingxin, Okuhara Tsuyoshi, Huang Wenbo, Ogihara Atsushi, Nagao Hikari Sophia, Okada Hiroko, Kiuchi Takahiro. (2025). Large Language Models in Dental Licensing Examinations: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.10.014

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of GPT-4 performance by examination origin country. The review reports "No significant difference was observed (P = .97)" between the two subgroups.

> "The integrate accuracy rate of GPT-4 in non-English-speaking countries' dental examinations was 73% (95% CI: 68%-78%), and in English-speaking countries' dental examinations, it was also 73% (95% CI: 68%-78%). No significant difference was observed (P = .97)"
