## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In a pilot study, the explicit response ratio was significantly higher for weak-directed interventions (M=1.21, SD=0.44) than undirected interventions (M=0.77, SD=0.58), d=0.85. [→ Tegos 2013](#tegos-2013)

## Evidence

### Tegos 2013

Tegos, S., Demetriadis, S., & Tsiatsos, T. (2013). A Configurable Conversational Agent to Trigger Students' Productive Dialogue: A Pilot Study in the CALL Domain. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0007-3

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pilot study discourse analysis of 14 small groups in a CALL activity; explicit response ratio (agent-induced explicit positions and arguments per agent intervention) compared across the two intervention modes using a paired sample T-test, showing "significant difference in favor of the WDI condition" with d=0.85.

> "A paired sample T-test identified significant difference in favor of the WDI condition ( n=14, M=1.21, SD=0.44) as compared to the UI condition ( n=14, M=0.77, SD=0.58); t(13)= −2.70, p=0.02, d=0.85."
