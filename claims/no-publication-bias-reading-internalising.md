## Evidence

### Francis 2019

Francis, D. A.; Caruana, N.; Hudson, J. L.; McArthur, G. M. (2019). The association between poor reading and internalising problems: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Clinical Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2018.09.002

`q3 · i?`

Publication-bias assessment across the three meta-analyses using funnel-plot inspection, Egger's test, and the trim and fill procedure. Egger's tests were non-significant for all three outcomes, indicating "publication bias was not present".

> "The funnel plots showed symmetry, and evaluation of Egger's test showed that publication bias was not present for internalising problems, t (12) = 0.05, p > 0.05, 95% CI [ −1.42, 1.35], anxiety, t (20) = 0.25, p > 0.05, 95% CI [ −2.22, 2.85], or depression, t (21) = 0.10, p > 0.05, 95% CI [ −2.69, 2.98]."
