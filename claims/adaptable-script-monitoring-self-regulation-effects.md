# Reflection increased in the adaptable and non-adaptable script conditions, but only the adaptable script had positive effects on monitoring, supporting overall positive effects on self-regulation

## Evidence

### Ludvigsen 2017

Ludvigsen, S., Law, N., Rosé, C. P., & Stahl, G. (2017). Frameworks for mass collaboration, adaptable scripts, complex systems theory, and collaborative writing. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn, 12, 127–131. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9257-7

`q2 · i?`

Reported by the editorial for Wang et al.'s experimental study with university students in educational science, comparing adaptable scripts, non-adaptable scripts, and non-scripted collaboration. No effect size is printed for the monitoring difference.

> "Planning is seen as one important aspect of self-regulation. However, reflection increases both in the adaptable and non-adaptable script conditions. Only the adaptable script had positive effects on monitoring."

### Ludvigsen 2017 (2)

Ludvigsen, S., Law, N., Rosé, C. P., & Stahl, G. (2017). Frameworks for mass collaboration, adaptable scripts, complex systems theory, and collaborative writing. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn, 12, 127–131. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9257-7

`q2 · i?`

The editorial attributes this overall conclusion to Wang et al., whose study compared the three script conditions with regard to effectiveness. This is the editorial's summary of the included study's conclusion.

> "Wang et al. conclude that overall, adaptable collaborative scripts have a positive effect on self-regulation skills."
