`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Hande et al., that the peer-assisted group "had higher post-test knowledge and OSPE scores in the peer-assisted group" compared with faculty-assisted ECE in a single-center randomized trial (n = 125).

> "Hande et al. [8] compared peer-assisted and faculty-assisted ECE and had higher post-test knowledge and OSPE scores in the peer-assisted group."
