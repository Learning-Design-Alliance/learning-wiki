### Siaulys Monica Maria 2019

Siaulys Monica Maria, da Cunha Lissandra Borba, Torloni Maria Regina, Kondo Mario Macoto. (2019). Obstetric emergency simulation training course: experience of a private-public partnership in Brazil. Reproductive Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12978-019-0689-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous four-point satisfaction questionnaires completed at the end of each module (Kirkpatrick level 1) in the before-and-after study, reported in Results with Table 4 percentages by domain (methods, expectations, instructors, resources). Descriptive data only; no inferential test or effect size.

> "The mean (SD) overall satisfaction (0 to 10 scale) of the participants was 9.6 (0.9), 9.8 (0.5), 9.7 (0.6) and 9.8 (0.8) in the hypertension, hemorrhage, sepsis and resuscitation modules, respectively."
