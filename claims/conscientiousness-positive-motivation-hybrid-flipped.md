### Alshareef Ali M. 2026

Alshareef Ali M., Alhasani Mona, Qamar Nabeel, Alhazmi Aiman. (2026). Tailoring hybrid flipped learning course design: exploring motivation, personality, and experiences of students and faculty. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09570-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

PLS-SEM analysis (SmartPLS, bootstrapping with 5000 resamples) of 149 student responses examining Big Five traits as predictors of IMI factors. The article reports "Conscientiousness showed significant positive relationships" across the three motivation dimensions; standardized path coefficients are printed but no standardized effect size for impact coding.

> "Conscientiousness showed significant positive relationships with Interest/Enjoyment (β = 0.24, p < .01), Effort/Importance (β = 0.22, p ≤ .05), and Perceived Competence (β = 0.31, p < .01)"
