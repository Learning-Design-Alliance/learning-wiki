Evidence¶

Florence Martin 2025¶

Florence Martin, Min Zhuang, Erik Byker, WeiChao Wang, Julie Bacak, Lynn Ahlgrim-Delzell. (2025). Multimedia-based cybersecurity and privacy professional development on educational technology for K-12 personnel. Education Tech Research Dev (2026) 74:849–873. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10575-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

Single-group pre-post evaluation of the online PD with 50 K-12 personnel; the Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used because data were not normally distributed. The article reports a statistically significant increase, with "a mean of 9.02 to the post-test with a mean of 10.48"; no effect size is printed.

"The Wilcoxon signed-rank test shows that the knowledge level of all participants increased significantly after taking this PD (V = 265.50, P = 0.012 < 0.05) from the pre-test with a mean of 9.02 to the post-test with a mean of 10.48."
