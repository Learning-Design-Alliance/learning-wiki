Table 1 prints component rates: Black young men's high school graduation rate is 73.3 percent, immediate four-year enrollment 38.6 percent, and bachelor's completion among immediate four-year enrollees 30.3 percent.

> "If current rates hold, 87 percent of Black young men in the current CPS ninth-grade class will not complete any post-secondary degree or certificate within 10 years. Fewer than one in ten (9 percent) will earn a bachelor's degree within 10 years."
