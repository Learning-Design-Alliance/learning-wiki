## Evidence

### Miyagami Taiju 2025

Miyagami Taiju, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Shikino Kiyoshi, Kataoka Koshi, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Naito Toshio, Tokuda Yasuharu (2025). A cross sectional study of the relationship between clinical scores and timing of guidance for resident physicians in outpatient settings. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30748-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable regression from the same 4,662-resident survey, with fixed covariates including gender, PGY, hospital characteristics, self-study time and duty hours. The reported adjusted "Estimated coefficient [EC] [−1.03, 95% confidence interval (CI) {−1.68 to −0.37}, p = 0.002]" supports the association.

> "participants who received teaching during the physical examination stage had significantly lower GM-ITE® scores than those during other stages (Estimated coefficient [EC] [−1.03, 95% confidence interval (CI) {−1.68 to −0.37}, p = 0.002])."
