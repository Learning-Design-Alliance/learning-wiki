## Evidence

### Edwards Daniel 2013

Edwards Daniel, Friedman Tim, Pearce Jacob. (2013). Same admissions tools, different outcomes: a critical perspective on predictive validity in three undergraduate medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-173

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across the three institutions (Table 2). Printed effect sizes: r = 0.41 (Year 1) and r = 0.39 (Year 2) at Institution B, where school achievement was "strongly correlated with GPA in Institution B"; at Institution C no significant school-GPA correlation was found.

> "As consistently found in studies of predictive validity [1,10,14], school achievement generally recorded the strongest correlations of the three measures used by the institutions in this study. In particular school achievement was strongly correlated with GPA in Institution B, with a correlation of 0.41 in Year 1 and 0.39 in Year 2."
