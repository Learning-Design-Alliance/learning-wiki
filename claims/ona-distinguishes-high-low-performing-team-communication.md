`q2 i3` In simulated MH crisis scenarios, the overall communication patterns (code frequencies and temporal sequences) of high-performing teams differed significantly from low-performing teams, with a large printed effect size (Cohen's d = 1.72). [→ Popov V 2025](#popov-v-2025)

## Evidence

### Popov V 2025

Popov V, Rochlen LR. (2025). From communication to action: using ordered network analysis to model team performance in clinical simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07062-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of 22 video-recorded anesthesiology simulation teams (5 high-performing, 17 low-performing) using ONA point positions in a projected multidimensional space. The two-sample t-test showed significantly different communication patterns, with "Cohen's d = 1.72" at the alpha = 0.05 level.

> "A two sample t test showed significantly different communication patterns between high performing teams (mean = 0.18, SD = 0.19, N = 5) and low performing teams (mean = −0.05, SD = 0.12, N = 17; t(5.01) = −2.65, p = 0.04, Cohen's d = 1.72; at the alpha = 0.05 level)."
