Pre/post-test error analysis on open-ended basis-change questions 6 and 7 across three cohorts. The article reports these "Common mistakes" on the pre-test and that "the vast majority of students correctly expressed the given state in the desired basis" on the post-test.

> "The most difficult concepts covered by the CQS involved changing basis, which were evaluated by questions 6 and 7 on the pre - and post-tests. Common mistakes included simply dividing the state by √2 or answering that the expansion coefficients are the same in both bases."
