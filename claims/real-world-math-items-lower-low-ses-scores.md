`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Muskens et al., a preregistered cross-sectional analysis of TIMSS data from 4th and 8th grade students in 58 countries. Contrary to hypothesis, low-SES students "scored 18%lower on items of real-world importance". The 18% figure is a percentage difference, not a standardized effect size.
