## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2020

Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Suzanne White Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Andrew Boudreaux, Chris DePalma, Victor LaSasso, Christopher Whitener, Scott Straguzzi. (2020). Using psychometric tools as a window into students' quantitative reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.smith_t](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.smith_t)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Module analysis for multiple-choice responses (MAMCR) applied to networks of correct responses only, using igraph, a LANS backbone (alpha = 0.05), and six community detection algorithms each run 100 times; modules were read from co-occurrence heatmaps. The article notes "both of the large modules in PreMech contain responses associated with all three of the PQL constructs."

> "The modules are not the same in the three different data sets, suggesting that students' knowledge and experience affect the ways in which they answer PIQL questions."
