Zhang, D., Ke, S. (E.), & Mo, Y. (2023). Morphology in Reading Comprehension Among School-Aged Readers of English: A Synthesis and Meta-Analytic Structural Equation Modeling Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 115(5), 683–699. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000797

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Multi-group path analysis compared bilingual (k = 46, N = 5,423) and monolingual (k = 60, N = 16,084) subgroups. The difference was significant (Δχ2(1) = 8.370, p = .015), consistent with bilinguals' constrained English exposure leading to shallower morphological representations.

> "The indirect effect of MA on reading comprehension via word reading was also stronger in monolinguals (β = .175) than in bilinguals (β = .077), Δχ2(1) = 8.370, p = .015."
