Evidence¶

Xin et al. 2015¶

Huaibo Xin, Monica Kempland, Faustina H. Blankson. (2015). ADAPTABILITY AND REPLICABILITY OF WEB-FACILITATED, HYBRID, AND ONLINE LEARNING IN AN UNDERGRADUATE EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY COURSE. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – January 2015, volume 14 issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057333

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of three sections (n=39 web-facilitated, n=24 hybrid, n=40 online) taught by the same instructor over three semesters; one-way ANOVA with Tukey HSD post hoc tests. The "mean difference = .05" between web-facilitated and each other section reached significance.

"The mean of the final exam of the web-facilitated section was significantly higher than the means of both hybrid and online sections (mean difference = .05, p < .05, mean difference = .05, p < .05)."
