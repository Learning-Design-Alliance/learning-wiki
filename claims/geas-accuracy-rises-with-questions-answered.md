### Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise 2015

Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise. (2015). An Online Tutor for Astronomy: The GEAS Self-Review Library. [doi:10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990](https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

An observational curves-of-growth analysis of 1,600 average curves built from 26 lectures and 61 students, sorting each student's questions in attempt order within each lecture; curves were truncated at 30 remaining samples and boxcar-smoothed. Average accuracy rises from around 55% toward 70–80% as questions are answered.

> "Our first, over-riding point is that the curves all rise. Over time, students learn new concepts and absorb new techniques for solving problems, and are able to apply them successfully."
