Liao Shu-Chen, Shao Shih-Chieh, Gao Shi-Ying, Lai Edward Chia-Cheng. (2024). Augmented reality visualization for ultrasound-guided interventions: a pilot randomized crossover trial to assess trainee performance and cognitive load. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05998-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-gender comparison within the AR-US modality in the crossover trial. Female trainees were faster at anatomy identification and IJV location, with a printed T1 mean difference of -10.79 s (p = 0.01).

> "female trainees using AR-US, compared to male trainees using AR-US, were able to identify the anatomy of the insertion site and locate the IJV in less time (T1(s): 14.87 (8.75) vs. 25.66 (19.89), mean difference: -10.79 (95% CI: -19.21 to -2.37), p = 0.01) (Fig. 6A)."
