This is the article's literature-review assertion about the state of the field, noting that most implementations are located in the UK, USA and Australia. The review states that "large-scale studies regarding the effectiveness of learning analytics are still lacking"; it offers no effect sizes for this field-level judgement.

"Although in the last five years, there has been an increase in the number of related research efforts (Gašević et al. 2015,2017,2019; Rienties and Toetenel 2016; Tempelaar et al. 2015), large-scale studies regarding the effectiveness of learning analytics are still lacking (Mah et al. 2019; Wong et al. 2019)."
