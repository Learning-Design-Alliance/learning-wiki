## Evidence

### Dilly Marc 2016

Dilly Marc, Tipold Andrea, Geuenich Katja. (2016). Stressed out or subjective acquisition of competence – how do veterinary students see their curative work placement? GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(1), Doc9. [doi:10.3205/zma001008](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001008)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 142 veterinary students from all five German veterinary institutes using the standardised, normed Burnout Screening Scales (BOSS I). The article reports that "More than half of the participants (n=78) described a significantly increased load"; no effect size is printed, only T values.

> "More than half of the participants (n=78) described a significantly increased load that deviated from the norm during their work placement."
