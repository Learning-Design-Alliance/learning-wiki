# Ten-year-old readers predominantly underline subordinate passage details rather than superordinate conceptual material, even when conceptual postquestions focus attention on topic sentences

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rickards 1979

Rickards, J.P., Denner, P.R. (1979). Depressive effects of underlining and adjunct questions on children's recall of text. Instructional Science, 8, 81–90. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00054983

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Experimental study of ten-year-old children (N=69) crossing postquestion presence with three underlining conditions. The reported result was that readers "preponderantly chose for underlining subordinate, passage details rather than superordinate, conceptual material" despite conceptual postquestions on topic sentences.

> "the readers preponderantly chose for underlining subordinate, passage details rather than superordinate, conceptual material, even when given conceptual postquestions focusing on the topic sentences of the passage"
