## Evidence

### Punja R 2026

Punja R, Punja D, Prasad Kamachi Christdoss Ruben. (2026). Large-group virtual anatomy via a smart digital podium: a preliminary cross-sectional study of student perspectives toward remote anatomage teaching in a medical lecture hall. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10019-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey (N=94) using a 5-point Likert item comparing Anatomage realism to cadaveric dissection. Nine students (9.6%) were neutral and 8 (8.5%) found it somewhat unrealistic. No inferential statistics were performed.

> "Of 94 students, 14 (14.9%) rated the Anatomage as ‘very realistic’ and 63 (67.0%) as ‘somewhat realistic’ compared to actual cadaveric dissection, giving a combined realism endorsement of 77/94 (81.9%)."
