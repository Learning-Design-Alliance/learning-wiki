## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The achievement gap between URMs and non-URMs in DFW rates was significant without PLTL (p < 0.001) and not significant with PLTL participation (p = 0.272). [→ Snyder JJ 2016](#snyder-jj-2016)

## Evidence

### Snyder JJ 2016

Snyder JJ, Sloane JD, Dunk RDP, Wiles JR. (2016). Peer-Led Team Learning Helps Minority Students Succeed. PLoS Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002398

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of DFW rates between URM and non-URM students who did not engage in PLTL, reported in the Our Findings section of this observational study. The figure caption adds that the gap "is closed when students participate in PLTL (p = 0.272)"; no effect size is printed.

> "the DFW rate was significantly higher for URMs than it was for non-URMs among those who did not engage in PLTL (x 2 = 14.157, n = 227, df = 1, p < 0.001), but not significantly different between URMs and non-URMs who did"
