## Evidence

### Dominique Bradley 2015

Dominique Bradley, Evan Crawford, Sara E. Dahill-Brown. (2015). Fidelity of Implementation in a Large-Scale, Randomized, Field Trial: Identifying the Critical Components of Values Affirmation. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562183

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity analysis across the four exercise administrations during the 2011-2012 school year found engagement declined by the fourth exercise for both students and teachers; the authors interpret this as a possible dampening effect on intervention impact.

> "We find notable declines in both student and teacher engagement with the exercise by the fourth affirmation exercise. We believe that these results could indicate a dampening effect on the impact of the intervention in our sample."
