## Evidence

### Alli Klapp 2017

Alli Klapp, Clive Belfield, Brooks Bowden, Henry Levin, Robert Shand, Sabine Zander. (2017). A benefit-cost analysis of a long-term intervention on social and emotional learning in compulsory school. International Journal of Emotional Education, 9(1), 3-19. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Benefit-cost results section of the BCA, combining ingredient-method costs with monetized drug-use benefits from the five-year follow-up. The article prints the ratio and net present value directly: "a benefit-cost ratio of 14:1 and a net present value of $6,970".

> "The baseline estimates per participant are at $540 for costs and $7,510 for benefits. This yields a benefit-cost ratio of 14:1 and a net present value of $6,970."

### Alli Klapp 2017 (2)

Alli Klapp, Clive Belfield, Brooks Bowden, Henry Levin, Robert Shand, Sabine Zander. (2017). A benefit-cost analysis of a long-term intervention on social and emotional learning in compulsory school. International Journal of Emotional Education, 9(1), 3-19. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cost analysis using the ingredients method (Levin & McEwan, 2001), discounted by 3.5% to year 1 in 2013 US dollars, based on 1,028 participating students. Teacher salaries are the highest cost component; facilities and materials are relatively low.

> "For the five -year intervention, the present value total cost is $555,260 and the average cost per student is $540."
