## Evidence

### Li Min 2025

Li Min, Liu Tao, Wen Yiqiong, Zhang Min, Zhu Wenye, Wang Xuming, Hou Yunjiao, Li Lin, Zu Anju, Sun Shibo. (2025). Combination of mentor-based and resident-based learning may benefit to improving the ability of clinical practice in resident training. BMC Medical Education 25:197. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06798-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trainee questionnaire (0–10 per item; KMO 0.650, Cronbach's α 0.790) administered at the end of teaching to all 72 trainees; all 72 collected. All four comparisons favored CMRL with printed p-values; no effect sizes printed.

> "the evaluation on teaching method of the CMRL group was significantly higher than that of the MBL group (satisfaction with teaching methods, 8.28 ± 1.30 scores vs. 7.53 ± 1.13 scores, p = 0.011; improving the ability of practice, 8.42 ± 1.66 scores vs. 7.47 ± 1.56 scores, p = 0.015; increasing theoretical knowledge, 7.22 ± 1.61 scores vs. 6.50 ± 1.25 scores, p = 0.037; increasing operational knowledge, 8.33 ± 1.39 scores vs. 7.50 ± 1.21 scores, p = 0.008; respectively)"
