## Subclaims
`q3 i?` None of the subgroup comparisons for medical family background were significant at simulation, interview or group stations, and no differences appeared in the prediction of OSCE results. [→ Knorr M 2019](#knorr-m-2019)

## Evidence

### Knorr M 2019

Knorr M, Meyer H, Sehner S, Hampe W, Zimmermann S. (2019). Exploring sociodemographic subgroup differences in multiple mini-interview (MMI) performance based on MMI station type and the implications for the predictive fairness of the Hamburg MMI. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1674-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear model of the MMI sample: the medical family background x station type interaction was significant (p = 0.004) but individual station-type contrasts were not (simulation p = 0.087, interview p = 0.053, group p = 0.572). The authors report no support for transfer of MMI-relevant knowledge from physician parents.

> "However, none of the subgroup comparisons were significant."
