## Evidence

### Vage A 2024

Vage A, Spence AD, McKeown G, Gormley GJ, Hamilton PK. (2024). Simulate to stimulate? A systematic review of stress, learning, and performance in healthcare simulation. The Ulster Medical Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11591218/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports this single enhanced-performance study (medical students, simulated ambulatory consultation with pulmonary embolism or aggressive-patient stressors) and offers the authors' own caveat that declining STAI-measured anxiety may mean stress was never induced.

> "The authors concluded that both intrinsic and extrinsic stressors had positive effects on performance. However, as perceived participant anxiety (measured by the STAI) showed a decline throughout the scenarios, these factors may have been ineffective at inducing sufficient stress."
