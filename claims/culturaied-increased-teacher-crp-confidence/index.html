Jiayi Wang 2025¶

Jiayi Wang, Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, Hanqi Li, Ying Jui Tseng, John Stamper, and Ken Koedinger. (2025). LLMs to Support K–12 Teachers in Culturally Relevant Pedagogy: An AI Literacy Example. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08083

q2 · i? · design · r2

Exploratory pilot with four K-12 teachers in 90-120 minute semi-structured Zoom sessions with pre- and post-surveys; survey data analyzed descriptively given the small sample (N = 4). Confidence "rose sig- nificantly from 3-5 in the pre-survey to all 5 in the post-survey." No effect size is printed.

"In specific, teachers’ confidence in "Iden- tifying opportunities for cultural responsiveness in learning activities" rose sig- nificantly from 3-5 in the pre-survey to all 5 in the post-survey."
