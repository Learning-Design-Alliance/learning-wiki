## Evidence

### Kwon Young Gyu 2026

Kwon Young Gyu, Choi Se Young, Park Hye Young, Shin Yu Seob, Kim Chan Woong. (2026). Experiential learning in urologic single-port robotic surgery transition training: a qualitative descriptive study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03484-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of open-ended questionnaires from 33 healthcare providers (11 physicians, 22 OR nurses) at a single Seoul tertiary center, with Kolb's cycle as a deductive sensitizing framework. The study reports that "Most experiences aligned with Kolb’s learning cycle" across all four stages.

> "Most experiences aligned with Kolb’s learning cycle: concrete experience with single-port articulation, reflective comparison of single- and multi-port routines, abstract conceptualization of single-cannula principles, and active experimentation with collision prevention and workflow strategies."
