Evidence¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg 2015¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg. (2015). An intervention Programme for Promoting More Effective Learning among Foundation Phase Learners in South Africa. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n2p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A true experiment with one multicultural English-medium Grade 1 class of 39 learners aged 6-7, of which "n=19 were placed in the experimental group and n=20 in the control group"; the experimental group received the intervention programme while controls listened to stories. Effect sizes were computed with Cohen's (1988) criteria; no significance test is reported.

"A comparison of the pre- and post-tests of both groups showed that the experimental group had markedly improved (see Tables 1 and 2 for effect sizes) (total: d = 1.30 for the control group as opposed to d = 1.89 for the experimental group)."

Ona Janse van Rensburg 2015 (2)¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg. (2015). An intervention Programme for Promoting More Effective Learning among Foundation Phase Learners in South Africa. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n2p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Graph 1 plots progress of both groups across all eight sub-sections of the Aptitude Test for School Beginners, showing a shared baseline of 28.5 and steeper progress for the experimental group over the ten weeks.

"This graph shows that both groups departed from the same baseline but that during the 10 weeks that the experiment lasted, the experimental group made better progress because of the intervention that took place."
