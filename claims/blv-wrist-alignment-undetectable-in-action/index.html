q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Findings from interviews and lesson observations with four musicians with BLV and three instructors; the article contrasts sighted beginners' mirror use with participants who "cannot feel whether their wrist is straight during playing."

"Sighted beginners can usually detect this quickly by watching themselves in a mirror, but participants reported that they often cannot feel whether their wrist is straight during playing and only learn about the issue when a teacher or another person observes them."
