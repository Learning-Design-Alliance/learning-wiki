`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Within class 2 of the qualitative analysis, students reported that watching colleagues revealed overlooked actions and that debriefing supported group reflection. One student said the debriefing session aided "group-based development of clinical reasoning".

> "students emphasized the importance of observing their peers during the simulation scenario, as well as the debriefing session as an opportunity for reflection and learning"
