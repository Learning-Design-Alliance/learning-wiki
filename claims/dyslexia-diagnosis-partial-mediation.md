### Graell M. et al 2026

Graell M. et al. (2026). Towards disentangling the polygenic contribution of dyslexia to school performance. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-026-04118-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mediation analysis in the INSchool cohort (n = 4274, including 413 diagnosed cases) tested whether dyslexia diagnosis mediated PGS effects on school performance using logistic and ordinal mixed-effect models. Dyslexia diagnosis was associated with PGSDYS_noEA (P = 3.26E-04) and PGSDYS_discordant (P = 4.25E-09) but not PGSDYS_concordant.

> "confirmed that the effect of both PGSDYS_noEA and PGSDYS_discordant on school performance was attenuated after considering the effect of the dyslexia diagnosis. These results suggest that dyslexia partially mediates the effect of these PGS on school performance (accounting for a reduction in effect size ranging from 10.44–12.91%)"
