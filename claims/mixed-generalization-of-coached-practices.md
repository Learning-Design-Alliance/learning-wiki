## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One teacher generalized coached practices to untrained activities at near-criterion levels, while the other two used practices above baseline but below criterion in untrained activities. [→ Hemmeter et al. 2015](#hemmeter-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Hemmeter et al. 2015

Mary Louise Hemmeter, Jessica K. Hardy, Alana G. Schnitz, Jessie Morris Adams, and Kiersten A. Kinder. (2015). Effects of Training and Coaching With Performance Feedback on Teachers' Use of Pyramid Model Practices. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121415594924

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Generalization was measured periodically with the checklists during an uncoached time of day the teacher was unaware was being observed. Bianca demonstrated practices around the 80% criterion in generalization activities; Kendra's levels were significantly lower than in intervention activities; Susan used practices above baseline but not near criterion.

> "The results related to generalization to other activities were mixed, with strong generalization demonstrated by Bianca and more limited results for the other two teachers."
