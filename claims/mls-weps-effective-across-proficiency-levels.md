## Evidence

### Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analyses within treatment groups controlling prior knowledge. Unit 1 outcomes and Unit 2 calculation "did not vary significantly" by English proficiency; only Unit 2 explanation showed a difference favoring proficiency level 5 over levels 1 and 3.

> "ELs in the Unit 1 treatment group did not differ significantly in their math outcomes by their English proficiency in the calculation ( Cohen sf2 = 0.41,power = 0.81) and explanation ( Cohen sf2 = 1.04,power = 1) parts. Similarly, the analysis (see Table 8) showed ELs’ calculation scores in the Unit 2 treatment group also did not vary significantly"
