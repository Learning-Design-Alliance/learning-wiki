Sporte 2016¶

Sporte, S.E., & Jiang, J.Y. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year 3 Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

q2 · i? · associational · r2

District-wide summative REACH ratings for tenured teachers receiving final ratings in 2014-15, compared with checklist ratings from 2003-04 to 2007-08 (Figure A and accompanying text).

"In that year, 83 percent of tenured teachers received ratings in the top two categories. In comparison, under the previous checklist system, 94 percent of tenured teachers received ratings in the top two categories (Superior or Excellent)."

REACH final ratings for non-tenured teachers across Years 1-3 compared with prior checklist ratings (Figure A); the brief reports a shift upward in ratings in Years 2 and 3.

"Under the previous checklist system, 87 percent of non-tenured teachers received ratings in the top two categories. Under REACH, 58 percent of teachers received ratings in top two categories in 2012-13."
