`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative descriptive (thematic) analysis of semi-structured interviews with seven willing students from the experimental group, conducted in Turkish at the end of the four-week treatment; responses were categorized into nine themes including "positive sides of situated learning".

> "Most of the students perceived that the activities and applications of situated learning were beneficial and that their communication and pronunciation improved, they learned better and their motivation increased."
