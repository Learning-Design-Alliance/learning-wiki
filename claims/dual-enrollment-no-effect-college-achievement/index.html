What Works Clearinghouse 2017¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2017). Dual Enrollment Programs. WWC Intervention Report, Transition to College. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/671

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Berger et al. (2014), an RCT comparing college grade point averages of intervention and comparison students; the study included 455 students in 282 schools for this domain. The WWC characterizes the finding as indeterminate, with an improvement index of –1 percentile points; no standardized effect size is printed.

"The authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, that there was not a statistically significant difference between intervention and comparison group students in the general academic achievement (college) domain."
