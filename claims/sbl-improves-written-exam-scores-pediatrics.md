## Evidence

### Elamin Abdelaziz 2025

Elamin Abdelaziz, Ali Maryam Fuad, Almarabheh Amer. (2025). Impact of simulation-based learning on the academic performance of medical students during pediatric clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07911-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of pre-simulation (n = 1001) and post-simulation (n = 1250) cohorts in the retrospective analysis; Table 2 reports effect size 0.890 for the written exam, the largest of the three components.

> "A statistically significant improvement was observed in written examination scores, with the mean increasing from 56.88 ± 15 in the pre-simulation group to 71.16 ± 16.85 in the post-simulation group (p < 0.001)."
