## Evidence

### Lengua Hinojosa Patricia 2022

Lengua Hinojosa Patricia, Eifinger Frank, Wagner Michael, Herrmann Jochen, Wolf Monika, Ebenebe Chinedu Ulrich, von der Wense Axel, Jung Philipp, Mai Aram, Bohnhorst Bettina, Longardt Ann Carolin, Hillebrand Georg, Schmidtke Susanne, Guthmann Florian, Aderhold Martina, Schwake Ida, Sprinz Maria, Singer Dominique, Deindl Philipp. (2022). Anatomic accuracy, physiologic characteristics, and fidelity of very low birth weight infant airway simulators. Pediatric Research 92(3):783-790. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-021-01823-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Bench physiology testing with a Leonie Plus neonatal ventilator at three ventilator settings, each measurement performed three times. The article reports "all simulator models had resistances that were up to 20 times higher" than the printed reference value of 61 ± 26 cm H2O/l/s.

> "all simulator models had resistances that were up to 20 times higher (Premature Anne: 806 ± 143, Premature AirwayPaul: 417 ± 90 cm H2O/l/s, Premie HAL S2209: 1291 ± 157 cm H2O/l/s, Preterm Baby: 459 ± 96 cm H2O/l/s, Reference: 61 ± 26 cm H2O/l/s)"

### Lengua Hinojosa Patricia 2022 (2)

Lengua Hinojosa Patricia, Eifinger Frank, Wagner Michael, Herrmann Jochen, Wolf Monika, Ebenebe Chinedu Ulrich, von der Wense Axel, Jung Philipp, Mai Aram, Bohnhorst Bettina, Longardt Ann Carolin, Hillebrand Georg, Schmidtke Susanne, Guthmann Florian, Aderhold Martina, Schwake Ida, Sprinz Maria, Singer Dominique, Deindl Philipp. (2022). Anatomic accuracy, physiologic characteristics, and fidelity of very low birth weight infant airway simulators. Pediatric Research 92(3):783-790. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-021-01823-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same ventilator bench measurements of static compliance at three ventilation intensities. The article reports "the compliance was up to 19 times too low" relative to the printed reference of 1.49 ± 0.4 ml/cm H2O/kg, with per-simulator values as listed.

> "whereas the compliance was up to 19 times too low (Premature AirwayPaul: 0.308 ± 0.008 ml/cm H2O/kg, Premature Anne: 0.286 ± 0.015 ml/cm H2O/kg, Premie HAL S2209: 0.087 ± 0.001 ml/cm H2O/kg, Preterm baby: 0.393 ± 0.011 ml/cm H2O/kg, Reference: 1.49 ± 0.4 ml/cm H2O/kg)"
