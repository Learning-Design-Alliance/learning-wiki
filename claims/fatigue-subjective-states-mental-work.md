### W. S. Painter 1914

W. S. Painter. (1914). Efficiency in Mental Multiplication Under Extreme Fatigue. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

First-person qualitative account of the final test's subjective conditions, reported in full by the author. Notes cover the first hour's little fatigue growth, second-hour bodily weariness, third-hour lethargy, and heavy breathing near the end.

> "My growing inability to banish from memory previous partial products and to hold the new ones that I wanted, inability to speed up, greater and greater length of time neces- sarily spent in repeating partial products and sums, loss of mem- ory so as to have to start problems over, etc., were all very promi- nent mental states."
