## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Video-coding analysis of 158 GTA-led classroom sessions in weeks 2, 4, 7, and 10 of the course. "Cold call was the most frequently used technique" at a median of 18 events per hour, with circulate next at 13 events per hour; no effect size is reported for these descriptive frequencies.

> "Cold call was the most frequently used technique (Supplemental Table 4), with a median frequency of 18 events per hour observed. Circulate was the next most common, at 13 events per hour"

### Becker Erin A. 2017 (2)

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive participation analysis across 45 observed classrooms. "Three-quarters of classrooms had an average participation level above 70%" overall and above 46% for cold call; the article also notes overall nonresponse to cold call was 4.4%.

> "Three-quarters of classrooms had an average participation level above 70% (range: 29–100%) and a cold-call participation level above 46% (range: 14–100%; Table 5)."
