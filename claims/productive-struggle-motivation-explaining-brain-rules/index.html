An English teacher reports that following Uncapher's advice, sharing the rationale for strategies with students increased their acceptance. Lonnemann added this helped take away some teacher-student power dynamics. Accounts are self-reported, with no outcome measures.

"Pierce says he “deliberately explains brain rules to the kids, so they are more willing to accept it” and become interested in trying new approaches to learning."
