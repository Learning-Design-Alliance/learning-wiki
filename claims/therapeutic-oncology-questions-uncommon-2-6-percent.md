`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive analysis of the coded oncology question pool across NBME self-assessments and sample questions; 101 questions referenced a therapy modality, 66 on USMLE Steps 1–3 and 35 on shelf exams.

> "Among the 3878 total questions and 337 oncology-related questions, 101 referenced at least one cancer therapy modality (2.6% and 30%, respectively). Sixty-six cancer therapy questions were on the USMLE Steps 1–3 examinations (65%), and 35 (35%) were on the NBME shelf exams."
