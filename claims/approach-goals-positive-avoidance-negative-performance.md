# Approach goals (mastery or performance) correlate positively and avoidance goals (performance or mastery) correlate negatively with nonself-report performance

## Subclaims
`q4 i1` Overall, MAp goals (r = .14) and PAp goals (r = .10) correlate positively with performance, while PAv goals (r = -.13) and MAv goals (r = -.07) correlate negatively. [→ Van Yperen NW 2014](#van-yperen-nw-2014)

## Evidence

### Van Yperen NW 2014

Van Yperen NW, Blaga M, Postmes T (2014). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Reported Achievement Goals and Nonself-Report Performance across Three Achievement Domains (Work, Sports, and Education). PLoS ONE 9(4): e93594. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0093594

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 98 papers, 112 samples, 33,983 participants, and 295 effect sizes using random-effects models with Fisher-Z transformed, inverse-variance-weighted correlations. Printed effect sizes: rMAp = .14, rPAp = .10, rPAv = -.13, rMAv = -.07; the article reports "overall positive correlations were found between MAp goals and performance" and negative correlations for both avoidance goals.

> "overall positive correlations were found between MAp goals and performance ( rMAp = .14, Z = 11.78, p,.001), and between PAp goals and performance ( rPAp = .10, Z = 7.93, p, .001). Overall negative correlations were observed between PAv goals and performance ( rPAv = 2.13, Z = 210.39, p,.001) and MAv goals and performance ( rMAv = 2.07, Z = 22.23, p = .026)."
