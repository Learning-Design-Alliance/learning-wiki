Evidence¶

Jorczak and Dupuis 2011¶

Robert L. Jorczak, Danielle N. Dupuis. (2011). Differences in Classroom Versus Online Exam Performance Due to Asynchronous Discussion. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Differences+in+Classroom+Versus+Online+Exam+Performance+Due+to+Asynchronous+Discussion

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis across the full sample of 104 students relating exam scores to total course points, excluding online discussion participation points. The printed effect size is r = 0.30 (p = .01); the authors interpret that students with better test scores tended to get better grades but the association is surprisingly weak.

"For the total sample, the correlation between exam scores and total course points earned in learning activities (excluding online discussion participation points for the online sections) was 0.30 (p = .01) suggesting that grades do not strictly follow assessed learning."
