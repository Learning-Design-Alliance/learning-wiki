`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study comparing four participant groups (IMT, IET, PMT, EPT) via content analysis of interviews. The findings section reports that "nearly all IETs emphasized the influence of AME-G attributions" while IMTs "predominantly focused on AME-P attributions."

> "nearly all IETs emphasized the influence of AME-G attributions on students’ success and struggles. Additionally, the majority of EPTs highlighted the significance of AME-S and AME-E attributions, whereas IMTs predominantly focused on AME-P attributions."
