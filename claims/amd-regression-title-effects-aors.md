## Evidence

### Öncül Hasan 2026

Öncül Hasan, Dertsiz Kozan Betül, Dağ Umut, Çağlayan Mehtap, Alakuş Mehmet Fuat, Temel Cengiz, Utlu Elif Sedanur. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (age, gender, experience, title as independent variables; adjusted odds ratios reported) in the 440-physician survey. The article prints "aOR = 3.02; 95% CI: 1.03–8.88; p = 0.045" for specialists versus general practitioners and "aOR = 0.41" for residents on the dry/wet classification item; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Family medicine specialists were significantly more likely than general practitioners to correctly respond to the statement “AMD is the leading cause of severe vision impairment in the elderly in developed countries” (aOR = 3.02; 95% CI: 1.03–8.88; p = 0.045). For the statement “AMD is classified as dry/wet type,” residents were significantly less likely than general practitioners to provide a correct response (aOR = 0.41; 95% CI: 0.21–0.79; p = 0.010)."
