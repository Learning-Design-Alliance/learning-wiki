## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2024

Thomaschewski M, Kist M, Zimmermann M, Benecke C, Kalff JC, Krüger CM, Mann B, Türler A, Keck T, Hummel R. (2024). Conception and prospective multicentric validation of a Robotic Surgery Training Curriculum (RoSTraC) for surgical residents: from simulation via laboratory training to integration into the operation room. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-023-01813-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Learning-curve analysis in the validation study: residents documented repetition times uploaded to the RoSTraC cloud. "At the beginning of the learning curve, there is a steady improvement in execution times until a plateau phase was reached after approximately 11 repetitions."

> "For the RAS procedure "suturing of a segment of small intestine", the median number of repetitions to reach the objectives (expert level) was 11 (range 3–15)"

### Thomaschewski M 2024 (2)

Thomaschewski M, Kist M, Zimmermann M, Benecke C, Kalff JC, Krüger CM, Mann B, Türler A, Keck T, Hummel R. (2024). Conception and prospective multicentric validation of a Robotic Surgery Training Curriculum (RoSTraC) for surgical residents: from simulation via laboratory training to integration into the operation room. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-023-01813-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same learning-curve analysis (Fig. 4): the ulcer closure, cholecystectomy, and gastroenterostomy procedures were each performed only 5 times due to model costs, and the article states "more repetitions should be discussed in a cost benefit comparison."

> "However, a plateau phase was not reached within the 5 repetitions in the learning curve"
