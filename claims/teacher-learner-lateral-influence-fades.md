## Evidence

### Okazaki Shuntaro 2019

Okazaki Shuntaro, Muraoka Yoshihiro, Osu Rieko. (2019). Teacher-learner interaction quantifies scaffolding behaviour in imitation learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44049-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed rank comparison of Akaike causal influences averaged over first-half versus second-half imitation-phase trials in 14 pairs; the article reports "significantly smaller in the second half of the trials than for the first half (p = 0.036, R = 0.73)".
