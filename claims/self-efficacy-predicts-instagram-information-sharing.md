## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model with self-efficacy and enthusiasm for teaching as predictors of the three collaboration types, estimated on the 249-teacher sample. Both independent variables together predicted 7.2% of the variance in sharing information; co-creating was related to neither predictor.

> "The results show that self-efficacy is positively related to sharing information. Furthermore, enthusiasm for teaching was also positively related to seeking information."
