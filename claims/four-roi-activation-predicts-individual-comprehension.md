## Evidence

### Keller 2024

Keller, T. A., Mason, R. A., Legg, A. E., & Just, M. A. (2024). The neural and cognitive basis of expository text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00232-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-validation of a stepwise regression trained on two passage presentations and tested on the held-out presentation, in the fMRI study of 31 participants. The full-model stepwise procedure selected L VMPFC as most influential (ß = −0.49), indicating "participants who performed more poorly on the comprehension test tended to have higher activation in thisarea".

> "Each of these cross-validated models demonstrated a re liable relationship between acti vation in the selected ROIs and comprehension performance ( R2 = 0.47, (F(4,26) = 5.67, MSE = 0.017, p = 0.00203), R2 = 0.49 ( F(4,26) = 6.18, MSE = 0.016, p = 0.00123), and R2 = 0.49 ( F(4,26) = 6.23, MSE = 0.016, p = 0.00114), for the three possib le leave-one-out folds)."
