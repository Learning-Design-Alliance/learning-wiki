## Evidence

### UMANODAN Rino 2014

UMANODAN Rino, SHIMAZU Akihito, MINAMI Masahide, KAWAKAMI Norito. (2014). Effects of Computer-based Stress Management Training on Psychological Well-being and Work Performance in Japanese Employees: A Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial. Industrial Health 52(6). https://doi.org/10.2486/indhealth.2013-0209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison of completers (n=116) and non-completers (n=26) in the intervention group using t-tests. The article reports the quoted t and p values; no differences were found on other variables (p>0.05).

> "Non-completers had significantly higher psychological distress scores (t=2.04, p=0.043) and lower scores for seeking social support (t=−2.63, p=0.009) and changing a point of view compared to completers (t=−2.70, p=0.008)."
