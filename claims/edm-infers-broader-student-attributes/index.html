Narrative review statement in Section 4 on student model improvement; the review attributes these inferences to the cited studies (Baker et al. 2004; McQuiggan et al. 2008; Baker 2007; D'Mello et al. 2008) without reporting their designs or effect sizes.

"researchers have used EDM methods to infer whether a student is gaming the system [Baker et al. 2004], experiencing poor self-efficacy [McQuiggan et al. 2008], off-task [Baker 2007], or even if a student is bored or frustrated [D'Mello et al. 2008]."
