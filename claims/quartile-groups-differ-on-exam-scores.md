# The four exam-performance quartile groups differed significantly on first-unit exam scores, validating the grouping used for behavioral comparisons

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` An ANOVA showed exam scores differed significantly across the four quartile groups (F(3, 478) = 1174.49, η² = 0.88), with each group's mean significantly higher than the next in the expected order. [→ Hou 2025](#hou-2025)

## Evidence

### Hou 2025

Hou, C., Kuhlmann, S. L., Greene, J. A., Bernacki, M. L., & Hogan, K. A. (2025). Process mining measures students' help-seeking transitions when completing assignments in an online learning and assessment platform. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09442-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

The 482 students were divided into quartiles by first-unit exam score (high n = 123, mid-high n = 122, mid-low n = 116, low n = 121); the ANOVA found "statistically significantly different" scores, η² = 0.88, and Tukey tests confirmed each group exceeded the next in order.

> "An analysis of variance (ANOV A) indicated that the exam scores for the four quartile groups were statistically significantly different from each other (F(3, 478) = 1174.49, p <.001, η 2 = 0.88)"
