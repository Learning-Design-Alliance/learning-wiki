Evidence¶

Gaylor et al. 2015¶

Erika Gaylor, Donna Spiker, Xin Wei, Erin Lease, Arthur Reynolds. (2015). Midwest Child-Parent Center (CPC) PreK-3rd Grade School Reform Model: Impacts on Child and Family Outcomes Over Time. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562345

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Multi-method implementation fidelity evaluation using observations, structured interviews, surveys, and administrative data, rated at the school level in fall and spring of each year. Fidelity ratings were given at the school level based on the six key elements using a five point scale.

"Overall, fidelity of implementation in year one was moderately high (3.9 on a scale from 1 to 5 across six components). Across all districts, the highest rated element was Effective Learning Experiences (4.0). The lowest rated element was Parent Involvement (3.5). When averaging across the six elements, 96 percent of sites met the moderate-to-high fidelity standard defined as an average rating of greater than 3."
