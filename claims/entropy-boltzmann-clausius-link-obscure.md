## Subclaims
`q1 i?` While students eventually understand the Boltzmann picture of entropy, how the number of microscopic states relates to the Clausius ratio of exchanged heat to temperature, and why that ratio never decreases for an isolated system, remains obscure. [→ A. Ercoli 2023](#a-ercoli-2023)

## Evidence

### A. Ercoli 2023

A. Ercoli, V. Lubicz. (2023). Thermodynamics and microscopic theory: An educational proposal for the high school. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.20505](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.20505)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors report, from their educational experience, that the equivalence of the Boltzmann and Clausius definitions of entropy is a critical obstacle for high-school students. No empirical measurement is reported.

> "it remains often obscure how the number of microscopic states is related to the Clausius deﬁnition of entropy, i.e. to the ratio ∆ Q/T between exchanged heat and temperature in a transformation"
