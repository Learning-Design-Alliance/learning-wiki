Figure 1 summarizes faculty responses on which e-learning resources need to be developed and shared in the nationwide survey. The discussion links the multimedia assessment item demand to South Korea's national licensing examinations adopting computer-based testing with multimedia items, and notes clinical videos "have been the most popular contents in e-MedEdu."

> "Over half of the respondents pointed out a need for more resources on multimedia assessment items and clinical videos. Others also chose virtual patients, which was followed by sharing best lecture videos across institutions and open resources such as Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs)."
