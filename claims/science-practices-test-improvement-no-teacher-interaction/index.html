Evidence¶

Martin 2019¶

Martin, Nicole D., Tissenbaum, Catherine Dornfeld, Gnesdilow, Dana, Puntambekar, Sadhana. (2019). Fading distributed scaffolds: the importance of complementarity between teacher and material scaffolds. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9474-0

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mixed-model ANOVA of pre/post science practices test scores (14 multiple-choice and 4 open-ended items, maximum 22 points, Cronbach's alpha = .89) in the same two sixth-grade classes. The article reports a significant main effect of time (F(1,70) = 72.16, p < .001) and no significant teacher effect or interaction (teacher × time p = .225).

"Both classes also significantly improved on the science practices test from pre- to post-test, as shown again by the significant main effect of time (F(1,70) = 72.16, p < .001); the teacher main effect and interaction between teacher and time were not significant."
