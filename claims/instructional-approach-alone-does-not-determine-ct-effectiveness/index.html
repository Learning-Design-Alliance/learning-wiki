Evidence¶

Dawit T. Tiruneh 2014¶

Dawit T. Tiruneh, An Verburgh & Jan Elen. (2014). Effectiveness of Critical Thinking Instruction in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intervention Studies. Higher Education Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n1p1

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's descriptive categorization of 33 studies by approach reports 80% of general-approach studies (n=4) and 67% of mixed (n=2) versus 56% of infusion (n=5) reported significant CT gains; half of immersion studies did. The review cautions these proportions should be interpreted with caution due to small study counts.

"the majority of the studies which employed the general approach reported significant student CT improvement (n=4, 80%). In the second and third place are studies that adopted the mixed (n=2, 67%) and the infusion (n=5, 56%) approaches"
