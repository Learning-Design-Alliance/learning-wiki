## Evidence

### Daly R 2026

Daly R, Tunney E, Spooner M, Offiah G, Flood K, Kent F. (2026). Case‐based learning (CBL) in undergraduate health professions education: A realist review. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70179

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

This realist review reports, citing Nordquist (2012), that in a surgical CBL implementation lacking institutional recognition — 'the preparation had to be undertaken outside scheduled work hours and without pay' — there was open faculty conflict, no significant knowledge improvement over didactic learning and a lack of student engagement.

> "A high level of conflict characterized the first semester. There were strong tensions and open conflict among the faculty regarding the new intervention"
