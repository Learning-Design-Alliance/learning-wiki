Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree 2013¶

Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree. (2013). Development of an Instructional Model for Online Task-Based Interactive Listening for EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n3p30

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-Samples T-test on pre-test means of the two intact classes (46 students each): experimental 57.30 (SD=8.897), control 57.17 (SD=8.16). No significant difference; equivalence was not formally tested beyond this comparison.

"Clearly, no significant differences were found between the average score of two classe s’ pre-test (P=0.942, P≤0.05), according to I ndependent-Samples T-test analysis of SPSS."
