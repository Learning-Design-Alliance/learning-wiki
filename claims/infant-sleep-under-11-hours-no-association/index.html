q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multivariable-adjusted regression in Project Viva (n=1046) testing infant sleep (<11 hours/day vs ≥12) against maternal- and teacher-reported BRIEF and SDQ at age 7. The article reports no worse outcomes for the shortest infant sleep category; equivalence was not tested.
