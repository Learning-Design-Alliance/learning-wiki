## Evidence

### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert-scale survey responses from Year 1 medical students (n = 253) in the first TBL rollout year. Findings show elements positively received (≥75% 'positive' or 'very positive') included ease of accessing learning resources, clearly defined ILOs, interactive sessions, team composition, facilitator support, variety of sessions and real-world applicability of themes.

> "Other elements positively received (≥ 75% ‘positive’ or ‘very positive’) included the ease of accessing learning resources (Q7), clearly defined ILOs (Q8), interactive and engaging TBL sessions (Q14), team composition, participation, effective communication, disagreement resolution and equal voice and respect"
