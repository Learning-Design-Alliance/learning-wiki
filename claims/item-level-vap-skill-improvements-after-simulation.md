## Evidence

### Topbaş Eylem 2026

Topbaş Eylem, Sarmasoğlu Kılıkçıer Şenay, Tunçbilek Zahide. (2026). Prevention of Ventilator‐Associated Pneumonia in Intensive Care Units: The Effect of Scenario‐Based Simulation on Nursing Students' Skill Development Process. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level comparison of 13 teams' pretest and posttest ratings on the 23-item checklist using McNemar and McNemar‐Bowker tests; Table 3 prints significant improvements for items such as checking the physician's order (p = 0.016), oral care with 0.12% chlorhexidine (p = 0.008) and ventilator circuit filter checks (p = 0.004).

> "The number of teams who implemented the VAP prevention strategies was seen to increase after the simulation training (Table 3)."
