Argument in the Ordering Bias section, illustrated by a simulation: sequences of comparable difficulty with minimal learning and sequences increasing in difficulty with learning can produce nearly identical observed data, complicating parameter fitting.

"When all students follow an identical task sequence, the observed data from these two scenarios may be virtually indistinguishable. ˇCechák and Pelánek (2019) provide a speciﬁc illustration of this effect using simulation."
