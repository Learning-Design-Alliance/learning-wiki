## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Strategic winners showed a higher degree of 'worse' desire of challenge than risky challengers, purposefully avoiding higher-rated opponents. [→ Matsuda 2013](#matsuda-2013)

## Evidence

### Matsuda 2013

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of Game Show process data for 44 Game Show-condition students. Authors ruled out involuntary strategic selection: no correlation between pre-test scores and challenge strategy, and final ratings of strategic winners varied widely (mean 32.9, SD 15.0).

> "strategic winners (SW) had a higher degree of worse desire than risky challengers (RC); MSW=0. 66 (SD=0.14) vs. MRC=0. 40 (SD=0.12); F(1, 40)=9.54, p<0.001"
