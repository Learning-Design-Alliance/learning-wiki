## Evidence

### Talent Search 2006

Talent Search. (2006). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://www.ed.gov/programs/triotalent/index.html

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental Florida study of five Talent Search projects: 900 participants matched on 13 characteristics to 42,514 nonparticipants from the same high schools; effective sample size 1,800 after weighting. Completion was "84% compared with 70%", effect size 0.49, statistically significant, improvement index +19.

> "The Florida study indicated that Talent Search participants completed school at a significantly higher rate than comparison group students—84% compared with 70%."
