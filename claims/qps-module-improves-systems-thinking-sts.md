Qureshi Muhammad Fazal Hussain; Asif Fozia; Khan Shahmeer Raza; Akhtar Munazza; Shah Muzna; Altaf Ahmed; Latif Asad. (2026). Strengthening patient safety competencies among physician interns through a structured quality and safety course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09875-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired analysis of the validated 20-item Systems Thinking Scale (0–80 range) among 166 interns before and after the QPS module; the overall mean rose from 3.96 ± 0.22 to 4.09 ± 0.14, a "mean difference = 0.13; p < 0.001". Significant domain gains included seeking diverse perspectives and proposing system-level solutions.

> "Overall systems thinking capacity improved significantly following the intervention, with the mean System Thinking Scale (STS) score increasing from 3.96 ± 0.22 to 4.09 ± 0.14 (mean difference = 0.13; p < 0.001). Significant gains were observed across several domains"
