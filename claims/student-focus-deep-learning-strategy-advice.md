## Evidence

### Päuler-Kuppinger and Jucks 2018

Lena Päuler-Kuppinger & Regina Jucks. (2018). Out-of-Class Instruction in Higher Education – Impact of Approaches to Teaching and Discipline. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the correlational part of the online study with 70 academics, linking ATI-R scores to content-coded email advice. The article reports a "positive association to the ATI-R CCSF scale" for deep-learning strategy advice; no effect size is printed.

> "Regarding the types of advice given, answers related to deep-learning strategies showed a positive association to the ATI-R CCSF scale."
