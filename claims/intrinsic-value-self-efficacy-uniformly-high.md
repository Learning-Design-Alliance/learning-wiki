## Evidence

### Witti MJ 2026

Witti MJ, Coleman J-C, Richters C, Schönberger J, de Ruijter M-A, Stortz A, Bauer M, Kiriakidou R, Stadler M. (2026). Simulation-Based Radiology Training for Medical Technical Assistant Trainees: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/97489

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t tests comparing specialization groups on intrinsic value and self-efficacy scales. No significant group differences emerged, and the authors note that "intrinsic value and self-efficacy were uniformly high across both groups and did not differentiate learning outcomes."

> "Radiology- and laboratory-specialized trainees did not differ significantly in intrinsic value (t45=1.49; P=.14; Cohen d=0.44) or self-efficacy (t44=0.97; P=.34; Cohen d=0.29)."
