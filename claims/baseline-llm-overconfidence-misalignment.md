### Elena Eleftheriou 2026

Elena Eleftheriou, George Pallis, and Marios Constantinides. 2026. Confidence Without Competence in AI-Assisted Knowledge Work. CHIWORK '26. [doi:10.1145/3808045.3808055](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808045.3808055)

Between-subjects evaluation of Deep3 with 85 participants: the Baseline condition showed an overconfidence effect, with "high perceived understanding (𝑀= 4.65in Task 1)" against the lowest graded understanding of all conditions on both tasks. No effect size is printed for this gap.

> "Participants reported high perceived understanding (𝑀= 4.65in Task 1) but achieved the lowest actual grades in the study (𝑀= 1.90for Task 1; 𝑀= 1.40for Task 2)"
