`q2 i?` Type B responses explicitly identifying the closed nature of the circuit rose from 20% to 80% (5th grade) and 40% to 75% (7th grade), significant by paired t-tests (5th: t(19) = 3.26, p = 0.004; 7th: t(19) = 2.51, p = 0.02).
