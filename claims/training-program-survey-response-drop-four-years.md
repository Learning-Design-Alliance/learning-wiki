### Stanisław D. Głażek 2008

Stanisław D. Głażek. (2008). Heuristic model of teaching. https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4796 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports a longitudinal study of the same training program used to estimate the minimal training period (Section III.D, citing [53]): mail-survey response fell from almost three quarters of participants after one year to about one third after four years. The article states it was not found why the number dropped.

> "a year after completion of the training in basic physics almost three quarters of the program participants eagerly responded to mail surveys concerning their subsequent experiences in teaching. However, only about one third responded after four years."
