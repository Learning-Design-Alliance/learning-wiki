## Evidence

### Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017

Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org/learning-studios

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student survey collaboration indicators (encouraging classmates, learning from classmates, helping brainstorm and solve problems) in the pilot. The report notes "ceiling effects on survey items limited insights about impacts in this area," with anecdotes from open-ended responses; 14 percent of students cited collaboration as a growth area.

> "Across these indicators, on average, over two-thirds of all students reported taking these actions more than a couple times in the Learning Studio. For high schoolers, that percentage was 74 percent."

### Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017 (2)

Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org/learning-studios

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-thinking prompts and pre/post survey items in the pilot. Students showed a statistically significant increase in comfort assembling objects without instructions, yet few differences appeared in open descriptions of a designer/maker, and most prompt solutions lacked key design-process characteristics.

> "Moreover, the great majority of students’ solutions to three distinct design-thinking prompts did not demonstrate a grasp of key characteristics of the design process, such as developing an understanding of the problem space, developing prototypes, testing and iterating the design."
