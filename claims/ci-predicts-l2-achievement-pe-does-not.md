### Li Gengchun 2025

Li Gengchun. (2025). Understanding the roles of grit and well-being in L2 achievement among Chinese university english learners. BMC Psychology, 13, 576. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02933-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structural equation modeling analysis of questionnaire data from 343 first-year English majors, with L2 achievement measured by National College Entrance Examination English scores. The path analysis found that "CI significantly positively predicted L2 achievement (β = 0.381, p < 0.05)" while PE's path was non-significant. No effect size was printed for this finding.

> "PE did not significantly predict L2 achievement (β = 0.119, p > 0.05), and CI significantly positively predicted L2 achievement (β = 0.381, p < 0.05)"
