Descriptive subgroup analysis from Table 1 (N = 63 treated students). Due to small cell sizes, the authors conducted no tests of statistical significance for the racial/ethnic subgroups, so these are untested descriptive shifts.

> "At Time 2, all racial and ethnic groups demonstrated a much greater likelihood of being unsure. Whites and Hispanics identified, on aver age, approximately 20 nationalities as "not sure," while Blacks identified approximately 22 and Asians approximately 19."
