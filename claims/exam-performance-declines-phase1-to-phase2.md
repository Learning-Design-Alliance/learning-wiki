### Bansal Sandeep 2025

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of average NBME examination-set scores in the same longitudinal cohort; the article reports a significant effect of time (p < 0.001) with a large effect size (ηp² = 0.16), and "a notable declining trend in examination scores from across the assessment sets in Phase 1 to end of Phase 2 assessment."

> "A Bonferroni adjusted post-hoc analysis revealed significant differences in performance between examination sets one and four (82 (10.37) v. 72.53 (18.90); p < 0.001), two and four (78.73 (8.57) v. 72.53 (18.90); p < 0.05), and three and four (77.76 (10.55) v. 72.53 (18.90); p < 0.05), suggesting a notable declining trend in examination scores from across the assessment sets in Phase 1 to end of Phase 2 assessment."
