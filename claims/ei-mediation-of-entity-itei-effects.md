Bootstrapping mediation analysis (N = 5000 samples) within the path model showed indirect effects through EI dimensions; the article reports the standardized indirect effects β = −.05 (Portuguese grades) and β = .03 (negative emotions), and notes the mediating effects were small.

> "EI attenuated entity conceptions of EI because EI weakened the negative link of entity ITEIs and Portuguese grades (β = −.05) and negative emotions towards school (β = .03, through an indirect effect)."
