### Suzane F. Pinto 2020

Suzane F. Pinto, Ronan S. Ferreira, Miguel M. Costa, and Agmael M. Silva. (2020). A proposal for an active methodology for physics labs. arXiv:2005.06532 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.06532](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.06532)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous Likert-scale survey of students who experienced the methodology. The split result shows most respondents did not associate the high freedom with failures, though a minority did; the article reports only response percentages with no test statistic.

> "In theItem 6 graph, we see that 10% of the interviewees responded that they totally disagree with the questioning, 40% that partially disagree, 30% partially agree and only 20% totally agreed that excess of freedom was a bad factor, contributing to failures in the project."
