## Evidence

### Mosher 2024

Mosher, D. M., Burkhauser, M. A., & Kim, J. S. (2024). Improving second-grade reading comprehension through a sustained content literacy intervention: A mixed-methods study examining the mediating role of domain-specific vocabulary. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(4), 550–568. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000868

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression of survey responses from the teacher subsample (N = 48 interviewed; survey completed by all 48), controlling for self-reported explicit teaching of target words and gender. The article reports "treatment teachers reporting a half standard deviation of greater exposure" (β1 = 0.54); the abstract labels this Effect Size [ES] = .54, a small effect. Survey results served as a fidelity proxy because COVID-19 closures prevented observations.

> "Results (Table 6) indicated significant differences of self-reported incidental exposure between treatment and control conditions, with treatment teachers reporting a half standard deviation of greater exposure (𝛽𝛽1 = 0.54, 𝑆𝑆𝑆𝑆 = 0.20, 𝑝𝑝 < .01)."
