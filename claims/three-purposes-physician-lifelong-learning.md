### O’Brien BC 2024

O’Brien BC, Collins S, Haddock LM, Sani S, Rivera JA. (2024). More Than Maintaining Competence: A Qualitative Study of How Physicians Conceptualize and Engage in Lifelong Learning. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1327

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 34 academic physicians at one institution, analyzed with an interpretivist approach; the authors report this three-purpose interpretation as their central finding, "serving three purposes".

> "We interpreted participants’ descriptions and examples of lifelong learning as serving three purposes: maintaining competence, supporting personal growth and fulfillment, and engaging in professional stewardship."
