### Ghimire 2025

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Using Unguided Peer Collaboration to Facilitate Early Educators' Pedagogical Development: An Example from Physics TA Training. [doi:10.3390/educsci15081038](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15081038)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of TA performance on MCS Q25, involving two parallel wires carrying current in an external magnetic field. The article reports d = 0.91 and a 37% gain; after discussion, all TA groups recognized the mistake of neglecting the force from wire 1's magnetic field.

> "The individual score of 63% increased to 100% after group work, as shown in Table 1. It had an effect size of 0.91 and a gain of 37%."
