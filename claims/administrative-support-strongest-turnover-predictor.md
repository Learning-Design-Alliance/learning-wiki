Multivariate logistic regression on SASS/TFS data (Table A-2 odds ratios) controlling for student, teacher, and school characteristics; the report states administrative support has "the strongest relationship with teacher turnover."

> "Teacher reports of a lack of administrative support have the strongest relationship with teacher turnover. In a model controlling for other factors, teachers who strongly disagree that their administration is supportive are more than twice as likely to leave their school or teaching than teachers who strongly agree"
