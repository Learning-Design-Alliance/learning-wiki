## Evidence

### Bösner S 2015

Bösner S, Pickert J, Stibane T. (2015). Teaching differential diagnosis in primary care using an inverted classroom approach: student satisfaction and gain in skills and knowledge. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0346-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Theme analysis of focus group and free-text responses from all 17 participants. One student explained that usually "you learn from a disease-oriented point of view…but the patient usually presents with a symptom", and another said epidemiology "helps to build a much stronger hierarchy of differential diagnoses".

> "All students appreciated the symptom-oriented approach, which was seen to reflect the reality of daily practice."
