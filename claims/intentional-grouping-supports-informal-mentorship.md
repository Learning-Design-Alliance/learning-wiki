## Evidence

### Talafian 2025

Talafian, H., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., Shafer, D., Stelzer, T., & Kuo, E. (2025). Responsive professional development: A facilitation approach for teachers' development in a physics teaching community of practice. Teaching and Teacher Education, 153, 104812. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tate.2024.104812

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

During the program's second summer workshop, facilitators formally paired teachers by school context and physics level, matching novices with veterans. Daily post-PD surveys revealed that "several pairs needed to be rear- ranged"; regrouping two overwhelmed novice teachers with veteran cohort-1 teachers produced survey feedback that the new pairing was more productive.

> "Upon surveying the groups, we found that several pairs needed to be rear- ranged to maximize collaboration."
