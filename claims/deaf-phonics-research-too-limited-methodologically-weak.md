`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Wang and Williams' systematic review of 11 qualitative and 39 quantitative meta-analyses across three learner groups. While hearing students' research supported phonemic awareness and phonics instruction particularly in prekindergarten through first grade, the review attributes to them the conclusion that deaf learner research "was too limited or too methodologically weak to permit conclusions to be drawn."

> "However, they also acknowledged that the research conducted with deaf learners, "was too limited or too methodologically weak to permit conclusions to be drawn" (p. 342)."
