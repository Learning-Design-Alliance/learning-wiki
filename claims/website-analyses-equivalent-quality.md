## Evidence

### Hsieh 2012

Hsieh, P.-H., & Chen, N.-S. (2012). Effects of reflective thinking in the process of designing software on students' learning performances. Computers & Education, 58(1), 419-431.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of written evaluations of a designated dynamic website before and after the course, via T-Test on word counts (pre-test 181, post-test 230 all students) and NVivo 7 coding. None reached significance.

> "even though the experimental group students wrote pre -test and post -test analyses of the dynamic website's design in greater volume than the control group students, the control group students were able to grasp the key points of design in the pre -test."
