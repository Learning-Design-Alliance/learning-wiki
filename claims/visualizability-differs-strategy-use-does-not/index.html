Evidence¶

Kucinkas 2025¶

Kucinkas, A., Retsa, C., Meijer, P. B. L., Wallace, M. T., Gori, M. & Murray, M. M. (2025). Learning visual to auditory sensory substitution reveals flexibility in image to sound mapping. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00385-4

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on debrief-questionnaire measures from 60 sighted adults; "a significant group effect for the ability to draw soundscapes" with printed effect size η²ₚ = 0.186 (partial eta-squared = 0.186), a large effect per the η² thresholds; post hoc Sidak comparisons showed Traditional (p = .041) and Reversed (p = .003) higher than Control.

"Results indicated a significant group effect for the ability to draw soundscapes (F(2,57) = 6.51, p = 0.003, η²ₚ = 0.186 )."

Kucinkas 2025 (2)¶

Kucinkas, A., Retsa, C., Meijer, P. B. L., Wallace, M. T., Gori, M. & Murray, M. M. (2025). Learning visual to auditory sensory substitution reveals flexibility in image to sound mapping. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00385-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Debrief-questionnaire ANOVAs from the same 60 sighted adults; "No significant differences were found for strategy use", confidence, or error awareness; the printed η²ₚ values are small (0.004–0.033). Non-significant tests in a sample of 60 do not show that the groups are the same on these measures.

"No significant differences were found for strategy use (F(2,57) = 0.48, p = 0.622, η²ₚ = 0.016), confidence on incorrect answers (F(2,57) = 0.96, p = 0.387, η²ₚ = 0.033), confidence on correct answers (F(2,57) = 0.10, p = 0.902, η²ₚ = 0.004), or awareness of errors during evaluation (F(2,57) = 0.88, p = 0.421, η²ₚ = 0.030 )."
