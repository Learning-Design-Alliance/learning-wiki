q3 · i? · review · r3

Frequency analysis of delivery actors across the described training interventions. The review reports Indigenous involvement in delivery in n = 35 (51%) of articles, counting Elders/mentors/cultural educators/community organizations and other categories.

"Finally, half of the articles that described cultural safety training reported that Indigenous people(s) were involved in delivering the intervention (n = 35; 51%)."

q3 · i? · review · r2

Frequency analysis of who delivered the training. The review reports university professors/administrators (36%), clinicians/clinical instructors (30%), and Indigenous Elders/mentors/cultural educators/community organizations (28%) as the most frequently mentioned deliverers.

"In terms of delivering cultural safety training interventions, university professors/administrators were mentioned in approximately twenty-five articles that described cultural safety training (25; 36%)."
