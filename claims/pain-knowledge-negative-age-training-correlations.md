## Evidence

### Ahmadi Sajjad 2023

Ahmadi Sajjad, Vojdani Parya, MortezaBagi Hamid Reza. (2023). The study of nurses' knowledge and attitudes regarding pain management and control in emergency departments. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-023-00793-y

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of knowledge scores against demographic variables in the 400-nurse cross-sectional survey. The article reports both correlations as significant and negative: "r= -0.104, P = 0.038" for age and "r= -0.148, P = 0.003" for prior retraining participation.

> "While older nurses had significantly less knowledge about pain control and management (r= -0.104, P = 0.038), and nurses who previously participated in pain retraining courses had significantly less knowledge of pain control and management (r= -0.148, P = 0.003)"
