Evidence¶

Strobel 2009¶

Strobel, J., & van Barneveld, A. (2009). When is PBL More Effective? A Meta-synthesis of Meta-analyses Comparing PBL to Conventional Classrooms. Interdisciplinary Journal of Problem-Based Learning, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.7771/1541-5015.1046

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

A qualitative meta-synthesis of prior meta-analyses comparing PBL with traditional instruction. The synthesis reports that "PBL was superior when it comes to long-term retention"; no pooled effect size is printed in the available text.

"Findings indicated that PBL was superior when it comes to long-term retention, skill development and satisfaction of students and teachers"
