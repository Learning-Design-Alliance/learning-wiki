Anne Podolsky and Leib Sutcher 2016¶

Anne Podolsky and Leib Sutcher. (2016). California Teacher Shortages: A Persistent Problem. Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606761

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey results on shortage subject areas and school levels (Figures 2 and 3): special education 88%, mathematics 58%, science 57%; high school 62%, middle school 60%, elementary 37%. The quoted sentence states the headline pattern.

"most districts report not having enough middle and high school teachers—especially in math and science, and nine out of 10 report shortages in special education"
