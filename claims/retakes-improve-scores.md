## Evidence

### Supriya K. 2024

Supriya K., Bang Christofer, Ebie Jessica, Pagliarulo Christopher, Tucker Derek, Villegas Kaela, Wright Christian, Brownell Sara. (2024). Optional Exam Retakes Reduce Anxiety but may Exacerbate Score Disparities Between Students with Different Social Identities. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11440740/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t tests comparing first-attempt and post-retake total exam scores in each course found "students scored higher on retakes compared with the first attempts" (all P < 0.05 except exam 1, Course 1), with average gains of 17.6, 26.4, and 38.1 points in Courses 1–3 respectively.

> "Overall, students scored higher on retakes compared with the first attempts (paired t tests, all P < 0.05, except for exam 1, Course 1) (Figure 3). The average difference in total exam score on first attempts and total exam score students received in the course after taking retakes into account was 17.6 points out of 390 (4.5 percentage points) in Course 1, 26.4 points out of 360 (7.3 percentage points) in Course 2, and 38.1 points out of 300 (12.7 percentage points) in Course 3."
