### Bangura Marie 2025

Bangura Marie, Ganyaglo Gabriel, Kupualor David, Boateng Alex, Sepenu Perez, Koranteng Isaac, Lawrence Emma R., Rooney Deborah, Schmidt Payton. (2025). Evaluating Fundamentals of Vaginal Surgery for Use Among Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Accra, Ghana. International Urogynecology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00192-025-06343-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported confidence from the 8-item survey administered to all 34 enrolled residents using a 6-point Likert scale (0 = not sure to 5 = extremely confident). The article reports all participants were "not at all confident" in vaginal hysterectomy and other vaginal surgeries.

> "All participants described being “not at all confident” in performing vaginal hysterectomy and other vaginal surgeries and “somewhat or slightly confident” in their surgical skills."

### Bangura Marie 2025 (2)

Bangura Marie, Ganyaglo Gabriel, Kupualor David, Boateng Alex, Sepenu Perez, Koranteng Isaac, Lawrence Emma R., Rooney Deborah, Schmidt Payton. (2025). Evaluating Fundamentals of Vaginal Surgery for Use Among Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Accra, Ghana. International Urogynecology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00192-025-06343-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey-reported median confidence ratings for specific vaginal surgeries among the 34 residents, presented as median values on the 6-point Likert scale. The article reports "median confidence level among both novice and experienced participants was 1" across the listed procedures.

> "The median confidence level among both novice and experienced participants was 1—“not at all confident”—in performing vaginal hysterectomy, anterior and posterior colporrhaphy, apical suspension, and Colpocleisis"
