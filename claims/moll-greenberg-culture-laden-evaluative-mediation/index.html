Evidence¶

Peter Smagorinsky 1995¶

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543065003191

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Narrative review attributing this to Moll and Greenberg (1990), who studied Southwestern Mexican American students in school and home community and found students engaged in complex tasks such as repairing farm machinery and managing budgets while being judged as failing in school. No effect size is printed.

"Moll and Greenberg (1990) concluded that the determination that the students were cognitively deficient was a function of culture-laden means of evaluative mediation"

Peter Smagorinsky 1995 (2)¶

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170682

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports, citing Moll and Greenberg (1990), that students judged 'failing' academically performed complex tasks such as repairing farm machinery and managing budgets at home, and that deficit conclusions stemmed from assessment means 'more congenial to students of European American origin'. Reported here as the review-style attribution it is; the study's design is not described in the article.

"Moll and Greenberg (1990) concluded that the determination that the students were cognitively deficient was a function of culture-laden means of evaluative mediation that were more congenial to students of European American origin and middle-class backgrounds"
