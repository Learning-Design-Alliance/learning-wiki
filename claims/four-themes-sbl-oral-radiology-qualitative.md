`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended responses independently coded by two researchers. Themes: (1) enhanced skill development and safe learning environment, (2) bridging theory with practice, (3) barriers, (4) looking ahead.

> "Thematic analysis supported quantitative findings and revealed four key themes. Students valued the role of SBL in skill development, providing a psychologically safe learning environment, and bridging theory and practice."
