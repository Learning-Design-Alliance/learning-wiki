Evidence¶

Peters 2018¶

Peters, O., Körndle, H. & Narciss, S. (2018). Effects of a formative assessment script on how vocational students generate formative feedback to a peer's or their own performance. Eur J Psychol Educ, 33, 117–143. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-017-0344-y

q2 · i? · causal · r?

In the same quasi-experimental study, students giving internal feedback to their own drafts with FAS support produced more comments and ignored fewer erroneous elements than unsupported students, per the article's summary of results.

"On the one hand, FAS-supported students generated more comments and ignored fewer erroneous elements in their own drafts."
