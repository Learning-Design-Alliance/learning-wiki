## Evidence

### Yue Huang 2024

Yue Huang, Joshua Wilson, & Henry May. (2024). Exploring the Long-Term Effects of the Statewide Implementation of an Automated Writing Evaluation System on Students' State Test ELA Performance. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00443-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model comparison of two SIPTW-adjusted initial growth models for 134,425 students' repeated SAGE ELA scores with cluster-robust standard errors. The log-time coefficient was 33.46 (p < 0.001) in the initial model comparison table.

> "The model with the logarithmic term demonstrated a smaller AIC of 966.25, compared to the AIC of 967.41 for the model with the quadratic term."
