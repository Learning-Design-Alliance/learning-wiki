## Evidence

### Öz 2018

Öz, H., & Özturan, T. (2018). Computer-based and paper-based testing: Does the test administration mode influence the reliability and validity of achievement tests? Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(1), 67-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1175793

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis of the same 60-item achievement test administered in both modes to the randomly assigned groups; split-half coefficients are reported as rp = .756 and rc = .903, which the authors describe as "significant level of reliability coefficients".

> "Split -half reliability method was utilized for each exam mode, and Table 4 gives the reliability coefficients ( rp = .756, rc=.903). As a result, it can be mentioned that both exam administration modes have significant level of reliability coefficients."
