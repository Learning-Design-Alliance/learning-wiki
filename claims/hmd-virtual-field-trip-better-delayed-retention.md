# Students who experienced the virtual field trip via a 3D HMD scored higher on a delayed posttest roughly three weeks later than 2D-video students

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The HMD group scored significantly higher than the 2D video group on the delayed posttest an average of 19.44 days later (d = 0.70). [→ Guido Makransky · Richard E. Mayer 2022](#guido-makransky-richard-e-mayer-2022)

## Evidence

### Guido Makransky · Richard E. Mayer 2022

Guido Makransky · Richard E. Mayer. (2022). Benefits of Taking a Virtual Field Trip in Immersive Virtual Reality: Evidence for the Immersion Principle in Multimedia Learning. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1771–1798. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09675-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test (Hypothesis 5) on the delayed posttest after the full six-lesson intervention, "an average of 19.44 days" after the field trip. The HMD group scored significantly higher, d = 0.70, a medium effect.

> "the HMD group (M = 18.90, SD = 4.35) scored significantly higher than the video group (M = 15.64, SD = 4.97) on the delayed posttest, t (100) = 3.507, p < 0.001, d = 0.70."
