## Evidence

### Alabi Oludare 2022

Alabi Oludare, Hill Rhodri, Walsh Michael, Carroll Camilla. (2022). Introduction of an ENT emergency—safe boot camp into postgraduate surgical training in the Republic of Ireland. Irish Journal of Medical Science, 191(1). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-021-02581-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymised post-boot-camp questionnaire self-report from the RCSI cohort; percentages are descriptive self-assessments with no statistical testing reported.

> "Ninety-two percent (46/50) of respondents said the course added to their previous knowledge while 6% were neutral. Seventy percent (38/54) of respondents agreed that it improved their technical skills; 22% were neutral regarding this. Eighty-five percent (45/53) of respondents felt they would be more confident managing ENT related issues when on call, 11% were neutral."
