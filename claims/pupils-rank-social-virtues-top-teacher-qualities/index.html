Evidence¶

Grace E. Bird 1917¶

Grace E. Bird. (1917). Pupils' Estimates of Teachers. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072302

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Written survey of 139 high school girls, 253 high school boys, and 150 normal school students asked "What are the five or six best qualities of the best teachers you have ever had?" Answers were unsigned and collected "without previous warning"; the ten qualities with the largest average percentage of votes formed the working basis.

"The percentages of frequency and their distribution show agreement that the ten most important qualities are fairness, kindness, sociability, sense of humor, good temper, ability to discipline, neatness, patience, adequate preparation, and ability to impart information."
