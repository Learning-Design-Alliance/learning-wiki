## Evidence

### Kimmo Eriksson 2019

Kimmo Eriksson, Ola Helenius, Andreas Ryve. (2019). Using TIMSS items to evaluate the effectiveness of different instructional practices. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9473-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-level (hierarchical linear modeling) analyses of Swedish eighth-grade TIMSS data from 2003, 2007, 2011 and 2015 waves, with class-level instruction variables as predictors. The article reports "consistently positive estimated effects" for ClassListen and ClassMemo and a consistently negative effect for ClassDaily; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Both ClassListen and ClassMemo had consistently positive estimated effects on achievement, whereas Class- Daily had a consistently negative estimated effect."
