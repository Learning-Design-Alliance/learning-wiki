## Evidence

### Cecilio-Fernandes D 2017

Cecilio-Fernandes D, Medema H, Collares CF, Schuwirth L, Cohen-Schotanus J, Tio RA. (2017). Comparison of formula and number-right scoring in undergraduate medical training: a Rasch model analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1051-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Figure-based numerical result visualized in Figs. 1 and 2 and described in the Results text: formula-scoring items "ranged from −2 to 2 logit" while number-right items showed wider ranges across Tests 1 and 2.

> "The items using formula scoring varied less in terms of difficulty than the items using number-right scoring, resulting in lower discrimination regarding student ability. Because of that, the students subjected to number-right scoring could be better differentiated in both tests"
