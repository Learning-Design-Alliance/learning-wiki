## Evidence

### de Laat 2007

de Laat, M., Lally, V., Lipponen, L. & Simons, R.-J. (2007). Online teaching in networked learning communities: A multi-method approach to studying the role of the teacher. Instructional Science, vol. 35, no. 3, pp. 257-286. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-006-9007-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' conclusion from the timeline analysis of the two NLCs, in which activity concentrated in the middle phase and participation and density patterns shifted across the three 10-day phase samples. This is the authors' interpretive conclusion rather than a tested comparison.

> "when describing processes it is important to take into account that these processes are not static throughout time but have diﬀerent dynamics at various stages of the collaborative work."
