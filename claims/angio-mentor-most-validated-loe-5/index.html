Evidence¶

Haiser Alexander 2022¶

Haiser Alexander, Aydin Abdullatif, Kunduzi Basir, Ahmed Kamran, Dasgupta Prokar. (2022). A Systematic Review of Simulation-Based Training in Vascular Surgery. The Journal of Surgical Research. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2022.05.009

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of validation studies for ANGIO Mentor in angioplasty and stenting. The review reports the simulator differentiated performance between 32 surgical trainees, 20 vascular surgeons and 29 medical students, and that "proficiency levels were retained up to 3 mo" in the Maertens randomized controlled trial.

"Maertens et al. was awarded an LoE of 5/5 as proficiency levels were retained up to 3 mo, alongside an improvement in patient outcomes and better operative performance."
