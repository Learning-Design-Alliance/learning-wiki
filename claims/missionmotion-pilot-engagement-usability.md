Enhancing Kinematics Understanding Through a Real-Time Graph-Based Motion Video Game. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot implementation in a two-hour session with mouse-only control, structured in an individual exploration phase followed by a collective competition phase, evaluated with the MEEGA+ questionnaire. The authors report "strong engagement, positive perceptions of usability" from the abstract-level summary of Figs. 5 and 6; no effect sizes are printed.

> "In 2025, we conducted a pilot test of the physical–computational environment with five 9th-grade groups from different educational settings: a public high school in Montevideo, a public high school outside the capital, and a private high school in Montevideo."
