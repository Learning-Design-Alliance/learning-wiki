## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The writing task factor (argumentative vs. unspecific) had no significant effect on the combined GAMMA and BSM measures. [→ Lehmann 2019](#lehmann-2019)

## Evidence

### Lehmann 2019

Lehmann, T., Pirnay-Dummer, P., & Schmidt-Borcherding, F. (2019). Fostering integrated mental models of different professional knowledge domains: instructional approaches and model-based analyses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09704-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The omnibus MANOVA on 92 participants found no significant effect of the writing task factor on the combined dependent measures, "Wilks' λ = .995, F(2,87) = .208".

> "The omnibus MANOVA indicated that participants' knowledge integration as assessed by GAMMA and BSM was not affected by the writing task, Wilks' λ = .995, F(2,87) = .208, p > .05, ηp2 = .005."
