## Evidence

### Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur 2025

Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur, Ozbey Furkan, Yilmaz Birkan Eyup. (2025). Effect of artificial intelligence-assisted personalized feedback on radiographic diagnostic performance of dental students: a controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07875-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons of 15-question pre- and post-tests in the randomized controlled study. "post-test scores significantly increased" in both groups; only p-values are printed, no effect sizes.

> "In the experimental group, post-test scores significantly increased following the personalized feedback generated by ChatGPT-4o (pre-test: 7.8 ± 1.2, post-test: 11.4 ± 1.1; p < 0.001). Similarly, in the control group, a significant improvement was observed in post-test scores following classical feedback (pre-test: 7.7 ± 1.3, post-test: 9.0 ± 1.4; p < 0.01)."
