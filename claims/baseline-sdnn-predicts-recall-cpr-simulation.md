### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Wacht Oren, Ben Shlomo Izhar, Gitler Asaf, Gidron Yori. (2025). Does heart rate variability predict and improve performance in pediatric CPR?—a simulation study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-025-01209-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in a randomized simulation study of 57 paramedic students and medics performing CPR on a manikin, controlling for group. The article reports "a significant positive weak correlation only between log-SDNN and recall memory score, r = 0.28 p = 0.04"; other HRV indices showed no significant correlations.
