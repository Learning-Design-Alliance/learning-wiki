Descriptive analysis of nationally representative teacher survey data (NTPS and Teacher Follow-up Survey) reported in the executive summary and introduction. The report states that "in 2021, 44% of teachers of color said they would “leave teaching as soon as possible”" and that 9% of Black teachers versus 7% of White teachers left in 2022.
