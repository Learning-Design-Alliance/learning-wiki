## Evidence

### Styles 2014

Styles, B., Stevens, E., Bradshaw, S., & Clarkson, R. (2014). Vocabulary Enrichment Intervention Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN67513320/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

On-treatment analysis using total delivery time from teacher logs as the dosage measure, replacing dichotomous group allocation. Dosage was "highly variable, ranging from 33 to 85 hours with a median dosage of 53 hours". No significant dosage effect was found.

> "The ‘on-treatment’ analysis, taking into account the extent of delivery and those that switched groups, revealed no significant effect of dosage (p=0.38)."
