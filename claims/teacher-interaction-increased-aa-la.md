## Evidence

### Velásquez-Rojas F 2022

Velásquez-Rojas F, Fajardo JE, Zacharias D, Laguna MF. (2022). Effects of the COVID-19 pandemic in higher education: a particular case from the perspective of complex systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0275352

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey-based comparison of teacher interaction (T) across contexts (Fig. 3c). The article reports that for AA and LA groups "the interaction with teachers increased in the virtual context"; Table 3 shows relative changes of -0.1% (HA), 12.8% (AA), 5.9% (LA).

> "Note that for the HA group it has the same weight in both contexts (face-to-face and virtual), while for the AA and LA groups the interaction with teachers increased in the virtual context."
