## Evidence

### Shakir T 2026

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The review reports a prospective cross-skill comparison of 60 participants (20 novices, 20 intermediates, 20 experts) performing 6 robotic suturing trials each, 360 trials total, with ForceSense force measurement. The quoted statistics are from that study.

> "Experts completed tasks quicker (22s vs. 41s, p = 0.003), and used lower force (19 N vs. 29 N, p = 0.032), reflecting gentler tissue handling."
