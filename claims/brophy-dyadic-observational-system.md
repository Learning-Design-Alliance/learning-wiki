# Brophy's dyadic observational system coded student responses to teacher questions as correct, partly correct, do not know, or error, and coded each teacher's response to those student responses

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Daniel Robinson 2009

Daniel Robinson. (2009). Editor's Note: Remembering Jere Brophy. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-009-9115-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Attributed by Rosenshine in his tribute: the instrument allowed observers to code each teacher's responses to student responses, and Brophy wove the correlational results into statements on which types of responses to student answers were most effective for achievement.

> "Jere and his colleagues developed a dyadic observational system that enabled observers to code both teacher and student behavior. Student responses to a teacher's question were coded as correct, partly correct, do not know, and error."
