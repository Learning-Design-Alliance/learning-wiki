## Evidence

### Geduk Gediz 2026

Geduk Gediz, Hasırcı Utku Cem, Kusay Didem Dumanlı, Aras Rabia Çayır, Çapar İsmail, Altın Edanur, Şeker Çiğdem. (2026). A comparative analysis of the performance of large Language models in the dentistry specialty examination. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37800-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 208 Turkish DUS multiple-choice questions answered by seven LLM chatbots, scored against a predetermined answer key. ChatGPT 4.0 led with 91.3% accuracy and "demonstrated a significantly higher accuracy rate compared to all other LLMs"; Grok was lowest at 75.5%.

> "The accuracy rates were as follows: 91.3% for ChatGPT 4.0, 82.2% for ChatGPT 4.5, 86.1% for Gemini, 82.2% for DeepSeek, 78.4% for Claude, 87% for Copilot, and 75.5% for Grok (Table 1). ChatGPT 4.0 demonstrated a significantly higher accuracy rate compared to all other LLMs."
