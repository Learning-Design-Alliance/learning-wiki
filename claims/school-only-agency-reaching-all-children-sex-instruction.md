`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

Theoretical argument from the essay's 'A Fundamental Requirement' section, offered by a former high school principal. The author reasons that millions of moral parents refuse or are unable to teach sex, and that 'free literature cannot meet the need' because quack literature already fills the field. No empirical data are offered.

> "The school is the only organized agency that touches all the children, and only through it, by regular or special teachers, can we assure ourselves that sex shall be taught."
