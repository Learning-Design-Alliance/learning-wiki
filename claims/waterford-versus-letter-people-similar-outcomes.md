# Waterford Early Reading Level One™ and Let's Begin with the Letter People® produce similar outcomes in oral language and print knowledge

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Level One™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/541

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

WWC-calculated head-to-head comparison from the same randomized controlled trial (16 classrooms; 241–251 children per measure). Oral language average improvement index was –1 percentile point; print knowledge average was –3 percentile points (range –13 to +2), all non-significant.

> "The differences between the Waterford Early Reading Level One™ and Let's Begin with the Letter People® groups were not statistically significant for either measure as calculated by the WWC, and the average effect size was neither statistically significant nor large enough to be considered substantively important according to the WWC criteria (that is, at least 0.25)."
