## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on the same within-group cohort, benchmarking each student's absolute error rate against the average error rate of a pre-pandemic reference group of 10,693 students. The relative error rate "significantly decreased by 2.43% during the school shutdown", contrary to the authors' hypothesis.

> "Contrary to our expectation, the relative error rate significantly decreased by 2.43% during the school shutdown compared to the same time window in the previous year (b = -1.21e-02; t = -5.06; p < .001)."
