## Evidence

### Wang Junyu 2025

Wang Junyu, Yang Yong, Wu Jun, Xie Ting, Guo Xuejun, Yuan Xun, Li Ting, Zhang Yu, Zhao Huiying, Zhuang Jie. (2025). Association between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity trajectories and academic achievement in Chinese primary school children: a 3-year longitudinal study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21487-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal cohort analysis of 202 children using the SAS Proc Traj group-based trajectory model on compositional-data-transformed accelerometer MVPA over three annual assessments. The model fit statistics supported "three distinct MVPA trajectories were identified" with the printed group proportions.

> "Based on the BIC, AIC, professional judgment, and the principle of summarizing the distinctive features of the data in the most parsimonious and useful manner [33], three distinct MVPA trajectories were identified: Low MVPA (74.8%), Decline MVPA (13.2%), and High MVPA (12.0%)"
