## Evidence

### Besser Ilan 2026

Besser Ilan, N., Shavit, H., Zohar, N., & Jaffe-Dax, S. (2026). Enhanced word learning and neural synchrony during children's storytelling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00417-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A supplementary remote experiment with 24 first-grade-age children repeated the within-subject design over video; the strategy effect on question type was non-significant (p = 0.59), with similar object-name scores across conditions. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The learning strategy had no significant effect on question type scores [F(1,23) = 0.3, p = 0.59, repeated measures ANOVA], with the Scaffolding condition yielding similar performances for object names as the Passive condition [2.1 ± 1.3 vs. 2.0 ± 1.4, t(23) = 0.13, p = 0.89]"
