Vaala, S., Ly, A., & Levine, M.H. (2015). Getting a read on the app stores: A market scan and analysis of children's literacy apps. New York, NY: The Joan Ganz Cooney Center at Sesame Workshop. https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/publication/getting-a-read-on-the-app-stores-a-market-scan-and-analysis-of-childrens-literacy-apps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Coding of descriptions and producer websites in the 183-app scan for quality benchmarks. The executive summary also states 29% mentioned an underlying curriculum and 24% mentioned research testing, "overwhelmingly usability or appeal testing rather than learning efficacy". No effect size printed.

> "The percent of apps that mentioned a child development, education, or literacy expert involved in app development ranged from 36% of Top 50 Paid apps to 20% and 18% of Top 50 Free and Expert-awarded apps, respectively."
