## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM analysis of confidence ratings (1–7 scale) given with first impression ratings in the same experiment. The descending-performance comparison was also non-significant, F(1,79) = 0.63, p = .43. This contradicts the suggested anchoring effect.

> "No significant differences in assessors’ confidence in their first impression ratings for ethnic minority and majority students were found, neither within ascending performances Mmaj = 5.10, SDmaj = .12; Mmin = 5.28, SDmin = .12), F(1,79) = 2.61, p = .11, nor descending performances"
