## Evidence

### Taylor Margaret C. 2026

Taylor Margaret C., Diaz Hannah L. (2026). Guiding Midwives to Precept Students: Two Evidence‐Based Models. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70075

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Jain et al (2019), an RCT comparing SNAPPS with traditional case presentation among 18 residents in a medical residency program; the "SNAPPS group showed improved student diagnostic hypothesis" and management discussion. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The SNAPPS group showed improved student diagnostic hypothesis and improved discussion of patient management while maintaining thoroughness."

### Taylor Margaret C. 2026 (2)

Taylor Margaret C., Diaz Hannah L. (2026). Guiding Midwives to Precept Students: Two Evidence‐Based Models. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70075

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Shindala et al (2024), an RCT with 30 Family Medicine residents and 6 faculty in which the intervention group of 15 residents and all faculty received SNAPPS training; the SNAPPS group "performed better in terms of time efficiency" and related measures. Both preceptors and residents gave superior ratings. No effect sizes printed.

> "The participants in the SNAPPS group performed better in terms of time efficiency, number of case details covered, and reasoned diagnoses, as well as in asking questions and seeking information."
