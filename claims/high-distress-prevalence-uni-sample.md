Tindle, R., Castillo, P., Doring, N., Grant, L., & Willis, R. (2022). Developing and validating a university needs instrument to measure the psychosocial needs of university students. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1550–1570. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12515

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of K10 scores in the 511-participant cross-sectional survey. The sample mean was M = 26.39 (SD = 8.96), and "35.6% (n = 182) reported very high distress" with a further 31.1% high, 21.5% moderate and 11.5% low, using ABS categorisation bands.

> "There was a high level of psychological distress within the sample (M = 26.39; SD = 8.96; 95% CI [25.58, 27.19]). Of the 511 participants, 35.6% (n = 182) reported very high distress, 31.1% (n = 159) reported high distress, 21.5% (n = 110) reported moderate distress, and 11.5% (n = 59) reported low distress."
