## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Holding covariates constant, the odds of BIOME students taking CHEM 1B on time were 1.92 times those of non-enrolled students in the unmatched sample (p < 0.001) and 2.07 times in the matched sample (p < 0.001). [→ Wilton Mike 2021](#wilton-mike-2021)

## Evidence

### Wilton Mike 2021

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel logistic regression of on-time Winter-quarter CHEM 1B enrollment (retention proxy) on BIOME membership across three cohorts (2017–2019); unmatched-sample odds ratio = 1.92, p < 0.001. Descriptively, 331 of 414 BIOME students (80%) and 1,670 of 2,403 other students (70%) took CHEM 1B on time; the article's "75% more likely" is the unadjusted odds ratio, not a ratio of rates.

> "In fact, holding all covariates constant, the odds of students enrolled in BIOME taking CHEM 1B on time are double those of students who are not enrolled in BIOME (odds ratio = 1.92, p < 0.001)."

### Wilton Mike 2021 (2)

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score–matched logistic regression of on-time CHEM 1B course taking (Table 6) reports an odds ratio of 2.07 for BIOME in text, while Table 6 prints 1.72 (p = 0.008); the article does not reconcile this discrepancy.

> "Here, holding all covariates constant, the odds of a student in BIOME taking CHEM 1B on time are 2.07 times those of students in the matched sample (p < 0.001; Table 6)."
