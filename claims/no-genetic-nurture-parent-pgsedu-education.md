## Evidence

### Austerberry 2026

Austerberry, C., Zayats, T., Ronald, A., Corfield, E., Smajlagic, D., Havdahl, A., Andreassen, O. A., Magnus, P., Njølstad, P. R., Bekkhus, M., & Fearon, P. (2026). Evocative effects of children's education‐associated genetics on maternal parenting: results from the Norwegian mother, father and child cohort study. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(2), 158–170. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Trio SEM (Figure 1A) in MoBa trios; authors conclude the incidental null direct parent-PGS effects "offered little evidence of genetic nurture". Assortative mating was evident (parent PGS association β = 0.15, p < .001). This diverges from genetic-nurture findings in older samples.

> "only the child PGSedu was significantly associated with child education (PGSchild: β = 0.13, 95% CI [0.11, 0.16], p < .001; PGSmother: β = 0.01, 95% CI [−0.01, 0.03], p = .373; PGSfather: β = −0.02, 95% CI [−0.04, 0.01], p = .145)"
