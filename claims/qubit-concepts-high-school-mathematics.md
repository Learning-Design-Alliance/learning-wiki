## Evidence

### Wolfgang Dür 2013

Wolfgang Dür, Stefan Heusler. (2013). What we can learn about quantum physics from a single qubit. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.1463](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.1463)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical/pedagogical argument in the introduction: comparing qubit states, operations and measurements with the classical bit makes central quantum concepts accessible, using "simple mathematics involving only two-dimensional vectors, matrix multiplication and scalar products". No empirical test is reported; this is the authors' design argument.

> "In the case of a single qubit, this can be done with help of simple mathematics involving only two-dimensional vectors, matrix multiplication and scalar products - usually available at high school."
