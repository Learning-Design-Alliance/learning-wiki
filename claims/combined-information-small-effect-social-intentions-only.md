# Combined (descriptive plus explanatory) information yields only a small effect on social intentions and no effect on general attitudes towards people with Asperger syndrome

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Combined information produced higher SAQ social scores than descriptive information, but no significant main effect of information type was found on any ASQ attitude component. [→ Eleni Fleva 2014](#eleni-fleva-2014)

## Evidence

### Eleni Fleva 2014

Eleni Fleva. (2014). Imagined Contact Improves Intentions towards a Hypothetical Peer with Asperger Syndrome but not Attitudes towards Peers with Asperger Syndrome in General. World Journal of Education, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v5n1p1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the 416-participant ANOVA, combined information outperformed descriptive information on SAQ social scores (η2= .01). The article reports "No significant main effect of information type was found throughout the analysis of the ASQ."

> "A significant main effect of information type was also found with combined information resulting in higher scores than descriptive information [M= 19.4 vs. M= 18.5, F (1, 400) = 7.84, p= .005, η2= .01]"
