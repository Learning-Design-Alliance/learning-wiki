## Evidence

### Aljamani Sanaa 2025

Aljamani Sanaa, Al-Sawaeir Sereen, Nassar Rahaf, Rawajbeh Khetam, Saeed Amal, Ismail Noor H., Sartawi Samiha Yousef, Qdais Laith Abu. (2025). Assessment of procedural errors and student satisfaction in preclinical root canal treatment: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07596-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis across four radiographic obturation parameters in 307 students. Table 6 reports p < 0.001 for length, voids/density, and taper, and "p = 0.269" for coronal cut.

> "The Chi square test has shown that the radiographic quality of the length, density and taper of obturation have significantly affected the satisfaction of the students (p < 0.05). However, the quality of the coronal cut level of the root filling did not significantly affect student satisfaction (p > 0.05)."
