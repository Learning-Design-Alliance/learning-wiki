# Problem posing interventions show the largest positive effect on knowledge-based mathematics achievement (mean Hedges' g = 1.31)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across four weighted effect sizes, problem posing interventions had a large positive effect on knowledge-based mathematics achievement, with a mean Hedges' g of 1.31 (95% CI 1.04-1.59). [→ Roslinda Rosli 2014](#roslinda-rosli-2014)

## Evidence

### Roslinda Rosli 2014

Roslinda Rosli, Mary Margaret Capraro & Robert M. Capraro. (2014). The Effects of Problem Posing on Student Mathematical Learning: A Meta-Analysis. International Education Studies, 7(13). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/ies.v7n13p227

`q3 · i3`

Meta-analysis of four knowledge-based weighted effect sizes from experimental studies of problem posing interventions in mathematics. The review reports "mean Hedge's g effect size of 1.31" with a 95% CI of 1.04 to 1.59, the largest of the four outcome categories.

> "The comparisons among the learning outcomes showed that problem posing had the largest positive impact on knowledge-based, mean Hedge's g effect size of 1.31 with a 95% confidence interval of 1.04 to 1.59."
