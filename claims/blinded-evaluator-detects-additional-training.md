## Evidence

### Polivka L 2024

Polivka L, Delcour C, Dufresne H, Bartoli S, Bataille P, Bekel L, Bonigen J, Deladrière E, Dimarcq S, Felix A, Havas C, Le Goff H, Levy M, Riback E, Welfringer-Morin A, Houdouin V, Hadj-Rabia S, Bodemer C, Faye A, Melki I. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blind assessment by the evaluator on the same OSCE day: "88.2% (15 of the 17)" of the workshop group versus 21.4% of controls were judged to have received additional training, p = 0.0003. The authors note this bias-free assessment suggests the training was detectable in performance.

> "The proportion of students judged by the evaluator to have received additional training in breaking bad news was 88.2% (15 of the 17) in the interventional group and 21.4% (3 of the 14) in the control group (p = 0.0003)"
