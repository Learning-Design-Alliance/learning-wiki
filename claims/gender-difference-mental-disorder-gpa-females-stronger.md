## Evidence

### Yan Li 2026

Yan Li, Niemi Ripsa, Gutvilig Mai, Elovainio Marko, Flykt Marjo, Hakulinen Christian. (2026). Mental disorders in childhood or adolescence and subsequent comprehensive school achievement. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(11), 1796-1806. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70181

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Gender-stratified linear regression in the same nationwide cohort (411,054 females, 426,492 males), adjusted for the same confounders. The printed standardized differences were −0.45 for females and −0.37 for males; the association "was more pronounced in females than in males".

> "the association between having at least one mental disorder diagnosis and school achievement was more pronounced in females than in males (female: −0.45 [95% CI, −0.46 to −0.45] vs. male: −0.37 [95% CI, −0.38 to −0.37])."
