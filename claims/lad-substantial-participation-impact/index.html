Review of nine participation studies; the quoted quasi-experimental study exposed an experimental group to a dashboard showing LMS participation patterns versus engaged peers and class average. Reported "access to the LMS (d=0.821)" and "access to discussion boards (d=0.916)".

"The results revealed large significant effect sizes across multiple measures of student engagement, such as access to the LMS (d=0.821), number of discussion threads (d=0.379), and access to discussion boards (d=0.916)"
