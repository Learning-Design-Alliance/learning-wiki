## Evidence

### John Hattie 2025

John Hattie, Timothy O'Leary. (2025). Learning Styles, Preferences, or Strategies? An Explanation for the Resurgence of Styles Across Many Meta-analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10002-w

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Takase and Yoshida (2021), a meta-analysis of nursing students' learning approaches: positive correlation r = 0.26 for deep approaches (nine studies, N = 1830) and a statistically significant negative correlation r = -0.25 for surface approaches (11 studies, N = 2138).

> "Summarizing findings from nine studies with 10 cohorts (N = 1830), they found a statistically significant positive correlation (r = 0.26) between academic achievement and deep approaches to learning and academic achievement."
