## Evidence

### Duncombe Justin M.T. 2026

Duncombe Justin M.T., Camaly de Brosses Pierre, Kassam Al-Amin M., Harris David J., Buckingham Gavin. (2026). Investigating Hip Arthroplasty Femur Preparation Training Using a Haptic-Enabled Virtual Reality Simulation. Surgical Innovation, 33(2), 169-180. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251383830

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled study of 20 undergraduate medical students; the SawBone hemiarthroplasty assessment measured implant depth error via 3D scanning. The test showed "no significant differences in IDerror between the VR (median = 16 mm, IQR = 35) and control groups" with r = 0.085.

> "A subsequent Mann-Whitney U test indicated no significant differences in IDerror between the VR (median = 16 mm, IQR = 35) and control groups (median = 25 mm, IQR = 41), z = 0.378, P = .705, r = 0.085"
