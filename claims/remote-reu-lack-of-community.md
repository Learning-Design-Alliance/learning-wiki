## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alaee 2023

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview data from the remote REU study indicate participants "persistently lacked community throughout the REU program"; professional development sessions brought students into the same Zoom room but did not form peer community, and group chats went silent after week one.

> "However, our data indicate that REU participants persis-tently lacked community throughout the REU program."

### Alaee 2023 (2)

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview data across the program; because in-person interaction was absent, mentees "had to engage in substantial independent learning," reaching mentors by email or Zoom; mentees such as Caleb and David described becoming more independent in working things out alone.

> "Due to the lack of in-person interaction between mentees and other members of the lab community, they are had to engage in substantial independent learning."
