### Bajko et al. 2016 (2)

Robert Bajko, Jaigris Hodson, Katie Seaborn, Pamela Livingstone, Deborah Fels. (2016). Edugamifying Media Studies: Student Engagement, Enjoyment, and Interest in Two Multimedia and Social Media Undergraduate Classrooms. Information Systems Education Journal 14(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135365

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative student commentary from the open-ended survey items, which the authors compare to Cheong, Filippou & Cheong (2013). Students said elements "allowed them to apply or experience concepts in a practical way"; this is self-reported perception, not an objective performance measure.

> "our students stated that the gamification elements allowed them to apply or experience concepts in a practical way, rather than just reading about theory."
