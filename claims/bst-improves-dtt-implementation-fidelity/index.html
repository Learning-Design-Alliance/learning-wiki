Evidence¶

Joelle Fingerhut 2022¶

Joelle Fingerhut, & Mariola Moeyaert. (2022). Training Individuals to Implement Discrete Trials with Fidelity: A Meta-Analysis. Focus on Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities, 37(4), 239–250. https://doi.org/10.1177/10883576221081076

q2 · i?

HLM meta-analysis of 46 SCED studies estimating the mean baseline DTT implementation fidelity across participants and studies. The article reports "the baseline level ... was estimated to be 40.61%", which it characterizes as low accuracy.

"The baseline level was consistent across all models and was estimated to be 40.61%, γ̂000 = 40.61, t(3400) = 16.76, p < .0125."
