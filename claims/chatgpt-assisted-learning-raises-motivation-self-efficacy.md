## Evidence

### Qiao J 2025

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of questionnaire posttest scores (5 items each on 5-point Likert scales; Cronbach α 0.76 and 0.73) between the randomized groups. Pretest scores showed no between-group differences in "learning motivation (Z=0.31, P=.76) and self-efficacy (Z=0.69, P=.48)".

> "Furthermore, in comparison to the control group, the ChatGPT group exhibited higher posttest scores for both learning motivation (Z=2.32, P=.02) and self-efficacy (Z=2.03, P=.04)."
