Univariate group comparisons (Student's t-test) on the knowledge dimension. Lecture attenders scored 18.08 ± 2.45 versus 17.31 ± 2.51 for non-attenders; the comparison of "participation in enteral nutrition lectures" was statistically significant for knowledge.
