Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2022¶

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland, Karyn Lewis, Erik Ruzek, Angela Johnson. (2022). The COVID-19 school year: Learning and recovery across 2020-2021. AERA Open, 8. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584221099306

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The study's modeling of achievement trends for 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3-8 found that "growth in math was more variable than in prior years," and that "much of the gains occurred among initially high-performing students pulling further ahead." No effect size is printed for this differential pattern.

"Furthermore, growth in math was more variable than in prior years, and much of the gains occurred among initially high-performing students pulling further ahead."
