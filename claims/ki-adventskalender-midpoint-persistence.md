Longitudinal progression analysis of the two annual deployments (Results §3.1, Figure 2). The article reports that after "the midway point (Day 12)" participants "showed high resilience" and that "over 76% of users active of Day 12 stayed until the final challenge" in 2025. No effect size is printed.

> "in both years, once participants reached the midway point (Day 12), they showed high resilience, with progression stabilizing significantly. For those who reached the second half of the calendar, the completion probability was exceptionally high, particularly in 2025, where over 76% of users active of Day 12 stayed until the final challenge."
