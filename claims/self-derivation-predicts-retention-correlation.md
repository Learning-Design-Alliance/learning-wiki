# Self-derivation performance is positively correlated with stem-fact memory and predicts retention of self-derived knowledge one week later

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Open-ended self-derivation was strongly correlated with open-ended stem-fact recall within Session 1 in Experiment 1 (r = .66) and Experiment 2 (r = .79). [→ Bauer 2020](#bauer-2020)
`q3 i?` Session 1 self-derivation and stem-fact memory predicted Session 2 retention, with correlations among the measures ranging from .44 to .57. [→ Bauer 2020 (2)](#bauer-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Bauer 2020

Bauer, P. J., Esposito, A. G., & Daly, J. J. (2020). Self-derivation through memory integration: A model for accumulation of semantic knowledge. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101271

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in Experiment 1 (96 children). "open-ended self-derivation was related to recall of the stem facts (r = .66)" and forced-choice measures correlated r = .58. In Experiment 2 the open-ended correlation was r(93) = .79, p < .001.

> "within Session 1, open-ended self-derivation was related to recall of the stem facts (r = .66). The same relation was observed when both self-derivation and stem fact memory were tested using forced-choice (r = .58)."

### Bauer 2020 (2)

Bauer, P. J., Esposito, A. G., & Daly, J. J. (2020). Self-derivation through memory integration: A model for accumulation of semantic knowledge. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Predictive correlations in Experiment 1 between Session 1 performance and Session 2 retention. "performance at Session 1—both self-derivation and stem-fact memory—was predictive of recall and recognition of self-derived knowledge at Session 2" with correlations ranging from .44 to .57.

> "performance at Session 1—both self-derivation and stem-fact memory—was predictive of recall and recognition of self-derived knowledge at Session 2. The correlations among the measures ranged from .44 to .57."
