## Evidence

### Renninger 2026

Renninger, K. A., Barton, L. K., Bautista, G., Garcia-Barrios, M., Ogunyinka, I., & Riley, K. R. (2026). Interest, Self-efficacy, and Belonging in Classroom Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10102-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-method pilot study of 12 Black middle-school-age youth (9 girls, 3 boys) in a five-week, inquiry-informed science workshop facilitated by a Black professor, using interviews, a knowledge test, and daily observations. The article reports that "interest, self-efficacy, and belonging were increasingly coordinated over time." No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

> "Findings showed that during learning, interest, self-efficacy, and belonging were increasingly coordinated over time."
