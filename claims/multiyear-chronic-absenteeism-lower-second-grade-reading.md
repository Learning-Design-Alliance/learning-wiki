# Students chronically absent for multiple years between preschool and second grade have significantly lower second-grade reading outcomes, approaching the threshold for needing intensive reading intervention

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who are chronically absent between preschool and second grade have significantly lower learning outcomes at the end of second grade, and those chronically absent every year since preschool are close to the threshold for needing intensive reading intervention to read at grade level by third grade. Longitudinal analysis of CPS students linking early attendance patterns to second-grade reading outcomes. The report states "students who are chronically absent between preschool and second grade have significantly lower learning outcomes at the end of second grade."
