`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Task-level analysis of the 45 video-recorded simulated consultations. Table 3 shows the largest gains for offering written information (p = 0.004), offering a nurse appointment (p = 0.000), and explaining OA does not inevitably worsen (p = 0.01).

> "The score for six other tasks increased after the workshops at one-month (McNemar test: one-month v baseline, p < 0.05), with the increase for three of these tasks sustained at five-months (McNemar test: five-month v baseline, p < 0.05)"
