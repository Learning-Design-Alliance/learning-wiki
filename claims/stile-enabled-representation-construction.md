### Cirkony 2022

Cirkony, C., Tytler, R., & Hubber, P. (2022). Designing and delivering representation-focused science lessons in a digital learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10094-z

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Video-based ethnographic case study of three Grade 9 students in an Australian all-girls school. In Activity 5.2 all three chose the interactive canvas, and "The case group generated a variety of drawings using the paint tools of the interactive canvas"; responses were unique though with noted similarities such as colour and annotations.

> "The case group generated a variety of drawings using the paint tools of the interactive canvas (e.g., pen, shapes, lines, text, image, colour) accessible through their laptop cursor and trackpad."
