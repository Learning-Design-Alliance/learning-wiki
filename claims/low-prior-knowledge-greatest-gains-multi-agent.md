## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z.; Cao, J.; Li, R.; Yu, J.; Liu, Z.; Zhang, Y. (2025). Mapping Student-AI Interaction Dynamics in Multi-Agent Learning Environments: Supporting Personalised Learning and Reducing Performance Gaps. https://osf.io/preprints/eduRxiv

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing learning gains across three prior-knowledge groups (top/bottom 30% plus medium) in the MAIC course. The article reports "partial η² = .400" and group means of 1.88, 0.36, and –1.09 for low, medium, and high groups.

> "The results revealed a significant main effect of prior knowledge level on learning gain, F(2, 69) = 22.95, p < .001, partial η² = .400. Students in the low prior knowledge group achieved the greatest learning gains (M = 1.88, SD = 1.36), followed by the medium group (M = 0.36, SD = 1.34), while the high group experienced a learning loss (M = –1.09, SD = 1.37)"
