## Evidence

### Förster C 2020

Förster C, Eismann-Schweimler J, Stengel S, Bischoff M, Fuchs M, Graf von Luckner A, Ledig T, Barzel A, Maun A, Joos S, Szecsenyi J, Schwill S. (2020). Opportunities and challenges of e-learning in vocational training in General Practice – a project report about implementing digital formats in the KWBW-Verbundweiterbildungplus. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001390](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001390)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report attitude item in the online trainee evaluation; the article reports "the attitude towards e-learning had changed positively" for 52% (n=53), with reasons including exceeded expectations (n=13) and being a good alternative in the current situation (n=5).

> "For 52% (n=53) of the trainees, the attitude towards e-learning had changed positively. The main advantages mentioned were no travel, save in time and costs as well as increased flexibility."
