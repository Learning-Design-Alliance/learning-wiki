## Evidence

### Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen 2013

Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen. (2013). Leveraging a Relationship with Biology to Expand a Relationship with Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video-recorded lab episode (Clip 4) in the first month of the course, analyzed as a cascade of affect, identity, and epistemology (Figure 4); Violet and her partner had been doing productive sense-making before the TA's definitional question. The authors' interpretation of the sequencing, not a tested result.

> "The sequencing of these events suggests that Violet's moves to distance herself from the physics (calling herself physics stupid and disengaging from the conversation) is in part a response from the TA's call for a particular kind of knowledge (definitional) as relevant and important to making sense of the situation."
