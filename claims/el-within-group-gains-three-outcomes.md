# Within-group gains over time occurred in both groups for academic performance, but only the embodied learning group showed significant within-group gains in attitudes and self-efficacy.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both groups showed statistically significant within-group improvements in academic performance over time (treatment p<0.050; comparison p=0.037). [→ Tutal Özgür 2026](#tutal-ozgur-2026)
`q3 i?` Only the treatment group showed a statistically significant within-group improvement in attitudes toward educational measurement (p=0.006); the comparison group's gain was non-significant (p=0.369). [→ Tutal Özgür 2026](#tutal-ozgur-2026)
`q3 i?` Only the treatment group showed a statistically significant within-group increase in self-efficacy (p<0.001); the comparison group's improvement was marginal and non-significant (p=0.355). [→ Tutal Özgür 2026](#tutal-ozgur-2026)

## Evidence

### Tutal Özgür 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests comparing pre- and post-measures in both groups across the three outcomes. The treatment group's attitude mean rose from 127.13 (SD=17.20) to 144.87 (SD=7.88); the comparison group's from 122.25 (SD=17.90) to 127.50 (SD=9.40). Only p-values were printed for these tests.

> "Although both groups showed gains over time, only the treatment group exhibited a statistically significant improvement (p = 0.006)."
