## Evidence

### Teach For America 2016

Teach For America. (2016). WWC Intervention Report: Teach For America. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.teachforamerica.org

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

This RCT randomly assigned students in 45 secondary schools to a math class taught by a TFA teacher or a similar class taught by a non-TFA teacher; the analytic sample included 4,573 students. The report states: "The authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, a positive and statistically significant difference between the TFA group and the comparison group", with an average improvement index of +3.
