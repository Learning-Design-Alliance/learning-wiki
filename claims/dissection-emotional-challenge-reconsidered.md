### Papa Veronica 2013

Papa Veronica, Vaccarezza Mauro. (2013). Teaching Anatomy in the XXI Century: New Aspects and Pitfalls. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/310348

Narrative review attributing this to Lempp's qualitative study of students' reactions to their own dissection experiences: most students enjoyed the experience and valued close supervision and small-group learning. The review concludes the view that dissection is emotionally challenging needs reconsideration, citing corroborating studies.

> "the majority of the students appeared to have enjoyed and really appreciated the close personal supervision of the anatomy demonstrators and the more experienced permanent anatomy staff as well as the opportunities to learn together in small groups"
