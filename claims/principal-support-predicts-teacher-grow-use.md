# Principal support for Grow predicts teacher Grow use above and beyond teacher demographics and technology factors

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Principal use of Grow significantly predicts teacher use of and belief in Grow in the school-level HLM (coefficient 0.19, p < 0.000), holding even among teachers with less technology professional development and limited technology use. [→ John Q. Easton 2004](#john-q-easton-2004)

## Evidence

### John Q. Easton 2004

John Q. Easton, Stuart Luppescu. (2004). Teacher and Principal Responses to the Grow Network: A Preliminary Evaluation of Use in Chicago Public Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-and-principal-responses-grow-network-preliminary-evaluation-use-chicago-public

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-level HLM effects (282 principals; school-level df 216) show Principal use of Grow at coefficient 0.19 (SE 0.05, p < 0.000). The brief states this holds "above and beyond" teacher demographics and technology factors, suggesting principal support may reflect a leadership style encouraging innovation. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The relationship between Principal leadership for Teacher Use of and Belief in Grow is statistically significant above and beyond the other factors discussed thus far."
