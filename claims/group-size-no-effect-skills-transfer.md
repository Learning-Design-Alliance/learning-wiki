# Group size (one to four trainees) did not significantly affect transfer test performance on OSAUS or GRS scores after simulation-based ultrasound training

## Evidence

### Noerholk LM 2022

Noerholk LM, Morcke AM, Kulasegaram K, Nørgaard LN, Harmsen L, Andreasen LA, Pedersen NG, Johnsson V, Vamadevan A, Tolsgaard MG. (2022). Does group size matter during collaborative skills learning? A randomised study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14791

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome of a randomised controlled trial with pre-post-transfer-test design; 97 participants completed the transfer test on a different simulator. One-way anova found no significant group-size effect, with ηp2 = 0.06 for OSAUS and ηp2 = 0.05 for GRS; equivalence was not tested.

> "Group size did not significantly influence performance measured by OSAUS scores (F(3,93) = 1.94, p = 0.13, ηp2 = 0.06) or GRS scores (F(3,93) = 1.46, p = 0.23, ηp2 = 0.05) on the transfer test"
