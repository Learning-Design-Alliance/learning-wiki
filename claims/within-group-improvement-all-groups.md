## Evidence

### Hillemans V 2025

Hillemans V, Verhoeven DJ, van de Mortel X, Bökkerink GMJ, Botden SMBI, Joosten M, de Blaauw I. (2025). Optimizing skill acquisition: the role of self-assessment during a continuous training program for laparoscopic suturing. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11682-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group Friedman ANOVA comparisons across the three test moments. The control group's time fell from "pre-test 614.0 s, baseline-test 167.0 s, after-test 178.0 s, p = 0.001"; the periodic and continuous SA groups showed similarly significant time and smoothness changes (p = 0.001–0.005). Across all tests, handedness (p 0.285–0.433) and distance between tips (p 0.053–0.797) showed no significant between-group differences.

> "The control group scored a significant shorter time at the baseline-test and after-test, compared to the pre-test (pre-test 614.0 s, baseline-test 167.0 s, after-test 178.0 s, p = 0.001)"
