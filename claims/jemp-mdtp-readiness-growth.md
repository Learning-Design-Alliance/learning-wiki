## Evidence

### Bowens 2016

Bowens, B. D., & Warren, S. R. (2016). Toward Social Justice: The Characteristics of an Effective Mathematics Intervention Program for Urban Middle School Students. Journal of Urban Learning Teaching and Research, 12, 35-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1119158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistical analysis of MDTP pre/post diagnostic readiness tests for JEMP participants in summers of 2011 and 2012 (N = 136 pre-algebra; N = 108 algebra). Students entered "below the readiness level deemed sufficient by the MDTP guidelines (70%)" and on average reached or passed it by the end. Only descriptive statistics are reported for this growth: means, SDs and percentages, with no significance test and no comparison group, so the pre/post rise cannot by itself show that the program caused it (the article's separate ANCOVA comparisons on state test scores are other claims).

> "Table 3 illustrates the mean growth for 7th grade students participating in the pre-algebra intervention was from 62.15% to 75.48% readiness and for 8th grade algebra intervention students was from 61.10% to 70.08%."
