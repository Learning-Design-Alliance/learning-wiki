Rolla 2024¶

Rolla, A., & Mottesi, G. (2024). Bilingual Beginnings: Planning Kindergarten Entry Assessments for Spanish–English Speakers. WestEd, Region 15 Comprehensive Center. https://csaa.wested.org/resource/kindergarten-entry-assessments-an-examination-of-best-practices-national-trends-and-recommendations/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief's own national policy scan, last updated August 2024, used publicly available information from state department and board of education websites, published materials, legislative regulations, and administrative codes. It reports that "Forty-one states lack the explicit inclusion of Spanish–English bilinguals in their KEAs" and that only 10 states explicitly include them (Table 1).

"Forty-one states lack the explicit inclusion of Spanish–English bilinguals in their KEAs, provide accommodations for English Learners that do not fully include Spanish–English bilinguals, or make no mention of either group in their assessments."

Interpretive conclusion drawn from the scan's Table 1 counts, which categorized states as explicitly including, implicitly including, or not including Spanish–English bilinguals. The authors state that "Spanish–English bilingual students in the majority of states may not have access to assessments that adequately reflect their language development."

"These numbers suggest that Spanish–English bilingual students in the majority of states may not have access to assessments that adequately reflect their language development."
