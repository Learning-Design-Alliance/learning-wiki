## Evidence

### Guill Karin 2019

Guill Karin, Lüdtke Oliver, Köller Olaf. (2019). Assessing the instructional quality of private tutoring and its effects on student outcomes: Analyses from the German National Educational Panel Study. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12281

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Regression models on NEPS longitudinal data (N = 11,358) predicting end-of-year Grade 10 grades, controlling for midterm grades, cognitive ability, helplessness, interest, and family background. The mathematics coefficient for private tutoring was β = −0.065 and for German β = −0.037, both significant, showing "tutored students did not outperform non‐tutored in any of the subjects".

> "As the regression models for students' grades at the end of Grade 10 reveal (see Table 2), contrary to Hypothesis 1a, tutored students did not outperform non‐tutored in any of the subjects. Tutored students even had significantly lower grades than non‐tutored students."
