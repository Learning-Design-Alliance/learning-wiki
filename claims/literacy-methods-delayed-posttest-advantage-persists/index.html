Ramirez 2013¶

Ramirez, H., & Jones, D. (2013). Effects of reading strategies and the writing process with written recasts on second language achievement. Administrative Issues Journal: Education, Practice, and Research, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5929/2013.3.1.7

Independent-samples t test on delayed posttest scores administered two weeks after the posttest in the same 116-student middle-school Spanish study; the printed difference of 8.59 points was "statistically significant (t(114) = 2.93, p = .03)". The abstract reports d = .51 for this contrast.

"Likewise, the difference of 8.59 points in favor of the experimental group on the delayed posttest was also statistically significant (t(114) = 2.93, p = .03)."
