## Evidence

### Kaldaras 2025

Kaldaras, L., & Wieman, C. (2025). Directed Self-Guided Learning of Blended Math-Science Sensemaking for Historically Marginalized STEM Learners. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1186/s40594-025-00565-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00565-z)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Study of 27 students; transfer was evaluated with an immediate transfer task in a different phenomenon and a delayed assessment. The article reports that 25 of the 27 students "completed the post MSS assessment between one and four weeks after doing the activity and the transfer task". No effect size is printed.

> "We evaluated long and short-term MSS transfer by asking student to complete transfer tasks immediately following and at least 1 week post activity."
