Evidence¶

Doğan Yunus 2026¶

Doğan Yunus, Kılıç Zeynep, Kalınkara Yusuf, Talan Tarık. (2026). The Impact of Artificial Intelligence-Supported Instruction on Student Learning in STEM: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence14060109

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Duration moderator analysis across the 35 studies (between-group Q = 16.95, df = 5, p = 0.004). Interventions of more than 1 up to 2 months yielded the largest pooled effect, g = 0.833, 95% CI [0.67, 0.99].

"Regarding intervention duration, the highest effect size was observed in interventions lasting more than 1 month and up to 2 months (g = 0.833, 95% CI [0.67, 0.99], Z = 10.30, p < 0.001)."

Doğan Yunus 2026 (2)¶

Doğan Yunus, Kılıç Zeynep, Kalınkara Yusuf, Talan Tarık. (2026). The Impact of Artificial Intelligence-Supported Instruction on Student Learning in STEM: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence14060109

q4 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

In the same duration moderator analysis, the 1–7 day group showed a small, non-significant pooled effect (g = 0.256, p = 0.070); equivalence was not tested. Short-term interventions of ≤5 h were significant (g = 0.621, p = 0.003).

"However, for interventions lasting more than 1 and up to 7 days, the effect was weaker and not statistically significant (g = 0.256, p = 0.070)."
