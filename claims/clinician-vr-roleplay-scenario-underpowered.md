## Evidence

### Sapkaroski Daniel 2021

Sapkaroski Daniel, Mundy Matthew, Dimmock Matthew Richard. (2021). Immersive virtual reality simulated learning environment versus role‐play for empathic clinical communication training. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.555

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intergroup comparison of clinician communication scenario scores (CVR n = 5, CRP n = 4; Table 3 reports P = 0.17, d = 1.03). The study states the result "was not significant" and attributes this to "the small group size"; intergroup significance was not tested due to the small clinician sample.

> "The CVR group’s performance relative to that of the CRP group showed a similar underlying trend; however the result was not significant. The lack of significance for the clinician group may have been attributed to the small group size."
