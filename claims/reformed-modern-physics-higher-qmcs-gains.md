### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Reforming a large lecture modern physics course for engineering majors using a PER-based design. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1063/1.2508685](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2508685)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post QMCS analysis of six modern physics courses (Table 1), comparing reformed engineering sections (Sp06 <g>=0.49, Fa05 <g>=0.54, N=156 and 162) with four traditional baseline courses with gains from 0.21 to 0.37. "all are consistently lower than in the reformed courses."

> "The values of <g> for the reformed courses are consistent with typical normalized gains on the Force Concept Inventory (FCI) in reformed introductory physics courses [2]. There are wide variations in <g> for the traditional courses, but all are consistently lower than in the reformed courses."
