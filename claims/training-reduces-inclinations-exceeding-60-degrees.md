## Evidence

### Kozak Agnessa 2017

Kozak Agnessa, Freitag Sonja, Nienhaus Albert. (2017). Evaluation of a Training Program to Reduce Stressful Trunk Postures in the Nursing Professions: A Pilot Study. Annals of Work Exposures and Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/annweh/wxw002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CUELA pre/post field measurement in the same 12-ward pilot: inclinations >60°, classified unacceptable under ISO 11226/DIN EN 1005-4, fell from a median of 2.5% (IQR 1.1–4.6) to 1.0% (0.8–1.7) of shift time; the article reports they were "reduced by 60% (P = 0.002)".

> "The proportion of very pronounced inclinations exceeding 60° was reduced by 60% (P = 0.002), from 288 to 135 inclinations per shift."
