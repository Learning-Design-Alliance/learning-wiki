`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Frequency analysis across the 60 included articles in the scoping review. The review reports scenario-based learning in "nine articles", resource access and narrated PowerPoint in eight each, and computer simulation or virtual reality and gamification in three articles each; five studies used multiple enhancing options.

> "The most commonly used enhancing technology category was scenario-based learning (nine articles) [31, 32, 42, 51, 53, 54, 59, 62, 76], followed by resource access (eight articles) [24, 26, 28, 33, 35, 53, 59, 60] and narrated PowerPoint (eight articles)"
