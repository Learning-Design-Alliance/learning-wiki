Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim 2025¶

Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim, Sidi Muhammad Yusoff Azli Shah, Jane Tze Yn Lim, Tuti Iryani Mohd Daud. (2025). Online-Based and Technology-Assisted Psychiatric Education for Trainees: Scoping Review. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/64773

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review describes the First European Psychiatric Association MOOC on CBT, a month-long self-paced course: "the self-paced MOOC had large participation, with 7116 participants enrolling from at least 49 countries. Although the eventual completion rate was 26%, a large number of participants (n=1828) completed the MOOC and the average score for the tests increased steadily from 21.4 out of 25 in the first week to 23.13 out of 25 in the final week."
