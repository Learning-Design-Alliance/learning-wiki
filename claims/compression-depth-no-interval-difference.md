## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The percentage of compressions with adequate depth increased significantly from session 1 to 4 in all intervals, but by session 4 there were no differences based on training timing (F = 2.35, df = 3,378, p >.05). [→ Oermann Marilyn H. 2020](#oermann-marilyn-h-2020)

## Evidence

### Oermann Marilyn H. 2020

Oermann Marilyn H., Krusmark Michael A., Kardong-Edgren Suzan, Jastrzembski Tiffany S., Gluck Kevin A. (2020). Training interval in cardiopulmonary resuscitation. PLoS ONE 15(1): e0226786. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226786

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis in the randomized trial: adequate-depth percentages rose from roughly 66-72% at session 1 to about 90-95% at session 4 in all intervals, with "no differences... based on the timing of their training". No effect size printed.

> "However, in contrast to overall compression score, by session 4, there were no differences in students' ability to compress with an adequate depth based on the timing of their training (F = 2.35, df = 3,378, p >.05)."
