Correlational analysis of the nationwide survey responses; the article reports the significant association between gonioscopy frequency and confidence with the printed p values and no effect size.

> "the low rate of performing gonioscopy correlated significantly with a low level of confidence in performing the examination (p < 0.001), but not with the setting (hospital compared to a community or private clinic, p = 0.534) or the number of years of experience among NGS respondents (p = 0.257)."
