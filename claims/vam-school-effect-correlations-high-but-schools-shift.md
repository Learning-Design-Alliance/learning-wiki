### George Leckie and Lucy Prior 2022

George Leckie and Lucy Prior. (2022). A Comparison of Value-Added Models for School Accountability. [doi:10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763](https://doi.org/10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Pearson correlations between school effects from the five models fitted to the 2019 English census (Table 3); adjacent-model correlations are .81, .90, .96, .91 and the Raw versus CVA-X correlation is lowest at .41.

> "All correlations are positive and high (range .41 to .96) as to be expected given all models purport to measure school effects on student learning, albeit in different ways."

### George Leckie and Lucy Prior 2022 (2)

George Leckie and Lucy Prior. (2022). A Comparison of Value-Added Models for School Accountability. [doi:10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763](https://doi.org/10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Figure 2 scatterplots of school effects and ranks for each pair of adjacent models. The article notes grammar schools (162 schools or 5% nationally) "appear far less impressive in terms of their VA school effects than they do in terms of their Raw school effects".

> "These plots show that many individual schools are dramatically affected by each set of adjustments despite the high correlations."
