Evidence¶

Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017¶

Romualdas K. Malinauskas, Dalius R. Juodsnukis. (2017). Education of Social Responsibility among Sports Schools Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.289

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Overall social responsibility comparison in the Results section of the educational experiment, reported alongside Figure 1. Groups were homogeneous before the program; after it the experimental group was significantly higher. No effect size is printed.

"social responsibility level of control and experimental groups did not differ significantly before experiment (t (50) = 0.26; p = .07), but after experiment significant differences were found between experimental and control groups: t (50) = 2.37; p = .02"

Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017 (2)¶

Romualdas K. Malinauskas, Dalius R. Juodsnukis. (2017). Education of Social Responsibility among Sports Schools Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.289

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent-samples t tests on overall social responsibility before and after the program for each group, reported in the Results section and Figure 1. The experimental group improved significantly; the control group's change was not significant. No effect size is printed.

"Overall results of social responsibility showed significant effects of educational program for experimental group (t (50) = -3.15; p = .003) and no significant effects for control group ( t (50) = -0.66; p = .51)"
