## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of the instructor-students shift matrix (Figure 8) built from frame sequences in the 50 episodes, tabulating how instructor framing leads to student framing. The instructor's conceptual physics row contains the most entries, and its largest entry is follow-the-leader behavior in which students continue in CP after she uses it.

> "Follow-the-leader is the dominant mode here: the largest entries are the diagonal ones, and they represent 52% of all tran- sitions in this matrix. If frames were uncorrelated with each other, follow-the-leader would occur about 20% of the time."
