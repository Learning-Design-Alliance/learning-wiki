### Kostantinos Mattas 2025

Kostantinos Mattas, Antonio Lucas-Alba, Tomer Toledo, Óscar M. Melchor, Shlomo Bekhor, Biagio Ciuffo. (2025). Learning to Car-Follow Using an Inertia-Oriented Driving Technique: A Before-and-After Study on a Closed Circuit. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest observations on the JRC closed circuit and in the WDC simulator showed participants following the oscillating lead vehicle with pronounced speed peaks and valleys, the DD pattern the authors describe as the taught, default strategy.

> "In the pretest phase of this study, the participants (3-6 years old) drove according to the DD principle. This result has been consistently observed in previous studies: the DD technique is the default technique."
