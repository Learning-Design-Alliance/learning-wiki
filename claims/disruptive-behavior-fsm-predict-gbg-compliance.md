## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Disruptive behavior significantly predicted moderate compliance at T3 (OR = 1.74) and high compliance at both time points (OR = 2.60; OR = 2.69). [→ Ashworth Emma 2020](#ashworth-emma-2020)

## Evidence

### Ashworth Emma 2020

Ashworth Emma, Panayiotou Margarita, Humphrey Neil, Hennessey Alexandra. (2020). Game On—Complier Average Causal Effect Estimation Reveals Sleeper Effects on Academic Attainment in a Randomized Trial of the Good Behavior Game. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-019-01074-6

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Logistic prediction of the latent compliance variable in the trial's CACE models showed "Disruptive behaviors were shown to predict moderate compliance at T3" with OR = 1.74 and high compliance with OR = 2.60 (T2) and OR = 2.69 (T3); FSM eligibility predicted high compliance (OR = 1.52; OR = 1.67).

> "Disruptive behaviors were shown to predict moderate compliance at T3 (b = 0.56, p < .01; odds ratio [OR] = 1.74) and high compliance at both time points (T2 b = 0.95, p < .01; OR = 2.60; T3 b = 0.99, p < .01; OR = 2.69)."
