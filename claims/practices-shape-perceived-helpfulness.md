## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students identifying working with peers as helpful averaged 22.3% more group work; students citing discussion section size or activities that helped learning were more likely to have experienced more lecture and individual work. [→ Mueller 2026](#mueller-2026)

## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simplex comparison of coded responses against perceived activity allocation (Tables 7 and 8). Students experiencing more lecture were also more likely to report problems with modality; group work was not always the key to perceived success.

> "we found that students who identified working with peers as a helpful aspect of discussion sections tended to be students with much higher amounts of group work in their discussion sections (22.3% more group work on average)."
