# The number of items groups placed in their representations was negatively correlated with the average quality of those items.

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Including more items in a representation was associated with significantly lower average item quality (r = -.66). [→ Jeroen Janssen 2008](#jeroen-janssen-2008)

## Evidence

### Jeroen Janssen 2008

Jeroen Janssen, Gijsbert Erkens, Paul A. Kirschner, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Effects of representational guidance during computer-supported collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9078-1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the group-level representation data from the same 39-group study; the article reports "a signiﬁcantly negative correlation, r =- .66, p = .00" between number of items and average item quality.

> "Additionally, we correlated the number of items produced with the average quality of these items and found a signiﬁcantly negative correlation, r =- .66, p = .00, meaning that when groups attempted to included a large number of items in their representations, this had a negative effect on the quality of their representations."
