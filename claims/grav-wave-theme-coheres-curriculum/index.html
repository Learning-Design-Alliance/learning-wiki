Evidence¶

Farr 2012¶

Farr, Schelbert, & Trouille. (2012). Gravitational-wave science in the high school classroom. https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3720 doi:10.48550/arxiv.1109.3720

q1 · i? · design · r2

The authors' interpretive report (type e) from their year-long implementation in 8 classes of about 25 students each, with no prerequisites and mixed math proficiency. They also report students were fascinated by gravitational waves and colliding black holes, resulting in significant self-motivation; no measures or tests are reported.

"By explicitly connecting existing topics in the curriculum to gravitational-wave science whenever possible, these lessons ga ve coherency to units that before may have seemed disconnected."
