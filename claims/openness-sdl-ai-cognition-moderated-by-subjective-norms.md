`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Conditional process models (Hayes Model 7, bootstrap CIs) in the same cross-sectional survey of 1,208 students. The openness pathway was "the only one exhibiting statistically significant moderated mediation"; the index of moderated mediation was −0.009, 95% CI [−0.015, −0.003], with indirect effects significant at low (0.048), mean (0.039), and high (0.031) moderator levels.

> "Conditional process analyses identified the openness → AI cognition → SDL pathway as the only one exhibiting statistically significant moderated mediation, with subjective norms attenuating the indirect association."
