Evidence¶

Iman 2017¶

Iman, J. N. (2017). Debate Instruction in EFL Classroom: Impacts on the Critical Thinking and Speaking Skill. International Journal of Instruction, 10(4), 87-108. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2017.1046a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent sample t-test comparing posttests of the debate group (mean 16.16) and control group (mean 13.89), n = 24 each in the quasi-experimental design. The article reports a mean difference of "2.270" and t-obtained 4.316 (p<0.000); no effect size is printed.

"the mean difference of critical thinking post-test between the experimental and control group was 2.270 and t -obtained 4.316 (p<0.000)"
