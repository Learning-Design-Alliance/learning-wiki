## Evidence

### Zhou Zhenfei 2024

Zhou Zhenfei, Tavan Hamed, Kavarizadeh Forouzan, Sarokhani Mandana, Sayehmiri Kourosh. (2024). The relationship between emotional intelligence, spiritual intelligence, and student achievement: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05208-5

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of correlation coefficients from 27 included studies; the pooled spiritual intelligence–achievement correlation was "r = 0.36 (95% CI: 0.18–0.51)". The article reports the correlation as positive and significant (p < 0.001).

> "The correlation coefficients between spiritual intelligence, emotional intelligence, and student achievement were r = 0.36 (95% CI: 0.18–0.51) and r = 0.50 (95% CI: 0.28–0.67), respectively."
