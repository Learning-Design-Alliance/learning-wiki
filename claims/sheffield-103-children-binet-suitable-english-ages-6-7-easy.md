`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The bibliography reports Taylor's study of Sheffield children aged 5 to 14 using the 1911 scale: "the scale is suitable for English children," though ages 6 and 7 seemed too easy and tests above 11 unsatisfactory. Taylor also discussed the pictures, problems and definitions tests, dullness and precocity, and criticized marking.

> "The Sheffield results suggest that, as a whole, the scale is suitable for English children." Tests for ages 6 and 7 seem too easy. Tests above 11 seem unsatisfactory."
