Pooled multiple-choice results from the 45-participant usability questionnaire show the operating-temperature question was answered correctly by 23 of 45 (51%), described as "only close to half of all participants"; other weak points concerned test workflow steps.

> "the weakest point of user comprehension was related to the operating temperature in the intended use, with only close to half of all participants identifying the correct operating temperature for the test"
