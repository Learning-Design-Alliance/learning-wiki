# Quantum informatics concepts can be categorized within the Great Principles framework with almost no need for re-categorization

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` An expert categorization of quantum informatics key concepts within Denning's seven windows required almost no re-categorization, suggesting the framework accommodates quantum informatics. [→ Paparo 2024](#paparo-2024)

## Evidence

### Paparo 2024

Paparo, G., Finsterhoelzl, R., Waldvogel, B., and Grillenberger, M. (2024). Teaching Quantum Informatics at School: Computer Science Principles and Standards. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Expert categorization process: the first author categorized quantum informatics concepts [22] within the Great Principles, reviewed by co-authors with quantum information theory and computer science education expertise and an external computer science expert. The review found "almost no need for re-categorization". No empirical test of the categorization was conducted.

> "The categorization was then critically reviewed, and re-categorization and the addition of new concepts were discussed. There was almost no need for re-categorization."
