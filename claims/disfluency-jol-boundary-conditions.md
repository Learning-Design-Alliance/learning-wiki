# Disfluency affected metacognitive monitoring (JOLs) only under specific conditions: within-subjects manipulation with unrelated word pairs, or on screen rather than paper

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Disfluency reduced JOLs only when manipulated within-subjects and only for unrelated word pairs, because item relatedness otherwise replaced disfluency as a monitoring cue. [→ Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel 2016](#tim-kuhl-alexander-eitel-2016)

## Evidence

### Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel 2016

Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel. (2016). Effects of disfluency on cognitive and metacognitive processes and outcomes. Metacognition and Learning, 11, 1–13. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9154-x

Narrative review of Magreehan et al. (2015) and related studies; the review reports that disfluency affected JOLs only under within-subjects manipulation and unrelated word pairs. No effect size is printed.

> "disfluency affected monitoring only when it was manipulated within-subjects, because only then, the learner recognized a difference between fluent and disfluent presentation."
