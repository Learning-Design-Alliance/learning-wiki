`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Attendance data collected by the Plymouth Parent Partnership across the six cohorts of the trial; the report judges this low attendance rate might have contributed to the lack of overall impact on the whole group.

> "The average attendance rate indicated that only 57% of the parents participated in five or more of the ten sessions, which was judged by the development team to be the level of attendance required to be considered a ‘completer’ of the SPOKES programme."
