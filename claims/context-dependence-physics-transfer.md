# Physics learning is context-dependent: beginning students may not map a principle learned in one context (spinning ballerina) onto an isomorphic problem in another context (collapsing neutron star)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Beginning students need effective instructional approaches to help them focus on the "deep" features of problems, because they may not immediately discern that two superficially different problems map onto the same principle. [→ Chandralekha Singh 2016](#chandralekha-singh-2016)

An illustrative argument from the column's "Learning is context-dependent" section, using the angular momentum conservation example of a spinning ballerina versus a collapsing neutron star. It is the author's expert illustration, not a reported study.

> "To an instructor who has vast experience, recognizing that these two problems can be mapped onto each other is easy, but beginning students need effective instructional approaches to help them focus on the “deep” features of the problems."
