## Evidence

### Mahoney Mary T. 2024

Mahoney Mary T., Linkowski Lauren C., Wu Trudy C., Chen Jie Jane, Neilsen Beth K., Thompson Petria S., Mix Michael D., Sura Karna T., Mattes Malcolm D. (2024). Analysis of Oncology and Radiation Therapy Representation on the National Board of Medical Examiners Official Practice Material for the United States National Standardized Medical Board Examinations. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-024-02475-0

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of all commercially accessible NBME self-assessments and open-access sample questions for USMLE Steps 1–3 and shelf exams; the article reports "337 unique oncology-related questions out of the 3878 questions assessed across all practice material (8.7%)" (the abstract reports 8.6%).

> "There were a total of 337 unique oncology-related questions out of the 3878 questions assessed across all practice material (8.7%). Oncology questions were identified on every examination type evaluated."

### Mahoney Mary T. 2024 (2)

Mahoney Mary T., Linkowski Lauren C., Wu Trudy C., Chen Jie Jane, Neilsen Beth K., Thompson Petria S., Mix Michael D., Sura Karna T., Mattes Malcolm D. (2024). Analysis of Oncology and Radiation Therapy Representation on the National Board of Medical Examiners Official Practice Material for the United States National Standardized Medical Board Examinations. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-024-02475-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis subgroup analysis comparing oncology question frequency across USMLE Steps; Step 1 had 132/1320 (10%), Step 2 CK 65/920 (7.1%), Step 3 16/313 (5.1%), p = 0.006.

> "Among USMLE exams, there was a significantly higher frequency of oncology questions within the Step 1 questions (n = 132/1320, 10%) compared to Step 2 CK (n = 65/920, 7.1%) and Step 3 (n = 16/313, 5.1%) practice questions (p = 0.006)."
