## Evidence

### Lim HM 2025

Lim HM, Teo CH, Lee YK, et al. (2025). Implementation Outcomes of Reusable Learning Objects in Health Care Education Across Three Malaysian Universities: Evaluation Using the RE-AIM Framework. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/63882

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Google Analytics fidelity analysis during implementation. Completion rates ranged from 5.6% (10/179) to 85% (78/92); nonbounced users ranged from 16.3% (165/1014) to 88.5% (370/418).

> "RLOs integrated into the curriculum as teaching and learning materials achieved a completion rate greater than 70 % (RLO 8 and RLO 23), suggesting that learners are more invested in completing these RLOs."
