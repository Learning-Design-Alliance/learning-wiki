`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

Editorial argument, not an empirical study. The author lists reasons lecturing persists, including that it "satisfies the pupil's craving for authority, for excuse from self-exertion", and adds that the fundamental reason is the teacher's feeling that the method is successful.

> "There are many reasons for this pedagogical error; lecturing is easy as compared with the skillful eliciting of a pupil's reaction; it gives opportunity for the exploiting of personal opinion; it satisfies the pupil's craving for authority, for excuse from self-exertion, and for the ready-made pellet of serviceable knowledge."
