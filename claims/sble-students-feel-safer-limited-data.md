The review's plain language summary reports that "students in schools with SBLE tended to feel safer at school." The abstract notes the pooled estimate (g = 0.18) came from only seven effect sizes drawn from two correlational studies.

> "We also found that students in schools with SBLE tended to feel safer at school, although this finding is less trustworthy because it is based on very little data."
