Evidence¶

Duan 2026¶

Duan, N., Li, L., Lin, G. & Chen, H. (2026). The impact of AI anxiety on career decisions of college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37648-y

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

PROCESS macro Model 5 (SPSS) on the 315-student survey, controlling six demographic variables and the mediated path. The AI anxiety × self-efficacy interaction was non-significant: "β value of 0.015" with a CI including zero; H5 was not supported.

"The interaction term between AI anxiety and self-efficacy had a β value of 0.015, a t-value of 0.530, and a p-value of 0.596. Its 95% confidence interval was [−0.042, 0.073] (which includes 0)."

Duan 2026 (2)¶

Duan, N., Li, L., Lin, G. & Chen, H. (2026). The impact of AI anxiety on career decisions of college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37648-y

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Robustness check using the Latent Moderated Structural Equations method in Mplus 8.5. The interaction "remained non-significant ( β = 0.018, p = 0.612)", confirming the PROCESS result despite measurement-error concerns.

"we conducted supplementary analysis using the Latent Moderated Structural Equations (LMS) method in Mplus 8.5. The interaction term remained non-significant ( β = 0.018, p = 0.612, 95% CI = [−0.048, 0.085])"
