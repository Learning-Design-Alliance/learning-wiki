## Evidence

### Sermier Dessemontet 2019

Sermier Dessemontet, Rachel; Martinet, Catherine; de Chambrier, Anne-Françoise; Martini-Willemin, Britt-Marie; Audrin, Catherine. (2019). A meta-analysis on the effectiveness of phonics instruction for teaching decoding skills to students with intellectual disability. Educational research review, no. 26, p. 52-70. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2019.01.001

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of eight single-case and six experimental or quasi-experimental group studies, "encom- passing a total of 297 participants with intellectual disability". The pooled effect on decoding skills was "large: g = 1.42 (95% CI: 0.75, 2.10)".
