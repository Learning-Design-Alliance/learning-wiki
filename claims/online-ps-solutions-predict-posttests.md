## Evidence

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A stepwise regression using online PS solution, EB reasoning, and data recording as predictors found "the online PS solutions was the only factor that predicted students’ ECT posttest score" with printed β = 0.45 and R² accounting for 20% of variance.

> "the online PS solutions was the only factor that predicted students’ ECT posttest score (β = 0.45, p < 0.001), accounting for 20% of the variance (F = 27.38, p < 0.001)"

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023 (2)

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same stepwise regression found online PS solutions predicted "EPST posttest score" with printed β = 0.27, accounting for 7% of the variance; EB reasoning and data recording did not enter the models.

> "EPST posttest score (β = 0.27, p < 0.01), accounting for 7% of the variance (F = 8.59, p < 0.001)"
