## Evidence

### Mou Qiaoxing 2024

Mou Qiaoxing, Zhuang Jie, Wu Qunhong, Zhong Yaqin, Dai Qianqian, Cao Xin, Gao Yuexia, Lu Qingyun, Zhao Miaomiao. (2024). Social media addiction and academic engagement as serial mediators between social anxiety and academic performance among college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01635-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regression predicting academic engagement (R² = 0.160, F = 101.506, p < 0.001) found "social anxiety was significantly negatively related to academic engagement (β = -0.307, p < 0.001)" in the survey of 2,661 students.

> "As predicted by hypothesis H3, social anxiety was significantly negatively related to academic engagement (β = -0.307, p < 0.001). This suggested that for per one SD increase in social anxiety, academic engagement would decrease by 0.307 SD."
