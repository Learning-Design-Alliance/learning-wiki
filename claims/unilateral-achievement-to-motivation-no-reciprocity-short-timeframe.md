## Evidence

### Vu 2025

Vu, T., Meeter, M., Hofman, A., Jansen, B., Magis‐Weinberg, L., van Triest, E., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Academic motivation–achievement cycle and the behavioural pathways: A short‐timeframe experiment with manipulated perceived achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 683–722. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Registered RI-CLPM analysis of the eight-time-point experiment with 309 Dutch undergraduates learning English vocabulary. The article reports "a unilateral effect of achievement on motivation (i.e., no reciprocity)" stable across time points; no effect size is printed for this finding.

> "We found a unilateral effect of achievement on motivation (i.e., no reciprocity), which remained stable across the time points."

### Vu 2025 (2)

Vu, T., Meeter, M., Hofman, A., Jansen, B., Magis‐Weinberg, L., van Triest, E., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Academic motivation–achievement cycle and the behavioural pathways: A short‐timeframe experiment with manipulated perceived achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 683–722. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics across the eight blocks of the same experiment show weak, inconsistent motivation–performance correlations; the article prints "r ranging from .11 to .23" and notes the correlations were not always present.

> "Performance and motivation did not always correlate and when they did, only weakly so (r ranging from .11 to .23)."
