Final ranking task after all four clips in the 49-participant within-subject study (Fig. 4). On a 1-7 liking scale, Standard Captions were liked best (MD = 7, SD = 1.01), both highlight variants at median 6, and Timed Keywords at 3 (SD = 1.71).
