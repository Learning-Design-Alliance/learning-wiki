## Evidence

### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article reports its prior survey work over one semester across two survey weeks, describing student motivations for completing or not completing HWCs. Non-completers' most common reported reasons were forgetting and time limitations.

> "all students who completed HWCs reported they were at least partially motivated by improving their grade, but a notable portion of students also reported being motivated by improving their understanding of the content and learning from their mistakes."
