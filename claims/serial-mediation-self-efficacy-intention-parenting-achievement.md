## Evidence

### Hayek J 2022

Hayek J, Schneider F, Lahoud N, Tueni M, de Vries H. (2022). Authoritative parenting stimulates academic achievement, also partly via self-efficacy and intention towards getting good grades. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265595

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Serial mediation analysis (Hayes PROCESS model 6, 5000 bootstrap samples) on n = 338, adjusted for age, school type, religion and parents' education, with self-efficacy and intention as sequential mediators. The authors report the relationship "is partially mediated by self-efficacy and intention, sequentially."

> "Finally, the last indirect effect of parenting style (neglectful vs authoritative) via the sequence of self-efficacy (M1) and intention (M2) leading to academic achievement is statistically significant (IE = -0.04; CI = -0.11, -0.003)."
