## Evidence

### Adhikari Yadav Saroj 2022

Adhikari Yadav Saroj, Poudel Sangeeta, Pandey Oshna, Jaiswal Dhiraj Prasad, Malla Bhupendra Prakash, Thakur Brajesh Kumar, Gautam Swotantra, Pandey Sirish Raj. (2022). Performance and preference of problem-based learning (PBL) and lecture-based classes among medical students of Nepal. F1000Research 11:183. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.107103.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative responses in the perception questionnaire of 107 students; Table 2 shows high non-response for CHS (48.60%) and ICM (37.38%), with few preferences for either PBL or lectures.

> "Regarding CHS, students mentioned they learn public/community health better in community postings and lecture sessions with a group of faculty members as a part of Community-Based Learning Education (CBLE). Regarding ICM they prefer it on hospital wards and bedside teaching."
