Li Tingting 2022¶

Li Tingting, Wang Weidong, Li Zhijie, Wang Hongmiao, Liu Xiaodan. (2022). Problem-based or lecture-based learning, old topic in the new field: a meta-analysis on the effects of PBL teaching method in Chinese standardized residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03254-5

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Satisfaction with teaching was the most-studied questionnaire outcome: 25 studies with 859 intervention and 824 control residents, pooled OR = 1.34 (95% CI 1.16–1.55), a statistically significant difference favoring the intervention group.
