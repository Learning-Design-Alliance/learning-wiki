## Evidence

### Kim Seo-Yoon 2020

Kim Seo-Yoon, Shin Dongmin, Kim Hyun Jeong, Karm Myong-Hwan. (2020). Changes of knowledge and practical skills before and after retraining for basic life support: Focused on students of Dental School. International Journal of Medical Sciences 17(18): 3082-3090. https://doi.org/10.7150/ijms.47343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the pre-retraining baseline in their one-group pretest-posttest study of 98 dental students, whose pre-retraining knowledge and skills scores were low (43.2 ± 11.0 for skills). The article attributes non-maintenance to "lack of retraining and less real-time experience of cardiac arrest."

> "The present results reveal that the educational effect was not maintained appropriately after 2 years due to lack of retraining and less real-time experience of cardiac arrest."
