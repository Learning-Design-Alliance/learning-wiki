## Evidence

### Abbasi M 2025

Abbasi M, Taghadosi M, Atoof F, Hosseiniara R. (2025). A quasi-experimental comparison of Kolb and Peyton educational approaches on CPR knowledge and performance among nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06996-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 of the repeated measures ANOVA results: total scores rose within all three groups (within-group p = 0.001 for Kolb and Peyton, 0.001 for control), and between-group p-values were 0.96 at baseline, 0.001 at one week, and 0.00 at six weeks.

> "Total score Kolb 48.31 ± 9.71 67.28 ± 6.99 69.08 ± 5.99 0.001 Peyton 40.81 ± 12.39 65.69 ± 7.68 69.39 ± 5.21 0.001 Control 53.95 ± 8.23 54.6 ± 7.87 61.66 ± 7.52 0.001 *p-value 0.96 0.001 0.00 -"
