## Evidence

### Sattler 2025

Sattler, Kierra; Font, Sarah; Panlilio, Carlomagno. (2025). Foster care entry and later academic achievement among infants involved with child protective services. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Within-race logistic regression models (Table 4) with predicted probabilities plotted in Figure 1. The article reports the reading and math odds ratios for Black children quoted above; among White children only absenteeism was significantly associated. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Among Black children, experiencing foster care during infancy (OR =1.88, p < .001) or toddlerhood (OR =1.45, p < .05) is associated with higher odds of meeting basic or above reading skills. Foster care during infancy is also related to basic or above mathematics skills (OR =1.48, p < .01) among Black children."
