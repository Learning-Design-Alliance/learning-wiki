## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Model 3, PCPs with dementia care experience had higher PPV (OR = 2.649, 95%CI: 1.288–5.445), and only among GPs did those with experience score higher on skills than those without (P < 0.001); no standardised effect size is printed. [→ Qiu Yanglin 2025](#qiu-yanglin-2025)
`q2 i?` Knowledge, attitudes and skills scores did not significantly differ between public health physicians, nurses, and social workers with and without dementia care experience. [→ Qiu Yanglin 2025 (2)](#qiu-yanglin-2025-2)
