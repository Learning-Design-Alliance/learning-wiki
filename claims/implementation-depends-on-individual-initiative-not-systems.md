## Evidence

### Jacob 2026

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., Schneider, H., & Peters Hinton, V. (2026). Institutional conditions for integrating emerging technologies in higher education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/294

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 120 complete responses distributed via AAC&U's member directory to faculty, staff, and administrators. Fewer than 30% agreed technology policy was clearly communicated, while some faculty reported "lots of freedom and encouragement to try new digital tools".

> "Survey respondents reported a wide range of practices even within the same campus. Some faculty described having lots of freedom and encouragement to try new digital tools, yet less than 30% agree or strongly agree that technology decisions and policy are clearly communicated to the campus community."
