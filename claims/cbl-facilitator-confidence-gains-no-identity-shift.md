## Evidence

### Delany Caroline 2025

Delany Caroline, Illing Jan, McIlroy Marie, Daly Ronan, Bruen Catherine, Alamuddin Naji, Condron Clare, Crehan Martina, Doherty Sally, Meagher Frances, Offiah Gozie, O'Neill Sarah, Stuart Ellen, Torrens Christopher, Morgan Maria P. (2025). Exploring the lived experience of faculty implementing case-based learning: A phenomenological study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07364-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the IPA analysis of ten facilitator interviews: participants "did not acknowledge a shift in personal identity" but described increased confidence in professional ability, awareness of forgotten teaching skills, and new techniques transferable to other teaching roles.

> "Participants/interviewees found it difficult to answer if their role as facilitator resulted in an identity shift. The most resounding opinion amongst interviewees was that the role created benefits and opportunities which contributed to their perceptions of themselves as teachers."
