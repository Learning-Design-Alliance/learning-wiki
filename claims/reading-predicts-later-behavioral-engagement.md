## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Preschool reading achievement significantly predicted first-grade behavioral engagement, and third-grade reading achievement significantly predicted fifth-grade behavioral engagement, in a cross-lagged panel model controlling autoregressive effects. [→ Guo 2015](#guo-2015)

## Evidence

### Guo 2015

Guo, Y., Sun, S., Breit-Smith, A., Morrison, F. J., & Connor, C. M. (2015). Behavioral engagement and reading achievement in elementary-school-age children: A longitudinal cross-lagged analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 332–347. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0037638

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged structural equation modeling (Model 3, the final retained model) of NICHD SECCYD data from 1,160 children assessed at preschool, first, third, and fifth grades. The model found "the two positive and statistically significant paths from reading achievement to behavioral engagement."

> "Of greatest interest were the two positive and statistically significant paths from reading achievement to behavioral engagement (see Figure 3). Specifically, a child’s reading achievement in preschool and third grade significantly predicted behavioral engagement at a later time"
