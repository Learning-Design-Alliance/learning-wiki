### Alfadda 2021

Alfadda, Hind Abdulaziz, & Mahdi, Hassan Saleh. (2021). Measuring Students’ Use of Zoom Application in Language Course Based on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-020-09752-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis (Table 4) of the 75-student questionnaire data testing Hypothesis 1. The printed Pearson correlations include r = .42 for computer self-efficacy with attitude and r = .49 with behavioral intention: "Computer Self-Efficacy and Attitude (.42) and between Computer Self-Efficacy and Behavioral Intention (.49)"; the hypothesis was fully supported.
