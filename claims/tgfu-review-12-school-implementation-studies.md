Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines with the PICO strategy, searching Taylor and Francis, ERIC, SCOPUS and Web of Science for publications between January 2014 and December 2019; 449 initial publications were screened and "12 articles were fully assessed". The review reports a "great contrast between theoretical research or research outside school contexts and those carried out in schools".
