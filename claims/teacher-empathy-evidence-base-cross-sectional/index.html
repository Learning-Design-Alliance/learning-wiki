Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann. (2022). Is Empathy the Key to Effective Teaching? A Systematic Review of Its Association with Teacher-Student Interactions and Student Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09649-y

Study-characteristics synthesis of the 41 included records: the review reports that the majority of studies "reported only cross-sectional analyses" of the empathy–outcome link, with only rare longitudinal or intervention designs, which limits causal conclusions about teacher empathy.
