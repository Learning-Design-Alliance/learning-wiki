## Evidence

### James K. Freericks and Leanne Doughty 2023

James K. Freericks and Leanne Doughty. (2023). Should we trade off higher-level mathematics for abstraction to improve student understanding of quantum mechanics?. [doi:10.1117/12.2670601](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2670601)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors argue that the Quantum Mechanics Concept Assessment and a one-dimensional survey assume state preparation, exact position measurement, and subsequent wavefunction evolution — scenarios 'very far from real experiments'. Their course instead teaches time-of-flight measurement and destructive single-particle detection, so 'we cannot employ standard assessment instruments to gauge student learning in our course'.

> "those instruments are based on a technical approach that uses the Copenhagen interpretation and assumes the measurements are nondestructive. Very few of the experimental methods to actually measure individual quanta operate in this fashion"
