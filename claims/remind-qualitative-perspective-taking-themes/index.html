Inductive thematic analysis of in-situ reflection prompts in the play-testing study; "94% of participants (17 out of 18) felt sad for JITTER", with intercoder reliability of 94% agreement and Cohen's κ = 0.93.

q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of Phase 2 reflection prompts on why the avatar did nothing: "over half (55%) attributed it to low skills or confidence", and 38% of children indicated fear of the bully's retaliation.

"over half (55%) attributed it to low skills or confidence (e.g., “maybe she didn’t feel confident, because of FUSE’s rude words. ”[P9], or“she doesn’t know how to stand up. ”[P2])."
