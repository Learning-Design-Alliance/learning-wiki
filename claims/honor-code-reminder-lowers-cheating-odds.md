## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Displaying a honor code reminder during an online test significantly reduces the odds of cheating by 50% compared to no intervention. [→ Mukherjee 2023](#mukherjee-2023)

## Evidence

### Mukherjee 2023

Mukherjee, S., Rohles, B., Distler, V., Lenzini, G., & Koenig, V. (2023). The effects of privacy-non-invasive interventions on cheating prevention and user experience in unproctored online assessments: An empirical study. Computers and Education, 207, 104925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104925

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In a between-subjects experiment (N = 997) simulating a high-stakes unproctored online test, logistic regression showed the honor code reminder condition had an odds ratio of 0.502 (p = 0.006) versus control, a "50% decrease in odds" of cheating.

> "Honor code reminders (p = 0.006) and warning messages (p = 0.016) had significant negative effects on cheating (50% and 44% decrease in odds, respectively)."
