Descriptive analysis of Table 2 pretest/posttest means for the four ability-by-environment groups; the article states learning environment "had greatest impact on the high constructivist group" and "the least impact on the low transmissive group". Descriptive only; no significance test reported for this ordering.

> "The post-test grand mean is 24.16 and the direction of gains in scores over grand means from the highest to the lowest is in this order: high constructivist (6.15) > high transmissive (2.23) > low constructivist (-3.64) > low transmissive (-4.75)."
