### Ying Hu 2026 (2)

Ying Hu, Yuhang Shu & Xin Zhao. (2026). Children consider changes in performance over time when reasoning about academic achievements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00401-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 improving condition replicated the developmental evaluation pattern with a significant age effect (B = 2.44, p < 0.001); children favored the constant character up to about 5 years and the improving character from around 7 years.

> "the ﬁnal model revealed a signiﬁcant main effect of age (B = 2.44, SE = 0.63, 95% CI [1.20, 3.68], p < 0.001; β = 3.85), showing a developmental trajectory along which children increasingly evaluated the improving character more favorably with age."
