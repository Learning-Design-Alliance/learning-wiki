# The alerting & guiding dashboard reduced teachers' overall workload more than the mirroring dashboard in authentic settings (H2 supported)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Overall RTLX workload dropped from 35.6 (no dashboard) to 24.4 (alerting & guiding), a significant difference with Cohen's d = 1.6 (p = 0.000003). [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023](#reet-kasepalu-2023)
`q2 i3` Compared to the mirroring-only dashboard, the alerting & guiding condition showed lower overall workload with Cohen's d = 1.1 (p = 0.002). [→ Reet Kasepalu 2023 (2)](#reet-kasepalu-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Reet Kasepalu 2023

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Unpaired T-Tests (not all teachers completed all three conditions) compared RTLX workload in the quasi-experiment's three dashboard conditions; the no dashboard vs alerting & guiding comparison was statistically significant "with a big effect (Cohen's d = 1.6)". Mean workload fell from 35.6 to 24.4 in Table 9.

> "Having compared the no dashboard and an alerting & guiding dashboard condition, a statistically significant difference (p = 0.000003) with a big effect (Cohen's d = 1.6)"

### Reet Kasepalu 2023 (2)

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Unpaired T-Test comparing the mirroring-only and alerting & guiding conditions found workload "decreased in the alerting & guiding dashboard condition, having a big effect (1.1) and being statistically significant (p = 0.002)", i.e. Cohen's d = 1.1.

> "when comparing the mirroring only and alerting & guiding dashboard conditions, the overall workload of the teachers decreased in the alerting & guiding dashboard condition, having a big effect (1.1) and being statistically significant (p = 0.002)"
