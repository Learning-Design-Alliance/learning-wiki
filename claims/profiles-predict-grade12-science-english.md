Jun Wu, Shuoli Qi and Yueshan Zhong. (2022). Intrinsic motivation, Need for cognition, Grit, Growth Mindset and Academic Achievement in High School Students: Latent Profiles and Its Predictive Effects. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/mheg7](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/mheg7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression with high intrinsic motivation students as reference group tested profile effects on 12th-grade outcomes. The article reports "the profiles of students have a significant predictive effect on Science and English scores in 12th grade."

> "Students low in grit tends to be significantly correlated with English and Science performance in 12th grade (p = .000). Students low in need for cognition and high in intrinsic motivation tends to correlate significantly with the English score in 12th grade."
