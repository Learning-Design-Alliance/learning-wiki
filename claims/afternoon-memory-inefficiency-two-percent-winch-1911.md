## Evidence

### W. H. Winch 1911

W. H. Winch. (1911). Mental Fatigue in Day School Children as Measured by Immediate Memory. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled school experiment with two equal groups of a Standard IV class (51 boys) matched on four preliminary consonant-memory tests, one tested mornings (9.45–10 A.M.) and one afternoons (4–4.15 P.M.) on four final tests. The article reports "an advantage of 2 per cent, in improvability of the morning over the afternoon workers"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Group A—the morning group—improves on its preliminary record to the extent of 3 per cent.: Group B—the afternoon group—improves on its preliminary record to the extent of 1 per cent. There is apparently an advantage of 2 per cent, in improvability of the morning over the afternoon workers."
