## Evidence

### Sweller and & Cooper 1985

John Sweller and Graham A. Cooper. (1985). The Use of Worked Examples as a Substitute for Problem Solving in Learning Algebra. Cognition and Instruction, 2(1), 59-89. https://www.jstor.org/stable/3233555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 3, a two-group study with 22 Year 9 students (11 worked example, 11 conventional), found a significant acquisition-time difference: "U (11,11) = 4", replicating Experiment 2. The difference was due almost entirely to the limited time spent studying worked examples.

> "The difference between the two groups in time spent in acquisition is significant, U (11,11) = 4, replicating the results of Experiment 2."
