## Evidence

### Turaev 2026

Turaev, S., Aldabet, S., John, M., Musthafa, N., Awad, M., Zaki, N., & Shuaib, K. (2026). Measuring Curriculum–Labor Market Alignment at the Scale of a Program Portfolio. Preprint. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7122139](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7122139)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blind validation in which two faculty raters coded a stratified sample of 300 competencies (150 random, 150 hard cases) without sight of the model's assignments. Inter-rater agreement was κ 0.912 (domain) and 0.858 (level); validity agreement showed κ 0.444 but PABAK 0.907 and Gwet's AC1 0.949 under a skewed marginal.

> "The extraction is correct on 98% of validity judgments, 99% of domain assignments, and 99% of cognitive levels overall; it is essentially perfect on the random stratum and holds between 96% and 99% on the deliberately hard stratum and on the demand side"
