## Evidence

### Winstone 2014

Winstone, N. E., Nash, R. A., Parker, M., & Rowntree, J. (2014). Supporting Learners' Agentic Engagement with Feedback: A Systematic Review and a Taxonomy of Recipience Processes. Educational Psychologist (in press).

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Nelson and Schunn (2009), a comparison of first and second essay drafts of history undergraduates following critical feedback. Relatedly, learners reported preferring feedback that directly identifies issues to be addressed over surface-feature comments such as spelling and grammar.

> "They found that students were more likely to put their feedback into practice when the problems had been clearly located in the essay, solutions were proposed, and a summary was presented"
