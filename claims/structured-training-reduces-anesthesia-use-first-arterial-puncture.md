## Evidence

### Rolland-Debord C 2026

Rolland-Debord C, Juret L, Simon M, El Mouhajer A, Londner C, Morélot-Panzini C, Chenivesse C, Similowski T. (2026). Impact of a Structured Training Program on Medical Student Confidence and Behavior During Their First Radial Arterial Puncture: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78086

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative observational study of 67 third-year medical students assigned by clinical placement; anesthesia use compared with the Fisher exact test. The study found "Anesthesia use was significantly less frequent in group 1 compared to group 2 (7/20, 35% vs 28/36, 77.8%; P=.003)."
