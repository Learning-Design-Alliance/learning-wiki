### Larose 2025

Larose, Marie‐Pier; Schuurmans, Isabel; Barker, Edward D.; Garcia Mondragon, Liliana; Tiemeier, Henning; Waldman, Irwin; Cecil, Charlotte. (2025). Associations between genetic predisposition to mental health problems and academic achievement: a developmental perspective using two population‐based cohorts. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-cohort comparison of PGS-EXT associations with symptom mediators: "We found no significant association between PGS‐EXT and mental health symptoms in the Generation R Study", whereas in ALSPAC PGS-EXT was associated mainly with EXT symptoms, with the strongest association with CD. The authors interpret this as the PGS's predictive value emerging in adolescence when the behaviors it was built on (e.g., alcohol consumption) appear.
