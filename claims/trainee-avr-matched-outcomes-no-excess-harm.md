`q3 i?` In the matched AVR cohort, mortality (1.9% vs 1.2%, p<0.001) and postoperative dialysis (2.4% vs 1.8%, p=0.001) were lower in trainee first-operator cases, with no significant differences in return to theatre, cerebrovascular events, deep sternal wound infection or length of stay.
