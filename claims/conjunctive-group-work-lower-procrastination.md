# Conjunctive group work shows mixed evidence of lower task procrastination than individual work among high trait-procrastinators

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In an exploratory ANOVA, conjunctive group work produced lower task procrastination than individual work, with a small effect (d = .35). [→ Koppenborg 2026](#koppenborg-2026)
`q2 i?` The preregistered regression showed a significant negative coefficient for conjunctive group work but a non-significant overall model fit. [→ Koppenborg 2026 (2)](#koppenborg-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Koppenborg 2026

Koppenborg, Markus, Hüffmeier, Joachim, & Klingsieck, Katrin B. (2026). Is procrastination among students lower in group work? Evidence from a registered field experiment. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70069

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory one-way ANOVA in the Results comparing conjunctive group work against individual work on task procrastination in the registered field experiment (N = 218 high trait-procrastinators). The effect was significant, F(1, 120) = 3.71, p = .029, "d = .35" described as "a small effect".

> "A one‐way ANOVA (conjunctive group work vs. individual work) revealed a significant effect of experimental condition on task procrastination, F(1, 120) = 3.71, p = .029, d = .35 (i.e., a small effect; Cohen, 1988)."

### Koppenborg 2026 (2)

Koppenborg, Markus, Hüffmeier, Joachim, & Klingsieck, Katrin B. (2026). Is procrastination among students lower in group work? Evidence from a registered field experiment. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered OLS regression (Model 1) with task procrastination as criterion, controlling for preference for group work. The overall model fit was non-significant while the conjunctive group work coefficient was significant, so evidence on H1a is inconclusive.

> "Results revealed a non‐significant overall model fit and a significant coefficient for conjunctive group work (see Model 1 in Table 5)."
