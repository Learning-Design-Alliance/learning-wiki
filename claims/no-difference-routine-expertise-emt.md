## Evidence

### Aliaga L 2024

Aliaga L, Bavolek RA, Cooper B, Mariorenzi A, Ahn J, Kraut A, Duong D, Burger C, Gisondi MA. (2024). Error Management Training and Adaptive Expertise in Learning Computed Tomography Interpretation: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.31600

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial secondary outcome on the familiar posttest cases mimicking training cases. Scores were 75.1%, 73.6%, and 74.4% across the three cohorts with P = .84; no effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). Authors note prior studies suggested error-based methods hindered routine expertise, so this null is reassuring.

> "Mean posttest scores on the routine expertise cases were 75.1% (95% CI, 72.0%-78.2%) in the difficult EMT cohort, 73.6% (95% CI, 69.3%-77.9%) in the easy EMT cohort, and 74.4% (95% CI, 71.3%-77.4%) in the EAT control cohort (P = .84)."
