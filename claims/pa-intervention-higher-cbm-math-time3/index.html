Evidence¶

Erwin et al. 2012¶

Heather Erwin, Alicia Fedewa, Soyeon Ahn. (2012). Student Academic Performance Outcomes of a Classroom Physical Activity Intervention: A Pilot Study. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 4(3), 473-487. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068595

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pilot with 29 third graders in two classrooms (intervention N = 16, control N = 13) over 20 weeks. Simple-effects follow-up to a significant three-way interaction showed the "intervention group" significantly higher on CBM math only at time 3.

"the intervention group ( M = 24.56, SD = 2.21) scored significantly higher on CBM scores than the control group ( M = 13.69, SD = 2.45) at time 3 ( M diff = 10.87, p = .003), but not time 1 ( M diff = 2.75, p = .39) or time 2 ( M diff = 2.16, p = .49)"
