# Face-to-face students scored substantially higher than remote students on practical skills in a hybrid chest physiotherapy seminar

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The face-to-face group scored better than the remote group on the practical skills checklist (median difference 4, effect size 2.05). [→ Bellini Raffaella 2024](#bellini-raffaella-2024)

## Evidence

### Bellini Raffaella 2024

Bellini Raffaella, Rossettini Giacomo, Letafatkar Amir, Dell’Isola Andrea, Battista Simone. (2024). Physiotherapy students’ academic performance and satisfaction after following a chest physiotherapy course in hybrid mode: a case-control study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-76051-3

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Practical skills were assessed the day after the seminar by a blinded expert tutor using an ad hoc five-item checklist. Quartile regression showed a median difference of 4 in favour of face-to-face, "and effect size of 2.05 [95%CI 1.35;2.76]".

> "At the checklist, the remote group presented a median of 5 [3–7] and the face-to-face of 8.5 [8–10], with a median difference of 4 [95%CI 2.31;5.68] in favour of the face-to-face group, and effect size of 2.05 [95%CI 1.35;2.76]."
