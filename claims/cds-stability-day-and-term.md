## Evidence

### Gonzalez Villasanti 2020

Gonzalez Villasanti, H., Justice, L. M., Chaparro-Moreno, L. J., Lin, T.-J., & Purtell, K. (2020). Automatized analysis of children's exposure to child-directed speech in preschool settings: Validation and application. PLoS ONE, 15(11), e0242511. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242511

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Application of the validated CIDR system to uncoded Fall PM, Winter AM, and Winter PM recordings for n = 9 children with all four sessions; rANOVA found no significant differences for most adult CDS measures. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "We conducted repeated measures analysis of variance (rANOVA) with time as the between-subjects factor for each measure, revealing no significant differences for most CDS measures during interactions with adults between morning and afternoon and fall to winter."

### Gonzalez Villasanti 2020 (2)

Gonzalez Villasanti, H., Justice, L. M., Chaparro-Moreno, L. J., Lin, T.-J., & Purtell, K. (2020). Automatized analysis of children's exposure to child-directed speech in preschool settings: Validation and application. PLoS ONE, 15(11), e0242511. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242511

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same rANOVA application with n = 9, focal child TNW and NDW differed between morning and afternoon; the authors state the validity of these tests is unclear due to small sample size and modest predicted-reference correlation.

> "However, measures concerning focal child speech were different between morning and afternoon for TNW (p = 0.04) and NDW (p = 0.03)."
