## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trainees in the pedagogical group took less time to complete the assessment of models, had fewer failures and higher scores, completed laparoscopic sutures and knots faster, and were more proficient with laparoscopic instruments (P < 0.050). [→ Pan Ruijun 2024](#pan-ruijun-2024)

## Evidence

### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Operational assessment of models (One Track Transfer, One Tunnel Pass, High and Low Pillars, Laparoscopic Suturing and Knotting) in the randomized trial, n = 30 per group, scored blindly by three trained assessors. For example, suturing and knotting time was 248.00(46.35) versus 301.07(77.27) s, t = −3.23, P = 0.002.

> "Trainees in the pedagogical group took less time to complete the assessment of models, had fewer failures and higher scores, completed laparoscopic sutures and knots faster, were more proficient in the management of laparoscopic instruments and completed them at higher quality (P < 0.050)."

### Pan Ruijun 2024 (2)

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 of the same trial reports One Tunnel Pass completion time of 230.43(107.68) s in the pedagogical group versus 274.50(130.99) s in the traditional group, t = −1.42, P = 0.160 — no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.
