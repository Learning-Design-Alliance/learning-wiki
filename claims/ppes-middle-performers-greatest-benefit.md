### George Ann 2025

George Ann, Blaauw Duane, Dangor Ziyaad, Lala Sanjay G. (2025). Evaluation of the impact of an online introductory paediatric physical examination skills course on students’ OSCE performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08177-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Propensity score matched sub-group analysis of the 2022 cohort; the middle 60% (n=195) had a matched ATE of "8.2% [95% CI: 4.3–12.2]"; lowest and top 20% subgroups were non-significant (p=0.217 and p=0.501). No standardised effect size printed for the subgroup contrast.

> "The sub-group analysis (Table 5) suggests that the course had the greatest impact on the middle 60% of the class in terms of previous academic performance, with a matched ATE of 8.2% [95% CI: 4.3–12.2]. The impact did not appear statistically significant for students from schools in quintiles 1 and 5, rural areas, or those from poorer feeder schools."
