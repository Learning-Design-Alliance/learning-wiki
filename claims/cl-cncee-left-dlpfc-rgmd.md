## Evidence

### Wang Song 2017

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain VBM multiple regression of CNCEE scores on regional gray matter density, covarying gender, age and total GMV, found that "CNCEE score was positively related to the rGMD of the left DLPFC" (r = 0.38, p < 0.001) in the middle frontal gyrus.

> "CNCEE score was positively related to the rGMD of the left DLPFC (the middle frontal gyrus) (r = 0.38, p < 0.001) (see Table 2 and Fig. 1), after controlling for gender, age and the total GMV."

### Wang Song 2017 (2)

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prediction analysis using four-fold balanced cross-validation with linear regression showed left DLPFC rGMD "can reliably predict individuals’ CNCEE scores" with r(predicted, observed) = 0.35, p < 0.001, after the same covariates.

> "The results showed that the rGMD of the left DLPFC can reliably predict individuals’ CNCEE scores [r(predicted, observed) = 0.35, p < 0.001], after adjusting for gender, age and the total GMV."
