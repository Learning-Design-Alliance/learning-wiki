`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null findings from the same multiple linear regression (n = 176). Table 5 prints non-significant p-values for age (0.572), gender (0.326), hospital type (0.591), job title (0.484), and working unit (0.452); no effect sizes are reported for these predictors.

> "In contrast, demographic factors such as age, gender, job position, and type of hospital did not significantly predict knowledge scores, suggesting that these characteristics alone do not have a substantial impact on warfarin-related knowledge"
