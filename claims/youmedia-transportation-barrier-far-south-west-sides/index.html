Kimberly Austin 2011¶

Kimberly Austin, Stacy B. Ehrlich, Cassidy Puckett, and Judi Singleton. (2011). Reimagining Learning, Literacies, and Libraries: A Snapshot of Year 1. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://youmediachicago.org

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Findings from formal and informal interviews with teens during the nine-month observation period. Teens from distant locations reported lacking fare for public transportation, and some participated primarily online because of transportation issues.

"However, some teens, particularly those from the far South and West Sides of Chicago, reported that the distance was a barrier to more frequent YOUmedia visits."
