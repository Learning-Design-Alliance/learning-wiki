## Evidence

### Maayan Pereg 2024

Maayan Pereg, Uri Hertz, Ido Ben-Artzi and Nitzan Shahar. (2024). Disentangling the contribution of individual and social learning processes in human advice-taking behavior. npj Science of Learning 9:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00214-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical Bayesian logistic regression on the two-session student-teacher bandit task (153 participants) comparing coherence rates in revealed vs concealed advice conditions, showing a "causal influence of advice on compliance" with posterior median difference 0.29 (HDI 89% = 0.25 to 0.33); no standardized effect size was printed.

> "participants were more likely to choose the card advised by the teacher when the teacher's advice was revealed (66% coherence rate) compared to when the advice was concealed (48% coherence rate; posterior median estimation for the difference in the population was 0.29"
