## Evidence

### Yu Jian 2025

Yu Jian, Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang. (2025). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of dental residents in managing traumatic dental injuries. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07382-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Knowledge competency section of the survey (five clinically relevant items) among 256 residents. The authors call the splinting-duration gap "a pronounced knowledge gap", since most residents chose 4 weeks or longer.

> "Nearly all residents identified appropriate storage media for an avulsed tooth: 93.4% recognized milk and 90.2% chose placement in the patient's mouth (saliva) as suitable media, and 86.3% identified normal saline."

### Yu Jian 2025 (2)

Yu Jian, Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang. (2025). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of dental residents in managing traumatic dental injuries. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07382-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same knowledge section: 96.5% appreciated the urgency of replantation within a narrow timeframe, though responses were split between immediate and 30 minutes, indicating ambiguity about the optimal window.

> "58.2% of residents correctly responded that replantation should be done ''immediately'', while another 38.3% answered ''within 30 minutes''."
