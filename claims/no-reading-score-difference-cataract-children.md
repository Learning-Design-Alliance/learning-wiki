## Evidence

### Al-Bakri Moug 2023

Al-Bakri Moug, Skovgaard Anne Mette, Bach-Holm Daniella, Larsen Dorte Ancher, Siersma Volkert, Kessel Line. (2023). School performances in children with cataract: results from a population-based cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Pooled analysis of Danish national reading tests (grades 2, 4, 6 and 8). The adjusted mean difference was −0.71 (95% CI −3.21 to 1.79, p=0.576) per table 2; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "We did not find significant differences in pooled test scores between children with cataract and children without cataract in reading (grades 2, 4, 6 and 8 pooled) neither in crude nor adjusted analyses, see table 2."
