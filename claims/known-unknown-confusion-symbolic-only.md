## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2011

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2011). How numbers help students solve physics problems. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview analysis: three students solving the symbolic Tortoise and Hare problem started with d = v₀t and d = ½at² and eliminated d, leaving unknowns a and t, and were surprised the result matched no answer option. In symbolic problems both known and unknown symbols are letters, so the distinction is implicit.

> "These types of errors were not observed in the numeric versions because known and unknown quantities were easily identiﬁed by either using a number or a letter, respectively."
