### Brown J 2025

Brown J, De-Oliveira S, Mitchell C, Cesar RC, Ding L, Fix M, Stemen D, Yacharn K, Wong SF, Dhillon A. (2025). Barriers to and Facilitators of Implementing Team-Based Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation Simulation Study: Exploratory Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/57424

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of presurvey records (27 used for exploratory factor analysis) conducted by the study biostatistician, with a 0.4 loading cutoff and factors chosen by eigenvalue and Scree plot. The article prints the α values 0.686 and 0.717.

> "Using 27 records for exploratory factor analysis, only 1 question, “Q5,” did not reflect the underlying factor. Standardized Cronbach α was 0.686 when using all 6 questions. After excluding Q5, the standardized Cronbach α is 0.717."
