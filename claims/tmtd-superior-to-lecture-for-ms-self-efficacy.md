## Evidence

### Dehghani Ali 2024

Dehghani Ali, Fakhravari Fariba, Hojat Mohsen. (2024). The Effect of the Team Members Teaching Design vs. Regular Lectures method on the Self-efficacy of the Multiple sclerosis Patients in Iran. Randomised Controlled Trial. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the RCT of 48 MS patients. Table 4 reports a mean difference of 4.65 between Lecture - TMTD with p=0.002, supporting "the superiority of self-care training is determined with the TMTD" over lectures.

> "Considering that there is a significant difference between the intervention groups of TMTD and lectures; therefore, the superiority of self-care training is determined with the TMTD over the lecture method"
