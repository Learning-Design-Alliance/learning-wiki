Evidence¶

Jimmy de la Torre and Chia-Yi Chiu 2016¶

Jimmy de la Torre and Chia-Yi Chiu. (2016). A General Method of Empirical Q-matrix Validation. Psychometrika, Volume 81, Issue 2, June 2016. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11336-015-9467-8

q1 · i? · design · r2

The abstract's motivating statement: expert Q-matrix construction "remains a subjective process," and unchecked misspecification carries practical consequences. The article offers this as motivation without printed statistics in the available text.

"Although construction of Q-matrix is typically performed by domain experts, it nonetheless, to a large extent, remains a subjective process, and misspecifications in the Q-matrix, if left unchecked, can have important practical implications."
