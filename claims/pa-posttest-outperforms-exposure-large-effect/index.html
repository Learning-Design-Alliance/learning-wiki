Independent t-test comparing posttest means of treatment (n=36, M=42.64, SD 17.06) and control (n=31, M=6.39, SD 6.01) groups in the quasi-experiment; t=11.920, p=0.001, a statistically significant between-group difference.

"T-test scored 11.92 with a P-value of 0.001 (less than 0.05), as illustrated in Table 5. This clearly shows there is a statistically significant difference between both groups giving evidence that using phonological awareness has a significant effect on the sample's EFL literacy."
