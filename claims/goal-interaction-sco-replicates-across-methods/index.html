Evidence¶

Darnon 2010¶

Darnon, C., Dompnier, B., Gilliéron, O., & Butera, F. (2010). The Interplay of Mastery and Performance Goals in Social Comparison: A Multiple-Goal Perspective. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018161

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The article's two studies converge on the same interaction pattern: Study 1 measured self-set goals and found "the interaction between mastery goals and performance-approach goals", while Study 2 "manipulated goal conditions" and found the mastery effect only under performance-approach conditions. The supplied text prints no effect sizes.

"Results are discussed in terms of the importance of multiple-goal pursuit in academic settings."
