International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19105987

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariate estimates from the conditional LPA model (Table 3, N = 827). Sex and baseline age effects on the five distal outcomes are reported with estimates and standard errors; no standardized effect sizes were printed.

> "Girls had showed significantly higher levels of basic reading skills, reading comprehension, and behavioral engagement compared to boys. Older age at baseline was associated with lower levels of basic reading skills and reading comprehension but with higher levers of behavioral engagement during the school-age years."
