## Subclaims
`q3 i1` On the tumour pathology subscore of the formal examination, intervention students scored 0.47 points higher than controls (p = 0.007), versus 0.17 points higher on other topics. [→ Olde Bekkink M 2011](#olde-bekkink-m-2011)

## Evidence

### Olde Bekkink M 2011

Olde Bekkink M, Donders R, van Muijen GNP, Ruiter DJ. (2011). Challenging medical students with an interim assessment: a positive effect on formal examination score in a randomized controlled study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9291-6

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Mixed-model analysis of the tumour pathology subscore in the same RCT (effect size 0.31 in Table 1) showed "0.47 points higher (p = 0.007)" for intervention students, with a smaller 0.17-point difference on unrelated topics.

> "For the questions in the formal examination related to the topic of tumour pathology, the score amounted to 0.47 points higher (p = 0.007), whereas it was 0.17 points higher for the questions of the other topics on general pathology."
