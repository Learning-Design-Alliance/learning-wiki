## Evidence

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative framing analysis of transcribed episodes from the Uncertain Group F, who were consistently on-topic and obtained beach ball data early, yet never meaningfully discussed causes of the discrepancy. The authors describe "a stable conﬁrmation frame throughout the lab session" despite instructor bids.

> "In contrast to groups that successfully problema- tize, this group remains in a stable conﬁrmation frame throughout the lab session and does not take up the epis- temic agency for which the lab makes space."

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021 (2)

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Transcribed discussion of the Social Group G's beach ball data, which occurred with only 12 minutes left in the session. They acknowledged the data was inconsistent with both models but discussed "the answer" while writing notes, which the authors read as a hoops frame.

> "Taken together, their conversation reﬂects a frame of jumping through hoops to complete the lab in the allotted time."
