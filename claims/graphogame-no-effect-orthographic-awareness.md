## Evidence

### Jere-Folotiya 2014

Jere-Folotiya, J., Chansa-Kabali, T., Munachaka, J. C., Sampa, F., Yalukanda, C., Westerholm, J., Richardson, U., Serpell, R., & Lyytinen, H. (2014). The effect of using a mobile literacy game to improve literacy levels of grade one students in Zambian schools. Education Tech Research Dev, 62, 417-436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-014-9342-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA of orthography posttest with pretest covariate across united and individual intervention groups (df 3,307 and 5,305). Group effects were non-significant; the article prints no effect size for these null findings, so equivalence was not tested.

> "A signiﬁcant effect of playing the game was observed on participants’ decoding skills but not on orthographic awareness, on which limited exposure to the game ( \100 min) cannot be expected to yield any substantial improvements."
