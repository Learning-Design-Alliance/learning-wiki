# Weekly group data on PBL interactions showed substantial between-group variability (ICC 0.4), requiring multilevel growth models

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Groups varied considerably in interaction trajectories, and a multilevel growth model was necessary; the baseline model showed negligible explanatory value. [→ Mohammed Saqr 2026](#mohammed-saqr-2026)

## Evidence

### Mohammed Saqr 2026

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Tiina Törmänen. (2026). A temporal network approach to reveal the longitudinal dynamics of CSCL group regulation and productive collaboration. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09464-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Multilevel modeling setup for the course's weekly interaction data: the random intercept baseline yielded an "ICC was 0.4" and "pseudo-R 2 was 0", with BIC 1556.3 and AIC 1060.7; given the high ICC the article deemed an MLM necessary. Growth models predicted each interaction type from time and pattern.

> "The ICC was 0.4, indicating considerable variability among the groups and time points. The model pseudo-R 2 was 0, the Bayesian information criterion (BIC) was 1556.3, and the Akaike information criterion (AIC) was 1060.7."
