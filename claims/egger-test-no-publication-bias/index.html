Zha 2019¶

Zha, Shenghua; Estes, Michele D.; and Xu, Ling. (2019). A meta-analysis on the effect of duration, task, and training in peer-led learning. Journal of Peer Learning, 12. https://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol12/iss1/2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Bias assessment of the 28 included studies using Egger's regression: the intercept was positive (z = 1.57), the asymmetry was not statistically significant (p = 0.11), and the authors concluded there was no significant evidence of publication bias.

"Hence, we concluded that no significant evidence of publication bias was found from the studies collected for the analysis."
