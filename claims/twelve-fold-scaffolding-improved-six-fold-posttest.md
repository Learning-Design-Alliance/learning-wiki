# Additional scaffolding on a twelve-fold symmetry problem in the tutorial improved interviewed students' performance on the six-fold symmetry posttest problem

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students had difficulty recognizing symmetry and determining the correct number, positions, magnitudes, and signs of image charges. [→ Kashyap 2026](#kashyap-2026)
`q2 i?` After the tutorial added a twelve-fold symmetry problem with hints, interviewed students showed improved performance on the six-fold symmetry problem in Test B as posttest. [→ Kashyap 2026 (2)](#kashyap-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Kashyap 2026

Kashyap, J. S., Devaty, R. P. and Singh, C. (2026). Instructor Framing and Incentives Shape Physics Students' Engagement and Learning Gains from an Inquiry-Based Electrostatics Tutorial on the Method of Images. [doi:10.3390/educsci16040594](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16040594)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview and classroom observations: students recognizing the method's applicability still struggled to determine the correct number, positions, magnitudes, and signs of image charges; several initially claimed two image charges sufficed where three were needed.

> "even when students correctly identified that the method is applicable for a given configuration, many struggled, e.g., to determine the correct number, positions, magnitudes, and signs of the image charges."

### Kashyap 2026 (2)

Kashyap, J. S., Devaty, R. P. and Singh, C. (2026). Instructor Framing and Incentives Shape Physics Students' Engagement and Learning Gains from an Inquiry-Based Electrostatics Tutorial on the Method of Images. [doi:10.3390/educsci16040594](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16040594)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

RQ3 evidence: a twelve-fold symmetry problem (eleven image charges between grounded planes at 30 degrees) with hints was added to the tutorial; interviewed students appeared to benefit and showed improved performance on the six-fold Test B posttest. Qualitative, hedged as 'appeared to benefit'.

> "interviewed students appeared to benefit from the scaffolding in the tutorial with the problem involving 12 -fold symmetry and showed improved performance in the posttest."
