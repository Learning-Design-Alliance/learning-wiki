Evidence¶

Alsamadani 2011¶

Hashem Ahmed Alsamadani. (2011). The Effects of the 3-2-1 Reading Strategy on EFL Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p184

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 85 low-intermediate Saudi EFL students (42 experimental, 43 control), matched on proficiency and taught by the same teacher; pre-test scores used as covariate. The ANCOVA showed a "significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups" with η² = .61 (partial eta squared = .61), a large printed effect size.

"The results of the ANCOVA (as shown in table 2) show that there was a significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups on post-reading test scores, F (1, 82) = 130.4, p = .000, partial eta squared = .61."
