## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an exploratory mediation analysis, the difference in justification quality between the LLM and web search conditions was fully mediated by germane cognitive load: a significant indirect effect (β = 0.15; p = 0.020) with no significant direct effect (β = 0.19; p = 0.095).
