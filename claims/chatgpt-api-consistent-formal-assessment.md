Qualitative analysis of the automated API-based evaluation of laboratory reports from the Experimental Physics I course, in the formal and structural integrity dimension. The article reports the model praised clear titles, well-formulated objectives and methodology sections, and "consistently verifying that reports adhered to the expected structure".

> "These examples illustrate how GPT was able to accurately identify strengths in writing, organization, and formal presentation, consistently verifying that reports adhered to the expected structure of a standard laboratory report."
