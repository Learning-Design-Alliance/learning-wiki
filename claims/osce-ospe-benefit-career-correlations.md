## Evidence

### Alrashed FA 2026

Alrashed FA, Ahmad T, Alsubiheen AM, Alhammad SA, Aldaihan MM, Albishi AM, Hasan Z. (2026). Medical students’ perspectives on the role of OSPE and OSCE in the educational journey and contribution to career development: A cross-sectional study. Medicine 2026;105:3(e47233). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047233

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis (Table 5) in the cross-sectional survey. The article also reports the overall perceived OSPE benefit had "a strong positive correlation with contribution to career development impact score (r = 0.748, P = .000)". These are observational correlations, not causal findings.

> "The overall perceived benefit of OSCEs for medical education and contribution to career development had a small negative correlation with OSPEs benefit score (r = −0.033, P = .48), a significant positive correlation with OSCEs benefit score (r = 0.580, P = .221), and a moderate positive correlation with contribution to career development impact score (r = 0.410, P = .000)"
