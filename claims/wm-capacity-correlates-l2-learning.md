## Evidence

### Ibarra Santacruz 2018

Ibarra Santacruz, D., & Martínez Ortega, D. (2018). Can working memory strategies enhance English vocabulary learning? HOW, 25(2), 29-47. https://doi.org/10.19183/how.25.2.410

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Narrative literature review attributing this pattern to cited studies; the review reports, citing Harrington and Sawyer (1992), that subjects with larger WM capacity scored higher on TOEFL reading measures, and reports positive correlations between WM capacity and translating to and from a new lexicon (Martin and Ellis, 2012).

> "individuals with larger working memory capacity are better able to learn vocabulary (in both first and second languages), write better, and have better foreign language (L2) reading and listening comprehension (Atkins & Baddeley, 1998; Daneman & Hannon, 2007)."
