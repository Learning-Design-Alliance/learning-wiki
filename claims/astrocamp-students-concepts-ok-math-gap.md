Lecturer-reported feedback from ten camp editions teaching Special and General Relativity to selected 15-18 year-old students. The author reports that students "follow conceptua l aspects very well" while difficulties pertain to mathematical background; no quantitative evaluation is printed.

> "Camp students usually follow conceptua l aspects very well (even when ﬁnding some of them counter-intuitive); any diﬃcult ies usually pertain to their mathematical background, which is partially correla ted with their age."
