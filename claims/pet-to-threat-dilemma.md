The paper summarizes Thomas and colleagues' study of five black women from a larger study of faculty women across race: "the socially assigned role of pet was characterized by five themes: tokenism, invisibility, nurtured/overprotected status, conformity/assimilation, and mistreatment". Threat-stage themes were challenging the status quo and microaggressions.
