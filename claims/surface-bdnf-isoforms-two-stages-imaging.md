# Activity induces surface proBDNF shortly after tetanus and surface mBDNF 3 h later, and blocking intracellular but not extracellular cleavage prevents mBDNF appearance at stage II

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within 10 min of l-TBS, proBDNF was the dominant isoform on the neuronal surface, whereas 3 h after l-TBS most surface-bound BDNF was mBDNF. [→ Pang 2016](#pang-2016)
`q2 i?` Pretreatment with the cell-permeable furin inhibitor FIN II, but not the membrane-impermeable PAI-1, prevented cell-surface mBDNF 3 h after stimulation. [→ Pang 2016 (2)](#pang-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Pang 2016

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-permeable immunofluorescence in cultured hippocampal neurons (14 DIV) transfected with HA-tagged BDNF; the majority of surface-bound BDNF measured 3 h after l-TBS was mBDNF.

> "After 10 min of 1-TBS, anti-HA antibody revealed many proBDNF-positive puncta on the surface of neuronal processes, with minimal mBDNF staining (Figure 5b)."

### Pang 2016 (2)

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Neurons pretreated with FIN II or PAI-1, stimulated with l-TBS and stained 3 h later; PAI-1 pretreatment did not significantly change the proBDNF/mBDNF proportion, while FIN II did.

> "Unlike the control condition (Figure 5b), pretreatment with FIN II markedly reduced the amount of mBDNF (green) on the cell surface (Figure 5c). ProBDNF became the dominant isoform secreted at this stage."
