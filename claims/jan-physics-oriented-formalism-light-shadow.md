Eight weeks after the first episode, the same group worked the Light and Shadow tutorial. Jan said she was "just trying to make it like physics- physics-oriented," yet her homework rays stopped at the blocking object, contradicting the understanding she claimed during discussion.

> "She does not attempt to answer the question further and when asked to explain her prediction writes, "I don't know how else to think about it except for the rays from the light bulb.""
