Assessment of ChatGPT-4 in Family Medicine Board Examinations Using Advanced AI Learning and Analytical Methods: Observational Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/56128

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational evaluation of two ChatGPT-4 versions on 300 AAFP Family Medicine Board Review Questions, with responses scored against AAFP answer keys. The Custom Robot achieved "88.67% of the questions" correct and the Regular version 87.33%, both above the printed passing range of 57.7%-61.0%. No effect size is printed.

> "the ChatGPT-4 Custom Robot version correctly answered 88.67% of the questions (95% CI 85.08%-92.25%), while the Regular version achieved a correct response rate of 87.33% (95% CI 83.57%-91.10%)."
