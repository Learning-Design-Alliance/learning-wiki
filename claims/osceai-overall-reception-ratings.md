Overall assessment items from the same anonymized Likert survey (n = 37) of first-year medical students, reported with 95% confidence intervals and visualized in Figures 3F and 3G. The article reports "a rating of 4.62 (95% CI: 4.46 to 4.79) out of 5" and 4.78 for recommendation likelihood.

> "The overall reception of OSCEai as a learning tool received a rating of 4.62 (95% CI: 4.46 to 4.79) out of 5 (Fig. 3F). Furthermore, the likelihood of students recommending the platform was 4.78 (95% CI: 4.62 to 4.94) out of 5 (Fig. 3G)."
