`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

A multilingual-considerations statement in the Test Design section. The report adds that oral language development in both Spanish and English "is essential for reading development and is not merely supportive," and that vocabulary and comprehension should not be interpreted as deficits without considering language experience.

> "Cross-linguistic transfer is skill-specific rather than automatic. Foundational skills such as phonological awareness and print concepts are more likely to transfer across languages, whereas vocabulary and comprehension are more language-dependent and should be interpreted within each language context."
