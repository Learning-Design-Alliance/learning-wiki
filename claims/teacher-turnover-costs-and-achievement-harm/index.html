Podolsky 2016¶

Podolsky, A., Kini, T., Bishop, J., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2016). Solving the Teacher Shortage: How to Attract and Retain Excellent Educators. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/solving-teacher-shortage

q2 · i? · review · r2

A cost-analysis study of district data cited in the report's turnover-costs section. The report states replacement costs "ranged from $4,366 in a small rural district to nearly $18,000 in a large urban district in 2007", about $8.5 billion today adjusted for inflation.

"replacement costs for teachers ranged from $4,366 in a small rural district to nearly $18,000 in a large urban district in 2007—at a national price tag of $7.3 billion a year."

Podolsky 2016 (2)¶

Podolsky, A., Kini, T., Bishop, J., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2016). Solving the Teacher Shortage: How to Attract and Retain Excellent Educators. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/solving-teacher-shortage

q2 · i? · review · r2

An eight-year study of 850,000 New York City 4th- and 5th-grade students cited in the report. It found turnover's negative effects "reached the students of 'stayers'" and new teachers alike; no effect size is printed.

"A recent study of 850,000 New York City 4th- and 5th-grade students over an eight-year period found that teacher turnover has a significant negative effect on student achievement, particularly in schools serving large populations of low-performing students and students of color."
