`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Known-groups validity testing in the 434-participant survey: participants were stratified into tertiles by CPBS scores using 33rd and 67th percentiles and compared with Kruskal-Wallis tests and Dunn's post-hoc comparisons. The article interprets this as objective knowledge being "less directly related to clinical practice behaviors."

> "the high-practice group demonstrated significantly higher SCS scores than both the middle and low-practice groups (all p < 0.001). However, OKTO scores showed only modest differences across CPBS groups (p = 0.017)"
