## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong 2024

Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong, Kantiwong Anupong. (2024). Development and validation of immediate self-feedback very short answer questions for medical students: practical implementation of generalizability theory to estimate reliability in formative examination designs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05569-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-facet fully crossed random-effects (P×I×O) generalizability analysis of 94 students, 10 items, 2 occasions, verified with EduG software. "The percentage of variance attributable to the universe score, students (P), is 16.47%" and residual variance was 55.38%. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The percentage of variance attributable to the universe score, students (P), is 16.47%, and items (I) account for 9.98% of the total variance. The percentage of variance in the interaction between students and items is 10.98%, while occasions (O) contribute only 2.10%, with a higher contribution from the residuals (55.38%)."
