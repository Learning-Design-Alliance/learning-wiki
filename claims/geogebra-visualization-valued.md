## Evidence

### Korner 2025

Korner, M., & Likos, C. N. (2025). On the design of a profession-oriented course on Theoretical Mechanics for physics education students. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.10102)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student feedback from the interviews and open-ended evaluation questions on the GeoGebra applications visualizing conic sections and a satellite/planet trajectory (Figures 4 and 5); the article reports everyone but a single student was familiar with the tool from school.
