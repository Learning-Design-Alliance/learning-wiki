## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2016

Yilmaz, E. O., & Yurdugul, H. (2016). Design and effects of a concept focused discussion environment in e-learning. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 353-374. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.20

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive frequency analysis of contribution styles (initiating, instant, insistent, only reading) from Table 3; totals were 455 messages in CFDE and 428 in TDE, with only three CFDE and four TDE students only reading. No statistical test is reported for these style differences.

> "learners using TDE are prone to send instant messages, but learners using CFDE are more likely to discuss instead of sending instant messages."
