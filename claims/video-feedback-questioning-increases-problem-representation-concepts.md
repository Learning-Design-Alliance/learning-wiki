# A video-feedback and questioning programme increases total and regulatory concepts in intermediate tennis players' problem representation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At post-test, the experimental group showed a significant multivariate effect on Concept Content-total variables in problem representation, with significantly higher total concepts (p <.01) and total regulatory concepts (p <.05) than the control group. [→ García-González Luis 2013](#garcia-gonzalez-luis-2013)

## Evidence

### García-González Luis 2013

García-González Luis, Moreno M. Perla, Moreno Alberto, Gil Alexander, del Villar Fernando. (2013). Effectiveness of a Video-Feedback and Questioning Programme to Develop Cognitive Expertise in Sport. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0082270

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental 2x2 Mixed MANOVA (rank-based Puri & Sen procedure) on 11 male Under-14 tennis players (experimental n=5, control n=6). Post-test problem representation showed "Total of concepts (p <.01) and Total regulatory concepts (p <.05)" higher in the experimental group; no effect size printed.

> "in post-test, there was a significant main effect for Group in Concept Content-total variables interaction (L(3) =8.74; p <.05). Univariate follow-up analyses of Concept Content-total variables in post-test (Table 4) show significant differences between experimental and control group in Total of concepts (p <.01) and Total regulatory concepts (p <.05)"
