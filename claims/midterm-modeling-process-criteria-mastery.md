## Evidence

### Hoskinson Anne-Marie 2010

Hoskinson Anne-Marie. (2010). How to Build a Course in Mathematical–Biological Modeling: Content and Processes for Knowledge and Skill. CBE-Life Sciences Education 9(3):333–341. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-03-0041

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Individual midterm exam question administered in each of four semesters asked students to define, prioritize, and explain criteria for evaluating a model. "More than 96% of students in each semester" included the four core process elements among their top six criteria.

> "More than 96% of students in each semester chose 1) model objective, 2) variables, 3) assumptions, and 4) sensitivity analysis among their top six criteria."
