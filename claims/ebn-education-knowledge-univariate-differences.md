## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Univariate t tests showed higher EBNCRS scores among nurses with EBN education/training and with perceived EBN knowledge. [→ Fu Liang 2020](#fu-liang-2020)

## Evidence

### Fu Liang 2020

Fu Liang, Wei Su, Xianghong Ye, Ming Li, Jun Shen, Chen Chen, Qi Guo, Liping Ye and Yuhong He. (2020). Evidence-Based Practice Competency and Related Factors Among Nurses Working in Public Hospitals. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/0046958020927876

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t tests in the 1036-nurse cross-sectional survey. Nurses with EBN education/training and perceived EBN knowledge "had higher score of EBNCRS" (t = −5.579 and t = −6.383, both P < .001); no effect size is printed.

> "Nurses who had received EBN education/training and with perceived EBN knowledge had higher score of EBNCRS than nurses who reported no EBN education/training and knowledge (t = −5.579, P < .001; t = −6.383, P < .001)."
