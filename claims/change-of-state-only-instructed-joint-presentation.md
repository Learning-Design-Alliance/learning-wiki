## Evidence

### Mason 2016

Lucia Mason & Maria Caterina Tornatora (2016). Analogical encoding with and without instructions for case comparison of scientific phenomena. Educational Psychology, 36(2), 391-412. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2014.953038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized experiment, second phenomenon (change of state). The MANOVA main effect of condition was significant (Wilks' λ = .92, F(10, 646) = 2.50, p = .006); only the "JP + I condition" outperformed SP on alignable differences.

> "Multiple comparison tests revealed that only learners in the JP + I condition were able to list more alignable differences (p = .001, d = .51) than learners in the SP condition. Learners in the JP condition did not differentiate from those in the other two conditions."
