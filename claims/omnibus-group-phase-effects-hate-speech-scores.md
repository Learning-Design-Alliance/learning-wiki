# Omnibus tests found significant group, phase and group-by-phase effects on questions 1 and 2, and predominantly group effects on questions 3 and 4

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For question 1 and question 2 the group factor, the phase factor and their interaction were all statistically significant; for question 3 only group was significant, and for question 4 group and the group-by-phase interaction were significant while phase alone was not. [→ Tatiana Íñiguez-Berrozpe 2025](#tatiana-iniguez-berrozpe-2025)

## Evidence

### Tatiana Íñiguez-Berrozpe 2025

Learning to deal with hate speech: An online collective intelligence experiment on the Collective Learning platform. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fixed-value omnibus tests (Table 2) on mean scores per group, question and phase in the Collective Learning experiment. For question 2 the same pattern held (group p = 0.011, phase p = 0.003, together p < 0.001); only test statistics are printed, "no effect size" is given.

> "For question 1, we found significant effects stemming from the independent variables “group” (p < 0.001) and “phase” (p < 0.001), as well as from the two taken together (p < 0.001)."
