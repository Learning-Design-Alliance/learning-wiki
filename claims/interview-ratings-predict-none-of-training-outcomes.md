## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across univariate tests and hierarchical regressions for 274 trainees, adding interview ratings never produced a significant change in R² or deviance (e.g. exam 1: F2,139 = 2.9, p = .056; case reports: χ2(2) = 2.1, p = .4). The 12 trainees with placement concerns showed no significant interview rating differences.

> "Referee ratings predicted only one of the seven outcomes examined, and interview ratings predicted none of the outcomes."
