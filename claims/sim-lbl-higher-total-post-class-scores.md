### Li Jialan 2025

Li Jialan, Wu Ningling, Lu Jianyun, Tong Xiaoliang, Gao Lihua, Zeng Jinrong. (2025). Implementation and evaluation of a Sim + LBL hybrid teaching model in dermatology and venereology: a prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08440-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study of 346 undergraduates: the 2019 cohort (n=182) received Sim + LBL and the 2018 cohort (n=164) received LBL for the same STD module. The article reports a "median difference = + 6.0" with δ = − 0.39, a medium effect; no standardized effect size beyond Cliff's δ is printed.

> "The Sim + LBL group achieved higher post-class scores than those in the LBL group (median difference = + 6.0, 95% CI [4.0–8.0], δ = − 0.39, medium effect)."
