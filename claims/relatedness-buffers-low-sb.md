`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup probit regressions after a median split on relatedness, reported in the findings section and Fig. 4. The authors describe a "buffering mediation pattern" in which strong peer or mentor connections stabilize persistence even when belonging varies.

> "Among students with high levels of Relatedness, IP remains consistently high across the entire range of Sense of Belonging (SB), as indicated by the near-flat slope (0.024)."
