Alghamdi Amal A., Alyousif Ghada F., AlQarni Amani M., Amer Fatma H., Alfadhel Taghreed O., Almutairi Rawan N., Almutairi Shatha M., Almutairi Anwar D., Hakami Nouf A., Al Ghamdi Kholoud. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive self-report results from the national cross-sectional survey of 1371 medical students on subject understanding. The article reports "79.72% (n = 1,093)" for recorded lectures and "n = 1,037, 75.64%" for multimedia such as educational videos and animated pictures; these are descriptive proportions with no inferential test.

> "79.72% (n = 1,093) of the students reported that the recorded lecture material enhanced their understanding of the subject. Moreover, three-quarters (n = 1,037, 75.64%) of the participants found that the use of multimedia (e.g. educational videos, animated pictures, and audios clips) enhanced their understanding"
