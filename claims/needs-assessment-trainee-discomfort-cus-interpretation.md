## Evidence

### Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean 2020

Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean. (2020). Impact of a web-based module on trainees’ ability to interpret neonatal cranial ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02400-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Needs assessment questionnaire of 12 Likert-scale questions plus one open-ended item, distributed online via RedCap to all 62 pediatrics residents and NPM fellows in 2016–2017; content validity reviewed by two neonatologists and one radiologist; Cronbach's alpha 0.71. No effect size is printed.

> "The survey results showed that the majority of trainees were not entirely comfortable with their neonatal CUS interpretation skills. Also, they see these skills as vital parts of their training and, therefore worthy of greater attention"
