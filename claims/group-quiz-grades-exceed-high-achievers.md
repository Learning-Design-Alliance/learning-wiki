# In paired individual-then-group quizzes, group quiz grades exceeded the individual grades of term high achievers, and matched the daily top performer's grades

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Group quiz grades (90±3%) were significantly higher than term high achievers' individual grades (80±4%) and all individual grades (68±3%). [→ Gaudet Andrew D. 2010](#gaudet-andrew-d-2010)

## Evidence

### Gaudet Andrew D. 2010

Gaudet Andrew D., Ramer Leanne M., Nakonechny Joanne, Cragg Jacquelyn J., Ramer Matt S. (2010). Small-Group Learning in an Upper-Level University Biology Class Enhances Academic Performance and Student Attitudes Toward Group Work. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-course analysis of 13 paired individual-then-group quizzes in the 2008 Refined SGL section, comparing group grades with individual grades using paired t tests (p<0.05 reported, no effect size). Group grades exceeded even term high achievers' individual grades.

> "Interestingly, we found even the term high achievers were out-performed by their groups on quizzes (all individuals: 68±3%; term high achievers: 80±4%; group: 90±3%)."

### Gaudet Andrew D. 2010 (2)

Gaudet Andrew D., Ramer Leanne M., Nakonechny Joanne, Cragg Jacquelyn J., Ramer Matt S. (2010). Small-Group Learning in an Upper-Level University Biology Class Enhances Academic Performance and Student Attitudes Toward Group Work. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison (n=8 groups) of group quiz grades with the daily top performer's individual grade across the same 13 paired quizzes, analyzed with paired t tests; the article reports no significant difference and equivalence was not tested.

> "Average group quiz grades were not significantly different from the individual grades of the daily top-performing student on each quiz (right panel)."
