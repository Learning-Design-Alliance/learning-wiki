## Evidence

### Kana'an et al. 2014

Basim Hamdan Ibrahim Kana'an, Salahud Din Abdul Rab & Ahlullah Siddiqui. (2014). The Effect of Expansion of Vision Span on Reading Speed: A Case Study of EFL Major Students at King Khalid University. English Language Teaching, 7(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test (SPSS version 20) on the trained test group's pre- and post-training reading speed, reported in Table 6 (Z = -3.485, Asymp. Sig. .000). The article reports "a highly significant difference between the pre-test and post-test reading speed results of the test group in favor of the post-test results"; no effect size is printed.

> "Table 6 shows that the reading speed of 6 out of 25 students is reduced, whereas the speed of 19 students increased after the training. The data of the table also proves hypothesis 6 because there is a highly significant difference between the pre-test and post-test reading speed results of the test group in favor of the post-test results."
