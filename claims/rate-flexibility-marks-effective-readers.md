## Subclaims
`q1 i?` On a four-part timed test involving change of set, pupils whose rates were nearly equal across parts were less effective readers than those who varied rate to suit material type. [→ Zirbes 1919](#zirbes-1919)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The author 'devised' a four-part test (simple story; nonsimple sentences with easy words; easy rhyme with repetition; a problem), each part timed separately; some pupils' rates across the four parts were nearly equal.

> "The more effective readers judged by other comprehension and reproduction tests were those who varied their rate to suit the type of material."
