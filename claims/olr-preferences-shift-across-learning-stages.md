## Evidence

### Wang Yizhen 2026

Wang Yizhen, Zhang Xiaomeng, Wang Chao, Xing Xiaoming, Nayak Vrinda. (2026). Online learning resource preferences across learning stages: a cognitive load theory perspective in UK medical students in early years. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09694-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 139 Year 1–2 medical undergraduates at the University of Exeter using Likert-scale preference ratings (1–4) analysed with repeated-measures ANOVA. The abstract reports that "interactive videos (3.16 ± 0.86, out of 4)" scored highest pre-class, "optional practice questions (3.65 ± 0.71, out of 4)" post-class, and "online mock exams (3.89 ± 0.38, out of 4)" pre-exam.

> "The highest-scoring OLRs were interactive videos (3.16 ± 0.86, out of 4) in the pre-class stage, optional practice questions (3.65 ± 0.71, out of 4) in the post-class stage, and online mock exams (3.89 ± 0.38, out of 4) in the pre-exam stage."

### Wang Yizhen 2026 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results section of the same cross-sectional survey: within the post-class revision stage, "participants preferred optional practice questions (3.65 ± 0.71)" with slides second at 3.32 ± 0.80, based on 4-point preference ratings analysed by repeated-measures ANOVA.

> "During the post-class revision stage (Table 1), participants preferred optional practice questions (3.65 ± 0.71) and slides (3.32 ± 0.80)."
