`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Mu et al. (2026), a dual-pathway model in which "capability was associated with greater perceived cognitive facilitation and lower integrity-related risk" while frequency showed weaker, less stable associations. The review draws on this to argue responsible use should be conceptualized through competence, exposure, purpose, and quality.

> "A dual-pathway account distinguishes capability-oriented familiarity from exposure-oriented frequency: capability was associated with greater perceived cognitive facilitation and lower integrity-related risk, whereas frequency showed weaker and less stable associations"
