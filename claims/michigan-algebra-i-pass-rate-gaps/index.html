q2 · i? · review · r1

Civil Rights Data Collection Algebra I pass rates for Michigan 9th and 10th graders (2021-22); the chart prints Black 64.4%, students with disabilities 66.9%, Latino 71.3%, English Learners 71.8%, white 78.2%, all students 74.1%. White students were "the sole group to surpass the state average."

"in the 2021-22 school year, only 64% of Black students passed Algebra I by 10th grade, the lowest rate among all reported groups and well below the statewide average pass rate of 74%."
