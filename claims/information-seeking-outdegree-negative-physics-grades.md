## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Out-degree, degree, gatekeeper and eigenvector centrality in the information-seeking network each showed a negative effect on physics grades. [→ J. Pulgar 2019](#j-pulgar-2019)

## Evidence

### J. Pulgar 2019

J. Pulgar, Cristian Candia, P. Leonardi. (2019). Undergrad classroom cooperation and academic performance: Beneficial for real-world-like problems but detrimental for algebra-based problems. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.06923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.06923)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS multiple regression analysis of 67 students' physics grades on network centrality measures from an information-seeking peer-nomination survey, controlling for UST scores, school type, major, city and gender. The article reports negative coefficients for log(out-degree), gatekeeper, log(degree) and eigenvector, with the log(degree) effect statistically significant.

> "In general, having a high number of social ties, either incoming or outgoing, shows to be negatively related to physics grades, as seen on model D for log(degree)."
