### Alali Faisal Mehsen 2025

Alali Faisal Mehsen, Nassani Mohammad Zakaria, Tarakji Bassel, Raqe Alqhtani Nasser, Robaian Ali, Saad Alqahtahni Abdullah, Alhedyan Faisal S., bin nabhan Abdullah, Alenzi Adel, Ayidh Alqahtani Khalid, Alrafedah Ali, Alagla Maram, Abuelqomsan Mohammed A. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07698-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based questionnaire (CHERRIES-guided, adapted from a validated instrument, piloted) of 506 senior dental students and interns scored with the adapted Eticha and Gemeda system; the article reports that "Only 24.2% of participants demonstrated a good level of knowledge" on the 9 oral-complication items. No effect size is printed for this proportion.

> "Only 24.2% of participants demonstrated a good level of knowledge by correctly answering at least 6 out of the 9 questions presented."
