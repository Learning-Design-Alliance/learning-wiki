### Whipple 1914

Whipple, G. M., & Curtis, J. N. (1914). Preliminary Investigation of Skimming in Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=preliminary+investigation+of+skimming+in+reading

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Main experiments page-by-page comparison (Table 7) for the eight passages running over two pages. The article attributes the quickening in part to warming up and in part to increased ease of skipping as context piled up.

> "By comparing the speed of skimming by pages, we find that it runs approximately 0.19 sec. per word for the first page, 0.17 sec. per word for the second page and 0.15 sec. per word in the third page"
