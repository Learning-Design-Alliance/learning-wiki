### Sascha Schneider 2021

Sascha Schneider, Maik Beege, Steve Nebel, Lenka Schnaubert, Günter Daniel Rey. (2021). The Cognitive-Affective-Social Theory of Learning in digital Environments (CASTLE). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09626-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the politeness effect, citing several studies, and explains it via Brown and Levinson's politeness theory: people strive to save face in interaction even with digital environments. Second-hand report; no effect sizes printed.

> "This effect describes that learners learn better with polite instructions (e.g., Mikheeva et al. 2019; Schneider et al. 2015b) or polite feedback (e.g., McLaren et al. 2011a, 2011b) rather than with direct language forms."
