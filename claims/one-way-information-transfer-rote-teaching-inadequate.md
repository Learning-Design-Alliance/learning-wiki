### Stanisław D. Głażek 2008

Stanisław D. Głażek. (2008). Heuristic model of teaching. https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4796 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical derivation within the MOT (Section III.C): the model pictures ineffective teaching as supplying the student with a sequence of coordinates valid in the teacher's frame without establishing the student's frame, described as "fundamentally inadequate"; the article equates this with rote memorization. No empirical test is reported.

> "Thus, the model makes it obvious that the process of ineﬀective teaching, based on transferring information only one way, is fundamentally inadequate."
