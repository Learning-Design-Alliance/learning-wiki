## Evidence

### M. B. Weissman 2021

M. B. Weissman. (2021). On Policy Recommendations from Causal Inference in Physics Education Research. arXiv preprint, in press in Phys. Rev. Phys. Educ. Res. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020118)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical demonstration using Markov equivalence: even fixing Identity as child of all but OCSE, 24 DAGs arise from permuting the other four variables in the saturated graph, and two reversed-OCSE-arrow members yield a zero causal impact.

> "Two of the equivalent DAGs would have reversed directions of all three arrows from OCSE. For those equivalent graphs "the impact of OCSE activities on physics identity" would be identically zero."

### M. B. Weissman 2021 (2)

M. B. Weissman. (2021). On Policy Recommendations from Causal Inference in Physics Education Research. arXiv preprint, in press in Phys. Rev. Phys. Educ. Res. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020118)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The author argues the Lock et al. graph was inherited from prior work that itself cautioned its 'effect' wording "cannot imply causality in a strict sense" and explored no equivalent graphs.

> "The graph choice could not have been based on some standard trade-off between fit quality and number of parameters, e.g. the Akaike Information Criterion(21), because elementary DAG rules(1) say that reversing the arrow from Competence to OCSE gives a graph in the same equivalence class."
