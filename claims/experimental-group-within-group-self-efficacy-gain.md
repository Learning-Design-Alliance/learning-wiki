### Onder Sensoy 2018

Onder Sensoy, Halil Ibrahim Yildirim. (2018). Impact of Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge Based Education Applications on Prospective Teachers' Self-Efficacy Belief Levels Toward Science Education. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i10.3433

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-factor repeated-measures ANOVA for the 32 experimental-group students; means rose from 111.813 at pretest to 121.719 at posttest and 119.094 at follow-up. The article reports the pretest contrasts "were significant".

> "it is seen that the difference of the points between pretest, posttest; pretest and measurement of change were significant (F(2-62)=13.077; p<.05). It is observed that there is no significant difference between post -test and measurement of change."
