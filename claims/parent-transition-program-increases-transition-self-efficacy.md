# A parent-focused transition program increases parental self-efficacy to manage the transition to primary school

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Parents who participated in the transition program reported significantly higher self-efficacy to manage the transition to school than control parents (d = 0.20). [→ Giallo 2010](#giallo-2010)

## Evidence

### Giallo 2010

Giallo, R., Treyvaud, K., Matthews, J., & Kienhuis, M. (2010). Making the Transition to Primary School: An Evaluation of a Transition Program for Parents. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 10, 1-17. https://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A cluster-randomized controlled trial of 21 primary schools in Victoria, Australia, comparing the AusParenting transition program to routine transition practices. MANCOVA with pretest covariate showed the intervention group scored higher on transition self-efficacy, with a small effect size (d = 0.20).

> "Follow-up univariate results revealed that at post-test the intervention condition had significantly higher Efficacy subscale scores than the control condition, F(1, 387)=9.97, p=.002, d=0.20, 95% CI (-0.02, 0.42)."
