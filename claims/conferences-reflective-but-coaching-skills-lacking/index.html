q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative findings from principal interviews, focus groups, and case studies in eight pilot elementary schools. Participants said conferences were "more reflective and objective than in the past," but the study found "many principals lack the instructional coaching skills required to have deep discussions about teaching practice."

"Principals and teachers said that conferences were more reflective and objective than in the past and were focused on instructional practice and improvement. However, many principals lack the instructional coaching skills required to have deep discussions about teaching practice."
