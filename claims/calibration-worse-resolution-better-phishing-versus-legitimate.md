### Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis 2019

Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis. (2019). Better beware: comparing metacognition for phishing and legitimate emails. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09197-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired comparison within the pooled sample of 250 participants across both experiments, from the Results section. The paired t-test contrasted calibration scores computed separately for the 19 phishing and 19 legitimate emails each participant judged.

> "Calibration was much worse for phishing emails ( M = 0.18) than for legitimate ones ( M =0 . 0 9 ) , p a i r e d t - t e s t ,t(249) = 6.65, p < .001."
