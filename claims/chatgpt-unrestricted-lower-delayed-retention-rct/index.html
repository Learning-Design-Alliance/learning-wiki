q2 · i2 · causal · r?

The review attributes to Barcaui's (2025) randomized controlled trial, with 120 undergraduates studying unfamiliar material, that "The ChatGPT condition scored lower on retention (57.5% vs. 68.5%; t(83) = −3.19, p = 0.002, Cohen’s d = 0.68) despite shorter study time."
