q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic synthesis of qualitative extracts. Teachers checked closely on learners "to ensure they are on task", used strategic seating to "minimize distractions", gave immediate feedback such as "good" or "try again", and used reinforcers including verbal praise, preferred activities, and rewards like stickers and sweets.

"A set of individualized strategies used by teachers in inclusive education involved providing learners with support and feedback targeted to their needs during teaching and tasks."
