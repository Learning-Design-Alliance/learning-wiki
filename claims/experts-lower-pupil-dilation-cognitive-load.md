Pupil dilation, used as a proxy for cognitive load, was measured with eye-tracking glasses during the nine-ECG pilot task. Experts showed smaller dilation changes than residents (4.83% ± 2.01% vs. 10.52% ± 4.18%; p = 0.015), with experts showing minimal fluctuation even on complex cases.

> "Experts displayed lower changes in pupil dilation (mean 4.83% ± 2.01% vs. 10.52% ± 4.18% for residents; p = 0.015), indicating reduced cognitive strain."
