Key findings summary of the DCPS principal-replacement analysis. The brief states math achievement "followed a similar trend, although the gains were smaller" than the 4 percentile-point reading gain; no separate math effect size is printed for the overall average.

"New principals boosted the average student's achievement in reading by 4 percentile points. Achievement in math followed a similar trend, although the gains were smaller."
