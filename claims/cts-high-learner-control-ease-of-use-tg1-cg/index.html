Evidence¶

Wambsganss 2025¶

Wambsganss, T., Benke, I., Maedche, A., Koedinger, K., & Käser, T. (2025). Evaluating the Impact of Learner Control and Interactivity in Conversational Tutoring Systems for Persuasive Writing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00409-x

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Laboratory experiment with 96 students randomized to TG1 (n=32) or CG (n=32). Welch's t-test on ease-of-use: "mean value of 5.552 (SD = 1.272)" vs "mean = 4.833, SD = 1.071, p = 0.017*", d = 0.61.

"The perceived ease-of-use of WritingTutor was rated with a mean value of 5.552 (SD = 1.272). The value is significantly higher than the results of the alternative static tutoring approach (mean = 4.833, SD = 1.071, p = 0.017*) according to a two-tailed Welch's t-test."
