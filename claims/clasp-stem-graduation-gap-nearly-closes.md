## Evidence

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical logistic regression (Eq. 4) with a weighted random control group of 39,074 NU students, grouped by entry term; all demographic groups show positive extra CLASP graduates per year (Table V), with "a higher graduation proba- bility if they took CLASP".

> "Even after controlling for university-wide changes in graduation rates, each demo- graphic group in Fig. 4 has a higher graduation proba- bility if they took CLASP than that group had after a NonCLASP class."

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (2)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-difference style comparison of HM versus NonHM graduation probabilities from the same regression; the article calls this evidence that CLASP "nearly closes the previously existing graduation gap".

> "This gap is estimated to be 7.3% ± 4% for the control students, 6.0% ± 2.3% for the NonCLASP students, but only 1.0% ± 3.4% for the CLASP students."

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (3)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of overall (any-major) graduation rates against Fig. 4 STEM-major graduation rates shows the increase is "almost totally due to more CLASP students from marginalized groups choosing to stay in a STEM major".

> "there were 359 students from marginalized groups in CLASP classes so from a comparison of their CLASP graduation (in any major) rate of 0.853 with the same ethnicities' NonCLASP graduation rate estimated to be 0.791 we see that 22 more students from marginalized groups graduated"
