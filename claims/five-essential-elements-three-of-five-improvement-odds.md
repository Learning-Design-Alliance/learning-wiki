# Elementary schools strong on at least three of five essential organizational elements were 10 times more likely to improve and 30 times less likely to stagnate

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The report attributes this finding to prior CCSR research examining 100 elementary schools that made significant progress over seven years and 100 that did not. It is reported second-hand as supporting context; the underlying study's design is not described in this report.
