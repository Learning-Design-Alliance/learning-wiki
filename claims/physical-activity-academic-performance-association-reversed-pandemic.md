# The association between physical activity and academic performance in Spanish adolescents reversed from positive before the Covid-19 pandemic to negative 1-year into it

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Physical activity was positively associated with academic performance before the Covid-19 pandemic (β = 0.24) but negatively associated 1-year into it (β = − 0.26), with a statistically significant difference in slopes (interaction β = − 0.40). ## Evidence

### Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel 2022

Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel, Sánchez-Oliva David, Sevil-Serrano Javier, Marques Adilson, Sánchez-Miguel Pedro Antonio. (2022). 24-h movement behaviours in Spanish youth before and after 1-year into the covid-19 pandemic and its relationship to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-21096-5

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mixed models with time, predictor, and time*predictor interaction, adjusting for age, sex, and BMI, compared the physical activity slope before versus 1-year after the pandemic. The article reports that "the association became negative (β = 0.24 + ( − 0.40) = − 0.26; p < 0.001)" with statistically significant slope differences.

> "However, the association became negative (β = 0.24 + ( − 0.40) = − 0.26; p < 0.001) among adolescents evaluated 1-year after Covid-19, that is, physical activity was negatively associated with academic performance, and differences in slopes were statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
