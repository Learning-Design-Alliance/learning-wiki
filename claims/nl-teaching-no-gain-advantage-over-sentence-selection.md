## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Gain scores did not differ significantly between the natural language condition (M = 5.33, SD = 3.34) and the sentence-selection condition (M = 5.61, SD = 3.74). [→ Rachel Love 2025](#rachel-love-2025)

## Evidence

### Rachel Love 2025

Rachel Love, Edith Law, Philip R. Cohen, Dana Kulić. (2025). Teaching a Conversational Agent using Natural Language: Effect on Learning and Engagement. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00461-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects user study (46 adults, counterbalanced order) comparing teaching modalities in the Curiosity Notebook on rock classification. A two-way repeated measures ANOVA found "no signiﬁcant difference in gain scores between conditions F(1, 39) = 0.925, p = .342". Hypothesis H1 was not supported.

> "The results showed that there was no signiﬁcant difference in gain scores between conditions F(1, 39) = 0.925, p = .342. Participants in the sentence-selection con- dition gained slightly more, M = 5.61 (SD = 3.74) points, compared to the natural language condition, M = 5.33(SD = 3.34) points."
