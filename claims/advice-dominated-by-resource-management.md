## Evidence

### Päuler-Kuppinger and Jucks 2018

Lena Päuler-Kuppinger & Regina Jucks. (2018). Out-of-Class Instruction in Higher Education – Impact of Approaches to Teaching and Discipline. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive content analysis of the 70 academics' email answers. The article reports resource management at 34% of advice, time management at 11% of all advice, and surface plus deep strategies together at less than 30% of advice.

> "Within the advice, academics most often mentioned strategies that referred to resource management (34% of mentioned pieces of advice)."
