`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Mini meta-analysis (following Goh et al., 2016) pooling the program's experimental studies contrasting prompted with unsupported journal writing, based on data of 238 participants. It showed "a medium-to-large effect size in favor of instructionally supported journal writing by prompts versus unsupported journal writing, Hedges ’s g of 0.78".
