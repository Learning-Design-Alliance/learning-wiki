Evidence¶

Berhenke 2011¶

Berhenke, Amanda; Miller, Alison L.; Brown, Eleanor; Seifer, Ronald; Dickstein, Susan. (2011). Observed Emotional and Behavioral Indicators of Motivation Predict School Readiness in Head Start Graduates. Early childhood research quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.04.001

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression of observed motivation indicators on teacher-rated learning-related behaviors in 131 kindergarteners; the article reports "Shame expressions predicted greater social competence and work-related skills."

"Shame expressions predicted greater social competence and work-related skills."

Berhenke 2011 (2)¶

Berhenke, Amanda; Miller, Alison L.; Brown, Eleanor; Seifer, Ronald; Dickstein, Susan. (2011). Observed Emotional and Behavioral Indicators of Motivation Predict School Readiness in Head Start Graduates. Early childhood research quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.04.001

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Regression on teacher-rated academic competence; the article notes emotion expressions as a group did not add significant predictive power, yet "more shame expressions predicted greater math and reading skills."

"While emotion expressions as a group did not increase the predictive power of the model at the p < .05 level, more shame expressions predicted greater math and reading skills."
