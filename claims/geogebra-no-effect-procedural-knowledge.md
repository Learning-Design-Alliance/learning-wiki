## Evidence

### Ocal 2017

Mehmet Fatih Ocal. (2017). The Effect of Geogebra on Students’ Conceptual and Procedural Knowledge: The Case of Applications of Derivative. Higher Education Studies, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n2p67

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on post-test procedural knowledge questions of the ADT in the same true experimental design found no significant group difference; equivalence was not tested. The author notes the experimental group mean was still one point higher.

> "there was no statistically significant mean difference for the scores gathered from the procedural knowledge questions for Geogebra (M=7.47, SD=3.899) and control group students (M=6.44, SD=2.965); t(53)=1.075, p=.287"
