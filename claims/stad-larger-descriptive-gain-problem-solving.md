Descriptive statistics from Table 2 show the STAD group's problem solving mean rose from 21.240 to 33.960 while the PBL group's rose from 20.400 to 24.360. The authors describe this as a huge difference in scores between the two teaching methods.

> "A huge difference in scores w as observed between two teaching methods and hence it can be concluded that STAD model of cooperative learning have high impact in improving problem solving ability of students."
