Burr Steven A, Brodier Elizabeth, Wilkinson Simon. (2013). Delivery and use of individualised feedback in large class medical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-63

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attitude survey across three year groups exploring reasons for not revisiting unachieved objectives (Figure 5, stacked bar chart, N = 44). Learners stated the main factors were that "the module had finished, a lack of time, and laziness".

> "Possible barriers hindering the use of the blueprinted report were explored, with learners stating that the main factors influencing their use of the feedback are that the module had finished, a lack of time, and laziness (Figure 5)."
