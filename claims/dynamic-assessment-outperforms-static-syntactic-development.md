## Evidence

### Jafary et al. 2012

Mohamad Reza Jafary, Noreen Nordin & Reza Mohajeri. (2012). The Effect of Dynamic versus Static Assessment on Syntactic Development of Iranian College Preparatory EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 5(7). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n7p149

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 60 male Iranian college-preparatory learners randomly assigned to experimental and control groups; t-test on post-test scores. The experimental group mean (17.73) exceeded the control group mean (13.83), and "null-hypothesis of this study was rejected at 0.99% of confidence level."

> "Table 7 reveals through the application of t-test, with 58 degree of freedom at 0.01% of significance. And the mean of experimental group (17.73) was more than the mean of control group (13.83)."
