## Evidence

### Jafary et al. 2012

Mohamad Reza Jafary, Noreen Nordin & Reza Mohajeri. (2012). The Effect of Dynamic versus Static Assessment on Syntactic Development of Iranian College Preparatory EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 5(7). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n7p149

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-group experiment with 60 male Iranian college-preparatory learners (aged 17-19) assigned to the experimental and control groups by numbering them 1 to 60 and putting odd numbers in one group and even in the other, which the authors call random assignment; the control group received deductive grammar rules over twelve sessions. The post-test means (Table 6, 30 per group) were 17.73 (SD 2.29) for the experimental group and 13.83 (SD 2.87) for the control group, and the text reports a t-test with 58 degrees of freedom by which "null-hypothesis of this study was rejected at 0.99% of confidence level." The tables are disordered: the matching test (t = 5.948, df = 58, p printed as .000, mean difference 3.90) appears in a table captioned as descriptive statistics for the pilot study (Table 5), Table 7, which the text cites for it, lists statistics for twelve tests with 20 students each, and Table 6 prints a control minimum (19) above its maximum (10). No effect size is printed.

> "Table 7 reveals through the application of t-test, with 58 degree of freedom at 0.01% of significance. And the mean of experimental group (17.73) was more than the mean of control group (13.83)."
