# Error Management framing during BLS training yields compression-depth performance statistically equivalent to Error Avoidance, with a trend toward EM superiority

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After BLS training, EM and EA groups showed equivalence in correct compression depth with a trend toward superiority of EM (proportional difference 23.3 percentage points, 95% CI 11.4%–34.2%). [→ Schmidt Michelle 2024](#schmidt-michelle-2024)

## Evidence

### Schmidt Michelle 2024

Schmidt Michelle, Schauwinhold Michael Tobias, Loeffler Leonie Anne Kathrin, Klasen Martin, Lambert Sophie Isabelle, Sopka Saša, Vogt Lina. (2024). Safety management within the scope of teaching practical clinical skills: framing errors for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training – a multi-arm randomized controlled equivalence trial. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2408458

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multi-arm parallel-group randomized controlled equivalence trial of first-year medical, dentistry, physiotherapy, and midwifery students (final sample n = 427). Correct compression depth at t1: "63.8% in the EM group" versus 40.5% in EA; equivalence with a trend toward EM superiority.

> "After the BLS training (t1), 40.4% in the control group, 63.8% in the EM group, and 40.5% in the EA group achieved the correct CD. The comparison of EA and EM results in a proportional difference of 23.3 percentage points (pp). The 95% CI for the proportional difference was 11.4% to 34.2%. The results indicate equivalence, with a trend towards the superiority of EM over EA."
