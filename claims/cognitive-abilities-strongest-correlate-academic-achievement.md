## Evidence

### Erdem Cahit 2026

Erdem Cahit, Kaya Metin, Polat Mustafa, Eğmir Eray. (2026). Thinking and feeling matter: a second-order meta-analysis of student characteristics across developmental domains and their effect on academic outcomes. BMC Psychology, 14, 919. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05035-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis comparing four characteristic domains across 71 effect sizes: cognitive ability (k = 22, r = 0.29), positive emotions (k = 30, r = 0.25), positive personality (k = 13, r = 0.20), and psychomotor (k = 6, r = 0.37). The discussion reports cognitive abilities account for "approximately 8.4% of the variance in academic achievement".

> "The mean effect sizes differed significantly in terms of student characteristics type (Q = 14.56; p < 0.01). The relationship between cognitive ability and academic achievement had higher effect size values (r = 0.29)."
