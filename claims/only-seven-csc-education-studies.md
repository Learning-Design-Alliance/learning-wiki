# Only seven population-level studies of educational outcomes of UK children in contact with CSC met inclusion criteria despite a broad search

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A search of 16 databases plus snowball, Google Scholar and grey-literature searches screened 5482 records and identified only seven eligible studies, of which three were peer-reviewed. [→ Jay Matthew A. 2019](#jay-matthew-a-2019)

## Evidence

### Jay Matthew A. 2019

Jay Matthew A., Mc Grath-Lone Louise. (2019). Educational outcomes of children in contact with social care in England: a systematic review. Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-019-1071-z

`q3 · i?`

Systematic review conducted per a pre-registered PROSPERO protocol (CRD42018089755) and reported per PRISMA. The review states it "screened 5482 records which resulted in seven studies being included"; only three were published in peer-reviewed journals.

> "In total, therefore, we screened 5482 records which resulted in seven studies being included in the narrative synthesis [12–18]."
