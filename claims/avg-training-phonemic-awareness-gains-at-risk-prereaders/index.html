Evidence¶

Bertoni 2024¶

Bertoni, S., Andreola, C., Mascheretti, S., Franceschini, S., Ruffino, M., Trezzi, V., Molteni, M., Sali, M. E., Salandi, A., Gaggi, O., Palazzi, C., Gori, S. & Facoetti, A. (2024). Action video games normalise the phonemic awareness in pre-readers at risk for developmental dyslexia. npj Science of Learning 9, 25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00230-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA on individual gain (ΔT1-T0) in phonemic discrimination across the four training groups of at-risk pre-readers, with T0 score as covariate. The omnibus test was significant, "F(3,74) = 5.175, p = 0.003, ηp2 = 0.173", a large between-group effect.

"A statistically significant different individual gain (ΔT1-T0) between groups was found in PD (F(3,74) = 5.175, p = 0.003, ηp2 = 0.173)."

Bertoni 2024 (2)¶

Bertoni, S., Andreola, C., Mascheretti, S., Franceschini, S., Ruffino, M., Trezzi, V., Molteni, M., Sali, M. E., Salandi, A., Gaggi, O., Palazzi, C., Gori, S. & Facoetti, A. (2024). Action video games normalise the phonemic awareness in pre-readers at risk for developmental dyslexia. npj Science of Learning 9, 25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00230-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bootstrap post-hoc pairwise comparisons after the significant omnibus ANCOVA. AVG gains exceeded "the WAIT (mean difference = 1.500...)", SNAVG and SPEECH, each pairwise difference significant at p < 0.05. No effect size was printed for the pairwise contrasts.

"Post-hoc analysis for 1,000 bootstrap resamples showed that individual gain after the AVG training was significantly higher compared to the WAIT (mean difference = 1.500, 95% CI = 0.391/2.785, p = 0.014), the SNAVG (mean difference = 1.015, 95% CI = 0.283/1.738, p = 0.009), and the SPEECH (mean difference = 0.818, 95% CI = 0.109/1.608, p = 0.039) groups."
