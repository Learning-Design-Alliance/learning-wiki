## Evidence

### Choi Seoheui 2023

Choi Seoheui, Shim Hoyeon, Lee Jang H. (2023). Efficiency analysis by training sequence of high-fidelity simulation-based neonatal resuscitation program (NRP). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0281515

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study comparing training orders; the article reports "Group B had a higher score than Group A, but the difference was not statistically significant (66.3±24.43 vs. 72.7±12.74 p = 0.360)". Total score differentiation was also non-significant (p = 0.824). Equivalence was not tested.
