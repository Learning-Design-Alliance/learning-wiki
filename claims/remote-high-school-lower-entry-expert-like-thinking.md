## Evidence

### Teemu Hynninen 2023

Teemu Hynninen, Henna Pesonen, Olli Lintu, and Petriina Paturi. (2023). Ongoing eﬀects of pandemic-imposed learning disruption on student attitudes. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison of pre-survey CLASS distributions (Fig. 4, top), comparing different student cohorts rather than a teaching effect; the limited-contact cohort of 2021 entrants "scored lower in all categories compared to the group in full contact teaching".

> "Comparing favorable answers in pre-surveys, we ﬁnd that the group that received limited contact teaching scored lower in all categories compared to the group in full contact teaching."

### Teemu Hynninen 2023 (2)

Teemu Hynninen, Henna Pesonen, Olli Lintu, and Petriina Paturi. (2023). Ongoing eﬀects of pandemic-imposed learning disruption on student attitudes. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same between-group pre-survey comparison using Cohen's d between different cohorts: the printed effect size was d ≈ 0.5 in several categories including the overall CLASS score, which the article describes as moderately significant.

> "In several categories, including the overall CLASS score,|d|≈ 0.5, meaning that the diﬀerence is moderately signiﬁcant."
