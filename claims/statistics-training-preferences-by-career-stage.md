## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Undergraduates prefer courses in astro/physics or math/stats departments, graduate students prefer learning statistical methods within their own department, and at the postdoc level course preference drops: workshop preference stays about the same, and mentoring and meetings with statisticians are preferred relative to courses. ## Evidence

### Eadie 2019

Eadie, G., Bahramian, A., Barmby, P., Craiu, R., Bingham, D., Hložek, R., Kavelaars, J., Stenning, D., Benincasa, S., Thomas, G., Thanjavur, K., Bovy, J., Cami, J., Carlberg, R., Lawler, S., Liu, A., Ngo, H., Rahman, M., & Rupen, M. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey Question 10 (Figure 2) asking how respondents would prefer to be trained at undergraduate, graduate, and postdoc stages, with check-all options including courses, online courses, mentoring, meetings with statisticians, and workshops. Descriptive percentages only; no effect size printed.

> "At the postdoctoral level, the desire to take courses in statistical methods drops dramatically, but the preference for workshops stays roughly the same. The relative preference for mentoring and meetings with statisticians at the postdoc level compared to courses is also much higher than at the undergrad and graduate level."
