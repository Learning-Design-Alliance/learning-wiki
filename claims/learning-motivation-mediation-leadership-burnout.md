### Wang Dongdong 2026

Wang Dongdong, Zhu Baitong, Song Chunyu, Xie Dandan, Lu Zhuangnian. (2026). Research on the relationships among teachers’ paternalistic leadership style, learning motivation and learning burnout of medical students on a standardized residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09250-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples, 95% CI) in AMOS 28.0 on the same 403-respondent dataset. All three confidence intervals excluded zero, confirming that "learning motivation plays a significant mediating role between all three leadership styles and learning burnout".

> "The indirect effect of moral leadership on learning burnout through learning motivation was − 0.058, with a 95% confidence interval of [–0.112, − 0.022], indicating a significant mediating effect (p = 0.001). The indirect effect of authoritarian leadership on learning burnout through learning motivation was 0.045, with a 95% confidence interval of [0.017, 0.090], and the interval does not include zero, demonstrating a significant mediating effect (p = 0.001)."
