# MAS-CMD was rated significantly higher than MAS-Roles on QM criterion 5.2C active learning by 20 expert teachers, with a large effect size

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a within-subject human evaluation, teachers rated MAS-CMD significantly higher than MAS-Roles on QM 5.2C active learning (F(2,38)=4.75, p=.014, η²p=.20). [→ Jiayi Wang 2025](#jiayi-wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Jiayi Wang 2025

Jiayi Wang, Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, John Stamper. (2025). Enabling Multi-Agent Systems as Learning Designers: Applying Learning Sciences to AI Instructional Design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16659 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.16659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2508.16659)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Human expert evaluation in which 20 US secondary math and science teachers rated all three systems on QM criteria using repeated-measures ANOVA. The article reports "a statistically significant effect of system on criterion 5.2" with η²p = .20, described as a large effect size.

> "ANOVA revealed a statistically significant effect of system on criterion 5.2, F (2, 38) = 4.75,p = .014,η2 p = .20, indicating a large effect size."
