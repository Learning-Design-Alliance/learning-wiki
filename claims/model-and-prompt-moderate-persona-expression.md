`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment I model-level (Table 13) and prompt-level (Table 14) descriptive analyses in the workday scenario. The article also reports that "the text-based prompt produced substantially higher self-report means and observer means than the scale-based prompt" for moderate-intensity personas. Descriptive comparisons; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "DeepSeek models produced higher observer ratings for moderate-intensity workday personas, whereas Claude produced notably lower ratings for the same condition. These model-level differences confirm that the model family substantially moderates the level of persona expression."
