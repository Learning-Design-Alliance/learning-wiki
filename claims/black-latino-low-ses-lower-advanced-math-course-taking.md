## Evidence

### Melodie Baker 2023

Melodie Baker, Ivy Morgan, and Gizella Wade. (2023). Opportunities Denied: High-Achieving Black and Latino Students Lack Access to Advanced Math. EdTrust and Just Equations. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641831

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the nationally representative HSLS:09 sample of over 23,000 ninth graders, with sampling weights applied. The report states these were "statistically significant differences between student groups in course-taking in middle and high school."

> "just 24% of Black students, 34% of Latino students, and 25% of students from low-socioeconomic (SES) backgrounds took Algebra I or higher in eighth grade, compared with 39% of White peers, 64% of Asian peers, and 57% of students from higher SES backgrounds."
