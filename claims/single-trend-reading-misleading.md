## Evidence

### Anthony S. Bryk 1998

Anthony S. Bryk, Yeow Meng Thum, John Q. Easton, Stuart Luppescu. (1998). Mining Productivity: How to Read Your Productivity Profile. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

The guide's interpretive walkthrough of illustrative school profiles (Schools A through D) in the Interpreting Productivity Profiles section concludes that readers must "look simultaneously at both the learning gain trends and output trends" to classify improvement efforts, because single trends read separately can mislead.

> "we need to look simultaneously at both the learning gain trends and output trends to classify improvement efforts. Taken together, these two trends provide a more detailed summary of changing school productivity over time."
