q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's discussion of the ITS-vs-teacher category found "seven out of eight articles reported a positive effect in favor of the ITS," though the review declined to tabulate recalculated effect sizes because designs varied too much across studies.

"In comparison between an ITS and teacher-led instruction, seven out of eight articles reported a positive effect in favor of the ITS, with a medium to large size effect."
