## Evidence

### Jose 2012

Jose, G. R., & Dharma Raja, B. W. (2012). Multimedia Modular Approach for Augmenting the Speaking Skill of the Student-Teachers. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070215

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A delayed post-test was conducted 20 days after the post-test to assess retention of learning. The experimental group's mean delayed post-test score remained significantly higher than the control group's, indicating that the multimedia modular approach supported better retention of speaking-skill learning.

> "In the Table 5, since p value is less than 0.01 in the speaking, the null hypothesis not accepted at 1% level of significance. Hence, it is concluded that there is significant difference in the delayed posttest scores of speaking skill between the control and the experimental groups."
