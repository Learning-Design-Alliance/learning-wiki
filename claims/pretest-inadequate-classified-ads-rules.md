## Evidence

### Khodabandeh 2016

Khodabandeh, F. (2016). Comparing the Effects of Four Instructional Treatments on EFL Students’ Achievement in Writing Classified Ads. English Language Teaching, 9(3), 139-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p139

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion of pre-test analysis across all four groups (N=72 total). The average number of abbreviation errors in pre-test papers was 9, 9, 10, and 10 for explicit, implicit, self-study, and task-based groups respectively, showing inadequate knowledge of English classified ads rules across all groups.

> "In their ads, the participants wrote simple sentences by using the articles, conjunctions, prepositions, verbs, pronouns, and copula instead of omitting them. The analysis also revealed that the explicit, implicit, self-study and task-based participants tended not to include any ab breviations in their pre-tests."
