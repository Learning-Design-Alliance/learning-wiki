Peter Goodyear 2015¶

Peter Goodyear. (2015). Teaching as design. HERDSA Review of Higher Education. https://www.herdsa.org.au/herdsa-review-higher-education-vol-2/27-50

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Authors' argument in the drivers-of-change section, illustrated in Figure 2 with the four forces: diversifying student needs and expectations, rising expectations about graduate capabilities, accelerating technological change, and intensification of pressures on teaching staff.

"These four sets of forces combine to place traditional teaching practices under stress. That is to say, teaching approaches that may have been the norm 20 or even 10 years ago no longer look affordable or appealing."

Peter Goodyear 2015 (2)¶

Peter Goodyear. (2015). Teaching as design. HERDSA Review of Higher Education. https://www.herdsa.org.au/herdsa-review-higher-education-vol-2/27-50

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

The article's central economic and educational argument from the overview section: investing in the planning phase as design is presented as a response to the four forces, not a tested result.

"Shifting resources towards design for learning, and adopting more effective design practices, is a credible strategy for improving the quality of higher education while managing with tighter funding."
