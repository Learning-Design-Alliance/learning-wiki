# Wiki-based instruction reduces writing anxiety of EFL business students relative to traditional instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the intervention, the experimental (wiki) group's writing anxiety scores were significantly lower than the control group's on the Writing Anxiety Inventory. [→ Kassem 2017](#kassem-2017)

## Evidence

### Kassem 2017

Mohamed Ali Mohamed Kassem. (2017). Developing Business Writing Skills and Reducing Writing Anxiety of EFL Learners through Wikis. English Language Teaching, 10(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n3p151

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on the post-application of the 12-item Writing Anxiety Inventory (five-point Likert scale) with the same 60 students. The wiki group's mean anxiety score was lower than the control group's, "t (58) = 12.15, p = 0.01".

> "The results, displayed in Table 3, showed statistically significant difference in the scores for the experimental group (M =29.17, SD =4.25) and the control group (M =48.47, SD = 7.59); t (58) = 12.15, p = 0.01 in favor of the experimental group."
