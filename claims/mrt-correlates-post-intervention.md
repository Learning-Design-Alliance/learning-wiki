## Evidence

### van Cappellen van Walsum AM 2022

van Cappellen van Walsum AM, Henssen DJHA. (2022). E-Learning Three-Dimensional Anatomy of the Brainstem: Impact of Different Microscopy Techniques and Spatial Ability. Anatomical Sciences Education 15(2):317-329. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2056

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kendall's tau B correlation analysis in the 38-student cohort: MRT scores correlated significantly with post-intervention scores ("rk  = 0.321; P < 0.05") and with pre-intervention scores (rk  = 0.236; P = 0.050); no standardized effect size beyond the correlation coefficient was printed.

> "A significant correlation could be found between the MRT score and pre- and post-intervention test scores (rk  = 0.236; P = 0.050 and rk  = 0.321; P < 0.05, respectively)."
