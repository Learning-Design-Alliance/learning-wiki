### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis between SVO (measured with the SVO Slider Measure) and self-other differences in RLDDM model-based weight in the 92-participant sample, showing printed effect size r(92) = -0.23. The logistic regression model also showed SVO modulated self-other differences in model-based strategies, bPreviousOutcome×SelfOther×SVO = -0.05, 95% HDI [-0.09, -0.01], with no effect on model-free differences.
