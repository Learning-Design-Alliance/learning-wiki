Tarek A. Elsayed. (2010). Interpreting Recoil For Undergraduate Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2997

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

A small informal evaluation with 18 students after instruction using the paper's approach. The author reports students were receptive and almost all said they learned something new and benefited. No systematic assessment instrument is reported.

> "After the aforementioned quiz, the concept of recoil was introduced to 18 students in the way described in the present paper and most of the previous examples were discussed with them. The students were receptive to the discussion and almost all of them said that they learned something new and benefited from the discussion."
