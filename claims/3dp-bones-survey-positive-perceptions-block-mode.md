## Evidence

### Tripodi N 2020

Tripodi N, Kelly K, Husaric M, Wospil R, Fleischmann M, Johnston S, Harkin K. (2020). The Impact of Three-Dimensional Printed Anatomical Models on First-Year Student Engagement in a Block Mode Delivery. Anatomical Sciences Education 13(6):769-777. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1958

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire of 111 first-year osteopathy students (77% response rate) using a five-point Likert-type scale adapted from allied health research, analyzed with Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit tests in SPSS; "all survey answers were found to be strongly positively skewed (P < 0.001; Table 1)".

> "Using a Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit Test all survey answers were found to be strongly positively skewed (P < 0.001; Table 1), barring the reduced need to take notes, which was moderately skewed toward a positive response"
