## Evidence

### Wang 2013

Wang, J., Lin, C.-F. C., Yu, W.-C. W., & Wu, E. (2013). Meaningful engagement in Facebook learning environments: Merging social and academic lives. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 14(1), Article 26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006268

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression analyses reported in the Results section. The article states time on Facebook and time on co-curricular activities were "significant predictors of student grades", and that Facebook time explained 27.7% of variance in grades.

> "The linear regression showed that the amount of time spent on Facebook (F (7, 125)  = 5.848, p<.01) (Table 6) and the amount of time spent on co-curricular activities (F  (7, 126)  = .6558, p<.001) (Table: 7) were significant predictors of student grades"
