Doyle, L., Easterbrook, M. J., & Harris, P. R. (2023). Roles of socioeconomic status, ethnicity and teacher beliefs in academic grading. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 93, 91–112. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12541

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory robust linear model adding malleability-of-intelligence beliefs and political beliefs alongside BSM as predictors of support for diverse teaching practices. The article reports these as correlational findings; no standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "Diverse teaching practices were significantly and negatively predicted by BSM, B = −.15, SE = .03, t = −5.84, p < .001, teachers' mindsets about the malleability of intelligence, B = −.12, SE = .03, t = −4.39, p < .001 and political beliefs, B = −.12, SE = .02, t = −5.81, p < .001, with the overall model accounting for 24% of the variance (R 2 = .24)"
