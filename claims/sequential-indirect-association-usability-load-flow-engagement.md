`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected bootstrap with 5000 resamples on the 2016-respondent SEM; the three-path indirect association was 0.116 (95% CI 0.095–0.140), while usability→load→engagement (−0.013) and usability→flow→engagement (0.003) were not significant.

> "Bootstrap analysis (Table 6) supported a sequential statistical indirect association from usability through extraneous cognitive load and flow to engagement (indirect association = 0.116). Because all variables were measured concurrently, this result does not establish temporal ordering or causation."
