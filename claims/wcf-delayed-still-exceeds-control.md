Descriptive comparison reported in the results section: the experimental group's delayed post-test mean remained 1.98 points above the control group's post-test mean, which the article reads as reflecting the efficacy of the feedback in improving writing skills.

> "Although the students’ scores were lower in the post-de- layed test than in the post-test, the average scores of the stu- dents in the experimental group were still higher than those of the students in the post-test control group, with a differ - ence of (1.98)."
