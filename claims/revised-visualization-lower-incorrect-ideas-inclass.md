## Evidence

### Antje Kohnle 2014

Antje Kohnle, Charles Baily and Scott Ruby. (2014). Investigating the Influence of Visualization on Student Understanding of Quantum Superposition. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.031](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.031)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In-class trials of the Interferometer Experiments simulation in 50-minute workshop sessions, original depiction in 2013 (N=28) and Visualization III in 2014 (N=78); written responses coded with an emergent scheme achieving Cohen's Kappa 0.62 to 1. The article reports χ2(4, N=104) =15.9, exact p=0.003; no effect size is printed.

> "An exact test for Pearson’s chi -square shows a significant difference in the two distributions: χ2(4, N=104) =15.9; exact p=0.003."
