## Evidence

### Lee Gun Tak 2022

Lee Gun Tak, Park Jong Eun, Woo Sook-young, Shin Tae Gun, Jeong Daun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se Uk, Yoon Hee, Hwang Sung Yeon. (2022). Defining the learning curve for endotracheal intubation in the emergency department. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19337-8

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable generalized linear mixed model with random intercept on 2077 ETI cases from an institutional airway registry (1979 patients, April 2014–March 2021). The adjusted association "remained significant after adjusting for ETI device, indication for ETI, and presence of difficult airway" with adjusted odds ratio = 1.010 per additional ETI case — a very small per-case effect size.

> "This association remained significant after adjusting for ETI device, indication for ETI, and presence of difficult airway (aOR 1.010 per additional ETI case; 95% CI 1.006–1.013; p < 0.001)."
