Ariyan Hossain 2026¶

Ariyan Hossain, Kazi Kamruzzaman Rabbi, Farig Sadeque, S M Taiabul Haque. (2026). Contaminated Collaboration: Measuring Gender Bias Transfer in LLM-Assisted Student Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15914 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.15914

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-task survey (5-point Likert items on perceived AI influence, counterfactual difference, and noticed anomalies) administered after essay submission in the 123-participant experiment. No inferential test is reported for these survey contrasts; ratings are descriptive.

"Despite exhibiting a substantially larger agency gap and higher SCR, biased con- dition participants reported lower perceived influ- ence on career recommendations (Q2: 3.50±1.33 vs. 3.71±1.23 ) and writing (Q3: 3.90±1.08 vs. 4.07±0.98 )"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • AI systems are non-neutral socio-technical artifacts embedding cultural assumptions and power relations — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • Students assisted by a gender-biased LLM produce career-plan essays with a significantly larger male-female agentic gap than students in neutral-LLM and no-AI control conditions — related
  • Biased LLM assistance increases students' gender-stereotypic occupational recommendations: 71.4% of biased-condition essays recommended a gender-stereotypic occupation versus 45.0% (control) and 39.0% (neutral) — related
  • A gender-biased system prompt induces large gender-differentiated language in LLM-generated career essays, while a neutral prompt does not — related
  • Bias transfer is asymmetric: prompt condition affects agency in female-target essays but not male-target essays, suppressing female agency while male writing remains stable — related
  • A neutrally configured LLM assistant produced the lowest rate of gender-stereotypic occupation suggestions, below even the no-AI control condition — related
  • The video-task awareness measure did not correlate with misconceptions or conceptual change and is considered exploratory — related
