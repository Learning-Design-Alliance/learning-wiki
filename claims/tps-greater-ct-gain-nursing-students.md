## Evidence

### Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013

Mahmoud Kaddoura. (2013). Think Pair Share: A teaching Learning Strategy to Enhance Students' Critical Thinking. Educational Research Quarterly, Vol. 36.4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061947

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 91 sophomore accelerated baccalaureate nursing students in two sections of the same Health Assessment course; the TPS section's mean gain was 42.9 versus 12.43 for the control, a difference of 30.5 that the independent samples t test found significant ("t = 4.327, df = 78, p < 0.001").

> "The t test establishes that the difference in the score increase, 30.5, is significant (t = 4.327, df = 78, p < 0.001)."

### Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013 (2)

Mahmoud Kaddoura. (2013). Think Pair Share: A teaching Learning Strategy to Enhance Students' Critical Thinking. Educational Research Quarterly, Vol. 36.4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061947

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Because CT scores slightly deviated from normal distribution, the researcher ran a Mann-Whitney test on the score increases, which was "consistent with the independent t-test result" (Z = 4.391, p < 0.001).

> "The Mann- Whitney result was consistent with the independent t-test result (Z = 4.391, p < 0.001)."
