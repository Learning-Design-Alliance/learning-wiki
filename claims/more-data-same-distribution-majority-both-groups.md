Emily M. Stump, Mark Hughes, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Comparing intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning about uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147)

Closed-response predictive reasoning question about how a histogram would change with 100 more students collecting data. The article reports majorities chose Same (61% intro, 60% beyond-intro), "which we consider to be the correct answer."

> "A majority of students indicated that the distribution would remain the same if 100 additional students were to collect data (61% of intro students and 60% of beyond-intro students), which we consider to be the correct answer."
