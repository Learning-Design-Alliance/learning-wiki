## Evidence

### Kalua Khumbo 2014

Kalua Khumbo, Gichangi Michael, Barassa Ernest, Eliah Edson, Lewallen Susan, Courtright Paul. (2014). A randomised controlled trial to investigate effects of enhanced supervision on primary eye care services at health centres in Kenya, Malawi and Tanzania. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-14-S1-S6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up status of supplies provided to all 137 facilities at baseline (Table 2); torches present in 19 (27.5%) intervention vs 6 (8.8%) non-intervention facilities, VA charts in 16 (23.2%) vs 4 (5.9%). Printed odds ratios OR=3.9 and OR=4.8.

> "The intervention groups were more likely to have a functioning torch (OR=3.9, 95% CI: 1.5, 10.6) and a visual acuity chart correctly placed on the wall (OR=4.8, 95% CI: 1.5, 15.3) than non-intervention facilities, although the majority of facilities no longer had these items."
