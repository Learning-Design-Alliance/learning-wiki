Evidence¶

Andria Shyjka 2021¶

Andria Shyjka. (2021). Student Experience with the High School Choice Process in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago Consortium Research Brief, July 2021. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-experience-with-the-high-school-choice-process-in-Chicago-Public-Schools

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

School Exploration section finding: use of GoCPS, the CPS website, and school websites was introduced via the 6to16 curriculum, and a majority of students described counselors or staff facilitating navigation of these online resources.

"All students in our sample reported relying heavily on online sources to research potential high schools."

Andria Shyjka 2021 (2)¶

Andria Shyjka. (2021). Student Experience with the High School Choice Process in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago Consortium Research Brief, July 2021. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-experience-with-the-high-school-choice-process-in-Chicago-Public-Schools

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Same focus-group study: students wanted to hear about experiences of students at schools they were considering; one summarized the limitation saying "we haven't learned like how it's gonna be when we get in the classes."

"Several students expressed frustration with high school websites, often finding them out of date or lacking information related to their consideration factors."
