## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Jennifer DeWitt, Charlotte Thorley, and Olivia Keenan. (2021). Evaluating participants' experience of extended interaction with cutting-edge physics research through the PRiSE 'research in schools' programme. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-147-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-147-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Free-response question asking students (n = 135) for adjectives describing their experience; teachers (n = 38) gave observations. Since 2016 this yielded 88 unique adjectives shown as a word cloud with high student–teacher agreement; top adjectives were "interesting, challenging, exciting, inspiring and fun".

> "The most cited adjectives were (in descending order) interesting, challenging, exciting, inspiring and fun, similar to those from the pilot (M. O. Archer, 2017)"
