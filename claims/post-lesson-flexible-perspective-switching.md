## Evidence

### Maike Schindler 2017

Maike Schindler, Stephan Hußmann, Per Nilsson, Arthur Bakker. (2017). Sixth-grade students' reasoning on the order relation of integers as influenced by prior experience: an inferentialist analysis. Math Ed Res J 29:471–492. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13394-017-0202-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' synthesis of the two post interviews, in which both students drew on the number line, an external representation crucial in their lesson series, illustrating the impact of school-based experiences on reasoning on the order relation.

> "Our results support the finding that students have the ability to switch perspectives, to reason differently, and to be able to draw flexibly on different prior experiences and inferences when comparing negative integers"

### Maike Schindler 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Tom's post-interview episode comparing −8 and −12: he gave the mathematically correct number-line answer and additionally noted that in the debts context −12 would be the greater debt, a distinction the authors call reasonable in certain situations.

> "the minus eight is greater because it is closer to zero. (5 s) Yes, and when a number is closer to zero, then it is greater. If there stood, however, when, say, er, which number, well which debts are greater, then it would be the minus twelve"
