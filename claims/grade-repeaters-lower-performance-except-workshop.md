Authors' interpretation of the age-by-group predicted performance results (Figure 4, Table 4), where predicted scores decline with age in control and doubling groups but stay near 4.3–4.4 across ages in the workshop group. "the academic performance was similar for older and younger students" only in the workshop arm.
