Detailed post-treatment analysis (Table 4) of six motivation elements in the same quasi-experiment; four elements showed significant differences favouring the experimental group, while self-efficacy beliefs (p=.564) and ought-to L2 self (p=.079) did not. No effect sizes are printed.
