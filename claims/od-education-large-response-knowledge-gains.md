## Evidence

### Dunn Kelly E. 2017

Dunn Kelly E., Yepez-Laubach Claudia, Nuzzo Paul A., Fingerhood Michael, Kelly Anne, Berman Suzan, Bigelow George E. (2017). Randomized controlled trial of a computerized opioid overdose education intervention. Drug and alcohol dependence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2016.12.003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat analysis of all randomized participants in the RCT comparing Pamphlet, Computer, and Computer + Mastery groups on a 51-item knowledge measure. The two-factor model showed the session effect: "mean percent correct increased from 41.8% to 73.8%"; no effect size was printed.

> "there was a large significant main effect of session (F(1,73) = 129.53, p < 0.001) on opioid OD response knowledge, where mean percent correct increased from 41.8% to 73.8% between the pre and post-test, respectively"
