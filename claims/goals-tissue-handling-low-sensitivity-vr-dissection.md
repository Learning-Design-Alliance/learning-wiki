## Evidence

### Mori Takashi 2022

Mori Takashi, Ikeda Koji, Takeshita Nobuyoshi, Teramura Koichi, Ito Masaaki. (2022). Validation of a novel virtual reality simulation system with the focus on training for surgical dissection during laparoscopic sigmoid colectomy. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01441-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of their own validation data, reported in the Discussion. They note tissue handling reached a plateau at a low level in prior GOALS literature, which may explain the failure to separate novices from experts on total score.

> "This result could reflect less sensitivity in “tissue handling” in the present simulator. The non-medical professionals received a high score of 4 (3.25–4) in tissue handling, although their scores for other items were lower than this."
