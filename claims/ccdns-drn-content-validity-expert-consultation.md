## Evidence

### Lai Jinjia 2025

Lai Jinjia, Chen Hongyi, Huang Yongqi, Ma Chaoqun, Zeng Ping, Chen Hanxi, Tan Yibing. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Core Competencies in Disaster Nursing Scale for Disaster Rescue Nurses in Mainland China. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9957270

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert consultation step with 10 experts from the Disaster Nursing Specialized Committee of the Chinese Nursing Association using a four-point relevance/clarity rating; abstract reports a total-scale content validity index of 0.980. Values met the article's thresholds (I-CVI above 0.78, S-CVI/UA above 0.80, S-CVI/Ave above 0.90).

> "The experts' consultation results showed that the score range of I-CVI of this scale was 0.78–1.0, S-CVI/UA was 0.85, and S-CVI/Ave was 0.98."
