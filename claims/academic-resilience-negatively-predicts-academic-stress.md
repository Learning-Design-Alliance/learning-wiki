## Evidence

### Trigueros Rubén 2020

Trigueros Rubén, Magaz-González Ana M., García-Tascón Marta, Alias Antonio, Aguilar-Parra José M. (2020). Validation and Adaptation of the Academic-Resilience Scale in the Spanish Context. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113779

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Criterion-validity SEM with a sample of 1248 university students, using the Spanish student stress inventory (behavioral, physiological, emotional factors) as the outcome. The article concludes resilience "was a negative predictor of academic stress"; no standardized path coefficient is printed.

> "To analyze the predictive validity of the scale, a SEM was carried out (Figure 2). Adjustment indices were as follows: χ2 (8. N = 1248) = 24.92, p < 0.001; χ2/df = 3.11; CFI = 0.97; IFI = 0.97; RMSEA = 0.052"
