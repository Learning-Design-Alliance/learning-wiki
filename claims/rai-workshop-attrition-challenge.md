Observation of the 2023 workshop offerings. The pilot had four 60-minute sessions with substantial attrition by the fourth session, attributed to work-related demands; the redesigned two-session format still lost nearly half of participants between sessions.

> "Unfortunately, attrition remained a challenge: with just over 50% of the participants from the first session returning for the second."
