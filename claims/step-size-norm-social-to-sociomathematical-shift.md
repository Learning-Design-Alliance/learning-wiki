Explanatory case study of the Inventing Measures episode in Phase II: measured and calculated total distances "didn't match" because the heel-to-toe convention "effectively measuring parts of the total distance twice (63 in. vs. 37 in. in Figure 3)", prompting invention of the toe-to-toe convention.

> "The heel-to-toe measurement convention produces an overlap, effectively measuring parts of the total distance twice (63 in. vs. 37 in. in Figure 3)."
