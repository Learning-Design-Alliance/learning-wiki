### Shao Y 2025

Shao Y, Zhu H, Kong W, Li W, Zhang C. (2025). How teacher caring behavior affects high school students’ English academic performance: the mediating role of academic emotions. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000042751

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 702 high school students in Yancheng, China, using AMOS with 5000-iteration bootstrapping. The direct effect was significant with a 95% CI of "1.394–4.792", confirming that teacher caring behavior positively predicts English academic performance.

> "the direct effect of TCB on high school students’ EAP was significant (P < .01), and the percentile 95% CI interval did not contain 0 (1.394–4.792), confirming Hypothesis 1"
