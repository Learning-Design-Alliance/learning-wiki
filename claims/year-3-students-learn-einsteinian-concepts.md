## Evidence

### Tejinder Kaur 2022

Tejinder Kaur, Aishwarya Banavathu, David Blair, Rahul Choudhary, and Alex Foppolli. (2022). Can 8-Year-Olds Understand Concepts of Einsteinian Physics?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Video-recorded oral question-and-answer session with Class 1 on the final day of the intervention, coded for movement, initiation, and response utterances, showed students were responsive and enthusiastic, with a "substantial uptake of Einsteinian concepts by students ages 7-8".

> "Video analysis of teacher-led Q&A sessions and feedback from the Class 1 teacher demonstrated a substantial uptake of Einsteinian concepts by students ages 7-8."

### Tejinder Kaur 2022 (2)

Tejinder Kaur, Aishwarya Banavathu, David Blair, Rahul Choudhary, and Alex Foppolli. (2022). Can 8-Year-Olds Understand Concepts of Einsteinian Physics?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

On the last day of the program, the team recorded a video asking students questions; students easily summarised concepts using phrases about spacetime and bulletiness, and responses included "momentum, photons".

> "The students' responses included the phrase momentum, photons, which was previously thought to be beyond the students' comprehension at this age."
