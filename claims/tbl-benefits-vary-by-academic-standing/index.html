Evidence¶

Xie Zhi-Bo 2025¶

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, citing one included study, that high achievers benefited from TBL in problem-solving while low achievers scored significantly lower than LBL counterparts. Another cited study found academically weaker students showed greater score increases with TBL.

"while high achievers in the TBL group performed better in problem-solving skills assessments compared to their counterparts in the traditional LBL group, low achievers in the TBL group had significantly lower scores in problem-solving exercises compared to those in the LBL group"
