Evidence¶

Yao X-C and Fan L 2026¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental classroom study with two pre-existing intact classes (48 students each) over three writing cycles. The Diagnostic Review class gained "a mean gain of 5.92 points" versus 3.58 in Control; the between-group gain difference was 2.35 points, Hedges' g = 1.12, p < 0.001.

"Diagnostic Review scores increased from 65.56 to 71.48, a mean gain of 5.92 points (SD= 1.87; 95% CI [5.38, 6.47]). The between-group gain difference was 2.35 points (Hedges' g =1.12, p < 0.001)."
