## Evidence

### Porcheret M 2018

Porcheret M, Main C, Croft P, Dziedzic K. (2018). Enhancing delivery of osteoarthritis care in the general practice consultation: evaluation of a behaviour change intervention. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0715-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Duration analysis of the 45 video-recorded simulated consultations using paired t tests. The authors state the increase in competency was not associated with longer consultations, which they call important for generalisability.

> "Mean duration of all videos was 14.46 min (range of 8.80 to 26.93 min) with no difference in duration at the three time-points (paired t test: baseline v one-month, p = 0.28: baseline v five-months, p = 0.63; one-month v five-months, p = 0.13)."
