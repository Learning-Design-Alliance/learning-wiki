# Algodoo impulse applications show internal consistency between impulse, momentum change, and graph-read values within small deviation

> **Currency** · newest study 2021 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the 4 N force application, impulse calculated as 8.28 N.s matched the momentum change read from the graph (8.267 N.s) with small deviation. [→ Atakan Çoban 2021](#atakan-coban-2021)
`q1 i?` In the falling 5 kg object application, impulse values 35.77 N.s (theoretical) and 35.93 N.s (graph area) matched momentum change 35.95 N.s. [→ Atakan Çoban 2021 (2)](#atakan-coban-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Atakan Çoban 2021

Atakan Çoban. (2021). Algodoo for Online Education: Impulse and Momentum Activities. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1e9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1e9)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

First impulse–momentum-change simulation: a 4 N force acted on a circular body from rest; "the final value of the momentum was 8.267 N.s" while calculated impulse was 8.28 N.s, equal "with a small margin of deviation" per the article.

> "The duration of the force on the graph was deter mined as 2.07 s and the final value of the momentum was 8.267 N.s."

### Atakan Çoban 2021 (2)

Atakan Çoban. (2021). Algodoo for Online Education: Impulse and Momentum Activities. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1e9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1e9)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Second impulse–momentum-change simulation: a 5 kg object fell under gravity 9.8 m/s² for 0.73 s; theoretical impulse "35.77 N.s" and graph-area impulse 35.93 N.s matched the momentum change ∆P=35.95 N.s, which the author describes as high internal consistency.

> "the impulse affecting the object during this movement is 35.77 N.s. Impulse was found as 35.93 N.s from the area under the force -time graph"
