## Evidence

### Chung KC 2025

Chung KC, Heiman A, Malay S, and the PRAISE Study Group. (2025). Proficiency and Difficulty Scoring Tools for Finger Replantation. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.40453

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SPS calculation comparison across the 65 surgeons' two case halves in the PRAISE cohort. The article printed the first-half mean of 1.40 (1.00), second-half mean of 1.49 (1.00), t-test P = .18, and separately a weak correlation "r = 0.27; P = .03".

> "The mean (SD) SPS for the first half of each surgeon's procedures was 1.40 (1.00) (range, −0.37 to 4.14). This was not significantly different from the second half (mean [SD], 1.49 [1.00]; range, −0.44 to 4.15) by t test (P = .18)"
