### Maghaminejad Farzaneh F 2026

Maghaminejad Farzaneh F, Dehghan-Nayeri Nahid, Delshad Zahra, Sabet Mahdi Shafiee, Rahimzadeh Mitra. (2026). Investigate the relationship between self-directed learning and emotional, spiritual, and moral intelligences among nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07238-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study of 300 nursing students in Kashan, Iran using Pearson correlation. The abstract reports a significant positive correlation between emotional intelligence and self-directed learning (r = 0.63), with spiritual (r = 0.43) and moral intelligence (r = 0.62) also significant.

> "There were significant positive correlations between self-directed learning and emotional (r = 0.63, p < 0.05), spiritual (r = 0.43, p < 0.05), and moral intelligence (r = 0.62, p < 0.05)."
