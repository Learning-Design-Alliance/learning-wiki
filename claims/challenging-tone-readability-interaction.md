### Antoun Yaacoub 2025

Antoun Yaacoub, Zainab Assaghir, Lionel Prevost, Jérôme Da-Rugna. (2025). Analyzing Feedback Mechanisms in AI-Generated MCQs: Insights into Readability, Lexical Properties, and Levels of Challenge. ESIEA Lab / Lebanese University. [doi:10.1109/iccsm66818.2025.00026](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccsm66818.2025.00026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis of Table 4 readability means: challenging feedback scored 8.0 Flesch-Kincaid for easy questions versus 9.5 (supportive) and 9.8 (neutral), converging with other tones at hard difficulty. The authors interpret this as prioritizing clarity in challenging feedback for simpler topics.

> "Most notably, challenging feedback has a lower readability score than the other tones for easy questions. This suggests that the AI prioritizes clarity and directness when challenging st udents on simpler concepts, avoiding unnecessary complexity."
