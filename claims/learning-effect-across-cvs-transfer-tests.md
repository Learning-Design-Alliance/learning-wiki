## Evidence

### Johnsson V 2026

Johnsson V, Petersen OB, Svendsen MBS, et al. (2026). To guide or not to guide: A randomized study on the use of needle guide for chorionic villus sampling training. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70108

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The linear mixed-effects model showed a significant main effect of testing sequence (F[3, 92.3] = 6.3, p < 0.001). Post hoc comparisons found "significantly higher mean performance scores in later transfer tests compared with the first test" (test two: 8.4 [2.5–14.4], p = 0.001; test three: 6.0, p = 0.04; test four: 7.3, p = 0.007).

> "Post hoc analyses demonstrated significantly higher mean performance scores in later transfer tests compared with the first test, indicating a learning effect across the four transfer tests (mean difference [95% CI]: test two, 8.4 [2.5–14.4], p = 0.001"
