Evidence¶

Finch 2016¶

Finch, J. E., Obradović, J., & Yousafzai, A. (2016). Home environment quality mediates the effects of an early intervention on children's social-emotional development in rural Pakistan. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567225

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' conclusion from the path analysis of the PEDS follow-up (N = 1302; monthly household income averaging $100, 37% food insecurity, 68% of mothers illiterate). The intervention improved home environment quality at age two, which was "then linked to social skills and behavior problems via home environment quality at age four".

"Despite the disadvantaged nature of the sample, we found sustained impacts of a zero-to-two intervention on children's social skills and problem behaviors in the preschool period"
