`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Outcome of the summative online long case examination sat by 273 of the 275 program participants in May 2020, examined over 2 days on Microsoft Teams with standardised mark sheets and an external examiner. Grade breakdown: 1H 37%, 2.1 19%, 2.2 15%, pass 23%, fail 7%.

> "Following completion of the program, 93% of students successfully passed the year 3 semester 2 online long case examination, facilitating onward progression through the degree program without delay."
