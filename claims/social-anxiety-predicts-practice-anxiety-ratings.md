### Hood 2021

Hood, S., Barrickman, N., Djerdjian, N., Farr, M., Magner, S., Roychowdhury, H., Gerrits, R., Lawford, H., Ott, B., Ross, K., Paige, O., Stowe, S., Jensen, M., & Hull, K. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Series of linear regressions on beginning-of-term survey data from the N = 227 sample, with self-reported GPA as covariate. Table 4 reports standardized coefficients for social anxiety of 0.34 (PRS group), 0.52 (volunteering), 0.54 (cold calling), 0.44 (muddiest point), and 0.28 (in-class quiz group).

> "When controlling for reported GPA, participants’ social anxiety significantly predicted mean anxiety ratings of several instructional practices made at the beginning of the semester. However, these effect sizes were modest (see Table 4)."
