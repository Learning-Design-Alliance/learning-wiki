### Anning Naomi 2024

Anning Naomi, Tehan Peta. (2024). Simulation in podiatry teaching and learning: A scoping review. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.70020

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Before–after quasi-experimental study (Lazzarini et al., as charted in Table 1) of a 2-day workshop using part-task stations, scenario-based simulations with foot models, and standardized patients with simulated foot ulcers. The review reports significant improvements in knowledge and practical skills; no effect size is printed.

> "FUST program was effective in increasing podiatrists' confidence in managing foot ulcers, with significant improvements observed in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills as assessed through simulation‐based training"
