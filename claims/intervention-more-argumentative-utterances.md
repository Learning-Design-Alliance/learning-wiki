## Evidence

### Larrain 2018

Larrain, A., Freire, P., Grau, V., López, P., Salvat, I., Silva, M., & Gastellu, V. (2018). The effect of peer-group argumentative dialogue on delayed gains in scientific content knowledge. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 162, 67–87. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20263

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of coded small-group dialogue from videotaped lessons in both classes. The intervention group showed "a significantly higher proportion of argumentative utterances per minute of group work" with "η2 = .203" (F(1/48) = 12.25, p = .001) and significantly more group-work time (F(1/50) = 276.76, p < .001, η2 = .847).
