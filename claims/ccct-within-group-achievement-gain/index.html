Evidence¶

Özgür Tutal 2026¶

Özgür Tutal. (2026). Artificial intelligence assisted design of a novel cooperative learning technique for higher education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50770-1

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test within the experimental CCCT group (n = 31), pre-test X = 43.39 (SD = 13.99) to post-test X = 51.45 (SD = 6.08). The improvement was "statistically significant only for the experimental group ( p = 0.005)" with g = 0.738.

"the improvement was statistically significant only for the experimental group ( p = 0.005). Furthermore, the effect size analysis revealed a moderate effect size (g = 0.738) for the improvement observed in the experimental group"

Özgür Tutal 2026 (2)¶

Özgür Tutal. (2026). Artificial intelligence assisted design of a novel cooperative learning technique for higher education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50770-1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test within the lecture-based control group (n = 36), pre-test X = 42.64 (SD = 10.03) to post-test X = 43.89 (SD = 10.76); the paired test was not significant (p = 0.613) and the article reports "a weak effect size (g = 0.119)".

"In contrast, the control group exhibited only a weak effect size (g = 0.119), indicating minimal practical significance in their score improvement."
