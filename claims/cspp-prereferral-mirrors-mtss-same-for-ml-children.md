# CSPP pre-referral processes mirror the K-12 MTSS approach and apply fundamentally the same steps to ML and non-ML children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviewees described an MTSS-style pre-referral process of multidisciplinary teams, baseline data review, and iterative interventions applied similarly to ML and non-ML children. [→ Burr 2023](#burr-2023)

## Evidence

### Burr 2023

Burr, E., Burho, J., & Perez, A. (2023). Pre-Referral Processes in California State Preschool Programs: How Practitioners Decide to Refer Multilingual Children for Special Education Evaluation. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655866

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of 13 coordinators overseeing CSPP, TK, or both across eight California LEAs. Interviewees reported that interpreters and bilingual specialists are involved for ML children, but "the steps of the process remain fundamentally the same."

> "The pre-referral process for determining whether to refer children in CSPP for special education evaluations consisted of similar steps for non-ML children and ML children alike."
