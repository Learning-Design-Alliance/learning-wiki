### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). Using machine learning to understand physics graduate school admissions. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.young](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.young)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Feature-importance analysis of a conditional inference random forest trained on fourteen application factors from 512 domestic applicants. Averaged importances over 30 runs showed factors such as "the applicant’s physics GRE score" and undergraduate GPA more important than any institution factor.

> "Using recursive backward elimi- nation to determine the meaningful factors, we ﬁnd the appli- cant’s physics GRE score and their undergraduate GPA to be the only meaningful factors."
