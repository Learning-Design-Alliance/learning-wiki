Discussion¶

The only entry here is a policy report's narrative summary of Cohen et al. (2009) and Goyer et al. (2017); it prints no effect size and does not mention null replications. A large same-district replication of the values-affirmation exercise, Hanselman et al. (2017), found no effect on grades; see A brief values-affirmation writing exercise raised African American seventh-graders' grades in two small field experiments, but a large same-district replication found no effect.
