## Evidence

### Luis E C Rocha 2018

Luis E C Rocha, Luana de F Nascimento. (2018). Assessing student's achievement gap between ethnic groups in Brazil. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baseline analysis of ENEM 2014 scores by self-declared sex, using the same income and parental-education categories. Gaps were "relatively larger gaps between male and female students" than ethnic gaps, roughly 6% to 10%, significant for all categories in Sao Paulo and low-income categories in Amazonas.

> "Here we observe relatively larger gaps between male and female students in comparison to gaps observed between ethnic groups in the previous sections (Table I and Table II). In all cases, male students perform better in mathematics whereas female students perform better in writing. There are no strong variations in the gaps between household income levels (approx. 6% to 10%)."
