## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). To Use or Not to Use Diagrams: The Effect of Drawing a Diagram in Solving Introductory Physics Problems. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified analysis of quiz scores by diagram-quality category on the one-dimensional electric field and force problem. T-tests compared students who drew expert-like diagrams with those who drew other diagrams, and the article reports both p values were small, showing statistically significant differences.

> "T-tests on the data in Table 4 reveal that students who drew expert-like diagrams (Categories 2 and 3) performed better than students who drew other diagrams (Category 1)."
