### Alhroub Wasef 2026

Alhroub Wasef, Jabareen Maaweya, Fasfoos Ahmad, Bseiso Omair, Deeik Joana, Ahmoud Ahmad Hasan, Al-Manasrah Amro K., Thalji Ibraheem Mohammed, Arish Ahmad Basil Abu, Adawi Iyad Moayad, Alqawasmeh Mustafa Murad, Hroub Monther M., Hroub Musbah, Ahmad Jamal. (2026). Lifestyle behaviors during exam periods and their association with academic performance among Palestinian medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09591-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of smoking before versus during exams in the 403-student sample: "Cigarette consumption tended to spike during exam periods", with light smokers decreasing and heavier-smoking categories increasing. These are small-count descriptive shifts with no test statistics.

> "Cigarette consumption tended to spike during exam periods. Outside of the exam, most cigarette smokers used low amounts, with approximately nine participants smoking one to five cigarettes per day and one participant smoking greater than 20 cigarettes"

### Alhroub Wasef 2026 (2)

Alhroub Wasef, Jabareen Maaweya, Fasfoos Ahmad, Bseiso Omair, Deeik Joana, Ahmoud Ahmad Hasan, Al-Manasrah Amro K., Thalji Ibraheem Mohammed, Arish Ahmad Basil Abu, Adawi Iyad Moayad, Alqawasmeh Mustafa Murad, Hroub Monther M., Hroub Musbah, Ahmad Jamal. (2026). Lifestyle behaviors during exam periods and their association with academic performance among Palestinian medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09591-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of waterpipe use frequency before versus during exams: outside exams use was occasional with no daily users, while during exams more frequent use was reported, including one person smoking daily. Small subgroup counts; no test statistics printed.

> "Waterpipe use followed a similar pattern: outside of the exam, the dominant use category was occasional (two people once a week, one person twice a week), and no one used it on a daily basis"
