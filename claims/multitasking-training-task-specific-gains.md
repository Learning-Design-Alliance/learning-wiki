## Evidence

### Angela D. Bender 2017

Angela D. Bender, Hannah L. Filmer, Claire K. Naughtin and Paul E. Dux. (2017). Dynamic, continuous multitasking training leads to task-speciﬁc improvements but does not transfer across action selection tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0015-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design session × group ANOVA on overall dual-task cost in the randomized experiment: the multitasking group's cost fell (15.86 to 8.79) while the single-task control's did not, with a significant "session × group interaction" and printed effect size ηp² = .185.

> "the improved multitasking performance was speciﬁc to the multitasking training group, as re ﬂected in the signiﬁcant session × group interaction ( F(1,37) = 8.42, p = .006, η2 P = .185)"
