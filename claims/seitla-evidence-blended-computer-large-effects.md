Random-effects subgroup analysis (Table 5) ranking SEITLA approaches by effect size on learning achievement. Evidence-based learning (1 study, g = 1.94), blended learning (1 study, g = 1.48) and computer-based learning (3 studies, g = 1.46) each showed significant effects at p < 0.01.
