## Evidence

### Lockie R.G. 2019

Lockie R.G., Balfany K., Bloodgood A.M., Moreno M.R., Cesario K.A., Dulla J.M., Dawes J.J., Orr R.M. (2019). The Influence of Physical Fitness on Reasons for Academy Separation in Law Enforcement Recruits. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16030372

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc comparisons across five academy classes (330 GRAD vs 18 SEPFI recruits). The article reports the between-group 75PR difference as significant with "a moderate effect"; Table 2 prints the GRAD-SEPFI 75PR effect size as 0.72.

> "With regards to the 75PR, the GRAD group was significantly faster than the SEPFI (p = 0.02) group, and this had a moderate effect."
