Wang Zhizhuo, Wu Peiyun, Shi Shaoyun, Zhang Weiwei, Lin Cheng. (2025). Experiences and perceptions of students in occupational therapy regarding the use of desktop virtual environments-based simulation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07495-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the thematic analysis of student interviews: a participant explained that "virtual simulation is better functional as a post-class rather than an in-class learning strategy", positioning it as reinforcement of PowerPoint-based knowledge acquisition.

> "The conventional PowerPoint learning can build the foundation of knowledge acquisition, while the virtual simulation can cement it for reinforcement. To be precise, virtual simulation is better functional as a post-class rather than an in-class learning strategy."
