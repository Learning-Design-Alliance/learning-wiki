# Encoding and retrieval P3 amplitudes are significantly correlated only in non-ADHD adolescents, though the between-group difference in correlations was not statistically discriminative

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Encoding early-P3 amplitude correlated with retrieval P3 amplitude across trial types, with the significant correlations driven by the non-ADHD group (r = 0.51 Same; r = 0.50 Different); ADHD correlations were not significant. [→ Ortega 2020](#ortega-2020)

## Evidence

### Ortega 2020

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

`q3 · i3`

Pearson correlations between encoding early-P3 and retrieval P3 amplitudes in the same sample: significant in the non-ADHD group (Same r = 0.50955, p = 0.03077; Different r = 0.49540, p = 0.03656) but "no significant correlation" in the ADHD group (r = 0.35457 and r = 0.25418, both ns).

> "Follow up analysis showed that these significant correlations were driven by the results from the non-ADHD group: Same (r = 0.50955, p = 0.03077) and Different (r = 0.49540, p = 0.03656). The equivalent analysis in the ADHD group showed no significant correlation: Same (r = 0.35457, p = 0.14881) and Different (r = 0.25418, p = 0.30877)"
