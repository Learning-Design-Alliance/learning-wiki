## Evidence

### Serçe Hüseyin 2026

Serçe Hüseyin, Aydoğmuş Mevlüt, Şentürk Cihad. (2026). Psychological predictors of professional readiness: modeling the effects of preservice teachers' 21st-century skills on self-efficacy and professional attitudes. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05039-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (N = 401) preceding the SEM; the composite 21st-century skills score showed moderate positive correlations with all self-efficacy subscales and attitudes, with "teacher self-efficacy (r = 0.49, p < 0.01)" the strongest.

> "the composite measure of 21st-century skills (total) revealed moderate positive correlations with student engagement (r = 0.42, p < 0.01), instructional strategies (r = 0.42, p < 0.01), classroom management (r = 0.45, p < 0.01), teacher self-efficacy (r = 0.49, p < 0.01) and attitudes toward teaching (r = 0.35, p < 0.01)."
