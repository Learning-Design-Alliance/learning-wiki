# The amount of corrective feedback an individual learner received correlated positively with that learner's post-test performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Among the 15 experimental-group learners, the amount of feedback received showed a strong positive Pearson correlation with post-test score (r = .747). [→ Nowbakht and Shahnazari 2015](#nowbakht-and-shahnazari-2015)

## Evidence

### Nowbakht and Shahnazari 2015

Mohammad Nowbakht, Mohammadtaghi Shahnazari. (2015). The Comparative Effects of Comprehensible Input, Output and Corrective Feedback on the Receptive Acquisition of L2 Vocabulary Items. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.103

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation within the experimental group (N = 15) between amount of feedback received during treatment and post-test score; the effect size was r = .747, significant at the 0.01 level (p = .001), leading the authors to reject their null hypothesis.

> "The results of the Pearson correlation show that there is a positive correlation (.747) between the amounts of feedback given to each individual learner and his performance in the post-test."
