Question-type analysis of the same TAQE administration, with questions classified into 4 categories (11 graph-based, 17 complex, 95 reverse logic, 177 standard). Both models scored below the passing threshold on graph-based items.

> "Question-type analysis revealed that both models underperformed on graph-based questions, with DeepSeek scoring 4 out of 11 (36% correct) and ChatGPT scoring 2 out of 11 (18% correct), both below the passing threshold."
