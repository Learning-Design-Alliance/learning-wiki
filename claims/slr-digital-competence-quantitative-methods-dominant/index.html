Zhao Yu 2021¶

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review analysis of research methods (Table 3) across the 33 selected publications. The review reports "24 of them used quantitative methodology" with questionnaire data collection, four applied partial least squares structural equation modeling, two qualitative, two mixed methods, and one Delphi study.

"It is important to note that 24 of them used quantitative methodology to investigate digital competence related content, and the collection of data was performed through a questionnaire."
