What Works Clearinghouse 2009¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). Headsprout® Early Reading (WWC Intervention Report). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/211

q2 · i? · review · r3

WWC review of 13 identified studies: one RCT met standards; 11 did not meet standards or eligibility screens; one single-subject design could not be reviewed. The positive-effects rating criterion requiring two or more studies was explicitly not met because only one study met evidence standards.

"One study of Headsprout ® Early Reading meets What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) evidence standards and no studies meet WWC evidence standards with reservations."
