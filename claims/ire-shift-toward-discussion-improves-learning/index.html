The primer's research-summary section asserts, citing Clarke et al. (2015), Dragnic-Cindric et al. (2024), Gillies (2019), and Windschitl & Calabrese Barton (2016), that "a shift away from IRE towards student participation in classroom discussion" significantly improves learning. No effect size is printed; the primer reports no data of its own.
