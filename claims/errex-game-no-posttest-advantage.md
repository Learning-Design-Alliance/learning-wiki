## Evidence

### Mogessie et al. 2020

Mogessie, M., Richey, J. E., McLaren, B. M., Andres-Bray, J. M. L., & Baker, R. S. (2020). Confrustion and Gaming While Learning with Erroneous Examples in a Decimals Game. Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_38

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-condition ANOVAs on test scores for 191 students in the Decimal Point game found "no significant differences across conditions on pretest" or posttest; equivalence was not tested. The authors state this did not replicate the earlier web-based tutor finding of better learning with erroneous examples.

> "A series of ANOVAs indicated no significant differences across conditions on pretest, F(3, 187) = 1.63, p = .18, or posttest, F(3. 187) = 1.65, p = .18."
