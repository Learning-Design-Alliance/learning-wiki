Evidence¶

Michael Rowe 2026¶

Michael Rowe. (2026). Problem-based learning and the structural conditions for productive AI integration. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/haet3_v1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in a conceptual essay: assessment systems treated artefacts as inferential evidence of learning, and AI-generated polished outputs no longer require the engagement they were designed to evidence. The essay calls this "a Goodhart dynamic".

"AI breaks the artefact-as-proxy for learning. The correlation between artefact quality and intellectual engagement has collapsed, not because AI is powerful, but because the proxy was already vulnerable and AI removed the friction that was masking it."
