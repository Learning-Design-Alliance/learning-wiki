### Junod Perron Noelle 2016

Junod Perron Noelle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). The quality of feedback during formative OSCEs depends on the tutors' profile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0815-x
