### Shiner N 2023

Shiner N. (2023). Time for a simulation strategy? Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.685

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Ketterer et al.'s randomised controlled trial of simulated versus traditional therapeutic radiography placements, statistically significant improvements in students' ability to communicate with patients on clinical placement. No effect size is printed.

> "Ketterer et al. 9 identified statistically significant improvements in the students' ability to communicate with patients on clinical placement, following simulations using a virtual environment for radiotherapy training, and simulated patients in a radiotherapy setting."
