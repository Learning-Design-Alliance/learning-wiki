## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2014

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2014). Developing an Interactive Tutorial on a Quantum Eraser. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pretest finding from the preliminary in-class evaluation of the quantum eraser QuILT: students expected fewer photons to reach the detectors, but they "incorrectly claimed that all of the photons reaching the detector would display interference."

> "42% of the students correctly claimed that the single polarizer would absorb some of the photons and thus fewer photons would reach the detectors, but they incorrectly claimed that all of the photons reaching the detector would display interference."
