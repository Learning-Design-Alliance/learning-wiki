## Evidence

### Akgül Nilgün 2025

Akgül Nilgün, Yilmaz Ebru, Akkurt Onur. (2025). Evaluation of Nursing Students' Knowledge on Dental Injuries. Dental Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1111/edt.13046

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher–Freeman–Halton comparison by year of study (test statistic 15.535). Overall, 124 students (41.8%) said the vaccine should be administered, 3 said no, and 164 were unsure; "Third‐year students, at a rate of 65.2%, were the group most in favor."

> "There is a statistically significant difference in the comparison of responses to the question regarding the administration of a tetanus vaccine after trauma (p = 0.01). Third‐year students, at a rate of 65.2%, were the group most in favor of administering a tetanus vaccine."
