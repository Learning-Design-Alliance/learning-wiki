## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Q tests were significant and I2 values of 85 (learning performance) and 69 (academic emotion) indicate medium-to-high heterogeneity, justifying a random effects model. [→ Tan Jing 2021](#tan-jing-2021)

## Evidence

### Tan Jing 2021

Tan Jing, Mao Jie, Jiang Yizhang, Gao Ming. (2021). The Influence of Academic Emotions on Learning Effects: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18189678

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity tests of the two outcome variables (Table 4: Q = 524.68, p < 0.00001, I2 = 85, Tau2 = 0.36 for learning performance; Q = 172.78, I2 = 69 for academic emotion). The review classifies both as "medium or high" heterogeneity.

> "For learning performance, the I2 value is 85, indicating that the variation caused by a real difference in effect size accounts for 85% of the total variation in learning performance."
