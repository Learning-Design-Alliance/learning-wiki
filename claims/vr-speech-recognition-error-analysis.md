## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 1,037 answers tracked in interaction logs, 42.7% were correctly allocated and 57.3% were errors; 75% of errors were system errors and 24.6% concept errors. [→ Graf Linda 2026](#graf-linda-2026)

Objective log-file analysis of VR conversations for 14 participants tracked by interaction logs, distinguishing concept errors (no predefined answer) from system errors (wrong allocation by wit.ai).

### Graf Linda 2026 (2)

Graf Linda, Golz Vera, Prehn Sophia, Masuch Maic, Gradl-Dietsch Gertraud. (2026). Virtual vs. standardized patients - a preliminary comparison of advantages and disadvantages of a VR and a conventional learning unit for medical students in child and adolescent psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08760-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Specification of system errors into four types in the log analysis; other causes were wording deviating from pre-formulated alternatives (24.2%), input interpreted too early (16.3%), and speech directed at the experimenter (12.5%).

> "Table 5 shows that most errors (37.1%) occurred because the system misinterpreted short utterances such as "Ehm" or "ok" - intended merely as acknowledgments - as new questions addressed to the virtual patient."
