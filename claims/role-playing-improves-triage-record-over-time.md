## Evidence

### Seo Yon Hee 2024

Seo Yon Hee, Lim Sun-Og. (2024). Korean Triage and Acuity Scale education using role-playing and its effects on triage competency: A quasi-experimental design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group repeated-measures comparison of triage record scores (12-item tool, CVI 0.91) for the 39 role-playing students only. The article reports the average score "increasing from 17.08 ± 2.45 at the first recording to 19.26 ± 2.06 at the second recording"; several sub-items also improved significantly.

> "The comparison of the triage record scores of the experimental group over time showed a significant difference, with the average score increasing from 17.08 ± 2.45 at the first recording to 19.26 ± 2.06 at the second recording (t = 0.561, p < .01)."
