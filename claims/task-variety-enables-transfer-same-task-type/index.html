q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

A theoretical assertion in the task-progression section of this conceptual paper, offered without empirical test; the article states that "learners acquire the ability to transfer their knowledge and skill to yet unencountered tasks of this type" through varied same-type tasks.

"It is by learning to do a variety of different tasks of the same type that learners acquire the ability to transfer their knowledge and skill to yet unencountered tasks of this type."
