## Evidence

### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of PISE survey scores between TWA and TWG students before and during COVID using t-tests and Cohen's d. The article reports "the gap actually grew, with the effect size increasing from 0.29 to 0.49" between the two periods.

> "However, when it came to Peer Influence on Self -Efficacy (PISE), the difference between TWA and TWG students stuck around. In fact, the gap actually grew, with the effect size increasing from 0.29 to 0.49."
