## Evidence

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative individual user-testing study of five junior oncologists (age range 31-37; 3 women) who tested interactive prototypes of an e-learning and a chatbot with a think-out-loud protocol. The study reports that "four expressed a willingness to use a digital training tool"; no effect size is printed.

> "Results revealed that, of the five participants, four expressed a willingness to use a digital training tool to acquire information-provision skills."
