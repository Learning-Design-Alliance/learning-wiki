Ünlüsoy, A., de Haan, M.J., Leander, K., & Völker, B. (2013). Learning potential in youth's online networks: A multilevel approach. Computers & Education, 69, 522-533. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.06.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analyses revealed a divide between variances of network activities and perceived learning potential, showing that individual-level analysis alone is insufficient for understanding digital learning.

> "The divide between the variances of our two dependent variables -network activities and perceived learning potential- underpin that if we want to understand how young people learn in digital environments, merely looking at the individual level is not enough."
