PROCESS Model 8 moderated mediation in the 5764-participant survey; the printed interaction coefficients show moderation "for escape motivation (b = −0.163, p < 0.001)" and the other two paths, with positive dependence–motivation associations weakening as literacy increased.

> "The interaction terms between AI dependence and AI literacy were significant for escape motivation (b = −0.163, p < 0.001), social motivation (b = −0.135, p < 0.001), and entertainment motivation (b = −0.045, p < 0.001), but not for instrumental motivation (b = 0.014, p = 0.228)."
