## Evidence

### Hawley Chelsea E. 2019

Hawley Chelsea E., Triantafylidis Laura K., Phillips Sarah C., Schwartz Andrea Wershof. (2019). Brown Bag Simulation to Improve Medication Management in Older Adults. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10857

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre/post evaluation of the final simulation with a class of 137 second-year medical students; 47 (34%) completed the optional presurvey and 32 (23%) the postsurvey. Confidence was rated on a 5-point Likert scale, with learners rating 4 or 5 counted as "confident"; chi-square analysis compared pre/post counts. The article reports "a significant increase across all four self-efficacy domains (p < .001)" with no effect size printed.

> "The average number of learners confident about medication management in older adults increased overall by 41%, with a significant increase across all four self-efficacy domains (p < .001)."
