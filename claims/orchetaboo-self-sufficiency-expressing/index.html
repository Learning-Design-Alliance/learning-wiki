Akkuzu 2016¶

Akkuzu, N. & Uyulgan, M.A. (2016). How to Improve Students' Comprehension concerning the Major Terms of Functional Groups?--In the Experiment of OrCheTaboo Game. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n2p196

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Interview question on perceived sufficiency, analyzed in categories (Table 9). The article states the game is effective on students' developing their chemistry language, since expressing ability was the leading reason given by students who felt sufficient.

"When the answers were given to the question “Do you consider yourself sufficient during the game OrCheTaboo while describing the term?” by the experimental group students, we found out that 53.0% (f: 17) of them consider themselves sufficient"
