Post hoc linkage of IC measures with previously obtained OSCE scores in the 21-student sample showed borderline associations with the VR station and none with the analog overall OSCE; only correlation coefficients and P values are printed, so no effect size is coded.

> "While both general and specific IC were moderately associated with performance in the VR-OSCE station at a borderline level of significance (r=0.41; P=.06 and r=0.37; P=.09, respectively), neither correlated meaningfully with overall OSCE performance (r=0.16; P=.48 and r=0.02; P=.92, respectively)."
