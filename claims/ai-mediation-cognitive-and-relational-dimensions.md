Inductive thematic analysis of co-design workshop transcripts and written artifacts from 12 university students in four interdisciplinary groups who first completed a 15-minute Discord probe session; the analysis yielded two overarching themes, one of which was that beyond cognitive organization, participants "valuedSpritz for softening the interpersonal pressure surrounding disagreement". No effect sizes are reported.
