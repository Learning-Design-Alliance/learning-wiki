## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The average number of non-capitalization of proper nouns errors rose from 0.08 (pre-LSA, N=87) to 0.34 (post-LSA, N=85), a statistically significant increase (p < 0.01). [→ Austin 2017](#austin-2017)

## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, T. L., Sigmar, L. S., Mehta, G. B., & Shirk, J. L. (2017). The impact of web-assisted instruction on student writing outcomes in business communication. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies, 20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1178728

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on non-capitalization of proper nouns errors: pre-LSA mean 0.08 (SD 0.463, N=87) vs. post-LSA mean 0.34 (SD 0.589, N=85), t = -3.224, p = 0.002.
