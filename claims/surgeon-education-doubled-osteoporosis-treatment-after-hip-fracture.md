# An education program for orthopedic surgeons more than doubled osteoporosis detection and treatment initiation rates after hip fracture

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Osteoporosis detection after hip fracture increased from 20.1% in the 2005 retrospective cohort to 45% in the 2007 prospective cohort after surgeons attended education sessions (P < 0.001). [→ Kim S-R 2011](#kim-s-r-2011)
`q2 i?` Initiation of osteoporosis medication at discharge increased from 15.1% in 2005 to 32.2% in 2007. [→ Kim S-R 2011](#kim-s-r-2011)

## Evidence

### Kim S-R 2011

Kim S-R, Ha Y-C, Park Y-G, Lee S-R, Koo K-H. (2011). Orthopedic Surgeon's Awareness Can Improve Osteoporosis Treatment Following Hip Fracture: A Prospective Cohort Study. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2011.26.11.1501

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective before-after cohort study of 208 hip-fracture patients at eight Jeju hospitals in 2007, compared with the authors' 2005 retrospective cohort of 174 patients, after 22 surgeons received education sessions, posters and brochures. The article reports "45% vs 20.1%" detection and "32.2% vs 15.1%" treatment initiation, with P < 0.001 for detection.

> "In 2007 the rate of osteoporosis detection was greater (P < 0.001) than that in 2005 (45% vs 20.1%) and the initiation rate of osteoporosis treatment also had increased (32.2% vs 15.1%)"
