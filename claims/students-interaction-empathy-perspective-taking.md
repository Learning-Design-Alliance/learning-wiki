## Evidence

### Sherman 2020

Sherman, P., & Boukydis, O. (2020). Framing Undergraduate Perspectives on Experiential Learning Within Soka Education Theory. IAFOR Journal of Education: Undergraduate Education, 8(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1272497 [doi:10.22492/ije.8.3.05](https://doi.org/10.22492/ije.8.3.05)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Study 2 thematic analysis of twelve student interviews produced Global Theme 1 from four organizing themes covering connection, community contribution, new perspectives, and empathy. One student described happiness from study abroad spreading as a "chain reaction".

> "Whether, for example, through interactive classroom learning, practicum placements, or study abroad, the practice of connecting with others enhances perspective, fosters empathy, and encourages a sense of community."
