### Kohl 1911

Kohl, C. C., & Hughes, P. (1911). Communications and discussions. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

`q1 · i?` · `design · r?`

Institutional observation cited in the marking-systems communication: after adopting the standard scale, "the percentage of failures continued below 3 per cent, instead of approaching the 10 per cent." No formal statistics are printed; the figures are descriptive percentages.

> "when the Michigan Department of Literature, Science and the Arts adopted the scale suggested above, there was an approach to the percentages named, except that the percentage of failures continued below 3 per cent, instead of approaching the 10 per cent, required by a symmetrical imagination"
