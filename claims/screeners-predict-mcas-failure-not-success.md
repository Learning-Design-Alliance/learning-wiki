# Literacy screener benchmarks predict which grade 3 students will not meet MCAS ELA expectations, but not necessarily who will meet them

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About 8 percent of students identified as at any level of risk at end of grade 3 met or exceeded MCAS ELA standards, versus about 60 percent of students who met screening benchmarks — so about 40 percent of benchmark-meeting students were 'false positives.' [→ Lemke 2023](#lemke-2023)

## Evidence

### Lemke 2023

Lemke, M., Murphy, D., Soo Ping Chow, A., Spencer, H., & Zhang, A. (2023). Comparing early literacy screening assessment benchmarks in Massachusetts. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638508

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression-based linking study of grade 3 screening benchmarks to MCAS ELA (2021/22 data, more than 4,000 students). Estimates of association show 'strong to very strong associations between the tests,' yet about 40 percent of benchmark-meeting students did not meet or only partially met MCAS expectations.

> "About 8 percent of students identified as at any level of risk on a literacy screening assessment at end of year (EOY) in grade 3 met or exceeded MCAS ELA standards, while about 60 percent who met screening assessment benchmarks met or exceeded expectations on MCAS (see Figure 3)."
