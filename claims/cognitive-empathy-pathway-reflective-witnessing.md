## Evidence

### Alia Hamdan 2024

Alia Hamdan, Ash Bista, Dina Newman, Scott Franklin. (2024). A Conceptual Framework for Understanding Empathy in Physics faculty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Constructivist grounded theory analysis of four rounds of faculty interviews; Results section defines the cognitive-pathway mediator. The paper states reflective witnessing is 'an explicitly cognitive process', and that shared experiences and emotions moderate it without being essential, as shown in Dylan's support of a neurodivergent student.

> "Reflective witnessing involves active listening, challenging narratives and assumptions and, importantly, self-differentiation in acknowledging one's own biases and experiences as different from another's."
