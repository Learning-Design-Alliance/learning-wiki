## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bacon Paul 2021

Bacon Paul, Lord Rachel N. (2021). The impact of physically active learning during the school day on children's physical activity levels, time on task and learning behaviours and academic outcomes. Health Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyab020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Momentary time-sampling observation (2-second sweeps with an in-ear metronome, behaviours marked on-task or off-task) during PAL and NAL teaching sessions in the counterbalanced design; the difference was significant at P < 0.001. No effect size printed.

> "There was a statistically significant 10% improvement (P < 0.001) 10% in ToT, suggesting greater attention to task during PAL (97%) than NAL (87%, see Table VI)."
