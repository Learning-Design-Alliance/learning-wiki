Motameni 2018¶

Motameni, R. (2018). The Combined Impact of the Flipped Classroom, Collaborative Learning, on Students' Learning of key Marketing Concepts. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 15(3). https://ro.uow.edu.au/jutlp/vol15/iss3/4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Correlational analysis of grades in the flipped experimental sections, comparing 'Flipped Total Assessment Grade' and 'Collaborative Marketing Plan Project Grade'. The article reports "correlation was equal to .964" with p-value = .000; the correlation is descriptive, not causal.

"The correlation analysis between these variables indicated that correlation was equal to .964. The result also indicated that the Pearson Correlation Coefficient was statistically significant (p-value = .000)."
