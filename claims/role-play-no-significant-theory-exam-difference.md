## Evidence

### Huang et al. 2018

Simin Huang, Peng Xu, Lie Feng, Chunting Lu & Jing Yang. (2018). An Effective Teaching Method to Enhance History-Taking Skills for Chinese Medical Students. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p144

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-semester theoretical examination covering history-taking and symptomatology knowledge, with medical terms, fill-in-the-blanks, multiple-choice, and case-analysis parts, scored out of 100 with blinded teacher evaluation. The difference between groups was not significant (P = 0.054); equivalence was not tested.

> "Comparison of theory examination result showed that the score in the observation group ( 74.06 ±  5.66) was higher than that in the control group (69.88 ±  10.98). However, the difference between the two groups was not significant (P = 0.054)"
