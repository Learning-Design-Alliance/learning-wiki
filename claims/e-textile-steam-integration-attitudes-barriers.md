## Evidence

### Mustafa Sat 2026

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attitude questionnaire (N = 36, 5-point scale) on interdisciplinary integration. The article reports "88.9% agreed or strongly agreed that limited resources (such as time and funding) made it difficult to implement new approaches" versus 38.9% citing curriculum standards; interviews cited inadequate spaces, materials, cost, and exam pressures.

> "88.9% agreed or strongly agreed that limited resources (such as time and funding) made it difficult to implement new approaches, like e-textiles-supported STEAM activities."

### Mustafa Sat 2026 (2)

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same attitude questionnaire (N = 36). Participants showed low reform skepticism (94.4% disagreed it was just another failing reform) and 100% willingness to receive training in interdisciplinary curriculum concepts.

> "Many agreed or strongly agreed that an interdisciplinary framework could improve their current curriculum (97.2%) and support the school’s learning goals (91.7%)."
