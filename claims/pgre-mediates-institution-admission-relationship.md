## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In bootstrapped logistic mediation models, the physics GRE partially mediates the selectivity–admission relationship and fully mediates the institution-size–admission relationship; GPA is not a significant mediator for either. ## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). The Physics GRE does not help applicants “stand out”. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5000 bootstrap samples, percentile 95% CIs) with logistic regression on admission status for the institutional features. The article finds the physics GRE "partially mediates the relationship between the selectivity" and admission and fully mediates the size–admission relationship; GPA mediation CIs contained zero.

> "We ﬁnd that the applicant’s physics GRE score partially mediates the relationship between the selectivity of their undergraduate institution and whether they were admitted and fully mediates the relationship between their institution’s size and whether they were admitted."
