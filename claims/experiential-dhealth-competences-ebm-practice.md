## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Experiential dHealth competences are significantly positively associated with medical students' practice of EBM (β = 0.42, p < 0.001). [→ Gerit Wagner 2025](#gerit-wagner-2025)

## Evidence

### Gerit Wagner 2025

Gerit Wagner, Mickaël Ringeval, Louis Raymond, Guy Paré. (2025). Digital health competences and AI beliefs as conditions for the practice of evidence-based medicine: a study of prospective physicians in Canada. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2459910

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 177 Université de Montréal medical students analyzed with PLS-SEM. The study reports "experiential dHealth competences was also strongly correlated with the practice of EBM (β = 0.42, p < 0.001)" while knowledge competences were not significant (β = 0.01).

> "The construct of experiential dHealth competences was also strongly correlated with the practice of EBM (β = 0.42, p < 0.001), while dHealth knowledge competences were not significantly related to the dependent variable (β = 0.01, p > 0.05, not significant), offering partial support for Hypothesis 1 (H1)."
