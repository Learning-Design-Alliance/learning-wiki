## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' interpretive analysis (not a tested result) linking the flipped Readiness Assessment Process to mastery-experience self-efficacy. They note "students know that they have been successful on the quiz immediately after they have completed it, as the team portion using the IF-AT cards provides immediate feedback" and that average individual readiness quiz scores are high (78% is the B threshold).

> "This immediate veriﬁcation that they can learn physics, on their own, would seem to be a reasonably strong mastery experience source of self-eﬃcacy."
