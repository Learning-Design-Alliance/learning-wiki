## Evidence

### Valdemar Stenberdt 2025

Valdemar Stenberdt, Ban Mouid Shiwalia, Guido Makransky. (2025). Re-Experiencing vs. Self-Explaining: Comparing Generative Learning Activities in VR. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10096-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory within-group correlation analyses (Appendix G) conducted after Welch t-tests found no between-group differences on other measured psychological variables. The correlations were specific to the re-experiencing group, suggesting embodiment "might be specifically relevant" there; exploratory, not confirmatory.

> "significant correlations between embodiment and written procedural knowledge (r = 0.34) and post-test self-efficacy ( r = 0.37) for this group, even after applying Holm correction."
