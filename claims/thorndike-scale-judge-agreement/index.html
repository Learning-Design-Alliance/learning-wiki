The reviewer's assessment of Thorndike's monograph reports that agreement among ten observers rating samples against the scale was notable: "the average will give a very accurate and constant determination". No effect size is printed.

"Yet it is surprising to note ihe agreement in the judgments of ten different observers, and the average will give a very accurate and constant determination"
