# Prior AI education shows no family-wise association with any baseline perception outcome, with at most small effect sizes, under both three-group and two-group codings

## Evidence

### Yongkyung Oh 2026

Yongkyung Oh, Lynn Talton, Alex Bui. (2026). Engagement Intensity as a Learner-Modeling Signal for Adaptive AI Ethics Instruction. KDD 2026 AI for Education Day, at the ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD '26). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18548

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis and Mann–Whitney analyses of the same 91-trainee pre-instruction survey under three-group (formal/informal/none) and collapsed two-group codings of prior AI education. All five Holm-corrected tests are non-significant and printed Cliff's δ values are below .19, i.e. negligible-to-small effect sizes.

> "A collapsed two-group contrast (any prior versus none), reported in the same table, reaches the identical conclusion (minimum𝑝Holm =.524 ) with at most small effect sizes (Cliff’s|𝛿|<.19 )."
