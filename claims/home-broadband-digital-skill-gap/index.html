Weaver 2022¶

Weaver, D'Andre J., Ph.D. (2022). Delivering on the Promise of Digital Equity. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/38939984-18ea-4af0-8081-c787ac30d48d

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report cites a study of rural Michigan school districts: "students with no home broadband access or who only use a cell phone to access the internet are on the losing end of a digital skill gap" equal to roughly three grade levels versus peers with slow home internet. The same study found these students less frequently plan to transition to colleges and universities.

"A study focused on rural school districts in Michigan found that students with no home broadband access or who only use a cell phone to access the internet are on the losing end of a digital skill gap when compared to those with even slow home internet access: about the difference in skills between an eighth and 11th grader."
