## Evidence

### Rohmer 2022

Rohmer, O., Doignon‐Camus, N., Audusseau, J., Trautmann, S., Chaillou, A.‐C., & Popa‐Roch, M. (2022). Removing the academic framing in student evaluations improves achievement in children with dyslexia: The mediating role of self‐judgement of competence. Dyslexia, 28(3), 309–324. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary ANOVA on the 183-student sample tested difficulty equivalence of the literacy task formats. The article reports a significant dyslexia × task-type interaction (F [1,181] = 6.39, p = .012), with a within-group difference only for students with dyslexia (t [30] = 3.71, p < .01; students without dyslexia t [151] = 1.55, p = .12). No effect size was printed.

> "An ANOVA with Dyslexia (with vs. without dyslexia) as between factor and the type of task (academic vs. non‐academic) as within factor revealed a significant interaction (F [1,181] = 6.39, p = .012)."
