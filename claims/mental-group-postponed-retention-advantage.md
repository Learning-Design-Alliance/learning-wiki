## Evidence

### Al-Balushi 2018

Al-Balushi, K. A., & Al-Balushi, S. M. (2018). Effectiveness of Brain-Based Learning for Grade Eight Students’ Direct and Postponed Retention in Science. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 525-538. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11336a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Postponed-test MANCOVA in the same quasi-experimental study detected significant differences between the three groups in knowledge (f=2.98), application (f=4.81), reasoning (f=4.81) and overall performance (f=4.13); LSD comparisons favoured the mental group, described as "in favour of the mental group".

> "The ad -hoc LSD analysis indicates that there were significant differences between the mental and control groups in the knowledge level, reasoning level and  the overall performance in the postponed test (in favour of the mental group)."
