## Evidence

### Graf Linda 2026

Graf Linda, Golz Vera, Prehn Sophia, Masuch Maic, Gradl-Dietsch Gertraud. (2026). Virtual vs. standardized patients - a preliminary comparison of advantages and disadvantages of a VR and a conventional learning unit for medical students in child and adolescent psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08760-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with 43 students (17 speakers, 26 observers) comparing both units, coded independently by three authors with consensus meetings. Reasons for preferring the conventional unit: insufficient speech recognition (56), lack of interaction (33), no real human (15), inexplicable reasons (14).

> "Results yielded in the majority of the students (81.4%) preferring the conventional unit with the standardized patient, confirming the quantitative results. 6.98% chose the VR unit with the virtual patient, 9.30% were indecisive"
