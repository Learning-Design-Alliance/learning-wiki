# Domain-specific ASQ gains favoured Montessori but were not statistically significant for communication, gross motor, fine motor, or problem solving

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Gross motor showed a non-significant but sizable gain of 3.6 points (p = 0.27) for the Montessori group. [→ Tobin 2015](#tobin-2015)
`q2 i?` Fine motor showed a net gain of 2.5 points (p = 0.56) and problem solving a difference of 4.3 points (p = 0.49) favouring the Montessori group; communication showed a gain of 0.05 points (p = 0.99). [→ Tobin 2015 (2)](#tobin-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Tobin 2015

Tobin, T., Boulmier, P., Zhu, W., Hancock, P., & Muennig, P. (2015). Improving outcomes for refugee children: A case study on the impact of Montessori education along the Thai-Burma border. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 14(3). http://openjournals.library.usyd.edu.au/index.php/IEJ/index

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the difference-in-difference analysis of the gross motor domain, the Montessori school improved by 4.5 points versus less than 1 point for control, "resulting in a non-significant but sizable gain of 3.6 points (p = 0.27)".

> "During the follow up, the students from the Montessori school improved their scores on the gross motor assessment by 4.5 points, whereas the students from the control school improved by less than 1 point, resulting in a non-significant but sizable gain of 3.6 points (p = 0.27)."

### Tobin 2015 (2)

Tobin, T., Boulmier, P., Zhu, W., Hancock, P., & Muennig, P. (2015). Improving outcomes for refugee children: A case study on the impact of Montessori education along the Thai-Burma border. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 14(3). http://openjournals.library.usyd.edu.au/index.php/IEJ/index

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

For the fine motor domain, the control group led by 9.5 points at baseline; at follow-up the Montessori group was "less than 7 points lower than those in the control group, for a net gain of 2.5 points (p = 0.56)". Problem solving and communication showed similarly non-significant gains.

> "In the follow up, the Montessori group scored less than 7 points lower than those in the control group, for a net gain of 2.5 points (p = 0.56)."
