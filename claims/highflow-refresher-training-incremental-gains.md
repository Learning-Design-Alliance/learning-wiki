## Evidence

### Ellington Laura E 2020

Ellington Laura E, Becerra Rosario, Mallma Gabriela, Tantaleán da Fieno José, Nair Deepthi, Onchiri Frankline, Nielsen Katie R. (2020). Knowledge acquisition and retention after a high flow training programme in Peru: a quasi-experimental single group pre–post design. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-035125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated cross-sectional assessments in the single group pre–post study: post-refresher acquisition comparisons were significant for 3-month (p=0.003) and 12-month (p<0.001) training. Despite between-session knowledge decline, refresher sessions lifted overall scores above the 80% minimum competency threshold.

> "Participant overall performance exceeded baseline performance after 3-month and 12-month refresher training sessions, (78% (70–87), 83% (74–87) and 87% (83–91) at baseline, 3 and 12 months, respectively), demonstrating incremental improvement and achieving minimum competency after 3-month and 12-month refresher training sessions"
