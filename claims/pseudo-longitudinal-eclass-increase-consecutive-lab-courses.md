### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Improvement or selection? A longitudinal analysis of students' views about experimental physics in their lab courses. arXiv:1706.03681. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pseudo-longitudinal analysis of all students in each course over eight semesters at CU Boulder (N = 2306, 188, 221), reported in Table III, titled "Pseudo-longitudinal E-CLASS averages (max score 30)". It reports preinstruction averages of 18.1, 19.5, and 20.1 and postinstruction averages of 15.8, 17.6, and 20.2.

> "Pseudo-longitudinal E-CLASS averages (max score 30) for all students who took each lab course during the data collection period."
