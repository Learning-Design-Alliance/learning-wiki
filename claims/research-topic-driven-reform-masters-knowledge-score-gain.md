## Evidence

### Xing Wenjing 2026

Xing Wenjing, Ju Huanyu, Pei Chunying, Guo Yongli, Tian Linlu, Ling Hong, Guo Tieyun, Cao Bo, Ma Jian. (2026). Research topic–driven curriculum content reform in graduate immunology to strengthen students' research and innovation competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08711-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Historical cohort comparison of basic knowledge test scores for master's students before (2020–2021) versus after (2022–2024) the reform; the article reports a significant improvement, "with scores increasing from 88.471 ± 3.145 (pre-reform) to 95.640 ± 3.053 (post-reform)" (Fig. 2C reports t = 7.38, p < 0.000001).

> "comparison of immunology basic knowledge test scores between master's students in the 2020 and 2021 cohorts (pre-reform) and the 2022, 2023, and 2024 cohorts (post-reform) revealed a significant improvement among master's students after the reform, with scores increasing from 88.471 ± 3.145 (pre-reform) to 95.640 ± 3.053 (post-reform)"

### Xing Wenjing 2026 (2)

Xing Wenjing, Ju Huanyu, Pei Chunying, Guo Yongli, Tian Linlu, Ling Hong, Guo Tieyun, Cao Bo, Ma Jian. (2026). Research topic–driven curriculum content reform in graduate immunology to strengthen students' research and innovation competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08711-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same historical cohort comparison, PhD subgroup (Fig. 2C: n = 4 pre-reform, n = 10 post-reform, t = 1.987, p = 0.07). The article reports the change "did not reach statistical significance"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "although PhD students' scores rose from 91.500 ± 2.380 (pre-reform) to 94.800 ± 2.936 (post-reform), this change did not reach statistical significance"
