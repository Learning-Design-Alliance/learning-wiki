## Evidence

### Rolland-Debord C 2026

Rolland-Debord C, Juret L, Simon M, El Mouhajer A, Londner C, Morélot-Panzini C, Chenivesse C, Similowski T. (2026). Impact of a Structured Training Program on Medical Student Confidence and Behavior During Their First Radial Arterial Puncture: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78086

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Patient-rated 10-cm VAS pain and apprehension for 61 patients punctured by students, compared by anesthesia use with Mann-Whitney U tests. The authors note the pain difference "exceeded the minimal clinically important difference on a VAS—typically estimated at 13 mm (1.3 cm)" despite nonsignificance.

> "Pain scores were higher in the group without anesthesia than in the group with anesthesia (median 4.8, IQR 2.1-6.4 vs median 2.1, IQR 1.2-4.0), although the difference did not reach statistical significance (P=.08)."
