### Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel 2025

Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel. (2025). Impact of Criterion-Based Reflection on Prospective Physics Teachers’ Perceptions of ChatGPT-Generated Content. TU Dresden. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of group discussions after the homework task of developing teaching-learning sequences on energy and power for 7th-grade students using ChatGPT. Students found, for example, that mentioned misconceptions were "nonsense" and the list "very incomplete", requiring literature consultation.

> "most students criticized the responses for lacking clarity, correctness, appropriate-ness, and precision. They applied the given criteria to highlight the deficiencies in ChatGPT’s answer quality."
