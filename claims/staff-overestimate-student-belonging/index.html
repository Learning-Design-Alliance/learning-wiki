Duchesneau 2023¶

Duchesneau, N., & Zapata, M. (2023). Creating Safer Schools: A Case Study. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641760

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Survey analysis comparing student and staff perceptions of belonging in both districts. The report states "Students were less likely than staff to report a strong sense of belonging in their schools," with most staff believing adults really care about every student. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size printed.

"Students were less likely than staff to report a strong sense of belonging in their schools. Specifically, roughly half (44% to 50%) of students in TSD and slightly more than half (52% to 62%) of students in RSD agree that they feel close to people at their school"
