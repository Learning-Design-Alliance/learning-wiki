### Janet Mannheimer Zydney 2025

Janet Mannheimer Zydney, Erin Rumpke, Gi Woong Choi, Lauren Angelone. (2025). Understanding interactions between scientists and elementary school students in a citizen science project. Instructional Science (2025) 53:949–971. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09710-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Density analysis of the one-mode co-participation network (Table 2) shows non-scientific density of 1.83 versus scientific density of 0.11; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The non-scientific discussion was much denser than the scientific discussion with many more students co-contributing to the same artifact."

### Janet Mannheimer Zydney 2025 (2)

Janet Mannheimer Zydney, Erin Rumpke, Gi Woong Choi, Lauren Angelone. (2025). Understanding interactions between scientists and elementary school students in a citizen science project. Instructional Science (2025) 53:949–971. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09710-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Sociograms of co-contribution networks (Figs. 5 and 6) show all students joined non-scientific exchanges, while the scientific discussion involved 12 students and both expert scientists, with 7 students not participating.

> "In the non-scientific discussion, all students co-contributed together; how- ever, the expert scientists did not contribute at all."
