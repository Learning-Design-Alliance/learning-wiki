## Evidence

### Hatlenes 2016

Hatlenes, L. T., & Eikeseth, S. (2016). Effects of Theory Training, Hands-on Supervision and a Self-instructional Treatment Manual on Staff Competency. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 9(2), 373-384. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126634

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group t-tests after the three-hour lecture showed significant improvement in both groups, but the between-group difference was "nonsignificant"; gains did not reach mastery levels.

> "After theory training, participants in both groups improved their teaching skills significantly ( t(5) = 2.696,  p = .043; t(5)  = 3.381, p = .020, respectively fo r the teaching manual group and the hands -on group). Mean correct teaching performance was 25 % for the self -instructional manual group and 56 % for the hands-on group, a nonsignificant between -group difference."
