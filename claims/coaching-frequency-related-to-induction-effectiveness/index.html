Evidence¶

Wechsler 2024¶

Wechsler, M. E., Patrick, S. K., Thompson, C., & Levin, S. (2024). On the path to leadership: California's administrator induction programs. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/636.224

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 4 years of CTC program completer survey data (6,812 completers, September 2017–August 2021; 85% of completers) reports the distribution of coaching field support frequency and finds "a positive relationship between the frequency of field support from coaches and ratings of program effectiveness." No effect size is printed.

"While over half (52%) of program completers received coaching field support once per week or more, 32% received field support twice per month, 13% received field support only once per month, and 3% received field support less than once per month. These differences matter, as there is a positive relationship between the frequency of field support from coaches and ratings of program effectiveness."
