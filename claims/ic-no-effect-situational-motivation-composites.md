## Evidence

### Oschwald 2025

Oschwald, A., Moeller, J., Kracke, B., Viljaranta, J., & Dietrich, J. (2025). Student motivation and instructional clarity: Linking experience sampling method data to objective behavioural observations. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S281–S299. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12775

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-classified multilevel models (M1–M3) linked 2227 ESM surveys from 155 students to video-coded instructional clarity at 81 learning-situation time points. Fixed-effect estimates for detail, variation, and logical inconsistency on expectancies, values, and costs all had credibility intervals spanning zero, e.g. detail on expectancies .032 [−.030; .093]. The article reports "neither predicted expectancies nor task values or costs at the learning situation‐level".

> "Contrary to our hypotheses, all three indicators of IC (detail of explanation, variation of explanation and logical inconsistency) neither predicted expectancies nor task values or costs at the learning situation‐level."
