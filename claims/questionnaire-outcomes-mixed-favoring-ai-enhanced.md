## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The AI-Enhanced Group reported significantly higher digital tutor recognition (F = 15.32), blended teaching acceptance (F = 6.74), and autonomous learning habits (F = 7.23), but no significant difference in knowledge organization ability (F = 2.86, p = 0.06). [→ Zhao Can 2026](#zhao-can-2026)
`q3 i?` A significantly higher proportion of the AI-Enhanced Group rated their autonomous learning ability as relatively consistent or above (89.0% vs 72.0% and 68.3%; χ² = 23.56, p < 0.001). [→ Zhao Can 2026 (2)](#zhao-can-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Zhao Can 2026

Zhao Can, Zhu Jianzhong, Liu Jianhui, Zhao Wentao, Pang Yin. (2026). Effectiveness of a generative AI-powered digital tutor integrated with a knowledge graph in anatomy education for nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09469-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blended Teaching Effectiveness Questionnaire (34 items, five-point Likert) across the three RCT arms; significant group differences on tutor recognition, acceptance, and learning habits, but "knowledge organization ability" did not differ significantly.

> "No statistically significant difference was found among the three groups in knowledge organization ability (F = 2.86, p = 0.06)."

### Zhao Can 2026 (2)

Zhao Can, Zhu Jianzhong, Liu Jianhui, Zhao Wentao, Pang Yin. (2026). Effectiveness of a generative AI-powered digital tutor integrated with a knowledge graph in anatomy education for nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09469-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported learning behavior analysis in the same RCT; the between-group difference in strong autonomous learning ability was statistically significant (χ² = 23.56, p < 0.001). No effect size printed.

> "Specifically, 89.0% of students in the AI-Enhanced Group rated their autonomous learning ability as “relatively consistent” or above, compared with 72.0% in the Blended Group and 68.3% in the Traditional Teaching Group."
