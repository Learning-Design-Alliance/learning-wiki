### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Matthew Dew, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Context affects student thinking about sources of uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scenario survey results: in the Stern-Gerlach setup only one measurement was predicted because one channel was blocked, yet "32% of students drew on principles to explain the continuous distribution of measurements." The authors interpret this high accessibility as potentially detrimental to reasoning.

> "In spite of this, 32% of students drew on principles to explain the continuous distribution of measurements in our fictitious data distribution for the Stern-Gerlach experiment."
