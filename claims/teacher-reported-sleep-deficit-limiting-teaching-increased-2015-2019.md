### Vik Frøydis N. 2022

Vik Frøydis N., Nilsen Trude, Øverby Nina C. (2022). Associations between sleep deficit and academic achievement - triangulation across time and subject domains among students and teachers in TIMSS in Norway. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-14161-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive trend analysis of teachers' questionnaire responses in the representative TIMSS Norway samples, in which teachers rated how much sleep deficits limited their teaching; 37.8% of science teachers reported sleep deficits "did not limit their teaching" in 2015 versus 23.6% in 2019.

> "With regards to sleep deficits, 37.8% of science teachers reported that students’ sleep deficits did not limit their teaching (“not at all”) in 2015, while only 23.6% said the same in 2019."
