# Students in the project groups were perceived by teammates as effective collaborators across all three rubric sub-scales

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean perceived collaboration scores were similar across sub-scales (task M=3.46, discussion M=3.46, team functioning M=3.43), indicating students perceived other group members as effective collaborators. [→ Yildirim 2003](#yildirim-2003)

## Evidence

### Yildirim 2003

Yildirim, Z. (2003). Relationship between Achievement Goal Orientation and Collaboration in Project-Based Learning Process. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED493521

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive analysis (Table 1) of the Effective Collaborative Rubric Survey among 48 students in 14 project groups; students "perceived other group members were effective collaborators (4th level)". No inferential test or effect size is printed for these means.

> "Students had similar scores on contribution of group’s tasks and completion of personal tasks (M=3.46); discussion skills and active listening (3.46); and contribution to group’s evaluation, problem solving and team functioning (3.43) sub-scales of collaboration rubric indicating that students perceived other group members were effective collaborators (4th level) in this process."
