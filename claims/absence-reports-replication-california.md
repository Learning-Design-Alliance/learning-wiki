### Attendance 2018

Replication study by Robinson, Lee, Dearing, & Rogers (2018) in ten urban, suburban, and rural elementary districts in a large California county; 10,504 at-risk students were randomly assigned, with a statistically significant effect on reducing absences of ES=+0.10.

> "Students determined to be at risk for absenteeism (n=10,504) were randomly assigned to receive Absence Reports, or to a control group. Effects on reducing absences were statistically significant (ES=+0.10)."
