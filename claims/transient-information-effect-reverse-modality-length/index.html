Evidence¶

John Sweller 2023¶

John Sweller. (2023). The Development of Cognitive Load Theory: Replication Crises and Incorporation of Other Theories Can Lead to Theory Expansion. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09817-2

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

First of two experiments by Leahy and Sweller (2011), replicating the reverse modality effect reported by Tabbers et al. (2004), in which the single modality group obtained higher test scores than the dual modality group. The article rules out redundancy as the cause; no statistics are printed in the quoted passage.

"In the first of two experiments, Leahy and Sweller (2011) replicated this failure to obtain the modality effect. It was not due to redundancy because neither Tabbers et al. nor Leahy and Sweller had used materials that incorporated redundant information."
