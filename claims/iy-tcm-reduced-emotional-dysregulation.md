## Evidence

### Wendy M. Reinke & Keith C. Herman & Nianbo Dong 2018

Wendy M. Reinke & Keith C. Herman & Nianbo Dong. (2018). The Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management Program: Outcomes from a Group Randomized Trial. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-018-0932-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group RCT with 105 teachers and 1817 students (K–3) in nine urban schools; three-level HLM on teacher-reported TOCA-C outcomes with pretest and demographics as covariates. The article reports reduced emotional dysregulation (b = −0.15, p < 0.001, d = −0.14) for IY TCM versus control.

> "Main effect analyses demonstrated that students in IY TCM showed significant improvement on teacher-reported prosocial behavior ( b = 0.14, p = 0.038, d = 0.13), overall social competence ( b = 0.14, p = 0.032, d = 0.13 ), and reduced emotional dysregulation ( b = − 0.15, p <0 . 0 0 1 , d = − 0.14) compared to students in the control condition."
