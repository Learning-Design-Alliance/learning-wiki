### Blanié Antonia 2020

Blanié Antonia, Amorim Michel-Ange, Benhamou Dan. (2020). Comparative value of a simulation by gaming and a traditional teaching method to improve clinical reasoning skills necessary to detect patient deterioration: a randomized study in nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1939-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome of a multicenter randomized controlled trial in which 73 nursing students per group were randomized to serious-game simulation or traditional teaching; the quoted means and p value are as printed, with no effect size reported.

> "Immediately after the session, the mean SCT scores were 58.9 ± 9.1 in the SG group and 57.8 ± 8 in the TT group with no significant difference (p = 0.43) (Fig. 3)."
