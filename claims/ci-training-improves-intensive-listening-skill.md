## Evidence

### Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu 2017

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu. (2017). The Impact of Consecutive Interpreting Training on the L2 Listening Competence Enhancement. English Language Teaching, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n1p72

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test-post-test experiment with 50 Translation and Interpreting majors at GDUFS randomly assigned to CG (n=24) and EG (n=26). After 16 weeks of CI training, paired-sample t-tests showed significant dictation gains for both groups (CG t=10.23, EG t=8.07, both p<0.001); the article reports "both groups improved their intensive listening skill".

> "A paired-sample t-test showed that both groups scored significantly higher in the post-test than in the pre-test in the dictation test (for CG , t=10.23, p.< 0.001; for EG , t=8.07, p.< 0.001)."

### Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu 2017 (2)

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu. (2017). The Impact of Consecutive Interpreting Training on the L2 Listening Competence Enhancement. English Language Teaching, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n1p72

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-tests comparing CG and EG on the dictation test found no significant difference at pre-test (t=-1.61, p=0.11) or post-test (t=-1.39, p=0.17), so the authors conclude both groups improved "by the similar margin". No effect size is printed.

> "An independent sample t-test showed that the two groups were not statistically different from each other in the pre-test (t=-1.61 , p.=0.11>0.05), or in the post-test (t=-1.39, p.=0.17>0.05)."
