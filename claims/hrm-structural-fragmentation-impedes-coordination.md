### Smylie 2006

Smylie, Mark A., and Stacy A. Wenzel. (2006). Promoting Instructional Improvement: A Strategic Human Resource Management Perspective. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/promoting-instructional-improvement-strategic-human-resource-management-perspective

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors draw on organization and management literature and education literature to argue that HRM departments in school central offices may be loosely connected to strategic planning or curricular improvement, and that "their self-interests for influence and survival may institutionalize structural fragmentation."
