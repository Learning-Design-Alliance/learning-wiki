Mühlberg Theres, Bachner Joachim, Mess Filip, Schmid-Ellinger Jan. (2026). Observing teaching principles in school - Development and Validation of an Observation Protocol (OPTIS). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355710

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Real-time piloting in a fourth-grade Bavarian elementary school where observers rated three lessons with the five-point and ten with the six-point scale. The article reports the six-point range as "0.801 to 1" versus "0.511 to 1" for the five-point.

> "For the real-time piloting, the observation protocols applied with the six-point rating scale could achieve slightly better inter-observer reliability values (0.801 to 1) than the observations with the five-point rating scale (0.511 to 1)."
