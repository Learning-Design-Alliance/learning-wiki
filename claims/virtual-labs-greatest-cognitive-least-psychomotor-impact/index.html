Evidence¶

Safaeipour Ruhollah 2025¶

Safaeipour Ruhollah, Aalaa Maryam, Mojtahedzadeh Rita, Mohammadi Aeen, Asadzandi Shadi, Mousavi Atekeh. (2025). Virtual or blended laboratories for healthcare students education: a systematized review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07937-7

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's Bloom's taxonomy synthesis of the 21 included studies reports cognitive-domain gains in "knowledge acquisition, conceptual understanding, problem-solving, and creative thinking" across thirteen studies; four studies covered the affective domain and three the psychomotor domain.

"Thirteen studies [4, 6, 28–30, 32–34, 36, 38, 41, 42, 44] reported improvements in knowledge acquisition, conceptual understanding, problem-solving, and creative thinking which indicating that virtual or blended laboratories can support higher-order cognitive skills, as well as the upper levels of Bloom's cognitive domain."
