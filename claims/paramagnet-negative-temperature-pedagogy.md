Authors' pedagogical interpretation of a spreadsheet calculation (Fig. 7) for a 100-atom two-state paramagnet, whose entropy-vs-energy graph shows temperature going to infinity and then becoming negative as energy is added; the article calls it "an invaluable peda- gogical tool".

> "The paramagnetic system can be an invaluable peda- gogical tool, since it forces students to abandon the naive notion that temperature is merely a measure of the aver- age energy in a system, and to embrace instead the more general relation (11), with all its connotations."
