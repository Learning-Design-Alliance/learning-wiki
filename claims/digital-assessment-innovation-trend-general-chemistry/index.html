q3 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis (RQ3) of cross-study patterns across the 30 included articles. The review reports that pandemic-era acceleration led to "digital question banks, machine learning-assisted assessment systems, LMS-based assessments, and game-based quizzes" and later virtual laboratory simulations.

"Significant developments began during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, which accelerated the adoption of digital assessment technologies, including digital question banks, machine learning-assisted assessment systems, LMS-based assessments, and game-based quizzes."

Oktariani O 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing the included study by Yik et al. (2021) on a machine learning-based tool to evaluate Lewis acid–base model use (article A13), that technology-assisted formative assessment supported reflection and efficiency. The review does not print effect sizes for this finding.

"The use of these technology-assisted formative assessments allows students to reflect on their achievements in summative assessments and makes assessment faster and more efficient."
