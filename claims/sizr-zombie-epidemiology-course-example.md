`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Classroom demonstration example: the SIZR model tracks susceptible, infected, zombie and removed populations, with initial zombies at "just 0.01% of the population"; with the printed parameter values the simulation output shows "the zombies very quickly overrun the human population." No statistical evaluation reported.

> "Given the values used here, the zombies very quickly overrun the human population in true apocalyptic fashion."
