q3 · i? · causal · r2

RM-ANOVA on the knowledge application test (eight open CDR scenarios, administered pre and post) found no significant time point × condition interaction for the worked example groups versus problem-solving; hypothesis H1.1b was rejected. No effect size printed for this finding.

"Concerning the application of CDR strategy knowledge (H1.1b), the worked example groups did not demonstrate a stronger improvement than the problem-solving group (no significant interaction between time point and condition)."
