# Binary logistic regression shows psychological capital and academic motivation (and specifically self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation) are protective: higher scores decrease the probability of non-high UAP

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In significant binary regression models, PsyCap (OR = 0.203) and academic motivation (OR = 0.321) reduce the probability of non-high UAP; self-efficacy (OR = 0.188) and IMS (OR = 0.435) are protective dimensions. [→ Deroncele-Acosta Angel 2025](#deroncele-acosta-angel-2025)

## Evidence

### Deroncele-Acosta Angel 2025

Deroncele-Acosta Angel, Norabuena-Figueroa Roger Pedro, Norabuena-Figueroa Emerson Damian. (2025). Women’s mental health in the doctoral context: Protective function of the psychological capital and academic motivation. Women’s Health. https://doi.org/10.1177/17455057251315318

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Binary logistic regression models (general and specific) estimated on the 108-woman survey; Table 6 reports significant models with odds ratios OR = 0.203 (PC), OR = 0.321 (AM), OR = 0.188 (self-efficacy), OR = 0.435 (IMS), showing "they decrease the probability of a “not high” level in the UAP."

> "the self-efficacy dimensions of the PsyCap variable and the intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation dimension of the academic motivation variable are protective dimensions, since as both dimensions jointly or individually increase in their valuation score, they decrease the probability of a “not high” level in the UAP."
