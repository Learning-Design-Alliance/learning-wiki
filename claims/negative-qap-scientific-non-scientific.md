### Janet Mannheimer Zydney 2025

Janet Mannheimer Zydney, Erin Rumpke, Gi Woong Choi, Lauren Angelone. (2025). Understanding interactions between scientists and elementary school students in a citizen science project. Instructional Science (2025) 53:949–971. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09710-1

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

A QAP correlation with 20,000 permutations compared the scientific and non-scientific discussion matrices; the printed correlation r = 0.14 (p < 0.05) indicates students who co-contributed non-scientific comments were a bit less likely to engage in scientific exchange.

> "There was a weak, negative relationship ( r = 0.14, p < 0.05) between participating in the scientific and non-scientific discussions about the artifacts."
