## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Philip I. Pavlik Jr. 2005

Philip I. Pavlik Jr., John R. Anderson. (2005). Practice and Forgetting Effects on Vocabulary Memory: An Activation-Based Model of the Spacing Effect. Cognitive Science 29. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0000_14

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of S2 data (repetitions × spacing × S2 trial × retention interval) in the authors' experiment. Mean S2 correctness was 0.64 for the 1-day and 0.52 for the 7-day interval.

> "this analysis showed that people forgot more when the retention interval went from 1 to 7 days, F(1, 38) = 4.26, p < .05. It also revealed a strong main effect of spacing, F(2, 76) = 58.2, p < .001."
