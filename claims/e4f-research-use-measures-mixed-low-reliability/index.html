Evidence¶

Lord 2017¶

Lord, P., Sims, D., White, R., & Roy, P. (2017). Evidence for the Frontline: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581162

q2 · i? · design · r2

Road-testing of two measures from the NFER standard EEF Research Use tool (Poet et al., unpublished), each comprising two items, administered in the September 2015 baseline and June/July 2016 follow-up surveys. The report concludes "Research-use measures would require further development for use in a future trial of E4F."

"In terms of impact on teacher attitudes and behaviour, two standard 'Research Use' factor measures analysed in the baseline and follow-up surveys had mixed results, with low reliability."
