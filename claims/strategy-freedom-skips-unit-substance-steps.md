## Evidence

### Borchers 2025

Borchers, C., Fleischer, H., Schanze, S., Scheiter, K., & Aleven, V. (2025). High scaffolding of an unfamiliar strategy improves conceptual learning but reduces enjoyment compared to low scaffolding and strategy freedom. Computers & Education, 236, 105364. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105364

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

An exploratory log-data analysis of practice transactions showed that when unit and substance specification was optional, students rarely engaged in it: "students specified units an average of 2.0 times and substances 0.6 times", versus over 22 steps of each type in the high-scaffolding condition.

> "In ORCCA, where specifying units and substances was optional, students specified units an average of 2.0 times and substances 0.6 times. In contrast, students using StoichTutor completed an average of 22.0 unit steps and 22.8 substance steps."
