In the same 27-intern pre/post study at UCLA RRMC and GLA VAHS, therapeutic reasoning responses were scored with a rubric awarding points for using at least three of four PEST criteria; improvements per question (e.g., p = 0.0743 for two UCLA questions) did not reach significance.

> "Overall interns demonstrated an improvement in their therapeutic reasoning, but this was not statistically significant (Fig. 3)."
