## Evidence

### Mayrhofer A 2016

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons of learners trained per home across 17 care homes. Ownership (p = 0.475), type (p = 0.423), size (p = 0.888) and prior EoL training (p = 0.475) showed "no statistical evidence for a difference"; equivalence was not tested.

> "More learners were trained in care homes owned by for profit organisations (median = 7.5) than in care homes owned by non-profit organisations (median = 5), but there was no statistical evidence for a difference (p = 0.475, Mann–Whitney U test)."
