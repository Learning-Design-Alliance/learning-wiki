## Evidence

### Zeng Qin 2023

Zeng Qin, Wang Kai, Liu Wei-xin, Zeng Jiu-zhi, Li Xing-lan, Zhang Qing-feng, Ren Shang-qing, Xu Wen-ming. (2023). Efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in advanced life support training: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04654-x

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs reporting knowledge at course conclusion, synthesized in RevMan 5.4 with a random-effect model after significant heterogeneity (I2 = 70%). The pooled estimate was "SMD = 0.38; 95% CI: 0.18–0.59, P = 0.0003". Subgroup and sensitivity analyses attributed heterogeneity to variation in course design, participant types, and outcome measures.

> "Pooled data from the RCTs demonstrated a benefit in improvement of knowledge for HF simulation when compared with LF simulation and traditional training (SMD = 0.38; 95% CI: 0.18–0.59, P = 0.0003, I2 = 70%]."
