## Evidence

### Duckworth Angela L. 2016

Duckworth Angela L., White Rachel E., Matteucci Alyssa J., Shearer Annie, Gross James J. (2016). A Stitch in Time: Strategic Self-Control in High School and College Students. Journal of educational psychology, 108(3), 329-341. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000062

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (Hayes and Preacher's Mediate Macro) in the Study 3 college experiment. Situation modification also reported less temptation than response modulation (d = 0.61) and control (d = 0.50); the response modulation group's mediation CI included zero.

> "We found that temptation partially mediated goal accomplishment for the situation modification group (indirect effect = 0.22; 95% bootstrapped confidence interval, CI: [0.05, 0.43]), but not for the response modulation group (indirect effect = −0.05; 95% bootstrapped confidence interval, CI: [−0.22, 0.10]), relative to the no-treatment condition"
