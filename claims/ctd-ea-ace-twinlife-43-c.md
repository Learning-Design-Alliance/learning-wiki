## Evidence

### Wolfram 2023

Wolfram, T. & Morris, D. (2023). Conventional twin studies overestimate the environmental differences between families relevant to educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 8, 24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00173-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-fitting of a CTD ACE structural equation model to twin-only data from the TwinLife 1990–1993 cohort, with educational attainment corrected for age and gender. The article reports "A = 34% (95% CIs: 23 –47%), C = 43% (31–53%), and E = 23% (20 –26%)".
