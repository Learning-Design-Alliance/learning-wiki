## Evidence

### Oliver and Young 2016

Rhonda Oliver, Shahreen Young. (2016). Improving reading fluency and comprehension in adult ESL learners using bottom-up and top-down vocabulary training. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 6(1), 111-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2016.6.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental classroom intervention (Experiment 1) with 20 adult ESL learners in two intact classes; posttest compared oral reading of a transfer text containing the 40 trained words against a control text. Reading time was "significantly slower" on the transfer text (M = 391.40 s vs M = 365.55 s; p = .002).

> "after training, the participants were significantly slower in terms of time taken in reading the transfer text (M = 391.40 seconds) than the control text (M = 365.55 seconds; p = .002)"
