The review's synthesis of 24 included comparative studies in school PE, summarized narratively by the authors: "Studies commonly showed that TGAs improved student outcomes in tactical skill; skill execution; affective, procedural knowledge and/or physi- cal activity". No pooled effect sizes are reported for this overall pattern.

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • A prior meta-analysis found tactical interventions significantly improved decision-making but not skill execution compared with technical approaches — related
  • Comparative TGA research is concentrated in Western Europe, equally split between primary and secondary schools, mostly single-sport and quasi-experimental — related
  • Reporting quality of comparative TGA studies was mostly low, with moderate to serious risk of bias — related
  • Teaching Games for Understanding improves tactical decision-making more than traditional physical education instruction. — related
  • Intervention length may matter: a prior review found positive associations for decision-making and skill execution for interventions longer than 8 hours — related
