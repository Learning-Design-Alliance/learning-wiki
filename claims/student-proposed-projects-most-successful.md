## Evidence

### Felipe Tobar 2021

Felipe Tobar, Felipe Bravo-Marquez, Jocelyn Dunstan, Joaquín Fontbona, Alejandro Maass, Daniel Remenik, Jorge F. Silva. (2021). Data Science for Engineers: A Teaching Ecosystem. [doi:10.1109/msp.2021.3053551](https://doi.org/10.1109/msp.2021.3053551)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' observational report from project-oriented courses, in which "the ﬁnal evaluation requires students to form groups (of 2-4 members) to complete a project" and students may propose their own themes. No success metric or comparison data is printed.

> "We have noticed that it is precisely those projects brought by the students that turn out to be the most successful, most likely because there is a genuine motivation to work on these rather than on a generic assignment."
