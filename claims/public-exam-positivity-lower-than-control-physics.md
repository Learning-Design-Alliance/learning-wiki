## Evidence

### Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert 2026

Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert. (2026). Adapting Public Exams for Introductory Physics: A Mixed-Methods Investigation of Student Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Statistical analysis of coded open-ended responses from 39 students, comparing positivity rates (positive divided by total comments) for Public Exam comments against comments unrelated to Public Exams. The authors note the data "suggests that the Public Exams could actually be less positive than the control", in contrast to Wiggins et al.'s significantly higher positivity rate.

> "From the T-test, the mean positivity rate for the Public Exam category (µ= 92.51, σ= 18.8) was lower than for the control group (µ= 97.95, σ= 5.47), with ap-value of 0.09."
