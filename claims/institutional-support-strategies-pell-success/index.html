Evidence¶

Andrew Howard Nichols 2015¶

Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). The Pell Partnership: Ensuring a Shared Responsibility for Low-Income Student Success. The Education Trust. https://www.edtrust.org/resource/pellgradrates

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Case-study profile of Smith College's AEMES program, which pairs upperclassman mentors with first-year students in lab work and other activities. The report reports participants "are much more likely (the likelihood ranges from 18 to 23 percentage points, depending on the major) to finish with a degree in the sciences"; no comparison group or test is described.

"Students of color who participate in the program are much more likely (the likelihood ranges from 18 to 23 percentage points, depending on the major) to finish with a degree in the sciences."

Andrew Howard Nichols 2015 (2)¶

Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). The Pell Partnership: Ensuring a Shared Responsibility for Low-Income Student Success. The Education Trust. https://www.edtrust.org/resource/pellgradrates

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Case-study profile of Western Oregon University's Western Tuition Promise Program, guaranteeing fixed tuition for four years, with about one-third of each incoming class participating. The report states "Retention rates have increased by 4 percentage points — to 72 percent"; no causal test is reported.

"Retention rates have increased by 4 percentage points — to 72 percent — and they hope it only continues to climb as more students participate."
