## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Student difficulties with determining expectation values in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from written responses and think-aloud interviews with upper-level UG and G students. Interviewed students with this response "often incorrectly claimed that an operator acting on a generic state (𝑄̂|Ψ〉) describes the measurement process".

> "some students wrote incorrect expressions for the operator 𝑄̂ acting on state |Ψ〉, e.g., 𝑄̂|Ψ〉 = 𝑞𝑛|𝑞𝑛〉, or 𝑄̂|Ψ〉 = 𝑞𝑛|Ψ〉 because they incorrectly reasoned that an operator 𝑄̂ acting on a generic state |Ψ〉 will yield an eigenstate and/or eigenvalue of the operator 𝑄̂"

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Student difficulties with determining expectation values in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`
