Evidence¶

Dwi Sulisworo 2017¶

Dwi Sulisworo, Novitasari Sutadi. (2017). Science Learning Cycle Method to Enhance the Conceptual Understanding and the Learning Independence on Physics Learning. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 6(1). http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control-group study with 30 experiment and 32 control vocational students. The experiment group's N-gain was 0.58 (medium) and the control group's 0.29 (low); the article reports "the gain for the control group is 0.29". Posttest means were 18.17 versus 14.09.

"it can be calculated the gain index which it obtained an increase in experiment group 0.58. That is an increase in students' conceptual understanding affected by the implementation of SLC method. It is in the medium category. But on the other hand, the gain for the control group is 0.29"
