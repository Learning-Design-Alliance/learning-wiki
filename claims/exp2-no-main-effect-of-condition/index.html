Evidence¶

Booth 2015¶

Booth, J., Oyer, M., Paré-Blagoev, E. J., Elliot, A. J., Barbieri, C., Augustine, A., & Koedinger, K. R. (2015). Learning Algebra by Example in Real-World Classrooms. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1055636

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Hierarchical linear modeling with 395 students nested in 28 classrooms (school free/reduced-lunch proportion as a Level-2 covariate) across four algebra units. The intent-to-treat Model 2 showed "no significant main effect of condition on posttest scores"; no effect size was printed for this null.

"while Model 2 revealed no significant main effect of condition on posttest scores, Model 3 yielded a significant main effect of pretest scores on posttest scores, and Model 4 additionally yielded a significant interaction between condition and pretest scores."
