`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis using ICC(2,k) with a two-way random-effects model across five board-certified surgeon raters; the total GOALS ICC was "0.795" (good), and all five subscale ICCs were moderate (0.601–0.677) and statistically significant.

> "The inter-rater agreement for the overall GOALS score was good with an ICC of 0.795 (95% CI 0.732 – 0.850, p < 0.001). For the subscales of the GOALS score, the inter-rater agreement was moderate albeit statistically significant in all items"
