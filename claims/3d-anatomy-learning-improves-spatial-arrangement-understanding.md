`q3 i?` Both the 2D and 3D display groups showed significantly lower post-learning than pre-learning total placement distance in the 3D puzzle task. [→ Muro S 2025](#muro-s-2025)

## Evidence

### Muro S 2025

Muro S, Miyosawa K, Yamaguchi K, Okamoto K, Okamoto S, Itamiya T, Akita K. (2025). Evaluation of glasses‐free 3D anatomy learning materials through a randomized control study with a puzzle‐like method. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70063

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial of 56 medical students (28 per group), pre-to-post total placement distance fell in both groups; the article reports significance via Mann–Whitney U test with "2D group: p=3.27×10−5, 3D group: p=1.51×10−6". No effect size is printed.

> "both the 2D and the 3D display groups showed improvements in Dpost compared to Dpre (2D group: Dpre450.01±156.88, Dpost275.55±101.96; 3D group: Dpre418.79±134.38, Dpost251.80±119.53)."
