### Suzanne White Brahmia 2020

Suzanne White Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, and Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). A framework for the natures of negativity in introductory physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010120)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Case-study analysis (review-reported, situated in sophomore-level mechanics) in which the interviewed student struggled with one-dimensional acceleration as a signed quantity; the article reports "roughly half of this group of physics majors" wanted an extra negative sign in the equation of motion. No effect size is printed.

> "The burden of these compound natures of the negative sign resulted in roughly half of this group of physics majors feeling it was necessary to include an extra negative sign in the equation of motion so that the mathematics would match the physics."
