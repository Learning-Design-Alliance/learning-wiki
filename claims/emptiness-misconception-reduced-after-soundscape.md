## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Natt Day, and Sarah Barnes. (2021). Demonstrating change from a drop-in space soundscape exhibit by using graffiti walls both before and after. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-57-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-57-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of graffiti wall responses before (n=535) and after (n=446) the soundscape. The "prevailing opinion before was that space is empty" and it "dramatically reduced" on both normalisations reported.

> "The prevailing opinion before was that space is empty and this dramatically reduced following the soundscape, both relative to the total responses (from 47± 2% to 2± 1% ) and within the theme (from 70± 3% to 8± 4%)."
