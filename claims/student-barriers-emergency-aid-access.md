## Evidence

### Sandra Perez 2026

Sandra Perez. (2026). How to Strengthen the Emergency Safety Net for Today's Students. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682437

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the brief's interview findings on student barriers, practitioners reported that "students don't see their institution as a primary source of emergency support," and described stigma, minimization of need, and discomfort with recounting poverty narratives as deterrents to applying.

> "Many students don't see their institution as a primary source of emergency support and instead turn to family, friends, or borrowing through loans or credit cards before exploring the resources their campus may offer"

### Sandra Perez 2026 (2)

Sandra Perez. (2026). How to Strengthen the Emergency Safety Net for Today's Students. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682437

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Practitioners in the interviews reported that funding constraints hinder promotion of emergency aid; one institution chose not to publicize its program due to limited funds, directing students to emergency loans first. Even with marketing materials, some students reported not knowing the program existed.

> "When emergency aid funds are scarce, institutions may not widely advertise these programs, leaving students unaware that support exists"
