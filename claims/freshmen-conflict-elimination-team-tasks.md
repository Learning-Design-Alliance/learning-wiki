### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In FGI data, students began tasks by understanding total workload and breaking it down per member, being very sensitive to all members performing tasks uniformly to keep the team atmosphere fair.

> "However, the work style of the participating students was more focused on “division of labor” than “collaboration.”"

### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023 (2)

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participants reported a culture of not adding comments to what others had said, even positive comments, both in class and everyday conversation.

> "In particular, students feel uncomfortable giving feedback on their peers’ performances. This was due to the belief that giving feedback was equivalent to criticizing others."

### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023 (3)

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Students did their "one's own portion" as best as possible because they would spend 6 years with colleagues and did not want a reputation for not participating fully.

> "Students not only focused on completing team assignments immediately but also considered their behavior’s impact on their future medical school years in the long term."
