`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective quasi-randomized cluster study of 54 fourth-year medical students (30 EyelearnMR, 24 control) on a 2-week ophthalmology rotation, compared on a bespoke 17-question video assessment. The article reports "median scores of 16, IQR 15-17, and 15, IQR 14-15" with P=.03.

> "The EyelearnMR group performed significantly better than the control group (median scores of 16, IQR 15-17, and 15, IQR 14-15, respectively; P=.03; Mann-Whitney U test)."
