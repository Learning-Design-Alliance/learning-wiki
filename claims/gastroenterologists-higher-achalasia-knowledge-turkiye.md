## Evidence

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional nationwide survey of 675 physicians in Türkiye; univariate chi-square comparisons by specialty. Gastroenterologists showed "higher achalasia knowledge (95.5% vs. 73.2%; P < .001)" and more frequent symptom inquiry; all gastroenterologists correctly identified HREM as the gold-standard test.

> "Consistently, gastroenterologists demonstrated higher achalasia knowledge (95.5% vs. 73.2%; P < .001) and more frequent symptom inquiry (80.3% vs. 15.4%; P < .001)."

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026 (2)

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on survey responses identified specialty and hospital level as independent predictors of achalasia and HREM understanding; for HREM, "gastroenterology specialty remained the strongest predictor (OR = 13.30; 95% CI: 1.81-97.75; P = .011)".

> "Gastroenterologists exhibited substantially higher odds compared with other specialties (OR = 5.73; 95% CI: 1.75-18.83; P = .004)"
