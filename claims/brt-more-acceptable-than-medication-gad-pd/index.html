Evidence¶

Lundervold 2013¶

Lundervold, D. A., Ament, P. A., Holt, P. S., & Hunt, L. S. (2013). Comparison of Younger and Older Adults' Acceptability of Treatment for Generalized Anxiety Disorder Co-Occurring with Parkinson's Disease. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, 8(2). https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1555-7855

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Mixed factorial ANOVA (two age groups × two treatments) on AARP ratings from 79 younger and 54 older adults who read a case scenario of an older adult with PD and comorbid GAD. The article reports "a significant main effect for treatment type" favoring BRT, with F(1,131) = 14.71, p < .001, η2 = .101.

"A significant main effect for treatment type with BRT rated as more acceptable than medication ( F(1,131) = 14.71, p < .001, η2 = .101, observed power = .980)."
