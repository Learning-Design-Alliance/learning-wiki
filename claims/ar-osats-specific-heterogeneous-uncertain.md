`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three studies reporting OSATS Specific scores. The pooled estimate favored AR, but "due to high heterogeneity (I2 = 71%)" the outcome was excluded from GRADE, and Egger's test was significant (P = 0.0047), suggesting publication bias.

> "The OSATS Specific score indicated a mean difference of −4.81 points (95% CI [−6.43, −3.29], p < 0.005)"
