### Bai et al. 2025

Youheng Bai, Zitao Liu, Teng Guo, Mingliang Hou, and Kui Xiao. 2025. Prerequisite Relation Learning: A Survey and Outlook. ACM Comput. Surv. 57, 11, Article 279. https://doi.org/10.1145/3733593

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The survey reports, citing Sayyadiharikandeh et al., a pioneering use of Wikipedia click-stream data with features such as direct click count and backward click count; the hypothesis is that "if users frequently navigate from concept B to concept A during learning" this indicates prerequisite direction. No effect size is printed.
