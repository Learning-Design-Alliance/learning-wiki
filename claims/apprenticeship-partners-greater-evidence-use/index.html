Evidence¶

Song 2023¶

Song, Yu. (2023). Apprenticeship As a Developmental Mechanism in Argumentation Skill Development. Ph.D. dissertation, Columbia University. https://academiccommons.columbia.edu/doi/Song_columbia_0054D_18218

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Multi-week intervention dissertation in which young adolescents engaged in anonymous one-on-one electronic dialogs; the experimental group had more skilled adult partners while the comparison group's partners were confined to similar ability peers. The abstract reports the experimental group "showed greater skills in using evidence to support a claim" in both dialogs and essays. No effect sizes are printed.

"In both the dialogs and essays, the experimental group showed greater skills in using evidence to support a claim, generating advanced counterarguments, and constructing integrative critical arguments coordinating two contrasting claims, relative to the comparison group."
