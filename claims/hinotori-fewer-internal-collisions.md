## Evidence

### Iwabuchi Serena 2025

Iwabuchi Serena, Okata Yuichi, Kameoka Yasuyuki, Miyauchi Harunori, Yoshimura Shohei, Komyo Yuki, Kajihara Keisuke, Nakai Yumiko, Bitoh Yuko. (2025). Initial adaptation and surgical performance of the hinotori™ robotic system among pediatric surgeons with minimal robotic exposure. Pediatric Surgery International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-025-06212-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Peg-transfer results from the ten-surgeon crossover simulation study: total internal collisions were significantly lower with Robo (median 0.5 vs 3.5, p = 0.002). For suturing, the article reports "fewer internal collisions during knot tying (median: 11.5 vs 21, p = 0.011, r = 0.81)".

> "The number of internal collisions was significantly lower in the Robo group (1st: 0 vs 2, p = 0.01; 2nd: 0 vs 0.5, p = 0.38; 3rd: 0 vs 1, p = 0.001; and total: 0.5 vs 3.5, p = 0.002) compared with the Lap group."
