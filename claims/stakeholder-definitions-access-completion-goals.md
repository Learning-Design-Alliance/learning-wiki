## Evidence

### Rauner 2018

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey and interview findings: 139 of 141 survey respondents and all 15 interviewees answered the open-ended definition query; responses were coded into goals/expected outcomes and program features, with multiple codes allowed.

> "The most frequently mentioned goal of California College Promise programs included in respondents' definitions of such programs was to increase college access (48 of 154 respondents)."

### Rauner 2018 (2)

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey and interview findings on program features; 55 respondents specified that programs provide more than financial support, including cohort models, academic counseling, block scheduling, and priority enrollment.

> "The majority of survey and interview respondents (94 of 154) referred to monetary support for students as a key feature of College Promise programs."
