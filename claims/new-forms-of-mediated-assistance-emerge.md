### Hill 2015

Hill, K. (2015). Integrating Instruction, Curricula, and Assessments in the L2 Classroom. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1074705

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A qualitative conversation analysis of DSA transcripts identified three new forms of mediated assistance: "(1) peer-to-peer MA, (2) shadowing by the learner, and (3) intonation, facial expressions, and fillers".

> "Most likely as a result of the practice sequence prior to each DSA, this follow-up study found three new forms of MA to emerge from the interaction between the assistor and pairs: (1) peer-to-peer MA, (2) shadowing by the learner, and (3) intonation, facial expressions, and fillers."
