### Aleo Giuseppe 2026

Identifying core teamwork competencies for community-based health and social care professionals: an e-Delphi study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08478-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Round 1 results from 21 experts (Table 2); the article reports "at least 80% of the experts fundamentally agreed" while suggesting amendments. Sub-area agreement levels printed in Table 2 range from 80.00% to 100%. No effect size.

> "Although at least 80% of the experts fundamentally agreed with the competencies (see Table 2), they, however, suggested amendments which were then discussed internally among the TEAMCARE project partners, including researchers and professors of interprofessional education."

### Aleo Giuseppe 2026 (2)

Aleo Giuseppe, Pagnucci Nicola, Walsh Niamh, Moriarty Frank, Giardulli Benedetto, Koutra Kleio, Przyłęcki Paweł, Fitzgerald Catherine, Illing Jan. (2026). Identifying core teamwork competencies for community-based health and social care professionals: an e-Delphi study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08478-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-Round-1 revision process: new competencies on artificial intelligence and green skills were added, and the Soft Skills area was renamed because the concept was "considered obsolete". Descriptive; no effect size.

> "Some new competencies were added, such as integrating the use of artificial intelligence in community-based healthcare services, and green skills contributing to environmental sustainability. Whereas some competencies under the Area ‘Soft skills’ were reworded or removed because the concept of ‘soft skills’ was considered obsolete. This Area was renamed ‘Other non-clinical skills’."
