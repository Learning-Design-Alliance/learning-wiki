Prioritization analysis of the same 275-respondent survey, with topics ranked priority 1 when rated most important by ≥80% of respondents (Table 3). The survey reports that "80% or more of the participants felt they needed training on" these three quality-management topics.

> "80% or more of the participants felt they needed training on (i) quality systems essentials in the medical laboratory, (ii) implementing a quality management system, and (iii) techniques to identify sources of error in laboratory procedures"
