## Evidence

### Okazaki Shuntaro 2019

Okazaki Shuntaro, Muraoka Yoshihiro, Osu Rieko. (2019). Teacher-learner interaction quantifies scaffolding behaviour in imitation learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44049-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis across the 14 pairs relating imitation-phase interaction measures to the learner's first learner-phase (solo) trial; both "noise covariance: R = −0.59, p = 0.028; causal influence: R = −0.77, p = 0.001" were significant and negative.

> "the variables were significantly correlated with the time taken to solve the puzzle at the first trial in the learner phase (LP) (noise covariance: R = −0.59, p = 0.028; causal influence: R = −0.77, p = 0.001)"
