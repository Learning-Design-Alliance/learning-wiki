Tam Nguyen 2026¶

Tam Nguyen, Hung Nguyen, Robert Ogburn. (2026). AI-accelerated End-to-End Framework for Rapid Professional Upskilling. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14044

q1 · i? · design · r2

Gap analysis in Section II-B, a narrative review of delivery-side, production-side, learning-side, and program-level frameworks. The authors report four gaps: fragmentation, missing verification where LLMs hallucinate on a majority of post-cutoff questions, shallow default LLM pedagogy, and outcomes rarely measured against anything external.

"No surveyed framework covers end-to-end results, from knowledge acquisition through successful industry assessment."
