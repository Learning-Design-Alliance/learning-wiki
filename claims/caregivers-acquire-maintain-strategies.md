## Evidence

### Hampton et al. 2020

Lauren H Hampton, Ann P Kaiser and Elizabeth A Fuller. (2020). Multi-component communication intervention for children with autism: A randomized controlled trial. Autism, 24(8), 2104–2116. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320934558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Caregiver strategy use coded from 10-min caregiver–child interactions at each assessment point in the RCT, by coders blind to assignment. Table 3 reports, for example, target-level language rising from 10% to 28% in the intervention group versus 7% in control at posttest.

> "Caregivers trained in the intervention group at post-intervention used significantly more target-level language (p < 0.001), matched turns (p  = 0.004), expansions (p < 0.001), and correct prompting strategies (p  = 0.003)."

### Hampton et al. 2020 (2)

Lauren H Hampton, Ann P Kaiser and Elizabeth A Fuller. (2020). Multi-component communication intervention for children with autism: A randomized controlled trial. Autism, 24(8), 2104–2116. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320934558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up caregiver outcomes from the same RCT. The discussion notes matched-turn responsiveness declined slightly and milieu prompting decreased markedly from intervention levels, suggesting booster training may be needed.

> "At follow-up, trained caregivers used significantly more target-level language, expansions, and time delay strategies (p < 0.05; Table 2)."
