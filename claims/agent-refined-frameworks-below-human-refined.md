Agent–agent reliability analysis: the GPT-4o and GPT-5.2 agents reached QWK = 0.841 during refinement, while cross-model agreement under agent-refined criteria "ranged from QWK = 0.655 to 0.790", wider than under human-developed criteria (QWK ≈ 0.85–0.89).

> "After applying their separately refined frameworks under zero-shot prompting, agreement ranged from QWK = 0.655 to 0.790, suggesting variability in annotation consistency across foundation models."
