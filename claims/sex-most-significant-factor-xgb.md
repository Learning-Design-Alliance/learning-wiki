### Sun Zhiyuan 2024

Sun Zhiyuan, Yuan Yunhao, Xiong Xuan, Meng Shuqiao, Shi Yifan, Chen Aiguo. (2024). Predicting academic achievement from the collaborative influences of executive function, physical fitness, and demographic factors among primary school students in China: ensemble learning methods. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-17769-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RFECV feature selection using the XGB algorithm on the 300-student dataset identified an eight-factor set including sex, push-up, vital capacity, and executive function task measures. The article reports "sex was ranked as the most significant factor." No effect size printed.

> "In Fig. 3(a), the XGB algorithm identified a crucial factor set consisting of eight factors. Sex, push-up, vital capacity, mean reaction time in congruent and 1-back task, and mean accuracy in homogeneous and heterogeneous trials were identified as influential factors. Notably, sex was ranked as the most significant factor."
