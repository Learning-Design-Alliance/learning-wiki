> "the sign ificant difference between the pre- and post-test results of the beginning level participants of the present study are in sharp contrast to Brown’s (1992) theoretical view that intensive courses cannot be beneficial for elementary learners since they are not familiar with basic rules and therefore need slower pace of instru ction."
