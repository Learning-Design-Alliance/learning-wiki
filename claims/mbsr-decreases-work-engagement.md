## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test AVEM work-engagement results in the waitlist-controlled study; Table 3 reports Cohen's d of -1.52 (willingness to work to exhaustion) and -0.48 (striving for perfection). Willingness to work to exhaustion decreased significantly in the intervention group while increasing in the control group.

> "Teachers in the intervention group reported decreases in professional engagement in all four scales with two of them reaching statistical significance."
