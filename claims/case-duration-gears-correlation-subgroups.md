### Laverty Robert B. 2025 (2)

Laverty Robert B., Chesnut Charles H., Karam Joseph R., L'Huillier Joseph C., Bonte Alexander, Clanahan Julie M., Park Jisuk, Yoon Brian, Krell Robert W. (2025). Evaluating the evaluators: does C-SATS measure up? Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12150-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup correlations in the Results section show significant negative duration-GEARS correlations only for practicing surgeons (RAC r = -0.58, p = 0.014) and the ≥ 50 case-volume group (r = -0.65 RAC; r = -0.44 RIHR); the printed |r| values of 0.58 and 0.65 correspond to a large correlation for the strongest subgroups.

> "Only significant negative correlations among these groupings between case duration and total GEARS scores are shown in the practicing surgeon group for RAC (r = - 0.58, p = 0.014), and those with ≥ 50 historic case volume (r = - 0.65, p = 0.011 for RAC; r = - 0.44, p = 0.04 for RIHR)"
