## Evidence

### Vinesh Rajpaul 2014

Vinesh Rajpaul, Saalih Allie, Sarah-Louise Blyth. (2014). The introductory astronomy course at the University of Cape Town: probing student perspectives. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-course retrospective 1–5 scale questions on met expectations (answered by 90 of 91) and interest (answered by 91 of 91), administered mid-May 2013 after the last lecture. The article reports the majority (> 80%) fell in categories 4 and 5 on both.

> "It was gratifying to note that the responses to the two questions did not diverge negatively, and that the majority (> 80%) of the class had a high level of their expectations met and also found the course interesting (categories 4 and 5 in Table III)."
