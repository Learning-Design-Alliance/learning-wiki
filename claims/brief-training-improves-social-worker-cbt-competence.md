## Evidence

### Armstrong G 2010

Armstrong G, Blashki G, Joubert L, Bland R, Moulding R, Gunn J, Naccarella L. (2010). An evaluation of the effect of an educational intervention for Australian social workers on competence in delivering brief cognitive behavioural strategies: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression analysis in the RCT (n = 30 analysed; 16 intervention, 14 control) of videotaped simulated consultations rated by blinded reviewers using the Cognitive Therapy Scale, adjusting for baseline competence; the difference was "14.20 points higher" with 95% CI 7.38–21.02, p < 0.001. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "On average, participants in the intervention group were rated 14.20 points higher than their control group counterparts at follow-up when controlling for baseline competency scores, a significant difference."
