### Melnikov Semyon 2026

Melnikov Semyon, Abed Elhai Reem, Hijazi Nashaat, Zaid Nagah, Furmanov Alex. (2026). Associations Between Perceived Knowledge, Interprofessional Collaboration, Autonomy and the Frequency of Nursing Activities in Paediatric Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70726

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable regression in the same cross-sectional study of 83 paediatric ECMO nurses showed perceived ECMO knowledge associated with calibration and monitoring, "B = 0.39, 95% CI [0.13, 0.64], p = 0.004". Knowledge was also positively associated with total ECMO nursing activities (B = 0.21, p = 0.016, Table 4).
