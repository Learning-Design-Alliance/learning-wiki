## Evidence

### Theobald EJ 2017

Theobald EJ, Eddy SL, Grunspan DZ, Wiggins BL, Crowe AJ. (2017). Student perception of group dynamics predicts individual performance: Comfort and equity matter. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181336

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordered logit mixed models (CLMM) on survey responses and pre-/post-test scores from 684 students in an introductory biology course, controlling for pre-score and topic. The probability of scoring at least one point higher was "17.8% lower when a student more strongly agreed that someone dominated their group". No standardized effect size printed.

> "students who more strongly agreed that someone dominated their group performed worse on the post-test than when they less strongly agreed that they had a dominator in their group"
