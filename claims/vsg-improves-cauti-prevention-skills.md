## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mercan Annak 2026

Mercan Annak, İ., and Bulut, H. 2026. "The Effect of Virtual Simulation Game on the Development of Nursing Students' Knowledge and Skills: A Randomized Controlled Trial." Nursing Open 13, no. 5: e70566. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70566

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

In the same randomized controlled trial (n = 75), skills were assessed by three trained instructors using a structured checklist (57–171 points) on a mannequin and standardized patient. The VSG group's post-test skill score was "higher than that of the control group (p < 0.001)" with d = 1.441; pre-test difference was not significant (p = 0.390).

> "However, regarding the post‐test measure of skill level, the VSG group's skill score was higher than that of the control group (p < 0.001). The effect size of the between‐group difference in skill level was large (d = 1.441)"
