Evidence¶

McEwan D. 2017¶

McEwan D., Ruissen G.R., Eys M.A., Zumbo B.D., Beauchamp M.R. (2017). The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training on Teamwork Behaviors and Team Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Controlled Interventions. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169604

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis (Table 3) of the number of teamwork dimensions targeted in the teamwork pool: one dimension d = 0.05 (k = 6, 95% CI −0.26 to 0.35, p = .775, not significant); two d = 0.65, three d = 0.98, four d = 0.57, all significant. The one-dimension result is a non-significant pooled estimate, not evidence that single-dimension training has no effect. Team performance improved regardless of number of dimensions (ds = 0.46–0.67).

"significant effects of training on teamwork were evident when two or more dimensions of teamwork were targeted (ds = 0.65–0.98) but not when only one dimension was targeted (d = 0.05)."
