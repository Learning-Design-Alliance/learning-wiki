E-Learning Satisfaction, Stress, Quality of Life, and Coping: A Cross-Sectional Study in Italian University Students a Year after the COVID-19 Pandemic Began. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression in a cross-sectional survey of 471 Italian university students confirmed that Religion "was positively related to e-learning satisfaction (beta = 0.303; p < 0.05)" after controlling for sociodemographics, stress, quality of life, and social presence.

> "The multivariable model, controlling for all factors, confirmed that "Religion" was positively related to e-learning satisfaction (beta = 0.303; p < 0.05)."
