### Abreu M 2026

Abreu M, Suárez A, Stari C and Martí A C. (2026). Exploring the potential of ChatGPT for feedback and evaluation in experimental physics. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae8110](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae8110)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the customized "Physics Instructor GPT" modality. The article reports it assessed titles, objectives, organization and figure numbering consistently, flagged improvements while recognizing strengths, and correctly described uncertainty propagation, though "greater variability was observed" in technical content.

> "These examples illustrate that model feedback can go beyond purely mechanical assessment, offering conceptually nuanced observations that reflect an understanding of the underlying physics."
