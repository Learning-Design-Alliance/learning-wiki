## Evidence

### Quan Zhou 2026

Quan Zhou, Liao Wangjingyi, Allegrini Andrea G., Rimfeld Kaili, Wertz Jasmin, Morris Tim T., Raffington Laurel, Plomin Robert, Malanchini Margherita. (2026). Non-cognitive skills mediate education-related polygenic score associations with academic achievement across development. Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72838-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mediation models in a longitudinal twin cohort (TEDS) tested whether latent education-specific non-cognitive skills mediate polygenic score associations with academic achievement. Self-reported mediated effects "increased from zero at age 9" to β = 0.08 at age 16, showing a developmental increase in mediation magnitude.

> "When considering self-reported measures, indirect (mediated) effects increased from zero at age 9, to β = 0.02 [95% CI, 0.01, 0.04], p < 0.001 at age 12 to β = 0.08 [95% CI, 0.06, 0.11], p < 0.001 at age 16."
