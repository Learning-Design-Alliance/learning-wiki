# Leadership and teaching quality monitoring are positively related to faculty's behavioral intention for online teaching, while strategic planning is not

## Evidence

### Miaoyun Li 2023

Miaoyun Li, Chun Lu, Harrison H. Yang, Di Wu, Xiao Yang. (2023). The influence of organizational factors on the acceptance of online teaching among college faculty during the COVID-19 pandemic: a nationwide study in mainland China. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10245-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Organizational-level MSEM path estimates from the nationwide faculty survey (N = 209,058; 858 institutions). Leadership and teaching quality monitoring "were found to be positively related to behavioral intention," whereas no significant influence of strategic planning on behavioral intention was found.

> "leadership (β  = .019, p < .01, 95% CI = [.005, .033]), teaching quality monitoring (β = .016, p < .05, 95% CI = [.001, .031]) and perceived usefulness (β  = .459, p < .001, 95% CI = [.373, .546]) were found to be positively related to behavioral intention, collectively accounting for a total of 23.4% variance of faculty's behavioral intention, while no significant influence was found between strategic planning (β  = .003, p = .599, 95% CI = [− .008, .013]) and behavioral intention."
