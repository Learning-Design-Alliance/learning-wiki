Descriptive analysis of 42 national gastroenterology training curricula retrieved from UEG member societies between February and December 2024. The study reports that "The median duration of gastroenterology training across UEG member societies was 60 months (IQR: 48–72 months)" and that only 7.1% (3/42) of curricula explicitly described part‐time training.

> "The median duration of gastroenterology training across UEG member societies was 60 months (IQR: 48–72 months). Only 7.1% (3/42) of curricula explicitly described the possibility of part‐time training."
