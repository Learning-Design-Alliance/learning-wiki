### Charles Baily 2017

Charles Baily, Qing X. Ryan, Cecilia Astolfi and Steven J. Pollock. (2017). Conceptual Assessment Tool for Advanced Undergraduate Electrodynamics. PHYS. REV. – PHYS. ED. RES. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of free-response answers to Q4b of the CURrENT, which asks about the divergence of the current density J inside a wire carrying a steady current. The article attributes errors to students being distracted by the increasing magnitude of J as the wire narrows, and to treating divergence as a global rather than local property.

> "More than half of students (51.2%, N=284) answered this question incorrectly. [26]"
