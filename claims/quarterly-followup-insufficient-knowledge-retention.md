### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Ratekin Carly Schade, Auko Erick, Wafula Meshack Malenya, Omwanda Wycliffe Okoth, Wamae Jane, Mudhune Sandra A, Rogers Ash, Mbeya Julius, Ouma Frederick Ochieng, Njoki Lillian, Young William, Starnes Joseph R. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-test of 21 of the original 26 trained providers (due to staff attrition) whose Knowledge Check scores were remeasured 9 months after training; the article reports "decreased to 92.67% (p=0.01)". No effect size is printed.

> "Knowledge Check scores at 9 months following training decreased to 92.67% (p=0.01), meaning that their knowledge of HBB was not sustained."
