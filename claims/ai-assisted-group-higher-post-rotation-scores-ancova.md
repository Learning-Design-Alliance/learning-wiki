Hu Shuai, Du Fei, Fang Can, Zheng Xiyan, Shi Xianjie, Zhou Zheng. (2026). Effectiveness of an AI-augmented teacher feedback model in improving medical students' clinical documentation skills: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09953-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Phase 3 used ANCOVA for comparing post-rotation AI scores between groups, controlling for baseline AI scores and case complexity. The AI group achieved a "significantly higher adjusted mean post-rotation AI score (11.15)" than the traditional group (9.09), with a mean difference of 2.058 points (p = 0.041).

> "The results showed a significant main effect of group on the post-test AI scores (F = 4.418, p = 0.041), with the AI group achieving a significantly higher adjusted mean post-rotation AI score (11.15) compared to the traditional group (9.09), representing an adjusted mean difference of 2.058 points (95% CI: 0.087–4.030)."
