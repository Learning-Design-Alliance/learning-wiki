The review's narrative synthesis of the two trials reporting long-term outcomes. Nimbalkar et al. found no differences in knowledge or skill after 3 months despite short-term gains; Rubio-Gurung et al. found significant improvement in technical and team performance after a median of 107 days.

> "Only two trials in our review measured the long-time retention of benefits, and their results were inconsistent."
