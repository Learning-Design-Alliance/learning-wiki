Focus group study asked participants where youth should learn social-emotional competencies. One student noted "you spend eight hours, five times a week at that place," and participants ultimately believed family and community are most responsible but adults in schools should support holistic development.

> "Initial answers were always at home with family and in the community, but secondary answers frequently suggested schools should play a role as well."
