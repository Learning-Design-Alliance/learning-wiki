## Evidence

### Takashige Abe 2019

Takashige Abe, Faizan Dar, Passakorn Amnattrakul, Abdullatif Aydin, Nicholas Raison, Nobuo Shinohara, Muhammad Shamim Khan, Kamran Ahmed, Prokar Dasgupta. (2019). The effect of repeated full immersion simulation training in ureterorenoscopy on mental workload of novice operators. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1752-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same repeated-measures simulation study, NASA-TLX questionnaires completed after each session showed "a continual improvement in the mental workload in both tasks without plateauing"; the MANOVA model gave task 1: p = 0.0005 and task 2: p = 0.0028. No effect size is printed.

> "During the training sessions, there was a continual improvement in the mental workload in both tasks without plateauing (MANOVA model, task 1: p = 0.0005, task 2: p = 0.0028)."
