### Doustmohammadian A 2022

Doustmohammadian A, Omidvar N, Keshavarz-Mohammadi N, Eini-Zinab H, Amini M, Abdollahi M. (2022). The association and mediation role of Food and Nutrition Literacy (FNLIT) with eating behaviors, academic achievement and overweight in 10–12 years old students: a structural equation modeling. Nutrition Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-022-00796-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM Model 2 results on the 803-student sample: the skill-domain subscales IFNL and FCL show printed associations with unhealthy and healthy eating behaviors respectively.

> "In our study, we also found that “Interactive Food and Nutrition Literacy” protected unhealthy eating behaviors (β = − 0.43, P < 0.001), and “Food Choice Literacy” predicted healthy eating behaviors (β = 0.13, P = 0.02) in children."

### Doustmohammadian A 2022 (2)

Doustmohammadian A, Omidvar N, Keshavarz-Mohammadi N, Eini-Zinab H, Amini M, Abdollahi M. (2022). The association and mediation role of Food and Nutrition Literacy (FNLIT) with eating behaviors, academic achievement and overweight in 10–12 years old students: a structural equation modeling. Nutrition Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-022-00796-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM Model 2 results section reports full mediation by Food Choice Literacy between demographic factors and healthy eating behaviors, with both pathway coefficients printed.

> "The finding also revealed the fully mediating role of “Food Choice Literacy” in the relationship between demographic factors and healthy eating behaviors (of demographic factors on “Food Choice Literacy”, β = 0.16, P = 0.02; of “Food Choice Literacy” on healthy eating behaviors, β = 0.13, P = 0.02)."
