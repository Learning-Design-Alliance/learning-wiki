# L2 learners style-shift in past tense accuracy within narrative discourse according to the amount of planning time available

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Style shifting in past tense morpheme use occurs within a single discourse mode (narrative) as a function of available planning time. [→ Rod Ellis 1987](#rod-ellis-1987)

## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 1987

Rod Ellis. (1987). Interlanguage Variability in Narrative Discourse: Style Shifting in the Use of the Past Tense. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 9, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100006483

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The study examined style shifting in three past tense morphemes by 17 intermediate L2 learners of English, collecting data under planned writing, planned speech, and unplanned speech conditions. The design isolated planning time "within a single discourse mode—narrative discourse".

> "It differs from previous studies in that style shifting is examined within a single discourse mode—narrative discourse—according to the amount of planning time made available."
