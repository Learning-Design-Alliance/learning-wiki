Owen Schochet, Katie Gonzalez. (2026). Early Childhood Educator Pay Equity Fund (PEF): Do Economic Returns Change Over Time?. Mathematica. none in text. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/early-childhood-educator-pef-do-economic-returns-change-over-time

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Comparison of program economics before and after the FY 2024 shift to a facility payroll model designed to align educator pay with a newly established public school-based salary scale. Both costs and benefit values rose year over year.

> "Although total program costs increased under the facility payment model, primarily due to the size of the educator wage payments as well as increased administrative expenditure, the size and economic value of the program's benefits also increased."
