### Kamphausen Anne 2026

Kamphausen Anne, Steg Julia, Bexten Tobias, Kubitz Jens, Engelen Christian. (2026). Challenges faced by prehospital emergency physicians providing emergency care to pediatric patients: Defining entrustable professional activities by identifying self-perceived competency strengths and deficits in prehospital emergency medicine. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01637-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online survey of 437 emergency physicians assessing self-perceived competence across twelve defined scenarios using a 5-point Likert scale. Results are displayed in Tables 3 and 4, with neonatal care showing 16% not competent at all.

> "The highest level of competency was reported in nontraumatic emergencies such as seizures, anaphylaxsis, and hyper or hypoglycemia, while survey participants felt confident in the treatment of fractures and injuries in the trauma scenarios."
