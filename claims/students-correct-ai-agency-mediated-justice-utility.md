# For students, AI agency reduced intention to use Correct-AI indirectly through lower perceived global utility and justice, with no direct effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the student Correct-AI mediation model, agency significantly negatively impacted global utility (β = -0.4261, p = 0.0011) and justice (β = -0.3768, p = 0.0261), and the indirect effects of agency on intention to use through global utility and through justice were both statistically significant. ## Evidence

### Alexander John Karran 2025

Alexander John Karran, Patrick Charland, Joé Trempe-Martineau, Ana Ortiz de Guinea Lopez de Arana, Anne-Marie Lesage, Sylvain Sénécal & Pierre-Majorique Léger. (2025). Multi-stakeholder perspective on responsible artificial intelligence and acceptability in education. npj Science of Learning 10:44. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00333-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Simple mediation analysis (Hayes Process Model 4, 5000 bootstrap samples, 95% CIs) of student responses to the Correct-AI vignette. The indirect effect through GU was significant (Effect = -0.1449, BootLLCI = -0.2805, BootULCI = -0.0441), as was the indirect effect through justice (Effect = -0.0576, BootLLCI = -0.1382, BootULCI = -0.0047). The direct effect of agency on ITU was not statistically significant (β = -0.1056, p = 0.2982).

> "the level of agency signiﬁcantly negatively impacted GU ( β = -0.4261, p = 0.0011), indicating that higher levels of agency were associated with lower levels of perceived GU"
