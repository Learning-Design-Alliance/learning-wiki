## Evidence

### Critten 2006

Critten, S., Pine, K., & Steffler, D. (2006). Spelling Development in Young Children: A Case of Representational-Redescription? Manuscript submitted for publication (Date of Submission: Thursday 3rd August 2006). https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.1.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Coding of 51 children's taped justifications in a one-to-one recognition task against RR-model criteria. Inter-rater reliability with an independent rater on 25 children produced a Kappa of .73; most participants were at E1A or E1B and no child was predominantly Implicit.

> "it became apparent that for many of the participants, level E1 was too broad and that actually, two distinct types of E1 seemed to exist.  They are labeled here as E1A and E1B"
