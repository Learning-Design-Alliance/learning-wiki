Evidence¶

Lea Waters 2015¶

Lea Waters, Adam Barsky, Amanda Ridd, Kelly Allen. (2015). Contemplative Education: A Systematic, Evidence-Based Review of the effect of Meditation Interventions in Schools. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-014-9258-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The review's own synthesis of 15 peer-reviewed studies of school meditation programmes, with effect sizes computed as Cohen's d in Comprehensive Meta-Analysis software. The results section states "a total of 762 effects of which 61 % were significant"; the abstract reports 76 effect sizes from 1,797 participants, an internal inconsistency in the article.

"Each study provided between 1 and 17 effects for a total of 762 effects of which 61 % were significant."
