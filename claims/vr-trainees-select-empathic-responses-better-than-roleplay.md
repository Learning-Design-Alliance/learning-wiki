## Evidence

### Sapkaroski Daniel 2021

Sapkaroski Daniel, Mundy Matthew, Dimmock Matthew Richard. (2021). Immersive virtual reality simulated learning environment versus role‐play for empathic clinical communication training. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intergroup comparison of mean communication scenario scores in a split-cohort training study (trainee n = 32 per arm). The study reports that "the results were only statistically significant for the trainees: P < 0.05", with VR trainees scoring 5% higher on average; Table 3 reports d = 0.51 for the trainee comparison.

> "Both VR training groups (TVR and CVR) performed better on average than their role‐play counterparts (5% and 11%); however, the results were only statistically significant for the trainees: P < 0.05."
