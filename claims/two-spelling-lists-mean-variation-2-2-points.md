## Evidence

### Starch 1910

Starch, D. (1910). The Measurement of Efficiency in Spelling, and the Overlapping of Grades in Combined Measurements of Reading, Writing and Spelling. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=The+Measurement+of+Efficiency+in+Spelling

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of the author's six-list spelling test: repeated testing of the same pupils with two different lists on successive days showed an average deviation between the two scores of 2.2 points, which the author calls "close agreement"; sample records for fourth-grade pupils on lists 1 and 5 are tabulated.

> "On the basis of tests made with 519 pupils, the mean variation between the scores from any two of the six lists is 2.2 points."
