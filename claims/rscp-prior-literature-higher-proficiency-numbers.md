## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article's narrative review of prior RSCP learning-curve studies reports proficiency thresholds ranging from about 10 cases (graphical inspection) to 78–84 cases (complication-based CUSUM), considerably higher than the 9 cases found here. [→ Park Kena 2024](#park-kena-2024)

## Evidence

### Park Kena 2024

Park Kena, Kwon Ji Young, Yoo Eun-Hee, Lee Seon Hwa, Song Jeong Min, Pyeon Seung Yeon. (2024). Application of CUSUM analysis in assessing learning curves in robot-assisted sacrocolpopexy performed by experienced gynecologist. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-024-02691-x

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Narrative comparison in the Discussion: the review reports, citing Akl, Geller, Linder, Sharma, and van Zanten, that prior studies found op-time plateaus after roughly 10–60 cases and complication-based proficiency after 78–84 cases; the article attributes the discrepancy to "surgeon experience and individual skill set". These prior studies were not re-analyzed here.

> "Previously reported numbers for proficiency are considerably higher than our results. The discrepancy in surgeon experience and individual skill set is a possible explanation."
