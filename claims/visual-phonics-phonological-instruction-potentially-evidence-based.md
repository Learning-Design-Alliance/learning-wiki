## Evidence

### Trezek and Mayer 2019

Beverly Trezek and Connie Mayer. (2019). Reading and Deafness: State of the Evidence and Implications for Research and Practice. Educ. Sci. 2019, 9, 216. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci9030216

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Standards-based examination by Trezek and Wang applying Council for Exceptional Children quality indicators to 30 intervention studies in reading and deafness published between 2000 and 2016. The review reports the Visual Phonics category was "identified as potentially evidence-based."

> "Three of these studies, one group comparison [51] and two single-subject [52,53], were identified in the category of phonological instruction supplemented by Visual Phonics. Based on the positive effects obtained in each of these studies, coupled with the study design and number of participants, this category of instruction was identified as potentially evidence-based."
