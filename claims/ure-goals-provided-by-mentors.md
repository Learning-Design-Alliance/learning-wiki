### N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman 2016

N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman. (2016). Examining and contrasting the cognitive activities engaged in undergraduate research experiences and lab courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Results on establishing research goals: few students discussed feasibility, presumably because the supervisor had already chosen the project. Students generally felt classwork had not prepared them for project content, though courses laid groundwork through terminology and broad perspective courses.

> "Students unanimously reported that the goals of their URE projects were provided to them by their research mentor."
