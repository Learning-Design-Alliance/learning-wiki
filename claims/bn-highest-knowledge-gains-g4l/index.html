Classroom study of the G4L intelligent tutoring system with 45 university students, randomized across three algorithm groups (BN, KST, WDDI). The article reports that "The Bayesian knowledge propagation algorithm demonstrated the highest knowl- edge gains" and that log analysis revealed significant SRL differences; no effect size is printed.
