## Evidence

### Johanna Casado 2022

Johanna Casado, Beatriz García, Poshak Gandhi, Wanda Díaz-Merced. (2022). A New Approach to Sonification of Astrophysical Data: The User Centred Design of SonoUno. American Journal of Astronomy and Astrophysics. https://doi.org/10.11648/j.ajaa.20210904.11

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group study in Southampton (April 2019) with nine participants in four groups, testing sonoUno on a linear function and an SDSS dataset. The article reports that "all the participants managed to correctly identify (via audio) a decreasing function" and three peaks. No effect size is printed.

> "all the participants managed to correctly identify (via audio) a decreasing function in the first data task set for them. In a second task, they correctly detected three peaks that correspond to three spectral emission lines."
