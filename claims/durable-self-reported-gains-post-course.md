## Evidence

### Hoskinson Anne-Marie 2010

Hoskinson Anne-Marie. (2010). How to Build a Course in Mathematical–Biological Modeling: Content and Processes for Knowledge and Skill. CBE-Life Sciences Education 9(3):333–341. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-03-0041

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The SALG survey used a different response URL for each of the four semesters so durability of perceived gains could be compared across cohorts surveyed up to 16 months after completion. Responses showed "self-reported gains in content and process were as strong" for earlier cohorts.

> "Notably, student opinions of curriculum and self-reported gains in content and process were as strong for those who had finished 12–16 mo before being surveyed as students just finishing the course."
