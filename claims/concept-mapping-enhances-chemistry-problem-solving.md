## Evidence

### Bamidele 2013

Bamidele, E.F. & Oloyede, E. O. (2013). Comparative Effectiveness of Hierarchical, Flowchart and Spider Concept Mapping Strategies on Students’ Performance in Chemistry. World Journal of Education, 3(1).

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test comparison across the three treatment groups (pre-test means 30.81–33.93; post-test means 51.57–55.87). The article attributes the gain to "the distinctive characteristics of the concept maps generally" and concludes the strategies enhanced problem-solving performance.

> "The mean scores of the three groups in the pre-test is much lower than the mean scores of the groups in the post-test."
