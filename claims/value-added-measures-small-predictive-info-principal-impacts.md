# School value-added and adjusted school value-added provide, at most, a small amount of information for predicting principals' contributions in the following year

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Measures that account for students' past achievement (school value-added and adjusted school value-added) provide, at most, a small amount of information for predicting principals' contributions to student achievement in the following year. [→ Hanley Chiang 2016](#hanley-chiang-2016)

## Evidence

### Hanley Chiang 2016

Hanley Chiang, Moira McCullough, Stephen Lipscomb, Brian Gill. (2016). Performance Measures Based on Test Scores – How Well Do They Predict Principals' Impacts? (In Focus Brief). Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/performance-measures-based-on-test-scores-issue-brief

The brief reports that the two measures accounting for students' past achievement "provide, at most, a small amount of information for predicting principals' contributions in the following year," based on the Pennsylvania statewide study of students and principals.

> "School value-added and adjusted school value-added—the measures that account for students' past achievement—provide, at most, a small amount of information for predicting principals' contributions in the following year."
