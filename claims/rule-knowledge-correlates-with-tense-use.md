## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among experimental learners, RAT scores correlated positively and significantly with GT (r = .513), OT (r = .790), and WT (r = .855). [→ Đặng 2012](#ang-2012)

## Evidence

### Đặng 2012

Đặng, T. T. Đ., & Nguyễn, H. T. (2012). Direct versus Indirect Explicit Methods of Enhancing EFL Students' English Grammatical Competence: A Concept Checking-based Consciousness-raising Tasks Model. English Language Teaching, 6(1), 112. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n1p112

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis among the 47 experimental learners relating rule analysis test scores to grammar, oral, and delayed written test scores; the printed correlation coefficients were r = .513 (GT), r = .790 (OT), and r = .855 (WT), all reported statistically significant.

> "There is a positive relationship between the RAT and the ot her three tests: GT (.513), OT (.790), and WT (.855) among the experimental learners and all were found to be statistically significant"

### Đặng 2012 (2)

Đặng, T. T. Đ., & Nguyễn, H. T. (2012). Direct versus Indirect Explicit Methods of Enhancing EFL Students' English Grammatical Competence: A Concept Checking-based Consciousness-raising Tasks Model. English Language Teaching, 6(1), 112. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n1p112

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis among the 47 control learners relating rule analysis scores to oral (r = .661), delayed written (r = .818), and grammar test (r = .472) scores; the article characterizes the RAT-GT relationship as weak. The authors note the CG correlations were lower than the EG's.

> "Although the correlation figures  of the OT (.661), WT (.818) were all statistically significant, the relationship between the RAT and the GT was weak; its correlative value was .472."
