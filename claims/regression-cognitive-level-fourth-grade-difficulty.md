# In fourth-grade stepwise regression, cognitive level predicted 12% of item difficulty variance, rising to 19% with item type added

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### İlhan et al. 2020

Mustafa İlhan, Nagihan Boztunç Öztürk, Melek Gülşah Şahin. (2020). The Effect of the Item’s Type and Cognitive Level on Its Difficulty Index: The Sample of TIMSS 2015. Participatory Educational Research, 7(2), 47-59. http://dx.doi.org/10.17275/per.20.19.7.2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on fourth-grade TIMSS 2015 item difficulty indices; the full model was significant, F=22.24, p<.01, with R2=.19 as printed, and the entry order reversed relative to eighth grade.

> "the regression analysis respecting fourth grade TIMSS items firstly included the variable of cognitive level and it could predict 12% of the variance regarding the item difficulty index [R2=.12]. In the second step, the variable of the item type as well as cognitive level were included in the regression model and the predicted variance increased to 19% [R2=.19]"
