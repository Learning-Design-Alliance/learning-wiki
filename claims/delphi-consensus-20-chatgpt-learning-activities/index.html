Related Claims¶

  • Most of the 20 ChatGPT learning activities (11 of 20) better satisfy competence, fewer satisfy autonomy, and the fewest satisfy relatedness. — related
  • Among the 20 ChatGPT learning activities, performance and self-reflection are each better supported by eight activities, and forethought by four. — related
  • Competence is the only SDT need that ChatGPT learning activities satisfy across all three SRL phases; relatedness and autonomy are not adequately satisfied at every phase. — related
  • Competence is the strongest independent psychological predictor of AI literacy performance, followed by autonomy; relatedness has no significant independent effect — related
  • Two Delphi rounds with 20 Chinese CDC experts achieved consensus, with Kendall's W rising significantly from 0.295 to 0.328 — related
