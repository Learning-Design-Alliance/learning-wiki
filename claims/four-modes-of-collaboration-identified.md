## Evidence

### Alanna Pawlak 2018

Alanna Pawlak, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2018). Development of a Modes of Collaboration framework. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video study in an introductory electricity and magnetism course at a large university; episodes were coded along the three dimensions and grouped into Modes. The authors report that "we identify four specific Modes that students engaged in within our context based on observation of a small set of video data from one day of class work." No inter-rater reliability statistics were computed.

> "nine unique groups of students were video recorded as they worked during their weekly laboratory sections, resulting in a total of 2 hours and 29 minutes of video."
