Evidence¶

Ji Hye Shin & Peggy Albers 2015¶

Ji Hye Shin & Peggy Albers. (2015). An Analysis of the Effect of a Cyber Home Learning System on Korean Secondary School Students' English Language Achievement and Attitude. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 32(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083964

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same disaggregated posttest analysis: intermediate (A-2 vs B-2) and lower (A-3 vs B-3) subgroups showed no significant differences. The article reports these nulls descriptively; equivalence was not tested.

"Participants in Group A-2 (M = 57.8, SD = 13.66) and B-2 (M = 59.9, SD = 11.27) showed no significant difference in test scores, t(156) = -1.05, p = .30. Likewise, the students in Group A-3 (M = 34.8, SD = 11.14) revealed no significant difference from those in Group B-3 (M = 33.9, SD = 10.66), t(139) = .46, p = .65."
