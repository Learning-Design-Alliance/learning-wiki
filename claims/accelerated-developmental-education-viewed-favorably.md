### Nodine 2012

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Among focus group participants discussing developmental education, few had enrolled in accelerated offerings: "Few focus group participants had enrolled in an accelerated course, but those who had were positive about the experience." One current student in fast-track courses credited the pace and instructor connection, and many who had not participated thought acceleration would be a better option than a traditional developmental education sequence.
