Evidence¶

Pooya Drood & Hanieh Davatgari Asl 2016¶

Pooya Drood & Hanieh Davatgari Asl. (2016). The Effects of Audio-visual Recorded and Audio Recorded Listening Tasks on the Accuracy of Iranian EFL Learners' Oral Production. English Language Teaching, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n9p110

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on post-test accuracy (percentage of error-free clauses) comparing the AV group (n=20) and A group (n=20) after five 30-minute treatment sessions. The article reports the null hypothesis of equal means rejected, with mean difference 5.02700.

"Mean scores of accuracy in group 1 is (M=85.88, SD=7.15), and in group 2 is (M=80.86, SD=3.66). Significance of the t-test is 0.008."
