# Participation in MTP-S eliminates the negative association between student disruptive behavior and teacher attainment projections

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The disruptive behavior × study condition interaction is significant, such that MTP-S teachers are less likely than control teachers to project poorer attainment for students reporting higher disruptive behavior. [→ Hafen 2015](#hafen-2015)

## Evidence

### Hafen 2015

Hafen, C. A., Ruzek, E. A., Gregory, A., Allen, J. P., & Mikami, A. Y. (2015). Focusing on teacher–student interactions eliminates the negative impact of students’ disruptive behavior on teacher perceptions. International Journal of Behavioral Development. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025415579455

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interaction analysis in the randomized trial adding study condition and a condition × disruptive behavior term to the two-level model. The article reports a significant interaction, b = .22, 95% CI [.12, .32], illustrated in Figure 1.

> "there was a significant interaction, b ¼ .22, 95 % CI [.12, .32], indicating that teachers in the MTP-S intervention were less likely than control teachers to project poorer attainment for students who reported higher disruptive behavior in the Fall"
