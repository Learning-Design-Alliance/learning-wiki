Evidence¶

Deborah Lowe Vandell 2021¶

Deborah Lowe Vandell, Megan Kuhfeld, Elizabeth Gershoff, Robert Crosnoe. (2021). Predictors and consequences of school mobility in middle childhood. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology, 76. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/predictors-and-consequences-of-school-mobility-in-middle-childhood/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the prospective longitudinal study (N = 1094), the authors tested whether a move during the previous year or a single move related to child functioning. The article reports "no evidence that a recent move or a single move was related" to academic or social functioning; no effect sizes are printed, so equivalence was not tested.

"There was no evidence that a recent move or a single move was related to children’s academic or social functioning."
