## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive ENACT-E observational analysis of 30 role-play and 55 CBR session assessments of ten lay workers over training and the 12-month pilot. Mean scores improved from 1.97 at week 1 to 2.50 at week 3, with the study reporting 2.77 at month 5 and "2.95 (SE 0.03) at month 11".

> "The mean ENACT score across all items was 1.97 (standard error, SE 0.07) at week 1, indicating that initially, on average, CBR workers were assessed as needing improvement (rating 1), but close to completing items partially well (rating 2)."
