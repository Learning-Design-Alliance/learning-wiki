`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of response distributions for all 33 topic questions on perceived knowledge, relevance and interest in the DACH subsample (Fig. 3), scored on a 1–5 scale. The overall knowledge score of 4.12 is descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "A consistent pattern of relevance > interest > knowledge was observed across nearly all domains, indicating that DACH HCPs broadly perceived geriatric topics as clinically important and expressed motivation to learn beyond their current level of self-reported knowledge. The overall knowledge score was 4.12."
