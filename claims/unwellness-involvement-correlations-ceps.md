## Evidence

### Liu Keqiao 2023

Liu Keqiao. (2023). Middle school students’ mental unwellness and academic performance in China: The effects of parental involvement. PLoS One 18(11): e0294172. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294172

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Weighted Pearson correlations (Table 2) on the CEPS panel sample; the article reports "r = -0.157, p < 0.05" for Chinese and similar values for math and English, describing all as weak. Involvement correlations range from 0.107 to 0.251.

> "mental unwellness in Grade 7 was significantly and negatively associated with their academic performance in Chinese (r = -0.157, p < 0.05), mathematics (r = -0.170, p < 0.05), and English (r = -0.179, p < 0.05) in Grade 8, though the correlations were weak."
