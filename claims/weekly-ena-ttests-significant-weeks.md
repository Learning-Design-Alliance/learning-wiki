# Weekly ENA mean comparisons showed significant high–low performer differences in Weeks 2, 3, 6, and 7 on mr1 and in Weeks 5, 6, and 10 on svd2

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Two-sample t-tests over 12 weeks found significant group differences on the x-axis (mr1) in Week 2 (d = 0.972), Week 3 (d = 0.747), Week 6 (d = 0.583), and Week 7 (d = 0.936). [→ Raković 2023](#rakovic-2023)
`q2 i3` Significant differences on the y-axis (svd2) were detected in Week 5 (d = 0.730), Week 6 (d = 0.295), and Week 10 (d = 1.221). [→ Raković 2023](#rakovic-2023)

## Evidence

### Raković 2023

Raković, M., Ahmad Uzir, N., Matcha, W., Eagan, B., Jovanović, J., Shaffer, D. W., Pardo, A., & Gašević, D. (2023). Network Analytics to Unveil Links of Learning Strategies, Time Management, and Academic Performance in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of Learning Analytics, 10(3), 64–86. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2023.7843

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Series of two-sample t-tests in a mean-rotated ENA space (Figure 14; Table 5) comparing high performers (Nabove90th = 15) and low performers (Nbelow25th = 31) on both exams across 12 active weeks. The article also reports significant svd2 differences in Weeks 5, 6, and 10, with Cohen's d up to 1.221.

> "We found a statistically significant difference between the two groups of students in Week 2 (t = 0.486, p = 0.028, Cohen's d = 0.972), Week 3 (t = 0.373, p = 0.039, Cohen's d = 0.747), Week 6 (t = 0.291, p = 0.010, Cohen's d = 0.583), and Week 7 (t = 0.468, p = 0.006, Cohen's d = 0.936)."
