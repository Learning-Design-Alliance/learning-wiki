Bühler 2025¶

Mapping Mind Wandering to the “Self-Regulated Learning Process, Multimodal Data, and Analysis Grid”: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10041-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Wong et al. (2022), that task-unrelated thoughts show a consistent negative association with test performance, "accounting for approximately 7% of the variation in learning outcomes." The 7% figure is a variance proportion, not a standardized effect size.

"Task-unrelated thoughts are consistently associated with lower test performance across different tasks, subjects, and age groups, accounting for approximately 7% of the variation in learning outcomes"
