### KC Ashish 2017

KC Ashish, Wrammert Johan, Nelin Viktoria, Clark Robert B., Ewald Uwe, Peterson Stefan, Målqvist Mats. (2017). Evaluation of Helping Babies Breathe Quality Improvement Cycle (HBB-QIC) on retention of neonatal resuscitation skills six months after training in Nepal. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-017-0853-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic regression on the 137-worker cohort (110 retained, 27 non-retained) relating QIC participation to 6-month retention found daily skill checkers "five times more likely to retain neonatal resuscitation skills" (RR-5.1, 95% CI 1.9–13.5); the article prints only the relative risk, no standardised effect size.
