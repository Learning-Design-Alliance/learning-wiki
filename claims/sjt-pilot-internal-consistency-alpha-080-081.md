### Soemantri Diantha 2022

Soemantri Diantha, Findyartini Ardi, Yolanda Sophie, Morley Emma, Patterson Fiona. (2022). Evaluation of Situational Judgment Tests in student selection in Indonesia and the impact on diversity issues. BMC Medical Education, 22:239. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03247-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis of the 30-scenario pilot SJT completed by two applicant cohorts under invigilated conditions. The article reports internal consistency was considered good, with Cronbach's alpha values of 0.80 and 0.81, respectively.

> "The internal consistency (reliability) of the SJT was considered good for each of the two pilot years, with Cronbach's alpha values of 0.80 and 0.81, respectively."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive statistics for the same two pilot cohorts: mean SJT scores were similar across both years with 419.9 (SD = 25.9) in 2017 and 422.6 (SD = 26.5) in 2018; no significance test is reported for this comparison.

> "The mean SJT scores were similar across both years with 419.9 (SD = 25.9) in 2017 and 422.6 (SD = 26.5) in 2018."
