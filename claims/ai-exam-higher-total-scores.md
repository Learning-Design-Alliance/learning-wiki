## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The AI examination group's mean total score (79.34) was significantly higher than the human group's (73.17), and AI-group scores spanned a narrower range (72.5–92.5 vs. 51.5–96.5). [→ Ma Xiang 2025](#ma-xiang-2025)

## Evidence

### Ma Xiang 2025

Ma Xiang, Pan Wei, Yu Xiao-ning. (2025). Evaluating AI-generated examination papers in periodontology: a comparative study with human-designed counterparts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07706-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples comparison of total scores in the randomized trial (63 students per group, 90-minute supervised tests). The AI group mean of 79.34 (95% CI 77.65–81.03) exceeded the human group mean of 73.17 (95% CI 70.91–75.43), t-test p = 0.027; AI-group scores were "more concentrated".

> "The AI group's total score average (M = 79.34, [95% CI: 77.65–81.03]) was significantly higher than that of the human group (M = 73.17, [95% CI: 70.91–75.43], t-test, p = 0.027; Fig. 6). The AI group's scores were more concentrated (range: 72.5–92.5) than the human group's scores (range: 51.5–96.5)"

### Ma Xiang 2025 (2)

Ma Xiang, Pan Wei, Yu Xiao-ning. (2025). Evaluating AI-generated examination papers in periodontology: a comparative study with human-designed counterparts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07706-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

By-item-type analysis of the same trial: case-based A3 and A4 scores differed significantly between groups (A3 p = 0.038; A4 p = 0.002, AI higher), while "No significant differences were found in question types A1, A2, and X-type questions".

> "Detailed analysis by question types showed significant differences in question types A3 (AI group: M = 17.31 [95% CI: 16.58–18.04]; Human group: M = 15.12 [95% CI: 14.28–15.96]; p = 0.038) and A4 (AI group: M = 22.50 [95% CI: 22.16–22.84]; Human group: M = 22.19 [95% CI: 21.83–22.55]; p = 0.002). No significant differences were found in question types A1, A2, and X-type questions"
