Group-level LMM on Scenario B data. PSgrp significantly predicted collaboration performance satisfaction negatively; adding PAgrp slightly improved fit (p = .086) but PAgrp was not statistically significant. Only a p-value is printed.

> "For collaboration performance satisfaction, PS grp had a signiﬁcant negative effect in Model 2.1 ( AIC = 226.50, BIC = 236.03, /Delta1χ2 = 4.09, p = .043), suggesting that higher group-level synchrony scores were associated with lower collaboration satisfaction ratings."
