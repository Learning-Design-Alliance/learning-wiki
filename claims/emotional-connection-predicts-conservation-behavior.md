## Evidence

### Kuo M 2019

Kuo M, Barnes M and Jordan C (2019) Do Experiences With Nature Promote Learning? Converging Evidence of a Cause-and-Effect Relationship. Front. Psychol. 10:305. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00305

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Otto and Pensini (2017), that "an emotional connection to nature... is a powerful predictor of children's conservation behavior, explaining 69% of the variance", whereas conservation knowledge is relatively unimportant in predicting conservation behavior. The 69% figure is variance explained, not a standardized effect size.

> "an emotional connection to nature, which may be more diﬃcult to acquire in a classroom, is a powerful predictor of children's conservation behavior, explaining 69% of the variance (Otto and Pensini, 2017)"
