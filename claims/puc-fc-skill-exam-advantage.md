> "In the three items of full crown tooth preparation, pit and fissure sealing, and intraoral suture, the scores of the PUC-FC group were significantly higher than those of the LBL group. Tukey test showed significant differences between groups (PUC-FC group > FC group > LBL group. (p < 0.001)"
