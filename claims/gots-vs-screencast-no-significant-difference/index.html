Evidence¶

Mery 2014¶

Mery, Y., DeFrain, E., Kline, E., & Sult, L. (2014). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Tools for Online Database Instruction. Communications in Information Literacy, 8(1). https://journals.lib.pdx.edu/index.php/informationliteracy

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-hoc Tukey HSD comparison in the same 90-participant randomized experiment. Screencast mean was 11.43 (71.46%) and GotS mean 10.77 (67.29%); the article reports "no difference in the effectiveness of the two types of instruction methods" on this measure.

"However, the differences between the screencast and GotS groups failed to reach the significant level ( p =.664), indicating that there was no difference in the effectiveness of the two types of instruction methods when measured with this questionnaire."
