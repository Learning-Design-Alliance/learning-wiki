## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Item 20 (providing students choice among materials) rose only from 1 to 2 student teachers, showing no significant increase. [→ Safak 2016](#safak-2016)
`q2 i?` Item 37 (assistive technology positioning) was performed by only 2 student teachers after feedback; the advisor's feedback did not sufficiently include technology use. [→ Safak 2016 (2)](#safak-2016-2)
