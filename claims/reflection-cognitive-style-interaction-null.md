## Evidence

### Blackburn 2015

Blackburn, J. J., Robinson, J. S., & Kacal, A. (2015). Determining the effects of reflection type and cognitive style on students' content knowledge. Journal of Agricultural Education, 56(3), 195-209. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2015.03195

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The two-way ANOVA in the completely randomized factorial 2x2 design tested the interaction of reflection type and cognitive style on the 25-item criterion-referenced test. The article reports the interaction effect was deemed non-significant (F(1,44) = 1.07, p = 0.31).

> "The interaction effect of type of reflection-in-action and cognitive style yielded an F(1,44) = 1.07, p = 0.31. The interaction effect was deemed non-significant and the researchers failed to reject the first null hypothesis."
