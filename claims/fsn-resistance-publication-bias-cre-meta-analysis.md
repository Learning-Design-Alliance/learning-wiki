# Fail-safe N analyses indicate the pooled effect is resistant to publication bias (Rosenthal FSN = 2139; Orwin FSN = 1048)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Rosenthal's fail-safe N was 2139 for 23 observed studies, and Orwin's fail-safe N was 1048, leading the authors to conclude the results are sufficiently tolerant for future studies. [→ Fulya Turk 2017](#fulya-turk-2017)

## Evidence

### Fulya Turk 2017

Fulya Turk. (2017). Evaluation of the Effects of Conflict Resolution, Peace Education and Peer Mediation: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Education Studies; Vol. 11, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p25

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias diagnostics for the 23-study meta-analysis: Rosenthal FSN = 2139, giving an N/(5k+10) value the authors calculate as "quite higher than 1"; Orwin's FSN = 1048 additional zero-effect studies would be needed to reduce the pooled effect to the "insignificant" criterion.

> "The fact that the value in this study is quite higher than 1 is an indication that it is quite strong for similar studies that will be carried out in the future."
