ANOVA across grade levels of students with intellectual disabilities (13 primary, 30 secondary). Sentence reading and vocabulary skills showed a significant increase from primary to secondary school, though weak readers exist at every grade level.

> "Significant differences are only present in test 4, which involves sentence reading (F(2) = 7.218, p < .01). Even in pairwise comparisons, the grade levels only show differences in test 4."
