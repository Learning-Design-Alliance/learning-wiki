## Evidence

### Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019

Eveline L. de Zeeuw, Kees-Jan Kan, Catharina E. M. van Beijsterveldt, Hamdi Mbarek, Jouke-Jan Hottenga, Gareth E. Davies, Michael C. Neale, Conor V. Dolan and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2019). The moderating role of SES on genetic differences in educational achievement in the Netherlands. npj Science of Learning 4:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0052-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Classical univariate twin model on the total sample: MZ and DZ twin correlations of 0.79 and 0.40, heritability of 72%, and a non-significant common environmental component after controlling for SES (Δ−2LL = 0.0, p = 0.963).

> "In the total sample twin correlations were 0.79 (MZ) and 0.40 (DZ) and individual differences in EA were highly heritable (72%). The common environmental variance (8%) was attributable to SES effects as shown by the non-signi ﬁcant contribution of the C variance component after controlling for SES ( Δ−2LL = 0.0, Δdf = 1, p = 0.963)."
