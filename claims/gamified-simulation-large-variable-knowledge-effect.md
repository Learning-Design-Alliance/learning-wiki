### Chen W.-L. 2026

Chen W.-L., Winitchayothin S., Cheng C.-H., Tonapa S.I., Chou F.-H., & Zimmerman P.-A. (2026). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effect of gamified clinical skill learning on learning outcomes in undergraduate nursing education. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000772

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of five RCTs (n=224) of gamified simulation-based learning in the knowledge meta-analysis. The review reports a large pooled effect, SMD = 0.79, quoting "a large effect (SMD=0.79, 95% CI [0.28, 1.30])" with substantial heterogeneity (I2=85%).

> "These interventions (five studies, n=224) exhibited a large effect (SMD=0.79, 95% CI [0.28, 1.30]) with substantial heterogeneity (I 2=85%). The considerable variability suggests significant differences in intervention design quality or implementation approaches"
