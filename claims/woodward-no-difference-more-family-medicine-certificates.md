## Evidence

### Tsigarides Jordan 2017

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

One of the first studies on this association (1987), comparing 2028 graduates from McMaster University's PBL curriculum with other Canadian non-PBL medical schools using the Canadian Medical Association Physician Manpower Databank. The review reports "no significant differences in speciality choice" but more family medicine certificates among PBL graduates.

> "It was found that there were no significant differences in speciality choice between the two curricula. They did, however, find that PBL graduates held a greater number of certificates in family medicine."
