## Evidence

### Emad 2016

Emad, G. R., & Roth, W.-M. (2016). Quasi-Communities: Rethinking Learning in Formal Adult and Vocational Education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9386-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic observation of test-review sessions, e.g. Dan reciting masthead-light ranges by vessel length and earning classmates' cheers, showed awareness of peers' knowledge eased communication; the authors note without the practice these resources "might have remained hidden."

> "The practice made visible each member's relevant knowing and competencies, which was used as a resource for the rest of the community."
