# Extracellular cleavage of proBDNF (blocked by PAI-1) is required at stage I, and intracellular cleavage by furin/PC1 (blocked by FIN II) at stage II, of L-LTP

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The tPA inhibitor PAI-1 blocked L-LTP when applied at stage I but was ineffective at stage II; BDNF co-application rescued the PAI-1 deficit. [→ Pang 2016](#pang-2016)
`q2 i?` The furin/PC1 inhibitor FIN II impaired L-LTP when applied at stage II but not stage I; BDNF rescued the FIN II deficit. [→ Pang 2016 (2)](#pang-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Pang 2016

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wild-type hippocampal slice recordings applying PAI-1 at each stage; BDNF applied with PAI-1 at stage I restored L-LTP (155.4 ± 14.8%, P o0.05), supporting tPA's role in proBDNF conversion.

> "The cell-impermeable tPA inhibitor PAI-1 (1 μg/ml), when applied at stage I, blocked L-LTP (Figure 6a, control, 156.0.4 ± 7.1%; PAI-1, 101.8 ± 15.3%, P o 0.05). In contrast, PAI-1 was completely ineffective when applied at stage II (Figure 6b, control, 156.0 ± 7.1%; PAI-1, 159.1 ± 16.9%, P = 0.867)."

### Pang 2016 (2)

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wild-type slices with the membrane-permeable inhibitor FIN II; BDNF applied 5 min after l-TBS completely reversed the FIN II impairment (165.6 ± 11.4%, P o0.05).

> "Application of FIN II (50 μM) to hippocampal slices at stage I had no effect on L-LTP expression (Figure 6d, control, 156.0 ± 7.1%; FIN II, 154.2 ± 17.4%, P = 0.926). However, when applied at stage II, this inhibitor markedly impaired L-LTP within a short period of time (Figure 6e, control, 156.0 ± 7.1%; FIN II, 107.6 ± 10.9%, P o0.05)."
