## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among participants who reported talking to someone during the six-week trial period, the intervention group reported significantly more mental health first aid actions than controls. [→ Davies E. Bethan 2018](#davies-e-bethan-2018)

## Evidence

### Davies E. Bethan 2018

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-ITT comparison of self-reported actions on a nine-item checklist in the pilot RCT. "The intervention group reported significantly more mental health first aid actions" than controls, Z = − 2.74, p = .006.

> "For those who reported talking to someone during the six-week trial period, the intervention group reported significantly more mental health first aid actions (Mdn = 7, M = 7.14 ± 1.77) compared to controls (Mdn 2.50, M = 3.36 ± 2.56: Z = − 2.74, p = .006)."
