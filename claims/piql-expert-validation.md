## Evidence

### White Brahmia 2021

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Group validation interviews with seven physics experts at Institution 1 and two mathematics experts, conducted as the PIQL reached steady state. Experts reached consensus on correct responses, judged items appropriate for the target population, and the interview led to removal of one item.

> "Experts agreed that, overall, the PIQL represents reasoning that they expect their students to de- velop during introductory physics courses and that is impor- tant in physics generally. We removed one item experts felt did not represent reasoning central to introductory physics."
