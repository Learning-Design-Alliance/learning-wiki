Systematic review of jigsaw studies from 1978 through 2022; the review's synthesis states that "the jigsaw classroom overall leads to positive effects" while "findings vary depending on the academic subjects and psychosocial variables measured." No pooled effect size is reported in the available text.

"If the results indicate that the jigsaw classroom overall leads to positive effects, findings vary depending on the academic subjects and psychosocial variables measured."
