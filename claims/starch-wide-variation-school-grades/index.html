Baldwin 1911¶

Baldwin, B. T. (1911). Educational Psychology at the Cleveland Meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Summary of Starch's grade-reliability study: the same papers graded by "142 teachers" (English) and "118 teachers" (geometry) showed marks ranging from 28 to 92; within one institution, ten instructors' grading showed a mean variation of 5.3, "not materially different from the above variation." No effect size is printed.

"papers in English graded by 142 teachers, and one in geometry by 118 teachers. A very wide variation was found, ranging from a mark of 28 to 92 in the case of the geometry paper."
