`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory moderator analysis of the prior cluster RCT (103 children, 35 schools), adding moderator-by-treatment interactions to the original multilevel model. The results section reports "no evidence of moderation by child, family, or school demographics" or IQ, language, or ASD diagnostic symptoms; Table 2 prints non-significant interaction coefficients for all these moderators.
