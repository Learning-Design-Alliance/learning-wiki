Evidence¶

Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho 2010¶

Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho. (2010). The Effect of Embossed Picture Technique on Reading Performance of Learners with Hearing Impairments: A Case of Kambui School for the Deaf. Journal of the American Academy of Special Education Professionals, Spring/Summer 2010. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137051

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experimental study with 18 class-one pupils with hearing impairment randomly assigned to embossed picture technique (n=9) or look-and-say control (n=9), taught 35 minutes weekly for 6 weeks, tested with a criterion-referenced pre/post test and analyzed with Mann-Whitney and Wilcoxon rank tests at .05. The article reports "a high statistical significant difference" favoring the embossed group; post-test means were 34 versus 7.56.

"The results revealed that there is a high statistical significant difference between pupils taught with embossed picture technique and those taught with the look and say method."
