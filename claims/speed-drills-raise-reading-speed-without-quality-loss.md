### Peters 1916

Peters, C. C. (1916). The Influence of Speed Drills Upon the Rate and the Effectiveness of Silent Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073150

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in Royersford, Pa. public schools, 1914-15: 207 pupils in grades three to six, drill and no-drill groups compared across four tests from November to June; the summary table expresses drill-group work as a percentage of the no-drill base, showing "a relative gain of 18.7% in speed".

> "This table shows a relative gain of 18.7% in speed but a trifling loss in quality as the result of the speed drills."
