## Evidence

### Herpin 2025

Herpin, S. A., Wynveen, K., & Berry, W. (2025). California's Proposition 28 Arts and Music Funding Guarantee and Accountability Act: Initial impact on arts organizations. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677596

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Preliminary analysis of open-ended survey responses from 78 arts organization respondents in a statewide WestEd survey. Respondents reported that school and district hesitation about spending and allowability slowed contracting; one noted partners "didn't fully understand how to use the funding."

> "Confusion remains at the school and district level related to allowable expenses under Prop 28 AMS, which resulted in less funding directed toward community arts organizations."
