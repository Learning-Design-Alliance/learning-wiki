Cross-sectional questionnaire administered to 176 nurses from the Murcian Health Service at the start of a blended-learning course. Descriptive findings show "only 19.9% of participants considered themselves to have sufficient and up-to-date knowledge", with 42% feeling inadequately trained and 38.1% uncertain.

> "Findings on training revealed that only 19.9% of participants considered themselves to have sufficient and up-to-date knowledge regarding diabetic foot management. In contrast, 42% felt inadequately trained, while 38.1% expressed uncertainty on this matter."
