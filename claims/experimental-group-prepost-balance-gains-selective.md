# Within the experimental group, pretest-post-test gains were significant for overall stability R, overall stability L and anterior posterior L, but not for anterior posterior R or medial lateral R

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paired pretest-post-test comparisons in the plyometric group reached significance for overall stability R (t=2.933, p=0.014), overall stability L (t=3.561, p=0.004) and anterior posterior L (t=3.872, p=0.003), while anterior posterior R and medial lateral R did not. [→ Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc 2018](#ersin-nacaroglu-onder-karakoc-2018)

## Evidence

### Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc 2018

Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc. (2018). Effects of Eight Week Plyometric Study on the Balance Performance of Hearing Impaired Athletes. International Education Studies, Vol. 11, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n6p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired Samples T-test on the experimental group's Biodex static balance parameters, reported in Findings Table 4. Overall Stability-R improved from 1.69±0.61 to 1.30±0.38; Overall Stability-L from 1.80±0.83 to 1.33±0.50; Anterior Posterior-L from 1.20±0.51 to 0.91±0.39. No effect sizes are printed.

> "When the static balance parameters of the experimental group were analyzed, overall stability R, overall stability L and anterior posterior L. values were found to be significant between the pretest and post-test (p <0.05). Anterior posterior R and medial lateral R values were not significant (p> 0.05)."
