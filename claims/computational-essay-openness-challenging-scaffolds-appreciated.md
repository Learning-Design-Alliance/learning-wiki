## Evidence

### Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). Computational Essays: An Avenue for Scientific Creativity in Physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview theme from the pilot's thematic analysis: students Jeffry and Gerald both reported difficulty identifying an interesting project topic, with Gerald saying it "was almost a bit too open."

> "We had the freedom to choose, of course. The problem is that it makes it hard to choose a topic. So I went back and forth, looked at different topics."

### Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero 2020 (2)

Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). Computational Essays: An Avenue for Scientific Creativity in Physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview quote from student Kurtis in the pilot study, indicating that provided simulations and the example essay served as starting points that helped students cope with the open brief.

> "It was good to have something to work around. As a starter . When, like, it was like, ’okay, use electromagnetics to do something’ and then we were like ’okay, what does that mean?’ It’s very good to have these examples to begin with."
