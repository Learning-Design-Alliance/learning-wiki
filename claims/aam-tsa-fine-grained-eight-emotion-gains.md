## Evidence

### Zhiyi Duan 2026

Zhiyi Duan, Xiangren Wang, Hongyu Yuan, Qianli Xing. (2026). Advancing Multimodal Teacher Sentiment Analysis: The Large-Scale T-MED Dataset & The Effective AAM-TSA Model. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1609/aaai.v40i3.37157](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i3.37157)

Fine-grained comparison (Table 2) of AAM-TSA versus MFMB Net on eight emotions; AAM-TSA improved on neutral, surprise, anger, sadness, joy, patience, enthusiasm and expectation, with enthusiasm rising from 47.44 to 60.00 and the authors reporting "the AAM-TSA improves 10.27% and 6.56% in M-F1 and W-F1".

> "Overall, the AAM-TSA improves 10.27% and 6.56% in M-F1 and W-F1, respectively."
