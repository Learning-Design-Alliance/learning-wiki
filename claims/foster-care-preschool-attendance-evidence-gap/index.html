q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Expert-interview report section on high-quality preschool. Experts stated the assumption of low attendance rests on evidence that is "unexamined" and "neglected," with one source showing only six percent of foster children under age six attend Head Start.

"As a group, the experts presumed the percentage of young children in foster care who attend high-quality preschool is low, but the evidence to support this assumption is mostly "unexamined" and "neglected.""
