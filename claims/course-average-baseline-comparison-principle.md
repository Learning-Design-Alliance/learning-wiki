`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of RQ1 findings from the qualitative teacher study. Teachers paid compliments when students did better than the course average and asked what was going on when a value dropped below it.

> "The teachers used the course average as a baseline to decide whether intervention was necessary or not, which is in line with the principle of comparison (Wise and Vytasek 2017)."
