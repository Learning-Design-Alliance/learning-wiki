`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the two blinded surgeons' video ratings of the 41 participants' porcine-model insertions. The article reports "an excellent agreement between the two ratings (ICC=0.96, 95% CI 0.91-0.98)"; the final score was the average of the two independent ratings.
