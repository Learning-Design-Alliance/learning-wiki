## Evidence

### Brown J 2025

Brown J, De-Oliveira S, Mitchell C, Cesar RC, Ding L, Fix M, Stemen D, Yacharn K, Wong SF, Dhillon A. (2025). Barriers to and Facilitators of Implementing Team-Based Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation Simulation Study: Exploratory Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/57424

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative arm: 4 focus groups conducted January–August 2022, including 2 trainee self-evaluations (n=7) immediately post-simulation, coded with grounded theory methodology. The article reports these two themes from the trainee sessions.

> "the simulation training resulted in (1) working together as a stronger and more confident team because of simulation and (2) the creation of a space to improve communications, decision-making, and express concerns"
