Executive summary additional findings on dosage and second-year self-funded continuation among the 49 schools that chose to pay.

"Of the 104 schools that took part initially, 49 chose to pay for the intervention over the second year. The main analysis was conducted on all schools after two years. Separate analysis for the 49 paying schools did not identify any additional benefit."
