## Evidence

### Jeanne Wanzek 2019

Jeanne Wanzek, Greg Roberts, Sharon Vaughn, Elizabeth Swanson, and Katherine Sargent. (2019). Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Model 4 of the latent moderated SEM analysis tested the moderating effect of Gates-MacGinitie reading achievement on treatment effects for ASK content reading comprehension, with pretest comprehension as latent covariate. The interaction was "nonsignificant (p = .708; ES = .006)" and follow-up tests at different moderator values were also nonsignificant.

> "The interaction of the B part of general reading comprehension and treatment X is nonsignificant (p = .708; ES = .006), as were the follow-up tests at different values of the moderator."
