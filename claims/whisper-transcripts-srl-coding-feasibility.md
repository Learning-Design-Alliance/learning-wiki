### Conrad Borchers 2024

Conrad Borchers, Jiayi Zhang, Ryan S. Baker, and Vincent Aleven. 2024. Using Think-Aloud Data to Understand Relations between Self-Regulation Cycle Characteristics and Student Performance in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. In The 14th Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK '24). ACM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability coding of utterances transcribed with Whisper from ten students' think-aloud sessions; the authors conclude unaltered automated transcriptions "have sufficient fidelity to be reliably coded for SRL processes by humans".

> "An acceptable inter-rater reliability was established after two rounds of coding (𝜅𝑝𝑟𝑜𝑐𝑒𝑠𝑠𝑖𝑛𝑔 = 0.78,𝜅𝑝𝑙𝑎𝑛𝑛𝑖𝑛𝑔 = 0.90, 𝜅𝑒𝑛𝑎𝑐𝑡𝑖𝑛𝑔 = 0.77, 𝜅𝑒𝑟𝑟𝑜𝑟 = 1.00)."
