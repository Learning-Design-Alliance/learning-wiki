### Yoo Dong Mi 2026

Yoo Dong Mi, Cho A Ra, Kim Sun. (2026). Enacted practices and developmental experiences of senior medical student tutors in a structured peer tutoring program. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of 24 tutor activity reports from two one-semester cycles (seven tutor–tutee pairs each in 2022 and 2023) identified the five practice domains; the article states these domains "illustrate how tutors integrated academic, behavioral, and emotional support throughout the tutoring process."

> "Across 24 activity reports, tutors consistently engaged in five major domains of tutoring practice: (1) diagnostic assessment of learning and routines, (2) structured academic coaching, (3) lifestyle and behavioral stabilization, (4) management of communication and relational challenges, and (5) iterative adjustment of goals and strategies."
