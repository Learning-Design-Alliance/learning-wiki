Christopher James MacLellan, Kimberly Stowers, Lisa Brady. (2024). Evaluating Alternative Training Interventions Using Personalized Computational Models of Learning. Advances in Cognitive Systems, 10. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.13684](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.13684)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Counterfactual simulation for the low performer across three interventions (Figure 7); the faded condition shows "a lower overall error and a faster decrease" than either alternative. No effect size printed.

> "error in the faded condition decreased to approximately 20% by opportunity 3, whereas this level is not achieved until opportunity 15 in the interleaved condition or until opportunity 5 in the blocked condition."
