Journal of Preventive Medicine and Hygiene. [doi:10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2021.62.2.1738](https://doi.org/10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2021.62.2.1738)

Attitude-item frequencies from the cross-sectional survey (Tab. II, n = 143). The article reports "76.9%" perceiving education as their responsibility versus "18.9%" confident in counseling, a gap the authors relate to low knowledge via Bandura's self-efficacy theory.

> "A majority of nurses perceived that patient education is a part of their job responsibilities (76.9%); however, only 27 (18.9%) of them confident in giving counseling to public and patients."
