## Evidence

### Ferguson M. A. 2014

Ferguson M. A., Henshaw H., Clark D. P. A., Moore D. R. (2014). Benefits of Phoneme Discrimination Training in a Randomized Controlled Trial of 50- to 74-Year-Olds With Mild Hearing Loss. Ear and Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1097/AUD.0000000000000020

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention analysis at t3 (4 weeks post-training) in the RCT, shown in Fig. 7. The article reports 52–75% of participants improved and that t3 results "remained significantly better than pretraining performance (t1)" with no significant change during t2–t3.

> "Of the measures that showed significant training-related improvement (training probe, GHABP Disability, TEA DTD, VLM tasks), between 52% and 75% of all participants showed some improvement"
