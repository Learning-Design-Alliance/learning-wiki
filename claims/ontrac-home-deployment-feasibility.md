## Evidence

### Mishra J 2016

Mishra J, Sagar R, Joseph A A, Gazzaley A, Merzenich M M. (2016). Training sensory signal-to-noise resolution in children with ADHD in a global mental health setting. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/tp.2016.45

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention seven-point Likert feasibility survey administered to ONTRAC completers (n=11), partial trainees (n=10) and active controls (n=7); the article reports enjoyment, frustration and initiation differences at P=0.04.

> "Comparing ONTRAC completers vs controls both of whom engaged in 30 h of intervention, the control group found their game-play more enjoyable (P=0.04), less frustrating (P=0.04), and easier to initiate (P=0.04) confirming our initial assumptions that control games may be more engaging (Table 1)."
