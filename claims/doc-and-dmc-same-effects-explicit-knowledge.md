## Evidence

### Jiang and Xiao 2014

Lin Jiang & Hailing Xiao. (2014). The Efficacy of Written Corrective Feedback and Language Analytic Ability on Chinese Learners’ Explicit and Implicit Knowledge of English Articles. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p22

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffe posthoc tests following one-way ANOVAs on error correction test scores for 92 learners. In Posttest 1 and 2, both treatment groups performed much better than the control group while no significant difference was found between the two treatment groups.

> "In the case of the error correction test, no significant difference was found between the two treatment groups in either Posttest 1 or Posttest 2, indicating that the two CF strategies had the same effects on the development of explicit knowledge."
