`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing the two training groups across five categories. Printed means: preparedness 8.44 vs 6.57 (t = 14.96), confidence 8.48 vs 6.62, adaptability 8.05 vs 5.99, technical proficiency 9.00 vs 7.13, problem-solving 8.61 vs 6.52, all p < 0.0001.
