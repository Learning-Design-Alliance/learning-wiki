# LSPPDM group scored higher on all five GOALS technical-skill dimensions in live-pig cholecystectomy

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The pedagogical group scored higher on depth perception, bimanual dexterity, efficiency, tissue handling and autonomy, and on mean GOALS score (all P < 0.050). [→ Pan Ruijun 2024](#pan-ruijun-2024)

## Evidence

### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GOALS technical-skill ratings in live-pig laparoscopic cholecystectomy, blindly and independently scored by five professionally trained assessors, n = 30 per group. Mean GOALS score was 4.24(0.44) versus 3.81(0.43); only t and P values are printed, no effect size.

> "Trainees in the pedagogical group scored higher in 'Depth perception' (t = 2.26, P = 0.028), 'Bimanual dexterity' (t = 3.23, P = 0.002), 'Efficiency' (t = 2.59, P = 0.012), 'Tissue handling' (t = 4.91, P = 0.000), 'Autonomy' (t = 2.78, P = 0.008) and 'Mean GOALS score' (t = 3.81, P = 0.000)."
