## Evidence

### Jourdi Georges 2025

Jourdi Georges, Selmi Mayssa, Gaussem Pascale, Truchot Jennifer, Margaill Isabelle, Siguret Virginie. (2025). Evaluation of the Inverted Classroom Approach in a Case-Study Course on Antithrombotic Drug Use in a PharmD Curriculum: French Monocentric Randomized Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67419

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Six-item satisfaction questionnaire at the end of the course, analyzed with Fisher exact test; IC students more often judged objectives unclear (28% vs 11%, P=.006) or unachieved (20% vs 2%, P<.001).

> "Indeed, 18/65 (28%) of the students found that the objectives of the case-study course not clearly defined and 13/65 (20%) considered that they were not achieved, in comparison to 10/93 (11%; P=.006) and 2/93 (2%; P<.001) in the control group, respectively."

### Jourdi Georges 2025 (2)

Jourdi Georges, Selmi Mayssa, Gaussem Pascale, Truchot Jennifer, Margaill Isabelle, Siguret Virginie. (2025). Evaluation of the Inverted Classroom Approach in a Case-Study Course on Antithrombotic Drug Use in a PharmD Curriculum: French Monocentric Randomized Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67419

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-course global satisfaction item in the randomized study; 88/93 (95%) control students satisfied versus 36/65 (55%) IC students, "P<.001" (Fisher exact test).

> "When it came to the question of the overall satisfaction of the educational approach, 88/93 (95%) of the students in the control group were satisfied versus (36/65) 55% in the IC group (P<.001)."
