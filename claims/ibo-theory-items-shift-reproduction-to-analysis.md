# IBO theoretical exam items shifted from pure reproduction of conceptual knowledge toward analyzing as the main cognitive process across the competition's history

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The share of 1990s theory items purely concerned with reproduction (remembering) fell from almost half to ca. 1% in most recent IBOs (η² = 0.28). [→ Opitz Sebastian 2020](#opitz-sebastian-2020)
`q2 i3` Items with analyzing as the main cognitive process increased from ca. 30% in the 1990s to 93% in 2017 (η² = 0.26). [→ Opitz Sebastian 2020 (2)](#opitz-sebastian-2020-2)
`q2 i?` Across all IBOs, required reproduction drew primarily on conceptual knowledge (mean 95%) rather than factual knowledge (mean 3%). [→ Opitz Sebastian 2020 (3)](#opitz-sebastian-2020-3)

## Evidence

### Opitz Sebastian 2020

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative-content-analysis item coding of six IBO assessment years, analyzed with one-factorial ANOVA and Games-Howell post hoc tests. Remembering fell from "almovst half of all tasks" to ca. 1% (F(5, 618) = 47.57, p < 0.001, η² = 0.28).

> "Among the theoretical exams in the 1990s, almovst half of all tasks were purely concerned with reproduction (remembering), while the share of such tasks (ca. 1%) is negligible in most recent IBOs (F(5, 618) = 47.57, p < 0.001, η² = 0.28)."

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (2)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

ANOVA of item codes across six IBO cohorts shows analyze rose from "1990s: ca. 30%; 2017: 93%" (F(5, 618) = 43.36, p < 0.001, η² = 0.26). The article attributes the decline of understand to this increase.

> "The subsequent decrease in this category is likely a result of a strong increase in items that included analyzing as the main cognitive process (1990s: ca. 30%; 2017: 93%; F(5, 618) = 43.36, p < 0.001, η² = 0.26), which is also visible in the analysis of scientific processes (Figure 3)."

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (3)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Knowledge-type coding of IBO items shows reproduction drew on conceptual knowledge ("mean: 95%") rather than factual knowledge (3%); descriptive means only, no test statistic reported.

> "However, a look at the underlying types of knowledge (Figure 5) revealed that, across all analyzed IBOs, the required reproduction did not primarily encompass factual (mean across all IBOs: 3%), but rather conceptual knowledge; that is, interrelationships between several facts (mean: 95%)."
