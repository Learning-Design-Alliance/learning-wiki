### Elakkiya Rajasekar 2026

Elakkiya Rajasekar. (2026). OBER+: Continuity-Aware Reporting and Traceable Continuous Improvement in Outcome-Based Education. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.03770](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.03770)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Alignment check of Eq. (13) on fifteen statement pairs from three consecutive-delivery transitions of two courses; among the ten pairs not identical character for character, numbering followed subject matter in only four cases. Several failures were wide, e.g. 0.955 against 0.933.

> "An ordering of cosine similarities says that in six of the ten outcomes whose wording changed at all, the number no longer attaches to the subject matter it carried in the previous delivery."
