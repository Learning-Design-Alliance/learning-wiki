Evidence¶

Brivael Hémon 2025¶

Brivael Hémon, Kevin de Checchi, Amaël Arguel, Bastien Trémolière, & Franck Amadieu. (2025). Relationships Between Critical Thinking Dispositions and Evaluation of Videos with Variation in the Level of Expertise of the Source Among Middle and High School Students. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105274

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis (Results §4.1) of the trustworthiness ratings on nine-point scales; "discernment score: M = 0.44, SD = 1.17" indicates a slight expert preference. No inferential test is reported for this contrast in the quoted text.

"Both videos featuring experts (M = 6.77, SD = 1.33) and laypersons (M = 6.33, SD = 1.27) are rather positively evaluated (i.e., above the median point), with a slight tendency to favor videos featuring experts (discernment score: M = 0.44, SD = 1.17)"
