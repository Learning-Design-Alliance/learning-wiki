`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate-adjusted mediation analysis with 5000 bootstrap samples on 421 newly graduated nurses, using PROCESS Model 4 with latent profile membership as a multicategorical independent variable. The article reports "Significant indirect associations through thriving at work were found for high versus low (B = 46.17, 95% bootstrap CI 36.73–55.58) and moderate versus low (B = 27.26, 95% bootstrap CI 21.30–33.45)."
