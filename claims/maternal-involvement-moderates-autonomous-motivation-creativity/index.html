Evidence¶

van der Zanden 2020¶

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a questionnaire study (N = 550, China), that "The effects were stronger for students with high maternal involvement." For senior high school students the positive effect of autonomous motivation was stronger with low paternal involvement.

"Liu et al. (2013) found that autonomous motivation related to creativity, but that the strength of the relationship depended on the level of parental involvement. The effects were stronger for students with high maternal involvement."

van der Zanden 2020 (2)¶

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a questionnaire study (N = 733, Korea), that perceived positive family processes were "positively associated with creativity." By contrast, Dai et al. (2012) found perceived teacher and parent support had no effect on creativity.

"Cho and Lin (2010) reported that adolescent perceptions of positive family processes (i.e., parental support and involvement) were positively associated with creativity."
