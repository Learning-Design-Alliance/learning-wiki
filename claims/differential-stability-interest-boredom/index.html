Evidence¶

Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025¶

Katharina Luisa Boehme, Thomas Goetz, Markus Feuchter, Franzis Preckel. (2025). Is Boredom the Opposite of Interest? A Longitudinal Reciprocal Effect Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09991-5

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Stability paths from the 2-variable latent cross-lagged models. In the combined 4-variable model, interest stabilities were 0.71–0.88, while boredom showed significant stability only from T1 to T2 (β = 0.29 and 0.34); later German boredom paths (β = 0.18, 0.17) did not reach significance. These are stability path coefficients, not effect sizes, so no magnitude code is assigned.

"In the mathematics model, interest displayed a generally much higher level of stability than boredom (boredom: βBO-M[T1] →BO-M[T2] = 0.53, βBO-M[T2] →BO-M[T3] = 0.32, βBO-M[T3] →BO-M[T4] = 0.26; interest: βINT-M[T1] →INT-M[T2] = 0.80, βINT-M[T2] →INT-M[T3] = 0.76, βINT-M[T3] →INT-M[T4] = 0.71)"
