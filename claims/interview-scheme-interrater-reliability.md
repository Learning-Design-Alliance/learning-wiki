## Evidence

### Bizrah M 2013

Bizrah M, Mukhtar; Iacoponi, Eduardo; Parker, Elizabeth; Rymer, Janice; Iversen, Amy; Wessely, Simon. (2013). A novel method of assessing quality of postgraduate psychiatry training: experiences from a large training programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-85

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater check in which both interviewers jointly scored 32 consultant trainers and 27 trainees before interviewing separately. The article reports "r = 0.74 for trainer scores and r = 0.92 for trainees' scores" and alphas of 0.82 and 0.96.

> "The Pearson's correlation coefficient for the two interviewers was r = 0.74 for trainer scores and r = 0.92 for trainees' scores. Cronbach's alpha internal consistency test was α = 0.82 for trainers and α = 0.96 for trainees."
