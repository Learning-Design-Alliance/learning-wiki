Rudolph 2025¶

Rudolph, J., Ismail, F., Tan, S., & Seah, P. (2025). Don't believe the hype. AI myths and the need for a critical approach in higher education. Journal of Applied Learning and Teaching, 8(1), 06-27. https://doi.org/10.37074/jalt.2025.8.1.1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Editorial analysis (critical essay, no empirical dataset reported) countering the myth that AI automatically produces democratic, equal, sustainable outcomes. The authors report their analysis reveals these technologies "tend to exacerbate existing inequalities". No effect size is reported.

"Contrary to claims that AI will automatically engender a more democratic, equal, and sustainable world, our analysis reveals that these technologies tend to exacerbate existing inequalities, environmental degradation, and labour precarity."
