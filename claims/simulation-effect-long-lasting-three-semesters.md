## Evidence

### Pajohideh Zahra Sadat 2023

Pajohideh Zahra Sadat, Mohammadi Solmaz, Keshmiri Fatemeh, Jahangirimehr Azam, Honarmandpour Azam. (2023). The effects of normal vaginal birth simulation training on the clinical skills of midwifery students: a quasi-experiment study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04319-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion conclusion drawn from the three-semester follow-up of the same 61 students, in which mean skill scores differed between groups in each semester (p < .001). The article frames this as evidence the simulation effect was "long-lasting".

> "The present study showed that simulation-based training as a preparation course before formal clinical education is associated with improving the students’ skills in normal vaginal delivery, and this effect was long-lasting."
