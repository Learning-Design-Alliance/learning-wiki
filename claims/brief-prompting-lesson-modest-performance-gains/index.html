Evidence¶

Keith Tran 2026¶

Keith Tran, Samiha Marwan, and Thomas Price. (2026). Teaching Prompt-Based Programming with LLMs: A 45-Minute Lesson with Guided Practice for End-User Programmers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30547

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled pre-test/post-test study with 55 engineering students (28 experimental, 27 control) analyzed with linear mixed-effects models; the experimental group improved by an estimated 0.87 problems versus 0.110 for control, "an effect larger than that of a full standard deviation of prior programming experience."

"The interaction between Time and Condition suggests that students in the experimental group showed greater gains from pre- to post-test than the control group ( 𝐵 = 0.756, 95% CI [−0.020, 1.533],𝑝 = 0.056)."
