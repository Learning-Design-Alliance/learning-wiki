Evidence¶

Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra 2022¶

Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra, No Yi, Wu Chien-Chih. (2022). The effectiveness of problem based learning in improving critical thinking, problem-solving and self-directed learning in first-year medical students: A meta-analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0277339

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of two studies of the problem-solving aspect between PBL and conventional learning methods, analyzed with random effects; heterogeneity was I2 = 86% (considerable). The pooled result was "more toward the conventional teaching" but not statistically significant.

"It is more toward the conventional teaching for enhancing problem-solving skills, but it was not statistically significant (Fig 4)."
