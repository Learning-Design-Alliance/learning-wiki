q3 · i? · causal · r2

Between-group comparison of writing quality gain in the 2×2 experiment (120 undergraduates, four conditions, T1–T3). Descriptive means show the ordering; the article reports the combined group's "strongest descriptive advantage in improving writing quality". No effect size value is printed for this contrast.

Related Claims¶

  • FRAC and the combined intervention shifted revision toward deeper levels, with deep revision (L3+L4) rising from 25% in the regular AI group to 55% in the combined group — related
  • FRAC and combined intervention advantages transferred to a new writing task and were retained after AI support was withdrawn — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • All three intervention groups converted AI feedback into effective revisions more successfully than regular AI use, with the combined group highest in effective adoption rate — related
  • In one 2×2 experiment with 120 undergraduates, an Assessment-Performance Calibration Activity (APCA) gave the most accurate self-assessment and least overconfidence, and adding a feedback-literacy script did not improve calibration further — related
  • The decisive empirical test of AI-augmented instruction frameworks is whether students retain representational competence when AI support is withdrawn — a broader claim this one bears on
