## Evidence

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled study training-behavior analysis: total HelpNeed steps averaged 25 (SD = 18) in Adaptive versus 43 (SD = 49) in Control, p = .02. The article reports this as the HNU predictor successfully predicting and preventing training unproductivity, unlike the prior HN study where no significant difference was found.

> "We found a signiﬁcant diﬀerence ( p = .02) in the HelpNeed steps between the HNU Adaptive condition ( Mean = 25, SD = 18) and the HNU Control ( Mean = 43, SD = 49)."

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022 (2)

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Step-behavior breakdown in the controlled study (Table 4): Adaptive had significantly more Expert steps and significantly fewer Far Off and Futile steps than Control; Strategic and Opportunistic steps showed no significant differences. Total training steps differed only marginally (p = .08).

> "T-tests show signiﬁcant diﬀerences between the two conditions in the Expert ( p = .02), Far Oﬀ ( p = .04), and Futile ( p = .02) steps."
