# In one group with no comparison group, vocabulary and pronunciation scores rose from pre-test to post-test after project-based CLIL, while grammar, fluency, and comprehensibility scores did not change

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paired t-tests showed significant pre-post differences on vocabulary and pronunciation aspects of oral ability, but not on grammar, fluency, or comprehensibility. [→ Yufrizal 2017](#yufrizal-2017)

## Evidence

### Yufrizal 2017

Yufrizal, H., Huzairin, & Hasan, B. (2017). Project Based - Content Language Integrated Learning (Clil) at Mathematics Department Universitas Lampung. English Language Teaching, 10(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n9p131

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests on five oral aspects for 88 students (Table 5): vocabulary p = .004, pronunciation p = .016, overall p = .032 significant; grammar p = .156, fluency p = .285, comprehensibility p = .208 not significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "There were no significant differences between the pre test score and the post test score on the aspects of gram mar, fluency, and comprehensibility. However, there was signiificant difference between the pre test score and the post test score in overall performance."
