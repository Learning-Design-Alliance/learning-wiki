Difficulty investigation (written responses and interviews). One interviewed student said "We cannot trust nondegenerate basis states for ﬁnding corrections to the energy", but any state in the nondegenerate subspace of H0 is a good state.

> "When students were asked to identify whether the originally chosen states were good states, roughly one-fourth of students were unable to correctly identify whether each state in the originally cho sen basis is a good state or not."
