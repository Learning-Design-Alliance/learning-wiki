## Evidence

### Baños Raúl 2020

Baños Raúl, Fuentesal Julio, Conte Luis, Ortiz-Camacho María del Mar, Zamarripa Jorge. (2020). Satisfaction, Enjoyment and Boredom with Physical Education as Mediator between Autonomy Support and Academic Performance in Physical Education. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17238898

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis (PROCESS model 4, 10,000-sample bootstrap) in a cross-sectional study of 374 girls and 374 boys in Nuevo León, Mexico. "enjoyment had an indirect, positive and significant effect" with IE = 0.058 and confidence intervals excluding zero; no standardized effect size printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "the interaction between autonomy support and PE grades was not mediated by boredom (IE = 0.002; IC = −0.0014; 0.0064). However, enjoyment had an indirect, positive and significant effect (IE = 0.058; IC = 0.0270; 0.0917) on the interaction between autonomy support and PE grades."
