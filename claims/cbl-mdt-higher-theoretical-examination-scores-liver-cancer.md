## Evidence

### Zhang Simin 2026

Zhang Simin, Li Jiao, Deng Cui, Zheng Sheng. (2026). CBL combined with MDT mode in clinical teaching of liver cancer: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09212-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled trial of 112 postgraduate students randomly assigned to experimental (n = 56) or control (n = 56) groups. Independent t-tests showed significantly higher theoretical examination scores in the experimental group, with a mean difference of 5.92 points (95% CI: 3.49–8.35).

> "Students in the experimental group achieved significantly higher theoretical examination scores (87.15 ± 5.34) compared to the control group (81.23 ± 6.12), with a mean difference of 5.92 points (95% confidence interval [CI]: 3.49–8.35, t = 3.41, P = 0.001)."
