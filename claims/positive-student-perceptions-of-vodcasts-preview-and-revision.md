Rae, M. G., & McCarthy, M. (2017). The impact of vodcast utilisation upon student learning of Physiology by first year graduate to entry medicine students. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 17(2), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v17i2.21125

Likert-scale attitudinal survey (86% response rate) measuring student perceptions of vodcasts as preview and revision tools. Descriptive percentages only; no inferential statistics or effect sizes reported.

> "nearly 64% of respondents either strongly agreed or agreed that previewing the vodcasts prior to attending lectures did improve their understanding of the material discussed in class, with only 6.6% disagreeing."
