Secondary null analyses in the same study: a one-way ANOVA on pre-test scores and an omnibus SEM comparing motivation (domain-specific academic self-concept and interest) across profiles; the motivation model comparison was not significant (χ2(10) = 10.56, p = .39). No effect sizes are printed.

> "We found no significant differences in prior knowledge in an one-way ANOV A with conceptual knowledge pre-test as an outcome measure and profile membership as predictor, F(5, 202) = 0.99, p = .43."
