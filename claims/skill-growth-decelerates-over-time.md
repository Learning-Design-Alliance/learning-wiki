### Connors 2025

Growth curve modeling of seven WJ-III subtest W-score outcomes over 5 years found significant fixed effects of intercept, linear, and quadratic growth in all seven outcomes; the article states "the negative coefficient for quadratic growth indicated that as time progressed, the rate of improvement for each skill slowed."
