## Evidence

### Thurston 2016

Thurston, A. (2016). Talk of the Town: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581531

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Cluster randomised controlled trial with 2696 pupils in 64 primary schools, schools randomised to intervention or business-as-usual. The report states "There was no evidence that Talk of the Town led to better reading levels" on the NGRT; the printed effect size was -0.03 (95% CI -0.46, 0.40).

> "There was no evidence that Talk of the Town led to better reading levels as assessed on the New Group Reading Test (GL NGRT), a test of reading comprehension, during the time period of the intervention."
