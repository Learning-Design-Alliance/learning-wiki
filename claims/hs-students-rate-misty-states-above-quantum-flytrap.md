## Evidence

### Dan-Adrian German 2026

Dan-Adrian German, Rebekah Randall, Charles Pope, Michele Roberts, John Phillips. (2026). Pathways to Quantum Science for High-School and Incoming College Students. Submitted to ACM SIGCSE '27. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.12437](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.12437)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student feedback section reports self-ratings from HS students in the dual-credit quantum classes on a 0-10 scale: flytrap 7.4, misty states with Qiskit 8.6, described as "considerably higher". Students also noted gameplay "would take over and become addictive". No effect size or significance test is printed.

> "On a scale from 0 (“learned nothing”) to 10 (”learned lots about quantum”)HSstudentsratedtheirquantumflytrapexperiencewith a 7.4 and mentioned that sometimes the gameplay would take over and become addictive. By contrast their experience with the misty states formalism (supported by [16] and Python implementation of circuits in IBM Qiskit) was an 8.6 (so considerably higher)."
