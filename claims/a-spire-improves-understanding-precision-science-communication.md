## Evidence

### Liu Minling 2026

Liu Minling, Mo Xiaocong, Li Tingwei, Dai Huiru, Fang Shuo. (2026). Bridging medical education and public health: a pedagogical model to develop future physicians' capacity in audience-tailored health communication. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1792396

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post Likert survey in a single-group pre-post study of an elective course session; pre (n=47) and post (n=54) questionnaires treated as independent samples. The article reports "dropped from 23.41 to 3.7%" and "rose from 29.27 to 87.04%" with P < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "The proportion of students “very/somewhat unfamiliar” with the concept dropped from 23.41 to 3.7%, while those “very/somewhat familiar” rose from 29.27 to 87.04% (P < 0.05)"

### Liu Minling 2026 (2)

Liu Minling, Mo Xiaocong, Li Tingwei, Dai Huiru, Fang Shuo. (2026). Bridging medical education and public health: a pedagogical model to develop future physicians' capacity in audience-tailored health communication. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1792396

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same pre-post questionnaire analysis (Mann-Whitney U for independent samples; Cronbach's alpha 0.73 pre, 0.80 post). The article reports the importance rating increase as significant with P < 0.05 and no effect size.

> "Recognition of its importance also increased significantly, with “very familiar” ratings rising from 44.68 to 64.81% (P < 0.05)"
