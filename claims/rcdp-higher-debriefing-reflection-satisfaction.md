## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoxue 2024

Wang Xiaoxue, Song Zixuan, Chen Xueting, Zhou Yangzi, Lou Yingze, Liu Tong, Zhang Dandan. (2024). Rapid cycle deliberate practice: application in forceps simulation training for gynecology and obstetrics residents. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2023.2301596

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-training anonymous questionnaire survey with a 100% response rate using the 18-item SSE scale. Table 4 prints Debrief and reflection 44.00 vs 41.00 (z = 5.239, p < .001) and total 87.00 vs 84.00 (z = 4.741, p < .001); Clinical reasoning and Clinical learning subscales did not differ significantly.

> "Particularly, the Debrief and Reflection subscale scores were notably higher in the RCDP group (p < .001), while no substantial differences emerged in the remaining subscales within both groups"

### Wang Xiaoxue 2024 (2)

Wang Xiaoxue, Song Zixuan, Chen Xueting, Zhou Yangzi, Lou Yingze, Liu Tong, Zhang Dandan. (2024). Rapid cycle deliberate practice: application in forceps simulation training for gynecology and obstetrics residents. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2023.2301596

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Visual Assessment Simulation (VAS) 0–100 self-rated confidence measure collected after training. The results report comparable confidence levels (z = 0.223); the printed p value of 8.24 appears inconsistent with a probability scale, as printed in the article.

> "Confidence levels in independent operations were comparable between RCDP and TTM groups (80.00 [range: 76.50–84.00]) vs. [80.0 (77.75–84.25)], z = 0.223; p = 8.24)"
