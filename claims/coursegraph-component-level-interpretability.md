# Component-level embeddings make CourseGraph interpretable: learning outcomes contributed most to an example overlap decision

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an interpretable mapping example, the learning outcomes contributed the most to the overlap decision between Distributed Data Systems (TUD) and Distributed Systems (LU). [→ Arthur Nijdam 2026](#arthur-nijdam-2026)

## Evidence

### Arthur Nijdam 2026

Arthur Nijdam, Paul Stankovski Wagner, Sara Ramezanian. (2026). CourseGraph: Finding overlaps and differences in Computer Science courses across universities. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05910 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.05910](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.05910)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case analysis of one CourseGraph mapping (Table II) shows cosine similarity of 0.81 for learning outcomes versus 0.70 for descriptions between the TUD and LU courses; the authors note the similarity "is significantly higher for the LOs than for the other course components".

> "We see that the LOs contributed the most to this particular decision. Upon inspecting the data, this makes sense: The description of the course at TUD is a lot longer and contains a more marketing-focused tone."
