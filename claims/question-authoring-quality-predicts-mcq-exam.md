## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Each unit increase in solo-authored question quality score predicted a 6.5% higher molecular biology MCQ exam mark in a marginal structural model with inverse-probability weighting. [→ Hilton 2022](#hilton-2022)

## Evidence

### Hilton 2022

Hilton, C. B., Goldwater, M. B., Hancock, D., Clemson, M., Huang, A., & Denyer, G. (2022). Scalable Science Education via Online Cooperative Questioning. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0249

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Marginal structural model (Tables 1 and Figure 1) on 634 students who authored questions and completed the final exam, with inverse-probability weighting to remove correlation between prior grades and authoring quality; the intercept was 36.434 and the authoring-score coefficient 6.545 (p < 0.001), "there is a predicted 6.5% improvement in the mark" per unit.

> "Holding the number of questions answered constant, for each unit increase in the question quality score (up to a maximum score of 3), there is a predicted 6.5% improvement in the mark"
