### Morris 2026 (2)

Morris, C., & Maes, P. (2026). When Peers Outperform AI (and When They Don't): Interaction Quality Over Modality. [doi:10.22318/cscl2026.989526](https://doi.org/10.22318/cscl2026.989526)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-condition comparison in the AI condition: the high-engagement group maintained higher question frequency and built on AI suggestions, while low-engagement participants adopted transactional patterns.

> "Despite receiving nearly identical chatbot behavior, participants diverged into the HQ-AI group (n=6) and LQ-AI group (n=6) based on their interaction patterns."
