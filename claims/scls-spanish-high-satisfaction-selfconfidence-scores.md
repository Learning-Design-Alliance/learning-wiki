Descriptive analysis of SCLS scores from 489 nursing students after clinical simulation sessions. The article reports the total mean of 54.4 (SD 6.2, range 19–65) and per-item dimension means of 4.2 and 4.1. Descriptive means only; no effect size printed.

> "The results have shown average scores above 4 for both dimensions, which shows that students satisfactorily value simulation during academic training"
