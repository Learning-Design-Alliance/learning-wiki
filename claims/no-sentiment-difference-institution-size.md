Yunjo An, Ji Hyun Yu and Shadarra James. (2025). Investigating the higher education institutions' guidelines and policies regarding the use of generative AI in teaching, learning, research, and administration. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00507-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Sentiment analysis of the 214-document corpus across institution sizes: small (mean = 0.975273), medium (mean = 0.961407), large (mean = 0.952171). The article reports the Kruskal–Wallis result as "no statistically significant differences in sentiment scores based on institution size"; the size trend is descriptive only.

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test was then applied, producing a statistic of 1.9726 (p = 0.3730). This indicates no statistically significant differences in sentiment scores based on institution size."
