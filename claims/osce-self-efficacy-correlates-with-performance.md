### Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis 2020

Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis, Pinto Rafael Zambelli, Arantes Paula Maria Machado, Vieira Érica Leandro Marciano, Teixeira Antônio Lúcio, Ferreira Fabiane Ribeiro, Vaz Daniela Virgínia. (2020). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02202-5

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the mixed-methods study of 31 students with complete self-efficacy and OSCE data; a 16-item confidence questionnaire was administered after the OSCE and before feedback. The printed r = 0.475 is a large correlation by the code table; the relationship is correlational, not causal.

> "A moderate and significant direct correlation (Fig. 1) was found for self-efficacy scores and OSCE scores (r = 0.475, p = 0.007)"
