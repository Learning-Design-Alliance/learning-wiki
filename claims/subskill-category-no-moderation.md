# Mathematics and writing subskill category (lower vs. higher order) does not significantly moderate the mathematics–writing relation

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Young-Suk Grace Kim 2024

Young-Suk Grace Kim, Dandan Yang, Jinkyung Hwang. (2024). Are Mathematics and Writing Skills Related? Evidence from Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09960-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderation analysis of mathematics subskill category across the 211 studies. No statistically significant difference between lower and higher order mathematics skills; equivalence was not tested. Writing subskill moderations were also non-significant (p = 0.77; p = 0.37).

> "the correlation with writing skills did not significantly differ for lower and higher order mathematics skills (b = − 0.02, SE = 0.03, p = 0.41)."
