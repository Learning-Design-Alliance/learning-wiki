## Evidence

### Pape-Koehler Carolina 2013

Pape-Koehler Carolina, Immenroth Marc, Sauerland Stefan, Lefering Rolf, Lindlohr Cornelia, Toaspern Jens, Heiss Markus. (2013). Multimedia-based training on Internet platforms improves surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-012-2672-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Secondary outcome analysis of the RCT using the OSATS global rating scale (five surgical criteria, 25 points maximum). The article reports significance without a test statistic or effect size, so no magnitude is asserted.

> "Except the control group, all the intervention groups showed significant improvement in the global rating scale (p ≤ 0.001)."

### Pape-Koehler Carolina 2013 (2)

Pape-Koehler Carolina, Immenroth Marc, Sauerland Stefan, Lefering Rolf, Lindlohr Cornelia, Toaspern Jens, Heiss Markus. (2013). Multimedia-based training on Internet platforms improves surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-012-2672-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive comparison of ΔOSATS global rating scale means across the three intervention groups as printed in the results and Table 6. The authors note this was surprising because the practical training group was assumed to have more practical experience on the rated motor skills; the comparison is reported descriptively with printed means and SDs.

> "An amazing result was that both groups undergoing multimedia-based training (multimedia-based and combination training) were better (respectively 6.94 ± 5.35 and 7.94 ± 6.35) than the practical training group (4.06 ± 4.09) in the global rating scale"
