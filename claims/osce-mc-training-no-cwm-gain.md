## Evidence

### Yanase Yukiko 2026

Yanase Yukiko, Hiragi Saki, Kawahara Yasuhiro. (2026). Effects of generic medical communication training on physiotherapy students’ ability to communicate in movement-based clinical scenarios: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08560-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison using Steel post-hoc tests on total CWM scores from a performance task with three standardized-patient tests. Both student groups had median 6; the article reports "p = 0.995, effect size: d = 0" and a post-hoc power of 0.050, and the conclusion states the study was underpowered to detect small or moderate effects.

> "There was no significant difference between the learned (median: 6) and unlearned (median: 6) groups (p = 0.995, effect size: d = 0, power: 1 − β = 0.050)."
