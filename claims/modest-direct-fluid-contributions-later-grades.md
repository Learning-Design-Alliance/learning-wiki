## Evidence

### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

QNTS path analysis. Direct standardized paths were small: visual-spatial skills to grade 3 (β = .08) and grade 4 (β = .12), short-term memory to grade 6 (β = .07), all significant beyond crystallized abilities and stability paths but each below .2.

> "cognitive visual-spatial skills predicted achievement in grade 3 (β = .08, p < .04) and grade 4 (β = .12, p < .001), and short-term memory predicted achievement in grade 6 (β = .07, p < .01)."

### Carpentier Philippe 2022 (2)

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

QNTS null result for the DCCS measure of cognitive flexibility, tested at 63 months; no significant direct path to any grade's achievement remained after crystallized abilities were in the model. No effect size was printed for this null.

> "Finally, cognitive flexibility did not significantly contribute to any school achievement measures beyond early crystallized abilities."
