## Evidence

### Mintesnot Helen 2024

Mintesnot Helen, Tadele Henok, Ahmed Hayat, Moges Tamirat, Gedlu Etsegenet. (2024). Electrocardiography interpretation competency among pediatric and child health residents at Addis Ababa University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06614-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Association analyses from the cross-sectional study of 95 residents; printed p-values are 0.018, 0.018, and 0.046, with no effect sizes. The authors interpret this as more exposure bringing expertise in ECG diagnosis.

> "The diagnosis of SVT was associated with the year of residency with more performance among junior residents, p = 0.018 and the number of monthly ECG orders, p = 0.018. The diagnosis of atrial fibrillation was associated with years of clinical/public service before residency enrollment with more years adding advantage, p = 0.046."
