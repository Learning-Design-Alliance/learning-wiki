David Racovan 2026¶

David Racovan, Ajay Rawat, Christopher K. May, Jeffrey A. Turkstra. (2026). Argus: Academic Integrity in the Era of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36073

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational comparison of students who attended Midterm 1 but neither later exam against completers, excluding Spring 2020; withdrawn students' mean H-score was 9.9 versus 3.9, and in Spring 2026 19.5 versus 11.4. Only the D statistic is printed, so no effect size is coded.

"A two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test indicates that the two groups are drawn from significantly different distributions (𝐷= 0.362,𝑝< 0.001)."
