## Evidence

### Coşkun and Eker 2018

İsmail Coşkun, Cevat Eker. (2018). The Effect of Teaching Activities Done by Using Activity Based Posters on the Students' Academic Achievements, Retention Levels in Their Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(4): 585-597. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.060402

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of the experimental group's pre-test (mean 9.63, SS = 4.38) and post-test (mean 19.8, SS = 4.71) on the 25-item Celebrities Achievement Test, n = 30. The article reports a significant difference with t = 9.19 and p < 0.05, calling the poster teaching "positively efficient in the success of the student". Note the article's sentence says control group but the table and section are the experimental group.

> "The difference between the pre- test and post-test scores of the control group was compared with the t test and it was found there was a significant difference with the value obtained (t = 9.19) and  p <0.05)."
