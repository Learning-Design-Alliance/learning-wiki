### Edda Sant 2019

Edda Sant. (2019). Democratic Education: A Theoretical Review (2006–2017). Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654319862493

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discursive analysis of the elitist version, following Schumpeter's and Lippmann's elitist democracy, finds that "Elitists recommend different educational practices for students conditioned by the social role that each student will pursue," citing Singapore's tiered citizenship programs as an example.
