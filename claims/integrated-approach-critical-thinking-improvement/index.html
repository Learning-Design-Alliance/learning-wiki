Yimwilai 2015¶

Supaporn Yimwilai. (2015). An Integrated Approach to Teaching Literature in an EFL Classroom. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p14

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Three-way ANOVA with one repeated measure on pretest/posttest critical thinking scores of 78 students in four groups (N = 24/24/15/15). The Pre-Post * Group interaction was significant with η2 = .718; Table 5 reports experimental improvements of 7.92 (UP) and 5.07 (UB) versus 1.29 and .57 for controls, each significantly higher.
