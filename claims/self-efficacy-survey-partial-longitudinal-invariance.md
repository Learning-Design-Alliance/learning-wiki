### Soland 2019

Soland, J. (2019). English Language Learners, Self-efficacy, and the Achievement Gap: Understanding the Relationship between Academic and Social-emotional Growth (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/english-language-learners-self-efficacy-and-the-achievement-gap-understanding-the-relationship-between-academic-and-social-emotional-growth/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal measurement invariance testing (Appendix C, Table A5) following Widaman, Ferrer, and Conger, with time periods as groups. Weak invariance constraints improved fit with non-significant LRTs; strong invariance produced a significant LRT decrement traced to three threshold constraints. No effect size is printed.

> "Given the model fit was excellent for partial invariance (RMSEA = .02), the partial model became the preferred model for subsequent analyses. Ultimately, these results indicate that fitting a growth model to the self-efficacy scores is justifiable."
