### Darie S. 2026

Darie S., Wainman B.C., Wohl G.R., Wang A.Y., Zhang B.Z., Li A., Ahmad E., Palombella A.S., Rockarts J.L., de SA D.L., Nguyen L.N., DeCarlo B., Downer N., Brewer‐Deluce D. (2026). Preliminary insights on high‐fidelity embalming solutions for surgical skills training—An evaluation using the McMaster Embalming Scale and mechanical tests. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70185

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA of percent mass change in the tissue dehydration test, a mechanical proxy for electrocautery, conducted on three epidermal–dermal samples per donor dried in a furnace to stable mass. The article reports "ICL lost significantly more fluid than the rest of the samples, whereas SRF lost significantly less" (partial η 2 = 0.969).

> "Specifically, ICL lost significantly more fluid than the rest of the samples, whereas SRF lost significantly less than the other samples (F(3, 8) = 83.582, p < 0.001, partial η 2 = 0.969, Figure 6)."
