## Evidence

### Härtl Anja 2015

Härtl Anja, Bachmann Cadja, Blum Katharina, Höfer Stefan, Peters Tim, Preusche Ingrid, Raski Bianca, Rüttermann Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Wünsch Alexander, Kiessling Claudia, GMA-Ausschuss Kommunikative und Soziale Kompetenzen. (2015). Desire and reality – teaching and assessing communicative competencies in undergraduate medical education in German-speaking Europe – a survey. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000998](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000998)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey result on feedback in assessment. In 17 programmes feedback also included results for each individual station; detailed feedback went to all students in eight programmes and to subgroups such as students who had not passed in twelve.

> "Feedback on OSCE results was given in the form of overall results to students in 24 of the 31 programmes in which this assessment format was implemented."

### Härtl Anja 2015 (2)

Härtl Anja, Bachmann Cadja, Blum Katharina, Höfer Stefan, Peters Tim, Preusche Ingrid, Raski Bianca, Rüttermann Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Wünsch Alexander, Kiessling Claudia, GMA-Ausschuss Kommunikative und Soziale Kompetenzen. (2015). Desire and reality – teaching and assessing communicative competencies in undergraduate medical education in German-speaking Europe – a survey. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000998](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000998)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Perspectives section, drawn from heterogeneous survey answers on the theoretical models underlying feedback delivery; a KusK task force is formulating recommendations on the basis of the latest literature and practical examples.

> "From the heterogeneity of the answers, the conclusion can be drawn that, in contrast with the SPIKES protocol, no feedback model has prevailed that has allowed for a standardisation of instruction."
