## Evidence

### Scicluna Helen A 2012

Scicluna Helen A, Grimm Michael C, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter, Pilotto Louis S, Jones Philip D, McNeil H Patrick. (2012). Clinical capabilities of graduates of an outcomes-based integrated medical program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-23

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-sample t-test comparison of the 2009 UNSW graduates' PHPQ means against published benchmark cohorts from other Australian medical schools. Ratings were "equivalent ratings on the 'prevention' and 'interpersonal skills' subscales" and significantly higher on confidence and collaboration.

> "graduates of the new outcomes-based UNSW program had equivalent ratings on the 'prevention' and 'interpersonal skills' subscales, but significantly higher ratings (P < 0.001) than published benchmarks for the 'confidence' and 'collaboration' subscales"
