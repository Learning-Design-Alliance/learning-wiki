# Students rate DiffGame as enjoyable and valuable, but intrinsic motivation does not correlate with performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 117 students, 60 completed the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory; enjoyment (M = 4.00, SD = 0.93) and value (M = 4.27, SD = 0.93) scales were high, but Spearman correlations between both scales and percentage of correct answers were insignificant. [→ Pedersen 2016](#pedersen-2016)
`q2 i?` Enjoyment and value scales each showed good internal consistency (Cronbach alphas of 0.91 and 0.90 respectively). [→ Pedersen 2016 (2)](#pedersen-2016-2)
