Project outlook reporting post-course activity: host sites running their own outreach and training with the donated detectors, groups interacting to share and analyze data toward a collaboration, and unvisited universities building their own detectors from the Escaramujo design.

> "Several sites have been organizing their own outreach and training sessions using the donated detector, enlarging the impact of the Escaramujo Project. As all the detectors are identical, most of the groups are starting to interact to each other to share and analyze the data they collect, organizing a collaboration."
