## Evidence

### Balideh Donya 2026

Balideh Donya, Farokhzadian Jamileh, Miri Sakineh, Shahraki Sedigheh Khodabandeh, Motamed‐Jahromi Mohadeseh. (2026). Effects of an Aesthetics‐Based Educational Intervention on Compassion Competence and Aesthetic Care Quality Among Psychiatric Nurses: A Quasi‐Experimental Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70854

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post-intervention between-group comparisons on the 16-item Compassion Competence Scale in the 90-nurse quasi-experiment. All three subscales favored the intervention group (all p < 0.001); the article reports "large effect sizes (Cohen's d = 1.26–1.68)" across compassion competence domains, and no significant control-group changes.

> "Specifically, communication increased to 25.91 ± 7.92 in the intervention group compared with 18.17 ± 3.47 in the control group (t = 5.99, p < 0.001). Likewise, sensitivity scores were significantly higher in the intervention group (19.46 ± 4.82) than in the control group (12.28 ± 2.38) (t = 7.71, p < 0.001)."
