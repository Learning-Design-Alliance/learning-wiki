Nick C. Ellis 2002¶

Nick C. Ellis. (2002). Frequency Effects in Language Processing: A Review with Implications for Theories of Implicit and Explicit Language Acquisition. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 24, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263102002024

The review argues that because learners show "sensitivity to frequency in all these domains", theories of implicit and explicit learning and their interaction must accommodate frequency effects. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
