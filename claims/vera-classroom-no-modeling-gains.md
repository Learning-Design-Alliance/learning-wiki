## Evidence

### An 2022

An, S., Bates, R., Rugaber, S., Hammock, J., Weigel, E., & Goel, A. K. (2022). Cognitive Assistance for Inquiry-Based Modeling. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same classroom quasi-experiment (N=52 paired pre/post). The article reports no significant gain on modeling and simulation questions, with "p value=0.501". The authors posit three possible reasons: learning may not have occurred, questions may have been inadequate, or the pre/post-test method may not capture modeling-skill gains.

> "we did not observe similar gains on questions pertaining to conceptual and simulation modeling as parts of the scientiﬁc process (p value=0.501)"
