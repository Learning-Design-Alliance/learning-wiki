## Evidence

### Zhang Ke 2026

Zhang Ke, Yang Meng, Li Liying. (2026). Optimization of academic performance and mental health in college students through an AI-driven personalized physical exercise and mindfulness intervention system. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37028-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Results of a 16-week stratified randomized trial with 328 undergraduates in three Chinese universities, analyzed with multi-level mixed-effects modeling. The results section reports patterns "favoring the AI-personalized intervention group compared to both standardized intervention and control conditions"; the abstract prints a 10.28% GPA increase, 95% CI [8.94, 11.62], d = 0.89, p < 0.001.

> "Academic performance outcomes revealed differential patterns favoring the AI-personalized intervention group compared to both standardized intervention and control conditions, though these between-group differences should be interpreted as associations rather than definitive causal effects given the quasi-experimental design."
