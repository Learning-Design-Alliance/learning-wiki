## Evidence

### Knorr Mirjana 2024

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis (Cronbach's alpha over 12 scenarios) in the German pilot sample of 582 participants across the 2020 and 2021 test dates. The article reports the alphas with 95% confidence intervals as "satisfactory in both years".

> "The internal consistency for Casper scenario scores was α=0.73, 95% CI [0.69, 0.77] in 2020 and α=0.82, 95% CI [0.79, 0.86] in 2021."

### Knorr Mirjana 2024 (2)

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Interrater agreement computed for 2021 responses each rated by two independent raters on a 9-point global scale. The discussion notes this was "only moderate" and diverged from the 0.95 agreement in the Canadian pilot study.

> "For responses evaluated by two raters in 2021, overall interrater agreement was ICC(1,2)=0.54."

### Knorr Mirjana 2024 (3)

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation across the one-year interval for the n=20 subsample sitting both test dates. The authors state the subsample was too small to draw definite conclusions and call for a targeted test-retest design.

> "Re-test reliability for twenty participants who completed Casper in both years was ρ=0.29 (Spearman's rank correlation)."
