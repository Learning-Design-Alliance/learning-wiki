## Evidence

### Bueckle 2022

Bueckle, Andreas; Buehling, Kilian; Shih, Patrick C.; Börner, Katy. (2022). Optimizing Performance and Satisfaction in Matching and Movement Tasks in Virtual Reality with Interventions Using the Data Visualization Literacy Framework. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2021.727344](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2021.727344)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

RUI VR study, Plateau phase results for the VR Tabletop and VR Standup setups: the article reports "no difference between the cohorts for completion time, position, and rotation accuracy for the two VR setups". Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes are printed.

> "We found no difference between the cohorts for completion time, position, and rotation accuracy for the two VR setups."

### Bueckle 2022 (2)

Bueckle, Andreas; Buehling, Kilian; Shih, Patrick C.; Börner, Katy. (2022). Optimizing Performance and Satisfaction in Matching and Movement Tasks in Virtual Reality with Interventions Using the Data Visualization Literacy Framework. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2021.727344](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2021.727344)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

RUI VR study, 2D Desktop setup: position accuracy (centroid distance) medians were 0.01 (experiment) vs. 0.013 (control), reported as "a not significant difference in position accuracy" (p = 0.085). No effect size is printed.

> "as well as a not significant difference in position accuracy (median experiment group: 0.01, IQR: [0.006 - 0.012], median control group: 0.013, IQR: [0.01 - 0.016], Mann-Whitney-U-Test, p = 0.085)"
