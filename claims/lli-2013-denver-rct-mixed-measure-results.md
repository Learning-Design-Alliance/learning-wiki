### Leveled Literacy Intervention 2017

Leveled Literacy Intervention. (2017). What Works Clearinghouse Beginning Reading intervention report. U.S. Department of Education. http://www.fountasandpinnell.com/intervention/

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of LLI in 13 urban elementary and K–8 schools in Denver, Colorado, with 163 LLI and 157 comparison students. Results varied by outcome measure; the report states that "For DRA2, the authors reported positive and statistically significant findings for students in kindergarten and no statistically significant findings for students in grades 1 and 2." The BAS domain average effect size was 0.29 and the DRA2 average was –0.02.
