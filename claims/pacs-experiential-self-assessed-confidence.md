## Evidence

### Chen Yingqian 2019

Chen Yingqian, Zheng Keguo, Ye Shanshan, Wang Jifei, Xu Ling, Li Ziping, Meng Quanfei, Yang Jianyong, Feng Shi-Ting. (2019). Constructing an experiential education model in undergraduate radiology education by the utilization of the picture archiving and communication system (PACS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1827-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment 5-point Likert questionnaire completed by all 40 experiential-group students and 47 of 61 controls. Table 2 items on DICOM viewer familiarity, CT sequences, reading sequence, and reconstruction methods all reached p < 0.001; confidence in reading CT during internship p = 0.047. No effect sizes printed.

> "In comparison to the control group, the experiential education group had increased familiarity with each of the following: the DICOM viewer, the sequences and reconstruction methods of CT imaging, the diagnosis thinking principle, and anatomy. These findings serve to strengthen students' self-confidence in their ability to perform future clinical work."
