## Evidence

### Paterna 2024

Paterna, Adrian; Alcaraz-Ibáñez, Manuel; Aguilar-Parra, Jose M.; Salavera, Carlos; Demetrovics, Zsolt; Griffiths, Mark D. (2024). Problematic smartphone use and academic achievement: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Behavioral Addictions. https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2024.00014

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 33 effect sizes from 29 cross-sectional studies (N = 48,490) retrieved up to February 2023, pooled with a random-effects model. The printed effect size is r = −0.110 (p < 0.001), described as "a negative small-sized effect" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 93.70).

> "Findings from the random-effects model (see Fig. 2) showed a negative small-sized effect (r = −0.110, p < 0.001; 95% CI = −0.156 to −0.066, I2 = 93.70)."
