Evidence¶

Hamit Yokus 2015¶

Hamit Yokus, Tuba Yokus. (2015). Polyrhythmic Tapping: Examining the Effectiveness of the Strategy of Organizing Rhythmic Structures through Synthesis. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.1.1917

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized pre-test/post-test control group experiment with N = 18 second-year music education students (n = 9 per group). Post-test total means were X = 85.67 (SD = 2.35) experimental versus X = 49.00 (SD = 8.80) control; the Mann Whitney U test showed "a significant difference between the groups" at p < .001.

"According to the Mann Whitney U test results, based on total post-test scores of the groups from the polyrhythmic tapping test, there was a significant difference between the groups in terms of all sub-dimensions and on the test as a whole. The experimental group did better on the test ( p < . 001)."
