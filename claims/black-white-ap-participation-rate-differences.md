Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive statistics from the CRDC across three academic years show Black students underrepresented at every stage: for example, in 2017-18, 14.2% of Black students vs. 26.3% of White students were enrolled in AP courses, and 37.4% vs. 66.0% passed at least one AP exam in 2015-16.

> "Differences in Black–White participation rates were significant across all metrics and years (Ps < 0.001)."
