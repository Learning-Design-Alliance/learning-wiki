## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gross D 2015

Gross D, Pietri ES, Anderson G, Moyano-Camihort K, Graham MJ. (2015). Increased Preclass Preparation Underlies Student Outcome Improvement in the Flipped Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0040

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of prerecorded lecture video access logs in the flipped-format course, excluding 14 students who never accessed the videos. The printed effect size is r(235) = 0.22, p < 0.001, for "a significant correlation between average exam score and video access."

> "there was a significant correlation between average exam score and video access, r(235) = 0.22, p < 0.001 (Figure 5A)"

### Gross D 2015 (2)

Gross D, Pietri ES, Anderson G, Moyano-Camihort K, Graham MJ. (2015). Increased Preclass Preparation Underlies Student Outcome Improvement in the Flipped Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0040

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Observational log analysis of video accesses by GPA quartile in the flipped course. The printed effect size is r(235) = 0.24, p < 0.001, for a "significant positive correlation between GPA and video accesses," with top-quartile students opening files an average of 20.8 times versus 8.2–14.3 for lower quartiles.

> "There is a significant positive correlation between GPA and video accesses (r(235) = 0.24, p < 0.001)."
