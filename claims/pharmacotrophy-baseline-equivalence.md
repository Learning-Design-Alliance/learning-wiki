## Evidence

### Delage Clément 2024

Delage Clément, Palayer Maeva, Lerouet Dominique, Besson Valérie C. (2024). “Pharmacotrophy”: a playful tournament for game- and team-based learning in pharmacology education - assessing its impact on students’ performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05157-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective baseline analysis of the 3rd-year fundamental pharmacology exam, comparing future tournament participants with the rest of the class in 2021 and 2022. The 2022 comparison shows “marks of 11.61 ± 0.43 (n = 20)” versus “10.73 ± 0.12 (n = 339)” with no significant difference.

> "Likewise, in 2022, 3rd-year participants achieved marks of 11.61 ± 0.43 (n = 20), which were similar to the mean mark of 10.73 ± 0.12 (n = 339) obtained by the rest of the class (p = 0.1033 b)."
