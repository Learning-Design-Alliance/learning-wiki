Patricia D. Simon 2026¶

Patricia D. Simon, Lily Min Zeng, Luke K. Fryer. (2026). A systematic review of student engagement research in adaptive learning platforms. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100360

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing its included studies, that "engagement increases when activities are tailored" to learner needs and emotional state; one cited study gave immediate feedback and alternative materials when negative emotions were detected.

"Results suggest that engagement increases when activities are tailored to the personal needs and emotional state of the learner [39–46]."
