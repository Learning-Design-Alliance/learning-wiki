Frequency counts from grounded theory coding of 1,054 student responses. The Output Overwhelm and Verification Strain indicators captured difficulty handling AI response volume and exhaustion from repeated checking; e.g., "I feel mentally exhausted after checking so many outputs" (n = 72).
