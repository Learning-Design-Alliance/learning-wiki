## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Social congruence of the tutor significantly influenced concept recall after the problem analysis, self-directed learning and reporting phases. [→ Chng Esther 2011](#chng-esther-2011)

## Evidence

### Chng Esther 2011

Chng Esther, Yew Elaine H. J., Schmidt Henk G. (2011). Effects of tutor-related behaviours on the process of problem-based learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9282-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of concept recall scores from 223 second-year polytechnic students in a one-day PBL Immunology cycle, with prior GPA as covariate and tutors ranked into low/medium/high social congruence groups. The article reports "a significant effect on the total number of concepts recalled" at all three phases, with the F statistics printed above; no effect size is reported.

> "Social congruence was found to have a significant effect on the total number of concepts recalled at the end of the problem analysis phase, F(2, 219) = 10.38, p < 0.01; self-directed learning phase, F(2, 219) = 9.83, p < 0.01; and reporting phase, F(2, 219) = 6.51, p < 0.01."
