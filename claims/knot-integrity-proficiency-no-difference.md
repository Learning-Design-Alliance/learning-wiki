## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Knot integrity points did not differ significantly between groups (3.18 ± 1.2 vs 3.4 ± 0.6, p = 0.76), nor did technical proficiency points (22.7 ± 5.6 vs 24.3 ± 2.9, p = 0.22). [→ Axt Steffen 2018](#axt-steffen-2018)
