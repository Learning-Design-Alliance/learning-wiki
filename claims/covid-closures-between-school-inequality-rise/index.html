Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Estimation of across-school inequality measures (Gini, 90/10 ratio, entropy) for the full 2015-2020 sample with full controls; the Gini increased by 0.01 (mathematics) and 0.02 (Dutch) from means of 0.14 and 0.11, and the 90/10 ratio increased by 0.1 and 0.03 respectively. No standardised effect size is printed.

"With an increase by 7% for mathematics and 18% for Dutch, this amounts to a similar effect size as the increase in within-school inequality."
