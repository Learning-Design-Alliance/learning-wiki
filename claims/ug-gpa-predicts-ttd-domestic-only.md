### Walters T 2022

Walters T, Abeyta A, Bean AJ, Wilson MA. (2022). The impact of holistic review on correlations between doctoral student outcomes, and GPA and GRE scores in the biomedical sciences. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0279258. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279258

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation of undergraduate GPA with time to degree across the 2007–2012 GSBS cohort; the domestic-student correlation (Table 2, p = 0.0307) was the only significant one, though the printed coefficient is very small (r = -0.005467).

> "The only statistically significant correlation that was observed was for domestic students with a p value of 0.0307. No statistically significant differences in slope or elevation were observed in any categories."
