Analysis of coded explanations (Figs. 4 and 5) from the survey; the Statistics code covers reasoning that considers statistical effects independently of the physical or measurement systems. The authors suggest students were applying a "more data is better" heuristic or a phenomenological primitive, possibly conflating standard deviation with the standard uncertainty of the mean.

> "In contrast, students who indicated the distribution would become more narrow primarily argued through purely statistical reasoning."
