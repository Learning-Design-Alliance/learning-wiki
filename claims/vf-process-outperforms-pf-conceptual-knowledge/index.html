Evidence¶

Christian Hartmann 2021¶

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experiment with N = 173 secondary students assigned to PF, VF-process, or VF-outcome; the ANCOVA contrast on conceptual knowledge post-test scores, controlling for mathematical ability and prior knowledge, showed VF-process "was actually more effective for conceptual knowledge acquisition than PF" (F(1, 166) = 6.91, p = 0.009).

  • Vicarious failure with high solution diversity is as effective as engaging in Productive Failure oneself for conceptual knowledge acquisition — related
  • Students observing high-diversity vicarious failure (VF-high) significantly outperform students in VF-low and DI conditions on conceptual knowledge — related
  • Collaborative and individual problem solving in traditional PF do not differ in conceptual knowledge after instruction — related
