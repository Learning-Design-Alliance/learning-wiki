Theoretical analysis in the review's Pathway 1 section, drawing on reviews of generative AI documenting both explanatory usefulness and vulnerability to fabricated content. The review hedges: direct evidence about how particular learners calibrate trust remains uneven, so the stronger claim is an expectation of miscalibration. No effect size is reported.

"Fluency is epistemi- cally persuasive, because users can mistake ease of process- ing for reliability. The educational risk is not error alone but the alignment of error, confidence and immediacy."
