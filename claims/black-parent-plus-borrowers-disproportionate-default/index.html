Victoria Jackson 2023¶

Victoria Jackson, Brittani Williams, and Jalil B. Mustaffa. (2023). Parent PLUS Loans Are a Double-Edged Sword for Black Borrowers. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/parent-plus-loans-are-a-double-edged-sword-for-black-borrowers

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The brief attributes this comparison to an Urban Institute report on reshaping Parent PLUS loans, reporting that "Twenty percent of Black Parent PLUS borrowers default versus only 5% of White parent borrowers." It notes overall Parent PLUS default rates are lower than student loan default rates, but Black PLUS borrowers' rates remain disproportionately high.
