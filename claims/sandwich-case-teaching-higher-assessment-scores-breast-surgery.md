## Evidence

### Li L 2026

Li L, Yang W, Jia H. (2026). Effect of traditional teaching and sandwich combination in clinical teaching of granulomatous mastitis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047762

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of 80 breast surgery interns (40 per group) taught by traditional versus sandwich joint case teaching from January 2023 to January 2024; end-of-semester assessments scored out of 100. The article reports "statistical significance (P < .05)" for all three assessments; Table 1 prints t values of 6.932, 9.657, and 2.247.

> "Both the theoretical assessment score and the practical assessment score of the experimental group were higher than those of the control group, and the comprehensive assessment score of the experimental group was also higher than that of the control group, with statistical significance (P < .05)"
