### Ross Andrew 2019

Ross Andrew, Dlungwane Thembelihle, Van Wyk Jacqueline. (2019). Using poster presentation to assess large classes: a case study of a first-year undergraduate module at a South African university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1863-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Items from the student questionnaire (n = 230 returned) in the cross-sectional case study: 201 of 226 respondents (89%) agreed group work helped them learn more than working alone; 211 of 226 (93%) agreed the poster preparation and presentation encouraged interaction with their group.
