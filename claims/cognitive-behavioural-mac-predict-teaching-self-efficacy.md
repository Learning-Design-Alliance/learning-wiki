## Evidence

### Fonseca-Pinto R and Moreno-Murcia JA 2024

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise linear regression predicting teaching self-efficacy from teaching methodologies and MAC in the same survey of aquatic educators; the article reports the standardized coefficients and "a total explained variance of 23%". No standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "cognitive (ß = .35, p < .01), behavioural (ß = .16, p < .01), and MAC (ß = .18, p < .01) methodologies positively predicted teaching self-efficacy with a total explained variance of 23%"
