## Evidence

### Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy 2022

Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy, Ziwen Han, Hammad Shaikh, Qi Yin Zheng, Lisa-Angelique Lim, Anna Rafferty, Andrew Petersen, and Joseph Jay Williams. 2022. How can Email Interventions Increase Students’ Completion of Online Homework? A Case Study Using A/B Comparisons. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22), March 21–25, 2022, Online, USA. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506874

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kolmogorov-Smirnov equality-of-distributions tests comparing each condition's homework start-time distribution to control (Figure 3 box plots; students who did not attempt removed). The article reports "a significant (p<.05) difference in start time dis- tribution" for sender (instructor), recommendation (no prompt), and advice (no prompt), and marginal significance (.05≤p<.1) for information and advice prompt conditions. No effect size is printed.

> "Our results show a significant (p<.05) difference in start time dis- tribution for sender (control vs. instructor), recommendation (con- trol vs. no prompt), and advice (control vs. no prompt) variations."
