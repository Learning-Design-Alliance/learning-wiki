# PBL produced better social science learning outcomes than Direct Instruction in fourth-grade elementary students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fourth-grade students taught with PBL scored higher on social science post-tests than students taught with Direct Instruction (F = 54.929 > 3.933). [→ Permatasari and Gunarhadi 2019](#permatasari-and-gunarhadi-2019)

## Evidence

### Permatasari and Gunarhadi 2019

Berti Dyah Permatasari, Gunarhadi, & Riyadi. (2019). The influence of problem based learning towards social science learning outcomes viewed from learning interest. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 8(1), 39-46. https://iaescore.com/journals/index.php/IJERE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with pre-test/post-test control group design; 50 experimental (PBL) and 59 control (DI) students. Post-test means were 74.24 (PBL) vs 51.80 (DI); the ANOVA model effect was significant: "F a = 54.929 > 3.933". No effect size is printed.

> "Based on the results of post-test on social studies subjects, the average student taught using the PBL model was higher and higher than the average student who was taught using DI (Direct Instruction) model, although both experienced an increase from the results of the pre-test."
