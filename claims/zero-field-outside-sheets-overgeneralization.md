Difficulty observed on post-test questions (4) and (5) about three infinite parallel sheets, probed in interviews where a student generalized from the parallel-plate capacitor case. The article reports "the most common mistake, especially in the comparison group, was assuming that the electric field is zero at a point outside". Qualitative evidence; no effect size.
