## Evidence

### Lim Hooi Min 2021

Lim Hooi Min, Ng Chirk Jenn, Teo Chin Hai, Lee Ping Yein, Kassim Puteri Shanaz Jahn, Nasharuddin Nurul Amelina, Yong Phelim Voon Chen, Sellappans Renukha, Yap Wei Hsum, Lee Yew Kong, Abu Hassan Zahiruddin Fitri, Krishnan Kuhan, Ghazali Sazlina Shariff, Idris Faridah, Hassan Nurhanim, Ayub Enna, Konstantinidis Stathis, Taylor Michael, Poussa Cherry, Karlgren Klas, Stathakarou Natalia, Mordt Petter, Nilsen Arne Thomas, Wharrad Heather. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253471

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Modified Delphi survey at three Malaysian universities with 43 educators and 377 students (Table 2, n = 420); the article reports that "For UPM, both the educators and students have prioritised the same topics in both rounds" while UM and TU Pharmacy showed a mismatch.

> "For UM and TU Pharmacy, there was a mismatch in the prioritised topics between the students and educators. For UPM, both the educators and students have prioritised the same topics in both rounds."
