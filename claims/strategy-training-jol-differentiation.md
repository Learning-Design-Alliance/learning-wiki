# Strategy training enabled JOL differentiation between later correct and incorrect answers in all age groups, whereas only adolescents and younger adults differentiated without training

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` A triple interaction of correctness of response, experimental condition, and age group was significant (η² = .06). [→ von der Linden et al. 2015](#von-der-linden-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### von der Linden et al. 2015

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on mean JOLs before correct vs. incorrect responses with correctness as within-subject factor (N = 160). The omnibus triple interaction of correctness, condition, and age group was significant, η2 = .06.

> "Furthermore, the triple interaction between correctness of response, experimental condition, and age group attained a significant level (F(3,151) = 3.43; p < .05; η2 = .06)"

### von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up analyses of the control condition only (n = 77 across four age groups). Children's JOLs did not differentiate (p = .426); adolescents' and younger adults' did, with η2 = .51 and η2 = .78 respectively; older adults were marginal (p = .05).

> "Subsequent analyses revealed that only the adolescents and the younger adults distinguished betw een correct and incorrect responses given that it was only for these two age groups that the factor correctness of response turned out to be significant (children: F(1,19) = 0.66; p = .426; η2 = .03; adolescents: F(1,19) = 19.82; p < .001; η2 = .51; younger adults: F(1,19) = 66.06; p < .001; η2 = .78; older adults: F(1,19) = 4.43; p = .05; η2 = .19)"
