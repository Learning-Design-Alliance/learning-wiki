## Evidence

### Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Gonnermann-Müller, J., Haase, J., Leins, N., Igel, M., Fackeldey, K., & Pokutta, S. (2026). FACET: Multi-Agent AI Supporting Teachers in Scaling Differentiated Learning for Diverse Students. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.24963/ijcai.2026/797](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2026/797)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive survey of 70 K–12 teachers rating five FACET worksheets; the enrichment worksheet for the high-performing, highly motivated profile scored lowest on all dimensions, with means from 3.10 to 3.87 on the 6-point scale. The authors interpret this as "a need for more engaging and varied materials for motivated learners."

> "Worksheet 3 (high-performing, highly motivated) received lower ratings across all dimen- sions (structure:M= 3.87; clarity:M= 3.57; format: M= 3.10; creativity:M= 3.30)."
