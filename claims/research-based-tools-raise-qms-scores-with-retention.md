## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The survey was given twice to the tool-using classes, once at the end of the first semester and again at the end of the second semester; scores did not deteriorate (71.5% then 69.4%), which the authors interpret as good retention of the concepts.
