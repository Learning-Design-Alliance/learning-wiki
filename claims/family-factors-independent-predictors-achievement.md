## Evidence

### Johnels Jakob Åsberg 2025

Johnels Jakob Åsberg, Kuja‐Halkola Ralf, Larsson Henrik, Chang Zheng, Brikell Isabel, Lundström Sebastian. (2025). Does the family situation impact academic achievement differently in students with versus without neurodevelopmental disorders? The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70050

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression (GEE framework, cluster-robust for twin pairs) in the 12,029-twin sample, with four models for the two outcomes and two diagnostic categories. The article reports "independent statistically significant main effects were obtained for all independent variables in all four models"; e.g., ADHD β = −36 for total grade score, conflict β = −6, parental education 3 β = 44 (Table 2).

> "The pattern of results was very similar in all these analyses, such that independent statistically significant main effects were obtained for all independent variables in all four models. That is, ADHD and autism, respectively, parent–child closeness, parent–child conflict, and parental educational attainment all made unique contributions to child academic achievement."
