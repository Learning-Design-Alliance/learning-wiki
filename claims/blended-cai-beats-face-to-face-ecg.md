## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2019

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects subanalysis of three studies (n=422) in which CAI was combined with face-to-face teaching. The pooled "SMD=0.84 (95% CI 0.54 to 1.14)" favoured blended CAI, with lower heterogeneity (I2=50%) than the overall analysis.

> "A subanalysis found a large positive effect size when CAI formed part of a blended learning strategy as compared with face-to-face teaching (SMD=0.84 (95% CI 0.54 to 1.14); three studies, n=422; I2=50%)"
