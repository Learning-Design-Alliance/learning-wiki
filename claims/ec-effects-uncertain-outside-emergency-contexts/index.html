Joly Louise 2026 (2)¶

Joly Louise, Nunes de Sousa Alexandra, Bardiau Marjorie, Dory Valérie, Bayot Marie, Lenoir Anne-Laure. (2026). Impact of emotional competence on clinical reasoning: a scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-113483

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis reporting the empathy–technical performance null across the review's included studies, predominantly student samples; an association appeared only when evaluation criteria incorporated interpersonal interactions with patients or the medical team.

"Empathy, based on evidence predominantly drawn from studies involving student samples, did not appear to be a predictive factor for the quality of clinical examination or diagnostic and therapeutic performance."
