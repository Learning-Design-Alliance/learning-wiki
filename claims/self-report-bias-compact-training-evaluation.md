## Evidence

### Basu Andrea 2014

Basu Andrea, Kennedy Lynne, Tocque Karen, Jones Sharn. (2014). Eating for 1, Healthy and Active for 2; feasibility of delivering novel, compact training for midwives to build knowledge and confidence in giving nutrition, physical activity and weight management advice during pregnancy. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2393-14-218

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' stated limitations of the feasibility evaluation: outcomes were self-reported, the sample was small (32 midwives) requiring response categories to be combined for analysis, and the ability to apply new knowledge in practice is yet to be demonstrated.

> "Interpretation of the self-reported post training questionnaire responses should acknowledge the possibility of a post course ‘feel good’ factor amongst those who participated. Also, as the questionnaires were administered by the trainer, social desirability may have influenced the results."
