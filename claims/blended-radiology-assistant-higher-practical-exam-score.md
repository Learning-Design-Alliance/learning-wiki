## Evidence

### Zhang Tonghua 2026

Zhang Tonghua, Fu Dingwei, Liu Huan, Ruan Yuanzhi, Liu Houjun, Mei Guangbao. (2026). Effectiveness of a Radiology Assistant–based blended learning model in medical imaging internship education: a prospective randomized comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09014-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Practical examination requiring interpretation of five anonymized imaging cases, scored by two faculty members blinded to group allocation, in the randomized study of 60 interns. Blended group scored "35.6 ± 3.8 vs. 32.4 ± 4.2, t = 2.876, P = 0.005"; no effect size was printed.

> "practical exam scores were also higher in the blended group (35.6 ± 3.8 vs. 32.4 ± 4.2, t = 2.876, P = 0.005)"
