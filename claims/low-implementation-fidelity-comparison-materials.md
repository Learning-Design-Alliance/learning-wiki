## Evidence

### Star 2013

Star, J. R., Rittle-Johnson, B., Durkin, K., Newton, K., Pollack, C., Lynch, K., & Gogolen, C. (2013). The Impact of a Comparison Curriculum in Algebra I: A Randomized Experiment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564086

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation fidelity was assessed via online logs after every use and monthly lesson videos. The article states "The lack of significant main effects may have been due to treatment teachers' failure to use our materials as frequently as we had intended."

> "Teachers reported using our materials during an average of 19 class periods (Range 4 – 56), indicating that teachers, on average, were using our materials less than once a week."
