The editorial reports, citing Schreder et al.'s qualitative study comparing video-assisted and verbal debriefings in mountain rescue training, that video "enhances precision and evidentiary power" while posing risks to psychological safety. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Schreder et al. (2025) compare video-assisted and verbal debriefings in mountain rescue training. While video enhances precision and evidentiary power, it also introduces challenges, including potential risks to psychological safety and a tendency to narrow reflection to individual rather than team performance."
