Evidence¶

morse-discussions¶

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Case-study analysis of discussion turns in one highly experienced middle school teacher's enactment (Lowell et al., 2022), as summarized by the review; qualitative, no effect sizes.

"across all three types of discussions (initial ideas, building understandings, and consensus), Mr. Morse focused primarily on surfacing and clarifying ideas, but his evaluation of students' ideas was absent from the discussions."

kelly-improvisation¶

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

q1 · i? · review · r3

Case study of one experienced teacher's enactment episodes (Cherbow, 2022) summarized by the review; the review notes evidence is "currently limited to a handful of detailed analyses of enactment in specific classrooms".

"The analysis revealed that Mr. Kelly's improvisations across the discussions supported students' epistemic agency in enactment."
