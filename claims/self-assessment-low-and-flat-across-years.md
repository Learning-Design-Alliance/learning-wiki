Descriptive analysis of the self-assessment question in the cross-sectional survey of 290 medical students; the study reports 72.78% rated their knowledge insufficient and no statistically significant differences in self-assessed knowledge across years (p > 0.05).

> "a significant majority—72.78% (211)—rated their knowledge as insufficient. 24.13% (70) considered their knowledge sufficient, while 2.41% (7) rated it as good, and only 0.68% (2) as very good."
