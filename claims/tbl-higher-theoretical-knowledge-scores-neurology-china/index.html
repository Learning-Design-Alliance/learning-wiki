Evidence¶

Fu Jie 2026¶

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs with 1237 students (619 TBL, 618 LBL) using a random-effects model due to heterogeneity (I2 = 89%). The pooled estimate favored TBL: "SMD = 1.16, 95% CI: 0.77 to 1.54, p < 0.00001".

"The results indicated that compared to traditional LBL, TBL was related to higher theoretical knowledge scores (SMD = 1.16, 95% CI: 0.77 to 1.54, p < 0.00001) (Fig. 3)."
