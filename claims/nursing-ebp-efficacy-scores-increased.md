## Evidence

### Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos, Patelarou Athina, Laliotis Aggelos, Wan Andrew C, Matalliotakis Michail, Tsiou Chrysoula, Patelarou Evridiki. (2016). Educational strategies for teaching evidence-based practice to undergraduate health students: systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5066070/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre-post evaluation of a multifaceted intervention (lectures, individual mentoring, small group and wrap-up conferences) for first-year nursing students (n=81) in Korea. EBP efficacy mean rose from 2.30 to 3.05, described as moving from 'a little confident' to 'confident'.

> "Before the intervention, the overall and individual subscale scores for EBP efficacy had an overall mean score of 2.30, indicating that the students were ‘a little confident.’ After the intervention, the scores increased to a mean of 3.05, indicating that students felt ‘confident’ regarding the EBP process."
