## Evidence

### Piburn B 2025

Piburn B, Harring A, Collins K, Zapata I, Thomson A, Mason NL. (2025). Physician Assistant Student Simulation Training in Ultrasound-Guided Procedures Using Synthetic Cysts and Formalin-Embalmed Cadavers. The Journal of Physician Assistant Education. https://doi.org/10.1097/JPA.0000000000000681

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey of 37 PA students in a single-center, nonrandomized, noncontrolled interventional study using 5-point Likert self-confidence items before and after a cadaver-based simulation workshop; the article reports "significantly higher confidence levels related to all aspects of the procedure" with no effect size printed.

> "Following training participants reported significantly higher confidence levels related to all aspects of the procedure (P = <0.0001)."
