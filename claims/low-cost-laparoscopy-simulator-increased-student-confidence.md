`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 51 medical students comparing pre- and post-simulation safety ratings with paired tests. The article reports a statistically significant confidence increase (p<0.0001); "76.4% claimed to have acquired some degree of confidence".

> "After the simulation, 39 out of 51 (76.4%) claimed to have acquired some degree of confidence in performing the procedure (p<0.0001) and 6 out of 12 (50.0%) who claimed to be insecure after the practice had a reduction in their level of insecurity."
