Seval Deniz KILIÇ & Hüseyin ALKAN. (2018). The Effects of Activities and Approachments Intended Performance Improvement on the Students' Performances in Mathematics. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n5p146

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The DBS was piloted on 150 students in the 2009-2010 school year; low-reliability questions were excluded, yielding a 23-item test with internal consistency of 0.822, later reduced to 21 items after two probability and statistics questions were discarded.

> "Thus, the test consisting of 23 questions with high reliability was formed. The internal consistency coefficient of the test was found to be 0.822. As a result, the test was considered to be highly reliable"
