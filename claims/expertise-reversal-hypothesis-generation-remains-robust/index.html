Chen 2016¶

Chen, Ouhao. (2016). The Worked Example Effect, the Generation Effect, and Element Interactivity. PhD thesis, School of Education, UNSW. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/55865

q1 · i? · causal · r?

A theoretical prediction stated in the thesis abstract, grounded in the claim that expertise reduces the effective element interactivity of materials. The abstract reports this pattern was supported across the five experiments.

"With an increase of learner expertise, the worked example effect may be eliminated or reversed because expertise reduces element interactivity, but the generation effect should be still robust."
