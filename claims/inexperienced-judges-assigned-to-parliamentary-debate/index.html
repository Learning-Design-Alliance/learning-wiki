This is the author's observational claim from tournament administration practice, not a statistical analysis; he writes that "a correlation exists" between judge experience and parliamentary assignment, with no coefficients printed.

"It appearsthat a correlation existsbetween the amount of debate experience(or perhaps credibilityas a debate judge) a person has and thelikelihood of being assignedto parliamentary debate--the lessexperience (or credibility), themore likely a parliamentarydebate judging assignment is given."
