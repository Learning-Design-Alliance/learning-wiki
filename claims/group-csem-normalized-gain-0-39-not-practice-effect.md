## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the IG treatment, group performance on the CSEM showed a normalized gain of 0.39 over individual performance, and an equivalent gain of 0.37 across treatment samples shows this is not a test-retest effect. [→ Chandralekha Singh 2002](#chandralekha-singh-2002)

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2002

Chandralekha Singh. (2002). Effectiveness of group interaction on conceptual standardized test performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental counterbalanced classroom study: 74 students took the CSEM individually then in pairs (IG), 54 took it group-first (GI). The IG normalized gain was 0.39, and comparing IG individual (55%) with GI group (71.7%) gave an indistinguishable gain of 0.37, ruling out a practice effect.

> "in the IG treatment, the normalized gain of 0 .39 in the group work is clearly not a “test-retest” eﬀect because considering the treatment samples to be equivalent, we can compare the individual performance on IG treatment (55%) with the group performance of GI treatment (71 .7%) which shows a gain of 0 .37"
