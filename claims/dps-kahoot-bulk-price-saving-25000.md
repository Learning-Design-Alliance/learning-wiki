# Bulk negotiation with the Kahoot! provider reduced the per-teacher price from $149.99 to $44.60, saving the district over $25,000 on licenses

### Noakes 2025

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study reporting of one cohort purchasing agreement during the 2024-2025 school year. The report prints the per-teacher prices and the total: "saving the district over $25,000 on Kahoot! licenses alone." No effect size is printed.

> "One example from the 2024-2025 school year was Kahoot!, which costs $149.99 per teacher when schools purchase the tool. When the district went into 2024 negotiations with the provider for a bulk price, they agreed to $44.60 per teacher, saving the district over $25,000 on Kahoot! licenses alone."
