## Evidence

### Qasem & Viswanathappa 2016

Qasem, A. A. A., & Viswanathappa, G. (2016). Blended Learning Approach to Develop the Teachers' TPACK. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(3), 264-276. https://doi.org/10.30935/cedtech/6176

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired pre-post t-tests within each group of 30 teachers showed significant improvement on all four ICT knowledge dimensions in both training conditions; for example the experimental TK mean rose from 9.50 to 35.67 (t=35.222, df=29, p=.000).

> "Table 3 indicates that there are significant differences between mean scores on pretest and posttest at 0.05 level of confidence on ICT Knowledge (TK, TPK, TCK, and TPACK)."
