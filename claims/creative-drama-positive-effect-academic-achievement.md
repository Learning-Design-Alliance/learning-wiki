# Creative drama has a large significant positive effect on academic achievement (overall weighted ES = 1.68 under a random effects model across 40 studies)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 40 studies analyzed with a random effects model, creative drama had a considerable positive effect on academic achievement with an overall weighted effect size of 1.68. [→ Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı 2015](#veli-batd-hacer-batd-2015)

## Evidence

### Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı 2015

Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı. (2015). Effect of Creative Drama on Academic Achievement: A Meta-analytic and Thematic Analysis. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.6.0156

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 40 studies (8 articles, 28 MA theses, 4 PhD theses) published 2000–2014, each with pretest–posttest control data, analyzed with the treatment effect method and a random effects model. The article reports "the overall weighted effect size was 1.68" (REM; 95% CI 1.36–2.00 per Table 1).

> "According to the quantitative findings, following the analysis of academic achievement scores from 40 studies with respect to REM, the overall weighted effect size was 1.68. Thus, it can be stated that creative drama had a considerable effect on academic achievement."
