## Evidence

### Arpornsujaritkun N 2026

Arpornsujaritkun N, Peamal S, Pootracool P, Leela-Udomlipi S, Tirapanich W, Horsirimanont S, Jirasiritham S, Pornwaragorn C, Kittitirapong N, Tepsamrithporn G, Gesprasert G, Sakulchairungrueng B, Choonu N, Wilasrusmee C. (2026). Enhancement of vascular anastomosis training using a novel simulation model with integrated leakage testing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09724-4

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective pre–post interventional study of 39 general surgery residents (PGY-1 to 4) performing end-to-end anastomoses on the simulation model before and after video instruction plus supervised practice; leakage graded under controlled pressure. The study reports "severe leakage (Grade 3) decreased significantly from 69.3% to 28.1% (p = 0.001)".

> "Following the educational intervention, the proportion of procedures demonstrating severe leakage (Grade 3) decreased significantly from 69.3% to 28.1% (p = 0.001)."
