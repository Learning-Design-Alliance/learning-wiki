# System-controlled adaptive instruction does not necessarily yield higher transfer than nonadaptive static approaches

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` System-controlled adaptive dynamic approaches do not necessarily lead to higher transfer performance than nonadaptive static approaches, and may deprive learners of self-regulation development opportunities. [→ Slava Kalyuga 2010](#slava-kalyuga-2010)

## Evidence

### Slava Kalyuga 2010

Slava Kalyuga, Alexander Renkl, Fred Paas. (2010). Facilitating Flexible Problem Solving: A Cognitive Load Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9132-9

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The review attributes this conclusion to Salden, Paas, van der Pal and van Merriënboer's studies of dynamic task selection in training; the reviewed studies' designs are not described here. The authors add that system control lacks "opportunities for the development of self-regulation skills."

> "Salden et al .( 2006a, b) concluded that system-controlled adaptive dynamic approaches do not necessarily lead to higher transfer performance than nonadap tive static approaches."
