## Evidence

### Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto 2025

Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto, Panúncio-Pinto Maria Paula, Bollela Valdes Roberto, de Almeida Troncon Luiz Ernesto. (2025). Faculty development for undergraduate student programmatic assessment: a Brazilian multi-centered longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07337-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional immediate post-workshop survey of 292 participants (81.3% of 359) rating seven workshop aspects on a five-point quality scale, with Fisher exact tests comparing cycles. Ratings were highest for facilitators' role (93.5% very good) and lowest for infrastructure and the virtual learning environment; the article reports "There were no significant differences between the results from both workshops, for any of the aspects evaluated."

> "Figure 1 shows that more than 75% of the 292 respondents attributed the maximum degree (“very good”) to five of the seven aspects. Lower degrees of quality were attributed to the infrastructure of the site and the virtual learning environment used as support."
