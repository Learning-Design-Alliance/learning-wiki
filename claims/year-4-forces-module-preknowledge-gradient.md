### Kaur 2024

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Ranked pre/post-test results for three Year 4 classes (66 students) taught the "May the forces be with you" module; the article describes the histogram as "somewhat anomalous compared with other years" due to high informal pre-knowledge.

> "The histogram is somewhat anomalous compared with other years. There was a high level of pre-knowledge, probably because students at this age often have informally learnt knowledge of electrical forces and gravity. Students with low pre-knowledge showed substantial improvement, but students with high pre-knowledge showed negligible improvement."
