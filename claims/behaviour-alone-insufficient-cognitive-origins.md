This is a conceptual argument in the article's Section 3.1, not an empirical study. The article offers no data for it; it reasons that an incorrect answer may stem from factual misunderstanding, interpretive ambiguity, conceptual conflict, value-related hesitation, or contextual uncertainty, which traditional analytics register as equivalent errors.
