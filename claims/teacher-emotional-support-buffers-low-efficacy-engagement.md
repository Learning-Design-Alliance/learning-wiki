## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 387 5th-graders and 63 math teachers, students with low math self-efficacy generally reported lower math engagement, but this association did not occur in classrooms observed to have higher teacher emotional support. [→ Fitz 2025](#fitz-2025)

## Evidence

### Fitz 2025

Fitz, J. (with Price, H.). (2025). Positive conditions for mathematics learning: An overview of the research. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/967.472

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observational study of 387 5th-grade students and their 63 math teachers, with independent observers rating teachers' emotional support; the buffering pattern for low-efficacy students was found "based on classroom observations."

> "Students who reported low math self-efficacy generally also reported lower math engagement than their more efficacious peers, but these associations did not occur for students in classrooms with higher teacher emotional support (based on classroom observations)."
