### Someko Hidehiro 2026

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of six Mini-Z 2.0 well-being items among 1,152 Japanese junior residents, adjusted for confounders. The article reports "the odds ratios comparing each group to residents without scholarly activity were similar". No standardized effect size was printed.
