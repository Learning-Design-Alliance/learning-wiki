## Evidence

### Suarez 2012

Suarez, E. and Otero, V. (2012). Physics as a Mechanism for Including ELLs in Classroom Discourse. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.3613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.3613)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video-based case analysis of one session of an Engineering Design unit with 21 first-grade SEIP students, focusing on discussion of Diana and Sarita's capsizing boat. The authors report that Ellie, a Chinese student who had typically refused to participate, made multiple evidence-based claims, and that "the tangible and shared aspects of students' observations were crucial in generating ideas and conversations about them."

> "These features of the learning environment allowed Ellie to experience productive disciplinary engagement as she spontaneously and substantially contributed to the conversation."
