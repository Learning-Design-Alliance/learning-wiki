### Ienghong Kamonwon 2022

Ienghong Kamonwon, Cheung Lap Woon, Tiamkao Somsak, Bhudhisawasdi Vajarabhongsa, Apiratwarakul Korakot. (2022). Integrating Point of Care Ultrasound Education into Clinical Practice at the Emergency Department. Tomography. https://doi.org/10.3390/tomography8020085

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective descriptive study at Srinagarind Hospital, Thailand (January–December 2021), reviewing electronic medical records, ultrasound images and video clips; 1024 patients with 3014 clips/images were assessed. The authors conclude the study "showed the measurable benefits of a POCUS course being integrated into real clinical practice."
