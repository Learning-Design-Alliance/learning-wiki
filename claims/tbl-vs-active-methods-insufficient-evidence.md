## Evidence

### Alizadeh Maryam 2024

Alizadeh Maryam, Masoomi Rasoul, Mafinejad Mahboobeh Khabaz, Parmelee Dean, Khalaf Rusul Jasim, Norouzi Ali. (2024). Team-based learning in health professions education: an umbrella review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06147-x

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Umbrella review synthesis: versus case-based learning and simulation-based learning "no significant differences were found in student scores"; one review reported knowledge outcomes favoring TBL over small group learning. Null results establish no equivalence.

> "Based on the reviews analyzed, it appears that there is insufficient evidence to draw a robust conclusion regarding the comparison of TBL with other active learning methods."
