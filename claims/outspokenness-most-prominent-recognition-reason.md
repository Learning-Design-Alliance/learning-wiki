Meagan Sundstrom and Logan Kageorge. (2024). Investigating peer recognition across an introductory physics sequence: Do first impressions last?. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of 46 fall and 29 spring non-blank nomination explanations using a coding scheme from the authors' prior study. The article reports that "in-class outspokenness is the most prominent reason" students recognize strong peers, with about 40% of explanations coded Participation in each semester.

> "Overall, in-class outspokenness is the most prominent reason for which students recognize their strong physics peers, with about 40% of explanations receiving the Participation code in each semester."
