## Evidence

### Szumski Grzegorz 2022

Szumski Grzegorz, Smogorzewska Joanna, Grygiel Paweł. (2022). Academic achievement of students without special educational needs and disabilities in inclusive education–Does the type of inclusion matter? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270124

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary main-analysis comparison of family SES across the three classroom types in the Polish longitudinal sample. The article reports "F(2, 1810) = 4.25, p = 0.014, η2 = 0.005", a significant but very small effect. Initial achievement did not differ significantly among groups.

> "Students learning in traditional classrooms had a higher level of family SES (M = 0.047) than respondents from inclusive classrooms without co-teaching (M = -0.03) or with co-teaching (M = -0.10): F(2, 1810) = 4.25, p = 0.014, η2 = 0.005."
