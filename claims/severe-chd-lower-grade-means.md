## Evidence

### Roustaei Z 2023

Roustaei Z, Heino A, Kiuru-Kuhlefelt S, Morris J K, Glinianaia S V, Garne E, Loane M, Rankin J, Gissler M. (2023). Educational achievement of children with selected major congenital anomalies and associated factors: a Finnish registry-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad149

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted linear regression for 761 children with severe CHD versus reference children; table 3 prints adjusted all-subjects difference −0.11 (−0.16 to −0.05) and foreign languages −0.18 (−0.26 to −0.10). Grade means for all CHDs combined were similar for many school subjects.

> "Children with severe CHD had lower grade means for all subjects except for social science (table 3)."
