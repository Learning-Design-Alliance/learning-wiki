# Between-group post-test knowledge differences favour the simulation-trained group for epistaxis and tracheostomy care but not nasogastric tube care

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For epistaxis management post-test, Group C had the highest mean of 24.3 and Group B the least at 21.3, a statistically significant ANOVA difference (p = 0.004) confirmed by Tukey test for Group B versus Group C (p = 0.003). [→ MK Goutham 2023](#mk-goutham-2023)
`q2 i?` For tracheostomy tube care post-test, Group A's scores were statistically significant (p = 0.002) and post-hoc tests showed the tracheostomy group outperforming the other groups. [→ MK Goutham 2023 (2)](#mk-goutham-2023-2)

## Evidence

### MK Goutham 2023

MK Goutham, Saldanha Marina, Bhat Vadisha S., A Rajeshwary, Vincent Mark Jittu, Ravikumar Aishwarya. (2023). Simulation-based training in ear, nose and throat skills and emergencies. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjorl.2022.01.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA with post-hoc Tukey test comparing post-test epistaxis scores across the three simulation groups (n = 20 each). The Tukey contrast of Group B versus Group C was significant (p = 0.003); Group A versus Group C was not.

> "Group C (Epistaxis group) had the highest mean value of 24.3 and Group B (NG tube group) had the least value of 21.3. This difference is statistically significant (p = 0.004) favouring Group C."

### MK Goutham 2023 (2)

MK Goutham, Saldanha Marina, Bhat Vadisha S., A Rajeshwary, Vincent Mark Jittu, Ravikumar Aishwarya. (2023). Simulation-based training in ear, nose and throat skills and emergencies. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjorl.2022.01.001

`q2 · i?`

One-way ANOVA and post-hoc Tukey comparison of post-test tracheostomy tube care scores across the three groups; Table 2 shows Tukey significance for Group A versus Group B (p = 0.005) and Group A versus Group C (p = 0.006).

> "Group A (Tracheostomy group) had statistically significant scores (p = 0.002). Post-hoc Tukey test comparing the three groups also showed that tracheostomy group outperforms the other groups."

### MK Goutham 2023 (3)

MK Goutham, Saldanha Marina, Bhat Vadisha S., A Rajeshwary, Vincent Mark Jittu, Ravikumar Aishwarya. (2023). Simulation-based training in ear, nose and throat skills and emergencies. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjorl.2022.01.001

`q2 · i?`

One-way ANOVA of post-test nasogastric tube care scores across the three groups (Table 2 prints p = 0.274 with all Tukey contrasts non-significant); the article reports no statistically significant difference.

> "There was no statistical significance between the three groups."
