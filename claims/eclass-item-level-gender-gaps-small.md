### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Research-based assessment of students’ beliefs about experimental physics: When is gender a factor?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Item-level effect sizes were "small ( d ≤ 0.3)" for Nitems = 23 items and moderate (0.3 < d < 0.4) for Nitems = 7 items; the article reports no obvious trend in the content of the seven larger-gap questions.

> "The magnitude of the gender gap was small ( d ≤ 0.3) for the major- ity of items ( Nitems = 23) and moderate for the rest (0.3 < d < 0.4, Nitems = 7)."
