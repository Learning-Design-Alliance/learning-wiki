# The confirmatory factor analysis of GRAS-DK did not replicate the original three-factor model of personal reflection

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a sample of 361 Danish medical students, CFA did not replicate the three-factor model (self-reflection, empathic reflection, reflective communication) proposed by Aukes and colleagues; only the RMSEA index showed good fit. [→ Andersen Nina Bjerre 2014](#andersen-nina-bjerre-2014)

## Evidence

### Andersen Nina Bjerre 2014

Andersen Nina Bjerre, O'Neill Lotte, Gormsen Lise Kirstine, Hvidberg Line, Morcke Anne Mette. (2014). A validation study of the psychometric properties of the Groningen Reflection Ability Scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-214

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis on GRAS-DK responses from 361 medical students tested the proposed three-factor model using CFI, TLI, RMSEA and WRMR. The article reports that "the only index that showed a good fit was the RMSEA" (RMSEA = 0.076; CFI = 0.885, TLI = 0.872, WRMR = 1.330).

> "The CFA did not replicate the three factor model proposed by Aukes and colleagues [21] and the only index that showed a good fit was the RMSEA (Table 2)."
