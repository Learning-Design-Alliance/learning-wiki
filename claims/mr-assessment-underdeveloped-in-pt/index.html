Authors' synthesis-based interpretation from the evaluation section of the narrative review. The review attributes the gap to "the small number of articles" (only four studies) and notes "existing methods do not seem to adequately account for the subtle interplay between tasks and focals".

"This shows that evaluation is probably underexploited in PT, especially given the small number of articles and the lack of qualitative or psychometric evaluation of the methods used."
