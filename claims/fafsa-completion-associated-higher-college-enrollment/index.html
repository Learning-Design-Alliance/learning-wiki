Victoria Jackson 2026¶

Victoria Jackson, Kyla Hurns, and Paulina Fraser. (2026). Understanding the Implementation of Universal FAFSA Completion Policies in Colorado, Illinois, Indiana, and Louisiana. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682438

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report cites prior survey-based research (attributed to NCAN via endnote) reporting that FAFSA completers are "84% more likely to enroll in college," rising to "127% more likely" for the lowest socioeconomic quintile. The report presents this as an association, not a causal estimate, and prints no standardized effect size.

"Overall, students who complete the FAFSA are 84% more likely to enroll in college, while students in the lowest socioeconomic quintile who complete the FAFSA are 127% more likely to do so."
