## Evidence

### Bitar Anas 2024

Bitar Anas, Barakat Fady, Hawat Angie, Alsaid Bayan. (2024). Dietary and smoking habits during the exam period and their effect on the academic achievement among Syrian medical students. BMC Medical Education 24:60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04950-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman's rank correlation among the 608 surveyed students found that "fast food consumption was found to be significantly and negatively correlated with students' grades (rs = -0.117, p = 0.004)". The article describes the association as weak; no standardised effect size beyond the correlation coefficient is printed.

> "fast food consumption was found to be significantly and negatively correlated with students' grades (rs = -0.117, p = 0.004)"

### Bitar Anas 2024 (2)

Bitar Anas, Barakat Fady, Hawat Angie, Alsaid Bayan. (2024). Dietary and smoking habits during the exam period and their effect on the academic achievement among Syrian medical students. BMC Medical Education 24:60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04950-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The multiple linear regression confirmed that "weekly consumption of fast food (B = -0.583, 95% CI -1.132 – -0.034, p = 0.038)" was negatively associated with grades, controlling for covariates. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "weekly consumption of fast food (B = -0.583, 95% CI -1.132 – -0.034, p = 0.038), were all negatively associated with students' grades"
