## Evidence

### Monib Wali Khan 2025

Monib Wali Khan, Qazi Atika, Apong Rosyzie Anna, Santos Jose Hernandez, Mahmud Malissa Maria. (2025). The MIND model a microlearning AI-integrated instructional design for enhanced learning outcomes. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-24910-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Thematic analysis in ATLAS.ti of written reflections from the experimental group; inter-rater reliability was κ = 0.689, p = .01. Themes included media richness, interaction, engagement, comprehension, self-concept, motivation and satisfaction.

> "Thematic analysis of reflections from the experimental group corroborated the quantitative findings, showing improvements in key learning outcomes—knowledge acquisition, comprehension, research skills, and problem-solving"

### Monib Wali Khan 2025 (2)

Monib Wali Khan, Qazi Atika, Apong Rosyzie Anna, Santos Jose Hernandez, Mahmud Malissa Maria. (2025). The MIND model a microlearning AI-integrated instructional design for enhanced learning outcomes. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-24910-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Within the same thematic analysis, a learner shared, "In the online class, I couldn't interact with my peer", indicating a perceived lack of social engagement in online delivery.

> "However, some learners noted limited peer interaction, particularly during online sessions."
