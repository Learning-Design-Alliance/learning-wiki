Survey item on communication skill development among 327 students: 36.7% reported moderate improvement, 29.1% significant improvement, 19.0% slight improvement, and 4.3% no impact. Higher engagement was positively correlated with communication improvement.

> "When asked about the development of communication skills in a medical context, 36.7% of students noted moderate improvement, and 29.1% indicated significant improvement."
