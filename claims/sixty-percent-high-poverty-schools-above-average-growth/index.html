Andy Hegedus 2018¶

Andy Hegedus, Ed.D. (2018). Evaluating the Relationships between Poverty and School Performance. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research-data-galleries/poverty-and-school-performance

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Stated in the study's Conclusions section, summarizing the growth-versus-poverty analyses of the MAP Growth school sample. The article reports "Sixty percent of schools with the highest percentages of students from impoverished communities also have above-average levels of student growth" and that a slightly larger percentage of high-poverty schools create growth beyond the 82nd percentile than schools from wealthy communities.
