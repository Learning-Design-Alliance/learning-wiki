Evidence¶

Yang 2022¶

Yang, Y., Zhu, G., Sun, D., & Chan, C. K. K. (2022). Collaborative analytics-supported reflective Assessment for Scaffolding Pre-service Teachers’ collaborative Inquiry and Knowledge Building. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09372-y

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of PSTs’ responses on metacognitive prompt sheets during two rounds of KBDeX-supported reflective assessment in weeks 9 and 13, in which groups analyzed "the KBDeX visualizations and data" of their own Knowledge Forum discourse and created rise-above notes with action plans.

"The analysis of the PSTs’ reflection using collaborative analytics and prompt questions showed that the design using KBDeX visualization and knowledge building rubrics helped them engage in productive collaborative knowledge building inquiry by involving them in continuous monitoring, analysis, negotiation, synthesis of inquiry, identification of promising routes for inquiry, and actions to guide further collective inquiry."
