## Evidence

### Bertini-Pérez D. 2024

Bertini-Pérez D., Martin-Ibañez L., Gómez Chica P., Dobarrio-Sanz I., Rodriguez-Arrastia M., Roman P., Rueda-Ruzafa L. (2024). Effectiveness of a Haemorrhage-Control Task Simulator for Training Nursing Students: A Quasi-Experimental before-after Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9730765

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group quasi-experimental before-after study of 103 final-year nursing students using the SBEAT knowledge questionnaire. Mean knowledge rose from "70.58 (10.24)" at PRE to 79.51 (8.53) at POST and 76.70 (8.95) at 3M, with Wilcoxon Z statistics reported; no effect size was printed.

> "statistically significant differences were found between the PRE and POST findings (Z = −5.84; p < 0.01), as well as at 3M when compared to the PRE phase (Z = −4.82; p < 0.01). The mean questionnaire score in the PRE phase was 70.58 (10.24), increasing to 79.51 (8.53) in the POST phase and 76.70 (8.95) at 3M"
