Motameni 2018¶

Motameni, R. (2018). The Combined Impact of the Flipped Classroom, Collaborative Learning, on Students' Learning of key Marketing Concepts. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 15(3). https://ro.uow.edu.au/jutlp/vol15/iss3/4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental Static Group Comparison over four semesters: two flipped sections (76 students) versus three lecture-based control sections (91 students) in Principles of Marketing. The article reports "Students in the flipped sections scored significantly higher than those in the unflipped classes".

"Students in the flipped sections scored significantly higher than those in the unflipped classes. Therefore, for the majority of students, their participation in flipped classrooms triggered better performance in exams."
