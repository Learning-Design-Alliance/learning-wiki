## Evidence

### Swanson 2014

Swanson, H. L., Orosco, M. J., & Lussier, C. M. (2014). The Effects of Mathematics Strategy Instruction for Children With Serious Problem-Solving Difficulties. Exceptional Children, 80(2), 149-168. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048482

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the randomized Grade 3 study relating pretest executive working memory to posttest problem-solving accuracy within each condition for children with MD. The article reports correlations (r) of r = .54, r = –.18, r = –.37, r = –.72 and r = .66 across the verbal-only, verbal+visual, visual-only, materials-only and control conditions, interpreting negative correlations as compensatory processing benefiting lower-WMC children.
