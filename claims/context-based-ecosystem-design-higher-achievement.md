## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Elif Ozata Yucel 2015

Elif Ozata Yucel, Muhlis Ozkan. (2015). Development and Implementation of an Instructional Design for Effective Teaching of Ecosystem, Biodiversity, and Environmental Issues. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.4.2579

`q2 · i?`

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group study with four experimental (N = 82) and four control (N = 83) seventh-grade groups. ANCOVA with RT as covariate found "a significant difference between the average scores achieved by the experimental group and the control group students in the post-test AT (F(1-162) = 27.78; p = .00 < .05)".

> "ANCOV A results (Table 7) showed that there was a significant difference between the average scores achieved by the experimental group and the control group students in the post-test AT (F(1-162) = 27.78; p = .00 < .05)."
