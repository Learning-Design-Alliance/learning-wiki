# Iterative coding of 27 AIL sources yields seven dimensions, with technical, application and critical-thinking dimensions most frequent and legal/regulatory knowledge rarest

## Subclaims

Integrative literature review with iterative open and axial coding by two independent coders of AIL conceptualizations in HE (n=27 sources, 2021-2024 plus foundational works). The review reports "Technical Knowledge and Skills (TKS), Application Proficiency (AP), and Critical Thinking Ability (CTA) emerged consistently" as core areas; Table 1 prints scores including EAR 20/27, IS 18/27, SIU 11/27, LRK 2/27.

> "As Table 1 indicates, Technical Knowledge and Skills (TKS), Application Proficiency (AP), and Critical Thinking Ability (CTA) emerged consistently in most of the frameworks analyzed, forming the core competency areas."
