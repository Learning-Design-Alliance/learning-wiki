### N. M. Chase 2024

N. M. Chase. (2024). Survey on the Teacher-Learner Collaboration. MCPHS University. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.02964](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.02964)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bar-graph analysis (Figures 2-5) of pooled survey responses from 650 students over 12 semesters of PHY 270 and PHY 274, showing proportions of responses by Amount Learned. The same group also reported timelier homework starts, more interesting material, clearer explanations, more helpful supplements, and higher overall ratings.

> "Students who reported that they learned the most from the course tended to report: be tter class attendance, completion of more reading and problem assignments, feelings that they deserv ed higher grades and expected to receive higher grades, better mathematics preparation"
