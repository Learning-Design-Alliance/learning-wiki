# Perceived usefulness strongly predicts faculty's behavioral intention and mediates the influence of teaching quality monitoring, with marginal mediation for strategic planning and leadership

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceived usefulness was positively related to behavioral intention (β = .459, p < .001); the indirect effect of teaching quality monitoring via perceived usefulness was significant (.011, CI [.003, .019]), while strategic planning's indirect effect was marginal (.005, CI [−.000, .011]). The influence of organizational factors on the acceptance of online teaching among college faculty during the COVID-19 pandemic: a nationwide study in mainland China. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Indirect-effect analysis (path coefficients with 95% CIs) on the same MSEM survey data. Teaching quality monitoring's indirect effect through perceived usefulness demonstrated mediation; strategic planning's indirect effect (.005, CI [−.000, .011]) was described as marginal.

> "the indirect effect of teaching quality monitoring was .011 with a 95% CI [.003, .019], which does not straddle a zero in-between, thereby demonstrating the existence of a mediation."
