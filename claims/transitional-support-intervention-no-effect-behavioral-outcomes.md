### Kelchner 2017

Kelchner, V. P., Evans, K., Brendell, K., Allen, D., Miller, C., & Cooper-Haber, K. (2017). The Effect of a School-Based Transitional Support Intervention Program on Alternative School Youth's Attitudes and Behaviors. The Professional Counselor, 7(2), 169-184. https://doi.org/10.15241/vpk.7.2.169

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures multivariate analysis of variance on four dependent variables (SAAS-R, percentage of courses passed, discipline referrals, attendance) in the 52-participant two-group pretest-posttest study. The article reports "no significant main effect due to treatment" with Wilks' Lambda = .890, F(4, 47) = 1.451, p = .232; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant main effect due to treatment (time by treatment/control): Wilks' Lambda = .890, F(4, 47) = 1.451, p = .232."
