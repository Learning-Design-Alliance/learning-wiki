`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Receiver operator analysis of individual OSATS elements in the 192-video cohort. "Gentleness (AUC: 0.719, P < 0.001)", "instrument handling (AUC: 0.595, P = 0.043)", "tissue exposure (AUC: 0.625, P = 0.009)", and the "summary score (AUC: 0.665, P < 0.001)" significantly predicted grade B/C DGE.

> "the significantly predictive OSATS elements were gentleness (AUC: 0.719, P < 0.001), instrument handling (AUC: 0.595, P = 0.043), tissue exposure (AUC: 0.625, P = 0.009), and the summary score (AUC: 0.665, P < 0.001)"

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis restricting the OSATS to its significant elements. The "adjusted OSATS score ... revealed a higher predictive value for DGE compared with the complete OSATS score (AUC: 0.713, P < 0.001 vs 0.668, P < 0.001, respectively)".

> "The adjusted OSATS score comprised of significantly predictive OSATS elements from the current study (gentleness, instrument handling, tissue exposure, and summary score), revealed a higher predictive value for DGE compared with the complete OSATS score (AUC: 0.713, P < 0.001 vs 0.668, P < 0.001, respectively)."
