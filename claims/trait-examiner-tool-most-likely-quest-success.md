### Cheng 2017

Cheng, Meng-Tzu; Rosenheck, Louisa D; Lin, Chen-Yen; Klopfer, Eric. (2017). Analyzing gameplay data to inform feedback loops in The Radix Endeavor. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.03.015

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Analysis of back-end gameplay data from high school students playing a genetics quest in The Radix Endeavor, using data mining techniques including the classification tree method. The authors report that "use of the trait examiner tool was most likely to lead to success, though a greater number of trait decoder tool uses could also lead to success."

> "The study identified that in this quest use of the trait examiner tool was most likely to lead to success, though a greater number of trait decoder tool uses could also lead to success, perhaps because in those cases players solving problems about genetic traits at an earlier point."
