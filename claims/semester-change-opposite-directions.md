# Student ratings change over the semester: GTAs gain on positive descriptors while faculty decline, with confident, engaging, respect, and uncertain moving in opposite directions for the two instructor types

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2013 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For confident, engaging, and respect, GTA ratings increased over the semester while faculty ratings decreased; for uncertain, GTA ratings decreased while faculty ratings increased. [→ Kendall 2013](#kendall-2013)
`q3 i?` GTAs specifically decreased in perceived nervousness and increased in perceived understanding over the semester. [→ Kendall 2013 (2)](#kendall-2013-2)
`q3 i?` Faculty specifically were rated more boring and less organized, relatable, relaxed, and strict by the end of the semester. [→ Kendall 2013 (3)](#kendall-2013-3)

## Evidence

### Kendall 2013

Kendall, K. D., & Schussler, E. E. (2013). Evolving Impressions: Undergraduate Perceptions of Graduate Teaching Assistants and Faculty Members over a Semester. CBE—Life Sciences Education 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-07-0110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on matched initial and final surveys found significant changes for both instructor types on confident (GTA Z = −3.547, p = 0.000; faculty Z = −2.657, p = 0.009), engaging, respect, and uncertain, with "GTAs increased in their ratings from the initial to final surveys, whereas faculty members decreased."

> "For the descriptors of confident, engaging, and respect, GTAs increased in their ratings from the initial to final surveys, whereas faculty members decreased in their ratings. For the descriptor of uncertain, GTAs decreased in their ratings, while faculty members increased"

### Kendall 2013 (2)

Kendall, K. D., & Schussler, E. E. (2013). Evolving Impressions: Undergraduate Perceptions of Graduate Teaching Assistants and Faculty Members over a Semester. CBE—Life Sciences Education 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-07-0110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed significant GTA-only changes for nervous (Z = −3.808, p = 0.000) and understanding (Z = −4.278, p = 0.000); "GTAs decreased in perceptions of nervousness and increased in perceptions of understanding from the initial to final surveys."

> "GTAs decreased in perceptions of nervousness and increased in perceptions of understanding from the initial to final surveys"

### Kendall 2013 (3)

Kendall, K. D., & Schussler, E. E. (2013). Evolving Impressions: Undergraduate Perceptions of Graduate Teaching Assistants and Faculty Members over a Semester. CBE—Life Sciences Education 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-07-0110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed significant faculty-only changes for boring (Z = −3.952, p = 0.000), organized (Z = −5.138, p = 0.000), relate (Z = −2.707, p = 0.007), relaxed (Z = −2.598, p = 0.009), and strict (Z = −2.408, p = 0.016); "faculty members were more boring by the end of the semester, and less organized, relatable, relaxed, and strict."

> "The student participants indicated that faculty members were more boring by the end of the semester, and less organized, relatable, relaxed, and strict than GTAs"
