## Evidence

### Namkung 2016

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc equality tests within the structural model compared unstandardized paths from language and incoming calculation skill to the two calculation outcomes in the same sample of 139 at-risk fourth graders. Both tests were significant, indicating the two outcomes rely on partly distinct cognitive resources; no effect sizes are printed.

> "We further tested equality of path coefficients for the significant and non-significant paths from language and incoming calculation skill to whole-number and fraction calculations using unstandardized path coefficients. These tests rejected the null of no difference for both predictors (p = .042 for language, p = .003 for incoming calculation skill)."
