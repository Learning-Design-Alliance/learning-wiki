## Evidence

### John Stewart 2018

John Stewart, Cabot Zabriskie, Seth DeVore, and Gay Stewart. (2018). Multi-Dimensional Item Response Theory and the Force Concept Inventory. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison on the revised-FCI items excluding the three uncategorized 1995 items (5, 18, 30): the authors'-structure model had AIC=75,260 and BIC=75,453 versus AIC=74,812 and BIC=75,277 for model 3 on the more restricted problem set.

> "ANOV A showed that the constrained MIRT model had signiﬁcantly better model ﬁt [ χ2(13) = 474, p < 0.001]. As such, while the model proposed by the FCI authors captured their motivation as the creators of the instrument, model 3 produced a better ﬁt for this student population."
