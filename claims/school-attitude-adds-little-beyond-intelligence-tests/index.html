Evidence¶

Arthur I. Gates 1923¶

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The correlations of achievement in school subjects with intelligence tests and other variables (Continued). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1923-02675-001

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Partial and multiple correlation analysis (Table VII) of teacher-rated school attitude (averaged judgments of six to eleven staff members) alongside Stanford Mental Age and group tests for grades IV-VI. The residual correlation is 0.12; the author reports "the multiple r, achieve- ment with (Mental Age + Group X School Attitude) is 0.611."

"When the elements of Mental Age and Group Tests which are identical with School Attitude are eliminated (rl4 — 23), the residual of school attitudes gives a correlation with Achievement of but 0.12."
