Lei Li; Rong Guo 2015¶

q1 · i? · design · r2

This is the article's stated research framework, not a tested result. The authors distinguish perceived engagement (questionnaire) from actual engagement (course management system activity such as login frequency and video-watching time) and hypothesize that perception turns into behavior. No comparison data are reported in the article.

"RQ1. In an online learning environment, student-centered guest lecturing activity can promote more student engagement than traditional guest lecturing can."
