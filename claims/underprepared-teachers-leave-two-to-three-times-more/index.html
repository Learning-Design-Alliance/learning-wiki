Evidence¶

The Federal Role in Advancing Education Equity and Excellence 2020¶

The Federal Role in Advancing Education Equity and Excellence. (2020). Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED610926

q2 · i? · theoretical · r1

The brief reports, citing research, that unprepared entrants to teaching are far more likely to leave, and that this revolving door is the norm in schools serving students of color and low-income families. No effect size or study design is printed.

"Teachers who enter without preparation are two to three times more likely to leave than those who are prepared. As a result, a revolving door of underprepared educators is the norm in many schools that serve students of color and those from low-income families."
