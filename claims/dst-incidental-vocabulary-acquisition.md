The review reports, citing Nami & Asadnia (2024), that digital storytelling (DST) may promote incidental vocabulary acquisition. This is the review's attribution of a primary study's finding; the review characterizes it with hedging ("may promote").

> "She asserts that DST may promote incidental vocabulary acquisition when learners search for or create multimedia resources to illustrate the practical applications of the learned vocabulary or when the word under focus is used in peer-made stories."
