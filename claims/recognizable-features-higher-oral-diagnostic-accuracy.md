## Evidence

### Farzan Ava 2026

Farzan Ava, Heidarzadeh Abtin, Nemati Shadman, Khoshrang Hossein, Basirat Maryam. (2026). Clinical decision-making skill in diagnosing of oral diseases among medical interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09589-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive item-level analysis (Table 4) of 66 interns' responses across 19 cases, reporting percent correct per condition. The results name the highest and lowest performers, and Table 4 prints GERD at 0.00% and BMS at 6.06% correct.

> "The highest diagnostic performance were the oral manifestations of Candidiasis due to HIV infection, denture stomatitis, aphthous stomatitis, and salivary gland tumor, respectively, while the lowest diagnostic performance were oral manifestations of gastric reflux diseases, burning mouth syndrome, and mouth dryness, respectively (Table 4)."
