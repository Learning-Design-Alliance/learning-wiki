## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Effect of Misconception on Transfer in Problem Solving. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07686](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07686)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Evaluation with 45 students given questions (1)-(4), adding explicit free body diagram questions to the car-on-incline pair. Although 89% and 69% correctly identified the free body diagrams, this did not help students see the similarity of the magnitude questions or challenge the friction misconception (~40% still believed friction had magnitude µsN and ~30% believed it was µkN).

> "Unfortunately, the percentage of correct responses for questions (2) and (4) were 69% and 14%, respectively, not statistically different from the corresponding percentages without the corresponding free body diagram questions."
