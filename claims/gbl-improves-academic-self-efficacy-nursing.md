## Evidence

### Rafi Alireza 2026

Rafi Alireza, Heidari Maryam, Behbahani Seyed Mohammad Salehi, Bostan Hassan, Tamimi Ali, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Naeimi Samaneh. (2026). Effectiveness of game-based learning in mental health nursing education on self-directed learning and academic self-efficacy among nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09923-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the intervention group (n = 20) showed a significant time effect for academic self-efficacy (F(2,38) = 42.51, P < 0.001, partial η² = 0.69), measured with the CASES instrument; the printed within-group effect size was "Cohen's d = 1.56".

> "academic self-efficacy increased significantly from pretest (103.00 ± 19.99) to posttest (115.50 ± 17.23; mean difference = 12.50, P < 0.001), and gains were sustained at the one-month follow-up (114.85 ± 16.89)"
