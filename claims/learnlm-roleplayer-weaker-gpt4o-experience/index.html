LearnLM Team 2024¶

LearnLM Team, Google. (2024). LearnLM: Improving Gemini for Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16429

q3 · i? · design · r2

Stage 2 survey data from participants who role-played learners (Figure 6), covering interest in the topic, willingness to use the tutor in the future, and perceived competence and warmth. The text reports participants "indicatedweaker experiences with GPT-4o" on interest, warmth, and usefulness, while experiences with the other three systems were relatively similar.

"In contrast, participants indicatedweaker experiences with GPT-4o in terms of its effects on their interest, its perceived warmth, and its perceived usefulness."
