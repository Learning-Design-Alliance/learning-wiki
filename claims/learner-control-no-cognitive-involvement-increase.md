## Evidence

### Huib K. Tabbers 2010

Huib K. Tabbers, Bastiaan de Koeijer. (2010). Learner control in animated multimedia instructions. Instructional Science, 38, 441–453. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9119-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 15-item semantic differential scale (Cronbach's alpha .88) administered after the lesson showed no group difference in cognitive involvement; the article reports "did not differ significantly", t(50) = 1.13, p = .26, d = 0.32.

> "Finally, the overall cognitive involvement score was quite high: 5.2 (SD = 0.7) on a scale from 1 to 7, but the two groups did not differ significantly (t(50) = 1.13, p = .26, d = 0.32)."
