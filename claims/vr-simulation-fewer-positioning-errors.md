### Rowe David 2023

Rowe David, Garcia Alejandra, Rossi Benito. (2023). Comparison of virtual reality and physical simulation training in first‐year radiography students in South America. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.639

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OSCE comparison of the two matched cohorts, where positioning errors were defined as any body-part position relative to the detector that the assessor would alter prior to exposure. The printed F ratio of 127.654 with P < 0.000 shows "Frequency of positioning error is significantly higher in the physical simulation cohort"; mean positioning errors were 0.917 (VR) versus 1.279.
