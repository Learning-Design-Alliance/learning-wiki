## Evidence

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group post-test on the satisfaction sub-dimension of the SCLS in the randomized controlled trial (Table 3), n = 99 per group. The discussion notes the control group only received information and did not actively use the simulation. No effect size printed.

> "Student Satisfaction post-test	17.74 ± 3.21	23.88 ± 1.79	t:–16.64; p:0.00"

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025 (2)

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Between-group post-test on the self-confidence sub-dimension of the SCLS in the randomized controlled trial (Table 3), n = 99 per group. Within the intervention group the repeated-measures change was also significant (t:–14.53; p:0.01). No effect size printed.

> "Self-confidence in learning post-test	27.79 ± 5.13	36.25 ± 3.73	t:–13.29; p:0.00"
