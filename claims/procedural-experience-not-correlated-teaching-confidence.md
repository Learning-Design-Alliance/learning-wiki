### Smith Christopher J. MD 2024

Smith Christopher J. MD, Rohlfsen Cory MD, Wardian Jana PhD, MSW, Sahak Khalid MD. (2024). Teaching Procedural Skills: Integrating Educational Theory Into Practice. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11476

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 2) of procedural experience against postworkshop survey responses in the 30-participant sample. Procedures performed "correlated strongly with the number of procedures supervised (r = .736, p < .01.)".

> "The number of procedures performed correlated strongly with the number of procedures supervised (r = .736, p < .01.), moderately with confidence in ability to perform procedures (r = .600, p < .01), and weakly with confidence in teaching procedural skills (r = .368, p = .04)."
