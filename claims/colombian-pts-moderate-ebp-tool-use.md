### Ramírez-Vélez R 2015

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported use results from the cross-sectional survey of Colombian physical therapists. "a monthly reading of 2-5 articles by 53.1%" was reported; 49.6% practised clinical decision-making supported by scientific literature, and 39.7% searched PubMed/MedLine 2-5 times per month while 5.5% did so more than 16 times per month.

> "Reading and reviewing scientific literature as a tool for incorporating EBP meant a monthly reading of 2-5 articles by 53.1% of those surveyed."
