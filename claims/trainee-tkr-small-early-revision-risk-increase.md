## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In fully adjusted FPM (Model 4), the hazard ratio for all-cause revision for trainee-performed versus consultant-performed TKR declines from 1.16 (95% CI [1.09, 1.23]) at 1 year to 1.05 (95% CI [0.99, 1.10]) at 4 years, indicating a time-dependent small early excess risk that attenuates by 4 years. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Flexible parametric survival modelling (Model 4, adjusted for patient, operation, and healthcare-setting factors) of NJR data, displayed as HR plots in Fig 3. Surgeon grade had a significant time-dependent association, so single hazard ratios are described as an oversimplification by the article.

> "In the fully adjusted Model 4, the HR declines from 1.16 (95% CI [1.09, 1.23]) at 1 year to 1.05 (95% CI [0.99, 1.10]) at 4 years."
