## Evidence

### Umoren RA 2025

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Before-after assessments of 70 nurses and midwives in Lagos before and immediately after a 2-day in-person WHO ENC 1 and ENC 2 course; the Results report all immediate posttraining medians "were significantly higher than the median (IQR) pretraining scores for all tests" with all P < .001. No effect size printed.

> "were significantly higher than the median (IQR) pretraining scores for all tests (BVM ventilation, 57% [29%-64%]; ENC 1 knowledge check, 80% [75%-85%]; ENC 2 knowledge check, 76% [72%-84%]; ENC 1 case scenario A, 39% [28%-50%]; and ENC 2 case scenario A, 33% [20%-45%]) (all P < .001)"
