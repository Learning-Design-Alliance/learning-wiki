## Evidence

### Blackburn 2015

Blackburn, J. J., Robinson, J. S., & Kacal, A. (2015). Determining the effects of reflection type and cognitive style on students' content knowledge. Journal of Agricultural Education, 56(3), 195-209. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2015.03195

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 10-item pre-test was administered before the treatment to determine prior biofuels content knowledge. An independent samples t-test compared the more adaptive and more innovative groups; the article reports no statistically significant difference (p = .718) between pre-test means of 5.04 and 4.84.

> "A statistically significant difference (p = .718) did not exist between the more adaptive (M = 5.04) students and the more innovative (M = 4.84) students in terms of biofuels content knowledge prior to the treatment."
