# Family climate and school attachment exert reciprocal positive influences on each other over the middle school years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` More positive family climate predicts increases in school attachment over time, and higher school attachment predicts increases in positive family climate, across four waves. [→ Xia 2016](#xia-2016)

## Evidence

### Xia 2016

Xia, M., Fosco, G. M., & Feinberg, M. E. (2016). Examining Reciprocal Influences Among Family Climate, School Attachment, and Academic Self-Regulation: Implications for School Success. Journal of Family Psychology, 30(4), 442-452. https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000141

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lag SEM over four waves of the longitudinal study of 979 6th graders showed "a consistent and reciprocal pattern of effects" between family climate and school attachment; specific cross-lag paths were significant in two of three intervals for each direction.

> "Our longitudinal findings for family climate and school attachment indicated a consistent and reciprocal pattern of effects over all four time points."
