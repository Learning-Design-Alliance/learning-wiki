### Cele 2026

Cele, W.D., Mbeje, P.N. & Mhlongo, E.M., 2026, 'Factors influencing the competence of student midwives in symphysis-fundal height measurement: A quantitative study', Curationis 49(1), a2855. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v49i1.2855

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey (N = 184) of perceptions of simulation in SFH learning; results summarised as frequencies and percentages with no statistical tests. Students also perceived simulation as beneficial "in reinforcing their understanding of SFH landmarks".

> "Following the SFH measurement simulation, 51.6% (n = 95) agreed and 35.9% (n = 66) strongly agreed that they felt confident in performing SFH measurements, reflecting the simulation’s practical applicability and effectiveness in skill development."
