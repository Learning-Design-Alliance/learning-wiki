## Evidence

### Kudish Philip 2016

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of National Student Clearinghouse enrollment data (captured November 2015) for before- and after-program cohorts of biology majors and minors versus all college graduates. The article notes the database "does not reliably identify specific programs," making this an imperfect measure of STEM persistence.

> "more than 50% of biology majors and minors from both URM and non-URM populations enrolled in subsequent educational programs within 2.5 years of their Swarthmore graduation (Figure 6), significantly different rates than the 40–45% of Swarthmore alumni overall (chi-square tests, before p < 0.0001, after p < 0.001, )."
