## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group mixed ANOVA within the intervention group (n = 96) on change in total knowledge. The hospital-type interaction was significant (F(1,94) = 6.94, p = 0.01, printed in Table 4); county-hospital clinicians started lower and gained more.

> "A significantly higher increase in total knowledge score was seen among the participants working in county hospitals or less specialised (lower level) hospitals compared to participants in provincial or municipal hospitals"

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018 (2)

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group ANOVAs in the intervention group found no significant interaction effects for the listed personal and job variables; all sub-groups increased knowledge significantly. A job-type interaction (p = 0.05) had no significant post hoc pairwise differences.

> "There were no significant differences in the change in knowledge scores for other sub-group variables, including age, seniority of job (job title), length of time working with breastfeeding mothers, years since obtaining professional qualification and previous breastfeeding training"
