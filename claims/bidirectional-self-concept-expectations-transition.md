## Evidence

### Dempsey 2026

Dempsey, C., & McCoy, S. (2026). The role of academic self‐concepts, parent expectations and teacher–student interactions in socio‐economic gaps in educational attainment. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 422–442. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate latent change score model (Model 3) of self-concepts and expectations in the GUI cohort; model fit exceeded CFI but not TLI or RMSEA criteria. Changes in the two constructs were also positively linked (Std. Est. = .098, p < .0001).

> "Parental expectations at T2 were predicted by both initial levels of children's self‐concepts, Std. Est. = .255, SE = .084, p < .001, and changes in children's self‐concepts, Std. Est. = .333, SE = .304, p < .001."
