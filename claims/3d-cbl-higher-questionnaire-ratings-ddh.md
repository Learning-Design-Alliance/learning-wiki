## Evidence

### Feng Shuo 2024

Feng Shuo, Sun Ying-Jin, Zhu Qi-Rui, Shi Si-Feng, Zhang Yong-Shuo, Yuan Feng. (2024). Integration of 3D printing and case-based learning in clinical practice for the treatment of developmental dysplasia of the hip. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05934-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-administered questionnaire distributed to all 120 students after teaching, with a 100% effective recovery rate; Table 3 prints t values (e.g., satisfaction t = 4.270, P < 0.001). The article states the questionnaire's standardisation and validation were not described in detail.
