Anonymous post-activity survey in an introductory Python course at a large Southeastern US university; 69 of 80 enrolled students completed it under an IRB-approved protocol. The paper reports 45.5% agreed the system provided useful suggestions, 47.9% agreed feedback was clear, and "53.6% reported that they would like to continue using the system".
