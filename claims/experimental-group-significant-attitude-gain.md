## Evidence

### Muthulakshmi and Veliappan 2015

P. Muthulakshmi, A. Veliappan. (2015). Effectiveness of an Interactive Multimedia Learning Package in Developing Attitude Towards Mathematics. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, Vol. 11, No. 3, December 2015 - February 2016. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131820

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-post comparison for the 21 multimedia-taught experimental students. Table 6 reports pre-test mean 189.19 (SD 29.378) and post-test mean 210.62 (SD 26.886), t = 7.762, p = .000, S at 5% level.

> "the calculated t-value (7.762) is greater than table value (0.5197) for df (20) at 5% level of significance.  Hence, the null hypothesis is rejected. It shows that, there is no significant difference between the pretest and post-test scores of the experimental group"
