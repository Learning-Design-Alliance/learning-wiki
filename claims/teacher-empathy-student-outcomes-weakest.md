## Evidence

### Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann 2022

Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann. (2022). Is Empathy the Key to Effective Teaching? A Systematic Review of Its Association with Teacher-Student Interactions and Student Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09649-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of student-outcome effects (Table 5: 11 cognitive, 39 psychosocial effects): cognitive outcomes showed no significant positive effects, and psychosocial outcomes showed scattered significant positive and a few negative effects. The review attributes the expectation of weaker links to the distal position of student outcomes in its working model.

> "because student outcomes are more distal to teachers’ empathy than teacher-student interactions are, we expected less pronounced associations"
