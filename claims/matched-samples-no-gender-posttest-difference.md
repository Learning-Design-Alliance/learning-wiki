## Evidence

### Traxler 2015

Traxler, A. L., Cid, X. C., Blue, J., Barthelemy, R. (2015). Enriching gender in PER: A binary past and a complex future. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports Blue and Heller's matched-samples study in an introductory university physics course, plus a comparable replication at a different university where end-of-course differences were explained by differences in knowledge, attitudes, and beliefs at the start of the course. No effect size is printed.

> "They found that when men and women were matched on a series of measures (three high-school variables; three pretests, including the FCI; their year in college; and locus of control), there was no difference in their performance on post-tests"
