## Evidence

### Gilardi N 2025

Gilardi N, Ballabio M, Ravera F, Ferrando L, Stabile M, Bellodi A, Talerico G, Cigolini B, Genova C, Carbone F, Montecucco F, Bracco C, Ballestrero A, Zoppoli G. (2025). Influence of medical educational background on the diagnostic quality of ChatGPT‐4 responses in internal medicine: A pilot study. Eur J Clin Invest. https://doi.org/10.1111/eci.70113

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Pilot experiment with 15 participants from a single institution summarising five NEJM cases before querying ChatGPT-4; three independent expert raters scored diagnostic ranking. "Residents obtained a significantly higher score compared to third‐year students" with OR = 2.33, p = .007.

> "Residents obtained a significantly higher score compared to third‐year students (odds ratio (OR) = 2.33, 95% confidence interval (CI) = 1.27–4.28, p‐value = .007)."
