## Evidence

### Pir Suhail Ahmed Sarhandi 2017

Pir Suhail Ahmed Sarhandi, Ayman Bajnaid & Tariq Elyas. (2017). Impact of Smartphone Based Activities on EFL Students' Engagement. English Language Teaching, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n6p103

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teachers rated displayed negative emotions on a Likert scale after each treatment session (n= 38). Fisher's exact test was "not statistically significant (n= 38), p= 0.099"; the article calls the control-group elevation "just a tendency". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Figure 3 shows that a greater number of students displayed negative emotions in the control group over the course of the two weeks, however, a Fisher's exact test  revealed that the differen ce between the two groups was not statistically significant (n= 38), p= 0.099, which indicates that the elevated number of student's exhibiting negative emotions in the control group was just a tendency."
