Descriptive analysis of the five-item adapted Smither face validity questionnaire completed by 196 (99.0%) participants. "Mean scores ranged from 3.4-4.4"; candidates agreed the test related to the medical student role but not that this was obvious to outsiders.

> "One hundred and ninety-six (99.0 %) applicants completed the acceptability questionnaire. Table 2 shows the average ratings given by candidates to the five questionnaire items. Mean scores ranged from 3.4-4.4. Cronbach's alpha reliability of the acceptability questionnaire was 0.77."
