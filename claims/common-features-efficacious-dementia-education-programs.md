Critical interpretive synthesis of 152 included papers on dementia education, underpinned by Kirkpatrick's return on investment model. The synthesis found "Common features of more efficacious educational programs" spanning relevance, active participation, theory, facilitation, duration, application support, and structured tools.

> "Common features of more efficacious educational programs included the need for educational programs to be relevant to participants' role and experience, involve active face-to-face participation, underpin practice-based learning with theory, be delivered by an experienced facilitator"
