# Exploratory cluster analysis identifies two student profiles differing in neuroticism/self-efficacy, comfort, and learning success

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An exploratory k-means cluster analysis (k = 2) identified two profiles: one with high neuroticism, low self-efficacy, low comfort, and low self-assessed learning success; the other with the reverse pattern (all p < .001). [→ Lenes A 2026](#lenes-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Lenes A 2026

Lenes A, Klasen M, Bohorquez-Mendoza G, Gecht J, Sopka S, Vogt L. (2026). Psychological determinants of successful practical teaching: personality traits, self-efficacy, and subjective perception in a hands-on clinical skills course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09788-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory k-means cluster analysis (k = 2) using three variables: composite neuroticism + self-efficacy, self-assessed learning success, and comfort in group learning settings. The study reports that "All three variables were significant" (all p < .001) in distinguishing the clusters.

> "Cluster 1 was characterized by high neuroticism combined with low self-efficacy, low comfort in group learning settings, and low self-assessed learning success. Cluster 2, in turn, was characterized by low neuroticism combined with high self-efficacy, high comfort in group learning settings, and high self-assessed learning success."
