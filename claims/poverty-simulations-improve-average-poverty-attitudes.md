## Evidence

### McQuistan 2026

McQuistan, M. R., Young, L. B., & Pendleton, C. (2026). Poverty Simulations: Are the Learning Outcomes Consistently and Uniformly Positive? Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70090

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests on survey data from 162 third-year dental students who completed the poverty attitudes measure at baseline (T1) and post-simulation (T2). The article reports "Mean poverty attitude scores increased by 9.4 points (SD = 16.6)" with p < 0.001.

> "Mean poverty attitude scores increased by 9.4 points (SD = 16.6) between T1 and T2 but also declined by 2.3 points (SD = 6.0) between T2 and T3."
