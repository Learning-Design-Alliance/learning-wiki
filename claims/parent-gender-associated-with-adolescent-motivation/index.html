Evidence¶

Benneker 2023¶

Benneker, I. M. B., Lee, N. C. & van Atteveldt, N. (2023). Mindset and perceived parental support of autonomy safeguard adolescents' autonomous motivation during COVID-19 home-based learning. npj Science of Learning 8:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00153-2

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the dyadic regression (N = 76), parent gender showed "β = 0.24, t (69) = 2.40, p = 0.020" (gender coded 1 = male, 2 = female). The interpretation is the authors' own, hedged with tended to; no effect size printed.

"This suggests that the involvement of mothers tended to relate to higher levels of autonomous motivation among adolescents during the home-based learning period than the involvement of fathers."
