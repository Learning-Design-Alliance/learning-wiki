# Students received more peer interventions and referred to the demonstration video more often while practicing intravenous cannulation than bag mask ventilation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students received more interventions from triad peers while practicing IVC than BMV (mean 3.6 vs 1.2; p = 7.30 × 10−15). [→ Chan Enoch 2021](#chan-enoch-2021)
`q3 i?` Significantly more students referred to the video while practicing IVC than BMV (p = 0.001). [→ Chan Enoch 2021 (2)](#chan-enoch-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Chan Enoch 2021

Chan Enoch, Botelho Michael George, Wong Gordon Tin Chun. (2021). A flipped classroom, same-level peer-assisted learning approach to clinical skill teaching for medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 1 behavioral comparison across 104 BMV and 107 IVC practice observations; Table 1 prints mean interventions of 1.2 (BMV) versus 3.6 (IVC) with Mann-Whitney p = 7.30 × 10−15. No effect size is printed.

> "Collectively, students received more interventions from their triad peers while practicing IVC, compared to BMV, and this trend was statistically significant based on Mann-Whitney U test."

### Chan Enoch 2021 (2)

Chan Enoch, Botelho Michael George, Wong Gordon Tin Chun. (2021). A flipped classroom, same-level peer-assisted learning approach to clinical skill teaching for medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 1B video-referral comparison by Pearson Chi-square test, p = 0.001; 15 of 107 referred during IVC versus 2 of 104 during BMV as printed in the table. No effect size is printed.

> "Moreover, there were significantly more students who referred to the video while practicing IVC in comparison to BMV, according to Pearson’s Chi-square test."
