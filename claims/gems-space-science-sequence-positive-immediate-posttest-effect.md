# GEMS® Space Science Sequence produces a statistically significant positive immediate-posttest effect on the Space Science Content test for grades 4–5 students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a cluster randomized controlled trial, grades 4–5 students taught with GEMS® Space Science Sequence scored significantly higher on the Space Science Content test immediately after instruction than comparison students (effect size 0.17, improvement index +7). [→ rct-immediate](#rct-immediate)

## Evidence

### rct-immediate

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Science intervention report: Great Explorations in Math and Science® (GEMS®) Space Science Sequence. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/612

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial in central Florida (2007–08 and 2008–09): 66 intervention and 59 comparison teachers; 1,418 GEMS® students versus 1,176 comparison students. The WWC-confirmed domain average effect size was 0.17 with an improvement index of +7 and p-value 0.00, described as "statistically significant positive effects".

> "Granger et al. (2010) reported, and the WWC confirmed, statistically significant positive effects of GEMS® Space Science Sequence on the Space Science Content test for students in grades 4 and 5."
