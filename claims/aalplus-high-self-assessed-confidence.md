## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Hundertmark Jan, Fahrbach-Veeser Johanna, Eicher Christiane, Tonchev Pencho, Kurczyk Sonia, Szecsenyi Joachim, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). The AaLplus near-peer teaching program in Family Medicine strengthens basic medical skills—A five-year retrospective study. PLoS ONE 15(5): e0233748. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233748

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Five-year survey of all AaLplus participants (n = 1534 questionnaires, 98.6% response rate) using 5-point Likert self-assessments administered directly after the formative OSCE. The study reports "mean 3.97, SD = 0.75" for structured anamnesis and means of 3.82–4.36 for examination skills.

> "After course completion, students felt able to take a patient’s history (mean 3.97, SD = 0.75) and perform physical examinations (means range 3.82–4.36, SDs range 0.74–0.89) as well as venepuncture (mean 4.12, SD = 0.88)."
