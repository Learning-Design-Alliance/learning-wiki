Do-Yong Park and Mira Park 2013¶

Do-Yong Park and Mira Park. (2013). Examining the Features of Earth Science Logical Reasoning and Authentic Scientific Inquiry Demonstrated in a High School Earth Science Curriculum: A Case Study. Journal of Geoscience Education. https://doi.org/10.5408/12-360.1

Cross-tabulation of authentic inquiry features with Earth Science logical reasoning methodologies (Table I). The article also reports 8 of 12 (66.7%) using analog models and 15 of 24 (62.5%) developing theories about mechanisms were associated with the abductive method; exceptions were making multiple observations (inductive) and complex transformation of observation (deductive).
