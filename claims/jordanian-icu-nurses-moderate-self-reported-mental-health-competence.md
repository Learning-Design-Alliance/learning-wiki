## Evidence

### Al‐Rawashdeh 2026

Al‐Rawashdeh, A. B., Shahin, M. A. H., Elgazzar, S. E., Ibrahim, A. M., & Hussien, R. M. (2026). Bridging Critical Care and Mental Health: Jordanian ICU Nurses' Self‐Reported Knowledge, Skills, Attitudes and Confidence in Caring for Patients With Mental Illness. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70948

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross‐sectional descriptive survey of 502 ICU nurses in 20 Jordanian hospitals using an adapted questionnaire. The abstract reports the three domain means; the article notes these are self‐reported perceptions, not objective competence.

> "Nurses reported moderate self‐reported perceived knowledge and beliefs (mean = 3.19 ± 0.70), self‐reported skills (mean = 3.20 ± 0.51) and attitudes (mean = 3.28 ± 0.44)."
