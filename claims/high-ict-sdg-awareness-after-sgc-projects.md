### Blanco 2024

Blanco, J. M., Domínguez, C., Jaime, A., & Usandizaga, I. (2024). Connecting sustainability and computer science curricula through website learning projects embedding different types of student-generated content. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10376-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey item measuring perceived ICT impact on SDGs among 301 students. The study reports a mean of "4.05 (0.71)" with "81.7% of the students report that they are quite important or very important". No effect size is printed for this descriptive finding.

> "the mean (standard deviation) of students' opinion on the impact that ICTs have on SDGs is 4.05 (0.71), reflecting a high awareness of the importance of technologies on SDGs; with 81.7% of the students report that they are quite important or very important"
