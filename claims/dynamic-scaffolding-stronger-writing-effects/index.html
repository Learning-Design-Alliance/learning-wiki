López 2026¶

López, P., Fidalgo, R., Álvarez-Moreno, C., Díaz-Tejedor, R., van den Bergh, H., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2026). Compositional Analysis of Writing Instructional Practices Performed with Digital Tools: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10153-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Three-level meta-regression moderator analysis of scaffolding features across 53 effect sizes; dynamic scaffolding was significant with the unstandardized coefficient "b = 0.601" and p = .041. All other scaffolding indicators were non-significant.

"Dynamic scaffolding—referring to actively interactive scaffolding—showed significant moderation: Fm(1, 19.91) = 4.77, p = .041; b = 0.601, 95% CI [0.027, 1.175])."
