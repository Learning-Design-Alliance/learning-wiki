## Evidence

### Saterbak Ann 2018

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Instructor scores on Statement 1 before and after the intervention (Fig. 3); "Forty percent scored an A, 51% scored a B, and only 9% scored a C". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size printed.

> "During the standard administration, 17% of the students scored an A (high/strongly agree), 49% of the students scored a B (medium/neutral), and 34% scored a C (low/strongly disagree). After the intervention, the scores shifted dramatically. Forty percent scored an A, 51% scored a B, and only 9% scored a C."

### Saterbak Ann 2018 (2)

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared tests of instructor scores for Statements 1–13 across standard and intervention conditions (Table 4); "statistically significant changes were evident in LO1 (Statements 1–3) and LO2 (Statements 6, 8)". No effect size printed.

> "As shown in Table 4 under RQ3: Student Performance, statistically significant changes were evident in LO1 (Statements 1–3) and LO2 (Statements 6, 8). For all five statements, student performance improved."
