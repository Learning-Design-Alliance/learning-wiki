## Evidence

### Kadomoto Noriko 2011

Kadomoto Noriko, Iwasa Hajime, Takahashi Miyako, Dulnuan Marcelyn M, Kai Ichiro. (2011). Ifugao males, learning and teaching for the improvement of maternal and child health status in the Philippines: an evaluation of a program. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-11-280

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA of Group 1 attitude scores (10-item scale, Cronbach α = 0.86) across pre-test, post-test and 3-month follow-up. Means were 3.99, 4.17 and 4.05; the article reports the F = 3.63, p = 0.027 result with no effect size.

> "For overall attitude, the score increased significantly only at T1-T2 (F = 3.63, p = 0.027), and decreased slightly at T3 with no significant difference between T1-T3."
