## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Medical students' correct knowledge and correct practice scores rose significantly from Y1 to Y5 and incorrect practice fell; incorrect knowledge was unchanged. Nursing students showed similar correct-score increases from early to late training. [→ Roye-Green Karen J. 2025](#roye-green-karen-j-2025)

## Evidence

### Roye-Green Karen J. 2025

Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey comparing scores across medical school years (Y1–Y5) and nursing early (Y2/3) vs. late (Y4) training using ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis as appropriate. A "significant increase was noted for correct knowledge (1.0±0.0 to 3.5±1.3, p = 0.024)"; nursing correct knowledge also rose (1.4±1.5 to 3.4±1.6, p = 0.007).

> "For medical students, a significant increase was noted for correct knowledge (1.0±0.0 to 3.5±1.3, p = 0.024) and correct practice (3.0±0.0 to 6.0±1.7, p < 0.001) scores, progressing from years 1 to 5 of training."
