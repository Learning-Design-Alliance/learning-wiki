`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Whole-test scorecard evaluation (Q9–Q11) by one engineering-specialized translator. Qualitative feedback added nuance: Test 1 "it was not that dicult to nd the correct choice", Test 2 was "basic" with ineciently used distractors, enabling elimination by cognate recognition rather than expertise.

> "In the assessment at the test level, for Q9, the evaluator rated all tests as “Very helpful”, or a 4 in the 5-point Likert scale. For Q10, all tests were deemed comprehensive enough to cover key areas of engineering translation."
