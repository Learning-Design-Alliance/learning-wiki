Evidence¶

Abaci 2021¶

Abaci, S., Robertson, J., Linklater, H., & McNeill, F. (2021). Supporting school teachers' rapid engagement with online education. Education Tech Research Dev, 69, 29-34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09839-5

q2 · i? · design · r1

Authors' observation (SF4 section) as experienced teacher educators running the webinar series: demand for TPD about digital learning shifted from low priority to "immediately relevant and highly sought after" during the pandemic. No quantitative measure is reported.

"Previously, it had been difficult to persuade teachers to prioritise their time for TPD about digital learning; during the pandemic it was immediately relevant and highly sought after"
