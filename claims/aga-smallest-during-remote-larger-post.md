Trends and gender disparities in grades and grade penalties among bioscience and health-related major students before, during, and after COVID-19 remote instruction. [doi:10.3390/educsci14101101](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci14101101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of institutional grade data computing average grade anomalies for twelve courses across three periods. The results section reports AGAs had the smallest magnitude during remote classes, and most courses had larger AGA magnitudes post-remote than pre-remote instruction.

> "AGAs had the smallest magnitude during remote classes, which can be seen in Figure 2 or Table 4-A in the appendix, and most courses had larger magnitudes of AGAs post-remote instruction compared to pre-remote instruction."
