### Hall-Quest 1918

Hall-Quest, Alfred L. (1918). Present Tendencies in Educational Psychology. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of college teachers of educational psychology; fifty-three professors completed it and fourteen replied they could not supply the information, so "In all sixty-seven replies were received." The survey covered course names, equipment, sub-topics, other subjects, standard-course conceptions, and seminar problems.

> "The questionnaires were filled out by fifty-three professors. Replies stating inability to supply the information requested or that no special courses in this subjects were offered came from fourteen. In all sixty-seven replies were received."
