# Worked example study combined with concept map completion outperforms worked example study combined with concept map study on near and far transfer in physiotherapy students

## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

2×2×2 mixed ANOVA on near transfer across immediate and delayed post-tests in 61 physiotherapy students randomized to completion or study after worked examples; the article reports a significant main effect with ηp² = .110 for learning condition, and p = .010 in the abstract.

> "Analyses of near transfer performance across both post-tests showed a significant main effect of the learning condition (medium effect size), with concept map completion outperforming the concept map study condition"

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017 (2)

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same 2×2×2 mixed ANOVA on far transfer; the article reports a large effect (ηp² = .190, p < .001) favoring concept map completion over concept map study across both post-tests, with no learning condition × test moment interaction.

> "There was a significant main effect (large effect size) of the learning condition on far transfer performance across both post-tests, with concept map completion outperforming the concept map study condition"
