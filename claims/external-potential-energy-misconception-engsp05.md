## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews in which students worked through the QMCS tunneling question; the authors report this misconception, that U(x) is not the potential energy of the electron itself, in all four ENGsp05 interviewees, not seen in any previous study, and not seen in any of the interview subjects from the other courses.

> "Of the four students interviewed from ENGsp05, all held a robust misconception not seen in any previous study, and not seen in any of the interview subjects from the other courses."

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006 (2)

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the first interview conducted in the study, a student drew an exponentially decaying curve over the potential energy graph and, when pressed, insisted the plotted bump was the external energy that the electron interacts with rather than the electron's own potential energy.

> "When pressed, he said that the “bump” was “the external energy that the electron interacts with” and insisted that it was not the potential energy of the electron itself"

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006 (3)

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews with the remaining ENGsp05 students were very similar, with all of them referring to the graph as either “the external potential energy” or “the potential energy of the medium,” and quickly dismissing that it was the electron's own potential energy.

> "The interviews with other ENGsp05 students were very similar, with all of them referring to the graph as either “the external potential energy” or “the potential energy of the medium,” and quickly dismissing the idea that it was the potential energy of the electron itself."
