# AI-generated revisions employ significantly more appraisal resources than peer-feedback revisions, which exceed first drafts

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 168 essays, overall appraisal resource frequency was highest in AI-generated revisions, intermediate in peer-feedback revisions, and lowest in first drafts (all ps < .001). [→ Zhao H and Ji J 2026](#zhao-h-and-ji-j-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhao H and Ji J 2026

Zhao H and Ji J (2026) Human-written and AI-generated revisions: an appraisal analysis of dialogic positioning in argumentative writing. Front. Educ. 11:1904852. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1904852

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on a corpus of 168 essays (14 participants × 4 tasks × 3 draft types), frequencies normalized per 100 words, found AI revisions (M = 10.811) "employed significantly more appraisal resources than peer-feedback revisions (M = 9.533)"; first drafts averaged 8.151. No effect size printed.

> "Bonferroni-adjusted post-hoc comparisons indicated that AI- generated revisions (M = 10.811) employed significantly more appraisal resources than peer-feedback revisions (M = 9.533), which in turn exceeded first drafts (M = 8.151) (all ps < .001)."
