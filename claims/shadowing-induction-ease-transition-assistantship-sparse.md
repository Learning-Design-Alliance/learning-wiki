## Evidence

### Monrouxe Lynn V 2017

Monrouxe Lynn V, Grundy Lisa, Mann Mala, John Zoe, Panagoulas Eleni, Bullock Alison, Mattick Karen. (2017). How prepared are UK medical graduates for practice? A rapid review of the literature 2009–2014. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013656

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of 11/87 manuscripts reporting shadowing data, mostly self-report and mostly pre-2012 when shadowing was variable in duration and definition. The review reports shadowing "considered an efficacious method", while prolonged shadowing could be ineffectual due to repetitive tasks.

> "Generally, these data suggest that shadowing is considered an efficacious method for developing graduates' preparedness."

### Monrouxe Lynn V 2017 (2)

Monrouxe Lynn V, Grundy Lisa, Mann Mala, John Zoe, Panagoulas Eleni, Bullock Alison, Mattick Karen. (2017). How prepared are UK medical graduates for practice? A rapid review of the literature 2009–2014. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013656

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of induction studies, largely large-scale multi-site self-report data suggesting high efficacy, yet inconsistent across trusts or wards. The review reports a possible correlation between inadequate induction and feelings of unpreparedness and error.

> "when induction is insufficient (either inadequate or absent), graduates can feel unprepared, disorganised, frustrated and anxious."

### Monrouxe Lynn V 2017 (3)

Monrouxe Lynn V, Grundy Lisa, Mann Mala, John Zoe, Panagoulas Eleni, Bullock Alison, Mattick Karen. (2017). How prepared are UK medical graduates for practice? A rapid review of the literature 2009–2014. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013656

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review found a paucity of evidence on assistantship efficacy: only one included paper reported data, describing benefits for anxiety relief and team integration; other authors were merely hopeful about assistantships.

> "Only one paper reported data on assistantships,44 which considered them beneficial in relieving anxieties and providing invaluable opportunities for incorporating students into multidisciplinary teams."
