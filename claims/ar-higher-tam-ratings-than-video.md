## Evidence

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey analysis using independent-samples t-tests on modified TAM responses from 24 participants after both sessions. AR was rated higher on perceived usefulness items: easier to learn (p < .001), understanding techniques (M = 6.68 vs 5.29, p = .0006), and confidence in applying skills (M = 6.04 vs 4.00, p < .001). Only means and p-values are printed, no effect sizes.

> "Participants felt that AR made welding easier to learn (M = 6.54, SD = 0.66) compared to Video (M = 4.87, SD = 1.07), a significant difference (p < .001)."

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026 (2)

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

TAM engagement subscale from the post-session survey of 24 participants: AR (M = 6.62, SD = 0.82) vs Video (M = 4.21, SD = 1.28), p < .001. Ease of use did not differ significantly (AR M = 6.13 vs Video M = 5.653, p = .090).

> "Ratings for engagement were markedly higher for AR (M = 6.62, SD = 0.82) than for Video (M = 4.21, SD = 1.28), a highly significant difference (p < .001)."
