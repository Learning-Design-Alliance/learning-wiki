### Hernández 2024

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive characterization of the four-profile LPA solution. The smallest profile, comprising "n = 52; 7%" of the sample, showed cultural and critical support scores "almost two SDs below the sample means" and teacher support about one SD below the mean.

> "a low general, devoid cultural & critical support (#1) profile (n = 52; 7%) had distinctly low promotion of cultural competence and critical consciousness socialization scores (almost two SDs below the sample means) and low teacher support (about one SD below the sample mean)."

### Hernández 2024 (2)

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive characterization of the highest-support latent profile. This profile, 9% of the sample, combined high teacher support with "the highest cultural and critically specific support" at roughly 1.6 to 1.7 SDs above the sample means.

> "The high general, cultural, & critical support (#4) profile (n = 64; 9%) had high teacher support (about one-third SD above the sample mean) and the highest cultural and critically specific support (about 1.6 to 1.7 SDs above the sample mean)."
