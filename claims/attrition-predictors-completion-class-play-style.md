## Evidence

### Nevin Christa R 2014

Nevin Christa R, Westfall Andrew O, Rodriguez J Martin, Dempsey Donald M, Cherrington Andrea, Roy Brita, Patel Mukesh, Willig James H. (2014). Gamification as a tool for enhancing graduate medical education. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2013-132486

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression of longitudinal attrition among UAB round-1 debut players, who lost 29% (n=27) after round 1 and 28% (n=26) after round 2. "Completing more questions (0.80 per 10% increase; 0.70 to 0.93) was associated with lower likelihood of attrition"; PGY3 class and non-daily play increased odds of loss.

> "In ordinal logistic regression analysis, completing more questions (0.80 per 10% increase; 0.70 to 0.93) was associated with lower likelihood of attrition. PGY3 versus PGY1 class (4.25; 1.44 to 12.55) and non-daily play (4.51; 1.50 to 13.58) were associated with increased odds of player loss."
