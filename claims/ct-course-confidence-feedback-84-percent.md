## Evidence

### Singh Veena Kumari 2025

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous Google Forms feedback with 9 Likert-scale items from all 52 participants. The article reports "a majority of students (84.61%) felt confident that they would be able to apply CT skills in their professional lives"; Table 3 shows 23.1% strongly agree and 61.5% agree.

> "Furthermore, it revealed that a majority of students (84.61%) felt confident that they would be able to apply CT skills in their professional lives based on the methods learned in the module"

### Singh Veena Kumari 2025 (2)

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective self-rating items (Q7 before, Q8 after, 1-6 scale) in the same feedback survey. The article reports the shift from 42% poor to none poor and 73.07% good post-course, both at p < 0.01; these are self-assessments, not objective measures.

> "Participants' self-assessment of CT knowledge before and after the course showed significant changes as before the course, 42% believed their understanding of CT was poor but post-course, none perceived their knowledge as poor (p < 0.01)."
