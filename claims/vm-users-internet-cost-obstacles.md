`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of the 322 students who reported using VM; Table 3 prints internet costs at 46.9% and internet access (technical limitations) at 37.0%, while only 1.8% cited no personal device. The article reports these as descriptive percentages with no test.

> "Most students cited internet access and its financial cost as the main obstacles when using VM. Relatively few surveyed VM users mentioned the lack of a personal device or unfamiliarity with the technology as an impediment for VM use."
