Evidence¶

Kocakaya and Gönen 2014¶

F. Kocakaya, S. Gönen. (2014). Influence of Computer-Assisted Roundhouse Diagrams on High School 9th Grade Students’ Understanding the Subjects of “Force and Motion”. Science Education International, 25(3), 283-311. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1044458

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control-group study in a Diyarbakır high school: experimental group n=30, control group n=17. Independent sample t-test of posttest means (6.4 vs 4.41) gave t=2.531, p=0.015; the authors report "medium effect size ( η2=0.12 d=0,38)".

"At the end of the study, the results of the Force and Motion Subject Achievement Test applied to the experimental and control groups revealed that the experimental group was more successful than the control group."
