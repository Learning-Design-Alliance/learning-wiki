### Matilda Knierim 2024

Matilda Knierim, Sahil Jain, Murat Han Aydoğan, Kenneth Mitra, Kush Desai, Akanksha Saran, and Kim Baraka. (2024). Prosody as a Teaching Signal for Agent Learning: Exploratory Studies and Algorithmic Implications. INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON MULTIMODAL INTERACTION (ICMI '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3678957.3685735

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis (Table 1) between mean pitch and ground-truth cumulative rewards for utterances in the single-demonstrator Atari study; significant correlations were found for Seaquest and Space Invaders. No consistent correlation pattern appeared for energy or loudness.

> ""yes" utterances with higher pitch have higher ground truth returns associated with them and "no" utterances with higher pitch have lower returns associated with them"
