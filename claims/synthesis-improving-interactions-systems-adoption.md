The meta-synthesis's final synthesis identified two main interaction improvements: teachers' personal interactions with curricular change at the micro-level, and teachers' beliefs in the long-term instrumentality of the change process.

> "Therefore, the synthesis pointed out that the improvement of the interactions is needed to enhance the coordination and establish the connection among the systems."
