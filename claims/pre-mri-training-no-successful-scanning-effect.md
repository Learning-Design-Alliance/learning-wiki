## Evidence

### Li Jie BS 2019

Li Jie BS, Li Qiancheng MD, Dai Xiuhong MD, Li Jiong MD, Zhang Xinxian MD. (2019). Does pre-scanning training improve the image quality of children receiving magnetic resonance imaging? A meta-analysis of current studies. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000014323

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis pooling of successful scanning (completion with full cooperation or little motion; sedation counted as failure) across the included trials. The pooled rates were described as "similar" and non-significant; no effect size was printed.

> "We also reported a similar rate of successful MRI scanning in subjects with (84.31%) and without training (68.18%) (P = .63, I2 = 65%)"
