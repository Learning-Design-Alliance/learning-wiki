Zirui Shan 2026¶

Zirui Shan, Vanessa Echeverria, Yuheng Li, Yi-Shan Tsai, and Roberto Martinez-Maldonado. (2026). Data Comics for Education: Evaluating Effectiveness, Benefits, and the Ethics of AI-Assisted Creation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02361

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Within-subjects comparison study (Study Part 3) in which 60 students answered questions about four matched pairs of visualisations, each participant serving as their own control with counterbalanced order. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed DC "indicating a large effect (U= 1705.5,Z= 5.143,p <0.001,r= 0.664)".

"participants achieved a significantly higherCorrectRatewith DC (median = 0.750, IQR = 0.333) than with CV (median = 0.333, IQR = 0.333), indicating a large effect (U= 1705.5,Z= 5.143,p <0.001,r= 0.664)"
