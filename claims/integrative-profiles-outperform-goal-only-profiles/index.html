Evidence¶

Linnenbrink-Garcia 2018¶

Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Wormington, S. V., Snyder, K. E., Riggsbee, J., Perez, T., Ben-Eliyahu, A., & Hill, N. E. (2018). Multiple Pathways to Success: An Examination of Integrative Motivational Profiles Among Upper Elementary and College Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 1026-1048. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000245

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Ancillary LPA comparison in the fifth-grade sample between goal-only and integrative solutions. Variance explained "was higher for the integrative profiles compared to the goals only profiles", notably 70% versus 29% for mastery goals.

"the amount of variance in the profiling variables that was explained by profile membership was higher for the integrative profiles compared to the goals only profiles, this was especially true for mastery goals (70% in the integrative profiles and 29% in the goal profiles, see Table 3 and Table S2)."

Linnenbrink-Garcia 2018 (2)¶

Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Wormington, S. V., Snyder, K. E., Riggsbee, J., Perez, T., Ben-Eliyahu, A., & Hill, N. E. (2018). Multiple Pathways to Success: An Examination of Integrative Motivational Profiles Among Upper Elementary and College Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 1026-1048. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000245

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Outcome prediction comparison of the two profile solutions in Study 1, reporting overall effect sizes only (no standardized effect size such as d). Integrative profiles had larger overall effect sizes for behavioral engagement (integrative = 0.56 vs goals = 0.47) and cognitive engagement (0.80 vs 0.43), while goal-only profiles were larger for mathematics growth (0.43 vs 0.30).

"the overall effect sizes were smaller for the goals profiles compared to the integrative profiles for both behavioral engagement (goals = 0.47; integrative = 0.56) and cognitive engagement (goals = 0.43; integrative = 0.80). For growth in mathematics achievement, the effect sizes were somewhat larger for the goals profiles (goals = 0.43; integrative = 0.30)."
