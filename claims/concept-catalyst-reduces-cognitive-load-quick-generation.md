# A scrutable concept-map interface reduced teachers' perceived cognitive load and helped them generate scaffolds quickly when creating classroom content with generative AI

## Evidence

### Gennie Mansi 2026

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exploratory Wizard-of-Oz study in which 10 middle and high school engineering teachers used a mock-up of Concept Catalyst during depth interviews. Affinity diagramming of interview transcripts yielded the efficiency theme: all teachers agreed it would reduce their cognitive load, with one noting the AI could "generate those questions for you".

> "All teachers agreed Concept Catalyst would reduce their cognitive load because “instead of having to create your own questions the AI could generate those questions for you” (P3)."

### Gennie Mansi 2026 (2)

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Findings from the same WoZ interview study, in the diversity/efficiency results section: four teachers said the interface structure helped them "quickly generate content" that was easier to adjust, which they linked to lowering barriers to innovation and freeing time with students.

> "P7, P8, P9, and P10 also voiced that the interface’s structure helped them quickly generate content that they could more easily adjust, lowering barriers to teacher innovation"
