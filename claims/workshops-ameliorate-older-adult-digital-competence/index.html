Evidence¶

Martínez-Alcalá CI 2018¶

Martínez-Alcalá CI, Rosales-Lagarde A, Alonso-Lavernia MÁ, Ramírez-Salvador JÁ, Jiménez-Rodríguez B, Cepeda-Rebollar RM, López-Noguerola JS, Bautista-Díaz ML and Agis-Juárez RA (2018) Digital Inclusion in Older Adults: A Comparison Between Face-to-Face and Blended Digital Literacy Workshops. Front. ICT 5:21. https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00021

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group pre-post comparisons using Wilcoxon tests in a study of 98 older adults (61 FFG, 37 BLG) showed significant improvement in both groups; the article reports the z statistics but no effect size. Both groups "had low and low-medium competence at the beginning of the study."

"When pre-post comparisons were made within each group with Wilcoxon tests, it was evident that the workshops ameliorated the older adult digital competence in both groups (z = − 6.79, p < 0.0001; z = − 5.30, p < 0.0001, for the FFG and the BLG, respectively)."
