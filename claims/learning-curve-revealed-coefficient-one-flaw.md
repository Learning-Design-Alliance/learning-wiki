# Learning-curve analysis of tutor logs revealed a flaw in the algebra cognitive model's handling of equations of the form ax = b when the coefficient is 1, and the tutor now provides targeted extra practice on that skill

## Evidence

### Ritter 2007

Ritter, S., Anderson, J. R., Koedinger, K. R., & Corbett, A. (2007). Cognitive Tutor: Applied research in mathematics education. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 14(2), 249-255. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03194060

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An analysis of tutor log data during algebra tutor development revealed the cognitive model overpredicted performance on some ax = b equations, traced to the coefficient-1 case; recognition of x as 1 times x was added to the model, and the tutor now tests and provides extra practice on that skill.

> "In the development of our algebra tutor, we discovered that the model was overpredicting student performance in solving some equations of the form ax  b. An analysis of the data revealed that the overprediction was due, in part, to the case in which a  1."
