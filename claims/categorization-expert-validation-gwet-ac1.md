# Expert validation shows high inter-expert reliability (Gwet's AC1 .96 overall) and high expert-cluster agreement (ARI .97, NMI .95) for the general education categorization

## Evidence

### Jianxiao Jiang 2025

Jianxiao Jiang, Yu Zhang. (2025). Student Development Agent: Risk-free Simulation for Evaluating AIED Innovations. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09183

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An expert-informed card-sorting evaluation with two education experts assessed the cluster results, using Gwet's AC1, ARI and NMI. The article reports overall ARI and NMI of .97 and .95, calling the validation strong, though no standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "Inter-expert consistency was high, with an overall Gwet's AC1 of .96; specifically, values were .80 for Learning Environment, .98 for Endowment Dimensions, and .94 for Developmental Dimensions."
