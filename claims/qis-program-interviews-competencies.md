### Sarah Blanchette 2024

Sarah Blanchette, Danièle Normandin, Michel Pioro-Ladrière, Lyne St-Hilaire, Armand Soldera, Karl Thibault and Dave Touchette. (2024). The Design and Implementation of a Quantum Information Science Undergraduate Program. Université de Sherbrooke. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.01874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.01874)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview study conducted in 2020 as part of program development. Industry representatives and students were asked open questions about skills needed in quantum; the article reports that "Required competencies to work in the quantum industry include programming skills (good code structure), project management, teamwork abilities and a solid foundation in quantum related fields." No effect sizes are reported.

> "close to 50 interviews were conducted back in 2020, 30 with representatives from large companies and startups, both local and international, and 20 with members of the student community."
