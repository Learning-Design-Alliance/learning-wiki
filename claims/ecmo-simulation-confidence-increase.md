## Evidence

### Brum Roberta 2015

Brum Roberta, Rajani Ronak, Gelandt Elton, Morgan Lisa, Raguseelan Nira, Butt Salman, Nelmes David, Auzinger Georg, Broughton Simon. (2015). Simulation training for extracorporeal membrane oxygenation. Annals of Cardiac Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.4103/0971-9784.154472

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert five-point scale feedback on the simulation experience from all 14 delegates. The authors report that "13 (93%) agreed that they felt more confident in dealing with ECMO patients following the course"; no statistical test is reported.

> "Prior to the course attendance, 7 (50%) of the delegates did not feel confident in dealing with ECMO patients and 5 (36%) were neutral in their response. At the end of the course 6 (43%) felt “very confident” in managing ECMO patients and 13 (93%) agreed that they felt more confident in dealing with ECMO patients following the course."
