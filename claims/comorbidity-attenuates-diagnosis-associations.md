## Evidence

### Bortes Cristian 2022

Bortes Cristian, Nilsson Karina, Strandh Mattias. (2022). Associations between children’s diagnosed mental disorders and educational achievements in Sweden. Scandinavian Journal of Public Health, 50(8), 1140–1147. https://doi.org/10.1177/14034948221089056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of crude, socioeconomically adjusted, and fully comorbidity-adjusted regression models in the register sample. The article reports attenuation without printing a standardised effect size for the attenuation itself.

> "Furthermore, the associations between all the exposures and outcomes were greatly attenuated in the fully adjusted model, that is, when all the diagnoses were included in the same model (cf. Supplemental material)."
