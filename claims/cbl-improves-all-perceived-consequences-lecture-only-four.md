Chen Lulin, Li Yizhao, Guo Xiaoyan, Liu Wei. (2025). Comparison of case-based learning and traditional lecture in teaching residents on research misconduct: a controlled before-and-after study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0322336

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group before-and-after comparisons (n=149 per group, matched) reported in Table 3. The CBL group's total perceived-consequences score rose from 35.00 (28.00,35.00) to 35.00 (35.00,35.00) with P < 0.001; the control total also rose (P = 0.015) but fewer items improved.

> "All the listed items showed significant score increases in the CBL group (P < 0.01), but "the rational allocation of research resources", "public trust in researchers", "the entire academic environment" and "research integrity throughout the society" only increased in the control group (P < 0.05)."
