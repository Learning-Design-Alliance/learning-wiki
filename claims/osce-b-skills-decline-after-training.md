## Evidence

### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Ratekin Carly Schade, Auko Erick, Wafula Meshack Malenya, Omwanda Wycliffe Okoth, Wamae Jane, Mudhune Sandra A, Rogers Ash, Mbeya Julius, Ouma Frederick Ochieng, Njoki Lillian, Young William, Starnes Joseph R. (2025). Evaluation of the Helping Babies Breathe programme in Migori County, Kenya. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003887

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests of providers' standardised HBB OSCE B simulation scores at 4 and 9 months versus their immediate post-training scores; table 2 prints means 93.21% to 85.63% (p=0.00, n=24) and 92.48% to 87.10% (p=0.04, n=21). No effect size is printed.

> "When comparing scores immediately following training to 4 months following training, and when comparing scores immediately following training to 9 months following training, mean scores decreased significantly, suggesting that follow-up training did not sustain providers' HBB skills."
