`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis of the total sample (N = 106), pre- versus post-intervention (Table 3). Impression manipulativeness fell significantly (rrb = 0.31); a separate result reports higher expressiveness after training with rrb = 0.36.

> "for certain communication styles such as Impression manipulativeness, the mean scores decreased significantly after participation in the training programme, with a medium effect size (rrb = 0.31)"
