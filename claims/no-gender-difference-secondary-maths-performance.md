## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Devine A 2012

Devine A, Fawcett K, Szűcs D, Dowker A. (2012). Gender differences in mathematics anxiety and the relation to mathematics performance while controlling for test anxiety. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-8-33

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANOVAs following a MANOVA on standardized MA, TA and performance scores in 433 pupils (165 girls, 268 boys) from Years 7, 8 and 10 found performance means of "−0.08 vs. 0.04 standard deviations" with p = 0.2, i.e. no significant gender difference.

> "According to univariate ANOVAs mathematics test performance was not different between girls and boys (−0.08 vs. 0.04 standard deviations; p = 0.2)."
