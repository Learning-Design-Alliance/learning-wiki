## Evidence

### Zachariou 2023

Zachariou, A., Bonneville-Roussy, A., Hargreaves, D., & Neokleous, R. (2023). Exploring the effects of a musical play intervention on young children's self-regulation and metacognition. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09342-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Facet-level repeated-measures ANCOVA of interview responses coded for knowledge of people, tasks and strategies; the strategies facet showed "a significant interaction between group and time on metacognitive knowledge of strategies, F (1, 93) = 21.44, p < .001, η2p = .19".

> "Multivariate effects revealed no main effect of time but a significant interaction between group and time on metacognitive knowledge of strategies, F (1, 93) = 21.44, p < .001, η2p = .19"
