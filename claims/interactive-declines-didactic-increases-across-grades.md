# Interactive instruction declines and didactic instruction increases across elementary grade levels

`q2 i?` Teachers used interactive instruction most frequently in lower elementary grades and didactic instruction most frequently in upper grades. [→ Julia B. Smith 2001](#julia-b-smith-2001)

## Evidence

### Julia B. Smith 2001

Julia B. Smith, Valerie E. Lee, and Fred M. Newmann. (2001). Instruction and Achievement in Chicago Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/instruction-and-achievement-chicago-elementary-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Survey-based analysis of instructional approach frequency by classroom grade level across Chicago elementary schools, presented in effect-size units. Interactive use "declined considerably in the upper grades (grades six through eight)" while didactic instruction showed the opposite trend.

> "Teachers used interactive instruction most fre- quently in the lower grades (grades two through four), but its use de- clined considerably in the upper grades (grades six through eight)."
