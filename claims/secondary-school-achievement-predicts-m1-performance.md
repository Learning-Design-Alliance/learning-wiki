## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct effect from the SEM of observed variables estimated by maximum likelihood on 644 MHH students. The direct effect of Abitur grade on M1 grade was "0.224" (SE 0.093, p = 0.017) in Table 2, "an increase of about 0.22 points in the M1 grade" per Abitur point.

> "A one-point deterioration in the final school grade is associated with an increase of about 0.22 points in the M1 grade."

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026 (2)

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM on the MHH sample: the direct effect of Gymnasium attendance on M1 grade was -0.241 (SE 0.066, p < 0.001); "perform significantly better" is the article's wording.

> "Students who obtained their school-leaving certificate at a Gymnasium perform significantly better than those graduating from other school types."
