## Evidence

### Andrews-Dickert Rebecca 2024

Andrews-Dickert Rebecca, Nagaraj Ranjini, Zhan Lilian, Knittig Laura, Zhao Yuan. (2024). Improving learning experience through implementing standardized team-based learning process in undergraduate medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06025-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive survey of 24 of 31 faculty (77.4%) using 16 Likert frequency items (1-never to 5-always) on TBL steps and components. The article reports "a large variation in how often specific steps and components of TBL were implemented"; Table 1 shows student peer evaluation process at mean 2.13 (SD 0.96) and use of appeals process at 2.20 (SD 1.33), the lowest means.

> "When asked how frequently faculty implement aspects of the TBL process as listed in Table 1, the data reveals a large variation in how often specific steps and components of TBL were implemented."
