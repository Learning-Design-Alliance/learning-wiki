Ninth Grade Course Performance Found to Be the Strongest Predictor of High School Graduation for Chicago English Language Learners 2012¶

Ninth Grade Course Performance Found to Be the Strongest Predictor of High School Graduation for Chicago English Language Learners. (2012). University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research / National High School Center. http://www.betterhighschools.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Cohort study of CPS students in ninth grade in 2004-05 followed five years to 2009. The report states on-track ELLs were "up to 3.5 times more likely to graduate than off-track students"; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

"English Language Learners who are on on-track at the end of their freshman year were up to 3.5 times more likely to graduate than off-track students"
