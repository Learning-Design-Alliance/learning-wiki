### RMC Research Corporation 2015

RMC Research Corporation. (2015). Underuse of Title VII funding for Indian education in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah. Phoenix, AZ: West Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559751

Qualitative telephone interviews with representatives of 39 LEAs in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah, analyzed for themes by funding status. The report states the "primary difference" between funded and unfunded eligible LEAs "was in terms of knowledge about the program."

> "The primary difference between eligible LEAs that received Title VII funding versus eligible LEAs that did not receive Title VII funding was in terms of knowledge about the program."
