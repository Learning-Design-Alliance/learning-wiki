## Evidence

### Losiak Wladyslaw; Losiak-Pilch Julia 2020

Losiak Wladyslaw; Losiak-Pilch Julia. (2020). Cortisol Awakening Response, Self-Reported Affect and Exam Performance in Female Students. Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-019-09449-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the same 25-student sample; the single 30-min post-awakening cortisol measure was significantly higher on the exam day, with "eta squared = 0.242".

> "we found significant difference in a single measure of cortisol namely cortisol level measured 30 min after awakening which was higher on the exam day compared to neutral day (F (1,24) = 7.644, p = .01, eta squared = 0.242)"
