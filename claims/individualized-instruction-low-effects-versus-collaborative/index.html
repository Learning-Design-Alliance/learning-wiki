Evidence¶

John Hattie 2025¶

John Hattie, Timothy O'Leary. (2025). Learning Styles, Preferences, or Strategies? An Explanation for the Resurgence of Styles Across Many Meta-analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10002-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review cites MetaX database averages to argue against the assumption that each learner needs tailored individualized teaching: individual instruction d = 0.24 and one-on-one laptops d = 0.16 versus cooperative learning d = 0.55 and collaborative learning d = 0.46. These are printed d values, not standardized effect sizes for a single test.

"Visible Learning MetaX shows the low effects of individualized programs: Individual instruction d = 0.24, one-on-one laptops d = 0.16 compared to cooperative learning d = 0.55, and collaborative learning d = 0.46."
