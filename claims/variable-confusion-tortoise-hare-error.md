## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2012

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2012). How numbers help students solve physics problems. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview analysis of the Tortoise and Hare symbolic problem: five students produced the incorrect result a = v²/(2L). When questioned, two said v was the Tortoise's velocity and three said the Hare's; the two displayed a dynamic interpretation, treating symbols in the final answer as general variables.

> "There were ﬁve students in the sample who made this error. Those ﬁve students were questioned in an attempt to determine if they had a consistent interpretation of the symbol. Two students said that the symbol represented the velocity of the Tortoise, and three said that"
