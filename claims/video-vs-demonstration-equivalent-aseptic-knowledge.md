## Evidence

### Ozsaker Esma 2025

Ozsaker Esma, Koze Burcak Sahin, Donmez Yelda Candan, Dolgun Eda, Ozbayir Turkan, Giersbergen Meryem Yavuz Van, Korkmaz Fatma Demir. (2025). An investigation of the effectiveness of two different methods in teaching surgical aseptic skills: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-02870-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 35 video-assisted and 32 demonstration students in a Turkish nursing faculty; both groups improved on the 12-item surgical asepsis knowledge test, with "no significant difference was found between the post-test mean scores" (p > 0.05). Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "However, no significant difference was found between the post-test mean scores of the two groups (p > 0.05), indicating that both teaching methods effectively enhanced students’ knowledge."
