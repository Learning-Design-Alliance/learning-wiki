# Intervention effects ran in opposite directions by profile: Profile 1 control students outperformed Profile 1 treatment students, while Profile 2 treatment students outperformed Profile 2 control students

## Evidence

### Kulesz 2024

Kulesz, P. A., Roberts, G. J., Francis, D. J., Cirino, P., Walczak, M., & Vaughn, S. (2024). Latent profiles as predictors of response to instruction for students with reading difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(3). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000832

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Examination of mean Gates post-test values for treatment and control within each profile in the conditional mixed model. Both within-profile contrasts were statistically significant (p = 0.041 and p = 0.043) with printed means and SEs; no effect size was reported for these contrasts.

> "For Profile 1 students, students assigned to the control condition (M = 98.03, SE = 2.13) outperformed students assigned to the treatment condition (M = 94.82, SE = 2.20; p = 0.041), while for Profile 2 students, students assigned to the treatment condition (M = 88.37, SE = 1.65) performed better than students assigned to the control condition (M = 86.25, SE = 1.66; p = 0.043)."
