## Evidence

### Fielding Shona 2018

Fielding Shona, Tiffin Paul Alexander, Greatrix Rachel, Lee Amanda J, Patterson Fiona, Nicholson Sandra, Cleland Jennifer. (2018). Do changing medical admissions practices in the UK impact on who is admitted? An interrupted time series analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023274

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational analysis of UKCAT-linked admissions data covering 24,346 admissions to 18 UK medical schools 2007–2014, analysed as annual proportions by target group; the article reports averages of 3.3% lower socioeconomic group, 51.5% non-selective school, 30.5% non-white and 43.8% male, with "no obvious changes" over the 8-year period.

> "when the data from all 18 medical schools were combined, there were no obvious changes in the proportion of admissions from each of the target groups over the 8-year period of study"
