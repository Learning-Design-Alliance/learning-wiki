## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Assessment, evaluation, and research are three distinct activities in leadership program evaluation, and conflating them is a caution the authors flag explicitly. [→ Hastings 2025](#hastings-2025)

## Evidence

### Hastings 2025

Hastings, L. J., and D. Rosch. 2025. "Framing Metrics, Outcomes, and Assessment Overview in Leadership Programs." New Directions for Student Leadership 2025, no. 187: 85–91. https://doi.org/10.1002/yd.70007

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' conceptual clarification distinguishes three activities, warning they are "not the same thing": assessment collects data indicating outcome accomplishment, evaluation values those data to judge effectiveness and drive improvement, and research uses programs to investigate broader concepts.

> "Assessment, evaluation, and research are not the same thing (DeSawal and Peck 2022), so be careful about conflating them."
