# MANOVA shows a significant multivariate class effect favoring the video group on SPS and concept mastery combined

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The multivariate tests (Pillai's Trace, Wilks' Lambda, Hotelling's Trace, Roy's Largest Root) all show a significant difference between the video and PPT groups (Sig. 0.000, F = 9.346 for Class). [→ Wilujeng 2020](#wilujeng-2020)

## Evidence

### Wilujeng 2020

Wilujeng, I., Suryadarma, I. G. P., Ertika, & Dwandaru, W. S. B. (2020). Local Potential Integrated Science Video to Improve SPS and Concept Mastery. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 197-214. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13413a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA in SPSS version 22 on gain scores of both dependent variables, after Box's M test confirmed homogeneous variance-covariance matrices (Sig. 0.463). All four multivariate statistics for Class reached Sig. 0.000 with F = 9.346.

> "Therefore, there are significant differences between the experimental group that was taught with the use of video and the control group that was taught using PPT. In conclusion, the use of video was effective to improve the students SPS and concept mastery."
