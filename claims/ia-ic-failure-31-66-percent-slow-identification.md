Fabian Santiago-Muñoz, Mikel Larrañaga, Jon A. Elorriaga, Ana Arruarte. (2025). Identification of Patterns to Prevent the Failure of Engineering Students. Int J Artif Intell Educ 35:3356–3405. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00502-9

Descriptive analysis of Registration Department records for 2,292 students (Table 1), reported in the Introduction. Failure percentages combine dropout and fail statuses for IA and IC across 2018–2022.

> "Academic failure ranges between 31% and 66%, including both drop out and failure. Although the Mathematics department of the institution implemented strategies to identify and support students with needs, this measure has not been effective enough, since the identification of cases with learning difficulties usually takes between 4 and 6 weeks from the start of the course."
