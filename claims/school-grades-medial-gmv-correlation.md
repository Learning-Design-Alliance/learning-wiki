# Poorer school grade average correlates with smaller grey matter volume in medial brain regions within weekend wake-up and bedtime ROI masks, but not the weekday time-in-bed mask

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` School grade average correlated with GMV within two of three sleep-habit ROI masks in medial regions (bilateral ACC/frontal superior medial; cingulate/paracentral clusters), with no significant correlation in the weekday time-in-bed mask. [→ Urrila 2017](#urrila-2017)

## Evidence

### Urrila 2017

Urrila, A. S. et al. (2017). Sleep habits, academic performance, and the adolescent brain structure. Scientific Reports 7, 41678. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep41678

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Region-of-interest correlation analyses (p < 0.05 FWE corrected) restricted to masks derived from the sleep-GMV results showed "School grade average correlated with GMV within two of the three applied region-of-interest masks in medial brain regions"; the weekday time-in-bed mask yielded no significant correlations.

> "School grade average correlated with GMV within two of the three applied region-of-interest masks in medial brain regions: (i) within the weekend wake-up time region-of-interest mask, in a cluster of the bilateral anterior cingulate cortex extending to the bilateral frontal superior medial cortices, and (ii) within the weekend bedtime region-of-interest mask, in three clusters located in the left paracentral lobule and middle cingulate, the left anterior cingulate, and the right anterior cingulate cortex"
