### Tseng 2026

Tseng, W.-T., Chen, S., & Dong, T. H. (2026). A meta-synthesis of automatic writing evaluation research: trends and developments over a decade. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10601-6

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Meta-analytic synthesis of five meta-analyses reporting pooled effect sizes for AWE on writing performance. The article reports "g = 0.59", "g = 0.55", "g = 0.42", "0.67", and "0.39" across the five meta-analyses.

> "Ngo et al. (2022) examined 24 AWE studies and generated a medium effect size of g = 0.59 (k = 85). In the same line, Fleckenstein et al. (2023) and Xue (2024) reported moderate effect sizes of g = 0.55 and g = 0.42, respectively. However, Huang et al. (2024) found a higher magnitude of 0.67, whereas Chen et al. (2025) identified a smaller effect size of 0.39 (k = 21)."
