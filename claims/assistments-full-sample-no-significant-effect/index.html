Evidence¶

Feng 2025¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental national study comparing 2,855 treatment students with a matched virtual comparison group built from the NWEA national database. End-of-year means were 223.95 versus 223.28, "a score difference of less than one point (0.67)"; equivalence was not tested.

"Across the full sample, treatment students and those in the VCG did not differ significantly on their end-of-year MAP Growth test scores after controlling for students' baseline math performance and student- and school-level covariates (p = 0.172; Figure 1)."
