Qualitative synthesis (RQ2) of the least-studied strategies. Social-network-based assignment uses forum metrics or class membership as indicators of social closeness, and bidding lets students choose which assignments to review; the review reports only one or two systems employ each, so the fairness and timeliness expectations remain empirically untested.

> "While the effect of social-network assignment has not been measured statistically as a separate variable, it can be expected to outperform random assignment in fairness and objectivity [3]."
