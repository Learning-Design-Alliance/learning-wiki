Evidence¶

Bai X and Hsieh T-S 2026¶

Bai X and Hsieh T-S (2026) AI teaching innovation behavior among college teachers: a structural equation modeling analysis based on the AI-TPACK framework, teaching self-efficacy, professional identity, and AI literacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1834827. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1834827

q2 · i? · associational · r2

PLS-SEM path analysis among the 898 teachers tested the direct paths among the three mediators and AI-TIB. The article reports "TSE, PI, and AIL were significantly associated with teachers' AI-TIB" and that H2–H4, H6, H9, and H12 were supported.

"Additionally, TSE, PI, and AIL were significantly associated with teachers' AI-TIB; PI and AIL were also associated with TSE, and AIL was associated with PI. Therefore, H2–H4, H6, H9, and H12 were supported."
