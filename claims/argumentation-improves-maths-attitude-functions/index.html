Emel Mercan 2017¶

Emel Mercan, Tevfik İşleyen. (2017). Analyzing the Efficiency of Argumentation Based Practices on 9th Grade Functions Subject. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(7). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050716

Mann-Whitney U test on MAS posttest scores of 30 experiment and 30 control students (scores not normally distributed), reported as significant with U=310.000, p=.038. Pretest differences were not significant (U=332.500, p>.05).
