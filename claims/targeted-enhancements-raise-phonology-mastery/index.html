Evidence¶

Shangchao Min 2026¶

Shangchao Min, Shaonan Wang, Xinyu Gao, Hui Wang, Zhiling Jin, and 2 more. (2026). Standardized Assessment of LLM English Proficiency. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8820245/v1

q3 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation of enhanced Qwen2.5 variants on CSEBench under the HO-GDINA diagnostic framework. The RAG variant, which augments inputs with knowledge snippets generated by DeepSeek-R1, achieves "near-ceiling mastery across all attributes, including phonology (0.9936)" and reaches CSE-6 in Table 1.

"In particular, Qwen2.5-Instruct-CrossAttn (RAG) achieves near-ceiling mastery across all attributes, including phonology (0.9936), and is competitive with the closed-source models."

Shangchao Min 2026 (2)¶

Shangchao Min, Shaonan Wang, Xinyu Gao, Hui Wang, Zhiling Jin, and 2 more. (2026). Standardized Assessment of LLM English Proficiency. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8820245/v1

q3 · i? · design · r2

Same diagnostic analysis of the multimodal CrossAttn variant trained on the UAIT synthetic dataset. The article reports phonology mastery "drops from 0.9679 (zero-shot) to 0.9132 (few-shot)", attributing this to possible interference from demonstrations.

"Qwen2.5-VL CrossAttn also performs strongly, but its phonology mastery drops from 0.9679 (zero-shot) to 0.9132 (few-shot), suggesting some sensitivity to the few-shot prompt format."
