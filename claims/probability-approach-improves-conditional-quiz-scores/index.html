Evidence¶

Wu 2012¶

Mei Wu. (2012). The Probability Approach to English If-conditional Sentences. English Language Teaching, 5(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n5p37

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled experiment with 65 freshman English majors in Sichuan; experimental class (N=33) received a 30-minute lecture, control class (N=32) did not. The lecture group "increased their scores by 30.36 points" versus 24.81 for controls, a 6-point difference attributed to the approach.

"research subjects who didn't receive the lecture increased their scores by 24.81 points (from a mean score of 59.19 to a mean score of 84), and those who received the lecture increased their scores by 30.36 points"
