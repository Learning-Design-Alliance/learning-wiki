# Students' first graphs from camcorder data had only three experimental points with no errors, improving later to about seven points with 15-20% uncertainties

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students initially prepared graphs with 3 experimental points with no errors, measuring only maximum amplitude and period or wavelength, and later presented graphs with about seven points and uncertainties of about 15% or 20%. [→ Di Renzone S. 2012](#di-renzone-s-2012)

## Evidence

### Di Renzone S. 2012

Di Renzone S., Frati S., Montalbano V. (2012). Disciplinary Knots and Learning Problems in Waves Physics. FFP12, Udine. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1201.3008](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1201.3008)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation from the home task in which students extracted data from videos they captured themselves of the Shive wave machine. The authors report students "measure d only the maximum wave amplitude and the peri od or the wavelengt h" and drew one half period on graph paper.

> "Students prepared two graphics with 3 experimental points with no errors, the points were 0, max, 0, i.e. they measure d only the maximum wave amplitude and the peri od or the wavelengt h, guessed the nodal point must be 0 and make graphic s by drawing one half period , like shown in Fig."

### Di Renzone S. 2012 (2)

Di Renzone S., Frati S., Montalbano V. (2012). Disciplinary Knots and Learning Problems in Waves Physics. FFP12, Udine. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1201.3008](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1201.3008)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Follow-up observation after discussion with the students: the second set of graphs had "about seven points and uncertainties of about 15% or 20%", which the authors contrast with the few-percent uncertainties a camcorder can provide.

> "The next time students presented graphics with about seven points and uncertainties of about 15% or 20%"
