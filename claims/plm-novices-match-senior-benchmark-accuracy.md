### Choi Kai Yip 2026

Choi Kai Yip, Chan Ka Wing, Yau Hiu Ching, Lau Yuet Yan, Leung Tsz Wing. (2026). Perceptual learning enhances diabetic retinopathy detection skills in novice optometry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08691-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on post-training scores across the four groups was significant (F(3,80) = 12.75, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.32). Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc tests showed trained novices "performed significantly better than the control group" and were statistically insignificant from the senior student (SS) benchmark group. At baseline the SS group was approximately 15% higher than the junior groups.

> "Post-hoc tests confirmed that following the intervention, the PL and PLC groups performed significantly better than the control group (both t > 4.74, p < 0.001, Cohen's d > 1.45). Most notably, the post-training accuracy of both the PL and PLC groups was statistically insignificant from the SS group (both p > 0.99)."
