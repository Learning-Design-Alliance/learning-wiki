Same pre-post survey (N = 13) with descriptive statistics. Scenario-specific comfort percentages improved across all three named clinical scenarios.

> "Specifically, residents felt more comfortable delivering bad news related to IUFD (61% vs 84%), cancer diagnosis (38% vs 69%), and previable PPROM (46% vs 77%), all reported as participants responding that they partially agreed or totally agreed."
