Akif ARSLAN. (2016). Effect of Jigsaw I technique on teaching Turkish grammar. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2709

The article reports, citing Kardaş and Cemal (2015), a count of 46 Turkish studies (10 doctoral theses, 10 master theses, 26 articles) of which 43 (93.31%) found collaborative learning techniques increase achievement; this is a second-hand citation not independently verified here.

> "It has been found that there are 46 studies (doctoral thesis f = 10, master thesis f = 10, article f = 26) conducted on the academic achievement of students by using collaborative learning techniques in Turkey. In 43 studies ( 93.31%), it is determined that collaborative learning techniques increase the academic achievement of students"
