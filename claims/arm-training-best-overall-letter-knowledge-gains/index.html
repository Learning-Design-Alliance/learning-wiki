Evidence¶

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro 2024¶

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro, Natacha Boissicat, Fanny Gimbert, Céline Pobel-Burtin, Marie-Caroline Croset, Marie-Line Bosse, and Cécile Nurra. (2024). Involving the Body to Improve Letter Knowledge and Script: An Experimental Study in French Kindergarten. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09923-9

q4 · i2 · causal · r3

Preregistered randomized-controlled experiment in 13 kindergarten classes; linear regression on a latent composite of letter knowledge and script, adjusted for baseline level and letter set (age and gender were tested and dropped from this model as non-significant). Arm training increased acquisitions by "+0.404 SD" versus the average student.

"the arm training condition had a significant, positive effect on overall acquisitions in letter knowledge and script on post-tests (ß = 0.148, d = 0.404, p = .008)"

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro 2024 (2)¶

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro, Natacha Boissicat, Fanny Gimbert, Céline Pobel-Burtin, Marie-Caroline Croset, Marie-Line Bosse, and Cécile Nurra. (2024). Involving the Body to Improve Letter Knowledge and Script: An Experimental Study in French Kindergarten. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09923-9

q4 · i1 · causal · r3

Same RCT, linear regression on the composite measure: arm-body training decreased acquisitions by "-0.383 SD" relative to average students; the likelihood ratio test for the model was significant.

"the arm-body training condition had a significant, negative effect on overall acquisitions (ß = -0.138, d = -0.383, p = .033)"
