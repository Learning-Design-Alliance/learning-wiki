Systematic review coding of assessment methods across the 25 included studies. Interviews predominated (n = 13), followed by reflective journals (n = 6), established scales (n = 6), self-developed scales (n = 4), and (video) observations (n = 2). The review notes this "highlights the qualitative nature of the research on teachers' emotions in PD processes."
