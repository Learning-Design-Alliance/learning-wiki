## Evidence

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Institution-level observational analysis identifying schools whose 3-year average USMLE scores fell outside the 95% prediction interval (residual ≥2 SD). Table 3 lists University of Hawaii–Manoa (+2.9 SD Step 1), University of Missouri (+2.8), Baylor (+2.0), Emory (+2.8 Step 2 CK), and University of Virginia (+2.0), with below-predicted institutions unnamed.

> "Using the GPA and MCAT regression model, we identified a subset of institutions with 3-year average institutional USMLE scores statistically above or below predicted (Table 3)."
