Wood 2026¶

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The paper presents this efficiency trap as a conceptual risk synthesized from cited studies, including Zhai et al.'s (2024) finding that users of GenAI often favor convenience over reflection. No new data are reported; the claim is the authors' synthesis of prior literature.

"However, as AI efficiency increases, human critical thinking may decline when AI performs most of the cognitive work. Learners risk becoming passive recipients rather than active evaluators of information"
