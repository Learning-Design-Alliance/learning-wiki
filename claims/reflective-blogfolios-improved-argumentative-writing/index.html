Evidence¶

Ammar 2016¶

Ammar, Abdullah Mahmoud Ismial. (2016). Reflective Blogfolios in the Language Classroom: Impact on EFL Tertiary Students' Argumentative Writing Skills and Ways of Knowing. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(5). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.5p.247

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-group quasi-experimental pre-post study of 51 EFL tertiary students in the Emirati context. Paired samples t-test on rubric-scored argumentative essays, mean scores 24.8039 pre vs 33.4902 post; "t -value = 16.908" with df 1, 50. No effect size is printed.

"A statistically significant difference between the mean scores obtained by EFL tertiary students on the pre-post assessments of their argument ative writing skills does exist. This statistically significant difference is in favor of the post -assessment scores (t -value = 16.908, P ≥ .05)."
