## Evidence

### Kim Young Mi 2013

Kim Young Mi, Ansari Nasratullah, Kols Adrienne, Tappis Hannah, Currie Sheena, Zainullah Partamin, Bailey Patricia, Semba Richard, Sun Kai, van Roosmalen Jos, Stekelenburg Jelle. (2013). Assessing the capacity for newborn resuscitation and factors associated with providers' knowledge and skills: a cross-sectional study in Afghanistan. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the 39-item simulation checklist; communication and post-procedure tasks were the weakest, with doctors and midwives completing "fewer post-procedure tasks (57.5% and 65.7%) than preparatory tasks (71.3% and 74.6%)".

> "Providers of all kinds performed poorly on interpersonal communication: only 47.1% of doctors and 52.4% of midwives told the mother what was being done and responded to her questions and concerns. Even fewer offered emotional support and reassurance (41.7% and 46.0%)."
