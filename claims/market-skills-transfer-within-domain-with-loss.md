## Evidence

### Banerjee 2025

Banerjee, A. V., Bhattacharjee, S., Chattopadhyay, R., Duflo, E., Ganimian, A. J., Rajah, K. & Spelke, E. S. (2025). Children's arithmetic skills do not transfer between applied and academic mathematics. Nature. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08502-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hypothetical market-problem interview with the Kolkata working children, using items and prices different from their own stall, with paper aids available. The article reports "52% of the children correctly solved the hypothetical transaction", showing transfer beyond rote memorization but with loss relative to real transactions.

> "On average, across both transactions, 52% of the children correctly solved the hypothetical transaction either on the first try or after self-correction"
