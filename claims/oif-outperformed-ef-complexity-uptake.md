## Evidence

### Akbarzadeh 2014

Akbarzadeh, R., Saeidi, M., & Chehreh, M. (2014). The effect of oral interactive feedback on the accuracy and complexity of EFL learners’ writing performance: Uptake and retention. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 105-126. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on revised-composition complexity (content words per T-Unit) with pre-test complexity as covariate; OIF group (M = 7.13) vs EF group (M = 5.99), "F = 28.75, p<.05". The article reports the effect size as .38.

> "There is a significant difference on complexity scores between the OIF group (M = 7.13 and SD = .15) and EF group (M = 5.99 and SD = .15), F = 28.75, p<.05, as Table 7 indicates."
