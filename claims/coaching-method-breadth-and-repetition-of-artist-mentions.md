## Evidence

### Faizrakhmanova 2018

Faizrakhmanova, A., Averianova, T., Aitov, V., Gibatova, G., & Lebedeva, I. (2018). Coaching Method in Teaching History of Visual Arts to Students. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 80-88. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181965

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of students' questionnaires in the same Kazan Federal University quasi-experiment. The text reports 63 artists mentioned at least once in the experimental group, "twice as many as in the control group", with Leonardo da Vinci mentioned 135 times by 100% of students. Table 2 prints 43 vs 26 for this parameter and 75 vs 27 for mentions 2-3 times.

> "In the experimental group, 63 artists were mentioned at least once (twice as many as in the control group). The number of times Leonardo da Vinci was mentioned is 135 (100% students)"
