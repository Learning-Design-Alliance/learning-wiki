# Pedagogically instructed preschoolers perform fewer kinds of actions and discover fewer non-demonstrated functions of a novel toy than children in non-pedagogical conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children in the Pedagogical condition performed fewer different kinds of actions on the toy than children in the Interrupted, Naive, or Baseline conditions. [→ Bonawitz Elizabeth 2011](#bonawitz-elizabeth-2011)
`q3 i?` Children in the Pedagogical condition discovered fewer non-demonstrated target functions during free play than children in the other conditions. [→ Bonawitz Elizabeth 2011 (2)](#bonawitz-elizabeth-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Bonawitz Elizabeth 2011

Bonawitz Elizabeth, Shafto Patrick, Gweon Hyowon, Goodman Noah D., Spelke Elizabeth, Schulz Laura. (2011). The Double-edged Sword of Pedagogy: Instruction limits spontaneous exploration and discovery. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.10.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 1's free-play coding, unique action kinds performed on the four-affordance toy were compared across the four conditions via planned linear contrast; the Pedagogical group performed "fewer different kinds of actions," F(1,81)=9.39, p<0.01. The effect held when only the first 60 seconds of play were analyzed (F(1,81)=7.18, p<0.05).

> "Children in the Pedagogical condition also performed fewer different kinds of actions on the toy (M=4.00) than children in the Interrupted (M=5.30), Naïve (M=5.90) or Baseline (M=6.15) conditions, F(1,81)=9.39, p<0.01)."

### Bonawitz Elizabeth 2011 (2)

Bonawitz Elizabeth, Shafto Patrick, Gweon Hyowon, Goodman Noah D., Spelke Elizabeth, Schulz Laura. (2011). The Double-edged Sword of Pedagogy: Instruction limits spontaneous exploration and discovery. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.10.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 scored discovery of the three non-demonstrated functions (light, music, mirror) on a 0–3 scale during free play. The Pedagogical condition "discovered fewer of these target functions" than all three non-pedagogical conditions, F(1,81)=4.58, p<0.05.

> "Children in the Pedagogical condition discovered fewer of these target functions (M=0.72) than children in the Interrupted (M=1.3), Naïve (M=1.2), or Baseline (M=1.15) conditions, F(1,81)=4.58, p<0.05."
