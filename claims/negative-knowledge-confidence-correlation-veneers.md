`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 8) in the cross-sectional survey of 134 interns; r = -0.201, p = 0.020, a small-to-medium negative association (|r| = .201) indicating the two constructs are related but distinct. The correlation is observational, not causal.

> "Pearson correlation analysis revealed a statistically significant weak negative correlation between objective knowledge scores and self-perceived clinical confidence scores (r = -0.201, p = 0.020). Higher theoretical knowledge scores were associated with slightly lower self-perceived clinical confidence."
