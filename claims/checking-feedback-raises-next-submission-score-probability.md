### Adam Zhang 2025

Adam Zhang, Heather Burte, Jaromir Savelka, Christopher Bogart, and Majd Sakr. (2025). Auto-grader Feedback Utilization and Its Impacts: An Observational Study Across Five Community Colleges. [doi:10.5220/0013276800003932](https://doi.org/10.5220/0013276800003932)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of consecutive submissions by the same student where the first did not get full score, comparing checked versus not-checked feedback. The article reports P(Higher|Check) = 38.46% and P(Higher|NotCheck) = 33.77%, a 4.69 percentage-point difference; no effect size is printed.

> "Students who check the auto-grader feedback af- ter a non-maximal, non-terminal submission are more likely to score higher in the subsequent submission (p = 0.0063 following a Fisher’s Exact Test)."
