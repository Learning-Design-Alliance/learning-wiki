# Direct conditions descriptively show higher post-task full comprehension than Socratic conditions, with Direct + Full Context highest and Socratic + Full Context lowest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 128 non-blank post-task explanations, 57% showed full comprehension; by condition, Direct + Full Context had the highest proportion of full-comprehension responses (67%) and Socratic + Full Context the lowest (48%). [→ Madeleine Eastwood 2026](#madeleine-eastwood-2026)

## Evidence

### Madeleine Eastwood 2026

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inductive coding of open-ended post-task explanations of the matrix debugging bug, administered immediately after task completion in the randomized experiment. Overall, 57% showed full comprehension, 30% partial/impure, 11% minimal, and 2% ungaugable; no statistical test of condition differences is reported.

> "the Direct + Full Context group had the highest proportion of full-comprehension responses (67%), followed by Direct + No Context (61%), Socratic + No Context (50%), and Socratic + Full Context (48%)."
