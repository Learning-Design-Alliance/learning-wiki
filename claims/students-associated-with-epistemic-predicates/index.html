Poudyal 2026¶

Poudyal, B. (2026). Who Acts, Who Knows, Who Answers? A Corpus-Assisted Discourse Analysis of Agency, Epistemic Responsibility, and Accountability in Generative AI Higher Education Research. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7210-5480

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Strict actor–predicate association analysis of the 366-document corpus, comparing AI and student actors across the three epistemic predicate dictionaries. The corpus shows "Knowledge, judgment, and verification together made up only 29 AI association references" versus 84 for students. Descriptive counts; no effect size printed.

"Knowledge, judgment, and verification together made up only 29 AI association references. Students, by contrast, received predicates for the same three epistemic domains on 84 occasions, positioning them as able to understand, judge, interpret, check, and verify their own work"
