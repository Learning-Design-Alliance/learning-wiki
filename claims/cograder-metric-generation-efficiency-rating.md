User study with 12 participants (four professors or lecturers, eight senior PhD students with teaching assistant experience) who graded five sample reports in CoGrader and completed a 7-point Likert questionnaire. The article reports "mean = 6.5, stdev = 0.522" for metric generation efficiency; no effect size is printed.
