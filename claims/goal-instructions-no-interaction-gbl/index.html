Evidence¶

Hu 2024¶

Hu, Y., Wouters, P., van der Schaaf, M., & Kester, L. (2024). The effects of achievement goal instructions in game-based learning on students' achievement goals, performance, and achievement emotions. Learning and Instruction, 93, 101943. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101943

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the randomized 2×2 experiment (N = 450), robust regression found no significant mastery × performance instruction interaction on any outcome; the authors state the specialized, additive, and interactive mechanisms of the multiple goal perspective were not supported.

"There were no statistically significant interaction effects on all learning processes and outcomes."
