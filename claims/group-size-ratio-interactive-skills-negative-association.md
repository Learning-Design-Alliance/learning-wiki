## Evidence

### Støve 2026

Støve, L. L., Dalgaard, N. T., Mohr, J. W., Hansen, J. E., Wendelboe, K. I., Hestbaek, E., Vieira, C. C., Væver, M. S., & Smith-Nielsen, J. (2026). Individual and Structural Factors Associated with Interactive Skills of Child-Care Professionals in Early Childhood Education and Care: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10162-3

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-analysis (ratio: k = 19, N = 3146, r = −.125, p = .032; group size: k = 16, N = 2308, r = −.195, p = .007). The authors argue larger groups "create additional challenges beyond those explained by ratios alone."

> "Higher child-caregiver ratios (more children per caregiver) ( r = − .13) and larger group sizes ( r = − .20) were significantly associated with lower observed levels of interactive skills."
