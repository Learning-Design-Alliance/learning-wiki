## Evidence

### Jackson L 2018

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Website activity tracking of registered learners. The article prints the keener counts and percentages above; the authors say they are unable to characterize differences between keeners and single-module learners due to minimal personal data collection.

> "The number of learners completing more than one module (keeners) was 387/502 (77.1%) with 43 (8.57%) doing four or more modules and two learners completing all nine."

### Jackson L 2018 (2)

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Website tracking data on module revisit behavior, supporting just-in-time use of the resources at the point of patient contact. Descriptive averages only; no effect size.

> "Despite having already passed the postmodule quiz, learners revisited the modules at later dates. The average number of visits to each module per learner was between 2.84 and 3.82"
