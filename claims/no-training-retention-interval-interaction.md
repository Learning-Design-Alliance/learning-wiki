## Evidence

### Rachatasumrit 2023

Rachatasumrit, N., Koedinger, K. R., & Carvalho, P. F. (2023). Evidence and theory for why the best example-problem ratio to optimize learning gain depends on knowledge content. https://osf.io/29wpe [doi:10.31234/osf.io/eyxfw](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/eyxfw)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 1's mixed-effects model, retention interval (uncontrolled trivia-task duration) did not moderate the training-by-KC interaction (d = 0.15, p = .410). Equivalence was not tested; the article notes prior research suggested retention interval could moderate practice benefits.

> "we found no interaction between type of training and retention interval, β = 0.01, t (124.52) = 0.834, p = .410, d = 0.15, and participants’ accuracy responding to fact vs. skill test questions did not vary with retention interval duration"
