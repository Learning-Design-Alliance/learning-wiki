## Evidence

### Faure Theresa 2024

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of the five-item STAI-SKD across nine measuring points found a significant group effect on anxiety, with IVFF below CG (t(168) = 4.47); no standardized effect size was reported.

> "As with stress, course-related anxiety was significantly affected by the study group (F(2, 165.57) = 10.22, p < 0.01, ICC(Model) = 0.621). Again, students in the IVFF had a lower course-related anxiety than those in the control group (t(168) = 4.47, p < 0.01)."

### Faure Theresa 2024 (2)

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The time-by-group interaction was significant and post hoc tests showed the anxiety advantage of IVFF over CG persisted from t2 to t8; the stress-management group differed from CG only at t4.

> "Moderation analysis indicated that study group differed between timepoints (F(16, 1115) = 10.22, p < 0.01). Anxiety was significantly lower in the IVFF than in the CG at t2 - t8 (ps < 0.01). There was also a significant difference between the CG and the IVSM at t4 (p < 0.05; Fig. 2; see also Additional file 2)."
