## Evidence

### Berat Ahi 2017

Berat Ahi. (2017). The Effect of Talking Drawings on Five-Year-Old Turkish Children’s Mental Models of the Water Cycle. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 349-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141375

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Frequency analysis of 13 codes in post-test drawings. The article reports that underground water was only depicted by children from the experimental group, which the author offers as possible proof of the efficacy of the talking drawings technique.

> "One of the more remarkable findings in the post -test is that the experimental group students included two of the important elements of the water cycle – evaporation (f = 6, 27.2%) and condensation ( f = 3, 6.8%) – in their drawings."
