## Evidence

### Prinz 2026

Prinz, M., Schäfer, E., Bürklein, S. & Donnermeyer, D. (2026) Endodontic diagnostics training in undergraduate dental education: An observational pilot study on AI‐driven virtual patient e‐learning. International Endodontic Journal, 59, 1145–1159. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14277

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' stated limitations in the Discussion: outcomes were self-perceived only, the sample was small and voluntary (possibly biased toward motivated students), and the drop-out rate from first to second questionnaire was 21.7%. The analysis was performed descriptively due to limited power. No evidence was offered beyond this acknowledgement.

> "However, this study focused solely on the students' perception of their own capabilities. Hence, a limitation could be a mismatch with an actual and objectively measured acquisition of skills."
