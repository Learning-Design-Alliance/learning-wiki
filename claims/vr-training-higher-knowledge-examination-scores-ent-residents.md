## Evidence

### Zhang Yunyun 2026

Zhang Yunyun, Ye Jingying, Qin He. (2026). Application of VR technology in standardized residency training for endoscopic procedures in otorhinolaryngology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09576-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT of 46 residents (VR n=24, MS n=22), the VR group outperformed the MS group on the written examination, with "significantly higher total scores [(74.45 ± 2.55) points]". No effect size was reported.

> "The VR group achieved significantly higher total scores [(74.45 ± 2.55) points] compared with the MS group [(67.38 ± 5.95) points], with the difference reaching statistical significance (P < 0.001)."
