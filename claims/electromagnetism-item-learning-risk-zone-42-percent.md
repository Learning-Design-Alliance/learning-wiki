Results for item 11 of the first electromagnetism-course assessment, summarized in a category-frequency bar chart (FIG 4). The article reports these percentages descriptively; no statistical tests are printed.

> "26% of the class chose the correct answer, and approximately 6% of the class gave the ideal response. There were 10 omissions (32% of the class) and it can be seen that all of the wrong answers fall in the learning risk zone, representing 42% of the class."
