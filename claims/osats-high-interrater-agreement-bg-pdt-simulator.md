## Evidence

### Kattan Eduardo MD 2019

Kattan Eduardo MD, MMEd; Vera Magdalena MD; Putz Francisca RN; Corvetto Marcia MD; De la Fuente Rene MD, MScEng; Bravo Sebastian MD. (2019). Design and Evaluation of a Low-Cost Bronchoscopy-Guided Percutaneous Dilatational Tracheostomy Simulator. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000399

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blind video assessment component of the multimodal evaluation: two ICU physicians with prior GRS experience in CVC training scored each video-recorded session with the OSATS scale; interrater agreement was assessed with Spearman r2.

> "Spearman r2 was of 0.91, meaning that both evaluators showed a high interrater agreement."
