## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean DNT fell from 22.4 to 17.4 min for inexperienced registrars and from 18.5 to 13.5 min for experienced registrars after simulation attendance. [→ Ajmi Soffien 2024](#ajmi-soffien-2024)

## Evidence

### Ajmi Soffien 2024

Ajmi Soffien, Kurz Martin, Lindner Thomas Werner, Dalen Ingvild, Ersdal Hege Langli. (2024). Does clinical experience influence the effects of team simulation training in stroke thrombolysis? A prospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-086413

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dichotomised unadjusted mixed model analysis of geometric mean DNT by simulation attendance and experience (experienced = 15 or more prior IVT treatments). Inexperienced registrars went from 22.4 min (95% CI 19.9 to 25.2) to 17.4 min (15.2 to 19.9).

> "Experienced registrars who had never attended simulation had a mean DNT of 18.5 min (16.2 to 21.1) compared with 13.5 min (11.9 to 15.2) for those who had."
