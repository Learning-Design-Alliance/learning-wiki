## Evidence

### Ding Kewen 2024

Ding Kewen, Chen Mingjing, Li Ping, Xie Zichuan, Zhang Haorong, Kou Ruixing, Xu Jionghui, Zou Ting, Luo Zhen, Song Haibo. (2024). The effect of simulation of sectional human anatomy using ultrasound on students' learning outcomes and satisfaction in echocardiography education: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05337-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot randomized controlled trial at West China School of Medicine with 18 first-year medical students randomized to traditional lecture (n = 8) or digital human (n = 10). Within-group Mann-Whitney comparisons of pre-test to post-test 1 showed significant gains, with the digital group's "absolute gain of 23.0 and a relative gain of 65.71%".

> "Both groups showed significant improvement in their scores on post-test 1 compared to pre-test (traditional group: from 33.1 ± 8.8 to 48.1 ± 13.1, P = 0.034 vs. digital group: from 35.0 ± 6.7 to 58.0 ± 13.2, P = 0.008)."
