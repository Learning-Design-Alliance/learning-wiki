## Evidence

### de la Fuente R. 2020

de la Fuente R., Fuentes R., Munoz-Gama J., Dagnino J., Sepúlveda M. (2020). Delphi Method to Achieve Clinical Consensus for a BPMN Representation of the Central Venous Access Placement for Training Purposes. Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 17(11):3889. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113889

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online Delphi survey with 13 experts (anesthesiology, intensive medicine, nephrology) from eight institutions in two rounds. The stopping criterion was met with "an average of the absolute percentage changes in all responses of 3.62%", below the predefined 15% threshold; percentage changes ranged from −14.58% to 4.25%.

> "The stopping criterion of the Delphi panel (Table 2) was met in the second round of surveys, which showed an average of the absolute percentage changes in all responses of 3.62% in relation to the responses to the first survey, with a standard deviation of 3.81%."
