## Evidence

### Melguizo-Ibáñez 2026

Melguizo-Ibáñez, E., Zurita-Ortega, F., Tadeu, P., Ubago-Jiménez, J. L., & Alonso Vargas, J. M. (2026). Classroom Active Breaks as a Physical-Educational Intervention for Executive Functions and Mathematical Outcomes: a Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10146-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

RVE meta-analysis across 16 inhibitory-control studies (n = 3798): pooled g = 0.21, non-significant (p = 0.18); the article notes precision decreased when modelling dependent effect sizes but the magnitude was preserved.

> "Neither the average model (g = 0.21, 95% CI [− 0.07, 0.50], Z = 1.47, p = 0.142) nor the RVE achieved statistically significant results (g = 0.21, 95% CI [− 0.11, 0.53], t(15) = 1.40, p = 0.18)."
