## Evidence

### Puro Vezolu 2025

Puro Vezolu, Nittala Noel Anurag Prashanth, A Hariharasudan. (2025). PROMPTa—A multifaceted assessment approach to develop writing skills. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337342

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Intra-rater reliability analysis with Pearson correlations for 46 participants, with effect sizes r = 0.743, r = 0.693, and r = 0.667 (all p < 0.001). The article reports "strong positive correlations across all three rating rounds" and notes a slight drift in scoring patterns over time while correlations stayed above 0.65.

> "The strongest correlation was observed between the first and second ratings (r = 0.743, p < 0.001), followed by the second and third ratings (r = 0.693, p < 0.001), while the first and third ratings showed a slightly lower but still strong correlation (r = 0.667, p < 0.001)."

### Puro Vezolu 2025 (2)

Puro Vezolu, Nittala Noel Anurag Prashanth, A Hariharasudan. (2025). PROMPTa—A multifaceted assessment approach to develop writing skills. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337342

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Inter-rater agreement analysis of ratings assigned by three different raters (Raters A, B, C) on the same tasks for 46 participants, with Pearson correlation effect sizes r = 0.752 (highest, Rater B–Rater C) and r = 0.569 (lowest, Rater A–Rater C), all significant at the 0.001 level.

> "The highest agreement was observed between Rater B and Rater C (0.752), while the lowest, though still moderate, was between Rater A and Rater C (0.569)."
