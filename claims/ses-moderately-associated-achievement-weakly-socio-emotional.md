Rémeau Margot, Raffy Gaël, Borst Grégoire. (2026). Academic Resilience in Contexts of Inequality: Motivational and Self‐Efficacy Profiles of Disadvantaged High Achievers. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.70190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive bivariate correlation analyses in the French panel of 8934 students showed that "family SES was moderately and positively associated with academic achievement (r = 0.42, p < 0.001)" while "the correlations between SES and socio‐emotional skills were weak."

> "family SES was moderately and positively associated with academic achievement (r = 0.42, p < 0.001), indicating that higher socio‐economic background is associated to better academic performance. In contrast, the correlations between SES and socio‐emotional skills were weak: negative for intrinsic motivation (r = –0.02, p < 0.05), slightly positive for extrinsic motivation (r = 0.01, p < 0.01), and more substantial for self‐efficacy (r = 0.08, p < 0.001)."
