## Evidence

### Pesch Megan H. 2020

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported baseline counseling practice on a 5-point ordinal scale from the pre-intervention survey of 53 obstetric providers. Only a small minority reported counseling more than rarely.

> "the majority of respondents reported that they never (51%, n = 26) or rarely (47%, n = 24) counseled expectant mothers about the risks and prevention of cCMV."

### Pesch Megan H. 2020 (2)

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported baseline testing frequency from the same pre-intervention survey, on a 5-point ordinal scale; testing of symptomatic expectant mothers was overwhelmingly rare or absent.

> "Most respondents reported that they never (25%, n = 13) or rarely (73%, n = 37) tested expectant mothers with cold-like symptoms for CMV."
