### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the Difficulty with results interpretation subcategory, 14 faculty and department heads reported problems interpreting RBA results; faculty described large fluctuations in scores from year to year and wanting a deeper understanding of what specific scores mean.

> "The biggest problem is that small student enrollments make it difficult to interpret the results because statistical analysis is not valid on these small numbers."
