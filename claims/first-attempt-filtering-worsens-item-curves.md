## Evidence

### Gerd Kortemeyer 2014

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2014). Extending Item Response Theory to Online Homework. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT analysis of homework data from the physics course, comparing item characteristic curves when homework items are scored as correct only if solved on the first attempt (Fig. 2) versus eventually solved. The article reports the curves "spread out further, making the result even less meaningful."

> "However, as Fig. 2 shows, using this approach, the curves spread out further, making the result even less meaningful."
