Item-level analysis of the same 101-participant VR scenario, per-question accuracy shown in Figure 2. The highest-scoring item reached "an accuracy of 94%" and the lowest (continued VF after 3 defibrillations) "an accuracy of 45%".

> "The question with the highest percentage of correct answers was number 14 ("What technique should be applied when approaching the patient and how should it affect the time to hospital?") with an accuracy of 94%, while the lowest percentage was number 9 ("What action should be taken if ventricular fibrillation continues after 3 defibrillations?"), with an accuracy of 45%."
