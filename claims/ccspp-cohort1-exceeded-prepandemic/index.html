Evidence¶

Klevan 2026¶

Klevan, S., Spitzer, N., Hernández, L., Rubinstein, C., & Swain, W. (2026). Community schools in Los Angeles Unified: Transforming teaching and learning. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/677.130

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Comparative achievement analysis reported in the report's findings section, contrasting CCSPP Cohort 1 schools' post-pandemic recovery trajectory with that of comparable non-CCSPP schools serving similar student populations. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"While all high-need schools in LAUSD have made progress recovering from pandemic-era achievement lows, by the 2023–24 school year, CCSPP Cohort 1 community schools’ math and ELA scores exceeded their prepandemic achievement levels—an outcome not observed in comparable non-CCSPP schools."

  • ELA gains in CCSPP schools (0.05 SD) were not statistically significant overall but were significant for English learners and, at p < 0.10, for socioeconomically disadvantaged and Black students — related
