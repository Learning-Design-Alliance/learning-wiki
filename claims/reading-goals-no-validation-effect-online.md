# Reading goals do not influence validation processes: no goal × congruency or goal × accuracy interactions on reading times

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No interactions between reading goal and congruency or accuracy were observed on target or spill-over reading times, providing no evidence that reading for study results in more extensive validation processes online. [→ Moort 2022](#moort-2022)

## Evidence

### Moort 2022

Moort, M. L. van, Koornneef, A. W., Wilderjans, T. F., & Broek, P. W. van den. (2022). Validation processes and reading purpose: Is validation against knowledge and prior text influenced by reading goal? Journal of Educational Psychology, 114(7), 1533-1552. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000769

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression on log-transformed reading times from the self-paced reading experiment found "no interactions between goal and congruency or accuracy" on target and spill-over sentences, disconfirming the hypothesis that reading for study produces more extensive validation processes during reading.

> "we did not observe evidence that supports Hypothesis 1 that reading for study results in more extensive validation processes online, as we observed no interactions between goal and congruency or accuracy"
