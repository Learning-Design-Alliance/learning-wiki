### Palmisano Francesco 2024

Palmisano Francesco, Santuari Nadia, Moletta Cristina, Ambrosi Elisa, Rizzoli Andrea. (2024). The effect of a team-based blended simulation training program on cardiopulmonary resuscitation on healthcare professionals' perception, performance, and costs: a mixed-method study. `q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding from the qualitative phase: participants emphasized including all department members to promote teamwork, coordination, and interprofessional cooperation, reporting that working with their own team made them feel more at ease. This is self-reported perception, not a measured performance outcome.

> "The simulations also highlighted that leadership can emerge from any team member, not just the doctor:“Even if the doctor was present, all the participants acted as team leaders”."
