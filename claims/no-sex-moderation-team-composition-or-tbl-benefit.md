## Evidence

### Heber Stefan 2024

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory extension of the trial's mixed model adding sex and all interactions; no interaction with sex reached significance for the team-composition results, with all interaction p-values above 0.15.

> "The exploratory extension of the model by the factor 'sex' and its interactions did not suggest that the above-mentioned results are different by sex (all interaction terms with sex p > 0.15)."

### Heber Stefan 2024 (2)

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory sex analyses of the TBL-effect models in the same trial; all interaction terms with sex had p-values above 0.67, with no evidence of a sex-effect.

> "There was no evidence of any sex-effect in these analyses (all interaction terms with sex p > 0.67)."
