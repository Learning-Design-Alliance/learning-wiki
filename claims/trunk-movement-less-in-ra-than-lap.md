`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis of the mid-thoracic sensor from 54 surgical novices performing three tasks on LAP and da Vinci Xi platforms (283 trunk observations). The analysis found "the trunk movement was less in RA tasks compared to LAP" with the printed p value; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Overall, the trunk movement was less in RA tasks compared to LAP (p < 0.0001)."
