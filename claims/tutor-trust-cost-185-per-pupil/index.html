Emily Buchanan 2015¶

Emily Buchanan, Jack Worth, Helen Aston. (2015). Tutor Trust Primary: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581540

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Cost information reported in the executive summary's cost section. In this project primary schools paid a flat fee of £1,000 for tuition irrespective of the number of pupils tutored and secondaries were offered tuition free of charge.

"At the time of the evaluation the Tutor Trust would charge primary or secondary schools between £18 and £26 for an hour of tuition. Based on groups of three pupils receiving 25 tuition sessions, the total cost of the intervention is estimated at approximately £185 per pupil."
