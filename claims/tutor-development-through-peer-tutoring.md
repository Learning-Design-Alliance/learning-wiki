## Evidence

### Yoo Dong Mi 2026

Yoo Dong Mi, Cho A Ra, Kim Sun. (2026). Enacted practices and developmental experiences of senior medical student tutors in a structured peer tutoring program. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of tutor interviews and activity reports in a structured near-peer tutoring program at a Korean medical school; the article reports that "Explaining study strategies promoted metacognitive development" and relational work fostered empathy.

> "Relational work—including rapport building and managing discouragement—required interpersonal skills beyond academic competence and fostered empathy and sensitivity to learner diversity. Explaining study strategies promoted metacognitive development."

### Yoo Dong Mi 2026 (2)

Yoo Dong Mi, Cho A Ra, Kim Sun. (2026). Enacted practices and developmental experiences of senior medical student tutors in a structured peer tutoring program. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the same interview-based thematic analysis of tutors' experiences and professional growth; tutors themselves drew parallels between tutoring and future clinical roles such as counseling and patient education.

> "Several tutors identified parallels with future clinical roles, especially communication and behavior-change counseling, contributing to early professional identity formation."
