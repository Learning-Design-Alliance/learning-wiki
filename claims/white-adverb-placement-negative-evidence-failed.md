## Evidence

### Young-Scholten 1999

Young-Scholten, M. (1999). Focus on Form and Linguistic Competence: Why Krashen Is Still Right about Acquisition. Paper presented at AAAL, Stamford. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED432919

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The paper reports, citing White (1991) and Schwartz and Gubala-Ryzak (1992), that classroom negative evidence on adverb placement failed: learners' "performance revealed changeswere purely superficial" despite needing to reorganize their grammars to stop verb raising.

> "Yet White (1991) attempted to accomplish just this, Schwartz and Gubala-Ryzak (1992) thenrevealed that the attempt failed.Learners needed to reorganize their grammars to stop raising main verbs as if English were French, but their performance revealed changeswere purely superficial."
