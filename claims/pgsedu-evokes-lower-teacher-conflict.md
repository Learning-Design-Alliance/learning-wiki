`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation models in the MoBa cohort (63,032 children; STRS data for ~4,300) tested whether PGSedu predicted teacher-reported relationship dimensions at age 5. The article reports "lower levels of student–teacher conflict" and "more positive teacher responses" were significant, while closeness was not (β = 0.01, p = .616).

> "children's PGSedu were significantly directly associated with lower levels of student–teacher conflict at age 5 (a: β = −0.08, 95% CI [−0.12, −0.05], p < .001) and more positive teacher responses to students (a: β = 0.07, 95% CI [0.03, 0.11], p = .001)"
