## Evidence

### Mohammed 2012

Mohammed, A. A., & Mostafa, A. A. (2012). Effectiveness of a Phonological Awareness Training Intervention on Word recognition ability of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. International Journal of Psycho-Educational Sciences, 1(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579061

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures analysis for Word Recognition Ability test showed significant differences between measures at the 0.01 level, including the between-measures effect (F = 643.039) and measures x groups interaction (F = 136.724).

> "The table shows that there are statistical differences between measures (pre - post- Follow – up ) at the level (0.01)."

### Mohammed 2012 (2)

Mohammed, A. A., & Mostafa, A. A. (2012). Effectiveness of a Phonological Awareness Training Intervention on Word recognition ability of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. International Journal of Psycho-Educational Sciences, 1(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579061

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffe multi-comparison tests showed gains from pre to post and pre to follow-up, but "no statistical differences between post and follow – up testing," indicating retained gains (post M=16.58, follow-up M=6.48 as printed in Table 5).

> "The table shows that there are statistical differences between pre and post me asures in favor of post test, and between pre and follow –up  measures in favor of  follow – up testing, but no statistical differences between post and follow – up testing."
