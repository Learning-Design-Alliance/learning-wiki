`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Mechanisms of Identity code distributions (Figure 3) from the normalized coding of facilitator interviews in the three programs. The authors relate the finding to research in which recognition as a community member can be determinative for identity formation.

> "Furthermore, across all three programs Negotiated Experiences and Community Membership are the most salient mechanisms, suggesting interactions and connections made through participation while engaging in the practices of the community support membership and identity development."
