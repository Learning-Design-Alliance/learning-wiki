### Montalbano 2012

Montalbano, V., Benedetti, R., Mariotti, E., Mariotti, M. A., & Porri, A. (2012). Attempts of Transforming Teacher Practice Through Professional Development. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1212.1042](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1212.1042)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Enrolment counts printed in Table 1 for the advanced course show totals of 17, 19, 14 and 3 across academic years 2004/05 to 2007/08. The authors attribute "the decreasing of enrolments" to changed teacher-ranking score rules and to online courses offering the same score at lower cost; no statistical test is reported.

> "The decreasing of enrolments corresponds to a change in rules for attributing score for teacher ranking and the proliferation of online courses that gave the same score with fewer costs and efforts for teachers."
