## Evidence

### Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026

Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Conditions for Effective Learning Without Upfront Instruction: How Practice with Feedback Supports Memory, Generalization, Motivation, and Metacognition. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10103-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Study 1 exploratory regression using lecture as reference showed "correct-response feedback was significantly less effective at promoting generalization, t(291) = −2.69, p =.008, a.36 SD difference (39% vs. 27%)", moderated by confidence (b = −.09, p =.017), with lecture also ineffective for less-confident students.

> "In the exploratory model comparing lecture to correct-response feedback, cor- rect-response feedback was significantly less effective at promoting generalization, t(291) = −2.69, p =.008, a.36 SD difference (39% vs. 27%)."

### Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026 (2)

Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Conditions for Effective Learning Without Upfront Instruction: How Practice with Feedback Supports Memory, Generalization, Motivation, and Metacognition. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10103-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Study 2's secondary model found correct-response feedback "7 points less effective at promoting generalization than lecture, although this effect was not significant in Study 2, t(294), p =.153". The direction replicated Study 1 but the test did not reach significance in the open-ended format.

> "In the secondary model comparing lecture directly to correct-response feed- back, correct-response feedback was found to be 7 points less effective at promot- ing generalization than lecture, although this effect was not significant in Study 2, t(294), p =.153."
