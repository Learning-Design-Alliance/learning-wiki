### Weckström Tarja 2023

Weckström Tarja, Elovainio Marko, Pulkki-Råback Laura, Suokas Kimmo, Komulainen Kaisla, Mullola Sari, Böckerman Petri, Hakulinen Christian. (2023). School achievement in adolescence and the risk of mental disorders in early adulthood: a Finnish nationwide register study. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02081-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multinomial logistic regression on the Finnish register cohort, with persons without any mental disorder as reference and comorbidity counted as the number of separate diagnoses; results displayed in Fig. 3. The article reports "a clear dose-response gradient"; no effect size is printed in the quoted text.

> "The associations followed a clear dose-response gradient, with higher school grades associated with a smaller risk of comorbid psychiatric disorders. As shown in Fig. 3, the steepest gradient was observed when the outcome was three or more comorbid diagnoses."
