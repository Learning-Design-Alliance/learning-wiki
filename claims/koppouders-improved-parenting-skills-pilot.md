## Evidence

### van der Zanden Rianne AP 2010

van der Zanden Rianne AP, Speetjens Paula AM, Arntz Karlijn SE, Onrust Simone A. (2010). Online Group Course for Parents With Mental Illness: Development and Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1394

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Pre/post pilot study of 48 parents with mental illness; completers-only paired t tests on the Parenting Scale. Laxness and overreactivity decreased significantly, "effect sizes were moderate (d = 0.52 and d = 0.48)"; intention-to-treat analyses confirmed the outcomes.

> "The parents’ laxness and overreactivity ratings decreased significantly in the course of the intervention; effect sizes were moderate (d = 0.52 and d = 0.48)."
