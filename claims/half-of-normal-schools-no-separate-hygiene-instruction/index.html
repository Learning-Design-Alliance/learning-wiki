Arthur Heche 1909¶

Arthur Heche. (1909). A Report on the Teaching and Practice of Hygiene in the Public Normal Schools of the United States. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducreducpsyc

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Questionnaire survey of normal schools; returns from 84 of 191 addressed. The report states that "Exactly one-half of the 84 schools heard from offer no hygiene courses" separate from physiology, and nine schools give no physiology at all.

"Exactly one-half of the 84 schools heard from offer no hygiene courses aside from the hygiene given with physiology or incidentally in courses on school management, methods of classes, psychology, etc."
