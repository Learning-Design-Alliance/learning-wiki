Evidence¶

Gilbert Valentin 2026¶

Gilbert Valentin, Philip Pierre, Llorca Pierre-Michel, Samalin Ludovic. (2026). Use of artificial intelligence–enhanced virtual patients in educational approaches to medical interview training: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08804-9

q3 · i? · review · r3

Randomized controlled study of 64 clinicians (Sarli et al. 2024, MMAT 4/5) with immediate, personalized feedback conditionally triggered for clinicians with low baseline empathy. The review reports "initially low-empathy clinicians showed significant improvement at follow-up"; no effect size is printed.

"Although emotional reactivity itself did not change, initially low-empathy clinicians showed significant improvement at follow-up, highlighting a targeted "corrective" effect."
