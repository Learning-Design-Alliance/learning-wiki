## Evidence

### Peng 2020

Peng Peng, Kievit Rogier A. (2020). The Development of Academic Achievement and Cognitive Abilities: A Bidirectional Perspective. Child Development Perspectives. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdep.12352

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A meta-analysis of executive function and reading from age 6 to adulthood (Follmer, 2018) found no age variation overall, with printed correlations r = .25 for adults and rs = .33–.38 for children and adolescents. Other meta-analyses of executive function and achievement similarly found no age effect.

> "The results did not indicate that this relation varied with age or age group (ages 6–11 vs. ages 12–17 vs. adult), although the relation was smaller for adults (r = .25) than for children and adolescents (rs = .33~.38)"
