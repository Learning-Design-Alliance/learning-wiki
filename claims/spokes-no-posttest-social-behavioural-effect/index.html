Evidence¶

Tracey 2016¶

Tracey, L., Chambers, B., Bywater, T., & Elliott, L. (2016). SPOKES Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581435

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Intention-to-treat analysis of the pupil-randomised trial's secondary outcomes (SDQ teacher ratings and PARYC parent questionnaire) at post-test; Table 10 reports effect sizes from -0.08 to +0.18, all non-significant, with up to 527 pupils in the models.

"In addition, there were no statistically significant differences between conditions for the secondary (social and behavioural) measures at immediate post-test (Table 10). The results were also inconsistent with both positive and negative effect sizes occurring ranging from -0.08 to +0.18."
