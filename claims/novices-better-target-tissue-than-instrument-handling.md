### Deuchler Svenja 2022

Deuchler Svenja, Scholtz Julia, Ackermann Hanns, Seitz Berthold, Koch Frank. (2022). Implementation of microsurgery simulation in an ophthalmology clerkship in Germany: a prospective, exploratory study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03634-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of simulator log values for Navigation Training Level 1 in 79 evaluated students. Mean Total Score was 25.7 (± 17.5) of 100; Completed Objects averaged 87.2 while Odometer reached the saturation value of 20 negative points for all students. No effect size printed.

> "Objective scores from Navigation Training Level 1 showed that students achieved better results in the criteria categories of Completing Objects and Tissue Treatment than in the categories of Instrument and Microscope Handling."
