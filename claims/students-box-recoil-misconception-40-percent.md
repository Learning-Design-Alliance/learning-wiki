### Tarek A. Elsayed 2010

Tarek A. Elsayed. (2010). Interpreting Recoil For Undergraduate Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2997

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Students were asked whether a large box enclosing the gas container (Fig. 2) would recoil when the piston was released. The author reports approximately 40% answered affirmatively, an error since no force acts on the large box.

> "Therefore, it is not surprising that approximately 40% of the students answered this question affirmatively."
