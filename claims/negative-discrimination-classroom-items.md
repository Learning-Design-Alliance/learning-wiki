### Slepkov 2021

Slepkov, A. D., Van Bussel, M. L., Fitze, K. M., & Burr, W. S. (2021). A baseline for multiple-choice testing in the university classroom. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1177/21582440211016838](https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440211016838)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-excluded discrimination analysis across 11,246 items from 182 classroom tests; the authors state that "the improper keying of an item is the most common culprit in negatively-discriminating classroom test items."

> "Indeed, we find that 8% of the surveyed items had 𝑟′< 0. There are several reasons why an item might display negative discrimination, wherein more knowledgeable students select the keyed response at a lower rate than do less-knowledgeable students."
