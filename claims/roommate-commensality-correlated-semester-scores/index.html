Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Commensality–performance associations are stronger for party/league members, former class leaders, and students with party-member parents, and weaker for ethnic minority students — related
  • The link between commensality and academic performance is stronger in the second semester than the first, suggesting it intensifies over time — related
  • Peer acceptance measured by commensality is significantly negatively associated with failed-subject ratio, and positively with major ranking and negatively with failed-subject counts — related
  • The association between peer commensality and academic performance is stronger for male than female students — related
  • Commensality with the most physically distant peers (same class, different floor or building) shows the strongest positive link to academic performance, contrary to the propinquity effect — related
