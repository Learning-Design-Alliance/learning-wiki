Mixed models on school-record grades (Language, English, Mathematics, Science, History) in 1296 adolescents. The "MedDiet cluster was the only one that achieved a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05) in all school subjects" versus WD and LFV-HSD.

> "The WD group underperformed in all academic analyses compared with the other groups. Notably, the MedDiet cluster was the only one that achieved a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05) in all school subjects compared to both the WD and LFV-HSD groups."
