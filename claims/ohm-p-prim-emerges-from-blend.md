### Michael C. Wittmann 2010

Michael C. Wittmann. (2010). Using conceptual blending to describe emergent meaning in wave propagation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1008.0216](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1008.0216)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The author's theoretical interpretation of the interview data: rather than claiming students activated the Ohm's p-prim, the data suggest the idea can be seen as "emerging in this context" through the Wave-Ball blend, in which "the idea emerges that greater effort leads to greater speed."

> "But, the data suggest that one can think of the idea not being activated (as Ohm’s p-prim) but as emerging in this context."
