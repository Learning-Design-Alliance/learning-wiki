Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević 2026¶

Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević. (2026). Agentivism: a learning theory for the age of artificial intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07813

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

A theoretical proposition derived from the delegated agency mechanism in the Empirically testable propositions section. The article states that "learning should be stronger when AI support preserves learner responsibility for problem framing, criteria setting, and justification than when AI support collapses these processes into direct answer delivery". This is a prediction, not a reported result.

"A first proposition is that learning should be stronger when AI support preserves learner responsibility for problem framing, criteria setting, and justification than when AI support collapses these processes into direct answer delivery"

Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević 2026 (2)¶

Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević. (2026). Agentivism: a learning theory for the age of artificial intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07813

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The sixth proposition, a longitudinal prediction from the theory. The article states that "repeated low-friction delegation without subsequent reconstruction should be associated over time with weaker calibration of one's own competence and greater dependence on external support, even when short-term productivity rises". The article itself calls this prediction more plausible than fully established.

"repeated low-friction delegation without subsequent reconstruction should be associated over time with weaker calibration of one's own competence and greater dependence on external support, even when short-term productivity rises"
