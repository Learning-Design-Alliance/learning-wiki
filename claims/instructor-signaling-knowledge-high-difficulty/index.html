"Additionally, a significant three-way interaction was observed for the topic of research designs (postcontrast2difficulty), B = 0.29, SE = 0.13, p =.027, suggesting that the positive effect of a visible instructor’s gestures and gaze shifts on knowledge acquisition improved as perceived difficulty increased."
