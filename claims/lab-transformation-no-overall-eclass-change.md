## Evidence

### Benjamin Pollard and H. J. Lewandowski 2019

Benjamin Pollard and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Transforming a large introductory lab course: impacts on views about experimental physics. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.pollard](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.pollard)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of E-CLASS overall post-test score distributions between Spring 2017 (original course) and Spring 2018 (transformed course), using the Mann-Whitney U test at the 5% significance level with Bonferroni correction. The authors report the distributions were "statistically equivalent"; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "We find that in overall score, the two post-test distributions are also statistically equivalent, suggesting that the course transformation did not have a marked effect on students' views about experimental physics when distilled into a single number."
