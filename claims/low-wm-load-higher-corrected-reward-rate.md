### Chengyan Yang 2025

Chengyan Yang, Tongran Liu, Mengxin Wen & Xun Liu. (2025). The distinct functions of working memory and intelligence in model-based and model-free reinforcement learning. npj Science of Learning 10:72. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00363-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing 50 participants' performance across low (2-pair) and high (4-pair) WM load blocks of a two-stage RL task. The article reports "higher corrected reward rates in the low WM load condition" with t(49) = 11.2, p < 0.001, d = 1.58.
