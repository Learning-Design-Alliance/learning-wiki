Mnguni L (2026) A Capability–Decision Model of teacher readiness for AI integration in teaching. Front. Educ. 11:1897632. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1897632

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the article's review of additive TPACK–belief integrations (Section 2), illustrated with four cited studies (Joo et al. 2018; Wangdi et al. 2023; Al-Abdullatif 2024; Runge et al. 2025) in which self-reported knowledge constructs were entered beside belief constructs. The article argues this "remains unclear whether to prioritise capability building, normative change, infrastructure provision, or coaching."

"When several predictors are statistically significant in an additive model, it remains unclear whether to prioritise capability building, normative change, infrastructure provision, or coaching."
