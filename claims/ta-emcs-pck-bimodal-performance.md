### Liam Doyle 2026

Liam Doyle, Alexandru Maries, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Exploring pedagogical content knowledge of physics teaching assistants using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1103/fgqc-dfbz](https://doi.org/10.1103/fgqc-dfbz)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Question-by-question analysis of TA PCK scores on the EMCS, reported in Figure 2 and Section III B. The article classified questions using cutoffs of 52% for poor and 67% for good normalized average PCK scores, shifted from prior studies' 50% cutoff because three questions scored near 50%.

> "TAs' performance was somewhat bimodal on the 18 questions with major or moderate difficulties, see Figure 2. In particular, on nine questions, TA PCK performance was poor, on two questions, TA PCK performance was moderate, and on seven questions, TA PCK performance was good."
