q3 · i? · causal · r2

Regression discontinuity (fuzzy RD) evaluation of SES offers in six oversubscribed districts, comparing post-test scores of applicants just below and above the assignment cutoff. The study "find no statistically significant impact of offering SES on student achievement in reading or in mathematics."

"For students in these oversubscribed districts in grades 3 –8 at the cusp of receiving an offer of services, w e find no statistically significant impact of offering SES on student achievement in reading or in mathematics."
