`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The essay's introduction cites multiple studies and a meta-analysis of group work in postsecondary courses attributing this to Springer et al. and others. The essay states "its implementation has been shown to increase average student performance compared with lecture alone" and that Driessen et al. (2024) demonstrated positive impacts significant across class size, group size, course level, student major, and instructor-assigned groups. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "its implementation has been shown to increase average student performance compared with lecture alone (Springer et al., 1999; Knight & Wood, 2005; Carmichael, 2009; Chaplin, 2009; Daniel, 2016; Marbach-Ad et al., 2016; Yapici, 2016; Donovan et al., 2018; Weir et al., 2019; Driessen et al., 2024)"
