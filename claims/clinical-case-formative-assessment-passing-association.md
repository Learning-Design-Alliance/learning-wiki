## Evidence

### Fernández Ros Nerea 2021

Fernández Ros Nerea, Lucena Felipe, Iñarrairaegui Mercedes, Landecho Manuel F., Sunsundegui Patricia, Jordán-Iborra Carlota, Pineda Iñigo, Quiroga Jorge, Herrero Jose Ignacio. (2021). Web-based formative assessment through clinical cases: role in pathophysiology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02691-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 96 students who sat the exam, of whom 65 passed. A multivariate logistic regression with gender, academic delay, GP score and number of cases done found both GP score and cases done independently associated with passing, with the number of cases done showing an "odds ratio: 1.393; 95% confidence interval: 1.101–1.763; P = 0.006".

> "In multivariate analysis score in GP in the previous year (odds ratio for each 10 points: 2.891; 95% confidence interval: 1.692–4.940; P < 0.001) and the number of cases done (odds ratio: 1.393; 95% confidence interval: 1.101–1.763; P = 0.006) were independently associated to the endpoint of passing the exam."
