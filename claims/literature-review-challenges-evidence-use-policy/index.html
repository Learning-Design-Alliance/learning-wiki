Julieta Lugo-Gil 2019¶

Julieta Lugo-Gil, Dana Jean-Baptiste, Livia Frasso Jaramillo. (2019). Use of Evidence to Drive Decision-Making in Government. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/use-of-evidence-to-drive-decision-making-in-government

q2 · i? · review · r?

Narrative literature review of grey and peer-reviewed literature conducted in Phase 1 of the project. The review identified challenges including "misalignment between the research conducted and the evidence that is needed for decision-making" and noted that evidence producers and decision-makers "rarely use frameworks that bridge the gap between research and practice." No effect sizes are reported.

"Findings from the literature review identified the following challenges in using evidence in policy decision-making: misalignment between the research conducted and the evidence that is needed for decision-making, many factors can impact the use of research evidence daring the complicated process of policy decision-making"
