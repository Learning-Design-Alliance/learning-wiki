Evidence¶

Rasikawati 2019¶

Ira Rasikawati (2019). Corpus-Based Data-Driven Learning to Augment L2 Students’ Vocabulary Repertoire. International Dialogues on Education, 6(2), 83-98. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1245249

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this corpus-based collocation instruction study, attributing to its authors the claim that chunk-based learning beat individual-word learning and that it "increases the quantity of learners' mental interaction". No effect size is printed.

"Ashouri, Armandi, and Rahimi (2014) who studied the impact of corpus -based collocation instruction found that students who learned lexical collocations – chunks of words that often appear together – improved their collocational knowledge better than those learning individual words."
