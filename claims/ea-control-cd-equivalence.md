## Evidence

### Schmidt Michelle 2024

Schmidt Michelle, Schauwinhold Michael Tobias, Loeffler Leonie Anne Kathrin, Klasen Martin, Lambert Sophie Isabelle, Sopka Saša, Vogt Lina. (2024). Safety management within the scope of teaching practical clinical skills: framing errors for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training – a multi-arm randomized controlled equivalence trial. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2408458

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Equivalence analysis of compression depth in the randomized equivalence trial (n = 427). Correct CD at t1 was 40.4% in control and 40.5% in EA; "significant equivalence between the EA and control groups".

> "The comparison of the EA and control groups resulted in a proportional difference of 0.1pp. The 95% CI for the proportional difference was −11.6% to 11.7%. The results indicated significant equivalence between the EA and control groups."
