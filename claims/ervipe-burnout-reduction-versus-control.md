`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Copenhagen Burnout Inventory measured at baseline, pre-intervention, postintervention, and 1-month follow-up in the 3-group quasi-experimental study (87 students). "Group B having a significantly lower score than Group A (mean difference=–2.02; P=.02)"; Group C differences were not significant.

> "Burnout scores also improved across groups, with Group B having a significantly lower score than Group A (mean difference=–2.02; P=.02). No significant burnout differences were found between Group C and Groups A or B."
