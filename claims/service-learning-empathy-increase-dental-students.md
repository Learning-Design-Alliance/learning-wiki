## Evidence

### Leiva Hernández Carolina 2026

Leiva Hernández Carolina, Aguilera Felipe Rodrigo, Aravena-Castillo Verónica, Hidalgo V. Viviana, Farías Orellana Javier, Stuardo-Parada Adriana. (2026). Dental students’ attitudes toward community service: a pre–post survey study of a service-learning experience in Chile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10109-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre–post survey of matched dental students (n = 77) in a Chilean service-learning program, analyzed with repeated-measures ANOVA; the empathy subscale rose from 3.18 to 4.34, "corresponding to a large effect (g = 1.58; 95% CI 1.25–1.92)".

> "Empathy increased from 3.18 (95% CI 2.99–3.36) to 4.34 (95% CI 4.20–4.48; p < 0.001), corresponding to a large effect (g = 1.58; 95% CI 1.25–1.92)."
