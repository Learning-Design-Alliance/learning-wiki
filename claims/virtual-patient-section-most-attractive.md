## Evidence

### Kang Sung-Yoon 2018

Kang Sung-Yoon, Kim Sae-Hoon, Kwon Yong-Eun, Kim Tae-Bum, Park Hye-Kyung, Park Heung-Woo, Chang Yoon-Seok, Jee Young-Koo, Moon Hee-Bom, Min Kyung-Up, Cho Sang-Heon. (2018). The virtual asthma guideline e-learning program: learning effectiveness and user satisfaction. The Korean Journal of Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.3904/kjim.2017.017

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive survey result from the same 158 physicians-in-training after 2 weeks of VLCAM use. Problem-based learning with a virtual patient was named "most attractive and interesting" by 40% of respondents, versus self-assessment at 29%, e-book at 28%, and reference room at 3%.

> "The section considered to be most attractive and interesting was problem-based learning with a virtual patient (40% of respondents), followed by self-assessment (29%), asthma guideline e-book (28%), and online reference room (3%)."
