## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among children receiving PFS, high dental anxiety (Modified CFSS-DS score ≥38) was less prevalent in the EL group (18.5%) than the TSD group (24.3%), with an adjusted OR of 0.65 (95% CI 0.46–0.93; P = 0.019). [→ Zhu Minmin 2020](#zhu-minmin-2020)

## Evidence

### Zhu Minmin 2020

Zhu Minmin, Yu Hongbing, Xie Bo, Li Hongwen, He Qian, Li Huimin, Su Jing, Li Xueqi. (2020). Experiential learning for children's dental anxiety: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-020-01204-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial primary outcome: mixed effect logistic regression with a random cluster effect of class and covariates of gender and school compared 396 EL children with 391 TSD children, showing "the OR was 0.65 (95% CI: 0.46–0.93; P = 0.019)". No standardized effect size printed.

> "The prevalence of high DA (Modified CFSS-DS scores ≥38) was 18.5% in the EL group and 24.3% in the TSD group. The mixed effect logistic regression showed that the OR was 0.65 (95% CI: 0.46–0.93; P = 0.019), after adjusting gender and school"
