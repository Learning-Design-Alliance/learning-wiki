Evidence¶

Iwatani 2020¶

Iwatani, E., Means, B., Romero, M. R., & Vang, M. C. (2020). Deepening Science Engagement with Challenge Based Learning: Research Report. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f928a607-e645-49d6-abbf-3d9b42cf7383 doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/93

q2 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative findings from 32 student focus groups. Students cited independence, real-world topics like global warming and pollinator loss, hands-on activities such as dissections and stream analysis, and group collaboration as key benefits.

"Students enjoyed participating in the challenge based science lessons which they saw as very different from their normal lessons."
