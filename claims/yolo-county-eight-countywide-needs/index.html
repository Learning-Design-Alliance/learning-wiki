Countywide needs assessment report. The team compiled needs from two rounds of community engagement sessions, categorized them by coded topics per Supervisorial District, and identified the eight countywide needs, "as well as needs specific to each Supervisorial District if the data supported" such needs.

"The Roadmap to the Future team identified eight countywide needs to help achieve the Roadmap to the Future's vision, as well as needs specific to each Supervisorial District if the data supported the development of District-specific needs."
