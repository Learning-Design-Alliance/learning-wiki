## Evidence

### Zhao Yu 2021

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of five studies analyzing factors influencing real digital skills; the review also lists age, attitude and technological and cognitive skills as affecting digital competence.

> "factors such as gender, previous digital experience, received training, number of research and innovation projects participated in, teaching experience and the use of technology (Videos) have been listed."

### Zhao Yu 2021 (2)

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports, attributing to Grande-de-Prado et al. (2020), a gender difference in self-perceived competence; the review also notes women used technologies for social purposes while men used them more for technical or training purposes.

> "Grande-de-Prado et al. (2020) indicated that men considered themselves more competent than women in the use of ICTs, reporting better information management and online collaboration skills"
