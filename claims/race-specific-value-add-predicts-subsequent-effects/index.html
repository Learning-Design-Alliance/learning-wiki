Evidence¶

Delgado 2023¶

Delgado, W. (2023). Is the Best Teacher the Best for Everyone? Variation of Teacher Effectiveness by Student Race. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/is-the-best-teacher-the-best-for-everyone

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Predictive validation using teacher moves across schools or grades within Chicago Public Schools data; the brief reports that students taught after the move "showed increases in their test scores by one standard deviation," mirroring the teacher's prior race-specific value-add.

"When a teacher whose prior performance showed they raised Black students' test scores by one standard deviation moved across schools or grades, Black students taught by the teacher in their new school or grade also showed increases in their test scores by one standard deviation."
