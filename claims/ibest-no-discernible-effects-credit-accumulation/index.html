In the Anderson et al. (2017) QED (Accelerating Opportunity, propensity score matched, 42,894 students), 45.7% of the intervention group versus 46.9% of the comparison group earned more than 12 credits, effect size -0.03, improvement index -1, p .13 — not statistically significant.
