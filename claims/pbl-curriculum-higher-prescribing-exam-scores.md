### Donker 2026

Donker, E. M., Piët, J. D., Brinkman, D. J., Richir, M. C., Papaioannidou, P., Likic, R., Sanz, E. J., Christiaens, T., Costa, J. N., De Ponti, F., Böttiger, Y., Kramers, C., van Agtmael, M. Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.70063

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison across 16 European medical schools comparing exam scores between curriculum types. The article reports the PBL advantage as "+11.4% score, P < 0.001" in Examination 1 and "+6.3% score, P < 0.001" in Examination 2.

> "Students taught with a problem‐based learning curriculum outperformed students taught with a traditional learning curriculum in both Examination 1 (+11.4% score, P < 0.001), and Examination 2 (+6.3% score, P < 0.001)."
