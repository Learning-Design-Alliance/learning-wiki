## Evidence

### Hossain Md. Ismail 2026

Hossain Md. Ismail, Ahmad Iftakhar. (2026). Education in Haor areas of Bangladesh: A mixed-methods study on academic performance, school attendance, and school intervention. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0348890

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression predicting regular school attendance in the 189-student Haor survey found parental education a positive predictor, "OR = 2.48, 95% CI [1.25, 4.93]". Chi-square also confirmed an association between attendance and parental education (χ² = 7.60, p = .022).

> "parental education positively predicted regular attendance (B = 0.910, p = .009, OR = 2.48, 95% CI [1.25, 4.93])"

### Hossain Md. Ismail 2026 (2)

Hossain Md. Ismail, Ahmad Iftakhar. (2026). Education in Haor areas of Bangladesh: A mixed-methods study on academic performance, school attendance, and school intervention. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0348890

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the same attendance regression, school distance "OR = 0.44, 95% CI [0.23, 0.84]" and peer help for study "OR = 0.44, 95% CI [0.20, 0.95]" negatively predicted regular attendance. Household work showed a marginal positive effect (p = .054).

> "whereas school distance (B = –0.819, p = .013, OR = 0.44, 95% CI [0.23, 0.84]) and peer help for study (B = –0.832, p = .037, OR = 0.44, 95% CI [0.20, 0.95]) negatively predicted attendance."
