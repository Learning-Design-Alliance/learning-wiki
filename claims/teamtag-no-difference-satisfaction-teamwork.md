## Evidence

### Freytag J 2021

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section 3.5 of the randomised trial: post-debriefing helpfulness ratings from the 32 students showed "high levels of satisfaction with the debriefings across all stations" in both conditions, with no significant differences (all p≥.06).

> "Participants in both conditions expressed high levels of satisfaction with the debriefings across all stations (M=2.37-2.85; all p≥.06, see attachment 2 , here table 1)."

### Freytag J 2021 (2)

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Final-survey relevance ratings (section 3.6) after all simulations: both conditions rated all 15 CRM principles relevant or very relevant, with no between-condition differences (all p≥.06); no effect size printed.

> "In the final survey, participants in both conditions rated all CRM principles as relevant or very relevant (M=1.94-2.77; all p≥.06, see attachment 2 , here table 2)."

### Freytag J 2021 (3)

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA (section 3.7) with the TEAM sum score of the 6th case as dependent variable, condition as independent variable, and CRM baseline, 1st-case TEAM score and 6th-case type as covariates: "no effect of the type of debriefing on teamwork", p=.23; no effect size printed.

> "The analysis revealed no effect of the type of debriefing on teamwork in the last case (F(1,1)=7.38, p=.23)."
