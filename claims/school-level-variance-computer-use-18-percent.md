## Evidence

### Hermans 2008

Hermans, R., Tondeur, J., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). The impact of primary school teachers’ educational beliefs on the classroom use of computers. Computers & Education, 51(4), 1499–1509. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.02.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The null multilevel model for the 525 teachers nested in 68 schools showed both teacher-level and school-level variances significantly different from zero (both p < 0.001), with 18.19% of variance between schools. In the final model this fell to 16.68%.

> "This results in 18.19% of the variance to be attributed to diﬀerences between schools, and 81.81% of the var- iance due to diﬀerences at the teacher level."
