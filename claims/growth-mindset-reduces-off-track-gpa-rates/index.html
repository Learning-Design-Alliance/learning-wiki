q4 · i? · causal · r3

Poor-performance rates (GPA below 2.0 on a four-point scale), a pre-registered secondary outcome from school administrative data, showed a "reduction from 46% to 41%", a relative risk reduction of 11% by the article's calculation; no standardized effect size is printed for this outcome, so no magnitude is coded.

"The model estimates that 5.3% (95% confidence interval = −1.7, −9.0), s.e. = 1.8, t = 2.95, P = 0.005 of 1.5 million students in the United States per year would be prevented from being 'off track' for graduation by the brief and low-cost growth mindset intervention, representing a reduction from 46% to 41%"
