## Evidence

### Yuchen Wang 2025

Yuchen Wang, Pei-Duo Yu, Chee Wei Tan. (2025). Future-Proofing Programmers: Optimal Knowledge Tracing for AI-Assisted Personalized Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.23996](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.23996)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Controlled semester-long study in a university AI course with 203 students randomly assigned to four model-assisted groups and a control group. Table III reports the COTUTOR-versus-control contrast as (2.78|0.01|0.62), i.e., t = 2.78, p = 0.01, Cohen's d = 0.62, a medium effect size exceeding the one-tailed critical t of 1.68.

> "The experiment group with COTUTORalso achieved the highest average assignment score (73.2%) across the five experiment groups, followed by Code-DKT (71.8%), DKT (70.4%), BKT (70.1%), and the control group (68.4%)."
