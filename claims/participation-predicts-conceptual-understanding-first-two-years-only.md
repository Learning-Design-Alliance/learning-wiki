## Evidence

### Baumgartner 2025

Baumgartner, V., Daguati, S., Trninic, D., Akveld, M., Caspar, A., Hungerbühler, N., Kapur, M. (2025). Problem-solving before instruction for learning linear algebra in university mathematics. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09709-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Linear and Bayesian regressions predicting performance on the two end-of-semester conceptual understanding tests from first-semester participation. Years 1–2 supported hypothesis H3 (e.g. BF10 = 216.29, R² = 0.05 for CU I Year 2); in Year 3 only BF10 = 1.04 slightly favoured the hypothesis for CU I, and in Year 4 both Bayesian factors favoured the null model.

> "In the fourth iteration, none of the predictions were significant."
