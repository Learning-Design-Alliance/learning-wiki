Discourse analysis of the six-faculty interviews found that mere exposure to the idea of incorporating ESR, along with empathetic listening and questions encouraging deeper reflection, prompted noticeable attitude change; the shift was most pronounced for two instructors, one of whom moved from skepticism to general agreement after a follow-up question.
