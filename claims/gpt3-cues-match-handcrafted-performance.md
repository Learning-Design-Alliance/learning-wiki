## Evidence

### Abdelghani 2023

Abdelghani, R., Wang, Y.-H., Yuan, X., Wang, T., Lucas, P., Sauzéon, H., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2023). GPT-3-driven pedagogical agents for training children's curious question-asking skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://hal.science/hal-03890447v1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise t-test from the same 75-student three-condition experiment comparing the two closed-cue agents on divergent QA percentage during training. The authors note only a 14% chance to see a difference between the two; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "results showed no significant difference between the two incentive agents, i.e. the hand-crafted one and the GPT-3-driven one (t=0.88, p-value=0.38 and power=0.14) suggesting that children's performances were similar"
