## Evidence

### Song 2025

Song, M., Li, L., He, D. & Cai, Q. (2025). Reading experience reveals shared and idiosyncratic neural patterns during text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00390-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Convergent-validity analysis in the main sample comparing tART exposure scores with a survey in which participants reported reading frequency per topic over the last three years on five options converted to 0–4, analyzed with Spearman's rank correlation. The printed correlation is r = 0.744.

> "Print exposure measured using tART was significantly correlated with participants’ self-reported exposure, Spearman r = 0.744, p < .0001, suggesting that the tART could reliably assess their topic-specific reading experience."
