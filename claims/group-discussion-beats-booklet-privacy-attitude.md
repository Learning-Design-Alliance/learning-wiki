## Evidence

### Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen 2016

Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen, Faraji Mona. (2016). Comparison of effect between group discussion and educational booklet on Iranian nursing students’ attitude and practice toward patient privacy. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.29

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group pretest–posttest study of 60 nursing students (55 analyzed) in Kashan, Iran; attitude measured with the 34-item SAPP questionnaire. Posttest attitude means were 30.38±4.73 (discussion) vs 23.34±8.97 (booklet), between-group P=0.001 by Mann–Whitney U-test, showing "significantly higher in students in the group discussion method".

> "After the intervention, the mean attitude (P=0.001) and practice (P=0.001) scores were significantly increased in the two groups. However, the mean posttest attitude and practice scores were significantly higher in students in the group discussion method."
