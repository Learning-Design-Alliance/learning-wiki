### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (2002). Investigating student understanding of quantum physics: Spontaneous models of conductivity. American Journal of Physics 70:3, 218-226. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.1119/1.1447542](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1447542)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Demonstration interview finding: of thirteen Physics 420 students, eleven had completed EE classes discussing semiconductors, yet only two described band diagrams in detail. Several used the electron-jump model; one student's electron pull reasoning still yielded a correct prediction about doped semiconductors.

> "Eleven of the thirteen interviewed students had completed electrical engineering classes in which semiconductors were discussed and used, but only two were able to describe the band diagram representation of semiconductors in any detail."
