Descriptive analysis of the 34 included review articles reporting distributions across educational levels and domains. The review reports that "most of the articles have focused on Higher education (88.2%)" and separately that language education and communication is addressed in 50% of studies.

"In case of education level, most of the articles have focused on Higher education (88.2%), some also included K-12 and early education (35.3%), very few talked about Professional training (8.8%)."
