## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2026

Xue Gang, Zhang Xin, Ji Shengyi, Qi Jin. (2026). Integrating problem-based learning with virtual simulation in trauma orthopedic education: effects on learning outcomes and student satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09558-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same assessor-blinded RCT of 50 clinical medical students. Teaching effectiveness was measured with an anonymous self-administered questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.87) and teaching satisfaction with a self-designed scale (Cronbach's α = 0.91). The experimental group's "overall teaching effectiveness scores" and "total teaching satisfaction scores" were significantly higher. The article reports Cohen's d = 4.40 and d = 2.18 respectively in the Results section.

> "overall teaching effectiveness scores (17.72 ± 1.24 vs. 12.72 ± 1.02,mean difference = 5.00,95% CI: 4.35 to 5.65,p < 0.01), and total teaching satisfaction scores (86.96 ± 6.46 vs. 70.24 ± 8.71,mean difference = 16.72,95% CI:12.35 − 21.09,p < 0.05)"
