## Evidence

### Onódi Zsófia 2025

Onódi Zsófia, Riba Pál, Ferdinandy Péter, Görbe Anikó, Varga Zoltán V. (2025). Implementing the flipped classroom model to enhance knowledge retention in pharmacology: a local case study at Semmelweis university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06913-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of Moodle platform activity dividing flipped students into preparation groups before each seminar; the study reports "no significant improvement among prepared students after FC #1 and FC #4".

> "we did not find a clear connection between pre-seminar preparation and test results in case of two topics as we observed no significant improvement among prepared students after FC #1 and FC #4 (Fig. 2C; Suppl Table 5)."

### Onódi Zsófia 2025 (2)

Onódi Zsófia, Riba Pál, Ferdinandy Péter, Görbe Anikó, Varga Zoltán V. (2025). Implementing the flipped classroom model to enhance knowledge retention in pharmacology: a local case study at Semmelweis university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06913-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparison of unprepared flipped students' 15-question test results against conventional groups; the article states "flipped classrooms were noninferior compared to conventional classrooms, even for students who did not prepare". The authors hypothesise student-student and student-teacher discussion may explain this.

> "we found that flipped classrooms were noninferior compared to conventional classrooms, even for students who did not prepare prior to seminars (Fig. 2D)."
