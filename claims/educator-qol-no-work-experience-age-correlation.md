### Abbasi Ebrahim 2026

Abbasi Ebrahim, Abbasifar Mahboubeh, Bazrafkan Lila. (2026). Relationship between midwifery educators’ quality of life and students’ clinical training quality. BMC Medical Education, 26, 1278. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09637-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman's non-parametric correlation test on data from 87 teachers tested the hypothesis linking educators' quality of life with work history. No significant correlation was found ("r = 0.179, p = 0.098"); equivalence was not tested. The age correlation was likewise non-significant (r = 0.128, p = 0.238).

> "Based on the coefficients of the correlation test, there is no significant correlation between the quality of life of trainers and their work experience. (r = 0.179, p = 0.098)"
