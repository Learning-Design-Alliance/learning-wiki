Evidence¶

Tuan 2010¶

Luu Trong Tuan. (2010). Enhancing EFL Learners’ Writing Skill via Journal Writing. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 3. www.ccsenet.org/elt. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081806

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative observations of journal entries and occasional interviews with experimental-group students; students also formed informal peer-editing groups and published entries on class and individual blogs.

"By the seventh week, a cooperative learning atmosphere pervaded the experimental group. Often overheard in the conversations at break time or after class were such utterances as “ Have you written your journal entry today?”"

Tuan 2010 (2)¶

Luu Trong Tuan. (2010). Enhancing EFL Learners’ Writing Skill via Journal Writing. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 3. www.ccsenet.org/elt. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081806

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of observational and interview data; one student wrote that the teacher corrected journal entries every week while the writing teacher had corrected only three essays during the semester.

"Not only was the cooperative learning atmosphere among the students created thanks to journal writing, but the student-teacher rapport was also strengthened through the researcher’s comments on and responses to their writings."
