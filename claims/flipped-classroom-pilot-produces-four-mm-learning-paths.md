## Evidence

### Vera Montalbano 2015

Vera Montalbano. (2015). Promoting Multimedia in Physics Teaching Through the Flipped Classroom in Pre-Service Education. University of Siena. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01328](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01328)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot study in the Physics Lab Didactics course of the TFA pre-service programme with four enrolled participants. In the results section the author reports that "Each teacher presented a different learning path", covering Tracker, Algodoo, Audacity and GeoGebra, with mandatory written worksheets. No effect size is reported.

> "Each teacher presented a different learning path: a study of a falling body using the software as tool for measuring and fitting from a video  (Letizia), a simulation  of different situation in gravity field for verifying a Galileo's  theorem  (Riccardo)"
