## Evidence

### Opitz et al. 2018

Moser Opitz, E., Grob, U., Wittich, C., Häsel-Weide, U., & Nührenbörger, M. (2018). Fostering the Computation Competence of Low Achievers Through Cooperative Learning in Inclusive Classrooms: A Longitudinal Study. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(1), 19-35. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1179942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive ICC analysis of computation competence in sampleCOUNT (126 students, 35 classes). The article attributes the low pretest ICC to "homogenization resulting from selecting low achievers from each class" and interprets the posttest ICC as "substantial amount of computation-related class specific differential development."

> "The ICC of the pretest scores was only 3 %, most certainly due to the homogenization resulting from selecting low achievers from each class. In contrast, ICC was 25 % in the posttest and 13 % in the follow-up."
