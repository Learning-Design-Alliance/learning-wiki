## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both groups improved over time on flanker reaction time and accuracy, but no group or group × time effects were found. [→ Wick Katharina 2018](#wick-katharina-2018)

## Evidence

### Wick Katharina 2018

Wick Katharina, Faude Oliver, Manes Susanne, Zahner Lukas, Donath Lars. (2018). I Can Stand Learning: A Controlled Pilot Intervention Study on the Effects of Increased Standing Time on Cognitive Function in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020356

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2 × 2 ANOVA on the Eriksen flanker task in the pilot. Time effects were significant for congruous reaction time (F(1; 34) = 4.41, p = 0.04) and congruous accuracy (F(1; 34) = 8.20, p = 0.01), but group p-values were 0.52–0.95 in Table 3.

> "For the group and group × time interactions no meaningful results could be found."
