## Evidence

### Goodrich 2019

Goodrich, J. M., Koziol, N. A., Yoon, H., & Leiva, S. (2019). Do Spanish-English Bilingual Children Outperform Monolingual English-Speaking Children on Executive Function Tasks in Early Childhood? A Propensity Score Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/edu

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive comparison of PreLAS English screening score distributions in the unmatched ECLS-K:2011 classified sample. Mean PreLAS was 19.75 (SD = .65) for monolingual and 18.20 (SD = 1.42) for bilingual children before matching, despite both groups meeting the same score-16 cutoff.

> "only 16.5% of monolingual children received a score between 16 and 19, whereas 83.5% received a score of 20. In contrast, 71.1% of bilingual children received a score between 16 and 19, with only 28.9% receiving a score of 20."
