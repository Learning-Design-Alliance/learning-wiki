### Phillips 1916

Phillips, F. M. (1916). Comparison of the work done in the successive minutes of a ten minute practice period in the fundamentals of arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072031

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A follow-up practice study with sixty fourth- and fifth-grade pupils given daily five-minute tests for three weeks. The article reports "the first minute accomplishment was often less than that of the later minutes" and that medians rose due to practice effect.

> "Five minute tests in addition and in multiplication were given daily for three weeks to sixty fourth grade and fifth grade pupils. During the latter part of the second week and the early part of the third week the first minute accomplishment was often less than that of the later minutes."
