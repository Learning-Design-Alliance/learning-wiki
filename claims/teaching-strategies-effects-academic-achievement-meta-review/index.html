Koçak 2021¶

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review of meta-analyses: 48 variables from 43 meta-analyses produced 82 effect sizes in the learning theories and teaching strategies category (Table 3). The review reports that "creative drama (ES= 1.453), constructivist learning (ES= 1.391), learning strategy instruction (ES= 1.250) and collaborative learning (ES= 1.230) have very large effects."

Related Claims¶

  • Across 169 meta-analyses, 254 variables with 427 effect sizes influence academic achievement; most variables have small or very small effects and none has a large, very large or huge negative effect — a broader claim this one bears on
  • SES and individual-characteristic variables relate to academic achievement, with grade retention (ES = 1.616) and college admissions test (ES = 1.416) showing very large effects and procrastination a small negative effect (ES = -.357) — related
  • Creative drama-based learning has a large positive effect on academic success in Turkish language instruction (REM ES = 1.712) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The drama approach has a strong positive effect on students' academic achievement in social studies courses compared with traditional teaching — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The creative drama method has a large positive effect on students' skill development — related
  • Creative drama method has a very large positive effect on Social Studies course achievement in favor of experimental groups (random-effects d = 1.171) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Creative drama has a large significant positive effect on academic achievement (overall weighted ES = 1.68 under a random effects model across 40 studies) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Thematic analysis of 14 studies of student views found creative drama produced influential outcomes across cognitive, creative/critical-thinking, learning-environment, and social-affective domains, along with negative aspects — related
