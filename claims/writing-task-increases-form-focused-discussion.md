### Dixon and Wu 2014

L. Quentin Dixon & Shuang Wu. (2014). Understanding Language Learning: Review of the Application of the Interaction Model in Foreign Language Contexts. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 11, 23-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137251

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Birjandi and Tabatabaei (2009), a study in which 40 Iranian English teaching majors completed three tasks, two oral and one combining group discussion with individual writing. The writing task drew "more discussion about linguistic forms"; no effect size is printed.

> "With the task requiring writing, the students had more discussion about linguistic forms in this task than the other two orally-oriented tasks."
