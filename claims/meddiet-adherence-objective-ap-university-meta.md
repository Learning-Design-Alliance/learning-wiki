## Evidence

### Peral-Martínez F. 2026

Peral-Martínez F., Bizzozero-Peroni B., Díaz-Goñi V., Olivo-Martins-de-Passos T., Peraile-Huerta A.M., Prieto-Ayuso A., Jiménez-López E., Martínez-Vizcaíno V., Mesas A.E. (2026). Adherence to the Mediterranean Diet and Academic Performance in University Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18172866

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis (DerSimonian-Laird) of 10 cross-sectional studies of objective AP (mean academic grade, GPA, or university entrance scores). The pooled estimate was "SMD = 0.49; 95% CI: 0.04 to 0.94"; heterogeneity was considerable (I2 = 98.2%; p < 0.001) and the prediction interval (−1.17 to 2.15) crossed the null.

> "Higher MedDiet adherence was significantly associated with better objective AP (SMD = 0.49; 95% CI: 0.04 to 0.94; p = 0.034), based on 10 studies including 10,217 university students"
