## Evidence

### Harb Gerlinde C. 2020

Harb Gerlinde C., Schultz Jon-Håkon. (2020). The nature of posttraumatic nightmares and school functioning in war-affected youth. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0242414. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242414

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory subsample analysis of the 40 girls who completed the CRIES-13, of whom 90% scored in the PTSD range. The study reports non-significant correlations of "r = -.06, p = .69" (teacher-rated) and "r = -.09, p = .56" (student-rated); no effect sizes beyond these correlations are printed for this null finding.

> "In an explorational analysis of the female-only subsample (N = 40), the severity of PTSD symptomatology was not related to either teacher-rated or student-rated functioning (r = -.06, p = .69; r = -.09, p = .56)."
