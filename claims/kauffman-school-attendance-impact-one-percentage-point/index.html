Matthew Johnson 2018¶

Matthew Johnson, Alicia Demers. (2018). Ewing Marion Kauffman School Year 6 Impacts. Oakland, CA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/ewing-marion-kauffman-school-year-6-impacts

Attendance outcome from the same matched-comparison evaluation of Kauffman students in grades 5-10 versus similar students in other Kansas City district and charter schools. The report prints the difference as "approximately 1 percentage point higher"; no test statistic or effect size is printed in the text provided.

"The Kauffman School had a positive impact on student attendance, such that the average attendance rate of Kauffman students was approximately 1 percentage point higher than that of comparison students."
