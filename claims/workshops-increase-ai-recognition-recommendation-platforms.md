## Evidence

### Christian Bergh 2026

Christian Bergh, Alexandra Vassar, Natasha Banks, Jessica Xu, and Jake Renzella. (2026). AI Literacy, Safety Awareness, and STEM Career Aspirations of Australian Secondary Students: Evaluating the Impact of Workshop Interventions. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29773-0_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29773-0_7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples Wald proportions tests comparing pre (N=205) and post (n=163) survey samples on whether each application uses AI; the study reports "significant increases were observed for Netflix (+27.1%, p < .001)" and the other listed platforms, with no effect size printed.

> "While recognition of overt AI tools like ChatGPT remained high (ceiling effect), significant increases were observed for Netflix (+27.1%, p < .001), TikTok (+12.8%, p < .001), Spotify (+16.2%, p < .001), and Face Unlock (+11.2%, p=.013)."
