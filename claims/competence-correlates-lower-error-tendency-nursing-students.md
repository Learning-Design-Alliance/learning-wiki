`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of CINS and METSN scores from a descriptive-correlational questionnaire study of 125 final-year nursing students. The article reports "r = .713, p < 0.01" and interprets it as competence increase accompanying decreased error tendency because METSN scoring is reversed.

> "A significant positive correlation was found between professional competence and medical error tendency (r = .713, p < 0.01)."
