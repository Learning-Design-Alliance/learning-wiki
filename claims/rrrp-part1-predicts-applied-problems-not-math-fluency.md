## Evidence

### Day 2017

Day, Stephanie L.; Connor, Carol M. (2017). Examining the relations between self-regulation and achievement in third grade students. Assessment for Effective Intervention, 42(2), 97-109. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508416670367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A separate two-level HLM model tested fall RRRP Parts 1 and 2 against spring WJ-III Math Fluency. The authors report that "fall RRRP scores on neither Parts 1 nor 2 predicted spring outcomes in Math Fluency"; no effect size is printed, so this is a non-detection, not a measured null effect.

> "For the Math Fluency model, fall RRRP scores on neither Parts 1 nor 2 predicted spring outcomes in Math Fluency."
