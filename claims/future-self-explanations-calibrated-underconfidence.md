### Elena Eleftheriou 2026 (2)

Elena Eleftheriou, George Pallis, and Marios Constantinides. 2026. Confidence Without Competence in AI-Assisted Knowledge Work. CHIWORK '26. [doi:10.1145/3808045.3808055](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808045.3808055)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Between-subjects experiment, Task 1 questionnaire and graded scores: Condition A showed an underconfidence effect, with "the lowest self-rating among all experimental conditions" yet high actual performance; the authors call this the most calibrated understanding. No effect size is printed for the calibration difference.

> "participants rated their own understanding at just𝑀= 4.32(on a 5-point scale) (Figure 9), which is the lowest self-rating among all experimental conditions, even though their actual test performance was quite high (𝑀= 2.36on a 3-point scale)"
