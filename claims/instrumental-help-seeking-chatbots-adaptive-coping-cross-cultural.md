## Evidence

### Huinan Hu 2026

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model on the whole sample (N = 2023) with conversation preference dummies as exogenous predictors, age and gender controlled; model fit CFI = 0.957, RMSEA = 0.048. The path from instrumental help-seeking to adaptive coping was "positively correlated with adaptive coping (β = 0.22, p < 0.001; β = 0.16, p < 0.001)".

> "Path analysis estimated that both self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking were positively correlated with adaptive coping (β = 0.22, p < 0.001; β = 0.16, p < 0.001)"

### Huinan Hu 2026 (2)

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped indirect effects (1000 bootstrap samples, 95% CIs) in the whole-sample SEM. The same table reports the indirect effect on prosocial behaviors as β = 0.09, p < 0.001; the maladaptive-coping mediator was not significant for either outcome.

> "adaptive coping significantly mediated the relationship between instrumental help-seeking and bullying (β = -0.02, p < 0.001)"

### Huinan Hu 2026 (3)

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group bootstrapped indirect effects (Table 3) show the instrumental help-seeking → adaptive coping → prosocial behaviors path is significant in the US sample (β = 0.08, p < 0.001) and HK sample (β = 0.09, p < 0.05), supporting cross-cultural stability.
