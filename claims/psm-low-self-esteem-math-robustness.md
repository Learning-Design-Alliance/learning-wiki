## Evidence

### Yu Wenjing 2022

Yu Wenjing, Qian Yiwei, Abbey Cody, Wang Huan, Rozelle Scott, Stoffel Lauren Ann, Dai Chenxu. (2022). The Role of Self-Esteem in the Academic Performance of Rural Students in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192013317

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score matching analysis with common support checking and balance tests (Figures 1 and 2), comparing low self-esteem students to matched peers. Kernel matching yielded "a decrease of 0.13 SD (p < 0.01)"; radius matching yielded −0.169 SD (p < 0.001). No standardized effect size beyond these matched coefficients is printed.

> "Using kernel matching, we find that low self-esteem decreased student academic achievement by 0.13 SD (p < 0.01) (Row 1). Similarly, when using nearest neighbor matching (Row 2), the coefficient was slightly larger, resulting in a decrease of 0.14 SD (p < 0.05)."
