### Math Corps Program Description 2024

Math Corps Program Description. (2024). https://www.evidenceforessa.org/program/math-corps

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

First randomized study of 489 students in grades 4-8 in urban, suburban, and rural Minnesota schools; 61% eligible for free or reduced-price lunch. The Math Corps group outperformed control with "effect size = +0.17" on STAR Math.

> "After one semester, students assigned to Math Corps demonstrated significantly higher scores relative to students assigned to the control group on the STAR Math assessment (effect size = +0.17)."
