## Evidence

### Li Juan 2025

Li Juan, Yin Kaiyu, Wang Yida, Jiang Xiaoqin, Chen Dongxu. (2025). Effectiveness of generative artificial intelligence-based teaching versus traditional teaching methods in medical education: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07750-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of skill scores from two RCTs (52 GAI-based and 51 traditional students). The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.63 (95% CI 0.10–1.16, p = 0.02, I² = 43%). The article flags the small study count as a limitation.

> "the meta-analysis, which involved only two studies (52 GAI-based students and 51 traditional teaching students), showed that the GAI-based teaching had significantly higher skill scores compared to the traditional teaching method (SMD 0.63, 95% CI 0.10 to 1.16; p = 0.02; I² = 43%)"
