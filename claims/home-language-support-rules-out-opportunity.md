# Multidisciplinary teams often provide home language support and delay referral to rule out lack of opportunity to learn before evaluating ML children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some teams wait until ML children have had more home language support before proceeding to a special education referral, using the response to help distinguish language need from disability. [→ Burr 2023](#burr-2023)

## Evidence

### Burr 2023

Burr, E., Burho, J., & Perez, A. (2023). Pre-Referral Processes in California State Preschool Programs: How Practitioners Decide to Refer Multilingual Children for Special Education Evaluation. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655866

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview study of CSPP coordinators. One coordinator described asking whether "the support in the home language helps with that process" and, if not, looking "more at the disability side of it."

> "Some interviewees explained that multidisciplinary teams often wait until ML children have had more support with home language development before proceeding to make a referral for special education evaluation."
