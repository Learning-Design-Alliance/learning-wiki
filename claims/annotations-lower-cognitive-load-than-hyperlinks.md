# Learners using annotations perceive lower cognitive load than learners using hyperlinks

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The annotation condition perceived significantly lower cognitive load than the hyperlink condition (η2 = 0.089). [→ Ruf 2023](#ruf-2023)

## Evidence

### Ruf 2023

Ruf, A., Zahn, C., Roos, A.-L., & Opwis, K. (2023). How do enhanced videos support generative learning and conceptual understanding in individuals and groups? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10275-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the one-item cognitive load scale (1-7) in the lab experiment; "learners in the annotation condition perceived a significant lower cognitive load (M = 3.75, SD = 1.2)". No effect for Setting, and adding the control group yielded no further significant results.

> "results yielded significance for Tool-use, F(1,92) = 9.000, p = 0.003, partial η2 = 0.089 (as expected in H1a), indicating that learners in the annotation condition perceived a significant lower cognitive load (M = 3.75, SD = 1.2) than learners in the hyperlink condition (M = 4.49, SD = 1.2)."
