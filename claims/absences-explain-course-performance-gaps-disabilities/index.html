Chapter 4 statistical modeling of the 2004 freshman cohort examining background characteristics, school type, absences, and study habits as explanations for course failure and GPA differences between students with and without disabilities.

"However, absences were the most important factor explaining these differences. Once we take into account the fact that students with disabilities miss many more days of school, their course failures and grades are similar to those of students without disabilities."
