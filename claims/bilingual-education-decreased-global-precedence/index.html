Evidence¶

I.K. Christoffels 2015¶

I.K. Christoffels, L. Steenbergen, W.P.M. van den Wildenberg, L.S. Colzato. (2015). Two is better than one: bilingual education promotes the flexible mind. Psychological Research, 79(3), 371-379. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-014-0575-3

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Same two-group comparison of Dutch high-school students on the global–local switching paradigm: the bilingually educated group showed "a decreased global precedence effect than the regular group". The abstract prints no effect size or test statistic for this contrast.

"As predicted, the bilingually educated group showed smaller switching costs (i.e., greater cognitive flexibility) and a decreased global precedence effect than the regular group."
