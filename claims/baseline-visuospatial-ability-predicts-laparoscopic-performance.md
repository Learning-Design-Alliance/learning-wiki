## Evidence

### Lepka Piotr 2026

Lepka Piotr, Szcześniak Dorota, Sikora Zofia, Matkowski Rafał. (2026). Effect of transcranial direct current stimulation on psychomotor skill acquisition during laparoscopic training: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12992-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear-model analysis in the randomized trial found the Vienna Test System 3D spatial test independently predicted day-7 CN and HE T-scores; the article prints p-values but no effect size for these predictors.

> "Visual–spatial ability measured by the 3D test at baseline was an independent predictor of final performance in both CN and HE (CN T4: p = 0.003; HE T4: p = 0.009) (Table 4)."

### Lepka Piotr 2026 (2)

Lepka Piotr, Szcześniak Dorota, Sikora Zofia, Matkowski Rafał. (2026). Effect of transcranial direct current stimulation on psychomotor skill acquisition during laparoscopic training: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12992-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations at baseline (T1) between the 3D visuospatial T-score and laparoscopic task scores were r = 0.397 for CN and r = 0.390 for HE, moderate-to-large positive associations.

> "Consistent with this, baseline correlations were positive between 3D scores and CN (r = 0.397) and between 3D scores and HE (r = 0.390) (Table 7)."
