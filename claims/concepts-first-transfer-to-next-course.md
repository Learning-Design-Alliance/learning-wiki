# Concepts-first organization transfers: AL&CF students earned higher z-grades in their next physics course (9B), an effect significant for the whole class but not for female or underrepresented minority subgroups

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2016

D. J. Webb. (2016). Learning Concepts First – A Course Structure with Improved Educational Outcomes in the Short, Medium, and Long Terms (Especially for Minority Groups Underrepresented in Physics). [doi:10.1119/1.4991371](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4991371)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Transfer analysis of grades in Physics 9B (waves, optics, thermodynamics), taught by other instructors with little conceptual overlap with 9A, comparing the same two sections with the same incoming controls. "the whole Class scoring better by 0 .22 ± 0.09" was significant, but the increases "for Females (0 .1 ± 0.2) and for UndRpMns (0 .14 ± 0.23)" were not statistically significant; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "all of the individual groups with large enough statistical power show AL&CF students performing better than ALOnly students with the whole Class scoring better by 0 .22 ± 0.09 which is a medium size eﬀect"
