Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). Ladders to Literacy for Kindergarten Students. WWC Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/259

q2 · i2 · review · r3

WWC synthesis of one quasi-experimental study (5 teachers/66 students in the analysis sample) reporting end-of-first-grade fluency. The report states it "found a positive, but not statistically significant effect"; the WWC-calculated effect size for fluency was 0.72 and the improvement index +26.

"O'Connor et al. (1996) found a positive, but not statistically significant effect of Ladders to Literacy on the fluency outcome (Test of Oral Reading Fluency)."
