## Evidence

### Rivière Etienne 2019

Rivière Etienne, Aubin Etienne, Tremblay Samuel-Lessard, Lortie Gilles, Chiniara Gilles. (2019). A new tool for assessing short debriefings after immersive simulation: validity of the SHORT scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1503-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Optimization (D) studies reported in the Results/Discussion, using the G-study variance components to project reliability for a single rater observing 2, 3, or 4 debriefings. Table 3 prints φ values of 0.77, 0.84, and 0.87 for D1–D3; the text states the D2 result is "compatible with high-stake exams".

> "the D1 (1 rater, 2 observations) study demonstrated coefficients with acceptable generalizability (φ ≥ 0.75), whereas the D2 study (1 rater, 3 observations) demonstrated a high reliability (φ ≥ 0.80) compatible with high-stake exams"
