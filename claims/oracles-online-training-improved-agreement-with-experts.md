### Nicola White 2020

Online training improves medical students' ability to recognise when a person is dying: The ORaClES randomised controlled trial. Palliative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/0269216319880767

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome analysis of the ORaClES online multicentre double-blind randomised controlled trial (per-protocol, NCT03360812; 135 analysed: 66 intervention, 69 control), regressing post-intervention MAD on intervention arm adjusted for baseline MAD. The mean of the MAD "in the intervention group was significantly less than in the control group" with the printed δMAD and confidence interval.

> "The mean of the MAD in the intervention group was significantly less than in the control group, post intervention (δMAD = −3.43, 95% CI −0.11 to −0.34, p = <0.001)."
