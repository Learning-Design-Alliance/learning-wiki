## Evidence

### Kelly J 2020

Kelly J, Oza SK, Feinn R, Cassese T. (2020). Implementation of a Hypothesis-Driven Physical Exam Session in a Transition to Clerkship Program. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11043

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Retrospective pre/post survey of 140 (73%) of 192 rising third-year students after a 3-hour HDPE workshop; ratings compared with a Wilcoxon signed rank test and Cohen's d for paired data. The article reports "p < .001, d = 0.52" and "d = 0.53".

> "Students were significantly more likely after than before the workshop to be confident in their ability to perform a problem-focused PE (p < .001, d = 0.52) and use a differential diagnosis to determine which PE maneuvers to complete (p < .001, d = 0.53; Table 1)."
