## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Perseverance of effort positively predicts schoolwork engagement (β = 0.65), while consistency of interests shows no significant relationship (β = 0.04). [→ Teuber Ziwen 2021](#teuber-ziwen-2021)

## Evidence

### Teuber Ziwen 2021

Teuber Ziwen, Nussbeck Fridtjof W., Wild Elke. (2021). The Bright Side of Grit in Burnout-Prevention: Exploring Grit in the Context of Demands-Resources Model among Chinese High School Students. Child Psychiatry and Human Development, 52(3), 464–476. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01031-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM main-effects results from the Chinese high school sample show perseverance of effort strongly related to engagement while consistency of interests is not. The authors note this matches prior findings that consistency of interests has weaker effects on engagement.

> "The relationship between engagement and perseverant grit was positive (β = 0.65, p < 0.001), whereas no relationship between engagement and consistency of interests was found (β = 0.04, p = 0.11)."
