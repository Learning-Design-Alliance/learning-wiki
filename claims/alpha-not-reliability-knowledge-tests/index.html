Evidence¶

Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025¶

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Reliability, But Not the Cronbach's Alpha, of Knowledge Tests Matters: Response to Zitzmann and Orona (2025). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10023-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in this commentary: for heterogeneous knowledge tests like the bMCU, item interrelatedness is influenced by how interrelated the construct facets are in learners' knowledge, so "it may be misleading to conclude that low item interrelatedness ... indicates high measurement error."

"Thus, it may be misleading to conclude that low item interrelatedness (i.e., low alpha) always and necessarily indicates high measurement error (i.e., low reliability)."
