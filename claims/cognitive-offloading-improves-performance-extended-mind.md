### Lebert 2024

Review-attributed evidence for the extended-mind thesis: the article reports that cognitive offloading research (studies of using physical elements to reduce task cognitive demand) shows performance improvements across perception, memory, mathematical thinking, and spatial reasoning. No effect sizes are printed.

### Lebert 2024 (2)

Lebert, A., & Vilarroya, Ó. (2024). The links between experiential learning and 4E cognition. Ann NY Acad Sci, 1541, 37–52. https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.15238

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports meta-level findings on metacognitive control of offloading: humans manage external resources by task difficulty and resource cost, increasing offloading with harder tasks and decreasing it when resources are costly, adjusting to context. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Humans seem to manage external resources according to the difficulty of the task and the cost of the resource, increase offloading with increased difficulty of the cognitive task, or decrease offloading if the external resource is costly."
