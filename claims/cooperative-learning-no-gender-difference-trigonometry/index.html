Comparative effects of concept mapping and cooperative learning strategies on senior secondary school students' achievement in mathematics-trigonometry in Kano State, Nigeria. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 4(1), 56-66. https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9453

Within the cooperative learning group (Table 6: 37 males, 33 females), females' mean gain of 5.20 exceeded males' 3.80, but Table 7 ANCOVA gives gender F = 0.20, p = 0.789 — "no statistical significant mean difference"; equivalence was not tested.

"the p‐value of 0.789 based on gender (males and females) in achievement in trigonometry from the use of cooperative learning strategy is strictly greater than α=0.05. This shows that there is no statistical significant mean difference in the achievement of the students in trigonometry based on gender"
