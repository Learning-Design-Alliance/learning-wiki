## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-González R 2023

Gutiérrez-González R, Zamarron A, Royuela A, Rodriguez-Boto G. (2023). Flipped classroom applied to Neurosurgery in undergraduate medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04158-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous Likert-scale survey (13 questions, 5-point scale, plus two free-response items) completed at the end of the last seminar by 53.6% of the 69 enrolled students. "The active learning activities based on clinical cases (problem-based learning) and the involvement of the teacher were positively emphasized."

> "Thus, the students rated the FC approach positively, considering it stimulating and useful for learning"

### Gutiérrez-González R 2023 (2)

Gutiérrez-González R, Zamarron A, Royuela A, Rodriguez-Boto G. (2023). Flipped classroom applied to Neurosurgery in undergraduate medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04158-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Free-response survey items coded by the authors: 37% identified video lessons as the main weakness, with "the time consumed for video-recorded lecture viewing (19%)" and the inability to rewind without rewatching (8%) mentioned as difficulties.

> "The main area for improvement was the video lessons, according to 37% of the students that answered the survey (Fig. 5)."
