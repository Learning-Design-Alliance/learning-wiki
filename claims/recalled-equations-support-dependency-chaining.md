### Gifford 2026

Gifford, K., Ehrlich, G. S., Bumbacher, E., & Kuo, E. (2026). “I forgot the formula:” How students can use coherence to reconstruct a (partially) forgotten equation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/yfxm-m4sw](https://doi.org/10.1103/yfxm-m4sw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focal case comparison from the interviews: Gabi chained "less distance→bigger electric field→more charge→larger capacitance" using V=Ed, concluding circuit D has twice the capacitance; Rene, attempting the same reconstruction, "was unable to conclude that these relationships ‘make sense.’" The article classifies Gabi's reasoning as mathematical sensemaking "precisely because they did not mathematically manipulate the equations."

> "Gabi’s recall of V=Ed, allowed them to make intermediate steps to- wards determining the relation between capacitance and distance."
