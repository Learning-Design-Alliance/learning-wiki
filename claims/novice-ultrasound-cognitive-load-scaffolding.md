### Park Hyangyu 2026

Park Hyangyu, Han Yejin, Cho Eunbyul. (2026). Toward a sustainable model for novice ultrasound training: mixed-methods analysis of student portfolios and objective outcomes in Korean Medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09583-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis in the discussion of the portfolio study found "novices experienced substantial cognitive load" combining psychomotor probe manipulation with 2D-to-3D visuospatial matching. The authors argue the instructor's role must extend beyond error correction to scaffolding that manages cognitive overload.

> "novices experienced substantial cognitive load while simultaneously performing psychomotor skills—specifically, fine probe manipulation—and the visuospatial task of matching 2D ultrasound images to actual 3D anatomical structures"
