## Evidence

### Al Hashmi Reem A. M. 2025

Al Hashmi Reem A. M., Ozturk Ilhan, Elmehdi Hussein M. (2025). Early detection of at-risk health sciences students: a machine learning-based predictive study using midterm grades. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08192-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive scatterplot analysis (Fig. 5) of Biology midterm scores versus high school average stratified by at-risk status in the 346-student dataset; a kernel density plot (Fig. 8) showed overlapping distributions peaking at 80–88% for at-risk versus 90–98% for not at-risk students.

> "In contrast, AT_RISK students (red) are more dispersed, with many scoring below 15/25 on the biology midterm, even among those with mid-to-high high school averages. This suggests that while prior GPA is informative, it is not sufficient for identifying risk without complementary early-term academic indicators"
