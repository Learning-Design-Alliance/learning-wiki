## Evidence

### Balideh Donya 2026

Balideh Donya, Farokhzadian Jamileh, Miri Sakineh, Shahraki Sedigheh Khodabandeh, Motamed‐Jahromi Mohadeseh. (2026). Effects of an Aesthetics‐Based Educational Intervention on Compassion Competence and Aesthetic Care Quality Among Psychiatric Nurses: A Quasi‐Experimental Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70854

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparisons on the 20-item, five-subscale Aesthetics of Nursing Care Scale in the 90-nurse quasi-experiment. Table 3 reports between-group Cohen's d values of 2.00–3.31 across the five dimensions, with the article stating effect sizes were "very large across all dimensions, indicating a substantial educational effect".

> "Following the intervention, nurses in the intervention group demonstrated significantly higher scores than those in the control group across all dimensions of aesthetic care, including compassionate commitment and competence, stress‐free care, humanistic attention to patients, patient satisfaction and comfort, and admirable commitment and competence (all p < 0.001)."
