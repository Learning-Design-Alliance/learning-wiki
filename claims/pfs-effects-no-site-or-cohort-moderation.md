### Feil 2014

Feil, E. G., Frey, A., Walker, H. M., Small, J. W., Seeley, J. R., Golly, A., & Forness, S. R. (2014). The Efficacy of a Home-School Intervention for Preschoolers With Challenging Behaviors: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Preschool First Step to Success. Journal of Early Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1053815114566090

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interaction terms for intervention condition × site and condition × cohort were tested and removed when nonsignificant. Slope-equivalence tests for baseline covariate interactions were also nonsignificant, supporting a uniform main-effect model across sites and cohorts.

> "Preliminary models examined whether site or cohort moderated program effects were nonsignificant. For all models the slopes of the regression lines were equivalent for both experimental and control conditions."
