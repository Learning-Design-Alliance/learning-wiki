## Evidence

### T. J. Atherton 2026

T. J. Atherton, I. Descamps, M. Webel, T. R. Holmes, C. L. Vizcarra, N. Sui, J. J. Foley IV. (2026). Faculty Orientations Shape Adoption of AI in Research and Teaching. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18140

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis (minimum residual estimation, oblique rotation) of 36 preprocessed variables from the faculty survey (n=90). The article reports a "unidimensional 9-item scale with strong loadings (0.45–0.94) explaining 55% of the variance" and "Cronbach'sα = 0.88".

> "The final EFA yielded a unidimensional 9-item scale with strong loadings (0.45–0.94) explaining 55% of the variance. The factor showed strong internal consistency (Cronbach'sα = 0.88, 95% CI[0.84,0.92]; McDonald'sω= 0.89)"
