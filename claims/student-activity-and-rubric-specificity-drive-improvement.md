Carlos Saiz, Silvia F. Rivas and Sonia Olivares. (2015). Collaborative learning supported by rubrics improves critical thinking. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v15i1.12905

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the quasi-experimental comparison between program versions; the design varied the two instructional factors together, so their individual contributions were not separately tested. The article itself frames this as speculation to be examined in the Discussion.

> "We observed that the only change in instruction, with version 2 of the program, was greater activity and specificity; all the rest remained equal. Accordingly it would be reasonable to speculate that these variables would be responsible for the results"
