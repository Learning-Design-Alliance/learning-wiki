Maike Schindler, Stephan Hußmann, Per Nilsson, Arthur Bakker. (2017). Sixth-grade students' reasoning on the order relation of integers as influenced by prior experience: an inferentialist analysis. Math Ed Res J 29:471–492. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13394-017-0202-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of a preliminary clinical interview with one male sixth-grade student (Tom) comparing pairs of integers. The analysis reports he drew on thermometer experiences, arguing numbers below zero are below the freezing point and smaller than 'normal' numbers.

> "Tom's reasons in judging the sizes of a negative and a positive integer seem to a great extent to be based on the role of zero, which serves as a significant concept in his inference."
