## Evidence

### J. H. Madden 2019

J. H. Madden, A. S. Won, J. P. Schuldt, B. Kim, S. Pandita, Y. Sun, T. J. Stone, and N. G. Holmes. (2019). Virtual Reality as a Teaching Tool for Moon Phases and Beyond. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.madden](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.madden)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post analysis in the randomized three-condition study: pre-test averaged around 36% in all conditions with no significant differences, and all conditions showed statistically significant increases (p<0.001). Only a p-value is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "The average score on the pre-test was around 36% in all three conditions, with no signiﬁcant differences between conditions (Fig.1). There were statistically signiﬁcant increases in performance in all three conditions between pre- and post-test ( p<0.001)."
