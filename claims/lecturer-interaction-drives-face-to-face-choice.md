## Evidence

### Keis Oliver 2017

Keis Oliver, Grab Claudia, Schneider Achim, Öchsner Wolfgang. (2017). Online or face-to-face instruction? A qualitative study on the electrocardiogram course at the University of Ulm to examine why students choose a particular format. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1053-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the guided interviews with ten medical students; interaction was seen as allowing "a more comprehensive understanding than the online course". No effect size reported.

> "three participants in the face-to-face course rated the opportunity for direct interaction with the lecturer in the form of questions and discussions as a key decision-making criterion for their choice of that format."
