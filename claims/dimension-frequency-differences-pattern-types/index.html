Frequencies of five-dimensional codes compared across 24 CPS activities (n = 5, 14, 5 for Types 1–3); CM means were 160.80 (Type 1), 97.36 (Type 2), 42.20 (Type 3), with significant ANOVA results on Int-B, KS, KM, TU, RM, CM, and OB. "there were statistically significant differences between the three types along the behavioural dimension".

"there were significant differences in the frequency of Int-B, KS, KM, TU, RM, CM, and OB (p < .05) with a Bonferroni correction using the Kruskal-Wallis test. Specifically, there were statistically significant differences between the three types along the behavioural dimension (RM, CM, OB)"
