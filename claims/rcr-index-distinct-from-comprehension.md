## Evidence

### Lefèvre 2024

Lefèvre, E., Law, J. M., Prado, J., Anders, R., & Cavalli, E. (2024). Reading Comprehension Resiliency in Adolescents with and without Dyslexia relates to Vocabulary, Listening Comprehension and Socioeconomic Status. https://osf.io/fzyeh/?view_only=84fc51248a0d48ba9a505835e525efdc

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A single-case analysis among the 95 participants identified 16 good comprehenders (8 with dyslexia, 8 typical readers); their RCR index values ranged from above +1 SD to negative values, showing "good comprehenders do not necessarily exhibit a highly positive RCR".

> "Within this sub-sample of 16 good comprehenders, the RCR index differentiated them: 7 participants had an RCR index superior to +1 SD above the mean, 5 had an RCR ranging from +0.95 SD to +0.34 SD and 4 had an RCR index ranging from -0.04 SD and -0.27 SD"
