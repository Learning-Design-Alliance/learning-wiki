The brief reports this from a 2022 UC Berkeley study (Vincent et al.) and, separately, a 2020 PPIC analysis (Gao & Lafortune) finding nearly 40 percent of California students attend schools that do not meet minimum facility standards. It adds that facility conditions and spending show racial, ethnic, socioeconomic, and geographic disparities nationwide.
