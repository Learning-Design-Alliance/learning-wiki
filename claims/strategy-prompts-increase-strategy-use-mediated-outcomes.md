# Strategy prompts strongly increase learners' use of the prompted strategies, and the effect on comprehension is mediated by strategy use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experiments varying prompt provision showed strategy prompts strongly increased use of the prompted strategies in learning journals, and the increased use was accompanied by enhanced learning outcomes. [→ Nückles 2020](#nuckles-2020)
`q3 i?` Several studies found the effect on comprehension (and sometimes retention) was mediated by the strategy use. [→ Nückles 2020 (2)](#nuckles-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Nückles 2020

Nückles, M., Roelle, J., Glogger-Frey, I., Waldeyer, J., & Renkl, A. (2020). The Self-Regulation-View in Writing-to-Learn: Using Journal Writing to Optimize Cognitive Load in Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09541-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Multiple experiments in the research program (e.g., Berthold et al. 2007; Glogger et al. 2009; Nückles et al. 2009; Schwonke et al. 2006) varied prompt provision and found prompts "strongly increased learners’use of the prompted strategies in the learning journals", e.g., an elaboration prompt increases elaboration.

> "Experiments that varied the provision of prompts clearly showed that strategy prompts strongly increased learners’use of the prompted strategies in the learning journals"

### Nückles 2020 (2)

Nückles, M., Roelle, J., Glogger-Frey, I., Waldeyer, J., & Renkl, A. (2020). The Self-Regulation-View in Writing-to-Learn: Using Journal Writing to Optimize Cognitive Load in Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09541-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mediation analyses across several studies: in Berthold et al. (2007) cognitive strategy use mediated learning outcomes; in Roelle et al. (2017) "the quality of organization strategies" mediated conceptual knowledge outcomes "in two experiments".

> "Roelle et al. ( 2017) found the quality of organization strategies to mediate learning outcomes in a conceptual knowledge test in two experiments"
