## Evidence

### Bordbar Shima 2024

Bordbar Shima, Ahmadinejad Parvin, Bahmaei Jamshid, Yusefi Ali Reza. (2024). The impact of mindfulness on academic achievement of students with the mediating role of adaptability: a structural equation modeling approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06192-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sobel test of mediation in the PLS SEM of 290 students; the indirect effect of mindfulness on achievement via adaptation was "calculated to be 0.049" in standard error, with a significant Sobel statistic "(P < 0.001, T = 7.551)".

> "Thus, the Sobel test statistic was found to be 7.551. Given the 1% error level and the T-value of the Sobel test is greater than 1.96, the mediator variable adaptation is significant in the relationship between mindfulness and students’ academic achievement (P < 0.001, T = 7.551)."
