Descriptive finding from the same faculty interview study (21 participants across sub-disciplines). The article observes that "faculty did not emphasize goals centered around specific physics content or lab techniques," and instead reports that "faculty made it clear that the primary attribute of a good experiment is that students use a deep knowledge of physics."

> "faculty made it clear that the primary attribute of a good experiment is that students use a deep knowledge of physics and develop expert-like habits of mind while carrying out experiments and using measurement tools."
