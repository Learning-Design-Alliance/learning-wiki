# High-school graduation does not explain the link between school belonging and NEET status

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` School belonging remains a significant predictor of NEET after controlling for high-school graduation (OR = 0.82), so graduation is not a practically meaningful mechanism. [→ Philip Parker 2021](#philip-parker-2021)

## Evidence

### Philip Parker 2021

Philip Parker, Kelly-Ann Allen, Rhiannon Parker, Jiesi Guo, Herbert W Marsh, Geetanjali Basarkod, and Theresa Dicke. (2021). School Belonging Predicts whether an Emerging Adult will be Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) after School. https://www.pwc.com.au/government/government-matters/youth-unemployment.html

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Final multilevel logistic model adding high-school graduation as a covariate (Table 4): belonging OR = 0.82 [0.79, 0.85]; the coefficient "barely changed", so indirect effects via graduation were not estimated.

> "While high-school graduation was indeed a strong predictor of NEET status, school belonging was still a significant predictor. Indeed, it was not even a notably weaker predictor in this model."
