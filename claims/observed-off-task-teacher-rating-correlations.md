## Evidence

### Kurki 2013

Kurki, A., Wang, W., Li, Y., & Poduska, J. (2013). Measurement of Child Behavior via Classroom Observations in the Good Behavior Game Professional Development Models Randomized Control Trial. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563300

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`
