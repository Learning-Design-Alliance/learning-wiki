# Didactic instruction is negatively associated with one-year achievement gains in reading and mathematics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Chicago elementary schools, students learned less in reading and mathematics when teachers used didactic instruction frequently. [→ Julia B. Smith 2001](#julia-b-smith-2001)

## Evidence

### Julia B. Smith 2001

Julia B. Smith, Valerie E. Lee, and Fred M. Newmann. (2001). Instruction and Achievement in Chicago Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/instruction-and-achievement-chicago-elementary-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM analysis of the same Chicago sample controlling for student, classroom, and school characteristics. Students in high-didactic schools learned "3.9 percent less in math and 3.4 percent less in read- ing than the city average."
