Evidence¶

Hernández 2026¶

Hernández, L., & Germain, E. (2026). Community schools in rural California: Leveraging shared resources in West Kern County. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/643.620

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

WestEd school climate survey results at Elk Hills, Lost Hills Union, and Semitropic, reported in the Additional Outcomes section. The mentorship program consists of monthly cross-grade groups of students gathering with an adult in the building.

"Between 87% and 94% of students enjoyed meeting with their mentor across the campuses, while 74% to 89% reported being supported by and receiving help from their mentor"

Hernández 2026 (2)¶

Hernández, L., & Germain, E. (2026). Community schools in rural California: Leveraging shared resources in West Kern County. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/643.620

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

WestEd-reported student survey perceptions of the social worker added to each campus, from the same Additional Outcomes section. The report also notes students reported feeling connected and supported by a trusting adult.

"Between 70% and 93% of students across campuses agreed that the social worker provides supports to students and reported feeling really comfortable "opening up to social workers" and using them as a resource when they were having a tough day emotionally"
