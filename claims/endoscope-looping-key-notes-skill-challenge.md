## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational interpretation from assisting during the simulations, explaining why the first navigation task showed no group difference while later tasks did: without luminal support the shaft forms loops, and the operator can rarely see that a loop has formed.

> "The assisting investigator noted that novices found navigation with looped shafts very challenging; as was clearly reflected by their increased operative time and endoscope path length compared to the experts."
