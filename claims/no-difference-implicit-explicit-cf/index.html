Asassfeh 2013¶

Sahail M. Asassfeh. (2013). Corrective Feedback (CF) and English-Major EFL Learners' Ability in Grammatical Error Detection and Correction. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p85

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-sample t-tests compared the two intact course sections (EIG vs EICG) after the 12-week treatment in the 139-participant quasi-experiment. No significant between-group difference on either measure; equivalence was established at pretest (detection t(135)=-1.11, p=.27; correction t(135)=-.89, p=.36).
