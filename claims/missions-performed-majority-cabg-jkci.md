Retrospective review of all 290 patients who underwent coronary revascularization at JKCI from May 2016 to December 2023, with data retrieved from patient files and theatre records. The study reports the operational split: "The international missions performed a total of 159 (54.8%) operations, while the local team operated a total of 131 (45.2%) patients."
