## Evidence

### James K. Hirons 2025

James K. Hirons, Jonathan D. Perry, Dawson T. Nodurft, Scott Crawford, William Bassichis, and Tatiana L. Erukhimova. (2025). Click, Watch, Learn: The Impact of Student Self-Study Materials on Physics E&M Course Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the log-transform regression (N = 1402), echoing the first model; the authors note this contrasts with prior studies that found positive correlations for video engagement, possibly because those videos were required pre-class components rather than optional supplements.

> "while the supplemental review and recitation-style problem-solving videos showed no significance, similar to what was observed in the first model."
