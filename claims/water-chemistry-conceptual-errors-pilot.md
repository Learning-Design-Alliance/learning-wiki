## Evidence

### Taş 2015

Taş, E., Gülen, S., Öner, Z., & Özyürek, C. (2015). The effects of classic and web-designed conceptual change texts on the subject of water chemistry. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(2), 263-280. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057892

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot three-stage test with 103 9th graders who had previously studied water chemistry. The most frequent error, "Hard water is harmful for human health", was held by 64 students (62.1%); other errors ranged lower (e.g., 24.2% for residues when warming dirty water).
