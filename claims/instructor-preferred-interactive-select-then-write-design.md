### Ruiwei Xiao 2025

Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, Runlong Ye, Majeed Kazemitabaar, Nicholas Diana, Michael Liut and John Stamper. (2025). Improving Student-AI Interaction Through Pedagogical Prompting: An Example in Computer Science Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey ratings from the 36-instructor formative study of preferred instructional delivery methods; the article reports these means on a 5-point scale and derives a select-then-write interaction flow from the top-2 choices.

> "While there are nuanced differences between a systemscaffolding prompt selection (M=3.30 out of 5) versus a system scaffolding prompt writing (M=3.24), both are rated higher than more passive instructional methods such as demo videos (M=2.69), spreadsheets (M=2.18), practice questions (M=2.61), or other methods (M=1.86)."
