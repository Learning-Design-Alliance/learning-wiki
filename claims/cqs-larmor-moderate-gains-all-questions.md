## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2020

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Development, validation and in-class evaluation of a sequence of clicker questions on Larmor precession of spin in quantum mechanics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In-class evaluation comparing pretest (after traditional lecture-based instruction) and posttest after the CQS with peer instruction in two upper-level QM classes (N=17 and N=39, N=56 combined). The article reports "all effect sizes above 0.3, and some even nearing 0.7" and normalized gains exceeding 0.3, most in the 0.6-0.9 range; per-question Cohen's d values are printed in Tables 1-3.

> "All three tables show moderate effect sizes from pre to posttest on each of the questions, with all effect sizes above 0.3, and some even nearing 0.7."
