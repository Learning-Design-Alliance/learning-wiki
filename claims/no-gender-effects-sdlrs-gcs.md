# No gender differences or year-by-gender interaction effects in SDLRS or GCS scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mixed-model ANOVAs found no effect of gender and no year-by-gender interaction for either SDLRS or GCS. [→ Kim S 2020](#kim-s-2020)

## Evidence

### Kim S 2020

Kim S, Yang Eunbae B. (2020). Does group cohesion foster self-directed learning for medical students? A longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1962-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-model ANOVA with gender as a between-subject factor and year as a within-subject factor in the 94-student cohort. The article reports "no effect of gender" and no interaction; a parallel GCS analysis likewise found no significant gender effect (p > .05).

> "a 3 (year: 1st vs. 2nd vs. 3rd) x 2 (gender: male vs. female) mixed-model ANOVA revealed that there was a significant main effect of year, F (2,180) = 3.387, MSe = .081, p < .05, but no effect of gender, F (1, 90) = .002, MSe = .324, p > .05, nor was there any interaction effect between year and gender, F (2, 180) = .307, MSe = .081, p > .05."
