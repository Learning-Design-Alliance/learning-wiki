## Evidence

### Plata Christopher 2021

Plata Christopher, Nellessen Martin, Roth Rebecca, Ecker Hannes, Böttiger Bernd W., Löser Johannes, Wetsch Wolfgang A. (2021). Impact of video quality when evaluating video-assisted cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a randomized, controlled simulation trial. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-021-00486-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis within the randomized double-blind simulation trial of 650 video-sequence ratings. The paper reports recognition of increased compression depth at "13.8% ... at low quality, 50% at medium quality and 42.9% at high quality", p = 0.006. No effect size is printed.

> "In contrast, 13.8% of increased chest compressions at low quality, 50% at medium quality and 42.9% at high quality level were classified correctly, revealing a significant difference in different video quality stages (p = 0.006)."
