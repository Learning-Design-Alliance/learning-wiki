## Evidence

### Celik 2016

Celik, S.S. & Aydin, S. (2016). Wiki effect on English as a foreign language writing achievement. Global Journal of Foreign Language Teaching, 6(4), 218-227. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED573959

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples comparison of post-test scores for 42 pre-service English teachers assigned to wiki-based or pen-and-paper conditions. The content post-test mean was 19.64 for the experimental group versus 17.09 for the control group, with a significant difference (p = .00).

> "The values in Table 5 showed that mean score for content significantly differed for content, while no significant correlations were found for organization, discourse markers, vocabulary, sentence construction, mechanics and total scores."
