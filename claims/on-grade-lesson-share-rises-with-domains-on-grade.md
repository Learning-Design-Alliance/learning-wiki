`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis (Table 2) of students' fall placements across the four math domains: 52.6 percent of students placed below grade level in all four domains, with the on-or-above lesson share rising from 0.9 percent (0 domains) to 99.8 percent (4 domains).

> "Only 1 percent of lessons completed by students placing below grade level are at grade level. That proportion increases as students place above grade level in more domains, reaching virtually 100 percent when placed on or above grade level in all domains."
