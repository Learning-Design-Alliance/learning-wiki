## Evidence

### Kim Seo-Yoon 2020

Kim Seo-Yoon, Shin Dongmin, Kim Hyun Jeong, Karm Myong-Hwan. (2020). Changes of knowledge and practical skills before and after retraining for basic life support: Focused on students of Dental School. International Journal of Medical Sciences 17(18): 3082-3090. https://doi.org/10.7150/ijms.47343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 98 third-year dental students retrained 2 years after initial AHA BLS education. The knowledge questionnaire total rose from "55.6 ± 16.7 to 81.2 ± 15.9 points" (p < 0.001); all 7 items improved significantly (p < 0.01).

> "The total score of BLS knowledge increased from 55.6 ± 16.7 to 81.2 ± 15.9 points when converted into a 100-point scale before and after retraining and was statistically significant (p < 0.001)"
