### Liu Ya Jie 2026

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of comprehensive flipped classroom assessment components (full score 100 points) between the AI-enhanced and standard flipped classroom groups using the Mann-Whitney U test. The article reports the pre-class preparation and presentation quality score was "significantly higher than that for the flipped classroom group (p < 0.05)", with no significant differences in the other two components.

> "In terms of pre-class preparation and presentation quality, the score for the AI-enhanced flipped classroom group was also significantly higher than that for the flipped classroom group (p < 0.05) (Fig. 2C). However, no significant differences were found between the two groups regarding in-class presentation performance (Fig. 2D) or mastery of professional knowledge and core content (Fig. 2E)."
