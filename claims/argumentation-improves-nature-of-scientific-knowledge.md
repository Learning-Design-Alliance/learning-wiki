### Balci 2016

Balci, C. & Yenice, N. (2016). Effects of the scientific argumentation based learning process on teaching the unit of cell division and inheritance to eighth grade students. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 2(1), 67-84. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105345

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the Nature of Scientific Knowledge Scale (Rubba & Anderson, 1978; 48 items, six dimensions) controlling for pretest scores showed significant differences favoring the experiment group on five dimensions; the combination dimension difference (F (1,74)= .017, p> .05) was not significant.

> "Table 4 indicates that when the pre -test scores of the groups are controlled there appears a statistically significant difference between the corrected mean post -test scores of both groups for five dimensions of the scale: ethics (F (1,74)= 6.407, p< .05), creativity (F (1,74)= 6.188, p< .05), development (F (1,74)= 7.933, p<  .05), simplicity (F (1,74)= 10.190, p< .05) and testability (F (1,74)= 9.128, p< .05)."
