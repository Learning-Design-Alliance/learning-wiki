# Training in cancellation of 3 and 5 produced neither transfer nor interference on cancellation of groups containing 4 and 7

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average gains of trained (15.0 seconds) and control (15.9 seconds) groups agreed, showing neither facilitation nor interference. [→ Poffenberger 1915](#poffenberger-1915)

## Evidence

### Poffenberger 1915

Poffenberger, A. T., Jr. (1915). The influence of improvement in one simple mental process upon other related processes. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(8), 459-474. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075269

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled training experiment: after 100 trials canceling 3's and 5's separately, groups were tested on canceling groups containing 4 and 7, a task with no identical elements and no need to break trained bonds. Gains agreed at 15.0 versus 15.9 seconds.

> "If the average gains in the trained and control group are compared, the two will be found to agree, 15.0 and 15.9 respectively. The probable errors of these averages show that they are fairly reliable."
