## Evidence

### Visaya 2018

Visaya, M. V. and Limbada, T. (2018). Gender Performance in Physics Practicals. Department of Pure and Applied Mathematics, University of Johannesburg. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1802.03929](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1802.03929)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Visual heat-map analysis of male and female orbits (Figure 3), with the gender variable dropped so variable 0 is OVM, shows density of visits to each state; the article states "females tend to get more distinction in OVM" and males show a lack of visits at MECH=1 and OVM=1.

> "It is clear that females tend to get more distinction in OVM as seen in the density of visits in OVM=1.  On the other hand, males tend not to get distinction in both Mechanics and their overall mark, as seen in the lack of visit in MECH=1 and OVM=1."
