## Evidence

### Zhan Leilei 2026

Zhan Leilei, Liang Ruiyin, Li Yimin, Huang Nuanqin. (2026). Research on the application of scenario-based simulation ward rounds combined with video feedback method in standardized residency training of gastroenterology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09434-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial of 80 residents, CTDI-CV (score range 70–420) was administered before the end of rotation. The intervention group's total score was significantly higher, with "a statistically significant difference (P < 0.001)".

> "the post-training critical thinking ability score of the intervention group was (307.20 ± 14.25) points, which was significantly higher than that of the control group (273.03 ± 13.20), with a statistically significant difference (P < 0.001)"

### Zhan Leilei 2026 (2)

Zhan Leilei, Liang Ruiyin, Li Yimin, Huang Nuanqin. (2026). Research on the application of scenario-based simulation ward rounds combined with video feedback method in standardized residency training of gastroenterology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09434-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dimension-level analysis of the CTDI-CV in the randomized trial showed significant between-group differences in six dimensions, while "no statistically significant differences was found in truth-seeking" (P = 0.365).

> "Significant differences were observed in six dimensions: analytical ability, self-confidence, open-mindedness, inquisitiveness, systematic ability, and cognitive maturity. However, no statistically significant differences was found in truth-seeking"
