Descriptive synthesis of health issues addressed by the included interventions, tabulated by decade of publication (Table 1). The review reports the shift in prevailing topics across roughly 40 years of publications.

> "Until 2000, the prevailing topics in health education were substance use and SRH, in the following decades also nutrition, issues connected with public health, physical activity, as well as somatic and mental health gained interested of teachers and stakeholders in the field."
