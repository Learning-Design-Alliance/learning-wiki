Evidence¶

Linn Damsgaard 2022¶

Linn Damsgaard, Anne-Mette Veber Nielsen, Anne Kær Gejl, Anne Sofie Bøgh Malling, Søren Kildahl Jensen, Jacob Wienecke. (2022). Effects of 8 Weeks with Embodied Learning on 5–6-Year-Old Danish Children's Pre-reading Skills and Word Reading Skills: the PLAYMORE Project, DK. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1709–1737. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09671-8

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Between-group mixed-model contrasts from the same RCT on the conditional letter-sound subscore (max 8). Both intervention groups beat CON post-intervention with "p < 0.01, d = 0.6, p < 0.01, d = 0.5"; HM beat CON at retention (p < 0.05, d = 0.5).

"both the hand motor movement group (HM) and the whole-body movement group (WM) improved significantly better at naming conditional letter-sounds compared to the control group (CON) from T1 to T2 (WMT1–T2 = 1050% and HMT1–T2 = 700% versus CONT1–T2 = 500%, p < 0.01, d = 0.6, p < 0.01, d = 0.5) and also from T1 to T3 for the HM group compared to CON"
