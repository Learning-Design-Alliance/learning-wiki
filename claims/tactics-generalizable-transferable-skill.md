### Sjöblom 2025

Sjöblom, B., & Lymer, G. (2025). Conceptual socialization in debriefing: tactics as an object of knowledge in wargame interactions. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09717-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of a joint end-of-exercise debriefing where teachers explained the fictional geography by saying "it's to train the tactics and not so much the actual terrain" and that all scenarios present the same problem, producing an account of tactics as generalizable and transferable.

> "the concept of tactics is invoked as an integral part of an argument that seeks to downplay an overwrought concern with realism, in the interest of fostering an orientation to transferable knowledge."
