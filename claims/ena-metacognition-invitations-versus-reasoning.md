### Zhanxin Hao 2025

Zhanxin Hao, Xiaobo Liu, Jiaxin Fan, Yun Long, Jifan Yu, Wenli Chen, Yu Zhang. (2025). Unpacking Interaction Profiles and Strategies in Human-AI Collaborative Problem Solving: A Cognitive Distribution and Regulation Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21288

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Epistemic network analysis of co-occurrence among coded behaviors (model fit Pearson correlation above 0.96), with pairwise Welch t-tests on the MR1 dimension showing significant separation among all three clusters (e.g., C1 vs C2 d = 1.91). Network subtractions showed "metacognitive behaviors in DR were more likely to co-occur with invitation-related actions."

> "metacognitive behaviors in DR were more likely to co-occur with invitation-related actions (e.g., reasoning invitation, elaboration invitation), whereas in CI, they were more frequently linked to human-initiated reasoning and elaboration."
