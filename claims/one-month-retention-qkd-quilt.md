Liam Doyle and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Improving student understanding via interactive learning tutorial on quantum key distribution using entanglement. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00552-5](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00552-5)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention analysis of 22 students from the second session of Class 4-QM who retook the identical post-test on a cumulative final exam about one month later. Table VII shows Q1-Q3 roughly stable (e.g., Q3 85% to 83%) and Q4 rising from 72% to 90%.

> "Interestingly, for Q4 student performance improved. This may be due to the fact that students received feedback on their initial post-test and learned from it."
