# ATI-UP increases average daily attendance in elementary schools after one semester (effect size +0.19)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Berg 2018

Berg. (2018). ATI-UP (Attendance Intervention-Universal Plus). https://www.evidenceforessa.org

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A dissertation study assigned 27 elementary schools in 15 Oregon districts to adopt ATI-UP or serve as a wait-list control group. The study reports "increased average daily attendance (effect size =+0.19)" compared to controls after one semester, qualifying the program for the Promising category.

> "After one semester, ATI-UP schools increased average daily attendance (effect size =+0.19) and decreased chronic absenteeism (effect size =+0.08), compared to controls."
