# Improvement teams with representation from teachers, principals, and district leadership gained broader buy-in than teams missing any level

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teams lacking district leadership, principals, or teachers struggled to gain support at the corresponding level. [→ Bugler 2021](#bugler-2021)

## Evidence

### Bugler 2021

Bugler, D. (2021). School District Collaboration: Lessons From Districts Participating in California Education Partners' California Language & Learning Initiative (CALLI). WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616075

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews with districts that continued into CALLI 201 found that rethought team composition mattered: teams that had lacked district-level leadership, principals, or teachers each struggled for buy-in at that level, per the article's interview findings. No effect size reported.

> "teams that had not included district-level leadership had struggled to gain broad support in the district. Similarly, teams without principals had struggled to gain support in schools, and those without teachers had struggled to get teacher buy-in."
