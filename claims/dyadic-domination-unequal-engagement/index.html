Akpinar 2014¶

Akpinar, Y. (2014). Different modes of digital learning object use in school settings: Do we design for individual or collaborative learning? International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 10(3), 87-95. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1747

Researcher observation during lab sessions in the quasi-experimental study; the article reports some pairs accepted the first idea produced without discussing both students' ideas, and researchers often had to notify pairs to elaborate mutually. The authors state the results agree with Ding (2009) and Edwards et al.
