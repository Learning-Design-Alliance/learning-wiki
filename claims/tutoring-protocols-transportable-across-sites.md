## Evidence

### Fuchs 2009

Fuchs, L. S., Powell, S. R., Seethaler, P. M., Cirino, P. T., Fletcher, J. M., Fuchs, D., Hamlett, C. L., & Zumeta, R. O. (2009). Remediating Number Combination and Word Problem Deficits Among Students With Mathematics Difficulties: A Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 101(3), 561-576. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014701

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity check based on audiotaped sessions sampled at 16.8%, scored by four research assistants using checklists of essential lesson points. The article reports "the mean percentage of points addressed was 98.1 (SD = 2.06) for NC tutoring and 98.4 (SD = 2.79) for WP tutoring" in Nashville, with Houston means of 99.5 and 99.2.

> "In Nashville, the site where the protocols had been developed, the mean percentage of points addressed was 98.1 (SD = 2.06) for NC tutoring and 98.4 (SD = 2.79) for WP tutoring."
