The article reports, citing Ripin (1996) and Miller & Stassun (2014), that standardized tests are "better predictors of gender and color than professional success." The article states these tests allow fast comparisons but keep women and minorities out of top schools.

> "The standardized tests, introduced since the 1940s as admis-sion requirements, were found to be better predictors of gen-der and color than professional success (Ripin 1996; Miller & Stassun 2014). These tests play a major role in increasing the gender gap by keeping women and minorities out of top schools."
