# Prior automatic MCQ generation approaches produce structurally simple questions (at most two stem entities) testing mainly recall

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A review of ontology-based and medical question-generation approaches found generated questions restricted to at most two stem entities and mostly testing recall of learned information. [→ J. Leo 2019](#j-leo-2019)

## Evidence

### J. Leo 2019

J. Leo, G. Kurdi, N. Matentzoglu, B. Parsia, U. Sattler, S. Forge, G. Donato, W. Dowling. (2019). Ontology-Based Generation of Medical, Multi-term MCQs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00172-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' narrative review of prior approaches (Papasalouros et al., Alsubait et al., Wang et al., Karamanis et al., and others) reports that generated question "stem contains at most two stem entities" and mostly test recall. This is the review's own qualitative assessment, not a measured comparison.

> "Most notable is the simplicity of the structure of auto-generated questions when compared to hand-crafted questions."
