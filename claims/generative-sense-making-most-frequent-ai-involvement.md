`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Structured classroom observation of 27 lower secondary students across six sessions, with 42 interactional episodes coded abductively into four categories of AI involvement (Table 1, Figure 2). The article states the distribution "indicates that AI is predominantly used as a support for interpretive engagement rather than as a full substitute for cognitive processing."

> "Generative sense-making is the most frequent category (30.95%), followed by AI-assisted sense-making (26.19%) and AI-driven summarization (23.81%), while cognitive acceleration is least frequent (19.05%)."
