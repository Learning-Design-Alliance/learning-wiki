# Children with developmental dyslexia show a significant inhibition impairment at the error z-mean composite level, which remains after controlling for processing speed

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Dyslexia is associated with a significantly higher inhibition z-mean error composite score than controls (Cohen's d = 1.01). [→ Doyle C 2018](#doyle-c-2018)
`q3 i?` The inhibition z-mean error composite difference remains significant after controlling for processing speed (no effect size printed for the controlled analysis). [→ Doyle C 2018 (2)](#doyle-c-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Doyle C 2018

Doyle C, Smeaton AF, Roche RAP and Boran L (2018) Inhibition and Updating, but Not Switching, Predict Developmental Dyslexia and Individual Variation in Reading Ability. Front. Psychol. 9:795. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00795

`q3 · i3`

Case-control ANOVA comparing 27 children with dyslexia and 29 controls aged 10-12 on inhibition z-mean error composites. The dyslexia group showed "a significantly higher inhibition z-mean error score than control participants" with Cohen's d = 1.01.

> "At the composite level, dyslexia is characterized by a significantly higher inhibition z-mean error score than control participants [F(1, 53) = 13.85, p = 0.000, Cohen's d = 1.01; Figure 1]."

### Doyle C 2018 (2)

Doyle C, Smeaton AF, Roche RAP and Boran L (2018) Inhibition and Updating, but Not Switching, Predict Developmental Dyslexia and Individual Variation in Reading Ability. Front. Psychol. 9:795. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00795

`q3 · i?`

ANCOVA controlling for processing speed (coding task) in the same sample. The inhibition composite difference remained significant, F(1, 52) = 9.29, p = 0.004, as did Picture Go No-Go and SART commission error differences; no effect size is printed for this analysis.

> "After controlling for individual variation in processing spe ed, signiﬁcant diﬀerences in the inhibition z-mean error score [F(1, 52) = 9.29, p = 0.004], commission errors during the Picture Go No-Go task [F(1, 52) = 8.50, p = 0.005] and commission errors during the SART task [ F(1, 53) = 5.91, p = 0.019] remain."
