## Evidence

### Ahn Shinhye 2026

Ahn Shinhye, Jeong Hye Won. (2026). Development and evaluation of a stepwise clinical competency development program for new nurses: A single-group repeated-measures quasi-experimental study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0342464

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group repeated-measures quasi-experiment with 49 new nurses at one Korean university hospital; repeated-measures ANOVA across four time points. The article reports "F = 40.01, p < .001" for basic clinical knowledge, with medical device operation showing "the largest effect size (ES = 1.56–1.65)".

> "Basic clinical knowledge improved significantly immediately and three and six months after program participation, compared to pre-program levels (F = 40.01, p < .001)."
