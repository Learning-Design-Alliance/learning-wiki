# The See One, Do One group improved significantly between T1 and T2 while the Mental Training group maintained an already high level

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the intragroup comparison, the See One, Do One group showed a significant increase in clinical examination skills from T1 to T2 (Cohen's d = 1.07 for Rater 1; d = 0.50 for Rater 2), whereas the Mental Training group showed no significant growth but maintained a very high level of competence. ## Evidence

### Nelskamp Arne 2021

Nelskamp Arne, Schnurr Benedikt, Germanyuk Alexandra, Sterz Jasmina, Lorenz Jonas, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Seifert Lukas B. (2021). Comparison of ‘Mental training’ and physical practice in the mediation of a structured facial examination: a quasi randomized, blinded and controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02603-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Intragroup T1-to-T2 comparison within the same quasi-experimental study. The control group's means rose from 33.5 to 39.2 (Rater 1), a "significant increase in clinical examination skills", while the Mental Training group's change was not significant and it maintained a high level.

> "The intragroup comparison between T1 and T2 revealed a significant increase in clinical examination skills in the ‘See One, Do One’ group (Rater 1 p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 1.07; Rater 2 p = 0.06; Cohen’s d = 0.50) (Table 1). The ‘Mental Training’ group did not show a significant growth in clinical examination skills from T1 to T2"
