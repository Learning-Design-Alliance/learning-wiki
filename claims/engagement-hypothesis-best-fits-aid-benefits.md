## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Larwin et al. 2008

Karen H. Larwin, David A. Larwin, Jennifer Gorman. (2008). Mnemonic aids during tests: Worthless frivolity or effective tool in statistics education? Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mnemonic+aids+during+tests+statistics+education

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the quasi-experimental results: the student-generated condition performed best, the no-aids condition beat instructor aids and open textbook, matching the engagement prediction except that two mean differences were not clearly significant. The article argues placebo and dependency accounts were "subsequently dismissed".

> "it is still the case that the pattern of results obtained in the present study best fits the student engagement explanation."
