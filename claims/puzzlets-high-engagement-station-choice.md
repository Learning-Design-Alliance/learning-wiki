# K-2 students showed sustained high engagement with Puzzlets, choosing it as their first station choice 27 out of 36 times

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students across grades K-2 were highly engaged with Puzzlets, with engagement sustained between the first and fifth gameplay sessions, and 27 of 36 students selected Puzzlets as their first station choice over Type Rocket and Scratch Jr. [→ Gadgil 2016](#gadgil-2016)

## Evidence

### Gadgil 2016

Gadgil, S., & Louw, M. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Improving Ed-Tech Products through Feedback. Learning Media Design Center, Carnegie Mellon University. Report to Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5364ec3a-0d6d-4252-8885-4484e657fde7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom observation study at Avonworth with structured observation coding sheets and student interviews across K-2 classrooms. In the station-choice preference task "27 out of 36 students selected Puzzlets as their first choice"; about a third of pairs kept playing after the rotation ended.

> "For the station-choice task in our first classroom observation, 27 out of 36 students selected Puzzlets as their first choice, 6 chose Type Rocket, and 3 chose Scratch Jr."
