## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2021

Danny Doucette, Sonja Cwik, and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). "Everyone is new to this": Student reflections on different aspects of online learning. [doi:10.1119/10.0005795](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0005795)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey and interview responses on assessment in fall 2020 online physics courses. The authors note that by decreasing anxiety, frequent low-stakes assessments may make introductory STEM courses more equitable.

> "Overall, the majority students in introductory, beyond ﬁrst year, and graduate classes preferred a strategy of frequent, low-stakes assessments. Frequent assessments provide plenty of feedback, low stakes keep anxiety low, and the ﬂexibility inherent in frequent low-stakes assessments can help students."
