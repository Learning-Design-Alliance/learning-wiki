Chiappe, A. and Lee, L.L. (2017). Open Teaching: a New Way on E-learning?. The Electronic Journal of e-Learning, 15(5), pp.369-383. www.ejel.org. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1157965

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Content analysis of the meta-synthesis corpus found flexibility and access in 47.2% of evidence items. In higher education, flexibility means learners may "pick the topics, place, pace and/or method", while access implies all interested learners receive educational services equally.

> "When teaching becomes 'open', it creates the possibility of broadening access opportunities to formal learning spaces by managing time and location in a flexible manner (Unesco, 2009)."
