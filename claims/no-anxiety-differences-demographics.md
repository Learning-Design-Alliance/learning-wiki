England Benjamin J., Brigati Jennifer R., Schussler Elisabeth E. (2017). Student anxiety in introductory biology classrooms: Perceptions about active learning and persistence in the major. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182506

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey comparisons using t-tests and ANOVA found no demographic differences; the authors note the sample skewed heavily female and that sample size "lacked the power to detect anxiety differences in gender, ethnicity, or year in school."

> "There were no general class anxiety or classroom practice anxiety item differences based on gender, ethnicity or year in school for the participants overall."
