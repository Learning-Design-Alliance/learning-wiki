# Human-label training produces higher accuracy than AI-label training during the training phase, and remains superior to control in the test phase

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the training phase, Human-label participants had better accuracy than AI-label participants (d = 0.29). [→ Spinde 2025](#spinde-2025)
`q3 i1` In the test phase, Human-label participants remained superior to controls (d = 0.25). [→ Spinde 2025 (2)](#spinde-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Spinde 2025

Spinde, T., Wu, F., Gaissmaier, W., Demartini, G., & Giese, H. (2025). Enhancing Media Literacy: The Effectiveness of (Human) Annotations and Bias Visualizations on Bias Detection. [doi:10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 1 label × phase interaction (F(467,2) = 5.06, p = .007, η2part = 0.021): Human-label training beat AI-label training in the training phase with a printed effect size of d = 0.29, partly because Human-label accuracy was directly evaluated against Human labels.

> "participants with Human label training had better accuracy than the ones with AI label (t(467) = 3.17, p = .0048, d = 0.29) and controls (t(467) = 5.32, p < .001, d = 0.49) in the training phase"

### Spinde 2025 (2)

Spinde, T., Wu, F., Gaissmaier, W., Demartini, G., & Giese, H. (2025). Enhancing Media Literacy: The Effectiveness of (Human) Annotations and Bias Visualizations on Bias Detection. [doi:10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Test-phase simple effects from Experiment 1: Human-label accuracy did not change systematically from training to test, yet Human labels "remained superior to the control in the test phase" with a printed effect size of d = 0.25.

> "Human labels remained superior to the control in the test phase (t(467) = 2.68, p = .0228, d = 0.25; Flabel\test(467,2) = 4.12, p = .0168, η2part = 0.02)."
