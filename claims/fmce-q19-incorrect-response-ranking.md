### Trevor I. Smith 2020

Trevor I. Smith, Kyle J. Louis, Bartholomew J. Ricci, IV, and Nasrine Bendjilali. (2020). Quantitatively ranking incorrect responses to multiple-choice questions using item response theory. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT ranking analysis of the combined FMCE data set for question 19, where the correct response is B, a constant negative force graph. The article reports that response E "is chosen by fewer than 1% of the data set" but these students otherwise show fairly strong understanding of Newton's laws.

> "According to these results, the best incorrect answer is A: a graph with a constant positive value (zero slope). The second-best incorrect response is E: a graph with a constant zero value (zero slope)."
