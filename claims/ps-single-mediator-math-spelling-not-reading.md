## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of 504 children with ADHD. The article reports PS was "a partial mediator... in mediation model 1, and a full mediator in mediation model 2" for mathematics; only bootstrap CIs are printed, no standardized effect size.

> "The single indirect effect of PS as mediator between inattention symptom severity and mathematics performance was significant for both serial mediation models. Thus, PS was a partial mediator in the relationship between inattention symptom severity and mathematics performance in mediation model 1, and a full mediator in mediation model 2."

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025 (2)

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Serial mediation model 2 for spelling in the same sample: PS mediated the inattention–spelling relation while WM did not; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The single indirect effect of PS as mediator in the relation between inattention symptom severity and spelling performance was significant. The single indirect effect of WM as mediator in the relation between inattention symptom severity and spelling performance was not significant."

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025 (3)

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the same SEM analysis: the PS-only indirect pathway to reading was non-significant in both models; the article prints no effect size, so the null is not evidence of equivalence.

> "The single indirect effect with PS as mediator between inattention symptom severity and reading performance was non-significant for both serial mediation models."
