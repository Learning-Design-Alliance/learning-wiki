### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear probability model adding high school fixed effects to academic performance, aspiration, and waiting-cost controls, neutralizing peer, neighborhood, and teacher effects; the study found financial aid beneficiaries were "4 percentage points less likely to always wait."

> "Our analysis showed that socioeconomic differences in waiting remained even when including high school fixed effects in addition to rational choice factors, with financial aid beneficiaries being 4 percentage points less likely to always wait (SE = 0.003, P < 0.001), see Table 4."
