## Evidence

### Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman 2021

Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman. (2021). Development of a Conceptual Framework for Knowledge Integration in Learning Newton's Third Law. The Ohio State University, Department of Physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical analysis of textbook and lecture language in the article's framework-development section. The authors argue the common definition "lacks a clear description of the mechanistic nature of N3L" and that textbooks still rely on action-reaction wording, which may directly lead to a belief in causality.

> "The action-reaction language itself implies a time separation between action and reaction and can often be interpreted as a causal relation."
