`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-arm comparison in the randomized trial's control group across two SCT production rounds. The authors suggest "a minimal training is necessary to enhance initial writing skills".

> "the SCTs produced during the second phase (with only 2 months after the first phase and despite the return of the reference teaching aid) appeared to be of significantly poorer quality than those initially created, with the rate of valid SCTs dropping from 31 to 16%"
