# Equated WCPM scores are more highly correlated across passages read by the same student than raw WCPM scores, suggesting greater reliability

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chen 2020

Chen, J., & Simpson, M. A. (2020). Equating words-correct-per-minute (WCPM) scores across passages of MAP Reading Fluency. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/equating-words-correct-per-minute-wcpm-scores-across-passages-of-map-reading-fluency/

Large-scale observational analysis of adaptive oral reading test records from students in grades K-3 across Fall 2018 through Spring 2019 (Table 3.1 reports pair Ns such as 22,073 and correlations such as 0.865 raw versus 0.894 equated for p1_p2 in Fall 2018). The report states "suggesting that the equated scores are more reliable."
