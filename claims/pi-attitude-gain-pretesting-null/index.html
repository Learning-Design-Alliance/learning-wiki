Evidence¶

M. J. Wangila 2015¶

M. J. Wangila, W. Martin, M. Ronald. (2015). Effect of Programmed Instruction on Students' Attitude Towards Structure of the Atom and the Periodic Table among Kenyan Secondary Schools. Science Education International, Vol. 25, Issue 4, 488-500. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086551

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis (means, standard deviations, mean gains) of SADQ scores in Table 4 for the four groups. The article also reports the E2-C1 post-test margin grew six fold to 16.3225 marks and that the unpretested E1-C2 margin of 15.5707 was comparable, implying pretesting had no effect. These are descriptive statistics with no test reported.

"The Table further reveals that while the mean gain of the entire sample was 11.0053, the mean gains of the pretested groups E2 and C1 were 17.0338 and 3.4861 respectively, which shows that the experimental group had a greater attitude change than the control group."
