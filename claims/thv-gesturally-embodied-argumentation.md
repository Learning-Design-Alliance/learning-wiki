### Walkington 2024

Walkington, C., Nathan, M. J., Huang, W., Hunnicutt, J., & Washington, J. (2024). Multimodal analysis of interaction data from embodied education technologies. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2565–2584. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10254-9

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Qualitative MAET case analysis of video footage of two high school students creating arm poses for the conjecture about rhombus diagonals in the Conjecture Builder. The episode was coded for collaborative gestures and talk moves; the authors state "much of the conversation is expressed in rich gesture sequences rather than in precise mathematical speech."

> "We coded this theme as “gesturally embodied argumentation spurred by pose creation,” and examined its conditions of incidence across the corpus"
