`q1 i?` Experimental group students used different strategies (cross-multiplication, creating equivalent fractions, unit fractions) with correct expressions, while control group students did not notice multiplicative relations and made incorrect additive solutions. [→ Şen 2017](#sen-2017)

## Evidence

### Şen 2017

Şen, C., & Güler, G. (2017). Effect of Strategy Teaching For the Solution of Ratio Problems on Students' Proportional Reasoning Skills. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 5(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1142503

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of homework problem-form solutions (Figures 1 and 2), presented as citations without scoring. The article reports that experimental students' solutions "support the scores they got from the PRT" and that control students' multiplicative relations were not noticed.

> "It was observed that control group students made additive operations and that their solutions were incorrect since they could not notice ratio and proportional relations."
