Evidence¶

Argentin et al. 2016¶

Gianluca Argentin, Gian Paolo Barbetta, Francesca Maci. (2016). Increasing parental involvement to promote dropout prevention. Lessons from an RCT in Italian lower secondary schools. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567232

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

An RCT in 15 Italian lower secondary schools randomly assigned 262 referred at-risk students, half to treatment. The abstract reports all these effects are "statistically and substantially significant (p-value< 0.05 and effect size>.20)".

"At the end of the first year, the intervention show ed to be effective on four outcomes: it increased parental involvement (as perceived by stu dents), it reduced their perception of teachers’ hostility and it improved their self-effi cacy about learning and improved their satisfaction about their school experience."
