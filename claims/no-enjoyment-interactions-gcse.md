### Tim T. Morris 2021

Tim T. Morris, Danny Dorling, Neil M. Davies and George Davey Smith. (2021). Associations between school enjoyment at age 6 and later educational achievement: evidence from a UK cohort study. npj Science of Learning 6:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00092-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Complete case interaction analyses of school enjoyment with social class, maternal education, cognitive ability and pupil sex on GCSE achievement. The quote prints all interaction p values; the article cautions these should be interpreted with caution given limited statistical power.

> "There was little evidence of interactions in either the linear or logistic complete case analyses between school enjoyment and each of social class (interaction p values = 0.265 and 0.473 respectively), maternal education ( p = 0.617; p = 0.591), cognitive ability (p = 0.867; p = 0.243) or pupil’s sex (p = 0.084; p = 0.174) on GCSE achievement."
