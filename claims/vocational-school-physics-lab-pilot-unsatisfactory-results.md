## Evidence

### Vera Montalbano 2013

Vera Montalbano, Maria De Nicola, Simone Di Renzone and Serena Frati. (2013). A pilot experience in physics laboratory for a vocational school. ICPE-EPEC 2013 Proceedings. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.4868](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.4868)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot case study in an Italian vocational school for commercial and tourist services, involving about 50 students ages 14-15 in two first classes. The authors report that "the learning process was not comparable to that obtained with students in other types of upper secondary schools". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Despite the care and attention paid in designing educational material and performing the activities in the lab, the learning process was not comparable to that obtained with students in other types of upper secondary schools or even with younger students in lower secondary school."
