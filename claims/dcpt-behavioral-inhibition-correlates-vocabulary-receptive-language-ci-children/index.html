Evidence¶

Horn 2004¶

Horn, D. L., Davis, R. A. O., Pisoni, D. B., & Miyamoto, R. T. (2004). Visual attention, behavioral inhibition and speech/language outcomes in deaf children with cochlear implants. Int Congr Ser. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ics.2004.07.048

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Retrospective analysis of clinical data from 47 prelingually deaf children with Nucleus 22 implants; bivariate correlations between dCPT error ratio and outcome measures at 1, 2, and 3 years of CI use. The article reports "strong and highly significant (p<0.01)" correlations; Table 1 prints vocabulary r = 0.69 (year 1) and receptive language r = 0.60 (year 1).

"Correlations between the dCPT and vocabulary and language measures were strong and highly significant (p<0.01) at most intervals of CI use."
