### Jacob Feinleib 2026

Jacob Feinleib, Matthew Dew, and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Share, Rotate, Split: The Effects of Group Work Role Distributions on Student Outcomes. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/1n3c-v2fj](https://doi.org/10.1103/1n3c-v2fj)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the constant-sum role distribution survey item (density plots in Fig. 3) across all courses. In Semester 1 sharing was reported at higher rates than other distributions, followed by splitting; in Semester 2 splitting was as frequent or more frequent than sharing, and rotating was least reported in both semesters.

> "In both semesters, rotating (orange) was the least frequently reported role distribution (evidenced by higher peaks at the lowest reported times)."
