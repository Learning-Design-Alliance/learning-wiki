### Huang Jichong 2019

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three RCTs comparing high-fidelity with low-fidelity simulation on skill performance. The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.64 (95% CI 0.06–1.21), a moderate benefit, with heterogeneity I2 = 63%.

> "Three RCTs [10, 14, 28] measured the skill performance and found a moderate benefit with high-fidelity simulation when compared with low-fidelity simulation (SMD 0.64; 95% CI 0.06–1.21)"
