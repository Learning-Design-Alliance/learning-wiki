### Brown 2009

Brown, D. and Neale, N. (2009). A Global Citizen of the Skies. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.5289](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.5289)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of saved sample constellations created during the activity (Figs. 1 and 2): a 'Natives' constellation called 'The Sun Rabbit' versus an 'Explorers' constellation called 'The Plane', both based on the same stars.

> "Already a clear impact of the cultural background of each group can be detected. The ‘Natives’ constellation targets an animal rather than an artificial object like a plane chosen by the member of the ‘Explorers’."
