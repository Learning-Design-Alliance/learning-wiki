## Evidence

### Kenichiro Aoki 2009

Kenichiro Aoki, Takahisa Mitsui and Yuki Yamamoto. (2009). Direct quantitative measurements of Doppler effects for sound sources with gravitational acceleration. https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.3819 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0911.3819](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0911.3819)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pendulum Doppler measurement (ℓ = 2.32 ± 0.01 m, R = 48 kHz, N = 2^14) in which peak frequencies track the swinging source; the article reports the observed frequency "agrees with the standard formula" and gives g = 9.8 ± 0.1 m/s2. The setup provides approaching, receding and accelerating sources in one experiment.

> "The observed oscillation freque ncy agrees with the standard formula, √ g/l/(2π). We can easily obtain g to 1 % accuracy or less and g = 9 .8 ± 0.1 m/s2 in this example."
