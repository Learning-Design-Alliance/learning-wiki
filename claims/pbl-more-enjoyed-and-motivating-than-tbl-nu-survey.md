## Evidence

### Almagribi Ahmad Zaker 2024

Almagribi Ahmad Zaker, Al-Qahtani Saad Misfer, Assiri Abdullah M., Mehdar Khlood Mohammed. (2024). Perceptions of medical students at Najran University on the effectiveness of problem-based learning and team-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06148-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kirkpatrick Level 1 (reactions) of a descriptive cross-sectional survey of 106 preclinical medical students at Najran University, using a 13-question online questionnaire. The reported percentages show "Most students enjoyed PBL (73.6%) and were highly motivated to join it (67.9%)" while TBL was least enjoyed (5.7%) and motivated 8.5%. Percentages are descriptive; no inferential test is reported.

> "Most students enjoyed PBL (73.6%) and were highly motivated to join it (67.9%). A portion of students (9.4%) preferred both educational approaches. A small percentage (11.3%) expressed a lack of interest in both PBL and TBL. TBL was the least enjoyable model (5.7%)"
