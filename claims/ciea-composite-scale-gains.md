# After CIEA implementation the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on Social Competence, Internalising Problems and General Adaptation composite scales

## Evidence

### Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić 2015

Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić. (2015). The Effects of the Comprehensive Inductive Educational Approach on the Social Behaviour of Preschool Children in Kindergarten. c e p s Journal, Vol.5, No1. https://journals.cepsj.si/index.php/cepsj

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed between-subject ANOVA on SV-O composite scales in the same quasi-experiment (EG n = 52, CG n = 48). The EG achieved higher scores on Social Competence and Internalising Problems after implementation; the article reports partial η² = .11 (printed as "partial h2 = .11") and partial η² = .08 (printed as "partial h 2 = .08").

> "the EG achieved significantly higher scores than the CG on two of the three composite scales  of social behaviour, i.e., Social Competence (Wilks' Lambda = .89, F = (1,99) = 12.67, p < .01, partial h2 = .11) and Internalising Problems (Wilks' Lambda = .92, F = (1,99) = 8.37, p < .01, partial h 2 = .08)"

### Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić 2015 (2)

Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić. (2015). The Effects of the Comprehensive Inductive Educational Approach on the Social Behaviour of Preschool Children in Kindergarten. c e p s Journal, Vol.5, No1. https://journals.cepsj.si/index.php/cepsj

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same ANOVA analysis of the overall General Adaptation score; the article reports partial η² = .07 (printed as "partial h2 = .07"), a medium effect size by the article's own Cohen-based classification. The EG post-test mean (57.69T) approached the 63T above-average marker.

> "A significant difference between the two groups in time was also identified for General Adaptation, which represents the general score of the questionnaire (Wilks' Lambda = .93, F = (1,99) = 7.31, p < .01, partial h2 = .07)"
