## Evidence

### Kunert W 2020

Kunert W, Storz P, Dietz N, Axt S, Falch C, Kirschniak A, Wilhelm P. (2020). Learning curves, potential and speed in training of laparoscopic skills: a randomised comparative study in a box trainer. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07768-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the fitted exponential decay parameters (plateau y0, learning potential A1, learning speed t1) from the randomised box-trainer study. The article states "Learning potentials varied widely depending on the corresponding initial values and learning plateaus" and that 2D speed was not an advantage.

> "Learning potentials varied widely depending on the corresponding initial values and learning plateaus. Learning speed in the 2D Group was generally higher, but was not an advantage, because it could only partly catch up with the generally much better starting conditions of the 3D Group."
