## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chang Zhu 2010

Chang Zhu, Martin Valcke and Tammy Schellens. (2010). A cross-cultural study of teacher perspectives on teacher roles and adoption of online collaborative learning in higher education. European Journal of Teacher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/02619761003631849

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cultural Environment Survey (power distance, collaboration, competition subscales) administered to 60 Chinese and 30 Flemish teachers. The article reports no significant group difference on power distance or collaboration and a significant difference on competition favouring higher Chinese emphasis ("p < .001").

> "The results show that power distance between teacher-student and student collaboration appear to be similar between the two cultural groups ( p > .05). However, compared to the Flemish teachers, Chinese teachers put more emphasis on competition among students (p < .001)."
