## Evidence

### de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M. 2020

de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M., Mesters Ilse, Govaerts Marjan J. B., Timmerman Angelique A., Muris Jean W. M., Kramer Anneke W. M., van der Vleuten Cees P. M. (2020). Supervisors' intention to observe clinical task performance: an exploratory study using the theory of planned behaviour during postgraduate medical training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02047-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis (Pearson's R) of the extended TPB measures in the survey of 82 GP supervisors. The article reports "no significant correlation between intention and the measures 'social pressure' and 'modelling'", while noting a positive modelling–normative beliefs correlation and a negative social pressure–attitude correlation.

> "From the additional measures only past behaviour had a significant correlation with intention; there was no significant correlation between intention and the measures 'social pressure' and 'modelling'."
