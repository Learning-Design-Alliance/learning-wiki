`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive within-person correlation analysis (between-person variation removed per Kenny and La Voie) of the six engagement variables across the nine lessons. The article states "vigour had a signi ﬁcant positive correlation with interest and a signi ﬁcant negative correlation with fatigue, depression, sleep frequency and mind-wandering"; Table 2 prints vigour–interest r = 0.49 and vigour–fatigue r = −0.39. Correlations are descriptive, not an intervention effect size.
