q2 · i? · review · r1

The report's introduction, a synthesis of learning-sciences research, asserts that "intensive remediation alone will not meet students' needs" and warns of harm when it is segregating and stigmatizing. No effect sizes or specific studies are printed for this assertion.

"Research on the science of learning and development indicates that intensive remediation alone will not meet students’ needs and—if conducted in a way that is segregating, stigmatizing, and separated from children’s real- life concerns— could even deepen inequalities and exacerbate trauma."
