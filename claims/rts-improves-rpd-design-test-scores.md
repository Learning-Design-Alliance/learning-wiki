# RTS-trained students scored significantly higher than controls on both theoretical and drawing tests of RPD framework design (Cohen d = 1.1)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` First-year stomatology master's students who used RTS for 90 minutes of self-study obtained significantly better theoretical and drawing test grades than students receiving instructor-led theoretical lessons and case practice. [→ Siu Yikchi 2025](#siu-yikchi-2025)

## Evidence

### Siu Yikchi 2025

Siu Yikchi, Bai Hefei, Yoon Jung-Min, Ye Hongqiang, Liu Yunsong, Zhou Yongsheng. (2025). Enhancing Preclinical Training for Removable Partial Dentures Through Participatory 3D Simulation: Development and Usability Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71743

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Randomized comparison of 26 newly enrolled stomatology master's students (13 per group, sealed-envelope allocation): training group used RTS for 2 credit hours of self-study, controls received instructor-led lessons. Training group scored higher on both tests, "P=.01", with Cohen d = 1.1 reported in Results.

> "Participants in the training group obtained better final grades compared to controls (theoretical test: 88.8, SD 2.3; 85.7, SD 3.3, respectively; P=.01; drawing test: 89.8, SD 4.5; 85.1, SD 4.3, respectively; P=.01)."
