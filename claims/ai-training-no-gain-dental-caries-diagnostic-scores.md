## Evidence

### Alkattan Rana 2026

Alkattan Rana, Alreshaid Lulwah, Alfallaj Hayam, Pillai Arun Gopinathan, Alqahtani Hussam M., Suhaym Omar, Almohefer Sami, Madfa Ahmed A. (2026). Artificial intelligence-assisted training for proximal caries diagnosis by undergraduate dental students: a randomized experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09982-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized experimental study of 79 undergraduate dental students (41 AI, 38 conventional) completing pre- and post-tests on 20 bitewing radiographs each. Mixed-design ANOVA found no significant group, time, or interaction effects on total exact-match scores (0–60).

> "Table 1 shows that there was no significant difference between the AI-assisted and conventional groups in pre-test and post-test scores. Each group also showed similar assessment scores across time points (pre- and post-intervention), with no significant difference"
