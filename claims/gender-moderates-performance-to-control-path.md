## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Grade 7 performance significantly predicted Grade 8 behavioral control for boys (βboy = 0.08, p < 0.001) but not girls (βgirl = 0.02, p > 0.05), and the Wald Chi-square test showed this was the only coefficient differing significantly by gender. [→ Yi Yang 2025](#yi-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Yi Yang 2025

Yi Yang. (2025). Reciprocal Relationships Between Parental Involvement and Academic Performance in Early Adolescence: A Two-Year Longitudinal Study in China. Journal of Youth and Adolescence, 54(4), 876–889. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02102-7

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Multi-group SEM with Wald Chi-square tests on the CEPS sample comparing boys (N = 4842) and girls (N = 4436). The article reports "βboy = 0.08, p < 0.001" versus a non-significant path for girls; the Wald test indicated only this path differed by gender.

> "academic performance only significantly predicted behavior control for boys (βboy = 0.08, p < 0.001), but not for girls (βgirl = 0.02, p > 0.05)."
