### Davor I 2026

Davor I (2026) Generative AI-supported instruction as a cognitive scaffold: effects on senior high school students' geometric reasoning and proof construction. Front. Educ. 11:1869961. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1869961

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA with post-test scores as dependent variable, instructional group as fixed factor, and pre-test scores as covariate in the same quasi-experimental study. The article states the "partial η2 = .377" indicates a large effect; the overall model explained approximately 50.3% of variance (R2 = .503).

> "after controlling for pre-test scores, a statistically significant effect of instructional group on post-test performance was observed, F (1, 83) = 50.30, p < .001, partial η2 = .377."
