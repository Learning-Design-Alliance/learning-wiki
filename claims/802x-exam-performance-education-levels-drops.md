## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Saif Rayyan 2013

Saif Rayyan, Daniel T. Seaton, John Belcher, David E. Pritchard, Isaac Chuang. (2013). Participation And performance In 8.02x Electricity And Magnetism: The First Physics MOOC From MITx. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Examination performance analysis by education level (Figure 5), dividing each level into Completers and Drops. The article reports relatively small but mostly statistically significant differences in Completers' mean grades across High School, BS, and MS/Prof. Deg. categories; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean grades of "Completers" have relatively small differences (but statistically signiﬁcant in most cases) with respect to level of education."

### Saif Rayyan 2013 (2)

Saif Rayyan, Daniel T. Seaton, John Belcher, David E. Pritchard, Isaac Chuang. (2013). Participation And performance In 8.02x Electricity And Magnetism: The First Physics MOOC From MITx. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same Figure 5 analysis: the article reports a noticeable drift to lower mean grades across the three midterms and final for all education levels, offered as an interpretation (difficulty or fatigue) rather than a tested result.

> "There is also a noticeable drift to lower mean grade for all levels of education, either indicating increasing exam difﬁculty or student fatigue over the 16 course weeks, or both."

### Saif Rayyan 2013 (3)

Saif Rayyan, Daniel T. Seaton, John Belcher, David E. Pritchard, Isaac Chuang. (2013). Participation And performance In 8.02x Electricity And Magnetism: The First Physics MOOC From MITx. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Figure 5 analysis of Drops' mean grades. The article reports many participants stop taking exams after a low grade, and notes future work will cross-validate this signal with daily activity data.

> "The mean grades of "Drops" in Fig. 5 show that many people stop taking exams (a proxy for course activity) after earning a low grade."
