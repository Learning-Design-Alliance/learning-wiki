## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Substituting the wave velocity v = E/√(2m(E−V)) into the wave equation for Ψ(r,t) = ψ(r)e^(−iEt/ℏ) analytically produces ℏ²/2m ∇²ψ + (E − V)ψ = 0, the stationary Schrödinger equation for a particle in a scalar potential field. [→ Jaume Masoliver 2009](#jaume-masoliver-2009)
