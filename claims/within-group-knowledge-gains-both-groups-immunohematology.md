## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Knowledge gains from pre-test to post-test were statistically significant within both the control group (p < 0.001) and the intervention group (p < 0.001). [→ Saniee Nadia 2026](#saniee-nadia-2026)

## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre–post comparisons using Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test found statistically significant knowledge gains in both the control and intervention groups; the article prints no effect size for these within-group tests.

> "the mean of knowledge before and after intervention was tested by the Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test. In the control group, the mean of knowledge before and after the intervention was statistically different (P-value < 0.001). In the intervention group, the mean of knowledge before and after the intervention was statistically different (P-value < 0.001)."
