The review attributes to Baker et al. (2011) a log-data analysis predicting transfer-posttest performance, finding "a negative relationship" between Help Avoidance and transfer-test performance, which the authors cannot reconcile with the persistence findings.

"To counterbalance these findings, however, a recent paper by Baker et al. ( 2011) looked at using tutor log data to predict student performance on a transfer posttest. Help Avoidance had a negative relationship with performance on the transfer test."
