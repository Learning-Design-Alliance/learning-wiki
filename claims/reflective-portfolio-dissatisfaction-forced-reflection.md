## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students and teachers become unsatisfied with reflective portfolios, less motivated and engaged, and regard them as just another assignment, because reflection is forced in an artificial fixed format with little opportunity to direct learning. [→ Driessen Erik 2016](#driessen-erik-2016)

## Evidence

### Driessen Erik 2016

Driessen Erik. (2016). Do portfolios have a future? Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9679-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Narrative synthesis in the Public relations section, citing Arntfield et al. (2015) and the author's earlier studies. The article reports that students do not value reflection when forced into an artificial and fixed format. No effect sizes are printed.

> "All this causes students and teachers to be unsatisfied with their reflective portfolio, and, consequently, to become less motivated and engaged (Arntfield et al. 2015). As a result, they regard portfolios as just another assignment they have to do."
