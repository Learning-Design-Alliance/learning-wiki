`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre-post assessment around the prospectively planned half-day refresher course for active birth attendants, which included algorithm review, video-based skill demonstration, and paired practice. The article reports 99% passed OSCE B after refresher training versus 81% before; no effect size is printed.

> "Prior to refresher training, the proportion passing fell to 81% (p < 0.0001), but after refresher training 99% passed the OSCE B."
