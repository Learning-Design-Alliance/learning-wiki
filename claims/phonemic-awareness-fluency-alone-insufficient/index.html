Evidence¶

Galuschka Katharina 2014¶

Galuschka Katharina, Ise Elena, Krick Kathrin, Schulte-Körne Gerd. (2014). Effectiveness of Treatment Approaches for Children and Adolescents with Reading Disabilities: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089900

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of three phonemic awareness instruction comparisons in school-aged children and adolescents with reading disabilities. The article reports the interventions "do not have a significant effect" on reading or spelling performance; no effect size was printed for this finding.

"The present results demonstrate that when phonemic awareness interventions are provided to school-aged children and adolescents with reading difficulties, they do not have a significant effect on a child's reading or spelling performance."
