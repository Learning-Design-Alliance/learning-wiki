## Evidence

### Laninga-Wijnen 2018

Laninga-Wijnen, L., Ryan, A., Harakeh, Z., Shin, H., & Vollebergh, W. A. M. (2018). The Moderating Role of Popular Peers' Achievement Goals in 5th- and 6th-Graders' Achievement-Related Friendships: A Social Network Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, vol. 110, no. 2, pp. 289-307. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000210

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Preliminary analyses in the Results section compared Grade 5 (27 classrooms) and Grade 6 (19 classrooms) subsamples; no significant differences in norms or friendship processes were found, so final analyses pooled both grades to gain power.

> "Furthermore, the presence and direction of friendship processes related to achievement did not differ signifi- cantly across Grade 5 and Grade 6. We also found that the role of peer norms in friendship processes was similar in Grade 5 and Grade 6 classrooms."
