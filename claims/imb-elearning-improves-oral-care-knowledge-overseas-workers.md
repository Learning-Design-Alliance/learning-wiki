## Evidence

### Sasayama Kiriko 2023

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot parallel randomized controlled trial with 36 intervention and 46 control participants. Table 2 reports oral care knowledge rising from a baseline mean of 49.10 (SD 20.29) to 72.69 (SD 19.98) in the intervention group versus 50.36 to 57.97 in controls, with "Difference Between Groups" MD -14.71, 95% CI -22.53 to -6.9; oral care knowledge "alone was improved in the e-learning group."

> "At the three months follow-up, oral care knowledge alone was improved in the e-learning group (Table 2)."
