## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Because multilingual status did not predict cognitive change from 12-14, cognitive skills could not mediate faster educational progress; evidence for cognitive mediation is limited. [→ Perry 2026](#perry-2026)

## Evidence

### Perry 2026

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' interpretation combining the mediation and null longitudinal findings: since SM and ML status did not predict cognitive change, "such skills could not be the mediating pathway for the faster academic progress of multilinguals".

> "Hence, the evidence for cognitive skills playing a role in multilingual educational benefits is limited."
