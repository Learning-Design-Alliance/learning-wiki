Descriptive questionnaire findings on the reflection phase. The article reports that "Thirty-one (94%) of the physiotherapy students and 15 (94%) of the occupational therapy students agreed or strongly agreed that the reflections afterwards provided them with learning benefits".

> "Thirty-one (94%) of the physiotherapy students and 15 (94%) of the occupational therapy students agreed or strongly agreed that the reflections afterwards provided them with learning benefits and that they learned from each other during the discussions."
