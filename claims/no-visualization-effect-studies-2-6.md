### David Menendez 2023

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Internal meta-analysis of Studies 2-6 found "no effect of the perceptual richness of the visualizations, d = 0.08" with a confidence interval spanning zero; no interactions involving visualization were significant.

> "There was no effect of the perceptual richness of the visualizations, d = 0.08 [-0.04, 0.201], with low between-study heterogeneity, I2 = 0.0%. No other effects were significant."
