## Evidence

### Arulselvi 2011

Evangelin Arulselvi. (2011). Effect of Instructional Media in the Learning of English Grammar on the Achievement of Teacher Training Students at Namakkal District. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 3 July - September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071032

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Component-wise analysis of the parallel-group experiment reports the experimental group's post-test comprehension mean of 35 against the control group's 29, with a t value of 2.68 significant at .05, leading to rejection of the second null hypothesis.

> "But after the treatment it was found that experimental group students were achieving high mean scores (M = 35) in comparison to conventional group students (M = 29). The computed 't' value is 2.68 which is significant at the .05 level of confidence."

### Arulselvi 2011 (2)

Evangelin Arulselvi. (2011). Effect of Instructional Media in the Learning of English Grammar on the Achievement of Teacher Training Students at Namakkal District. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 3 July - September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071032

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test analysis for comprehension shows both groups attaining nearly equal mean scores with a non-significant t value of 1.83, indicating the groups were "more or less same in comprehension before the treatment".

> "It is evident from the analysis that both groups were attaining nearly equal mean scores (M = 26, 25) for the test item comprehension. The 't' value is 1.83 which is not significant at the acceptable level of confidence."
