## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-to-post self-reported SSRS overall competence scores increased significantly in the ART group (F(1, 208) = 4.75, p < .05) but not in the control group (F(1, 208) = 1.34, p = .25). [→ Koposov 2014](#koposov-2014)

## Evidence

### Koposov 2014

Koposov, R., Gundersen, K. K. & Svartdal, F. (2014). Efficacy of Aggression Replacement Training among Children from North-West Russia. International Journal of Emotional Education, 6(1), 14-24. https://www.um.edu.mt/cres/ijee

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental evaluation with 232 children from six schools and four social institutions in North-West Russia, analysed by repeated measures ANOVA on SSRS self-report scores. The ART contrast was significant while "the control group) was not significant"; no effect size is printed.

> "The first contrast (i.e. pre vs. post scores within the ART group) indicated a significant effect, F (1, 208) = 4.75, p < .05. The second contrast (i.e. pre vs. p ost scores within the control group) was not significant, F (1 , 208) = 1.34, p = .25."
