# All three instructional methods reduced misconceptions, and comprehension and transfer effects were stable at delayed testing one week later

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Compared to before studying, the number of misconceptions was reduced at immediate and delayed testing with all three instructional methods, with a large main effect of time of assessment (η² = .34). [→ Anja Prinz 2021](#anja-prinz-2021)
`q3 i?` At delayed testing 6 to 8 days later, there was no significant overall interaction between misconceptions and instructional method on delayed comprehension or delayed transfer. [→ Anja Prinz 2021 (2)](#anja-prinz-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Anja Prinz 2021

Anja Prinz, Stefanie Golke, Jörg Wittwer. (2021). Counteracting detrimental effects of misconceptions on learning and metacomprehension accuracy: The utility of refutation texts and think sheets. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09535-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on misconception counts (before studying, immediate, delayed) in the randomized experiment with Huynh–Feldt correction: "F(1.63, 143.52) = 44.59, p < .001, η2 = .34 (large effect)". Simple effects showed misconceptions were reduced at immediate and delayed testing with all three methods (ps ≤ .010).

> "Yet, there was a significant main effect of time of misconceptions assessment, F(1.63, 143.52) = 44.59, p < .001, η2 = .34 (large effect)."

### Anja Prinz 2021 (2)

Anja Prinz, Stefanie Golke, Jörg Wittwer. (2021). Counteracting detrimental effects of misconceptions on learning and metacomprehension accuracy: The utility of refutation texts and think sheets. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09535-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delayed-session regression (N = 91, one participant missing) on delayed comprehension: "∆R 2 = .01, F(2, 85) = 0.95, p = .391"; the delayed-transfer analysis likewise showed no significant interaction (∆R2 = .04, p = .190), so the delayed hypotheses were not confirmed.

> "The analysis on delayed comprehension showed that there was no significant overall interaction between misconceptions and instructional method, ∆R 2 = .01, F(2, 85) = 0.95, p = .391."
