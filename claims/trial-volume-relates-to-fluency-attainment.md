## Evidence

### Rabbitt Louise 2020

Rabbitt Louise, Byrne Dara, O'Connor Paul, Gorecka Miroslawa, Jacobsen Alan, Lydon Sinéad. (2020). A pragmatic randomised controlled trial of SAFMEDS to produce fluency in interpretation of electrocardiograms. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02021-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison within the intervention group (n = 13) of the pragmatic RCT. The article reports fluency achievers completed "a higher average number of trials (M = 51.9)" than non-achievers "(M = 31.5)"; no statistical test of this difference is reported.

> "It is perhaps unsurprising that participants who achieved fluency completed a higher average number of trials (M = 51.9) than participants who did not achieve fluency (M = 31.5)."
