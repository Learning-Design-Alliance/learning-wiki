## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the analysis of 1427 Mini-CEX forms, 65.3% of feedback on students' strengths was general, 38.64% of development comments were general suggestions, and less than 30% of feedback for each domain was specific. [→ Soemantri Diantha 2018](#soemantri-diantha-2018)

## Evidence

### Soemantri Diantha 2018

Soemantri Diantha, Dodds Agnes, Mccoll Geoff. (2018). Examining the nature of feedback within the Mini Clinical Evaluation Exercise (Mini-CEX): an analysis of 1427 Mini-CEX assessment forms. GMS Journal for Medical Education, Volume 35, Issue 4, Doc47. [doi:10.3205/zma001193](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001193)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of 1427 collected Mini-CEX assessment forms from final-year medical students, with feedback coded using a manual based on Holmboe et al's categories; the study found "65.3% feedback on students’ strengths was general feedback" and 16.6% of assessors left the feedback column blank.

> "The application of the coding manual revealed that 65.3% feedback on students’ strengths was general feedback"
