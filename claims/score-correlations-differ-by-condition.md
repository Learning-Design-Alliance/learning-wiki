Correlational analysis of ChatGPT-condition participants (N=27) relating final main-test score to pretest score. The article reports "Kendall's rank correlation ( τ=.37, p=.02)"; the article prints no standardized effect size (d, r, η²) for this finding, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). The search engine condition showed τ=.27, p=.03 with self-assessed physics knowledge instead.

> "We found a large positive correlation between the final score and the pretest score, using Kendall's rank correlation ( τ=.37, p=.02)."
