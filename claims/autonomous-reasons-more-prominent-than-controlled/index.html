Janke 2024¶

Janke, S. (2024). Dynamic or Static Goal Regulation: Implications of Weak and Strong Bonds Between Autonomous/Controlled Reasons and Aims for Achievement Goal Striving. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09968-w

The author's first central observation from inspecting observed associations and reason mean values in Tables 1 and 2 of the scoping review. No effect size is printed for this mean-level contrast; supporting means appear in the tables per study.

"First, autonomous reasons emerge as more prominent than controlled reasons for goal striving regardless of whether the authors investigated mastery (approach) or performance (approach) aims."
