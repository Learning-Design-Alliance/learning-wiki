### Ashiqul Islam Dip 2016

Ashiqul Islam Dip, Mohammad Tomal Hossain, Md Forman Ullah, Md Salah Uddin. (2016). Gender Differences in Undergraduate Physics Courses: A Comparative Study of Persistence. Science Journal of Education. [doi:10.11648/j.sjedu.20170501.13](https://doi.org/10.11648/j.sjedu.20170501.13)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Attendance analysis of the five-day 1st Workshop on Classical Mechanics, with 47 male and 10 female participants on the starting day; exponential fits yielded per-group decrease rates and persistence indices. The female index was "42.6 % higher" than the male index.

> "The obtained decrease rate is  𝜆 = 0.310. And the obtained persistence index is ℘ = 3.226‡. This persistence index of the female participants is 42.6 % higher than the persistence index of the male participants of the same workshop."
