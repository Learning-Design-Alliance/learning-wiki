Descriptive results of the same cross-sectional telematic survey (n 445; 320 women, 125 men; mostly residing in Peru and Paraguay). Addiction was dichotomized at the 60th percentile of the Labrador JF questionnaire (cut-off 1.71) and performance at percentile 60 of the perception questionnaire (cut-off 3.7). The article prints "165 (37,08%) estudiantes" with addiction and "281 estudiantes (63,15%)" with poor performance.
