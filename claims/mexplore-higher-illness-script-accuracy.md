## Evidence

### Pang Xiao 2026

Pang Xiao, Liu Chang, Huang Yan, Liu MingYou, Liu Jiyuan. (2026). An Entity-Based Visual Analytics System Enhancing Medical Expertise Acquisition: Development and Verification Study. JMIR Medical Informatics. https://doi.org/10.2196/83785

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled user study with 20 second-year medical students randomly assigned to MExplore (MEX, n=10) or other-resources (OTH, n=10) groups; independent t tests showed the MEX group's "overall mean accuracy (68.3 out of 76 (90%) vs 63.1 out of 76 (83%), P<.001)".

> "Independent t tests confirmed significant improvements in overall mean accuracy (68.3 out of 76 (90%) vs 63.1 out of 76 (83%), P<.001)."
