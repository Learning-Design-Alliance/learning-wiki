Multiple linear regression analysis across six models for medical and dental students. The article identifies "the number of times of eating in the canteens, the times of visiting the library, and physical fitness" as the three most significant factors for academic performance.

> "The factors that impact academic performance were examined, and finally, three factors were found to be the most significant: the number of times of eating in the canteens, the times of visiting the library, and physical fitness."
