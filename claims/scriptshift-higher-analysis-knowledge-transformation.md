## Evidence

### Momin N. Siddiqui 2025

Momin N. Siddiqui, Vryan Feliciano, Roy Pea, and Hari Subramonyam. (2025). AI in the Writing Process: How Purposeful AI Support Fosters Student Writing. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_14](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_14)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis tests followed by pairwise Mann-Whitney U tests on coded essays from the three-condition RCT (30 participants per condition) revealed significant differences in Analysis scores across conditions (H = 8.72, p = .013), with "Script&Shift participants displayed significantly higher Analysis performance" than both comparison conditions.

> "Subsequent pairwise comparisons showed that Script&Shift participants displayed significantly higher Analysis performance compared to both Standard (U = 236.50, p = .007) and chat-based conditions (U = 271.50, p = .020)."
