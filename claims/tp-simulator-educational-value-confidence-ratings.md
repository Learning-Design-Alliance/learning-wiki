## Evidence

### Ong Kelly 2023

Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-023-04387-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Educational value was rated by the 7 validation-study participants on a 1–5 Likert scale. The abstract reports "85.7% of the participants strongly agree that the simulator improved their confidence in TP biopsies" and 100% of experts agreed simulator training would be beneficial. Ratings for improving TP biopsy skills were 4.57/5 and transferable skills 4.43/5.

> "The participants rated 4.86 out of 5 (range 4–5) in improving their confidence in performing this procedure. They also rated 4.86 out of 5 (range 4–5) on the benefit of introducing this simulator into TP biopsy training."
