## Evidence

### Zucca A 2024

Zucca A, Bryant J, Purse J, Szwec S, Sanson-Fisher R, Leigh L, Richer M, Morrison A. (2024). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Advanced Technology Clinical Simulation Manikins in Improving the Capability of Australian Paramedics to Deliver High-Quality Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation: Pre- and Postintervention Study. JMIR Cardio. https://doi.org/10.2196/49895

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive results showed ECC scores "highly skewed toward a maximal score of 100%" with mean overall compression score of 95 (SD 13.23) or above across all three time points; the authors interpret this ceiling as limiting sensitivity to improvement.

> "The high baseline performance (ceiling effect) of paramedics in this sample may have prevented the potential increase in skills and performance."
