# Interleaved practice improves delayed posttest performance compared with blocked practice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Interleaved practice led to less time and fewer trials needed in the posttest than blocked practice. [→ Ines Zeitlhofer 2026](#ines-zeitlhofer-2026)

## Evidence

### Ines Zeitlhofer 2026

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach. (2026). Germane processes moderate the effectiveness of complex desirable difficulty tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09807-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

The same MANOVA on posttest time and trials showed a main effect of desirable difficulty (η² = 0.09): "interleaved practice leads to less time" and fewer trials than blocked practice in the delayed posttest.

> "Multivariate results also show a main effect for desirable difficulty (F (2, 127) = 6.52, p = .002; η² = 0.09; Hypothesis 1.2) revealing that interleaved practice leads to less time ( F (1, 128) = 12.64, p < .001, η² = 0.09) and trials needed in the posttest"
