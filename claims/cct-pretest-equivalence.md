### Dağdelen and Kosterelioglu 2015

Orhan Dağdelen, İlker Kösterelioğlu. (2015). Effect of Conceptual Change Texts for Overcoming Misconceptions in “People and Management” Unit. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(1), 99-112. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078838

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on PMAT pre-test scores of the two intact 4th grade classes (n= 23 each), means Mexp= 10.00 and Mcontrol= 10.22. No effect size is printed for this comparison; the article says it "may point to the fact that both groups had equivalent levels of prior information".

> "Pre-test scores do not show meaningful differences between experimental and control group students ( t(44)= -0.272; p> 0.05)."
