### Medveczky D 2025

Medveczky D, Mitchell A, Leopardi E, Dawson A. (2025). Benefits of a near-peer program from the tutors’ perspective: a survey of Australian junior doctors in a regional teaching program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06762-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 26 NPMT tutors rating perceived improvements on visual analogue scales (0 = no improvement, 100 = strong improvement). "Respondents indicated the strongest self-perceived improvements" in communication and feedback delivery; weakest were own ability to learn (60.4) and technical skills (58.2).

> "Respondents indicated the strongest self-perceived improvements in the domains of clinical knowledge (mean 72.4, 95%CI 66.0–78.8), communication (mean 76.2, 95%CI 69.0–83.3) and feedback delivery (mean 75.1, 95%CI 67.9–82.3"
