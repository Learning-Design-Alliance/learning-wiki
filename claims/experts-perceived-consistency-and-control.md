## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mohi Reza 2025

Mohi Reza, Ioannis Anastasopoulos, Shreya Bhandari, Zachary A Pardos. (2025). PromptHive: Bringing Subject Matter Experts Back to the Forefront with Collaborative Prompt Engineering for Educational Content Creation. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3714051](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714051)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of pre- and post-interviews with ten experts; this is a perceived-consistency theme (F3), not a measured hint-quality comparison. Participants attributed human-hint variability to differing teaching styles and fatigue.

> "Several participants (P1, P7, P8 and P9) felt that the AI hints were more consistent than the human-only hints"

### Mohi Reza 2025 (2)

Mohi Reza, Ioannis Anastasopoulos, Shreya Bhandari, Zachary A Pardos. (2025). PromptHive: Bringing Subject Matter Experts Back to the Forefront with Collaborative Prompt Engineering for Educational Content Creation. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3714051](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714051)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview theme (F4) on perceived control. P4 found the model "steerable to an extent" but sometimes stubborn at textbook level, and easier to steer at lesson level; P5 struggled when prompts contained multiple opposing instructions.

> "Many participants (P2, 6, 8, 9, 10) reported that they strongly felt they could steer the output of the LLM and apply their prior subject matter expertise in authoring prompts and evaluating the model output"
