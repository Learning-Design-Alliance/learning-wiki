q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review asserts, citing several studies, that "online genre knowledge can shape readers’ expectations about credibility" and thereby guide credibility evaluations to some extent. This is a theoretical claim with cited empirical support not detailed in this article; no effect size is printed.

"Likewise, online genre knowledge can shape readers’ expectations about credibility and thus guide, at least to some extent, their credibility evaluations (Caruana, 2013; Flanagin & Metzger, 2007; Kiili et al., 2023; List et al., 2017)."
