## Evidence

### Ober J 2018

Ober J, Haubruck P, Nickel F, Walker T, Friedrich M, Müller-Stich B-P, Schmidmaier G, Tanner MC. (2018). Development and validation of an objective assessment scale for chest tube insertion under ‘direct’ and ‘indirect’ rating. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1430-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of construct validity using 'direct' ratings for nine 'advanced' and nine 'none' experience students. The study reports "a significant difference between both groups (p = 0.037)" with medians of 41.0 versus 34.0 points.

> "The median score reached by the ‘advanced’- group in ‘direct’ rating (N = 9) was 41.0 points (I50 = 7.0). The ‘none’-group reached an average score of 34.0 points (I50 = 10.0). There was a significant difference between both groups (p = 0.037)"

### Ober J 2018 (2)

Ober J, Haubruck P, Nickel F, Walker T, Friedrich M, Müller-Stich B-P, Schmidmaier G, Tanner MC. (2018). Development and validation of an objective assessment scale for chest tube insertion under ‘direct’ and ‘indirect’ rating. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1430-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of construct validity using 'indirect' video ratings for the same two experience groups. The study reports the Mann-Whitney U test "also showed significance (p = 0.013)" with medians of 44.0 versus 34.5 points.

> "the participants of the ‘advanced’ group (N = 9) reached a median score of 44.0 points (I50 = 7.3). The ‘none’ group (N = 9) reached 34.5 points (I50 = 7.5). For the difference between both groups Mann-Whitney U test also showed significance (p = 0.013)"
