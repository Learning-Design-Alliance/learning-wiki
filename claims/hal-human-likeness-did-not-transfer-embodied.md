Masum Hasan, Ron Epstein, Thomas Carroll, Ehsan Hoque. (2026). Scalable AI-based clinical communication training and automated assessment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22517 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.22517](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.22517)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Counterbalanced comparison of the two conversational backends within the 59-participant embodied study. "Measured 3E performance did not differ between the models", despite participants more often judging GPT-4o-mini (54%) more natural and human-like than HAL (22%).

> "Measured 3E performance did not differ between the models: mean overall scores were 0.437 for GPT-4o-mini and 0.438 for HAL (difference, 0.001; 95% CI,−0.034–0.036; one-sidedp= 0.481)."
