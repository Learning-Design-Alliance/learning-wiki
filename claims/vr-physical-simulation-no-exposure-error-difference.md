## Evidence

### Rowe David 2023

Rowe David, Garcia Alejandra, Rossi Benito. (2023). Comparison of virtual reality and physical simulation training in first‐year radiography students in South America. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.639

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statistical comparison of the two matched cohorts on the exposure criterion, which evaluated selection of SID, kVp and mAs. The printed F ratio of 0.074 with P = 0.785 supports "No significant difference in frequency of exposure errors between cohorts"; means were 1.006 (VR) versus 1.012. The authors note this was a difficult criterion to measure because many exposure errors still produce a diagnostic digital image.
