# Motivational climate towards sport is positively associated with engagement in physical activity, more strongly for task climate

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Task climate is positively associated with physical activity engagement in males (r = 0.336) and females (r = 0.238). [→ Castro-Sánchez M. 2019](#castro-sanchez-m-2019)
`q2 i1` Ego climate is also positively associated with physical activity engagement (males r = 0.198; females r = 0.089), though more weakly. [→ Castro-Sánchez M. 2019](#castro-sanchez-m-2019)

## Evidence

### Castro-Sánchez M. 2019

Castro-Sánchez M., Zurita-Ortega F., García-Marmol E., Chacón-Cuberos R. (2019). Motivational Climate in Sport Is Associated with Life Stress Levels, Academic Performance and Physical Activity Engagement of Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16071198

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Male structural model of the cross-sectional SEM shows both climate paths to physical activity engagement as "positive and direct", task climate r = 0.336 and ego climate r = 0.198, both p < 0.001; female values were r = 0.238 and r = 0.089.

> "significant associations are observed (p < 0.001) in the relationships examined between motivational climate and engagement in physical activity, these being positive and direct, as much in the case of task climate (r = 0.336) as in the case of ego climate (r = 0.198)"
