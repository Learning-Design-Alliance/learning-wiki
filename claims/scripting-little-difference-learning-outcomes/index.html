Evidence¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2010¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2010). A prism of CSCL research. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-010-9087-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, citing Haake and Pfister, a semester-long computer-science college course on the CURE platform comparing a scripted condition (technology-assigned phase leadership, role-specific interfaces) with an unscripted control group free to self-organize. No statistics are printed here.

"Despite this significant difference in scripting, little difference in learning outcomes is measured, suggesting to the authors that the use of scripting is secondary to the way that tasks are defined"
