## Evidence

### Jarc Anthony M. 2017

Jarc Anthony M., Curet Myriam J. (2017). Viewpoint matters: objective performance metrics for surgeon endoscope control during robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5090-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis in the Results section computing linear correlation coefficients between each camera metric and completion time while including new, intermediate, and experienced surgeon data, with Student's t tests for significance (p < 0.05).

> "CompTime was significantly correlated with CFrq in 21/25 exercises, CDur in 21/25 exercises, and CInt in 20/25 exercises. Pick and Place and Scaling did not correlate with CompTime for any camera metrics."

### Jarc Anthony M. 2017 (2)

Jarc Anthony M., Curet Myriam J. (2017). Viewpoint matters: objective performance metrics for surgeon endoscope control during robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5090-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis between camera metrics and the dVSS overall score across the same 25 exercises and 39 surgeons, reported in the Results section with printed correlation coefficients and p values in Tables 1–3.

> "OverallScore was significantly correlated with CFrq in 20/25 exercises, CDur in 19/25 exercises, and CInt in 20/25 exercises. Again, Pick and Place and Scaling did not correlate with OverallScore for any camera metrics."
