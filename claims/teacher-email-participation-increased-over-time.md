## Evidence

### Mike Ross 2014

Mike Ross, Ben Van Dusen, Valerie Otero. (2014). Becoming Agents of Change through Participation in a Teacher-Driven Professional Research Community. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1408.2502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1408.2502)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of group emails in the Streamline to Mastery community, coding sender, date, topic, and whether emails were new threads. The authors interpret the plotted patterns as showing that "teacher participation increased over time" and teacher initiative for leadership grew. No effect size is printed.

> "Figure 2 suggests that teacher participation increased over time, and Figure 3 suggests that the teachers increased their involvement and initiative for leadership."
