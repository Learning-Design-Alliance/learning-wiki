## Evidence

### Bingquan Zhang 2026

Bingquan Zhang, Xiaoxiao Liu, Yuchi Wang, Lei Zhou, Qianqian Xie, Benyou Wang. (2026). Human or LLM as Standardized Patients? A Comparative Study in Medical Education. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1243](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1243)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report questionnaire and survey analysis in the four-week user study; anxiety was rated on a scale where lower scores are better, and the article reports the difference as statistically significant (p < .01) without an effect size.

> "lower learning anxiety during EasyMED sessions than during human SP interactions (mean anxiety score 0.5 vs. 3.2, p < .01). Students reported feeling less concerned about making mistakes and more willing to ask exploratory or repeated questions."
