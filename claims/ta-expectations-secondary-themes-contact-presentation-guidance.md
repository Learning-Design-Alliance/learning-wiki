## Evidence

### Wilson 2025

Wilson, D., & Kardam, N. (2025). What do students expect from teaching assistants (TAs)? Considerations of gender, race, and international status. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334904

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative secondary-theme analysis of the same 1,678-response dataset, situated within Chickering and Gamson's seven principles and Gagné's nine instructional events. The study reports "more contact with TAs (72.2% of the 789 responses coded as interactions)"; presentation of content drew 50.5% and guidance 41.7% of preparation responses.

> "Of the five secondary themes identified within interactions, students most frequently called for more contact with TAs (72.2% of the 789 responses coded as interactions)."
