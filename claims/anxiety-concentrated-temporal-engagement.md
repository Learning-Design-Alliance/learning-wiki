### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Group 2 temporal analysis of anxiety: "a higher weekend log ratio" among anxious students (0.491 vs 0.401, d=0.798), with positive Spearman associations between BAI score and weekend log ratio (ρ=0.501, p=0.0016) and anxiety status and weekend log ratio (ρ=0.363).

> "Anxious students had a higher weekend log ratio than non-anxious students, with mean values of 0.491 versus 0.401, corresponding to a mean difference of 0.090 and a percentage difference of 22.46% (p=0.0292, d=0.798)."

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (2)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Group 1 analysis: anxiety was "associated with greater concentration of LMS activity in the afternoon" (191.28 vs 143.87, d=0.705; afternoon ratio d=0.663), and anxious students had higher average session duration (6,949.72 vs 5,529.07, d=0.577).

> "anxious students had higher afternoon log counts than non-anxious students, with mean values of 191.28 versus 143.87, corresponding to a mean difference of 47.41 and a percentage difference of 32.95% (p=0.0084, d=0.705)."
