`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review-level moderator analysis of training schedule across included studies: spaced-out simulator sessions spread over more than 6 months did not significantly reduce complication rates, while concentrated programs of more than 30 h over several weeks did.

> "Considering the entire surgical procedure, spaced-out sessions on a virtual reality simulator (i.e., spread over more than 6 months [20, 21]) did not significantly reduce complication rates, while studies reporting high minimal training duration (> 30 h) concentrated in a shorter period of time (several weeks) did find such a reduction [24, 26, 29]."
