## Evidence

### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 54 ophthalmology graduate students assigned to experimental (n=27) or control (n=27) groups. The initial assessment after the MOOC self-learning phase showed the experimental group "scored significantly higher than the control group for both modules" with larger differences in Refractive Classroom.

> "On the initial assessment (Test 1), the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group for both modules (Refractive Classroom: t = 4.10, P < 0.001; Amblyopia Classroom: t = 3.20, P = 0.002)."

### Yang K 2026 (2)

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-class assessment (Test 2) administered on Day 6 of the internship. The experimental group "performed better in both modules, with larger effects in the Refractive Classroom," consistent with the hands-on skill focus of that module.

> "In post-class tests, significant differences were observed for the Refractive Classroom (t = 5.74, P < 0.001) and Amblyopia Classroom (t = 2.67, P = 0.01). The experimental group performed better in both modules, with larger effects in the Refractive Classroom."

### Yang K 2026 (3)

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Final examination administered one week after course completion. The experimental group "scored significantly higher in the Refractive Classroom" and Amblyopia Classroom, indicating sustained knowledge retention beyond the immediate intervention period.

> "In the final exam, the experimental group scored significantly higher in the Refractive Classroom (t = 3.64, P < 0.001) and Amblyopia Classroom (t = 2.33, P = 0.02), with a larger difference in the Refractive Classroom."
