## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2018

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group comparison of the three tests in the EBM group (n = 39) after the course, using the Friedman test with post hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; the tests "substantially differed" in post-test percent-of-maximum scores (P < 0.001).

> "Regarding the estimates of post-test EBM knowledge expressed as percentage of maximum possible score on a scale, the tests also substantially differed (Friedman test, P < 0.001; post hoc tests P ≤ 0.036)."
