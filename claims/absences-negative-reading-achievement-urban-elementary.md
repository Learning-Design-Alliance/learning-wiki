## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2011

Michael A. Gottfried. (2011). The Detrimental Effects of Missing School: Evidence from Urban Siblings. American Journal of Education 117(2). https://doi.org/10.1086/657886

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Baseline value-added education production function regressions on the Philadelphia sibling sample (n = 6,048 in reading models), with student, neighborhood, teacher, and classroom covariates and grade, year, and school fixed effects. The article reports the effect sizes on absences "hover around ⫺0.07j in all models in table 3."

> "a one standard deviation increase in the number of absences is related to a statistically significant decline in reading test performance, holding all else constant."
