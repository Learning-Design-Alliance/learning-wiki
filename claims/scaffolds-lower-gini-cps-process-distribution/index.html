Evidence¶

Parks 2026¶

Parks, T., Shehab, S., & Mercier, E. (2026). Investigating Students' Collaborative Problem-Solving Interactions during Scaffolded and Non-Scaffolded Ill-Structured Tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09816-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on Gini coefficients for 11 groups across two tasks. The Gini coefficient measures deviation of the four-process distribution from a predetermined ideal (0.5 P3, 0.25 P4, 0.125 each P1 and P2). "a main effect of task, F(1,10) = 24.94, p =.001, η2 = 0.715" indicates less deviation from the ideal on scaffolded tasks.

"the groups had lower average Gini coefficients in the scaffolded task (M = 0.191, SD = 0.072) than in the non-scaffolded task (M = 0.337, SD = 0.085). A repeated measures analysis of variance was conducted, which indicated a main effect of task, F(1,10) = 24.94, p =.001, η2 = 0.715."
