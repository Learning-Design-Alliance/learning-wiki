### Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Gender-specific comparison across the two treatments, with single-gender groups formed because gender is correlated with spatial ability: in the test treatment results favored female groups on 4 of 6 factors, whereas in the control treatment male groups did better on 5 of 6 factors. The article concludes female students performed better using the AR platform. Descriptive comparison; no statistical test is printed.

> "in the second treatment (test group), in 4 (1 primary and all 3 secondary) out of 6 (primary and secondary) factors, the results were in favor of female groups, suggesting that female groups did a better job using the AR platform in comparison to male groups."
