Improving Tanzanian student nurse-midwives’ knowledge, skills and attitude in postpartum hemorrhage prevention through simulation-based education: a pre-post quasi experimental study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-025-07590-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression in the same quasi-experiment (N = 55), knowledge adjusted for age; p < 0.001 for both domains. The article reports adjusted mean differences with 95% CIs but no standardized effect size.

> "After adjusting for age, multiple regression analysis showed that knowledge and skills scores were significantly higher at endline compared to the baseline, with an adjusted mean difference (AβC) of 11.7(95% CI: 11.0–12.5) and (AβC) of 13.9(95% CI: 12.7–15.1)"
