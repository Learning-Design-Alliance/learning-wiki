## Evidence

### Elizabeth R. Kazakoff 2018

Elizabeth R. Kazakoff, Paul Macaruso, Pam Hook. (2018). Efficacy of a blended learning approach to elementary school reading instruction for students who are English Learners. Education Tech Research Dev 66:429–449. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9565-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RMANOVA on 68 students in grades 2–5 (38 ELs, 30 non-ELs) assessed on R-CBM at four time points across two school years. The overall effect of time was significant (F(2.539,162.513) = 34.299, p < 0.001); group effects were not significant; no standardized effect size for the growth contrast is printed.

> "The overall effect of EL status (F(1,64) = 0.000, p = 0.996; f = 0.00) and the interaction between time and EL status (Greenhouse–Geisser adjusted F(2.539,162.513) = 0.887, p = 0.435; f = 0.12) were not significant, indicating that ELs and non-ELs grew at a similar pace over the Year Two study."
