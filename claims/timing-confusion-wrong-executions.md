## Evidence

### Kremser Wolfgang 2021

Kremser Wolfgang, Kranzinger Stefan, Bernhart Severin. (2021). Design and Implementation of a Gesture-Aided E-Learning Platform. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s21238042

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the labeled videos and free-text comments from nine of nineteen participants, analyzed by small-scale inductive content analysis (Table 1). The authors state Wrong executions "seemed to stem from the participant not being aware that they should imitate at that moment"; this is an interpretation, not a tested result.

> "Furthermore, the system could have better indicated the right time to imitate the gesture, especially as most of the GEC executions rated “Wrong” seemed to stem from the participant not being aware that they should imitate at that moment."
