Evidence¶

Singh 2023¶

Singh, H.; Tayarani-Najaran, M.-H.; Yaqoob, M. (2023). Exploring Computer Science Students' Perception of ChatGPT in Higher Education: A Descriptive and Correlation Study. Educ. Sci. 2023, 13, 924. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13090924

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Pearson correlation analysis of scaled answers (Questions 2–7) from 430 students, reported in the Discussion. The correlation between academic use and language-related use was r = 0.61 with p-value 6.7 × 10−44, which the authors call "the strongest and most significant correlation observed among the data".

"At 0.61, with the p-value 6.7 × 10−44, this is the strongest and most significant correlation observed among the data."
