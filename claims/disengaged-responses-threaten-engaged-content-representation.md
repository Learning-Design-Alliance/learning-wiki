# Disengaged student responses raise the issue of whether engaged responses maintain the intended content representation

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` When students give disengaged responses to some test items, the degree to which the remaining engaged responses maintain the intended content representation becomes an open validity question. [→ Steven Wise 2020](#steven-wise-2020)

## Evidence

### Steven Wise 2020

Steven Wise. (2020). The impact of test-taking disengagement on item content representation. Applied Measurement in Education, 33(2), 83-94. https://doi.org/10.1080/08957347.2020.1732386

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r?`

This is the article's framing statement, offered as a conceptual argument rather than a tested result: disengaged responses to some items "raises the issue of the degree to which the set of engaged responses maintain the intended content representation."

> "Sometimes, however, students give disengaged responses to some of their test items, which raises the issue of the degree to which the set of engaged responses maintain the intended content representation."
