Evidence¶

Calvo Javier Sanz 2026¶

Calvo Javier Sanz, Renghea Mariana Alina, García-Madrid Mercedes Gómez del Pulgar. (2026). Assessment of Mental Health Nurses’ Competences: A Scoping Review. Actas Españolas de Psiquiatría. https://doi.org/10.62641/aep.v54i3.2069

q2 · i? · review · r3

Synthesis of a scoping review covering 23 studies (six systematic reviews and 17 primary studies, none randomized) with over 17,500 participants. The review reports that "educational interventions and assessment tools play a critical role in enhancing" nurse competences; no pooled effect size is printed.

"Across the twenty-three studies reviewed, evidence consistently demonstrated that educational interventions and assessment tools play a critical role in enhancing the knowledge, skills and attitudes of mental health nurses in key areas of practice."
