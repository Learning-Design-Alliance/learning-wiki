# Clinicians and engineers differ only on postural discomfort and ease of aligning the instrument to AR viewfinders

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Clinicians reported a neutral opinion on postural discomfort (median 3) while engineers disagreed that they experienced it (median 4, p=0.029), and clinicians rated aligning the surgical instrument to the AR viewfinders neutral (median 3) while engineers strongly agreed it was easy (median 5, p=0.023). Mann–Whitney U comparison of Likert responses between engineers and clinicians in the twenty-participant qualitative study; only items J (postural discomfort, p=0.029) and N (viewfinder alignment, p=0.023) differed significantly, with the medians as printed in Table 3.

> "Results show no statistically significant differences in answering tendencies between engineers and clinicians with an exception for the postural discomfort during the application and the ease of aligning the surgical instrument to the AR viewfinders."
