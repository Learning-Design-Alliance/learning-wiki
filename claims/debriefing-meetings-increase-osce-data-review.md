## Evidence

### Bernard Aaron W. 2017

Bernard Aaron W., Ceccolini Gabbriel, Feinn Richard, Rockfeld Jennifer, Rosenberg Ilene, Thomas Listy, Cassese Todd. (2017). Medical students review of formative OSCE scores, checklists, and videos improves with student-faculty debriefing meetings. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1324718

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Generalized estimating equations (binomial, logit link) compared review rates for OSCEs with versus without a debriefing meeting across 792 and 355 review opportunities. Table 1 reports combined video review rising from 28% to 92% (z = 16.34, p <.001, odds ratio 30.75), scores 64% to 88%, checklists 42% to 66%, all p <.001.

> "Frequency of review significantly improved when dedicated student-faculty debriefing meetings were scheduled"
