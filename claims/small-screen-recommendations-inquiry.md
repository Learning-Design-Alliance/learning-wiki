# Small-screen design recommendations were explicated through an inquiry in which teachers and students interacted with learning objects on handheld devices

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The author's small-screen recommendations emerged from an inquiry in which participants including school teachers interacted with learning objects on HP iPAQ handhelds, followed by progressive redesign, student observation, and interviews. [→ Daniel Churchill 2011](#daniel-churchill-2011)

## Evidence

### Daniel Churchill 2011

Daniel Churchill. (2011). Conceptual Model Learning Objects and Design Recommendations for Small Screens. Educational Technology & Society, 14(1), 203-216. https://www.worldcat.org/issn/1436-4522

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A qualitative design-inquiry in which learning objects demonstrating various modalities and interaction kinds were used with teachers on handheld devices to elicit small-display design issues; objects were then progressively redesigned in consultation with teachers and used by students, whose use was observed and followed by interviews. No effect sizes are reported.

> "In the earlier stage of the study, participants (including a number of school teachers) were engaged in interaction with a number of learning objects via handheld devices (HP iPAQ rw6828 Multimedia Messenger)."
