Evidence¶

Zhanyong Qi 2026¶

Zhanyong Qi, Jiayi Wang, Yue Du, Xinyue Zhang. (2026). The effect of blended learning approaches on vocational school students' practical skills performance and learning behaviors: virtual simulations or hands-on activities while accessing instructional videos. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09793-4

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Lag sequential analysis (GSEQ 5.1, z > 1.96) of coded learning behaviors across the four conditions; video review groups showed more significant sequences (14 and 22) than non-video groups (3 and 4).

"Without video review, the virtual simulation group exhibited 4 significant behavior sequences, compared to 3 in the hands-on activity group. The hands-on activity group showed 14 important behavior sequences, whereas the virtual simulation group showed 22 with video review."

Zhanyong Qi 2026 (2)¶

Zhanyong Qi, Jiayi Wang, Yue Du, Xinyue Zhang. (2026). The effect of blended learning approaches on vocational school students' practical skills performance and learning behaviors: virtual simulations or hands-on activities while accessing instructional videos. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09793-4

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Sequential analysis of behavior transitions: the hands-on with video review group showed significant erroneous-operation-to-correct-operation (EO→CO) transitions, while the virtual simulation group showed video-control sequences (PL→PS, PL→FF, FF→PL) indicating fast-paced video operations.

"Notably, the hands-on activity group showed significantly greater performance in the behavior sequences of EO→CO, suggest-ing that integrating hands-on tasks with video review facilitates deeper knowledge acquisi-tion."
