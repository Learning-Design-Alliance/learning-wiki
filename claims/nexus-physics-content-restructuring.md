`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of the content changes made in the NEXUS/Physics two-semester sequence, positioned after biology and chemistry prerequisites. Topics were filtered by coherence with biology and chemistry classes and by whether they "provide insight into a central and compelling biologically authentic problem"; projectile motion, universal gravitation, heat engines, magnetism, and relativity were cut back severely or eliminated.

> "It extends the discussion of energy to include interatomic potentials and chemical reactions, the discussion of thermodynamics to include enthalpy and Gibbs free energy, and includes a serious discussion of random vs. coherent motion including diffusion."
