### Næss S. Ø. 2026

Næss S. Ø., Sivertsen B., Hysing M., Dovran A., Tobiassen S., Hafstad G. S., Kjørstad K. (2026). School Functioning Among Norwegian Children With a History of Maltreatment. Journal of Interpersonal Violence. https://doi.org/10.1177/08862605251351669

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analyses of parent-reported data for 192 children aged 6–12 recruited from the Stine Sofie Center between 2021 and 2023 (plus retrospective invites). The article reports the printed percentages above; special education rate is described as higher than Norwegian population rates of 3.8%–10.5%.

> "In total, 15.6% of the children were reported to perform very poorly in at least one subject. Approximately one-fourth (27.1%) received special education, and 10.0% had been absent from school for at least 8 days in the last month."
