The editorial reports that Vosniadou et al., applying for the first time a conceptual change approach distinguishing teacher beliefs "consistent vs. inconsistent with SRL theory", concluded interventions should address opposing beliefs; attributed second-hand.

"the authors conclude that SRL interventions for teachers can become more effective when taking into account (pre-service) teachers' opposing beliefs about SRL as well in order to facilitate conceptual change."
