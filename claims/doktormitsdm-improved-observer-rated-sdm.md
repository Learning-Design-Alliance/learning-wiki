## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Observer ratings of doctors' SDM performance improved from baseline to the fourth consultation (P=.056), and dyad performance improved (P=.065). [→ Kasper J 2017](#kasper-j-2017)

## Evidence

### Kasper J 2017

Kasper J, Liethmann K, Heesen C, Reissmann DR, Geiger F. (2017). Training doctors briefly and in situ to involve their patients in making medical decisions—Preliminary testing of a newly developed module. Health Expectations. https://doi.org/10.1111/hex.12565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-cohort pre-post pretest: 10 doctors each recorded four consultations (N=40); improvement from C1 to C4 was tested with one-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. The article reports statistically significant improvement for "MAPPIN‐Qdoctor: P=.023" and marginal observer-level improvements for doctor and dyad scales; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The communication improved during training from the first to the fourth consultation according to observers rating the performance of doctors (MAPPIN‐Odoctor: P=.056) and doctor–patient–dyads (MAPPIN‐Odyad: P=.065) and to doctor questionnaires rating the dyads’ performance (MAPPIN‐Qdoctor: P=.023)."
