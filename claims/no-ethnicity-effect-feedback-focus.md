# Student ethnicity had no influence on the focus of examiners' feedback, with both Asian and white students receiving more communication-focused than knowledge-focused comments

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yeates Peter 2017

Yeates Peter, Woolf Katherine, Benbow Emyr, Davies Ben, Boohan Mairhead, Eva Kevin. (2017). A randomised trial of the influence of racial stereotype bias on examiners' scores, feedback and recollections in undergraduate clinical exams. BMC Medicine 15:179. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-017-0943-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Content analysis of free-text feedback (Cohen's kappa for focus codings = 0.85) coded segments blind to group and ethnicity; GLM GEE found no ethnicity effect on focus. No effect size was printed.

> "Students' ethnicity had no influence on the focus of feedback, with both groups receiving more feedback comments on communication than on factual knowledge: Asian students, 1.3 (1.2–1.5) vs white students, 1.3 (1.1–1.5), p = 0.87. The interaction of performance by student ethnicity for feedback focus was also non-significant, p = 0.82."
