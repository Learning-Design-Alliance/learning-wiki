## Evidence

### Speck I 2024

Speck I, Merk A, Burkhardt V, O Flayyih, Huber C, Widder A, Everad F, Offergeld C. (2024). Virtual reality cricothyrotomy - a case-control study on gamification in emergency education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05133-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report questionnaire based on the Münster questionnaire for evaluation, completed by the study group after the VR simulation. Percentages agreeing were Q1 96% (n = 71), Q2 97%, Q3 100%, Q5 81% (n = 60), Q6 92% (n = 68); 65% found the simulation realistic while 22% perceived it as unrealistic. These are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

> "The majority of students (Q1: 96%, n = 71) that performed the VR simulation very strongly agreed, strongly agreed or agreed with the statement: “I was able to transfer my theoretical knowledge of performing a cricothyrotomy to the VR simulation”"
