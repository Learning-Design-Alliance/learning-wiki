### Melody Wilson & Jamaal Sharif Matthews 2024

Melody Wilson & Jamaal Sharif Matthews. (2024). Black adolescents' motivation to resist the false dichotomy between mathematics achievement and racial identity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00219-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA of all continuous covariates on school type (with sex verified as unassociated with school type) "showed signi ﬁcant differences across groups" at both waves. Step-down analyses reported CCMI significantly lower in magnet schools at both time points, and stereotyping higher in magnet schools, approaching significance at T1 and reaching significance at T2.

> "The MANOVA showed signi ﬁcant differences across groups, both at T1 (Lawley–Hotelling trace = 0.47, F(4,75) = 8.77, p<0:05) and at T2 (Lawley–Hotelling trace = 0.75, F(4,71) = 13.26, p<0:05)."
