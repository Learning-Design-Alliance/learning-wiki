### Steven C. Pan 2023

Steven C. Pan, Shana K. Carpenter. (2023). Prequestioning and Pretesting Effects: a Review of Empirical Research, Theoretical Perspectives, and Implications for Educational Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09814-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's account of Huelser and Metcalfe (2012, experiment 2), where participants learned pairs via pretesting and reading then rank-ordered technique effectiveness; participants "ranked pretesting as the least effective" despite best memory for pretested pairs.

> "Although pretested word pairs were the best remembered (in the case of semantically related words), participants nevertheless ranked pretesting as the least effective"
