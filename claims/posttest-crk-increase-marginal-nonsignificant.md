## Evidence

### Rosenkränzer 2016

Rosenkränzer, F., Kramer, T., Hörsch, C., Schuler, S., & Rieß, W. (2016). Promoting Student Teachers' Content Related Knowledge in Teaching Systems Thinking: Measuring Effects of an Intervention through Evaluating a Videotaped Lesson. Higher Education Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v6n4p156

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the four-condition quasi-experiment at posttest: means rose from M=1.25 (SD=1.08) to M=1.77 (SD=1.42) on the six-mistake video task, "without any statistical significances F (3,103)=2.34; p=.08". No effect size printed for this test.

> "On average, the student teachers could identify only one of the six mistakes in the video before the intervention; M=1.25 (SD=1.08). After the intervention stud ent teachers found more mistakes M=1.77 (SD=1.42) but without any statistical significances F (3,103)=2.34; p=.08."
