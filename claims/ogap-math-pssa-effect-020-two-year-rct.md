### OGAP Math program description 2024

OGAP Math program description. (2024). Evidence for Learning. https://www.evidenceforlearning.org/ogap-math/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A two-year randomized controlled trial of OGAP included more than 8,000 students, with 3,488 in the two-year student achievement analytic sample, in grades 3–5 across 61 urban schools in and around Philadelphia. The article reports students in OGAP schools "scored significantly higher than peers on the Pennsylvania state math test (PSSA), with an effect size of +0.20 across two years."
