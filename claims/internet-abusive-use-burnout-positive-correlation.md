### Jafari F. 2022

Jafari F., Janatolmakan M., Khubdast S., Azizi S. M., Khatony A. (2022). The Relationship of Internet Abusive Use with Academic Burnout and Academic Performance in Nursing Students. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/2765763

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analytical study of 184 Iranian nursing students recruited by simple random sampling; Pearson correlation analysis showed Internet abusive use correlated positively with academic burnout ("r = 0.305, p < 0.001").
