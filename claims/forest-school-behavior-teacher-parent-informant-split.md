### Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia 2023

Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia. (2023). Psychological Benefits of Attending Forest School for Preschool Children: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09750-4

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

Review's account of Müller et al. (2017), which used the SSRS with parent and teacher forms; teacher ratings favored nature preschool on cooperation (ηp2 = 0.20) and self-control (ηp2 = 0.32), large printed effects.

> "teachers’ ratings reported that children in nature preschool showed higher scores in the cooperation ( p =  < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.20) and self-control (p =  < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.32) domains."
