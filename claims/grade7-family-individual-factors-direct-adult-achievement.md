`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Second structural equation model of LSAY cohort-2 longitudinal data (analyzed via FIML; model fit CFI = 0.993, RMSEA = 0.019, SRMR = 0.012), adjusting for gender, family structure, and ethnicity. The article reports "β = 0.095, 0.105, and 0.253, p < 0.001" for the three grade-7 predictors of adult educational achievement.

> "Figure 2 shows that grade-7 parental support for college education, family SES, and educational expectations of youths significantly and positively predicted educational achievement of youths in adulthood respectively, β = 0.095, 0.105, and 0.253, p < 0.001."
