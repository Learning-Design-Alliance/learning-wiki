# Students' autonomy need satisfaction fully mediates the effect of teacher autonomy support on autonomous motivation and positive academic emotions, but does not mediate the effect on negative emotions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Bootstrap mediation showed a significant indirect effect of teacher autonomy support on autonomous motivation through autonomy need satisfaction, with a non-significant direct effect (full mediation). [→ Wang Guoxia 2025](#wang-guoxia-2025)
`q3 i?` The indirect effect of teacher autonomy support on positive emotions through autonomy need satisfaction was significant, with a non-significant direct effect (full mediation). [→ Wang Guoxia 2025 (2)](#wang-guoxia-2025-2)
`q3 i0` The indirect effect of teacher autonomy support on negative emotions through autonomy need satisfaction was not significant. [→ Wang Guoxia 2025 (3)](#wang-guoxia-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Wang Guoxia 2025

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bias-corrected percentile Bootstrap mediation (5000 resamples) on the 42 valid student datasets. The article reports the indirect effect of "8.63, 95% CI 4.58–13.35" with a non-significant direct effect, concluding autonomy need satisfaction played a full mediating role.

> "the mediating effect of autonomy need satisfaction on the relationship between teachers’ autonomy support group and students’ autonomous motivation was 8.63, 95% CI 4.58–13.35, indicating that the mediating effect was significant."

### Wang Guoxia 2025 (2)

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrap mediation (5000 resamples) for positive academic emotions. The article reports the "indirect effect = 3.97, 95% CI 2.63–5.64" was significant while the direct effect was not, indicating full mediation.

> "Second, the mediating effect of students’ autonomy need satisfaction on positive emotions was significant (indirect effect = 3.97, 95% CI 2.63–5.64). The direct effect was not significant (direct effect = −1.20, 95% CI −2.88, 0.23)."

### Wang Guoxia 2025 (3)

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrap mediation (5000 resamples) for negative academic emotions. The article reports the indirect effect of "−1.46, 95% CI −3.36, 0.33" was not significant, so autonomy need satisfaction did not mediate this path.

> "Finally, the mediating effect of autonomy need satisfaction on negative emotions was not significant (indirect effect = −1.46, 95% CI −3.36, 0.33)."
