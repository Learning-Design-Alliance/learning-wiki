## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 2006

Rod Ellis, Shawn Loewen and Rosemary Erlam. (2006). Implicit and Explicit Corrective Feedback and the Acquisition of L2 Grammar. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060141

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA (pretest as covariate) on the oral imitation test, a measure of implicit knowledge, for 34 low-intermediate learners in recast, metalinguistic, and control groups. "there is on the delayed posttest, F (2, 34) = 7.975, p < .01" with metalinguistic significantly above recast and control.
