## Evidence

### Al-lawama Manar 2023

Al-lawama Manar, Kumwenda Ben. (2023). Decreasing the options’ number in multiple choice questions in the assessment of senior medical students and its effect on exam psychometrics and distractors’ function. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04206-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Timing outcome from the randomized comparison; students required 55 s per item with five options versus 40 s with three, implying about 7 s per option. The article reports this is a larger per-item saving than the 6 s reported by Vegada et al.

> "The mean time students needed to finish the five-option test was 45 min and 49 s, while it was 32 min and 58 s for the three-option test."
