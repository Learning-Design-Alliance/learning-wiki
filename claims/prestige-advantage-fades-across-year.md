`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the quasi-experimental regression comparison across activities (Fig. 7, Table VI), "physics prestige loses power in predicting grades across activities", indicating grade advantages were no longer exclusive to students deemed proficient.

> "Interestingly, physics prestige loses power in predicting grades across activities, thus suggesting that achieving high performance is no longer an exclusive reward for those who are deemed as proﬁcient in high school physics."
