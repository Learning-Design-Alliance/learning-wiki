Using an Experimental Evaluation of Charter Schools to Test Whether Nonexperimental Comparison Group Methods Can Replicate Experimental Impact Estimates. Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/using-an-experimental-evaluation-of-charter-schools-to-test-whether-nonexperimental-comparison-group-methods-can-replicate-experimental-impact-estimates

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The publisher's abstract describes the study's design: it reuses data from "Mathematica's experimental evaluation of charter schools" as a benchmark and tests whether four nonexperimental comparison group approaches reproduce the experimental impact estimates. No results, effect sizes, or replication outcomes are printed in the available text.

"Using data from Mathematica's experimental evaluation of charter schools, this methodological study examines the validity of four different comparison group approaches to test whether these designs can replicate findings from a well-implemented random assignment study."
