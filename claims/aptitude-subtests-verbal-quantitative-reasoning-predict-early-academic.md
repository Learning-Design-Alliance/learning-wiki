## Evidence

### Huang Pin-Hsiang 2024

Huang Pin-Hsiang, Arianpoor Arash, Taylor Silas, Gonzales Jenzel, Shulruf Boaz. (2024). Insights into undergraduate medical student selection tools: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.22

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of aptitude test subtest domains against program outcomes, with printed pooled values d = 0.704 (verbal reasoning), d = 0.643 (quantitative reasoning), and d = 0.276 (interpersonal reasoning) for early program academic results. End-of-program effects were smaller: "abstract reasoning and verbal reasoning had effect sizes of 0.211 ... and 0.305 ... respectively for end of program academic results".

> "Moreover, verbal reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and interpersonal reasoning had effect sizes of 0.704 (95% CI, 0.471 to 0.938), 0.643 (95% CI, 0.328 to 0.958), and 0.276 (95% CI, 0.056 to 0.496), respectively for early program academic results."
