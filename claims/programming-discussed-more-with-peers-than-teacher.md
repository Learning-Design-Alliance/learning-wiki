## Evidence

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Automated topic modelling plus manual labelling of 21378 MOOC forum messages from two instructor-paced Open edX courses. The printed counts show programming messages were more frequent in student-student than teacher-student interaction, as shown in Table 1.

> "Thus, in our data only 3650 messages were labelled Programming in teacher-student communication, while 6231 messages were labelled Programming in student-student communication."

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022 (2)

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of 924 messages from the blended collaborative course (Discord, Trello, GitHub). Table 2 prints 33 programming messages with the teacher versus 195 student-student, supporting that programming talk centred on peers.

> "With the teacher, students discussed such topics as course management and meetups topics (Table 2)."
