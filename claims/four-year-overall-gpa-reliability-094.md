## Evidence

### Bacon 2006

Donald R. Bacon and Beth Bean. (2006). GPA in Research Studies: An Invaluable but Neglected Opportunity. Journal of Marketing Education, Vol. 28 No. 1, April 2006 35-42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0273475305284638

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Internal-consistency analysis (ICC via ANOVA with students as the treatment, combined with the Spearman-Brown formula) of every grade earned by 157 BSBA marketing majors at one university. The study reports "Four-year overall GPA reliability is found to be quite high, at .94".

> "Four-year overall GPA reliability is found to be quite high, at .94. This level well exceeds the .70 level that Nunnally (1978) recommended for the early stages of research"
