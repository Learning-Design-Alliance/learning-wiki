Evidence¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani 2018¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani. (2018). On the Efficiency of Text Production in Vocabulary Learning: An Empirical Study on Iranian GFL-Learners. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n3p176

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test comparing an experimental group (n = 28) that completed all three steps including text production with a control group (n = 25) on the first control test; the EC scored "M = 10.07, SD = 3.16" versus CG M = 7.56, SD = 4.02, t(51) = -2.54, p = .014.

"in the first control test after thirty minutes of the learning phase in the EC (n = 28) higher values (M = 10.07, SD = 3.16) can be seen in comparison with the CG (n = 25, M = 7.56, SD = 4.02), which could be detected as a significant difference between the two groups in the first control test (t (51) = -2.54, p = .014"
