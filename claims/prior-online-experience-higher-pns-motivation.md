## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students with prior experience with online learning scored higher on autonomy, competence, relatedness, intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, and lower on amotivation than students without such experience. [→ Frikha Mohamed et al 2024](#frikha-mohamed-et-al-2024)

## Evidence

### Frikha Mohamed et al 2024

Frikha Mohamed et al. (2024). Towards improving online learning in physical education: Gender differences and determinants of motivation, psychological needs satisfaction, and academic achievement in Saudi students. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297822

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of 152 experienced vs 156 inexperienced online learners in the survey. PNS effects were medium (η2 = 0.416–0.433), intrinsic motivation higher in the experienced group (η2 = 0.382, medium), extrinsic motivation higher (η2 = 0.099, small), and "lower amotivation scores" in the experienced group (η2 = 0.428).

> "Welch’s t-test showed higher perceptions of autonomy, competence, and relatedness in the more experienced group with online learning (p = 0.001, η2 = 0.416, medium; p = 0.001, η2 = 0.433, medium; p = 0.001, η2 = 0.425, medium, respectively)"
