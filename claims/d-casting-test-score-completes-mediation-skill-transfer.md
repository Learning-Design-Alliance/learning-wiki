# The D-Casting module test score completely mediates the effect of instructing method on physical cast scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The direct effect from instructing method to cast scores was not significant, while the indirect and total effects were significant, indicating complete mediation through the D-Casting module test score. [→ Wang YiZhen 2025](#wang-yizhen-2025)
`q2 i?` Instructing method was significantly associated with the D-Casting module test score, which was significantly associated with the scores of the casts. [→ Wang YiZhen 2025 (2)](#wang-yizhen-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wang YiZhen 2025

Wang YiZhen, Li Yi, Ma ChaoYi, Zhang WeiYi, Yuan ZiYa, Zhu Qian, Zhao XiaoHan, Song Jinlin, Yang Sheng, Ji Ping, Pang MengWei. (2025). Effectiveness evaluation of a serious game for dental lost wax casting training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06643-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple mediation analysis with bias-corrected bootstrap (n = 5000) from the 78-participant randomized skill transfer assessment: direct effect 95% CI -3.448 to 2.980, indirect effect 95% CI 3.419 to 12.391, total effect P = 0.004, 95% CI 2.504 to 12.265.

> "the direct effect from the instructing method to the scores of the casts was not significant, but the indirect effect from the instructing method to the scores of the casts and total effect were significant. Therefore, as a mediator, the D-Casting module test score play a complete mediating effect between the instructing method and the score of the casts."

### Wang YiZhen 2025 (2)

Wang YiZhen, Li Yi, Ma ChaoYi, Zhang WeiYi, Yuan ZiYa, Zhu Qian, Zhao XiaoHan, Song Jinlin, Yang Sheng, Ji Ping, Pang MengWei. (2025). Effectiveness evaluation of a serious game for dental lost wax casting training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06643-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Standardized path coefficients from the same simple mediation model (Table 7) fitted on the skill transfer data; both paths were significant. No standardized effect size for the mediation itself is printed.

> "the instructing method was significantly associated with the D-Casting module test score (β = 0.808; t = 3.878; P < 0.001). The D-Casting module test score was significantly associated with the scores of the casts (β = 0.829; t = 11.619; P < 0.001)."
