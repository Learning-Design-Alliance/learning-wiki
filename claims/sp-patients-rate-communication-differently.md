## Evidence

### Kodikara Kaumudee 2023

Kodikara Kaumudee, Seneviratne Thilanka, Premaratna Ranjan. (2023). Pre-clerkship procedural training in venipuncture: a prospective cohort study on skills acquisition and durability. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04722-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Communication was assessed with a modified, translated Communication Assessment Tool by standardized patients after SBPT and by real patients one year later. SPs gave a median of 3.0 ("fair"); "the students were rated as good (median: 4.0; p < 0.01)" by patients.

> "When rated by patients in the clinical setting, the students were rated as good (median: 4.0; p < 0.01)."
