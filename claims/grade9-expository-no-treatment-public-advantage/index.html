Ghada M. Awada & Mar Gutiérrez-Colón 2017¶

Ghada M. Awada & Mar Gutiérrez-Colón. (2017). Effect of Inclusion Versus Segregation on Reading Comprehension of EFL Learners with Dyslexia: Case of Lebanon. English Language Teaching, 10(9), 49. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n9p49

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA of grade 9 expository-text comprehension (67 students, 3 with dyslexia). No treatment effect was detected; equivalence was not tested.
