# GPT-4o prompted only with SRL theory references generates a codebook accurately representing most key elements of both foundational SRL theories

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A theory-only prompt with full references led GPT-4o to propose constructs capturing all three phases of Zimmerman's model and three of four phases of Winne & Hadwin's model. [→ Zambrano 2026](#zambrano-2026)

## Evidence

### Zambrano 2026

Zambrano, A.F., Wei, Z., Zhang, J., Baker, R.S., Ocumpaugh, J., Barany, A., Liu, X., Ginger, J., Paquette, L., Zhou, Y., & Borchers, C. (2026). Data Plus Theory Equals Codebook: Leveraging LLMs for Human-AI Codebook Development. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 18, No 1. https://osf.io/g3z4x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Human evaluation of Step 1 (Theory-only) codebooks generated three times by GPT-4o from references to Winne & Hadwin (1998) and Zimmerman (2000), consolidated by two researchers. The authors report the codebook "accurately represents most of the key elements of both SRL theories", though the task-definition phase was not directly represented.

> "the codebook suggested by GPT based on the SRL foundational theory papers accurately represents most of the key elements of both SRL theories, indicating that the model's knowledge base includes appropriate and accurate representations of these theoretical frameworks."
