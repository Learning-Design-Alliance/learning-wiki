`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Assessor-blinded randomized controlled trial with 32 sixth-semester nursing students (EG n=17, CG n=15) completing a six-stage intervention including eight hours of theory and simulation. Mixed-model ANOVA showed a "significant increase in correct answers from pretest to posttest, irrespective of group", with printed effect size η2g = 0.333. Posttest medians were 15 in both groups.
