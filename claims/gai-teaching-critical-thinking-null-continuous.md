`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of three continuous-variable studies (I2 = 98%) reported in the critical thinking skills section found "no difference in critical thinking skills between the two groups"; the single dichotomous-variable study found a statistical difference (OR = 73.47).

> "The results of the meta-analysis showed no difference in critical thinking skills between the two groups (SMD = 0.82, 95% CI: −0.97–2.61, p = 0.37). However, only the study using dichotomous variables revealed a statistical difference between the two groups (OR = 73.47, 95%CI: 4.23–1276.95, p = 0.003)."
