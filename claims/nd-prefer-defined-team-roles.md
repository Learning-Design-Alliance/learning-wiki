# Neurodivergent students feel most comfortable in teams with defined roles rather than no specific roles

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ND students felt most comfortable when team roles were chosen from an instructor-provided set, independently chosen, or instructor-assigned, over having no specific roles. [→ Cynthia Zastudil 2026](#cynthia-zastudil-2026)

## Evidence

### Cynthia Zastudil 2026

Cynthia Zastudil, Srishty Muthusekaran, Rayhona Nasimova, and Stephen MacNeil. (2026). “I can’t read your mind”: A Study of Neurodivergent Computing Students’ Experiences with Collaborative Active Learning. Proceedings of the 31st ACM Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3803400.3809386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey distributions of comfort with role assignment options. ND students felt most comfortable when roles were "chosen either from a set of instructor- provided roles, independently chosen roles, or instructor-assigned roles over having no specific roles".

> "ND students felt the most comfortable when students had roles that had been chosen either from a set of instructor- provided roles, independently chosen roles, or instructor-assigned roles over having no specific roles."
