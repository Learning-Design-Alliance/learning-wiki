q3 · i? · associational · r2

SEM analysis of pooled data from Studies A, B, and C in the agent-based CDR simulation supports the content-knowledge paths for elicitation and sharing, but the article found the path from content knowledge to hypotheses sharing not statistically significant in either of the two studies where it was > 0.1, so "we conclude that Hypothesis 1c was not supported".

"The path from content knowledge to evidence elicitation was positive and > 0.1 in all three studies, as well as statistically significant in two of them; therefore, we consider Hypothesis 1a supported."
