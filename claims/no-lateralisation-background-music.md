## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A four-way ANOVA with Hemisphere, Group, Background, and Time factors on the EEG data of the main experiment found "none of the interactions of interest" significant; no effect size was printed for this null finding.

> "revealed that none of the interactions of interest (Hemisphere × Group, Hemisphere × Group × Background) turned out to be significant, even if the statistical threshold was lowered to p = 0.10"
