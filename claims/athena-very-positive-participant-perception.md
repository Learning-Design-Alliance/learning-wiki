### Andreas Müller 2020

Andreas Müller, Madeleine Rousset Grenon. (2020). Athéna – A pre-university study programme at the University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478)

Questionnaire evaluation of the three Athena editions, adapted from a questionnaire previously used to evaluate a German early-study programme, with all assessment items on a scale from 1 to 6; the overall view averaged "between 5.5 and 5.7" and "There was no gender difference".

> "Overall, the pupils have a very positive view of their participation in the Athena programme (see Fig. 3, rightmost column), with an average "grade" between 5.5 and 5.7. There was no gender difference regarding this or any of the following points."
