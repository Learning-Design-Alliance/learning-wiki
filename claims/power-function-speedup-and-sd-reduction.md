## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across three lexical decision experiments, mean reaction times and standard deviations both decreased from pre-test to post-test as power functions of the number of presentations, with a common exponent, in one uncontrolled pre-post study. [→ Logan 1988](#logan-1988)

## Evidence

### Logan 1988

Logan, G. D. (1988). Toward an instance theory of automatization. Psychological Review, 95(4), 492-527. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.95.4.492

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Summary of Experiments 1-3, lexical decision tasks with three schedules of repetition. The article reports the co-occurrence constraint between means and standard deviations was "confirmed in each experiment", for words and nonwords alike.

> "The means and standard deviations both decreased as power functions of practice, and the functions had the same exponent. This was confirmed in each experiment."
