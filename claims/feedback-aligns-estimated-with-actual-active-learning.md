### Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth 2017

Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth. (2017). A Vision and Change Reform of Introductory Biology Shifts Faculty Perceptions and Use of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0258

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analyses linking interview estimates to observed ALO for the eight instructors who taught every year: years 1 and 2 were nonsignificant, while the year-3 regression was "significant" (p = 0.001). No effect size was printed.

> "However, a significant regression analysis using the third year of data showed faculty a significant relationship between their estimate of their AL use and their actual AL use, (p = 0.001; Figure 2)."
