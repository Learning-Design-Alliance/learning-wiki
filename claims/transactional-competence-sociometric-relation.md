# The relation between oral communicative competence and sociometric status is expected to be interactional and transactional rather than unidirectional

Educational Psychology Review (2019) 31:699–724. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09479-z

`q1 · i?` · `review · r3`

Authors' interpretation, attributed to Parker et al. (2006): because antecedents and consequents of problematic peer relationships are extremely complex, neither direction alone is correct. This is a theoretical expectation, not a tested result; the review states there is still insufficient empirical evidence to answer the direction question.

> "It is, instead, expected that they are linked in an interactional and transactional manner in which both components are mutually strengthening and continue to influence each other"
