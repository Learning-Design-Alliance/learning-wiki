Qing Xiao 2026¶

Qing Xiao, Wenhan Xie, Ziyu Deng, Ruiwei Xiao, Ziyue Feng, Xie He, Shiyu Zhang, John Stamper, Hong Shen, and Xinying Hou. (2026). Exploring K-12 Teachers' Perceptions of Students' Relationships with AI Companions: Boundaries, Intervention Strategies, and Design Implications. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13479 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.13479

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview finding (RQ2). Perceived harm did not predict intervention: Romantic Partner was feared most yet eight would not get involved, while Study Buddy, which no teacher found concerning, drew the fewest conditional answers.

"Teachers applied three tests in roughly the same order across scenarios: whether the use was visible to them, either because it happened at school or because it had produced effects they could observe there; whether the matter was academic or relational; and whether a safety line had been crossed."
