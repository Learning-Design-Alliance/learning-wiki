## Evidence

### Adrian Madsen 2014

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2014). How physics instruction impacts students' beliefs about learning physics: A meta-analysis of 24 studies. https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6522

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Two-way type 2 ANOVA testing teaching method and pretest score effects on CLASS/MPEX shifts. The article reports "teaching method is the strongest predictor of shift, even when we take into account pretest score."

> "We found that pretest was marginally significant (F(1,45)=2.95, p=.09) and teaching method was highly significant (F(1,45)=36.4, p<.0001). So, teaching method is the strongest predictor of shift, even when we take into account pretest score."
