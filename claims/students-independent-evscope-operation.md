`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observational report of the Cyber-Cosmos summer camp, in which three students practiced telescope collimation and focusing nightly. The article reports that by the second week they could "operate the eVscope by themselves."

> "On the first night it was explained to the students how to collimate, and how to focus the eVscope. Night after night, they repeated these procedures with progressively less intervention from the supervisors. By the start of the second week they were able to operate the eVscope by themselves."
