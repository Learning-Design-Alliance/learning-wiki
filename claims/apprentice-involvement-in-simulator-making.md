### Knobel Roxana 2020

Knobel Roxana, Menezes Mariane de Oliveira, Santos Débora de Souza, Takemoto Maíra Libertad Soligo (2020). Planning, construction and use of handmade simulators to enhance the teaching and learning in Obstetrics. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3684.3302

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of observations from the project and pilot study, reported in the Discussion. They state that assembling a simulator requires putting knowledge of anatomy, obstetrics, surgical technique, and physiology into use, and that no similar data were found in the literature.

> "An unexpected finding of the original project and this study was the involvement of apprentices with the project and handmaking of the simulators, giving suggestions and actually participating in their development."
