## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hermsen 2006

Hermsen, L. M., & Franklin, S. V. (2006). Realizing a New Research Agenda for Writing-to-Learn: Embedding Process in Context. Manuscript submission to Educational Researcher. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609070](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609070)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A single-student case study using key-capture software and S-notation (Fig. 8), with a student not enrolled in Explorations in Physics but of comparable background. The article reports the student paused 30 seconds, deleted "I don't know anything about density," and concluded "this is the same procedure as before, but practiced in a different way." The authors state it would be foolhardy to draw general conclusions from a single case study.

> "The results strongly suggest an evolution in the student's understanding within a single writing episode."
