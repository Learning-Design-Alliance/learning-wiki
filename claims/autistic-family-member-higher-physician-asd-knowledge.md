## Evidence

### Domarecki Patryk 2025

Domarecki Patryk, Plata-Nazar Katarzyna, Sohl Kristin. (2025). An Assessment of the Knowledge of Autism Spectrum Disorder Among Polish Primary Care Physicians. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61040761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 36 physicians with versus 130 without an autistic family member in the survey sample. All four comparisons favored the family-connection group, e.g., general knowledge "11.89 vs. 9.67; p < 0.001".

> "Physicians with an autistic family member present significantly higher levels of general ASD knowledge (11.89 vs. 9.67; p < 0.001), as well as knowledge of ASD etiology, diagnosis, and support (2.74 vs. 2.15, p = 0.007; 3.69 vs. 2.81, p < 0.001; 5.46 vs. 4.72, p < 0.001, respectively)."
