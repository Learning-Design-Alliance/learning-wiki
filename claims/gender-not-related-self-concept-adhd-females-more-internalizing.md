## Evidence

### Houck 2011

Houck, G., Kendall, J., Miller, A., Morrell, P., & Wiebe, G. (2011). Self-concept in children and adolescents with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. Journal of Pediatric Nursing, 26(3), 239-247. [doi:10.1016/j.pedn.2010.02.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pedn.2010.02.004)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r?`

Correlation analysis in the descriptive correlational study of 145 children with ADHD (31 females, 21.4%). The article also states gender was not related to the self-concept score and did not uniquely predict self-concept in regression.

> "a small but significant correlation between gender and internalizing behavior problems, with females yielding higher scores (r = .17, p = .02); gender was not related to externalizing behavior problem scores."
