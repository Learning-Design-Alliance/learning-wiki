Intermediate behavioral outcome of the RCT: appointment scheduling and attendance from administrative records. Table 2 prints differences of 23.8*** (scheduled) and 18.1*** (attended) for workshop versus control and 26.0/20.2 for one-on-one versus control, which the text calls large and statistically significant.

"About 56 percent of students assigned to the workshop group scheduled a workshop appointment, and 48 percent attended one, while about 33 percent of control group students scheduled a counseling appointment, and 30 percent attended one (table 2)."

Related Claims¶

  • Workshop and one-on-one counseling with nudging show no significant difference in session attendance or academic plan completion, though the comparison is exploratory — related
  • Guaranteed one-on-one counseling with nudging increases academic plan completion by about 23 percentage points over control — related
  • Guaranteed workshop counseling with nudging increases academic plan completion by about 20 percentage points over a business-as-usual control group — related
  • Group workshop counseling is the most cost-effective option, with the lowest cost per academic plan completed — related
