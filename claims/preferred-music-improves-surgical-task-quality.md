Oomens P, Fu VX, Kleinrensink GJ, Jeekel J. (2019). The effect of music on simulated surgical performance: a systematic review. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-06868-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of two studies assessing task quality with blinded observers using predefined criteria. The review reports a significant benefit of preferred music on wound repair quality and no significant effect of classical music on knot quality. No effect sizes printed.

> "Wound, repair graded on a 1–5 scale by blinded plastic surgeons, was performed with significantly better quality when participants listened to their preferred music genre [22]. There was no statistically significant effect of classical music on the quality of a laparoscopically tied knot [24]."
