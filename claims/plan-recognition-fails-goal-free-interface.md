### VanLehn 2015

VanLehn, K. (2015). Reflections on Andes' Goal-free User Interface. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0067-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Evaluation of Andes1's Bayesian plan recognition: 40 snapshots of students' work at help requests were shown to physics instructors, who indicated the goal they thought students needed help on. Andes "agreed with the instructors on only 3" of the 21 agreement snapshots; instructors judged most students had no plan.

> "Instructors disagree on 19 snapshots, but in 21 snapshots where they did agree, Andes agreed with the instructors on only 3 (V anLehn et al. 2005)."
