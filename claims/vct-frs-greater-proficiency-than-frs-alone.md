## Evidence

### Nathan Arjun 2022

Nathan Arjun, Patel Sonam, Georgi Maria, Fricker Monty, Asif Aqua, Ng Alexander, Mullins William, Hang Man Kien, Light Alexander, Nathan Senthil, Francis Nader, Kelly John, Collins Justin, Sridhar Ashwin. (2022). Virtual classroom proficiency-based progression for robotic surgery training (VROBOT): a randomised, prospective, cross-over, effectiveness study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01467-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised cross-over study of 11 robotic surgery novices; five in Group A and six in Group B. The between-group comparison at time-point 1 showed "44.80 vs 35.33 points, p = 0.006" in favour of the FRS + VCT group. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "At time-point 1, Group A who received training with both the FRS and VCT sessions achieved a statistically significant greater mean proficiency score compared to Group B who received training under the FRS curriculum alone (44.80 vs 35.33 points, p = 0.006)."
