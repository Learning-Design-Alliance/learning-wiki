## Evidence

### Michael B. Bennett 2024

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-program interview analysis of general STEM affect. Only one QRX student, Mina, expressed she did not feel confident in her ability to succeed in quantum science, mostly because the field remained unfamiliar after participation.

> "11 of the 12 students interviewed expressed unequivocally that they felt they could succeed at their degree and at finding a job when the time came."

### Michael B. Bennett 2024 (2)

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis: three students per program attributed QIS confidence to general self-confidence; the others reported domain confidence for their degree field, and all students reporting change described positive impact from participation.

> "these students all reported that QRX participation positively impacted their confidence, either through exposure to QIS or through establishment of a supportive community."
