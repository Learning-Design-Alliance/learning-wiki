## Evidence

### Kim Kyeng-Jin 2025

Kim Kyeng-Jin, Choi Moon-Ji, Kim MinJi. (2025). Effects of virtual reality-based disaster simulation education on nursing students. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0329563

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-item 0–10 self-report confidence measure in the same quasi-experiment (n=67). The post-pre difference was "1.97 ± 1.94 in the experimental group 0.76 ± 1.72 in the control group", t = 2.69, p = .009. No effect size printed.

> "Disaster triage confidence increased from 5.52 ± 1.25 to 7.48 ± 1.18 in the experimental group and from 5.18 ± 1.24 to 5.94 ± 0.95 in the control group. The post-pre difference was 1.97 ± 1.94 in the experimental group 0.76 ± 1.72 in the control group, with a statistically significant difference between groups (t = 2.69, p = .009)."
