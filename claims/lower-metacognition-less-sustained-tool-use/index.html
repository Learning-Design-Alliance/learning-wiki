Evidence¶

Eelco Braad 2022¶

Eelco Braad, Nick Degens, Wolmet Barendregt, Wijnand IJsselsteijn. (2022). Improving metacognition through self-explication in a digital self-regulated learning tool. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10156-2

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Comparison of completers with withdrawers in the quasi-experiment. Table 6 shows withdrawers' tool use was significantly less frequent (d = .912), of shorter duration (d = .166), and with fewer metacognitive activities (d = .192). No significant differences were found for age, years in higher education, need-for-cognition, or self-efficacy.

"withdrawers (N = 72) had significantly lower a priori metacognitive awareness (M = 60.03, SD = 10.64) than completers (M = 64.39, SD = 10.17), t(190) = 2.829, p = .005, d = .422."
