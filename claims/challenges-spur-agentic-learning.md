`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme from template analysis of interviews and debriefs after four acute medical simulations with twelve second-year students. Participants described challenges such as unfamiliar equipment; the study found such stimuli "had the ability to spur learners to agentially think, and act, for themselves."

> "Whilst such problematics could impede their immediate progress, these stimuli had the ability to spur learners to agentially think, and act, for themselves."
