Qualitative analysis of a recorded fishbowl protocol from a humanities teacher whose students asked how to produce white light using a white bulb and coloured filters; overlapping all gel sheets blocked the light. The teacher "did not know how to answer the students' questions" and the question was left unresolved.

> "The vital evidence of this documentation is twofold: Tinkering sparked an essential and profound research question, and the teacher recognised her inadequacy in embracing this question."
