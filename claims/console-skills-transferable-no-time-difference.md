## Evidence

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossover pilot trial at the International Medical Robotics Academy with ten participants completing four Mimic simulation exercises on two consoles in counterbalanced order; the authors report no statistically significant differences in times between the groups, with the exception of one exercise.

> "Thread the rings 2 is the only exercise that shows discriminating power with all other exercises showing no significant difference for time to pass or time to proficiency metrics."
