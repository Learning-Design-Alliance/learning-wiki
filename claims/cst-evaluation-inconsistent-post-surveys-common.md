`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Frequency analysis of evaluation presence and focus across the 134 included papers. The review reports n = 61 (46%) described an evaluation, with the largest proportion (n = 19; 31%) aimed at understanding learners' general experiences, perceptions, needs, and/or preferences.

> "Nearly one-third of papers selected for this review described an evaluation of an Indigenous cultural safety training intervention (n = 61; 46%)."

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Frequency analysis of evaluation methodologies. The review reports mixed methods in n = 32 (52%), qualitative only in n = 20 (33%), quantitative only in n = 9 (15%), with post-surveys the most common collection method (two-thirds of relevant articles) and pre- and post-surveys the most common overall approach.

> "just over half of relevant articles described utilizing both qualitative and quantitative methodologies (n = 32; 52%)."
