## Evidence

### Dahn J 2018

Dahn J, Presseau J, ElChamaa R, Naumann DN, Mascaro C, Luconi F, Smith KM, Kitto S. (2018). Barriers and Facilitators to Self-Directed Learning in Continuing Professional Development for Physicians in Canada: A Scoping Review. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000002237

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

TDF-guided directed coding across the 17 included articles coded barriers and facilitators into 12 TDF domains; the review reports the printed frequencies above and identifies the four key domains from these counts.

> "In total, the barriers and facilitators were coded 318 times in the 17 articles: Barriers were coded 133 times (41.8%), and facilitators were coded 185 times (58.2%). They were most frequently coded in the environmental context and resources domain (86 times, 27.0%), followed by the social influences (73 times, 23.0%), beliefs about consequences (28 times, 8.8%), and behavioral regulation (22 times, 6.9%) domains."
