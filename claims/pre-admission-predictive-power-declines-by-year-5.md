## Evidence

### Sartania Nana 2014

Sartania Nana, McClure John D, Sweeting Helen, Browitt Allison. (2014). Predictive power of UKCAT and other pre-admission measures for performance in a medical school in Glasgow: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mutually adjusted regression analyses of years 1 and 5 outcomes (Additional files 3 and 4) in the Glasgow cohort: all three measures predicted year 1 written exam, none predicted year 1 Medical Independent Learning Exercise, only TSS predicted year 5 written exam, and "no pre-admissions measure was associated with the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)".

> "There were almost no significant associations between any pre-admissions measure and the year 5 exam results."
