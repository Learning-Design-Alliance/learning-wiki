# The selected sample shows higher examination performance (ES = .23) than Year grade difference (ES = .06), indicating standardized examinations discriminate achievement better than teacher grades

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The selected sample scored higher on both Year grade (ES = .06) and Year 9 State Examination (ES = .23), with the examination difference much larger. [→ Likhanov 2021](#likhanov-2021)

## Evidence

### Likhanov 2021

Likhanov, M. V., Tsigeman, E. S., Papageorgiou, K. A., Akmalov, A. F., Sabitov, I. A., & Kovas, Y. V. (2021). Ordinary extraordinary: Elusive group differences in personality and psychological difficulties between STEM‐gifted adolescents and their peers. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12349

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANOVAs on achievement measures: Year grade for the full samples and a separate ANOVA for Examination on the reduced subsample (447 unselected, 363 selected who had taken the exam). Table 3 prints partial η² = .055 for Year grade and .227 for Examination.

> "Our study also showed that the observed sample differences were greater for the Year 9 State Examination than for the Year grade (.23 vs. .06, respectively)."
