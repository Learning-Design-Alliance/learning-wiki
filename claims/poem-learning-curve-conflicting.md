## Evidence

### Yang D. 2020

Yang D., Wagh M.S., Draganov P.V. (2020). The status of training in new technologies in advanced endoscopy: from defining competence to credentialing and privileging. Gastrointestinal Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2020.05.047

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of the POEM learning-curve literature: it reports the discrepancy as second-hand and notes many studies used animal models or single-center, single-operator designs rather than studies designed to assess training and competency.

> "there is a wide discrepancy in the learning curve plateau reported, ranging from 7 to 100 cases"

### Yang D. 2020 (2)

Yang D., Wagh M.S., Draganov P.V. (2020). The status of training in new technologies in advanced endoscopy: from defining competence to credentialing and privileging. Gastrointestinal Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2020.05.047

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The review reports this animal-model study as cited evidence informing the Japan Gastroenterological Endoscopy Society guideline discussion; it is a preclinical swine-model result, not a human competency study.

> "in a single-center prospective study using both ex vivo and live swine models, mastery, defined as the absence of intraprocedural adverse events and a plateau in procedural time, was achieved by trainees after 26 cases"
