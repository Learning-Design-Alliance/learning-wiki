## Evidence

### Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell 2014

Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell. (2014). A comparison of integrated testlet and constructed-response question formats. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inter-rater reliability analysis of CR questions scored in duplicate: midterm by two professors without a shared rubric, final by two paid senior undergraduate students (with rubrics for two of six questions). Table II shows mean differences from -6 to +15 percentage points and difference standard deviations of 11–17 percentage points.

> "we ﬁnd bias between raters as high as 15 percentage points for paid student scorers for these more traditional physics “problems”"
