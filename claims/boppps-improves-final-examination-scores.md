## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Pooled analysis of 15 studies (1198 BOPPPS, 1122 control participants) found significantly higher final examination scores in the BOPPPS group (SMD 1.14, 95% CI 0.84–1.43, P < 0.001), a large effect by Cohen's guidelines. [→ Zhu Jing 2025](#zhu-jing-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhu Jing 2025

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 15 studies comparing BOPPPS with traditional teaching in undergraduate clinical and health disciplines. The pooled effect size was SMD = 1.14 (95% CI 0.84–1.43), which the authors interpret as "a large effect" under Cohen's guidelines; a prediction interval of [0.35, 1.93] was reported.

> "Significant improvement in final examination scores was noted within the BOPPPS group when compared to the control group (SMD 1.14, 95% CI 0.84–1.43; P < 0.001; Fig. 3) with heterogeneity of 91% (P < 0.001)."
