## Evidence

### Coyne 2019

Coyne, M. D., McCoach, D. B., Ware, S., Austin, C. R., Loftus-Rattan, S. M., & Baker, D. L. (2019). Racing Against the Vocabulary Gap: Matthew Effects in Early Vocabulary Instruction and Intervention. Exceptional Children, 85(2), 163–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918789162

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Multilevel moderation analysis of the efficacy and replication studies using the cross-level interaction between group-mean-centered fall PPVT and treatment. The article reports the differential effect sizes at one SD below, at, and one SD above the mean fall PPVT score.

> "Although the overall mean effect size for the impact of the intervention was 1.07, for students with fall PPVT scores 5 points below the mean, the effect size was .92; and for students with fall PPVT scores 5 points above the mean, the effect size was 1.21."
