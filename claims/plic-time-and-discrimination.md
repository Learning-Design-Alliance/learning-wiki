## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, C. Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Quantifying critical thinking: Development and validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis of valid closed-response responses (median completion 16.0 ± 0.4 min pre, 11.5 ± 0.3 min post). Ferguson's δ was 1 for both surveys; question-test correlations exceeded 0.38 (pre) and 0.42 (post), above the accepted r ≥ 0.20 threshold.

> "The correlation between total PLIC score and time to completion is r < 0.03 for both the pre- survey and the post-survey, suggesting that there is no relationship between a student's score on the PLIC and the time they take to complete the assessment."
