Evidence¶

James Soland 2021¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th grade: The role of social-emotional learning trajectories. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12463

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Growth-model analysis of longitudinal SEL data from a large urban district (about 3,000 students per timepoint) examining self-management trajectories. The article reports that "growth for self-management is also predictive above and beyond status on that construct"; no effect size is printed.
