Graham LA 2023¶

Graham LA, Gray C, Wagner TH, Illarmo S, Hawn MT, Wren SM, Iannuzzi J, Harris AHS. (2023). Applying cognitive task analysis to health services research. Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6773.14106

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

The article reports, citing Sullivan et al., that expert interviews are often incomplete, resulting in biased or incomplete information about the knowledge required to complete a task. No effect size is printed; the article offers this as motivating background for CTA.

"Many miss up to 70% of the subtasks (or “steps”) needed to describe a specific cognitive task for teaching purposes."
