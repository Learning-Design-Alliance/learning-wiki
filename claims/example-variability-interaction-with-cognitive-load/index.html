Evidence¶

Atkinson 2000¶

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Paas and Van Merrienboer, a four-group experiment (low/high variability by practice/example) with secondary technical school students learning geometry, assessed by a six-item transfer posttest; the review reports 'an interaction' favoring worked-example conditions.

"Consistent with their prediction, they also found an interaction: Students in the worked-example condition benefited more from lesson variability than students in the practice condition."
