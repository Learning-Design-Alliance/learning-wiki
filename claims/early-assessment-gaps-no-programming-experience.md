# Before the ChatGPT-assisted Python module, learners with no programming experience scored significantly lower than peers on early assessments

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On early practice quizzes, learners with no programming experience scored significantly lower than learners with Python or other programming experience. [→ Ian McCulloh 2025](#ian-mcculloh-2025)

## Evidence

### Ian McCulloh 2025

Ian McCulloh, Pedro Rodriguez, Srivaths Kumar, Manu Gupta, Viplove Raj Sharma, Benjamin Johnson, Anthony N. Johnson. (2025). Generative AI in Computer Science Education: Accelerating Python Learning with ChatGPT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.20329](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.20329)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA across 30 assessments for 86 adult learners, group as between-subjects factor. On the conditional-statements quiz the main effect of group was significant, F(2, 114) = 4.05, p = .020, with no-experience learners "scoring significantly lower than those with non-Python programming experience (p = .018)".

> "on the practice quiz on conditional statements, there was a significant main effect of group, F(2, 114) = 4.05, p = .020, with learners who had no programming experience scoring significantly lower than those with non-Python programming experience (p = .018)"
