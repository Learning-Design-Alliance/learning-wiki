### Serhatlıoğlu Seda 2026

Serhatlıoğlu Seda. (2026). Student-generated learning materials as active learning: a qualitative study of midwifery students’ experiences. BMC Medical Education 26:1504. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09749-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative sub-theme from written reflections of 26 midwifery students; the visual-learning sub-theme was coded in 9 of 26 reflections. As one student put it, "it was much easier for me to understand and explain."

> "Because I created a visual illustration in my material about the bones, sutures, and fuses of the fetal head, it was much easier for me to understand and explain."
