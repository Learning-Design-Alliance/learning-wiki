## Evidence

### Griffith 2025

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2025). Funding student needs: A review of state funding policies for English learners and students from low-income backgrounds. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/471.440

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's 50-state policy review identified "37 states provide dual funding" for students qualifying as both EL and low-income/at-risk; 36 add the separate weights (e.g., Oklahoma 0.25 EL plus 0.30 FRPM), and New Jersey adjusts the combination based on a commissioned finance study.

> "Our review found that 37 states provide dual funding for students who qualify as both English learners and being from low-income families. Thus, students in these states are eligible for additional funds offered to address the learning needs of being both an English learner and qualifying as at-risk/low-income."
