Uysal 2014¶

Uysal, M. P. (2014). Improving First Computer Programming Experiences: The Case of Adapting a Web-Supported and Well-Structured Problem-Solving Method to a Traditional Course. Contemporary Educational Technology, 5(3), 198-217. https://doi.org/10.30935/cedtech/6125

Correlational analysis within the same quasi-experimental study of 433 students; the printed correlation between achievement test scores and PSI scores was "r=.036; p>.05", a negligible effect size. The authors cite Heppner's inference that the PSI identifies at-risk students but does not predict course grades.
