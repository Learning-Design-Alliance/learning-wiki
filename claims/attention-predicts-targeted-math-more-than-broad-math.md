# Attention is more predictive of targeted than broad math, but equally predictive of both reading measure types

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An assessment type by subject interaction was significant, and attention was more predictive of targeted than broad math scores. [→ Gallen 2023](#gallen-2023)
`q3 i?` For reading, attention was equally predictive of targeted and broad measures (non-significant interaction). [→ Gallen 2023 (2)](#gallen-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Gallen 2023

Gallen, C. L., Schaerlaeken, S., Younger, J. W., Project iLEAD Consortium, Anguera, J. A., & Gazzaley, A. (2023). Contribution of sustained attention abilities to real-world academic skills in children. Scientific Reports 13, 2673. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-29427-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Omnibus assessment type by subject interaction test across the four academic measures in the school-based sample, reported with F(1, 2426) = 4.71, p = 0.03; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "there was an interaction between assessment type (targeted, broad) and subject (math, ELA/reading) on predicting attention (F(1, 2426) = 4.71, p = 0.03, R2 m (model) = 0.22, R2 c (model) = 0.24)"

### Gallen 2023 (2)

Gallen, C. L., Schaerlaeken, S., Younger, J. W., Project iLEAD Consortium, Anguera, J. A., & Gazzaley, A. (2023). Contribution of sustained attention abilities to real-world academic skills in children. Scientific Reports 13, 2673. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-29427-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up pairwise comparison for reading measures in the same sample: the attention by assessment-type interaction was non-significant, F(1, 1209) = 0.004, p = 0.94; equivalence was not formally tested and no effect size is printed.

> "when comparing the two types of academic assessments for reading, we found that attention was equally predictive of both types of reading measures (F(1, 1209) = 0.004, p = 0.94, R2 m (model) = 0.19, R2 c (model) = 0.20)"

### Gallen 2023 (3)

Gallen, C. L., Schaerlaeken, S., Younger, J. W., Project iLEAD Consortium, Anguera, J. A., & Gazzaley, A. (2023). Contribution of sustained attention abilities to real-world academic skills in children. Scientific Reports 13, 2673. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-29427-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up pairwise comparison for math measures in the same sample: the attention by assessment-type interaction favored the targeted math test, F(1, 1207) = 11.29, p < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "attention was more predictive of tar-geted than broad math scores (F(1, 1207) = 11.29, p < 0.001, R2 m (model) = 0.28, R2 c (model) = 0.29; Fig. 2g, Table S3)"
