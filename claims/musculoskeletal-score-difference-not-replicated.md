# Musculoskeletal course scores differed significantly across stages (Clerkship higher than both other groups), but the difference was not replicated in the Digestive course

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Musculoskeletal (R-ISAM) scores differed significantly across stages (p = 0.041), with Clerkship > Basic Sciences (p = 0.022) and Clerkship > Physiopathology (p = 0.034), while Digestive scores showed no significant differences (p = 0.886). ## Evidence

### Almasi-Turk Sahar 2026

Almasi-Turk Sahar, Roozbehi Amrollah. (2026). The Roozbehi Integrated Spiral Anatomy Model (R-ISAM): a five-phase framework for fully integrated anatomical sciences education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09833-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Objective course-score ANOVA with LSD post hoc tests across three educational stages (350–353 students per analysis); Digestive system scores showed no significant differences across stages (p = 0.886).

> "Musculoskeletal system scores differed significantly (p = 0.041), with Clerkship students scoring higher than both Basic Sciences (p = 0.022) and Physiopathology (p = 0.034) students. This isolated difference may reflect cohort or testing variability rather than a consistent effect of the model, as it was not replicated in the Digestive course"
