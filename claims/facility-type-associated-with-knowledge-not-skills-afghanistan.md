## Evidence

### Kim Young Mi 2013

Kim Young Mi, Ansari Nasratullah, Kols Adrienne, Tappis Hannah, Currie Sheena, Zainullah Partamin, Bailey Patricia, Semba Richard, Sun Kai, van Roosmalen Jos, Stekelenburg Jelle. (2013). Assessing the capacity for newborn resuscitation and factors associated with providers' knowledge and skills: a cross-sectional study in Afghanistan. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2431-13-140

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison across 78 facilities; knowledge but not clinical skills differed by facility level, with printed means of "57.9%" at CHCs/district hospitals versus "67.8%" and "67.9%" at larger hospitals, p < 0.01.

> "Overall knowledge of newborn resuscitation was significantly lower among providers working at CHCs and district hospitals (57.9%) than providers working at provincial hospitals (67.8%) or regional and specialized hospitals (67.9%) (p < 0.01, data not shown). However, there was no significant difference in providers' clinical skills at CHCs and district hospitals (65.9%), provincial hospitals (71.3%), and regional and specialized hospitals (70%)."
