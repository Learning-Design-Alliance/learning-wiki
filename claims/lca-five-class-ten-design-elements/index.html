Evidence¶

Alqassab 2023¶

Alqassab, M., Strijbos, J.-W., Panadero, E., Fernández Ruiz, J., Warrens, M., & To, J. (2023). A Systematic Review of Peer Assessment Design Elements. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09723-7

q2 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Latent class analysis (Latent GOLD 5.0) of 891 cases derived from the 449 studies, with model fit compared via BIC, CAIC, and classification error. The review reports the listed R² effect sizes, with privacy (R² = 0.204) and matching (R² = 0.217) completing the ten.

"The ten design elements are unit of assessment assessee ( R2 = 0.880), g roup constellation ( R2 = 0.681), revision (R2 = 0.476), unit of assessment assessor ( R2 = 0.444), purpose ( R2 = 0.370), scope of involvement (R2 = 0.363), contact (R2 = 0.315)"

Alqassab 2023 (2)¶

Alqassab, M., Strijbos, J.-W., Panadero, E., Fernández Ruiz, J., Warrens, M., & To, J. (2023). A Systematic Review of Peer Assessment Design Elements. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09723-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Interpretation of the LCA results: an additional LCA using instructional design variables only confirmed the same ten elements as most important contributors. The review attributes this to context, outcome, and moderator variables being less discriminating.

"The ten peer assessment design elements with the highest effect sizes were all instructional design variables."

Related Claims¶

  • Alignment, rewards, and setting do not discriminate between peer assessment design classes, because a single practice typically dominates each
  • Formative purpose dominates peer assessment studies, but summative purpose is relatively frequent in ICT, engineering, and health and welfare domains
  • Gender and ability/skills are the most investigated moderators/mediators of peer assessment
  • Written assignments are the most used object of peer assessment, and beliefs and perceptions are the most investigated outcome
