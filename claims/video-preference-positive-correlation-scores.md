## Evidence

### David James Woo 2026

David James Woo, Deliang Wang, Kai Guo. (2026). AI Slop or AI-enhancement? Student Perceptions of AI-generated Media for an English for Academic Purposes Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis matching survey preference rankings with course scores (academic score data N=36). Video preference "correlated positively with total academic scores (0.283), especially CA3 (0.321)", alongside positive correlations with PU, PEU and GL.

> "students who preferred using “Video” showed comprehensive and highly positive feedback: they found the materials extremely useful (PU correlation: 0.460) and easy to use (PEU correlation: 0.399); their deep thinking was significantly stimulated (GL correlation: 0.499); and most importantly, video preference correlated positively with total academic scores (0.283), especially CA3 (0.321)."
