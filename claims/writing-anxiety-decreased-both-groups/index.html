Evidence¶

Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018¶

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent samples t-test on the Writing Anxiety Scale (pre-test alpha .96, post-test .97) for the Facebook group (n=48). The article reports a significant difference and describes anxiety as positively changed.

"the experimental group writing anxiety pre-test mean score was 2.94, and the posttest mean score was 2.53. It was observed that there was a significant difference between these two scores [t(47)=4.506;p<.01]"

Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018 (2)¶

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent samples t-test on the same scale for the control group (n=48). The article reports a significant difference and concludes writing anxiety decreased in the control group.

"the control group writing anxiety pre-test mean score was 2.87, and the posttest mean score was 2.45. It was observed that there was a significant difference between these two scores [t(47)=4.211;p<.05]"
