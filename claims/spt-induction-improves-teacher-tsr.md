# The induction improved treatment teachers' relationships with focal students: more positivity (Cohen's d = 0.41) and less negativity (Cohen's d = -0.52)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Treatment teachers perceived more positive teacher-student relationships with their focal students than control teachers (Cohen's d = 0.41, 95% CI 0.01-0.86). [→ Gehlbach 2021](#gehlbach-2021)
`q3 i2` Treatment teachers perceived less negative teacher-student relationships with their focal students than control teachers (Cohen's d = -0.52, 95% CI -0.94 to -0.10). [→ Gehlbach 2021 (2)](#gehlbach-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Gehlbach 2021

Gehlbach, H., Mascio, B., & McIntyre, J. (2021). Social perspective taking: A professional development induction to improve teacher-student relationships and student learning. Journal of Educational Psychology (in press). https://osf.io/suhvj/
