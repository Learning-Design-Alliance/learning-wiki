## Evidence

### Puppart 2025

Puppart, B., & Aru, J. (2025). A Brief AI Literacy Intervention Does Not Significantly Reduce Over-Reliance and Increases Under-Reliance on ChatGPT: A Randomized Study. University of Tartu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test in the randomized study (n = 36) on the proportion of correct recommendations rejected: "t(34) = 2.35, p = .025, d = 0.78", a medium-to-large effect favouring higher under-reliance after the intervention text.

> "An independent samples t-test indicated that under-reliance was higher in the intervention group (M = 36.84, SD = 24.11) than in the control group (M = 19.12, SD = 20.78), and this difference was statistically significant, t(34) = 2.35, p = .025, d = 0.78."
