## Evidence

### Wang Song 2017

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

In the subsample that took a unified term examination, "this term examination score was highly correlated with CNCEE score (r = 0.71, p < 0.001)", supporting stability of academic performance across time.

> "Behaviorally, this term examination score was highly correlated with CNCEE score (r = 0.71, p < 0.001)."

### Wang Song 2017 (2)

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain regression on term examination scores showed the association with "the rGMD in the same region (the left DLPFC; r = 0.33, p < 0.001)", with no other significant regions reported.

> "whole-brain regression analyses showed that term examination score was related to the rGMD in the same region (the left DLPFC; r = 0.33, p < 0.001; see Table 2 and Fig. 2), after adjusting for gender, age and the total GMV."
