Weiwei Liu, Valentina C. Tassone, Pascal Frank, Renate Wesselink, Harm J.A. Biemans. (2025). The role of human agency in dealing with challenges in transdisciplinary learning for sustainability in higher education: A scoping literature review. Educational Research Review 49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100747

q2 · i? · review · r2

Characteristics analysis of the 36 included studies in the scoping review. The review reports 16 articles included educational staff viewpoints (44.4%), 12 studies explored societal partner perspectives (33.3%), and only seven articles (19.4%) included all three categories of participants.

"Most studies investigated participant's experiences in transdisciplinary learning for sustainability from a student perspective, including bachelor's, master's, and PhD students (N = 32, 88.9 %)."
