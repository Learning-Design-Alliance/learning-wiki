q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Publication-bias analysis aggregating mean effect sizes per dependent sample. The Egger regression test was significant (z = 4.26, p < 0.001), as was a multi-level Egger test with the modified predictor 2/√N (t(127) = 3.043, p = 0.003).

"The trim-and-fill analysis suggested that a small-study effect is present (gTrim & Fill = 0.28, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.23, 0.32]) with twelve studies missing on the left side (Fig. 2)."
