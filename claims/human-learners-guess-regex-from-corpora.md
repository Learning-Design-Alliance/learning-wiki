## Evidence

### Long Ouyang 2017

Long Ouyang, Michael C. Frank (2017). Pedagogical learning. 31st Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NIPS 2017). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.09401](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.09401)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Human-learning verification of the teaching corpora: learners saw each corpus and guessed the rule, with responses coded correct or incorrect. The authors conclude "people are capable of providing good evidence about complex rules using a handful of well chosen examples", without monetary incentive for teachers.

> "overall, it appears that people are capable of providing good evidence about complex rules using a handful of well chosen examples. This is particularly impressive given that we did not monetarily incentivize helpful examples."
