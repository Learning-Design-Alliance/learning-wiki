## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all four trials, dogs in each condition committed the perseveration error less than chance, suggesting learning of inhibitory control. [→ Neilands P. 2021](#neilands-p-2021)

## Evidence

### Neilands P. 2021

Neilands P., Kingsley-Smith O., Taylor A. H. (2021). Dogs’ insensitivity to scaffolding behaviour in an A-not-B task provides support for the theory of natural pedagogy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-79557-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bayesian binomial tests across all four test trials in the 30-dog experiment. Error rates per condition were 11/40, 10/40, and 13/40 trials, each below chance with BF < 0.333, indicating "the dogs learnt to inhibit their initial response".

> "Across all four trials (Fig. 1), the 10 dogs in each condition were substantially less likely than chance to commit the perseveration error (Control Condition: 11/40 trials, Bayesian Binomial Test: BF = 0.050; Pointing Condition: 10/40 trials, Bayesian Binomial Test: BF = 0.046; Demonstration Condition: 13/40 trials, Bayesian Binomial Test: BF = 0.061)"
