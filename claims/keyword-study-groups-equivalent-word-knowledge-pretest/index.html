Davoudi 2016¶

Davoudi, M., & Yousefi, D. (2016). The Effect of Keyword Method on Vocabulary Retension of Senior High School EFL Learners in Iran. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(11). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099563

Independent-samples t-test on the 60-item word knowledge pretest checklist administered before treatment; means were M=3.10 (experimental) and M= 3.00 (control), indicating the target words were almost new to all participants. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this non-significant result.
