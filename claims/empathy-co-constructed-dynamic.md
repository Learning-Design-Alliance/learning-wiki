## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The authors argue empathy is a dynamic, reciprocal process built through communication and trust, and identify this co-creation as beyond their model's individual perspective. [→ Alia Hamdan 2024](#alia-hamdan-2024)

## Evidence

### Alia Hamdan 2024

Alia Hamdan, Ash Bista, Dina Newman, Scott Franklin. (2024). A Conceptual Framework for Understanding Empathy in Physics faculty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretive claim (Section V.C) from the interview data: communication intersects all model components, can itself be empathetic action, and 'co-creation' of empathy moves beyond a simple learn-and-give dynamic. The model itself does not capture reciprocity; future research is proposed.

> "Empathy is a reciprocal process that relies on trust building and vulnerability even in educational settings."
