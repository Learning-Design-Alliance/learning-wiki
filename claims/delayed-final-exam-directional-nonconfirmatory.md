# Delayed final-exam differences favored Guided-LLM descriptively but were not statistically confirmatory, with confidence intervals including zero

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Guided-LLM exceeded No-LLM by 14.37 points (g = 0.61, Welch p = .127) and Unrestricted-LLM by 14.24 points (g = 0.71, Welch p = .086) on the delayed final exam, with both intervals including zero. [→ Mohammad Amanlou 2026](#mohammad-amanlou-2026)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delayed cumulative 11-question no-help final exam administered two weeks after Quiz 4 in the three-condition quasi-experiment. The article states that because both intervals included zero and omnibus tests were not significant, results are directional rather than statistically confirmatory.

> "Guided-LLM exceeded No-LLM by 14.37 points (95% bootstrap CI [−2.69, 31.07], Hedges’ g = 0.61, Welch p = .127) and Unrestricted -LLM by 14.24 points (95% bootstrap CI [−0.99, 28.67], Hedges’ g = 0.71, Welch p = .086)."
