## Evidence

### Muntean Lorena Mihaela 2022

Muntean Lorena Mihaela, Nireștean Aurel, Sima-Comaniciu Andreea, Mărușteri Marius, Zăgan Cătălin Andrei, Lukacs Emese. (2022). The Relationship between Personality, Motivation and Academic Performance at Medical Students from Romania. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9332223/

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square association analyses in the prospective pilot study of 179 medical students relating DECAS agreeableness and emotional stability dimensions to National Residency Exam score. The article reports "students with increased agreeableness are 2.791 times more likely to get a better grade" (p = 0.07) and "students with emotional stability within normal or high limits have a chance to get a better grade of 4.86 times higher" (OR = 4.863, p = 0.0003).

> "students with increased agreeableness are 2.791 times more likely to get a better grade on the National Residency Exam, with a medium effect size (d = 0.566), and students with emotional stability within normal or high limits have a chance to get a better grade of 4.86 times higher, with a large effect size (d = 0.872)"
