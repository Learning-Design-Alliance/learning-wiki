Rexigel 2024¶

Rexigel, E., Kuhn, J., Becker, S., & Malone, S. (2024). The More the Better? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of the Benefits of More than Two External Representations in STEM Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09958-y

In the moderator analyses conducted for each outcome category, support was coded as none, semantic, syntactic, or both; the analysis revealed that benefits "primarily depend on the provision of appropriate support." No effect-size magnitude for the moderator is printed.
