Evidence¶

Gonny Schellings 2011¶

Gonny Schellings. (2011). Applying learning strategy questionnaires: problems and possibilities. Metacognition and Learning, 6, 91–109. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9069-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Reliability analysis in the comparative study (16 ninth-graders): full instrument α=.87, Elaboration & evaluation α=.85, but Orientation & planning α=.52, Executing α=.26, Monitoring α=.36.

"The full instrument (58 items) resulted in a fair reliability ( α=. 87) and the Elaboration & evaluation scale also reached a fair reliability (α=. 85). However, the reliabilities for the other three subscales were moderate (Orientation & planning α=.52) to low (Executing α=.26; Monitoring α=.36)."

Gonny Schellings 2011 (2)¶

Gonny Schellings. (2011). Applying learning strategy questionnaires: problems and possibilities. Metacognition and Learning, 6, 91–109. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9069-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Larger-group study administering the questionnaire to 190 ninth-graders after silent text study: full instrument α=.79, subscales α=.54, .35, .40 and .69, corresponding to Study 1's reliabilities. The author states the subscales "were not clear indications of the constructs these subscales were intended to measure."

"The overall, internal consistency for the questionnaire was reasonably high (α=.79). However, the internal consistency for each subscale appeared to be moderate to low: Orientation and planning ( α=.54), Executing ( α =.35), Monitoring ( α =.40), Elaboration and evaluation (=.69)"
