## Evidence

### Su Weilin 2026

Su Weilin, Chen Chen. (2026). The mediating role of academic engagement and the moderating role of academic self-efficacy in the relationship between critical thinking and academic competence: evidence from Chinese college students. BMC Psychology, 14, 700. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04446-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and correlation analysis (Table 2, N = 363) provides preliminary support for the hypotheses. The printed correlations are r = 0.525 (critical thinking–engagement), r = 0.251 (critical thinking–competence), and r = 0.312 (engagement–competence), all p < 0.01.

> "there is a positive correlation between critical thinking of Chinese college students and their academic engagement (r = 0.525, p < 0.01), and there is also a positive correlation between critical thinking and competence (r = 0.251, p < 0.01). Similarly, academic engagement of Chinese college students demonstrates a positive correlation with their academic competence (r = 0.312, p < 0.01)."
