`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of 2013 California State Test results in OUSD, a district where nearly all schools exceeded the API target of 800. The report prints mathematics proficiency rates showing African Americans at 35% and Latinos at 45% versus Whites at 72% and Asians at 82%, with similar ELA disparities by socioeconomic disadvantage.

> "Whereas the district average proficiency is 62%, the proficiency rates for African Americans (35%) and Latinos (45%) are far below those of their White (72%) and Asian (82%) peers."
