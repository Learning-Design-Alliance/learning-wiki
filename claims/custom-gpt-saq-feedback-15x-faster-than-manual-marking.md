## Evidence

### Stephenson E 2026

Stephenson E, Morrigan K, Irfan A, Bascombe K, Okorie M. (2026). Artificial intelligence-assisted feedback in pharmacology education: a pilot evaluation of a custom generative model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09300-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective timing data from a pilot study of 20 students in a Physician Associate MSc programme; AI generation was timed in real time from prompt entry to output completion, and manual faculty marking was timed by a single faculty marker. The article reports raw AI generation was "approximately 15 times faster than fully manual faculty marking".

> "The mean AI feedback generation time for a four-question SAQ quiz was 34 s (SD 5 s). By contrast, fully manual faculty marking and feedback generation required a mean of 508 s per quiz (approximately 8.5 min, SD 110 s)."
