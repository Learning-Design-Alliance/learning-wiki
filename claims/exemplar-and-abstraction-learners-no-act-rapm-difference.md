`q3 i?` Abstractor and exemplar learners did not statistically differ on composite ACT scores (Ms = 28.75 and 27.71; t(145) = 1.28, p = 0.408) or RAPM scores (Ms = 6.52 and 5.67; t(127) = 0.99, p = 0.587). [→ McDaniel Mark A. 2022](#mcdaniel-mark-a-2022)

## Evidence

### McDaniel Mark A. 2022

McDaniel Mark A., Cahill Michael J., Frey Regina F., Limeri Lisa B., Lemons Paula P. (2022). Learning Introductory Biology: Students' Concept-Building Approaches Predict Transfer on Biology Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-12-0335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary comparison of the two learner groups' general intellectual ability measures before the exam analyses. No statistically significant group differences were detected on either measure; equivalence was not formally tested. The article notes the nonsignificant differences still favored abstraction learners, motivating the covariate analyses.

> "Abstractor and exemplar learners did not statistically differ on either composite ACT scores, Ms = 28.75 and 27.71, respectively; t(145) = 1.28, p = 0.408; or RAPM scores, Ms = 6.52 and 5.67, respectively, t(127) = 0.99, p = 0.587"
