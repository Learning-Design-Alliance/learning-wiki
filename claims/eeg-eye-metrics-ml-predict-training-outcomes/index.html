q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Wu et al, that cognitive and behavioral metrics captured by EEG and eye-tracking combined with machine learning predicted surgical training outcomes with 72.5% accuracy; the review does not describe the study's design in detail.

"For example, Wu et al found that using cognitive and behavioral metrics through EEG and eye-metrics, with machine-learning can predict training outcomes with a 72.5% accuracy."
