## Evidence

### McKenzie 2025

McKenzie, R. & Allensworth, E. (2025). Variability in the Grades Teachers Give: Teacher Grade Value-Add, Mismatch, and Long-Term Effects. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312251345094

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Value-added analysis of CPS administrative records for ninth-grade cohorts 2011-12 to 2017-18, using adjusted standard deviations. The article reports total grade effect variation of 0.379-0.421 letter grades per SD and about 0.8 GPA points (0.758-0.841) at two SDs, "higher or lower than average ninth-grade teachers give to similar students in similar courses."

> "A ninth-grade teacher with a total grading effect one standard deviation above or below the mean gives grades that are, on average, 0.379 - 0.421 of a letter grade different than an average ninth-grade teacher gives to students with similar incoming achievement and demographics in similar classes"
