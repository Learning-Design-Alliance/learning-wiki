## Evidence

### Lei Fan and Fangxue Liu 2026

Lei Fan and Fangxue Liu. (2026). Impacts of generative AI skill development on academic adaptation and learning outcomes among university students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-person Wilcoxon contrast (efficiency vs. evaluative growth) in the 313-respondent retrospective questionnaire; the paired mean difference was 0.335 points, dz = 0.413, Holm-adjusted p < 0.001. The article states dz describes mean differences rather than representing an effect size for the Wilcoxon test.

> "Endorsement was highest for improved efficiency (89.1%), followed by effective prompting (83.1%) and handling complex tasks (78.6%), while fewer students reported improvement in evaluating output accuracy (74.4%)."
