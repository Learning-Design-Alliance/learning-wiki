`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Finding from Johnson's quasi-experimental analyses of the LCFF rollout, reported in the Success of the LCFF section. The report frames this as evidence that sustained exposure to increased funding compounds benefits across school-age years.

> "Moreover, the study demonstrated that improvements in student outcomes were more pronounced for cohorts that were exposed to funding increases for more of their school-age years."
