The review reports, citing Branisa and Jenisova (2015), a mixed-methods comparison in which "students practiced manual graph construction based on experimental data performed better than students who were provided with computer-constructed graphs." No effect size is printed.

"Branisa and Jenisova (2015) compared manual graphing with automatically generated graphs and found that students practiced manual graph construction based on experimental data performed better than students who were provided with computer-constructed graphs."
