## Evidence

### Or 2023

Or, C. (2023). The role of attitude in the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology: A meta-analytic structural equation modelling study. International Journal of Technology in Education and Science (IJTES), 7(4), 552-570. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijtes.504

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Comparison of variance explained (Table 3, 5, 7, 8) between the original UTAUT model and the three OSMASEM models with attitude. The original model's "adjusted R2 of 74% for BI" exceeded all extended models; the article concludes the inclusion of attitude "did not enhance its explanatory power."

> "The original UTAUT model performed at an adjusted R2 of 74% for BI. The UTAUT model in this study only attained an R2 of 45.8%."
