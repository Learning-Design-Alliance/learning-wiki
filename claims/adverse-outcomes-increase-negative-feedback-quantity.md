## Evidence

### van Sassen C 2025

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on feedback quantity in the 62-supervisor vignette experiment found a significant outcome-by-valence interaction (η² = 0.21); negative feedback (TIPS) showed "2.35 [0.11] vs 1.80 [0.09]" idea units for adverse versus favorable outcomes, while positive feedback did not differ (P = .78).

> "There was also a significant interaction between the clinical outcome of the vignette and feedback valence (F1,61 = 16.16, P < .001, η2 = 0.21), meaning that the influence of knowledge of the clinical outcome of the vignette on the number of idea units differed for positive and negative feedback."

### van Sassen C 2025 (2)

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Main effects in the same feedback-quantity ANOVA: adverse outcomes elicited more idea units (η² = 0.18) and positive feedback contained more idea units than negative (η² = 0.09).

> "adverse clinical outcomes resulted in more idea units than favorable clinical outcomes (2.31 vs 2.05, F1,61 = 13.14, P < .001, η2 = 0.18), and positive feedback contained more idea units than negative feedback (2.29 vs 2.07, F1,61 = 6.32, P = .02, η2 = 0.09)."
