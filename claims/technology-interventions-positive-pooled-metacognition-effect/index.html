Evidence¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis of 33 studies contributing 94 effect sizes from randomised and quasi-experimental intervention studies in formal K–12 and post-secondary settings; the pooled estimate was "g = 0.653, 95% CI [0.520, 0.786]".

"The three-level model estimated a positive pooled association between technology-supported interventions and metacognitive outcomes, g = 0.653, 95% CI [0.520, 0.786], t(32) = 9.98, p <.001."

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (2)¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Heterogeneity analysis within the same three-level model: "total I² = 72.7%", with between-study variance σ²₃ = 0.104 and a 95% prediction interval of approximately [−0.02, 1.32]; the authors state the pooled estimate is an average across substantively heterogeneous studies.

"Statistical heterogeneity was substantial, Q(93) = 298.06, p <.001, with total I² = 72.7%."
