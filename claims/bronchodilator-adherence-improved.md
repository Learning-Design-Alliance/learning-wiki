`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of adherence indicators 14 and 12 in the Sicilian GP cohort. Indicator 14's change was not statistically significant (p=0.105 and 0.060 per Table 4); indicator 12 decreased significantly at 24 months (p=0.047).

> "COPD patients with a low adherence to LABA and/or LAMA treatment (plus ICS or less) (indicator 14) decreased from 61.6% before intervention to 54.7% after 24 months, in line with HSD data (55.0%)."
