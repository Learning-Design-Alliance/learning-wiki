`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of studies of preoperative educational briefing frameworks (PREDICT; GAPS), reporting an 88% improvement in resident performance areas. The review frames the mechanism as a hypothesis: debriefs create opportunities to identify areas for improvement.

> "Impacts of these structured perioperative dialogue models led to an 88% improvement in resident performance in areas such as goal setting, autonomy, and confidence"
