# Education level does not significantly moderate the effect of formative assessment on student learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pinar Karaman 2021

Pinar Karaman. (2021). The Effect of Formative Assessment Practices on Student Learning: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Journal of Assessment Tools in Education. https://doi.org/10.21449/ijate.870300

`q3 · i?`

Mixed-effects subgroup analysis across primary (k = 8), secondary (k = 28), and tertiary (k = 11) education levels. The article reports that "comparison of subgroups at different education levels did not make a significant contribution to the variance"; the highest mean effect was at primary level and the lowest at tertiary level, but the difference was untested as significant.

> "The results of the heterogeneity test yielded that comparison of subgroups at different education levels did not make a significant contribution to the variance (Q=.66, df=2, p= .71)."
