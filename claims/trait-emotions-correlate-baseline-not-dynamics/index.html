The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting 2022¶

The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting, Performance, and Emotions in Self-Regulated Learning: A Computational Modeling Approach — Supplementary Materials. (2022). https://gpemodel.shinyapps.io/gpe-model/

q2 · i3 · associational · r?

Correlational analysis in Study 1 relating CESD, TAI, and BFI:NE sum scores to individual parameters from the best-fitting valence-aggregated model. The article reports rs = -.37 for all three traits with the emotion baseline parameter (r = -.37, a large correlation) and states that "none of the trait-like/habitual emotions measures were correlated with temporal changes".

"those who reported higher levels of depression, anxiety, and negative emotionality were less likely to report stronger positive states (rs = -.37, -.37, -.37, ps < 0.05)."
