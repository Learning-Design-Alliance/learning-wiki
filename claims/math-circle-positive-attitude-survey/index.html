Evidence¶

Eugene Kennedy & Lawrence Smolinsky 2016¶

Eugene Kennedy & Lawrence Smolinsky (2016). Math Circles: A Tool for Promoting Engagement among Middle School Minority Males. Eurasia Journal of Mathematics, Science & Technology Education, 12(4), 717-732. https://doi.org/10.12973/eurasia.2016.1223a

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

End-of-semester survey administered during the last month of circle meetings to case study participants; only 10 of the 24 participants completed it. Responses were descriptive counts, with "9 of 10" agreeing mathematics was important and "6 of 9" reporting confidence.

"Most respondents (9 of 10) indicated that they thought mathematics was important [Item 1], were interested in mathematics [Ite m 8; 5 of 8], liked mathematics [Item 2; 9 of 10] and had confidence in their ability to solve mathematics problems [Item 3; 6 of 9]."
