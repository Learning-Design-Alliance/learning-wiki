### George Efthimiou 2026

George Efthimiou, Katerina Plakitsi. (2026). Preservice Early Childhood Educators' Engagement with Generative AI in Science Activity Design: The Case of Magnetism. https://www.mdpi.com/journal/educationsciences

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of post-interaction Likert reflection items from 131 preservice educators after a 90-minute AI-supported magnetism activity design workshop. The lowest-rated items were trust, accuracy and consistency; the highest-rated were logistical, with time saving at "M = 3.95, SD = 0.87".

> "Conversely, trust in the LLM yielded the lowest mean score in the dataset ( M = 3.08, SD = 0.82), alongside perceived accuracy (M = 3.18, SD = 0.83) and consistency (M = 3.26, SD = 0.78)."
