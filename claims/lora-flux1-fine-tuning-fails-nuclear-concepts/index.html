Mohammed I. Radaideh 2026¶

Mohammed I. Radaideh, Jeremy Moon, Andre Gala-Garza, Emma Son, Yug Shah, Majdi I. Radaideh. (2026). NuclearDiffusion: Text-to-Image Foundation Models for Learning Nuclear Energy Concepts. Preprint submitted to Peer Review. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04030

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative assessment across 300 prompts and 16 checkpoints of the LoRA-tuned Flux.1 model; the article states Flux.1 "failed in all 300 images" and was excluded from the checkpoint-scoring panel. No effect size is printed.

"The qualitative assessment of the images generated by Flux.1 showed that the model is not learning and is using the wrong concept and building on it."
