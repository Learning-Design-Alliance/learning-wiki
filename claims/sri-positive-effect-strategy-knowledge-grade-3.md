# The SRI program produced a statistically significant positive effect on knowledge of reading strategies after one year of intervention in Grade 3

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After one year of SRI, the experimental group showed a significantly greater learning gain on the reading strategies questionnaire than the control group (b = 2.471, p < .001). [→ Droop 2016](#droop-2016)

## Evidence

### Droop 2016

Droop, M.; van Elsäcker, W.; Voeten, M. J. M.; Verhoeven, L. (2016). Long-Term Effects of Strategic Reading Instruction in the Intermediate Elementary Grades. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1065528

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the cluster-randomized trial (40 schools, 62 classrooms) testing the intervention effect on the 25-item reading strategies questionnaire at the end of Grade 3, controlling for background variables. The article reports "b D 2.471, z D 4.893, p < .001" in favor of the experimental group.

> "At the end of third grade, there was a statistically significant difference in mean learning gain between the experimental and the control group in favor of the experimental group (SRI End Grade 3: b D 2.471, z D 4.893, p < .001 )."
