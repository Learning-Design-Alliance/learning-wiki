# Cognitive-only AI support yields medium effects on knowledge (g = 0.52), skill (g = 0.44), and affect (g = 0.48) and a large effect on perception (g = 0.85)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` AI-based cognitive-only support has a medium effect on knowledge acquisition (g = 0.52, k = 12) and near-medium effects on skill acquisition (g = 0.44, k = 11). [→ Heng Zhang 2025](#heng-zhang-2025)
`q3 i3` Cognitive-only support shows a large effect on perception (g = 0.85, k = 10) and a small-to-medium effect on affective experience (g = 0.48, k = 31). [→ Heng Zhang 2025 (2)](#heng-zhang-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Heng Zhang 2025

Heng Zhang, Yuhan Liu, Meilin Jiang, Juanjuan Chen, Minhong Wang, Fred Paas. (2025). Emotional Artificial Intelligence in Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10086-4

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of Category 2 studies (cognitive-only AI support) on knowledge acquisition, k = 12. The pooled "g = 0.52" with CI [0.17, 0.87] is a medium effect; skill acquisition pooled at g = 0.44 (k = 11).

> "The weighted mean ES ( g) is 0.52 (95% CI [0.17, 0.87], k = 12, τ² = 0.32, I² = 35.5%), with individual ESs ranging from − 0.42 to 2.19."

### Heng Zhang 2025 (2)

Heng Zhang, Yuhan Liu, Meilin Jiang, Juanjuan Chen, Minhong Wang, Fred Paas. (2025). Emotional Artificial Intelligence in Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10086-4

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of Category 2 studies on perception, k = 10, pooled "g = 0.85" (large). Affective experience pooled at g = 0.48 (95% CI [0.33, 0.63], k = 31), a small-to-medium effect; task performance pooled at g = 0.35 (k = 3).

> "The weighted mean ES ( g) for students’ perception is 0.85 (95% CI [0.50, 1.20], k = 10), suggesting a large effect, with individual ESs ranging from 0.03 to 2.37."
