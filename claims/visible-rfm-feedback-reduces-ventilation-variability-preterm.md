## Evidence

### alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane 2026

alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane. (2026). Impact of real-time respiratory function monitoring on neonatal mask ventilation training: a multicentre simulation-based crossover study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004894

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multicentre crossover simulation study of 49 analysed clinicians; Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of hidden versus visible conditions in the hidden-first group during 30-s ventilation periods with the preterm manikin. The article reports "the SD of Vte decreased from 1.54 mL/kg to 0.5 mL/kg (p=0.005; r=−0.43)" and the SD of leak decrease with p=0.028; r=−0.33.

> "During ventilation, the SD of Vte decreased from 1.54 mL/kg to 0.5 mL/kg (p=0.005; r=−0.43), and the SD of leak decreased from 21.25% to 9.15% (p=0.028; r=−0.33)."
