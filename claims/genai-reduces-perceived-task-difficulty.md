## Evidence

### Dawid Ratajczyk 2026

Dawid Ratajczyk, Agata Dymarska, Adam Matłoka, Michał Tomczyk, Małgorzata Wiącek. (2026). Thinking With AI, Reasoning Without It: Cognitive Offloading to Generative AI Weakens Effort Regulation. Preprint. https://osf.io/59qtm/ [doi:10.31234/osf.io/83jum_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/83jum_v1)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Stage 1 perceived difficulty, self-reported on a 7-point scale in the same preregistered lab experiment. A 2×2 ANOVA showed a significant main effect of genAI access; the quoted means show lower difficulty ratings in the genAI group, with no standardised effect size printed for this contrast.

> "Participants in the no-genAI group reported higher perceived difficulty (𝑀= 4.71,𝑆𝐷= 1.42) than participants in the genAI group (𝑀= 4.17,𝑆𝐷= 1.48; Figure5)."
