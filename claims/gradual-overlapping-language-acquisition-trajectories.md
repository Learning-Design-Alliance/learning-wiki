## Evidence

### Dupoux 2018

Dupoux, E. (2018). Cognitive science in the era of artificial intelligence: A roadmap for reverse-engineering the infant language-learner. Cognition, 173, 43-59. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2017.11.008

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of forty years of empirical infant studies: infants differentiate native from foreign consonants and vowels at 6 months while phonotactic learning and early word comprehension begin before phonetic tuning is complete.

> "instead of a stage-like developmental tableau, the evidence shows that acquisition takes places at all levels more or less simultaneously, in a gradual and largely overlapping fashion."

### Dupoux 2018 (2)

Dupoux, E. (2018). Cognitive science in the era of artificial intelligence: A roadmap for reverse-engineering the infant language-learner. Cognition, 173, 43-59. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2017.11.008

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review observation: although input amount varies across cultures and socio-economic strata, sometimes exceeding an order of magnitude, canonical babbling onset ranges only from 6 to 10 months, a less than 1 to 2 ratio, with similarly reduced ranges for word production and word combinations.

> "This suggests a surprising level ofresilience in language learning, i.e., some minimal amount of input is su ﬃcient to trigger certain landmarks."
