`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sign-test comparisons of raw error and help frequencies on QUADRATIC transfer tasks (§3.5). For the quadratic general case errors increased (23 increases vs 6 decreases, z = 2.96, P < 0.003); for cubics "help seeking which declined signi®cantly". The article states these results provide evidence that transfer is occurring.

> "When it came to the cubic cases, it was help seeking which declined signi®cantly (18 seeking less help and 4 more, z = 2.77, P < 0.006)."
