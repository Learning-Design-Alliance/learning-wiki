## Evidence

### Emily M. Smith 2019

Emily M. Smith, Martin M. Stein, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Surprise! Shifting students away from model-verifying frames in physics labs. arXiv preprint arXiv:1807.04710. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Individual semi-structured interview with Ali six months after the activity, conducted with her group's lab notes in hand. The authors say she "learned to incorporate critical thinking about disagreements between data and models rather than merely verifying models."

> "From this experience, she learned that " it's important to perhaps like when data is like not leading in the direction that you were thinking of to like explore why it is doing that instead of... doing the same exact thing and hope that it goes... in your favor ,""

### Emily M. Smith 2019 (2)

Emily M. Smith, Martin M. Stein, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Surprise! Shifting students away from model-verifying frames in physics labs. arXiv preprint arXiv:1807.04710. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.smith)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Individual semi-structured interview with Ben six months after the activity. The article reports he now accepts inconclusive data, saying he "would be more inclined to say... 'you know what, I think this data is inconclusive.'"

> "Ben reﬂects that he learned to " look at the data on its own and look past any preconceived ideas about what the outcome should be. ""
