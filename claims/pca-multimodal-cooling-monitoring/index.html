A student project (Hari et al.) collected acoustic emission signals, hydrophone signals, and high-speed boiling images, then applied PCA models to the three input types for boiling monitoring. The article reports that "PCA was useful for two -phase cooling system monitoring based on multi-modal input." No effect size is printed.
