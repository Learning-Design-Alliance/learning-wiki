# Year 9 students showed a statistically significant improvement in Einsteinian physics conceptual knowledge after the 20-lesson Einstein-First programme in both 2013 and 2014

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paired pre/post conceptual test scores rose significantly in both cohorts (2013 mean 27% to 73%; 2014 mean 23% to 91%). [→ Tejinder Kaur 2017](#tejinder-kaur-2017)

## Evidence

### Tejinder Kaur 2017

Tejinder Kaur, David Blair, Ron Burman, Warren Stannard, David Treagust, Grady Venville, Marjan Zadnik, Warwick Mathews and Dana Perks. (2017). Evaluation of 14 to 15-Year-Old Students' Understanding and Attitude towards Learning Einsteinian Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post questionnaire study of Year 9 classes in 2013 and 2014 (final sample 102 students). Table I reports means rising from 27% to 73% (2013) and 23% to 91% (2014), with paired t-tests t(44) = 20.1 and t(56) = 30.3, both "statistically significant".

> "The paired sample t-test confirmed that there was a statistically significant increase in scores from pre-test to post-test as shown in Table I."
