# Experimental-group students outperformed control-group students on post-test feedback literacy after CEA implementation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on the post-test of feedback literacy (M = 79.666 vs M = 71.762; partial η2 = 0.218). [→ I Putu Yoga Laksana 2026](#i-putu-yoga-laksana-2026)

## Evidence

### I Putu Yoga Laksana 2026

I Putu Yoga Laksana, Ni Made Ratminingsih, Made Hery Santosa & I Putu Indra Kusuma. (2026). Aligning ChatGPT with E-Portfolio Assessment as EFL Learning Model: Its Effect on Student's Speaking Performance and Feedback Literacy. Technology in Language Teaching & Learning, 8, 103616. https://doi.org/10.29140/tltl.2026.103616

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate post-hoc analysis of the feedback literacy questionnaire, adapted from Molloy et al. (2020), administered pre and post in the quasi-experiment. The experimental group (M = 79.666) "outperformed their counterparts in the control group (M = 71.762)" on the post-test.

> "Table 4 also shows a statistical difference between the participants' feedback literacy in the experimental and control groups as can be seen from F (1) = 16.135, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.218."
