## Evidence

### Kelli Shar 2020

Kelli Shar, Rosemary S. Russ, James T. Laverty. (2020). Student Epistemological Framing on Paper-Based Assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of nine students attempting the Gravitron problem, which asked for reasoning shown through diagrams, equations, and words. The authors report this "failed to shift students towards reasoning in a Conceptual Physics frame"; most did not solve the problem and no student shifted their initial frame.

> "Notice that in the Gravitron problem (Figure 3) students are explicitly asked to show their rea- soning through words, but this failed to shift students towards reasoning in a Conceptual Physics frame."
