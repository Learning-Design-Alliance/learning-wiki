## Evidence

### SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK 2007

SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.gse.buffalo.edu/org/buildingblocks/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Head-to-head curriculum comparison within the 2006 randomized trial (21 teachers/152 children). Appendix A4 reports means of 57.32 versus 53.77, mean difference 3.55 from an author-conducted HLM analysis, effect size 0.49, statistically significant, improvement index +19.

> "Clements and Sarama (2006) analyzed group differ-ences between the Building Blocks for Math group and the Pre-K Mathematics combined with DLM Express group for one math outcome measure (Early Mathematics Assessment). The difference between groups was statistically significant and favored children in the Building Blocks for Math group."
