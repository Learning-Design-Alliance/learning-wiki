### Crawford and Skipp 2014

Claire Crawford, Amy Skipp. (2014). LIT Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581139

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Process evaluation findings from a web-based survey of all participating schools and 15 follow-up depth interviews with treatment schools, analysed using Framework qualitative methods. These are teacher perceptions, not measured outcomes.

> "Teachers felt that the programme facilitated ‘healthy debate’ within the classroom, increased confidence in pupils who struggled with core literacy skills, and promoted independent learning. However, they did not feel it worked well with children with underlying cognitive issues requiring intensive vocabulary support."
