Pei-Yu Tseng 2026¶

Pei-Yu Tseng, Mahir Akgun, and Peng Liu. (2026). Why Machines Misread Pedagogical Quality: Human–Machine Alignment in LLM-Based Pretest Question Evaluation. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23629 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.23629

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 2 (revised rubric, direct evaluation) re-scored machine-generated question pools against human instructor ratings. The quote reports the printed agreement, bias and MSE changes; no standardised effect size is printed.

"openness agreement increased from 55.7% to 83.3%, bias decreased from 0.66 to 0.22, and MSE from 1.08 to 0.32. For depth, agreement increased from 70.5% to 95.0%, with very small remaining error."
