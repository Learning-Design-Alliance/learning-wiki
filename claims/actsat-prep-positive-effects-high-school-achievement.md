### ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review by the What Works Clearinghouse using WWC Procedures and Standards Handbook version 3.0. Six studies (three RCTs without reservations, three studies with reservations) with 65,603 students yielded the "positive effects" rating; Table 1 reports an average improvement index of +9 percentile points (range -3 to +19). No pooled standardized effect size is printed, so no effect size is stated.

> "ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs were found to have positive effects on general academic achievement (high school) for high school students, with a medium to large extent of evidence."

The review's effectiveness summary states that "three studies showed statistically significant positive effects, and three studies showed indeterminate effects" within the domain, so the positive rating rests on findings that do not uniformly replicate across studies.

> "Thus, for the general academic achievement (high school) domain, three studies showed statistically significant positive effects, and three studies showed indeterminate effects."
