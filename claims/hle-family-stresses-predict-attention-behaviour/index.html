Evidence¶

Dilnot 2017¶

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression predicting parental ratings of attention and behaviour (SDQ hyperactivity and conduct subscales at age 4½). The article reports "home literacy environment and family stresses accounted for 11% of the variance at the first step".

"In the model predicting attention and behaviour, home literacy environment and family stresses accounted for 11% of the variance at the first step and family3risk accounted for no further variance."
