Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of platform logging data (logins, completed learning units, total usage time) during the one-week intervention. The article reports the login comparison and that both groups completed almost 80% of learning units (means 24.93 YOA, 24.50 OA).

> "With a mean of 5.71, the OA recorded significantly more logins during the one-week intervention period than the YOA, who had a mean number of logins of 3.86 (U = 50.00, Z = −2.227, p = 0.026)."
