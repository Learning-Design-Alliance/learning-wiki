### Ying Cao 2021

Ying Cao, Brant E. Hinrichs. (2021). Analyzing students' collaboratively solving spherical unit vector problems in upper-level E&M through a lens of shared resources. 2021 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2021.pr.cao](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2021.pr.cao)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Explorative analysis of three small-group think-aloud interviews in which seven volunteers (six physics majors, one math major/physics minor) solved three conceptual problems on a large whiteboard. The authors report that "the students collaborated together to successfully find the answers" to problems previously difficult individually.

> "we noticed that in these group interviews, the students collaborated together to successfully find the answers to these conceptual problems that had been in general quite difficult for previous students to complete individually"
