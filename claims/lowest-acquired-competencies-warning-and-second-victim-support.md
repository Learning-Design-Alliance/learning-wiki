`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Phase 1 consensus conference descriptive ratings (N=173 mentors). The knowledge items on assertive warning (mean 3.1) and dealing with a bad reaction (mean 2.9) scored lowest; corresponding skills items scored 3.2 and 3.1.

> "mentors believed that trainees had difficulties in knowing how to assertively warn another health care professional (of the same or a higher level) of the risk of ignoring an important patient safety rule (mean 3.1, SD 1.0; CV=33.1) and how to deal constructively with their possible bad reaction (mean 2.9, SD 1.1; CV=38.6)"

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Phase 1 descriptive ratings: the peer-support skill item scored mean 3.1, SD 1.3, with the highest coefficient of variability (CV=41.5) among skills, indicating varied mentor perceptions across institutions.

> "another of the least trained skills was offering peer support to a colleague involved in an adverse event to prevent or minimize the second victim response (mean 3.1, SD 1.3; CV=41.5)"
