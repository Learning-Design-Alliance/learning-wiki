Cowhy 2017¶

Cowhy, J.R. & de la Torre, M. (2017). The Success Project: The Implementation and Early Outcomes of a Middle Grade Program. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/success-project-implementation-and-early-outcomes-middle-grade-program

q2 · i? · design · r3

Formative evaluation of 10 CPS Success schools using administrative data to monitor attendance and on-track during the program's first two years. The report states "positive changes in the Success schools, especially in Year 2." Analyses are descriptive, not causal.

"Students’ attendance and middle grades on- track rates point to positive changes in the Success schools, especially in Year 2."
