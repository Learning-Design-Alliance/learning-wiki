`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of the multicenter expert-consensus study: sixteen judges rated 100 DCN competencies ("A total of 3,200 responses were thus collected") for OSCE and vOSCE suitability, with unresolved items resolved in virtual meetings. Consensus favored the OSCE for "97% and 92% of these items, respectively."

> "Via the use of virtual meetings (Delphi and focus groups), consensus was reached regarding the capacity of the OSCE and vOSCE to evaluate 97% and 92% of these items, respectively."
