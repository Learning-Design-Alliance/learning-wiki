## Evidence

### Salem 2013

Salem, A. A. M. S. (2013). The Impact of Multiple Intelligences-Based Instruction on Developing Speaking Skills of the Pre-Service Teachers of English. English Language Teaching, 6(9), 53-66. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n9p53

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-post quasi-experiment (n = 60). Making-suggestions pretest M = 8.48 (SD = 3.481), posttest M = 11.50 (SD = 0.624), t = 6.808, p ≤ .001, effect size 0.516, i.e. "a statistically significant difference between mean scores of the subjects of the study on the pre and posttest on the making suggestions component."
