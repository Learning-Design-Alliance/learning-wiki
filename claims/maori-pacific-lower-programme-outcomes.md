## Evidence

### Wikaire Erena 2016

Wikaire Erena, Curtis Elana, Cormack Donna, Jiang Yannan, McMillan Louise, Loto Rob, Reid Papaarangi. (2016). Patterns of privilege: A total cohort analysis of admission and academic outcomes for Māori, Pacific and non-Māori non-Pacific health professional students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0782-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of programme outcomes (graduation from FMHS and from intended programme) in the 2686-student cohort, tested by chi-square with nMnP as reference.

> "A higher proportion of nMnP students (80 %) graduated from the FMHS compared to 71 % of Māori (p = 0.0078) and 72 % of Pacific (p = 0.0023) students."

### Wikaire Erena 2016 (2)

Wikaire Erena, Curtis Elana, Cormack Donna, Jiang Yannan, McMillan Louise, Loto Rob, Reid Papaarangi. (2016). Patterns of privilege: A total cohort analysis of admission and academic outcomes for Māori, Pacific and non-Māori non-Pacific health professional students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0782-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same observational analysis of year 2–4 programme GPA (0–9 scale), tested by ANOVA with pairwise comparisons against the nMnP reference group.

> "The average GPA from year 2 to completion was significantly lower for Māori (mean = 4.36, p < 0.0001) and Pacific (mean = 3.48, p < 0.0001) students compared to nMnP students (mean = 5.21)."
