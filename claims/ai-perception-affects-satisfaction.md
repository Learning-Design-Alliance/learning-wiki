Kanzow Philipp, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Wilkinson Jenny, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Nourredine Mikaïl. (2026). Voice Cloning Using AI vs Traditional Audio Recording for Prerecorded Courses in Medical Pedagogy: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/86569

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is an author interpretation based on a single participant's reaction, not a tested result. The authors note that "individual opinions on AI can shape the learner's experience."

> "In our study, while overall satisfaction remained high, one participant explicitly identified the voice as AI generated and expressed marked discontent. This reaction illustrates that, even when objective measures of learning are unaffected, individual opinions on AI can shape the learner's experience."
