## Evidence

### Medveczky D 2025

Medveczky D, Mitchell A, Leopardi E, Dawson A. (2025). Benefits of a near-peer program from the tutors’ perspective: a survey of Australian junior doctors in a regional teaching program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06762-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 26 junior doctor tutors on future teaching aspirations using visual analogue scales. "Respondents indicated a fairly strong likelihood to make teaching a major part of their future careers" and reported the NPMT experience somewhat strongly influenced this desire (mean 74.7).

> "Respondents indicated a fairly strong likelihood to make teaching a major part of their future careers (mean 84, 95%CI 78.6–89.4) and to pursue formal teaching opportunities in the future (mean 84.3, 95%CI 77.1–91.5)"
