Network analysis within demographic cluster 1 (n=10, younger, less teaching experience, diverse nationality) from the 25-participant study. Printed effect size τ = 0.79 (p = 0.01): "a significant positive correlation between the amount of GPT keywords used and their scores in cooperativity".
