## Evidence

### Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Gonnermann-Müller, J., Haase, J., Leins, N., Igel, M., Fackeldey, K., & Pokutta, S. (2026). FACET: Multi-Agent AI Supporting Teachers in Scaling Differentiated Learning for Diverse Students. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.24963/ijcai.2026/797](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2026/797)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive online survey in which 70 in-service K–12 teachers (M age = 40.5, SD = 9.5; 73% female) rated five FACET-generated grade-7 mathematics worksheets on four dimensions using a 6-point Likert scale; data were analyzed descriptively with means and standard deviations, no inferential tests.

> "Didactic structure and clarity received consistently favorable ratings, exceeding the scale midpoint across worksheets for profiles 1–4 (range:3.5−4.50)."
