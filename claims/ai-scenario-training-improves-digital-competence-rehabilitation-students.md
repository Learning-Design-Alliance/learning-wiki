## Evidence

### Yıldız Rıdvan 2026

Yıldız Rıdvan, Ni̇şanci Onur Seçgin. (2026). The effect of artificial intelligence–based scenarios on the clinical education of rehabilitation students: an anatomy-based randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09220-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group ANOVA of change scores in a randomized controlled study with 90 rehabilitation students (30 per group), reported in the Results section. The traditional-only group showed "lower improvements compared to Groups 1 and 2" in digital competence.

> "Between-group analyses showed a significant difference only for digital competence change scores (p<.001), with Group 3 showing lower improvements compared to Groups 1 and 2."

### Yıldız Rıdvan 2026 (2)

Yıldız Rıdvan, Ni̇şanci Onur Seçgin. (2026). The effect of artificial intelligence–based scenarios on the clinical education of rehabilitation students: an anatomy-based randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09220-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired t-tests reported in the Results section of the same randomized controlled study. Digital competence improved significantly in the AI-supported and internet-supported groups, while "no significant change was observed for group 3".

> "a significant change was observed in the digital competence-self-assessment variable for groups 1 and 2 (p < .05), while no significant change was observed for group 3 (p > .05)."
