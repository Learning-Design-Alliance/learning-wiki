Survey of 708 responses from summer 2020 remote introductory physics and astronomy courses at two institutions, using 5-point Likert items with 95% confidence intervals. The article reports "a slight preference for synchronous classes over asyn- chronous classes" at Institution A and a stronger preference at Institution B.
