## Evidence

### Duan D  2025

Duan D , Gan MJS , Lim LZ , Foong KWC . (2025). Simulator practice of temporary anchorage device (TAD) insertion on inter‐radicular sites improves learning and confidence in dental residency. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire with five-point Likert-type confidence ratings from 18 residents; means of 1.9 and 3.45 out of 5 on pretest and post-test. The article reports a significant increase (p < 0.05) and no significant between-group difference on Fisher's exact test.

> "There was a significant increase in participants' confidence after two rounds of simulation testing on the TAD simulator (p < 0.05). When comparing the post‐test confidence levels between intervention and control groups, the Fisher's exact test showed no significant difference between the two groups."
