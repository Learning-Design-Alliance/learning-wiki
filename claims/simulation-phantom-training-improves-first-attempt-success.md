## Evidence

### Oh Eun Jung 2020

Oh Eun Jung, Lee Jong-Hwan, Kwon Eun Jin, Min Jeong Jin. (2020). Simulation-based training using a vessel phantom effectively improved first attempt success and dynamic needle-tip positioning ability for ultrasound-guided radial artery cannulation in real patients: An assessor-blinded randomized controlled study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234567

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Assessor-blinded randomized controlled trial: 21 anesthesiology residents randomized to simulation (n = 11) or control (n = 10); each performed four cannulations in real patients, 84 total. The "first attempt success rate in real patients was significantly higher in the simulation group" (36/44 vs 20/40).

> "The first attempt success rate in real patients was significantly higher in the simulation group compared to the control group (81.8% vs. 50.0%, P = 0.002)."
