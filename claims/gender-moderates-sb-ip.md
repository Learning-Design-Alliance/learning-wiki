## Evidence

### Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2025

Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Sense of Belonging and Intent to Persist: Mediating Role of Motivation and Moderating Role of Gender in Physics and Astronomy Graduate Students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup probit regressions with gender coded binary (1 for women, 0 for men), reported in the findings section and Fig. 5. Women's slope was 1.221 versus 0.412 for men; at low belonging "women report lower IP than men" and their intent to persist eventually exceeds men's.

> "The SB–IP slope is substan- tially steeper for women than for men (1.221 vs. 0.412). At lower levels of SB, women report lower IP than men, sug- gesting a greater vulnerability to social disconnect."
