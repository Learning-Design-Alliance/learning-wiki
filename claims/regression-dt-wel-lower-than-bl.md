## Evidence

### Balakrishnan Athira 2021

Balakrishnan Athira, Nair Sreedharan, Kunhikatta Vijayanarayana, Rashid Muhammed, Unnikrishnan M. K., Jagannatha P. S., Chandran Viji P., Khera Kanav, Thunga Girish. (2021). Effectiveness of blended learning in pharmacy education: An experimental study using clinical research modules. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256814

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression with total score as dependent variable and BL as reference category; the model "showed an R2 value of 0.41". DT coefficient β3 = -5.32 (p = 0.007) and WEL coefficient β4 = -12.03 (P<0.01) as printed in Table 3.

> "The coefficient on Intervention (β3 = -5.32) means that, holding all other variables constant, the score on the final test of a student who was taught via the DT is expected to decrease 5.32 points compared to a student who received the BL intervention."
