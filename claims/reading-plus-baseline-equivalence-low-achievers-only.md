### What Works Clearinghouse 2010

What Works Clearinghouse. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Reading Plus®. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/419

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The report's footnote on the Reading Plus (2008) quasi-experiment states that "the intervention and comparison groups were not shown to be equivalent at baseline" for these analyses. The WWC therefore based effectiveness ratings only on the 6,070 low-achieving intervention and 7,058 comparison students.

> "Analyses for the whole sample, for each grade (5, 6, 7, 8, and 9), and for subpopulations (by ethnicity, for the average/high achieving subgroup), and for students receiving 40 or more Reading Plus ® lessons, do not meet WWC evidence standards because the intervention and comparison groups were not shown to be equivalent at baseline."
