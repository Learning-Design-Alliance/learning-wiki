## Evidence

### Butti 2025

Butti, N., Urgesi, C., Decio, A., Pezzani, L., Milani, D., and Montirosso, R. (2025). A Further Characterisation of the Neuropsychological Profile, Social Perception, and Academic Skills in Sotos Syndrome. Journal of Intellectual Disability Research, 69: 664–674. https://doi.org/10.1111/jir.13250

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Paired-sample t test comparing the two NEPSY-II social perception subtests (theory of mind, affect recognition) in the cross-sectional Sotos syndrome cohort. The difference was non-significant with a printed negligible effect (Cohen's d = 0.17); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Comparable scores were observed in the two subtests of social perception (t 25 = 0.85, p = 0.406, Cohen's d = 0.17)."
