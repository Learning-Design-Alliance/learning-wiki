## Evidence

### Belle Dang 2024

Belle Dang, Andy Nguyen and Sanna Järvelä. (2024). Deliberative Interactions for Socially Shared Regulation in Collaborative Learning: An AI-Driven Learning Analytics Study. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8393

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Epistemic network analysis compared structural connections of deliberative codes across cluster types. The deliberative ENA centroid difference was significant with "r = 0.68"; the regulatory network showed a weaker but significant difference (r = 0.34). Type 1's weighted region focused on "Establish strategy," "Educate each other," "Agree and implement" and "Monitoring."

> "the Mann-Whitney test showed that the mean square centroid of Type 1 (MdnType 1 = -0.26) is significantly different from Type 2 (MdnType 2 = -0.03, U = 550.00, p < .001, r = 0.68)."
