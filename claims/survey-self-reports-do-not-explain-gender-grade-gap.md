## Evidence

### Easton 2023

Easton, J.Q. & Diaz, B. (2023). Lasting differences: Math grades and gender. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/lasting-differences

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sequential regression models on 28,517 students with 5Essentials Survey measures (academic effort and work, social well-being, math instruction) added incrementally. The report states adding the controls "explain a small portion of the gender difference, but the difference largely remains."

> "The basic model, only comparing average differences between genders with no controls estimates a gender difference of 0.273 GPA points. The model with all controls, Model 7 estimates a gender difference of 0.236 GPA points."
