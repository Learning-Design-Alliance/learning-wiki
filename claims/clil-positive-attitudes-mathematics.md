### Ouazizi 2016

Ouazizi, K. (2016). The Effects of CLIL Education on the Subject Matter (Mathematics) and the Target Language (English). Latin American Journal of Content and Language Integrated Learning, 9(1), 110-137. https://doi.org/10.5294/laclil.2016.9.1.5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report questionnaire administered to the 10-student CLIL class, covering attitudes, aptitude, and out-of-class English use. The author reports "all students’ attitudes either changed from neutral to slightly positive or they maintain a positive attitude".

> "there is no single student whose attitude changed from positive to negative during his/her experience with CLIL education. Instead, we notice that all students’ attitudes either changed from neutral to slightly positive or they maintain a positive attitude"
