## Evidence

### Laaksonen M 2025

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null findings from the survey's variable-association analyses (Table 4); no equivalence testing was reported, so these results indicate non-significant associations only.

> "There was no statistically significant association between variables concerning the educational level of respondents (p = .326), the number of years working in nursing (p = .908), and whether their education included genetics (p = .099) in relation to the KS in this study."

### Laaksonen M 2025 (2)

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null results from the demographics analysis (Table 6): age T-tests also non-significant for ethics (p = .148) and equity (p = .184); only test statistics were printed, so no effect sizes.

> "Working experience in nursing did not have a statistically significant association with either the understanding ethics (Chi-Square Test p = .372) or equity issues (Chi-Square Test p = .943)."
