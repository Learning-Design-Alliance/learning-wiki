Survey analysis of 1,108 LEAs (N = 1,108; response rate = 100% for this item). On site offerings, "most LEAs (59%) plan to offer TK at all sites offering kindergarten." On day length, 68% planned only full-day TK, 11% both, and 21% only part-day.

> "most LEAs (59%) plan to offer TK at all sites offering kindergarten, and a fifth (20%) plan to offer TK at some of their sites."
