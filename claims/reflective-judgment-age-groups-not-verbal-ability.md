## Evidence

### Kitchener 1993

Kitchener, K. S., Lynch, C. L., Fischer, K. W., & Wood, P. K. (1993). Developmental Range of Reflective Judgment: The Effect of Contextual Support and Practice on Developmental Stage. Developmental Psychology, 29(5), 893-906. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1993-98188-001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA controlling the WISC-R/WAIS-R Vocabulary scaled score; the covariate, condition, and age effects were all significant (e.g., age F(12, 258) = 25.40, p < .001). The correlation between RJI mean and Vocabulary scores at first testing was .33, p < .001. No standardized effect size for the age effect was printed.

> "These analyses suggest that the age differences in RJI mean scores could not be statistically attributed to higher verbal ability in the successively older groups, nor could verbal ability account for the higher reflective judgment scores of those in the experimental groups versus the test-retest control group."
