Evidence¶

Ashley Chen 2024¶

Ashley Chen, Suchita E. Kumar, Rhea Varkhedi, Dillon H. Murphy. (2024). The Effect of Playback Speed and Distractions on the Comprehension of Audio and Audio-Visual Materials. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09917-7

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Experiment 4 assigned 222 undergraduates to audio-visual clips at 1x or 2x speed with or without realistic on-screen distractions (texts, calls, emails, news alerts) occupying about 12.5% of the screen. The multilevel model found "no differences in performance" between distraction conditions.

"There were no differences in performance between participants who watched videos with (M = 0.50, SD = 0.15) and without distractions (M = 0.52, SD = 0.16), F(1, 214) = 1.03, p = 0.312, /u1D6FD = 0.02."
