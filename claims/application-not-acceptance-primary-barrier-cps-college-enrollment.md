# Application, not acceptance, is the primary barrier preventing CPS students from enrolling in four-year colleges

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among CPS seniors who applied to a four-year college, 86 percent were accepted, indicating that failure to apply rather than rejection is the dominant barrier. [→ Roderick 2011](#roderick-2011)

## Evidence

### Roderick 2011

Roderick, M., Coca, V., & Nagaoka, J. (2011). Potholes on the Road to College: High School Effects in Shaping Urban Students' Participation in College Application, Four-year College Enrollment, and College Match. Sociology of Education, 84(3), 178-211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0038040711411280

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive tracking of the sequential college application process for 2005 CPS graduates aspiring to four-year degrees. Students who did not apply vastly outnumbered those rejected, consistent with Manski and Wise's (1983) conclusion.

> "Of students who report that they applied to a four-year college, 86 percent were accepted (0.51/0.59)."
