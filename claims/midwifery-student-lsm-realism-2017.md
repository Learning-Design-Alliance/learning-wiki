## Evidence

### Sadovnikova A 2020

Sadovnikova A, Chuisano SA, Ma K, Grabowski A, Stanley KP, Mitchell KB, Eglash A, Plott JS, Zielinski RE, Anderson OS. (2020). Development and evaluation of a high-fidelity lactation simulation model for health professional breastfeeding education. International Breastfeeding Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13006-020-0254-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Two 3-h breastfeeding workshops with lectures and eight clinical lactation skills cases; consent from 12 of 15 students, repeated measures analysis for nine students (HUM00148905). Students rated look realism "engorged (6.3/7) and not engorged (6.5/7)". All correctly identified two scars; descriptive ratings only.

> "Students agreed that both LSMs looked like a breastfeeding mother's chest both when engorged (6.3/7) and not engorged (6.5/7), but were not sure (4.3/7) if the skin felt realistic"
