## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rodrigo 2014

Rodrigo, V., Greenberg, D., & Segal, D. (2014). Changes in reading habits by low literate adults through extensive reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 26(1), 73-91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey item on liking reading administered pre, post, and follow-up; Chi-square showed no group difference at any point (p > .05), and no difference by native/non-native language. The authors call the positive baseline attitude a surprising finding given adult education research.

> "Table 2 shows that a majority of the participants in each group said they did (96% in ER group vs. 89% in No-ER groups), and only some indicated that they did not. Chi-square shows that the two groups did not significantly differ in attitude toward reading at the beginning of the treatment (p > .05)."
