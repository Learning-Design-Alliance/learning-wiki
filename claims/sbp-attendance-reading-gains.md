Review of a Wisconsin elementary school study (Bartfeld et al.) exploiting within-school variation over time; printed estimates were a 3.5%pt reduction (p = 0.015) and reading scores "0.080 SD higher" among boys (p = 0.018), with improvements greater among lower-income students.

> "A linear regression found that SBP availability was associated with a reduction in the percentage of students with low attendance (3.5%pt reduction; p = 0.015) and an increase in reading test scores among boys (0.080 SD higher; p = 0.018) [32]."
