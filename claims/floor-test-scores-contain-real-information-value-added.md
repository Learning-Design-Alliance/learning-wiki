## Evidence

### Alexandra Resch 2014

Alexandra Resch, Eric Isenberg. (2014). How Do Test Scores at the Floor and Ceiling Affect Value-Added Estimates? Working Paper 33. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/how-do-test-scores-at-the-floor-and-ceiling-affect-value-added-estimates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Analysis of data from a large urban district finds that "students who score at the floor in one year score at or near the bottom of the scale, on average, in other years," so floor scores are informative rather than uninformative for value-added estimation.

> "test scores at the floor contain real information about students' performance, because students who score at the floor in one year score at or near the bottom of the scale, on average, in other years"
