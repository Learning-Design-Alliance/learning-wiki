He 2018¶

Ping He. (2018). On Reading Comprehension Teaching for English Majors under Relevance Theory. English Language Teaching, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n1p46

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-experiment baseline assessment using a questionnaire survey and a simulated TEM-4 reading comprehension test administered to both classes of English majors. The printed means (70.2 and 69.7) are descriptive; no statistical equivalence test is reported.
