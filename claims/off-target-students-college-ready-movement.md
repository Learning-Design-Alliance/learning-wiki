## Evidence

### Dye 2013

Dye, J., Cheatham, T., Rowell, G. H., Barlow, A. T., & Carlton, R. (2013). The Impact of Modeling Instruction within the Inverted Curriculum on Student Achievement in Science. Electronic Journal of Science Education, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1188400

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Categorical movement analysis (Table 9) of students more than two points below the PLAN science benchmark; the article states neither ACT data nor baseline statistics predicted this growth in the treatment groups. Percentages are descriptive.

> "Table 9 shows that 8% of the baseline group who were ―off -target‖ in science on the PLAN, tested college ready in science on the ACT. Comparable numbers for Treatments 1 and 2 are 18% and 21% , respectively."
