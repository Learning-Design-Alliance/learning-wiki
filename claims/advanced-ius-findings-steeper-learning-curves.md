`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same multicenter trainee cohort; advanced parameter group agreement improved from "κ = 0.728 (95% CI: 0.649-0.806)" to "κ = 0.854 (95% CI: 0.804-0.905)", with most gains in the latter half of training.

> "More pronounced improvements were observed for advanced variables, which increased from κ = 0.728 (95% CI: 0.649-0.806) in Period 1 to κ = 0.854 (95% CI: 0.804-0.905) in Period 3. Most improvements for advanced variables occurred during the latter half of the training period"
