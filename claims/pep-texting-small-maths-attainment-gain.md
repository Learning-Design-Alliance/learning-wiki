# Texting parents about tests, homework and lesson content produced a small positive effect on maths attainment (ES 0.067) in a cluster RCT

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Children whose parents received PEP texts experienced about one month of additional progress in maths compared to control children, a small positive effect (ES 0.067, 95% CI 0.041–0.092). [→ Miller 2016](#miller-2016)

## Evidence

### Miller 2016

Miller, S., Davison, J., Yohanis, J., Sloan, S., Gildea, A. and Thurston, A. (2016). Texting Parents: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581121

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Results table of a two-armed multi-site cluster randomised controlled trial (randomisation at Key Stage level) reporting the maths outcome for 5,613 analysed pupils: an effect size of "0.067" with confidence interval (0.041, 0.092), equivalent to about one month of additional progress.
