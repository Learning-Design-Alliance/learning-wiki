### Dahl A. 2019

Dahl A., Brownell C. A. (2019). The Social Origins of Human Prosociality. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721419830386

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review synthesis of developmental research on infant helping. The authors propose that infants' earliest acts of helping stem from "a motivation to engage with others rather than a prosocial motive to promote others' welfare," making them precursors rather than prosocial acts.

> "The first major developmental transformation in helping is from participatory acts without prosocial intent, evident by the first birthday, to acts motivated by prosocial intent, evident by the second birthday."
