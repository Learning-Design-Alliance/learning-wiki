## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression analysis using county-level teacher bias estimates derived from Project Implicit data from approximately 181,000 White teacher participants across 2,834 counties in 2017-18. The association held across all three years.

> "County-level teacher implicit racial bias was associated with greater Black–White disparities in AP course enrollment across all 3 y (Ps < 0.001; Fig. 1, Left)."

### Park Yeji 2026 (2)

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression analysis using the same county-level teacher bias estimates. The association was consistently driven by lower enrollment rates for Black students, but not associated with enrollment rates for White students.

> "County-level teacher explicit racial bias was also associated with greater Black–White disparities in AP course enrollment across all 3 y (Ps < 0.001; Fig. 1, Left)."
