`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, contrasting their forced-choice ranking results with the randomized controlled study by Boehler et al. on student reactions to different kinds of feedback. This is the authors' attribution, not a re-analysis.

> "They found that students preferred praise over constructive behavior-orientated feedback, but the results of the instant study diverge from that findings as the students in this study evaluated feedback and gave it the highest ranks in our forced choice question after the practical training."
