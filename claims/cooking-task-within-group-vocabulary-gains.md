## Evidence

### Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse 2012

Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse. (2012). Interactive Digital Kitchen: The impact on Language learning. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://digitalinstitute.ncl.ac.uk/ilablearn/kitchen

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t-test on the experimental group's pre- and post-test scores in the one-hour cooking session. The article states "the difference in the students' performance after the intervention is highly significant."

> "the mean score of the experimental group pre-test and post-test were 7.39 and 17.6 respectively, with a level of significance value of 0.00 (t = - 24.02, df = 27, p < .05)"

### Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse 2012 (2)

Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse. (2012). Interactive Digital Kitchen: The impact on Language learning. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://digitalinstitute.ncl.ac.uk/ilablearn/kitchen

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t-test on the control group's scores. The article notes "the control group test scores also improved although they were not exposed to the Digital Kitchen."

> "the mean score of the control group pre-test was 7.27 and of the post-test was at 15.65. In fact, the difference between the two means was also statistically significant ( t = - 16.58, df = 25, p < .05)"
