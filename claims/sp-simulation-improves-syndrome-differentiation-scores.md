# SP-based simulation improved TCM syndrome differentiation and therapeutic regimen scores, with the SSP-TCM advantage not significant in 2018

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SSP-TCM and OSP-TCM trainees scored higher than TM trainees on TCM syndrome differentiation and therapeutic regimen in 2019 and 2020, but the 2018 SSP-TCM versus TM difference was not statistically significant (Pa = 0.057). [→ Zeng Jinhao 2023](#zeng-jinhao-2023)

## Evidence

### Zeng Jinhao 2023

Zeng Jinhao, Liang Shuang, Fu Xiaoxu, Guo Jing, Bai Yaolin, Zhou Shan, Du Quanyu, Wang Zhenxing, Zhang Xiyu, Peng Sihan, Wen Lijuan, Li Wenyuan, Li Bin, Yang Han, Zhang Yi. (2023). Student standardized patients versus occupational standardized patients for improving clinical competency among TCM medical students: a 3-year prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04198-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Written syndrome differentiation and therapeutic regimen scored on a standardized 100-point scale. ANOVA was significant in all three years; the 2018 SSP-TCM versus TM post-hoc p was 0.057, above the 0.05 threshold, so that contrast was not significant.

> "the results of one-way ANOVA for comparing the differences among the three groups (2018/19/20, F = 4.171/4.681/4.223, P = 0.022/0.014/0.020) and post-hoc analysis revealed that trainees assigned to SSP-TCM and OSP-TCM groups scored higher than those assigned to TM group (2018/19/20, Pa=0.057/0.037/0.036; Pb=0.032/0.024/0.043)"
