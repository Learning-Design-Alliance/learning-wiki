## Evidence

### Dong Jia 2026

Dong Jia, Yang Xiqing, Yang Xi, Tan Dingliang, Wang Kexin. (2026). The relationship between growth mindset and mathematics achievement in Chinese middle school students: The role of achievement goals and grit. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05153-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses in the 691-student cross-sectional sample (gender controlled) showed growth mindset positively predicted three goal orientations and negatively predicted performance-avoidance goals, with all four β coefficients significant as printed.

> "growth mindset was significantly and positively associated with mastery-approach goals (β = 0.1527, p < 0.001), mastery-avoidance goals (β = 0.1180, p < 0.01), and performance-approach goals (β = 0.1500, p < 0.001), while it was significantly and negatively associated with performance-avoidance goals (β = -0.1275, p < 0.001)."
