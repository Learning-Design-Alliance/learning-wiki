`q2 i?` Participants' writing content was rated higher after outlining training (mean 3.8) than after depicting training (mean 3.5), a statistically significant difference. [→ Hung 2018](#hung-2018)

## Evidence

### Hung 2018

Hung, B. P., & Van, L. T. (2018). Depicting and Outlining as Pre-writing Strategies: Experimental Results and Learners' Opinions. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 451-464. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11231a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
