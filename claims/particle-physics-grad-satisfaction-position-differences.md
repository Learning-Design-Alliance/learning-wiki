## Evidence

### O. Bitter 2022

O. Bitter, E.V. Hansen, S. Kravitz, V. Velan, and Y. You (2022). Transforming U.S. Particle Physics Education: A Snowmass 2021 Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests of independence on survey ratings from 333 graduate-education respondents found position differences in satisfaction: "tenured faculty were statistically more likely than other groups to rate their overall and particle-speciﬁc graduate education as ‘extremely satisﬁed’". Median ratings were 'somewhat satisfied' overall.

> "tenured faculty were statistically more likely than other groups to rate their overall and particle-speciﬁc graduate education as ‘extremely satisﬁed’ ( p = 0.01, p < 0.01 respectively). Graduate students were statistically more likely than other groups to rate their overall graduate education as ‘somewhat satisﬁed’ ( p < 0.01)"
