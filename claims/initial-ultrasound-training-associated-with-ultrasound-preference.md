Multivariable logistic regression on 454 trainees from a multicenter cross-sectional survey in Eastern China, with prespecified covariates of age, sex, standardized training certificate, and initial learning method. The article reports "aOR = 6.83; 95% confidence interval (CI), 3.85–12.11" for IJV cannulation.
