### Yifan Liu 2026

Yifan Liu, Jaime Arguello, Orland Hoeber, Chang Liu, Soo Young Rieh, Luanne Sinnamon, et al. (2026). Report on CHIIR 2026 Workshop on Generative AI and Academic Search (GAI&AS). ACM SIGIR Forum. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08936

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The report describes a qualitative analysis of prompts issued to a GenAI system by people learning about diffusion and osmosis (Urgo et al., 2026), presented in a lightning talk. Participants also issued retrieval-like prompts and prompts uniquely suited to GenAI, such as clarifying confusion and testing understanding. No effect size is printed.

> "Finally, participants asked the GenAI to perform tasks that may have been important for them to do themselves. Participants asked the GenAI system to differentiate between concepts (e.g., Differences between diffusion and osmosis.); provide ideas about what they should learn (e.g., What is important to know?); and simplify a previous response (e.g., Too long, one paragraph.)"
