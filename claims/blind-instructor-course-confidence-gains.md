### JooYoung Seo 2024

JooYoung Seo, Sile O'Modhrain, Yilin Xia, Sanchita Kamath, Bongshin Lee, and James M. Coughlan. (2024). Designing Born-Accessible Courses in Data Science and Visualization: Challenges and Opportunities of a Remote Curriculum Taught by Blind Instructors to Blind Students. EUROVIS 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02568)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative analysis (Section 4.1) of pre- and post-course self-report Likert confidence surveys from nine blind participants, compared with the Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test. The authors report "statistically significant improvements in participants' confidence" for the named chart tasks; medians in Table 2 show, for example, bar plot creation "increased from 2 to 5*".

> "These results indicate statistically significant improvements in participants' confidence in creating bar plots, box plots, and scatter plots, and interpreting box plots as a result of attending the data science summer course."
