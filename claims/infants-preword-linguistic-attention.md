### Young-Scholten 1999

Young-Scholten, M. (1999). Focus on Form and Linguistic Competence: Why Krashen Is Still Right about Acquisition. Paper presented at AAAL, Stamford. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED432919

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

A review of high amplitude sucking and head-turning studies (discussed in Jusczyk 1997), summarized in Table 1, shows that "the child amasses a good deal of information about its language before words are produced", including prosody, word boundaries and phonotactic cues.

> "Findings such as those shown in Table 1. indicate that the child amasses a good deal of information about its language before words are produced between the ages of 10 and 12 months, and even before words are comprehended between 8 and 10 months."
