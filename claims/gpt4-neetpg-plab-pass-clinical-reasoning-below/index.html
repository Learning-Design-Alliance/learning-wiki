Bodke Parag 2026¶

Bodke Parag, Jumle Rahul. (2026). Large language models in undergraduate medical education: a systematic review of examination performance, knowledge acquisition, and learner perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09999-7

q3 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of five NEET-PG and three PLAB benchmark studies. The review reports "GPT-4 exceeded the PLAB 1 pass threshold in all studies (range: 72%–75%)" and mean NEET-PG accuracy of 74%, described as modestly lower than USMLE performance.

"Across three UK PLAB studies, GPT-4 exceeded the PLAB 1 pass threshold in all studies (range: 72%–75%) while falling below equivalent pass marks for clinical communication and procedural reasoning items"
