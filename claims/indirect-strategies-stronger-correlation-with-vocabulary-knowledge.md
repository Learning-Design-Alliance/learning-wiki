## Evidence

### Teng 2015

Teng, F. (2015). Assessing the Relationship between Vocabulary Learning Strategy Use and Vocabulary Knowledge. PASAA, 49, 39-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077911

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Repeated two-tailed Pearson correlation between direct and indirect strategies and depth and breadth of VK for the 145 participants, answering research question 3. All correlations exceeded 0.50 (P<0.05), regarded as a 'large correlational effect size' (Cohen, 1988, p. 80); indirect strategies correlated more highly with both dimensions than direct strategies.

> "However, participants' use of indirect strategies had a higher correlation with the depth of VK ( r=0.78) and breadth of VK ( r=0.66) than the use of direct strategies."
