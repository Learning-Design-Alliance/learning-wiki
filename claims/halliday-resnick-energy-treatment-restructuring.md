# Later editions of Halliday-Resnick restructured the energy treatment toward energy conservation, moving energy chapters later and adding internal energy and center-of-mass energy sections

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` A structural change in the treatment of energy appears from the fifth English edition of Halliday-Resnick, with energy chapters moved after particle-system chapters and a novel chapter treatment previewing the first law of thermodynamics. [→ Suárez 2020](#suarez-2020)

## Evidence

### Suárez 2020

Suárez, A., Baccino, D., Martí, A. C., & Monteiro, M. (2020). Inconsistencies and errors in traditional approaches to energy in our introductory courses. Avances en la Enseñanza de la Física, 2(1), en. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.09480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.09480)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Documentary overview in section 6 of the tables of contents of the first five Spanish editions of Halliday-Resnick (1960s through 2000s). The authors identify two subsets of editions and report that "a change of structure in the treatment of the topic of energy is evidenced from the fifth English edition".

> "A change of structure in the treatment of the topic of energy is evidenced from the fifth English edition (corresponding to the fourth Spanish edition) (Resnick, Halliday y Krane, 2002)."

### Suárez 2020 (2)

Suárez, A., Baccino, D., Martí, A. C., & Monteiro, M. (2020). Inconsistencies and errors in traditional approaches to energy in our introductory courses. Avances en la Enseñanza de la Física, 2(1), en. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.09480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.09480)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Same documentary overview in section 6: in the first editions the focus is on conservation of mechanical energy, whereas in the more recent editions the focus is on the idea of the conservation of energy, with sections on work done on a system, internal energy, frictional work, and energy of the center of mass.

> "Perhaps the most relevant conceptual change observed in our general overview of Halliday-Resnick is that the last chapter on energy in the 5 th English editions is closer to living up to its name than in the first editions."
