# Medical students' self-perceived weight management counseling skill is discordant with their observed OSCE performance for weight-related advising tasks

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Despite most students self-reporting as moderately or very skilled in identifying and informing patients that their weight is a health concern, only a minority performed this task on the OSCE (34.4% MME, 33.4% TE). [→ Ockene JK 2021](#ockene-jk-2021)
`q3 i?` A similar discordance was found for advising weight loss (26.4% MME, 25.5% TE performed it) and for discussing weight history and prior weight loss experience (24.6% MME, 19.7% TE). [→ Ockene JK 2021 (2)](#ockene-jk-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Ockene JK 2021

Ockene JK, Pbert L, Crawford S, Frisard CF, Pendharkar JA, Sadasivam RS, Faro J, Okuliar C, Eno C, Margo K, Shaw MA, Soleymani T, Stadler DD, Warrier S, White K, Geller AC. (2021). Teaching Medical Students to Help Patients Manage Their Weight: Outcomes of an Eight-School Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-020-06571-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of Table 2 self-report percentages with Table 1 OSCE checklist performance in the same trial; the authors describe this as a discrepancy between students' perception of their skills and actual performance. No effect size printed.

> "Despite the majority of students in both groups self-reporting as moderately, or very skilled, in identifying and informing patients with obesity that their weight is a health concern, only a minority performed this task on the OSCE."

### Ockene JK 2021 (2)

Ockene JK, Pbert L, Crawford S, Frisard CF, Pendharkar JA, Sadasivam RS, Faro J, Okuliar C, Eno C, Margo K, Shaw MA, Soleymani T, Stadler DD, Warrier S, White K, Geller AC. (2021). Teaching Medical Students to Help Patients Manage Their Weight: Outcomes of an Eight-School Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-020-06571-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of OSCE item percentages (Table 1) alongside self-reported skill; they contrast this with their prior smoking cessation trial where students performed well at advising.

> "A similar discordance was found for advising weight loss, which contrasts with our prior study in smoking cessation education where medical students showed high performance."
