### Chunliang Yang 2018

Chunliang Yang, Rosalind Potts and David R. Shanks. (2018). Enhancing learning and retrieval of new information: a review of the forward testing effect. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0024-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports paired-associate forward testing experiments by Weinstein et al. and Yang et al. using four lists of face-name pairs with Interim Test and Interim Distractor conditions; the quote gives the doubled name recall, and the review adds that distractor groups showed substantially more PI and that the effect extends to Swahili-English and Euskara-English pairs.
