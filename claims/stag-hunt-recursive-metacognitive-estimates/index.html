Evidence¶

Frith Chris D 2012¶

Frith Chris D. (2012). The role of metacognition in human social interactions. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2012.0123

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review describes Yoshida et al.'s computational model (as cited) of the stag-hunt game, in which optimal responding is achieved by estimating the partner's degree of recursion from sequential choices. Certainty about this estimate is characterized as metacognitive knowledge.

"the two key parameters that you need to estimate for optimal play of this game are your partner's degree of recursion and your degree of certainty in this estimate."
