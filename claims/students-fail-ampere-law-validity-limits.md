## Evidence

### Álvaro Suárez 2024

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C. Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2024). Learning difficulties among students when applying Ampère-Maxwell's law and its implications for teaching. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of written answers to question 1, in which two charged spheres connected by a wire are encircled by surfaces S1 and S2 bound by the same curve. Only "Category A (21,6%) includes answers that correctly understand Ampère's law and its limitations"; the rest showed incomplete or no comprehension.

> "Category A (21,6%) includes answers that correctly understand Ampère's law and its limitations."

### Álvaro Suárez 2024 (2)

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C. Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2024). Learning difficulties among students when applying Ampère-Maxwell's law and its implications for teaching. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' discussion of the question 1 results, reporting that "the confusion between flux and circulation" affected "approximately one in every four students (categories B2 and C2)". The discussion also notes this difficulty converges with results from the study by Hernández et al.

> "We identified several explanations that show a limited or incorrect comprehension of Ampère's law, highlighting the confusion between flux and circulation for approximately one in every four students (categories B2 and C2)."
