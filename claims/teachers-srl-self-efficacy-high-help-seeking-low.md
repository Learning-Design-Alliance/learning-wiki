## Evidence

### Avivit Arvatz 2025

Avivit Arvatz, Roee Peretz, Yehudit Judy Dori. (2025). Self-regulated learning and reflection: a tool for teachers and students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09415-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Related-sample Friedman's two-way ANOVA on the aggregated teacher sample (N = 154) yielded significant differences between factors (χ2(5) = 126.24, p < 0.000); Dunn's pairwise post hoc tests with Bonferroni correction showed self-efficacy differed significantly from all other factors, and self-assessed reflection was medium.

> "The tendency was to rate help-seeking as the lowest practiced factor in the teachers' SRL actions (between "seldom true for me" and "sometimes true for me"), and self-efficacy—as the highest"
