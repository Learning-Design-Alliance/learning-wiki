Analysis of transfer-rate trends and conservative rate estimates counting all transfers and alternative diploma recipients as non-graduates. Even under the most conservative estimation, nearly a 20 percentage point increase in diploma earning remains.

"During the last four years, transfer rates have dropped each year, with 14 percent of students transferring from the 2014 cohort, compared to 18 percent in 2010. Thus, the most recent rise in graduation rates could not have come from coding more students as transfers than in the past."
