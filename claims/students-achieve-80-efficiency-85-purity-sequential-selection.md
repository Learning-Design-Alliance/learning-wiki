`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reported outcome of the selection stage of the Manchester/Imperial teaching laboratory, where students use simulation truth information and visualisation tools to tune topological cuts; the article states students "can typically achieve an efficiency of approximately 80% and a purity of around 85%". No effect size is printed.

> "Students are encouraged to assess the discriminating power of the available variables, select those most suitable for event selection, and use data visualisation tools to investigate correlations between variables to further optimise the selection requirements. Through this process, students can typically achieve an efficiency of approximately 80% and a purity of around 85%."
