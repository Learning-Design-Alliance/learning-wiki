## Evidence

### Rachel Love 2025

Rachel Love, Edith Law, Philip R. Cohen, Dana Kulić. (2025). Teaching a Conversational Agent using Natural Language: Effect on Learning and Engagement. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00461-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second exploratory two-way repeated measures ANOVA on untaught-feature learning. Sentence-selection produced significantly higher proportional learning of features never explicitly taught to the agent, suggesting incidental exposure benefits from reading full sentences.

> "participants in the sentence-selection condition learned signiﬁcantly more features they did not teach compared to the natural language condition (sentence-selection M = 23.6% (SD = 14.4); natural language M = 17.3% (SD = 10.3)), F(1, 39) = 7.51, p = .009."
