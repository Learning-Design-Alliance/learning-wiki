### Lima Rodrigo Antunes 2020

Lima Rodrigo Antunes, Soares Fernanda Cunha, Bezerra Jorge, de Barros Mauro Virgílio Gomes. (2020). Effects of a Physical Education Intervention on Academic Performance: A Cluster Randomised Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17124287

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis by age using linear mixed models, adjusted for baseline performance, sex, maternal education, sports court coverage and clustering. "students from the workshop intervention group presented higher academic performance" from age 16, with printed mean differences of 1.3 and 1.4 points; no effect size or p-value printed for this contrast.

> "From 16 years of age, students from the workshop intervention group presented higher academic performance in comparison to their peers in the doubling PE classes (1.3 points on average) and the control groups (1.4 points on average)."
