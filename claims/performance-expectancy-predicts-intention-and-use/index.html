Evidence¶

Vo 2020¶

Vo, H. N. (2020). Understanding higher education learners' acceptance and use of mobile devices for language learning: A Rasch-based path modeling approach. Computers and Education, 146, Article 103761. doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103761

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

In the path analysis of 293 learners' Rasch-calibrated measures, the direct path to use behavior was significant ("β = .22"), contrary to prior UTAUT studies where performance expectancy had no direct effect on use.

"The direct path from performance expectancy to use behavior was also significant at p < .001 level (β = .22)."
