## Evidence

### Pulfrey 2011

Pulfrey, C., Buchs, C., & Butera, F. (2011). Why grades engender performance-avoidance goals: The mediating role of autonomous motivation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(3), 683–700. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023911

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 2's mixed-effects model, interactions between task-level self-efficacy (Midgley et al. PALS scale, α = .83) and the two contrasts were tested; no fixed effect besides the grade contrast reached significance, and the authors report perceived competence "did not influence the adoption of performance-avoidance goals".

> "pretask perceptions of perceived competence in class exercises did not influence the adoption of performance-avoidance goals. Nor did they moderate or suppress the relationship between assessment style and pretask performance-avoidance goals"
