Gonny Schellings 2011¶

Gonny Schellings. (2011). Applying learning strategy questionnaires: problems and possibilities. Metacognition and Learning, 6, 91–109. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9069-5

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of four case-study students' protocol activity counts versus questionnaire scale means; the expected trend was visible across students. The author adds that the Orienting & planning scale "might be affected by social desirability."

"the student executing the highest number of activities (student 2: 292 activities) showed the highest overall score on the questionnaire ( M=2.17), whereas the student who executed the least number of activities (student 4: 74 activities) ended up with the lowest overall score on the questionnaire ( M=1.64)."
