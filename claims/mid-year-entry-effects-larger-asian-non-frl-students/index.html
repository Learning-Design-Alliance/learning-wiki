The study explores the moderating influence of stable students' characteristics and reports impacts are "somewhat larger for Asian students and those who do not qualify for free or reduced-price lunch." No subgroup effect sizes are printed on this page.

"These impacts are not driven by mid-year entrant characteristics and are somewhat larger for Asian students and those who do not qualify for free or reduced-price lunch."
