## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang S‐Y 2021

Yang S‐Y. (2021). Effectiveness of neonatal emergency nursing education through simulation training: Flipped learning based on Tanner's Clinical Judgement Model. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.748

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment (N = 65); knowledge measured with Yoo's 30-item neonatal emergency care knowledge tool (KR-20 = 0.71 in this study). The experimental group's mean rose from 13.23 to 15.06 while the control group's fell slightly; the ANCOVA difference was not significant.

> "ANCOVA using the prior knowledge score as a covariate revealed no significant mean differences scores (F = 0.64, p = .524) between the two groups. Thus, Hypothesis 1 was rejected."
