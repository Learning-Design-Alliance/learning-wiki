## Evidence

### Nadja Lindner 2025

Nadja Lindner, Korbinian Moeller, Frauke Hildebrandt, Marcus Hasselhorn, Jan Lonnemann. (2025). Training children's spatial language skills improves their numerical magnitude understanding. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102191. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102191

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post control group intervention; robust mixed-design analysis of gains on the number words comparison task assessing relative numerical magnitude understanding. The article reports "a significantly higher performance increase was observed for the intervention group" with r = .319.

> "When comparing the two groups regarding differences in performance between pre- and posttest, a significantly higher performance increase was observed for the intervention group (W = 1323, p = .002, r = .319)."
