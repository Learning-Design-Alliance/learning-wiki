Evidence¶

Walsh 2015¶

Walsh, M. E., Lee-St. John, T. J., Raczek, A., & Foley, C. (2015). The long-term impact of systematic student support in elementary school: Reducing high school dropout. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562544

q2 · i? · causal · r2

This article cites prior peer-reviewed evaluation work (Walsh, Madaus, Raczek et al., 2014) reporting that City Connects students demonstrate "higher middle school report card and statewide standardized test scores (both in English language arts and math)" relative to never-enrolled peers.

"Additionally, students enrolled in City Connects during elementary schools demonstrate higher middle school report card and statewide standardized test scores (both in English language arts and math) relative to students never enrolled in City Connects (Walsh, Madaus, Raczek et al. 2014)."

Walsh 2015 (2)¶

Walsh, M. E., Lee-St. John, T. J., Raczek, A., & Foley, C. (2015). The long-term impact of systematic student support in elementary school: Reducing high school dropout. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562544

q2 · i? · causal · r2

This article cites prior evaluation work (Center for Optimized Student Support, 2010) reporting that growth curve analysis showed City Connects students have "significantly greater improvement over time in report card outcomes in reading, writing, and math during elementary school than comparison students."

"Growth curve analysis showed that City Connects students have significantly greater improvement over time in report card outcomes in reading, writing, and math during elementary school than comparison students (Center for Optimized Student Support, 2010)."
