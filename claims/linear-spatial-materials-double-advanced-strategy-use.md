# Use of advanced addition strategies more than doubles from pretest to posttest among children practicing with linear-spatial materials, but does not change with irregular materials

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A time by condition interaction on percentage of problems using advanced strategies (count-on, decomposition, retrieval) showed an increase only in the linear-spatial condition. [→ Schiffman J 2018](#schiffman-j-2018)
`q3 i3` Children in the linear-spatial condition also increased the number of problems on which they accurately executed advanced strategies, while the irregular condition did not. [→ Schiffman J 2018 (2)](#schiffman-j-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Schiffman J 2018

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANCOVA controlling for numeracy screener in the randomized experiment. There was "a time by condition interaction, F(1,26) = 5.87, p = .02, ηp2 = .18"; advanced strategy use rose from 19% to 52% of problems in the linear-spatial condition (Hedges' g = .81) but not in the irregular condition (14% to 19%, g = .18).

> "For use of advanced strategies, however, there was a time by condition interaction, F(1,26) = 5.87, p = .02, ηp2 = .18."

### Schiffman J 2018 (2)

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Accuracy-of-execution analysis in the same experiment: the ANCOVA "revealed a condition by time interaction, F(1,26) = 5.37, p = .03, ηp2 = .17". Accurate advanced strategy use rose from 1.50 to 4.50 problems in the linear-spatial condition (p < .001, Hedges' g = .81) but not in the irregular condition (p = .26).

> "A repeated measures ACNOVA on the number of problems, out of 12 total, for which children used an advanced strategy to reach a correct sum revealed a condition by time interaction, F(1,26) = 5.37, p = .03, ηp2 = .17."
