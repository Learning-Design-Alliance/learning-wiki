## Evidence

### Montroy Janelle J. 2016

Montroy Janelle J., Bowles Ryan P., Skibbe Lori E., McClelland Megan M., Morrison Frederick J. (2016). The Development of Self-Regulation across Early Childhood. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000159

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Growth mixture modeling of HTKS scores from 1,386 children across three samples, with exponential shape specified and fit judged via global fit statistics, bootstrapped LRTs, entropy, and interpretability, found the "three trajectory solution fit best in all samples". Children showed early, intermediate, or later gains relative to sample peers.

> "Results indicated that the three trajectory solution fit best in all samples based on the evaluation of global fit statistics in association with bootstrapped LRTs"
