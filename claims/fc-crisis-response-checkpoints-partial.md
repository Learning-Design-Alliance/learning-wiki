## Evidence

### Wang Yun 2025

Wang Yun, Tian Hong, Li Jianyun, Zhou Chongzhi, Feng Yiwen, Li Jinbao, Huang Lina. (2025). Effect of flipped classroom to anesthesia crisis management simulation education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07011-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of 2 × 2 cross tables on a six-item instructor checklist administered during the 45-minute pulmonary embolism simulation, each participant assessed individually. Two of six checkpoints favored FC with the printed pass rates; no effect size is printed.

> "The pass rates of Responses 03, ‘Recognize acute pulmonary embolism and deal with it accordingly’, and Response 05, ‘Provide circulatory support and heparin treatment’, were significantly higher in the FC group than in the CL group (FC vs. CL: ‘Recognize acute pulmonary embolism and deal with it accordingly’, 95% vs. 60%, p = 0.020; ‘Provide circulatory support and heparin treatment’, 100% vs. 75%, p = 0.047)."

### Wang Yun 2025 (2)

Wang Yun, Tian Hong, Li Jianyun, Zhou Chongzhi, Feng Yiwen, Li Jinbao, Huang Lina. (2025). Effect of flipped classroom to anesthesia crisis management simulation education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07011-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The remaining four checkpoints (recognize the situation, call for help, blood gas analysis and TTE/TEE, perform CPR) showed no significant group differences in the same chi-square analysis (p values 0.487, 1.000, 0.341, 0.301 in Table 3).

> "No significant difference was observed between the two groups in other responses (Table 3)."
