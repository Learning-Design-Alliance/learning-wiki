# Instructor body posture affects students' perceived instructor communion on both the vertical and the horizontal dimension

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the pilot experiment, perceived communion was significantly affected by both posture dimensions, with the strongest difference between the high and low power conditions. [→ Traulsen 2024](#traulsen-2024)
`q3 i1` In the preregistered experiment, perceived communion showed significant vertical, horizontal, and interaction effects, revealing the strong influence of the horizontal dimension. [→ Traulsen 2024 (2)](#traulsen-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Traulsen 2024

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Pilot experiment, 2×2 ANOVA on perceived communion (α = .92); both dimensions significant (partial η2 = .021 and .056), no interaction, and communion lowest in the slumped/closed condition.

> "Perceived communion was significantly affected by the vertical dimension, F (1, 190) = 3.996, p = .047, partial η2 = .021, and the horizontal dimension, F (1, 190) = 11.304, p < .001, partial η2 = .056."

### Traulsen 2024 (2)

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered experiment with 434 students; both main effects significant (partial η2 = .021 and .128) plus a significant interaction (partial η2 = .036), with the largest pairwise difference involving slumped/closed.

> "there were significant main effects of the vertical dimension, F (1, 430) = 9.087, p = .003, partial η2 = .021, and the horizontal dimension, F (1, 430) = 63.046, p < .001, partial η2 = .128, on perceived communion."
