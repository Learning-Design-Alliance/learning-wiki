Meta-analysis of all results of average scores of models 1, 2, and 3 on Thai language communicative performance (reading, writing, listening, speaking) across the northeast and southern province implementations. The article reports "73% of the stud ents in the control group schools scored lower than the mean score" in the northeast and 74% in the south; no standardized effect size is printed for this comparison.

### Rattanavich 2015 (2)

Rattanavich, S. (2015). Concentrated Language Encounter Approach in Practice for Global Teaching of Literacy: Lighthouse Strategy Implementation. International Education Studies, 8(7), 21. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v8n7p21

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Design of the Rotary-funded five-year project in four northeastern provinces: experimental and control groups of students and teachers were randomly drawn from matched pairs of schools, from both language-problem and non-language problem areas. The article prints "2,945 students in 44 schools" experimental and "2,397 students in 47 sc hools" control.

> "the experimental group consisted of 98 teachers (98 classes), with 2,945 students in 44 schools, while the control group consisted of 95 teachers (95 cl asses), with 2,397 students in 47 sc hools."
