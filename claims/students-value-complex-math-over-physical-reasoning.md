## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2015

Edward F. Redish. (2015). Analysing the Competency of Mathematical Modelling in Physics. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Video data of two physics-major students in an upper-division electricity and magnetism class independently solving a Griffiths text problem and discussing their answers; Student A folded in response to the more mathematically sophisticated reason. No effect size is reported.

> "The students' expectations that the knowledge in the class was about learning to do complex math was supported by many class activities."
