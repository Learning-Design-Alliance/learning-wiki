### Carver-Thomas 2025

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & García, E. (2025). Supporting and sustaining a diverse teacher workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/216.666

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of teacher-to-other-college-educated-worker wage ratios by race/ethnicity, averaged 2016–20 in 2020 dollars (Table 8). The report states "teachers of color earned from as little as 61% of non-teacher wages" to 72%, versus 74% for White teachers.

> "In recent years (2016–2020), all teachers, on average, earned just 73% of what the average non-teacher college graduate earned, after adjusting for state cost-of-living differences. In contrast, teachers of color earned from as little as 61% of non-teacher wages for Native American/Alaska Native teachers to 72% for Latino/a teachers, while White teachers earned just above the average (74%)."
