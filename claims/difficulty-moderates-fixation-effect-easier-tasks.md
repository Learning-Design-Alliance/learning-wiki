## Evidence

### Guo 2025

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel logistic regression Model 4 adding fixation-by-difficulty and flexibility-by-difficulty interactions improved fit over Model 3 (Δdeviance(2) = 16.39, p < 0.001) for task success.

> "the interaction of difficulty with fixation was significant and positive, indicating that the detrimental effect of fixation on task success was stronger for tasks with a lower difficulty level."

### Guo 2025 (2)

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the parallel ideal-manipulation analyses, difficulty again moderated fixation, with the detrimental effect stronger on easier tasks.

> "Besides, task difficulty level again moderated the effect of fixation, indicating that the detrimental effect of fixation on ideal manipulation was stronger for tasks with a lower difficulty level."
