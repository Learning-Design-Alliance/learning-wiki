## Evidence

### Breuer Sonja 2023

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analyses A found "a significantly smaller difference between scores from OE and CE formats for stem-equivalent items" (d diff = −0.36, p = 0.004), a difference between pooled effects.

> "Overall, analyses revealed a significantly smaller difference between scores from OE and CE formats for stem-equivalent items than for non-stem-equivalent items ( d diff = −0.36, p = 0.004), as hypothesized."

### Breuer Sonja 2023 (2)

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analyses B found stem-equivalency "did not significantly moderate the relation between scores from OE and CE assessments"; no difference was detected, equivalence not tested.

> "the items’ stem-equivalency (non-stem-equivalent versus stem-equivalent; table 5 ) did not significantly moderate the relation between scores from OE and CE assessments, indicating a large association between scores from OE and CE response formats irrespective of stem-equivalency."
