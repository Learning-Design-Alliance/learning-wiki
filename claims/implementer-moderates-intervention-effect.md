## Evidence

### Yijun Ruan 2024

Yijun Ruan, Urs Maurer, Catherine McBride. (2024). Effectiveness of Reading Interventions on Literacy Skills for Chinese Children with and Without Dyslexia: a Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09915-9

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression on intervention implementer as moderator across the 28 RCTs, with implementers grouped into researchers, parents, teachers, and combined implementers. Effects were largest for combined implementers (g = 1.11) and smallest for parents (g = 0.27).

> "Children gained more from interventions administered by researchers (g = 0.85) or combined implementers (g = 1.11) than by parents (g = 0.27)."
