Background-item comparison across the two anonymous questionnaire rounds. The shift is reported as a record of carry-over between sessions rather than used to adjust any comparison, since the single TTS lesson was many students' first encounter with machine-read instruction.

"Reported prior exposure to machine-read instructional material, by contrast, rose sharply between the two rounds, from 44.5% to 69.9% answering that they had encountered such material (𝜒2(1)=27.37 , 𝑝 < .001 )."
