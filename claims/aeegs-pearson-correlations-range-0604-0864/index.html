Olaoye DD 2026¶

Olaoye DD, Owolabi HO and Olaoye OT (2026). Automated software scoring of senior school certificate examination mathematical items in economics using a contextual similarity model. Front. Educ. 11:1669504. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1669504

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Pearson correlation analysis comparing each of the 12 human raters' scores on their 84 scripts with AEEGS scores for the same scripts. The article reports coefficients "range from 0.604 to 0.864", all significant at the 0.01 level (2-tailed).

"Table 5 revealed that the correlation coefficients range from 0.604 to 0.864, which implies that there is a very strong relationship between the scores of all human raters and AEEGS scores."
