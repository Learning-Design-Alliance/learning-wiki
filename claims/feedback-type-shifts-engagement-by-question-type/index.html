Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2601.15280

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Log-data analysis of submit/update actions during the learning-by-doing stage of the randomized experiment (RQ2). Overall means were 21.39 (SD 9.05) vs. 21.94 (SD 5.78) actions; no effect size printed.

"A Mann–Whitney U test was conducted (Table 1) and showed no significant overall difference between Original Educator Feedback and AI Multimodal Feedback (𝑈= 4075.5,𝑝= 0.0738), indicating comparable aggregate levels of active-learning behavior across conditions."

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Question-type breakdown of submission counts in RQ2 (Table 1: MCQ means 13.78 vs. 11.92; OEQ 7.61 vs. 8.44). The OEQ difference was a non-significant trend; equivalence was not tested.

"for multiple-choice items, partici- pants in the baseline condition submitted more attempts than those in the AI condition (𝑈= 5660.5,𝑝= 0.02*), consistent with greater trial-and-error under human feedback; for open-ended items, the AI condition showed a trend toward more submissions (𝑈= 4060.5, p = 0.066)."

Related Claims¶

  • The AI feedback system returns responses in under a second for MCQs and about six seconds for open-ended questions, and its reusable prompt is roughly 2.77 times more time-efficient to prepare than equivalent educator text — related
  • Feedback improves learning by a medium amount on average, but its effect varies widely with the information it carries, and a substantial share of feedback effects are negative — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Layered AI feedback led to significantly more tasks requiring three or more submissions, while total submissions, learning time and edit distance did not differ — related
