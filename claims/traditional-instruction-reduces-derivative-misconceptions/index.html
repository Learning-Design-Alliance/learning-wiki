Evidence¶

Kaplan 2015¶

Kaplan, A., Ozturk, M., & Ocal, M. F. (2015). Relieving of misconceptions of derivative concept with derive. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 1(1), 64-74. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105126

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental control group taught by traditional direct lecturing with paper-pencil; pre-test and post-test percentages compared in MS Excel across six attainments. The article reports a decrease in misconceptions and an increase in the “understanding” category for all attainments, e.g. a 39% understanding increase for the first attainment.

"According to the pre-test and post-test results, there was a decrease among the students who received traditional instruction method for all attainments in the number of students who had misconceptions. In addition, there was an increase in the “understanding” category for all attainments."
