## Evidence

### Saab 2012

Saab, N., van Joolingen, W., & van Hout-Wolters, B. (2012). Support of the collaborative inquiry learning process: influence of support on task and team regulation. Metacognition and Learning, 7, 7-23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9068-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same MANCOVA comparing conditions on task regulation frequencies; the univariate team regulation effect was significant but task regulation was not. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Another interesting result is the lacking of significant differences in task regulation between the different conditions. Clearly, the provided support does not influence the task regulation process."
