`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the meta-analysis by Wittwer and Renkl (2010) on instructional explanations in worked examples. The effect is described as "significant, but small, positive"; no numeric effect size is printed in the review, so no magnitude is reported.

> "They found that adding instructional explanations to worked examples had a significant, but small, positive effect on learning, was more helpful for acquiring conceptual than procedural knowledge, and was not necessarily more effective than prompting students to provide self-explanations"
