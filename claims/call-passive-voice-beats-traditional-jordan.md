## Evidence

### Abdallah M Abu Naba'h 2012

Abdallah M Abu Naba'h. (2012). The impact of computer assisted grammar teaching on EFL pupils’ performance in Jordan. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology (IJEDICT), 8(1), 71-90. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu//viewarticle.php?id=1408

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 212 first-secondary pupils in two public schools in Zarqa; two sections taught the passive voice via computer and two via the traditional method, analysed by 3-way ANCOVA on post-test scores. The "computed (F) value was (75.47)". Post-test means were 26.21 (computerized) vs 23.95 (traditional), a difference of 2.26.

> "The computed (F) value was (75.47) which is statistically significant at  (α < 0.05). This shows that there is a significant effect of the use of a computerized software program on the achievement of  students."
