### Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019

Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen. (2019). Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the Geometrical Creativity Test in the three-condition quasi-experiment. Scores rose on average across conditions; class-level variation in growth was not explained by the MACE lesson series (t(91) = 0.74, p = 0.46) or the PD program (t(91) = −1.28, p = 0.20).

> "multilevel analyses showed alinear relationship between time and geometrical creativity (t(3977) = 3.04,p < 0.01). On average, students'scores on the GCT increased between the pre- and posttests."
