## Evidence

### Satman Ilhan 2013

Satman Ilhan, Akalin Sema, Cakir Bekir, Altinel Serdar, Study Group The diaVAX. (2013). The effect of physicians' awareness on influenza and pneumococcal vaccination rates and correlates of vaccination in patients with diabetes in Turkey. Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics 9(12). https://doi.org/10.4161/hv.25826

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized before-after registry study (diaVAX) of 5,682 adults with diabetes enrolled by 44 physicians; prospective phase after a half-day physician training session. The article reports uptake "was 63.3% for influenza and 40.7% for IPD" during the study period, versus 27% and 9.8% in the prior five years. No effect size or significance test for the before-after change is printed.

> "During the study period, the physicians had recommended influenza and pneumococcal vaccines to 97.6% and 95.1% of the patients, respectively. However, the vaccination uptake rate by the patients was 63.3% for influenza and 40.7% for IPD (Fig. 1)."
