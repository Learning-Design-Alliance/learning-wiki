## Subclaims
`q2 i0` None of the pairwise interaction effects between achievement motivation, major satisfaction, and successful intelligence on sustainable education reached statistical significance, indicating each variable's contribution does not depend on the levels of the others. ## Evidence

### Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed 2025

Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed, Almulla Mazen Omar. (2025). Exploring the interplay between achievement motivation, students’ satisfaction with their major, and successful intelligence in supporting the sustainability of university education in SAUDI ARABIA. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03835-1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Regression moderation analysis within the same 529-student survey. All three interaction terms were non-significant: "achievement motivation and successful intelligence (β = –0.03, p =.436)", and the other two pairs likewise.

> "For example, the interaction between achievement motivation and successful intelligence (β = –0.03, p =.436), major satisfaction and successful intelligence (β = 0.04, p =.336), and achievement motivation and major satisfaction (β = –0.01, p =.877) were all non-significant."
