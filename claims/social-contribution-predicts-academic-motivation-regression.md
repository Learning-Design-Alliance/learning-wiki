## Evidence

### Rahimi Hamed 2025

Rahimi Hamed, Etedali Hooman, Latifhadad Mohammad. (2025). The impact of social well-being on students’ academic motivation and academic achievement: a case study from Iran. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08109-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis in the 261-student study, adjusted for age, gender, and semesters passed; the article reports "social contribution had a significant explanatory power for students' academic motivation" (P < 0.05) with β = 0.38 (Table 4).

> "Analyses revealed that the predictor variables collectively explained 28.5% and 11.1% of the variance in academic motivation (ADJ.R2 = 0.285) and achievement (ADJ.R2 = 0.111), respectively."
