### Okuyama Ayako 2011

Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of tool descriptions reports that "Twenty of these tools assessed the two highest Miller levels ('shows how' and 'does')". Table 2 details the distribution: seven tools at 'knows', three at 'knows how', nineteen in simulation at 'shows how', and two using direct observation at 'does'.

> "Twenty of these tools assessed the two highest Miller levels ('shows how' and 'does') and four tools were directed at multiple levels. Most of the tools that aimed at the higher levels assessed the skills of working in teams (17 tools), risk management (15 tools), and communication (11 tools)."
