### S. G. Djorgovski 2013

S. G. Djorgovski, Piet Hut, Rob Knop, Giuseppe Longo, Steve McMillan, Enrico Vesperini, Ciro Donalek, Matthew Graham, Asish Mahabal, Franz Sauer, Charles White, Crista Lopes. (2013). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' report on their first-year weekly professional seminar series in SL, with a typical attendance of ~30 avatars, used as a device to introduce colleagues to VWs. They report that although speakers found the experience rewarding, "the majority did not" remain active, and the series was stopped in favor of ad hoc seminars.

> "The great majority of those who accept our invitation to speak at the MICA seminars in SL found the experience to be interesting and rewarding. However, while some of the colleagues we attracted in this way remained active in exploring the scientific uses of VWs, the majority did not."
