Quantitative phase of the mixed-methods study: 61 GP registrars (74% response rate) completed a 30-item BSRE combined with a 65-item clinical exam. Descriptive results showed "scores varied across basic science disciplines, with the lowest in anatomy" and the highest in biochemistry; no effect size was printed.
