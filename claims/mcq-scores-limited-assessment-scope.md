Zhang Z, Zhang Z, Yu Y, Jiang M, Deng L, Guo L. (2024). Impact of different teaching modes on medical students' performance under the scoring criteria for multiple-choice questions: A meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041008

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

The authors' stated limitation that using multiple-choice question scores as the sole assessment criterion "may overlook other important forms of assessment" and "may not fully represent the students' overall performance."

> "While multiple-choice questions have certain advantages in assessing knowledge mastery, they are not well-suited to measuring students' clinical decision-making abilities, practical skills, and problem-solving abilities"
