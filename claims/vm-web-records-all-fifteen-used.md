Web statistics from the ePBL platform were used to determine users and use rates of the VMs for the projects. The records showed "all of the VMs are used in the EG for all projects" with frequencies between 17 and 28, and VM14 and VM15 had similar usage rates for Project 3.

> "As shown on Table 9, all of the VMs are used in the EG for all projects in which the usage frequency is between 17 and 28. VM5 was most frequently preferred in doing Project 1. VM8, VM9, and VM12 were the most commonly used for studying on Project 2."
