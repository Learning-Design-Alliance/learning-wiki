Evidence¶

Gheith and Aljaberi 2017¶

Eman Gheith & Nahil M. Aljaberi. (2017). The Effectiveness of an Interactive Training Program in Developing a Set of Non-Cognitive Skills in Students at University of Petra. International Education Studies, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n6p60

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Null findings on three of the six scale dimensions (Table 1 F-values 0.39, 0.5, and 0.64, all non-significant). The article attributes this to students' prior knowledge of these skills and insufficient development strategies.

"It is evident that the training program did not contribute to developing planning, locus of control, and self-confidence skills in students, in spite of it being highly imperative in achieving success and developing problem-solving skills which students will practice in the future."
