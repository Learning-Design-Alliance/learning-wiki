## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cost analysis using the Accelerate cost tool estimated per pupil costs of about $1,700 (society) and $1,500 (school), tutoring efficiency of 7.5 hours (Louisiana) to 11.9 hours (Texas) per month of learning, and 1.3-2.4 additional months of learning per $1,000 invested. ## Evidence

### Neitzel 2026

Neitzel, A.J., Storey, N., & Wang, X. (2026). Air Reading: A randomized evaluation of a virtual tutoring model in Louisiana and Texas schools. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://education.jhu.edu/crre

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cost analysis conducted with the Accelerate cost analysis tool, assuming 17 weeks of dosage and an average of 27.1 hours of tutoring per student as reported by the program developer. The article reports Air Reading appeared somewhat less efficient and slightly less cost effective in Texas than Louisiana.

> "In both Louisiana and Texas, the per pupil cost to society was approximately $1,700, while the cost to school was approximately $1,500."
