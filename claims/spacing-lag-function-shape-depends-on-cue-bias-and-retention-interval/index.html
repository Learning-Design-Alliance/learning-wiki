Evidence¶

Glenberg 1979¶

Glenberg, A. M. (1979). Component-level theory of the effects of spacing of repetitions on recall and recognition. Memory & Cognition, 7(2), 95-112. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03197590

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The article reviews Glenberg's (1976) continuous recognition memory experiment, in which lag between the first two presentations was factorially combined with retention interval. The review reports that "the probability of eorreet1y reeognizing an old item" showed the predicted shapes. No effect sizes are printed.

"As predieted, the probability of eorreet1y reeognizing an old item was an inverted U-shaped funetion of lag when tested after a short retention interval, and a monotonically inereasing funetion of lag when tested after a long retention interval."
