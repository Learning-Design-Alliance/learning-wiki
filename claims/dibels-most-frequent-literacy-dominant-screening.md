### Keles 2025

Keles, S., ten Braak, D., Hagen, Å. M., & Melby-Lervåg, M. (2025). Validity of Early-Grade Screening Tools in the Three Tier (RTI/MTSS) Model: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Literacy, Language, and Mathematics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10077-5

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive synthesis (RQ1) of screening tools used across the 127 eligible studies, all used in Tier 1. The review reports "DIBELS was by far the most frequently reported tool (29 studies, 22.8%)" and that "In total, literacy was the most assessed domain; 118 literacy tools were used across the studies, followed by language (32 tools), and mathematics (26 tools)."

> "DIBELS was by far the most frequently reported tool (29 studies, 22.8%), followed by Test of Word Reading Efficiency (TOWRE; 11 studies), Word Identification Fluency (WIF; 10 studies), Comprehensive Test of Phonological Processing (CTOPP; 10 studies)"
