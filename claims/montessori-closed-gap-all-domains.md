## Evidence

### Tobin 2015

Tobin, T., Boulmier, P., Zhu, W., Hancock, P., & Muennig, P. (2015). Improving outcomes for refugee children: A case study on the impact of Montessori education along the Thai-Burma border. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 14(3). http://openjournals.library.usyd.edu.au/index.php/IEJ/index

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of ASQ domain scores across the two schools at baseline and nine-month follow-up. The Montessori students, who started with "severe disadvantage going into school" (208.5 vs 240 total points), lessened or closed the gap across all five domains.

> "During the baseline assessment, students at the Montessori school all scored lower than those at the control school. In the follow-up assessment, the students at the Montessori school lessened or closed the gap across all five domains."
