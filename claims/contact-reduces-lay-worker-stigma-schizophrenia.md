## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of FGDs; a CBR worker described carrying "something in my bag to defend myself" until direct contact with participants dispelled fears. The article links this to the slower development of 'Promotion of hope' competencies and cites contact-based stigma reduction, but could not test stigma–ENACT relationships directly.

> "Even after I got the training, I was not thinking that [people with mental illness] don't attack anyone, until I begin working with them. … Even I had a plan to have something in my bag to defend myself."
