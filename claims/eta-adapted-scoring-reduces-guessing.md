## Evidence

### Vanderoost J 2018

Vanderoost J, Janssen R, Eggermont J, Callens R, De Laet T. (2018). Elimination testing with adapted scoring reduces guessing and anxiety in multiple-choice assessments, but does not increase grade average in comparison with negative marking. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203931

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression models for non-doubt answers (adjusted R² = 0.444 Paediatrics, 0.512 Gynaecology) showed a large significant scoring-method coefficient; the authors call this "indirect support" for reduced guessing.

> "The scoring method also significantly predicts the number of non-doubt answers for both exams with a large regression coefficient in each model."
