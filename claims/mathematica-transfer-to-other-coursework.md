### R. W. Robinett 2007

R. W. Robinett. (2007). Using Physics to Learn Mathematica©R to Do Physics: From Homework Problems to Research Examples. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2358 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.2358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.2358)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report question on a final survey in the single Spring 2007 offering. Reported uses included quantum mechanics and complex analysis integrals for juniors and Special/General Relativity and electronics electives for seniors, but 'the majority seemed to use Mathematica©R in either ''graphing calculator'' or ''math handbook'' modes, and not for further extensive programming.'

> "The vast majority of students replied that they had used it some- what or even extensively in their other courses that semester."
