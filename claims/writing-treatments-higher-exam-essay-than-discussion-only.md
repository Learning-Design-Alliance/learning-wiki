## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students performed better on exam essay questions when the activity included a writing component (WO or DW) than when it was discussion-only (DO). [→ Linton 2014](#linton-2014)

## Evidence

### Linton 2014

Linton, D. L., Pangle, W. M., Wyatt, K. H., Powell, K. N., & Sherwood, R. E. (2014). Identifying Key Features of Effective Active Learning: The Effects of Writing and Peer Discussion. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-12-0242

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis of exam essay data from 346 consenting students in three sections, with treatment, instructor, and aptitude as independent variables. The article reports "The WO and DW treatments resulted in higher performance on written exam components than DO" (χ2 = 7.2, p = 0.027); no effect size is printed.

> "The treatments received during the lecture activity did have a significant effect on how well students performed on the exam writing (Figure 5; χ2 = 7.2, df = 2, p = 0.027). The WO and DW treatments resulted in higher performance on written exam components than DO."
