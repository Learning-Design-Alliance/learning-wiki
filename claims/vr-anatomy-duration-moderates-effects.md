## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Subgroup analysis shows VR interventions lasting at least 1 day had moderately-to-large effects on scores (SMD = 0.71) versus a small effect for interventions under 1 day (SMD = 0.35). [→ Zhao Jingjie 2020](#zhao-jingjie-2020)

## Evidence

### Zhao Jingjie 2020

Zhao Jingjie, Xu Xinliang, Jiang Hualin, Ding Yi. (2020). The effectiveness of virtual reality-based technology on anatomy teaching: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1994-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis by intervention duration across the 15 trials, reported in Table 2. The review reports "SMD = 0.71; 95% CI 0.42–1.10" for at least 1 day versus "SMD = 0.35; 95% CI 0.18–0.52" for under 1 day.

> "VR interventions for at least 1 day had moderately-to-large effects on scores (SMD = 0.71; 95% CI 0.42–1.10, p < 0.001), whereas those which were < 1 day had only a small effect (SMD = 0.35; 95% CI 0.18–0.52, p < 0.001)"
