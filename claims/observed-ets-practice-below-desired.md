### Mwakanyanga Emelia T. 2018

Mwakanyanga Emelia T., Masika Golden M., Tarimo Edith A. M. (2018). Intensive care nurses' knowledge and practice on endotracheal suctioning of the intubated patient: A quantitative cross-sectional observational study. PLoS ONE 13(8): e0201743. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structured observation of 35 nurses performing ETS in natural ICU settings across the five hospitals, using a standardized checklist scored against recommendations; "85.7% of the observed participants scored less than the desired level". Descriptive percentages only.

> "Overall, the majority (n = 30, 85.7%) of the observed participants scored less than the desired level (< 50% score), only 5 participants (14.3%) scored at the moderate level (50–75% score) and none scored at the desirable level (76–100% score)."
