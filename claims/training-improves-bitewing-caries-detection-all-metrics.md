### Nagyová Valéria 2026

Nagyová Valéria, Blaňár Dominik, Kybic Jan, Schwendicke Falk, Tichý Antonín. (2026). Student Performance in Bitewing Caries Detection: Artificial Intelligence Versus Alternative E-learning. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109567

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Permutation tests comparing pre- and posttraining annotations of 52 dental students against a 3-expert reference standard showed significant improvement on all metrics; e.g., mean errors fell from 85.4 ± 51.2 to 63.2 ± 27.2 (P < .0001) and "statistically significant improvements (P < .05) in all diagnostic metrics" were found.

> "The analysis of all 52 students combined showed statistically significant improvements (P < .05) in all diagnostic metrics when comparing their results before and after training"
