Usher 2023¶

Cohort analysis of all CPS high school students, including charter and Options school students, counting graduates who completed high school within four years of their first-time ninth-grade year. The 2022 rate of 84.0% is described as "the highest rate in recent history," having risen from 72.5% in 2012 aside from a slight decline in 2021.

"The high school graduation rate of CPS students increased by over 2 percentage points, from 81.8% in 2021 to 84.0% in 2022."
