Evidence¶

Amogh Sirnoorkar and Omkar Mamidpalliwar 2026¶

Amogh Sirnoorkar and Omkar Mamidpalliwar. (2026). Students' Epistemological Beliefs and their Chatbot Preferences in AI-mediated Physics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29385

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Dunn's post-hoc tests on the two axes with significant omnibus results; Table II prints Axis 4 means of 71.6% (Combination) versus 68.2% (Answer) and Axis 5 means of 82.5% versus 79.3%. No pairwise effect size is printed.

"we observed statistical significant difference between the scores associated with Combination and Answer-preferring groups (Axis 4: p = 0.038; Axis 5: p = 0.021). Across both axes, students preferring combination exhibited substantially higher scores than those preferring answer-providing chatbots."
