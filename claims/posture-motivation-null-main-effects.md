# Instructor body posture shows no significant main or interaction effects on students' motivation, though pairwise contrasts favour conditions over slumped/closed

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Traulsen 2024

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered experiment with 434 students; 2×2 ANOVAs on motivation (SALES questionnaire, α = .92) found no significant main or interaction effects (all partial η2 ≤ .009).

> "The same was found for motivation, which was neither significantly affected by variations of the vertical dimension, F (1, 430) = 2.292, p = .131, partial η2 = .005, the horizontal dimension, F (1, 430) = 3.184, p = .075, partial η2 = .007, and the interaction of both dimensions, F (1, 430) = 3.761, p = .053, partial η2 = .009."

### Traulsen 2024 (2)

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Pairwise contrasts in the preregistered experiment showed each of the three other conditions differed significantly from slumped/closed on motivation, with small effects (d = 0.328, d = 0.315, d = 0.359).

> "Yet, pairwise comparisons showed that the groups upright/open vs. slumped/closed, t (430) = 2.349, p = .039, d = 0.328, upright/closed vs. slumped/closed, t (430) = 2.450, p = .039, d = 0.315, and slumped/open vs. slumped/closed, t (430) = 2.546, p = .039, d = 0.359, differed significantly"
