# Pilot user norms provide basic contextual information but are drawn from a limited, non-representative pool of test events

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The pilot user norms, released with the July 2019 Spanish MAP Growth Reading test, are drawn from a limited pool of test events and are not nationally representative, providing only basic contextual information about fall and spring performance and growth. [→ NWEA 2021](#nwea-2021)

## Evidence

### NWEA 2021

NWEA. (2021). Spanish MAP Growth Reading Technical Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/spanish-map-growth-reading-technical-report/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chapter 9 describes the pilot user norms built from the 2018–2019 pilot year of test data. The report states they are "drawn from a limited pool of test events and not a nationally representative" sample, limiting interpretation to basic contextual information.

> "Although they are drawn from a limited pool of test events and not a nationally representative like the general MAP Growth norms, they provide basic contextual information about student performance in the fall and spring and growth between fall and spring."
