# Students' spendency negatively predicts in-system achievement levels over and above prior strategy knowledge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Spendency was a significant negative predictor of achievement levels in iSTART-ME over and above pretest strategy knowledge, accounting for 17% of additional variance. [→ Snow 2015](#snow-2015)

## Evidence

### Snow 2015

Snow, E. L., Allen, L. K., Jackson, G. T., & McNamara, D. S. (2015). Spendency: Students' Propensity to Use System Currency. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 25, 407–427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0044-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical linear regression on the 40 iSTART-ME students' process data, with pretest strategy knowledge entered in Model 1 and spendency in Model 2. Spendency was "a significant (negative) pre- dictor of student achievement levels over and above prior strategy knowledge" (R 2 change=0.17, F(1,37)=20.77, p<.01).

> "Results from this analysis indicated that spendency was a significant (negative) pre- dictor of student achievement levels over and above prior strategy knowledge (R 2 change=0.17, F(1,37)=20.77, p<.01; see Table 3)."
