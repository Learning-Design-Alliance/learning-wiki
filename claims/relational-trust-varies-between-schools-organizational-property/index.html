Three-level hierarchical linear model analysis of 1994 Chicago elementary teacher survey responses decomposing variance into measurement error, within-school, and between-school components. The article reports "the proportion of variance between schools is about 3094" (printed as such, evidently 30%), with teacher-parent trust highest at 34%.
