# No significant post-test difference between groups on science process skills despite significant within-group gains in both groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-test overall SPOF scores did not differ significantly between experimental and control groups (t = -1.06, p = .296), and neither did any subscale (all p > .05). [→ Unal 2018](#unal-2018)
`q2 i?` Both groups improved significantly within themselves on SPOF: experimental total z = -2.93, p = .003; control total z = -3.29, p = .001. [→ Unal 2018 (2)](#unal-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Unal 2018

Unal, M., & Saglam, M. (2018). Examination of the effect of the GEMS program on problem solving and science process skills of 6 years old children. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(3), 567-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.3.567

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t test on post-test overall SPOF scores; Table 11 prints t = -1.06, p = .296 (experimental mean 2.89, control 2.84). Table 10 shows all three subscales also non-significant (e.g., SPC U = 67.00, p = .575).

> "When Table 11 was examined, it was observed that the difference between the post -test mean scores of the children in experimental and control groups for the overall Science Processes Observation Form was not significant."

### Unal 2018 (2)

Unal, M., & Saglam, M. (2018). Examination of the effect of the GEMS program on problem solving and science process skills of 6 years old children. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(3), 567-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.3.567

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed Rank tests on pre-post SPOF scores: the experimental group's total gain was significant (z = -2.93, p = .003, Table 12) and the control group's total gain was also significant (z = -3.29, p = .001, Table 13), so both programs were followed by within-group improvement.

> "the difference between SPC(z: -3.30, p .05), PSC (z:-2.88, p .05), SAOI (z:-3.30, p .05) and total scores (z: -3.29, p .05) obtained by the children in the control group from the Science Processes Observation Form before and after the experiment was significant."
