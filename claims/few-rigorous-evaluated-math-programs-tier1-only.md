## Evidence

### Ben Clarke 2014

Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Mari Strand Cary, Derek Kosty, Scott Baker, Hank Fien, and Keith Smolkowski. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students: Using a Theory of Change to Guide Formative Evaluation Activities. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567775

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' own review of What Works Clearinghouse evaluations of elementary mathematics programs, reported in the introduction. They note fewer than 10% of programs had sufficiently rigorous evaluations and all four positive-effect programs were Tier 1 core programs.

> "We reviewed 78 elementary math programs that had been evaluated by the What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) and found that only 7 of them were evaluated by studies that met the WWC standards, with only 4 programs showing potential positive effects on student achievement."

### Clarke 2014

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Strand Cary, M., Kosty, D., Baker, S., Fien, H., & Smolkowski, K. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://www.nasponline.org/journals/spr

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' own narrative review of WWC-evaluated elementary mathematics programs. The review reports "only 7 of them were evaluated by studies that met the WWC standards" and that all four positively rated programs were core (Tier 1) programs, not designed for at-risk students.

> "We reviewed 78 elementary math programs that had been evaluated by the What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) and found that only 7 of them were evaluated by studies that met the WWC standards, with only 4 programs showing potential positive effects on student achievement."
