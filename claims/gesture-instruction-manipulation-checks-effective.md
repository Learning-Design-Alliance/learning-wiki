## Evidence

### Thakore 2024

Thakore, K., Das, T., Jahan, S., & Sweller, N. (2024). From Hands to Mind: How Gesture, Emotional Valence, and Individual Differences Impact Narrative Recall. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 111. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09948-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 mixed design ANOVA manipulation check on gestures produced during reading aloud (N = 100): instructed participants gestured far more (M = 25.88) than spontaneous participants (M = 0.18), "with a large effect size, η p 2 = 0.49".

> "those in the instructed gesture condition (M = 25.88, SD = 2.62) produced significantly more gestures than those in the spontaneous gesture condition (M = 0.18, SD = 0.16), F(1, 98) = 95.68, p < 0.001, with a large effect size, η p 2 = 0.49."

### Thakore 2024 (2)

Thakore, K., Das, T., Jahan, S., & Sweller, N. (2024). From Hands to Mind: How Gesture, Emotional Valence, and Individual Differences Impact Narrative Recall. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 111. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09948-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 face-to-face manipulation check (N = 98) confirmed the instruction manipulation: instructed participants produced far more gestures (M = 75.25) than spontaneous participants (M = 3.59), "with a very large effect size, η p 2 = 0.83".

> "participants in the instructed gesture condition produced significantly more gestures (M = 75.25, SD = 21.49) than those in the spontaneous gesture condition (M = 3.59, SD = 8.47), F(1, 96) = 471.39, p < 0.001, with a very large effect size, η p 2 = 0.83."
