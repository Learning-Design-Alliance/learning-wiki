Descriptive frequency analysis of primary barriers in the RQ1/RQ2 subset (N = 155), reported in Results. The quote prints "n = 70; 45.2%" for infrastructure/access, 20.6% for limited transfer, and 17.4% for insufficient training. The review concludes constraints concentrate around material access, transfer, preparation and implementation conditions.

"Infrastructure, connectivity or limited access remained the most frequent barrier (n = 70; 45.2%), followed by limited transfer to practice or sustainability (n = 32; 20.6%), insufficient training or digital competence (n = 27; 17.4%)"
