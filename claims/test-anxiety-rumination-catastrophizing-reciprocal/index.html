Putwain 2025¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Test anxiety, emotion regulation, and achievement: Lagged and contemporaneous reciprocal relations. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://repository.essex.ac.uk/40904/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the contemporaneous cross-lagged panel models estimated for each emotion regulation strategy, "Rumination and catastrophizing showed positive contemporaneous reciprocal relations with test anxiety at T2 and T4", supporting Hypothesis 3 for these non-adaptive strategies.
