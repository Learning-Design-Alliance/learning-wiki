Sibieta 2016¶

Luke Sibieta. (2016). REACH: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581275

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Intention-to-treat analysis of the two-phase randomised controlled trial (287 pupils randomised; estimates based on 202 pupils in 21 completing schools). Table 1 reports an effect size of "0.51 (0.34; 0.68)" for the language comprehension version versus standard provision, equated to "6 months" of progress.
