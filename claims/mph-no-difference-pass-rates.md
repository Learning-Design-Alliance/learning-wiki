## Evidence

### Sambeth A 2026

Sambeth A, Toth M, Blokland A (2026). Methylphenidate (Ritalin) does not improve exam performance in an experimental setting. Psychopharmacology 243(3):593–600. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-025-06864-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fisher exact tests compared pass counts (grade of 5.5 out of 10 counted as a pass) between the placebo group and each MPH group separately in the same experiment, with printed "Cramer's V = 0.03" and "Cramer's V = 0.10".

> "The number of students passing the exam was not different when the PLAC-PLAC group (10 pass, 15 fail) was compared with the MPH -PLAC group (10 pass, 14 fail; X2 = 0.42, p > 0.10, Cramer's V = 0.03). Also, when comparing the PLAC-PLAC with the PLAC-MPH group (15 pass, 12 fail), no difference between the groups was found (X2 = 0.27, p > 0.10, Cramer's V = 0.10)"
