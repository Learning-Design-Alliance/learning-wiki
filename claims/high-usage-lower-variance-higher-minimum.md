Hunter McNichols, Fareya Ikram, and Andrew Lan. 2026. The StudyChat Dataset: Analyzing Student Dialogues With ChatGPT in an Artificial Intelligence Course. In LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785029

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression and descriptive comparison of students grouped by total utterances (bottom 10%, middle 80%, top 10%). Group features gave a borderline significant improvement for spring e1 (F=4.08, p=0.05; group means 77.8%, 81.0%, 88.1%). High users' minimum average assignment score (92.53) and exam score (72.33) exceeded the other groups' minima.

> "Our analysis demonstrates that although interaction level is not a dominant predictor of outcome, high engagement with the LLM is associated with reduced variability in course outcomes and higher minimum course outcomes."
