# Cross-term WCPM score differences are more consistent across passage positions after equating, supporting progress monitoring

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Means and SDs of term-to-term differences in equated WCPM scores are much more consistent across the three passage positions than those of raw WCPM score differences. [→ Chen 2020](#chen-2020)

## Evidence

### Chen 2020

Chen, J., & Simpson, M. A. (2020). Equating words-correct-per-minute (WCPM) scores across passages of MAP Reading Fluency. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/equating-words-correct-per-minute-wcpm-scores-across-passages-of-map-reading-fluency/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of score differences between terms by passage position, using students with valid scores on the respective passage from both terms (Table 3.5). For Winter minus Fall, raw difference means "ranged from 0.307 to 11.206" versus 11.948 to 12.627 equated.

> "Overall, the mean and SD of the differences in equated WCPM scores of each of the three passages across two terms were much more consistent than those of the differences in raw WCPM scores."
