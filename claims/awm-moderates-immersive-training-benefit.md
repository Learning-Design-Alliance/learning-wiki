## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant two-way interaction between training modality and auditory working memory, with post-hoc tests showing significantly greater SNR improvement after immersive training for lower-AWM participants, but no modality difference for higher-AWM participants. ## Evidence

### Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud. (2025). Immersive auditory-cognitive training improves speech-in-noise perception in older adults with varying hearing and working memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00306-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc comparisons (emmeans) on the linear mixed model of training-induced SNR change, treating AWM as a continuous moderator; the interaction Δχ²(1) = 9.143, p = 0.002, with the quoted post-hoc at p < 0.001. Participants were not grouped into high/low categories.

> "a participant with lower AWM capacity (i.e., 1 SD below the mean) experienced a signi ﬁcantly greater improvement in SNR f ollowing the immersive training compared to the auditory training (AWM 1 SD below average: ChangeAU– ChangeIM:Δβ = −0.677, SE = 0.148,t = −4.561, p < 0.001)"
