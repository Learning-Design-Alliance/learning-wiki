# Combining GPA or course failure rates with attendance provides the best prediction of high school graduation for most EL groups, while GPA plus test scores best predicts college degree attainment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPA-plus-attendance or course-failure-plus-attendance combinations gave the best graduation prediction for most EL groups, except long-term ELs with IEPs. [→ Allensworth 2025](#allensworth-2025)
`q2 i?` GPA combined with test scores was the best predictor of eventual college degree attainment for all EL groups. [→ Allensworth 2025 (2)](#allensworth-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Allensworth 2025

Allensworth, E.M., de la Torre, M., Franklin, K., & Xu, J. (2025). Predictive power of grades and other indicators for English Learners. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1241

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression models predicting graduation separately by EL subgroup, comparing pseudo-R2 across indicator combinations. Combination indicators best predicted graduation except for "long-term English Learners with IEPs."

> "Combining GPA and attendance data, or course failure rates and attendance data, provided the best prediction of high school graduation for most English Learner groups. (except long-term English Learners with IEPs)"

### Allensworth 2025 (2)

Allensworth, E.M., de la Torre, M., Franklin, K., & Xu, J. (2025). Predictive power of grades and other indicators for English Learners. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1241

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same modeling approach identified GPA plus test scores as the best combination for predicting college degree attainment across all EL groups. The study notes this relationship partially weakens when comparing students within the same high schools.

> "The combination of GPA with test scores was the best prediction of which students eventually attained a college degree for all English Learner groups."
