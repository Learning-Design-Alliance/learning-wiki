## Evidence

### Charlotte E. Wolff 2016

Charlotte E. Wolff, Halszka Jarodzka, Niek van den Bogert, Henny P. A. Boshuizen. (2016). Teacher vision: expert and novice teachers' perception of problematic classroom management scenes. Instr Sci 44:243-265. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9367-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Eye-tracking experiment in which 67 teachers viewed four authentic lesson videos; a mixed-design repeated measures ANOVA on fixation dispersion average showed a main effect for expertise (F(1, 60) = 6.04, p = 0.017) with the printed effect size g2 = 0.10 (the article's notation for eta-squared, in the medium range). The article reports "Novices' fixations were significantly more dispersed than experts".

> "Statistical analysis with a mixed-design ANOVA showed a main effect for expertise with ‘fixation dispersion average’, F(1, 60) = 6.04, p = 0.017, g2 = 0.10."
