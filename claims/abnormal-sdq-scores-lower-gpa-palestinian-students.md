## Evidence

### Nawajah Inad 2025

Nawajah Inad. (2025). The statistical relationship between sweet consumption and academic achievement among Palestinian university students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07425-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fully adjusted regression model among the 220 students; mental health was screened with the Arabic version of the Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire, classified as normal, borderline, or abnormal. Abnormal SDQ scores were "independently associated with lower GPA" (β = −0.31; p = 0.004); 29.5% of students scored borderline or abnormal.

> "Among covariates, lower physical activity (β = − 0.22; p = 0.008) and abnormal SDQ scores (β = − 0.31; p = 0.004) were independently associated with lower GPA."
