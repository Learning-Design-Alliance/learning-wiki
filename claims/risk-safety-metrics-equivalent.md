## Evidence

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossover trial risk and safety analysis of total console drops and instrument collisions across the four simulation activities, analysed as absolute risk metrics by console due to the small sample number.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in the number of drops or instrument collisions when comparing console A and console B."

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022 (2)

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossover trial analysis of instruments out of view and excessive instrument force as average scores across attempts; the only significant difference was on matchboard for the console B first group (B = 3.11 vs A = 0.0058, p = 0.019).

> "Across the four simulation activities participants were only significantly less likely to have instruments out of view on the matchboard exercise if on console B first (B = 3.11 vs A = 0.0058, p = 0.019)."
