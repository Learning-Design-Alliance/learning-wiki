## Evidence

### Quiñones-Camacho Laura E. 2019

Quiñones-Camacho Laura E., Fishburn Frank A., Camacho M. Catalina, Wakschlag Lauren S., Perlman Susan B. (2019). Cognitive flexibility-related prefrontal activation in preschoolers: A biological approach to temperamental effortful control. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6969345/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of 123 preschoolers' fNIRS data during a Pet Store Stroop task found "greater effortful control predicted less DLPFC activation during cognitive flexibility" (β = -.26, p = .019).

> "Effortful control predicted activation on the Stroop-NonStroop contrast (β = -.26, SE = 10.15, p = .019), such that greater effortful control predicted less DLPFC activation during cognitive flexibility."
