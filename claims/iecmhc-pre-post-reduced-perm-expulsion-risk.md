## Evidence

### Trigg 2026

Trigg, A. B., Keyes, A. W., Gray, S., Hatch, V., Brunet, K. T., & Heller, S. S. (2026). The Effects of Early Childhood Mental Health Consultation on Early Childhood Teachers' Perceptions of Children's Challenging Behaviors, Expulsion Risk, and the Moderating Role of Teaching Stress. Infant Mental Health Journal, 47, e70079. https://doi.org/10.1002/imhj.70079

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression (children nested in individuals, classrooms, centers) with 20 imputations; Table 4 reports PERM Classroom Disruption falling from 3.12 to 2.68 (Est .44, p=.000), Fear of Accountability from 2.32 to 2.03 (p=.000), Hopelessness from 2.12 to 1.92 (p=.000), and Teacher Stress from 2.28 to 1.99 (p=.000).

> "lower child‐level factors related to an increased likelihood of expulsion (i.e., PERM scales of classroom disruption, teacher stress, teacher hopelessness, and teacher fear of accountability, all scores related to the child's behavior)."
