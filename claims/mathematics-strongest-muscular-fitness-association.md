## Evidence

### Robinson Katie 2023

Robinson Katie, Riley Nicholas, Owen Katherine, Drew Ryan, Mavilidi Myrto F., Hillman Charles H., Faigenbaum Avery D., Garcia-Hermoso Antonio, Lubans David Revalds. (2023). Effects of Resistance Training on Academic Outcomes in School-Aged Youth: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Sports Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-023-01881-6

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

School-subject moderator analysis within the secondary meta-analysis (mathematics k = 18, languages k = 16, combined k = 15). Printed effect sizes: mathematics SMD = 0.17 (95% CI 0.08–0.25), languages SMD = 0.10, combined SMD = 0.12. The authors link the mathematics result to working memory demands of complex mathematical concepts.

> "In addition, we found a small positive association for studies that included mathematics (SMD 0.17, 95% CI 0.08–0.25), languages (SMD 0.10, 95% CI 0.01–0.18) and combined academic results (SMD 0.12, 95% CI 0.02–0.22)."
