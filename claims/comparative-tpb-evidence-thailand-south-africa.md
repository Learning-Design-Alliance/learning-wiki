The article reports, citing Mnguni et al. (2024b), that zero-order associations of intention with subjective norm (ρ = .144) and perceived behavioural control (ρ = .149) were small, whereas its association with behavioural beliefs was moderate (ρ = .331), and that the ordinal logistic regressions differed by country as quoted.

> "the overall TPB model was significant in the Thai sample (n = 95), χ²(6) = 34.039, p < .001, Nagelkerke pseudo-R² = .404, but not in the South African sample (n = 97), χ²(6) = 4.252, p = .643, Nagelkerke pseudo- R² = .051."
