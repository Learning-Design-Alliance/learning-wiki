`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-year survey of participating and non-participating teachers: "87 percent of participating teachers" reported risk-taking is encouraged, and participating teachers were significantly more likely to believe this than non-DLP teachers; no effect size printed.

> "By the end of the pilot year, 87 percent of participating teachers reported that risk-taking is encouraged in their school . Not only that, but compared to non-DLP teachers, participating teachers were significantly more likely to believe that risk-taking is encouraged in their school"

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

> "At the end of the pilot year, coaches felt less confident that the teachers they coached were taking risks, with 54 percent of coaches reporting that teachers are very or extremely willing to take risks"
