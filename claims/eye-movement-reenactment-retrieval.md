## Evidence

### Björn B. de Koning & Huib K. Tabbers 2011

Björn B. de Koning & Huib K. Tabbers. (2011). Facilitating Understanding of Movements in Dynamic Visualizations: an Embodied Perspective. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9173-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Laeng and Teodorescu's two experiments in which participants inspected a visual scene and later imagined it while looking at a blank screen; participants fixated similar locations in a similar order across tasks, and eye movements predicted memory accuracy. No effect size is printed.

> "Moreover, the eye movements during imagery and perception tasks predicted memory accuracy. Stated differently, participants ' eye movements in the imagery task reenacted the eye movements from the initial study task."
