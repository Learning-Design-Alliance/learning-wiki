## Evidence

### Blobstein 2025

Blobstein, A., Facciotti, M. T., Igo, M., Karger, D., Ravi, P., Sripathi, K., & Gal, K. (2025). #let's-discuss: Analyzing Students' Use of Emoji when Interacting with Course Readings. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00401-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-level frequency analysis of 83,380 unique student posts collected from two offerings of the FYBIO course, of which 63,702 contained at least one emoji. The analysis found "about 73% of posts included at least one emoji"; 10% contained two and under 1% contained three or four. No effect size is printed.

> "By analyzing students’ posts (Fig.2) we have found that about 73% of posts included at least one emoji. Ten percent of the posts contained two emoji and less than 1% of the posts contained 3 or 4 emoji."
