### Melnick 2022

Melnick, H., García, E., & Leung-Gagné, M. (2022). Building a well-qualified transitional kindergarten workforce in California: Needs and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/826.674

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Projection analysis using California Department of Education TK enrollment data and Department of Finance population projections, with three uptake scenarios (lower bound 65%, observed 2019–20 rate about 71%, upper bound 80%). The report states enrollment "could be between 291,000 and 358,000 children by 2025–26."

> "In 2025–26, more than 447,700 children will be eligible for TK (see Figure 1). Based on the uptake assumptions described in Table 3, this would mean that TK enrollment could be between 291,000 and 358,000 children by 2025–26."
