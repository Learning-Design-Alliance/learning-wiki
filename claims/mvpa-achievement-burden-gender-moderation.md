## Evidence

### Zhang Danqing 2022

Zhang Danqing, Hong Jintao, Chen Sitong, Liu Yang. (2022). Associations of physical activity with academic achievement and academic burden in Chinese children and adolescents: do gender and school grade matter? BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-13886-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stratified logistic regressions from the same cross-sectional survey by gender. The article reports boys "have greater likelihood of attaining above-average AA (OR = 1.43, 95% CI: 1.01–2.03)" with "Similar results... among girls (OR = 2.22, 95% CI: 1.43–3.44)"; no standardised effect size printed.

> "Compared with those who did not meet the PA guidelines, boys who met the PA guidelines have greater likelihood of attaining above-average AA (OR = 1.43, 95% CI: 1.01–2.03). Similar results were also observed among girls (OR = 2.22, 95% CI: 1.43–3.44)."

### Zhang Danqing 2022 (2)

Zhang Danqing, Hong Jintao, Chen Sitong, Liu Yang. (2022). Associations of physical activity with academic achievement and academic burden in Chinese children and adolescents: do gender and school grade matter? BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-13886-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender-stratified logistic regression in the same survey: girls meeting the guideline had "greater likelihood of reporting no AB (OR = 1.99, 95% CI: 1.17–3.39)". Table 3 shows the boys' AB association was not significant (OR = 1.44, 95% CI: 0.88–2.33); no standardised effect size printed.

> "Compared with those who did not meet the PA guidelines, girls who met the PA guidelines have greater likelihood of reporting no AB (OR = 1.99, 95% CI: 1.17–3.39)."
