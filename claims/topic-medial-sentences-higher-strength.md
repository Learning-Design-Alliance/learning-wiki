### Catrysse 2025

Catrysse, L., van Daal, T., Jarodzka, H., Kaakinen, J. K., Donche, V., & Gijbels, D. (2025). How Eye Read: A Social Network Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10000-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc Tukey comparisons (Bonferroni corrected) following the Poisson mixed model on strength, from 31 readers of three expository texts; descriptive means were 9.375 (medial) versus 6.973 (initial) and 6.634 (final).

> "Post hoc tests (Table 6) indicate that topic-medial sentences have a higher strength compared to topic-initial (p < 0.001) and topic-final sentences (p < 0.001)."
