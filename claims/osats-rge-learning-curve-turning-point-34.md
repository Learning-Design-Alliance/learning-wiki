Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000006692

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cumulative sum analysis of OSATS scores over consecutive procedures identified "a turning point after 34 RPDs", followed by "a stable plateau" and a downward slope after 51 cases. Grade B/C DGE was 25.7% before versus 11.5% after the turning point (P = 0.035).

> "Cumulative sum analysis of the OSATS data over time identified a turning point after 34 RPDs. Hereafter, there was a stable plateau, and after 51 cases, the curve demonstrated a downward slope."
