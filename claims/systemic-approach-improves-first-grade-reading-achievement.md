## Evidence

### Al-Hajaya 2012

Nail Al-hajaya. (2012). Impact of the Systemic Approach on Literacy Achievement of Jordanian 1st Graders at Mu'tah University Model School. International Education Studies, Vol. 5, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p100

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with all 45 first graders at Mu'tah University Model School assigned to classrooms by kindergarten GPA. The systemic-approach group mean was "M=15.74; SD=1.71" versus 12.41 (SD=2.79) for the traditional group; two-way ANOVA showed a significant difference in favor of the experimental group.

> "As shown in Table 2, mean grade (M=15.74; SD=1.71) of students taught with system approach method was higher than student who were taught traditionally (M=12.41; SD=2 .79)."
