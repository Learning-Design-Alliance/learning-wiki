Evidence¶

Jacob 2026¶

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., Schneider, H., & Peters Hinton, V. (2026). Institutional conditions for integrating emerging technologies in higher education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/294

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey finding from the 120-respondent instrument: about half of faculty disagreed that resources existed for understanding AI in workforce settings; strong AI offerings were concentrated in computer science while humanities, nursing, and social science students encountered AI peripherally or not at all.

"One administrator put it plainly: “STEM students are getting all the love... but no one is really looking at scale at the non-STEM students.” In the survey, approximately half of faculty disagreed to some extent that their institution provided resources to help them understand how AI is used in workforce settings."
