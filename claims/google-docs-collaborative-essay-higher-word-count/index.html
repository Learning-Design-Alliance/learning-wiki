Evidence¶

Martínez-Sáez 2018¶

Martínez-Sáez, A., Corral-Esteban, A., & Vinagre-Laranjeira, M. (2018). Exploring virtual collaborative writing in the EFL classroom. In P. Taalas, J. Jalkanen, L. Bradley & S. Thouësny (Eds), Future-proof CALL: language learning as exploration and encounters – short papers from EUROCALL 2018. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2018.26.835

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental comparison of 84 first-year EFL students at Autónoma University of Madrid, randomly divided into a Google Docs group and a pen-and-paper group, each producing eleven collaborative essays analysed with LIWC2015. The article reports "the total number of words written by the students in the experimental group was 2,452" versus 1,451 for the control group; no effect size or significance test is printed for this contrast.
