# Performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence and facilitating conditions predict vocational students' LMS acceptance, with performance expectancy the most influential

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ANN analysis identified performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence and facilitating conditions as important predictors of students' behavioral intention to use LMS. [→ Ozkan 2020](#ozkan-2020)
`q2 i?` Performance expectancy was the most important predictor (normalized importance 100%), followed by facilitating conditions (65.2%), effort expectancy (54.4%) and social influence (39.3%). [→ Ozkan 2020](#ozkan-2020)

## Evidence

### Ozkan 2020

Ozkan, U. B., Cigdem, H., & Erdogan, T. (2020). Artificial neural network approach to predict LMS acceptance of vocational school students. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 21(3), Article 11. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANN analysis of questionnaire data from 387 vocational school students (training 62.8%, testing 27.4%, validation 9.8%) ranked predictor importance: performance expectancy 100%, facilitating conditions 65.2%, effort expectancy 54.4%, social influence 39.3%. The article reports these as "the most important independent variable in the ANN". No effect sizes printed.

> "Analysis of both Table 3 and Figure 4 reveals students' performance expectations as the most important independent variable in the ANN structured for students' acceptance of LMS use."
