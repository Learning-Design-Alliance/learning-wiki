## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a stepwise regression on the expert data set, barrel movement AUC, trigger control AUC, and firing stance (prone vs. kneeling) entered the equation and were significantly related to shot group size performance, with a multiple correlation of .452. [→ Goldberg 2018](#goldberg-2018)

## Evidence

### Goldberg 2018

Goldberg, B., Amburn, C., Ragusa, C., & Chen, D. (2018). Modeling Expert Behavior in Support of an Adaptive Psychomotor Training Environment: a Marksmanship Use Case. Int J Artif Intell Educ 28:194–224. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0155-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Stepwise regression (SPSS Statistics 19) on the full expert shot data set, with individual shot performance as the dependent variable. The authors report a multiple correlation coefficient of .452, indicating approximately 20% of variance in individual shot score accounted for.

> "At step 3 of the analysis the behaviors of barrel movement and trigger, as represented by the AUC value, and the stance from which an expert shot from (prone vs. kneeling) entered into the regression equation and were found to be significantly related to shot group size performance."

### Goldberg 2018 (2)

Goldberg, B., Amburn, C., Ragusa, C., & Chen, D. (2018). Modeling Expert Behavior in Support of an Adaptive Psychomotor Training Environment: a Marksmanship Use Case. Int J Artif Intell Educ 28:194–224. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0155-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same stepwise regression: gear setup and breathing control were exclusion variables, with t(1555) = −1.326, p > .1 for gear setup and t(1555) = −1.444, p > .1 for breathing control; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The gear setup and breathing AUC factors were not found to significantly impact performance and were therefore not entered into the regression equation; they were instead listed as exclusion variables (gear setup, t(1555) = −1.326, p > .1; breathing control, t( 1 5 5 5 )= −1.444, p >. 1 )."
