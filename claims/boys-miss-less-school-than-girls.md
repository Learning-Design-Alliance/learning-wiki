## Evidence

### Russell Kelly 2019

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial regression on attendance log book data identified sex as a significant correlate of missed school: "boys missed significantly less school than girls (IRR: 0.56; 95% CI: 0.39, 0.82)", regardless of injury type; sex also confounded the injury-type effect. No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "Sex was also significantly associated with missing school where boys missed significantly less school than girls (IRR: 0.56; 95% CI: 0.39, 0.82), regardless of their type of injury."
