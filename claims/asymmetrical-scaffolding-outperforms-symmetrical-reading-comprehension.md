## Evidence

### Pishghadam 2011

Pishghadam, R., & Ghadiri, S. (2011). Symmetrical or asymmetrical scaffolding: Piagetian vs. Vygotskyan views to reading comprehension. Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097243

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparing post-test scores of the S group (n=24, mean 26.67) and AS group (n=28, mean 30.18) after eight sessions of pair scaffolding with the same teacher and materials. The article reports "a significant difference between two groups (t=-2.5, p<.05)"; no effect size is printed.

> "As the results of Table 4 show, there is a significant difference between two groups (t=-2.5, p<.05). It implies that the AS group outperformed the S group."
