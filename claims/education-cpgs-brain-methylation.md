## Evidence

### Jenny van Dongen et al 2018

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Look-up of the 58 top CpGs in two previously published Illumina 450k datasets of foetal brain development and matched post-mortem blood/brain samples. At 18 CpGs (31%) methylation changes as a function of foetal age (r = −0.57 to 0.38, p < 7.7 × 10−4); the strongest blood–brain correlation was cg05951221 (ALPPL2, r = 0.63 with superior temporal gyrus).

> "At 10 of our top CpGs (17%), methylation level in blood correlates signiﬁcantly with DNA methylation level in at least one out of four assessed brain regions, with correlations ranging from r = 0.36 to r = 0.63"
