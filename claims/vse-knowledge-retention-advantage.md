## Evidence

### Li Kaiyue 2025

Li Kaiyue, Li Qun, Wang Linding, Wang Xiaonan, Ding Xiaojuan, Liu Boyu. (2025). Virtual simulation experiments in medical education: technology acceptance, learning outcomes, and motivational impacts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08025-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Knowledge retention was tested two weeks after the six-week course ended in the same randomized study of 90 medical students. The VSE group was "significantly outperforming the control group's score of 80.4 ± 4.8 (t = 5.551, p < 0.01)"; the immediate post-test also favored VSE (87.4 ± 4.4 vs. 81.8 ± 5.0, t = 5.669).

> "the experimental group achieved an average score of 85.6 ± 4.1 on the knowledge retention post-test, significantly outperforming the control group's score of 80.4 ± 4.8 (t = 5.551, p < 0.01)"
