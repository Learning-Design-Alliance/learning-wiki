Core-periphery and overlapping-community analysis of the 51×51 CSS network (Fig. 7), from 1000 community ensembles at γ = 1. The article says this "verifies the existence of multiple cores composed of engineering disciplines synergetic to each other".

> "we find that the disciplines classified as basic nature science: physics, chemistry, life science, and statistics, are located in the periphery, as shown in Fig. 7(b). In contrast, the disciplines related to engineering, which are assigned to multiple communities, show high core scores."
