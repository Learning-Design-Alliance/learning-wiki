### Servant-Miklos 2018

Servant-Miklos, V. F. C. (2018). Problem solving skills versus knowledge acquisition: the historical dispute that split problem-based learning into two camps. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 24(3), 619–635. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-018-9835-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Historical analysis reporting Elstein and Schwarz (2002). Most problems, he realised, "were actually resolved on the basis of pattern recognition or the construction of a mental model of the problem", both based on the extent of the clinician's knowledge rather than mastery of heuristics.

> "In 2002, Elstein became his own harshest critic when he acknowledged that the theory that medical problem-solving was based on hypothetico-deduction processes was in large part erroneous."
