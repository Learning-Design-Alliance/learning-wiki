Qualitative case analysis of Student 17's exam response (Figure 6), the most complete response in the class. The authors report the depiction "was neither in the textbook, nor was it written by the teacher" and suggest cognitive transfer from math class; the breadth and depth of discussion strongly suggests Advanced sensemaking.

> "The visual depiction of the factor ten increase in the decibel scale was neither in the textbook, nor was it written by the teacher. This suggests that cognitive transfer may have taken place and was imported information from their math class."
