## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

T-test comparison of 95 investigated general practitioners and 376 volunteer doctors on five outcomes. The largest gap was overall OSCE: "Effect size r = −0.61" (Cohen's d = −1.53); knowledge test d = −0.21; basic life support d = −1.14; communication d = −0.87; physical examination d = −0.99.

> "Doctors in the fitness to practise group on average scored less than doctors in the volunteer group on all outcome measures (Table 2)."
