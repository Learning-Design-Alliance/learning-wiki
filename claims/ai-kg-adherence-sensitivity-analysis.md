### Yuanyuan Yu 2026

Yuanyuan Yu, Ying Yuan. (2026). Application and effectiveness analysis of AI Intelligent tutoring system combined with knowledge graph in basic medical pathology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09348-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analysis within the RCT: ten students failing compliance criteria for two consecutive weeks were assigned to a low-adherence group and the remaining 170 to a high-adherence group. The high-adherence mean was "84.4, compared with 81.6 in the low-adherence group".

> "The final examination scores of the two groups were compared. The results showed that the high-adherence group had a mean score of 84.4, compared with 81.6 in the low-adherence group, a difference of 2.8 points. The low-adherence group accounted for only 5.6% of the total experimental group, and all students ultimately received the complete intervention."
