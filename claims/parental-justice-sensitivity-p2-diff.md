### Minkang Kim 2021

Minkang Kim, Jean Decety, Ling Wu, Soohyun Baek and Derek Sankey. (2021). Neural computations in children's third-party interventions are modulated by their parents' moral values. npj Science of Learning 6:38. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00116-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian SEM (Model 2, intergenerational model, ppp = 0.40) adding parent dispositions. The Parental justice sensitivity → Central P2diff path had posterior mean 0.409 with a 90% CI of 0.090 to 0.728; the cognitive empathy path was not significant. Perpetrator sensitivity correlated with P2 diff at ρ = 0.378, p < 0.05 in Table 1.
