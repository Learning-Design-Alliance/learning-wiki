### Sahota G 2023

Sahota G, McLachlan J, Patterson F, Tiffin P. (2023). Evidence-based appraisal of the role of SJTs in selection. Clinical Medicine. https://doi.org/10.7861/clinmed.2023-0295

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article argues that Sam et al's claim that random guessing equals five years of undergraduate achievement is misleading: "Only ranking questions obtain the stated score for random guessing", and other question styles have lower or no guessing impact.

> "Only ranking questions obtain the stated score for random guessing and, because the test is not speeded, this means that all candidates obtain at least this value on average; discrimination comes from the subsequent value candidates add by choosing the appropriate options."
