## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-intervention chat transcript word counts did not differ significantly between the feedback and control groups, supporting baseline comparability. [→ Brügge Emilia 2024](#brugge-emilia-2024)

## Evidence

### Brügge Emilia 2024

Brügge Emilia, Ricchizzi Sarah, Arenbeck Malin, Keller Marius Niklas, Schur Lina, Stummer Walter, Holling Markus, Lu Max Hao, Darici Dogus. (2024). Large language models improve clinical decision making of medical students through patient simulation and structured feedback: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06399-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline equivalence check in the randomized trial comparing scenario-1 chat transcript word counts between groups; the t-test showed "no significant difference between the groups". Sociodemographics likewise showed no significant group differences. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond these checks.

> "A t-test for independent samples showed no significant difference between the groups, t(18) = 0.219, p = .829, indicating that both groups were comparable at baseline."
