## Evidence

### Althewini Abdulaziz A 2025

Althewini Abdulaziz A. (2025). How well do admission criteria predict student performance in one medical course? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08378-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First block of the hierarchical multiple regression on course grades. GAT and SAAT jointly explained 6% of variance ("R2 = 0.06, F(2, 207) = 6.58, p = 0.002"); neither test was a significant individual predictor.

> "In Model 1, GAT and SAAT together explained 6% of the variance in biology performance (R2 = 0.06, F(2, 207) = 6.58, p = 0.002)."

### Althewini Abdulaziz A 2025 (2)

Althewini Abdulaziz A. (2025). How well do admission criteria predict student performance in one medical course? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08378-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second block of the same hierarchical regression. Adding the three English proficiency measures significantly improved fit ("ΔR2 = 0.20, p < 0.001"), raising total explained variance in biology grades to 26.6%.

> "In Model 2, adding English proficiency measures significantly improved model fit (ΔR2 = 0.20, p < 0.001), raising the total variance explained to 26.6% (R2 = 0.266, F(5, 204) = 14.79, p < 0.001)."
