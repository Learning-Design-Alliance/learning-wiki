## Evidence

### Kılıç Gülşen 2026

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Students reported feeling distressed witnessing restrained patients struggling and by the absence of explanations to patients, with one noting "No explanation was given there."

> "Certain practices—such as physical restraint, holding patients forcibly, or failing to provide adequate explanations—triggered significant ethical and moral discomfort. These experiences led students to question the patient-centeredness of care and foster feelings of emotional distress."
