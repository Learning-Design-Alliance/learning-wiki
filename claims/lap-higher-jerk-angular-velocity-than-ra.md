## Evidence

### Amirthanayagam Anumithra 2026

Amirthanayagam Anumithra, Zecca Massimiliano, Chitrakar Anisha, Chavda Vijay, Moss Esther L. (2026). Looks like a surgeon? Impact of anthropometry on the kinematics of surgical novices: counterbalanced cross-over design study of minimally invasive surgery. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-04077-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests on participant-level means of angular velocity, jerk and acceleration from 54 surgical novices performing three tasks on LAP and da Vinci Xi platforms. The analysis found "Significantly higher kinematic values were recorded during LAP compared to RA" with the printed p-value range; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Significantly higher kinematic values were recorded during LAP compared to RA (p < 0.0001 to < 0.01). RA was associated with significantly lower jerk and angular velocity compared to LAP tasks (p < 0.0001 to < 0.01). The only exception was the non-dominant arm in Task 1 (p = 0.196; Fig. 3a)."
