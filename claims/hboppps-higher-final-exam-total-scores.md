### Liu Xiao-Yu 2022

Liu Xiao-Yu, Lu Chunmei, Zhu Hui, Wang Xiaoran, Jia Shuwei, Zhang Ying, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng. (2022). Assessment of the effectiveness of BOPPPS-based hybrid teaching model in physiology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03269-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A three-cohort comparison of final examination scores among 1576 clinical medicine students, analyzed by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett T3 test. The "significant increase in the total score of Class 2019" was the headline result; no effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "There was a significant increase in the total score of Class 2019 (83.9 ± 0.5) compared to Class 2017 (81.1 ± 0.6, P < 0.01) and Class 2018 (82.0 ± 0.5, P < 0.05) evaluated by ANOVA and Dunnett T3 test"
