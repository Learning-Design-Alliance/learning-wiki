### Fischer H 2014

Fischer H, Bachmann K, Strunk G, Neuhold S, Zapletal B, Maurer C, Fast A, Stumpf D, Greif R. (2014). Translation of ERC resuscitation guidelines into clinical practice by emergency physicians. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-22-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression of the same refresher-course survey (Table 2) shows working status predicts hypothermia initiation, "OR = 2.550, p < 0.01"; descriptively 43% of working versus 16.9% of inactive OOHEP reported initiating hypothermia (p < 0.001 bivariately).

> "They have also initiated more mild therapeutic hypothermia after successful resuscitation (OR = 2.550, p < 0.01), and their knowledge concerning defibrillation, tested with the MCQ question, was higher (OR = 2.292, p < 0.01)."
