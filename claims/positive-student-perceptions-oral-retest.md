## Evidence

### St James Andrew R 2026

St James Andrew R, Widener Camille. (2026). Oral examination as a tool for mastery learning in undergraduate microbiology. FEMS Microbiology Letters. https://doi.org/10.1093/femsle/fnag010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualtrics Likert-scale and open-ended survey of the 18 students who took at least one oral retake. The article reports "18/18 students fully agreed" on fairness and demonstrating knowledge; descriptive survey data with no inferential statistics.

> "Encouragingly, 18/18 students fully agreed with the statements "The oral retakes were fair" and "The oral retakes gave me an opportunity to demonstrate my knowledge more fully," and 18/18 students agreed to at least some extent with the statement "I felt proud of myself whenever I successfully completed an oral retake.""

### St James Andrew R 2026 (2)

St James Andrew R, Widener Camille. (2026). Oral examination as a tool for mastery learning in undergraduate microbiology. FEMS Microbiology Letters. https://doi.org/10.1093/femsle/fnag010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey item on relative testing anxiety, reported as percentages of respondents; the article notes it did not measure absolute anxiety, so unchanged responses cannot be interpreted. Descriptive data only.

> "While a minority of students were more anxious about testing in general, most students (80%) reported being either equally anxious or less anxious than in other classes (Fig. 2)."
