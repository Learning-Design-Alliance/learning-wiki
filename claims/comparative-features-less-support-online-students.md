# Comparative dashboard features found less support among online students than recommendations and contact features

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparative features received varied and overall weaker endorsement from students, who disagreed about which data should be shown comparatively, while material recommendations and contact points were strongly endorsed. [→ Herodotou 2025](#herodotou-2025)

## Evidence

### Herodotou 2025

Herodotou, C., Shrestha, S., Comfort, C., Andrews, H., Mulholland, P., Bayer, V., Maguire, C., Lee, J. & Fernandez, M. (2025). A Participatory Approach to Designing a Student-Facing Dashboard for Online and Distance Education. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8481

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group frequency analysis of 20 students showed no agreement on which data should be presented comparatively; some valued comparing assignment performance or tutorial attendance, others disliked unfavourable comparisons. The article reports "comparative features found less support with students."

> "Overall, comparative features found less support with students, aligning with existing studies (Divjak et al., 2023; Herodotou, Boroowa, et al., 2020)."
