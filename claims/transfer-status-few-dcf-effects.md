In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3662028

Observational comparison of DCF and achievement-rate (AR) group differences for transfer status in the Economics major. The article reports "multiple significant differences in AR (17/48) which are not present in the DCF plot [Top] (4/48)", suggesting A-grade gaps stem mainly from trait-level differences rather than course-specific difficulty.

> "Comparing DCF effects to ARΔ, the ARs of the two groups (Figure 5 [Bottom]) show multiple significant differences in AR (17/48) which are not present in the DCF plot [Top] (4/48)."
