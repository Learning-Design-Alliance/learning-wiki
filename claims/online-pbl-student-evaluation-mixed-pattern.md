Descriptive analysis of the university eVALUate survey (148 of 521 students responded, 28.4%); statistical tests were not conducted due to small cell sizes. The article also reports fully online agreement with assessment and teaching-quality items "were lower than the face‐to‐face and university averages" in 2014, 2015, 2018 and 2019.
