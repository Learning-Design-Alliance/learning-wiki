## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey administration to 226 students in 14 classes at ten universities, counting only the first score of students who took it twice. The article reports the average score "is 45%" and that item difficulty ranged between 0.2 and 0.8, with most percentages around 0.4.

> "The average score on the survey for all 226 students regardless of instruction (including only the first score of students who took it twice) is 45%."

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the 226-student survey administration. The article reports the reliability coefficient is 0.91, "which is quite good by the standards of test design", and the discrimination coefficients quoted here indicate reasonably good item discrimination.

> "The point biserial discrimination coefficient ranged from approximately 0.3 to 0.6 with about 3/4 of the questions with point biserial discrimination coefficients higher than 0.4."
