Interview finding (Subtheme 2a) from the thematic analysis of six physician interviews; no effect size printed. Participants described being pulled in multiple directions and needing more manpower for training.

### Abou Farah Joy 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding (Subtheme 2b); one participant described "More structure, more guaranteed exposure—versus luck of the draw in the ICU." No effect size printed.

> "The availability of cases significantly influences the number of procedures a trainee can perform, which leads to variable exposure."

### Abou Farah Joy 2026 (3)

Abou Farah Joy, Lee Kipum, Kim-Mozeleski Jin E., El Khoury Alexia, Albar Zainab, Ladikos Christopher, Yianas Demetra, Carty Noah, Lewis Tina M., Gardiner Kendra Marianne, Saade Elie. (2026). > "Access to high-quality tools, including manikins, procedural kits, and ultrasound machines, is inconsistent, impacting the training quality."
