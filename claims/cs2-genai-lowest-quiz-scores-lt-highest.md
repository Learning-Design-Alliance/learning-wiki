## Evidence

### Quinton Yong 2026

Quinton Yong, Anthony Estey, and Miguel Nacenta. (2026). Characterization and Effects of CS2 Learning with GenAI, Visualization, and Human Support. [doi:10.1145/3765964.3811663](https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811663)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects comparison of quiz scores across three learning-support conditions in a pre-registered study of twelve CS2 students; Bayesian post hoc comparisons gave "a probability of 97.3%" for LT over GenAI and 96.1% for AV over GenAI; LT vs AV was inconclusive (59.4%).

> "The LT condition resulted in the highest quiz scores (5.6/10 average), followed by AV (5.4/10 average), with GenAI at the bottom (4.4/10 average). Post hoc comparisons show that LT scores are, on average, larger than GenAI with a probability of 97.3%."
