PLS-SEM structural results from the Lesotho survey (n = 842) report hedonic motivation and habit as the largest intention predictors, each with printed f2 = 0.061, and habit's use-behavior path with f2 = 0.061, per Table 8.

"Hedonic motivation (H5: β = 0.210, t = 5.769, p < 0.001) and habit (H6a: β = 0.220, t = 6.655, p < 0.001) emerged as particularly strong predictors of behavioral intention, while habit also had a substantial direct effect on use behavior (H6b: β = 0.210, t = 6.111, p < 0.001)."
