### Harris 2023

Harris, C.J., Schmidt, J.A., Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Marchand, G.C., Cabrera, L., McKinney, D., & Liu, P. (2023). Equipping science teachers to support student motivation in NGSS classrooms: Insights from the Development of the M-PLANS program. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638504

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Video-recorded lesson observations collected across M-PLANS classrooms so the development team could examine how MDPs were translated into practice. "different teachers often enacted the same MDP in different ways and in varying situations".

> "Analysis of classroom video recordings showed that different teachers often enacted the same MDP in different ways and in varying situations, suggesting the value of the M-PLANS comprehensive, flexible, and context-driven approach to teacher instructional decision-making."

### Harris 2023 (2)

Harris, C.J., Schmidt, J.A., Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Marchand, G.C., Cabrera, L., McKinney, D., & Liu, P. (2023). Equipping science teachers to support student motivation in NGSS classrooms: Insights from the Development of the M-PLANS program. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638504

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interviews and surveys asked teachers to reflect on efforts to incorporate MDPs, usability of resources, and changes in teacher-student interactions. Teachers reported barriers including how to "routinize strategies" and calibrate MDP use for maximal benefit during lessons.

> "While teachers strongly agreed that each of the MDPs was useful for their teaching, they also perceived some barriers to consistent implementation, including how to routinize strategies learned in the M-PLANS program so that they become part of daily practice"
