## Evidence

### Johnson 2020

Johnson, Angela, and Diana Mercado-Garcia. (2020). The Effects of Early College Opportunities on English Learners. (EdWorkingPaper: 20-232). Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai20-232

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences-in-differences estimates (Table 5) netting out non-EL DiD effects in the same schools; college credits, graduation, and immediate attendance estimates were small and insignificant. The four-year reduction persisted but lost significance.
