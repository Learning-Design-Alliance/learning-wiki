`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis computed separately by sex explored relationships between self-concept domains and sedentary time patterns; the authors report that "the most consistent correlations were found among girls for intellectual and anxiety-free domains of self-concept, as well as for the total self-concept".
