# A four-hour e-textile workshop significantly increased young novices' ability to draw a functional circuit, from 12 of 22 (55%) at pre-test to 19 of 22 (86%) at post-test

`q2 i?` Students' ability to create accurate representations of a simple circuit significantly increased from pre- to post-test after a four-hour e-textile workshop (p = .016), with functional circuit drawing rising from 12 (55%) to 19 (86%) students. [→ Ioannou 2025](#ioannou-2025)

## Evidence

### Ioannou 2025

Ioannou, A., Miliou, O., Georgiou, Y., Timotheou, S., Barkhuus, L., & Rode, J. (2025). Learning design for short-duration e-textile workshops: outcomes on knowledge and skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 443–463. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10411-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post knowledge exercise with n = 22 children (11 boys, 11 girls, mean age 12.7, SD = 1.26) in a four-hour workshop; dichotomous coding per Peppler and Glosson's rubric analyzed with McNemar's tests. "19 students (86%) were able to draw a functional circuit, up from 12 (55%)"; no control group.

> "Our findings showed that students' ability to create accurate representations of a simple circuit significantly increased (p = .016) from pre- to post-test (see for example, Fig.  3). Specifically, by the end of the e-textile workshop, 19 students (86%) were able to draw a functional circuit, up from 12 (55%), prior to the workshop."
