Evidence¶

Seufert 2024¶

Seufert, T., Hamm, V., Vogt, A., & Riemer, V. (2024). The Interplay of Cognitive Load, Learners' Resources and Self-Regulation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09890-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression in the same within-subject study: the article reports a significant linear total effect of task difficulty on metacognitive strategy use and no significant quadratic total effect, so the inverted-U prediction was not supported for metacognitive strategies.

"we revealed the expected linear total effect (H2a) of perceived task difficulty on learning strategy use (linear: γ0(β1) = 0.16, SE = 0.05, p = 0.001). With increasing perceived task difficulty, the use of meta-cognitive strategies increased. However, we found no significant quadratic total effect (quadratic: γ0(β2) =—0.06, SE = 0.03, p = 0.063; see Table 4 in the appendix)."

Related Claims¶

  • Perceived task difficulty relates to cognitive strategy use with both a linear and a negative quadratic (inverted U) component, and the quadratic relationship is fully mediated by cognitive load — related

  • For metacognitive strategy use, resources competitively mediate the linear effect and cognitive load partially mediates the linear and mediates the quadratic relationship — related
