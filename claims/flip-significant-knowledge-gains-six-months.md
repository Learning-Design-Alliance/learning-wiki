## Evidence

### Stumbras K 2026

Stumbras K, Hollander R, Newcomer L, Ameden L, Vijayakumar NP, Ali Q, Tucker A, Essel K. (2026). Beyond the Classroom: A Food Security–Focused Experiential Learning Program for Developing Health Equity–Minded Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11575

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched pre/post survey analysis (Fisher's exact test on 5-point Likert response frequencies) of the 2021–2022 onboarding cohort; 31 students completed the presurvey and 22 the 6-month postsurvey. The article reports only p-values and no standardized effect sizes.

> "We observed statistically significant improvements in student knowledge across multiple domains after at least 6 months of participation, including knowledge of FI (p < .001), the impact of FI on health (p < .001), familiarity with FI community resources (p < .001), and approaches to addressing FI (p < .001)."
