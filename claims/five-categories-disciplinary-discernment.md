## Evidence

### Urban Eriksson 2014

Urban Eriksson, Cedric Linder, John Airey, Andreas Redfors. (2014). Introducing the anatomy of disciplinary discernment: an example from astronomy. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 2, No. 3, 167-182. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9409](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9409)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Hermeneutic interpretive analysis of the on-line survey descriptions, using a constant comparative method with NVivo software; coding continued until saturation, and independent sorting by the other authors showed "extremely good agreement so only very minor changes were made". In total 137 participants from 9 countries participated.

> "This continued until ‘saturation’ was reached (Guba & Lincoln, 1985, p. 350), which ended with th e formulation of five qualitatively different categories. At this point the other authors took sa mples of the data and inde pendently sorted it in terms of these five categories."
