## Evidence

### Hawkins Whitney 2021

Hawkins Whitney, W.; Goddard, K.; Favero, C. (2021). A Cocurricular Program That Encourages Specific Study Skills and Habits Improves Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Undergraduates in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-06-0117

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention analysis of the two aggregated Bio101 cohorts comparing withdrawal rates of full CoC participants and nonparticipants; no withdrawals among participants.

> "while none of the students who attended CoC dropped the course at midterm, 11.3% of students who did not participate in CoC withdrew from the course and received a final grade of “W” or “WF” (n = 27/238)."

### Hawkins Whitney 2021 (2)

Hawkins Whitney, W.; Goddard, K.; Favero, C. (2021). A Cocurricular Program That Encourages Specific Study Skills and Habits Improves Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Undergraduates in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-06-0117

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same retention analysis restricted to the low-preparedness group: zero withdrawals among the 15 full participants versus 13 of 40 nonparticipants withdrawing.

> "none of the full participants in this group withdrew from the course (n = 0/15) compared with 32.5% of nonparticipants (n = 13/40)."
