## Evidence

### Sridhar Ashwin N. 2017

Sridhar Ashwin N., Briggs Tim P., Kelly John D., Nathan Senthil. (2017). Training in Robotic Surgery—an Overview. Current Urology Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11934-017-0710-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a prospective randomized controlled study by Lendvay et al. in which trainees and experts with the same robotic training were divided into a warm-up group and a group that read a book; the warm-up group "improved task performance and reduced errors". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Lendvay et al. [26] showed that a warm up practice session on the VR simulator improved task performance and reduced errors in the dry lab not only for basic but also for complicated skills such as robotic suturing."
