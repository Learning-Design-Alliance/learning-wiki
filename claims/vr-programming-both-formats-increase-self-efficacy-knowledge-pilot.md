## Evidence

### Kevin Baron 2024

Kevin Baron. (2024). Comparing Visual Metaphors with Textual Code For Learning Basic Computer Science Concepts in Virtual Reality. UW iSchool, March 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.11975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.11975)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot study with six CS-novice subjects split into two groups using pre/post self-efficacy questionnaires and knowledge tests around a VR intervention. The authors state findings are preliminary and that "further iterations of the study with a larger sample size would be needed to confirm any results."

> "preliminary findings from the pilot study suggest that both methods of teaching basic programming concepts in VR can lead to increased levels of self -efficacy and knowledge regarding CS, and can contribute toward productive mental states."
