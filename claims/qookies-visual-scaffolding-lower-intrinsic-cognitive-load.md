## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Intrinsic cognitive load was significantly lower in the verbal+visual scaffolding group than in the verbal scaffolding group (d = 0.60), but the difference against the control group was not significant (p = .081). [→ Caroline Wermann 2026](#caroline-wermann-2026)

## Evidence

### Caroline Wermann 2026

Caroline Wermann, Karina E. Avila, Sebastian André, Julia C. Draeger, Alvar Goetze, Jochen Kuhn, Maite Maurer, Sascha Mehlhase, Nikola Merkas, Fabian Schrodt, and Stefan Küchemann. (2026). AI-based Verbal and Visual Scaffolding in a Serious Game: Effects on Learning and Cognitive Load. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.08893](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.08893)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on self-reported ICL (adapted Leppink items, 0–6 Likert) with Bonferroni post-hoc tests in the randomized experiment. The omnibus ICL effect was significant (F(2, 148) = 4, p = 0.0203, η2 = 0.051); the verbal vs verbal+visual contrast gave "a significant difference" with d = 0.60.

> "The Bonferroni post-hoc test showed no significant difference between the control group and the verbal scaffolding group ( t(148) = −0.87, p = 1 .00) as well as the control group and the verbal and visual scaffolding group (t(2, 148) = 2.235, p = .081), but a significant difference between the verbal scaffolding group and the verbal and visual scaffolding group ( t(148) = 2.66, p = .026, d = 0.60)."
