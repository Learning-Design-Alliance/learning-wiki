## Evidence

### Hujala 2020

Hujala, M., Knutas, A., Hynninen, T., & Arminen, H. (2020). Improving the quality of teaching by utilising written student feedback: A streamlined process. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103965

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative evaluation in which three authors applied theory-driven codes from Grebennikov & Shah (2013) and Pan et al. (2009) to the 12 most characteristic feedback texts for each topic, then compared the resulting themes with SET determinants from previous studies, finding that "each topic has a coherent theme".

> "As shown, each topic has a coherent theme, and similar constructs can be found in extant literature. The main themes were good content, dissatisfaction with personnel or the course, workload, stress and good teaching methods."
