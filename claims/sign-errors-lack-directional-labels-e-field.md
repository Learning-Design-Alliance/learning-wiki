## Evidence

### Vignal 2022

Vignal, M., & Wilcox, B. R. (2022). Investigating Unprompted and Prompted Diagrams Generated by Physics Majors During Problem Solving. arXiv. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of unprompted E-Field diagrams found "None of the 5 students with a sign error labeled their axes" or depicted directions, while 4 of 7 students without sign errors did, an association between depicted direction and avoiding sign errors.

> "None of the 5 students with a sign error labeled their axes or in anyway depicted positive or negative directions on their diagrams, whereas 4 of 7 students who did not have a sign error (the six who selected the correct answer and 1 who had only a magnitude error) labeled axes or directions."
