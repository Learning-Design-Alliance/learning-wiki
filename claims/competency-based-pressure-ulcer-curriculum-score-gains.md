`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, from van Zuilen et al.'s four-year mandatory competency-based geriatric curriculum with 645 medical students, that "Competency scores improved from a Score of 17.3 after Phase 1 to 19.8 after Phase 3 (p < 0.001)"; every student achieved basic competency.
