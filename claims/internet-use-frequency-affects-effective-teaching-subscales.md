## Evidence

### Arabacioglu 2014

Arabacioglu, T. & Akar-Vural, R. (2014). Using Facebook as a LMS?. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1022959

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way MANOVA on the same 42-participant quasi-experiment found significant effects of internet-use frequency on good teaching (F=4.909, η2=.214, p=.013), generic skills (F=5.609, η2=.238, p=.008), and e-learning (F=6.258, η2=.258, p=.005).
