Evidence¶

Cummins 1917¶

Cummins, R. A. (1917). 'Bright' and 'Slow' Pupils in Elementary and High School. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Expectation calculation from the school's records of 116 graduates and 184 non-graduates. The author calculates that of every 100 boys entering "Early" 41 will graduate, against 36 of 100 "Late"-entering boys; no effect size is printed.

"Of every 100 boys who enter "Early" 41 will graduate and 59 will not. Of every 100 boys who enter "Late" 36 will graduate and 64 will not."

Cummins 1917 (2)¶

Cummins, R. A. (1917). 'Bright' and 'Slow' Pupils in Elementary and High School. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Expectation calculation from the same record set, reported for girls. Of every 100 girls entering "Early" 60 will graduate, while of 100 "Late"-entering girls "only 30 will graduate"; no effect size is printed.

"Of every 100 girls who enter "Early" 60 will graduate and 40 will not. Of every 100 girls who enter "Late" only 30 will graduate, the other 70 will not."
