### Hege Inga 2018

Hege Inga, Kononowicz Andrzej A., Berman Norman B., Lenzer Benedikt, Kiesewetter Jan. (2018). Advancing clinical reasoning in virtual patients – development and application of a conceptual framework. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(1):Doc12. [doi:10.3205/zma001159](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001159)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the Teaching/Assessment analysis of the framework applied to VPs, the article reports that VPs are used especially for formative assessment and include methods such as multiple choice questions and decision points, yet "the quantitative methods often implemented in VPs, do not sufficiently reflect" clinical reasoning's non-linearity. No effect size is reported.

> "However, the non-linearity of clinical reasoning is a challenge for scoring and feedback and the quantitative methods often implemented in VPs, do not sufficiently reflect this."
