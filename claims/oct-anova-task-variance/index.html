Shantha and Mekala 2017¶

S. Shantha, S. Mekala. (2017). The Role of Oral Communicative Tasks (OCT) in Developing the Spoken Proficiency of Engineering Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 8(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.2p.161

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on the experimental group's task performances across nine oral communicative tasks; Table 3 prints F values of 9.564 (fluency), 8.828 (grammar), 8.04 (idea/content), 6.221 (volume) and 6.208 (pronunciation), all at .01 significance.

"Table 3 reveals that there is a significant variance among the tasks in terms of speaking components such as fluency, grammatical acceptability, ability to expand the idea, volume and pronunciation at .01 levels."
