# OBM2 did not alter grades for clear pass or clear fail performance, and raised the minimum passing pattern from two to three clear pass marks per station

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2018

Shulruf Boaz, Damodaran Arvin, Jones Phil, Kennedy Sean, Mangos George, O'Sullivan Anthony J., Rhee Joel, Taylor Silas, Velan Gary, Harris Peter. (2018). Enhancing the defensibility of examiners' marks in high stake OSCEs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1112-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the discussion of the 2016 OSCE implementation (N=259), the authors state that "the OBM2 did not impact any grade given to a student when the level of performance was clearly pass or clearly fail", and separately note that the traditional method permitted a station pass with only two clear pass marks out of five, whereas students passing at least three of five items could not fail under OBM2.

> "the OBM2 did not impact any grade given to a student when the level of performance was clearly pass or clearly fail. This means that no student who performed well was negatively impacted by the OBM2."
