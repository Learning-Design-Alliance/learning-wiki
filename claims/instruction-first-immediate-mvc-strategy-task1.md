## Evidence

### Saba 2025

Saba, J., Kapur, M., & Roll, I. (2025). Learning about multivariable causality with interactive simulations: exploration before instruction may hurt immediate gains but benefits transfer. Instructional Science, 53, 1603–1632. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09726-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA on Task 1 log files comparing Exploration-first (n = 35) and Instruction-first (n = 36), with virus-spread pretest as covariate, showed "a significant difference between conditions" with ηp2 = 0.236; the covariate effect was not significant.

> "There was a significant difference between conditions: F(1,69) = 5.03, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.236 with a large effect size. There was no significant effect of the covariate F(1,69) = 0.279, p = 0.890, ηp2 = 0.017."
