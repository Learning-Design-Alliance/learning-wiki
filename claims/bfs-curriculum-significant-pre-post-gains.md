### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Kimia Fazeli, Tirth Bhatt, Jennifer Albert, Marnie Hill, Tiffany Barnes, Shiyan Jiang, and Bita Akram. (2026). Democratizing Foundations of Problem-Solving with AI: A Breadth-First Search Curriculum for Middle School Students. Preprint, Accepted to AIED 2026. `q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Analysis of matched pre- and post-assessments from 41 students with consent and completed measures, administered before Activity 1 and after Activity 4 in I-SAIL. Scores rose from "pre:M= 0.683" to "post:M= 0.790", significant on a paired t-test (t(40) = 2.90, p=.006) with "a moderate within-subject effect size (Cohen's dz = 0.453)".

> "Students demonstrated improved performance from pre- to post-test (pre:M= 0.683,SD= 0.219; post:M= 0.790,SD= 0.232). This improvement was statistically significant in both a paired t-test (t(40) = 2.90,p=.006)"
