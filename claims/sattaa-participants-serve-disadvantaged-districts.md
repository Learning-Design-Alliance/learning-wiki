## Subclaims

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of participating San Antonio teachers' school district demographics (Table 2/Figure 2) against United States Census Bureau city-wide figures, showing populations that are "overwhelmingly non-white and substantively economically disadvantaged". SATTAA2021 teachers elsewhere in Texas taught students who were more likely to be white (40.7%) and somewhat more affluent (36% economically disadvantaged).

> "the participants' school districts were home to student populations that are overwhelmingly non-white and substantively economically disadvantaged"
