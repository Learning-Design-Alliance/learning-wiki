### Y D Kristanto 2019 (2)

Y D Kristanto, M M Melissa, and A H Panuluh. (2019). Discovering the formal definition of limit through exploration in dynamic geometry environments. IOP Conf. Series: Journal of Physics: Conf. Series 1180. https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1180/1/012004

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative observation of student responses gathered through the dynamic geometry software: students substituted familiar limit rules for definitional proof, with "substitution rule" named as an example.

> "They tend to use limit rules, e.g. substitution rule, and table to prove the statement instead of using the limit definition."
