## Evidence

### Betül Toker 2015

Betül Toker, Raşit Avcı. (2015). Effect of Cognitive-Behavioral-Theory-based Skill Training on Academic Procrastination Behaviors of University Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.0077

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study with matched-pair design; 13 experimental and 13 control students. ANCOVA on APS posttest adjusted means found the experimental group lower: "a statistically significant difference was found between the posttest adjusted means in favor of the control group" (F(1–23) = 10.223, p < .01).
