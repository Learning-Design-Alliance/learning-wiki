# Voluntary tutor usage is positively correlated with Unit 1 assessment grades among adult learners (Transactions coefficient 0.26, p < 0.001)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A linear regression with tutor transactions as the sole predictor found a significant positive relationship between amount of tutor use and Unit 1 grade, though tutor usage accounts for only a small proportion of grade variance. ## Evidence

### Adit Gupta and Christopher MacLellan 2025

Adit Gupta and Christopher MacLellan. (2025). Intelligent Tutors Beyond K-12: An Observational Study of Adult Learner Engagement and Academic Impact. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.16613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.16613)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on 3,510 students (520 tutor users, 214 with Unit 1 interactions); Table 2 reports Transactions estimate 0.26, error 0.08, t-value 3.39, p < 0.001. Tutor users achieved slightly higher mean Unit 1 grades than non-users (Figure 9).

> "Our results show there is a significant relationship between amount of tutor use and grade. Specifically, the Transactions variable is positively correlated with a higher grade."
