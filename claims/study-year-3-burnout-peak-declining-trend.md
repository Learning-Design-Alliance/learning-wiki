## Evidence

### Ilić IM 2023

Ilić IM, Ilić MD. (2023). The relationship between the burnout syndrome and academic success of medical students: a cross-sectional study. Archives of Industrial Hygiene and Toxicology. https://doi.org/10.2478/aiht-2023-74-3719

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the full 760-student sample, adjusted for sociodemographic and academic covariates. The article reports OR=6.09 (95% CI=2.40–15.49, P=0.000) for study year 3 versus year 1 as reference.

> "Multivariate logistic regression analysis singled out study year 3 as a significant independent predictor of high burnout risk for all medical students(OR=6.09; 95 % CI=2.40–15.49; P=0.000)"

### Ilić IM 2023 (2)

Ilić IM, Ilić MD. (2023). The relationship between the burnout syndrome and academic success of medical students: a cross-sectional study. Archives of Industrial Hygiene and Toxicology. https://doi.org/10.2478/aiht-2023-74-3719

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "but the risk has a significantly decreasing trend with higher years of study (P=0.000) (Table 4)."
