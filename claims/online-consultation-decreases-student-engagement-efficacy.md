## Evidence

### Warren et al. 2013

Jeffrey M. Warren, Edwin R. Gerler, Jr. (2013). Effects of School Counselors' Cognitive Behavioral Consultation on Irrational and Efficacy Beliefs of Elementary School Teachers. The Professional Counselor, 3(1), 6-15. https://doi.org/10.15241/jmw.3.1.6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same study, the online group's ESE mean (M = 5.94, SD = .87) was significantly less than the control group's (M = 7.09, SD = .77), while the face-to-face group's mean was not significantly different from control. Table 3 reports d = 1.46 for this effect.

> "A statistically significant difference was found on efficacy in student engagement (ESE) when the three groups were compared, F(2, 33) = 4.52, p = .018, accounting for 22% of the variance."
