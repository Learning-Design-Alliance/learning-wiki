## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lin Pei-Chao 2024

Lin Pei-Chao, Wung Shu-Fen, Lin Pei-Chen, Lin Yi-Ching, Lin Cheng-Yu, Huang Hsiao-Ling. (2024). Virtual reality-based simulation learning on geriatric oral health care for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Oral Health 24, Article 627. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04249-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the Chinese-version System Usability Scale completed by the 25 experimental-group participants. The article reports means and percentages only, with no standardized effect size. The no-prior-VR-experience comparison showed no significant difference (t = 0.45, p = 0.656).

> "The mean satisfaction score of the VR Oral Health Care Learning System among 25 participants was 79.1 ± 10.36 (range: 57.5–100). Twenty-two (88%) and 11 (44%) participants rated 68 and 80 points or over on their satisfaction with the VR Oral Health Care Learning System, respectively."
