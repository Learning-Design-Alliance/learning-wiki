## Evidence

### Jussila Juuso J 2023

Jussila Juuso J, Pulakka Anna, Halonen Jaana I, Salo Paula, Allaouat Sara, Mikkonen Santtu, Lanki Timo. (2023). Are active school transport and leisure-time physical activity associated with performance and wellbeing at secondary school? A population-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression with orthogonal polynomial trend analysis in the population-based cross-sectional cohort showed the odds "increased linearly with the amount of weekly PA", from OR 1.18 (95% CI 1.02–1.36) at 30 weekly minutes to OR 1.86 (95% CI 1.66–2.08) for the most active adolescents versus inactive ones.

> "Even the smallest dose of leisure-time MVPA, 30 weekly minutes, was associated with 18% higher odds of high perceived academic performance compared with inactivity (OR 1.18, 95% CI 1.02–1.36). The odds increased linearly with the amount of weekly PA, being 86% higher for the most physically active group (OR 1.86, 95% CI 1.66–2.08)."
