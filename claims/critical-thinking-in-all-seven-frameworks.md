## Evidence

### Markle 2013

Markle, R., Brenneman, M., Jackson, T., Burrus, J., & Robbins, S. (2013). Synthesizing Frameworks of Higher Education Student Learning Outcomes. ETS Research Report Series, ETS RR-13-22. http://search.ets.org/researcher

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Document review of seven higher education outcome frameworks (QAA-FHEQ, Bologna, LEAP, CAS, DQP, ATC21S, USDOL-ETA). The authors report "all seven of the frameworks addressed similar concepts" for critical thinking, and define the domain as thinking critically, solving problems, synthesizing information, and sense-making.

> "There is a great deal of consistency when looking across frameworks for the inclusion of critical thinking, problem solving, and decision making as all seven of the frameworks addressed similar concepts."
