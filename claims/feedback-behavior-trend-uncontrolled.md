> "we mined historical data and sampled 1,116 users who have taken at least 10 valid tests (deﬁned earlier) on our platform. In Fig. 10, we show how undesirable behavior parameters, such as wasted attempts, unused time, overtime incorrects, trend down as marks scored increases test-on-test."
