## Evidence

### Timonen Tomi 2022

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Modified 20-domain 5-point Likert questionnaire completed by all ten participants after both methods, compared with Wilcoxon signed rank test. Mean overall scores were 4.3 (VR) vs 3.2 (PACS) for novices and 4.1 vs 3.4 for experts, at "p < 0.001".

> "The mean overall scores for VR were significantly better than those for cross-sectional viewing in both novice and expert groups (p < 0.001)."

### Timonen Tomi 2022 (2)

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 4 shows novices rated learning of anatomy 4.8 in VR versus 3.2 in PACS ("0.039*"), while the expert contrast (4.6 vs 3.2) was marginal (p = 0.059). Novices also rated understanding of structures (p = 0.034) and their relationships (p = 0.041) significantly higher in VR.

> "Learning of anatomy	4.8 (0.4)	3.2 (0.7)	0.039*	4.6 (0.5)	3.2 (0.4)	0.059"
