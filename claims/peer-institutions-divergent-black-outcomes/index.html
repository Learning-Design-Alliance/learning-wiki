Evidence¶

Nichols 2016¶

Nichols, A. H., Eberle-Sudré, K., & Welch, M. (2016). Rising Tide II: Do Black Students Benefit as Grad Rates Increase? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566672

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparative analysis of peer institutions from the College Results Online database (Tables 3 and 4). The report contrasts Missouri and NC State, and Rutgers (black rates up 11.9 points to 72.5 percent) with Purdue (down 5.0 points to 52.5 percent), concluding "what institutions do to serve the students they admit matters … a lot."

"In the last decade, graduation rates for black students at NC State have increased 12.5 points, reaching 64.2 percent. This has also led to a smaller gap (10.0 points) between white and black students."
