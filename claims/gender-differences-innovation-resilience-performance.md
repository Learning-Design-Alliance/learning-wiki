## Evidence

### Qi Chunlin 2025

Qi Chunlin. (2025). The impact of adolescent innovation on academic resilience, distance learning self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91542-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-tests in the 906-student study (442 male, 464 female). The article reports η2 = 0.32 for academic resilience and η2 = 0.31 for academic performance, both favoring males at p < 0.001 (Table 5).

> "Specifically, males scored significantly higher in online learning innovativeness (M = 3.97, SD = 1.25) compared to females (M = 2.47, SD = 0.88), with a mean difference (MD) of 0.34 (t = 17.46, η2 = 0.33)."

### Qi Chunlin 2025 (2)

Qi Chunlin. (2025). The impact of adolescent innovation on academic resilience, distance learning self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91542-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-test for digital learning self-efficacy in the same study, printing η2 = 0.05. The article states the small effect size indicates "gender has a relatively minor impact on this variable" despite statistical significance.

> "Males achieved slightly higher scores (M = 3.68, SD = 0.85) than females (M = 3.29, SD = 1.06), with an MD of 0.39 (t = 6.16, η2 = 0.05)."
