## Evidence

### Gouzi F 2019

Gouzi F, Hédon C, Blervaque L, Passerieux E, Kuster N, Pujol T, Mercier J, Hayot M. (2019). Interactive whiteboard use in clinical reasoning sessions to teach diagnostic test ordering and interpretation to undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1834-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility study analyzing 22 IWB/CRL session paperboards against French College of University Teachers learning objectives. Reviewers blindly assessed items with Lin concordance coefficients from 0.86 to 0.97. The paperboards "revealed the questions, hypotheses, and diagnostic tests suggested by the students."

> "The .pdf files of the IWB/CRL sessions revealed the questions, hypotheses, and diagnostic tests suggested by the students, as well as the extra-clinical signs identified on the raw test data."
