## Evidence

### García‐Robles P 2025

García‐Robles P, Obrero‐Gaitán E, Cortés‐Pérez I, Ibancos‐Losada MdR, Díaz‐Fernández Á, Osuna‐Pérez MC. (2025). The effectiveness of immersive virtual reality as a student‐centered tool for learning neuroanatomy: A single‐blind randomized controlled trial with physiotherapy students. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow‐up assessment in the same RCT after sample loss (n = 22 IVR, n = 23 control). The article reports "no statistically significant differences" and that both groups' averages "remained low, not exceeding 50% on the knowledge test"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Four weeks after the interventions, no statistically significant differences were reported between groups in the general test (p = 0.37) (Table 2, Figure 2). The values of both groups were equal in each of the dimensions of the questionnaire and in their overall score."
