Evidence¶

Bosma et al. 2017¶

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated measures analysis of the 60 trained children's prompt counts across five problem series; children received on average 32.8 prompts (SD = 19.07) over the training. The main effect for time was significant with printed effect size partial η² = .69, with means falling from 8.30 in series 1 to 1.90 in series 5.

"A significant main effect for time was found F (4, 56) = 32.17, p < .001, partial η² = .69, indicating that, as expected (2a), the number of prompts decreased across series 1-5."
