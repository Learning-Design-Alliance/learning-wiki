## Evidence

### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Post-test conceptual questions on four electric and five magnetic field situations (N=19, Table VI). Only one student could correctly determine where the electric-field divergence was non-zero in all five situations; 53% (10) made at least one mistake on Faraday's law and 53% (10) did not check every box under ∇·B.

> "Only one student did not make a single mistake, while all other 18 students made at least three errors."
