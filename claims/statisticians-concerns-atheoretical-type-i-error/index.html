Evidence¶

Iwatani 2018¶

Iwatani, Emi. (2018). Overview of Data Mining's Potential Benefits and Limitations in Education Research. Practical Assessment, Research & Evaluation, 23(15). http://pareonline.net/getvn.asp?v=23&n=15

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review synthesizes statisticians' concerns (Hand 1998, 2000; Zhao & Luan 2006) from the section on traditional statistics: data mining is "less confined in presumptions about the relations among variables" and exploratory in nature.

"They often do not assume a sampling theory, so they cannot make convincing statistical generalizations about a larger population. Without reliance on background and sampling theories, there is no hypothesis testing or significance values (often construed as "statistical rigor") attached to results."

Iwatani 2018 (2)¶

Iwatani, Emi. (2018). Overview of Data Mining's Potential Benefits and Limitations in Education Research. Practical Assessment, Research & Evaluation, 23(15). http://pareonline.net/getvn.asp?v=23&n=15

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review cites Hand (1998) describing how exhaustive searches "will certainly throw up patterns of some kind," many products of random fluctuation; repeated exploration of the same data further risks interpreting spurious relationships as valid.

"The most serious concern about data mining from the perspective of traditional statistics is the inflation of Type I error due to data dredging."
