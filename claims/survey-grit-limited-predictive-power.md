# The survey grit measure has limited predictive power over platform performance in this sample and does not predict pandemic-era performance declines

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Survey grit is significantly associated with only one of four performance measures — 0.8 points in pre-Covid science (p < 0.10) — and this small association disappears after Covid. [→ Chen 2024](#chen-2024)
`q2 i?` Survey grit does not predict the change in performance during Covid-19, being essentially uncorrelated or slightly negatively correlated with resilience. [→ Chen 2024 (2)](#chen-2024-2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions in Table 3 (N = 1723, 1508, 1710, 1490) show survey grit coefficients of 0.5, 0.8, 0.5, and 0.5 for pre/post math and science, with only pre-Covid science approaching significance (p < 0.10).

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`
