Evidence¶

Panadero 2023¶

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Standard random-effects meta-analysis of four effect sizes from four studies on self-efficacy, with a pooled effect of g = 0.18, below d = 0.2. The article notes this set of studies is "particularly strong in terms of research design" with substantial samples (up to 762 participants), but urges caution given the small number of effect sizes.

"The overall effect was 0.18 (SE = 0.05, Z = 3.31, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.07, 0.29], 95% PI [0.07, 0.29], /uni0302.s1/u1D70E2 between = 0.000), which is small in magnitude."
