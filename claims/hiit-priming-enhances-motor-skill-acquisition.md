# A preceding HIIT bout enhances skill acquisition on a novel visuomotor task relative to an active control, with group differences emerging from Block 4 of practice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Skill improved across blocks and the Exercise Group × Block interaction superseded main effects, with HIIT exceeding controls from Block 4 onwards. [→ Brooks 2026](#brooks-2026)

## Evidence

### Brooks 2026

Brooks, E., Hawi, Z., Hosler, J. et al. (2026). High-intensity cardiovascular exercise facilitates online motor skill learning, with no effect of BDNF genotype. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00432-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model of standardised SVIPT skill (Skill ~ Group x Block + (1 | Participant)) in 51 young adults pseudo-randomly allocated to HIIT (n = 27) or active control (n = 24); the "interaction between Exercise Group and Block" reached p = .040. Post-hoc block differences were uncorrected.

> "We observed a main effect of Block (F5, 245 = 11.34, p < .001), no main effect of Exercise Group (F1,49 = 2.78, p = .10), but importantly there was an interaction between Exercise Group and Block ( F5, 245 = 2.37, p = .040)  that superseded the main effects."
