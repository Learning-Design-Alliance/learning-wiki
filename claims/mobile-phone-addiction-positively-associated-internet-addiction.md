## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (SEM) of the Czech sample (n = 1075) found mobile phone addiction positively predicted internet addiction; Table 2A reports B = 0.61 [0.57, 0.65], P < 0.001. The article states "addiction to one form of technology often co-occurs with addiction to another". No standardised effect size is printed for this path, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Mobile phone addiction was found to be positively associated with internet addiction. This interconnected relationship suggests that addiction to one form of technology often co-occurs with addiction to another"

### Kvintova Jana 2024 (2)

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of the Chinese sample (n = 710) found internet addiction increased with anxiety and mobile phone addiction; Table 2B reports IAD ~ MPA B = 0.25 [0.16, 0.35], P < 0.001, supporting the "positive relationship between internet and smartphone addiction" hypothesis. No standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "In addition, Internet addiction increased with greater anxiety and mobile phone addiction."
