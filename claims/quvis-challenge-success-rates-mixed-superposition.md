## Evidence

### Kohnle 2014

Kohnle, A., Baily, C., Campbell, A., Korolkova, N., and Paetkau, M. J. (2014). Enhancing student learning of two-level quantum systems with interactive simulations. [doi:10.1119/1.4913786](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4913786)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In-class trial of the Superposition states and mixed states simulation as a homework assignment in an Advanced Quantum Mechanics course, measuring success on the two built-in unknown-state challenges. The article reports "16 of 20 students (80%) correctly identified the mixture" and 75% correctly identified the superposition state.

> "16 of 20 students (80%) correctly identified the mixture and 15 of 20 students (75%) the correct fractions in the mixture. 15 of 20 students (75%) correctly identified the superposition state"
