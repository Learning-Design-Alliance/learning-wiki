## Evidence

### Simonsen Bjoerg O 2014

Simonsen Bjoerg O, Daehlin Gro K, Johansson Inger, Farup Per G. (2014). Improvement of drug dose calculations by classroom teaching or e-learning: a randomised controlled trial in nurses. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis using the study's certainty-based risk-of-error measure: overall risk fell from 1.5 (0.3) to 1.4 (0.3) (p<0.001). Between-group risk after the course did not differ (p=0.77). The 22% with increased risk was within what could occur by chance (24%).

> "The overall risk of error decreased after the course from 1.5 (0.3) to 1.4 (0.3) (p<0.001), but 41 nurses (22%) showed an increased risk, 20 from the e-learning group and 21 from the classroom group."
