## Evidence

### Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell 2014

Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell. (2014). A comparison of integrated testlet and constructed-response question formats. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of concept-equivalent IT and CR question pairs deployed in midterm and final exams of an introductory physics course (155 midterm, 131 final students). The article reports mean p′ of 0.56 for ITs versus 0.39 for CR questions, "statistically-signiﬁcant, with large eﬀect size". No numeric effect size is printed.

> "On average, the IT and CR questions yield mean p′ values of 0.56 and 0.39, respectively; the diﬀerence being statistically-signiﬁcant, with large eﬀect size"
