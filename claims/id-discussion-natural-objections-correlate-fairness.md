### Brian C. Thomas 2007

Brian C. Thomas. (2007). An In-Class Discussion Activity on the Nature of Science and Intelligent Design. Evolution: Education and Outreach. https://www.ksde.org/Default.aspx?tabid=144 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0710.2631](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0710.2631)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison of written responses across the two parts of the same activity. The author reports that about 4 students used a "both sides" or "fairness" argument and that these statements tended to correlate with Part 1 objections to the word natural.

> "A few (about 4) couched this in terms of a “both sides” or “fairness” argument, which is a common tactic of advocates of ID.  These statements tended to correlate with statements in Part 1 that the word “natural” was problematic in the definition of science."
