## Evidence

### Favier V. 2020

Favier V., Kimmoun A., Gatin A., Gallet P. (2020). Percutaneous tracheostomy simulation training for ENT physicians in the treatment of COVID-19-positive patients. European Annals of Otorhinolaryngology, Head and Neck Diseases. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anorl.2020.06.002

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational report from the authors' third training session, described in the Discussion. After the 15-min low-tech pre-training step, "there was only 1 extubation in the 4 scenarios and no balloon piercing" and procedure time stayed under 20 minutes. No statistical test or effect size is reported.

> "In the third group, which had had a short 15-min experience with the low-tech simulator, there was only 1 extubation in the 4 scenarios and no balloon piercing, and procedure time was never more than 20 minutes."
