## Evidence

### Boal Matthew W. E. 2024

Boal Matthew W. E., Afzal Asma, Gorard Jack, Shah Aishwarya, Tesfai Freweini, Ghamrawi Walaa, Tutton Matthew, Ahmad Jawad, Selvasekar Chelliah, Khan Jim, Francis Nader K. (2024). Development and evaluation of a societal core robotic surgery accreditation curriculum for the UK. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02062-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent-samples t test benchmarking the M-GEARS tool, comparing summative dry-task and cyst-model scores of pass versus fail participants; the printed "mean difference 16.05" was significant at p < 0.001. Thirty participants (63.82%) passed, including 13 of 16 surgeons. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The means were 86.05% and 70% for pass and fail participants respectively (mean difference 16.05, SE 2.06, 95% CI 11.90–20.22, p < 0.001)."
