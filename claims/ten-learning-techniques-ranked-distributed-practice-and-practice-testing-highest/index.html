Across ten learning techniques, distributed practice and practice testing have the largest effects and underlining and summarization the smallest

Across 242 studies (1,619 effects, 169,179 participants) the overall mean was d = 0.56: distributed practice 0.85, practice testing 0.74, elaborative interrogation 0.56, imagery 0.56, self-explanation 0.54, mnemonics 0.50, re-reading 0.47, interleaved practice 0.47, underlining 0.44, summarization 0.44.
