### Kaito Kawakami 2024

Kaito Kawakami, Francesca Procopio, Kaili Rimfeld, Margherita Malanchini, Sophie von Stumm, Kathryn Asbury, Robert Plomin. (2024). Exploring the genetic prediction of academic underachievement and overachievement. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00251-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Domain-specific analyses in TEDS using English−GPS and mathematics−GPS discrepancy scores against domain achievement slopes (Fig. 6). Both magnitudes were similar to the overall GPAΔ result; the English correlation was significantly lower than mathematics.

> "The correlations were also similar for English and mathematics but signiﬁcantly lower for English [r = −0.416, (CI95%[−0.440, −0.390], p < 0.001)] than for mathematics [r = −0.478, (CI95%[−0.501, −0.454], p < 0.001)]."
