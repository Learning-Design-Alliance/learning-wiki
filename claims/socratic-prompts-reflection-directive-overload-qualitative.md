## Evidence

### Han Xue 2026

Han Xue, Cao Jing, Wang Zi, Luo Heng. (2026). The Effects of Agent Type and Feedback Style on Self-Directed Learning: A Mixed-Methods Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Thematic analysis of reflection reports and interviews from the same course-embedded study. Students described a shift from surface-level revision to deeper reasoning, though deep logic reflection codes also appeared across all groups, with relatively higher frequencies in directive groups.

> "Socratic feedback was characterized by dialogic prompting, which appeared to support reflective monitoring. Follow-up questioning was observed exclusively in the Socratic feedback groups (C1 and C2), with no instances identified in the directive feedback groups."

### Han Xue 2026 (2)

Han Xue, Cao Jing, Wang Zi, Luo Heng. (2026). The Effects of Agent Type and Feedback Style on Self-Directed Learning: A Mixed-Methods Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis found overload and resistance codes more prevalent in directive feedback groups (C3 and C4); students described difficulty managing multiple suggestions simultaneously and frustration at continuous identification of issues.

> "In contrast, directive feedback was more frequently associated with perceptions of overload and resistance."
