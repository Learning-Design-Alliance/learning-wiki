## Evidence

### Zhang Shun 2020

Zhang Shun, Orita Hajime, Egawa Hiroyuki, Matsui Ryota, Yamauchi Suguru, Yube Yukinori, Kaji Sanae, Takahashi Toru, Oka Shinichi, Inaki Noriyuki, Fukunaga Tetsu. (2020). Effectiveness and safety of a laparoscopic training system combined with modified reconstruction techniques for total laparoscopic distal gastrectomy. World Journal of Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.3748/wjg.v26.i13.1490

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Surgical outcomes comparison in the retrospective cohort of 92 TLDG cases. Table 2 reports median operation time of 270 min (range 199-512) for trainees versus 239 min (range 154-375) for trainers, with "significantly longer in the trainee group"; no conversions occurred in either group.

> "The operation time was significantly longer in the trainee group compared with the trainer group (P = 0.002). No patients required conversion to open gastrectomy in either group."
