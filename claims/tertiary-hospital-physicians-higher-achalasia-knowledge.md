## Evidence

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression from the nationwide survey of 675 physicians; achalasia knowledge modeled by gender, specialty, discipline, and hospital level, with primary care as reference.

> "tertiary teaching and research hospitals (OR = 2.09; 95% CI: 1.16-3.76; P = .014) and tertiary university hospitals (OR = 1.87; 95% CI: 1.08-3.26; P = .026) outperformed primary care hospitals, while secondary hospitals did not differ significantly (OR = 1.27; 95% CI: 0.72-2.25; P = .402)"

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026 (2)

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression model for HREM familiarity in the same survey; hospital level was significant overall (P = .003), with all non-primary levels showing higher odds than primary care.

> "tertiary teaching and research (OR = 2.49; 95% CI: 1.35-4.58; P = .003) and tertiary university hospitals (OR = 2.09; 95% CI: 1.19-3.68; P = .011) exceeding primary care, and secondary hospitals showing moderate increases (OR = 2.04; 95% CI: 1.12-3.71; P = .020)"
