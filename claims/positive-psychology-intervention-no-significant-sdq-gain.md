## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The hypothesis that the VIA Survey combined with three class lectures on positive psychology would significantly raise SDQ scores from pretest to posttest was not validated. [→ Abed 2016](#abed-2016)

## Evidence

### Abed 2016

Mohaned Ghazi Abed. (2016). Inculcating Positive Thinking in the Self-Concept of Children with Learning Difficulties. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 10, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1138669

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this pretest/posttest experimental study of 40 boys with learning difficulties (20 per group), t-test analysis of SDQ scores found "no statistically significant difference" in improvement, so the stated hypothesis was not validated. No effect size is printed.

> "Findings from the current work, however, did not validate the hypothesis, with no statistically significant difference having been found with regard to the improvement of SDQ scores."
