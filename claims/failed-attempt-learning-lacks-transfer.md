Authors' interpretation offered in the discussion to explain the weak association for the transfer conceptual problem; the article presents this as "a possible explanation" rather than a tested result, alongside possible reliance on memorization of answers.

> "A possible explanation is that students' learning from a failed quiz attempt is highly specific to the problem format, and generally lacks the ability to transfer to a new problem context. Some students may have also relied on memorization of problem answers."
