# Negative affective experience items were dropped due to floor effects and poor discrimination

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All four items assessing negative affective experiences failed psychometric criteria: low means (M = 1.94–2.22), positive skewness (0.46–0.77), 65.0%–72.3% of participants choosing the lowest two options, corrected item-total correlations below 0.30, and EFA loadings below 0.40. [→ Liu Qingqi 2026](#liu-qingqi-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu Qingqi 2026

Liu Qingqi, Miao Wenjiao, Li Jingjing, Lei Yuju. (2026). The Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire (SFAILQ): Assessing AI Literacy in Adolescents, Young Adults, and Midlife Adults. Behavioral Sciences, 16(7), 1110. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item reduction analysis in Sample 1 (N = 1217) on the four negatively keyed affective-experience items, which showed a pronounced floor effect and poor discriminative power, warranting their removal from subsequent analyses.

> "the proportion of participants choosing the lowest two response options ("Strongly disagree" or "Disagree") ranged from 65.0% to 72.3%, while the highest two options ("Agree" or "Strongly agree") were selected by fewer than 15% of respondents."
