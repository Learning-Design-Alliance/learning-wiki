Epistemic stances toward group work in learning physics: Interactions between epistemology and social dynamics in a collaborative problem solving context. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the article's case study analysis, the group successfully completed symbolic integrals and reached correct answers (conceptually productive), yet Penny was interrupted, explained to, and positioned as less knowledgeable. The authors state this directly as a motivation for expanding the definition of productivity to include equitable access to participation roles. Qualitative finding; no effect size.
