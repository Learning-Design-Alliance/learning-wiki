`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Demonstration task in Chapter III: the authors prompted GPT-4V with a photo of a learner conducting a chemical experiment and asked it to observe, assess resources and environment, and evaluate safety rules. The article reports GPT-4V rightly said the learner is wearing a lab coat, noted a fume hood partly visible in the picture, and correctly could not confirm safety glasses or gloves due to a mosaic on the learner's face.

> "the clutter on the benches could be a violation of a good laboratory practices, as it may lead to spills or mix-ups of chemicals."
