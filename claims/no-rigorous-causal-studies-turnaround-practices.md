## Evidence

### Herman 2008

Herman, R., Dawson, P., Dee, T., Greene, J., Maynard, R., Redding, S., and Darwin, M. (2008). Turning Around Chronically Low-Performing Schools: A practice guide (NCEE #2008-4020). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The panel's own assessment of the available evidence base, stated in the Overview. It reports that recommendations were drawn from "less rigorous case study research and theory" covering 10 case studies of 35 schools, and that no study yielded valid causal inference.

> "Unfortunately, the research base on effective strategies for quickly turning around low-performing schools is sparse. The panel did not find any empirical studies that reached the rigor necessary to determine that specific turnaround practices produce significantly better academic outcomes."
