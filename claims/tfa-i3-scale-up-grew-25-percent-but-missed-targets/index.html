Zukiewicz 2015¶

Zukiewicz, M., Clark, M. A., & Makowsky, L. (2015). Implementation of the Teach For America Investing in Innovation Scale-Up. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/implementation-of-the-teach-for-america-investing-in-innovation-scaleup

Implementation study of TFA's i3 scale-up during the first two years of the grant, reporting progress toward scale-up goals. The report states TFA expanded "the size of its corps by 25 percent" but "fell short of its annual targets" for teacher numbers and teacher effectiveness.

"TFA made notable progress in attaining its goals during the first two years of scale-up, expanding the size of its corps by 25 percent. However, it fell short of its annual targets for increasing the number of teachers in its program and increasing teacher effectiveness."
