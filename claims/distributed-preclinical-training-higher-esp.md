## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students trained under the distributed longitudinal schedule (oGDLR) achieved higher effectiveness of simulated plaque removal than students under the massed block schedule (nGDLR) at the post-training evaluation, and the adjusted concept effect was β = +5.16% (p = 0.001). ## Evidence

### Glevitzky 2026

Glevitzky, L., Cyris, M., Sutor, S., Springer, C., Kahl, M., Mekhemar, M., Sälzer, S. & Graetz, C. (2026). Effect of massed versus distributed preclinical training on subgingival instrumentation skill across the preclinical-to-clinical transition. Scientific Reports 16, 23355. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-62235-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable linear regression with cluster-robust SE in a non-randomised sequential two-cohort in vitro study of 102 dental students (136 evaluations), adjusting for semester, sex, age, previous education and loupe use. The adjusted concept effect was "β = +5.16%; 95% CI 2.24–8.09; p = 0.001" favouring the distributed schedule; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "After adjustment, curriculum concept remained an independent predictor of ESP (oGDLR vs. nGDLR: β = +5.16%; 95% CI 2.24–8.09; p = 0.001)"
