# After approximately 6 weeks of embedded support, underprepared students' midsemester scores were not significantly different from college-ready peers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On Group 2 common questions given midsemester, the difference between underprepared and college-ready students was not statistically significant. [→ Hesser 2016](#hesser-2016)

## Evidence

### Hesser 2016

Hesser, T. L., & Gregory, J. L. (2016). Instructional Support Sessions in Chemistry: Alternative to Remediation. Journal of Developmental Education, 39(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-test on Group 2 common questions (Chapters 5, 6, and 7) given midsemester after approximately 6 weeks of support sessions: underprepared M = 2.23 (SD = 1.14, N = 69) vs college-ready M = 2.54 (SD = 1.19, N = 249), t(316) = -1.90, p = .057. Equivalence was not tested; the result is marginal.

> "by midsemester there was no significant difference between mean correct responses for the student groups on Group 2 common questions"
