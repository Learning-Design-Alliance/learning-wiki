`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory pre-registered secondary analysis estimating the BA×Post interaction within each image pose category of the counterbalanced experiment. Portraits had one of the lowest baselines (69.3%), and the article reports the largest gain there.

### Negar Kamali 2026 (2)

Negar Kamali, Candice Rockell Gerstner, Jessica Hullman, Matthew Groh. (2026). Generative AI Literacy Training Improves Intelligence Analysts' Discrimination of Real and AI-Generated Images. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same exploratory pose-category analysis. The article treats the negative directional estimates as exploratory: their confidence intervals include zero, so they are not interpreted as evidence that training harmed performance on these categories. Candid images showed a non-significant 8.7-point gain.

> "Full-body images and posed group images exhibited directional non-significant estimated effects of -11.6 percentage points (95% CI: [-24.2, 1.0]) and -5.2 percentage points (95% CI: [-18.6, 8.2]), respectively."
