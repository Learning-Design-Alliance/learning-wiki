## Evidence

### Hornstra 2017

Hornstra, Lisette; Majoor, Marieke; Peetsma, Thea. (2017). Achievement goal profiles and developments in effort and achievement in upper elementary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 606-629. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12167

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel transition analysis comparing students who changed profiles with students with stable profiles (reference category) in language achievement. The article calls d = .67 "a large difference". Only 46 students made the multiple→moderate→moderate transition in language, so the transition group was small.

> "students who transitioned from a multiple goals to a moderate/indifferent profile also showed a stronger increase in language achievement over time as apparent from the significant interaction with time (b = 4.04, p = .012) compared to students who maintained a multiple goals profile. Cohen's d for this difference was .67"
