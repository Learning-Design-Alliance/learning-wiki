## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-González R 2025

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of the early drop-out rate (R² = 0.029) across the same predictors; the model detected no significant effect for any factor. No effect size printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "None of the factors demonstrated a significant effect on early drop-out rates."

### Gutiérrez-González R 2025 (2)

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the percentage-of-video-viewing regression, embedded questions showed a non-significant positive trend; the non-access model showed a non-significant reduction of −2.66% (p = 0.055).

> "The presence of embedded questions showed, on average, 2.38 additional percentage points of video viewing (p = 0.068), whereas videos shorter than 5 minutes were associated with a non-significant increase after adjusting for all the variables (+2.24%, p = 0.093)."
