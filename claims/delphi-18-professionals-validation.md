### Nicolai B. Plintz 2026

Nicolai B. Plintz, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2026). Empowering children online: a holistic skills framework for cybersecurity. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10565-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-round Delphi study with anonymous participation and feedback loops. Participants came from countries including Estonia, Germany, Italy, the USA, France, and Hungary; "18 professionals from the fields of cybersecu- rity (n = 6), cybersecurity education (n = 5), and general education (n = 6)". No effect sizes reported.

> "The study, conducted using Microsoft Forms, involved 18 professionals from the fields of cybersecu- rity (n = 6), cybersecurity education (n = 5), and general education (n = 6), with one addi- tional participant from another area."

### Nicolai B. Plintz 2026 (2)

Nicolai B. Plintz, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2026). Empowering children online: a holistic skills framework for cybersecurity. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10565-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Round 2 validation in which professionals assessed all 30 matrix fields; the article reports that "Most of the skills identified by the participants were adopted" and that feedback flagged missing or overly complex skills. No effect sizes reported.

> "We subsequently gave the professionals each of the 30 fields for evaluation, asking them to assess them for completeness, age appropriateness, and correct categorization."
