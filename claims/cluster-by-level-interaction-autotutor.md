## Evidence

### Fang 2021

Fang, Y., Lippert, A., Cai, Z., Chen, S., Frijters, J. C., Greenberg, D., & Graesser, A. C. (2021). Patterns of Adults with Low Literacy Skills Interacting with an Intelligent Tutoring System. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00266-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model with cluster, theoretical level, and their interaction predicting proportion correct; subjects as random effect. The interaction was statistically significant, F(9, 65) = 4.04, p < .001.

> "For proportion correct scores, there was a statistically significant interaction between cluster and theoretical level, F (9, 65) = 4.04, p <0 . 0 0 1 ."

### Fang 2021 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model with cluster, theoretical level, and their interaction predicting time per question; subjects as random effect. The interaction was statistically significant, F(9, 651) = 11.41, p < .001.

> "For time per question, there also was a statistically significant interaction between cluster and theoretical level, F (9, 651) =11.41, p < 0.001."
