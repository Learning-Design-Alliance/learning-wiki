## Evidence

### Hursen 2017

Hursen, C., & Gezer Fasli, F. (2017). Investigating the Efficiency of Scenario Based Learning and Reflective Learning Approaches in Teacher Education. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.264

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interpretive descriptive analysis of tape-recorded face-to-face interviews (10–15 minutes) with volunteers from both groups at the end of the application; views were classified into the four themes shown in Figure 2.

> "The views of the prospective teachers are grouped in four different themes. They are: ―satisfaction regarding the application‖, ―strengths of t he applications‖, ―weaknesses of the applications‖ and ―recommendations of prospective teachers regarding the applications‖."

### Hursen 2017 (2)

Hursen, C., & Gezer Fasli, F. (2017). Investigating the Efficiency of Scenario Based Learning and Reflective Learning Approaches in Teacher Education. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.264

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative finding from the reflective learning group interviews: a minority reported fears such as 'I felt very desperate when I first came across with the reflective learning applications', which the authors say disappeared after some time.

> "Only few of the prospective teachers who joined reflective learning applications declared that they had  some difficulties at the beginning and felt uneasy about the approach because they had never encountered such an approach before."
