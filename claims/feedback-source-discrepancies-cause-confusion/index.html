Yeon Su Park 2026¶

Yeon Su Park, Sieun Kim, Keighley Overbay, Seoyoung Kim, Sewook Wee, Daho Jung and Juho Kim. (2026). Supporting Tutors in the Gig Economy with Automated Feedback: A Case Study on Ringle. The 2026 Workshop on Pedagogical Evaluation of Automated Feedback (PEAF2026). https://osf.io/zsc3b/overview?view_only=dcf09d1a9fe64765b013df02af6bc382

q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses found "confusion over discrepancies between the two feedback sources", with higher learner ratings often leading tutors to distrust automated feedback (P13, P18, P19, P27, P32, P34), consistent with prior findings on preferences for positive feedback.

"Many tutorsreported confusion over discrepancies between the two feedback sources, particularly because both used comparable 5-point scales"

Yeon Su Park 2026 (2)¶

Yeon Su Park, Sieun Kim, Keighley Overbay, Seoyoung Kim, Sewook Wee, Daho Jung and Juho Kim. (2026). Supporting Tutors in the Gig Economy with Automated Feedback: A Case Study on Ringle. The 2026 Workshop on Pedagogical Evaluation of Automated Feedback (PEAF2026). https://osf.io/zsc3b/overview?view_only=dcf09d1a9fe64765b013df02af6bc382

q2 · i? · design · r2

Tutors preferred learner feedback because they felt "the automated system could not account for varying lesson dynamics"; lacking contextual awareness such as learner requests and time constraints, automated feedback was perceived as subjective, and tutors wanted clearer explanations of discrepancies (P3, P18, P33).

"the automated system could not account for varying lesson dynamics"
