(Meta) Competences for Digital Practice: Educational Scenarios for the Workplace of the Future Exemplified by Building Information Modeling Work Processes. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' conclusion from the pilot field study comparing two cohorts (N=11) on a joint BIM practice task; final presentation assessments were 91% (BIM) vs 87% (VirtuIng). The fragment "might be (at least partially) substituted" is hedged as printed.

> "the results suggest that missing domain competences might be (at least partially) substituted by enhanced meta competences. This finding emphasizes the importance of imparting meta competences in formal education."
