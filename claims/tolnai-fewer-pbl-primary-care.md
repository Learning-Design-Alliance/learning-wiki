Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

Retrospective cohort study comparing 342 graduates from two Canadian medical schools, McMaster University (PBL) and University of Ottawa (non-PBL). The review reports "significantly fewer graduates from a PBL curriculum working in primary care" (P = 0.05).
