`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive grade comparison from the spring 2012 CM 1 offering (70 students), reported in the section on student performance on final projects. The averages are descriptive; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "Overall, student performance on the final project (avg. 83.8 ± 2.5%) was on par with homework performance (avg. 85.9 ± 1.9%) and exam grades (avg. 80.0 ± 1.6%)"
