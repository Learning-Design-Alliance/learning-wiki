# Academic self-efficacy predicts final course grade and partially mediates the association between social anxiety and final grade

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Controlling for GPA, academic self-efficacy at the beginning of term significantly predicted actual final course grade (ß = 0.21), while social anxiety did not. [→ Hood 2021](#hood-2021)
`q3 i?` Mediation analysis showed a significant indirect effect of social anxiety on final grade through academic self-efficacy (b = −0.35, bootstrapped 95% CI [−0.69, −0.08]), though the standardized indirect effect was small (−0.06). [→ Hood 2021 (2)](#hood-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Hood 2021

Hood, S., Barrickman, N., Djerdjian, N., Farr, M., Magner, S., Roychowdhury, H., Gerrits, R., Lawford, H., Ott, B., Ross, K., Paige, O., Stowe, S., Jensen, M., & Hull, K. (2021). “I Like and Prefer to Work Alone”: Social Anxiety, Academic Self-Efficacy, and Students’ Perceptions of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression predicting instructor-reported final grade (n = 158, three classes) with self-reported GPA as covariate; Table 6 reports ß = 0.21 for academic self-efficacy and ß = −0.11 for social anxiety.

> "For actual final grade received in the course, level of academic self-efficacy was a significant predictor, whereas social anxiety at the beginning of term was not when controlling for GPA (Table 6)."

### Hood 2021 (2)

Hood, S., Barrickman, N., Djerdjian, N., Farr, M., Magner, S., Roychowdhury, H., Gerrits, R., Lawford, H., Ott, B., Ross, K., Paige, O., Stowe, S., Jensen, M., & Hull, K. (2021). “I Like and Prefer to Work Alone”: Social Anxiety, Academic Self-Efficacy, and Students’ Perceptions of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis using PROCESS in SPSS with bootstrapping (5000 samples), controlling for self-reported GPA. The direct effect of social anxiety on final grade was not significant (b = −0.64, p = 0.15); the authors note this violates one of Baron and Kenny's conditions.

> "there was a significant indirect effect of academic self-efficacy in accounting for the relation between social anxiety and final grade in the course (b = −0.35, bootstrapped 95% CI [−0.69, −0.08]). However, the size of this mediation effect was small (standardized indirect effect = −0.06, bootstrapped 95% CI [−0.12, −0.01])."
