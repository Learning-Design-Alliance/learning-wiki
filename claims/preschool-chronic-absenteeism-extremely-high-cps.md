Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative attendance data for all preschool students in 2011-12. The report states "45 percent of three-year-old preschool students and 36 percent of four-year-old preschool students were chronically absent" using a 10-percent-of-enrolled-days cutoff.

> "In 2011-12, 45 percent of three-year-old preschool students and 36 percent of four-year-old preschool students were chronically absent—meaning they missed at least 10 percent of their enrolled days, or a minimum of 15 days, over the course of a full school year."
