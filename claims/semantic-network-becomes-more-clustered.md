### Yoo Hyo Hyun 2018

Yoo Hyo Hyun, Shin Sein, Lee Jun-Ki. (2018). Effects of medical communication curriculum on perceptions of Korean medical school students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.106

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semantic network analysis of open-ended responses using NetMiner showed densities rising from 0.042 (pre-LBC) to 0.099 (post-LBC) and 0.091 (post-CBC); "Network density doubled post-CBC." Sub-communities gained far more connections between and within them post-LBC.

> "The visualized pre-LBC, post-LBC, and post-CBC network densities were 0.042, 0.099, and 0.091, respectively. Network density doubled post-CBC."
