## Evidence

### Gibson Louisa 2021

Gibson Louisa, Porter Melanie. (2021). Maternal drinking and smoking. Can it explain the exceptional academic performance of LBOTE children? A preliminary analysis. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-021-05544-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparisons in a preliminary secondary analysis of LSAC longitudinal data (5107 infants recruited 2004), restricted to breastfed babies, using Kruskal–Wallis H tests; the article reports "LBOTE children performed better than ELB children" on those three tests. Table 2a prints Grade 3 spelling χ2 = 19.56, p < 0.001 and Grade 5 spelling χ2 = 14.28, p < 0.001.

> "It was found that LBOTE children performed better than ELB children on grade 3 NAPLAN writing and spelling tests, as well as grade 5 NAPLAN spelling tests."
