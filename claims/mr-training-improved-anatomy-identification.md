## Evidence

### Huang Xiaojing 2023

Huang Xiaojing, Yan Zhaoxia, Gong Chao, Zhou Zheliang, Xu Hua, Qin Chunhui, Wang Zhenwei. (2023). A mixed-reality stimulator for lumbar puncture training: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04173-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In this pilot study of 40 trainees, pre- vs post-training comparisons on a standard mannequin without MR overlay showed correct anatomy identification improved from 12/40 to 38/40 (p = 0.0001, Table 4).

> "There were significant differences in correct identification of the anatomy, correct angle and direction of the puncture needle and the numbers of the puncture attempts before and after training (p < 0.05) (Table 4)."
