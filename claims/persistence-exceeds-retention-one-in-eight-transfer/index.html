Descriptive comparison across entering cohorts in the Clearinghouse data. The report computes the average persistence-retention gap and states "nearly one in eight students who start college in any fall term, transfer to a different institution by the following fall."

"For the included years, the overall persistence rate is about 1 3 percentage points higher, on average, than the retention rate. Thus, nearly one in eight students who start college in any fall term, transfer to a different institution by the following fall."
