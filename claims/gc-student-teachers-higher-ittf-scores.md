## Evidence

### Doxey 2026

Doxey, J., Baty, B. J., Bisordi, K., & Dixon, S. (2026). Assessing genetic counselors' teaching experience and approaches to teaching in the classroom. Journal of Genetic Counseling. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.70207

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent samples t-test among 89 ATI-R respondents comparing teachers of GC students versus non-GC students. The article reports the ITTF difference as significant, "t(87) = -2.64, p = 0.01", and no effect size is printed; the CCSF comparison was not significant (p = 0.06).

> "However, GCs who teach GC students had higher ITTF scores (M = 3.4, SD = 0.7) than GCs who do not teach GC students (M = 2.5, SD = 0.5); t(87) = -2.64, p = 0.01."
