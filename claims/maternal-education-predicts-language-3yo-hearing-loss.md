## Evidence

### Ching Teresa Y. C. 2010

Ching Teresa Y. C., Crowe Kathryn, Martin Vivienne, Day Julia, Mahler Nicole, Youn Samantha, Street Laura, Cook Cassandra, Orsini Julia (2010). Language development and everyday functioning of children with hearing loss assessed at 3 years of age. International journal of speech-language pathology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3094718/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise forward regression with PLS-4 total language scores as dependent variable and maternal education, socio-economic status, severity of hearing loss, and age of intervention as predictors. The article reports "maternal education was significant (Beta = −.22, p = .005)"; socio-economic status and age of intervention did not contribute significantly.

> "After accounting for the effect of severity of hearing loss (Beta = −.43, p < .0001), maternal education was significant (Beta = −.22, p = .005). No other factors contributed significantly to language scores."
