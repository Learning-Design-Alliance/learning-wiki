### Eiring Lisa 2026

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of peer-teaching-based exam training. The abstract reports that "high numbers planned to include it in their prospective preparation for oral exams".

> "Most students appreciated the peer-group format being effective and high numbers planned to include it in their prospective preparation for oral exams."

### Eiring Lisa 2026 (2)

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey data from the first training round (T1). A large majority of students (78.3%) expressed the intention to prepare for future exams in peer groups, with only 6.8% denying this intention.

> "The majority of students (78.3%) stated that they wanted to prepare for future exam formats in a peer group (category 1), while only 6.8% of students denied this (category 5)."
