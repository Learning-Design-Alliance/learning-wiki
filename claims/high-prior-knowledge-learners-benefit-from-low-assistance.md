## Evidence

### Tetzlaff and Peters 2025

Leonard Tetzlaff, Bianca Simonsmeier, Tabea Peters, Garvin Brod. (2025). A cornerstone of adaptivity – A meta-analysis of the expertise reversal effect. Learning and Instruction 98 (2025) 102142. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102142

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Same meta-analytic dataset (60 studies, 5924 participants); the pooled standardized mean difference for high prior knowledge learners comparing high vs. low assistance was "d = - 0.428, 95 % CI [-0.647, − 0.209], k = 88, j = 60", with strong heterogeneity (I2 = 87.55 %).

> "Individuals with a high level of prior knowledge showed worse learning outcomes when learning with high assistance instructions as opposed to low assistance instruction (d = - 0.428, 95 % CI [-0.647, − 0.209], k = 88, j = 60)."
