### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students become proficient problem solvers Part I: A brief review. [doi:10.3390/educsci13020156](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13020156)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Eylon and Reif's 1984 study in which students learned content presented hierarchically or sequentially and then completed complex and local acquisition tasks; students "performed significantly better on the complex tasks" after hierarchical instruction. No effect size is printed.

> "students learned the same content with knowledge presented either hierarchically or sequentially. Students then worked on both “complex” and “local” acquisition tasks and the researchers found that students performed significantly better on the complex tasks when taught the content with knowledge presented hierarchically."
