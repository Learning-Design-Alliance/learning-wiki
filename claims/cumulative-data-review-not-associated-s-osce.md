## Evidence

### Bernard Aaron W. 2017

Bernard Aaron W., Ceccolini Gabbriel, Feinn Richard, Rockfeld Jennifer, Rosenberg Ilene, Thomas Listy, Cassese Todd. (2017). Medical students review of formative OSCE scores, checklists, and videos improves with student-faculty debriefing meetings. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1324718

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression predicting end-of-year S-OSCE score from total reviews, controlling for first F-OSCE performance. Year-1 model R-square 0.32 (F = 0.72, p = .580); year-2 R-square 0.14 (F = 3.21, p = .017); no review coefficient significant. The authors note S-OSCE scores fell in a narrow range, limiting ability to detect a difference.

> "For both groups of students, the total number of reviews during the year for each modality was not associated with improved S-OSCE performance (Table 3)."
