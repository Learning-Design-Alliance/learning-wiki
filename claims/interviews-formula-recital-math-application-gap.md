## Evidence

### Rahmani Fateme 2025

Rahmani Fateme, Ahmadi Kalateh Fatemeh. (2025). Assessing students’ understanding of the concept of electric potential difference based on the SOLO taxonomy in upper-secondary students for a targeted assessment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.13723](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.13723)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative interviews with two of the 92 students, each asked three questions; Student 1 successfully wrote all relevant formulas but "struggled with the mathematical application", and Student 2 showed confusion about the role of force in potential-difference changes on question 4-A.

> "Interviews revealed that students need additional tools to visualize abstract concepts, such as potential difference, to better overcome their difficulties."
