# Adding a PhET activity to pre-class reading, in either order, did not significantly affect post-test learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no significant effect of treatment (text-only, PhET-then-text, or text-then-PhET) on post-test scores when controlling for pre-test and topic. [→ Jared B. Stang 2016](#jared-b-stang-2016)
`q2 i2` Pre-test score was a highly significant predictor of post-test learning, with η2 = .067, a medium effect size. [→ Jared B. Stang 2016 (2)](#jared-b-stang-2016-2)

## Evidence

Jared B. Stang, Megan Barker, Sarah Perez, Joss Ives, and Ido Roll. (2016). Active learning in pre-class assignments: Exploring the use of interactive simulations to enhance reading assignments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.078](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.078)
