## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Despite a slight difference in pre-test means, the Mann-Whitney U test indicated no significant difference between the experimental and control groups on the pre-test. [→ Asmalı 2018](#asmal-2018)

## Evidence

### Asmalı 2018

Mehmet Asmalı. (2018). Integrating Technology into ESP Classes: Use of Student Response System in English for Specific Purposes Instruction. Teaching English with Technology, 18(3), 86-104. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1186385

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison in the same quasi-experimental study: pre-test means were 48.95 (experimental, n=19) and 42.70 (control, n=24), and the Mann-Whitney U test found "no significant difference" between the groups, so both groups started statistically equal on the 76-question test.

> "The results indicated no significant difference between the experimental (Mdn = 50) and the control group (Mdn = 42.10), U = 161.500, p = .103"
