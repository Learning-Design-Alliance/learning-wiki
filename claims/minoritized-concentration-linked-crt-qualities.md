## Evidence

### Abacioglu 2020

Abacioglu, C. S., Volman, M., & Fischer, A. H. (2020). Teachers' multicultural attitudes and perspective taking abilities as factors in culturally responsive teaching. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12328

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlation analysis reported in the Results (Table 2) of the 143-teacher survey; the printed correlations with minoritized student concentration are r = .289** for attitudes, r = .284** for perspective taking, and r = .345** for culturally sensitive teaching. Background qualities were not related to the outcomes.

> "Teachers who reported more positive Multicultural Attitudes, higher Perspective Taking Abilities, and more frequent Culturally Responsive Teaching worked in schools that had higher Concentration of Minoritized Students."
