### Carlos González 2023

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the results section on the pedagogical application of digital technologies, the interview study reports teachers implemented strategies and "employed digital tools to promote interaction during synchronous online classes"; asynchronous delivery used video capsules, class recordings, and LMS uploads, with WhatsApp groups for between-session interaction.

> "To overcome these problems, teachers implemented several strategies. Some teachers employed digital tools to promote interaction during synchronous online classes, given that interaction was difficult to achieve. Tools used for this purpose included Kahoot!, Miro, Padlet, Mentimeter, Online Surveys, and Online Group Rooms, among others."
