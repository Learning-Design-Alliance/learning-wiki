Gibson 2025¶

Gibson, D. C., Azukas, M. E., & Yilmaz Soylu, M. (2025). A Taxonomy of Metacognitive Learning Scenarios in Professional Contexts: Integrating Systems Theory with Empirical Constraints. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24142

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' interpretive analysis in the Developmental Trajectories discussion section. The specialist pathway emphasizes bottom-up monitoring with rich external engagement without full bidirectional integration; the strategic pathway emphasizes top-down control and knowledge-driven performance.

"The existence of multiple viable trajectories challenges linear developmental models and suggests that metacognitive development may exhibit equifinality, multiple pathways leading to effective professional performance."
