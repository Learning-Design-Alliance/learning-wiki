H. P. A. Boshuizen 2016¶

H. P. A. Boshuizen. (2016). Teaching as regulation and dealing with complexity. Instructional Science, 44, 311–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9377-x

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The editorial reports, citing Herppich et al. (this issue), that pedagogical experts generated less deep learning than less pedagogically expert tutors in a biological domain. Reported second-hand; design not described here.

"Herppich et al. found that pedagogical experts revert to explanations when tutoring in a biological domain and generate less deep learning than tutors with less pedagogical expertise, who happened to be more interactive and rather scaffold their tutee’s learning."
