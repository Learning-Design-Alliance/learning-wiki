## Evidence

### Ronimus 2019

Ronimus, M., Eklund, K., Pesu, L., & Lyytinen, H. (2019). Supporting struggling readers with digital game-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 639–663. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09658-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis tests comparing training-period gain scores of children training only at home (n = 3), only at school (n = 4), or both (n = 10). No significant differences for word reading, spelling, reading fluency (p = .262), or reading comprehension (p = .103).

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test found no significant differences in the four gain scores between the three groups trained at different places: word reading (H = 4.22, df = 2, p = .121), spelling (H = 1.43, df = 2, p = .489)"
