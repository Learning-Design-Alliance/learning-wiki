## Evidence

### Sun Shanshan 2024

Sun Shanshan, Tao Lei, Yang Lin, Zhang Zhigang. (2024). Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice (KAP) of ICU Nurses towards Tracheal Intubation Patients' Postextubation Dysphagia: A Cross-Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9981458

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 627-nurse survey data with very satisfied as reference: "fair: β = −0.320, p < 0.001" for the total score; job satisfaction also entered the knowledge, attitude, and practice models.

> "job satisfaction (more satisfied: β = −0.235, p < 0.001; fair: β = −0.320, p < 0.001; not very satisfied: β = −0.261, p < 0.001; very dissatisfied: β = −0.259, p < 0.001)"
