Adama 2026¶

Adama, V., Biddle, R., Arachchilage, N., & Lottridge, D. (2026). Confident yet Concerned: Inconsistencies in Computing Students' Attitudes on Cybersecurity. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18541

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey of 236 computing students using seven objective ability questions adapted from the Pew Research Center. The article reports "Over 75% of the students correctly answered five or more out of the seven selected questions", with the highest concentration scoring 5–6 of 7; in the original Pew survey only one question was answered correctly by 75% of participants.

"Over 75% of the students correctly answered five or more out of the seven selected questions. The highest concentration of participants scored between 5 and 6 (out of 7 questions) on the scale"
