# Test anxiety mediated the effect of URM status on exam performance only at inclusive institutions, not at community colleges or selective institutions

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` URM students at inclusive institutions had higher test anxiety (β = 0.30, p = 0.001), and the resulting indirect effect on exam scores was statistically significant but small (mediation path β = −0.04, p = 0.04); no such mediation appeared at the other institution types. [→ Salehi S. 2021](#salehi-s-2021)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

SEM group analysis by institution type on the survey subsample; the mediation path was significant only at inclusive institutions (β = −0.04, p = 0.04), whose magnitude the article computes as 0.3 × 0.13 = 0.04 SD.

> "These relations imply that test anxiety mediated the effect of URM status on exam scores only at the inclusive institutions, but not at community colleges or selective institutions. The size of the mediation path can be calculated by multiplying the coefficients included in each path (βCC = −0.04 ± 0.04, p = 0.22; βinclusive = −0.04 ± 0.02, p = 0.04; βselective = −0.06 ± 0.05, p = 0.22)."
