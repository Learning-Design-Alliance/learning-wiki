## Evidence

### Limeri 2025

Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation models (original measure, ULTrA, and combined models) on the 1537-student survey dataset, with gender and race as controls. "The ULTrA predicted more variance in every outcome"; Table 5 reports ΔR2 of 0.09 for belonging and mastery-approach, 0.06 self-handicapping, 0.04 evaluative concern, 0.05 intent to persist, 0.03 course grade, 0.01 performance-avoid.

> "The ULTrA predicted more variance in every outcome, and predicted variance above and beyond what the original mindset measure could predict for all of the outcomes. Variance in the academic outcomes (intent to persist in science and course grade) was predicted only by ULTrA, and not by the original mindset measure."
