The editorial's synthesis of the four constituent studies: a common commitment to activity structures affording RSM generation without expecting students to develop canonical RSMs. This is the authors' interpretive claim, not a single tested result.

> "while processes of RSM generation may not lead to canonical solutions, they can still be germane for learning."
