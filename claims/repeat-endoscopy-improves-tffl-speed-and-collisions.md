## Evidence

### Deutschmann Michael W 2013

Deutschmann Michael W, Yunker Warren K, Cho John J, Andreassen Meri, Beveridge Shari, Bosch John Douglas. (2013). Use of a Low-fidelity simulator to improve trans-nasal fibre-optic flexible laryngoscopy in the clinical setting: a randomized, single-blinded, prospective study. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/1916-0216-42-35

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 32 learners in the simulation group and 33 in the control group; all learners performed a baseline and a second endoscopy on different patients. The article reports a statistically significant improvement in "time to adequate visualization of the glottis and number of mucosal contacts" with no effect size printed.

> "Overall, there was a significant improvement both in the time to adequate visualization of the glottis and number of mucosal contacts between the first and second endoscopies, irrespective of simulator use (84.8 ± 38.0 sec vs. 68.0 ± 33.9 sec, p < 0.01; 5.0 ± 4.6 vs. 3.2 ± 3.0, p < 0.01; Table 3)."
