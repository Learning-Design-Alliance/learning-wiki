Same quasi-experiment; ANCOVA on EPQ posttest scores adjusted for baseline showed "EPQ (F1,35=6.00, η²=.146; P=.02)" favoring the experimental group. Within-group EPQ increased in the experimental group (P=.005) and was unchanged in controls (P=.11).
