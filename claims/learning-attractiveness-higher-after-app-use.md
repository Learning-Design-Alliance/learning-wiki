## Evidence

### Raditya Bayu Rahadian 2023

Raditya Bayu Rahadian, C. Asri Budiningsih. (2023). DEVELOPMENT OF CLASSROOM MANAGEMENT BASED ON STUDENT LEARNING STYLE DATABASE. Multidisciplinary Scientific Journal for Innovative Research, Vol. 1, No. 1, February 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.04057](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.04057)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Learning-attractiveness questionnaire administered to 168 randomly selected junior high school students before and after their teachers used the application. The article states attractiveness was "much higher" after use but prints no means, statistics, or effect size, so the comparison is untested as reported.

> "The usefulness of the product from the student's point of view can be seen from the level of attractiveness of learning that takes place after the teacher uses the product, which is much higher than the level of attractiveness of learning before the teacher uses the product."
