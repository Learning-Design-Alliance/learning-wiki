### Martin O. Archer 2022

Martin O. Archer, Cara L. Waters, Shafiat Dewan, Simon Foster, and Antonio Portas. (2022). GC Insights: Space sector careers resources in the UK need a greater diversity of roles. [doi:10.5194/gc-5-119-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-5-119-2022)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The 36-role set, compiled from industrial partners, sector reports, vacancies and online research, was tested with the same chi-squared procedure (Figure 1a fifth stacked plot, n=36). The article reports the set "is indeed consistent with this aim" of near-equal numbers per category.

> "These reveal that our set is indeed consistent with this aim, hence better represents the sector's diversity. Consequently, it is significantly different from the space census, though importantly no majority category from the census is over-represented."
