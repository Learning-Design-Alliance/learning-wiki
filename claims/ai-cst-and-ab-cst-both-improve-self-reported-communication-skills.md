## Evidence

### Tyrrell Edward G 2025

Tyrrell Edward G, Sandhu Sardip K, Berry Kathryn, Ghannam Suzan F, Lewis Sarah A, Crowfoot Daniel, Sahota Gurvinder Singh, Carson Julie, Wilson Emma E, Taggar Jaspal. (2025). Web-Based AI-Driven Virtual Patient Simulator Versus Actor-Based Simulation for Teaching Consultation Skills: Multicenter Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/71667

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover study of 396 third-year medical students at 2 UK medical schools, with pre-post 11-item surveys anchored to the Calgary-Cambridge model. The article reports "increased by 1.14 (95% CI 0.97-1.32) points" for AI-CST and 1.50 (95% CI 1.35-1.66) for AB-CST, both P<.001. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The pre-post aggregated mean difference scores from AI-CST increased by 1.14 (95% CI 0.97-1.32) points and from AB-CST by 1.50 (95% CI 1.35-1.66) points (both P<.001)."
