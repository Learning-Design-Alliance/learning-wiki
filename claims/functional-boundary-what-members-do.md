### Richard E. West 2017

Richard E. West, Gregory S. Williams. (2017). 'I don't think that word means what you think it means': A proposed framework for defining learning communities. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9535-0

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The review argues functional boundaries are "particularly useful with online communities": a distributed, asynchronous group can work on the same project and share functional assignment despite little social presence or attachment. Functional communities may disband when the shared work ends unless other ties bind members.

> "A distributed and asynchronously meeting group can still work on the same project and perhaps feel a shared purpose along with a shared functional assignment, sometimes despite not sharing much online social presence or interpersonal attachment."
