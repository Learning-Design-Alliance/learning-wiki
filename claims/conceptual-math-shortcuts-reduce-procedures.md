## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reasoning conceptually about mathematical properties, such as orthogonality of sine functions, lets students determine results without computing them, reducing procedures and lessening writing while enabling more sense-making discussion. [→ Bahar Modir 2017](#bahar-modir-2017)

## Evidence

### Bahar Modir 2017

Bahar Modir, John D. Thompson, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2017). Students’ epistemological framing in quantum mechanics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analytical description of the conceptual math frame, illustrated by an episode in which a group argued from the behavior of exponential functions that terms would blow up at different rates without evaluating integrals. The authors state a "shortcut" solution "reduces the procedures and lessens the writing" and expands discussion.

> "Creating a “shortcut” solution11 to the problem reduces the procedures and lessens the writing. Concurrently, discussing mathemat- ical problems conceptually gives students more opportu- nities for sense-making discussions with other members of the group 5,7."
