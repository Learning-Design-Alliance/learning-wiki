Integrating Social 2019¶

Integrating Social, Emotional and Academic Development: An Action Guide for School Leadership Teams. (2019). Aspen Institute. https://hthgse.edu/empathy-interviews-learn-about-the-problem-from-a-users-perspective/

q2 · i? · review · r1

The guide's research summary for its Learning Environment and Climate section asserts, citing foundational research, that "Students learn more in schools with a positive climate, and climate is both measurable and malleable." No effect sizes or individual studies are reported in the guide itself.

"The strongest impact principals have on student learning is through building and maintaining a strong learning climate in their schools. Students learn more in schools with a positive climate, and climate is both measurable and malleable."
