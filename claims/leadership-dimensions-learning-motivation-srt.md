## Evidence

### Wang Dongdong 2026

Wang Dongdong, Zhu Baitong, Song Chunyu, Xie Dandan, Lu Zhuangnian. (2026). Research on the relationships among teachers’ paternalistic leadership style, learning motivation and learning burnout of medical students on a standardized residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09250-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

SEM path analysis of the 403-questionnaire dataset examining direct effects on learning motivation. The model found "authoritarian leadership has a significant negative effect on learning motivation (β = − 0.250)" while benevolent and moral leadership had significant positive effects.

> "the results indicate that benevolent leadership has a significant positive effect on learning motivation (β = 0.141, S.E. = 0.037, C.R. = 2.472, p = 0.013). Moral leadership also has a significant positive effect on learning motivation (β = 0.310, S.E. = 0.040, C.R. = 5.224, p < 0.001). In contrast, authoritarian leadership has a significant negative effect on learning motivation (β = − 0.250, S.E. = 0.037, C.R. = − 4.242, p < 0.001)."
