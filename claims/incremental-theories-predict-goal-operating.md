## Evidence

### Burnette 2012

Burnette, J. L., O'Boyle, E., VanEpps, E. M., Pollack, J. M., & Finkel, E. J. (2012). Mindsets Matter: A Meta-Analytic Review of Implicit Theories and Self-Regulation. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029531

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The random-effects meta-analysis (k = 113; total unique N = 28,217) reports incremental theories predicted goal operating, printing "helpless-oriented strategies, r = -.238; mastery-oriented strategies, r = .227". Both are small-to-medium associations (|r| .23-.24).
