### Fornasari Maria Grazia 2025

Fornasari Maria Grazia, Midiri Mauro, Albano Giuseppe Davide, Barberi Marika Triscari, Malta Ginevra, Roccella Giovanni, Cannella Roberto, Zerbo Stefania, Argo Antonina, Lo Re Giuseppe. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Lesion-based ROC/AUC analysis of 960 findings from 10 firearm-death PMCT cases at a single center, with autopsy as reference standard and four blinded readers. The paper reports "Their respective AUC values (0.898 and 0.862) were significantly higher" for forensic-trained readers.

> "Their respective AUC values (0.898 and 0.862) were significantly higher than those of radiologists without forensic expertise (R2: 0.716; R4: 0.756)."
