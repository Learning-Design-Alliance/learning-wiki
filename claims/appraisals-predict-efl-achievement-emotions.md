## Evidence

### Wu Qiong 2026

Wu Qiong. (2026). The impact of teacher-student relationship quality on achievement emotions in English as a foreign language within a Confucian heritage cultural context: an examination of the mediating roles of cognitive appraisals. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04561-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural paths in the LSEM of 1103 Chinese college EFL learners. The article reports "EFL-related self-concept was positively associated with enjoyment (β = 0.487, p < .001)" and parallel negative paths for anxiety and boredom.

> "In turn, EFL-related self-concept was positively associated with enjoyment (β = 0.487, p < .001) and negatively associated with anxiety (β = − 0.430, p < .001) and boredom (β = − 0.232, p < .001). Value appraisals were positively related to enjoyment (β = 0.341, p < .001) and negatively related to anxiety (β = − 0.267, p < .001) and boredom (β = − 0.382, p < .001)."
