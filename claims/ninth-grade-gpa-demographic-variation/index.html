Easton 2017¶

Easton, J.Q., Johnson, E., & Sartain, L. (2017). The predictive power of ninth-grade GPA. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/predictive-power-ninth-grade-gpa

Descriptive, unadjusted analyses of pooled 2006-13 CPS cohorts (Figures 2-7): gender, race/ethnicity, neighborhood SES quartile, eighth-grade test quartile, math course level, and across-school distributions. The quote gives the honors-versus-regular contrast; gender figures (47.4 vs 31.8 percent A/B) and the 2.5-times Asian-Black ratio are printed in the same chapter.

"about twice as many students who took (at least some) honors classes received A or B GPAs than students in non-honors classes (61 percent compared to 31.8 percent)"
