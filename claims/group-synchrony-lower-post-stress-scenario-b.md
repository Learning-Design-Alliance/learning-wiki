Lixiang Yan · Dragan Gašević · Vanessa Echeverria · Linxuan Zhao · Yueqiao Jin · Xinyu Li · Roberto Martinez-Maldonado. (2025). In Sync or Out of Sync? Understanding Stress and Learning Performance in Collaborative Healthcare Simulations through Physiological Synchrony and Arousal. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00475-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group-level LMM analysis of heart-rate data from nursing students in high-fidelity simulations, controlling for pre-simulation stress. Adding PSgrp improved fit over the null model; higher group physiological synchrony was related to lower stress levels post-simulation. Only a p-value is printed, so no effect size is available.

> "For post-stress levels, Model 2.1 showed a signiﬁ-cant negative association between PSgrp and stress (AIC = 260.79, BIC = 270.32, /Delta1χ2 = 5.69, p = .017), indicating that higher group physiological synchrony was related to lower stress levels post-simulation."
