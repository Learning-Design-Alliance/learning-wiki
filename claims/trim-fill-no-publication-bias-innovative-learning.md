## Evidence

### Ridwan 2021

Ridwan, M. R., Retnawati, H., Hadi, S., & Jailani (2021). The effectiveness of innovative learning on mathematical problem-solving ability: A meta-analysis. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 7(3), 910-932. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijres.2287

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias analysis of the 31 grouped studies using the trim and fill method with a random-effects model. The funnel plot's "closed circles forming a symmetrical structure" indicated no publication bias and no missing or unpublished studies.

> "The results of the funnel plot consist of closed circles forming a symmetrical structure, which indicates that there is no publication bias. The results provide information that no studies are missing or unpublished."

### Ridwan 2021 (2)

Ridwan, M. R., Retnawati, H., Hadi, S., & Jailani (2021). The effectiveness of innovative learning on mathematical problem-solving ability: A meta-analysis. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 7(3), 910-932. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijres.2287

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Comparison of forest plots before and after applying the trim and fill model (Figure 3a and 3b). The summary effect mean was unchanged at 0.95, which the authors state shows the validity of the difference in innovative and conventional learning effectiveness.

> "The forest plot results above provide information about the same summary effect mean based on the random-effect model before and after using the trim and fill model, namely 0.95."
