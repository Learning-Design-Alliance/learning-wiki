## Evidence

### Alfstad E 2025

Alfstad E, Lockertsen V, Johannessen A-K, Werner A. (2025). The applicability of a structured learning programme focusing on improving observational competencies to strengthen patient safety: a qualitative study with professionals in primary healthcare services. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12692-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study (17 healthcare professionals, Malterud's systematic text condensation); an assistant nurse describes how group exercises with colleagues of varied experience built mutual confidence and made it safe to admit gaps: "You never felt stupid or anything like that if there were things you didn't know."

> "By sharing different experiences, talking with each other, and exercising together in a group of three, we became more confident in each other. You never felt stupid or anything like that if there were things you didn't know."
