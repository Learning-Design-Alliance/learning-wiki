Lachner, A., Sibley, L., & Wagner, S. (2024). Practice Recommendations or Not? The LoGeT Model as Empirical Approach to Generate Localized, Generalized, and Transferable Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09876-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

In the article's schooling example (Sibley et al., 2023a), a mixed-methods study at one school (a second joined later) involved 183 students with pre-posttest knowledge tests co-designed by the teams, analyzed with cluster-robust fixed-effect models and moderation analyses. Gains were significant across subjects, larger for low-prior-knowledge students and high fidelity.

> "Overall, the quantitative findings showed significant learning gains across the teaching units regardless of the subject domain of the teaching units. Additionally, larger increases were obtained for students with low prior knowledge and when the implementation fidelity was high."
