# At the second face-to-face assessment, SOCA scores did not differ between groups, and reviewing nonverbal behavior feedback did not influence group B's results

## Evidence

### Liu C 2016

Liu C, Lim RL, McCabe KL, Taylor S, Calvo RA. (2016). A Web-Based Telehealth Training Platform Incorporating Automated Nonverbal Behavior Feedback for Teaching Communication Skills to Medical Students: A Randomized Crossover Study. J Med Internet Res 18(9):e246. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6299

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison at the second face-to-face consultation after both groups had completed EQClinic training. "the mean total SOCA scores from the second face-to-face consultation did not differ between the groups"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Following group B exposure to the EQClinic, the mean total SOCA scores from the second face-to-face consultation did not differ between the groups (group A: mean 13.28; group B: mean 13.53, P>.05)."

### Liu C 2016 (2)

Liu C, Lim RL, McCabe KL, Taylor S, Calvo RA. (2016). A Web-Based Telehealth Training Platform Incorporating Automated Nonverbal Behavior Feedback for Teaching Communication Skills to Medical Students: A Randomized Crossover Study. J Med Internet Res 18(9):e246. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6299

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-second-assessment comparison of group B reviewers versus non-reviewers of the nonverval behavior feedback. Reviewers (mean 13.62) "did not differ from those in group A or group B who did not complete the nonverbal review (mean 13.47; 17/30, P>.05)".

> "Mean SOCA scores of group B students (group B + NVBF: mean 13.62; 13/30, 43.3%) who reviewed the NVBF component of the EQClinic before their second face-to-face consultation did not differ from those in group A or group B who did not complete the nonverbal review (mean 13.47; 17/30, P>.05)."
