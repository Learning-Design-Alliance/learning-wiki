## Evidence

### Allensworth 2025

Allensworth, E.M., de la Torre, M., Franklin, K., & Xu, J. (2025). Identifying Indicators to Support Educational Attainment for Different Groups of English Learners in High School. (Working Paper). Providence, RI: Annenberg EdExchange. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1241

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the study's relationship analysis of ninth-grade indicators and graduation for the 185,441-student CPS cohort sample, students across English Learner groups with GPAs above 2.0 had about a 90% graduation likelihood, except students with IEPs, for whom the ~90% likelihood did not hold.

> "English Learners with greater than a 2.0 GPA were very likely (~90%) to graduate high school, except for students with Individualized Education Plans (IEPs)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The study's threshold analysis of ninth-grade attendance and graduation shows active English Learners required attendance above 95% to reach ~90% graduation likelihood, a stricter threshold than the 90% attendance sufficing for former and never English Learners.
