Evidence¶

Carlos González 2023¶

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Results section on transversal competencies: teachers reported that safe environments enabled students to ask questions and make mistakes, accessibility meant availability for questions and feedback, and awareness of students' differing living conditions, caregiving burdens, and financial issues fostered empathy and flexibility.

"The deployment of transversal competencies—particularly the capacity to establish a safe learning environment, ensure accessibility to students, and have the disposition to understand students' situations (with regard to both personal and academic matters) were emphasized by teachers as crucial in the exceptional circumstances that were in effect during COVID-19."
