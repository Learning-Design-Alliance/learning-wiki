## Evidence

### Büssing Oliver 2021

Büssing Oliver, Ehlers Jan P., Zupanic Michaela. (2021). The prognostic validity of the formative for the summative MEQ (Modified Essay Questions). GMS Journal for Medical Education 38(6), Doc99. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001495.shtml

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U group comparisons on professional-background variables in the two-cohort sample. Students with vocational training (N=36) scored higher on the MEQ* (d=.466) and on the MEQ-1 (d=.607); the pre-study advantage (d=.437) did not persist to the MEQ-1.

> "Students with a completed vocational training (N=36) scored higher on the MEQ* (U=685, p=.033, d=.466), as well as one semester later with significant difference on the MEQ-1 (U=615, p=.009, d=.607)"
