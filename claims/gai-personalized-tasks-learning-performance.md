## Evidence

### Osman Tasdelen 2025

Osman Tasdelen, Daniel Bodemer. (2025). Generative AI in the Classroom: Effects of Context-Personalized Learning Material and Tasks on Motivation and Performance. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00491-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on post-test scores in the classroom experiment; an ANCOVA with pre-test score as covariate did not alter the result. Personalized tasks (M = 2.98) beat standard tasks (M = 1.53), with effect size "η 2 p = .56".

> "a main effect for task type was found, F(1, 112) = 143.85, p <. 001,η 2 p = .56. Students who worked on context-personalized tasks had signiﬁcantly higher learning performance scores (M = 2.98, SD = 1.60) than those who worked on standard tasks (M = 1.53, SD = 1.25)"
