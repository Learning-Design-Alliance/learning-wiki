## Evidence

### Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019

Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen. (2019). Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the Geometrical Ability Test pre- and posttest in the three-condition quasi-experiment. Scores increased on average: "t(3831) = 18.71, p <0 .001". Growth varied by class but was not explained by either condition.

> "Multilevel analyses showed that there was a linear relation between time and students'scores on the GA T, t(3831) = 18.71, p <0 .001."
