## Evidence

### My Reading Academy: Evidence Rating 2023

My Reading Academy: Evidence Rating. (2023). https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study in prekindergarten and kindergarten classrooms across 4 districts (two in Virginia, two in Texas), with 30 treatment and 52 comparison classrooms. Propensity score weighting produced a final analytic sample of 1,097 students; kindergarteners "outperformed the comparison group" with effect size +0.17.

> "Kindergarten students used the program on average for 40 minutes per active week over 21 active weeks and outperformed the comparison group on the state-administered end-of-year literacy assessments (effect size =+ 0.17)"
