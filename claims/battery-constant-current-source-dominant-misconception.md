## Evidence

### Engelhardt 2003

Engelhardt, P. V., and Beichner, R. J. (2003). Students' understanding of direct current resistive electrical circuits. North Carolina State University, Department of Physics. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0304040](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0304040)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of semi-structured think-aloud interviews with 17 university and 11 high school students using a subset of 10 questions from version 1.0, coded with 88% inter-rater agreement. The article reports the constant-current-source idea was "used most often in solving the interview problems."

> "Interview results indicated that students use the idea that the battery is a constant current source most often in solving the interview problems. Students were found to use different misconceptions depending on the problem presented."
