## Evidence

### Faherty Aileen 2020

Faherty Aileen, Counihan Tim, Kropmans Thomas, Finn Yvonne. (2020). Inter-rater reliability in clinical assessments: do examiner pairings influence candidate ratings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02009-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results of a fully-crossed quasi-experimental mock assessment in which 12 examiners scored three video performances alone and then in six consensus pairs; box-and-whisker plots and Table 1 ranges showed "the variability with individual examiners" was greater.

> "Variability between scores given by examiner pairs (N = 6) was less than the variability with individual examiners (N = 12)."
