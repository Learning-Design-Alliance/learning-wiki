## Evidence

### Li Causi 2025

Li Causi, Gianluca. (2025). Explaining quanta with optical illusions. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.08583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.08583)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design argument from the spin section: the illusion of movement arises from a pattern of light and dark stripes exploiting different retinal response speeds to high- and low-contrast colors; pattern variants yield different apparent directions used for spin up and spin down.

> "While looking at these figures the spectator perceives a clockwise or counterclockwise rotation even if the drawings do not rotate, in close correspondence with the notion of quantum spin that I outlined."
