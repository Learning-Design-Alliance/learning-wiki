## Evidence

### Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown 2018

Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown. (2018). Flipped learning in an EFL environment: Does the teacher's experience affect learning outcomes? The EUROCALL Review, Volume 26, No. 1. https://www.eurocall-languages.org/the-eurocall-review

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test-post-test study of 21 second-year Japanese university students (NFE group) in a 15-week flipped English composition course; compositions scored by three blind raters (α = .95). Paired-samples t test showed "t(20) = 7.73, p < .001, d = 1.61".

> "Analyses showed that significantly significant improvements in proficiency with strong effect sizes and no overlaps in the 95% Confidence Intervals (95% CI) at the post- test in comparison to the pre -test had been achieved for both the NFE group, t(20) = 7.73, p < .001, d = 1.61"
