# Team-teaching grammar instruction does not produce significantly better grammatical proficiency than single-teacher instruction among Iranian junior-high EFL learners

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After ten sessions of team-taught grammar instruction, the experimental co-taught group did not significantly outperform the single-teacher control group on a textbook-based grammar achievement test. [→ Mohammad Aliakbari and Ali Mansouri Nejad 2013](#mohammad-aliakbari-and-ali-mansouri-nejad-2013)

## Evidence

### Mohammad Aliakbari and Ali Mansouri Nejad 2013

Mohammad Aliakbari and Ali Mansouri Nejad. (2013). On the Effectiveness of Team Teaching in Promoting Learners' Grammatical Proficiency. Canadian Journal of Education / Revue canadienne de l'éducation 36:3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057969

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 58 male first-level junior-high EFL students in Ilam, Iran: 28 co-taught (team-teaching model) versus 30 single-taught over ten sessions. The independent-samples t test on grammatical accuracy gave "t-observed (f = 0.864)", with Table 2 printing DF 56, Sig (2-tailed) 0.391, mean difference –1.242; no effect size was reported.

> "Data presented in Table 2 indicated that there was no meaningful difference between the two groups of students with respect to the t-observed (f = 0.864)."
