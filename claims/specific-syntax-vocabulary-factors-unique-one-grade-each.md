Structural analysis of the bifactor model tested whether residual variance in syntax and vocabulary uniquely related to comprehension after controlling for the general oral language factor; the article reports unique prediction "in only seventh grade" for vocabulary and "in only one grade (i.e., fourth grade)" for syntax.
