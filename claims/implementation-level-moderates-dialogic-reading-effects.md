### WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading 2007

WWC Intervention Report: Dialogic Reading. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education, Revised February 8, 2007. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Secondary analysis within the Lonigan and Whitehurst (1998) RCT (75 children analyzed), where study authors divided centers into high and low compliance based on frequency of Dialogic Reading sessions. The review reports that "level of implementation of Dialogic Reading has an impact on child outcomes".

> "These analyses suggest that level of implementation of Dialogic Reading  has an impact on child out-
comes in the oral language domain."
