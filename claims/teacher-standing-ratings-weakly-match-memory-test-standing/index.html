Patterson 1909¶

Patterson, T. L. (1909). Pedagogical Suggestions from Memory Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Teachers in the Oxford and Freeland Street Schools and Worcester high schools rated pupils' general standing as good, fair, or poor; ratings were cross-classified with memory-test classes expressed as percentages of the teachers' classes. Descriptive percentages only.

"Only eight and a half per cent, more of the pupils who were clas- sed A by the teachers have been classed A rather than C by the memory tests."
