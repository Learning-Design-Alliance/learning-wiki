## Evidence

### Pasqualotto 2025

Pasqualotto, A., Cochrane, A., Venuti, P. et al. (2025). Impaired audio-visual associations in dyslexia: evidence beyond linguistic processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00382-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian mixed-model offset coefficient for the Block 2 stimulus introduction across both waves (mean = -0.163, CI95 = [-0.300, -0.039]). The effect was not reliable in Wave 1 alone (mean = -0.057) but was evident in Wave 2 (mean = -0.295, CI95 = [-0.482, -0.113]).

> "The introduction of a new stimulus , in which previously used sounds are recombined to match a new visual form, in Block 2, led to a reliable decrease in accuracy only among the reading-matched controls."
