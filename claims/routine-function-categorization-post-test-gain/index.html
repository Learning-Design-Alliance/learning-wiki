Üstünbaş 2017¶

q2 · i0 · causal · r1

A second Mann-Whitney U comparison in which participants categorized pragmatic routines according to their functions in pre- and post-tests; the pre-test difference printed d=0.0, and the article states this finding "supports the previous one with more evidence."

"while there was no significant difference between the two groups in the pre-test (U=1153.5, p< .05, d=0.0), there was a significant difference in the post test ( U= 726, p=.000) with a medium effect size (d=0.3) (Cohen, 1988)."
