Evidence¶

Kana'an et al. 2014¶

Basim Hamdan Ibrahim Kana'an, Salahud Din Abdul Rab & Ahlullah Siddiqui. (2014). The Effect of Expansion of Vision Span on Reading Speed: A Case Study of EFL Major Students at King Khalid University. English Language Teaching, 7(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p57

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the untrained control group's pre- and post-test reading speed (Table 5: Z = -.955, Asymp. Sig. .339; 15 of 25 students slower, 10 faster, 0 ties). The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"Table 5 clearly shows that there is no significant difference in the reading speed of the subjects of the control group before and after the training, and hence, hypothesis 5 is also proved accurate."
