Fleck, L., Amelung, D., Fuchs, A., Mayer, B., Escher, M., Listunova, L., Schultz, J.-H., Möltner, A., Schütte, C., Wittenberg, T., Schneider, I., & Herpertz, S. C. (2024). Interactional competencies in medical student admission– what makes a “good medical doctor”?. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the N = 70 validation study, correlations between IC-MD total score and the two major cognitive selection criteria were non-significant (TMS/GPA correlations range-restriction-corrected using Thorndike's Case II formula). Equivalence was not tested.

> "IC-MD total scores were not significantly associated with cognitive ability as assessed by the TMS (r = .01, p =.967) or with high school GPA (r = .14, p =.316)."
