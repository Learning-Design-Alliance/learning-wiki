## Evidence

### Saputri 2020

Saputri, W., Corebima, A. D., Susilo, H., & Suwono, H. (2020). QASEE: A potential learning model to improve the critical thinking skills of pre-service teachers with different academic abilities. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(2), 853-864. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.2.853

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA main effect of academic ability (grouped by placement test into upper, middle, lower) on critical thinking skills in the same 107-participant quasi-experiment; LSD corrected means were 61.465 (upper), 57.316 (middle), 54.046 (lower). The article reports the academic ability p-level as 0.000.

> "the corrected mean score of the upper academic ability is 7.23% higher than that of the middle academic ability pre-service teachers, and 13.72% higher than that of the lower academic ability pre-service teachers"
