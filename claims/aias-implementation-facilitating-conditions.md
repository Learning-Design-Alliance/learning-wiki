### Perkins 2026

Perkins, M., Postma, D., Roe, J., Sisay, S., & Holdcroft, C. (2026). 'A bit of chaos and madness': The AI Assessment Scale and the work of assessment reform. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26729 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.26729](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.26729)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 of the hybrid thematic analysis of five focus groups. Staff reported marking pressure made monitoring laborious and questioned how student AI use could be evaluated; the authors note ambiguity "eroded staff confidence" and raised fairness concerns.

> "Institutional environments shaped how staff managed uncertainty around GenAI and the AIAS, particularly regarding policy clarity, workload, and the ability to monitor student use."
