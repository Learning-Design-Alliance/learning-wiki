### Yibo Meng 2026

TibetCPR: A Multimodal Tactile Feedback System to Enhance Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training in High-Altitude Regions of Tibet. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07765 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.07765](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.07765)

Randomised controlled study (T1 pre-test, T2 10-minute intervention, T3 post-test) with 40 lay community members in Tibet; experimental n=20, control n=20. The between-group change-score contrast gave "Cohen's𝑑=− 1.52"; an ANCOVA controlling for T1 mirrored it (F(1,37)=54.64, partial η²=.596).

> "A direct between-group comparison of the change scores ( Δ=𝑇 3−𝑇 1) confirmed that the experimental gain substantially exceeded any change observed in control,𝑡( 38)=− 4.80,𝑝<. 001, Cohen's𝑑=− 1.52"
