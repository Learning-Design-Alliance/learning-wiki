## Evidence

### Yuanyuan Yu 2026

Yuanyuan Yu, Ying Yuan. (2026). Application and effectiveness analysis of AI Intelligent tutoring system combined with knowledge graph in basic medical pathology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09348-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 1 of the cluster-randomized trial reports baseline prerequisite (Human Anatomy and Physiology) scores, mid-term scores (79.8 ± 7.0 vs 71.5 ± 7.3, t = 11.28, P < 0.001), and final scores. Baseline difference was 0.4 points with "P = 0.59".

> "the two groups were comparable at baseline in terms of prerequisite course scores (71.2 ± 6.8 vs. 70.8 ± 7.1, mean difference = 0.4, 95% CI: -1.2 to 2.0, P = 0.59), confirming that any post-intervention differences cannot be attributed to baseline general academic ability."
