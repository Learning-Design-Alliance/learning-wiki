Authors' reflection on environmental requirements drawn from their implementation experience: noise degraded speech recognition and produced inconsistent or inaccurate AI responses, motivating designated simulation spaces.

Authors' noted limitation of the voice-only system: prompt-described nonverbal cues preceded verbal responses, but dynamic facial expressions and gestures were absent, and the authors suggest AI avatars as a future enhancement.

> "While verbal communication was effectively simulated, the lack of dynamic facial expressions and gestures reduced the realism of certain interactions."
