## Evidence

### Feola 2016

Elvia Ilaria Feola. (2016). Digital Literacy and New Technological Perspectives. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2174-2180. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040929

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative R-I-Z-A assessment of the same 162-student one-group e-learning course, comparing competence profiles at the beginning and end of the trial. The article reports "a significant improvement (86%)" in the resources component, keyed to identifying, defining and linking key concepts. The percentage is descriptive.

> "taking into consideration the evaluation of resources, implementing a comparison on the digital skills  of learners at the beginning of the trial and the end, you can highlight a significant improvement (86%), especially as regards the identification of the key concepts, their definition and the linking."

### Feola 2016 (2)

Elvia Ilaria Feola. (2016). Digital Literacy and New Technological Perspectives. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2174-2180. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040929

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interpretation-structures component of the R-I-Z-A evaluation, based on the difference of perceptions students expressed at the beginning and end of the experiment. The article reports that "81% of participants achieved excellent performance levels" across media-text analysis, value judgments, emotion recognition and narration.

> "It was found that 81% of participants achieved excellent performance levels in analyzing a media text, to provide value judgments on the medial relationship, recognizing the private emotions and in narrating the experience."

### Feola 2016 (3)

Elvia Ilaria Feola. (2016). Digital Literacy and New Technological Perspectives. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2174-2180. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040929

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Action-structures component of the R-I-Z-A evaluation, derived from differences in learners' ability to design a media product or apply a storytelling model. The article additionally reports that 72% could discriminate facts and actions from personal opinions and 80% could select relevant information, discarding the superfluous.

> "The 76% of the learners, at the end of the experiment is capable of producing a medial message using the media linguistic rules and evaluate a message."
