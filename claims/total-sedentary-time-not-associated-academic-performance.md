## Evidence

### Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira 2022

Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira, Werneck André de Oliveira, da Silva Danilo Rodrigues Pereira, Oyeyemi Adewale Luqman, Zambrin Lidyane Ferreira, Fernandes Romulo Araújo, Serassuelo Helio Junior, Romanzini Marcelo, Ronque Enio Ricardo Vaz. (2022). Association between patterns of sedentary time and academic performance in adolescents: the mediating role of self-concept. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2022/40/2021106IN

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the regression analysis of the cross-sectional study, total sedentary time was not a significant predictor of academic performance in either sex; the authors state that "while patterns of ST were associated with academic achievement, it was observed that total ST was not". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Interestingly, while patterns of ST were associated with academic achievement, it was observed that total ST was not, which corroborates previous studies."
