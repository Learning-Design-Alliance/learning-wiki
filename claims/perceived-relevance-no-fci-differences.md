## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2020

Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Learning goals and perceived irrelevance to major within life science majors in introductory physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.mason](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.mason)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

T-test comparisons and Cohen's d between Relevant (n = 168) and Irrelevant (n = 50) groups on FCI: pretest d = 0.12 (p = 0.44), posttest d = 0.11 (p = 0.49), gain d = 0.06 (p = 0.70); all F-test equal variance. This null result persists within life science majors and within learning orientations, though d ~ 0.3 favored relevant-perceiving students within Framework and Vague groups descriptively.

> "There are no statistically significant differences on the FCI between pretest scores (which are close to random -chance), post -test scores, or normalized gains."

### Andrew J. Mason 2020

Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Perceived irrelevance and mastery vs. performance achievement goals: Two mindset variables within attitudinal experiences of life science majors in introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of FCI pre/post scores and normalized gains between 50 irrelevant-perceiving and 168 relevant-perceiving students; Table 2 reports small effect sizes (d = 0.12 pretest, 0.11 posttest, 0.06 gains) with non-significant p-values. Subdivisions by major and orientation also showed no significant results.

> "There was no statistical significance between groups on either the pretest or the posttest, and the effect sizes on both the pretest and the protest were weak."
