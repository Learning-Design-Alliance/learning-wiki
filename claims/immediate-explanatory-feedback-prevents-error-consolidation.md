Rodríguez Rincón, Y., Munárriz, A., Campión Arrastia, M. J., & Goicoechea López-Vailo, M. I. (2025). Instructional design for tutoring on interactive platforms: creating educational interventions overcoming the digital gap. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10516-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

In the discussion, the article argues that explanatory feedback provided immediately — a characteristic Moreno and Mayer (2007) highlight of interactive virtual tutoring platforms — prevents error consolidation and supports subsequent exercises with correct reasoning structures.

> "This allows the student to avoid the consolidation of erroneous concepts and, in turn, to be able to work on the following exercises with the appropriate reasoning structures that the feedback has provided, making this strategy an effective resource, as recommended by Gros Salvat and Cano García (2021)."
