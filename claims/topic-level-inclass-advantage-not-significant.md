## Evidence

### Arponen Heidi 2026

Arponen Heidi, Juuri Emma. (2026). Student Preference on Teaching Mode and the Impact of Remote Teaching on Academic Performance in Undergraduate Orthodontics Course, a Follow‐Up Study. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13995

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Topic-level analysis (n = 19 topics) in the Results section of the cohort of 51 students. The authors report a printed correlation effect size of rs = −0.492 (p = 0.032) for the topic association, while the group comparison "did not reach statistical significance (U = 42.5, p = 0.842)".

> "The number of students with a correct answer to both questions on each of the 19 topics was higher in those topics taught in‐class as compared to the topics taught remotely (r s = −0.492, p = 0.032, n = 19), indicating a meaningful association although the difference did not reach statistical significance (U = 42.5, p = 0.842)."
