Authors' retrospective assessment in the Discussion of the 2019 course: each student developed a poster with a project idea, and participants and tutors examined the posters and provided questions or suggestions via post-it notes; participants were free to take or reject suggestions.

> "The projects all improved signiﬁcantly after the execution of this activity."
