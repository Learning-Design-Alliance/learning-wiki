Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2009). Analyzing Problem Solving Using Math in Physics: Epistemological Framing via Warrants. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.5.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.5.020108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Observational report from the video data set (Section IV.E): new information from classmates, interviewers, or the student's own associations can trigger reframing, so framings vary widely in duration. Durations are as printed; no effect size is reported.

> "As a result, the epistemological framings observed in these students' work can extend over a range of time periods. We have found examples in our data set ranging from ten seconds to ten minues."
