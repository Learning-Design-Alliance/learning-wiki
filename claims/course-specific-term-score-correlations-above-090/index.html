q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of observed RIT scores and true score model estimates (Table 3.2) for Algebra 1, Geometry, and Algebra 2. Printed correlation coefficients (r) include r = 0.91 (Algebra 1 fall–winter, true score model) versus r = 0.86 observed, with model-based coefficients "above 0.90 for almost all tests" and corrected for attenuation.

"the Pearson correlation coefficients computed based on the estimates from the true score models are above 0.90 for almost all tests, suggesting that scores from each administration were strongly correlated"
