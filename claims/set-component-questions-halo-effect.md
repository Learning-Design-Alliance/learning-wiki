## Evidence

### Gilbert R. O. 2025

Gilbert R. O., Gilbert D. R. (2025). Student evaluations of teaching do not reflect student learning: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06896-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Component-question correlation analysis within the same 160-course dataset; the authors interpret the high intercorrelations as suggesting "a mindless completion of the evaluation once a student had formed an overall opinion of the course ("halo / horn effect")".

> "Notably, responses to all component questions of the student evaluation were significantly correlated to each other. Spearman correlation coefficients ranged from 0.63 to 0.95, and for all P < 0.0001."
