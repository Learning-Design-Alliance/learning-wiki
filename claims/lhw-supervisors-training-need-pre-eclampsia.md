## Evidence

### Salam Rehana A. 2016

Salam Rehana A., Qureshi Rahat Najam, Sheikh Sana, Khowaja Asif Raza, Sawchuck Diane, Vidler Marianne, von Dadelszen Peter, Zaidi Shujaat, Bhutta Zulfiqar, the CLIP working group. (2016). Reproductive Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12978-016-0214-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from questionnaires and focus group discussions with LHW supervisors and traditional birth attendants, reported in the Results section on gaps in training. Supervisors "recognised the need for training, with a focus on identifying woman at high-risk" and noted pre-eclampsia and eclampsia "had received negligible attention during their training", while "very few TBAs perceived the need to learn about pre-eclampsia and eclampsia".

> "Many LHSs recognised the need for training, with a focus on identifying woman at high-risk. Also, they claimed that pre-eclampsia and eclampsia are very important subjects; however, they had received negligible attention during their training. In contrast, very few TBAs perceived the need to learn about pre-eclampsia and eclampsia."
