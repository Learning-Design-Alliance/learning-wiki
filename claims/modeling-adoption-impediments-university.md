## Evidence

### Eric Brewe 2006

Eric Brewe. (2006). Modeling theory applied; modeling instruction in university physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0602086](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0602086)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The author's interpretive assessment of barriers to adoption, offered without empirical data. The article states that with the exception of Chabay and Sherwood's Matter and Interaction series, "textbooks actually hinder instructors' abilities to run a model-centered physics course by ignoring the role of models and omitting extensive use of multiple representations," and that "Currently, a complete set of such activities does not exist."

> "This is not the only difficulty with adopting modeling, however, as there are not significant resources available to facilitate adoption at the university level.  The high-school modeling curriculum is valuable, but is inadequate for a university-level modeling course."
