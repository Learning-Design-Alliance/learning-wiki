Evidence¶

Melodie Baker 2023¶

Melodie Baker, Ivy Morgan, and Gizella Wade. (2023). Opportunities Denied: High-Achieving Black and Latino Students Lack Access to Advanced Math. EdTrust and Just Equations. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641831

q2 · i? · associational · r2

HSLS:09 survey analysis of ninth-grade math teacher practices among high-achieving students in public schools offering advanced math. Endnotes state all goals-clarity differences are statistically significant (chi-square test, p<.05); Figures 4A–4C show emphasis differences, some significant at .05 or .1 levels.

"nearly 100% of students who took advanced math courses (of each group of interest — Black and Latino students and students from low-SES backgrounds) had math teachers who agreed or strongly agreed that math teachers make goals clear to students, compared with 92% of Black students, 95% of Latino students, and 94% of students from low-SES backgrounds who did not take advanced math courses."
