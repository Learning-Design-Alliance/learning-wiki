The review reports, citing Tian and Zhang (2025), that AI dependence was "negatively associated with critical thinking partly through cognitive fatigue," with information literacy buffering the association but intensifying fatigue at high dependence. This is presented as evidence for the substitution side of the controversy.

"AI dependence was negatively associated with critical thinking partly through cognitive fatigue; information literacy buff- ered the critical-thinking association but, at high dependence, intensi- fied fatigue"
