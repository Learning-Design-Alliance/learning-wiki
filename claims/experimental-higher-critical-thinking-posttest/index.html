Evidence¶

Chaipichit et al. 2015¶

Dudduan Chaipichit, Nirat Jantharajit and Sumalee Chookhampaeng. (2015). Development of learning management model based on constructivist theory and reasoning strategies for enhancing the critical thinking of secondary students. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2193

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Cluster-randomized comparison of two grade 9 classrooms: the experimental group (n = 33) taught with the developed model and the control group (n = 30) taught customary instruction. The experimental group's post-test mean was 46.48 versus 34.43 for the control group, with t = 9.418, p = .000.

"According to Table 2 and Figure 3, the experimental group had a higher mean score on the post-test than the control group at .05 level of significance."
