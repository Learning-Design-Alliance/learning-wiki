## Evidence

### Wodtke 2011

Wodtke, Geoffrey T.; Harding, David J.; Elwert, Felix. (2011). Neighborhood Effects in Temporal Perspective. American Sociological Review. https://doi.org/10.1177/0003122411420816

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of moves between quintiles of the composite disadvantage index in the PSID cohort (Table 3, lower panel). The authors state these data "reveal frequent neighborhood mobility," demonstrating the need for IPT-weighted estimation.

> "Only 37.53% of blacks and 16.52% of nonblacks never moved between neighborhood quintiles throughout the early life course, whereas 29.86% and 44.24% of blacks and nonblacks, respectively, moved between different neighborhood contexts at least three times."
