# Latent profile analysis identifies four AI-context learner subgroups — Active Adopters, Cognitively Advanced but Constrained, Passive Followers, and Disengaged — with markedly different self-directed learning levels

## Evidence

### Li Chenrui 2026

Li Chenrui, Hu Fan, Qiao Yongxia, Zhang Meihui, Gao Quyige, Wang Fengyi, Du Naiya, Xia Yunyi, Pan Meizhu, Cai Yong, Wang Ying. (2026). The role of AI-mediated learning contexts in the personality–self-directed learning relationship among Chinese medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09414-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis (mclust, 1–6 profile solutions) on AI cognition and subjective norms in the cross-sectional survey. "Active Adopters (16.5%) scored high on both dimensions" with the highest SDL (M = 145.2); Passive Followers were 77.2% (M = 116.1); Disengaged were 1.9% (M = 67.6).

> "Active Adopters (16.5%) scored high on both dimensions (z = 1.23 and 1.38) and exhibited the highest SDL levels (M = 145.2). Cognitively Advanced but Constrained (4.5%) had above-average AI cognition (z = 0.40) but very low subjective norms (z = − 2.18), with intermediate SDL (M = 132.7)."
