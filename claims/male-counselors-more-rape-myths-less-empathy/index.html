Evidence¶

Litam 2019¶

Litam, S. D. A. (2019). She's Just a Prostitute: The Effects of Labels on Counselor Attitudes, Empathy, and Rape Myth Acceptance. The Professional Counselor, 9(4), 396-415. https://doi.org/10.15241/sdal.9.4.396

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate correlational analysis in the full sample relating counselor gender to rape myth acceptance; the negative correlation reflects higher acceptance among males given scoring direction.

"Gender and the IRMA-SF were significantly correlated (r = -.269, p < 01), with counselor gender significantly correlating with 19 out of 22 items (86%) on the IRMA-SF. Based on these results, male counselors were more likely to accept rape myths compared to female counselors."

Litam 2019 (2)¶

Litam, S. D. A. (2019). She's Just a Prostitute: The Effects of Labels on Counselor Attitudes, Empathy, and Rape Myth Acceptance. The Professional Counselor, 9(4), 396-415. https://doi.org/10.15241/sdal.9.4.396

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate correlations between gender and empathy subscales; the article interprets that female counselors reported greater perspective taking, affective response, and overall empathy than males.

"On the EAI, gender was significantly correlated to the Perspective Taking (r = .161, p = < .01) and Affective Response (r = .142, p < .01) subscales, in addition to the overall EAI measure (r = .112, p < .05)."
