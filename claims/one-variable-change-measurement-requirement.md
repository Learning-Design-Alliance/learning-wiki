Design-rule argument from Section 5.4, grounded in simulation failures where probes made a second change (the Moon, outer space, ice under the bus) and "blurred which concept was being tested". This is an analytical design argument, not a measured comparison.

> "A probe is an experiment, and an experiment that changes two variables at once cannot say which one caused the result."
