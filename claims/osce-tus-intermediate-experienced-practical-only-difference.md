## Evidence

### Pietersen Pia Iben 2023

Pietersen Pia Iben, Bhatnagar Rahul, Andreasen Freja, Konge Lars, Laursen Christian Borbjerg, Rahman Najib, Nielsen Anders Bo. (2023). Objective structured clinical examination in basic thoracic ultrasound: a European study of validity evidence. BMC Pulmonary Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-022-02285-4

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent t-tests between intermediates and experienced in the 25-candidate validation study. The practical difference was "significantly different, p = 0.009" and overall "p = 0.005"; the theoretical difference (20.5 vs. 23.5) was not significant, p = 0.64.

> "the difference in the practical mean scores (58.5 vs. 65.5 points) was 6.9, 95% CI (− 12.0, − 1.9) which was significantly different, p = 0.009, and the difference in overall test score (79.1 vs. 89.0 points) was 9.9, 95% CI (− 16.6, − 3.4) which was also significantly different, p = 0.005."
