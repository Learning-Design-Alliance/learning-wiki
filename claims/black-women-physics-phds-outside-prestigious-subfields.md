The paper reports, citing Prescod-Weinstein (2020) and Valentine-Miller (2021), that 'the majority of these degrees were in areas that are not on the desirable side of prestige asymmetry' among the fewer than two hundred Black American women holding physics PhDs. No effect size is printed.

> "of the less-than two hundred black American women to complete a PhD in Physics, the majority of these degrees were in areas that are not on the desirable side of prestige asymmetry (Prescod-Weinstein, 2020, p. 425; Valentine-Miller, 2021)."
