The Superkids Reading Program 2009¶

The Superkids Reading Program. (2009). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/the-superkids-reading-program/

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Summary of two large matched comparison studies in kindergarten, both finding "significant positive outcomes on measures of sound and word reading." The article prints the average effect size compared to controls as +0.23 (a small effect, d = 0.23), qualifying the program for the Moderate category.

"Two large matched studies have evaluated The Superkids Reading Program in kindergarten. Both found significant positive outcomes on measures of sound and word reading. The average effect size compared to controls was +0.23"
