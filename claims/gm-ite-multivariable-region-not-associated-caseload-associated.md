## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Region of graduation was not significantly associated with GM-ITE total score (Non-Japan Asia p = 0.408; Europe and Others p = 0.455), whereas PGY-2 status (p = 0.011), six or more night shifts per month (p = 0.047), and ten or more assigned inpatients (p < 0.001 to p = 0.013) were associated with higher scores.
