## Evidence

### Perry 2026

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional structural equation model of age 16 GCSE attainment in 1,673 SCAMP adolescents, language status explaining 1% of variance. "SM effects on age 16 attainment were small and positive" and the effect grew to β = .19 with SES and ethnicity controls.

> "SM effects on age 16 attainment were small and positive (ϐ = .10, p < .001), whilst ML effects were non-significant and close to zero (ϐ = -.02, p = .434)."

### Perry 2026 (2)

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional SEM of age 11 SATs attainment in 1,673 SCAMP adolescents; the unadjusted SM effect versus monolingual was non-significant, but the effect was "apparent in the adjusted model controlling for ethnicity and SES" with β = .11, p = .003.

> "SM effects on age 11 attainment were, however, apparent in the adjusted model controlling for ethnicity and SES (ϐ = .11, p = .003)."
