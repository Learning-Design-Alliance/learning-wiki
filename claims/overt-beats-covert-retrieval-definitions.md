# Overt retrieval practice produces higher delayed recall of definitions than covert retrieval practice for learners studying declarative concepts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2`–`r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 1, criterion-test recall of definitions was higher after overt retrieval than after covert retrieval (gav = 0.47). [→ Michelle L. Rivers 2025](#michelle-l-rivers-2025)
`q3 i2` In Experiment 2, the overt advantage for definitions replicated (gav = 0.44). [→ Michelle L. Rivers 2025 (2)](#michelle-l-rivers-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Michelle L. Rivers 2025

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 1 (98 undergraduates, 3 practice modalities within-participants, criterion test two days later), recall of definitions was higher after overt retrieval (M = 0.41) than covert retrieval, with printed effect size gav = 0.47. Overt also beat restudy, and covert beat restudy.

> "Recall was also higher following overt retrieval compared to covert retrieval (M = 0.41, SE = 0.03), t(51) = 3.78, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.04, 0.14], g av = 0.47."

### Michelle L. Rivers 2025 (2)

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

In the high-powered Experiment 2 (382 undergraduates), for participants who practiced and were tested on definitions, overt retrieval (M = 0.43) beat covert retrieval with printed effect size gav = 0.44; covert also beat restudy (gav = 0.16).

> "Recall was also higher following overt retrieval compared to covert retrieval (M = 0.43, SE = 0.02), t(100) = 5.66, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.06, 0.14], g av = 0.44."
