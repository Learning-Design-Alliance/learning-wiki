## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Prompting self-explanation improves transfer of general patterns to new tasks but reduces recall of item details, as shown in preschool causal-reasoning and adult category-learning experiments. [→ Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr 2017](#bethany-rittle-johnson-abbey-m-loehr-2017)
`q2 i?` Self-explanation prompts caused learners to overlook exceptions to probabilistic category rules, applying the general rule even to conflicting instances. [→ Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr 2017 (2)](#bethany-rittle-johnson-abbey-m-loehr-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr 2017

Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr. (2017). Eliciting explanations: Constraints on when self-explanation aids learning. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 24, 1501–1510. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-016-1079-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the Legare & Lombrozo (2014) experiment in which preschool children were prompted to self-explain on a causal reasoning task or observed without prompting. Self-explainers showed "poorer recall of details" alongside better transfer of the causal mechanism.

> "Children in the self-explanation condition had poorer recall of details, such as the color of the objects, but better transfer of the causal mechanism to a new task."

### Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr 2017 (2)

Bethany Rittle-Johnson & Abbey M. Loehr. (2017). Eliciting explanations: Constraints on when self-explanation aids learning. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 24, 1501–1510. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-016-1079-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Williams et al. (2013) college-student experiments on learning categories of artifacts and person characteristics. Prompted explainers "had more difficulty learning to correctly categorize examples" violating the general rule and were more likely to ignore exceptions.

> "People who were prompted to self-explain, rather than think aloud or prepare to explain later, had more difficulty learning to correctly categorize examples that did not follow the general category rule"
