# Learners in both sequencing conditions predominantly preferred and were satisfied with DI-PS sequencing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 80% of the DI-PS group and 83% of the PS-DI group stated they (would have) preferred DI-PS, and the DI-PS group reported higher retrospective satisfaction with the instructional design. [→ Sabrina Ludwig 2026](#sabrina-ludwig-2026)

## Evidence

### Sabrina Ludwig 2026

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instructional preference measured with a 13-item scale (Cronbach's alpha .74) in the initial questionnaire and a single retrospective satisfaction item in the final questionnaire. Both groups "show an instructional preference towards DI-PS" (M 2.61 vs 2.72, p = .31, d = .11).

> "In line with the instructional preferences surveyed at the beginning, 80 % of the DI-PS group and 83 % of the PS-DI group stated that they (would have) preferred DI-PS."
