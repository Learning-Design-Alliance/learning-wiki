## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kontur 2014

Kontur, F. J. (2014). Writing Science Fiction Stories to Motivate Analysis of Journal Articles. mailto:frederick.kontur@usafa.edu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.2435](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.2435)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Administration of the standardized Colorado Upper-Division Electrostatics diagnostic at the start of fall 2012. The pre-test score of 24% is compared descriptively to published norms; the author attributes the low score to scheduling gaps at USAFA.

> "On the pre-test, they scored 24%, which is 6-19% below pre-test scores for students in equivalent courses."

### Kontur 2014 (2)

Kontur, F. J. (2014). Writing Science Fiction Stories to Motivate Analysis of Journal Articles. mailto:frederick.kontur@usafa.edu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.2435](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.2435)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-semester CUE administration in the same fall 2012 class. The post-test score of 51% matches the published average for comparable courses, which the author offers as descriptive evidence against harm; no statistical test is reported.

> "Nevertheless, despite their low pre-test scores, they scored 51% on the post-test, which is equal to the average post-test score for upper-division E&M students at other institutions."
