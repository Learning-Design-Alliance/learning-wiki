q2 · i? · review · r2

Review analysis of intervention outcomes by Kirkpatrick level across the included studies, finding no study reached Levels 3 or 4. The quote states "nine out of ten studies" reached Level 2 in the review's own words.

"the highest level achieved among the reviewed studies was Level 2, reported in nine out of ten studies, which assessed knowledge, skills, and attitudes [31–40]. The remaining study reached only Level 1"
