## Evidence

### Turkmen 2015

Turkmen, H. & Buyukaltay, D. (2015). Which one is better? Jigsaw II versus Jigsaw IV on the subject of the building blocks of matter and atom. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 1(2), 88-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105340

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-group t-test on the 24-student experiment group taught 'Atoms - the Basic Unit of Matter' with Jigsaw IV; pre-test mean 5.45 (SD 2.39), post-test mean 7.12 (SD 2.36), t = -3.646, p = 0.001. The article reports "This difference occurred in favor of the post -test".

> "A statistically significance difference at level of 0.05 was found in the dependent group t-test carried out for achievement pre-test and post-test of the experiment group. This difference occurred in favor of the post -test."
