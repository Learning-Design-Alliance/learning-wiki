`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 8 semi-structured interviews (40–60 minutes) with physical therapist practice educators and tutors at 5 hospital sites across Ireland, conducted October 2021–February 2022. The analysis "revealed that simulation supported the transition to practice in 3 ways" as the study's overarching thematic structure.

> "Our analysis revealed that simulation supported the transition to practice in 3 ways: (1) priming for clinical environments, (2) enhancing feedback literacy in the workplace, and (3) tackling complexity of clinical practice."
