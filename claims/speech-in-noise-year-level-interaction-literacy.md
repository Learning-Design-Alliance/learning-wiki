## Evidence

### Tomlin D 2025

Tomlin D, Bowers P, Zonca B, Graydon K, Phillips J, Rance G. (2025). Speech-in-noise recognition as a predictor of academic achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324998

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mixed effects regression (Model 1) on 108 children's NAPLAN scores, with school as random effect and year level as fixed effect. Standardized interaction effect sizes were 0.483 (reading, p = 0.022), 0.614 (grammar/punctuation, p = 0.0021) and 0.490 (writing, p = 0.017); spelling was marginal at 0.367 (p = 0.06). The study reports "a significant interaction with multiple NAPLAN metrics".

> "Speech-in-noise performance showed a significant interaction with multiple NAPLAN metrics (reading, grammar/punctuation and writing), and this interaction term showed a marginal effect for spelling."
