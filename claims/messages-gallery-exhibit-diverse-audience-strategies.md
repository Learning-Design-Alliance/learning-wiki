The article's summary section describes an education and outreach project report of its three named strategies for a gallery exhibit. It states the team sought "to reach a diverse audience by employing three strategies", the third being bilingual communication with a science communicator.

> "We leveraged existing partnerships to reach a diverse audience by employing three strategies. First, we explored both the science and the people who do it from the perspective of two accomplished, international artists. Second, we involved teens from underrepresented groups to develop ownership, and engage in a meaningful mentoring experience."
