## Evidence

### Özgül Balcı 2017

Özgül Balcı. (2017). The Effects of Learning-Style Based Activities on Students' Reading Comprehension Skills and Self-Efficacy Perceptions in English Foreign Language Classes. Higher Education Studies, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n4p35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA on post-test total Self-Efficacy Scale for English scores, controlling for pre-test, in the same 78-student matching-only quasi-experiment. Adjusted means were 101.36 (experimental) versus 92.62 (control); F(1,75)=8.77, p<0.05.

> "As Table 7 shows, the ANCOVA result indicates that English self-efficacy of the experimental group was significantly higher than that of the control group [F(1,75)=8.77, p<0.05]."
