## Evidence

### Zachariae R 2015

Zachariae R, O'Connor M, Lassesen B, Olesen M, Kjær LB, Thygesen M, Mørcke AM. (2015). The self-efficacy in patient-centeredness questionnaire – a new measure of medical student and physician confidence in exhibiting patient-centered behaviors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0427-x

`q2 · i0` · `design · r3`

Cross-sectional comparison of the physician convenience sample (Sample 5, N = 101, response rate 44%) with student samples, plus a DMA-course comparison within physicians. The article reports Cohen's d of "0.55, 0.45, 0.48" for Factors 1, 2, and Total, and d = 0.19 for Factor 3, a negligible effect size.

> "Physicians exhibited statistically significantly higher scores than medical students on all SEPCQ scores (p < 0.001) (data not shown), and physicians who had participated in the DMA communication course had statistically significant higher scores on Factor 1, 2, and SEPCQ total (Cohen's d: 0.55, 0.45, 0.48; p: 0.007, 0.03, 0.02), but not Factor 3 (d = 0.19; p = 0.30)"
