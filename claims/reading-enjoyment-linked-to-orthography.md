## Evidence

### Miccoli 2023

Miccoli, L., Arias, M. Á. P., & Santiago, J. (2023). A network analysis on digital media use, reading enjoyment, and orthography precision in a highly educated sample. Computers & Education, 207, 104932. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104932

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the abstract's summary of the network findings in 840 adults: books read per year was "strongly associated with subjective reading enjoyment and devoting free time to reading". The three reading enjoyment measures intercorrelated >.63.

> "As the latter was also strongly associated with subjective reading enjoyment and devoting free time to reading, the findings therefore also indicate that greater reading pleasure significantly contributes to better written language skills."
