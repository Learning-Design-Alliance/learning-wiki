Thalluri 2014¶

Thalluri, Jyothi; O'Flaherty, Jackie A.; and Shepherd, Paul L. (2014). Classmate peer-coaching: "A Study Buddy Support scheme". Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 92-104. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/8

Qualitative analysis of open-ended evaluation responses from Buddies, who cited being able to ask questions in a comfortable environment and knowing they were not alone. Comments were analysed manually by the authors.
