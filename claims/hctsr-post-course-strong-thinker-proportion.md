### Singh Veena Kumari 2025

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course-only HCTSR ratings by two trained faculty evaluators of a written essay and a verbal case-scenario presentation, averaged. The article reports "69.2% (n = 36) of PGs demonstrated strong critical thinking ability with a score of 4"; no pre-course HCTSR baseline was collected.

> "Notably, 69.2% (n = 36) of PGs demonstrated strong critical thinking ability with a score of 4. An acceptable critical thinker score of 3 was given to 11.5% (n = 6) of participants, while 11.5% (n = 6) were classified as unacceptable critical thinkers with a score of 2."
