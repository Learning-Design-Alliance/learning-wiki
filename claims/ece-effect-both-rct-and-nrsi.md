## Evidence

### Pang Xuefei 2026

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Design-based subgroup analysis within the same random-effects meta-analysis. Both design types showed significant positive pooled effects, with very high heterogeneity in each subgroup (I² = 90.9% and 97.8%).

> "Results indicated a generally positive trend of ECE in both RCTs (SMD = 1.06, 95% CI = 0.53-1.58, I² = 90.9%, P < .001) and non-RCTs (SMD = 1.37, 95% CI = 0.78-1.97, I² = 97.8%, P < .001)"
