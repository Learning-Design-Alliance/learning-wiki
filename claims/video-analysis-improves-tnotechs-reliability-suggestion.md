Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, offered as a possible explanation for their higher ICCs relative to Steinemann et al.'s live-observer study; they also cite that study's simulated-resuscitation results (ICC 0.44 live vs 0.71 video) as further support. This is an untested suggestion, not a result of the present study.
