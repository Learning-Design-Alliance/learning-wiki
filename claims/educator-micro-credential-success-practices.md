`q1 i?` Digital Promise reports that successful educator use of micro-credentials focuses on competency-based micro-credentials requiring a portfolio of evidence and a rigorous assessment and feedback process, educator autonomy in choosing which micro-credentials to pursue, incentives such as stipends and advancement pathways, and recognition toward licensure, recertification, and professional learning hours.
