## Evidence

### Nicola Brocca 2026

Nicola Brocca, Davide Garassino. (2026). LLMs in text linguistics teaching: an exploratory study with genAI novices in higher education. Computers and Education Open 11 (2026) 100414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100414

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' discussion of Dataset 1 (five pair reports) interprets the discontinuation of refinement as overgeneralization, noting previous research indicates LLMs generally outperform human novices on anaphora detection, so the students' conclusion underestimated model capabilities.

> "When the revised prompts did not yield better results, the students concluded that the LLMs were unable to detect anaphors and discontinued further prompt refinement. This conclusion appears to reflect an overgeneralization from their own prompting experience."

### Nicola Brocca 2026 (2)

Nicola Brocca, Davide Garassino. (2026). LLMs in text linguistics teaching: an exploratory study with genAI novices in higher education. Computers and Education Open 11 (2026) 100414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100414

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of the eight end-of-term reflections (Dataset 2, approximately 2800 words) found that in output evaluation, "only one student attributed the lack of satisfactory results to weaknesses in their prompt strategy".

> "Interestingly, only one student attributed the lack of satisfactory results to weaknesses in their prompt strategy"
