## Evidence

### Bilas Paul 2026

Bilas Paul. (2026). Unveiling Gender Dynamics in Introductory Physics Labs. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae2f14](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae2f14)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-lab survey in which students ranked four core tasks; chi-square test of independence on aggregated first and second rankings by gender. The article reports male students gravitating toward "hands-on and procedural tasks" and female students toward documentation and data analysis. No effect size printed.

> "The analysis revealed a statistically significant association between gender and laboratory task preference, [χ 2(df= 3) = 9.548, p= 0.023, α= 0.05]."
