Ahmet Basal, Selahattin Yilmaz, Asli Tanriverdi, & Lutfiye Sari. (2016). Effectiveness of Mobile Applications in Vocabulary Teaching. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(1), 47-59. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/cet

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Narrative literature review attributing this pattern to Lu (2008) and Zhang et al. (2011), who compared SMS vocabulary instruction with printed materials. The review reports posttest advantages for SMS groups but "no significant difference in the delayed posttests"; the reviewed studies' designs are not described here in detail.

> "Results of the ir studies showed that SMS groups outperformed the control groups who were given printed materials in the posttest;  however, there was no significant difference in the delayed posttests."
