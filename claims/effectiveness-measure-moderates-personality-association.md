# Associations between teacher personality and effectiveness are strongest for evaluations of teaching and near zero for academic achievement and classroom observation

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lisa E. Kim 2019

Lisa E. Kim, Verena Jörg & Robert M. Klassen. (2019). A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Teacher Personality on Teacher Effectiveness and Burnout. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9458-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis reported in Table 2 of the random-effects meta-analysis: for extraversion, the evaluations-of-teaching subgroup pools k = 10 effect sizes at r = 0.32 (95% CI 0.10–0.50), with high heterogeneity (I² = 95.48%). Comparable AA and CO subgroups pool near-zero effects (e.g., extraversion AA r = 0.10, CO r = 0.01).
