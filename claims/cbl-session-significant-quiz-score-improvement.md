## Evidence

### Merritt E. 2024

Merritt E., McNulty M.A., Byram J.N. (2024). Integrated Case-Based Learning Session for Breast and Upper Limb Anatomy. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11399

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post quiz comparison using a Wilcoxon signed rank test among 51 prematriculation students. Averages rose from 57% on the presession quiz to 69% on the postsession quiz, and "Students performed significantly better on the postsession quiz" with p < .001. No effect size was reported.

> "Students performed significantly better on the postsession quiz, with the averages at 57% and 69%, respectively (p < .001)."
