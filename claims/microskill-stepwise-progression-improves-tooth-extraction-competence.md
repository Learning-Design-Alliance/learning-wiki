## Evidence

### Wu Zongxi 2026

Wu Zongxi, Wang Mingyue, Luo Xueting, Liu Xiangqi, Yang Le, Gao Siyong, Zhang Sien, Zheng Guangsen. (2026). Structured Microskill Stepwise Progression for Undergraduate Tooth Extraction: A Randomized Mixed-Methods Study. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.111097

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pilot study of 40 fifth-year dental students allocated 1:1 during an 8-week oral surgery rotation. End-of-rotation competence was assessed using a modified DOPS scale by two blinded examiners. The intervention group scored higher, with

> "The mean modified DOPS score was 94.60 ± 3.97 in the experimental group and 88.25 ± 6.41 in the control group. Welch's independent-samples t test showed a significant between-group difference, with a mean difference of 6.35 points (95% CI, 2.91-9.79; P < .001; Cohen's d = 1.19)"
