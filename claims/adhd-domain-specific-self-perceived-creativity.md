## Evidence

### Boot Nathalie 2017

Boot Nathalie, Nevicka Barbara, Baas Matthijs. (2017). Creativity in ADHD: Goal-Directed Motivation and Domain Specificity. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054717727352

`q3 · i3`

Study 2 multivariate ANOVA on the Kaufman Domains of Creativity Scale (50 items, five domains) comparing 46 adults with ADHD and 44 controls. The omnibus test was significant (Wilk's Λ = 0.83, ηp2 = .18), with the ADHD group higher in the performance and mechanical/scientific domains.

> "After correction for multiple comparisons (adjusted α = .01), we found significant group differences in the performance, F(1, 88) = 8.72, p = .004, ηp2 = .09; and mechanical/scientific domain, F(1, 88) = 8.50, p = .005, ηp2 = .09."

### Boot Nathalie 2017 (2)

Boot Nathalie, Nevicka Barbara, Baas Matthijs. (2017). Creativity in ADHD: Goal-Directed Motivation and Domain Specificity. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054717727352

`q3 · i?`

Same Study 2 K-DOCS analysis. The article reports no group differences in the everyday, scholarly, or artistic domains; the reliability of these subscales was suboptimal, which the authors note may have influenced the results.

> "No group differences were observed in the everyday, F(1, 88) = 0.54, p = .464, ηp2 = .01; scholarly, F(1, 88) = .59, p = .443, ηp2 = .01; and artistic domains, F(1, 88) = 2.82, p = .097, ηp2 = .03."
