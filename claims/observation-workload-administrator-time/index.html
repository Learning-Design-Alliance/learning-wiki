q2 · i? · associational · r2

Spring 2013 administrator surveys. Based on reported time and observation counts, "the typical elementary school administrator spent approximately 120 hours—or two full weeks—solely on observations," while the typical high school administrator spent approximately three full weeks.

"Administrators reported spending about six hours per formal observation cycle, including the observation, pre- and post-observation conferences, and data management."
