## Evidence

### Frey 2015

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Feil, E. G., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., & Forness, S. (2015). First Step to Success: Applications to Preschoolers at Risk of Developing Autism Spectrum Disorders. Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397-407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581234

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Responder analysis using Jacobson and Truax's Reliable Change Index and movement into the normative range on the SSiS Autism subscale in the randomized subsample. The article reports the parent-report contrast as not statistically significant; no children displayed deterioration.

> "Overall, based on parent report, ﬁve intervention children (38%) recovered or improved as compared to one (7%) comparison child"

### Frey 2015 (2)

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Feil, E. G., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., & Forness, S. (2015). First Step to Success: Applications to Preschoolers at Risk of Developing Autism Spectrum Disorders. Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397-407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581234

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teacher-report responder analysis in the same subsample using the Reliable Change Index and normative-range movement on the SSiS Autism subscale. The article reports this contrast as not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "Thus, based on teacher report, six (46%) children from the interven- tion condition recovered or improved as com- pared to four (31%) children in the control condition"
