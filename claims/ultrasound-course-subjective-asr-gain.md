## Evidence

### Weimer Johannes 2024

Weimer Johannes, Ruppert Johannes, Vieth Thomas, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Buggenhagen Holger, Künzel Julian, Rink Maximilian, Lorenz Liv, Merkel Daniel, Ille Carlotta, Yang Yang, Müller Lukas, Kloeckner Roman, Weimer Andreas. (2024). Effects of undergraduate ultrasound education on cross-sectional image understanding and visual-spatial ability - a prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05608-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Seven-level Likert self-assessment questionnaires at T1 and T2 in the same prospective observational study (n = 141). The article reports the ASR increase as significant (p = 0.009) and the overall subjective increase as not significant; no effect size is printed.

> "A subjective increase in competency was recorded in the overall score, but without statistical significance. The largest, significant increase in the competencies surveyed was achieved for ASR (p = 0.009)."
