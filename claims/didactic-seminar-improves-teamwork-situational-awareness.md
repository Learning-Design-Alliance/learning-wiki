`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hagemann et al. (2017), an examined-scenario study comparing a single didactic NTS seminar to a control group of medical students; the "NTS group significantly improved their teamwork and situational awareness", but patient outcome did not differ significantly. No effect size is printed.

> "The NTS group significantly improved their teamwork and situational awareness, as well as “handling errors”. However, both groups saw a reduction in stress and better decision making. Medical endpoints and patient outcome did not differ significantly between groups"
