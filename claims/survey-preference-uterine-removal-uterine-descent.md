## Evidence

### Rotem Reut 2024

Rotem Reut, Carey Michael O., McCarthy Claire M., O'Reilly Barry A., Daykan Yair, O'Sullivan Orfhlaith E. (2024). Preserving Essential Skills: The Future of Vaginal Hysterectomy Training in Urogynaecology. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17974

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional 2019 email survey of IUGA and EUGA members (471 respondents, mostly urogynaecologists) analysed with descriptive statistics. The survey found "297 (63%) respondents highlighted a preference for uterine removal in the presence of uterine descent", with patient preference (362, 77%) the leading factor.

> "In total, 297 (63%) respondents highlighted a preference for uterine removal in the presence of uterine descent. The main factors influencing decisions were patient preference (362, 77%), patient age (259, 55%), and prolapse score (198, 42%)."

> "Additionally, 118 (25%) indicated that surgical training impacted their decision‐making regarding hysterectomy, while 157 (33%) were influenced by recurrence rates, which varied across different surgical methods."
