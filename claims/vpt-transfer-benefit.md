## Evidence

### Wickens 2013

Wickens, C. D., Hutchins, S., Carolan, T., & Cumming, J. (2013). Effectiveness of Part-Task Training and Increasing-Difficulty Training Strategies: A Meta-Analysis Approach. Human Factors, 55(2), 461-470. https://doi.org/10.1177/0018720812451994

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of VPT conditions evaluated against fixed-difficulty whole-task control using both measures. The article reports "a substantial benefit was observed (TR = 1.27*, k† = 12; g = +0.74*, k† = 7)"; the printed effect size g = +0.74 is medium.

> "However, when the effectiveness of VPT was evaluated in its own right (compared with fixed-difficulty control), a substantial benefit was observed (TR = 1.27*, k† = 12; g = +0.74*, k† = 7)."
