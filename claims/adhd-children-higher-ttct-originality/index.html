Gonzalez-Carpio 2017¶

Gonzalez-Carpio, G., Serrano, J. P., & Nieto, M. (2017). Creativity in Children with Attention Déficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). Psychology, 8, 319-334. https://doi.org/10.4236/psych.2017.83019

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

One-factor ANOVA on TTCT-Figurative Originality in the same 34-versus-34 comparison; the ADHD group's mean was "116.5 ± 13.11" versus 103.5 ± 13.96 for controls, F = 15.450, p = 0.000, partial η² = 0.190 (large by the article's stated threshold of 0.14).
