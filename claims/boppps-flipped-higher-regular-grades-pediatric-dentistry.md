### Qiu Xili 2026

Qiu Xili, Li Tongjun, Li Simin, He Yuqing, Li Fenghua, Liu Jing, Yu Yang. (2026). Integration of BOPPPS and flipped classroom teaching models for enhanced learning outcomes in pediatric dentistry education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08752-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of 150 experimental vs 145 control fourth-year stomatology students; regular grades compared by nonparametric rank-sum test (Z = 4.20, p < 0.00 in Table 3). The article reports "mean regular academic grades (74.63 ± 9.97 vs. 67.66 ± 12.26)". No effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The experimental group achieved significant higher scores than the control group, with mean regular academic grades (74.63 ± 9.97 vs. 67.66 ± 12.26) and final exam scores (80.98 ± 6.23 vs. 77.68 ± 7.22), respectively (p < 0.05)."
