Taghi Ali, Atia Mohammad, Mohaisen Mais Alreem, Al-Noufi Bushra, Sibai Mohamad, Nouh Sara, Arfeh Yaman, Alhamid Ahmad Abdul Hakim, Taghi Olya, Kheder Karam, Mahfouz Fawaz Ali. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive chi-square analyses of training preferences in the 500-student survey (Tables 11–12): hands-on workshops preferred by 86.6% pre-clinical vs 90.7% clinical students (p = 0.120) and 91.9% public vs 88.6% private (p = 0.455); about 80% willing to attend future training.

> "Hands-on practical workshops were the dominant preferred format (approximately 87–92%) with no meaningful differences by academic stage or university type (Table 12)."
