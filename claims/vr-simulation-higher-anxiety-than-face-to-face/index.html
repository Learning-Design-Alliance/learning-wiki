Evidence¶

Pérez de los Cobos Cintas C 2025¶

Pérez de los Cobos Cintas C, Vuillerme N, Thomann G, Di Marco L. (2025). Effect of an Extended Reality Simulation Intervention on Midwifery Students' Anxiety: Systematic Review. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/68984

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this randomized pretest-posttest trial of 56 nursing students comparing VR clinical simulation with face-to-face manikin simulation, in which "anxiety scores... were higher for students in the VR simulation".

"However, the new technology rather than the VR simulation itself might have caused the increased anxiety."
