## Evidence

### Lazarides 2018

Lazarides, R., Gaspard, H., & Dicke, A.-L. (2018). Dynamics of classroom motivation: Teacher enthusiasm and the development of math interest and teacher support. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.012

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unconditional multilevel latent change models on 1000 students in 47 classrooms surveyed at the beginning of Grades 5 and 6; Table 1 prints latent change means of −0.14 (interest) and −0.13 (teacher support), each p < .001. The article reports "decrease significantly at the class level".

> "the mean of the latent change variables indicates that both mathematics interest and perceived teacher support decrease significantly at the class level from Grade 5 to 6."
