## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the Higher Mathematics 3 pilot's first in-person session, the lecturer ascertained by a show of hands that most students had watched all videos before the session; one student had technical problems with ILIAS, and the lecturer's questions on video content were answered correctly. ## Evidence

### Braun 2012

Braun, I., Metzger, G., Ritter, S., Vasko, M., & Voss, H.-P. (2012). Inverted Classroom an der Hochschule Karlsruhe - ein nicht quantisierter Flip. In J. Handke & A. Sperl (Hrsg.), Das Inverted Classroom Model: Begleitband zur ersten deutschen ICM-Konferenz. Oldenbourg Verlag. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1209.4451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1209.4451)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case observation of the first inverted session of the Higher Mathematics 3 pilot (about 20 third-semester students), documented by a SKATING employee using an observation sheet. Verbal show-of-hands questioning plus YouTube usage statistics indicated "Die meisten Studierenden" had watched all videos; content questions were correctly reproduced.

> "Die meisten Studierenden hatten sich alle Videos vor der Veranstaltung angesehen (gemäß Frage mit Handzeichen und Nutzungsstatistiken), eine Person hatte technische Probleme bei der Verwendung von ILIAS."
