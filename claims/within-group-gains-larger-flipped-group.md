# Both groups improved significantly from pre- to post-test, with a larger within-group gain in the flipped classroom group (d = 1.87 vs. d = 0.86)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Within-group pre- to post-test improvements were significant in both groups, with a larger effect size in the flipped classroom group (d = 1.87) than the control group (d = 0.86). [→ Mohammadi Shabnam 2026](#mohammadi-shabnam-2026)

## Evidence

### Mohammadi Shabnam 2026

Mohammadi Shabnam, Mastour Haniye, Dadgar Moghadam Maliheh, Emadzadeh Ali. (2026). Changing the landscape of medical anatomy education: impact of flipped classroom pedagogy on learning outcomes and perceptions of medical students in practical gastrointestinal anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09522-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests within each group showed significant pre- to post-test gains. The intervention group's within-group effect ("Cohen's d = 1.87") exceeded the control group's ("Cohen's d = 0.86").

> "Within-group analyses demonstrated significant improvements from pre- to post-test in both groups (control: 14.80 ± 3.05 to 17.42 ± 3.22, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.86; intervention: 14.65 ± 2.97 to 20.34 ± 3.10, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.87)"
