`q3 i?` D-Casting test scores differed significantly across four groups of dental and dental technology students (P < 0.001), with the experienced Group 4 scoring significantly higher than all three novice groups. [→ Wang YiZhen 2025](#wang-yizhen-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang YiZhen 2025

Wang YiZhen, Li Yi, Ma ChaoYi, Zhang WeiYi, Yuan ZiYa, Zhu Qian, Zhao XiaoHan, Song Jinlin, Yang Sheng, Ji Ping, Pang MengWei. (2025). Effectiveness evaluation of a serious game for dental lost wax casting training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06643-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional construct validity assessment of 145 dental and dental technology students in four groups of differing casting experience, tested after one week with the exam version of D-Casting. Group 4 (students who had practiced casting) scored 96.39 (8.419) versus 86.67 (15.832) for inexperienced Group 1. The article reports significance values but no standardized effect size.

> "The D-Casting test scores of all groups exhibited statistically significant differences (86.67[15.832] vs. 88.62 [11.252] vs. 82.24 [15.674] vs. 96.39 [8.419]; P < 0.001). Pairwise comparisons revealed that the D-Casting test scores of Group 4 were significantly higher than the scores of Group 1"
