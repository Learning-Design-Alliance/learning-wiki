# At student level, SES has significant positive indirect effects on both recommendation types through students' prior performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At student level, SES has significant positive indirect effects on test‐based recommendations through prior performance, including the school leavers' test, reading comprehension, and mathematics. [→ van Leest 2021](#van-leest-2021)
`q2 i?` At student level, SES has significant positive indirect effects on judgement‐based recommendations through reading comprehension and mathematics performance. [→ van Leest 2021 (2)](#van-leest-2021-2)

## Evidence

### van Leest 2021

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel mediation analysis (1–1–1 model, MLmed macro, Monte Carlo confidence intervals) at student level revealed significant positive indirect SES effects through prior performance for test‐based recommendations, including the school leavers' test pathway.

> "the models revealed significant positive indirect effects (path ab) of students' SES on test‐based recommendations through students' prior performance (school leavers' test: b  = .08, p < .001; reading comprehension: b  = .03, p < .001)"

### van Leest 2021 (2)

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The same student‐level mediation models showed 'Similar results were reported for judgement‐based recommendations', with significant indirect effects through reading comprehension and mathematics performance.

> "Similar results were reported for judgement‐based recommendations (reading comprehension: b = .07, p < .001; mathematics: b = .10, p < .001)."
