Descriptive characterization of Cluster 1 (226 learners, 58.9% of the sample) from the log-file and pre/post-test data. The cluster's z-standardized characteristics sit around 0 with small error bars, and its members "show almost no gain in conceptual knowledge"; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

"The usage patterns show that the learners have invested the minimum learning time with the interaction of I3Learn. Compared to the other clusters, the learners of Cluster 1 tend to use less text. They show almost no gain in conceptual knowledge."
