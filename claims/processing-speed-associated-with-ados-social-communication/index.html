Evidence¶

Haigh Sarah M. 2018¶

Haigh Sarah M., Walsh Jennifer A., Mazefsky Carla A., Minshew Nancy J., Eack Shaun M. (2018). Processing Speed is Impaired in Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder, and Relates to Social Communication Abilities. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3515-z

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Bivariate correlational analysis among 74 adults with ASD linking the processing speed composite to ADOS Communication and Reciprocal Social Interaction scores; the article reports the effect size "r = − .34" (r(74) = − .34, p = .003). The partial correlation controlling for IQ, age, and sex was r(71) = -.23, p = .047.

"Processing speed composite scores were negatively correlated with ADOS—Communication and Reciprocal Social Interaction scores, r (74) = − .34, p = .003."
