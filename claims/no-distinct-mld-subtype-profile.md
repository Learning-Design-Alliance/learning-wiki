# No profile distinguishes more severe, persistent, or specific MLD from less severe MLD on cognitive skills

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Number-sense deficits did not vary with specificity of the sample (p = .174) or with sample age entered alone, contradicting the expectation that the most severe, persistent, specific MLD constitutes dyscalculia with a core number-sense deficit. [→ Kroesbergen 2023](#kroesbergen-2023)

## Evidence

### Kroesbergen 2023

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analyses on number-sense effect sizes. Seriousness and persistence as single predictors yielded no significant relations; only seriousness × persistence interactions emerged, and the authors conclude a profile distinguishing more serious, persistent, or specific MLD was not detected.

> "Group differences between groups with MLD and typically performing groups (effect sizes) in number sense did not vary as a function of the specificity of difficulties of the MLD group that was included in studies, p  = .174"
