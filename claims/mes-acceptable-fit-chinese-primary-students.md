### Xia et al. 2022

Qi Xia, Hongbiao Yin, Ruonan Hu, Xiuhan Li, and Junjie Shang. (2022). Motivation, Engagement, and Mathematics Achievement: An Exploratory Study Among Chinese Primary Students. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440221134609

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis (Mplus 7.4, maximum likelihood) of the 44-item Chinese-translated MES with 1,538 students found the second-order model reached "an acceptable level of data fit". All composite reliabilities exceeded .60 and all CFA loadings reached 0.40.

> "the hierarchical second-order model of the MES scale was examined and found to reach an acceptable level of data fit (x2= 2,580.47, df= 802, x2/ df=3.22, CFI=0.92, TLI=0.91, RMSEA=0.038, SRMR = 0.055)"
