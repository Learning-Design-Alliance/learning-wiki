Plebanek 2017¶

Plebanek, D. J., & Sloutsky, V. M. (2017). Costs of Selective Attention: When Children Notice what Adults Miss. Psychological Science, 28(6), 723-732. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617693005

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the General Discussion, citing Rock & Gutman (1981) and Hoffman & Rehder (2010): "selectivity reduces the ability to encode unattended information" and induces learned inattention impeding future learning. No new data are presented for this claim in this article.

"However, selectivity reduces the ability to encode unattended information (Rock & Gutman, 1981) and induces learned inattention, which makes future learning more difficult (Hoffman & Rehder, 2010)."
