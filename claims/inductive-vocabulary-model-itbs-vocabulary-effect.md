## Evidence

### Michael R. Vitale & Nancy R. Romance 2013

Michael R. Vitale & Nancy R. Romance. (2013). Accelerating Vocabulary Development and Reading Comprehension in Grades 3-4-5 through an Inductive Vocabulary Model. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563063

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental school-wide study with HLM 7 analyses of ITBS Vocabulary pre-post scores, students nested in classrooms, with minority status, free/reduced lunch, grade and prior ITBS as covariates. The follow-up General Linear Hypothesis test was "significant, Chi-Square (2df) = 11.43, p < .0003". No effect size was printed.

> "The result of this follow-up analysis was significant, Chi-Square (2df) = 11.43, p < .0003 and confirmed the overall impact of the intervention on student ITBS Vocabulary achievement."
