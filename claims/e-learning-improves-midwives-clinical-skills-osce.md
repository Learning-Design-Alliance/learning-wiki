Effect of E-learning clinical management of substance-dependent pregnant women on the knowledge and clinical skill performance of midwives: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-024-07130-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc correction on OSCE scores from the same randomized controlled trial (93 midwives). Table 3 reports between-group Cohen's d of 2.83 immediately after and 3.11 one month after the intervention; intervention means rose from 15.32 (5.82) to 28.30 (3.69).

> "There was a significant difference in the scores between the two groups immediately after the intervention and one month after the intervention (P < .001)."
