### Eric Matthew Hickok 2016

Eric Matthew Hickok. (2016). Teaching practices of graduate teaching assistants. San José State University Master's Project. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Time-series analysis of a Python script over Physics 50W observation data showing what interactions directly followed Passive and Active Observations (Fig. 4.5). No effect sizes are printed.

> "Observing led to Closed Dialogue , Clarifying Instruction , Explaining Content, and Listening to Questions with nearly the same frequency. This means that when the TAs saw an error or student diﬃculty, they were just as likely to attempt to correct it directly as they were to include the students in the reasoning process."
