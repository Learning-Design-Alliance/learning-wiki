## Evidence

### Hayek Joyce 2021

Hayek Joyce, de Vries Hein, Tueni Maya, Lahoud Nathalie, Winkens Bjorn, Schneider Francine. (2021). Increased Adherence to the Mediterranean Diet and Higher Efficacy Beliefs Are Associated with Better Academic Achievement: A Longitudinal Study of High School Adolescents in Lebanon. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18136928

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model analysis of the same longitudinal adolescent sample found the self-efficacy × time interaction statistically significant (p < 0.001), indicating different effects at the two follow-ups. The 12-month effect estimate was B 0.48 (95% CI 0.32, 0.65); the 6-month estimate was not significant. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "for every additional unit increase in self-efficacy, academic achievement additionally increased by 0.48 (effect estimate (B) 0.48; 95% CI 0.32, 0.65). Yet, at 6 months we did not find this effect (B 0.13, 95% CI −0.04, 0.30)"
