## Evidence

### Becker 2025

Becker, E. S., Hascher, T., Goetz, T., & Staub, F. C. (2025). Exploring antecedents of student teachers' emotions during instructional experiences: A situation‐specific analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S133–S152. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12738

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three multilevel structural equation models (one per emotion) on 511 lessons from 178 student teachers, with class motivation and discipline as latent within-level predictors. The article reports "the relationships between classroom conditions and student teachers' emotions were weak" and R² = .08 for enjoyment, non-significant for anxiety and anger.

> "Overall, the relationships between classroom conditions and student teachers' emotions were weak. Notably, there was no significant relationship between classroom conditions and anxiety."
