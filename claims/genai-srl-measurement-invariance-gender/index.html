Xiaoqi Wang 2026¶

Xiaoqi Wang, Lawrence Jun Zhang, Yanan Zhang. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence-supported self-regulated learning (GenAI-SRL) in L2 writing: scale development, validation, and short-form construction. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-026-09481-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Measurement invariance testing via configural, metric, and scalar multi-group CFA on Sample two, grouped by gender. The article reports the constraint sequence produced ΔCFI ≤ 0.003, ΔTLI ≤ 0.001, ΔRMSEA = 0.000, indicating the scale "reached measurement invariance across gender groups."

"Successive constraints on factor loadings and item intercepts did not result in considerable changes in fit indices (ΔCFI ≤ 0.003, ΔTLI ≤ 0.001, ΔRMSEA = 0.000), supporting metric and scalar invariance."
