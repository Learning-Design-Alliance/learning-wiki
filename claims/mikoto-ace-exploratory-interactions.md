`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ACE tool scores assessed by endoscopy specialists at each site and analyzed with mixed-effects linear models in the cluster-randomized trial. Both groups improved in multiple motor and cognitive domains; the article labels ACE findings exploratory given limited trainees, no assessor blinding, and multiple item-level comparisons.

> "For selected ACE items, group‐by‐period interactions were statistically significant, suggesting greater improvement in the mikoto group for some insertion‐related motor and cognitive skills."
