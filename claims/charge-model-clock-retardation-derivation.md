## Evidence

### D. J. Miller 2009

D. J. Miller. (2009). A constructive approach to the special theory of relativity. https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0902 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Sec. II C: using the relativistic form of Newton's second law with the restoring force of Eq. (10), the centre charge executes simple harmonic motion with period Tv = gamma*T0 (Eq. 12). The authors extend the result to real clocks only as a suggestion: moving clocks "will run slowly for similar reasons".

> "It runs more slowly by the factor √ 1 − β2 when it is moving in S than when it is at rest and it is reasonable to suggest that more elaborate moving clocks will run slowly for similar reasons."
