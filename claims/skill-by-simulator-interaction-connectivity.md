# Skill level interacts with simulator technology on directed connectivity from LPMC to RPFC and SMA to LPFC

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Two-way MANOVA found a statistically significant skill-level × simulator-technology interaction on LPMC-to-RPFC connectivity (F(1,15)=8.523, p=0.011, partial eta-squared=0.362). [→ Anil Kamat 2022](#anil-kamat-2022)
`q3 i3` The interaction was also significant for SMA-to-LPFC connectivity (F(1,15)=6.824, p=0.020, partial eta-squared=0.313). [→ Anil Kamat 2022](#anil-kamat-2022)

## Evidence

### Anil Kamat 2022

Anil Kamat, Basiel Makled, Jack Norfleet, Steven D. Schwaitzberg, Xavier Intes, Suvranu De, Anirban Dutta. (2022). Directed information flow during laparoscopic surgical skill acquisition dissociated skill level and medical simulation technology. npj Science of Learning 7:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00138-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

The repeated-measure two-way MANOVA (seven experts, six novices; physical and VR simulators) tested the interaction term; the article reports both significant interaction effects with the printed F, p, and partial η2 values (0.362 and 0.313).

> "two-way MANOVA found a statistically sig- niﬁcant effect of the interaction between the skill level and the simulator technology on the interregional directed functional connectivity from the LPMC to RPFC ( F (1, 15) = 8.523, p = 0.011; partial η2 = 0.362) and SMA to LPFC ( F (1, 15) = 6.824, p = 0.020; partial η2 = 0.313)"
