## Evidence

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Independent-samples t-tests on NASA-TLX subscales from 24 participants. AR rated higher on performance (p = .0012), effort (M = 4.83 vs 3.71, p = .0008), and frustration (M = 4.88 vs 3.83, p = .024); the authors interpret higher scores as greater perceived success, less exertion, and less frustration. No effect sizes printed.

> "A main effect of training condition was observed for performance, with AR (M = 5.00, SD = 1.22) rated higher than Video (M = 3.92, SD = 1.28; p = .0012)."

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026 (2)

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null NASA-TLX results from the same survey: no significant differences on mental demand (p = .100), physical demand (p = .31), or temporal demand (p = .913). The authors note AR was not perceived as clearly less mentally, physically, or temporally demanding than video; equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant differences were found for mental demand (AR: M = 3.95 SD= 1.51; Video: M = 4.5, SD = 1.38; p = .100), physical demand (AR: M = 3.66, SD = 1.55; Video: M = 3.66, SD = 1.55; p = .31), or temporal demand (AR: M = 4.5, SD = 1.31; Video: M = 4.45, SD = 1.61; p = .913)."
