## Evidence

### Luppescu 2005

Luppescu, S., & Hart, H. (2005). Improving Chicago's Schools 2005 Survey Report: Details of Student and Teacher Responses. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/improving-chicagos-schools-sample-high-school-details-2005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary comparison of the Teacher-Parent Interaction scale, measuring how often teachers meet with, communicate with, and call parents about students. The report adds that in the lowest-rated schools 'only 12 percent of the teachers reported frequent interaction, and 35 percent said their interaction was minimal.'

> "In the top-rated schools, only 11 percent of teachers said they had minimal interaction with parents."

### Luppescu 2005 (2)

Luppescu, S., & Hart, H. (2005). Improving Chicago's Schools 2005 Survey Report: Details of Student and Teacher Responses. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/improving-chicagos-schools-sample-high-school-details-2005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary comparison of the Teacher-Parent Trust scale, measuring mutual support and respect between parents and teachers. Forty percent of top-school teachers rate trust as strong and about one-third say minimal or no trust; in bottom-quartile schools two-thirds describe no or minimal trust.

> "Even in the highest-rated schools, only 27 percent of teachers classify the trust level between teachers and parents as very strong."
