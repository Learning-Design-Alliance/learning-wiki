### Eitel 2025

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review's synthesis of reward techniques reports that "meta-analyses revealed positive effects of gamified learning on motivation and learning outcomes," citing two meta-analyses. Gamification raises intrinsic value by integrating game elements such as badges and leaderboards.

> "Accordingly, meta-analyses revealed positive effects of gamified learning on motivation and learning outcomes (Sailer & Homner, 2020; Yu et al., 2024)."

### Eitel 2025 (2)

Eitel, A., Krebs, M.-C., Schöne, C. (2025). Introducing the FoRe-Squares Model: Focus Support and Reward as Key Predictors of Effective Technology-Augmented Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10045-z

`q2 · i0 · negligible effect (−0.01 recall, 0.03 transfer)` · `theoretical · r3`

Perceptual fluency is one of the reward-related techniques the review places under its reward factor; it reports meta-analytic evidence that making instruction more fluent "did not directly affect learning outcomes" while increasing success expectancy, citing Xie et al. (2018). The review's table of meta-analytic effect sizes gives fluency −0.01 for recall (k = 39) and 0.03 for transfer (k = 8), neither marked significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Nonetheless, meta-analytic evidence suggests that making an instruction (perceptually) more fluent did not directly affect learning outcomes, but it did increase success expectancy (Xie et al., 2018)."
