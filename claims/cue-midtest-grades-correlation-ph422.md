Correlational analysis of CUE scores against course grades at OSU. The midtest–PH422 grade correlation is printed as "r = 0.53, p< 0.001, N = 85"; the authors use it to argue additional PH431 material does not drive CUE performance.

> "We found a strong correlation between the ﬁrst CUE midtest scores and ﬁnal grades for PH422 (r = 0.53, p< 0.001, N = 85) and no statistically signiﬁcant relationship between the CUE post-test scores and ﬁnal grades in PH431 (r = 0.18, p> 0.05, N = 36)"
