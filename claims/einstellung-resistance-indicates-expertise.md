## Evidence

### Sweller and & Cooper 1985

John Sweller and Graham A. Cooper. (1985). The Use of Worked Examples as a Substitute for Problem Solving in Learning Algebra. Cognition and Instruction, 2(1), 59-89. https://www.jstor.org/stable/3233555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 Einstellung test: 16 problems with two critical Einstellung problems inserted after 12 practice problems. Group differences were significant for a + (a + b) - ef/g = c, X2(2) = 41.0, and for (a + b)(c + d) = (a + b) + (g), X2(2) = 25.9.

> "In summary, increased expertise was associated with a superior memory of actual algebraic equations (but not random symbol strings) and an increased resistance to Einstellung effects in operating on equations."
