### Oriyama Sanae 2025

Oriyama Sanae. (2025). Influence of the COVID-19 pandemic on nursing skills experience and subjective attainment among final-year nursing students in Japan: a questionnaire-based multicenter cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07832-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of subjective attainment (two-point scale: can/cannot do without help) between the before-pandemic sample (237) and during-pandemic sample (303). Six skills did not differ significantly between the two groups.

> "of the 31 nursing skills, more than 50% of the students judged they could perform 21 (67.7%) without help before, compared with 13 skills (41.9%) during the pandemic."
