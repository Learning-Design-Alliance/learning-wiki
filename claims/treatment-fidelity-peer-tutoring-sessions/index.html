Evidence¶

Mackiewicz 2011¶

Mackiewicz, S. M., Wood, C. L., Cooke, N. L., & Mazzotti, V. L. (2011). Effects of Peer Tutoring With Audio Prompting on Vocabulary Acquisition for Struggling Readers. Remedial and Special Education, 32(4), 345–354. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932510362507

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Treatment fidelity check on peer tutoring sessions: the first and fourth authors observed 25.8% of sessions distributed evenly across pairs using a 210-item checklist, finding "a mean accuracy of 93.4%".

"Results indicated that tutoring sessions were implemented with a mean accuracy of 93.4% (ranging from 75.7% to 99.5%)."
