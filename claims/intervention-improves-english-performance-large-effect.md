## Evidence

### Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza 2026

Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza, Weldmeskel Fisseha Mikre. (2026). Gender differences in causal attributions, self-regulation, achievement motivation, and academic performance following an intervention. BMC Psychology, 14, 588. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04357-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the 40-item English achievement test (n = 84) showed a mean difference of 3.99 points, t(83) = 8.54, p < .001, Cohen's d = 0.93. The authors report "a large effect size (d = 0.93)" following the interventional training.

> "Students’ scores increased by an average of nearly 4 points, with a large effect size (d = 0.93), suggesting a substantial positive change in their English achievement following the interventional training"
