## Evidence

### Sahan Bulathwela 2020

Sahan Bulathwela, María Pérez-Ortiz, Aldo Lipani, Emine Yilmaz and John Shawe-T aylor. (2020). Predicting Engagement in Video Lectures. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.00592](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.00592)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distributional analysis of the VLN dataset (Figures 3-5) splitting lectures into short (<5000 words) and long groups by word count. The authors add that "the model is better at comparing between short-short lecture pairs compared to long-long lecture pairs" and that long lectures have a more skewed target distribution concentrated closer to 0.

> "It can be seen that, as anticipated, the percentage of watch time tends to be shorter for long lectures."
