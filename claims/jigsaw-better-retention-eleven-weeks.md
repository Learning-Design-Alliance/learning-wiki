## Evidence

### Yapıcı 2016

Hakkı Yapıcı. (2016). Use of jigsaw technique to teach the unit "science within time" in secondary 7th grade social sciences course and students' views on this technique. Educational Research and Reviews, 11(8), 773-780. https://www.academicjournals.org/ERR

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same quasi-experiment administered swtAT as a retention test after 11 weeks to both groups (experimental n=25, control n=28). The article reports a significant difference favoring the jigsaw group; no effect size is printed.

> "there is a significant difference between the experimental and control groups in terms of retention of knowledge after 11 weeks (t (51)= 9,74; p<0,05)"
