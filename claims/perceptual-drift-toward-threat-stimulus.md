# Perceived size of stimuli drifts toward the threat-associated stimulus's size over the experiment, with no drift when the same stimuli are safety cues

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group-level perceived size increased when S3 served as threat stimulus and decreased when S8 served as threat stimulus, with 95% CIs excluding zero; the drift was absent when the same stimuli served as safety stimuli. [→ Kenny Yu 2024](#kenny-yu-2024)

## Evidence

### Kenny Yu 2024

Kenny Yu, Wolf Vanpaemel, Francis Tuerlinckx & Jonas Zaman. (2024). The representational instability in the generalization of fear learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00287-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian analysis of size-estimation data from the differential conditioning experiment (N = 80). The article reports the drift parameter's credible intervals exclude zero for threat stimuli, while for safety stimuli the estimates cover zero (e.g., S3 safety 95% CI [−0.18, 0.29]).

> "the group-level (across blocks) parameter μδμ, governing the overall changes in the mean of perceived size throughout the experiment, was estimated as positive when S3 served as threat stimulus (95% CI [1.74, 4.04], median = 2.80) and negative when S8 served as threat stimulus (95% CI [−5.05,−1.19], median =−2.90)."
