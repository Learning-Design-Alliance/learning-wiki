## Evidence

### Clarke 2017

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Kosty, D., Kurtz Nelson, E., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Turtura, J. (2017). Testing the efficacy of a kindergarten mathematics intervention by small group size. AERA Open, 3(2), 1–16. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858417706899

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article frames group size within Warren, Fey, and Yoder's treatment-intensity framework (dose, dose form, dose frequency, duration) and notes few studies isolate treatment-intensity variables in mathematics. The trial's own results showed intensity differences without outcome differences.

> "Small group instruction is considered crucial to the provision of interventions because it represents a mechanism for individualizing and intensifying instruction"
