### Pywell S. 2025 (2)

Pywell S., Wallace C., Wallace S., Davies M. (2025). Inclusive and effective teaching of Nature-Based Social Prescribing for allied health professional students within digital environments. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1694834

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cluster-map interpretation in Results reporting the VR-based activities cluster. Participants valued VR for empathy practice and scaffolding while positioning it as supplementary to direct nature experience; no test statistics printed.

> "VR-based activities suggested by participants included "through Virtual Reality put yourself in the shoes of someone who does not use nature daily for empathy and reflection," "Virtual Reality as a steppingstone for students to scaffold learning" and "Virtual Reality is not a replacement or a complete alternative. It's an addition.""
