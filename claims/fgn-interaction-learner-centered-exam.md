## Evidence

### Jackson Mallory A. 2026

Jackson Mallory A., Liu Hongjiao, Moon Sungmin, Doherty Jennifer H., Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2026). Use of a specific set of learner-centered evidence-based teaching practices correlates with higher exam performance across seven STEM departments. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327269

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up LMM with a first-generation-status by PC2-quartile interaction; the overall PC2 association differed between FGN and non-FGN students (p < 0.001, likelihood ratio test). The article reports both interaction contrasts as printed; no standardized effect size is given.

> "The average score increase comparing a medium-high level of learner-centered activity (Q3 of PC2) to a low level of learner-centered activity (Q1 of PC2) was significantly larger in non-FGN students than in FGN students (4.03 exam points versus 3.21 exam points; p < 0.05)."
