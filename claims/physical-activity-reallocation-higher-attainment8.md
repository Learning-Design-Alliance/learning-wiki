## Evidence

### Maloney Ciaran M. C. 2026

Maloney Ciaran M. C., Atkin Andrew J., Beaumont Lee C., Dainty Jack R., Warburton Victoria E. (2026). Adolescents’ time-use and academic attainment: A longitudinal, compositional analysis in the Millennium Cohort Study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346302

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Weekday isotemporal substitution analysis (n=1644) of 20-minute reallocations between six behaviour sets. The article reports "the strongest association of −.81" for adding sleep from physical activity (1.5% of an Attainment 8 score), and β = .29 (95%CI .24 to .34) for adding physical activity from sleep.

> "The strongest associations were observed in models that simulated the removal of time from physical activity. The substitution of 20 minutes to sleep from physical activity produced the strongest association of −.81, equating to a 1.5% reduction in Attainment 8 score."
