Longitudinal multiple regression predicting kindergarten-exit math from kindergarten-entry EF Touch in 64 children followed to exit. The article reports the entry EF Touch "significantly predicted later math" with unstandardized coefficients; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Longitudinal analysis revealed that EF Touch at kindergarten entry significantly predicted later math (B = 10.67, SE = 4.42, p = .021) at kindergarten exit, controlling for the covariates."
