## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Freisberg L 2026

Freisberg L, Meier-Diedrich E, Heinze M, Schönbeck J, Schubert D, Speck J, Strudwick G, Torous J, Schwarz J. (2026). Expected Competencies and Personal Attributes of Digital Health Navigators to Support Digital Mental Health Care: Focus Group and Interview Study With Patients and Health Care Professionals. JMIR Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.2196/83073

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of the hard skills domain (35 participants). "10/35, 28.57%" named data protection understanding; participants wanted DHNs to explain where data are stored and used, and some suggested advanced data protection training beyond existing internal clinic training.

> "The DHNs' technical and digital knowledge should be supplemented by a basic understanding of data protection, data security, data processing, and access rights relevant to DTx, according to the study participants (10/35, 28.57%)."
