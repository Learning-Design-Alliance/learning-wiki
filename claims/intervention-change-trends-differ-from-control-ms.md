### Dehghani Ali 2024

Dehghani Ali, Fakhravari Fariba, Hojat Mohsen. (2024). The Effect of the Team Members Teaching Design vs. Regular Lectures method on the Self-efficacy of the Multiple sclerosis Patients in Iran. Randomised Controlled Trial. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures comparison across the three randomized groups (n=16 each) in the RCT. Table 4 reports mean differences of -10.275 (Control-TMTD, p=0.001) and -5.621 (Control-Lecture, p=0.001) for self-efficacy change.

> "there is a significant difference between the change trend of the control group and TMTD. There is a significant difference between the change trend of the control group and the lecture group"
