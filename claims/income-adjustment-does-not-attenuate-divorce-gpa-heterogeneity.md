## Evidence

### Nilsen 2020

Nilsen, S. A., Breivik, K., Wold, B., Askeland, K. G., Sivertsen, B., Hysing, M., & Bøe, T. (2020). Divorce and adolescent academic achievement: Heterogeneity in the associations by parental education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229183

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 3 added equivalized disposable income and both parents' net incomes (Tables 2–4). The income-adjusted interaction estimates remained essentially unchanged, suggesting the heterogeneity is not explained by measured income differences.

> "Adjusting the analyses by measures of parental income hardly changed the estimated interactions between divorce and parental educational levels on the adolescents’ GPA."
