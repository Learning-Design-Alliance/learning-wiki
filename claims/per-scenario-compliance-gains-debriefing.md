## Evidence

### Gamboa Oscar Andrés 2018

Gamboa Oscar Andrés, Agudelo Sergio Iván, Maldonado María Jose, Leguizamón Diana C., Cala Sandra M. (2018). Evaluation of two strategies for debriefing simulation in the development of skills for neonatal resuscitation: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3831-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis (generalized estimating equations model) of compliance percentage across the three scenarios in the randomized trial. The printed per-episode increases were "26.29%" (oral) and "33.55%" (video-assisted).

> "the participants of the oral group increased their score by 26.29% (95% CI 20.44% to 32.14%) after each debriefing episode, while those of the video group increased by 33.55% (95% CI 27.9% to 39.2%)."
