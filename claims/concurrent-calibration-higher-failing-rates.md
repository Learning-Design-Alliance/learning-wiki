`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Figure 5 and Tables 2-3 failing-rate comparison across administrations. For 1992F summed scoring, %Fail was 10.54 under CH_S and SM_S but 24.44 under CR_S, consistent with the printed pattern of "higher percentage of failing examinees" under CR.

> "CR produced relatively less fluctuation and higher percentage of failing examinees; larger differences were observed between CR and the two equating transformations for the older tests than the middle (i.e. tests for 2002F and 2009S) or the newer tests (i.e. 2009F and later tests)."
