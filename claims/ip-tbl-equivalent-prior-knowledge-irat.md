## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, Kalman E, Haq I, Leaver A, Roberts C, Bleasel J. (2020). Interprofessional team-based learning (TBL): how do students engage? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02024-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way between groups ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc test compared pre-class quiz scores of the two disciplines in the pilot TBL session. The article reports "no difference between Physiotherapy and Medical students' IRAT scores"; equivalence was asserted by the authors on this basis.

> "An analysis of variance revealed no difference between Physiotherapy and Medical students’ IRAT scores, suggesting that prior content knowledge of both disciplines was equivalent."
