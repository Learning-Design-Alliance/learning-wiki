## Evidence

### Liu Sha 2026

Liu Sha, Zhang Jiale, Shang Yuqi, Jiang Yufei, Zhang Xueyan, Hu Zhiyong, Chen Caiyun, Mi Wei. (2026). Practice and application of blended teaching based on RG-UClass in a preventive medicine course. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1885005

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SES questionnaire completed by all 107 students; independent t-test on total learning scores found the intervention group higher, with the printed means and "t = 3.997, p = 0.003".

> "the total learning scores of the students in the intervention group was higher (58.69 ± 11.34) than that in the control group (50.96 ± 10.09) (t = 3.997, p = 0.003)"

### Liu Sha 2026 (2)

Liu Sha, Zhang Jiale, Shang Yuqi, Jiang Yufei, Zhang Xueyan, Hu Zhiyong, Chen Caiyun, Mi Wei. (2026). Practice and application of blended teaching based on RG-UClass in a preventive medicine course. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1885005

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dimension-level SES comparisons with Bonferroni-corrected thresholds; the learning methods dimension difference was not statistically significant (p > 0.0083), so the total difference was driven by the other three dimensions.

> "The students in the intervention group presented high-performance scores in the three dimensions of learning emotions, learning engagement, and learning performance (p < 0.0083)"
