# Student VLE usage in Algebra Nation falls into four latent classes, with instructional-video-focused usage the largest (65.17%)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` LCA of student system-log usage identified four classes: Instructional Video Focused (27,828 students, 65.17%), Video and Practice Focused (8,912, 20.87%), Pre-Algebra Video Focused (399, 0.93%), and Review Focused (5,563, 13.03%). [→ Walter L. Leite 2021](#walter-l-leite-2021)

## Evidence

### Walter L. Leite 2021

Walter L. Leite, Zeyuan Jing, Huan Kuang, Dongho Kim & A. Corinne Huggins-Manley (2021). Multilevel Mixture Modeling with Propensity Score Weights for Quasi-Experimental Evaluation of Virtual Learning Environments. Structural Equation Modeling: A Multidisciplinary Journal, 28:6, 964-982. https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2021.1919895

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Latent class analysis of Spring 2017 Algebra Nation system logs for 42,698 students, with indicators selected by a hybrid Ant Colony Optimization algorithm minimizing ICL. The four-class model had entropy of 0.985; the "Review Focused" class "more frequently reviewed their incorrect questions".

> "The first latent class, labelled “Instructional Video Focused”, included 27,828 students (65.17%) who mainly watched VLE instructional videos which exclude the pre-algebra content."
