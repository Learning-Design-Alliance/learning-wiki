## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Time until first attempt (rs = −0.20) and time used for the first attempt (rs = −0.15) are significantly inversely correlated with final examination performance across performance deciles. [→ Pavo Noemi 2022](#pavo-noemi-2022)

## Evidence

### Pavo Noemi 2022

Pavo Noemi, Niedermaier Thomas, Seitz Stefanie, Jäger Harald, Strametz-Juranek Jeanette, Rieder Anita, Anvari-Pirsch Anahit. (2022). Gender differences in examination behavior of 4th grade medical students: Results from an observational study on ECG e-learning. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-021-01959-z

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation of quiz-related behavioral variables with final examination score groups (deciles of educational performance) in the same 1315-student cohort. Students with "increasing scores at the final examination" accessed the quiz earlier and spent less time on the first attempt, with printed effect sizes rs = −0.20 and rs = −0.15 (both p < 0.001).

> "Both the time until first attempt as well as the time used for the first attempt were significantly lower for students with increasing scores at the final examination (rs = −0.20, p < 0.001 and rs = −0.15, p < 0.001)"
