Evidence¶

Benden 2026¶

Benden, D. K., & Lauermann, F. (2026). The Interplay of Personal and Situational Factors in Situated Expectancy-Value Theory: Cross-Classified Analyses of Students' Situation-Specific Motivations in the Math Domain. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001032

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Unconditional cross-classified random intercept models (Model 1) of situation-specific motivation and performance from beginning STEM students in NRW, Germany, decomposed variance into student, test-content, and situation levels, showing "57% and 62% of the variance ... accounted for by between-student differences" and 3% to 9% by content areas.

"between 57% and 62% of the variance in students' ability self-concept, task values, and test performance was accounted for by between-student differences, whereas 3% to 9% was accounted for by differences between the test content areas"
