## Evidence

### ElHassan NO 2018

ElHassan NO, Bai S, Gibson N, Holland G, Robbins JM, Kaiser JR. (2018). The impact of prematurity and maternal socioeconomic status and education level on achievement-test scores up to 8th grade. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198083

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction tests in the multivariate mixed models, reported in Results and Table 2. The article reports the literacy advantage of HH social factors declined from 149.9 at grade 3 to 144 at grade 5 and 122.1 at grade 8; the interaction was not significant in the mathematics model.

> "However, the interaction between social factors and grade level was significant in the literacy model implying that the influence of social factors varied across grades. The coefficients of interaction for any social factors and grade level decreased with advancing grades."
