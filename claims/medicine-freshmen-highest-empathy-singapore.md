## Evidence

### Müller AM 2026

Müller AM, Tan CGL, Lau STL, Gallagher PJ. (2026). Selecting for empathy? A cross-sectional study on empathy and empathic care intentions in newly enrolled dentistry, medicine, nursing, and pharmacy students in Singapore to inform admissions and training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09510-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 646 newly enrolled students at the National University of Singapore using the JSE-HPS. The Results section reports the pairwise discipline comparisons: "Medicine students scored significantly higher than students from all other disciplines".

> "Pairwise comparisons revealed that Medicine students scored significantly higher than students from all other disciplines (p < 0.001 for comparisons with Nursing and Pharmacy students, p = 0.014 for the comparison with Dentistry students)."
