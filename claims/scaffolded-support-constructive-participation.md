## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, Z., Li, H., Xiao, Z., Qu, H., & Xie, X. (2026). Informal learning emerges in everyday human–LLM interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17643

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Covariate-adjusted within-setting models from Results Section 2.3 of the observational corpus, where scaffolded support appeared in 31.7% of assistant turns. Associations held within both explicitly and non-explicitly framed conversations (rate ratios 1.356–1.703, all p<.001).

> "scaffolded-support presence was associated with higher constructive-turn counts, with Poisson count ratios ranging from 1.569 to 2.491, and with higher odds that a conversation contained at least one constructive user turn, with logistic odds ratios ranging from 1.437 to 2.140"
