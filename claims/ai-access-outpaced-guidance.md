## Evidence

### Jacob 2026

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., Schneider, H., & Peters Hinton, V. (2026). Institutional conditions for integrating emerging technologies in higher education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/294

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey finding (120 respondents): over 70% reported campus provision of paid generative AI tools, yet the report states "the guidance has not kept up" and faculty described no universal AI policy, leaving students with mixed messages across courses.

> "AI tools have become more available across institutions over the past year, with many enabling campus-wide subscriptions or integrating AI into learning management systems. In fact, over 70% of survey respondents reported that their campus provides students with access to paid versions of generative AI tools."
