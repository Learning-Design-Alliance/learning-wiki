Evidence¶

EL-Andari R 2023¶

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

q2 · i? · review · r2

Construct-validity comparison using a low-fidelity mitral valve replacement trainer: times to completion, needle angle, accuracy, suture placement and knot tying were compared between general surgery residents, cardiothoracic residents and attending surgeons, with "significant differences between groups for all scores measured".

"The scores achieved were 32.9 (standard deviation [SD] 11.4), 65.1 (SD 11.5) and 88.3 (SD 7.8) for general surgery residents, cardiothoracic surgery residents and attending surgeons, respectively (p < 0.001)."
