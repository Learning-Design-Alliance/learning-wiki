## Evidence

### Yen-Fen Lee 2025

Yen-Fen Lee, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Pei-Ying Chen. (2025). Technology-based interactive guidance to promote learning performance and self-regulation: a chatbot-assisted self-regulated learning approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10478-x

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t test on the Kember et al. reflective-thinking questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.90) after a Levene test; chatbot group mean 3.96 vs general SRL group mean 3.69, "with a t-value of 2.05", d = 0.56 (Table 10).

> "the results showed that the mean score of students in the SRL mode based on the feedback mechanism of the chatbot was 3.96, and the mean score of students in the general SRL mode was 3.69, with a t-value of 2.05. This shows that the introduction of the SRL mode based on the feedback mechanism of the chatbot significantly improved students' reflection."
