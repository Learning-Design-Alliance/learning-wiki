Same multinational survey comparison as the task-management finding. The article prints descriptive percentages for the four remaining domains and states "no other significant differences between the LA and NA/US surveys were observed"; no effect sizes are reported.

> "The second highest value was placed on self-management competencies (87.45%), followed by social responsibility competencies (86.83%). The second lowest rate was found for innovation competencies (86.69%), and the lowest for leading others competencies (83.31%). Except for task management, no other significant differences between the LA and NA/US surveys were observed."
