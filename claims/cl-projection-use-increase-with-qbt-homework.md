## Evidence

### Giaco Corsiglia 2022

Giaco Corsiglia, Steven Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). Effectiveness of an online homework tutorial about changing basis in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Cross-semester comparison of method use on the change-of-basis exam question (Table I) in upper-division QM. The Fall 2018-to-Fall 2021 increase, between semesters taught by the same instructor, was "not statistically significant given this sample size" (p = 0.095, V = 0.15).

> "The increase in the fraction of students using projection from Fall 2018 to Fall 2021 is not statistically significant given this sample size (p = 0.095) and shows a weak effect size (V = 0.15)."

### Giaco Corsiglia 2022 (2)

Giaco Corsiglia, Steven Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). Effectiveness of an online homework tutorial about changing basis in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same exam-question comparison across semesters with and without the QBT homework: the Fall 2018-to-Spring 2022 increase in projection use was "significant (p< 0.01) with a moderate effect size (V = 0.34)"; these semesters had different instructors.

> "The increase from Fall 2018 to Spring 2022 is significant (p< 0.01) with a moderate effect size (V = 0.34)."

### Giaco Corsiglia 2022 (3)

Giaco Corsiglia, Steven Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). Effectiveness of an online homework tutorial about changing basis in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.corsiglia)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Comparison between the two QBT-assigned semesters on the same exam question found a significant difference (p < 0.05, V = 0.20); the authors suggest "factors beyond the QBT" account for the higher Spring 2022 rate.

> "The difference in the fraction of students using projection between the two semesters with the ACE Physics QBT (Fall 2021 and Spring 2022) is also significant ( p < 0.05) with a moderate effect size (V = 0.20)"
