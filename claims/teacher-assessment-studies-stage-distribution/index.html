Analysis of the 59 selected studies addressing RQ1 (teacher assessment), coded by formative assessment process. The review reports "nearly half of the learning analytics–supported formative teacher assessment studies concentrated on the initial stage of formative assessment, 'where the learner is now' (45%, N = 27)" and 40% (N = 23) on 'now' plus 'going'.
