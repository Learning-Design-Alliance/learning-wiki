# After PD, teachers rated computer vision as more similar to human vision, with computer vision cluster 1 matching human vision cluster 1 on seven constructs post-PD versus one pre-PD

## Evidence

### Kurz 2026

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of pre- and post-PD dendrograms for the two elements shows the overlap between the most descriptive computer vision cluster and the most descriptive human vision cluster grew from one shared construct to seven (constructs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, and 12). The authors interpret this as participants rating computer vision as more similar to human vision after PD.

> "After PD, the computer vision post-PD cluster 1 became more similar to the human vision post-PD cluster 1. The computer vision post-PD cluster 1 contained some of the same constructs as the human vision post-PD cluster 1 (both contained: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, and 12). This was an increase from one matching construct pre-PD to seven matching constructs post-PD."
