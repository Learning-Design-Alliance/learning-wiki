`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive analysis of sample characteristics across included articles (Table 1): 197 of 275 mentions concerned students, 55 teachers, 15 students and teachers, and 8 students and parents. The review states "Teachers were rarely assessed" and calls for more simultaneous perspectives to reduce self-report bias.

> "Most of the studies employing school-based intensive longitudinal research focused on students. Teachers were rarely assessed, and even fewer studies incorporated the simultaneous perspectives of students and their teachers or parents."
