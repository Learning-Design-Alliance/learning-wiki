Strydom 2026¶

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article's concluding argument, offered without empirical data: the epistemological account "lays the foundation for alternative approaches to teaching and assessment, student interactions, professional development, and AI governance and policy". The author simultaneously flags the framework as needing validation.

"I conclude by arguing that this understanding of human-GAI engagement, as explained through epistemological beliefs, lays the foundation for alternative approaches to teaching and assessment, student interactions, professional development, and AI governance and policy, while noting that the framework remains an exploratory heuristic requiring empirical validation."
