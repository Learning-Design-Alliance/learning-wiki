q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Comparison reported in the discussion section of this systematic review, contrasting its dental examination results with a prior meta-analysis on medical licensing examinations. The review reports "54% accuracy rate of GPT-3 and 72% of GPT-4 in dental licensing examinations are both lower than the accuracy rates reported in another meta-analysis on LLMs' performance in medical licensing examinations".

"54% accuracy rate of GPT-3 and 72% of GPT-4 in dental licensing examinations are both lower than the accuracy rates reported in another meta-analysis on LLMs' performance in medical licensing examinations (GPT-3.5: 59%, GPT-4: 81%)."
