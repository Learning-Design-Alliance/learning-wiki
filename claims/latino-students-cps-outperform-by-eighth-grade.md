Observational analysis of 2006 ISAT scores for Latino students in CPS versus the rest of Illinois; Table 3 prints standardized effect sizes of d = -0.14 (grade 3 reading) rising to d = 0.16 (grade 8 reading), both below d = 0.2 in magnitude.

> "In reading, Chicago Latino students in third grade underperform Latinos in the rest of Illinois by about three score points. For eighth-graders, Chicago Latino students outperform their counterparts by four score points."
