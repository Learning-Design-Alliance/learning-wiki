### Seyyed Mahdi Erfani 2012

Seyyed Mahdi Erfani. (2012). Pictures Speak Louder than Words in ESP, Too! English Language Teaching, 5(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n8p164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest equivalent-group quasi-experiment with 65 intermediate-level physics students at Damghan University; experimental group (N=29, M=25.58, SD=1.90) versus control (N=31, M=22.93, SD=2.21) on the posttest, with "a remarkable difference between the two groups at the posttest stage".

> "Considering (t-observed = 1.16 < t-critical = 2.00) at the pretest and (t-observed = 4.08 > t-critical =2.00) at the posttest shows that a remarkable difference between the two groups at the posttest stage is obvious."
