# Transformed DATA Lab courses produced higher expert-like attitudes about experimental physics than traditional lab courses after controlling for incoming E-CLASS scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANCOVA controlling for E-CLASS pretest scores showed a significant post-test difference between traditional and transformed courses in both DL1 (7% difference) and DL2 (9% difference), with ps < 0.001. [→ Kelsey Funkhouser 2019](#kelsey-funkhouser-2019)
`q3 i?` Descriptively, expert-like responses increased from pre to post in the transformed courses (4% in DL1, 6% in DL2) while decreasing in the traditional courses (3% in DL1, 1% in DL2). [→ Kelsey Funkhouser 2019 (2)](#kelsey-funkhouser-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Kelsey Funkhouser 2019

Kelsey Funkhouser, William M. Martinez, Rachel Henderson, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). Design, Analysis, Tools, and Apprenticeship (DATA) Lab. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab2f0d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab2f0d)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ANCOVA comparing post-instruction E-CLASS expert-like response percentages between the traditional and transformed courses, controlling for pretest scores. DL1 showed a "signiﬁcant 7% post-test diﬀerence"; DL2 showed a significant 9% difference; both ps < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "results demonstrated a signiﬁcant 7% post-test diﬀerence in expert-like responses between the traditional course and the transformed course after controlling for ECLASS pretest scores"

### Kelsey Funkhouser 2019 (2)

Kelsey Funkhouser, William M. Martinez, Rachel Henderson, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). Design, Analysis, Tools, and Apprenticeship (DATA) Lab. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab2f0d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab2f0d)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched pre/post E-CLASS data (1,377 DL1 and 925 DL2 students) administered as an online extra-credit assignment. Expert-like views "increased by 4% in DL1 and by 6% in DL2", while traditional courses decreased 3% and 1%. Descriptive percentages; no test statistic accompanies them.

> "in the transformed DATA Lab courses, the students’ expert-like views of experimental physics increased by 4% in DL1 and by 6% in DL2"
