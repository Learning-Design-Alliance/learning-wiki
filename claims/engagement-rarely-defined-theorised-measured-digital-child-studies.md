Systematic scoping review of 2661 screened records yielding 88 included studies of children aged 5-8 using digital technology in formal or semi-formal educational settings. The review reports: "A measure of engagement was provided in 31 studies (35.2 %), a definition in 18 studies (19.3 %), and a theory of engagement in eight studies (9.1 %)."
