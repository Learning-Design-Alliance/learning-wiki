### Çamlar Mahmut 2025

Çamlar Mahmut, Sevgi Umut Tan, Erol Gökberk, Karakaş Furkan, Doğruel Yücel, Güngör Abuzer. (2025). Comparative performance of neurosurgery-specific, peer-reviewed versus general AI chatbots in bilingual board examinations: evaluating accuracy, consistency, and error minimization strategies. Acta Neurochirurgica 167(1):241. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-025-06628-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 392 SBA-R questions in the six-model benchmark (Table 1). ChatGPT-o1pro attained the highest correct response rate of 81.6%, followed by ChatGPT-4 (78.1%) and AtlasGPT (77.6%); the article states Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 each retained less than 52% correct answers.

> "The accuracy rates of ChatGPT-o1pro, ChatGPT-4, and AtlasGPT were not significantly different but were significantly higher than those of Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 (p < 0.001)."
