## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Three-test sequence in Fall 2005: a pre-test before lab-tutorial 7, an ungraded post-test the day after lab-tutorial 7, and an examination six weeks later. Table III reports the free cart correct response (equally likely in all regions) at 52%, 52%, and 88% for N = 38, 41, and 43; the incorrect higher-at-edges response at 33%, 48%, and 10%. Students giving the correct response "also gave correct reasoning by saying that the speed is the same in all regions".
