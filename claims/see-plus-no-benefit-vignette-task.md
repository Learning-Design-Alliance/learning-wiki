# SEE+ shows no benefit on the cognitive and affective theory of mind vignette task

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to predictions, SEE+ did not differ from control groups on the fifth booklet vignette task; Stop & Think outperformed TAU. [→ Mayer Sveta 2025](#mayer-sveta-2025)

## Evidence

### Mayer Sveta 2025

Mayer Sveta, Dumontheil Iroise, Wilkinson Hannah R., Porayska-Pomsta Kaska, Farran Emily K., Tolmie Andrew K., Mareschal Denis, the UnLocke Team. (2025). SEE+ computerized classroom-based training enhances 7- to 10-year-olds' socio-emotional cognition through observation and inference. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0330934

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ANOVA on the fifth booklet (cognitive and affective ToM vignettes) found a main effect of Condition (F(2, 5579) = 4.19, p = .015, ηp2 = .001), but "the SEE+ ... group did not differ from the control groups". In the multilevel model the Condition effect fell to trend level (F(2, 192) = 2.88, p = .059).

> "post-hoc Bonferroni corrected pairwise comparisons indicated that the SEE+ (M = 3.289 (SE = 0.024)) group did not differ from the control groups, but the S&T group (M = 3.322 (SE = 0.018)) outperformed the TAU group (M = 3.231 (SE = 0.026), p = .008)"
