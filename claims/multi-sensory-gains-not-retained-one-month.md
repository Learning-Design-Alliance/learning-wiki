Alenizi, M.A. (2019). Effectiveness of a Program Based on A Multi-Sensory Strategy in Developing Visual Perception of Primary School Learners with Learning Disabilities: A Contextual Study of Arabic Learners. International Journal of Educational Psychology, 8(1), 72-104. https://doi.org/10.17583/ijep.2019.3346

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the 30-learner experimental group, reported in Table 4 of the Results section, comparing visual perception across two measurements after the program. The table prints t = 0.067 and statistical significance 0.947; the accompanying text states the values "did not reach the values of "t" at the statistical significance level of 0.05 α".
