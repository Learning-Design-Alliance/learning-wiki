## Evidence

### Cepeda 2006

Cepeda, Nicholas J., Pashler, Harold, Vul, Edward, Wixted, John, & Rohrer, Doug. (2006). Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A Review and Quantitative Synthesis. Psychological Bulletin. https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3rr6q10c. [doi:10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354)

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of massed vs. spaced comparisons (271 comparisons, 23 effect sizes) using independent samples t-tests; at retention intervals under one minute spaced study improved final-test performance by "9 percent, compared to massed presentations".

> "Spaced presentations led to markedly better final-test performance, compared to massed presentations. For retention intervals less than one minute, spaced presentations improved final-test performance by 9 percent, compared to massed presentations"
