## Evidence

### Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang 2026

Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang. (2026). TutorLoop: Regulating Student Learning Behaviors via Sensor-in-the-Loop Generative Feedback. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Feedback presence ratio (seconds of displayed feedback over the 600-second session) was compared across groups in the N=187 study; DRL Only over-generated feedback relative to TutorUp (p<.001). Temporal analysis showed Full delivered less feedback early and more later, tracking declining attention and rising workload.

> "we observed a near-zero correlation between feedback presence ratio and learning outcomes (𝑟= 0.010,𝑝= 0.893). This indicates that more frequent feedback does not necessarily lead to better outcomes"
