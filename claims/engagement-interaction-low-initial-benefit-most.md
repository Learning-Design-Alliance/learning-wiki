## Evidence

### Rojas 2016

Natalia Rojas. (2016). The Association of Peer Behavioral Regulation with School Readiness Skills in Preschool. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567502

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-level interaction models (Table 1, Block 3) added to the HLM analysis of the ParentCorps preschool sample. The interaction of child engagement with class engagement was significant across all four Speed DIAL-3 outcomes; the child attention-impulse control interaction was not significant.

> "There was a significant interaction between children's initial engagement and classroom-level engagement predicting spring overall school readiness skills (d = 0.03, p = 0.007), motor skills (d = 0.04, p = 0.013), content knowledge (d = 0.05, p = 0.001) and language (d = 0.05, p = 0.002). This indicated that when initial child engagement was low, the association between high classroom engagement and child school readiness growth was stronger."
