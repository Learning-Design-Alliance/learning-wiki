## Evidence

### Gedamu Tigist Enyew 2025

Gedamu Tigist Enyew. (2025). The impact of nutritional and socioeconomic status on academic performance of primary urban school children in northern Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02175-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional logistic regression analysis of 399 urban primary school children in Debre Tabor Town, Ethiopia, with academic performance dichotomized at the mean. The article reports that "height for age (HAZ) anthropometric nutritional status does not statically predicted academic performance in the final model"; severe stunting had AOR 1.01 (0.37–2.78) and moderate stunting AOR 1.35 (0.54–3.35).

> "Variables like breakfast habit, grade, family support and height for age (HAZ) anthropometric nutritional status does not statically predicted academic performance in the final model."
