# CoI-based blended learning improved skill performance and management efficiency subscores of practical ability more than face-to-face teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Experimental-group students scored significantly higher on skill performance (t = 4.05, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.54) and management efficiency (t = 4.10, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.48) than controls. [→ Liu Weichu 2021](#liu-weichu-2021)

## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t tests on two NCEE checklist indicators in the two-group quasi-experimental trial, both favouring the CoI-based blended learning group with the printed Cohen d values. Experimental preparation and case analysis subscores showed no significant differences.

> "in terms of skill performance (t = 4.05, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.54) and management efficiency (t = 4.10, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.48) were reported significant differences for the two teaching strategies as well"
