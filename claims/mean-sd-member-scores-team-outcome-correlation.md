# The mean of member scores correlates with team outcomes (r = 0.60) while the standard deviation correlates only weakly (r = 0.36)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Average member performance in the individual round correlates with the team outcome after the first collaboration (r = 0.60, p < 0.0001). [→ Hyewon Jang 2026](#hyewon-jang-2026)
`q3 i2` The standard deviation of member performance hardly correlates with the team outcome after the first collaboration (r = 0.36, p = 0.016). [→ Hyewon Jang 2026 (2)](#hyewon-jang-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Hyewon Jang 2026

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of two-stage exam data (44 teams, 218 students) plotting team scores after the first collaboration against the mean of member individual scores. The printed effect size was r = 0.60 with p < 0.0001.

> "Figure 3(a) shows that the average member performance in the individual round correlates with the team outcome after the first collaboration in the team round (𝑟=0.60,𝑝<0.0001)."

### Hyewon Jang 2026 (2)

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same correlational analysis of 44 teams: the standard deviation of member scores showed a weak printed effect size of r = 0.36 with p = 0.016, described by the author as hardly correlating.

> "Figure 3(c) shows that the standard deviation of member performance hardly correlates with the team outcome after the first collaboration (𝑟= 0.36,𝑝=0.016)."
