## Evidence

### Star 2013

Star, J. R., Rittle-Johnson, B., Durkin, K., Newton, K., Pollack, C., Lynch, K., & Gogolen, C. (2013). The Impact of a Comparison Curriculum in Algebra I: A Randomized Experiment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564086

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models on CC subcomponents (procedural, conceptual, flexibility knowledge) with dosage as Level 2 predictor. The article concludes "spending additional time with our materials seemed particularly helpful for improving students' conceptual and flexibility knowledge."

> "Dosage did not significantly predict procedural knowledge (β = 0.02, t =1.44, p = 0.159), but it did predict conceptual and flexibility knowledge (β = 0.03, t = 2.82, p = 0.008 and β = 0.03, t = 3.17, p = 0.003, respectively)."
