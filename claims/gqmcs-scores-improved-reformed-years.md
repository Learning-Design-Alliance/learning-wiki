### L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan 2008

L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan. (2008). Graduate quantum mechanics reform. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2628 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0806.2628](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0806.2628)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Essay-style 24-question GQMCS given as an extra-credit pre-final each year of the longitudinal study. The authors note average scores were not particularly useful for comparison since different courses led to proficiency on different questions.

> "The average scores on the GQMCS for years 1–3 were 68% ± 5%, 64% ± 3%, and 78% ± 5%, respectively. The diﬀerence between years 2 and 3 is weakly statistically signiﬁcant, according to a two-tailed t-test ( p = 0.05)."
