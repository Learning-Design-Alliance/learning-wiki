# College preparatory elective (G) courses showed markedly lower CALPADS-UC alignment (23-30%) than other A-G areas

## Evidence

### Fong 2018

Fong, A., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2018). Examination of data usability options for assessing eligibility for higher education in California. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592524

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subject-area alignment analysis for UC applicants (Table 3, 2011-12). The report prints "Only 23% of the students had the same number of annualized units in CALPADS and the UC dataset" for G courses, with 41% in UC data only and 24% in CALPADS only.

> "Only 23% of the students had the same number of annualized units in CALPADS and the UC dataset. A high percentage of records corresponding to college preparatory elective (G) courses in the UC admissions dataset were not found in CALPADS (41%) for the same students; likewise, a high percentage of these records in CALPADS were not found in the UC dataset (24%)."
