Multivariate regression Model 2 added a CAQ × SAQ interaction term for both outcomes; the interaction was non-significant for paper folding (p = .31) and habits of mind (p = .13), and the authors state "a multiplicative effect ... was not supported in the current study."

> "The possibility that there is a multiplicative effect of participating in spatial activities during both childhood and adolescence was not supported in the current study."
