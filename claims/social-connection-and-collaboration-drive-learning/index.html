Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019¶

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

q2 · i? · review · r?

The synthesis reports that a "positive, supportive learning community" can reduce anxiety and that trusting relationships support self-worth and belonging, recommending cooperative learning and trauma-informed practices.

"When students feel that they are part of a positive, supportive learning community, this can reduce anxiety, allowing them to focus on learning."

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019 (2)¶

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

q2 · i? · review · r1

The synthesis reports that collaboration toward a common goal, rather than splitting projects into individual parts, engages the social brain and produces more learning than individual work; preparing to teach is described as a powerful way to engage it.

"Working collaboratively towards a common goal, rather than dividing a project into parts that can be done individually, encourages students to discuss, think about ideas they might not have considered, and learn more than they would if working individually."
