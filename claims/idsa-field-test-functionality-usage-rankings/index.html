Evidence¶

Karrenbauer 2023¶

Karrenbauer, C., Brauner, T., König, C. M., & Breitner, M. H. (2023). Design, development, and evaluation of an individual digital study assistant for higher education students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10255-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational usage-data analysis during the 3-month rollout in the local learning management systems of three German higher education institutions. Of 1,036 users, 643 allowed their data to be used for research purposes; 482 (75%) were undergraduates. The analysis "revealed that the functionalities… were the most used" with printed user counts per functionality.

"The usage data revealed that the functionalities “Organization of Learning” (R.1.1), “Interests” (R.1.4, R.1.6), “OER” (R.1.5), and “Personality” (R.1.2) were the most used in that each of these had between 509 and 555 users."
