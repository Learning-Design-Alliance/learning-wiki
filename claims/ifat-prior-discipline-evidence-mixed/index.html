Narrative literature attribution by the present authors, not a re-analysis. The article attributes the positive findings to studies in nursing, physics and strategic HR, and reports that in the athletic and pharmacy studies "student scores were equivalent to those students not using the I F-AT format", noting Bowman & Laurent's sample of only 23 students.

"In the studies involving nursing, calculus -based physics, and strategic human resources, the authors found that that the IF -AT format was significantly more effective than the traditional testing in enhancing learning."
