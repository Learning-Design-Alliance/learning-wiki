## Evidence

### Figueroa 2025

Figueroa, R. B., Jr., & Jung, I. (2025). Integrating VR tours in online language learning: A design-based research study. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 41(1). [doi:10.14742/ajet.9498](https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.9498)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Friedman rank-sum test on 10-point single-item spatial presence ratings plotted for each learner across Lessons 2 to 10 in the DBR study. The test showed "no statistically significant variation across the lessons (F(8) = 10.43, p = 0.24)"; no effect size printed.

> "This upward trend is likely due to improved self-efficacy in later lessons, as they became more adept at navigating the tours and less hindered by earlier technical difficulties. Nevertheless, the Friedman test indicated no statistically significant variation across the lessons (F(8) = 10.43, p = 0.24)."
