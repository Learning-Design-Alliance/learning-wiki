## Evidence

### Paul Denny 2023

Paul Denny, Hassan Khosravi, Arto Hellas, Juho Leinonen, Sami Sarsa. (2023). Can We Trust AI-Generated Educational Content? Comparative Analysis of Human and AI-Generated Learning Resources. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same length analysis applied to explanation components showed the opposite direction: "students producing a median of 303 characters for their explanations compared to 395 characters for the AI", statistically significant at p < .001.

> "For the explanations, the difference is not as large although it is still statistically significant (U = 55834.5, p < .001), and the direction is the opposite, with students producing a median of 303 characters for their explanations compared to 395 characters for the AI."
