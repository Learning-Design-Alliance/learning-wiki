Validation of gAI outputs by an external evaluation committee of four ESSQS-certified supervisors against consensus reference standards on five predefined scenes; Dice coefficients "ranged from 0.71 to 0.88" and median Likert ratings of 4 for pancreas and DMP. No effect size printed.

> "Quantitative evaluation revealed that Dice coefficients between gAI output and the reference standards for the pancreas ranged from 0.71 to 0.88 across categories (Fig. 2A). External evaluation by four independent EEC members demonstrated median scores ≥ 3 for all anatomical landmarks across categories, with the pancreas and DMP achieving median scores of 4 (Fig. 2B)."
