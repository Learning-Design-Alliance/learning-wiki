## Evidence

### Carenzo L 2021

Carenzo L, Cena T, Carfagna F, Rondi V, Ingrassia PL, Cecconi M, Violato C, Della Corte F, Vaschetto R. (2021). Assessing anaesthesiology and intensive care specialty physicians: An Italian language multisource feedback system. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250404

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric feasibility study of the new Italian MSF instruments with 64 anaesthesiology residents at one Italian program (91.4% of those invited). "605 medical colleague questionnaires" and 543 coworker surveys were returned, and mean item ratings exceeded 4.0/5 on both instruments.

> "Overall 605 medical colleague questionnaires (mean of 9.3 ±1.9) and 543 coworker surveys (mean 8.4 ±1.4) were collected providing high mean ratings for all items (> 4.0 /5.0)."
