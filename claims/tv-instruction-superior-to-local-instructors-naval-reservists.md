### Fordham University 1953

Fordham University. (1953). Training by Television: the Comparative Effectiveness of Instruction by Television, Television Recordings, and Conventional Classroom Procedures. Special Devices Center, Port Washington, L. I., New York. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED000002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The study compared comparable groups of Naval Air Reservists taught a series of training lessons by live television, kinescope recordings, or conventional classroom instruction, with effectiveness evaluated by information tests and trainee comments. The abstract reports "Instruction by live TV and TV recording were superior to local instructors."
