The article's authors' assertion, attributed to PER-based reform work it cites (Beatty 2004; Dufresne et al. 1996; Mazur), that classroom response systems can convert lecture into discussion "even with hundreds of students in the room". No study data or effect size is presented in this article.

> "Employed wisely classroom response systems—technology for posing questions to students in class, and electronically collecting and displaying their answers—can transform a classroom from a largely one-way lecture to a dynamic discussion with engaged students, even with hundreds of students in the room"
