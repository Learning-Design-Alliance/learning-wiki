## Evidence

### DeVore 2019

DeVore, S., Marshman, E., & Singh, C. (2019). The challenge of engaging all students via self-paced interactive e-learning tutorials for introductory physics. [doi:10.3389/fict.2018.00003](https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00003)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Twenty-two 2-3 hour think-aloud interviews with volunteers from algebra- and calculus-based courses, in which a researcher ensured tutorials were used as intended. "an average score of above 80% on all the paired problems"; Table 1 prints 86.0% (SD 15.9), 95.5% (SD 11.8), and 83.3% (SD 16.0).

> "Table 1 shows that students in a one-on-one interview setting had an average score of above 80% on all the paired problems."
