### Nitschke Ina 2025

Nitschke Ina, Holter Martin, Sobotta Bernhard, Jockusch Julia. (2025). Simulation of Age‐Related Limitations of Patients in Patient‐Centred Dental Education. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13075

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student questionnaire rankings of GP stations (Figures 2 and 3, n=195) reported in the Discussion. "students found the station for learning to transfer from a wheelchair to a dental chair to be the most useful"; clinical nutrition was least useful. No effect size reported.

> "When asked to assess the most and least useful GP stations for their future professional lives, students found the station for learning to transfer from a wheelchair to a dental chair to be the most useful. The clinical nutrition station was rated as the least useful."
