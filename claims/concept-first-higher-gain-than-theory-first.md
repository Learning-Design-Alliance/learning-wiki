### Dringoli 2024

The impact of content sequencing on quantitative problem-solving: A case study in Electromagnetism using an online learning platform. arXiv version of November 2024, first posted 2019 as "Content Sequencing and its Impact on Student Learning in Electromagnetism: Theory and Experiment". [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1910.00145](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1910.00145)
