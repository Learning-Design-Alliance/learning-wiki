`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication bias assessment using funnel plot, Egger's regression intercept (t(121) = 0.39, p = .348), trim-and-fill, and fail-safe N (20,731 missing studies needed) showed the adjusted correlation remained negative.

> "Duval and Tweedie 's trim-and-fill technique (31 studies trimmed at the right side) resulted in an adjusted correlation from − .355 to − .227 (95% CI [ − .291, − .162]."
