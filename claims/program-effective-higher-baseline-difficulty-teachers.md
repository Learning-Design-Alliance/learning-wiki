## Evidence

### Kageyama Masako 2023

Kageyama Masako, Matsushita Atsunori, Kobayashi Ayuna, Sakamoto Taku, Endo Yasuhiro, Sakae Setsuko, Koide Keiko, Saita Ryotaro, Kosaka Hiyuka, Iga Satoko, Yokoyama Keiko. (2023). Video-based e-learning program for schoolteachers to support children of parents with mental illness: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-023-15426-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analysis by median baseline score (< 13 vs >= 13), n = 67 lower and n = 104 higher difficulty. Higher-difficulty LSmean difference I-C = -1.79; only p-values printed, no effect size.

> "an analysis of the interaction between baseline and intervention effects on the Sense of Coping Difficulty subscale found a significant interaction (p = 0.001): for those with lower scores (lower difficulty), there was no significant difference between groups (p = 0.812); for those with higher scores (higher difficulty), the intervention group had a significantly greater effect than the control group (p = 0.001)"
