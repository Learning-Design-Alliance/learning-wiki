## Evidence

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mean IPSA domain scores with 95% confidence intervals for 47 graduating biochemistry majors taking the program exit IPSA "The Lorrat"; a score of 7 of 10 was defined as satisfactory performance. The average student in this sample performed satisfactorily only in the Evaluate and Reflect domains.
