### Yan Haichao 2025

Utilization of animal experiment-based simulation surgery platforms in the education of endoscopic thyroidectomy: a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07935-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Structured questionnaire administered at the end of the rotation in the randomized trial (n = 30 per group); satisfaction and enjoyment both favored the simulation group with P < 0.01 (d = 0.90 and 0.89 in Table 2).
