## Evidence

### Baghus A 2020

Baghus A, Giroldi E, Muris J, Stiggelbout A, van de Pol M, Timmerman A, van der Weijden T. (2020). Identifying Entrustable Professional Activities for Shared Decision Making in Postgraduate Medical Education: A National Delphi Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000003618

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In Round 3 of the Delphi study, experts rerated 2 reformulated behavioral indicators; the own-preferences indicator reached only "56% of the experts scored >= 6". The main argument was that the resident's preference might influence the patient too much since it is hard to state a preference in a neutral manner.

> "No consensus was reached on the behavioral indicator "the resident introduces own preferences where indicated" because only 56% of the experts scored >= 6 on the statement "I do like to include this behavioral indicator in the educational curriculum.""
