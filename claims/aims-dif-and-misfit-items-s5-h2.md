## Evidence

### Romine William L. 2016

Romine William L., Miller Michele E., Knese Shawn A., Folk William R. (2016). Multilevel Assessment of Middle School Students' Interest in the Health Sciences: Development and Validation of a New Measurement Tool. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0034

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Ordinal logistic regression DIF testing at α = 0.05 on the pre–post Rasch data found uniform DIF on S1 and S5 and nonuniform DIF on H2 (b = 0.267, χ2(1) = 5.41, p = 0.02, OR = 1.31), indicating reduced discrimination after the intervention.

> "Uniform DIF was detected on items S1 (b = −0.48, χ2(1) = 4.56, p = 0.03, OR = 0.62) and S5 (b = −0.46, χ2(1) = 6.34, p = 0.012, OR = 0.63), suggesting that these items were slightly more agreeable at the beginning of the intervention."

### Romine William L. 2016 (2)

Romine William L., Miller Michele E., Knese Shawn A., Folk William R. (2016). Multilevel Assessment of Middle School Students' Interest in the Health Sciences: Development and Validation of a New Measurement Tool. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0034

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Rasch mean-squares analysis showed S5 (infit 1.79, outfit 1.77) and H2 (infit 1.72, outfit 1.98) exceeded the 1.5 fit threshold; the authors attribute S5's misfit to its school-context wording and H2's to negative wording.

> "All items display satisfactory fit with the Rasch model (mean-squares fit below 1.5), except for S5 and H2 (Table 2)."
