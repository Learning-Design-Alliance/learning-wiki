## Evidence

### Pless Anina 2021

Pless Anina, Hari Roman, Brem Beate, Woermamm Ulrich, Schnabel Kai P. (2021). Using self and peer video annotations of simulated patient encounters in communication training to facilitate the reflection of communication skills: an implementation study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001451

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Implementation in the 2019 Autumn term: all 247 students participated in one of 24 seminars; the online evaluation found "The annotation task was deemed to be easy to understand (88%) and easy to implement (82%) by most students." Annotations took a mean of 64 minutes.

> "The annotation task was deemed to be easy to understand (88%) and easy to implement (82%) by most students. It took students on average 64 minutes (mean) to complete their online annotations."
