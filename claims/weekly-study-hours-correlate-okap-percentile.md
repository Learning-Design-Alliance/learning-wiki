### Zafar Sidra 2019

Resident and program characteristics that impact performance on the Ophthalmic Knowledge Assessment Program (OKAP). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1637-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of US ophthalmology residents (183 providing exact percentiles); Pearson correlation of self-reported weekly study hours with self-reported 2017 OKAP percentile. The article reports "significantly correlated with OKAP percentile (p = 0.007)"; Table 2 prints r = 0.201.

> "The average number of hours per week spent on studying for the OKAP was significantly correlated with OKAP percentile (p = 0.007)."
