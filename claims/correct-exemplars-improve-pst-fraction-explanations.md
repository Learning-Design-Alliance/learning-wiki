# Correct and complete exemplar supports produced greater improvement in preservice teachers' fraction comparison explanations than supports displaying common misconceptions and errors

## Evidence

### Guba 2026

Guba, T.-P., Barbieri, C. A., Jansen, A., & Morris, A. (2026). Supports for preservice teachers' metacognitive monitoring and explanations of fraction comparisons. Instructional Science (2026) 54:18. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09773-0

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Experiment with elementary preservice teachers (N = 87) in a 2 (within, time point) x 3 (between, condition) design; participants revised fraction comparison explanations after studying condition-specific supports. The article reports "Hedges' g effect sizes ranged from medium to large (g = 0.64 to 0.95) for the three comparison tasks."

> "PSTs who were provided with instructional supports that provided a correct and complete exemplar of a fraction magnitude comparison showed greater improvements in their explanations than those who studied example explanations that displayed common misconceptions and errors. Correct and complete exemplars also supported greater detection and correction of errors and omissions in their explanations than those provided with a list of steps focused on structural aspects of the explanation. Hedges' g effect sizes ranged from medium to large (g = 0.64 to 0.95) for the three comparison tasks."
