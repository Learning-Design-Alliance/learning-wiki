## Evidence

### Papasideris M. 2021

Papasideris M., Ayaz H., Safati A. B., Morita P. P., Hall P. A. (2021). Examining the relationships among adolescent health behaviours, prefrontal function, and academic achievement using fNIRS. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience, 50, 100983. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.100983

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Conditional process model (Section 3.3) in the adolescent study; active minutes had a stronger effect on MSIT SD for those in the highest BMI category (β = .431, SE = .192, ρ = .030). All additional moderated mediation models were null.

> "A significant moderation effect emerged regarding BMI on the indirect effect of average active minutes on English and Math grades through MSIT SD (ΔR2 = .086, F = 6.318 (1,45), ρ = .016)."
