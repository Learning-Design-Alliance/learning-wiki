### Puchalski Ritchie Lisa M. 2016

Puchalski Ritchie Lisa M., van Lettow Monique, Barnsley Jan, Chan Adrienne K., Schull Michael J., Martiniuk Alexandra L. C., Makwakwa Austine, Zwarenstein Merrick. (2016). Lay Health Workers experience of a tailored knowledge translation intervention to improve job skills and knowledge: a qualitative study in Zomba district Malawi. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0580-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same qualitative interview study. Participants cited gaining the ability to dispense drugs correctly alone, calculate drug-collection time periods, and follow "one universal documentation of TB drugs dispensed" after case-based discussions with fellow lay health workers.

> "In addition to increased disease specific knowledge, participants reported gaining job-specific knowledge as a result of case-based discussions with their fellow LHWs. Reported job-specific knowledge gains included: increased awareness of the need for confidentiality, and improved understanding of job-specific tasks, including treatment documentation and medication dispensing."
