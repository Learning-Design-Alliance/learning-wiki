`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of complications (Clavien-Dindo classification) across included studies. The overall estimate favored advanced learning curves with printed effect size OR 2.06 (95% CI 1.04–4.09; p = 0.038; I² = 70%), but "the subgroup analysis showed no differences" (RALP OR 2.06, p = 0.038; LAP OR 3.53, p = 0.119).

> "we could see a reduced rate of complications in the group that underwent surgery performed by surgeons more advanced in the learning curve (OR 2.06; 95% CI 1.04–4.09; p = 0.038; I² = 70%), although the subgroup analysis showed no differences"
