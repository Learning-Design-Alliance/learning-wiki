## Evidence

### Yıldız 2021

Yıldız, M. (2021). Language choices of language teachers and learners: Meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 17(Special Issue 1), 472-492. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1285162

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-synthesis of 34 qualitative studies coded in NVivo 12.05; the article reports that "the factors such as educational context, the language proficiency of teachers and students, the subject to be taught" affect language choices and amount of L1 use. No effect sizes are printed, as is typical of qualitative synthesis.

> "Based on the findings of those studies, the factors such as educational context, the language proficiency of teachers and students, the subject to be taught were pointed out to affect their language choices and also the amount of L1 use in L2 classes."
