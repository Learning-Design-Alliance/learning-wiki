### Odden 2022

Odden, T. O. B., Silvia, D., & Malthe-Sørenssen, A. (2022). Using Computational Essays to Redistribute Epistemic Agency in Undergraduate Science. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1002/tea.21821](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21821)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of two UiO engineering students who scored 11 (low agency). They spent extensive time on background reading, writing, and graphics but left the provided simulation code mostly untouched due to time constraints and difficulty implementing a car-like structure. They scored 0 or 1 across Programming, Investigation, and most Modeling sub-categories, showing they 'focused their attention exclusively on the communicative aspects of their computational essay.'
