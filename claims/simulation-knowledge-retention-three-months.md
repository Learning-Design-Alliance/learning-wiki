Follow-up assessment of the same quasi-experimental cohort: 36 of 43 students (83.7% response rate) completed the 3-month posttest. Table 4 reports M3 = 16.2 +/- 2.57 versus M1 = 10.6, p < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.
