Evidence¶

Sara Coemans 2017¶

Sara Coemans, Karin Hannes. (2017). Researchers Under the Spell of the Arts: Two Decades of Using Arts-Based Methods in Community-Based Inquiry with Vulnerable Populations. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.08.003

q2 · i? · review · r2

Descriptive numerical synthesis of the 131 included studies (Table 2). Art forms were categorized as visual art (still images, moving images, 3D artefacts), performing art, literary art, and multiple methods. The review reports "a multiple methods approach" combining genres in 64% of studies, and counts such as n = 81 for still images and n = 31 for theatre.

"Table 2 shows that the most popular visual art form was photography, whereas theatre and poetry were the most used performing and literary art forms."
