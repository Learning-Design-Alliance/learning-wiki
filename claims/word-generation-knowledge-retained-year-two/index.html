Snow 2014¶

Catherine E. Snow. (2014). Extended discourse in first and second language acquisition: A challenge and an opportunity! Journal of Japanese Linguistics 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED571513

q2 · i? · review · r2

Longitudinal follow-up in the same six Boston schools, with a subset of year-one target words incorporated into the year-two test. The article reports students "maintained knowledge of the words taught over a second school year" with better performance at the end of year two.

"We found that both English-only and language minority students maintained knowledge of the words taught over a second school year, showing in fact better performance at the end of year two"
