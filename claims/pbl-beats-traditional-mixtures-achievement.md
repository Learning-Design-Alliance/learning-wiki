## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 16 course hours, the experimental (PBL) group scored significantly higher than the control (traditional) group on the mixtures achievement posttest. [→ Üce and Ates 2016](#uce-and-ates-2016)

## Evidence

### Üce and Ates 2016

Musa Üce, İsmail Ateş. (2016). Problem-based Learning Method: Secondary Education 10th Grade Chemistry Course Mixtures Topic. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 4, No. 12. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i12.1939

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 24 students per group at an Anatolian High School; independent-samples t-test on posttest means (control 17,920 vs experimental 22,040) showed "a significant difference in favor of the experimental group" (p<0.05). No effect size is printed.

> "In consequence of the statistical analysis of posttest realized after the application, a significant difference in favor of the experimental group was found in the achievement test on mixtures topic. (p<0.05, Table:2)."
