## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Spike count in the spontaneous window decreased as conditioning progressed (initial vs. final p = 1.82−5), and spike count in the late window decreased from initial to halfway (p = 6.33−5). [→ Parras 2024](#parras-2024)
`q3 i?` Spike count increased from halfway through the final session in both the CS and CS-US time windows (CS initial-final p = 2.14−5; CS-US initial-final p = 2.92−5). [→ Parras 2024](#parras-2024)

## Evidence

### Parras 2024

Parras, G. G., Delgado-García, J. M., López-Ramos, J. C., Gruart, A., & Leal-Campanario, R. (2024). Cerebellar interpositus nucleus exhibits time-dependent errors and predictive responses. npj Science of Learning 9, 12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00224-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of average spike counts per time window (spontaneous, CS, CS-US, late) with rank-sum tests across 188 recorded single units grouped by session. The article reports rank-sum p values (e.g., 1.82−5 for spontaneous initial-final); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The p values for comparison between initial-halfway, halfway-ﬁnal, and initial-ﬁnal were 0.4543, 3.21 −5, and 1.82 −5, respectively. However, we observed an increase in spike count from halfway through the ﬁnal conditioning session for both the CS and CS-US time windows."
