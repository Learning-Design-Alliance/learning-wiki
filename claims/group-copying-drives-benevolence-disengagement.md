## Evidence

### Caroline Pulfrey 2018

Caroline Pulfrey, Kevin Durussel, and Fabrizio Butera. (2018). The Good Cheat: Benevolence and the Justification of Collective Cheating. Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. 110, No. 6. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000247

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplementary dummy-coded regression analyses on the Study 2 vignette data compared each collective condition with the individual cheating condition. The article reports that "benevolence value adherence tended to predict moral disengagement to a greater extent for group collectively copying off other people compared with individual copying off other people", with the other contrasts marginal.
