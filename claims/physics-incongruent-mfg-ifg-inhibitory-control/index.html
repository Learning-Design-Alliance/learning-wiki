Evidence¶

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette 2021¶

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette, Lorie-Marlène Brault Foisy, Patrice Potvin, Martin Riopel, Marilyne Larose, Steve Masson. (2021). An fMRI study of scientists with a Ph.D. in physics confronted with naive ideas in science. npj Science of Learning 6:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00091-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Physics-domain t-contrasts in the 25-participant fMRI study; areas more activated for incongruent than congruent statements included left supplementary motor area, left superior frontal gyrus, bilateral MFG, left IFG and left middle occipital gyrus, while only the right middle occipital gyrus was more activated for congruent statements.

"Two brain areas, the MFG and IFG, associated with inhibitory control mechanisms were signi ﬁcantly more activated by incongruent statements."

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette 2021 (2)¶

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette, Lorie-Marlène Brault Foisy, Patrice Potvin, Martin Riopel, Marilyne Larose, Steve Masson. (2021). An fMRI study of scientists with a Ph.D. in physics confronted with naive ideas in science. npj Science of Learning 6:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00091-x

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation in the discussion: the greater involvement of frontal and prefrontal regions is read as evidence of increased attentional demand and suppression of the prepotent naive response; this is an interpretive claim, not a tested result.

"One plausible explanation is enhanced recruitment of cognitive control processes for suppressing a dominant response, the naive idea, and selecting a relevant but less salient response."
