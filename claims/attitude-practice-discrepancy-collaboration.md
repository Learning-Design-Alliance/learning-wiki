### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Remy Dou and Eric Brewe. (2018). Student perceptions of the value of out-of-class interactions: Attitudes vs. Practice. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spring 2017 SEQ self-reports: 92% valued teamwork, yet 60% reported studying together before the group exam and 55% on group assignments. Percentages are descriptive; no inferential test or effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "even though students perceive teamwork as a valuable skill for success ( 92% for Spring 2017), only about half of them reported working with peers, even when the teamwork was expected (60% of students reported studying together before the group exam) or explicitly requested (55% reported working with peers on group assignments)."
