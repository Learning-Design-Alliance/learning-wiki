## Evidence

### Someko Hidehiro 2026

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis stratified by program requirement among 1,152 Japanese junior residents. The article reports "0.09 points (95% CI: -0.19 to 0.37)" for those with requirements. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "the adjusted difference in ACE tool scores was 0.34 points (95% CI: 0.09 to 0.58) for residents who engaged in scholarly activities without requirements and 0.09 points (95% CI: -0.19 to 0.37) for those with requirements"

### Someko Hidehiro 2026 (2)

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis by program requirement among 1,152 Japanese junior residents. The article reports "higher odds of future academic interest (OR: 1.52 [95% CI: 1.02 to 2.25])". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "residents with requirements showed higher odds of future academic interest (OR: 1.52 [95% CI: 1.02 to 2.25]), compared to the reference group"
