Evidence¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker 2025¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker, Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2025). Enhancing enthusiasm for STEM education with AI: Domain-specific chatbot as personalized learning assistant. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2025.100315

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mann-Whitney U group comparison of post-intervention emotion scores in the cluster-randomized study (N = 195). Descriptively, EG positive-activating emotions were M = 2.37 (SD = 1.03) versus CG M = 2.10 (SD = 0.96); no effect size was printed, so the null is reported without equivalence claims.

"The Mann-Whitney U test revealed no significant differences between the EG and the CG for positive-activating (p = 0.053) or negative- deactivating emotions (p = 0.265)."
