### Leachman 2025

Leachman, M., Wolters, A., & Kim, Y.-S. G. (2025). The Relation Between Text Reading and Reading Comprehension Varies as a Function of Developmental Phase, Orthographic Depth, and Measurement Characteristics: Evidence From a Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000932

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 587 efficiency, 210 accuracy, and 258 rate effect sizes; the average correlations r = .65, r = .59, and r = .54 for efficiency, accuracy, and rate respectively, all with ps < .001.

> "When the measures of text reading were examined separately, the average correlations were .65, .59, and .54 (ps < .001) for text reading efficiency, text reading accuracy, and text reading rate, respectively."
