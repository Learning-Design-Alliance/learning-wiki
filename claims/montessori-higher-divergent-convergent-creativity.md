## Evidence

### Denervaud 2021

Denervaud, S., Christensen, A. P., Kenett, Y. N. & Beaty, R. E. (2021). Education shapes the structure of semantic memory and impacts creative thinking. npj Science of Learning 6:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00113-8

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Independent t test on EPoC divergent creativity scores from the 67 children (judges scored drawings, Krippendorff's alpha = 0.905). The Montessori sample scored "on average higher" on divergent creativity, t(64) = 3.24, p = 0.02, d = 0.58.

> "We found that the groups differed on both divergent and convergent creativity tests, with the Montessori sample scoring on average higher (M div = 9.6, M conv = 5.3) than the traditional sample (M div = 7.1, M conv = 2.6), t (64) = 3.24, p = 0.02, d = 0.58,"
