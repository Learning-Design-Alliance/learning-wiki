## Evidence

### Iordanou 2010

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar tests on the Kuhn et al. (2000) epistemological-understanding items administered at initial and final assessment (Table 2a, 2b). Only McNemar p-values are printed, so no effect size is coded. In the science domain, SCI evaluativist positions rose from 38.50% to 65%.

> "That is, participants in the SCI showed significant advance in the science domain only, whereas participants in the SOC showed significant advance in the social domain only, and participants in the control condition showed no advance."
