## Evidence

### Feng 2025

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., Huang, C.-W., Luttgen, K., Grady, J., Yang, S., Lavine, A., Collins, K., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments: Evaluation final report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677852

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Baseline equivalence analysis in the Methods section of the quasi-experimental study, comparing treatment students (n = 2,855) with matched virtual comparison students on the NWEA MAP Growth pretest. The printed effect size was "effect size = -0.03", which the report notes satisfies the WWC baseline equivalence requirement.

> "The baseline equivalence test on the pretest revealed that the average mean of VCGs was very close to the treatment group (effect size = -0.03), implying that the matching algorithm worked as expected"
