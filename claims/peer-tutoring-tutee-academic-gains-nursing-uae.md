### Dias Jacqueline Maria 2025

Dias Jacqueline Maria, Al Kaabi Fatema Salim Bati Salim, Al Hooti Sara Tamim, Hammadi Nada Osamah, Karim Rana, AlYateem Nabeel, Reefat Ahmed Fatma, Sara Abraham Mini, Seshan Vidya, Arsyad Sub Muhammad, Dias Ambrose Richard, Mottershead Richard (2025). Exploring peer tutoring experiences in improving nursing students’ academic success and performance: A qualitative study among Emirati undergraduate nursing students. F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of semi-structured interviews with undergraduate nursing tutees at the University of Sharjah; tutees cited examples such as simplified biostatistics calculations and a midterm score of 25/30. The study found peer tutoring "improved their performance in exams" as self-reported experience.

> "Many participants noted that peer tutoring significantly enhanced their understanding of complex subjects, improved their performance in exams, and provided new study strategies that supported their academic success."
