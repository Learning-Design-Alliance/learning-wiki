q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Stahl (2008), that lower elementary students used KWL charts both individually and collaboratively without improved performance on the three named outcome tasks. The review suggests younger students may require more structured activation support.

"However, in this study, KWL did not lead to improved performance on maze, vocabulary recognition, or free recall tasks."

"open-ended prompts appeared to facilitate text comprehension more consistently for high school (Martin et al., 1986) and undergraduate students (Hattan & Alexander, 2018; Pressley et al., 1990)."
