## Evidence

### Romero-Zaldivar 2012

Romero-Zaldivar, V.-A., Pardo, A., Burgos, D., & Delgado Kloos, C. (2012). Monitoring student progress using virtual appliances: A case study. Computers & Education, 58(4), 1058–1067. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2011.12.003

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational deployment study in a second-year engineering course (Fall 2010). The monitoring mechanism ran 15 weeks inside the students' virtual appliances; the authors report that "Almost 90,000 events were recorded for a total of 172 students" and conclude the approach was successfully deployed. No effect size is reported.

> "Almost 90,000 events were recorded for a total of 172 students. Thus, we conclude that the approach can be successfully deployed in a real course and students embrace it with no problems."
