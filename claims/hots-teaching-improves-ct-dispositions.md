## Evidence

### Miri 2007

Miri, B., David, BC. & Uri, Z. (2007). Purposely Teaching for the Promotion of Higher-order Thinking Skills: A Case of Critical Thinking. Res Sci Educ 37, 353–369. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-006-9029-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Disposition outcomes measured with critical thinking assessment instruments in the same three-group longitudinal case study (experimental n = 57; science control n = 41; non-science control n = 79). The article reports significance for named subscales without printing effect sizes.

> "the experimental group showed a statistically significant improvement on critical thinking skills components and disposition towards critical thinking subscales, such as truth-seeking, open-mindedness, self-confidence, and maturity, compared with the control groups."
