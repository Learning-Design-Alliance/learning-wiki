## Evidence

### Salomez-Ihl Cordélia 2025

Salomez-Ihl Cordélia, Chapuis Claire, Bedouch Pierrick, Albaladejo Pierre, Picard Julien. (2025). Assessment of a simulation-based implementation method to support the introduction of a new invasive device: a prospective cross-over study. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective mono-centric cross-over study of 66 ICU nurses randomised to two sequences: group A (n=33) used the new IABPS on a manikin scenario before explanations, group B (n=33) received explanations first. The article reports "The average number of errors was different in GA compared with GB (p-value<0.01)"; no effect size is printed.
