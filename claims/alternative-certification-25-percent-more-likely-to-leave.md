# Alternatively certified teachers are 25% more likely to leave their schools and the profession, controlling for other factors

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers entering through alternative certification pathways are 25% more likely to leave their schools and the profession, even after controlling for students, schools, and teaching conditions. [→ Carver-Thomas 2017](#carver-thomas-2017)

## Evidence

### Carver-Thomas 2017

Carver-Thomas, D. & Darling-Hammond, L. (2017). Teacher turnover: Why it matters and what we can do about it. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-turnover

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on SASS/TFS data controlling for student, teacher, and school characteristics; the report attributes the difference to alternative pathways' "less coursework and student teaching, on average."

> "Teachers who enter the profession through alternative certification pathways—who have had less coursework and student teaching, on average, than teachers who are prepared through traditional programs—are 25% more likely to leave their schools and the profession"
