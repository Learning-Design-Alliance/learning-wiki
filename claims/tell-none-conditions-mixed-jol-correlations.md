# Tell and no-agent conditions showed no significant JoL–confidence correlations, with mixed results for progress and mastery

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the tell condition, no correlation between JoL and any system metric was significant (confidence r = −0.075, progress r = 0.164, mastery r = 0.143). [→ Graf 2026](#graf-2026)
`q2 i2` In the none condition, results were mixed: confidence correlation was negative and non-significant, while progress (r = 0.312) and mastery (r = 0.256) correlations were significant. [→ Graf 2026 (2)](#graf-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Graf 2026

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Across 80 JoLs in the tell condition, no JoL–system-metric correlation reached significance; the article reports progress r = 0.164 (p = 0.146) and mastery r = 0.143 (p = 0.206), "again showing no significant improvement".

> "In contrast, the tell condition ( n = 80 JoLs) did not show significant correlations with any of the system metrics. The correlation for confidence was negative and not significant, r = − 0.075 (p = 0.511)."

### Graf 2026 (2)

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Across 118 JoLs in the no-agent condition, confidence correlated negatively and non-significantly, while progress and mastery correlations were significant (mastery r = 0.256, p = 0.005).

> "The none condition (n = 118 JoLs) showed mixed results. For confidence, the correlation was negative and not significant, r = − 0.055 (p = 0.551). However, for progress, there was a highly significant positive correlation, r = 0.312 (p = 0.001)."
