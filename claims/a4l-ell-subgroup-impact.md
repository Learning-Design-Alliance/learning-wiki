# The A4L impact on grade 4 CCU outcomes differed significantly by ELL status in years 1 and 2, with substantially larger effects for ELL students, but not in year 3

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In years 1 and 2 the program impact was substantially higher for ELL students (effect sizes 0.87 and 0.69) than for non-ELL students (0.23 and 0.31); the difference was not significant in year 3. [→ Jonathan Nakamoto 2015](#jonathan-nakamoto-2015)

## Evidence

### Jonathan Nakamoto 2015

Jonathan Nakamoto, Sandy Sobolew-Shubin, Martin Orland. (2015). The Beaverton School District Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project, An Investing in Innovation (i3) Development Grant: Student Impact Findings from Years 1, 2, and 3. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED560945

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analyses using subgroup-by-treatment interaction terms in HLM models with grade 4 CCU data only. The report prints ELL effect sizes of 0.87 and 0.69 versus 0.23 and 0.31 for non-ELL students in years 1 and 2, calling the ELL effects large.

> "the impact of the program differed significantly across ELL and non-ELL students in years 1 and 2, but not in year 3. In years 1 and 2, the impact of the A4L Lessons Project was substantially higher for ELL students"
