### Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza 2019 (2)

Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza, Ayroza-Ribeiro Helizabet Abdala, Lima Raquel Ferreira, Yonamine Aline Estefane Eras, Ohara Fabio, Ayroza-Ribeiro Paulo Augusto Galvão. (2019). RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0039-1700587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group comparison of mean uterine volume in the chart review: 217.8 cm3 (I-1) versus 154.2 cm3 (I-2), "p = 0.03"; no difference between I-2/I-3 or I-1/I-3. The discussion notes trained residents resected higher-volume uteri with shorter times.

> "We observed a decrease in uterine volume in group I-2 compared to group I-1: 217.8 cm3 and 154.2 cm3 respectively (p = 0.03)."
