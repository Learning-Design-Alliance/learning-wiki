### Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ 2019

Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ. (2019). Recurring questions that sustain the sensemaking frame. arXiv:1807.03137v2 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interpretive claim from the discussion of the single case study of Jake and Liam working a lightning-safety thought experiment in a clinical interview: the authors argue that although the questions were "formulated slightly differently each time," they all refer back to "the same perceived inconsistency: a missing mechanism for how charge could safely leave the car." No effect size is printed.

> "Although the questions were formulated slightly differently each time, we argue that they all are referring back to the same perceived inconsistency: a missing mechanism for how charge could safely leave the car."

### Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ 2019 (2)

Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ. (2019). Recurring questions that sustain the sensemaking frame. arXiv:1807.03137v2 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Conversational-analysis claim from the case study section, where the authors interpret Jake's three restatements of "how does the charge leave the car" as serving distinct functions: focusing attention, opening avenues, and expressing dissatisfaction. Qualitative interpretation with no effect size.

> "the first instance of the question served to focus the pair's attention on a particular gap in their understanding; the second instance opened up new avenues for their explanation; and the third instance allowed Jake to express his dissatisfaction with the explanation they had generated."

### Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ 2019 (3)

Tor Ole B. Odden and Rosemary S. Russ. (2019). Recurring questions that sustain the sensemaking frame. arXiv:1807.03137v2 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2019.1589655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the third recurrence, shortly after two long silences, when Jake said "I just don't know the mechanism by where this charge goes"; they read it as a bid to keep sensemaking going. Interpretive (type e) claim, no effect size.

> "by expressing dissatisfaction with the explanation so far, it seems to be a bid to simply keep their sensemaking going—to continue moving towards an explanation by any means possible."
