Narrative review reporting Pardos and Bhandari's expert-review procedure for GPT-3.5-generated hints, with "between 25% and 47%" discarded depending on topic. The review separately cites evidence that AI-generated hints matched human-generated hints in effectiveness.

"Pardos and Bhandari [56] required human experts to review GPT-3.5-generated hints before implementation, ultimately discarding between 25% and 47% of generated hints, depending on the topic."
