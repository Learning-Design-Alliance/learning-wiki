## Evidence

### Sarah Mehrotra 2021

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

State-by-state descriptive comparison of Black versus non-Black student enrollment in schools with greater than 10% uncertified teachers, using CRDC 2017-18 data. The report finds the disparity in "32 of the 41 states," citing Massachusetts (32% vs 13%), Mississippi (25% vs 6%), and Maryland (20% vs nearly 3%).

> "In 32 of the 41 states included in this analysis, Black students are more likely to attend schools with high percentages of uncertified teachers than their non‑Black peers."
