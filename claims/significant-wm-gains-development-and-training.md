## Evidence

### Bruno Sauce 2021

Bruno Sauce, John Wiedenhoeft, Nicholas Judd and Torkel Klingberg. (2021). Change by challenge: A common genetic basis behind childhood cognitive development and cognitive training. npj Science of Learning 6:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00096-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of the developmental sample (n = 160, ages 6–25) with WM assessed twice at a 2-year interval; baseline subtracted from follow-up. The article reports "β = 0.60, p < 0.001" and a "mean increase of 14% (±2.5)".

> "This showed that, regardless of starting age, participants had a signiﬁcant longitudinal increase in their WM after 2 years ( β = 0.60, p < 0.001), with a mean increase of 14% (±2.5)."

### Bruno Sauce 2021 (2)

Bruno Sauce, John Wiedenhoeft, Nicholas Judd and Torkel Klingberg. (2021). Change by challenge: A common genetic basis behind childhood cognitive development and cognitive training. npj Science of Learning 6:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00096-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the training sample (n = 184, mean 24.7 days of Cogmed WM training, SD = 1.06), comparing baseline (days 2–3) to end-of-intervention performance. The article reports "β = 2.10, p < 0.001" and a "mean increase of 34% (±1.5)".

> "Training led to a large average improvement in the performance of the trained WM tasks at the end of the intervention ( β = 2.10, p < 0.001), with a mean increase of 34% (±1.5)."
