Descriptive report by the authors across all deployed Planeterrellas (abstract and Section 3.1 estimates). The authors state "about 50,000 people in Europe have been able to see live demonstrations of the formation of auroral lights", with nine copies in four countries; many more saw TV broadcasts, for which no count is printed.

> "Today, nine Planeterrellas are operating or under construction in four diﬀerent countries, and more are in the pipleline. In ﬁve years, about 50,000 people in Europe have been able to see live demonstrations of the formation of auroral lights, picture the space environment and get an introduction to space weather with this experiment."
