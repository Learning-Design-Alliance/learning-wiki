# AI assistance impairs unassisted performance and persistence in reading comprehension, replicating the fraction-task effects in a second domain

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In reading comprehension, the AI condition solved fewer unassisted test problems than controls (Cohen's d = -0.42). [→ Grace Liu 2026](#grace-liu-2026)
`q3 i2` The AI condition also skipped more test problems than controls (Cohen's d = 0.42). [→ Grace Liu 2026 (2)](#grace-liu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Grace Liu 2026

Grace Liu, Brian Christian, Tsvetomira Dumbalska, Michiel A. Bakker, Rachit Dubey. (2026). AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.04721](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.04721)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

RCT (201 recruited, 168 analyzed) replicating the design with SAT reading-comprehension questions; AI condition used a GPT-5 sidebar for 5 problems, then 3 unassisted. Solve-rate impairment replicated, "Cohen's d=− 0.42".

> "Participants in the AI condition had a lower solve rate (mean 0.76, s.d. 0.34) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.89, s.d. 0.25; t(166) =− 2.72, P= 0.007; Cohen's d=− 0.42, 95% CI [−0.73,−0.11])."

### Grace Liu 2026 (2)

Grace Liu, Brian Christian, Tsvetomira Dumbalska, Michiel A. Bakker, Rachit Dubey. (2026). AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same reading-comprehension RCT; answers given in under 5 seconds were also counted as skipped. The AI condition's higher skip rate, "Cohen's d= 0.42", suggests reduced persistence in reading tasks.

> "also had a higher skip rate (mean 0.08, s.d. 0.21) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.01, s.d. 0.08; t(166) = 2.69, P= 0.008; Cohen's d= 0.42, 95% CI [0.11, 0.72])."
