`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A post-training survey of the 10 VR-group participants used six statements on a 5-point Likert scale; mean scores ranged from 3.8 to 4.5, with tactile feedback lowest at 3.2 ± 1.2. The survey was not formally validated.

> "Notably, 90% agreed or strongly agreed that VR improved autonomous learning ability, and 80% reported enhanced learning interest and interactivity. However, 20% disagreed that VR provided realistic tactile feedback, suggesting a need for supplementary hands-on practice."
