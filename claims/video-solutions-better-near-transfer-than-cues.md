## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2015

Tianlong Zu, Elise Agra, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2015). Effect of visual cues and video solutions on conceptual tasks. 2015 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher's Exact Test on correctness rates of N=33 students randomly assigned to three training conditions (Cue + Video, Video Only, Cue Only), each solving two problem sets with initial, near and far transfer tasks. The article reports "significant differences between the three groups" on near transfer, χ2 (2, 66) = 9.21, p = .008; no effect size is printed.

> "For the near transfer problem, we found significant differences between the three groups, χ2 (2, 66) = 9.21, p = .008. Both the Video + Cue and Video Only groups contributed to the significant difference, as determined by the adjusted residuals."
