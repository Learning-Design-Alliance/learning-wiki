Evidence¶

Bloemer 2017¶

Bloemer, W., Day, S., & Swan, K. (2017). Gap analysis: An innovative look at gateway courses and student retention. Online Learning, 21(3), 5-14. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v21i3.1233

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Among courses with average DFW rates in the four-year institutional records, one course's DFW students persisted only 2% below comparable students rather than the expected ~18% drop; "Students with a DFW grade in this course did not show the expected drop in persistence of about 18%, but rather persistence fell by only 2%." The authors call this an intriguing possibility for "guardrail" courses.
