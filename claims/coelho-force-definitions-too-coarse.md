## Evidence

### Lars Rikard Stavrum 2015

Lars Rikard Stavrum, Berit Bungum, Jonas R. Persson. (2015). “Never at rest”: developing a conceptual framework for definitions of 'force' in physics textbooks. Programme for Teacher Education, Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.06562](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.06562)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A qualitative content-analytic finding: applying Coelho's three definition types to the textbook corpus showed they did not suffice, and new categories were added, refined and adjusted, some divided into subcategories, yielding the eight-category framework. The analysis demonstrated these definitions are "too coarse".

> "The analysis demonstrated that these definitions are too coarse and fail to take the gradual development of the concept in textbooks into account."
