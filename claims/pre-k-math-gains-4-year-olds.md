# The Pre-K Mathematics intervention produces greater math knowledge gains in 4-year-olds than business-as-usual control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 4-year-olds who received Pre-K Mathematics (I-2 and I-1 conditions) showed greater gains in the pre-K year than control children (C condition), on the CMA (ES=1.08) and TEMA-3 (ES=.59). [→ Starkey et al. 2013](#starkey-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### Starkey et al. 2013

Prentice Starkey, Alice Klein, & Lydia DeFlorio. (2013). Changing the Developmental Trajectory in Early Math Through a Two-Year Preschool Math Intervention. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized comparison of treatment (I-2 and I-1) versus control 4-year-olds in Head Start classrooms, assessed on the CMA and TEMA-3; printed effect sizes were "ES=1.08" and "ES=.59".

> "It was found that greater gains in math knowledge were experienced by 4-year-old treatment children (I-2 and I-1 conditions) in the pre-K year than by 4-year-olds who did not receive this intervention (C condition). This was found both on the CMA (ES=1.08) and the TEMA-3 (ES=.59)"
