Evidence¶

HEW Khe Foon; LO Chung Kwan 2018¶

HEW Khe Foon; LO Chung Kwan. (2018). Flipped classroom improves student learning in health professions education: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1144-z

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analyses using the random-effects model across the 28 studies. The article reports no significant variation for research design (Q = 0.52, p = 0.77), student equivalence (Q = 0.43, p = 0.81), or instructor equivalence (Q = 4.72, p = 0.09).

"Experiments where students were randomly assigned to flipped classrooms produced results that were indistinguishable from quasi-experiments and historical controls."
