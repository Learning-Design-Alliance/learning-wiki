# AG measure largely does not moderate goal–performance relationships, indicating the domain moderation cannot be explained by the measure used

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` The overall correlations between each achievement goal and performance were not qualified by AG measure, so the observed domain moderation cannot be attributed to the AG measure utilized. [→ Van Yperen NW 2014](#van-yperen-nw-2014)
`q4 i0` Undifferentiated performance goals (Button et al. measure) show no correlation with performance (r = -.02, p = .33), consistent with opposite-valence PAp and PAv effects averaging to zero. [→ Van Yperen NW 2014 (2)](#van-yperen-nw-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Van Yperen NW 2014

Van Yperen NW, Blaga M, Postmes T (2014). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Reported Achievement Goals and Nonself-Report Performance across Three Achievement Domains (Work, Sports, and Education). PLoS ONE 9(4): e93594. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0093594

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analyses across all established AG measures (Tables 3–6). The negative relationships for PAv and MAv goals held regardless of whether items were framed as standards, reasons, or negative affect.

> "The overall correlations between either achievement goal and performance (see Tables 3–6) were not qualified by AG measure."

### Van Yperen NW 2014 (2)

Van Yperen NW, Blaga M, Postmes T (2014). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Reported Achievement Goals and Nonself-Report Performance across Three Achievement Domains (Work, Sports, and Education). PLoS ONE 9(4): e93594. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0093594

`q4 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Separate meta-analysis of the 12 samples using the Button et al. measure, whose performance subscale confounds PAp and PAv goals. The article reports "the observed correlation between undifferentiated performance goals and performance attainment was r = 2.02, p = .33".

> "the observed correlation between undifferentiated performance goals and performance attainment was r = 2.02, p = .33"
