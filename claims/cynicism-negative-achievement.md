# Cynicism is significantly negatively related to academic achievement (rc+ = −.24)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Madigan 2020

Madigan, D. J. & Curran, T. (2020). Does burnout affect academic achievement? A meta-analysis of over 100,000 students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09533-1

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Robust variance random-effects meta-analysis pooling cynicism–achievement correlations. The pooled corrected correlation was "rc+ = -.24, 95% CI = -.32, -.16"; heterogeneity was small (τ2 = .02, I2 = 91.80).

> "For cynicism (N = 10,888), analyses revealed a small-to-medium negative weighted mean effect size (rc+ = -.24, 95% CI = -.32, -.16)."
