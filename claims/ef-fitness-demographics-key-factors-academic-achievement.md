## Evidence

### Sun Zhiyuan 2024

Sun Zhiyuan, Yuan Yunhao, Xiong Xuan, Meng Shuqiao, Shi Yifan, Chen Aiguo. (2024). Predicting academic achievement from the collaborative influences of executive function, physical fitness, and demographic factors among primary school students in China: ensemble learning methods. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-17769-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RFECV feature-selection analysis of 300 primary school students (151 boys, 149 girls) assessed on executive function tasks, CNSPFS physical fitness tests, and standardized exam scores. The article reports these seven factors were "considered key factors by no less than half of the machine learning algorithms." No effect sizes are printed.

> "The results indicated that sex, body mass index, muscle strength, cardiorespiratory function, inhibition, working memory, and shifting were considered key factors by no less than half of the machine learning algorithms. Additionally, the shifting aspect of executive function was identified as the most important factor by most algorithms."
