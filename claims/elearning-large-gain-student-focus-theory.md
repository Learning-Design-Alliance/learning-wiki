# Medical students' theoretical FOCUS competencies rose significantly from pre-test to post-test, with a large pre-post difference, in the student group of a controlled study after e-learning preparation

## Evidence

### Ruppert Johannes 2025

Ruppert Johannes, Krüger Rebecca, Göbel Sebastian, Wolfhard Susanna, Lorenz Liv-Annebritt, Weimer Andreas Michael, Kloeckner Roman, Waezsada Elias, Buggenhagen Holger, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Weimer Johannes Matthias. (2025). The effectiveness of e-learning in focused cardiac ultrasound training: a prospective controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07409-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre-post analysis of the student study group (n=48) in a prospective controlled study, using paired t-tests on theory test scores. The gain was "significant increase in total competencies" with Cohen's d = 3.43, 95% CI [2.68, 4.18].

> "From T1 to T2, the study group achieved a significant increase in total competencies (T1: 23.5 ± 6.3 vs. T2: 62.1 ± 14.6; p < 0.001) with a large effect (Cohen’s d = 3.43, 95% CI [2.68, 4.18])"
