Chenguang Pan 2026¶

Chenguang Pan, Airui Meng, and Youmi Suk. (2026). Distilling Black-Box Machine Learning into a Small, Self-Explaining Language Model for Learning Analytics. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21165

q2 · i? · design · r2

Empirical analysis of the nationally representative HSLS:09 dataset with an analytical sample of 9,167 students, estimating the effect of AP/IB math coursework on four-year college enrollment using an X-learner. "The estimated effect declines monotonically across quartiles of both leading moderators." Table 5 shows Q1−Q4 gaps of +0.156 (SES) and +0.141 (prior math achievement); fANOVA variance shares are prior math 0.17, SES 0.13, expectations 0.05, climate 0.04. No standardised effect size is printed.

"The estimated effect declines monotonically across quartiles of both leading moderators (Table 5), so the advanced mathematics benefit most the students who are less likely to enroll in college."
