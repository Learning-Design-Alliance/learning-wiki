## Evidence

### Kim Erin 2025

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis analysis of the 5-item GRS and 3-point final competency rating across the same 52-user cohort by experience level. The article reports P < 0.001 for both components; no effect size is printed for these comparisons.

> "The GRS summed scores (N = 13.0 vs I = 16.9 vs E = 18.6; P < 0.001) and the final ratings (N = 1.27 vs I = 1.64 vs E = 1.69; P < 0.001) effectively discriminated the experience levels."
