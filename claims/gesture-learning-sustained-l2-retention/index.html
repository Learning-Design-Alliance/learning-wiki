Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020¶

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review attributes this to Mayer et al., reporting neurocognitive differences between L2 vocabulary learning with gestures (activating superior temporal sulcus and premotor areas) and without gestures (activating right lateral occipital cortex only). The review offers no effect size for the behavioral retention difference.
