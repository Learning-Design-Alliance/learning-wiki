# All four ANTS domains (task management, team working, situation awareness, decision-making) correlate with technical performance under stressors but not under control conditions

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Krage Ralf 2017

Krage Ralf, Zwaan Laura, Tjon Soei Len Lian, Kolenbrander Mark W, van Groeningen Dick, Loer Stephan A, Wagner Cordula, Schober Patrick. (2017). Relationship between non-technical skills and technical performance during cardiopulmonary resuscitation: does stress have an influence? Emergency Medicine Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/emermed-2016-205754

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Domain-level Pearson correlations (table 3) in the 30-participant cross-over simulator study. Under stressors, domain correlations ranged from r=0.43 (decision-making) to r=0.63 (task management and team working); under control conditions none was significant.

> "Likewise, all scores of the domains of the ANTS framework showed a significant positive relationship with the technical performance when additional stressors were present, but not under control conditions (table 3)."
