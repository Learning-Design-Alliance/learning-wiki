## Evidence

### Linn Zayar 2022

Linn Zayar, Tashiro Yasura, Morio Kunimasa, Hori Hiroki. (2022). Peer evaluations of group work in different years of medical school and academic achievement: how are they related?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03165-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis in the 340-student cohort for the contribution-to-discussion domain. The discussion notes the book review correlation was "very weak or negligible" while PBL discussion scores were significantly related to achievement.

> "Paper test scores were found to be correlated with peer evaluation scores for contribution to group discussion in book review sessions (rs = 0.146, p = 0.007) and to those in PBL sessions (rs = 0.515, p < 0.001)"
