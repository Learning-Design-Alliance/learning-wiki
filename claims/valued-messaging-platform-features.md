### Juan J. Merelo 2023

Juan J. Merelo, Pedro A. Castillo, Antonio M. Mora, Francisco Barranco, Noorhan Abbas, Alberto Guillén and Olia Tsivitanidou. (2023). Chatbots and messaging platforms in the classroom: an analysis from the teacher's perspective. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Second survey (N=205) feature ratings in Table 8: sustainability 76.6%, officiality 73.2%, horizontality 65.4%, familiarity 59.0% positive; analytics (52.7%), connectivity (58.0%) and hidden phone number (55.1%) split responses roughly evenly.

> "However, for the features: familiarity, horizontally, sustainability and oﬃciality the majority provided a positive response, designating their preference to these features."

### Juan J. Merelo 2023 (2)

Juan J. Merelo, Pedro A. Castillo, Antonio M. Mora, Francisco Barranco, Noorhan Abbas, Alberto Guillén and Olia Tsivitanidou. (2023). Chatbots and messaging platforms in the classroom: an analysis from the teacher's perspective. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same second-survey feature ratings (Table 8): official training 20.5%, pluggability 31.7%, unidirectionality 13.2% and synchrony 22.0% positive, so majorities rejected these features. Significant chi-square associations among features are reported in Table 9.

> "On the other hand, the majority of teachers seems to consider not valuable the presence of oﬃcial formation, pluggability, unidirectionality and synchrony in the interaction media features of messaging apps."
