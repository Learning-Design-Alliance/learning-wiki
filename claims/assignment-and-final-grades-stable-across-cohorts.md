### Boxuan Ma 2026

Boxuan Ma, Huiyong Li, Gen Li, Li Chen, Cheng Tang, Atsushi Shimada, and Shin'ichi Konomi. (2026). Three Years with Classroom AI in Introductory Programming: Shifts in Student Awareness, Interaction, and Performance. [doi:10.1145/3862769](https://doi.org/10.1145/3862769)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cohort-level comparison of averaged weekly assignment scores against a pre-GenAI baseline offering (n=39, early 2023) and three AI-enabled cohorts (Table 2). The article reports "no statistically significant differences across cohorts (H= 3.23,p= 0.357)"; no effect size printed.

> "assignment scores were consistently high and tightly clustered across cohorts. A Kruskal–Wallis test found no statistically significant differences across cohorts (H= 3.23,p= 0.357)."
