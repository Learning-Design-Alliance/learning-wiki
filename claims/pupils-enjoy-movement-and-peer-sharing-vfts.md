## Evidence

### Norris E. 2015

Norris E., Shelton N., Dunsmuir S., Duke-Williams O., Stamatakis E. (2015). Teacher and pupil perspectives on the use of Virtual Field Trips as physically active lessons. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1698-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Three focus groups (two Year 4, n=12; one Year 5, n=6; nine boys, nine girls) discussed a sample Olympic-themed VFT. All pupils commented on VFTs as an opportunity to have "fun" with peers and suggested rotating teams to socialise; some felt tired after the demonstration.

> "Pupils indicated familiarity with Google Earth software but described liking the novelty of using their bodies during the lesson and to answer questions."
