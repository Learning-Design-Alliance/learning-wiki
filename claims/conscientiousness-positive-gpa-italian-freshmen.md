## Evidence

### Corazzini Luca 2021

Corazzini Luca, D'Arrigo Silvia, Millemaci Emanuele, Navarra Pietro. (2021). The influence of personality traits on university performance: Evidence from Italian freshmen students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258586

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression of standardized GPA on standardized Big Five scores for 3,242 freshmen at the University of Messina, with controls for demographics, school background, parental characteristics and field of study; conscientiousness coefficient 0.093 (p < .001) in that specification, and 0.092 when residence controls are added. The article reports "an increase of 9.3 percent of a standard deviation in GPA."

> "Specifically, one standard deviation increase in conscientiousness is associated with an increase of 9.3 percent of a standard deviation in GPA."
