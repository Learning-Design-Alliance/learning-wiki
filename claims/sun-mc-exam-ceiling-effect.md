### Batiza Ann Finney 2013

Batiza Ann Finney, Gruhl Mary, Zhang Bo, Harrington Tom, Roberts Marisa, LaFlamme Donna, Haasch Mary Anne, Knopp Jonathan, Vogt Gina, Goodsell David, Hagedorn Eric, Marcey David, Hoelzer Mark, Nelson Dave. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classical test theory item analysis of posttest responses from the treatment group (n = 19) computed item difficulty and discrimination. The analysis found "14 of them were answered correctly by almost all teachers," and the authors state they identified items with unsatisfactory discrimination.

> "Overall, these items were quite easy for teachers after the workshop. Out of 25 items, 14 of them were answered correctly by almost all teachers. Even the hardest item received 13 correct responses out of 19 teachers."
