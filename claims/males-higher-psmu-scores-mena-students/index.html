Narrative synthesis of five studies examining gender differences in PSMU. The review reports males "scored 4.52 points higher on the BSMAS" in one study and a 2.10-point mean difference in another; no standardized effect sizes are printed.

"Alfaya et al. (2023) reported that they scored 4.52 points higher on the BSMAS, Alhrahsheh and Al Majali (2023) reported a mean difference of 2.10 points in their favor, and Azizi et al. (2019) observed significantly higher scores across all BSMAS subscales"
