# Mental differences between bright and dull pupils can be ascertained by means of simple group tests

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Group tests administered in several high schools and grades reveal measurable mental differences between pupils selected as most and least successful by school marks. [→ Pyle 1917](#pyle-1917)

## Evidence

### Pyle 1917

Pyle, W. H. (1917). A Psychological Study of Bright and Dull Pupils. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q2 · i?`

Comparative group testing across six Missouri schools, with bright and dull groups selected by school marks or teacher judgment. The author concludes from the combined tables that "the mental differences between bright and dull pupils can be ascertained by means of simple group tests."

> "A study of the results shown in the preceding tables makes it clear that the mental differences between bright and dull pupils can be ascertained by means of simple group tests."
