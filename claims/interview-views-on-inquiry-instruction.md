## Evidence

### Ceylan ŞEN 2016

Ceylan ŞEN, Gülşah SEZEN VEKLİ. (2016). The Impact of Inquiry Based Instruction on Science Process Skills and Self-efficacy Perceptions of Pre-service Science Teachers at a University Level Biology Laboratory. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(3): 603-612. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040319

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with all 12 experimental-group participants in week 15, analyzed in NVivo with expert-checked coding. Positive opinions included that lectures were "more fun" and participants "more active"; negatives included that filling sheets was "very tiring and time-consuming".

> "When the difficulties encountered in the application of Inquiry based instruction approach are taken into consideration, it is seen that pre-service science teachers have negative opinions about this method because of its time-consuming nature and the challenge it brings about for accessing reliable resources."
