## Evidence

### Levin 2025

Levin, O., Frei-Landau, R., & Goldberg, C. (2025). "No pain, no gain": Simulation-based learning in teacher education: The mediating role of simulation hindrances. PLoS ONE, 20(1), e0317255. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317255

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null SEM finding in the N = 370 sample (16.8% actors, 83.2% observers). The article reports "no significant relationship was found"; no effect size was printed, so the null indicates a non-detected effect, not a measured small one. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Furthermore, no significant relationship was found between the participants' role in the simulation, their simulation-related hindrance, or their learning outcomes (i.e., communication skills or collaborative learning insights)."
