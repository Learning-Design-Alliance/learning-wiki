## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Class-average perceived teacher support in Grade 5 positively predicts the change in class-level mathematics interest (β = .46, p = .003). [→ Lazarides 2018](#lazarides-2018)
`q3 i0` Class-average mathematics interest in Grade 5 does not significantly predict change in perceived teacher support (β = .09, p = .59). [→ Lazarides 2018 (2)](#lazarides-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Lazarides 2018

Lazarides, R., Gaspard, H., & Dicke, A.-L. (2018). Dynamics of classroom motivation: Teacher enthusiasm and the development of math interest and teacher support. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.012

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel latent change Model 3 testing bidirectional paths at the classroom level; the teacher-driven path is significant with printed β = .46, SE = .15, p = .003.

> "the class average student-perceived teacher support in Grade 5 was significantly and positively related to the class average change in the students' mathematics interest (β = .46, SE = .15, p = .003)."

### Lazarides 2018 (2)
