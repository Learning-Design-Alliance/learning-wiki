# Chapter outline video usage showed a consistent negative correlation with E&M exam performance (β = -0.084 to -0.109)

> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among the supplemental videos, only the chapter outline videos showed a statistically significant effect on exam performance, and it was negative across all three models. [→ James K. Hirons 2025](#james-k-hirons-2025)

## Evidence

### James K. Hirons 2025

James K. Hirons, Jonathan D. Perry, Dawson T. Nodurft, Scott Crawford, William Bassichis, and Tatiana L. Erukhimova. (2025). Click, Watch, Learn: The Impact of Student Self-Study Materials on Physics E&M Course Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression on 1368 students; outline-video usage had β = -0.084, p < 0.001, and remained negative in the log model (β = -0.106) and hybrid model (β = -0.109). The authors call this an unexpected result.

> "Among the supplemental videos, only the Chapter outline videos showed a statistically significant effect, with a negative correlation ( β = -0.084)."
