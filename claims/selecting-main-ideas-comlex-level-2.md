Pearson correlational analysis of LASSI subscales and COMLEX-USA Level 2 scores for Cohorts 2024–2026 (n = 278) found a statistically significant correlation for Selecting Main Ideas; the article reports "r = 0.16, p = 0.007".

> "Statistically significant correlations were found between Selecting Main Ideas and COMLEX-USA Level 2 (r = 0.16, p = 0.007, n = 278); and Information Processing and COMLEX-USA Level 3 (r = 0.40, p = 0.014, n = 62)."
