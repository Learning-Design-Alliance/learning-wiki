`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis hypothesis test comparing experimental (scaffolding) and control (expository approach) classes on conceptual understanding of electromagnetism. Hcal = 1.95 fell on the right side of the critical value at α = 0.90, df = 1, so H0 was rejected. No effect size is attached to this test statistic.
