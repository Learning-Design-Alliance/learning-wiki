Mary Walsh, Anastasia Raczek, Erin Sibley, Terrence Lee-St. John, Chen An, Bercem Akbayin, Eric Dearing, Claire Foley. (2015). City Connects: Building an Argument for Effects on Student Achievement with a Quasi-Experimental Design. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 used two-level weighted linear regression models with propensity score weights applied at the student level to predict report card scores in Grades 3 to 5. The article reports positive impacts on "some of the report card measures in Grade 4 and on most report card scores in Grade 5." No effect size is printed for these findings.
