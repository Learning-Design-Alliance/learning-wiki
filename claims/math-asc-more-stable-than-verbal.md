## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within five studies measuring both domains, verbal ASC showed a significantly higher proportion of state variance than mathematical ASC (Δverbal-math = 0.10, p < .01). [→ Jansen et al. 2020](#jansen-et-al-2020)
`q2 i?` Mathematical ASC showed a significantly higher model-implied 1-year retest correlation than verbal ASC in each study and in the pooled estimate (Δverbal-math = −.11, p < .01). [→ Jansen et al. 2020 (2)](#jansen-et-al-2020-2)
