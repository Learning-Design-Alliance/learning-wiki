## Evidence

### Mareike Ehlert 2025

Mareike Ehlert, Carola Grunschel, Friederike Koehler. (2025). The Role of Teachers' Emotions and Their Assessment in Professional Development Research: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10048-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of temporal focus across 25 studies: retrospective (n = 10), current (n = 8), a combination of time points (n = 5), all three (n = 2), and prospective (n = 0). The review reports the absence of prospective assessment as a gap in understanding how anticipated emotions influence future engagement in PD.

> "No study focused exclusively on examining prospective emotions that teachers might anticipate feeling in their PD experience."
