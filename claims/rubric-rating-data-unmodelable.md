## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young 2021

Nicholas T. Young, N. Verboncoeur, Dao Chi Lam, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2021). Rubric-based holistic review represents a change from traditional graduate admissions approaches in physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.06886](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.06886)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional inference forest on rubric ratings generated by faculty for 321 domestic applicants (43% admitted) in 2018-2020, with all 18 rubric features treated as categorical. The authors note the null accuracy is smaller than for data set 1a because applicants with incomplete applications tended to be not admitted.

> "We ﬁnd that the testing AUC was 0.664 ± 0.007 and the testing accuracy was 0.675 ± 0.007 (null accuracy 0.553± 0.006)."
