## Evidence

### Zamani F 2025

Zamani F, Dehghani A, Eslami Akbar R, Heydari H. (2025). Effect of Teaching Intervention Based on Health Action Process Approach on Self-care behavior in Older People with Hypertension: A Quasi-experimental Study. International Journal of Community Based Nursing and Midwifery. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12048912/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 70 older adults with hypertension (35 intervention, 35 control) at an Iranian health services center, measured with the Hypertension Self-care Profile at baseline and 2 months. The intervention group's median rose from 50 to 73 while the control stayed at 50; the article reports "significantly different between the intervention" and control groups (P<0.001).

> "Two months after the intervention, the median of self-care was significantly different between the intervention (median: 73(IQR 74-71)) and control groups (median: 50(IQR 54-45)) (P<0.001)."
