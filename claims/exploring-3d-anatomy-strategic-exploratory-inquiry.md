## Evidence

### Zhang J 2026

Zhang J, Sachdeva M, McCaig C, Gordon E, Shapiro L, Keenan ID. (2026). Exploring 3D anatomy: A free online course supporting novice learner inquiry and spatial conceptualization. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70288

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interpretative phenomenological analysis of two focus groups (n = 8 total) constructed the Inquiry theme as a dichotomy or spectrum between Strategic and Exploratory sub-themes. One participant noted that with limited time, students "do want to be doing things that ultimately are going to help you pass your exams," while another argued anatomy "should be something that you can work out" rather than "a rote learning exercise."

> ""Strategic inquiry" focused on anatomy knowledge in absolute and objective terms and described learning approaches that participants considered would directly improve their assessment performance and progression."
