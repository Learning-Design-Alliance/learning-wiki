Rajhans Vidyut, Memon Usman, Patil Vidula, Goyal Aditya. (2020). Impact of COVID-19 on academic activities and way forward in Indian Optometry. Journal of Optometry 13(4). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optom.2020.06.002

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of parallel findings from the 2018 and 2020 surveys (Table 4). The 2018 survey found 30 of 44 (82.5%) were teacher-centric; the 2020 survey found "62 out of 73 (84.93%) considered consulting students before switching to e-learning mode". The authors state no statistical comparison was calculated.
