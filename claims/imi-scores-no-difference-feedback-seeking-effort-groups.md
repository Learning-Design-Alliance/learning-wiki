## Evidence

### Henry Duncan 2018

Henry Duncan, Vesel Travis, Boscardin Christy, van Schaik Sandrijn. (2018). Motivation for feedback-seeking among pediatric residents: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1253-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative ANOVA comparison of IMI sub-scores across three feedback-seeking groups (n=34; groups of 12, 12, 10) in a simulation-based team-training program. The article reports "no significant differences between groups on one way ANOVA (p > 0.2 for all comparisons)"; no effect size printed.

> "Overall, residents demonstrated moderate levels of intrinsic motivation (average subscale scores between 3.87 and 4.94, 1–7 scale) without significant differences between study groups."
