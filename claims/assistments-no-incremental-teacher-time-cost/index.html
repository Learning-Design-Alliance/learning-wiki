Evidence¶

Feng 2024¶

Feng, M., Weiser, G., & Collins, K. (2024). Cost and cost effectiveness of ASSISTments online math support: Analysis from a randomized controlled study in middle school. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655721

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of teachers' implementation logs and a postimplementation survey measuring self-reported instructional behavior. The report concludes "there is no incremental cost of teacher time for ASSISTments above business-as-usual instruction," reflected as negative teacher opportunity cost in Table 1.

"Statistically significant differences were observed in the amount of time dedicated to homework practices in the classroom and in planning time to support instruction."
