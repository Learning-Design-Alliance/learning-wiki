`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final-evaluation agreement data from a training study in which two internal medicine residents in Ghana each interpreted 20 randomly selected studies compared to a paediatric cardiology fellow. The article reports "high agreement across all aspects" with the printed kappas and p-values listed above.

> "At the final evaluation, there was high agreement across all aspects: left ventricular structure (70%, kappa 0.52, p = 0.01), left ventricular function (80%, kappa 0.65, p = 0.004), right ventricular structure (90%, kappa 0.71, p = 0.002), right ventricular function (100%, kappa 1.00, p < 0.001), and presence of effusion (100%, kappa 1.00, p < 0.001)."
