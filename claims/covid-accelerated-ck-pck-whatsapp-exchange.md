### Zahava Scherz · Asaf Salman · Giora Alexandron · Yael Shwartz 2023

Zahava Scherz · Asaf Salman · Giora Alexandron · Yael Shwartz. (2023). WhatsApp Discourse Throughout COVID-19: Towards Computerized Evaluation of the Development of a STEM Teachers Professional Learning Community. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00320-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of 6599 L-PLC WhatsApp messages annotated by two experts (IRR > 95%) across four semesters, two pre- and two during COVID-19. The authors report "significantly higher" CK and PCK tags in cohorts III and IV vs pre-COVID cohorts, via chi-square comparisons (alpha set to 0.01); no effect size is printed.

> "the number of CK and PCK tags was significantly higher in cohorts III and IV compared to pre-COVID cohorts, suggesting that knowledge exchange was accelerated by the COVID-19 outbreak."
