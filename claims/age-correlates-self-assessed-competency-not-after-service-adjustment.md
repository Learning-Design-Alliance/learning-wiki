## Evidence

### Liang Zhanming 2018

Liang Zhanming, Blackstock Felicity C., Howard Peter F, Briggs David S, Leggat Sandra G, Wollersheim Dennis, Edvardsson David, Rahman Aziz. (2018). An evidence-based approach to understanding the competency development needs of the health service management workforce in Australia. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3760-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate and multivariate predictor analyses in the MCAP study; the article reports "correlation coefficients range: 0.25 to 0.35" for age, but "age ceased to be significantly related (p values range: 0.56 to 0.186)" after adjustment for length of service. Sex, service length, education and professional membership were not statistically associated.

> "Age was positively correlated with the means of the self-assessed six core competencies and the combined competency scores (correlation coefficients range: 0.25 to 0.35; p values ranging from 0.017 to 0.001)."
