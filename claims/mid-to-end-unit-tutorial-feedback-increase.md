## Evidence

### Posner Mareike G. 2023

Posner Mareike G., Dempsey Nina C., Unsworth Amanda J. (2023). The Benefits of Using Case Study Focussed, Problem Based Learning Approaches to Unit Design for Biomedical Science Students. British Journal of Biomedical Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10340524/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Within-cohort comparison of anonymised mid-unit versus end-of-unit Likert feedback for case study-based tutorials in the PBL-designed unit. The article reports 44% versus 69% selecting “Definitely agree”; these are descriptive percentages with no test statistic or effect size.

> "We also observed an improvement in student feedback regarding the case study-based tutorials from the “mid-unit” feedback to “end of unit” feedback surveys (44% (mid-unit) vs. 69% (end of unit) of students selecting “Definitely agree”)."
