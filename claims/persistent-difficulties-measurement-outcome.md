## Evidence

### Hu 2022

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with measurement uncertainty of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Across both years, some students stated on both pre- and post-test that measuring Sx in state |−x⟩ would yield an outcome of −ℏ/2|−x⟩. The authors note this difficulty was outside the CQS scope and may relate to conflating an operator's action with measurement.

> "It is interesting that these students’ answers remained the same on the pre-test and the post -test. This difficulty  did not fall within the scope of this CQS, but it was addressed in another CQS which will be reported upon in a future publication."
