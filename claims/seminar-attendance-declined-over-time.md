## Evidence

### Bouwmeester Rianne A. M. 2016

Bouwmeester Rianne A. M., de Kleijn Renske A. M., van Rijen Harold V. M. (2016). Peer-instructed seminar attendance is associated with improved preparation, deeper learning and higher exam scores: a survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0715-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive headcount data across the course's three organ-system topics, plus wiki sharing rates (90% Respiratory, 55% Circulatory, 37% Urinary), which varied between seminar groups from 98% to 43%. Teachers expressed concern about decreasing attendance.

> "Seventy-nine percent of the students attended both seminars concerning the Respiratory system, 71 % attended both seminars involving the Cardiovascular system, whereas 22 % (27 students) attended all three seminars involving the Urinary system."
