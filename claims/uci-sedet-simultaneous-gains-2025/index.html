Observational before-and-after comparison of official SEI-GDF statistics consolidated in Report No. 1/2026 of SEDET/GAB/UCI, comparing 2025 (first year under the method) with 2024. The article reports "simultaneous gains across all aggregate indicators", including dispatches rising from 102 to 163 (+60%).

"Average processing time fell from 34 days to 17 days (−50%). Processing capacity rose from 256 to 336 cases processed (+31%). Total documentary output grew from 251 to 419 documents (+67%). The output of the densest piece, the technical report, rose from 66 to 122 (+85%)"
