## Evidence

### Andrej Favia 2013

Andrej Favia, Neil F. Comins, Geoffrey L. Thorpe. (2013). The Elements of Item Response Theory and its Framework in Analyzing Introductory Astronomy College Student Misconceptions. I. Galaxies. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

PCA loadings (Figure 7b) show sA227 loads most strongly on the third factor with sA226; the authors interpret this as students reasoning about spatial distribution of stars rather than their brightness. No effect size is printed.

> "To our surprise, sA227, the misconception that we see all the stars that are in the Milky Way, is best correlated with sA226, the misconception that the galaxies are randomly distributed, than with any particular statement in Factor 1, most of which relate to visual properties of galaxies."
