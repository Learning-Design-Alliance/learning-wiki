Prospective cohort study evaluating GPs' ability to perform FLUS after a training program. Practical skills were assessed "under standardised conditions" during training using the Lung Ultrasound-Objective Structured Assessment of Ultrasound Skills score, with the printed range of 96% to 100% (median = 99%).

> "The GPs obtained LUS-OSAUS scores between 96% to 100% of max. score (median = 99%)."
