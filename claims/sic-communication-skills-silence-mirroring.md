Susanna Pusa, Rebecca Baxter, Sofia Andersson, Erik K. Fromme, Joanna Paladino, Anna Sandgren. (2024). Core Competencies for Serious Illness Conversations: An Integrative Systematic Review. Journal of Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/08258597241245022

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Thematic synthesis of included articles under the conversation-resources theme: the review reports that deep and active listening, intentional silence, open-ended questions, and emotion handling skills were described as necessary competencies, including that "Mirroring was a conversation skill" supporting patient reflection.

> "Mirroring was a conversation skill that clinicians could apply to support patients to articulate and reflect upon their thoughts and emotions."
