### Price 2024

Price, H., Burns, D., Loewe, S., Shields, P., Kaplan, J., & Lee, H. (2024). Long-term English learners in California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/496.998

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CALPADS enrollment data for 330,733 students designated LTEL7 compared to 1,780,481 other ever-ELs in 2022–23. The report states "boys were overrepresented among LTEL7 students at 56%" versus around 51% of other ever-ELs. No inferential test reported.

> "In terms of gender, the proportion of ever-ELs who were boys was similar to the overall California school population—at around 51%. however, boys were overrepresented among LTEL7 students at 56%."
