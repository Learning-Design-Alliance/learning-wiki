Random-effects moderator analysis over all effect measures combined; Table 1 reports per-domain effect sizes, with vocabulary (k = 31) and general language proficiency (k = 9) among the larger estimates and significant Q-statistics indicating heterogeneity for all outcomes. Listening, speaking and grammar were combined into one oral proficiency measure because they were rarely measured.
