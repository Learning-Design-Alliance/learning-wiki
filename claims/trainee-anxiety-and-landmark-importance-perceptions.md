### Liu Enci 2026

Liu Enci, Qin Junjie, Li Xiaoyu, Chi Zhanghuan, An Xiujun, Chen Junping, Jiang Jingyan, Lu Bo. (2026). Ultrasound reliance and landmark-based proficiency in vascular access training: a multicenter cross-sectional survey of anesthesia trainees and teaching faculty in Eastern China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09702-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trainee survey responses (n = 454) using a single non-validated five-level Likert item, with 'very worried' and 'worried' combined as reported anxiety. The article notes no validated scale such as the State-Trait Anxiety Inventory was used.

> "Among trainees, 43.17% (selecting ‘very worried’ or ‘worried’) reported anxiety about performing vascular access in hospitals without ultrasound equipment"

### Liu Enci 2026 (2)

Liu Enci, Qin Junjie, Li Xiaoyu, Chi Zhanghuan, An Xiujun, Chen Junping, Jiang Jingyan, Lu Bo. (2026). Ultrasound reliance and landmark-based proficiency in vascular access training: a multicenter cross-sectional survey of anesthesia trainees and teaching faculty in Eastern China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09702-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trainee survey responses (n = 454) on perceptions and preferences regarding vascular access, reported in Table 3. The article reports 411 of 454 trainees (90.53%) perceived necessity of landmark-based cannulation.

> "Most trainees reported that landmark-based cannulation remains necessary in current anesthesia practice (90.53%), and 85.24% supported strengthening landmark-based training during residency (Table 3)."
