# Weak initial organization enables experimentation with alternative graphical organizing principles

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Group 4, which applied no organizing principle during the first phase, could freely move cards and experiment with principles based on card type and contributor. [→ Overdijk 2008](#overdijk-2008)

## Evidence

### Overdijk 2008

Overdijk, M. & van Diggelen, W. (2008). Appropriation of a shared workspace: Organizing principles and their application. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9038-4

`q2 · i?`

Episode analysis of group 4 shows one student (Vera) introduced a graphical principle based on card type (arguments for the claim left, against right), then one based on contributor; the group ultimately settled on card-type organization, which was maintained until the end of the discussion.

> "Because the group applied a weak principle during the first phase of the discussion — notably, they used no links, they were able to freely move the cards through the workspace."
