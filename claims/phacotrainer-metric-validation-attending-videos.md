## Evidence

### Yeh Hsu-Hang 2025

Yeh Hsu-Hang, Sen Simmi, Chou Jonathan C., Christopher Karen L., Wang Sophia Y. (2025). PhacoTrainer: Automatic Artificial Intelligence-Generated Performance Ratings for Cataract Surgery. Translational Vision Science & Technology. https://doi.org/10.1167/tvst.14.5.2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

A validation analysis on newly collected attending videos compared metrics from manually labeled true positions with pipeline-generated metrics; the printed effect sizes are Pearson r = 0.988, r = 0.957, and r = 0.769: “strong correlations in the area, total path length, and max velocity metrics(Pearson correlation coefficient 0.988, 0.957, 0.769, respectively).”

> "Our analysis demonstrates strong correlations in the area, total path length, and max velocity metrics(Pearson correlation coefficient 0.988, 0.957, 0.769, respectively) (Supplementary Fig. S1)."
