### Torre Gibney 2024

Torre Gibney, T., Fong, P., Lewis, J., Maduli, D., & Castellano, M. (2024). The landscape of middle school career and technical education in Nebraska. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673552

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 2023/24 NDE course enrollment data. The report finds that "10 courses accounted for more than three-fourths (76%) of all middle school CTE enrollments", with Middle School Career Development at 43,212 enrollments. Individual course participation ranged from tens of thousands to an enrollment of one.

> "Specifically, 10 courses accounted for more than three-fourths (76%) of all middle school CTE enrollments. Middle School Career Development was by far the most popular course, with 43,212 enrollments, accounting for 22 percent of all enrollments."
