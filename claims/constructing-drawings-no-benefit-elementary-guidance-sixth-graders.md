## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2021

Garvin Brod. (2021). How Can We Make Active Learning Work in K–12 Education? Considering Prerequisites for a Successful Construction of Understanding. Psychological Science in the Public Interest. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100621997376

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports, citing Van Meter et al. (2006), a study of fourth- and sixth-graders that "found no benefit of actively drawing relative to inspecting provided drawings in a later problem-solving test."

> "a study in which fourth- and sixth-graders had to construct drawings corresponding to a biology text found no benefit of actively drawing relative to inspecting provided drawings in a later problem-solving test (Van Meter et al., 2006)"

### Garvin Brod 2021 (2)

Garvin Brod. (2021). How Can We Make Active Learning Work in K–12 Education? Considering Prerequisites for a Successful Construction of Understanding. Psychological Science in the Public Interest. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100621997376

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports that in the same drawing study, "additional guidance boosted problem-solving performance among the sixth-graders" but not the fourth-graders.

> "Whereas additional guidance boosted problem-solving performance among the sixth-graders, this was not the case for the fourth-graders (for similar findings, see Van Essen & Hamaker, 1990)"
