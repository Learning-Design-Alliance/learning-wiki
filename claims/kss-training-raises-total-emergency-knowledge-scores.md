### Hu Yaojia 2022

Hu Yaojia, Zheng Bingya, Zhu Lihui, Tang Shuo, Lu Qi, Song Qingqing, Zhang Na, Zhong Yan. (2022). The effectiveness of emergency knowledge training of pediatric medical workers based on the knowledge, skills, simulation model: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03267-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison in a controlled quasi-experimental study of 1448 hospital medical workers trained with the KSS model over 28 days; the results section reports the overall median score improvement, with the Wilcoxon Z of -29.57 and P = 0.00 shown in Table 6. The article reports "an overall improvement in median score".

> "Table 6 compares the scores for BLS, ALS, and other variables before and after the training, and shows an overall improvement in median score to between 75.00 (62.50, 85.00) and 100.00 (95.00, 100.00)."
