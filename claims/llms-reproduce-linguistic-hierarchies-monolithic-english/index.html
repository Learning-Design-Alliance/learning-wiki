Ugwuanyi 2025¶

Ugwuanyi, K., Mair, C., Dovchin, S., Erdocia, I., Kuteeva, M., & Airemionkhale, E. (2025). Generative AI and linguistic diversity in academic writing and publishing: Perspectives from World Englishes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28505 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28505

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Expert contribution to the structured scholarly dialogue: a sociolinguist working on linguistic diversity and AI states, from professional experience and cited literature, that LLMs "reproduce linguistic hierarchies" and produce monolithic English. No empirical test is reported in the article itself.

"As currently developed, LLMs reproduce linguistic hierarchies: they are built on dominant standard English varieties, often mainstream American English, and produce a monolithic version of English that ignores variation across other regional varieties of English."
