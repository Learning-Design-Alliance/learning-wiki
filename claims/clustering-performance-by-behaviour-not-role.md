## Evidence

### alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane 2026

alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane. (2026). Impact of real-time respiratory function monitoring on neonatal mask ventilation training: a multicentre simulation-based crossover study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004894

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory affinity-propagation clustering on standardised mean Vte and median mask leak, performed separately for hidden and visible conditions and shown in figures 1 and 2. The article reports clustering "showed behavioural heterogeneity, with participants grouped by ventilation performance rather than professional role"; in the term manikin high-leak clusters persisted across conditions.

> "The visual-RFM experiments redistributed participants toward low-leak, in-range performance profiles in the preterm model, indicating that the benefits of real-time feedback were observed across participants irrespective of role."
