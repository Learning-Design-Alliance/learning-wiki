## Evidence

### Teng 2015

Teng, F. (2015). Assessing the Relationship between Vocabulary Learning Strategy Use and Vocabulary Knowledge. PASAA, 49, 39-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077911

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Repeated two-tailed Pearson correlation among the four variables (direct and indirect strategies, depth and breadth of VK) for the 145 participants. High and positive intercorrelations existed among the four variables, with direct strategies correlating significantly and positively with indirect strategies (r=0.75).
