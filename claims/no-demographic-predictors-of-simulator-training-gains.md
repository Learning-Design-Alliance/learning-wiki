### Rahm Stefan 2018

Rahm Stefan, Wieser Karl, Bauer David E., Waibel Felix WA, Meyer Dominik C., Gerber Christian, Fucentese Sandro F. (2018). Efficacy of standardized training on a virtual reality simulator to advance knee and shoulder arthroscopic motor skills. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-018-2072-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of questionnaire demographic data against metric and ASSET scores of the final basic test in 20 residents. The article reports no correlations for any of the examined background variables; no correlation coefficients are printed for these null findings.

> "There were no positive or negative correlations of the data regarding previous real or simulated experience, the handedness, the sports activity and the video games experience to the improvement of the scores or the final score."
