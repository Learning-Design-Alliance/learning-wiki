## Evidence

### de Greeff 2018

de Greeff, J. W., Bosker, R. J., Oosterlaan, J., Visscher, C., & Hartman, E. (2018). Effects of physical activity on executive functions, attention and academic performance in preadolescent children: a meta-analysis. Journal of Science and Medicine in Sport, 21(5), 501-507. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsams.2017.09.595

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The meta-analysis of 31 intervention studies distinguished four executive function subdomains, three attention subdomains and three academic performance subdomains, and reports that "The effects did depend on the subdomain." No per-subdomain effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "The effects did depend on the subdomain."
