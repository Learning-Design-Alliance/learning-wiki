## Evidence

### Sayan 2015

Sayan, H. (2015). The effects of computer games on the achievement of basic mathematical skills. Educational Research and Reviews, 10(22), 2846-2853. [doi:10.5897/ERR2015.2172](https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2172)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The author's own interpretation identifies two reasons for the null finding: the small sample size (n=22 per group) and the control group's slightly higher pretest score. The author suggests that "if the number of the students is increased the results can tend to be positive for the experimental group".

> "We could not obtain any evidence if one group is better than the other for two reasons: First, the sample size is rather small (n=22); the experiment should be repeated using bigger sample size."
