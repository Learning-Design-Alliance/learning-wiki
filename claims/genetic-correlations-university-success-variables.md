# University success variables show moderate to high genetic correlations (rg up to 0.76), while non-shared environmental correlations are mainly low

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Multivariate twin analysis shows a high genetic correlation between entrance exam achievement and university quality (rg = 0.76) and a moderate genetic correlation between entrance exam achievement and university achievement (rg = 0.49). [→ Smith-Woolley 2018](#smith-woolley-2018)
`q3 i?` Non-shared environmental correlations between the university success variables are mainly non-overlapping (re = 0.03-0.09) except for a moderate correlation between entrance exam achievement and university quality (re = 0.35). [→ Smith-Woolley 2018 (2)](#smith-woolley-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Smith-Woolley 2018

Smith-Woolley, E., Ayorech, Z., Dale, P. S., von Stumm, S. & Plomin, R. (2018). The genetics of university success. Scientific Reports, 8, 14579. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-32621-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A correlated-factors multivariate twin model, the best fitting of three designs tested, estimated genetic correlations among the success variables; the article reports the "genetic correlation between entrance exam achievement and university quality (rg = 0.76)" and rg = 0.49 with university achievement. A genetic correlation is not a standardized individual-difference effect size.

> "As indicated by the genetic correlations (Table S11, part a), there was a high degree of genetic correlation between entrance exam achievement and university quality (rg = 0.76)."

### Smith-Woolley 2018 (2)

Smith-Woolley, E., Ayorech, Z., Dale, P. S., von Stumm, S. & Plomin, R. (2018). The genetics of university success. Scientific Reports, 8, 14579. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-32621-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same correlated-factors multivariate twin model estimated non-shared environmental correlations; the article reports correlations were "mainly non-overlapping for entrance exam achievement and university achievement (re = 0.03)" and re = 0.09 between quality and achievement.

> "Non-shared environmental correlations (Table S11, part c) were moderate between entrance exam achievement and university quality (re = 0.35), however they were mainly non-overlapping for entrance exam achievement and university achievement (re = 0.03) and between university quality and university achievement (re = 0.09)."
