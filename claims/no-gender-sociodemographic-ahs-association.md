Thematic synthesis for Research Question 2. The review reports that of five studies examining gender, "none found statistically significant associations", and studies of race, age, native language, school location, department, study preference and ethnicity also established no significant relationships.

> "Only five studies explicitly examined the relationship between gender and AHS (Astatke, 2018; Karabıyık, 2020; Liu, 2016; Onivehu et al., 2018; Tercanlioglu, 2005), and none found statistically significant associations."
