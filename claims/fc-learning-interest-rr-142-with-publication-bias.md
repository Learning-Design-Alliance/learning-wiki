## Evidence

### Zheng Hui 2026

Zheng Hui, Ouyang Yun, Zeng Yifei, He Xinyu, Song Liping, Yu Xiangyuan. (2026). Systematic evaluation of the teaching effectiveness of flipped classroom mode in undergraduate preventive medicine courses in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896815

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Fixed-effects meta-analysis of 10 studies (low heterogeneity, I2 = 13.7%) comparing learning interest in FC vs LBL, reporting "a pooled RR of 1.42 (95% CI: 1.33–1.53)"; no standardized effect size was printed, so no magnitude is coded. A second analysis found "significant publication bias was detected for learning interest (PBegg’s = 0.049)", suggesting possible overestimation.

> "The results showed that the FC group had significantly greater learning interest than the LBL group, with a pooled RR of 1.42 (95% CI: 1.33–1.53), indicating that FC increased students’ learning interest by about 42%"
