Elsamanoudy Ayman; Alhazimi Awdah; Hassanien Mohammed; Almarabheh Amer; Al-Aqidi Sultan; Alwalidi Raghad; Alrefaie Zienab zeinab. (2026). Implementation and evaluation of customized artificial intelligence personal tutor (chat GPT physiology companion) for medical students in the reproduction module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09088-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Formative assessment perception items (n = 47). The article reports "93.6% agreed or strongly agreed that the quality and types of questions offered by the GPT companion were good (mean = 4.28, median = 4)" and an identical percentage felt it enhanced problem-solving and critical thinking skills (mean = 4.23); 89.4% rated post-quiz feedback as very useful (mean = 4.38).

> "A strong majority of students (93.6%) agreed or strongly agreed that the quality and types of questions offered by the GPT companion were good (mean = 4.28, median = 4), and an identical percentage felt that the companion enhanced their problem-solving and critical thinking skills (mean = 4.23)."
