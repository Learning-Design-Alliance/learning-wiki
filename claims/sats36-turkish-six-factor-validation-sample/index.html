Evidence¶

Serhat Aydın 2016¶

Serhat Aydın. (2016). Using Excel in Teacher Education for Sustainability. Journal of Teacher Education for Sustainability, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1515/jtes-2016-0017

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis of pretest SATS-36 responses from the 102 pre-service science teachers. The article prints sufficient F2/sd = 3,79, RMSEA = 0.06, GFI = 0.96, AGFI = 0,96 and CFI = 0,97; the six-factor solution was retained for comparability with the scale's reported structure.

"CFA confirmed 6 factor solution with sufficient overall goodness-of-fit parameters."

Serhat Aydın 2016 (2)¶

Serhat Aydın. (2016). Using Excel in Teacher Education for Sustainability. Journal of Teacher Education for Sustainability, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1515/jtes-2016-0017

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Reliability analysis of the SATS-36 in this sample; point estimates were "found between .72 and .85", which the article describes as acceptable to good internal reliability, with confidence intervals between [.72, .74] and [.84, .86].

"In this study Cronbachís alpha, Mc Donaldís omega and GLB point estimates were found between .72 and .85."
