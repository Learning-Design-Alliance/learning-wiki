Results from the first classroom use of the activity in August 2007 with 27 elementary education majors working in groups of 3-4. The author reports the consensus policy statements groups produced, noting "No group or individual stated that ID should be defined as science."

> "Most groups came to a statement along the lines of, “No, ID is not science.  Maybe teach in another area as an idea about origins, etc., but not in science class.”  No group or individual stated that ID should be defined as science."
