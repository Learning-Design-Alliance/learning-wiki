Related Claims¶

  • Impression narratives rely on weakly diagnostic suspicion heuristics such as response speed, fluency, and perceived scriptedness — related
  • Students tend to rely on non-diagnostic cues when judging their own performance, resulting in inaccurate judgments — related
  • Teachers judging student performance mostly use performance cues and, to a lesser extent, student cues — related
  • When making accurate judgments, teachers used diagnostic cues to a greater extent, but also used non-diagnostic cues (IQ and reading self-concept) — related
  • Teachers are largely aware which cues are diagnostic, but often mistakenly treat students' interest in the text topic as diagnostic — related
  • Representational similarity analysis shows identity judgments are organised around subjective impressions, orthogonal to diagnostic cue structure — related
  • Performance cues were found most diagnostic for students' reading comprehension performance — related
  • Judgments of comprehension in oral reading rest on unaided subjective judgment with no guarantee of consistency across times or judges — related
