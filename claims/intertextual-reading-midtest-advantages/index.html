Evidence¶

Akdal 2014¶

Akdal, D. & Şahin, A. (2014). The effects of intertextual reading approach on the development of creative writing skills. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 54, 171-186. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ejer

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Essays written after each of the three coursebook texts (The Foundation of the Union; Atatürk Became Children; The Old Holiday Fragrances) were scored as Midtest 1–3; the article reports the test group higher at every midtest on both dimensions. Scheffe comparisons in Tables 4 and 6 mark several contrasts p<.05.

"It was also found that midtest 1, midtest 2, and midtest 3 mean scores of the test group are higher than the mean scores of the control group and these differences are statistically significant."
