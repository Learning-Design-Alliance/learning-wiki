q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized trial in which 50 schools were assigned to four interventions and students within schools were randomly assigned to treatment or control; Table 2 shows a statistically significant Word Attack impact of 5.3 points for all interventions combined in the third-grade cohort. The report states the interventions "had impacts on phonemic decoding, word reading accuracy and fluency, and reading comprehension."

"For students in the third-grade cohort, the four interventions combined had impacts on phonemic decoding, word reading accuracy and fluency, and reading comprehension, although impacts were not detected for all measures of accuracy and fluency or comprehension"
