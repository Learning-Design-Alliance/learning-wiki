## Evidence

### Rindermann Heiner 2015

Rindermann Heiner, Pichelmann Stefan. (2015). Future Cognitive Ability: US IQ Prediction until 2060 Based on NAEP. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138412

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical projection analysis (Tables 5 and 6) regressing IQ-converted NAEP scores on two past intervals and weighting by projected ethnic group proportions. The article reports "The average rise per decade is dec = 0.76 or 0.45 IQ points (optimistic vs. pessimistic model)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Table 5 shows the projected IQ scores from 2012 to 2060 for each group as well as the whole population based on 1978/80 to 2012 NAEP scores and the National Population Projections. Table 6 shows the projected IQ scores based on the NAEP scores of 1992 to 2012. The average rise per decade is dec = 0.76 or 0.45 IQ points (optimistic vs. pessimistic model)."
