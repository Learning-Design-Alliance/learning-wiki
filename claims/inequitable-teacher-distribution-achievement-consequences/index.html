Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley 2012¶

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley. (2012). Building and Sustaining Talent: Creating Conditions in High-Poverty Schools That Support Effective Teaching and Learning. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543216

q2 · i? · review · r1

The report cites Los Angeles data showing “a low-income student is more than twice as likely to have a bottom-performing, English- language arts (ELA) teacher as is a higher income peer,” with similar patterns by race and ethnicity.

"a low-income student is more than twice as likely to have a bottom-performing, English- language arts (ELA) teacher as is a higher income peer."

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley 2012 (2)¶

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley. (2012). Building and Sustaining Talent: Creating Conditions in High-Poverty Schools That Support Effective Teaching and Learning. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543216

q2 · i? · review · r1

The L.A. data show below-basic students’ trajectories diverge by teacher sequence: “three bottom-quartile teachers in a row” versus “three top-quartile teachers in a row” produce persistently below-basic versus well-above-proficient performance.

"Students who start at the below-basic level and have three bottom-quartile teachers in a row remain below basic, while similar students who have three top-quartile teachers in a row end up performing well above the proficient level."
