## Evidence

### T. Rindler-Daller 2020

T. Rindler-Daller. (2020). Understanding CMB physics through the exploration of exotic cosmological models: a classroom study using CLASS. arXiv:1908.05042v3 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab6c6b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab6c6b)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom study in a winter 2018/19 lecture course at the University of Vienna: students ran CLASS for ΛCDM and three exotic models, plotted density parameters, CMB and matter power spectra, and wrote reports; "a total of 19 students completed successfully". The author reports all students grasped the main spectral changes.

> "the students were asked to study and interpret their results, compare the diﬀerent models with each other, and summarize the procedure, their results and their interpretation in a written report (a total of 19 students completed successfully the lectur e course)."
