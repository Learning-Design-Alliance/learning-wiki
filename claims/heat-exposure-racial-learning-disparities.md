# Prior-year heat exposure reduces learning, with about 3 times greater impact on Black and Hispanic/Latine students than White students

## Evidence

### Perez 2024

Perez, Alyssa. (2024). How school facilities impact student health and performance: advancing equity with green infrastructure. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655919

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

The brief attributes this to a 2019 National Bureau of Economic Research causal study (Goodman et al., 2018), which also found air conditioning could effectively mitigate the negative impacts.

> "cumulative heat exposure from the year prior (and even multiple years prior) reduces students’ rate of learning as measured by standardized test scores"

### Perez 2024 (2)

Perez, Alyssa. (2024). How school facilities impact student health and performance: advancing equity with green infrastructure. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655919

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

Same causal study as reported in the brief; disparities appeared tied to school investment in HVAC systems and to the geographic distribution of racially marginalized students in hotter locations.

> "heat exposure from the previous year has about a 3 times greater impact on Black and Hispanic/Latine students’ learning than it does on White students’ learning"
