### Patricia D. Simon 2026

Patricia D. Simon, Lily Min Zeng, Luke K. Fryer. (2026). A systematic review of student engagement research in adaptive learning platforms. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100360

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing included studies, evidence "for gamification having the greatest impact on student engagement"; the discussion notes contrary evidence that gamification had only minimal effect in another study, where exercise completion time decreased with continued use.

> "Gamification, on the other hand, is the integration of game-like elements, mechanics, into a non-game system such as adaptive learning platforms. Evidence for gamification having the greatest impact on student engagement exists [49]."
