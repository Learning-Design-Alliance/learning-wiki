## Evidence

### Kruse 2013

Kruse, G. & Drews, D. (2013). Using Performance Tasks to Improve Quantitative Reasoning in an Introductory Mathematics Course. International Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 7(2), Article 19. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2013.070219

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis in Study 2 of recorded time spent on the pre and post tests against rubric scores (n = 53). The article reports post-test time correlated with total score and most dimensions, and concludes student motivation to engage the task contributes to QR scores.

> "On the post test, time spent was positively related to total score, r = .398, n = 53, p = .003, "evidence", r = .396, n = 53, p = .003, "conclusion", r = .399, n = 53, p = .003, "alternatives", r = .329, n = 53, p = .016, and "completeness", r = .426, n = 53, p = .001."
