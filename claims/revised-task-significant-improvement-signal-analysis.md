## Evidence

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer 2018

Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2018). Investigating student understanding of bipolar junction transistor circuits. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404)

`q3 · i2` · `qualitative · r2`

Comparison of U1 physics students' performance on the revised task (N = 29, two semesters) against the original task (31% of U1 students correctly ranked Vout,B > Vout,A); the same reasoning is required in both tasks.

> "Using a χ2 test, there is a statistically significant difference in performance between the tasks (p < .0001, χ2 = 20.45) with a large effect size (Φ = .57)."

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer 2018 (2)

Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2018). Investigating student understanding of bipolar junction transistor circuits. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Within the revised task (N = 29), 86% answered both dc questions and 64% both signal questions correctly; the authors caution the small sample size limits the claims that may be made.

> "there is not a statistically significant difference in performance on the two portions of the task (p = .59)"

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer 2018 (3)

Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2018). Investigating student understanding of bipolar junction transistor circuits. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same revised task; when explicitly asked to consider both analyses, most students recognized the dc/small-signal distinction, but 14% used identical reasoning across both kinds of comparisons.

> "We found that 64% of students arrived at different answers for dc and small-signal conditions when comparing both pairs of circuits (B vs. A and C vs. A), and the majority of these students provided correct responses."
