## Evidence

### Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist 2024

Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist. (2024). Reducing Workload in Short Answer Grading Using Machine Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00322-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results section: time estimates from the timed retake-exam grading were used to compare grading a complete exam with and without machine learning support. The authors report a saving of "74% of the time spent on grading", with training-data grading, review of correct scores, and correction of misclassifications all included.

> "Using these estimates, grading a complete exam without support would take 36.0 hours, and grading with support would take 9.2 hours, saving 74% of the time spent on grading."
