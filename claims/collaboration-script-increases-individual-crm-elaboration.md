# A collaboration script during observational phases of simulation-based training increases medical students' individual elaboration of CRM heuristics

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Scripted learners took significantly more notes coded as CRM heuristic strategies during observational phases than control learners (d=1.73). [→ Zottmann Jan M. 2018](#zottmann-jan-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Field study with control group design; 34 medical students in four simulation-based emergency courses, two with and two without the script. Individual elaboration was measured by counting coded note segments; none of the control condition's notes contained segments codable as heuristic strategies.

> "Regarding the individual elaboration of CRM heuristics during the learning phase, however, we found a significant difference between the script condition and the control condition, t(13)=-4.13, p=.001, d=1.73."
