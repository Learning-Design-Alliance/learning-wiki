## Evidence

### Puppart 2025

Puppart, B., & Aru, J. (2025). A Brief AI Literacy Intervention Does Not Significantly Reduce Over-Reliance and Increases Under-Reliance on ChatGPT: A Randomized Study. University of Tartu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 19 intervention-group and 17 control-group students. The Welch's t-test on over-reliance yielded "t(28.78) = −0.73, p = .471, d = −0.25", a small non-significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "Welch's t-test indicated that over-reliance was lower in the intervention group (M = 48.68, SD = 24.26) than in the control group (M = 55.88, SD = 33.69), but the difference was not statistically significant, t(28.78) = −0.73, p = .471, d = −0.25"
