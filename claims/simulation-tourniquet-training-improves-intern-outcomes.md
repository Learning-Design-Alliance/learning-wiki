## Evidence

### Sahin Abdul Samet 2026

Sahin Abdul Samet, Yilmaz Mutlu, Imamoglu Melih, Beser Muhammet Fatih, Karaca Yunus, Pasli Sinan, Gunduz Abdulkadir. (2026). Effectiveness and retention of extremity and junctional tourniquet training among medical interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09111-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-group pre-test/post-test study of 32 medical interns; Friedman tests across three time points showed significant improvement on all outcome measures, with Kendall's W effect sizes ranging from 0.18 (attitude) to 0.87 (extremity skill).

> "Following training, significant improvements were observed in knowledge, skills (extremity and junctional), attitudes, self-confidence, and self- efficacy scores (p < 0.001)."
