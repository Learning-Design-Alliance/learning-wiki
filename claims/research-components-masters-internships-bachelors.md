# Research components are more common in master's IT degrees while internships are more common in bachelor's IT degrees, with master's internships rising with specialization level

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The overall percentage of IT degrees with a research component is 57.3% at the master's level versus 23.9% at the bachelor's level. [→ Pak-Lok Poon 2026](#pak-lok-poon-2026)
`q2 i?` Internships are less common at the master's level (20.5%) than at the bachelor's level (44.3%), and at master's level the percentage rises from GL-degrees (9.5%) through MA-degrees (20.0%) to SP-degrees (23.9%). [→ Pak-Lok Poon 2026 (2)](#pak-lok-poon-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Census counts at degree level (Table 14) of research components defined as honors year projects, master's minor theses, or major research projects; 84.6% of universities offer a research component at one or both levels.

> "the overall percentages of all IT degrees with a research component at the bachelor's and master's levels are 23.9% (=(7+47+8)/(29+184+46)) and 57.3% (=(14+75+40)/(21+133+71)), respectively."

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026 (2)

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Census of internship availability across degree types at both levels (Table 15); at the master's level the percentage "starts from 9.5% for GL-degrees, rises to 20.0% for MA-degrees, and finally reaches 23.9% for SP-degrees".

> "internships are less common at the master's level (overall 20.5%) than at the bachelor's level (overall 44.3%)."
