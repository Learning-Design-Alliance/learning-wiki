Evidence¶

Roohani 2022¶

Roohani, A. (2022). Effect of (Un)Focused Tasks on L2 Learners' Meaning Negotiation and Negotiation Strategy. PASAA, 63. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/2287-0024 doi:10.58837/chula.pasaa.63.1.12

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Chi-square test on strategy frequencies (confirmation checks, clarification requests, comprehension checks) coded by two raters from the 36 learners' task transcripts; confirmation checks were the most frequent and comprehension checks the least frequent strategy in both task types; no effect size printed.

"the relationship between the type of negotiation strategies and task type (focused vs. unfocused) was statistically significant, (χ2 = 7.293, p = .0260)"
