## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Control group gains were statistically significant on intratextual, nontextual and total RCES scores, but the increase in intertextual understanding scores was not statistically significant. [→ Pilten 2016](#pilten-2016)

## Evidence

### Pilten 2016

Gulhiz Pilten. (2016). The Evaluation of Effectiveness of Reciprocal Teaching Strategies on Comprehension of Expository Texts. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i10.1791

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent samples t-tests on the 28 control students' pre- and post-test RCES scores after 11 weeks of curriculum-projected activities. Intratextual, nontextual and total gains were significant; the intertextual dimension was not, with no effect size printed.

> "The increase in the “intertextual understanding scores” dimension is not statistic ally significant [t=.372, p>.05]."
