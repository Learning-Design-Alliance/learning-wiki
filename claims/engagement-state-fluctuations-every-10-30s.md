## Evidence

### Ido Davidesco 2026

Ido Davidesco, Yushuang Liu, Kim Chaloner, Emma Laurent, Gabriella A. Ali, Sarah C. Creider, Sean Hughes, Laura Noejovich, Henry Valk, Dana Bevilacqua, David Poeppel & Suzanne Dikker. (2026). The dynamics of student engagement in cooperative science learning: a multimodal approach. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00457-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Video coding analysis of 30 students recorded in 5-s segments during the collaborative cell-model task. The article reports engagement is "a dynamic process" with rapid fluctuations; only means and SDs are printed, no effect size.

> "Students typically remained in the same state for about 10 –30 s before switching, with a mean continuous duration of 27.07 s (SD = 13.18) for on-task, 12.41 s (SD = 5.95) for off-task, and 9.42 s (SD = 4.41) for idle periods."
