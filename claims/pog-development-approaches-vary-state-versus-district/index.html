A document scan of publicly available state and district POG documents and websites as of October 2024, summarized in the brief's review of "State- and District-Level Development of Portraits of a Graduate." The brief reports that "About a dozen state education agencies" have led standardized statewide frameworks, while in other states adoption is district-led and optional.
