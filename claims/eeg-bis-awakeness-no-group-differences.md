## Evidence

### Wang Chris 2020

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

EEG BIS recordings taken during the 20-minute learning session were compared across the three groups with a one-way ANOVA: no significant differences, F(2,49) = 0.374, p = 0.690; equivalence was not tested and no effect size printed. Descriptive means were MR M = 95.44, 3DM M = 94.88, text-only M = 95.31.
