## Subclaims
`q3 i2` At the between level, T2 threat evaluation negatively predicted GCSE mathematics grade (β = -.34, p = .006); a supplementary contextual analysis showed a moderate effect (ES = -.65). [→ Putwain 2021](#putwain-2021)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2021

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Kutuk, G. (2021). Warning Students of the Consequences of Examination Failure: An Effective Strategy for Promoting Student Engagement? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329876

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Between-level (class-aggregated) ML-SEM paths. Threat evaluation was the only significant predictor of GCSE grade at the class level. A supplementary contextual analysis estimated the T2 threat effect "ES = -.65", comparable to Cohen's d, after accounting for the within-level effect.

> "GCSE mathematics grade was negatively predicted by T2 threat (β = -.34, p =.006), but not T2 challenge (β = .04, p =.66), T2 behavioral engagement (β = -.10, p =.25), or T2 fear appeals (β = .05, p =.50)"
