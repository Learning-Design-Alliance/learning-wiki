## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Pre-to-post gains were significant in both groups (p < 0.001), with r = 0.33 (medium) in the control group and r = 0.49 (medium to large) in the experimental group. [→ Aldo Gordillo and Daniel López-Fernández 2024](#aldo-gordillo-and-daniel-lopez-fernandez-2024)

## Evidence

### Aldo Gordillo and Daniel López-Fernández 2024

Aldo Gordillo and Daniel López-Fernández. (2024). Are Educational Escape Rooms More Effective Than Traditional Lectures for Teaching Software Engineering? A Randomized Controlled Trial. IEEE Transactions on Education. https://doi.org/10.1109/TE.2024.3403913

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-ranks tests on paired pre/post scores in each group of the randomized controlled trial. The article reports "a medium effect size ( r = 0.33)" for the lecture group and "a medium to large effect size ( r = 0.49)" for the escape room group.

> "Statistically signiﬁcant differences ( p-value < 0.001) were observed in both groups when comparing the post-test and pretest scores, indicating substantial learning gains. In the control group this difference had a medium effect size ( r = 0.33), whereas in the experimental group it had a medium to large effect size ( r = 0.49)."
