Anggoro 2012¶

Anggoro, F. K., Stein, N. L., & Jee, B. D. (2012). Cognitive factors that influence children's learning from a multimedia science lesson. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 93-108. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068621

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The same ANCOVA revealed a "marginally significant interaction between Grade and Reading Condition" with "p = .08". Fourth graders showed a larger Tutor-Read vs. Self-Read difference than seventh graders.

"The analysis also revealed a marginally significant interaction between Grade and Reading Condition, F(1, 279) = 3.15, MSE = .31, p = .08, ηp2 = .01. This trend is due to the fact that 4th grade participants showed a larger difference in gain scores between the Tutor-Read condition (M = .77, SD = .30) and Self-Read condition (M = .36, SD = .41) than the 7th graders (M = .70, SD = .36 for Tutor-Read; M = .55, SD = .40 for Self-Read)."
