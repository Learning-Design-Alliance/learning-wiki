## Evidence

### Kainulainen et al. 2017

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Latent transition probability analysis (Table 5) between Times 1-2 and 2-3 showed students in the Mixed and Low classes most often moved to the High class, which the authors read as these classes being intermediary steps toward correct concepts.

> "In fact, the most likely transition for those students in the Mixed group at both time points is to the High class; this is also the case for students in the Low group at Time 2."
