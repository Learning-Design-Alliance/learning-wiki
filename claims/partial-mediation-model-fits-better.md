## Evidence

### Samuel Falcon 2023

Samuel Falcon, Jaime Leon. (2023). How do teachers engaging messages affect students? A sentiment analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10230-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ML-SEM model comparison in Mplus 8.6 contrasting a full mediation model (Fig. 1) with a partial mediation model (Fig. 2). The partial model shows lower AIC and BIC, RMSEA of 0.10 versus 0.25, and CFI/TLI above 0.90.

> "Comparisons of these two models in terms of fits favours the partial mediation model in all aspects. Adding partial mediation resulted in a decrease in information criteria values: AIC (Akaike, 1974) and BIC (Schwarz, 1978) were lower in the partial mediation model (4295.33 and 4339.18) than in the full mediation model (4353.97 and 4393.44)."
