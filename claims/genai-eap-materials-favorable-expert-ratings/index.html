Gao X (2026) From unified to differentiated materials: generative AI–supported adaptation of EAP reading materials. Front. Psychol. 17:1887565. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1887565

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Expert review of 15 anonymized material versions by three independent EAP reviewers with at least 5 years of tertiary EAP experience, blind to condition. Ratings included "academic ﬁdelity (M = 4.24, SD = 0.41)" and teachability M = 4.31; inter-rater reliability was ICC(2,k) = 0.84, 95% CI [0.76, 0.91].

"They rated the materials favorably for academic ﬁdelity (M = 4.24, SD = 0.41), proﬁciency appropriateness (M = 4.36, SD = 0.38), and teachability (M = 4.31, SD = 0.44); the overall quality mean was M = 4.30 (SD = 0.37)."
