## Evidence

### Delcker 2025

Delcker, J., Heil, J., & Ifenthaler, D. (2025). Evidence-based development of an instrument for the assessment of teachers' self-perceptions of their artificial intelligence competence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10418-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A survey study of N = 480 vocational school teachers in Germany using confirmatory factor analysis. The initial six-factor model did not meet fit criteria (RMSEA = .097, CFI = .764, TLI = .748); "After the removal of items, the fitness scores of the model improved and were closer to the desired cut-off values."

> "The results of the six-factor confirmatory factor analysis suggest that the items used for the different factors can partially be grouped into the six theoretical dimensions (TH, LF, IP, AT; TL, PF) of perceived AI competence."
