## Evidence

### McLaughlin 2018

McLaughlin, J.A., Boothroyd, L.G. and Philipson, P.M. (2018) 'Impact arising from sustained public engagement: A measured increase in learning outcomes' Research for All, 2 (2): 244–256, https://doi.org/10.18546/RFA.02.2.04

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-programme questionnaire completed by 50 AS-level Physics students who attended all five sessions across two Further Education Colleges. Self-reported interest: "54% of responses indicated that they were more (or much more) interested in science as a subject than they were before".

> "54% of responses indicated that they were more (or much more) interested in science as a subject than they were before, as a direct result of Dr McLaughlin's outreach programme (10%= much more; 44%=more; 44%=about the same; 2% less; 0% much less)."

### McLaughlin 2018 (2)

McLaughlin, J.A., Boothroyd, L.G. and Philipson, P.M. (2018) 'Impact arising from sustained public engagement: A measured increase in learning outcomes' Research for All, 2 (2): 244–256, https://doi.org/10.18546/RFA.02.2.04

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 50-questionnaire Likert survey; the statement was "My interest in science has been stimulated as a direct result of Dr McLaughlin's outreach programme", with 64% agreeing or strongly agreeing.

> "64% o f responses agreed (or strongly agreed) with this statement (14%=strongly agreed; 50%=agreed; 26%=neither agree nor disagree; 8% disagree; 2% strongly disagree)."
