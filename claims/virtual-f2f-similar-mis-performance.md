### Das Bibek 2024

Das Bibek, Ledesma Frances, Naik Ravi, Law Sarah, Soleimani-Nouri Payam, Khan Omar A, Mylonas George, Pai Madhava, Ashrafian Hutan, Spalding Duncan, Fehervari Matyas. (2024). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative assessment in the final course (n=20; F2F n=8, online n=12) scored from video recordings by Consultant Surgeons blinded to teaching method. The article reports "no significant differences in all other MISTELS and SUTT performance measures between groups."

> "There were no significant differences in all other MISTELS and SUTT performance measures between groups. Cognitive workload parameters (SURG-TLX score, heart rate, and pupil metrics) were also similar between groups."
