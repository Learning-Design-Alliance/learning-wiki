Rhee Jiyoung, Park Hyun Soo. (2025). Standardized learner simulation for debriefer training through video conference. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.321

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: they argue the method provides recordings, deliberate hands-on experience, and trainer feedback "regardless of distance or time." The article offers this as an argument rather than evidence from a comparison.

> "Furthermore, SL simulation is a flexible training model that can be adapted for trainees at different levels of debriefer expertise, novice, advanced beginner, or other professions (nursing, pharmacy, medicine, and so forth)."
