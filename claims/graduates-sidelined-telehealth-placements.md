## Evidence

### Jones 2025

Jones, C. and Cominos, N. (2025). The Future of Healthcare—Simulation‐Based Learning Curricula and Preparing Future Midwives for Effective Rapport‐Building in Telehealth Settings. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16927

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interview data in the translation-into-clinical-contexts theme (Section 5.4). Graduates "uniformly described negative clinical experiences with telehealth care on placements", with some told telehealth was outside student scope of practice; one said she "mainly just felt like I was just kind of sitting there watching what was going on".

> "it's really hard to be involved in telehealth appointments as a student in practice… you kind of end up just being pushed to the side… just observe"
