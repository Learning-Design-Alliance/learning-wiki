## Evidence

### Hong Wei Han 2015

Hong Wei Han, Vadivelu Jamunarani, Daniel Esther Gnanamalar Sarojini, Sim Joong Hiong. (2015). Thinking about thinking: changes in first-year medical students' metacognition and its relation to performance. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27561

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired sample t-test on total MSI scores of 159 first-year students; the article reports "t(158)=1.96, p=0.052", just above the 0.05 threshold, with means of 241.97 and 237.67. No effect size is printed.

> "there was no significant difference in the scores for the total score of metacognitive skills scores in first-year medical students at the beginning of the academic year (M=241.97, SD=25.21) and at the end of the academic year (M=237.67, SD=28.14) conditions; t(158)=1.96, p=0.052"
