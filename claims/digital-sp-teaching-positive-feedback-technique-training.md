# Digital SP-based teaching units on medical conversation received positive feedback from students and SPs and allowed conversation techniques to be trained

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hartmann Lilly 2021

Hartmann Lilly, Kaden Jens J., Strohmer Renate. (2021). Authentic SP-based teaching in spite of COVID-19 – is that possible?. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001417](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001417)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Oral feedback gathered after the digital teaching units (May–August 2020) from students in academic years 3–5 who conducted SP interviews via HeiConf. Students described the experience as "surprisingly similar to the learning experience with the usual classroom teaching" and reported training conversation techniques well.

> "Students stated that the online teaching units on medical conversation with SPs were surprisingly similar to the learning experience with the usual classroom teaching. Thus they could use and train learned conversation techniques well in the discussion."

### Hartmann Lilly 2021 (2)

Hartmann Lilly, Kaden Jens J., Strohmer Renate. (2021). Authentic SP-based teaching in spite of COVID-19 – is that possible?. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001417](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001417)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-unit oral feedback from the 18 SPs who portrayed 15 different complex roles in the digital teaching units. All 18 stated the units "worked out better than they had expected" and reported no restriction in role portrayal; no effect sizes were reported.

> "All 18 SPs interviewed stated that the teaching units worked out better than they had expected. In addition, they did not feel restricted in their role portrayal by the digital format."
