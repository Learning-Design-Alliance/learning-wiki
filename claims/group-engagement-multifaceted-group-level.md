`q2 · i?`

The editorial reports on Sinha et al.'s study: statistical correlations based on ratings of several aspects of group engagement measured in five-minute intervals, plus thick descriptions of discourse excerpts. Discourse details such as one group's use of "we" versus another's "I" reflected the rating distinction. The editorial offers no effect sizes.

> "The construct of group engagement developed in this paper allows the authors to identify this contrast and to analyze it using both quantitative and qualitative methods."
