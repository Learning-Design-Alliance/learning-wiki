## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, W. Handley, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). A Research-Based Curriculum for Teaching the Photoelectric Effect. https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2165 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0706.2165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0706.2165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Error-category analysis of incorrect responses to the first exam question across the three semesters; the second most common error (5%) was claiming intensity alone can eject electrons when photon energy is below the work function.

> "The most common error, made by nearly half (42%) of the students who answered at least one part of the first exam question incorrectly, was the misapplication of Ohm's law, for example claiming that a voltage is necessary for current flow or to overcome the work function of the metal."
