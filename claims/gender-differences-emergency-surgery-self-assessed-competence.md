## Evidence

### Wachter N. 2024

Wachter N., Güsgen C., Geis C., Penzkofer L.S., Oldhafer K., Willms A.G., Huber T. (2024). Status quo of operative training in emergency surgery in Germany – results of a survey. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03360-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square subgroup analyses of self-assessed competence items in the DGAV survey. The article reports men "answered significantly more often with “totally agree” compared to women" on all four competence statements.

> "Men answered significantly more often with “totally agree” compared to women (54% vs. 30%, p = 0.009). The same was shown in the evaluation of the statements “I master laparoscopic surgery” (41% vs. 14%, p < 0.001), “I master open surgery” (47% vs. 18%, p < 0.001) and “I master the conversion to an open operation” (50% vs. 18%, p < 0.001)."
