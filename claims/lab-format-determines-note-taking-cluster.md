## Evidence

### Katherine N. Quinn 2019

Katherine N. Quinn, Kathryn L. McGill, Michelle M. Kelley, Emily M. Smith, and N. G. Holmes (2019). Who does what now? How physics lab instruction impacts student behaviors. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.quinn](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.quinn)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational cluster analysis of student profiles: traditional labs used paper worksheets handed in individually while inquiry labs used one electronic lab notebook per group. The description quotes the printed composition result; the authors call the logistical difference the biggest effect on cluster composition.

> "the Laptop cluster is composed entirely of students in the inquiry labs, and that the Paper cluster is composed entirely of students in the traditional labs."
