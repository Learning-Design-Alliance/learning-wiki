Brasiel 2016¶

Brasiel, S., Jeong, S., Ames, C., Lawanto, K., Yuan, M., & Martin, T. (2016). Effects of educational technology on mathematics achievement for K-12 students in Utah. Journal of Online Learning Research, 2(3), 205-226. https://www.learntechlib.org/j/JOLR/

Analysis of monthly usage data collected from providers for 196,625 distributed licenses, of which 152,276 showed evidence of usage. Table 3 reports percent meeting fidelity benchmark by product, ranging from 2% for ALEKS to 67% for Catchup Math, with a total of 9%.
