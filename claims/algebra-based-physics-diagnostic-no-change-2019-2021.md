### Eric W. Burkholder 2022

Eric W. Burkholder, Carl E. Wieman. (2022). Absence of a COVID-induced academic drop in high-school physics learning. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of summer 2019 (N = 214) and summer 2021 (N = 212) diagnostic scores for students intending algebra-based physics, tested with a t-test and Cohen's d. The article reports "d = -0.027 and is not statistically significant (p = 0.78)"; no equivalence test was reported.

> "In 2019, the average score was 17.3 out of 36 (standard deviation = 6.63 points; N = 214). In 2021, the average score was 17.1 points (s. d. = 6.89 points; N = 212). The difference between these two means as a fraction of the standard deviation is d = -0.027 and is not statistically significant (p = 0.78)."
