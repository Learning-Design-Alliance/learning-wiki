# Grade 3 students showed small positive but statistically non-significant CCU impacts across all three study years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Grade 3 treatment students had higher CCU scores than control students in years 1 through 3, but none of the differences were statistically significant. [→ Jonathan Nakamoto 2015](#jonathan-nakamoto-2015)

## Evidence

### Jonathan Nakamoto 2015

Jonathan Nakamoto, Sandy Sobolew-Shubin, Martin Orland. (2015). The Beaverton School District Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project, An Investing in Innovation (i3) Development Grant: Student Impact Findings from Years 1, 2, and 3. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED560945

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory CCU analyses in 12 schools; Exhibit E-2 prints grade 3 effect sizes of 0.22, 0.24, and 0.14, none reaching significance at the Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted thresholds. Only the test outcomes are printed, so impact is null.

> "The students in grade 3 had higher scores than the control students in years 1 through 3 and, based on the effect sizes, the impacts would be considered small positive program impacts. However, none of the differences concerning grade 3 students were statistically significant."
