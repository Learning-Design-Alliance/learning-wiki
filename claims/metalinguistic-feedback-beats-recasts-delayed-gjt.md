# Explicit metalinguistic feedback outperforms recasts on the delayed grammaticality judgment posttest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the delayed grammaticality judgment posttest, the metalinguistic group differed significantly from the recast group, with a trend toward metalinguistic over control. [→ Rod Ellis 2006](#rod-ellis-2006)

## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 2006

Rod Ellis, Shawn Loewen and Rosemary Erlam. (2006). Implicit and Explicit Corrective Feedback and the Acquisition of L2 Grammar. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060141

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on the untimed grammaticality judgment test (a measure of explicit knowledge) at delayed posttest for the same 34 learners. "the metalinguistic group differed significantly from the recast group" with F (2, 34) = 4.493, p < .05.

> "there is for the delayed posttest, F (2, 34) = 4.493, p < .05. The post hoc contrasts for the delayed posttest showed that the metalinguistic group differed significantly from the recast group and that there was a trend toward significance for metalinguistic over the control."
