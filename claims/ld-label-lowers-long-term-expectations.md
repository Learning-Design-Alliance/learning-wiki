## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kashikar 2025

Kashikar, L., Lüke, T., & Grosche, M. (2025). Effects of Diagnostic Labels for Students With Learning Problems on Teachers' Stereotypes and Performance Expectations. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194251315187

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the highest expected graduation level in the 413-teacher vignette experiment found a main effect of label group, ηp2 = .035, with the LG0–LG3 post hoc difference significant at p = .001. A parallel ANOVA on likelihood of completing a university degree found a main effect of the LG, ηp2 = .027, with only the LG0–LG3 difference significant, p = .006.

> "The post hoc test for the effect of the LG showed a significant difference between LG0 and LG3, p = .001. This means teachers who remembered the LD label expected a lower graduation level than teachers who read the vignette without a label"
