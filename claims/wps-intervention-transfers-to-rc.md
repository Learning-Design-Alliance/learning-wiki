# WPS text-structure intervention transfers to reading comprehension (cross-domain far transfer versus control)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` WP.INT produced significant cross-domain transfer effects on RC Notes and RC Retell versus control, with g = 0.91 and 0.54. [→ Fuchs 2024](#fuchs-2024)

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2024

Fuchs, L. S., Seethaler, P. M., Sterba, S. K., Fuchs, D., Cutting, L. E., Mancilla-Martinez, J., Martin, B. L. N., & Espinas, D. (2024). Transfer between Reading Comprehension and Word-Problem Solving among Children with Learning Difficulty in Both Domains. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000911

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel main effects models showed significant WP.INT-versus-CON slopes on RC outcomes (RC Notes p < .001; RC Retell p = .001); the article reports "WP.INT’s ES (vs. control) on RC were g = 0.91 and 0.54." No prior study had investigated this transfer direction.
