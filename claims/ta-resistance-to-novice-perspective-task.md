## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Rethinking Tools for Training Teaching Assistants. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02946](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02946)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational notes taken immediately after discussion in the TA training course: many graduate students called the task "useless or meaningless and had no relevance to their TA duties" and asserted it was impossible or required mind-reading. No effect size printed.

> "Not a single graduate student stated that they saw merit in the task or said anything in favor of why the task may be relevant for a TA training course."
