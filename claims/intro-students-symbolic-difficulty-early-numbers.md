## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying College Introductory Physics Students' Attitudes and Approaches to Problem Solving. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results for Questions 30 and 31 (Figures 6 and 7) comparing introductory students with faculty and Ph.D. students. Interviews indicated students had "difficulty keeping track of the variables they are solving for" when several symbols were involved, motivating early substitution.

> "Only approximately 35% of the introductory students disagreed with the statement that it is more difficult to solve a problem symbolically and 45% agreed with the statement that they prefer to solve the problem symbolically first and only plug in the numbers at the very end."
