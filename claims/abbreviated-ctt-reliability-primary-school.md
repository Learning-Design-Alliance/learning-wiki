## Evidence

### Tsarava 2022

Tsarava, K., Moeller, K., Román-González, M., Golle, J., Leifheit, L., Butz, M. V., & Ninaus, M. (2022). A Cognitive Definition of Computational Thinking in Primary Education. Computers & Education, 179, 104425. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104425

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the abbreviated CTt in 192 third and fourth graders. Alpha was .637 and the omega coefficient was .638 (95% CI [.55, .71]); the authors judge this adequate "considering the young population it was administered to and its speeded administration".

> "The reliability of the abbreviated CTt, as indicated by Cronbach's alpha, was a = .637."

### Tsarava 2022 (2)

Tsarava, K., Moeller, K., Román-González, M., Golle, J., Leifheit, L., Butz, M. V., & Ninaus, M. (2022). A Cognitive Definition of Computational Thinking in Primary Education. Computers & Education, 179, 104425. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104425

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis of the abbreviated CTt scores; the scree plot and elbow criterion supported a one-factor solution, in line with prior findings on the original 28-item CTt in older students.

> "Inspection of the scree plot (see Fig. 4) and based on the so -called elbow criterion, results of the exploratory factor analysis substantiated a one-factor solution."
