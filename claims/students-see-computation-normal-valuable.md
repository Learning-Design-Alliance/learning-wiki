### W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley 2022

W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley. (2022). Student Representations of Computation in the Physics Community. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exploratory semistructured interview study of five physics majors conducted 2-3 months after fall 2020 computationally integrated courses (Astrophysics I, Mathematical Physics, Modern Physics), analyzed by constant comparison. The authors report students "saw computation as a normal and valuable part of physics practice" with benefits "in alignment with the physics community." No effect sizes are reported; themes are supported by interview excerpts.

> "We ﬁnd that these students saw computation as a normal and valuable part of physics practice, identiﬁed beneﬁts of using computation in alignment with the physics community, struggle with conﬁdence with regards to computation"
