### Stoo Sepp 2025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Aggregated data-access opinion means were 2.69 (control), 2.50 (text), and 2.48 (label) on a 1–4 scale; the Kruskal-Wallis test found "non-significant difference s between groups". H4 was not supported; the p-value was near the .05 threshold.

> "With assumptions for a traditional ANOVA not met, a resulting Krusal-Wallis test suggested non-significant difference s between groups in terms of who should have access to the participants’ educational data (χ 2(2) = 5.578, p = 0.061)."
