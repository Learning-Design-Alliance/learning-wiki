## Evidence

### O’Brien A 2023

O’Brien A, Forde C. (2023). Health science staff and student experiences of teaching and assessing clinical skills using digital tools: a qualitative study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2023.2256656

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview findings: participants described struggles building confidence in skills dependent on "haptic awareness and manual dexterity", requiring equipment familiarity, expert feedback and in-person practice, citing mechanical memory of the hands. No effect size is reported.

> "Students struggled however to build confidence in their knowledge of skills that were dependent on haptic awareness and manual dexterity. Haptic training was perceived as particularly difficulty as these skills required familiarity with equipment, haptic and expert feedback and in-person practice."
