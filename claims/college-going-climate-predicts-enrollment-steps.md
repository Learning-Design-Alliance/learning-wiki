## Evidence

### Melissa Roderick 2009

Melissa Roderick, Jenny Nagaoka, and Vanessa Coca. (2009). College Readiness for All: The Challenge for Urban High Schools. The Future of Children. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/college-readiness-all-challenge-urban-high-schools

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the authors' Chicago work, college-going climate (measured by percent of prior graduates attending four-year colleges or teachers' reports of college-focused work) was the single most consistent predictor of enrollment steps, controlling for ACT scores, GPAs, and demographic and socioeconomic characteristics. Percentages are associations, not standardized effect sizes.

> "was associated with a 7 to 9 percent increase in the likelihood that a student who aspired to a four-year college degree would take each step in the college enrollment process and a 30 percent increase in the probability that a student would enroll in a college with a selectivity level that matched or exceeded his or her qualifications"
