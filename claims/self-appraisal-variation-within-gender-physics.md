### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive distributional analysis of survey responses from 1845 students in algebra-based and calculus-based introductory physics at a large public research university, shown in Fig. 2. The article reports that "respondents collectively used the full range of the scale."

> "However, the distributions also showed overlap between women and men, indicating that students' self-appraised femininity and masculinity did not simply align with binary gender identity."
