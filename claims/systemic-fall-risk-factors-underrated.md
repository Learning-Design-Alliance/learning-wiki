Assessing physical therapists' knowledge in fall prevention among older adults in Saudi arabia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06668-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Perceived-importance ratings for 16 fall risk factors in the cross-sectional survey (N = 384) showed a hierarchical pattern: balance/gait disorders highest (M = 3.58), urinary incontinence lowest (M = 2.23), a 1.35-point spread.

> "Balance/gait disorders emerged as the most recognized risk factor, followed by neuromuscular conditions. The 1.35-point spread between the highest and lowest rated factors indicates substantial variability in perceived importance."
