## Evidence

### Morrison Nariell 2019

Morrison Nariell, Machado Michelle, Blackburn Clare. (2019). Student perspectives on barriers to performance for black and minority ethnic graduate-entry medical students: a qualitative study in a West Midlands medical school. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032493

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group narratives from the study of 24 BME graduate-entry medical students; students described social networks as providing access to clinical learning opportunities and learning resources regarded as essential to performance.

> "If you're not in the right friendship groups or Facebook groups, you've got less access to peer resources […] Being BME and isolated, you're more likely to be disadvantaged."
