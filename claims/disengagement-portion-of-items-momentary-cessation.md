`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The chapter reports, from research using response-time-based identification of disengaged responses, that "test takers generally disengage during only a portion of their items" and that such responses reflect a momentary cessation of measurement. No effect size or sample statistics are printed in the available text.

> "Research using this capability has revealed that test takers generally disengage during only a portion of their items and that these disengaged responses reflect a momentary cessation of measurement."
