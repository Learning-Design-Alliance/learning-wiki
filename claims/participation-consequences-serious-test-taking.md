## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Having consequences for not participating in the BPT increases the odds of taking the test seriously by 153% (OR = 2.53) compared with no consequences, controlling for other variables. [→ Schüttpelz-Brauns 2020](#schuttpelz-brauns-2020)

## Evidence

### Schüttpelz-Brauns 2020

Schüttpelz-Brauns, K., Hecht, M., Hardt, K., Karay, Y., Zupanic, M., & Kämmer, J. E. (2020). Institutional strategies related to test-taking behavior in low stakes assessment. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 25(2), 321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09928-y

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same mixed effects logistic regression of 108,140 participations; the printed effect size for consequences for not participating is OR = 2.53 (estimate 0.93, SE 0.46, p = 0.043, 95% CI 1.03–6.20), described as a "153%" odds increase.

> "the odds of taking the test seriously increases by 153% if there are consequences for not participating in the BPT as compared to no consequences for not participating."
