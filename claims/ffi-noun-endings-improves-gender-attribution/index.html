Evidence¶

Roy Lyster 2004¶

Roy Lyster. (2004). DIFFERENTIAL EFFECTS OF PROMPTS AND RECASTS IN FORM-FOCUSED INSTRUCTION. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 26, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263104263021

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental classroom study of four teachers and eight classes of 179 fifth-grade immersion students (10–11 years old); analyses of pretest, immediate-posttest, and delayed-posttest results showed "a significant increase in the ability of students exposed to FFI to correctly assign grammatical gender."
