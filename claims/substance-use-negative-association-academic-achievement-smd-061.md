`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis (DerSimonian and Laird) of three studies reporting means and SDs of academic achievement for substance consumers versus non-consumers, finding a "negative and significant correlation" with a medium pooled SMD of -0.61 (p = 0.008).

> "we had 1,219 subjects in the control group and 350 substances consumers. The finding reveals a negative and significant correlation of substance consumption on academic achievement. The strength of association can be considered of medium level (SMD = -0.61; 95% CI -0.84 to -0.38; p = 0.008)."
