## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Vera Montalbano 2013

Vera Montalbano, Maria De Nicola, Simone Di Renzone and Serena Frati. (2013). A pilot experience in physics laboratory for a vocational school. ICPE-EPEC 2013 Proceedings. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.4868](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.4868)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-path assessment of students' personal worksheets in the pilot vocational classes. The article reports that "very few (not more than two or three) acquired confidence with cal orimetric measurements", while a consistent number used units correctly. No standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "Finally, very few (not more than two or three) acquired confidence with cal orimetric measurements or were able to compare correctly two measures."
