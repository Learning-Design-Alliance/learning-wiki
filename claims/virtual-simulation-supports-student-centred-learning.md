## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors state that using web-based virtual simulation makes the teaching and learning process student-centred rather than teacher-centred, requiring students to think, analyze situations and think critically. [→ Ziden and Rahman 2013](#ziden-and-rahman-2013)

## Evidence

### Ziden and Rahman 2013

Azidah Abu Ziden, Muhammad Faizal Abdul Rahman. (2013). The Effectiveness of Web-Based Multimedia Applications Simulation in Teaching and Learning. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085400

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

This is the authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, not a separately measured outcome; the article offers no test of student-centredness or critical thinking. It is offered as an explanation of why the simulation supported learning.

> "By using the  web-based virtual simulation,  the teaching and learning process is no longer teacher centered but is  a student-centered. In this simulation process they are required to think, analyze situations and think critically on how to resolve the problem."
