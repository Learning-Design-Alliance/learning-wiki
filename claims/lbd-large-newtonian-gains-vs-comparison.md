## Evidence

### Camp 2010

Camp, P. J. (2010). U-shaped Development of Newtonian Concepts: Implications for Pedagogical Design and Research Practice. Spelman College. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.1647](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.1647)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post t-test comparison of an LBD honors science class (N=90 in 2002-03) against a non-LBD honors class in the same school, with classes assigned by school administration. The article reports a statistically significant difference, with an effect size described as "quite large: 0.83 by Pearson‟s r and 3.0 by Cohen‟s d".

> "The LBD classes experienced a large gain, whereas the comparison classes experienced a minimal gain, consistent with zero. Again, p < .001. The effect size is quite large: 0.83 by Pearson‟s r and 3.0 by Cohen‟s d."
