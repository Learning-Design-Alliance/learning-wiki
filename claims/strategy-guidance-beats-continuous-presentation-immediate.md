# Strategy guidance yields better immediate recall and comprehension and higher efficiency than continuous presentation without learner control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Compared to the continuous presentation group, the strategy guidance group demonstrated significantly better performance in the immediate recall and comprehension tests and higher performance efficiency in both these tests. [→ Lin 2022](#lin-2022)

## Evidence

### Lin 2022

Lin, YC., Liu, TC. & Kalyuga, S. (2022). Strategies for facilitating processing of transient information in instructional videos by using learner control mechanisms. Instructional Science, 50, 863–877. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09600-w

`q3 · i?`

Between-subjects experiment (N = 90 undergraduates, three groups). The strategy guidance group significantly outperformed the continuous presentation group, which had no learner control mechanism, on immediate recall and comprehension performance and on performance efficiency in both tests. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
