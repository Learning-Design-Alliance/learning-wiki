Evidence¶

Frank Fischer 2005¶

Frank Fischer, Heinz Mandl. (2005). Knowledge Convergence in Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning: The Role of External Representation Tools. The Journal of the Learning Sciences, 14(3), pp.405-441. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190855

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experimental comparison of videoconferencing versus face-to-face collaboration conditions with 64 university students. The article reports that "Learners in the computer-mediated collaboration used a wider range of resources." No effect size is printed in the available text.
