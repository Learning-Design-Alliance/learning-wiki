# After one year, reading and mathematics software products did not increase or decrease test scores by amounts statistically different from zero

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On average, after one year of use, the studied reading and mathematics software products had effects on test scores that were not statistically different from zero. [→ Dynarski 2007](#dynarski-2007)

### Dynarski 2007

National experimental study mandated by the No Child Left Behind Act in which "volunteering teachers randomly assigned to use or not use selected products"; after one year average test-score effects were statistically indistinguishable from zero. No effect size is printed in the available text.
