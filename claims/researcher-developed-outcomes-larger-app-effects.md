## Evidence

### James Kim 2021

James Kim, Joshua Gilbert, Qun Yu, Charles Gale. (2021). Measures Matter: A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Educational Apps on Preschool to Grade 3 Children's Literacy and Math Skills. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211004183

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

RVE meta-regression (Table 5) controlling for peer review, RCT design, active control, and domain; standardized outcome coefficient −0.419, p < .001. Discussion reports "nearly 0.28 SDs larger" effects for researcher-developed outcomes in the final model.

> "studies using standardized outcomes produced impacts that were, on average, about 0.42 standard deviations (p < .01) lower than researcher developed outcomes"
