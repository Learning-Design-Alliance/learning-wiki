Correlational 2002 and 2003 study comparing TRIO SSS students enrolled in an IL course plus introductory biology with biology-only students in the same course and term. The article states statistical analysis was not applied to the grade distribution data, so these are descriptive comparisons.

> "Students concurrently enrolled in the IL and biology courses outperformed their counterparts enrolled only in biology. The mean grade in the biology class for the IL group was 83% as compared with 70% for the biology-only group."
