Evidence¶

Wengaard E 2017¶

Wengaard E, Kristoffersen M, Harris A and Gundersen H (2017) Cardiorespiratory Fitness Is Associated with Selective Attention in Healthy Male High-School Students. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 11:330. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2017.00330

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linear mixed model analysis of reaction times from 54 male high-school students performing a Posner cue paradigm task, adjusted for video-game playing, testing time-point and alertness. The analysis found "Higher ˙VO2max was associated with faster RT for stimuli following invalid cue (Estimate =−2.69, SE = 1.03, p = 0.011)".

  • Higher cardiorespiratory fitness is associated with faster reaction times for stimuli following valid cues in healthy male high-school students — related

  • Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) is the physical fitness measure most strongly correlated with cognitive functioning in adolescents — a broader claim this one bears on
  • A significant main effect of stimuli category exists on reaction time in the Posner cue task — related
  • Later time-point of cognitive testing is associated with faster reaction times, with significantly slower RT in the morning — related
