## Evidence

### Petersen Sandra L. 2018

Petersen Sandra L., Erenrich Evelyn S., Levine Dovev L., Vigoreaux Jim, Gile Krista. (2018). Multi-institutional study of GRE scores as predictors of STEM PhD degree completion: GRE gets a low mark. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206570

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA of time to degree across GRE quartiles in the 1805-student cohort (Table 5). Mean times to degree ranged roughly 5.7–6.2 years across quartiles for both genders; the article reports no significant differences and no effect size.

> "there were no differences in time to degree for men or women based on either GRE V or GRE Q quartile rankings (Table 5)."
