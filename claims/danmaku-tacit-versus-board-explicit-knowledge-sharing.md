# Danmaku primarily carries tacit knowledge sharing via personal experience while the discussion board carries explicit mathematical knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Tacit knowledge (self-disclosed experiences and personal opinions) accounted for the majority of danmaku knowledge sharing, while explicit math knowledge was mainly shared on the discussion board. [→ Bo Yang 2024](#bo-yang-2024)

## Evidence

### Bo Yang 2024

Bo Yang. (2024). Combining Danmaku and Discussion Boards: Toward A Scalable and Sociable Environment for Mass Collaboration in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09426-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of knowledge-sharing excerpts found "Tacit knowledge accounted for the majority of knowledge sharing in danmaku," while the discussion board mainly shared explicit knowledge such as math formulas. This stands in contrast to Wu et al. (2018), who found danmaku predominantly shared explicit knowledge.

> "Tacit knowledge accounted for the majority of knowledge sharing in danmaku in the form of self-disclosed experience and personal opinions about learning"
