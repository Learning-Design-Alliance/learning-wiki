360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program 2017¶

360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bf98d40e-980c-4f54-9985-46bf5675d2fa

q2 · i? · design · r2

Exploratory online survey documentation of the Fall 2016 program, with over 400 student and educator respondents, reported educator and student perceptions of skill gains; the survey found "100% of educators indicated that their students learned new technical and creative production skills" and 88% of students agreed the project was a great way to learn something new.

"100% of educators indicated that their students learned new technical and creative production skills through their participation in the program."
