## Evidence

### Choi Kai Yip 2026

Choi Kai Yip, Chan Ka Wing, Yau Hiu Ching, Lau Yuet Yan, Leung Tsz Wing. (2026). Perceptual learning enhances diabetic retinopathy detection skills in novice optometry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08691-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Retention assessment in the PL group only, with 19 of 20 participants (95%) returning at three months. A repeated measures ANOVA showed a significant effect of time for diagnostic accuracy (F(2,36) = 38.42, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.68); post-hoc tests showed "the performance gains were durable" with no significant post-to-retention decline.

> "Importantly, there was no significant decline in accuracy between the post-training assessment and the three-month retention assessment (t(18) = 0.67, p > 0.99). The accuracy remained significantly improved when compared with baseline (t(18) = 7.23, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.64), demonstrating that the performance gains were durable."
