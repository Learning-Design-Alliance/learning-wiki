Evidence¶

Dilli 2016¶

Dilli, R. (2016). Conducting museum education activities within the context of developing a nature culture in primary school students: MTA natural history museum example. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(2), 75-84. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijese.2016.292a

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative dimension of the study: two-dimensioned artistic activities and aesthetic insight worksheets (e.g., asking which feature of the elephants students liked best) were analyzed by content analysis; students stated they were affected by the trucks, teeth, and skeleton of the elephants. One student expressed enthusiasm in a poem.

"The artistic activities realized by using the information and clues collected during the previous activities were the most creative and enjoyed activities as stated by the students."
