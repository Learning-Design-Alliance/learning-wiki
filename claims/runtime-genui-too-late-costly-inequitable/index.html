q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the abstract and introduction of this position paper: content fixed in one modality before delivery cannot be equitably adapted at runtime, and unreviewed generation risks inaccuracy. The paper offers argument, not empirical data, for this claim.

"For learners who might need audio-first, simplified text, interactive, or low-bandwidth representations, runtime adaptation is too late and too costly to be equitable at scale, and might lead to inaccurate learning content due to the inability to conduct verification at scale."
