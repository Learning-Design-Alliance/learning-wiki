# The linear slope of the HSGPA–college graduation relationship does not vary significantly across high schools, so HSGPA provides a consistent signal across schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The strong linear HSGPA–graduation trend (coefficient 0.703) does not vary significantly by high school; only the small quadratic term varies slightly (0.103). [→ Allensworth 2019](#allensworth-2019)

## Evidence

### Allensworth 2019

Allensworth, Elaine M., & Clark, Kallie. (2019). Are GPAs an Inconsistent Measure of College Readiness across High Schools? Examining Assumptions about Grades versus Standardized Test Scores. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/are-gpas-inconsistent-measure-achievement-across-high-schools-examining-assumptions

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Random-slope hierarchical model of students nested in high schools (n=17,753). The article states the "noise that is introduced by variation in the linear and quadratic components is small relative to the signal from the linear slope (0.703)".

> "The strong linear trend (coefficient of 0.703), does not vary significantly by high school. The quadratic term (coefficient of 0.062) does vary slightly across schools (0.103)."
