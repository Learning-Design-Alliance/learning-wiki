# Team regulation correlates positively with learning results only in the CHT condition

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the CHT condition, team regulation correlated positively with learning results (r=.68, p<.01); correlations in Control and RIDE conditions were not significant. [→ Saab 2012](#saab-2012)

## Evidence

### Saab 2012

Saab, N., van Joolingen, W., & van Hout-Wolters, B. (2012). Support of the collaborative inquiry learning process: influence of support on task and team regulation. Metacognition and Learning, 7, 7-23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9068-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson product-moment correlations between regulation frequencies and SWLEP computed per condition (Table 7). Only the CHT condition's team regulation correlation was significant (r = .68); task regulation correlations were −.28, −.20, and .32, none significant.

> "There was a positive correlation between Team regulation and the learning results in the CHT condition ( r=.68, p<.01)."
