`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same 2018 PISA regression analysis (430,264 adolescents, 73 countries) applied to standardized science scores with the same sociodemographic covariates. The article reports "increments of 0.09 SD (95% CI 0.08, 0.10)" and "0.20 SD (95% CI 0.19, 0.21)" for two or more years; no standardised effect size statistic is printed for this finding.

> "Estimated associations were almost identical for science scores, with increments of 0.09 SD (95% CI 0.08, 0.10) for at least 1 year, and 0.20 SD (95% CI 0.19, 0.21) for 2 or more years of pre-primary programme exposure."
