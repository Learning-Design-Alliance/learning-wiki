`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Frequency analysis of physics terms in original and back-translated FCI versions. The original English contained "speed(s)" 39 times (47 after editing for self-contained questions), yielding 2,491 occurrences across 53 translations. The authors say these patterns show translation "can subtly shift terminology."

> "After translation and back-translation, the English translation contained the term “speed” 1,514 times. Amongst those cases were 10 cases of “speeds up” and two cases of “speed up”. The term “velocity” was used 702 times."
