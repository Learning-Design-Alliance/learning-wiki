## Evidence

### Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian 2015

Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian. (2015). Efficiency of Using a Web-Based Approach to Teach Reading Strategies to Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p30

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subsection analyses of the paired pre/posttraining questionnaire (Table 4): skimming t(29) = -6.60, p = .000; inferring p = .001; context clues p = .000. Only test statistics and p-values are printed; no effect sizes.

> "A substantial change in the degree of frequency among the participants towards the use of inferring and using context clues strategies in the pretraining and posttraining questionnaires were also observed due to the fact that the p-values for these two strategies are .001 and .000, respectively."

### Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian 2015 (2)

Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian. (2015). Efficiency of Using a Web-Based Approach to Teach Reading Strategies to Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p30

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null result for the note-taking subsection: paired t(29) = -1.54, p = .13, means 7.83 to 8.00. The article attributes this to participants already applying note-taking at lower proficiency levels; equivalence was not tested.

> "Because the p-value is greater than the alpha level (.13 > .05) for the not-taking subsection of the questionnaire, it could be argues that the difference was less marked between the participants in applying this strategy in the pretraining (M = 7.83, SD =.59) and posttraining (M = 8.00, SD = .00) questionnaires, t (29) = -1.54."
