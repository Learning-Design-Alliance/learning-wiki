## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen 2018

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen. (2018). Systemic inequities in introductory physics courses: the impacts of learning assistants. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

HLM interaction analysis of 4,365 students in 93 courses from the LASSO platform; the gender (γ11 = -0.25, p = 0.862) and race/ethnicity (γ21 = -0.21, p = 0.884) LA-support interaction terms were not significant. The article reports gaps "not reliably (p >0.8) or meaningfully (d ∼0.01) smaller than in non-LA-supported courses", a negligible printed effect size.

> "While students performed better overall in LA-supported, the model shows the predicted performance gaps were not reliably (p >0.8) or meaningfully (d ∼0.01) smaller than in non-LA-supported courses."
