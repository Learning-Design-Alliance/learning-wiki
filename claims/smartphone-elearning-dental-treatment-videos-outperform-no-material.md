## Evidence

### Takenouchi A 2020

Takenouchi A, Otani E, Sunaga M, Toyama T, Uehara H, Akiyama K, Kawashima T, Ito K, Izuno H, Kinoshita A. (2020). Development and evaluation of e‐learning materials for dental hygiene students in six schools: Using smartphones to learn dental treatment procedures. International Journal of Dental Hygiene. https://doi.org/10.1111/idh.12452

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 293 dental hygiene students in six schools, randomized after a preliminary examination into test and control groups. Combined inter-group change comparison reached "p = .002"; test groups in all schools showed significant pre-to-post gains (e.g., C: p = .0001; F: p = .0006).

> "Combined data of all school showed that the changes from pre‐ to post‐examination scores of the test groups were significantly higher than those of the control groups (p = .002)."
