GGM network analysis with graphical LASSO regularization and EBIC optimization on achievement subgroups. Motor coordination was "the central node" for insufficient achievers and age "the most central node" for high achievers; centrality indices are structural, not effect sizes.

> "In the insufficient school achievement network, motor coordination was the central node, exhibiting the highest values for betweenness (2.13), closeness (1.85) and strength (1.49), while alternating selective attention had the highest expected influence (1.85). In the high school achievement network, age was the most central node across betweenness (1.33), closeness (1.41) and strength (1.20)."
