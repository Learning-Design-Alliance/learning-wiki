## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intervention group showed significant within-group improvement in all seven OSATS categories, including instrument handling, knowledge of instruments and knowledge of specific procedural steps, where the control group did not improve significantly. [→ Chartrand G 2021](#chartrand-g-2021)
`q3 i?` No between-group difference appeared in any individual OSATS category at either time point. [→ Chartrand G 2021](#chartrand-g-2021)

## Evidence

### Chartrand G 2021

Chartrand G, Soucisse M, Dubé P, Trépanier J-S, Drolet P, Sideris L. (2021). Self-directed learning by video as a means to improve technical skills in surgery residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02524-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures two-way ANOVA on seven OSATS category scores in the RCT (11 per group). Table 2 prints category-level mean improvements, e.g. knowledge of procedure "5.7 [3.4, 8.1], P = 0.001" for the intervention group. However, "no difference was found between the groups" in any category.

> "both groups improved significantly in four of the seven categories (respect of tissue, time and motion, flow of operation and use of assistant) while only the SDL-V group improved in the three others (instrument handling, knowledge of instruments and knowledge of specific procedural steps)"
