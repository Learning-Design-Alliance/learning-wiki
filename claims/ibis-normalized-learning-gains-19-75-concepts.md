## Evidence

### Shaw Tarren J. 2019

Shaw Tarren J., Yang Suann, Nash Troy R., Pigg Rachel M., Grim Jeffrey M. (2019). Knowing is half the battle: Assessments of both student perception and performance are necessary to successfully evaluate curricular transformation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210030

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Four-year pre/post concept assessment of 724 enrolled students (542 matched pre-post responses, 74.9%), analyzed with a generalized linear mixed effects logistic regression with test as fixed effect and student identifier as random effect. The article reports "the normalized learning gains were 19–75% each year" for concepts and 30–50% for skills.

> "however, in each year, students were more likely to answer concept inventory questions correctly after the course (p < 0.05 for all years). For concepts, the normalized learning gains were 19–75% each year (Fig 1)."
