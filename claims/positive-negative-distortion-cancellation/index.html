Evidence¶

Steven Wise 2019¶

Steven Wise, James Soland, Yuanchao Bo. (2019). The (non)impact of differential test taker engagement on aggregated scores. International Journal of Testing 20 (1), 55-77. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-nonimpact-of-differential-test-taker-engagement-on-aggregated-scores/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

This is the second factor the study identifies for the stability of school means. The abstract reports that disengagement "produced both positive and negative distortion of individual student scores," tending to cancel the net effect on the school mean.

"Second, and most interestingly, disengagement produced both positive and negative distortion of individual student scores, which tended to cancel out much of the net distortive effect on the school’s mean."
