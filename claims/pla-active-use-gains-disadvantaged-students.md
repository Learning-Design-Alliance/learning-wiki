The commentary reports, citing Herodotou et al. (2019) and Hlosta et al. (2021), an evaluation of the OU Analyse system at the Open University: active educator use of PLA "can lead to substantial gains for disadvantaged students", but adoption is limited. No effect sizes are printed.

> "In the case of OU Analyse, a recent evaluation study showed that if educators actively use the PLA, it can lead to substantial gains for disadvantaged students; but only a fraction of educators are willing to use the system and many of them are unable to use it effectively"
