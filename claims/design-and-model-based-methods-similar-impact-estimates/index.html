Re-analysis of data from nine past education RCTs, estimating impacts with design-based, HLM, and robust standard error methods. The study "finds that the design- and model-based methods yield very similar impact estimates and levels of statistical significance". No effect size is printed.

"The study re-analyzes data from nine past education RCTs covering a wide range of evaluation designs. Impacts are estimated using design-based, HLM, and robust standard error methods. The study finds that the design- and model-based methods yield very similar impact estimates and levels of statistical significance"
