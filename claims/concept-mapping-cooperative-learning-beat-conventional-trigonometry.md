## Evidence

### Bot & Eze 2016

Bot, T. D., & Eze, J. E. (2016). Comparative effects of concept mapping and cooperative learning strategies on senior secondary school students' achievement in mathematics-trigonometry in Kano State, Nigeria. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 4(1), 56-66. https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9453

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test non-equivalent control group study of 210 SSII students in three Kano secondary schools, taught in intact classes for four weeks. Descriptive results in Table 1 show concept mapping post-test mean of "29.75" versus control 22.71, and cooperative learning 29.98.

> "the subjects in experimental group one gained from the use of the concept mapping with a post‐test mean score of 29.75 compared with their pretest mean score of 13.40 which is lower. Experimental group two also gained from the use of cooperative learning strategy with a post‐test mean score of 29.98"

### Bot & Eze 2016 (2)

Bot, T. D., & Eze, J. E. (2016). Comparative effects of concept mapping and cooperative learning strategies on senior secondary school students' achievement in mathematics-trigonometry in Kano State, Nigeria. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 4(1), 56-66. https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9453

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test as covariate (Table 2) showed a significant main effect of teaching method; the article reports "F2, 207) =586.046" with p = 0.000, and Scheffé post-hoc tests (Table 3) found both experimental groups significantly above the conventional group. The table prints R² = 0.28 (adjusted 0.199) for the whole model, which includes the pre-test covariate, but no effect size for teaching method itself, so no impact magnitude is coded (`i?`, which does not mean no effect). The ANCOVA table is not internally consistent: the method row gives the same value for its sum of squares and its mean square despite 2 degrees of freedom, and the corrected-model and total sums of squares imply an R² near 0.81, not 0.28.

> "based on the main effect of teaching method (F2, 207) =586.046, p‐value of 0.000 is less than α=0.05, there is a significant mean difference between the post‐test achievement scores of the two experimental groups and the control group"
