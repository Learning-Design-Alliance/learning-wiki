# Exploratory analyses suggest NDL increased requested funding, reduced reviewer burden, and shifted proposal quality ratings for two GEO programs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The two GEO NDL programs requested $265,000 to $294,000 more funding, required 0.9 to 1.8 fewer reviewers, and were less likely to be rated fair (by 4 to 9 percentage points) or underperforming (by one percentage point) relative to counterfactuals. [→ Fesler 2022](#fesler-2022)
`q2 i?` There were not consistent patterns for number of collaborative proposals per project or exceptional reviews across the two programs. [→ Fesler 2022 (2)](#fesler-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Fesler 2022

Fesler, Lily and Lindsay Fox. (2022). No-deadlines Synthetic Control and Exploratory Outcomes Analysis and Recommendations for a Future Rigorous Evaluation [Memorandum]. Alexandria, VA: National Science Foundation. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/no-deadlines-synthetic-control-and-exploratory-outcomes-analysis-and-recommendations-for-a-future

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same exploratory synthetic control analysis of the two GEO NDL programs; the memo reports the programs "requested $265,000 to $294,000 more funding" and "required 0.9 to 1.8 fewer reviewers" relative to their synthetic counterfactuals, with no significance tests.

> "Model estimates suggest that NDL programs received 123 to 128 fewer proposals, requested $265,000 to $294,000 more funding, required 0.9 to 1.8 fewer reviewers, and were less likely to be rated as fair (by 4 to 9 percentage points) or underperforming (by one percentage point)."

> "There were not consistent patterns for number of collaborative proposals per project and exceptional reviews."
