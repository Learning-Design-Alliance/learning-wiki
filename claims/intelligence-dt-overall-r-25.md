## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-analysis of 849 coefficients from 112 studies (N = 34,610), corrected for attenuation and modeled with between- and within-study variance components. The estimate was "r = .25, 95%-CI [.21, .30]" with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 92.07%).

> "The estimated overall correlation between DT and intelligence was r = .25, 95%-CI [.21, .30] (m = 112, k = 849)."
