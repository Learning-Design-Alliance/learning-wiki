The authors' account of a 2-day invitational meeting on guidelines for journal authors making practice recommendations. The group concluded that "even prescriptives that were based on the strongest evidence for causality were fraught with numerous generalization problems." No data are reported; this is the participants' expert judgment.

> "However, it soon became apparent that even prescriptives that were based on the strongest evidence for causality were fraught with numerous generalization problems."
