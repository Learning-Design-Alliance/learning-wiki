## Subclaims
`q3 i?` HSRT trainees distributed training over 7 ± 3 days on average versus two scheduled days in CIRT (days with training 2 ± 0 vs 7 ± 3, p < 0.001), while median total training times did not differ (368 vs 330 min; p = 0.89). [→ Sloth Sigurd Beier 2021](#sloth-sigurd-beier-2021)

## Evidence

### Sloth Sigurd Beier 2021

Sloth Sigurd Beier, Jensen Rune Dall, Seyer-Hansen Mikkel, Christensen Mette Krogh, De Win Gunter. (2021). Remote training in laparoscopy: a randomized trial comparing home-based self-regulated training to centralized instructor-regulated training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08429-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Training-pattern analysis from logged SurgTrac data in the randomized trial. The printed medians, means and p values are quoted; only 28% of HSRT sessions fell on protected training-time days. No standardized effect size printed.

> "There were no significant differences in median total training times (368 vs. 330 min; p = 0.89), total training time to pass (373 vs. 312 min; p = 0.39), or mean training variation (6 vs. 6 different exercises; p = 0.86) between the two groups (Table 2). While the participants in CIRT group could only train on the two scheduled days, the participants in the HSRT group, on average, distributed their training over 7 ± 3 days"
