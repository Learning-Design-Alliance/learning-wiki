`q3 i?` After reducing from five to four options, overall MCQ scores were slightly lower in Chem A, slightly higher in A&P A, and similar in the other two courses, with no statistically significant difference in two courses. [→ Fozzard Nicolette 2018](#fozzard-nicolette-2018)

## Evidence

### Fozzard Nicolette 2018

Fozzard Nicolette, Pearson Andrew, du Toit Eugene, Naug Helen, Wen William, Peak Ian R. (2018). Analysis of MCQ and distractor use in a large first year Health Faculty Foundation Program: assessing the effects of changing from five to four options. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1346-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of overall MCQ scores between sequential cohorts (first offering five-option, second offering four-option) using independent t-tests per course. Chem A scores were "slightly lower at second offering (by 0.8 marks out of 25)" and A&P A slightly higher by 1.3 marks out of 60; Chem B and A&P B showed no significant difference.

> "For the Chem A exam, the student scores were slightly lower at second offering (by 0.8 marks out of 25), and in A&P A overall scores were slightly higher (by 1.3 marks out of 60). In the other two courses, marks were similar between offerings, with no statistically significant difference between the cohorts (p > .05)."
