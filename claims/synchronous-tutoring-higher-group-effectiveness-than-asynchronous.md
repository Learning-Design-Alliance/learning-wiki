## Evidence

### Edelbring Samuel 2020

Edelbring Samuel, Alehagen Siw, Mörelius Evalotte, Johansson AnnaKarin, Rytterström Patrik. (2020). Should the PBL tutor be present? A cross-sectional study of group effectiveness in synchronous and asynchronous settings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02018-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of TGEI scores among 221 Swedish specialist nursing students, merging the two synchronous groups (n = 153) against the asynchronous consultant-tutor group (n = 68), using the Mann–Whitney U test. Medians for cognitive, motivational and overall effectiveness all favoured the synchronous groups; demotivational aspects were reported as n.s.

> "When merging the data from the two synchronous groups that had a tutor either present in the room (A) or online (B) and comparing to the asynchronous group with a consultant tutor (C), the synchronous group had significantly higher scores on cognitive aspects, motivational aspects and overall rating of group effectiveness"
