## Evidence

### Eckardt F 2026

Eckardt F, Gerhardt M, Priglinger S. (2026). Teaching ophthalmic microsurgical skills: conventional microscopy versus heads-up visualization. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10269-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-center crossover study of 16 medical students and 16 residents without prior microsurgical experience; paired analyses of EyeSi-generated objective metrics. The article reports no significant difference in overall scores, with no effect size printed for this contrast.

> "The overall performance scores did not differ between the heads-up display and the conventional microscopic visualization (p = 0.376)."
