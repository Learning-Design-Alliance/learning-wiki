# Interview respondents identify collaborative relationships, trust in evidence and its users, clarity on research types, and supportive leadership as facilitators of evidence use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r?` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Factors facilitating evidence use include collaborative relationships between evidence producers and decision-makers, trust in the evidence and in the decision-makers using it, clarity on how different research types can be used, and leadership that supports evidence use with staff buy-in. [→ Julieta Lugo-Gil 2019](#julieta-lugo-gil-2019)

## Evidence

### Julieta Lugo-Gil 2019

Julieta Lugo-Gil, Dana Jean-Baptiste, Livia Frasso Jaramillo. (2019). Use of Evidence to Drive Decision-Making in Government. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/use-of-evidence-to-drive-decision-making-in-government

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Phase 2 qualitative interviews with experts in evaluation, dissemination, and evidence use in government. Respondents pointed to facilitators including "building trust in the evidence that researchers produce" and "having leadership that supports and promotes the use of evidence and buy-in from the organization's staff." No effect sizes are reported.

> "establishing collaborative relationships between evidence producers and decision-makers, building trust in the evidence that researchers produce and in the decision-makers that use the evidence within the government, improving clarity on how different types of research and their findings can be used in decision-making"
