## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The transition from lab to home working environments, isolation, and absent work norms introduced motivational challenges that reduced mentees' progress toward their goals. [→ Alaee 2023](#alaee-2023)

## Evidence

### Alaee 2023

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of remote REU mentees; the authors report the shift to home working "introduced several motivational challenges," compounded by lack of social interaction and missing peer pressure to work; some mentees felt discouraged and less productive at home.

> "The sudden transition from working in a lab environment to working at home in pajama pants and hanging out with family members introduced several motivational challenges."
