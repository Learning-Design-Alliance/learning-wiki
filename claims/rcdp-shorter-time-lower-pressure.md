Blinded post-training competency assessment in the same 24-resident RCT found "the reduction pressure was lower, and the procedure time was shorter in the RCDP group", with the printed means and P values. Pre-post within-group procedural-time effect sizes were 4.92 (RCDP) and −3.19 (TTM) as printed in Table 3.

> "The reduction pressure was lower, and the procedure time was shorter in the RCDP group (5.83 ± 0.27 kPa vs. 6.40 ± 0.10 kPa, P = 0.023 and 74.26 ± 8.25 s vs.133.41 ± 9.07 s, P = 0.044, respectively)."
