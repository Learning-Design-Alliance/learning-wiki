Marion Décaillet 2024¶

Marion Décaillet, Solange Denervaud, Cléo Huguenin-Virchaux, Laureline Besuchet, Céline J. Fischer Fumeaux, Micah M. Murray & Juliane Schneider. (2024). The impact of premature birth on auditory-visual processes in very preterm schoolchildren. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00257-3

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Kruskall–Wallis comparison of overall accuracy between FT (M = 92.3%, SD = 8.45) and VPT (M = 93.2%, SD = 6.69) children: χ2(2) = 0.01, p = 0.94, "similar" for each condition separately as well; adding SES and fluid intelligence as covariates did not change the pattern.

"The Kruskall– Wallis test showed that the general accuracy of FT children (M =9 2 . 3 % ,SD = 8.45) was similar to the accuracy of VPT children (M = 93.2%,SD = 6.69), (χ2(2) = 0.01,p = 0.94), as well as for each condition separately"

Related Claims¶

  • Very preterm schoolchildren show generally slower and more variable reaction times than full-term children across auditory, visual, and multisensory detection conditions — related
  • Visual dominance predominates in both VPT and FT schoolchildren, and sensory dominance does not differ between groups — related
  • Both VPT and FT schoolchildren exhibit multisensory facilitation, with statistically similar percentage gains in mean reaction times and variability between groups — related
  • Multisensory facilitation in full-term but not very preterm schoolchildren significantly violated the race model, indicating integrative processes beyond probability summation in FT children only — related
  • Multisensory gain in very preterm schoolchildren does not correlate with standardized cognitive or clinical measures — related
