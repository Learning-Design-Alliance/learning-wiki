Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia. (2023). Psychological Benefits of Attending Forest School for Preschool Children: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09750-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of two quasi-experimental pre-post studies (Wojciehowski & Ernst, 2018; Ernst & Burcak, 2019) using the TCAM task, as reported in the review's Creativity section. The review reports that "only the former reported significant improvements in all three creative dimensions" at posttest.

> "At the posttest, participants in forest preschools demonstrated significantly higher scores compared to the pretest for all the creative dimensions assessed (fluency, originality, and imagination; p < 0.001 for all dimensions), while those in indoor preschools did not."
