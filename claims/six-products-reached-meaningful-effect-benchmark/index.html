Evidence¶

Brasiel 2016¶

Brasiel, S., Jeong, S., Ames, C., Lawanto, K., Yuan, M., & Martin, T. (2016). Effects of educational technology on mathematics achievement for K-12 students in Utah. Journal of Online Learning Research, 2(3), 205-226. https://www.learntechlib.org/j/JOLR/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Impact analysis of 44,497 full-sample and 4,470 fidelity-sample students across six products, using propensity-score matching and logistic regression on state assessment proficiency. The authors use 0.16, the effect size from the Cheung and Slavin (2013) meta-analysis, as the point of comparison for educational meaningfulness.

"All six products included in the impact study, had at least one sample reach or exceed the 0.16 effect size level for having an effect on the proportion of students meeting proficiency on the state mathematics assessment."
