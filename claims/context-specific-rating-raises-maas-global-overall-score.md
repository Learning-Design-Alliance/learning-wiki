`q2 i?` In a paired re-rating of 40 consultations, the mean overall MAAS-Global score increased from 2.98 (standard rating) to 3.66 (context-specific rating), a statistically significant increase with effect size 0.84. [→ Essers Geurt 2013](#essers-geurt-2013)

## Evidence

### Essers Geurt 2013

Essers Geurt, Kramer Anneke, Andriesse Boukje, van Weel Chris, van der Vleuten Cees, van Dulmen Sandra. (2013). Context factors in general practitioner - patient encounters and their impact on assessing communication skills - an exploratory study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-65

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample re-rating of 40 videotaped Dutch GP consultations by the same rater, comparing standard MAAS-Global rating with a context-specific rating. The "mean overall score in the standard rating was 2.98" versus 3.66 (p < 0.00); the article reports the effect size for the total mean score was 0.84, a large effect.

> "The mean overall score in the standard rating was 2.98, while in the context-specific rating it was 3.66 (p < 0.00). Effect sizes were large for three items and moderate for another four items."
