# High reinforcement improves STM and WM performance in both ADHD subtypes but not in controls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` High reinforcement (feedback + 10 euros) improved STM and WM performance in both ADHD-C and ADHD-I children. [→ Dovis 2015](#dovis-2015)
`q3 i?` High reinforcement did not improve CE-related performance in either subtype. [→ Dovis 2015](#dovis-2015)

## Evidence

### Dovis 2015

Dovis, S., Van der Oord, S., Wiers, R.W., & Prins, P.J.M. (2015). ADHD subtype differences in reinforcement sensitivity and visuospatial working memory. Journal of Clinical Child and Adolescent Psychology, 44(5), 859-874. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2014.895940

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

This is the reinforcement-manipulation result of the experiment comparing a standard level (feedback only) with a high level of reinforcement (feedback + 10 euros) in 27 children with ADHD-I, 70 with ADHD-C, and 40 controls. The article reports that "High reinforcement improved STM and WM performance in both subtypes but not in controls" and that "This improvement was equally pronounced in both subtypes." It also reports that "High reinforcement did not improve CE-related performance." No effect sizes are printed in the supplied text, so impact is null.
