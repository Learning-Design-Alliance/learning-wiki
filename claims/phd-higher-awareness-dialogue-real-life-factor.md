# PhD teachers show significantly higher awareness than MA teachers of incorporating dialogue and learners' real life into second language instruction

## Evidence

### Mahmoodarabi 2015

Mahmoodarabi, M., & Khodabakhsh, M. R. (2015). Critical Pedagogy: EFL Teachers' Views, Experience and Academic Degrees. English Language Teaching, 8(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n6p100

`q3 · i1`

Between-subjects ANOVA on Factor 1 among the 403 validated respondents. Descriptives showed PhD teachers (M = 5.05) highest, followed by BA (M = 4.71) and MA (M = 4.69); Scheffe tests found the PhD–MA difference significant (MD = .36, P < .05).

> "Based on the results displayed in Table 3 (F (2, 400) = 3.71, P < .05, Partial η2 = .016 representing a weak effect size), it can be concluded that there were significant differences between the BA, MA and PhD teachers' awareness of the first factor of critical pedagogy."
