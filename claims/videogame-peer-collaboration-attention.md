## Evidence

### Vélez-Agosto 2018

Vélez-Agosto, N. M. & Rivas-Vélez, A. (2018). Benefits and meanings of educating with videogames in a Puerto Rican sixth grade public school classroom. International Journal of Educational Technology, 5(1), 9-19. http://educationaltechnology.net/ijet/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant observation in the EG recorded competition (e.g. a student showing her screen saying "Ok, you'll see") and peer support such as giving game instructions; the authors state the game fomented positive interaction between students and that such exchange is important because learning is socially constructed.

> "Our qualitative findings showed students engaging in competition and supporting each other during the intervention."

### Vélez-Agosto 2018 (2)

Vélez-Agosto, N. M. & Rivas-Vélez, A. (2018). Benefits and meanings of educating with videogames in a Puerto Rican sixth grade public school classroom. International Journal of Educational Technology, 5(1), 9-19. http://educationaltechnology.net/ijet/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observer-as-participant field notes recorded long periods of silence and focused posture during play, interpreted as "careful attention to the game or immersion"; the teacher and director interviews corroborated that students seemed engaged most of the time.

> "Our qualitative findings show that students pay more attention to learning through the use of videogames."
