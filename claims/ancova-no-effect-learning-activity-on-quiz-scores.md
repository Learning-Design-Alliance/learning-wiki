## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An analysis of covariance was performed to adjust for potential confounders (previous neuroscience experience and device used); it found "no significant effect of the learning activity" on any quiz outcome, with the printed F and p values as quoted.

> "After adjusting for device used and previous neuroscience experience there remained no significant effect of the learning activity on the test score of the first quiz (F(1, 24) = 0.42, p = 0.5), second quiz (F(1, 20) = 0.78, p = 0.4), or difference between the first and second quiz (F(1, 20) = 0.01, p = 0.9)."
