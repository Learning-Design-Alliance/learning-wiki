## Evidence

### Basoz 2016

Basoz, T. & Can, T., D. (2016). The effectiveness of computers on vocabulary learning among preschool children: A semiotic approach. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science, 11(1), 02-08. http://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/cjes/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on the CAVI experimental group (n=16) in a quasi-experimental pre/post study. The article reports "A statistically significant difference" between pre- and post-test, with eta squared (.09) described as a large effect size; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between the group’s pre-test (M= .31, SD= .70) and post-test scores (M=13.31, SD= 4.28), t (15)= -13.57, p< .05 (two-tailed). The eta squared statistic (.09) indicated a large effect size."
