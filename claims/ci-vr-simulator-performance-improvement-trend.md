### Frendø M 2022

Frendø M, Frithioff A, Konge L, Cayé-Thomasen P, Sørensen MS, Andersen SAW. (2022). Cochlear implant surgery: virtual reality simulation training and transfer of skills to cadaver dissection—A randomized, controlled trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group analysis of the intervention group's CI VR training performances (n = 9 per Table 1), rated on the CISAT. Mean scores 'improved by 22.5% from 25.3 points' at procedure one to 30.4 at procedure three; LMM P =.065.

> "Performance gradually improved during the VR CI simulation training: mean scores improved by 22.5% from 25.3 points [95% confidence intervals (23.1–27.6)] at procedure one to 30.4 [95% confidence interval (27.7–33.2)] points at procedure three (P =.065, LMM)."
