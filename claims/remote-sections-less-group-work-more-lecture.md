### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

Student survey data (4585 analyzed responses) plotted in simplex space; permutation testing compared modality centers in the activity tetrahedron. Remote sections showed "21.6% less group work" than in-person sections, equivalent to 108 min less over a quarter, with a permuted p < 0.00001.

> "We found that students from remote discussion sections reported experiencing 21.6% less group work than students from in-person discussion sections, or the equivalent of 108 min less of group work over a 10-week quarter."
