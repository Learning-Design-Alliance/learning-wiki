## Evidence

### Sörman K 2025

Sörman K, El Alaoui S, Mårtensson S, Larsson M, Knez R, Ljudvåg M, Mac R, Dahlström K, Nowak YE, Fors U, Eckerström J. (2025). Virtual patient simulation strengthens confidence in clinical conversations among undergraduated nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08413-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted linear mixed-effects model on the 15-item weighted MCQ knowledge test in the RCT (N = 54). The main Group effect was significant while the interaction was not, "indicating that both groups improved at a similar rate but the VP arm maintained an ≈ 3.6-point advantage at every wave". No standardized effect size printed.

> "Objective knowledge was significantly higher in the VP group overall by an average of 3.6 points (F[1, 35.58] = 6.07, p = 0.019), although the group × time interaction was nonsignificant (p = 0.230), suggesting parallel improvement across groups"
