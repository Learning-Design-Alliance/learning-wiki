## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2013

Ayush Gupta, Andrew Elby, Luke D. Conlin. (2013). How substance-based ontologies for gravity can be productive: A case study. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The authors' interpretive analysis (type e) of the Episode 1 small-group discussion: Lynn felt her intuition that gravity pulls harder on heavier objects was at odds with the known answer. The authors identify the group's "tendency to seek coherence between multiple ideas" as a key epistemological resource.

> "this tendency to seek coherence between multiple ideas is shared by the other members in her small group and is a key epistemological resource that enables them to make significant progress towards resolving this tension."
