## Evidence

### Dietrich EL 2025

Dietrich EL, McWatt SC. (2025). Exploring perceptions of alternative assessment and grading in graduate anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11797536/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of student interviews (n = 9) identified unclear instructor intentions as a subtheme; this student quote came from experience in a quiz-based block. Students felt clear communication of goals was essential for learning outcomes and stress reduction.

> "It made me a little more stressed throughout because I didn't know what was expected of me, like I had to fill in those gaps for myself."

### Dietrich EL 2025 (2)

Dietrich EL, McWatt SC. (2025). Exploring perceptions of alternative assessment and grading in graduate anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11797536/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Under the instructor approachability theme, students described how perceived instructor investment shaped motivation and how low approachability made requesting feedback stressful, with one student unable to perceive instructor comments "as a motivator for my future studies."

> "Asking for feedback kind of [felt] like you're failing because you didn't meet [their] expectations once."

### Dietrich EL 2025 (3)

Dietrich EL, McWatt SC. (2025). Exploring perceptions of alternative assessment and grading in graduate anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11797536/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Under the effort-outcome alignment theme, students reported disconnects between preparation effort and feedback or grades received in a feedback-based block, and that less detailed feedback than expected left them unmotivated.

> "It added to my stress because when I kept getting, like, 'meeting expectations,' and not 'exceeding,' I was thinking 'what am I doing wrong,' like, I'm trying my best."
