## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The longer a MOOC video is, the higher the in-video dropout rate, i.e. learners are less likely to finish watching it. [→ Simon Fauvel 2015](#simon-fauvel-2015)

## Evidence

### Simon Fauvel 2015

Simon Fauvel, Han Yu. (2015). A Survey on Artificial Intelligence and Data Mining for MOOCs. Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The survey reports a data-mining study of video watching activities [21] that found "a strong correlation between video length and engagement". The same study found re-watches lead to higher in-video dropout rates than first-time viewing, and produced second-by-second plots of play, pause and skip events.

> "[21] also finds a strong correlation between video length and engagement: the longer the video, the higher the in-video dropout rate (i.e. a learner does not finish watching the video)."
