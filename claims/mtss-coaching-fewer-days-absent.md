# Students in MTSS-coached schools had significantly fewer days absent in both implementation years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Treatment-school students had significantly fewer days absent than PSM comparison students in both 2021/22 (g = 0.86) and 2022/23 (g = 0.91). [→ Gage 2024](#gage-2024)

## Evidence

### Gage 2024

Gage, N. A., Salomonson, K., Ballew, T., Clavenna-Deane, B., & Grasley-Boy, N. (2024). The Impact of Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) on Student Attendance and Behavior. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655758

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models of days absent for students present before the pandemic, using 2019/20 absences for baseline equivalence (baseline means 12.6 vs 14.8 days; equivalence g = 0.138) and controlling for demographics and baseline absences. Treatment estimates were -12.68 (2021/22) and -13.19 (2022/23) days, both p < .001.

> "We found a statistically significant treatment effect for both implementation years, suggesting that students in treatment schools had significantly fewer days absent than PSM students in comparison schools. The effect sizes were g = 0.86 for the 2021/22 school year and g = 0.91 for the 2022/23 school year."
