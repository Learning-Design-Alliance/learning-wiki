# Structural paths are stable when continued-use intention and current use are modeled as separate outcomes, and AI anxiety is not directly related to continued use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i0`–`i1`

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` With intention and current use as separate outcomes, every structural path retains sign and significance (e.g., literacy: β = 0.17 intention, β = 0.14 current use). [→ Peichen Hu 2026](#peichen-hu-2026)
`q3 i0` AI anxiety is essentially unrelated to continued use directly (r = −0.06, ns), so anxious students do not use ChatGPT less. [→ Peichen Hu 2026 (2)](#peichen-hu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Peichen Hu 2026

Peichen Hu. (2026). Psychological predictors of continued ChatGPT use among university students: the roles of AI literacy, trust, and academic self-efficacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1900190. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1900190

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Re-estimation of the full structural model with intention and current use as separate, correlated outcomes in the same 450-user sample; fit equivalent to the main model and all printed standardized paths (e.g., "β = 0.17", "β = 0.14") retained sign and significance.

> "AI literacy predicted intention (β = 0.17, p = 0.002) and current use (β = 0.14, p = 0.009); trust predicted intention (β = 0.32, p < 0.001) and current use (β = 0.30, p < 0.001); and academic self-eﬃcacy predicted intention (β = 0.24, p < 0.001) and current use (β = 0.32,p <0.001)."

### Peichen Hu 2026 (2)

Peichen Hu. (2026). Psychological predictors of continued ChatGPT use among university students: the roles of AI literacy, trust, and academic self-efficacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1900190. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1900190

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation reported in the discussion of the moderation result for the 450-user sample: "r =− 0.06, ns" between AI anxiety and continued use, a printed correlation below r = .10.

> "AI anxiety was essentially unrelated to continued use directly (r =− 0.06, ns), so the moderation does not say that anxious students use ChatGPT less."
