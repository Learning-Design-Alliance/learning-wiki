## Evidence

### Gauer JL 2017

Gauer JL, Jackson JB. (2017). The association of USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK scores with residency match specialty and location. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1358579

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of USMLE scores and residency match data from five graduating classes at the University of Minnesota Medical School (N = 1054). The MANOVA used Pillai's Trace due to unequal group sizes, and the article reports "partial eta squared = 0.132" for the omnibus effect.

> "A one-way MANOVA found a statistically significant difference between residency specialties on the combined dependent variables, F (38, 2068) = 8.307, p < 0.001; Pillai’s Trace = 0.265; partial eta squared = 0.132."
