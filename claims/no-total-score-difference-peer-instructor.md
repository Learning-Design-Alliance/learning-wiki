## Evidence

### Yu Ji Hye 2021

Yu Ji Hye, Lee Mi Jin, Kim Soon Sun, Yang Min Jae, Cho Hyo Jung, Noh Choong Kyun, Lee Gil Ho, Lee Su Kyung, Song Mi Ryoung, Lee Jang Hoon, Kim Miran, Jung Yun Jung. (2021). Assessment of medical students’ clinical performance using high-fidelity simulation: comparison of peer and instructor assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02952-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test comparison of total scores across 34 groups rated by peers and instructors. The article reports the peer mean of 3.50 ± 0.68 and instructor mean of 3.50 ± 0.51 with "no statistically significant differences" (t = -0.033, p = 0.974 in Table 5).

> "The peer rating score was 3.50 ± 0.68, while the instructor evaluation score was 3.50 ± 0.51, indicating no statistically significant differences."
