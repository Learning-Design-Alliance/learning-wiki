## Evidence

### Cassandra Paul 2013

Cassandra Paul, Wendell H. Potter & Brenda Weiss. (2013). Grading by Category: A simple method for providing students with meaningful feedback on exams in large courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1304.0222](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1304.0222)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' description of an affordance of the GBC spreadsheet: category counts per item quantify how many students made each error. They contrast this with traditional grading, where the best the instructor could do is determine the average score for each problem.

> "With GBC, the grade spreadsheet can easily reveal the number of students who make the same error, which gives the instructor information regarding class-wide misconceptions or difficulties."
