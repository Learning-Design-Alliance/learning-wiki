## Evidence

### Kuo 2013

Kuo, E., Hull, M.M., Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2013). How students blend conceptual and formal mathematical reasoning in solving physics problems. Science Education, 97(1), 32-57. https://doi.org/10.1002/sce.21043

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study of Alex's think-aloud and written solution to the Two Balls Problem, where she computed 50 m/s and 52 m/s and subtracted. Her follow-up comments showed conceptual reasoning that was not stably connected to the equation.

> "We tentatively conclude that, during this segment, Alex used the velocity equation as a tool for numerical computation."
