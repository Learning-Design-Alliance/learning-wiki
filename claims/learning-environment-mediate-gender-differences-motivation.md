Yangqiuting Li, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How learning environment predicts male and female students' physics motivational beliefs in introductory physics courses. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multi-group SEM first showed strong measurement invariance and no difference in any regression coefficients by gender, permitting the gender mediation analysis. Female students had significantly lower scores on all environment constructs (peer interaction, recognition, belonging) with medium effect sizes (d = 0.49, 0.46, 0.56).

> "Fig. 2 reveals that the gender differences in students' post-self-efficacy, post- interest, and post-identity shown in Table I were mediated by the different components of the learning environment"
