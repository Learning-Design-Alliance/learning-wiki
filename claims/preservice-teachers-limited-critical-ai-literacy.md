`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Navío-Inglés et al. (2025), a mixed-methods study in which 154 pre-service teachers evaluated AI- versus human-written texts. The review attributes the finding that many participants "failed to identify AI-authored work"; the study's design is described only as reported in the review.

> "Found pre-service teachers rated ChatGPT-generated texts higher on structure and coherence but lower on emotional expression. Many failed to identify AI-authored work, underscoring limited critical literacy."
