The authors' recommendation for advanced pre-university courses, noting Big Bang nucleosynthesis explains the abundance of light elements and provides "a very good method of testing the quantitative predictions of Big Bang theory". An author advocacy statement, not a tested result.

> "We advocate that teachers include nucleosynthesis in any advanced level course as, taken in combination with the Hubble relation and the cosmic microwave background radiation, they make the Big Bang theory appear highly watertight."
