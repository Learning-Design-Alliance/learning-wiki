Evidence¶

Karle 2026¶

Karle, V. L., Suggate, S. P., Winter, R., & Stoeger, H. (2026). When Fine is Large and Gross is Small: A Meta-Analysis of Links Between Fine and Gross Motor Skills with Academic-Cognitive Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10161-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Exploratory mixed-effects regression with a motor × cognitive-skill (intelligence k = 69 / executive functions k = 438) interaction, controlling for study quality (which was non-significant, p = .810). The interaction contrast is reported in Fisher's Z.

"Fisher’s Z scores and post-hoc ANOV A comparisons indicated that FiMS linked more strongly to intelligence than GMS did, Z = 0.291 vs. 0.128, p < .001, with a similar albeit much smaller difference for executive functions, Z = 0.229 vs. 0.208, p = .007."
