## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The transition from face-to-face to VW-based teaching is a gradual, multi-faceted, non-linear process in which lecturers alternate between contexts and reshape pre-existing teaching beliefs and practices. [→ Dai 2023](#dai-2023)

## Evidence

### Dai 2023

Dai, K., Garcia, J., & Olave-Encina, K. (2023). In-between worlds: Chilean university lecturers' experiences of teaching transition between face-to-face and virtual reality contexts during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10217-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview and focus-group study of 18 Chilean lecturers using Second Life; thematic analysis of participant narratives supports this claim. The authors state the transition "was a non-linear itinerant process".

> "Based on the participants' experiences transitioning between a face-to-face and a VW-based context was a non-linear itinerant process in which the senses of identity and agency were (re)shaped and influenced by the VW in which the teaching took place."
