# FMS subdomain correlations vary within academic domains: nonword reading strongest, letter reading weakest, intelligence strongest among cognitive skills

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Within reading, nonword reading showed the strongest FMS correlation (r = .332) and letter reading the weakest (r = .282); within cognition, intelligence correlated most strongly (r = .366), exceeding executive functions and vocabulary (both p < .001).
