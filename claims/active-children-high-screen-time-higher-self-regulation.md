### López-Gil 2020

López-Gil, J.F., Oriol-Granado, X., Izquierdo, M., Ramírez-Vélez, R., Fernández-Vergara, O., Olloquequi, J., & García-Hermoso, A. (2020). Healthy Lifestyle Behaviors and Their Association with Self-Regulation in Chilean Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17165676

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA with Bonferroni pairwise post-hoc comparisons across four combined PA and screen time groups in 1561 children, adjusted for covariates. Both active groups "showed higher SR compared to the inactive/high ST group".

> "Active groups both with low (p = 0.002) and high ST (p = 0.020) showed higher SR compared to the inactive/high ST group."
