# Teachers used Forms to support social-emotional learning through daily well-being check-ins and anonymous voice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One elementary teacher sent a daily open-text well-being question via Forms and credited peer anonymity with students feeling safe sharing feelings. [→ Digital Promise 2018](#digital-promise-2018)
`q2 i?` A third-grade teacher used a Forms survey of preferred work partners to identify socially isolated students and organize support. [→ Digital Promise 2018 (2)](#digital-promise-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Digital Promise 2018

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence from one elementary teacher who asks “How are you feeling today?” each morning via Forms; he believes students feel safe because of the peer anonymity Forms provides.

> "Now, within a matter of minutes at the beginning of a day I already know how they’re all feeling."

### Digital Promise 2018 (2)

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence from a third-grade teacher who used a Form asking students to choose three work-well-with friends to detect students not chosen by peers and coordinate colleague support.

> "I had five students whose names did not get picked for anyone’s top three and so I reached out to a few colleagues to kind of adopt those students and to set goals with them to try and boost their morale."
