## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ginty 2022

Ginty, A. T., Tyra, A. T., Young, D. A., Brindle, R. C., de Rooij, S. R. & Williams, S. E. (2022). Cardiovascular reactions to acute psychological stress and academic achievement. Psychophysiology, 59, e14064. https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.14064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unadjusted regressions in the prospective sample; the article reports no statistically significant associations for "SV reactivity", "SBP reactivity", or "DBP reactivity". SV showed a non-significant trend (p = .075) and accounted for 2.9% of variance in adjusted models.

> "There were no statistically significant associations of SV reactivity (β = 0.170, p = .075, R 2 = 0.029), SBP reactivity (β = 0.058, p = .506, R 2 = 0.003), or DBP reactivity (β = −0.016, p = .859, R 2 = 0.000) with A‐Level performance."
