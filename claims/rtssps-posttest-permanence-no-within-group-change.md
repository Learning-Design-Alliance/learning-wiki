## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sarican 2018

Sarican, G., Akgunduz, D. (2018). The impact of integrated STEM education on academic achievement, reflective thinking skills towards problem solving and permanence in learning in science education. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(1), 94-113. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1196043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-group t-tests within the 44-student quasi-experiment: CG t = 0.233, p = 0.818 (52.86 vs 52.25); EG t = 2.323, p = 0.074 (51.09 vs 54.75). Neither within-group change reached significance.

> "there was no significant difference between the average scores of RT SSPS post-test and RTSSPS PT in the control group students (p > 0.05). There was no significant difference between the RTSSPS post-test and RTSSPS PT scores of the students in the experimental group (p > 0.05)."
