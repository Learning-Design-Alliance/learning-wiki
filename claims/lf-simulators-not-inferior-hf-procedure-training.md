### Lefor Alan Kawarai 2020

Lefor Alan Kawarai, Harada Kanako, Kawahira Hiroshi, Mitsuishi Mamoru. (2020). The effect of simulator fidelity on procedure skill training: a literature review. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5ea6.ae73

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A PRISMA-guided literature review of 17 randomized studies directly comparing HF and LF simulators for procedural skill training, abstracted to a standard data sheet. The review's synthesis found "15/17 studies have the similar conclusion" that HF training was not superior to LF training.

> "This review includes studies of a wide range of procedure skill training studies and shows that 15/17 studies have the similar conclusion, that training with an HF simulator does not have outcomes superior to training with an LF simulator."
