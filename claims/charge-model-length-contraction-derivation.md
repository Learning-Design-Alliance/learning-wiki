## Evidence

### D. J. Miller 2009

D. J. Miller. (2009). A constructive approach to the special theory of relativity. https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0902 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Sec. II A: the equilibrium condition Ex(xv) = 0 is solved using the electric and magnetic fields of charges in uniform motion obtained directly from Maxwell's equations, giving xv = sqrt(1 - beta^2)L = L/gamma. The article states the "equilibrium separation of the + Q charges is reduced" as derived, not measured.

> "Hence, when the charge assembly is moving with constant speed v, the moving “measuring rod” represented by the equilibrium separation of the + Q charges is reduced to 2 √ 1 − β2L from their separation, the length 2 L of the “measuring rod”, when at rest."
