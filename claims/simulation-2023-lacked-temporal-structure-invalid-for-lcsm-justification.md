# Sorjonen et al.'s (2023) simulation lacked a temporal structure and therefore cannot justify replacing autoregressive effects with covariances in the LCSM

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 2023 simulation data were generated without temporal structure, so they do not demonstrate that AR effects distort LCSM parameters; they show only that dynamic parameters are uninterpretable for non-temporal data. [→ Núñez-Regueiro 2026](#nunez-regueiro-2026)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Design inspection of the Sorjonen et al. (2023) simulation: X1 and X2 were independently generated from the same latent variable, so the data "lacked a temporal structure" and did not model the change processes at stake in LCSM methodology.

> "inspection of their simulation design reveals that their simulated data did not reflect change scores (nor an LCSM), because it lacked a temporal structure"
