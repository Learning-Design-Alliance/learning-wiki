Categorization of 246 player–AI chat queries by two independent raters (κ = 0.78–0.80) using a scheme based on level reference, phrasing, and the ICAP framework. The article reports "No query to the AI was categorized as hypothesis or idea formation"; 124 queries were recommendations for actions and 157 were level-related.

> "No query to the AI was categorized as hypothesis or idea formation. Users therefore never formulated their own assumptions, conjectures, or ideas to the AI."
