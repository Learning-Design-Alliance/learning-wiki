## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Creupelandt Hanne 2019

Creupelandt Hanne, Anthierens Sibyl, Habraken Hilde, Sirdifield Coral, Siriwardena Aloysius Niroshan, Christiaens Thierry. (2019). A tailored e-learning gives long-term changes in determinants of GPs’ benzodiazepines prescribing: a pretest-posttest study with self-report assessments. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2019.1663591

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest self-report study of 244 Flemish GPs analyzed with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests at six-month follow-up: participants "disagreed more strongly with the statement that the advantages of BZDs outweigh their disadvantages (Z = −2.853, p = .004)".

> "Participants disagreed more strongly with the statement that the advantages of BZDs outweigh their disadvantages (Z = −2.853, p = .004) and they judged more strongly that non-drug alternatives are as effective as BZDs (Z = −4.487, p = .000)."
