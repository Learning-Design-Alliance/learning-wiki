Vicens-Blanes, F., Miró-Bonet, R., & Molina-Mula, J. (2021). Analysis of Nurses' and Physicians' Attitudes, Knowledge, and Perceptions toward Fever in Children: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182312444

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of five educational-program studies for nurses (random-effects model, SMD at 95% CI). The review reports a statistically significant change in attitudes; the printed heterogeneity for attitudes was I2: 88.25%. No effect size value is printed.

> "In general, meta-analysis revealed that educational methods cause a statistically significant change in knowledge and attitudes, as opposed to perceptions which did not show a statistically significant change."
