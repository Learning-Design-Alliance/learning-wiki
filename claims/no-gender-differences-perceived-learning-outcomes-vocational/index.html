Evidence¶

Song Yafeng 2026¶

Song Yafeng, Zhao Kangjian, Li Li, Dong Wei. (2026). Research on the Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence Usage Behavior on the Learning Outcomes of Higher Vocational Students. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071166

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Independent-samples t-test comparing male (n = 388) and female (n = 722) students in the 1110-response survey. The article reports p > 0.05 for all sub-dimensions and the overall score; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

"Test results were p > 0.05, indicating no statistically significant differences between male and female students in knowledge mastery, skill application, competency development, or overall perceived learning outcomes."
