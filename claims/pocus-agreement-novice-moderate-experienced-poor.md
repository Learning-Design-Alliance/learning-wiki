Subgroup analysis of the same 59-video, seven-rater reliability study. Agreement was higher for novices than for experienced users; the article suggests novices' clear lack of skill "may have demonstrated a clear lack of POCUS skill that was easy for the raters to distinguish".

> "The agreement between seven raters for the novice and experienced POCUS users ranged from 0.53 to 0.69 (n = 39) and − 0.08 to 0.17 (n = 20) respectively."
