q2 · i? · design · r2

Rasch analysis (Winsteps, Version 3.90.2) of the combined CPS (1,153 teachers) and FACES (n=363) spring 2015 pilot samples assessed measure reliability. Measures retained but below 0.80 were revised for a new round of testing.

"Most measures on the teacher survey (19 of 27) had reliabilities at or above our preferred threshold of 0.80, and all but one measure met our 0.70 minimum threshold. The one measure with reliability below 0.70—Early Childhood Discipline—has been removed from the survey for future iterations"
