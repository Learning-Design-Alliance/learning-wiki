## Evidence

### Isaranuwatchai W 2016

Isaranuwatchai W, Alam F, Hoch J, Boet S. (2016). A cost-effectiveness analysis of self-debriefing versus instructor debriefing for simulated crises in perioperative medicine in Canada. J Educ Eval Health Prof. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.44

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cost analysis from the hospital perspective within a randomized controlled trial of 50 anesthesia residents in Canada, reported in the Results section. The self-debriefing group's average cost was "lower than that of the instructor-debriefing group" (Can$233.50±2.30 vs Can$437.60±2.26, P<0.0001).

> "The average cost for the self-debriefing group (Can$233.50±2.30) was lower than that of the instructor-debriefing group (Can$437.60±2.26) (P<0.0001)."
