### Rebecca Schneider 2025

Rebecca Schneider, Sebastian Weirich, Poldi Kuhl, Stefan Schipolowski, Petra Stanat. (2025). COVID-19-related differences in students' verbal achievements: Comparisons of different cohorts of students with SEN based on large-scale assessment data from Germany. Learning and Instruction 100 (2025) 102228. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102228

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression of 2021 Grade 4 verbal achievement on pandemic learning conditions with background covariates (N = 24.854 as printed). The article reports that "better technical equipment and space for undisturbed learning at home was associated with higher achievement scores."

> "Better technical equipment and space for undisturbed learning at home was associated with higher achievement scores in reading, listening, and orthography."

### Rebecca Schneider 2025 (2)

COVID-19-related differences in students' verbal achievements: Comparisons of different cohorts of students with SEN based on large-scale assessment data from Germany. Learning and Instruction 100 (2025) 102228. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102228

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interaction terms in the Grade 4 multilevel regressions. The article reports "significant negative interaction effects of student group (non-SEN vs. SEN-L-S) and learning conditions" only for teacher-rated distance learning in reading and orthography.

> "Significant negative interaction effects of student group (non-SEN vs. SEN-L-S) and learning conditions (Table 5) only emerged for the teacher-rated functioning of distance learning in reading and orthography."
