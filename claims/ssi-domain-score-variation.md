Descriptive statistics from the validation sample (n=372 for SSI): total mean 43.9 of a maximum 69 (64%). The lowest scores on concentration and reading, domains relevant to independent learning, were interpreted against Frambach et al.'s findings on Arab students needing more guidance.

> "For the five factors (domains), students had the highest mean score on the time management domain (95% of the maximum score) and the lowest score on the concentration (32%) and reading domains (57%)"
