## Evidence

### O'Malley A 2025

O'Malley A, Fitzgerald N, Moylett E, Gaffney G, McCarthy G, Murphy A.W., Geoghegan R, Hallahan B. (2025). A comparison of objective structured clinical examinations (OSCEs) for medical students, modified during the COVID-19 pandemic. Irish Journal of Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03976-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of anonymised results from the three ACS OSCE sittings (2019, 2020, 2021); Table 1 prints Cronbach's alpha of 0.78, 0.80 and 0.75 for 2019, 2020 and 2021 respectively. No effect size is printed.

> "The reliability of the OSCEs as measured utilising Cronbach's alpha was similar across the three examinations."
