## Evidence

### van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014

van Vonderen Jeroen J., Witlox Ruben S., Kraaij Sascha, te Pas Arjan B. (2014). Two-Minute Training for Improving Neonatal Bag and Mask Ventilation. PLoS ONE 9(10): e109049. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109049

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Manikin experiment with 25 inexperienced caregivers (junior registrars, neonatal and obstetric nurses) ventilating a leak-free manikin for 30 s before and after the two-minute training, measured with a respiratory function monitor. The article reports "51 (7-91)% vs. 11 (2-71)%; p<0.01"; no effect size is printed.

> "In the inexperienced group mask leak was large before training and significantly decreased after training (51 (7-91)% vs. 11 (2-71)%; p<0.01)"
