Chen H 2026¶

Chen H (2026) When AI threatens my future career: growth mindset and perceived employability in Chinese college students' career decision making anxiety. Front. Psychol. 17:1933842. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1933842

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Moderation analysis with mean-centered variables in the 526-student survey found the interaction "signiﬁcantly and positively predicted perceived employability" (b = 0.122, p = 0.014), indicating growth mindset buffered the negative effect, supporting H5.

"The interaction between AI employment threat perception and growth mindset signiﬁcantly and positively predicted perceived employability, b= 0.122, SE = 0.049, t = 2.473, p = 0.014."
