### Sherman 2011

Sherman, J. (2011). Signing for Success: Using American Sign Language to Learn Sight Vocabulary. SRATE Journal, 20(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ959527

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of interns' anecdotal observations and field notes across the five sessions. Interns reported children signing at home and teaching signs to others; one first grade teacher described a previously reluctant student's increased self-confidence as "probably a bigger plus than learning the words".

> "In general, the level of enthusiasm for learning the ASL vocabulary words was extremely high."
