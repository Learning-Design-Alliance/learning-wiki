### Gordia AP 2015

Gordia AP, de Quadros TMB, Silva LR, dos Santos GM. (2015). Knowledge of pediatricians regarding physical activity in childhood and adolescence. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rpped.2015.02.001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire results (Table 4) from the 210-pediatrician congress sample: 61.6% (109/177) selected 30 min, 21.5% (38/177) selected the correct 60 min, and 14.1% selected 45 min for the minimum daily duration question based on ACSM recommendations.

> "Most of the assessed pediatricians incorrectly reported that 30 min is the minimum daily time for physical activity for children and adolescents."
