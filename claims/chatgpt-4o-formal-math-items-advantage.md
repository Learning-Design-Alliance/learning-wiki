## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini 2025

Giulia Polverini, Jakob Melin, Elias Önerud, and Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT on tasks involving physics visual representations: the case of the Brief Electricity and Magnetism Assessment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative item-level comparison between ChatGPT-4o (coded by meaning) and the 12214-student sample. The article reports students' performance on these six items was poor at 28% on average while ChatGPT-4o was relatively successful at 93% on average.

> "Among the items where ChatGPT-4o outperforms students, those that exhibit the largest gap (> 50 percentage points) are items 12, 14, 16, 17, 27, and 31. All these items require formal and mathematical reasoning, which are known to be challenging for students."
