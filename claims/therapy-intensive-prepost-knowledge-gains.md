### Glasgow KW 2025

Glasgow KW, Brady AB. (2025). UAB/SNMMI Nuclear Medicine Therapy Intensive: Advancing Competence and Innovation in Radiopharmaceutical Therapy. Journal of Nuclear Medicine Technology. https://doi.org/10.2967/jnmt.125.271222

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program evaluation of the 2025 cohort's preassessment and postassessment (designated Not Human Subjects Research by the UAB IRB). Baseline gaps in areas such as "waste contamination control" and "therapy-specific imaging interpretation" were followed by "near-universal mastery" after the intensive. Only accuracy percentages are reported; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "By the conclusion of the program, postassessment results showed near-universal mastery, with more than 90%, and in some cases 100%, accuracy, in segmentation, prostate-specific membrane antigen biology, therapy workflows, and interprofessional team roles."
