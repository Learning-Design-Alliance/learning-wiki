Focus-group finding: not even staff in a single school agreed on what was best; the misinformation resulted in a handful of students ending up with acceptances to schools not of high desirability, prompting second-round participation.

> "Several students shared confusion and frustration about the correct approach to take when ranking the schools in GoCPS. This confusion reflected disparate messaging from adults about how to engage with the ranking process"
