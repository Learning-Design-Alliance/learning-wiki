`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Review's account of Lo et al.'s (2018) FC approach based on the First Principles of Instruction, applied in high school courses of four subject areas. The review reports "greater student achievements in the flipped courses in three of the four subject areas"; no effect size is printed and the result did not hold in one subject area.

> "They reported greater student achievements in the flipped courses in three of the four subject areas, and attributed the results to self-paced learning of basic materials before class and group-based problem-solving activities during class."
