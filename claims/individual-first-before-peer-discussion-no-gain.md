## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Impact of peer interaction on conceptual test performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two experimental protocols: 148 students in the IG protocol (individual then group) and 84 students in the GI protocol (group then individual), each working 50 minutes per phase. The article reports the IG group average of 74% and GI group average of 71% as "statistically indistinguishable".

> "The average performance on the IG and GI protocols are statistically indistinguishable, which suggests t hat giving students an opportunity to think alone before peer discussion did not improve th eir group performance signiﬁcantly."
