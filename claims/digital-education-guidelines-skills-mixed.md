# Skills outcomes for digital education on guidelines are mixed: simulation modules outperform printed guidelines and no intervention, but video demonstration underperforms peer teaching

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` A simulation-based module showed large beneficial effects on skills versus no intervention (SMD 0.93) and versus printed guidelines (SMD 1.13). [→ Tudor Car Lorainne 2019](#tudor-car-lorainne-2019)
`q3 i2` Computer-based video demonstration scored lower than peer teaching on post-intervention skills (SMD −3.72). [→ Tudor Car Lorainne 2019 (2)](#tudor-car-lorainne-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Tudor Car Lorainne 2019

Tudor Car Lorainne, Soong Aijia, Kyaw Bhone Myint, Chua Kee Leng, Low-Beer Naomi, Majeed Azeem. (2019). Health professions digital education on clinical practice guidelines: a systematic review by Digital Health Education collaboration. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-019-1370-1

`q3 · i2`

RCT of 45 anaesthesiology residents using an online simulation module versus printed guidelines for resuscitation, measured on a Megacode performance checklist. The review reports a large beneficial effect at moderate quality.

> "One study (n = 45) evaluating the use of simulation-based learning module reported large beneficial effect of digital education (SMD = 1.13, 95% CI 0.50, 1.76, moderate quality of evidence) in comparison to printed guidelines [39]."

### Tudor Car Lorainne 2019 (2)

Tudor Car Lorainne, Soong Aijia, Kyaw Bhone Myint, Chua Kee Leng, Low-Beer Naomi, Majeed Azeem. (2019). Health professions digital education on clinical practice guidelines: a systematic review by Digital Health Education collaboration. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-019-1370-1

`q3 · i2`

RCT of 88 medical students comparing video demonstration with peer teaching for paediatric basic life support, measured by OSCE. The review reports higher skills in the peer-teaching control group at low quality.

> "The other study assessed the effectiveness of computer-based video demonstration compared to peer teaching and reported higher post-intervention skills score in the control group (SMD = − 3.72, 95% CI − 4.42, 3.02, low quality of evidence) [41]."
