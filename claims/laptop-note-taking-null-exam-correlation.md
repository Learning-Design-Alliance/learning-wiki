## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Day Alison J. 2021

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Observational correlation of tracked note-taking duration with exam average; the printed correlation r = .002 (p = 0.99) is negligible, and the sensitivity analysis including ambiguous "Document1" files remains non-significant: "still not reliable, r(103) = -0.129, p = 0.193".

> "If we add these ambiguous documents to the note-taking category, the correlation with exam score changes from r = .002 to r = -.129, but is still not reliable, r(103) = -0.129, p = 0.193."
