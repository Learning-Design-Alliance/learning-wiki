### Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza 2019

Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza, Ayroza-Ribeiro Helizabet Abdala, Lima Raquel Ferreira, Yonamine Aline Estefane Eras, Ohara Fabio, Ayroza-Ribeiro Paulo Augusto Galvão. (2019). RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0039-1700587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive volume comparison across the three time-frame groups of the chart review; the discussion adds that "the mean annual number of procedures more than tripled after the implementation of the training program."

> "The increase in TLHs performed per year at our hospital between periods I-1 and I-2 was of 129%, and between I-2 and I-3, it was of 100%."

### Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza 2019 (2)

Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza, Ayroza-Ribeiro Helizabet Abdala, Lima Raquel Ferreira, Yonamine Aline Estefane Eras, Ohara Fabio, Ayroza-Ribeiro Paulo Augusto Galvão. (2019). The Impact of Systematic Laparoscopic Skills and Suture Training on Laparoscopic Hysterectomy Outcomes in a Brazilian Teaching Hospital. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0039-1700587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the chart-review data: routine bilateral salpingectomy (adopted to decrease ovarian cancer risk) added complexity without raising time, stay, or complications.

> "The complexity of the surgeries also increased, with bilateral salpingectomy becoming routine in 2013, but it did not increase the surgical time, hospital stay, or the complication rates."
