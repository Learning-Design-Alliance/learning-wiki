# 16PF-derived predicted-effectiveness indices correlate with supervisor and client ratings (.41, .87), but the review judges use of the 16PF for trainee selection premature

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Wittmer and Lister found a significant correlation (r = .41) between supervisor ratings on the CERS and a 16PF specification-equation index of predicted counseling effectiveness. [→ Rowe 1975](#rowe-1975)
`q2 i3` Shelton reported a high correlation (r = .87) between the client-rating criterion and the 16PF specification equation. [→ Rowe 1975 (2)](#rowe-1975-2)

## Evidence

### Rowe 1975

Rowe, Wayne; Murphy, Harry B.; De Csipkes, Robert A. (1975). The Relationship of Counselor Characteristics and Counseling Effectiveness. Review of Educational Research, 45(2). https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543045002231

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review reports the Wittmer and Lister (1971) study of 53 subjects, in which GRE scores were unrelated to the CERS but the 16PF specification-equation index showed a printed correlation of r = .41 with supervisor ratings. The review attributes the finding to that study.

> "While the scores on the GRE were unrelated to the CERS, a significant correlation (.41) was found between supervisor ratings on this scale and an index of predicted counseling effectiveness derived from the 16PF specification equation (McClain, 1968)."

### Rowe 1975 (2)

Rowe, Wayne; Murphy, Harry B.; De Csipkes, Robert A. (1975). The Relationship of Counselor Characteristics and Counseling Effectiveness. Review of Educational Research, 45(2). https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543045002231

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review reports Shelton's (1973) study using a composite client-rating on the Counseling Evaluation Inventory as criterion, which yielded a printed correlation of r = .87 with the 16PF specification equation. The review attributes this finding to Shelton.

> "In addition, Shelton reported a high correlation (.87) between the criterion and the 16PF specification equation."
