Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik 2026¶

Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik. (2026). Comparing perceptions of the school librarian using the future ready school—librarian framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10670-7

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Survey analysis (N = 186) showed teachers (M = 3.15, SD = 1.24) rated librarians' fulfillment of Personalized Professional Learning higher than librarians rated themselves (M = 2.63, SD = 1.10); "a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.031)". No effect size was printed.

"Post hoc testing using pair-wise analysis and Tukey HSD identified a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.031) on the construct of Personalized Professional Leadership, but did not show a statistically significant difference between the administrator and teacher roles (p =.919)"
