## Evidence

### Rauf A 2026

Rauf A, Backhaus J, Hammer S, König S. (2026). Comparing the Weighted Gain Score and a Rasch-Based Approach for Estimating Learning Outcomes in Medical Education: Quantitative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/75516

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis between WGS and RLG scores across three µ calibrations in an empirical dataset (n=170) and a simulated binomial dataset (n=1000), showing strong positive correlations identical across µ-values.

> "In the empirical dataset, the Pearson correlation coefficient was consistently high (r=0.93; P<.001). A similarly strong relationship was observed in the simulated dataset (r=0.92; P<.001)."
