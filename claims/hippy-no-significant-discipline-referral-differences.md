## Evidence

### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

T-tests compared discipline referrals (in-school suspensions, out-of-school suspensions, alternate education placements) for 516 HIPPY and 516 matched comparison children. No statistically significant differences were found for any of the three referral types at any grade level, though both groups showed high suspension percentages in 7th and 9th grades.

> "Independent samples t-tests revealed no statistically significant difference between the HIPPY and comparison groups for in-school suspensions."
