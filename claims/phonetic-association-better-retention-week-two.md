## Evidence

### Bozavlı 2017

Ebubekir Bozavlı. (2017). Effect of Phonetic Association on Learning Vocabulary in Foreign Language. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i1.1968

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second-week retention test given to the same 66 participants after a short interview, with no re-teaching. The article reports that the control group's success "ranged from 12% to 52%" while the experimental group's ranged between 52% and 96%, which the authors read as evidence of permanency.

> "In the study conducted in the second week to determine the permanency of learning success, the ra te of success of the control group ranged from 12% to 52% while that of the experimental group had a range between 52% and 96%."
