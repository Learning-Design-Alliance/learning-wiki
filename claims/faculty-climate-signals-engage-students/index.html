At the course level, faculty agreement that there is no such thing as a question too elementary positively predicted engagement, while agreement that success is primarily up to students and that there is not enough time for individualized attention negatively predicted it (all p < 0.05); tenured faculty's students reported more engagement than those of non-tenure-track faculty. → Gasiewski 2012
