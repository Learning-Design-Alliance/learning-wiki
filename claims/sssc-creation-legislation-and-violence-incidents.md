Descriptive survey finding on reasons for starting SSSCs (Exhibit 3), based on a write-in response item. The article reports "over 65 percent of respondents shared that their SSSC was started because of state legislation" and 28 percent citing highly publicized school violence incidents; other reasons included grant/university funding (10%) and leadership and stakeholder desire (10%).
