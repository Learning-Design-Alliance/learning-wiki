# Both one-model and two-model productive failure groups significantly improved weather and climate declarative knowledge, with no difference between conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Both treatment groups improved climate declarative knowledge from pretest to posttest (Time effect), with no Condition effect or Condition × Time interaction. [→ Jacobson 2020](#jacobson-2020)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2020

Jacobson, Michael, Goldwater, Micah, Markauskaite, Lina, Lai, Polly, Kapur, Manu, Roberts, Gareth, & Hilton, Courtney. (2020). Schema abstraction with productive failure and analogical comparison: Learning designs for far across domain transfer. Learning and Instruction, 65, Article number: 101222. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101222

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-model analysis of the five declarative climate items (subject nested within classroom as random effect) in the quasi-experimental study. Both groups improved from pretest to posttest; the article reports "no effect of Condition" and no Condition × Time interaction, so the two-model design conferred no declarative knowledge advantage.

> "there was an effect of Time, F (1,92) = 10.873, p = 0.001, η2 = .12, indicating that both groups demonstrated learning of weather and climate knowledge, as evidenced by higher performance on the posttest (M = 0.935, SD = .26) compared to the pretest (M = 0.835, SD = .24). There was no effect of Condition, F(1,92) = 1.001 , p = 0.319, η2 = .01"
