### Heni Nurhaeni 2016

Heni Nurhaeni, Dinarti, Dwi Priharti. (2016). The Family Parenting Influenced Adolescent Brawls Behavior. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). [doi:10.11591/ijere.v5i2.4531](https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v5i2.4531)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative analysis in the T test results section of a quasi-experimental study of 108 randomly sampled high school students in four schools. The article prints the correlation effect size r = -0.616, reporting "a relationship between these two variables at -0616", a negative correlation it describes as quite high (between 0.50 and 1.0).

> "Based on the basis of decision making correlation test it can be concluded that there is a relationship between these two variables at -0616, meaning sign (-) means that the test results are negative correlation."
