# Missing data in the analysis samples occurred largely at random

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Analyses of missingness, including twelve Bonferroni-corrected significance tests and checks against pretest scores, supported the conclusion that missing data occurred largely at random. [→ Stebner 2022](#stebner-2022)

## Evidence

### Stebner 2022

Stebner, F., Schuster, C., Weber, X.-L., Greiff, S., Leutner, D., & Wirth, J. (2022). Transfer of metacognitive skills in self-regulated learning: effects on strategy application and content knowledge acquisition. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 715-744. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09322-x

`q3 · i?`

In the participants section of the training study, the authors report twelve significance tests at a Bonferroni-corrected alpha of 0.004, of which only three reached significance, and t tests showing missingness unrelated to pretest scores (all p values ≥ 0.367). They conclude missing data occurred largely at random.

> "Only three significance tests (tests on measurement scales) reached significance. In a last step we checked that missingness in the analysis sample for RQ 1 was unrelated to pretest scores"
