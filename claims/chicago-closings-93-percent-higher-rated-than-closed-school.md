### de la Torre 2015

de la Torre, M., Gordon, M. F., Moore, P., & Cowhy, J. (2015). School Closings in Chicago: Understanding Families' Choices and Constraints for New School Enrollment. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://www.cps.edu/qualityschools/Pages/Parents.aspx

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Administrative-data analysis of all K-7 students from the 47 closed Chicago elementary schools (10,062 reenrolled in CPS in fall 2013), comparing performance policy points of attended, closed, and designated schools. The report finds "93 percent" attended higher-point schools and 7 percent lower-point schools.

> "Nearly all displaced students (93 percent) attended schools with higher performance policy points than the closed schools, with 7 percent of students attending a school with lower points than their school that closed."
