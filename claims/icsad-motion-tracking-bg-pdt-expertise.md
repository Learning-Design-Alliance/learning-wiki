## Evidence

### Kattan Eduardo MD 2019

Kattan Eduardo MD, MMEd; Vera Magdalena MD; Putz Francisca RN; Corvetto Marcia MD; De la Fuente Rene MD, MScEng; Bravo Sebastian MD. (2019). Design and Evaluation of a Low-Cost Bronchoscopy-Guided Percutaneous Dilatational Tracheostomy Simulator. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000399

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ICSAD-derived total path length and number of movements collected during simulator sessions; the authors note no benchmark proficiency values exist for BG-PDT and that experts had higher NM and TPL than in published CVC training, which may indicate BG-PDT is a more complex procedure.

> "Because this is the first use of motion tracking devices in BG-PDT training, there are no benchmark values of operatory proficiency. When compared with experiences published in CVC training, in both procedures the experts present higher movement economy and perform the procedure in less time than novices."
