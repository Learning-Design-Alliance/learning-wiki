Browning 2020¶

Browning, A., & Romer, N. (2020). To create safe and healthy schools during a pandemic, prioritize educator wellbeing. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED610071

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The brief asserts, citing prior work (endnote 2), that educator wellbeing contributes to "warm, positive student-teacher relationships" and trusted adults modeling self-regulation. No data are presented in the brief itself; this is an attributed assertion, not a tested result.
