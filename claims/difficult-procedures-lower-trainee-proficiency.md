## Evidence

### Skjold-Ødegaard B 2024

Skjold-Ødegaard B, Ersdal HL, Assmus J, Søreide K. (2024). Internal and external factors affecting the performance score of surgical trainees doing laparoscopic appendectomy: a prospective, observational cohort study in a structured training programme. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11007-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic mixed-effects model of 142 evaluation forms from the structured LapApp training programme. The final model printed OR 0.14 (95% CI 0.02, 0.89), p = 0.037 for high difficulty, i.e. "procedure difficulty was inversely associated with the achievement of proficiency".

> "In the multivariable analysis (Table 2), procedure difficulty was inversely associated with the achievement of proficiency."
