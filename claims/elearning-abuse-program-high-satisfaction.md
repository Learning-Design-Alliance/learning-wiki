Post-program satisfaction questionnaire (N = 71, five-point scale) covering design, system functions, overall satisfaction, applicability to clinical practice, and recommendation. The article reports "85.9% were satisfied overall" and the same percentage would recommend it; these are descriptive percentages with no effect size.
