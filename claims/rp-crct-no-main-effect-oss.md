Huang, F., Gregory, A., & Ward-Seidel, A. R. (2023). The Impact of Restorative Practices on the Use of Out-of-School Suspensions: Results from a Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.1007/s11121-023-01507-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-023-01507-3)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered multilevel linear probability model from the CRCT of 18 K-12 schools (analytic sample 5,878 students; 2,919 control, 2,959 intervention), with random slopes for prior OSS and residual variances by school type. The main effect was "no statistically significant differences in the likelihood of suspension" (B = -0.001, p = .95); no effect size was printed.

> "Main effects of the MLM (see Table 3) indicated no statistically significant differences in the likelihood of suspension between students in the intervention and control schools (B = -0.001, p = .95)."
