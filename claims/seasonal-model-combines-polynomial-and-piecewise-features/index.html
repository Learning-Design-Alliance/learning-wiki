Evidence¶

James Soland 2022¶

James Soland, Yeow Meng Thum. (2022). Estimating and comparing growth using longitudinal interim achievement data with seasonal trends. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness 15(3) 635-654. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2021.2018744

q2 · i? · design · r?

Definitional statement of the model's design from the abstract: it combines polynomial features estimating "year-to-year growth" with piecewise features that "directly estimate within-year gains/losses." This is a design description, not a measured comparison.

"The model is designed to combine features from traditional polynomial models that estimate year-to-year growth but ignore within-year gains and losses with those from piecewise models, which directly estimate within-year gains/losses but do not include terms for year-to-year growth."
