Experiment 1 exploratory bivariate correlations showed "moderate-to-large correlations" between gJOLs and test performance in both conditions (r = .59 and r = .60), indicating appropriate relative metacognitive monitoring despite the calibration differences between conditions.

> "Individual learners demonstrated moderate-to-large correlations between their gJOL and final test performance when learning individually, r (61) = .59, p < .001, as did Paired learners, r (86) = .60, p < .001."
