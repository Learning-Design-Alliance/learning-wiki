### Martin 2012

Effects of the Connected Mathematics Project 2 (CMP2) on the Mathematics Achievement of Grade 6 Students in the Mid-Atlantic Region (NCEE 2012-4017). `q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Secondary outcome in the same cluster RCT, measured with the PTV subscale of a validated mathematics engagement survey for 5,584 students. The article reports "effect size = 0.09" with no statistically significant difference between intervention and control groups.

> "Results for the secondary research question indicate that CMP2 was also no more effective than business as usual in improving students’ mathematics PTV (effect size = 0.09; p= .109)."
