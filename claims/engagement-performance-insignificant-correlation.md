## Evidence

### Angela Y. He 2017

Angela Y. He. (2017). Overcoming Barriers to Self-Directed Student Engagement with Educational Games: A Mixed-Methods Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r?`

Correlational analysis in the quantitative results of the case study, with ten students measured on a 25-item Likert engagement assessment adapted from Jennett et al.'s (2008) immersion questionnaire and pretest-posttest performance scores. The article reports "a positive yet insignificant correlation between engagement and performance" (r=0.27).

> "Pearson product-moment correlations revealed a positive yet insignificant correlation between engagement and performance (r=0.27, n=10, p=0.45). Engagement in Group B had a stronger correlation with performance (r=0.29, n=5, p=0.64) than that of Group A (r=0.1, n=5, p=0.87)."
