Open-ended bonus-question survey administered to 207 algebra-based students before instruction on the thermodynamics laws, previously reported in [37] and repeated here as motivation; 134 students (65%) responded to at least some questions.

> "Of the students who responded to the first law question, 52% stated that energy is conserved, e.g., “Energy cannot be created or destroyed,” but only 5% made a statement that included heat transfer as part of this energy conservation law [37]."
