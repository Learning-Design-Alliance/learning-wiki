Evidence¶

Matthew L. Bernacki 2023¶

Matthew L. Bernacki, Megan Claire Cogliano, Shelbi L. Kuhlmann, Jenifer Utz, Christy Strong, Jonathan C. Hilpert, Jeffrey A. Greene. (2023). Relations between undergraduates’ self-regulated learning skill mastery during digital training and biology performance. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09356-9

q2 · i1 · associational · r1

The article attributes this to its prior randomized study (Bernacki et al., 2020), reporting the training "conferred an effect of about 1/3 of a letter grade on subsequent quizzes and exams" with printed effect size d ~ 0.3, roughly a B to a B+ shift. Reported here as background for the present re-analyses.

"conferred an effect of about 1/3 of a letter grade on subsequent quizzes and exams (d ~ 0.3; e.g., from a B to a B + and corresponding grade point average difference of 3.4 to 3.7 on the 0 to 4 scale at U.S. universities)"
