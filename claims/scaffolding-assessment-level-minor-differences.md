## Evidence

### Belland Brian R. 2017

Belland Brian R., Walker Andrew E., Kim Nam Ju. (2017). A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis to Synthesize the Influence of Contexts of Scaffolding Use on Cognitive Outcomes in STEM Education. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317723009

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Assessment level moderator analysis (Figure 7 and Table 5): credible intervals were relatively tight given the large number of trials, except for application versus control; the article states the magnitude of difference among assessment levels is minor and there is little evidence scaffolding is more effective at any particular level.

> "Scaffolding led to strong pre–post gains across assessment levels, with the lowest effect size estimate at the application level (ḡ = 0.74), and the highest at the concept level (ḡ = 0.87; see Figure 7)."
