## Evidence

### Hsu Ming-Sung 2025

Hsu Ming-Sung, Yeh Chun-Liang, Cheng Shih-Jung, Lin Chun-Pin. (2025). Transforming Dental Morphology Education: The Impact of Three-Dimensional Digital Simulation on Practical Skill Development. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103904

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory 15-item five-point Likert satisfaction survey administered in Week 4 after both groups had used the digital resources; internal reliability Cronbach's α = 0.86. Only descriptive means are printed; no inferential test.

> "They found the course engaging (mean = 4.0 ± 0.8), beneficial for understanding complex dental morphology (mean = 3.9 ± 0.8), and effective in complementing traditional instructor demonstrations (mean = 4.0 ± 0.8). However, students remained neutral regarding the digital course's capacity to entirely replace hands-on instruction (mean = 3.2 ± 0.9)"
