## Evidence

### Abbasi Ebrahim 2026

Abbasi Ebrahim, Abbasifar Mahboubeh, Bazrafkan Lila. (2026). Relationship between midwifery educators’ quality of life and students’ clinical training quality. BMC Medical Education, 26, 1278. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09637-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation test with the age variable of respondents, using Spearman's coefficient on data from 183 students. The coefficient was inverse and significant ("r = 0.259, p = 0.001"), so younger students reported higher perceived clinical education quality than older peers.

> "according to the correlation coefficient (r = 0.259, p = 0.001), there is an inverse and significant correlation between the variable of clinical education quality of midwifery students and their age level"
