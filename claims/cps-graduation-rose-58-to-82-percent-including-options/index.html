Nagaoka 2020¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2020). The Educational Attainment of Chicago Public Schools Students: 2019. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/2019/#/milestones

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Figure 7 takeaway from this descriptive report on four-year graduation rates of CPS ninth-grade cohorts, 2007–2019. With roughly the same number of students (approximately 26,000), the class of 2019 had over 5,000 more graduates than the class of 2007 (21,608 vs. 16,598).

"The percentage of CPS students completing high school in four years has steadily increased; for the graduating class of 2019, the rate reached 82 percent compared to 58 percent for students who graduated from high school in 2007, an increase of almost 24 percentage points."
