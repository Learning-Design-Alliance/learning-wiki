Leung-Gagné 2025¶

Leung-Gagné, M., Maria Castillo, M., Patrick, S. K., Carver-Thomas, D., Kini, T., Bingener, C., & Giani, M. V. (2025). Strengthening the educator pipeline through service scholarships: California's Golden State Teacher Grant Program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/518.478

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of recipient data after the July 2024 reduction finds "only 47% of their unmet needs were covered by the grant" for $10,000-cap recipients, who also relied more on loans and employment than those under the $20,000 cap.

"for GSTG recipients who were subject to the $10,000 award cap, only 47% of their unmet needs were covered by the grant; they were also more likely than those subject to the $20,000 cap to rely on loans and take on a job."

Leung-Gagné 2025 (2)¶

Leung-Gagné, M., Maria Castillo, M., Patrick, S. K., Carver-Thomas, D., Kini, T., Bingener, C., & Giani, M. V. (2025). Strengthening the educator pipeline through service scholarships: California's Golden State Teacher Grant Program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/518.478

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The report states the original $20,000 maximum award "covered, on average, 80% of recipients' tuition fees and other living expenses that were not covered by other aid", and quotes an EPP leader describing borrowing dropping to 10–15% of students.

"The original maximum award grant of $20,000 covered, on average, 80% of recipients’ tuition fees and other living expenses that were not covered by other aid."
