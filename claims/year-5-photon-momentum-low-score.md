`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One Year 5 class (30 students) taught the "Fantastic photons" module; the momentum question followed class activities on photon momentum. The article attributes the low score to "insufficient emphasise on language" and revised lesson plans accordingly.

> "We were surprised that only 23% of students were able to explain that light is made up o f photons and that they have momentum, allowing light to push things, because previous studies with interventions led by members of the project team much higher results were obtained"
