### Kalra Anika 2022

Kalra Anika, Siju Manju, Jenny Alisa, Spindler Hilary, Madriz Solange, Baayd Jami, Handu Seema, Ghosh Rakesh, Cohen Susanna, Walker Dilys. (2022). Super Divya to the rescue! `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Accessibility theme from the qualitative interviews with 17 NMS. One participant reported that after difficulty playing the video, "later on I changed the phone setting and it worked," and two NMS suggested creating an app to access the videos.

> "When discussing accessibility to Super Divya, the majority of NMS responded positively to viewing modules on their phones. Access to strong internet connectivity was cited as an important enabler."
