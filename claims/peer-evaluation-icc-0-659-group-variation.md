### Yoon Hyun Bae 2018

Yoon Hyun Bae, Park Wan Beom, Myung Sun-Jung, Moon Sang Hui, Park Jun-Bean. (2018). Validity and reliability assessment of a peer evaluation method in team-based learning classes. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.78

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ICC analysis of peer ratings within 18 fixed groups across 11 TBL classes among 141 students. The article reports group ICCs ranged from 0.390 to 0.863 with overall average 0.659 and significant between-group differences (p<0.001).

> "The average ICCs of each group ranged from 0.390 to 0.863, and the overall average was 0.659. There were significant differences among the average ICCs across groups (p<0.001)."
