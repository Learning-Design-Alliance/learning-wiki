## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall perception performance (mean scores) for Events six through seven, which occurred while watching students study independently, was superior to that for Events one through five, which occurred during a lecture with livelier, more diverse student behaviours. [→ Liu 2025](#liu-2025)

## Evidence

### Liu 2025

Liu, T.-C., & Lin, Y.-C. (2025). Development and implementation of an IVR-based assessment system for student teachers' professional vision. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10463-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Comparison across instructional situations in the implementation case study: during the lecture activity (Events 1–5) VR students were assigned livelier, more diverse random behaviours, whereas during independent study (Events 6–7) behaviours were more consistent and static, making misbehaving students 'more easily noticed.' The article reports the mean-score superiority of Events 6–7 over Events 1–5; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "The overall perception performance (mean scores) for Events six through seven was superior to that for Events one through five."
