## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students who employed GAI tools for DST creation achieved higher overall CPS scores than those using traditional methods, with a large effect size (η² = 0.16). [→ Wei 2025](#wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Wei 2025

Wei, X., Wang, L., Lee, L.-K., & Liu, R. (2025). The effects of generative AI on collaborative problem-solving and team creativity performance in digital story creation: an experimental study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 22:23. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00526-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA with pretest CPS as covariate compared 30 experimental (GAI tools) vs 30 control (traditional tools) students in a 20-week randomized design. The article reports "a significant difference between the two groups in students' overall CPS scores (F = 10.69, p < 0.01) with a large effect size (η2 = 0.16)."
