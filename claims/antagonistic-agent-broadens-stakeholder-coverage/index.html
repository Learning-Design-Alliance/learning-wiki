Howard Ziyu Han 2026¶

Howard Ziyu Han, Nikolas Martelaro. (2026). Enacting Constructive Conflicts with AI Agents to Enhance Reconsideration among Novice Interaction Designers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04166 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.04166

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative comparison of stakeholders considered during the intervention across the three conditions: the Self Reflection group surfaced few stakeholders, and the agent consistently introduced accessibility, privacy, reluctance toward automation, and preferences for alternative reporting channels.

"This suggests that the agent can function as an interactive checklist for common stakeholder concerns beyond what a single human designer could think of."
