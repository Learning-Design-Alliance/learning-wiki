Single-response scenario items in the survey of 88 students. The results state that "Direct pulp capping was the most frequently selected option for 0–1 mm exposures (84.1%)" with preferences shifting toward pulpotomy and root canal treatment as exposure size increased.

> "Direct pulp capping was the most frequently selected option for 0–1 mm exposures (84.1%), partial pulpotomy for 1–2 mm exposures (67.0%), and total pulpotomy (39.8%) or root canal treatment (35.2%) for exposures larger than 2 mm."
