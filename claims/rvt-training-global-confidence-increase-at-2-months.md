## Evidence

### Rashid Basima 2026

Rashid Basima, Janke Natasha, Gohar Basem, Blokland Kirsten, Taylor Becky, Coe Jason B. (2026). A professional skills and knowledge training programme enhances the communication confidence of registered veterinary technologists/technicians. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1002/vetr.70493

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of the global communication self-confidence item in the same 29-RVT single-group survey study (p < 0.001; F[2, 56] = 10.29), with means rising from 71.33 pre-programme to 83.57 at the 2-month follow-up; post hoc pairwise t-tests with Bonferroni adjustment were significant.

> "Post hoc analyses revealed a statistically significant increase in confidence between the pre‐programme and 2‐month follow‐up responses (p = 0.001), and between the post‐programme and 2‐month follow‐up responses (p = 0.004)."
