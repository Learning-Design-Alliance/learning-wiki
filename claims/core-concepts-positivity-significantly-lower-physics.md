### Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert 2026

Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert. (2026). Adapting Public Exams for Introductory Physics: A Mixed-Methods Investigation of Student Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

T-test on coded open-ended responses from the NMSU physics course, comparing positivity rates for comments about Public Exams directing to core concepts (mean (84.4±30.1)%) against the control group (mean (98.0±5.5)%). The authors contrast this with Wiggins et al.'s ratios of 11.0 for Public Exams versus 1.95 for control.

> "The change in positivity rate for Public Exams directing to core concepts compared to control was highly statistically significant:p= 0.009."
