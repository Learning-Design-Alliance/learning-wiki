## Evidence

### Meijuan Xie 2025

Meijuan Xie, Liling Luo. (2025). The Status Quo and Future of AI-TPACK for Mathematics Teacher Education Students: A Case Study in Chinese Universities. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13533 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2503.13533](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2503.13533)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Descriptive statistical analysis of the post-test survey of 412 MTES from seven universities. Means of all six variables were medium level, and the study concludes "the current status of AI-TPACK for MTES in China is at a basic, preliminary stage", with students predominantly using elementary AI tools such as question-and-answer systems (N=327).

> "The mean values are range from 3.7 to 5.3, which are all at the medium level. However, a comparison of the six variables reveals that teaching beliefs and self-efficacy exhibit higher levels than the AI-TPACK components (AI-TK, AI-TCK, AI-TPK, and AI-TPACK). The mean value of teaching beliefs (M=5.18) is the highest, while that for AI-TK (M=4.18) is the lowest."
