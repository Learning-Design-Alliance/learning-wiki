## Evidence

### Steve Bennoun 2026

Steve Bennoun, Veronica X. Yan, & Alice Xu. (2026). Mixing problems increases performance of all students but especially of low-achieving ones in college calculus. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00450-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of final exam z-scores (Model 2) including the Homework Type × Midterm Score interaction. The article reports β = −0.26, 95% CI = [−0.37, −0.15], t(552) = −4.53, p < 0.001, covering "83% of the students".

> "interleaving was particularly beneﬁcial for students with a midterm score either below average or up to 0.96 standard deviation above average (see Fig.3), representing 83% of the students, as evidenced by a signiﬁcant Homework Type × Midterm Score interaction term ( β = −0.26, 95% CI = [ −0.37, −0.15], t(552) =−4.53, p < 0.001)."
