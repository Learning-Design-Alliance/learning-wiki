## Evidence

### Kartal 2018

Kartal, O. Y., Yazgan, A. D., & Avci, E. (2018). An Investigation into the Relationship between Adults' Levels of Education-Related Epistemic Freedom and Epistemic Violence. International Education Studies, 11(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n10p96

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive analysis of survey responses from 129 adults who had finished formal education, collected face-to-face with the epistemic violence-freedom scale. The distribution of acceptance of education-related epistemic violence was "moderate", "high", and "very high" at 48.1%, 40.3%, and 1.6%, with no participant at the very low level.

> "The results also yielded a "moderate", "high", and "very high" level of acceptance (48.1%, 40.3%, and 1.6%, respectively)."
