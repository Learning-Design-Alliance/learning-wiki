Guo 2026¶

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article reports, citing Cristia et al. (2017), a randomized evaluation of the One Laptop per Child program in Peru. The review attributes the null: computers per student rose "from 0.12 to 1.18" with "no measurable effect on mathematics or language test scores". No effect size is printed.

"across 318 rural Peruvian schools, the intervention raised computers per student from 0.12 to 1.18, and after fifteen months produced no measurable effect on mathematics or language test scores"
