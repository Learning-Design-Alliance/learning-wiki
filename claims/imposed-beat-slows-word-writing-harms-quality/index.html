Evidence¶

West 1918¶

West, P. V. (1918). The Relation of Rhythm to the Handwriting Movement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22The+Relation+of+Rhythm+to+the+Handwriting+Movement%22

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Small exploratory trial with a few subjects following a rhythmic beat while writing words. The article states the induced regularity contrasted markedly with normal behavior and resulted in uncertainties of speed progress that affected form quality harmfully. No effect size is printed.

"Experimentation with a few subjects in the following of a rhythmic beat while writing words showed that a writer with habituated speed and rhythm would be slowed down by any beat that was slow enough to be consciously followed."
