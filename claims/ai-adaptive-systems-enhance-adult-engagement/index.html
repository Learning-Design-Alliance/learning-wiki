Theodora 2026¶

Theodora, A., & Tselios, N. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Lifelong Learning: Opportunities and Challenges in Adult Education Policy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16296 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.16296

The review reports, in its section on adult learning theory and AI integration, that empirical research indicates engagement, motivation, and outcome benefits from adaptive systems, intelligent tutoring, and analytics. The claim is attributed to the cited literature; no effect sizes are printed.

"Empirical research indicates that such systems can enhance engagement, motivation, and learning outcomes, especially when aligned with learners’ goals and prior knowledge ."
