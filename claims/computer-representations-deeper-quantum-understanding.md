## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Converting quantum mechanics concepts into constructible, evaluable, analyzable computer representations can support understanding at a deeper level than more abstract representations. [→ Schmied 2019](#schmied-2019)

## Evidence

### Schmied 2019

Schmied, R. (2019). Using Mathematica for Quantum Mechanics: A Student's Manual. University of Basel. [doi:10.1007/978-981-13-7588-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-13-7588-0)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Stated pedagogical goal in the preface of a Master's/PhD lecture manual; the author writes the book is "an attempt to help students transform" concepts into computer representations. This is a design rationale, not an evaluated outcome; no learning measurements are reported.

> "This book is an attempt to help students transform all of the concepts of quantum mechanics into concrete computer representations, which can be constructed, evaluated, analyzed, and hopefully understood at a deeper level than what is possible with more abstract representations."
