## Evidence

### Lia 2024

Lia, Z. R.-Y., Barry, C., & Cukurova, M. (2024). Adult learners' recall and recognition performance and affective feedback when learning from an AI-generated synthetic video. Technical Report. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc estimated marginal means with Tukey's HSD across the four conditions in the recall-item MANCOVA of the 400-participant experiment. For Question 4 the article reports "∆𝑀C2-C4 = 2.06, p <.001, d = .85", the largest of the three significant C2 contrasts.

> "in Question 4, the C2 human-generated video condition performed noticeably better than the other conditions (∆𝑀C2-C1 = 1.65, p <.001, d = .71; ∆𝑀C2-C3 = 2.02, p <.001, d = .75; ∆𝑀C2-C4 = 2.06, p <.001, d = .85)"

### Lia 2024 (2)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

> "in Question 7, C4 performed the best among the other conditions (∆𝑀C4-C1 = 1.46, p <.01, d = .52; ∆𝑀C4-C2 = 1.35, p < .05, d = .47; ∆𝑀C4-C3 = 1.35, p < .05, d = .43)"

### Lia 2024 (3)
