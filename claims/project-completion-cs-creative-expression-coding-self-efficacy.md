Correlation analysis within a quasi-experimental virtual study of two teachers' eight ECS classes (survey N=144; DbD N=90, comparison N=54). Table 1 reports the printed coefficients: "CS creative expression .284** .363**" and "E-Textiles coding self-efficacy .321** .335*".

> "For students doing either DbD or comparison activities, correlation results show that the extent to which they completed the project was related to significant increases in CS creative expression and e -textiles coding self -efficacy"
