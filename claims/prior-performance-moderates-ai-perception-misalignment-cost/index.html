Laura Graf, Ramona Beinstingel, Stephan Krusche, Oleksandra Poquet. (2026). Students' Perception Accuracy of Partners' AI Use and its Relation to Collaboration Performance. Preprint, Technical University of Munich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23237

Second-order polynomial response surface (Fig. 1) with heteroscedasticity-robust standard errors, described by the authors as an illustrative complement (model R² = .37, F = 10.30) given a modest sample and discrepancy scores concentrated near zero; regression coefficients are printed but no standardised effect size.

"Consistent with this pattern, the interaction term of student A AI use perception discrepancy and student B discrepancy was negative and significant (bAB = −121.05, SE = 49.82, p = .015), whereas the remaining polynomial terms were non-significant."
