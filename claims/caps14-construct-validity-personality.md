## Evidence

### Vecchione Michele 2021

Vecchione Michele, Vacca Mariacarolina. (2021). An Italian adaptation of the Child-Adolescent Perfectionism Scale: Testing measurement invariance across grade levels and exploring associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255814

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order and partial Pearson correlations of the CAPS-14 subscales with conscientiousness and neuroticism in both samples. The article reports the pattern "was consistent across grade levels" and clearer once overlapping subscale variance was controlled; partial correlations showed conscientiousness correlated null or weakly negative with SOP-Critical and SPP.

> "As expected, SOP-Striving was positively related to conscientiousness, whereas SOP-Critical and SPP were positively related to neuroticism. This correlation pattern was consistent across grade levels and turned out to be clearer once the overlapping variance between the three CAPS scale scores was controlled."
