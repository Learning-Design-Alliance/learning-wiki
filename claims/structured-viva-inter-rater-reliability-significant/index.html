Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan 2023¶

Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan, Nabag Wisal Omer Mohamed. (2023). Structured viva validity, reliability, and acceptability as an assessment tool in health professions education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04524-6

Narrative review of inter-rater reliability findings from included studies. The review reports significant inter-rater correlations of "r = 0.78 to 0.91" (p < 0.0001) and an inter-rater agreement percentage of 86.7% in one structured viva exam.

"A study showed significant inter-rater reliability for each pair of examiners and each question (r = 0.78 to 0.91; p < 0.0001) (27). Another study showed Interrater agreement of 86.7% as described by Roh et al. [22]."
