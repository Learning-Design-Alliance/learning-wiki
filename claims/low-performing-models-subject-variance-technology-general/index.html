Lelièvre 2025¶

q2 · i? · design · r2

Subject-level sub-score analysis of CDPK accuracy for the top 15 and bottom 15 performing models, shown in Figure 10. The article reports that "the bottom 15 models demonstrate a much higher performance variance across subjects" and peak in Technology and General categories, while top models show less variance and more diverse peak subjects.

"Firstly, the bottom 15 models demonstrate a much higher performance variance across subjects, evidenced by their wider 'Performance Range' bars. Secondly, these lower-performing models consistently achieve their best scores (indicated by stars) in the Technology and General categories."
