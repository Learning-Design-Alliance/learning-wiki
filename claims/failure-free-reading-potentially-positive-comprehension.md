## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Failure Free Reading. https://www.failurefreeonline.com/index_parents.php

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the Torgesen et al. (2006) randomized controlled trial (93 third-grade students), the GRADE Passage Comprehension subtest showed an effect size of 0.35 (improvement index +14) and the WRMT-R Passage Comprehension subtest an effect size of 0.18 (improvement index +7); the domain average effect size was 0.26 with an average improvement index of +10 percentile points, though neither outcome was statistically significant.

> "Torgesen et al. (2006) examined two outcomes in this domain (WRMT-R: Passage Comprehension subtest and Group Reading Assessment and Diagnostic Evaluation (GRADE): Passage Comprehension subtest) and reported no statistically significant effects. The average effect size across the two outcomes was large enough to be considered substantively important."
