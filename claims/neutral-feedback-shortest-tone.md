Antoun Yaacoub, Zainab Assaghir, Lionel Prevost, Jérôme Da-Rugna. (2025). Analyzing Feedback Mechanisms in AI-Generated MCQs: Insights into Readability, Lexical Properties, and Levels of Challenge. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics by feedback tone (Table 2) over all 14,832 feedback fields. Neutral averaged 16.2 characters, supportive 20.5, challenging 21.3; challenging feedback was "slightly longer, on average than supportive feedback (20.5 characters)". No inferential test is printed for the tone contrast alone.

> "A clear pattern emerges: neutral feedback is consistently the shortest, averaging 16.2 characters. This aligns with the intended nature of neutral feedback, which aims for brevity and objectivity, avoiding the more extensive explanations or affective language used in other tones."
