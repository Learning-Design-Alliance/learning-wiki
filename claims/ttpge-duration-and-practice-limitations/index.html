Evidence¶

Sayı 2018¶

Sayı, A. K. (2018). Teachers’ Views about the Teacher Training Program for Gifted Education. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n4p262

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Open-ended questionnaire responses coded qualitatively. Twenty-five participants called for extended duration, and in the course-progress theme “more practice (n = 37) was needed on the curriculum.”

"They stated that the program had sufficient information about the application (n = 34) but nevertheless the program duration had to be extended (n = 25)."
