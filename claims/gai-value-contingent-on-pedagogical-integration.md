The article reports this as broad agreement across experimental and review studies, citing affordances such as accessibility, personalisation, automation, and interactivity alongside risks including over-reliance and integrity concerns. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Across both experimental and review-based studies, there is broad agreement that GAI can enhance learning when embedded within appropriate pedagogical designs.
