Lorna Uden · Gwo-Jen Hwang. (2026). The LEARN framework for responsible use of generative AI in education: a neuroscience-informed model for problem-based learning. Journal of Computers in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40692-026-00398-x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Narrative synthesis of prior GAI-in-education literature in the introduction. The article reports this as broad agreement across experimental and review studies, citing affordances such as accessibility, personalisation, automation, and interactivity alongside risks including over-reliance and integrity concerns. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Across both experimental and review-based studies, there is broad agreement that GAI can enhance learning when embedded within appropriate pedagogical designs. However, without explicit instructional and assessment structures, the use of GAI may undermine effortful cognitive processing and reflective engagement."
