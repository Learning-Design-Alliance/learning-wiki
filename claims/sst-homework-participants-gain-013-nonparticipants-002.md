## Evidence

### G.L. Lippi 2018

G.L. Lippi. (2018). Multiple choice homework as a cost-effective and efficient tool for student self-training. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.04968](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.04968)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison within the 2017-18 Waves class (68 enrolled, 21 returned homework) reported in the Analysis section. Group 1 (homework) moved from Glt = 0.39 to Wf = 0.52; group 2 moved from 0.31 to 0.29, a "deepening of the hiatus between the two groups". No statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "The final grade shows, however, a deepening of the hiatus between the two groups: the first one gained 0.13 points, while the other one lost 0.02 points between the level test and the final grade"
