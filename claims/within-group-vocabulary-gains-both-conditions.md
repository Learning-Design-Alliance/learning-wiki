## Evidence

### Nam and Trinh 2012

Ta Thanh Nam & Lap Q. Trinh. (2012). PowerPoint as a potential tool to learners' vocabulary retention: Empirical evidences from a Vietnamese secondary education setting. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 4, October - December. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070209

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test within the control group (n = 34) comparing pre-test (M = 6.82) and post-test (M = 7.53) vocabulary retention; the article reports the mean difference was "statistically significant".

> "Besides, this mean difference (MD = -0.71) was statistically significant (t = -3.35, df = 33, p = .002)."

### Nam and Trinh 2012 (2)

Ta Thanh Nam & Lap Q. Trinh. (2012). PowerPoint as a potential tool to learners' vocabulary retention: Empirical evidences from a Vietnamese secondary education setting. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 4, October - December. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070209

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test within the experimental group (n = 34) comparing pre-test (M = 6.87) and post-test (M = 8.92) vocabulary retention; the article reports the mean difference was "statistically significant".

> "In addition, the Paired Samples T-Test gives statistical evidences that this mean difference (MD = -2.05) was statistically significant (t = -14.31, df = 33, p = .00)."
