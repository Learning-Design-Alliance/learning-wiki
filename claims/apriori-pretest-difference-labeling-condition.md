## Evidence

### Clinton 2016

Clinton, V., Morsanyi, K., Alibali, M. W., & Nathan, M. J. (2016). Learning about Probability from Text and Tables: Do Color Coding and Labeling through an Interactive-user Interface Help? Applied Cognitive Psychology, 30(3), 440-453. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3223

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A 2 (color coding) × 2 (labeling) ANOVA on pretest scores found no color coding difference but an a priori labeling difference, "Cohen’s d = .51", so pretest was used as a covariate in subsequent analyses.

> "participants in the labeling condition had lower pretest scores than did participants in the no labeling condition, F(1, 97) = 6.45, p = .01, Cohen’s d = .51."
