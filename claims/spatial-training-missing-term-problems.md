# Spatial training's math effect is concentrated in missing term problems

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The spatial training group significantly outperformed controls on missing term problems (η² = .12), but not on number fact or multi-digit calculation problems. [→ Cheng 2014](#cheng-2014)

## Evidence

### Cheng 2014

Cheng, Y.-L., & Mix, K. S. (2014). Spatial Training Improves Children's Mathematics Ability. Journal of Cognition and Development, 15(1), 2-11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580905

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Separate ANCOVAs per problem type in the same randomized study found the missing term advantage ("p = .007, η2= .12"); no significant differences emerged for number fact or multi-digit calculation problems.

> "There was a significant difference favoring the spatial training group on missing term problems (e.g.,  2 + ____ = 7 or ____= 9 – 4) (F (1, 55) = 7. 80, p = .007, η2= .12)."
