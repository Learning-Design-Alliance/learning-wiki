`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory signal detection analysis of response criterion in the same sample. The article interprets the increased false alarm rate as potentially explained by a more liberal response bias toward categorizing faces as old, independent of visual selection processes indexed by eye movements.

> "larger response bias c was correlated with older age (r(299) = .109, p = .061; p = .043 with two age outliers ), but not with eye movement pattern or consistency measures  (ps > .300)"
