### Gill Monique 2024

Gill Monique, Hunt Anne, Duncan Andrea. (2024). Innovation and Competency Development in Occupational Therapy Fieldwork During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Canadian Journal of Occupational Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1177/00084174231190768

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative multi-case study of 28 occupational therapy learners (16) and preceptors (12) interviewed over Zoom about innovative pandemic fieldwork placements. Cross-case analysis of in-depth interviews led the authors to conclude that "innovative fieldwork placements can support competency development."

> "Our findings indicate that innovative fieldwork placements can support competency development in occupational therapy; however, this development is complex and contextually based."
