# In interviews, most CWP students initially reacted negatively to workshop sessions but reported improved feelings after about a month of experience

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Approximately 75% of interviewed students described initial feelings toward CWP with words like bad, negative, afraid, or scared, and the most reported cause (80%) was a violated expectation of recipe or verification labs. [→ Hitt 2014](#hitt-2014)
`q2 i?` After 3-4 weeks of CWP sessions and one team rotation, most (72%) reported that their overall feeling toward the workshop sessions improved. [→ Hitt 2014 (2)](#hitt-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Hitt 2014

Hitt, G. W., Isakovic, A. F., Fawwaz, O., Bawa'aneh, M. S., El-Kork, N., Makkiyil, S., & Qattan, I. A. (2014). Secondary implementation of interactive engagement teaching techniques: Choices and challenges in a Gulf Arab context. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Post-instruction interview study of 18 of 78 CWP students after the Spring 2011 pilot, using a common set of talking points with audio-recorded, transcribed sessions. Reports percentages as printed.

> "Approximately 75% used words like "bad", "negative", "afraid", or "scared" to describe their feelings and 88% believed the tasks outlined in workshop session agendas were "very hard", "very difficult", or "very stressful"."

### Hitt 2014 (2)

Hitt, G. W., Isakovic, A. F., Fawwaz, O., Bawa'aneh, M. S., El-Kork, N., Makkiyil, S., & Qattan, I. A. (2014). Secondary implementation of interactive engagement teaching techniques: Choices and challenges in a Gulf Arab context. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same interview study: most students attributed improvement to getting used to the sessions and adjusting pre-session preparation such as reading the text and discussing strategy with team members.

> "After 3-4 weeks of experiencing the CWP sessions and having one rotation of team membership, most (72%) report that their overall feeling toward the workshop session improved"
