# Across guided conditions, participants corrected most rule violations in a subsequent meter-only password task, with group-level tip compliance of 91.9% (T1), 91.8% (T2), and 93.9% (T3)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized experiment, group-level tip compliance in the post-test password was high in all three guided conditions (T1 91.9%, T2 91.8%, T3 93.9%). [→ Qian Ma 2026](#qian-ma-2026)

## Evidence

### Qian Ma 2026

Qian Ma, Yingfan Zhou, Shubhang Kaushik, Aamod Joshi, Aditya Majumdar, Noah Apthorpe, Yan Shvartzshnaider, Sarah Rajtmajer, Brett Frischmann. (2026). Pedagogical Friction in Password Creation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.06650](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.06650)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Randomized experiment with 128 participants across four interface conditions; this result reports group-level tip compliance (proportion of triggered test-phase tips whose rules were satisfied in the post-test password), with cluster-robust 95% CIs. The article reports "tip compliance rates were high in all guided conditions".

> "tip compliance rates were high in all guided conditions: T1 91.9%, T2 91.8%, and T3 93.9%. All three guidance designs led most tipped rules to be corrected in the post-test password."
