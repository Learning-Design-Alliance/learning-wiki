## Evidence

### Beattie Kirsty L. 2022

Beattie Kirsty L., Hill Andrew, Horswill Mark S., Grove Philip M., Stevenson Andrew R. L. (2022). Aptitude and attitude: predictors of performance during and after basic laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08668-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression on total training score (time plus error penalties) for the same 34 novices. The article reports that "as psychological flexibility increased, the total training score (time + errors) significantly decreased (i.e. performance improved)". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The final model was statistically significant, F(4, 29) = 17.12, p < 0.001, and explained 66.2% of the variance in total training score, RAdj2=0.662 (large effect)."
