### Todor M. Mishonov 2023

Todor M. Mishonov, Albert M. Varonov, Peter V. Peshev, Vasil G. Yordanov, Stojan G. Manolev, Riste Popeski-Dimovski. (2023). Studying of well-known and widely used technical patent. The principle of operation of auto-zero and chopper-stabilized DC amplifiers. https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04328

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Authors' observational summary of ten EPO years (Appendix A, Final remarks). The solution appendix states of the application item: "This item related to possible technical applications was not solved by any participant."

> "When the problem is given at our Olympiads there are always several students capable to understand and solve the problem to great detail which is very encouraging. However, everybody experienced difficulty to guest what could be the technical application, which indicate the need to complement the education in physics."
