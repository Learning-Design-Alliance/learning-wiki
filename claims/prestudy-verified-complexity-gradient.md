## Evidence

### Krieglstein 2025

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Wesenberg, L., Rey, G. D., & Schneider, S. (2025). The Distorting Influence of Primacy Effects on Reporting Cognitive Load in Learning Materials of Varying Complexity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09980-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pre-study (N=67 students) with three system-paced learning units on space phenomena presented in randomised order; ICL rated after each unit. The linear contrast was significant, t(132) = [4.05, 13.56], p < 0.001. Means rose from 3.29 (low) to 4.38 (medium) to 6.90 (high), confirming "the hypothesized linear increase in complexity across the three learning units".

> "the medium to large effect sizes found for contrast 1 (d = 0.60), contrast 2 (d = 2.00), and contrast 3 (d = 1.40) provide further support for the hypothesized linear increase in complexity across the three learning units"
