The brief summarizes research in Chicago and nationally on predictors of college outcomes, reporting that "a student’s high school GPA is by far the strongest predictor" of admission, persistence, and graduation compared with ACT and SAT scores. The brief also notes students need at least a B average to have even a chance of obtaining a four-year degree.
