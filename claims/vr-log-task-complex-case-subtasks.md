### Huh Yeon-Ju 2026

Huh Yeon-Ju, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Narae, Jeong Seoi, Kong Hyoun-Joong, Myung Sun Jung. (2026). Immersive Virtual Reality Training to Improve Novice Physicians’ Emergency Response Skills: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71455

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis of head-mounted display log data from the VR group (usable data from 32 simple and 39 complex cases, 29 participants with both). The quote reports the printed subtask comparison; task duration (151.05 vs 132.95 seconds) showed no significant difference (P=.11).

> "The average number of tasks completed out of the 6 subtasks was 3.19 in the simple case and 4.33 in the complex case (t69=−3.02, P=.002)."
