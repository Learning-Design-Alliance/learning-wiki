## Evidence

### Batdi 2020

Batdi, V. & Elaldi, S. (2020). Effects of drama method on social communication skills: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 6(3), 435-457. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1258528

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis via document review of five of the 24 studies (4 MA theses and 1 article) using Maxqda and categorical analysis. Cohen's kappa per theme ranged from .786 to .861, e.g. ".847 for the theme” Negative Aspects of Using Drama".

> "The themes were as follows: (1) Contributions of the Affective Domain, (2) Contributions of the Cognitive Domain, (3) Impact on the Social Domain, (4) Contributions of the Delivery of Course, and (5) Negative Aspects of Using Drama."
