## Evidence

### Frendø M 2022

Frendø M, Frithioff A, Konge L, Cayé-Thomasen P, Sørensen MS, Andersen SAW. (2022). Cochlear implant surgery: virtual reality simulation training and transfer of skills to cadaver dissection—A randomized, controlled trial. The Journal of International Advanced Otology. https://doi.org/10.5152/iao.2022.21429

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Component analysis of the primary outcome via LMM: on drilling-related items (maximum 24 points), 'the intervention group scored a mean of 12.2 points' versus 11.5 for controls; P =.63.

> "Evaluating the drilling components separately, the intervention group scored a mean of 12.2 points [95% confidence interval (10.1–14.3)] while the control group scored 11.5 points [95% confidence interval (9.4–13.6)]; P =.63; LMM) of the maximum drilling-related 24 points."

### Frendø M 2022 (2)

Frendø M, Frithioff A, Konge L, Cayé-Thomasen P, Sørensen MS, Andersen SAW. (2022). Cochlear implant surgery: virtual reality simulation training and transfer of skills to cadaver dissection—A randomized, controlled trial. The Journal of International Advanced Otology. https://doi.org/10.5152/iao.2022.21429

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Component analysis of the primary outcome via LMM: on insertion-related items (maximum 20 points), intervention scores were 10.7 versus 10.3 for controls; P =.63, not statistically significant.

> "Scores for insertion where 10.7 points in the intervention group [95% confidence interval (9.4–12.0)] vs. 10.3 points in the control group [95% confidence interval (9.0–11.6); P =.63; LMM)] of a maximum insertion-related score of 20 points."
