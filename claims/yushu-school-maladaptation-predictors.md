# Bereavement and burial under a collapsed building predicted poor school adaptation, while being female, senior, or participating in reconstruction predicted better adaptation

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In multivariate logistic regression, bereavement (OR = 1.77) and burial under debris (OR = 1.47) were associated with higher risk of poor school adaptation, while female gender (OR = 0.73), senior grade (OR = 0.48) and post-disaster reconstruction participation (OR = 0.68) were associated with lower risk. [→ Liu Shou 2019](#liu-shou-2019)

## Evidence

### Liu Shou 2019

Liu Shou, Lu Li, Bai Zheng-Zhong, Su Min, Qi Zheng-Qing, Zhang Shi-Yu, Chen Yuan, Ao Bing-Yu, Cui Feng-Zhen, Lagarde Emmanuel, Lii Kehshin. (2019). Post-Traumatic Stress and School Adaptation in Adolescent Survivors Five Years after the 2010 Yushu Earthquake in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214167

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the 1976-participant survey with ASAS as dependent variable; the printed ORs for female, senior, and reconstruction participation were 0.73, 0.48, and 0.68, and for burial 1.47 and bereavement 1.77.

> "Logistic multivariate regression showed that female (OR = 0.73, 95% CI: 0.60–0.89, p = 0.002) and senior (OR = 0.48, 95% CI: 0.39–0.59, p < 0.001) students, and those who where involved in post-disaster reconstruction (OR = 0.68, 95% CI: 0.54–0.85, p = 0.001), were less likely to have school maladaptation"
