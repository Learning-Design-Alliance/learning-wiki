Evidence¶

Murray 2023¶

Murray, E., Horner, A. J., & Göbel, S. M. (2023). A Meta-analytic Review of the Effectiveness of Spacing and Retrieval Practice for Mathematics Learning. https://osf.io/

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of the retrieval-versus-restudy subset (6 studies, 20 effect sizes), mostly university settings. The weighted mean was "g = 0.221 (se = 0.108)" and the authors state the confidence interval crossed zero, so the effect is not robust.

"the overall weighted mean effect size is g = 0.221 (se = 0.108) (95% confidence interval [-0.091, 0.532]). Note that the 95% confidence intervals crossed the zero line."
