## Evidence

### Escalante 2023

Escalante, J., Pack, A., & Barrett, A. (2023). AI-generated feedback on writing: insights into efficacy and ENL student preference. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00425-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis by three researchers of Study 2's open-ended survey data; students cited personal interaction as "more engaging", the ability to ask follow-up questions, and developing writing and speaking skills simultaneously. One student worried AI revision could erase personal voice.

> "Perhaps the most prominent was the affordances of sitting down face to face and interacting with a human. For some this was viewed as beneficial because they found personal interaction to be "more engaging" and "a fun way to learn""
