`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this to Fivush et al. (2009), a study of families reminiscing about emotions with 9 to 12-year-old children, quoted from the authors' summary at p. 228. The review argues this complexity could be obscured by global coding.

> "maternal elaborations and evaluations on the factual aspects of events are related to higher well-being, but paternal elaborations, evaluations, and repetitions of emotional aspects of negative events are related to lower child well-being."
