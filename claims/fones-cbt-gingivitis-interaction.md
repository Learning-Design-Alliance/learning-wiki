## Evidence

### Harnacke Daniela 2012

Harnacke Daniela, Mitter Simona, Lehner Marc, Munzert Jörn, Deinzer Renate. (2012). Improving Oral Hygiene Skills by Computer-Based Training: A Randomized Controlled Comparison of the Modified Bass and the Fones Techniques. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0037072

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized, examiner-blinded ANCOVA analysis of n=56 students comparing Fones, Bass, and control groups on percentage of bleeding sites (PBI), with baseline values as covariates. The article reports a "significant group×time interaction" with η2 = 0.114.

> "Overall ANCOVAs revealed a significant group×time interaction (F(4/102) = 3.267; p = 0.016; ε = 0.957; η2 = 0.114) for gingivitis (Figure 2)"
