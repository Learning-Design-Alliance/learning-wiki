## Evidence

### Schaller Michael D. 2023

Schaller Michael D., Gencheva Marieta, Gunther Michael R., Weed Scott A. (2023). Training doctoral students in critical thinking and experimental design using problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04569-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blind-graded identical pre/post experimental design tests (maximum score 16, three raters, ICC 0.82) in the second course offering of 27 enrolled students; scores compared with a one-tailed Mann Whitney U test, and the article reports the difference "did reach statistical significance (p = 0.0329)".

> "In contrast, the difference between the pre- and post-test in 2021 did reach statistical significance (p = 0.0329)."

### Schaller Michael D. 2023 (2)

Schaller Michael D., Gencheva Marieta, Gunther Michael R., Weed Scott A. (2023). Training doctoral students in critical thinking and experimental design using problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04569-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In the first course offering (30 students, 18 post-tests), blind-graded pre/post tests compared via one-tailed Mann Whitney U test showed no statistically significant difference; the article notes post-test scores "trended higher".

> "The average scores of the pre- and post-test in 2020 were not statistically different (p = 0.0673), although the post-test scores trended higher."
