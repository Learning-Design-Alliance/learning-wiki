## Evidence

### Twilhaar 2020

Twilhaar, S. E., Belopolsky, A. V., de Kieviet, J. F., van Elburg, R. M., & Oosterlaan, J. (2020). Voluntary and Involuntary Control of Attention in Adolescents Born Very Preterm: A Study of Eye Movements. Child Development, 91(4), 1272-1283. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13310

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analyses (PROCESS macro, 5,000 resamples) relating very preterm birth to parent-rated inattention, IQ, and academic performance through antisaccade performance (proportion of erroneous saccades toward the onset). The printed values are partially standardized indirect effects in SD units, not standardized effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Partially standardized indirect effects showed that very preterm birth was associated with a 0.16 (95% CI [−0.34, −0.02]), 0.11 (95% CI [−0.26, −0.01]), and 0.11 SD (95% CI [−0.23, −0.01]) decrease in inattention score, IQ, and academic performance through its effect on antisaccade performance, respectively."
