### Robbrecht M 2025

Robbrecht M, Van Winckel M, Mulder A, Embo M. (2025). What is the learning effect of video review in postgraduate medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06515-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of 11 studies (4 RCTs, 7 single-group pretest–posttest) of video review in clinical PGME settings. The review's narrative synthesis reports "positive learning effects associated with video review, with 8 studies reporting significant results" across Kirkpatrick levels 2 to 4. No pooled effect size was calculated due to heterogeneity.

> "The results indicate positive learning effects associated with video review, with 8 studies reporting significant results [10, 30–32, 35–38]. Positive effects are reported for levels 2 to 4 of Kirkpatrick’s model for evaluation."
