Correlation analysis in the study of 457 Mexican high school students found "statistically significant and positive correlations with school satisfaction in all subjects, the highest being MATH (r = 0.331 **) and the lowest being PE (r = 0.134 **)"; r = 0.331 is a medium correlation.
