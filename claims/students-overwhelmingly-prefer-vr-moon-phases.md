## Evidence

### Madden J. 2020

Madden J., Pandita S., Schuldt J. P., Kim B., S. Won A., Holmes N. G. (2020). Ready student one: Exploring the predictors of student learning in virtual reality. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229788

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

After completing the post-test, participants tried all three conditions and chose a favorite; 78% chose VR, 12% desktop, and 10% hands-on. Common reasons were that VR was "easier to visualize", "more realistic", "more immersive", and "the most accurate".

> "Consistent with previous work [3] participants overwhelmingly (Chi2 = 152, p < 0.00001) preferred the VR condition as their favorite learning method independent of their study condition (Fig 5)."
