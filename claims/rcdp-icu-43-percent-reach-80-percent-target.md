## Evidence

### Manetti Previero Bruno 2026

Manetti Previero Bruno, Spataru Ana, Pinto Ruxandra, Piquette Dominique. (2026). Rapid cycle deliberate practice: a complement to clinical exposure in critical care education. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.1093/atsscholar/aapaf003

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective observational educational initiative at a Canadian tertiary ICU: 23 learners completed the week-4 individual formative assessment scenario scored on a 22-task checklist. The article reports "Ten of 23 learners (43.5%; 95% CI, 23.2–63.7)" met the ≥80% threshold; the overall mean score was 77% (95% CI 73–80).

> "Ten of 23 learners (43.5%; 95% CI, 23.2–63.7) displayed ≥80% of the targeted key clinical behaviors during the assessment scenario on week #4 (Figure 1)."
