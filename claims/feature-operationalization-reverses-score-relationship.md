## Evidence

### Sowmya Vajjala 2016

Sowmya Vajjala. (2016). Automated assessment of non-native learner essays: Investigating the role of linguistic features. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of regression feature weights (Table 12) for the FCE model: document length is a negative-valued predictor while sentence length is positive, reversing the TOEFL11 pattern where document length had a high positive weight.

> "An interesting aspect of this list of features is that, docum ent length is a negative valued predictor, while sentence length is a positive predictor for FCE texts."

### Sowmya Vajjala 2016 (2)

Sowmya Vajjala. (2016). Automated assessment of non-native learner essays: Investigating the role of linguistic features. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors conclude from the FCE weight table (e.g., TTR negative, corrected TTR positive) that "the exact operationalization of t he features can change the direction of the relationship with the score variable".

> "Thus, the exact operationalization of t he features can change the direction of the relationship with the score variable."
