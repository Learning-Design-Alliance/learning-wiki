## Evidence

### Ciaran Hughes 2020

Ciaran Hughes, Joshua Isaacson, Anastasia Perry, Ranbel Sun, and Jessica Turner. (2020). Teaching Quantum Computing to High School Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey item on interest in further QC learning (5 indicating maximum interest), shown in the bottom right plot of Fig. 2 for the 45-student cohort. Only descriptive medians and distributions are reported; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

> "Prior to completing course, the median was 4, and this did not change after completion. The change in distribution demonstrates that students successfully learned about QC and some wished to continue studying QC further at university while others learned enough to satisfy their curiosity."
