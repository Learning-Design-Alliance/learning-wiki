## Evidence

### Dominique Bradley 2015

Dominique Bradley, Evan Crawford, Sara E. Dahill-Brown. (2015). Fidelity of Implementation in a Large-Scale, Randomized, Field Trial: Identifying the Critical Components of Values Affirmation. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562183

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The authors' own district-wide randomized trial: 7th-grade students in an urban Midwestern district were randomized within schools to treatment or control, with 1,049 consented students across 11 schools. The article reports the trial "revealed important, but relatively modest, impacts" relative to earlier studies.

> "our district-wide randomized trial of values-affirmation revealed important, but relatively modest, impacts compared to previous studies (Cohen et al. 2006, 2009; Miyake et al. 20010; Sherman et al, 2013)"
