Evidence¶

Ramjaun Aliya 2015¶

Ramjaun Aliya, Sudarshan Monisha, Patakfalvi Laura, Tamblyn Robyn, Meguerditchian Ari N. (2015). Educating medical trainees on medication reconciliation: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4373246/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Curriculum-integrated clerkship exercise (n=98) with didactic sessions, faculty role-play demonstration, self-study, direct patient care and small-group reflection; questionnaire before and after the clerkship showed "overall confidence scores increased from an average of 2.7 out of 5" to 4.0 (p < 0.01).

"overall confidence scores increased from an average of 2.7 out of 5 (SD = 1.0) to 4.0 out of 5 (SD = 0.8) (p < 0.01) [26]"

Ramjaun Aliya 2015 (2)¶

Ramjaun Aliya, Sudarshan Monisha, Patakfalvi Laura, Tamblyn Robyn, Meguerditchian Ari N. (2015). Educating medical trainees on medication reconciliation: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4373246/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Curriculum-integrated interactive learning exercise (n=158) for second-year students: pharmacist instruction, standardized patient demonstration, small-group medication history-taking and class reflection. Survey ratings showed "knowledge level as having increased by 27%" and comfort by 20% (p < 0.001).

"students also completed a survey in which they rated their knowledge level as having increased by 27% (p < 0.001), and their comfort in performing medication reconciliation as having increased by 20% (p < 0.001) [27]"

Related Claims¶

  • Experiential education interventions for medical residents showed little effect on medication reconciliation competence and skills — related
  • Only seven studies of medication reconciliation education initiatives for medical trainees were eligible for inclusion, indicating a lack of published education initiatives — related
  • A case-based workshop without an experiential patient-care component increased medical students' reported independence in conducting medication reconciliation — related
  • Only 37.2% of residents were high performers in medication reconciliation after a standardized patient discharge encounter, with primary care program residents more likely to be high performers — related
  • Curricula predominantly featured interactive and experiential teaching methods such as simulation and project-based learning — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Experiential learning in authentic clinical environments (community engagement, home visits, outreach rotations) was reported to produce the greatest positive impact on student attitudes, confidence, and perceived competence, but is the most resource-intensive — a broader claim this one bears on
