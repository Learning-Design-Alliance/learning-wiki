# After the Alice/4C-ID treatment, logical reasoning (ECDL) improved significantly in the control group but not in the experimental group

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Costa and Miranda 2019

Costa, J. M., & Miranda, G. L. (2019). Using Alice Software with 4C-ID Model: Effects in Programming Knowledge and Logical Reasoning. Informatics in Education, 18(1), 1-15. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2019.01

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-Student test on ECDL scores before and after the two-month treatment in the very-low-SES experimental group (n = 6). The article reports the change was not significant; no effect size is printed.

> "In the experimental group, the improvement wasn't significant (t (5) = 1.00; p = 0.182). Before the experimental treatment this group obtained the average of 12.50 (SEM = 1.565) and after the experimental treatment the average was 12.83 (SEM = 1.778)."
