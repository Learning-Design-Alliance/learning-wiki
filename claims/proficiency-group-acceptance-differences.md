# Low- and intermediate-proficiency students report higher perceived usefulness, ease of use, and satisfaction for AI-assisted English tools than high-proficiency students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Both Low- and Intermediate-proficiency groups reported higher perceived usefulness than the High-proficiency group (mean differences 0.377 and 0.381), while Low and Intermediate did not differ. [→ Wu J 2026](#wu-j-2026)
`q2 i2` Perceived ease of use and satisfaction also differed across proficiency groups by omnibus ANOVA (PEOU η2 = 0.051; satisfaction η2 = 0.036), with High-proficiency students lowest. [→ Wu J 2026 (2)](#wu-j-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Wu J 2026

Wu J, Wang Y, He Y, Yin X, Chen F and Wan B (2026) Acceptance of AI-assisted English language learning tools in higher education: psychological correlates across disciplinary and proficiency groups. Front. Psychol. 17:1806457. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1806457

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Tukey HSD post hoc comparisons following a significant omnibus ANOVA for perceived usefulness, F(2, 207) = 4.25, p = 0.016, η2 = 0.039; the quote reports the "mean diﬀerence = 0.377" and 0.381 contrasts against the High group. High-proficiency n = 30.

> "Tukey HSD comparisons indicated that both the Low-proﬁciency group (mean diﬀerence = 0.377, p = 0.009) and the Intermediate-proﬁciency group (mean diﬀerence = 0.381, p = 0.006) reported higher perceived usefulness than the High-proﬁciency group."

### Wu J 2026 (2)

Wu J, Wang Y, He Y, Yin X, Chen F and Wan B (2026) Acceptance of AI-assisted English language learning tools in higher education: psychological correlates across disciplinary and proficiency groups. Front. Psychol. 17:1806457. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1806457

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus one-way ANOVA across Low (n = 77), Intermediate (n = 103), and High (n = 30) proficiency groups on perceived ease of use shows a significant difference with "η2 = 0.051"; satisfaction also differed significantly (F = 3.85, p = 0.023, η2 = 0.036), with the High group lowest on both.

> "For perceived ease of use, mean levels also diﬀered across proﬁciency groups, F(2, 207) = 5.59, p = 0.004, η2 = 0.051."
