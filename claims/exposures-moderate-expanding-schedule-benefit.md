### Latimier 2021

Latimier, A., Peyre, H., & Ramus, F. (2021). A Meta-Analytic Review of the Benefit of Spacing out Retrieval Practice Episodes on Retention. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator meta-regression on subset 2 (54 effect sizes comparing expanding and uniform schedules). The review reports that "the number of exposures of an item during retrieval practice explains inconsistencies between studies", with more testing favouring the expanding schedule.

> "Moderator analyses on this subset showed that the number of exposures of an item during retrieval practice explains inconsistencies between studies: the more learners are tested, the more beneficial the expanding schedule is compared with the uniform one."
