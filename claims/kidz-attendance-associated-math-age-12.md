# Attendance at the KiDZ preschool intervention is positively associated with mathematical competencies at age 12

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Children attending KiDZ showed better mathematical outcomes at age 12 than control-group children, controlling for child and family background and home and primary school learning environments. [→ Lehrl 2017](#lehrl-2017)

## Evidence

### Lehrl 2017

Lehrl, Simone, Kluczniok, Katharina, Rossbach, Hans-Guenther, & Anders, Yvonne (2017). Long term persistence of preschool intervention on children's mathematical development: Results from the German model project "Kindergarten of the Future in Bavaria." Global Education Review, 4(3), 70-87. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158198

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pathway model (model 1) in MPlus predicting age-12 DEMAT6+ scores from KiDZ participation, controlling for gender, age, maternal education, early home learning environment, school type, and grade-1 instructional quality, using FIML on all 191 recruited children. The article reports "b = .22, p < .05"; no standardized effect size beyond the coefficient is printed.

> "Results indicate that KiDZ participation is significantly associated with mathematical competencies ( b = .22, p < .05) at age 12 suggesting that children in the KiDZ group show better mathematical outcomes at age 12."
