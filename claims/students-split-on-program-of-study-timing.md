### Nodine 2012

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Across the three student types, most participants had enrolled without clear education or career goals. The report states "Participants were split in their perspectives about the tension between exploring options and having more structure." Many said with hindsight they wished they had entered with more-focused goals, while others said students need time to explore education and career areas.
