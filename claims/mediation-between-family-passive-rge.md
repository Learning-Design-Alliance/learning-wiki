# Environmental mediation of polygenic score effects on achievement is nearly exclusively observed between families, consistent with passive gene-environment correlation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multilevel mediation models clustering dizygotic twins within families, nearly all mediation effects were captured at the between-family level rather than the within-sibling level. [→ Zhou Quan 2024](#zhou-quan-2024)

## Evidence

### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

1-1-1 two-level mediation models on dizygotic twins (families ranging between 1278 and 3356 depending on age and measures), separating within- from between-family effects. The study found "nearly all mediation effects were captured at the between-family level", with PGS effects attenuated within families as expected.

> "We also observed that nearly all mediation effects were captured at the between-family level for the EA PGS prediction of achievement across development (Fig. 4 and Supplementary Table 18) and the same was observed for Cog and NonCog PGS effects"
