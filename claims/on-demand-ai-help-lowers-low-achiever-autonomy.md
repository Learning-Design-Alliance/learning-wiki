## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Lower-achieving students using on-demand AI interventions showed a significant decline in learner autonomy (d = -0.383, p < 0.05). [→ Xusheng Dai 2025](#xusheng-dai-2025)
`q2 i2` The autonomy decline was particularly large in the technical-psychological dimension (d = -0.549, p < 0.05), which overlaps with self-regulation. [→ Xusheng Dai 2025 (2)](#xusheng-dai-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Xusheng Dai 2025

Xusheng Dai, Zhaochun Wen, Jianxiao Jiang, Huiqin Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). How Students Use AI Feedback Matters: Experimental Evidence on Physics Achievement and Autonomy. Preprint, under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup autonomy result from Experiment 2's randomized trial, measured with a Chinese-translated four-dimension learner autonomy questionnaire (Murase, 2015, adapted; dimension alphas 0.901, 0.866, 0.720, 0.831). The article reports autonomy "notably declined among lower achievers" with an overall effect of d = -0.383.

> "autonomy notably declined among lower achievers exposed to on-demand AI interventions (d = -0.383, p < 0.05), particularly in the technical-psychological dimension (d = -0.549, p < 0.05)"

### Xusheng Dai 2025 (2)

Xusheng Dai, Zhaochun Wen, Jianxiao Jiang, Huiqin Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). How Students Use AI Feedback Matters: Experimental Evidence on Physics Achievement and Autonomy. Preprint, under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same Experiment 2 subgroup analysis; the technical-psychological dimension, which the article notes "has a large overlap with self-regulation," showed the largest decline (d = -0.549, p < 0.05).

> "autonomy notably declined among lower achievers exposed to on-demand AI interventions (d = -0.383, p < 0.05), particularly in the technical-psychological dimension (d = -0.549, p < 0.05)"
