Evidence¶

Pang Xuefei 2026¶

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Competency-domain subgroup analysis of the 27 included studies, assessed via examinations and questionnaires. All three domains were significant, with self-efficacy showing the largest pooled SMD and very high heterogeneity.

"ECE had significant positive effects on theoretical knowledge acquisition (SMD = 1.02, 95% CI = 0.58-1.46, I² = 93.9%, P < .001), clinical practice performance (SMD = 1.47, 95% CI = 0.98-1.96, I² = 96.7%, P < .001), and self-efficacy (SMD = 1.62, 95% CI = 0.92-2.32, I² = 96.1%, P < .001)"
