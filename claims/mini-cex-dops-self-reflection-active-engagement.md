Nie Shengxiao, Ma Shengmiao, Wang Lei. (2026). Perspectives and challenges in implementing nursing-specific mini-CEX and DOPS: a descriptive qualitative study of clinical nursing educators and novice nurses in a tertiary hospital in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-113912

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the novice nurse growth theme; one novice reported a habit of continuous self-reflection enhancing surgical performance, and an educator stated "self-reflection on performance is more impactful than external feedback".

> "Immediate feedback optimises learning via active, learner-centric engagement, shifting from passive to active participation. During feedback sessions, novice nurses are actively involved in self-reflection, identifying strengths and areas for improvement before receiving educator input."
