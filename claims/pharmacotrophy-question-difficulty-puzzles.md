## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceived difficulty differed by question type (pANOVA < 0.0001), with puzzles rated more difficult than MCQ-S, MCQ-M, T/F and A/B/Both. [→ Delage Clément 2024](#delage-clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Delage Clément 2024

Delage Clément, Palayer Maeva, Lerouet Dominique, Besson Valérie C. (2024). “Pharmacotrophy”: a playful tournament for game- and team-based learning in pharmacology education - assessing its impact on students’ performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05157-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2022 participant survey of interest and difficulty for online question types, analysed with one-way ANOVA and Tukey’s multiple comparisons. The article reports puzzles “rated significantly more difficult than the MCQ-S” and the other formats, while interest did not differ by type.

> "Puzzle questions, which were both reported challenging and confusing, were rated significantly more difficult than the MCQ-S (p < 0.0001), MCQ-M (p = 0.0181), T/F (p < 0.0001) and A/B/Both (p = 0.0003)."
