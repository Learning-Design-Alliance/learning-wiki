## Evidence

### Carrie Bansom Squire 1910

Carrie Bansom Squire. (1910). Graded Mental Tests. Part III. Judgment, Conclusions and Summary of Results. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Graded+Mental+Tests+Squire

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Individual testing of the alternative judgment test across sixth- to thirteenth-year groups, unretarded and retarded. The article reports very high correct judgment in the youngest group, with average errors as low as 0.5 in some unretarded groups, and notes "the ability to judge correctly was manifested to a very high degree".

> "As these alternatives present but two possibilities, one of which is bound to be right, and as the experiences involved were familiar to the youngest child, the ability to judge correctly was manifested to a very high degree in the youngest group."
