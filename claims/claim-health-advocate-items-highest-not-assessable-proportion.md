### Esteves 2021

Esteves, A., McConnell, M., Ferretti, E., Garber, A., & Fung-Kee-Fung, K. (2021). "When in Doubt, Ask the Patient": A Quantitative, Patient-Oriented Approach to Formative Assessment of CanMEDS Roles. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11169

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An analysis of scoring inference across 69 patient questionnaires and 70 fellow questionnaires found that "The health advocate competency had the highest proportion of "Not Assessable" responses for both patient and self-assessment questionnaires". None of the communicator and professional items exceeded 30%.

> "The health advocate competency had the highest proportion of "Not Assessable" responses for both patient and self-assessment questionnaires. More specifically, of a total eight items, six items were rated as "Not Assessable" more than 30% of the time for the patient questionnaires (health advocacy items 1–6), and four items were rated as "Not Assessable" more than 30% of the time for self-assessments (items 2–5)."
