## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Effect sizes depended on control group condition: they were higher when the control received education as usual and lower when the control received a reading treatment. [→ Verhoeven 2022](#verhoeven-2022)

## Evidence

### Verhoeven 2022

Verhoeven, L.T.W.; Voeten, M.J.M.; Segers, P.C.J. (2022). Computer-assisted word reading intervention effects throughout the primary grades: A meta-analysis. Educational Research Review, 37, article 100486. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2022.100486

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Control-condition moderator analysis in the meta-analysis of 67 studies; the review reports effects were "higher in the case of a control condition with education as usual and lower in cases of a reading treatment control condition". No effect size for this moderator is printed in the available text.

> "The effect sizes were dependent on the control group condition in that the effect sizes were higher in the case of a control condition with education as usual and lower in cases of a reading treatment control condition."
