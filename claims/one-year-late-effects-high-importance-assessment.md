## Evidence

### Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto 2025

Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto, Panúncio-Pinto Maria Paula, Bollela Valdes Roberto, de Almeida Troncon Luiz Ernesto. (2025). Faculty development for undergraduate student programmatic assessment: a Brazilian multi-centered longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07337-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online questionnaire one year after the workshops, answered by 121 participants (33.7%), rating 36 outcomes on a five-point importance scale. The instrument showed Cronbach's alpha = 0.90 overall (0.931–0.943 per dimension) and was judged for face and content validity by a panel of seven judges. Outcomes above 90% included blueprinting, formative assessment, feedback, and programmatic assessment systems.

> "In the follow-up evaluation one year later, more than 90% of the 121 (33.7%) respondents to the reliable instrument (Cronbach’s α = 0,90) attributed high importance of participation to their work in student assessment."
