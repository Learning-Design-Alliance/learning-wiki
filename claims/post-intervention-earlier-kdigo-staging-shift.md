Comparison of KDIGO staging distribution between 138 pre-intervention and 18 post-intervention cases. The article reports the staging proportions and p-values, and notes post-intervention patients were older with higher baseline creatinine.

> "Significant differences between intervention periods included KDIGO staging distribution (p < 0.001), with post-intervention patients showing more Stage 1 cases (22.2% vs. 2.2%, p = 0.015) and fewer Stage 3 cases (50.0% vs. 79.7%, p = 0.054)"
