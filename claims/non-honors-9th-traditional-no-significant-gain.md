## Evidence

### Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson 2008

Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson. (2008). Effectiveness of Ninth-Grade Physics in Maine: Conceptual Understanding. The Physics Teacher. http://scitation.aip.org/tpt/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null result from the Maine seven-school comparison (Table II): the non-honors, non-Modeling 9th-grade group (N=80) moved from a pretest mean of 5.6 to a post-test mean of 6.3, a normalized gain of 3% with p-value 0.072. "For this sub-group only, the post-test scores are not significantly different from the pretest scores."

> "All of the normalized gains are significant except for those of the non-honors-level 9th graders that did not receive Modeling-based instruction. For this sub-group only, the post-test scores are not significantly different from the pretest scores."
