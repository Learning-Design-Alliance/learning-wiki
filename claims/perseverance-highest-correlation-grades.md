## Evidence

### Huéscar Hernández E. 2020

Huéscar Hernández E., Moreno-Murcia J. A., Cid L., Monteiro D., Rodrigues F. (2020). Passion or Perseverance? The Effect of Perceived Autonomy Support and Grit on Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17062143

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis (Table 1) for the 443 students. Grit-perseverance correlated with academic performance at r = 0.20 (squared correlation 0.04), the highest of the grit factors.

> "Both grit factors displayed a significant positive correlation with academic performance, perseverance being the factor with the highest correlation coefficient (0.20)."

### Huéscar Hernández E. 2020 (2)

Huéscar Hernández E., Moreno-Murcia J. A., Cid L., Monteiro D., Rodrigues F. (2020). Passion or Perseverance? The Effect of Perceived Autonomy Support and Grit on Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17062143

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis (Table 1) for the same sample. Autonomy support correlated with BPN satisfaction, intrinsic motivation, and perseverance but not with academic performance (r = −0.01) or passion (r = 0.10).

> "According to the correlation matrix, autonomy support was positively correlated with BPN satisfaction, intrinsic motivation, and perseverance, but not with passion and academic performance."
