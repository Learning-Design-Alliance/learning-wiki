Fully adjusted multivariable logistic regression in the FAST national cross-sectional survey. The article reports males "significantly more likely than females" for overall participation, oral presentations, leadership and audits/QIPs, while "No gender differences were seen in publication rates" (OR 1.08, p=0.39). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "males were significantly more likely than females to participate in extracurricular activities (OR 1.16, CI 1.05 to 1.29, p=0.005) including oral presentations (OR 1.25, CI 1.1 to 1.43, p=0.0007), leadership roles (OR 1.2, CI 1.04 to 1.38, p=0.001) and audits/QIPs (OR 1.2, CI 1.05 to 1.37, p=0.008). No gender differences were seen in publication rates (OR 1.08, CI 0.9 to 1.3, p=0.39)."
