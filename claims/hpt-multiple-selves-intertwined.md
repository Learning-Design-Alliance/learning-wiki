# HPT is not a simple shedding of presentist perspectives: multiple selves of one learner intertwine with the target's perspective

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Protocol analysis shows a single learner engages the HPT target through multiple simultaneous selves (hypothetical, factual-seeming, and reflective), contradicting a dichotomous learner-versus-target view. [→ Nilsen 2016](#nilsen-2016)

## Evidence

### Nilsen 2016

Nilsen, A. P. (2016). Navigating windows into past human minds: A framework of shifting selves in historical perspective taking. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 25, 372-410. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2016.1160830

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of Callie's response to Józefów Document A, in which she moved among hypothetical past self, now-self, and imagined past self while refining hypothesized feelings (betrayed, weird, sad, victimized, attacked). The article concludes "Multiple “Callie’s” engaged with the HPT target."

> "Multiple “Callie’s” engaged with the HPT target and intertwined perspectives that were hypothetical, entertained as factual, and at the same time, reflective on the experience."
