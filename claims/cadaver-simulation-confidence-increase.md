### Stathas SpiroAnthony 2024

Teaching medical students ultrasound-guided needle aspiration of synthetic cysts: effect of a formalin-embalmed cadaver simulation model. Pre- and post-training 5-point Likert self-confidence surveys administered in the same 30-student interventional study; the article reports the increase was significant across all items by Wilcoxon signed-rank test, with "overall mean Likert scores rose from 1.18 (95% CI 0.96 to 1.39) to 4.31 (95% CI 4.09 to 4.53)".
