Evidence¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K 2026¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K (2026). Empowering teachers to use AI for differentiation: changes in teachers’ acceptance of AI-based technologies following participation in a short professional development training session. Front. Educ. 11:1875856. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1875856

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-training descriptive ratings on a five-item scale (α = .83) in the pilot evaluation. Percentages combine the two highest agreement categories; the article also reports 95% perceived the content as (very) relevant across disciplines and 91% across school types.

"Overall, 88% of participants reported that the content of INSIGHT was (very) helpful for them personally (M = 3.29, SD = .67). Similarly, 78% considered the content (very) useful for their future lesson planning and instructional design (M = 3.03, SD = .72)."
