## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The motivational QCM subdimension probability of success partially overlaps with the cognitive load measure, because both reflect perceived task difficulty. [→ Rey 2011](#rey-2011)

## Evidence

### Rey 2011

Günter Daniel Rey, Florian Buchwald. (2011). The Expertise Reversal Effect: Cognitive Load and Motivational Explanations. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0022243

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports this overlap as an empirical finding of the present experiment and explains it theoretically: QCM probability-of-success items depend on perceived task difficulty, and the adapted Paas (1992) scale has learners rate task difficulty, so "both motivational and CL measures partially reflect, at minimum, the learner's perceived task difficulty."

> "Furthermore, a partial overlap was found between the motivational subdimension, “probability of success,” and a cognitive load measure."
