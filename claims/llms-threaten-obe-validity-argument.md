`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Theoretical argument in the Discussion, not a tested result of this review; all included studies predate major LLMs. The authors argue AI can readily answer short-answer recall questions and that unproctored use raises fairness and validity concerns.

> "LLMs have the potential to inflate scores by enabling students to answer questions without a deep understanding of the subject."

### Mizori R 2025 (2)

Mizori R, Sadiq M, Ahmad MT, Siu A, Ahmad RR, Yang Z, et al. (2025). STEM exam performance: Open‐ versus closed‐book methods in the large language model era. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.13839

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Authors' interpretation citing external evidence (e.g. poor LLM performance on biomedical NLP tasks) that test design influences LLM impact. No data in this review tests this; the authors note LLM assistance will become more sophisticated over time.

> "Test questions may need to evolve from traditional formats to more complex, application‐based scenarios that LLMs struggle to navigate effectively."
