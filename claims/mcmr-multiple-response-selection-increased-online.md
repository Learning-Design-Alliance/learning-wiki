### Alexis Olsho 2020

Alexis Olsho, Suzanne White Brahmia, Charlotte Zimmerman, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). Online administration of a reasoning inventory in development. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the percentage of students selecting more than one response on MCMR items between in-person and online administrations. The article reports increases "by 9%, 22%, 9% and 16%" on the four items with significant difficulty decreases.

> "For items Q15, Q18, Q19, and Q20—the four MCMR items for which we saw signiﬁcant decreases in CTT item difﬁculty—the fraction of students who selected more than one answer choice increased by 9%, 22%, 9% and 16%, respectively, from the In-person to the Online administration."
