# Child gender predicts self-regulation trajectory membership, with girls more likely to be earlier developers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Montroy Janelle J. 2016

Montroy Janelle J., Bowles Ryan P., Skibbe Lori E., McClelland Megan M., Morrison Frederick J. (2016). The Development of Self-Regulation across Early Childhood. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000159

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression predicting trajectory class membership in the Michigan longitudinal sample, controlling for age, race/ethnicity, and primary language, found girls were "1.79 times more likely" to be preschool versus later developers (OR = 1.79). In the Oregon sample girls were 2.34 times more likely to be early versus intermediate developers (OR = 2.34); no other gender comparisons were significant.

> "Girls included in the Michigan longitudinal sample were 1.79 times more likely to be classified as preschool developers (following either an intermediate or early developers’ trajectory) versus later developers."
