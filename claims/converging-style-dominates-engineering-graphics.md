Goodridge, W. H., Lawanto, O., & Santoso, H. B. (2017). A Learning Style Comparison between Synchronous Online and Face-to-Face Engineering Graphics Instruction. International Education Studies, 10(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n2p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive statistics from Kolb's LSI 3.1 administered to 108 students in six sections showed Converging was the predominant style: 23 of 30 synchronous online students (77%) and 46 of 78 face-to-face students (59%). The article reports these as descriptive percentages with no effect size.

> "Findings revealed that most synchronous online students, 23 out of 30 students (77%), were categorized into Converging."
