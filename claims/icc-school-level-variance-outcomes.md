### Granvik Saminathen Maria; Plenty Stephanie; Modin Bitte 2020

Granvik Saminathen Maria; Plenty Stephanie; Modin Bitte. (2020). The Role of Academic Achievement in the Relationship between School Ethos and Adolescent Distress and Aggression: A Study of Ninth Grade Students in the Segregated School Landscape of Stockholm. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8116286/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ICC analysis preceding the ML-CFA, based on the analytic sample of 9776 students in 150 schools. The article prints the three ICC percentages quoted; these are variance proportions, not standardised effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The ICCs revealed that 23.2% of the variance in academic achievement was explained at the school-level. For psychological distress and aggressive behaviour, a lower proportion of variance was attributable to the school level, 2.4% and 6.4%, respectively. Thus, differences between schools were greater for aggressive behaviour than for psychological distress."
