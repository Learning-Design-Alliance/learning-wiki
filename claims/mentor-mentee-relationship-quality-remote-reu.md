### Dina Zohrabi Alaee and Benjamin M. Zwickl 2022

Dina Zohrabi Alaee and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2022). Toward a Longitudinal Multifaceted View of Remote Undergraduate Research Experiences During the COVID-19 Pandemic: The perspectives of doing research online. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2207.02733](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2207.02733)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the community node found mentor-mentee communication "developed gradually in the remote context"; five mentees described collaborative, independent, communicative mentor relationships as facilitating research progress, and five mentors used informal professional conversations to build relationships.

> "Several mentees (N=6) stated the importance of having a quality relationship with their mentors in addition to how their mentors provide various resources and guidance."
