Evidence¶

field-theme1¶

Harry Feng, Yuan Tian, and Erica Zhao. 2026. From Answer Generators to Reasoning Facilitators: Designing AI Tutors for Mathematical Reasoning in High-Stakes Environments. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01692

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative theme from a 12-day field deployment of AITutor with 12 junior-high students (10 interviews, 8 observations), triangulated with telemetry. The authors report that "checking the final answer first served as a critical orientation device" rather than mere shortcutting.

"While often stigmatized in education as shortcutting, checking the final answer first served as a critical orientation device."

field-theme1 (2)¶

Harry Feng, Yuan Tian, and Erica Zhao. 2026. From Answer Generators to Reasoning Facilitators: Designing AI Tutors for Mathematical Reasoning in High-Stakes Environments. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01692

q2 · i? · design · r2

Incident from the field study's joy/frustration table: participant P03 used the final answer to locate a calculation error in a correctly set-up function-graph problem, supporting self-diagnosis rather than copying.

"Lilly had already set up a function-graph problem correctly but made a calculation error. Seeing the final answer first helped her identify where her work went wrong (P03)."
