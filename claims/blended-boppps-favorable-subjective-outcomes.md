## Evidence

### Li Shuze 2024

Li Shuze, Wei Wei, Li Xiaofeng, Ma Li, Li Qiujuan, Sun Xiance, Chen Xin. (2024). Impacts of blended learning with BOPPPS model on Chinese medical undergraduate students: a comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis of 44 studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05917-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis pooling questionnaire surveys from 16 studies (2169 students) for autonomous learning ability and 4 studies (482 students) for learning interest, among other subjective outcomes. Pooled SMDs ranged from 0.524 to 0.851 across the eight abilities.

> "autonomous learning ability (SMD = 0.825; 95% CI = 0.610–1.041; I2 = 81.9%; P < 0.001), learning interest (SMD = 0.735; 95% CI = 0.350–1.119; I2 = 74.1%; P = 0.009)"
