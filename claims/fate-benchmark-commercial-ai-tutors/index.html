Evidence¶

Tahmid Al Hannan 2026¶

Tahmid Al Hannan, Diego Garcia, Alex Njoroge, Suha Al Juboori, Tarek Sakakini. (2026). Knowledge Distillation for Automated AI Tutor Evaluation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10647 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.10647

q2 · i? · design · r2

FATE evaluation of tutor responses generated by Claude Sonnet 4.6, ChatGPT 5.5 Instant, Gemini 2.5 Flash, and DeepSeek V4 Flash on BEA 2025 conversation histories (Table 3). Gemini's average accuracy was 82.88% versus Claude 74.00%.

"Gemini achieved the highest overall average ac- curacy, leading in Mistake Identification, Mistake Location, and Actionability, while DeepSeek per- formed best on Providing Guidance."

Tahmid Al Hannan 2026 (2)¶

Tahmid Al Hannan, Diego Garcia, Alex Njoroge, Suha Al Juboori, Tarek Sakakini. (2026). Knowledge Distillation for Automated AI Tutor Evaluation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10647

q2 · i? · design · r2

Same FATE benchmark of commercial tutors (Table 3): the article reports "Actionability remained the weakest category" for all models, with per-model actionability accuracies ranging from 46.50 (Claude) to 63.50 (Gemini).

"Across all models, Actionability remained the weakest category, indicating that providing concrete next-step guidance continues to be a challenge for current AI tutors."
