## Evidence

### Aldarsouni Fayez G. 2024

Aldarsouni Fayez G., Alkhulaiwi Hani, Alruwaite Hosam, Alsubaie Shara, Abdulkareem Faye, Alsubaie Norah. (2024). Advanced trauma life support training outcomes in Saudi Arabia: a four-year multicenter analysis of influential characteristics and factors (2019-2023). Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2024.394

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression analysis among physicians excluding interns (Table 5). Each additional year of age increased likelihood of success by a factor of 2.2 (P<.01); each pre-test point by 0.13 (P<.05); refresher attendance by 4.8 (P<.01).

> "The model identified three variables that were significantly predictive of success: age, pre-test scores, and attendance in the course as a refresher course."

### Aldarsouni Fayez G. 2024 (2)

Aldarsouni Fayez G., Alkhulaiwi Hani, Alruwaite Hosam, Alsubaie Shara, Abdulkareem Faye, Alsubaie Norah. (2024). Advanced trauma life support training outcomes in Saudi Arabia: a four-year multicenter analysis of influential characteristics and factors (2019-2023). Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2024.394

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (Table 6) controlling for confounders: refresher course OR=2.17 (P=.0276), general surgeons OR=3.26 (P=.0331), consultants of trauma-related specialties OR=6.98 (P=.0153).

> "In the multivariate analysis, which controlled for confounding variables, attending a refresher course remained a significant predictor of success (OR=2.17, 95% CI [1.09–4.321, P=.0276)."
