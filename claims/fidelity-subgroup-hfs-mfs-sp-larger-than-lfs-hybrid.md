## Evidence

### Kim Junghee 2016

Kim Junghee, Park Jin-Hwa, Shin Sujin. (2016). Effectiveness of simulation-based nursing education depending on fidelity: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0672-7

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of the 40-study meta-analysis comparing effect sizes by fidelity level as coded from the physical equipment used. The results section reports HFS ES = 0.86, MFS ES = 1.03, and SP ES = 0.86, stating "HFSs (0.86), MFSs (1.03), and SPs (0.86) had large effect sizes" while low-fidelity (0.35) and hybrid (0.34) studies had smaller ones.

> "Studies using HFSs (0.86), MFSs (1.03), and SPs (0.86) had large effect sizes, whereas low-fidelity (0.35) and hybrid (0.34) simulation studies had smaller effect sizes."
