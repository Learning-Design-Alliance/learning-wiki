### Seth Poulsen 2023

Seth Poulsen, Yael Gertner, Benjamin Cosman, Matthew West, and Geoffrey L. Herman. (2023). Efficiency of Learning from Proof Blocks Versus Writing Proofs. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1145/3545945.3569797](https://doi.org/10.1145/3545945.3569797)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Conditional and robustness analyses in the same randomized controlled trial: familiarity subgroups (never heard, n = 96; somewhat familiar, n = 214), each rubric section, and each of the two test questions separately. The article reports "All analyses showed exactly the same results"; the very-familiar subgroup (n = 22) was not re-analyzed as too small.

> "All analyses showed exactly the same results, indicating that the learning happened roughly equally across all parts of the rubric, across all three experimental groups"
