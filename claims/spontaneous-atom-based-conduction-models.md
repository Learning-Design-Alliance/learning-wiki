## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (2002). Investigating student understanding of quantum physics: Spontaneous models of conductivity. American Journal of Physics 70:3, 218-226. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.1119/1.1447542](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1447542)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Demonstration interviews with thirteen Physics 420 students (nine pre-instruction, four post-instruction) on current flow through various materials. The article reports that seven students "described variations on the idea" that the battery pulled bound electrons off atoms, an atom-based rather than free-electron view.

> "Of the thirteen students, seven (including post-instruction students) described variations on the idea that the “energy” (or “power” or “voltage”) of the battery pulled the bound electrons off the atoms, allowing them to move through a wire."
