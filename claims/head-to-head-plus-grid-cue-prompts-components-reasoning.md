# A question combining a head-to-head arrangement with a grid prompted two students to discuss vector components and consider changing methods

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Question 8, the first question with both a head-to-head arrangement and a grid, prompted Student F and Student H to discuss components of the vectors, each mentioning only one component direction, and prompted Student G to attempt switching methods on gridded questions. [→ Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009](#jeffrey-m-hawkins-2009)

## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, and Michael C. Wittmann. (2009). Students’ Consistency of Graphical Vector Addition Method on 2-D Vector Addition Tasks. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings (venue not printed in text). [doi:10.1063/1.3266704](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266704)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview observation across students on Question 8. Student F discussed only horizontal components; Student H discussed only vertical components, describing the resultant as "up one square" using components reasoning before returning to head-to-tail. The authors flag the cue combination as needing further investigation.

> "Question 8 was the first question presented to these students that had both a head-to-head arrangement of vectors and a grid. There may be something about the cues or cue combinations in Question 8 that causes students to consider changing methods on this particular question"
