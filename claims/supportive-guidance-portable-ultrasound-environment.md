`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Framework analysis of reflective narratives in the mixed-methods portfolio study found students "generally valued supportive guidance" from faculty; some students reported lack of direct preceptor feedback. Portable device feedback was mixed: some cited low resolution and touch-screen inconvenience, others found it functional.

> "Regarding interactions with faculty, students generally valued supportive guidance. It was noted that "the instructor's encouragement and kind explanations, despite my lack of skill, helped me gain confidence in ultrasound""
