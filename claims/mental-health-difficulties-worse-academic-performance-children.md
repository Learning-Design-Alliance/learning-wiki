## Evidence

### Muntaner-Mas Adrià 2025

Muntaner-Mas Adrià, Valenzuela Pedro L., Pinto-Escalona Tania, Erickson Kirk I., Martínez-de-Quel Óscar. (2025). Mental health mediates the association between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic performance in European schoolchildren. Jornal de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2024.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional linear regression analysis in 507 schoolchildren (51.5% girls, 7.4 ± 0.4 years) from 20 schools in five European countries, adjusted for sex and socioeconomic status. Mental health was measured with the parent-rated SDQ; academic performance by 10-point teacher grades. The study reports "β ranging from −0.121 to −0.325, p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size beyond these regression coefficients is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Specifically, mental health difficulties were associated with poorer academic performance indicators (β ranging from −0.121 to −0.325, p < 0.05) and lower CRF (β ranging from −0.121 to −0.189, p < 0.05), except for conduct problems."
