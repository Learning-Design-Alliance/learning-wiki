## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Özlem Salehi 2022

Özlem Salehi, Zeki Seskir, and Ilknur Tepe. (2022). A Computer Science-Oriented Approach to Introduce Quantum Computing to a New Audience. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1109/te.2021.3078552](https://doi.org/10.1109/te.2021.3078552)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre/post-test design over 22 two/three-day workshops; 317 participants filled both tests out of 430 completers. Paired t-test compared pre-test (M = 32.32) and post-test (M = 71.08) scores, with "ngain score is calculated as 0.56" and "d = 1.68" reported as large.

> "Paired t-test is used in order to compare the pre-test (M = 32.32, SD = 25.03) and post-test (M = 71.08, SD = 20.83) scores. The results indicate a significant difference in the gain scores of the participants. ngain score is calculated as 0.56, suggesting a medium gain. The effect size for the analysis (d = 1.68) was found to be large according to Cohen's convention."
