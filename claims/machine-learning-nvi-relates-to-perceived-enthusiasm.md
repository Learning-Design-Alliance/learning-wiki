# Machine-learning classified teacher nonverbal immediacy during a lesson is positively related to student-perceived enthusiastic teaching behaviours

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Teachers' NVI (Time 2) was positively related to class-level enthusiastic teaching behaviours reported after the lesson, in both models and in the two-lesson robustness check. [→ Lazarides 2025](#lazarides-2025)

## Evidence

### Lazarides 2025

Lazarides, R., Frenkel, J., Petković, U., Göllner, R., & Hellwich, O. (2025). 'No words'—Machine‐learning classified nonverbal immediacy and its role in connecting teacher self‐efficacy with perceived teaching and student interest. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S15–S31. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12732

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Robustness check using only the first two videorecorded lessons, reported in supplemental Tables S.3 and S.4. The article reports the relation as 'positively and significantly related'; in the main models the standardized coefficients were β = .42 (p = .007) in Model 1 and β = .45 (p = .001) in Model 2.

> "These additional analyses can be seen as a robustness check and reveal that machine‐learning classified NVI was in Model 1 and 2 positively and significantly related to student‐perceived enthusiastic teaching behaviours."
