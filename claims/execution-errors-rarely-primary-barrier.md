## Evidence

### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exam-solution coding of scalar potential questions found 60% of mathematical errors were fundamental rather than small, but 71% of these followed earlier activation or construction difficulties. This is "consistent with the ﬁndings of our prior work that suggested diﬃculty performing integrals was rarely the primary barrier."

> "just over half of the students who made mathematical errors made more fundamental errors (60%, N = 28 of 47); however, for roughly three quarters of these students (71%, N = 20 of 28), the mistake made in the execution stage was preceded by a signiﬁcant diﬃ- culty in the activation or construction components."
