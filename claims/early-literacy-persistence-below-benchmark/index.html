Evidence¶

Lemke 2023¶

Lemke, M., Murphy, D., Soo Ping Chow, A., Spencer, H., & Zhang, A. (2023). Opportunity Gaps: Findings From Initial Analysis of Student Performance in Early Literacy in Massachusetts. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638519

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of within-year benchmark trajectories in 2020/21 and 2021/22 district screening data. The brief reports that group-specific persistence percentages "range from 67 percent to 80 percent" for the historically marginalized groups, with Asian students less likely to stay below benchmark.

"Across grade levels and years, 60 percent of students below benchmark at the beginning of the year also did not meet the benchmark at the end of the year."
