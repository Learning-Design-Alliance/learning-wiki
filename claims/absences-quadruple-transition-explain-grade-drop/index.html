Stevens 2014¶

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The Focus on Freshmen Study, following about 22,000 students from eighth grade (spring 2008) through ninth grade (2008-09), documented the transition decline. The brief reports "Students’ unexcused absences quadruple" and that this explains most of the drop in grades and pass rates.

"Students’ unexcused absences quadruple when they move from eighth to ninth grade, and this explains the vast majority of the large drop in grades and pass rates."

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The same district-wide record analysis found the effort and grade decline was universal across subgroups: "in every racial/ethnic group," among high- and low-achieving students, both genders, all subjects, and high- and low-performing schools. The brief argues this pattern indicates the school context plays a large role.

"This decline is observed among high- and low-achieving students, among boys and girls, across all subjects, in high-per -forming schools and low-performing schools, and in every racial/ethnic group."
