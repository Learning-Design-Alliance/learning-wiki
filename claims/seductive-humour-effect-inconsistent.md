## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Study 1, the irrelevant-humour group scored worse than the control group on transfer (seductive details effect), but in Study 2 there was no significant difference from control on recall or transfer. [→ Bender 2026](#bender-2026)

## Evidence

### Bender 2026

Bender, L., Renkl, A., & Endres, T. (2026). Punchline with(out) purpose: Integrating research on instructional humour and seductive details. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-study comparison reported in the abstract and general discussion. Study 1 (n = 115 adults) showed the seductive details effect on transfer; Study 2 (n = 151 youths) showed moderate Bayesian evidence favouring the null (BF01 = 3.45 recall; BF01 = 3.59 transfer).

> "The irrelevant-humour group scored worse than the control group in study 1 (i.e., seductive details effect), but not in study 2."
