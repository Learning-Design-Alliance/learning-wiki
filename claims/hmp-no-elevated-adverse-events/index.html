Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022¶

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Exploratory logistic regression models with preregistered covariates on N=662, categorizing symptoms by normative T-score cutoffs. Depressive symptoms showed the same pattern (OR=0.65 at T5; OR=0.82 at T6, p=.278).

"Assignment to the HMP predicted significantly lower odds of experiencing increased anxiety symptoms at T5 (Odds ratio (OR)=0.42 [0.30 to 0.60], p<.001) with no difference in the odds of a categorical symptom increase in symptoms at T6 (OR=0.78 [0.56 to 1.10], p=.143)"
