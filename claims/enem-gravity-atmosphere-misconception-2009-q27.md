# On ENEM 2009_Q27, most completers chose a distractor reflecting the misconception that gravity requires an atmosphere, and the item showed slightly negative discrimination

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Distractor A, asserting weight is small because gravity does not act in space, was chosen by 42% of completers overall and peaked at 60% around a score of 660. [→ Barroso 2018](#barroso-2018)
`q2 i?` Item 2009_Q27 had slightly negative discrimination: higher-aptitude students were less likely to answer correctly. [→ Barroso 2018](#barroso-2018)

## Evidence

### Barroso 2018

Barroso, M. F., Rubini, G., & Silva, T. (2018). Dificuldades na aprendizagem de Física sob a ótica dos resultados do Enem. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Distractor-choice analysis of ENEM 2009 question 27 (astronaut/Hubble item, correct answer D at about 14%). The article reports "a discriminação do item é ligeiramente negativa" and concludes completers associate gravity's existence with the existence of an atmosphere.

> "o distrator (A), escolhido por 42 % do total dos concluintes do ensino médio , é a mais marcada na faixa de escores de 260 a 760 pontos, com um pico em 660 pontos – quando é escolhida por 60% dos concluintes."
