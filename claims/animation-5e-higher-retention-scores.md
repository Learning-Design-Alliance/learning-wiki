## Evidence

### Özcan and Kiliç 2017

Mehmet Fatih Özcan, Latife Kirbaşoğlu Kiliç. (2017). The Effects of Animation Supported 5E Model on Teaching 'Indicative and Subjunctive Moods' in 7th Grade Turkish Lesson. Universal Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051407

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention test applied to both groups five weeks after the post-test in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports the experimental group's retention scores were significantly higher, attributing greater permanence to the animation-supported 5E method; no test statistic or effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "it was determined that the retention scores of the experimental group students were significantly higher than those of the control group students. In other words, the 5E method supported with animations used in the experimental group became more effective in ensuring the permanence of the information"
