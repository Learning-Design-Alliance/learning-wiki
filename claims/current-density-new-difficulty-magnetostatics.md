## Evidence

### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exam-solution coding of the vector potential question requiring students to express volume current density found 90% of C2 difficulties involved current density, with 63% leaving J without transitioning to I. The authors interpret this as "a totally new diﬃculty" because JdV′ lacks a clear physical interpretation unlike dm.

> "diﬃculties around the current density represented the majority of the diﬃculties with element C2 (90%, N = 27 of 30 solutions exhibiting diﬃ- culties with C2). This diﬃculty most often (63%, N = 17 of 27) manifested as students simply leaving J in their ﬁnal expression without transitioning to the current I carried by the wire segment"
