Kruskal-Wallis analysis of the quasi-experimental three-group pre/post design (15 students per group) at the immediate post-test; the comparison across the self-monitoring, seeking-help and traditional groups was "significant (sig<0.05)". Chi-square was 14.81, df 2 (Table 4).
