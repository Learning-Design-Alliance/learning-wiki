Kahn P 2026¶

Kahn P, Carrigan M, Wyman I, Liu R, Smith P, Andonegui AR, Murtagh L and Song F (2026) Still emerging: understanding Generative AI use in Higher Education. Front. Educ. 11:1885253. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885253

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey comparison using the validated Perception of Students' Use of GAI scale (staff) and Use of GAI for Academic Purposes scale (students). The article reports means of 5.23 versus 3.11 at p = 0.000, highlighting "the limited knowledge that staff possess in relation to student use of GAI tools."

"Students perceived that other students use GAI for academic purposes to a significantly lesser extent than staff members perceive students use GAI (see Figure 4). The mean for this scale for staff was 5.23, compared to 3.11 for students (with a t-test confirmed at p = 0.000)."
