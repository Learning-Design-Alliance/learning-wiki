Evidence¶

Fery Muhamad Firdaus 2017¶

Fery Muhamad Firdaus, Wahyudin, Tatang Herman. (2017). Improving primary students' mathematical literacy through problem based learning and direct instruction. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.3072

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA (Table 5): Model source F = 333.039, Sig. 0.000, H0 rejected; the article reports the model effect as significant.

"There was a significant difference in students' mathematical literacy improvement due to differences in teaching model given."

Fery Muhamad Firdaus 2017 (2)¶

Fery Muhamad Firdaus, Wahyudin, Tatang Herman. (2017). Improving primary students' mathematical literacy through problem based learning and direct instruction. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.3072

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA (Table 5): School located F = 2.961, Sig. 0.054; Interaction F = 2.021, Sig. 0.135; both H0 accepted, so no significant location or interaction effect.

"there was no interaction effect between the teaching model with school location factors to the increase in students' mathematical literacy."
