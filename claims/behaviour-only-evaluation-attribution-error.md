### Ginsburg S 2014

Ginsburg S. (2014). Duty hours as viewed through a professionalism lens. BMC Medical Education 14(Suppl 1):S15. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S15

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is the review's theoretical argument, drawing on social psychology, that evaluations ignoring context misattribute behaviour to traits. The review states that "most of the behaviours we observe are shaped by the situation", citing meta-analytic work on when attitudes predict behaviour. No effect size is printed.

> "Thus, if we focus only on behaviour without taking context into account, we risk making what social psychologists refer to as the fundamental attribution error: that is, we underestimate the influence of the situation and greatly overestimate the influence of the person."
