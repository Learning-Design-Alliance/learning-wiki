# Comparison target, sample size, and publication year do not significantly moderate the BFLPE

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither whether class-average or school-average achievement is used, nor sample size, nor publication year significantly changes the size of the BFLPE. [→ Fang J 2018](#fang-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Fang J 2018

Fang J, Huang X, Zhang M, Huang F, Li Z and Yuan Q (2018) The Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect on Academic Self-Concept: A Meta-Analysis. Front. Psychol. 9:1569. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01569

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic moderator tests across the 56 effect sizes found no significant moderation by comparison target (Q = 0.01, df = 1, p > 0.05), sample size (Q = 0.00, df = 1, p > 0.05), or publication year (Q = 0.35, df = 1, p > 0.05). No effect sizes were printed for these null results.

> "There was no signiﬁcant inﬂuence of comparison target:Q = 0.01 (df = 1, p > 0.05), which meant that whether the study takes class-average achievement or school-average achiev ement as comparison target has little inﬂuence on the size of BFLPE."
