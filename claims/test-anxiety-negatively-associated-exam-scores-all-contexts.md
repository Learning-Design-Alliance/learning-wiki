Salehi S., Berk S. A., Brunelli R., Cotner S., Creech C., Drake A. G., Fagbodun S., Hall C., Hebert S., Hewlett J., James A. C., Shuster M., St. Juliana J. R., Stovall D. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling on the survey subsample found test anxiety "universally negatively associated with exam scores"; coefficients printed for each institution type, strongest at selective institutions (β = −0.33). No standardized effect size beyond these path coefficients is printed.

> "In this model, test anxiety was universally negatively associated with exam scores (βCC = −0.27 ± 0.02, p < 0.001; βinclusive = −0.13 ± 0.03, p = 0.007; βselective = −0.33 ± 0.02, p < 0.001)."
