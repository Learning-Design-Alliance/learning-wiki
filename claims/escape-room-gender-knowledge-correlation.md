### Baessler F 2025

Baessler F, Hornung TP, Schuster TJ, Hardt N, Felter E, Anzenberger E, Ely DR, Lichtenstein T, Steiner K, Koelkebeck K, Flasbeck V, Spitzer P, Speerforck S, Pinilla S, Khatamzas E, Mueller B, Zafar A. `q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlational analyses in the study of 45 medical students (82.2% female) linked gender to test scores; the reported means were 61.63 (male) vs 65.81 (female) pre-test and 67.00 (male) vs 72.73 (female) post-test, showing "Female participants scored higher than male students".

> "Female participants scored higher than male students in the pre-test (r =.306; p =.041, 95% CI [0.014, 0.550]) as well as in the post-test (r =.4404, p =.0024, 95% CI [0.169, 0.650])."
