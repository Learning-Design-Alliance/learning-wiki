Narrative synthesis of the review's own included studies: of 11 studies included after screening 1,800 records, most learner-facing studies reported favorable responses. The review states favorable responses were "expressed through satisfaction, confidence, perceived safety, motivation, self-efficacy and willingness to engage in repeated practice." No pooled effect estimate was computed.
