## Evidence

### Newman 2016

Newman, D., Lazarev, V., & Zacamy, J. (2016). Comparing a Program Implemented Under the Constraints of an RCT and in the Wild. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567633

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of 11 RCT treatment schools and 31 Scale-up schools in one state using NCES demographic data and t-tests; Table 1 reports e.g. %FRPL 34.54% vs 31.79% and average teacher experience 10.09 vs 10.90 years, none significant at 0.05.

> "We did not find any differences in school characteristics significant at 0.05 level. Comparative descriptive statistics for RCT and Scale-up schools is presented in Table 1 in Appendix B."
