### Dewi Sari Puspa 2024

Dewi Sari Puspa, Amanda Wilson, Robbert Duvivier, Brian Kelly, Conor Gilligan. (2024). Do the teaching, practice and assessment of clinical communication skills align? BMC Medical Education 24:609. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05596-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Component-level analysis of variance across the same 28-student observational dataset found only two CCOG tasks differed significantly across settings: "providing structure and closing the session (p = 0.005 and p = 0.02, respectively)". No effect sizes were printed.

> "Further analysis showed that the performances in the separate components were not statistically significantly different across settings, except for providing structure and closing the session (p = 0.005 and p = 0.02, respectively)."
