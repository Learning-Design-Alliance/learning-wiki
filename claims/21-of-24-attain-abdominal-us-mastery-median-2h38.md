`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-centre simulation training study of 24 third-year medical students with no previous ultrasound experience, trained independently on a ScanTrainer VR simulator with only automated feedback until the mastery level was "achiev[ed]... twice". Medians reported: "2h38min" and "seven attempts" (range 3–11).
