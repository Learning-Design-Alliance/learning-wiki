## Evidence

### García Sanjuan 2018

García Sanjuan, F.; El Jurdi, S.; Jaén Martínez, FJ.; Nácher-Soler, VE. (2018). Evaluating a tactile and a tangible multi-tablet gamified quiz system for collaborative learning in primary education. Computers & Education. 123:65-84. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.04.011

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Two independent observers rated nine collaboration dimensions on a 4-point Likert scale. Krippendorff's α exceeded 0.8 for most dimensions, though sustaining mutual understanding and dialogue management showed α = 0 due to reduced intra-coder variability.

> "the analysis of this figure leads us to accept H0 C2, and H0 C4 to H0 C9, since there was, on average, good collaboration quality in terms of the corresponding dimensions (scores > 0)"
