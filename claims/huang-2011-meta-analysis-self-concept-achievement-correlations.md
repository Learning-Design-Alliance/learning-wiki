## Evidence

### Hübner 2023

Hübner, N., Wagner, W., Zitzmann, S., & Nagengast, B. (2023). How Strong Is the Evidence for a Causal Reciprocal Effect? Contrasting Traditional and New Methods to Investigate the Reciprocal Effects Model of Self-Concept and Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09724-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

The review reports Huang's meta-analysis of "data from 39 independent samples", which found "average correlations ranging from r = .20 to .27" in one direction and r = .19 to .25 in the other, interpreted as reciprocal relationships.

> "The study reported average correlations ranging from r = .20 to .27 between prior self-concept and later achievement and correlations ranging from r = .19 to .25 between prior achievement and subsequent self-concept, all of which were interpreted as evidence of reciprocal relationships."
