## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Despite a 20% response rate, the within-quiz co-presence of reinforced and unreinforced questions limited selection bias, though the benefit may have been underestimated if readers and quiz-takers differed. [→ Matos Jason 2017](#matos-jason-2017)

## Evidence

### Matos Jason 2017

Matos Jason, Petri Camille R., Mukamal Kenneth J., Vanka Anita. (2017). Spaced education to improve competency in medical knowledge. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181418

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' limitation analysis of the randomized trial, in which an average of 31 house officers responded per quiz (20% response rate). They state the paired-question design "substantially minimizes the bias that a low response rate might otherwise produce".

> "However, the fact that each quiz included both intervention and control questions substantially minimizes the bias that a low response rate might otherwise produce. In addition, we had no means of determining who read the weekly or monthly Pearls."
