Evidence¶

Supandi 2018¶

Supandi, S., Waluya, B., Rochmad, R., Suyitno, H., & Dewi, K. (2018). Think-Talk-Write Model for Improving Students' Abilities in Mathematical Representation. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 77-90. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1136a

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Simple-effect Scheffe tests in the 2x3 quasi-experiment rejected all three strategy comparisons within self-efficacy levels (Fcount 23.06, 103.95, 186.33). The low self-efficacy contrast shows the largest gap: "53.00 < (A2 B3) = 72.73". No effect size is printed.

"The third comparison, (A1 B3) = 53.00 < (A2 B3) = 72.73, shows that the students with low self-efficacy who applied the think -talk-write strategy have an average proficiency score that is greater than that for the students who employed the expository strategy."
