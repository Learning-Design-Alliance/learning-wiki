## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 82 anaesthesiologists at one university hospital in 2016, comparing ventilator-setting answers of a conceptual group (73 respondents reporting LPV knowledge) versus a non-conceptual group (8). Median VT/kgIBW differed in "laparoscopic surgery" (p = 0.033).

> "Among cases of laparoscopic surgery, the median VT/kgIBW in the conceptual group was lower than that in the non-conceptual group, at 8.2 (8.0–9.2 [7.1–10.3]) vs. 9.2 (9.1–10.1 [7.6–10.1]) mL/kg; (p = 0.033)."

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018 (2)

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same survey: in non-laparoscopic surgery cases there was "no significant difference" in median VT/kgIBW between groups (p = 0.533); equivalence was not tested.

> "In non-laparoscopic surgery cases, there was no significant difference between the conceptual and non-conceptual groups in terms of median VT/kgIBW, at 6.9 (6.3–7.6 [5.9–8.6]) vs. 6.8 (6.1–7.6 [6.0–7.6]) mL/kg (p = 0.533)"
