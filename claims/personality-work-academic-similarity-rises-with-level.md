## Evidence

### Poropat 2009

Poropat, A. E. (2009). A Meta-Analysis of the Five-Factor Model of Personality and Academic Performance. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014996

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Comparison of this meta-analysis's scale-corrected correlations with Barrick et al.'s (2001) work-performance correlations using Westen and Rosenthal's alerting and contrast indexes; agreement "increased from primary" through secondary to tertiary levels, with significance calculated from the average aggregate sample sizes in Tables 1 and 2.

> "Interestingly, the degree of agreement between the correlations reported here and those reported by Barrick et al. (2001) increased from primary (ralerting-CV = .37; rcontrast-CV = .04; p \< .05) to secondary (ralerting-CV = .60; rcontrast-CV = .11; p \< .001) and tertiary levels of education (ralerting-CV = .15; rcontrast-CV = .79; p \< .001)."
