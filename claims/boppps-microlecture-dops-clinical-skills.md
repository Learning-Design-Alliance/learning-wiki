# The BOPPPS-based micro-lecture group outperformed controls on eight of eleven DOPS clinical skills indicators (p < 0.005), with preoperative preparation at the significance threshold and no differences on informed consent or patient communication

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the DOPS joint-puncture assessment, the experimental group scored significantly higher on eight indicators including clinical operation skills, aseptic technique, and overall performance of clinical operations (p < 0.005 Bonferroni corrected); preoperative preparation was marginal (p = 0.005) and two communication-related indicators showed no significant difference. ## Evidence

### Yang Xu 2026

Yang Xu, Liu Tianyang, Li Ying, Gao Fuqiang. (2026). A BOPPPS-based micro-lecture teaching intervention for orthopaedic postgraduates in China. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2616194

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical skills were assessed by a complete joint puncture on a manikin, scored on a unified 8-point DOPS scale by two preceptors who did not teach. The article reports the experimental group "outperformed CSs" on all eight items (p < 0.005); Table 3 prints e.g. clinical operation skills 6.63 ± 0.92 vs 5.29 ± 0.75 (F = 30.118, p = 0.000).

> "The results indicate that ESs outperformed CSs in familiarity with anatomy and indications, appropriate analgesia or sedation anaesthesia, clinical operation skills, seeking help when needed, post-operative management, aseptic techniques, consideration of patient feelings, and overall performance of clinical operations (all eight items) (p < 0.005)."
