## Evidence

### Matthew T. McCrudden 2011

Matthew T. McCrudden. (2011). Do specific relevance instructions promote transfer appropriate processing? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9158-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized experiment with 52 undergraduates assigned to what-question, why-question, or control pre-reading conditions; cued recall showed the similarity advantage the abstract reports: "participants in the experimental conditions did better on questions that had greater similarity to relevance instructions".
