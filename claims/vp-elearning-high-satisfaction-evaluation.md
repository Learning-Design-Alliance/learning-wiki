## Evidence

### Schulz Marco S. 2025

Schulz Marco S., Pantelmann Ragna, Hillebrecht Anna-Lena, Reissmann Daniel R. (2025). Bridging the gap to clinical practice: a concept for virtual patients in preclinical education in prosthetic dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08097-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation questionnaire completed by the 24 users at follow-up, using 6-point Likert items on functionality and satisfaction adapted from comparable studies. Highest mean was 5.3 for expansion and recommendation; technical support reached the lowest mean with 3.9.

> "The total mean score was 4.9 and the overall agreement 75.0%."

### Schulz Marco S. 2025 (2)

Schulz Marco S., Pantelmann Ragna, Hillebrecht Anna-Lena, Reissmann Daniel R. (2025). Bridging the gap to clinical practice: a concept for virtual patients in preclinical education in prosthetic dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08097-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scope items answered on a 5-point Likert scale by the 24 users, where positive disagreement meant items were rated (rather) too high, long, detailed or difficult. No negative disagreement was recorded in the total row.

> "The overall agreement was 87.5% with a positive disagreement of 12.5%."
