Same in silico study; each model's self-reported confidence (0%–100%) recorded for all 123 questions and compared using the Friedman test with post hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and Bonferroni adjustment.

> "Confidence scores also differed significantly among the models (Friedman test, χ2(2)=213.40, P < 0.001, Kendall's W=0.87), indicating a large effect size. Gemini showed the highest confidence scores (99.51 ± 1.49), followed by ChatGPT (90.88 ± 9.19), whereas Claude showed the lowest confidence scores (80.22 ± 11.10)"
