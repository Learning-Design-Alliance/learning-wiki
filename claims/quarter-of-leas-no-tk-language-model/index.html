Evidence¶

Wang 2024¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey results on TK classroom language models (Figure 2); respondents could select multiple options. The report states more than one fourth (27%) of LEAs did not provide any classroom language model to support multilingual students.

"fifty-seven percent of LEAs provided English-only instruction with home language support, 10% offered dual language programs only, 5% offered a combination of language models, and 1% offered a home language program only."

Wang 2024 (2)¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey finding on the composition of LEAs offering no language model. The report frames these data as indicating "a need for support for serving multilingual learners," noting 59% of California children under 5 are multilingual learners.

"On average, these LEAs have an English learner enrollment of 15%, lower than the state average; however, 38% of them have student populations in which one third or more of the students are English learners."
