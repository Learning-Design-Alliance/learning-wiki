## Evidence

### Nilsson Annika 2015

Nilsson Annika, Engström Maria. (2015). E-assessment and an e-training program among elderly care staff lacking formal competence: results of a mixed-methods intervention study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0843-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons in the intervention study; the intervention group's job satisfaction factor scores for personal development and position in the group rose from 2010 to 2011, reaching significance at "p = 0.031" and p = 0.048 respectively. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Staff in the intervention group also rated their job satisfaction as better over time for the factors "personal development" (2010 Mean = 65.6, SD = 13.0 vs. 2011 Mean = 71.6, SD = 10.3; p = 0.031) and "position in the group" (2010 Mean = 56.0, SD = 13.1 vs. 2011 Mean = 59.5, SD = 13.2; p = 0.048)."
