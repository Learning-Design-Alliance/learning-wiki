## Evidence

### Wang X 2021

Wang X, Welch V, Li M, Yao L, Littell J, Li H, Yang N, Wang J, Shamseer L, Chen Y, Yang K, Grimshaw JM. (2021). The methodological and reporting characteristics of Campbell reviews: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1134

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Item-level AMSTAR 2 assessment across the 96 reviews found the funding-sources and ROB-impact items "only achieved in 15% and 24% of the reviews"; the authors interpret this as these methodological issues not receiving enough attention before. Descriptive percentages only.

> "AMSTAR 2.0 includes two new items (funding sources of included studies and impact of ROB in individual studies); these were only achieved in 15% and 24% of the reviews, a relatively low proportion compared with the other items."
