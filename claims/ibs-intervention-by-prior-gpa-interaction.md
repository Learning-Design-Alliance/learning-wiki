## Evidence

### Ranellucci 2017

Ranellucci, J., Hall, N., Muis, K., Lajoie, S., & Robinson, K. (2017). Mastery, Maladaptive Learning Behaviour, and Academic Achievement: An Intervention Approach. Canadian Journal of Education, 40(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1166656

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Step 2 interaction term) on Time 2 interest-based studying in the randomized pre-post study. Low-achieving students reported less interest-based studying in the intervention group (M = 2.68) than controls (M = 3.20); high-achieving students reported more (M = 3.57 vs. M = 3.29).
