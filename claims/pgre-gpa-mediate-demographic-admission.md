# The physics GRE partially mediates the relationships between gender and admission and between race and admission, and GPA partially mediates the race–admission relationship but not the gender–admission relationship

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The physics GRE partially mediates both the gender–admission and race–admission relationships, and GPA partially mediates the race–admission relationship but not the gender–admission relationship. ## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). The Physics GRE does not help applicants “stand out”. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis with gender and race as binary independent variables and the physics GRE as mediator of admission status. The article finds the physics GRE score "partially mediates the relationship between both gender and admission" and race and admission.

> "We ﬁnd that the applicant’s physics GRE score partially mediates the relationship between both gender and admission and race and admission."

### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020 (2)

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). The Physics GRE does not help applicants “stand out”. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis with race as the binary independent variable and GPA as mediator of admission status. The article finds "GPA partially mediates the relationship between race and admission" but finds no such mediation for gender.

> "We also ﬁnd that GPA partially mediates the relationship between race and admission but not gender and admission."
