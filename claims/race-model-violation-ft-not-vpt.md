## Evidence

### Marion Décaillet 2024

Marion Décaillet, Solange Denervaud, Cléo Huguenin-Virchaux, Laureline Besuchet, Céline J. Fischer Fumeaux, Micah M. Murray & Juliane Schneider. (2024). The impact of premature birth on auditory-visual processes in very preterm schoolchildren. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00257-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Race model inequality analysis of cumulative probability distributions in the VPT group (N = 28). There was "slight violation" at several quantiles, but "in no case" did multisensory cumulative probability significantly exceed model predictions (paired one-tailed t-tests, Table 4).

> "There was slight violation of the Race Model inequality in VPT children for the 0, 10, 15, 25, 30, and 35 quantiles (Fig. 2). However, in no case was the cumulative probability for the multisensory condition signi ﬁcantly higher than the cumulative prob-ability for the values predicted by the race model"

### Marion Décaillet 2024 (2)

Marion Décaillet, Solange Denervaud, Cléo Huguenin-Virchaux, Laureline Besuchet, Céline J. Fischer Fumeaux, Micah M. Murray & Juliane Schneider. (2024). The impact of premature birth on auditory-visual processes in very preterm schoolchildren. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00257-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Race model inequality analysis in the FT group (N = 23): the race model was "significantly violated" across quantiles 0–40%, with multisensory cumulative probability significantly exceeding predictions, favouring co-activation. The article's own text says "these eight quantiles" while listing nine, an internal inconsistency.

> "By contrast, the race model was signi ﬁcantly violated in FT children for the 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, and 40 quantiles. The cumulative probability for the multisensory condition was signi ﬁcantly higher than the cumu-lative probability for the values predicted by the race model for these eight quantiles"
