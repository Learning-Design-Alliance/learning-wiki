## Evidence

### Gerdes 2016

Gerdes, A., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & van Binsbergen, L. T. (2016). Ask-Elle: an Adaptable Programming Tutor for Haskell Giving Automated Feedback. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0080-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Log-file analysis of Ask-Elle usage in a second-year university class at Utrecht University (September 2013), with 3466 diagnosis interactions from 116 of 285 registered students across 26 exercises. "Currently, 56.1% of the interactions (and 46.3% of the attempts) are recognised to be correct." The authors note this ratio is a lower bound due to possible undetected incorrect solutions in the Tests passed and Discarded classes.
