### Martin E. Horn 2003

Martin E. Horn, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2003). Problems When Studying Holography. German Physical Society Spring Conference Dresden 2000. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0310132](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0310132)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Same questionnaire study of 166 Berlin comprehensive-school students: approx. 22 % related hologram to movie scenes; descriptions characterized the alleged holographic picture as "a handling personality" and sometimes assigned material properties to figures.

> "Almost twice as many of the questioned students (approx. 22 %) related the term hologram to scenes in a movie. These alleged holograms induce a series of problematic perceptions. In addition to creating something from nothing via the computer (see Figure 3), the most noticeable student descriptions characterize the alleged holographic picture as a handling personality."
