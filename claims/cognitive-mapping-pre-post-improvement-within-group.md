## Evidence

### Wu Bian 2016

Wu Bian, Wang Minhong, Grotzer Tina A., Liu Jun, Johnson Janice M. (2016). Visualizing complex processes using a cognitive-mapping tool to support the learning of clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0734-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-tests comparing learning products for first versus last case (n = 26 per group). Experimental group: overall paired difference 0.16, Cohen's d = 0.82; improvement in both reasoning-process and knowledge-construction dimensions. Control group: "no significant difference between the first and last cases in any aspect" (e.g., overall p = .583).

> "learners improved from the first to the last case in several aspects involving data capture, concept identification, concept relationships, and overall performance (paired difference = 0.16, t = 2.61, df = 25, p < 0.05)"
