## Evidence

### Wei Yaru 2025

Wei Yaru, Peng Zhengjun. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom model based on Bloom’s Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in endodontics education for undergraduate dental students. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison (10-point scale, 100% response rate in both groups of 16) analyzed with t-tests in SPSS 26.0. The flipped group's "self-rating of theoretical knowledge" was higher, t = 2.912, p < 0.006; no effect size printed.

> "the self-rating of theoretical knowledge reported by students in the flipped classroom cohort increased by 10.9%, from 7.1 ± 0.8 to 7.9 ± 0.7 (t = 2.912, p < 0.006)"
