## Evidence

### Curiosity Corner 2009

Curiosity Corner. (2009). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.successforall.org/early/early_curiosity.htm

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (PCER Consortium, 2008) with 18 preschools; print knowledge assessed with TERA-3, WJ III Letter-Word Identification, and WJ III Spelling subtests. The report confirms "no discernible effects on print knowledge."

> "The study shows, and the WWC confirms, no statistically significant or substantively important differences between treatment and control groups on any of these measures, and thus, no discernible effects on print knowledge."
