## Evidence

### Tseng Pei-Ling 2026

Tseng Pei-Ling, Yang Hui-Fang, Cheng Shao-Yi, Lai Hsiang-Ru, Huang Chiu-Mieh, Chen Jia-Yi, Tung Chen-Yin. (2026). Patients' Perceptions of Physicians' Health Literacy Competencies and "Good" Communication in Family Medicine: A Q Methodology Study. Inquiry. https://doi.org/10.1177/00469580251411672

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Q-factor analysis of patient Q-sorts; Factor I (n = 12 per Table 1) was labeled the Paternalistic Model – Physician-Dependent type, matching the paternalistic model of Emanuel and Emanuel and the active-passive model of Szasz and Hollender. Participants stated they "follow whatever the doctor instructs us to do."

> "Participants within this factor tend to follow traditional authoritarian thinking, placing primary responsibility for their health entirely in their physicians' hands."
