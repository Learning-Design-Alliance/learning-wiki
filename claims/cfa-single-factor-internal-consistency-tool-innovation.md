# Single latent factor models fitted both task success and ideal manipulation well, suggesting robust internal consistency, though Cling-subtract loaded weakly on task success

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Single latent factor CFA models fitted both task success and ideal manipulation measures well, indicating robust internal consistency across the nine tasks. [→ Guo 2025](#guo-2025)
`q3 i?` The Cling-subtract task had a low factor loading (0.17) on task success, suggesting it is less effective and subject to improvement. [→ Guo 2025 (2)](#guo-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Guo 2025

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

CFA with WLSMV estimation on dichotomous task data from 139 children; revised measurement models allowed correlated error terms and fit significantly better than the initial models for both measures.

> "Single latent factor models fitted well both measures of task success and ideal manipulation, suggesting robust internal consistency in measuring children's tool innovation performance."

### Guo 2025 (2)

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the revised task-success CFA model, the Cling-subtract loading was 0.17, which the authors flag as a weakly performing task needing further improvement.

> "Note, however, the factor loading of Cling-subtract was lower than the factor loadings of other tasks (0.17), indicating that this task might be less effective in measuring task success and is subject to further improvement."
