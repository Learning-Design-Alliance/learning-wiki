### Hausmann 2007

Hausmann, L.R.M., Schofield, J.W. & Woods, R.L. (2007). Sense of Belonging as a Predictor of Intentions to Persist Among African American and White First-Year College Students. Research in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11162-007-9052-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In this randomized intervention study, students "were randomly assigned to a group that received an intervention to enhance students' sense of belonging or to one of two control groups," and the intervention group's belonging decline was smaller. No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "However, the decline in sense of belonging was smaller for students in the intervention group."
