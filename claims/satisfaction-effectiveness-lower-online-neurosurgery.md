## Evidence

### Shen Jun 2025

Shen Jun, Zhang Shaolin, Sun Dongdong, Ge Ruixiang, Chen Sansong, Fang Jincheng, An Qian. (2025). Comparison of face-to-face teaching and online teaching in neurosurgery education for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06817-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course satisfaction survey (very dissatisfied to very satisfied) reported in Results and Fig. 1; the face-to-face group was significantly more satisfied, with the printed χ2 and P values.

> "41.38% of students in the face-to-face group reported being very satisfied, while 37.93% expressed satisfied with the curriculum. In contrast, 28.57% of students in the online group reported being very satisfied, and 29.76% of students were satisfied with the curriculum. The difference between the two groups was statistically significant (χ2 = 9.11, P = 0.028)."
