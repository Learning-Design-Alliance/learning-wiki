> "In contrast, substantial differences were identified with respect to word counts and response times (p = 0.000). Gemini Advanced attained the highest level of accuracy at 96.28%, followed by ChatGPT-4o Plus with 93.62%, whereas DeepSeek demonstrated the lowest performance at 86.70%."
