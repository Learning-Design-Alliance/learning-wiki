## Evidence

### Kryklywy James H. 2021

Kryklywy James H., Roach Victoria A., Todd Rebecca M. (2021). Assessing the efficacy of tablet-based simulations for learning pseudo-surgical instrumentation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0245330

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Response-time rm-ANOVA (F(1.74,61.02) = 4.574, p = 0.014 interaction) in the 36-subject experiment; both interactive conditions beat text at both tests (all pMMs < 0.014). The article suggests tablet training may be less susceptible to performance decline.

> "This interaction was driven by differences between real-world and tablet-based training during immediate testing (pMM = 0.011) but not during delayed testing (pMM = 1.00; pTOST = 0.008)."
