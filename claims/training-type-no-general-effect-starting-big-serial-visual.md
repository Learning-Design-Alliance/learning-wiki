## Evidence

### Lukics Krisztina Sára 2022

Lukics Krisztina Sára, Lukács Ágnes. (2022). Modality, presentation, domain and training effects in statistical learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-24951-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main effect of Training Type in the serial-presentation ANOVA of the 360-participant experiment: "The effect of Training Type was not significant"; only ηp2 = 0.01 is printed, no standardised effect size.

> "The effect of Training Type was not significant, F(2,228) = 1.51, p = 0.223, ηp2 = 0.01, BF10 = 0.914."

### Lukics Krisztina Sára 2022 (2)

Lukics Krisztina Sára, Lukács Ágnes. (2022). Modality, presentation, domain and training effects in statistical learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-24951-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Tukey comparisons of Training Type within serial visual presentation (F(2,117) = 5.32, p = 0.006, ηp2 = 0.08): "starting small was less efficient than starting big training"; random did not differ from either.

> "Tukey pairwise comparisons showed that starting small was less efficient than starting big training, p = 0.005, BF10 = 13.47, but learning with random and starting small, and random and starting big training did not differ from each other, p = 0.110, BF10 = 1.96 and p = 0.459, BF10 = 0.41, respectively."
