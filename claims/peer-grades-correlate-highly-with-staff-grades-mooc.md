Kulkarni, C., Koh Pang Wei, Le, H., Chia, D., Papadopoulos, K., Cheng, J., Koller, D., Klemmer, S. R. (2015). Peer and Self Assessment in Massive Online Classes. In: Design Thinking Research, Understanding Innovation. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-06823-7_9

Reported experience from two iterations of a large online class using peer and self assessment. The paper reports that "peer grades correlated highly with staff-assigned grades" and that in the second iteration 42.9% of grades were within 5% and 65.5% within 10% of staff grades.

> "In this class, peer grades correlated highly with staff-assigned grades. The second iteration had 42.9 % of students’ grades within 5 % of the staff grade, and 65.5 % within 10 %."
