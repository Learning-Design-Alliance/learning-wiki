## Evidence

### Mao Chengqiong 2026

Mao Chengqiong, Liu Yang, Zhang Chao. (2026). Open science practices and feedback-based pedagogy in nanomedicine education: a moderated mediation model of glioma treatment competency. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09948-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-arm parallel-group RCT across six Chinese medical colleges with 342 clinical medicine graduate students randomized to feedback-based pedagogy plus open science platform (n = 171) or traditional didactic instruction (n = 171) for 12 weeks. The main-effect regression model showed "feedback-based pedagogy (β = 0.28, p < 0.001, f2 = 0.09)" significantly predicted knowledge mastery, with the model explaining 58.7% of variance (R² = 0.587).

> "Table 7 shows that feedback-based pedagogy (β = 0.28, p < 0.001, f2 = 0.09) and open science practice (β = 0.33, p < 0.001, f2 = 0.12) both significantly predicted knowledge mastery"
