The article's framing section enumerates four skills, stating that "Learning to use graphs as effective tools in science requires 4 general skills" spanning construction, interpretation, graph-equation connection, and appropriate use. This is the author's synthesis of PER and cognitive science literature, not a measured result.

> "Learning to use graphs as effective tools in science requires 4 general skills: ● Constructing the representation and understanding how it codes mathematical information ● Interpreting what the information coded in the graph is telling you about the physical system ● Connecting the visual representation in the graph to equations describing the phenomena ● Knowing when and how to use graphs appropriately in physical problems."
