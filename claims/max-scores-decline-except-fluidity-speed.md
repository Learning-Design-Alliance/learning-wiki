## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The number of participants achieving maximum scores declined at three months and one year, except in fluidity and speed, which rose significantly (fluidity χ2=6.53, p=0.0106; speed χ2=8.33, p=0.0039). [→ Annoh R 2023](#annoh-r-2023)

## Evidence

### Annoh R 2023

Annoh R, Buchan J, Gichuhi S, et al. (2023). The impact of simulation-based trabeculectomy training on resident core surgical skill competency. Journal of Glaucoma. https://doi.org/10.1097/IJG.0000000000002114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

McNemar's test comparisons of maximum-score proportions in the intervention arm (Figure 5 analysis): maximum scores in most skills declined after primary intervention, but fluidity and speed rose significantly (χ2=6.53, p=0.0106; χ2=8.33, p=0.0039).

> "The exception was in fluidity and speed, where participants achieving maximum scores in these skills were significantly higher at three months than at the time of primary intervention (χ2 = 6.53, p=0.0106 and χ2= 8.33, p=0.0039 respectively, McNemar's test)."
