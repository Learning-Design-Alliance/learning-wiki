## Evidence

### Liu B. 2019

Liu B., Hunt L. M., Lonsdale R. J., Narula H. S., Mansaray A. F., Bundu I. and Bolkan H. A. (2019). Comparison of surgical skill acquisition by UK surgical trainees and Sierra Leonean associate clinicians in a task‐sharing programme. BJS Open 3(2): 218–223. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs5.50122

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-quantitative qualitative analysis (Burnard's method) of assessors' comments, categorized by Miller's method into cognitive patterns, behavioural patterns and adherence to instructions (Table 5). No effect sizes are printed; negative comments did not differ significantly between groups (P = 0·072).

> "Sierra Leonean trainees showed a preference for verbal learning, talking through the steps of the procedure and their cognitive processes. They interacted more with their assistant and were bolder in their actions."
