# The Prop 28 AMS requirement to spend 80% of funds on staffing has negatively impacted community arts organizations, including two preparing to close

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 80% staffing requirement left at most 20% of district allocations for external services, negatively impacting community arts organizations, with two respondents reporting their organizations were preparing to close. [→ Herpin 2025](#herpin-2025)

## Evidence

### Herpin 2025

Herpin, S. A., Wynveen, K., & Berry, W. (2025). California's Proposition 28 Arts and Music Funding Guarantee and Accountability Act: Initial impact on arts organizations. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677596

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey findings from 78 arts organization respondents. Districts serving 500 or more students must use at least 80% of funds on staffing, leaving up to 20% for supplies, materials, or partnerships with community arts organizations. Two respondents reported preparing to close.

> "Several community arts organization respondents noted this 80% staffing requirement had a negative impact on their organization, including two who indicated their organization was preparing to close its doors."
