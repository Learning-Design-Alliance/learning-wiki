Evidence¶

Nancy C. Jordan 2024¶

Nancy C. Jordan, Nancy Dyson, Taylor-Paige Guba, Megan Botello, Heather Suchanec-Cooper, Henry May. (2024). Exploring the Impact of a Fraction Sense Intervention in Authentic School Environments: An Initial Investigation. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2024.105954

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-hoc item-level analysis of the comparison task in the classroom RCT, reporting descriptive percentages (e.g., FSI 35% to 72% vs. control 46% to 58% on 1/3 vs. 1/2); these are descriptive gains with no printed effect size or test statistic.

"Despite the lack of mean differences, analysis of performance on specific fraction comparison items revealed that FSI children made relative gains on all three common numerator items relative to the control group (e.g., Which is bigger, 1 3 or 1 2 ? the FSI group went from 35% to 72% while the control children went from 46% to 58%, just above chance;"
