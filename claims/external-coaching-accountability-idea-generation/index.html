q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative finding from interviews with Design Team members across the LEAs. Participants reported coaches "offered ideas no one else in the room had thought to bring up" and appreciated coaching situated in local LEA context, including slowing conversations to personify the experiences of young BIPOC educators.

"One theme universally acknowledged across the participating LEAs was that monthly coaching led by Digital Promise staff has held LEA Teacher of Color Design Teams accountable for making progress and meeting milestones on work that would otherwise have taken longer."
