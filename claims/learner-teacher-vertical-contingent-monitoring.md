## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In vertical motion, causal influence from learner to teacher was significantly greater than teacher-on-learner influence in the second half of imitation trials. [→ Okazaki Shuntaro 2019](#okazaki-shuntaro-2019)

## Evidence

### Okazaki Shuntaro 2019

Okazaki Shuntaro, Muraoka Yoshihiro, Osu Rieko. (2019). Teacher-learner interaction quantifies scaffolding behaviour in imitation learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44049-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed rank comparison of bidirectional vertical-motion causal influences in 14 pairs during the imitation phase; the learner-to-teacher direction was "significantly greater than for teacher on learner for the second half (p = 0.021, R = 0.75)".

> "In addition, the causal influence from learner to teacher was significantly greater than for teacher on learner for the second half (p = 0.021, R = 0.75)."
