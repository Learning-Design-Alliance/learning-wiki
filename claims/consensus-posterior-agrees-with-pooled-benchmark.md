Benchmark comparison on operational Duolingo English Test data across four quarterly calibration periods, restricted to converged item parameters. The article reports "r=.998 for d and.991 for loga 1" with RMSE 0.129 and 0.081 for posterior means.

> "Posterior means agreed closely (r=.998 for d and.991 for loga 1; RMSE 0.129 and 0.081), indicating that the per-draw linking and the precision-weighted aggregation introduce no systematic distortion of location."
