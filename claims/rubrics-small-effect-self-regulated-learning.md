# Rubric use has a small, statistically non-significant pooled effect on self-regulated learning (g = 0.23)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Panadero 2023

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three-level meta-analysis of 17 effect sizes from five studies on self-regulated learning. The pooled effect of 0.23 was "small in magnitude" and not statistically significant (p = 0.156); the article still interprets it as "a positive effect of using rubrics on students' self-regulated learning."

> "The overall standardized mean difference was 0.23 (SE = 0.123, t = 1.82, p-value = 0.156, 95% CI [-0.15, 0.60], 95% PI [-0.65, 1.10]), which is small in magnitude."
