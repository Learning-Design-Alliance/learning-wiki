## Evidence

### Yu Yuan 2025

Yu Yuan, Lili Zhao, Wei Chen, Guangting Zheng, Kai Zhang, Mengdi Zhang, Qi Liu. (2025). Simulating Human-Like Learning Dynamics with LLM-Empowered Agents. Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.05622](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.05622)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation experiment over a 12-month English grammar curriculum with four LLM learner agents. Monthly tests included 15 trap questions assessing deep understanding; the article reports Deep Learner achieves the highest accuracy there while Surface and General Learners struggle the most, revealing "superficial and fragile knowledge mastery".

> "On Trap questions designed to assess deep understanding and knowledge mastery, Deep Learner achieves the highest accuracy, demonstrating a strong ability to generalize principles rather than rely on rote learning."
