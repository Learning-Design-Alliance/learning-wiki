## Evidence

### Naeimi and Foo 2015

Maki Naeimi & Thomas Chow Voon Foo. (2015). Vocabulary Acquisition through Direct and Indirect Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p142

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Effect size analysis of the pre-post improvement for the 30-student indirect strategies group. The article reports η2 = 0.03 and classifies it as a moderate effect size against Cohen (1988) criteria, though the printed value falls in the small (0.01–0.06) band by those same criteria.

> "The magnitude of the differences in the means was large (η2 = 0.03). Comparing this eta-squared value obtained (η2 = 0.03) to Cohen criteria (0.01 = small effect, 0.06 = moderate effect, and 0.14 = large effect), it is obvious that the effect size obtained is a moderate effect size."
