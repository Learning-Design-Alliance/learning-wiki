## Evidence

### Brame CJ 2015

Brame CJ, Biel R. (2015). Test-Enhanced Learning: The Potential for Testing to Promote Greater Learning in Undergraduate Science Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-11-0208

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Roediger and Karpicke (2006a), a laboratory experiment in which undergraduates read ~250-word passages and were assigned to repeated study (SSSS), mixed (SSST), or repeated retrieval (STTT) conditions. At a 1-week delayed recall test, "the STTT group performing ∼5% higher than the SSST group and ∼21% higher than the SSSS group"; at 5 min the ordering favored study.

> "The researchers found that, when the final test was delayed by a week, the results were reversed, with the STTT group performing ∼5% higher than the SSST group and ∼21% higher than the SSSS group."
