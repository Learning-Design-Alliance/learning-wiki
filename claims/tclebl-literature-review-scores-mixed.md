## Evidence

### Huang Tianlong 2024

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blind evaluation of written OT review articles by three instructors (average score taken), six criteria totaling 100 points, compared by independent t-test. Four criteria favored TCLEBL at p < 0.001; no effect sizes printed.

> "There were significant differences between the LBL and TCLEBL groups, respectively, in terms of literature review and citations (12.683 ± 2.207 vs. 16.302 ± 1.095, p < 0.001), argument and perspective (12.55 ± 1.572 vs. 16.333 ± 1.354, p < 0.001)"

### Huang Tianlong 2024 (2)

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same blind-evaluation study: two of the six criteria showed no statistically significant difference between LBL and TCLEBL groups; equivalence was not tested. Means printed as quoted.

> "there were no statistically significant differences in the scores for structure and organization (10.667 ± 0.717 vs. 11.175 ± 1.047, p = 0.079) and clarity of expression and language fluency (6.567 ± 0.81 vs. 7.016 ± 0.806, p = 0.083)"
