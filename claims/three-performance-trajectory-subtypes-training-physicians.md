Latent profile analysis (mclust, Gaussian finite mixture modeling) on z-transformed scores of eight variables for 160 physicians. The 3-class model was chosen after BIC and Lo–Mendell–Rubin comparisons; the article reports the class sizes as "average performance (n = 106)".

> "This model included subgroups representing average performance (n = 106), consistently high achievers (n = 37, top 30%), and those adapting to challenges (n = 17, lowest 10%)."
