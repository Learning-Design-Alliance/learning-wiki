Demonstration experiment (Vignette 1, Figure 1) contrasting AI tutor output for two learner personas with opposing language and mathematics proficiency profiles; a qualitative demonstration, not a measured comparison.

> "given the same mathematics word problem, the AI tutor produces two distinct explanations: one for Alex, a learner with strong language skills but weaker in mathematics, where the solution is framed through narrative and concept-building; and another for Blake, who is strong in mathematical reasoning but has weaker language comprehension"
