`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post skills testing of birth attendants on a neonatal simulator using the BMV Performance Evaluation (seven of seven steps required). The article reports only "4.6% of BAs" passed pre-training versus near-universal passing post-training, p < 0.0001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Prior to the initial HBB training, only 4.6% of BAs (3% of physicians and 5% of nurses) could ventilate a newborn mannequin effectively, as measured by the straightforward BMV skills test, but all physicians and 96% of nurses passed the test after initial training (p < 0.0001 pre- vs. post-initial training)."
