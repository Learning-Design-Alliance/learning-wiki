Carver-Thomas 2020¶

Analysis of California Commission on Teacher Credentialing data on permits and credentials issued shows the growth of substandard authorizations; the report states "the growing number of substandard credentials issued by the state is a reliable indicator that teacher supply continues to be insufficient to meet the demand for those positions."
