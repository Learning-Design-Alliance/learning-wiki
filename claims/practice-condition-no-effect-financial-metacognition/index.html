Evidence¶

Kang 2023¶

Kang, S. H. K., Eglington, L. G., Schuetze, B. A., Lu, X., Hinterstoisser, T. M., & Huaco, J. (2023). Using Cognitive Science and Technology to Enhance Financial Education: The Effect of Spaced Retrieval Practice. Journal of Financial Counseling and Planning, 34(1), 20–31. https://doi.org/10.1891/JFCP-2021-0032

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Final-assessment metacognition questions (0–10 scale) in the same field experiment. Confidence in knowledge did not differ across conditions, "F[2, 172] = 1.046, p = .354"; confidence in managing credit likewise showed F < 1. No effect size was printed.

"Participants’ level of subjective confidence in their own knowledge/understanding of credit did not vary significantly across the spaced practice ( M = 6.0), massed practice ( M = 6.2), and control ( M = 6.5) groups ( F[2, 172] = 1.046, p = .354)."
