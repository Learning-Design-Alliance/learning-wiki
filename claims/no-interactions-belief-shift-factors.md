### Adrian Madsen 2014

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2014). How physics instruction impacts students' beliefs about learning physics: A meta-analysis of 24 studies. https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6522

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Three-way type I ANOVA on course-level shift data ordered by educational changeability: population, class size, teaching method. The article reports "no significant three-way interaction or two-way interactions."

> "We found no significant three-way interaction or two-way interactions in our analysis. We tested the main effects of each factor. All three factors were found to be significant at the p < .01 level (Table 2)."
