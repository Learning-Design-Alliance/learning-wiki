## Evidence

### Duitsman 2019

Duitsman, M. E., van Braak, M., Stommel, W., ten Kate-Booij, M., de Graaf, J., Fluit, C. R. M. G., & Jaarsma, D. A. D. C. (2019). Using conversation analysis to explore feedback on resident performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09887-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Conversation-analytic analysis of eight audio-recorded evaluation meetings (262.42 min total) between program directors and residents in Dutch postgraduate medical education. The authors report that "Feedback as unidirectional activity occurred in 41 and feedback as a dialogic activity in 21 of the 62 fragments", with the remainder of conversations concerning practical issues.
