## Evidence

### Zhelev Zhivko 2013

Zhelev Zhivko, Garside Ruth, Hyde Christopher. (2013). A qualitative study into the difficulties experienced by healthcare decision makers when reading a Cochrane diagnostic test accuracy review. Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1186/2046-4053-2-32

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of think-aloud interviews identified layout problems including that "Figures had no key, which made them difficult to interpret" and mismatched values between abstract, results and summary tables. The article notes this affected all participants regardless of knowledge level.

> "These difficulties were further exacerbated by poor layout and presentation, which affected even those with relatively good knowledge of DTARs and had a negative impact not only on their understanding of the reviews but also on their motivation to engage with the text."
