Bennett-Conroy 2012¶

Waveline Bennett-Conroy. (2012). Engaging Parents of Eighth Grade Students in Parent–Teacher Bidirectional Communication. School Community Journal, 22(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001614

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Phase 1 qualitative interviews with 17 parents randomly chosen from a staff list of parents not known to have at-school involvement. The author links this to prior literature on staff underestimation of parent involvement.

"However, nearly all parents interviewed reported some form of at-school involvement, and in most cases discussed specific episodes and the general character of their at-school involvement in some detail."
