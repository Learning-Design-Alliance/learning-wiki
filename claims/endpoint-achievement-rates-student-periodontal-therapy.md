Herz Marco M., Schamuhn Judith, Krumm Boris, Bartha Valentin. (2025). Student-performed periodontal therapy: retrospective cohort study on outcomes and related recommendations for enhancing undergraduate periodontal education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07699-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results section of the retrospective cohort study of 107 student-treated stage III periodontitis patients, evaluating treatment success per the Feres et al. (2020) treat-to-target framework at re-evaluation and last supportive care visit. The article states the BOP < 20% endpoint "was achieved in 49% of all patients" at T1, falling to 37% at T2, while the PD endpoint fell from 29% to 26%.

> "Analysing treatment success according to Feres et al. (2020) at T1 revealed that a defined endpoint of BOP < 20% was achieved in 49% of all patients, and the endpoint of ≤ 4 sites with PD ≥ 5 mm in 29% of all patients (Fig. 4a). At T2, the endpoint of BOP dropped to 37% and to 26% in terms of PD"
