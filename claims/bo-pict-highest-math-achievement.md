## Evidence

### Nandwa et al. 2015

Odhiambo marble Nandwa, Dr. Duncan Wekesa Wasike, Professor Toili William Wanjala. (2015). Influence of Instructional Practices on Secondary School Students’ Achievement in Mathematics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.26. https://www.iiste.org/Journals/index.php/JEP/article/view/31336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of post-test means showed E3 (BO-PICT, N=83) scored highest at 66.4, with a mean gain of 35.3, compared to E1 (60.2), E2 (63.2) and control (42.4).

> "A close scrutiny reveals that E3 tha t used BO- PICT was superior to the rest of the experimental g roups. To test whether the means of groups were dif ferent, an ANOVA on the posttest results was calculated and results shown in table 5."

### Nandwa et al. 2015 (2)

Odhiambo marble Nandwa, Dr. Duncan Wekesa Wasike, Professor Toili William Wanjala. (2015). Influence of Instructional Practices on Secondary School Students’ Achievement in Mathematics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.26. https://www.iiste.org/Journals/index.php/JEP/article/view/31336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests showed E3 significantly outperformed E1 (t = 2.87) and E2 (t = 1.84) on the post-test MAT, confirming the combined strategy's superiority over either component alone.

> "That is between C and E1 (t,  1,161 =8.62), C and E2 (t 1, 163 =9.77), C and E3 (t 1,164=12.66), E1 and E3 (t I, 162 =2.87) as well as E2 and E3 (t 1,164=1.84)."
