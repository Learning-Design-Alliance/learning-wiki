## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent t-tests on the 20-item Likert-scale competency measure in the intervention group (n = 153 at T2 of 219 at T1). Table 3 prints item-level means, e.g. collaboration with other care providers 2.5 (±1.0) to 3.0 (±0.9), pain 3.3 (±0.8) to 3.6 (±0.8), all p < .01 except delirium (p = .05).

> "Apart from “dealing with delirium”, all competencies in the survey were rated significantly higher after the intervention."
