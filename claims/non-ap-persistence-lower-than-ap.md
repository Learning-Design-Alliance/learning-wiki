## Evidence

### Kudish Philip 2016

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of graduation majors/minors in the after-program cohort, comparing AP and non-AP groups. The study also reports higher NSE graduation rates for the AP group (69%) than the non-AP group (56%; chi-square = 8.4, p < 0.01).

> "59% of the more-prepared group (who reported AP Biology scores) graduated with majors or minors in biology, compared with only 44% of the less-prepared (non-AP) group (chi-square = 10.1, p < 0.01)."

### Kudish Philip 2016 (2)

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of SGM attendance and biology retention within the non-AP after-program population. The study reports r = 0.112, p = 0.07, a marginal association; in the AP population, outcomes were not related to attendance rates.

> "For the subset of students in the less-prepared (non-AP) population, however, there was a small correlation between attendance and retention in biology (r = 0.112) that approached significance (p = 0.07)."
