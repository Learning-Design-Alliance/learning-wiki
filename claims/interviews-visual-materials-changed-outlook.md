## Evidence

### Aykut Emre Bozdogan 2011

Aykut Emre Bozdogan. (2011). The Effects of Instruction with Visual Materials on the Development of Preservice Elementary Teachers' Knowledge and Attitude Towards Global Warming. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932241

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of 4-item semi-structured interviews with 6 randomly selected experimental-group students (3 female, 3 male): all reported a larger perspective on global warming's causes and results, more attentive news following, and four reported giving up CO2-contributing habits. All "emphasized that vi sual materials had a significant effect on this process".
