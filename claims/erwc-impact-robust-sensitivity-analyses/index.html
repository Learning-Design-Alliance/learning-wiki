Evidence¶

Anthony B. Fong 2015¶

Anthony B. Fong, Neal D. Finkelstein, Laura M. Jaeger, Rebeca Diaz, Marie E. Broek. (2015). Evaluation of the Expository Reading and Writing Course: Findings from the Investing in Innovation Development Grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559522

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Sensitivity analyses in the impact evaluation chapter varied the matching approach (including the number of matches per ERWC student and matching without replacement) and the analytic sample. The report finds "the positive, statistically significant results were robust to a number of different sensitivity analyses."

"In addition, the positive, statistically significant results were robust to a number of different sensitivity analyses. These sensitivity analyses included varying the methods used to match ERWC and non-ERWC students and varying the types of students included in the analytic sample."
