### Gupta 2018

Gupta, P. K., and Muhuri, P. K. (2018). Computing with words for student strategy evaluation in an examination. Granular Computing 4, no. 2, 167-184. [doi:10.1007/s41066-018-0109-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41066-018-0109-2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' comparison of four CWW approaches (extension principle, symbolic method, 2-tuple, Per-C) for student strategy evaluation, stated in the paper's introduction with a case study in which "we consider 25 students, in the first semester of Master's course in Computer Science stream". The article states Per-C generated "unique recommendations in 100% cases"; no effect size is printed.

> "through the c omparison of the recommendations generated by different CWW approaches, we found that Per-C outperforms the others CWW approaches by generating unique recommendations in 100% cases as well as modeling the word uncertainty in the best possible way , which other approaches failed to do."
