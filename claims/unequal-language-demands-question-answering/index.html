Amrein 2000¶

Amrein, Audrey. (2000). Dual Language Asymmetry: Symbolic Inequalities. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED440548

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Observational finding from the six classrooms: because English-speaking teachers were monolingual, they "had to force the language because they were monolingual," while bilingual Spanish teachers accepted questions in either language.

"the Spanish-speaking students were being required to both speak and comprehend English while the English-speaking students were only required to listen to the Spanish."
