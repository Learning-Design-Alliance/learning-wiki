# EFL students taught grammar and vocabulary with Pixton comic strips scored significantly higher on a post-test than control students taught without Pixton

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a four-month quasi-experiment with 163 Ecuadorian junior-high EFL students, the experimental group using Pixton significantly increased its post-test score (t= 3.9294, p= 0.0001) relative to the control group. [→ Cabrera 2018](#cabrera-2018)

## Evidence

### Cabrera 2018

Cabrera, P., Castillo, L., González, P., Quiñónez, A., & Ochoa, C. (2018). The impact of using Pixton for teaching grammar and vocabulary in the EFL Ecuadorian context. Teaching English with Technology, 18(1), 53-76. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170640

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control-group study in a public high school in southern Ecuador; 85 experimental students used Pixton-designed materials and 78 controls used posters, flashcards and PowerPoint. The post-test mean was 11.911 (experimental) versus 9.803 (control), with "t= 3.9294   p= 0.0001" as printed in Table 2.

> "As regards the post-test administered to both group s, the results show that students in the experimental group significantly increased thei r score (t= 3.9294, p= 0.0001)."
