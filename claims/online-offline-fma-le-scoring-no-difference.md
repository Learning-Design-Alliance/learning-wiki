### Lee Haesu 2026

Lee Haesu, Woo Young Keun, Park Kyue-nam, Kim Sujin. (2026). Online versus offline Fugl-Meyer lower extremity training for physical therapy students: a randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09634-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-arm parallel randomized trial with 44 undergraduate physical therapy students (online n = 20, offline n = 24) analyzed by linear mixed-effects model. The article reports "no significant group x time interaction was observed (p > .05)"; no effect size for the null comparison beyond partial η² = 0.03.

> "the main effect of group was not significant (F(1,42) = 1.23, p = .273, partial η2 = 0.03), and no significant group x time interaction was observed (p > .05)"
