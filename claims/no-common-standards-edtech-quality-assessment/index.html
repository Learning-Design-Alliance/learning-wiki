Second challenge surfaced in the report's listening tour with more than 50 stakeholders. Stakeholders quoted in the report said that with common standards there would be "a real opportunity to create this notion of smart demand"; no quantitative measures are reported.

"Education leaders, educators, and investors do not have established common language or indicators to assess the quality of edtech products. Without common methods to compare products in the edtech industry, these stakeholders struggle to make evidence-based decisions about the quality and value of edtech products."
