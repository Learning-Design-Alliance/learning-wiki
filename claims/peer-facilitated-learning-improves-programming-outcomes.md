`q3 i?` Students in the peer-facilitated learning group scored significantly higher on programming skills (M=78.379 vs 72.275, t=3.433, p=.001), enjoyment of learning (M=3.283 vs 2.952, t=2.210, p=.029), and intention to learn (M=4.652 vs 4.075, t=2.386, p=.018) than the non-peer-facilitated group.
