## Evidence

### Devine 2025

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent variable measurement modeling at two time points in the same 252-child longitudinal sample. The article reports the combined one-factor well-being model "did not fit the data at Time 1 or Time 2." No effect size is printed for this fit comparison.

> "the model in which caregiver- and child-rated well-being loaded onto one factor did not fit the data at Time 1 or Time 2, suggesting that caregiver ratings of well-being and child ratings of well-being captured related but distinct constructs."

### Devine 2025 (2)

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Latent factor correlations between informant versions of the HIFAMS in the same sample. The article reports the association was "weak at Time 1, r = .20" and "moderate at Time 2, r = .37, p < .0001."

> "Associations between child-rated well-being (T1, Ω = .85; T2, Ω = .84) and caregiver-rated well-being (T1, Ω = .88; T2, Ω = .91) were weak at Time 1, r = .20, p = .017, and moderate at Time 2, r = .37, p < .0001."
