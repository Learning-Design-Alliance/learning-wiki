## Evidence

### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 1076 Japanese adults (mean age 47.2, SD 11.4) recruited from a research company panel. After reading a hypothetical friend's disclosure scenario, the top-ranked need was "how to interact with friends diagnosed with cancer" (317/1076, 29.5%).

> "After reading scenarios depicting hypothetical friends with cancer, the participants identified the following 5 most sought types of information: “how to interact with friends diagnosed with cancer” (317/1076, 29.5%), “types of cancer treatment” (288/1076, 26.8%)"
