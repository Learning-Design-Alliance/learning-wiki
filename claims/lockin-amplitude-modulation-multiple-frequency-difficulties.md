## Evidence

### Seth DeVore 2015

Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. [doi:10.1119/1.4934957](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4934957)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview-based identification of difficulties with amplitude-modulated input signals, reported in the tutorial's student difficulties section prior to instruction.

> "Students commonly believe that amplitude modulation necessarily affects both the x -channel and y -channel outputs equally. Students also believe that amplitude modulation should not be affected by the low-pass filter or completely forget to consider the effects of the low-pass filter on this signal."

### Seth DeVore 2015 (2)

Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. [doi:10.1119/1.4934957](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4934957)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The same interview study reports, as a final difficulty category, cases with multiple frequencies present in the input signal, described as an aspect with which practically all students had difficulty.

> "One final aspect of the lock-in that practically all students had difficult y with are cases in which multiple frequencies are present in the input signal."
