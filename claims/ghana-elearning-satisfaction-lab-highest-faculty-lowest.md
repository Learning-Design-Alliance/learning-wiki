Rotated varimax factor analysis of Likert satisfaction items from the 363-respondent survey generated six factors compared across demographic groups. "satisfaction with the computer laboratory" was most favorable and teaching faculty least, with significant group differences.

> "The results show that the component with the most favorable rating was satisfaction with the computer laboratory while the least rated component was satisfaction with teaching faculty. There were statistically significant differences in the ratings in terms of gender, school, age and year of study by the students"
