## Evidence

### de Leng W E 2018

de Leng W E, Stegers-Jager K M, Born M Ph, Themmen A P N. (2018). Integrity situational judgement test for medical school selection: judging 'what to do' versus 'what not to do'. Medical Education. [doi:10.1111/medu.13498](https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13498)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Williams' tests comparing HH-based and CD-based SJT score correlations in the 402-applicant validation study. The CD-based score won on two named measures with "d = 0.47" and "d = 0.41"; the workplace deviance difference was non-significant (p = 0.130).

> "The Williams’ test indicated that the CD-based SJT score correlated significantly more strongly than the HH-based SJT score with the HIT questionnaire (t (168) = 3.07, p = 0.003, d = 0.47) and with the ICB (t (171) = 2.69, p = 0.008, d = 0.41)."
