# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, tutees of student-tutors trained by a course, an internship, or both had significantly higher ultrasound scanning skills at post-test in all three groups, with comparable gains

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In all three tutor-training groups (course-only, internship-only, course-plus-internship), tutees' scanning OSCE scores improved significantly and by comparable amounts, more than doubling their pre-course scores. [→ Celebi N 2019](#celebi-n-2019)

## Evidence

### Celebi N 2019

Celebi N, Griewatz J, Ilg M, Zipfel S, Riessen R, Hoffmann T, Malek NP, Pauluschke-Fröhlich J, Debove I, Muller R, Fröhlich E. (2019). Three different ways of training ultrasound student-tutors yield significant gains in tutee's scanning-skills. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(6):Doc77. [doi:10.3205/zma001285](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001285)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective quasi-randomized pre-post study of tutees taught by tutors from three training routes; 75 of 124 fifth-year students (60.5%) completed both voluntary OSCEs. All groups "improved significantly by more than doubling their pre-score results", with paired t-tests all p<.0001; gains were 9.19±5.73, 9.77±4.81 and 8.97±5.49 points.

> "In all three groups, the tutees improved significantly by more than doubling their pre-score results and comparably (Gains: C-group 9.19±5.73 points, p<.0001, I-group 9.77±4.81 points, p<.0001, CI-group 8.97±5.49 points, p<.0001)."
