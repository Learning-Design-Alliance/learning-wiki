## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 12 semi-structured interviews with nurse mentors conducted in April 2017 in Patna. Mentors reported mentees confused preeclampsia diagnostic criteria, struggled with magnesium sulfate dose calculation, and faced staff and supply shortages including absent doctors.

> "All mentors perceived the strict hierarchy between nurses and doctors as a significant barrier to high quality care. The majority also perceived that poor relationships between nurses and patients in PHCs were key barriers."
