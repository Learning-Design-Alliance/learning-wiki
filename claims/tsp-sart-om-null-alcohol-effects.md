# TSP (complex spatial problem solving) and SART-OM (vigilance for frequent events) showed no significant alcohol effects, an outcome the authors report as unexpected

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` TSP and SART-OM failed to show significant effects of alcohol intoxication at any dose level. [→ Dry MJ 2012](#dry-mj-2012)

## Evidence

### Dry MJ 2012

Dry MJ, Burns NR, Nettelbeck T, Farquharson AL, White JM (2012) Dose-Related Effects of Alcohol on Cognitive Functioning. PLoS ONE 7(11): e50977. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0050977

`q3 · i?`

In the 56-participant experiment, the TSP main effect was non-significant (F = 2.55, p = 0.11) and SART-OM near zero (F = 0.04, p = 0.83); the authors call the failure unexpected and note comparable null results in prior work.

> "The failure of TSP and SART-OM to show any significant effects was unexpected, although other researchers have reported null results at BAC 0.048% [82] and for BACs between 0.059 to 0.067 [83], for similarly complex tasks involving higher order cognitive processes."
