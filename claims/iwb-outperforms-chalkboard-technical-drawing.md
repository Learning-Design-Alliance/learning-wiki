## Evidence

### Gambari 2014

Gambari, I. A., Balogun, S. A., & Alfa, A. S. (2014). Efficacy of Interactive Whiteboard on Psychomotor Skills Achievement of Students in Isometric and Orthographic Projection. Contemporary Educational Technology, 5(4), 316-330. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105500

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on posttest scores of 94 JSS-1 students in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent group design, with pretest as covariate. The treatment main effect was significant, "F (1, 97) = 74.640, p < .05".

> "Table 3 shows that an F (1, 97) = 74.640, p < .05 for the main effect (treatment) was significant, this indicates that the method of instruction produced a significant effect on the achievement scores of students when covariate effect (pre -test) was controlled."
