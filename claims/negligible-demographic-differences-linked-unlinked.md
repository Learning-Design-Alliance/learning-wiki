## Evidence

### Sinclair Jeanne 2023

Sinclair Jeanne, Davies Scott, Janus Magdalena. (2023). Student achievement trajectories in Ontario: Creating and validating a province-wide, multi-cohort and longitudinal database. International Journal of Population Data Science. https://doi.org/10.23889/ijpds.v8i1.1843

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation analysis comparing the fully linked dataset (n = 86,778) with unlinked EDI cases (n = 170,865) using phi coefficients, interpreted as small at 0.10. All demographic effect sizes were negligible, with the largest for "English learner status" at a proportional difference of 0.05.

> "The effect sizes comparing linked and unlinked cases on EDI demographic variables (Table 3) were only negligible, less than 0.10. The largest effect sizes were for English learner status (mean difference in proportion of 0.05) and special needs status (mean difference of 0.03)"
