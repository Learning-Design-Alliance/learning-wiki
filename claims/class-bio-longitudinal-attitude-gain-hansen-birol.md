The review reports, citing Hansen and Birol (2014), a four-year longitudinal CLASS-Bio study at a major research university in which matched first-to-fourth-year respondents gained from 64.5 to 72% more expert-like responses. These are descriptive percentages; no effect size is printed.

> "The group of matched respondents who took the CLASS-Bio in both their first and fourth years exhibited a positive overall gain from 64.5 to 72% more expert-like responses, with the largest improvements in the real-world connection and enjoyment (personal interest) categories."
