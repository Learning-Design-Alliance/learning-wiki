Pre-/post-test case study in an advanced quantum mechanics course; the pre-test was given after traditional lecture but before the time-development QuILT, with nine students taking both tests. The article reports that "the average student performance improved from 53% to 85% after working on the QuILT".

> "Table 1 shows that in the case study in which nine students took both the pre-/post-tests, the average student performance improved from 53% to 85% after working on the QuILT."
