Case narrative of the ASSISTments team's experience, describing a randomized controlled trial in which ASSISTments provided online math homework support (citing Roschelle et al., 2016). Practitioners flagged homework as a perennial issue and detailed configuration needs; "the fundamental research question in the study arose out of an interaction between the ASSISTments team and practitioners."

"The focus on mathematics homework arose from interacting with practitioners: They expressed that "homework" is a perennial hot button issue in their schools and that research that showed how to make homework more productive would be especially valuable."
