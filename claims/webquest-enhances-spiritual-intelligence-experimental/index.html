Evidence¶

Jwaifell et al. 2015¶

Mustafa Jwaifell, Reham Al-Mouhtadi & Intisar Aldarabah. (2015). Effectiveness of Web Quest in Enhancing 4th Grade Students' Spiritual Intelligence. World Journal of Education, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v5n2p10

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental two-groups pretest-posttest design with an untreated control group; 134 4th graders in Ma'an, Jordan, distributed into 6 groups. ANCOVA on posttest scores adjusting for pretest gave F = 84.814, Sig = 0.000 for Group, described as "significant differences between the Means of Experimental and Control Groups".

"ANCOVA analyses showed significant differences between the Means of Experimental and Control Groups and the interaction between Gender and Group (Experimental/Control)."
