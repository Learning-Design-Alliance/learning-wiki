`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group pre-test–post-test study of 45 medical students in an emergency medicine clerkship; paired t-tests on OSCE scores before and after an online flipped classroom course. The article reports the score rose "from 25.97 (SD = 2.40) to 30.41 (SD = 3.37)" with "a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.51)".

> "the total mean score increased from 25.97 (SD = 2.40) to 30.41 (SD = 3.37), reflecting a robust educational gain with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.51). The mean difference of 4.44 (95% CI: 3.94–4.92)"
