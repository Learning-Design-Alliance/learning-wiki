## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ba Ruikai 2026

Ba Ruikai, Feng Xinran, Yuan Lixian, Tong Juan, Ban Jinghao, Wu Meiling, Han Bing, Xu Xinyue, Qiu Xinyu, Guo Hao, Xuan Kun. (2026). A novel extended flipped classroom model helps dental undergraduates grow into dentists. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08525-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nineteen-item 1–5 Likert satisfaction questionnaire (α 0.93, ω 0.94) compared between the 2022 and 2023 cohorts with chi-square tests on dichotomised scores and Wilcoxon tests, with Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment. The largest chi-square improvement was Q17 (3.97 vs 4.67, delta 0.69).

> "Additionally, Wilcoxon rank-sum tests indicated significant improvements in 18 out of 19 items (p < 0.05), supporting the overall enhancement in satisfaction following the implementation of the flipped classroom."
