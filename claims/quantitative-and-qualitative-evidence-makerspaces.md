### Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip 2024

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip. (2024). Analyzing properties of success for assessment development in maker-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10286-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding on what makes success visible: participants named both count-based metrics and in-situ qualitative observations as evidence. The article reports educators "rely on quantitative metrics" and "also rely on qualitative evidence."

> "They rely on quantitative metrics, like "How many times [a kit] gets used" and "We had 100 story times," but they also rely on qualitative evidence, "[Patrons are] jumping around, but that passion, that excitement from whatever inspired them is still there…In the midst of them going from here to here and then…there is still curiosity.""
