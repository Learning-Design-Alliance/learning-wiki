Michelene T. H. Chi & Ruth Wylie. (2014). The ICAP Framework: Linking Cognitive Engagement to Active Learning Outcomes. Educational Psychologist, 49(4), 219-243. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2014.965823

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article's central hypothesis, derived conceptually from the taxonomy and its underlying knowledge-change processes. The authors state that "empirical validation for the hypothesis is provided by examining laboratory and classroom studies" focusing on note taking, concept mapping, and self-explaining.

> "The ICAP hypothesis predicts that as students become more engaged with the learning materials, from passive to active to constructive to interactive, their learning will increase."
