Comparative reliability analysis across annotation stages: GPT-5.2 with ICL prompting showed human–machine QWK of 0.593–0.655 across stages and criteria versions, with the human-refined framework yielding "only small changes in reliability" for LLMs.

> "The human-refined framework produced only small changes in reliability from Criteria Version 1 to Version 2 (overall ∆QWK≈0.01–0.04), in contrast to the larger gains observed for human–human agreement."
