## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group taught via schema-based instruction scored significantly higher than the control group taught via translation-based instruction on the 96-item unseen achievement test (F = 312.247, df = 59, p < .001). [→ Khodadady and Elahi 2012](#khodadady-and-elahi-2012)

## Evidence

### Khodadady and Elahi 2012

Ebrahim Khodadady, Majid Elahi. (2012). The Effect of Schema-Vs-Translation-Based Instruction on Persian Medical Students' Learning of General English. English Language Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n1p146

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental two-group pretest-posttest study of sixty freshman anesthetics students; one-way ANOVA on the 96-item achievement test showed the SBI group (M = 81.90) far above the TBI control (M = 54.13), a "superior performance" on the achievement test.

> "instructing the experimental group based on the microstructural approach of schema theory has resulted in their superior performance not only on the post test schema-based cloze MCIT (F = 217.451, df =59, p <.001) but also on the achievement test (F = 312.247, df =59, p <.001)"
