## Evidence

### Brener A 2020

Brener A, Lewnard I, Mackinnon J, Jones C, Lohr N, Konda S, McIntosh J, Kulinski J. (2020). Missed opportunities to prevent cardiovascular disease in women with prior preeclampsia. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-020-01074-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective chart review of well-woman visit notes, past medical history, obstetric history and problem lists for women ages 18-48 seen at academic-affiliated clinics (n = 89). The review found "23.6% of women were asked about a history of preeclampsia" versus near-universal ascertainment of traditional risk factors, significant across each comparison (all p < 0.001).

> "When assessment of pregnancy history was obtained, 23.6% of women were asked about a history of preeclampsia while 98.9% were asked about diabetes or smoking and 100% were asked about chronic hypertension (Fig. 1)."
