### Næss S. Ø. 2026

Næss S. Ø., Sivertsen B., Hysing M., Dovran A., Tobiassen S., Hafstad G. S., Kjørstad K. (2026). School Functioning Among Norwegian Children With a History of Maltreatment. Journal of Interpersonal Violence. https://doi.org/10.1177/08862605251351669

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Contingency table analysis in the same 192-child sample examining the association between parent-reported very poor performance and formal special education decisions; the article reports an odds ratio of OR = 19.1 (95% CI [7.16, 51.15]), i.e., children were "19.1 times more likely" to have a special education decision.

> "Children who performed very poorly in at least one subject were 19.1 times more likely (95% CI [7.16, 51.15]) to have a decision for special education compared to their peers without reported poor performance."
