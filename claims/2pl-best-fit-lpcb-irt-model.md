## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Heri Retnawati. (2024). Validating Light Phenomena Conceptual Assessment Through The Lens of CTT and IRT Framework. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Model comparison of five dichotomous IRT models fitted to the LPCA pretest responses using AIC, item fit (chi-square) and person fit (Zh statistic). The 2-PL had the lowest AIC (10424.97 vs 10431.12 for Rasch/1-PL) and, per the authors, "the number of infit items from the 2-PL model was the lowest among the others".

> "The best fit IRT model on the LPCA data would have the lowest AIC value, thus the 2-PL model should meet."
