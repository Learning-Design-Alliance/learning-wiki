## Evidence

### Knight 1921

Knight, F. B., & Franzen, R. H. (1921). Pitfalls in rating schemes. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073817

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Rating study of 110 junior university students who ranked 34 interests for themselves, for the ideal junior, and for the typical junior; pooled orders were correlated. The article reports the introspective-objective correlation was "+0.13" and interprets it as very little self-other association.

> "The correlation between the order of interests taken introspectively and that taken objectively was +0.13. Then there is very little association between what the junior thinks he is and what others think he is."
