## Evidence

### Ellington Laura E 2020

Ellington Laura E, Becerra Rosario, Mallma Gabriela, Tantaleán da Fieno José, Nair Deepthi, Onchiri Frankline, Nielsen Katie R. (2020). Knowledge acquisition and retention after a high flow training programme in Peru: a quasi-experimental single group pre–post design. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-035125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single group pre–post study in a Peruvian PICU; 80 participants completed baseline pre-training assessment and 76 the post-assessment. Wilcoxon rank-sum comparisons of pre- vs post-baseline scores showed significant improvement overall and in all subtopics except clinical application of knowledge (p<0.001); median overall score rose from 61 (52–65) to 78 (70–87).
