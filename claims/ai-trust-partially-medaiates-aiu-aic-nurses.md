### Liu 2026

Liu, X., Chen, Y., Guan, W., Jiang, P., Yan, L., Fan, M., & Zhou, Q. (2026). The Mediation of AI Trust on AI Uncertainties and AI Competence Among Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70250

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 4, bootstrap 5000) of 550 nurses found AIU affected AIT (B = 0.39) and both AIU and AIT affected AIC (B = 0.25 and 0.67), confirming that "The relationship between AIU and AIC mediated by the AIT was confirmed."

> "The relationship between AIU and AIC mediated by the AIT was confirmed. AIU affected AIT (B = 0.39, p < 0.0001), with an explained variation of 10%. AIU and AIT affected AIC (B = 0.25 and 0.67, p < 0.0001), with an explained variation of 63%."
