### Mabuza LH 2020

Mabuza LH, Mntla PS. (2020). Generalist practitioners' self-rating and competence in electrocardiogram interpretation in South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 12(1), a2421. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v12i1.2421

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results on ECG emergency tracings in the cross-sectional questionnaire study of 93 generalist practitioners. The article reports asystole at "69.9%", acute antero-septal MI at 34.9%, atrial fibrillation at 22.9%, and third-degree A-V block lowest.

> "the parameter with the highest number of correct answers in the ECG emergency category was the 'asystole' (69.9%), followed by the 'acute antero-septal' MI (34.9%) and lastly 'atrial fibrillation' (22.9%). The parameter with the least correct answers was the 'third degree A-V block'."
