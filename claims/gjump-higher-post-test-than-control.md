### Jafarpoor Hasanali 2026

Jafarpoor Hasanali, Falavarjani Hossein Ghasemi, Shokrani Amirhossein, Mortazavi Yosef, Kazemzadeh Asal, Darzi Fatemeh Hasaninejad, Behnam Fatemeh. (2026). Integrating the Triple Jump Exercise and gamification (G-Jump) to enhance learning outcomes among anesthesia students: a preliminary quasi-experimental study. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 45 anesthesia students in three groups of 15 (control, TJE, G-Jump), stratified by pre-test performance. ANOVA showed significant post-test group differences (F = 6.998, p = 0.002); the quote shows "G-Jump outperformed the control group (p = 0.0016)" with other pairs non-significant. Post-test means were 16.14 ± 1.72 (G-Jump), 14.57 ± 1.64 (TJE), 13.3 ± 2.55 (control).

> "Tukey’s post-hoc test indicated that in the post-test, G-Jump outperformed the control group (p = 0.0016), whereas other pairwise differences were non-significant."
