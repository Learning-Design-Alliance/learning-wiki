# Students perceived FC+CBL as more beneficial than LBC for motivation, comprehension, critical thinking, patient management and teamwork

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` More FC students believed the approach improved learning motivation (P = .019), knowledge comprehension (P < .001), critical thinking (P = .024), patient management (P < .001) and teamwork (P < .001) compared with LBC students. [→ Cai L 2022](#cai-l-2022)

## Evidence

### Cai L 2022

Cai L, Li Yl, Hu Xy, Li R, Khoshnood A. (2022). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning: a promising and effective teaching modality in undergraduate pathology education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000028782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Online 11-item 5-point Likert questionnaire (WeChat) with 100% response rate in both groups; Mann–Whitney tests compared perceptions. FC was "believed to significantly enhance" comprehension, critical thinking and patient management; teamwork (P < .001) and motivation (P = .019) also favored FC. No effect sizes printed.

> "the FC model was believed to significantly enhance the students' abilities of comprehension of knowledge (P < .001), critical thinking (P = .024), and patient management (P < .001) compared with the LBC model"

### Cai L 2022 (2)

Cai L, Li Yl, Hu Xy, Li R, Khoshnood A. (2022). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning: a promising and effective teaching modality in undergraduate pathology education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000028782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same questionnaire: no significant group difference on self-active study (P = .208) or memorization of fundamental knowledge (P = .183); equivalence was not tested. No effect sizes printed.

> "compared with LBC, more students believed that the FC approach improved their learning motivation (P = .019), with no difference in increasing self-active study (P = .208)"
