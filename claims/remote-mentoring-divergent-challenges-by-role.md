# Mentors and mentees in remote STEM mentoring perceived different sets of challenges: mentees time management and logistics, mentors STEM content delivery and social challenges

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mentees reported time management and logistics as main hurdles, while mentors identified lack of face-to-face interaction and STEM concept delivery as main challenges. [→ Khare 2013](#khare-2013)

## Evidence

### Khare 2013

Khare, R., Sahai, E., Pramanick, I. (2013). Remote Mentoring Young Females in STEM through MAGIC. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1304.7547](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1304.7547)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of narrative data from 23 pairs identified themes reported in the challenges section: "Many mentees experienced difficulty in assigning time to MAGIC assignments and projects". Mentors separately reported remote delivery of STEM content and logistics such as software compatibility as challenges; no effect sizes are reported.

> "Many mentees experienced difficulty in assigning time to MAGIC assignments and projects, and reported that they remained busy with several after school activities, full time jobs, and travelling."
