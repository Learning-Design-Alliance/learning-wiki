## Evidence

### Awsaf 2026

Awsaf, A.A., Giansanti, N., Sunbury, S., & Dou, R. (2025, March). Concurrent phenomenological analysis of STEM career aspirations in underrepresented youth: Role of experiences and identity [Presentation]. 2025 NARST Annual International Conference, National Harbor, MD. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.06035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.06035)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case narrative from the phenomenological interviews: Mercy, a non-STEM participant, wanted a STEM career from middle school until the beginning of high school, and "a lack of support and recognition from math teachers inspired her to choose a non-STEM career." The authors interpret this as a potential decay of performance-competence.

> "The most significant misrecognition events students described experiencing were associated with formal mathematics education."
