## Evidence

### Schüttpelz-Brauns 2020

Schüttpelz-Brauns, K., Hecht, M., Hardt, K., Karay, Y., Zupanic, M., & Kämmer, J. E. (2020). Institutional strategies related to test-taking behavior in low stakes assessment. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 25(2), 321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09928-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same mixed effects logistic regression of 108,140 participations, Presentation type (Est. 0.36, p = 0.333) and Individuality of discussed results (Est. 0.81, p = 0.275) were not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "The main effects for the strategies “Presentation type” and “Individuality of discussed results” had nonsignificant regression coefficients, thus, they are unrelated to the seriousness of the test-taking behavior."
