Evidence¶

Barbosa Ana 2020¶

Barbosa Ana, Whiting Stephen, Simmonds Philippa, Scotini Moreno Rodrigo, Mendes Romeu, Breda João. (2020). Physical Activity and Academic Achievement: An Umbrella Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17165972

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Umbrella review of 41 systematic reviews and meta-analyses; 13 meta-analyses provided effect sizes for academic achievement. The review reports the overall effect of different modes of PA had "null [28,32,33] or small to medium [27,30,33,35,37] effects on AA"; no pooled effect size is printed.

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Mathematics is the subject where positive associations with physical activity are found most consistently in systematic reviews, though meta-analytic effects range from null to medium
  • Increasing school physical education time shows small-to-medium effects on achievement while active classrooms show mixed effects compared with traditional sedentary classrooms
  • Across 169 meta-analyses, 254 variables with 427 effect sizes influence academic achievement; most variables have small or very small effects and none has a large, very large or huge negative effect
  • Chronic physical activity shows a medium positive effect on academic achievement while acute physical activity shows a null effect — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Classroom-based physical activity has a huge effect on academic achievement (ES = 2.987) and computer-aided teaching shows huge positive effects (ES = 1.690), while school mobility has a medium negative effect (ES = -.748) — reports the opposite
  • Meta-analyses of physical activity effects on youth cognition and academic performance show small effect sizes (0.1–0.3) with substantial heterogeneity — related
  • A single bout of acute physical activity increases overall academic outcomes in school-aged youth (Hedge's g = 0.35) — related
  • Subgroup analyses by mode of ACS, age, and sex show no significant differences in pooled effect sizes for academic outcomes — related
