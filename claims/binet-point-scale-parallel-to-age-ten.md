# The Binet-Simon and Yerkes Point Scale ratings of children run parallel and are practically identical up to age ten

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The findings of the Binet-Simon and Point Scale tests run parallel until age ten, so both scales are reliable for children aged ten or below. [→ Zimmerman 1914](#zimmerman-1914)

## Evidence

### Zimmerman 1914

Zimmerman, J. (1914). The Binet-Simon Scale and the Yerkes Point Scale: A Comparative Study Based on the Examination of 100 Cases. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 100 clinic cases examined by the same examiner with both scales, summarized by age group in Chart III with average departure from age for each scale. The author states the findings of the two scales "run parallel till the age of ten".

> "Chart III further indicates what was said before, namely that the findings of the two scales run parallel till the age of ten. Hence we may assume that both scales are reliable till that age."
