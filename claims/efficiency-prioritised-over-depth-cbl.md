Taylor A. W. R., Patel F. Y., Smyth E. K., Gay S., and Bird T. (2026). AI in the Classroom: Observing Preclinical Students' Use of ChatGPT During Case‐Based Learning at a UK Medical School. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 2: e70360. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70360

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from field notes and focus groups; students reported ChatGPT was more useful than Google because multiple resources did not need to be checked, and one student avoided reading around topics because there were too many questions.

> "Although depth and breadth were occasionally valued, the pressure of completing the workbook and covering the whole syllabus led to the prioritisation of speed and specificity."
