## Subclaims
`q3 i?` During the rotation phase, PMBR showed a continuous significant decline in the reward condition, while the error condition showed a non-significant trend; baseline and washout PMBR did not differ between conditions. [→ Federico Nardi 2025](#federico-nardi-2025)

## Evidence

### Federico Nardi 2025

Federico Nardi, A. Aldo Faisal & Shlomi Haar. (2025). Motor learning mechanisms are not modified by feedback manipulations in a real-world task. npj Science of Learning 10:73. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00373-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

EEG time-frequency analysis of PMBR (beta-band rebound after movement cessation) over rotation-phase blocks, with 28 participants in the error and 30 in the reward condition after preprocessing. The reward condition declined significantly; the error condition trend was not significant.

> "The reward sessions showed a continuous decline (bblock = −1.955,p < 0.001, 95% CI: [−2.863,−1.047]), while participants in the error condition did not exhibit a significant trend (bblock = −1.185, p = 0.09, 95% CI: [-2.534, 0.164])."
