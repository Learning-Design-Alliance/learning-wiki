`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing attendees and non-attendees of in-service spiritual care education within the 220-nurse cross-sectional survey. The article reports the association as "significantly associated with higher SCCS" with the printed t statistic and confidence interval; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Similarly, in-service spiritual care education was significantly associated with higher SCCS (mean difference=−3.39, t (218)=−2.390, 95% CI (−6.18 to –0.59), p=0.018)."
