## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 82 anaesthesiologists: PEEP application in laparoscopic surgery was reported by 69/72 conceptual-group versus 5/8 non-conceptual-group respondents (p = 0.012; OR = 13.80 [95% CI, 2.19–86.88]).

> "(69/72 [95.8%] vs. 5/8 [62.5%]; p = 0.012; odds ratio [OR] = 13.80 [95% CI, 2.19–86.88])."

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018 (2)

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same survey, routine PEEP question: 65/73 conceptual-group versus 4/8 non-conceptual-group respondents routinely applied PEEP (p = 0.015; OR = 8.13 [95% CI, 1.69–38.90]).

> "The conceptual group used PEEP more often than the non-conceptual group in terms of the number of respondents who routinely applied PEEP during anaesthesia (65/73 [89.0%] vs. 4/8 [50.0%]; p = 0.015; OR = 8.13 [95% CI, 1.69–38.90])."
