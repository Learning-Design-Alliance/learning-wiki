## Evidence

### Benjamin M. Zwickl 2012

Benjamin M. Zwickl, Noah Finkelstein, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2012). The Process of Transforming an Advanced Lab Course: Goals, Curriculum, and Assessments. [doi:10.1119/1.4768890](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4768890)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Weekly survey data collected during Spring 2012 from the 21 upper-division Physics and Engineering Physics majors enrolled in the redesigned course. Students reflected on what they learned most and what to change; the article reports that "only 65% said they enjoyed the activities." In response, the authors will spread the activities over a larger number of days.

> "while 83% of students thought the Mathematica and LabVIEW activities were very helpful for learning, the time allotted for completion was too short, which students found frustrating (only 65% said they enjoyed the activities)."
