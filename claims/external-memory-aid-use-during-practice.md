Badali, S., Rawson, K.A. & Dunlosky, J. (2023). How do Students Regulate Their Use of Multiple Choice Practice Tests?. Educational Psychology Review 35, 43. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09761-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Post-experiment question in Experiment 2 asked whether participants used an external memory aid in either session; self-reported examples included notes and web sources. Tables 2 and 3 report descriptive and inferential statistics for Experiment 2 excluding participants who answered yes. No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Many participants who answered “yes” to this question indicated they took notes and/or used a web source to help them with the answers."
