## Subclaims
`q3 i1` A one-way ANOVA on BNT raw scores across third-, fourth-, and fifth-grade word-reading levels showed significant group differences, with the fifth-grade group outperforming the third-grade group. [→ Hall 2012](#hall-2012)

## Evidence

### Hall 2012

Hall, R., Greenberg, D., Laures-Gore, J., & Pae, H. K. (2012). The Relationship Between Expressive Vocabulary Knowledge and Reading Skills for Adult Struggling Readers. Journal of Research in Reading. [doi:10.1111/j.1467-9817.2012.01537.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9817.2012.01537.x)

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA comparing BNT Total Scores across three word-reading-level groups (n = 103, 78, 51). The omnibus test was significant, F(2, 229) = 3.22, p = .042, η2 = .027; post hoc tests showed only the 3rd-vs-5th contrast was significant (mean difference 2.84, p = .023).

> "Results showed significant group differences, F(2, 229) = 3.22, p = .042, η2 = .027. Post hoc analyses revealed a significant difference between the 3rd and 5th grade groups with a mean difference of 2.84 (p = .023), with the 5th-grade group performing better on the BNT."
