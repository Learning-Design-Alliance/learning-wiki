Kate Hamilton 2026¶

Kate Hamilton, Irene Hou, Dev Patel, Sheena Nnam, Hena Patel, and Stephen MacNeil. (2026). “Stuck in a Spiral”: Shame and Guilt as Social Regulators of AI Use in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14920 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.14920

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Results on cycles of use: the interview study found that "Rather than acting as a deterrent, these negative emotions often became part of a cycle" of resolve, return to use under deadlines, and renewed shame, with participants describing feeling stuck in a spiral.

"Rather than acting as a deterrent, these negative emotions often became part of a cycle in which students would resolve to avoid AI, only to return to it again under the time pressures of coursework and deadlines."
