## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2021

Viljoen Charle André, Millar Rob Scott, Manning Kathryn, Hoevelmann Julian, Burch Vanessa Celeste. (2021). Clinically contextualised ECG interpretation: the impact of prior clinical exposure and case vignettes on ECG diagnostic accuracy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02854-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of the same 176 participants asking when last they had seen each of the 25 conditions during clinical rotations. The article reports junior students were "exposed to 52% of the conditions tested, senior students 63.4% and residents 96.9%"; Table 1 gives per-condition percentages.

> "Prior ECG exposure depended on their level of training, i.e., junior students were exposed to 52% of the conditions tested, senior students 63.4% and residents 96.9%."
