## Evidence

### Singh‐Weldon J. 2022

Singh‐Weldon J., Tsianakas V., Murrells T., Grealish A. (2022). Preparing children's nurses for working with children and adolescents who self‐harm: Evaluating the ‘our care through our eyes’ e‐learning training package. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.13041

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the SHAS and Reynolds Empathy Scale in the single-group pre–post study (e.g. agreement that 'I help self‐harming patients feel positive about themselves' rose from 51% to 73%). The article reports "small increases in participants' empathy and positive attitudes".

> "Collectively, these findings demonstrate that following the training there were small increases in participants' empathy and positive attitudes, small decreases in their negative attitudes, and an improved understanding of the function that self‐harm fulfils for individuals."
