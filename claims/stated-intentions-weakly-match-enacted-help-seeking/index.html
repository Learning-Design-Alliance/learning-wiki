q2 · i? · associational · r?

Comparison of students' pre-chat multi-select intent selections with their subsequently coded request types in the LLM chat. Correspondence was near chance: "32.9% of the intention–behavior mass fell on matching categories" versus 32.4% expected from marginals.

"Overall correspondence between intended and enacted categories was weak: 32.9% of the intention–behavior mass fell on matching categories, close to the 32.4% expected from their marginal distributions."
