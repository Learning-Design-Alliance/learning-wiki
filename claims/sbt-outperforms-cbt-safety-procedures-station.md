`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results of station 4 (safety procedures, simulated radioactive spill management) in the quasi-experimental cohort comparison. "The SBT group achieved a mean score of 8.317 out of 12, compared with 2.000 for the CBT group"; the difference was significant and survived Bonferroni correction.

> "The SBT group achieved a mean score of 8.317 out of 12, compared with 2.000 for the CBT group. An independent-samples t-test with Welch's correction showed a statistically significant difference between groups (t = 11.20, df = 34, p < 0.001), which remained statistically significant after Bonferroni correction."
