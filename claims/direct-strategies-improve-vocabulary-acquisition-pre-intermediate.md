## Evidence

### Naeimi and Foo 2015

Maki Naeimi & Thomas Chow Voon Foo. (2015). Vocabulary Acquisition through Direct and Indirect Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p142

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study of 30 Iranian EFL university students taught with direct strategies over 10 sessions. The article reports the pre-test mean of 31 out of 40 improved to 36 in the post-test, with a paired sample t-test showing statistical significance (p<0.05).
