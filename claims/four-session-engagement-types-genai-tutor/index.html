Evidence¶

Youjie Chen 2026¶

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Clustering analysis of 113,255 conversation sessions using ten engineered features and six principal components retaining 82.3% of variance yielded four types. The abstract reports "10.4% of them were 'shallow engagement' where copy-pasting behavior was prevalent." Cluster assignments were stable across 50 random initializations (mean Adjusted Rand Index = 0.999).

"we identified the following four engagement types in the 113,255 conversation sessions: Deep, Shallow, Routine-Learning, and Exam-Driven Engagement."
