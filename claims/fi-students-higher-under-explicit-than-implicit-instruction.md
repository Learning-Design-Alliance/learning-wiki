## Evidence

### Kusumawati 2017

Kusumawati, E., & Widiati, U. (2017). The Effects of Vocabulary Instructions on Students’ Reading Comprehension across Cognitive Styles in ESP. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(2). https://journals.iiste.org/index.php/JEP/article/view/2894

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive posttest comparison of FI students in the quasi-experimental study: FI under EVI (n=18) mean 81.2500 (SD 5.44018) vs FI under IVI (n=21) mean 71.7857 (SD 5.37022). The authors report the FI-under-EVI mean as “significantly higher than the FI under IVI group.”

> "Based on the result of posttest of FI groups under the EVI group and FI under the IVI group, it was shown that the mean scores for FI under EVI group is 81.2500 and FI under IVI group is 71.7857 respectively."
