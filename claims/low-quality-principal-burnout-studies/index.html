Evidence¶

Rogers 2025¶

Rogers, L. K., Nguyen, T. D., Timmer, M., & Samson, J. (2025). Principal Burnout: A Systematic Review. Working paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED666666

q3 · i? · review · r3

Quality assessment using a modified Lipsey and Wilson (2001) approach across 99 studies. "Slightly less than half (47%) of studies were subjected to peer review." The review states "We found no replication studies in our search" and that much gray literature "imitated previously published studies within different samples and settings."

"Across the 99 included studies, 53 were holistically assessed by the coding team to be high quality, 14 of average quality, and 32 studies were judged to be low quality based on one or more of these criteria."
