## Evidence

### Khalil Mohammed K. 2019

Khalil Mohammed K., Wright William S., Spearman Kelsey A., Gaspard Amber C. (2019). Relationship between students’ perceptions of the adequacy of M1 and M2 curricula and their performance on USMLE step 1 examination. BMC Medical Education 19:358. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1796-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey-plus-score-plot comparison at one medical school: 99 students' closed- and open-ended insufficiency ratings were compared with disciplines and organ systems at or below −0.2 SD on the Step 1 score plot; the authors report the areas "coincide with the least performing disciplines and organ systems".

> "Interestingly, areas of insufficiencies coincide with the least performing disciplines and organ systems on Step 1 examination (Table 2)."
