## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schönburg S 2021

Schönburg S, Anheuser P, Kranz J, Fornara P, Oubaid V. (2021). Cognitive training for robotic surgery: a chance to optimize surgical training? A pilot study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-020-01167-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SPI ratings after test 2 in the randomized pilot (n=25). Self-reflection differed significantly between the control group and the cognitive training group (F(2.22) = 8.56; p < 0.005); the repeated-measures MANOVA gave p < 0.02 with Greenhouse–Geisser and Huynh–Feldt both 1.00. No effect size printed.

> "In test 2, significant group differences occurred at ‘self-reflection’ (F (2.22) = 8.56; group 3 vs. 2; p < 0.005). From all 3 groups, probands of group 3 achieved the highest increase in accurate self-assessed performance and communicated experience"
