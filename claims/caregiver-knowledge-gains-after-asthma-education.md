## Evidence

### Agusala Vijay 2018

Agusala Vijay, Vij Priyanka, Agusala Veena, Dasari Vivekanand, Kola Bhargavi. (2018). Can interactive parental education impact health care utilization in pediatric asthma: A study in rural Texas. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518773621

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post telephone survey (n = 102) of caregivers in the APT program; reported education rose from 20.6% to 93.1% and trigger knowledge from 52.9% to 91.2% (χ2-test comparisons). Confidence, action-plan understanding, peak-flow knowledge, and perceived control also increased significantly. Only test statistics are printed; no effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "There were statistically significant increases in the frequency of parents/caregivers who underwent the educational program who reported receiving education (χ2 = 109.86, P < 0.001), knowing what triggers an asthma exacerbation (χ2 = 37.03, P < 0.001)"
