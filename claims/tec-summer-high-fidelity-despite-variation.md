`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Implementation findings from classroom observations and teacher interviews across the 12 sites: "nearly all participating teachers implemented the TEC Summer Learning Program as planned" despite program diversity.

### McCarthy 2011 (2)

McCarthy, B., Michel, L., Tiu, M., Atienza, S., Rice, J., Nakamoto, J., & Tafoya, A. (2011). Evaluation of The Electric Company Summer Learning Program. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED533408

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The report states "Student outcomes were consistently positive, even with variations in the training teachers received and the delivery of the number of episodes." Reported obstacles included inconsistent attendance, technical difficulties running Prankster Planet, and student lack of computer knowledge.

> "Student outcomes were consistently positive, even with variations in the training teachers received and the delivery of the number of episodes of The Electric Company ."
