# Trained teachers allocated about 20 percent of class time to the feedback-corrective/enrichment loop after staff development, having devoted virtually none before

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After training, treatment teachers on average allocated about 20 percent of class time to the feedback-corrective/enrichment loop, which was extremely rare in both groups before training. [→ Fitzpatrick 1985](#fitzpatrick-1985)

## Evidence

### Fitzpatrick 1985

Fitzpatrick, Kathleen A. (1985). Group-Based Mastery Learning: A Robin Hood Approach to Instruction? Paper presented at the annual meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, Illinois. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED255556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom observation study of 21 trained and 19 control secondary mathematics teachers, with one class per teacher observed for five days pre and twice post. The observation instrument coded time at one-minute intervals across instructional functions; the trained teachers' loop allocation rose from near zero to about "20 percent of class tiro".

> "Following the training program, however, the trained teachers on averageallocated about 20 percent of class tiro to thisinstructional component."
