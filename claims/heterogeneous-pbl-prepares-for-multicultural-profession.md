## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students perceive that working in diverse PBL tutorials develops the collegiality needed for internship and community service in multicultural public-sector healthcare. [→ Singaram Veena S. 2010](#singaram-veena-s-2010)

## Evidence

### Singaram Veena S. 2010

Singaram Veena S., van der Vleuten Cees P. M., Stevens Fred, Dolmans Diana H. J. M. (2010). “For most of us Africans, we don’t just speak”: a qualitative investigation into collaborative heterogeneous PBL group learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9262-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Student focus groups reported that tutorial interaction builds collegiality and the ability to "adjust and comply with team members" for future internship and community service; one student said he should be able to relate to colleagues not from his background.

> "Being able to adjust and comply with team members in diverse resource constraint environments is an essential skill especially during their Internship and Community Service years."
