q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, from Sytek et al.'s mixed-methods study assessing orthodontic residents' performance and attitudes in orthognathic surgery treatment planning, a null performance comparison plus qualitative interview findings on adoption attitudes; equivalence was not tested.

"The mixed-methods research reported no significant difference in overall performance of residents among 2D, 3D, and VR simulations. In addition, the evidence from semi-structured interview demonstrated that the residents also tended to be ready to adopt VR simulation"
