## Evidence

### Misra Satya Ranjan 2026

Misra Satya Ranjan, Das Rupsa, Das Subhra Jyoti. (2026). Preparedness of dental interns to identify oral potentially malignant disorders and oral cancer: a multi-state cross-sectional survey in India. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08681-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 1220 dental interns across India. The knowledge scale (8 items) showed modest internal consistency (KR-20 = 0.62). Mean score was "5.48 ± 1.08" with a ceiling effect noted by the authors.

> "The mean knowledge score was 5.48 ± 1.08 (range 1–7; median 6). Only 20 interns (1.6%) scored ≤ 3, while 910 (74.6%) scored ≥ 6."

### Misra Satya Ranjan 2026 (2)

Misra Satya Ranjan, Das Rupsa, Das Subhra Jyoti. (2026). Preparedness of dental interns to identify oral potentially malignant disorders and oral cancer: a multi-state cross-sectional survey in India. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08681-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item Q7 on leukoplakia transformation rate was the lowest-scoring knowledge item. The authors attribute the gap to reliance on "older textbook figures" and recommend updating teaching with current epidemiological data.

> "However, only 70.5% (n = 860) correctly identified the malignant transformation rate of leukoplakia as 1–5% (Q7); 27.0% (n = 330) believed it to be 10–20%."
