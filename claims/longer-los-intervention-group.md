Secondary outcome from the retrospective record review of 149 patients in the randomized trial: "median hospital LOS was longer in the intervention group compared with the control group" (19 vs. 13 days; p = 0.009), an unexpected finding the authors describe as possibly a learning-curve effect.

> "However, median hospital LOS was longer in the intervention group compared with the control group (19 days [IQR 11-39 days] vs. 13 days [IQR 7-25 days], respectively; p = 0.009)."
