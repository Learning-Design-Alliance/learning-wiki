## Evidence

### Baba Kaori 2023

Baba Kaori, Kataoka Yaeko. (2023). An E-Learning Program for Continuing Midwifery Education on Handling High-Risk Abuse Cases: A Pretest–Posttest Design. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20136317

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest study of 71 obstetric midwives and nurses near Tokyo; knowledge measured by a 15-item test (0–15 points) before and after the e-learning program. Mean rose from 8.1 (SD 2.0) to 11.6 (SD 1.7), and the paired t-test showed the post-test scores were "significantly higher (t = 14.4, p < 0.001)" with "d = 1.71".

> "A paired t-test demonstrated that the post-test scores were significantly higher (t = 14.4, p < 0.001). The effect size was large for the post-test (d = 1.71)"
