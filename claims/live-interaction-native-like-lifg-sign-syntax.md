## Evidence

### Ana Zappa 2025

Ana Zappa, Mel Slater & Antoni Rodriguez-Fornells. (2025). Social interaction shapes and boosts second language learning: virtual reality can show us how. npj Science of Learning 10:90. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00381-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

An fMRI study reviewed by the article (Yusa et al., 2017; Fig. 1D) compared learning Japanese sign language syntax with a live native deaf signer versus instructive videos, both groups physically signing; native-like left inferior frontal gyrus activation appeared "in the live exposure group but not in the video viewing group". No effect size printed.

> "Syntactic processing post-training led to activations in the left inferior frontal gyrus, similar to that displayed by native listeners in the live exposure group but not in the video viewing group."
