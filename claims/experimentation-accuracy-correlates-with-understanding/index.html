Evidence¶

Flegr 2023¶

Flegr, S., Kuhn, J., & Scheiter, K. (2023). How to foster STEM learning during Covid-19 remote schooling: Combining virtual and video experiments. Learning and Instruction, 86, 101778. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101778

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Exploratory analysis (RQ2) of N = 148 submitted worksheets scored by an experienced physics teacher (30 points total). The condition ANOVA gave η2 = 0.01, and accuracy showed "a strong correlation, r s(146) = .61, p < .0001" with post-test scores; the follow-up correlation was rs(69) = .41.

"The ANOVA showed no difference between conditions, F(2, 145) = 1.10; MSE = 1030.35; p = .34; η2 = 0.01 (small effect). We furthermore calculated the correlation of students' accuracy of experimentation and their post-test scores, yielding a strong correlation, r s(146) = .61, p < .0001."
