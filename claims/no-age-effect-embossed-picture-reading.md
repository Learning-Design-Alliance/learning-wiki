## Evidence

### Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho 2010

Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho. (2010). The Effect of Embossed Picture Technique on Reading Performance of Learners with Hearing Impairments: A Case of Kambui School for the Deaf. Journal of the American Academy of Special Education Professionals, Spring/Summer 2010. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137051

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis one-way ANOVA on post-test scores of the experimental group by age band (8-9 vs 10-11) at significance level 0.05; the article reports the p-value is > 0.10 and the null hypothesis was not rejected. Descriptively, the 8-9 group's top score (60) exceeded the 10-11 group's (50), but the difference was not significant.

> "The results revealed that there is no significant age effect on reading performance of pupils with hearing impairment of different ages taught with embossed picture technique."
