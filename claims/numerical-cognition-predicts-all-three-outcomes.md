### Spencer Mercedes 2022

Spencer Mercedes, Fuchs Lynn S., Geary David C., Fuchs Douglas. (2022). Connections between Mathematics and Reading Development: Numerical Cognition Mediates Relations between Foundational Competencies and Later Academic Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000670

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct effects from the final pruned path analysis mediation model on 370 children. The article reports "For each SD increase in NC, children’s later calculations, WPS, and reading performance increased by 0.388, 0.188, and 0.149 SD, respectively," with the strongest relation to calculations (0.388 SD per SD of NC); these are unstandardized betas, and no standardized effect size is printed.
