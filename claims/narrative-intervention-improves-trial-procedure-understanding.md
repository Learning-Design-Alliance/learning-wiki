## Evidence

### Ndebele Paul M 2012

Ndebele Paul M, Wassenaar Douglas, Munalula Esther, Masiye Francis. (2012). Improving understanding of clinical trial procedures among low literacy populations: an intervention within a microbicide trial in Malawi. BMC Medical Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6939-13-29

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot randomized intervention study: 36 low-scoring former microbicide-trial participants randomly assigned to intervention (n=18) or standard-consent control (n=18). Cross-tabulation of intervention by composite score showed "13 of the participants in the intervention arm managed to obtain high scores" versus none of the controls, p=0.0001.

> "Table 4 below indicates that 13 of the participants in the intervention arm managed to obtain high scores, while no participant from the non-intervention arm managed to score 75% and above. A p-value of 0.0001 was obtained using both Pearson's chi-square test and Fischer's exact test"
