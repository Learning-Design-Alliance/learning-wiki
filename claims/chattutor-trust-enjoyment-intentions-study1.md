### Guido Makransky 2025

Guido Makransky, Ban M. Shiwalia, Tue Herlau, Steven Blurton. (2025). Beyond the "Wow" Factor: Using Generative AI for Increasing Generative Sense-Making. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10039-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Independent-samples t-test in Study 1 comparing the two GenAI conditions on the four-item trust measure showed "the ChatTutor group (M = 3.64, SD = 0.58) scored significantly higher than the ChatGPT group (M = 3.29, SD = 0.59) on trust t = 2.955; p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 0.60."

> "the ChatTutor group (M = 3.64, SD = 0.58) scored significantly higher than the ChatGPT group (M = 3.29, SD = 0.59) on trust t = 2.955; p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 0.60."

### Guido Makransky 2025 (2)

Guido Makransky, Ban M. Shiwalia, Tue Herlau, Steven Blurton. (2025). Beyond the "Wow" Factor: Using Generative AI for Increasing Generative Sense-Making. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10039-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Study 1 t-test on the three-item enjoyment measure at follow-up showed "The ChatTutor group (M = 3.65, SD = 0.91) also scored significantly higher than the ChatGPT group (M = 2.99, SD = 0.52) on enjoyment t = 3.304; p < 0.001, d = 0.86."

> "The ChatTutor group (M = 3.65, SD = 0.91) also scored significantly higher than the ChatGPT group (M = 2.99, SD = 0.52) on enjoyment t = 3.304; p < 0.001, d = 0.86."
