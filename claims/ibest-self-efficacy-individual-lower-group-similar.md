## Evidence

### Richmond Helen 2016

Richmond Helen, Hall Amanda M., Hansen Zara, Williamson Esther, Davies David, Lamb Sarah E. (2016). Using mixed methods evaluation to assess the feasibility of online clinical training in evidence based interventions: a case study of cognitive behavioural treatment for low back pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0683-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory RCT, self-efficacy Likert scale (0–10) for the individual assessment session (n=16 workshop, n=15 iBeST); "participants trained with iBeST reported lower self-efficacy to deliver the single BeST individual assessment session", printed effect size −0.95.

> "Table 2 shows that participants trained with iBeST reported lower self-efficacy to deliver the single BeST individual assessment session (MD 1.73, 95 % CI 0.43, 3.03)."
