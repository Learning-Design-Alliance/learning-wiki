### Emilio Ferrer 2023

Emilio Ferrer, Bennett A. Shaywitz, John M. Holahan and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Early reading at first grade predicts adult reading at age 42 in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00205-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal trajectories (Fig. 2) and growth curve parameters show a large first-grade discrepancy (mean intercepts µ0 = 13.62 vs 6.92) with similar grade 1-5 increases (µs = 12.00 vs 13.10), so fifth-grade levels remain disproportionate and the gap persists at age 42.

> "The reading gap between typical and dyslexic readers that is already evident in 1 st grade never closes during the school years. When reading is examined in adulthood, the gap still remains."
