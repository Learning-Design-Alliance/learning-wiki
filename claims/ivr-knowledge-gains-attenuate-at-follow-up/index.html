Subgroup analysis of the knowledge-acquisition meta-analysis by assessment timing. Substantial heterogeneity persisted in both subgroups (I² = 73.6% immediate; I² = 74.4% later follow-up).

"When stratified by follow‐up timing, the immediate post‐intervention assessment showed a significant positive effect (Hedges’ g = 0.39, 95% CI: 0.03–0.74), whereas the later follow‐up assessment demonstrated no significant benefit (Hedges’ g = 0.19, 95% CI: −0.35–0.72)"
