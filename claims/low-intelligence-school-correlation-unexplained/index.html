E. B. Skaggs 1918¶

E. B. Skaggs. (1918). The Correlation of General Intelligence Tests and School Standing. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

The author's interpretive discussion of the low correlation: possible factors named are the mostly female sample (the Army tests being especially for men), the one-term basis of school standing, and Stern's point that school performance depends on factors beyond intelligence. Some investigators find very satisfactory correlation while others find very low correlation.

"Why we have obtained such a low correlation is difficult to explain. We cannot blame the grading system, for the Normal School upholds a good standard."
