## Evidence

### Al-Mugheed K 2025

Al-Mugheed K, Al-Oweidat I, Alzoubi MM, Alnaeem MM, Alshehri AS, Hendy A, Baghdadi NA, Farghaly Abdelaliem SM. (2025). Effectiveness of a high-fidelity simulation in terms of cardiac auscultation knowledge and skills among nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000044970

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experimental study of 60 fourth-year and internship nursing students randomized by coin toss to simulation (n = 30) or traditional instruction (n = 30). The ANCOVA-adjusted knowledge contrast was significant: "the ANCOVA-adjusted post-test results remained statistically significant (P < .01) in all domains".

> "After controlling for baseline scores, the ANCOVA-adjusted post-test results remained statistically significant (P < .01) in all domains."
