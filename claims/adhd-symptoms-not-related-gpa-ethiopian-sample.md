### Kassaw Chalachew 2025

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2025). Psychosocial and cognitive predictors of academic achievement among higher education students in Southern Ethiopia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335968

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Nonparametric group comparison in the 30-student sample using ASRS v1.1 classifications. The with/without ADHD contrast was non-significant; equivalence was not tested. The Kruskal–Wallis test across ADHD groups was also non-significant (H(2) = 4.08, p = .253).

> "The Mann–Whitney U test comparing GPA between students with and without ADHD symptoms yielded a non-significant result (U = 108.00, p = .897)."
