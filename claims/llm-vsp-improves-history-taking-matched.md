## Subclaims
`q3 i2` After propensity score matching, students practicing with LLM-VSPs scored higher on end-of-term OSCE history taking than controls (mean difference 3.98 points, Cohen d=0.52). [→ Stone Alicia 2026](#stone-alicia-2026)

## Evidence

### Stone Alicia 2026

Stone Alicia, Vicente Maria Asuncion, Xia Oudong, He Yuchen, Chen Chen, Yin Rong, Zhang Wuyang, Chang Haoli, Yang Wei, Li Fei, Li Xinhua, Xia Zhuying, Xie Xiaoyun, Huang Jing, Zeng Qiuming, Yang Guang, Wu Jing. (2026). Effect of Large Language Model–Powered Virtual Standardized Patients on History-Taking Among Undergraduate Medical Students: Propensity-Matched Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/92486

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Propensity-matched comparison (40 pairs) of end-of-term OSCE scores with real standardized patients in a prospective cohort of medical students. The matched intervention group scored higher, with "mean difference 3.98, 95% CI 0.55-7.40; Cohen d=0.52".

> "The average total score of group A in MHT was 3.98 points higher than that of group B (mean 87.71, SD 7.29 vs mean 83.74, SD 8.06; P=.02; mean difference 3.98, 95% CI 0.55-7.40; Cohen d=0.52)."
