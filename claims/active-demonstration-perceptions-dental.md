`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-test Likert-scale perceptions (four-point scale) from D2 students. The article reports means for helpfulness "(m = 3.47)", engagement "(m = 3.41)", visualisation "(m = 3.43)", and clinical management tool "(m = 3.40)".

> "As shown in Figure 3C, students typically found the activity helpful (m = 3.47) and engaging (m = 3.41). Responses also indicated overall agreement with statements about how the activity provided a tangible visualisation of space management (m = 3.43) and a tool for clinical management (m = 3.40)."
