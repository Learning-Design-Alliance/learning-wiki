## Evidence

### Zemin Guo 2026

Zemin Guo, Wenhui Li, Laure Lu Chen, Qianru Liang, & Yuxuan Wu. (2026). Reconceptualizing digital literacy in the AI era: A systematic review of validated frameworks from an ecological perspective. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 11, 100679. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100679

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Descriptive cross-framework mapping of 28 student/citizen frameworks onto DigComp 2.2 competence areas, compared by World Bank income classification; one framework using DigComp 2.2 itself was excluded from comparison. Coverage rates are descriptive prevalence, not significance tests.

> "information and data literacy showed high coverage in both groups (developing: 86.7%, n = 13; developed: 91.7%, n = 11), whereas communication and collaboration were covered more widely in developed (83.3%, n = 10) than in developing regions (60.0%, n = 9)."
