## Evidence

### Jones Gareth G. 2018

Jones Gareth G., Logishetty K., Clarke S., Collins R., Jaere M., Harris S., Cobb J. P. (2018). Do patient-specific instruments (PSI) for UKA allow non-expert surgeons to achieve the same saw cut accuracy as expert surgeons? Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-018-3031-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sawbone experiment in which trainees with no prior UKA experience used 3D-printed PSI; the results section reports no compound error difference versus experts using conventional instruments (Table 1: 5.5° (3.4°) vs 7.7° (2.3°), p = 0.396), i.e. equivalence on this measure was observed rather than formally tested.

> "There was no difference in the overall compound error between trainees using PSI and experts using conventional instrumentation."
