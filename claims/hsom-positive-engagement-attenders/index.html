Evidence¶

Menzies et al. 2016¶

Victoria Menzies, Adetayo Kasim, Dimitra Kokotsaki, Catherine Hewitt, Nasima Akhter, Clare Collyer, Kirsty Younger, Andy Wiggins and Carole Torgerson. (2016). Hallé SHINE on Manchester: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581134

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative process evaluation (observations and stakeholder reports) of attended Saturday sessions; findings are perceptual reports rather than measured attainment gains. The report adds that more challenging feedback may have helped pupils' learning.

"Evaluators observed good working relationships between the teachers and pupils , and positive and purposeful learning environments in lessons. All stakeholders felt pupils were making noticeable improvements in behaviour, confidence, and development of social skills."
