## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, E. A., Zwolak, J. P., Dou, R., & Brewe, E. (2017). Linking engagement and performance: The social network analysis perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A null result from the bootstrapped simple regression models across all five collections: "betweenness is never a signiﬁcant predictor, even when GPA is not included." Because it was not significantly correlated in the simple model, betweenness was excluded from full-model analysis; Table I notes it was omitted throughout.

> "Finally, betweenness is never a signiﬁcant predictor, even when GPA is not included."
