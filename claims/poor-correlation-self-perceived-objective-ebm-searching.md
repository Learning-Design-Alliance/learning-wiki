Lai Nai Ming, Teng Cheong Lieng. (2011). Self-perceived competence correlates poorly with objectively measured competence in Evidence Based Medicine among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-25

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 45 final-year Malaysian medical students completing a self-perceived competence questionnaire and adapted Fresno test concurrently. Pearson correlation in the searching domain was r = 0.13 (p = 0.4), not statistically significant.

> "The correlation coefficient (r) between the students' self-rating and their Fresno test scores in the domain of "searching for evidence" was 0.13 (p = 0.4)."
