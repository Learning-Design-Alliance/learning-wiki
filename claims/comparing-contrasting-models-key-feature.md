## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Why we should teach the Bohr model and how to teach it effectively. https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1541 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-study comparison with a German secondary-school study (77% drew a quantum model) and a Greek teacher-course study (99%), both of which used curricula contrasting models including the Bohr model. The authors call this feature "a necessary component" likely needed to move beyond the Bohr model.

> "In comparing our curriculum with the curricula used in these previous studies, one key feature stands out: all three emphasized comparing and contrasting the Bohr model with later models."
