The Education Trust 2022¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: California. EDTRUST.ORG. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A comparative table of California schools serving the most versus fewest Black students, and of Black versus non-Black students, reports these descriptive percentages; the brief states students of color and low-income students are more likely to attend schools with more novice teachers.

"Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers 22% 16% Percent of Students in schools with High (≥10%) percentages of Uncertified Teachers 10% 6%"

The Education Trust 2022 (2)¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: California. EDTRUST.ORG. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A parallel comparative table for Latino versus non-Latino students in California prints the same two measures, showing higher exposure to novice and uncertified teachers for Latino students. Schools serving the most Latino students also show 13% versus 10% novice teachers.

"Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers 17% 14% Percent of Students in schools with High (≥10%) percentages of Uncertified Teachers 8% 5%"
