## Evidence

### Jayasinghe S 2016

Jayasinghe S. (2016). Describing complex clinical scenarios at the bed-side: Is a systems science approach useful? Exploring a novel diagrammatic approach to facilitate clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0787-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation with 11 postgraduate students (nine completed both sessions) who drew CRMs on a paper case and a real ward patient over two meetings, assessed by self-addressed questionnaire. The article reports "All found it useful or very useful" and unanimity on differential diagnosis and investigation selection. Perceptions only; no performance outcome was tested.

> "All found it useful or very useful to understand the interconnected nature of problems patients have, and pathogenesis of the illnesses and disorders."
