q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multivariate regression on NTPS/TFS data using the job and workplace satisfaction index (positive school environment, lack of stress and burnout, willingness to stay), reported in the report's summary of its multivariate results. The report prints "8.0% vs. 22.0%" predicted turnover.

"The predicted probability of turnover for teachers with high job and workplace satisfaction was less than half that of teachers with low job and workplace satisfaction (8.0% vs. 22.0%)."
