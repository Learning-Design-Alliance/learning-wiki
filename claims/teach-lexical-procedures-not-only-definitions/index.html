Evidence¶

Robinson 1988¶

Robinson, Peter J. (1988). Procedural and Declarative Knowledge in Vocabulary Learning: Communication and the Language Learner's Lexicon. Paper presented at the 22nd Annual TESOL Convention, Chicago. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED296585

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the paper's introduction: Robinson contends that traditional approaches concentrate on building a store of definitional meanings, and that 'a communicative view of the interactive nature of lexical negotiation' demands equal attention to procedures. No empirical data are offered; the support is conceptual.

"A communicative view of the interactive nature of lexical negotiation requires thatwe focus pedagogically as much on procedures as we do on the more narrowly defined declarative meanings which specialist words have."
