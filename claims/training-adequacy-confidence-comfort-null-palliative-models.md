### Mascio R 2026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null findings from the same Mann–Whitney comparison of combined model groups in the 21-nurse palliative sample; the article prints only Z statistics and p-values, with no effect sizes, so equivalence was not tested.

> "However, the combined groups did not differ significantly from each other in training adequacy (Z = 1.66, p = 0.12), confidence (Z = 0.09, p = 0.59), comfort (Z = 1.47, p = 0.11), or nursing experience (Z = 1.42, p = 0.10)."
