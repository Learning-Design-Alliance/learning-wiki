## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2020

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Development, validation and in-class evaluation of a sequence of clicker questions on Larmor precession of spin in quantum mechanics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In-class pre/posttest evaluation in two upper-level QM classes averaged over both classes (N=56). Pretest averages of "30-60% on the pretest" rose to posttest averages "exceeded 70% on the posttest (roughly 90% on Q# 1-3)" after the CQS with peer instruction.

> "On average, student performance on test questions range from 30-60% on the pretest, showing that there is much room for improvement after traditional instruction.  After the implementation of the CQS, average scores on test questions exceeded 70% on the posttest (roughly 90% on Q# 1-3)."
