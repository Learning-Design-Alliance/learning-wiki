## Evidence

### Michail Maria 2016

Michail Maria, Tait Lynda. (2016). Exploring general practitioners' views and experiences on suicide risk assessment and management of young people in primary care: a qualitative study in the UK. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009654

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the focus group findings on challenges in assessment and management, the authors report GPs' "general uncertainty among GPs about distinguishing between what they called 'truly suicidal behaviour' and a 'cry for help' in young people", with knock-on uncertainty about referrals. Qualitative data; no effect size printed.

> "Linked to this was a general uncertainty among GPs about distinguishing between what they called 'truly suicidal behaviour' and a 'cry for help' in young people. This meant they often felt uncertain about management options, that is, when to make a referral to secondary mental health services and how to appropriately respond to a 'cry for help' particularly when it comes to young people engaging in self-harming behaviour."
