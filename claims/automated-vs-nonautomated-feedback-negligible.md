### Vermylen JH 2026

Vermylen JH, Cohen ER, Cook DA, McGaghie WC, Issenberg SB, Arnold J, Ballard H, Bayoumi M, Beestrum M, Bremner R, Crawford S, Einstein N, Mannarino C, Misra A, Tomita TM, Waldron H, Yanko F, Kessler DO. `q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 7 studies (15%) comparing competency-based simulations with automated versus nonautomated feedback, using each study's best outcome. Inconsistency was high (I2 = 79%, effect sizes −1.38 to 1.07); no sensitivity analysis was conducted given the small number of studies.

> "Figure 4 shows the meta-analysis for the best outcome for the automated feedback group compared with the nonautomated feedback group with a pooled SMD of 0.11 (95% CI = −0.31 to 0.52, P = 0.62), a negligible effect favoring automated feedback"
