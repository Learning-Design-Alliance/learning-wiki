## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2015

Tianlong Zu, Elise Agra, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2015). Effect of visual cues and video solutions on conceptual tasks. 2015 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher's Exact Test comparing correctness rates of the three training conditions on the far transfer problem across both problem sets. The article reports "no significant differences between the three groups on the far transfer problem"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Finally, we found no significant differences between the three groups on the far transfer problem, χ2 (2, 66) = 2.5, p = .388."
