Evidence¶

Oudeng Jia 2025¶

Oudeng Jia, Qingsong Tan, Sihan Zhang, Ke Jia & Mengyuan Gong. (2025). The precision of attention selection during reward learning influences the mechanisms of value-driven attention. npj Science of Learning 10:49. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00342-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Controlled lab experiment (N = 40) with feature search training among similar distractors and horizontal/vertical target bars. The feature-match effect was "only a marginally signiﬁcant effect of reward (F(2, 78) = 2.92, p =0 . 0 6 0" and relation-match t-tests were non-significant; no effect size was printed for the null relational result.

"a one-way repeated-measures ANOVA (reward history: high-, low- vs. no-reward distractor) on RTs from the feature-match condition (Fig. 3b, middle) revealed only a marginally signi ﬁcant effect of reward (F(2, 78) = 2.92, p =0 . 0 6 0 ,ηp 2 = 0.070). Paired t-test analyses on RTs from the relation-match condition (Fig.3c, middle) revealed no signiﬁcant differences between reward conditions"
