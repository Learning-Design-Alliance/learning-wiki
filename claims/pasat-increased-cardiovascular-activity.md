### Ginty 2022

Ginty, A. T., Tyra, A. T., Young, D. A., Brindle, R. C., de Rooij, S. R. & Williams, S. E. (2022). Cardiovascular reactions to acute psychological stress and academic achievement. Psychophysiology, 59, e14064. https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.14064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Manipulation check in the laboratory phase: repeated-measures ANOVAs on baseline and task values. Table 2 reports, for example, HR F = 415.13, p < .001, η² = 0.759, and participants rated the task stressful (M = 4.53) and difficult (M = 4.23).
