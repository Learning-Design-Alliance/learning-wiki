## Evidence

### Kashikar 2025

Kashikar, L., Lüke, T., & Grosche, M. (2025). Effects of Diagnostic Labels for Students With Learning Problems on Teachers' Stereotypes and Performance Expectations. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194251315187

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the four-item intention-to-foster-performance scale in the 413-teacher vignette experiment; the label-group main effect was significant, ηp2 = .021, with the LG2–LG3 post hoc contrast at p = .025. The teaching profession main effect was also significant, ηp2 = .015, with SETs intending to foster more than GETs.

> "We found a significant effect of the LG, ηp2 = .021. The post hoc test showed a significant difference between LG2 and LG3, p = .025. This means teachers who remembered the SLN label intended to foster performance more than teachers who remembered the LD label."
