Evidence¶

Tsompanoudi et al. 2015¶

Despina TSOMPANOUDI, Maya SATRATZEMI, Stelios XINOGALOS. (2015). Distributed Pair Programming Using Collaboration Scripts: An Educational System and Initial Results. Informatics in Education. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.17

q2 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation questionnaires completed by the 48 students at the final meeting. The article reports 94% believe DPP can help students overcome difficulties in learning programming, and that students "found less useful the chat functionality and hints (M = 3.47, SD = 1.30 and M = 3.33, SD = 1.13 respectively)".

"As an overall experience DPP was rated with an average score of 4.40 (SD = 0.58) on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good)."
