# Two-way, genuine district-teacher communication is associated with more effective TPE implementation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviewees report implementation is more effective where communication is two-way, and that top-down communication reduces buy-in and frames TPE as compliance. [→ Slotnik 2015](#slotnik-2015)

## Evidence

### Slotnik 2015

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Key leader interviews with 20 stakeholders in 12 districts plus focus groups and case-study interviews. The report adds that where communication appears top-down, “there is less buy-in from school site practitioners and TPE is often perceived as a compliance activity.”

> "Where the teacher/district communication is two-way and considered genuine and respectful, interviewees indicate that implementation is more effective."
