## Evidence

### Walensky Rochelle P. 2018

Walensky Rochelle P., Kim Younji, Chang Yuchiao, Porneala Bianca C., Bristol Mirar N., Armstrong Katrina, Campbell Eric G. (2018). The impact of active mentorship: results from a survey of faculty in the Department of Medicine at Massachusetts General Hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1191-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression predicting currently having a mentor from the faculty survey (64.9% reported currently having a mentor). Bivariately, 89.3% of investigator-pathway faculty versus 61.7% of non-investigator faculty reported a current mentor (p < 0.001).

> "Similar results were demonstrated for those on the non-investigator pathways (OR 0.15, 95% CI: 0.08–0.27, p < 0.001) in that they had over a 6-fold decreased odds of currently having a mentor, compared to those on the investigator pathway"
