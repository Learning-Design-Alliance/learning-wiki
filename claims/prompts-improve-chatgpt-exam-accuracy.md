## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Subgroup 2 meta-analysis found pooled accuracy with prompts of 68% (GPT-3.5) and 85% (GPT-4) versus 54% and 79% without prompts, both significant; meta-regression coefficients were 0.54 (P=.01) for GPT-3.5 and 0.46 (P=.02) for GPT-4. [→ Mingxin Liu 2024](#mingxin-liu-2024)

Subgroup 2 meta-analysis comparing studies with and without prompts, random-effects models.
