### Jiang Qinxu 2022

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 40-participant interview study; classmates were described as 'family', helpers, study partners and information sources about exams and learning materials.

> "Seventy-eight percent of the participants considered that having classmates around created both a competitive and a collaborative learning environment in which they were motivated to study."

### Jiang Qinxu 2022 (2)

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same interview study, language barriers and social grouping between African and Indian students limited interaction across nationalities.

> "Some participants (10%) stated that their classmates could be discouraging when they interacted, disturbed others by talking and making noises, created negative competition, or made others feel less intelligent."
