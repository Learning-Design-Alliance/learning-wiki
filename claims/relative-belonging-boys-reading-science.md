## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Among boys, a 1-point increase in relative sense of belonging above the boys' school average is associated with 8.54 higher reading (p < .001, ES = 0.10) and 6.71 higher science scores (p < .01, ES = 0.08). [→ Zhao Yushan 2025](#zhao-yushan-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhao Yushan 2025

Zhao Yushan, Gee Kevin A., Yuan Caleb W. (2025). Relative Sense of Belonging and the Academic Achievement of Chinese Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02287-5

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Gender-stratified two-level HLM fitted to the boys-only PISA 2018 sample (n = 6,283 unweighted), with random slopes for relative belonging. The article reports "effect sizes of 0.10 and 0.08, respectively" for reading and science.

> "a 1 point increase in boy's relative sense of belonging above the school average belonging score for boys in the same school is associated with higher reading scores by 8.54 points (p < .001) and science scores by 6.71 points (p < .01), representing effect sizes of 0.10 and 0.08, respectively"
