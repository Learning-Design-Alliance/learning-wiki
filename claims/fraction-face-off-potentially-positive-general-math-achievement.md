# Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement, with a WWC-calculated effect size of 0.64 and improvement index of +24 on NAEP selected items

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, Fraction Face-Off! produced a positive and statistically significant effect on general mathematics achievement measured by NAEP selected items (effect size 0.64, improvement index +24, 1,152 students). [→ naep-1](#naep-1)

## Evidence

### naep-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, March). Fraction Face-Off!. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/702

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Findings from the one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, using a collection of 19 researcher-selected items from the 1990-2009 NAEP fourth-grade assessment with 1,152 fourth-grade students. The intervention group mean was 14.83 versus 12.36 for comparison, effect size 0.64, p < .01.
