## Evidence

### Alabi Oludare 2022

Alabi Oludare, Hill Rhodri, Walsh Michael, Carroll Camilla. (2022). Introduction of an ENT emergency—safe boot camp into postgraduate surgical training in the Republic of Ireland. Irish Journal of Medical Science, 191(1). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-021-02581-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post reflective questionnaire completed by all participants of the RCSI boot camp; descriptive medians only, no statistical test reported. The article states there was "a greater increase in self-assessed competence scores" for post-tonsillectomy bleeds.

> "Trainees’ self-assessment based on the reflective questionnaire showed an increase in median confidence scores from 3/5 to 4/5 for epistaxis and post-thyroidectomy bleeds. There was a greater increase in self-assessed competence scores from 2/5 to 4/5 for the management of post-tonsillectomy bleeds."
