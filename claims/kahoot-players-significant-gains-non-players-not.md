### Meg Foster 2023

Meg Foster, Philip von Doetinchem, and Sandra von Doetinchem. (2023). Virtual physics laboratory courses: An evaluation of students' self-efficacy and intelligence mindset. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey study of a remote introductory physics laboratory course comparing matched pre/post modified CLASS scores by Kahoot! participation group. The Players group "had positive and significant gains (p = 0.002)" while Non-Players' change was not statistically significant (p = 0.18). No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Further analysis revealed that the Players group had positive and significant gains (p = 0.002), while the Non-Players did not. Though the Non-Players did show improvement in mean self-efficacy as a group, the diﬀerence in means from pre- to post-survey was not statistically signiﬁcant ( p = 0.18)."
