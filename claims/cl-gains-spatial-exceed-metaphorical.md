## Evidence

### Hung 2018

Hung, B. P., Vien, T., & Vu, N. N. (2018). Applying Cognitive Linguistics to Teaching English Prepositions: A Quasi-Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 327-346. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11323a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group comparison of pretest-to-posttest gains by semantic type in the same 50-student quasi-experiment. The article reports "the cognitive treatment was more valid in teaching the spatial meanings than metaphorical meanings" and that TRAD's metaphorical improvement was insignificant.

> "COG's mean scores for the spatial meanings and metaphorical meanings rose by 4.36 and 2.60 respectively, but those of TRAD rose by only 0.24 and 1.84."
