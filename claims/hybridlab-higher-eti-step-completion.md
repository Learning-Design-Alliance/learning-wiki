## Evidence

### Mankute A 2022

Mankute A, Juozapaviciene L, Stucinskas J, Dambrauskas Z, Dobozinskas P, Sinz E, Rodgers DL, Giedraitis M, Vaitkaitis D. (2022). A novel algorithm-driven hybrid simulation learning method to improve acquisition of endotracheal intubation skills: a randomized controlled study. BMC Anesthesiology 22:42. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01557-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study with 77 participants (34 conventional, 43 hybrid) evaluated on real patients in the OR with a 16-step checklist by a blinded anesthesiologist. The Results section reports completion was "significantly better performed by the subjects in experimental group".

> "Completion of all the actions of endotracheal intubation were significantly better performed by the subjects in experimental group when compared to those from the control group."

### Mankute A 2022 (2)

Mankute A, Juozapaviciene L, Stucinskas J, Dambrauskas Z, Dobozinskas P, Sinz E, Rodgers DL, Giedraitis M, Vaitkaitis D. (2022). A novel algorithm-driven hybrid simulation learning method to improve acquisition of endotracheal intubation skills: a randomized controlled study. BMC Anesthesiology 22:42. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01557-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 2 reports the overall checklist score as a median of 53% (IQR 23%) in the conventional group versus 92% (IQR 13%) in the hybrid group, with p < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Overall Median (interquartile range) 53% (23%) 92% (13%) < 0.001"
