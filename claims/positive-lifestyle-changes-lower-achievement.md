The SEM found positive lifestyle changes were "significantly and weakly associated with academic achievement, although in the opposite direction expected" (β = −0.08, p < 0.001). The authors suggest children who benefited from the pandemic may have been those less strongly oriented toward school.

> "lifestyle changes were significantly and weakly associated with academic achievement, although in the opposite direction expected (β = −0.08, p < 0.001). More specifically, positive lifestyle changes predicted lower academic achievement postcrisis."
