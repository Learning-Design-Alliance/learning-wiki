## Evidence

### Baten 2020

Baten, E., Vansteenkiste, M., De Muynck, G., De Poortere, E., Desoete, A. (2020). How Can the Blow of Math Difficulty on Elementary School Children's Motivational, Cognitive and Affective Experiences be Dampened? The Critical Role of Autonomy-Supportive Instructions. Simple-effects follow-ups within the autonomy-supportive condition: the difficulty difference in autonomy frustration was absent (p = .999; ηp² = .00), while competence satisfaction (ηp² = .08) and competence frustration (ηp² = .03) differences remained, though minimal.

> "Specifically, when the exercises were introduced in an autonomy -supportive way, the 24 difference between the difficult and easy-medium exercises in autonomy frustration was absent 25 22 (p = .999; ηp ² = .00), while being minimal in the case of competence satisfaction (p < .001; ηp ² 1 = .08), and competence frustration  (p = .010; ηp ² = .03)."
