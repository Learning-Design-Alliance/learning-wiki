## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Binnie Rebecca S. L. 2021

Binnie Rebecca S. L., Bonsor Stephen J. (2021). The implementation of a flipped classroom approach at a UK dental school. British Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41415-021-3125-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Service evaluation comparing student course evaluation form (SCEF) comments before and after the flipped classroom transition in the ADM&B course at Aberdeen. Comments fell into four categories: "teaching delivery method, teaching time, accessibility to information and content". No numerical scores were collected before 2019/20.

> "Regarding teaching delivery method, prior to the change, more students commented that they were dissatisfied than satisfied. Following the change, four times the number of students commented that they were satisfied rather than being dissatisfied."
