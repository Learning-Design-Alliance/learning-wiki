# Hybrid PBL+LBL pedagogy yields significantly higher comprehensive ability scores than LBL alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students taught with PBL+LBL scored significantly higher on comprehensive ability measures than students taught with LBL alone (RR = 9.13, 95% CI 8.42–9.84, P < .00001). [→ Liu CX 2020](#liu-cx-2020)

## Evidence

### Liu CX 2020

Liu CX, Ouyang WW, Wang XW, Chen D, Jiang ZL. (2020). Comparing hybrid problem-based and lecture learning (PBL + LBL) with LBL pedagogy on clinical curriculum learning for medical students in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000019687

`q3 · i?`

Meta-analysis of the 8 studies reporting the comprehensive ability score, analyzed with a random effects model (I2 = 97%). The pooled relative risk favored PBL+LBL: "RR = 9.13, 95% CI: 8.42∼9.84".

> "As shown by meta-analysis results, the comprehensive ability performance of students was significantly superior in PBL+LBL group to in LBL group (RR = 9.13, 95% CI: 8.42∼9.84, P < .00001)"
