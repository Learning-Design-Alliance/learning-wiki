`q2 i0` Across effects reported both ways, the correlation between OE–CE correlations and OE–CE standardized mean differences was small and non-significant. [→ Breuer Sonja 2023](#breuer-sonja-2023)

## Evidence

### Breuer Sonja 2023

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q2 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A secondary correlational analysis in Meta-Analyses B across 39 effects found "a small non-significant relation of −0.03"; equivalence or independence was not tested.

> "The Pearson's product-moment correlation between 39 effects that were available as both standardized mean differences and as correlation coefficients revealed a small non-significant relation of −0.03."
