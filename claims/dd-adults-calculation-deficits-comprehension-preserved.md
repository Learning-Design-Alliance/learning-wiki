# Arithmetic deficits in adults with DD concentrate in mental calculation, arithmetical rules, and written calculation, with number comprehension and transcoding preserved

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i0`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Adults with DD scored significantly lower than controls in total mental calculation (r = -0.614, p < 0.001) and total written calculation (r = -0.500, p < 0.001). [→ Vigna Giulia 2022](#vigna-giulia-2022)
`q3 i0` The two groups did not differ in number comprehension (r = -0.082, p = 0.278) or transcoding. [→ Vigna Giulia 2022 (2)](#vigna-giulia-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Vigna Giulia 2022

Vigna Giulia, Ghidoni Enrico, Burgio Francesca, Danesin Laura, Angelini Damiano, Benavides-Varela Silvia, Semenza Carlo. (2022). Dyscalculia in Early Adulthood: Implications for Numerical Activities of Daily Living. Brain Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci12030373

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison on the NADL Formal mental calculation subtest (26 DD vs 26 controls): DD mean 15.15 (SD 1.67) versus 17.23 (SD 1.14) for controls, effect size "−0.614", p < 0.001. Written calculation showed a similar significant difference (r = −0.500).
