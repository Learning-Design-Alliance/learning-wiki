Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study with 30 experimental and 30 control participants (independent samples t test on posttest). Posttest means were 42.27 (experimental) and 33.60 (control); the article reports "a significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups on grammar posttest." No effect size is printed.
