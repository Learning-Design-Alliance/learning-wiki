### Sonja Cwik 2022

Sonja Cwik, Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Role of inclusiveness of learning environment in predicting students' outcomes in courses in which women are not underrepresented. [doi:10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666](https://doi.org/10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis via multi-group SEM with Likelihood Ratio Tests across measurement-invariance levels, followed by mediation analysis with gender as a direct predictor. The article reports "Fig. 2 shows that gender does not directly predict any of the outcomes." No effect size is printed for the null moderation.

> "There were no group differences at the level of weak and strong measurement invariance at the level of regression coefficients, so we proceeded with mediation analysis"
