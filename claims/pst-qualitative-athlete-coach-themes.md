### Miçooğullari and Kirazci 2016

Bülent Okan Miçooğullari, Sadettin Kirazci. (2016). Effects of 6 Weeks Psychological Skill Training on Team Cohesion, Self-confidence & Anxiety: A Case of Youth Basketball Players. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(12): 2761-2768. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.041210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative follow-up interviews with six purposively selected athletes and the coach, analyzed via constant comparison with open, axial, and selective coding; themes supported the quantitative cohesion results. Athletes also reported benefits in studies, relations, and life generally.

> "According to results of athletes’ two important themes affirmed to improve team cohesi on perceptions , those themes were “spending time with team members at outside of the trainings and matches” and “helpful practices to know each other better”."
