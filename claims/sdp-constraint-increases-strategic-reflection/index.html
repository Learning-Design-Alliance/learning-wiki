Evidence¶

Hossein Talebzadeh 2025¶

Hossein Talebzadeh. (2025). The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Green Education: Optimizing Teacher Workflow and Enhancing Pedagogical Design under Sustainable Development Pedagogy (SDP) Constraints. 3rd National Conference on Green Industries Innovation, University of Mazandaran. https://conf.umz.ac.ir/gii doi:10.35542/osf.io/x6qzy_v1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired t-test on the Strategic Reflection index (cognitive depth in navigating pedagogical rigor plus sustainability, including rationales for AI prompt engineering choices) for the 28 teams. Table 2 reports Baseline M = 2.86 (SD 0.50) versus SDP M = 4.66 (SD 0.52), t(27) = 15.97, p < 0.001, d = 3.40. The authors interpret this as educators adopting systemic thinking patterns.

"the substantial advancement in Strategic Reflection validates that resolving the dual problem matrix (reconciling pedagogical complexity with strict environmental sustainability requirements) successfully forced educators to adopt advanced systemic and structural thinking patterns"
