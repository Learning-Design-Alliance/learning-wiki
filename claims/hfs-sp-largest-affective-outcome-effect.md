### Kim Junghee 2016

Kim Junghee, Park Jin-Hwa, Shin Sujin. (2016). Effectiveness of simulation-based nursing education depending on fidelity: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0672-7

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of affective learning outcomes by fidelity level in the 40-study meta-analysis (Table 4). The affective effect sizes were HFS ES = 0.80 and SP ES = 0.73; the results section reports "HFS (0.80) and SP (0.73) had the largest effect sizes" for the affective domain, with LFS and hybrid smaller.

> "Regarding affective outcome, HFS (0.80) and SP (0.73) had the largest effect sizes, whereas LFS (0.39) and hybrid (0.35) simulation studies had smaller effect sizes."
