# The relationship between time on task and posttest score differs by condition: essentially uncorrelated for Proof Blocks but moderately correlated for hybrid and written-proof activities

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For the Proof Blocks activity, time on task and posttest score were essentially uncorrelated (r = 0.10, p = 0.32). [→ Seth Poulsen 2023](#seth-poulsen-2023)

## Evidence

### Seth Poulsen 2023

Seth Poulsen, Yael Gertner, Benjamin Cosman, Matthew West, and Geoffrey L. Herman. (2023). Efficiency of Learning from Proof Blocks Versus Writing Proofs. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1145/3545945.3569797](https://doi.org/10.1145/3545945.3569797)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis within the randomized controlled trial, visualized in Figure 4. For the Proof Blocks condition time and posttest score were "essentially uncorrelated" (r = 0.10); correlations were r = 0.64 (hybrid, p < 0.001) and r = 0.51 (written proofs, p < 0.001).

> "time spent on the activity and posttest score were essentially uncorrelated (𝑝 = 0.32, 𝑟 = 0.10, 95% CI [−0.09, 0.28])"
