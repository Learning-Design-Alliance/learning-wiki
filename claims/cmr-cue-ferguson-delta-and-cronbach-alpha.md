## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Validation and analysis of the coupled multiple response Colorado upper-division electrostatics (CUE) diagnostic. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Whole-test discrimination analysis of the score distribution (N=421), which spanned from a minimum of 4.3% to a maximum of 92.5%, showing students well distributed across the range of possible scores.

> "For the full population of students, Ferguson's Delta is 0.99. Delta can take on values between [0,1] and anything above 0.9 indicates good discriminator power [2]."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Validation and analysis of the coupled multiple response Colorado upper-division electrostatics (CUE) diagnostic. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal-consistency analysis using Cronbach's Alpha as a conservative measure, since it assumes a unidimensional assessment and multidimensionality tends to drive alpha downwards. The CMR CUE demonstrates an acceptable level of internal consistency.

> "For our population of students, we calculate a value of α = 0 .82. For a test used for low-stakes testing of individuals rather than just groups, the commonly accepted threshold is α > 0.8 [10]."
