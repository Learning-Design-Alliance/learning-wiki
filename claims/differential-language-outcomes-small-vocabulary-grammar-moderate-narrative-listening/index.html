Rogde K 2019¶

Rogde K, Hagen ÅM, Melby‐Lervåg M, Lervåg A. (2019). The effect of linguistic comprehension instruction on generalized language and reading comprehension skills: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1059

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of the 43 included studies comparing effect sizes across language outcome domains. The review reports "small effects on vocabulary and grammatical knowledge" and "moderate effects on narrative and listening comprehension"; no numeric effect sizes for these domain contrasts are printed in the available text.

"Analysis of differential language outcomes shows small effects on vocabulary and grammatical knowledge and moderate effects on narrative and listening comprehension."
