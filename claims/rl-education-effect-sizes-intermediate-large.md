`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's effect-size analysis (Fig. 9) covers Cohen's d and 95% confidence intervals for post-test score comparisons on learning-related outcomes, restricted to "live" evaluations; for seven papers, effect sizes were calculated from descriptive values using Lenhard and Lenhard's online calculator.

> "We identified 15 papers to be suitable for our overview of effect sizes (see Fig. 9). It is notable that a majority of publications finding significant differences reported at least one intermediate (n = 5) or large effect (n = 6)."
