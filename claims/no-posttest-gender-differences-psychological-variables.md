## Evidence

### Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza 2026

Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza, Weldmeskel Fisseha Mikre. (2026). Gender differences in causal attributions, self-regulation, achievement motivation, and academic performance following an intervention. BMC Psychology, 14, 588. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04357-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing males (n = 37) and females (n = 47) at post-test across 18 psychological subscales found all p-values above .05. The authors note "small effects favoring females" with Cohen's d values from approximately −0.20 to −0.31 on several variables.

> "there are no statistically significant gender differences across any of the post-test psychological variables examined, as all p-values exceed the 0.05 threshold"
