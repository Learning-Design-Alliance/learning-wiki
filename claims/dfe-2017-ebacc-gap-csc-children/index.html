Jay Matthew A. 2019¶

q2 · i? · review · r3

Descriptive cross-sectional statistics from Department for Education annual statistical releases, cited in the review's background as motivation. The review quotes the percentages directly; these are descriptive figures with no adjusted comparison.

"in 2017, just 2.7% of children who had been looked after for 12 months or more and 3.9% of CiN achieved the English Baccalaureate (i.e. General Certificates of Secondary Education [GCSEs] in English language and literature, maths, science, geography or history, and a language; GCSEs are sat at age 15/16 at the end of secondary schooling) compared to 21.9% of the general population."
