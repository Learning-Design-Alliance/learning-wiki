Merging Cognitive Science and Curriculum to Strengthen Middle School Math 2014¶

Merging Cognitive Science and Curriculum to Strengthen Middle School Math. (2014). WestEd R&D Alert, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559573

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A 2012 study involving 120 teachers at 87 schools in 17 states randomly assigned each teacher to provide data from one original and one revised middle school math unit, measuring pre- and post-test math scores. The article reports only that trends favor the redesigned curriculum; no statistics are printed.

"Data analysis is still underway, says Davenport, but “we are seeing trends going in the expected direction,” in favor of the redesigned curriculum."
