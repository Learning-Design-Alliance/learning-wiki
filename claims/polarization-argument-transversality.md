### Lior M. Burko 2008

Lior M. Burko. (2008). Transversality of Electromagnetic Waves in the Calculus-Based Introductory Physics Course. https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2993 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0808.2993](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0808.2993)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Physical argument (Section II.A) based on the standard two-polaroid demonstration: a longitudinal component would be transmitted through both polaroids even when orthogonal. A variant considering oscillating brightness as polarizers rotate was given by Schutz. The argument requires students to understand qualitatively how wire-grid polaroids work.

> "Therefore, with a longitudinal component the screen will never get totally dark when the two polaroids are in a plane perpendicular to the direction of propagation of the light."
