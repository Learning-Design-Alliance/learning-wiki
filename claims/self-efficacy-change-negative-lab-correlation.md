## Evidence

### Smaragdi Loutou 2018

Smaragdi Loutou, Nikolaos Tselios, Panagiota Altanopoulou. (2018). The effect of twitter-mediated activities on learning outcome and student engagement: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis relating change in the 19-item internet self-efficacy measure to final laboratory grade in the 17-student case study. The article reports "a negative correlation between variables", with Pearson's r = -0.48, p = 0.05, interpreting that students already highly skilled showed little change and performed well.

> "Interestingly, there is a negative correlation between variables (Pearson's r = -0,48, p = 0,05, s)."
