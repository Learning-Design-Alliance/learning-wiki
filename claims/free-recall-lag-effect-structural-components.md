## Evidence

### Glenberg 1979

Glenberg, A. M. (1979). Component-levels theory of the effects of spacing of repetitions on recall and recognition. Memory & Cognition, 7(2), 95-112. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03197590

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports Glenberg's (1977) free-recall comparison of subjects encouraged versus prevented from forming associations among list items, with lags out to 17. The review interprets the attenuated lag effect as elimination of differential storage of structural components. No effect sizes are printed.

> "As predicted, the subjects eneouraged to form associations produced a strong lag effeet in reeall. The other group showed an increase in reeall from Lag 2 to Lag 5, and then no improvement (out to Lag 17)."
