## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of individual (N=25, avg 4.21, sd 0.57) and group (N=12 groups, avg 4.67, sd 0.59) PCK scores out of a maximum of 6.70; the t-test shows the difference "statistically significant (p=0.033)" with Cohen's d of 0.78.

> "A t-test indicates that the difference in performance is statistically significant (p=0.033). In addition, calculation of Cohen‟s d gives a reasonable effect size of 0.78."

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013 (2)

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 74 instances in which two or three graduate students who had not all selected the same answer individually converged to one answer in the group task; in 45 instances (61%) they "selected the incorrect answer which was more common among introductory students".

> "In 45 of those instances (61%), they selected the incorrect answer which was more common among introductory students who did not know the correct answer."
