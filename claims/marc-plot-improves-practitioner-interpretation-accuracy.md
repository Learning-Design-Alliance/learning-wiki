# In a pre-registered experiment, visualization type significantly affected education practitioners' accuracy in interpreting meta-analytic evidence, with the MARC plot outperforming bar plots, forest plots, and rainforest plots

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Visualization type (Factor A) is a significant source of variation in practitioners' correctness scores, and the MARC plot yielded the highest average score (5.3 of 7 correct). [→ Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022](#kaitlyn-g-fitzgerald-and-elizabeth-tipton-2022)
`q2 i?` In Tukey pairwise comparisons, the MARC plot outperformed the bar plot by an estimated difference of 0.49 and the forest and rainforest plots by 0.85 each, all statistically significant; BP-FP and BP-RFP differences were not significant. [→ Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022 (2)](#kaitlyn-g-fitzgerald-and-elizabeth-tipton-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022

Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton. (2022). The Meta-Analytic Rain Cloud Plot: A New Approach to Visualizing Clearinghouse Data. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2022.2031366

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-registered blocked factorial online experiment (177 eligible respondents: 83 practitioners, 94 researchers); practitioners answered 7 correctness questions for each of four plot types. The article reports "visualization type (Factor A) is a significant source of variation in participant scores."

> "ANOVA results for Research Question 2 are given in Table 6, which indicate that visualization type (Factor A) is a significant source of variation in participant scores."

### Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton 2022 (2)

Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald and Elizabeth Tipton. (2022). The Meta-Analytic Rain Cloud Plot: A New Approach to Visualizing Clearinghouse Data. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2022.2031366

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Tukey pairwise comparisons of the four visualization means (Table 7): "The MARC plot performed better than BP with an estimated difference of 0.49 and better than FP and RFP with an estimated difference of 0.85 each." Adjusted p-values for MARC contrasts were 0.029 (MARC-BP) and 0.000 (MARC-FP, MARC-RFP).

> "The MARC plot performed better than BP with an estimated difference of 0.49 and better than FP and RFP with an estimated difference of 0.85 each."
