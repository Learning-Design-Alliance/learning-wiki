## Evidence

### Chang Hui-Ping 2025

Chang Hui-Ping, Lin Tzu-Wen, Chiu Yi-Hsiang, Chou Chien-Chih, Chen Jui-Fu, Pan Cheng-Chen. (2025). Associations of physical fitness with sustained overt attention and academic performance in children with learning disabilities. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19549

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional sample of 71 children with LD; higher DAUF scores indicate poorer sustained attention (longer reaction times), and the printed correlations are "r = −.51 & −.43, p < .01".

> "Zero-order correlations show that DAUF sustained attention is moderately and significantly negatively correlated with Chinese language and mathematics (r = −.51 & −.43, p < .01)."
