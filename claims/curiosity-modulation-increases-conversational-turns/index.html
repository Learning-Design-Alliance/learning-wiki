Engagement-depth analysis (§5.1) across 270 conversations reported in Table 2: overall mean turns 6.66 (Baseline) vs 16.12 (CURIOBOT), with per-condition means ranging from 4.74 to 20.00. Descriptive means reported; no inferential test statistic printed for this contrast.
