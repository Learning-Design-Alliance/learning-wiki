## Evidence

### Ablordeppey E 2025

Ablordeppey E, Terian E, Murray CT, Wallace L, Huang W, Blustein E, Croft A, Romo E, Agarwal M, Theodoro D. (2025). Low Frequency, High Complexity: Assessing Skill Decay in Transesophageal Echocardiography Post-Simulation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.35857

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre/post natural experiment with seven rTEE-naive emergency physicians; unadvertised simulator assessment after a six-month COVID-19 washout with no practice or clinical use. The article reports "a 41% decay" with mean 19 of 32 (SD ±7), P < .001.

> "At month 6, the mean score was 19 of 32 (SD ±7), reflecting a 41% decay (95% confidence interval (CI) −54%, −27%; P < .001) for eight standard rTEE views."
