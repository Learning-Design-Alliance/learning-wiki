## Evidence

### Tim Kühl 2025

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 1 (126 university students, Klepsch et al. scales, α = 0.84 ECL and α = 0.67 GCL) found "neither differences between conditions" on either scale, with η2p = 0.006 and 0.005.

> "Concerning cognitive load, one-factorial ANOVAs with the factor instructor presence revealed neither differences between conditions for the ECL-scale, F < 1, p = 0.696, η2p = 0.006, nor for the GCL-scale, F < 1, p = 0.723, η2p = 0.005."

### Tim Kühl 2025 (2)

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 2 (219 Prolific participants, two-item ECL and GCL scales, α = 0.66 and 0.79) again showed "neither differences between conditions", with η2p = 0.014 and 0.002.

> "Concerning cognitive load, a one-factorial ANOVA with the factor instructor presence revealed neither differences between conditions for the ECL-scale, F(2, 216) = 1.58, p = 0.208, η2p = 0.014, nor for the GCL-scale, F < 1, p = 0.817, η2p = 0.002."
