### Melnick 2017

Melnick, H., Cook-Harvey, C. M., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2017). Encouraging social and emotional learning in the context of new accountability. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. http://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/sel-new-accountability

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report cites a 2011 meta-analysis of 213 SEL programs reporting positive effects on social competence, behavior, and academics; in the 37 achievement studies, students showed "an 11 percentile-point improvement in academic performance" on grades and test scores. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In an oft-cited 2011 meta-analysis of 213 SEL programs that focused on different aspects of SEL, researchers found that these programs had positive effects on students' social competence, behavior, and academics."
