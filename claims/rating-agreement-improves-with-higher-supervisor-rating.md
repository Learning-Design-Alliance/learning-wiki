## Evidence

### Castanelli Damian J 2024

Castanelli Damian J, Woods Jennifer B, Chander Anusha R, Weller Jennifer M. (2024). Trainee anaesthetist self-assessment using an entrustment scale in workplace-based assessment. Anaesthesia and Intensive Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/0310057X241234676

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlational analysis in the results section of 113 pairs found "a significant negative correlation, r = −0.35 (95% CI −0.50 to −0.17)" between rating difference and supervisor rating; the authors interpret this as self-assessment improving with expertise.

> "We did find a significant negative correlation, r = −0.35 (95% CI −0.50 to −0.17) between the difference in ratings and supervisor rating, supporting a higher correlation in ratings as trainee expertise increased."

### Castanelli Damian J 2024 (2)

Castanelli Damian J, Woods Jennifer B, Chander Anusha R, Weller Jennifer M. (2024). Trainee anaesthetist self-assessment using an entrustment scale in workplace-based assessment. Anaesthesia and Intensive Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/0310057X241234676

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square testing in the results section compared over-rating frequency across supervisor-rating groups: 26% of trainees with supervisor ratings below the mean rated themselves higher versus 8.45% when the supervisor rating was above the mean; "χ2 (2, N = 113) = 8.67, P = 0.013".

> "A higher-than-expected percentage of trainees with supervisor ratings below the mean rated themselves higher than their supervisors, and this result is statistically significant, χ2 (2, N = 113) = 8.67, P = 0.013."
