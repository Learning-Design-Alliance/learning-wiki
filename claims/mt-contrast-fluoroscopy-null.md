## Evidence

### Crossley Robert 2019

Crossley Robert, Liebig Thomas, Holtmannspoetter Markus, Lindkvist Johan, Henn Pat, Lonn Lars, Gallagher Anthony Gerald. (2019). Validation studies of virtual reality simulation performance metrics for mechanical thrombectomy in ischemic stroke. Journal of Neurointerventional Surgery 11(8):775-780. https://doi.org/10.1136/neurintsurg-2018-014510

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null result from the same VIST construct validity study: trainees used "11% more contrast agent" (median 40 vs 36 mL), reported as "not statistically significant (P=0.46)". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Although the trainees used 11% more contrast agent (consultant median=36 (25th PR=29 and 75th PR=39) vs trainee median=40 (25th PR=29 and 75th PR=77) this difference was not statistically significant (P=0.46)."

### Crossley Robert 2019 (2)

Crossley Robert, Liebig Thomas, Holtmannspoetter Markus, Lindkvist Johan, Henn Pat, Lonn Lars, Gallagher Anthony Gerald. (2019). Validation studies of virtual reality simulation performance metrics for mechanical thrombectomy in ischemic stroke. Journal of Neurointerventional Surgery 11(8):775-780. https://doi.org/10.1136/neurintsurg-2018-014510

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null result from the same VIST construct validity study: consultants used "24% less fluoroscopy than the trainees" (median 11 vs 15 min), reported as not statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The consultant group also used 24% less fluoroscopy than the trainees but this difference was not statistically significant (consultant median=11 (25th PR=10 and 75th PR=12) vs trainee mean=15 (25th PR=11 and 75th PR=22)."
