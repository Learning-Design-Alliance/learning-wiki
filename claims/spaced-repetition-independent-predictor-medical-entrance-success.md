## Evidence

### Burel Julien 2025

Burel Julien, Trost Olivier, Demeyere Matthieu, Rives Nathalie, Estour François, Ladner Joel, Crampon Frédéric, Deneuve Sophie, Maquet Charles. (2025). Spaced repetition and other key factors influencing medical school entrance exam success: insights from a French survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07605-w

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective single-center cohort survey of 523 candidates (84.6% of 618) at the University of Rouen; multivariate logistic regression identified independent predictors. The article reports "a statistically significant relationship was found between success and spaced repetition" with adjusted Odds Ratio = 2.09 (95%CI = 1.26–3.48).

> "In logistic regression, a statistically significant relationship was found between success and spaced repetition (adjusted Odds Ratio = 2.09; 95%CI = 1.26–3.48; P = 0.01)."
