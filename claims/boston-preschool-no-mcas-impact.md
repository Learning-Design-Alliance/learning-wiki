## Evidence

### Gray-Lobe 2021

Gray-Lobe, G., Pathak, P. A., & Walters, C. R. (2021). The Long-Term Effects of Universal Preschool in Boston. NBER Working Paper No. 28756. http://www.nber.org/papers/w28756

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

2SLS estimates from Table 6 using MCAS scores standardized to mean zero and standard deviation one among all Massachusetts test-takers in each grade and year, finding "no statistically detectable impact on these achievement levels". Non-offered applicants' elementary scores are around −0.3σ to −0.4σ; the stacked model rules out impacts larger than 0.14σ in Math and 0.12σ in ELA.

> "we find that preschool attendance has no statistically detectable impact on these achievement levels. This result is consistent with Weiland et al. (2019) who report that attendance at a first-choice BPS preschool did not affect third-grade MCAS performance for cohorts applying between 2007 and 2011"
