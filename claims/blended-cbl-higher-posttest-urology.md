## Evidence

### Zhu P 2025

Zhu P, Jiang B, Lu R, Wang B, He Y, Yang K, Zhang Q, Zhao P, Huang B. (2025). A competency-based instructional design: A progressive blended case-based teaching method. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043920

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 258 fifth-year clinical medical students assigned to progressive blended case-based teaching (n = 123) or traditional lectures (n = 135) for a 4-week urology module. "post-test scores were significantly higher in the experimental group" with means 85.6 ± 5.0 vs 81.3 ± 5.2, P < .001; no effect size printed.

> "In contrast, post-test scores were significantly higher in the experimental group (85.6 ± 5.0) than in the control group (81.3 ± 5.2) (P < .001)."
