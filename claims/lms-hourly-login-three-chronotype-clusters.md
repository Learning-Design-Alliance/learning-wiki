## Evidence

### Mandalapu 2022

Mandalapu, V., Chen, L. K., Shetty, S., Chen, Z., & Gong, J. (2022). Student-centric Model of Learning Management System Activity and Academic Performance: from Correlation to Causation. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.15430](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.15430)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Clustering analysis of student hourly login percentages using X-means with Dynamic Time Warping distance, reported in Section 6.1; the article states "the X-means algorithm found three clusters to be optimal for the input data." No effect size is printed for this descriptive result.

> "The results of the clustering algorithm are shown in Figure 3. As shown, the X-means algorithm found three clusters to be optimal for the input data."

### Mandalapu 2022 (2)

Mandalapu, V., Chen, L. K., Shetty, S., Chen, Z., & Gong, J. (2022). Student-centric Model of Learning Management System Activity and Academic Performance: from Correlation to Causation. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.15430](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.15430)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same clustering analysis in Section 6.1; the authors label the three clusters "Afternoon to Night," "Afternoon to Evening," and "Active Afternoon" based on when each cluster's login activity rises, peaks, and declines across the 24-hour day.

> "Based on the patterns found in these three clusters, we can classify cluster 0 as ”Afternoon to Night,” cluster 1 as ”Afternoon to Evening,” and cluster 2 as ”Active Afternoon.”"
