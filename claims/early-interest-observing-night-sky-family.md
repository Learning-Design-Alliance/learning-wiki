Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly. (2025). Astronomy Identity Framework for Undergraduate Students and Researchers. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/wf14-c1yp](https://doi.org/10.1103/wf14-c1yp)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic finding from the transcendental phenomenological interviews: participants such as Luis and Cathy recalled observing stars and celestial bodies with family, which the authors describe as fostering early astronomy interest. The article notes "positive shared experiences with their families" as a recurring theme.

> "Students often developed interest in astronomy at an early age through observing the night sky directly. This is often associated with positive shared experiences with their families, which suggests a cultural synergy with the scientific world."
