Pearson correlation analysis (Table 2) in the 233-nurse cross-sectional sample; the article describes the r = 0.42 correlations as moderate effect sizes, all significant at the 0.01 level.

> "SCI was positively associated with AIASN (r = 0.42), CSES (r = 0.34), and perceived clinical competence (r = 0.42). These findings suggest that nurses’ perceptions of their clinical competence are associated with both individual characteristics and their perceived organizational environment."
