Triarchic model of grit, math self-efficacy beliefs, and math engagement in Chinese children in Macau. School Psychology International, 46(6), 582–602. https://doi.org/10.1177/01430343251386902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis in the 794-student Macau sample controlling for age, gender, and conscientiousness. The article reports that "consistency of interests negatively, albeit weakly, asso- ciated with social math engagement"; the specific coefficient is not printed in the text provided.

> "In contrast to Hypothesis 2, consistency of interests negatively, albeit weakly, asso- ciated with social math engagement, suggesting that students who are more inclined to stick to their originally identified interests or goals are less likely to engage in social inter- actions with their peers in math activities."
