## Evidence

### Wei Yingying 2025

Wei Yingying, Deng Na, Zheng Hongmei, Guo Haijun, Zhang Huiling, Li Bing, Wu Daiqin, Li Wei, Si Xiaoyun. (2025). Comparison of teacher-standardized patients and peer role-play in assessing medical students' history-taking skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07758-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire of 627 valid responses (α = 0.962) on 5-point Likert items; teamwork dimension differed across groups with spRP significantly higher than TSP (p < 0.05) and no spRP–non-spRP difference (all p > 0.05).

> "There were statistically significant differences in the mean scores of students’ evaluation of teamwork in each group (F = 3.048, p = 0.048), and the scores were in descending order for the spRP, non-spRP, and TSP groups"

### Wei Yingying 2025 (2)

Wei Yingying, Deng Na, Zheng Hongmei, Guo Haijun, Zhang Huiling, Li Bing, Wu Daiqin, Li Wei, Si Xiaoyun. (2025). Comparison of teacher-standardized patients and peer role-play in assessing medical students' history-taking skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07758-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same questionnaire, the overall course-attitude dimension showed no significant group difference (F = 2.157, p = 0.117), and the clinical-skills dimension likewise showed no difference (F = 0.788, p = 0.455); equivalence was not tested.

> "The difference in the mean scores of students'overall attitude toward the course was not statistically significant (F = 2. 157, p = 0. 117)."
