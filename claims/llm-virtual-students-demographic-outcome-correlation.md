### Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang 2023

Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Leveraging generative artificial intelligence to simulate student learning behavior. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.19206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.19206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation study using the OpenAI GPT-3.5-Turbo API on a dataset of N = 145 students' demographics and final grades, validating correlation between virtual and real students rather than prediction accuracy. The authors report "highly aligned (Pearson correlation: r > 0.7) behaviors in most factors".

> "Interestingly, we find highly aligned (Pearson correlation: r > 0.7) behaviors in most factors such as age, transportation, living condition, parents jobs, learning habits, etc, as is depicted in Fig. 1(C)."
