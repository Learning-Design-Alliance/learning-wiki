Classification of the pedagogical role of the 21 games (Table 3). Most games served a complementary role alongside lectures; the remainder were independent devices where "the game was the centre of the educational process".

> "More than 71.4% of them were designed to play a complementary role in a traditional class as reinforcement and review of topics studied, or as training for exams. The rest of the games were implemented as independent devices, i.e., the game was the centre of the educational process, and there was no need for a lecture"
