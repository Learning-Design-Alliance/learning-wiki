## Evidence

### Emily M. Smith and N.G. Holmes 2017

Emily M. Smith and N.G. Holmes. (2017). Seeing the real world: Comparing learning from verification labs and traditional or enhanced lecture demonstrations. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.03174](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.03174)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors' analytical argument: because verification labs aim to confirm previously taught physics, predictions apply lecture, teaching-assistant, or textbook sources, unlike enhanced demonstrations where students commit to individual recorded predictions and confront feedback. This is an interpretation, not a tested result.

> "The predicted outcomes, therefore, come from applying authoritative sources—lecture, teaching assistants, or the textbook—rather than being generated from students' individual ideas."
