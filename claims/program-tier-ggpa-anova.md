## Evidence

### Mike Verostek 2021

Mike Verostek, Casey Miller and Benjamin Zwickl. (2021). Analyzing admissions metrics as predictors of graduate GPA and whether graduate GPA mediates PhD completion. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020115](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020115)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA with program tier (NRC-ranking terciles) as the independent variable among US students, followed by Tukey post hoc tests. Tier 1 and Tier 2 did not differ significantly (p = 0.97).

> "the main eﬀect of program tier on GGPA is signif­icant, F (2, 1949) = 26 .31, p < .001, which reﬂects the upward trend in GGPA from Tier 3 to Tier 1 and 2 pro­grams. A Tukey post hoc test reveals that the GGPA was signiﬁcantly higher for students at Tier 2 (M = 3.59, SD = 0.40, p<. 001) and Tier 1 (M = 3.60, SD = 0.42, p < .001) institutions than those at Tier 3 institutions (M = 3.41, SD = 0.59)."
