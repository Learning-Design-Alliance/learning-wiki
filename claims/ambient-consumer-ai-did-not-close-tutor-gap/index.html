Evidence¶

Shihao Yang 2026¶

Shihao Yang, Marshall Van Alstyne, and Chrysanthos Dellarocas. (2026). When AI Tutors Speak: Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23958 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.23958

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Weekly holdout check-in surveys measured consumer-AI tool use (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and similar) as a manipulation check and preregistered contamination covariate, defining the estimand as the tutor's marginal value over a realistic self-study environment.

"Holdout students used consumer AI freely throughout the study (21–42% in any given week), yet the tutor's random-ized advantage holds over that realistic environment, and removing ambient-AI users from the control group leaves the estimate essentially unchanged."
