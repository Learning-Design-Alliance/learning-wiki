## Evidence

### Caskurlu 2025

Caskurlu, S., Ocak, C., & Dai, C.-P. (2025). The Scope of Multimodal Learning Analytics in K–8: A Systematic Review. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2), 224–236. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8505

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of the 14 studies found that most did not explicitly address data fusion, "including the type of fusion and techniques used for fusing data, which leads to a lack of transparency in explaining their methodological decisions." Only two studies explicitly reported fusion (Moon et al., 2022; Olsen et al., 2020).

> "even though studies explicitly stated how multimodal data were collected and analyzed, they rarely mentioned the data fusion techniques and its process, and ethics and transparency"
