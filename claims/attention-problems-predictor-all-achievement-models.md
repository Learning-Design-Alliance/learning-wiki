# Self-reported attention problems are the only externalizing-symptom predictor present in all regression models of overall and subsector academic achievement in both primary and secondary school

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Attention problems significantly predict academic achievement in every regression model (overall GPA and all six subsectors) in primary and secondary school, with standardized betas of −.106 to −.377. [→ Flores Jerome 2022](#flores-jerome-2022)

## Evidence

### Flores Jerome 2022

Flores Jerome, Caqueo-Urízar Alejandra, López Verónica, Acevedo Daniel. (2022). Symptomatology of attention deficit, hyperactivity and defiant behavior as predictors of academic achievement. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-022-03714-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of 1754 primary school students using SENA self-report scales and Ministry of Education SIGE grades. Table 4 prints standardized β = −.290 for attention problems in the overall GPA model (p = .000), ranging to β = −.160 in physical education. The article reports attention problems as "the most important predictor in almost all models."

> "Attention problems are the most important predictor in almost all models, except for visual arts and physical education, where age is of greater magnitude."
