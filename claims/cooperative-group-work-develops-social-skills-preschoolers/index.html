Evidence¶

Tarım 2015¶

Kamuran Tarım. (2015). Effects of Cooperative Group Work Activities on Pre-school Children’s Pattern Recognition Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.1.0086

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview findings from the two experimental-group teachers (T1, T2) at the end of implementation. Teachers "felt that the children’s sharing and solidarity, listening skills and task concentration had also improved"; these are teacher perceptions, not independently measured outcomes.

"Similarly, they stated that they had observed improvements in the children’s pattern recognition skills and also felt that the children’s sharing and solidarity, listening skills and task concentration had also improved."
