Evidence¶

Bosma et al. 2017¶

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with randomized block design (N = 120 second graders with arithmetic difficulties, lowest 20% on a national math test); repeated measures analysis of insertions and measurements. The session × condition interaction on measurements was significant with printed effect size partial η² = .26; the insertion effect was not.

"There was a significant interaction effect between session and condition on the variable number of measurements, F (1,116) = 39.70, p < .001, partial η² =. 26; number of insertions did not show an effect (F (1,116) = 1.44, p= ns)."
