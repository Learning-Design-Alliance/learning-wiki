## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 1, Pre-During OBR suppression correlated positively with Post win rates in the relearning old condition after wins (r = .361). [→ Zhang 2026](#zhang-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 (n = 76) correlational analysis: suppression predicted relearning-old Post win rate after wins, "r = .361, p < .001", which the authors describe as inconsistent with their original prediction.

> "OBR suppression between Pre - During was also positively correlated with Post phase win rates in the relearning old condition following wins (r = .361, p < .001) but not following losses (r = .065, p = .577)."

### Zhang 2026 (2)

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (75% reinforcement, n = 72 analysed): suppression negatively correlated with relearning-old Post win rate after wins (r = -.372) and losses (r = -.351), attributed to the reinforcement received in the During phase.

> "the degree of OBR suppression was now significantly and negatively correlated with Post phase win rate when relearning old information both after wins (r = -.372, p < .001) and after losses (r = -.351, p = .003; Figure 2d)."
