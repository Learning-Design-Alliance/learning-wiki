Zhang Zhigang, Fei Jing, Zhang Jiaojiao, Zhu Xiaoqing, Fang Huibin, Zhu Ying. (2026). The effectiveness of the combined problem-based learning (PBL) and case-based learning (CBL) teaching method in the clinical practical teaching for gynecology resident. Satisfaction was measured on a 10-point scale questionnaire with a 100% response rate in both groups. The PBL-CBL group "exhibited higher levels of satisfaction" (82.92 ± 4.9 vs. 79.67 ± 3.49, P = 0.01).

> "the PBL-CBL group exhibited higher levels of satisfaction in comparison to the traditional group (PBL-CBL group vs. traditional group: The mean values for the two groups were 82.92 ± 4.9 and 79.67 ± 3.49, respectively. A two-tailed t-test was used to analyse the data, and P = 0.01 was recorded"
