# Connect with Kids shows statistically significant positive effects on middle and high school students' reports of their own and classmates' behavior

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Middle and high school students in Connect with Kids classrooms showed statistically significant positive differences on both student survey measures of behavior (effect sizes 0.61 and 0.48). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2006](#what-works-clearinghouse-2006)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Connect with Kids. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/104

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study (Page & D'Agostino, 2005) comparing more than 800 students in 24 intervention and 22 comparison classrooms. WWC-confirmed significance for "middle/high school student survey part I and part II", with effect sizes 0.61 and 0.48 and improvement indices +23 and +19.

> "The study reported statistically significant differences favoring the intervention group for middle and high school students’ reports of their own and their classmates’ behavior (middle/high school student survey part I and part II)."
