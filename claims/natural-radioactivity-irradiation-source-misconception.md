## Evidence

### Mirofora Pilakouta 2011

Mirofora Pilakouta. (2011). TEI Piraeus students' knowledge on the beneficial applications of nuclear physics: Nuclear energy, radioactivity - consequences. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item Q3 on the main contributor to lifetime human irradiation: 34% correct (natural radioactivity), 40% medical examinations, 15% nuclear plants or accidents, 11% 'don't know'. The author relates this to fear of medical radiation and poor knowledge of natural radioactivity.

> "Only one out of three (34%) of the students knows that the main contribution to human irradiation comes from natural radioactivity. The majority, 40%, b e-lieve that the main source of irradiation comes from medical examinations, 15% from nuclea r plants or nuclear accidents and a minority 11% answers that they do not know."
