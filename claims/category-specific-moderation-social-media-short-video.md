# Exploratory analyses suggest the digital moderation of the EF–reappraisal link is concentrated in social media and short-form video, not gaming or educational use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In exploratory, unadjusted category-specific SEMs, social media (EF × Category β = −0.18, p = .001) and short-form video (β = −0.21, p < .001) showed significant negative moderation, while gaming and educational apps did not. [→ Shi Guochun 2026](#shi-guochun-2026)

## Evidence

### Shi Guochun 2026

Shi Guochun, Shi Zimo, Wang Qianqian. (2026). Cross-sectional bidirectional associations between executive function and emotion regulation in adolescents: the moderating role of digital engagement intensity. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05038-8

`q2 · i2`

Exploratory, unadjusted and non-preregistered analyses re-estimating the SEM separately with each of five activity-category durations as moderator. The article reports the pattern "appears to be driven primarily by social media and short-form video use"; educational use showed a small positive, non-significant coefficient.

> "the negative moderation pattern observed for total screen time appears to be driven primarily by social media and short-form video use—the two categories for which both the EF × Category and the Reappraisal × Category interactions reached significance."
