Evidence¶

Matsuda 2013¶

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-design analysis of test scores (N=69) in a randomized classroom study of APLUS, comparing pre, post, and delayed test times. Procedural scores rose significantly; "the difference between the post- and delayed-test was not statistically significant"; conceptual knowledge showed no test-time or condition difference.

"For the procedural skill test, the average scores of both post-test (MPost=.4 5,SD=.22) and delayed-post test (MDelayed=.46, SD=.23) were significantly higher than the pre-test (MPre=.3 8,SD=.2 0);t(68)= −3.61, p<0.001 and t(68)= −4.31, p<0.001 for pre vs. post and pre vs. delayed respectively."
