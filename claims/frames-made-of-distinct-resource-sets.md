## Evidence

### Kelli Shar 2020

Kelli Shar, Rosemary S. Russ, James T. Laverty. (2020). Student Epistemological Framing on Paper-Based Assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of 67 coded problem-solving attempts from think-aloud interviews, yielding 70 frame instances coded by behavior and resources by utterance. The authors report that "Particular frames are associated with particular groups of resources diﬀerent from other frames," consistent with frames as local coherences of resources.

> "Particular frames are associated with particular groups of resources diﬀerent from other frames. This provides empiracle evidence for our claim as predicted from our theoretical perspective."
