## Evidence

### Siegel 2015

Siegel, J., & Siegel, A. (2015). Getting to the bottom of L2 listening instruction: Making a case for bottom-up activities. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(4), 637-662. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.4.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests on the CASEC listening test (combining main-idea and specific-information sections, maximum 500 points) showed significant gains for the treatment group and no significant change for the contrast group.

> "Similar to the dictation test results, the effect of the BU instruction based on a paired samples t test was statistically significant (t(17) = -2.172, p < .05), while the CG showed no significant differences (t(25) = 1.019, p = 0.318)."

### Siegel 2015 (2)

Siegel, J., & Siegel, A. (2015). Getting to the bottom of L2 listening instruction: Making a case for bottom-up activities. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(4), 637-662. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.4.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive statistics showed the treatment group began with a lower mean (229.22 vs. 244.58) but surpassed the contrast group at posttest (248.83 vs. 238.69), suggesting BU instruction supports global comprehension beyond dictation-specific skills.
