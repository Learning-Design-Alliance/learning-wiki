Evidence¶

Reigal RE 2020¶

Reigal RE, Moral-Campillo L, Juárez-Ruiz de Mier R, Morillo-Baro JP, Morales-Sánchez V, Pastrana JL and Hernández-Mendo A (2020) Physical Fitness Level Is Related to Attention and Concentration in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00110

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Correlation analysis (Pearson and Spearman) of the total sample of 210 adolescents, relating three Eurofit fitness tests to d2 attention measures. The printed effect size is a correlation of r ≥ 0.40: "the correlation level between maximum oxygen consumption with TOT and CON are moderate ( r≥ 0.40)"; jump and speed correlations were weak.

"In the general sample, the correlations among horizontal jump and speed test with the main measures of D2 test are weak. However, the correlation level between maximum oxygen consumption with TOT and CON are moderate ( r≥ 0.40)."

Related Claims¶

  • Omissions, commissions and variation index of the d2 test are not related to any physical fitness variable — related
  • VO2max is the main linear-regression predictor of attention and concentration, with strength also predictive in boys — related
  • Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) is the physical fitness measure most strongly correlated with cognitive functioning in adolescents — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Adolescents in the higher physical fitness cluster score better on attention and concentration than the lower-fitness cluster, in both boys and girls — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Cardiorespiratory fitness predicts nearly all cognitive and psychosocial measures, with anxiety/insomnia predicted by horizontal jump and life satisfaction by fat mass — related
  • Adolescents with high weekly physical exercise hours (more than 4 h/week) show significantly better selective attention, concentration, and processing speed than lower-exercise groups, with no differences between low and moderate groups — related
  • GHQ social dysfunction and severe depression show no group differences by exercise hours but are significantly related to physical fitness in correlation and regression — related
  • Fitness–attention relationships are stronger in boys than girls, with higher explained variance in boys' regression models — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
