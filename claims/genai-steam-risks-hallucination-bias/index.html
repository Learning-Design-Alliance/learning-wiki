Dahal N 2026¶

Dahal N, Hasan MK and Islam MT (2026). GenAI and AI tools for STEAM educators: evidence-informed strategies and resources for the future of teaching. Front. Educ. 11:1948064. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1948064

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis: the review attributes these failure modes to multiple cited reviews and conceptual analyses, describing GenAI outputs as probabilistic rather than inherently truthful, which requires active verification and contextual judgment. No effect sizes are printed.

"Yet the same systems can hallucinate, amplify bias, encourage superficial completion strategies, and complicate the validity of assessment (Dwivedi et al., 2023; Farrokhnia et al., 2024; Kasneci et al., 2023; Lo, 2023; Tlili et al., 2023)."
