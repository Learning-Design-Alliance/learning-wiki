## Evidence

### Fraction Face-Off! 2021

Fraction Face-Off!. (2021). Evidence for ESSA program description. https://www.evidenceforessa.org/programs/mathematics/fraction-face-off

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Two 12-week studies in Nashville compared Fraction Face-Off! to ordinary teaching without supplemental lessons, with 425 students overall. The review reports the program "had an average effect size of +0.51" on the NAEP-derived measure, meeting the Strong evidence category.

> "On this measure, Fraction Face-Off! had an average effect size of +0.51, qualifying it for the "Strong" evidence category and for an evidence badge (at least two studies meeting the "Strong" category)."
