### Somm Irene 2024 (2)

Somm Irene, Hajart Marco, Fehr Folkert, Weiß-Becker Christoph. (2024). Perceptions of supervision and feedback in PaedCompenda, the competency-based, post-graduate curriculum in pediatrics (www.paedcompenda.de). GMS Journal for Medical Education, 41(5). https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001710.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Grounded-theory analysis of trainee interviews and videotaped feedback conferences. Trainers routinely relativized critical comments, making criticism ambivalent; the study reports trainees remembered observed feedback situations as "an uncomfortable learning environment with limited benefits" and used lack of time to avoid the trainer's attention.

> "In such an atmosphere of inhibited criticism, a natural culture of observation and feedback can hardly be established: Physician trainees primarily remember the situations where they were observed and received feedback as an uncomfortable learning environment with limited benefits."
