## Evidence

### Bismilla Zia 2015

Bismilla Zia, Dubrowski Adam, Amin Harish J. (2015). Program directors’ perceptions of importance of pediatric procedural skills and resident preparedness. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1499-8

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations across the 31 procedures relating curriculum presence to mean importance and preparedness ratings in the 16-respondent survey. Correlations are associational; the article notes the relationship between perceptions and true competence remains questionable.

> "curriculum being strongly correlated with mean importance (r = 0.7, p < 0.0001). Residents were likewise perceived to achieve greater preparedness for those procedures with a curriculum in place, with presence of a formal curriculum being strongly correlated with mean perceived preparedness (r = 0.8, p < 0.0001)"
