# Evidence on child independence is inconclusive: positive under fixed-effect but not random-effect assumptions

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The FDK–independence association was d = 0.38 (fixed-effect, CI excluding 0) but d = 0.28 with a CI including 0 under random effects, and no adjusted tests exist. [→ Harris Cooper 2010](#harris-cooper-2010)

## Evidence

### Harris Cooper 2010

Harris Cooper, Ashley Batts Allen, Erika A. Patall, and Amy L. Dent. (2010). Effects of Full-Day Kindergarten on Academic Achievement and Social Development. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309359185

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of four samples (four effect sizes, reports 1983–2005, sample sizes 29–123), all unpublished, each measured with a single parent- or teacher-questionnaire item; all comparisons were unadjusted. The random-effects estimate was "not significantly different from 0".

> "Using fixed-effect assumptions, the average d-index was 0.38 (95% CI = 0.15/0.61). Using a random-effects model, the average d-index was not significantly different from 0 (d = 0.28, 95% CI = –0.25/0.80)."
