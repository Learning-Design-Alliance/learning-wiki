## Evidence

### Edwards 2025

Edwards, R. J., Yeates, P., Lefroy, J., & McKinley, R. (2025). Understanding contexts and mechanisms through which video based benchmarking promotes alignment of examiners' scoring in objective structured clinical exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10454-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

After benchmarking, examiners judged a target video replicating the first live student; receptive, engaged participants "perceived that their judgements... was more aligned with the expected standard". Perceptions only, not measured scores.

> "When receptive participants thoroughly engaged with well-constructed benchmark information, they perceived that their judgements on the subsequent student performance (i.e. the proxy for live students in the OSCE) was more aligned with the expected standard."
