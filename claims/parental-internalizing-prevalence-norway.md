## Evidence

### Nordmo 2023

Nordmo, M., Kleppestø, T., Sunde, H. F., Flatø, M., Demange, P. & Torvik, F. A. (2023). The association between parental internalizing disorders and child school performance. npj Science of Learning 8:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00182-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population-wide register analysis of all Norwegian children born 1992–2002 with registered GPA (N = 667,955 denominator). The article reports 18.8% exposed in the last three years and 11.5% yearly, with "a mother (7.9%) or a father (4.3%)". No effect size applies.

> "We found that 18.8% of children had experienced a parent with an internalizing disorder diagnosis in the last three years of primary education. In an average year between 2006 – 2018, 11.5% of children had a mother (7.9%) or a father (4.3%) with an internalizing disorder."
