## Evidence

### Stenseng 2022

Stenseng, F., Tingstad, E. B., Wichstrøm, L., & Skalicka, V. (2022). Social withdrawal and academic achievement, intertwined over years? Bidirectional effects from primary to upper secondary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1354–1365. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12504

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Pooled RI-CLPM results before multi-group analysis, in the TESS sample analysed in Mplus 8.1 with FIML for missing data. The model fit the data well, and "children with higher AA manifested less SW (r = −.34, 95% CI: −.49 to −.19)".

> "At the between‐person level, children with higher AA manifested less SW (r = −.34, 95% CI: −.49 to −.19). At the within‐person level, no significant prospective cross‐lagged associations were detected between AA and SW."
