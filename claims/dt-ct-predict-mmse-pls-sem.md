## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wangda Zhu 2025

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

PLS-SEM analysis in SmartPLS on the 26-participant sample's pre-task questionnaire scores; the model explained R2 = 0.808 of MMSE variance, with DT showing "a very large effect (f2 = 2.288)".

> "The structural model (Figure 2) revealed that both DT (β= 0.730,p < .001) and CT (β= 0.300,p < .001) were significant positive predictors of MMSE."

### Wangda Zhu 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same PLS-SEM analysis of pre-task questionnaires; effect sizes f2 were calculated for each predictor, with CT showing "a medium to large effect (f2 = 0.387) on MMSE"; no standardized effect size in d, g, or r units is printed for this predictor, so impact is not coded.

> "Effectsizes(f 2)werecalculatedtoevaluatethemagnitude of each predictor's contribution: DT showed a very large effect (f2 = 2.288), while CT showed a medium to large effect (f2 = 0.387) on MMSE."
