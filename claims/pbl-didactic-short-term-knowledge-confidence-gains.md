# Dental students' knowledge and confidence scores rose significantly from pre-course to post-course for didactic lecture-based and PBL items over a Head and Neck Anatomy course, in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean knowledge scores increased significantly from pre-course to post-course for both lecture-based items (p < 0.001) and problem-based items (p = 0.001). [→ Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018](#al-madi-ebtissam-m-2018)
`q3 i?` Mean confidence scores increased significantly from pre-course to post-course for both lecture-based and problem-based items (p < 0.001). [→ Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018 (2)](#al-madi-ebtissam-m-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired 't' tests in a pilot cohort of 33 first-year dental students comparing pre- and post-course MCQ scores. Lecture-based knowledge rose from "3.45 ± 1.73 to 6.76 ± 2.36" and problem-based from "4.42 ± 1.66 to 5.97 ± 1.99"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The mean knowledge score (lecture based) increased from 3.45 ± 1.73 to 6.76 ± 2.36 with statistical significance (p < 0.001), and the mean knowledge score (problem based) increased from 4.42 ± 1.66 to 5.97 ± 1.99 with statistical significance (p = 0.001)"

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018 (2)

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same paired 't' test analysis of 5-point Likert confidence items attached to each MCQ in the same cohort of 33 students. Both instructional methods showed "statistical significance (p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean confidence score (lecture based) increased from 18.06 ± 7.84 to 33.97 ± 10.17, and the mean confidence score (problem based) increased from 11.15 ± 8.86 to 29.42 ± 10.4 with a statistical significance (p < 0.001)"
