Evidence¶

Kersey 2019¶

Kersey, A. J., Csumitta, K. D., & Cantlon, J. F. (2019). Gender similarities in the brain during mathematics development. npj Science of Learning 4:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0057-x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Whole-brain Levene's variance test across the 104-child fMRI sample. The cluster was small (15 voxels; TAL peak 18, −64, 43; F(1,102) ≥ 6.89) and girls showed more variance at equal neural amplitudes, which did not produce a mean difference.

"Levene's Test of Variance revealed one small region of right posterior parietal cortex where groups differed in variance but not in mean activation, with girls showing greater variance than boys"
