## Evidence

### Maizel Julien 2014

Maizel Julien, Guyomarc'h Laurianne, Henon Pierre, Modeliar Santhi Samy, de Cagny Bertrand, Choukroun Gabriel, Slama Michel. (2014). Residents learning ultrasound-guided catheterization are not sufficiently skilled to use landmarks. Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/cc13741

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Time-course analysis of venous return time and procedure time across procedures (Figure 2). The article reports venous return time showed "a dramatic increase when residents started to perform the LM technique" while procedure time kept decreasing, indicating partial skill transfer.

> "the venous return time decreased progressively with increasing experience with the UG technique followed by a dramatic increase when residents started to perform the LM technique"
