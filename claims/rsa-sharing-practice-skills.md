## Evidence

### Charumanee 2014

Charumanee, N. (2014). IDEA SHARING: THE USE OF READ-SHARE-ACT TO PROMOTE EXTENSIVE READING. PASAA, 47. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077997

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings from student comments on the S (Share) stage: weekly sharing of 10-12 pages kept reading at an "optimum level", and students became more aware of communicating with simple spoken language, note-taking, and revising notes into journals.

> "when looking at the whole process of sharing content and maintaining journals of the stories, all the students highly valued the opportunities to practice listening, speaking, and writing skills."
