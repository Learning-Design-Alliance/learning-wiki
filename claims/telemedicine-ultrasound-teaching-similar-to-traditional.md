## Evidence

### Duarte ML 2022

Duarte ML, dos Santos LR, Iared W, Peccin MS. (2022). Comparison of ultrasonography learning between distance teaching and traditional methodology. An educational systematic review. São Paulo Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1590/1516-3180.2021.1047.R.19052022

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of 16 comparative studies (randomized trials, one pseudorandomized trial, one prospective cohort) assessed with the Kirkpatrick model and Buckley's quality indicators. The review concludes telemedicine teaching methods are "similar to the traditional method" except for venous access procedures.

> "From gathering this data together, we can infer that ultra-sound telemedicine teaching methods are similar to the traditional method, except in the case of teaching venous access procedures, for which the studies did not show agreement."
