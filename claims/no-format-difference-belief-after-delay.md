## Evidence

### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 2 between-within ANOVA follow-ups: "no significant difference in post-correction belief across the refutation and truth sandwich conditions either immediately or after a two-week delay" (ps ≥ .206); equivalence was not directly tested.

> "As predicted, there was no significant difference in post-correction belief across the refutation and truth sandwich conditions either immediately or after a two-week delay (ps ≥ .206)."

### Swire-Thompson 2025 (2)

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Bayesian ANOVA excluding the control in Experiment 2 "provided strong evidence for a null effect of correction condition (BF01 = 20.64)" and moderate evidence for the null three-way interaction (BF01 = 9.56).

> "Furthermore, conducting a 2 × 2 × 2 analysis as a Bayesian ANOVA provided strong evidence for a null effect of correction condition (BF01 = 20.64), and moderate evidence for the null for the three-way interaction (BF01 = 9.56)."
