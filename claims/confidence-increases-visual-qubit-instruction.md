## Evidence

### Linda Qerimi 2025

Linda Qerimi, Sarah Malone, Eva Rexigel, Jochen Kuhn and Stefan Küchemann. (2025). Comparing Visual Qubit Representations in Quantum Education: The Bloch sphere Enhances Task Efficiency. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA with repeated measures on response confidence across pretest, Posttest 1, and Posttest 2 in the follow-up sample; the printed effect size was η = .29, and post-hoc tests showed differences between pretest and both posttests (p < .001) but none between the posttests. The descriptive group difference after the learning unit was not significant (p = .066).

> "It showed a significant main effect of the test time, F (2, 186) = 39 .36, p < .001,η = .29, indicating a significant increase in re- sponse certainty over time."
