## Evidence

### Deiglmayr 2011

Deiglmayr, A., & Spada, H. (2011). Training for fostering knowledge co-construction from collaborative inference-drawing. Learning and Instruction, 21(3), 441-451. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2010.06.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Outcome measure from the testing phase of the 36-dyad experiment: two thirds of dyads solved the murder-mystery correctly, but condition differences were "not statistically significant" (Fisher's exact test, p = .12). Descriptively, "the rate of correct solutions increased from the Control condition to the Tutored Training condition, with the notable exception of the Instructed Training condition".

> "Differences in the distribution of wrong vs. correct solutions between conditions were not statistically significant given the relatively small numbers of observations (Fisher’s exact test, p = .12)"
