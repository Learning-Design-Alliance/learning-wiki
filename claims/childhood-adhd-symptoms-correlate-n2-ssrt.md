## Evidence

### Einziger Tzlil 2021

Einziger Tzlil, Ben-Shachar Mattan S., Devor Tali, Shmueli Michael, Auerbach Judith G., Berger Andrea. (2021). "My Brain Can Stop": An ERP Study of Longitudinal Prediction of Inhibitory Control in Adolescence. Brain Sciences, 11(1), 100. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci11010100

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis (Table 3) in the longitudinal sample. The article reports that N2 and SSRT "were significantly associated with ADHD symptoms throughout childhood"; no effect size is printed for these correlations.

> "both were significantly associated with ADHD symptoms throughout childhood; those who had consistently high levels of ADHD symptoms throughout childhood showed reduced N2 amplitude (i.e., a less negative amplitude) and a longer SSRT at 17 years of age."
