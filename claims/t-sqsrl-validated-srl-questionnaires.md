## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The teacher questionnaire TQsrl reduced 42 items to 31 across six factors with high internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha 0.94 in EFA and 0.93 in CFA). [→ Avivit Arvatz 2025](#avivit-arvatz-2025)
`q2 i?` The student questionnaire SQsrl reduced to 15 items across five factors (alpha 0.91 EFA, 0.88 CFA), splitting reflection into reflection on action and reflection for action. [→ Avivit Arvatz 2025 (2)](#avivit-arvatz-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Avivit Arvatz 2025

Avivit Arvatz, Roee Peretz, Yehudit Judy Dori. (2025). Self-regulated learning and reflection: a tool for teachers and students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09415-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis with 84 in-service teachers (TG1) grouped 31 of the original 42 items into six factors; the article reports "The overall Cronbach's alpha was 0.94" and CFA on 70 teachers (TG2) yielded alpha 0.93 with acceptable fit indices.

> "According to preliminary results, 31 of the original 42 items in TQsrl were grouped into these six factors, and they comply with the original factors of the TQsrl structure."

### Avivit Arvatz 2025 (2)

Avivit Arvatz, Roee Peretz, Yehudit Judy Dori. (2025). Self-regulated learning and reflection: a tool for teachers and students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09415-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

EFA on 89 seventh-graders (SG1) grouped 15 of 18 items into five factors; a CFA on 119 students (SG2) gave overall alpha 0.88, and "two distinct factors emerged: a factor for reflection on action and a factor for reflection for action."

> "After checking for 4, 5, and 6 factors 15 out of the original 18 items in SQsrl, the items were grouped into the five factors with an overall Cronbach's alpha of 0.91."
