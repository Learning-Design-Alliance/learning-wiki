Chi-square comparison of viewing rates by link-distribution timing across 15 months (EM and non-EM bound combined, n=113 vs n=99 per Table 3b); the difference was not statistically significant.

> "Participation was 60.2% (33.3–92.9) when lecture links were given three days in advance and 68.7% (47.1–98.9) when links were given two weeks in advance (Table 3b; p=0.197)."
