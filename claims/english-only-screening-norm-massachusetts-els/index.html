Soo Ping Chow 2025¶

Soo Ping Chow, A., Faulkner-Bond, M., Lemke, M., LeBeau, B., & Quesen, S. (2025). How English learners are screened for risk of reading difficulty: Use of English- and Spanish-language screeners in the 2023/24 school year. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677598

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of 2023/24 screening assessment data for an analytic sample of 23,955 English learners with BOY, MOY, and EOY scores. The brief reports "Ninety percent of English learners were assessed only in English," with 8 percent screened in both languages and 2 percent in other patterns.

"Ninety percent of English learners were assessed only in English, 8 percent were screened in English and Spanish at each time period, and 2 percent were either screened alternately between English and Spanish or consistently screened in one language"
