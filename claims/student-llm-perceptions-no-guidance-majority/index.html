Bodke Parag 2026¶

Bodke Parag, Jumle Rahul. (2026). Large language models in undergraduate medical education: a systematic review of examination performance, knowledge acquisition, and learner perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09999-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of 28 perception studies (surveys, focus groups, mixed methods), only 7 using validated instruments. The review reports median 74% positive, 61% integrity concern, 11% dishonest use, 59% hallucination concern, 37% identification, and "82% of students reported receiving no formal guidance".

"82% of students reported receiving no formal guidance, policy communication, or LLM-related training from their medical school—a finding consistent across 25 of 28 studies and observed uniformly across high-income and LMIC settings"
