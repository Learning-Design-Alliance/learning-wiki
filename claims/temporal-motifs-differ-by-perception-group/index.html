Evidence¶

Zamecnik 2024¶

Zamecnik, A., Kovanović, V., Joksimović, S., Grossmann, G., Ladjal, D., Pardo, A. (2024). The perceptions of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09424-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Motif analysis of team interaction log data from 194 teams using the SNAP library; five Kruskal–Wallis tests with group assignment as the independent variable (Dyads χ2 = 17.447, p = 0.000; Star Triad χ2 = 17.057, p = 0.000), followed by Dunn post hoc tests.

"The results in Table 7 show significant differences between perception groups for each motif group. The reported size effects are shown where Dyads and Star Triads have moderate magnitudes, while Chaotic, Switch and Ordered Triads have small magnitudes."

Zamecnik 2024 (2)¶

Zamecnik, A., Kovanović, V., Joksimović, S., Grossmann, G., Ladjal, D., Pardo, A. (2024). The perceptions of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09424-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation in the discussion of their motif and perception findings, aligning with Zamecnik et al. (2023): teams with higher triadic interactions also display higher performance scores. This is presented as corroborating that prior work.

"the dyadic motifs are a characterisation for teams that maintain differences in perceptions (i.e. Multifaceted) and have lower performance and task cohesion scores. In contrast, teams with similar cohesive perceptions (i.e. Aligned), characterised by triadic motifs, have higher performance and task cohesion scores."
