## Evidence

### Almashraqi Abeer A. 2026

Almashraqi Abeer A., Jethlia Ankur, Glanville Rebecca, Daas Hanin, Ali Kamran. (2026). Simulation-Based Learning in Oral Radiology: Students' Perceptions of Training in Intraoral Radiographic Techniques Across Two Dental Institutes. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70138

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional mixed-methods survey of 44 third-year dental students (14 Institution A, 30 Institution B) with 100% response rate. Combined overall perception mean was 4.27 (SD 0.760), with 85.86% of responses between "strongly agree and agree".

> "A total of 44 dental students responded to the questionnaire, with 14 from Institution A and 30 from Institution B, yielding a response rate of 100% at both participating institutions."
