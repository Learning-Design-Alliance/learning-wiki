# Schooling showed no significant interaction with cogPGS or SES on any intelligence domain, and Bayesian ROPE analysis found interaction effects under 0.05 SD with 95% probability

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` No significant two-way (schooling-cogPGS, schooling-SES, cogPGS-SES) or three-way interactions were found for cIQ, fIQ, or WM; almost all interaction terms fell entirely within a 0.05 SD ROPE boundary. [→ Nicholas Judd 2022](#nicholas-judd-2022)
`q3 i?` A 0.02 SD effect could not be ruled out: most interaction distributions substantially overlap the 0.02 ROPE boundary. [→ Nicholas Judd 2022 (2)](#nicholas-judd-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Nicholas Judd 2022

Nicholas Judd, Bruno Sauce and Torkel Klingberg. (2022). Schooling substantially improves intelligence, but neither lessens nor widens the impacts of socioeconomics and genetics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00148-5

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Bayesian hierarchical mixed-effects models of the two-way interaction terms of interest, with weakly informative zero-centered priors. No frequentist interactions were significant for any domain (Eqs. 3 and 4).

> "Almost all interaction terms were entirely within a ROPE boundary of 0.05 (Fig. 3). This means that with a 95% probability, the interaction effect was less than 0.05 SD, adding evidence for the null hypothesis"

### Nicholas Judd 2022 (2)

Nicholas Judd, Bruno Sauce and Torkel Klingberg. (2022). Schooling substantially improves intelligence, but neither lessens nor widens the impacts of socioeconomics and genetics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00148-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian ROPE analysis used two boundaries (0.05 and 0.02) because of a lack of consensus on minimal effect size of interest and because schooling is a cumulative process. The exceptions slightly outside 0.05 were schooling-cogPGS for fIQ (3%) and WM (5%).

> "most of the terms have a substantial part of their distribution overlapping with the ROPE boundaries of 0.02. This means we cannot conﬁrm nor disprove the null hypothesis for a maximal effect size of interest of 0.02 SDs."
