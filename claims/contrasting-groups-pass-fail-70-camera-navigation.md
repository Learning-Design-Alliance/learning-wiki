Using virtual reality simulation to ensure laparoscopic camera navigation skills of new surgical assistants—a validation study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12073-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Standard-setting analysis of the composite score in the validation trial, using the intersection of normal curves of the novice and experienced groups. The article states the "pass/fail score was calculated to be 70%" with one false positive and no false negatives; consequences were explored with Fisher's exact test.

> "Using the contrasting groups’ method, the pass/fail score was calculated to be 70% (Fig. 2). The consequences of this standard were that one novice passed the test (i.e., one false positive) and no experienced failed the test (i.e., no false negatives)."
