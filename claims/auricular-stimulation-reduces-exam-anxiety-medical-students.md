## Evidence

### Usichenko Taras 2020

Usichenko Taras, Wenzel Anna, Klausenitz Catharina, Petersmann Astrid, Hesse Thomas, Neumann Nicola, Hahnenkamp Klaus. (2020). Auricular stimulation vs. expressive writing for exam anxiety in medical students – A randomized crossover investigation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238307

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Results of a randomized three-period crossover trial in 37 medical students before anatomy exams, with paired Bonferroni-adjusted t-tests. AS produced "lower than after no intervention" and "in comparison with expressive writing" reductions, with "MD = 5.9; 95% CI 3.3, 8.5; t31 = 4.7; P < 0.001; d = 0.8" and "MD = 6.5; 95% CI 3.4, 9.7; t35 = 4.2; P < 0.001; d = 0.7".

> "On the morning of the exam (Time III), state anxiety level after AS was also lower than after no intervention: MD = 5.9; 95% CI 3.3, 8.5; t31 = 4.7; P < 0.001; d = 0.8, and in comparison with expressive writing: MD = 6.5; 95% CI 3.4, 9.7; t35 = 4.2; P < 0.001; d = 0.7"

### Usichenko Taras 2020 (2)

Usichenko Taras, Wenzel Anna, Klausenitz Catharina, Petersmann Astrid, Hesse Thomas, Neumann Nicola, Hahnenkamp Klaus. (2020). Auricular stimulation vs. expressive writing for exam anxiety in medical students – A randomized crossover investigation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238307

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Within-condition comparison from the same crossover trial: state anxiety "decreased in comparison with baseline values" after AS with "MD = 4.0; 95% CI 1.2, 6.6; t35 = 3.0; P = 0.015; d = 0.5"; EW and NI values remained comparable with baseline at Time II.

> "After AS, state anxiety, measured 5 hours later, decreased in comparison with baseline values: mean difference (MD) = 4.0; 95% CI 1.2, 6.6; t35 = 3.0; P = 0.015; d = 0.5."
