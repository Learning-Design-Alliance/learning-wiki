### Schoenborn Nancy L. 2015

Schoenborn Nancy L., Boyd Cynthia, Cayea Danelle, Nakamura Kelly, Xue Qian-Li, Ray Anushree, McNabney Matthew. (2015). Incorporating prognosis in the care of older adults with multimorbidity: description and evaluation of a novel curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0488-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-only acceptability survey administered immediately after the curriculum to all 20 intervention group residents; descriptive statistics only, no effect size printed. Residents rated quality and relevance at "mean 4.5, SD 0.5" and "mean 4.5, SD 0.7" respectively.

> "On a 5-point Likert scale (1 = poor, 5 = excellent), the residents rated the curriculum highly (mean 4.5, SD 0.5) and found it highly relevant (mean 4.5, SD 0.7)."
