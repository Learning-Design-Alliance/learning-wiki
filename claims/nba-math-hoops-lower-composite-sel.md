# NBA Math Hoops students scored significantly lower than controls on a composite SEL measure (−0.21 SD), though remaining at moderately high levels

## Evidence

### McKinney 2026

McKinney, D., Walters, K., Powell, K., Sabalburo, E., & Morton, C. (2026). The impact of NBA Math Hoops on students' math achievement: A randomized controlled trial. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681343

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same RCT measured SEL via student survey. Treatment students scored "on average, 0.21 standard deviations lower" than controls on the composite SEL measure (β = −0.21, p < 0.05), though predicted post-means remained "above the midpoint of the scale."

> "Regression estimates indicate that there was a significant difference between the composite SEL score of students in the treatment condition as compared to the control condition (𝛽 = 0. −21, 𝑝 < 0.05), controlling for pre-math composite score, pre-SEL composite score, gender, and IEP status."

### McKinney 2026 (2)

McKinney, D., Walters, K., Powell, K., Sabalburo, E., & Morton, C. (2026). The impact of NBA Math Hoops on students' math achievement: A randomized controlled trial. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681343

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subscale analyses found the largest SEL contributors were self-management and social awareness (β = −0.39, p < 0.001) and math interest value (β = −0.21, p < 0.05). Raw predicted differences were 0.28 and 0.19 scale points respectively.

> "exploratory analyses indicated that the largest contributors to the significant difference in the composite SEL score were self-management and social awareness (𝛽 = −0.39, 𝑝 < 0.001) and math interest  v(𝛽 = −0.21, < 0.05). The average impacts on the other SEL scales were also negative."
