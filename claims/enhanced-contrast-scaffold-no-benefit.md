`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on accuracy across eight 25-image training blocks for the PL and PLC groups (Fig. 4). A significant main effect of time (F(7,273) = 9.04, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.19) confirmed steady improvement, but "the overall learning trajectory and final performance level were statistically equivalent for both training groups."

> "However, there was no significant main effect of group (F(1,39) = 1.12, p = 0.30) and no significant Group x Time interaction (F(7,273) = 0.84, p = 0.55). This result indicates that the overall learning trajectory and final performance level were statistically equivalent for both training groups."
