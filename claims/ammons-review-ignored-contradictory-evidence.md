`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Historical narrative review of early feedback-intervention research. The authors document that Ammons ignored disconfirming evidence (e.g., Pressy's report that FI decreased Russian vocabulary learning) and that a manual SSCI search yielded 100 citations of Ammons's paper since 1965. No effect size is printed for this historical claim.

> "Ammons's review was an unfortunate milestone in FI research because he reached the two most influential conclusions in the area—FI improves learning as well as motivation—without serious consideration of the contradictory evidence that was available to him"
