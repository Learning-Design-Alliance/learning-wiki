# Preservice teachers chose the easy video significantly more often for an ethnic minority student than for an ethnic majority student, all other information being equal

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Ethnic minority background students were significantly more often given the easy video than ethnic majority background students in a between-subjects vignette experiment. [→ Sabine Schlag 2024](#sabine-schlag-2024)

## Evidence

### Sabine Schlag 2024

Sabine Schlag, Sabine Glock. (2024). The roles that students' ethnicity and achievement levels play in teachers' choice of learning materials in online teaching: evidence from two experimental studies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09681-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experimental vignette study with 108 preservice teachers in a between-subjects design with factor ethnicity. Frequencies of easy vs. difficult video choice differed; the article reports 82% chose the easy video for the minority student versus 11% for the majority student, "χ2(df = 1) = 101.32, p < .001".

> "A non-parametric test revealed differences in the frequencies, χ2(df = 1) = 101.32, p < .001."
