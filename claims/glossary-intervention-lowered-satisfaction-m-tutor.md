## Subclaims

## Evidence

### David Wenhao Huang 2004

David Wenhao Huang, Hans Aagard, Heidi Diefes-Dux. (2004). Impact of Cognitive-based Instructional Intervention on Learning Motivation: The Implementation of Student-made Glossary in a Programming-oriented Engineering Problem-solving Course and Its Impact on Learning Motivation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED485015

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-group pre/post study in ENGR 106 (Fall 2002) using the adapted IMMS; Table 4 reports Pre Mean 0.75, Post Mean 0.57, F = 10.717, significance level 0.001, N = 957, showing the glossary "significantly lowered students' satisfaction".

> "The analysis indicated that the use of the glossary significantly lowered students' satisfaction as shown in Table 4 based on the aforementioned frequency method, which contradicts our hypothesis that the satisfaction level would increase"
