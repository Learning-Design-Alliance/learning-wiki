## Evidence

### Albart Stephenie Ann 2025

Albart Stephenie Ann, Looi Irene. (2025). Knowledge, practice, and facilitating factors of evidence-based medicine among Malaysian doctors: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06865-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nationwide cross-sectional online survey of 409 Malaysian doctors using the validated EBMQ, with percentage scores categorised by Bloom's cutoffs. The survey found "53.8% (n = 220) of doctors had a moderate level of knowledge" while practice was high; mean practice percent score was 83.7% (SD = 13.06%) versus roughly 71% for knowledge and facilitating factors.

> "Based on the categories, 53.8% (n = 220) of doctors had a moderate level of knowledge, while about two-thirds demonstrated a higher level of practice (67.2%, n = 275) and a moderate level of facilitating factors (67.0%, n = 274)."
