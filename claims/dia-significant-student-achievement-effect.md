## Evidence

### Antoniou 2011

Antoniou, P., Kyriakides, L., & Creemers, B. (2011). Investigating the Effectiveness of a Dynamic Integrated Approach to Teacher Professional Development. ceps Journal, 1(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130737

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis (students within classes within schools) of end-of-year mathematics scores for 2356 students, with prior achievement, SES and other covariates controlled. The DIA dummy coefficient was "0.24 (0.08)"; model 5 explained 38.5% of variance with a significant chi-square change (p<0.001).

> "The DIA was found to have a statistically significant effect on student achievement compared with the HA. Specifically, the effect of this variable is 0.24 (0.08), indicating that the students of teachers employing this approach have better results in their achievement than those whose teachers employed the Holistic Approach."
