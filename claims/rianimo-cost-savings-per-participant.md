### Palmisano Francesco 2024

Palmisano Francesco, Santuari Nadia, Moletta Cristina, Ambrosi Elisa, Rizzoli Andrea. (2024). The effect of a team-based blended simulation training program on cardiopulmonary resuscitation on healthcare professionals' perception, performance, and costs: a mixed-method study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective cost analysis comparing delivery costs: total delivery costs were €165,024.97 (€195.31 per participant) for BLSDr versus €159,108.99 (€133.59 per participant) for riAniMO. The article states the cost per professional is 31.6% lower; no statistical test is reported.
