### Su Xiaoqun 2026

Su Xiaoqun, Hu Bing, Liu Chengcheng, Ren Hongmiao. (2026). Application of integrated whole-course clinical learning combined with case-based learning in undergraduate otolaryngology clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09871-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Assessment of admission note documentation quality in the same 79-student controlled intervention; IC median 3.21 vs LC 2.91, adjusted p < 0.01. Subdomains including vital signs documentation (p = 0.04) and otoscopic findings (p = 0.02) also favored IC.

> "Compared with those in the LC group, the students in the IC group achieved higher satisfaction scores (median [IQR]: 3.21 [3.17–3.29] vs. 2.91 [2.85–3.06]); Mann–Whitney U = 12.50; adjusted p < 0.01)."
