Ergen 2017¶

Ergen, B., & Kanadli, S. (2017). The effect of self-regulated learning strategies on academic achievement: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 55-74. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis comparing nine empirical-design studies (d = 0.767) with twelve relational-design studies (d = 0.905); Qb = 0.521, p = 0.470 under the random effects model.

"Similarly, the common effect size of the studies shows no significant difference according to whether they are relational or empirical or conducted in primary schools, secondary schools, or undergraduate programs."
