### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regularized Gaussian graphical model (EBICglasso) estimated at domain level on the 336-priest sample, with expected influence centrality and 1000-iteration bootstrap stability checks. Pastoral care was the most central node overall (standardized EI = 1.72); EI values are not standardized effect sizes.

> "On the exposure side, exposure to suicidal communication exhibited the greatest centrality (standardized EI = 0.82), whereas indirect exposure to suicide attempt/death (standardized EI =  − 0.34) and direct exposure to suicide attempt/death (standardized EI =  − 0.67) were comparatively peripheral"

### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026 (2)

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Edge-weight inspection of the same GGM showed most cross-domain partial correlations shrunk to zero; the largest bridging edge was "the indirect exposure–pastoral care edge (r = 0.145)", converging with the dimensional moderation results for untrained clergy.

> "The most prominent conditional association bridging exposure and competency domains was the indirect exposure–pastoral care edge (r = 0.145)"
