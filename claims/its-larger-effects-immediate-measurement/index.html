Evidence¶

Walter L. Leite 2025¶

Walter L. Leite, Huibin Zhang, Shibani Rana, Yide Hao, Amber D. Hatch, Lingchen Kong, Huan Kuang. (2025). Do intelligent tutoring systems benefit K-12 students? A meta-analysis and evaluation of heterogeneity of treatment effects in the U.S. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04997

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup meta-regression across measurement timing (immediate K=23, end of school year K=47). Immediate measurement showed a significantly larger effect; one study measured at end of unit and six did not report timing.

"Outcomes measured immediately after the intervention produced a larger and effect size (g=0.460) than outcomes measured at end of the school year (g=0.155). Both effects were statistically significant."
