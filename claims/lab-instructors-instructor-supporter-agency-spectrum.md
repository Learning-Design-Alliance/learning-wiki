Thematic analysis of the Personal agency code across 11 interviews. The article states "Instructor vs supporter" "represents a di- chotomy between two types of roles that lab instructors perform," with interviewees' jobs falling "at var- ious locations on a spectrum between those two roles." One staff interviewee described control over learning goals depending on which instructor they worked with that term.
