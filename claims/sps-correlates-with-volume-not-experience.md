## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chung KC 2025

Chung KC, Heiman A, Malay S, and the PRAISE Study Group. (2025). Proficiency and Difficulty Scoring Tools for Finger Replantation. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.40453

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis among the 65 surgeons' first-half SPS, years of experience, and case volume in the PRAISE cohort. The article printed "r = 0.32; P = .008" for volume and "r = −0.17; P = .19" for experience.

> "Pearson correlation coefficient showed significant correlations between SPS and prior case volume (r = 0.32; P = .008), but did not indicate significance between SPS and years of experience (r = −0.17; P = .19)."
