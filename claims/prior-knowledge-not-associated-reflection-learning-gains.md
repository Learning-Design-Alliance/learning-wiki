## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C. 2021

Fink Maximilian C., Heitzmann Nicole, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Learning to diagnose accurately through virtual patients: do reflection phases have an added benefit? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02937-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-tailed Pearson correlations across the two reflection conditions in the three-group experiment: gains in diagnostic accuracy were "not correlated with either pretest conceptual knowledge (r = .12, p = .139) or strategic knowledge (r = .10, p = .207)". Correlation magnitudes below r = .10 threshold are reported as printed; the article prints no standardized effect size framing, so i is null.

> "Across both reflection groups, the gain in diagnostic accuracy from pretest to posttest was not correlated with either pretest conceptual knowledge (r = .12, p = .139) or strategic knowledge (r = .10, p = .207)."
