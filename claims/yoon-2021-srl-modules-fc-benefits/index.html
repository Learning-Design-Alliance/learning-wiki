Evidence¶

Yan Shen 2025¶

Yan Shen, Dan Spencer, Jennifer Tagsold, Hyunsong Kim. (2025). Integrating cognition, self-regulation, motivation, and metacognition: a framework of post-pandemic flipped classroom design. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10485-y

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Review's account of Yoon et al.'s (2021) study facilitating SRL in a three-week flipped learning unit of a pre-service teacher course on technology integration, using a dashboard module visualizing progress and quiz scores and a reflection module prompting assessment, goal setting, and transfer. The experimental group outperformed the control group; no effect size is printed.

"They reported that the experimental group using the two modules exhibited higher levels of SRL skills, behavioral and cognitive engagement, and learning performance than the control group."
