`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary endpoint analyses of the cluster-randomized trial (mixed-effects models). Detection outcomes and patient pain scores were unchanged in both groups; the authors attribute this to the curriculum targeting insertion rather than withdrawal technique or lesion recognition.

> "No statistically significant pre‐ to post‐training changes were observed for ADR, mean adenomas per patient, PDR mean polyps per patient, or patient‐reported pain score in either group (Table 3)."
