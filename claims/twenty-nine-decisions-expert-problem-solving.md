## Evidence

### Argenta M. Price 2021

Argenta M. Price, Candice J. Kim, Eric W. Burkholder, Amy V. Fritz, and Carl E. Wieman. (2021). A Detailed Characterization of the Expert Problem-Solving Process in Science and Engineering; Guidance for Teaching and Assessment. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1187/cbe.20-12-0276](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-12-0276)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study: 31 semi-structured retrospective interviews with practicing scientists, engineers, and physicians, coded via directed content analysis for decisions made. The authors report "a total set of 29 decisions-to-be-made" present across all disciplines.

> "We identified a total set of 29 decisions-to-be-made (plus 5 other themes), all of which were identified in a large fraction of the interviews across all disciplines (Table 2, Fig. 2)."
