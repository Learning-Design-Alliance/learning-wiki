## Evidence

### Yilmaz Merve 2026

Yilmaz Merve, Korkmaz Fatos. (2026). Newly Graduated Nurses' Professional Competency and Caring Perception: A Cross‐Sectional Correlational Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70614

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression (proportional odds model) on data from 249 newly graduated nurses, with socio-demographic, educational and CINS total score entered as predictors of CBI-30 caring perception. The CINS score retained significance (OR = 1.07, 95% CI 1.05–1.08, p = 0.001) while other predictors did not retain statistical significance after adjustment.

> "In the multivariable model, higher professional competency, as measured by the total Competency Inventory of Nursing Students (CINS) score, was independently associated with higher levels of caring perception."
