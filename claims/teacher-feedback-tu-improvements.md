### Bellas 2023

Bellas, F., Guerreiro-Santalla, S., Naya, M., & Duro, R. J. (2023). AI Curriculum for European High Schools: An Embedded Intelligence Approach. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00315-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article reports that teacher feedback forms were analyzed and led to changes including increased duration, two levels of student guidance ('a higher one, where more information is provided... and a lower one, where more room is given to their creativity'), simplified rubrics, and App Inventor tutorials.

> "in general, the duration of the TUs has been increased by 50% with respect to the original one proposed by the UDC team."
