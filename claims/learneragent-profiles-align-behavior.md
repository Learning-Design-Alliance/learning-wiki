Simulation analysis of reasoning responses along reasoning length (average tokens per explanation) and logical connector density, shown in Figure 6. The article reports Deep Learner has the highest connector density and Lazy Learner the lowest, with Deep Learner employing more contrastive connectors.

> "Deep Learner consistently produces the longest explanations, suggesting strong cognitive engagement and a focus on explanation. Surface and Lazy Learners, in contrast, produce significantly shorter responses, reflecting minimal effort or surface-level recall."
