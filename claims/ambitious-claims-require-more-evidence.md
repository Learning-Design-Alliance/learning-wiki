### Kane 2013

Michael T. Kane. (2013). Validating the Interpretations and Uses of Test Scores. Journal of Educational Measurement. https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument from the article's framing of the argument-based approach. A limited interpretation (expected performance on tasks like the test's) needs mainly generalizability data; a construct interpretation needs evidence evaluating the theory and consistency of scores with it; predictive claims need predictive evidence.

> "The kinds of evidence required for validation are determined by the claims being made, and more-ambitious claims require more evidence than less-ambitious claims."
