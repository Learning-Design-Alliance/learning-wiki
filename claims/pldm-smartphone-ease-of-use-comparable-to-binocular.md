## Evidence

### Awad L 2024

Awad L, Abdi Z, Langridge BJ, Karoshi A, Butler PEM. (2024). A Comparison of Commercially Available Digital Microscopes for Their Use in Bench-Model Simulation of Microsurgery. Journal of Reconstructive Microsurgery. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0044-1787980

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Dunn's multiple comparisons from the 31-participant survey (Table 2) show nonsignificant ease-of-use differences for LTM vs. PLDM (mean rank difference 24.00, p = 0.1160) and LTM vs. smartphone (2.604, p > 0.9999); the article reports these devices were "deemed valuable training tools by the participants."

> "Ease of use of the PLDM and smartphone were found to be statistically comparable to the LTM and were deemed valuable training tools by the participants with physical dimensions suitable for practice, scoring better than the LTM in these domains."
