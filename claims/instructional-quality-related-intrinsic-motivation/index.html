Evidence¶

Burić 2020¶

Burić, Irena and Kim, Lisa (2020) Teacher self-efficacy, instructional quality, and student motivational beliefs: An analysis using multilevel structural equation modeling. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101302

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Same climate-effect ML-SEM analysis: paths from the three instructional quality dimensions to class-average intrinsic motivation were β = .539, .510, and .446, all p < .001. The authors note each of the three basic dimensions is "similarly important in explaining student intrinsic motivation".

"and intrinsic motivation (β = .539, p < .001; β = .510, p < .001; and β = .446, p < .001, respectively)."
