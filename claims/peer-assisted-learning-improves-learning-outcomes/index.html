Gaofeng & Yeyu 2007¶

Gaofeng, R., & Yeyu, L. (2007). An online peer assisted learning community model and its application in ZJNU. ERIC ED500172 (Online Submission). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED500172

q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding · design · r1

The article's review section defines peer assisted learning as "acquiring knowledge and skills via peers' assistance and support" and enumerates its forms, citing Topping and Ehly. The effectiveness assertion is the article's own framing rather than a tested result.

"Peer assisted learning refers to acquiring knowledge and skills via peers' assistance and support, including peer tutoring, peer modeling, peer education, peer counseling, peer monitoring and peer assessment (Topping, K & Ehly, 1998)."
