Evidence¶

Langensee 2024¶

Langensee, L., Rumetshofer, T. & Mårtensson, J. (2024). Interplay of socioeconomic status, cognition, and school performance in the ABCD sample. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00233-x

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Ordinal regression models adding cognition-by-SES interactions to the ABCD sample (N = 5001). Tables 4–5 print Cognition*Education OR 1.08 (1.00, 1.16), p = 0.04 at 2-year and OR 1.08 (1.01, 1.16), p = 0.02 at 3-year follow-up; cognition-by-ITN and cognition-by-ADI interactions were not significant.

"The results, shown in Tables 4 and 5, indicate that at both timepoints cognitive ability and parental education interact with one another in their effect on the odds of reporting higher grades in school."
