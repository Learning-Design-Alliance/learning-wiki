## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, C. Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Quantifying critical thinking: Development and validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135)

`q3 · i1` · `design · r3`

Analysis of matched closed-response pre- and post-surveys from 1911 students across 27 institutions. Pre-survey average was 5.25 ± 0.05 and post-survey 5.52 ± 0.05; the paired t-test gave p < 0.001 with "a small eﬀect size (Cohen's d = 0.23)".

> "The pre- and post-survey means are statistically diﬀerent (pairedt-test,p< 0.001) with a small eﬀect size (Cohen's d = 0.23)."
