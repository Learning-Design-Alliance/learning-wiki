## Evidence

### Liu Ganwei 2025

Liu Ganwei, Yang Feng, Zhou Zuli, Jiang Guanchao. (2025). A Novel Competency-Based Simulation Model for Thoracoscopic Lung Resection. The Thoracic and Cardiovascular Surgeon. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2702-2239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 52 surgical residents without prior thoracoscopic experience, assigned 1:1 to lung model or FLS trainer groups. The lung model group scored "87.60 ± 4.75 vs. 78.19 ± 5.54" on the posttraining anatomy test; baseline scores were similar (65.42 ± 6.10 vs. 66.12 ± 5.92; p = 0.710). No effect size is printed.

> "the lung model group demonstrated significantly greater improvement in anatomical understanding, scoring higher in the posttraining test compared with the FLS group (87.60 ± 4.75 vs. 78.19 ± 5.54; p < 0.001; Table 2 )"
