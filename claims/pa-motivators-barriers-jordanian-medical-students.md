`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results (Table 2) from the 1,209-student study: 663 (54.8%) reported engaging in physical activity; 61.8% did vigorous exercise and 74.8% moderate activities; 42.7% exercised more than 2.5 hours weekly. The authors note these findings align with prior studies reporting lack of time and energy as leading barriers.

> "The main motivations for doing exercise included maintaining health (39.9%) and stress relief (31.8%), while barriers included a lack of time (33.1%) and energy (26.9%)."
