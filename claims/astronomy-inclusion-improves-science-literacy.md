## Evidence

### Müller 2018

Müller, A. (2018). (Science) Teacher Education in the Knowledge Society: Good Practice at the University of Geneva. University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974)

`q2 · i3` · `review · r1`

The article reports, from studies of astronomy inclusion in classrooms and planetariums from primary pupils to teacher students, "positive effects for general science literacy" and specific competencies, "with many effect sizes ≥ 0.8". Table 1 lists settings including planetariums, science centres and classroom courses.

> "Results show positive effects for general science literacy (eg. the origin of seasons or the motion of Earth and Moon) or more specific competencies (eg. spatial abilities), with many effect sizes ≥ 0.8."
