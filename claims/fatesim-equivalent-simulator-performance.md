`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized trial's practical assessments on the Vimedix simulator at T2, the article reports "both groups scored well" with no significant between-group differences on either the standard cross-section test or the pathology-recognition cases.

> "Overall, both groups scored well in the DOPSSim (study: 64 ± 7 vs. control: 64 ± 8; p = 0.89) and in the DOPSSimPatho (study: 37 ± 5 vs. control: 36 ± 5; p = 0.23)."
