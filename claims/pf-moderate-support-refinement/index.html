Evidence¶

Sunita G. Chowrira 2019¶

Sunita G. Chowrira, Karen M. Smith, Patrick J. Dubois and Ido Roll. (2019). DIY productive failure: boosting performance in a large undergraduate biology course. npj Science of Learning 4:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0040-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Instructor-reported design refinement in the Methods description of the Activity phase; "PF implementation was re ﬁned to include “moderate support ”" — prompts asking what information students had, needed, and where to find it — so failure did not lead to giving up.

"During the ﬁrst iteration of PF implementation, many students found the task to be too dif ﬁcult and gave up on the activity out of sheer frustration. Taking note of this, PF implementation was re ﬁned to include “moderate support ” during subsequent iterations"
