# Four structural paths (IQ→PU, IQ→LS, PU→LS, PU→CI) differ significantly across the three online course modalities

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Multi-group analysis revealed significant between-group differences in the IQ→PU, IQ→LS, PU→LS, and PU→CI paths. [→ Moon 2026](#moon-2026)

## Evidence

### Moon 2026

Moon, Eunkyung and Shin, Won Sug. (2026). Exploring synchronous, asynchronous, and conventional online courses in higher education. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0345955

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM with cross-group equality constraints on nine paths, comparing nine constrained models against an unconstrained baseline for the 795-respondent sample. The study reports these four "significantly different between the three groups" with the printed Δχ2 and p values; no effect sizes for the differences are printed.

> "the paths from IQ to PU (P2; Δχ2 = 9.334, p = 0.009), IQ to LS (P6; Δχ2 = 7.236, p = 0.027), PU to LS (P7; Δχ2 = 14.47, p = 0.001), and PU to CI (P9; Δχ2 = 7.03, p = 0.03) were significantly different between the three groups."
