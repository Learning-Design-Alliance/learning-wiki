## Evidence

### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2023

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Tan Wen-Wu, Tan Hung-Jia, Khoo Chia-Wee, Afrashtehfar Kelvin I. (2023). Innovative Pedagogical Strategies in Health Professions Education: Active Learning in Dental Materials Science. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20032041

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RCT (80 second-year undergraduate students, India, per Table 2) comparing flipped classroom with traditional classroom; perceptions favored FC but knowledge assessments were comparable, with no significant difference reported and no effect size printed.

> "Joshi, J.P. et al. [18] revealed that students perceived the FC model to be better than the traditional classroom model. However, the knowledge assessments among students were comparable across both models."

### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2023 (2)

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Tan Wen-Wu, Tan Hung-Jia, Khoo Chia-Wee, Afrashtehfar Kelvin I. (2023). Innovative Pedagogical Strategies in Health Professions Education: Active Learning in Dental Materials Science. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20032041

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled before-and-after study (Binnie et al.) evaluating undergraduate dental students' satisfaction after implementing a flipped classroom in an applied dental materials and biomaterials course; findings were satisfaction perceptions only.

> "Most students expressed satisfaction with the FC delivery method, duration of teaching, material accessibility, and content understanding. Most students also concurred that the FC strategy was enjoyable and effective."
