## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of 4 experts vs 10 nonexperts (novices plus gastroenterologists) on simulator metrics. Endoscope path length contrasts were also significant (e.g., 50 vs 191 cm, P = .01; 250 vs 527 cm, P = .04).

> "time from exiting stomach to application of first clip (74 vs 194 seconds, P = .04), time from application of first clip to application of last clip (83 vs 228 seconds, P = .04), time from application of last clip to completed dissection of gallbladder from liver bed (333 vs 683 seconds, P = .02)"
