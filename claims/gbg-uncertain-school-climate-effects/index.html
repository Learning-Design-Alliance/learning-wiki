What Works Clearinghouse 2023¶

q2 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of one study (Smith et al., 2018), a cluster randomized trial of the PAX Good Behavior Game in 73 after-school programs in Pennsylvania, grades 2 to 5, with 10 observed climate measures; the summary reports an average effect size of 0.15 that is not statistically significant, described as "uncertain effects 0.15 +6 No (p=0.43)".
