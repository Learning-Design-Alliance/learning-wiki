## Evidence

### Abshire Martha A. 2020

Abshire Martha A., Li Xintong, Basyal Pragyashree Sharma, Teply Melissa L., Singh Arun L., Hayes Margaret M., Turnbull Alison E. (2020). Actor feedback and rigorous monitoring: Essential quality assurance tools for testing behavioral interventions with simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233538

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Poisson regression of QA error counts with calendar dates grouped into four periods around the three group feedback sessions; the period before the first session was the reference. The article reports incidence in "periods 1–3" was "significantly lower than the incidence prior to the first group feedback session (Table 2)" with P for trend <0.001 in Table 2.

> "The incidence of errors in simulations performed after one, two, or three group feedback sessions (periods 1–3) were also significantly lower than the incidence prior to the first group feedback session (Table 2)."

### Abshire Martha A. 2020 (2)

Abshire Martha A., Li Xintong, Basyal Pragyashree Sharma, Teply Melissa L., Singh Arun L., Hayes Margaret M., Turnbull Alison E. (2020). Actor feedback and rigorous monitoring: Essential quality assurance tools for testing behavioral interventions with simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233538

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same adjusted Poisson analysis of major errors across study periods. The article reports the adjusted incidence rate of major errors "decreased by 75% (IRR = 0.25, 95% CI = 0.14–0.42)" after the first group feedback session, the largest period effect reported; the IRR is a rate ratio, not a standardized effect size.

> "This improvement was most evident after the first group feedback session when the adjusted incidence rate of major errors decreased by 75% (IRR = 0.25, 95% CI = 0.14–0.42)."
