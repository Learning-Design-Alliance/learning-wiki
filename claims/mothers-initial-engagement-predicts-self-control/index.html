Tiago Ferreira 2018¶

Tiago Ferreira, Joana Cadima, Marisa Matias, Joana Marina Vieira, and Teresa Leal; Karine Verschueren; Paula Mena Matos. (2018). Trajectories of Parental Engagement in Early Childhood Among Dual-Earner Families: Effects on Child Self-Control. Developmental Psychology, 54(4), 731–743. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000458

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent growth curve model linking mothers' engagement trajectory to teacher-rated child self-control at T3, controlling for T1 self-control, age, and gender. The article reports "the initial levels of engagement (intercept) had a positive effect on child self-control" with a standardized coefficient of .25.

"For mothers, the initial levels of engagement (intercept) had a positive effect on child self-control (b /H11005.35, p /H11021.001, 95% CI [.22, .47], /H9252/H11005.25)."
