## Evidence

### Ekmark Sergel S 2026

Ekmark Sergel S, Lundberg M, Magnusson C, Ådén U, Bergman G, Siljehav V. (2026). Academic Achievements in Adolescents With Congenital Heart Disease: A Total Population‐Based Cohort Study. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.049927

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Exploratory birth-cohort stratification in the register cohort, reported in Results with adjusted relative risks by era (Figure 3). The analysis "did not reveal any change in the prevalence of qualification for higher education over time"; no effect size is printed for this null comparison.

> "In our exploratory analysis, stratifying by surgical eras based on birth year, before (1987–1991), during (1992–1996), and after the centralization of pediatric heart surgery (1997–2005), did not reveal any change in the prevalence of qualification for higher education over time across all complexities of CHD in children without noncardiac MCA (Figure 3)."
