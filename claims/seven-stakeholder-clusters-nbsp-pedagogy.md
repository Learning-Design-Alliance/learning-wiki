## Evidence

### Pywell S. 2025

Pywell S., Wallace C., Wallace S., Davies M. (2025). Inclusive and effective teaching of Nature-Based Social Prescribing for allied health professional students within digital environments. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1694834

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Hierarchical cluster analysis (Ward's Method) of sorting and rating data in the GCM software produced the seven-cluster map. The study reports SD values per cluster, with the "nature as part of self-development" and "experiencing nature" clusters showing the lowest SD of 0.063, indicating "highest congruence". No effect size is printed.

> "The cluster map (Figure 3) of participant responses illustrates 7 key themes created by participants alongside Standard Deviation (SD) values: 1. Environmental careers (SD = 0.079), 2. Other benefits from this approach and learning (SD = 0.10), 3. Politics (SD = 0.15), 4. Educators should provide (SD = 0.096), 5. Nature as part of self-development and curriculum learning (SD = 0.063), 6. Experiencing nature to foster connectedness with nature (SD = 0.063), 7. Virtual Reality-based activities (SD = 0.14)."
