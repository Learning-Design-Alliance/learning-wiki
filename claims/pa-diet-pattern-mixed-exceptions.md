### Chacón-Cuberos 2018

Chacón-Cuberos, R.; Zurita-Ortega, F.; Martínez-Martínez, A.; Olmedo-Moreno, E.M.; Castro-Sánchez, M. (2018). Adherence to the Mediterranean Diet Is Related to Healthy Habits, Learning Processes, and Academic Achievement in Adolescents: A Cross-Sectional Study. Nutrients 10(11):1566. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu10111566

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 2 t-tests comparing active and less active adolescents: the article's prose calls the differences "higher intake of fruit or fruit juice every day" and "a higher consumption of sweets", but the printed means (0.30 vs. 0.49 fruit; 0.79 vs. 0.63 sweets) show the active group lower on fruit and higher on sweets. No standardised effect size printed.

> "On the other hand, adolescents who engaged in PA also reported higher intake of fruit or fruit juice every day (0.30 ± 0.45 vs. 0.49 ± 0.50, p < 0.001), consumption of legumes (0.12 ± 0.33 vs. 0.21 ± 0.40, p < 0.001) and a higher consumption of sweets (0.79 ± 0.40 vs. 0.63 ± 0.48, p < 0.001)."
