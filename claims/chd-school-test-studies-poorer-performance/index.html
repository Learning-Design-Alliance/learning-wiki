Academic achievement and needs of school‐aged children born with selected congenital anomalies: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

The review's narrative synthesis of four large school‐test studies (NAPLAN, Arkansas Benchmark, Danish and North Carolina end‐of‐grade tests). The review reports consistent poorer performance in children with CHD compared to reference children; no pooled effect size was printed for this narrative synthesis.

"Large studies from the USA, Denmark and Australia using school test results were consistent in reporting poorer academic performance in children with CHD compared to the reference children (Lawley et al., 2019; Mulkey et al., 2016; Olsen et al., 2011; Oster et al., 2017) (Table 2)."
