Evidence¶

Roessingh 2006¶

Roessingh, H. (2006). BICS-CALP: An Introduction for Some, a Review for Others. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 23(2), Spring 2006. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v23i2.57

q1 · i? · review · r1

The author offers an explicit estimate, stating "My guess is somewhere around 10,000-12,000 words" for the critical mass of vocabulary needed for fluent reading, tied to a reading age of grade equivalent 5. This is an expert estimate, not a tested result.

"My guess is somewhere around 10,000-12,000 words, and this includes the 3,000-5,000 high-frequency words, the begin- nings of the academic word list (AWL), and a few thousand common, but not high-frequency, words."
