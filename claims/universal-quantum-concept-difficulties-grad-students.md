## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing and improving student understanding of quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05664](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05664)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written survey of 202 first-year graduate students at seven universities plus think-aloud interviews with 15 beginning graduate students or physics seniors. The author reports common difficulties "regardless of where they are enrolled" despite between-university performance differences.

> "Although students from some universities performed better on average than others, we ﬁnd that students have common difﬁcul- ties with many basic concepts in quantum mechanics, re- gardless of where they are enrolled."
