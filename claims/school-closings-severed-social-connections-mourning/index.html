q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Findings from interviews at six welcoming schools in the qualitative portion of the study. Interviewees reported closures severed "the longstanding social connections that families and staff had with their schools and with one another".

"When schools closed, it severed the longstanding social connections that families and staff had with their schools and with one another, resulting in a period of mourning."

Molly F. Gordon 2017 (2)¶

UChicago Consortium Research Report. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview evidence from staff at six designated welcoming schools. Staff requested more support for community merging because integrating different school cultures created "us" vs. "them" dynamics in many cases.

"Staff wished for more support in merging closed and welcoming school communities because, in many cases, integrating the different school cultures created challenging "us" vs. "them" dynamics."
