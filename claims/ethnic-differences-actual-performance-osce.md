## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2014

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Myers Gil, Khatib Yasmin, Dacre Jane. (2014). How well do doctors think they perform on the General Medical Council's Tests of Competence pilot examinations? A cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-004131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA on actual scores by ethnic background in the 524-doctor study, followed by Fisher's least significant difference tests. The article reports significant differences on actual knowledge test performance (F=(5516)=4.46, p<0.01) and OSCE performance (F=(5518)=11.48, p<0.01), and that white doctors outperformed all other ethnic groups on OSCE performance.

> "A one-way ANOVA confirmed that there were significant differences by ethnic background on actual knowledge test performance (F=(5516)=4.46, p<0.01) and OSCE performance (F=(5518)=11.48, p<0.01)."
