`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of the agency-supportive theme family (118 of 123 included studies) in this scoping review. The review reports that GenAI may support self-regulated learning, self-efficacy, and feedback literacy, with benefits emerging "when learners use it as a learning scaffold rather than as a substitute for task completion."

> "Multiple studies suggest that GenAI can facilitate goal setting, planning, monitoring, and self-evaluation when learners use it as a learning scaffold rather than as a substitute for task completion."
