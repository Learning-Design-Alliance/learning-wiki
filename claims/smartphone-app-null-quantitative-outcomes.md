# Access to a novel smartphone application for six weeks produced no statistically significant difference from control in postgraduate clinical psychology trainees' academic self-efficacy or self-reported learning outcomes at weeks 1, 3, or 6

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Academic self-efficacy (NGSES-E) did not differ significantly between experimental and control groups at any of weeks 1, 3, or 6. [→ Choo Carol C. 2019](#choo-carol-c-2019)
`q2 i?` Self-reported learning outcomes (SLO) did not differ significantly between experimental and control groups at any of weeks 1, 3, or 6. [→ Choo Carol C. 2019 (2)](#choo-carol-c-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Choo Carol C. 2019

Choo Carol C., Devakaran Bhavani, Chew Peter K. H., Zhang Melvyn W. B. (2019). Smartphone Application in Postgraduate Clinical Psychology Training: Trainees' Perspectives. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214206

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the randomized eight-participant pilot, six Mann-Whitney U tests compared NGSES-E scores across weeks 1, 3 and 6; week 6 gave "U = 4; z = −0.95, p = 0.341", and weeks 1 and 3 were likewise non-significant (p = 0.44, p = 0.442). No effect size is printed.

> "In week 6, Academic SE level of the experimental group (Mean Rank = 3.9) was not significantly different to those in the control group (Mean Rank = 5.5), U = 4; z = −0.95, p = 0.341, two tailed."

### Choo Carol C. 2019 (2)

Choo Carol C., Devakaran Bhavani, Chew Peter K. H., Zhang Melvyn W. B. (2019). Smartphone Application in Postgraduate Clinical Psychology Training: Trainees' Perspectives. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214206

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same series of six Mann-Whitney U tests, SLO scores showed no significant group difference at week 6 ("U = 3.5; z = −1.2, p = 0.230"); weeks 1 and 3 were also non-significant (p = 0.88, p = 0.180). Descriptive means appear in Table 1; no effect size is printed.

> "SLO level of the experimental group (Mean Rank = 5.3) was not significantly different to those in the control group (Mean Rank = 3.17), U = 3.5; z = −1.2, p = 0.230, two tailed."
