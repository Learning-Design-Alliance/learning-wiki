## Evidence

### Jiang N 2024

Jiang N, Zhang Y, Liang S, Lyu X, Chen S, Huang X, Pan H. (2024). Effectiveness of Virtual Simulations Versus Mannequins and Real Persons in Medical and Nursing Education: Meta-Analysis and Trial Sequential Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/56195

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 5 RCTs assessing communication skills; all 5 compared VSs to real persons. Pooled SMD=–0.02 (95% CI –0.62 to 0.58) was not significant; TSA showed the inner wedge area had been reached. Leave-one-out analysis was consistent.

> "the pooled effect size did not reflect a significant difference between VSs and mannequins or RPs on the outcome of procedural skills (SMD=–0.02; 95% CI –0.62 to 0.58; I2=86%; P<.01)"
