# The LCAS discovery and relevance scale differentiates CURE students from traditional lab students with a medium-large effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` CURE students rated their courses significantly higher on the discovery and relevance scale than traditional lab students (p < 0.001, d = 0.71). [→ Corwin Lisa A. 2015](#corwin-lisa-a-2015)

## Evidence

### Corwin Lisa A. 2015

Corwin Lisa A., Runyon Christopher, Robinson Aspen, Dolan Erin L. (2015). The Laboratory Course Assessment Survey: A Tool to Measure Three Dimensions of Research-Course Design. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0073

`q3 · i2` · `design · r3`

Known-groups comparison of students who could be clearly identified as CURE (n = 72) versus traditional lab (n = 61) participants, using Welch-corrected t tests. The discovery and relevance comparison reached p < 0.001 with "d = 0.71", described by the authors as medium to medium-large.

> "CURE students also rated their courses higher on the discovery and relevance (p < 0.001, d = 0.71) and iteration (p < 0.05, d = 0.39) scales than students in traditional lab courses."
