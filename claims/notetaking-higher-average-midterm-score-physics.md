## Evidence

### Adhikari 2025

Adhikari, C. M. (2025). Effective Note-taking and its Impact on Learning Undergraduate Introductory Physics Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21326](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21326)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Statistical comparison in Table II of the two teaching approaches: averages of 75.3 (no notetaking, n=69) versus 85.4 (notetaking, n=66), standard deviations 14.4 versus 11.0, lowest scores 31 versus 55. The article reports "The average score in the class improved significantly (∼10%)"; no standardized effect size is printed.
