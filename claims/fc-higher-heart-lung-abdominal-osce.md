## Evidence

### Zhang Weihao 2021

Zhang Weihao, Gu Jiaoyang, Li Fan, Feng Feifei, Chen Huiqiang, Xing Xiaowei, Liu Lan. (2021). The effect of flipped classroom in multiple clinical skills training for clinical interns on Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE). Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.2013405

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two independent sample t-tests on OSCE station scores from the randomized study (TC n = 37, FC n = 42) showed "significant differences in heart physical examination", lung, and abdominal examination, all P < 0.050, with FC means higher on each station.

> "There were significant differences in heart physical examination (96.670 ± 1.300 vs. 95.000 ± 2.380, P < 0.050), lung physical examination (96.830 ± 2.219 vs. 93.510 ± 1.938, P < 0.050), abdominal physical examination (96.550 ± 1.292 vs. 94.620 ± 1.831, P < 0.050)"
