## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tudge 1986

Tudge, Jon. (1986). Beyond Conflict: The Role of Reasoning in Collaborative Problem Solving. Paper presented at the Annual Symposium of the Jean Piaget Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED275395

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of paired conditions against the control group of 41 individuals in a pretest-treatment-two-posttest design with 156 children aged 5-9 on a balance-beam task; the reported difference was not significant. The article notes effects were "a good deal less apparent than is the case with interaction between conservers and nonconservers".

> "Compared to the control group of individuals, pairs did only slightly better - a difference that was not significant."
