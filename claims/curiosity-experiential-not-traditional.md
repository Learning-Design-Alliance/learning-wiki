### Okur-Berberoglu 2017

Okur-Berberoglu, E. (2017). Outdoor experimental environmental education: An adult-centred intervention for the affective domain. International Electronic Journal of Environmental Education, 7(1), 34-58. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130600

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of reflections from the three groups in the 2011 TUBITAK-funded case study. The article reports "8 participants from EG and 3 from CG1" expressed curiosity, with no curiosity expressions in CG2.

> "The outdoor and indoor education programmes were effective in terms of stimulating the affective domain concept of curiosity among participants, while the traditional education methods did not yield any results in this domain. The EG’s outdoor experiential education programme proved to be more effective than the indoor version. 8 participants from EG and 3 from CG1 reflected themselves with data referring to curiosity about the environmental subjects they were taught (Table 1)."
