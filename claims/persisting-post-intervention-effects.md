## Evidence

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RQ4 comparison of the post-intervention block to the first baseline in the goal-reminder condition: median daily visits fell from 29.4 to 9.8, "p = 0.003, r = 0.72". Interviews suggest a habit of asking about one's purpose when visiting.

> "In terms of amount of use, participants in C goal made fewer daily visits post-intervention compared to baseline (me- dian number of daily visits in first baseline = 29.4, median in post-intervention block = 9.8, p = 0.003, r = 0.72, Wilcoxon signed rank test)."

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020 (2)

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RQ4 comparison of post-intervention to baseline in the newsfeed-removal condition: reported passive newsfeed browsing was lower after the intervention ended, "p = 0.007, r = 0.78"; some participants said they felt less attracted by the newsfeed when it returned.

> "In terms of patterns of use, participants in C no-feed reported less passive browsing of the newsfeed post-intervention com- pared to baseline (p = 0.007, r = 0.78, Wilcoxon signed rank test)."
