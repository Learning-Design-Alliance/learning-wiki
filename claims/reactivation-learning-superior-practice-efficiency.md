## Evidence

### Gilad Schrift 2022

Gilad Schrift, Dror Dotan and Nitzan Censor. (2022). Brief memory reactivations induce learning in the numeric domain. npj Science of Learning 7:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00136-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 one-way ANOVA on practice efficiency, defined as improvement between baseline and retest divided by practice time in minutes, comparing n = 21 reactivation learners with n = 29 standard practice learners; F 1,48 = 22.73, P = 0.00002. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "subjects who exhibited reactivation-induced learning (n = 21) were characterized by superior ef ﬁciency relative to the standard group subjects who showed learning (n = 29; F 1,48 = 22.73, P = 0.00002)"
