## Evidence

### Chen Minjian 2018

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. `q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sensitivity analysis in which each of the 13 studies was deleted in turn; the significant TBL effect was unchanged (SMD 2.26–2.67). A fixed-effects model also gave a significant effect (SMD = 1.63, 95% CI: 1.51–1.76), and Begg's test gave P = 0.059.

> "When any research was removed from the model, the significant results of TBL effect on the students’ examination scores were unchanged in these models (SMD: 2.26–2.67, 95% CI: 1.32–3.67, n = 13)"
