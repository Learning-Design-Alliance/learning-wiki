# The pooled TBL effect on examination scores is robust to leave-one-out sensitivity analysis and model choice, and publication bias was not detected

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Deleting any single study left the significant TBL effect unchanged (SMD range 2.26–2.67), and a fixed-effects model yielded a consistent significant effect (SMD = 1.63). [→ Chen Minjian 2018](#chen-minjian-2018)
`q3 i?` Begg's test did not reveal funnel plot asymmetry (P = 0.059), making publication bias unlikely. [→ Chen Minjian 2018](#chen-minjian-2018)

## Evidence

### Chen Minjian 2018

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. (2018). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of team-based learning on medical education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1179-1

`q3 · i?`

Sensitivity analysis in which each of the 13 studies was deleted in turn; the significant TBL effect was unchanged (SMD 2.26–2.67). A fixed-effects model also gave a significant effect (SMD = 1.63, 95% CI: 1.51–1.76), and Begg's test gave P = 0.059.

> "When any research was removed from the model, the significant results of TBL effect on the students’ examination scores were unchanged in these models (SMD: 2.26–2.67, 95% CI: 1.32–3.67, n = 13)"
