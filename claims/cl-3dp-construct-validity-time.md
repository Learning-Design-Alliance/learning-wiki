## Evidence

### Massey Patrick A. 2025

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial arm with five medical students and five expert surgeons on the 3D-printed simulator; tasks were videorecorded and timed. Students "took longer to complete arthroscopic tasks than the expert surgeons on the 3DP simulator" (385 s ± 122 vs. 248 s ± 45, P = 0.046).

> "The medical students also took longer to complete arthroscopic tasks than the expert surgeons on the 3DP simulator (385 s ± 122 vs. 248 s ± 45, P = 0.046; Figure 3)."
