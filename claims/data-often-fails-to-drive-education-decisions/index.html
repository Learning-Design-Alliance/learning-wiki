Evidence¶

Various authors 2013¶

Various authors. (2013). Developing a Coherent Plan for Effectively Using Data (In Focus Brief). Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/developing-a-coherent-plan-for-effectively-using-data

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The brief's framing statement, offered as expert observation without cited data or studies. It asserts that data can "leave educators and policymakers unmoved" or drown them in extraneous information rather than drive decisions improving instruction and achievement.

"In practice, however, it is all too easy for data to leave educators and policymakers unmoved—or, worse yet, to drown them in extraneous information, rather than drive decisions that will improve classroom instruction, school performance, and student achievement."

Mathematica Policy Research 2013¶

Mathematica Policy Research. (2013). Developing and Enhancing Data Systems (In Focus Brief). Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/developing-and-enhancing-data-systems

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The brief asserts, without presenting evidence or data, that it is "all too easy" for data to fail to drive decisions in education; it reports no frequency. This is the authors' framing statement for why guidance on developing and enhancing data systems is needed.

"In practice, however, it is all too easy for data to leave educators and policymakers unmoved—or, worse yet, to drown them in extraneous information, rather than drive decisions that will improve classroom instruction, school performance, and student achievement."
