## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors assert that because the student population differs, the goals, skills, and content of the algebra-based course should differ, weighting topics with health-science applications such as fluid flow, optics and waves. [→ Thacker 2007](#thacker-2007)

## Evidence

### Thacker 2007

Thacker, B., Diaz, A., & Eligon, A. (2007). The Development of an Inquiry-based Curriculum Specifically for the Introductory Algebra-based Physics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702247](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702247)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the authors' design assertion, not a tested result. They operationalize it in four goals, including that "students learn physics concepts that will be useful to them in their future careers," e.g. more time on fluid flow, optics and waves because of health science applications.

> "Because the needs (both content and skills), motivation, interests and learning styles of the students in the algebra-based physics course are significantly different from those of other populations of physics students, the goals of the course should be significantly different than the goals of other physics courses."
