Martín Monteiro, Cecilia Stari, Arturo C. Marti. (2022). Mobile-device sensors: an innovative tool in the teaching of physical sciences. Modelling in Science Education and Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.12064](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.12064)

In the Conclusions the authors argue the advantages are numerous: students perceive the devices as ordinary objects, availability keeps prices accessible, and several sensors can be used simultaneously without other communication instruments. They state physics-education research must still evaluate "si se observan mejoras en los resultados de aprendizaje"; this is the authors' interpretation, with no learning-outcome data reported in the article.
