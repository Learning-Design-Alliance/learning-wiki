## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2019

Chrispeels H. E., Chapman J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2019). Peer Teaching Increases Knowledge and Changes Perceptions about Genetically Modified Crops in Non–Science Major Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post assessment of a single cohort (n = 37 analyzed) in a nonmajors biology course with no control group. Before SLP participation, "46% agreed with the statement that GMOs should be part of our food supply."

> "Before SLP participation, 46% agreed with the statement that GMOs should be part of our food supply, 27% disagreed, and 11% responded that they were uncertain"

### Chrispeels H. E. 2019 (2)

Chrispeels H. E., Chapman J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2019). Peer Teaching Increases Knowledge and Changes Perceptions about Genetically Modified Crops in Non–Science Major Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-SLP assessment of the same cohort (n = 37) showed "97% agreed with the statement that GMO crops should be part of the food supply" and only one student conditionally agreed.

> "After SLP participation, 97% agreed with the statement that GMO crops should be part of the food supply, and 3% (1 student) conditionally agreed."
