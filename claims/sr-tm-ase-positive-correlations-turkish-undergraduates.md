### Çobanoğlu Necati 2026

Çobanoğlu Necati, Gökalp Serkan. (2026). Psychological skills and their educational outcomes in adolescence: self-regulation, time management, and academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04368-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of survey data from 450 undergraduates at a public university in Turkey. The study reports a "strong, positive, and statistically significant relationship between self-regulation and time management (r = 0.81, p<0.001)".

> "Correlation analysis revealed a strong, positive, and statistically significant relationship between self-regulation and time management (r = 0.81, p<0.001)."

### Çobanoğlu Necati 2026 (2)

Çobanoğlu Necati, Gökalp Serkan. (2026). Psychological skills and their educational outcomes in adolescence: self-regulation, time management, and academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04368-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same Pearson correlation analysis of the 450-student sample. The article reports "moderate, positive, and statistically significant correlations between self-regulation and academic self-efficacy (r = 0.56, p<0.001)".

> "There were also moderate, positive, and statistically significant correlations between self-regulation and academic self-efficacy (r = 0.56, p<0.001), as well as between time management and academic self-efficacy (r = 0.69, p<0.001)."
