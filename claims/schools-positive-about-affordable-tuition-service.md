`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Findings from the process-evaluation interviews with senior leaders, classroom teachers, pupils and tutors. The report adds that "All teaching staff and senior leaders interviewed believed that there was a need for more affordable, high quality tuition".

> "Schools involved in the qualitative interviews were positive about the tuition, keen to work with the Tutor Trust again, and largely confident that the tuition was beneficial for their pupils."
