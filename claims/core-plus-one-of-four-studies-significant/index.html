Core-Plus Mathematics evidence review 2019¶

Core-Plus Mathematics evidence review. (2019). https://www.coreplusmath.org

q2 · i? · review · r?

Evidence review of the qualifying studies, in which students were tested each year on a math subscale of the Iowa Test of Educational Development focusing on mathematical thinking and problem solving. The review reports that "one out of four studies found significant positive outcomes."

"On the ITED, one out of four studies found significant positive outcomes but this was sufficient to qualify Core-Plus Mathematics for the “Moderate” category."
