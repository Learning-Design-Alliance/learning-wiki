## Evidence

### Rogan A 2026

Rogan A, Watt E, Murphy S, Wheeler E, Woods L, Galwankar S, Peckler B. (2026). Worth the Wait? Comparison of Emergency Department Patients' Waiting Room Tolerance for Real Patient Care vs Training/Simulation Scenarios. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.48916

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise comparisons from the mixed-effects model on 827 responses. The 0–20 minute option was chosen for "simulation scenarios (Q3 = 41.6%, Q7 =. 49.8%)" versus "4.5–34.6%" for other medical scenarios; the paper reports P < .001 for this contrast.

> "Respondents were more likely to indicate that a wait time of 0–20 minutes was acceptable for simulation scenarios (Q3 = 41.6%, Q7 =. 49.8%) than for other medical scenarios (ranging from 4.5–34.6%; see Table S2)."
