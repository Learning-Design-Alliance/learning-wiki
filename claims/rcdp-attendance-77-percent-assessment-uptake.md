Participation outcomes in the observational study of non-critical care learners rotating October 2022 to June 2023. The article reports 77% average attendance among available learners and 23/85 (27%) completing the week-4 assessment; assessed and non-assessed learners did not differ in training level (P = 0.98).

> "The average attendance to the RCDP sessions was 77% among available learners. Twenty-three (23) learners completed the assessment scenario on week #4, representing 27% (23/85) of the overall cohort of learners who rotated in our ICU during the study period."
