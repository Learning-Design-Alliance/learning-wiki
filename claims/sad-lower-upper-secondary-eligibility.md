Logistic regression on the compulsory-education subcohort comparing eligibility recorded in the National School Register. The article prints "77.3% in the exposed group were eligible for a vocational programme, compared to 91.4%" with the corresponding adjusted odds ratios for both programme types.

> "77.3% in the exposed group were eligible for a vocational programme, compared to 91.4% in the unexposed group, aOR = 0.31 (95% CI 0.30–0.33); and 46.4% in the exposed group were eligible for an academic programme, compared to 62.7% in the unexposed group, aOR = 0.52 (95% CI 0.50–0.55)"
