# Pleasure and Certainty ratings are strongly inter-correlated, questioning the independence of the subjective-experience dimensions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In RAPM data, Pleasure correlated with Certainty at r = .820, so only Certainty was retained in further analyses. [→ Adam Chuderski 2021](#adam-chuderski-2021)

## Evidence

### Adam Chuderski 2021

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational validation of the four ratings: Pleasure and Certainty correlated r = .712 on insight problems and r = .782 on analytic problems (each BF10 > 999), and r = .820 in the RAPM data, so Pleasure was dropped as redundant.

> "This conjecture was confirmed by checking the RAPM data, which yielded an even stronger correlation between the two ratings, r = .820."
