## Evidence

### Kate Knill 2024

Kate Knill, Diane Nicholls, Mark J.F. Gales, Mengjie Qian, Pawel Stroinski. (2024). Speak & Improve Corpus 2025: an L2 English Speech Corpus for Language Assessment and Feedback. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.11986](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.11986)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of corpus selection in Section 4.2: Dev and Eval sets were defined from full test submissions only, extended with SLA-only submissions, selected so the grade distribution was as even as possible while reflecting platform users. Table 4 shows the Dev set majority in the B1 to B2+ range.

> "300 fully annotated test submissions were selected for each of the Dev and Eval test sets. These were extended for spoken language assessment (SLA) with a further (approximately) 150 submissions which had undergone phase 1 holistic proficiency scoring only."
