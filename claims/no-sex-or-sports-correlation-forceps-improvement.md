## Evidence

### Kamimura Go 2025

Kamimura Go, Maeda Koki, Aoki Masaya, Imamura Satomi, Morizono Shoichiro, Tokunaga Takuya, Umehara Tadashi, Harada-Takeda Aya, Nagata Toshiyuki, Ueda Kazuhiro. (2025). Importance of future motivation for skill acquisition among medical students undergoing endoscopic forceps training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07048-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of the speed of skill acquisition in the 98-student cohort found "no correlation between sex (p = 0.18) or sports activity (p = 0.64) and improvement in endoscopic forceps skills"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Focusing on the speed of skill acquisition, there was no correlation between sex (p = 0.18) or sports activity (p = 0.64) and improvement in endoscopic forceps skills."

### Kamimura Go 2025 (2)

Kamimura Go, Maeda Koki, Aoki Masaya, Imamura Satomi, Morizono Shoichiro, Tokunaga Takuya, Umehara Tadashi, Harada-Takeda Aya, Nagata Toshiyuki, Ueda Kazuhiro. (2025). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sports-activity stratum of the same analysis of skill-acquisition speed in the 98-student cohort: "no correlation between ... sports activity (p = 0.64) and improvement in endoscopic forceps skills"; no effect size printed.

> "Focusing on the speed of skill acquisition, there was no correlation between sex (p = 0.18) or sports activity (p = 0.64) and improvement in endoscopic forceps skills."
