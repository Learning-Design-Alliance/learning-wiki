q2 · i? · design · r2

Interactive polling during the session, including a game-like activity asking attendees to judge whether images were AI-generated. The article reports attendees "correctly identified AI-generated images in 82% of responses" and gave rationales such as images being "too perfect". No inferential statistics were printed.

"Attendees correctly identified AI-generated images in 82% of responses and also explain their rationales."

q2 · i? · design · r2

Authors' characterization of the polling design in the session feedback section: polling prompted attendees to externalize uncertainty and reasoning, creating "a shared learning space where participants could build understanding collectively".

"To conclude, the polling activities served as a scaffold for discussion instead of a measurement tool."
