Evidence¶

Reisslein 2004¶

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

In the 2x2 factorial experiment with 51 high school students without prior circuit-analysis knowledge, an ANOVA on near-transfer posttest scores found a significant prompt-format effect, "F(1,47) = 6.50, MSE = 9.69, p = .01", with Cohen's f = .37 approaching a large effect.

"This difference on the near transfer posttest scores was statistically significant F(1,47) = 6.50, MSE = 9.69, p = .01. Cohen's f statistic for these data yields an effect size estimate of .37 for the near -transfer posttest problems, which approaches a large effect."
