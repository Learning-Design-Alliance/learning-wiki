## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student understanding of symmetry and Gauss’s law of electricity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07376](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07376)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Distractor analysis of Problem 13 responses on the multiple-choice test; interviews indicate students retained a shielding notion despite reminders that the object is non-conducting, and some drew Gaussian surfaces inside the cube to argue zero field.

> "Problem 13 was the most diﬃcult question on the test and only 21% of the introductory students responded correctly. 55% of the students believed that the electric ﬁeld inside a non-conducting hollow cube with charge uniformly distributed on its surface will be zero everywhere."
