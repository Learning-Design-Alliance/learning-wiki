Cross-site qualitative workforce finding from the case studies. Local resources and government structures influenced sites' ability to improve ECE workforce preparation and compensation; San Francisco used city dollars for citywide professional development and coaching, and its local commercial tax enabled at-scale raises.

"However, large disparities in pay persist between TK teachers and other publicly funded preschool teachers. San Francisco is the only site that has been able to raise teacher qualifications and compensation at scale due to its local commercial tax."
