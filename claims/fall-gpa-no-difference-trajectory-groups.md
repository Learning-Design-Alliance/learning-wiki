### Yunusova Asal 2026

Yunusova Asal, Huang Caitlin, Price Stephen, Joshi Kunal, Dutcher Janine M., Brown Kirk Warren, Villalba Daniella K., Tumminia Michael J., Creswell Kasey G., Cohen Sheldon, Creswell J. David. (2026). Depression trajectories and their associations with academic performance and sleep patterns among first-year college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04404-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baseline-equivalence ANOVA on registrar-obtained fall GPA among N = 271 first-year students across the four trajectory groups, reported before the spring GPA analysis. Only a test statistic is printed; no effect size, and equivalence was not tested.

> "Prior to examining spring term GPA, we examined whether trajectory groups were associated with fall term GPA to understand whether students in these groups were heading into the spring semester with similar academic achievement. A one-way ANOVA revealed there was no difference in previous academic (fall) term GPA between the four groups (F(3, 267) = 1.50, p = 0.22)."
