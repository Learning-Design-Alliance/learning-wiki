The NAEP 2019 district-level results report changes since 2017 for the 27 participating Trial Urban District Assessment districts, stating scores were "relatively stable since 2017" with most declines in grade 8 reading. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Average mathematics and reading scores across the TUDA districts were relatively stable since 2017. Across both grades, 7 districts had higher mathematics scores as opposed to 1 increase in reading at grade 8 only."
