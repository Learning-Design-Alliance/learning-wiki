## Evidence

### Stella Y. Kim 2026

Stella Y. Kim, Ji Yae Bong, Daniel Maxwell, Beth Oyarzun. (2026). Development and validation of Generative Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Perception (GenAI-RP) Scale for faculty and students in higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10713-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CFA of the student benefit scale (N=454) yielded an excellent fit (CFI 0.91, RMSEA 0.063); the faculty version (N=130) fit reasonably except RMSEA of 0.116, which the authors note may be inflated with small samples. The article reports the factor correlation 'was estimated at 1.00'.

> "However, the correlation between two factors was estimated at 1.00 (Fig. 2), suggesting that two factors may essentially assess the same construct. Upon reviewing the items, the research team concluded that the two factors are qualitatively different, and the two-factor structure was maintained."
