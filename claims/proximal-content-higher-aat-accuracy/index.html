Evidence¶

Britt Hadar 2023¶

Britt Hadar, Maayan Katzir, Sephi Pumpian, Tzur Karelitz and Nira Liberman. (2023). Psychological proximity improves reasoning in academic aptitude tests. npj Science of Learning 8:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00158-x

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Observational analysis of 41,209 examinees on publicly administered AATs from 2014–2017, with verbal reasoning questions coded as proximal or distal by two judges (r = 0.88). A GLMM showed "accuracy was higher with proximal than with distal questions" (means 0.71 vs. 0.68), p < 0.0001.

"Critically, accuracy was higher with proximal than with distal questions, B = −0.049, SE = 0.007, z = −6.80, p < 0.0001, ( M-proximal = 0.71, SE-proximal = 0.01, M-distal = 0.68, SE-distal = 0.01)."
