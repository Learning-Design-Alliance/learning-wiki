Evidence¶

Akkuzu 2016¶

Akkuzu, N. & Uyulgan, M.A. (2016). How to Improve Students' Comprehension concerning the Major Terms of Functional Groups?--In the Experiment of OrCheTaboo Game. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n2p196

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study with 62 university students (control N=30, experimental N=32) in an Organic Chemistry Laboratory course. Posttest means were 334.83 (experimental) vs 232.78 (control). The article prints the effect sizes for this difference: "The effect size coefficient (Cohen's d) between the FGTTs scores of both groups was calculated as 1.30" and "the eta squared (η 2) value was calculated as .29".

"According to the t-test results, Table 5 shows that there have been a significant difference determined between the mean scores the experimental and control groups got from FGTTs (t= -5.03, p(.00)<.05)."
