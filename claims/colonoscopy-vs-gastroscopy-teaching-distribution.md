## Evidence

### Mapiour Deng 2014

Mapiour Deng, Prytula Michelle, Moser Michael. (2014). A classification of the verbal methods currently used to teach endoscopy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-163

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of standardized category proportions between 19 colonoscopies and 14 gastroscopies, analyzed with the Mann–Whitney U test. The article reports the printed percentages and p-values for demonstration and tips/tricks/pointers favoring colonoscopy.

> "A greater proportion of the teaching for colonoscopy involved demonstration (13.7% vs. 2.7%, p = 0.040) and tips/tricks/pointers (26.6% vs. 12.4%, p = 0.012) compared to gastroscopy."

### Mapiour Deng 2014 (2)

Mapiour Deng, Prytula Michelle, Moser Michael. (2014). A classification of the verbal methods currently used to teach endoscopy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-163

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The same procedure-type comparison of category distributions reports that motor instructions were used more in gastroscopy teaching, with the printed p-value of 0.0001; no effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Motor instructions were used more in the teaching of gastroscopy than in colonoscopy (p = 0.0001)."
