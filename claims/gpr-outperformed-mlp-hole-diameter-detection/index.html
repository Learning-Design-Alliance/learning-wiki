Changgen Li 2025¶

q2 · i? · design · r2

A student project published after the Fall 2021 course (Hoskins et al.) trained MLP and GPR models on datasets of 300, 600, 900, and 1210 data points from laser-processed steel substrates. The article reports that "The study finds that GPR significantly outperforms MLP in terms of detection accuracy and training speed." No effect size is printed.
