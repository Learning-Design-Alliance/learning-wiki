`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Categorization of explanations to questionnaire question D1 across all four groups (SHS, 1MDM, 1MDP, PT); explanations were grouped into categories constructed through a negotiation phase among the authors, not given a priori. The quote reports the absence of Lorentz-force reasoning.
