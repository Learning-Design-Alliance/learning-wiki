Evidence¶

Murray-Harvey 2013¶

Murray-Harvey, R., Pourshafie, T., & Santos Reyes, W. (2013). What teacher education students learn about collaboration from problem-based learning. Journal of Problem Based Learning in Higher Education, 1(1), 114-134. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1108330

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of students' written reflections on PBL. The abstract and results report that "Features of the Knowledge dimension were: generation, application, and dissemination of knowledge.", with pedagogical knowledge split into declarative and procedural sub-categories in Table 2.

"Features of the Knowledge dimension were: generation, application, and dissemination of knowledge. Pedagogical knowledge was also evident through learning connections made by students to their future teaching practice."
