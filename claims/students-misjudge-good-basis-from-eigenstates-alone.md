# Students wrongly judge a basis good from completeness of eigenstates of H0 alone, or by examining only H0 or only H′

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Several students incorrectly stated the originally chosen basis is a good basis merely because it consists of a complete set of eigenstates of H0, without considering degeneracy; others examined only H0 or only H′ rather than both. [→ Christof Keebaugh 2017](#christof-keebaugh-2017)

## Evidence

### Christof Keebaugh 2017

Christof Keebaugh, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Developing and evaluating an interactive tutorial on degenerate perturbation theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

From the same difficulty investigation (32 written responses, 10 interviews). Students also gave general answers such as "it forms a complete Hilbert space" or that "the basis vectors should be orthogonal", ignoring degeneracy of H0.

> "However, several students incor- rectly stated that the originally chosen basis is a good basis because it consists of a complete set of eigenstates of ˆH 0 ( ˆH 0 was diagonal in the original basis) without any considerati on for whether ˆH 0 had any degeneracy"
