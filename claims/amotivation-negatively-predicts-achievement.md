## Evidence

### Jang Yujin 2021

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same SEM of the KELS panel, Table 4 prints amotivation ⇒ academic achievement as −10.14 (S.E. 0.52), standardized −0.27, p < 0.001; the discussion states "Amotivation had a significant negative impact on academic achievement". No d/r-family effect size is printed.

> "Self-regulation had a positive effect on academic achievement, while amotivation had a negative effect."

### Jang Yujin 2021 (2)

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? > "Self-regulation had a positive effect on academic achievement, while amotivation had a negative effect."
