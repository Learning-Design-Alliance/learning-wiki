## Evidence

### Gunarhadi 2016

Gunarhadi, Sunardi, Mohammad Anwar, Tri Rejeki Andayani, Abdull Sukor Shaari. (2016). +THE EFFECT OF CLUSTER-BASED INSTRUCTION ON MATHEMATIC ACHIEVEMENT IN INCLUSIVE SCHOOLS. International Journal of Special Education, 31(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099973

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test only control group study with two intact third-grade classes of 34 students each in Surakarta, Indonesia. Independent-samples T-test showed the CBI group's mean was significantly higher: "t = 6.16, p < .01". No effect size was printed.

> "mathematic achievement of students in the CBI setting (mean = 7.01 , SD =  1.37) was significantly better than that of students in the FII setting ( mean = 5.04, SD = 1.53 ), t = 6.16, p < .01)"
