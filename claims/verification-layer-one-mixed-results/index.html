Evidence¶

Tam Nguyen 2026¶

Tam Nguyen, Hung Nguyen, Robert Ogburn. (2026). AI-accelerated End-to-End Framework for Rapid Professional Upskilling. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14044 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.14044

q2 · i? · design · r2

Layer 1 verification executed as a scripted pass producing six reports scored against explicit numeric targets, reported in Section III-C. The authors deliberately report this mixed picture, arguing criterion-referenced checks that can fail are evidence the instrument measures rather than ratifies.

"platform accuracy scored 95% on a 20-example sample and cross-chapter integrity found 0 invalid references among 268 checked; conversely, only 5 of 10 chapters met the 70% pedagogical-progression bar, and assessment materials stood at 63% of the assessment-bank target at that time."
