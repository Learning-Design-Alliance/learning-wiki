## Evidence

### Vural Mutlu 2017

Vural Mutlu, Koşar Mustafa Feridun, Kerimoğlu Orhan, Kızkapan Fatih, Kahyaoğlu Serdar, Tuğrul Sevil, İşleyen Hasan Burak. (2017). Cardiopulmonary resuscitation knowledge among nursing students: a questionnaire study. Anatolian Journal of Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.14744/AnatolJCardiol.2016.7156

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-class comparison in the Results section for the importance-of-CPR section: "The scores in 12th class were significantly better than those in 11th class", with grade-band percentages and p=0.002.

> "The scores in 12th class were significantly better than those in 11th class (5.1% vs. 13.3% for <60 points; 7.7% vs. 19.4% for 60–80 points; 87.2% vs. 67.3% for >80 points, respectively; p=0.002)."

### Vural Mutlu 2017 (2)

Vural Mutlu, Koşar Mustafa Feridun, Kerimoğlu Orhan, Kızkapan Fatih, Kahyaoğlu Serdar, Tuğrul Sevil, İşleyen Hasan Burak. (2017). Cardiopulmonary resuscitation knowledge among nursing students: a questionnaire study. Anatolian Journal of Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.14744/AnatolJCardiol.2016.7156

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-class comparison for the third questionnaire section (indications, methods, effectiveness), mean 65.01±18.81: 12th class again significantly better, p=0.001.

> "These scores in 12th class were significantly better than those in 11th class (17.9% vs. 40.5% for <60 points; 42.3% vs. 34.6% for 60–80 points; 39.7% vs. 24.9% for >80 points, respectively; p=0.001)."
