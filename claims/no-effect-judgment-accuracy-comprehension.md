# Judgment accuracy of comprehension did not depend on misconceptions or instructional method

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither the overall interaction nor the individual interactions between misconceptions and instructional method were significant for judgment accuracy of comprehension. [→ Anja Prinz-Weiß & Elisa Röhm 2025](#anja-prinz-weiß-elisa-röhm-2025)

## Evidence

### Anja Prinz-Weiß & Elisa Röhm 2025

Anja Prinz-Weiß & Elisa Röhm. (2025). To what extent can think sheets with or without reflection prompts support self-regulated learning from text? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09419-z

`q3 · i?`

Regression analysis on judgment accuracy of comprehension (bias, scored from -4.00 to +4.00) in the online experiment found "no significant overall interaction"; individual interactions and the think-sheet vs. reflection comparison were also not significant, and main effects were not significant.

> "The analysis on judgment accuracy of comprehension showed that there was no significant overall interaction between number of misconceptions before studying and instructional method, ΔR2 = .02, F(2, 86) = 1.11, p = .335."
