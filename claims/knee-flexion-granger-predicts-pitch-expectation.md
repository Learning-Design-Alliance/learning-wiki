## Evidence

### Moura 2023

Moura, N., Vidal, M., Aguilera, A. M., Vilas-Boas, J. P., Serra, S. & Leman, M. (2023). Knee flexion of saxophone players anticipates tonal context of music. npj Science of Learning 8:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00172-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Granger causality analysis of motion-capture knee curves against model-derived pitch expectation profiles in the expressive condition; the article reports "p = 0.006, F = 2.344" for P1 and similar statistics for P2–P4.

> "The following interactions were found when testing for Granger causality in the EXP condition: P1 [ p = 0.006, F = 2.344], P2 [p = 0.063, F = 1.702], P3 [ p = 0.009, F = 2.307] and P4 [ p = 0.002, F = 3.30]"
