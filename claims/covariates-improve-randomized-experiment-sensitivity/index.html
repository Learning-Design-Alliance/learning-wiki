A methodological argument from the paper's framework section, grounded in the power-analysis literature (Hedges & Hedberg, 2007; Porter & Raudenbush, 1987; Raudenbush et al., 2007); the article offers no new empirical test of this statement here.

"Covariates are not required for randomized experiments, but when they explain a substantial proportion of variance in outcomes (R2), they are a very efficient way to improve (i.e., decrease) the MDES and to reduce the required sample size to achieve a certain level of statistical power"
