Questionnaire items 5 and 6 in Table 3 show no significant between-group difference for clinical thinking improvement (P = 0.81) or reduced learning pressure (P = 0.67). The article attributes improved clinical thinking in both groups to the CBL component shared by both methods. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.
