## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schubach F 2017

Schubach F, Goos M, Fabry G, Vach W, Boeker M. (2017). Virtual patients in the acquisition of clinical reasoning skills: does presentation mode matter? A quasi-randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1004-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of a quasi-randomized parallel-group trial (n = 56 analyzed; 30 KF, 26 systematic) measuring clinical reasoning with a script concordance test normalized to expert mean 80. The article reports "Cohen’s d at 0.337" and a "statistically non-significant" difference; the per-protocol analysis was similarly non-significant (p = 0.245).

> "students in the key feature arm attained a mean of 57.4 points (95% confidence interval: 50.9–63.9), and in the systematic arm, 62.7 points (57.2–68.2), with Cohen’s d at 0.337. The difference is statistically non-significant (p = 0.214)."
