# Year Three students taught angles with Geometer's Sketchpad improved significantly in van Hiele level of geometric thinking from pretest to posttest on all three angle concepts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large
> **Currency** · newest study 2013 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The experimental group's van Hiele level improved significantly for right angles (effect size 0.38), acute angles (0.62), and obtuse angles (0.91) after six Geometer's Sketchpad lessons. [→ Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013](#poh-geik-tieng-leong-kwan-eu-2013)

## Evidence

### Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013

Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu. (2013). Improving Students' Van Hiele Level Of Geometric Thinking Using Geometer's Sketchpad. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 2(3). www.mojet.net. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086363

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-tests in the findings section for the experimental group (n = 16) across three pre/post tests. Obtuse angles showed "The effect size is 0.91"; right angles t(15) = 3.00, p = .01, effect size 0.38; acute angles t(15) = 4.98, p < .01, effect size 0.62.

> "The paired samples t-test analysis showed that the students' van Hiele level of geometric thinking on obtuse angles after the intervention using Geometer's Sketchpad ( M = 2.00, SD = 0.00) was significantly higher than the students' van Hiele level of geometric thinking regarding obtuse angles before the intervention"
