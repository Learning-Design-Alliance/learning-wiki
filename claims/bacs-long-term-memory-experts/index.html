Evidence¶

Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner 2022¶

Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner. (2022). Toward Synergizing Educational Research and Movement Sciences: a Dialogue on Learning as Developing Perception for Action. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09668-3

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Picture-sorting study with tennis experts by Schack and Mechsner (2006), as reported in this article, identifying eleven basic action concepts across the serve's pre-activation, strike, and final swing phases. No effect size or sample size is printed in the present article.

"Using a sophisticated picture-sorting task, Schack was able to show that these BACs are actually present in the long-term memory of tennis players. In skilled players, these are integrated into a coherent memory network (Schack & Mechsner, 2006)."
