Evidence¶

Ortiz-Bonnin 2025¶

Ortiz-Bonnin, S., & Blahopoulou, J. (2025). Chat or cheat? Academic dishonesty, risk perceptions, and ChatGPT usage in higher education students. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-025-10080-2

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional online survey of 468 ChatGPT users at a Spanish university; hypothesis-testing regression within a mediation model. The article reports perceived risk was "significantly and negatively associated with use frequency (β = − 0.20, p <.001)".

"First, perceived risk was significantly and negatively associated with use frequency (β = − 0.20, p <.001) and intention to use ( β = − 0.46, p <.001), supporting H1a and H1b."

q3 · i? · associational · r2
