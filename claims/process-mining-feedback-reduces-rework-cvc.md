## Evidence

### de la Fuente Rene 2025

de la Fuente Rene, Galvez-Yanjari Victor, Delfino Alejandro, Lira Ricardo, Hurtado Claudia, Munoz-Gama Jorge, Sepúlveda Marcos. (2025). Process-oriented feedback for ultrasound-guided central venous access training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03341-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intergroup comparison in this randomized trial of 20 residents found "lower activity rework than did the CG" at posttest (post reworks P value pre-vs-post 0.027 in POG vs 0.240 in CG; WMW odds ratio 2.20).

> "However, the intergroup comparison (Table 1) revealed that the POG had greater similarity with the reference model (Fig. 2) and lower activity rework than did the CG at the end of the training program (Fig. 3)."

### de la Fuente Rene 2025 (2)

de la Fuente Rene, Galvez-Yanjari Victor, Delfino Alejandro, Lira Ricardo, Hurtado Claudia, Munoz-Gama Jorge, Sepúlveda Marcos. (2025). Process-oriented feedback for ultrasound-guided central venous access training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03341-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same trial reports that the "CG showed no significant improvement in the process indicators for these categories postintervention," specifically in activities such as vein puncture with trocar, blood return, guidewire install, and wire checks.

> "In contrast, the CG showed no significant improvement in the process indicators for these categories postintervention."
