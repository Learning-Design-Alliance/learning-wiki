Duchesneau 2023¶

Duchesneau, N., & Zapata, M. (2023). Creating Safer Schools: A Case Study. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641760

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theoretical argument in the report's recommendations section, citing research on workplace and school violence (pathway adapted from Torchstone Global): grievance, violent ideation, research and planning, preparation, probing and breaching, then attack. This is an attributed research synthesis, not a new empirical test.

"Most strategies geared toward hardening schools against these attacks, such as placing police in schools and adding metal detectors, target the end of this path, when an attack is ready to occur. However, these efforts intervene too late in the pathway, when a situation has already escalated too far."
