## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, M. & Soland, J. (2019). Using Assessment Metadata to Quantify the Impact of Test Disengagement on Estimates of Educational Effectiveness. (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/using-assessment-metadata-to-quantify-the-impact-of-test-disengagement-on-estimates-of-educational-effectiveness-paper/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The same HLMs re-run on filtered scores reversed the gap: "Black students showed significantly greater fall-to-fall growth than White students (.43 RIT points)." Effort-moderated estimates differed from unadjusted by .19 units and were not significantly different.

> "By comparison, when disengaged scores were filtered out, Black students showed significantly greater fall-to-fall growth than White students (.43 RIT points)."
