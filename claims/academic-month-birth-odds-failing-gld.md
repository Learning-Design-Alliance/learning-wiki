## Evidence

### Pettinger Katherine J 2019

Pettinger Katherine J, Kelly Brian, Sheldon Trevor A, Mon-Williams Mark, Wright John, Hill Liam J B. (2019). Starting school: educational development as a function of age of entry and prematurity. Archives of Disease in Childhood, 105(2), 160-165. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2019-317124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression in the Born in Bradford cohort (n=10 337) showed academic month of birth significantly predicted failing to attain a GLD (OR 1.17, 95% CI 1.15 to 1.18 in step 1, p<0.001), persisting after gestational age was added. The article reports "these odds also increased by 1.17 (95% CI 1.16 to 1.18)" per month later in the academic year.

> "In addition, for every month later in the academic year the child was born, these odds also increased by 1.17 (95% CI 1.16 to 1.18)."
