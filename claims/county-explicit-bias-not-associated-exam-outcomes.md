## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression analysis of CRDC data combined with Project Implicit county-level explicit bias estimates. In the two significant instances the coefficients were negative, and in both cases explicit bias was not associated with outcomes for either racial group when examined separately.

> "County-level explicit racial bias was generally not associated with Black–White disparities in AP exam taking (Fig. 1, Middle) and passing (Fig. 1, Right). In the two of five instances where the association was significant (2015–16 exam taking and passing), the coefficients were negative, such that greater explicit racial bias was associated with smaller Black–White disparities in these outcomes."
