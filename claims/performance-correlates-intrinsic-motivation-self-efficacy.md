## Evidence

### Usán Pablo 2022

Usán Pablo, Salavera Carlos, Quílez-Robres Alberto, Lozano-Blasco Raquel. (2022). Behaviour Patterns between Academic Motivation, Burnout and Academic Performance in Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191912663

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 3) in the same 398-student sample, with performance measured as average first-term marks (0–10). The study reports correlations of "intrinsic knowledge motivation (r = 0.160) and achievement (r = 0.277), and with self-efficacy (r = 0.310)".

> "Finally, academic performance was shown to be positively correlated with intrinsic knowledge motivation (r = 0.160) and achievement (r = 0.277), and with self-efficacy (r = 0.310)."
