## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Social interdependence and workgroup process measures are unrelated to specialization measures, while role interdependence correlates weakly and negatively with specialization (r = -.36 or -.28). [→ Pennings 1974](#pennings-1974)

## Evidence

### Pennings 1974

Pennings, Johannes M. (1974). Differentiation, Interdependence, and Performance in Formal Organizations. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED099988

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of questionnaire and profile data from 40 brokerage offices (Table 1, N=40). The article reports "a weak but consistent correlation between role interdependence and specialization" (r = -.36 or -.28), suggesting heterogeneous offices have fewer collaborative, role interdependent employees.

> "While the measures of social interdependence and "workgroup process" are not related to the specializationmeasures, there is a weak but consistent correlation between role interdependence and specialization"
