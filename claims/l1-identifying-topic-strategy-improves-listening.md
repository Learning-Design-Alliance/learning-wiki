## Evidence

### Bozorgian 2013

Bozorgian, H., & Pillay, H. (2013). Enhancing Foreign Language Learning through Listening Strategies Delivered in L1: An Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085363

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the closest-to-meaning section (5 multiple-choice questions) of the post-test in the same 60-participant experimental study. Instruction of identifying-topic strategy in Persian "had an effect on EFL listening comprehension".

> "Table 6 indicates that the experimental class achieved better results than did the control class (M = 3.73, SD = 1.11 vs. M = 2.36, SD = .61). In fact, the difference between the two was statistically significant as the t-test was run t (29) = 1.185, p = .000, suggesting that instruction of identifying topic strategy in Persian had an effect on EFL listening comprehension"
