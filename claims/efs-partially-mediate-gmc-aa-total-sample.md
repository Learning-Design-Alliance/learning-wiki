## Evidence

### Fernández‐Sánchez 2022

Fernández‐Sánchez, A.; Redondo‐Tébar, A.; Sánchez‐López, M.; Visier‐Alfonso, M. E.; Muñoz‐Rodríguez, J. R.; Martínez‐Vizcaíno, V. (2022). Sex differences on the relation among gross motor competence, cognition, and academic achievement in children. Scandinavian Journal of Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/sjop.12827

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multifactorial SEM on the total sample of 451 children, controlling for age and maternal education level, showed partial mediation. The direct path "decreased, although maintaining its significance, when EFs were included as a mediator (c′; β = 0.16, p = 0.009)"; the GMC→EFs path was β = 0.32, p = 0.020 and EFs→AA was β = 0.48, p < 0.001.

> "The direct path from GMC to AA was significant (c; β = 0.22, p = 0.022). This path coefficient decreased, although maintaining its significance, when EFs were included as a mediator (c′; β = 0.16, p = 0.009) for the total sample."
