Akif ARSLAN 2016¶

Akif ARSLAN. (2016). Effect of Jigsaw I technique on teaching Turkish grammar. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2709

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental control group pretest-posttest study with 56 grade-8 students (30 experimental, 26 control) in Kars, Turkey. Mann Whitney U test on final AST scores: U = 297.000, p > 0.05, "non -significant"; means were 19.52 (experimental) and 17.31 (control), descriptive only.

"It is seen that the U value regarding the difference in the success scores of the students in experiment and control groups in the final test 297.000 and p>0 .05 were found to be non -significant."
