Evidence¶

Kulesz 2024¶

Kulesz, P. A., Roberts, G. J., Francis, D. J., Cirino, P., Walczak, M., & Vaughn, S. (2024). Latent profiles as predictors of response to instruction for students with reading difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(3). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000832

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Conditional mixed model on the 269 RCT students (nested in 66 teachers within 9 schools) with Gates post-test standard score as outcome, latent profile and condition as predictors, and language proficiency, special education, and gender as covariates. The printed "Treatment by Profile interaction" statistic is β = .42, F(1, 181) = 4.33, p = .039; no standardized effect size was reported.

"The results of the random intercept model treatment effects are moderated by latent profiles (Treatment by Profile interaction, β = .42; F(1, 181) = 4.33; p = .039), when controlling for other terms in the model."
