# References to document sources showed a significant sequence-type by position interaction, driven by first-position texts in the HT-LT sequence; the hypothesized increase in mixed sequences was only partly supported

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` DS references showed a significant Sequence Type × Position interaction; first-position HT-LT texts were mentioned more than texts of other sequence types. [→ Franco Londra 2025](#franco-londra-2025)

## Evidence

### Franco Londra 2025

Franco Londra, Magalí Martínez, Gaston Saux. (2025). Sources within sources: The dynamic influence of multiple documents trustworthiness when evaluating and using an embedded source. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09427-z

`q3 · i?`

Mixed logistic model of DS references in essays (n = 76). The article reports "a significant interaction between Sequence Type and Position within the sequence, z (152) = 2.62, p =.009", with first-position HT-LT texts mentioned more than other sequence types; no effect size is printed.

> "the model showed a significant interaction between Sequence Type and Position within the sequence, z (152) = 2.62, p =.009. Among the texts in first position the one from HT-LT condition was mentioned more than those of other Sequence Types, all p <.049"
