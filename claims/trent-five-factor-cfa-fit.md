## Evidence

### Ferguson E 2014

Ferguson E, Buttery A, Miles G, Tatalia C, Clarke DD, Lonsdale AJ, Baxendale B, Lawrence C. (2014). The Temporal Rating of Emergency Non-Technical skills (TRENT) index for self and others: psychometric properties and emotional responses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0240-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot-study CFA with 150 F2 doctors completing self- and peer-assessment forms (analysis N = 144 for self-report). The five-dimension model fit improved after removing three non-significant loadings (CFI = .92, RMSEA = .065, WRMR = .94); the peer model with one modification also fit well (CFI = .92, RMSEA = .08, WRMR = 1.0). The article reports "the five dimension model had a good fit for the self-assessment".

> "Confirmatory factor analysis in Mplus 7 [34] using diagonally weighted least squares (WLSM) showed that the five dimension model had a good fit for the self-assessment when two addition loadings are specified after consulting modification indices (N =144: CFI = .90, RMSEA = .067, WRMR = .97)."
