# DCN visualization reduces perceived intrinsic and extraneous cognitive load during Hadamard-gate task solving

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Perceived intrinsic cognitive load was significantly lower with visualization, a small-to-medium effect (d = 0.48). [→ Bley 2026](#bley-2026)
`q2 i2` Perceived extraneous cognitive load was significantly lower with visualization, a medium effect (d = 0.75). [→ Bley 2026 (2)](#bley-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bley 2026

Bley, J., Rexigel, E., Arias, A., Krupp, L., Longen, N., Lukowicz, P., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, M., and Widera, A. (2026). Visualization Enhances Problem Solving in multi-Qubit Systems. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/hqzq-fbrb](https://doi.org/10.1103/hqzq-fbrb)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test of intrinsic cognitive load after each main-phase block in the within-subject eye-tracking study (N=31). ICL fell from "0 .76 ± 0.18" without to "0 .67 ± 0.19" with visualization, t(30) = 2.87, p(adj.) = .0074, d = 0.48.

> "ICL 0 .76 ± 0.18 0 .67 ± 0.19 2.87 .0037 (.0074) 0.48"

### Bley 2026 (2)

Bley, J., Rexigel, E., Arias, A., Krupp, L., Longen, N., Lukowicz, P., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, M., and Widera, A. (2026). Visualization Enhances Problem Solving in multi-Qubit Systems. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/hqzq-fbrb](https://doi.org/10.1103/hqzq-fbrb)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test of extraneous cognitive load in the same within-subject study (N=31). ECL fell from "0 .60 ± 0.21" without to "0 .44 ± 0.23" with visualization, t(30) = 5.02, p < .001, d = 0.75.

> "ECL 0 .60 ± 0.21 0 .44 ± 0.23 5.02 < .001 0.75"
