The trial's abstract reports that perceived competence "was positively correlated with interest/emotion and negatively correlated with pressure/tension in the positive feedback group", with the opposite pressure pattern under negative feedback.

> "the perceived competence of trainees was positively correlated with interest/emotion and negatively correlated with pressure/tension in the positive feedback group, while the perceived competence of trainees was positively correlated with pressure/tension in negative feedback group"
