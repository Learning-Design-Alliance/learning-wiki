## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, P., Ganushchak, L., Welie, C., & van Steensel, R. (2026). Learning proximal cyclic sighing buffers anxiety across learning moments and boredom across days. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00446-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate multilevel model of pooled EMA data (n = 84) controlling for time-of-day and contextual factors, extracting within-person residual correlations among the three emotions. The printed within-person effect sizes are rwithin ≈ −0.41 (anxiety–enjoyment), rwithin ≈ −0.28 (anxiety–boredom), and rwithin ≈ +0.61 (enjoyment–boredom), the last described as unexpected; the article calls the emotions "an interdependent system".

> "higher-than-usual anxiety coincided with lower enjoyment (rwithin ≈ −0.41) and lower boredom (rwithin ≈ −0.28). Somewhat unexpectedly, higher-than-usual enjoyment coincided with higher boredom (rwithin ≈ +0.61)."
