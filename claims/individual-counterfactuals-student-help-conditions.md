# Individualised counterfactual inferences estimate whether a specific student would have performed differently under different help conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For a student who solved Q5 at the 2D level with scheme help, the counterfactual probability of solving Q5 at 2D without help is one; for Q12 solved at 1D with scheme help, the counterfactual probability of reaching 2D with feedback help is between 0 and 0.76, indicating uncertainty. ## Evidence

### Francesca Mangili 2026

Francesca Mangili, Alessandro Antonucci, Rafael Cabañas de Paz. (2026). Causal Modelling of Support Interventions for Student Competency Assessment. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24632

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Individualised counterfactual inference for a single student from the CAT assessment (109-student dataset), conditioning on the student's full set of observed answers and hint usage. For Q12 the counterfactual probabilities were [0,0], [0.24,1] and [0,0.76] for 0D, 1D and 2D, indicating uncertainty about performance with more assistance.

> "The former yields probability one forQ 5 = 2D, i.e., we are certain that the student would have been able to use a 2D algorithm even without help."
