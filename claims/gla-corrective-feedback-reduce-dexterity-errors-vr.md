## Evidence

### Valdemar Stenberdt 2025

Valdemar Stenberdt, Ban Mouid Shiwalia, Guido Makransky. (2025). Re-Experiencing vs. Self-Explaining: Comparing Generative Learning Activities in VR. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10096-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Dexterity analysis on 146 participants (six extreme outliers excluded) in the VR pipetting experiment; z-score-normalized error counts. Post-hoc t-tests showed re-experiencing improved (d = 0.868) and self-explaining improved (d = 0.712), both p < 0.001. The post-GLA group difference (p = 0.043, d = 0.339) "disappeared after Bonferroni correction, and there was no significant interaction effect".

> "A mixed ANOV A with Time as a within-subjects factor and Condition as a between-subjects factor showed a significant effect of Time, F(1, 144) = 57.060, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.284."
