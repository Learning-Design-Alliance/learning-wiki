## Evidence

### M. Young and V. Dulewicz 2025

M. Young and V. Dulewicz. (2025). A model of command, leadership and management competency as a predictor of the performance, potential and rate of advancement of Royal Navy senior officers. Military Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/08995605.2025.2493392

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis (Table 9) with the four CLM supra-competency clusters as IVs and Performance, Potential and ROA as DVs. The article reports Multiple Rs range from .170 to .354, with final models explaining 7.5% to 12.5% of variance.

> "Turning to the DV Potential, the multiple R for the four supra-competencies combined (model 4) is .354 and they account for 12.5% of the total variance on the DV Potential."
