Evidence¶

Maria Adelina Ianos 2026¶

Maria Adelina Ianos, Andrei Rusu, Ramona Paloş, Cristina Petreñas, Clara Sansó. (2026). Linking Language Attitudes to Proficiency Across Learning Contexts: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Correlational Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10193-w

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level meta-analysis of 132 effect sizes from 46 named samples (N = 20,505), pre-registered protocol, RVE confirmatory analyses converged (r = .32). The pooled estimate "r =.34, 95% CI [.29, .39]" falls in the small-to-medium range by L2 benchmarks, explaining roughly 11% of variance in proficiency.

"A three-level meta-analysis simultaneously modeling 132 raw effect sizes nested within 46 named samples (N = 20,505) yielded a significant, small to medium positive association between language attitudes and proficiency ( r =.34, 95% CI [.29, .39], t(131) = 12.69, p <.001)."
