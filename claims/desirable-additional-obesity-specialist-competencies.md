Consensus statement from the Results section of the working-group report. The article states it is "desirable that HCPs with an obesity speciality demonstrate an understanding of novel technologies" alongside cost knowledge, stigma recognition and psychological constructs, so patients can be counselled effectively. This is an expert recommendation, not a tested result.

> "the working group agreed that it is desirable that HCPs with an obesity speciality demonstrate an understanding of novel technologies that could be used to innovate and improve obesity care, knowledge about the costs of obesity intervention and/or prevention, and recognition of the social stigma and personal values and attitudes towards obesity."
