### Tongxi Liu 2026

Tongxi Liu, Luyao Ye, Wei Yan. (2026). A framework for evaluation of large language models in essay assessment: Reliability, alignment, and causal reasoning. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baseline inter-rater reliability assessment using ICC(3,1) among three expert graders in educational psychology who independently scored all 60 essays with the IELTS rubric, then calibrated through iterative discussions, reported in Results §4.1.

> "Task Response achieved the highest reliability (ICC = 0.929), followed closely by Coherence Cohesion (ICC = 0.910). Lexical Resource (ICC = 0.897) and Grammatical Accuracy (ICC = 0.893) also demonstrated excellent reliability according to established guidelines (Koo & Li, 2016)."
