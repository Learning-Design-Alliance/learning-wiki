Evidence¶

Donald E Grant Jr. & Jerell B. Hill 2020¶

Donald E Grant Jr. & Jerell B. Hill. (2020). Activating Culturally Empathic Motivation in Diverse Students. Journal of Education and Learning, 9(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v9n5p45

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article reports this as a finding from stereotype-threat studies in which standardized test sessions controlled for expectation and preconception. It is reported second-hand with no effect size printed.

"When presented with tests of innate athletic ability, White men executed measurably worse than other men because they were performing under the threat of fulfilling the stereotype related to their performance in activities of athleticism."
