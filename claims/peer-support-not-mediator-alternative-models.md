## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across ten alternative models with peer support as mediator between MRSs and achievement, no source of peer support significantly mediated the relationship. [→ Villar 2025](#villar-2025)

## Evidence

### Villar 2025

Villar, E., Real‐Deus, E., Martínez‐López, Z., Mayo, M. E., & Tinajero, C. (2025). Perceived peer support, motivational self‐regulation and academic achievement in adolescents. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1325–1346. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ten alternative mediation models tested with the same criteria and covariates as the study models, using the same 463-student sample; outcome was mid-term achievement obtained after the questionnaires.

> "Results indicated that no source of peer support significantly mediated the relationship between the MRSs and achievement, either because peer support was not predicted by a given MRS, or peer support could not predict achievement (4 models), or because the mediation effect of peer support was not significant (6 models)"
