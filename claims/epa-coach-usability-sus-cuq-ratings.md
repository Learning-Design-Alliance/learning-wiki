## Evidence

### Perotti Luis 2025

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

System Usability Scale (German version, 10 items) completed at follow-up by all 28 participants. The article reports the mean of 64.04 and interprets it as "OK" or "high marginal" perceived usability according to Bangor.

> "The mean SUS score for the usability evaluation of the ePA Coach learning platform was 64.04 (SD = 19.57, Mdn = 70.00, IQR = 24.40) for all study participants."

### Perotti Luis 2025 (2)

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chatbot Usability Questionnaire completed by the nine participants (four YOA, five OA) who interacted with the chatbot during the intervention week; 130 chatbot interactions were recorded. The article interprets the OA score as "poor" or "not acceptable" usability.

> "The mean CUQ score for these nine participants was 53.1 (SD = 17.68). The YOA had a mean CUQ score of 64.1 (SD = 10.90) and the OA of 44.4 (SD = 17.90)."

### Perotti Luis 2025 (3)

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation between SUS score and skill-test completion-time mean difference among the 14 OA. The article reports a strong effect size, rs = −0.60 (p = 0.025), and a further moderate correlation between SUS and hints given for all participants (rs = 0.40, p = 0.036).

> "A significant correlation, with a strong effect size, was present between the SUS scoring and the mean difference in the completion time of the skill evaluation among the group of OA (rs(12) = −0.60, p = 0.025)."
