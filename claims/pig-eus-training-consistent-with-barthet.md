Xu Wei, Liu Yan, Pan Peng, Guo Yan, Wu Ren-pei, Yao Yin-zhen. (2015). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article compares its randomized trial results with the prior Barthet et al. live-pig study (17 trainees), reporting consistency for diagnostic and FNA improvements but a discrepancy for CPN precision, attributed to differences in baseline experience and training quality.

> "Our results showed increased precision for CPN while Barthet reported no significant improvement for CPN. This slight difference may be due to differences in the baseline endoscopic operation experience and quality of training between the studies."
