# Receiving additional training in physical activity support is associated with higher capability and motivation of direct support professionals to support physical activity in people with ID

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants who received additional training in physical activity support scored 0.56 points higher on capability and 0.47 points higher on motivation than untrained participants in multivariate models. [→ Bossink 2019](#bossink-2019)

## Evidence

### Bossink 2019

Bossink, L. W. M., van der Putten, A. A. J., Paap, M. C. S., and Vlaskamp, C. (2019). Factors associated with direct support professionals' behaviour in the physical activity support provided to people with intellectual disabilities. Journal of Intellectual Disability Research, 63: 981–991. https://doi.org/10.1111/jir.12616

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multivariate linear regression in a sample of 217 direct support professionals, with latent trait scores for capability, opportunity and motivation estimated by graded response models. The article reports trained professionals were expected to score "increased by a 0.56 point for capability and a 0.47 point for motivation". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In general, the expected latent trait scores were increased by a 0.56 point for capability and a 0.47 point for motivation for the participants who had received additional training in physical activity support compared with those who did not."
