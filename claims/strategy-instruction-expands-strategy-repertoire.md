### Agor 2014

John T. Agor. (2014). Language-Learner Strategy Instruction and English Achievement: Voices from Ghana. English Language Teaching, 7(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n6p175

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video-taped post-intervention writing task analysis of the same two classes. The experimental group exhibited 4 listening, 5 speaking, 2 reading and 6 writing strategies; the control group exhibited fewer in each category, including only one listening strategy (guessing) and one writing strategy (conferencing).
