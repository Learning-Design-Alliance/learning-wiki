`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 1,272 Black borrowers conducted for the National Black Student Loan Debt Study, analyzed with frequencies and descriptive statistics. Respondents reported they "have yet to see positive returns on their student loans (51%)" and 66% regret borrowing.

> "Other study participants said they have yet to see positive returns on their student loans (51%) and/or regret having taken out education loans (66%) that now seem “unpayable,” “not worth it,” and ultimately like an inescapable burden."
