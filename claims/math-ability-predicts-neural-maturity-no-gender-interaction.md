## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A whole-brain regression found math ability predicted neural maturity in IPS, prefrontal cortex, and middle temporal gyrus in both gender groups, and no cortical regions showed significant math-ability-by-gender interactions. [→ Kersey 2019](#kersey-2019)

## Evidence

### Kersey 2019

Kersey, A. J., Csumitta, K. D., & Cantlon, J. F. (2019). Gender similarities in the brain during mathematics development. npj Science of Learning 4:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0057-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain regression entering math ability, gender, and their interaction as predictors of neural maturity in the 104-child fMRI sample. Girls showed r(48) ≥ 0.36 and boys r(45) ≥ 0.37 relations between ability and maturity; the article concludes the relation does not depend on gender.

> "No cortical regions showed significant interactions between math ability and gender (t(93) ≤ 2.63, threshold: voxel-wise p ≤ 0.01, cluster corrected to p < 0.05, threshold = 110 voxels), indicating that the relation between math ability and neural maturity does not depend on gender."
