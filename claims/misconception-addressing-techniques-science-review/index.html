The review's coding found misconception-addressing work concentrated in science, where misconceptions are particularly prevalent. The review defines augmented activation as telling students the text may differ from what they know, refutation texts as stating and refuting common misconceptions, and analogical reasoning as using analogies to address misconceptions.
