## Evidence

### Li P 2026

Li P, Taoyu J, Yaxin L, Xiao-li P, Muzi L, Haoying D. (2026). Evaluation of the Bridge-in, Objective, Preassessment, Participatory Learning, Postassessment, and Summary–Based Instructional Model in Disaster Nursing: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/77728

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pretest-posttest paired comparisons using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests across all 77 participants. All three groups showed statistically significant improvement in disaster nursing competency scores from pretest to posttest, with large effect sizes (r=0.874).

> "Wilcoxon signed-rank tests revealed significant within-group differences across all groups: control and comparison groups (z=−4.37, r=0.874, P<.001) and experimental group (z=−4.54, r=0.874, P<.001)."
