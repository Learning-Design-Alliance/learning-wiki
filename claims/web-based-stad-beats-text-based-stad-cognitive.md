## Evidence

### Bambang Hariadi 2016

Bambang Hariadi, Tutut Wurijanto. (2016). Influence of Web Based Cooperative Learning Strategy and Achiever Motivation on Student Study Outcome. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3, pp. 189~199. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group factorial study of 67 undergraduates in the Organizational Behaviors course; ANOVA in Table 5 reports Learning Strategy F = 9.501, Sig. .003. The web-based group mean was "average score of 71.37" for the textual group versus 78.12 for web-based.

> "application of web -based STAD type of cooperative learning strategy accomplishes average score is 78.12. Whereas textual STAD type of cooperative learning strategy accomplishes average score of 71.37."
