Evidence¶

El-Sakka 2016¶

El-Sakka, S. M. F. (2016). Self-Regulated Strategy Instruction for Developing Speaking Proficiency and Reducing Speaking Anxiety of Egyptian University Students. English Language Teaching, 9(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n12p22

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Pearson correlation computed on the same forty participants' posttest scores. The article reports r = -0.704, significant at the 0.01 level, and concludes improvement in speaking proficiency led to reduction in speaking anxiety.

"The correlation coefficient for the speaking proficiency posttest and speaking anxiety posttest was - 0.704. This coefficient is significant at the 0.01 level."
