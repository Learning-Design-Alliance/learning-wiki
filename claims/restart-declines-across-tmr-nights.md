## Evidence

### Göldi 2019

Göldi, M. & Rasch, B. (2019). Effects of targeted memory reactivation during sleep at home depend on sleep disturbances and habituation. npj Science of Learning 4:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0044-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of self-reported restart counts in 66 participants across three nights: night main effect "F(2,63) = 14.51, P < 0.001, η2 = 0.19" and group main effect "F(2,63) = 10.84, P < 0.001 η2 = 0.26"; night × group interaction not significant (P = 0.056).

> "The reduction in the number of restarting across the three nights was highly signiﬁcant (main effect of night: F(2,63) = 14.51, P < 0.001, η2 = 0.19). In addition, participants who reported disturbances by the words played during sleep restarted signiﬁcantly more often (main effect group: F(2,63) = 10.84, P < 0.001 η2 = 0.26"
