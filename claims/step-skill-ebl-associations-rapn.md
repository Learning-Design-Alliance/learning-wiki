### Wang Yuzhi 2024

Wang Yuzhi, Wilder Samantha, Hijazi Mahmoud, Myles Marquisha D., Mirza Mahin, Van Til Monica, Maatman Thomas, Ghani Khurshid R., Lane Brian R., Rogers Craig G. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Step-level regression analysis (eTable 1) linking domain-specific skill scores to risk-adjusted outcomes. The article reports "lower rates of extensive EBL" for higher skill in hilar dissection (−2.1%), clamping and tumor resection (−1.4%), and renorrhaphy (−2.4%), all P < .001.

> "Higher technical skill in the hilar dissection, clamping and tumor resection, and renorrhaphy steps were significantly associated with lower rates of extensive EBL (−2.1% [95% CI, −2.4% to −1.8%] for hilar dissection step"
