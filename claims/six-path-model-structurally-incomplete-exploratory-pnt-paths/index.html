Post-hoc exploratory structural extension on the student sample. In this model 'PNT remained positively associated with LOU, β = 0.544', and both LOU and PNT were positively associated with ACS (β = 0.311 and β = 0.304).

"Adding a direct path from PNT to ACS substantially improved model fit, Δχ2(1) = 52.87, p < 0.001, and reduced SRMR from 0.055 to 0.045."

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Second exploratory extension adding direct paths from PNT to SRL and CT (Δχ2(2) = 9.05, p = 0.011; SRMR decreased to 0.044). PNT remained positively associated with LOU (β = 0.535) and with ACS (β = 0.297).

"In the fully expanded model, the PNT → SRL and PNT → CT associations were small, β = 0.092, p = 0.030, and β = 0.103, p = 0.018, respectively."
