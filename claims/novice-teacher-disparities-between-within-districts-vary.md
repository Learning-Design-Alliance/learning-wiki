# Whether disparities in access to novice teachers occur between districts or within districts varies widely by state

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In almost half of states more variation in novice-teacher percentages lies between districts than within; in Arizona, Louisiana, North Carolina, Ohio, Rhode Island, and Utah more than 75% of variation is between districts, while in nine named states less than 25% is. [→ Sarah Mehrotra 2021](#sarah-mehrotra-2021)

## Evidence

### Sarah Mehrotra 2021

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Decomposition analysis (intraclass correlation of novice-teacher percentages within districts) of CRDC 2017-18 data. The report finds "more than 75% of the variation" is between districts in six named states, while Florida, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Nevada, Oregon, South Carolina, South Dakota, and Wyoming show less than 25% between-district variation.

> "in Arizona, Louisiana, North Carolina, Ohio, Rhode Island, and Utah, more than 75% of the variation in student access to experienced teachers occurs between districts."
