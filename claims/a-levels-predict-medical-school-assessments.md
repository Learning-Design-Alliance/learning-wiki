## Evidence

### Davies DJ 2022

Davies DJ, Sam AH, Murphy KG, Khan SA, Choe R, Cleland J. (2022). BMAT's predictive validity for medical school performance: A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14819

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariable robust linear regressions with A-level scores as predictor in the ICSM incremental validity sample (1013 eligible students) reported in Results Section 3.1.4. The article found "moderate associations" for written performance with standardised coefficients β = 0.34–0.43, a Year 5 coefficient β = 0.26, and small associations (0.1–0.2) with all clinical assessments.

> "we found moderate associations between A‐level scores and written assessment performance in Years 1, 2, 3 and 6 (coefficients ranging from 0.34–0.43) and a small association with written assessment performance in Year 5 (coefficient = 0.26, 95% CI 0.2–0.32)."
