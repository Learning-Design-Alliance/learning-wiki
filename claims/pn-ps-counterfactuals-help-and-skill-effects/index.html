Group-level counterfactual inference (generalised probabilities of necessity and sufficiency) computed via twin networks over the compatible FSCMs for the CAT assessment with 109 students, reported in Table 2. Some intervals are wide (e.g. PN of S_alg=1D for Q6=1D between 0.11 and 0.95), but most inferences are informative.
