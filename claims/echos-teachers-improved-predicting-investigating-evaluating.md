## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Repeated-measures ANOVAs showed significant Group x Time improvements for ECHOS teachers on predicting (F(1,84) = 4.54, p = .012), investigating (F(1,84) = 4.11, p = .018), and evaluating (F(1,84) = 3.54, p = .031), with no significant effects for wondering, describing, observing, or total score. ## Evidence

### Brown 2013

Brown, J. A., Greenfield, D. B., Bell, E., Juárez, C. L., Myers, T., & Nayfeld, I. (2013). Early Childhood Hands-On Science Efficacy Study. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563045

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First year of a randomized controlled trial with 91 Head Start classrooms (45 ECHOS, 46 control); teachers were observed with the PreSCOT at baseline, winter, and spring. The article reports "ECHOS teachers significantly improved their scores in predicting, investigating, and evaluating" with F values as printed in Table 1.

> "A series of repeated-measures analysis of variance (ANOVA) revealed that ECHOS teachers significantly improved their scores in predicting, investigating, and evaluating across the year, while control group teachers did not"
