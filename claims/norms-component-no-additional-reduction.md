## Evidence

### Rogers and Feller 2016

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller. (2016). Parent Beliefs and Student Absences: Large Absence-Reduction Field Experiment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2016/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized field experiment, the Encourage + Self + Norms arm (7,666 students) received mail comparing the student's absences to the typical student in their school and grade. No significant additional effect versus Encourage + Self; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there was no additional reduction in absences caused by the Norms component (p=0.24), meaning that the Encourage + Self and the Encourage + Self + Norms mailings had statistically indistinguishable impacts on attendance."
