### Joyeux Luc 2021

Joyeux Luc, Javaux Allan, Eastwood Mary P., De Bie Felix R., Van den Bergh Gert, Degliuomini Rebecca S., Vergote Simen, Micheletti Talita, Callewaert Geertje, Ourselin Sebastien, De Coppi Paolo, Van Calenbergh Frank, Vander Poorten Emmanuel, Deprest Jan. (2021). Validation of a high-fidelity training model for fetoscopic spina bifida surgery. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-85607-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity analysis of 52 single-port simulated repairs by two novices and 18 multi-port repairs by three experts, using the Competency Cumulative Sum test with control limit hC = 3. The article reports "The two groups were also significantly different for all surgical outcomes measured when applying the group-splitting method".

> "As C-CUSUM scores of the three experts remained below the competency control limit hC = 3, they were considered competent. In contrast, scores of both novices reached a higher score of 3.07 after the 6th case and were therefore not competent."
