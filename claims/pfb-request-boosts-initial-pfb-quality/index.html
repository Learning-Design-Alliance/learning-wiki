Gielen 2015¶

Gielen, M. & De Wever, B. (2015). Scripting the role of assessor and assessee in peer assessment in a wiki environment: Impact on peer feedback quality and product improvement. Computers & Education, 88, 370-386. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.07.012

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel repeated-measures analysis of PFB scores in the 2x2 wiki peer assessment study. The results section reports that "the PFB scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 2 for the request condition with 11.88" and 7.50 for the combination condition.

"the PFB scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 2 for the request condition with 11.88 (χ2=16.381, df=1, p<.001), and with 7.50 for the combination condition (χ2=5.936, df=1, p=.015)"
