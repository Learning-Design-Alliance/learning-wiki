Gerry Stahl 2015¶

Gerry Stahl. (2015). A decade of CSCL. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-015-9222-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The editorial asserts, based on accumulated CSCL research evidence it attributes to the field at large, that teacher development takes several years and effective curriculum requires many redesign iterations. These costly processes, it argues, require national institutional and international funding commitment. No specific studies or statistics are cited here.

"CSCL research has established that the development of teachers who can create collaborative classrooms takes several years and the development of curriculum that works effectively in small-group interaction requires many iterations of trial and redesign."
