### Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). The impact of perceived recognition by physics instructors on women's self-efficacy and interest. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of the 38 interviews identified this subtheme in 17 of 38 students (45%). Students reported that comments using "easy", "trivial", and "obvious" made them hesitate to ask questions because they worried their questions would look stupid.

> "In our analysis, 45% of the interviewed female students reported experiences that were coded under the subtheme feeling belittled for questions or efforts, which includes two codes: belittling students' questions or struggles and belittling students' efforts."
