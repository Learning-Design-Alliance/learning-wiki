## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Team-group patients had statistically significantly shorter times from ER arrival to blood routine report and to other blood tests than control-group patients. [→ Hong Yucai 2017](#hong-yucai-2017)

## Evidence

### Hong Yucai 2017

Hong Yucai, Cai Xiujun. (2017). Effect of team training on efficiency of trauma care in a Chinese hospital. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060517717401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Raw-cohort rank-sum comparison of 51 team versus 93 control grade I trauma patients. Median time to blood routine report was 8 versus 13 minutes and to other blood tests 21 versus 31 minutes; the article reports these as significantly shorter, with P < 0.01 for both.

> "blood routine report (8 [5–10.25] vs. 13 [10–21] min; P < 0.01), other blood tests (21 [14.75–25.75] vs. 31 [25–37] min; P < 0.01)"
