# Integrated model raises satisfaction, clinical thinking, practical skill self-ratings and engagement, with no difference in resource utilization

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Experimental group reported higher satisfaction (d = 0.78), clinical thinking improvement (d = 1.08), and participation (d = 1.07). [→ Peng Yizhong 2026](#peng-yizhong-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant difference in teaching resource utilization ratings (d = −0.13, P = 0.454). [→ Peng Yizhong 2026 (2)](#peng-yizhong-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Peng Yizhong 2026

Peng Yizhong, Hu Juanjuan, Wang Minjie, Zhou Xing, Bing Kaijian, Li Song, Yu Haiyan. (2026). Evaluation of an integrated micro-skills training and clinical pathway model in surgical internship education. PeerJ 14:e21501. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21501

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Five-item 5-point Likert questionnaire (S-CVI 0.92, Cronbach’s α = 0.87) administered after assessments. Participation showed "Cohen’s d = 1.07"; satisfaction, practical skills and clinical thinking were also significantly higher.

> "the student participation rate of the experimental group (4.63 ± 0.52) was also significantly higher than that of the control group (3.90 ± 0.79) (mean difference 0.73, 95% CI [0.50–0.96]; Cohen’s d = 1.07, 95% CI [0.70–1.43]; P < 0.001)"

### Peng Yizhong 2026 (2)

Peng Yizhong, Hu Juanjuan, Wang Minjie, Zhou Xing, Bing Kaijian, Li Song, Yu Haiyan. (2026). Evaluation of an integrated micro-skills training and clinical pathway model in surgical internship education. PeerJ 14:e21501. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21501

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Resource utilization showed "no statistically significant difference", which the authors read as indicating resource allocation was basically consistent between groups.

> "there was no statistically significant difference in the evaluation of teaching resource utilization between the two groups (4.15 ± 0.74 vs. 4.24 ± 0.63, mean difference −0.09, 95% CI [−0.33 to 0.15]; Cohen’s d = −0.13, 95% CI [−0.47 to 0.21]; P = 0.454)"
