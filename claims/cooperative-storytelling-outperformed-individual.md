### Yu-Ju Lan 2018

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The editorial reports, citing Liu, Huang, and Xu, a study with 55 sixth graders in which 28 completed individual tasks and the others cooperative tasks, collecting four kinds of data including knowledge test scores, language learner autonomy, foreign language classroom anxiety, and emotion. Cooperative students "outperformed those working individually in all the aforementioned elements"; no effect size is printed.
