## Evidence

### Xiao Meixia 2026

Xiao Meixia, Gao Yujian, Wang Yashu, Lei Ziwei, Shi Shengming. (2026). A study of the effectiveness of the BOPPPS model combined with FC-CBL in the standardized training of general medicine residents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1856743

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomised trial of 38 general-practice residents (19 per group) in one hospital in China, comparing a 12-week BOPPPS model combined with flipped-classroom case-based learning against lecture-based learning. These two outcomes are items on an anonymous questionnaire the residents completed at the end of training, so they are self-ratings, not assessed ability; the authors list reliance on self-report questionnaires for non-cognitive competencies as a limitation. Table 4 gives means of 4.08 ± 0.34 vs 3.24 ± 0.16 for teaching satisfaction (d = 1.48) and 3.91 ± 0.15 vs 3.15 ± 0.26 for self-directed learning capacity (d = 1.62), which the authors read as favouring BOPPPS/FC-CBL. Two inconsistencies in the paper limit how far these can be relied on: the methods define the five-point scale as 1 = "very satisfied" and 5 = "very dissatisfied", under which the higher intervention-group means would indicate less satisfaction; and the methods name a chi-square test for these items while the Table 4 note names independent-samples t-tests and the Mann–Whitney U test.

> "large to very large effect sizes were observed for teaching satisfaction (d = 1.48) and self-directed learning capacity (d = 1.62), indicating a substantively meaningful educational impact beyond statistical significance"
