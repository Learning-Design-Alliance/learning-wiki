### Resnick PJ 2010

Resnick PJ, Janney AW, Buis LR, Richardson CR. (2010). Adding an Online Community to an Internet-Mediated Walking Program. Part 2: Strategies for Encouraging Community Participation. J Med Internet Res 12(4):e72. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1339

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive counts from the case study's analysis of staff rhetorical tactics: the article reports the staff had "mixed success" with the question ploy, with community-wide solicitations performing somewhat better than direct follow-up questions.

> "Of 39 staff responses that posed a question back to the original poster, only 12 elicited a response from the original poster, and 6 elicited a response from someone else. Staff responses that explicitly solicited replies from the whole community were somewhat more effective: Of 19 such messages, 5 elicited a response from the original poster, and 10 elicited a response from someone else."
