## Evidence

### Sussman S 2026

Sussman S, Melaragno L, Nisenbaum E, Malara M, Herster R, Orban C, Marquardt M, Haring C, Harmon K, VanKoevering K. (2026). Pilot Simulation Task Trainer for Prehospital Management of Neck Hemorrhage. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.47912

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second phase of the hands-on simulation, after the venous session and debrief: a catastrophic arterial hemorrhage was simulated and participants identified and managed it. "participants could observe the difference in brain perfusion with the 1- and 2-finger techniques".

> "They again explored the wound and were able to quickly identify the high-pressure and pulsatile bleeding of an arterial hemorrhage. They were guided to then digitally explore the wound and practice the 1- and 2-finger techniques for direct digital control of an arterial hemorrhage followed by gauze packing."
