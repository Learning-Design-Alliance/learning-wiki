## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The level of adaptation interacted with difficulty condition on task correctness, η2p = .39, with correctness rising when difficulty decreased and falling when it increased. [→ Koskinen 2023](#koskinen-2023)

## Evidence

### Koskinen 2023

Koskinen, A., McMullen, J., Hannula-Sormunen, M., Ninaus, M., & Kiili, K. (2023). The strength and direction of the difficulty adaptation affect situational interest in game-based learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104694

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA on task correctness (manipulation check) across nine basic/adapted level pairs found "significant interaction effect between the level of adaptation and difficulty condition" with a large printed effect η2p = .39; Table 5 shows d = 1.23 (level −3) and d = 1.05 (level +2).

> "there was a significant interaction effect between the level of adaptation and difficulty condition, F (5, 1428) = 184.24, p < .001, η2p = .39"
