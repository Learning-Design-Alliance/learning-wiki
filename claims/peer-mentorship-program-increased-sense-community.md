## Evidence

### M. K. Rodriguez Wimberly 2022

M. K. Rodriguez Wimberly, Alexander L. Rudolph, Carol Hood, Rachel E. Scherr, and Christine Pfund. (2022). A model of mentorship for students from historically underrepresented groups in STEM. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.03395](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.03395)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-year evaluation surveys of the pilot Peer Mentorship Program: 22 respondents in AY 19-20 and 20 in AY 20-21. "95% of survey respondents (20) felt they had connections within the Cal-Bridge Scholar community"; workshop positive-impact ratings dropped from 98% (15) to 78% (20).

> "In the first year no more than 50% of survey respondents (22) felt the programming had a positive impact on the strength of their relationships with scholars and alumni. In AY 20-21, 95% of survey respondents (20) felt they had connections within the Cal-Bridge Scholar community for whom they could reach out to for help."
