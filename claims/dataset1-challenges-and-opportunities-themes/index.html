Evidence¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen 2025¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen, Noha M. A. M. Hassan, Minna Huotilainen, Jaana-Maija Koivisto & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Design of generative AI-powered pedagogy for virtual reality environments in higher education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00326-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview-based thematic analysis of researcher notes from 30 meetings with 31 university teachers across 9 faculties and one research institute; the authors report that "the primary theme identiﬁed was challenges in current teaching," with categories of resources, knowledge, and digital tools (Table 1).

"From Dataset 1, the primary theme identiﬁed was challenges in current teaching. According to the lack of resources, the educators desired more teachers and additional funding to pay for course developers and to set deadlines for the projects."

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen 2025 (2)¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen, Noha M. A. M. Hassan, Minna Huotilainen, Jaana-Maija Koivisto & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Design of generative AI-powered pedagogy for virtual reality environments in higher education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00326-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Within the opportunities theme of Dataset 1, teachers described using VR to "demonstrate spatial and temporal scale" to make complex concepts such as cause and effect easier, and to do things that are impossible in the classroom (for example, showing the journey of a molecule from the body to wastewater, or walking inside a leaf to experience the carbon cycle).

"Teachers would also like to be able to demonstrate spatial and temporal scale, e.g., in biological processes: some effects happen slowly, some in the long term."
