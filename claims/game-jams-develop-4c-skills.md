## Evidence

### Contreras-Espinosa 2022

Contreras-Espinosa, R.S.; Eguia-Gomez, J.L. (2022). Game Jams as Valuable Tools for the Development of 21st-Century Skills. Sustainability 2022, 14, 2246. https://doi.org/10.3390/su14042246

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis (Atlas.ti, hermeneutic approach) of pre/post surveys, 40 interviews and direct observation across three game jams with 90 students aged 12–16. The authors conclude "participating in game jams activates creativity and critical thinking in participants" and builds communication and collaboration skills.

> "This study suggests that participating in game jams activates creativity and critical thinking in participants, and they contribute to the development of communication and team collaboration skills."
