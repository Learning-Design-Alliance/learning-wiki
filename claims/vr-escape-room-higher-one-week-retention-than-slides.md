## Subclaims
`q3 i3` At the one-week follow-up test the MetaRoundWorm group maintained significantly higher scores than the Interactive Slides group (r = .37). [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025](#xuanru-cheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Xuanru Cheng 2025

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-week follow-up test identical in content to the post-test, in the same between-subjects study of 48 participants; the VR group "maintained significantly higher scores than the Interactive Slides group (U = 133, p < .05, r = .37)".

> "However, the MetaRoundWorm group maintained significantly higher scores than the Interactive Slides group (U = 133, p < .05, r = .37)."

### Xuanru Cheng 2025 (2)

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired comparisons at follow-up showed "no significant differences were observed between post-test and follow-up test scores" for either condition; only test statistics were printed, so no within-group effect size beyond the reported d values is interpreted here.

> "While both groups showed slight declines from their post-test levels, no significant differences were observed between post-test and follow-up test scores (MetaRoundWorm: t(20) =−.96, p = .348, d = −.21); Interactive Slides: t(21) =−.82, p = .419, d = −.18)."
