## Evidence

### McCaffrey 2011

McCaffrey, D. F. and Lockwood, J. R. (2011). Missing data in value-added modeling of teacher effects. The Annals of Applied Statistics, 5(2A), 773-797. https://doi.org/10.1214/10-AOAS405

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analytical/illustrative calculation using posterior means of ν² and σ²t from the MAR model to compute regression-style weights for adjusted scores. The article reports average weights "3.69, 4.08 and 4.33" for three, four and five observed scores, concluding scores from five-score students have about three times the weight of one-score students.
