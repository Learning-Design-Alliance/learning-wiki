## Evidence

### Zhongzhou Chen 2023

Zhongzhou Chen, Emily Frederick, Colleen Cui, Munaimah Khan, Christopher Klatt, Mercedith Huang, Shiyang Su. (2023). Reforming Physics Exams Using Openly Accessible Large Isomorphic Problem Banks created with the assistance of Generative AI: an Explorative Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 2 CTT analysis on mid-term exam 2, where Q4 was a near transfer and Q8 a far transfer problem compared against open-bank problem Q6. The article reports the "boundary on the estimated impact of the op en problem bank approach to be between 5% and 10%"; the 5% Q4–Q6 difference was not statistically significant.

> "the results set the boundary on the estimated impact of the op en problem bank approach to be between 5% and 10%. Note that this variation is comparable to the observed variance in difficulty between different isomorphic versions of the same item."

### Zhongzhou Chen 2023 (2)

Zhongzhou Chen, Emily Frederick, Colleen Cui, Munaimah Khan, Christopher Klatt, Mercedith Huang, Shiyang Su. (2023). Reforming Physics Exams Using Openly Accessible Large Isomorphic Problem Banks created with the assistance of Generative AI: an Explorative Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 CTT analysis on mid-term exam 1 with both versions of Q4 and Q8 combined. The transfer problem Q8 had a correct rate of 34% versus 44% for open-bank Q4, "statistically significant (McNemar test, 𝜒2=10.26, p<0.01, df=1)".

> "Q8 ( 34%) also has a 10% lower correct rate than Q4 (44%), which is statistically significant (McNemar test, 𝜒2=10.26, p<0.01, df=1)."
