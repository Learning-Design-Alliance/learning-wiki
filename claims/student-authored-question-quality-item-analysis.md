Item analysis with Iteman software of the 100 most answered questions in each of three academic years, using Pearson point-biserial correlation and difficulty p values. Mean r-pbis values were "0.485 (2013–2014, year 1), 0.446 (2014–2015, year 2) and 0.480 (2014–2015, year 1)"; only two of 300 questions had r-pbis <0.20.
