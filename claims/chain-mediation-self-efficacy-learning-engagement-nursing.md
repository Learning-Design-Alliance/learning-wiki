### Zou Liqin 2024

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression (Table 2) including both mediators; the article reports "PI has a significant positive predictive effect on AA (β = 0.130, p < 0.01), indicating the presence of chained mediation between GSE and LE".

> "After including GSE and LE in the regression equation, PI has a significant positive predictive effect on AA (β = 0.130, p < 0.01), indicating the presence of chained mediation between GSE and LE. Hypothesis 4 is supported."

### Zou Liqin 2024 (2)

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Deviation-corrected percentile bootstrap test (repeated sampling 5,000 times) of the three-path chain; the confidence interval [0.013, 0.028] excludes 0, and "the mediating effect value is 0.020, indicating that the chain mediating effect between PI and AA of nursing college students is significant".

> "For PI→GSE→LE→AA, the confidence interval is [0.013, 0.028], excluding 0, and the mediating effect value is 0.020, indicating that the chain mediating effect between PI and AA of nursing college students is significant."
