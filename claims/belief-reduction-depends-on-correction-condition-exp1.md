### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus 2 × 3 within-subjects ANOVA on belief in misinformation in Experiment 1 showed a "pre/post by correction condition interaction" with ηp2 = 0.52, "showing that belief reduction depended on condition".

> "There was a pre/post by correction condition interaction, F(1.99, 1025.14) = 567.59; p < .001, MSE = 1.72, ηp2 = 0.52, showing that belief reduction depended on condition."
