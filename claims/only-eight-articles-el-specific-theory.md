Synthesis of theory use across the 100 included articles in the scoping review. The review reports that 43 articles explicitly used a theory, framework, or model, of which eight used an EL-specific framework, split between KELT and the Leicester Model.

> "Eight articles used a theoretical framework and/or model for EL; two were based specifically on KELT [57, 146] and six were based on the Leicester Model that used Kolb's work as a foundation [48, 61, 65, 100, 129, 131]."
