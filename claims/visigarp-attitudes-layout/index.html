Anders Bouwer 2010¶

Anders Bouwer, Bert Bredeweg. (2010). Graphical means for inspecting qualitative models of system behaviour. Instr Sci 38:173–208. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9083-4

q2 · i? · design · r2

A 14-item attitude questionnaire (1 = negative to 5 = positive) administered after the treatment in the 30-participant study. Overall mean was "M = 3.70, SD = 1.14"; the article separately reports layout clarity at M = 2.63 (SD = 1.30) and ease of adjusting layout at M = 4.03 (SD = 1.00).
