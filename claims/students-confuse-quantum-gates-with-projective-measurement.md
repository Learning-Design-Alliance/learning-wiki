## Evidence

### Hu 2024

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2024). Investigating and improving student understanding of the basics of quantum computing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative analysis of student responses to Questions 2b and 3d on constructing single-qubit gates. The authors observed a course-dependent split: nearly all Physics students gave matrix answers while only about half of QCQI students did.

> "provided a gate represented by a matrix for such questions, which almost always were projective measurement gates such as (1 00 0) or one of the familiar Pauli gates. A few students even explicitly mentioned that the gate should collapse the given state to the desired outcome, not recognizing the significance of the unitarity of quantum gates."
