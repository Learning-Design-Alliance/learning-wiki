Maher, C. A., Palius, M. F., Maher, J. A., Hmelo-Silver, C. E., & Sigley, R. (2014). Teachers Can Learn to Attend to Students' Reasoning Using Videos as a Tool. Issues in Teacher Education, 23(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1045688

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Breakdown of experimental growth rates by argument type (Table 5): Case Argument 1 contributed 38.9% (K-8 in-service), 26.9% (secondary pre-service), and 14.9% (K-5 pre-service). Both cases and inductive argument types contributed to growth rates.
