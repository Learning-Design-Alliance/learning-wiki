## Evidence

### Schmidt Jessica N. MD 2015

Schmidt Jessica N. MD, MPH; Kendall John MD; Smalley Courtney MD. (2015). Competency Assessment in Senior Emergency Medicine Residents for Core Ultrasound Skills. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.28587

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Re-analysis of the same nine-resident OSCE data separating CORD-defined core from advanced skills. Specific deficits included missed ovaries on pelvic US, inability of two residents to obtain apical four-chamber and parasternal short-axis ECHO views, and few residents identifying the superior mesenteric artery or celiac trunk. The re-analysis showed scores "went from 74 to 85.7%".

> "When the data was re-analyzed excluding those aspects of the exams that were considered advanced skills, overall scores went from 74 to 85.7%."
