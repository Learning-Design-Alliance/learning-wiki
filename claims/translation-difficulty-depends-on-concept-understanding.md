## Evidence

### Sinaga 2015

Sinaga, P., Suhandi, A., & Liliasari. (2015). The Effectiveness of Scaffolding Design in Training Writing Skills Physics Teaching Materials. International Journal of Instruction, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085295

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire and interview data gathered after the translation stage. 87% of students reported difficulty depending on concept understanding; 76% reported difficulty selecting an intermodal representation. These are self-report percentages, not effect sizes.

> "almost all students (87%) stated that they sometimes experienced difficulties depending on whether they understood the concepts contained in the initial representation mode given"

### Sinaga 2015 (2)

Sinaga, P., Suhandi, A., & Liliasari. (2015). The Effectiveness of Scaffolding Design in Training Writing Skills Physics Teaching Materials. International Journal of Instruction, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085295

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of normalized gains by source representation mode in electromagnetism (mathematic equation to other modes: gain 18, low) and waves and optics (graph to other modes: lowest). The authors conclude the most difficult translation differed by domain while overall translation ability did not depend on domain.

> "On the domain of electromagnetism, the most difficult translation among modes of representation was that from mathematic equation mode to other representation modes, while for the domain of waves and optics, the most difficult one was from representation mode of graph into other modes of representation"
