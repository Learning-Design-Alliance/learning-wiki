## Evidence

### Tadese Mesfin 2022

Tadese Mesfin, Yeshaneh Alex, Mulu Getaneh Baye. (2022). Determinants of good academic performance among university students in Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03461-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the multivariable logistic regression of the 615-student cross-sectional study, the 20–24 age group showed reduced odds relative to the 25–29 reference group; the article reports "AOR = 0.43, 95% CI = 0.22-0.91" (p = 0.026).

> "Students aged between 20 and 24 years were 56% less likely to score good academic performance compared to those who were aged between 25 and 29 years (AOR = 0.43, 95% CI = 0.22-0.91)."
