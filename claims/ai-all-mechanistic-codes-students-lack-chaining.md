Student and AI responses to physics problems examined through the lenses of sensemaking and mechanistic reasoning. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Quantitative distribution of mechanistic reasoning codes (Table 4) across 16 student and 16 AI responses to the open-ended and scaffolded Gravitron task. The article reports "eleven student responses across the two problem formats did not evidence the higher hierarchical code of chaining" while AI responses evidenced all codes.

> "Furthermore, eleven student responses across the two problem formats did not evidence the higher hierarchical code of chaining, primarily because of not being able to solve the problem completely and make the required prediction."
