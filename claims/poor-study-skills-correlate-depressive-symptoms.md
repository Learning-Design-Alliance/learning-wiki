## Evidence

### AlFaris Eiad 2018

AlFaris Eiad, Irfan Farhana, AlSayyari Shuaa, AlDahlawi Waad, Almuhaideb Shahad, Almehaidib Alanood, Almoqati Shaikha, Ahmed Abdullah M. A., Ponnamperuma Gominda, AlMughthim Muhannad, Shaffi Ahamed Shaik, Al Maflehi Nassr, van der Vleuten Cees. (2018). Validation of a new study skills scale to provide an explanation for depressive symptoms among medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0199037

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study (Year 2016) of 328 medical students who completed the SSI and BDI-II via Survey Monkey with no missing items. The "low-moderate, negative correlation" (r = -0.348, p<0.0001) indicates poorer study skills accompany higher depressive symptoms.

> "There was a statistically significant, low-moderate, negative correlation between the BDI-II score and the SSI total score (r = -0.348, p< 0.0001)."
