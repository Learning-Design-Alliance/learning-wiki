## Evidence

### Yang Sun-Yi 2023

Yang Sun-Yi. (2023). Impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic on Neonatal Nursing Practicum and Extended Reality Simulation Training Needs: A Descriptive and Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 20(1), 344. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20010344

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison (independent t-tests) in the cross-sectional survey of 132 students: on-site mean neonatal performance 2.86 ± 0.50 versus off-site 1.70 ± 0.68; training experience 2.70 ± 0.56 versus 1.70 ± 0.68. On-site was "significantly higher among students who had an on-site clinical practicum."

> "Neonatal nursing performance (t = 9.39, p < 0.001) and NICU training experience (t = 9.03, p < 0.001) were significantly higher among students who had an on-site clinical practicum than an off-site clinical practicum."
