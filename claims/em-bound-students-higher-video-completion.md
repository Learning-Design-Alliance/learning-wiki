### Rose E 2018

Rose E, Jhun P, Baluzy M, Hauck A, Huang J, Wagner J, Kearl YL, Behar S, Claudius I. (2018). Flipping the Classroom in Medical Student Education: Does Priming Work? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.8.35162

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of Zaption video-log viewing rates between months with exclusively EM-bound rotators and months with no EM-bound rotators; the printed rates were 71.5% versus 55.6% with p = 0.033.

> "During the 10 months in which exclusively EM-bound students were rotating (n=144), 71.5% (47.1–92.9) of students viewed the lectures. In the four months in which students were not EM-bound (n=54), 55.6% (33.3–71.4) of students viewed the lectures (Table 3a, p=0.033)."
