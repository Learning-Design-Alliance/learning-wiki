## Evidence

### Richard E. Mayer 2008

Mayer, R. E. (2008). Applying the science of learning: Evidence-based principles for the design of multimedia instruction. *American Psychologist, 63*(8), 760–769. [doi:10.1037/0003-066x.63.8.760](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066x.63.8.760)

`q3 · review summarising controlled experiments` · `i3 · large effect, median d = 0.85` · `n=5 experiments` · `review · r2`

Controlled experiments adding a pre-lesson giving the name, location, and behavior of each component before computer-based lessons on brakes, pumps, and geology; the article reports the quoted 5-of-5 result and that "The median effect size was 0.85, which is a large effect."

> "In 5 of 5 experiments, involving computer-based lessons on brakes, pumps, and geology, pretrained learners outperformed nonpretrained learners on transfer tests"

## Discussion

The one entry is Mayer's summary of five experiments, all with computer-based lessons on mechanical and geological systems (brakes, pumps, geology). The entry gives no sample sizes, test delays or settings for them; [Pretraining learners on a system's key parts and terms](pretraining-improves-transfer.md) records a later randomised experiment in immersive VR.
