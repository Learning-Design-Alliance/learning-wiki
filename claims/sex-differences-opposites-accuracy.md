Table IV reports average times and penalties for 41 men and 59 women: easy lists 136 vs 169 seconds and penalties 31 vs 44; hard lists 357 vs 296 seconds and penalties 427 vs 453. The men were mostly of higher scholastic rank (8 faculty, 18 graduate students).
