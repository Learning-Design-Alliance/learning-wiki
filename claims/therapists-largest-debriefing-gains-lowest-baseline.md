## Evidence

### Gamboa Oscar Andrés 2018

Gamboa Oscar Andrés, Agudelo Sergio Iván, Maldonado María Jose, Leguizamón Diana C., Cala Sandra M. (2018). Evaluation of two strategies for debriefing simulation in the development of skills for neonatal resuscitation: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3831-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Profession-stratified analysis of the randomized trial (Table 2). Therapists gained 42.71% (video) and 36.46% (oral) per scenario; physicians 25.81% and 22.18%; nurses 32.14% and 20.24%. The authors attribute this to therapists' lower starting scores.

> "the two debriefing strategies increased their compliance percentages and that the therapists increased their scores by the greatest percentages after each scenario (Table 2)."
