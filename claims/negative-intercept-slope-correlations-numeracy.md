### Verkerk 2026

Verkerk, J. C., van de Rijt, B., & van Viersen, S. (2026). Development of numerical relational and counting skills as components of early numeracy. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the univariate LGCMs of the population sample, "a weak negative correlation between the intercept and linear slope of NRS and a moderate negative correlation between the intercept and linear slope of CS was found", meaning children with higher initial levels grew more slowly linearly.
