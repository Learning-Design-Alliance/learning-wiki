## Evidence

### Puttaert 2025

Puttaert, N., Dachet, D., Baye, A., Quertemont, E., Leduc, L., Nyssen, A.-S., & Verpoorten, D. (2025). Effects of SI-PASS on a high-risk course – A randomized controlled trial. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102042. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102042

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test comparison on the adapted French SACQ and MSSCWS dimensions (performance, environment, social adjustment, application, attachment, affects, climate, somatization) between 86 experimental and 74 control students. "No p value reaches significance level" across all eight dimensions.

> "Table 5 displays the effect sizes of all dimensions in the post-test when comparing the two groups. No p value reaches significance level, thus failing to reject the null hypothesis."
