Authors' stated limitation in the Limitations section. They note the absence of a benchmark questionnaire prevented criterion-related validity testing, and that the study "was conducted within the Chinese cultural context", limiting generalizability.

> "First, due to the absence of a universally accepted validated questionnaire as a benchmark, our questionnaire did not undergo standard criterion-related validity testing."
