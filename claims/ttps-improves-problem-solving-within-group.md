## Evidence

### Bostic et al. 2016

Jonathan D. Bostic, Stephen J. Pape, Tim Jacobbe. (2016). Encouraging Sixth-Grade Students' Problem-Solving Performance by Teaching Through Problem Solving. Investigations in Mathematics Learning, Volume 8, Number 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094896

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures t-tests on a one-month teaching experiment with 18 intervention and 40 comparison students. The intervention group's gain was statistically significant with "d = .48", while the comparison group's pre-post change was not significant.

> "Students in the intervention group performed better on the problem-solving posttest than the pretest, t(17) = 2.65, p = .02, d = .48, whereas their peers in the comparison group did not improve, t(39) = 0.52, p = .61"
