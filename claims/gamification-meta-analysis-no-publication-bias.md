## Evidence

### Liuyufeng Li 2024

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Four publication-bias analyses were run on the meta-analytic dataset: funnel plot, classic fail-safe N test, Egger's regression, and Begg and Mazumdar's rank correlation. All indicated no bias; the fail-safe N was 281 null studies.

> "For the overall effect to be statistically nonsignificant, 281 missing studies with zero mean effect size were required. We thus concluded that the overall average effect size observed in our study was not exaggerated by publication bias"
