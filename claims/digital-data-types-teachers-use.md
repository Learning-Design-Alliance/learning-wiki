## Evidence

### Alina Hase 2024

Alina Hase, Poldi Kuhl. (2024). Teachers’ use of data from digital learning platforms for instructional design: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10356-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis answering the review's second research question on what digital data teachers use. Data spanned individual student, classroom, and school levels; the review also reports that "Colors were primarily used to classify student performance into different levels", e.g., green, red, and grey icons.

> "The use of progress data such as information on students’ mastery of skills, misconceptions, and time spent with the learning platform, was explicitly mentioned in the studies of Hinkle (2021), Jewitt et al. (2011), Molenaar and Knoop-van Campen (2018) and Xhakaj et al. (2016, 2017)."
