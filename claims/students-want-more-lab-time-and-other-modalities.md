Sudha R, Kumar GG, Ratnajeet C, Tushar K, Kishlay KM, Das AK, Shekhar S. (2024). Comparative Analysis of Multidimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy: A Randomized Control Trial. Annals of African Medicine 23(3):459-465. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_214_23

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the open-ended questionnaire items in the same trial; the article reports the printed percentages of 95% and 83% and the reciprocal cross-group preferences, with about 30% of dissection students describing the experience as the start of their surgical career.

> "In open-ended questions, the majority of students (95%) needed more time in the laboratory and also wanted to try other teaching modalities (83%) for better knowledge. However, students of the dissection group preferred 3D software during clarification of anatomy in the upcoming semesters."
