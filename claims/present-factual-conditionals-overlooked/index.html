Wu 2012¶

Mei Wu. (2012). The Probability Approach to English If-conditional Sentences. English Language Teaching, 5(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n5p37

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The article cites Zhang Bin's (2005) empirical study of 70 Chinese college students finding that many mistook present factual conditionals for predictive ones by adding modal will. Together with Zhao Xiuzhen (1996), this supports that present factual conditionals are overlooked despite high frequency.

"quite a lot of participants mistook present factual conditionals as predictive conditionals, and the evidence was that they added modal will in the main clause of the present factual conditionals"
