`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

A research brief, not the study itself: it describes getting-to-know-you surveys administered at the beginning of the year (families, interests, hobbies, goals, educational strengths) and reports the results of "one study" secondhand, without naming it or describing its design. The gap-closure figure of more than 60% is printed in the brief, but no standardized effect size is, so impact is coded `i?` (not reported), which does not mean the effect was null. The brief's wording reports more positive relationships and higher grades; it does not establish that the surveys caused them.

> "In one study, students and teachers who learned about their commonalities reported more positive relationships with each other. Students in that study also earned higher grades when teachers learned what they had in common, and achievement gap disparities for Black and Latinx students closed by more than 60%."
