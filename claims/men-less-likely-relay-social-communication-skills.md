Chi-Square Tests of Independence conducted via SPSS on the project's broader-ecosystem survey (N=310 for these tests) found "men indicated that they were less likely than women to effectively relay their social skills" and communication skills. Only test statistics are printed; no effect size.

> "In addition, analysis of gender differences found that men indicated that they were less likely than women to effectively relay their social skills (X2 (2, N = 310) = 11.84, p < .003) and communication skills (X2 (2, N = 310) = 7.69, p < .02)."
