`q3 i?` In an RCT of older adults, linear mixed-effects models showed significant group × time interactions for health knowledge, health behaviours, health skills, health intentions and total health literacy, favouring the active learning intervention group over the pamphlet-only control group. ## Evidence

### Jin Shengxuan 2025

Jin Shengxuan, Fa Ruobing, Chao Jianqian, Zhang Na, Bao Min, Wu Yanqian, Wang Leixia, Tan Gangrui. (2025). A multistage research on factors influencing and active learning intervention on health literacy of community-residing elderly adults in Nanjing. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-23394-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-arm parallel RCT: 120 older adults randomized to a 12-week active learning program (n=60) or pamphlet-only control (n=60); 57 intervention participants analyzed at follow-up. The mixed-effects analysis found "significant group × time interactions for HK, HBV, HS, HI, and HL"; no effect sizes were printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Furthermore, the linear mixed-effects model revealed significant group × time interactions for HK, HBV, HS, HI, and HL, indicating that the intervention group achieved more significant improvements in these dimensions by the end of the trial."
