# Effectiveness of inquiry-based curricula in K–12 is higher when learners receive more guidance and when learners are older, but meta-analytic findings are mixed and underwhelming

## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2021

Garvin Brod. (2021). How Can We Make Active Learning Work in K–12 Education? Considering Prerequisites for a Successful Construction of Understanding. Psychological Science in the Public Interest. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100621997376

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reviews meta-analyses (Alfieri et al., 2011; D'Angelo et al., 2014; Furtak et al., 2012; Lazonder & Harmsen, 2016; Rönnebeck et al., 2016) finding effectiveness "mixed and underwhelming" overall, but higher with more guidance and at higher grades.

> "Findings regarding effectiveness of these curricula in improving student performance have been mixed and underwhelming. A common thread across these meta-analyses is that effectiveness is higher (a) when learners receive more guidance and (b) when learners are older (i.e., in higher grades)."
