## Evidence

### Horner 2018

Horner, R. H., Newton, J. S., Todd, A. W., Algozzine, B., Algozzine, K., Cusumano, D., & Preston, A. (2018). A Randomized Waitlist Controlled Analysis of Team-Initiated Problem Solving Professional Development and Use. Behavioral Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/0198742917745638

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on DORA-II Meeting Foundations scores across four observations of 38 teams. The follow-up contrast favored trained teams; the article reports "ES = .68" for that comparison.

> "higher at the O3 follow-up observation for the IM (M = 0.87) than for the WL (M = 0.74) teams (t O3 = 2.42, df = 35, p = .02, ES = .68)"
