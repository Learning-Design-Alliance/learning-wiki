Curtis J Jones 2016¶

Curtis J Jones, Michael Christian, Andrew Rice. (2016). The Results of a Randomized Control Trial Evaluation of the SPARK Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567484

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Interaction terms in the grade-level PALS models tested whether SPARK's literacy impact differed by baseline literacy and achievement; the printed statistic is t = 5.61, p < .001, with no effect size printed for the interaction itself.

"The pooled interaction term of SPARK participation and baseline PALS scores/ MAP scores was again significant (t = 5.61, p < .001)."

Curtis J Jones 2016 (2)¶

Curtis J Jones, Michael Christian, Andrew Rice. (2016). The Results of a Randomized Control Trial Evaluation of the SPARK Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567484

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Split-sample PALS models for students in the lower half of baseline achievement, reported in Table 8, show an overall unstandardized effect of 10.47 points, described as "10.5 points" with "an effect size (Hedge's g) of .66".

"The unstandardized effect of SPARK on PALS for these students in the lower half of achievement at baseline was 10.5 points (Table 8). This corresponds to an effect size (Hedge's g) of .66."
