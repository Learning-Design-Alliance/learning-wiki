Emily Zohar 2026¶

Emily Zohar, Paul Bloom & Michael Inzlicht. (2026). Against frictionless AI. Communications Psychology 4:39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44271-026-00402-1

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the comment, citing prior work on training language models for warmth. The authors argue that "They sycophantically agree with nearly everything we say" and that real friends provide the corrective feedback sycophantic AI companions lack. No new data are reported.

"AI companions, by contrast, are frictionless. They sycophantically agree with nearly everything we say, even when we say and believe dan- gerous things11."
