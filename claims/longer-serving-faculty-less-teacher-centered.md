## Evidence

### Pacifico JL 2020

Pacifico JL, Donkers J, Jacobs J, van der Vleuten C, Heeneman S. (2020). Understanding teaching and learning conceptions among clinical faculty as a means to improve postgraduate training. International Journal of Medical Education, 11, 175-185. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5f2a.76eb

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of cluster properties from the K-means analysis of 172 survey respondents. The nurturer cluster had the longest average years in practice (13.2) and the lowest teacher-centeredness score (TC = 3.26 vs 4.49 and 4.29 in other clusters).

> "Among the 3 clusters, this group has the longest number of years in practice, 13.2 years. It also has the lowest preference for TC items in the COLT."
