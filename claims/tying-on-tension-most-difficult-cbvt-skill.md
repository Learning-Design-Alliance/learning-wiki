`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA comparison across skills in the 221-student cohort, reported alongside Fig. 3: "Our data suggested that the most difficult skill to master was tying against tension". Tension-tying GRS reached only 2.51 versus ≥3.25 for other skills.

> "Despite a significant increase from pre-course scores, we found a lower average increase in PQS (P = 0.027), GRS (P < 0.0010) and speed (P < 0.001) for the tying on tension skill compared to the other knot tying skills, which were not significantly different from each other."
