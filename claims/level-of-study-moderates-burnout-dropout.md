### Daniel J. Madigan 2026

Daniel J. Madigan, Hanna L. Glandorf. (2026). Student Burnout and Educational Dropout: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Dropout Behavior and Intentions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10191-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis within the multilevel meta-analysis of dropout intentions effects. The article reports level of study was significant, F(3,33) = 4.45, p < .01, with postgraduate education showing a significant positive effect versus secondary education; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Level of study ( F(3,33) = 4.45, p < .01) and burnout dimension (F(3,33) = 17.74, p < .0001) showed to have a significant effect on the overall pooled effect. When Secondary education was set as the reference level, Postgraduate education had a significant positive effect ( z = .24, p = .04)."
