Evidence¶

Marvin Roski 2024¶

Marvin Roski, Ratan Sebastian, Ralph Ewerth, Anett Hoppe, Andreas Nehring. (2024). Learning analytics and the Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A clustering approach. Computers & Education 214 (2024) 105028. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105028

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis of log-file-derived usage durations from 384 learners on the I3Learn platform. Suitability was supported by the Bartlett test (chi-square = 5389.13, p < 0.001) and a "KMO value = 0.677". The authors reduced a five-factor elbow solution to four factors for interpretability.

"The results of both the Bartlett test (chi-square = 5389.13, p < 0.001) and the Kaiser-Mayer-Olkin coefficient (KMO value = 0.677) indicate the general feasibility of EFA with the data set"

Marvin Roski 2024 (2)¶

Marvin Roski, Ratan Sebastian, Ralph Ewerth, Anett Hoppe, Andreas Nehring. (2024). Learning analytics and the Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A clustering approach. Computers & Education 214 (2024) 105028. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105028

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The authors' interpretation of the EFA factor structure: Factor 1 captured tasks with help and Factor 3 tasks without help, while Factor 2 captured videos and Factor 4 texts, implying that "learners remain consistent in decision-making behavior throughout using I3Learn". This is an interpretation of factor content, not a tested comparison.

"Factor 1 mainly contains tasks with help, Factor 3 mainly without help. This implies that learners remain consistent in decision-making behavior throughout using I3Learn: They tend to choose the tasks with or without help, regardless of where the tasks are located on their learning journey."
