Michael F. J. Fox, Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Teaching labs during a pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020 and an outlook for the future. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
