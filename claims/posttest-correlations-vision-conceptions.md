# After training, better professional vision and reasoning are correlated with more learning-centered conception changes and fewer misconceptions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Post-training, teachers with better professional vision and reasoning showed conception changes toward less transmission and innate-quality beliefs and more support of knowledge construction, and better reasoners had fewer misconceptions (r = −0.246, p = 0.050). [→ Heinonen 2023](#heinonen-2023)

## Evidence

### Heinonen 2023

Heinonen, N., Katajavuori, N., Murtonen, M., & Södervik, I. (2023). Short pedagogical training in supporting university teachers' professional vision: A comparison of prospective and current faculty teachers. Instructional Science, 51, 201–229. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09603-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of post-test scores among the 64 participants. The study reports correlations between professional vision/reasoning and conception changes, e.g. transmission beliefs r = −0.290 and r = −0.344, innate quality r = −0.285 and r = −0.289, knowledge construction r = 0.248 and r = 0.254, misconceptions r = −0.246.

> "teachers who had better professional vision and reasoning skills changed in their scores concerning conceptions about (a) teaching as the transmission of subject knowledge (r = −0.290, p = 0.020; r = −0.344, p = 0.005"
