The review reports, citing Feldman-Maggor et al. (2025), that disciplinary expertise was necessary but insufficient for recognizing representational bias, quoting "the teachers' TPACK was not enough to critically evaluate the chat output." The article attributes this to a chemistry teacher education context.

> "Feldman-Maggor et al. (2025) identified hallucination and gender-racial representational bias in ChatGPT outputs as challenges for chemistry teacher education, showing that disciplinary content expertise is necessary for teachers to detect content errors and fabricated references, yet insufficient for recognizing representational bias"
