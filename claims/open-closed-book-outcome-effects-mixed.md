The review reports a mixed pattern of learning-outcome effects across format-comparison studies: "significant effects favoring the open-book format" in some studies, no significant differences in others (e.g., Arnold et al., 2021; Roelle & Nückles, 2019; Waldeyer et al., 2020), and closed-book advantages described as few and far between. No pooled effect size is printed.
