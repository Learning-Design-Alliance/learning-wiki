## Evidence

### Yang Yi 2025

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression in the results section predicting knowledgeable versus not-knowledgeable status. The model reports "OR 1·42 [95% CI 1·40-1·44]" for age and "3·71 [2·64-5·19]" for male gender, with experience OR 1·41 [1·35-1·46], all p<0·001.

> "knowledgeable practitioners were significantly older (45·4 vs 36·2 years; OR 1·42 [95% CI 1·40-1·44]; p<0·001) and predominantly male (61·9% vs 30·5%; 3·71 [2·64-5·19]; p<0·001)"

### Yang Yi 2025 (2)

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same multivariable logistic model, practice-setting contrast with academic hospital as reference. Community-hospital OR "0·12 [0·08-0·20]" and private-practice OR "0·30 [0·18-0·52]"; tertiary centers did not differ significantly (OR 1.06, p=0.85).

> "Practice setting significantly influenced knowledge levels (p<0·001), with academic hospitals showing higher competency compared to community hospitals (0·12 [0·08-0·20]; p<0·001) and private practices (0·30 [0·18-0·52]; p<0·001)"
