## Evidence

### Kaijser Mirjam A. 2024

Kaijser Mirjam A., Slagter Nienke, de Heide Loek J.M., van Beek André P., Pierie Jean-Pierre E.N., Emous Marloes. (2024). Measuring Small Bowel Length in Bariatric Surgery: An Ex Vivo Laparoscopic Training Experiment. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506241244854

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman test comparison of repeated rope measurements across the three time points in the ex vivo training experiment. The article reports "P = 1.0"; no effect size is printed, and equivalence was not tested.

> "the residents estimated the baseline rope measurements with an absolute deviation of 10% [5-22] from the goal length. Second and final rope measurements showed similar results of 9 [6-21] and 11% [7-18] deviation (P = 1.0)."
