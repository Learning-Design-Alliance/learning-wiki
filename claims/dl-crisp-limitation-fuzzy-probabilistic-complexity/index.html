Evidence¶

Tatyana Ivanova 2026¶

Tatyana Ivanova. (2026). Ontology-Based Layered Hybrid AI-Driven Knowledge Model for Personalized E-Learning. Mathematics 2026, 14, 808. https://doi.org/10.3390/math14050808

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical statement in the article's section on description logic and ontologies, explaining why fuzzy and probabilistic DL extensions exist. No empirical data are reported.

"One of the main limitations of classical Description Logics lies in their restriction to crisp knowledge representation, where assertions are either completely true or completely false, thus preventing the direct modeling of vagueness, uncertainty, or partial truth."

Tatyana Ivanova 2026 (2)¶

Tatyana Ivanova. (2026). Ontology-Based Layered Hybrid AI-Driven Knowledge Model for Personalized E-Learning. Mathematics 2026, 14, 808. https://doi.org/10.3390/math14050808

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article's assessment of fuzzy DL tooling and scalability in its fuzzy DL section, which also notes that most practical ontology-based systems prefer crisp ontologies because few fuzzy tools are standardized, optimized, and well supported. The article separately states that "Probabilistic DLs significantly increase reasoning complexity in comparison to its corresponding crisp DLs."

"Usually, fuzzy reasoning over large ontologies becomes inefficient because of the high (exponential or higher) computational complexity."
