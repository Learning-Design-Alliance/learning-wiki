## Evidence

### A. Lisboa 2021

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Measurements taken by the blind student at two string tensions (Tables 1 and 2), analyzed by linear regression of f versus 1/λ. The fractional errors were 'ξ = 14% and ξ = 10% respectively', matching the setup's estimated uncertainty.

> "the value of ξ reported for T = 3.43 N and T = 4.41 N are ξ = 14% and ξ = 10% respectively. These error values are consistent with the experimental setup and with the error estimated in the previous section."
