## Evidence

### Dubuc Marie-Maude 2019

Dubuc Marie-Maude, Aubertin-Leheudre Mylène, Karelis Antony D. (2019). Lifestyle Habits Predict Academic Performance in High School Students: The Adolescent Student Academic Performance Longitudinal Study (ASAP). International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17010243

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson's partial correlations at baseline in the 3-year ASAP study (187 completers; females n = 116, males n = 71), controlled for age, pubertal status, socioeconomic status, and ethnicity. The authors report "negatively associated with academic performance in female students", with discussion noting r values up to 0.44 (e.g., cellphone WE with language r = −0.42).

> "Almost all of the screen time variables were negatively associated with academic performance in female students, whereas very few associations were noted in male students."
