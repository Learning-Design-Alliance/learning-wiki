Rahimli M, Gumbs AA, Perrakis A, Al-Madhi S, Dölling M, Stelter F, Lorenz E, Andric M, Franz M, Arend J, Croner RS. (2025). Learning curve analysis of 100 consecutive robotic liver resections. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11551-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Conversion analysis across the four learning phases in the 100-case single-surgeon series; the sole Full Mastery conversion was due to intraoperative portal vein infiltration requiring resection and reconstruction. The phase contrast in Table 4 was p = 0.054.

> "Notably, 8 (88.9%) out of 9 conversions occurred before the 66th procedure, spanning the Competency, Proficiency, and Early Mastery phases. In the Full Mastery phase (Cases 66–100), only one conversion was observed."
