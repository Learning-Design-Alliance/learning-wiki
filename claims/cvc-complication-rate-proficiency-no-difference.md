### De Cassai Alessandro 2021

Safety in training for ultrasound guided internal jugular vein CVC placement: a propensity score analysis. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01460-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis in the same propensity-matched cohort (1060 NPO vs 1060 PO procedures), outcomes retrieved from the procedure register, chest radiography, procedure chart and patient diary. All four complication types showed non-significant differences between non-proficient and proficient residents; no hemothorax was reported.

> "Moreover, cohorts had the same rate of hematomas, catheter tip malposition, arterial puncture and pneumothorax. No cases of hemothorax were reported."
