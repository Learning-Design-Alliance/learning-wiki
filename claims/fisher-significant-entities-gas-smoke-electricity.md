# Only 'gas', 'smoke' and 'electricity' show statistically significant response differences across educational levels, indicating formal instruction shapes association of radioactivity with intermediate ontological categories

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the entities 'gas', 'smoke' and 'electricity' — belonging to the intermediate 'dispersed' and 'immaterial' categories — the dissimilarity of response distributions among educational levels is statistically significant, while all other entities show no significant differences.
