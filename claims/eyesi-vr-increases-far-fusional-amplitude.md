### Giannuzzi Federico 2025

Giannuzzi Federico, Hu Lorenzo, Carlà Matteo Mario, Cestrone Valentina, Marcelli Sofia, De Vico Umberto, Savastano Alfonso, Crincoli Emanuele, Savastano Maria Cristina, Rizzo Stanislao. (2025). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Orthoptic assessment of 34 trainees at baseline (T0) and 30 min after simulation (T1). The article reports "Far fusional amplitude without striated lenses significantly increased from 19.4 ± 8.8 Δ (T0) to 23.9 ± 9.7 Δ (T1; p = 0.003, Fig. 2)" and a parallel significant increase with striated lenses.

> "Far fusional amplitude without striated lenses significantly increased from 19.4 ± 8.8 Δ (T0) to 23.9 ± 9.7 Δ (T1; p = 0.003, Fig. 2). With striated lenses, far fusional amplitude also increased significantly, from 18.2 ± 10.4 Δ to 20.5 ± 9.1 Δ (p = 0.02, Fig. 3)."
