Evidence¶

Stephanie M. Jones 2017¶

Stephanie M. Jones, Sophie P. Barnes, Rebecca Bailey, and Emily J. Doolittle. (2017). Promoting Social and Emotional Competencies in Elementary School. The Future of Children, Vol. 27, No. 1, Spring 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1144815

q3 · i? · review · r2

The article cites this large-scale national study as an example of null findings from gold-standard elementary SEL evaluations. It reports that SACD "examined seven SEL programs over three years and found no differences" between intervention and non-intervention groups. No effect sizes are printed, so the null is reported as a non-difference, not a measured zero effect.

"the Social and Character Development Research Consortium (SACD) examined seven SEL programs over three years and found no differences between the groups receiving the interventions and those who did not."
