### Karsikas 2025

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results (Table 2, N=116): in this dimension 38.8% of managers rated their competence "fair," 42.2% "good" and 19.9% "very good" — the weakest of the five dimensions of the MCKM instrument.

> "Among the different dimensions of KM competence, planning competence and cooperation received the lowest rating, with a median of 2.719 and a range of 3.63."
