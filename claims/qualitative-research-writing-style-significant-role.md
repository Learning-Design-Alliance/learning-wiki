## Evidence

### Cooper et al. 2012

Robin Cooper, Ronald J. Chenail, and Stephanie Fleming. (2012). A Grounded Theory of Inductive Qualitative Research Education: Results of a Meta-Data-Analysis. The Qualitative Report. http://www.nova.edu/ssss/QR/QR17/cooper52.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative writing findings from the meta-data-analysis of 25 published articles. Students described learning to write qualitative research as uncomfortable because it deviates from academic writing norms, and reported multiple responsibilities in writing up findings, supporting the proposal that "learning a new writing style plays a significant role in learning a new research paradigm".

> "We propose these examples of the researchers' findings related to the experience of students learning qualitative research suggest that learning a new writing style plays a significant role in learning a new research paradigm."
