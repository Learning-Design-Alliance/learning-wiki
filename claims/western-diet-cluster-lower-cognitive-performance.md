### Peña-Jorquera Humberto 2024

Peña-Jorquera Humberto, Martínez-Flores Ricardo, Espinoza-Puelles Juan Pablo, López-Gil José Francisco, Ferrari Gerson, Zapata-Lamana Rafael, Lofrano-Prado Mara Cristina, Landaeta-Díaz Leslie, Cigarroa Igor, Durán-Agüero Samuel, Cristi-Montero Carlos. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu16050608

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of 1296 Chilean adolescents comparing four dietary clusters on four cognitive domains (NCPT battery), with school type as random effect. The WD cluster "showed lower performance across all cognitive domains than the other groups"; the WD–MedDiet contrast was not significant for inhibitory control (p = 0.142).

> "The WD group showed lower performance across all cognitive domains than the other groups. In particular, the WD group reported a negative difference in all domains compared with the MedDiet group (p < 0.05) except for inhibitory control (p = 0.142)."
