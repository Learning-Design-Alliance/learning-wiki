The column reports, citing Singh's 2005 peer-interaction study, that group work yields co-construction of knowledge. No new data are presented in this article; the finding is attributed to the cited PER literature.

> "Effective group work has been demonstrated to result in co-construction of knowledge3—that is, students working together can figure out answers to questions even without instructor's help that they would not have figured out on their own."
