Evidence¶

Sehrish Basir Nizamani 2026¶

Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Zannah Ziew, Saad Nizamani, and Khyati Goyal. (2026). Less Deliberate in Teams: Student LLM Use Across Individual and Collaborative Work. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30860 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.30860

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Survey measure of verification methods across individual and team assignments; the quote reports the printed effect size "V=0.357, medium effect". Non-verification more than quadrupled to 32.6% and "Test-based verification fell from 33.2% to 13.8%, a drop of 19.4 percentage points".

"Output verification showed the most pronounced shift of any behavioral dimension measured, with the largest effect size across all measures (𝜒 2(3)=92.99,𝑝<.0001,𝑉=0.357, medium effect)."

Sehrish Basir Nizamani 2026 (2)¶

Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Zannah Ziew, Saad Nizamani, and Khyati Goyal. (2026). Less Deliberate in Teams: Student LLM Use Across Individual and Collaborative Work. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30860

q2 · i1 · associational · r1

Sensitivity reanalysis restricted to student-assignment instances where the LLM was used, ruling out TP1 non-use as an artifact; the quote reports the printed effect size "V= 0.205, small effect" and the among-users percentages.

"The shift remains significant (𝜒 2(3)= 26.01, 𝑝<. 0001,𝑉= 0.205, small effect). Among users only, non-verification rose from 3.7% to 8.1%, and test-based verification among users fell from 59.5% to 20.5%, a larger drop than the unconditional figure."
