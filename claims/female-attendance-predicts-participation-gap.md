## Evidence

### Bailey E. G. 2020

Bailey E. G., Greenall R. F., Baek D. M., Morris C., Nelson N., Quirante T. M., Rice N. S., Rose S., Williams K. R. (2020). Female In-Class Participation and Performance Increase with More Female Peers and/or a Female Instructor in Life Sciences Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0266

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model analysis with observation as the statistical unit (n = 100 observations from 34 classrooms), classroom as random intercept (ICC 0.25), and AICc model selection (Table 1, Figure 4A). The regression coefficient for percent female attendance was 0.716 ± 0.333; class size, upper-level course, and instructor gender were not retained.

> "only the percentage of female students in attendance was retained as a fixed effect in the best model, with females being more likely to participate when there was a greater percentage of females in attendance"
