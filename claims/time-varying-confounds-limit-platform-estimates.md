# Time-varying confounds (e.g., hiring a tutor, school policy changes) remain an uneliminated threat to the platform's estimated effects

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The within-student longitudinal design eliminates time-invariant confounding but cannot rule out time-varying confounds such as tutoring or policy changes. [→ Weatherholtz 2024](#weatherholtz-2024)

## Evidence

### Weatherholtz 2024

Weatherholtz, K., Grimaldi, P., Yamkovenko, B., Hill, K.M. (2024). Evaluating the Impact of a Mathematics Mastery Learning Platform on Student Achievement: A Large-Scale Longitudinal Analysis. EC-TEL 2024, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol 15159, Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-72315-5_32

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' own stated limitation of the longitudinal within-student design: it removes confounding from time-invariant student factors but not from time-varying ones. The abstract names "hiring a tutor or a change to school policy" as examples.

> "A limitation of this approach is that we cannot rule out time-varying confounds, such as hiring a tutor or a change to school policy"
