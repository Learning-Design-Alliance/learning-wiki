### Bailey E. G. 2020

Bailey E. G., Greenall R. F., Baek D. M., Morris C., Nelson N., Quirante T. M., Rice N. S., Rose S., Williams K. R. (2020). Female In-Class Participation and Performance Increase with More Female Peers and/or a Female Instructor in Life Sciences Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0266

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on the 34-class observation data (Table 2, Figure 4B) with classroom as random intercept; the coefficient for overall call rate was 0.995 ± 0.375 (n = 100 observations from 34 classrooms). Group work, instructor questions, and participation grading policy were not retained. The article suggests females may speak up more when they do not have to compete to be called on.

> "only overall call rate was included in the best model, and a high overall call rate predicted more female verbal participation"
