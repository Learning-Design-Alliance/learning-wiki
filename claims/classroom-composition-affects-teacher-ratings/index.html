Evidence¶

Delgado 2026¶

Delgado, W., & Sartain, L. (2026). Classroom composition affects teacher performance ratings. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 48(2). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/classroom-composition-affects-teacher-performance-ratings

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental analysis of five years of Chicago Public Schools administrative and survey data comparing the same teacher across years as classroom composition changed, holding teacher and school constant. The study reports the index "raised classroom observation scores by 0.07 SD and student survey ratings by 0.13 SD" — printed standardized effects below d = 0.2.

"A one standard deviation (SD) increase in classroom quality index (based on baseline academic and behavioral measures) raised classroom observation scores by 0.07 SD and student survey ratings by 0.13 SD."
