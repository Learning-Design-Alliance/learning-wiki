## Evidence

### What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review 2012

What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review. (2012). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_memory

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

Review reports the Kane et al. (2007) experience-sampling study in which participants carried devices responding at unpredictable times during the day; low-span individuals reported more mind-wandering from tasks they tried to focus on.

> "It was found that low-span individuals were more likely to report that their minds were wandering away from the tasks on which they were trying to focus attention."

### What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review 2012 (2)

What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review. (2012). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_memory

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Same experience-sampling study: when participants reported being bored and not wanting to pay attention, mind-wandering was just as prevalent for high spans as for low spans, limiting the span difference to goal-relevant tasks.

> "The span-related difference in attending was only for tasks in which they reported that they wanted to pay attention."
