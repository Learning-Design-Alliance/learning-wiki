## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2022

Martin O. Archer, Cara L. Waters, Shafiat Dewan, Simon Foster, and Antonio Portas. (2022). GC Insights: Space sector careers resources in the UK need a greater diversity of roles. [doi:10.5194/gc-5-119-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-5-119-2022)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-squared tests against the Space Census 2020 distribution (n=1540) found the Edge Barrow School, RAS, and SpaceCareers.uk resources significantly unrepresentative; the article states "all of them over-represent scientific careers". The misconception link is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.

> "Comparing the resources to the space census, we find that all of them over-represent scientific careers. Given the low levels of young people aspiring towards being a scientist from an early age (Archer and DeWitt, 2017), it appears that these resources may perpetuate misconceptions about the usefulness of science."
