Evidence¶

Hsu 2015¶

Hsu, P.-S., Van Dyke, M., & Chen, Y. (2015). Examining the effect of teacher guidance on collaborative argumentation in middle level classrooms. RMLE Online, 38(9). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1074885

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Narrative literature review attributing this finding to Kulatunga, Moog, and Lewis (2013) and Tawfik and Jonassen (2013); the article reports it as prior research without restating study designs or effect sizes.

"Research indicated that, compared to the control (unguided) group, each type of teacher guidance improved different aspects of collaborative argumentation. The use of argumentative scripts improved the development of argumentation skills but did not improve the extent of participation and interactivity"
