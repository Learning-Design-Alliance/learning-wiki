Internal consistency was assessed in the construct validation sample of 207 nursing students. The article reports that 'The Cronbach's α‐statistic for the total scale yielded a value of 0.832 (95% CI 0.791–0.864)' and that McDonald's ω yielded an overall value of 0.841 (95% CI: 0.806–0.876).
