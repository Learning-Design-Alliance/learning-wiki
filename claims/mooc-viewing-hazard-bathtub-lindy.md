# MOOC viewing-duration hazard functions simultaneously exhibit a bathtub curve and a Lindy-effect decreasing tail

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The hazard function of empirical MOOC viewing durations is dominated by a concave bathtub curve but decreases in the tail, the latter known as the Lindy effect. [→ Zheng Xie 2020](#zheng-xie-2020)

## Evidence

### Zheng Xie 2020

Zheng Xie. (2020). Modelling the Dropout Patterns of MOOC Learners. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.26599/tst.2019.9010011](https://doi.org/10.26599/tst.2019.9010011)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survival analysis of viewing-duration log data from eight iCourse courses, with hazard functions plotted per course in Fig. 1. The text states that "The hazard function of empirical data is dominated by a concave function and has a decreasing tail (Fig. 1)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The hazard function of empirical data is dominated by a concave function and has a decreasing tail (Fig. 1). The shape of the concave part is known as bathtub curve"
