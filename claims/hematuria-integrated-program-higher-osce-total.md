## Evidence

### Peng Shuang 2026

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental between-group Welch t-test comparison of the primary endpoint in 80 urology clerkship students (n = 40 per group). The intervention group scored higher on the modified OSCE total score (85.39 ± 4.02 vs. 80.00 ± 5.16).

> "the intervention group had better performance than the control group in the modified OSCE total score (85.39 ± 4.02 vs. 80.00 ± 5.16, P = 0.000002)"

### Peng Shuang 2026 (2)

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Covariate-adjusted linear regression with HC3 robust standard errors on OSCE total score, adjusted for group, age, sex, major/training track, and weekly test 1. The group effect stayed significant, with the intervention group scoring 5.56 points higher on average (95% CI 2.92–8.21).

> "Compared with the control group, the intervention group scored 5.56 points higher on average (95% CI 2.92–8.21, P = 0.000037; R square = 0.267)"
