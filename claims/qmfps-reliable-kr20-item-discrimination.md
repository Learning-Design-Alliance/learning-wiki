## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2019

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Validation and Administration of a Conceptual Survey on the Formalism and Postulates of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis during large-scale administration of the final survey form. The KR-20 exceeded the design standard of 0.7, and "The average point-biserial is 0.41 and ranges from 0.20 – 0.62", which the authors read as reasonably good item discrimination.

> "The KR-20 for the QMFPS is 0.87, indicating that the survey is very reliable."
