Allensworth 2026¶

Allensworth, E., Gordon, A. & Young, C. (2026). Combining early grade assessments to study literacy skills: Addressing the variability in tests taken across schools and students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 19(1), 1-26. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/combining-early-grade-assessments-to-study-literacy-skills

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Predictive analysis of Chicago data using teacher-assigned literacy-standard grades on a 5-point A-F scale as the third grade outcome. MAP scores showed similar relationships to latent scores; other assessments slightly smaller.

"Most assessments, and the latent scores in grades K to 2, are also predictive of students’ ELA grades in third grade, with correlations ranging from 0.52 in kindergarten to 0.63 in second grade."
