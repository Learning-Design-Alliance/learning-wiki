## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Despite higher participation rates, treatment teachers did not show superior teaching practices compared with control teachers. [→ Hong 2013](#hong-2013)

## Evidence

### Hong 2013

Hong, Y., & Hong, G. (2013). Making Sense of the “Zero Effect” of Comprehensive Teacher Induction Programs: A Mediational Analysis. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563315

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' observation from the reanalysis of the Mathematica study data, where treatment and control groups exhibited similar teaching practices in spring of the first year.

> "However, we notice that a higher level of participation rate in the treatment group apparently did not lead to superiority in teaching practices in comparison with the control group."
