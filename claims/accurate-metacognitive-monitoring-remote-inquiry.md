# Seventh-graders in remote inquiry learning accurately monitor their own learning, with no differences between experimentation conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students' monitoring accuracy did not differ between conditions and did not differ from zero in two of three conditions, indicating very accurate self-monitoring of learning. [→ Flegr 2023](#flegr-2023)

## Evidence

### Flegr 2023

Flegr, S., Kuhn, J., & Scheiter, K. (2023). How to foster STEM learning during Covid-19 remote schooling: Combining virtual and video experiments. Learning and Instruction, 86, 101778. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis (RQ4) comparing judgement-of-learning bias (judgement × 10 minus actual post-test performance) across conditions. No condition difference emerged; only Video-Virtual showed slight overestimation (p = .03), and the judgement-of-learning correlated moderately with post-test scores, rs(134) = 0.33.

> "An ANOVA showed no significant difference between conditions, F(2, 133) = 1.21; MSE = 581.06; p = .30; η2 = 0.02 (small effect). In the condition Video-Virtual, students slightly overestimated their performance (p = .03; one-sample t-test)."
