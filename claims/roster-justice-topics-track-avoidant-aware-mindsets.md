# In Roster Justice, Avoidant-mindset participants described roster disparities in racially neutral terms while Aware-mindset participants named the racialized nature of the disparity

`q2 · i?`

Topic-prevalence analysis of Roster Justice responses (N = 7,606 sentence rows) against the Avoidant-Aware survey scale. Aware-mindset participants more often mentioned that class composition did not match school demographics, stereotypes about computer scientists, and the scheduling system perpetuating racial imbalance; Avoidant-mindset participants described differences in racially neutral terms.

> "Participants with more of an Aware attitude, by contrast, were more likely to see the racialized nature of the disparity."
