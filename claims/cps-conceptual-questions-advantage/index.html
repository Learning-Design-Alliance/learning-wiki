Docktor 2015¶

Docktor, J. L., Strand, N. E., Mestre, J. P., & Ross, B. H. (2015). Conceptual problem solving in high school physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 11, 020106. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.11.020106

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Written conceptual questions assessment in the three-school quasi-experiment; school A free-response and schools B–C multiple-choice formats. School C showed a non-significant 11% difference favoring CPS.

"The conceptual questions test showed a significant difference at two of the schools, a 10% difference at school A [tð35Þ¼ 2.54, p< 0.05] and a substantial 20% difference at school B [tð15Þ¼ 3.53, p< 0.005]."
