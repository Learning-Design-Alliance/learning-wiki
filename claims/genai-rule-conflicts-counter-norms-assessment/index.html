CHAT interpretation in the teacher-role theme. A student described teachers noticing AI-generated patterns, grading differently, and students adapting: "Now, I still take inspiration and write in a similar way, but in a way that feels more human."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Student suggestion quoted in the teacher-role theme, manifesting a contradiction between students' and teachers' rules. Another student reported teachers being inconsistent, from ChatGPT being completely forbidden to conditional use with verified calculations.

"I think it' s more about improvement for those teachers whose questions can simply be fed into ChatGPT—they may need to refine their assignments rather than impose restrictions that can never truly be enforced."

Related Claims¶

  • GenAI's immediate answers reduce engineering students' teacher-student interactions for generalizable tasks, while teachers remain preferred for complex problem-solving — related
  • Engineering students develop implicit rules for using GenAI as a self-directed, efficient learning tool when traditional educational tools fall short — related
  • Engineering students treat GenAI as acceptable when it stimulates reflection but view directly copying outputs as cheating, leveraging its fallibility to prompt double-checking — related
  • Nearly all students valued learning through effort while feeling drawn toward AI, and split into those who limited use and those whose use conflicted with stated values — related
  • Generic binary GenAI declarations fail to capture the diversity of ways students use GenAI in academic work — related
