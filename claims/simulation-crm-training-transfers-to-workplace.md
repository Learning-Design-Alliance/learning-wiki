Systematic review synthesis of four studies measuring transfer of simulation-based CRM learning into the clinical setting (Kirkpatrick Level 3), reporting that "simulation-enhanced CRM training was found significantly more effective than no intervention or didactic teaching" in three of them. No meta-analysis was performed because of heterogeneity of study design and outcome measures; a narrative summary was conducted instead.
