Stevens 2015¶

Stevens, W. D., Sartain, L., Allensworth, E. M., & Levenstein, R., with Guiltinan, S., Mader, N., Huynh, M. H., & Porter, S. (2015). Discipline Practices in Chicago Schools: Trends in the Use of Suspensions and Arrests. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/discipline-practices-chicago-schools-trends-use-suspensions-and-arrests

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of My Voice, My School survey responses from spring 2007 and 2009 through 2014, alongside CPS administrative suspension data. The report states perceptions "have been improving since the 2008-09 school year; this is also the period during which OSS rates declined in high schools."

"At the high school level, student perceptions of safety and teacher perceptions of order have been improving since the 2008-09 school year; this is also the period during which OSS rates declined in high schools."
