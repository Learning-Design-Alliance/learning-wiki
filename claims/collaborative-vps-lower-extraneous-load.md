### Fuoad Sura Ali 2022

Fuoad Sura Ali, El-Sayed Walid, Marei Hesham. (2022). Effect of different teaching/learning approaches using virtual patients on student’s situational interest and cognitive load: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03831-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intergroup comparison of extraneous cognitive load measured with the Leppink et al. (2013) 8-item questionnaire on a 1–10 Likert scale, administered at the end of each session to both cohorts (n=69, n=70).

> "The results also showed that the collaborative VPs session has the lowest extraneous cognitive load than the independent VPs and lecture sessions and this difference was a significant difference with P-value equal to 0.000 (P < 0.001)."
