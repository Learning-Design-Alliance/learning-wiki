## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2019

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Wells Cameron I., Wilkinson Tim J. (2019). Publication in a medical student journal predicts short- and long-term academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1704-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Short-term outcome of the matched-cohort study, comparing pre-graduation PubMed-indexed publication rates identified via standardised name and affiliation searches. Printed effect size OR = 5.38 favouring NZMSJ authors over non-publishing students.

> "One-third (32.7%) of NZMSJ authors identified had also co-authored at least one PubMed®-indexed publication prior to graduation (median 0 articles, range 0–23), compared with only 8.2% of students who had not published in the NZMSJ (Univariate OR 5.38, 95% CI 2.12–13.69, p < 0.001)."
