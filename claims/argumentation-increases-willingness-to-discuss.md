### Balci 2016

Balci, C. & Yenice, N. (2016). Effects of the scientific argumentation based learning process on teaching the unit of cell division and inheritance to eighth grade students. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 2(1), 67-84. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105345

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on the Argumentation Test (Infante & Rancer, 1982) controlling for pretest scores showed the experiment group's corrected post-test mean (71.14) was significantly higher than the control group's (66.78), indicating greater willingness to participate in discussions.

> "Table 6 indicates that when the pre -test scores of the groups are controlled there appears a statistically significant difference between the corrected mean post -test scores of the groups (F (1,74)= 10.264, p< .05)."
