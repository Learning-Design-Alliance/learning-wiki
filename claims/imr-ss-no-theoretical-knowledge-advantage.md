## Evidence

### Qian He 2025

Qian He, Jiahao Li, Ye Xiao, Jingjing Tian, Ningbo Mao, Sheng Lu, Yu Zhang, Yu Zhao. (2025). An immersive mixed reality surgical self-training system for precise pedicle screw placement: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07978-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT's assessment session, both groups completed a 12-item multiple-choice quiz; Fisher's exact test on aggregate correct:incorrect answers (183:9 IMR vs. 174:18 control) found no significant difference (p = 0.109). The authors attribute this to identical textual and graphic content and prior anatomy coursework.

> "There was no significant difference between the two groups in the quiz session. The participants might have relevant knowledge as they had learned systematic anatomy"
