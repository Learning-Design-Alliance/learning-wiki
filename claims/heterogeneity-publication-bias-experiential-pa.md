## Evidence

### Varman Sumantla D. 2023

Varman Sumantla D., Jones Rachel A., Kelly Bridget, Hammersley Megan L., Parrish Anne-Maree, Stanley Rebecca, Cliff Dylan P. (2023). The effect of experiential learning interventions on physical activity outcomes in children: A systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294987

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity analysis in SPSS 28.0 using Cochran Q-tests, Tau-squared, H-squared and I-squared. The review reports "statistically significant heterogeneity between studies for behaviour outcomes" (I2 = 79.9) and low heterogeneity for attitudes.

> "the Q-statistics values (Q = 36.552, df = 9, p < 0.001) were found to be statistically significant"

### Varman Sumantla D. 2023 (2)

Varman Sumantla D., Jones Rachel A., Kelly Bridget, Hammersley Megan L., Parrish Anne-Maree, Stanley Rebecca, Cliff Dylan P. (2023). The effect of experiential learning interventions on physical activity outcomes in children: A systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294987

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication bias assessed via forest and funnel plots, Egger's test (t = -0.85, P < 0.4 for behaviour) and Duval and Tweedie's trim and fill. The review reports "any potential publication bias was considered minimal" for behaviour outcomes.

> "The trim and fill method suggested that no adjustments were required to correct the asymmetry of the funnel plot. Therefore, any potential publication bias was considered minimal."
