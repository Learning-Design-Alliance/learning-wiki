M.H. Giménez, A. Vidaurre, J. Riera, and J.A. Monsoriu. (2007). Visualizing the Doppler Effect. Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702036](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702036)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Theoretical argument in the conclusions section: the authors contend that traditional resources show only static images, and that experiments or video mix different aspects involved in the process, making it difficult to show the essential aspects the teacher wants to transmit. No empirical data are offered for this claim.

> "The understanding of wave motion requires space- time visualization, which is very difficult to obtain using the traditional teaching resources blackboard, transparencies, slides... because these techniques only allow to show static images."
