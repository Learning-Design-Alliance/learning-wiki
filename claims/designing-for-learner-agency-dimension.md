Qualitative strand of an embedded mixed-methods study of teachers in an eight-week AI professional development program; hybrid thematic analysis of reflections and artifacts. A participant reported, "Instead of giving students answers, I started using AI to help them formulate better questions." The article notes these accounts indicate intentions, not students' experienced agency.
