Evidence¶

Loturco 2022¶

Loturco, I.; Montoya, N.P.; Ferraz, M.B.; Berbat, V.; Pereira, L.A. (2022). A Systematic Review of the Effects of Physical Activity on Specific Academic Skills of School Students. Educ. Sci. 2022, 12, 134. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci12020134

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effect meta-analysis (RevMan 5.4.1) pooling 24 included studies that used mathematics tests, with effects expressed as standardized mean differences between intervention and control groups. The review reports that "performance in the mathematics tests signiﬁcantly improved after the interventions when compared with control groups." No effect size value is printed in the reported text.
