## Evidence

### Symonds 2021

Symonds, J. E., Schreiber, J. B., & Torsney, B. M. (2021). Silver linings and storm clouds: Divergent profiles of student momentary engagement emerge in response to the same task. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000605

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression on the four largest profiles (engaged as reference) from the 196-student single-task sample. "Being female predicted being in the attentive disinterested profile (β = -2.79, OR = .06, p = < .001)", with the disengaged profile not predicted by gender. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Being female predicted being in the attentive disinterested profile (β = -2.79, OR = .06, p = < .001) and the attentive disaffected profile (β = -.90, OR = .41, p = < .001) but not the disengaged profile."

### Symonds 2021 (2)

Symonds, J. E., Schreiber, J. B., & Torsney, B. M. (2021). Silver linings and storm clouds: Divergent profiles of student momentary engagement emerge in response to the same task. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000605

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multinomial logistic regression with individual characteristics entered simultaneously as controls. "Being Irish predicted being in the attentive disaffected profile (β = -1.05, OR = .35, p = < .001)" and the disengaged profile, relative to the engaged reference profile. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Being Irish predicted being in the attentive disaffected profile (β = -1.05, OR = .35, p = < .001) and the disengaged profile (β = -1.30, OR = .27, p = < .01)."

### Symonds 2021 (3)

Symonds, J. E., Schreiber, J. B., & Torsney, B. M. (2021). Silver linings and storm clouds: Divergent profiles of student momentary engagement emerge in response to the same task. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000605

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "There were no age nor perceived economic differences between students in the engaged profile and the other three profiles."
