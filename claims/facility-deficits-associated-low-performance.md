## Evidence

### Kassaw Chalachew 2023

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2023). Determinants of academic achievement among higher education student found in low resource setting, A systematic review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294585

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 120 medical students at Bahir Dar University reviewed in the synthesis: "large class size", "inadequate classroom facilities", "low internet access", and an inadequate anatomy-teaching model were significantly associated with low anatomy exam performance; dormitory crowdedness was not significant (p = 0.11).

> "large class size (AOR = 2.36; 95% CI: 1.11–4.64 p = 0.005), inadequate classroom facilities (AOR = 1.56; 95% CI: 1.51–4.91, p = 0.001), low internet access (AOR = 1.99; 95% CI: 1.07–3.22, p = 0.015) and inadequate anatomy-teaching model (AOR = 2.63; 95% CI: 1.17–6.12, p = 0.003) were significantly associated with low performance of students in human anatomy course exam"
