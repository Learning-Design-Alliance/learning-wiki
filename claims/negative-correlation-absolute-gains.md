## Evidence

### Simonsmeier 2022

Simonsmeier, B. A., Flaig, M., Deiglmayr, A., Schalk, L., & Schneider, M. (2022). Domain-specific prior knowledge and learning: A meta-analysis. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2021.1939700

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic integration of absolute-gain correlations from 50 studies (307 effect sizes); Table 2 reports the printed effect size rAG+ = −.263, 95% CI [−.370, −.149], I² = 98.05. The article attributes the lower rAG to high-prior-knowledge learners having less room for improvement on the scale.

> "The correlation between prior knowledge and absolute knowledge gains could be computed for 50 studies with 307 effect sizes ranging from rAGþ ¼ -.954 to rAGþ ¼ .623."
