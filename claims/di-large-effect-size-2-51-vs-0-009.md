## Evidence

### Magableh 2020

Magableh, I. S. I., & Abdullah, A. (2020). On the Effectiveness of Differentiated Instruction in the Enhancement of Jordanian Students' Overall Achievement. International Journal of Instruction, 13(2), 533-548. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13237a

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Cohen's d computed from the same quasi-experimental study's pre/post data; the article reports the experimental group's effect size as "2.5132" and the control group's as "0.009295", the latter below the 0.2 small-effect threshold the article cites.

> "the effect size of the one- size-fits-all method on the control group was 0.009295 which was even below 0.2 indicating that it had a very limited effect size on students' learning. However, the effect size of the experimental group which was taught following differentiated instruction was 2.5132"
