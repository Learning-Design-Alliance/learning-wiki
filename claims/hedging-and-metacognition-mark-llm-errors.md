LIWC Insight analysis of LLM reasoning for correct vs. incorrect annotations. Metacognitive-dense reasoning was "overwhelmingly more likely to be incorrect"; the authors call this failure mode performative reasoning, where the model narrates its own cognitive state instead of deriving an answer.

> "Insight was a significantnegativesignal (p <0.001). Reasoning that contained high densities of metacognitive terms like " I think," " I realize," and " understand" were overwhelmingly more likely to be incorrect."
