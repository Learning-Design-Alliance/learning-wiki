## Evidence

### Li Tingting 2022

Li Tingting, Wang Weidong, Li Zhijie, Wang Hongmiao, Liu Xiaodan. (2022). Problem-based or lecture-based learning, old topic in the new field: a meta-analysis on the effects of PBL teaching method in Chinese standardized residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03254-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of skill scores by department (Table 2): the diagnostic imaging subgroup (2 studies, 48 intervention and 48 control residents) had WMD = 1.30, 95% CI: -0.11–2.71, the only subgroup not reaching significance; "Only diagnostic imaging did not display a statistical significance in SS".

> "Only diagnostic imaging did not display a statistical significance in SS, while the data from the other subgroups differed significantly compared to the LBL group"
