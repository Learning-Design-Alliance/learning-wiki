Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023¶

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Linear regression of success on final-block consistency in the RB task with 2D-Simple as baseline; the article reports only unstandardized regression coefficients (βconsistency = 0.83, Exp 1), so no standardized effect size is printed. The 1D-Temporal slope was significantly weaker (Exp 1: β = −0.61, p < 0.0001; Exp 2: β = −0.56, p = 0.0075).

"the more consistently participants used a 2D-Simple strategy, the more successful they were (Experiment 1: βconsistency = 0.83, SE = 0.072, p < 0.0001; Experiment 2: βconsistency = 0.64, SE = 0.12, p < 0.0001)"

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Linear regression in the II task with 2D-Complex as baseline: consistent 2D-Complex use predicted success in both experiments (unstandardized β only; no standardized effect size printed). Consistent 2D-Simple use was unrelated to success and significantly weaker (Exp 1: β = −0.92, p = 0.017; Exp 2: β = −0.54, p = 0.0064).

"the more consistently participants used a 2D-Complex strategy, the more successful they were (Experiment 1: βconsistency = 0.82, SE = 0.19, p < 0.0001; Experiment 2: βconsistency = 0.79, SE = 0.15, p < 0.0001)"

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023 (3)¶

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

ANOVA on II-task success by final-block strategy with printed effect size ηG² = 0.34 in Experiment 1 and ηG² = 0.19 in Experiment 2; Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc tests showed 2D-Complex and 1D-Temporal users had significantly higher accuracy than 2D-Simple users. In contrast, in the RB task success did not significantly differ between 2D-Simple and 1D-Temporal users.

"there were signi ﬁcant differences in learning success based on learners ’ strategies (Experiment 1: F(2, 73) = 18.8, p < 0.0001, ηG2 = 0.34; Experiment 2: F(2, 81) = 9.50, p < 0.0001, ηG2 = 0.19)"
