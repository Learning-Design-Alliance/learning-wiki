Item-oriented interpretation of the ASES backbone network via weighted degree centrality. The article reports that X01 and X03, previously not loaded into any PCA component, "play important roles in the network structure" and should be reconsidered for inclusion in survey distribution.

> "In contrast, items which were previously not loaded into any PCA component, such as X01 and X03, play important roles in the network structure, as confirmed by their centrality measure and placement in the network."
