## Evidence

### Zhou Haixia 2022

Zhou Haixia, Xian Chengyao, Zhang Kai-Jun, Yang Zhouwen, Li Wei, Tian Jing. (2022). The frequency of assessment tools in arthroscopic training: a systematic review. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2022.2085317

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Context analysis (Table 4) of the 28 included studies across simulator, cadaver, animal model and patient settings. The review reports the frequency split between objective metrics and subjective tools by testing context.

> "In simulated environments, objective metrics were more frequently used than subjective tools. Among the objective indicators in clinical environments, only completion times and hand movements were reported [38,46,47], but all studies related to patients used GRS or checklists."
