# Prior VR training significantly speeds femoral nailing synthetic-bone performance in both cohorts (about 7 minutes faster, P<.01; about 4 minutes faster, P=.05)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In cohort 1, participants who trained on the VR module before the femoral synthetic-bone exercise were on average approximately 7 minutes faster (P<.01). [→ Sandipan Chatterjee 2026](#sandipan-chatterjee-2026)
`q3 i?` In cohort 2, participants were on average approximately 4 minutes faster on the femoral synthetic-bone exercise after VR training (P=.05). [→ Sandipan Chatterjee 2026 (2)](#sandipan-chatterjee-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover comparison in cohort 1 (AO Trauma Basic Principles course): femoral synthetic-bone completion time with prior VR practice (mean 20.71 min) versus without (mean 27.55 min), P<.01 (Table 3).

> "Participants from cohort 1 who trained on the VR module prior to performing the synthetic bone practical exercise were, on average, approximately 7 minutes faster in completing the femoral nailing exercise (P<.01; Table 3)."

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026 (2)

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover comparison in cohort 2 (AO Trauma Advanced Principles course): femoral synthetic-bone completion time after VR training (mean 16.51 min) versus without (mean 20.73 min), P=.05; VR module times did not differ significantly (P=.54).

> "Similarly, in cohort 2, participants were on average approximately 4 minutes faster in completing the synthetic bone practical exercise after training on the VR module (P=.05; Table 3), and no significant difference in the VR module completion time was noted (P=.54; Table 3)."
