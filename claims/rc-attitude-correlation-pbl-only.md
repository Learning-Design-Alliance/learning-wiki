## Evidence

### Lin 2017

Lin, L.-F. (2017). Impacts of the Problem-based Learning Pedagogy on English Learners’ Reading Comprehension, Strategy Use, and Active Learning Attitudes. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2320

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 7) for each treatment group. The PBL group's r = .773 was significant at the .01 level; the non-PBL group's r = .064 was not significant. The correlation is reported, not manipulated, so it is not causal.
