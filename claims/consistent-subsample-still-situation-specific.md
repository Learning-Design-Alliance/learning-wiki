## Evidence

### Cutrona 1985

Cutrona, C. E., Russell, D., & Jones, R. D. (1985). Cross-Situational Consistency in Causal Attributions: Does Attributional Style Exist? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 47(5), 1043-1058. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.47.5.1043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 subsample analysis: 337 subjects (29.6% of the sample) with the lowest variance in attributions across three ASQ events were selected, and a three-factor model was fit to their remaining three events. The model fit poorly (A = .531), and substantial situational specificity remained.

> "This model was not found to provide a very good fit to the data, x2 (24, N = 337) = 117.41, p \< .001, A = .531."
