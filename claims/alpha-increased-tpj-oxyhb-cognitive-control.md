## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

fNIRS monitoring (44 channels) during the BIAT in the quasi-experiment; interaction effects were significant in nine temporoparietal junction channels with η2 between 0.09 and 0.16, and "haemodynamic responses in the temporoparietal junction regions were enhanced after the intervention (p < 0.05)", while in the control group responses did not differ between pre- and post-test.
