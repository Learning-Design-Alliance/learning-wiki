Evidence¶

Kostopoulos 2025¶

Kostopoulos, G., Gkamas, V., Rigou, M., & Kotsiantis, S. (2025). Agentic AI in Education: State of the Art and Future Directions. IEEE Access. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3620473

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Darvishi et al., a case study with 12 educators in which agentic assessors using analytic rubrics and generative feedback achieved "acceptable rater agreement (Cohen's 0.78) between AI and human scoring". Cohen's 0.78 is an agreement statistic, not an effect size.

"Darvishi et al. [33] (case study, N = 12 educators) found acceptable rater agreement (Cohen's 0.78) between AI and human scoring."

Kostopoulos 2025 (2)¶

Kostopoulos, G., Gkamas, V., Rigou, M., & Kotsiantis, S. (2025). Agentic AI in Education: State of the Art and Future Directions. IEEE Access. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3620473

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review reports, citing Kaldaras et al., a controlled classroom deployment with 45 learners in which AI-generated feedback received "comparable feedback helpfulness ratings to human graders". The review characterizes such systems as emerging reliability at mid-TRL ≈ 4.

"Kaldaras et al. [34] (controlled classroom deployment, N= 45 learners) showed comparable feedback helpfulness ratings to human graders."
