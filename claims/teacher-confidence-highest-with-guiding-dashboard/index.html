Evidence¶

Reet Kasepalu 2023¶

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of confidence ratings collected with each adapted SAGAT statement (scale 1 just guessing to 4 very confident) across the three conditions; "the teachers feel the most confident in the alerting & guiding dashboard condition", with the authors noting a possible ceiling effect. No test statistic printed.

"In Fig. 7 we can see that the teachers feel the most confident in the alerting & guiding dashboard condition, but also that they are generally quite confident (we may be witnessing a 'ceiling effect')."
