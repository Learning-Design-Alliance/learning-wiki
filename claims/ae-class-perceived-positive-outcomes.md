### Dysterheft 2016

Dysterheft, J., Chaparro, G., Rice, L., & Rice, I. (2016). Investigating the Outcomes and Perceptions of an Inclusive Aquatic Exercise Class for University Students with Physical Disabilities. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 31(1), 41-56. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Investigating+the+Outcomes+and+Perceptions+of+an+Inclusive+Aquatic+Exercise+Class

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of post-intervention interviews with the six intervention participants, coded by three researchers with an independent auditor. The analysis "resulted in three primary themes and eight subthemes based on 19 codes"; all participants reported physical and psychological benefits, and four of six reported peer benefits.
