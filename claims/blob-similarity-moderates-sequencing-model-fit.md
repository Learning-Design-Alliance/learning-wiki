# In blob-figure learning, category similarity moderates sequencing effects, and the a-AFM+ppe model best captured the reversed posttest pattern (r2 = 0.73)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For low-similarity new items participants generalized better for blocked items, while for high-similarity stimuli they performed better on interleaved items; adding attentional factors and ppe improved high-similarity posttest prediction most. [→ Meng Cao 2024](#meng-cao-2024)

## Evidence

### Meng Cao 2024

Meng Cao, Wei Chu, Philip I. Pavlik Jr., Liang Zhang. (2024). Integrating Attentional Factors and Spacing in Logistic Knowledge Tracing Models to Explore the Impact of Training Sequences on Category Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.15020](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.15020)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Replication analysis on Carvalho and Goldstone's blob figures dataset (40,320 observations from 60 undergraduates) via student-stratified cross-validation; Table 5 shows a-AFM+ppe r2 = 0.73, and the article reports "the model integrating both attentional factors and ppe showed the most improvement in posttest predictions".

> "for high-similarity stimuli, participants exhib- ited a reversed pattern, showing improved performance on interleaved items, contrary to the learning session"

### Meng Cao 2024 (2)

Meng Cao, Wei Chu, Philip I. Pavlik Jr., Liang Zhang. (2024). Integrating Attentional Factors and Spacing in Logistic Knowledge Tracing Models to Explore the Impact of Training Sequences on Category Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.15020](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.15020)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest patterns differed by stimulus similarity: "In instances of low similarity new items , participants demonstrated enhanced generalization for blocked items, mirroring the learning session", while the basic AFM predicted low similarity relatively accurately but faltered for high similarity stimuli (r3 = 0.36).

> "In instances of low similarity new items , participants demonstrated enhanced generalization for blocked items, mirroring the learning session"
