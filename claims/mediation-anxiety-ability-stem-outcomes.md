## Evidence

### Daker 2021

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Sokolowski, H.M., Green, A.E., & Lyons, I.M. (2021). First-year students' math anxiety predicts STEM avoidance and underperformance throughout university, independently of math ability. npj Science of Learning 6:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00095-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (10,000 iterations, mediation package in R) with the same covariates as the primary regressions; the printed proportion mediated was "%C = .126" for % STEM Courses and %C = .115 for STEM Grades (p = .100, non-significant).

> "only 12.6% of the association between Math Anxiety and % STEM Courses could be specifically attributed to Math Ability (%C = .126, CI 95 of %C: .005, .308, p = .040)"

### Daker 2021 (2)

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Sokolowski, H.M., Green, A.E., & Lyons, I.M. (2021). First-year students' math anxiety predicts STEM avoidance and underperformance throughout university, independently of math ability. npj Science of Learning 6:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00095-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reverse-direction mediation models on the same sample: Math Anxiety mediated 40.9% of the Math Ability–% STEM Courses association and 40.0% of the Math Ability–STEM Grades association (%C = .400, p = .010); both indirect effects were significant.

> "40.9% of the association between Math Ability and % STEM Courses could be specifically attributed to Math Anxiety (%C = .409, CI 95 of %C: .172, .950; p = .001)"
