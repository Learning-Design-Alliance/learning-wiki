## Evidence

### Sabrina Ludwig 2026

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Log file analysis of mouse clicks and keystrokes during the problem-solving scenario, deriving behavioural indicators such as note-taking and document reading. Reference book use showed "d = .83" (M = 4.51 for PS-DI vs 1.3 for DI-PS); bi-gram sequence analysis yielded no additional significance.
