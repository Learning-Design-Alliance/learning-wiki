Null result from the crossover trial's time outcome: "There were no significant differences in the task completion time between the various feedback modalities" in either task; subgroup analyses were also non-significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant differences in the task completion time between the various feedback modalities for either the peg transfer task or the laparoscopic suture and knot task."
