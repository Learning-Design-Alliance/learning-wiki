Interview thematic analysis of five graduate students. Student quotes ranged from Andrew's report that professors always do "checking to make sure that people are paying attention" to Ashley's account of questions that "never gets answered," showing mixed responsiveness.

> "These three quotes show the variety of instructional quality that these students have experienced in their collective time spent in graduate coursework. Some professors are actively looking for student confusion and attempting to address it, while others are not putting as much effort into informal formative assessment during their lectures."
