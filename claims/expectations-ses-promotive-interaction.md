## Evidence

### Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L 2023

Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L. (2023). Family and Individual Contexts of Middle-School Years and Educational Achievement of Youths in Middle-Aged Adulthood. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20043279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis within the first structural equation model of LSAY cohort-2 data. The article reports "βint = 0.052, 0.049, and 0.048" for the expectations-by-SES interactions on English, mathematics, and science grade scores.

> "interaction analysis found that only educational expectations of youths significantly interacted with family SES in prediction of youths' grade-9 English, mathematics, and science grade scores, which are in a promotive but not buffering way (Table 3), βint = 0.052, 0.049, and 0.048 p < 0.01 and 0.05."

### Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L 2023 (2)

Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L. (2023). Family and Individual Contexts of Middle-School Years and Educational Achievement of Youths in Middle-Aged Adulthood. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20043279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis within the second structural equation model of LSAY cohort-2 data. The article reports significant positive expectations-by-SES interactions on grade-9 educational performance and adult educational achievement, described as promotive but not buffering.

> "Besides, grade-7 educational expectations of youths significantly interacted with family SES in prediction of youths' higher grade-9 educational performance, βint = 0.003, p < 0.01, and also their educational achievement in adulthood, βint = 0.002, p < 0.01."
