## Evidence

### Jiang 2016

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing 60 cooperative-writing students with 60 solitary-writing students after an 18-week intervention. The post-test anxiety difference was significant (p = 0.001), with the experimental group mean of 84.35 versus 91.97 for comparison.

> "In the post-test period, when CL had been adopted in the experimental group while traditional teaching mode was conducted in the comparison group, the difference of anxiety between the two groups reached a statistical significance (p = 0.001<0.05)."

### Jiang 2016 (2)

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests within each group show the experimental class reduced anxiety significantly (mean difference 10.43, p = 0.005), while the comparison class change of 1.12 was not significant (p = 0.088).

> "Table 3 shows a significant reduction in the experimental group, but not with the comparison group."
