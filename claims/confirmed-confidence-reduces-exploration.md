## Evidence

### Berweger 2023

Berweger, B., Kracke, B., & Dietrich, J. (2023). Preservice Teachers' Epistemic and Achievement Emotions When Confronted With Common Misconceptions About Education. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplementary analysis (Appendix B.4) of the 275-participant sample examining confidence effects on exploration; the coefficient is unstandardized with a 95% BCI, and contrary to Vogl et al. (2020) no effect of high-confidence errors on exploration was found.

> "We found a negative main effect of confidence on knowledge exploration after correct answers (-.172 [-.326, -.019]), suggesting that participants explored less when their expectation of accuracy was confirmed"
