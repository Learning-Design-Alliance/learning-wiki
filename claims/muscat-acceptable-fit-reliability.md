## Evidence

### Sakakida Chika 2021

Sakakida Chika, Tadaka Etsuko, Arimoto Azusa. (2021). Development and validation of a new Multidisciplinary Approach Competency Scale for Prevention of Child Abuse from Pregnancy (MUSCAT). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249623

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis performed on the same cross-sectional survey sample used for the exploratory analysis (n = 229). The article reports the fit indices verbatim: "GFI of 0.963, AGFI of 0.939, CFI of 0.988, and RMSEA of 0.043". The abstract additionally reports total-scale Cronbach's alpha of 0.903 and subscale alphas of 0.840–0.875.

> "The model fit indices showed a GFI of 0.963, AGFI of 0.939, CFI of 0.988, and RMSEA of 0.043, which nearly satisfied the appropriate criteria for all respondents."
