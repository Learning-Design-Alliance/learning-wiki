## Evidence

### Galada 2025

Galada, Aditi and Baytar, Fatma. (2025). Design and evaluation of a problem-based learning VR module for apparel fit correction training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression models predicting FCST post-test score in the same 40-participant study, reported in the Results section. The article found "the gender or interaction of gender with the pre-test FCST score was not statistically significant"; no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "As seen in Fig 6, the regression models for predicting the FCST post-test score indicate that the gender or interaction of gender with the pre-test FCST score was not statistically significant."
