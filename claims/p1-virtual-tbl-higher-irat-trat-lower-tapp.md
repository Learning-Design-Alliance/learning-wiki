## Evidence

### Shoair OA 2024

Shoair OA. (2024). Academic performance among pharmacy students using virtual vs. face-to-face team-based learning. Annals of Medicine 56(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2349205

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted generalized linear model comparison of the P1 cohort (FTF n = 26, virtual n = 42) in PHAR 7203. The article reports iRATs higher in the virtual group (Z = 6.97; p < .001) and that "tAPP scores were significantly lower in the virtual TBL group compared to the FTF TBL group".

> "tAPP scores were significantly lower in the virtual TBL group compared to the FTF TBL group (Z = −4.76; p < .001), after adjusting for students' age, sex, race, and cGPA."

### Shoair OA 2024 (2)

Shoair OA. (2024). Academic performance among pharmacy students using virtual vs. face-to-face team-based learning. Annals of Medicine 56(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2349205

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted GLM results for the P1 cohort show no significant difference in summative exam or total course scores; equivalence was not tested. Table 2 prints medians of 82 vs 81 for exams and 85 vs 84 for total course score, descriptive only.

> "No significant difference was observed in summative exam scores or total course scores between the FTF TBL and virtual TBL groups."
