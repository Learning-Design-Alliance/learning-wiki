## Evidence

### Mandinach 2012

Mandinach, E. B., & Gummer, E. S. (2012). Navigating the Landscape of Data Literacy: It IS Complex. White paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED582807

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative finding from a hands-on activity in which participants produced Venn diagrams on transparency sheets labeled data literacy and assessment literacy. The activity aimed to determine "the extent of overlap between the two constructs as well as unique components of each" and to engage participants in discussing distinctions.

> "The findings also indicated that there are complex interactions between data literacy and assessment literacy, with considerable overlap and at least the potential for differing knowledge and skills across the two forms of literacy."
