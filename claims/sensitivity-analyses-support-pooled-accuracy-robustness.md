Sensitivity analysis within the meta-analysis: leave-one-out influence analysis showed pooled estimates of 64.13%-65.66%, with the most influential study altering results by 1.96% upon exclusion. Trim-and-fill imputed six hypothetical missing studies, adjusting pooled accuracy to 65.43%.
