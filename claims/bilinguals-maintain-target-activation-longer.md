## Evidence

### Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011

Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica. (2011). Bilingualism influences inhibitory control in auditory comprehension. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3582323/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Eye-tracking timecourse analysis in the 60-participant picture-word identification study. "bilinguals maintained the target active longer than monolinguals, t(58) = 1.7, p < .05 (1-tailed)", with bilinguals at 16.7% target fixations versus monolinguals at 14.0% in the late window.

> "bilinguals maintained the target active longer than monolinguals, t(58) = 1.7, p < .05 (1-tailed), as illustrated by more fixations to the target in bilinguals (16.7%, SE = 1.2) than in monolinguals (14.0%, SE = 1.0) between 1099 ms and 2734 ms post-stimulus onset"
