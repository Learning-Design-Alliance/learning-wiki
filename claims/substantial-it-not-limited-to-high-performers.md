`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive analysis of rubric-coded IT depth across the three performance categories of the 14 teams; the article reports averages of 26 substantial connections for high- and low-performing teams versus 18 for mid-performing teams, and concludes "substantial integration was not limited to academically higher-performing teams."

> "When examining the depth of integration, both high- and low-performing teams demonstrated the highest average number of substantial (Level 3) IT connections, with an average of 26 connections. In comparison, mid-performing teams reported a lower average of 18 substantial IT connections."
