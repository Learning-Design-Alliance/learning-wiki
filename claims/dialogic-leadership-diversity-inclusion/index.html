Evidence¶

Shiza Khaqan 2024¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

q2 · i? · review · r2

Inductive thematic analysis across the review's 27 articles; the review reports, citing Shields (2004), that "diversity in this context is an asset to be valued". Children from Roma and Moroccan-Muslim communities led dialogically and developed respect for differences. No effect size is printed.

"Dialogic leadership enables individuals to embrace and appreciate diversity as Shields (2004), in her conceptualization of leadership through dialogue, asserts that diversity in this context is an asset to be valued."
