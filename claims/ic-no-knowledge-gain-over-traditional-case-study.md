## Evidence

### Jourdi Georges 2025

Jourdi Georges, Selmi Mayssa, Gaussem Pascale, Truchot Jennifer, Margaill Isabelle, Siguret Virginie. (2025). Evaluation of the Inverted Classroom Approach in a Case-Study Course on Antithrombotic Drug Use in a PharmD Curriculum: French Monocentric Randomized Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67419

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-MCQ pretest at the start of the in-class session in the randomized study; control mean 1.17 (SD 0.66) versus IC 1.24 (SD 0.72) out of 5, with no difference between groups.

> "The mean and SD of the prescore (out of 5) did not differ between the control group (1.17, SD 0.66) and the IC group (1.24, SD 0.72; Figure 2)."

### Jourdi Georges 2025 (2)

Jourdi Georges, Selmi Mayssa, Gaussem Pascale, Truchot Jennifer, Margaill Isabelle, Siguret Virginie. (2025). Evaluation of the Inverted Classroom Approach in a Case-Study Course on Antithrombotic Drug Use in a PharmD Curriculum: French Monocentric Randomized Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67419

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Immediate posttest with the same 5 MCQs after the case-study course; both groups improved significantly (P<.001, Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test), and "Knowledge improvement was comparable in students from both groups".

> "The mean score improved from the prescore (P<.001), in both the control group 2.45 (SD 0.61) and the IC group 2.35 (SD 0.73; Figure 2). Knowledge improvement was comparable in students from both groups."
