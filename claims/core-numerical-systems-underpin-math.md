## Evidence

### Elsbeth Stern 2017

Elsbeth Stern (2017). Individual differences in the learning potential of human beings. npj Science of Learning 2:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0003-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports preferential-looking and infancy findings on core knowledge, quoting the ratio-dependence of approximate magnitude discrimination at 6 months and, separately, precise tracking of sets up to three elements. No effect size is printed.

> "As early as at 6 months of age, infants show an ability for the approximate representations of numerical magnitude, which allow them to discriminate two magnitudes depending on their ratio."

### Elsbeth Stern 2017 (2)

Elsbeth Stern (2017). Individual differences in the learning potential of human beings. npj Science of Learning 2:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0003-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's account of how core knowledge meets academic learning states that "Mathematical competencies emerge as a result of combining both core systems" with cultural number words. No effect size is printed.

> "Mathematical competencies emerge as a result of combining both core systems and linking them to number words provided by the respective culture."
