## Evidence

### Wolff 2011

Ulrika Wolff. (2011). Effects of a Randomised Reading Intervention Study: An Application of Structural Equation Modelling. Dyslexia 17: 295–311. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.438

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-lagged testing within the SEM of the randomised trial; only these cross-lagged paths were significant. The article concludes phonological awareness appears to underpin spelling and reading comprehension one year later, even at older ages.

> "Testing for cross-lagged relations over time showed a significant relation from phonological awareness at post-test to spelling and reading comprehension at follow-up, and from reading comprehension at post-test to spelling at follow-up. No other cross-lagged relations were found."
