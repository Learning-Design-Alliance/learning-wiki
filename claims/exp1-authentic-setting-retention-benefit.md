Merkt, M., Lux, S., Hoogerheide, V., van Gog, T., & Schwan, S. (2020). A Change of Scenery: Does the Setting of an Instructional Video Affect Learning? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000414

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Experiment 1 compared learning from a video about floral diagrams and floral formulas shot in an authentic (greenhouse) versus a neutral (white wall) setting. The article reports "a beneficial effect of the authentic setting on retention, but not on the application of the video content."
