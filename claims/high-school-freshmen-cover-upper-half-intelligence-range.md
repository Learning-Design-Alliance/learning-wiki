### Cobb 1922

Cobb, Margaret V. (1922). The Limits Set to Educational Achievement by Limited Intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 8. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=The+Limits+Set+to+Educational+Achievement+by+Limited+Intelligence

Section II reports Army Alpha distributions of high school freshmen in Michigan and in Illinois, Wisconsin and Iowa (Tables IV-VII, Figure 1). The text notes a freshman "may score anything from 35" to 185, and that fewer than 7.5 per cent lack median intelligence or better.

> "The pupils in academic high schools are, in fact, a limited group, which just about covers the upper half of the whole range of American intelligence."
