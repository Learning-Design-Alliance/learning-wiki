### Heppt 2025

Heppt, Birgit; Schwarzenthal, Miriam; Scharf, Jan. (2025). Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02147-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression (Model 1a, Table 2) showed a significant negative classroom-level aggregate effect of discriminatory climate on reading comprehension (β = -0.14, p = 0.028) beyond the student-level effect, with classroom-level R2 = 0.78 and ICC = 0.35.

> "students from classrooms who perceived a more discriminatory climate performed more poorly on the reading comprehension test than did students from classrooms who perceived a less discriminatory climate, even when considering students' individual perceptions of discriminatory climate along with important control variables at the student and classroom levels"
