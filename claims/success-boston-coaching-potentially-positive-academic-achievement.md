### What Works Clearinghouse 2020

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020). Success Boston Coaching (WWC 2021-001). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/708

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

WWC synthesis of cumulative grade point average outcomes from two QED studies totaling 5,543 students, with an outcome average improvement index of +4; the 2019 study's GPA effect was not statistically significant (p = 0.82). No effect size is printed in the quoted finding sentence.

> "The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards with reservations showed evidence of a positive and statistically significant effect of Success Boston Coaching on academic achievement (Linkow et al., 2017), and one study that meets WWC group design standards with reservations showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Success Boston Coaching on academic achievement (Linkow et al., 2019)."
