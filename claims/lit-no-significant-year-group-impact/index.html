Evidence¶

Crawford and Skipp 2014¶

Claire Crawford, Amy Skipp. (2014). LIT Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581139

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial comparing all Year 7 pupils in 19 treatment schools with 15 control schools (2,889 vs 1,524 pupils with useable data). The effect size of 0.09 (95% CI -0.04, 0.22) was not significant, equivalent to +1 month's progress. The evaluation reports "statistical tests indicate that this estimate is not ‘significant’".

"the reading scores of Year 7 pupils in schools running the LIT programme rose 0.09 standard deviations more over the course of the year than those of similar Year 7 pupils in control schools. This effect size of 0.09 is equivalent to 1 month’s additional progress in reading. However, statistical tests indicate that this estimate is not ‘significant’"
