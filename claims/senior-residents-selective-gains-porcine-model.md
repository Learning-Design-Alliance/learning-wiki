## Evidence

### Huang Yue 2024

Huang Yue, Jia Jin, Zhan Jun, Li Bo, Gu Qiong, Li Zhengyu. (2024). Porcine stomach surgical simulation model for cesarean section and cervical laceration suturing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05936-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post questionnaire ratings from "Ten senior OB/GYN residents (fourth- to fifth-year)" in the same skill course. Cervical laceration understanding rose from median 3.3 to 7.5 and confidence from 2.6 to 6.6 (P < 0.01); uterus suturing ratings were unchanged (P = 0.168 and 0.104). No effect size printed.

> "For senior OB/GYN residents, their understanding and confidence of performing uterus suturing in cesarean section were not significantly improved (P > 0.05), while their understanding and confidence of performing cervical laceration suturing were significantly improved (P < 0.01)."
