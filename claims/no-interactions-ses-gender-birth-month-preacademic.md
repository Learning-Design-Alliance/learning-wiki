## Evidence

### Rémeau 2026

Rémeau, M., Schmitt, P., & Borst, G. (2026). Executive functions as mediators of early educational disparities by SES, gender and birth month. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00422-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression model including two-way and three-way interactions, run on the same 2,618-child national sample. All four interaction terms were non-significant; equivalence was not tested. Main effects remained significant (all ps<.001) when interactions were added.

> "we found no interaction between family SES and gender ( β =.08, p =.62), family SES and birth month (β =.05, p =.75), gender and birth month (β =.02, p =.84) or between family SES, gender and birth month (β =-.11, p =.49) on pre-academic scores."
