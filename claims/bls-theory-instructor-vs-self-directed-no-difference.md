# Theoretical BLS knowledge outcomes do not differ between instructor-led sessions and self-directed computer CD/DVD learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants showed no differences in acquired knowledge between those receiving instructor-led theoretical sessions and those learning independently via computer CDs or DVDs. [→ García-Suárez 2019](#garcía-suárez-2019)

## Evidence

### García-Suárez 2019

García-Suárez, M., Méndez-Martínez, C., Martínez-Isasi, S., Gómez-Salgado, J., & Fernández-García, D. (2019). Basic Life Support Training Methods for Health Science Students: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16050768

`q2 · i?`

The review's conclusions section synthesises knowledge outcomes across the included trials, reporting no differences between instructor-led theory and self-directed CD/DVD learning; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "With regard to the knowledge acquired, participants did not show differences between those who received a theoretical session with an instructor and participants who acquired knowledge independently through computer CDs or DVDs."
