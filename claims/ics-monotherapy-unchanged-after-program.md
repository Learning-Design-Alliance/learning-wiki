`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of therapeutic indicator 11 in the Sicilian GP cohort; Table 4 shows 9.6% baseline vs. 9.9% at 24 months with non-significant p-values (0.923 and 0.478), and higher than HSD (7.7%). No effect on this indicator was detected.

> "The proportion of COPD patients treated with ICS in monotherapy (indicator 11) was almost stable during the study period and it was higher than what reported in HSD."
