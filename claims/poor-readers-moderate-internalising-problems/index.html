Evidence¶

Francis 2019¶

Francis, D. A.; Caruana, N.; Hudson, J. L.; McArthur, G. M. (2019). The association between poor reading and internalising problems: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Clinical Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2018.09.002

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 14 studies comparing poor reading and control groups on general internalising measures (n poor reading groups = 14, sample size = 1147). The pooled effect was "positive, moderate, and statistically significant (d = 0.41)" with low heterogeneity (I2 = 35.07%).

"The overall effect was positive, moderate, and statistically significant (d = 0.41), 95% CI [0.31, 0.51], p < 0.001, and low heterogeneity was also present I2 = 35.07%, Q (13) = 20.02, p > 0.05."
