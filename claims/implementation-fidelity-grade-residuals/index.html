Barbara Means 2026¶

Barbara Means, Julie Neisler, Daniel Parker, Vanessa Peters Hinton, and Emily Pressler. (2026). Lessons From Implementing Courseware in Gateway Statistics Courses. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/12bb84f5-0073-40e6-85bb-41561222891b

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 2024-25 classes assessed against seven fidelity criteria developed with Lumen Learning. Students of instructors meeting "four or more criteria" had "grades about 0.24 points higher than expected"; those meeting only two criteria had grades "about 0.34 grade points lower."

"Students of instructors who met four or more criteria for implementation fidelity had an unadjusted course pass rate of 88% and course-level grades about 0.24 points higher than expected based on the prior achievement."
