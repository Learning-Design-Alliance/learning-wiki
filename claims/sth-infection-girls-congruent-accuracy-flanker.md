### Gerber Markus 2021

Gerber Markus, Lang Christin, Beckmann Johanna, du Randt Rosa, Gall Stefanie, Seelig Harald, Long Kurt Z., Ludyga Sebastian, Müller Ivan, Nienaber Madeleine, Nqweniso Siphesihle, Pühse Uwe, Steinmann Peter, Utzinger Jürg, Walter Cheryl. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-10779-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mixed linear regression for girls (n = 613); STH infection estimate for congruent accuracy was −0.17, p = 0.006 (p = 0.047 after chance-level exclusion). The article reports infected girls "had lower accuracy scores in response to congruent stimuli than non-infected girls."

> "Finally, girls who were infected with STH had lower accuracy scores in response to congruent stimuli than non-infected girls. However, no significant differences were found in the other Flanker task domains."
