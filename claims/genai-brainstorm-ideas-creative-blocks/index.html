Evidence¶

Luo 2025¶

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 13 instructional designers (RQ3). Seven participants "indicated they use GenAI to brainstorm ideas and draft content," finding it helpful for generating ideas, providing a starting point when stuck, and reducing cognitive load.

"Participants (N = 7) indicated they use GenAI to brainstorm ideas and draft content. IDs utilized it to assist with idea generation, overcoming creative blocks, and facilitating tasks related to writing, editing, and content creation."
