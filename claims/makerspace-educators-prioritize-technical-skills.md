### Turakhia 2024

Turakhia, D., Ludgin, D., Mueller, S., & Desportes, K. (2024). Understanding the educators' practices in makerspaces for the design of education tools. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10305-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with seven educators across five makerspaces. The findings section reports technical skills as the most-discussed competency, with educators wanting students 'to know their tools and their materials, and especially know when to use the right tool'.

> "Educators referenced the importance of building technical skills the most out of the set of competencies they discussed."
