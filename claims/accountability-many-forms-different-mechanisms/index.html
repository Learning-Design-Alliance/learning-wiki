The authors' review of the behavioral science literature on accountability, presented as a synthesis in the paper's framing. The paper states the literature "makes clear, first, that accountability comes in many forms that activate different mechanisms."

"That literature makes clear, first, that accountability comes in many forms that activate different mechanisms, and second, that accountability can produce positive or negative effects, depending on the accountability type, the decision context, and the nature of the task."
