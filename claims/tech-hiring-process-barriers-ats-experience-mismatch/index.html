Bria Carter 2024¶

Bria Carter, Britney Jacobs, Zohal Shah, Chioma Aso-Hernandez. (2024). Lift Every Voice in Tech: Co-Designed Recommendations to Support Black Workers and Learners Seeking to Enter and Advance in Technology Industry Career Pathways. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5dcf2bc8-f40c-4942-96a4-c4a5bda965e3 doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/219

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of worker and learner interviews, surveys, and design sessions. Participants described "biasedATS (or Applicant tracking system) disparities, where a resume sent through an ATS may get rejected" while direct recruiter emails succeeded, and mismatches between entry-level labels and experience requirements.

"Participants also talked about how hiring processes can be extremely lengthy, sometimesinvolving four to five interviews with no guarantee of getting a job."
