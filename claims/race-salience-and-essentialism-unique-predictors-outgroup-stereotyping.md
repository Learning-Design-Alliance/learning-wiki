## Evidence

### Pauker 2010

Pauker, K., Ambady, N., & Apfelbaum, E. P. (2010). Race Salience and Essentialist Thinking in Racial Stereotype Development. Child Development, 81(6), 1799–1813. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8624.2010.01511.x

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on out-group stereotyping with 74 subjects (12 did not complete racial constancy measures due to time constraints; 3 had incomplete racial justification answers). The final model was significant, F(9, 64) = 6.67, R2 = .48, p < .001, with "sorting by race (β = .34, p = .003)" among the retained significant predictors alongside both racial constancy measures.

> "As predicted, the factors that remained significant in the final model were those related to race salience and essentialist thinking: sorting by race (β = .34, p = .003), racial constancy for the self (β = −.37, p = .005), and racial constancy for others (β = .49, p < .0001)."
