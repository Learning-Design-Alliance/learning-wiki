## Evidence

### Laurence Viennot 2020

Laurence Viennot. (2020). How to choose which explanation to use with students? Discussing the tensiometer with beginning teachers. International Journal of Science Education. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of seven student teachers near the end of a master's programme; criteria-sheet data (Table 4) on choices for themselves. The author reports participants "unanimously valued for themselves" consistency and relative completeness of Expl. 1 and Expl. 3. No effect size is reported.

> "Table 4 indicates that participants unanimously valued for themselves what might be called the ‘consistency’ of Expl. 1 and Expl. 3—that is, the absence of indirect contradiction of a law—along with their relative completeness, despite the simplicity and mnemonic value of Expl. 2."
