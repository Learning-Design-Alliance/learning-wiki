## Evidence

### Innab Adnan 2026

Innab Adnan, Hakamy Essa, Abdulgafor Nedaa, AlOtaibi Nora Ghalib. (2026). The influence of self-efficacy and satisfaction with e-learning on academic performance among nursing students: a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09236-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study of 163 nursing students using multiple linear regression. The article reports the model "explained 35% of the variance" with both predictors significant at p<.001.

> "The regression analysis explained 35% of the variance and indicated that both general self-efficacy (β = 0.047, p<.001) and satisfaction with e-learning (β = .37, p <.001) were significantly associated with perceived academic performance."
