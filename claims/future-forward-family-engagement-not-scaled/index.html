Jones 2021¶

Jones, C. J., & Li, D. (2021). Testing the Impact and Scalability of the Future Forward Literacy Program. University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. https://uwm.edu/sreed

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Implementation analysis of family contact data through March 2020. The typical student's family was contacted 1.6 times each month, below the two-contacts-per-month implementation expectation.

"Only 30% of students’ families were successfully contacted by FF staff at least two times per month (Table 6; Figure 3). None of the 14 sites met the 80% threshold requirement for full implementation (Figure 4)."
