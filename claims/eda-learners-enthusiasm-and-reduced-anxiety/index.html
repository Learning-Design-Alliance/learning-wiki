Evidence¶

Parisa Abdolrezapour 2013¶

Parisa Abdolrezapour, Mansoor Tavakoli, Saeed Ketabi. (2013). Enhancing learners' emotions in an L2 context through emotionalized dynamic assessment. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 3(2), 213-243. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135884

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative analysis of transcripts and video data from the mediation stage, including Extract 4 in which a learner said she could "have an exam without being anxious anymore" and that "negative emotions will not hamper my performance". This is the authors' observational interpretation, not a tested result.

"The learners in the EDA condition clearly showed their enthusiasm and interest in performing the reading comprehension activities and attempts to find the meaning of other emotional words."
