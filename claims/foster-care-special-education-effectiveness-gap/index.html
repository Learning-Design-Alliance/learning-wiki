q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Expert-interview report section on special education. Experts offered reasoned hypotheses for overrepresentation, including the "misguided perception that special education leads to more services" and financial incentives for group home operators.

"As a group, the experts reasoned that children and youth in foster care are likely overrepresented in special education for a number of reasons: the assessment challenge of making a determination when information from parents and teachers is incomplete or unobtainable;"

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Same special education section: the report states foster children enter special education at higher rates and exit at lower rates than the general K-12 population, with the experts concluding "There is no direct clear evidence that special education works for them."

""Foster children in special education are adrift." They enter at higher rates than the general K-12 population and exit at lower rates, but "we don't yet know why.""
