## Evidence

### Raper Jaron D. MD 2024

Raper Jaron D. MD, Khoury Charles A. MD, Marshall Anderson MD, Smola Robert MD, Pacheco Zachary MD, Morris Jason MD, Zhai Guihua PhD, Berger Stephanie MD, Kraemer Ryan MD, Bloom Andrew D. MD. (2024). Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice Training for Simulated Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation in Resident Education. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11000545/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 55 residents in 10 teams; timed metrics compared by Student t-test or Wilcoxon test on video-extracted times. For example first defibrillation 93.00 vs 150.60 seconds (P=0.25) in Table 2; no effect sizes printed.

> "Prior learner experience between RCDP and IS groups was similar. Times to completion of the first pulse check, chest compression initiation, backboard placement, pad placement, initial rhythm analysis, first defibrillation, epinephrine administration, and antiarrhythmic administration were similar between RCDP and IS groups."
