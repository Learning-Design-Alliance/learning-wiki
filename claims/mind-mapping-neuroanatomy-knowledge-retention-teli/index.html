Evidence¶

Aljamal Husam 2025¶

Aljamal Husam, Alawneh Rama, Derbas Afnan, Edaibes Mohammad, Ahmed Aya, Amer Lama, Alzoubi Hiba, Serhan Hashem Abu Habuser. (2025). Efficacy of mind maps and concept maps in enhancing academic performance among undergraduate medical students in the preclinical stage: a systematic review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10437-4

q3 · i? · review · r3

RCT included in the review: 80 first-year MBBS students, 40 in a 45-minute mind mapping session and 40 in a didactic lecture, learning neuroanatomy. The review reports a significant improvement in knowledge retention in the intervention group with "mean score: 82.4% vs. 69.8%, p < 0.0001"; no effect size printed.

"Teli et al. (Indian Journal of Clinical Anatomy and Physiology 7(2):243–246, 2020) observed a significant improvement in knowledge retention in the intervention group (mean score: 82.4% vs. 69.8%, p < 0.0001)."
