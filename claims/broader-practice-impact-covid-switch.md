`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 5 of the thematic analysis. Physiotherapists felt "well equipped for this COVID situation" and "ahead of the curve", and some taught colleagues about telehealth; 4 physiotherapists commented they had not experienced increased demand for telehealth.
