## Evidence

### Oakley NJ 2020

Oakley NJ, Kneale D, Mann M, Hilliar M, Dayan C, Gregory JW, French R. (2020). Type 1 diabetes mellitus and educational attainment in childhood: a systematic review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033215

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Secondary analysis in Persson et al using those without diabetes as reference, showing graded differences by age at diagnosis. Dahlquist's conditional means (lowest 2.97 for diagnosis before age 2) were not significant between age groups; the review reports the effect as significant only in Persson.

> "Those diagnosed aged 10–15 years were found to have 0.06 lower attainment, those diagnosed aged 5–9 years were found to have 0.07 lower attainment and those diagnosed aged 0–4 years were found to have 0.15 lower attainment."
