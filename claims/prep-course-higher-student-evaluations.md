## Evidence

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparison of end-of-semester student evaluation interpolated medians for first-time physics GTAs, no-intervention group (N = 51, 2011–2012) versus intervention group (N = 69, 2013 or later); the prep-course group scored higher "across the board". No effect sizes printed.

> "We found that GTAs who partici- pated in the GTA prep course receive higher student evalua- tion scores across the board. The differences are statistically signiﬁcant for nine of the evaluation categories, and not sta- tistically signiﬁcant in three categories."
