## Evidence

### Wei 2026

Wei, Y., and Perkins, M. (2026). Generative AI and student collaboration: a scoping review of group work processes, outcomes, and risks. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00217-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's thematic synthesis of team-interaction studies describes anonymity effects, quoting "increased anonymity contributing to a greater sense of comfort and protection and reducing the fear of peer judgment," and frames GenAI as a social agent shaping group interaction.

> "GenAI can help users conceal their identity (Scala et al. 2025), with increased anonymity contributing to a greater sense of comfort and protection and reducing the fear of peer judgment."

### Wei 2026 (2)

Wei, Y., and Perkins, M. (2026). Generative AI and student collaboration: a scoping review of group work processes, outcomes, and risks. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00217-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports concerns from He et al. (2024) and Kiesler et al. (2025) that students risk "missing the opportunities to build a strong social community" and reduced human support; these are stated concerns, not measured outcomes.

> "In the long run, students could be missing the opportunities to build a strong social community, have reduced human support in their learning, or lack the ability to interact in a social situation (Kiesler et al. 2025)."
