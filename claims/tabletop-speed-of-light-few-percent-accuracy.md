Kenichiro Aoki and Takahisa Mitsui. (2007). A small tabletop experiment for a direct measurement of the speed of light. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.3996 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0705.3996](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0705.3996)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Error analysis of the described setup: the dominant error source is the time-interval measurement, with Δτ ≈ 0.2 ns on the 200 MHz oscilloscope used. The article states "the relative error in the velocity of light is 3%" and that precision is "quite satisfactory for our purposes."

> "With L = 1 m and τ = 6 .7 ns, the relative error in the velocity of light is 3%. The error can be reduced by using larger L or a faster oscilloscope."
