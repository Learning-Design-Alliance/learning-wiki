## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second set of hierarchical regressions using observed (log-data) predictors for course marks among 271 undergraduates: "the observed preparation-oriented approach (β = 0.27, p < 0.001) and observed weekly learning time (β = 0.21, p < 0.001) significantly and positively predicted course marks".

> "In model 2, while perceptions of integration were not significant (β = 0.09, p = 0.11), the observed preparation-oriented approach (β = 0.27, p < 0.001) and observed weekly learning time (β = 0.21, p < 0.001) significantly and positively predicted course marks, explaining around 22% of the variance in the course marks."
