Li, X., Li, T., Yan, L., Li, Y., Zhao, L., Raković, M., Molenaar, I., Gašević, D., & Fan, Y. (2025). FLoRA: An Advanced AI-Powered Engine to Facilitate Hybrid Human-AI Regulated Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07362

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Epistemic network analysis examined the impact of different feedback tools on learners' writing outcomes in the ESL writing study, finding that the design of formative writing analytics tools significantly influenced learners' self-assessment processes.

> "The results demonstrated that tools offering high-affordance feedback, such as clear grammatical corrections, supported accurate self-assessment andsubstantiveimprovementinwriting.Conversely,low-affordancetools,includingthoseintegrating Bloom's taxonomy classifications, often hindered effective self-reflection and revision due to lack of actionable guidance or potential for confusion"
