Evidence¶

Dietrichson 2021¶

Dietrichson, J., Filges, T., Seerup, J. K., Klokker, R. H., Viinholt, B. C. A., Bøg, M., & Eiberg, M. (2021). Targeted school‐based interventions for improving reading and mathematics for students with or at risk of academic difficulties in Grades K‐6: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1152

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis with robust-variance estimation of 205 included studies (202 intervention-control, 3 comparison designs) totalling 226,745 student observations; the review prints the pooled short-term estimate as "ES = 0.30, 95% confidence interval [CI] = [0.25, 0.34]".

"The overall average effect sizes (ES) for short‐term and follow‐up outcomes were positive and statistically significant (ES = 0.30, 95% confidence interval [CI] = [0.25, 0.34] and ES = 0.27, 95% CI = [0.17, 0.36]), respectively)."

Related Claims¶

  • Peer-assisted instruction and small-group instruction by adults have large, robust short-term effect sizes for K-6 struggling students — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Follow-up effects of targeted K-6 interventions remain positive up to 2 years after intervention ends, with evidence of fadeout — related
  • Targeted school-based interventions for Grades 7–12 students with or at risk of academic difficulties have positive, statistically significant short-run effects on standardised reading and maths tests (ES = 0.22) — related
  • Effect sizes across the included studies were highly heterogeneous (I2 = 97.30%), requiring a random effects model — related
  • Effect sizes based on mathematics tests are significantly larger than effect sizes based on reading tests in targeted secondary interventions — related
  • Interventions in higher Grades tend to have somewhat lower effect sizes; other study characteristics show few significant moderator effects — related
  • Substantial unexplained heterogeneity characterises short-term effects of targeted K-6 interventions — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The 2018 review reported a larger pooled effect (SMD = 0.30) that was not sustained at longer-term follow-up — related
  • Longer-run effects of targeted secondary interventions are small and not statistically significant, with only seven studies providing follow-up evidence — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Intensive mathematics interventions for K-3 students with or at risk for MD yield an overall pooled mean effect size of β = 0.61 with substantial heterogeneity — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Tailored small-group mathematics instruction significantly improves math outcomes for low achievers in grades 2 and 8 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Mathematics interventions for students with mathematics difficulty in grades 4–12 show a statistically significant pooled treatment effect of 0.85 under a random-effects model — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Summer math programs have a small significant positive overall effect on students' math scores (d=0.08). — related
  • School feeding programs show mixed, statistically nonsignificant pooled effects on academic performance (1.314, CI crossing null) — related
