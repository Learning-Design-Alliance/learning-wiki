In the same sex-stratified RI-CLPM, all four girls' cross-lagged paths had confidence intervals including zero. No effect sizes were printed for these non-significant findings, so equivalence was not tested.

> "For girls, no cross-lagged effects were significant. Video game playing did not predict subsequent academic motivation from ages 7 to 8 years (β = .012, 95% CI = −.091 to .115), and from ages 8 to 10 years (β = .009, 95% CI = −.069 to .087)."
