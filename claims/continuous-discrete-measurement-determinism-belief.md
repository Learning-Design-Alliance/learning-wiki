## Evidence

### Singh 2016

Singh, C. (2016). Student Difficulties with Quantum Mechanics Formalism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence from the think-aloud protocol: one student correctly stated that measurement collapses the system into an eigenstate, but then claimed continuous-spectrum observables "produce “somewhat” deterministic outcomes" over short times, unlike discrete ones such as spin.

> "students often believed that successive measurements of continuous variables, e.g., position, produce “somewhat” deterministic outcomes whereas successive measurements of discrete variables,e.g., spin, can produce very different outcomes."
