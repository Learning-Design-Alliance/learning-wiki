## Evidence

### Selman L 2016

Selman L, Robinson V, Klass L, Khan S, George R, Shepherd K, Burman R, Koffman J. (2016). Improving confidence and competence of healthcare professionals in end-of-life care: an evaluation of the 'Transforming End of Life Care' course at an acute hospital trust. BMJ Support Palliat Care. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjspcare-2015-000879

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic content analysis of postcourse free-text intentions (n=236 asked): participants named what they were "more likely to do" and less likely to do, with communication and general EoLC quality dominant themes.

> "The most frequent themes were 'Provide better EoLC generally' (occurring 94 times), 'Communicate better with patients and families' (57 times) and 'Use AMBER/Liverpool Care Pathway properly' (52 times)"
