`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression (n = 133) adjusting for age, gender, experience, sector, and consultations; risk assessment model R² = 0.132. Testing (β = -0.951, p = 0.0024) and attitude (β = -0.281, p = 0.0319) showed the same pattern.

> "Physicians who had not diagnosed or treated any STI patients within the preceding six months demonstrated significantly lower risk assessment scores compared to those who had managed 11 or more cases (β = -0.888, 95% CI: -1.472 to -0.304, p = 0.0031)."
