### Toronyi 2020

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11.

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-synthesis finding across six studies; students' L1 served as a rich background illustrating literacy levels crucial for teachers working with ELLs in content area classes.

> "Six of the studies pressed the necessity of acknowledging ELLs’ first language (L1) as a springboard for advancing their L2 acquisition"

### Toronyi 2020 (2)

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11.

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of Ramirez et al. (2016) and Silva and Kucer (2016), echoing Cummins's (2008) BICS/CALP distinction; PSTs learned academic language proficiency develops over a longer period.

> "PSTs in study conducted by Ramirez et al. (2016) not only realized that CALP takes much longer to develop, but were willing to tap into ELLs’ lived experiences and L1 to motivate students to embrace the challenges of learning academic vocabulary"
