### Leopold D.R. 2024

Leopold D.R., Kim H., Carlson K.W., Rowe M.A., Groff B.R., Major M.P., Willcutt E.G., Cutting L.E., Banich M.T. (2024). Stimulus shapes strategy: Effects of stimulus characteristics and individual differences in academic achievement on the neural mechanisms engaged during the N-back task. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2024.101372

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Whole-brain and executive-mask control analyses in the same 126-youth fMRI sample tested whether accuracy differences across stimulus types drove activation effects. The study reports "neither of these control analyses identified any regions" (all clusters' p>.30). No effect size printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "As neither of these control analyses identified any regions whose activity was associated with task accuracy (i.e., all clusters’ p>.30), we do not discuss these analyses in the Results section below."
