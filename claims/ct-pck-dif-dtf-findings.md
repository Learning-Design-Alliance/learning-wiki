# The overall CT–PCK Survey functioned similarly across teacher characteristic groups, though some items showed raw differential item functioning above 0.64 logits

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Differential test functioning showed similar overall test difficulty for teachers of similar ability across characteristics, and no item showed statistically significant DIF after multiple-comparison correction; however, several items showed raw difficulty differences greater than 0.64 logits. ## Evidence

### Witherspoon 2026

Witherspoon, E.B.; Dracopoli, M., Hawkins, M., Dahlke, K. (2026). Developing a measure of middle school math teachers' pedagogical content knowledge of computational thinking (REL 2026–007). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/developing-measure-middle-school-math-teachers-pedagogical-content-knowledge-computational-thinking

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Differential item and test functioning analyses across seven teacher characteristics (tables A12–A15). After correction for multiple comparisons, "no statistically significant differences in the probability of a correct response on any individual item" were found, but raw differences exceeded ETS moderate-to-large DIF thresholds for several items.

> "several items did show raw differences in item difficulty by teacher characteristics that were greater than 0.64 logits, which is above Educational Testing Service thresholds indicating moderate to large differential item functioning"
