### John Self 2015

John Self. (2015). The Birth of IJAIED. Int J Artif Intell Educ (2016) 26:4–12. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0040-5

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

This is Gordon McCalla's invited postscript recollection of the field's history. He describes the 1987 Pittsburgh conference — the first in North America, with only abstracts reviewed and no formal proceedings — as a coming together of a community that "up until then didn't really know it existed", after which the Amsterdam 1989 AIED and the 1988 ITS conferences imposed the more rigorous standards he lists.

> "Both these conferences had a goal of imposing more rigorous computer science standards, with a more serious attempt to widely publicize the conference, to require full papers with archival proceedings, and to institute a thorough peer review process under the auspices of an international program committee."
