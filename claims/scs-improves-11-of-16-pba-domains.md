## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of the 16 ISCP PBA domains assessed, 11 showed statistically significant greater improvement in the intervention group than in the control group. [→ Cragg J 2021](#cragg-j-2021)

## Evidence

### Cragg J 2021

Cragg J, Mushtaq F, Lal N, Garnham A, Hallissey M, Graham T, Shiralkar U. (2021). Surgical cognitive simulation improves real-world surgical performance: randomized study. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrab003

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial comparing mean change scores across 16 ISCP PBA domains between seven intervention and seven control trainees, with Wilcoxon rank-sum for non-normal domains and false discovery rate correction at 0.050. The Results state improvement "in 11 of 16 individual scoring domains (Fig. 4)"; Fig. 4 marks the significance cut-off.

> "A greater improvement was observed in the intervention group in terms of procedure times (–15.98 min in intervention group versus –1.14 min in control group; P = 0.024) (Fig. 2), global performance scores (+1.742 versus +0.357; P = 0.011) (Fig. 3 and Table 2), and in 11 of 16 individual scoring domains (Fig. 4)."
