Evidence¶

Lasha Labadze 2023¶

Lasha Labadze, Maya Grigolia and Lela Machaidze. (2023). Role of AI chatbots in education: systematic literature review. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00426-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis in the review's student-advantages section. The review reports chatbots "provide individualized guidance and feedback" and that students can access ChatGPT anytime, with interactive conversational nature enhancing engagement and motivation per Kasneci et al. (2023).

"AI-powered chatbots in general are now able to provide individualized guidance and feedback to students, helping them navigate through challenging concepts and improve their understanding."

Related Claims¶

  • Students primarily benefit from AI chatbots in three key areas: homework and study assistance, personalized learning, and skills development — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Chatbot-based instruction enhanced secondary students' learning experience by enabling self-paced study with less stress, time savings, and maintained motivation — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • AI serves two complementary roles in learning: cognitive partner and personalized recommendation mechanism — related
  • Adapting instruction to individual learners requires more advanced measurement of expertise and cognitive load — related
  • GenAI can provide personalised feedback in group work, but the depth of personalisation is contested — related
  • AI is implemented in SRL research primarily as an intervention, most often through adaptive systems and personalization — related
  • Reviewed studies indicate AI-enabled personalization benefits both academic performance and student motivation — related
