Cimermanová 2018¶

Cimermanová, I. (2018). The Effect of Learning Styles on Academic Achievement in Different Forms of Teaching. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 219-232. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11316a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of GRSLSS scores after data screening in SPSS 23, reported for the in-class and online groups with printed means and SDs; these are descriptive group profiles, not tested contrasts.

"The most dominant and pre ferred style in in -class group was collaborative (x ̅=3,97; SD=0,78), the least preferred was avoidant (x ̅=1,99; SD=0,49). In the online group the most preferred style was independent (x ̅=3,55; SD=0,72) and the least preferred was competitive (x ̅=1,98; SD=0,78)."
