Ed Trust and New Leaders 2022¶

Ed Trust and New Leaders. (2022). Increasing School Leader Diversity in Georgia. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626443

q2 · i? · associational · r2

District-level Ed Trust analyses cited in the policy-recommendations section to illustrate why statewide averages are insufficient. The brief contrasts Atlanta Public Schools (97% access) with Columbia County (57% access).

"in Atlanta Public Schools, where 83% of students are of color, nearly all students (97%) have access to a school leader of color; on the other hand, in Columbia County, where 41% of students are of color, 57% of students have a school leader of color."
