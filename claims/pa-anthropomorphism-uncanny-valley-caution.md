## Evidence

### Sikström 2024

Sikström, P., Valentini, C., Sivunen, A., & Kärkkäinen, T. (2024). Pedagogical Agents Communicating and Scaffolding Students' Learning : High School Teachers' and Students' Perspectives. Computers and Education, 222, Article 105140. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105140

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the communication characteristics theme: students said communication errors diminish trust, yet "if a PA's communication is too perfect and anthropomorphic, it could be unsettling." One student called humanlike empathy in a PA "a little dystopic."

> "In fact, many students mentioned that if a PA's communication is too perfect and anthropomorphic, it could be unsettling and create an uncanny valley effect."
