### Moßhammer D 2017

Moßhammer D, Graf J, Joos S, Hertkorn R. (2017). Physical examination in undergraduate medical education in the field of general practice – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1074-1

`q2 · i?`

Scoping review following PRISMA-P protocol searched Medline using the syntax ("physical examination" AND "medical education" AND "undergraduate" AND general practice). The review "included 117 articles" published between January 2000 and March 2015, with "nearly half of the publications originated from the United States (n = 54)".

> "One-hundred seven articles were included. The annual number of publications ranged from 4 to 14 and had a slightly rising trend since 2000. Nearly half of the publications originated from the United States (n = 54), 33 from Canada and the United Kingdom."
