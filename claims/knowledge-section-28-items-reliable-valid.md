# The knowledge section of the liberal birthing position questionnaire shows good reliability and structural validity, with four factors explaining 41.0% of variance and Cronbach's alpha of 0.890

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exploratory factor analysis with 350 midwives extracted four common factors explaining 41.0% of the variance, and items were revised to 28 after removing low-loading items. [→ Luo Taizhen 2025](#luo-taizhen-2025)
`q2 i?` The revised 28-item knowledge section achieved a total Cronbach's alpha of 0.890, with each dimension above 0.6. [→ Luo Taizhen 2025 (2)](#luo-taizhen-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Luo Taizhen 2025

Luo Taizhen, Gong Yang, Dai Sidi, Xia Hua-an, Jiang Zhiyan, Gao Lingling. (2025). Questionnaire development: midwives' knowledge and attitude on liberal birthing position in 2nd stage. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07466-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis of the knowledge section in a formal survey of 350 midwives from 14 public hospitals in Guangzhou; KMO was 0.868 and Bartlett's test significant (p < 0.001). The analysis found "four common factors were extracted, and the interpretable variation was 41.0%" and nine items loaded below 0.5, leading to a 28-item revision.

> "When the eigenvalue > 1, four common factors were extracted, and the interpretable variation was 41.0%. After factor analysis, there were 9 items in the questionnaire (items 3, 5, 19, 22, 26, 27, 29, 30, 31) whose factor load value was less than 0.5."

### Luo Taizhen 2025 (2)

Luo Taizhen, Gong Yang, Dai Sidi, Xia Hua-an, Jiang Zhiyan, Gao Lingling. (2025). Questionnaire development: midwives' knowledge and attitude on liberal birthing position in 2nd stage. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07466-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability test (Step 7) on the formal survey data reports internal consistency for the revised knowledge section; dimension alphas were 0.788 (Concepts), 0.625 (Functions), 0.736 (Indications), 0.773 (Contraindications), with the total at 0.890.

> "The total Cronbach's coefficient of the revised knowledge section is 0.890, and the Cronbach's coefficient of each dimension is greater than 0.6, indicating that the internal consistency of the questionnaire is right"
