Trinh Anh T, Roberts Christine L, Ampt Amanda J. (2015). Knowledge, attitude and experience of episiotomy use among obstetricians and midwives in Viet Nam. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-015-0531-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six-item knowledge score (0–6) derived from questions on episiotomy's association with haemorrhage, perineal pain, wound complications, fetal distress, urinary incontinence and prolapse, compared with a two-sample t test. The quote reports the means, SDs and non-significant p value; the paper adds correct responses ranged from 30% to 67%.

> "Overall knowledge scores ranged from 0 to 6 with a mean of 3 and did not differ significantly among obstetricians (3.3 ± 1.6) and midwives (3.5 ± 1.3, p = 0.5)."
