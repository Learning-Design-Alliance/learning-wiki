Replication attempt in the Twins Early Development Study (N = 2330 for science), whose age-14 attainment was teacher-rated rather than nationally standardised. The article reports the failure to replicate, noting "the smaller sample and different phenotype complicates interpretation".

> "We failed to replicate the genome-wide significant SNP (rs9529641) and gene-level associations for science attainment in this independent longitudinal cohort, although the smaller sample and different phenotype complicates interpretation"
