## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2015

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2015). Unpacking Symbolic Equations in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.00535](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.00535)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Speak-aloud problem-solving interviews with thirteen introductory physics students using modified versions of questions from an earlier final exam study. The author reports students lost track of "known and unknown quantities" in symbolic problems, eliminating the known d; three students were surprised their result matched no answer option.

> "During the interviews students often lost track of the known and unknown quantities. For example, while solving the Tortoise and Hare symbolic problem three students started with the equations d = v0t and d = 12 at2, and incorrectly eliminated the known quantity ( d) leaving the two unknowns ( a and t)."
