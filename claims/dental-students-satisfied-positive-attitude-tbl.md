## Evidence

### Fouda Shaimaa M. 2025

Fouda Shaimaa M., Madi Marwa, Ellakany Passent, Aly Nourhan M., Bahgat Hala A., Elembaby Abeer E., Virtanen Jorma I., AlHumaid Jehan. (2025). The perception of dental students and educators about e-learning during COVID − 19 pandemic. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06152-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 431 dental students (92.1% response rate) at one college of dentistry, April–May 2023, using an adapted questionnaire; satisfaction and attitude were binary overall ratings. "Most students were satisfied with the TBL experience" and 90% reported websites helped their learning.

> "Most students were satisfied with the TBL experience, 90% reported that websites help them with their learning, 81.4% reported that modern education is inconceivable without using computers, and 77.3% believe they achieved the expected learning outcomes."
