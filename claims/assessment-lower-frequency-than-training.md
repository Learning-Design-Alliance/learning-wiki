Descriptive comparison across the survey's dichotomised training and assessment items, illustrated in Figs. 2–3 and Tables 1–2; for example, GEMS coping-with-fatigue training was 22.6% versus 19.9% assessment, and HEMS 44.0% versus 42.2%.
