### Powis Kathleen M. 2023

Powis Kathleen M., Lebanna Lesedi, Schenkel Sara, Masasa Gosego, Kgole Samuel W., Ngwaca Martha, Kgathi Coulson, Williams Paige L., Slogrove Amy L., Shapiro Roger L., Lockman Shahin, Mmalane Mompati O., Makhema Joseph M., Jao Jennifer, Cassidy Adam R. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc sensitivity analysis of the adjusted logistic regression in the FLOURISH cohort, reported in the Discussion. In the cohort, 17.9% of children HEU versus 100% of children HUU had ever been breastfed; the study reports that removing the breastfeeding covariate restored a significant HIV-exposure association. No effect size is printed for this analysis.

> "Interestingly, when the covariate breastfeeding was removed from the model, HIV exposure was noted to be significantly associated with lower academic performance, highlighting the known protective effect of breastfeeding on neurodevelopment"
