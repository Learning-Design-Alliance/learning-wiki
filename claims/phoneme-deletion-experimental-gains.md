## Evidence

### Pfenninger 2015

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post Wilcoxon comparisons on the L2/L3 phoneme deletion tasks (maximum = 7) in the intervention study; both experimental groups improved while neither control group did.

> "There were sta- tistically significant pre- and posttest differences for the Test Dyslexic group ( z = -2.78, p = .005) and the Test Nondyslexic group (z = -2.78, p = .005), in contrast to the two control groups (in all cases p > .05)"

### Pfenninger 2015 (2)

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparisons between the two experimental groups on the L3 phoneme deletion task in the intervention study; the pretest gap closed after training, with the Test Dyslexic group reaching 6.2 (88.57%) and the Test Nondyslexic group 6 (85.71%).

> "While statistically significant dif- ferences were observed between the results of the pre-PHON-L3, with the Test Nondyslexic group outscoring the Test Dyslexic group ( U = 76, p = . 0 5 3 ), t h e results of the post-PHON-L3 indicated no more differences between the two experimental groups"
