# Older adults produce fewer internal details than younger adults during elaboration across both experiments

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Younger adults generated more internal details than older adults for memory (d = 1.43) and problem solving (d = 1.21). [→ Peters 2019](#peters-2019)

## Evidence

### Peters 2019

Peters, S. L., Fan, C. L., & Sheldon, S. (2019). Episodic memory contributions to autobiographical memory and open-ended problem-solving specificity in younger and older adults. Memory & Cognition, 47, 1592–1605. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-019-00953-1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-design ANOVA on elaboration details in the memory experiment found younger adults generated more internal details than older adults, "d = 1.43"; the problem-solving contrast was d = 1.21.

> "For the memory experiment, younger adults (M =3 1 . 6 0 ,SE = 1.80) generated more internal details than older adults (M =1 8 . 7 0 ,SE =1 . 8 2 ;t(47) =4 . 9 8 ,p <. 0 0 1 ,d = 1.43)"
