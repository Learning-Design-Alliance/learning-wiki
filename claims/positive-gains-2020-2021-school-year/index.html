Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2022¶

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland, Karyn Lewis, Erik Ruzek, Angela Johnson. (2022). The COVID-19 school year: Learning and recovery across 2020-2021. AERA Open, 8. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584221099306

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Trend modeling of test scores from 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3 through 8 found that "the average student demonstrated positive gains in math and reading during the 2020-2021 school year," yet spring 2021 achievement remained below prepandemic averages. No effect size is printed for the gains themselves.

"Although the average student demonstrated positive gains in math and reading during the 2020-2021 school year, students were still behind typical (prepandemic) averages by spring 2021"
