`q3 · i?`

Same VIST construct validity study assessing procedure steps completed. Consultants completed "20% more procedure steps", median 34 versus trainee median 28, reported as "Mann–Whitney U=12.0, Z=−2.5, P=0.012".

> "Figure 2C shows that the consultant INRs also completed 20% more procedure steps and the difference was statistically significant (consultant median=34 (25th PR=32 and 75th PR=35) vs trainee median=28 (25th PR=25 and 75th PR=32), Mann–Whitney U=12.0, Z=−2.5, P=0.012)."
