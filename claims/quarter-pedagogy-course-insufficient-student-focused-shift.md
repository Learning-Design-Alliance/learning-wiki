## Evidence

### Star W. Lee 2019

Star W. Lee. (2019). The Impact of a Pedagogy Course on the Teaching Beliefs of Inexperienced Graduate Teaching Assistants. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-07-0137

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the eight postcourse concept maps from the same Biology 301 cohorts. Although instructive ideas dominated, the article reports beliefs "remained mainly teacher focused" and no terms were coded as responsive or reform based.

> "The terms on the postcourse maps were categorized as follows: 12.17% traditional, 76.97% instructive, and 10.86% transitional (Figure 2). There were no terms that were categorized as responsive or reform based."
