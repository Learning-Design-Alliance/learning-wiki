## Evidence

### Sintayehu Y 2020

Sintayehu Y, Assefa D, Biftu G, Getahun T, Haymanot M, Kasiye S, Teshale M. (2020). Basic neonatal resuscitation skills of midwives and nurses in Eastern Ethiopia are not well retained: An observational study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236194

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 427 participants; 20.8% of those who had ever performed NR retained skills versus 2.7% of those who had not, and "participants who ever performed NR were three times more likely to retain NR skill (AOR, 3.33 [95% CI: 1.09, 10.15])". The article prints an adjusted odds ratio but no standardized effect size.
