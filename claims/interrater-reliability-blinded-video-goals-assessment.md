## Evidence

### Sharma Mitesh MS 2012

Sharma Mitesh MS, Macafee David MA MD, Pranesh Nagarajan MD, Horgan Alan F. MD. (2012). Construct Validity of Fresh Frozen Human Cadaver as a Training Model in Minimal Access Surgery. JSLS : Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/108680812X13462882735818

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interrater reliability analysis for intracorporeal knot tying clips, comparing a principal assessor with a second postlaparoscopic-fellowship consultant surgeon using the same modified GOALS scale. Table 3 prints Kendall's tau-b of .520 and Spearman's rho of .636 (N = 106), significant at the 0.01 level; no standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "The association between ranks by 2 assessors was significant (P < .001), indicating that the 2 assessors ranked the individual clips in a similar way (Table 3)."
