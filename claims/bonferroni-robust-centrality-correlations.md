Nathan Crossette, Lincoln D. Carr, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2023). Correlations between student connectivity and academic performance: a pandemic follow-up. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010106](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010106)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bonferroni correction applied to the measured correlations in all three courses, reported in Table II of the results. The stricter threshold was "p < . 005"; correlations passing it are marked especially strong and resistant to false-positive analysis. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The test divides the initial p-value threshold (of .05) by the number of stati sti- cal tests performed which in this case was ten tests per cours e for a stricter threshold of p < . 005."
