### Qureshi Muhammad Fazal Hussain; Asif Fozia; Khan Shahmeer Raza; Akhtar Munazza; Shah Muzna; Altaf Ahmed; Latif Asad 2026

Qureshi Muhammad Fazal Hussain; Asif Fozia; Khan Shahmeer Raza; Akhtar Munazza; Shah Muzna; Altaf Ahmed; Latif Asad. (2026). Strengthening patient safety competencies among physician interns through a structured quality and safety course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09875-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-arm quasi-experimental study of 166 postgraduate medical interns completing a two-day QPS module with pre- and post-module assessments; paired t-tests showed significant score gains in each cohort (mean increases of 2.25, 2.21, and 2.60 points), with "pre- to post-test differences were statistically significant (p < 0.001 for all)".

> "Participants graduating in 2023, 2024, and 2025 demonstrated comparable baseline pre-test scores, with mean increases of 2.25, 2.21, and 2.60 points, respectively. Post-test mean scores were consistently higher across cohorts, and the pre- to post-test differences were statistically significant (p < 0.001 for all)"
