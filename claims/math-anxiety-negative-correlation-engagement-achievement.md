Zhang Zhengdong, Gao Chenghua, Wu Hongwei, Ren Dong. (2026). The impact of teacher support on high school students’ mathematics achievement: the mediating roles of mathematics anxiety and learning engagement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04776-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among 1,340 high school students. The study reports that "mathematical anxiety was significantly and negatively correlated with learning engagement (r = − 0.469, p < 0.001)" and with mathematics achievement (r = − 0.165).
