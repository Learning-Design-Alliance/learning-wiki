## Evidence

### Mehrak Rahimi & Elham Soleymani 2015

Mehrak Rahimi & Elham Soleymani. (2015). The Impact of Mobile Learning on Listening Anxiety and Listening Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p152

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 50 intermediate EFL learners assigned by device access: experimental group (n=25) listened to 12 podcasts on portable devices, control group (n=25) used desktop computers for twelve weeks. ANCOVA with FLLAQ pretest as covariate showed "a significant difference between two groups in anxiety questionnaire posttest" with a large printed effect size, partial eta squared = .450 (η² = .450). Posttest means were 2.903 (experimental) vs 3.912 (control).

> "the result of ANCOV A revealed that there was a significant difference between two groups in anxiety questionnaire posttest [F (1, 47) =38.511; p=.000; partial eta squared=.450]"
