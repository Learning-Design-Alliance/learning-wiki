Students' epistemologies about experimental physics: Validating the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. arXiv:1512.06869. Comparison of FY (N = 2433) and BFY (N = 1158) subgroups from the matched dataset (Table VI): BFY pre 17.7 vs FY pre 15.8, post 17.5 vs 14.4, with printed effect sizes 0.2 (FY) and 0.04 (BFY within-course shift).

> "The diﬀerence between the E-CLASS scores of the FY and BFY courses is a statistically significant ( p << 0.05) for both the pre- and post-surveys."
