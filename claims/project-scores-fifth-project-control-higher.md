## Evidence

### Tsompanoudi et al. 2015

Despina TSOMPANOUDI, Maya SATRATZEMI, Stelios XINOGALOS. (2015). Distributed Pair Programming Using Collaboration Scripts: An Educational System and Initial Results. Informatics in Education. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney tests on the five submitted programming assignments in the 24-pair evaluation. The article notes "the performance of two groups declined significantly in the last project" in the experimental condition, affecting overall performance.

> "In the first four projects no significant difference between the grades (p > 0.05) was found, but in the fifth project the test revealed that students of the control group performed better than the experimental condition (U = 30.5, p = 0.015)."
