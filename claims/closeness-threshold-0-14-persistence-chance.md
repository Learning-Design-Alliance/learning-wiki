# An out-of-class closeness threshold of about 0.14 separates intermediate-grade students with about 63% versus about 92% chances of persisting

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the intermediate cohort, students with out-of-class closeness below about 0.14 have about a 63% chance of persisting, versus about 92% above that value; the split is confirmed by chi-squared test (χ2(1) = 7.46, P = 0.006). [→ Justyna P. Zwolak 2018](#justyna-p-zwolak-2018)

## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Michael Zwolak, and Eric Brewe. (2018). Educational commitment and social networking: The power of informal networks. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Threshold analysis of out-of-class closeness for the intermediate grade cohort in the MI-M persistence models. The article reports the 0.14 split with χ2(1) = 7.46, P = 0.006, and persistence chances of about 63% below versus about 92% above; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "It comes at about 0.14 [the signiﬁcance of this split is conﬁrmed by the chi-squared test: χ2(1) = 7.46,P = 0.006]. Below this value, students have about a 63 % chance of persisting. Above this value, students have about a 92 % chance of persisting."
