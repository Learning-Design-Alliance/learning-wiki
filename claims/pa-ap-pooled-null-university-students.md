## Subclaims
`q3 i0` The random-effects pooled correlation between physical activity and academic performance did not differ significantly from zero (z' = 0.07, 95% CI −0.06–0.20) and showed considerable heterogeneity (I2 = 75.8%). [→ Wunsch K. 2021](#wunsch-k-2021)

## Evidence

### Wunsch K. 2021

Wunsch K., Fiedler J., Bachert P., Woll A. (2021). The Tridirectional Relationship among Physical Activity, Stress, and Academic Performance in University Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18020739

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of four included studies (total 1952 participants) pooling self-reported PA and GPA-type AP measures. The pooled coefficient "was 0.07 (95% confidence interval (CI): −0.06–0.20" with I2 = 75.8%, so the average did not differ significantly from zero.

> "The estimated average Fisher r-to-z transformed correlation coefficient based on the random-effects model for the relationship between PA and AP, PA and stress, and stress and AP was 0.07 (95% confidence interval (CI): −0.06–0.20, Q5 = 14.31, p = 0.01; I2 = 75.8%)"
