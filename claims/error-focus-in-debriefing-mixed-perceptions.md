## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Responses were mixed on whether the facilitator focused more on what the learner did right or wrong; almost a third perceived debriefers focusing on mistakes, and slightly more than half reported a focus on technical points. ## Evidence

### Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John 2021

Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John. (2021). Simulation debriefing: a perspective from emergency medical care students at three South African Higher Education Institutions. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2021.38.97.23009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire survey of 153 students; frequency analysis. Table 3 shows 41 (26.8%) agreed and 12 (7.8%) strongly agreed the facilitator focuses on what was done wrong; 70 (45.8%) agreed the facilitator focuses on technical points and small things.

> "However, almost a third of participants perceived debriefers focusing on what they did wrong rather than what they did right. There is a fine line between debriefing errors in a constructive way and the student perceiving the debrief as focusing on their mistakes."
