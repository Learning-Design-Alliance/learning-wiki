### Garet 2017

Garet, M.S., Wayne, A.J., Brown, S., Rickles, J., Song, M., and Manzeske, D. (2017). The Impact of Providing Performance Feedback to Teachers and Principals (NCEE 2018-4001). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year 2 impact analysis in a randomized experiment in which the study team video-recorded lessons in treatment and control schools and coded them with the two observation rubrics used to provide feedback; the report states the intervention moved teachers "from the 50th to the 57th percentile" on one rubric.

> "The intervention had a positive impact on teachers' classroom practice on one of the two observation rubrics, moving teachers from the 50th to the 57th percentile, but it had no impact on practice as measured by the other rubric."
