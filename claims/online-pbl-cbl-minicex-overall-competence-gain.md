## Evidence

### Liu Yutong 2026

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mini-CEX assessments in the same RCT, each lasting 30 minutes and conducted by two non-teaching instructors using standardized 9-point scales. Overall clinical competence showed a moderate effect size (d = 0.601) in Table 3.

> "The experimental group obtained a significantly higher overall clinical competence scores in Mini-CEX assessments (7.32 ± 0.56 vs. 6.92 ± 0.76, P < 0.05; Table 3)."

### Liu Yutong 2026 (2)

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section reporting all six Mini-CEX sub-dimensions as non-significant. The authors state that "these non-significant results cannot be regarded as evidence of no actual differences between the groups" given the small sample size, and that clinical examination skills showed a moderate effect size despite P = 0.074.

> "The experimental group also scored higher in all other dimensions (interviewing skills, clinical examination skills, humanistic qualities, clinical judgement, counseling skills, and organizational efficiency), but none of these differences reached statistical significance (P > 0.05)."
