## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Classroom observation over four weeks showed that nearly all students who had produced the target sounds on tests did not use them during regular English lessons. [→ Kayaoglu 2013](#kayaoglu-2013)

## Evidence

### Kayaoglu 2013

Kayaoglu, M. N. & Caylak, N. (2013). What is needed for correct pronunciation: a model or a concern? Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 53, 269-290. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1060386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Structured classroom observation conducted over four weeks between post-test and delayed test, checking whether students pronounced /theta/ and /eth/ in passages similar to test materials. Almost no students used the target sounds spontaneously, despite having demonstrated them on tests.

> "With the exception of four students in the first week and two in the second, third, and forth weeks, none of the students used the target sounds, inter-dental fricatives."
