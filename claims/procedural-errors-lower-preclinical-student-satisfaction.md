## Evidence

### Aljamani Sanaa 2025

Aljamani Sanaa, Al-Sawaeir Sereen, Nassar Rahaf, Rawajbeh Khetam, Saeed Amal, Ismail Noor H., Sartawi Samiha Yousef, Qdais Laith Abu. (2025). Assessment of procedural errors and student satisfaction in preclinical root canal treatment: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07596-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional chi-square analysis of 307 third-year students performing root canal treatment on extracted premolars. The study found "only 18.1% of students with procedural errors were satisfied" versus 77.8% satisfaction among error-free students (p < 0.001).

> "Among students with no procedural errors, 77.8% reported satisfaction, while 22.1% were unsatisfied. In contrast, only 18.1% of students with procedural errors were satisfied, and 81.9% were unsatisfied."
