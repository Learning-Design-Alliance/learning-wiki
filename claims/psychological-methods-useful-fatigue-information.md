### G. M. W 1910

G. M. W. (1910). Abstracts and Reviews: Some Recent Literature upon Fatigue; Studies in Arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069029

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Offner's conclusion (p. 35) about psychological measurement methods, including the continuous-work method favored by Kraepelin and followers, that they can "assist us directly in the regulation of our work." No effect sizes are printed.

> "the psychological methods can afford useful average values and reliable information concerning the course and effect of fatigue, and can. accordingly, assist us directly in the regulation of our work"
