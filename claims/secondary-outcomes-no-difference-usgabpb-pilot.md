O’Sullivan O, Iohom G, O’Donnell BD, Shorten GD. (2014). The effect of simulation-based training on initial performance of ultrasound-guided axillary brachial plexus blockade in a clinical setting – a pilot study. BMC Anesthesiology 14:110. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2253-14-110

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 8-participant comparison from the pilot RCT, confidence on a ten-point verbal rating scale did not differ (means 2.45 vs 2.83, p = 0.587); the article also reports "no difference in the secondary outcomes measured".

> "Participant confidence did not differ statistically between the group 2 (2.45), and 2.83 (2.64) [mean (std dev)] in the Simulation and Control Groups respectively (p = 0.587)."
