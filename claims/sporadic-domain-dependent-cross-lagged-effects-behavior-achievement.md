# Cross-lagged effects between behavioral problems and achievement are sporadic and domain-dependent, not consistently reciprocal, across elementary school

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-lagged panel analyses of 461 students across four school years found no consistent reciprocal effects between internalizing/externalizing behavioral problems and achievement; effects depended on the achievement domain. [→ Kulakow 2023](#kulakow-2023)

## Evidence

### Kulakow 2023

Kulakow, S., Mahlau, K., & Kocaj, A. (2023). The longitudinal relationship between internalizing and externalizing behavioral problems with academic achievement in elementary school. https://osf.io/cyqak/?view_only=37eade9dd7c142e88fe9bef9451c125e

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel models (15 models, N = 461, five measurement points from grade 1 to grade 4, controlling for cognitive ability, attention, and gender) showed that "cross-lagged effects depended on the achievement domain" rather than showing uniform reciprocal paths.

> "we did not find conclusive support for the reciprocal effects model as hypothesized. Instead, cross-lagged effects depended on the achievement domain and were inconsistent concerning externalizing and internalizing behavioral problems."
