## Evidence

### Heni Martin 2012

Heni Martin, Lammerding-Köppel Maria, Celebi Nora, Shiozawa Thomas, Riessen Reimer, Nikendei Christoph, Weyrich Peter. (2012). Focused didactic training for skills lab student tutors – which techniques are considered helpful? GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 29(3), Doc41. [doi:10.3205/zma000811](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000811)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective online survey of 23 experienced student tutors (11 females, 12 males) using a 6-point Likert scale (1=totally agree). The presentation module scored "1.79±0.71" versus 2.35-2.68 for the other four modules; no effect size was reported.

> "The module “How to present effectively” was rated to improve the tutors’ general didactic qualification most (1.79±0.71), while there were only little differences between the other four modules provided (2.35-2.68)."
