### Yufei Guo & Qun Ye 2026

Yufei Guo & Qun Ye. (2026). Meta-cognitive insights into cognitive offloading: mechanisms, interventions, and educational implications. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-06621-5

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Experimental study (Dunn & Risko, 2016) manipulating word rotation and layout frame rotation with reading tasks. Experiments 3–4 showed subjective effort, time and accuracy judgments matched offloading behavior, with "head turning" having greater expected benefits in the co-rotation condition.

> "Results showed that metacognitive evaluations of task performance and effort expenditure, rather than objective performance or effort, drove spontaneous off- loading behaviors."
