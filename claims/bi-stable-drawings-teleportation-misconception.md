## Evidence

### Li Causi 2025

Li Causi, Gianluca. (2025). Explaining quanta with optical illusions. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.08583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.08583)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Design argument from the teleportation section: photon B carried to destination was in superposition, so all identities were physically carried in one move; a spectator looking at the paired drawings sees that moving either drawing moves both shapes. No protocol replication with three drawings was found.

> "bi -stable drawings turn to be very helpful to show that quantum teleportation does not actually transport anything, because the identity of the teleported photon is already at destination since we set the quantum channel."
