## Subclaims

Tukey's HSD test across the six sequences with 15 hypotheses tested, using data from the four McLEAP assignments. The article reports the CTE vs TCE contrast is "a signiﬁcant increase in average of gains" but not for gain of averages.

> "We ﬁnd that CTE gives a signiﬁcant increase in average of gains TCE (p = 0.03), but ﬁnd a below signiﬁcant re- sult for gain of averages ( p = 0.08). We thus ﬁnd no conclusive evidence against our null hypothesis."
