## Evidence

### Kim M. Mitchell 2023

Kim M. Mitchell, Sharon Zumbrunn, Danielle N. Berry, Lisa Demczuk. (2023). Writing Self-Efficacy in Postsecondary Students: a Scoping Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09798-2

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of the 35 studies (70%) measuring writing performance reports weak average correlations; r values for in-class or take-home samples ranged from −0.11 to 0.41 (mean r = 0.22), and regression β scores ranged from −0.02 to 0.60 (mean β = 0.26).

> "On average, the correlation between the two variables are weak with only seven studies (20% of all studies measuring performance) achieving an r, rs, or β > 0.40"
