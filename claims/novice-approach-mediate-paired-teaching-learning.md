## Evidence

### Jared B. Stang and Linda E. Strubbe 2015

Jared B. Stang and Linda E. Strubbe. (2015). Paired teaching for faculty professional development in teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.05948](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.05948)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study of four paired-teaching arrangements; authors' open-coded interview analysis. They report that Instructors A and B "went in with the deliberate intention of learning about teaching from their expert counterpart," while Instructor D "focused not on professional development in teaching but on creating a good product for the students."

> "we suggest that an important consideration in the success of paired teaching, which likely mediates the amount of pedagogical learning that occurs, is the approach and perception of the novice instructor."
