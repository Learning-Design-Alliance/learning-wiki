## Evidence

### Maloney S 2013

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Usage audit of resource types accessed by third- and fourth-year students in 2009 and 2010, reported in Figure 3. Students accessed "lecture slides and practical videos considerably more" than other resource types.

> "Third- and fourth-year students both accessed lecture slides and practical videos considerably more than the other resources available on Physeek. There was minimal access of unit guides and self-directed learning modules, whereas a small number of practical pre-readings were accessed"

### Maloney S 2013 (2)

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests comparing pooled resource uptake between 2009 and 2010 found "significantly more practical videos accessed in 2010" (P=.02) and more lecture slides in 2009 (P=.01); no significant differences for unit guides (P=.26), modules (P=.76), or pre-readings (P=.41).

> "a Chi-square test showed a significant difference in the number of lecture slides accessed in 2009 compared to 2010 (P=.01), with a greater number being accessed in 2009. Conversely, there were significantly more practical videos accessed in 2010 compared to 2009 (P=.02)."
