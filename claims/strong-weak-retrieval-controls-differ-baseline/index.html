P. Lopes da Cunha 2018¶

P. Lopes da Cunha, D. Ramirez Butavand, L. B. Chisari, F. Ballarini and H. Viola (2018). Exams at classroom have bidirectional effects on the long-term memory of an unrelated graphical task. npj Science of Learning 3:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0036-7

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Post-hoc analysis of control groups found CTRw groups homogeneous (F(6,150) = 0.74, p = 0.62) and CTRs groups homogeneous (F(6,167) = 1.72, p = 0.12), but CTRw vs CTRs means differed significantly (t = 6.65, p < 0.001), so each control type was compared only with its paired EXM groups.

"When we compared CTRw vs CTRs means, we found a signi ﬁcant difference ( p < 0.001, t = 6.65), reason why these control groups were analyzed separately and compared with their corresponding EXM groups."
