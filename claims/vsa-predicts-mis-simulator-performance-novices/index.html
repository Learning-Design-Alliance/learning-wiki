Evidence¶

Vajsbaher Tina 2018¶

Vajsbaher Tina, Schultheis Holger, Francis Nader K. (2018). Spatial cognition in minimally invasive surgery: a systematic review. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-018-0416-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of 22 correlational and 4 comparative studies (26 included, 1214 participants) on spatial cognition in MIS. The review reports that all VSA studies found an impact on simulator performance, though "they were inconsistent in regards to which element was found to be most prominent".

"All studies in this review investigating the relationship between VSA and MIS performance and learning, found VSA to have an impact on operative simulator performance."
