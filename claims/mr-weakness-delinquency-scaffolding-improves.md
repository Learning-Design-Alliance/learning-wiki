`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative synthesis across studies using MR proxies (self-efficacy, self-awareness, planning/monitoring) found that "when MR is supported through structured opportunities for reflection and scaffolding, psychosocial functioning and adaptive engagement reliably improve". No effect sizes printed.

> "Across studies, a consistent empirical pattern emerges, when MR -related capacities are weak, adolescents are more likely to exhibit maladaptive risk expressions such as disengagement, aggression, and delinquent behavior; conversely, when MR is supported through structured opportunities for reflection and scaffolding, psychosocial functioning and adaptive engagement reliably improve."
