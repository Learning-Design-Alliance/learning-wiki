### Kassaw Chalachew 2023

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2023). Determinants of academic achievement among higher education student found in low resource setting, A systematic review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294585

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study at Hawassa University (n = 659) reviewed in the synthesis: "Students aged between 20 and 24 years" and "medical/ health faculty" were significant associates of good academic performance; non-smokers were three times more likely to score good grades (AOR = 3.15).

> "Students aged between 20 and 24 years (AOR = 0.43, 95% CI = 0.22–0.91), and medical/ health faculty (AOR = 2.46, 95% CI = 1.45–4.20) were significant associates of good academic performance."
