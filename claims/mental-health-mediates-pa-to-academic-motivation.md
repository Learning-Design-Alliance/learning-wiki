## Evidence

### Ma Dongmin 2025

Ma Dongmin, Akram Huma, Li Shengji. (2025). Assessing the role of physical activity in shaping students’ academic motivation: the mediating role of mental health. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-25541-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Structural equation modeling with bootstrapping (5,000 resamples) on the three-wave data from the 450-student study, with PA as predictor, MH as mediator and motivation as outcome. The article reports indirect paths of "ꞵ = 0.12, p = 0.01" for IM and "ꞵ = 0.17, p < 0.01" for EM; no standardized effect size in d, r or η² form is printed for these indirect effects, so no magnitude code is assigned.

> "The results showed that mental health at T2 significantly mediated the relationship between PA at T1 and academic motivation for both intrinsic motivation (ꞵ = 0.12, p = 0.01) and extrinsic motivation (ꞵ = 0.17, p < 0.01) at T3."
