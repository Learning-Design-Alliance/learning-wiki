Fajriah and Churiyah 2016¶

Ulfah Nur Fajriah, Madziatul Churiyah. (2016). Utilizing Instructional Media for Teaching Infrastructure Administration. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.6. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092480

q2 · i? · design · r2

Validation study of the developed multimedia module using expert questionnaires and a limited group trial, scored against Riduwan's feasibility criteria. The article reports "97.7% from material expert, 76.5% from media expert and 93.06% from limited trial group".

"The results showed that the assessment from expert validator and limited group testing to the fourth aspects gained an average sco re 97.7% from material expert, 76.5% from media expert and 93.06% from limited trial group, which means that the media is categorized as very valid and reliable to use."
