### Tan Kate M 2015

Tan Kate M, Flack Felicity S, Bear Natasha L, Allen Judy A. (2015). An evaluation of a data linkage training workshop for research ethics committees. BMC Medical Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12910-015-0007-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Deferred web-based survey (T3, n = 48 of 84 emailed) conducted 8-17 months after training; 69% had completed a review of a linked data application since training, and 91% reported improved understanding of the data linkage process. T3 could not be linked to T1/T2 as no numeric identifiers were used.

> "Confidence in reviewing data linkage applications improved as a result of the training for 87% of respondents."
