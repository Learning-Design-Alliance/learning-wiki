Morra 2019¶

In Study II with 92 sixth- and seventh-graders, item analysis identified "2 subsets of items that demand a capacity of 4 or 5 units, respectively." This links specific rational-number item demands to working memory capacity units.

"We also identified 2 subsets of items that demand a capacity of 4 or 5 units, respectively."
