`q2 i?` Sites whose participants practiced most (Filipstad, over 15 times) showed the largest improvements, while sites practicing least (Barcelona, Amersfoort) showed non-significant improvement. [→ Smart JR 2015](#smart-jr-2015)

## Evidence

### Smart JR 2015

Smart JR, Kranz K, Carmona F, Lindner TW, Newton A. (2015). Does real-time objective feedback and competition improve performance and quality in manikin CPR training – a prospective observational study from several European EMS. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0160-9

Observational comparison across the 10 sites using self-reported practice counts between stages (overall mean 6.2 adult, 5.5 infant, with wide site variability, sd 6.8 and 6.2). "At Filipstad the site co-ordinator reported that each participant had practiced the most (over 15 times)".
