## Evidence

### Marija Cubric 2015

Marija Cubric, and Amanda Jefferies. (2015). The benefits and challenges of large-scale deployment of electronic voting systems: University student views from across different subject groups. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 590 students across 11 schools at a UK university; non-linear PCA (CATPCA) on 31 Likert-scale items after removing one variable. Four components were retained explaining 60.86% of variance in total.

> "CATPCA was then conducted on the remaining 31 items, resulting in 6 components with the eigenvalues higher than 1."
