# In the simulations, final group performance depends more on initial opinion diversity than on initial group performance, with a threshold diversity of about 0.5 above which final performance is high regardless of initial score

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For fixed initial group performance, final performance increases with initial opinion diversity, and when initial diversity is sufficiently high (V̄(t=0) ≳ 0.5) final performance converges at a higher level regardless of initial group performance. [→ Jibeom Seo 2025](#jibeom-seo-2025)

## Evidence

### Jibeom Seo 2025

Jibeom Seo, Beom Jun Kim. (2025). Opinion dynamics model of collaborative learning. [doi:10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Systematic simulation generating 1,000 independent trajectories for each combination of initial performance Sz(t=0) and initial diversity V̄(t=0), with results shown as a heat map in Fig. 7. The article reports the threshold finding that "the final group performance does not depend strongly on the initial group performance when the initial opinion diversity is sufficiently high."

> "the final group performance does not depend strongly on the initial group performance when the initial opinion diversity is sufficiently high ( ¯V (t = 0) ≳ 0.5). This suggests that the threshold ¯V (t = 0) ≈ 0.5 represents the optimal level of opinion diversity"
