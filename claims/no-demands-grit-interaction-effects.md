## Evidence

### Teuber Ziwen 2021

Teuber Ziwen, Nussbeck Fridtjof W., Wild Elke. (2021). The Bright Side of Grit in Burnout-Prevention: Exploring Grit in the Context of Demands-Resources Model among Chinese High School Students. Child Psychiatry and Human Development, 52(3), 464–476. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01031-3

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Latent-interaction SEM (LMS/XWITH approach) in the Chinese high school sample tested buffering and boosting hypotheses; all four demands × grit interactions were non-significant, and model comparison favored the main-effects model. The buffering hypothesis was not supported.

> "It turned out that none of the latent interaction terms reached significance: perseverant grit × demands on exhaustion (β = -0.03, p = 0.41) and on engagement (β = 0.02, p = 0.37)"
