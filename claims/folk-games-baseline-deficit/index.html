Wei 2016¶

Xia Wei. (2016). Research on the Boost of Development on Young Children's Fine Motor by Folk Games. International Education Studies, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n9p111

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline survey in the same quasi-experiment: the class with the lower average was assigned as the intervention group. At the intermediate test after one month, the article reports the intervention class at 1.01 and the control class at 1.07, still lower despite improvement.
