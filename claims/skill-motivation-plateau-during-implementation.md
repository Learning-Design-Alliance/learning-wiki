# Skill and motivation/work engagement scores plateaued during the implementation phase after EBP training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall skill (p = 0.709) and motivation and work engagement (p = 0.210) did not change significantly from after-training to implementation phase, while knowledge and practice continued to rise (all p < 0.001) in one single-group pre-post study with no comparison group. ## Evidence

### Akhter N 2026

Akhter N, Zhou X, Elhabashy S, Huq KATE, Okamoto M, Latif MA, Babaita AO, Moriyama M. (2026). Effect of evidence-based education program with mentoring system for prevention of ventilator-associated pneumonia on nurses' competency to improve quality care for patients in the intensive care unit: a quasi-experimental study in a tertiary hospital in Bangladesh. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1674223

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed ranks tests comparing after-training to implementation-phase scores in the same 52-nurse pre-post study; overall skill and motivation "were not improved during implementation phase after training." Four skill components (patient positioning, circuit changes, early ambulation, weaning) did continue improving (all p < 0.05).

> "The overall skill (p = 0.709) and some of its components (hand hygiene; p = 0.269, oral care; p = 0.083; endotracheal tube suction; p = 0.150, ulcer and DVT prophylaxis; p = 0.124) and motivation and work engagement (p = 0.210) were not improved during implementation phase after training."
