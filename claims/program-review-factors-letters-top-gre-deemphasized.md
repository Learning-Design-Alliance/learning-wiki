`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program survey question on factors in identifying top candidates (Figure 7). Application essays were rated important or very important by 84%; 51% ranked reputation of the undergraduate institution as important.

> "letters of recommendation were the most highly cited as "very important", by 78% of programs."

### Emily M. Levesque (chair) 2025 (2)

Emily M. Levesque (chair), Rachel Ivie, Christopher Johns-Krull, Grace Krahm, Laura A. Lopez, Meredith A. MacGregor, Sebastian Monzon, Daniel R. Piacitelli, Seth Redfield, and Tom Rice. (2025). AAS Graduate Admissions Task Force: Final report and recommendations. American Astronomical Society. [doi:10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc](https://doi.org/10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program survey on test requirements. Only 30% of programs rated the GRE/PGRE as slightly important or important in review, and none listed it as very important.

> "None of the programs that responded to our survey reported requiring the Physics GRE; 11% describe it as "recommended", 46% as "optional", and 43% as "not used"."
