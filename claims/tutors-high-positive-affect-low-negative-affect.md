# Tutors maintain consistently high positive affect and low negative affect during teaching

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Positive affect is high before and after sessions and only slightly, though significantly, lower over measurement days, with senior and novice tutors reporting the same amount. [→ Hundertmark Jan 2019](#hundertmark-jan-2019)
`q3 i?` Negative affect is low and decreases over the course's measurement period, with novice tutors higher than seniors only at the first pre-session measurement. [→ Hundertmark Jan 2019 (2)](#hundertmark-jan-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PANAS short-version ratings at multiple measurement points across three course days, analysed with RM-ANOVAs. The article reports positive affect "generally high", a post-recovery drop "presumably due to lower activation and wakefulness", and a slight but significant decrease over days.

> "Tutors’ positive affect is generally high, both 30 min before the start of the sessions, M = 3.45 (SD = 0.65), and after the end of the sessions, M = 3.36 (SD = 0.80). However, positive affect drops to M = 2.48 (SD = 0.71) after the recovery phase"

### Hundertmark Jan 2019 (2)

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PANAS negative-affect subscale across measurement points and days in the same RM-ANOVA analysis. The article notes no general experience effect except "novice tutors show higher levels than senior tutors" 30 min before session start, t(178) = 2.89, p = .006.

> "Tutors report low negative affect that decreases over a course’s measurement period: M = 1.41 (SD = 0.45) before the start of sessions, M = 1.18 (SD = 0.35) after sessions, and M = 1.15 (SD = 0.36) after recovery phases"
