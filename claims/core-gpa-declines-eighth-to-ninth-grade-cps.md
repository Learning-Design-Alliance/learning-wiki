# CPS students' core GPAs continue to decline between eighth and ninth grade despite steady improvement in both eighth- and ninth-grade grades

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Between 2012 and 2017, average ninth-grade core GPA rose from 2.18 to 2.58 and eighth-grade core GPA rose from 2.52 to 2.89, yet the eighth-to-ninth decline persisted (0.34 points in 2012; 0.31 points in 2017). [→ Seeskin 2018](#seeskin-2018)

## Evidence

### Seeskin 2018

Seeskin, A., Nagaoka, J., & Mahaffie, S. (2018). Hidden risk: Changes in GPA across the transition to high school. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/hidden-risk-changes-gpa-across-transition-high-school

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of CPS administrative grade data for six freshman cohorts (2011-12 through 2016-17, N=15,914 to 18,969 per cohort), non-charter schools only. The report states "the average ninth-grade core GPA increased 0.40 GPA points, from 2.18 to 2.58" while eighth-grade GPA rose from 2.52 to 2.89, with declines of 0.34 (2012) and 0.31 (2017) points persisting.

> "Between 2012 and 2017, the average ninth-grade core GPA increased 0.40 GPA points, from 2.18 to 2.58, the rough equivalent of going from a C+ to a B-"
