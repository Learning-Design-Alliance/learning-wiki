# Adolescents in the higher physical fitness cluster score better on attention and concentration than the lower-fitness cluster, in both boys and girls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Among boys, the higher-fitness cluster outperformed the lower-fitness cluster on d2 measures (e.g., TOT: p < 0.001, d' Cohen = 1.01). [→ Reigal RE 2020](#reigal-re-2020)
`q3 i2` Among girls, the higher-fitness cluster outperformed the lower-fitness cluster on d2 measures (e.g., TOT: p < 0.01, d' Cohen = 0.61). [→ Reigal RE 2020 (2)](#reigal-re-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Reigal RE 2020

Reigal RE, Moral-Campillo L, Juárez-Ruiz de Mier R, Morillo-Baro JP, Morales-Sánchez V, Pastrana JL and Hernández-Mendo A (2020) Physical Fitness Level Is Related to Attention and Concentration in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00110

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

t-test comparison of d2 scores between k-means fitness clusters among 92 boys (45 low-fitness, 47 high-fitness). The higher-fitness group scored better, with printed effect size d' Cohen = 1.01 for TOT ("TOT ( p < 0.001, d’ Cohen = 1.01, 95% CI [0.57, 1.44])") and d' Cohen = 0.89 for CON.

> "there were diﬀerences for boys between clusters in the measures TR ( p < 0.001, d’ Cohen = 1.05, 95% CI [0.62, 1.49]), TA ( p < 0.001, d’ Cohen = 0.90, 95% CI [0.47, 1.33]), TOT ( p < 0.001, d’ Cohen = 1.01, 95% CI [0.57, 1.44]), CON (p < 0.001, d’ Cohen = 0.89, 95% CI [0.46, 1.32])"

### Reigal RE 2020 (2)

Reigal RE, Moral-Campillo L, Juárez-Ruiz de Mier R, Morillo-Baro JP, Morales-Sánchez V, Pastrana JL and Hernández-Mendo A (2020) Physical Fitness Level Is Related to Attention and Concentration in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00110

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

t-test comparison of d2 scores between fitness clusters among 118 girls (57 low-fitness, 61 high-fitness). The higher-fitness group scored better, with printed effect size d' Cohen = 0.61 for TOT ("TOT
(p < 0.01, d’ Cohen = 0.61, 95% CI [0.24, 0.98])"); effects were smaller than in boys.

> "Also, there were diﬀerences for girls between clusters in the measures TR ( p < 0.01, d’ Cohen = 0.58, 95% CI [0.21, 0.95]), TA ( p < 0.01, d’ Cohen = 0.61, 95% CI [0.24, 0.98]), TOT
(p < 0.01, d’ Cohen = 0.61, 95% CI [0.24, 0.98]), CON (p < 0.01,
d’ Cohen = 0.58, 95% CI [0.21, 0.95])"
