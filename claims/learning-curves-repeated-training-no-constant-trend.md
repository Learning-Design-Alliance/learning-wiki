### Higuchi M 2020

Higuchi M, Abe T, Hotta K, et al. (2020). Development and validation of a porcine organ model for training in essential laparoscopic surgical skills. International Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1111/iju.14315

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Learning curves of task 1 were examined in 15 participants who underwent the training multiple times (from two to six times). The article reports 'No constant trend was observed among the participants' with increasing GOALS/ALL and decreasing NASA-TLX tendencies in several participants. The same results were observed in tasks 2 and 3 (data not shown).
