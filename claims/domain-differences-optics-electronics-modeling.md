Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2018). The Modeling Framework for Experimental Physics: Description, development, and applications. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aae3ce](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aae3ce)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

From the national instructor interview study, the article reports domain differences in the comparisons subtask: statistical fitting in optics versus qualitative performance checks in electronics. Electronics instructors often framed modeling subtasks as aspects of troubleshooting.

> "optics instructors were more likely to describe engaging students in rigorous statistical analyses (e.g., ﬁtting curves to data and evaluating the goodness of ﬁt). In contrast, electronics instructors said that comparisons typically involved qualitative checks of circuit performance because building functional circuits was more important than achieving precise agreement"
