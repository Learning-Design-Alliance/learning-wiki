### Manu Kapur 2023

Manu Kapur, Janan Saba and Ido Roll. (2023). Prior math achievement and inventive production predict learning from productive failure. npj Science of Learning 8:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00165-y

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

MANCOVA of seventh-grade PSLE math grade and total score revealed a significant multivariate effect between schools, F(2, 109) = 282.97, p < 0.001, confirming the sampling of schools with very different achievement profiles.

> "Compared to students from School B, students from School A achieved significantly higher PSLE scores, F(1, 110) = 464.42, p < 0.001, d = 5.34, and PSLE math grade, F(1, 110) = 110.44, p < 0.001, d = 2.12."
