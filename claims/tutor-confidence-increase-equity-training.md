## Evidence

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Do Tutors Learn from Equity Training and Can Generative AI Assess It?. In LAK25: The 15th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2025), March 03–07, 2025, Dublin, Ireland. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706531

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test on survey responses of the 35 of 81 tutors who completed the post-lesson confidence survey; confidence averaged 3.44 (SD = 1.09) before and 4.51 (SD = 0.56) after the lesson. The article reports t(34) = 6.82, p < .001; no effect size is printed.

> "A paired sample t-test of those 35 tutors revealed a statistically significant difference between pretest and posttest confidence level (t(34) = 6.82, p < .001), indicating a reliable improvement in tutors’ self-reported confidence in their knowledge attending to students experiencing potential inequities."

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025 (2)

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Do Tutors Learn from Equity Training and Can Generative AI Assess It?. In LAK25: The 15th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2025), March 03–07, 2025, Dublin, Ireland. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706531

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-lesson survey item (5-point Likert, strongly disagree to strongly agree) on the same 35 respondents, measuring perceived ability to apply lesson content in real-life tutoring. The mean of 4.71 (SD = 0.46) is descriptive; no test statistic is printed.

> "The average self-reported score for this measure was 4.71 (SD = 0.46), indicating a high level of perceived ability to apply the acquired knowledge from the scenario to their own real-life tutoring."
