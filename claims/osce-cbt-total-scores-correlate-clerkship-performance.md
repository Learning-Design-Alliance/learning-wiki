## Evidence

### Komasawa Nobuyasu 2020

Komasawa Nobuyasu, Terasaki Fumio, Nakano Takashi, Kawata Ryo. (2020). Relationships between objective structured clinical examination, computer-based testing, and clinical clerkship performance in Japanese medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230792

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective correlational analysis of scores from 94 medical students who took the OSCE and CBT in 2017 and the basic 32-week clerkship in 2018, compared using Pearson's correlation test. The article reports that "Total scores for OSCE and CBT were significantly correlated with CC scores"; no effect size is printed in this sentence.

> "Total scores for OSCE and CBT were significantly correlated with CC scores (P<0.001 each). When analyzed by OSCE components, medical interview and chest examination scores were significantly correlated with CC scores"
