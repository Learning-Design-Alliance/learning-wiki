q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Subgroup analyses of KS2 maths fine grade scores for boys, girls, FSM pupils, and pupils categorised as high, average or low achievers by dividing KS1 maths scores into three similar-sized levels (Table 5). All p-values ranged from 0.43 to 0.92; none significant.

"Further analyses were conducted for each sub-group: no significant impact of MathsFlip on KS2 maths scores was detected. Almost all outcomes had positive effect sizes but there was a fractionally negative effect of -0.01 for low achievers and -0.03 for high achievers. However, none of these effects were significant."
