## Evidence

### Tessa H. S. Eysink 2020

Tessa H. S. Eysink, Alieke M. van Dijk, Ton de Jong. (2020). BE COOL! a digital learning environment to challenge and socially include gifted learners. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09754-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Usability study in which 37 fourth, fifth, and sixth graders of varying ability levels worked on different types of inquiry assignments while thinking aloud, with actions and choices recorded on a checklist. The article reports that "gifted children needed less support than lower-ability children."

> "The study showed that, independently of age, gifted children needed less support than lower-ability children, and if support was needed, they were satisfied with more abstract prompts than the lower-ability children, who preferred and needed more concrete instructions for successful completion of the inquiry assignments."
