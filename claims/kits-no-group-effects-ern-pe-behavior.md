## Evidence

### McDermott 2017

McDermott, J. M., Pears, K. C., Bruce, J., Kim, H. K., Roos, L. E., Yoerger, K., & Fisher, P. A. (2017). Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. Applied Neuropsychology: Child. https://doi.org/10.1080/21622965.2017.1286239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on response-locked ERN for incorrect trials in the RCT ERP subsample. Only a time trend emerged; no group effect or interactions were significant. The authors note the ERN was not clearly delineated at T1 in this sample.

> "The main effect for time showed a trend toward significance with a more negative ERN at T2 (M = -0.76 µV, SD = 7.45 µV) compared to T1 (M = 1.46 µV, SD = 6.55 µV). Neither the main effect for group nor any of the interactions were significant."

### McDermott 2017 (2)

McDermott, J. M., Pears, K. C., Bruce, J., Kim, H. K., Roos, L. E., Yoerger, K., & Fisher, P. A. (2017). Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. Applied Neuropsychology: Child. https://doi.org/10.1080/21622965.2017.1286239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on the Pe for incorrect trials in the same subsample found no significant main or interaction effects, so the intervention did not change this internal error-processing component.

> "For the incorrect trials, there were no significant main or interaction effects."

### McDermott 2017 (3)

McDermott, J. M., Pears, K. C., Bruce, J., Kim, H. K., Roos, L. E., Yoerger, K., & Fisher, P. A. (2017). Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. Applied Neuropsychology: Child. https://doi.org/10.1080/21622965.2017.1286239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on percent correct on the flanker task (congruent vs. incongruent, T1 vs. T2) in the RCT subsample. Contrary to hypotheses, "neither a significant main effect of" group nor a significant time x group interaction emerged; equivalence was not tested.
