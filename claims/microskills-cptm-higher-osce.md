Peng Yizhong, Hu Juanjuan, Wang Minjie, Zhou Xing, Bing Kaijian, Li Song, Yu Haiyan. (2026). Evaluation of an integrated micro-skills training and clinical pathway model in surgical internship education. PeerJ 14:e21501. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21501

In the same assessor-blinded randomized trial, the OSCE comparison favored the experimental group, which "achieved a significantly better score (89.90 ± 4.00 vs. 84.99 ± 4.69)" with "Cohen’s d = 1.11".

> "In terms of OSCE, the experimental group also achieved a significantly better score (89.90 ± 4.00 vs. 84.99 ± 4.69; mean difference 4.91, 95% CI [3.35–6.47]; Cohen’s d = 1.11, 95% CI [0.74–1.47]; P < 0.001)"
