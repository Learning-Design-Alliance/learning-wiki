# The relationship between the WEC index and academic achievement is stable across student year levels and census years, with no meaningful interaction effects

## Evidence

### Marrone 2024

Marrone, Lam, Barthakur, Brinkman, Dawson, & Gabriel. (2024). The Relationship between Well-being and Academic Achievement: A Comprehensive Cross-Sectional Analysis of System Wide Data from 2016–2019. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8357

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

In the hierarchical mixed-model analysis, year-level-by-predictor and census-year-by-predictor interaction terms were modelled and no meaningful effects were attributed to either; the article reports this suggests the WEC–achievement relationship has been stable over time and over student year level.

> "Interaction effects of student year level and predictor, and census year and predictor were modelled (not shown) and no meaningful effects could be attributed to either interaction."
