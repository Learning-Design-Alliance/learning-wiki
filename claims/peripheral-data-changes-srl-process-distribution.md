# Adding peripheral data to navigational logs sharply changes the detected distribution of SRL processes, revealing many more Organisation and far fewer First-reading processes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Compared to Nav_only, the Enhanced_log channel detected 43.03% Organisation duration versus 0%, and 19.14% First-reading duration versus 65.09%. [→ Yizhou Fan 2022](#yizhou-fan-2022)

## Evidence

### Yizhou Fan 2022

Yizhou Fan, Lyn Lim, Joep van der Graaf, Jonathan Kilgour, Mladen Raković, Johanna Moore, Inge Molenaar, Maria Bannert, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević. (2022). Improving the measurement of self-regulated learning using multi-channel data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09304-z

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive comparison of SRL process duration medians across data channels for 25 participants in a 45-minute lab learning session. The article attributes the shift to more NOTE_EDITING and WRITE_ESSAY actions labelled "mostly based on keyboard strokes".

> "We detected many more Organisation processes (43.03% based on Enhanced_log compared to 0% based on Nav_only), and far fewer First-reading processes (19.14% based on Enhanced_log compared to 65.09% based on Nav_only)."
