`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports the Lillard et al. (2017) longitudinal lottery study in Hartford, CT, where children were equal on all measures at outset; by end of kindergarten Montessori children performed better in achievement and social cognition, and "the income–achievement correlation in Montessori was half" that of comparison schools.

> "The income–achievement correlation in Montessori was half what it was in business-as-usual schools, and even less than half what it was in the 16 other public magnet schools in the study"
