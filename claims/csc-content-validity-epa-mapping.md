Validating the Calgary Simulation Curriculum: A Retrospective Review of Face and Content Validity of a Surgical Simulation Curriculum in Otolaryngology—Head and Neck Surgery. `q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Phase I curriculum-mapping analysis of the five CSC sessions against the 2022 RCPSC EPA Guide for Otolaryngology—Head and Neck Surgery. The mapping showed that "The PAFB and NF/SPAL sessions aligned with both Foundations of Discipline and Core of Discipline EPAs" while ND, FESS, and rhinoplasty aligned primarily with Core EPAs. No effect sizes were reported for this document-analysis result.
