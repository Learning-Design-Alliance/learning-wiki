## Evidence

### Richmond Helen 2016

Richmond Helen, Hall Amanda M., Hansen Zara, Williamson Esther, Davies David, Lamb Sarah E. (2016). Using mixed methods evaluation to assess the feasibility of online clinical training in evidence based interventions: a case study of cognitive behavioural treatment for low back pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0683-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory RCT of 35 physiotherapists randomised to iBeST (n=15 with follow-up data) or workshop (n=16); knowledge measured with a bespoke multiple-choice questionnaire (scale 0–31). "no statistically significant difference between training groups on knowledge"; scores ranged 19.5–30 (iBeST) and 20–30.5 (workshop).

> "We found no statistically significant difference between training groups on knowledge (Mean Difference (MD) 0.97, 95 % CI −1.33, 3.26)). Scores ranged from 19.5 to 30 in the iBeST group, and from 20 to 30.5 in the workshop group."
