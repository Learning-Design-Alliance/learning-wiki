### Schulz 2011

Schulz, B. R., & McDonald, M. J. (2011). Weight Loss Self-Efficacy and Modelled Behaviour: Gaining Competence through Example. Canadian Journal of Counselling and Psychotherapy, 45(1), 53-67. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ930789

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The mixed-design ANOVA found a significant three-way interaction; treatment-homework participants' WEL means rose from Time 2 (M = 6.21) to Time 3 (M = 6.48), while control-homework participants dropped from M = 6.31 to M = 5.84.

> "Looking at mean scores for treatment-homework participants from Time 2, when the homework was assigned, to Time 3 mean WEL total scores, the increase in self-efficacy (MT2 = 6.21, MT3 = 6.48) was greatest for these individuals."
