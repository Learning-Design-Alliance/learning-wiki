q2 · i? · review · r?

Review synthesis of predictive studies (Box 1.1), citing University of California data showing high school grades predicted college GPA and graduation better than SAT scores, class rank, and family background. The review attributes the pattern to grades capturing "qualities of motivation and perseverance" and study habits.

"Likewise, high school grades are stronger and more consistent predictors of college persistence and graduation than college entrance examination scores or high school coursetaking (Geiser & Santelices, 2007; Roderick, Nagaoka, & Allensworth, 2006)."
