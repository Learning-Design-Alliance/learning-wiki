Evidence¶

Yao X-C and Fan L 2026¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Cycle-level Welch t-tests in the quasi-experimental two-class study. Diagnostic Review showed "means of 0.696, 0.721, and 0.775" versus Control means of 0.590, 0.619, and 0.619, with large standardized differences in each cycle.

"Diagnostic accuracy was higher in the Diagnostic Review group across all three cycles, with means of 0.696, 0.721, and 0.775, compared with 0.590, 0.619, and 0.619 in the Control group. Standardized group differences were large in each cycle (Hedges' g = 1.35, 1.17, and 1.71; all p < 0.001)."

Yao X-C and Fan L 2026 (2)¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Cycle-level comparisons of feedback actionability and comprehension. Actionability means were "3.42, 3.65, and 3.73" in Diagnostic Review versus 2.80–3.02 in Control; comprehension g values were 1.04, 1.33, and 1.36, all p < 0.001.

"Actionability means in the Diagnostic Review group were 3.42, 3.65, and 3.73 across the three cycles, compared with 2.80, 2.91, and 3.02 in the Control group. Hedges' g values ranged from 1.36 to 1.74, withp < 0.001 in all cycles."
