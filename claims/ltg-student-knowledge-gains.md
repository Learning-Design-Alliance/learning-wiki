## Evidence

### Seago 2013

Seago, N. (2013). Supporting Teachers' and Students' Knowledge of Geometric Similarity. Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584497

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Three-level hierarchical linear model of student assessment data from 162 treatment and 104 comparison students, accounting for nesting in classrooms. Gains were 6.55 percentage points higher for treatment students; the article reports effect size = 0.49 pooled standard deviation units.

> "Adding a time by group interaction term to the analysis revealed a significant difference (t = 2.31, p < .05) in gains between pre- and post-assessment scores for students of teachers who were engaged with LTG compared to students of teachers who were not involved in the PD"
