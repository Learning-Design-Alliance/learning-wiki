The impact of interactive digital tools on genetics education: a field study across large and small learning groups. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09168-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-activity survey item on the value of presenting normal alongside pathological cases. The authors interpret this as supporting "comparative reasoning as an effective learning approach in virtual cytogenetics education". Explanations after errors were also rated very useful by 59.81% in Zaragoza.

> "In Zaragoza, 57.62% of students responded very much, while 40.48% assessed positively, totaling a combined 98.1% of affirmative responses. Only 1.43% remained neutral, and just 0.48% responded negatively."
