# Theoretically augmented game designs significantly outperform standard versions of the same games (g = 0.34), with enhanced scaffolding designs showing g = 0.41

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 40 value-added effect sizes from 20 studies, enhanced game designs significantly outperformed standard versions (g = 0.34). [→ Clark Douglas B. 2016](#clark-douglas-b-2016)
`q3 i2` Enhanced scaffolding designs, the only well-represented category, showed a significant effect (g = 0.41). [→ Clark Douglas B. 2016 (2)](#clark-douglas-b-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Clark Douglas B. 2016

Clark Douglas B., Tanner-Smith Emily E., Killingsworth Stephen S. (2016). Digital Games, Design, and Learning: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315582065

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Value-added meta-analysis of 40 effect sizes from 20 studies comparing augmented game versions to equivalent standard versions, using the same RVE meta-regression models. The printed effect size is g = 0.34 (95% CI [0.17, 0.51]) favoring enhanced designs.

> "Overall, the comparison of all enhanced versions versus standard versions showed a significant positive effect size for the enhanced designs (g¯ = 0.34, 95% CI [0.17, 0.51])."

### Clark Douglas B. 2016 (2)

Clark Douglas B., Tanner-Smith Emily E., Killingsworth Stephen S. (2016). Digital Games, Design, and Learning: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315582065

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup of 20 scaffolding-enhancement effect sizes from 9 studies; the printed effect size is g = 0.41 (95% CI [0.18, 0.64]). Enhanced scaffolding was defined broadly to include personalized scaffolding, intelligent agents, and adapting game experiences to student needs. None of the other enhancement categories was individually significant, likely due to small numbers.

> "Specific comparisons of enhanced scaffolding demonstrated a significant overall effect size of similar magnitude to the overall value-added findings (g¯ = 0.41, 95% CI [0.18, 0.64])."
