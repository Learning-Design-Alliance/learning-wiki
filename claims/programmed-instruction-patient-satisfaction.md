## Evidence

### Zhang Ying 2026

Zhang Ying, Zhang Yixuan, Wang Xiaofang, Luo Baoping. (2026). Skinner's programmed instruction improves diabetes knowledge and skills of undergraduate nursing students: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09031-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

An 11-item Patient Satisfaction with Nursing Student Care questionnaire (adapted from the Risser scale, CVI = 0.89, α = 0.91) was completed by patients at discharge in the RCT; all 11 items favoured the experimental group (e.g. item 1: χ² = 8.66, P = 0.003).

> "For item 2, satisfaction rates were 75.0% (30/40) in the control group and 95.0% (38/40) in the experimental group (χ² = 6.27, P = 0.012). Overall satisfaction scores were significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group (P < 0.05)"
