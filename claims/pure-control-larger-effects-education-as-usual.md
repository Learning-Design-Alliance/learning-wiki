## Evidence

### Ngo Hazel 2025

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression of control group type in the pairwise meta-analysis (Table 2: pure control k = 47, SMD .71; education as usual k = 41, SMD .37; Q = 6.65, p = .04). The article reports "larger average effects were found for studies which used pure control groups" versus education-as-usual groups. This was not found in previous empathy meta-analyses.

> "a statistically significant effect was found for control group type: larger average effects were found for studies which used pure control groups (i.e., no intervention; d = 0.71, 95% CI: 0.49, 0.93) versus studies which used education-as-usual groups (d = 0.37, 95% CI: 0.25, 0.49)."
