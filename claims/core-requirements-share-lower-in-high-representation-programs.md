### Albert Lionelle 2024

Albert Lionelle, McKenna Quam, Carla Brodley, and Catherine Gill. 2024. Does Curricular Complexity in Computer Science Influence the Representation of Women CS Graduates?. In Proceedings of the 55th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 1 (SIGCSE 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3626252.3630835

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of degree-map requirements across representation groups. The article reports programs making their entire lower-division set a prerequisite for any upper-division course had extremely high complexity and often low representation of women, while higher-representation programs had significantly greater flexibility.

> "For Group 1, core CS courses6 made up 26% of the total credits required whereas, for Group 3, they make up 33% of total credits."
