## Evidence

### Vosniadou 2024

Vosniadou, S., Bodner, E., Stephenson, H., Jeffries, D., Lawson, M. J., Darmawan, I. G. N., Kang, S., Graham, L., & Dignath, C. (2024). The promotion of self-regulated learning in the classroom: a theoretical framework and an observation study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09374-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Utterance-level coding of teacher talk in 23 filmed lessons identified 1,126 direct SRL promotion instances (17 to 80 per teacher, mean 50). The article reports "Only 3.4% of the instances of direct strategy promotion involved explicit strategy promotion" and 6% involved knowledge/beliefs promotion.

> "A total of 1,126 instances of direct SRL promotion were identified. They ranged from 17 to 80 instances per teacher with a mean of 50. The most frequent SRL direct promotion types were implicit strategy, metacognitive support, and metacognitive reflection, with the frequency of each of these being around 30%."

### Vosniadou 2024 (2)

Vosniadou, S., Bodner, E., Stephenson, H., Jeffries, D., Lawson, M. J., Darmawan, I. G. N., Kang, S., Graham, L., & Dignath, C. (2024). The promotion of self-regulated learning in the classroom: a theoretical framework and an observation study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09374-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Of the 38 explicit strategy instances coded, most targeted cognitive capabilities and were domain-specific, usually delivered by direct verbal instruction or modeling, unlike other promotion types which tended to be domain-general.

> "Focusing on the teaching of explicit strategies we see that 85.5% of the total 38 instances observed referred to cognitive capabilities and 81.6% were domain-specific, an important difference from all other promotion types, which tended to be domain-general."
