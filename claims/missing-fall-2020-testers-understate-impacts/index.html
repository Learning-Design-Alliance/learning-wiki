Megan Kuhfeld 2021¶

Megan Kuhfeld, Erik Ruzek, Karyn Lewis, Jim Soland, Angela Johnson, Beth Tarasawa, & Lindsay Dworkin. (2021). Understanding the initial educational impacts of COVID-19 on communities of color. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/understanding-the-initial-educational-impacts-of-covid-19-on-communities-of-color/

Descriptive finding from the authors' prior analysis of the same NWEA data, reported in the introduction: "approximately one in four students who had tested last year were missing in the fall 2020 data." The report argues its earlier study therefore likely did not fully capture outcomes of the hardest-hit students, and this report used multiple imputation to address differential missingness.

"A key finding from our research was that approximately one in four students who had tested last year were missing in the fall 2020 data, and that a higher percentage of missing test takers were Black or Latinx and attended schools serving a higher proportion of students living in poverty."
