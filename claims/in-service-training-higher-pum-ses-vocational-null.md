# In-service training and courses are associated with higher PUM-SES scores, but training received during vocational education is not significantly related

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Nurses who received in-service training or took courses scored significantly higher on PUM-SES sub-dimensions and total than those who did not (p < 0.05). [→ Yurdagül Gülistan 2025](#yurdagul-gulistan-2025)
`q2 i?` PU management training received during vocational training showed no significant relationship with PUM-SES sub-dimension and total scores (p > 0.05). [→ Yurdagül Gülistan 2025 (2)](#yurdagul-gulistan-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Yurdagül Gülistan 2025

Yurdagül Gülistan (2025). Investigation of Nurse's Pressure Ulcer Management Self‐Efficacy Status and Influencing Factors: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal 22, no. 7: e70714. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70714

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons of PU training status against PUM-SES scores in the 436-nurse cross-sectional study (Table 3); in-service training and course comparisons each reached p = 0.001 on all sub-dimensions and total. No effect sizes printed.

> "It was seen that the scale sub‐dimension and total scores of those who received in‐service training and those who took a course were significantly higher than those who did not receive in‐service training and a course."

### Yurdagül Gülistan 2025 (2)

Yurdagül Gülistan (2025). Investigation of Nurse's Pressure Ulcer Management Self‐Efficacy Status and Influencing Factors: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal 22, no. 7: e70714. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70714

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing training received during vocational training with PUM-SES scores in the same study; all sub-dimension and total comparisons were non-significant (e.g., total U: 5879.0, p: 0.068). Equivalence was not tested.

> "it was seen that there was no significant relationship between the scale sub‐dimension and total scores (p > 0.05)"
