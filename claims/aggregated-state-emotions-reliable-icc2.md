# Most variance in students' momentary classroom emotions lies at the within-person level, yet person-aggregated state measures are fairly reliable (ICC(2) from 0.735 to 0.901)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ICC(1)s showed the majority of variance in momentary emotional experience was within-person, with ICC(1) ranging from 0.195 to 0.443 (Md = 0.255). [→ Keller 2025](#keller-2025)
`q3 i?` Person-aggregated emotional states were fairly reliable, with ICC(2)s ranging from 0.735 to 0.901 (Md = 0.82). [→ Keller 2025 (2)](#keller-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Keller 2025

Keller, M. M., Yanagida, T., Lüdtke, O., & Goetz, T. (2025). How Similar Are Students' Aggregated State Emotions to Their Self-Reported Trait Emotions? Results from a Measurement Burst Design Across Three School Years. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09995-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive multilevel analysis of the experience-sampling data (2668/1543/1258 state datapoints across waves). The article reports ICC(1)s "ranging between 0.195 (anger at T3) and 0.443 (pride at T3)", comparable to prior classroom-emotion studies.

> "the majority of variance in students' momentary emotional experience was on the within-person level as indicated by ICC(1)s ranging between 0.195 (anger at T3) and 0.443 (pride at T3) (Md ICC1 = 0.255)."

### Keller 2025 (2)

Keller, M. M., Yanagida, T., Lüdtke, O., & Goetz, T. (2025). How Similar Are Students' Aggregated State Emotions to Their Self-Reported Trait Emotions? Results from a Measurement Burst Design Across Three School Years. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09995-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same study's reliability analysis of aggregated state emotions via ICC(2) (Spearman-Brown applied to ICC(1) with number of measurements). The article concludes aggregated states were "fairly reliable, i.e., larger than 0.70".

> "findings suggest that using person-aggregated emotional states was fairly reliable, i.e., larger than 0.70, with ICC(2)s ranging from 0.735 (anger at T3) to 0.901 (pride at T3) (MdICC2 = 0.82)."
