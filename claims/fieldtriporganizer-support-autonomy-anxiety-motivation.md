## Evidence

### Noemi Mauro 2024

Noemi Mauro, Liliana Ardissono, Federica Cena, Livio Scarpinati, Gianluca Torta. (2024). An Intelligent Support System to Help Teachers Plan Field Trips. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34, 793–824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00366-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-task questionnaire (Q11) administered after each task in the 18-teacher within-subjects study; the article reports "Most participants (66.67%) claimed that our application is useful as a support" with M=3.89 versus M=1.61 for the baseline.

> "Most participants (66.67%) claimed that our application is useful as a support to organize a field trip (ratings ≥ 4, M=3.89). Only one person claimed that it was useless. In comparison, Klarna.Trips was rated very low (ratings ≤ 3, M=1.61) by 17 participants out of 18."

### Noemi Mauro 2024 (2)

Noemi Mauro, Liliana Ardissono, Federica Cena, Livio Scarpinati, Gianluca Torta. (2024). An Intelligent Support System to Help Teachers Plan Field Trips. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34, 793–824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00366-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anxiety item (Q13) of the post-task questionnaire in the same study; the article reports the anxiety-reduction goal "was reached in 55.5% of the cases" for FieldTripOrganizer versus failure in 77.78% of cases with Klarna.Trips.

> "According to the answers, the goal was reached in 55.5% of the cases, with ratings ≥ 4 (M=3.39). Only 1 person stated that the application did not work for this goal; however, in comparison, this happened in the 77.78% of cases when using Klarna.Trips (M=1.89)."

### Noemi Mauro 2024 (3)

Noemi Mauro, Liliana Ardissono, Federica Cena, Livio Scarpinati, Gianluca Torta. (2024). An Intelligent Support System to Help Teachers Plan Field Trips. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34, 793–824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00366-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Motivation item (Q14) of the post-task questionnaire; the article reports FieldTripOrganizer "motivated teachers in organizing science field trips in 77.78% of cases" (M=4.06) against M=1.94 for the baseline.

> "FieldTripOrganizer motivated teachers in organizing science field trips in 77.78% of cases: 15 users gave a rating ≥ 4 (M=4.06). Differently, Klarna.Trips received a high score from a single participant (M=1.94)."
