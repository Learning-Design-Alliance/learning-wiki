## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The percentage of correct answers on a 15-item multiple-choice knowledge assessment increased significantly from 46.7% pre-training to 86.7% post-training (P < 0.001). [→ Tokuno Junko 2025](#tokuno-junko-2025)

## Evidence

### Tokuno Junko 2025

Tokuno Junko, Knobovitch Ryan M., Botelho Fabio, Fried Howard B., Carver Tamara E., Fried Gerald M. (2025). Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation for Medical Student Procedural Training: Assessment of Cognitive Load and Usability. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251339920

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparison in the VR-trained subgroup (n = 22) using a 15-item multiple-choice assessment. The article reports the increase was "significant" with P < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "The percentage of correct answers in the knowledge assessments increased significantly from 46.7% (40.0-53.3) pre-training to 86.7% (80.0-90.3) post-training, (P < 0.001)."
