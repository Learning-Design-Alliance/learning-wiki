## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Repeated measures ANOVA found no significant main effect of time, dietary restraint, self-compassion, or writing task on positive affect (TPAS). [→ Dennis Relojo & Angelo Emil Gagani 2016](#dennis-relojo-angelo-emil-gagani-2016)

## Evidence

### Dennis Relojo & Angelo Emil Gagani 2016

Dennis Relojo & Angelo Emil Gagani. (2016). Power of Pen: Expressive Writing as an Intervention Tool on Exposure to Thin-Ideal Images with Dietary Restraint and Self-Compassion as Covariates. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 6, No. 3, July-September 2016. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131412

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the same 140-participant randomised design with positive affect (TPAS) as dependent variable. The article reports "no significant main effect" for time or any interaction despite large printed F values for some terms; no effect size supports a magnitude claim.

> "Data analysis reveals that there was no significant main effect based on time, F(1, 135) = 1.11, p = .293; time and dietary restraint, F(1, 135) = 24.28, p =  .163;  time and self-compassion, F(1, 135) = 7.70,p = .431; and, time and writing task, F(2, 135) = 42.58, p = .181."
