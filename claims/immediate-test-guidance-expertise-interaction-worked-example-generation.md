## Evidence

### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects test within the significant expertise x guidance interaction of the immediate-test ANOVA in this quasi-experimental school study: "the mean score for high guidance was significantly higher than for low guidance", d = .87, a worked example effect for novices.

> "for Year 10 students, the mean score for high guidance was significantly higher than for low guidance, t(42) = 3.18, SEdiff = 3.46, p = .002, d = .87 (see Figure 4), reflecting a worked example effect"

### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016 (2)

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects test for the more knowledgeable Year 11 group in the same immediate-test ANOVA: "the mean score for low guidance was significantly higher than for high guidance", d = .59, a generation effect.

> "for Year 11, the mean score for low guidance was significantly higher than for high guidance, t(48) = 2.16, SEdiff = 3.61, p = .04, d = .59 (see Figure 5), reflecting a generation effect"

### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016 (3)

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA on immediate test scores found no significant three-way interaction; equivalence was not tested. The expertise x guidance two-way interaction (F(1, 90) = 4.38, p = .039) carried the immediate-test pattern.

> "The three-way interaction of element interactivity, guidance and expertise was not significant, F(1, 90) = .489, MSe = 421.94, p = .486, Wilks' Lambda = .995, ηр²  = .005."
