### Lassen Christoph L. 2024

Lassen Christoph L., Jaschinsky Fabian, Stamouli Elena, Lindenberg Nicole, Wiese Christoph H. R. (2024). Anesthesiological Preoperative Interview with a Palliative Care Patient: A Simulation-Based Experiment Using Standardized Patients. Medicina (Kaunas). https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina60101577

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 32 recorded and transcribed standardized-patient interviews at a German university hospital: "Nausea and vomiting were noticed by all anesthesiologists spontaneously", and the article states almost all participants addressed the port catheter and pain control topics.

### Lassen Christoph L. 2024 (2)

Lassen Christoph L., Jaschinsky Fabian, Stamouli Elena, Lindenberg Nicole, Wiese Christoph H. R. (2024). Anesthesiological Preoperative Interview with a Palliative Care Patient: A Simulation-Based Experiment Using Standardized Patients. Medicina (Kaunas). https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina60101577

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 32-interview simulation study, "25% of the anesthesiologists recommending additional therapeutic measurements for pain perioperatively", mostly epidural analgesia; the fentanyl patch was left in place and no participant recommended removal.

> "When discussed, the fentanyl patch was left in place, with 25% of the anesthesiologists recommending additional therapeutic measurements for pain perioperatively (mostly regional anesthesia, i.e., epidural analgesia)."
