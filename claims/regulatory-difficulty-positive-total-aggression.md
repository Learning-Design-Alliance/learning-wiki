### Dong Minghua 2026

Dong Minghua, He Huitao, Wei Bowen. (2026). When digital engagement becomes dysregulated: associations with aggression, achievement motivation, and academic performance in adolescents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896602

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Raw-score linear regression in a cross-sectional study of 309 Chinese secondary students. Regulatory difficulty positively associated with total aggression (β = 0.380) and negatively with achievement motivation (β = −0.330). The "numerically largest standardized association was observed for verbal aggression" among exploratory dimensions.

> "Regulatory difficulty was positively associated with total aggression and negatively associated with achievement motivation. Among the exploratory aggression dimensions, the numerically largest standardized association was observed for verbal aggression"
