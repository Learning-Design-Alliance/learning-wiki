## Evidence

### Sajja 2026

Sajja, R., Sermet, Y., Fodale, B., & Demir, I. (2026). Evaluating AI-Powered Learning Assistants in Engineering Higher Education: Student Engagement, Ethical Challenges, and Policy Implications. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-usage survey ratings on a 5-point scale (N = 47, Figure 5) in two undergraduate civil and environmental engineering courses. The article reports "34% of students rated it as worse than human support" and only 17% as better.

> "When asked to evaluate the quality of help received from the Educational AI Hub, 34% of students rated it as worse than human support, while 49% felt it was about the same."

### Sajja 2026 (2)

Sajja, R., Sermet, Y., Fodale, B., & Demir, I. (2026). Evaluating AI-Powered Learning Assistants in Engineering Higher Education: Student Engagement, Ethical Challenges, and Policy Implications. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-usage survey convenience ratings (N = 47); 21% rated it equally convenient and 11% less convenient. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "A majority of students (68%) reported that the Educational AI Hub was more convenient to access compared to human assistance."

### Sajja 2026 (3)

Sajja, R., Sermet, Y., Fodale, B., & Demir, I. (2026). Evaluating AI-Powered Learning Assistants in Engineering Higher Education: Student Engagement, Ethical Challenges, and Policy Implications. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39237-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-usage survey comfort item (N = 47); 38% reported no difference and 15% felt less comfortable. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size printed.

> "Nearly half of the respondents (47%) indicated that they felt more comfortable using the AI Hub than asking for help from human resources."
