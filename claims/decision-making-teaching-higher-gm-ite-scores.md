## Evidence

### Miyagami Taiju 2025

Miyagami Taiju, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Shikino Kiyoshi, Kataoka Koshi, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Naito Toshio, Tokuda Yasuharu (2025). A cross sectional study of the relationship between clinical scores and timing of guidance for resident physicians in outpatient settings. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30748-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable regression with GM-ITE score as dependent variable, adjusting for gender, age, PGY, hospital location and type, beds, on-call frequency, inpatients, self-study time, and duty hours. The adjusted "EC: 2.05, 95% CI [1.15 to 2.94], p = 0.002" is reported.

> "when teaching was provided during the decision-making stage, the GM-ITE® score was significantly higher than those during other stages (EC: 2.05, 95% CI [1.15 to 2.94], p = 0.002; Table 2)."
