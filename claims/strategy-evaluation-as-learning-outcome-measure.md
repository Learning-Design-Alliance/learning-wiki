### Gupta 2018

Gupta, P. K., and Muhuri, P. K. (2018). Computing with words for student strategy evaluation in an examination. Granular Computing 4, no. 2, 167-184. [doi:10.1007/s41066-018-0109-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41066-018-0109-2)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design argument in the paper's introduction for the proposed Per-C based evaluation system: the strategy score enables "comparison and ranking of the students based on their performances" and the linguistic recommendation describes the strategy in words. The article offers this as a design rationale, not a tested empirical result; no effect size is printed.
