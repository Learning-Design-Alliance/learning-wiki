van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 65 peer-reviewed studies identified via an ERIC search following PRISMA procedures. The review reports: "the vast majority of studies in our sample (n = 61, 94 %) did not take into account the role played by social contextual factors." Only four articles (6%) explicitly described this role.

> "We also noted that the vast majority of studies in our sample (n = 61, 94 %) did not take into account the role played by social contextual factors."
