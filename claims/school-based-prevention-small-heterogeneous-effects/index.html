Evidence¶

Wilson 2001¶

Wilson, D.B., Gottfredson, D.C. & Najaka, S.S. (2001). School-Based Prevention of Problem Behaviors: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Quantitative Criminology. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1011050217296

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of 165 studies of school-based prevention activities. The synthesis reports that practices "appear to be effective in reducing alcohol and drug use, dropout and nonattendance, and other conduct problems." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"The study shows, however, that school-based prevention practices appear to be effective in reducing alcohol and drug use, dropout and nonattendance, and other conduct problems."

Wilson 2001 (2)¶

Wilson, D.B., Gottfredson, D.C. & Najaka, S.S. (2001). School-Based Prevention of Problem Behaviors: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Quantitative Criminology. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1011050217296

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analytic synthesis across four outcomes. The article reports the "average effect for each of the four outcomes was small" and heterogeneity persisted "even within program type after adjusting for measured method and population differences." No standardized effect size value is printed.

"The size of the average effect for each of the four outcomes was small and there was considerable heterogeneity across studies in the magnitude of effects, even within program type after adjusting for measured method and population differences."
