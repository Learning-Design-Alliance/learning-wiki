## Subclaims
`q2 i2` WBLP students rated teacher–student interaction (d = 0.54), patient–doctor communication (d = 0.48), and humanistic care (d = 0.34) significantly higher than controls. [→ Wang Ji-nuo 2026](#wang-ji-nuo-2026)

## Evidence

### Wang Ji-nuo 2026

Wang Ji-nuo, Lv Hanbing, Sun Yi, Cao Wen, Jiang Luhua. (2026). A workplace-based learning program on clerkship of internal medicine improves medical student's clinical competence: a retrospective comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08719-5

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Satisfaction questionnaire analysis (75 valid returns): "Cohen's d = 0.48" for patient communication and "Cohen's d = 0.54" for teacher–student interaction; humanistic care was also higher (4.18 ± 1.14 vs. 3.74 ± 1.42, p = 0.019, d = 0.34).

> "The WBLP group reported significantly better "communication with patients" (4.37 ± 0.85 vs. 3.84 ± 1.30, p = 0.040; Cohen's d = 0.48, 95% CI 0.03–0.94) and "teacher–student interaction" (4.39 ± 0.82 vs. 3.86 ± 1.13, p = 0.023; Cohen's d = 0.54, 95% CI 0.08–0.99)."
