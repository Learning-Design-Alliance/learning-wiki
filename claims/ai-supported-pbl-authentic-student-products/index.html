Schiner 2025¶

Schiner, N. (2025). Empowering the future: powerful technology for powerful AI teaching and learning [Project report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/261

q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative case description of Andrea Stanczyk's classroom project on environmental impact at YCHS, where students explored local issues and created awareness or solution products. The report describes the process narratively; no outcome measures are printed.

"Throughout the process, AI tools supported their work through generating draft images, suggesting layout options, and helping the students shape content through iterative prompting. Students would then analyze, revise, rethink, and refine what was generated and make it their own."
