`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory theme-level analysis in the ITT population using eight theme-specific t-tests with Bonferroni correction; effect sizes ranged from negligible to large (|d| = 0.064–0.970 in ITT), with heart murmur showing the largest effect. The article labels these analyses hypothesis-generating only.

> "Student performance was significantly better in the Student-gen exam in the heart murmur, upper gastrointestinal tract, and pregnancy curricular themes, while student performance was significantly better in the AI-gen exam in the nutrient absorption theme."
