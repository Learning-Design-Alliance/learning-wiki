## Evidence

### Simon Veenman 1996

Simon Veenman. (1996). Effects of multigrade and multi-age classes reconsidered. Review of Educational Research, 66(3), 323-340. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170526

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three studies assessing short-term and long-term effects of a staff development program for teachers in multigrade classes, using pretraining and posttraining classroom observation. The article reports a significant treatment effect for time-on-task and teacher skills, with no significant achievement differences against untrained single-grade classes; no effect size is printed.

> "Based on pretraining and posttraining classroom observa­tion, these studies revealed a significant treatment effect for the time-on-task levels of the students in the multigrade classes and for the instructional and management skills of the teachers. However, no significant differences in achieve­ment were found between the students in classes with trained multigrade teachers and the students in classes with untrained single-grade teachers"
