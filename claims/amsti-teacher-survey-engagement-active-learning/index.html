Surveys with Likert-based items were administered to teachers in both the treatment and comparison groups as part of the Newman et al. (2012) research. Findings are self-reported teacher perceptions, not independently measured outcomes.

"AMSTI teachers were more likely to report their students as engaged, had greater content knowledge in math or science, and increased their use of active learning streategies with students (by almost 25 minutes per week in math and 20 minutes per week in science)."
