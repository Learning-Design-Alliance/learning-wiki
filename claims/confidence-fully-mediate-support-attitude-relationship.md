`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up mediation analysis using the Baron and Kenny method and Sobel test on the same survey data: support predicted confidence (V = 0.58, p < .001), confidence predicted attitudes (V = 0.85, p < .001), and the direct support-attitude effect became non-significant (V = 0.03, p = .385), indicating full mediation.

> "The indirect effect of institutional support on attitudes through confidence was statistically significant (a× b = 0.4961), as confirmed by a Sobel test (z = 12.63, p < .001)."
