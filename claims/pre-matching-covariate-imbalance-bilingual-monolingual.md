## Evidence

### Goodrich 2019

Goodrich, J. M., Koziol, N. A., Yoon, H., & Leiva, S. (2019). Do Spanish-English Bilingual Children Outperform Monolingual English-Speaking Children on Executive Function Tasks in Early Childhood? A Propensity Score Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/edu

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Balance checks before matching in the ECLS-K:2011 subsample (N = 7,113 across 756 schools). Bilingual Spanish kindergarteners had lower household SES, lived in more dangerous neighborhoods, were read to less often, and attended schools with lower average SES than monolingual English kindergarteners.

> "There were statistically significant group differences on 27 of the 48 (56.3%) covariates, with SMDs of .25 in absolute value or greater for 17 (35.4%) of the covariates."
