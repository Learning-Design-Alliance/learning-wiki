# Female students with GPA below 3.0 show higher SCL-90 positive rates across all ten dimensions; male differences appear in only five dimensions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among female students, those with GPA below 3.00 had higher positive rates across all ten SCL-90 dimensions than those with GPA ≥ 3.00 (overall 47.1% vs 40.0%, p < 0.001). [→ Cen Shanduo 2025](#cen-shanduo-2025)
`q3 i?` Among male students with poor academic performance, higher positive rates appeared in five dimensions (SOM, HOS, PHOA, PSY, sleep/diet) and overall positivity (41.5% vs 37.2%, p = 0.043). [→ Cen Shanduo 2025](#cen-shanduo-2025)

## Evidence

### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of SCL-90 positivity rates by academic performance group (Fig. 2), analyzed separately by gender. The study reports "47.1% in the lower academic performance group versus 40.0% in the higher performance group (p < 0.001)" for females, and 41.5% vs 37.2% (p = 0.043) for males.

> "The overall positive rate for mental health symptoms was 47.1% in the lower academic performance group versus 40.0% in the higher performance group (p < 0.001)."
