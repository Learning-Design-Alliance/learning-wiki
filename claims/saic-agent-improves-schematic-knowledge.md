## Evidence

### Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna 2016

Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna. (2016). From Interactive Open Learner Modelling to Intelligent Mentoring: STyLE-OLM and Beyond. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:332–349. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0087-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled user study in an after-school reading club comparing two versions of a multimedia learning system, with and without the SAIC agent, using schema activation pre- and post-tests with children reading astronomy text. The article reports the "difference between control and experimental group was statistically significant, U-test"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that SAIC facilitated more improvements of students’ schematic knowledge in terms of developing more complete and more specific schemas of domain concepts (difference between control and experimental group was statistically significant, U-test)."
