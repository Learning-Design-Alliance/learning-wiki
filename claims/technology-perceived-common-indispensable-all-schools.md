## Evidence

### Sandra de Koster 2017

Sandra de Koster, Monique Volman, Els Kuiper. (2017). Concept-guided development of technology in 'traditional' and 'innovative' schools: quantitative and qualitative differences in technology integration. Education Tech Research Dev 65:1325–1344. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9527-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of three rounds of semi-structured focus group interviews with teacher teams at each school. The article reports "no differences were found between the school types" on these two labels, with teachers saying they 'could not do without' the technology.

> "With respect to the labels ‘technology per-ceived as common’ and ‘technology perceived as indispensable’ no differences were found between the school types."
