# Performance on inhibitory control measures improves with age, with some tasks reaching performance plateaus beginning around 4 years of age

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Average POM performance on inhibitory control measures improved with age, and some tasks plateaued at later ages beginning around 4 years, indicating ceiling effects for older children. [→ Petersen 2016](#petersen-2016)

## Evidence

### Petersen 2016

Petersen, I. T., Hoyniak, C. P., McQuillan, M. E., Bates, J. E., & Staples, A. D. (2016). Measuring the development of inhibitory control: The challenge of heterotypic continuity. Developmental Review, 40, 25-71. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dr.2016.02.001

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic POM-by-age curves (Figs. 1-3) across 198 studies show mean-level improvement with age and plateaus at later ages. The review notes researchers should be thoughtful about task choice at ages 4-8 years to avoid ceiling effects. No effect size is printed.

> "First, in general and as expected, performance on inhibitory control measures improved with age. Second, performance on some tasks reached plateaus at later ages (beginning in some cases around 4 years of age)."
