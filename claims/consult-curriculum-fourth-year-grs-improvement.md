## Evidence

### Carter Keme MD 2015

Carter Keme MD, Golden Andrew BS, Martin Shannon MD, Donlan Sarah MD, Hock Sara MD, Babcock Christine MD, Farnan Jeanne MD, Arora Vineet MD. (2015). Results from the First Year of Implementation of CONSULT: Consultation with Novel Methods and Simulation for UME Longitudinal Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.25520

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post simulation evaluation in the fourth-year EM clerkship: 117 students called 170 simulated consultations, each rated by two independent evaluators using the Global Rating Scale, creating 170 composite evaluations. The "Average GRS, increased significantly following the implementation of the curriculum (3.05 vs. 3.70, p<0.001)."

> "the combined average score of the criteria measured in the GRS, or Average GRS, increased significantly following the implementation of the curriculum (3.05 vs. 3.70, p<0.001)."
