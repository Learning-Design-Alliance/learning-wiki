## Evidence

### Wodarz M.N. 2025

Wodarz M.N., Komarova N.L., Ma T. (2025). The functional form of the association between K-12 student performance and household income in U.S. school districts. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0329296

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation coefficients between ELA and Math district scores computed within each state (Results, Fig 7(a),(b)). The article reports PCC >0.75 in more than three-fourths of states in 3rd grade and in more than half in 8th grade; these are descriptive correlations, not effect sizes with standardised magnitudes.

> "We observe a strong correlation in all the states in the 3rd grade, with more than three-fourths of all the states having PCC >0.75. The correlation weakens somewhat in the 8th grade but remains significant with more than half of the states with PCC >0.75."
