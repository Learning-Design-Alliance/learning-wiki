## Evidence

### Monod Cécile 2013

Monod Cécile, Voekt Cora A., Gisin Martina, Gisin Stefan, Hoesli Irene M. (2013). Optimization of competency in obstetrical emergencies: a role for simulation training. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-013-3111-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational study of six one-day simulation courses; 168 participants, 156 returned questionnaire a (92.9%) and 74 questionnaire b (36.3%), five-point Likert self-assessment compared with Fisher's exact test. The article reports "statistically significant improvement in self-perceived competency" for the first three questions only.

> "In the overall analysis, there was statistically significant improvement in self-perceived competency 3 months after the course compared to directly after the course for the first three questions (p ≤ 0.05). There was a trend toward improvement in team communication competency 3 months after the course compared to directly after the course; however, this did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.07)"
