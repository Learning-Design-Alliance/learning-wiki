## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing mean belonging and stigma consciousness across negative, zero, and positive masculinity discrepancy groups (Fig. 4). Parallel femininity analysis (Fig. 5) showed the negative femininity group lowest in belonging (F(2, 1800) = 14.30, p < 0.001) and highest in stigma consciousness (F(2, 1808) = 30.20, p < 0.001).

> "A one-way ANOVA indicated significant group differences in sense of belonging, F(2, 1854) = 12.20, p < 0.001. Tukey post hoc tests showed that the positive discrepancy group reported significantly lower sense of belonging than both the zero discrepancy group (p < 0.001) and the negative discrepancy group (p = 0.037)."
