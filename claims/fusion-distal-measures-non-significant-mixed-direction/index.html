Evidence¶

Strand Cary 2017¶

Strand Cary, M. G., Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Baker, S. K. (2017). A practitioner implementation of a Tier 2 first grade mathematics intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 211–224. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Time-by-condition analyses of the same 253-student quasi-experimental sample on four distal outcomes. The article reports "Positive non-significant effects" on EN-CBM and SAT 10 procedures and "Negative non-significant results" on easyCBM and SAT 10 problem solving, with the printed g values.

"Positive non-significant effects were found on the distal EN-CBM (p = .13; g = .34) and SAT 10 procedures (p = .71; g = .13) measures. Negative non-significant results were found on the distal easyCBM (p = .49; g = -.21) and SAT 10 problem solving (p = .51; g = -.27) measures."

  • The intervention produced no significant group differences on distal problem-solving measures, as the authors predicted because problem solving was not directly taught — related
