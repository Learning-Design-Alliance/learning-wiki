### Jonathan Bougie 2020

Jonathan Bougie, Asim Gangopadhyaya, Sherita Moses, Robert D. Polak, Gordon P. Ramsey, Weronika Walkosz. (2020). A First-Year Research Experience: The Freshman Project in Physics at Loyola University Chicago. arXiv:1906.03302. [doi:10.1119/10.0001611](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001611)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

First-person reflective vignette by Dr. Weronika Walkosz, who participated in FP as a student and later as a faculty mentor. She reports professional relationships, mentorship, communication skills, and continued research leading to scholarships; this is self-reported testimony with no quantitative measures.

> "First, my early involvement in research as a freshman student helpe d me become easily integrated into Loyola’s Physics community. By working closely with ot her freshmen and a mentor on a common project, I was able to form professional relat ionships that helped me navigate a demanding physics curriculum."
