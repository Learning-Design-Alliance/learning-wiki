## Evidence

### Haywood Xonné 2021

Haywood Xonné, Pienaar Anita Elizabeth. (2021). Long-Term Influences of Stunting, Being Underweight, and Thinness on the Academic Performance of Primary School Girls: The NW-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 18(17):8973. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA with post hoc Bonferroni adjustment in the NW-CHILD longitudinal study of 198 girls, comparing stunted (n = 26) and normal weight (n = 123) groups across grades 1, 4, and 7. The "S-group performed significantly poorer throughout (F = 10.43; p = 0.002)". No effect size was printed.

> "Significant group differences were only found between the stunted and normal weight group in all academic measures at each time point and over time, where the S-group performed significantly poorer throughout (F = 10.43; p = 0.002)."
