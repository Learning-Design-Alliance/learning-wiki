## Evidence

### Núñez 2017

Casañ Núñez, J. C. (2017). Testing audiovisual comprehension tasks with questions embedded in videos as subtitles: a pilot multimethod study. The EUROCALL Review, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.7062

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up Wilcoxon Signed Rank tests with Bonferroni-adjusted alpha (.025) on the 19 experimental test-takers' helpfulness rankings; the two low-ranked questions measured recognizing feelings and appeared at the end of the videos without a focalising character.

> "Wilcoxon tests revealed that embedded question 4 was ranked significantly lower than question 2, z = -2.311, p (exact, two tailed) = .024, r = -.375, and question 3, z = -2.443, p (exact, two tailed) = .018, r = -.396."
