# Pre- and post-test scores are positively correlated in the whole sample (r=0.63, p<0.01) and in the middle school subsample (r=0.63, p<0.01)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Pearson correlations between pre- and post-test scores were r=0.63 (p<0.01) for the whole sample and r=0.63 (p<0.01) for the middle school subsample, with a reported very strong relationship for the high school sample (r=0.95, p=0.08). [→ Corlu 2011](#corlu-2011)

## Evidence

### Corlu 2011

Corlu, M. S., Capraro, R. M., & Corlu, M. A. (2011). Developing computational fluency with the help of science: A Turkish middle and high school grades study. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2), 72-81. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932227

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis within the same quasi-experiment: "a correlation between pre- and post-test scores for the whole sample (r=0.63, p<0.01)" and the same for middle school; the high school correlation (r=0.95) was not statistically significant (p=0.08). These correlations fed into the effect size calculation.

> "there was a correlation between pre- and post-test scores for the whole sample (r=0.63, p<0.01), as well as for middle (r=0.63, p<0.01) and a very strong relationship for the high school sample (r=0.95, p=0.08)"
