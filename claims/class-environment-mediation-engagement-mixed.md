## Evidence

### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the mastery-avoidance-goal mediation model fitted to the 1,193-student survey, class environment significantly predicted learning engagement (β = 0.25, p < 0.001), alongside the strong perceived teacher support to class environment path (β = 0.47, p < 0.001).

> "Class environment also is significantly positively related to mastery avoidance goal (β = 0.30, p < 0.001) and learning engagement (β = 0.25, p < 0.001)."

### Liu Zihan 2026 (2)

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the mastery-approach-goal model of the same survey, the class environment path to learning engagement was not significant (β = 0.03, p = 0.20), so the mediator's direct path was inconsistent across the four goal-specific models.

> "Class environment is significantly positively related to mastery approach goal (β = 0.36, p < 0.001) but not on learning engagement (β = 0.03, p = 0.20);"
