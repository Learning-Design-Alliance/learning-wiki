# Flipped learning produced no statistically significant difference in post-test video-production skill and knowledge scores compared with traditional lectures

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-test scores of pre-service teachers taught with flipped learning did not differ significantly from those taught by traditional lectures (U=675.5, p=.407). [→ Almutairi et al. 2020](#almutairi-et-al-2020)
`q2 i?` Pre-test scores also showed no statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups (U=620.5, p=.168). [→ Almutairi et al. 2020 (2)](#almutairi-et-al-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Almutairi et al. 2020

Faisal M. Almutairi, Abdullah A. Almodaires & Alaa J. Zeyab. (2020). Effectiveness of Flipped Learning: Improving Pre-Service Teachers' Prowess in Producing Videos. International Education Studies, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v13n7p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 78 pre-service teachers (37 EG; 41 CG) in a six-week video-production workshop; Mann Whitney U test compared post-test scores between the flipped-learning group and the lecture-based control group, finding "no statistical difference (U=675.5; p=.407>.05)". No effect size was printed.

> "the results of the Mann Whitney U test applied to the post-test scores of the pre-service teachers in the experimental and control groups did not show any statistical difference (U=675.5; p=.407>.05)"

### Almutairi et al. 2020 (2)

Faisal M. Almutairi, Abdullah A. Almodaires & Alaa J. Zeyab. (2020). Effectiveness of Flipped Learning: Improving Pre-Service Teachers' Prowess in Producing Videos. International Education Studies, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v13n7p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same quasi-experiment's pre-test comparison using the Mann Whitney U test found "no statistical difference (U=620.5; p=.168>.05)"; the authors note the rank averages (43.23 vs 36.13) were not close, suggesting unequal starting levels, yet the null hypothesis was not rejected. No effect size was printed.

> "the results of Mann Whitney U test for the pre-test scores of the pre-service teachers in the experimental and control groups did not show any statistical difference (U=620.5; p=.168>.05)"
