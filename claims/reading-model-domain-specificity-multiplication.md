### Rautiainen 2026

Rautiainen, J., Aro, T., Aro, M., Tolvanen, A., & Koponen, T. (2026). Hierarchical Nature of Arithmetic Skills: Similarities and Differences Between Arithmetic Operations. Journal of Educational Psychology, Early online. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001066

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reading fluency contrast model in the longitudinal SEM study (n = 207), designed to probe domain-specificity because reading shares timed performance and retrieval demands but lacks numerical content. The article notes the reading construct consumed more covariance (β = .45) than addition (β = .30) and subtraction (β = .28); R2 = .33 (R2 = .40 prior residualization).

> "Remarkably, for working memory, no significant association existed; this pathway emerged in the arithmetic models in contrast to the baseline model. However, the reading fluency construct consumes more covariance (β = .45) than addition (β = .30) and subtraction"
