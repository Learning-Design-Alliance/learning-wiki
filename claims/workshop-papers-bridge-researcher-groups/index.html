q2 · i? · associational · r2

Network comparison of the organizers' coauthorship graph with and without the three workshop-created edges (Table 2). Average path length rose from 3.047 to 3.430 ("12.55%") and diameter from 5.000 to 6.000 ("20.00%"), while transitivity changed only "2.14%".

"the removal of workshop papers eliminated only three coauthorship edges out of approximately 4,000 total edges. Despite this minimal reduction in the number of collaborations, several indicators of overall network connectivity changed. In particular, the average path length"
