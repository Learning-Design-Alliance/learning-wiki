`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

High School English study with 94 seniors: teachers' grades in English for the previous semester served as criterion English Ability E. The highest raw correlation was with Thorndike-McCall Reading (+0.40 ± 0.05 in the table, 0.49 per the conclusion); the article offers three possible explanations, including low reliability of teachers' judgments.

> "As far as raw correlations with English ability are concerned, using Teachers' grades in English as the criterion, none of the correla- tions for any of the tests is marked, the highest being 0.49."
