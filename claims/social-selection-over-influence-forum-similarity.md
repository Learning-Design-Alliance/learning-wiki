## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Stochastic actor-oriented modelling shows that learners interacting over several semesters are more likely to form ties with similar-performing peers (social selection) than to change their performance toward peers' performance (social influence). [→ Poquet 2021](#poquet-2021)

## Evidence

### Poquet 2021

Poquet, O., Nguyen, Q., Kovanovic, V., Brooks, C., Dawson, S., & Biotteau, A. (2021). Social Architectures of University Forums: Grade-Based Similarity in Online Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09675

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

SAOM analysis of repeated interaction networks found that "learners interacting over several semesters were more likely to interact with others similar to them in performance (social selection)" rather than converging toward peers' performance levels.

> "Modelling showed that learners interacting over several semesters were more likely to interact with others similar to them in performance (social selection), rather than be change their performance towards that of the peers they interact with (social influence)."
