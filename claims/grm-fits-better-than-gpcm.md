## Evidence

### Lai Jinjia 2025

Lai Jinjia, Chen Hongyi, Huang Yongqi, Ma Chaoqun, Zeng Ping, Chen Hanxi, Tan Yibing. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Core Competencies in Disaster Nursing Scale for Disaster Rescue Nurses in Mainland China. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9957270

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Model-fitting comparison of two polytomous IRT models on the 401-nurse response data; Table 4 prints AIC 21232.99 vs 21522.92, BIC 21891.99 vs 22181.92, and logLik −10451.49 vs −10596.46, favouring the graded response model.

> "Compared with GPCM, the AIC and BIC of GRM were smaller, and the value of log-likelihood (logLik) was larger, indicating that the fitting effect of the GRM was better (Table 4). Therefore, GRM is used for IRT in this study."
