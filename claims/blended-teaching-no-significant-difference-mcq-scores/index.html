Zhang Z 2024¶

Zhang Z, Zhang Z, Yu Y, Jiang M, Deng L, Guo L. (2024). Impact of different teaching modes on medical students' performance under the scoring criteria for multiple-choice questions: A meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041008

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials and crossover trials comparing blended teaching with traditional teaching. The pooled analysis found "the multiple-choice test scores in blended teaching were lower than those in traditional teaching, but there was no significant difference".

"The analysis results indicated that the multiple-choice test scores in blended teaching were lower than those in traditional teaching, but there was no significant difference, with a SMD of −0.63, 95% CI (–1.85, 0.59)"
