## Evidence

### Ghassemi 2013

Mojtaba Ghassemi. (2013). The impact of Cooperative Materials Adaptation on Listening Comprehension Performance of Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n2p45

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First administration of the attitude questionnaire before treatment, analyzed with Chi-Square (Pearson Chi-Square 138.895, df 4, Sig. .000) and Spearman's rho (.620 positive, .848 negative with total). The author concludes students had "an overall negative attitude toward listening" at baseline.

> "the correlation between the overa ll score and the negative section is much higher than the correlation between the overall score and the score of the positive section at the significant level of 0.01. This suggests that the students have an overall negative attitude toward listening."
