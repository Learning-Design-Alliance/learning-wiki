## Evidence

### Burkow TM 2015

Burkow TM, Vognild LK, Johnsen E, Risberg MJ, Bratvold A, Breivik E, Krogstad T, Hjalmarsen A. (2015). Comprehensive pulmonary rehabilitation in home-based online groups: a mixed method pilot study in COPD. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1713-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre–post analysis of SGRQ scores for n = 9 participants (one patient participating <6 weeks excluded), using paired t tests; the “mean change in the SGRQ total score of −6.53” with p = 0.04 exceeded the four-unit clinical threshold. No standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Only the mean change in the SGRQ total score of −6.53 (CI 95 % −0.38 to −12.68, p = 0.04) indicates a probable clinically significant effect."

### Burkow TM 2015 (2)

Burkow TM, Vognild LK, Johnsen E, Risberg MJ, Bratvold A, Breivik E, Krogstad T, Hjalmarsen A. (2015). Comprehensive pulmonary rehabilitation in home-based online groups: a mixed method pilot study in COPD. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1713-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same pre–post analysis showed non-significant changes in the symptoms (−8.61, p = 0.21), activity (−3.13, p = 0.22) and impact (−6.79, p = 0.06) domains; the article calls these differences “not significant” and the overall outcome results inconclusive.

> "The improvement in the other SGRQ scores is promising, even though the differences are not significant."
