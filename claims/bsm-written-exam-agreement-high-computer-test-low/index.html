Evidence¶

Millán-Valldeperas 2013¶

Millán-Valldeperas, E.; Descalço, L.; Castillo, G.; Oliveira, P.; Diogo, S. (2013). Using Bayesian networks to improve knowledge assessment. Elsevier. http://hdl.handle.net/10630/7068

q2 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation study in which 152 students took both a PMatE computerized test and a written exam graded by three experts; Bayesian student models estimated knowledge on the same 12 concepts and "inter-rater agreement" was computed across measures. The article reports a high degree of agreement for the written-exam BSM and a low degree for the computer-test BSM; no effect size is printed.

"Results show a high degree of agreement among the scores given by the experts and also among the diagnosis provided by the BSM in the written exam and expert’s average, but a low degree of agreement among the diagnosis provided by the BSM in the computer test and expert’s average"
