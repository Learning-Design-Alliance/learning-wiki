Evidence¶

Kate Place 2023¶

Kate Place, Andrew Gothro, Gregory Chojnacki, Kara Conroy. (2023). Testing New Approaches to Math Tutoring: Lessons from Eight Evaluations. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/testing-new-approaches-to-math-tutoring-lessons-from-eight-evaluations

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Cross-program analysis of student reports across the eight tutoring evaluations. The brief reports that in high-attendance, positive-effect programs, "a large share of students reported strong relationships and a sense of belonging in their tutoring sessions." No percentages or effect sizes are printed.

"Strong student–tutor relationships may be a key component of successful tutoring. In programs with high attendance and positive effects on learning, a large share of students reported strong relationships and a sense of belonging in their tutoring sessions."
