## Evidence

### Hermans 2008

Hermans, R., Tondeur, J., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). The impact of primary school teachers’ educational beliefs on the classroom use of computers. Computers & Education, 51(4), 1499–1509. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.02.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the final multilevel model, gender, computer experience and general computer attitudes each remained significant determinants of classroom computer use among the 525 teachers. The article reports the χ² statistics shown but no standardised effect sizes for these predictors.

> "Other signiﬁcant determinants of the ‘Class Use of Computers’ were ‘Gender’ ( v2 = 12.96, d f =1 , p < 0.001), ‘Computer Experience’ ( v2 = 11.85, d f =1 , p < 0.001), and ‘Computer Attitudes’ ( v2 = 5.71, df =1 , p < 0.05)."
