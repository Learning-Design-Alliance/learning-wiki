## Evidence

### Haelermans Carla 2022

Haelermans Carla, Korthals Roxanne, Jacobs Madelon, de Leeuw Suzanne, Vermeulen Stan, van Vugt Lynn, Aarts Bas, Prokic-Breuer Tijana, van der Velden Rolf, van Wetten Sanne, de Wolf Inge. (2022). Sharp increase in inequality in education in times of the COVID-19-pandemic. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0261114

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's narrative review of country studies: Italy (15 weeks closed) reports 0.19 SD losses, the Netherlands 0.08 SD, while Belgium with only 8.5 weeks closed reports a 0.19 SD mathematics reduction similar to Italy. The authors conclude "more research based on country comparisons is needed".

> "Altogether, more research based on country comparisons is needed to be able to state that longer lockdowns result in larger learning deficits and an increase in educational inequalities."
