# Self-perceived TPACK-21 domain scores do not differ significantly by faculty age group or appointment type

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-way ANOVAs found no statistically significant differences across any TPACK domain by age group (all p > .05, η² ≤ .05) or by career type (all p > .05, η² ≤ .01). [→ Sutedjo A 2026](#sutedjo-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Sutedjo A 2026

Sutedjo A, Chowdhury M and Liu SP (2026) Assessing faculty self-perceived knowledge in using generative AI to teach 21st-century skills. Front. Educ. 11:1913310. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1913310

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Age-group ANOVAs (Tables 5) and career-type ANOVAs (Table 6) in the 127-faculty survey; career-type effect sizes were "negligible to small (η² ≤ .01)". Equivalence was not tested; only non-significance reported.

> "The analyses indicated no statistically significant differences across any of the TPACK domains (p > .05). Effect sizes were uniformly small (η² ≤ .05), suggesting minimal practical variation in technological, pedagogical, content, or integrated knowledge across age groups."
