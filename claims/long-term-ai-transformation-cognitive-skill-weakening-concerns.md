### Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' synthesis of the long-term effects theme across both interview groups. Participants anticipated structural change in education and professions, with weakening of skills, economic and professional uncertainties, and calls to update curricula reported for both groups.

> "faculty members and students shared common concerns that AI could weaken cognitive skills in the long term, that curricula need to be updated, and that uncertainties regarding professional life have arisen."
