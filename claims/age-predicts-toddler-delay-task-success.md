## Evidence

### Mulder 2019

Mulder, H., van Ravenswaaij, H., Verhagen, J., Moerbeek, M., & Leseman, P. P. M. (2019). The process of early self-control: an observational study in two- and three-year-olds. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 239–264. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09199-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary logistic regression of snack delay performance on age, gender, chair height, and caregiver presence (χ2(4) = 20.4, p < .001); age was the only significant predictor. For gift delay (χ2(4) = 13.8, p = .017), age was again the only significant predictor (B = −0.17, p = .012).

> "Age was the only significant predictor, as older children had lower odds of failing the task (B =−0.24; SE =0.12; p < .001; 95% CI of B = −0.54 to −0.14)."
