## Evidence

### M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna 2014

M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna. (2014). Reform-Based-Instructional Method and Learning Styles on Students' Achievement and Retention in Mathematics: Administrative Implications. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 2(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.2n.2p.48

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test randomized control group study of 119 year-one NCE students in Anambra State; experimental group (n=61) taught coordinate geometry with reform-based method, control (n=58) with traditional expository method. The ANCOVA showed "the f-calculated value for achievement is 45.712" with p = .000, so the null hypothesis was rejected.

> "the f-calculated value for achievement is 45.712 with probability value of 0.000 which is less than 0.05. The hypothesis was therefore not retained. Hence, the students' achievement in the unit of Mathematics studied was significantly influenced by the instructional method used."
