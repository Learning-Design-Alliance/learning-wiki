Reiter Manuel 2025¶

Reiter Manuel, Busch Dorothee, Erfurt‐Berge Cornelia. (2025). Educational Interventions for Medical Students in Chronic Wound Care—A Scoping Review. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70760

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's own study-identification results: 3353 records found, 605 duplicates removed, 40 full texts screened, and "a total of nine studies were eligible for inclusion" after PRISMA-ScR screening by three independent reviewers.

"Six databases were searched and a total of 3353 papers were identified. Six hundred five duplicates were removed and from the 2748 studies, a total of 40 remained for full‐text screening after the abstract/title check and application of exclusion criteria. After the final full‐text screening process, a total of nine studies were eligible for inclusion"
