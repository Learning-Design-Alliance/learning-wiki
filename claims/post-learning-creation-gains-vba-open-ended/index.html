Rohaeti 2019¶

Rohaeti, E.E., Bernard, M., & Primandhika, R.B. (2019). Developing interactive learning media for school level mathematics through open-ended approach aided by Visual Basic application for Excel. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 59-68. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1204890

Descriptive analysis of the 68 students' media products before and after the ICT Application course. The article reports "the gain of creativity of their creat ion" from an initial 8.69% independent creation and 60.86% example-dependence to 31.43% new-idea creation and 20% animation-based props after learning.

Related Claims¶

  • On an essential mathematical concept mastery test, 73.91% of the 68 students understood arithmetical operations while 60.86% followed the example picture rather than creating their own — related
  • Students' creativity in creating interactive learning media combines mastery of school-level mathematics with the ability to apply Visual Basic Application for Excel — related
  • Prospective-teacher students learning with an open-ended VBA-for-Excel approach created more creative interactive learning media than students taught by an expository VBA-based method — related
  • Post-learning media products spanned multiple mathematics topics: 42.8% geometry-related props, 28.57% arithmetic-related props, 51.43% could explain prop creation, and 60% could use shapes to run their code — related
