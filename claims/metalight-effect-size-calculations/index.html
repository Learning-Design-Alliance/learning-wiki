Evidence¶

Thomas James 2012¶

Thomas James, Graziosi Sergio, Higgins Steve, Coe Robert, Torgerson Carole, Newman Mark. (2012). Teaching meta-analysis using MetaLight. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/1756-0500-5-571

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article describes MetaLight's analytical functionality in the Results and discussion section. The software computes six named effect size types from continuous or binary two-group data, with Hedges g and mean difference calculated from group sample sizes, means and standard deviations.

"It calculates the following effect sizes from either continuous or binary data assuming a standard two group (typically intervention and control) study design: Hedges g, r (correlation), mean difference, odds ratio, risk ratio, risk difference."
