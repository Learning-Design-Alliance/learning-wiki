## Evidence

### de Goede 2026

de Goede, M. L., Boelen, P. A., Litz, B. T., & ter Heide, F. J. J. (2026). Mapping moral injury: Development and feasibility of the Clinical Interview for Moral Injury—military version (CIMI-M). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359553

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

General Inductive Approach analysis of semi-structured debrief interviews with eight veterans and seven clinicians, coded from 27 codes to 10 labels grouped under feasibility, acceptability, and clinical utility. The clinical-utility theme records that the interview "helped them express their experiences"; no quantitative effect sizes are reported.

> "Veterans mentioned that sharing their story was hard but found that the interview helped them express their experiences and the subsequent impact, both during the study and subsequent treatment."
