## Evidence

### Kjellenberg Karin 2025

Kjellenberg Karin, Helgadóttir Björg, Ekblom Örjan, Nyberg Gisela. (2025). Fitness and Screen Time at Age 13 Relates to Academic Performance at Age 16. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70024

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multilevel logistic regression in the same longitudinal cohort (n = 1007), with low weekday screen time as reference; the article reports "odds ratio: 0.401" for the high versus low weekday screen time contrast, p = 0.001.

> "adolescents who had high screen time (≥ 5 h) during weekdays at age 13 had lower odds of receiving grades A–D at age 16 (odds ratio: 0.401), compared to the low screen time group (≤ 2 h)."
