## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within 1478 twins adjusted for smoking, education–methylation effect sizes correlated with population effect sizes (r = 0.83) and averaged 0.76 of population estimates; in monozygotic pairs they fell to 0.28 of population estimates. ## Evidence

### Jenny van Dongen et al 2018

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-pair analysis in 1478 twins (492 monozygotic and 247 dizygotic pairs, mean age 37) regressing within-pair methylation differences on within-pair educational-attainment differences, adjusted for smoking, which controls shared prenatal/childhood environment and part of genetic variation.

> "The effect sizes within twin pairs were on average 0.76 of the effect sizes in the entire population"
