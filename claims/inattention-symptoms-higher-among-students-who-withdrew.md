## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Compared to participants who withdrew before graduating, participants who graduated had lower inattention symptoms (d = 0.10), but not lower hyperactivity or impulsivity symptoms. [→ Henning Colin 2021](#henning-colin-2021)

## Evidence

### Henning Colin 2021

Henning Colin, Summerfeldt Laura J., Parker James D. A. (2021). ADHD and Academic Success in University Students: The Important Role of Impaired Attention. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547211036758

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Mixed ANOVA (gender × graduation status × ADHD symptom type) on CAARS scores from 3,688 first-year undergraduates, with graduation status drawn from official registrar records six years later. The interaction showed "participants who graduated had lower inattention symptoms (d = 0.10)"; the effect was small by Cohen's conventions.

> "There was also a significant interaction between type of ADHD symptomatology and graduation status, F(1, 7368) = 4.09, p = .017, ηp2 = .001, with planned comparisons showing that, compared to participants who withdrew before graduating, participants who graduated had lower inattention symptoms (d = 0.10), but not hyperactivity or impulsivity symptoms."
