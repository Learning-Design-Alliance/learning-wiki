Evidence¶

Escalante 2023¶

Escalante, J., Pack, A., & Barrett, A. (2023). AI-generated feedback on writing: insights into efficacy and ENL student preference. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00425-2

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Six-week repeated-measures quasi-experiment: experimental group (n = 23) received GPT-4 feedback, control group (n = 25) received human tutor feedback. The RM-ANOVA found "no significant interaction effect between group and time" (F = 3.094, p = 0.085, ηp2 = 0.063).

"The results of the mixed 2 × 2 RM-ANOVA analysis (Table 2) revealed there was no significant interaction effect between group and time (F = 3.094, p = 0.085, ηp2 = 0.063)"
