# California residency program completers are more likely to identify as candidates of color than completers of other preparation pathways

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across CTC program completer survey data, residency-prepared candidates were more likely to identify as candidates of color than candidates from other pathways. [→ Yun 2025](#yun-2025)

## Evidence

### Yun 2025

Yun, C., & Fitz, J. (2025). Successful teacher residencies: What matters and what works. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/480.783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of California Commission on Teacher Credentialing 2021–22 and 2022–23 program completer survey data, including programs not funded by the TRGP, found residency completers "more likely to identify as candidates of color" than other-pathway completers. No effect size is printed.

> "Candidates who were prepared through a residency were more likely to identify as candidates of color and rate their preparation as very effective compared with those who took other pathways."
