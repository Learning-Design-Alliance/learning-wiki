# Optimal behaviour is more frequent after wins than after losses, but the after-win advantage is absent in the Post phase and under high reinforcement

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, after-win OBR was significantly higher than after-loss OBR in Pre and During phases but not in Post. [→ Zhang 2026](#zhang-2026)
`q3 i?` Across experiments, OBR proportions tended to be higher after wins than losses, though the after-win advantage was not present under high reinforcement (75%, 88%). [→ Zhang 2026 (2)](#zhang-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase x Outcome interaction in Experiment 1's three-way repeated measures ANOVA (n = 76): after-win OBR exceeded after-loss OBR in Pre and During but not Post, per Tukey's HSD; no effect size printed.

> "after-win OBR was significantly higher than after-loss OBR in Pre (69.19% vs. 62.62%) and During (61.61% vs. 53.20%), but not in the Post phase (67.87% vs. 64.75%; Tukey's HSD; p > .05)."

### Zhang 2026 (2)

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 (44% condition, n = 70 analysed) replicated the outcome main effect: OBR was higher after wins (66.62%) than losses (63.34%); descriptive means only, no effect size printed.

> "As in Experiments 1 (50%) and 2 (25%), OBR was higher after wins (66.62%) relative to losses (63.34%)."
