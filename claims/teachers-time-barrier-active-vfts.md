Norris E., Shelton N., Dunsmuir S., Duke-Williams O., Stamatakis E. (2015). Teacher and pupil perspectives on the use of Virtual Field Trips as physically active lessons. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1698-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of teacher interviews (n=12) identified time as the leading barrier; some teachers reported PE classes being removed in favour of other academic lessons, and one summarised the timetable as too full to fit more in.

> "All teachers described children as insufficiently active during school hours due to a "packed curriculum" and academic pressures."
