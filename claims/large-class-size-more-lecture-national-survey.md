## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Class size has a small-to-medium effect on percentage of class time spent in lecture, with the largest classes (100+ students) showing the highest percentage of lecture. [→ Apkarian 2021](#apkarian-2021)

## Evidence

### Apkarian 2021

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on self-reported percentage of class time spent lecturing across six enrollment bins in a national survey of introductory chemistry, calculus, and physics instructors. The article reports a "small/medium" effect (η2 = 0.04) and states that "the largest classes (those with 100+ students) have the highest percentage of lecture."

> "Class size is a main effect on lecture, F(5,3630) = 27.9, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.04 (small/medium)."
