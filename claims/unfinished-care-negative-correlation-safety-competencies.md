`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the cross-sectional study of 1442 students. The article reports "overall missed nursing care (UNC4S) was weakly negatively correlated with all patient safety dimensions" with subscale-level associations "generally small and inconsistent".

> "In contrast, overall missed nursing care (UNC4S) was weakly negatively correlated with all patient safety dimensions (r = −0.107 to −0.171; p < 0.001), reflecting weak inverse relationships between unfinished nursing care and perceived competencies."
