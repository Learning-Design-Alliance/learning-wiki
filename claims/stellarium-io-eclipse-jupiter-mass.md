# Students using Stellarium eclipse timing of Io determine Jupiter's mass as MJ = (1,9 ± 0,5) × 10^27 kg, consistent with the literature value

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r?` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Data collected in Stellarium over one month yield a mean orbital period for Io of <T> = (42,5 ± 0,3) hours and a mean orbital radius <r> = (42 ± 4) × 10^4 km, from which the mass of Jupiter is obtained via Kepler's third law and Newtonian gravitation. [→ Oliveira 2020](#oliveira-2020)
`q2 i?` The obtained Jupiter mass agrees with the literature value of about 1,898 × 10^27 kg. [→ Oliveira 2020 (2)](#oliveira-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Oliveira 2020

Oliveira, A.M., Kemeicik, C., Monteiro, A.C.T., Benincá, T.S., Mattos, C.D.S., Schmidt, G.L. (2020). Sequências didáticas para o ensino de Astronomia utilizando o Stellarium. Cadernos de Astronomia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.01157](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.01157)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Data-collection activity in the J5–J6 steps of the didactic sequence, using Stellarium eclipse timings of Io and distances measured from a Jupiter-surface reference frame. The mean period was found as "<T >= (42, 5 ± 0, 3) horas" and the mean orbital radius as <r> = (42 ± 4) × 10^4 km, with standard-deviation error estimates.

> "A partir dos dados expostos na tabela acima, encontramos o período orbital médio de Io, o qual é <T >= (42, 5 ± 0, 3) horas."

### Oliveira 2020 (2)

Oliveira, A.M., Kemeicik, C., Monteiro, A.C.T., Benincá, T.S., Mattos, C.D.S., Schmidt, G.L. (2020). Sequências didáticas para o ensino de Astronomia utilizando o Stellarium. Cadernos de Astronomia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.01157](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.01157)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Substituting the measured period and orbital radius into the Kepler/Newton relation, the sequence obtained MJ = (1,9 ± 0,5) × 10^27 kg; the article states this "está de acordo com o resultado esperado e apresentado na literatura".

> "O valor encontrado para a massa de Júpiter está de acordo com o resultado esperado e apresentado na literatura, algo em torno de 1, 898 × 1027kg."
