# Video-modeling of collaboration, especially with prompted reflection, produced more lasting learning about collaboration than scripted practice once script supports were withdrawn

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl 2008

Gerry Stahl. (2008). Yes we can! Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9055-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial describes the Rummel, Spada and Hauser experiment with dyads of a medical student and a psychology student taught to share complementary expertise across five conditions — scripting, modeling, each with prompted self-explanation, and control — and compares outcomes after supports were withdrawn. "The results raised doubts about scripting."

> "at that point it seemed that students had more lasting learning outcomes about how to collaborate by watching the video model — especially with prompted reflecting on it — than by being marched through a scripted process."
