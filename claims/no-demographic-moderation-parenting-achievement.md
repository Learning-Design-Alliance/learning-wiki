## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No significant interaction was found between parenting style and age, gender, school type, religion or parents' education in predicting achievement; moderation was not detected. [→ Hayek J 2022](#hayek-j-2022)

## Evidence

### Hayek J 2022

Hayek J, Schneider F, Lahoud N, Tueni M, de Vries H. (2022). Authoritative parenting stimulates academic achievement, also partly via self-efficacy and intention towards getting good grades. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265595

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analyses using SPSS macro PROCESS model 1 in the two-wave sample (n = 345), testing interactions of each demographic variable with parenting style and the mediators. No interaction reached significance; equivalence was not separately tested.

> "For each potential moderator, four interactions were tested: 1) an interaction with parenting style on academic achievement, 2) an interaction with parenting style on the mediator (socio-cognitive factor), 3) an interaction with the mediator on parenting style, 4) an interaction with the mediator on the sequential mediator. There was no significant interaction with any of the chosen demographic variables."
