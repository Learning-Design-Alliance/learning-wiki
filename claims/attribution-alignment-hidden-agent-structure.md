## Evidence

### Alexandra O. Cohen 2020

Alexandra O. Cohen, Kate Nussenbaum, Hayley M. Dorfman, Samuel J. Gershman and Catherine A. Hartley. (2020). The rational use of causal inference to guide reinforcement learning strengthens with age. npj Science of Learning 5:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00075-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic mixed-effects analysis of trial-wise hidden-agent attributions in the gold-mining task (N = 90, ages 7–25). The interaction between reward outcome and territory was significant, "p < 0.0001", confirming attributions tracked each environment's structure.

> "there was a signi ﬁcant reward outcome by territory interaction ( χ2(2, N = 90) = 87.69, p < 0.0001) indicating that participants attributed negative outcomes most often to the Robber, to a lesser extent to the Sheriff, and rarely to the Millionaire"
