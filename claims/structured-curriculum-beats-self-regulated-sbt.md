The review describes a randomized trial by Grover and colleagues comparing structured versus self-regulated VR simulation curricula, reporting that "structured curriculum led to better acquisition of technical and non-technical skills that transferred to the clinical setting."

> "Grover et al[30] investigated SBT as part of a structured curriculum compared to self-regulated curriculum, and found that structured curriculum led to better acquisition of technical and non-technical skills that transferred to the clinical setting."
