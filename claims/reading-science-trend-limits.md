Same regression framework applied to reading (comparable since 2000) and science (since 2006). The authors caution both were minor domains in PISA 2022 with less precise measurement, and robustness checks confirm non-linear time trends in these domains.

> "In reading, the overall impact of the pandemic is similar to that for mathematics (Supplementary Table 5). Also, the effects associated with school closures tend to be similar. In science, the overall effect of the pandemic is insignificant."
