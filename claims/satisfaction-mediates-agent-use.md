# In the TAM structural model, satisfaction mediated sustained use: usability strongly predicted usefulness (β = 0.93), usefulness predicted satisfaction (β = 0.76), but usefulness did not directly predict use intention (β = 0.02, p = .896)

## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, X., Liu, Z., Jiang, S., Chen, J., & Chen, W. (2026). The impact of an LLM-based educational agent on learning achievement, cognitive dynamics, and student perceptions in computer science education. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00641-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SEM (semopy v2.3.11) on 151 valid survey responses, acceptable fit (χ2/df = 2.994, CFI = 0.923, TLI = 0.906, RMSEA = 0.105), shows usability strongly predicted usefulness and usefulness predicted satisfaction, while direct effects on use intention and usage "did not reach the significance level".

> "Usability had a strong and significant positive effect on usefulness (β = 0.93, p < .001). Usefulness, in turn, significantly influences satisfaction (β = 0.76, p < .001), although its direct effects on use intention ( β = 0.02, p = .896) and usage (β = 0.39, p = .053) did not reach the significance level."

### Li 2026 (2)

Li, X., Liu, Z., Jiang, S., Chen, J., & Chen, W. (2026). The impact of an LLM-based educational agent on learning achievement, cognitive dynamics, and student perceptions in computer science education. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00641-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SEM path analysis identifies satisfaction as "a critical mediating factor" with strong direct effects on use intention (β = 0.76, p < .001) and usage (β = 0.60, p < .001); direct paths from usability to satisfaction, usefulness to use intention, and use intention to usage were not significant.

> "Satisfaction serves as a critical mediating factor, exhibiting a strong direct impact on both use intention (β = 0.76, p < .001) and usage (β = 0.60, p < .001)."
