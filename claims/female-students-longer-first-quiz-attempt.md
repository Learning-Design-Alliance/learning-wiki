## Evidence

### Pavo Noemi 2022

Pavo Noemi, Niedermaier Thomas, Seitz Stefanie, Jäger Harald, Strametz-Juranek Jeanette, Rieder Anita, Anvari-Pirsch Anahit. (2022). Gender differences in examination behavior of 4th grade medical students: Results from an observational study on ECG e-learning. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-021-01959-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of Moodle log data from 1315 fourth-year medical students (686 female, 629 male) taking a compulsory ECG e-learning quiz, analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U-test. The study reports "female students spent more time on the first attempt compared to their male colleagues" (medians 1592 vs 1405 sec, p = 0.002).

> "The time until the first attempt was comparable between both sexes (16 days [Q1–Q3: 7–24] for males vs 14 days [Q1–Q3: 8–12] for females, p = 0.096); however, female students spent more time on the first attempt compared to their male colleagues (1592 sec [Q1–Q3: 999–2536] vs 1405 sec [Q1–Q3: 828–2395], p = 0.002)."
