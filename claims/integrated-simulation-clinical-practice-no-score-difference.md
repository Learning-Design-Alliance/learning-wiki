## Evidence

### Yang et al. 2018

Jing Yang, Si-min Huang, Ze-jian Li, Lie Feng & Chun-ting Lu. (2018). Integrating Simulation Scenarios and Clinical Practices Guided by Concepts of Translational Medicine. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(1), 10. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n1p10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 42 observation-group and 38 control-group third-year medical students at Jinan University; the article reports no statistically significant differences (P>0.05) across five test indices, e.g. PE "86.24± 8.86" vs 84.92±6.34.

> "As illustrated in Table 2, there were no statistically significant differences ( P>0.05) between the observation group and the control group in practical tests of PE, internal punctures and case analysis or in theory-based examinations of diagnostics and internal medicine."
