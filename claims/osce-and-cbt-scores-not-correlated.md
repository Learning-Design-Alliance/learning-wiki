### Komasawa Nobuyasu 2020

Komasawa Nobuyasu, Terasaki Fumio, Nakano Takashi, Kawata Ryo. (2020). Relationships between objective structured clinical examination, computer-based testing, and clinical clerkship performance in Japanese medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230792

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null-result Pearson correlation analysis between OSCE and CBT scores in the same retrospective study of 94 students. The article states it "found no significant correlations between them" and between each component and CBT; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "We evaluated correlations between OSCE and CBT scores, and found no significant correlations between them (Table 3). There also were no significant correlations between each OSCE component score and CBT score"
