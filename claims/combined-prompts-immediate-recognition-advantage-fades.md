## Evidence

### Colin Lescarret 2022

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA three-way interaction on recognition scores with printed effect size "η²p=.031" (F(1,253)=8.008, p=.005). Students with both prompts scored significantly higher at immediate posttest than the ERS-only (p=.025) and video-only (p=.007) groups, but showed tendential deterioration (p=.051) and did not differ from the ERS-only group at delayed posttest (p=.641).

> "An Experimental Phase*Instructional Video*Embedded Rating Scales interaction effect was also observed, F(1,253)=8.008, p=.005, η²p=.031"
