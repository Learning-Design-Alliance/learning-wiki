### He Peipei 2026

He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired comparison of patient quality improvement among 36 nurses using the paired t-test. The incidence of peritonitis decreased from 0.16 ± 0.03 to 0.13 ± 0.02 times/year (t = 5.429, P < 0.001).

> "Following implementation of the retraining program, the incidence of peritonitis and catheter-related infections among patients at the peritoneal dialysis centers where the nurses worked decreased significantly (all P < 0.001)."
