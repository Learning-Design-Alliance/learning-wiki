`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Item analysis of the Fall 2000 post-test for 1170 calculus-based students (α slightly more than 0.75; 0.68 for 186 algebra-based students) found item difficulty "ranged from 0.2 to 0.8" and point biserial discrimination "ranged from 0.21 to 0.48". These are descriptive psychometric ranges, not effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The PBD ranged from 0.21 to 0.48 with only one item with PBD less than 0.28, which is good by the standards of achievement test design."
