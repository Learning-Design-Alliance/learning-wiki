## Evidence

### Bartlett Jonathan D. 2018

Bartlett Jonathan D., Lawrence John E., Khanduja Vikas. (2018). Virtual reality hip arthroscopy simulator demonstrates sufficient face validity. Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00167-018-5038-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study of 25 orthopaedic surgeons after one diagnostic arthroscopy task on the Simbionix Arthro Mentor. Agreement (Likert ≥7) was 80% for external instrumentation, 84% for the visual experience of arthroscopy, 72% for the procedure being realistic, and only 48% for "the tactile feedback from the soft tissue to be realistic".

> "This simulator was found to have an acceptably high degree of realism in all parameters apart from the tactile feedback received from the soft tissue (Table 2). 20 (80%) of the surgeons who participated agreed (Likert score of ≥ 7) that the external instrumentation of simulator was realistic."
