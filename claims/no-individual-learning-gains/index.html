Evidence¶

B. Slof 2010¶

B. Slof, G. Erkens, P. A. Kirschner, J. G. M. Jaspers. (2010). Design and effects of representational scripting on group performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9148-3

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired t test on 20-item pre-test (a = .60) and post-test (a = .79) scores of 90 students; means were 14.87 (SD = 2.33) pre and 14.69 (SD = 2.40) post, described as "no individual learning gains"; equivalence was not tested.

"The t test showed that the overall post-test score of 90 students (not all 96 students were present when the pre-test and/or post-test were administered) was not signiﬁcantly higher than the overall pre-test score ( t(90) = 0.72, p[ .05). There were, thus, no individual learning gains."
