### Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting 2024

Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a meta-analysis of 120 studies finding a "modest but positive relationship between creativity... and academic achievement", with stronger effects for psychometric tests, standardized achievement tests, verbal measures, and middle school.

> "a meta-analysis of 120 studies reported a modest but positive relationship between creativity—which was largely based on divergent thinking measures—and academic achievement (Gajda et al., 2017)."

### Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting 2024 (2)

Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a study in which creativity, measured by psychometric and self-report measures, "accounted for a significant proportion (18%)" of adolescents' standardized academic performance after controlling for personality and grade point average.

> "accounted for a significant proportion (18%) of 15–18-year-old Australian children's performance on standardized academic assessments of school taught subjects (e.g. English, maths, arts, technologies, sciences and humanities) even after controlling for personality and grade point average (Kaufman et al., 2023)."
