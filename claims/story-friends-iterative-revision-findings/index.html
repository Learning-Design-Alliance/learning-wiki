Evidence¶

Kelley 2015¶

Kelley, E. S., Goldstein, H., Spencer, T. D., & Sherman, A. (2015). Effects of automated Tier 2 storybook intervention on vocabulary and comprehension of preschool children with limited oral language skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 31, 47-61. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.12.004

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' report of findings from earlier iterations of Story Friends (Spencer et al., 2013 and unpublished pilots) that informed revisions to program materials; no effect size is printed. Five rarely learned words were replaced and lessons for seven words revised.

"we found that lessons that included photograph examples, as opposed to illustrations, were more successful in providing clear contexts for word meanings."
