### Boshuizen 1995

Boshuizen, H. P. A., Schmidt, H. G., Custers, E. J. F. M., & van de Wiel, M. W. (1995). Knowledge Development and Restructuring in the Domain of Medicine: The Role of Theory and Practice. Learning and Instruction. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/learning-and-instruction

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Experiment 1 design; explanations matched against an ideal explanation to count model concepts and detailed concepts. The article states advanced students "used more detailed concepts than experts and junior students".

> "the number of model concepts applied in the case explanations increased monotonically with expertise level (F(3, 84)=19.37, p=O.OOOl), while the number of more detailed biomedical concepts applied had an inverted U-shaped relation with expertise level"
