## Evidence

### Karjanto 2022

Karjanto, N.; Acelajado, M.J. (2022). Sustainable Learning, Cognitive Gains, and Improved Attitudes in College Algebra Flipped Classrooms. Sustainability 2022, 14, 12500. https://doi.org/10.3390/su141912500

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests on post-test means (df = 53, tcrit = 2.0057). Factorization favored CTM (|t| = 3.9076, p = 0.0000); rational expressions favored FCP (|t| = 2.8595, p = 0.0061); radical operations and applied problems showed no significant difference. No effect sizes printed.

> "the result of the t-test for independent samples applied to the post-test mean scores indicate a statistically significant difference between the FCP and CTM in the delivery of factorization and rational expressions, but no significant difference in radical operations and solving applied problems."
