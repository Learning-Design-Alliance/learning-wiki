Strasser Livia Maria, Anschuetz Wilma, Dennstädt Fabio, Hastings Janna. (2026). Performance Evaluation of Large Language Models in Multilingual Medical Multiple-Choice Questions: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/81399

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative Framework Method analysis by a three-reviewer panel of incorrect answers from GPT4o and Claude-Sonnet-3.7 on the web interface, coded with the error typology. The article reports Claude-Sonnet-3.7 produced reasoning errors despite factual accuracy in "69% of the erroneous cases (9/13)" versus 42% (5/12) for GPT4o in English prompts, and 62% versus 27% in German prompts.

> "In the English prompt condition, Claude-Sonnet-3.7 produced this type of error in 69% of the erroneous cases (9/13), compared to 42% (5/12) for GPT4o. The pattern persisted in the German prompt condition, with Claude-Sonnet-3.7 showing this error in 62% of the erroneous cases (5/8), while GPT4o’s rate was lower at 27% (3/11)."
