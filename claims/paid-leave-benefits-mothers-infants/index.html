The article reports, citing research without naming studies, that paid family leave significantly increases the length of time mothers nurse, with health benefits for infants. It also states studies show paid leave aids maternal recovery and improves workforce retention and earning potential. No effect sizes are printed.

"Research also indicates that paid family leave significantly increases the length of time that mothers nurse, which can have important health benefits for infants."
