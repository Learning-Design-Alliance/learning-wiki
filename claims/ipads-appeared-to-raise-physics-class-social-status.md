### Van Dusen 2012

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative observation reported in FINDINGS under social status. A student described peers asking how she made Photobooth pictures, learning "we have iPads in our physics class."

> "It was common for students in non-physics classes to express a desire to be involved with the iPad classroom environment."

### Van Dusen 2012 (2)

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Anecdotal observation reported by the teacher: a prospective student's stated enrollment decision changed after hearing about the iPad physics classes; school visitors regularly observed the classes.

> "One prospective student was overheard by the teacher saying that he thought the school was “ghetto” and was not going to go there until he heard that it had “the physics classes with iPads.”"
