## Evidence

### Zielina M 2025

Zielina M, Pinkasová T, Dostál D, Ivanová K, Macháček M, Matějek J, Řebíková B, Kuchař J, Škoda J, Pospíšilová K, Volejníčková RM, Doležal A. (2025). Assessing moral competence in medical and psychology students: effects on anxiety and test duration in online versus paper-based testing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08237-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mixed-effects regression on trait anxiety (STAI-X2) in 717 Czech medical and psychology students, analyzed on a reduced anxiety sample. The between-field difference grew from approximately 0.37 standard deviations in first year to d = 0.64 in fifth year, but the Field × Year interaction was not significant (p = 0.249).

> "in the first year, the difference was approximately 0.37 standard deviations (small to medium effect), t(21.80) = 2.68, p = 0.014, whereas in the fifth year, the difference was almost doubled (d = 0.64, medium to large effect; t(78.91) = 3.25, p = 0.002). However, the interaction test did not yield statistically significant results, t(676.84) = 1.15, p = 0.249"
