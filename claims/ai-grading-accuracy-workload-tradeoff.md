Exploratory study of AI-assisted grading of one high-stakes ETH Zurich thermodynamics exam; the article reports that AI "can achieve a coefficient of determination of R2 ≈ 0.91 when handling half of the grading load, and R2 ≈ 0.96 for one-fifth of the load." No effect size beyond these R² values is printed.
