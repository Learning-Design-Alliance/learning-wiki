# Perceived effort is higher for interleaved than blocked practice and decreases over time for interleaved practice only

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Perceived effort was significantly higher for interleaved practice than for blocked practice. [→ Onan 2022](#onan-2022)
`q3 i?` Perceived effort decreased over time for interleaved practice but not for blocked practice (strategy × time interaction). [→ Onan 2022 (2)](#onan-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Onan 2022

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Linear mixed model on perceived effort ratings collected after each of twelve study units, with participants as random effect. The main effect of strategy was significant, F(1, 289) = 380.39, p < 0.001; no effect size was printed.

> "We observed a significant main effect of learning strategies: per - ceived effort was significantly higher for interleaved practice than for blocked prac- tice, F(1, 289) = 380.39, p < 0.001."

### Onan 2022 (2)

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The same LMM showed the time effect was driven by the strategy × time interaction, F(1, 1498) = 135.22, p < 0.001, indicating effort declined across units only under interleaved practice; no effect size was printed.

> "the main effect of time appeared to be driven by the strategy × time inter action, F(1, 1498) = 135.22, p < 0.001, whic h indicated that perceived effort decreased over time for inter - leaved practice, but not for blocked practice."
