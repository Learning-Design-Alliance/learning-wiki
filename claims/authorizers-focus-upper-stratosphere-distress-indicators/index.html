Evidence¶

National Charter School Resource Center 2020¶

National Charter School Resource Center, lead authors Aimee Evan, Laura Groth, and Hannah Sullivan. (2020). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools: Part 1 The Role and Perspective of Charter School Authorizers. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED609911

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study: nine authorizers across policy contexts, 20 individuals interviewed, with school performance monitoring documents collected and analyzed using a coding scheme derived from literature on core charter school functions. The report states authorizers focus on "leadership, board governance, operations, and finances."

"We found that authorizers tend to focus on indicators of distress related to leadership, board governance, operations, and finances when determining whether a school is struggling."
