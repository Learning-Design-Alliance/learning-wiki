# After three years with a new principal, the average DCPS student's reading achievement increased by 4 percentile points (0.09 standard deviations) relative to retained-principal comparison schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Replacing principals in DCPS raised average student reading achievement by 4 percentile points (0.09 SD) after three years compared to what students would have achieved had the original principal been retained. [→ Elias Walsh 2014](#elias-walsh-2014)

Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/in-focus-brief-the-impact-of-replacing-principals-on-student-achievement-in-dc-public-schools

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental analysis comparing achievement changes in DCPS schools before and after principal replacement to changes in a sample of comparison schools within DCPS that kept the same principal. The brief reports a "4 percentile points (0.09 standard deviations)" reading gain after three years; the printed effect size of 0.09 standard deviations is below d = 0.2.

> "We found that after three years with a new principal, the average student's reading achievement increased by 4 percentile points (0.09 standard deviations) compared to how the student would have achieved had DCPS not replaced the previous principal."
