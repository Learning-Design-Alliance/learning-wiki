## Evidence

### Hardon S. F. 2019

Hardon S. F., van Kasteren R. J., Dankelman J., Bonjer H. J., Tuynman J. B., Horeman T. (2019). The value of force and torque measurements in transanal total mesorectal excision (TaTME). Techniques in Coloproctology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10151-019-02057-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null finding from the pilot construct-validity study with seven novices, reported in the Discussion as a contrast with previous box-trainer and virtual-reality studies that found significant task-time reductions. The article prints no test statistic or effect size for the time comparison; equivalence was not tested.

> "Within our pilot study, a reduction in task time is not observed, and the average task time in the novice post measurements even seem to increase."
