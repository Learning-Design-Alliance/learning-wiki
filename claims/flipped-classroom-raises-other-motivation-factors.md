## Evidence

### Wang Zhonger 2025

Wang Zhonger, Lu Jin, Yu Shenchen. (2025). The impact of the flipped classroom on the motivation and academic performance of Chinese college English learners. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0322094

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent t test between flipped and control groups on the instrumental motivation factor after the four-month quasi-experiment; "a Cohen's d of 0.689", t = 2.881, P = 0.005.

> "in terms of instrumental motivation, the mean value of the post-test was 3.34, with a SD of 0.91, a t-value of 2.881, a P-value of 0.005** and a Cohen's d of 0.689;"
