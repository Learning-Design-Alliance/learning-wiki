Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman. (2021). The Role of Direct Strategy Instruction and Indirect Activation of Self-Regulated Learning—Evidence from Classroom Observation Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09534-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In-depth 7-month observation study of two mathematics teachers with data triangulation across classroom observations, teacher interviews, and student ratings and achievement. The review reports a "significant interaction effect (time x class)" on students' metacognitive strategy use; no differences between classrooms were found for students' progress in mathematics.

> "there was a significant interaction effect (time x class) regarding students' use of metacognitive strategies, indicating that students apply more metacognitive strategies when teachers address these strategies regularly and explicitly"
