## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fourth, fifth- and sixth-year medical students scored higher on subjective and objective numeracy than first- and third-year students in a cross-sectional comparison. [→ Buljan Ivan 2019](#buljan-ivan-2019)

## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2019

Buljan Ivan, Tokalić Ružica, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2019). Health numeracy skills of medical students:cross-sectional and controlled before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1902-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 272 students comparing numeracy across study years using the SNS and NUMi; Kruskal-Wallis testing showed year differences (P < 0.001) and the authors report that "fourth, fifth- and sixth-year medical students were always superior to first- and third-year students". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Finally, fourth, fifth- and sixth-year medical students were always superior to first- and third-year students (Table 2)."
