Evidence¶

Sunardi 2018¶

Sunardi, Herman J. Waluyo, Astini Suudi, Nugraheni Eko Wardani. (2018). Suggestopedia Based Storytelling Teaching Model for Primary Students in Salatiga. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, Volume 6, Issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165473

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental effectiveness test in four Salatiga primary schools: SDN Kutowinangun 01 and SDN Mangunsari 01 as control, SD Kristen Satya Wacana and SDN Ledok 2 as experimental. The article reports "a significant increase of 3.03 or 8.11%" for the experimental group versus 2.51 (6.36%) for control.

"The effectiveness test showed a significant increase of 3.03 or 8.11% in the experimental group’s mean score, from 18.94 in the pretest to 21.97 in the posttest. Meanwhile, the control group only experienced a 2.51 or 6.36 % in the mean score, from 18.50 in the pretest to 21.05 in the posttest."
