## Evidence

### Higuchi M 2020

Higuchi M, Abe T, Hotta K, et al. (2020). Development and validation of a porcine organ model for training in essential laparoscopic surgical skills. International Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1111/iju.14315

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subjective mental workload was measured using the NASA-TLX after each training session in 54 participants across three experience groups. The article reports 'Higher NASA Task Load Index scores were observed in novices' with Kruskal–Wallis tests showing significant differences in tasks 1 and 2 and marginal differences in task 3.

> "Higher NASA Task Load Index scores were observed in novices, and there were significant differences in tasks 1 (Kruskal–Wallis test, P = 0.0004) and 2 (P = 0.0002), and marginal differences in task 3 (P = 0.0745) among the three groups."
