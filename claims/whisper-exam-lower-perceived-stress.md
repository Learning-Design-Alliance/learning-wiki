### Vorbeck L 2026

The Frankfurt ‘whisper exam’ - a case-based collaborative summative examination to assess clinical decision-making skills in hygiene, microbiology and virology– feasibility and evaluation. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive survey of 193 respondents (54.3% of 350 students) using a four-point Likert scale administered immediately after the exam; “Most respondents reported lower” anxiety, with the printed counts and percentages as stated. No effect size was reported.

> "Most respondents reported lower (n = 89/193, 46.1%) or substantially lower (n = 62/193, 32.1%) levels of pre-exam anxiety compared to traditional individual written examinations."
