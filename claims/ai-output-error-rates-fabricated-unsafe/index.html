Marcus Kubsch 2026¶

Marcus Kubsch. (2026). AI as a Partner in Learning about, Doing, and Engaging with Science: Vigilance as the Key to Productive Augmentation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16822 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.16822

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The paper cites two audits as evidence that AI errors exist in research and everyday settings: the review reports, citing Ghareeb et al. (2026), that 44.5% of references in frontier-model-drafted proposals were fabricated, and, citing Draelos et al. (2026), that 5–13% of public chatbot medical answers were judged outright unsafe. Second-hand; designs not described.

"a check of research proposals drafted by a frontier model without grounding in the literature found 44.5% of their references fabricated (Ghareeb et al., 2026), and a physician audit of the public chatbots people actually consult judged 5–13% of their answers to patient-posed medical questions outright unsafe (Draelos et al., 2026)."
