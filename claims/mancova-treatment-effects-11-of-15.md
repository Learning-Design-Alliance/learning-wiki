# MANCOVA showed significant treatment effects for 11 of 15 dependent measures with consistently high effect sizes, while L2 reading speed, L2 spelling, L2 rapid digit naming and L3 stimulated vocabulary retrieval did not change as a function of the intervention

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Treatment effects were significant for 11 of the 15 dependent variables with large effect sizes (e.g., TOWRE F = 57.19, p < .001, ηh2 = .436; LISTEN F = 145.92, p < .001, ηh2 = .440). [→ Pfenninger 2015](#pfenninger-2015)
`q2 i?` DIGRAN-L2, SLRT-I reading speed, SLRT-II spelling and RETRIEVE-L3 did not change as a function of the intervention (e.g., SLRT-I F = 2.38, p = .143; RETRIEVE-L3 F = 1.74, p = .197). [→ Pfenninger 2015 (2)](#pfenninger-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Pfenninger 2015

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA follow-up tests (df = 1) with posttest scores as dependent variables, pretest scores as covariates, and treatment and reading ability as independent variables; Table 12 prints F and ηh2 values for all 15 measures.

> "Table 12 shows that there were significant results for 11 of the 15 measures and consistently high effect sizes for the treatment variable"

### Pfenninger 2015 (2)

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same MANCOVA analysis identified four measures on which the treatment effect was not significant; Table 12 prints SLRT-I F = 2.38, p = .143 and RETRIEVE-L3 F = 1.74, p = .197 among them.

> "rapid automatized digit naming in German (DIGRAN-L2), L2 reading speed (SLRT-I), L2 spelling (SLRT-II-S), and stimulated vocabulary retrieval in English (RETRIEVE-L3) did not change as a function of the intervention"
