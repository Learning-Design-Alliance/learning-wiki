## Evidence

### Gaspar et al. 2018

Tania Gaspar, Ana Cerqueira, Cátia Branquinho & Margarida G. Matos. (2018). The Effect of a Social-Emotional School-Based Intervention upon Social and Personal Skills in Children and Adolescents. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n6p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post repeated-measures ANOVA (SPSS 20) in a research-action study of the ISC program with 960 Portuguese children and adolescents. The article reports "significant differences between the competences before and after the intervention" in Interpersonal Relationships (F = 5.954**) and Defining Goals (F = 10.319***); other dimensions were n.s.

> "the intervention group, which was the target of the intervention, reveals significant differences between the competences before and after the intervention, namely in the Interpersonal Relationships and Definition of Goals related skills"

### Gaspar et al. 2018 (2)

Tania Gaspar, Ana Cerqueira, Cátia Branquinho & Margarida G. Matos. (2018). The Effect of a Social-Emotional School-Based Intervention upon Social and Personal Skills in Children and Adolescents. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n6p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Waiting-list comparison group of 171 children and adolescents (46.2% boys, mean age 14.7, SD 3.3) assessed before and after without intervention; all six scale dimensions were n.s. across pre and post evaluation.

> "While in the waiting-list group, which was not the target of the intervention, there were no significant differences between evaluations."
