## Evidence

### Sergej Faletič 2025

Sergej Faletič, Assen Kyuldjiev, Thomas Lindner, Dorottya Schnider, Éva Izsa, Mihály Hömöstrei, Péter Jenei, Boyka Aneva, Daniela D. Ivanova, Hynek Němec, František Kundracik, and Martin Plesch. (2025). Development of soft and hard skills of high-school students via Young Physicists' Tournament. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.15324](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.15324)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 308 students, analysed on a subset of 62 who participated in YPT or solved at least one YPT problem, from Bulgaria, Czechia, Hungary, Slovakia and Slovenia (plus 32 Slovak respondents from a 2020 preparatory seminar, soft skills not collected). Students rated skills on a -2 to +2 scale.

> "On the global level, students' comparison of YPT versus regular classes follows the same pattern as the comparison of teachers (i.e., a clearly significant contribution of YPT for the development of the high-level hard skills and soft skills, and a neutral assessment for the curriculum-related hard skills)."
