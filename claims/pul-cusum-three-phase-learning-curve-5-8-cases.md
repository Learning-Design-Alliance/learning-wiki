## Evidence

### Hsieh Tsung-Yi 2025

Hsieh Tsung-Yi, Hong Jian-Hua, Chueh Jeff Shih-Chieh. (2025). Comparing the clinical outcomes and learning curves of prostatic urethral lift performed by experienced versus naïve urologists. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-025-05895-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM analysis of operative times for the single naïve urologist with the most cases during the study period, plotted against consecutive case number (Fig. 1b). The first five cases had operative times consistently above the mean; the analysis is based on one learner.

> "For the naïve urologist, a three-phase learning curve may be identified from the CUSUM chart: an initial upward trend reaching the turning point at case 5 (learning phase), followed by stabilizing performance (proficiency phase), followed by a strong downward trend after case 8 (competency phase)"

### Hsieh Tsung-Yi 2025 (2)

Hsieh Tsung-Yi, Hong Jian-Hua, Chueh Jeff Shih-Chieh. (2025). Comparing the clinical outcomes and learning curves of prostatic urethral lift performed by experienced versus naïve urologists. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-025-05895-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM chart for the experienced urologist (>100 prior PUL cases) in the same cohort (Fig. 1a). The authors interpret this as the diminishing marginal benefit of additional experience once competency is reached.

> "For the experienced urologist, there was a gradual upward trend with fluctuations initially, followed by a gradual downward trend after 26 cases (Fig. 1a)."
