## Evidence

### Karimian Z 2025

Karimian Z, Momeni M, Zarifsanaiey N. (2025). Enhancing radiographic interpretation: effects of gamification on medical students' knowledge, skills, and satisfaction — a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07523-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two groups of 41 fifth-year medical students; the gamification group's post-test mean was 18.52 ± 1.14 versus 16.67 ± 1.45 in the in-person group, with "the gamification method was significantly more effective than the routine method (P < 0.001)".

> "However, comparing the post-test scores between the two groups revealed that the gamification method was significantly more effective than the routine method (P < 0.001)."
