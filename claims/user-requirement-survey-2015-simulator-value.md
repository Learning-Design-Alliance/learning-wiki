`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

User-requirement survey (2015) completed by five physicians (N = 5) at the Academy of Breastfeeding Medicine conference, using 7-point Likert items based on Baby-Friendly Hospital "Step 2" educational guidelines. Respondents rated the simulator "valuable (6.6/7) and relevant (6.0/7)". No effect size reported.

> "The respondents agreed that a breastfeeding simulator could be a valuable (6.6/7) and relevant (6.0/7) training tool. Respondents preferred a wearable LSM shaped like a torso instead of a single breast"
