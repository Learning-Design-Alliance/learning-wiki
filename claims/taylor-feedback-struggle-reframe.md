`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case illustration from weekly reflections and instructor feedback in the second course implementation. Taylor's Class 3 reflection expressed self-doubt when stuck; after Class 5 instructor feedback normalizing struggle, Taylor's Class 6 reflection stated "struggle is good; it means that youre learning something," and the student studied mistakes instead of getting frustrated.

> "Through this interaction, the student was able to shift their model of learning from seeing struggle as a sign of weakness to seeing struggle as a sign that learning was taking place ."
