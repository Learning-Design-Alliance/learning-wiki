## Evidence

### Tony Rothman and Stephen Boughn 2008

Tony Rothman and Stephen Boughn. (2008). The Lorentz Force and the Radiation Pressure of Light. https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1310 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0807.1310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0807.1310)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerically plotted solutions of the analytic velocity expressions (figures 2-4) for various ωc/ω and phase angles; the plots show that "regardless of φ, vz is always positive" with an x-average dependent on phase, consistent with the small-ratio expansion.

> "Notice that regardless of φ, vz is always positive, but that there is also a nonzero vx whose average can be positive, negative or zero depending on φ"
