## Evidence

### Liu Yutong 2026

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

DOPS assessments in the same RCT using a 9-point scoring system. Table 4 shows moderate effect sizes for all six significant dimensions (d = 0.634 to 0.764), though the authors note these "showed moderate effect sizes with limited absolute magnitude, suggesting modest clinical impact."

> "the experimental group achieved significantly higher scores in six DOPS dimensions: indication/contraindication comprehension (P = 0.030), sedation/analgesia management (P = 0.018), operational capability (P = 0.016), aseptic technique (P = 0.026), postoperative care (P = 0.010), and communication skills (P = 0.011)."

### Liu Yutong 2026 (2)

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section reporting non-significant DOPS findings. The Discussion attributes the lack of divergence to three factors: procedural fluency requiring long-term clinical experience, the non-linear scoring system, and a ceiling effect because the control group already achieved a high baseline score (7.60 ± 0.65).

> "The remaining four domains and the overall DOPS score showed no significant difference (P > 0.05), which may also be partly attributable to insufficient statistical power rather than true equivalence."
