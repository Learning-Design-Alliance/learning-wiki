## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students whose Pr-LEI and Pc-LEI scores fell in the same quadrant (PrAPc_PCF) performed better on the FNLAT than students whose scores fell in different quadrants (PrDPc_PCF), with a medium-to-large effect size reported. [→ Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016](#yeh-ting-kuang-2016)

## Evidence

### Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016

Yeh Ting-Kuang, Huang Hsiu-Mei, Chan Wing P, Chang Chun-Yen. (2016). Effects of congruence between preferred and perceived learning environments in nursing education in Taiwan: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open 6(5): e009925. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009925

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preferred-perceived fit (PCF) quadrant-based grouping of the 124 students (73 congruent, 51 discordant) followed by comparison of FNLAT scores; "The PrAPc_PCF group performed better in FNLAT" with a reported medium-to-large effect size. The authors state this "supported the results obtained by the PCS method".

> "Seventy-three students had Pr-LEI and Pc-LEI scores in the same quadrant and were assigned to the PrAPc_PCF group, and 51 students had Pr-LEI and Pc-LEI scores in different quadrants and were assigned to the PrDPc_PCF group. The PrAPc_PCF group performed better in FNLAT than the PrDPc_PCF group (medium-to-large effect size)."
