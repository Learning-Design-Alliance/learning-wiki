### Farey John E. 2018

Farey John E., Bui David T., Townsend David, Sureshkumar Premala, Carr Sandra, Roberts Chris. (2018). Predictors of confidence in anatomy knowledge for work as a junior doctor: a national survey of Australian medical students. BMC Medical Education 18:174. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable ordinal regression in the national survey: students exposed to dissection had adjusted OR 1.39 (95% CI 1.08–1.78) for confidence; unadjusted OR 1.42. The authors call the dissection-preparedness link a new finding.

### Farey John E. 2018 (2)

Farey John E., Bui David T., Townsend David, Sureshkumar Premala, Carr Sandra, Roberts Chris. (2018). Predictors of confidence in anatomy knowledge for work as a junior doctor: a national survey of Australian medical students. BMC Medical Education 18:174. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1280-5
