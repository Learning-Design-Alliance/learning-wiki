Behavior-defined enacted-group analysis of no-help quiz scores in the quasi-experimental three-condition study (final analytic n = 37). The Quiz 4 omnibus tests were ANOVA p = .055 and KW p = .045; Guided-LLM had the highest mean in all three intervention quizzes.

> "Exploratory pairwise contrasts favored Guided -LLM over No -LLM by 2 3.17 points (95% bootstrap CI [5.29, 39.49], Hedges’ g = 0.95, Welch p = .022) and over Unrestricted -LLM by 16.42 points (95% bootstrap CI [−0.42, 32.84], Hedges’ g = 0.73, Welch p = .079)."
