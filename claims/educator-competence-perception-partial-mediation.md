## Evidence

### Liao Ting 2026

Liao Ting, Zou Jinmei, Wang Min, Song Yuqing, Cai Li, Yin Jin, Chen Yao, Wang Yating. (2026). Professional Identity, Educator Competence Perception and Willingness to Engage in Geriatric Nursing Among Nursing Students: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5000 samples) within the SEM of 842 nursing students; the indirect effect PI→educator competence perception→willingness was 0.230 with a 95% CI of [0.164, 0.296], excluding zero. The article reports partial, not full, mediation.

> "In a cross‐sectional mediational model, educator competence perception accounted for an indirect association between PI and willingness (standardised indirect effect = 0.230, 95% bootstrap CI [0.164, 0.296]); after model adjustment and controlling for age, gender, and grade, the final structural model yielded good fit (χ 2/df = 2.954, p < 0.001, GFI = 0.961, TLI = 0.977, CFI = 0.983, NFI = 0.975, RMSEA = 0.048)."
