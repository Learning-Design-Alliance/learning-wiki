## Evidence

### Erdem Cahit 2026

Erdem Cahit, Kaya Metin, Polat Mustafa, Eğmir Eray. (2026). Thinking and feeling matter: a second-order meta-analysis of student characteristics across developmental domains and their effect on academic outcomes. BMC Psychology, 14, 919. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05035-x

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Second-order meta-analysis moderator analysis: the psychomotor domain contributed k = 6 effect sizes (r = 0.37, LL = 0.29, UL = 0.45) but all from one first-order meta-analysis. The authors state the finding "should be interpreted with caution due to limited statistical power and restricted generalizability".

> "The relationship between psychomotor skills and academic achievement appeared relatively strong; however, this effect size was derived from a single first-order meta-analysis and therefore was not subjected to moderator or robustness analyses."
