## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Large-scale observational analysis of matched E-CLASS pre/post responses from N = 3601 students in 60 first-year course instances at 29 institutions. The article prints an effect size of d = -0.3, a small effect, for the "negative shift from pre- to post-instruction" in Traditional courses, with p much less than 0.01.

> "Students in Traditional courses had a statistically signiﬁcant ( p ≪ 0.01, d = −0.3), negative shift from pre- to post-instruction."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same national E-CLASS data set of matched first-year pre/post responses. The Transformed-course pre-to-post comparison shows no significant shift; only a p-value is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Alternatively, students in Transformed courses did not shift signiﬁcantly from pre- to post-instruction ( p = 0.2)."
