Theoretical argument in the review's identity-threat pathway section. The authors state this mechanism "has not yet been tested directly at the peer level" and cite adjacent research on teachers' fixed mindsets and stereotype threat as early indirect support.

> "We propose that fixed mindset peer cultures may act as a social amplifier of stereotype threat. When students are surrounded by peers who believe that ability is fixed and unchangeable, the social environment may begin to feel like a place where students are either naturally capable or not"
