### Lewis 2018

Lewis, F. (2018). FTP and NSO: Astronomy With (Several) Robotic Telescopes. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.00762](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.00762)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive statement of the NSO access model from the NSO section: schools access the 2-metre Liverpool Telescope on La Palma via a guided observing system using 10% of LT time. No outcome evaluation is reported.

> "Established in 2004, the NSO provides schools in the UK and Ireland with access to the Liverpool Telescope through a guided observing system, using 10% of the LT's time."

### Lewis 2018 (2)

Lewis, F. (2018). FTP and NSO: Astronomy With (Several) Robotic Telescopes. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.00762](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.00762)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive usage and expansion statement from the NSO section: the project reports "over 4,000 users" and a 2017 opening of registration to schools and teachers outside the UK and Ireland, free of charge.

> "Already with over 4,000 users, new in 2017 is the ability for non-UK/Ireland based schools and teachers to register affording free access to both the observing portal and resources."
