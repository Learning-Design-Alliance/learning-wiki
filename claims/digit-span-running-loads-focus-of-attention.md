# Digit span running loaded on the focus of attention factor rather than the phonological factor, supporting Cowan's claim that running span performance reflects attentional capacity

## Evidence

### Gray Shelley 2016

Gray Shelley, Green Samuel, Alt Mary, Hogan Tiffany P., Kuo Trudy, Brinkley Shara, Cowan Nelson. (2016). The Structure of Working Memory in Young Children and Its Relation to Intelligence. Journal of Memory and Language. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5157932/

`q3 · i?`

Local fit analysis of the combined hybrid CFA model in the same 168-child sample. The digit span running task "was significantly and moderately related to the second factor" (focus of attention) but nonsignificantly related to the phonological factor.

> "Finally the digit span running task was significantly and moderately related to the second factor, consistent with the Cowan model, but nonsignificantly and less strongly related to the "phonological" factor, which fails to support the Baddeley and Hitch model."
