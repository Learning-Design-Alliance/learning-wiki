## Evidence

### Huéscar Hernández E. 2020

Huéscar Hernández E., Moreno-Murcia J. A., Cid L., Monteiro D., Rodrigues F. (2020). Passion or Perseverance? The Effect of Perceived Autonomy Support and Grit on Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17062143

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-step CFA-then-SEM assessment using Mplus 7.4 with the MLR estimator. Fit indices printed in Results are: measurement model χ2 = 451.308 (310), CFI = 0.959, TLI = 0.953, SRMR = 0.043, RMSEA = 0.031; SEM χ2 = 546.378 (319), CFI = 0.93, TLI = 0.927, SRMR = 0.080, RMSEA = 0.039.

> "The measurement model did provide adequate fit to the data: (χ2 = 451.308 (310), p ≥ 0.001; CFI = 0.959, TLI = 0.953, SRMR = 0.043, RMSEA = 0.031 (CI 90% = 0.025, 0.037)). Thus, we moved forward on testing the structural model."
