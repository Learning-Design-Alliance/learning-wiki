# Studying instructional and video modeling examples improved heredity problem-solving performance and self-assessment accuracy from pretest to posttest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall problem-solving performance rose from 24.36% correct at pretest to 64.74% at posttest. [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021](#lisette-wijnia-2021)
`q2 i?` Mean self-assessment accuracy improved from 1.33 at pretest to 1.17 at posttest (lower scores indicate higher accuracy). [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)](#lisette-wijnia-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Lisette Wijnia 2021

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-group pretest-posttest design with 342 secondary students; four isomorphic heredity problems at pretest and posttest. "students obtained a mean score of 24.36% correct (SD = 20.24) on the pretest and 64.74% correct (SD = 24.02) on the posttest, indicating that students improved their performance after studying the video modeling examples."

> "students obtained a mean score of 24.36% correct (SD = 20.24) on the pretest and 64.74% correct (SD = 24.02) on the posttest, indicating that students improved their performance after studying the video modeling examples."

### Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive pretest-posttest comparison of self-assessment accuracy (absolute difference scores, 0 = perfect accuracy) in the same one-group design. "students had a self-assessment accuracy score of 1.33 (SD = 0.76) on the pretest and 1.17 (SD = 0.85) on the posttest, indicating that students became more accurate in their self-assessment after studying the video modeling examples."

> "students had a self-assessment accuracy score of 1.33 (SD = 0.76) on the pretest and 1.17 (SD = 0.85) on the posttest, indicating that students became more accurate in their self-assessment after studying the video modeling examples."
