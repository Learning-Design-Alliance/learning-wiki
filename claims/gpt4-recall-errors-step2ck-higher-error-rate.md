`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the same 38-item evaluation, graphically represented in Fig. 2B, splitting errors by complexity type and by USMLE step. The article reports the recall-type contrast and the higher Step 2 CK error rate descriptively, with no test statistic or effect size printed.

> "In the subgroup analysis, GPT-4 made errors in recall type questions, whereas GPT-4o did not. Besides, both versions of GPT have similar errors in the test questions of Step 1 and Step 2 CK, but the error rate is higher in Step 2 CK."
