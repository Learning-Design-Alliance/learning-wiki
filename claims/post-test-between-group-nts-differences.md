# Post-test non-technical skill performance of the flipped learning group is significantly better than the lecture-based group on teamwork/leadership and team orientation, but not on planning/problem-solving

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At post-test the intervention group scored significantly better than the control group on AS-NTS dimensions two and three, while the groups did not differ significantly on dimension one. [→ Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021](#moll-khosrawi-parisa-2021)

## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021

Moll-Khosrawi Parisa, Zöllner Christian, Cencin Nadine, Schulte-Uentrop Leonie. (2021). Flipped learning enhances non-technical skill performance in simulation-based education: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02766-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group post-test comparison in the randomised controlled trial (intervention n = 35, control n = 45). The article reports the intervention group "significantly different (better) on dimensions two and three" at p = .026, with no printed effect size for these contrasts; dimension one did not differ significantly (p = .114).

> "The comparison of the post-test NTS performance between the groups showed that the scores of the intervention group were significantly different (better) on dimensions two and three (dimension two: F (1, 78) = 5.12, p = .026; dimension three F (1, 78) = 5.20, p = .026) compared to the control group. On dimension one, the post-test scorings of the two groups did not differ significantly (F = (1, 78) = 2.56, p = .114)."
