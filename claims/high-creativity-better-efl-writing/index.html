Jaelani 2017¶

Selamet Riadi Jaelani. (2017). Treating of Content-Based Instruction to Teach Writing Viewed from EFL Learners' Creativity. English Language Teaching, 10(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n11p156

Descriptive results from the same quasi-experiment: the high-creativity group's writing mean was 75.75 (SD 8.605) versus 67.5 (SD 7.511) for the low-creativity group. These are descriptive statistics; the article prints no effect size for this contrast.

"the data of B 1 shows that the score is 55 up to 83. The mean is 75.75 and the standard deviation is 8.605, the data of B 2 shows that the score is 53 up to 80. The mean is 67.5 and the standard deviation is 7.511"
