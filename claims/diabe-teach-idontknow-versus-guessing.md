## Evidence

### Mariam S 2025

Mariam S, Heba I, Mohamed FHN. (2025). Diabe-teach: a randomized controlled trial of a gamified approach to enhance medical undergraduates’ knowledge and comprehension of diabetes mellitus. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06602-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of response patterns in the RCT (Table 2: 'I don't know' t = −7.0; incorrect responses t = 6.6, both p < 0.001). The 'I don't know' option was “one correct answer, 3 distractors and one ‘I don’t know’ option to mitigate forced guessing,” recorded separately and excluded from test scores.

> "a striking difference was noted in the “I do not know” responses, which were significantly higher (p < .001) in the intervention group, whereas choosing wrong answers was significantly more common (p < .001) in the self-study group"
