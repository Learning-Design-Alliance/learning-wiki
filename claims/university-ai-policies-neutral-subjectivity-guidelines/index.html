Lydia Manikonda and Dominique Outlaw 2026¶

Lydia Manikonda and Dominique Outlaw. (2026). Policy Fragmentation or Institutional Alignment? Institutional Governance of AI in Universities and Business Schools. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03584 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.03584

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Subjectivity scoring (0 fully objective to 1 fully subjective) of the 130-policy university corpus. The caption reports subjectivity "mean=0.45, min=0.12, and max=0.63". The authors note these policies are presented as suggested guidelines.

"The distribution in Figure 1(b) suggests that 80% of the policies are mostly neutral (with neither being highly subjective nor highly objective ) with a subjectivity score of >0.4 ( mean=0.452)."
