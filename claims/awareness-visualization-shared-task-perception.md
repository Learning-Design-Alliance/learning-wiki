## Evidence

### Cress 2018

Cress, U., Stahl, G., Rose, C., Law, N., & Ludvigsen, S. (2018). Forming social systems by coupling minds at different levels of cognition: Design, tools, and research methods. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 13, 235–240. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9284-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Editorial summary of Hadwin, Bakhtiar, and Miller's study of scripting shared task perception in online collaboration, where standardized answers about individual task perceptions feed an awareness visualization summarizing perceptions across group members.

> "The study showed that without these kinds of visualizations, individuals rated planning as more problematic, and they encountered more challenges."

### Cress 2018 (2)

Cress, U., Stahl, G., Rose, C., Law, N., & Ludvigsen, S. (2018). Forming social systems by coupling minds at different levels of cognition: Design, tools, and research methods. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 13, 235–240. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9284-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the same study, groups given the awareness visualization differed from groups without it: they reported higher strategy success and "expected planning and teamwork strategies to be equally effective"; pathway analysis traced transitions from challenge to strategy selection to enactment.

> "This group reported higher levels of success with their strategies and it expected both planning and teamwork strategies to be equally effective."
