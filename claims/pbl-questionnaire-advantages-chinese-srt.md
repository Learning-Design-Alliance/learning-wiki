## Evidence

### Li Tingting 2022

Li Tingting, Wang Weidong, Li Zhijie, Wang Hongmiao, Liu Xiaodan. (2022). Problem-based or lecture-based learning, old topic in the new field: a meta-analysis on the effects of PBL teaching method in Chinese standardized residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03254-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of questionnaire surveys (yes/no items, pooled as odds ratios) from 37 studies; Table 3 reports e.g. teamwork ability OR = 2.35 (95% CI 1.65–3.34, 14 studies), learning interest OR = 1.49 (21 studies), clinical diagnostic thinking OR = 1.42 (11 studies).

> "the intervention group is superior to the LBL group with respect to theoretical knowledge mastery, clinical diagnostic thinking, teamwork ability, ability to analyze and solve problems, ability to consult documents, learning interest, satisfaction with teaching, and learning efficiency, with a statistically significant difference"
