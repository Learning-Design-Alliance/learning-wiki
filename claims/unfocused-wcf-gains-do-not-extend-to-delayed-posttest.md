# Accuracy gains from unfocused WCF during revision do not extend to a new piece of writing after a one-month interval at the elementary level

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On a new posttest writing task produced after a four-week interval without feedback, the WCF group and control group showed no statistically significant difference in grammatical accuracy. [→ Khanlarzadeh and Nemati 2016](#khanlarzadeh-and-nemati-2016)

## Evidence

### Khanlarzadeh and Nemati 2016

Mobin Khanlarzadeh, Majid Nemati. (2016). The effect of written corrective feedback on grammatical accuracy of EFL students: An improvement over previous unfocused designs. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 4(2). https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way between-subjects ANOVA comparing the two intact classes (experimental n = 16, control n = 17) on pretest and posttest scores. The study reports the groups were "not significantly different" at the end of the course; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "the two groups were not significantly different with respect to their grammatical accuracy at the beginning (F = .26, p = .61) and at the end of the course (F = .41, p = .53)."
