### Alhassan Robert Kaba 2021

Alhassan Robert Kaba, Ayanore Martin Amogre, Diekuu John-Bosco, Prempeh Emmanuel B. A., Donkor Ernestina Safoa. (2021). Leveraging e-Learning technology to enhance pre-service training for healthcare trainees in Ghana: evidence from a pilot project and pointers to policy reforms. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-021-07224-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 363 healthcare trainees at a Ghanaian public university who experienced the SMART e-Learning pilot, reporting usage frequencies. The study found "writing of interim assessments (IAs) (52 %)" was the most used mobile-phone function and live chats with lecturers the least (5%).

> "The most frequently used e-Learning functions by respondents via their personal mobile phones were writing of interim assessments (IAs) (52 %), download learning materials (38 %), upload class assignments (32 %), live chats with lecturers (5 %) and live chats with colleague students (17 %)."
