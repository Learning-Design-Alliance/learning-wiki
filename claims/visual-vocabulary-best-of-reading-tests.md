`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grade VIII reading study conclusion: Visual Vocabulary correlated +0.62 ± 0.07 with Reading Ability T and 0.76 with Reading Ability C, the highest of the so-called reading tests under both criteria. These are raw correlations with probable errors, so no standardised magnitude is coded.

> "Of the so-called Reading tests used, the best of them as a test of reading ability is Thorndike's Visual Vocabulary, while Monroe's Rate of Silent Reading test shows almost no correlation with our criteria for reading ability."
