Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary 2015¶

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary. (2015). Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581287

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation combining lesson observations (13 lessons in 8 schools), facilitator interviews, teacher telephone interviews, and teacher and student online surveys; the report concludes the level of implementation was low, which may have diluted any potential impact on reading comprehension.

"Few, if any, schools implemented the programme as designed, suggesting that substantial adaptation may be required if the approach is to gain wider adoption in English schools."
