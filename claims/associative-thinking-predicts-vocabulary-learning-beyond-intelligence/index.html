Evidence¶

Luchini 2025¶

Luchini, S. A., Kaufman, J. C., Goecke, B., Wilhelm, O., Kenett, Y. N., Lei, D., Benedek, M., van Hell, J. G., & Beaty, R. E. (2025). Creativity supports learning through associative thinking. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00334-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation model in Study 1 (N = 146 undergraduates) added semantic distance to a latent intelligence model predicting LET learning; the article reports that "semantic distance showed a significant (albeit small) effect on learning ( β = 0.182, p = 0.037)" while Gc remained non-significant.

"Although Gf strongly predicted learning (β = 0.399, p = 0.002), semantic distance showed a significant (albeit small) effect on learning ( β = 0.182, p = 0.037)"

Luchini 2025 (2)¶

Luchini, S. A., Kaufman, J. C., Goecke, B., Wilhelm, O., Kenett, Y. N., Lei, D., Benedek, M., van Hell, J. G., & Beaty, R. E. (2025). Creativity supports learning through associative thinking. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00334-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Pearson correlations in Study 1, visualized in Fig. 1, showed that "associative thinking (semdis) possessed medium correlations with both day 1 learning" and day 2 learning as printed; no effect sizes beyond these correlations are printed.

"associative thinking (semdis) possessed medium correlations with both day 1 learning (r = 0.33; p < 0.001) and day 2 learning ( r = 0.38; p < 0.001)"

Related Claims¶

  • Prior learning of word pairs reduces the semantic distance of goal-directed (but not free) associations, suggesting interference from newly acquired knowledge — related
  • Only goal-directed, not free, association correlates with vocabulary retention; the associative thinking–learning link is driven by deliberate rather than spontaneous association — related
  • Vocabulary retention declines from immediate to 24-hour-delayed no-feedback testing, and goal-directed associations are more semantically distant but slower to produce than free associations — related
  • Fluid intelligence predicts paired-associate word learning, but crystallized intelligence does not once associative thinking is controlled — related
  • A latent creativity factor (writing, drawing, openness) predicts paired-associate vocabulary learning, controlling for general intelligence — related
