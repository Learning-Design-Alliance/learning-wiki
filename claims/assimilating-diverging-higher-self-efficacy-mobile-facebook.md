## Evidence

### Chen 2015

Chen, Y. (2015). Linking Learning Styles and Learning on Mobile Facebook. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 16(2). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/1973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The Facebook Use Questionnaire was administered to 134 students after the nine-week treatment. A composite score from three self-efficacy items showed "a statistically significant difference in students’ self-efficacy toward learning in Facebook" across learning styles, with Assimilating and Diverging groups scoring higher.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in students’ self-efficacy toward learning in Facebook among students with different learning styles (Diverging, Assimilating, Converging, and Accommodating) (F3,130=108.54, p< 0.01)."
