Logistic regression of the refresher-course survey shows no statistically significant working-versus-inactive difference for pocket-guide use; fewer than 11% of participants referred to a pocket guide. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Again, the use of pocket guides is the only dependent variable without significant working vs. inactive status. However, the p-value is only slightly above the 0.05 threshold (OR = 1.937, 0.05 < p < 0.06)."
