## Evidence

### Daker 2023

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Necka, E.A., Green, A.E. & Lyons, I.M. (2023). Does anxiety explain why math-anxious people underperform in math? npj Science of Learning, 8:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00156-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

In-lab experimental session with 83 participants (from an initial 288 who completed online screening), measuring trait math anxiety via questionnaire and performance on difficult mental math blocks. The printed effect size "r(81) = −0.469" (partial "rpartial(79) = −0.432, p = 6E−5") ruled out general anxiety and global cognitive underperformance as explanations.

> "We observed a significant negative correlation between trait-level math anxiety and math performance ( r(81) = −0.469, p = 8E−6), showing that, consistent with decades of past research 4–8, math-anxious people underperformed in math."
