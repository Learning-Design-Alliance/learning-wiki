### Yang Bin 2025

Yang Bin, Song Shoujun, Zhang Lili, Xu Chunze. (2025). The relationship between resilience and professional well-being among early childhood teachers: the chain mediating role of teacher pedagogical competence and self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03227-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 1,377 early childhood teachers in Jinan, China, analyzed with hierarchical regression (PROCESS model 6). The article reports that "teacher resilience significantly and positively predicted the level of teachers' professional well-being" with β = 1.05, t = 42.03, p < 0.001.
