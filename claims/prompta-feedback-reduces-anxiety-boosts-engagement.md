## Evidence

### Puro Vezolu 2025

Puro Vezolu, Nittala Noel Anurag Prashanth, A Hariharasudan. (2025). PROMPTa—A multifaceted assessment approach to develop writing skills. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337342

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of questionnaires and interviews with the experimental cohort, coded using Krismonica et al.'s internal/external framework. Post-intervention, "82% finding the prompts engaging and 88% appreciating the structured nature of PROMPTa"; 78% found peer assessment very helpful. No standardized effect sizes were reported.

> "Anonymity emerged as a crucial factor in boosting confidence, reducing anxiety and fear of judgement, allowing them to write more freely."
