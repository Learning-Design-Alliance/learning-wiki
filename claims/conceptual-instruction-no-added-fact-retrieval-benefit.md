## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Across MD subtypes, fact retrieval practice with or without conceptual instruction produced comparable fact retrieval outcomes (ES = 0.08). [→ Powell 2009](#powell-2009)

## Evidence

### Powell 2009

Powell, S. R., Fuchs, L. S., Fuchs, D., Cirino, P. T., & Fletcher, J. M. (2009). Effects of Fact Retrieval Tutoring on Third-Grade Students with Math Difficulties with and without Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 24(1), 1-11. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.01272.x

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of the two fact retrieval conditions in the randomized trial (n = 139) found a negligible effect size, "ES = 0.08", despite the conceptual condition requiring more minutes per session.

> "Overall, across MD-only and MDRD students, no significant differences between fact retrieval practice, with or without conceptual instruction, emerged (ES = 0.08)."
