## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a structural equation model of nurses' frailty management, knowledge affected practice directly (direct effect = 0.499, p < 0.001) and indirectly via attitude (indirect effect = 0.125), with a total effect of 0.624 and a mediating effect of 20.03% of the total effect. ## Evidence

### Huang Xueyan 2025

Huang Xueyan, Zhou Haifang, Feng Ying, Li Mengchi, Wang Rui, Fang Ge, Lu Lumeng, Chen Jian'ao, Jiang Wenhui. (2025). Knowledge, attitude, and practice of frailty management among clinical nurses: a cross-sectional study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-05776-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling in R 4.3.3 on the 524-nurse survey data, with bias-corrected nonparametric percentile bootstrap mediation testing (200 trials); the model fitted the data (χ2 = 0, CFI = 1). The article reports "the total effect was 0.624 (p < 0.001)" and a mediating effect of 20.03% of the total effect; these are unstandardized path effects, so no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Knowledge influences both the practices of frailty management directly (direct effect = 0.499, p < 0.001,) and indirectly through the mediating effect of attitude (indirect effect = 0.125, p < 0.001), with the total effect was 0.624 (p < 0.001)."
