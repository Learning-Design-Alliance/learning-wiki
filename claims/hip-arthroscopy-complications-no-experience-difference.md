## Evidence

### Ramadanov N 2026

Ramadanov N, Heinz M, Hable R, Becker R, Banke IJ. (2026). Surgeon experience in hip arthroscopy improves operative efficiency and reduces conversion to total hip arthroplasty: A meta‐analysis. Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70431

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of six studies reporting overall complications, where individual odds ratios ranged from 0.09 to 2.15 and most studies showed lower rates in late cases. The pooled model "did not demonstrate a statistically significant difference", with substantial heterogeneity (I² = 81%).

> "The pooled random‐effects model did not demonstrate a statistically significant difference in overall complication rates between late and early cases (OR 0.42; 95% CI 0.12 to 1.51)."
