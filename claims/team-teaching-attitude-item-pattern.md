Descriptive analysis of the 25 experimental students' post-treatment questionnaire responses (means out of 5). The item on teachers controlling a crowded classroom scored highest (4.76), enjoyment of reading comprehension followed (4.75), and feeling more independent scored lowest (3.16); total post-treatment mean was 4.03.

> "Table 9 shows that the highest mean scores was (4.76) for Item No (6) "I feel team teachers can control a crowded classroom in reading compressions lessons", followed by item No (32) "I n team teaching, reading comprehension is more enjoyable" (mean score 4.75), while the lowest mean score was (3.16) for items No (36) "In team teach ing, I feel more independent"."
