## Evidence

### Crawford 2016

Crawford, C., Edwards, A., Farquharson, C., Greaves, E., Trevelyan, G., Wallace, E., & White, C. (2016). Magic Breakfast: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN14385822

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Findings section of the cluster-randomised controlled trial reporting KS2 secondary (teacher-assessed) outcomes. The report states these positive writing and English results "would be unlikely to occur by chance", while KS2 reading (ES 0.103) and maths (ES 0.075) had confidence intervals crossing zero.

> "However, on measures of writing and English, KS2 students in breakfast club schools experienced the equivalent of around two months' progress compared to Year 6 children in the other schools in the trial."
