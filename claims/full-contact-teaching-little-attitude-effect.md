## Subclaims
`q2 i1` During prepandemic full contact teaching, shifts in expert-like thinking were small: effect sizes were typically close to zero and at most |d| = 0.26, with practically no gain in the overall score. [→ Teemu Hynninen 2023](#teemu-hynninen-2023)

## Evidence

### Teemu Hynninen 2023

Teemu Hynninen, Henna Pesonen, Olli Lintu, and Petriina Paturi. (2023). Ongoing eﬀects of pandemic-imposed learning disruption on student attitudes. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post CLASS comparison for the combined full-contact cohorts (N = 70, academic years 2018–2019 and 2019–2020); the printed effect size was small, at most d = 0.26, with effect sizes "typically close to zero", and overall favorable scores were 74 %±4 % pre and 75 %±4 % post.

> "We see positive and negative values for both favorable and unfavorable answers, but the eﬀect sizes are typically close to zero and at most |d| = 0.26, which is still considered to be a small eﬀect."
