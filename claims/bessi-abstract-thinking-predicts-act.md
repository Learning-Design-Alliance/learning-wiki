### Yoon Hee Jun 2024

Yoon Hee Jun, Roberts Brent W., Sewell Madison N., Napolitano Christopher M., Soto Christopher J., Murano Dana, Casillas Alex. (2024). Examining SEB skills' incremental validity over personality traits in predicting academic achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0296484

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Model 2b) predicting ACT composite from demographics plus five BESSI skill scales in the high school sample. The article reports "Abstract Thinking Skill (β = .30, p < .05)" as the only significant positive skill predictor; no effect size is printed.

> "When the BESSI skills were stepped in simultaneously in model 2b, only Abstract Thinking Skill (β = .30, p < .05) positively and statistically significantly predicted the ACT Composite scale score."
