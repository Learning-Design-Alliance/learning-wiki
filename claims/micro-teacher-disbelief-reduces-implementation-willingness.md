### Yedigöz-Kara 2022

Yedigöz-Kara, Z., & Bümen, N. T. (2022). The search for an effective curricular change adoption in foreign language education: A meta-synthesis. International Journal of Curriculum and Instructional Studies, 12(2), 337-366. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1373082

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Teachers' disbelief concerned the value of communicative approaches over grammar-based instruction, and teachers noted their needs were not reflected in the curriculum, particularly regarding speaking and listening.

> "When teachers believe that the curricular change will not foster a student's improvement, they display an unwillingness to implement it, which was best illustrated in the following example: "I feel grammar provides the basic framework for your communication.""
