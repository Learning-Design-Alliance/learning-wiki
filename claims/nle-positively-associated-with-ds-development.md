Path analysis (lavaan, maximum likelihood) in the 2615-student cohort with covariates; the model showed "positively associated with DS (β = 0.117, p < 0.001)" and a good fit (RMSEA = 0.048, CFI = 0.980). The standardized β = 0.117 is below d = 0.2, hence the small-magnitude coding.

> "NLEs were negatively associated with social support (β = −0.346, p < 0.001) and positively associated with DS (β = 0.117, p < 0.001)."
