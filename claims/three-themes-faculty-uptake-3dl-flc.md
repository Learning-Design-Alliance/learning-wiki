## Evidence

### Lydia G. Bender 2020

Lydia G. Bender, James T. Laverty. (2020). Influences on Faculty Uptake from a Faculty Learning Community. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2010.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2010.10384)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative multiple case study of three physics faculty (Ron, Charlie, Molly) in the STEM Teaching & Learning Fellowship, using interviews, assignments, meetings, and forum data coded into PR&A stages. The cross-case analysis "revealed three themes that were inﬂuencing all three of our Fellows."

> "Our data analysis revealed three themes that were inﬂuencing all three of our Fellows. These themes include alignment of assessment and instructional practices, material placement and participation in instruction, and engagement in social interactions."
