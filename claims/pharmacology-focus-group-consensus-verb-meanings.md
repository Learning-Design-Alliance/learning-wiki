### Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín 2025

Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín, Flaherty Caoimhe C., Herzel Lea, and Barry Órla P. (2025). Deciphering the Language of Assessment in Pharmacology Summative Written Assessments. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives 13, no. 6: e70116. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70116

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Facilitator-led, two-hour, audio-recorded focus group guided by fifteen semi-structured questions; "Nine pharmacology educators attended a face‐to‐face focus group" to review and reach consensus on surveyed meanings.

> "Nine pharmacology educators attended a face‐to‐face focus group so the surveyed meanings (both common and diverse, Tables 4 and 5) assigned to the frequently used action verbs and their collocated words could be reviewed."

### Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín 2025 (2)

Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín, Flaherty Caoimhe C., Herzel Lea, and Barry Órla P. (2025). Deciphering the Language of Assessment in Pharmacology Summative Written Assessments. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives 13, no. 6: e70116. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70116

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative transcript analysis of the focus group; for discuss and describe, where two survey respondents had seen no difference, "agreed meanings have now been reached", and explicit meanings were set for explain and key collocations.

> "Most notably for “discuss” and “describe” where previously two respondents did not see any difference, agreed meanings have now been reached (Figure 8)."
