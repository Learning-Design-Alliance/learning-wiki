`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Updated quantitative analyses on the cleaned practice-semester dataset from the cluster randomized trial. The earlier poster-reported aptitude-by-treatment interaction was "no longer statistically significant: Estimate = -0.04, p = 0.71". No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Since the conference, we have cleaned more data and have conducted analyses on this updated set. These analyses indicated that the aptitude by treatment interaction that was reported on the poster was no longer statistically significant: Estimate = -0.04, p = 0.71."
