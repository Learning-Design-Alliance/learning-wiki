Frequency analysis of the main focus of the 478 included studies in this systematic review. The review found "Implementation of EPAs" the main focus in 52.9% (n = 253) and Development in 33.3% (n = 159); impact assessment of implementation was a focus in 11.9% (n = 57).

> "One of the main focuses of 52.9% (n = 253) of the studies was the Implementation of EPAs, whereas the Development of EPAs was found to be a main focus of 33.3% (n = 159)."
