## Evidence

### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's discussion cites a before–after OHCA clinical study, reporting that pit-crew training "alongside real-time feedback and postevent debriefing" was associated with improved neurologically intact survival (OR 2.3, 95% CI 1.3 to 4.0); the article characterises previous studies as observational and multi-component.
