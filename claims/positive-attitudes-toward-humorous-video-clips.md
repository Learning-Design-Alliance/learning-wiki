## Evidence

### Malihe Neissari 2017

Malihe Neissari, Hamid Ashraf & Mohammad Reza Ghorbani. (2017). Humorous Videos and Idiom Achievement: Some Pedagogical Considerations for EFL Learners. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 5(3), 109-127. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1156181

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive survey of the 33 EG participants using a modified 15-item Attitude/Motivation Test Battery (Gardner, 2005) on a 5-point scale. Participants strongly agreed with items one and nine and only moderately agreed with the other items; the overall mean was 3.89 (SD = 0.32). No inferential test was reported.

> "The overall mean score of 3.89 indicates their positive attitudes toward the application of humorous video clips in idiom instruction."
