## Evidence

### Graziano 2016

Graziano, P. A., & Hart, K. (2016). Beyond behavior modification: Benefits of social–emotional/self-regulation training for preschoolers with behavior problems. Journal of School Psychology, 58, 91–111. http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2016.07.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

ANOVAs on attendance and satisfaction in the randomized trial found no overall attendance differences but marginally higher PT satisfaction for the enhanced group; the quote reports the printed means.

> "parents of children who were in the STP-PreK Enhanced reported marginally higher levels of satisfaction with PT (M = 4.68, SD = .26) compared to parents of children who were in the regular STP-PreK (M = 4.38, SD = .44)"
