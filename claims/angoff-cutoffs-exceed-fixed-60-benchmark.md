## Evidence

### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2026

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Hisham Abdul Rauf Badrul, Majid Muhammad Nazmi Abdul, Foong Chan Choong, Ho Ting Khee, Friedlander Lara T. (2026). Refining competency benchmarks: a scoping review of Angoff standard-setting in dental education. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08195-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports from Yousef et al.'s retrospective studies of Saudi dental MCQ examinations that Angoff cut-offs were consistently higher than the fixed 60% passing score, and that "a cut-off score of 74.31% would have led to a 41% failure rate".

> "Indeed, the Angoff method significantly affected student outcomes; for instance, a cut-off score of 74.31% would have led to a 41% failure rate in an endodontic examination [26]."
