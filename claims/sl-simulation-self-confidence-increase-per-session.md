## Evidence

### Rhee Jiyoung 2025

Rhee Jiyoung, Park Hyun Soo. (2025). Standardized learner simulation for debriefer training through video conference. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.321

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures survey analysis (generalized estimating equations model, session as independent variable, trainees as clustering variable) of seven single-item 5-point self-confidence surveys collected before and after e-learning and after each of five sessions. The article reports "estimate=0.114, standard error=0.020, p<0.001"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The self-confidence significantly increased with each additional session (estimate=0.114, standard error=0.020, p<0.001), indicating a steady positive trend over time."
