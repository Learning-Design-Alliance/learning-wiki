What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Level One™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/541

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

WWC analysis of the Fischel et al. randomized controlled trial across six print knowledge measures (19 classrooms/270 children). The domain average effect size was 0.16 (ns) and the average improvement index was +7 percentile points (range –4 to +13).

"Furthermore, the average effect size was neither statistically significant nor large enough to be considered substantively important according to the WWC criteria (that is, at least 0.25)."

What Works Clearinghouse 2007 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Level One™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/541

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

For the Get Ready to Read! Screen (19 classrooms/268 children), the study authors found significant differences favoring Waterford (effect size 0.32, improvement index +13), but WWC recalculation with clustering correction found it not statistically significant.
