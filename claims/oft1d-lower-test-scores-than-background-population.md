### Spangmose Anne Lærke 2022

> "O-mT1D and O-fT1D had lower mean test scores than O-BP (mean differences −1.59 [95% CI −2.48 to −0.71, p < 0.001] (Cohen's d = −0.06) and −0.78 [95% CI −1.48 to −0.08, p = 0.03] (Cohen's d = −0.03), respectively)"
