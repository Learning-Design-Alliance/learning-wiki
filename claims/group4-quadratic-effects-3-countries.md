### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group 4 of the TIMSS 2019 analysis: the 'Quadratic effects of x and y' family estimates quadratic effects of autonomy and control while constraining other second- and third-order effects to null. The model "showed excellent fit to the data" with structural invariance across the three countries.

> "explaining 7.2% of achievement levels (R2 = .072), representing an increase in explained variance of 11% relative to the linear model (R2 = .065)."
