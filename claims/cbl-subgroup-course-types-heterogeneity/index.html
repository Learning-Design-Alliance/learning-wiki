Evidence¶

Wu Fanghui 2023¶

Wu Fanghui, Wang Tao, Yin Danxu, Xu Xiaoxiao, Jin Cancan, Mu Nan, Tan Qingrong. (2023). Application of case-based learning in psychology teaching: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04525-5

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of the 15 RCTs split into basic theory courses (n = 2) and applied courses (n = 13); both subgroups favored CBL, but after sub-grouping "the heterogeneity in the basic theory group became non-significant (I2 = 0.00%, p = 0.64), while it remained high in the applied course group (I2 = 81.80%, p = 0.00)".

"in basic theory courses, the students in the CBL group had significantly higher exam scores than did those in the control group [Hedges’ g = 0.61, 95%CI (0.33, 0.89), p < 0.00]. Likewise, in the applied psychological courses, the exam scores in the CBL group were obviously higher than those in the traditional lecture group [Hedges’ g = 0.69, 95%CI (0.47, 0.92), p < 0.00]."
