## Evidence

### Gonçalves Lia Lopes 2017

Gonçalves Lia Lopes, Voos Mariana Callil, de Almeida Maria Helena Morgani, Caromano Fátima Aparecida. (2017). Massage and Storytelling Reduce Aggression and Improve Academic Performance in Children Attending Elementary School. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/5087145

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing mean grades across all curricular disciplines, tested in April (before) and September (after) the interventions, for the three classroom groups. The article reports η = 0.149 for the significant group × period interaction.

> "We found a significant interaction between groups and periods, with F2,117 = 10.19; p < 0.001; η = 0.149."

### Gonçalves Lia Lopes 2017 (2)

Gonçalves Lia Lopes, Voos Mariana Callil, de Almeida Maria Helena Morgani, Caromano Fátima Aparecida. (2017). Massage and Storytelling Reduce Aggression and Improve Academic Performance in Children Attending Elementary School. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/5087145

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc Tukey tests on mean grades. The massage group (p = 0.038) and storytelling group (p = 0.045) each scored significantly higher than the control group after the interventions; no effect size is printed for these pairwise contrasts.

> "The post hoc Tukey test showed that the mean grade was significantly higher in the MG (p = 0.038) and SG (p = 0.045) compared to the CG after the interventions (Figure 3)."
