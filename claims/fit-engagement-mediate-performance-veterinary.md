## Evidence

### Muca Edlira 2023

Muca Edlira, Molino Monica, Ghislieri Chiara, Baratta Mario, Odore Rosangela, Bergero Domenico, Valle Emanuela. (2023). Relationships between psychological characteristics, academic fit and engagement with academic performance in veterinary medical students. BMC Veterinary Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-023-03695-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapping with 2,000 replications in the SEM of 231 veterinary students confirmed "all the indirect effects were statistically significant", including Loc -> AcFit -> Eng -> GPA (Est. 0.06, p = 0.029) and SEff -> AcFit -> Eng -> GPA (Est. 0.10, p = 0.004).

> "Table 2 reports the results of the bootstrapping procedure, which confirmed that all the indirect effects were statistically significant."
