Evidence¶

Cherry Youn 2022¶

Cherry Youn, Andrew D. Grotzinger, Christina M. Lill, Lars Bertram, Florian Schmiedek, Martin Lövdén, Ulman Lindenberger, Michel Nivard, K. Paige Harden, Elliot M. Tucker-Drob. (2022). Genetic associations with learning over 100 days of practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00121-2

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of pre- to post-test standardized difference scores in the COGITO intensive-practice study (N = 131, ages 20–31 and 65–80). The article reports that "All mean differences for each of the nine cognitive tasks are positive", with standard deviations ranging from 0.75 to over 1 indicating individual differences in learning.

"All mean differences for each of the nine cognitive tasks are positive, suggesting that on average, performance improved during the practice phase and most individuals scored higher at post-test than at pre-test"
