`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on treatment-group-only data (21 males, 26 females) by gender. Adjusted means were 57.90 for males and 54.38 for females; the article reports no significant difference, and equivalence was not tested. No effect size is printed.

> "Summary of result in Table 5 indicates that the F.c alculated value is 1.238 while the F.critical value  at 95% confidence level is 4.08. Since the calculated valu e is less than the critical value the researcher up holds the null hypothesis and concludes that there is no significant difference in the mean Igbo Language reading comprehension scores of male and female students taught using the cloze approach."
