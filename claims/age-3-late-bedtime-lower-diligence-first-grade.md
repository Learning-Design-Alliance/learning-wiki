## Evidence

### Nishiyama Masahiro 2023

Nishiyama Masahiro, Kyono Yuki, Yamaguchi Hiroshi, Kawamura Aoi, Oikawa Shizuka, Tokumoto Shoichi, Tomioka Kazumi, Nozu Kandai, Nagase Hiroaki. (2023). Association of early bedtime at 3 years of age with higher academic performance and better non-cognitive skills in elementary school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple logistic regression in the non-cognitive-skills sample (adjusted n = 3036; 450 children, 11.0%, reported low diligence). Odds of low diligence rose monotonically with later bedtime, from OR 1.98 (21:00) to OR 2.33 (≥23:00), all confidence intervals excluding 1.

> "Children who went to bed later had low diligence (21:00 vs. 18:00–20:00, OR: 1.98, 95% CI 1.27–3.09; 22:00 vs. 18:00–20:00, OR: 2.15, 95% CI 1.37–3.38; and ≥ 23:00 vs. 18:00–20:00, OR: 2.33, 95% CI 1.29–4.20)."
