In the interview discourse analysis, participants invoked four common structures: the K12 education system, socioeconomic factors, parenting expectations, and historical legacies of overt sexism and racism. One participant acknowledged helplessness, saying "you can't just change society."

> "Another common way they did this was to attribute the causes of inequity to large societal structures over which no one could easily have influence, particularly the participants themselves."
