## Evidence

### Howie Erin K. 2023

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Pre-post natural-experiment study (Erwin et al., 2019) with 728 K-6 students in Kentucky where recess increased from one to two 15-minute periods; mixed-effects models showed discipline referrals increased and math achievement improved. No effect size was printed.

> "Discipline referrals increased and math achievement improved with increased recess."

### Howie Erin K. 2023 (2)

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Study of discipline-referral timing (Fedewa et al., 2021) with 607 K-6 students in the same Kentucky natural experiment found more referrals were made as time elapsed from the scheduled recess. No effect size was printed.

> "Discipline referrals increased with more time elapsed since recess period"
