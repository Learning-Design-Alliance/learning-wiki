# Responses from non-cheaters in an RP-RCT are differentially private with privacy loss determined by the FRR parameters

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among non-cheaters, the privatized response generated by an RP-RCT is (ln(2/(r+r'-1)), 0)-differentially private, with the privacy loss set by the investigator's choice of FRR parameters. [→ Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang 2021](#manjusha-kancharla-and-hyunseung-kang-2021)

## Evidence

### Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang 2021

Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang. (2021). A Robust, Differentially Private Randomized Experiment for Evaluating Online Educational Programs With Sensitive Student Data. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.02452](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.02452)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical result (Theorem 1) deriving the differential privacy guarantee of the RP-RCT for non-cheaters. The proof shows the privatized outcome "would be private up to some privacy loss parameter" determined by the FRR parameters r and r', which investigators choose. No empirical data are involved.

> "In words, Theorem 1 states that regardless of the non-cheater's true response Yi, the privatized outcome generated from a RP-RCT, ~Yi would be private up to some privacy loss parameter."
