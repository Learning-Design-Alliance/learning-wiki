## Evidence

### LM Dana 2023

LM Dana, Benjamin Pollard, and Sara Mueller. (2023). More than technical support: the professional contexts of physics instructional labs. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey respondents answered a 10-item perceived-value scale on a 5-point Likert scale; average scores were plotted by role (Figure 3). Faculty responses peaked at a score of 4 and staff responses peaked in the 3.5 bin, but the article reports: "by both ANOVA and Mann-Whitney testing there is no signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the distributions." Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, by both ANOVA and Mann-Whitney testing there is no signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the distributions."

### LM Dana 2023 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Psychometric check of the authors' 10-item instructional labs value scale (Table IV), with items 7 and 9 reverse coded. The article states: "Responses to this scale were well behaved with a Cronbach's alpha of 0.828."

> "Responses to this scale were well behaved with a Cronbach's alpha of 0.828, which corresponds to a good internal consistency between responses on items."
