q2 · i? · review · r3

Review category 1: eighteen studies (samples where reported ranging from 12 to 277 participants) conducted ISS to assess performance and identify risks such as medication errors and latent safety threats. The review reports "Authors reported positive benefits of conducting ISS to identify risks and hazards in clinical environments".

"Eighteen studies conducted ISS as a method of assessment (Table 2). Studies were conducted in the US, Canada, Denmark, Sweden, UK, Germany, Switzerland. Most studies were observational (n = 17), with one study reporting a quasi-experimental design"
