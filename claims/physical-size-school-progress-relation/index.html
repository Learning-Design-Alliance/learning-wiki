Evidence¶

Bird T. Baldwin 1922¶

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of school grades and physical measurements from two schools, treating pedagogical age as an equivalent of mental development. The author states the conclusion "is based on 21,682 final term grades and 5000 physical measurements on 125 boys and girls."

"This conclusion is based on 21,682 final term grades and 5000 physical measurements on 125 boys and girls from the Horace Mann School at Teachers College, Columbia, University, and The Francis Parker School in Chicago."
