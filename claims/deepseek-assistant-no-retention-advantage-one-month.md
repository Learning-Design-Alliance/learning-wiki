### Fan Hao 2025

Fan Hao, Du Wenqin, Yan Jia, Jiang Hong. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-based AI teaching assistant in teaching anesthesiology theories. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08494-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same stratified RCT, a follow-up test one month after the course compared retention in both groups; the independent-samples t-test showed "no statistically significant difference was found in knowledge retention between the two groups". Table 2 reports 30-day means of 64.00 ± 10.48 vs 60.58 ± 11.03; no effect size is printed, so the modest 3.4-point gap was not tested as significant.

> "However, no statistically significant difference was found in knowledge retention between the two groups (P = 0.277 > 0.05)."
