# Welcoming-school students whose schools relocated into closed-school buildings transferred out at higher rates in the merger year; closures did not affect mobility in later years

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Molly F. Gordon 2017

Molly F. Gordon, Marisa de la Torre, Jennifer R. Cowhy, Paul T. Moore, Lauren Sartain, and David Knight. (2017). School Closings in Chicago: Staff and Student Experiences and Academic Outcomes. UChicago Consortium Research Report. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quantitative portion using administrative data from 2008-09 through 2016-17 for all students in closed and welcoming schools, compared to their prior outcomes and to students in similar schools. Only the relocation subgroup showed elevated transfers, in the merger year only.

> "Students attending welcoming schools that relocated into the building of closed schools transferred out at higher rates in the year of the merger; mobility was not affected by school closures in subsequent years for either group of students."
