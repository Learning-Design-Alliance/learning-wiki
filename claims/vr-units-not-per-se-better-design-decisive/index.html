Evidence¶

Thomas Keller 2019¶

Thomas Keller, Fabian Hagen and Elke Brucker-Kley. (2019). A FIELD STUDY ABOUT THE IMPACT OF A VR LEARNING UNIT. 16th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2019). https://doi.org/10.33965/celda2019_201911L038

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive conclusion from the field experiment, in which the VR unit was equivalent to conventional teaching overall. This is the article's own stated takeaway rather than a separately tested result.

"Finally, it must be noted that the use of VR learning units does not per se lead to better learning success. As with conventional teaching methods, the quality and design of such VR learning units plays a decisive role."
