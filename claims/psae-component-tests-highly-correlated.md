# PSAE component-test scores are highly correlated within and across subjects, with ACT science and ISBE science correlating at r = 0.74 in 2004

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of 2004 CPS PSAE component scores; the printed correlation is r = 0.74, a large effect size. The report states "The correlation of students' scores on the ACT science test with scores on the ISBE-developed science test was 0.74 in 2004," explaining 55 percent of shared variability against a maximum possible of 0.87 given reliability.

> "The correlation of students' scores on the ACT science test with scores on the ISBE-developed science test was 0.74 in 2004."

Cross-tabulation of 2004 math and reading performance levels (Figure 12, raw scale-score correlation 0.75). The report states "44 percent were in the academic warning category and 53 percent were below standards on the PSAE reading test" among math academic-warning students; 9,165 students did not meet standards on either test.

> "In 2004, 20 percent of all students who took the math portion of the PSAE received the academic warning designation. Of these students, 44 percent were in the academic warning category and 53 percent were below standards on the PSAE reading test."
