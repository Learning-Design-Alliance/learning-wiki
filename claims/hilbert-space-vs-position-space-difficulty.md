## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving student understanding of addition of angular momentum in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Multiple-choice question administered to 33 undergraduate students before instruction on addition of angular momentum but after instruction on relevant concepts. "only 48% of the students chose the correct answer A" and about 25% selected option E. The correct Hilbert space for a 1D infinite square well is infinite dimensional.

> "However, only 48% of the students chose the correct answer A. About 25% of the students selected the answer E and incorrectly believed that both the energy eigenstates and position eigenstates form a basis in a 1D Hilbert space."
