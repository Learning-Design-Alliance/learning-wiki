## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, Kalman E, Haq I, Leaver A, Roberts C, Bleasel J. (2020). Interprofessional team-based learning (TBL): how do students engage? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02024-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses; each TBL class had facilitators including one rheumatologist, one physiotherapist and one academic with basic science expertise. Closed items showed 76% agreed they received useful and timely feedback from the tutor.

> "Students felt having an interprofessional team of facilitators who provided immediate feedback helped to consolidate student learning and promoted clinical reasoning."
