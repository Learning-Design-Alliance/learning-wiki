## Evidence

### Rybak Alexis 2025

Rybak Alexis, Decavele Maxens, Taytard Jessica, Allaire Manon, Sabourdin Nada, Serresse Laure, Nathan Nadia, Tamzali Yanis, Renaud Marie-Christine, Carrié Alain, Foy Jean-Philippe, Monsel Antoine, Bottemanne Hugo. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective single-center cohort study of SPs across three OSCE sessions at a French medical university, with surveys at T2 (exam day) and T3 (one week later). The study reports "VSs were reported for 37 participants at T2 and/or T3 (20%)"; day-of-exam median intensity was 30 (IQR 20–50) and 26/224 (12%) reported VSs at T2.

> "Among the 180 SPs who responded to both T2 and T3, VSs were reported for 37 participants at T2 and/or T3 (20%). Of note, only 5/180 (3%) participants reported symptoms both the day of the exam and the following week."
