## Evidence

### Strohmaier 2025

Strohmaier, A. R., Schons, C., Knabbe, A., Vogel, M., Saado, K., & Obersteiner, A. (2025). The relation between text comprehension and computation in mathematical word-problem solving. Learning and Instruction, 101, 102235. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102235

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same eye-tracking mixed-model results: numerical difficulty on situational text in second phase, estimate = 1.02, p < .001; mathematical abilities estimate = -0.49, p = .002. The article reports this as the attention-crossover effect "predicted by integrated models". No standardized effect size printed.

> "In addition to canonical effects, we found that mathematical problem difficulty and abilities also affected the processing of situational text. This attention crossover effect was predicted by integrated models."
