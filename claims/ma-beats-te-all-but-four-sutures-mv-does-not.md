## Evidence

### Cheng Meng 2025

Cheng Meng, Wang Xinle, Zhang Man, Li Sainan, Geng Cuizhi. (2025). Video based on the combination of animation and modules is effective in teaching suture technologies to novices without faculty involvement: a new surgical video course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06915-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of pre-to-post self-evaluation score gains in the randomized study; Table 2 prints pairwise p-values (e.g., MA vs. TE p = 0.008 for HMS; TE vs. MV p = 0.794 for SIS). The article reports the MA advantage over TE "for all suturing methods except SIS, FIS, CMS, and PSS" and no MV-vs-TE advantage.

> "When contrasted with Group TE, the instructional approach integrating modular demonstrations and animated simulations proved markedly superior in boosting post-course scores for all suturing methods except SIS, FIS, CMS, and PSS. However, this advantage did not manifest when comparing Group MV with Group TE"
