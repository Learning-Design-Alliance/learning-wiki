## Evidence

### Sutcher 2017

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's narrative review states that multiple studies of effective principal preparation programs found aspiring principals learn in cohorts, with positive effects including "an increased likelihood of participants completing the program," perceiving they are better prepared, and more collegial and supportive learning environments. Cohorts let isolated school leaders find "support, reflection, and insight" from peers facing the same challenges.
