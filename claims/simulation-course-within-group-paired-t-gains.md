### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests within the experimental group (n = 58) in the non-randomized pretest–posttest study. Post-test means rose from 26.50 to 37.34 (confidence), 90.05 to 111.28 (self-efficacy), and 57.21 to 67.79 (competence), all "p < 0.001".

> "For the paired t-test analysis, the post-test scores for the experimental group were significantly higher than the pre-test scores for communication confidence, self-efficacy, and competence (p < 0.001) for all three variables."
