# Medical students rated the standardized patient unit significantly higher than the VR unit on estimated learning success

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a within-subjects study, the conventional standardized patient unit was rated significantly higher on perceived learning than the VR virtual patient unit (η2p = 0.67). [→ Graf Linda 2026](#graf-linda-2026)

## Evidence

### Graf Linda 2026

Graf Linda, Golz Vera, Prehn Sophia, Masuch Maic, Gradl-Dietsch Gertraud. (2026). Virtual vs. standardized patients - a preliminary comparison of advantages and disadvantages of a VR and a conventional learning unit for medical students in child and adolescent psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08760-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA of questionnaire data from 50 medical students in a within-subjects comparison of a VR and a conventional unit, with role (speaker vs. observer) as between-subject factor. The conventional unit was "rated higher on average than the VR unit condition for all participants"; no interaction or role effect.

> "Regarding the estimated learning success (Fig. 3), the learning units (VR and conventional) significantly differ, F (1, 44) = 89.9, p < .001, η2p = 0.67."
