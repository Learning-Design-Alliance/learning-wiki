## Evidence

### Arham Muslim 2020

Arham Muslim, Mohamed Amine Chatti, Mouadh Guesmi. (2020). Open Learning Analytics: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Perspectives. University of Duisburg-Essen. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Objectives (Why?) template-analysis section of the review. Monitoring and analysis dominates because basic statistical indicators are relatively easy to provide; reflection and awareness follows (37 tools), then assessment and feedback (25) and prediction and intervention (20).

> "The main LA objective focused by almost 110 reviewed tools is 'Monitoring and Analysis'. The reason for this high attention is that it is relatively easy to provide basic statistical indicators for the purpose of monitoring different aspects of learners' and educators' activities."

### Arham Muslim 2020 (2)

Arham Muslim, Mohamed Amine Chatti, Mouadh Guesmi. (2020). Open Learning Analytics: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Perspectives. University of Duisburg-Essen. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Same objectives analysis, reviewing 137 tools. The review concludes objectives are predefined; about half of the tools (70) address more than one objective, and reflection/awareness and assessment/feedback objectives gained attention starting 2017 with learner-focused tools.

> "In general, the surveyed tools support predefined objectives and there is a lack of flexible mechanisms to address new objectives of different stakeholders."
