## Evidence

### Anderson Steven Mark 2025

Anderson Steven Mark, Gillanders Sarahlouise, Hickey Anne, Offiah Gozie, Davis Niall. (2025). Video Killed the Radio Star—A Meta‐Analysis on Video‐Based Coaching to Improve Surgical Skill. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.12494

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of nine RCTs reporting final performance scores, with 94 VBC and 89 control participants; only final OSATS scores were used where multiple assessments existed. The pooled effect size was SMD 0.71 (95% CI 0.37, 1.04), showing scores "significantly higher than the controls".

> "Overall, the average performance scores for VBC were significantly higher than the controls, with a SMD of 0.71 (95% CI 0.37, 1.04, Z = 4.15, p < 0.0001, and Figure 3)."
