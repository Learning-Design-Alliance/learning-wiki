## Evidence

### Prado 2020

Prado, X., Domínguez, J. M., Area, I., Edelstein, J., Mira, J., & Paredes, Á. (2020). Learning and Teaching Einstein's Theory of Special Relativity: State of the Art. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.15149](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.15149)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Villani & Pacca (1987), that students "have problems in accepting that measurements of length and time may depend on the reference frame", attributing absolute character to rest-frame measurements. No effect size is printed.

> "Villani & Pacca (1987) found that students have problems in accepting that measurements of length and time may depend on the reference frame and, at any rate, attribute an “absolute” character to the measurements performed in a reference frame at rest with respect to the observer"
