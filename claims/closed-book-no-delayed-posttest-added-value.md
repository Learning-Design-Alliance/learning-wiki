# Closed-book self-explanation prompts show no added value on delayed posttest performance, even with prior active processing of instructional explanations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to hypothesis 3b, Experiment 2 did not find closed-book prompts superior on a delayed posttest when learners had received active processing prompts beforehand. [→ Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020](#sara-hiller-stefan-rumann-kirsten-berthold-julian-roelle-2020)
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, no significant interaction with prompt format emerged for delayed posttest performance. [→ Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020 (2)](#sara-hiller-stefan-rumann-kirsten-berthold-julian-roelle-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020

Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle. (2020). Example-based learning: should learners receive closed-book or open-book self-explanation prompts? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09523-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (N = 124) removed the immediate posttest to avoid confounding the delayed posttest; despite hypothesis 3b, the authors report "not superior to the open-book prompts" in the active-processing groups. Delayed posttest was one week later.

> "contrary to our expectation, the closed-book self-explanations prompts were not superior to the open-book prompts when the learners received active processing of instructional explanation prompts beforehand"

### Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020 (2)

Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle. (2020). Example-based learning: should learners receive closed-book or open-book self-explanation prompts? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09523-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on delayed posttest performance in Experiment 1 found no format × active-processing interaction ("F(1, 77) = 1.05, p = .307, ηp2 = .01"), unlike at the immediate posttest.

> "However, other than at the immediate posttest, there was no statistically significant interaction effect, F(1, 77) = 1.05, p = .307, ηp2 = .01."
