Chapman 1921¶

Chapman, J. Crosby and Dale, A. Barbara. (1921). A Further Criterion for the Selection of Mental Test Elements. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=A+Further+Criterion+for+the+Selection+of+Mental+Test+Elements

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The authors derive this ranking from the matched-groups ratio analysis of the five National Intelligence Test subtests, reporting "the order of merit of the tests as measures of native intelligence" as printed.

"the order of merit of the tests as measures of native intelligence, rather than of environmental training. These rank as follows: (1) Opposites Test, (2) Sentence Completion, (3) Logical Selection, (4) Arithmetical Problems, (5) Symbol-digit Substitution."
