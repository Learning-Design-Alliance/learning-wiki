## Evidence

### Xu Binbin 2020

Xu Binbin, Zhang Jinghui, Hou Jianmei, Ma Mengdan, Gong Zhihong, Tang Siyuan. (2020). Nurses' knowledge of peripherally inserted central catheter maintenance and its influencing factors in Hunan province, China: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalised linear model (gamma distribution, log link) on the 4110-nurse survey data, with the knowledge score as dependent variable. The article reports unstandardised B coefficients and Wald χ2 values for each factor; the county-versus-provincial gap is called the most significant finding.

> "Multivariate analysis showed that female nurses (B=0.082, p<0.01) exhibited a higher PICC maintenance knowledge score than male nurses. A lower level of PICC maintenance knowledge was noted for nurses under 25 years of age (B=−0.054, p<0.05), those with a professional title of nurse (B=−0.074, p<0.01), those working in county hospitals (B=−0.028, p<0.01) and those who had not received PICC maintenance training previously (B=−0.075, p<0.01)"
