# Secondary students in AI learning fall into three distinct SDT-based motivational profiles — Disengaged, Developing, and Self-Determined — at both pre- and post-test

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Latent profile analysis identified three qualitatively distinct need-satisfaction profiles at both time points, differing strongly on all five motivational indicators (η²p = 0.55–0.59). [→ Liang 2026](#liang-2026)

## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, S., Yau, K. W., Meng, H., Chiu, T. K. F., King, I., Yam, Y., & Chai, C. S. (2026). From disengaged to self-determined: A latent transition analysis of students' AI learning motivation. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13998-y

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA validation of the three latent profiles among 2,086 matched secondary students showed significant multivariate differences across profiles on all five indicators, with η²p = 0.55, 0.57, 0.56, 0.58, and 0.59 for AICF, AIKG, AISG, AIET, and AIBI respectively (F(2, 2085) values as printed). Profile sizes were Disengaged 7.3% (N = 152), Developing 62.6% (N = 1307), Self-Determined 30.2% (N = 630).
