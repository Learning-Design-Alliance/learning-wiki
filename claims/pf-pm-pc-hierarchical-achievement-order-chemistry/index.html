Evidence¶

Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu 2011¶

Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu, B. Joseph. (2011). Assessment of the Effects of Problem Solving Instructional Strategies On Students' Achievement and Retention in Chemistry With Respect to Location in Rivers State. World Journal of Education, 1(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p74

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test, post-test non-equivalent control group study of 428 SS II students in intact classes randomly assigned to PF, PM and PC. Results in Table 1 show post-test means exceeded pre-test means, and "the hierarchical order of achievement is PF, PM and PC". Table 2 reports the strategy main effect as significant (F = 178.214).

"Based on this observation the subjects in both urban and rural schools made some gains in achievement and for both groups the hierarchical order of achievement is PF, PM and PC."
