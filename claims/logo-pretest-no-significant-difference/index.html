Boo Jia Yi 2016¶

Boo Jia Yi, Leong Kwan Eu. (2016). Effect of Using Logo on Pupils' Learning in Two-Dimensional Shapes. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 4(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1106476

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on pre-test scores in the quasi-experiment: experimental M = 54.28 (SD = 5.42), control M = 57.17 (SD = 4.66). "t(34) = -1.72, p = .09"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

"The independent t-test indicated that the pre-test mean scores were not statistically significant for the control and experimental group, t(34) = -1.72, p = .09. Thus the null hypothesis that the pre -test mean scores were the same cannot be rejected."
