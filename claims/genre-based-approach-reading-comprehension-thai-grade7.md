## Evidence

### Yutthasak Sawangsamutchai & Saowalak Rattanavich 2016

Yutthasak Sawangsamutchai & Saowalak Rattanavich. (2016). A Comparison of Seventh Grade Thai Students' Reading Comprehension and Motivation to Read English through Applied Instruction Based on the Genre-Based Approach and the Teacher's Manual. English Language Teaching, 9(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n4p54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized control group pretest-posttest study with two clusters of seventh graders (30 per group) at Wat Rajadhivas School. Experimental post-test mean was 18.57 (S.D. 5.23) versus control 10.90 (S.D. 2.71); between-group "t 7.13* .000" and within-experimental "9.10* .000" as printed in Table 1.

> "The data presented in Table 1 demonstrate that the experimental group registered significantly different reading comprehension scores than did the control group at the .05 confidence level."
