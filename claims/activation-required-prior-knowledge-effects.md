## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2021

Garvin Brod. (2021). Toward an understanding of when prior knowledge helps or hinders learning. npj Science of Learning 6:24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00103-w

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Review-reported experiment: in the Bransford and Johnson washing-clothes passage study, participants who received the topic hint beforehand showed strongly enhanced comprehension ratings and recall relative to those given the hint afterward or not at all. The commentary attributes this to activation of relevant prior knowledge.

> "Comprehension ratings and recall performance were strongly enhanced in participants who were given the hint beforehand, which provides evidence that available prior knowledge needs to be activated in order to affect learning."
