The review narrates meta-analytic links between conscientiousness and mental health, printing "d = -1.01" (Kotov et al., 2010) and "r = .22" (Steel et al., 2008), and attributes mechanisms to goal persistence, emotional regulation, and healthier lifestyles.

> "Individuals high in conscientiousness tend to display greater goal persistence, better emotional regulation (Barańczuk, 2019), and healthier lifestyles (Bogg & Roberts, 2004), all of which contribute to improved mental health outcomes (Kotov et al., 2010: d = -1.01; Steel et al., 2008: r = .22)."
