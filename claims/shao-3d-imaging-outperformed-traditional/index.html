Effectiveness of technology-enhanced learning in neuroanatomy education among health professionals - a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09764-w

q3 · i? · review · r3

Study of 60 clinical students randomised across traditional instruction, hand-held scanner 3D imaging and 2D-fitting 3D methods, assessed by objective exam papers and subjective questionnaires. The 3D scanner group "outperformed the traditional teaching group (P < 0.01)".

"In terms of exam outcomes, clinical practice assessment, and teacher satisfaction, the hand-held scanner 3D imaging group outperformed the traditional teaching group (P < 0.01)."
