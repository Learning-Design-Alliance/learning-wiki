Kang and Noh 2017¶

Houn Tae Kang, Suk Goo Noh. (2017). The Effect on Elementary Science Education Based on Student's Pre-inquiry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050908

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest comparison of the awareness-of-science subelements in the two-class quasi-experiment; the awareness of science education subelement favored the experimental group with a "significant difference". Awareness of science (3.72 vs 3.12) and of science-related jobs (3.77 vs 3.22) were also significant.
