### Barbara A. Oakley and Terrence J. Sejnowski 2019

Barbara A. Oakley and Terrence J. Sejnowski. (2019). What we learned from creating one of the world's most popular MOOCs. npj Science of Learning 4:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0046-0

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the authors' interpretive proposal, grounded in their LHTL production experience and cited attention research, not a tested result. In LHTL, rarely do more than 30 s pass before some type of motion or pictorial change unfolds.

> "It is reasonable to posit that motion and other visual or audio change and surprises might help viewers maintain attention on a video via "bottom-up" attentional processes, allowing them to more easily resist mind wandering without applying conscious effort."
