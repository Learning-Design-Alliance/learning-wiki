## Evidence

### Cerutti Bernard 2019

Cerutti Bernard, Stollar Fabiola, Escher Monica, Blondon Katherine, Aujesky Susanne, Nendaz Mathieu, Galetto-Lacour Annick. (2019). Comparison of long-menu and single-best-answer multiple choice questions in computer-based summative assessments: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1651-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the distractors chosen in the long-menu format during the trial: 616 choices (45.6%) matched Type A distractors and 736 (54.4%) did not. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Among the options chosen by the students in the long-menu format questions, 616 (45.6%) were distractors listed in the Type A questions, and 736 (54.4%) were not proposed as distractors in the Type A questions."
