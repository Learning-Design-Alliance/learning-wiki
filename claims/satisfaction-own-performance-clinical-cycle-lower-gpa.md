## Evidence

### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square and rank-sum comparisons of 80 dissatisfied vs 38 satisfied students (Table 8): "students in clinical academic years were significantly less satisfied; p < 0.019" and dissatisfied students had lower GPAs (p < 0.012). No effect size is printed.

> "The significant satisfaction differences were related to the position in the academic cycle (students in clinical academic years were significantly less satisfied; p < 0.019) and to the GPA itself (dissatisfied students had lower GPAs; p < 0.012)."
