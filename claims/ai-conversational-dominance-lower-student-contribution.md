# Despite longer AI-led sessions, the proportion of student-generated dialogue was markedly and significantly lower with the AI than with the teacher in both grades

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student word proportion was significantly lower with the AI for Grade 4 (t(11)=−4.89, p<.001) and Grade 5 (t(6)=−5.62, p=.001), and sentence proportion was drastically lower (Grade 4 t(11)=−6.15, p<.001; Grade 5 t(6)=−8.21, p<.001). [→ Liao 2026](#liao-2026)

## Evidence

### Liao 2026

Liao, C.-Y. (2026). Beyond a single role: Justifying a role-adaptive framework for ai companions through a comparative study in elementary book talk. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13958-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests on student-level means from the same within-subjects study (N=19) compared word and sentence contribution proportions across AI-led and teacher-led sessions. In Grade 5, "student contribution was less than a third of that in teacher-led sessions." No effect size printed.

> "Student Word Proportion: The percentage of words contributed by students was significantly lower with the AI for both Grade 4 (t(11) = −4.89, p <.001) and Grade 5 ( t(6) = −5.62, p =.001). Student Sentence Proportion: Similarly, the proportion of sentences from students was drastically lower in the AI condition for both Grade 4 (t(11) = −6.15, p <.001) and Grade 5 (t(6) = −8.21, p <.001)."
