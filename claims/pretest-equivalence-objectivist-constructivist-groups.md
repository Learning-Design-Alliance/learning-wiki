# Pretest scores showed the two groups were statistically equivalent before the intervention

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The constructivist-based and objectivist-based groups were equivalent in achievement levels prior to the teaching intervention. [→ DePriter 2012](#depriter-2012)

## Evidence

### DePriter 2012

DePriter, T. (2012). An Investigation of Teaching Strategy in the Distance Learning Mathematics Classroom. Journal of Educators Online, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020037

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A two-tailed t-test compared pretest means (constructivist M = 13.89, SD = 4.96; objectivist M = 15.06, SD = 3.70) with n1 = 18 and n2 = 17. The article reports the homogeneity-of-variance Sig. Value (.437) exceeded alpha (.05), supporting group equivalence.

> "The Sig. Value (.437) exceeded the alpha level (.05), so it can be concluded that the groups were equivalent in terms of achievement levels prior to the teaching intervention"
