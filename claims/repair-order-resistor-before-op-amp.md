### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2016

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Kevin L. Van De Bogart, MacKenzie R. Stetzer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Investigating the role of model-based reasoning while troubleshooting an electric circuit. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

In the same two-minute-interval coding analysis shown in Fig. 6, filled triangles and stars mark replacement times of the 100 Ω resistor and faulty op-amp. The article reports the order pattern verbatim, including that most resistor replacements occurred "while they were still formulating the problem description." Counts are as printed; no effect sizes are reported.

> "For example, all pairs replaced the resistor and all-but-one replaced the op-amp. In each case, the resistor was replaced before the op-amp was replaced. Indeed, most pairs replaced the resistor early in the troubleshooting process, while they were still formulating the problem description and before they started generating causes."
