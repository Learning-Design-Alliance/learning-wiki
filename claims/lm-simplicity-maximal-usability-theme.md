## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In qualitative interviews, students described Learning Moment as easy to use and intuitive, and reported that any steps perceived as extraneous felt overly burdensome and disengaged them. [→ Chu Andrew 2020](#chu-andrew-2020)

## Evidence

### Chu Andrew 2020

Chu Andrew, Biancarelli Dea, Drainoni Mari-Lynn, Liu James H., Schneider Jeffrey I., Sullivan Ryan, Sheng Alexander Y. (2020). Usability of Learning Moment: Features of an E-learning Tool That Maximize Adoption by Students. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2019.6.42657

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis using grounded theory of semi-structured interviews with 13 medical students; participants praised the minimalist interface, while "any steps perceived as extraneous felt overly burdensome and disengaged students," with attitudes shaped by time scarcity and competing priorities.

> "Any steps perceived as extraneous felt overly burdensome and disengaged students. Attitudes were shaped by time scarcity, alternative learning tools, and competing priorities."
