Kohs 1914¶

Kohs, S. C. (1914). The Binet-Simon Measuring Scale for Intelligence: An Annotated Bibliography. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Binet-Simon+Measuring+Scale+for+Intelligence%22+Kohs+annotated+bibliography

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to Kuhlmann's 1913 International Congress on School Hygiene paper (also in the Journal of Psycho-Asthenics) the argument that the mental-age/chronological-age difference 'does not represent the true degree of deficiency'; no data or effect sizes are printed in the annotation.

"The difference between mental age and chronological age does not represent the true degree of deficiency. The "rate of mental progress" is chosen as much more representative."

  • IQs obtained by dividing mental by chronological age remain fairly constant up to the age of 14, which supports treating yearly increments of intelligence as equal absolute units up to about age 14. — related
