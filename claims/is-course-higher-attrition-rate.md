## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students enrolled in General Chemistry-IS withdrew at a higher rate (25.0%) than college-ready students (17.8%) and than in previous fall semesters. [→ Hesser 2016](#hesser-2016)

## Evidence

### Hesser 2016

Hesser, T. L., & Gregory, J. L. (2016). Instructional Support Sessions in Chemistry: Alternative to Remediation. Journal of Developmental Education, 39(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Withdrawal analysis of the 399 initially enrolled students: 69 of 92 General Chemistry-IS students persisted (25.0% attrition) versus 249 of college-ready students (17.8% attrition). The article reports previous fall withdrawal rates of 13.6% (2013) and 12.0% (2012), and states students in General Chemistry-IS "withdrew from General Chemistry at a higher rate than college-ready students in 2014, 2013, and 2012."

> "These course withdrawals constituted a 25.0% attrition rate for students enrolled in General Chemistry-IS, whereas students enrolled in General Chemistry had a 17.8% attrition rate."
