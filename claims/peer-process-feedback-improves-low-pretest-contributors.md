## Evidence

### Kamp Rachelle J. A. 2012

Kamp Rachelle J. A., Dolmans Diana H. J. M., Van Berkel Henk J. M., Schmidt Henk G. (2012). The effect of midterm peer feedback on student functioning in problem-based tutorials. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-012-9364-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed between-within subjects ANOVA on M-PARS total scores of 74 second-year Health Sciences students divided into low (N = 24), moderate (N = 25) and high (N = 25) pretest groups, with peer feedback in week 4. The interaction effect was significant with "partial eta squared = 0.12".

> "There was a significant interaction effect between group and time, Wilks’ Lambda = 0.88, F (2, 71) = 4.70, p = 0.012, partial eta squared = 0.12."

### Kamp Rachelle J. A. 2012 (2)

Kamp Rachelle J. A., Dolmans Diana H. J. M., Van Berkel Henk J. M., Schmidt Henk G. (2012). The effect of midterm peer feedback on student functioning in problem-based tutorials. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-012-9364-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test within the low-score group (N = 24) in the same mixed-methods explanatory study. The low-score group rose from pretest M = 3.36 to posttest M = 3.52, a "significant, but small increase"; no effect size was printed.

> "Only the group with an initial low score on the M-PARS showed a significant, but small increase in their quality of individual contributions (M = 3.36, SD = 0.15; M = 3.52, SD = 0.33), t(23) = −2.29, p = 0.031)."
