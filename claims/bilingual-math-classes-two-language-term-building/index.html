Baschek 2022 (2)¶

Baschek, E. (2022). Using the US standard system for measurement in Germany – Bilingual education in primary school. https://math-primwq-bilingual.sd.uni-frankfurt.de/length-laengen/introduction-einleitung/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Author's theoretical argument extending the linguistic-subject linkage of monolingual mathematics classes to bilingual settings, where term conceptions develop through linguistic negotiation in two languages. No data reported.

"Technical terms can be built up in two language structures, whereby nuances and differences in meaning can support the learning of terms."
