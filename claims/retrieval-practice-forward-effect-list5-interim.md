# Overt retrieval practice enhances interim recall of new List-5 material compared to restudy, and does not differ from JOLs with word stems

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Retrieval practice after Lists 1–4 improved interim List-5 recall relative to restudy (d = 0.59). [→ Veit Kubik 2022](#veit-kubik-2022)
`q3 i?` Overt retrieval practice and JOLs with word stems did not significantly differ in interim List-5 recall (d = 0.08, p = .725). [→ Veit Kubik 2022 (2)](#veit-kubik-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Veit Kubik 2022

Veit Kubik, Kenneth Koslowski, Torsten Schubert, Alp Aslan. (2022). Metacognitive judgments can potentiate new learning: The role of covert retrieval. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:1057–1077. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09307-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Planned contrast in the same N = 161 multiple-list experiment: the retrieval group "trumped restudy" on interim List-5 free recall, t(78) = 2.59, p = .006, d = 0.59 (one-tailed).

> "In addition, the data showed that practicing retrieval, t(78) = 2.59, p = .006, d = 0.59, 95% CI [0.20, ∞] (one-tailed), trumped restudy."

### Veit Kubik 2022 (2)

Veit Kubik, Kenneth Koslowski, Torsten Schubert, Alp Aslan. (2022). Metacognitive judgments can potentiate new learning: The role of covert retrieval. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:1057–1077. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09307-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Planned contrast comparing overt retrieval practice with JOLs with word stems on interim List-5 recall in the same experiment: "both practice types did not significantly differ, t(78) = 0.353, p = .725, d = 0.08".

> "A planned contrast revealed that both practice types did not significantly differ, t(78) = 0.353, p = .725, d = 0.08, 95% CI [-0.36, 0.52]."
