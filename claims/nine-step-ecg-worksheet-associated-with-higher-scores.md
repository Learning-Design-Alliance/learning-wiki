`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative analysis within the same randomized study of worksheet users versus non-users on the post-rotation evaluation test. The article reports "Students using ECG interpretation worksheet achieved mean score of 12.5±2.8 compared with 7.9±2.1 among those who did not (p < 0.001)"; the authors frame this as an association rather than a causal effect of worksheet use.

> "Students using ECG interpretation worksheet achieved mean score of 12.5±2.8 compared with 7.9±2.1 among those who did not (p < 0.001), suggesting that the use of the worksheet is associated with better performance."
