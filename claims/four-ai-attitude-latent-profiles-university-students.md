# Latent profile analysis of six AI-attitude indicators yields four profiles: Positive Empowerment, Anxious Acceptance, Low-Perception Detached, and High-Perception High-Vigilance

## Evidence

### Song Hongfeng 2026

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis of Wave 2 data from 379 first-year business-administration students, with six standardized indicators. The four-profile solution showed "an entropy value of 0.824" and average posterior probabilities from 0.883 to 0.949; the smallest profile represented 6.1% of the sample.

> "Latent profile analysis supported four profiles: Positive Empowerment (19.2%), Anxious Acceptance (37.5%), Low-Perception Detached (37.2%), and High-Perception High-Vigilance (6.1%)."
