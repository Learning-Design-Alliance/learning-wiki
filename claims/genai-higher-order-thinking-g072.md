## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Pooled effect sizes show GenAI-assisted education significantly improves higher-order thinking abilities (problem-solving, creativity, critical thinking) relative to non-GenAI approaches, with a moderate-to-large effect. [→ Ying Dong 2026](#ying-dong-2026)

## Evidence

### Ying Dong 2026

Ying Dong. (2026). Generative AI technologies and educational outcomes: a comprehensive meta-analysis comparing traditional and AI-driven approaches. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-06903-y

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of the included comparison studies for higher-order thinking. The pooled estimate was "g = 0.72; 95% CI = 0.30 ~ 1.13, moderate-to-large effect"; the article defines higher-order thinking as problem-solving, creativity, and critical thinking.

> "higher-order thinking abilities (g = 0.72; 95% CI = 0.30 ~ 1.13, moderate-to-large effect), learning motivation (g = 0.81; 95%CI = 0.20 ~ 1.41, large effect)"
