`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The article states, citing prior studies, that researchers report the root of exclusion is "a lack of knowledge about the additional legal, ethical, and methodological issues in trials involving this population". This motivated the training development; no new data are reported for this statement.

> "Whilst there are several challenges associated with conducting research with adults with impaired capacity to consent [13, 14], researchers report that the root of this exclusion is a lack of knowledge about the additional legal, ethical, and methodological issues in trials involving this population"
