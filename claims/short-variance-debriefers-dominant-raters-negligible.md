## Evidence

### Rivière Etienne 2019

Rivière Etienne, Aubin Etienne, Tremblay Samuel-Lessard, Lortie Gilles, Chiniara Gilles. (2019). A new tool for assessing short debriefings after immersive simulation: validity of the SHORT scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1503-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Generalizability study variance-component analysis (Table 2) of the 22 rated short debriefings. The article reports the debriefer facet at "B = 32.1%", items at 12.0%, observations nested in debriefers at 13.5%, and states rater-related effects are "insignificant or null (max. 0.2%)".

> "The largest source of variance is debriefers (B = 32.1%). This is expected and desirable, since debriefers are the objects of the measurement, i.e., those being assessed."
