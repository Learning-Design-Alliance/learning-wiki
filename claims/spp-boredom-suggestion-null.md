# Suggesting the SPP at detected boredom did not change its use: students were almost never bored, so SPP use was not reliably different between conditions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an experiment where the tutor suggested the SPP upon detecting deactivating negative affect, students self-reported almost never being bored, so the tutor almost never made suggestions and total SPP use did not reliably differ between experimental and control groups. [→ Arroyo 2014](#arroyo-2014)

## Evidence

### Arroyo 2014

Arroyo, I., Woolf, B. P., Burelson, W., Muldner, K., Rai, D., & Tai, M. (2014). A Multimedia Adaptive Tutoring System for Mathematics that Addresses Cognition, Metacognition and Affect. Int J Artif Intell Educ 24:387–426. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0023-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experimental evaluation of the SPP as a metacognitive intervention with an affective cross-over purpose: the experimental condition received SPP suggestions at detected boredom while controls had the same page available via a button. The article reports "the total amount of use of the SPP was not reliably different" between groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "students were almost never bored in this experiment, so Wayang almost never suggested that they use the SPP . As a result, the total amount of use of the SPP was not reliably different between the experimental and control groups of students."
