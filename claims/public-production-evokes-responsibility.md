`q2 i?` Most students reported feeling responsibility toward their readership and felt differently about publicly readable assignments than other writing, which motivated concern for accuracy and credible sourcing. [→ Andrea Forte 2015](#andrea-forte-2015)

## Evidence

### Andrea Forte 2015

Andrea Forte. (2015). The new information literate: Open collaboration and information production in schools. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. 10:35–51. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-015-9210-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview reflections across both studies on the Science Online wiki projects; students said public accessibility "puts more pressure on you" to ensure information is true and accurate. The author ties this to motivating information assessment.

> "Most of the students reflected in interviews that they felt a sense of responsibility toward their readership and that they felt differently about a homework assign- ment that could be read by others than about other writing assignments."
