# Trained teachers reallocated time unevenly: significantly more to presenting/developing lessons and quizzes, less to homework review, independent practice, and transitions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Following training, treatment teachers allocated significantly more time to presenting and developing lessons and to administering quizzes, and less to reviewing/correcting homework and independent practice. [→ Fitzpatrick 1985](#fitzpatrick-1985)

## Evidence

### Fitzpatrick 1985

Fitzpatrick, Kathleen A. (1985). Group-Based Mastery Learning: A Robin Hood Approach to Instruction? Paper presented at the annual meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, Illinois. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED255556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the treatment group's time allocations across five instructional functions (excluding the loop), recalculated from observation frequencies. The chi-square test on the 2 x 5 contingency table was significant (64.788, 4 df); Table 2 shows review/correcting homework fell from 43.0 to 35.6 percent and development/guided practice rose from 27.9 to 36.8 percent.

> "A significant Chi-square computed for the 2 x 5,contingency table (64.788, 4 df) leads to rejection of thehypothesis that the pre and post distributions were alike."
