## Evidence

### Pantelidis P 2016

Pantelidis P, Staikoglou N, Paparoidamis G, Drosos C, Karamaroudis S, Samara A, Keskinis C, Sideris M, Giannakoulas G, Tsoulfas G, Karagiannis A. (2016). Medical students’ satisfaction with the Applied Basic Clinical Seminar with Scenarios for Students, a novel simulation-based learning method in Greece. J Educ Eval Health Prof. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.13

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of the ABCS3 course: 176 of 230 attendees answered the DREEM (53 full, 123 observer category). Mann-Whitney U tests showed "full participants scoring higher than OB participants on the overall DREEM score (P=0.038)" and on SPL, SASP, Q1, and Q2.

> "full participants scoring higher than OB participants on the overall DREEM score (P=0.038), in the SPL subscale (P=0.041), in the SASP subscale (P=0.033), and on Q1, Q2 (P=0.026 and P=0.022, respectively)"
