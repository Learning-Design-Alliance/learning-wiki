Evidence¶

de la Torre 2024¶

de la Torre, M., Blanchard, A., Franklin, K., Angeles, C., & Allensworth, E.M. (2024). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: A Spotlight on High School Students. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/english-learners-in-chicago-public-schools-a-spotlight-on-high-school-students

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of CPS cohorts. Non-English Learners with IEPs showed the same pattern with a Freshman OnTrack rate of 84% and graduation rate of 70%, pointing to similar performance among students with IEPs.

"Long-term English Learners with IEPs had a Freshman OnTrack rate similar to the district average (86%). However, this did not translate to a graduation rate (69%) similar to the district average."

de la Torre 2024 (2)¶

de la Torre, M., Blanchard, A., Franklin, K., Angeles, C., & Allensworth, E.M. (2024). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: A Spotlight on High School Students. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/english-learners-in-chicago-public-schools-a-spotlight-on-high-school-students

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Six-year graduation analysis found 77% of this group graduated, similar to other students with an IEP (79%) but substantially below the six-year district average of 89%.

"When calculating six-year graduation rates to allow for two more years to fulfill the graduation requirements, we found that 77% of long-term English Learners with IEPs graduated."
