## Evidence

### F. Escalante 2021

F. Escalante. (2021). Repeat a physics course more than once: A diagnosis of the most frequent misconceptions in newtonian mechanics of first and second-year engineering students. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational pre-test analysis of 600 engineering students in three consecutive physics courses, using the dominant incorrect answer method on FCI wrong answers classified by attempt number. The study concludes "there is no big diﬀerence" in misconception choice across attempts.

> "This means that, if we consider the choice of misconceptions, there is no big diﬀerence between a student on their third attempt and a student on their ﬁrst attempt in each course."
