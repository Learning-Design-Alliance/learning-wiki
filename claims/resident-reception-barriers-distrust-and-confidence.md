> "A total of 77 residents participated in the questionnaire survey, with 71 valid responses after excluding six participants who had not completed outpatient residency training in oral and maxillofacial surgery."

### Long Huimin 2025 (2)

Long Huimin, Nie Xin, Wang Chi. (2025). Learning needs of residents in oral and maxillofacial surgery outpatient clinics: a dual-source survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06980-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Categorized questionnaire responses on the main factors restricting clinical reception; "The primary limiting factors were patients’ distrust (42.25%) and residents’ lack of confidence (36.62%)", together accounting for 78.87% of responses.

> "Most trainees (71.8%) reported seeing 1–5 patients, while 21.13% saw ≥ 6 patients and 7.04% saw no patients. The primary limiting factors were patients’ distrust (42.25%) and residents’ lack of confidence (36.62%), accounting for 78.87% of responses."
