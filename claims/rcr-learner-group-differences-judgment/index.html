Hypothesis H11 tested in the judgment meta-regression (Table 4: school/citizens 1.02* [0.24, 1.79]; HE/professionals -0.30** [-0.50, -0.11]; mixed -0.53* [-0.98, -0.07], relative to researchers). The coefficients are regression coefficients, not standardized effect sizes.
