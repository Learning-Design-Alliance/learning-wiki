Abramson SL, Sora-Cardenas J, Nandakumar P, Bruce JE, Gopinath N, Zangari LG and Feijóo-García PG (2026). Trust and interaction design in AI-enabled systems: a systematic literature review. Front. Psychol. 17:1932655. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1932655

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review (PRISMA) of 33 included studies; four independent researchers coded each paper's primary interaction design factor and grouped them inductively. The review reports "Explainability 20" as the highest count in its factor table, followed by "Anthropomorphism 5" and "Conﬁdence 3".
