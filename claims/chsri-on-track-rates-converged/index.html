Comparative analysis of freshman on-track rates (five credits earned and no more than one semester F in a core course). In 2007-08 the CHSRI rate was 61.0 percent versus 60.7 percent for similar students; no effect size is printed.

"Historically, CHSRI ninth-graders have had higher on-track rates than similar students at similar non-CHSRI schools, although the difference typically has not been statistically significant"
