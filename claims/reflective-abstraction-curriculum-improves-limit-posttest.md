## Evidence

### Cappetta 2013

Cappetta, R.W., and Zollman, A. (2013). Agents of Change in Promoting Reflective Abstraction: A Quasi-Experimental Study on Limits in College Calculus. REDIMAT – Journal of Research in Mathematics Education, 2(3), 343-357. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2013.35

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental posttest analysis of all 35 participating students (16 experimental, 19 traditional) scored on a two-point rubric. The experimental mean was 21.18 versus 16.95 traditional; "Cohen's d = 0.877 so there was a large effect."

> "Using a significance level of p < .05, a one -tailed t-test for equality of means was performed. T he results of the t -test are t (32) = 2.63 and p < .01. Cohen's d = 0.877 so there was a large effect."
