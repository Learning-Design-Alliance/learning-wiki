Artsi Yaara 2024¶

Artsi Yaara, Sorin Vera, Konen Eli, Glicksberg Benjamin S., Nadkarni Girish, Klang Eyal. (2024). Large language models for generating medical examinations: systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05239-y

The review reports Klang et al.'s blind assessment of Chat-GPT 4 MCQs across internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics & gynecology, psychiatry, and pediatrics, using few-shot learning from a former exam and five specialist physicians as evaluators who did not know the questions' AI origin.
