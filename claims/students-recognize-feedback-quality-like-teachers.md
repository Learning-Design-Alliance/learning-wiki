## Evidence

### Himmelbauer M 2018

Himmelbauer M, Seitz T, Seidman C, Löffler-Stastka H. (2018). Standardized patients in psychiatry – the best way to learn clinical skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1184-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire comparison of feedback ratings from 529 students and 29 teachers after the workshop-seminars. The article reports both groups rated feedback very well and no significant student–teacher difference was shown.

> "Both, students and teachers evaluated the quality of the actor’s feedback very well. Only the statement “SP gave constructive criticism and suggestions for improvement” was rated as rather good by both. No significant difference could be shown between the students’ and teachers’ evaluations of the SP’s feedback."
