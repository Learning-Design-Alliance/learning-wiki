## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2016

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux, Stephen E. Kanim. (2016). Obstacles to mathematization in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level result on the Square Buildings question in the large-N multiple-choice assessment of freshman engineering students. The authors note the same tendency on the Force Vectors item, where one-half of students gave the difference-based response, quoting "more than two-thirds selected an incorrect comparison based on the difference between the sides."

> "Only 17% of the freshman engineering students selected the ratio-based answer, however, while more than two-thirds selected an incorrect comparison based on the difference between the sides."
