## Evidence

### Lin Rongmao 2024

Lin Rongmao, Chen Yanping, Shen Yilin, Hu Ting, Huang Ying, Yang Yishan, Yu Xueting, Ding Jinliang. (2024). Academic grit scale for Chinese middle- and upper-grade primary school students: testing its factor structure and measurement invariance. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01622-y

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Structural regression model in the full sample (model fit: CFI = 0.967, RMSEA = 0.033), testing criterion-related validity of the AGS one-factor model. Academic grit was "positively associated with" all three criteria, explaining 46.70%, 65.50%, and 18.10% of their variance.

> "academic grit was positively associated with positive high arousal of academic emotions (β = 0.68, p < .001), positive low arousal of academic emotions (β = 0.81, p < .001), and academic achievement (β = 0.43, p < .001), explaining 46.70%, 65.50%, and 18.10% of the variance in the three variables, respectively."
