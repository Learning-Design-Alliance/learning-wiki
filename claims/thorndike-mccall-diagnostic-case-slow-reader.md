## Evidence

### Arthur I. Gates 1921

Arthur I. Gates. (1921). An Experimental and Statistical Study of Reading and Reading Tests (Continued). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0068367

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Single-case study of S.S., a grade VIII girl with IQ above 125 and oral reading poor for grade III, who scored 65 on the Thorndike-McCall against a grade mean of 63.4 but scored far below class means on Courtis (24 vs 55.6), Monroe (15 vs 38) and Burgess (8 vs 67.8). The authors conclude it is "an adequate measure of her" comprehension excluding speed.

> "We are convinced that this is an adequate measure of her " comprehension," excluding speed. No other test of comprehension gave her a high score."
