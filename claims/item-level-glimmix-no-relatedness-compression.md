## Evidence

### Gupta 2026

Gupta, M.W., Pan, S.C., & Rickard, T.C. (2026). Semantic relatedness and the efficacy of retrieval practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00416-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalized linear mixed model (SAS Proc GLIMMIX) over all high-relatedness-group items with random intercepts for subjects and items; significant effects of relatedness (F(1, 8733) = 24.54) and training condition (F(1, 8733) = 82.77), but "The interaction, however, was not statistically significant."

> "The interaction, however, was not statistically significant, F(1, 8733) = 0.05, p = .82. Hence there is no evidence for compression of the TE for higher relatedness items."
