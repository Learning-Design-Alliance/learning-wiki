# Included PBL studies in Chinese radiology education are of low methodological quality with very high heterogeneity

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both pooled analyses showed very high heterogeneity (I2 = 95%, P < .00001), and the included studies were not randomized controlled trials, with groups divided by classes rather than individual students. [→ Zhang Song 2018](#zhang-song-2018)

## Evidence

### Zhang Song 2018

Zhang Song, Xu Jiancheng, Wang Hongwei, Zhang Dong, Zhang Qichuan, Zou Liguang. (2018). Effects of problem-based learning in Chinese radiology education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000010069

`q3 · i?`

Homogeneity testing of the 16 knowledge-score studies using the Q and I2 statistics showed I2 = 95% (P < .00001); the skill-score analysis showed the same I2 = 95%. The authors state the included studies "were relatively low in study quality as they were not randomized controlled trials."

> "Since a high degree of heterogeneity was noted across the included studies (I2 = 95%, P < .00001), the random-effects model was used for the meta-analysis."
