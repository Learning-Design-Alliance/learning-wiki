### Joseph T. Lizier 2018

Joseph T. Lizier, Michael S. Harré, Melanie Mitchell, Simon DeDeo, Conor Finn, Kristian Lindgren, Amanda L. Lizier, and Hiroki Sayama. (2018). An interview based study of pioneering experiences in teaching and learning Complex Systems in Higher Education. [doi:10.1155/2018/7306871](https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/7306871)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis across the interview set found these projects shared a structure: students chose their own problem or data set, implemented a model or analysis, and assessments were staged with a proposal, presentation, and final report. The paper reports them as "very successful and popular with students" based on feedback, enrolments, and teaching awards.

> "The principle observation to emerge regarding approaches to assessment was that major student- driven creative projects were seen in coursework units acro ss all interviews with teachers and in the report of Porter [9]."
