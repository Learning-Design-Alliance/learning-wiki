Observational analysis of CPS administrative enrollment records (Section I). The report states "enrollment dropped by 11.5 percent" from 106,429 to 94,223 students; Figure 1 shows the decline began in fall 1997-98 after stable enrollment in the early 1990s. No effect size is printed.

"In total, enrollment dropped by 11.5 percent over the last four years of the decade, from 106,429 students in the fall of the 1995–96 school year, to 94,223 students in the fall of 1999–00"
