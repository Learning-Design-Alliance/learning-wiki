Evidence¶

Campoli 2022¶

Campoli, A. K., & Darling-Hammond, L. (with Podolsky, A., & Levin, S.). (2022). Principal learning opportunities and school outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/438.376

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Logistic regression on individual teacher records from the California preparation sample of approximately 200 early-career principals, 6,000 teachers, and 59,000 students, controlling for teacher, school, and district characteristics. Table 2 prints an overall preparation quality odds ratio of 1.130, with developing people at 1.148 and meeting needs of diverse learners at 1.111*. The report notes "An odds ratio greater than 1 means that the teacher is more likely to stay."

"These relationships are statistically significant for overall preparation quality as well as for preparation in developing people and meeting the needs of diverse learners. (See Table 2.)"
