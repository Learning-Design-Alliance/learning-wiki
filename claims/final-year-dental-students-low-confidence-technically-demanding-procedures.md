In the same census survey of 46 final-year dental students, the lowest mean confidence scores were for "stainless steel crown placement (2.46 ± 0.96)" and "pulpotomy (2.72 ± 1.05)" on the 5-point Likert scale.

> "The lowest mean confidence scores were recorded for stainless steel crown placement (2.46 ± 0.96), pulpotomy (2.72 ± 1.05), and preparedness to independently perform pediatric dental treatment after graduation (2.91 ± 1.03)."
