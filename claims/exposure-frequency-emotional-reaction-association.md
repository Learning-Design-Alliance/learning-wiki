`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup comparison of emotional reactions (Question 14) across frequency-of-exposure groups (Question 13) in the COSTRUIRE survey. The article reports that frequent communicators "predominantly reported constructive emotions" while rare communicators "exhibited higher rates of distress." No inferential statistics were performed.

> "Participants with frequent exposure ("Yes, often") predominantly reported constructive emotions, with 73.8% feeling calm and confident and 31.0% feeling tranquil. Conversely, those with rare exposure exhibited higher rates of distress, including insecurity (21.4%), frustration (21.4%), embarrassment (19.6%), and fear (10.7%)."
