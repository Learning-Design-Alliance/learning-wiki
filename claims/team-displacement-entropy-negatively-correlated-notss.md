## Evidence

### Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD 2024

Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD, Dias Roger D. MD PhD, Kennedy-Metz Lauren R. PhD, Varni Giovanna PhD, Gombolay Matthew PhD, Yule Steven PhD, Salas Eduardo PhD, Zenati Marco A. MD. (2024). Deep Learning Analysis of Surgical Video Recordings to Assess Nontechnical Skills. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.22520

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of Shannon entropy of team displacement, computed over nonoverlapping 60-second windows of the separation-from-bypass phase, against NOTSS scores in 30 cases. The printed "r = −0.52 (P = .004)" was significant at the study's threshold.

> "In contrast, team displacement entropy was inversely related to NOTSS scores, with a correlation coefficient of r = −0.52 (P = .004)."
