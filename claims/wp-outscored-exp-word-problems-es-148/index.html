Evidence¶

Fuchs 2015¶

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., Hamlett, C. L., Jordan, N. C., Siegler, R., Gersten, R., & Changas, P. (2015). Supported Self-Explaining during Fraction Intervention. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED557580

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Analysis of covariance on the word-problem posttest (controlling pretest and the pretest-by-condition interaction) in the RCT. The article reports "WP outscored EXP (ES = 1.48)"; EXP did not differ significantly from CON.

"WP outscored EXP (ES = 1.48) and CON (ES = 1.20), with no significant difference between EXP and CON (ES = 0.12)."

Fuchs 2015 (2)¶

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., Hamlett, C. L., Jordan, N. C., Siegler, R., Gersten, R., & Changas, P. (2015). Supported Self-Explaining during Fraction Intervention. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED557580

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Normative comparison using not-at-risk peer data from the same district; gaps expressed as raw-score differences divided by the not-at-risk SD. The article reports the pretest gap was "~0.90 for each condition" and closed only for WP.

"The pretest gap on fraction word problems was ~0.90 for each condition. At posttest, the gap closed for WP, but increased somewhat for EXP and CON."
