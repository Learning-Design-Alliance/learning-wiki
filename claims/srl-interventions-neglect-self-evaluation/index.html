Evidence¶

van Houten‐Schat Maaike A 2018¶

van Houten‐Schat Maaike A, Berkhout Joris J, van Dijk Nynke, Endedijk Maaike D, Jaarsma A Debbie, Diemers Agnes D. (2018). Self‐regulated learning in the clinical context: a systematic review. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13615

q3 · i? · review · r3

Synthesis of nine studies addressing interventions to support SRL in medical students and residents. The review identified three intervention types and found "none on supporting self‐evaluation", a gap the authors call regulatory appraisal.

"Three types of intervention were identified (coaching, learning plans and supportive tools). However, all interventions focused on goal setting and monitoring and none on supporting self‐evaluation."
