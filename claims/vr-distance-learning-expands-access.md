### Jordan Talan 2025

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 of the 16-interview thematic analysis reports distance learning as a significant driver of use behavior; participants wanted education “deliverable over long distances to people in other parts of the world.”

### Jordan Talan 2025 (2)

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 reports that VR expanded access during the COVID-19 pandemic in response to social distancing needs and significantly accelerated adoption; the authors conclude VR afforded equitable access across diverse settings.

> "Finally, VR simulation also expanded access to education during the COVID-19 pandemic, responding to the need for social distancing, and significantly accelerating VR adoption."
