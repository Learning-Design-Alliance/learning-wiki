Evidence¶

Briesch 2019¶

Briesch, A.M., Daniels, B. & Beneville, M. (2019). Unpacking the Term “Self-Management”: Understanding Intervention Applications Within the School-Based Literature. J Behav Educ 28, 54–77. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10864-018-9303-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 56 single-case studies of self-management interventions. The review reports that "strong mean effect sizes were identified across intervention configurations"; no specific effect size values are printed in the available text.

"Although strong mean effect sizes were identified across intervention configurations, there was also often a substantial degree of effect size heterogeneity."

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Same systematic review of 56 single-case studies: alongside the strong mean effects, the review notes "a substantial degree of effect size heterogeneity" across intervention configurations; no heterogeneity statistics are printed in the available text.
