## Evidence

### Simon Bates 2012

Simon Bates, Robyn Donnelly, Cait MacPhee, David Sands, Marion Birch, Niels R. Walet. (2012). Gender differences in conceptual understanding of Newtonian mechanics: a UK cross-institution comparison. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/34/2/421](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/34/2/421)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of matched pre-instruction FCI scores for male and female sub-cohorts at Edinburgh, Hull and Manchester, tested with contingency-table and distribution-free tests, found gaps 'ranging from +10% to +19%', with positive gaps denoting males outperforming females.

> "However, all three institutions show a consistent performance gender gap (G positive) on the basis of pre-instruction test results, ranging from +10% to +19%."
