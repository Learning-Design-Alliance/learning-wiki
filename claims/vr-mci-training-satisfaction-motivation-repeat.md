### Heldring Sara 2025

Heldring Sara, Jirwe Maria, Wihlborg Jonas, Lindström Veronica. (2025). Acceptability and applicability of using virtual reality for training mass casualty incidents- a mixed method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07319-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Summative content analysis of an open-ended questionnaire question answered after the VR scenario by the 95 participants. The analysis found "Most participants (90%) were either satisfied or very satisfied with the VR training of MCIs," and 65% expressed being thrilled and wanting more VR training.
