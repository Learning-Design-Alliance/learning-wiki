## Subclaims
`q2 i?` EM residents (n = 8) reported lower stress with ultrasound guidance (2.38 ± 1.11 vs. 3.50 ± 1.00; p = 0.016) and PGY trainees (n = 13) showed the same pattern (3.69 ± 0.61 vs. 4.46 ± 0.63; p = 0.014). [→ Huang C-Y 2026](#huang-c-y-2026)

## Evidence

### Huang C-Y 2026

Huang C-Y, Ho S-W, Chen P-M, Chen C-C. (2026). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture training using a flipped classroom: a single-arm pilot programme evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10070-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective single-item self-report questionnaire administered once at programme end; stress compared with paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. The article notes these are "exploratory self-report indicators" using non-validated single-item measures.

> "EM residents (n = 8) reported significantly lower stress with ultrasound guidance (2.38 ± 1.11 vs. 3.50 ± 1.00; Wilcoxon p = 0.016), with a non-significant increase in confidence (3.50 ± 1.41 vs. 3.00 ± 0.87; p = 0.547)"
