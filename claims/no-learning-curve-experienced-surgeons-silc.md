## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Deutsch G.B. 2015

Deutsch G.B., Sathyanarayana S.A., Giangola M., Akerman M., DeNoto G., Klein J.D.S., Zemon H., Rubach E. (2015). Competence Acquisition for Single-Incision Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective review of 290 SILCs by 4 surgeons; chronological procedure times plotted per surgeon and tested with the runs test. For surgeons 1, 2, and 3, times were "randomly scattered above and below the median," consistent with no learning curve.

> "As shown in Figure 1, the procedure times were randomly scattered above and below the median procedure time for surgeons 1, 2, and 3."
