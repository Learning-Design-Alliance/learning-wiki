# Students perceived the educational context progressively better across traditional lecture-based, integrated lecture-based, and integrated PBL-based curricula, with large effect sizes for integration and PBL

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Total DREEM perception scores were highest in the integrated PBL curriculum (136.3 ± 17.3), intermediate in the integrated lecture-based curriculum (123.3 ± 19.8), and lowest in the traditional lecture-based curriculum (105.4 ± 21.3), with effect sizes up to 1.63. ## Evidence

### Gustin Marie-Paule 2018

Gustin Marie-Paule, Abbiati Milena, Bonvin Raphael, Gerbase Margaret W., Baroffio Anne. (2018). Integrated problem-based learning versus lectures: a path analysis modelling of the relationships between educational context and learning approaches. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1489690

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of DREEM total scores across the three educational contexts (Table 2), with change scores and effect sizes: A to C 30.8 [27.5;34.2] (1.63), A to B 17.9 [14.7;21.1] (0.88), B to C 12.9 [10.2;15.7] (0.69). All dimension differences between contexts were statistically significant.

> "It was better in the PBL-based integrated curriculum (C: total 136.3 ± 17.3), followed by the lecture-based integrated curriculum (B: 123.3 ± 19.8) and finally by the lecture-based traditional curriculum (A: 105.4 ± 21.3)."
