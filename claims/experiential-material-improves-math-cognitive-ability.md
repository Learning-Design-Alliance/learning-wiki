## Evidence

### Mutmainah 2019

Mutmainah, Rukayah, Mintasih Indriayu. (2019). Effectiveness of experiential learning-based teaching material in Mathematics. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol. 8, No. 1, March 2019, pp. 57-63. http://iaescore.com/journals/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental matching-only pretest-posttest control group study in two Gunungpati elementary schools; N-gain prerequisite tests passed, then an independent sample t-test on N-gain gave t count 6.321 with significance "0.000", so H0 was rejected.

> "The result of independent sample t-test obtained the significance level of 0.000 (Sig. > 0.05), so it can be concluded that H0 is rejected which states that there is no different score between the experimental and control group after being given the different treatment."
