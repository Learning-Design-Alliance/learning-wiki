## Evidence

### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression of teacher expectation accuracy on maternal education in the ALSPAC imputed sample. The article reports that pupils "with less educated mothers underperformed their teacher’s expectations relative to pupils with degree educated mothers", with associations consistent at Key Stage 3 (e.g. O-level −0.16, CSE −0.28).

> "At Key Stage 2 pupils with less educated mothers underperformed their teacher’s expectations relative to pupils with degree educated mothers (O-level: − 0.13, 95% CI − 0.23, − 0.034; Vocational: − 0.29, 95% CI − 0.41, − 0.16; CSE: − 0.38, 95% CI − 0.49, − 0.26)."
