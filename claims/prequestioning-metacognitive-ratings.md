## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pan 2024

Pan, S. C., Han, J. Y. & Fung, F. M. (2024). Prequestioning Enhances Undergraduate Students' Learning in an Environmental Chemistry Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02788](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02788)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exit-survey Wilcoxon signed rank tests (Table 6) comparing prequestioning and retrieval practice ratings; the article reports "no significant differences in all cases" for learning and memory ratings, and 40% of students reported being moderately or very uncomfortable with prequestioning.

> "With respect to students’ comfort level with attempting prequestions versus postquestions, however, the ratings favored retrieval practice over prequestioning (p = .00042)."
