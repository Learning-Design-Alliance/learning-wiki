## Evidence

### Sparn Moritz B. 2024

Sparn Moritz B., Teixeira Hugo, Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2024). Virtual reality simulation training in laparoscopic surgery – does it really matter, what simulator to use? Results of a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 24, 589. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05574-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Groupwise comparison by first simulator (six participants per group) using Welch's t-test. The article reports path-length p = 0.47 for VirtaMed LaparoS, p = 0.003 for LAP Mentor III, p = 0.004 for LapSIM; time p-values 0.008, <0.001, <0.001.

> "In all groups, there was a significant reduction in time taken to complete the exit exercises compared to the entry examinations. Path lengths were significantly reduced in participants using LAP Mentor III and LapSim, but not in participants starting on the LaparoS."

### Sparn Moritz B. 2024 (2)

Sparn Moritz B., Teixeira Hugo, Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2024). Virtual reality simulation training in laparoscopic surgery – does it really matter, what simulator to use? Results of a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 24, 589. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05574-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the null path-length result for LaparoS: they attribute it to "the specific complexity of the exercises given".

> "The overall improvement of path lengths did not reach statistical significance on the LaparoS®, possibly due to the specific complexity of the exercises given."
