# DID estimates show pilot-area primary care workers gained 8.0 more knowledge points than non-pilot counterparts, with no significant pilot effect for physicians

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The knowledge improvement of primary care workers in the pilot area was significantly higher than in the non-pilot area, with an average difference-in-difference improvement of 8.0 points. [→ Wang Zi-Yue 2021](#wang-zi-yue-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-difference model on pooled pre-post survey data (primary care sample n = 721), controlling for township-level fixed effects and covariates; the printed Time × Area interaction was 7.97*** and the article reports "an average improvement of 8.0 points (P < 0.001)".

> "the improvement of knowledge level among primary care workers in the pilot area was significantly higher than that in the non-pilot area, with an average improvement of 8.0 points (P < 0.001)."

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021 (2)

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DID model results for clinical physicians (sample size 334) and public health physicians (sample size 168): printed Time × Area coefficients were −2.16 and −4.09, both non-significant, so the pilot intervention showed no detected benefit for these groups.

> "the improvement of the knowledge level of pilot area is slightly lower than that of non-pilot area for both clinical physicians and public health physicians, and the difference is not significant."
