# Fear of appearing incompetent acts as both a barrier and a facilitator to physicians' engagement in feedback-related SDL

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the emotions domain, fear of appearing incompetent or not knowledgeable hindered physicians' asking for feedback, yet some physicians reported it as motivational for engaging in SDL. [→ Dahn J 2018](#dahn-j-2018)
