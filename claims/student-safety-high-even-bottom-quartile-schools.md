# Most students, even in bottom quartile schools, report feeling mostly safe at school

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Nearly 85 percent of students in bottom quartile schools rate their experience mostly safe and none report feeling not safe, a pattern similar to top quartile schools where more than 80 percent feel mostly safe. [→ Sebring 1995](#sebring-1995)

## Evidence

### Sebring 1995

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Prairie School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-prairie-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the four-item student safety scale covering classes, hallways and bathrooms, travel between home and school, and areas around the school. Top quartile schools show a similar pattern with a little over 10 percent feeling very safe.

> "Even in the bottom quartile schools, nearly 85 percent of the students rate their experience mostly safe (scores of 6-8), and one percent claim very safe (scores of 9-10). Fifteen percent indicate their schools are somewhat safe (scores of 3-5), and no students report their schools are not safe (scores of 1-2)."
