Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12018-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-training questionnaire of the 52 participants using 5-point Likert items on comprehensibility and acceptability (Q1 mean 4.08, Q2 mean 3.88). The study reports "88% (46/52)" and "75% (39/52)".

> "88% (46/52) of the participants found the feedback easy to understand, and 75% (39/52) satisfied with the assessment outcomes."

### Ebina Koki 2025 (2)

Ebina Koki, Abe Takashige, Hotta Kiyohiko, Lingbo Yan, Kamijo Chihiro, Higuchi Madoka, Kon Masafumi, Kikuchi Hiroshi, Miyata Haruka, Matsumoto Ryuji, Osawa Takahiro, Murai Sachiyo, Senoo Taku, Komizunai Shunsuke, Tsujita Teppei, Sase Kazuya, Chen Xiaoshuai, Konno Atsushi, Shinohara Nobuo. (2025). External validation of a motion capture-based surgical skill assessment system in laparoscopic simulation training environments. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12018-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple-choice comparative utility items (Q3 most helpful, Q4 least helpful) quantified as +1/−1/0; three-tier classification and raw metrics had 95% confidence intervals extending below zero. The study reports GOALS scores "emerged as the most informative feedback".

> "Among feedback comments, estimated GOALS scores emerged as the most informative feedback, followed by principal component scores and SHAP-derived commentary."
