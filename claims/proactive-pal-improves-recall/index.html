Evidence¶

Kim 2006¶

Kim, Y., Baylor, A. L., & PALS Group. (2006). Pedagogical Agents as Learning Companions: The Role of Agent Competency and Type of Interaction. Educational Technology Research and Development, 54(3), 223-243. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-006-8805-z

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Univariate ANOVA (F(1, 68) = 9.67, p < .01) from the 2x2 experiment; recall was the count of idea units in student answers, converted to z-scores because the two PALs delivered unequal numbers of ideas. Means and d = 0.78 are printed in Results.

"Students in the proactive condition (M = 2.18, SD = 2.17) scored significantly higher than students in the responsive condition (M = 1.61, SD = 2.3). The standardized effect size for this difference was Cohen 's d = 0.78"
