Evidence¶

Michael J. Baker 2024¶

Michael J. Baker, Peter Reimann. (2024). CSCL has come of age. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2024) 19:1–10. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09419-2

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

The editorial attributes this finding to Keune's video-analysis study of youth engaging in fibre crafts (weaving) from a posthumanist perspective, on computational collaborative learning with materials seen as active agents.

"By analysing video data of youth engaging in fibre crafts, the study uncovers how human-material interactions produce algorithmic outcomes and evolve over time. The research highlights the significance of recognising materials as collaborative actors in computational production"
