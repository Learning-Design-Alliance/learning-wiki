Yuan Liu 2024¶

Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Granger Causality Analysis on ROIs from the resting-state FC results (SM_cortex integrating S1M1_R and SMA_L, thalamus, cerebellum, Frontal_Mid_L). "signiﬁcant bi- directional increase of causal effect between the SM_cortex with the THA and frontal lobe" was found; SM_cortex to Cere also increased while Cere to SM_cortex decreased (p = 0.024).

"Compared with theﬁnger group, the BCI-SRF group showed signiﬁcant bi- directional increase of causal effect between the SM_cortex with the THA and frontal lobe. (SM_cortex to THA: p = 0.01; THA to SM_cortex: p = 0.023; SM_cortex to Frontal:p = 0.02; Frontal to SM_cortex:p =0 . 0 1 5 )"

Related Claims¶

  • BCI-SRF training increased functional connectivity between the left primary sensorimotor cortex and left middle frontal gyrus, and between right-hemisphere intrahemispheric and interhemispheric sensorimotor regions, relative to finger training — related
  • BCI-SRF training produces a substantially larger improvement in novel control-sequence accuracy than inborn finger training, with the BCI-SRF group's improvement 350% of the Finger group's — related
  • Four specific sensorimotor connections showed significant TIME × GROUP interaction effects, with post-training increases only in the BCI-SRF group — related
  • Training sequence accuracy improved in both groups at Post, but only the BCI-SRF group retained a significant improvement at follow-up relative to Pre — related
