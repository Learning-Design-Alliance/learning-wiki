### Sulistyorini Dewie 2025

Sulistyorini Dewie, Huq K A T M Ehsanul, Babaita Abdulfatai Olamilekan, Aivey Sadia A, Huiying Gao, Kazawa Kana, Fukushima Yasuko, Kako Mayumi, Moriyama Michiko. (2025). Effectiveness of education programme to increase competency of health cadres in Indonesia: a cluster non-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095428

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparisons of the 35-item knowledge questionnaire (0–35) between groups; "no significant difference in the IG and CG knowledge at midline (27 vs 26; p=0.106) and endline (25.5 vs 27; p=0.371)". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Between group comparisons, there was no significant difference in the IG and CG knowledge at midline (27 vs 26; p=0.106) and endline (25.5 vs 27; p=0.371)."

### Sulistyorini Dewie 2025 (2)

Sulistyorini Dewie, Huq K A T M Ehsanul, Babaita Abdulfatai Olamilekan, Aivey Sadia A, Huiying Gao, Kazawa Kana, Fukushima Yasuko, Kako Mayumi, Moriyama Michiko. (2025). Effectiveness of education programme to increase competency of health cadres in Indonesia: a cluster non-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095428

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive trajectory of intervention-group knowledge medians across the three timepoints in Results; the discussion states this "indicates that periodical learning sessions are necessary to keep their knowledge accurate and updated".

> "in the IG, the median knowledge score improved at midline and slightly decreased at endline (baseline: 23; midline: 27; endline: 25.5)."
