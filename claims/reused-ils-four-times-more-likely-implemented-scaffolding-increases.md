The editorial reports, on the Rodríguez-Triana et al. large-scale Go-Lab study tracing about 40,000 ILSs, that "Scaffolding increased the implementation rate" and re-use was linked to about four times higher implementation likelihood. The editorial attributes both findings to the study.

> "Re-used ILSs were about four times more likely to have been implemented than those created from scratch. Scaffolding increased the implementation rate."
