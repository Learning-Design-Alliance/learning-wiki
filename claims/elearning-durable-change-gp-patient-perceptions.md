## Evidence

### Creupelandt Hanne 2019

Creupelandt Hanne, Anthierens Sibyl, Habraken Hilde, Sirdifield Coral, Siriwardena Aloysius Niroshan, Christiaens Thierry. (2019). A tailored e-learning gives long-term changes in determinants of GPs’ benzodiazepines prescribing: a pretest-posttest study with self-report assessments. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2019.1663591

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest self-report study of 244 GPs, long-term follow-up: "participants were less convinced that it took a drug prescription to satisfy a patient (Z = −2.919, p = .004)" and agreed less that motivating patients to choose a non-drug treatment was difficult.

> "participants were less convinced that it took a drug prescription to satisfy a patient (Z = −2.919, p = .004) and they agreed less that it was difficult to motivate patients to choose a non-drug treatment (Z = −4.592, p < .001)."
