### Stockinger 2026

Stockinger, K., Daumiller, M., & Dresel, M. (2026). Relations between university teachers' teaching‐related coping strategies and well‐being over time: A cross‐lagged panel analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 54–72. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null cross-lagged finding from the CLPM of 489 university teachers; the article notes avoidance strategies are typically listed among maladaptive strategies but select studies have also reported zero relations with subjective well-being. Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, neither distraction nor social diversion was related to subsequent changes in well‐being, neither positively nor negatively."
