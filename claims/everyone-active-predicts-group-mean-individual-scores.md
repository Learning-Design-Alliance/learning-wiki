## Evidence

### Webb 1993

Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group-level analysis of 15 groups' behavior profiles and mean individual test scores. Related correlations printed include r = .57 (number solving aloud unassisted, p < .02) and r = .73 (one or more nonparticipants, p < .001); group mean ability was not significantly related (r = .20, p < .24).

> "The single variable that best predicted group mean scores on the individual achievement test was whether everyone in the group participated actively during group work. This variable predicted 53% of the variance in group mean scores on the individual test."
