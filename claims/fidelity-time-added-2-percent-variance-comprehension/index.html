Evidence¶

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi 2025¶

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi, Lynn Gelzheiser. (2025). Comparison of more effective and typical teachers' instructional quality and implementation of engaged reader processes. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09725-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple regression with 85 comprehension-needs-only students, controlling QRI-5 pre-test and group heterogeneity; predictors were CLASSIC percentage and fidelity of implementation. The article reports the added variables explained "only increased the explanation of the variance by 2%" (R2 change 0.02, F change 0.82, p > .05).

"Regression results indicate that adding the variables of CLASSIC and fidelity only increased the explanation of the variance by 2%, a total of 27% with the control variables added."
