## Evidence

### Gärtner Julia 2021

Gärtner Julia, Prediger Sarah, Harendza Sigrid. (2021). Development and pilot test of ComCare – a questionnaire for quick assessment of communicative and social competences in medical students after interviews with simulated patients. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001464](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001464)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent samples t-tests comparing female (n=65) and male (n=38) final-year students found "No significant differences ... for any item"; equivalence was not tested. The authors note this contrast with prior findings of slightly better female scores and call for further study.

> "No significant differences were found between female and male participants for any item of ComCareP and ComCareD."

### Gärtner Julia 2021 (2)

Gärtner Julia, Prediger Sarah, Harendza Sigrid. (2021). Development and pilot test of ComCare – a questionnaire for quick assessment of communicative and social competences in medical students after interviews with simulated patients. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001464](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001464)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

In a cross-sectional comparison of the three final-year thirds (n=53, n=17, n=32), the item "responding to the patient's needs satisfactorily" was "significantly higher (p<.01)" in the second than the third four-month group, Cohen's d=.977. All other items showed no significant group differences.

> "The mean for this item was significantly higher (p<.01) for students in the second four months of their final year (3.23±.53) than for students in the third four months of their final year (2.75±.47) with a large effect (Cohen’s d=.977)."
