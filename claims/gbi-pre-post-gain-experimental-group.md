## Evidence

### Rezvan Adelnia 2016

Rezvan Adelnia, Hadi Salehi. (2016). Improving Iranian High School Students' Reading Comprehension Using the Tenets of Genre Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.4p.187

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on the 40 experimental participants' pre and posttest scores (Table 4: t = -5.536, df = 39, Sig. = .000). The article states this rejects the first null hypothesis and that "the difference between the performance of the experimental group in pretest and posttest is statistically significant".

> "As it is seen in Table 3, the mean difference of the participants in experimental group in pre and posttest is 15.38, with the mean score of pretest being 52.12 and that of posttest being 67.50."
