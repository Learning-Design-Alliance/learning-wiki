### Ishizuka K 2026

Impact of inpatient volume on residents' In-training examination scores and burnout in Japanese community hospitals: a nationwide cross-sectional study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel multivariable logistic regression with a positive Mini-Z 2.0 burnout screen (score ≥ 3) as the dependent variable; raw burnout prevalence was 13.5% (Very Low) vs 13.1%, 12.8%, and 12.1% in the comparison groups. No effect size printed.

> "The multivariable analysis revealed no significant differences in burnout symptoms between the Very Low-Volume group and the Low-Volume (adjusted odds ratio (aOR): 0.974; 95% CI: 0.493–1.925, p = 0.940), Moderate-Volume (aOR: 0.967; 95% CI: 0.492–1.903, p = 0.923), or High-Volume (aOR: 0.994; 95% CI: 0.485–2.034, p = 0.986) groups"
