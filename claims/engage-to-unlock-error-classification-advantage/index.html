Binomial GLMM with crossed random intercepts for participant and passage on the 779 detected-flaw trials from the N=398 experiment. Overall judgment accuracy ranged from 61.61% to 66.36% with no significant differences between conditions, and neither complete-diagnosis contrast was significant.

"Participants in Engage-to-Unlock classified detected errors more accurately than those in Standard Chatbot (90.43% versus 78.12%). The planned GLMM contrast indicated higher odds of correct classification under Engage-to-Unlock (𝑝Holm =. 003)."
