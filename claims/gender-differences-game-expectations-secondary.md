### Romero 2019

Romero, M. (2019). Computers in Secondary Schools: Educational Games. In Encyclopedia of Education and Information Technologies. Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The chapter reports, citing Karakus, Inal and Cagiltay (2008), a descriptive study of Turkish high school students, that pedagogical expectations for Digital Game-Based Learning differ by gender. The quoted contrast names female and male students as the two sides.

> "Karakus, Inal and Cagiltay (2008, p. 2522) observe that “female students expect games to have instructive elements, while males desire elements that are entertaining, competitive, and multi-player”."
