Related Claims¶

  • Controlled GenAI virtual-patient and communication studies reported short-term skill gains, but positive findings were not universal — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In an RCT reviewed by this study, an LLM-based digital patient improved ophthalmology medical history-taking skills relative to control — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Immersive VR-haptic and ITS modalities are more often associated with objective psychomotor and cognitive gains, while generative AI platforms are more often associated with affective outcomes — a broader claim this one bears on
  • In an RCT reviewed here, adding AI-generated feedback to a GenAI virtual patient improved medical students' clinical decision-making relative to simulation alone — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Multi-agent AI standardized patient training improved final OSCE-aligned examination scores compared with a structured non-LLM control condition — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Colleges adopted new technology platforms to increase efficiencies, but face challenges sustaining their use after the grant ends — related
  • OSCE use in orthodontic education improves diagnostic accuracy, procedural proficiency, and communication skills through simulated patient interactions — related
  • Technology augmentation studies, including needle tip tracking, all found significant improvement in skill outcomes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Digital clinical skills teaching produces higher student satisfaction and engagement across multiple modalities, despite mixed efficacy for skills — related
