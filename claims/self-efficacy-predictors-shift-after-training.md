## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In regressions, pretest self-efficacy was significantly associated with awareness (β=.19, P=.03) and knowledge (β=.22, P=.02), while self-efficacy change was associated with skills change (β=.19, P=.04) and attitude change (β=.36, P<.001). [→ Ali-Saleh Ola 2025](#ali-saleh-ola-2025)

## Evidence

### Ali-Saleh Ola 2025

Ali-Saleh Ola, Massalha Layalleh, Halperin Ofra. (2025). Evaluation of a Simulation Program for Providing Telenursing Training to Nursing Students: Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67804

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two multiple linear regressions (N=114) with computer knowledge entered first: pretest model R²=0.23 (F6,107=5.20, P<.001); change model R²=0.21 (F6,107=4.64, P<.001). No standardized effect size beyond β values is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Regarding the pretest score, higher awareness and more knowledge of telenursing were associated with the perception of higher self-efficacy in telenursing. Regarding the change score, greater improvement in the perceived skills required for telenursing and a higher positive change in the attitudes regarding telenursing were associated with a greater improvement in the perception of self-efficacy in telenursing."
