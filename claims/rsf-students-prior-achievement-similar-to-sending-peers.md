### Young 2008

Renaissance Schools Fund-Supported Schools: Early Outcomes, Challenges, and Opportunities. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CPS student-level files comparing RSF entrants' prior ITBS and ISAT scores with students remaining at sending schools. The report finds that "the prior average reading and mathematics performance of students at the majority of RSF-supported schools was similar" to sending-school peers, with exceptions at Schools 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, and 14.

> "with a few exceptions, the prior average reading and mathematics performance of students at the majority of RSF-supported schools was similar to that of their counterparts at their previous schools."
