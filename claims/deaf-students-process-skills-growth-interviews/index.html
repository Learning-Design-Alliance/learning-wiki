Bolat 2016¶

Bolat, M. (2016). The Development and Implementation of a Model for Teaching Astronomy to Deaf Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i7.1506

q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative analysis of interviews with students S7, S12 and S20 conducted during the experimental activities on three dates. The authors interpret this as evidence that activities designed to develop scientific process skills were effective.

"While the students were not able to make any “estimates” and “inferences” in their interviews in 21 st June, they began to make “estimates” and “inferences” in their interviews in 23 rd September and especially 21 st December."
