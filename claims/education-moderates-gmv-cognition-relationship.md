## Evidence

### Corley E 2023

Corley E, Fahey L, Fitzgerald J, Holleran L, Walton E, Morris DW, Donohoe G. (2023). The impact of early adversity and education on genetic and brain morphological predictors of cognitive ability. Genes, Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12850

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM (Model 3) comparing participants with (n implied by 3530 of 5273 coded) and without a college/university degree; measurement invariance held across groups. The ELA×GMV→IQ interaction was significant only in the college/university group (β = −0.015, p < 0.001).

> "A significant moderating effect of ELA on the association between GMV and cognitive ability was observed in the group who had been to college/university (indirect effect β = 0.015, 95% CIs = −0.022; −0.008, SE = 0.004, p < 0.001). However, there was no evidence of a moderated mediation effect of ELA on the association between total GMV, IQ‐PGS and cognitive ability in either group"
