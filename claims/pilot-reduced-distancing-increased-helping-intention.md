## Evidence

### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative pilot study using think-aloud sessions and interviews among Japanese adults recruited by snowball sampling; interview transcripts were analyzed using content analysis. The program "was found to reduce participants' distancing attitudes and increase their intention to support hypothetical friends".

> "Furthermore, the interview data showed that the intervention program was found to reduce participants’ distancing attitudes and increase their intention to support hypothetical friends [50]."

### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026 (2)

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud feasibility, usability, and acceptability testing of the deployed web program. Visualization, content, quantity, and written feedback "were well received among the program participants"; some older participants preferred printed or PDF materials.

> "The program’s visualization, content, quantity, and written feedback on worksheets were well received among the program participants. However, some older participants expressed a preference for printed or PDF materials to review in advance [50]."
