Evidence¶

Cepeda 2006¶

Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006). Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A Review and Quantitative Synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354-380. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic comparison collapsing massed-versus-spaced differences over seven retention interval ranges (Table 1). At retention intervals under 1 min, spaced exceeded massed 50.1% vs. 41.2% correct, t(208) = 3.7, p < .001. The article notes purported massing benefits may occur only at retention intervals of 2-8 s.

"there is no hint that massed presentation was preferable to spaced, whether retention interval was very short (less than 1 min) or very long (over 30 days)"
