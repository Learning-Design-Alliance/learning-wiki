## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aleven 2016

Aleven, V., Roll, I., McLaren, B. M., & Koedinger, K. R. (2016). Help Helps, But Only So Much: Research on Help Seeking with Intelligent Tutoring Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0089-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Classroom studies integrating the Help Tutor with the Geometry Cognitive Tutor measured help-seeking behavior during and after the intervention via tutor log data and post-test measures, finding students "spent more time per hint level and when they asked for help, requested fewer hint levels".

> "We found that this feedback led to a lasting improvement in help-seeking behavior, even months after the Help Tutor was turned off. Specifically, students who had received feedback on help seeking used the hints more deliberately: They spent more time per hint level and when they asked for help, requested fewer hint levels."

### Aleven 2016 (2)

Aleven, V., Roll, I., McLaren, B. M., & Koedinger, K. R. (2016). Help Helps, But Only So Much: Research on Help Seeking with Intelligent Tutoring Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0089-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

In the same classroom studies, domain-level learning was assessed with a pre/post-test of geometry skill while the intervention was in effect; the article reports no statistically significant improvement, and equivalence was not tested.

> "However, we did not find improved domain-level learning due to feedback on help seeking."
