### Tannir 2013

Tannir, A., & Al-Hroub, A. (2013). Effects of character education on the self-esteem of intellectually able and less able elementary students in Kuwait. International Journal of Special Education, 28(1), 47-59. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1023237

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison in the traditional instruction condition. Table 3 reports the less able group's mean difference as -2.10, though the text states "a slight regression of 0. 53 points," an internal inconsistency in the article.

> "The intellectually able group who received the traditional instructional program showed a progress of 1.22 points (22.33-21.11) between pre-treatment and post-treatment surveys. On the other hand, the intellectually less able group showed a slight regression of 0. 53 points (19.8-21.90) after they received the traditional instructional program."
