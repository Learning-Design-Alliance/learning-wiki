# WhatsApp's most cited benefit is enabling learning anytime and anywhere, but intrusiveness, message volume and cognitive load impede learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Twelve articles reported technical/logistical data; the most cited benefit was new learning opportunities when face-to-face meeting is not possible, described as anytime, anywhere learning. [→ Coleman E. 2019](#coleman-e-2019)
`q2 i?` The same studies reported intrusiveness, message flooding, and high volume of material impeding learning, which the review relates to cognitive load theory. [→ Coleman E. 2019 (2)](#coleman-e-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Coleman E. 2019

Coleman E., O'Connor E. (2019). The role of WhatsApp® in medical education; a scoping review and instructional design model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1706-8

`q2 · i?`

Review's synthesis of technical/logistical survey data from twelve included articles. The most cited benefit was creating learning opportunities when "meeting face-to-face is not possible", termed anytime, anywhere learning.

> "The most cited benefit of WhatsApp® was its ability to create new learning opportunities, when geographical or time constraints meant that "meeting face-to-face is not possible" (p569) [25], described as "anytime, anywhere learning" [34, 38]."

### Coleman E. 2019 (2)

Coleman E., O'Connor E. (2019). The role of WhatsApp® in medical education; a scoping review and instructional design model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1706-8

`q2 · i?`

Review's theoretical interpretation of reported drawbacks: intrusiveness, "message flooding" and large volumes of learning material impeding learning, framed through cognitive load theory with the familiar platform minimising extraneous load.

> "Cognitive load theory [49] was relevant to studies where the high volume of learning material was thought to impede learning [23–25]. The user-friendly, familiar platform minimised extraneous cognitive load, prioritising the germane load of the online learning activities."
