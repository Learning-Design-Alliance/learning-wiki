Rodriguez et al. (2012) compared two computer-based Lexia intervention groups over eight weeks; participants in both groups experienced significant growth in English word reading regardless of instruction language, and the Spanish-support group "achieved significantly higher scores in English reading comprehension". No effect size is printed.
