### Jessica R. Hoehn and H. J. Lewandowski 2020

Jessica R. Hoehn and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Framework of goals for writing in physics lab classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Framework section (type d, definitional statement about the resulting framework). The authors state that 'the categories are not mutually exclusive' and give examples of goals such as Diﬀerent modes of writing and Argumentation that fall under both the Communication and WAP categories.

> "the categories are not mutually exclusive—they represent distinct, but interrelated, ideas and thus there are several instances of overlap between them."

### Jessica R. Hoehn and H. J. Lewandowski 2020 (2)

Jessica R. Hoehn and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Framework of goals for writing in physics lab classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

> "Further, the goals in the framework are of diﬀerent grain sizes."
