Evidence¶

Manuela Glaser 2025¶

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Preregistered Experiment 1 (n = 48), one-tailed Mann–Whitney U-test on a six-item multiple-choice text-picture integration test matching portrait details to statements about inventions. No significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

"Regarding text-picture integration, the results showed no significant differences between spatial audio settings (M = .74, SD = .22) and normal audio settings (M = .63, SD = .31), U = 237.50, p = .145, r = .15."
