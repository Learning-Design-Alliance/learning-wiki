## Evidence

### Bansal Sandeep 2025

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis linking each student's SMMS-R score at four time points to the average examination score of the corresponding NBME set in the same 51-student cohort; the article reports "No correlation was found between strength of motivation and examination scores for each measurement point," with R² values from 0.000 to 0.008.

> "No correlation was found between strength of motivation and examination scores for each measurement point (SMMS1 v. ExamSet1 (R2 = 0.000), SMMS2 v. ExamSet2 (R2 = 0.006), SMMS3 v. ExamSet3 (R2 = 0.008), SMMS4 v. ExamSet4 (R2 = 0.001)) (Fig. 1)."
