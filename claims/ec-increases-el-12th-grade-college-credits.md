## Evidence

### Johnson 2020

Johnson, Angela, and Diana Mercado-Garcia. (2020). The Effects of Early College Opportunities on English Learners. (EdWorkingPaper: 20-232). Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai20-232

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences analysis of administrative records for 15,090 ever-EL students across 17 high schools, comparing pre/post cohorts in three treated versus untreated schools. The OLS DiD coefficient was "10.518***" (SE 0.733); synthetic control estimates were very close in magnitude and significance.

> "EC participation led to an increase of 10.5 college credits earned in the 12th grade, equivalent to two semester-long courses (column 1)."
