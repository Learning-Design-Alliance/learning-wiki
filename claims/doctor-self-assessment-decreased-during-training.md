Kasper J, Liethmann K, Heesen C, Reissmann DR, Geiger F. (2017). Training doctors briefly and in situ to involve their patients in making medical decisions—Preliminary testing of a newly developed module. Health Expectations. https://doi.org/10.1111/hex.12565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Feasibility questionnaire with 10 doctors, including self-assessment of performance per consultation on a 0–6 scale; Table 1 prints declining means (2.8 to 2.1) that are descriptive and untested. This is the doctors' subjective report, not a tested change.

> "Contrasting the observer ratings and the results of the doctors’ own detailed assessment in the questionnaire, self‐assessment indicated a decrease in communication quality from the first to the last consultation."
