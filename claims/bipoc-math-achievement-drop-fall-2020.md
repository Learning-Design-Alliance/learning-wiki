## Evidence

### Megan Kuhfeld 2021

Megan Kuhfeld, Erik Ruzek, Karyn Lewis, Jim Soland, Angela Johnson, Beth Tarasawa, & Lindsay Dworkin. (2021). Understanding the initial educational impacts of COVID-19 on communities of color. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/understanding-the-initial-educational-impacts-of-covid-19-on-communities-of-color/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of MAP Growth scores from 2.1 million BIPOC students in grades 3-8 comparing fall 2020 percentile ranks to fall 2019 against pre-pandemic national norms. The report states "the largest drops (ranging from 3 to 13 percentile points) were observed in the earliest grade levels we examined"; AIAN, Latinx, and Native Hawaiian groups were most affected.

> "Overall, math achievement, when benchmarked against a national sample from before the pandemic, was lower in fall 2020 than in fall 2019 for nearly all grade levels examined in this report. However, the largest drops (ranging from 3 to 13 percentile points) were observed in the earliest grade levels we examined"
