Thematic analysis of think-aloud interviews with three neurodivergent physics students (Moonbeam, Ray, Milo), using an a priori code called a “DEP event.” The article reports “multiple instances of communication breakdown” across all three interviews, both during problem-solving and in semi-structured interview anecdotes.

> "We find that the 3 neurodivergent students, experience communication breakdowns in their interactions with peers and instructors, and in interpreting problem statements."
