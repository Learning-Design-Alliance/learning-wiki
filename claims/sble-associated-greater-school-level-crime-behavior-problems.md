`q3 i?` In the review's meta-analysis, school-level crime and behavior outcomes showed a small positive pooled effect (g = 0.09, p = 0.023), meaning schools with SBLE had more crime and behavior problems. [→ Fisher 2023](#fisher-2023)

## Evidence

### Fisher 2023

Fisher, B. W., Petrosino, A., Persson, H., Guckenburg, S., Fronius, T., Benitez, I., & Earl, K. (2023). School‐based law enforcement strategies to reduce crime, increase perceptions of safety, and improve learning outcomes in primary and secondary schools: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 19, e1360. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1360

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Robust variance estimation meta-analysis of 520 school-level effect sizes from 27 reports (Table 2). The weighted mean effect size was "School‐level 0.09" with p = 0.023 and 95% CI [0.01, 0.16], indicating more crime and behavior problems where SBLE was present.
