## Evidence

### Cobos Alvarado 2016

Cobos Alvarado, F., Peñaherrera León, M., & Ortiz Colon, A. M. (2016). DESIGN AND VALIDATION OF A QUESTIONNAIRE TO MEASURE RESEARCH SKILLS: EXPERIENCE WITH ENGINEERING STUDENTS. Journal of Technology and Science Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.3926/jotse.227

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content and semantic validation phase in which 8 experts from two Spanish universities and one Ecuadorian university judged the scale's semantics, content, structure, item comprehension and format. The article reports the resulting item reduction; no effect size is printed.

> "In this process valuation experts recommended the removal 20 items falling in 36 of the 56 items initially plated on the scale."

### Cobos Alvarado 2016 (2)

Cobos Alvarado, F., Peñaherrera León, M., & Ortiz Colon, A. M. (2016). DESIGN AND VALIDATION OF A QUESTIONNAIRE TO MEASURE RESEARCH SKILLS: EXPERIENCE WITH ENGINEERING STUDENTS. Journal of Technology and Science Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.3926/jotse.227

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Homogeneity analysis during factorial validation, applying the Elbel (1965) recommendation to drop items correlating below .200 with the total scale. This produced the final 20-item scale with alpha of .91; no effect size is printed.

> "The homogeneity of the questionnaire was calculated, allowing us to remove 16 items that had a low level of discrimination and therefore a correlation <200 with the total scale, according to the recommendations of Elbel (1965). The scale was composed of 20 items with Cronbach's alpha reliability of .91."
