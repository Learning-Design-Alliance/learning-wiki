## Evidence

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

General linear mixed model on POMS fatigue subscale changes in the RCT (OLP N = 79, control N = 75). The article reports "− 2.40 ± 0.83, t(153) = − 2.90, p = 0.004, d = − 0.47", a medium effect.

> "Further subscale analyses for the POMS revealed a significantly smaller increase in fatigue (group × time, OLP compared to control, − 2.40 ± 0.83, t(153) = − 2.90, p = 0.004, d = − 0.47)"

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021 (2)

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

General linear mixed model on POMS confusion-bewilderment subscale changes in the same RCT. The article reports "− 2.12 ± 0.55, t(153) = − 3.83, p < 0.001, d = − 0.62", a medium-to-large effect.

> "and in confusion scores (group × time, OLP compared to control, − 2.12 ± 0.55, t(153) = − 3.83, p < 0.001, d = − 0.62; see Fig. 4) in the OLP than the control group"
