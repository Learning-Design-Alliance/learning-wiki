The article reports, citing Prather et al., an eye-tracking and think-aloud study of novice programmers observing a "widening gap" in outcomes. The article uses this to argue that a single label such as 'AI use' obscures qualitatively different modes of engagement; the study's details are second-hand here.

"In an eye-tracking and think-aloud study of novice programmers, Prather et al. [3] observe a "widening gap" in outcomes: students with stronger metacognitive skills use GenAI to accelerate work they could already conceive, while struggling students experience new metacognitive difficulties, including interruption, being misled by suggestions, and an illusion of competence."
