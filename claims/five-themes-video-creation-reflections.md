### Ramón-Arbués E 2025

Ramón-Arbués E, Blázquez-Ornat I, Sagarra-Romero L, Benito-Ruiz E, Antón-Solanas I, Gómez-Torres P. (2025). Students´ perceptions of creating educational videos as a teaching and learning strategy. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001812

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of reflective narratives from participants in the video-creation activity, coded line by line in ATLAS.ti by two authors with a third resolving differences; the analysis "were classified into 5 themes or codes" as listed. Student quotes illustrate each theme.

> "The analysis and interpretation of the students’ narrative reflections were classified into 5 themes or codes, namely (1) student learning, (2) assessment of the utility of videos as an educational tool, (3) information and digital skills, (4) teamwork and problem-solving skills, and (5) challenges."
