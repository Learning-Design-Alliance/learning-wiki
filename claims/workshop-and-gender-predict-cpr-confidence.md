## Evidence

### Khattak Osama 2026

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression predicting confidence in performing CPR (very confident vs not confident) among 400 dentists; the model accounted for 23.9% of the variance (Nagelkerke R2) with 70.0% classification accuracy. The printed effect size is an adjusted odds ratio, aOR = 6.93 (95% CI [4.19–11.46], p < 0.001), a large association.

> "Attendance at a BLS/CPR workshop within the last 5 years was the strongest predictor, with dentists nearly seven times more likely to report being very confident compared to those whose training was more than 5 years ago (aOR = 6.93; 95% CI [4.19–11.46]; p < 0.001)."

### Khattak Osama 2026 (2)

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same regression model predicting CPR confidence; gender was entered with female as reference. The printed effect size is an adjusted odds ratio, aOR = 0.59 (95% CI [0.37–0.93], p = 0.023), a small-to-moderate negative association. General dentists showed a borderline effect versus specialists (aOR = 2.18, p = 0.054).

> "Male dentists were less likely than females to report confidence (aOR = 0.59; 95% CI [0.37–0.93]; p = 0.023)."
