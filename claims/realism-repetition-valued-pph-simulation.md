## Evidence

### Egenberg Signe 2017

Egenberg Signe, Karlsen Bjørg, Massay Deodatus, Kimaro Happiness, Bru Lars Edvin. (2017). “No patient should die of PPH just for the lack of training!” Experiences from multi-professional simulation training on postpartum hemorrhage in northern Tanzania: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0957-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group content analysis at two Tanzanian hospitals; informants across cadres described scenario realism, and nurse midwives discussed how the training "allowed them to rehearse, repeat, and reinforce their knowledge, and thereby instantly improving their performance."

> "Most of the informants described the training scenarios as realistic, with the operator of the birthing simulator performing as a real patient and mimicking the severity of PPH."
