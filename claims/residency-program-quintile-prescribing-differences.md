## Evidence

### Burk-Rafel J 2026

Burk-Rafel J, Park H, Cornwell M, Holmboe ES, Yamazaki K, Santen SA, Park YS, Andrews JS, Hogan SO, Richardson J. (2026). Residency Competency Ratings and Early-Career Physician Antibiotic Prescribing. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.34365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unadjusted program-level analysis of mean yearly antibiotic prescription rates across graduates of 1090 residency programs, with program quintiles calculated within each specialty. The internal medicine contrast was 28.5 vs 43.4 per 100 beneficiaries, a difference of 14.9 (P < .001).

> "Among graduates of family medicine programs, the corresponding prescribing rates were 21.6 (95% CI, 21.1-22.1) antibiotic prescriptions per 100 beneficiaries in quintile 1 and 40.4 (95% CI, 39.6-41.2) antibiotic prescriptions per 100 beneficiaries in quintile 5"
