## Evidence

### Bittencourt Ig Ibert 2020

Bittencourt Ig Ibert, Cukurova Mutlu, Muldner Kasia, Luckin Rose, Millán Eva, Mogessie Michael, Richey Elizabeth J., McLaren Bruce M., Andres-Bray Juan Miguel L., Baker Ryan S. (2020). Confrustion and Gaming While Learning with Erroneous Examples in a Decimals Game. Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_38

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on 191 students' pretest and posttest scores across the four conditions; the article reports that "students across all conditions improved significantly from pretest to posttest". Means rose, e.g., PS from 23.37 to 28.72.

> "a repeated-measure analysis of variance (ANOVA) indicated that students across all conditions improved significantly from pretest to posttest, F(3, 187) = 167.04, p < .001."
