### McMahon 2024

McMahon, L. H., Pautz Stephenson, S., & Corrigan, S. (2024). The future of math inclusion: The promise of digital math tools for universally accessible mathematics instruction [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/211

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative cross-case analysis of nine grade 4-12 teacher partners' written commitments and focus-group data. The article reports that "All nine of their commitments included a desire to use the Mathspace feature" and that teachers' hypotheses expected Mathspace to increase engagement, support multiple representations, and embed assessment and feedback.

> "After the first focus group, teachers spent 4 weeks trying something new with Equatio in their practice. All nine of their commitments included a desire to use the Mathspace feature."
