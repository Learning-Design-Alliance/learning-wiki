### Sacks 2025

Sacks, D. (2025). Deliberate Practice Supervision to Enhance the Effectiveness of Behavioral Activation for Depression: A Case Study. Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1002/jclp.23786

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Case-vignette evidence from Supervision Session 2: four successive role-play attempts against the same recorded client statement, with supervisor feedback between attempts and self-rated difficulty of 8, then 7, then 6-7. The final attempt, quoted here, used the client's friends' names and specific painful thoughts as the supervisor suggested.

> "Robert [attempt #4]: That must be so deflating, you made an effort to meet Alex and Mickey, but all you could think of was how far behind them you are. With all those thoughts it's hard to just enjoy the moment you are in."
