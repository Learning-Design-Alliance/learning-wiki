# Gender is related to sixth graders' thinking styles in the function, form and scope dimensions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Chi-square analysis found gender significantly related to thinking styles in the function, form and scope dimensions at the .05 level. [→ Chaiyapornpattana 2009](#chaiyapornpattana-2009)

## Evidence

### Chaiyapornpattana 2009

Chaiyapornpattana, N., & Wongwanich, S. (2009). Development of a multidimensional thinking styles scale based on theory of mental self-government for sixth grade students. Research in Higher Education Journal. http://www.aabri.com

`q3 · i?`

Chi-square tests related background factors to styles in each dimension for the 1,545-student sample, with significance at the .05 level. The results section states "Gender was related to styles of thinking in the dimension of function, form, scope"; the discussion adds male students were most legislative and external while female students were most judicial and internal.

> "Gender was related to styles of thinking in the dimension of function, form, scope and grade was related to styles of thinking in the dimension of function, form, level and leanings."
