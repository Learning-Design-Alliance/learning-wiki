## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the nine-point Mini-CEX, the PBL-ChatGPT group outperformed the traditional teaching group on medical interviewing skills (6.29 ± 1.10 vs 5.33 ± 1.39, p = 0.02). [→ Zeng H 2025](#zeng-h-2025)

## Evidence

### Zeng H 2025

Zeng H, Zhu Z, Hu J, Cui Y. (2025). Application of ChatGPT-assisted problem-based learning teaching method in clinical medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06321-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mini-CEX assessment (nine-point scale, single evaluator) of all 21 interns per group after the two-week rotation. Medical interviewing skills means were "6.29 ± 1.10" versus "5.33 ± 1.39", p = 0.02. No effect size was printed.

> "The PBL-ChatGPT group showed statistically significant improvements in medical interviewing skills, clinical judgment and overall clinical competence compared to the Traditional Teaching group."
