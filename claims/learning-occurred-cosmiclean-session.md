### Hu 2024

Hu, Y., Wouters, P., van der Schaaf, M., & Kester, L. (2024). The effects of achievement goal instructions in game-based learning on students' achievement goals, performance, and achievement emotions. Learning and Instruction, 93, 101943. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101943

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test across all 450 participants comparing pretest and posttest knowledge scores in the CosmiClean chemistry game session showed a significant gain, "t = 8.19, df = 449, p < 0.001, d = 0.42".

> "A paired t-test shows that there was a statistically significant difference between pretest performance and posttest performance (t = 8.19, df = 449, p < 0.001, d = 0.42), so, learning happened."
