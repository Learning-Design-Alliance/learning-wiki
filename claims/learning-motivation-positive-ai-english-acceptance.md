# Learning motivation is positively associated with acceptance outcomes for AI-assisted English learning tools in adjusted regression models

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In full-sample adjusted OLS models of 210 undergraduates, learning motivation showed statistically significant positive associations with perceived usefulness, ease of use, behavioral intention, and satisfaction (all p < 0.001). [→ Wu J 2026](#wu-j-2026)

## Evidence

### Wu J 2026

Wu J, Wang Y, He Y, Yin X, Chen F and Wan B (2026) Acceptance of AI-assisted English language learning tools in higher education: psychological correlates across disciplinary and proficiency groups. Front. Psychol. 17:1806457. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1806457

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Full-sample adjusted OLS regression (Table 4) predicting satisfaction from learning motivation, adjusted for gender, discipline, CET-4 score, and use frequency, with robust standard errors. The model reports a standardized coefficient of β = 0.810, adjusted R2 of 0.671, and N = 210; all four motivation models were significant at p < 0.001.
