## Evidence

### Haycock 1998

Haycock, Kati. (1998). Good Teaching Matters: How Well-Qualified Teachers Can Close the Gap. Thinking K-16, v3 n2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED457260

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Goldhaber and Brewer's analysis using National Educational Longitudinal Study of 1988 data, finding a significant positive relationship between teachers' degrees and student achievement in technical subjects. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "in mathe- matics and science, it is the teacher subject-specific knowledge that is the important factor in determining tenth-grade achievement."

### Haycock 1998 (2)

Haycock, Kati. (1998). Good Teaching Matters: How Well-Qualified Teachers Can Close the Gap. Thinking K-16, v3 n2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED457260

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

A Hawaii study asking social studies teachers to rate their own understanding of historical periods and teaching methods, then comparing teacher expertise to student achievement; students performed best where teachers reported the most expertise. No effect size is printed.

> "students per- formed best in the domains where teachers indicated the most expertise."
