## Evidence

### Giannotti Domenico 2013

Giannotti Domenico, Patrizi Gregorio, Di Rocco Giorgio, Vestri Anna Rita, Semproni Camilla Proietti, Fiengo Leslie, Pontone Stefano, Palazzini Giorgio, Redler Adriano. (2013). Play to Become a Surgeon: Impact of Nintendo WII Training on Laparoscopic Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0057372

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Task 4 (complete virtual cholecystectomy) in the randomized simulator study. Table 2 reports session-2 perforations p=0.001 and safe cautery p=0.014 between groups; the Discussion states "the training reduced the complication rate and the unsafe cautery rate".

> "In full procedures, the training reduced the complication rate and the unsafe cautery rate, which are probably the most frequent avoidable incidents for novice laparoscopists."
