## Evidence

### Dybowski Christoph 2017

Dybowski Christoph, Sehner Susanne, Harendza Sigrid. (2017). Influence of motivation, self-efficacy and situational factors on the teaching quality of clinical educators. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0923-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression in the same observational study (94 lessons, 55 teachers, 500 ratings). Teachers' perceptions of students' competencies was the only situational variable significantly predicting teaching quality, "significantly associated with students' ratings of teaching quality (p = .004)"; categories above 'hardly applies' each showed significantly higher ratings.
