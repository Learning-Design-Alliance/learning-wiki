Design, implementation, and evaluation of an integrated PBL bridge course in basic medical sciences: a seven-year project evaluation with longitudinal follow-up. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive project evaluation of seven consecutive cohorts (N = 1,072) completing the integrated PBL bridge course. The article reports that "Total scores ranged from 90.06 (SD = 4.89, Class of 2023) to 91.63 (SD = 4.50, Class of 2020)" and that 81.06% (869/1072) achieved an 'Excellent' grade, with no student failing the course in any cohort.
