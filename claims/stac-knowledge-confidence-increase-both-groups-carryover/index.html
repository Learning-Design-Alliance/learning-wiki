Evidence¶

Midgett 2016¶

Midgett, A., Doumas, D., Trull, R., & Johnston, A. (2016). A Randomized Controlled Study Evaluating a Brief, Bystander Bullying Intervention with Junior High School Students. Boise State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146195

q3 · i? · causal · r1

GLM repeated measures ANOVA in a randomized controlled study of 57 junior high students (n = 52 analyzed per Table 1) comparing STAC training to wait-list control from baseline to 30-day follow-up. The main effect for Time was significant ("Wilks' Lambda = .76, F(1, 51) = 16.34, p < .001, η 2 p = .24"), but the interaction was not.

"The interaction effect for Time x Group, however, was not significant, Wilks' Lambda = .98, F(1, 51) = 1.19, p = .28, η 2 p = .02. Contrary to our hypothesis, students in both the intervention group and the control group reported an increase in knowledge of bullying, knowledge of the STAC strategies, and confidence to intervene in bullying situations."
