Evidence¶

Daluba 2013¶

Daluba, Noah Ekeyi. (2013). Effect of Demonstration Method of Teaching on Students' Achievement in Agricultural Science. World Journal of Education, 3(6). doi:10.5430/wje.v3n6p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test post-test control group study of 480 SSII students in six Nigerian secondary schools, analyzed with ANCOVA. The calculated F-ratio of 273.73 exceeded the table value, so the null hypothesis of no difference was rejected.

"Since the calculated F-ratio was greater than table F-value at df1, 476 at 0.05 level of significance, the stated null hypothesis is therefore rejected meaning there was significant difference between the mean achievement scores of students taught with demonstration method and conventional lecture method."
