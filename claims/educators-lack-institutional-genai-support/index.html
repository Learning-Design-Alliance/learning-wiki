q2 · i? · associational · r?

The review reports, citing Lee et al. (2024), a mixed-methods survey and interview study of 30 university educators. The review attributes the findings on uncertainty, policy calls, and that "Less than a quarter felt equipped for integration"; the study is described only as reported in the review.

"Higher education educators expressed uncertainty about GenAI and called for clear institutional policies. Less than a quarter felt equipped for integration."
