## Evidence

### Kenyon Kristy L. 2016

Kenyon Kristy L., Onorato Morgan E., Gottesman Alan J., Hoque Jamila, Hoskins Sally G. (2016). Testing CREATE at Community Colleges: An Examination of Faculty Perspectives and Diverse Student Gains. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0146

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre/post CTT scored by blind rubric in the pooled 2-yr CREATE courses. The article reports these illogical-justification ESs directly and suggests students "used fewer inappropriate support statements on their postcourse surveys."

> "Pooled groups made fewer illogical claims on posttests, with stronger effects in the Bio pool (ES = 0.24) than the overall (all respondents, ES = 0.12; Table 6). Subsets of biology majors and nonmajors both made fewer illogical claims postcourse. ESs were comparable in the two groups (0.25 and 0.24), but the effect was significant at p < 0.05 only for majors."
