## Evidence

### Hutton John S 2018

Hutton John S, Huang Guixia, Phelan Kieran J, DeWitt Thomas, Ittenbach Richard F. (2018). Shared reading quality assessment by parental report: preliminary validation of the DialogPR. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1298-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rasch rating-scale item analysis of all eight DialogPR items in the sample of 49 dyads, reported in the item-analysis section with Table 2. The article prints difficulty estimates from −2.00 to 1.06 and point-measure correlations from 0.44 to 0.73; no effect size is printed.

> "Rasch estimates of item difficulty ranged from − 2.00 (less difficult) to 1.06 (more difficult). Point-measure correlations ranged from 0.44 (item 2) to 0.73 (item 4), suggesting a moderate relationship between each of the DialogPR items and the entire scale."

### Hutton John S 2018 (2)

Hutton John S, Huang Guixia, Phelan Kieran J, DeWitt Thomas, Ittenbach Richard F. (2018). Shared reading quality assessment by parental report: preliminary validation of the DialogPR. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1298-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive item-endorsement pattern reported in the Discussion for the same 49-dyad sample. The article names the most-endorsed items (2, 8, 6, 5) and, in the following sentence, the least-endorsed (item 4 recall, item 1 discussion before reading, item 3 completion).

> "Initial evidence suggests that the most frequently endorsed items for these parents, reflecting the most common or least "difficult" shared reading behaviors, were item 2 (frequency of pauses during reading to respond to a child's questions or comments, discussed above), item 8 (discussion after reading), item 6 ("wh-" questions), and item 5 (open-ended questions)."
