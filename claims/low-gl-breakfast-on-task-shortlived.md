## Evidence

### Adolphus 2013

Adolphus, K., Lawton, C. L., and Dye, L. (2013). The effects of breakfast on behavior and academic performance in children and adolescents. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00425

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review describes a randomized acute experimental study (attributed to Benton et al., 2007) in 19 young children (mean age 6 years 10 months) from a low SES school comparing three isocaloric breakfasts of differing GL: "Children spent signiﬁcantly more time on-task following a low GL breakfast meal compared with medium and high GL breakfast meals."

> "Children spent signiﬁcantly more time on-task following a low GL breakfast meal compared with medium and high GL breakfast meals. This effect was speciﬁc to the ﬁrst 10 min of the observation."
