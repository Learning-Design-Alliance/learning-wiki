Steven Wise 2017¶

Steven Wise, Lingyun Gao. (2017). A general approach to measuring test-taking effort on computer-based tests. Applied Measurement in Education, 30(4), 343–354. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/a-general-approach-to-measuring-test-taking-effort-on-computer-based-tests/

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article's abstract reports, citing prior work, that Response Time Effort (RTE) "has been shown to provide an assessment of effort down to the level of individual item responses." The article describes this as established rather than newly demonstrated in the current study.

"In particular, Response Time Effort (RTE) has been shown to provide an assessment of effort down to the level of individual item responses."
