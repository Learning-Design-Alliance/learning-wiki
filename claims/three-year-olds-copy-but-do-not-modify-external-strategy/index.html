Evidence¶

Armitage 2023¶

Armitage, KL; Suddendorf, T; Bulley, A; Bastos, APM; Taylor, AH; Redshaw, J. (2023). Creativity and Flexibility in Young Children's Use of External Cognitive Strategies. Developmental Psychology, 59 (6), pp. 995-1005. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001562

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Preregistered experimental study of 3–8-year-olds (N = 72) in Australia; children watched an experimenter demonstrate marking a hidden prize's location, then had six test trials. The article reports that "most 3-year-olds deployed the demonstrated strategy" yet none modified it for transfer.

"Although most 3-year-olds deployed the demonstrated strategy in the initial test phase, none of them modified that strategy to solve the transfer task."
