### Poteat 2025

Poteat, V. P., Calzo, J. P., Yoshikawa, H., Kellogg, D., Marx, R. A., Richburg, A., & Lipkin, A. (2025). Youth experiences in gender–sexuality alliances predict academic engagement but not disaffection through social–emotional wellbeing. Child Development, 96, 847–864. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14209

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

This null moderation finding comes from the study's exploratory moderation analyses among 627 youth in 51 GSAs, testing whether GSA experience–wellbeing associations differed by demographic group. The article reports that "none of the interactions between youth's GSA experiences and their sexual orientation identity, gender identity, or race or ethnicity were statistically significant in predicting their wellbeing."

> "Finally, none of the interactions between youth's GSA experiences and their sexual orientation identity, gender identity, or race or ethnicity were statistically significant in predicting their wellbeing."
