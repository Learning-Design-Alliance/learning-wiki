## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exploratory tests of whether L2 fear appeals moderated L1 relations between fear appeals and evaluations, and between evaluations and engagement, found no statistically significant interactions (ps > .05). [→ Putwain 2021](#putwain-2021)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2021

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Kutuk, G. (2021). Warning Students of the Consequences of Examination Failure: An Effective Strategy for Promoting Student Engagement? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329876

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary exploratory analysis testing whether within-level relations were amplified in classes where teachers made more frequent fear appeals. The article reports no significant moderation for any tested cross-level path.

> "All cross-level interactions were, however, not statistically significant (ps >.05)"
