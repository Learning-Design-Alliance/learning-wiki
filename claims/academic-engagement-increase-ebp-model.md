`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of institutional academic management system records between a 2019 pre-reform baseline cohort (n = 1,223) and a 2024 post-implementation cohort (n = 1,237). The article reports "academic activity participation increased from 78.5 % to 97.3 %" as descriptive evidence without formal statistical significance testing due to the single-arm design.

> "From baseline (2019) to post-implementation (2024), academic activity participation increased from 78.5 % to 97.3 %, evidence-based graduation theses rose from 18.6 % to 30.0 %, and approved projects grew from 8 to 22."
