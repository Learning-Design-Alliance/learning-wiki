`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory time-trend analysis within the same log-file-based mixed-model study of the Reminder Group; the article reports that "the effect of the reminders increased with time" (b = 0.31, p = 0.046). No effect size is printed.

> "We also explored changes in the effect of the reminders over time and found that the effect of the reminders increased with time ( b = 0.31, 95% CI [0.01, 0.62], χ2(1) = 3.78, p = 0.046)."
