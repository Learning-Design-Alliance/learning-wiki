## Evidence

### Maureen A. Conroy 2019

Maureen A. Conroy, Kevin S. Sutherland, James Algina, Crystal Ladwig, Brittany Werch, Jose Martinez, Gabriel Jessee, Maria Gyure. (2019). Outcomes of the BEST in CLASS Intervention on Teachers' Use of Effective Practices, Self-Efficacy, and Classroom Quality. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2018-0003.V48-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Teacher-report analysis in the same cluster randomized trial (n = 185 for TSES), using the complex procedure in Mplus 8.0 with cluster-robust standard errors and BHFDR correction. The article prints treatment effect sizes ".50 or larger for all three TSES variables"; the discussion reports "the largest effect size was reported for the Classroom Management subscale of the TSES (0.78)".

> "For each of the TSES subscales, BEST in CLASS resulted in higher teacher self-efficacy, with larger effects at Research Site B than at Research Site A. Effect sizes for the treatment effect were .50 or larger for all three TSES variables."
