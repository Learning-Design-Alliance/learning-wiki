Evidence¶

DaisyQuest 2006¶

DaisyQuest. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: DaisyQuest. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized trial of 27 students in a child-care facility assigned to DaisyQuest or regular program, using two phonological awareness tests. The report states effects were found "in favor of the DaisyQuest group" on both measures, confirmed by WWC calculations.

"For both measures, the authors found, and the WWC conﬁrmed, positive, statistically signiﬁcant effects in favor of the DaisyQuest group."

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children 2006¶

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial of 27 low- to middle-income children at a childcare center in Orem, Utah; intervention group used DaisyQuest for 20 sessions of approximately 20–25 minutes. Appendix A3 reports effect sizes of 0.93 (PAT) and 0.87 (STOPA-E), both "Statistically significant," with improvement indices of +32 and +31.

"The authors reported statistically significant differences favoring the intervention group on both outcomes, and this statistical significance was confirmed by the WWC."
