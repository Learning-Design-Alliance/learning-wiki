Joep van der Graaf, Michelle Taub, Yizhou Fan. (2023). Introduction to special issue on facilitating self-regulated learning with scaffolds: Recent advances and future directions. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09364-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The introduction reports, citing Sijmkens et al. (2023), a study with university physics students given a digital learning companion after solving textbook problems; the companion scaffolded reflection via discipline- and topic-specific multiple-choice prompts and provided feedback on learners' reflections. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Learners who used the prompts frequently showed a better strategic problem-solving approach and a larger conceptual understanding. Self-reported effort regulation was positively related to both strategic problem-solving and conceptual understanding."
