## Evidence

### S. Andrew Garbacz 2015

S. Andrew Garbacz, Susan M. Sheridan, Natalie A. Koziol, Kyongboon Kwon, Shannon R. Holmes. (2015). Congruence in Parent–Teacher Communication: Implications for the Efficacy of CBC for Students With Behavioral Concerns. School Psychology Review, 44(2), 150–168. https://www.nasponline.org/publications/periodicals/spr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null moderation result from the same three-level models, for the parent-reported social skills outcome. The article prints non-significant three-way interactions for both congruence components; no standardized effect size is printed. The authors note this was contrary to their hypothesis.
