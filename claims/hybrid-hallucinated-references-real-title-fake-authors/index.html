Discussion-section analysis of the 30 verified hallucinated references. The article reports the fabrication pattern: 13 entirely fabricated and 17 hybrid, and notes that in seven hybrid cases at least one listed author was correct.

"Thir- teen of the 30 appeared to be entirely fabricated, with no corre- sponding publication record found. The remaining 17 were hybrid references, combining a real title with fabricated or incorrect author- ship, venue, or year information."
