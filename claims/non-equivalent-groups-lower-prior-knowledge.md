## Evidence

### Corell A 2018

Corell A, Regueras LM, Verdú E, Verdú MJ, de Castro JP. (2018). Effects of competitive learning tools on medical students: A case study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0194096

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparison of pretest grades (three first-year courses) between control (n = 113) and experimental (n = 172) groups; Table 2 prints means of 5.82 vs 5.28 with p = 0.004. The article notes this difference worked against the assumption that competitive-itinerary students were the best students.

> "Our results showed that the students of the control group had a slightly higher previous knowledge level in Biochemistry, Genetics and Cellular Biology, and moreover, this difference was significant (p < 0.005)."

### Corell A 2018 (2)

Corell A, Regueras LM, Verdú E, Verdú MJ, de Castro JP. (2018). Effects of competitive learning tools on medical students: A case study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0194096

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparisons of self-reported hard-working level and class attendance level between control (n = 113) and experimental (n = 172) groups; Table 2 prints p = 0.407 and p = 0.307. The article reports the differences were "not significant (p > 0.05)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results showed that the difference between the two groups was not significant (p > 0.05)."
