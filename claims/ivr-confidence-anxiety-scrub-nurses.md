## Evidence

### Edwards Thomas C. 2021

Edwards Thomas C., Patel Arjun, Szyszka Bartosz, Coombs Alexander W., Liddle Alexander D., Kucheria Rakesh, Cobb Justin P., Logishetty Kartik. (2021). Immersive virtual reality enables technical skill acquisition for scrub nurses in complex revision total knee arthroplasty. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-021-04050-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre- and post-training questionnaire in the single-arm study; participants rated confidence, anxiety and knowledge on a 5-point Likert scale before and after the iVR curriculum. The quote reports the component-assembly and procedural-knowledge confidence gains; anxiety reduction is reported in the same section with p < 0.001.

> "Confidence increased in component assembly (mean pre-simulation 1.5 ± 0.85 to 3.8 ± 0.44 post-simulation, p < 0.001) and knowledge of the procedural steps (mean pre-simulation 1.60 ± 0.97 to 3.89 ± 0.60, p < 0.001)."
