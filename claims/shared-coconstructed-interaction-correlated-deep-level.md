# Shared and co-constructed intra-group interaction were significantly correlated with deep-level metacognitive activities during pre-task regulation

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Frequency of shared interaction correlated with frequency of deep-level metacognitive activities (r = 0.70, p < 0.05) and co-constructed more strongly (r = 0.84, p < 0.01) during pre-task regulation. [→ Kielstra 2022](#kielstra-2022)

## Evidence

### Kielstra 2022

Kielstra, J., Molenaar, I., van Steensel, R., & Verhoeven, L. (2022). 'Supporting socially shared regulation during collaborative task‑oriented reading'. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 17, 65–105. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09365-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlation at group level between interaction type frequencies and depth of metacognitive activity, calculated to check the assumption linking depth to sharedness; r = 0.70 approaches the strong threshold. The authors note "shared and co-constructed interaction co-occur more frequently in deep-level metacognitive interactions".

> "the frequency of shared (r = 0.70, p < 0.05) and co-constructed (r = 0.84, p < 0.01) intra-group metacognitive interaction was significantly correlated with the frequency of deep-level metacognitive activities."
