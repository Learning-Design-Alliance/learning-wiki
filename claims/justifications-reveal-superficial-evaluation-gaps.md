### Yan Tao 2026

Yan Tao, Jennifer Meyer & René F. Kizilcec. (2026). Think Before You Accept: Can Written Justification Reduce Uncritical Uptake of AI Writing Suggestions? Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke's framework) of written justifications from the justification condition, focused on flawed suggestions. The most common rejection reason for Suggestion C was that "the suggested theory contradicts or is irrelevant to the proposal hypothesis"; vague endorsement dominated acceptance justifications.

> "Among those responses (n= 24), 18 gave this as the sole reason for rejection, without recognizing that the theory itself was fabricated or misrepresented."
