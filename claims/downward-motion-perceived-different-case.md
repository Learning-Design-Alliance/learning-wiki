## Evidence

### Alicia M. Montecinos 2013

Alicia M. Montecinos. (2013). Free fall misconceptions: results of a graph based pre test of sophomore civil engineering students. [doi:10.20308/ejpe.27388](https://doi.org/10.20308/ejpe.27388)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of correct-model frequencies between part I (free fall from rest) and part II/downwards motion in the 14-student pre-test. The author concludes students "perceive this motions as different cases, being that they have the exact same qualities".

> "for the position model, there was a decrease of 30% on the frequency of parabolic mod els, and a decrease of 70% correct answers. In the case of velocity, a decrease of 75% in linea l models is found, with a decrease of 40% on  correct answers."
