Blitz survey after e-textbook selection; Question 3 on positive characteristics (fig. 9) shows ratings such as arrangement of interactive exercises at 74% and design at 59%. The article concludes the e-textbook generally satisfies the needs of educational process but that practice teachers consider it necessary to saturate it with more interactive exercises and more variability.
