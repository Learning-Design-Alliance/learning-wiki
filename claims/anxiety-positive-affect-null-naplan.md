### Cárdenas 2022

Cárdenas, D., Lattimore, F., Steinberg, D., & Reynolds, K. J. (2022). Youth well-being predicts later academic success. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-05780-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal ML analysis of the same matched cohort testing each well-being component separately; no effect sizes are printed for these null findings in the main text, so equivalence was not tested. The article adds that "anxiety may have a small positive effect on NAPLAN scores (though this association was not significant)" when depression is held fixed.

> "In contrast, self-reported anxiety or positive affect did not significantly predict any NAPLAN score (see Supplementary Table 4 in Supplementary Material)."
