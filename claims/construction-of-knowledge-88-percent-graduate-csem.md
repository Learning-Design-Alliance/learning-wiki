### Ghimire 2024

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2024). How often does unguided peer interaction lead to correct response consensus? An example from Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad336c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad336c)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the same in-class graduate study (42 individuals, 20 groups), where construction was defined as a mixed pair (one correct, one incorrect individually) producing a correct group response. The article reports "in 88% of the eligible cases across all questions, construction happened"; per-item construction rates ranged up to 100% (Table 1).
