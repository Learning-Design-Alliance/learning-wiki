## Evidence

### Eddy SL 2015

Eddy SL, Brownell SE, Thummaphan P, Lan M-C, Wenderoth MP. (2015). Caution, Student Experience May Vary: Social Identities Impact a Student's Experience in Peer Discussions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-05-0108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study I model-averaged multinomial results using total exam points (z-scored) as a competency control. The article reports that "As biology competency increased, students were 1.6 times more likely to prefer to be leaders/explainers" relative to listeners, with only the leader/explainer comparison significant.

> "As biology competency increased, students were 1.6 times more likely to prefer to be leaders/explainers (p = 0.027), 1.4 times more likely to prefer to be collaborators (p = 0.09), and 2.0 times more likely to prefer to be recorders (p = 0.051) relative to listeners."
