## Evidence

### Guill Karin 2019

Guill Karin, Lüdtke Oliver, Köller Olaf. (2019). Assessing the instructional quality of private tutoring and its effects on student outcomes: Analyses from the German National Educational Panel Study. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12281

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Because the structural equation model did not converge for the small German tutoring subsample (N = 148), a path model with manifest scale mean scores was used (αstructure = .50, αchallenge = .77, αsupport = .57). Support related to satisfaction but "There were no significant effects on end‐of‐year grades".

> "Support in private tutoring was positively associated with satisfaction with school and had a marginally positive effect on satisfaction with family life. There were no significant effects on end‐of‐year grades"
