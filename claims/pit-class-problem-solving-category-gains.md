# After PITs were introduced, CLASS-physics categories associated with mathematical reasoning showed small positive pre-to-post gains (Cohen's d = +0.2)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Each of the five CLASS categories addressing mathematical reasoning in physics showed a small positive effect size of Cohen's d = +0.2, significant at the 95% confidence level. [→ Suzanne White Brahmia 2017](#suzanne-white-brahmia-2017)

## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2017

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux and Stephen E. Kanim. (2017). Developing Mathematical Creativity with Physics Invention Tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.02033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.02033)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Matched pre/post CLASS-physics data (combined Fall 2013 and Fall 2014, n=121) from the large-enrollment EAP course after PITs were implemented; appendix reports category changes such as "+6.0 ± 2.4" for PS-General with p = .01, and the authors attribute the gains to "the modified ISLE curriculum enriched by the PITs".

> "The pre-to-post comparison in each of these five categories has a small positive effect size (Cohen's d = +0.2) and is significant at the 95% confidence interval using a two -tailed repeated t-test of significance."
