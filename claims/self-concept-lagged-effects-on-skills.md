RI-CCLPM analysis of ProFAN mathematics data (N = 9,142). Self-concept "predicted future skill gains in the form of lagged effects" (β2 = [.111, .159], p < .001) across approximately 8-month semester intervals; the contemporaneous path was fixed to zero. No standardized effect size is printed for this path in this section.

> "Mathematics self-concept, in turn, predicted future skill gains in the form of lagged effects (β2 =[ .111,. 159], p<. 001), whereas contemporaneous effects were nonsignificant and fixed to zero for parsimony."
