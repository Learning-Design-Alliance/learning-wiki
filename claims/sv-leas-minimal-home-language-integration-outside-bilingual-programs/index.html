Expanding Equitable Access to Bilingual Education in California's Silicon Valley: Analysis and Recommendations. (2026). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681371

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey and interview data from participating Silicon Valley LEA staff show bilingual program availability. The report states "43 percent offer bilingual programs— a higher rate than the region overall," with dual language immersion most common at 29 percent.

"Among LEA staff participating in the interview or survey, 43 percent offer bilingual programs— a higher rate than the region overall. Dual language immersion represents the most common model, at 29 percent."

In the LEA survey, respondents compared their programs to a low-integration example; "half of the LEA respondents considered their programs to be similar," indicating minimal home language integration outside formal bilingual programs.

"When asked to compare their own programs to an example in which bilingualism is reflected only in the occasional use of individual words or phrases or translation tools, half of the LEA respondents considered their programs to be similar"
