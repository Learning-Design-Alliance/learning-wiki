Yao Yuanyuan, Rui Min, Zhou Xiangyong, Han Zixin, Luo Ge, Liu Yi, Tao Jiachun, Guo Jin, Tang Biyun, Zheng Bin, Wang Yi, Yan Min. (2025). Perioperative simulation-based medical education in China: a nationwide cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08445-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results reported with Figure 3A and B: obstacles faced by administrators and instructors in perioperative simulation training. The finding is descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "For administrators, the main challenges included limited space, simulators, and faculty shortages. Instructors similarly pointed to faculty shortages and space and simulator constraints as their major hurdles."

### Yao Yuanyuan 2025 (2)

Yao Yuanyuan, Rui Min, Zhou Xiangyong, Han Zixin, Luo Ge, Liu Yi, Tao Jiachun, Guo Jin, Tang Biyun, Zheng Bin, Wang Yi, Yan Min. (2025). Perioperative simulation-based medical education in China: a nationwide cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08445-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis stratified by hospital level with two-proportion z-test comparisons (Supplemental table 4); tertiary-hospital administrators reported "more severe faculty, space, and simulator shortages". No effect size is printed in the supplied text.

> "Our subgroup analysis revealed that administrators at tertiary teaching hospitals experienced more severe faculty, space, and simulator shortages during perioperative SBME, possibly due to heavier teaching responsibilities and a greater emphasis on SBME in these institutions (Supplemental table 4)."
