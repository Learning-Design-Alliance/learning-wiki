Training Sri Lankan public health midwives on intimate partner violence: a pre- and post-intervention study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1674-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section report on the same 408-PHM pre/post survey. The article states that six months after the intervention 89.7% followed up at least one sufferer and 24.5% followed up all of the IPV sufferers.

> "Before the intervention, 46.8% of the PHMs followed up at least one IPV sufferer after discussing IPV; none followed up all of the sufferers with whom they discussed IPV."
