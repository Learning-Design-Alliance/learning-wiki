## Evidence

### Cooper Z. 2015

Cooper Z., Doll H., Bailey-Straebler S., Kluczniok D., Murphy R., O'Connor M.E., Fairburn C.G. (2015). The development of an online measure of therapist competence. Behaviour Research and Therapy, 64, 43–48. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2014.11.007

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

ROC analysis using Rasch person locations from 22 clinicians independently judged competent versus the rest of the sample, with equal weight given to sensitivity and specificity. S-24 had AUC = 0.972 (sens = 0.909, spec = 0.907) and S-25 AUC = 0.969 (sens = 0.909, spec = 0.895).

> "the best cut points (BCPs) overall, differentiating the 'competent' from the other 'non-competent' clinicians, were a location (ability level) of 0.27 for S-22 (AUC = 0.964, sens = 0.909, spec = 0.881)"
