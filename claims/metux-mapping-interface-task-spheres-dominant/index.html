Evidence¶

Glawe 2025¶

Glawe, F., Schmeckel, T., Brauner, P., & Ziefle, M. (2025). Human Autonomy and Sense of Agency in Human-Robot Interaction: A Systematic Literature Review. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22271

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's discussion maps the 22 studies' measures and implemented factors onto METUX's six spheres; it reports measures concentrate on interface and task spheres, adoption was rarely examined through an autonomy lens, and behaviour and life spheres appear only in qualitative interviews.

"none of the included studies explicitly grounded their work in METUX, employed its sphere-specific instruments, or used the guidelines to design interactions. Nevertheless, most mea- sures used map to the interface or task spheres."
