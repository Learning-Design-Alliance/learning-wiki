Two-mode SNA projecting participants onto the five Interaction Analysis Model strategies (Figs. 9–11, Table 3). The study reports 3D VR co-creators did not attempt C5 until week 3 while 2D co-creators employed it in week 2; no effect size was printed.

"It is noteworthy that C5, as the highest cognitive strategy, was never the targeted strategy used in 3D VR co-creation, suggesting the 3D VR co-creators' difficulty in dealing with knowledge co-construction and troubleshooting at the same time."
