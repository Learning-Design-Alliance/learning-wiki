## Evidence

### Zhao 2025

Zhao, H., Lin, X., Yuan, K., Hu, X., Wang, X., Su, W., Wang, Q., & Lu, L. (2025). Updating social evaluation during sleep. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00356-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control experiment in which a third group heard only the cued name during SWS. Comparing that group's uncued-name facial attractiveness ratings with the experimental group's revealed "no significant difference" (F(1, 66) = 0.74, p = 0.393); no standardized effect size was printed.

> "This analysis revealed no signiﬁcant difference in the facial attractiveness ratings for the uncued name between the mere exposure group and the experimental group (F(1, 66) = 0.74,p = 0.393, see Supplementary Fig. 3)"
