Hyun Jin Kim 2024¶

Hyun Jin Kim, Yuyi Park, Jihyun Lee. (2024). The Validity of Heart Rate Variability (HRV) in Educational Research and a Synthesis of Recommendations. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09878-x

q3 · i? · review · r3

Study-features analysis of the 48 included studies: 44 focused on stress (91.7%) and 12 on attention (25%), with eight addressing both. Counts are as printed in the review.

"Of the studies, 44 out of 48 focused on stress (91.7%), while a few focused on attention (n = 12, 25%)."
