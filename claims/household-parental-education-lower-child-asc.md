## Evidence

### Horoz 2025

Horoz, N., van Atteveldt, N. M., Oude Groeniger, J., Koot, J. R., ..., & Buil, J. M. (2025). Household- and school-level parental education and academic self-concept development in elementary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00354-x

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel latent growth model main-effect analysis (N = 679, 18 schools) found household-level parental education significantly associated with the child-level ASC intercept (β = −0.138, B = −0.051, p = 0.010); "these differences remained stable from fourth to sixth grade". Slope associations were never significant.

> "children of lower-educated parents reported less positive views about their academic abilities than children of higher-educated parents in fourth grade, and these differences remained stable from fourth to sixth grade."
