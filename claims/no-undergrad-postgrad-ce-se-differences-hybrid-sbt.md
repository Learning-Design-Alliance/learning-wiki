## Evidence

### Tam 2025

Tam, C. Y. W., Dizon, J. I. W. T., Sin, S. W. C., Tang, G. K. Y., Lai, V. Y. W., Wong, Y. Y., Ganotice, F. A., & Yeung, N. P. (2025). Implementation of hybrid simulation training to promote collective and self-efficacy in medical students: a two-pronged approach. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2025.2527362

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent two-sample t-tests comparing degree holders (n = 20) vs non-holders (n = 74); CE also showed no significant differences at baseline (p = 0.444) or posttest (p = 0.17). No effect size is attached to the null findings.

> "there was also no significant differences in SE scores between undergraduate students vs postgraduate students, at baseline (25.31 ± 6.52 vs 27.56 ± 6.26, p = 0.173), and after progressing through hybrid SBT (31.2 ± 5.31 vs 33.15 ± 4.58, p = 0.138)"
