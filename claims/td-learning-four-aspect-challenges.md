Synthesis of 36 empirical studies in a scoping review. The review reports that responding to sustainability issues was unsettling for many students, and that complexity and uncertainty could lead to undesirable coping behaviors, such as oversimplifying the problem or failing to involve all relevant societal partners.

> "First, responding to sustainability issues was unsettling for many students because it required they to reflect on their assumptions about consumption, race, gender, and other social issues"
