## Evidence

### Babayiğit 2026

Babayiğit, S., & Trenkic, D. (2026). Vocabulary knowledge is key to understanding and addressing disparities in higher education. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 127–151. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 179 students rating three statements about academic English skills on a collapsed 3-point scale, analysed with Fisher's exact test and chi-square. The article reports the group association was "statistically significant for all three statements, with medium effect sizes"; EFL students "felt most strongly that their English skills were limiting their academic performance".

> "The association between language group and the degree of agreement with these statements was statistically significant for all three statements, with medium effect sizes."
