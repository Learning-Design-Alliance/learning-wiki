### Stephen Lipscomb 2015

Stephen Lipscomb, Jeffrey Terziev, Duncan Chaplin. (2015). Measuring Teachers' Effectiveness: A Report from Phase 3 of Pennsylvania's Pilot of the Framework for Teaching. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-teachers-effectiveness-a-report-from-phase-3-of-pennsylvanias-pilot-of-the-framework

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis merging FFT observation scores with teachers' estimated contributions to student achievement growth for 6,676 teachers. The report prints correlation coefficients (r) of 0.19 to 0.22 by domain, all positive and generally statistically significant; the printed effect size r = 0.19 to 0.22 corresponds to a small correlation.

> "The correlations of the FFT scores with value-added measures scores were all positive and generally statistically significant, ranging from 0.19 to 0.22 by domain."
