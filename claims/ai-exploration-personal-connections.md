`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Instructor report from classroom implementation of the exploration assignment in the asynchronous introductory course. The authors describe students making personal connections, including a student nail technician connecting physics to work tasks, and conclude this showed material was connected to everyday experiences.

> "One of the strongest aspects of the assignment was that it helped students connect the content to their personal lives. In several cases, students used the AI interaction to explore how physics related to things they cared about outside of academia."
