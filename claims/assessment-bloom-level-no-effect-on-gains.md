## Evidence

### Holt Emily A. 2015

Holt Emily A., Young Craig, Keetch Jared, Larsen Skylar, Mollner Brayden. (2015). The Greatest Learning Return on Your Pedagogical Investment: Alignment, Assessment or In-Class Instruction? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0137446

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis across the 15 sections testing weighted assessment Bloom scores as a predictor of section post-quiz scores; the test was not significant (F1,9 = 0.83, p = 0.38). No effect size was printed, so non-significance does not establish equivalence.

> "Bloom scores did not explain variation in students' post-quiz scores by section (learning objective Bloom scores: F1,9 = 1.13, p = 0.31; assessment Bloom scores: F1,9 = 0.83, p = 0.38)."
