# Topic-final sentences show higher harmonic centrality than topic-medial sentences and lower local clustering coefficient than other sentence types

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Topic-medial sentences have lower harmonic centrality than topic-final sentences (p = 0.004), meaning readers transition less often from topic-ending sentences. [→ Catrysse 2025](#catrysse-2025)
`q3 i?` Topic-final sentences have a lower local clustering coefficient than topic-initial (p = 0.048) and topic-medial (p = 0.008) sentences, indicating less rereading around topic-final sentences. [→ Catrysse 2025 (2)](#catrysse-2025-2)
