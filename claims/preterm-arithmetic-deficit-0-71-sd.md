## Evidence

### Twilhaar E Sabrina 2018

Twilhaar E Sabrina, de Kieviet Jorrit F, Aarnoudse-Moens Cornelieke SH, van Elburg Ruurd M, Oosterlaan Jaap. (2018). Academic performance of children born preterm: a meta-analysis and meta-regression. Archives of Disease in Childhood. Fetal and Neonatal Edition. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2017-312916

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random effects meta-analysis of 12 studies reporting arithmetic in standardised, norm-referenced tests; the printed effect size is SMD = −0.71, a medium effect, with high heterogeneity (I2=81.91), so the pooled effect should be cautiously interpreted. The quote states "0.71 SD below scores of full-term peers".

> "Arithmetic scores of preterm children were 0.71 SD below scores of full-term peers (z=−7.67, p<0.001), indicating a medium effect."
