## Evidence

### Stuart Webb and Anna C.-S. Chang 2015

Stuart Webb and Anna C.-S. Chang. (2015). HOW DOES PRIOR WORD KNOWLEDGE AFFECT VOCABULARY LEARNING PROGRESS IN AN EXTENSIVE READING PROGRAM? Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 37, Issue 4, pp. 651-675. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263114000606

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the 60-participant extensive reading study over 37 weeks, even the low-level group achieved a relative gain of 28.12% from pretest to immediate posttest, showing vocabulary learning occurred at all three prior-knowledge levels.

> "the relative gains from pretest to immediate posttest were 63.18%, 44.64%, and 28.12% for the high-, intermediate-, and low-level groups, respectively"
