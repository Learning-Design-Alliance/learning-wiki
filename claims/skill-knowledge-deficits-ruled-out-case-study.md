### Gupta 2010

Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2010). Beyond deficit-based models of learners' cognition: Interpreting engineering students' difficulties with sense-making in terms of fine-grained epistemological and conceptual dynamics. Submitted to Journal of Engineering Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.4288](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.4288)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of interview segments rules out deficit explanations one by one: Jim "quickly and correctly reasoned" the implication of positive h, solved the two-ball problem correctly, chose down as positive there, and articulated that pressure increases with depth from snorkeling experience.

> "Jim quickly and correctly reasoned that, assuming h is positive (and all the constants are positive), the equation p = pat top + ρgh implies that the pressure is greater at a depth of 7 meters than at a depth of 5 meters."
