### Ebrahimpour F 2025

Ebrahimpour F. (2025). Nursing students’ experiences using photovoice with team-based learning in pediatric nursing education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08347-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative component: semi-structured interviews with 10 of the 36 students, purposively sampled with maximum variation, analyzed via content analysis to thematic saturation; the article reports "five main themes" as listed.

> "Qualitative analysis identified five main themes: (1) perceived novelty and pedagogical creativity; (2) enhanced engagement through active and experiential learning; (3) increased critical awareness of environmental factors influencing child health; (4) deeper learning through visual contextualization and peer discussion; and (5) practical challenges related to photography in public settings and group collaboration."
