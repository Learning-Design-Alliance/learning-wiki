# Programs substitute simulation for required skills more often than for clinical or field internship hours, and most programs want to use more simulation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Simulation was substituted for clinical or field skills at least sometimes by 43% of programs, versus 13% for clinical hours and 8% for field internship hours; 78% of respondents felt they should use more simulation, and this desire was significantly related to perceived inadequacy of faculty education (χ2 (1, N = 371) = 9.665, p < 0.01) and personnel support. [→ McKenna K. D. 2015](#mckenna-k-d-2015)

> "A chi-square analysis demonstrated a significant relationship between wanting to use more simulation and the perceived adequacy of faculty education (χ 2 (1, N = 371) = 9.665, p < 0.01)."
