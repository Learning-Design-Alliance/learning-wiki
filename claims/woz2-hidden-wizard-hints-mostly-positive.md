Sandra Schulz · Bruce M. McLaren · Niels Pinkwart. (2023). Towards a Tutoring System to Support Robotics Activities in Classrooms – Two Wizard-of-Oz Studies. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 33. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00305-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative transcript analysis of video-recorded WOZ II lab sessions in which 6 students in pairs received hints from a hidden wizard via Slack. Positive reactions were coded when students “read out the message and started directly with the suggestion” (N = 32).

> "One positive reaction was coded in the sub-category “use the hints directly” when the students read out the message and started directly with the suggestion ( N = 32 ), as in Example 2."
