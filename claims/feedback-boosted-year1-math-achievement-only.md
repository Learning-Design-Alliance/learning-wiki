## Evidence

### Garet 2017

Garet, M.S., Wayne, A.J., Brown, S., Rickles, J., Song, M., and Manzeske, D. (2017). The Impact of Providing Performance Feedback to Teachers and Principals, Executive Summary (NCEE 2018-4000). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Year 2 estimates represent the cumulative two-year impact, controlling for baseline achievement. The article states there is "no evidence that the cumulative impact on achievement increased from the first to the second year".

> "In Year 2, in mathematics, students in treatment schools scored at the 51.2nd percentile, compared to the 48.9th percentile for control students, a 2.3-point difference, similar in magnitude to the impact in Year 1 but not statistically significant (p = 0.055)."
