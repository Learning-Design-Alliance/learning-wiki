## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2026

Xue Gang, Zhang Xin, Ji Shengyi, Qi Jin. (2026). Integrating problem-based learning with virtual simulation in trauma orthopedic education: effects on learning outcomes and student satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09558-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dimension-level teaching satisfaction results in the same assessor-blinded RCT of 50 clinical medical students, outlined in Table 4 and Fig. 6. The experimental group "reported higher scores than the control group across all dimensions of teaching satisfaction", with printed means and standard deviations for each of the five domains. No effect size is printed for the individual dimensions.

> "The experimental group reported higher scores than the control group across all dimensions of teaching satisfaction, including teaching effect (17.52 ± 1.53 vs. 14.08 ± 2.06, P < 0.05), learning interest (17.28 ± 1.59 vs. 13.80 ± 2.02, P < 0.05), teaching content (17.36 ± 1.52 vs. 14.12 ± 2.15, P < 0.05), self-value (17.20 ± 1.83 vs. 13.96 ± 1.84, P < 0.05), and humanistic competence and teamwork (17.60 ± 1.55 vs. 14.28 ± 2.25, P < 0.05)"
