Initial observational investigation comparing engineering programs' US News & World Reports rankings with their structural complexities. The article reports an "inverse correlation between the perceived quality of engineering programs" and their structural complexities, contradicting the assumption that complexity equals quality, and notes the relationship merits further investigation.

> "Our initial investigation of this relationship, however, reveals quite the opposite. Specifically, we have observed an inverse correlation between the perceived quality of engineering programs (as determined by US News & World Reports ranking) and the structural complexities of these programs."
