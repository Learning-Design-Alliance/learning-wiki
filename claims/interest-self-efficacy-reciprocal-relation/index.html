Renninger 2026¶

Renninger, K. A., Barton, L. K., Bautista, G., Garcia-Barrios, M., Ogunyinka, I., & Riley, K. R. (2026). Interest, Self-efficacy, and Belonging in Classroom Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10102-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Nuutila et al. (2020), a study of elementary students' pre-post survey responses and mathematics tasks in which "triggers for situational interest contributed to participants' feelings of self-efficacy and boosted performance." The review also cites Fryer and Ainley's (2019) longitudinal survey study as evidence the relation is reciprocal.

"Nuutila et al. ( 2020) examined elementary students' pre-post survey responses and their work with mathematics tasks, they reported that triggers for situational interest contributed to participants' feelings of self-efficacy and boosted performance."
