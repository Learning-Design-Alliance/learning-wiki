## Evidence

### Wakeford Richard 2015

Wakeford Richard, Denney MeiLing, Ludka-Stempien Katarzyna, Dacre Jane, McManus I C. (2015). Cross-comparison of MRCGP & MRCP(UK) in a database linkage study of 2,284 candidates taking both examinations: assessment of validity and differential performance by ethnicity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0281-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Database linkage study of 2,284 candidates who took both examinations, MRCP(UK) typically taken 3.7 years before MRCGP. The reported "disattenuated correlations" of 0.748, 0.698 and 0.636 indicate substantial shared variance between the separate examinations.

> "Correlations between MRCGP and MRCP(UK) were high, disattenuated correlations for MRCGP AKT with MRCP(UK) Parts 1 and 2 being 0.748 and 0.698, and for CSA and PACES being 0.636."
