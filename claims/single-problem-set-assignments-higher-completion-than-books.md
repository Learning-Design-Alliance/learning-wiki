## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2023

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Moeller Korbinian, Musslick Sebastian. (2023). Assignment strategies modulate students’ academic performance in an online learning environment during the first and second COVID-19 related school closures. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284868

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic mixed-model analysis of Bettermarks log data for the first 2020 closure versus the same period in 2019, with nested random intercepts for students, classes and books. The time window by assignment interaction was significant, with "larger for students who got assigned single problem sets versus entire books".

> "Finally, the interaction of time window and assignment was significant (b = .13; z = 5.20; p < .001), suggesting that the difference in completion rates between school closures and control periods was larger for students who got assigned single problem sets versus entire books."

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2023 (2)

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Moeller Korbinian, Musslick Sebastian. (2023). Assignment strategies modulate students’ academic performance in an online learning environment during the first and second COVID-19 related school closures. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284868

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Replication analysis for the second 2021 closure versus the same window in 2020, same mixed-model design. The interaction replicated with a larger coefficient, the difference being "larger for single problem set assignments as compared to book assignments".

> "The interaction of time window and assignment was significant (b = .30; z = 12.48; p < .001), suggesting that the difference in completion rates as a function of time window was larger for single problem set assignments as compared to book assignments."
