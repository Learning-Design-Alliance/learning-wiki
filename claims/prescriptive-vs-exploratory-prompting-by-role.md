## Evidence

### Razan Hamed 2025

Razan Hamed, Amogh Sirnoorkar, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Dual-Role Dynamics in Prompting: Elementary Pre-service Teachers' AI Prompting Strategies for Representational Choices. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Emergent coding classified prompts as "prescriptive" (instructing AI to generate a chosen representation) or "exploratory" (letting AI decide). In the teacher role 17 of 20 prompts were prescriptive versus 11 in the student role; only three participants were exploratory in both roles.

> "The data reflected 17 out of 20 participants' prompts to be prescriptive in the teacher role as compared to 11 in the student role."
