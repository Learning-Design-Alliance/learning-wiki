### Si Jihyun 2020

Si Jihyun. (2020). Course-based research experience of undergraduate medical students through project-based learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2020.152

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Nine group research reports were scored by two professors using a nine-criterion research skill rubric (levels 1–3), with consensus scoring; descriptive statistics only, no inferential test.

> "The mean of the research skill scores of each group was 19.11, with the scores ranging from 13 to 24. The mean of the individual research skills score was 2.12, with the skill to "undertake literature search on topics" earning the highest score and the skill to "effectively present data" earning the lowest score."
