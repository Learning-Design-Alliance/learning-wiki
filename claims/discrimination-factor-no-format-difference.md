## Evidence

### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item analysis of the same 29-student dental cohort; ANOVA found no significant discrimination difference among formats. Descriptively EMQs were optimal (0.37 ± 0.23) while SAQs and MCQs "needed revision due to low discrimination factor".

> "The difference in the discrimination factor among the formats did not reach the significance threshold (F = 2.91, P = 0.058). Based on the mean discrimination factor of each question format, it can be concluded that the SAQs and MCQs with a mean of 0.29 ± 0.20 and 0.25 ± 0.19, respectively, needed revision due to low discrimination factor. However, the EMQs with a mean discrimination factor of 0.37 ± 0.23 were optimal."
