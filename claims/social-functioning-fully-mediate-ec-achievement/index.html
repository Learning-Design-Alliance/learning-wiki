Evidence¶

Valiente Carlos 2011¶

Valiente Carlos, Eisenberg Nancy, Haugen Rg, Spinrad Tracy L., Hofer Claire, Liew Jeffrey, Kupfer Anne. (2011). Children's Effortful Control and Academic Achievement: Mediation Through Social Functioning. Early Education and Development, 22(3), 411-433. https://doi.org/10.1080/10409289.2010.505259

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation modeling with full information maximum likelihood in MPlus (N=214) tested mediation; the confidence-interval method of MacKinnon and colleagues was used, with confidence intervals of 0.37 and 2.29 supporting mediation. The model fit the data well, χ2(59)=76.27, p<.09, CFI=.97, RMSEA=.04.

"Children's T2 social functioning fully mediated the relation between T1 EC and T3 academic achievement in a structural equation model."
