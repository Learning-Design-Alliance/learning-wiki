`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression results for Course 2 (n = 614 analyzed students) show that "students who worked were still less likely to retake exams in this course" even though retakes were scheduled during class time or online, and Black/African American students were also significantly less likely to retake.

> "In Course 2, where retakes were scheduled during class time or administered online, students who worked were still less likely to retake exams in this course (Figure 2). Moreover, Black/African American students were significantly less likely to retake exams in this course."
