`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

SEM mediation test in the randomized trial: the intervention was significantly negatively associated with T2 ineffective parenting, which was in turn negatively associated with T4 parent involvement. The article reports the indirect path "was significant (z = 2.00, p = .045)".

> "This indirect path from the intervention to parent involvement in kindergarten was significant (z = 2.00, p = .045; standardized estimate = .03, 95% confidence interval = .001 - .06)."
