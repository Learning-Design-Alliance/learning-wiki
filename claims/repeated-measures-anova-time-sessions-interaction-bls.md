## Evidence

### Ranjbar Fataneh 2024

Ranjbar Fataneh, Sharif-Nia Hamid, Shiri Maryam, Rahmatpour Pardis. (2024). The effect of spaced E-Learning on knowledge of basic life support and satisfaction of nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05533-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the quasi-experimental study of 106 nursing students assessed BLS knowledge at pretest and three post-tests; the omnibus results were "influenced by time (p < 0.001)", sessions, and their interaction, all significant.

> "the results highlighted that the changes in knowledge scores in both groups were influenced by time (p < 0.001), the number of sessions (p < 0.001), and their interactive effect (p < 0.001)"
