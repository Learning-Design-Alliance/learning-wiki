`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity was assessed by ten expert surgeons using eight items covering critical skills and main purposes of LDSRS training. The recommendation item received a perfect score (5.00 ± 0.00).

> "The model also demonstrated strong content validity, as evaluated by the experts, with all items receiving mean scores above 4.0 (Table 3). Experts strongly agreed that the model effectively improved technical skills, enhanced confidence, accelerated the learning process, and reduced patient risk."
