## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Groups that plotted drownings and ice cream sales versus month identified temperature as the underlying cause, while groups that plotted drownings versus ice cream sales failed to identify the hidden variable. [→ Álvaro Suárez 2026](#alvaro-suarez-2026)

## Evidence

### Álvaro Suárez 2026

Álvaro Suárez, Marcelo Vachetta. (2026). Uncovering Hidden Variables: A Physics Classroom Activity on Correlation and Causation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1119/5.0244433](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0244433)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom observation during the implementation: three teams plotted drownings versus ice cream sales while two teams plotted both variables versus month; the month-plotting teams saw that "the underlying cause was the variation in temperature over the year". No statistical test or effect size is reported.

> "The groups that plotted the number of deaths and ice cream sales over the months were able to see that although the two variables were correlated, the underlying cause was the variation in temperature over the year."
