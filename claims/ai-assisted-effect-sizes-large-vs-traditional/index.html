Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2026¶

Dimitrios Gousopoulos. (2026). Can AI-Assisted Inquiry Enhance Students' Decision-Making Skills in Socio-Scientific Issues? A Three-Group Experimental Study on Climate Change. International Journal of Advanced Multidisciplinary Research and Studies. https://doi.org/10.62225/2583049X.2026.6.3.6516

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Three-group experiment (270 students, 90 per group) reporting between-group effect sizes on decision-making gains; the article states they were "large for AI-assisted versus traditional instruction and moderate-to-large for AI-assisted versus inquiry-only." No numeric effect-size values are printed in the available text.

"Between-group effect sizes on gains were large for AI-assisted versus traditional instruction and moderate-to-large for AI-assisted versus inquiry-only, with the clearest advantages on stakeholder engagement, alternatives, implementation, and monitoring."
