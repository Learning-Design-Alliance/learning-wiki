Manual categorisation study: three expert coders, trained in rounds of new messages until over 80% agreement without negotiations, classified the LCT MOOC sample into the five cognitive presence categories using an adapted coding rubric, reaching "an overall percentage agreement of 77.15% and Fleiss' κ of 0.763".
