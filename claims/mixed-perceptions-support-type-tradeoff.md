## Evidence

### Xinying Hou 2025

Xinying Hou, Zihan Wu, Xu Wang, and Barbara J. Ericson. (2025). Personalized Parsons Puzzles as Scaffolding Enhance Practice Engagement Over Just Showing LLM-Powered Solutions. Proceedings of the 56th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 2 (SIGCSE TS 2025). https://doi.org/10.1145/3641555.3705227

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Perception data from the classroom study: some PC students felt puzzles "still provided too much support" while others asked for additional help placing blocks; CC students were similarly split between valuing quick solutions and feeling solutions blocked independent learning.

> "some PC students felt that the puzzles still provided too much support, and they would rather have a more lightweight approach, like subtle hints. However, another group of PC students required more guidance on how the blocks should be rearranged."
