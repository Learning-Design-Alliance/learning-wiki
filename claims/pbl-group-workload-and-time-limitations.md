Qualitative comments from a few respondents in the perception questionnaire, reported in the discussion section; the authors relate these to Silva et al.'s report that teamwork and time can limit PBL learning.

> "A few of the respondents qualitatively reported in this study that some students in the PBL group worked harder than other members of the same group to prepare and participate in discussions. They also found the time allocated for each topic was not sufficient at times."
