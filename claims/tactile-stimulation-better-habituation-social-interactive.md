## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 5 days, habituation and social interactive mean scores of the tactile stimulation group were higher than the routine-care control group, a highly statistically significant difference (t=0.000). [→ Atyat Mohammed Hassan Sayed 2015](#atyat-mohammed-hassan-sayed-2015)

## Evidence

### Atyat Mohammed Hassan Sayed 2015

Atyat Mohammed Hassan Sayed, Magda Mohamed E. Youssef, Farouk El-Sayed Hassanein & Amal Ahmed Mobarak. (2015). Impact of Tactile Stimulation on Neurobehavioral Development of Premature Infants in Assiut City. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(8). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082720

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 25 stimulated versus 25 control premature infants at four Assiut NICUs. The study-group means after 5 days (e.g. 5.0+0.8 for animate auditory) exceeded control means (e.g. 3.8+0.5), and the text reports the between-group differences as "highly  statistically significant (t=0.000)".

> "After 5days, it was observed that the mean values o f the premature infants’ habituation and social interactive increased for both the study and contro l groups."

### Atyat Mohammed Hassan Sayed 2015 (2)

Atyat Mohammed Hassan Sayed, Magda Mohamed E. Youssef, Farouk El-Sayed Hassanein & Amal Ahmed Mobarak. (2015). Impact of Tactile Stimulation on Neurobehavioral Development of Premature Infants in Assiut City. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(8). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082720

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experiment at discharge: study-group means ranged from 8.1+0.8 (animate auditory) to 7.2+0.7 (inanimate visual) while the printed control means were 5.7+0.6 to 5+0.7, and the article reports "highly statistical significant differences found between the both groups (t= 0.001)".

> "For the control group, the me ans ranged from 5.7+0.6 for animate auditory to 5+0 .7 for animate visual. There were highly statistical significant differences found between the both groups (t= 0.001)."
