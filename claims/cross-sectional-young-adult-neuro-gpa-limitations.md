### Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung 2025

Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung. (2025). Impact of impulsivity on the relationship of the brain structures with school performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00344-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' stated limitations in the Discussion: the sample "consisted solely of young adults, which may limit the generalizability of the results to other age groups", and "the cross-sectional design of the study did not allow for causal inferences regarding the relationship between brain changes and GPA". Confounders such as nutritional status, socioeconomic status, and IQ were not accounted for due to lack of data.
