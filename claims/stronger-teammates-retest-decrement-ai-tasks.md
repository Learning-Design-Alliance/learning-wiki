# Stronger teammates showed a marginally significant absolute retest decrement for AI-learned tasks, while weaker teammates' decrement was not significant

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For the 11 stronger teammates, absolute retest performance was marginally significantly lower for AI-learned tasks (g = -0.28). [→ Gardella 2026](#gardella-2026)
`q3 i1` For the 11 weaker teammates, the lower mean retest performance for AI-learned tasks was not statistically significant (g = -0.20). [→ Gardella 2026 (2)](#gardella-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Gardella 2026

Gardella, N., Prather, J., Leinonen, J., Denny, P., Pettit, R., & Riggs, S. L. (2026). Fast and Forgettable: A Controlled Study of Novices' Performance, Learning, Workload, and Emotion in AI-Assisted and Human Pair Programming Paradigms. [doi:10.1145/3765964.3811664](https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811664)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup regression on the 11 participants identified as the stronger teammate in each human-human pair. The AI-condition coefficient was -3.40 points, marginally significant after adjustment (p_adj = .054), with printed effect size "-0.28".

> "Absolute Retest Performance for Stronger Teammates Only Constant 88.29 2.74 82.35 92.39 Session 1 Teammate = AI -3.40 1.57 -6.88 -0.40.027.054 -0.28"

### Gardella 2026 (2)

Gardella, N., Prather, J., Leinonen, J., Denny, P., Pettit, R., & Riggs, S. L. (2026). Fast and Forgettable: A Controlled Study of Novices' Performance, Learning, Workload, and Emotion in AI-Assisted and Human Pair Programming Paradigms. [doi:10.1145/3765964.3811664](https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811664)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup regression on the 11 weaker teammates. The AI-condition coefficient was -6.18 points, not statistically significant (p_adj = .606), with printed effect size "-0.20"; variability was high, with some retesting better on AI-learned tasks.

> "Absolute Retest Performance for Weaker Teammates Only Constant 55.01 10.42 31.87 77.38 Session 1 Teammate = AI -6.18 11.52 -30.46 18.96 .606 .606 -0.20"
