## Evidence

### Lee Hyunkyu 2015

Lee Hyunkyu, Boot Walter R., Baniqued Pauline L., Voss Michelle W., Prakash Ruchika Shaurya, Basak Chandramallika, Kramer Arthur F. (2015). The Relationship between Intelligence and Training Gains Is Moderated by Training Strategy. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0123259

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on the session-by-session correlation between fluid intelligence and within-session gain (pre vs post test block of each session). The article reports "ΔR2 = .101, ΔF (1, 26) = 3.80, b = -.034, t(26) = -1.950, p = .062", only marginally significant.

> "The centered interaction term using training regimen and session was added to the regression model and accounted for a marginally significant amount of variance in the correlation between fluid intelligence and training gain, ΔR2 = .101, ΔF (1, 26) = 3.80, b = -.034, t(26) = -1.950, p = .062"
