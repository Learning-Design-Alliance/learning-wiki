Hoot Reading — Evidence Summary 2025¶

Hoot Reading — Evidence Summary. (2025). https://www.hootreading.com

q4 · i1 · causal · r?

Student-level randomized controlled trial in Kansas City Public Schools during the 2024–25 school year, with 956 MTSS Tier III students among 1,384 total students in grades 1–4. Tier III students assigned to Hoot Reading "significantly outperformed their control peers on the i-Ready assessment (effect size = +0.08)".

"Among Tier III students, those assigned to Hoot Reading significantly outperformed their control peers on the i-Ready assessment (effect size = +0.08). These results qualify Hoot Reading-MTSS Tier III for a Strong rating."
