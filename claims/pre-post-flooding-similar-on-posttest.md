## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There is no significant difference between pre-presentation and post-presentation input flooding groups on the past tense post-test. [→ Rikhtegar 2015](#rikhtegar-2015)

## Evidence

### Rikhtegar 2015

Rikhtegar, O., & Gholami, J. (2015). The Effects of Pre- versus Post-Presentation Input Flooding via Reading on the Young Iranian EFL Learners' Acquisition of Simple Past Tense. English Language Teaching, 8(3), 80-88. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p80

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA pairwise comparison of the two experimental groups on the recognition post-test: mean difference "0.89" with p = 0.55, not significant. Equivalence was not tested; only the difference test was reported.

> "the estimated means difference of pre and post-presentation groups (0.89) is not significant. According to this finding, it may be concluded that there is not any significant difference between pre-presentation inputs flooding via reading vs. post-presentation input flooding via reading in terms of their effect on the acquisition of the past tense."
