Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2021). College during the pandemic: Immediate enrollment and retention of CPS Graduates in Fall 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/nsc-iccb-technical-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of NSC data for CPS classes 2016–20 (Figure 1 takeaways). The report estimates around 900 additional students would have enrolled in two-year colleges at 2019 rates; no statistical test is reported.

> "The decline in overall enrollment was mostly driven by a decline in the rate of immediate enrollment in two-year colleges, which decreased by 3.6 percentage points from 20.0 percent in 2019 to 16.4 percent in 2020."
