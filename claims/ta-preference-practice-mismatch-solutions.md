# TAs' self-reported preferences for solution features do not match the solutions they write: few provided overviews or justifications and none checked their answers

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2011

Shih-Yin Lin, Chandralekha Singh, William Mamudi, Charles Henderson, Edit Yerushalmi. (2011). TA-Designed vs. Research-Oriented Problem Solutions. 2011 PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3680043](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680043)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Examination of the 23 TA-written solutions collected at the start of the TA training course, before TAs saw the three example solutions. The authors report a discrepancy between self-reported preferences and actual practice: "only 3 out of 23 TAs provided some outline of the sub-problems" and only 6 provided principle justifications.

> "In total, on ly 3 out of 23 TAs provided some outline of the sub-problems (F3) either at the very beginning or along the solution progression, and only 6 of the 23 TAs provided any justification for the principles used (F5)."
