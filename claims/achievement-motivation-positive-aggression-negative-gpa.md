## Evidence

### Dong Minghua 2026

Dong Minghua, He Huitao, Wei Bowen. (2026). When digital engagement becomes dysregulated: associations with aggression, achievement motivation, and academic performance in adolescents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896602

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Final block of the cluster-adjusted hierarchical regression in 309 students. Achievement motivation (β = 0.397, p < 0.001) and total aggression (β = −0.122, p = 0.031) were both significant predictors of school-recorded GPA.

> "Within the same final model, achievement motivation showed the largest absolute standardized association with GPA (β = 0.397), followed by regulatory difficulty (β = −0.214) and total aggression (β = −0.122)."
