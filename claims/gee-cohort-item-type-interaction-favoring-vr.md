Ito T, Kawasaki H, Yamazaki A, Mizoguchi Y, Yamaguchi K, Kaneko E, Tsutsumi T. (2026). Metaverse‐based virtual reality and spatial learning in cervicofacial anatomy: A curriculum‐embedded comparative study in undergraduate medical students. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Population-averaged GEE (binomial logit) of item-level correctness with cohort, item type, their interaction, and anatomical region fixed effects, clustering on student with robust standard errors (Table S5). The article reports the interaction as significant without printing its coefficient in the main text.

> "Item‐level GEE analyses corroborated a significant cohort × item‐type interaction favoring VR on spatial items; additional model details and psychometric indices are reported in the Supplemental Methods and Tables S4–S7"
