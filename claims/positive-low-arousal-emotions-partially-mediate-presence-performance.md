## Evidence

### Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua 2025

Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua. (2025). Mediating role of online academic emotions between online presence and learning performance in blended learning environments. Scientific Reports 15, 29875. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-15947-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM mediation analysis (AMOS, Model 4 PROCESS, 5,000 bootstrap resamples) on the 971-student survey. Paths: presence→positive-low arousal emotions β = 0.728, presence→performance β = 0.586, emotions→performance β = 0.208, all p < 0.001; indirect effect CI (0.052, 0.161). Unstandardised β coefficients are printed, not standardised effect sizes.

> "online presence has a significant positive effect on positive-low arousal academic emotions (β = 0.728, p < 0.001) and learning performance (β = 0.586, p < 0.001). In addition, positive-low arousal academic emotions have a significant positive effect on learning performance (β = 0.208, p < 0.001)."
