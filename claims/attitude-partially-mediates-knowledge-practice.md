## Evidence

### Song Changli 2026

Song Changli, Zhang Wei, Zhu Lijuan, Ge Teng, Cao Min, and Xu Haihong. (2026). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices Regarding Enteral Nutrition Management Among ICU Nurses. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70260

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling of KAP survey data under the KAP theoretical framework, with reported moderate model fit (RMSEA = 0.073, SRMR = 0.057, TLI = 0.748, CFI = 0.762). The standardised path coefficients show attitude carries both "direct" and "indirect" effects linking knowledge to practice.

> "Direct effects were found between knowledge and attitude (β = 0.275, 95% CI: 0.165–0.384, p < 0.001), knowledge and practice (β = 0.198, 95% CI: 0.088–0.307, p < 0.001), as well as attitude and practice (β = 0.462, 95% CI: 0.372–0.553, p < 0.001). Knowledge also had an indirect effect on practice (β = 0.127, 95% CI: 0.072–0.182, p < 0.001)."
