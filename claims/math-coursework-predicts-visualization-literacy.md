`q3 i?` More prior math courses led to higher predicted performance, and the association did not differ between sample groups. [→ Brockbank 2025](#brockbank-2025)

## Evidence

### Brockbank 2025

Brockbank, E., Verma, A., Lloyd, H., Huey, H., Padilla, L., & Fan, J. E. (2025). Evaluating convergence between two data visualization literacy assessments. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-025-00622-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the combined sample (N=1,113) grouping participants by reported count of algebra, calculus, and statistics courses; accuracy rose from 65.1% with zero courses to 77.1% with three. The article reports the χ2 statistic, not an effect size.

> "the number of math courses an individual had taken was a reliable predictor of overall test performance (χ2(3) = 40.04, p < .0001), with more prior math courses leading to higher predicted performance (0: 65.1%, 1: 72.5%, 2: 75.9%, 3: 77.1%)"
