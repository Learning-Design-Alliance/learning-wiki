Gwynne 2022¶

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 84,793 students enrolled in grades 4-8 in both 2019-20 and 2020-21 in district-run CPS elementary schools, comparing spring 2020 and spring 2021 no-credit outcomes. The report finds students "more than twice as likely to earn at least one no-credit grade the following year" (34 percent vs. 13 percent).

"But students who earned at least one no-credit grade during the first term of remote learning (spring 2020) were more than twice as likely to earn at least one no-credit grade the following year, in spring 2021, compared to their peers who passed all their classes (34 percent vs. 13 percent; see Figure B)."
