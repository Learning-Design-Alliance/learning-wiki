The article's application section reports a case in which a blended Distance Education course used the SimChoice Distributed Learning System with classroom instruction, online collaborative learning, and demonstration and assessment stages. It concludes that "Peer assisted learning community is effective in promoting interpersonal interactions and informal learning." No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.
