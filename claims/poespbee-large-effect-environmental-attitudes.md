# POESPBEE produced significantly higher environmental attitudes than teacher-centered environmental education, with large effect sizes at post-test and follow-up

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Eighth-grade students receiving POESPBEE scored significantly higher on the ASTE than the control group at post-test and follow-up, with large effect sizes (η2=0.22 and η2=0.31). [→ Calis 2020](#calis-2020)

## Evidence

### Calis 2020

Calis, D. & Yildirim, H.I. (2020). The Effect of Prediction, Observation, Explanation Supported Project-Based Environmental Education on the Levels of Attitude and Behavior Toward the Environment. Educational Policy Analysis and Strategic Research, 15(1), 22-43. https://doi.org/10.29329/epasr.2020.236.2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing the experimental (n=34) and control (n=28) groups' ASTE scores after a ten-week quasi-experimental application. The article reports η2=0.22 at post-test and η2=0.31 at follow-up, both above 0.14, indicating a large effect size.

> "there was a significant difference between the groups’ post-test scores (t (60)= -4.05; p<.05) and follow-up test scores (t (60)= -5.25; p<.05) in favor of the experimental group."
