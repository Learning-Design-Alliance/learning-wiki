Evidence¶

Rina Oktaviyanthi 2015¶

Rina Oktaviyanthi, Yani Supriani. (2015). Utilizing Microsoft Mathematics in Teaching and Learning Calculus. IndoMS-JME, Volume 6, No. 1, January 2015, pp. 53-76. https://journal.unnes.ac.id/nju/index.php/ujme

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two randomly divided classes of first-year students (22 total) at Universitas Serang Raya; the article reports the experimental group's mean gain of 5.14 versus "which is 0.82" for controls, with no test statistic printed.

"In the experimental group can note that the average of the difference between pre test and post test is 5.14 that are significantly higher than in the control group, which is 0.82."
