## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Weinberg D. 2019

Weinberg D., Stevens G. W. J. M., Finkenauer C., Brunekreef B., Smit H. A., Wijga A. H. (2019). The pathways from parental and neighbourhood socioeconomic status to adolescent educational attainment: An examination of the role of cognitive ability, teacher assessment, and educational expectations. PLoS ONE 14(5): e0216803. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216803

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling (WLSMV, bootstrapped 2,000 times) on the PIAMA cohort with SES as a formative indicator of maternal and paternal education; the concurrent model printed "cognitive ability (30.0%), teacher assessment (28.5%), and educational expectations (1.2%)" of the total association, with excellent fit (CFI = 1, RMSEA < .01).

> "Finally, Model 2d showed that these associations remained significant once all hypothesised mediators were included in a model concurrently, with the combined mediational effects of cognitive ability (30.0%), teacher assessment (28.5%), and educational expectations (1.2%) explaining 59.8% of the total association between SES and educational attainment."
