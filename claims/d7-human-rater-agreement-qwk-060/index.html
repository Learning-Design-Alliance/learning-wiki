Kumar 2020¶

Descriptive analysis of D7's hand-graded rubric scores: Table 7 prints pairwise QWK among rubrics (Ideas–Organization 0.79 highest; Ideas–Conventions 0.46 lowest) and per-rubric averages (Ideas 0.62, Organization 0.73, Style 0.69, Conventions 0.60).
