`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cephalometric analysis outcome of a randomised prospective crossover study in which 61 dental students each completed a digital and an analogue case; the digital group's overall success rate was "55.74 ± 11.06 vs. 46.80 ± 11.69%" with p < 0.0001.

> "Similarly, group D reached a significantly higher overall success rate than did group A (55.74 ± 11.06 vs. 46.80 ± 11.69%; p < 0.0001; Fig. 5)."

### Lang Norbert Alexander 2025 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same crossover study's cephalometric deviation analysis: group D's mean deviation was "3.03 ± 1.97 mm" versus 4.12 ± 3.37 mm for group A, reported alongside the success-rate comparison for Table 5.

> "The results revealed that, compared with group A, group D yielded more precise results in analysing the LCRs, with a mean deviation of 3.03 ± 1.97 mm from the correct values, with a mean deviation of 4.12 ± 3.37 mm (Table 5)."
