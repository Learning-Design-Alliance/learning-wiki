### Lazowski 2015

Lazowski, R. A., & Hulleman, C. S. (2015). Motivation Interventions in Education: A Meta-Analytic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315617832

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Narrative background in the review's introduction: the review attributes the grade-level motivation decline to Lepper et al. (2005) and Jacobs et al. (2002), and cites Robbins et al. (2004) finding motivation predictive of college performance and persistence. These are second-hand reports of studies not read here.

> "Lepper, Corpus, and Iyengar (2005) demonstrated that both intrinsic and extrinsic motivation declined from 3rd to 8th grade, and Jacobs, Lanza, Osgood, Eccles, and Wigfield (2002) revealed that student's perceived competence in, and value for, math and English declined from 1st to 12th grade."
