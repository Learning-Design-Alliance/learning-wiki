### Wallin 1915

Wallin, J. E. W. (1915). Age Norms of Psycho-Motor Capacity. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://search.worldcat.org/search?q=Age+Norms+of+Psycho-Motor+Capacity

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative analysis of form-board records across ability groups among the over 4000 children tested. The author reports the full ordering of group means and adds that "of all types of children studied by the writer the epileptic respond the most slowly."

> "The bright children surpass the average children, the average surpass the dull or backward, the backward surpass the feeble-minded, the higher grades of feeble-minded surpass the lower grades, and the feeble-minded surpass the epileptic."
