## Evidence

### Jayson M. Nissen 2016

Jayson M. Nissen, Robert M. Talbot, Amreen Nasim Thompson, and Ben Van Dusen. (2016). A comparison of normalized gain and Cohen's d for analyzing gains on concept inventories. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.09180](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.09180)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of course-level effect sizes from the LASSO database of concept inventory scores found a large positive relationship between g and d, r = 0.75, p < 0.001, with shared variance r2 = 0.56, meaning the two metrics "shared approximately half of their variance in common" and can support divergent inferences.

> "Investigating the relationship betweeng and d indicated that there was a large positive re- lationship between the two measures and it was statistically reliable, r=0.75, p<0.001. This indicated that d and g shared approximately half of their variance in common ( r2=0.56)."
