`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

In the review's construct-targeting analysis, theory-informed studies showed a pluralistic focus: "intrinsic value beliefs (n=11) at a similar level to extrinsic value beliefs (n=9)", whereas non-theory studies targeted only intrinsic value beliefs.

> "Specifically, studies that used a motivation theory or model targeted intrinsic value beliefs (n=11) at a similar level to extrinsic value beliefs (n=9) and, to a lesser extent, competence and control beliefs (n=6). By contrast, studies that did not use a theory or model focused solely on intrinsic value beliefs (n=10) compared to extrinsic value beliefs (n=0) and competence and control beliefs (n=0)."
