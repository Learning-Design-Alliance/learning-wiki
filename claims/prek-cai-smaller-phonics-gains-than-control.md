## Evidence

### Trotti 2017

Trotti, J., Hendricks, R., & Bledsoe, C. (2017). Emergent Literacy Development and Computer Assisted Instruction. SRATE Journal, 26(1), 30-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134394

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment with 162 pre-K students randomly placed in Imagine Learning (n = 51), Waterford (n = 60), or control (n = 51) groups, 15 minutes daily CAI for four months. The article reports "F(2, 159) = 10.196, p < 0.001" with "eta squared = 0.114" considered moderate.

> "The ANOVA results reveal ed that instructional approach had a significant effect on phonics gain, F(2, 159) = 10.196, p < 0.001 Tukey's post hoc test indicated that the Control group mean of 7.8 was significantly higher than both the Imagine group mean (M = 3.1) and the Waterford group mean (M = 4.5)."
