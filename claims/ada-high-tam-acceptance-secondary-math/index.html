Evidence¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker 2025¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker, Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2025). Enhancing enthusiasm for STEM education with AI: Domain-specific chatbot as personalized learning assistant. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2025.100315

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive post-intervention analysis within the experimental group (n = 102) of the cluster-randomized study, using four TAM constructs on 4-point Likert scales. All four means exceeded 3.0, with "perceived ease of use (M = 3.44, SD=0.83)" the highest-rated dimension; no inferential test was reported for these descriptives.

"Students reported favorable assessments of ADA’s perceived ease of use (M = 3.44, SD=0.83), perceived usefulness (M 3.03, SD=0.81), attitudes toward use (M = 3.01, SD 0.85), and perceived competence (M 3.09, SD=0.83)."
