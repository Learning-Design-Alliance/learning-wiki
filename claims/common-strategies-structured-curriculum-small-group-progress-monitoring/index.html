Descriptive coding analysis of the 18 reviewed interventions reports that "8 of the 18 interventions are designed to be delivered in small-group format only, 6 are designed to be delivered individually only, and 4 can be delivered individually or in small groups", plus five with embedded progress monitoring tools and eight with discrete assessment tools.

"The study team found that of the 18 interventions examined, 12 incorporate a structured curriculum. These curricula are designed to cover essential reading components, such as comprehension, fluency, phonemic awareness, phonics, and vocabulary."
