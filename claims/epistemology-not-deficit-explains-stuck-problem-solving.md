Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2011). Beyond deficit-based models of learners' cognition: Interpreting engineering students' difficulties with sense-making in terms of fine-grained epistemological and conceptual dynamics. Submitted to Journal of Engineering Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.4288](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.4288)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Case-study analysis of a clinical interview in which Jim, an engineering major, solved a hydrostatic pressure problem. The authors' fine-grained transcript analysis argues his failure to reconcile math with common sense "stems in part from his epistemological views."

> "We argue that his lack of mathematical sense-making—specifically, translating and reconciling between mathematical and everyday/common-sense reasoning—stems in part from his epistemological views, i.e., his views about the nature of knowledge and learning."
