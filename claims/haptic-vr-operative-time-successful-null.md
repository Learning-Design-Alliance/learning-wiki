## Subclaims
`q3 i2` For participants with IDerror ≤ 15 mm, operative time did not differ significantly between VR (M = 758 s) and control (M = 886 s) groups, with a printed d = 0.420. [→ Duncombe Justin M.T. 2026](#duncombe-justin-mt-2026)

## Evidence

### Duncombe Justin M.T. 2026

Duncombe Justin M.T., Camaly de Brosses Pierre, Kassam Al-Amin M., Harris David J., Buckingham Gavin. (2026). Investigating Hip Arthroplasty Femur Preparation Training Using a Haptic-Enabled Virtual Reality Simulation. Surgical Innovation, 33(2), 169-180. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251383830

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Operative time was analysed only for successful insertions (IDerror ≤ 15 mm) in the randomized SawBone assessment. The t-test showed "no significant difference in operative time between the VR (M = 758 s, SD = 314) and control (M = 886 s, SD = 294) groups", d = 0.420, non-significant.

> "A subsequent t-test revealed no significant difference in operative time between the VR (M = 758 s, SD = 314) and control (M = 886 s, SD = 294) groups, t(7) = 0.626, P = .551, d = 0.420."
