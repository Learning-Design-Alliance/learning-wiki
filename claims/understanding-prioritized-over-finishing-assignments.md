`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the same five-student interview study, Alice's advice excerpt contrasts "understanding what you're doing" with finishing assignments and "getting the graphs". The authors classify this as prioritizing cognitive computational literacy over material computational literacy.

> "I would say that understanding what you're doing is more important than doing it all. . . . Understanding how something works, understanding why it works is going to benefit you a lot more later in the class and in your future,"
