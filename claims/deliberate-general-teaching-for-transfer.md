William H. Metzler. (1912). Problems in the Experimental Pedagogy of Geometry. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070947

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument on transfer: the author contends that experiments on transfer of accuracy are uninterpretable unless the standpoint and object of teaching accuracy are known, and that a habit taught across every subject "will go with the pupil all through life." No data are presented.

> "It is not sufficient to leave a power or habit to purely accidental acquirement. It must be taught as something in itself worthy of possession."
