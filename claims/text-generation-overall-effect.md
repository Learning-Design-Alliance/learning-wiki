## Evidence

### Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter 2023

Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter. (2023). Text Generation Benefits Learning: a Meta-Analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09758-w

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis of k = 129 effect sizes nested in 74 unique samples (N = 3,551 participants) from 20 experimental studies comparing text generation to reading control conditions. The article reports "a medium-sized positive overall text generation effect" with significant heterogeneity, Q(128) = 528.26, p < 0.001.

> "We found a medium-sized positive overall text generation effect, k = 129; g = 0.41, SE = 0.05, p < 0.001; 95% CI [0.31, 0.52]."
