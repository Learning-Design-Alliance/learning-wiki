## Subclaims
`q2 i3` AP physics students showed a statistically significant pre-to-post improvement in wave characteristics content performance after transverse and longitudinal wave simulation activities. [→ Espinoza 2020](#espinoza-2020)

## Evidence

### Espinoza 2020

Espinoza, F. (2020). Impact of Guided Inquiry with Simulations on Knowledge of Electricity and Wave Phenomena. European Scientific Journal, ESJ, 16 (33), 1. https://doi.org/10.19044/esj.2020.v16n33p1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Study II: 21 students completed a 10-question wave characteristics pre/post assessment after simulations on frequency, amplitude, wavelength, speed, and transverse versus longitudinal motion, including a fixed/loose-end pulse task. The paired t-test was significant, "t = 5.28, p <.001", with Cohen's d = 1.26 in Table 5.

> "for the Electric Fields it is t = 6.70, p <.001, and for the wave characteristics  t = 5.28, p <.001"
