### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Using Analogical Problem Solving with Different Scaffolding Supports to Learn about Friction. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06390)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Examination of intervention 1 students' first-attempt answers to the steeper-incline prediction and consistency-check sub-problems. Student reasoning fell into three categories: daily experience with correct interpretation, daily experience with incorrect interpretation, and answers based on the calculated result; the article reports the questions worked "in the way we had intended for some students, but n ot for all of them", while students still benefited overall from intervention 1.
