## Evidence

### Alhusayni AI 2026

Alhusayni AI. (2026). Association of physical activity and mental health symptoms to the academic performance of physical therapy students in Saudi Arabia: A cross-sectional study. Medicine 2026;105:1(e46934). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000046934

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable binary logistic regression with GPA as low (≤3.5–4) versus high (>3.5–4), adjusting for age, sex, BMI, smoking, living arrangement, and chronic disease. Table 3 reports PARS-3 OR = 0.993 (P = .87), GAD-7 OR = 1.116 (P = .27), PHQ-9 OR = 0.965 (P = .66).

> "The results did not indicate that PARS-3, GAD-7, and PHQ-9 scores were independent predictors of academic performance (Table 3)."
