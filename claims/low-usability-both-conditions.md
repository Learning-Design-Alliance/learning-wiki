# System usability was rated low in both conditions, with no significant difference between them

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

In Rief et al. (2026), seventh graders practised algebra in the "Math with Matt" tutoring system. The Mindful version added an LLM-based mindful chat, in which the pedagogical agent Matt gave context-sensitive emotional support, and mindful (not only evaluative) feedback and hint messages; the Cognitive version gave cognitive support only.

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The Mindful version received higher but not statistically significant SUS ratings than the Cognitive version (d=-0.48). [→ Rief 2026](#rief-2026)

## Evidence

### Rief 2026

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

System Usability Scale ratings in the 42-student experiment: Mindful M=55.56 (D grade) versus Cognitive M=43.96 (F grade) on the curved scale; the difference was not statistically significant, with printed d=-0.48.

> "Although the Mindful version received higher SUS ratings than the Cognitive version, the difference was not statistically significant (t(39.74)=-1.594,p=.119, d=-0.48)."
