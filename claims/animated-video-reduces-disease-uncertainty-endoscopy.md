## Evidence

### Qian Xiaoqing 2025

Qian Xiaoqing, Wu Lixin, Xu Shuguang. (2025). Impact of simulated animated video education on patients’ disease uncertainty, anxiety, and sleep quality in digestive endoscopy examination. BMC Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12876-025-03718-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort study of 166 patients divided into conventional education (n = 77) and animated video education (n = 89), assessed with the Mishel's Uncertainty in Illness Scale before, after, and one month after education. "disease uncertainty" was lower in the video group at both timepoints.

> "the simulated animated video education group exhibited greater reductions in disease uncertainty (post-education: 86.69 ± 2.65 vs. 85.65 ± 3.46, P = 0.031; follow-up: 82.57 ± 4.57 vs. 81.21 ± 3.69, P = 0.038)"
