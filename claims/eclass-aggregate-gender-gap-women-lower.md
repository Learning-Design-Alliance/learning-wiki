### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Research-based assessment of students’ beliefs about experimental physics: When is gender a factor?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Aggregate analysis of matched pre- and postinstruction E-CLASS responses from seven semesters of national data found the overall-score gap was statistically significant and represented a "moderate eﬀect size [38] with women scoring lower".

> "As shown in Table IV, there was a statis- tically signiﬁcant gap between men and women’s overall scores, and the magnitude of this gap represents a mod- erate eﬀect size [38] with women scoring lower."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Research-based assessment of students’ beliefs about experimental physics: When is gender a factor?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020130)
