# Only a minority of Dutch seventh graders read for leisure, with lower prevalence in the pre-vocational track (19.5%) than the pre-academic track (32.5%)

## Evidence

### Mol SE and Jolles J 2014

Mol SE and Jolles J (2014) Reading enjoyment amongst non-leisure readers can affect achievement in secondary school. Front. Psychol. 5:1214. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01214

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 1,071 Dutch seventh graders (548 boys, 523 girls) who selected activities from a list of ten; students checking reading were categorized as leisure readers. The article reports "a minority of only 19.5% of students in the lower and 32.5% in the higher educational track."

> "First, we found that a minority of only 19.5% of students in the lower and 32.5% in the higher educational track indicated that they read during their leisure time."
