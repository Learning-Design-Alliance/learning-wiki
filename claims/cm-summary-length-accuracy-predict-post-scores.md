## Evidence

### Rachel Sortino 2026

Rachel Sortino, Christina Kim, Thalia Guettler, Katie McClyman, Lorna Quandt, Alicia Wooten & Rachel Pizzie. (2026). Producing concept-motivated signs supports learning of STEM content in American Sign Language. npj Science of Learning 11:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00418-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

LMM of signed-summary duration on test scores in the within-participant study; for CM lessons the relation to post scores was significant, while for EM lessons duration was not related to pre- or post-test scores (all p's > 0.40).

> "However, the length of their explanation was signiﬁcantly related to their post scores (X2(1) = 11.02,p < 0.001). Longer summaries were related to higher post-test scores (β = 0.02, t(28.73) = 3.32)."

### Rachel Sortino 2026 (2)

Rachel Sortino, Christina Kim, Thalia Guettler, Katie McClyman, Lorna Quandt, Alicia Wooten & Rachel Pizzie. (2026). Producing concept-motivated signs supports learning of STEM content in American Sign Language. npj Science of Learning 11:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00418-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of rated accuracy of participants' signed explanations; for CM lessons accuracy related to both time points, whereas for EM lessons there was no relationship (all p's > 0.20). The authors read the pre-test link as reflecting prior-knowledge foundations.

> "However, for topics learned in the CM signing condition, summary score was related to both pre-test (X2(1) = 5.03,p =0 . 0 2 5 ;β = 0.99,t(45.71) = 2.25) and post-test scores (X2(1) = 18.03,p < 0.001; β = 2.10, t(54.15) = 4.25)."
