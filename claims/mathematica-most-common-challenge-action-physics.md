## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When asked what was most challenging about learning action physics, students most commonly chose Using Mathematica (58 or 62%), followed by calculus of variations and other mathematics (40 or 43%) and quantum mechanical concepts (39 or 42%). ### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016

Multi-select item on the 93-student end-of-module survey listed five non-exclusive challenge options; "the most commonly chosen was "Using Mathematica" (58 or 62%)", ahead of variations calculus and quantum mechanical concepts.

> "out of five non-exclusive options the most commonly chosen was "Using Mathematica" (58 or 62%). The next two most common were "Calculus of variations and other mathematics" (40 or 43%) and "Quantum mechanical concepts" (39 or 42%)."
