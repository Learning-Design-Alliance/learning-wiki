### Feucht 2017

Feucht, F. C., Lunn Brownlee, J., & Schraw, G. (2017). Moving beyond reflection: Reflexivity and epistemic cognition in teaching and teacher education. Educational Psychologist, 52(4), 234-241. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2017.1350180

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual argument summarizing Lunn, Ferguson and Ryan's article in the special issue. Calibration in teaching is defined as supporting teachers 'to increase awareness of beliefs and knowledge in general and the extent to which their teaching practices reflect those beliefs/knowledge' (Brownlee et al., 2011, p. 15). No empirical data are reported in this article.

> "This means that the internal dialogue associated with reflexivity (Archer, 2010), needs to include reference to both teaching practices and epistemic cognition as a process of calibration that leads to action in the classroom."
