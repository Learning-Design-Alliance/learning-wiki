## Evidence

### Janssen J 2025

Janssen J, Lackenbauer W, Gasselich S, Lickel ME, Schabel L, Beikircher R, Keip C, Wieser M, Mazuquin B, Yeowell G. (2025). Improving physiotherapists’ ability to recognise the presence of a serious pathology with a digital educational training: a mixed methods feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08101-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility outcome of a randomised mixed-methods feasibility study in which 30 of 39 registered Austrian physiotherapists completed the training. The "overall median FIM score was 5 (completely agree)", with only 'seems easy to use' rated lower at a median of 4.

> "The overall median FIM score was 5 (completely agree) (Table 2). The domains of the FIM: ‘seems implementable’, ‘seems possible’ and ‘seems doable’ received a median rating of 5"
