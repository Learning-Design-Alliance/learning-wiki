### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment silencing PBN CGRP neurons via Cre-dependent TetTox in CalcaCre/+ mice (n = 7 per footshock intensity cohort) versus eYFP controls across 0.1-0.5 mA: "TetTox mice displayed signi ﬁcantly higher avoidance success rates at 0.4 and 0.5 mA".

> "Consistent with these latency measures, TetTox mice displayed signi ﬁcantly higher avoidance success rates at 0.4 and 0.5 mA (Fig.4g), indicating that CGRP silencing expanded the range of aversive intensities over which active avoidance behaviour could be successfully learned and expressed."

### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026 (2)

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus two-way ANOVA on retention test 3 success rate (Fig. 4g) reported a significant CGRP manipulation × Footshock Intensity interaction, F(4,48) = 13.65, ***p < 0.001, with Bonferroni post-hoc group differences at 0.4 and 0.5 mA.

> "Two-way ANOVA: signi ﬁcant main effects of CGRP manipulation [F(1,12) = 7.24, *p < 0.05], Footshock Intensity [F(4,48) = 10.53, ***p < 0.001], and their interaction [ F(4,48) = 13.65, ***p < 0.001]."
