Evidence¶

K. Thiyagu 2012¶

K. Thiyagu. (2012). Effectiveness of Co-operative Learning Method in Learning of Mathematics among Eighth Standard Students. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 5 No. 4, February - April 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102329

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/post comparison in the same quasi-experiment (N=20 per group). Table 3 reports pre-test means 8.85 vs 8.53 (t = 0.33, Not Sig.) and post-test means 15.31 vs 18.46 (t = 3.83, Sig.) at the 5% level.

"It is inferred from Table 3 that there is no significant difference between Pre-test scores of control group and experimental group students. But there is significant difference between Post-test scores of control group students and experimental group students."
