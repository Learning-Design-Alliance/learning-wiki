## Evidence

### Loeb Daniel 2025

Loeb Daniel, Shoemaker Jamie, Ely Kelly, Zackoff Matthew. (2025). Deconstructing Participant Behaviors in Virtual Reality Simulation: Ethnographic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65886

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic analysis of the 15 recorded VR sepsis simulations found this dynamic reliance pattern: "participants shifted to a heavier reliance on the monitor readings" when examination findings did not change as expected. Qualitative finding; no effect size printed.

> "if the virtual patient’s perfusion did not improve after administration of a fluid bolus, participants shifted to a heavier reliance on the monitor readings, potentially discounting some qualitative physical examination cues in favor of objective data."
