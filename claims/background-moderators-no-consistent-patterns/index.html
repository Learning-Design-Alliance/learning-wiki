Emely Hoch 2023¶

Emely Hoch, Yael Sidi, Rakefet Ackerman, Vincent Hoogerheide, Katharina Scheiter. (2023). Comparing Mental Effort, Difficulty, and Confidence Appraisals in Problem-Solving: A Metacognitive Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09779-5

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Footnote in the experiments' method section reporting that exploratory moderator tests of self-perception and belief measures (e.g., need for cognition, test anxiety, growth mindset) produced "no consistent patterns" and were therefore not reported in detail.

"Background variables were also exploratively examined as possible moderators in all three experiments. However, since few significant results and, in particular, no consistent patterns emerged, the results are not reported for the sake of brevity."
