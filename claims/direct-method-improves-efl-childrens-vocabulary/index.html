Naeini and Shahrokhi 2016¶

Nazgol Nekoui Naeini, Mohsen Shahrokhi. (2016). Relationship between Gender and Vocabulary Teaching Methodology among Iranian EFL Children: A Comparison of TPR and Direct Method. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.1p.60

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test on the 20-student Direct Method group; Table 3 reports pretest M = 8.10 and posttest M = 11.10 (SD = 3.655 and 3.508). The article reports "statistically significant differences ( p < .05)" between pretest and posttest scores.

"The results of the t-test, illustrated in Table 4.4, reveal that there are statistically significant differences ( p < .05) between the two sets of scores in the direct method group."
