### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interviews with 16 students who had passed a general physics course covering ideal fluids, using three specially designed problem scenarios. "50% of the students considered that the ﬂuid made an accelerated movement", justified via energy conservation, upstream fluid-column weight, or Bernoulli's equation without the pressure term.

> "In the interviews, 50% of the students considered that the ﬂuid made an accelerated movement (E1.1). The ar- guments that explicitly justify this statement can be di- vided into three groups."
