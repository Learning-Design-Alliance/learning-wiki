`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical analysis in Section 4.3.1 of this conceptual paper. The article reasons that retrieval can fail, models can misapply retrieved content, and source material may contain ambiguities, so "Principle 2 (human final authority) and Principle 3 (bounded verification only) remain necessary safeguards even where RAG is implemented".

> "RAG therefore narrows the space in which errors occur, but it does not guarantee correctness."
