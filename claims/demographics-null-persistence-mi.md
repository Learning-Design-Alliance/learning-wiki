## Evidence

### Zwolak 2016

Zwolak, J. P., Dou, R., Williams, E. A., & Brewe, E. (2016). Students' network integration as a predictor of persistence in introductory physics courses. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Backward-elimination multiple logistic regression across six MI sections found "grade to be the only one that made a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence"; gender, ethnicity, and major showed no impact. The authors attribute this null to the atypical sample (46% female, 81% traditionally underrepresented ethnicities).

> "We also tested the impact of other factors, such as student demographics and ﬁnal grades, and found grade to be the only one that made a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence."
