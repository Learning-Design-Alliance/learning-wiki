The review's PRISMA 2020 flowchart: 539 records identified, 89 full texts evaluated with 50 excluded (potential bias 21, methodological limitations 12, eligibility non-compliance 17), and 39 studies included totaling 9927 participants, "sample sizes ranging from 7 to 3268".

"the last search was carried out in August 2025, closing with the identification of 539 studies; of these, 39 met the inclusion criteria. Risk-of-bias assessment of the 39 included studies used an expert-validated instrument scored on a vigesimal scale, yielding 22 low-risk, 17 moderate-risk, and 0 high-risk classifications. No study scored below 12 points was retained.

"A validated instrument was applied to assess risk of bias, classifying studies as low risk (n = 22), moderate (n = 17), and high risk (n = 0)."
