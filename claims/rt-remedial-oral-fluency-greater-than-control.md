## Evidence

### Chou 2013

Chou, C. (2013). A study on the effectiveness of applying "Readers' Theater" as English remedial instruction for underachievers. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 10(1), 77-103. http://english.moe.gov.tw/ct.asp?xItem=7773&ctNode=516&mp=1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent-groups study of 49 freshman English underachievers (25 experimental, 24 control) at a Taiwanese university; one-minute oral reading assessments (160-word passages) measured WCPA pre and post. The independent-samples post-test comparison favored the RT group, with the article reporting "the t -value is 6.28" at the .001 level.

> "it is clear from the independent t -test post-test results in Table 4 that the t -value is 6.28, which reached the .001 significance level and indicates that the oral fluency (M=126.40) of the experimental group students is significantly greater than that of students in the control group (M=103.25)."
