Sabourin, J. L., Shores, L. R., Mott, B. W., & Lester, J. C. (2013). Understanding and Predicting Student Self-Regulated Learning Strategies in Game-Based Learning Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 23, 94–114. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0004-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on Presence Questionnaire involvement and interest subscales across SRL groups. "High SRL students reported experiencing significantly more immersion and involvement than either Medium or Low SRL students".

> "ANOV As indicated significant differences in both of these metrics, ( F(2, 257) =4 . 5 9 ,p=0.01) and (F(2, 257) =6 . 4 4 ,p<0.01), respectively. Tukey post-hoc comparisons indicated that High SRL students reported experiencing significantly more immersion and involvement than either Medium or Low SRL students."
