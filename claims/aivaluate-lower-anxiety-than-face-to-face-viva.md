## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a counterbalanced within-subjects quasi-experiment with 35 pre-university students, students experienced significantly lower anxiety during AIvaluate sessions compared with traditional face-to-face assessments. [→ Habeeb Yusuf 2026](#habeeb-yusuf-2026)

## Evidence

### Habeeb Yusuf 2026

Habeeb Yusuf, Arthur Money, Damon Daylamani-Zad. (2026). Exploring student anxiety and experience in performance-based assessments using AIvaluate: an LLM-augmented emotionally intelligent pedagogical AI conversational agent. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10634-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Counterbalanced quasi-experimental within-subjects study in which each of 35 students completed both a face-to-face viva and an AIvaluate-mediated viva (Wizard of Oz technique), with real-time slider self-reports of anxiety analysed via weighted means and paired-sample t-tests. The article reports students "experienced significantly lower anxiety during AIvaluate sessions". No effect size is printed in the provided text.

> "Results indicated that students experienced significantly lower anxiety during AIvaluate sessions, and usability scores for the tool were rated in the “good” range."
