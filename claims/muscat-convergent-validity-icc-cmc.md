## Evidence

### Sakakida Chika 2021

Sakakida Chika, Tadaka Etsuko, Arimoto Azusa. (2021). Development and validation of a new Multidisciplinary Approach Competency Scale for Prevention of Child Abuse from Pregnancy (MUSCAT). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249623

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional survey assessing criterion-related validity against the ICC-CMC. The article reports "moderate significant correlations" with printed correlation coefficients of r = 0.552 (Factor 1), r = 0.478 (Factor 2), and r = 0.565 (total, p < 0.01). Note the article's own adequacy criterion for this validity check was a correlation ≥0.700.

> "We found moderate significant correlations between the two MUSCAT factors and the ICC-CMC: 0.552 for Factor 1, 0.478 for Factor 2, and 0.565 for the total scale (p < 0.01)."
