Evidence¶

Arsalidou 2016¶

Arsalidou, M. & Pascual-Leone, J. (2016). Constructivist developmental theory is needed in developmental neuroscience. npj Science of Learning 1, 16016. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.16

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing the study, that predictive regions were associated with the basal ganglia and thalamus rather than typical attentional-network prefrontal and parietal areas. No effect size is printed.

"A study of participants aged 6 to 20 years shows that (compared with cognitive measures closely tied to past learning) the indices of structural maturation and brain activity are better able to predict cognitive performance 2 years later"
