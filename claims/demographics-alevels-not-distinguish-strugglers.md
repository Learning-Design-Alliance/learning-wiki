## Evidence

### Yates Janet 2011

Yates Janet. (2011). Development of a 'toolkit' to identify medical students at risk of failure to thrive on the course: an exploratory retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-95

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison in the Results section (Table 2) of the retrospective case study. A-level data were available for 149 students, of whom 127 (85%) had at least two 'A' grades; "There was no suggestion that the non-completers were less well qualified, in terms of A-levels."

> "There were no major differences between the course-completers and non-completers. The proportion of males was slightly higher than in the entire intake (43% compared with 37%), as was the proportion of overseas students (15% compared to 10%), but with small numbers these differences were not statistically significant with Chi-square tests."
