### Biesma Regien 2019

Biesma Regien, Kennedy Mary-Claire, Pawlikowska Teresa, Brugha Ruairi, Conroy Ronan, Doyle Frank. (2019). Peer assessment to improve medical student's contributions to team-based projects: randomised controlled trial and qualitative follow-up. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1783-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative recommendation theme: focus group participants proposed a voting-style pool of extra marks so high performers could gain marks "that were not sacrificed by colleagues", which they expected would reduce tension and pressure.

> "Finally, teams also recommended alternative approaches of peer assessment, such as the implementation of additional or floating marks that were not sacrificed by colleagues."
