Evidence¶

Marín 2023¶

Marín, V. I., Buntins, K., Bedenlier, S., & Bond, M. (2023). Invisible borders in educational technology research? A comparative analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10195-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Corresponding-author analysis of the three country samples. The Spanish sample had "81.9% were Spanish corresponding authors"; UK articles had 69.5% UK corresponding authors and Germany 72.8%, per the same results section.

"Finally, the Spanish sample shows that 81.9% were Spanish corresponding authors; and therefore, the sample is the one out of the three with less international collaborations (e.g., with the UK, Chile and Colombia)."
