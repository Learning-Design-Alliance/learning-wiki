`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Findings on HMD use among the France-based learners provided low-cost VR spectacles in the DBR study. Learners reported discomfort and dizziness; Denis could not use one for physiological reasons and Eito found the setup distracting within the 30–40 minute lessons.

> "The impact of immersive capability on spatial presence was limited, as learners did not consistently use HMDs. Some, like Emma and Eiman, found HMDs to be uncomfortable and caused dizziness."
