# Enrolling in CPS pre-k before age four is associated with stronger kindergarten readiness in English language development and early reading for ELs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ELs enrolled in pre-k prior to age four showed stronger kindergarten readiness in English language development and early reading skills than those enrolling at age four. [→ de la Torre 2021](#de-la-torre-2021)

## Evidence

### de la Torre 2021

de la Torre, M., Freire, S., & Blanchard, A. (2021). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: An exploration of the influence of pre-k and early grade years. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/English-learners-in-Chicago-Public-Schools-an-exploration-of-the-influence-of-pre-K

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The report compares ELs enrolling in CPS pre-k "prior to age four vs. enrolling at age four" and reports earlier enrollment supported kindergarten readiness in English language development and early reading skills. No effect size is printed; the comparison is observational.

> "Enrolling earlier in CPS pre-k (prior to age four vs. enrolling at age four) supported ELs’ kindergarten readiness in terms of English language development and early reading skills."
