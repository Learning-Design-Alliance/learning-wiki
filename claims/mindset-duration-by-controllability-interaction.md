# Controllability and duration interact: acute stress is seen as more debilitating than chronic stress only when the stressor is uncontrollable

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jenkins Anna 2021

Jenkins Anna, Weeks Molly S., Hard Bridgette Martin. (2021). General and specific stress mindsets: Links with college student health and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256351

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Simple-effects tests following a significant multivariate interaction (Wilks' λ = .989, F(1, 481) = 5.47, p = .020, partial η2 = .011) in the 498-participant study. The interaction was driven by the acute uncontrollable situation (M = 1.39, SD = .80).

> "stress from acute stressors was perceived as more debilitating than stress from chronic stressors specifically when the stressor was also uncontrollable (Wilks' λ = .961, F(1, 481) = 19.33, p < .001, partial η2 = .039), but not when it was controllable"
