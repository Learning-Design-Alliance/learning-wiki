# A short visual-imagery strategy training improves paired-associate recognition performance across age groups, most strongly for recombined and new word pairs

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants who received strategy instruction recognized significantly more word pairs correctly than controls (η² = .13 overall). [→ von der Linden et al. 2015](#von-der-linden-et-al-2015)
`q3 i2` The training effect was significant for recombined word pairs (η² = .11) and new word pairs (η² = .04). [→ von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)](#von-der-linden-et-al-2015-2)

## Evidence

### von der Linden et al. 2015

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on overall recognition rates in the life-span experiment (N = 160, four age groups, random assignment to strategy instruction or control). Trained participants recognized more pairs (.80 vs. .72), a "main effect of the experimental condition" with η2 = .13.

> "a main effect of the experimental condition was found (F(1,152) = 21.82; p < .001; η2 = .13), indicating that those participants who had received the strategy instruction recognized significantly more word pairs correctly than participants who had not received such an instruction (.80 vs. .72, respectively)"

### von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Separate ANOVAs by word-pair type in the same experiment. The strategy group recognized more recombined pairs (.75 vs. .61, η2 = .11) and more new pairs (.94 vs. .88, η2 = .04).

> "For the recombined word pairs, only the main effect of strategy instruction reached the significance level (F(1,152) = 5.24; p < .001; η2 = .11), with subjects in the strategy instruction group recognizing more of the recombined word pairs correctly (.75) than subjects who  had not received the strategy instruction (.61)"
