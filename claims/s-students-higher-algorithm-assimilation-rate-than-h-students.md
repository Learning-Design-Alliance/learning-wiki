### Katai 2020

Katai, Z. (2020). Promoting computational thinking of both sciences- and humanities-oriented students: an instructional and motivational design perspective. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09766-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 48 undergraduates (25 S, 23 H) using software-registered errors and help requests during the black-box orchestration task. The article reports "95.65% of the S-students (group-Sa) assimilated the studied algorithm" versus 73.91% for H-students; no significance test is printed for this contrast.

> "Analyzing the on-line reports we concluded that 95.65% of the S-students (group-Sa) assimilated the studied algorithm. In the case of H-students this percentage was 73.91% (group-Ha)."
