### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Chi-square comparison of SCL-90 dimension positivity rates between male and female students (Fig. 1). The study reports "DEPR (14.4% for males vs. 17.0% for females, p = 0.039)" and higher male rates for PARI and PSY; the overall positive rate was 41.2%.

> "Significant differences in positive rates between male and female students are only found in three dimensions: DEPR (14.4% for males vs. 17.0% for females, p = 0.039), PARI (14.6% for males vs. 12.8% for females, p = 0.006), and PSY (11.8% for males vs. 9.0% for females, p < 0.001)."
