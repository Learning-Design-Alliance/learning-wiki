Evidence¶

Caldarella 2017¶

Caldarella, P., Larsen, R. A., Williams, L., Wills, H. P., Kamps, D., & Wehby, J. (2017). Effects of CW-FIT on teachers’ ratings of elementary school students at risk for emotional and behavioral disorders. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300717723353

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Robust Means Modeling analysis of the multi-site RCT (160 teachers, 350 students at risk for EBD, 19 schools in three states) comparing treatment and control post-test ratings. The article prints the effect size: Cohen's d = 0.20 to 0.27, which it calls small effect sizes favoring treatment.

"The RMM results showed statistically significant outcomes favoring the treatment group over the control group for (a) SSBS-2 Social Competence Total, (b) SSBS-2 Peer Relations, (c) SSIS Communication, (d) SSIS Cooperation, (e) SSIS Assertion, (f) SSIS Empathy, and (g) SSIS Engagement. The Cohen’s d for these measures ranged from 0.20 to 0.27"
