## Evidence

### Brod 2025

Brod, G., Holstein, E., Weindorf, L., Colantonio, J., Bonawitz, E. & Theobald, M. (2025). Do it yourself: discerning the effects of self-directed activity on conceptual learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00364-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

In the exploratory analysis of separate transfer tasks, active condition children "clearly out- performed their counterparts in the yoked condition in the receptive transfer task" (d = 0.40). Productive transfer (d = 0.24, p = 0.127) and explicit conceptual task (Cramer's V = 0.20, p = 0.079) did not reach significance; a repeated measures ANOVA showed no significant task × condition interaction.
