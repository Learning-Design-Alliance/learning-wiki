### Linton Debra L. 2014

Linton Debra L., Farmer Jan Keith, Peterson Ernie. (2014). Is Peer Interaction Necessary for Optimal Active Learning? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-10-0201

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same two-section quasi-experiment; multiple-choice percent scores compared by ANCOVA with key concepts pretest as covariate. "no significant differences between treatments on the multiple-choice parts of any of the four exams" (exam p values 0.89, 0.13, 0.14, 0.29). Equivalence was not tested. n = 102 cooperative, 98 individual.
