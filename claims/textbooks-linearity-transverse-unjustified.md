# Most textbooks do not justify the linearity of the Lorentz transformation, and many present invariance of transverse coordinates as obvious

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The majority of surveyed textbooks see no need to justify linearity; when justified it is derived from homogeneity of space and time or from Newton's first law. [→ Alfred Ziegler 2007](#alfred-ziegler-2007)
`q2 i?` Many texts present invariance of the transverse coordinates as obvious rather than demonstrating it, although it embodies the relativity principle. [→ Alfred Ziegler 2007 (2)](#alfred-ziegler-2007-2)
