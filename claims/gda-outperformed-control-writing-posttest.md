### Shabani 2018

Karim Shabani. (2018). Group Dynamic Assessment of L2 Learners' Writing Abilities. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 6(1), 129-149. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165517

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact groups of 22 freshmen each, scored with Jacobs et al.'s scale by two raters. The article reports the experimental group "outperformed the control group" with t (42) = -4.63, p = 0.002; no effect size is printed.

> "The results suggested that the experimental group (M=13.68, SD=4.11) outperformed the control group (M=9.04, SD=5.25); t (42) = -4.63, p = 0.002."
