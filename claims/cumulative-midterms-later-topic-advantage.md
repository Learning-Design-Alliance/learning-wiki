## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bailey Elizabeth G. 2021

Bailey Elizabeth G., Greenall Rebeka F., Tullis Madeleine M., Williams Kurt R. (2021). The retention benefits of cumulative versus non-cumulative midterms in introductory biology may depend on students’ reasoning skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed models with random intercepts for student and topic predicted shared midterm performance by topic (Table 3); the interaction coefficient was about 0.7, an advantage of less than 1% per later topic, and was not retained for final exam scores by topic.

> "the positive coefficient for the interaction in Table 3 suggested that students who had cumulative midterms did better than students who took non-cumulative midterms, but only on later topics."
