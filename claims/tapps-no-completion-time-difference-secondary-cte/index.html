Evidence¶

Pate and Miller 2011¶

Michael L. Pate, Greg Miller. (2011). Effects of Think–Aloud Pair Problem Solving on Secondary–Level Students’ Performance in Career and Technical Education Courses. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2011.01120

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent t test comparing successful students only: silent students averaged 12.7 min (SD 8.4), TAPPS students 16.5 min (SD 7.8). Levene's test showed equal variances were met (F(6,3) = .05; p = .82); the calculated Cohen's d (0.45) indicated a moderate treatment effect.

"Among students who successfully completed the troubleshooting task, there was no significant difference in mean time to completion between groups (t (9) = –.74, p = .48)."
