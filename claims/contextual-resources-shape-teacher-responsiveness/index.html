Evidence¶

Ilana Seidel Horn 2025¶

Ilana Seidel Horn, Yeliz Günal Aggül, Shuqin Li, Christine E. Hood. (2025). Teachers’ subjectivities in responsive instruction: when ambitious practice encounters challenging teaching situations. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09742-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Comparative case analysis of two teachers in similar sociocultural contexts with high commitment to responsive instruction, but with important differences in the relational agency made available to them, which shaped their epistemic agency and informed the theoretical model.

"Our analysis highlights how contexts differently offer resources for epistemic and relational agency, which, in turn, shapes teachers’ interpretations of students’ behavior, thus shifting their possibilities for responsiveness."
