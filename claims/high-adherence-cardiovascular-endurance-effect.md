## Evidence

### Yli-Piipari 2016

Yli-Piipari, S., Layne, T., McCollins, T., & Knox, T. (2016). The Impact of Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Middle School Students’ Health-Related Fitness: An Xbox One Kinetic Delivered 4-Week Randomized Controlled Trial. JTRM in Kinesiology, 12:1-18. http://www.health.gov/paguidelines/pdf/paguide.pdf

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of covariance comparing the high-adherence experimental subgroup (students attending at least 13 of 20 sessions) with the control group in the same 4-week randomized trial. The article reports a significant effect on cardiovascular endurance, "η2 = .30", a large effect by the printed η² guidelines.

> "The analysis for the high adherence group, reaching the 13 session benchmark, showed that intervention had a significant effect on students’ cardiovascular endurance (F[2,52] = 23.95, p < .001, η2 = .30)"
