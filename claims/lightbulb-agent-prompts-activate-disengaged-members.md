### Shuang Li 2026

Shuang Li, Haiyang Xin, Yimeng Sun, Qiannan Niu, Lingyun Huang, Gaowei Chen, Yibing Zhang, Ching Sing Chai. (2026). MIRACLE: Multi-Agent Intelligent Regulation to Advance Collaborative Learning Environment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.22318/cscl2026.255772](https://doi.org/10.22318/cscl2026.255772)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interview finding from the same six-group reflexive thematic analysis. Students said the "prompts were seen as warm encouragement" and that disengaged peers started cooperating after seeing the Lightbulb; prompts were personalized to their whiteboard work.

> "Most notably, the Lightbulb's visible interventions provided crucial socio-emotional scaffolding, fostering a sense of unity and activating disengaged members. Its prompts were seen as warm encouragement that helped students engage in teamwork: "It told me no need to be anxious and just take my time" (Student F), and motivated reluctant participants to rejoin the collaborative effort: "At first, some people in our group weren't doing anything. But when they saw the Lightbulb... they started cooperating with us" (Student G)."
