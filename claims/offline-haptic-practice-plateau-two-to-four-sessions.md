## Evidence

### Liu Luwei 2025

Liu Luwei, Qin Jinwei, Pan Yongchu, Wang Hua, Lu Xiaoqing, Chu Fengqing, Wang Lin, Hao Aimin, Yan Bin, Yuan Siyang. (2025). Evaluating a preclinical orthodontic training using haptic-enhanced VR simulation system: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07036-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures comparison across six weekly offline VSTES sessions (two-way ANOVA / Kruskal-Wallis with LSD post hoc). The article reports scores "have reached a plateau after 2 to 4 times of the practice" for 'rinsing time', 'cleaning', 'coating adhesive', 'etching time' and 'rinsing off acid etchant'.

> "a post hoc test (Least Significant Difference, LSD) was performed and suggested that students' scores for these items have reached a plateau after 2 to 4 times of the practice (P < 0.05, Table S1, see Additional file 1)"
