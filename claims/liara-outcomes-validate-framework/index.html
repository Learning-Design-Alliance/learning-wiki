Case-study description of the LI(A)RA project within LIA. The team, originating from the STI undergraduate course, achieved fourth place in the MAPC final, documented its solution in a book chapter, and built a self-instructional online course; the article reports the course "has completed four editions." No effect sizes or comparative evaluation are reported.

"The course continues to be offered by UFSC and, as of 2025, has completed four editions, extending the educational impact of the original competition project to a much broader audience."
