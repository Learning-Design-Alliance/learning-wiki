## Evidence

### Oliván Blázquez Bárbara 2019

Oliván Blázquez Bárbara, Masluk Barbara, Gascon Santiago, Fueyo Díaz Ricardo, Aguilar-Latorre Alejandra, Artola Magallón Isabel, Magallón Botaya Rosa. (2019). The use of flipped classroom as an active learning approach improves academic performance in social work: A randomized trial in a university. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214623

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported approximate study hours collected after the seven weeks of teaching in the cluster-randomized trial. With similar study time, the authors note the FC group "achieved a better grade"; they also flag that "social desirability has influenced student responses" as a possible bias. No effect size was printed.

> "With regards to the study time used for preparation for the exam, no statistically significant differences were found, since the students invested an average of 14.87 (SD: 10.26) and 15.11 (SD: 11.56) hours in the FC and LB groups respectively."
