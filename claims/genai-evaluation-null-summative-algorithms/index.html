Randomized A/B crossover study in a junior-level algorithms course; independent-samples t-tests after assumption checks. Midterm showed "a negligible effect size (Cohen's𝑑=− 0.08)" with CI [-4.8, 2.6], indicating no significant group difference.

Related Claims¶

  • Prior GenAI-solution evaluation showed no significant benefit on exam problems structurally aligned with the homework, with the midterm comparison inconclusive due to a floor effect — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Midterm self-efficacy (comfort and confidence) did not differ between conditions after three assignments, though both varied significantly across algorithmic topics — related
  • Students received significantly higher homework scores during GenAI-evaluation periods, but this advantage did not transfer to downstream exams — related
  • The instructor's own final exam showed the same direction of advantage on the intact randomized sample, with no difference on the pre-treatment midterm — reports the opposite
