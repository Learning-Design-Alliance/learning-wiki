## Evidence

### Middleton et al. 2011

James A. Middleton, Mona Toncheff, Susan Haag. (2011). Growth in Secondary Teachers' Content Knowledge and Practice in Discrete Mathematics. Proceedings of the 33rd Annual Meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://www.pmena.org/pmena-proceedings/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post quasi-experiment with a matched comparison group; 27 project teachers completed all 135 PD hours with complete data, and 40 comparison teachers received no discrete mathematics or modeling PD. The Discrete Math Content Measure (alpha = 0.70) showed "a significant difference between the 2 groups"; participant gains were significant while the comparison group's change was not (p = .24).

> "Results from the DMCM analysis shows a significant difference between the 2 groups ( p- value = .00) as post -test scores for the Participant ( M = 18.29) were higher than the comparison group (M = 11.95)"
