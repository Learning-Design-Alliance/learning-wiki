## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gaubert Sinead et al 2021

Gaubert Sinead et al. (2021). Positive effects of lumbar puncture simulation training for medical students in clinical practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02452-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post single-group evaluation of 115 fifth-year students at Université de Paris trained in groups of six on Kyoto Kagaku LP simulators. Self-assessed knowledge rose from 4.3 to 7.7 and confidence from 3.7 to 6.8 out of 10, "p < 0.0001" (Mann–Whitney test); no effect size printed.

> "Post-test self-assessments were significantly improved compared to pre-tests, for theoretical knowledge (mean score out of 10: 7.7 [SD 1.2] versus 4.3 [SD 1.9], respectively, p < 0.0001), and for confidence level in performing LP (mean score out of 10: 6.8 [SD 1.4] versus 3.7 [SD 2.5], respectively, p < 0.0001)"
