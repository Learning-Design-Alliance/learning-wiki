q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Stakeholder testimony gathered through the Consortium's consultation interviews and focus groups, reported in the school governance section. Principals said current LSC members were mostly former PTA activists, and non-LSC parents were seen as having fewer voice opportunities than under the PTA.

"However, many participants indicated that parents had actually become less involved since the reform act was implemented."
