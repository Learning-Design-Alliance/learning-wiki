q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effects model with hint type × course part interaction on log time to success (Figure 3, Table 11). Part 2 (New Content) covered technologies released after the GPT-4o cutoff; Part 3 (Old Content) was in training data. Single-Issue × Old Content β=−0.281.

"All hint types show improvement over the No Feedback baseline for both course parts, with Single-Issue Feedback statistically significant for Old Content . The Diagnostic Feedback performs significantly better for New Content , likely due to the fact that the model could correctly identify errors in the submission"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Advanced students showed reduced attempts with all hint types, while novices did not clearly benefit and made slightly more submissions with Single-Issue Feedback — related
  • Attempt-reduction patterns differed across course parts: Diagnostic and General Feedback were associated with fewer attempts for New Content, Single-Issue for Old Content — related
  • Model selection experiments show newer models are not strict improvements, with component-specific effects on quality metrics — related
  • All three LLM-generated feedback types are associated with faster time to solution than a compiler-errors-only baseline, with Single-Issue Feedback showing the largest reduction — related
  • All three LLM feedback types credibly reduced attempts to success relative to the baseline, with the largest reduction for Diagnostic Feedback — related
  • Feedback type did not affect whether students solved assignments; solution rates were very similar across all conditions — related
