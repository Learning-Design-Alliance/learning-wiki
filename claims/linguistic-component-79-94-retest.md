## Evidence

### Fahrutdinova 2014

Fahrutdinova, R. A., Yarmakeev, I. E., & Fakhrutdinov, R. R. (2014). The Formation of Students’ Foreign Language Communicative Competence during the Learning Process of the English Language through Interactive Learning Technologies (The Study on the Basis of Kazan Federal University). English Language Teaching, 7(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n12p36

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-year re-testing of the linguistic component, the component the authors call fundamental in communicative competence. Vocabulary tasks reached 79% and grammar 94% correct, up from 54% and 66% at the ascertaining stage; no effect size is printed.

> "As the analysis of re-testing at the end of the school  year showed, the most successful tasks were the ones on testing the linguistic component, namely, the knowledg e of vocabulary and gramma r (79%, 94%), whereas we observe the greatest increase of knowledge and skills in the experimental group."
