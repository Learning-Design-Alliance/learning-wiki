## Evidence

### Diego Ramírez 2023

Diego Ramírez, Eugenio J. Guzmán-Lavín, Javier Pulgar, Cristian Candia. (2023). Affinity-Based Groups in Secondary Education: Increased Stability at the Expense of Collaboration. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis (Models 7-8) of within-group physics-prestige in-degree at t1. The article reports that gender, average grade, and team friendship were the main predictors "regardless of the treatment condition." Females received less prestige recognition, and males "tend to enjoy such recognition."

> "For prestige (Models 7-8), gender negatively affects prestige (p < 0.05), while average grade and team friendship have a positive effect (p < 0.001 for average grade, p < 0.05 for team friendship). These models explain 28% and 29% of the variance."
