## Evidence

### Christopher A. Wolters 2025

Christopher A. Wolters, Anna C. Brady, Hyun Ji Lee. (2025). Time Management and Achievement Motivation: A Review of What We Know and Directions for Where to Go. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10032-4

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that cross-sectional studies using the MSLQ task value and time and study environment management subscales support a positive association (rs 0.42–0.44 in cited US college studies), and that "this association was supported by a large meta-analysis" reporting r = 0.47.

> "Most convincing, this association was supported by a large meta-analysis evaluating research that utilized the MSLQ (r = 0.47; Credé & Phillips, 2011)."

### Christopher A. Wolters 2025 (2)

Christopher A. Wolters, Anna C. Brady, Hyun Ji Lee. (2025). Time Management and Achievement Motivation: A Review of What We Know and Directions for Where to Go. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10032-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of situated expectancy-value theory research found no studies operationalizing expectancies strictly aligned with the theory alongside time management. Only one published study included discrete cost and time management indicators, finding a small end-of-term correlation (r = 0.14) and no cross-lagged prediction.

> "We found no studies that examined students' expectancies for success and their time management together."
