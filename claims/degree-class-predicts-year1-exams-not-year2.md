## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Degree class significantly predicted exam 1 marks (B = 0.41, p = .001) and exam 2 marks (B = 0.45, p < .001) in the pre-course models, but did not appear among significant predictors of the year 2 exams (Tables 7 and 8).

> "Although there was less variance in degree scores than in A-level scores, degree performance also predicted exam performance independently from A-levels on year 1 but not year 2 exams."
