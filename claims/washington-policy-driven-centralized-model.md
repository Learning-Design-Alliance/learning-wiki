### Harrington 2026

Harrington, H., Willis, J., Bishop, J. P., & Cazares-Minero, M. (2026). Highly mobile youth: How state policy and local implementation can work together to support youth. WestEd; Center for the Transformation of Schools, UCLA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681372

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Washington case study drawing on interviews with 9 leaders and a policy scan. A senior Treehouse leader describes how the legislatively mandated PEI workgroup organizes systems around shared educational conditions of foster, homeless, and incarcerated youth, targeting educational parity by 2027.

> "Project Education Impact organizes our systems around the fact that youth in foster care, youth experiencing homelessness, and youth who are incarcerated have similar educational conditions and outcomes."
