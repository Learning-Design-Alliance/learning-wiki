### Wang Ji-nuo 2026

Wang Ji-nuo, Lv Hanbing, Sun Yi, Cao Wen, Jiang Luhua. (2026). A workplace-based learning program on clerkship of internal medicine improves medical student's clinical competence: a retrospective comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08719-5

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Post-clerkship satisfaction questionnaire (7 domains, 5-point Likert) from 75 valid returns in the retrospective cohort: "Cohen's d = 0.73" for interest in learning and "Cohen's d = 0.49" for in-depth study; independent study p = 0.85 and self-confidence p = 0.060 were not significant.

> "scores for "interest in learning" (4.74 ± 0.65 vs. 4.00 ± 1.29, p = 0.047; Cohen's d = 0.73, 95% CI 0.27–1.19) and "in-depth study" (4.47 ± 0.89 vs. 3.97 ± 1.14, p = 0.037; Cohen's d = 0.49, 95% CI 0.04–0.94) were significantly elevated."
