Evidence¶

Kosmas 2025¶

Kosmas, P., Nisiforou, E. A., Kounnapi, E., Sophocleous, S., & Theophanous, G. (2025). Integrating artificial intelligence in literacy lessons for elementary classrooms: a co-design approach. Education Tech Research Dev (2025) 73:2589–2615. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10492-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative analysis of teacher focus groups (n = 25, coded per Saldaña 2009) after a six-hour co-design workshop. Teachers reported that "the co-design approach to developing AI-integrated educational material was significant", with 84% (21) citing interaction with colleagues and researchers, 88% (22) citing professional development, 60% (15) ownership, and 72% (18) material quality.

"the co-design approach to developing AI-integrated educational material was significant, especially regarding interaction with colleagues and researchers in the field (84% of teacher participants, a total of 21)"
