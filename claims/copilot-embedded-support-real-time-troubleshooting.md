`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Case observation reported at the March 2025 ribbon cutting: a student on only his second day with an industry-grade 3D creation tool used Copilot chat prompts for 'step-by-step instructions and links to additional tutorials.' The report offers this as an illustrative anecdote, not a measured comparison.
