## Evidence

### Prakash Shivesh 2017

Prakash Shivesh, Bihari Shailesh, Need Penelope, Sprick Cyle, Schuwirth Lambert. (2017). Immersive high fidelity simulation of critically ill patients to study cognitive errors: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0871-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Friedman tests on the five Ottawa GRS categories across four quarters of the intern year. The article prints "No significant trend was seen in situational awareness (x2 = 1.9, P = 0.6)" alongside significant improvements in the other named categories.

> "there was improvement in skills involving leadership (x2 = 28, P < 0.001), problem solving (x2 = 8.1, P = 0.04), resource utilization (x2 = 19.7, P < 0.001) and communication skills (x2 = 19.4, p < 0.001). No significant trend was seen in situational awareness (x2 = 1.9, P = 0.6)."
