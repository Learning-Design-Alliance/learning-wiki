Julián Méndez 2026¶

Julián Méndez, Lukas Gerlach, Tobias Wieland, Alex Ivliev, Markus Krötzsch, Raimund Dachselt. (2026). Evaluating a Visual Query Tracer and Builder for Learning Declarative Logic Programming. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18864 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.18864

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Preregistered qualitative study: 14 participants (3 female, 11 male) in semi-structured think-aloud interviews solved seven tasks on a faulty Datalog program. "All participants completed all the tasks within the time limit", and SEQ/UX assessments were very positive; no effect sizes are reported.

"All participants completed all the tasks within the time limit. Figure 3 shows the average TD scores, while Figure 4 shows the equivalent for UX."
