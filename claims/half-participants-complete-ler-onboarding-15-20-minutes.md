### onboarding-time-1

Hodge, M., Shah, Z., Patterson, M., Luke Luna, C. (2023, September). Onboarding and Education Design Principles for Learning and Employment Records. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/194

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational findings from two live, in-person co-design workshops (Denver and Little Rock) in which participants onboarded to LearnCard or SmartResume while notetakers transcribed reactions. The report states "roughly half of the participants were able to complete in that time," attributing variation to information-sharing choices and technical issues.

> "We allotted 15–20 minutes to move through the onboarding experience, and roughly half of the participants were able to complete in that time."
