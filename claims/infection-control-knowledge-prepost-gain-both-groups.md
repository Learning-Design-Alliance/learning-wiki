`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post analysis of the 10-item infection control written test across the pooled phase 1 sample (N=107) in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports the combined means and "t106=–7.08; P<.001" for the pre-to-post change.

> "The combined results of all participants showed an average pretest knowledge score of 7.58 (SD 1.13) and a posttest knowledge score of 8.58 (SD 1.16), indicating improved knowledge after course completion (t106=–7.08; P<.001)."
