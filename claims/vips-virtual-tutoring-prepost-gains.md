Shayan Majidy. (2024). Addressing misconceptions in university physics: A review and experiences from quantum physics educators. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Evaluation of the Virtual Physics System (ViPS), which combines pulley simulations with tutoring using pre-test, tutoring, and post-test phases with a dynamic student model. Results concern the combined simulation-and-tutoring activity, not the simulation medium alone.

> "The virtual-only group improved from pretest to posttest. In the sequence comparison, students beginning with ViPS had a larger initial gain than those beginning with physical pulleys, but the final gains did not differ significantly between sequences."
