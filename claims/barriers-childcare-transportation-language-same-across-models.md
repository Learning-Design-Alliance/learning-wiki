## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The top three barriers were the same for current prenatal care and CP (child care 44.3% and 42.0%; transportation 41.4% and 39.1%; language issues 29.9% and 28.1%), yet adopters and nonadopters differed significantly on language issues when considering a switch to CP (χ2 = 6.343, df = 2, P = 0.042). ## Evidence

### Xaverius Pamela K. 2014

Xaverius Pamela K., Grady Mary Alice. (2014). Centering Pregnancy in Missouri: A System Level Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/285386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 223 respondents rating items as strengths, barriers, or uncertain for current and CP care (Table 5). The article reports the top-three barrier percentages for both models and the adopter/nonadopter language-issues difference (χ2 = 6.343, df = 2, P = 0.042), with 29.5% of adopters versus 51.9% of nonadopters seeing language issues as a strength; no effect size is printed.

> "While 29.5% of adopters saw language issues as a strength, 51.9% of the nonadopters saw language issues as a strength."
