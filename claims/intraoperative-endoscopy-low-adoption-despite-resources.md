Felsenreich DM, Batista Dantas AC, Shahabi S, Abdelbaki TN, Zakeri R, Nagy ME, Gero D, Ruiz-Úcar E, Dong Z-Y, Yang W, Jenkins M, Chiappetta S, on behalf of Young-IFSO Collaborative Group. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported practice data from the cross-sectional survey of 273 early-career MBS surgeons. The contrast between resource availability and IOE use is reported descriptively with no inferential test.

> "Most notably, despite most of them (71.3%) having an endoscopy tower available in the operating room (OR), only 23.1% routinely perform intraoperative endoscopy (IOE) (e.g., leak test, evaluation of staple line bleeding) at the end of MBS."
