`q2 i?` Generalizability analysis demonstrated that both questionnaires produced reliable scores with G-coefficients of 0.926 and 0.928, exceeding the recommended standard. [→ Esteves 2021](#esteves-2021)

## Evidence

### Esteves 2021

Esteves, A., McConnell, M., Ferretti, E., Garber, A., & Fung-Kee-Fung, K. (2021). "When in Doubt, Ask the Patient": A Quantitative, Patient-Oriented Approach to Formative Assessment of CanMEDS Roles. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11169

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A generalizability theory analysis of 69 patient questionnaires and 70 fellow questionnaires across 70 unique encounters with nine fellows found that the patient and self-questionnaires had "G-coefficient = 0.926 and 0.928, respectively", exceeding the recommended standard.

> "The variance components were then used to determine the overall reliability of both the patient and self-questionnaires (G-coefficient = 0.926 and 0.928, respectively), which exceeded the recommended standard"
