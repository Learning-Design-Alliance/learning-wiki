## Evidence

### Bjork 2016

Bjork, R. A., & Yue, C. L. (2016). Commentary: Is disfluency desirable? Metacognition Learning, 11, 133–137. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9156-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Lehmann, Goussious, and Seufert had participants read a passage in fluent or disfluent font, measuring working memory capacity plus retention, comprehension, and transfer (as summarized in this commentary). Disfluency benefits emerged only for high-capacity learners.

> "Participants did benefit from disfluency on the retention and comprehension tests, but only if they had high working memory capacity."
