Feedback survey of the 28 game-group participants using nine 1–5 Likert items, validated by four experts and piloted with 15 students; overall “Cronbach’s alpha was 0.81”. Results were analysed categorically with maximum-response percentages, including “almost 92% of the students felt that the game encouraged them to communicate their ideas more clearly”.

> "All the students (100%) unanimously agreed that the “Diabe-teach” game significantly boosted their confidence in their ability to discuss complex concepts and enhanced their ability to correlate different pieces of information"
