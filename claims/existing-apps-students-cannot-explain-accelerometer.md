## Evidence

### Colleen L. Countryman and M. A. Paesler 2016

Colleen L. Countryman and M. A. Paesler. (2016). Enhancing Students’ Understanding of Scientific Equipment: Smartphones in the Laboratory. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.03736](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.03736)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary study of laboratory sections using smartphones in lieu of conventional equipment, with no direct instruction on the accelerometer's inner workings. A small sample of n = 10 students gave explanations that "lack detail or physical explanations," e.g. invoking GPS, wifi, or Bluetooth, or "Magic."

> "When students used some existing free accelerometer apps13,14 with a traditional curriculum adapted minimally for the inclusion of this technology, students were not able to describe the inner-workings of the accelerometer."
