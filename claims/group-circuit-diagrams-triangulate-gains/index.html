Evidence¶

Ioannou 2025¶

Ioannou, A., Miliou, O., Georgiou, Y., Timotheou, S., Barkhuus, L., & Rode, J. (2025). Learning design for short-duration e-textile workshops: outcomes on knowledge and skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 443–463. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10411-8

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative evaluation of group circuit diagrams from 11 pairs, with post-test diagrams drawn without facilitator input; three of the seven consistently-correct pairs had benefited from facilitators' scaffolding before the test. Findings triangulated the individual exercise results.

"three pairs improved their circuit design, from an incorrect drawing at pre-test to a correct one at post; one pair designed a flawed circuit in the post-test (conductive thread crossing, therefore making the circuit non-functional); and seven pairs drew a correct circuit both before and after."
