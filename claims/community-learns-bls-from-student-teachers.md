## Evidence

### Veloso Sérgio Geraldo 2019

Veloso Sérgio Geraldo, Pereira Gabriel Santos, Vasconcelos Nathália Nascimento, Senger Maria Helena, de Faria Rosa Malena Delbone. (2019). Learning by teaching basic life support: a non-randomized controlled trial with medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1500-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Community assessment results: 155 of 240 participants completed both theoretical tests, improving from 7.9 ± 3.6 to 13.3 ± 3.2 (p < 0.001); the practical assessment mean was 11.7 ± 3.2. Community members taught by case versus control students did not differ (p = 0.113 theoretical, p = 0.833 practical).

> "Of the 240 people in the community, 155 performed both the theoretical pre- and post-tests, with a mean of 7.9 ± 3.6 and 13.3 ± 3.2(maximum = 20), respectively (p < 0.001). There was no difference in the theoretical performance of the community that was taught by the case or control group (p = 0.113)."
