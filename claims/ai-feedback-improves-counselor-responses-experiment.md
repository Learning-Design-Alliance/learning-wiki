## Evidence

### Anqi Li 2026

Anqi Li, Ruihan Wang, Zhaoming Chen, Yuqian Chen, Yu Lu, Yi Zhu, Yuan Xie, Zhenzhong Lan. (2026). Multi-dimensional Assessment and Explainable Feedback for Counselor Responses to Client Resistance in Text-based Counseling with LLMs. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.21638](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.21638)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed between-within experiment with 43 novice counselors (control N=21, experimental N=22) responding to 10 resistance utterances pre and post; Linear Mixed-Effects Models analyzed repeated measures. The article reports a "statistically significant Condition×Phase interaction" across all mechanisms.

> "the analysis reveals a statistically significant Condition×Phase interaction across all communication mechanisms. While both groups performed comparably at baseline, the interaction term confirms that counselors in the experimental group achieved substantially greater gains in the post-test phase than those in the control group."
