## Evidence

### Gupta P 2025

Gupta P, Sheth N, AlAhmadi R, Yao X, Heiferman MJ. (2025). The Effect of Experience on Visual Search Patterns in Retinal Imaging Analysis. Ophthalmic Surg Lasers Imaging Retina. https://doi.org/10.3928/23258160-20250228-03

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group eye-tracking analysis of image coverage in the same five-group observational study. The ANOVA found attendings "significantly higher than PGY2s' F(4,20) = 55.06, P < 0.0001"; regression gave r(3) = 0.95, P = 0.014. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Attendings fixated on the greatest percentage of the image area, with an average of 49.43% ± 7.34%, significantly higher than PGY2s' F(4,20) = 55.06, P < 0.0001 (Figure 2K). The linear regression analysis (Figure 2L) portrayed a significant positive association between experience level and the percentage of image area fixated upon r(3) = 0.95, P = 0.014."
