ASPECT: A Survey to Assess Student Perspective of Engagement in an Active-Learning Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0244

Linear mixed-effects model results for the instructor-contribution construct from the same repeated-measures comparison of short- and long-activity days, reported in Table 3 with white students and the short activity as reference levels.

> "on average, students perceive less IC on the long activity compared with the short activity (Table 3g-i), Asian-American students perceive more IC than white students (Table 3, h and i), and international students perceive more IC on the long activity than white students (Table 3i)."
