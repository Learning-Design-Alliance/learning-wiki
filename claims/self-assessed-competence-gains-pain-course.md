## Evidence

### Kurz Sandra 2021

Kurz Sandra, Lohse Jana, Buggenhagen Holger, Schmidtmann Irene, Laufenberg-Feldmann Rita, Engelhard Kristin. (2021). Improving competence and safety in pain medicine: a practical clinical teaching strategy for students combining simulation and bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02554-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment on a five-point Likert scale (1 = very certain, 5 = very uncertain) before and after the course; Table 2 prints improvements on all five skills, each with p < 0.05, e.g. responsibility 2.8 to 1.8 and expert knowledge 3.2 to 2.0. No effect sizes are printed.

> "After the training, all students felt more competent regarding all topics."
