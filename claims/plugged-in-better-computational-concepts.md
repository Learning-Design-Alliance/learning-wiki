## Evidence

### Sigayret 2022

Sigayret, K., Tricot, A., & Blanc, N. (2022). Unplugged or plugged-in programming learning: A comparative experimental study. Computers and Education, 184, 104505. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104505

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 217 grade-5 students in classes randomly assigned to conditions; one-way ANOVA on the Mastery of computational concepts Test showed "significant in terms of total test performance (F 1, 202) = 31.86; p < .001; d = 0.78".

> "The differences observed between the two groups were significant in terms of total test performance (F 1, 202) = 31.86; p < .001; d = 0.78). For items measuring the mastery of the concept of algorithm, the differences were not significant."
