## Evidence

### Johnmarshall Reeve 2025

Johnmarshall Reeve, Geetanjali Basarkod, Hye-Ryen Jang, Rafael Gargurevich, Hyungshim Jang, Sung Hyeon Cheon. (2025). Specialized Purpose of Each Type of Student Engagement: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09989-z

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The well-being meta-analysis included only 16 effect sizes from 4 studies. Engagement type significantly moderated the relation, χ2(3) = 8.97, p = 0.030; emotional engagement correlated at M Fisher's z = 0.84 versus behavioral z = 0.60, cognitive z = 0.55, agentic z = 0.53. The article reports "This result confirms H4." No standardized effect size was printed for the pairwise differences.

> "the predictive power of emotional engagement was significantly greater than it was for agentic engagement (p = 0.002), cognitive engagement (p = 0.003), and behavioral engagement (p = 0.016). This result confirms H4."
