# Writing explanations required more mental effort than retrieving the material

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students who wrote an explanation in either the standard written or chat condition invested more mental effort than students who retrieved the material. [→ Jacob Leonie 2021](#jacob-leonie-2021)

## Evidence

### Jacob Leonie 2021

Jacob Leonie, Lachner Andreas, Scheiter Katharina. (2021). Does increasing social presence enhance the effectiveness of writing explanations? PLoS ONE 16(4): e0250406. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250406

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

ANOVA on perceived mental effort (9-point scale) in the four-condition experiment; the article reports ηp2=.11 and Scheffé post-hocs showing both written conditions exceeded retrieval (standard written p = .006; chat p = .008).

> "Results showed a significant difference among conditions, F(3, 133) = 5.63, p = .001, ηp2=.11. Post-hoc comparisons (Scheffé) revealed that students who wrote an explanation investigated more mental effort than students who retrieved the material"
