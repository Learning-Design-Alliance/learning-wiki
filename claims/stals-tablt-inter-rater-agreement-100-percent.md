### Liang Yao 2024

Liang Yao, Huang Hua, Tan Yu-Bao, Li Tang, Huang Wan, Zhang Qing-Long, Liu Zhuo-Wei, Kuang Ming. (2024). Construction and implementation of a laparoscopic skill training course based on a smartphone application and virtual reality. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06066-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Agreement analysis of pass-or-fail judgments by two senior surgeons, each with over 10 years of laparoscopic experience, assessing the 91 trainees. The article reports "The inter-rater agreement (pass or fail) between the two senior surgeons reached 100%".

> "The inter-rater agreement (pass or fail) between the two senior surgeons reached 100%, which is unsurprising given that the TABLT's scoring parameters are binary, utilizing 'YES' or 'NO' for each error."
