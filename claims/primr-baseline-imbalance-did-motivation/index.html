Stephanie Simmons Zuilkowski & Benjamin Piper 2014¶

Stephanie Simmons Zuilkowski & Benjamin Piper. (2014). Improving Reading Outcomes in Kenya: First-Year Effects of the PRIMR Initiative. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562806

q3 · i? · causal · r2

January 2012 baseline analysis of 2335 pupils in 117 schools designed to test for pretreatment differences between randomly assigned groups. The article reports the printed differences and p-values and calls them "small but statistically significant differences in outcomes."

"our analyses showed that treatment schools outperformed control schools by 2.7 WCPM in English reading (p-value .04), 1.7 WCPM in Kiswahili reading (p-value .04), 2.7% in English comprehension (p-value .08), and 5.4% in Kiswahili comprehension (p-value .07)"
