## Evidence

### Tosif Shidan 2020

Tosif Shidan, Jatobatu Anna, Maepioh Anita, Gray Amy, Sobel Howard, Mannava Priya, Duke Trevor. (2020). Healthcare worker knowledge and skills following coaching in WHO early essential newborn care program in the Solomon Islands: a prospective multi-site cohort study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-020-2739-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational subgroup comparison of post-coaching to evaluation score differences by cadre (23 nurses, 15 doctors, 8 midwives, 7 nurse aides) using the Kruskal-Wallis test. The article reports nurse aides' "median score reduction of − 2%" versus doctors' − 18%, p = 0.237; Non-Breathing Baby differences p = 0.440.

> "In the breathing baby scenario, nurse aides had a median score reduction of − 2% (IQR − 18 to 2), midwives − 10% (IQR − 16 to − 1), nurses − 11% (IQR − 43 to 0) doctors − 18% (IQR − 31 to − 4) (p = 0.237)."
