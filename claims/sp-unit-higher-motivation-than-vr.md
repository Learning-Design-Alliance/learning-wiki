# Students reported significantly higher motivation during the standardized patient unit than during the VR unit

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Interest/enjoyment motivation ratings were significantly higher for the conventional unit than the VR unit (η2p = 0.44), with no speaker-observer difference. [→ Graf Linda 2026](#graf-linda-2026)

## Evidence

### Graf Linda 2026

Graf Linda, Golz Vera, Prehn Sophia, Masuch Maic, Gradl-Dietsch Gertraud. (2026). Virtual vs. standardized patients - a preliminary comparison of advantages and disadvantages of a VR and a conventional learning unit for medical students in child and adolescent psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08760-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on the IMI interest/enjoyment subscale in the same within-subjects questionnaire study of 50 students. Figure 4 showed "a higher motivation during the conventional unit, than the VR unit"; no interaction or role effect was found.

> "Regarding the motivation during the learning units, ratings significantly differ between the two units (F (1, 43) = 33.7, p < .001, η2p = 0.44), but there was no interaction effect"
