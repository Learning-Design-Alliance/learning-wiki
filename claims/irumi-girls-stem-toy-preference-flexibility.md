`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Heterogeneous-effects estimates by gender (Eq. 2, Table 6) from the cluster RCT. The article reports a printed effect size of 0.152 SD for girls' STEM Toy Preference Index and a printed effect size of 0.196 SD for the STEM Toy Gender Flexibility Index ("Irûmi increased the STEM Toy Gender Flexibility Index by 0.196 SD (0.009 SD + 0.187 SD)").

> "For girls, the impact was both positive and statistically significant, with Irûmi increasing the STEM Toy Preference Index by 0.152 SD (0.022 SD + 0.130 SD)."
