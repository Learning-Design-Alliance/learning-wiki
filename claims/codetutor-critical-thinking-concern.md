# Students rated CodeTutor highly for comprehension and syntax support but significantly lower for fostering critical thinking

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Agreement levels differed significantly across the five attitude questions (χ² = 32.99, p < 0.05), with critical thinking rated significantly lower than comprehension, syntax help, and TA replacement. [→ Wenhan Lyu 2024](#wenhan-lyu-2024)

## Evidence

### Wenhan Lyu 2024

Wenhan Lyu, Yimeng Wang, Tingting (Rachel) Chung, Yifan Sun, and Yixuan Zhang. 2024. Evaluating the Effectiveness of LLMs in Introductory Computer Science Education: A Semester-Long Field Study. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3662036

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test across five attitude questions (χ² = 32.99, p < 0.05) with Dunn post-hoc tests on 48 respondents' ratings. Descriptively, 35% disagreed CodeTutor helped them think critically while 19% agreed.

> "Our post-hoc tests (see Figure 6) further reveal that students were significantly less in agreement about CodeTutor's assistance in fostering critical thinking compared to its ability to understand, help in learning syntax and serving as a replacement for a teaching assistant."
