## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of problem-solving performance across the two pre-tests in the 61 participating second-year physiotherapy students, confirming the a priori categorization into novice and advanced learners based on the first pre-test and clinical experience.

> "mean performance in the advanced learners group (Mean ± SD: 70.7 ± 14.8%) was higher (t (60) = 8.838, p < .001, two-tailed, Cohen's d = 2.281: large effect size) than in the novice learners group (44.7 ± 11.2%)"
