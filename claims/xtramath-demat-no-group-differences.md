## Evidence

### Jaekel Julia 2020

Jaekel Julia, Heuser Katharina M., Zapf Antonia, Roll Claudia, Nuñez Francisco Brevis, Bartmann Peter, Wolke Dieter, Feldhoff-Mueser Ursula, Huening Britta. (2020). Preterm children’s long-term academic performance after adaptive computerized training: an efficacy and process analysis of a randomized controlled trial. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-020-01114-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis of DEMAT standardized math tests in the same RCT, using regressions with baseline DEMAT as covariate. The article reports "no relevant group differences in math test score growth" with B = 0.05, p = 0.697 post test and B = 0.16, p = 0.346 at follow-up; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "There were no relevant group differences in math test score growth according to training condition (baseline DEMAT entered as covariate) post test: B = 0.05 [−0.20 to 0.29], p = 0.697; 12-month follow-up: B = 0.16 [−0.13 to 0.49], p = 0.346)"
