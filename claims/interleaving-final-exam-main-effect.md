## Evidence

### Steve Bennoun 2026

Steve Bennoun, Veronica X. Yan, & Alice Xu. (2026). Mixing problems increases performance of all students but especially of low-achieving ones in college calculus. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00450-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of final exam z-scores (Model 1) restricted to second-half topics, with midterm score added as a direct prior-knowledge measure. The article reports β = 0.21, 95% CI = [0.11, 0.31], t(553) = 4.12, p < 0.001, and states "interleaving was bene ﬁcial for all students".

> "interleaving was bene ﬁcial for all students resulting in a signiﬁcant coefﬁcient for the main effect of Homework Type (β = 0.21, 95% CI = [0.11, 0.31], t(553) = 4.12, p < 0.001)."
