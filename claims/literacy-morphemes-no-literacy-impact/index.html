Worth 2015¶

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

q3 · i0 · causal · r?

Primary outcome analysis of the same cluster-randomised trial, measured on the Progress in English 7 (short form) test. The report states the literacy programme had "no significant impact on the primary literacy outcome"; the printed effect size of -0.05 is negligible. Equivalence was not tested.

"In contrast, the literacy programme had no significant impact on the primary literacy outcome, with an effect size of -0.05 (-0.18–0.08)."
