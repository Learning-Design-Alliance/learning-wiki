`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of the no feedback conditions (Table 4), using revised measures with reduced construct overlap. The article reports the null associations for self-efficacy and threat, and that challenge-effort associations held in Experiment 3 but not Experiment 4.

> "self-efficacy after problem solving was not associated with invested effort, expected effort required, or willingness to invest effort. Third, challenge was positively associated with invested effort, expected effort required, and willingness to invest effort in Experiment 3 but not in Experiment 4."
