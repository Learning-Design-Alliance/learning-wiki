## Evidence

### Pumsopa 2025

Pumsopa, N., Jirapongsuwan, A., Kalampakorn, S., Siri, S., and Klainin‐Yobas, P. (2025). The Effectiveness of Community Nurse Training Programs to Improve Nurses' Competency in Community Settings: a Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. International Journal of Nursing Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijn.70090

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta‐analysis of 10 studies (16 comparisons; 1437 participants) found effect sizes differed significantly across delivery subgroups (Chi2 = 36.42, p < 0.001), with "The hybrid subgroup effect sizes ranged from 0.21 to 2.21" and "no effects of the onsite subgroup were observed".

> "The analysis revealed that community nurse training programs improved nurses' knowledge via hybrid pedagogical (SMD = 0.15–2.21), online (SMD = 0.12–0.29) and onsite methods (SMD = −0.51 to 0.11)"
