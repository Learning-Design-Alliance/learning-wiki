### Gregory L. Heileman 2018

Gregory L. Heileman, Chaouki T. Abdallah, Ahmad Slim, Michael Hickman. (2018). Curricular Analytics: A Framework for Quantifying the Impact of Curricular Reforms and Pedagogical Innovations. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.09676](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.09676)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational linear regression across all programs offered at the University of New Mexico, correlating structural complexity rankings with actual six-year graduation rates. The article reports that "every 17 point decrease in structural complexity corresponds to a 1% increase in the six-year graduation rate" and says this served as an impetus for programs to reduce structural complexity.

> "At the University of New Mexico we ranked these curricula according to their structural complexities, and we then correlated them to the actual six-year graduation rates of actual students using linear regression. The resulting model indicated that every 17 point decrease in structural complexity corresponds to a 1% increase in the six-year graduation rate."
