Evidence¶

Kaya 2016¶

Kaya, S., & Avci, R. (2016). Effects of cognitive-behavioral-theory-based skill-training on university students' future anxiety and trait anxiety. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 66, 281-298. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.66.16

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Mixed-design ANOVA on PFEI scores of 14 experimental and 14 control students in a quasi-experimental matched-pair design. The "group*measure interaction effect" was significant, F(1-26)=23.351, p<.001; the between-group main effect was not. Experimental PFEI rose from 15.36 to 21.21.

"the main effects were found to be insignificant [F(1 -26)=3.03; p> .05], but the mea surement main effect [F(1 -26)=14.302; p< .001] and group*measure interaction effect [F(1 -26)=23.351; p< .001] were found to be significant"
