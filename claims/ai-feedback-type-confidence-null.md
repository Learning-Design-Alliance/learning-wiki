### Omar Alsaiari 2026

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Omid Noroozi, Dragan Gašević, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). DIRECTIVE, METACOGNITIVE OR A BLEND OF BOTH? A COMPARISON OF AI-GENERATED FEEDBACK TYPES ON STUDENT ENGAGEMENT, CONFIDENCE, AND OUTCOMES. A PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

CLMM on five-point self-ratings of resource quality collected immediately after revision or prior to submission, with student ID as random intercept. EMMs were 3.63 (Directive), 3.25 (Metacognitive), 3.55 (Hybrid); observed medians were identical (Mdn= 4.0) and no pairwise contrast reached significance.

> "A cumulative link mixed model (CLMM) with a logit link tested whether confidence ratings (1–5 scale) differed by feedback group. The overall effect of group was not statistically significant, χ2 = 0.96, p=.62 ."
