## Evidence

### Kurz 2026

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results-section analysis of the computer vision dendrograms shows that Construct 15 (sensing the surroundings) moved from being the sole member of the lowest cluster pre-PD to cluster 2 post-PD. The authors state "Participants viewed this as not descriptive of computer vision" before PD but "more descriptive after PD," reflecting a specific change in how teachers conceptualized computer vision's capabilities.

> "In the pre-PD dendrogram, Computer vision can be described as…sensing the surroundings (Construct 15) was the only construct in cluster 4. Participants viewed this as not descriptive of computer vision. After PD, the construct moved to cluster 2, the cluster with the second highest mean. The participants viewed this as more descriptive after PD."
