Coefficient-comparison argument by the authors, drawing on the transition-to-advisorship regression (Model 2), reporting a female-scholar coefficient of "-0.113***" versus "-0.132***" for students ranked 6-10 compared to the firstborn. The Hispanic comparison is also reported for Model 4.
