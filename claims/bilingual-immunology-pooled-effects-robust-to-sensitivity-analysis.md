## Evidence

### Ji Pengyu 2026

Ji Pengyu, Li Qianqian, Li Na, Zhang Lin. (2026). The efficacy of bilingual immunology teaching on undergraduates in China: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08725-7

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Assessment of publication bias via funnel plots and Egger's linear regression tests (p = 0.14, 0.54, 0.21) and robustness via sequential one-study-omission sensitivity analysis in Stata 17.0. The authors caution Egger results warrant caution given low statistical power with small samples.

> "Sensitivity analysis revealed that the results remained stable upon omission of any single study, confirming the robustness of the findings (Additional file 1. supplementary appendix 7.1–7.3)."
