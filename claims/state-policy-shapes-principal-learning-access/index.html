Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2022¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Wechsler, M. E., Levin, S., Leung-Gagné, M., & Tozer, S. (2022). Developing effective principals: What kind of learning matters? [Report]. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/641.201

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-state survey comparison found California principals reported greater access to content including equity and teaching diverse learners, reflecting recent changes to state licensure and accreditation policies; after years of budget cuts, North Carolina principals reported far less access to nearly every kind of professional development.

"compared to principals nationally, a greater percentage of California principals reported that they had access to preparation and professional development in nearly every important content area, including areas focused on equity and teaching diverse learners, and a greater percentage reported that they had authentic, job-based learning opportunities in both pre- and in-service contexts."
