The department conducted a student survey with pre-test and post-test analytics to measure knowledge gained in the project management specialization core, with sample results shown in Figure 4. Students rated their knowledge before joining the program and confidence at the end.

### Vijay Kanabar 2015 (2)

Vijay Kanabar, PhD, Anatoly Temkin, PhD. (2015). Computer Science Programs, Goals, Student Learning Outcomes and their Assessment. CSECS 2015, Boston, MA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.07793](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.07793)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article reports that assessment of the project management program resulted in curriculum changes: a new Agile Software Development course was added to the core, the Software Architecture course was enhanced and revised, and the Distributed Software Development course was strengthened by at least 50%. The department planned to reassess in 2016.

> "The Distributed Software Development course was strengthened by at least 50%."
