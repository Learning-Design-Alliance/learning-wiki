## Evidence

### Carl Wieman and N.G. Holmes 2015

Carl Wieman and N.G. Holmes. (2015). Measuring the Impact of Introductory Physics Labs on Learning. [doi:10.1119/1.4931717](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4931717)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Synthesis of two studies by the same authors using the same ratio methodology: the earlier course with content-reinforcement goals and this redesigned modeling-focused course. Both found no significant ratio difference between lab and non-lab students on lecture-course exams.

> "this paper, in combination with the previous paper [2], demonstrate that a lab course that is distinct from the lecture part of the course does not provide added value to learning the course material."
