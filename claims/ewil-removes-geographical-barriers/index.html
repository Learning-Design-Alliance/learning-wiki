Evidence¶

Schuster 2017¶

Schuster, Lisa & Glavas, Charmaine (2017). Exploring the dimensions of electronic work integrated learning (eWIL). Educational Research Review, 21, pp. 55-66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.04.001

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of prior work (attributed to Holmes et al. 2012, Irwin et al. 2012 and Peach et al. 2013) argues technology extends WIL access; Patrick et al. (2008) found rural students face travel and living expense barriers to face-to-face WIL.

"Employing technology to support and deliver WIL can remove geographical barriers (Holmes et al., 2012; Irwin et al., 2012) and allows, for example, rural (regional) and external students to engage in WIL based activities (Peach et al., 2013)."
