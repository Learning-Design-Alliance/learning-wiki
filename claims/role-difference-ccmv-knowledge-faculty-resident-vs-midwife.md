### Pesch Megan H. 2020

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA comparing baseline knowledge across provider roles among the 53 respondents, with midwives and one APP grouped together. The midwife/APP group mean was 11 points with a statistically significant role difference (p = .012).
