# The Lap-PASS LP-100 VR dissection simulator discriminates surgeons from non-medical professionals on all GOALS items

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Surgeons scored significantly higher than non-medical professionals on the total GOALS score and on each of the four GOALS items when performing medial meso-sigmoid dissection on the VR simulator. [→ Mori Takashi 2022](#mori-takashi-2022)

## Evidence

### Mori Takashi 2022

Mori Takashi, Ikeda Koji, Takeshita Nobuyoshi, Teramura Koichi, Ito Masaaki. (2022). Validation of a novel virtual reality simulation system with the focus on training for surgical dissection during laparoscopic sigmoid colectomy. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01441-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective single-center validation study: 44 surgeons and 6 non-medical professionals performed medial dissection of the meso-sigmoid colon on the VR simulator; three blinded expert raters scored performances with GOALS. The article reports "significant differences in all GOALS items between the non-medical professionals and surgeons" (P values 0.0007–0.044).

> "The total score in surgeons was significantly superior to the non-medical professionals (16.0 (14.0–17) vs. 11.5 (9.5–12), with a 95% confidence interval (CI), p = 0.001)."
