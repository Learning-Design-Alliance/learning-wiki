Roy Lyster 2013¶

Roy Lyster, Kazuya Saito, and Masatoshi Sato. (2013). Oral corrective feedback in second language classrooms. Language Teaching, Volume 46, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0261444812000365

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review organizes its effectiveness evidence in two strands: "laboratory studies assessing the effects of recasts" and classroom studies assessing "the effects of different types of CF". This separation signals that recast effects are examined under controlled and ecologically valid conditions respectively.

"A brief review of laboratory studies assessing the effects of recasts is then presented before we focus on classroom studies assessing the effects of different types of CF."
