# Illinois Black and Latino students have less access to non-novice and certified teachers than their peers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Black students in Illinois are about twice as likely as non-Black students to attend schools with high (≥20%) percentages of novice teachers (22% vs 12%) and five times as likely for high (≥10%) uncertified-teacher schools (10% vs 2%). [→ EdTrust 2022](#edtrust-2022)
`q2 i?` Latino students are also more likely than non-Latino students to attend schools with high percentages of novice (16% vs 14%) and uncertified (6% vs 3%) teachers. [→ EdTrust 2022 (2)](#edtrust-2022-2)
