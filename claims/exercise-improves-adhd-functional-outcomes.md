## Evidence

### Vysniauske 2020

Vysniauske, R., Verburgh, L., Oosterlaan, J., & Molendijk, M. L. (2020). The Effects of Physical Exercise on Functional Outcomes in the Treatment of ADHD: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of attention disorders, 24(5), 644-654. https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054715627489

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of studies on exercise effects on motor skills and executive functions in children with ADHD, identified through Cochrane, PsycInfo, PubMed and Web of Science; "Ten publications were selected." The pooled effect was "g = 0.627", a modest positive impact.

> "There was a significant effect of exercise on ADHD functional outcomes (g = 0.627)."
