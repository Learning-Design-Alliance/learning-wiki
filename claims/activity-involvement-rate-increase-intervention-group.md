## Evidence

### Arakawa Hiromi 2020

Arakawa Hiromi, Anme Tokie. (2020). The effect of an experiential learning program on motivations and activity involvement among dementia supporters in Japan. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244337

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of activity rates at three months in the interventional study (Table 4); the intervention group's active rate rose to 70.3% while the control group's remained 34.1%, and "there was a significant difference in participant activity rates between the intervention and control groups."

> "With regard to activity involvement (Table 4), 43.2% of the intervention group participants were active before the program, and this figure increased to 70.3% after the program."
