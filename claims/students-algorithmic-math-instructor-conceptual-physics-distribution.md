## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational video analysis across all 50 coded episodes of small-group problem solving in two offerings of an upper-division E&M course. Students' total coded time was 5h42m and the instructor's 2h05m; the article reports students spent "the greatest fraction of their time (34%)" in AM while the instructor's most common framing was CP, "covering 59% of her framing" with 58 entries averaging 77 seconds.

> "Overall, students spend the greatest fraction of their time (34%) in the algorithmic math (AM) frame; in contrast, the instructor spends most of her time in the conceptual physics (CP) frame (ﬁgure 7) when interacting di- rectly with student groups during problem solving."
