## Evidence

### David Miller and Matthew Schraeder 2015

David Miller and Matthew Schraeder. (2015). Research on Group Learning and Cognitive Science: A Study of Motivation, Knowledge, and Self-Regulation in a Large Lecture College Algebra Class. The Mathematics Educator, Vol. 24, No. 2, 27-55. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085089

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational data collected for all College Algebra students since Spring 2007; Figure 2 plots success rates versus SP days attended and Table 1 reports course averages by attendance, peaking at 77.838% for 12 days. "the success rates—the number of students that receive at least a C divided by the total number of students—exceeded 65% range for students that attended 9 or more SP days".

> "Additionally, the success rates—the number of students that receive at least a C divided by the total number of students—exceeded 65% range for students that attended 9 or more SP days in a given semester."
