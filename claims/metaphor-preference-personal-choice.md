## Evidence

### Herodotou 2025

Herodotou, C., Shrestha, S., Comfort, C., Andrews, H., Mulholland, P., Bayer, V., Maguire, C., Lee, J. & Fernandez, M. (2025). A Participatory Approach to Designing a Student-Facing Dashboard for Online and Distance Education. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8481

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of Activity 2 mock-up discussions found opposing and supporting views for each of three metaphors: the tree metaphor was seen as "less judgmental" and showing growth, the weather metaphor as more neutral, and sad faces as potentially upsetting. The article concludes metaphor choice should be made by each student.

> "Overall, it was noted that the choice over which metaphor to be used should be made by each student."
