## Evidence

### Herpin 2025

Herpin, S. A., Wynveen, K., & Berry, W. (2025). California's Proposition 28 Arts and Music Funding Guarantee and Accountability Act: Initial impact on arts organizations. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677596

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended survey responses about future impact. Respondents said the funding allows schools to invest in qualified arts educators, materials, and standards-based arts programs; one commented that if fully invested, Prop 28 AMS will enhance the local arts education ecosystem.

> "Survey respondents noted several negative ways that Prop 28 AMS funding will impact their organizations; however, the majority of respondents agreed that this funding will likely have a positive impact on students."
