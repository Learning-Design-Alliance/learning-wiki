## Subclaims
`q3 i3` All three treatment groups significantly outperformed the Control group on the cloze task (ds 1.90–2.00) and did not differ from each other. [→ Craigo L. 2017](#craigo-l-2017)

## Evidence

### Craigo L. 2017

Craigo L., Ehri, L. C., & Hart, M. (2017). Teaching community college students strategies for learning unknown words as they read expository text. Higher Learning Research Communications, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v7i1.350

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of cloze posttests on the three treatment passages in the randomized experiment; Table 3 prints Cohen's effect sizes d = 2.00 (S+D), d = 1.90 (S), and d = 1.90 (D), all marked significant at p < .05.

> "Bonferroni tests showed that all three treatment groups significantly outperformed the Control group but did not differ from each other."

### Craigo L. 2017 (2)

Craigo L., Ehri, L. C., & Hart, M. (2017). Teaching community college students strategies for learning unknown words as they read expository text. Higher Learning Research Communications, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v7i1.350

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The treatment-by-passage interaction on cloze reached partial η² = .17 in Table 4; it was traced to Control means of 35%, 67%, and 40% across the three texts versus treatment means of 81% to 92%, and the article states "Why this happened is not clear."

> "Results of the ANCOVA revealed a significant main effect of treatment and a significant interaction between treatment and passage but no main effect of passage (see Table 4)."
