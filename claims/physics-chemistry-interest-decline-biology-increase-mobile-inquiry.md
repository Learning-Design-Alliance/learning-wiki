## Evidence

### Laine et al. 2017

Erkka Laine, Marjaana Veermans, Aleksi Lahti, Koen Veermans. (2017). Generation of student interest in an inquiry-based mobile learning environment. Frontline Learning Research Vol.5 No. 4 (2017) 42 - 60. https://doi.org/10.14786/flr.v5i4.306

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA and paired t-tests on the digital learning group (N = 18) across three time points in one school year. The article reports a "significant decrease in experimental group students’ interest in physics and chemistry" (d = 0.83) and a significant biology increase (d = 0.73).

> "Paired sample t-tests showed a significant decrease in experimental group students’ interest in physics and chemistry from time point 1 (M = 4.17, SD = .79) to time point 2 (M = 3.48, SD = .91); t(18) = -2.99, p = .008, Cohen’s d = 0.83. During the same time period, students’ interest in biology increased from time point 1 (M = 2.72, SD = .90) to time point 2 (M = 3.38, SD = .90); t(18) = 2.60, p = .019, Cohen’s d = 0.73."

### Laine et al. 2017 (2)

Erkka Laine, Marjaana Veermans, Aleksi Lahti, Koen Veermans. (2017). Generation of student interest in an inquiry-based mobile learning environment. Frontline Learning Research Vol.5 No. 4 (2017) 42 - 60. https://doi.org/10.14786/flr.v5i4.306

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same repeated measures analysis found no significant changes in interest levels between time points 2 and 3 in the spring semester; equivalence was not tested.

> "During the spring semester, between time point 2 and time point 3, no significant changes in interest levels were detected."
