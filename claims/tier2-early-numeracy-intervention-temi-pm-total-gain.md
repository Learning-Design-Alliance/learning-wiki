# First-graders with mathematics difficulties receiving the Tier 2 early numeracy intervention outperformed comparison students on the aligned TEMI-PM Total Score progress-monitoring measure (g* = .50)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Treatment students scored statistically significantly higher than comparison students on the TEMI-PM Total Score after Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment, with a printed effect size of g* = .50. [→ Bryant 2011](#bryant-2011)

## Evidence

### Bryant 2011

Bryant, D. P., Bryant, B. R., Roberts, G., Vaughn, S., Pfannenstiel, K. H., Porterfield, J., & Gersten, R. (2011). Early numeracy intervention program for first-grade students with mathematics difficulties. Exceptional Children, 78(1), 7-23. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577119

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pretest-posttest control group study with 139 treatment and 65 comparison first-graders; ANCOVA with fall TEMI-PM Total Score as covariate. The article reports the TEMI-PM Total Score difference as "p < .01; g* = .50" in favor of treatment; the printed effect size is g* = .50, a medium standardized mean difference.

> "Number Sequences (/> = < .00001; ^ = .47), and the TEMI-PM Total Score (p < .01; g* = .50). No differences were found on the Magnitude Comparisons subtest (p = .16; g* = .\8)."
