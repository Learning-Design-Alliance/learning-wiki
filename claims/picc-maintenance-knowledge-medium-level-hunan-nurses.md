Xu Binbin, Zhang Jinghui, Hou Jianmei, Ma Mengdan, Gong Zhihong, Tang Siyuan. (2020). Nurses' knowledge of peripherally inserted central catheter maintenance and its influencing factors in Hunan province, China: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 4110 registered nurses (63.0% response rate from 6524 eligible) in 91 hospitals at three levels in Hunan Province, using a self-designed 50-item questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.873). The article reports a "mean score of PICC maintenance among 4110 nurses was 72.86±14.86".

> "The mean score of PICC maintenance among 4110 nurses was 72.86±14.86. According to the scoring system, 83.5% of them passed the exam with a score of 60 or above and 34.1% of them exhibited a good grade with a score of 80 or above."
