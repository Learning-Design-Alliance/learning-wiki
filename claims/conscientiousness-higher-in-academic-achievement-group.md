## Evidence

### Boonyapison 2025

Boonyapison, K., Sittironnarit, G., & Rattanaumpawan, P. (2025). Association between the big five personalities and academic performance among grade 12 students at international high school in Thailand. Scientific Reports. [doi:10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate comparison of Big Five Inventory domain scores between the AA-group (GPA ≥ median, n = 102) and AN-group (GPA < median, n = 101) in a questionnaire study of 203 Grade-12 students. The quote reports the "significantly higher score in the conscientiousness domain"; agreeableness was not significant (p = 0.31).

> "The respondents in the AA-group had a significantly higher score in the conscientiousness domain (3.31 ± 0.56 vs. 3.12 ± 0.58, p-value = 0.02) and the slightly higher score in the agreeableness domain (3.59 ± 0.59 vs. 3.50 ± 0.55, p-value = 0.31) than the AN-group."
