## Evidence

### Creupelandt Hanne 2019

Creupelandt Hanne, Anthierens Sibyl, Habraken Hilde, Sirdifield Coral, Siriwardena Aloysius Niroshan, Christiaens Thierry. (2019). A tailored e-learning gives long-term changes in determinants of GPs’ benzodiazepines prescribing: a pretest-posttest study with self-report assessments. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2019.1663591

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest self-report study of 244 Flemish GPs: participants "felt more strongly that they had the expertise to use non-pharmacological interventions (Z = −7.851, p < .001)", rising "from 21.3% at baseline to 57% several months after the intervention".

> "and felt more strongly that they had the expertise to use non-pharmacological interventions (Z = −7.851, p < .001). The number of GPs who felt they had such expertise almost tripled: from 21.3% at baseline to 57% several months after the intervention."
