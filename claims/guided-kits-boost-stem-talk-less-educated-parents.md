## Evidence

### Karen L. Bierman 2025

Karen L. Bierman, Lynn S. Liben, Meg Small, Jennifer Connell, Brenda Heinrichs, Jessica Menold, Scarlett Rae Miller, Morgan Mannweiler. (2025). Guided activity kits impact parents’ scaffolding of child STEM play. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102082

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Moderated intervention effect from the randomized study of 75 parent-child dyads solving novel building challenges before and after the 10-week kit period. The article reports that "parents with less formal education assigned to guided activity kits produced significantly more STEM talk at the post-intervention assessments than did those given basic kits." No effect size is printed.
