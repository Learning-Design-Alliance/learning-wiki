### Shyjka 2022

Shyjka, A. (2022). Reaching for improvement: Teacher evaluation and its role in instructional improvement. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/talent/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interviews with 44 teachers across 7 CPS schools found that "Positive, trusting relationships between teachers and evaluators lessened anxiety around teacher evaluation and encouraged engagement in the process." Teachers with trusting relationships took a "benefit of the doubt" view of REACH.
