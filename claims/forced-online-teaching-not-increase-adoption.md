### Trevisan 2023

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, drawing on Rogers' (2003) stages of adoption: the pandemic forced faculty in various adoption stages to teach online, and the article reports that good institutional support "can positively influence adoption". No effect size is printed for this interpretation.
