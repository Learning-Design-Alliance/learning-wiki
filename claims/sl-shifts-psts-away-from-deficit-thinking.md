### Toronyi 2020

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1267630

Synthesis of Wu and Guerra's (2017) and Wall's (2016) studies within the meta-synthesis; PSTs' deficit beliefs were challenged by ELLs' eagerness to engage in learning.

> "Many of the PSTs went through a process of change realizing that the deficit-thinking model guiding their initial beliefs, assuming ELLs and their families somehow contribute to their own inability to learn English at the rate as expected of them"
