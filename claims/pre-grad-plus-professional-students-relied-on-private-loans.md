Historical descriptive analysis of borrowing before Grad PLUS (created 2006), when the federal annual limit was $18,500: "30% of professional degree recipients took out private student loans," versus 7% of master's and 5% of doctoral earners; 42% at private institutions had private loans.

> "In the 2003-04 academic year, 7% of master's degree earners, 5% of doctoral degree earners, and 30% of professional degree recipients took out private student loans."
