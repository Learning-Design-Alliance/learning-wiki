## Evidence

### Daumiller 2019

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analyses with the MLR estimator in a sample of 1,066 German university instructors from 74 public universities showed that "a model with all theoretically possible 10 goal classes (cf. Figure 1) fit the data well", with robustness confirmed via three-fold cross-validation reported in an electronic supplement.

> "Altogether a model with all theoretically possible 10 goal classes (cf. Figure 1) fit the data well.  We additionally confirmed the robustness of this model by conducting a three -fold cross-validation (Grimm, Mazza, & Davoudzadeh, 2016; details can be found in an electronic supplement)."
