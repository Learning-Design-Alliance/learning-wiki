### F. Bouquet 2021

F. Bouquet, G. Creutzer, D. Dorsel, J. Vince and J. Bobroff. (2021). Enhance your smartphone with a Bluetooth Arduino nano board. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac35af](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac35af)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

This is the authors' design rationale, not a tested comparison: the article states the three issues (risk of damaging a student's smartphone, sensors varying a lot depending on the smartphone, and missing sensors such as "thermometer or hygrometer") and presents the nano board as "a low-cost solution that help solving these three issues". No controlled evaluation is reported.

> "We present here a low-cost solution that help solving these three issues and increases the flexibility offered by using a smartphone as a visualization and measuring device."
