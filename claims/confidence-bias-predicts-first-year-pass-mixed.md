## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2019

Tiffin Paul A, Paton Lewis W. (2019). Does 'online confidence' predict application success and later academic performance in medical school? A UK-based national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034437

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cohort analysis of first-year academic outcomes among entrants to UKCAT consortium medical schools (year 1 outcome data for 1252 candidates). The univariable OR was 4.37 (1.54 to 12.42, p=0.006); the article reports the effect "remained statistically significant when controlling for performance on the 'decision analysis' subtest" with OR 3.24 (1.08 to 9.72, p=0.04).

> "This effect remained statistically significant when controlling for performance on the 'decision analysis' subtest of the UKCAT (OR 3.24, 1.08 to 9.72, p=0.04)."

### Tiffin Paul A 2019 (2)

Tiffin Paul A, Paton Lewis W. (2019). Does 'online confidence' predict application success and later academic performance in medical school? A UK-based national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034437

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same cohort analysis with fuller covariate adjustment. The article reports confidence bias was "no longer significantly predictive" of passing year 1 at first attempt (OR 1.96, 0.43 to 9.03, p=0.4), so the adjusted effect did not replicate under the fuller model.

> "Once the potential influence of other background variables were controlled for, 'confidence bias' scores were no longer significantly predictive of the odds of passing year 1 of medical school at the first attempt (OR 1.96, 0.43 to 9.03, p=0.4)."
