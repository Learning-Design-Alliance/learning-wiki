## Evidence

### W. S. Painter 1914

W. S. Painter. (1914). Efficiency in Mental Multiplication Under Extreme Fatigue. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Final-test record (Table II) of 28 four-place by five-place problems solved between 11:00 P.M. and 3:07+ A.M., with times in seconds and wrong figures per answer. The text reports "a gradual rise in time per problem from the first to the eighth, a relatively steady period from the 11th to the 18th problem and a marked rise" afterward.

> "There appears to be no obvious correlation between the time and the accuracy scores."
