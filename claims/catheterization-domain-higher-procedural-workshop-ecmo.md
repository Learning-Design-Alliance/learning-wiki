# The catheterization domain score is significantly higher after procedural workshop training than after high-fidelity simulation training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The catheterization domain median score was significantly higher in the procedural simulation workshop group than in the high-fidelity simulation group. [→ Bouras Marwan 2026](#bouras-marwan-2026)

## Evidence

### Bouras Marwan 2026

Bouras Marwan, Senage Thomas, Meule Emma, David Charles-Henri, Guimbretière Guillaume, Cadiet Julien, Bazin Olivier, Rozec Bertrand, Lejus-Bourdeau Corinne, Vourc’h Mickael. (2026). Full-Scale high-fidelity simulation compared to procedural simulation workshop for veno-arterial ECMO cannulation during refractory cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09618-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized trial's blinded video assessment, the catheterization domain median differed significantly between groups (6.0/7 vs 5.0/7, "p = 0.006"), favoring the procedural workshop group.

> "The “catheterization” domain revealed a median score of 6.0/7 (IQR 5.5–6.0) for the procedural simulation workshop group, which was significantly higher than the 5.0/7 (IQR 4.0–5.0) observed in the high-fidelity simulation group (p = 0.006)"
