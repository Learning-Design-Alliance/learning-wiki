The authors report that "16 were newly added (Table 1)" and that the comparison was independently checked by a second reviewer.

> "Overall, 43 core competency areas were identified for the updated framework (see Supplementary Appendix B, available in the online version only). Of these, 18 remained unchanged, 16 were newly added (Table 1), and 9 were renamed or adapted."
