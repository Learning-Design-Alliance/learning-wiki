## Evidence

### Ian D. Beatty and Lauren A. Harris 2014

Ian D. Beatty and Lauren A. Harris. (2014). Pwning Level Bosses in MATLAB: Student Reactions to a Game-Inspired Computational Physics Course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.006](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.006)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding analysis of 11 CP1 transcripts: co-occurrence of the design-feature code self-paced with the emergent code self-discipline, which students read as a causal relationship. Quotes include "the student needs to take responsibility for their own work" and one noting other deadlines took priority.

> "The most prevalent co-occurrence was the conjunction of "self-paced" with "self-discipline," explicitly mentioned by seven of the eleven respondents."
