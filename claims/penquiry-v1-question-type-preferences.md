# In Study 1, learners preferred Penquiry V1 for part-scoped, specific-target and short questions, and favored the baseline for general topics and longer interpretive questions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 1 causal `r1`, 1 design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants showed an overwhelming preference for V1 when asking Part-scoped questions. [→ scope-pref](#scope-pref)
`q3 i?` Preference shifted with question complexity: V1 was preferred for short Definition and Description questions, while a trend toward the baseline emerged for longer, more interpretive questions. [→ content-pref](#content-pref)

## Evidence

### scope-pref

Jeongmin Rhee, Changhee Lee, Hyunwoo Kim, Kiroong Choe, Bohyoung Kim, Sungahn Ko, and Jinwook Seo. 2026. Penquiry: A Pen-based Interactive In-situ Q&A System Leveraging LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 question-scope analysis (N=16, within-subjects) classifying targeted elements as Whole or Part; Figure 4(B) preference data show V1 strongly preferred for Part-scoped questions where spatial referencing is crucial. For general Topic targets participants favored the baseline.

> "According to the preference data (Figure 4(B)), participants showed an overwhelming preference for usingPenquiryV1 when asking thesePart-scoped questions."

### content-pref

Jeongmin Rhee, Changhee Lee, Hyunwoo Kim, Kiroong Choe, Bohyoung Kim, Sungahn Ko, and Jinwook Seo. 2026. Penquiry: A Pen-based Interactive In-situ Q&A System Leveraging LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19870

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 1 question-content analysis using the Scardamalia and Bereiter framework; Figure 4(C) shows V1 preferred for short Text-based questions, while "a trend toward preferring the baseline emerged" for interpretive questions, which participants said were faster to type.

> "According to the preference data (Figure 4(C)), participants showed a significant preference forPenquiryV1 when asking shorter questions, such as Text-based (Definition) and Text-based (Description)."
