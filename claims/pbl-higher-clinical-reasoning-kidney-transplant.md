### Gao C 2026

Gao C, Li J, Shui K, Xie X, Peng L. (2026). Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in specialist physician training for kidney transplantation. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047684

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structured case-analysis assessment scored in 4 domains (0–25 each) by trained faculty using standardized rubrics, in the same retrospective cohort of 115 trainees. The PBL group's "overall clinical reasoning score" exceeded the control group's; a higher proportion reached ≥80 points (81.4% vs 53.6%; χ2 = 10.164, P = .001).
