Evidence¶

Hadar Ram 2024¶

Hadar Ram, Guy Grinfeld & Nira Liberman. (2024). Anticipated variability increases generalization of predictive learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00269-z

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Preregistered online experiment (final N = 91, random assignment) with vignettes manipulating whether a second bacteria batch was collected minutes or a year later; the distal condition expected more difference. A paired-sample test also favored the "more likely" over "less likely" statement, t(90) = 8.59, p < 0.001, d = 3.45.

"An independent-samples t-test revealed that participants in the distal condition rated the likelihood that the second batch would be different as higher (M =6 . 6 1 ,SD = 2.61) than participants in the proximal condition (M = 3.88, SD = 1.95),t(89) = 5.57, p < 0.001,d = 2.32."
