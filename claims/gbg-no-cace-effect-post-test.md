`q3 i?` At post-test, CACE models showed no statistically significant effects for moderate (β = 0.01) or high compliance (β = 0.20), indicating the null ITT result was not due to insufficient exposure. [→ Ashworth Emma 2020](#ashworth-emma-2020)

## Evidence

### Ashworth Emma 2020

Ashworth Emma, Panayiotou Margarita, Humphrey Neil, Hennessey Alexandra. (2020). Game On—Complier Average Causal Effect Estimation Reveals Sleeper Effects on Academic Attainment in a Randomized Trial of the Good Behavior Game. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-019-01074-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multilevel mixture CACE models at post-test (T2) in the cluster RCT found "no statistically significant effects for moderate (β = 0.01, p > .05) or high compliance (β = 0.20, p > .05)"; classes showed high posterior probabilities (> 90%) and entropy of 0.73–0.78.
