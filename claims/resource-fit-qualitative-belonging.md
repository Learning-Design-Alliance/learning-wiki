### Alexander S. Browman 2025

Alexander S. Browman. (2025). Centering Social Fit, Self-Concept Fit, Goal Fit, and Resource Fit as Core Elements of Students' Experiences of Belonging at School. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10009-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports qualitative work with working-class university students (G. M. Bettencourt, 2021; Baker & Sgoutas-Emch, 2014) in which "the fluid nature of social class and academic billing cycles" backgrounded belonging to basic needs. Fernández et al. (2023) found female students who experienced sexual assault and harassment without university support reported feelings that they did not belong; Matthews et al. (2021) found Black and Latinx students cited teachers' consciousness of students' resource needs.

> "the fluid nature of social class and academic billing cycles meant that participants constantly thought about future expenses, subsequently backgrounding belonging to more basic needs in times of financial strife."
