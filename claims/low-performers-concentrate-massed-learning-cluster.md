## Evidence

### Peach 2019

Peach, R. L., Yaliraki, S. N., Lefevre, D. and Barahona, M. (2019). Data-driven unsupervised clustering of online learner behaviour. npj Science of Learning 4:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0054-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A posteriori observational comparison of final marks with the data-driven 6-cluster partition of 81 learners' task-completion time-series. The authors report that "6 out of 7 low-performance learners lie in the 'Crammers' cluster"; one low performer (learner 77) sat in a distributed cluster.

> "Figure 5b shows that 6 out of 7 low-performance learners lie in the 'Crammers' cluster associated with massed learning and reduced task completion."

### Peach 2019 (2)

Peach, R. L., Yaliraki, S. N., Lefevre, D. and Barahona, M. (2019). Data-driven unsupervised clustering of online learner behaviour. npj Science of Learning 4:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0054-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same a posteriori evaluation of the 81-learner cohort: high performers (top 15%, >70%) were mapped onto the clustering. "9 out of the 13 high-performing learners" were in distributed-learning clusters, while the rest were spread across other clusters.

> "Still, 9 out of the 13 high-performing learners are found in the 'Early Birds' or 'On time' clusters characterised by a sequential approach to their learning with minimal massed learning sessions."
