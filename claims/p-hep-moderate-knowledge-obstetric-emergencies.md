`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional knowledge test of 97 Tehran Emergency Center personnel using a 25-item researcher-developed questionnaire; the article reports the mean knowledge score was "46.6%" with 69% at a moderate level.

> "The mean score of knowledge questionnaire among the participants was 46.6%. According to the first and third quartiles of the obtained scores, 15.5% of the personnel reported a poor level of knowledge, 69% had a moderate level, and 15.5% demonstrated a good level of knowledge."
