## Evidence

### C. Love 2013

C. Love, A. Murphy, S. Bonora. (2013). Evaluating Astronomy Literacy of the General Public. Preprint submitted to the Astronomical Journal. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.2195](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.2195)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive age-group comparison from the museum survey, shown per question in Figure 3. The authors state the statistics need improvement and the older group is roughly one-eighth the size of the younger group.

> "participants with an age of 56 and over had an average score of 78 ± 4% compared to participants under the age of 56 that were found to have an average score of 82 ± 2%. The statistics need to be improved here and the number of participants older than 55 are roughly one-eight the number of participants 55 and younger."
