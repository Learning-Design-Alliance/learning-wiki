## Evidence

### Bustamante-Balén M 2019

Bustamante-Balén M, Satorres C, Puchades L, Navarro B, García-Morales N, Alonso N, Ponce M, Argüello L, Pons-Beltrán V. (2019). Non-guided self-learning program for high-proficiency optical diagnosis of diminutive and small colorectal lesions: A single-endoscopist pilot study. World Journal of Gastroenterology. [doi:10.3748/wjg.v25.i10.1278](https://doi.org/10.3748/wjg.v25.i10.1278)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective single-endoscopist pilot study in a population-based colorectal cancer screening program with 152 patients and 522 lesions; pathology served as gold standard. The "proportion of agreement on surveillance interval between OD and pathological diagnosis was higher than 95%" across guidelines.

> "The negative predictive value for the OD of adenoma in rectosigmoid lesions diagnosed with high confidence was 91.7% [95% confidence interval (CI): 87.3-96.6]. The proportion of agreement on surveillance interval between OD and pathological diagnosis was higher than 95%."

### Bustamante-Balén M 2019 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Diagnostic accuracy analysis of high-confidence optical diagnoses of diminutive lesions against pathology in the same prospective pilot; the article reports "Overall accuracy for diminutive lesions diagnosed with high confidence was 89.5%".

> "Overall accuracy for diminutive lesions diagnosed with high confidence was 89.5% (95%CI: 86.3-92.7)."
