## Evidence

### Salim Nabhan 2026

Salim Nabhan, Anita Habók. (2026). Language teachers' AI literacy: A psychometric study based on the ED-AI framework. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence 10 (2026) 100583. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100583

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis (PCA with varimax rotation) with sample 1 (N = 165); KMO = 0.906 and Bartlett's test chi-square = 2737.390 (df = 435, p < 0.001). The six-factor model "explained 63.965% of the total variance" with factor alphas 0.759–0.867.

> "Overall, the six-factor model explained 63.965% of the total variance, with each factor demonstrating acceptable to strong internal consistency, as indicated by Cronbach's alpha values ranging from 0.759 to 0.867."
