### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018

The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's implication-for-implementation synthesis. The review itself cautions the conclusion is stretched because many ML studies come "from the same study group" with large heterogeneity of other designs.

> "ML-based studies create the most consistent positive results in all Kirkpatrick/Phillips levels and thus appeal as the preferable learning strategy. As so many studies are investigating the same learning strategy and from the same study group, this would be stretching the conclusion a bit."

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018 (2)

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's interpretation (type e) of the Barsuk/Cohen ML studies: pretesting and immediate feedback until a predefined mastery level, in a four-hour simulation-based course, is credited with transfer to clinical performance and cost-efficiency.

> "a four-hour course of dedicated simulation-based ML was used for practicing central venous catheterization. These studies adhere to the principles of ML as defined by Bloom in his original work by using pretesting and training with immediate feedback until a predefined mastery level is reached."
