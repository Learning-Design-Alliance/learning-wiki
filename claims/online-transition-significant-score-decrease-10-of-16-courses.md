Kim Do-Hwan, Lee Hyo Jeong, Lin Yanyan, Kang Ye Ji. (2021). Changes in academic performance in the online, integrated system-based curriculum implemented due to the COVID-19 pandemic in a medical school in Korea. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2021.18.24

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative observational study of major-examination percent-correct scores in 16 required courses at Hanyang University College of Medicine, comparing 2019 (face-to-face) with 2020 (online) cohorts using t-tests and Cohen D. "a significant decrease in scores was found in 10 courses (62.5%) in 2020." Per-course Cohen D values are printed in Table 2 but no single effect size is printed for this aggregate finding.

> "When comparing the test results from the 16 courses, a significant decrease in scores was found in 10 courses (62.5%) in 2020 (Table 2, Dataset 1). There was no significant difference in 3 courses, and a significant increase was found in 3 courses."
