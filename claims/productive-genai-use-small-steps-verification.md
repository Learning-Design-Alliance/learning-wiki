## Evidence

### Karl Henrik Fredly 2026

Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). How undergraduate physics students use generative AI for computational modeling. [doi:10.1103/vg1v-dkrh](https://doi.org/10.1103/vg1v-dkrh)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the interviews found "the most productive students used genAI to fix small, concrete issues" where they understood the problem, rather than offloading substantial modeling work.

> "As the genAI was limited in what it was able to fix, the most productive students used genAI to fix small, concrete issues where they had some understanding of the problem and a sense of what a solution should look like."

### Karl Henrik Fredly 2026 (2)

Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). How undergraduate physics students use generative AI for computational modeling. [doi:10.1103/vg1v-dkrh](https://doi.org/10.1103/vg1v-dkrh)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis reports nine students who felt they got the most out of genAI for coding "used genAI to help implement specific, well-understood parts of the model", such as small code transformations or specific plots.

> "The nine students who felt that they got the most out of using genAI for coding all used it in a similar way. These students used genAI to help implement specific, well-understood parts of the model."
