## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Medical freshmen taught with flipped classroom plus the web-based learning system achieved significantly higher mean post-quiz scores than peers in traditional classroom teaching (91.44 ± 6.25 vs 86.13 ± 11.67, P < .05). [→ Yang C 2020](#yang-c-2020)

## Evidence

### Yang C 2020

Yang C, Yang X, Yang H, Fan Y. (2020). Flipped classroom combined with human anatomy web-based learning system shows promising effects in anatomy education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023096

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized allocation of 89 freshmen into experimental (n=45) and control (n=44) classes over one semester; the experimental class used flipped classroom with the web-based system. Post-quiz means were "91.44 ± 6.25 and 86.13 ± 11.67, respectively, with a statistically significant difference (P < .05". No effect size is printed.

> "After anatomy course education, the mean exam scores (post-quiz score) of experimental and control group were 91.44 ± 6.25 and 86.13 ± 11.67, respectively, with a statistically significant difference (P < .05, Table 2)."
