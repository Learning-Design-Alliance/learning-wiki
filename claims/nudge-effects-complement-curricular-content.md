# Treatment effects were larger for previously higher-performing students and for schools with prior online activities, consistent with complementarity between curricular content and socio-emotional skills

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Test scores deteriorated more among above-median schools (0.25 s.d. more) and above-median students (0.19 s.d. more) in control schools; the intervention reduced differential losses by about half for these groups. [→ Lichand 2024](#lichand-2024)
`q3 i?` The effect was negative but not significant for schools without prior online activities (-0.11 s.d.) and much larger for schools that had them (0.30 s.d.). [→ Lichand 2024](#lichand-2024)
`q3 i?` Effects did not significantly vary by student gender. [→ Lichand 2024](#lichand-2024)
