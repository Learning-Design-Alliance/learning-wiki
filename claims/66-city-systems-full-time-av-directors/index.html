q2 · i? · associational · r2

Questionnaire survey of city school superintendents described in the dissertation abstract. The inquiry "revealed that 66 city school systems had full-time audio-visual directors," and 60 of those directors completed the Audio-Visual Director Information Form. No effect sizes are printed.

"A post card inquiry was sent to all (499) city school system superintendents in cities of 10,000 or more population located in the North Central Association geographical area. This inquiry revealed that 66 city school systems had full-time audio-visual directors."
