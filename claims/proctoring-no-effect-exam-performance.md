# Proctored online exams show no effect on students' exam performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No fixed effects of online or proctored exams on exam grade were found once student and course effects were accounted for. [→ Conijn 2022](#conijn-2022)

## Evidence

### Conijn 2022

Conijn, R., Kleingeld, A., Matzat, U., & Snijders, C. (2022). The fear of big brother: The potential negative side-effects of proctored exams. Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, 38(6), 1521-1534. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcal.12651

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Crossed-random-intercepts multi-level model of final exam grades (available for 709 grades in a subset of 22 courses; 34% of variance at student level, 16% at course level). The study reports "no fixed effects were found for online ( p = 0.89) and proctoring ( p = 0.39)"; hypothesis 1.3 was not supported.

> "While accounting for the course and student effects, no fixed effects were found for online ( p = 0.89) and proctoring ( p = 0.39)."
