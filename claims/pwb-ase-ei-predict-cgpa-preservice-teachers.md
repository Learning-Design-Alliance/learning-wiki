### Quansah Frank 2025

Quansah Frank, Essel Daniel William, Gyapong Nancy Asieduwaa, Yabana Isaac N‐Nandi, Dzakadzie Yayra, Asamoah Patrick, Addison Kingsley. (2025). Enhancing Teacher Training Through Self‐Efficacy and Emotional Intelligence: A Conditional Process Model of Pre‐Service Teachers' Well‐Being and Academic Achievements. Brain and Behavior, 15(5), e70578. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70578

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional process analysis (Hayes PROCESS model 8, 10,000 bootstrap samples) of cross-sectional survey data from 660 Ghanaian pre-service teachers, controlling for age and sex. The CGPA model prints "PWB also significantly predicted CGPA (B = 0.1979, BootSE = 0.0131, BootCI [0.1722, 0.2236])."

> "In addition, PWB also significantly predicted CGPA (B = 0.1979, BootSE = 0.0131, BootCI [0.1722, 0.2236])."
