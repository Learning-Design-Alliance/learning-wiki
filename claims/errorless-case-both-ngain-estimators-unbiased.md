### Jairo Navarrete 2024

Jairo Navarrete, Valentina Giaconi, Gonzalo Contador, Mariano Vazquez. (2024). Another reason why normalized gain should continue to be used to analyze concept inventories (and estimate learning rates). arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.07730](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.07730)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivations in Section III (central limit theorem, delta method, tower property) show F is unbiased and the average-learner estimator is asymptotically unbiased: "any difference between them is due only to randomness".

> "To summarize, when there are no measurement errors, both estimators ng and cng are unbiased. It is worth emphasizing, in this case, that any difference between them is due only to randomness."
