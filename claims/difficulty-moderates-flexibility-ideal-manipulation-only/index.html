Evidence¶

Guo 2025¶

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

q3 · i? · design · r2

In the ideal-manipulation multilevel logistic regressions, the difficulty-by-flexibility interaction was significant, whereas the same interaction was not significant in the task-success analyses — a scope limit across outcome measures.

"However, in this line of analyses, the interaction effect of difficulty level with flexibility was significant (while it was not significant for task success). This implies that the facilitating effect of behavioral flexibility on ideal manipulation was more salient for tasks with a higher difficulty level."
