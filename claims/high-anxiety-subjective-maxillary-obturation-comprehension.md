## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of self-reported items during maxillary root canal treatment. Obturation difficulty (U = 702.0, p = 0.020) and comprehension (U = 714.0, Z = −2.276, p = 0.023) both significantly favored the low-anxiety group; other items did not differ.

> "First, Obturation scores were lower in the high-anxiety group (mean rank = 43.14) compared to the low-anxiety group (mean rank = 55.10), with median scores of 1 (IQR = 1–2) versus 2 (IQR = 1–2), respectively (U = 702.0, Z = − 2.322, p = 0.020)."
