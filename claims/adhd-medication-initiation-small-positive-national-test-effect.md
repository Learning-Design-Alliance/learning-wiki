## Evidence

### Varnet Pérez 2025

Varnet Pérez, Tomás; Øvergaard, Kristin Romvig; Frigessi, Arnoldo; Biele, Guido. (2025). Long-term effect of pharmacological treatment on academic achievement of Norwegian children diagnosed with ADHD: a target trial emulation. International Journal of Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyaf010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main analysis (g-formula with BART, multiple imputation) on the washout analytic sample of 8458 children with complete washout, from linked Norwegian registries. Results: "0.050 (CI95, 0.016; 0.084), 0.078 (CI95, 0.042; 0.117), 0.073 (CI95, 0.035; 0.108) standardized score points for the domains of English, numeracy, and reading, respectively" — small positive point estimates for all three domains.

> "For the never-medicated-before sample, the SATE had a posterior mean (and compatibility interval) of 0.050 (CI95, 0.016; 0.084), 0.078 (CI95, 0.042; 0.117), 0.073 (CI95, 0.035; 0.108) standardized score points for the domains of English, numeracy, and reading, respectively"

### Varnet Pérez 2025 (2)

Varnet Pérez, Tomás; Øvergaard, Kristin Romvig; Frigessi, Arnoldo; Biele, Guido. (2025). Long-term effect of pharmacological treatment on academic achievement of Norwegian children diagnosed with ADHD: a target trial emulation. International Journal of Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyaf010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analysis on the broader no-washout sample of 11,835 observations, same g-formula/BART model. The article reports "0.037 (CI95, −0.003; 0.076), 0.063 (CI95, 0.016; 0.111), 0.071 (CI95, 0.03; 0.111)" for English, numeracy and reading — the English effect is no longer clearly positive.

> "Regarding results for the no-washout sample, the corresponding SATE and compatibility intervals were of 0.037 (CI95, −0.003; 0.076), 0.063 (CI95, 0.016; 0.111), 0.071 (CI95, 0.03; 0.111) standardized score points for the domains of English, numeracy, and reading, respectively"
