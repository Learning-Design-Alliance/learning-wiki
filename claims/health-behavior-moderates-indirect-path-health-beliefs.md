## Evidence

### Li Heyu 2025

Li Heyu, Lu Yang, Tian Yang, Zhou Li, Wang Danna. (2025). Mechanism of an integrated PBL-KAP-AI spiral health education model for breast cancer patients. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1687956

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

PROCESS macro Model 7 moderated mediation analysis with 5,000 bootstrap samples on 244 participants. The satisfaction × health behavior interaction significantly predicted quality of life (B = 0.01, p < 0.001).

> "at low (M − 1 SD), moderate (M), and high (M + 1 SD) levels of health behavior, the indirect effects of satisfaction on health beliefs via quality of life were 0.07, 0.14, and 0.22, respectively. All bootstrap confidence intervals excluded zero, confirming that the mediation effects were significant across different levels of the moderator."
