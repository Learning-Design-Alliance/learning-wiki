## Evidence

### Becker J 2022

Becker J, Becker C, Oprescu F, Wu C-J, Moir J, Shimwela M, Gray M. (2022). Silent voices of the midwives: factors that influence midwives' achievement of successful neonatal resuscitation in sub-Saharan Africa: a narrative inquiry. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-021-04339-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 findings from the narrative interviews: midwives described newly rotated staff as unskilled, with one noting midwives trained on the ward are moved to another unit within months. The hospital handles a minimum of 40-80 deliveries daily. No effect sizes printed.

> "Staff at the research hospital are frequently rotated in and out of the labor ward, so a midwife or nurse who has just joined the labor ward may be unskilled in neonatal resuscitation, which can be a major hindrance to providing effective skilled care for both mothers and neonates."

### Becker J 2022 (2)

Becker J, Becker C, Oprescu F, Wu C-J, Moir J, Shimwela M, Gray M. (2022). Silent voices of the midwives: factors that influence midwives' achievement of successful neonatal resuscitation in sub-Saharan Africa: a narrative inquiry. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-021-04339-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 interview finding, supported by a midwife's quote: "If I don't get enough resources, I feel bad because my work will be hindered." Reported barriers include gloves, medications, running water and 24/7 electricity being unreliable. Qualitative, no effect sizes.

> "An environment with a regular lack of resources was identified as having a negative impact on performance and morale:"
