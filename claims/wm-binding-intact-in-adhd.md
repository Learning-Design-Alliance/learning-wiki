# Visual WM binding of color and shape is intact in ADHD: no group differences behaviorally or electrophysiologically, with Bayesian support for null Group x Condition interactions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant Group x Condition interaction on accuracy, and no binding-dependent ERP group differences, indicating binding processes seem intact in ADHD. [→ Ortega 2020](#ortega-2020)
