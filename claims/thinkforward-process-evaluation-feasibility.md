## Evidence

### Demack et al. 2016

Sean Demack, Colin McCaig, Claire Wolstenholme, Anna Stevens and Laura Fumagalli. (2016). ThinkForward: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation/projects/think-forward

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative process evaluation with coaches, school leads and participating pupils across both intervention schools. Coaches and teachers felt support from "a trusted adult" helped pupils when considering GCSE options in Year 9 and after leaving school.

> "Teachers reported benefits, particularly during t he later months of the pilot, where they observed impacts on the intervention pupils’ behaviour."
