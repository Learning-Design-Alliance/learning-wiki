## Evidence

### Lisa A DelSignore 2016

Lisa A DelSignore, Traci A Wolbrink, David Zurakowski, Jeffrey P Burns. (2016). Test-Enhanced E-Learning Strategies in Postgraduate Medical Education: A Randomized Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6199

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary subgroup analysis within the randomized cohort: after adjustment for gender and training year, second-year residents in the short answer questions group had "greater percent improvement in posttest scores compared with first-year residents" (63% [SD 13] vs 39% [SD 16], P=.02).

> "Adjustment for gender and postgraduate training year did not alter the overall results, although second-year residents randomized to the short answer questions group had greater percent improvement in posttest scores compared with first-year residents (63% [SD 13] vs 39% [SD 16] vs P=.02)."
