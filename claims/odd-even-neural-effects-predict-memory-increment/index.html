Evidence¶

Yafeng Pan 2023¶

Yafeng Pan, Ning Hao, Ning Liu, Yijie Zhao, Xiaojun Cheng, Yixuan Ku and Yi Hu. (2023). Mnemonic-trained brain tuning to a regular odd-even pattern subserves digit memory in children. npj Science of Learning 8:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00177-8

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pearson correlations between neural odd-even effects (post-training) and memory increment (d'post − d'pre): MT group r = 0.66, p = 0.009 (P200) and r = 0.52, p = 0.04 (F3 theta); NC group r = 0.13 and r = 0.06. A Fisher's z test showed the MT P200 correlation was "signi ﬁcantly larger than that in NC, z = 1.72, p = 0.04".
