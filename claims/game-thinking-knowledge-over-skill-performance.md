### Nylén-Eriksen Mats 2025

Nylén-Eriksen Mats, Stojiljkovic Marko, Lillekroken Daniela, Lindeflaten Katrin, Hessevaagbakke Elisabeth, Flølo Tone Nygaard, Hovland Olav Johannes, Solberg Ada Marie Svarstad Hansen Sylvia, Bjørnnes Ann Kristin, Tørris Christine. `q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis comparing knowledge and skill performance subcategories. The article reports "Pooled ES = 1.06" for knowledge and "Pooled ES = 0.54" for skills, with overlapping confidence intervals.

> "The most significant effect of game-thinking on academic achievement was observed in the academic knowledge performance of nursing students (Pooled ES = 1.06, [95%CI 0.55, 1.57]), followed by academic skill performance (Pooled ES = 0.54, [95%CI 0.06, 1.03])."
