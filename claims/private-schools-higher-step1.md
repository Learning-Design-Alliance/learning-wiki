## Evidence

### Ghaffari-Rafi Arash 2019

Ghaffari-Rafi Arash, Lee Rachel Elizabeth, Fang Rui, Miles J. Douglas. (2019). Multivariable analysis of factors associated with USMLE scores across U.S. medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1605-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate comparison of school type in the 100-school dataset found a significant Step 1 difference (P < .0001), with private schools at 233.2 (SD 6.3) and public at 228.6 (SD 5.0). The Step 2 difference (241.3 vs. 239.2) was only marginally significant (P = 0.051).

> "On average, private schools have around a five-point higher average Step 1 score, compared to public schools (233.2 vs. 228.6)."
