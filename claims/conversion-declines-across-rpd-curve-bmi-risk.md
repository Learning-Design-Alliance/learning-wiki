## Evidence

### González-Abós C 2026

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis across all 100 attempts in the single-surgeon series. The phase contrast was significant: "23.3% in Group A vs. 7.5% in Group B; P = 0.033"; conversions clustered during resection-phase steps on video review.

> "The overall conversion rate was 17% (23.3% in Group A vs. 7.5% in Group B; P = 0.033), with no emergency conversions."

### González-Abós C 2026 (2)

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Inverse-probability-weighted logistic regression of conversion on baseline covariates. The printed effect size for BMI was OR = 1.20 (95% CI 1.01–1.43, P = 0.044), a small association; uncinate tumour location was non-significant (OR 3.21, P = 0.109).

> "the variable most strongly associated with intraoperative conversion was BMI OR 1.20 (95% CI 1.01–1.43), P = 0.044 with higher values increasing the likelihood of conversion"
