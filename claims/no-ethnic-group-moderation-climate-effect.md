## Evidence

### Heppt 2025

Heppt, Birgit; Schwarzenthal, Miriam; Scharf, Jan. (2025). Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02147-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression with exploratory interaction terms (Ethnic majority × discriminatory climate, p = 0.705; other minoritized groups × discriminatory climate, p = 0.373) tested moderation of the climate effect on reading comprehension; both interactions were nonsignificant, and equivalence was not tested.

> "as indicated by the nonsignificant interaction terms in Model 1a (Interact 1) and Model 1a (Interact 2) in Table 2, the effect of perceived discriminatory climate on reading comprehension did not differ across ethnic groups"
