## Evidence

### Conlin 2019

Conlin, L. D., Kuo, E., and Hallinen, N. R. (2019). Nothing's plenty: The significance of null results in physics education research. Preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subsidiary analysis of the same middle-school experiment: histograms of strategies by condition show students "much more likely to use the General Principle Strategy if they were in the GP condition", supporting that GPS is teachable.

> "on the Ramp posttest, there was an association between condition (GP or CV) and which strategies students used to make comparisons (GPS, CVS, or Neither) 𝜒"	(2,𝑁=11)=6.14,𝑝<.01."
