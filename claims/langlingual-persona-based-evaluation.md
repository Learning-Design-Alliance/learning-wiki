`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Persona-based evaluation (Section 5.2): a human impersonates each of three personas and interacts with LangLingual; findings are qualitatively analysed. For example, for Yuki the system "consistently provided short, focused exercises tailored to her learning level" and politely corrected grammar while maintaining conversational tone.

> "These personas were used to simulate common use-case scenarios, evaluating the system's contextual appropriateness, clarity of feedback and accuracy of responses. One of the authors of the paper assigned the scores based on pre-defined criteria."
