## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, Kalman E, Haq I, Leaver A, Roberts C, Bleasel J. (2020). Interprofessional team-based learning (TBL): how do students engage? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02024-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses on the most difficult features of the session. Students suggested more structured discussion with questions targeting each discipline, a more diverse mix of students, and more interprofessional TBL sessions.

> "Students highlighted that the uneven ratio of medical and physiotherapy students, and this being the physiotherapy students’ first introduction to TBL may have hindered their contributions."
