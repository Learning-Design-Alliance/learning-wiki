## Evidence

### Ratminingsih 2018

Ratminingsih, N. M., Marhaeni, A. A. I. N., & Vigayanti, L. P. D. (2018). Self-Assessment: The Effect on Students’ Independence and Writing Competence. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 277-290. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11320a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA in a post-test-only control-group experiment comparing an experimental self-assessment group with a conventional-assessment group on an independence questionnaire. The article reports "the value of F = 4.485" with significance 0.038, rejecting the null hypothesis at sig. < 0.05.

> "The result of the calculation using one -way ANOVA shows that the difference between students’ independence treated by using self -assessment and conventional assessment shows the value of F = 4.485 and the value of significance 0 .038."
