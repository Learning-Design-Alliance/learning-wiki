# Across 29 controlled studies, positive overall effects of school-based PA on mathematics performance appear in 45% of studies and neutral effects in 52%, with only one negative subgroup result

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Of 29 studies involving 11,264 participants, 13 (45%) reported positive overall effects and 15 (52%) neutral effects on mathematics performance; only one study reported a significant negative result, for a subgroup in the first half of the intervention. [→ Sneck S. 2019](#sneck-s-2019)
