### Naeini and Shahrokhi 2016

Nazgol Nekoui Naeini, Mohsen Shahrokhi. (2016). Relationship between Gender and Vocabulary Teaching Methodology among Iranian EFL Children: A Comparison of TPR and Direct Method. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.1p.60

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test on pretest and posttest scores of the 20-student TPR group in a quasi-experimental study; Table 1 reports pretest M = 10.10 and posttest M = 15.30 (SD = 2.732 and 2.203). The article reports a statistically significant increase, "statistically significant ( P < .05) for the TPR group".

> "The results of the paired sample t-test in Table 4.2 reveal that the differences between pretest and posttest scores are statistically significant ( P < .05) for the TPR group."
