## Evidence

### Paolo Boffi 2026

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Four-week high-school deployment of Physics Playground with 74 students randomized at class level into IVR, DVR, and control. All conditions improved from pre- to post-test (t=−9.6, p<.001), but the article reports "without differences between conditions in the post-test".

> "Post-test scores were significantly higher than pre-test ones for all conditions (Student's t-test,t=−9.6,p < .001), without differences between conditions in the post-test (Welch's Anova,F= 1.14,p=.328)"

### Paolo Boffi 2026 (2)

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RHOME-VR study (161 participants), overall learning did not differ by condition; Mann-Whitney tests against the slideshow control were not significant for either IVR or DVR, and a Welch's ANOVA between IVR and DVR was also non-significant.

> "Mann-Whitney tests against control were not significant (IVR vs control:U= 1254.5,p=.16; DVR vs control:U= 1199,p=.25)"
