`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face-validity assessment in which the authors surveyed introductory physics textbooks and used the initial NoNIP to categorize all instances of negative-sign use. The article reports "all uses of the nega- tive sign could be categorized satisfactorily" with the noted exception of negative exponents; a Negative Exponents subcategory was added in the revised version.

> "With one notable excep- tion (negative exponents), we found that all uses of the nega- tive sign could be categorized satisfactorily using the NoNIP."
