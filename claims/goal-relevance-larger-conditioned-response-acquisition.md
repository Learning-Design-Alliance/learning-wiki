## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Conditioned stimuli made goal-relevant in a spatial cueing task elicited a larger differential conditioned response than goal-irrelevant stimuli during acquisition (η2p = 0.072). [→ Yoann Stussi 2019](#yoann-stussi-2019)

## Evidence

### Yoann Stussi 2019

Yoann Stussi, Aude Ferrero, Gilles Pourtois and David Sander. (2019). Achievement motivation modulates Pavlovian aversive conditioning to goal-relevant stimuli. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0043-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

GLM analysis of the acquisition phase of a differential Pavlovian aversive conditioning experiment in 72 participants, with SCR-indexed conditioned response. The contrast showed "a larger CR than goal-irrelevant stimuli", F(1, 71) = 5.49, with printed effect size η2p = 0.072; means were descriptive.

> "A planned contrast analysis showed that goal-relevant valid (contrast weight: +1) and goal-relevant invalid (contrast weight: +1) stimuli ( M = 0.11, SD = 0.15) led to the acquisition of a larger CR than goal-irrelevant stimuli (contrast weight: −2; M = 0.04, SD = 0.23), F(1, 71) = 5.49, p = 0.022, η2p = 0.072"
