# Guardrails in the GPT Tutor largely eliminate the negative exam effect, though no positive exam effect is observed

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` GPT Tutor students' unassisted exam performance was statistically indistinguishable from control (point estimate −0.004), so the guardrails mitigated but did not reverse the harm into a benefit. [→ Bastani Hamsa 2025](#bastani-hamsa-2025)

## Evidence

### Bastani Hamsa 2025

Bastani Hamsa, Bastani Osbert, Sungu Alp, Ge Haosen, Kabakcı Özge, Mariman Rei. (2025). Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Intention-to-treat regression on unassisted exam performance; the GPT Tutor coefficient was −0.004 and not statistically significant. The article states "this negative effect is essentially eradicated in the GPT Tutor arm, though we still do not observe a positive effect."

> "Student performance in the GPT Tutor arm was statistically indistinguishable from that of the control arm, and the point estimate was smaller by an order of magnitude (−0.004)."
