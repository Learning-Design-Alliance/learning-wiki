## Subclaims
`q3 i?` With concurrent shared variance between emotions controlled, only anxiety showed reciprocal relations with mathematics achievement; enjoyment and boredom did not predict subsequent performance. [→ Putwain 2021](#putwain-2021)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2021

Putwain, D. W., Wood, P., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Achievement emotions and academic achievement: Reciprocal relations and the moderating influence of academic buoyancy. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000637

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the simultaneous three-emotion SEM, "reciprocal relations with mathematics achievement were only shown for anxiety"; the authors attribute this partly to intercorrelations among emotions reducing unique variance for enjoyment and boredom.

> "When the variance shared between emotions measured concurrently was controlled for in this way, reciprocal relations with mathematics achievement were only shown for anxiety."
