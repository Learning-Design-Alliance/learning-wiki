Scoping review following PRISMA-ScR guidelines, synthesizing 37 included studies. The review reports the seven application domains with their occurrence counts, stating "Dental examinations (n = 24)" as the most frequent application of LLMs in dental education.

> "This review identified seven major application scenarios of LLMs in dental education, as summarized in Table 3: (1) Dental examinations (n = 24); (2) Educational information resources (n = 5); (3) perception of LLMs in dental education (n = 4); (4) assessment of students' assignments (n = 3); (5) preclinical support (n = 3); (6) generation of test questions (n = 2); (7) virtual patients (n = 1)"
