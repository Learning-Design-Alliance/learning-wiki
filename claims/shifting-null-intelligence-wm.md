## Evidence

### Ger Ebru 2023

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analyses in the N = 147 sample found no associations between shifting measures (mixed-block accuracy, shift cost) and intelligence or either WM component. The shift cost itself was not significantly different from zero (p = 1), consistent with Roebers (2022) finding no shift cost in 6-year-olds.

> "neither accuracy in the mixed block of HF nor the shift cost (i.e., time taken to shift between rules) was associated with either intelligence or WM (all ps > .181)"
