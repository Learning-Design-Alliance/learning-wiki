## Evidence

### Dehghani Ali 2024

Dehghani Ali, Fakhravari Fariba, Hojat Mohsen. (2024). The Effect of the Team Members Teaching Design vs. Regular Lectures method on the Self-efficacy of the Multiple sclerosis Patients in Iran. Randomised Controlled Trial. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Double-blind randomized controlled trial of 48 MS patients randomized to TMTD (n=16), regular lecture (n=16), or control (n=16). Repeated measures analysis across three time points showed "a significant increase compared to before the intervention" in both intervention groups, with no change in controls (p<0.05 reported in the abstract).

> "The results of the repeated measurements showed that the mean of the self-efficacy and its dimensions in both TMTD and regular lectures methods immediately and one month after the intervention had a significant increase compared to before the intervention, while no such change was observed in the control group"
