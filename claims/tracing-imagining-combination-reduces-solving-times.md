The review cites a recent study (Wang et al., 2022) combining a minor motor activity with a mental activity during worked-example-based mathematics learning. The combination reduced problem-solving times versus no tracing/imagining; no effect size is printed.

> "the combination of tracing for a first set of problems and tracing with closed eyes (i.e., imagining) for the rest of the task can reduce problem-solving times compared to not using tracing and imagining during the acquisition of knowledge using worked examples in the context of mathematics."
