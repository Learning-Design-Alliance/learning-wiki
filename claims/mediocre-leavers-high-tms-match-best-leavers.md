### Kadmon Guni 2016

Kadmon Guni, Kadmon Martina. (2016). Academic Performance of Students with the Highest and Mediocre School-leaving Grades: Does the Aptitude Test for Medical Studies (TMS) Balance Their Prognoses? GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(1):Doc7. [doi:10.3205/zma001006](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001006)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 3 compared performance by TMS categories across both groups (boxplots in Figure 2 with Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney post-hoc tests); mediocre leavers with TMS ≥125 matched the best leavers, though the authors warn small subgroup sizes may have masked true differences by type II error.

> "A similar level of academic performance was achieved by the mediocre school leavers with TMS scores ≥125. Their pre-clinical grades did not differ significantly from those of the best school leavers including those with TMS scores ≥125."
