### Esse Ciego 2026

Esse Ciego, Shubbhi Taneja, Wilson Wong, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Student Perceptions and Preferences Regarding AI-Generated Instructional Videos in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28203

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-survey item administered after perception ratings, with students not told a priori that the videos were AI-generated. The item showed "the highest variance of any item" (𝜇= 3.31, 𝜎=1.36), with responses split across agreement levels.

> "detectability was the only item without consensus: just half of students (50%) agreed they could clearly tell the videos were AI-generated, while 35% disagreed and 15% were neutral (𝜇= 3.31, 𝜎=1.36) — the highest variance of any item."
