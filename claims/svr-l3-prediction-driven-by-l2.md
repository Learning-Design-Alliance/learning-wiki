## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In tenfold cross-validated SVR, L2-only prediction of L3 Global scores was significant (mean cc = 0.278, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 5.22), while L1-only prediction was much weaker (mean cc = 0.052, Cohen's d = 0.96). [→ Xin Kang 2021](#xin-kang-2021)

## Evidence

### Xin Kang 2021

Xin Kang, Stephen Matthews, Virginia Yip and Patrick C. M. Wong. (2021). Language and nonlanguage factors in foreign language learning: evidence for the learning condition hypothesis. npj Science of Learning 6:28. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00104-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Support vector regression with tenfold cross-validation and 10,000 iterations predicted L3 Global scores. All-predictor and L2-only models significantly outperformed null; the L1-only model's effect was at least five times smaller, converging with LCH.

> "from the null distribution (mean = −0.001, SD = 0.065, p < 0.001), but the effect size (Cohen ’s d = 0.96) was at least ﬁve times smaller than in the models with all predictors (Cohen ’s d = 6.39) or only the L2 (English) HKDSE grades (Cohen ’s d = 5.22) as predictors."
