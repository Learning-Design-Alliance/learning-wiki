## Evidence

### Singaram Veena S. 2010

Singaram Veena S., van der Vleuten Cees P. M., Stevens Fred, Dolmans Diana H. J. M. (2010). “For most of us Africans, we don’t just speak”: a qualitative investigation into collaborative heterogeneous PBL group learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9262-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis of student perceptions: students "segregate because all students from the same culture stuck together"; one student observed that white, Indian and black students each sat together and did not integrate despite expectations.

> "Students within the tutorial groups segregate because all students from the same culture stuck together, perhaps due to psychological divisions and past prejudices. Polarizing around the tutorial table along racial lines decreases group morale."
