Evidence¶

Nadja Lindner 2025¶

Nadja Lindner, Korbinian Moeller, Frauke Hildebrandt, Marcus Hasselhorn, Jan Lonnemann. (2025). Training children's spatial language skills improves their numerical magnitude understanding. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102191. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102191

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Additional robust one-way ANCOVA (10% trimmed means) comparing group improvement difference scores with age as covariate. The article reports effects "tended to show up in the younger rather than the older children"; detailed results are hosted on OSF.

"However, for both comprehension of spatial language and verbal number skills, there is evidence of an influence of age suggesting that for these two variables, effects of training tended to show up in the younger rather than the older children"
