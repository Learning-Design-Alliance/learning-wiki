`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of five focus groups with nineteen Dutch educational professionals, coded against the COPES model with strong interrater agreement (the article reports "a Kappa of 0.84"). Task definition codes received zero mentions while goal setting, enactment, and adaptation themes were discussed extensively.

> "Notably, the initial phase, 'task definition,' was absent in all focus groups, suggesting potential oversight or minimal emphasis on this aspect."
