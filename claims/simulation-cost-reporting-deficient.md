Bugaj T. J., Nikendei C. (2016). Practical Clinical Training in Skills Labs: Theory and Practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(4) Doc63. [doi:10.3205/zma001062](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001062)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Zendejas et al.'s 2013 systematic review: of 967 identified comparative studies, only 6.1% mentioned intervention cost, with a median of two reported cost factors against 21 identified as relevant, and only 1.6% compared costs across training forms.

> "The same meta-analysis from 2013 shows that from a total of 967 identified comparative studies only a fraction (6.1%) even mention the cost of the underlying intervention, with a median of two reported cost factors, although the meta-analysis' authors identify 21 relevant factors."
