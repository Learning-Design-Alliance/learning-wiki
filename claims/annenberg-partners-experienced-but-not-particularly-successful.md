## Evidence

### Karin Sconzert 2004

Karin Sconzert, Mark A. Smylie, & Stacy A. Wenzel. (2004). Working for School Improvement: Reflections of Chicago Annenberg External Partners. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/working-school-improvement-reflections-chicago-annenberg-external-partners

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Multi-year qualitative study sampling 19 external partners interviewed in 1997, 1999, and 2001. The report notes the sample accumulated "some 210 years of experience working with schools to help them improve."

> "As a group of 45, across the six years of the Challenge, Annenberg partners accumulated some 210 years of experience working with schools to help them improve."

### Karin Sconzert 2004 (2)

Karin Sconzert, Mark A. Smylie, & Stacy A. Wenzel. (2004). Working for School Improvement: Reflections of Chicago Annenberg External Partners. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/working-school-improvement-reflections-chicago-annenberg-external-partners

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The report attributes the overall null pattern to other reports of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project, not to its own outcome analysis; it treats the partners' experiences as instructive despite limited success.

> "While these other reports indicate that as a whole Annenberg’s external partners were not particularly successful in promoting improvement across the large number of schools, it is nevertheless instructive to look to them to learn from their experiences"
