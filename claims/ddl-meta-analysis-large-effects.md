## Evidence

### Rasikawati 2019

Ira Rasikawati (2019). Corpus-Based Data-Driven Learning to Augment L2 Students’ Vocabulary Repertoire. International Dialogues on Education, 6(2), 83-98. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1245249

`q3 · i3` · `review · r2`

The article reports the Boulton & Cobb (2017) meta-analysis, the first on corpus use in language learning, drawing on 64 meta-analyzable studies with over 3,000 participants. It found "large overall effects for control/experimental group comparisons" with d = 0.95 and d = 1.50 for pre/post designs.

> "The result of 64 meta -analyzable studies from a pool of 205 studies showed large overall effects for control/experimental group comparisons ( d = 0.95) and for pre/posttest designs ( d = 1.50)"
