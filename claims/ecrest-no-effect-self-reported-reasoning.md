# eCREST showed no significant effect on self-reported clinical reasoning skills (FIT scale) over time

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mixed factorial ANOVA found no significant effect of group allocation, time, or group × time interaction on self-reported clinical reasoning scores. [→ Plackett R 2020](#plackett-r-2020)

## Evidence

### Plackett R 2020

Plackett R, Kassianos AP, Kambouri M, et al. (2020). Online patient simulation training to improve clinical reasoning: a feasibility randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02168-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed factorial ANOVA on the Diagnostic Thinking Inventory FIT sub-scale in the feasibility RCT (n = 136): intervention means 84.1 vs 82.4 at Time 1 and 84.4 vs 82.0 at Time 2, all non-significant. "There was no significant effect of group allocation" and no interaction effect.

> "There was no significant effect of group allocation, (F (1)=0.00, p = 0.97, n = 136) time, (F (2)=0.01, p = 0.99, n = 136) or interaction between group allocation and time, F (2)=0.48, p = 0.62, n = 136."
