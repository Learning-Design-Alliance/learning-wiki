## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study (Hsiao et al. 2024, Northern Taiwan) of a 100-minute immersive cinematic escape room course integrating movie clips, escape-room puzzles, teamwork, and simulation-based activities, in 115 registered nurses (55 experimental, 60 control). The review reports "GEE interaction p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Experimental group showed significantly greater improvement in disaster preparedness over time compared to control (GEE interaction p < 0.05). Nurses without prior disaster training in the ICER group outperformed those with prior training. Self‐efficacy improved in both groups, with greater short‐term gains in the ICER group, though between‐group differences were not consistently statistically significant at follow‐up."
