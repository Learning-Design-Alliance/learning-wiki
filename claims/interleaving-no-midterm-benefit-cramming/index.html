Evidence¶

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021¶

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of two midterm exams in the same experiment, completed in individual and group stages with individual-stage data reported. The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

"On high-stakes midterm exams occurring 3 days after each criterial test, scores did not signiﬁcantly differ between the blocked and interleaved conditions (post-Stage 1 midterm, d = 0.20, 95% CI [ −0.04, 0.43], t(288) = 1.68, p = 0.0944), and post-Stage 2 midterm, d = 0.02, 95% CI [ −0.21, 0.25], t(284) = 0.16, p = 0.8758)."
