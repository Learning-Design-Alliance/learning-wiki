Related Claims¶

  • Many English-only students would fail locally determined cutoff scores often applied to English Learner students under California's Criterion 4 basic-skills requirement — related
  • ELPAC overall Performance Level 4 is the most robust indicator that language is no longer the primary driver of academic achievement differences among California's reclassification criteria — related
  • Educators in 2023-24 listening sessions repeatedly identified Criterion 4 as a barrier to reclassification that many English-only students could not meet — related
  • English Learner students at ELPAC PL 4 perform as well as, or better than, matched English-only peers on the state English language arts achievement test — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Students from socioeconomically disadvantaged backgrounds fail dual enrollment at higher rates than equally achieving peers, with the gap widening as achievement rises (the readiness illusion) — related
  • English learner students with disabilities are more likely to struggle academically and less likely to participate in activities than both other students with disabilities and other English learners — related
  • Low-income students, students of color, multilingual families' students, and students with disabilities are more likely to be chronically absent — related
