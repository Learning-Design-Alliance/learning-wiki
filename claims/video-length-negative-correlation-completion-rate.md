## Evidence

### Zheng Xie 2018

Zheng Xie, Xiao Xiao, Jianping Li, Jinying Su. (2018). Profiling MOOCs from viewing perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of viewing behavior data from eight iCourse courses (01/01/2017–10/11/2017), plotted in Figure 1, shows "Both C1 and C2 negatively correlate to average video length", attributed to limited human attention spans.

> "Both C1 and C2 negatively correlate to average video length (Fig. 1). In fact, human attention spans are limited. A long video is hard to attract learner attentions from beginning to end."
