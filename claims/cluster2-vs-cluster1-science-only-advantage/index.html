Giang Pham 2026¶

Giang Pham, Franziska Maria Locher, Dimitra Kolovou, Eliane Arnold. (2026). COVID-19: A cross-national analysis with PISA data. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102366. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102366

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Double-selection lasso regression contrasting Cluster 2 vs Cluster 1 (N = 47 countries) with covariate controls. The article reports "a statistically significant difference was observed only in science (approximately 20 PISA points, p = .002)"; no significant differences in the other two domains.

"Compared with countries characterised by more Family-Supported Asynchronous Virtual Instruction (Cluster 1), countries in Cluster 2 demonstrated higher student performance in 2022 across all domains. However, a statistically significant difference was observed only in science (approximately 20 PISA points, p = .002)"
