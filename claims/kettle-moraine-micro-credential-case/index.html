Barnett Berry and Karen Cator 2016¶

Barnett Berry and Karen Cator. (2016). Micro-credentials: Driving teacher learning & leadership. Center for Teaching Quality and Digital Promise. http://teachingquality.org/micro-credentials

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

A single-district case study of an early adopter, reported in the policy section. The district ties micro-credentials to a vision of "shared ownership of the responsibility of educating our children"; no comparative data or effect size is printed.

"For the past year, Kettle Moraine School District in Wisconsin has been implementing micro-credentials to provide educators with access to personalized learning opportunities and recognition. To sweeten the deal, the district provides educators with an increase in their base pay for every micro-credential they earn. The district has seen greater collaboration and collegiality within schools."
