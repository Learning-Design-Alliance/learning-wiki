## Evidence

### Li Xinyan 2025

Li Xinyan, Pei Xiaoge, Zhao Jing. (2025). Intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy as pathways to innovative teaching: a mixed-methods study of faculty in Chinese higher education. BMC Psychology, 13, 859. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03177-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis of 357 faculty surveys. The article reports controlled motivations "exhibited a small but significant direct positive effect on innovative teaching practices (β = 0.10, p < 0.05)"; the same model found no significant path from controlled motivations to self-efficacy (β = 0.08, p = 0.173).

> "Controlled motivations also exhibited a small but significant direct positive effect on innovative teaching practices (β = 0.10, p < 0.05), indicating that both direct and indirect pathways exist through which motivations influence innovative teaching practices."
