### Ostoich Katherine Connor 2025

Ostoich Katherine Connor, Kamrul Rejina, Clay Adam. (2025). Do doctors know that it takes more than an apple a day? Impact of formal nutrition training on family medicine residents’ nutrition knowledge, confidence, attitudes, and counselling abilities: a single site study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08113-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post evaluation of a 4-hour online course with SurveyMonkey knowledge tests (score out of 30; post-course median 25.7). The authors report a related samples Wilcoxon signed-rank test showing knowledge increased after the nutrition course (p = 0.027). No effect size is printed.
