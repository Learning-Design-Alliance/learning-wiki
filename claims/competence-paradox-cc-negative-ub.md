# The competence paradox: creative competence positively supports intention but shows a significant negative direct effect on actual use behavior

## Subclaims

### Liu Y 2026 (2)

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Explanatory qualitative follow-up interviews with 20 purposively selected students from anomalous survey profiles. Higher perceived-CC participants described T2I as a phased execution tool and tended "to conﬁne T2I to early ideation stages and low-risk tasks".

> "Among participants with higher perceived CC, stronger capability was not necessarily associated with greater reliance on T2I. Instead, it often coincided with more critical judgment,"
