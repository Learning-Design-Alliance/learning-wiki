## Evidence

### Gaylor et al. 2015

Erika Gaylor, Donna Spiker, Xin Wei, Erin Lease, Arthur Reynolds. (2015). Midwest Child-Parent Center (CPC) PreK-3rd Grade School Reform Model: Impacts on Child and Family Outcomes Over Time. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562345

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental study of approximately 1,000 children (n=1054, 72% intervention) with pretest and posttest data at kindergarten entry, using propensity score matching to find comparable schools. Preliminary findings from district-collected data on school readiness show that PreK is linked to higher mastery across all domains.

> "Preliminary findings from district-collected data on school readiness show that PreK is linked to higher mastery across all domains (e.g, literacy, math) compared to a subset of children in the comparison preschool settings."
