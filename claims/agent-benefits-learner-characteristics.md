## Evidence

### Kim 2015

Kim, Y., & Baylor, A. L. (2015). Research-Based Design of Pedagogical Agent Roles: a Review, Progress, and Recommendations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0055-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Kim and Lim (2013), that middle-grade females and ethnic minorities increased learning significantly after working with an agent compared to white males; a meta-analysis it cites found agent presence more positive for K-12 than college students. No effect sizes printed here.

> "Middle grade females and ethnic minorities expressed their comfort in learning from an agent, improved their self-efficacy in learning algebraic concepts and positive attitudes, and increased learning significantly after working with the agent, compared to white males"
