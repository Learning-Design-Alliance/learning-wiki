# Reducing Likert answer choices from five to three categories improved Rasch threshold ordering in the IGCQ

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The initial 5-point answer scale showed disordered thresholds, and merging categories to three produced improved threshold order and acceptable fit statistics. [→ Horton William B. 2019](#horton-william-b-2019)
`q2 i?` Prospective testing of the 3-category scale in a new cohort showed no disordered thresholds, confirming three categories as the better scale. [→ Horton William B. 2019 (2)](#horton-william-b-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Horton William B. 2019

Horton William B., Law Sidney, Darji Monika, Conaway Mark R., Kubiak Nancy T., Kirby Jennifer L., Thigpen S. Calvin. (2019). Construction and preliminary evaluation of the inpatient glycemic control questionnaire (IGCQ): a survey tool assessing perceptions and knowledge of resident physicians. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1657-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Rasch Partial Credit Model analysis of pilot study (phase 2) data from 182 residents at three centers. The article reports that "RPCM analysis demonstrated disordered thresholds for several items using the initial 5-point answer choice scale" (Fig. 2).

> "RPCM analysis demonstrated disordered thresholds for several items using the initial 5-point answer choice scale"

### Horton William B. 2019 (2)

Horton William B., Law Sidney, Darji Monika, Conaway Mark R., Kubiak Nancy T., Kirby Jennifer L., Thigpen S. Calvin. (2019). Construction and preliminary evaluation of the inpatient glycemic control questionnaire (IGCQ): a survey tool assessing perceptions and knowledge of resident physicians. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1657-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Prospective phase 3 administration at University of Louisville to 64 residents. "RPCM was then applied to UL cohort data and revealed no disordered thresholds", confirming the 3-choice scale for non-knowledge questions.

> "For prospective evaluation of the revised IGCQ, the survey was administered to 64 resident physicians at UL. RPCM was then applied to UL cohort data and revealed no disordered thresholds"
