## Evidence

### Gerardi Davide 2025

Gerardi Davide, Torge Diana, Bernardi Sara, Bianchi Serena, Macchiarelli Guido. (2025). Use of two virtual dissection tables in a postgraduate oral anatomy course: feedback from the students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07840-1

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' interpretive argument in the Discussion, contrasting VDT practice with cadaver dissection where trainees fear irreversibly damaging the specimen. The advantage is the student's awareness that they "can make errors without consequences".

> "Digital simulators using VDT, on the other hand, allow students to practice specific procedures by simulating them with the possibility of making mistakes, recognizing them, and quickly reverting virtually to repeat the practical activity."
