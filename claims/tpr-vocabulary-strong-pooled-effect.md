## Evidence

### Tugba Inciman Celik 2021

Tugba Inciman Celik, Tolga Cay & Sedat Kanadli. (2021). The Effect of Total Physical Response Method on Vocabulary Learning/Teaching: A Mixed Research Synthesis. English Language Teaching, 14(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v14n12p154

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 13 quasi-experimental studies (2008-2020) comparing TPR-organized lessons with traditional methods on vocabulary achievement, combined under a random effects model. The pooled ES was "1.131 (95% CI: 0.737 to 1.525)", significant at z=5.62, p<.01, and classified as strong by the authors' Cohen et al. (2007) rubric.

> "The estimated common effect size based on the random-effects model was 1.131 (95% CI: 0.737 to 1.525). As seen in the figure 2 the common effect size differed  significantly from zero (z=5.62, p<.01)."
