## Evidence

### Leyva 2014

Leyva, D., Yoshikawa, H., Snow, C. E., Treviño, E., Rolla, A., Barata, M. C., & Weiland, C. (2014). Can we improve preschool classroom quality in Chile? A cluster-randomized trial evaluation of a professional development program. SREE Spring 2014 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562728

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial with schools randomly assigned within municipality; multi-level models accounted for nesting of classrooms within schools. The printed effect size for Emotional Support was 0.81, with Full UBC classrooms "scored 0.81 of a standard deviation higher than Comparison classrooms on Emotional Support" (p < .05), and moderate effect sizes of 0.45 and 0.43 for Classroom Organization and Instructional Support (the latter p < .10).

> "One effect size was in the large range – Full UBC classrooms scored 0.81 of a standard deviation higher than Comparison classrooms on Emotional Support. Effect sizes for Classroom Organization and Instructional Support were in the moderate range (0.45 and 0.43 respectively)."
