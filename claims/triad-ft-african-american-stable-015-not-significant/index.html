Subgroup HLM estimates from Table 2: FT vs CTRL for African-Americans was .150 (p=.46) in Fall 2011 and .149 (p=.28) in Spring 2012. The article describes the effect as "about 0.15 of a standard deviation" but not statistically significant at either time point; the printed magnitude 0.15 is below d = 0.2.

"For African-Americans, the effect of FT seems to be stable in both Fall 2011 (fall of 4th grade) and Spring 2012 – about 0.15 of a standard deviation but it is not statistically significant at either time point."
