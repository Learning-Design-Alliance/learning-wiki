# Pre-service clinical teachers who viewed videotapes of their teaching with guided feedback provided more total feedback to students than teachers in a seminar-only group and a control group across eight teaching episodes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group One (seminar and video) scored significantly higher on total feedback than Groups Two and Three, with significant ANOVA results for total feedback and every weekly teaching episode. [→ Andrews 2011](#andrews-2011)
`q2 i?` Pairwise comparisons showed Group One differed from both Group Two and Group Three on total feedback and all eight episodes, with larger mean differences versus the control group. [→ Andrews 2011 (2)](#andrews-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Andrews 2011

Andrews, A., Bobo, L., & Spurlock, A. (2011). Use of video feedback in the training of pre-service teachers. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. (no URL printed in the article text). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056349

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized three-group experiment with 30 pre-service athletic training teachers videotaped over eight weekly 20-minute clinical teaching episodes; one-way ANOVAs with Tukey HSD compared groups on total feedback. The article reports a statistically significant group difference in total feedback score (F = 28.046, p < .001) and for each episode, e.g. "first ( F = 4.644, p < .05)".

> "When comparing differences among the groups, statistically significant results were noted in total feedback score ( F = 28.046, p < .001), and total feedback for each episode including first ( F = 4.644, p < .05)"

### Andrews 2011 (2)

Andrews, A., Bobo, L., & Spurlock, A. (2011). Use of video feedback in the training of pre-service teachers. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. (no URL printed in the article text). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056349

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise Tukey comparisons among the three randomized groups in the same eight-week experiment, reported in Results and Table 3 with mean differences (e.g. total feedback One and Three 92.60, p < .001). The article notes "Group One to Group Two had lower mean differences than Group One did to Group Three".

> "Examining pairwise differences among the groups for total feedback time found that Group One to Group Two had lower mean differences than Group One did to Group Three for every teaching episode except for episode four."
