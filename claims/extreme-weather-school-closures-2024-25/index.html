The report documents recent U.S. school closures from extreme weather, stating that "hurricanes closed schools for weeks in 20 districts in North Carolina" and that over 1,000 Southern California schools closed for multiple days due to wildfires. It adds that many districts delayed their first day of school due to extreme heat and over 1,000 more schools closed for other extreme weather events.

"In the 2024-25 school year, hurricanes closed schools for weeks in 20 districts in North Carolina, while over 1,000 schools in four counties in Southern California were closed for multiple days due to wildfires."
