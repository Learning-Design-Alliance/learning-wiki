Demirbag 2014¶

Demirbag, M., & Gunel, M. (2014). Integrating Argument-Based Science Inquiry with Modal Representations: Impact on Science Achievement, Argumentation, and Writing Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(1), 386-391. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.1.1632

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on scored ABSI activity reports (gravity, heat and temperature, cohesion of liquids, electricity, buoyancy), rated by a faculty member and seven graduate students with 90% to 95% absolute inter-rater agreement; "the treatment group outscored the comparison group".

"ANOV A results yielded that there were statistical performance differences between the groups on activity reports scores (F(1,116)=19.16 p<0.05) where the treatment group outscored the comparison group."
