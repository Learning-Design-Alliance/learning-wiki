# The main dropout-contagion findings remained essentially unchanged across five robustness checks including unmatched samples, IPTW, and conditional regression

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Rerunning the regression models on the full sample including unmatched controls and on exposures after the risk index yielded stronger effect sizes, consistent with matching yielding conservative estimates. [→ Dupéré 2021](#dupere-2021)

## Evidence

### Dupéré 2021

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness tests reran the regressions on the full sample (N = 545) including unmatched controls, and on exposures occurring after administration of the risk index to probe reverse causality. The article reports "the effect sizes and significance levels tended to be stronger, a result consistent with scholarship showing that matching tends to yield conservative estimates".

> "In both tests, the effect sizes and significance levels tended to be stronger, a result consistent with scholarship showing that matching tends to yield conservative estimates"

### Dupéré 2021 (2)

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alternative confounder-handling specifications — risk-index items entered separately and inverse probability of treatment weighting, after which exposed and non-exposed participants were successfully rebalanced — left the main findings stable; conditional logistic regression also left "the main findings were essentially unaffected".

> "With both tests, the main findings were essentially unaffected."
