## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-lagged paths connecting prior mathematics achievement to subsequent intrinsic motivation were small but statistically significant from grades 1 to 2 and from grades 2 to 4. [→ Garon‐Carrier 2016](#garoncarrier-2016)

## Evidence

### Garon‐Carrier 2016

Garon‐Carrier, G., Boivin, M., Guay, F., Kovas, Y., Dionne, G., Lemelin, J. P., ... & Tremblay, R. E. (2016). Intrinsic Motivation and Achievement in Mathematics in Elementary School: A Longitudinal Investigation of their Association. Child Development, 87(1), 165-175. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12458

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged structural equation modeling of the Quebec Longitudinal Study of Child Development cohort (N between 1323 and 1478) across grades 1, 2, and 4, controlling for non-verbal cognitive abilities, showed "small, but significant cross-lagged paths connecting prior achievement to subsequent intrinsic motivation."

> "The non-constrained model showed small, but significant cross-lagged paths connecting prior achievement to subsequent intrinsic motivation."
