### Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The synthesis argues the learning process needs to be "productively diﬃcult", comparing learning to deliberate sports training, and reports that edge-of-mastery work with high expectations supports deep learning.

> "Having students work at the edge of their mastery while maintaining high expectations pushes them past their current abilities, engages the brain deeply, and lays the foundation for strong learning."

### Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019 (2)

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The synthesis reports that reframing failure as information about what students "do not know" supports perseverance, and recommends process-focused feedback and growth-mindset reminders.

> "When students see failure as an opportunity to ﬁnd out what they do not know (and adjust their learning strategies accordingly), rather than as an indication of self-worth, they are more likely to persevere."
