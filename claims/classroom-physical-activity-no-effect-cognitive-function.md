## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2017

Watson Amanda, Timperio Anna, Brown Helen, Best Keren, Hesketh Kylie D. (2017). Effect of classroom-based physical activity interventions on academic and physical activity outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-017-0569-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis combining five acute and chronic studies (fewer than five reported chronic effects, so results were combined). The review reports "no effect on cognitive function" with SMD = 0.33 (95% CI: -0.11,0.77): the pooled estimate is positive but its interval includes zero, so the result is non-significant and inconclusive rather than evidence of no effect. Across the narrative review the effects were inconsistent: active breaks had an acute positive effect on selective attention in 3 of 4 studies, while other attention, executive-function and fluid-intelligence outcomes showed no or inconsistent effects. The authors note that most included studies were of low methodological quality.

> "Results from the meta-analysis indicate classroom-based physical activity had no effect on cognitive function (standardised mean difference = 0.33 (95% CI: -0.11,0.77)"
