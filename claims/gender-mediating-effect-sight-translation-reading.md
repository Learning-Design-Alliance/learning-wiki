## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The article states the female group's mean posttest reading ability was significantly higher than the male group's, interpreting this as gender slightly modifying the effect of sight translation. [→ Fatollahi 2016](#fatollahi-2016)

## Evidence

### Fatollahi 2016

Moslem Fatollahi. (2016). Applying Sight Translation as a Means to Enhance Reading Ability of Iranian EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p153

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
