### Rees Charlotte E. 2019

Rees Charlotte E., Lee Sarah L., Huang Eve, Denniston Charlotte, Edouard Vicki, Pope Kirsty, Sutton Keith, Waller Susan, Ward Bernadette, Palermo Claire. (2019). Supervision training in healthcare: a realist synthesis. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09937-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

CMOC extracted from Eckstrom et al. (2006), a qualitative quasi-experimental longitudinal pre/post-test study of 24 experimental and 44 control internal medicine preceptors; preceptors "fell back to their previous patterns of supervisory behaviours" without reinforcement.

> "Internal medical preceptors [C] who have participated in the 1-min preceptor (OMP) workshop [I] fell back to their previous patterns of supervisory behaviours [− O] if their new skills were not reinforced [− M]"

### Rees Charlotte E. 2019 (2)

Rees Charlotte E., Lee Sarah L., Huang Eve, Denniston Charlotte, Edouard Vicki, Pope Kirsty, Sutton Keith, Waller Susan, Ward Bernadette, Palermo Claire. (2019). Supervision training in healthcare: a realist synthesis. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09937-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

CMOC extracted from Sandau and Halm (2011), a mixed-methods study of a mandatory 8-h preceptor workshop in a US hospital; orientees experienced negative orientation because preceptors lacked protected supervision time.

> "Nurses from a large hospital [C] who participated in a mandatory 8-h preceptor workshop [I] led to nursing orientees experiencing negative orientation [− O] because their preceptors did not have protected supervision time [− M]"
