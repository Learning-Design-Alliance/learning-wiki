## Evidence

### Lockie R.G. 2019

Lockie R.G., Balfany K., Bloodgood A.M., Moreno M.R., Cesario K.A., Dulla J.M., Dawes J.J., Orr R.M. (2019). The Influence of Physical Fitness on Reasons for Academy Separation in Law Enforcement Recruits. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16030372

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc across five academy classes (330 GRAD, 28 SEPPR, 18 SEPFI). Table 1 prints MSFT shuttles of 52.75 ± 16.69 for GRAD versus 41.54 ± 10.74 (SEPPR) and 39.94 ± 13.03 (SEPFI); Table 2 prints effect sizes of 0.80 and 0.86.

> "The GRAD group also completed significantly more MSFT shuttles than the SEPPR and SEPFI groups (both p = 0.01 with moderate effects)."
