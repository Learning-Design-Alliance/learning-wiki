Descriptive analysis of timed suture-ligation attempts among 28 trainees in the multi-institution training program. The article reports "25trainees (89%) achieved an RTR of ≥ 20%" and a median reduction time rate of 57%.

> "The median suture ligation time was 145 s (range: 41–388 s) for the first attempt, 77 s (43–172 s) for the second attempt, and 51 s (33–105 s) for the third attempt (Table 1)."
