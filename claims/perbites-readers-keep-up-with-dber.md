Nicholas T. Young, Briley L. Lewis, Emily Kerr, Prasanth H. Nair. (2022). Using blogs to make peer-reviewed research more accessible. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.young_n](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.young_n)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A 23-item website survey of PERbites readers; of 33 people who agreed to participate, 24 completed it, and the authors report only raw numbers. "all participants agreed or strongly agreed" on the first two dissemination questions; on the third, 5 of 24 did not agree.

> "For the ﬁrst two questions, all participants agreed or strongly agreed that PERbites helps them keep up with DBER studies and that PERbites allows them to read about papers they otherwise would not have."
