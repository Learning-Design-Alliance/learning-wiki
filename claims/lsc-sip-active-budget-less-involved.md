# Most LSCs are active in School Improvement Plan development, but fewer than a quarter are highly involved in the budget process

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` More than half of LSC members report being active or very active in school improvement planning, while about 10 percent report their LSC is inactive in the SIP process. [→ Susan Ryan 1997](#susan-ryan-1997)
`q2 i?` Less than a quarter of members report their council is highly involved in the budget, and about a fifth report minimal involvement or no involvement. [→ Susan Ryan 1997 (2)](#susan-ryan-1997-2)

## Evidence

### Susan Ryan 1997

Susan Ryan, Anthony S. Bryk, Gudelia Lopez, Kimberley P. Williams, Kathleen Hall, Stuart Luppescu. (1997). Charting Reform: LSCs - Local Leadership at Work. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-lscs%E2%80%94local-leadership-work

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of six questions on the School Improvement Plan (Tables 22-23): active members assist in developing the plan, hold a community forum, approve it, and monitor implementation; 14 percent very active, 41 percent active, about a third somewhat active, 10 percent inactive.

> "More than half of the LSC members report that they are active or very active in the school improve- ment planning process (see Table 22)."

### Susan Ryan 1997 (2)

Susan Ryan, Anthony S. Bryk, Gudelia Lopez, Kimberley P. Williams, Kathleen Hall, Stuart Luppescu. (1997). Charting Reform: LSCs - Local Leadership at Work. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-lscs%E2%80%94local-leadership-work

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey on budget involvement (Tables 24-25): nearly 60 percent report moderate involvement, while about a fifth report minimal involvement or no involvement; these councils largely defer budget setting and monitoring to the principal.

> "When we asked members about the budget process, less than a quar- ter report that their council is highly involved (see Table 24)."
