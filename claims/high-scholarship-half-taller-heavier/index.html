Stewart 1917¶

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparison of upper-four and lower-four scholarship groups within eight boys who entered high school in September 1910 and graduated in June 1914 (Chart VII), the largest same-age same-class group with complete measurements. The higher-scholarship half "averaged taller" all four years.

"Throughout the four years the half which ranked higher in scholarship also averaged taller. And, except in the Junior year, they also averaged heavier."
