## Evidence

### Wang Ting-Huai 2025

Wang Ting-Huai, Xu Xiao-Dan, Long Zhi-Yuan, Cao Ying-Nan, Lai Ning-Yi, Ma Yong-Hong, Tan Xiao-Qiu, Xin Min, Jiang Jin-Yan, Yang Da-Ya. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post quiz comparison within the interprofessional OFST course (n = 149 compliant responses), comparing identical knowledge points after self-regulated learning (Quiz 1) and after team learning (Quiz 2) via chi-square test. The article reports the correct rate "increased from 48.3% (72/149) in Quiz 1 to 80.5% (120/149) in Quiz 2".

> "For fundamental questions, such as those related to “factors influencing coronary blood flow,” the correct rate increased from 48.3% (72/149) in Quiz 1 to 80.5% (120/149) in Quiz 2 (χ² = 33.74, P < 0.001)"
