`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sequence analysis of Explorers' ordered response languages identified 10 distinct switching trajectories; Table 1 reports start-to-end transitions, showing "more learners began the course using English than Leb-Lango (63% vs. 37%)".

> "more learners began the course using English than Leb-Lango (63% vs. 37%) and ended the course using English than Leb-Lango (68% vs. 32%)."

### Christine Kwon 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Step-level binary switch indicators summarized across Explorers, shown in Figure 2(A): switching was "highest at the introductory step (33.4%)" and declined to 7.9% at Step 5, suggesting early experimentation then stabilization.

> "Switching was highest at the introductory step (33.4%) and remained relatively high at Step 1 (33.1%). After Step 2, the proportion of learners switching languages decreased, reaching a low of 7.9% at Step 5."
