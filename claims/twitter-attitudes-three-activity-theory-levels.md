# Students in the Twitter-supported blended course reported positive attitudes at the technological, individual, and community levels of activity theory

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the technological level, experimental students found the blended process easy to use and useful in their academic life. [→ Amry 2018](#amry-2018)
`q2 i?` At the individual level, students viewed Twitter as an integrated system for sharing instructional information, and at the community level they reported high social presence and favorable social interaction. [→ Amry 2018 (2)](#amry-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Amry 2018

Aicha Amry. (2018). The Effect of Twitter Activities in a Blended Learning Classroom Guided by Activity Theory on Students' Achievement and Attitudes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176184

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive questionnaire results from the experimental group (N = 36) organized by the article's three questionnaire levels. The article reports positive attitudes at the technological level, including that Twitter gives students opportunities to communicate with the learning community.

> "Based on the results of the technological level, the blended learning process (70% in person + 30% Twitter learning activities) is easy to use."

### Amry 2018 (2)

Aicha Amry. (2018). The Effect of Twitter Activities in a Blended Learning Classroom Guided by Activity Theory on Students' Achievement and Attitudes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176184

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Community-level questionnaire results reported for the experimental group: students believed good social relations positively affect social network use and that they could participate without the supervisor's presence while respecting roles and rules.

> "Based on students' experience in the use of Twitter in education, students believe that there is a high standard of social presence, and they believe that this application further strengthens social interaction in the educational group."
