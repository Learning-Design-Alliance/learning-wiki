# In the review's coding, equal total and per-condition sample sizes signalled either a correlational study or a repeated-measures experiment, and repeated measures were coded when a pretest entered analysis or a within-subjects design was used

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r?` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Equal total and per-condition sample size indicated a correlational study or a repeated-measures design. [→ Allison Zengilowski 2020](#allison-zengilowski-2020)
`q1 i?` Repeated measures were coded when researchers incorporated a pretest into post-test analyses or used within-subjects designs; one study's GPA covariate was coded similarly despite no pretest. [→ Allison Zengilowski 2020 (2)](#allison-zengilowski-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Allison Zengilowski 2020

Allison Zengilowski, Brendan Schuetze, Brady Nash, Diane Schallert. (2020). Coding Notes for “A Critical Review of the Refutation Text Literature: Methodological Confounds, Theoretical Problems, and Possible Solutions”. Version 2. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22A+Critical+Review+of+the+Refutation+Text+Literature%22

`q1 · i?` · `review · r?`

A coding convention stated in the notes: equal total and per-condition sample sizes "represents either a correlational study" or a repeated-measures experiment in which participants experienced both conditions.

> "If total sample size is equal to sample size per condition, then that represents either a correlational study, where no experimental treatment was assigned, or a repeated-measures experiment where participants experienced both treatment and control conditions."

### Allison Zengilowski 2020 (2)

Allison Zengilowski, Brendan Schuetze, Brady Nash, Diane Schallert. (2020). Coding Notes for “A Critical Review of the Refutation Text Literature: Methodological Confounds, Theoretical Problems, and Possible Solutions”. Version 2. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22A+Critical+Review+of+the+Refutation+Text+Literature%22

`q1 · i?` · `review · r?`

The notes define repeated-measures coding as applying when a pretest entered analysis of post-test differences or a within-subjects design was used, with the Diakidoy et al. (2003) GPA covariate as a coded exception.

> "For one study (Diakidoy et al., 2003), GPA was used similarly as a covariate, and thus was coded as using repeated measures despite not including a pretest in their design."
