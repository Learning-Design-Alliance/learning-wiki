# Latent mean comparisons show only partial grade-level development in CPS, with Grade 9 students performing worst on both dimensions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Performance on knowledge acquisition and knowledge application did not increase consistently across grades; Grade Level 9 performed worst on both dimensions, deviating from the expected monotonic development. [→ Greiff 2013](#greiff-2013)

## Evidence

### Greiff 2013

Greiff, S., Wüstenberg, S., Molnár, G., Fischer, A., Funke, J., & Csapó, B. (2013, February 18). Complex Problem Solving in Educational Contexts—Something Beyond g: Concept, Assessment, Measurement Invariance, and Construct Validity. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031856

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent mean comparisons using the strong factorial invariance model compared grade levels 5/6, 7/8, 9, and 10/11 on both MicroDYN dimensions. "Grade Level 9 performed the worst" on knowledge application, and knowledge application dropped for Grade Level 10/11 relative to Grade Level 7/8 while knowledge acquisition remained stable; Hypothesis 3 was only partially supported.

> "In summary, findings were not as straightforward as expected because performance on all measures did not increase consistently in higher grade levels. In addition to the generally low performance in Grade Level 9 on all measures, measures of knowledge application scores dropped for Grade Level 10/11 compared to Grade Level 7/8, whereas measures of knowledge acquisition remained stable."
