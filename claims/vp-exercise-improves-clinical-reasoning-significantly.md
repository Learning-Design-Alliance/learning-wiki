## Evidence

### Malik Tayyaba Gul 2024

Malik Tayyaba Gul, Mahboob Usman, Khan Rehan Ahmed, Alam Rabia. (2024). Virtual patients versus standardized patients for improving clinical reasoning skills in ophthalmology residents. A randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05241-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

VP-arm within-group result of the randomized trial: residents completed a clinical reasoning exercise via AAO virtual patient scenarios for 30 minutes (extendable to 45 minutes), with 10-MCQ pretest and posttest per scenario. Improvement was statistically significant per "p value of < 0.00".

> "The mean pretest score for the VP was 12.7 ± 3.84, which improved to 16.30 ± 3.19 in post-test with p value of < 0.00. Clinical reasoning skills significantly improved with the use of the VP."
