Evidence¶

Shakir T 2026¶

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports an RCT in which 20 surgical trainees were randomised to 3-hour tips-and-tricks videos or no instruction before performing nine dVSS tasks across 10 cycles. The quoted scores and CUSUM plateau finding are from that study.

"Mean overall score was 90.8 vs. 72.4 in the video and control groups respectively (p < 0.001). CUSUM analysis of the learning curve for TTC reported the video group plateaued within 2 cycles, with the control group plateauing at 4 cycles."

Shakir T 2026 (2)¶

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's discussion of the video-review study reports improved accuracy with longer time-to-completion and offers the authors' own developmental-stage interpretation; no effect size is printed for the TTC increase.

"Findings from a small scale study showed improved technical accuracy but longer TTC, likely reflecting the transition from unconscious to conscious incompetence—a necessary developmental stage rather than training failure."
