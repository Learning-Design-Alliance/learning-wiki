Authors' interpretation (type e) of the mixed-model covariate results, where high-school math and physics coefficients were not significant once undergraduate performance was included. The article offers no direct measure of the ceiling effect; it is a proposed explanation, noting high-school matriculation grades clustered near the maximum (means 92.66 and 93.49).
