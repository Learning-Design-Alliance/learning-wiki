J. E. Wallace Wallin 1905¶

J. E. Wallace Wallin. (1905). The Doctrine of Formal Discipline: Two Neglected Instances of Transfer of Training. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org

q1 · i? · review · r?

Narrative review attributing these findings to named earlier researchers (Volkmann, Coover and Angell, Thorndike and Woodworth, Judd, Ruediger, Winch, Meumann and others); the reviewed studies' designs are not described here. The article notes some results are discrepant with its conclusion.

"The fact of transference has been established in operations as diverse as the following: In spatial discrimination, transfer is possible from the finger tips of the left hand to the right, or from the third phalanx to the first phalanx (Volkmann);"
