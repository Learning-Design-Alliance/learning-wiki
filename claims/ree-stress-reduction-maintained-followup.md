### Ilechukwu LC 2021

Ilechukwu LC, Egenti NT, Aloh HE, Uwakwe RC, Obande-Ogbuinya N, Eke CL, Kalu IA, Ejionueme LK, Iremeka FU. (2021). Rational emotive education for reducing stress of undergraduate students of religious education program: An experimental study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000026177

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group independent samples t test (Table 2, Time 3) from the same randomized controlled trial, with follow-up measured after a 3-month interval followed by four weekly follow-up sessions. The treatment group's follow-up mean was "mean = 11.79 ± 3.49" against "mean = 36.49 ± 2.43" in the control group; no effect size was printed.

> "the follow-up results revealed more sustenance of the significant decrease in mean perceived stress scores of the treatment group (mean = 11.79 ± 3.49) compared with participants in the control group (mean = 36.49 ± 2.43); t(59) = –50.332, P < .001."
