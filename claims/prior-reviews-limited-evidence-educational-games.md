Boeker Martin, Andel Peter, Vach Werner, Frankenschmidt Alexander. (2013). Game-Based E-Learning Is More Effective than a Conventional Instructional Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial with Third-Year Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0082328

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports, citing the 2010 systematic review of Akl et al., that only five RCTs of low-to-moderate methodological quality were eligible and three suggested a positive effect on medical students' knowledge; the quoted conclusion is the review's own.

> "Due to the limited number of studies, their low-to-moderate methodological quality, and the inconsistent results, the evidence is unlikely to support a general recommendation for the use of educational games in medical schools."
