Evidence¶

Ibtesam Al-Mashaqbeh and Muneera Al Shurman 2015¶

Ibtesam Al-Mashaqbeh and Muneera Al Shurman. (2015). The Adoption of Tablet and e-Textbooks: first grade core curriculum and school administration attitude. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079146

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Open interview with the school principal and principal assistant after the semester, using two open questions with follow-ups. Reported points include that it was "a motivated tool for learning process", students used the device and e-textbook easily, used colors and pen easily, and did not have to carry heavy paper textbooks. Qualitative; no effect size.
