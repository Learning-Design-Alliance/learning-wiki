# Focus group data indicate student tutors show more cognitive congruency than staff tutors, explaining content more clearly and in student-friendly terms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students reported that student tutors give clearer explanations, use less difficult terminology, formulate clearer questions, and better gauge students' prior knowledge than staff tutors. [→ Catherine De Rijdt 2012](#catherine-de-rijdt-2012)

## Evidence

### Catherine De Rijdt 2012

Catherine De Rijdt, Janine van der Rijt, Filip Dochy, Cees van der Vleuten. (2012). Rigorously selected and well trained senior student tutors in problem based learning: student perceptions and study achievements. Instructional Science, 40, 397-411. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9173-6

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-structured focus group interview with randomly selected first-year students, transcribed and coded by the four perception factors. Students said "Student tutors can explain things more clearly, because to staff tutors everything is self-evident" and that student tutors better understand prior knowledge.

> "Students indicate that student tutors show more cognitive congruency than do staff tutors."
