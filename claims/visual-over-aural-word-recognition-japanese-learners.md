## Evidence

### Mihara 2015

Mihara, K. (2015). Effects of Phonological Input as a Pre-Listening Activity on Vocabulary Learning and L2 Listening Comprehension Test Performance. TESL-EJ 19(2). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej74/a1.pdf

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article reports, citing Ikemura (2001), that Japanese university students recognized only 27% of vocabulary presented orally but 71% of words presented visually, and that 86% understood the word cotton visually while only 6% recognized it aurally.

> "He demonstrated that on average, students recognized only 27% of vocabulary presented orally but 71% of words presented visually."
