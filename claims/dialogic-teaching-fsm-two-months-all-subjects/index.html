Jay et al. 2017¶

Tim Jay, Ben Willis, Peter Thomas, Roberta Taylor, Nick Moore, Cathy Burnett, Guy Merchant, Anna Stevens. (2017). Dialogic Teaching: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581114

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of FSM pupils within the same cluster trial. Table 1 reports FSM-only effect sizes of "0.16" for maths (p = 0.03), "0.11" for science (p = 0.14), and "0.12" for English (p = 0.21); the report states the smaller FSM number limits security.

"Children eligible for free school meals (FSM) made two additional months’ progress in English, science, and maths compared to FSM children in control schools. The smaller number of FSM pupils in the trial limits the security of this result."
