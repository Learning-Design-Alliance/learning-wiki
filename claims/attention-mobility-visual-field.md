The author reports experimental demonstrations of attentional mobility by Hebb, Lashley, Kohler, and Zinchenko and Vergiles, quoting Kohler's result on comparing objects with fixed eyes. He presents this as evidence for an active element in the perceiver and builder of knowledge.

> "we can see objects that are in different parts of our visual field and, without moving our eyes, compare one with the other. Our attention obviously has the power to move within the visual field just as it can move among and select from speech sounds that arrive at the same time."
