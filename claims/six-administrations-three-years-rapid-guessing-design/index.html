James Soland 2019¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2019). Do students rapidly guess repeatedly over time? A longitudinal analysis of student test disengagement, background, and attitudes. Educational Assessment, 24(4), 327-342. https://doi.org/10.1080/10627197.2019.1645592

The study's design as stated: longitudinal achievement test data spanning "six administrations over three years" used to contrast a trait-like account of rapid guessing with a situational one. This design description supports the study's framing rather than a measured comparison.

"we use achievement test data from six administrations over three years to investigate whether rapid guessing is a stable trait-like behavior or if rapid guessing is determined mostly by situational variables"
