The editorial summarizes the meta-analysis by Prinz, Golke, and Wittwer (this issue) of situation-model-approach interventions on metacomprehension accuracy. The review reports a "medium-sized positive effect on metacomprehension accuracy" and a small direct comprehension effect; no standardized effect size value is printed.
