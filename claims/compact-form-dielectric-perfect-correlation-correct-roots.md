# Among 16 graduate students solving a plasma physics homework problem, obtaining the compact form of the dielectric function perfectly correlated with correctly solving for its roots

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All students who used the compact form of the dielectric function solved for the roots correctly, and all seven students who had only the expanded form solved incorrectly. [→ Ayush Gupta 2007](#ayush-gupta-2007)

## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2007

Ayush Gupta, Edward F. Redish, and David Hammer. (2007). Coordination of Mathematics and Physical Resources by Physics Graduate Students. 2007 PERC, AIP Conference Proceedings, Volume 951. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0012 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of submitted homework from 16 students in a graduate plasma physics survey course, summarized in Table 1: 3 students obtained form 1(a) only and 6 obtained both forms, all solving correctly, while 7 with form 1(b) only all erred. The authors note a "100% correlation in our data".

> "We observed a 100% correlation in our data between obtaining the dielectric function in the compact form and being able to correctly solve for its roots."
