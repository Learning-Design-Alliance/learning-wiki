`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological interview study of 12 current and former managers of teaching hospitals affiliated with Shiraz University of Medical Sciences, sampled purposively to theoretical saturation. The results description reports that "Peer learning significantly improved decision-making, managerial performance, inter-organizational interactions, and continuous updating of specialized knowledge."

> "Peer learning significantly improved decision-making, managerial performance, inter-organizational interactions, and continuous updating of specialized knowledge. Exchanging real-world experiences enhanced critical thinking, professional attitudes, and self-efficacy among managers."
