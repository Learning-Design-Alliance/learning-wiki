Evidence¶

Robinson 2026¶

Robinson, Carly D., David Gormley, Ana Trindade Ribeiro, and Susanna Loeb. (2026). Access is Not Enough: Human Support Improves Engagement with AI Tutoring. EdWorkingPaper No. 26-1451, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai26-1451

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ITT regressions on preregistered engagement outcome (stories read per week) in two RCTs; Table 1 shows effects of 0.201 (p<0.05) and 0.916 (p<0.05). The article reports results were robust to alternative specifications (Tables A9 and A10).

"In District A, treatment students read 0.20 more stories per week than control students, a 71% increase relative to the control mean of 0.28 stories per week and equivalent to roughly 4.4 additional stories over the intervention. In District B, treatment students read 0.92 more stories per week, an 80% increase relative to the control mean of 1.14 stories per week."
