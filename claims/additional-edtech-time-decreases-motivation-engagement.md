### Robert Fairlie and Prashant Loyalka 2020

Robert Fairlie and Prashant Loyalka. (2020). Schooling and Covid-19: lessons from recent research on EdTech. npj Science of Learning 5:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00072-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This finding comes from the Russia randomized experiment comparing no EdTech, a base level (~45 min per week), and a doubled level of EdTech among students ages 9–11. The article reports that additional time on EdTech "decreases schoolchildren ’s motivation and engagement in subject material"; no effect size is printed.

> "We find that additional time on EdTech even decreases schoolchildren ’s motivation and engagement in subject material."
