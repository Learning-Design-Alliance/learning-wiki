## Evidence

### R. Akoglu 2017

R. Akoglu, S. Habib Mazharimousavi, and M. Halilsoy. (2017). Cycloid Experiment for freshmen physics labs. [doi:10.3906/fiz-1711-5](https://doi.org/10.3906/fiz-1711-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Physical demonstration experiment with a billiard ball rolling along eight grooved track paths between fixed points O and A, timed with a digital timer and fork-type light barriers. Table I prints times from 0.740 s (P1) down to "reaching a minimum at P4" at 0.652 s, then rising to 0.677 s at P8.

> "We observe that as we go from P1 to P4 with exact tangential touches to the axes, the time decreases, reaching a minimum at P4. From P5 on, the time starts to increase again toward P8"
