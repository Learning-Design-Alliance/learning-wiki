## Subclaims

## Evidence

### de Jong Zuzana 2010

de Jong Zuzana, van Nies Jessica AB, Peters Sonja WM, Vink Sylvia, Dekker Friedo W, Scherpbier Albert. (2010). Interactive seminars or small group tutorials in preclinical medical education: results of a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-79

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 107 year-three medical students randomly allocated to small group tutorials (n = 53) or interactive seminars (n = 54); 48 test takers per arm. "In both groups, the mean overall grade was 6.6"; overall grade difference P = 0.768 (Table 2).

> "The RCT participants in the small groups and interactive seminars did not differ in overall test grades or sub-scores. In both groups, the mean overall grade was 6.6 (on a ten-point scale)."
