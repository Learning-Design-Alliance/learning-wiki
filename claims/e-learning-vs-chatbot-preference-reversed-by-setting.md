## Evidence

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Two group assessment sessions with six hemato-oncologists judging mock-ups of the e-learning, chatbot, and physical reminders. The sessions found participants "favored the conventional e-learning over the chatbot", judging the e-learning easier for focusing skill acquisition.

> "Based on the presentation, participants in both user group assessment sessions favored the conventional e-learning over the chatbot."

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020 (2)

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

The individual think-aloud sessions with five junior oncologists produced the opposite preference: "Four out of the five test participants favored the chatbot", describing it as faster, informal, low threshold, and easier to integrate in daily clinical routine.

> "Four out of the five test participants favored the chatbot over the e-learning."
