### Alexandru Maries 2022

Alexandru Maries, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Gender Inequities Throughout STEM: Compared to Men, Women with Significantly Higher Grades Drop STEM Majors. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.02438](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.02438)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Table 2b compares cumulative STEM GPA of degree earners from the same institutional dataset: women vs men who earned degrees, d = 0.10 (bio), 0.27 (CS/engineering), 0.15 (math/physical science), with the article reporting "only a small effect size."

> "This is in contrast with comparisons of men and women who persisted and earned degrees in STEM majors, where women still had higher grades, but with only a small effect size, as shown in Table 2b."
