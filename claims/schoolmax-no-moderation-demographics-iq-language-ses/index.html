Evidence¶

Lopata 2020¶

Lopata, C., Donnelly, J. P., Thomeer, M. L., Rodgers, J. D., Lodi-Smith, J., Booth, A. J., & Volker, M. A. (2020). Moderators of School Intervention Outcomes for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology, 48, 1105-1114. https://link.springer.com/journal/10802

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory moderator analysis of the prior cluster RCT (103 children, 35 schools), adding moderator-by-treatment interactions to the original multilevel model. The results section reports "no evidence of moderation by child, family, or school demographics" or IQ, language, or ASD diagnostic symptoms; Table 2 prints non-significant interaction coefficients for all these moderators.

"There was no evidence of moderation by child, family, or school demographics, or child IQ, language ability, or ASD diagnostic symptoms."
