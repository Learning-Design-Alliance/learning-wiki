## Evidence

### Filiz Demirci 2017

Filiz Demirci, Cengiz Özyürek. (2017). The Effects of Using Concept Cartoons in Astronomy Subjects on Critical Thinking Skills among Seventh Grade Student. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 10(2). https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017236119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-groups t-test on CCT-X scores of the 28-student experimental group in a quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test design; means rose from Mpre-test= 31.11 to Mpost-test= 34.50, a statistically significant increase with no printed effect size.

> "there is a statistically significant difference between the pre -test and post-test scores for the students in the experimental group in favour of the post -test scores ( t(27)= -2.07, p≤0.05)"
