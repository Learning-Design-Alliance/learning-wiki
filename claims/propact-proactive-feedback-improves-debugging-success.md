## Evidence

### Anahita Golrang 2026

Anahita Golrang, Kshitij Sharma, Simon Dehaen, Olga Viberg. (2026). ProPACT: A Proactive AI-Driven Adaptive Collaborative Tutor for Pair Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02703 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.02703](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.02703)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects experiment with 26 pair-programming dyads comparing ProPACT feedback and no-feedback control conditions; paired t-test on debugging success showed a significant difference, with "debugging success is significantly higher in the feedback condition than in the control condition". No effect size printed.

> "a t-test with the feedback category as the independent variable and debugging success as the dependent variable shows a clear difference in the debugging success among the feedback categories (t[49.96] = -13.51, p<.0001, figure 3a). The debugging success is significantly higher in the feedback condition than in the control condition."
