### Kim 2015

Kim, Y., & Baylor, A. L. (2015). Research-Based Design of Pedagogical Agent Roles: a Review, Progress, and Recommendations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0055-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

Experiment 2 with 71 undergraduate pre-service teachers learning instructional design skills compared the three agent roles (the article reports random assignment for Experiment 1 and does not say how Experiment 2 learners were assigned; it refers to the original paper for the method). The article reports that motivational agents "led to improved learner self-efficacy," but no effect size is printed.

> "Specifically, the motivational agents (Motivator and Mentor) were perceived as more human-like and led to improved learner self-efficacy. However, the affective encouragement and support were not sufficient for the learners to achieve the learning goals"
