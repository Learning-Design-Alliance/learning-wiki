## Evidence

### O'Hare et al. 2017

Dr Liam O'Hare, Dr Patrick Stark, Professor Carol McGuinness, Dr Andy Biggart, Professor Allen Thurston. (2017). Spaced Learning: The Design, Feasibility and Optimisation of SMART Spaces — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581430

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Review of animal neuroscience evidence reported in the background section: the report attributes to Fields (2009) the finding that spaced stimulation of rat hippocampal synapses produced twice the voltage of electrical activity versus massed stimulation, with CREB and zif268 present after spaced stimulation.

> "Fields found that the rat synapses produced twice the voltage of electrical activity after being stimulated in three bursts with ten-minute spaces than when they were stimulated in a massed pattern."
