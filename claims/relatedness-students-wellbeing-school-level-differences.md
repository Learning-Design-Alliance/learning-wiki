### Rhoda Lai 2026 (2)

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's summary of Klassen et al. (2012; Studies 1 and 2, 409 and 455 teachers) reports student relatedness was more associated with work engagement than colleague relatedness for both school levels; for secondary teachers, student relatedness but not colleague relatedness was significantly associated with emotional exhaustion.

> "Relatedness with students was significantly more associated with work engagement than relatedness with colleagues – this was true for secondary as well as primary school teachers."
