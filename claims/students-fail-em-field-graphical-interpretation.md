## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For graphical representations of electromagnetic fields with cylindrical symmetry, the number of correct answers was very small and students tended to change strategy between questions while failing to apply the strategies correctly. [→ Laurens Bollen 2015](#laurens-bollen-2015)

## Evidence

### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-test electromagnetic-field graphical questions (N=19, Fig. 3 and Table V). Students misapplied Ampère's law and misread the paddle-wheel idea; the most common electric-field error (37%) was thinking divergence was non-zero everywhere because the arrows converge.

> "The number of correct answers is very small: only a few students could correctly determine both the divergence and the curl"
