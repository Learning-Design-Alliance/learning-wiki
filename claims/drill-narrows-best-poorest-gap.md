### Brown 1915

Brown, J. C. (1915). An Investigation on the Value of Drill Work in the Fundamental Operations of Arithmetic. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Gross-gain comparison of the best and poorest 19 pupils of drill versus non-drill sections, based on first-test records. Best drill pupils gained 18 to 46 points (average 26, median 22) versus 6 to 19 points (average 10, median 9) for best non-drill pupils; poorest drill pupils averaged -3.1 versus -8 for poorest non-drill.

> "The best 19 of the drill classes made a gain ranging from 18 to 46 points, with an average of 26 points, and a median gain of 22 points."

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of best and poorest 19 of the drill sections across the two tests: the best-to-poorest score ratio was 3.1 times on the first test and 1.9 times on the second test, indicating a narrowed gap under drill. Figures are descriptive ratios; no test statistic is printed.
