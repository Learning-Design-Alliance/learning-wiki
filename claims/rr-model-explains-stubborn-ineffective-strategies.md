## Evidence

### Critten 2006

Critten, S., Pine, K., & Steffler, D. (2006). Spelling Development in Young Children: A Case of Representational-Redescription? Manuscript submitted for publication (Date of Submission: Thursday 3rd August 2006). https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.1.207

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Theoretical argument, not a new empirical test: the authors argue that stubborn use of failing strategies such as sounding out matches the E1 level's abstraction and over-application of a theory, and that the RR model "can account for this stubborn use of ineffective strategies in a way that Rittle-Johnson and Siegler (1999) could not".

> "Rittle-Johnson and Siegler (1999) could not explain: "why children persist in using time-consuming backup strategies that initially do little to improve performance" (p. 345)"
