## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2020

Zhao Xin, Wang Haien, Maes Joseph H. R. (2020). Training and transfer effects of extensive task-switching training in students. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post group comparisons on Stroop, Flanker, go/no-go, WM updating, and Raven's tasks in trained (n=31) versus active control (n=29) groups; the abstract summarizes the null far-transfer results confirmed by the ANOVAs reported in Table 1.

> "There were no beneficial effects on far-transfer tasks measuring interference control, response inhibition, working memory, and general IQ."
