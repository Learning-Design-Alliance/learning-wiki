## Evidence

### Wilson 2018

Wilson, K. E., Martinez, M., Mills, C., D'Mello, S., Smilek, D., & Risko, E. F. (2018). Instructor presence effect: Liking does not always lead to learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.011

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 1, mind wandering was measured with thought probes at 13 points during the lecture. The article reports no significant impact of lecture format on the proportion of time MW; no effect size beyond the printed η2 was interpreted.

> "Lecture format, however, did not significantly impact the proportion of time MW, F(2, 166) = 0.06, p = 0.95, η2 = .001."
