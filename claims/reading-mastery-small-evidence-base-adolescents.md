The WWC's literature search reflects documents publicly available by August 2009 and reviewed under WWC Evidence Standards Version 2.0. The report states "the remaining 173 studies do not meet either WWC evidence standards or eligibility screens."

> "A total of 175 studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of Reading Mastery on adolescent learners. One study (Stockard, 2010) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC evidence standards, and one study (Yu & Rachor, 2000) is a quasi-experimental design that meets WWC evidence standards with reservations."
