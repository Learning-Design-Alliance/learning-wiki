q3 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the included evidence on methodological diversification. The review further reports that many instruments "lack formal validation", that a considerable proportion of studies rely on indirect measures such as student perceptions or self-assessments, and that longitudinal assessment is absent in most studies.

"Nevertheless, this diversity is accompanied by marked heterogeneity in assessment methods, which limits comparability between studies and highlights the need to advance towards more standardised evaluative frameworks."
