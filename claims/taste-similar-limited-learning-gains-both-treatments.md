## Evidence

### Bekaert 2025

Bekaert, H., Van Dooren, W., Van Winckel, H., Poessei, M., Thiering, I., Nicolini, M., Artioli, E., Avgerinou, D., Balla, E., & De Cock, M. (2025). Teaching the apparent motion of Sun and stars across four European countries. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.32374/2025.5.1.084ra](https://doi.org/10.32374/2025.5.1.084ra)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-treatment pretest-posttest study of 262 students (group 1 N=90 school-only; group 2 N=172 school plus planetarium). Paired-samples t-tests showed significant pre-post gains; the article reports "learning gains in the two groups do not differ significantly (p > 0.05)". No effect size is printed.

> "The overall learning gain in the two treatment groups is very similar, 14% and 13% respectively. An independent samples t-test reveals that learning gains in the two groups do not differ significantly (p > 0.05)."
