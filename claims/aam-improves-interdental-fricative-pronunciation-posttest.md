## Evidence

### Kayaoglu 2013

Kayaoglu, M. N. & Caylak, N. (2013). What is needed for correct pronunciation: a model or a concern? Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 53, 269-290. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1060386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group design with 36 vocational school students randomly assigned to experimental (n=18) and control (n=18) groups. The experimental group received three AAM pronunciation lessons. Post-test comparison showed a significant difference between groups (p=0.007), indicating the AAM instruction improved pronunciation of inter-dental fricatives.

> "In the post-test scores, there was a significant difference between the control group and experimental group at the specified level 0.05, p=0.007."
