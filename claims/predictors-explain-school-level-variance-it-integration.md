## Evidence

### Hew Khe Foon 2016

Hew Khe Foon, Tan Cheng Yong. (2016). Predictors of Information Technology Integration in Secondary Schools: Evidence from a Large Scale Study of More than 30,000 Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sequential HLM models showed progressive increases in explained Level 2 variance from 3.12% (controls only) to 15.90% (full model). The null model showed 85.29% of variance was within-school and 14.71% between-school, supporting the use of HLM for this nested data structure.

> "the predictors (including the controls) explained a total of 15.90% of the school-level variance in levels of IT integration. Among the variables, access to school IT resources (5.91%) and teachers' pedagogical beliefs and practices (4.45%) account for a larger proportion of the between-school IT integration variance, as compared to IT curricular expectations (1.53%), parents' expectations (0.36%), or the presence of IT policies in schools (0.53%)"
