Susanne de Mooij 2025¶

Susanne de Mooij, Joni Lämsä, Lyn Lim, Olli Aksela, Shruti Athavale, Inti Bistolfi, Flora Jin, Tongguang Li, Roger Azevedo, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević, Sanna Järvelä, Inge Molenaar. (2025). q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 142 studies coded by approach and publication year. The review reports the integrated approach "growing from 4 studies (13.8%) in earlier years to 29 studies (28.4%) more recently", with unimodal approaches declining from 37.9% to 25.4% over the same periods.

"A significant development across the years is the increased use of an integrated approach in SRL research, growing from 4 studies (13.8%) in earlier years to 29 studies (28.4%) more recently."
