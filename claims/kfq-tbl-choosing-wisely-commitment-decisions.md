## Evidence

### N. Mitchell 2025

N. Mitchell, M. Rashid, J. Foulds, and K. Forbes. (2025). Thinking About the Why: A Qualitative Study on Students' Perspectives of Paediatric Team‐Based Learning Discussions. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70161

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme analysis found students saw the five-choice KFQ format as practice in strategic prioritization, unlike the "shotgun approach" of early training, and that committing to specific diagnoses "was uncomfortable, but I think relevant".

> "Being able to relate their decisions and commitment to a limited number of responses reflected choosing wisely, where students had to prioritize and justify decisions, recognizing that resources in authentic clinical settings are not unlimited."
