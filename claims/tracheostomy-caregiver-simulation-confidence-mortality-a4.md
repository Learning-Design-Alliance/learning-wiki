Study A4 (level IV evidence), in which caregivers of children with a new tracheostomy all received standard classroom training and one group also received high-fidelity simulation training with a human simulator. The review reports "Post-test confidence was significantly higher in the simulation group"; no effect size is printed.

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Same tracheostomy caregiver study (A4), reported as a comparison between children whose caregivers did and did not participate in simulation. The review prints the mortality contrast but no effect size or confidence interval; equivalence was not tested.

> "Mortality in family homes was nearly five times higher for children whose caregivers did not participate in simulation."
