Evidence¶

Mozhgan Madhkhan & Seyed Mojtaba Mousavi 2017¶

Mozhgan Madhkhan & Seyed Mojtaba Mousavi. (2017). The Effect of Implimentation of TBLT in Reading Comprehension Classes of Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 10(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n11p119

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Controlled experiment: 70 learners randomly assigned to experimental (task-based) and control (traditional) groups, same texts, 20 sessions, reading post-test compared via t-test. The table reports t = 5.9, Sig. (2-tailed) = .000, mean difference 4.7000, showing "a statistically significant difference between the two groups". No effect size printed.

"T-test results make it clear that experimental group got higher mean scores than the control group, which indicates a statistically significant difference between the two groups. It implies that using TBLT is superior to the traditional method in reading classes."
