## Evidence

### O'Dwyer 2016

O'Dwyer, L. M., Lee-St. John, T., Raczek, A. E., Luna Bazaldua, D. A., & Walsh, M. (2016). Examining the role of early academic and non-cognitive skills as mediators of the effects of City Connects on middle school academic outcomes. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567236

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model 3 multilevel regressions (Tables 5 and 6) predicting grade 8 mathematics from each grade 5 mediator separately. All six mediators had positive, statistically significant Path b coefficients.

> "The results indicate that, holding all other variables in the model constant, each of the grade 5 hypothesized mediators were positive predictors of students' grade 8 mathematics scores (Path b)."
