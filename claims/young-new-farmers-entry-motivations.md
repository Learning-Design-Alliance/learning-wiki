### Luo Tao 2026

Luo Tao, Huang Qingpeng, Mai Jingyan. (2026). Development status of young new farmers from the perspective of vocational ability: A research analysis based on 21 cities in Guangdong Province. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of work motivations among 302 young new farmers found policy response (36.5%) as the primary entry motivation, followed by green production values (26.9%), social responsibility (20.3%), and professional expertise (16.3%).

> "the primary reason for engaging in agriculture is to respond to national policies, accounting for 36.5%; the second is the recognition of the concepts of green production and high-quality products, accounting for 26.9%; those motivated by a sense of social responsibility account for 20.3%; and those hoping to leverage their professional expertise account for 16.3%. The results of the chi-square test indicate that there are significant differences among the various types of motivation (χ² = 28.34, p < 0.001)."
