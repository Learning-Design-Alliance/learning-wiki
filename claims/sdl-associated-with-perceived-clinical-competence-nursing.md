### El-Sayed Boshra Karem Mohamed 2026

Enhancing perceived clinical competence of nursing students in the AI era: the role of AI acceptance and self-directed learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09453-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 550 nursing students at Alexandria University, Egypt, analyzed with structural equation modeling. The direct path from self-directed learning to perceived clinical competence was significant, supporting H1.

> "Self-directed learning ability was significantly associated with clinical competence (β = 0.452, p < 0.001), and AI acceptance was positively associated with perceived clinical competence (β = 0.489, p < 0.001)."
