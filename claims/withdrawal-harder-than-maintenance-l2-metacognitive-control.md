## Evidence

### Palladino 2025

Palladino, P., Trotta, E., Bonvino, A., Carlucci, L., & Cottini, M. (2025). How do you feel during English class? Emotions and metacognition in primary school children learning English as a second language. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09414-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Additional t tests compared metacognitive control of correct versus incorrect responses within each administration point, using the green-box/red-box maintenance-withdrawal procedure. The authors suggest detecting incorrect responses may develop later than detecting correct ones.

> "it was easier for children to keep correct responses than to withdraw incorrect responses, t(227) = 7.16, p <.001, d = 0.47, for Time 0, and t(226) = 6.12, p <.001, d = 0.41, for Time 1."
