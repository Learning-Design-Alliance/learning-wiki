# Team readiness assurance test (TRAT) scores are significantly higher than individual readiness assurance test (IRAT) scores in an interprofessional TBL session

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Total TRAT scores of interprofessional groups were significantly higher than total IRAT scores of medical and physiotherapy students. [→ Burgess A 2020](#burgess-a-2020)

## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, Kalman E, Haq I, Leaver A, Roberts C, Bleasel J. (2020). Interprofessional team-based learning (TBL): how do students engage? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02024-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way between groups ANOVA with Tukey's HSD compared IRAT and TRAT scores from the single interprofessional TBL session. The article reports "significantly higher than total IRAT scores", with TRAT M = 98.5 versus medical IRAT M = 81.5 and physiotherapy IRAT M = 70.7.

> "Total TRAT scores were significantly higher than total IRAT scores (F (2, 27)=11.3, p = 0.000266), Post-hoc comparison using Tukey’s HSD revealed that this was true when medical (M = 81.5, p < 0.05) or physiotherapy students'(M = 70.7, p < 0.001) total IRAT scores were compared with the total TRAT (M = 98.5) scores of the interprofessional groups."
