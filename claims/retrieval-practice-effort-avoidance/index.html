Evidence¶

de Bruin 2020¶

de Bruin, A. B. H., Roelle, J., Carpenter, S. K., & Baars, M. (2020). Synthesizing Cognitive Load and Self-regulation Theory: a Theoretical Framework and Research Agenda. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09576-4

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The editorial summarizes the review by Carpenter, Endres, and Hui (this issue) of students' experiences of learning from retrieval practice. The review reports the effort perception and the task-type difference in avoidance; no effect size is printed.

"During both types of tasks, students generally experience retrieval practice as more effortful than restudy. Although this leads to avoidance of retrieval practice in self-regulated learning contexts for memory-based tasks, this is less the case for problem-solving tasks."
