## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exploratory t-tests found some GSs elicited significantly higher mPFC activity than the acquisition-phase CS− (e.g., crossmodal GS1: t19 = 3.761, p = 0.001), while others showed trend-level differences; no gradient or group differences emerged. [→ Xin Liu 2025](#xin-liu-2025)

Exploratory paired-sample t-tests on fNIRS mPFC HbO during the generalization phase, using the acquisition CS− as a non-threat baseline; the main GS ANOVA showed no significant effects (e.g., stimulus type F2,76 = 1.191, p = 0.315).
