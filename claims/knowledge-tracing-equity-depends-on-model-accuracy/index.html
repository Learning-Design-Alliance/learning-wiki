Claims

Knowledge-tracing mastery decisions are more equitable than fixed practice but can still under-serve some students when the learner model is fit to the aggregate population or makes wrong assumptions

In simulations, knowledge-tracing algorithms were substantially more equitable than giving every student the same amount of practice, but Bayesian knowledge tracing and the N-consecutive-correct heuristic became inequitable when they relied on inaccurate models, while knowledge tracing with the additive factor model may be more equitable.
