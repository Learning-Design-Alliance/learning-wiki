# Written presentation of objectives and direct instruction are instructional activities linked to stronger effects of ICT-supported writing interventions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### López 2026

López, P., Fidalgo, R., Álvarez-Moreno, C., Díaz-Tejedor, R., van den Bergh, H., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2026). Compositional Analysis of Writing Instructional Practices Performed with Digital Tools: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10153-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

One-by-one meta-regression moderator analysis across 53 effect sizes found written presentation of objectives significant. The unstandardized coefficient was "b = 0.815" with p = .049; the feature was present in 14 of 53 effects.

> "presentation of objectives/instructions in written form as a separate activity (present 14 out of 53): Fm(1, 11.624) = 4.782, p = .049; b = 0.815, 95% CI [> 0,009 1.631])"

### López 2026 (2)

López, P., Fidalgo, R., Álvarez-Moreno, C., Díaz-Tejedor, R., van den Bergh, H., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2026). Compositional Analysis of Writing Instructional Practices Performed with Digital Tools: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10153-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

In the same one-by-one meta-regressions, direct instruction significantly moderated effects with the unstandardized coefficient "b = 0.516" and p = .049, present in 48 of 53 effect sizes.

> "direct instruction (present 48 out of 53): Fm(1, 5.116) = 6.563, p = .049; b = 0.516, 95% CI [0.002, 1.03])."
