## Evidence

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossover trial analysis of mean economy of motion and mean master workspace range across four simulation exercises on both consoles; the authors report no significant differences for these quality and efficiency metrics overall.

> "For these metrics the results are not significantly different indicating equivalent performance of both platforms."

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022 (2)

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossover trial quality and efficiency analysis; the console B group showed a significant master workspace range difference on pick and place (B = 9.37 cm vs A = 5.96 cm, p = 0.000), which the authors attribute to adaptation to the second platform.

> "For the console B group master workspace range was different at a level of statistically significance for pick and place (B = 9.37 cm vs A = 5.96 cm, p = 0.000) indicating adaption to the second platform for this exercise."
