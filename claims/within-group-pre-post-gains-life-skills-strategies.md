## Evidence

### Abdelhalim 2015

Safaa M. Abdelhalim. (2015). Children Literature Based Program for Developing EFL Primary Pupils’ Life Skills and Language Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p178

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests (N = 31 per group, df = 30) showed significant pre-to-post gains on all four measures in both experimental groups, with effect sizes ranging from η² = .96 to .99. The article describes these as large effects.

> "Table 4 indicates that there were statistically significant  difference in life skills and language learning strategies between the mean scores of the two experimental groups on the pre- and post-tests in favor of the post-test scores"
