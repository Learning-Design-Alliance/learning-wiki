## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Dependent t-tests showed a statistically significant difference in favour of the post-test between AAT post-test and permanence scores in the experimental group (p < 0.05), but no significant difference in the control group (p > 0.05). [→ Sarican 2018](#sarican-2018)

## Evidence

### Sarican 2018

Sarican, G., Akgunduz, D. (2018). The impact of integrated STEM education on academic achievement, reflective thinking skills towards problem solving and permanence in learning in science education. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(1), 94-113. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1196043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-group t-tests within each group of the 44-student quasi-experiment: EG t = 2.767, p = 0.012 (post-test 14.23 vs PT 12.86); CG t = 4.095, p = 0.124 (post-test 12.50 vs PT 10.25). Only the experimental group's decline was statistically significant.

> "There was no significant difference between the AAT post-test and AAT PT scores of the control group students (p > 0.05), but there was a statistically significant difference in favour of post-test between the AAT post-test and AAT PT scores of the students of the experimental group (p < 0.05)."
