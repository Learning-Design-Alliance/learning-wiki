Feature-importance analysis from the gradient boosting model over 94 engineered features (Figure 3). The article reports "an importance amount of 0.712" for other financing sources and notes institutional need-based aid and merit-based awards are in the top 12 but with "quite minor" impacts compared to other financial features, suggesting applicants carry a financial burden.

"the most important factor in terms of contributing to applicants' decision to enroll at the university is "other financing sources", typically loans, which applicants need to acquire to supplement federal and institutional awards and aid"
