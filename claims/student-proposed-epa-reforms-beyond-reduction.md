`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses; students suggested global weekly evaluations instead of single-encounter EPAs, mandatory staff completion, and explicit wording so that "subjective variability" in scale interpretation is reduced.

> "A further subtheme that emerged was moving the onus for completing the EPA observations from students to preceptors so that students are not penalised for staff not completing them."
