## Evidence

### Nicholas Feroce 2025

Nicholas Feroce, Jenny Liu, Rajendra Chattergoon. (2025). Exploring the impact of a CALL tool for emergent bilinguals. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10462-5

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis of the full sample's oral (speaking/listening) and written (reading/writing) ELPAC subdomain scores with demographic and school fixed-effect controls. Both coefficients were significant, with effect sizes of 0.224 SDs (oral) and 0.153 SDs (written); the article notes the oral effect exceeds the written effect, consistent with the program's oral focus.

> "students who used the CALL program were predicted to score, on average, 0.224 SDs above their grade-specific means on the ELPAC oral subdomain and 0.153 SDs on the ELPAC written subdomain compared to students who did not use the CALL program. Both these effects were statistically significant, and the larger effect for the oral subdomain aligns with the program's emphasis on development of oral language skills."
