## Evidence

### Fonseca 2025

Fonseca, Braz, Vieira, de Souza Ferreira, Damian. (2025). Do CBCT and Clinical Experience Impact Decision‐Making in Endodontic Diagnosis and Treatment Planning? A Before‐After Study. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12966

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Binary logistic regression with students as reference category, across both evaluator groups (ten evaluators). The article reports professionals had "approximately 42% lower odds" of diagnostic change, with β = −0.543 and OR = 0.58 (95% CI 0.36–0.94, p = 0.028; Table 2).

> "For diagnostic options, professionals had approximately 42% lower odds of modifying their diagnostic choices when assessing CBCT images compared to students. This finding was statistically significant (p = 0.028), suggesting that clinical experience plays a role in diagnostic decision‐making for endodontic cases."

### Fonseca 2025 (2)

Fonseca, Braz, Vieira, de Souza Ferreira, Damian. (2025). Do CBCT and Clinical Experience Impact Decision‐Making in Endodontic Diagnosis and Treatment Planning? A Before‐After Study. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12966

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same logistic regression model for treatment-plan change; Table 2 shows β = −0.442, OR = 0.64 (95% CI 0.40–1.04), p = 0.070. The article reports the association "did not reach statistical significance"; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, for treatment plans, the association did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.070), indicating that CBCT may influence the therapeutic decision‐making of both students and professionals to a similar extent in the evaluation of endodontic cases"
