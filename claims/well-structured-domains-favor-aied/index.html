Sergey Sosnovsky & Bruce M. McLaren & Vincent Aleven 2014¶

Sergey Sosnovsky & Bruce M. McLaren & Vincent Aleven. (2014). Preface-Emerging Technologies and Landmark Systems for Learning Mathematics and Science: Dedicated to the Memory of Erica Melis-Part 2. Int J Artif Intell Educ 24. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0025-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

This is the preface's technological-perspective argument (type (e), authors' interpretation), not a tested result: the article offers this analysis without data, explaining why ITS development has concentrated on math and science.

"Such tasks have unambiguously correct answers and well-defined (even if possibly large) sets of solution paths to reach those answers. In these domains, it is typically easier than in ill-defined domains, such as language acquisition, psychology, and art, to identify complementary knowledge components constituting a complex skill, or the target skill trained by a problem step."
