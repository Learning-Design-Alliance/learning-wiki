Qualitative case study based on semistructured interviews and document review in Ohio. Interviewed leaders reported the inclusion of early literacy in accountability "created new urgency and clarity around reading instruction and intervention," and one district leader described a "trifecta" effect with the science of reading and dyslexia mandates. Perceived impacts only; no formal impact evaluation is reported.
