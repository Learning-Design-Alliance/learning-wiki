## Evidence

### M. Rais Ridwan 2022

M. Rais Ridwan, Samsul Hadi, Jailani Jailani. (2022). A meta-analysis study on the effectiveness of a cooperative learning model on vocational high school students' mathematics learning outcomes. Participatory Educational Research, 9(4). http://dx.doi.org/10.17275/per.22.97.9.4

`q3 · i2`

Moderator analysis by class/sample size in the random-effects meta-analysis (Table 4), with effect sizes of "0.94" for 1-30 students and "0.83" for more than 30 students. The article reports "learning with cooperative models is more effective than learning with more than 30 students when the sample size is 1 -30."

> "the effectiveness of learning using the cooperative model on VHS student learning outcomes dependent on class size was 0.94 [95% -CI: 0.79; 1.09] and 0.83 [95% -CI: 0.46; 1.19]"
