## Evidence

### Jacobs Fabian 2014

Jacobs Fabian, Stegmann Karsten, Siebeck Matthias. (2014). Promoting medical competencies through international exchange programs: benefits on communication and effective doctor-patient relationships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-43

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive analysis combining the interview and survey data for RQ3. Interviewees described reflection or "mirroring" of their own thinking patterns; the authors conclude "the perceived differences between the two cultures and the learning environments and the crisis experienced by participants seem to be important intermediaries of learning."

> "the perceived differences between the two cultures and the learning environments and the crisis experienced by participants seem to be important intermediaries of learning."

### Jacobs Fabian 2014 (2)

Jacobs Fabian, Stegmann Karsten, Siebeck Matthias. (2014). Promoting medical competencies through international exchange programs: benefits on communication and effective doctor-patient relationships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-43

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three descriptive process items in the quasi-experimental survey of 29 participants. For "By interacting with locals, I learned something that I would not have learned otherwise" (M = 4.72; SD = 0.80), 24 strongly agreed and 4 agreed; means for all items fell between agree and strongly agree.

> "Twenty-four participants strongly agreed and 4 participants agreed with the statements "By interacting with locals, I learned something that I would not have learned otherwise." (M = 4.72; SD = 0.80)"
