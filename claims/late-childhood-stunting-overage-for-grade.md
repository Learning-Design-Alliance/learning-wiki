### Sunny BS 2018

Sunny BS, DeStavola B, Dube A, Kondowe S, Crampin AC, Glynn JR. (2018). Does early linear growth failure influence later school performance? A cohort study in Karonga district, northern Malawi. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0200380

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cohort analysis of stunting at ages 4–8 years and age-for-grade at age 11 using multinomial logistic regression. The article reports children were "2–4 times more likely to be overage than underage/on time for grade" with p<0.01.

> "Stunting in late childhood was also associated with being 2–4 times more likely to be overage than underage/on time for grade by the age of 11, even after adjusting for other socio-economic and other confounders (p<0.01)."
