# Self-reported preparedness to repair OASIS increased after simulation training, with most participants moving from not ready to ready

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the simulation, 15 of 23 respondents rated themselves ready to repair OASIS versus 4 before, a statistically significant shift (p < 0.05). [→ Knobel Roxana 2018](#knobel-roxana-2018)

## Evidence

### Knobel Roxana 2018

Knobel Roxana, Volpato Lia Karina, Gervasi Liliam Cristini, Viergutz Raquel de Almeida, Trapani Alberto Júnior. (2018). A Simple, Reproducible and Low-cost Simulator for Teaching Surgical Techniques to Repair Obstetric Anal Sphincter Injuries. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1668527

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-evaluation questionnaire administered three to six months after the simulation, analyzed with descriptive statistics. Before the simulation 14 (60.87%) felt not ready and 4 (17.39%) ready; after, 1 (4.35%) not ready and 15 (65.22%) ready, reported as "p < 0.05". No effect size is printed.

> "Table 1 Self-evaluation of the participants of the simulation regarding their preparedness to suture a severe laceration Do you feel ready to repair OASIS? Before simulation After simulation n (%) n (%) No 14 (60.87) 1 (4.35) Partially 5 (21.74) 7 (30.43) Yes 4 (17.39) 15 (65.22) p < 0.05."
