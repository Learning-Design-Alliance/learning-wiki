### Xia et al. 2022

Qi Xia, Hongbiao Yin, Ruonan Hu, Xiuhan Li, and Junjie Shang. (2022). Motivation, Engagement, and Mathematics Achievement: An Exploratory Study Among Chinese Primary Students. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440221134609

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA across grades 4-6 (n=1,538) showed significant grade-level differences on disengagement, learning focus, persistence, planning, self-belief, valuing, adaptive motivation, and adaptive engagement, with small printed effect sizes (hp2=.004-.026).

> "the fourth-grade students scored higher on learning focus (F (2, 1,530) = 11.00, p\.001, hp2= .014), persistence (F (2, 1,530)=15.92, p\.001, hp2= .020), and adaptive motivation (F (2, 1,530)=12.34, p\.001, hp2=.020) than students in senior grades"
