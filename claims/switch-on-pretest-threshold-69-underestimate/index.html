Gorard et al. 2014¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Crossplot analysis of pre- and post-test scores (Figure 2) in the randomised trial; 94 of 308 pupils scored 69 at pre-test, and the report states this means "the progress of some of them will have been underestimated".

"There are pupils with a pre -test score of 69 with post -test scores ranging from 69 to 105. The flattening of the graph shows that some pre -test scores have been constrained by an artificial threshold that is higher than in reality."
