Observational usage analysis of the two-week deployment (Table 2 reports 254 sessions; mean duration 15 minutes 45 seconds, median 11 minutes 4 seconds). The authors state "The feedback from optional forms was notably positive", citing forum posts from teachers, one of whom reported using feedback hints to get more student replies in a second session.
