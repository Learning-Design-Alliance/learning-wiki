Peter Seban 2025¶

Peter Seban, Kamila Urban, Radovan Sikl. (2025). Comparing the effectiveness of multiple text reading and rereading on knowledge retention and metacognitive accuracy. Instructional Science, 53, 337–364. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09686-4

Post-hoc pairwise contrast from the experiment's Results section (descriptive means: MTR WD 12.9 vs RR WD 10.1). The article states participants in the multiple text reading group "achieved higher scores, but this difference was not formally significant"; equivalence was not tested.

"A comparison between the performance of the multiple text reading and rereading group in the one-week delayed test showed that participants in the multiple text reading group achieved higher scores, but this difference was not formally significant."
