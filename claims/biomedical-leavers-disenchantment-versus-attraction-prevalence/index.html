Evidence¶

Rosenzweig 2021¶

Rosenzweig, E. Q., Harackiewicz, J. M., Hecht, C. A., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., Asher, M. W., & Hyde, J. S. (2021). College Students’ Reasons for Leaving Biomedical Fields: Disenchantment with Biomedicine or Attraction to Other Fields? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000456

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive coding of interview responses from 192 students who left biomedical fields, classified by two trained coding teams into disenchantment, attraction, or both. The study reports "thirty-seven students (25.2%) described only “attraction” factors" and 92 described only disenchantment.

"Ninety-two students (62.5%) described only “disenchantment” factors in their explanations of why their plans changed, thirty-seven students (25.2%) described only “attraction” factors, and 18 students (12.2%) described both types of factors with equal weight."
