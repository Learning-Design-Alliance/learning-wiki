Evidence¶

Fateme Shir Mohammadi 2017¶

Fateme Shir Mohammadi. (2017). The Effect of Authentic Problem-Based Vocabulary Tasks on Vocabulary Learning of EFL Learners. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 5(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1154085

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of immediate posttest scores: experimental group (M= 19.62, SD= 1.99) versus control group (M= 18.13, SD= 1.54); the t-test showed "a significant difference between the posttest scores" of the groups.

"independent samples t-test results (t(45)=2.861, Sig=.006) indicate a significant difference between the posttest scores of the control group and the experimental group"
