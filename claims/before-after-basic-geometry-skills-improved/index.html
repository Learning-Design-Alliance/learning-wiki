Evidence¶

Nurwijayanti 2019¶

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

q2 · i? · design · r3

Broad-scale before-after test of basic geometry skills on curved-solid objects, with 85 students before and 82 after. After normality and homogeneity checks, the t-test rejected the null hypothesis, so "the after treatment was 6.66" was better than the before mean of 4.48.

"The average score from the group before treatment was 4.48 and t he after treatment was 6.66."
