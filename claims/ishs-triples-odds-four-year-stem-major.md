### Means 2018

Means, Wang, Wei, Iwatani, & Peters. (2018). Broadening Participation in STEM College Majors: Effects of Attending a STEM-Focused High School. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418806305

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Propensity score-weighted hierarchical model on Texas higher education records for 867 ISHS and 1,997 comparison school survey respondents, controlling for demographics and Grade 8 achievement. The model-adjusted estimate shows "odds ratio = 2.80" for a four-year college STEM major (p = .0008 in Table 4).
