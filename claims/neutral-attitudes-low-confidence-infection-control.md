Attitude results from the cross-sectional survey (N = 420), measured on a nine-item five-point Likert scale scored 9–45 (mean 31.0, SD 8.4). The study reports "The majority of participants (43.8%) demonstrated a neutral attitude" and low strong agreement on curriculum adequacy (12.9%) and comfort confronting breaches (22.4%).
