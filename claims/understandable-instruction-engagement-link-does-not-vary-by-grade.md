Likelihood-ratio test comparing Model 4 (with grade × understandable-science interactions) to Model 2 in the multilevel analysis of 6,465 students; Model 2 was retained. No statistically significant grade differences were detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "none of the interactions of model four is significant (χ obe 2 = 15.685, df = 9, p = 0.07), which means there is no statistically significant difference in the effect of perceptions of understandable science teaching on emotional engagement between grade levels."
