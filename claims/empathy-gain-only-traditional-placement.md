## Evidence

### Quail Michelle 2016

Quail Michelle, Brundage Shelley B, Spitalnick Josh, Allen Peter J, Beilby Janet. (2016). Student self-reported communication skills, knowledge and confidence across standardised patient, virtual and traditional clinical learning environments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0577-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests on the JSE-HPS in the randomised three-condition study; Table 1 prints nursing home empathy d = .40 (95% CI [0.07, 0.32]*), "only members of the nursing home condition self-reported higher post-placement empathy", versus d = -.14 for both SP and VLE, which did not reach significance.

> "However, only members of the nursing home condition self-reported higher post-placement empathy."
