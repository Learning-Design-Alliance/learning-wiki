## Evidence

### Ritchie 2018

Ritchie, S. J., & Tucker-Drob, E. M. (2018). How much does education improve intelligence? A meta-analysis. Psychological Science, 29(8), 1358-1369. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797618774253

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis explicitly included studies "estimating education-intelligence associations after controlling for earlier intelligence" as one of its three design categories, addressing the reverse-causality interpretation of the correlation.

> "We meta-analysed three categories of quasi-experimental studies of educational effects on intelligence: those estimating education-intelligence associations after controlling for earlier intelligence, those using compulsory schooling policy changes as instrumental variables, and those using regression-discontinuity designs on school-entry age cutoffs."
