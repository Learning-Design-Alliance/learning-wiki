## Evidence

### Mahler Daniela 2018

Mahler Daniela, Großschedl Jörg, Harms Ute. (2018). Does motivation matter? – The relationship between teachers' self-efficacy and enthusiasm and students' performance. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0207252. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0207252

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unconditional multilevel models decomposing variance in structural system thinking, procedural system thinking and concept-map correspondence-coefficient scores for 1036 students clustered in 48 teachers' classes. Percentages are as printed; no effect size applies.

> "The results indicate that between-class variance accounts for 27.3, 22.7 and 23.9% of the total variance in these scores. Accordingly, we specified models that account for a multilevel structure."
