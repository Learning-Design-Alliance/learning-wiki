### Mary H. Sawyer 1991

Mary H. Sawyer. (1991). A Review of Research in Revising Instructional Text. Journal of Reading Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1080/10862969109547744

`q2 · i?`

The review reports Mayer's (1979) argument that organizers work only when learners lack prior knowledge subsumers, and that Barnes and Clawson's negative conclusion was criticized for using recall or achievement tests.

> "The best test of advance organizers occurs when material is unfamiliar, technical or otherwise difficult for the learner to relate to his or her existing knowledge"
