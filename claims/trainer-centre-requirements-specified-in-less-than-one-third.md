Descriptive analysis of training centre and trainer requirements across the 42 national curricula. The study reports that "A total of 26.2% (11/42) of the curricula specified requirements for training centres", that trainer qualifications were described in 23.8% (10/42), and that structured mentoring programmes were referenced in 26.2% (11/42).

> "A total of 26.2% (11/42) of the curricula specified requirements for training centres, including the need for a university hospital setting and case complexity criteria."
