## Evidence

### Lautrette Alexandre et al 2020

Lautrette Alexandre et al. (2020). Impact of take-home messages written into slide presentations delivered during lectures on the retention of messages and the residents' knowledge: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02092-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the randomized controlled study: resident knowledge scored per lecture from MCQs written by each lecturer (22 MCQs intervention, 26 control), out of 100 points, one month after the lecture. The means were "63.8 ± 26.2 vs 61.1 ± 31.4 points".

> "The level of knowledge in the intervention lectures did not have higher than that in the control lecture (63.8 ± 26.2 vs 61.1 ± 31.4 points, P = 0.75)."
