## Evidence

### Abbiati Milena 2016

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis (Table 5) of the 311-student cohort prints OR 1.4 [1.0; 1.8], p=0.035 for the intellectually flexible facet, while Table 4 shows it did not significantly differ between selected and non-selected students (p=0.113).

> "However, in logistic regressions, it seemed to significantly increase students’ odds of being selected."
