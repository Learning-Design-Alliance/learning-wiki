## Evidence

### Sinaga 2015

Sinaga, P., Suhandi, A., & Liliasari. (2015). The Effectiveness of Scaffolding Design in Training Writing Skills Physics Teaching Materials. International Journal of Instruction, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085295

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post-test results on making multiple representations of concept, measured with 6 limited essay items (reliability 0.84). Waves and optics showed a medium normalized gain (41%) with d = 0.97; electromagnetism showed a low gain (18%) with d = 0.80.
