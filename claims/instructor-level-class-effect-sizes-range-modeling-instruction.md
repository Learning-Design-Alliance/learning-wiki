### Eric Brewe 2013

Eric Brewe, Adrienne Traxler, Jorge de la Garza, Laird H. Kramer. (2013). Extending positive CLASS results across multiple instructors and multiple classes of Modeling Instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Cohen's d with 95% confidence intervals calculated per instructor across the nine FIU Modeling Instruction sections; effect sizes plotted by instructor in Figure 2, ranging "from d = 0.08 for instructor 5 to d = 0.95 for instructor 4".

> "These are plotted in Figure 2 and range from d = 0.08 for instructor 5 to d = 0.95 for instructor 4.  Based on these effects, students of all instructors – except Instructor 5 – showed positive shifts on the overall CLASS score."

### Eric Brewe 2013 (2)

Eric Brewe, Adrienne Traxler, Jorge de la Garza, Laird H. Kramer. (2013). Extending positive CLASS results across multiple instructors and multiple classes of Modeling Instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

The null result for Instructor 5 in the same multi-section dataset: d = 0.08 with confidence intervals including d = 0, i.e. no detectable attitude shift for that instructor's section.

> "Instructor 5 showed no shift as indicated by the effect of d = 0.08 and the confidence intervals including d = 0."
