### de Lama Carlos 2020

de Lama Carlos, González-Gaya Cristina, Sánchez-Lite Alberto. (2020). An Experimental Test Proposal to Study Human Behaviour in Fires Using Virtual Environments. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s20123607

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-experience self-report from the 300-user evaluation: "100% of the users who completed the experience acknowledged, due to the immersion experience, that the decision-making in a real fire would have been very similar or identical". This is a perceived-validity rating, not a measured transfer test.
