### Emre Sezgin 2019

Emre Sezgin, Simon Lin. (2019). Technology-Based Interventions, Assessments, and Solutions for Safe Driving Training for Adolescents: Rapid Review. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/11942

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis of 16 peer-reviewed studies selected from 828 records in Web of Science and PubMed. The review reports that "parental involvement for promoting safe driving behavior is highly effective" and that smartphone and gamification approaches need further study.

> "Our findings suggest that social influence is effective in technology-based interventions; parental involvement for promoting safe driving behavior is highly effective. However, the use of smartphones and gamification needs more study regarding their implementation and sustainability."
