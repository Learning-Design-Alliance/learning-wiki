## Evidence

### Hernández 2024

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bivariate correlational analysis of survey variables in the full sample (Table 1). The three support dimensions "were positively correlated with behavioral and emotional engagement, whereas teacher support was positively correlated with academic aspirations and expectations"; the cultural and critical dimensions were not linked to aspirations or expectations.

> "Teacher support, school promotion of cultural competence, and critical consciousness socialization were positively correlated with behavioral and emotional engagement, whereas teacher support was positively correlated with academic aspirations and expectations."
