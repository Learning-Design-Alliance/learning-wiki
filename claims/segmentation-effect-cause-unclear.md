## Subclaims

The review reports, citing Spanjers et al. (2010), that segmentation studies "have thus far always incorporated pauses between segments", confounding processing time with the segment-end signal; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Spanjers et al. (2010) review the literature on the segmentation effect, which shows that studies have thus far always incorporated pauses between segments. They point out that as a consequence, the cause of the positive effect of segmentation is not entirely clear"
