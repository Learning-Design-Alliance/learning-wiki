## Evidence

### EL-Andari R 2023

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre-post simulation program reported in the review: 11 first- to third-year cardiothoracic surgery residents used a 3-pronged approach of instructional videos, a plastic mitral valve model, and evaluation on a porcine heart model with annuloplasty rings; performance videos from peripheral and head-mounted cameras were used for assessment.

> "There was a significant improvement in feedback scores on all 11 points of the assessment. Furthermore, mean time to completion was significantly reduced after the program (31 [SD 9] min v. 25 [SD 6] min) (p = 0.03)."
