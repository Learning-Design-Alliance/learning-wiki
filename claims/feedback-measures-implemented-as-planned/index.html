q2 · i? · causal · r2

Implementation documentation from vendor online systems and attendance records in the eight study districts. Teachers present in spring of Year 2 averaged 6.8 sessions across two years instead of the intended eight, primarily due to teacher mobility.

"The average number of feedback sessions per teacher was 3.7 in Year 1 and 3.9 in Year 2."
