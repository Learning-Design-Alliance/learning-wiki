# A warning message about potential AI mistakes increases seventh-graders' hint requests in a math intelligent tutoring system

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students shown a warning that the pedagogical agent can make mistakes requested hints more frequently than students shown a neutral message, even though system behavior was identical. [→ Tomohiro Nagashima 2026](#tomohiro-nagashima-2026)

## Evidence

### Tomohiro Nagashima 2026

Tomohiro Nagashima, Mirella Hladký, and Vera Rief. (2026). Warning About AI Fallibility Increases Help-Seeking in an Intelligent Tutoring System. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03822

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom experiment at a secondary school in Tokyo with 252 seventh-grade students randomly assigned to Mistake or NoMistake tutor versions; linear regression on log data showed "students in the Mistake condition requested hints more frequently than those in the NoMistake condition" (β=−0.33, t(235)=−2.33, p=.02).
