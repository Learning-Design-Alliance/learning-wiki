Comparison of correct-answer percentages across Tables 4.2-4.5 for the written survey: 74 US students versus 52 Romanian students. The authors themselves flag the unequal group sizes as disqualifying the comparison.

> "American students did in average slightly better than Romanian students did (see Tables 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5), even though a comparison of this type has no relevance, the number of Romanian students being smaller than the number of American students."
