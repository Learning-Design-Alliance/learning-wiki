Scerif 2023¶

Making the Executive 'Function' for the Foundations of Mathematics: the Need for Explicit Theories of Change for Early Interventions. q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of longitudinal studies (e.g. Clark et al. 2013; Fuhs et al. 2014; Coolen et al. 2021; Ribner et al. 2017) reporting that "preschool EF as a robust predictor of mathematical skills" across ages 3 to school entry and beyond.

"Overall, the extant literature converges on preschool EF as a robust predictor of mathematical skills, both during preschool and later in development."
