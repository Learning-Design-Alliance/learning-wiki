## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Having hope for the future is associated with higher self-rated academic performance among Japanese eighth graders (β = 0.21, 95% CI 0.10 to 0.32 after PS matching). [→ Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022](#kashiwabara-tomoka-2022)

## Evidence

### Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022

Kashiwabara Tomoka, Fujiwara Takeo, Doi Satomi, Yamaoka Yui. (2022). Association between Hope for the Future and Academic Performance in Adolescents: Results from the K-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191911890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity-score-matched analysis of the population-based K-CHILD cross-sectional survey (3477 eighth graders in Kochi Prefecture, 2016). The abstract reports the matched estimate: "β = 0.21, 95% confidence interval (Confidence Interval (CI) = 0.10 to 0.32)" for self-rated academic performance.

> "In the model after PS matching, the significant difference in both academic performance and resilience remained (both p < 0.001)."
