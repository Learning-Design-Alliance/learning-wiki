`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same expert-test qualitative analysis applied to ChatGPT4: none of its free responses fell in the highly confused category, 20 of 23 were "exactly correct," and the three remaining responses were "nearly perfect."

> "ChatGPT4, by contrast, gave no answers in this “highly confused” category. We assessed 20 of its 23 free responses as being “exactly correct,” as one would expect from an expert physicist."
