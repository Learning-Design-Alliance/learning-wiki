## Evidence

### Frey 2015

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Feil, E. G., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., & Forness, S. (2015). First Step to Success: Applications to Preschoolers at Risk of Developing Autism Spectrum Disorders. Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397-407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581234

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Subsample analysis (n=34; 17 intervention, 17 control) of a randomized controlled trial of Preschool First Step, using covariate-adjusted regression models. "Hedges' g effect sizes ranged from" -0.65 to -1.12 for teacher-reported symptom reductions; Table 3 reports SSiS Autism g = -0.65 (p = .020), ESP MBI g = -1.12 (p = .006), ESP ABS g = -0.98 (p = .004).

> "Children at risk for developing ASD who received the First Step intervention improved on all teacher-reported measures assessing symptoms (autism, maladaptive, and aggressive behavior) and on two of the four measures of functioning compared to children in the control condition."
