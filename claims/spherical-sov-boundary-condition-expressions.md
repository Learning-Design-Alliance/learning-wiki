### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with Separation of Variables. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of exam solutions using SoV in spherical coordinates: "almost two-thirds (61%, N=298 of 488) included correct expres- sions for all explicit and implicit boundary conditions". The article reports most remaining solutions never expressed the implicit conditions at r = 0 and/or r = ∞, though most of those still correctly eliminated the Al or Bl terms.

> "Of the so- lutions that utilized SoV on the exams (N=488), almost two-thirds (61%, N=298 of 488) included correct expres- sions for all explicit and implicit boundary conditions."
