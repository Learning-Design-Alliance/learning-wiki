## Evidence

### Jiang 2016

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test of post-test writing achievement: experimental mean 84.67 versus comparison mean 78.57, t = 4.48, p = 0.007, favoring the cooperative writing group.

> "However, after the adoption of cooperative writing, the difference reached a statistical significance in the post-test (p =0.007<0.05) as shown in Table 4."

### Jiang 2016 (2)

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests show the experimental group improved significantly (mean gain 6.58 points, p = 0.009) while the comparison group's change of 0.38 was not significant (p = 0.324).

> "Table 5 clearly illustrates the significant difference in the experimental group (p =0.009<0.05) and the insignificant difference for the comparison group (p = 0.324>0.05), which proves that cooperative writing approach has increa sed the participants’ writing ability effectively."
