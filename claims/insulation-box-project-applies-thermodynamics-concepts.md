## Evidence

### Mouhamadou Thiam 2021

Mouhamadou Thiam. (2021). Basic thermodynamics to save lives. Science Activities, 58(3), 89–97. https://doi.org/10.1080/00368121.2021.1957756

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Teacher-reported evaluation of an eighteen-period classroom activity with 42 grade-seven students in two intact groups, who designed, built, and tested insulation boxes keeping sheep organs between 4 °C and 8 °C. The author concludes students were "sufficiently challenging for students"-tested, i.e. able to apply the concepts and practices; no effect sizes are reported.

> "It is clear that this activity was sufficiently challenging for students and that they were able to apply their understanding of basic thermodynamics concepts and scientific and engineering practices."
