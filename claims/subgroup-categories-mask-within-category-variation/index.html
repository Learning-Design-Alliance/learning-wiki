Conceptual discussion with worked examples: 2024–2025 federal FRL income guidelines ranging from less than $26,572 to $97,532, and multilingual learners varying from zero English experience to near the classification's top boundary. Descriptive, not empirical.

"Although a singular category—such as students eligible for free- or reduced-price lunch (FRL) or multilingual learners—may seem straightforward, that category is comprised of students who occupy a spectrum of values within that category."
