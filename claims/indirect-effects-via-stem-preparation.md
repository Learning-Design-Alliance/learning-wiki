## Evidence

### Rozek 2016

Rozek, Svoboda, Harackiewicz, Hulleman, & Hyde. (2016). Utility-value intervention with parents increases students' STEM preparation and career pursuit. PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1607386114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis within the longitudinal SEM tested total indirect effects of the intervention on each 5-y outcome through the two high-school preparation variables. Three of four were significant; the STEM-major path was not. No effect sizes printed.

> "Specifically, there were significant indirect effects of the intervention on college STEM course-taking ( z = 2.54, P = 0.01), STEM career aspirations ( z = 2.19, P = 0.03), and perceived STEM value ( z = 2.15, P = 0.03). The total indirect effect on the likelihood of desiring a STEM major was not significant ( z = 1.67, P = 0.09)."
