## Evidence

### Dahl-Leonard 2024

Dahl-Leonard, K., Hall, C., & Peacott, D. (2024). A meta-analysis of technology-delivered literacy instruction for elementary students. Education Tech Research Dev (2024) 72:1507–1538. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10354-0

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 53 experimental or quasi-experimental studies of technology-delivered literacy instruction for K-5 students, restricted to norm- or criterion-referenced outcome measures, estimated with Hedges' g. The pooled effect was "g = 0.24 (95% CI [.15, .32], p < .001)".

> "The average effect on combined outcomes was estimated as g = 0.24 (95% CI [.15, .32], p < .001), indicating a small positive and significant effect of technology-delivered literacy instruction on elementary student outcomes."
