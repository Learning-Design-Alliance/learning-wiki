Melnick 2024¶

Melnick, H., & García, E. (2024). Progressing toward universal prekindergarten in California [Brief]. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/progressing-toward-universal-prekindergarten-california

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of TK enrollment and eligibility data from the California Department of Education and Census estimates. The brief states the "uptake rate" declined because "the number of eligible children has increased more rapidly than the number of additional children enrolling."

"While the numbers of TK enrollees has risen, the proportion of eligible 4-year-olds enrolled in TK (the uptake rate) has declined over the past 2 years. This is because the number of eligible children has increased more rapidly than the number of additional children enrolling."
