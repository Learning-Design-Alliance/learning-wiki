Evidence¶

Abdelghani 2023¶

Abdelghani, R., Wang, Y.-H., Yuan, X., Wang, T., Lucas, P., Sauzéon, H., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2023). GPT-3-driven pedagogical agents for training children's curious question-asking skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://hal.science/hal-03890447v1

q3 · i? · causal · r?

ANCOVA with percentage of divergent questions as dependent variable and curiosity trait score (parent-reported) as covariate, across the three conditions of the 75-student experiment. Fisher z-transformation showed the open agent's correlation differed significantly from both incentive agents.

"Results indicate a significant interaction between the divergent-QA performance and the curiosity scores, within the three conditions (F(2,71) = 4.06, p-value=0.02). Pairwise comparisons are then conducted in order to identify groups that are statistically different : we apply Bonferroni's multiple test correction. The post-hoc analysis show a statistically-significant difference only for the open agent condition (p-value=0.006)"
