## Evidence

### Daly R 2026

Daly R, Tunney E, Spooner M, Offiah G, Flood K, Kent F. (2026). Case‐based learning (CBL) in undergraduate health professions education: A realist review. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70179

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of clinician-facilitator evidence, citing Hay (2001), reports an RCT in which students in clinician-facilitated CBL had significantly higher examination scores than those with a non-clinical tutor; the review states clinicians' experience gave facilitators credibility, building trust in the learning achieved.

> "A statistically significant difference in examination performance was found by randomised control trial in students participating in CBL facilitated by a clinician compared to non‐clinicians."
