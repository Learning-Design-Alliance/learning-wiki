## Evidence

### Choi Kai Yip 2026

Choi Kai Yip, Chan Ka Wing, Yau Hiu Ching, Lau Yuet Yan, Leung Tsz Wing. (2026). Perceptual learning enhances diabetic retinopathy detection skills in novice optometry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08691-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analyses between fixation hits and diagnostic accuracy (Fig. 5). In the SS group the correlation was non-significant (r = −0.29, p = 0.20) and at PL baseline non-significant (r = 0.14, p = 0.55); after training a moderate significant positive correlation emerged in the PL group (r = 0.50, p = 0.03), and the Fisher's Z-test "confirmed that this post-training correlation in the PL group was significantly different" from the senior group's.

> "A Fisher's Z-test confirmed that this post-training correlation in the PL group was significantly different from the insignificant correlation observed in the SS group (Z = −2.47, p = 0.01)."
