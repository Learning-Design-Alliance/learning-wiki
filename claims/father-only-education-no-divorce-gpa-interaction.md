## Evidence

### Nilsen 2020

Nilsen, S. A., Breivik, K., Wold, B., Askeland, K. G., Sivertsen, B., Hysing, M., & Bøe, T. (2020). Divorce and adolescent academic achievement: Heterogeneity in the associations by parental education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229183

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS models using the mother/father/both categorization above ISCED 2 (Table 3; Fig 1B). Interactions were significant for only-mother (b = -0.236, p < 0.05) and both-parents (b = -0.248, p < 0.05) but not father-only (b = -0.111, non-significant); equivalence was not tested.

> "If only the father had above ISCED 2 qualifications, however, no significant interaction with parental divorce was observed (see Fig 1B)."
