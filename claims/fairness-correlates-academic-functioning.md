### Martinez 2026

Martinez, A. C., Green, C. D., Peugh, J. L., & Becker, S. P. (2026). Perceived School Fairness and Academic Functioning in Early Adolescents: Differential Associations for Adolescents with or Without ADHD? Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01419-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlations (Table 2) among 341 adolescents; the table prints r = −0.42 for fairness with attitude to school, r = 0.23 with study skills, and r = −0.29 with learning problems, all p < .001.

> "Lower perceived school fairness was associated with a more negative attitude to school (p <.001), poorer study skills (p <.001), and higher learning problems (p <.001)"
