# Domain-driven explanations significantly increase teachers' understandability, trust, and acceptance compared to data-driven explanations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed statistically significant increases in understandability (W = 80.5, p = 0.005), learned trust (W = 52, p = 0.002), and acceptance (W = 22.5, p = 0.003) after domain-driven explanations following data-driven ones. ## Evidence

### Yael Feldman-Maggor 2025

Yael Feldman-Maggor, Mutlu Cukurova, Carmel Kent, Giora Alexandron. (2025). The Impact of Explainable AI on Teachers' Trust and Acceptance of AI EdTech Recommendations: The Power of Domain-specific Explanations. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00486-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject staged experiment with 41 in-service chemistry teachers using GrouPer; one-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests compared each construct after data-driven versus domain-driven explanations. The article reports "statistically significant results for all the variables" with the printed W and p values; no effect size was printed.

> "The test yielded statistically significant results for all the variables: Understandability—W = 80.5, p = 0.005**; Learned trust—W = 52, p = 0.002**; Acceptance—W = 22.5, p = 0.003**."
