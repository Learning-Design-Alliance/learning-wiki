# Exam scores correlate positively with correct submissions and negatively with incorrect submissions and solution access

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Correct submissions were positively correlated with exam 1 scores (r = .28), incorrect submissions were negatively correlated with exam scores (r = −.40), and incorrect submissions were negatively correlated with accessing solutions (r = −.21). [→ Hou 2025](#hou-2025)

## Evidence

### Hou 2025

Hou, C., Kuhlmann, S. L., Greene, J. A., Bernacki, M. L., & Hogan, K. A. (2025). Process mining measures students' help-seeking transitions when completing assignments in an online learning and assessment platform. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09442-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate correlational analysis of 482 students' trace frequencies and first-unit exam scores found "incorrect submissions were statistically significant negatively correlated with exam scores ( r = −.40, p <.01)"; correct submissions correlated positively (r = .28) and incorrect submissions correlated negatively with solution access (r = −.21).

> "Correct submissions were statistically significantly positively correlated with exam one scores (r =.28, p <.05), incorrect submissions were statistically significant negatively correlated with exam scores ( r = −.40, p <.01), and incorrect submissions were statistically significantly negatively correlated with accessing solutions (r = −.21, p <.01)"
