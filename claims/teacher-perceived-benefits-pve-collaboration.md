## Evidence

### Al-Saaideh M 2015

Al-Saaideh M, Nasr-allah Al-Zyoud MS. (2015). Benefits of teaching interdisciplinary subjects collaboratively in Jordanian pre-vocational education. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2461

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with the five participating teachers; themes extracted from transcripts reviewed by the teachers themselves. Reported benefits include better motivation and enthusiasm, better integration between vocational and academic subjects, better vocational guidance, better observation of behavior and time efficiency, and better training on skills; "teachers agreed that collaborative teaching enhances PVE delivery".

> "In addition, aforementioned benefits of collaborative teaching in PVE, teachers agreed that collaborative teaching enhances PVE delivery."
