In the matched pre–post analysis (n = 77), the seven remaining subscales showed no significant change; pre-test scores ranged from 4.13 to 4.54 and "effect sizes were negligible (all |g| ≤ 0.17)".

> "Of the ten CSAS subscales, three showed significant pre–post change — norms, empathy, and costs — whereas the remaining seven (awareness, actions, ability, connectedness, benefits, seriousness, and intention) did not (all p > 0.05)"
