`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The introduction reports, citing Van der Graaf et al. (2023), a read-and-write task study with university students in a general course using five pop-up scaffolds in a digital learning environment; scaffold purposes followed SRL stages, timing came from a previous study's data, and content was personalized based on learners' preceding SRL activities. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Compared to no and non-personalized scaffolds, personalized scaffolds showed higher compliance, which was linked to better essay scores."
