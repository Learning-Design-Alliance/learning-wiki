## Evidence

### Baljon 2021

Baljon, A.R.C., Alter, J., & Bresciani Ludvik, M. (2021). Embodied engagement with scientific concepts: An exploration into emergent learning. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Transcript analysis of the three focus groups found science students reasoned through abstract formulas while dancers expressed themselves through movement; students reported that a covalent bond shown as "holding hands" helped understanding. No effect size is reported.

> "The students therefore had a hard time finding the words to fill the gap between the disciplines. They bridged that gap successfully by using visuals."
