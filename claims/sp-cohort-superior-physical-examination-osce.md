### Taylor Silas 2019 (2)

Taylor Silas, Haywood Matthew, Shulruf Boaz. (2019). Comparison of the effects of simulated patient clinical skill training and student roleplay on objective structured clinical examination performance among medical students in Australia. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2019.16.3

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretive explanation (Discussion) for the unexpected procedural-skills advantage: rapport established through good generic communication enables examinations to be conducted sensitively and fluently. This is an interpretation, not a tested mediation.

> "Whilst performing physical examinations and conducting procedures on patients may at first glance appear to be related purely to technical proficiency, these tasks do require patient compliance and cooperation. Hence, good generic communication skills (including non-verbal communication skills) can be expected to facilitate improved performance beyond this domain"
