### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory multiple regressions on clinical tests: "individual learning parameters predicted speed in reading a text passage" and single-word reading at the One-minute test, which the authors link to item-based processing supporting fluent reading in Italian.

> "In particular, individual learning parameters predicted speed in reading a text passage and the ability to read correctly and rapidly single words at the One-minute reading test"

### Marinelli CV 2021 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory regressions on spelling: learning parameters "significantly predicted the accuracy in spelling a meaningful text passage" and total DDO-2 accuracy, which includes the section of words with ambiguous transcription.

### Marinelli CV 2021 (3)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696
