WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.positiveaction.net

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

School-level archival data from the Hawaii randomized controlled trial (20 schools, 2,660 grade 5 students) show retention of "1 to 1,000 students" in Positive Action schools versus 6 per 1,000 in comparison schools, a statistically significant difference with WWC-computed effect size 1.09 and improvement index +36.
