# Linguistically varying an instructional video into easy, moderate, and difficult versions does not significantly affect students’ expectancies of success or intrinsic task value compared with the moderate version

## Evidence

### Lina Wirth 2026

Lina Wirth, Timo Ehmke, Jan Retelsdorf, Poldi Kuhl. (2026). Linguistic demands of instructions: Effects on students’ expectancy-value beliefs. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09799-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized experiment assigned 123 pre-service teachers to linguistically easy (n = 42), moderate (n = 43), or difficult (n = 38) versions of a statistics video. In a path model, "the effects on students’ expectancies of success  and intrinsic task value  were not significant," so equivalence was not tested.

> "The results showed that the effects on students’ expectancies of success  and intrinsic task value  were not significant."
