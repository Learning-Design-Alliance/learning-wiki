## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests found that scores rose from pre-test to post-test for diagnosis (effect size r=0.738), treatment planning (r=0.686), and patient counseling (r=0.420), all P<.001. [→ Sapci A Hasan 2026](#sapci-a-hasan-2026)

Sapci A Hasan, Gupta Gaurav Kumar, Tran Tu K, Vejdanihemmat Mohsen, Qunaibi Eyad A, Al-Qaaneh Ayman M, Ismail Baraa F, Muhidat Hussam I, Rageh Farhia S, Musallam Najwa A, Fawzy Alaa K. (2026). `q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Skill-specific analysis of the same 326-participant pre-post crossover study, reported in Table 3 and Figure 3 with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. Table 3 prints effect sizes r=0.738 (diagnosis), r=0.686 (treatment planning), and r=0.420 (patient counseling), all P<.001.

> "Significant score increases were observed across skills of diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient counseling (Table 3; Figure 3; P<.001 for all)."
