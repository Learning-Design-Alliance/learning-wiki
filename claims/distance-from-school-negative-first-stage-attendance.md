## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First-stage regression of days present on distance in miles and all covariates, with school, year, and grade fixed effects (Table 5). The article reports coefficients on distance that "hover around a value of approximately -0.50"; these are unstandardized regression coefficients, and no standardized effect size is printed for this first-stage finding.

> "The negative and statistically significant coefficients on distance indicate that as a student's mileage from school increases, the number of days that a student is present decreases."
