### Todd E. Elder and Darren H. Lubotsky 2009

Todd E. Elder and Darren H. Lubotsky. (2009). Kindergarten Entrance Age and Children’s Achievement: Impacts of State Policies, Family Background, and Peers. Journal of Human Resources. https://doi.org/10.3368/jhr.44.3.641

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The article reports a family-background moderator: the entrance-age association is “especially pronounced among children from upper-income families.” The authors link this to greater pre-kindergarten skill accumulation in those families, consistent with their prior-skills interpretation. No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "The association between achievement test scores and entrance age appears during the first months of kindergarten, declines sharply in subsequent years, and is especially pronounced among children from upper-income families, a group likely to have accumulated the most skills prior to school entry."
