# Fluency Formula™ shows a negative but statistically non-significant effect on second-grade reading comprehension (effect size –0.28, improvement index –11)

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the same randomized controlled trial, Fluency Formula™ students scored lower than comparison students on the Woodcock-Johnson III Passage Comprehension subtest, with an effect size of –0.28 that was not statistically significant. ## Evidence

### Fluency Formula™ WWC Intervention Report 2007

Fluency Formula™ WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://teacher.scholastic.com/products/fluencyformula/pdfs/FF_EffectivessReport.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Randomized controlled trial (Sivin-Kachala & Bialo, 2005), 128 second-grade students; WWC-computed comprehension effect size on the Woodcock-Johnson III Passage Comprehension subtest was –0.28, "ns", improvement index –11 (Appendix A3.2).

> "The WWC did not find a statistically significant difference between the groups. However, the effect was negative and substantively important (that is, an effect size of at least –.25)."
