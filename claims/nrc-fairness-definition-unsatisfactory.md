### Mathieu Bouville 2008

Mathieu Bouville. (2008). Exam fairness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4235 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Critical analysis of the NRC definition in the Possible Interpretations section: the article lists four defects including reliance on undefined concepts and unexplained groups, and concludes "This definition is therefore unsatisfactory."

> "in order to know whether one 'overestimate[s] the competencies' one needs to know independently what these competencies are, which may require another test (which would have to meet the same criterion of fairness); and (iv) it does not specify what these "particular groups" are"
