## Evidence

### Wu Zeyu 2024

Wu Zeyu, Huang Yiling, Lyu Ling, Huang Yu, Ping Fan. (2024). The efficacy of simulation-based learning versus non-simulation-based learning in endocrinology education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06010-z

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 14 randomized controlled trials reporting post-intervention skill test scores in endocrinology education. Pooled results indicated "the superiority of SBL over NSBL" with SMD = 1.56 (95% CI 1.11–2.01) and very high heterogeneity (I2 = 93%).

> "14 studies [15, 16, 19–24, 28–30, 32, 33, 35] reported post-intervention skill test scores (801 subjects in the SBL cohort and 793 subjects in the NSBL cohort), with the pooled results indicating the superiority of SBL over NSBL (SMD = 1.56; 95% CI: 1.11–2.01, P < 0.00001, I2 = 93%)"
