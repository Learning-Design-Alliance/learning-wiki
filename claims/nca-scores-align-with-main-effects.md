# NCA scores of IIU input vectors order action types identically to the main-effects ordering (Spearman's ρ = 1)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The ordering of action types by mean NCA scores of IIU vectors perfectly matches the ordering by average probability of correct responses, with Spearman's rank correlation ρ = 1. [→ Lu 2024](#lu-2024)

## Evidence

### Lu 2024

Lu, Y., Tong, L., & Cheng, Y. (2024). Advanced Knowledge Tracing: Incorporating Process Data and Curricula Information via an Attention-Based Framework for Accuracy and Interpretability. Journal of Educational Data Mining, 16(2). https://osf.io/mdpzc/

`q2 · i1`

Analysis of real action logs: IIU vectors were saved, resampled with SMOTE (reference class "answer requested", count 686862), and transformed via Neighborhood Components Analysis (Table 7). The authors report "a perfect Spearman’s rank correlation (ρ = 1)".

> "This alignment is further substantiated by a perfect Spearman’s rank correlation (ρ = 1), indicating a direct correspondence between the two orders."
