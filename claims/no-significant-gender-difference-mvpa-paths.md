## Evidence

### Byman 2026

Byman, A., Ulmanen, S., Pyhältö, K., Syväoja, H., Kulmala, J., & Tammelin, T. H. (2026). Gendered patterns in the associations between in-school physical activity, classroom peer ecology, and academic achievement among primary school students. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351971

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Satorra–Bentler scaled chi-square difference tests in the MVPA model showed the gender equality constraints did not significantly worsen fit. The article reports that "the associations did not significantly differ by gender".

> "A model comparison showed that constraining the significant paths to be equal across genders did not significantly worsen model fits (both p > .05). Thus, although parameter estimates differed between boys and girls, the results suggest that the associations did not significantly differ by gender."
