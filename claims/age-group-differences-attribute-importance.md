### Conlon Peter 2012

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA across five age categories (Wilk's Lambda = .89, F(44, 4963.95) = 3.58, p < .001) with Tukey HSD post hocs. The article also reports the 51–60 group scored ethical behaviour higher than the 20–30 and 31–40 groups (d's = 0.39, 0.31) and the >61 group scored knowledge of the veterinary profession higher than the 51–60 group (d = 0.29).

> "20–30 year-old respondents, 31–40 year-old respondents, and 41–50 year-old respondents scored communication skills significantly higher than both 51–60 (all ps < .001, d’s= 0.46, 0.46, 0.34 respectively) and >61 year-olds (all ps < .001, d’s = 0.55, 0.54, 0.44 respectively)"
