## Evidence

### Seo Yon Hee 2024

Seo Yon Hee, Lim Sun-Og. (2024). Korean Triage and Acuity Scale education using role-playing and its effects on triage competency: A quasi-experimental design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 78-student quasi-experiment, triage performance ability was measured with a 10-item case-based tool (CVI 0.90) covering KTAS Levels 1–4. Post-test means were 5.31 (role-playing) versus 4.26 (lecture), and the article reports the experimental group "showed significant improvement".

> "When the pre-post triage performance ability of the two groups was verified, the experimental group showed significant improvement (t = 3.106, p = .003)."
