Qualitative strand findings on AI-supported learning design from lesson plans and reflections in the professional development study. As one teacher stated, "AI became a starting point for thinking. I did not simply copy the outputs." Another emphasized that teachers must decide what is educationally meaningful.

"AI became a starting point for thinking. I did not simply copy the outputs, but used them to improve and reshape my lesson design."
