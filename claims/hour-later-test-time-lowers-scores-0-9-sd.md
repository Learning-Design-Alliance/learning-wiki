## Evidence

### Sievertsen HH 2016

Sievertsen HH, Gino F, Piovesan M. (2016). Cognitive fatigue influences students' performance on standardized tests. PNAS 113(10): 2621–2624. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1516947113

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS analysis of all Danish National Tests 2009/10–2012/13, controlling for school, grade, subject, day-of-week and test-year fixed effects plus parental education, income, birth weight, sex, spring child and origin. The model estimates that the "test score is reduced by 0.9% (95% CI, 0.7–1.0%) of an SD for every hour" later in the day. The sample comprises 2,034,964 observations from 2,105 schools and 570,376 students. No standardized effect size is printed.
