## Evidence

### Akçay 2016

Nilüfer Okur Akçay. (2016). Implementation of Cooperative Learning Model in Preschool. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p83

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison from the same semi-experimental pretest-posttest study (JG N=20, CG N=18). The pretest t-test found "no significant difference between them (t(36)=-1.21; p>0.05)"; equivalence beyond the non-significant test was not tested.

> "it was determined that the arithmetic mean of the pretest results of the experimental group was 1.70 and standard deviation was 1.34; the arithmetic mean of the control group was 2.22 and the standard deviation was 1.30, and there was no significant difference between them (t(36)=-1.21; p>0.05)"
