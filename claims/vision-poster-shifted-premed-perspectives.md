### Jessica L. Rosenberg 2025

Jessica L. Rosenberg, Nancy Holincheck. (2025). Building Student Understanding of Quantum Information Science and Engineering through Projects on Applications to Medical Technologies. arXiv. [doi:10.1109/qce65121.2025.20527](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce65121.2025.20527)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative small-group interview at the end of the in-person Pathways week; a student (Vivienne) reported the poster research "completely shifted my perspective of quantum" away from a premed-only route. Similar statements were made by Chloe and another student.

> "It's completely shifted my perspective of quantum. It's hard to determine what I want to do, but when I was looking into stuff for the Quantum World Congress, it was, I used to be very focused on like a doctor route, right? Premed, stuff like that."
