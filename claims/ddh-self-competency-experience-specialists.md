## Evidence

### Şükrü Gönen E 2025

Şükrü Gönen E, Taşkın Egici M, Gönen E. (2025). Knowledge and Awareness of Family Physicians as Key Stakeholders Regarding Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip and the Nationwide Screening Program. Acta Orthop Traumatol Turc. https://doi.org/10.5152/j.aott.2025.24034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 401 family physicians using a 0–10 self-competency rating; 321 (80.0%) rated themselves 5 or above, and group comparisons reached significance for experience, specialist status, and DDH follow-up.

> "It was found that physicians with over 10 years of experience, specialist physicians, and those who followed up with children diagnosed with DDH considered themselves more competent (P = .003; P = .020; P = .011; P < .001, respectively)."
