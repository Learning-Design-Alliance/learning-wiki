### Song Hongfeng 2026

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

R3STEP multinomial logistic regressions on the three-wave sample (N = 379) estimating odds of profile membership per one-SD increase in each Wave 1 resource; learning agility showed the clearest differentiation across profiles.

> "Higher learning agility was associated with greater odds of Positive Empowerment relative to Anxious Acceptance (OR = 1.899) and Low-Perception Detached (OR = 4.873)."

### Song Hongfeng 2026 (2)

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

R3STEP results (Table 5) show a scope limit: digital literacy failed to separate Anxious Acceptance from Low-Perception Detached while the other resources did, so the resource pattern is not uniform across profiles.

> "Learning agility, future work self-salience, and perceived university digital support also favored Anxious Acceptance over Low-Perception Detached, whereas digital literacy did not significantly differentiate these two profiles."
