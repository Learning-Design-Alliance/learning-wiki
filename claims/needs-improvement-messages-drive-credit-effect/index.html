Kraft and Rogers 2013¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Separate ITT estimates by treatment arm from the same blocked randomized field trial. The improvement information effect was "8.8 percentage point (p=0.016)"; the positive information effect was 4.5 percentage points (p=0.236), not significant.
