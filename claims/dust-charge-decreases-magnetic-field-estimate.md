`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical estimation (not a direct measurement): students solved the charging equation using Langmuir-probe values of electron density and temperature, assumed a constant radius of 100 nm because dust size was not measured, and computed dust charge from the surface potential. The article describes the plotted trend as gradual decrease.

> "The result is plotted in figure 7 which also shows a gradual decrease in dust charge with an increasing magnetic field strength."
