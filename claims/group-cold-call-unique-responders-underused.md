# Group cold calling produced a higher proportion of unique responders than individual cold calling, yet GTAs used it less frequently

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` GTAs were significantly more likely to call on new (unique) students after group work than without group discussion time (Cliff's d = −0.41). [→ Becker Erin A. 2017](#becker-erin-a-2017)
`q3 i2` Despite producing more unique participants, group cold call was used less frequently than individual cold call (Cliff's d = 0.54). [→ Becker Erin A. 2017 (2)](#becker-erin-a-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Participation-type analysis across 45 observed sections comparing individual versus group cold call; mean unique responder rates were 81% and 89% respectively, with "Cliff's d = −0.41" (a medium effect).

> "GTAs were significantly more likely to call on new students after group work than when no group work time was provided (Cliff's d = −0.41, 95% CI [−0.6, −0.18], p = 8 × 10−4; Figure 1A)."

### Becker Erin A. 2017 (2)

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Frequency comparison of the two cold-call mechanisms in the same 45-section dataset: "the group cold-call participation mechanism was used less frequently by GTAs than individual cold call" with Cliff's d = 0.54, a medium effect.

> "Despite being more effective in producing unique participants, the group cold-call participation mechanism was used less frequently by GTAs than individual cold call (Cliff's d = 0.54, 95% CI [0.33, 0.71], p = 9.2 × 10−6; Figure 1B)."
