## Evidence

### Meurling Lisbet 2013

Meurling Lisbet, Hedman Leif, Sandahl Christer, Felländer-Tsai Li, Wallin Carl-Johan. (2013). Systematic simulation-based team training in a Swedish intensive care unit: a diverse response among critical care professions. BMJ quality & safety 22(6):485-494. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2012-000994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple regression on hospital HR data (fiscal years 2006-10) for the intervention ICU and a comparable control ICU. Nurses quitting fell by a mean of 2.2% per year in the intervention ICU (95% CI −0.05 to 0.01, p=0.06) versus an increase of 1.7% per year in the control ICU (p=0.04).

> "The yearly changes in nurses quitting their job was significantly different between the two wards, p=0.006. No significant change for nurse assistants was observed."
