M. Young and V. Dulewicz. (2025). A model of command, leadership and management competency as a predictor of the performance, potential and rate of advancement of Royal Navy senior officers. Military Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/08995605.2025.2493392

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 7) of the Interact supra-competency scale score against the three DVs. The article reports both Performance and Potential correlations are "negative" while the ROA correlation is significantly positive (r = .120**).

> "The Interact Scale Score is significantly correlated at the 5% level with Performance and at 1% with assessed Potential for next rank but both relationships are negative. However, as noted above, the Interact Scale Score is significantly positively correlated at the 1% level with previous ROA."
