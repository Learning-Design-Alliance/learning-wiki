Cirkony, C., Tytler, R., & Hubber, P. (2022). Designing and delivering representation-focused science lessons in a digital learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10094-z

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive case-study analysis of the case group's responses to Activity 5.4, following a video animation of particles in motion. "All three case-study students were able to express the idea of spatial proximity and motion of particles for all three states" in both text and diagrams.
