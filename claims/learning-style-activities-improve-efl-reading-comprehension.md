## Evidence

### Özgül Balcı 2017

Özgül Balcı. (2017). The Effects of Learning-Style Based Activities on Students' Reading Comprehension Skills and Self-Efficacy Perceptions in English Foreign Language Classes. Higher Education Studies, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n4p35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental matching-only pretest-posttest control group study with 39 experimental and 39 control freshmen (Elementary Mathematics Education, Necmettin Erbakan University). One-way ANCOVA controlling for pre-test scores found post-test means of "22.33 for the experimental group" versus 18.74 for controls, F(1,75)=6.58, p<0.05.

> "The ANCOVA result shows that the reading comprehension achievement of the experimental group was significantly better than that of the control group [F(1,75)=6.58, p<0.05], as shown in Table 5."
