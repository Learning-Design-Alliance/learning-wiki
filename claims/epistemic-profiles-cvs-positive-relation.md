`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CVS covariate means across profiles in four samples (Fig. 4). The sophisticated profile consistently showed above-average means; the highly sophisticated and intermediate profiles clearly showed above-average estimates in three samples.

> "Overall, these results point to a positive relation between the level of sophistication of the epistemic belief profiles and the understanding of the CVS."
