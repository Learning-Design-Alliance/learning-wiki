Evidence¶

Vermunt 2004¶

Vermunt, J. D. & Vermetten, Y. J. (2004). Patterns in Student Learning: Relationships Between Learning Strategies, Conceptions of Learning, and Learning Orientations. Educational Psychology Review. https://dspacelibrary.uu.nl/handle/1874/11958

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review reports Beishuizen et al. (1994) had Psychology students complete the ILS and perform a task with a study text on computer, forming four processing-regulation combination groups. Congruent combinations achieved good results; the incongruent combinations "performed much worse." No effect sizes printed.

"The results showed that students who combined self-regulation with deep processing, and students who combined external regulation with stepwise (“surface”) processing achieved good results on this task."

Vermunt 2004 (2)¶

Vermunt, J. D. & Vermetten, Y. J. (2004). Patterns in Student Learning: Relationships Between Learning Strategies, Conceptions of Learning, and Learning Orientations. Educational Psychology Review. https://dspacelibrary.uu.nl/handle/1874/11958

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review reports Lindblom-Yläne and Lonka (2000) found among advanced medical students in a traditional curriculum a discrepancy between meaning-directed conceptions and reproduction-directed strategies; interviews showed students changed their study practices. No effect sizes printed.

"This inner contradiction between beliefs and behavior led to a high level of dissatisfaction and tension among the students concerned."
