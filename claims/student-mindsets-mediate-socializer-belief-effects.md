### Lee 2025

Lee, H. J., Kim, S., & Bong, M. (2025). Social antecedents of children's mindsets, motivation and achievement in math: Investigating parental beliefs and perceived teacher beliefs. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 849–870. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12764

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Monte Carlo-tested indirect effects (20,000 repetitions) within the multilevel path analysis of 507 students in 28 classrooms: "all possible indirect paths through students' fixed mindsets were significant" except the marginal achievement path; no standardised effect sizes were printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "all possible indirect paths through students' fixed mindsets were significant at the student‐level, except for the path from parent expectations to student achievement via students' fixed mindsets which was only marginally significant."
