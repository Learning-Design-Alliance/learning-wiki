## Evidence

### Quinn Antonia 2019

Quinn Antonia, Kaminsky Jennifer, Adler Andrew, Eisner Shirley, Ovitsh Robin. (2019). Cardiac Auscultation Lab Using a Heart Sounds Auscultation Simulation Manikin. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10839

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Perceptual evaluation survey of 183 second-year medical students per cohort on a 5-point Likert scale, a general curricular survey not specific to the lab. The article reports that "the reviews of the lab improved appreciably" after the manikin's introduction.

> "After introduction of the auscultation manikin, the reviews of the lab improved appreciably. When the session was first introduced, 35% of the students rated it as “very effective” and 26% as “extremely effective.”"
