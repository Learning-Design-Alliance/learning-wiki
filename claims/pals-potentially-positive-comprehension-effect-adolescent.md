`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Findings table in Appendix C for the Fuchs et al. (1997) cluster-randomized study of 12 schools and 120 students. The intervention group mean was 6.02 (1.79) versus 5.18 (1.66) for comparison, a mean difference of 0.84, effect size 0.48, improvement index +19, with the original study reporting p < 0.05.

> "CRAB: Questions Correct Average age: 9.8 years 12 schools/ 120 students 6.02 (1.79) 5.18 (1.66) 0.84 0.48 +19 < 0.05"

### Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies 2012 (2)

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Adolescent Literacy — Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

WWC re-analysis of the same study, correcting for clustering of students within schools. The note states the adjusted effect "was not statistically significant (p = 0.12)", so significance differs from the original study's report; equivalence was not tested.

> "When adjusted for clustering, the WWC-calculated effect on the Questions Correct score of the CRAB was not statistically significant (p = 0.12)."

### Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies 2012 (3)

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Adolescent Literacy — Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The Effectiveness Summary section states the WWC rating rationale: one study showed substantively important positive effects and no studies showed negative effects, yielding a rating of "potentially positive effects, with a small extent of evidence."

> "Thus, for the comprehension domain, one study showed substantively important positive effects. This results in a rating of potentially positive effects, with a small extent of evidence."
