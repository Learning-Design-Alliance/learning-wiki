## Evidence

### Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson intercorrelations at pre-test (Table 2): the age–RES correlation was r = .616 and the IQ–ToM Task Battery correlation r = .393, supporting links between individual characteristics and social cognition.

> "CA correlates negatively and significantly with „consciousness‟, „openness‟, and „agreeableness‟ and positively and significantly with RES scores. The IQ is positively linked to „openness‟, „agreeableness‟, and „extraversion‟, to the ToM Task Battery, t o the EASE, to family income and to fathers‟ education level."

### Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016 (2)

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test intercorrelations (Table 2) show EB negatively related to four personality factors and social adjustment positively linked to ToM Task Battery and ToMI; the article prints no effect sizes for these specific contrasts beyond the correlation table.

> "EB is negatively and significantly correlated with „consciousness‟, „openness‟, „agreeableness‟ and „emotional stability‟. Social adjustment is positively linked to ToM Task Battery and ToMI, and family characteristics."
