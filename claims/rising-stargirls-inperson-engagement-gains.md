## Evidence

### Maya Silverman 2024

Maya Silverman, Aomawa L. Shields, Jessica N. Howard, Vidya Venkatesan and Kiana Whitfield. (2024). Rising Stargirls: Benefits of a Creative Arts-Based Approach to Astronomy Education for Middle-School Girls from Underrepresented Groups. Astronomy Education Journal, Vol. 04, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.32374/2024.4.1.089ra

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Unmatched retrospective pre/post survey analysis of three in-person workshops (Winter 2015, Summer 2015, Fall 2016), analyzed with Hake's gain and dAw. The article reports "a small gain ⟨g⟩ = 0.2344" and "small-to-moderate effect size of dAw = 0.4268".

> "Collectively analyzing all in-person workshop responses ( NB, NA) = (26, 24) showed a small gain ⟨g⟩ = 0.2344 and small-to-moderate effect size of dAw = 0.4268."
