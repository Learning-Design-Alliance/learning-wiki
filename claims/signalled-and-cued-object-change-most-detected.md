Descriptive eye-tracking findings from the multimedia animation study: in the signalled-object scene, 10 of 12 participants (83.33%) detected the change, and participants "easily detected changes on the objects that were verbally described and signalled".

> "It was found that the most detected change was on the object being verbally described and 10 participants out of 12 (83.33%) were found to detect the change (See Table 1)."
