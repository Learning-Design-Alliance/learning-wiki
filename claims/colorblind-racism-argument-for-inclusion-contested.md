# The argument that excluding demographic predictors amounts to color-blind racism is contested: inclusion can also produce racist decision-making

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Yu et al. (2021) and others argue that excluding protected attributes from dropout prediction models can be construed as subscribing to a colorblind ideology criticized as racist. [→ Baker 2023](#baker-2023)
`q1 i?` The authors argue demographic predictor variables may instead be interpreted through cultural racism and minimization-of-racism frames, attributing outcomes to immutable factors. [→ Baker 2023 (2)](#baker-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Baker 2023

Baker, R. S., Esbenshade, L., Vitale, J., & Karumbaiah, S. (2023). Using Demographic Data as Predictor Variables: a Questionable Choice. Journal of Educational Data Mining, 15(2). https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/619

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article quotes Yu et al. (2021, p. 98) arguing that "the deliberate exclusion of protected attributes" can be construed as colorblind ideology; it notes the same claim appears in Andrus & Villaneueve (2022) and Schwobel & Remmers (2022).

> "the deliberate exclusion of protected attributes from dropout prediction models can be construed as subscribing to a 'colorblind' ideology, which has been criticized as a racist approach that serves to maintain the status quo."

### Baker 2023 (2)

Baker, R. S., Esbenshade, L., Vitale, J., & Karumbaiah, S. (2023). Using Demographic Data as Predictor Variables: a Questionable Choice. Journal of Educational Data Mining, 15(2). https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/619

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors' counter-argument (type e): given widespread implicit color-blind racism, "demographic predictor variables may be inte rpreted in accordance with Bonilla-Silva’s cultural racism and minimization of racism frames", communicating that a student is at risk due to immutable factors beyond school control, so actual racist practices are overlooked.

> "Instead, demographic predictor variables may be inte rpreted in accordance with Bonilla-Silva’s cultural racism and minimization of racism frames."
