# Korbit (full) with personalized feedback produced higher raw and normalized learning gains than a lecture-and-quiz MOOC platform

A New Era: Intelligent Tutoring Systems Will Transform Online Learning for Millions. arXiv:2203.03724 [cs.CY]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.03724](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.03724)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized head-to-head study of a 3-hour linear regression course; 50 analyzed participants completed pre- and post-quizzes. Raw learning gains on Korbit (full) were "around 90% higher than on MOOC with 95% conﬁdence (p=0.04)".

> "the learning gains on Korbit (full), which provided participants with personalized feedback and explanations relevant to their solutions, are around 90% higher than on MOOC with 95% conﬁdence (p=0.04)"

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same study, normalized learning gains (Hake's measure): Korbit (full) gains were "2.48 times higher than the average normalized gains for MOOC participants", significant at the 95% confidence level (p=0.01).

> "the average normalized learning gains gnorm for Korbit (full) participants are 2.48 times higher than the average normalized gains for MOOC participants (with the difference being statistically signiﬁcant at the 95% conﬁdence level ( p=0.01)."
