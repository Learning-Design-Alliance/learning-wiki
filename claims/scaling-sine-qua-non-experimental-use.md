## Evidence

### Franzen 1921

Franzen, R., & Knight, F. B. (1921). Criteria to Employ in Choice of Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075312

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical argument that equal units across the scale enable comparing progress, which underlies comparison, experiment, classification, diagnosis and quantitative definition. The authors add that teachers and administrators generally misinterpret unscaled scores as scaled; no empirical data reported.

> "it is a sine qua non in the use of a test for most experimental purposes, since progress along a scale is generally involved. If we cannot compare progress, and we cannot unless a test is scaled, then we cannot gain much from comparison, experiment, classification, diagnosis or quantitative definition."
