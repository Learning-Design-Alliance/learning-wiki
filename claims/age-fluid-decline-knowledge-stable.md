## Evidence

### Salthouse 2012

Salthouse, T. (2012). Consequences of Age-Related Cognitive Declines. Annual Review of Psychology, 63, 201-226. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-120710-100328

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of cross-sectional age-cognition research. The review reports the meta-analytic estimate: "their estimate of the age-reasoning correlation was −0.40", consistent with −0.39 in the Salthouse data and −0.43 in the WAIS-IV normative sample of 1,577 adults.

> "Verhaeghen & Salthouse (1997) reported a meta-analysis of 38 correlations with a total sample size of over 9,000 adults, and their estimate of the age-reasoning correlation was −0.40"

### Salthouse 2012 (2)

Salthouse, T. (2012). Consequences of Age-Related Cognitive Declines. Annual Review of Psychology, 63, 201-226. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-120710-100328

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of cross-sectional age trends in composite measures from the Salthouse studies and the WAIS-IV norms, showing two distinct patterns: monotonic decrease for fluid measures and increase-then-decline for knowledge measures.

> "different age trends are apparent in measures of acquired knowledge, as the functions typically increase until about age 60 and then gradually decline"
