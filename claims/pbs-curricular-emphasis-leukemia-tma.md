Consensus recommendations on peripheral blood smear review: defining curricular standards and fellow competency. Blood Advances. https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2023009843

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results of the competency rating by all 8 focus group participants; features beyond competency included Sézary cells, Pappenheimer bodies, hypogranular platelets, and the monocyte/monoblast distinction. Consensus stated "specific curricular emphasis should be placed on the morphologic presentation of acute leukemias and hemolytic anemias, including TMA".

> "Only 4 morphologic features (4/50, 8%) and 6 diagnoses (6/26, 23%) were felt to be beyond the level of competency for a graduating hematology fellow."
