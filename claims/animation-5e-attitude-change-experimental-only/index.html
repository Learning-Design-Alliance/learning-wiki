Evidence¶

Özcan and Kiliç 2017¶

Mehmet Fatih Özcan, Latife Kirbaşoğlu Kiliç. (2017). The Effects of Animation Supported 5E Model on Teaching 'Indicative and Subjunctive Moods' in 7th Grade Turkish Lesson. Universal Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051407

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/post attitude scale comparison within each group in the quasi-experiment, using the Ministry of National Education (EARGED) Turkish Lesson Attitude Scale. The article reports a significant pre-post difference only in the experimental group; no statistic or effect size is printed.

"It was seen that there was no significant difference in the pre- and post-practice attitude scores of the control group in which the current teaching program was applied. 3. A significant difference was found in the pre - and post-practice attitude scores of the experimental group in which the animation- assisted 5E application was used"
