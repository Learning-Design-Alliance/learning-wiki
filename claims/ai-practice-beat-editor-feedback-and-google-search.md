# Practicing with AI improved unassisted writing skill more than practicing with Google Search for cover letter examples and tips

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` On a no-AI writing test, participants who practiced with AI wrote higher-quality cover letters than those who practiced with Google Search (d=0.46). [→ Benjamin Lira 2025](#benjamin-lira-2025)

## Evidence

### Benjamin Lira 2025

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered randomized experiment (N=2,997, Prolific). After practicing, all participants took a no-AI writing test. Participants "wrote higher-quality cover letters" than Google Search (d=0.46); editors averaged 25 years of experience.

> "participants who had practiced with the AI tool wrote higher-quality cover letters than those who had practiced with Google Search (d= 0.46,p<.001), and even outperformed those who had practiced with personalized feedback from professional editors (d= 0.20,p<.001)"
