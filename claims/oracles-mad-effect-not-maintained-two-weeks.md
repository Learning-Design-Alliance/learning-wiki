### Nicola White 2020

Nicola White, Linda JM Oostendorp, Christopher Tomlinson, Sarah Yardley, Federico Ricciardi, Hülya Gökalp, Ollie Minton, Jason W Boland, Ben Clark, Priscilla Harries, Patrick Stone. (2020). Online training improves medical students' ability to recognise when a person is dying: The ORaClES randomised controlled trial. Palliative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/0269216319880767

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Maintenance analysis of the same randomised trial, modelling MAD at the 2-week follow-up (t = 3) adjusted for baseline MAD. "there is no evidence of a learning effect on the MAD scores" with the printed δ′MAD, confidence interval and p-value; equivalence was not tested.

> "Whilst there is a similar trend to the primary analysis, there is no evidence of a learning effect on the MAD scores from the intervention ( δ′MAD=1.50 , 95% CI −0.87 to 3.86, p = 0.21)."
