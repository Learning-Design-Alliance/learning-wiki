## Evidence

### Kalani Hettiarachchilage 2023

Kalani Hettiarachchilage, Neel Haldolaarachchige. (2023). Effective Model with Personalized Online Teaching and Learning Science in the Era of ChatGPT. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of approximately 800 students across four courses over two-to-three semesters, tracking seven assignment requirement objectives from beginning to end of semester via graded work. The authors report descriptive percentages showing improvement with instructor feedback; no statistical test is applied.

> "Writing handwritten answers started from around 50% for PHY116, PHY156, and PHY206 and improved to 98% for both PHY116 and PHY156 and 80% for PHY206. PHY114 started from around 25% and improved to 85%."
