## Evidence

### van de Pol 2024

van de Pol, J., & Oudman, S. (2024). Teachers' judgment accuracy of students' monitoring skills: a conceptual and methodological framework and explorative study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09349-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The review attributes this to Fleury-Roy and Bouffard (2006), in which 74 primary school teachers classified 684 students into pessimist, optimist, or realist categories; '51.32%' were classified correctly. The reviewed study's design is described only as the article describes it.

> "It turned out that 51.32% of the students were classified correctly by their teachers, meaning that almost 50% of the students were wrongly classified."

### van de Pol 2024 (2)

van de Pol, J., & Oudman, S. (2024). Teachers' judgment accuracy of students' monitoring skills: a conceptual and methodological framework and explorative study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09349-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The review attributes this to Jamain (2019), a follow-up study with 13 primary school teachers classifying 292 students separately for Mathematics and French performance.

> "Overall, 45.55% (Mathematics) and 41.78% (French) of the students were classified correctly by the teachers."
