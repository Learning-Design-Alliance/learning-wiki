### James-Brabham 2024

James-Brabham, E., Jay, T., & Sella, F. (2024). No evidence that playing a linear number board game improves numerical skills beyond teaching as usual: a randomized controlled trial in four- to five-year-old primary school children. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000857

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Mixed ANOVA results from the RCT (n=249): the main effect of Session was always significant, indicating gains in the Number Knowledge index, WIAT-III Numeracy, and YARC Letter-Sound subtest across all conditions, with no printed effect size for the Session effect.

> "Children improved their scores in the primary outcome measures between pre -test and post-test, and this improvement did not vary among conditions (Figure 3)."
