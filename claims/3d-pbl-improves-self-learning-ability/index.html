Li Ting 2025¶

Li Ting, Hu Yidan, Song Ruohong, Liu Hangyu, Gao Xin, Wang Fei, Xiao Chengwei, Liu Xilin. (2025). Unveiling the ultimate advantage: a meta-analysis of 3D visualization and problem-based learning in orthopedic education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06654-5

q2 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of four RCTs (N = 186) with a fixed-effect model found "students demonstrated stronger self-learning abilities with 3D + PBL teaching compared to LBL teaching". GRADE certainty was very low; no significant heterogeneity was observed (I2 = 0%).

"The meta-analysis found that students demonstrated stronger self-learning abilities with 3D + PBL teaching compared to LBL teaching (SMD = 1.28, 95% CI: 0.96–1.60, P < 0.00001; see Figure S1)."
