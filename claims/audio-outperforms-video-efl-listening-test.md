## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Başal 2015

Başal, A., Gülözer, K., & Demir, İ. (2015). Use of Video and Audio Texts in EFL Listening Test. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i6.1001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest-only control group design with 57 first-grade ELT students randomly assigned to AOT (n=30) or VOT (n=27). Independent t-test on total correct answers across four texts showed "a statistically meaningful difference was discovered" favoring audio delivery; no effect size was printed.

> "In general, when considering all 20 items, a statistically meaningful difference was discovered (t = -2,1695, df = 55, p > 0.05), indicating that the audio delivery was more favorable."
