# Confirmatory factor analysis supports retaining the ANTSdk four-category, 16-element structure in the Turkish version

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CFA of ANTStr ratings retained the original grouping of 16 elements into four categories, with all standardised factor loadings between 0.52 and 0.94 and all t-values significant at the 0.01 level. [→ Celik N 2025](#celik-n-2025)
`q3 i?` After establishing covariance between the 12th and 13th elements, first-level CFA fit indices met the article's criteria for acceptable fit (χ²/df=1.67, CFI=0.92, TLI=0.912, IFI=0.930, RMSEA=0.092). [→ Celik N 2025 (2)](#celik-n-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Celik N 2025

Celik N, Sarmasoglu Kilikcier S, Tasdelen Teker G, Keskin G, Akın M, Ozmert S, Elcin M, Senel E. (2025). Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form: cross-cultural adaptation, reliability and validity of Turkish version in paediatric surgery team. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003249

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of ANTStr ratings from real paediatric surgery and urology operating room teams. The article reports that 'the factor loadings of the items in the scale range from 0.52 to 0.94', so no items were excluded from the scale.

> "Therefore, CFA was conducted to evaluate whether the scale's original structure, consisting of 16 elements and four categories, holds. The results indicated that the factor loadings of the items in the scale range from 0.52 to 0.94"

### Celik N 2025 (2)

Celik N, Sarmasoglu Kilikcier S, Tasdelen Teker G, Keskin G, Akın M, Ozmert S, Elcin M, Senel E. (2025). Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form: cross-cultural adaptation, reliability and validity of Turkish version in paediatric surgery team. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003249

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

First-level CFA fit indices reported after establishing covariance between the 12th and 13th elements; table 2 classifies χ²/df as perfect fit and CFI, TLI, IFI and RMSEA as acceptable fit for the four-category model.

> "the goodness-of-fit indices obtained from the first-level CFA were as follows: χ²/df=1.67, CFI=0.92, NNFI=0.95, IFI=0.96, RMSEA=0.092 and IFI=0.93"
