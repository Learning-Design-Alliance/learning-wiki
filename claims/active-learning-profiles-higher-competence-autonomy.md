## Evidence

### Stieha Vicki 2024

Stieha Vicki, Earl Brittnee, Hagens Harrisen, Haynes Meagan, Ulappa Amy, Bond Laura, Oxford Julia Thom. (2024). An exploration of the relationship between active learning and student motivation in STEM: a mixed methods study. Advances in Physiology Education. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00247.2022

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA across seven instructors in four undergraduate biology courses, with post-hoc Tukey tests, found significant instructor differences in competence and autonomy support composite scores. For perceived competence, instructors A/C/D/G (interactive and student-centred profiles) scored higher than didactic instructors E/F; for autonomy support, A/C/D/G differed from E, but only D differed from F. Instructors A and D were observed in all three profiles and C in two, so the profiles are not cleanly separate groups. "significantly higher for INSTs A/C/D/G compared to INSTs E/F" was reported with ANOVA F values but no effect size.

> "Mean scores for students’ perceived competence were significantly higher for INSTs A/C/D/G compared to INSTs E/F. The mean scores for autonomy support were statistically different between INSTs A/C/D/G and INST E"
