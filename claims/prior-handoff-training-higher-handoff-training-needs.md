## Evidence

### Yang Sun-Yi 2023

Yang Sun-Yi. (2023). Impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic on Neonatal Nursing Practicum and Extended Reality Simulation Training Needs: A Descriptive and Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 20(1), 344. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20010344

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test comparison in the 132-student survey: prior handoff training group 3.57 ± 0.46 versus no training 3.40 ± 0.49. Needs were "significantly higher among students who had prior handoff training."

> "Neonatal and family handoff training needs (t = 2.07, p = 0.041) were significantly higher among students who had prior handoff training."

### Yang Sun-Yi 2023 (2)

Yang Sun-Yi. (2023). Impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic on Neonatal Nursing Practicum and Extended Reality Simulation Training Needs: A Descriptive and Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 20(1), 344. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20010344

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item-level independent t-tests (Table 4) comparing 55 students with versus 77 without prior handoff training; all eight named nursing-diagnosis items favored the experienced group with the printed t and p values.

> "Students with prior handoff training showed higher training needs for constipation (t = 2.29, p = 0.023), deficient knowledge (t = 2.56, p = 0.011), hopelessness (t = 2.93, p = 0.004), anxiety (t = 2.70, p = 0.008), death anxiety (t = 3.04, p = 0.003), fear (t = 2.68, p = 0.008), powerlessness (t = 3.28, p = 0.001), and impaired resilience (t = 3.20, p = 0.002)."
