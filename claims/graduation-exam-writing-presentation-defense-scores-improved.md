## Evidence

### Xing Wenjing 2026

Xing Wenjing, Ju Huanyu, Pei Chunying, Guo Yongli, Tian Linlu, Ling Hong, Guo Tieyun, Cao Bo, Ma Jian. (2026). Research topic–driven curriculum content reform in graduate immunology to strengthen students' research and innovation competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08711-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expert-assessed performance during master's graduation examinations, historical cohort comparison (Fig. 4D: n = 17 pre-reform, n = 6 post-reform; the figure legend reports t = 8.219, t = 5.743 and t = 6.198 for the three skills). The article reports "significantly higher scores in academic writing skills" and the other two skills.

> "students demonstrated significantly higher scores in academic writing skills (6.765 ± 0.504 pre-reform vs. 8.750 ± 0.524 post-reform), presentation skills (3.335 ± 0.387 vs. 4.333 ± 0.288), and oral defense skills (11.382 ± 0.490 vs. 13.000 ± 0.707) following implementation of the curriculum reform"
