# Among 2014 CPS graduates who immediately enrolled in a bachelor's-degree-granting institution, 30% completed within four years versus 51% within six years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fewer than one-third of 2014 CPS immediate college enrollees completed a bachelor's degree within four years (30%), rising to over one-half (51%) within six years. [→ Nagaoka 2023](#nagaoka-2023)

## Evidence

### Nagaoka 2023

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Arneson, A. (2023). The four years fallacy: Four-year vs. six-year bachelor's degree completion rates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-four-years-fallacy

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of National Student Clearinghouse data for 2014 CPS graduates who immediately enrolled in a bachelor's-degree-granting institution. The report finds "fewer than one-third of students completed a bachelor's degree within four years (30%)" versus 51% within six years. No effect size is printed.

> "Among 2014 CPS graduates who immediately enrolled in a bachelor's-degree-granting institution, fewer than one-third of students completed a bachelor's degree within four years (30%); however, the proportion of students completing rose to over one-half when we extended the time period to six years (51%; see Figure 1 )."
