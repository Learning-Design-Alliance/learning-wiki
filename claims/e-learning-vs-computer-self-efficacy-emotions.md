# E-learning self-efficacy relates to both positive and negative achievement emotions, while computer self-efficacy relates only to positive emotions

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural paths in the SEM of 17019 higher education students: "both e-learning self-efficacy (β = 0.33, p < .001) and computer self-efficacy (β = 0.10, p < .001) were significantly and positively linked to positive emotions"; only e-learning self-efficacy predicted negative emotions.

> "both e-learning self-efficacy (β = 0.33, p < .001) and computer self-efficacy (β = 0.10, p < .001) were significantly and positively linked to positive emotions, while only e-learning self-efficacy (β = −0.38, p < .001) was significantly and negatively linked to negative emotions"
