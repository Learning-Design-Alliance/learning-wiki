### Li Jiuxiang 2024

Li Jiuxiang, Wang Rufeng. (2024). RETRACTED ARTICLE: Determining the role of innovative teaching practices, sustainable learning, and the adoption of e-learning tools in leveraging academic motivation for students' mental well-being. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01639-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Mediation hypothesis test (H7) with 2000 bc-bootstraps in Mplus on the 308-respondent survey. The ACM–MW indirect effect via sustainable learning was "insignificant" (p = 0.070) and the 95% CI (−0.002–0.052) passed through zero; equivalence was not tested.

> "the indirect effect proved to be insignificant, i.e., β = 0.027, SE = 0.015, t-value = 1.81 and p-value = 0.070"
