## Evidence

### Luo Bin 2025

Luo Bin, Zhou Fangyao, Zheng Jiabin, Hu Weixian, Lv Zejian, Yang Zifeng, Feng Xingyu. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom combined with BOPPPS model in standardized residency training for gastrointestinal surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07855-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Instructor comprehensive evaluation using a 10-item questionnaire scored on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Fail to 5 = Excellent), comparing the two cohorts at end of rotation.

> "Compared to the control group, the experimental group demonstrated superior performance in several key areas: synthesizing clinical cases during morning handovers (p = 0.040), managing emergency and critical care situations while on-call (p = 0.038), and adhering to intraoperative aseptic principles during operations (p = 0.011)"
