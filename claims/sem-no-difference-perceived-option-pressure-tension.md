## Evidence

### Bilgin and Dalkiran 2017

Nevruz Bilgin, Oğuzhan Dalkıran. (2017). Examining Attitudes of Students Regarding the Sports Education Model and Direct Teaching Model. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i12.2718

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test on the Perceived Option dimension after the 12-week intervention; experiment mean rank 12.72, control 18.73. The article reports "not any significant difference" (U=58.0; p>.05); equivalence was not tested.

> "It is determined that there was not any significant difference between the groups according to perceived option attitudes of the experiment group and control group students towards the physical education lessons (U=58.0; p>.05)."

### Bilgin and Dalkiran 2017 (2)

Nevruz Bilgin, Oğuzhan Dalkıran. (2017). Examining Attitudes of Students Regarding the Sports Education Model and Direct Teaching Model. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i12.2718

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test on the Pressure/Tension dimension in the same quasi-experiment; experiment mean rank 16.08, control 13.23. The article reports "not any significant difference" (U= 79.50; p>.05); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was not any significant difference between the groups according to the pressure/tension dimension attitudes of the experiment group and control group students towards physical education lessons (U= 79.50; p>.05)."
