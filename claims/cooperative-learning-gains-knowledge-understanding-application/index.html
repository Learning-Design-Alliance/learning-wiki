K. Thiyagu 2012¶

K. Thiyagu. (2012). Effectiveness of Co-operative Learning Method in Learning of Mathematics among Eighth Standard Students. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 5 No. 4, February - April 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102329

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Objective-wise analysis of the same 20-versus-20 quasi-experiment. Table 2 reports knowledge means 3.46 vs 5.00 (t = 2.13), understanding 1.78 vs 3.13 (t = 2.93), application 1.54 vs 2.61 (t = 2.23), each marked "*Significant" at the 5% level.
