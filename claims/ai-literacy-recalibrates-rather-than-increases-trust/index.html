Pierre Haritz 2026¶

Pierre Haritz, Hendrik Krone, and Thomas Liebig. (2026). Addressing Trust in AI Systems through Education: A Didactic Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02453 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.02453

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Narrative review attributing this finding to Chiang and Yin's study of ML literacy interventions on laypeople's reliance. The article reports the study showed literacy "cause[s] users to become more discerning rather than more trusting"; no effect sizes are printed.

"Chiang and Yin showed that literacy interventions cause users to become more discerning rather than more trusting: participants relied on ML models when the models performed well and withheld trust when outputs were uncertain or flawed"

Pierre Haritz 2026 (2)¶

Pierre Haritz, Hendrik Krone, and Thomas Liebig. (2026). Addressing Trust in AI Systems through Education: A Didactic Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02453

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Narrative review attributing this cross-sectional finding to Yao et al.'s study integrating digital, AI, and scientific literacy in medical AI trust. The article reports AI literacy specifically reduced trust in medical AI; no effect sizes are printed.

"A further study showed that digital literacy increased trust in medical AI, but AI literacy specifically reduced it, as deeper awareness of risks generated what the authors call informed skepticism rather than blind confidence"
