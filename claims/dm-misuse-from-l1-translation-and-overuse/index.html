Nader Assadi Aidinlou & Hakimeh Shahrokhi mehr 2012¶

Nader Assadi Aidinlou & Hakimeh Shahrokhi mehr. (2012). The Effect of Discourse Markers Instruction on EFL Learners' Writing. World Journal of Education, Vol. 2, No. 2. http://dx.doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n2p10

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of pre-test and control-group cloze performance, citing Demirci and Kleiner (1997); the article reports "most cases of overuse and misuse of DMs" and notes some control-group DMs "overshadowed the text and made it difficult to understand".

"With most cases of overuse and misuse of DMs in learners' cloze test, the writer follows Demirci and Kleiner (1997) who suggest that DMs are used in their writing as a result of translation from t heir first language to target language."
