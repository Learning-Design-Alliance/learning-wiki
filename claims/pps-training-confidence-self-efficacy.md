`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cross-sectional feasibility questionnaire reported by the review (Morriss et al., 2006); practitioners also reported increased confidence and self-efficacy, with 48% naming better ways of making the link as a new learning achievement.

> "Better or alternative ways of making a link between interrelated factors (i.e., between symptoms and psychosocial stressors) was reported to be a new learning achievement by almost half (48%) of healthcare practitioners who took part in the training [31]."
