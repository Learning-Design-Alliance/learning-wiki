Evidence¶

van der Zanden 2020¶

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the 46 educational-setting studies (71% of the sample). The review describes the balanced pedagogical approach theme: "teachers providing adolescents with opportunities for exploration and taking risk while at the same time providing them with necessary guidance and direction," citing multiple studies.

"this balance refers to teachers providing adolescents with opportunities for exploration and taking risk while at the same time providing them with necessary guidance and direction"

van der Zanden 2020 (2)¶

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a questionnaire study (N = 658, Taiwan) on mobile learning environments, that "communication and collaboration with peers had a positive association with creativity," mediated by peer interaction processes and supported by content meeting students' needs.

"Hwang et al. (2018) found that communication and collaboration with peers had a positive association with creativity."
