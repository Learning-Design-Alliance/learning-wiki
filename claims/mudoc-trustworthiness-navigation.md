### Karan Taneja 2025

Karan Taneja, Anjali Singh, and Ashok K. Goel. (2025). Towards a Multimodal Document-grounded Conversational AI System for Education. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey responses comparing perceived trustworthiness of the two systems. The article adds that document-grounded images could not be hallucinated, and that side-by-side chat and PDF display was preferred by 29 participants mainly because it enabled verification and increased trust.

> "Finally, 20 out of 30 participants favored MuDoCfor trustworthiness and reliability of responseswhile only 3 preferred TexDoC. The ability to navigate to the source of images and texts using MuDoC was cited as the top reason for its higher credibility."
