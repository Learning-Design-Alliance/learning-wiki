Moderator analysis of theoretical framework reported in Table 2 across 15 frameworks. Transformative experiences (k = 4) averaged d = 0.74, self-determination (k = 11) d = 0.70, and interest (k = 2) d = 0.69; the article reports only descriptive differences across frameworks, not significant ones.
