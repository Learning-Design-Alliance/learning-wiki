In the discussion, the review argues against relying on trainee evaluations of teacher performance, citing two stated reasons, and additionally notes data from a randomised controlled trial suggesting trainee feedback can be manipulated by introducing "seduction" in teaching.

> "Two reasons students' feedback is inappropriate as a measure of teacher quality are that they may not measure all that the teachers are trying to achieve and often do not also provide useful information for teachers about what they need to know to teach more effectively [8,24]."
