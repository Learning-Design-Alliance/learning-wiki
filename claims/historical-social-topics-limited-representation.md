## Evidence

### Tanji Reed Marshall 2023

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tool-based coding of the 137 books in the sample that include historical or social topics, on criteria of avoiding sanitization, including marginalized perspectives, and connecting topics to student realities. The report states "The majority (80%) of books that include historical or social topics do not meet those criteria"; 137 books (46%) include such topics and 16% are complex.

> "The majority (80%) of books that include historical or social topics do not meet those criteria, demonstrating what define as limited representation, and in some cases, represent intellectual condescension"
