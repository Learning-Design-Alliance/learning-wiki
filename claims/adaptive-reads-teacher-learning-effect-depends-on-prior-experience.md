# The effect of Adaptive READS versus Core READS on teachers' intervention-related literacy learning depends on teachers' prior experience with Core READS, supporting the scaffolded hypothesis

## Subclaims

`q3 i2` Among teachers with past READS experience, Adaptive READS promoted more literacy-related learning than Core READS (ES = .46 SD, p = .052), while for teachers new to READS the Adaptive effect was negatively signed and not significant. [→ Quinn and Kim 2017](#quinn-and-kim-2017)
`q3 i?` The Adaptive READS × READS experience interaction on the literacy learning index was statistically significant (coefficient 0.750, p < .05). [→ Quinn and Kim 2017](#quinn-and-kim-2017)

`q3 i?` The effect of Adaptive READS on the literacy learning index interacted significantly with teachers' prior READS experience. [→ Quinn 2016](#quinn-2016)

`q3 i?` Adaptive READS structures and activities were more beneficial for experienced teachers than were Traditional READS structures. [→ Quinn 2016](#quinn-2016)

## Evidence

### Quinn and Kim 2017

David M. Quinn, James S. Kim. (2017). Scaffolding Fidelity and Adaptation in Educational Program Implementation: Experimental Evidence From a Literacy Intervention. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831217717692

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression models with cluster-robust standard errors predicting the standardized literacy learning index (α = .84) in a cluster-randomized trial of 27 schools (N = 112 teachers). The interaction term Adaptive READS × READS Experience was 0.750 (SE 0.307, p < .05); the printed effect size for the Adaptive effect among experienced teachers was ES = .46 SD (p = .052).

> "While the effect of Adaptive READS was negatively-signed and not significant for teachers new to READS, the effect of Adaptive READS was positive for teachers with past READS experience (ES = –.29 1 .75 = .46 SD, p = .052)."

### Quinn 2016

Quinn, D. M., & Kim, J. S. (2016). Predictors and Moderators of Teacher Learning and Changes in Practice: Evidence from a Randomized Trial of a Teacher-Adapted Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567622

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression on the standardized literacy learning index (N=122 per Table 2) with an Adaptive*READS Experience interaction term; the printed interaction coefficient is 0.677* (p<0.05). The interaction is depicted graphically in Figure 1.

> "the effect of Adaptive READS differed significantly depending on whether teachers had past experience with READS. As hypothesized, the structures and activities of Adaptive READS were more beneficial for experienced teachers than were the structures and activities of Traditional READS"
