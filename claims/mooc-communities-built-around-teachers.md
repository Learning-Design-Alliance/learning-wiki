## Evidence

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Community structure analysis (Leiden algorithm) of the MOOC communication network, where forum responses and comments formed edges; students tended to communicate only with one tutor.

> "It is important to note that student-teacher communication is very intensive and communities are built around the teachers."

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022 (2)

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Network and message-volume analysis of the MOOC forums showed participation was concentrated in a few active communities, with most students communicating much less or passively.

> "The analysis revealed that there were several very active communities of students who created most of the messages, while most of the students communicated much less or used passive communication patterns like upvoting messages."
