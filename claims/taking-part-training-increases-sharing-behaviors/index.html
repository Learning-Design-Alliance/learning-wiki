Social Skills Training 2013¶

Social Skills Training. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Early Childhood Education Interventions for Children with Disabilities. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/578

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized comparison of three classrooms receiving the Taking Part curriculum plus reinforcement against three classrooms receiving reinforcement only (38 children with developmental delays); the printed means are 5.69 versus 2.76, effect size 1.12, p < 0.05, described as "statistically significant" in the domain average row.

"Frequency of "sharing" behaviors Preschoolers 6 classrooms/ 38 children 5.69 (2.55) 2.76 (2.55) 2.93 1.12 +37 < 0.05"

Social Skills Training 2013 (2)¶

Social Skills Training. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Early Childhood Education Interventions for Children with Disabilities. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/578

q3 · i? · causal · r2
