Evidence¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analyses were conducted via three-level multiple meta-regression for pooled and strategy-type meta-analyses, testing all moderators simultaneously. The review reports that "Operationalization of variables as well as setting and work-relatedness of the CE activity were identified as moderators."
