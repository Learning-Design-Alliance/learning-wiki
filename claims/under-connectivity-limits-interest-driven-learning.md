`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The chapter reports, citing Rideout and Katz (2016), a survey of low- and moderate-income families showing that many with nominal internet access were "under-connected in one or more ways" (slow access, computer sharing, mobile-only access, limited data plans), and that mobile-only children used the internet for interest-driven learning far less. Only descriptive percentages are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "Rideout and Katz (2016) found that only 35% of children with mobile-only access would often use the Internet for interest-driven learning as compared to 52% of those who had desktop or laptop access."
