## Evidence

### Jansink Renate 2013

Jansink Renate, Braspenning Jozé, Laurant Miranda, Keizer Ellen, Elwyn Glyn, Weijden Trudy van der, Grol Richard. (2013). Minimal improvement of nurses’ motivational interviewing skills in routine diabetes care one year after training: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-44

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel regression on one-year follow-up consultation recordings (n = 175 consultations); judges rated patients' readiness to change on a five-point scale from a predetermined scoring list. The readiness association with additional MI items is reported as B-estimate=1.41 (s.d.=0.58, p=0.015); direction is associative, not causal.

> "Table 3 shows that nurses used significantly more MI skills measured by the BECCI if the consultation took more time (B-estimate= 0.13; s.d.= 0.05), and when more time was spent discussing lifestyle factors (B-estimate = 8.97; s.d.= 0.71)."
