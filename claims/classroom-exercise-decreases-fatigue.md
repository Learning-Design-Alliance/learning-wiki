### Ayame Tamura 2022

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same linear mixed-effects analysis of the nine-lesson within-subjects intervention as the vigour finding. The intervention showed a "signiﬁcant negative effect on fatigue" (B = −0.09, t = −2.61, P < 0.05); participants experienced less fatigue after exercising. No standardised effect size is printed.
