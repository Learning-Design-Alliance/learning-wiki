## Evidence

### Wang Wei 2020

Wang Wei, Ma Hucheng, Ren Haozhen, Wang Zhongxia, Mao Liang, He Ningning. (2020). The Impact of Surgical Boot Camp and Subsequent Repetitive Practice on the Surgical Skills and Confidence of Residents. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00268-020-05669-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of two sequential resident cohorts at one institution (28 experimental, 24 control). The experimental cohort passed "89.62% (95/106)" of rotation assessments versus 79.00% (79/100) for controls; the article reports the difference as significant (p < 0.05) with no printed effect size.

> "Our results showed that residents in the experimental group received a total of 106 department rotation assessments in 2017, and their overall pass rate was 89.62% (95/106). The pass rate of the control group was 79.00% (79/100) in 2016."
