## Evidence

### Lin Chen 2019

Lin Chen, Gao Junyi, Zheng Hua, Zhao Jun, Yang Hua, Zheng Yue, Cao Yihan, Chen Yufei, Wu Guoliang, Lin Guole, Yu Jianchun, Li Hanzhong, Pan Hui, Liao Quan, Zhao Yupei. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time spent answering was recorded by the online test system in the randomized controlled trial. The article reports the printed means and "MD = −188, p = 0.01" for PGY1 and no differences for later years (PGY2 MD = −37, P = 0.71). No standardized effect size was printed.

> "the PGY1 residents in the 3D group required 1267 ± 105 s to complete the test, whereas those in the 2D group required 1455 ± 157 s (PGY1, 3D vs. CT: MD = −188, p = 0.01)"
