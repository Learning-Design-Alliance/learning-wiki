### Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies 2012

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Adolescent Literacy — Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The Research Summary section reports the scope of reviewed research: 97 studies were examined, of which one met WWC evidence standards with reservations and the rest did not meet standards or eligibility screens.

> "Ninety-seven studies reviewed by the WWC Adolescent Literacy topic area investigated the effects of Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. One study (Fuchs, Fuchs, Mathes, & Simmons, 1997) is a randomized controlled trial with randomization problems that meets WWC evidence standards with reservations."

### Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies 2012 (2)

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Adolescent Literacy — Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study description of the Fuchs et al. (1997) trial: 22 schools stratified by reading level and socioeconomic status were randomized, but only 12 schools (20 treatment and 20 control teachers) participated, with 60 PALS and 60 comparison students. The report attributes the with-reservations rating to nonrandom selection of students after random assignment.

> "Although random assignment of 22 schools was conducted, only 12 schools were included in the study. These 12 schools were equally divided between the Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies and control conditions and among the high-, middle-, and low-level designations."
