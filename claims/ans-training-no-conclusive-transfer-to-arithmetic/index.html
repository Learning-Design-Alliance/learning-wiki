Evidence¶

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021¶

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review attribution. The review states that interventions focusing on training this approximate number system "have not provided conclusive evidence" of transfer to arithmetic, citing a critical review; no effect sizes are printed.

"Consequently, (cognitive) interventions that merely focus on training this approximate number system with the aim to transferring improvements to arithmetic have not provided conclusive evidence (for a critical review see ref. 65)."

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021 (2)¶

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's own synthesis of the causal-status literature: it reports the proof of a unidirectional ANS foundation for symbolic numerical and arithmetic development is "rather weak", noting some argue it is non-existent; no effect sizes are printed.

"the empirical proof that the approximate number system serves as a unidirectional neurobiological foundation for the development of symbolic numerical and arithmetic abilities is rather weak"
