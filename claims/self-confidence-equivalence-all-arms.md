## Evidence

### Schmidt Michelle 2024

Schmidt Michelle, Schauwinhold Michael Tobias, Loeffler Leonie Anne Kathrin, Klasen Martin, Lambert Sophie Isabelle, Sopka Saša, Vogt Lina. (2024). Safety management within the scope of teaching practical clinical skills: framing errors for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training – a multi-arm randomized controlled equivalence trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome from online questionnaires (6-point Likert scale) in the randomized equivalence trial. All pairwise comparisons between EM, EA, and control were significantly equivalent for both confidence items.

> "Mean differences (t1) between the EM and EA group were 0.02 points (95% CI: −0.27 to 0.23) regarding confidence in one's CPR performance and 0.01 points (95% CI: −0.30 to 0.26) in terms of confidence in an emergency situation. The results indicated significant equivalence between the EM and EA groups for both items."
