# Knowledge scores of nurses improved significantly from pre-test to post-test after the PPH training, with average correct answers rising from 3.8 of 15 to 13.24 of 15

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among the 37 nurses, average correct answers on the 15-question PPH knowledge test rose from 3.8 (range 1-9) at pre-test to 13.24 (range 9-15) at post-test, a statistically significant result (p = 0.00). [→ Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025](#coelho-tatiane-da-silva-2025)

## Evidence

### Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025

Coelho Tatiane da Silva, Fonseca Luciana Mara Monti, Cardoso Maria Vera Lúcia Moreira Leitão, Aquino Priscila de Souza, da Costa Camila Chaves, Maciel Nathanael de Souza, Damasceno Ana Kelve de Castro. (2025). Clinical simulation for nurses' knowledge on postpartum hemorrhage: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0214

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-trial before-and-after comparison in a randomized controlled trial of 37 nurses at a tertiary maternity hospital. Pre-test average was "a média de acertos foi de 3,8"; post-test average was 13.24 of 15 with a statistically significant Wilcoxon result (p = 0.00).

> "No pré-teste, dentre as 15 questões objetivas, a média de acertos foi de 3,8 e um intervalo de 1-9 acertos, sendo que nenhum enfermeiro conseguiu acertar mais que 60% das questões apresentadas no pré-teste."

### Coelho Tatiane da Silva 2025 (2)

Coelho Tatiane da Silva, Fonseca Luciana Mara Monti, Cardoso Maria Vera Lúcia Moreira Leitão, Aquino Priscila de Souza, da Costa Camila Chaves, Maciel Nathanael de Souza, Damasceno Ana Kelve de Castro. (2025). Clinical simulation for nurses' knowledge on postpartum hemorrhage: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0214

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test results from the same RCT: "the average number of correct answers was 13.24" of 15, with post-intervention performance between 60% and 100% and a statistically significant result (p = 0.00).

> "In the post-test, out of the 15 questions presented, the average number of correct answers was 13.24, with a range of 9-15 correct answers, with a minimum of 60% and a maximum of 100% cognitive performance. A statistically significant result was observed (p = 0.00) for the post-intervention results (Table 3)."
