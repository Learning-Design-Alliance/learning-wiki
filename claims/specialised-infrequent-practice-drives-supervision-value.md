## Evidence

### Burke 2025

Burke, K. L. A., Snowdon, D. A., Milne, S. C., & Farlie, M. K. (2025). Exploring Physiotherapists' Perspectives of Group Clinical Supervision Effectiveness for Aquatic Physiotherapy Skill Development: A Qualitative Study. Physiotherapy Research International. https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70044

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group theme on specialised area of practice: rotating staff found pool sessions rushed, and group supervision offered "a dedicated time to formally reflect on practice" in this specialised area. Participants perceived little benefit when not practising aquatically.

> "Group clinical supervision sessions were reported to provide participants with protected time to discuss aquatic physiotherapy processes, equipment, terminology and principles that are not otherwise encountered in their daily practice."
