From the same knot-tying film repetition experiments; the abstract hedges the result as "some evidence to suggest" and "somewhat superior," so it is reported with those qualifiers.

"There was some evidence to suggest that differing presentations of a task may be somewhat superior to repeating the same presentation when level of verbaliza- tion is varied. These differing presentations should probably progress from low to high verbalization."
