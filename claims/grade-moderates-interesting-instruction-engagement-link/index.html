Evidence¶

Xin Xia 2026¶

Xin Xia, Robert Tai. (2026). Exploring the association of student perceptions of science instruction and emotional engagement: The variance between grade level. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09800-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Model 3 interaction terms (grade × interesting science instruction) in the multilevel model of 6,465 students; the likelihood-ratio test favored Model 3 over Model 2 (χ² = 28.396, df = 9, p < 0.001). Interactions for grades 4, 6, 7, 8, and 12 were significant; grade 4's b = 0.095, p < 0.05.

"students in grade 6 ( b = 0.134, p < 0.01), grade 7 ( b = 0.163, p < 0.001), grade 8 ( b = 0.101, p < 0.05), and grade 12 ( b = 0.100, p < 0.05) with higher perceptions of interesting science teaching rate higher emotional engagement but the rate of change is different than third-grade students."

Xin Xia 2026 (2)¶

Xin Xia, Robert Tai. (2026). Exploring the association of student perceptions of science instruction and emotional engagement: The variance between grade level. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09800-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation of the plotted significant interactions in Fig. 2, which visualized only statistically significant grade × interesting-instruction interactions. The article suggests transition-year students' emotional engagement is most sensitive to perceived interestingness of science class.

"Grade 6, which is the transition year in the United States for most schools, shows the steepest increase in emotional engagement relative to how interested they feel about science instruction."
