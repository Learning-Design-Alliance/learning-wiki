## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Model 1, positive cases screened by public health physicians (OR = 2.927, 95%CI: 1.091–7.854) and nurses (OR = 3.712, 95%CI: 1.141–12.069) were more likely to be true positives than those screened by GPs, but associations did not persist in Model 3; the article prints no standardised effect size. ## Evidence

### Qiu Yanglin 2025

Qiu Yanglin, Fan Xinyu, Wu Yanjuan, Wang Shibin, Tan Wenyan, Liao Jing. (2025). Variation in dementia screening outcomes: the influence of primary care providers' occupations and knowledge, attitudes, skills. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-025-02886-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 1 multilevel logistic regression of PPV among 595 older adults completing second-stage diagnosis, nested in PCPs, with GPs as reference. The article notes these associations "did not persist after further adjusting for PCPs' characteristics in Model 3" (public health physician OR = 2.112, P = 0.139; nurse OR = 2.995, P = 0.098); no standardised effect size is printed.

> "older adults screened positive by public health physicians were 2.927 times (OR = 2.927, 95%CI: 1.091–7.854) more likely to be true positive than GPs, and nurses were 3.712 times (OR = 3.712, 95%CI: 1.141–12.069) more likely than GPs."
