Alhadithi Ahmed 2025¶

Alhadithi Ahmed, Almansour Mohammed. (2025). Leadership and management competency frameworks in undergraduate medical education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08489-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of teaching and learning methods across the 16 included publications, reported in the Results and Discussion. The review states there was "broad consensus favoring interactive and experiential learning approaches over traditional didactic methods."

"This review identified broad consensus favoring interactive and experiential learning approaches over traditional didactic methods in the teaching of MLM. Common strategies included simulations, mentorship, student-led projects, and quality improvement initiatives, all of which were employed to foster active engagement and real-world leadership skills."
