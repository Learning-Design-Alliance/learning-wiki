# Average user engagement time in a learning app rose by around 68% from leaderboard off to leaderboard on in a one-group pre-post study of 10 students with no comparison group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Weekly average user engagement time in the UPSC Pre app increased from a total mean of 24.35 (leaderboard off) to 39.58 (leaderboard on), an increase of around 68%, and a paired sample t-test indicated the difference was statistically significant. [→ Venkata Sai Bhargav Bathini 2024](#venkata-sai-bhargav-bathini-2024)

## Evidence

### Venkata Sai Bhargav Bathini 2024

Venkata Sai Bhargav Bathini, Lokesh Meesala, Ahamad Shaik, Hrushyang Adloori. (2024). The Impact of Virtual Achievements on Online Learning Applications. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.05877](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.05877)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject experiment with 10 students using the UPSC Pre app, one month without and one month with a leaderboard, engagement time logged via Firebase Analytics. The authors report the increase as "around 68%" and state the paired t-test showed the difference was statistically recognizable; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "From Figure 2, we can observe that the average user engagement time has increased after implementing the leaderboard to the app by around 68%. The Figure 3 shows that the t-test proves successfully prove that the difference cannot be by chance but statistically recognizable with leaderboard implementation."
