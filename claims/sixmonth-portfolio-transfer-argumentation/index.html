Daniel Cebrián-Robles; Antonio-Joaquín Franco-Mariscal; Ángel Blanco-López 2018¶

Daniel Cebrián-Robles; Antonio-Joaquín Franco-Mariscal; Ángel Blanco-López. (2018). Preservice Elementary Science Teachers' Argumentation Competence: Impact of a Training Programme. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9446-4

q2 · i? · causal · r2

This finding is stated in the abstract; the paper's results text reporting the Fisher's exact test on transfer falls beyond the provided excerpt. The comparison describes a slight improvement for the PESTs of the experimental group in relation to the control group; no effect size or test statistic is printed in the quoted span, so no magnitude is reported.

"A 6-month transfer task showed a slight improvement for the PESTs of the experimental group in relation to the control group in their ability to transfer argumentation to practice, especially to the extent to which they mentioned argumentation in their practice portfolios."

Daniel Cebrián-Robles; Antonio-Joaquín Franco-Mariscal; Ángel Blanco-López 2018 (2)¶

Daniel Cebrián-Robles; Antonio-Joaquín Franco-Mariscal; Ángel Blanco-López. (2018). Preservice Elementary Science Teachers' Argumentation Competence: Impact of a Training Programme. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9446-4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Documentary analysis of 25 E-PEST and 29 C-PEST practice portfolios, searched in PDF via the keyword fragment argu and coded on a three-category progression (C1: not mentioned; C2: mentioned but not part of teaching-unit activities; C3: part of the teaching-unit activities actually implemented).

"In this way, 25 portfolios from E-PESTs and 29 from C-PESTs were accessed."
