A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. Random effects meta-analysis of correlations between individual station scores and total OSCE scores. The pooled estimate was "0.71 (95% CI: 0.61, 0.79)" with moderate heterogeneity I2 = 54.17%. No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

"The pooled estimate of Pearson Correlation between each station score and the total score based on all the available data was 0.71 (95% CI: 0.61, 0.79) with moderate statistical heterogeneity, I2 = 54.17% (Fig 5)."
