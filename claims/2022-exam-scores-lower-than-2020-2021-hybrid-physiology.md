## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoran 2024

Wang Xiaoran, Liu Jiangheng, Jia Shuwei, Hou Chunmei, Jiao Runsheng, Yan Yan, Ma Tengchuang, Zhang Ying, Liu Yanyan, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng, Zhu Hui, Liu Xiao-Yu. (2024). Hybrid teaching after COVID-19: advantages, challenges and optimization strategies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05745-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Three-year comparison of objective final exam scores via one-way ANOVA with post-hoc tests among Physiology students taught by the same team. The article reports Pharmacy means of "70.7 ± 1.27% in 2020" falling to 59.5 ± 1.28% in 2022, ES = 0.591, P < 0.01 in ANOVA.

> "the average score of students in 2022 decreased significantly compared to those in 2020 and 2021, both in Pharmacy and Clinical pharmacy students (Pharmacy: 70.7 ± 1.27% in 2020, n = 92; 70.9 ± 1.58% in 2021, n = 85; 59.5 ± 1.28% in 2022, n = 94, ES = 0.591, P < 0.01 in ANOVA"
