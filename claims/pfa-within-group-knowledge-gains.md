## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Intervention-group knowledge scores rose significantly from T0 to T1 (r = 0.56) and remained significantly above baseline at T2 (r = 0.35), while the control group showed no significant change. [→ He Yazhou 2026](#he-yazhou-2026)

## Evidence

### He Yazhou 2026

He Yazhou, Ding Yulan, Yan Jiaqin, Xiong Xiaoning, Huang Shan, Li Sijian. (2026). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Psychological First Aid Training for Community Nurses in China: A Quasi‐Experimental Study on Knowledge and Skills Enhancement. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70497

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group analysis of the intervention group in the quasi-experiment, comparing knowledge across T0, T1 and T2. The T0–T1 gain was significant at "Z = 5.538, p < 0.01, r = 0.56", and T2 remained above baseline (r = 0.35).

> "Within‐group analyses showed PFA knowledge scores significantly increased from T0 to T1 in intervention group (Z = 5.538, p < 0.01, r = 0.56, median difference = 3.00, 95% CI: 2.00–3.50) and remained significantly higher at T2 compared to baseline"
