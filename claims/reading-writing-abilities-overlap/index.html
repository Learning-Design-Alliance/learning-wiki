The article cites Tierney and Shanahan (1991) as background support, reporting that their study suggests a 25% to 50% overlap between reading and writing abilities; the review attributes this to the cited study and reports no further detail of its design.

"Tierney and Shanahan’s study (1991) suggests that there is 25% to 50% overlap between reading and writing abilities. Teaching reading based on writing has theoretical basis."
