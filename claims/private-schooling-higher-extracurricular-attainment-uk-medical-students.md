## Evidence

### Ferreira Tomas 2025

Ferreira Tomas, Collins Alexander M, Handscomb Arthur, French Benjamin, Bolton Emily, Fortescue Amelia, Plumb Ella, Feng Oliver, The FAST Collaborative. (2025). Socioeconomic and demographic predictors of extracurricular achievements among UK medical students (FAST study). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-103062

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on survey responses from 8,395 UK medical students across all 44 UK medical schools, adjusted for gender, ethnicity, schooling, intercalation, previous degree, year of study and family in medicine. The article reports participation "significantly higher among students educated at private (fee-paying) schools" with OR 1.35 (p<0.0001); no standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "In every activity evaluated, levels of participation and attainment were found to be significantly higher among students educated at private (fee-paying) schools (OR 1.35, CI 1.2 to 1.53, p<0.0001) and grammar schools (OR 1.18, CI 1.04 to 1.33, p=0.008) than those who attended comprehensive state schools, controlling for all other characteristics."
