## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Debate instruction produced a statistically significant pre-to-post increase in WTC (t=6.76, p=.000). [→ Shamsudin et al. 2017](#shamsudin-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Shamsudin et al. 2017

Mardziah Shamsudin, Moomala Othman, Maryam Jahedi & Dalia Aralas. (2017). Enhancing English Learners' Willingness to Communicate through Debate and Philosophy Inquiry Discussion. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p145

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test on pre/post WTC questionnaire scores for the Debate group (n=16) in a pretest-posttest experimental design; the article reports t=6.76, p=.000, a "statistically significant difference in means between the pre- and the post-test".

> "For Debate, the result (Table 2) shows that there is a st atistically mean difference for WTC (t=6.76, p < 0.05)."

### Shamsudin et al. 2017 (2)

Mardziah Shamsudin, Moomala Othman, Maryam Jahedi & Dalia Aralas. (2017). Enhancing English Learners' Willingness to Communicate through Debate and Philosophy Inquiry Discussion. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p145

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test for the PI discussion group (n=16); Table 3 reports pre-test 51.29, post-test 59.80, t=3.16, Sig. .004, so the null hypothesis was rejected for the PI group as well.

> "Similarly, for PI discussion, the result (Table 3) shows that there is a statistically  mean difference for WTC."
