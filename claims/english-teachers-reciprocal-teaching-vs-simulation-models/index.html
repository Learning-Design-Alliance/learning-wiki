Zendler 2018¶

Descriptive analysis of mean ratings across all 20 × 6 method × knowledge-process combinations (Figure 3), following recommended procedures for cross-contextual research. The article reports that "The English teachers have rated reciprocal teachings rated much better" while rating models method and computer simulation much worse; no effect sizes are printed for these contrasts.
