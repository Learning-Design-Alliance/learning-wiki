Evidence¶

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023¶

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic comparison of pooled EF effect sizes for C-RD (0.35), C-MD (0.40), and C-RDMD (0.71). The comorbid group was "significantly more impaired than both the MD and RD group (both p < 0.01)"; no pairwise effect size is printed.

"Two of these three effects differed significantly from each other: the comorbid group was sig-nificantly more impaired than both the MD and RD group (both p < 0.01)."

Related Claims¶

  • Executive function deficits in comorbid RDMD show some evidence of under-additivity, especially in inhibition, shifting, and updating — related
  • The math deficit profile of children with comorbid RDMD is additive: it equals the sum of the deficits of isolated RD and MD groups — related
  • The reading deficit profile of children with comorbid RDMD is additive relative to isolated RD and MD — related
  • The comorbid RD+MD group is more impaired than groups with RD only or MD only on seven of ten neuropsychological composites — related
  • In reading-related outcomes, group differences involving RD or RDMD are largest for reading tasks and phonological processing and smallest for RAN — related
  • Group differences in math skills between LD and comorbid groups are more pronounced in symbolic than non-symbolic math tasks — related
  • Children with MD only receive extra math assistance far less often than children with comorbid RD+MD — related
