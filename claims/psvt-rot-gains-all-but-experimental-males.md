## Evidence

### Jennifer Wilhelm 2009

Jennifer Wilhelm, Christa Jackson, Amber Sullivan, Ron Wilhelm. (2009). Factors Influencing Middle School Students' Spatial Mathematics Development While Participating in an Integrated STEM Unit. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Factors+Influencing+Middle+School+Students+Spatial+Mathematics+Development

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

PSVT-Rot pre-post results for the control group (Table 7): mean 43.7% to 49.5%, F = 10.8, p = 0.002, partial eta-squared 0.135. Control males (F = 6.26, p = 0.017) and control females (F = 4.47, p = 0.042) also gained significantly.

### Jennifer Wilhelm 2009 (2)

Jennifer Wilhelm, Christa Jackson, Amber Sullivan, Ron Wilhelm. (2009). Factors Influencing Middle School Students' Spatial Mathematics Development While Participating in an Integrated STEM Unit. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Factors+Influencing+Middle+School+Students+Spatial+Mathematics+Development

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

PSVT-Rot pre-post results for the experimental group (Table 7): mean 35.6% to 40.1%, F = 7.035, p = 0.009, partial eta-squared 0.060. Experimental females gained significantly (p = 0.038) but experimental males did not (p = 0.086).

> "Exp. All 111 35.6  17.4 40.1  20.3 4.5 7.035 0.009 0.060"
