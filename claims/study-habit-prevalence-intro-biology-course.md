## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2021

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from exam reflections (n = 422 and n = 456). The article reports that most students (73%) used four or more active strategies and very few used none.

> "On average, students used approximately four active strategies for exam 1 (M = 4.212, SD = 1.510) and exam 2 (M = 4.239, SD = 1.501)."

### Walck-Shannon 2021 (2)

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Strategy prevalence from Table 1 (exam 1 n = 424; exam 2 n = 471): reading notes was most prevalent (94.58% exam 1), followed by active strategies such as completing problem sets (86.08%) and completing old exams (82.08%).

> "our results showed that, after reading notes, the most frequently used strategies were active strategies."
