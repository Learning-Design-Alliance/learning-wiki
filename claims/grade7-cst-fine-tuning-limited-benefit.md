## Evidence

### Chun-Wei Huang 2016

Chun-Wei Huang, Jason Snipes, Neal Finkelstein, WestEd. (2016). Using assessment data to guide math course placement of California middle school students. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of placement predictions across grade 6 CST, grade 7 CST, and MDTP scores in the analytic sample; the study reports "The vast majority of students (over 80 percent) who were predicted to succeed in Algebra I based on one assessment were also predicted to succeed based on others."

> "The vast majority of students (over 80 percent) who were predicted to succeed in Algebra I based on one assessment were also predicted to succeed based on others. Moreover, the probability of grade 8 success for students with different predictions based on different tests was only about 50 percent."
