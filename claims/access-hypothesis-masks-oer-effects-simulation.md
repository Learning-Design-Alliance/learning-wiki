Simulation analysis reported by Grimaldi, Basu Mallick, Waters, and Baraniuk (2019) and summarized in the commentary; the authors suggest failure to account for the access hypothesis may contribute to the large number of no significant difference findings in OER research.

> "Using simulation analysis, they demonstrate that if researchers fail to account for students who would have had access to required course materials even had they been expensive, they will likely be unable to detect any effect of OER adoption on student outcomes."
