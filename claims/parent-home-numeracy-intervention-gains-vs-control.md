## Evidence

### Lore 2016

Lore, M. D., Wang, A. H., & Buckley, M. (2016). Effectiveness of a Parent-Child Home Numeracy Intervention on Urban Catholic School First Grade Students. Journal of Catholic Education, 19(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.15365/joce.1903082016

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way between-groups ANCOVA comparing the treatment group (n = 12) and control group (n = 48) on posttest GMADE Total Test scores with pretest as covariate. The article reports "a large effect size (partial eta-squared = .13)". Posttest means were 45.67 (treatment) vs 32.17 (control).

> "after adjusting for the preintervention GMADE Total Test scores, there was a significant difference between the experimental group and the control group on the GMADE Total Test score (F(1, 58) = 8.59, p = .005) with a large effect size (partial eta-squared = .13)"
