Khalifeh, F., Santiago, R., & Palau, R. (2026). Redefining personalized learning in the artificial intelligence era: an updated systematic review from 2019 to 2025. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-026-00440-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's Research Question 7 synthesis across included studies. It concludes personalized learning is most effective when AI provides adaptive precision while teachers ensure pedagogical coherence, ethical oversight, and human involvement, with the two functioning as interdependent partners.

"Findings across studies indicate that personalized learning functions as a collaborative human–AI model in which teachers and AI systems play complementary roles."
