### Gonçalves Mário Rui 2025

Gonçalves Mário Rui, Marinho Ricardo, Reis Sofia Gaspar, Viveiros Ricardo, Teixeira Manuel Moutinho, Andrade Ana Kam, do Carmo Girão Maria, Rodrigues Pedro Pina-Vaz, Castelo-Branco Sousa Miguel. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course questionnaire answered by 32 of 35 participants (91%) using five-point Likert scales. "There was a strong agreement between participants" on model adequacy (M = 4,56) and face and content validation (M = 4,5 and M = 4,44). Ratings are descriptive; no inferential test is reported.

> "There was a strong agreement between participants for: model adequacy for this type of simulation/course (M = 4,56, SD = 0.504); face and content validation (M = 4,5, SD = 0,58; M = 4,44, SD = 0,58, respectively)"
