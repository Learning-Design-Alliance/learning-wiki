Van de Sande, E., Kleemans, T., Verhoeven, L., & Segers, E. (2019). The linguistic nature of children's scientific reasoning. Learning and Instruction, 62, 20-26. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.02.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (SEM) in 269 sixth graders; the final parsimonious model "demonstrated a strong fit: x2 (6) = 8.09,p= .23, RMSEA = 0.036, CFI = 0.99, and GFI = 0.99". Standardised path coefficients are shown in Fig. 1; no effect sizes are printed.
