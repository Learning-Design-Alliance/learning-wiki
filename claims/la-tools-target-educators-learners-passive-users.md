## Evidence

### Arham Muslim 2020

Arham Muslim, Mohamed Amine Chatti, Mouadh Guesmi. (2020). Open Learning Analytics: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Perspectives. University of Duisburg-Essen. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Stakeholders (Who?) template-analysis section of the review of 137 tools. The review reports educators as the main target (101 tools), learners second (79 tools), administrators third (16 tools), and notes the focus is shifting toward learners due to open and networked learning environments.

> "The main target stakeholders of the reviewed LA solutions are ' Educators' (101 tools) and 'Learners' (79 tools)."

### Arham Muslim 2020 (2)

Arham Muslim, Mohamed Amine Chatti, Mouadh Guesmi. (2020). Open Learning Analytics: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Perspectives. University of Duisburg-Essen. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-41099-5_1)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Same stakeholder analysis. The review states most tools treat stakeholders as passive, with user involvement "limited to the interaction with the provided visualizations"; only few tools actively involve users, e.g. by letting educators create personalization rules or stakeholders self-define custom indicators.

> "The majority of the surveyed tools view the stakeholders as passive users. User involvement is in most of the cases limited to the interaction with the provided visualizations."
