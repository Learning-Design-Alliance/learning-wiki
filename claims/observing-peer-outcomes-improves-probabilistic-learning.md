### Rodriguez Buritica 2024

Rodriguez Buritica, J. M., Eppinger, B., Heekeren, H. R., Crone, E. A. & van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K. (2024). Observational reinforcement learning in children and young adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00227-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects general linear model of choice accuracy in 30 children and 30 young adults performing the probabilistic learning task, controlling for intelligence. The model showed "significant main effects of learning condition (β = 0.12, t = 5.2, p < 0.001)", with no age × condition interaction (p = .9).
