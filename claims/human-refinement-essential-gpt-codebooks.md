# Human review remains essential after GPT-based codebook generation, producing a final refined SRL codebook of eleven constructs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two human coders merged overlapping constructs, renamed codes, and excluded inapplicable ones, reducing the GPT-generated codebook to eleven constructs. [→ Zambrano 2026](#zambrano-2026)

## Evidence

### Zambrano 2026

Zambrano, A.F., Wei, Z., Zhang, J., Baker, R.S., Ocumpaugh, J., Barany, A., Liu, X., Ginger, J., Paquette, L., Zhou, Y., & Borchers, C. (2026). Data Plus Theory Equals Codebook: Leveraging LLMs for Human-AI Codebook Development. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 18, No 1. https://osf.io/g3z4x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Step 3 human refinement by two coders evaluated specificity and applicability of GPT's constructs, collapsing overlapping codes (e.g., Planning and Goal Setting) and renaming Calculation & Conceptual Understanding as Domain Knowledge Recall. The authors conclude "human review remains essential for code merging and codebook refinement".

> "This refinement process resulted in a final codebook consisting of eleven constructs."
