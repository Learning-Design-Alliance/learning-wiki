## Subclaims

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Step 3 human refinement by two coders evaluated specificity and applicability of GPT's constructs, collapsing overlapping codes (e.g., Planning and Goal Setting) and renaming Calculation & Conceptual Understanding as Domain Knowledge Recall. The authors conclude "human review remains essential for code merging and codebook refinement".

> "This refinement process resulted in a final codebook consisting of eleven constructs."
