The review's RQ2 results report per-form study counts in Table 3 and running text: passive participation 12 studies, solo active participation "a total of 45 studies assessed," cognitive interactions 61 studies, metacognitive interactions 26 studies in text (the table prints 22), socioemotional interactions 19, coordinative interactions 16. The inconsistency between the text count for metacognitive interactions and Table 3 is the source's own.

"Cognitive interactions reflect a proactive tendency to interact with group members about the cognitive aspects of the project. Cognitive interactions are the most common aspect that studies focused on to measure individual participation, with 61 studies adopting this perspective"
