## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The child-PGS effect on literacy-focused parenting and its mediation of educational outcomes were significant in the main analysis and the 96,577-trio sensitivity analysis but not in the 19,884-trio analysis. [→ Austerberry 2026](#austerberry-2026)

## Evidence

### Austerberry 2026

Austerberry, C., Zayats, T., Ronald, A., Corfield, E., Smajlagic, D., Havdahl, A., Andreassen, O. A., Magnus, P., Njølstad, P. R., Bekkhus, M., & Fearon, P. (2026). Evocative effects of children's education‐associated genetics on maternal parenting: results from the Norwegian mother, father and child cohort study. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(2), 158–170. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two preregistered sensitivity analyses: 19,884 trios with all three members genotyped, and 96,577 trios with some data. The mediated effect via literacy-focused parenting also failed significance in analysis 1 (a*b β = 4 × 10−3, p = .173); all other findings were similar.

> "the effects of children's PGSedu on cognitively simulating parenting (which were significant in analysis 2 [β = 0.03, 95% CI [0.01, 0.05], p = .019] and the main analysis) were not statistically significant in analysis 1 (β = 0.02, 95% CI [−0.01, 0.05], p = .180)"
