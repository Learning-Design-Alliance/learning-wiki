## Evidence

### Nasit V 2021

Nasit V, Vongsurbchart T, Myszka A, Richter P, Rogula T. (2021). Medical student experience with robot-assisted surgery after limited laparoscopy exposure. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-020-01129-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expert benchmark established by the senior investigator completing each task three times on the Senhance system. The article reports the expert "completed the ball placement task without any errors or interruptions in 43.3 ± 3.1 s and used the clutch 2.0 ± 1.0 times"; novice group means were far higher.

> "For reference, the expert completed the ball placement task without any errors or interruptions in 43.3 ± 3.1 s and used the clutch 2.0 ± 1.0 times (mean ± standard deviation of three attempts)."
