## Evidence

### Bergman EM 2013

Bergman EM, de Bruin ABH, Herrler A, Verheijen IWH, Scherpbier AJJA, van der Vleuten CPM. (2013). Students' perceptions of anatomy across the undergraduate problem-based learning medical curriculum: a phenomenographical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-152

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus-group finding among senior (and some junior) students: regional teaching left nerves and blood vessels seen only in fragments, and students described trouble applying knowledge when interpreting X-rays, CT scans or MRIs.

> "According to the students, a specific shortcoming of PBL was that anatomy was taught by region, sometimes with months between different regions. As a result, they did not form a clear coherent picture of the human body."
