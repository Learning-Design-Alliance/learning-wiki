`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of TARS log data through July 2017 for 21 grantees in the 2014 cohort, with usage categorized by natural breaks into low/medium/high, compared across jurisdiction type with chi square tests. The comparison names "tribal grantees" against "state and county grantees"; only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "As shown, tribal grantees are more likely to be high users of TA as compared with state and county grantees (χ2 = 12.85, p = 0.007)."
