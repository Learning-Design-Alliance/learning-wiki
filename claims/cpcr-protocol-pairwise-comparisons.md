# Workshops combining lecture, human model, and film (T3 and T4) were significantly better than lecture-plus-model alone (T1) and film alone (T2), while T1 outperformed T2 and T3 did not differ significantly from T4

## Evidence

### Hasani H 2015

Hasani H, Bahrami M, Malekpour A, Dehghani M, Allahyary E, Amini M, Abdorahimi M, Khani S, Kalantari Meibodi M, Kojuri J, Durante A. (2015). Evaluation of Teaching Methods in Mass CPCR Training in Different Groups of the Society, an Observational Study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000859

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section of the observational comparison of 4 protocols in 3 groups; reports the T1-versus-T2 contrast as significant with P value < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "In 3 groups of the study, the 1st method of CPCR training was significantly better than the 2nd method (P value < 0.05)."

### Hasani H 2015 (2)

Hasani H, Bahrami M, Malekpour A, Dehghani M, Allahyary E, Amini M, Abdorahimi M, Khani S, Kalantari Meibodi M, Kojuri J, Durante A. (2015). Evaluation of Teaching Methods in Mass CPCR Training in Different Groups of the Society, an Observational Study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000859

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same results section: T3 and T4 each significantly beat T1 and T2 (P value < 0.05), while T3 versus T4 showed "no significant difference" (P value > 0.05); equivalence was not tested.

> "Although the 4th method had better results in CPCR training, there was no significant difference between the efficacy of 3rd and 4th training methods in practical and theoretical examinations (P value > 0.05). On the contrary, the 3rd and 4th method significantly were better than 1st and 2nd methods of training (P value < 0.05)."
