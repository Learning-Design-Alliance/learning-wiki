## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Electronics lab instructors' approaches to troubleshooting instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis of 20 instructors' characterizations of proficiency; many described strategic knowledge like the forward topographic and split-half strategies, and many described demeanor and emotion regulation: students who are weak "lack confidence and patience while students who are good at troubleshooting work independently."

> "Many participants described non-cognitive aspects of troubleshooting proﬁciency, including facets of students’ demeanor as well as students’ ability to regulate their own emotions."
