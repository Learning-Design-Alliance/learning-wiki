Ponniah 2011¶

Ponniah, R. J. (2011). The Effectiveness of the Comprehension Hypothesis: A Review on the Current Research on Incidental Vocabulary Acquisition. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, 1(2), April-June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071042

q2 · i? · review · r1

Narrative review of incidental vocabulary acquisition research (Krashen 2004; Lee 2009; Kweon & Kim 2008; Day et al. 1991). The review "confirms that incidental acquisition of vocabulary is more powerful than intentional learning" across multiple cited studies.

"A wealth of research have supported the claim that vocabulary can be acquired subconsciously while receiving comprehensible input"
