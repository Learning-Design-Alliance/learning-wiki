## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2023

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Progression in self-efficacy, interest, identity, sense of belonging, perceived recognition and effectiveness of peer interaction of physics majors and comparison with non-majors and Ph.D. students. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression models on post-responses (Table 5) predicting sense of belonging from recognition, self-efficacy, and peer interaction. Self-efficacy betas range 0.26-0.71; R2 values range 0.51-0.65. Year 2 majors are again an exception pattern, as in the self-efficacy models.

> "in every model except for year 2 majors, the best predictor of sense of belonging is self-efficacy, with peer interaction being the next best predictor (except in year 4 when it is not statistically significant)."
