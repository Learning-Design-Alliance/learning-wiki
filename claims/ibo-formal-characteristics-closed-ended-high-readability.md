## Evidence

### Opitz Sebastian 2020

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Formal-characteristics coding of six IBO cohorts shows a stable strong focus on closed-ended formats in theory exams ("> 90% of items"); descriptive share, no test statistic reported.

> "The item type remained stable across analyzed IBO years with a strong focus on closed-ended formats (single choice, multiple choice, [multiple] true-false) in the theoretical exams (> 90% of items)."

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (2)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Automated readability scoring (averages of Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning-Fog, and SMOG) showed reading load high across all years, roughly matching contestants' ages, yet many items' reading level was "significantly higher than a typically appropriate reading level for this age group."

> "Nonetheless, many items exhibited such a large share of advanced technical terms that the respective reading level was significantly higher than a typically appropriate reading level for this age group."
