## Evidence

### Tsukamoto Atsushi 2026

Tsukamoto Atsushi, Zan Thum Su, Nitta Makie, Yoshida Hiromitsu, Katahira Hirotaka, Fujita Yoshiharu, Takagi Satoshi, Nakamura Shinichiro. (2026). Educational efficacy of training videos and simulators for teaching basic mouse experimental skills to novice veterinary students. Experimental Animals. https://doi.org/10.1538/expanim.25-0093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 149 novice veterinary students rating five preparatory resource categories on a five-point Likert scale. Contribution scores showed the same trend: dynamic visual (4.7), auditory (4.7), still images (4.5), kinesthetic (4.3), notes (3.6).

> "The percentage of students who rated each preparatory resource as “effective” or “very effective” decreased in the following order: dynamic visual information (96%), auditory information (94%), still images (92%), kinesthetic information (83%), and self-written notes (57%)."
