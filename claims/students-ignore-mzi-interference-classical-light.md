## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Interactive tutorial to improve student understanding of single photon experiments involving a Mach-Zehnder Interferometer. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pretest data from upper-level undergraduate (N = 44) and Ph.D. (N = 45) quantum mechanics courses on a question about a beam of light with intensity I through the MZI. The study reports "66% of the undergraduate students and 56% of the Ph.D. students incorrectly agreed" with the I/2-per-detector statement.

> "However, Table 1 shows that  66% of the  undergraduate students and 56% of the Ph.D. students  incorrectly agreed with this statement in the pretest, indicating that they did not take into account the interference phenomenon taking place at the detectors."
