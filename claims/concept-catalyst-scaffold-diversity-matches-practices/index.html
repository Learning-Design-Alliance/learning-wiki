Gennie Mansi 2026¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · design · r2

Diversity findings from the WoZ interview study: teachers reported the concept-map-driven generation matched their content emphases, including technical definitions, teamwork, student interests, and prompts connecting to personal experience and higher-order thinking.

"Teachers observed Concept Catalyst matched their content focuses, such as specific definitions of “criteria” and “constraints” (P1, P4, P9), incorporating soft skills such as teamwork (P1), tailoring content for student interests (P6)"

Gennie Mansi 2026 (2)¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

In the same study, teachers generated scaffolds for discussions, homework, assessments, and projects, and adapted them to teaching styles—some editing into scripted presentations, others building a "library of prompts" to draw on during class.

"They also created scaffolds for various in class activities, including verbal discussions (P4, P8, P9), homework assignments (P4, P8, P9), assessments (P8, P10), and longer projects (P2)."
