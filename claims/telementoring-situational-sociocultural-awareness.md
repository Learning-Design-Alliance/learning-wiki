`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative finding from reflexive thematic analysis of field notes, observations and semi-structured interviews with seven POCUS trainees, two department heads and two mentors over a twenty-one-month project in two Ugandan healthcare centers. Participants reported that telementoring required orchestrating the situation — positioning of technology, camera angles, patient and caregivers — to create digital proximity, and that situational awareness "is something one has to learn while they engage with this technology."

> "As the project progressed, participants increasingly recognized that effective telementoring required both trainees and mentors to actively cultivate awareness beyond the POCUS probe and in a broader sociocultural context."
