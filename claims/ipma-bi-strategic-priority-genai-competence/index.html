Importance-performance map analysis (IPMA) of total effects on digital competence in the 434-teacher PLS-SEM model, shown in Fig. 3. BI ranked highest; PU and PE showed "notable values"; SE and PEOU had good performance but limited predictive effect; AOU showed low relevance.

"The Fig. 3 shows that BI has the greatest impact on DC, combining high importance (≈ 0.49) and high performance (≈ 86), suggesting that this construct should be considered a strategic priority in the design of training interventions, since its improvement would significantly impact the DC factor."
