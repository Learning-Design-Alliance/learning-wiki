## Evidence

### Jesper Bruun 2013

Jesper Bruun, I.G. Bearden. (2013). Time Development of Early Social Networks: Link analysis and group dynamics. arXiv:1308.4259. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0112775](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112775)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical link analysis shown in Figure 3(b) of the weekly networks: "the fraction of completely new links ... is shown to decrease over time." With 1214 unique links being roughly 5% of possible links, the authors argue the decrease is not due to link saturation.

> "This is supported by Figure 3 (b), where the fraction of completely new links, Ltot−Lree Ltot , is shown to decrease over time. The number of unique links for all weeks is 1214, which is roughly 5% of the total number of possible links"
