## Evidence

### Lin 2025

Lin, T., Aafjes‐van Doorn, K., Heckman, T. G., Antebi‐Lerman, E., & Anderson, T. (2025). Are Therapists Less Skilful in Teletherapy Than In‐Person Therapy Scenarios? A Latent Profile Analysis of Facilitative Interpersonal Skills. Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpp.70047

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired comparison of the 19-item TISS self-report completed for both formats by the same 223 therapists and trainees; coefficient alpha was 0.95 for both ratings. The study reports "p < 0.001, d = 0.41".

> "therapists self‐reported lower therapeutic interpersonal skills (i.e., TISS) in teletherapy versus in‐person therapy (p < 0.001, d = 0.41)."
