Roderick 1997¶

Roderick, M., Arney, M., Axelman, M., DaCosta, K., Steiger, C., Stone, S., Villarreal-Sosa, L., & Waxman, E. (1997). Habits Hard to Break: A New Look at Truancy in Chicago's Public High Schools. Student Life in High Schools Project, School of Social Service Administration, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/habits-hard-break-new-look-truancy-chicagos-public-high-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Transcript-based analysis of second-semester ninth grade attendance distinguishing full-day absences from cutting in a major subject. Extreme truants followed three patterns: 26 percent with 20+ full-day absences, 10 percent combining moderate absences with cutting, and 6 percent attending school but cutting frequently.

"The percentage of CPS ninth graders classified as extreme truants increases from 26 to 42 percent when we consider class cutting as well as full day absences."
