## Evidence

### Hu Bibi 2024

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pooled post-intervention analysis of the 92-student randomized study found the grade effect held within each teaching-method subgroup as well (per Fig. 4). The authors attribute it to Grade 5 students having more clinical rotation experience and Grade 4 students retaining more theoretical knowledge.

> "students in Grade 4 performed better in the general knowledge test (Grade 4: 96.67 ± 4.42 vs. Grade 5: 90.16 ± 3.33, P < 0.01), whereas students in Grade 5 performed better in the clinical case scenarios tests (Grade4: 70.50 ± 16.02 vs. Grade 5: 81.82 ± 13.64, P < 0.01)."
