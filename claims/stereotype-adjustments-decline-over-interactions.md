Cluster-based statistical analysis (corrected for multiple comparisons across seven partner transitions) of block-wise stereotype-driven adjustment estimates in the same 95-participant communication game experiment, testing the temporal dynamics of stereotype use.

> "This analysis identified a cluster of positive adjustments spanning the second and third transition (Fig. 1C), suggesting that adjustments to stereotype beliefs were transiently prominent at the onset of interactions and gradually declined as interactions unfolded."
