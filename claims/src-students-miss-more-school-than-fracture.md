## Evidence

### Russell Kelly 2019

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective cohort analysis of student-maintained school attendance log books, in which students recorded only injury-related days missed. Concussion students missed a "median 4 full or partial days of school" versus one day for fracture students (p<0.0001).

> "Students who sustained a SRC missed significantly more school (median 4 full or partial days of school [IQR: 1, 7] compared with students who sustained a SRF (median 1 full or partial day [IQR: 0, 4], p<0.0001)."

### Russell Kelly 2019 (2)

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial regression with backwards elimination on attendance log book data, controlling for age, sex, depression history, school type, headache history and previous concussion, found "twice the risk of missing school" for SRC students (IRR: 2.09; 95% CI: 1.30, 3.36); no standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "students with a SRC had twice the risk of missing school compared to those with a SRF (IRR: 2.09; 95% CI: 1.30, 3.36)."
