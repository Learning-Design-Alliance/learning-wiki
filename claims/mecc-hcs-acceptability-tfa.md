BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training value ratings (n = 23) and Theoretical Framework of Acceptability questionnaire scores at 6-month follow-up (n = 11); the mean acceptability score was 3.9 (/5). Median scores for all seven TFA constructs were 4, including perceived effectiveness and self-efficacy.

> "On a scale of 1 to 5 (1 = not at all valuable, 5 = extremely valuable), 78% of physiotherapists (18/23) rated the MECC HCS training a 4 or 5, thus, valuable or extremely valuable."
