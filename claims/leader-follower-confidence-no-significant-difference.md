## Evidence

### Vattanavanit Veerapong 2020

Vattanavanit Veerapong, Khwannimit Bodin, Nilmoje Thanapon. (2020). Comparison of knowledge and confidence between medical students as leaders and followers in simulated resuscitation. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5e01.f00c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 139 fifth-year medical students measuring confidence on a 5-point Likert scale after simulation; the article reports the leader-follower contrast as "not statistically significant (t(137)=1.70, p=0.09)". No effect size was printed.

> "The student confidence level as leaders (M=3.63, SD=0.55) was higher than that of the followers (M=3.41, SD=0.64) but not statistically significant (t(137)=1.70, p=0.09)."
