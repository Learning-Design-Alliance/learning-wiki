## Evidence

### Gärtner Julia 2021

Gärtner Julia, Prediger Sarah, Harendza Sigrid. (2021). Development and pilot test of ComCare – a questionnaire for quick assessment of communicative and social competences in medical students after interviews with simulated patients. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001464](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001464)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Across all participants, simulated patients' general "satisfaction with the consultation" exceeded their ComCare skill-item mean, while students' self-assessment "showed inverted results". The authors conclude other factors, e.g. physicians' personality, could contribute to patient satisfaction.

> "For all participants, regardless of their sex or their advancement in the final year, the ComCareP’s mean for general “satisfaction with the consultation” was higher than the total mean score for the ComCare items while ComCareD showed inverted results."
