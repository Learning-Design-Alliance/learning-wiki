`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional face-validation survey of twelve expert urologists at EUREP 2025 using an 11-item 4-point Likert questionnaire. The study's prose says "mean scores were above 3.4 for all items, with overall agreement rates surpassing 90%", but its own Table 1 contradicts this for one item: the anatomical appearance of the prostate lobes and bladder neck scored 3.25 ± 0.75 with 83.3% agreement. The other ten items had means of 3.42 to 3.75 and 91.7% to 100% agreement. "Cronbach’s alpha was calculated at 0.92". No effect size is printed.

> "Twelve expert urologists participated in and completed both validation surveys. The face validity survey revealed a strong consensus among experts about the Aquablation model’s realism and its effectiveness for training. The descriptive analysis indicated that mean scores were above 3.4 for all items, with overall agreement rates surpassing 90%. Cronbach’s alpha was calculated at 0.92, reflecting excellent internal consistency."
