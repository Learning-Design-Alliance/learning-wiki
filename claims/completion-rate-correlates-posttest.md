# Completion rate (percentage of screen cards viewed) correlated with post-test score, but usage time and formative scores did not

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Post-test score correlated with the percentage of screen cards viewed (Spearman R = 0.33; p = 0.03), but not with total usage time (R = 0.17; p = 0.26) or formative question scores (R = 0.03; p = 0.83). [→ Kononowicz AA 2012](#kononowicz-aa-2012)
