# Medical students taught rhomboid flap design with multimedia CAL software earn higher OSATS checklist scores than students taught with printed text

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled study, the CAL group scored higher on the 10-item OSATS checklist than the printed-text group (mean 7.72±2.05 vs 4.08±4.0, p<0.002). [→ de Sena David P. 2013](#de-sena-david-p-2013)

## Evidence

### de Sena David P. 2013

de Sena David P., Fabricio Daniela D., Lopes Maria Helena I., da Silva Vinicius D. (2013). Computer-Assisted Teaching of Skin Flap Surgery: Validation of a Mobile Platform Software for Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0065833

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 50 medical students randomized by sealed envelopes into two groups of 25; blinded board-certified surgeons scored a bench-model rhomboid flap with the OSATS checklist. The CAL group's "average proportion of correct responses was superior in the CAL group for all items."

> "The mean raw score of all 10 checklist items was 4.08±4.0 for the printed text group vs. 7.72±2.05 for the CAL group (p<0.002), as shown in figure 5 and table 1."
