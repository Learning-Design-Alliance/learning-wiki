`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 192 students (132 women, 60 men) disaggregated by gender. Women reported significantly higher rates of negative effects across multiple functional domains and higher STAI-Y trait scores.

> "Women reported a higher impact on attention and concentration (92% vs 63% in men, p=0.0001), sleep quality (84% vs 68% in men, p=0.008), learning capacity (72% vs 47% in men, p=0.001) and eating patterns (55% vs 35% in men, p=0.01)."
