### Wenting Ma 2014

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

ITS-characteristics moderator analysis (Table 2). Under both fixed- and random-effects models, use of ITS produced statistically significant effect sizes whether or not it provided feedback (no feedback k = 10; feedback k = 86) and whether or not it modeled misconceptions (no k = 21; yes k = 58); the between-levels difference for misconception modeling was not statistically significant under either model.
