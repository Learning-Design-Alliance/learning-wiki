## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across five weighted effect sizes, problem posing interventions had a large positive effect on skill-based outcomes such as problem solving performance, with a mean Hedges' g of 0.83 (95% CI 0.58-1.08). [→ Roslinda Rosli 2014](#roslinda-rosli-2014)

## Evidence

### Roslinda Rosli 2014

Roslinda Rosli, Mary Margaret Capraro & Robert M. Capraro. (2014). The Effects of Problem Posing on Student Mathematical Learning: A Meta-Analysis. International Education Studies, 7(13). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/ies.v7n13p227

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of five skill-based weighted effect sizes from experimental and quasi-experimental studies of problem posing interventions. Table 3 reports a mean effect size of .83 with a standard error of .13 and a 95% CI of .58 to 1.08 for skill-based outcomes.

> "The results showed that the problem posing intervention had a positive and statistically significant impact on the student learning outcomes, specifically knowledge-based, skill-based, ability-based, and attitude-beliefs."
