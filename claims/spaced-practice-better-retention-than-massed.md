`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites a systematic review (Cecilio-Fernandes et al.) on spacing of surgical training sessions, reporting that "novice learners who participated in spaced practice sessions performed better on retention tests than novices who underwent massed practice". No effect size is printed.

> "In a recent systematic review comparing massed versus distributed practice for retention of surgery-related technical skills, novice learners who participated in spaced practice sessions performed better on retention tests than novices who underwent massed practice [77]."
