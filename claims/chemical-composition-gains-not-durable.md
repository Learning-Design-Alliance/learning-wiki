## Evidence

### Yakmaci-Guzel 2013

Yakmaci-Guzel, B., & Adadan, E. (2013). Use of multiple representations in developing preservice chemistry teachers' understanding of the structure of matter. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 8(1), 109-130. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008597

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Frequency analysis of inappropriate classifications (Table 8: n=15 pre, n=2 post, n=13 delayed post) plus the non-significant pre-to-delayed test on part c (z=1.81, p>0.05). The article states the change "was not durable" as some participants reverted to initial classifications.

> "such change in preservice teachers' understandings of chemical composition of matter was not durable (see Table 8), because 17 months after the instruction, some of the participants who misclassified the given representations with respect to the chemical composition of matter on the pretest reverted back to their initial classifications"
