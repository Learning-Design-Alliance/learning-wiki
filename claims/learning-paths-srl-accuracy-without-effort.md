# Learning paths used for SRL improved accuracy and performance without increasing effort, and tutoring-system benefits required sustained practice

## Evidence

### Rebecka Rundquist 2024

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Molenaar et al.'s (2020) comparison of two groups using a DLS with learning paths against three groups using the same DLS without them, with lessons beginning by setting goals from extracted analytics.

> "Results indicated that using the learning paths for SRL did not increase student effort, but their accuracy improved, resulting in increased learning performance and transfer."

### Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (2)

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a secondary calibration outcome from Molenaar et al. (2020): no improvement in calibration, with a shift from overestimation toward underestimation of abilities.

> "Although students did not improve in their calibration of their abilities , they did show less overestimation but, at the same time, higher levels of underestimation."

### Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (3)

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Qushem et al.'s (2022) finding on dosage when the tutoring system was used as a complement to regular classroom activity, and that for young students use supported transfer of arithmetic fluency to more advanced domains.

> "students needed to practise for at least 69 days for a minimum of three minutes a day in order to make substantial progress"
