Predictive linear mixed-effects model built on Senior 1's data, plotting estimated console time across case order while holding covariates constant. The model showed "console time decreased by 33.2 min over the first 14 procedures" with stabilisation at case 26 (<0.5 min/case).

> "Based on modelled values, console time decreased by 33.2 min over the first 14 procedures (20.5% without lymphadenectomy and 18.4% with lymphadenectomy), with a mean estimated reduction of 2.6 min per case, defining the point of initial deceleration (marginal reduction <1.0 min/case)."
