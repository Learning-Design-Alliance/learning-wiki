### Melanie Good 2018

Melanie Good, Andrew Mason, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Comparing introductory physics and astronomy students’ attitudes and approaches to problem solving. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unnormalized survey percentages for AAPS Question 10 on reflecting upon principles when stuck, part of the metacognition-and-enjoyment factor the article reports accounted for more of the variance than any other factor in the validation study.

> "a favorable response to this question would be to agree, as 89% of introductory astronomy students did. By comparison, only 65% of introductory physics students gave a favorable response to this question, which can be seen in Figure 4."

### Melanie Good 2018 (2)

Melanie Good, Andrew Mason, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Comparing introductory physics and astronomy students’ attitudes and approaches to problem solving. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unnormalized percentages for AAPS Question 27 on enjoying physics/astronomy problems despite challenges, in the metacognition and enjoyment factor. Interviewed astronomy students reported enjoying astronomy problems more even when equally challenging.

> "As can be seen in Figure 5, while 72% of astronomy students agreed with this statement, only 40% of physics students agreed."
