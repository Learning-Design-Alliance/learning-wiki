## Evidence

### Pearson 2013

Pearson, D., Young, R. B., & Richardson, G. B. (2013). Exploring the Technical Expression of Academic Knowledge: The Science-in-CTE Pilot Study. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(4), 162-179. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.04162

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group randomized trial with 29 agricultural education teachers randomly assigned to experimental (15) or control (14) groups; HLM analysis of 363 students' TerraNova science posttest scores controlled for student- and classroom-level predictors. The article reports a statistically significant positive impact with no standardized effect size for the omnibus quartile result.

> "The results of our HLM model analyses indicated that the intervention had a statistically significant positive impact on posttest science achievement for students in the second, third, and fourth quartiles on pretest science achievement"
