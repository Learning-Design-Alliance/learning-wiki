Descriptive trend analysis of mean ITBS grade equivalents by age group, 1992-1999, adjusted for bilingual inclusion rules (Part 2, Figure 2, Tables C and D). The brief reports "continuing, long-term improvement trends across all grades in math" and upward trends for 13- and 14-year-olds. No effect size is printed.

"Overall, CPS elementary school test scores continue to improve after the adjustments described above. Figure 2 indicates continuing, long-term improvement trends across all grades in math."
