## Evidence

### Ilana Ram 2025

Ilana Ram, Adam Shwartz, Ido Roll. (2025). Minding the gender gap in advanced STEM courses: effects of student preparedness and activity level. Instructional Science (2025) 53:1457–1478. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09748-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regressions (Enter method, theory-driven blocks) on four final exam scores, pooled across 10 imputations. Block transitions to LMS activity added ΔR² of 0.004–0.017 and to Gender×LMS of 0.003–0.015 across courses; the study reports LMS*Gender variables "did not add to explained variance".

> "LMS contribute only modestly to explained variance and LMS*Gender variables did not add to explained variance indicating that observed activity–performance links were similar for men and women."
