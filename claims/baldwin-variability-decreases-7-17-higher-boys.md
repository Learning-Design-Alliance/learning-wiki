The review reports, on Baldwin's growth study covering 400 individual growth curves in traits including height, weight, and strength, that coefficients of variability "tend, on the whole, to decrease from seven to seventeen" and to be higher in boys than girls. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The coefficients of variability tend, on the whole, to decrease from seven to seventeen, and, in general, to be higher in boys than in girls."
