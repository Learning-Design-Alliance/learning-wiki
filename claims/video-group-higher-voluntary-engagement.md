### Donkin Rebecca 2019

Donkin Rebecca, Askew Elizabeth, Stevenson Hollie. (2019). Video feedback and e-Learning enhances laboratory skills and engagement in medical laboratory science students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1745-1

`q3 · i?`

Frequency analytics of the 2017 cohort's in-class and online participation, reported by percentage for each group in Fig. 3. Tutorial attendance was "95.1%" for the video group versus "64.0%" for controls.

> "The tutorials were well attended by the video group (95.1%) in comparison to the control group (64.0%)."

### Donkin Rebecca 2019 (2)

Donkin Rebecca, Askew Elizabeth, Stevenson Hollie. (2019). Video feedback and e-Learning enhances laboratory skills and engagement in medical laboratory science students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1745-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analytics from Blackboard and SmartSparrow captured module usage across the 13-week semester. Total module time on task was "6 h and 48 min (control = 1 h 21 m, video =5 h 27 m)", with seven video-group students repeating it more than once.

> "In total, the module was completed 29 times, total time spent on task was 6 h and 48 min (control = 1 h 21 m, video =5 h 27 m) and was repeated more than once by seven students in the video group."
