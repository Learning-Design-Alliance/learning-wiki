## Evidence

### Foster 1911

Foster, W. T. (1911). The Spelling of College Students. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070538

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Error-classification analysis of the 10,000 Bowdoin themes. Omission errors move toward phonetic spelling; insertion errors arise from analogies with approved spellings the author calls "precisely as irrational and wasteful."

> "These two classes of errors—those due to the omission of silent letters in the direction of an ideal system, and those due to the insertion of silent letters thru analogies with correct forms—comprise 853, or 55 per cent., of the total real errors in spelling."
