### Yu Jiaying 2026

Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the cross-sectional survey of 208 valid responses from nursing interns in five Chinese Grade A tertiary hospitals, using the TAS-20, GSES, and the Clinical Communication Competence Assessment Scale. Means are "55.48 (± 10.31)" and "84.67 (± 10.66)" as printed; no effect sizes reported.

> "Nursing interns scored an average of 55.48 (± 10.31) on alexithymia, 28.21 (± 4.64) on self-efficacy, and 84.67 (± 10.66) on clinical communication skills. Twenty-seven point four percent of the individuals had alexithymia scores equal to or greater than 61, whereas 42.3% fell within the 51–61 range."
