### Ho Dang Khanh Ngan 2022

Ho Dang Khanh Ngan, Lee Yu-Chieh, Chiu Wan-Chun, Shen Yi-Ta, Yao Chih-Yuan, Chu Hung-Kuo, Chu Wei-Ta, Le Nguyen Quoc Khanh, Nguyen Hung Trong, Su Hsiu-Yueh, Chang Jung-Su. (2022). `q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis of the first-semester exam estimates (n = 65) comparing 2-D and 3-D calorie estimates per food item. The printed effect sizes are Spearman r = 0.69–0.93 (all p < 0.05); the article pre-specified coefficients >0.7 as indicating high correlation.

> "The Spearman correlation analysis showed moderate to strong correlations of estimated total calories (0.69~0.93; all p < 0.05) between the 3-D and 2-D models across all food items."
