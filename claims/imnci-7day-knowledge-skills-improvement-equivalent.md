# Abridged 7-day IMNCI training improved knowledge and clinical skills as much as the standard 11-day course, with no statistically significant difference in either measure

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean knowledge score improvement did not differ significantly between the 7-day group (31.6, 95% CI 24.3–38.8) and the 11-day group (29.4, 95% CI 23.9–34.9), p = 0.630. [→ Ariff Shabina 2021](#ariff-shabina-2021)

## Evidence

### Ariff Shabina 2021

Ariff Shabina, Sadiq Kamran, Jiwani Uswa, Ahmed Khalil, Nuzhat Khadija, Ahmed Shakeel, Nizami Qamruddin, Khan Iqtidar A., Ali Nabeela, Soofi Sajid Bashir, Bhutta Zulfiqar A. (2021). Evaluation the Effectiveness of Abridged IMNCI (7-Day) Course v Standard (11-Day) Course in Pakistan. Maternal and Child Health Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10995-021-03276-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental training comparison in districts of Sindh and Punjab, Pakistan; knowledge improvement was analysed for 27 providers in the 7-day group and 30 in the 11-day group. Table 4 reports improvement of "31.6 (18.4)" versus "29.4 (14.8)", p = 0.630, not significant.

> "Improvement Knowledge 27 31.6 (18.4) 24.3–38.8 30 29.4 (14.8) 23.9–34.9 0.630"

### Ariff Shabina 2021 (2)

Ariff Shabina, Sadiq Kamran, Jiwani Uswa, Ahmed Khalil, Nuzhat Khadija, Ahmed Shakeel, Nizami Qamruddin, Khan Iqtidar A., Ali Nabeela, Soofi Sajid Bashir, Bhutta Zulfiqar A. (2021). Evaluation the Effectiveness of Abridged IMNCI (7-Day) Course v Standard (11-Day) Course in Pakistan. Maternal and Child Health Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10995-021-03276-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experimental comparison; clinical skills improvement was analysed for 26 providers in the 7-day group and 31 in the 11-day group. Table 4 reports improvement of "23.8 (11.0)" versus "23 (11.0)", p = 0.784, not significant.

> "Clinical skills 26 23.8 (11.0) 19.3–28.2 31 23 (11.0) 18.9–27.0 0.784"
