Immediate postworkshop formative feedback from 20 of 27 learners responding to prompts on module comments and what to keep doing. Common themes included "Learners praised the interactive, case-based format and appreciated the handouts," with comments such as liking the interactivity (N = 5) and the patient case (N = 3).

> "At the conclusion of the workshop, 20 of the 27 learners provided optional formative workshop feedback in the immediate postworkshop evaluation. Learners praised the interactive, case-based format and appreciated the handouts."
