Thorpe Alistair PhD; Kassianos Angelos P PhD; Plackett Ruth PhD; Krishnamurthy Vinodh PhD; Kambouri Maria A PhD; Sheringham Jessica FFPH, PhD. (2025). Comparison of Physician Assistant and Medical Students' Clinical Reasoning Processes Using an Online Patient Simulation Tool to Support Clinical Reasoning (eCREST): Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/68981

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of 159 medical and 54 PA students completing eCREST Case 1 (chest pain). Essential questions averaged around 70% for both groups; physical exams (P=.59) and bedside tests showed no significant difference, with bedside tests showing only weak evidence of a PA advantage (P=.053).

> "We found no evidence of differences between PA students (mean 73.2, SD 18.9) and medical students (mean 70.1, SD 22.2) in the proportion of essential questions asked by each group (P=.33)."
