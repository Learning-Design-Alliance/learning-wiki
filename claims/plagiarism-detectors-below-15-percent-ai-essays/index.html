Evidence¶

Chunpeng Zhai 2024¶

Chunpeng Zhai, Santoso Wibowo and Lily D. Li. (2024). The effects of over-reliance on AI dialogue systems on students' cognitive abilities: a systematic review. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00316-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Second-hand finding reported in the review's discussion of plagiarism concerns: Khalil and Er's studies of Turnitin and iThenticate detection of plagiarism in literature-based essays. The review attributes the less-than-15% detection rate to these authors and links it to lack of transparency in the algorithms driving plagiarism detection.

"Khalil and Er (2023) have reported that popular plagiarism detection tools, such as Turnitin and iThenticate, show a significant limitation in identifying plagiarism in essays that are based on existing literature. Concerningly, their studies reveal that these tools could only detect plagiarism in less than 15% of cases."
