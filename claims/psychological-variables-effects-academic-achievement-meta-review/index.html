q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review of meta-analyses: 87 effect sizes for 64 psychological variables were drawn from 25 meta-analyses (Table 2). The review reports that "self -efficacy (ES= 1.173) and academic emotions positive low-arousal (ES= .812) have positive and huge effects on academic achievement."
