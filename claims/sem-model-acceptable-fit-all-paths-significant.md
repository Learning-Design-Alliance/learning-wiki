## Evidence

### Moreno-Murcia 2018

Moreno-Murcia, J.A., Huéscar Hernández, E., & Ruiz, L. (2018). Perceptions of Controlling Teaching Behaviors and the Effects on the Motivation and Behavior of High School Physical Education Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15102288

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Maximum likelihood structural equation modeling using the covariance matrix among items, tested with AMOS 21.0 on 416 students; controlling teaching style was the exogenous variable and the remaining five variables endogenous. All proposed paths were significant.

> "The proposed model achieved an acceptable level of fit, χ2(149, N = 416) = 464.68, p = 0.00; χ2/df = 3.12; CFI = 0.91; NFI = 0.87; TLI = 0.90; Root mean square error of approximation (RMSEA) = 0.07 with each of the proposed relationships attaining significance."
