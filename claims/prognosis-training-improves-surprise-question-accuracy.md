## Evidence

### Kupf Irma 2024

Kupf Irma, Thanner Gabriele, Gerken Michael, Crispin Alexander, Braess Jan. (2024). How accurate is clinical prognostication by oncologists during routine practice in a general hospital and can it be improved by a specific prognosis training programme: a prospective interventional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-081661

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-centre interventional study comparing untrained phase 1 with trained phases 2 and 3 in the same physician group; the article reports a "highly significant (p<0.001) improvement in accuracy" for the whole group, with no effect size printed.

> "Physicians achieved 72.6% accuracy in phase 1, 74.0% in phase 2 and 84.3% in phase 3. There was a highly significant (p<0.001) improvement in accuracy over time during the 3 phases for the whole group."
