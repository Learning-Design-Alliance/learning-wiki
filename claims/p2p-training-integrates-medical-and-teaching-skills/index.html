Review content finding from the in-depth analysis of the 18 studies: medical subject content such as anatomy [8] or internal medicine [11] is explicitly part of training programs, and technical skills such as handling ultrasound devices [33] and interprofessional content such as structured patient handover according to ISBAR [7] also count as medical contents.
