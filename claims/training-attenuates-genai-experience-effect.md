`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robust linear interaction model (Magee's pseudo R² = 0.605) on RCT data. The significant "Group × GenAI experience interaction" (β = -3.445, p = 0.034) indicates training attenuated the prior-experience effect; the interaction plot showed a flatter slope for the experimental group (β = 0.015 vs. 3.460).

> "only the Group × GenAI experience interaction was statistically significant ( β = -3.445, 95% CI = [-7.137, -0.264], p = 0.034). The Group × SRL skills interaction ( β = 1.161, 95% CI = [-3.884, 5.623], p = 0.681) and Group × Pretest CSEM score interaction ( β = -1.476, 95% CI = [-4.535, 2.812], p = 0.547) were not statistically significant"
