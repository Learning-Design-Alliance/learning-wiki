## Evidence

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026

Zhao Zhengqi, Wu Baijing, Tian Shuyuan, Hu Haigen, Hao Pengyi. (2026). Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive repeated-use analysis in the voluntary follow-up (n=18 completing 1 case per week for 4 weeks); medians ranged from 13.40 to 20.00 across attempts 1-5, and "All CIs were above zero".

> "All CIs were above zero, indicating positive within-session score differences at each attempt."

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026 (2)

Zhao Zhengqi, Wu Baijing, Tian Shuyuan, Hu Haigen, Hao Pengyi. (2026). Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95039

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject comparison of formative CR between attempts 1 and 2 in the follow-up sample: "CR increased markedly from attempt 1 to attempt 2" with Cohen d=1.739; AP change was smaller and nonsignificant (P=.15, d=0.411).

> "CR increased markedly from attempt 1 to attempt 2 (median 3.50 to 4.50; Hodges-Lehmann median difference=1.00, 95% CI 1.00‐1.50; Wilcoxon P<.001; Cohen d=1.739)"

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026 (3)

Zhao Zhengqi, Wu Baijing, Tian Shuyuan, Hu Haigen, Hao Pengyi. (2026). Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Friedman tests across attempts 2-5 in the follow-up sample detected "no statistically significant within-subject trends" for CR, AP, or the overall evaluation sum; the authors caution these nulls do not indicate stabilization.

> "From attempt 2 onward, no statistically significant within-subject trends were detected for CR (Friedman χ²3=6.484, P=.09), AP (Friedman χ²3=2.088, P=.55), or the overall sum (Friedman χ²3=3.034, P=.39)."
