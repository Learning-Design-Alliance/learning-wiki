### Vanderstraeten Rob 2025

Vanderstraeten Rob, Fourré Antoine, Demoulin Christophe, Michielsen Jozef, Bastiaens Hilde, Roussel Nathalie, Anthierens Sibyl. (2025). What do physiotherapists find useful in e-learning interventions to improve their knowledge, attitudes and beliefs on low back pain management: a nested qualitative study from an educational randomized clinical trial. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-025-03032-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended post-intervention survey responses from 269 physiotherapists (140 EEI, 129 TEI) in the nested process evaluation. Under the 'Practical application' theme, EEI participants found the communication strategies "highly useful and practical to employ in practice", while TEI participants named their absence a major shortcoming.

> "Physiotherapists in the EEI said that they considered the communication strategies (e.g., use of metaphors, 'tips and tricks', how to reassure, how to impart pain education, etc.) to be highly useful and practical to employ in practice."
