## Subclaims
`q3 i?` History-taking, communication, humanity and professionalism, and management mini-CEX scores were not significantly different between the simulation and control groups (p = .37, p = .18, p = .61, p = .57 respectively). [→ Yamauchi Kazuyo 2021](#yamauchi-kazuyo-2021)

## Evidence

### Yamauchi Kazuyo 2021

Yamauchi Kazuyo, Hagiwara Yoko, Iwakura Nahoko, Kubo Saori, Sato Azusa, Ohtsuru Tadahiko, Okazaki Ken, Okubo Yumiko. (2021). Using peer role-playing to improve students’ clinical skills for musculoskeletal physical examinations. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02742-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison of simulation (N = 64) versus control (N = 26) mini-CEX domain scores as the primary outcome analysis; these four domains showed no statistically significant difference. The article reports "not significantly different between the groups" for communication, professionalism and management; history-taking p = .37 is also printed. Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, communication, humanity and professionalism, and management scores of the mini-CEX assessment were not significantly different between the groups (communication: p > .05, p = .18, humanity and professionalism: p > .05, p = .61, management: p > .05, p = .57) (Fig. 2)."
