## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM direct-effect estimate (b-path) in Table 2: SQ coefficient 0.298 (SE 0.064, p < 0.000) relative to AQ, "on average 0.3 points lower" on the M1 grade, holding modelled direct and indirect influences constant.

> "Students admitted via SQ, where cognitive criteria receive less weight relative to factors such as prior vocational experience or documented social engagement, achieve M1 grades that are on average 0.3 points lower than those admitted through AQ"

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026 (2)

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM, Table 2: WQ direct effect on M1 of 0.247 (SE 0.147); the article attributes the non-significance (p = 0.09) to "the comparatively small number of students admitted through this pathway" (n = 84).

> "For WQ, we observe a coefficient of similar magnitude. However, the difference relative to AQ does not reach statistical significance (p = 0.09)"
