## Evidence

### Domen 2020

Domen, J., Hornstra, L., Weijers, D., van der Veen, I., & Peetsma, T. (2020). Differentiated need support by teachers: Student‐specific provision of autonomy and structure and relations with student motivation. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(2), 403–423. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12302

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within‐level path from the multilevel model for autonomous motivation. The printed coefficient was b = .83, p < .001; at the between level the corresponding path was b = .50, p = .004. Teacher and student perceptions were not congruent yet both predicted motivation.

> "The expected positive relation between student‐perceived need support and students' autonomous motivation (b = .83, p < .001) was confirmed."
