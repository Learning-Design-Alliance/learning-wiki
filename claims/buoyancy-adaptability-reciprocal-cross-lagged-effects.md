## Evidence

### Martin 2026

Martin, A. J., Malmberg, L.-E., Holliman, A. J., Teig, N., Putwain, D. W., Collie, R. J., Burns, E. C., Bostwick, K. C. P., Kennett, R., & Pearson, J. (2026). Academic buoyancy and adaptability: A multi-sample study of reciprocal effects—Supplementary materials. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Latent cross-lagged SEM analysis on a subset of pooled data with parallel multi-item factors for both constructs (Studies 1, 2, 5, 6), estimating buoyancy and adaptability as latent constructs with correlated item residuals across time. Both cross-lagged paths were "β = .07 (p < .01)" and are described as "medium cross-lagged effect sizes". No participant n is printed in this section.

> "The cross-lagged path from Time 1 buoyancy to Time 2 adaptability was β = .07 (p < .01), and the cross-lagged path from Time 1 adaptability to Time 2 buoyancy was β = .07 (p < .01)—both medium cross-lagged effect sizes (Orth et al., 2024)."
