# Neither the classroom accountability component nor volunteer rate was significantly associated with student exam scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a regression model of 946 students, neither the accountability component (p = 0.49) nor percent volunteer rate (p = 0.061) significantly predicted exam points. [→ Becker Erin A. 2017](#becker-erin-a-2017)

## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple linear regression of overall course exam points on PCA-derived classroom-practice components plus GPA, gender, and first-generation status for 946 students (95.5% of enrolled); "neither of these two components was significantly correlated with student learning outcomes"; no effect size is printed.

> "Although neither of these two components was significantly correlated with student learning outcomes at the p < 0.05 confidence level (Table 6), the indication of a possible negative trend between student exam scores and volunteer rates warrants further research."
