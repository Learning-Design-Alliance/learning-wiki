## Evidence

### Lucieer Susanna M. 2016

Lucieer Susanna M., van der Geest Jos N., Elói-Santos Silvana M., de Faria Rosa M. Delbone, Jonker Laura, Visscher Chris, Rikers Remy M. J. P., Themmen Axel P. N. (2016). The development of self-regulated learning during the pre-clinical stage of medical school: a comparison between a lecture-based and a problem-based curriculum. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21, 93-104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9613-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Baseline comparison of LB-2 (n=111) and PBL-2 (n=66) in Table 5 shows effort F = 12.112, p = .001, "η2 = .065" (medium by the article's thresholds), means 24.2 vs 26.3; all other subscales were not significant. The limitations section cites this comparability to defend the cross-sectional design.

> "In addition, except for the skill effort, the students’ self-regulated learning skills were comparable in the 2nd semester, which indicates that the level of self-regulated learning of the second semester students was comparable."
