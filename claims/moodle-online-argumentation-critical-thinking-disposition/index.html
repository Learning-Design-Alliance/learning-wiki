Evidence¶

Fettahlıoğlu 2018¶

Fettahlıoğlu, P., & Kaleci, D. (2018). Online Argumentation Implementation in the Development of Critical Thinking Disposition. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i3.2904

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test non-equivalent control group study with 22 experimental and 21 control students; independent samples t-test on CCTDI scores. The article reports "a statistically significant difference was identified between their post-test points" favouring the experimental group, with no pre-test difference.

"When the pre -test points were analysed, while no statistically significant difference was identified between the experimental and control groups teacher candidates’ points [t(41)= .42 p>.05], a statistically significant difference was identified between their post-test points[t(41)= 2.69 p<.05] in favour of the experimental group."
