### Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman 2021

Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman. (2021). Development of a Conceptual Framework for Knowledge Integration in Learning Newton's Third Law. The Ohio State University, Department of Physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article's review of documented student difficulties states many students choose any pair of forces equal in magnitude and opposite in direction and call them the interaction pair, illustrating a pathway connecting surface features to a memorized rule without distinction.

> "In situations such as a box on a horizontal surface, this rule is often applied by students to the weight and the normal force exerted on the box. This type of understanding reveals a fragmented knowledge structure relying on memorized rules."
