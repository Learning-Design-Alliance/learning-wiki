Evidence¶

Wilson 2018¶

Wilson, K. E., Martinez, M., Mills, C., D'Mello, S., Smilek, D., & Risko, E. F. (2018). Instructor presence effect: Liking does not always lead to learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.011

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

The combined analysis pooled z-score standardized comprehension and MW data from Experiments 1 and 2, including only audio and audio+instructor conditions (N=316). The article reports "a significant comprehension cost for the lecture containing the instructor" with η2 = .02.

"Analysis of the standardized scores revealed a significant comprehension cost for the lecture containing the instructor, audio+instructor, relative to the audio only lecture, F(1, 315) = 6.80, p = 0.01, η2 = .02."
