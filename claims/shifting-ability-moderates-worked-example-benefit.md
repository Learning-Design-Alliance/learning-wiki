# The benefit of worked examples over problem-solving increases with decreasing shifting ability, reproducing Schwaighofer et al. (2016)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The direct effect of worked examples on learning gains was moderated by shifting ability (b = 0.005, p = .002), with worked examples particularly beneficial for learners with lower shifting ability. [→ Bichler 2022](#bichler-2022)

## Evidence

### Bichler 2022

Bichler, S., Stadler, M., Bühner, M., Greiff, S., & Fischer, F. (2022). Learning to solve ill-defined statistics problems: does self-explanation quality mediate the worked example effect? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09579-4

`q3 · i?`

In the conditional process model with shifting as moderator (N = 74), the treatment × shifting interaction on learning gains was significant. Because higher shifting scores indicate lower ability, the article concludes the benefit of worked examples over problem-solving increases with decreasing shifting ability, reproducing Schwaighofer et al. (2016).

> "However, the direct effect was moderated by shifting ability b = 0.005, pone-tailed = .002, 95%  CIboot [0.001, 0.01]."
