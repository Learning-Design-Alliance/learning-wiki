Meszaros Mariana de Jesus; de Almeida Angélica Olivetto; da Silva Ruana Luiz Ferreira; Eduardo Aline Helena Appoloni; Lima Maria Helena de Melo; Oliveira-Kumakura Ana Railka de Souza. (2023). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot testing with eight nurses using the 20-item Simulation Design Scale covering five factors (objectives/information, support, problem solving, feedback/reflection, realism); descriptive means and SDs only, with Scenario 1 at "a total score of 4.73 (SD=0.07)". No inferential test reported.

> "Scenario 1 presented a total score of 4.73 (SD=0.07) in relation to the degree of agreement and 4.81 (SD=0.08) in relation to the degree of importance of the elements relevant to the design of the simulation; and Scenario 2 obtained a total score of 4.80 (SD=0.05) and 4.86 (SD=0.08), respectively"
