Results section of the user study with 14 instructors (ages 18-54), each completing two tasks: building a tutor interface and training an expert model via demonstrations, feedback, and labels. The article reports that "All 14 participants were able to fully recreate both tutors" and 13 of 14 authored solving models.

> "All 14 participants were able to fully recreate both tutors using the tutor builder and 13 of out 14 were able to author expert models that could solve both tasks."
