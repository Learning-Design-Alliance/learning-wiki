## Evidence

### Pulfrey 2011

Pulfrey, C., Buchs, C., & Butera, F. (2011). Why grades engender performance-avoidance goals: The mediating role of autonomous motivation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(3), 683–700. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field experiment in a Swiss professional school: 115 EFL students in eight classes randomly assigned by class to graded versus nongraded listening comprehension, analyzed with a mixed-effects model controlling for class. The grade contrast was significant (p < .05), "with students reporting higher levels of performance-avoidance goals in the grade condition" (M = 5.27) than the no-grade condition (M = 4.62).
