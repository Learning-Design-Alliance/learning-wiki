`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup LMM restricted to introductory lower-division STEM courses, using the same model as the primary analysis. The article reports the Q2 association significant and the Q3 association "not significant (2.19 points; p = 0.0899)", unlike the overall analysis.

> "a medium level of learner-centered activity (Q2 of PC2) was correlated with higher exam scores (3.24 points; p < 0.001). However, the association between a medium-high level of learner-centered activity (Q3 of PC2) and exam scores was not significant (2.19 points; p = 0.0899)"
