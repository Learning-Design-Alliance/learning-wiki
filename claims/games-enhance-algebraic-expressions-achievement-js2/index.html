Okpube Nnaemeka Michael 2016¶

Okpube Nnaemeka Michael, Anugwo M. N. (2016). Card Games and Algebra Tic Tacmatics on Achievement of Junior Secondary II Students in Algebraic Expressions. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test, post-test, non-randomized control group study of 240 JS2 students (136 experimental, 104 control) in Ebonyi State, Nigeria; ANCOVA with pretest as covariate rejected the null hypothesis. The experimental group's adjusted mean was 72.51 (SD 10.52) versus 54.08 (SD 7.04) for controls, showing "significant difference in achievement of students between the experimental group and the control group".
