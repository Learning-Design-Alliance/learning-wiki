## Evidence

### İlter İlhan 2025

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

PLS-SEM structural model of 363 sixth-graders; the self-efficacy path to mathematics performance was significant (β = 0.252, p < 0.001), supporting H3. Only path coefficients are printed.

> "Both ASE (β = 0.252, p < 0.001) and AB (β = 0.334, p < 0.000) had significant positive effects on MP, providing support for hypotheses H3 and H4."
