Evidence¶

Wang 2026¶

Wang, P., Ganushchak, L., Welie, C., & van Steensel, R. (2026). Learning proximal cyclic sighing buffers anxiety across learning moments and boredom across days. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00446-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bayesian multilevel phase-contrast analysis of EMA boredom ratings from the 20-day RCT (n = 84). The breathwork group's boredom stayed near baseline (β = 0.02 intervention; β = 0.07 post); the intervention-phase within-day between-group difference was not credible (β = 0.05).

"In the control group, boredom rose from Baseline during the Intervention phase (β = 0.16, 95% HPD [0.02, 0.31]) and remained higher in the Post phase (β = 0.11, 95% HPD [−0.07, 0.28])."
