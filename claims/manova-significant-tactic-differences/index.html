Yizhou Fan 2021¶

Yizhou Fan, Wannisa Matcha, Nora’ayu Ahmad Uzir, Qiong Wang, Dragan Gašević. (2021). Learning Analytics to Reveal Links Between Learning Design and Self-Regulated Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 31, 980–1021. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00249-z

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

One-way MANOVA with learning tactics as the independent variable and nine learning actions as dependent variables, using Pillai's trace due to violated homogeneity of covariances. "The multivariate effect size was estimated at multivariate η2 = .55", and all nine follow-up ANOVAs with Bonferroni corrections were statistically significant.

"A statistically significant MANOVA effect was obtained, Pillai’s Trace = 1.65, F(27, 603081) = 27326.18, p = 0.0000. The multivariate effect size was estimated at multivariate η2 = .55."
