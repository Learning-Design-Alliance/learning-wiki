## Evidence

### Anton 2026

Anton, J., Cosentino, G., Gelsomini, M., Sharma, K., Giannakos, M. N., & Abrahamson, D. (2026). Towards minding the disability achievement gap: the case of technology-enhanced inclusive embodied design for integer arithmetic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10704-0

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

A two-way mixed ANOVA on pre/post-test scores of 30 middle-school students found that "Achievement scores increased significantly from pre-test to post-test for children with disa-bilities" with d = 1.44, but not for children without disabilities (d = 0.10).

> "Achievement scores increased significantly from pre-test to post-test for children with disa-bilities (t(28) = − 3.06, p =.0048, d = 1.44, mean gain = 1.33 points, 95% CI [0.44, 2.23]) but not for children without disabilities (t(28) = −0.33, p =.7409, d = 0.10, mean gain = 0.10 points, 95% CI [− 0.49, 0.68]"
