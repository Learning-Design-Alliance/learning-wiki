## Evidence

### Kawabata Masato 2021

Kawabata Masato, Lee Kerry, Choo Hui-Cheng, Burns Stephen F. (2021). Breakfast and Exercise Improve Academic and Cognitive Performance in Adolescents. Nutrients 13(4). https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13041278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fingertip blood glucose measured in 57 of the 82 participants at start and end of morning; breakfast groups rose while fasted groups declined significantly (both p < 0.001).

> "Compared with the B-E group, blood glucose fell over the morning in the F-S and F-E groups (F-S: β11 = −1.45, p < 0.001; F-E: β13 = −1.28, p < 0.001). Mean blood glucose was ≥ 1.19 mmol∙L−1 greater in both breakfast groups at the end of the morning than the two fasted groups."
