Evidence¶

Kalaian 2018¶

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

q3 · i2

Subgroup analysis of publication type (Table 1) across the 26 effect sizes: published articles (18, d = 0.58, p = 0.00) versus theses and dissertations (8, d = 0.05, p = 0.62).

"18 effect sizes were extracted from articles published in peer-reviewed journals with a significant and positive d-value of 0.58. This average effect size was much larger than the average effect size of the remaining eight effect sizes that were extracted from PhD dissertations with a nonsignificant d-value of 0.05"

Kalaian 2018 (2)¶

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

q3 · i2

Subgroup analysis of research design (Table 1): quasiexperimental two-group pre-post studies (10 effect sizes, d = 0.52, p = 0.00) versus nonexperimental comparative post-only studies (16, d = 0.40, p = 0.00).

"the quasiexperimental studies (two-group pre–post research design) produced much larger effect sizes with a significant d-value of 0.52 than the nonexperimental (comparative post-only) studies with a significant d-value of 0.40"
