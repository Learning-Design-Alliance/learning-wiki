## Evidence

### Konstantopoulos 2015

Konstantopoulos, S., Li, W., Miller, S., & van der Ploeg, A. (2015). Effects of Interim Assessments on the Achievement Gap: Evidence from an Experiment. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562166

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade 3-8 quantile regression analysis of ISTEP+ mathematics scores from the cluster-randomized trial. The article reports significant positive effects across quantiles that "appear to be uniform across the mathematics achievement distribution," with lower-tail estimates "nearly one fifth of a SD."

> "However, the estimates produced by grade 3-8 and 3-6 analyses point to significant positive effects in mathematics across quantiles. Treatment effects in these grades appear to be uniform across the mathematics achievement distribution."
