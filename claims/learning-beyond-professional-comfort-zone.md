Findings from thematic analysis of focus group discussions (n = 25) in the ACoRD project; the discussion interprets the FGD data, noting providers' technologists "gained tacit knowledge in teaching and research" while training receivers. Qualitative, no effect size printed.

> "In our study, it is interesting to find out that there was bidirectional knowledge transfer where technologists from the provider team gained tacit knowledge in teaching and research via the experience of training the receivers and being involved in research activities."
