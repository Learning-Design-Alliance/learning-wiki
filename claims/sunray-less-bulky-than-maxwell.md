# Sunray diagrams are less bulky than Maxwell's parallelogram sliding while deriving the same identities

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The two graphical languages are equally capable of deriving partial derivative identities, but sunray diagrams use vector sliding while Maxwell's diagrams use two-form sliding, making the sunray derivation considerably less bulky. [→ Kim 2021](#kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Kim 2021

Kim, J.-H. and Nam, J. (2021). Thermodynamic identities with sunray diagrams. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/abce1e](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/abce1e)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' comparative assessment in the conclusion, based on their worked derivations: vectors make addition and decomposition trivially easy to draw, whereas translating Maxwell's diagrams involves the reciprocal-scaling intricacies of differential forms.

> "As a result, the sunray diagram is considerably less bulky than Maxwell's parallelogram sliding."
