## Evidence

### Greenberg AL 2022

Greenberg AL, Syed SM, Alseidi A, O'Sullivan PS, Chern H. (2022). Robotic training for medical students: feasibility of a pilot simulation curriculum. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01508-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility study at one institution using a loaned da Vinci Xi trainer for three capped sessions of six students each. Implementation was assessed by accessibility, resources, and student sign-up and completion: "Students could sign-up for the course and complete each of the course components." No effect size is printed.

> "Though dependent on the infrastructure of and excess capacity from the existing resident robotic training, the loaner robot was accessible for the three medical student sessions."
