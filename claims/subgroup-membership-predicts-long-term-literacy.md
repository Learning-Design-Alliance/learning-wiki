## Evidence

### Tan 2026

Tan, A.S.-C., & Ali, F. (2026). Uncovering subgroup heterogeneity in dyslexia intervention outcomes using explainable machine learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00429-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal follow-up of 41 recruited participants; Responder Subgroup 2 and Non-Responder Subgroup 4 were omitted due to n = 1. Follow-up duration (T1-T2) averaged 68.2 months. Retained participants did not differ significantly from non-participants (p > 0.05; χ²(5) = 7.45, p = .19).

> "the subgroups, based solely on the collection of predictor importance (not actual predictor values directly), were also able to demonstrate subsequent predictive potential of the longitudinal outcomes for Responder Subgroup 1, Non-responder Subgroup 1, 2 and 3."
