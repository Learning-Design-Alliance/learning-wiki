Translation, cross-cultural adaptation, and validation of the Palliative Care Knowledge Questionnaire for PEACE (PEACE-Q) in Brazilian Portuguese. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test-retest validation study: 102 residents completed the questionnaire and 39 were excluded for nonresponse to the retest, leaving a final sample of 63. The article reports "The ICC was 0.71 (95%CI= [0.51-0.82])" and KR-20 of 0.60, which the authors judge satisfactory against a >0.70 ICC benchmark.

> "The ICC was 0.71 (95%CI= [0.51-0.82]), and the instrument's internal consistency (KR-20) was 0.60 (95%CI= [0.54-0.66])."
