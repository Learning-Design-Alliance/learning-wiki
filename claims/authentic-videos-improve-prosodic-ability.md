## Evidence

### Namaziandost 2018

Namaziandost, E., Esfahani, F. R., & Hashemifarnia, A. (2018). The Impact of Using Authentic Videos on Prosodic Ability among Foreign Language Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 375-390. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 40 Iranian intermediate EFL learners (experimental n=20, control n=18/20 as printed) taught over 12 sessions; independent samples t-test on post-test totals gave t = 2.495, p = .017, mean difference 9.50000, favouring the video group — "a significant difference is observed". No effect size is printed.
