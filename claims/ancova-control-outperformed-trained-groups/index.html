Evidence¶

Benson et al. 2013¶

Benson, S., Fischer, D., Geluso, J., & Von Joo, L. (2013). Effects of communication strategy training on EFL students' performance in small-group discussions. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 3(2), 245-259. doi:10.14746/ssllt.2013.3.2.5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA with pretest scores as covariate and posttest scores as response variable, on 76 first-year Japanese EFL students in three intact-class-derived groups. Table 4 prints pairwise p-values: control vs explicit 0.772, explicit vs co-constructed 0.030, control vs co-constructed 0.018; no effect size is reported.

"As Table 4 illustrates, despite the more frequent employment of the formulaic sequences representative of the conversation strategies in experimental groups as shown in Table 2, the control group outperformed both experimental groups. The difference between the control group and Experimental Group 1, however, did not reach levels of significance."
