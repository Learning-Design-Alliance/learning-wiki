`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

23-item Course Experience Questionnaire administered after the final exam across five subscales. The article reports a significant difference in Appropriate Assessment only, with no printed effect size.

> "The CBL group perceived significantly more appropriate assessment in the course (p=0.02); all other subscales were comparable between the CBL and non-CBL groups, although there was a statistical trend (p=0.08) for students in the CBL group to perceive good teaching lower."
