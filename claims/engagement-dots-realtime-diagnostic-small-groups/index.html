Coach self-reports and focus group data from the implementation study. The Coursemojo engagement dots (blue, yellow, orange, red) are described as a vital diagnostic support, letting teachers see students "really struggling with close reading."

"A southern district coach shared her "enjoyment of access to real-time data" through these "color-coded bubbles/circles," noting that a significant benefit is that it allows for scaffolding and "small group pulls" in the moment."
