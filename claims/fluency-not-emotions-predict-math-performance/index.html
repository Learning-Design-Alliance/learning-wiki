Evidence¶

Rawlings 2023¶

Rawlings, A. M., Niemivirta, M., Korhonen, J., Lindskog, M., Tuominen, H., & Mononen, R. (2023). Achievement emotions and arithmetic fluency – Development and parallel processes during the early school years. Preprint Version 3, Accepted for publication, 27 March 2023. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101776

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Final latent growth curve model predicting curriculum-based mathematics test scores (49 items) at t4, controlling for gender, in the 232-student sample. The article reports "the initial level (β = .44, p = .003) and slope (β = .37, p < .001) of arithmetic fluency" as the only predictors; β values are unstandardised-style printed coefficients, no effect size given.

"The only predictors of mathematics performance were the initial level (β = .44, p = .003) and slope (β = .37, p < .001) of arithmetic fluency."
