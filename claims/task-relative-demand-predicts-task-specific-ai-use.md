## Subclaims
`q2 i1` A focal task one point above the same teacher's mean demand has higher odds of corresponding AI use (OR = 1.163). [→ Holster 2026](#holster-2026)

## Evidence

### Holster 2026

Holster, J. (2026). Where Artificial Intelligence Enters Teacher Work. https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/talis-2024-database.html [doi:10.35542/osf.io/a39rp_v1](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/a39rp_v1)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Survey-weighted within-teacher (person-mean centered) allocation model on 24,058 AI users across 46 systems, one row per teacher-by-task. The article reports "OR = 1.163, 95% CI [1.127, 1.200], p < .001".

> "A focal task one point above the same teacher’s mean demand was associated with higher odds of corresponding AI use than a task at that teacher’s mean (OR = 1.163, 95% CI [1.127, 1.200], p < .001)."

### Holster 2026 (2)

Holster, J. (2026). Where Artificial Intelligence Enters Teacher Work. https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/talis-2024-database.html. [doi:10.35542/osf.io/a39rp_v1](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/a39rp_v1)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-teacher term from the same allocation model among AI users. The article reports "OR = 1.032 (95% CI [0.995, 1.070], p = .090)", which does not reach significance; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "A one-point increase in average focal-task demand was associated with OR = 1.032 (95% CI [0.995, 1.070], p = .090) for reporting AI use on a focal task."
