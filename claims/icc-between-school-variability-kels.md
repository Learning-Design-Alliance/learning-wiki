`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Unconditional (null) HLM models on the KELS 6th-grade dataset (7,065 students, 446 schools). The article reports ICCs of 0.143 (literacy) and .195 (math); ICCs are variance proportions, not effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Regarding literacy achievement, the Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC) was 0.143; thus, about 14% of the variability in literacy achievement was between schools. In addition, regarding math achievement, the ICC was .195 or about 19% of the variability in math achievement between schools."
