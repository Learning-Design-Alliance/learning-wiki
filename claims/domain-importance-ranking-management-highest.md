Descriptive analysis of domain scores in the cross-sectional survey of 2,625 physicians rating 74 competencies on a 1-7 scale. The article reports "Physicians rated the management domain as the highest (with a median of 106)"; communication gained the least importance (median 40).

> "Physicians rated the management domain as the highest (with a median of 106), followed by the domain of essential competencies (with a median of 90) and the public health domain (with a median of 80)."
