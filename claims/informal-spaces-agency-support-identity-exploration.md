### Fracchiolla 2020

Fracchiolla, C., Prefontaine, B., and Hinko, K. (2020). Community of Practice: A framework for understanding identity development within informal physics programs. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020115](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020115)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theoretical argument in the introduction: informal physics spaces allow voluntary engagement and elements of agency and autonomy, so an identity can form outside constraints traditionally found in academic settings. The article offers this as a reasoned framing for the study rather than a tested result; participants can opt in or out and have a say in content and engagement.

> "Informal learning (sometimes referred to as “free-choice” learning) is characterized by learner agency and is often learner-centered."
