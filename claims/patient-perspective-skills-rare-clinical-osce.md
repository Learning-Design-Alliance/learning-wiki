## Evidence

### Dewi Sari Puspa 2024

Dewi Sari Puspa, Amanda Wilson, Robbert Duvivier, Brian Kelly, Conor Gilligan. (2024). Do the teaching, practice and assessment of clinical communication skills align? BMC Medical Education 24:609. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05596-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational ratings of the CCOG micro-skills for understanding the patient's perspective showed a declining gradient across settings; in the OSCE "only one of sixteen students performed well in this area". In clinical observations these micro-skills appeared in only three of seventeen encounters.

> "In the OSCE, only one of sixteen students performed well in this area (average scores were 1.5, 0.8 and 0.7, respectively in classroom, clinical settings and OSCE)."
