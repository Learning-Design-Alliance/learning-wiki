## Subclaims
`q3 i?` From week 4 to 8 of training, proper neck extension increased 13.5 percentage points, PPV with visible chest rise increased 19.0 percentage points, and heart rate assessment increased 11.6 percentage points (all significant). [→ Vail Brennan 2018](#vail-brennan-2018)

## Evidence

### Vail Brennan 2018

Vail Brennan, Morgan Melissa C., Spindler Hilary, Christmas Amelia, Cohen Susanna R., Walker Dilys M. (2018). The power of practice: simulation training improving the quality of neonatal resuscitation skills in Bihar, India. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1254-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 113 PHC-matched week 4 and 8 video-recorded assessment simulations using McNemar's Test for paired proportions among nurse mentees; the study reports "a 13.5 percentage-point increase in proper neck extension" and the other gains listed.

> "From week 4 to 8 of training, there was a 13.5 percentage-point increase in proper neck extension (p = 0.01), a 19.0 percentage-point increase in PPV with visible chest rise (p < 0.01), and an 11.6 percentage-point increase in assessment of heart rate during resuscitations (p < 0.01)."
