## Evidence

### Manjareeka Magna 2025

Manjareeka Magna, Dasgupta Subhronil, Kanungo Pratyush, Das Rama Chandra. (2025). Perceived stress and academic achievement among medical students with different chronotypes: a cross sectional study on first year medical students from India. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07281-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 148 first-year medical students; chi-square test of chronotype (MSFsc cutoff 4.28) against performance category. The article reports "χ² = 6.96, p =.03", with 49.1% of Early Larks versus 29.0% of Night Owls in the Excellent group.

> "An association was also found between chronotype and academic performance (χ² = 6.96, p =.03). A larger percentage of Average performers were Evening chronotypes (38.7%) compared to Excellent performers (21.8%). The distribution of Excellent performers was notably higher in the Early Lark group (49.1%) than among Night Owls (29.0%)."
