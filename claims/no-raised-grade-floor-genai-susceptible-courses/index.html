Evidence¶

Dumlao 2026¶

Dumlao, J. M. Z., Wang, M., Xie, Z., Hu, J., Bar, I., Chaney, G., III, Gold, H., & Teplitskiy, M. (2026). Generative AI Availability, Grades, and Student Satisfaction at a Large University. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21534

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Grade-distribution DiD analysis estimating the preferred specification on letter-grade threshold indicators. Both bottom-end outcomes "are both null under both bounds" and pass the parallel pre-trends test (COVID-excluded p = 0.458 and 0.255), which the authors call a credibly identified null.

"The bottom of the distribution is cleaner. The probability of clearing the passing margin (at least a D) and the probability of a failing grade or withdrawal are both null under both bounds."
