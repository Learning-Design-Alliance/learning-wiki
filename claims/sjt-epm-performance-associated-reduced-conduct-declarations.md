## Evidence

### Paton Lewis W. 2018

Paton Lewis W., Tiffin Paul A., Smith Daniel, Dowell Jon S., Mwandigha Lazaro M. (2018). Predictors of fitness to practise declarations in UK medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1167-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariable logistic regression from the national UKMED cohort; the results section reports these protective associations for conduct-related declarations. The authors argue the SJT "can be thought of as a measure of knowledge of professional behaviour".

> "EPM ranking (OR 0.65), performance on the foundation programme SJT (OR 0.82), higher scores in abstract reasoning (OR 0.91), and being non-white (OR 0.77) were all associated with lower odds of declaring a conduct-related issue."
