Mann–Whitney U comparison of Likert responses between the 2021 (N=69) and 2022 (N=54) cohorts. The 2022 group consistently rated the 3D IMs more positively: "Q1—visualising designs: 2022 cohort mean rank = 78.65; 2021 = 40.72 (p < 0.001, Cohen's ds = 1.584; very large effect)"; Q2 ds = 1.370; Q3 ds = 0.775.
