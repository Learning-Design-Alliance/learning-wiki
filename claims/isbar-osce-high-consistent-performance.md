## Evidence

### Sun Jui-Chiung 2026

Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention OSCE performance of 57 nursing students across two stations, each scored to a maximum of 30 points converted to a percentage scale. Mean scores were high with low variability across both scenarios.

> "Antenatal assessment and ISBAR handover: mean score = 87.75, SD = 5.23; normal spontaneous delivery postpartum assessment and ISBAR handover: mean score = 87.26, SD = 5.24."
