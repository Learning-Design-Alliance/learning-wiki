Kurt Squire 2023¶

Kurt Squire, Garrison Wells, Maria J. Anderson-Coto, and Constance Steinkuehler. 2023. Casual Games, Cognition, and Play across the Lifespan: A Critical Synthesis. ACM Games: Research and Practice, Volume 1, Issue 2, Article 14. https://doi.org/10.1145/3594534

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of Pokémon Go research reports that eleven studies found improved social well-being, belonging, or connection, with better peer connections than non-players, and decreased anxieties about leaving the house and interacting with strangers. No effect size is printed.
