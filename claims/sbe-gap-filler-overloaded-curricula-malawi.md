`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In-depth interviews with lecturers and clinical instructors in an exploratory descriptive qualitative study; a senior lecturer explained that "we can cover so many topics with just one scenario" and that hours in simulation centres would still be counted by the regulatory council.

> "Simulation could be ideal in lightening the pressure of our overloaded curricula because we can cover so many topics with just one scenario."
