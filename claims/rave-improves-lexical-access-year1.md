## Evidence

### McKeown 2018

McKeown, M. G., Crosson, A. C., Moore, D. W., & Beck, I. L. (2018). Word Knowledge and Comprehension Effects of an Academic Vocabulary Intervention for Middle School Students. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831217744181

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Computer-based lexical decision task with sixth graders comparing RAVE words to frequency- and complexity-matched words. The group-by-word-type interaction was significant, F(1, 97) = 7.35, p = .008, and the RAVE group was faster than controls on RAVE words, t(97) = 2.75, p = .04.

> "Students in the RA VE group had sig- nificantly faster reaction times for RA VE compared to matched words, t(97) = 5.06, p \ .0001, while the control group did not, t(97) = .44, p = .97 (see Figure 1)."
