## Evidence

### Daniel Dziob 2015

Daniel Dziob, Łukasz Kwiatkowski, Dagmara Sokołowska. (2015). Studying the impact of the class tournament as an assessment method on student achievements in physics courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bivariate linear regression on the n = 28 sample (Table IV): FT coefficient 0.5878 (p = 0.0078), TNT coefficient 0.6750 (p = 0.0378), R2 = 0.6386. The authors note about 64% of control-test results is explained by former tests and tournament performance.

> "if a student scored higher in FT (likewise, TNT) by 1 pp, then he/she would score also higher in CT by ca. 0.59 pp (0.68, respectively). The results are (positively) significant at  equal 0.01 and 0.05, correspondingly."
