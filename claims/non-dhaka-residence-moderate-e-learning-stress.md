## Evidence

### Kabir Humayun 2021

Kabir Humayun, Hasan Md. Kamrul, Mitra Dipak Kumar. (2021). E-learning readiness and perceived stress among the university students of Bangladesh during COVID-19: a countrywide cross-sectional study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2021.2009908

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multinomial logistic regression results from the 1145-student survey; "moderate (AOR = 1.87, 95% CI: 1.07–3.28)" was significant, while the high-stress interval (0.99–3.71) crossed 1. No standardised effect size was printed. The authors cite poor e-learning infrastructure outside Dhaka as a possible stressor.

> "Students from other than Dhaka had a 87% higher chance of reporting moderate (AOR = 1.87, 95% CI: 1.07–3.28) and a 92% higher chance of reporting high-level of e-learning stress (AOR = 1.92, 95% CI: 0.99–3.71) compared to the student from Dhaka."
