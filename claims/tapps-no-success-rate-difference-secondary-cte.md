## Evidence

### Pate and Miller 2011

Michael L. Pate, Greg Miller. (2011). Effects of Think–Aloud Pair Problem Solving on Secondary–Level Students’ Performance in Career and Technical Education Courses. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2011.01120

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized posttest-only control group experiment with 34 secondary students across five Iowa schools; seven of 18 silent students and four of 16 TAPPS students succeeded. The article reports "no significant difference in success rate" with a "weak" association (ϕ = .14).

> "There was no significant difference in success rate between TAPPS students and students in the control group ( χ2 (1) = .747, p = .39)."
