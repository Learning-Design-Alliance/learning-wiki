## Evidence

### Abdulghani 2015

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of flawed items in 240 pre- versus 240 post-training MCQs across the three courses. The article reports flaws "reduced significantly (n 55)" with χ²=38.04, p<0.0001; Table 2 shows 19.6% versus 2.1%.

> "The MCQs with item writing flaws (IWFs) were around ten times more in numbers (n 547) before workshop training program and reduced significantly (n 55) after the training program (x2538.04, p ,0.0001)."

### Abdulghani 2015 (2)

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-course ANOVA (Table 3) for item writing flaws: respiratory F=25.711, p=0.000; cardiovascular F=18.231, p=0.000; renal F=3.833, p=0.050. The article states flaws "have significant variation between pre- and post-training in all three block examination".

> "Similarly, item writing flaws also have significant variation between pre- and post-training in all three block examina- tion (Table 3)."
