## Evidence

### Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022

Kashiwabara Tomoka, Fujiwara Takeo, Doi Satomi, Yamaoka Yui. (2022). Association between Hope for the Future and Academic Performance in Adolescents: Results from the K-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191911890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional logistic regression on PS-matched K-CHILD pairs for dichotomized game time (≥1 h/day vs less), with alpha = 0.05/6 = 0.0083 applied across six outcomes.

> "The association between hope for the future and time playing games was marginal: those who have hope for the future were 19% less likely to play games for 1 h or more per day (OR = 0.81, 95% CI = 0.67 to 0.98), which was not statistically significant after the Bonferroni correction."
