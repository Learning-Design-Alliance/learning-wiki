### Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh 2026

Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). What does it mean to think like a physicist? Insights from physics graduate students. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae4e70](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae4e70)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with seven physics PhD students at one U.S. public research university, analyzed via phenomenographic structural coding. A particle-theory participant stated that "Math is more of a tool to get you to the final answer," with concepts considered first; four participants emphasized conceptual understanding as central.

> "When you're trying to think like a physicist, you have to really think about the concepts of the scenario before you can really apply any math to it. Math is more of a tool to get you to the final answer"
