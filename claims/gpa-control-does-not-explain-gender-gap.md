## Subclaims

## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul 2023

David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul. (2023). Attributing equity gaps to course structure in introductory physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

HLM fit of Equation 8 with incoming GPA as a control (b_GPA = 1.108, z = 89.12) found b_Female*Retake = 0.174, z = 3.02, P = 0.003, so the retake classes remained about 3.02 standard errors above the background set by the regular classes. The authors conclude a Student Deficit model is of no use in understanding these differences.
