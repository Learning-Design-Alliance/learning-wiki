Evidence¶

Olive Emil Wetter 2009¶

Olive Emil Wetter, Fritz Koerner, Adrian Schwaninger. (2009). Does musical training improve school performance? Instr Sci 37:365–374. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9052-y

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Grade-wise analysis (Fig. 3) of overall average marks across grades 3–6; from fourth grade on the music group achieved significantly better marks than controls; the authors read this as evidence that duration of musical training is relevant.

"By contrast, the respective values in control group 2 differed signiﬁcantly (5.33 in grade 3 and 4.80 in grade 6 with t(35) = 3.90, p\ .001) as illustrated in Fig. 3."
