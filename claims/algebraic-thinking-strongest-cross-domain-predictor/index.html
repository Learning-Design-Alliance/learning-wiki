Cross-lagged panel model path coefficients (Table 3) show AL→NO (.25) and NO→AL (.25) as the largest cross-lagged lag-1 paths; Table 4 shows AL→OV (.21) and NO→OV (.21) as the best predictors of overall Spring Grade 2 performance, all p < .001.

"Overall, AL could be considered as the strongest predictor of the subsequent domain-level performance, followed by NO."

In Table 3, the AL→GE lag-1 path (.25 at F1→W1) was comparable in size to the GE→GE autoregressive path (.24), an exception to the general dominance of autoregressive paths for Geometry.

"It was noticeable that AL’s contribution to predicting student performance on GE was comparable to GE’s."
