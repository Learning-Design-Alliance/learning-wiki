`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of FGDs and IDIs in Badin; participants reported no antibiotic supply for five years and said medicine availability strengthens community credibility: "you don’t give us medicines; you pocket them yourselves".

> "Lack of training, delayed salaries and insufficient medicines and other supplies were identified as major factors impeding appropriate knowledge and skill development for iCCM of childhood diarrhoea and pneumonia."
