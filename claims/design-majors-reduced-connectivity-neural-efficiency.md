## Evidence

### Jing Teng 2025

Jing Teng, Xinuo Qiao, Kelong Lu, Tuo Liu, Xinyue Wang, Zhenni Gao, Tingting Yu & Ning Hao (2025). Neural mechanisms underpinning the association between visual arts education and creativity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00388-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Weighted independent-samples t-tests on neural coupling (Pearson correlations between channels), FDR-corrected, in the matched sample. The quote illustrates the reduced-connectivity pattern in the design group; the article notes book cover effects were very small (d = 0.099 to 0.157) while product-task effects were larger (e.g., d = 0.336), and three negligible results (d about −0.03) were not emphasized.

> "For the book cover thinking phase I - second half stage (Fig. 6d), significant group differences were detected in NC values for CH10-CH61 (Right dorsolateral prefrontal lobe - Right primary motor cortex), t (75.62) = 0.44, pcorr = 0.008, Cohen's d = 0.099, which represents a very small effect and should be interpreted with caution"
