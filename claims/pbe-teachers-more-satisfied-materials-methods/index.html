Evidence¶

Finkelstein 2011¶

Finkelstein, N., Hanson, T., Huang, C.-W., Hirschman, B., and Huang, M. (2011). Effects of Problem Based Economics on high school economics instruction (NCEE 2010-4002rev). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED533126

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Teacher survey outcome in the randomized controlled trial; the two-item satisfaction scale was rated on a five-point scale and summed (range 2–10). Intervention teachers' model-adjusted mean of 8.35 exceeded the control mean of 6.88, "an effect size of 1.09".

"Statistically significant differences in favor of the intervention group teachers on a measure of satisfaction with the teaching materials and methods (model-adjusted means were 8.35 and 6.88 for the intervention and control group teachers, respectively; the difference of 1.47 corresponded to an effect size of 1.09)."
