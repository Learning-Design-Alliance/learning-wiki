## Evidence

### Al-Saaideh M 2015

Al-Saaideh M, Nasr-allah Al-Zyoud MS. (2015). Benefits of teaching interdisciplinary subjects collaboratively in Jordanian pre-vocational education. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2461

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test t-test on a 100-item achievement test (difficulty 0.26–0.79, discrimination 0.30–0.85). Table 2 reports means of 83 (experimental) vs 70 (control), SDs 14.00 and 10.25, t = 4.84, significance 0.001, in favor of "the experimental group that was taught by the team of teachers".

> "Results shows that there is a statistically significant difference between the mean scores of the experimental group that was taught by the team of teachers and the control group that was taught by the PVE teacher only."
