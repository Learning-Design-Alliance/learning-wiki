## Evidence

### Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019

Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen. (2019). Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analyses across all outcome measures found no effect of the PD program condition on student growth; for the VAA aspects the PD coefficients were non-significant (e.g. space t(94) = −1.02, p = 0.31). The authors suggest the elaborate manual left little added value for the PD.

> "However, the MACE PD program did not show such an effect (space, t(94) = − 1.02, p = 0.31; space suggestion, t(94) = 1.13, p = 0.26; composition, t(94) = − 0.30, p =0 . 7 6 )."
