## Evidence

### Fraj-Andrés E. 2018

Fraj-Andrés E., Lucia-Palacios L. & Pérez-López R. (2018). How extroversion affects student attitude toward the combined use of a wiki and video recording of group presentations. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.12.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (Preacher and Hayes, 5000 samples) found attitude fully mediates both paths; R2 of satisfaction increased when mediation was included. H4a and H5a supported, H4b and H5b not.

> "The results show a total mediation effect of attitude. Attitude acts as a mediator between relative advantages and satisfaction, and between enjoyment and satisfaction."
