# Inter-trial variability decay showed reward-like signatures in the error-feedback condition, especially when learning was matched across conditions (Round 2)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The error-feedback condition showed a positive decay in inter-trial variability (a marker traditionally associated with reward-based learning), significantly different from the reward condition overall and in Round 2, but not in Round 1. [→ Federico Nardi 2025](#federico-nardi-2025)

## Evidence

### Federico Nardi 2025

Federico Nardi, A. Aldo Faisal & Shlomi Haar. (2025). Motor learning mechanisms are not modified by feedback manipulations in a real-world task. npj Science of Learning 10:73. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00373-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon tests on inter-trial variability decay (corrected variability difference between first and last rotation blocks) in the EVR billiards task, N = 32. Error feedback showed a positive decay ("W = 142, p = 0.02"), the reward condition showed the opposite trend, and the condition difference was significant (W = 97, p = 0.001).

> "We observed as well a positive decay in variability when providing error feedback (W = 142, p = 0.02), while the opposite trend was displayed for the reward feedback condition (W = 122, p < 0.001). Finally, we obtained a significant difference in this metric between the two conditions (W = 97,p = 0.001, Fig. 4b)"
