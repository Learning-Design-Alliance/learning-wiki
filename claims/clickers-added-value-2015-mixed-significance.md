## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Added Values in 2015 were higher for clickers-preferring students (median 2.22) than handwritten-homework students (median −7.67); the u-test difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.2018) while the t-test was significant at α = 0.1 (p = 0.08499).
