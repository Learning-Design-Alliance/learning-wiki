## Evidence

### Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle 2014

Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle. (2014). More network science for teenagers. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Timing observation from the University workshop sessions: the epidemic-spreading topic was never reached in one hour, and the fast pace made the material demanding for some groups; the authors say a decision on selectivity must be made beforehand.

> "In the University sessions we were not able to get to the Epidemic spreading part in 1 hour, and that going a little bit too fast through the material, which made following it a little bit demanding for some of the groups."

### Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle 2014 (2)

Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle. (2014). More network science for teenagers. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Both presenters independently decided to skip the configuration model in every session because they lacked good examples and time; the authors state "A good pre-worked example of that would be needed to enter that topic".

> "did not have nice examples for it, it was always something a bit com- plicated and given that we were running short of time, we skipped it"
