q3 · i? · associational · r?

The review reports this national longitudinal study of more than 36,000 7th- to 12th-grade students as central evidence that connectedness protects against violence and related risks.

"the National Longitudinal Study of Adolescent health, a study of health and well-being among more than 36,000 7th- to 12th-grade students, found that school connectedness proved to be the strongest protective factor to decrease school absenteeism, substance abuse, violence, and risk of unintentional injury"

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports this study on preventing school-based attacks as evidence that positive relationships improve communication of potential threats to adults.

"A study on preventing school-based attacks found that students who had prior knowledge of a potential threat were more likely to report that threat if they had positive relationships with one or more adults in the school and felt as though they would be taken seriously."
