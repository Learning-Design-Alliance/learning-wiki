q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports this study on preventing school-based attacks as evidence that positive relationships improve communication of potential threats to adults.

"A study on preventing school-based attacks found that students who had prior knowledge of a potential threat were more likely to report that threat if they had positive relationships with one or more adults in the school and felt as though they would be taken seriously."
