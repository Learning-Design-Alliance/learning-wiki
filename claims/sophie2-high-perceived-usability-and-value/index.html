Exit-survey Likert results from the 59 completers of the two-session self-service study. "92% of participants felt engaged"; the usability scale showed 86% found it easy to use and 85% believed it could be learned quickly.

"As shown in Figure 3a, 92% of participants felt engaged, 80% considered the scenarios appropriate for their clinical roles, and 83% reported that the virtual patients elicited skills used in real clinical communication."
