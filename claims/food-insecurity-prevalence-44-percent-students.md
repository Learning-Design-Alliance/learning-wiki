Prevalence synthesis across the 47 included studies in the systematic review; the review reports a weighted mean prevalence of 44.03% and that prevalence "consistently exceeded national estimates". Among USA studies only, the weighted mean was 46.42%.

> "Food insecurity prevalence among the study populations varied by institution but consistently exceeded national estimates, ranging from 8% to 72.3%, yielding an overall weighted mean prevalence of 44.03% across all studies."
