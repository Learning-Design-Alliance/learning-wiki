## Evidence

### Charlotte Zimmerman 2025

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2025). Assessing physics quantitative literacy development in algebra-based physics. [doi:10.1103/lnd6-pxyt](https://doi.org/10.1103/lnd6-pxyt)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Statistical validation of GERQN v3.0 administrations across the introductory sequence (PreMech N = 746, PostMech N = 573, PostEM) reports Cronbach's alpha of "0.67 ± 0.05", 0.69 ± 0.06, and 0.73 ± 0.07, Ferguson's δ of 0.95-0.96, and Pearson correlations of 0.95, 0.91, and 0.89.

> "In general, a Cronbach's α of 0.7 or above is recommended; each course meets this benchmark within a 95% confidence range."
