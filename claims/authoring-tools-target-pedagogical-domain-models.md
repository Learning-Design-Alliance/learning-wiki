### Diego Dermeval 2018

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

RQ1 categorisation of the 33 included studies against Woolf's four ITS components. The review reports the pedagogical model at "81.82%; 27 studies" and domain model at "75.75%; 25 studies", with student and interface models much lower; a study could meet more than one component.

> "Most of the papers use authoring tools to design the Pedagogical model of ITS (81.82%; 27 studies) and Domain model (75.75%; 25 studies), followed by Student model (18.18%; 6 studies)"

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

RQ1 analysis of component coverage combinations. The review states that "none of them addressed all four classic ITS components", with four papers covering domain, pedagogical and interface models and one covering domain, pedagogical and student models.

> "Among all 33 studies, none of them addressed all four classic ITS components."
