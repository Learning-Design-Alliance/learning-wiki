## Evidence

### Smith Daniel T 2018

Smith Daniel T, Tiffin Paul A. (2018). Evaluating the validity of the selection measures used for the UK’s foundation medical training programme: a national cohort study. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021918. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of proportions awarded outcome 6 by decile in the national cohort (n=14 131 and n=14 128), displayed in figures 2 and 3 with 95% CIs calculated by the Newcombe and Altman method. The discussion adds that for EPM the slopes are steepest at the extreme ends.

> "Figures 2 and 3 illustrate the relationship between completion of the foundation programme and the SJT z-score and EPM deciles: for both measures those applicants achieving at the lowest decile have a reduced proportion of ‘outcomes 6’ compared to those at the top decile, but the relationship for the intermediate deciles is non-linear."
