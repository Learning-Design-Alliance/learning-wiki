Evidence¶

Guido Makransky · Gustav Bøg Petersen 2023¶

Guido Makransky · Gustav Bøg Petersen. (2023). The Theory of Immersive Collaborative Learning (TICOL). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09822-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Bayro et al. (2022) and Sun et al. (2022), studies comparing collaborative settings and finding "perceived presence and co-presence in a collaborative setting turned out to be higher in XRCL than in computer-based collaborations". No effect sizes are printed in the review.

"Other studies have found that perceived presence and co-presence in a collabo- rative setting turned out to be higher in XRCL than in computer-based collabora- tions (Bayro et al., 2022; Sun et al., 2022)."
