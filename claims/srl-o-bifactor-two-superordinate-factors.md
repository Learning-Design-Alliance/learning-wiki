# The SRL-O supports both ten lower-order subscales and two superordinate factors (motivational beliefs and learning strategies)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Broadbent 2023

Broadbent, J., Panadero, E., Lodge, J. M., & Fuller-Tyszkiewicz, M. (2023). The self-regulation for learning online (SRL-O) questionnaire. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09319-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Secondary bifactor CFA in the validation sample (n = 321) tested 44 items loading onto ten specific factors plus two global factors reflecting learning and motivation; the article reports the fit of this model was also acceptable with CFI = 0.901 and RMSEA = 0.050.

> "The fit of this model was also acceptable; χ2(802) = 1435.52, p  <  0.001, χ2/df = 1.79, CFI = 0.901, RMSEA = 0.050."
