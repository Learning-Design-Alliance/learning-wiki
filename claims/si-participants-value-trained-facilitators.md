### Pfund Christine 2015

Pfund Christine, Spencer Kimberly C., Asquith Pamela, House Stephanie C., Miller Sarah, Sorkness Christine A. (2015). Building National Capacity for Research Mentor Training: An Evidence-Based Approach to Training the Trainers. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-10-0184

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Annual post-event surveys of National Academies Summer Institute participants, aggregated across 2006–2010: "97% rated \"having a trained facilitator for group work\" as valuable", from 159 of 212 total participants responding.

> "Participants in the SIs between 2006 and 2010 overwhelmingly noted the importance of having a facilitator work with them: 97% rated "having a trained facilitator for group work" as valuable (n = 159 respondents of 212 total participants)."
