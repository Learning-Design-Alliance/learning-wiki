## Evidence

### García-Ojeda ME 2022

García-Ojeda ME, Nishiguchi MK. (2022). The Metamorphosing Professor: Adapting Teaching to Fulfill the Promise of Biology Education. Integrative and Comparative Biology. https://doi.org/10.1093/icb/icac149

`q2 · i1` · `review · r1`

The review reports, citing Freeman et al. 2014, a meta-analysis of 225 studies of STEM classes. It states the "mean failure rate in courses taught using active learning is 21.8%" versus 33.8% for lecture, and reports an odds ratio of 1.95 for the risk of failing a STEM course.

> "They showed that the mean failure rate in courses taught using active learning is 21.8%, as opposed to lecture courses, which had a mean failure rate of 33.8%. The study also showed that active learning increased performance on tests, while lecturing increased failure rates by 55%."
