### Junting Yin 2025 (2)

Junting Yin, Zheyu Jin, Yuxuan Zhang, Xuening Li, Yangzhuo Li, Guoping Zhang & Junlong Luo. (2025). Applying, not ignoring: how feedback uptake and neural synchrony drive creativity. npj Science of Learning 10:61. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00351-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

2 (IF, OF) × 3 (ignore, copy, apply) ANOVA on INS extracted per uptake level, with printed effect size η2P = 0.234 for the uptake main effect. Apply elicited stronger INS than copy and ignore; in the IF group, apply and ignore INS both exceeded the OF group's corresponding values.

> "The main effect of feedback uptake was also signiﬁcant ( F(2, 112) = 17.100, p < 0.001,η2 P = 0.234). Speciﬁcally, the"apply"(0.39 ± 0.01) elicited stronger INS than "copy" (0.35 ± 0.01, p = 0.010) and "ignore" (0.30 ± 0.01, p < 0.001)."
