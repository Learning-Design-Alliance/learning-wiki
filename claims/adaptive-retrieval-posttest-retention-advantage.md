### Mahir Akgun and Sacip Toker 2026

Mahir Akgun and Sacip Toker. (2026). Do Gains from Generative AI–Enabled Adaptive Pretesting Persist? Evidence from a Retention Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22328 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.22328](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.22328)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANCOVA on the seven-week posttest (parallel 14-item multiple-choice forms, 0–100 scale) in the same experiment. The article reports "partialη 2 =.128" and adjusted means of 78.19 (G1), 74.55 (G2), 67.28 (G3).

> "ANCOVA also revealed a significant effect of condition on posttest performance,F(2,85) = 6.24,p=.003, partialη 2 =.128. Adjusted means indicated G1 achieved the highest posttest scores (M = 78.19, SE = 2.37), followed by G2 (M = 74.55, SE = 2.33) and G3 (M = 67.28, SE = 2.12)."
