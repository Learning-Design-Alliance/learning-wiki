## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Putwain 2021

Putwain, D. W., Wood, P., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Achievement emotions and academic achievement: Reciprocal relations and the moderating influence of academic buoyancy. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000637

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Competitive SEM model comparison in the four-wave longitudinal data showed the reciprocal relations model "showed a good fit to the data that was superior to all other models", supporting bidirectional emotion–achievement relations.

> "The RRM showed a good fit to the data that was superior to all other models (Table 4). Furthermore, the RRM showed a significantly better fit than the other models using the Satorra–Bentler scaled χ2 difference test (TRd; Bryant & Satorra, 2012) and an improved relative fit on the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC)."
