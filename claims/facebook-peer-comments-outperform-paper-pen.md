## Evidence

### Ho 2020

Ho, P. V. P., Phung, L. T. K., Oanh, T. T. T., & Giao, N. Q. (2020). Should Peer E-Comments Replace Traditional Peer Comments? International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 295-314. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13120a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test scores of the two intact classes (control n=40, experimental n=32); MD=-.499, t=-2.501, p=.015, leading the article to conclude Facebook-based peer comment activities are more effective than traditional ones.

> "The students’ average post -test score of the experimental group (M= 6.994; SD= .692) is higher than that of the control gr oup (M=6.495; SD= .942), and the Sig. for the comparison of the average post -test scores of the two groups is p= .015 <.05."
