`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The article reports, citing Miller & Stassun (2014), that the Fisk-Vanderbilt bridge program replaced GRE cutoffs with a 30-minute interview and achieved "a high retention rate of women and minorities of over 80% towards the PhD."

> "Programs such as the Fisk-Vanderbilt (Miller & Stassun 2014), which oﬀer a master that acts as a bridge to the PhD, employ a 30-minute interview instead of a Graduate Record Exami-nations (GRE) cut-oﬀ. It proved to be very successful with a high retention rate of women and minorities of over 80% towards the PhD."
