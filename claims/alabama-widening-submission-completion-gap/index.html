Sayda Martinez-Alvarado 2025¶

q2 · i? · associational · r1

EdTrust's analysis of Alabama FAFSA data reports the pre/post gap in submission versus completion rates: "the average gap between submission and completion rates was 3.12%, but it increased to 4.37% afterward." Descriptive percentages only; no test reported.

"Before this requirement was implemented, the average gap between submission and completion rates was 3.12%, but it increased to 4.37% afterward."

Related Claims¶

  • Illinois's universal FAFSA policy produced a mixed first-year result: submissions rose 0.8% while completions fell 0.2% — related

  • California's LEA-based FAFSA/CADAA requirement for the class of 2023 coincided with a 7.8% rise in submissions and 6.7% rise in completions — related
