## Evidence

### Ruesseler M. 2025

Ruesseler M., Britz V., Herrmann-Werner A., Festl-Wietek T., Weckwerth C., Bepler L., Beaugé Y., Sterz J. (2025). A flipped classroom improves medical students’ skills how to manage medical emergencies—an intervention-control study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07898-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective intervention-control study in which 165 fifth-year students (79 flipped, 86 classic) completed a formative OSCE of six emergency case stations; t-tests for independent samples showed the flipped group "scored significantly (p < 0.001) higher in the total score". No effect size is printed.

> "The results of the t-test for independent groups showed, that participants of the flipped classroom training scored significantly (p < 0.001) higher in the total score at all six Emergency Case OSCE stations (Fig. 1)."
