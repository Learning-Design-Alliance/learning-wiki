## Evidence

### Lazartigues Laura 2021

Lazartigues Laura, Mathy Fabien, Lavigne Frédéric. (2021). Statistical learning of unbalanced exclusive-or temporal sequences in humans. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effect model on transition times to the second dot across ten blocks of 40 trials in the touchscreen experiment; the authors interpret the decrease as the first and second stimuli being paired during learning.

> "the mixed model 1 (Table 3) showed a significant effect of block number on RTTT1, with RTs that decreased during the learning phase of the experiment (F(1,14650) = 153.5, p <.001)"

### Lazartigues Laura 2021 (2)

Lazartigues Laura, Mathy Fabien, Lavigne Frédéric. (2021). Statistical learning of unbalanced exclusive-or temporal sequences in humans. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First-block analysis of the same experiment found the lowest first-order TP value (.29) yielded longer response times than the three higher TP values; a global analysis also found "TT2 being shorter than TT1 (F(1,32104) = 1897.5, p <.001)".

> "the lowest value of first-order TP produced RTs (RTTT1+TT2) longer than the three other highest values of first-order TPs (F(3,2905) = 19.8, p <.001)"
