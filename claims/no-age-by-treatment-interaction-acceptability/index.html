Lundervold 2013¶

Lundervold, D. A., Ament, P. A., Holt, P. S., & Hunt, L. S. (2013). Comparison of Younger and Older Adults' Acceptability of Treatment for Generalized Anxiety Disorder Co-Occurring with Parkinson's Disease. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, 8(2). https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1555-7855

q3 · i0 · causal · r1

The omnibus two (age) × two (treatment) mixed factorial ANOVA tested the interaction of age group and treatment type on AARP scores. The article reports "nor was there a significant interaction between age group and treatment type ratings," with observed power = .277, meaning both cohorts rated BRT above medication similarly.
