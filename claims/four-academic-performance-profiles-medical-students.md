## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Ward's minimum variance hierarchical clustering on nine standardized variables identified four subgroups with different final written exam scores (Cluster 4 best at 85.8 ± 1.0; Cluster 1 worst at 75.1 ± 1.2). [→ Goudarzi Houman 2026](#goudarzi-houman-2026)

## Evidence

### Goudarzi Houman 2026

Goudarzi Houman, Onozawa Masahiro, Ito Yoichi M., Takahashi Makoto. (2026). Clustering of students in pre-clinical medical education and identification of academic performance profiles: does online education work for all medical students? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08737-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Person-centered analysis of the same 321-student cohort using Ward's minimum variance hierarchical clustering on nine standardized variables; Table 3 ANOVA shows final exam scores differing across clusters (Cluster 1: 75.1 ± 1.2, n = 59; Cluster 2: 76.8 ± 1.2, n = 54; Cluster 3: 80.7 ± 0.92, n = 103; Cluster 4: 85.8 ± 1.0, n = 78; p < 0.0001). No effect size printed.

> "Cluster analysis identified four subgroups (Table 3; Fig. 1, and Figure E4)."
