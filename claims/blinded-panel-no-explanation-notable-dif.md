## Evidence

### Hope D 2018

Hope D, Adamson KA, McManus IC, Chis L, Elder A. (2018). Using differential item functioning to evaluate potential bias in a high stakes postgraduate knowledge based assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1143-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Expert review of the eight medium- or large-effect DIF questions by ten clinicians of diverse backgrounds (UK and non-UK trained, male and female, ethnically white and non-white), blinded to DIF type, magnitude and disadvantaged group. The article reports "No reviewer was able to suggest any cogent explanation for the DIF for any item."

> "Reviewers were not able to consult with other panel members, and knew only that DIF was present – not the type or magnitude. No reviewer was able to suggest any cogent explanation for the DIF for any item."
