## Evidence

### Thapa R. 2023

Thapa R., Nikolli K., McMahon D., Blakemore S., Tamang S., Bhatta S., Gautam P., Shrestha R., Rajbhandari R. (2023). Novel on-site follow-up and enhancement program (FEP) improves knowledge, clinical skills and enabling environment of skilled birth attendants in Nepal. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0285653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intergroup comparative analysis in the Results section comparing Cohort A2017 (n = 73) with Cohort B2017 (n = 93) from three districts that had not previously had FEP.

> "In terms of clinical skills, the SBAs that had undergone FEP achieved total scores of 75% compared to 41% scored by those that were yet to undergo FEP (p<0.001)."

### Thapa R. 2023 (2)

Thapa R., Nikolli K., McMahon D., Blakemore S., Tamang S., Bhatta S., Gautam P., Shrestha R., Rajbhandari R. (2023). Novel on-site follow-up and enhancement program (FEP) improves knowledge, clinical skills and enabling environment of skilled birth attendants in Nepal. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0285653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same intergroup comparison in the Results section; enabling environment was 77.89 vs 57.74 (p<0.001). Total deliveries showed a 5.46 increase for FEP SBAs that was not statistically significant (p = 0.10).

> "The SBAs that had already undergone FEP had total knowledge scores of approximately 84% compared to the 80% total knowledge scores achieved by the SBAs that were yet to undergo FEP (p = 0.04)."
