## Evidence

### Solberg Runar Barstad 2021

Solberg Runar Barstad, Steene-Johannessen Jostein, Anderssen Sigmund Alfred, Ekelund Ulf, Säfvenbom Reidar, Haugen Tommy, Berntsen Sveinung, Åvitsland Andreas, Lerum Øystein, Resaland Geir Kåre, Kolle Elin. (2021). Effects of a school-based physical activity intervention on academic performance in 14-year old adolescents: a cluster randomized controlled trial – the School in Motion study. BMC Public Health 21, 871. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-10901-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of the cluster RCT tested a timepoint x group x sex interaction term; the article reports "p < 0.001 for interaction" in all academic performance models, prompting sex-stratified reanalysis. No effect size is printed for the interaction.

> "A statistically significant interaction between sexes was evident in all academic performance models (p < 0.001 for interaction). Consequently, we repeated the analysis stratified by this variable."

### Solberg Runar Barstad 2021 (2)

Solberg Runar Barstad, Steene-Johannessen Jostein, Anderssen Sigmund Alfred, Ekelund Ulf, Säfvenbom Reidar, Haugen Tommy, Berntsen Sveinung, Åvitsland Andreas, Lerum Øystein, Resaland Geir Kåre, Kolle Elin. (2021). Effects of a school-based physical activity intervention on academic performance in 14-year old adolescents: a cluster randomized controlled trial – the School in Motion study. BMC Public Health 21, 871. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-10901-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sex-stratified mixed models in the same trial: PAL numeracy gains of "1.0 points (95% CI: 0.3 to 1.8)" for girls versus 2.4 points for boys; DWBH showed a similar pattern (1.4 vs 2.7 points). Only boys in DWBH showed a significant reading effect.

> "the mean differences in change for numeracy in the PAL intervention were 1.0 points (95% CI: 0.3 to 1.8) and 2.4 points (95% CI: 1.5 to 3.3) for girls and boys respectively"
