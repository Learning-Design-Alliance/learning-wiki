## Evidence

### Knight 2016

Knight, S., Buckingham Shum, S., Ryan, P., Sándor, Á., & Wang, X. (2016). Designing Academic Writing Analytics for Civil Law Student Self-Assessment. *International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28*(1), 1–28. [doi:10.1007/s40593-016-0121-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0121-0)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation from student self-assessment cover sheets in the UTS law course, reported as motivating the project. The article frames this as evidence that students found self-assessment for rubric criteria challenging, overestimating their performance, and that instructors providing formative feedback on all drafts would be prohibitively time-consuming.

> "However, we identified that when students were invited to self-assess their own writing using the formal rubric they tended to over-rate their writing."
