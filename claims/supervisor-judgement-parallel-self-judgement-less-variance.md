### Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun 2024

Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun, Oeser Reinhard, Seemann-Hlawati Barbara, Varga Katja, Wagner-Menghin Michaela. (2024). Self-directed learning in post-graduate medical education: Self-judgement and supervisor judgement of competence development in Austrian nine-month basic training. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4): Doc42. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001697.shtml

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same longitudinal logbook study; Spearman correlation of supervisor-judged completion (LC–SP) with training time gave "r=0.25 (df=880, p<0.001, n=882, r2=0.06, very weak effect)". Each junior physician was judged by at least three supervising physicians across rotations.

> "The corresponding results for the global judgement of the level of training goal completion by the supervising SPs (LC–SP) and training time (6 measurement points) are r=0.25 (df=880, p<0.001, n=882, r2=0.06, very weak effect)."

### Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive box-plot comparison of supervisor (blue) versus self-judgement (red) ratings at six time points; supervisor means were similar but with "less variance", which the authors attribute to supervisors' experience with the ÖÄK objectives.

> "The MW of the supervisors’ judgements displayed less variance than those of the JPs’ self-judgements. However, they were similar"
