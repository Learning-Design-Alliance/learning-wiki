### Pavlík 2024

Pavlík, V., Vošmera, J., Gráf, T., & Křížová, R. (2024). Fostering Innovation, Inclusion, and Diversity in Astronomy Education: The Czech Astronomy Olympiad Experience. [doi:10.22201/ia.14052059p.2024.57.05](https://doi.org/10.22201/ia.14052059p.2024.57.05)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive gender-balance statistics for the CzAO school rounds reported in the participation section. The article reports "around 40 to 50 % female students in the school rounds" and 54% and 55% in the last two years, interpreting this as balanced STEM education.

> "For several years now, we have had around 40 to 50 % female students in the school rounds. In the last two years, it was even 54 % and 55 %, respectively, which shows that STEM education in our country is balanced at schools."

### Pavlík 2024 (2)

Pavlík, V., Vošmera, J., Gráf, T., & Křížová, R. (2024). Fostering Innovation, Inclusion, and Diversity in Astronomy Education: The Czech Astronomy Olympiad Experience. [doi:10.22201/ia.14052059p.2024.57.05](https://doi.org/10.22201/ia.14052059p.2024.57.05)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics on female representation in CzAO national finals, shown in Fig. 2 and reported in the participation section. The article notes large year-to-year fluctuations and that "younger categories usually have a higher percentage of female finalists than high school categories."

> "The average number of women who progress to the national finals is then lower, between 20 to 40 % (see Fig. 2). Despite the large fluctuations between the years, it is apparent that younger categories usually have a higher percentage of female finalists than high school categories."
