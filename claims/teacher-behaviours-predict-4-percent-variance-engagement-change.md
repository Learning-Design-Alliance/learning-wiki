## Evidence

### Michael Noetel 2023

Michael Noetel, Philip Parker, Theresa Dicke, Mark R. Beauchamp, Nikos Ntoumanis, Ryan M. Hulteen, et al. (2023). Prediction Versus Explanation in Educational Psychology: a Cross-Theoretical Approach to Using Teacher Behaviour to Predict Student Engagement in Physical Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09786-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal predictive study in which 1324 Year 7 students (52% female) from 17 low socio-economic status schools rated 71 teacher behaviours mid-year, with engagement measured at the start and end of the year. The article reports that "teacher behaviours predicted 4.39% of the variance in students' change in engagement" on held-out data.

> "We trained elastic-net regression models on 70% of the data and then assessed their predictive validity on the held-out data (30%). The models showed that teacher behaviours predicted 4.39% of the variance in students' change in engagement."
