## Evidence

### Sun Ning 2026

Sun Ning, Zhou Xintong, Yang Zhongqian, Zhou Yu, Ma Ruihang, Wang Zhong. (2026). Application of AI-based virtual standardized patients in physician-patient communication training: a study based on the SEGUE framework. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1768518

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired comparisons in the RCT (AI-VSP n = 40, traditional SP n = 42) after 2 h of training following a 3-week ethics course. The article reports "significant improvements across all SEGUE dimensions" with no effect size printed for the within-group gains.

> "After the intervention, both groups demonstrated significant improvements across all SEGUE dimensions, total scores, and Content and Process sub-scores (all, P < .05)."
