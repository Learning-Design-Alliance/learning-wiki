### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of their descriptive count and state-flow analyses of 135 dVSS trials by 18 surgeons: expert and novice trials were less distinct at the maneuver level than at the gesture level. This is an interpretive statement in the Discussion, not a tested comparison.

> "In addition, objectively studying a surgical task at the gesture level was more discriminative of skill than at the maneuver level. Expert and novice trials were less distinct from each other when analyzing maneuvers in comparison to gestures based on the counts and visual examination of transitions."
