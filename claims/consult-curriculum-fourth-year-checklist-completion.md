## Evidence

### Carter Keme MD 2015

Carter Keme MD, Golden Andrew BS, Martin Shannon MD, Donlan Sarah MD, Hock Sara MD, Babcock Christine MD, Farnan Jeanne MD, Arora Vineet MD. (2015). Results from the First Year of Implementation of CONSULT: Consultation with Novel Methods and Simulation for UME Longitudinal Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.25520

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fourth-year simulation cohort with consultations scored on the 13-component 5 Cs checklist by two independent raters per consultation. "After receiving the curriculum, students completed higher proportions of the 5 Cs checklist," with overall completion increasing significantly (62% vs. 77%, p<0.001).

> "After receiving the curriculum, students completed higher proportions of the 5 Cs checklist compared to their consultation evaluations prior to receiving the curriculum (Figure 4). The overall checklist completion increased significantly (62% vs. 77%, p<0.001)"

### Carter Keme MD 2015 (2)

Carter Keme MD, Golden Andrew BS, Martin Shannon MD, Donlan Sarah MD, Hock Sara MD, Babcock Christine MD, Farnan Jeanne MD, Arora Vineet MD. (2015). Results from the First Year of Implementation of CONSULT: Consultation with Novel Methods and Simulation for UME Longitudinal Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.25520

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same fourth-year cohort, per-section checklist completion. "The Contact (30% vs. 59%, p<0.001), Communicate (85% vs. 90%, p<0.05), and Closing the Loop (74% vs. 89%, p<0.001) sections had significantly higher completion" after the curriculum.

> "the Contact (30% vs. 59%, p<0.001), Communicate (85% vs. 90%, p<0.05), and Closing the Loop (74% vs. 89%, p<0.001) sections had significantly higher completion by the students after the implementation of the curriculum."
