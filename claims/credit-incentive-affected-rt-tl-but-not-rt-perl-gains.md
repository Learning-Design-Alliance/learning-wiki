## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2012

Beth Thacker, Hani Dulli, Dave Pattillo, and Keith West. (2012). Lessons from a Large-Scale Assessment: Results from Conceptual Inventories. Physics Department, Texas Tech University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chronological analysis of algebra-based FCI data split at Fall 2010, when small amounts of credit based on post-test scores began; the article reports gains of "0.17 +/- 0.01" and "0.20 +/- 0.01" for RT/TL and no significant difference for RT/PERL. No effect size is printed.

> "The RT/TL data taken before Fall 2010 had a normalized gain of 0.17 +/- 0.01 and after Fall 2010, it had a normalized gain of 0.20 +/- 0.01. It is possible that this difference is due to the credit given."
