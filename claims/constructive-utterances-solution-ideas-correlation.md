# In young students' collaborative PF, dyads that produced more constructive utterances generated more solution ideas

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The absolute number of constructive utterances per dyad correlated positively with the number of solution ideas the dyad generated (r = 0.411). [→ Claudia Mazziotti 2019](#claudia-mazziotti-2019)

## Evidence

### Claudia Mazziotti 2019

Claudia Mazziotti, Nikol Rummel, Anne Deiglmayr and Katharina Loibl. (2019). Probing boundary conditions of Productive Failure and analyzing the role of young students' collaboration. npj Science of Learning 4:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0041-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory Pearson correlation analysis of coded dialog transcripts from 34 PF-Coll dyads (audio for three dyads lost). The correlation was significant with printed effect size "r = 0.411" (p = 0.008); all other utterance types were not significantly associated with solution ideas.

> "a statistically signi ﬁcant positive correlation between the absolute number of constructive utterances (i.e., sum of constructive utterances of both dyad members) and the number of solution ideas students collabora- tively generated (both variables are on the dyad level); r(32) = 0.411, p = 0.008)"
