Evidence¶

Scheibe 2022¶

Scheibe, D. A., Fitzsimmons, C. J., Mielicki, M. K., Taber, J. M., Sidney, P. G., Coifman, K., & Thompson, C. A. (2022). Confidence in COVID problem solving: What factors predict adults’ item-level metacognitive judgments on health-related math problems before and after an educational intervention? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09300-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory mediation analysis reported in the Discussion and detailed in the article's supplemental file, from the secondary analysis of the 1,177-participant dataset. The article reports that "math anxiety mediated the effect of gender on pretest monitoring judgments"; no mediation coefficients or effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"Further exploratory analyses (see Supplemental File) revealed that math anxiety mediated the effect of gender on pretest monitoring judgments."
