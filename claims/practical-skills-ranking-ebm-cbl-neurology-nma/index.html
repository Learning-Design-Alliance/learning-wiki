Li Xiangyu 2024¶

Li Xiangyu, Zhang Longsheng, Sun Wenjun, Lei Min, Li Ying, Zhang Jiacheng, Huang Xing. (2024). Comparison of the effects of different teaching methods on the effectiveness of teaching neurology in China: a bayesian network meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06397-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Bayesian NMA results section for practical skill scores: the printed mean differences and credible intervals show EBM with the largest effect versus LBL, followed by CBL. Unstandardized mean differences only; no standardized effect size printed.

"In particular, EBM demonstrated the most notable effect [MD = 13.34, 95% CI (7.59,18.95), P < 0.001], followed by the CBL teaching method [MD = 12.90, 95% CI (10.24,15.54), P < 0.01]"

Li Xiangyu 2024 (2)¶

Li Xiangyu, Zhang Longsheng, Sun Wenjun, Lei Min, Li Ying, Zhang Jiacheng, Huang Xing. (2024). Comparison of the effects of different teaching methods on the effectiveness of teaching neurology in China: a bayesian network meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06397-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

SUCRA-based ranking from the Bayesian NMA of practical skill scores across 31 RCTs; Table 2 gives EBM a 55.4% probability of being the best method for practical skills versus CBL's 42.0%. The ranking is a printed ordering, not an effect size.

"The ranking evaluation, detailed in Table 2; Fig. 4 (d), positions the teaching methods as follows: CBL > EBM > PBL > TBL > FCM > CP > LBL."
