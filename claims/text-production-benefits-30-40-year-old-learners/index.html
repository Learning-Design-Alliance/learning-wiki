Evidence¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani 2018¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani. (2018). On the Efficiency of Text Production in Vocabulary Learning: An Empirical Study on Iranian GFL-Learners. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n3p176

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA (2 with/without text production × 2 age group) for the 30–40-year age group (CG n = 8, EC n = 5); the article reports "F (1, 10) = 8.69, p = .015" with η2 = .46 for short-term retention and significant effects at the other intervals.

"A significant influence is noticeable on the short-term retention performance (F (1, 10) = 8.69, p = .015 (p ≤ 0.01), η2 = .46), as well as on the medium-term retention performance (F (1, 10) = 6.02, p = .034 (p ≤ 0.05), η2 = .38)"
