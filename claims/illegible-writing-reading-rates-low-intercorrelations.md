## Evidence

### Downey 1914

Downey, J. E., and Anderson, J. E. (1914). Form and Position in Handwriting Interpretation. Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Form+and+position+in+handwriting+interpretation%22+Downey+Anderson

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

In the illegible-writing test, 16 reagents read aloud three unmutilated business-letter specimens (A 1, B 1, C 1) with stop-watch timing; speed and error were put on an equal basis for ranking. The results section prints correlation coefficients r = .552, r = .410, and one 'too low to be significant', and the authors conclude 'the need of better standardization in the tests is evident'.

> "the highest is the correlation of A 1 and B 1 (.552), next comes that of A 1 and C 1 (.410); the correlation of B 1 and C 1 gave a coefficient too low to be significant."
