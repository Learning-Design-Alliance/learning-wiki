Cross-sectional observational study of 19 PGY and emergency medicine residents intubating four manikins; Kruskal-Wallis comparisons across models. The results report "the longest intubation time (58.84 ± 22.63 seconds, P <.001)" and lowest success rate for Model C.

> "Model ‘C’ had the highest difficulty score (4.430 points), the longest intubation time (58.84 ± 22.63 seconds, P <.001), and the lowest success rate (57.89%, P <.001), and was rated most difficult by participants."
