Evidence¶

dePaoli 2023¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q4 · i? · review · r?

The review reports this overview of 12 meta-analyses, plus a second 2023 meta-analysis of universal SEL programs finding similar results and improvement in school functioning including achievement, study skills, attendance, and on-task behavior.

"A 2023 overview of 12 independent meta-analyses on school-based learning programs finds strong evidence that they promote the development of social and emotional competencies; reduce rates of behavior problems, including bullying and aggression; and increase prosocial behavior."

dePaoli 2023 (2)¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q3 · i? · review · r?

The review reports this meta-analysis of longer-term SEL effects as evidence of durable benefits. It also cites a 2018 survey finding students whose schools strongly promoted social-emotional skills were more likely to say they felt safe in school.

"A meta-analysis that examined the longer-term effects of social and emotional learning—ranging from 6 months to 18 years after the program—found sustained positive impacts on behavioral, mental health, and academic outcomes."
