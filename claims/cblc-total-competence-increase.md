## Evidence

### Sellitto Giovanni 2026

Sellitto Giovanni, Galeoto Giovanni, Deodato Manuela, Di Bari Mauro, Garaventa Daniela, Testa Marco, Pelosin Elisa, Pillastrini Paolo, Gatti Roberto, Trucco Marco. (2026). Exploring the Case-Based Learning Challenge (CBLC): a digitally mediated, inter-university simulation to enhance clinical reasoning in physiotherapy students - a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08610-3

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Inferential analysis of paired pre–post responses on the full ten-item Competence Questionnaire in the same one-group quasi-experimental design. The article reports the total score "showed a significant increase, from a pre-intervention mean of 7.05 ± 1.390 to 7.47 ± 1.879 at T1", with "Cohen's d = 0.29".

> "the total score across all ten items showed a significant increase, from a pre-intervention mean of 7.05 ± 1.390 to 7.47 ± 1.879 at T1 (p < 0.001), indicating a small but statistically robust gain in competence, with a small effect size (Cohen's d = 0.29; 95% CI: 0.18 to 0.40)"
