## Evidence

### Martinho S 2026

Martinho S, Nóbrega S, Barroso C, Correia-Pinto J. (2026). Trainee involvement in laparoscopic pyloromyotomy does not compromise outcomes: evidence from a longitudinal institutional experience. Pediatric Surgery International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06435-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Temporal institutional analysis using CUSUM based on an expected composite adverse outcome rate of p₀ = 0.08, across three epochs (2011–2016, 32 patients; 2017–2020, 24 patients; 2021–2025, 21 patients). The study reports epoch rates of "9.4%, 4.2%, and 9.5%" and no sustained drift (Fig. 2).

> "Importantly, neither the raw nor the smoothed CUSUM curve displayed a sustained upward drift after trainee integration, indicating no increase in cumulative morbidity during the training era."
