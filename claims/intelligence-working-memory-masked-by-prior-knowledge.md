## Evidence

### Geary 2021

Geary, D. C., Hoard, M. K., Nugent, L., & Scofield, J. E. (2021). In-Class Attention, Spatial Ability, and Mathematics Anxiety Predict Across-Grade Gains in Adolescents' Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(4), 754-769. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000487

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian model comparison across analysis steps in the 317-student sample; the authors interpret that intelligent students with strong working memory acquired more sixth-grade knowledge, masking direct effects.

> "Prior to inclusion of sixth-grade mathematical competence, the Bayesian analyses indicated that intelligence and working memory (backward digit span) were strong independent predictors of seventh-grade mathematics achievement, in addition to spatial abilities."
