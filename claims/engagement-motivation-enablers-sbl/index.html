Stenseth Hege Vistven et al 2025¶

Stenseth Hege Vistven et al. (2025). Simulation-Based Learning Supported by Technology to Enhance Critical Thinking in Nursing Students: Scoping Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/58744

q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic analysis of RQ 3 data from included studies; the review reports that "TESs provided a safe learning environment" where immersion and curiosity "facilitated the development of critical thinking for nursing students."

"Across several studies (13/42, 31%), nursing students reported that TESs provided a safe learning environment [87,88,109,110,112,122,124,126] where a high level of immersion [92,97,103,108,110,112,126], sparked curiosity [108,113,126], and the activation of thoughts and feelings [92,108,122,131] enabled involvement and engagement and facilitated the development of critical thinking for nursing students."
