# A K-means clustering of students based on pre- and post-project SDG knowledge identifies three distinct learning profiles, with the largest cluster showing the greatest knowledge gain.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Three clusters emerged: 'from very little I learn something' (n=74), 'from almost nothing I learn a lot' (n=183), and 'from the intermediate I learn something' (n=44), with uneven distribution across universities. [→ Blanco 2024](#blanco-2024)

## Evidence

### Blanco 2024

Blanco, J. M., Domínguez, C., Jaime, A., & Usandizaga, I. (2024). Connecting sustainability and computer science curricula through website learning projects embedding different types of student-generated content. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10376-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

K-means clustering of 301 students using pre- and post-project SDG knowledge ratings. The study reports three clusters with the largest being "from almost nothing I learn a lot" (n=183). Cluster sizes and means are reported in Table 6. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The result of this classification with a clearer interpretation had three clusters. The option with two and four clusters was discarded because the first one grouped two clusters quite differently and the second one produced two clusters with a very similar interpretation."
