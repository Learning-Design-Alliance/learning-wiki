Germana Bertoli 2025¶

Germana Bertoli, Ilaria Amelia Caggiano, Francesca Lagioia, Riccardo Rovatti, Giovanni Sartor, Emiliano Troisi. (2025). What out-of-the-box LLMs can(t) do in law? A Turing test in Italian exams for lawyers, judges and notaries. https://github.com/SSIGPRO/LLMs-in-legal-professions

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Failure-pattern analysis of the notary exam's inter vivos assignment, where notarial law mandates a sign-language interpreter under penalty of nullity for a deaf-mute purchaser who can read and write.

"All models, including those that correctly identified legal sources, incorrectly stated that an interpreter was not needed, and failed to mention one in the deed. By falling into such a trap, LLMs exhibited a considerable degree of gullibility."
