## Evidence

### Rusch Peter 2017

Rusch Peter, Kimmig Rainer, Lecuru Fabrice, Persson Jan, Ponce Jordi, Degueldre Michel, Verheijen René. (2017). The Society of European Robotic Gynaecological Surgery (SERGS) Pilot Curriculum for robot assisted gynecological surgery. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-017-4612-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings from tele-conferences with fellows and tutors at 6 and 12 months and from implementation reports during the pilot fellowship; the article states "only one tutor followed the curriculum with structural assessment" and that feedback was not given systematically after each procedure.

> "In contrast to the outline of the Pilot Curriculum, regular assessment of progress in training was more done by open feedback than by use of GEARS/OSATS and NOTSS."
