Emergency Department Slit Lamp Interdisciplinary Training Via Longitudinal Assessment in Medical Practice. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.18514

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post pilot study of 15 board-certified emergency physicians at two institutions, using a 20-item checklist scored at baseline (Time 0) and final in-person assessment (Time 2). "a significant increase between the checklist scores" was tested with Wilcoxon signed-rank; medians rose from 12.0 [10, 16] to 19.0 [19, 20].

> "We found a significant increase between the checklist scores before and after the education initiative, 12.0 to 19.0, P = 0.002."
