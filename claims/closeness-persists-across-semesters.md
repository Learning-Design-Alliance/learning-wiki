## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Closeness at the beginning of Spring semesters was significantly positively correlated with Fall MI participation regardless of controlling for section, year, or instructor, and centralities were correlated across semesters (closeness R²adj = 0.48). [→ Zwolak 2016](#zwolak-2016)

## Evidence

### Zwolak 2016

Zwolak, J. P., Dou, R., Williams, E. A., & Brewe, E. (2016). Students' network integration as a predictor of persistence in introductory physics courses. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression of Spring SNA1 centralities on Fall centralities found three of five significant predictors; Table VIII reports closeness B = 0.36, F(1,146) = 138.9, R²adj = 0.48, the most robust measure, with closeness "signiﬁcantly correlated with the predicting factor" in every model. Ties involving returning students accounted for about 90% of all interactions.

> "In each case closeness was signiﬁcantly correlated with the predicting factor. Moreover, each measure was posi-tively correlated with its counterpart from the last collection of the Fall semester"
