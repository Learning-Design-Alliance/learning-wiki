Vysniauske 2020¶

Vysniauske, R., Verburgh, L., Oosterlaan, J., & Molendijk, M. L. (2020). The Effects of Physical Exercise on Functional Outcomes in the Treatment of ADHD: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of attention disorders, 24(5), 644-654. https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054715627489

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Moderator analyses in the random-effects meta-analysis of ten publications on exercise and ADHD functional outcomes found no relation for three moderators: "exercise intensity, mean age of participants, or gender distribution." No effect sizes are printed for these tests.
