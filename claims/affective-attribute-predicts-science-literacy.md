### Bayu Setiaji 2023

Bayu Setiaji, Purwoko Haryanto Santoso, Khafidh Nur Aziz, Heri Retnawati, and Moh Khairudin. (2023). Using multilevel modeling to evaluate science literacy and technology course of the Indonesian non-science students. Identifier: none printed in text. [doi:10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457](https://doi.org/10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-level multilevel model (Model 5, Table 4) on 160 students found affective attribute "significantly influencing students' science literacy" with printed coefficient 1.18 (SE 0.18); the three-level model 6 replicated this (𝛿200, p < 0.05). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Affective attribute (𝛾20, p < 0.05) was discovered significantly influencing students' science literacy and this is consistent as reported by Fives et al. (2014)."

### Bayu Setiaji 2023 (2)

Bayu Setiaji, Purwoko Haryanto Santoso, Khafidh Nur Aziz, Heri Retnawati, and Moh Khairudin. (2023). Using multilevel modeling to evaluate science literacy and technology course of the Indonesian non-science students. Identifier: none printed in text. [doi:10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457](https://doi.org/10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA screening (Table 3) showed no significant department or faculty mean difference for the affective measure (e.g., department F = 0.162, p = 0.922); the affect variable was still retained in the multilevel models because of its Pearson correlation with performance.

> "Second, we find no significant mean difference of students' affective attribute among department and faculty."
