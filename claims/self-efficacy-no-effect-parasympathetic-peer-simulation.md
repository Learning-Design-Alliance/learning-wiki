## Evidence

### Nakayama 2023

Nakayama, N., Ejiri, H., Arakawa, N., & Makino, T. (2023). Role of peer learning and self‐efficacy in parasympathetic activity during the simulation learning process in nursing students. Nursing Open, 10, 552–559. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1321

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two‐way repeated‐measures ANOVA compared HF component changes across phases among low, intermediate and high general self‐efficacy groups. The ANOVA "revealed no statistically significant difference in parasympathetic activity among the three groups in either Scene 1 or Scene 2".

> "The two‐way repeated‐measures ANOVA revealed no statistically significant difference in parasympathetic activity among the three groups in either Scene 1 or Scene 2 (p = .502, .356)"
