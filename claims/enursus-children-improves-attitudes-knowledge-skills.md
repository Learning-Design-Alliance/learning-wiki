## Evidence

### Álvarez-García C. 2019

Álvarez-García C., Álvarez-Nieto C., Kelsey J., Carter R., Sanz-Martos S., López-Medina I.M. (2019). Effectiveness of the e-NurSus Children Intervention in the Training of Nursing Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214288

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental time-series pre-post study of 267 nursing students (110 Spain, 157 UK) in 2018; Wilcoxon matched-pairs tests on SANS_2, ChEHK-Q, and ChEHS-Q scores. The article reports that "the attitudes, knowledge and skills of nursing students improved after the intervention with a large effect size". Non-parametric tests were used because most data were not normally distributed.

> "In all cases, the attitudes, knowledge and skills of nursing students improved after the intervention with a large effect size (Table 3)."
