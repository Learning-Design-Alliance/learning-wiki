## Evidence

### Hong Wei Han 2015

Hong Wei Han, Vadivelu Jamunarani, Daniel Esther Gnanamalar Sarojini, Sim Joong Hiong. (2015). Thinking about thinking: changes in first-year medical students' metacognition and its relation to performance. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27561

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations between MSI subscales and year-end results for 159 students, reported in Table 2 as 0.375, 0.335 and 0.373, all significant at the 0.01 level; the text calls these "a weak correlation". The printed r values are small effects.

> "the relationship between knowledge of metacognition (r=0.38), regulation of metacognition (r=0.34), and metacognitive skills (r=0.37) with students' academic results revealed a weak correlation as shown in Table 2"
