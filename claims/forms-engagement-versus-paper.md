# Teachers reported increased student engagement when using Forms instead of paper-based assessments and surveys

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers across grade levels reported greater student engagement and application when Forms replaced paper and pencil for assessments and surveys. [→ Digital Promise 2018](#digital-promise-2018)

## Evidence

### Digital Promise 2018

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview report: multiple teachers, including those of first-graders, described students as more engaged or applied with Forms; a third-grade pollution survey showed students their own contribution to the problem via automatic visual graphics.

> "A middle-school teacher from Scotland, for example, described increased engagement among her students when using Forms as opposed to paper and a pencil."
