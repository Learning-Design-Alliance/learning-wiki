## Evidence

### Ramsburg 2015

Ramsburg, J. T., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Category Change in the Absence of Cognitive Conflict. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Category+Change+in+the+Absence+of+Cognitive+Conflict%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 2 replicated Experiment 1 with 61 introductory psychology students (21 per condition meeting criterion) plus revised instructions and a labeled-image handout. The chi-squared test showed "the groups did not differ in learning the target, χ2 (42) = .11, p = .739".

> "We examined whether conditions differed in learning the target via a chi-squared test-of-independence that showed that the groups did not differ in learning the target, χ2 (42) = .11, p = .739."

### Ramsburg 2015 (2)

Ramsburg, J. T., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Category Change in the Absence of Cognitive Conflict. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Category+Change+in+the+Absence+of+Cognitive+Conflict%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Because of the small sample, t-tests replaced a repeated-measures ANOVA for blocks 6–10 in Experiment 2. The Confirmatory-Only advantage appeared on block 6 and marginally on block 7, with no differences on blocks 8–10.

> "The results of the t-tests revealed that participants in the confirmation condition performed better than the Complete condition for block 6, t (40) = 4.56, p < .001 and marginally better on block 7 for a one-tailed t-test, t (40) = 1.67, p = .051. There were no differences between the groups for blocks 8, 9, and 10, t < 1"
