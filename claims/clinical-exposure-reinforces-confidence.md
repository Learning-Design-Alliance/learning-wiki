`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sensitivity analysis in the GEE model entering naturally occurring delirium care experience (78 of 108 participants reported such care) as a binary covariate. The article notes this exposure was "naturally occurring rather than experimentally assigned"; no effect size is printed.

> "After adjustment for postintervention delirium care experience, participants who had cared for patients with delirium reported higher confidence at follow‐up than those without such experience (B = 0.79, 95% CI [0.03, 1.54], p = 0.040)."
