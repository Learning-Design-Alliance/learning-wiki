q2 · i? · causal · r1

Linear regression of the 11-item Patient History Information Quiz (immediate and 2-week delayed, 52 observations) with time and condition as predictors. Immediate means were 9.40 (ePBLM) and 10.10 (genAI) of 11; delayed 7.94 and 8.63. The condition coefficient was 0.69, p = .052 — not significant.

"A significant effect was found for time only (Table 4). Across conditions, students’ recall decreased significantly from immediate to delayed assessment, and the rate of forgetting did not differ by condition."
