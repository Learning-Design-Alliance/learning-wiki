Sarah E Jones, Penny K Campbell, Alexander J Kimp, Kim Bennell, Nadine E Foster, Trevor Russell, Rana S Hinman. (2021). Evaluation of a Novel e-Learning Program for Physiotherapists to Manage Knee Osteoarthritis via Telehealth: Qualitative Study Nested in the PEAK (Physiotherapy Exercise and Physical Activity for Knee Osteoarthritis) Randomized Controlled Trial. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 1 of the thematic analysis of the 15 physiotherapist interviews. Participants valued the self-paced web-based structure, while 3 physiotherapists described the modules as "slow going", "tedious", or "fatiguing".

> "the self-paced nature of the program was highly regarded and enabled the physiotherapists to fit the training into their busy daily lives and complete it as time allowed"
