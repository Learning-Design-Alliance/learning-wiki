Kehagias D., Baldari L., Cassinotti E., Boni L., Lampropoulos C., Kehagias I. (2025). Learning curve for lateral lymph node dissection in rectal cancer – a systematic review of literature. Techniques in Coloproctology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10151-025-03214-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A systematic review of eight retrospective studies using the CUSUM method found that

> "Regardless of learning outcome, surgical competence for robotic LLND was achieved after 12–53 cases, for laparoscopic LLND after 19 cases, and for the open approach no inflection point was identified."
