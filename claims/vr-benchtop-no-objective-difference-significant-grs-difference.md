The review reports, citing Middleton et al., a comparison of VR-trained versus bench-top-trained subjects on a final VR simulator wireless objective motion analysis assessment. Objective metrics showed no difference; GRS showed a significant difference.

> "Middleton et al. [35] found that there was no difference in objective performance between virtual reality (VR) trained and bench-top trained subjects on the final VR simulator wireless objective motion analysis assessment, but a significant difference was seen in the GRS."
