Evidence¶

Krieglstein 2023¶

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Rey, G. D., Sanchez-Stockhammer, C., & Schneider, S. (2023). Development and Validation of a Theory-Based Questionnaire to Measure Different Types of Cognitive Load. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09738-0

q2 · i? · design · r3

Confirmatory factor analysis with robust ML estimation on 15 items, 158 Prolific participants rating a nerve-cell learning material. The article states "The initial model showed an acceptable fit of the data" with the printed indices.

"The initial model showed an acceptable fit of the data (CFI = 0.92; RMSEA = 0.10; GFI = 0.92; SRMR = 0.07). Factor loadings revealed that all items load on the intended factor ( p < 0.001)."

q2 · i? · design · r3

Convergent validity check in Study 2 correlating the proposed instrument with the Klepsch et al. (2017) questionnaire. The article reports "evidence for moderate convergent validity could be found", with ECL satisfactory, ICL lower but acceptable, and GCL lowest.

"In sum, evidence for moderate convergent validity could be found."
