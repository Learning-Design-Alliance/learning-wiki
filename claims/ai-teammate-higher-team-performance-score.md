## Evidence

### Gardella 2026

Gardella, N., Prather, J., Leinonen, J., Denny, P., Pettit, R., & Riggs, S. L. (2026). Fast and Forgettable: A Controlled Study of Novices' Performance, Learning, Workload, and Emotion in AI-Assisted and Human Pair Programming Paradigms. [doi:10.1145/3765964.3811664](https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811664)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Regression of treatment on Session 1 team performance in the within-subjects study of 22 participants programming HumanEval tasks under time pressure. The AI-teammate coefficient was 14.09 points of 100 with "<.001 <.001 .99" for p, adjusted p, and Hedges' g = .99, a large effect.

> "Actual Team Performance (Session 1) Constant 74.30 5.08 62.67 84.05 Teammate = AI 14.09 3.96 6.46 22.89 <.001 <.001 .99"
