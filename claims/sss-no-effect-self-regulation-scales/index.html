Evidence¶

Webb 2014¶

Webb, L., Carey, J., Villares, E., Wells, C., & Sayer, A. (2014). Results of a Randomized Controlled Trial of Student Success Skills. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562995

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Null findings from the HLM analyses of student self-rating and teacher-rating scales in the cluster-randomized trial; no effect sizes are printed for these outcomes, so equivalence was not tested. The authors flag possible "instrumentation issues with the Student Engagement in School Success Skills survey."

"these HLM analyses failed to find evidence that SSS influenced students' Metacognitive Activity, Self-Efficacy, Self-Management of Learning, Support of Classmates' Learning, Self-Regulation of Arousal, or Self-Efficacy for Self Regulated Learning, Inattention or Self-Control."
