Spearman correlation analysis of ground-truth metrics across the 543-problem dataset, visualized in a force-directed Fruchterman–Reingold layout (Fig. 6). The article prints ρ = 0 .43 for chosen with includes-solution-strategy.

Same correlation analysis. The article prints ρ = −0.71 with includes-solution-strategy and ρ = −0.68 with Bloom level, mirroring that radio items are less likely to be selected when a structured quantitative alternative exists.

> "problem-type-is-radio, which correlates inversely with nearly every node in Cluster A (“negative hub”), most pronounced with includes-solution-strategy (ρ = −0.71) and Bloom level ( ρ = −0.68)."
