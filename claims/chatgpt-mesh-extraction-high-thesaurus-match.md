## Evidence

### Roselló Atanet Iván 2025

Roselló Atanet Iván, Tomova Mihaela, Sieg Miriam, Sehy Victoria, Mäder Patrick, März Maren. (2025). Generating learning guides for medical education with LLMs and statistical analysis of test results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06978-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of ChatGPT 4.0 output for all 200 PT46 questions, matched against the 2024 MeSH thesaurus. The study reports "1,354 (83%) fully matched descriptors", with further partial and entry-term matches; the 169 non-matching terms were discarded.

> "Out of the 1,639 concepts and keywords extracted, 169 (10%) did not match any descriptors or entry terms, while 1,354 (83%) fully matched descriptors included in the current MeSH thesaurus."

### Roselló Atanet Iván 2025 (2)

Roselló Atanet Iván, Tomova Mihaela, Sieg Miriam, Sehy Victoria, Mäder Patrick, März Maren. (2025). Generating learning guides for medical education with LLMs and statistical analysis of test results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06978-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Manual validation of ChatGPT-extracted terms for 28 randomly chosen questions (two per organ system). The authors report "215 out of 216 concepts and keywords (99.54%)" related to the questions' medical content.

> "Therefore, 215 out of 216 concepts and keywords (99.54%) were found to be related to the medical content of their associated questions; the remaining keyword is also related to its associated question, but refers to the scenario described in the vignette"
