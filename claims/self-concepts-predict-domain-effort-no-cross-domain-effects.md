## Evidence

### Lohbeck 2019

Annette Lohbeck. (2019). Social and dimensional comparison effects on academic self-concepts and self-perceptions of effort in elementary school children. Educational Psychology, 39:1, 133-150. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2018.1527018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (Model 3) in the same 200-child sample: self-concept-to-effort paths within domain were significant (e.g. MSC to MSE b = 0.77, GSC to GSE b = 0.66 for competence models), while cross-domain paths were non-significant or slightly positive. The article calls the absence of negative cross-domain effects "a novel finding".

> "Irrespective of the component of academic self-concepts, both academic self-concepts showed significantly positive effects on self-perceptions of effort of corresponding domains, while there were no significantly negative cross-domain self-concept effects on self-perceptions of effort. The effects of academic self-concepts were even (significantly) positive."
