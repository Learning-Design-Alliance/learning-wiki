## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kenny Yu 2024

Kenny Yu, Wolf Vanpaemel, Francis Tuerlinckx & Jonas Zaman. (2024). The representational instability in the generalization of fear learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00287-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports that incorporating perceptual memory data into perceptual distance calculations "did not enhance model performance"; three perceptual-distance assumptions with different memory treatments were compared in the mixture framework.

> "Although most participants demonstrated generalization aligned with perceptual rather than physical stimulus distances, incorporating perceptual memory data into perceptual distance calculations did not enhance model performance."
