Retrospective multicenter analysis (LOCALIZATION01, NCT05942105) of 3,195 patients across 12 Breast Units, comparing margin status by operating surgeon level within each localization technique; per-technique comparisons in Table 3 were non-significant (e.g., ROLL p = 0.30, MSL p = 0.11). The article reports "no statistically significant differences in surgical margin status" between the two surgeon groups.
