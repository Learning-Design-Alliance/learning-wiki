## Evidence

### McGlashan H.L. 2017

McGlashan H.L., Blanchard C.C.V., Sycamore N.J., Lee R., French B., Holmes N.P. (2017). Improvement in children's fine motor skills following a computerized typing intervention. Human Movement Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2017.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on serial reaction time task Z-scores for 21 children (intervention n = 7, control n = 14) retested after the intervention period. The article reports no significant interactions involving group; the slope ANOVA likewise showed no time × group interaction (F(1,20) = 0.23, p = .64).

> "However, there were no significant interactions between condition and group, F(1,20) = 1.07, p = .313, time and group, F(1,20) = 0.343, p = .564, condition and time, F(1,20) = 0.344, p = .564, or condition, time, and group, F(1,20) = 1.49, p = .236."
