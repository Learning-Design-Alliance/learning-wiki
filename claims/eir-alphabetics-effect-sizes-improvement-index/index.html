Evidence¶

Early Intervention in Reading® 2008¶

Early Intervention in Reading®. (2008). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.earlyinterventioninreading.com

q3 · i? · causal · r?

WWC calculations for the Taylor et al. (1991) randomized controlled trial report that the "average improvement index for alphabetics is +36 percentile points" with a range of +29 to +42; the improvement index is based only on effect size, regardless of significance or design.

"The average improvement index for alphabetics is +36 percentile points for the one study, with a range of +29 to +42 percentile points across findings."

Early Intervention in Reading® 2008 (2)¶

Early Intervention in Reading®. (2008). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.earlyinterventioninreading.com

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

WWC calculations for the Taylor et al. (1991) randomized controlled trial (Appendix A3.1) report a vowel-sounds mean difference of 4.18 favoring EIR®, an "effect size" of 1.39, statistically significant, with an improvement index of +42.

"Vowel sounds Grade 1 12/56 10.62 (3.18) 6.44 (2.72) 4.18 1.39 Statistically significant +42"
