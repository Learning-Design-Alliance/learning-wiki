### Kienitz 2023

Kienitz, A., Krebs, M.-C., & Eitel, A. (2023). Seductive details hamper learning even when they do not disrupt. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09632-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The ANCOVA on transfer scores found no significant difference between control and no-prompt conditions (F(3,108) = 2.19, p = .142, ηp2 = 0.020). The authors attribute this null to the transfer test's low internal consistency (ω = 0.56).

> "Against our hypothesis, our data did not show a seductive details effect for transfer. We suspect this to be the case because of low internal consistency of the multiple-choice transfer test (ω = 0.56)."
