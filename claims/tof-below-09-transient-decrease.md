## Evidence

### Thomsen JLD 2022

Thomsen JLD, Mathiesen O, Hägi‐Pedersen D, Skovgaard LT, Østergaard D, Gätke MR; the INVERT collaborator group. (2022). Improving neuromuscular monitoring and reducing residual neuromuscular blockade via e‐learning: A multicentre interrupted time‐series study (INVERT study). Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interrupted time-series analysis of all departments combined for the proportion with last recorded TOF ratio <0.9, which had already fallen from 31.8% to 22.2% pre-intervention (−6.6% per year, p < .001). The post-intervention decrease was transient. The authors attribute the transient effect to the Hawthorne effect.

> "There was a sudden further negative change after the intervention (p = .007), but it was followed by a positive trend post‐intervention (p = .006)."
