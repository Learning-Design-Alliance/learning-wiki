# German physics teacher education follows a stable two-phase structure of university study followed by a structured induction phase

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The typical German pathway to teaching comprises a university preparation phase followed by a structured induction phase of one and a half to two years at a seminar and school. [→ Ben Van Dusen 2021](#ben-van-dusen-2021)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2021

Ben Van Dusen, Christoph Vogelsang, Joseph Taylor, and Eva Cauet. (2021). How to Teach a Teacher: Challenges and Opportunities in Physics Teacher Education in Germany and the USA. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-87391-2_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-87391-2_3)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive account of the German teacher education pathway, similar across federal states. The induction phase lasts one and a half to two years, with trainees teaching at schools under mentors while attending seminar courses, ending in the second state examination. The quote confirms the two-phase structure: "studies at a university followed by a structured induction".

> "Teacher education is organised in two consecutive phases. The first phase of initial preparation involves studies at a university followed by a structured induction to the field at a particular school in the second phase."
