## Evidence

### Rachel E. Scherr 2001

Rachel E. Scherr, Peter S. Shaffer, and Stamatis Vokos. (2001). Student understanding of time in special relativity: simultaneity and reference frames. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1371254

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of written responses and interview transcripts across student groups reveals that "many students very strongly associate the time of an event with the time at which an observer receives a signal from the event." This error appeared in both graduate and introductory students.

> "The responses to the written questions and interviews show that many students very strongly associate the time of an event with the time at which an observer receives a signal from the event."
