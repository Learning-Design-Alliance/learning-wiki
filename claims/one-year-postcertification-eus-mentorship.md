Rationale given by the Delphi panel for its recommendation (strong, very low evidence, 89% consensus) that "Newly certified EUS practitioners should have a minimum period of mentorship lasting 1 year." Certified practitioners should perform 100 cases per year with audit of KPIs, M&M presentation, 360 assessments and annual appraisal.

> "Performance of EUS continues to improve after certification during the early part of independent practice before aspirational standards may be reached, it follows therefore that there should be provision for mentorship and performance review for recently certified EUS practitioners."
