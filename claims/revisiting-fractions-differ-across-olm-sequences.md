## Evidence

### Tom Zhang 2021

Tom Zhang, Michelle Taub, and Zhongzhou Chen. (2021). Measuring the Impact of COVID-19 Induced Campus Closure on Student Self-Regulated Learning in Physics Online Learning Modules. Learning Analytics and Knowledge '21. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1145/3448139.3448150](https://doi.org/10.1145/3448139.3448150)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cochran's Q test on the fraction of students with at least one revisiting event per OLM sequence, followed by pairwise McNemar tests. The article reports that in 2020 S1 and S8 revisiting fractions were significantly higher than the rest, S5 lower than all except S4, and in 2019 S4 and S5 significantly lower than the rest.

> "Cochran’s Q test indicated that there were significant differences between the OLM sequences in each semester, 𝜒 22020(8) = 222.514, 𝑝 < 0.001 and𝜒 22019(8) = 92.888, 𝑝 < 0.001."
