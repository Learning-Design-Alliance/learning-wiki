# Using games in mathematics teaching has a medium positive effect (g = 0.792) on academic achievement in Turkey, per random-effects meta-analysis

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The average effect size of game use on mathematics academic achievement is 0.792 (SE = 0.077) under a random-effects model, a medium effect by the article's criteria. [→ Sedat Turgut 2017](#sedat-turgut-2017)

## Evidence

### Sedat Turgut 2017

Sedat Turgut, Özlem Dogan Temur. (2017). The Effect of Game-Assisted Mathematics Education on Academic Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017236115

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 31 effect sizes from 26 experimental pretest-posttest studies in Turkey, computed with Hedges's g at 95% confidence in CMA and MetaWin. The pooled "0.792" effect was classified as "medium effect according to Cohen et. al. (2007)" by the authors.

> "The average effect size value was calculated as 0.792 with 0. 077 standard error according to random effect model."
