### Chang Yen-Lin 2026

Chang Yen-Lin, Lin Yi-Chen, Hung Liang-Ching, Chen Hsiu-Mei, Hsieh Tsu-Yi, Yen Chih-Ho. (2026). Virtual reality simulation for chemotherapy drug spill management training in pharmacy education: A cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09864-7

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory Mann–Whitney U comparison of second-phase change scores after crossover in 37 pharmacy interns; p values ranged from 0.221 to 0.956 across the four items. Median second-phase changes were 0.0 in both cohorts for Q1–Q3. No effect sizes printed.

> "Following the crossover, no statistically significant between-cohort differences in second-phase change scores (T2 − T1) were observed for any of the four items (p values ranged from 0.221 to 0.956; Table 5)."

### Chang Yen-Lin 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
