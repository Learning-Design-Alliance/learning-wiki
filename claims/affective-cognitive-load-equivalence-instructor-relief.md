### Fazlollahi AM et al 2022

Fazlollahi AM et al. (2022). Effect of Artificial Intelligence Tutoring vs Expert Instruction on Learning Simulated Surgical Skills Among Medical Students: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.49008

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Cognitive Load Index self-reports after training across the three randomized arms found no significant between-group differences in intrinsic, extrinsic, or germane load; the article reports no effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "No between-participant difference in intrinsic, extrinsic, and germane cognitive load were found."

### Fazlollahi AM et al 2022 (2)

Fazlollahi AM et al. (2022). Effect of Artificial Intelligence Tutoring vs Expert Instruction on Learning Simulated Surgical Skills Among Medical Students: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.49008

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Medical Emotion Scale self-reports before, during, and after training, analyzed by two-way mixed ANOVA. The instructor group reported significantly stronger positive deactivating emotions than both the VOA and control groups, though this did not translate into greater performance. No standardized effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The significant interaction effect in positive deactivating emotions demonstrated that instructor group participants felt more relieved and relaxed during training compared with learners in VOA (mean difference, 0.75 [95% CI, 0.19 to 1.31] points; P = .006) and control groups (mean difference, 0.71 [95% CI, 0.14 to 1.27] points; P = .01)"
