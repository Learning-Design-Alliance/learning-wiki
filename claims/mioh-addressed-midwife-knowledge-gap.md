## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On completing the MIOH course, midwives identified a gap in their prior knowledge of the impact of oral health on pregnancy outcomes and reported new knowledge gains, including mother-to-child transmission of cariogenic bacteria. [→ Heilbrunn-Lang 2015](#heilbrunn-lang-2015)

## Evidence

### Heilbrunn-Lang 2015

Heilbrunn-Lang, de Silva, Lang, George, Ridge, Johnson, Bhole, Gilmour. (2015). Midwives’ perspectives of their ability to promote the oral health of pregnant women in Victoria, Australia. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-015-0536-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses, feedback forms and emails from the Victorian pilot; midwives reported new knowledge of physiological changes in pregnancy, pregnancy-specific oral care, and "the transmission of mutans streptococci to infants from the mother".

> "On completion of the MIOH course midwives identified and discussed a gap in their prior knowledge and understanding of the potential impact of oral health on pregnancy outcomes for the mother and baby, and the lack of awareness of the importance of maternal oral health."
