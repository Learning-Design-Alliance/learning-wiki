Shan Li 2026¶

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Narrative review attribution within the coaching section on positive reinforcement. The article adds that when learners have no motivation at all, external rewards may serve as an appropriate starting point, and recommends informational feedback regardless of initial motivational state.

"Research has shown that external rewards like gifts and prizes can undermine intrinsic motivation and impede long-term motivational development for learners who are already motivated (Deci, 1971; Hendijani et al., 2016)."
