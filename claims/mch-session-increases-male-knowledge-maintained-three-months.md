## Evidence

### Kadomoto Noriko 2011

Kadomoto Noriko, Iwasa Hajime, Takahashi Miyako, Dulnuan Marcelyn M, Kai Ichiro. (2011). Ifugao males, learning and teaching for the improvement of maternal and child health status in the Philippines: an evaluation of a program. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-11-280

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures analysis of variance on Group 1 (N = 140 males completing all three surveys) after a five-hour MCH session with PowerPoint and participatory activities. The article reports "a significant increase in overall knowledge" with F = 54.97, p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant increase in overall knowledge at T1-T2 and T1-T3 (F = 54.97, p < 0.001). After analyzing this result per questions, 14 out of 17 showed this effect."
