# All six GenAI-SRL dimensions correlate positively with learners' perceived writing improvement (r = 0.275–0.338)

> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xiaoqi Wang 2026

Xiaoqi Wang, Lawrence Jun Zhang, Yanan Zhang. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence-supported self-regulated learning (GenAI-SRL) in L2 writing: scale development, validation, and short-form construction. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-026-09481-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Criterion-validity analysis using Pearson correlations between the six GenAI-SRL dimensions and a three-item perceived writing gains measure (α = 0.861) on Sample two. The article reports "all six dimensions correlated positively with perceived improvement" (r = 0.275–0.338, p < 0.001); inter-dimension correlations were 0.428–0.558.

> "all six dimensions correlated positively with perceived improvement (r = 0.275–0.338, p < 0.001). Correlations among the six dimensions ranged from 0.428 to 0.558 (p < 0.001)"
