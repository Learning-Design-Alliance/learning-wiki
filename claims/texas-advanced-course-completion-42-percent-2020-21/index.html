Kristen Hengtgen 2024¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2024). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework in Texas. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641978

q2 · i? · associational · r2

EdTrust analysis of Texas Education Agency Texas Academic Performance Reports comparing 2018-19 and 2020-21 statewide completion, finding completion "fell only slightly, with 42% of students completing an advanced course in 2020-21." The report attributes the decrease to widening disparities in supports.

"From 2018-19 to 2020-21, the first full school year navigating pandemic restrictions, advanced coursework completion fell only slightly, with 42% of students completing an advanced course in 2020-21."
