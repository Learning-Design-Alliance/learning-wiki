# A high proportion (median 54%) of teacher questions in CBL seminars receive no student response

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Median non-response to teacher questions across seminars was 54%; elaborative and reproductive responses were each about 17%. [→ Gartmeier Martin 2019](#gartmeier-martin-2019)

## Evidence

### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Pfurtscheller Theresa, Hapfelmeier Alexander, Grünewald Marc, Häusler Janina, Seidel Tina, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Teacher questions and student responses in case-based learning: outcomes of a video study in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1895-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results on student reactions (Fig. 4, RQ2) across 19 seminars with 1688 coded question–response events: teacher questions "remained unanswered by students (MD = 54%)" while elaborative and reproductive responses were each at MD = 17%.

> "it is apparent that a relatively high degree of teacher questions remained unanswered by students (MD = 54%), whereas the amount of elaborative and reproductive responses was almost equal (MD = 17%)"

### Gartmeier Martin 2019 (2)

Gartmeier Martin, Pfurtscheller Theresa, Hapfelmeier Alexander, Grünewald Marc, Häusler Janina, Seidel Tina, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Teacher questions and student responses in case-based learning: outcomes of a video study in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1895-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Discussion restates the overall non-response rate of 54% and notes the phenomenon is under-researched in medical education and contradicts descriptions of CBL as interactive and student-centred.

> "Student non-response is a phenomenon which is not well researched in medical education, but which was observed with a high frequency (54% overall) in the present study"
