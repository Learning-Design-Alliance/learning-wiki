## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2012

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2012). Using an Isomorphic Problem Pair to Learn Introductory Physics: Transferring from a Two-step Problem to a Three-step Problem. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Recitation-quiz study with 202 algebra-based students randomly divided into a comparison group and three intervention groups; rubric scores rose significantly (p < 0.05) from 2.5 to 4.4, 5.4, and 5.2 out of 10. Only a p-value is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "In the algebra-based course, even though the scores went up significantly (p < 0.05) from 2.5 (in the comparison group) to 4.4, 5.4, and 5.2 in the three intervention groups, respectively, there is still much room for improvement."
