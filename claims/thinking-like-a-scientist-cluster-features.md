### Amin Bayat Barooni 2018

Amin Bayat Barooni, Joshua Von Korff, Brian D. Thoms, Zeynep Topdemir, Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2018). Three research-based approaches to the design of physics activities. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cluster-level pattern of coding frequencies from the k-means analysis of 66 activities. The authors state these features show "an emphasis on students performing scientific practices such as experiment design, reaching decisions by collaboration, and examining results and processes for accuracy".

> "Compared with the other clusters, the most frequent features observed were Design, Produce Spoken Representation, and Qualifications (as described in Table I)."

### Amin Bayat Barooni 2018 (2)

Amin Bayat Barooni, Joshua Von Korff, Brian D. Thoms, Zeynep Topdemir, Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2018). Three research-based approaches to the design of physics activities. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cluster membership reported in the Results section and Table I. The most distinct features versus the other two clusters are "the prevalence of the Design and Qualifications features", tied to designers' goals of scientific process skills.

> "Activities in this cluster include all six CL, five SCL, and three ISLE labs (Table I)."
