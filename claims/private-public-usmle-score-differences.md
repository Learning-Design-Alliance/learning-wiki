`q2 i?` Across 2012–2014, private schools' mean Step 1 (233.1) and Step 2 CK (240.0) scores exceeded public schools' (227.5 and 237.2), with P < .001 and P < .01 respectively; private schools' mean MCAT was 33.5 vs. 30.9 for public schools. [→ Burk-Rafel J 2019](#burk-rafel-j-2019)
