## Evidence

### Zheng 2020

Zheng, Binbin, Ward, Amy, & Stanulis, Randi. (2020). Self-regulated learning in a competency-based and flipped learning environment: learning strategies across achievement levels and years. Medical Education Online, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1686949

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focused-coding content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 26 first- and second-year medical students about their pre-class independent learning, coded against a modified SRL framework. The study reports "the most frequently used learning strategy was adjustment (73%), followed by task analysis (62%)"; monitoring strategies organizing (11%), summarizing (8%), and applying (4%) were least reported. Percentages are descriptive; no effect sizes or inferential tests were printed.

> "the most frequently used learning strategy was adjustment (73%), followed by task analysis (62%), connecting (31%), and self-evaluation (19%). These learning strategies fall under the phases of planning and reflection."
