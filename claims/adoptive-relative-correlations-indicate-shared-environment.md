Beyond additive genetic effects: Explaining family resemblance in school performance across millions of pairs of Norwegian relatives. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Registry-based correlation analysis of adoptive relative pairs on national test scores; Fisherian models predict adoptive relatives to be uncorrelated. The article reports adoptive sibling correlations "at ~0.15" and notes adoption selection could mean the shared-environmental component is larger.

> "The correlations between adoptive siblings, at ~0.15, are hard to explain without environmental effects. And the true shared-environmental variance component could also be larger than this"
