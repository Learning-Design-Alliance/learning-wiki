## Evidence

### Jeong Somi 2025

Jeong Somi, Kim Seung Jung, Lee Kyoung Eun, Ko Ara. (2025). The moderating effect of team-based learning participation frequency on the relationship between medical students’ perception of systematic team-based learning class management and active class participation. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis (PROCESS Macro model 1) of feedback data from 575 medical students, controlling for academic year and semester. The regression explained 18.84% of variance (F=26.417, p<0.001); the "interaction term" was significant (β=–0.214, p<0.001), adding 2.7% explanatory power (F=15.863, p<0.001).

> "Importantly, the interaction term between perception of systematic TBL management and TBL participation frequency was also significant (β=–0.214, p<0.001), indicating that the relationship between systematic TBL management and active participation is moderated by participation frequency."
