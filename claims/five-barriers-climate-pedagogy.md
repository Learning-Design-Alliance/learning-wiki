A qualitative synthesis by the author, drawing on cited literature (e.g., Leiserowitz et al.; Cook et al.) and classroom experience, identifying knowledge pollution, disciplinary silos, emotional trauma, paradigm-bound thinking, and lack of faculty training as barriers. The article names "significant conceptual confusion and ignorance" as the first barrier.
