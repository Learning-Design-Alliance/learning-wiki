Evidence¶

Amy Ogan & W. Lewis Johnson 2015¶

Amy Ogan & W. Lewis Johnson. (2015). Preface for the Special Issue on Culturally Aware Educational Technologies. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0036-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, describing Ogan et al. (2014), that cross-context comparison of ITS help-seeking models found them "not transferrable across contexts"; the editors attribute the result to that paper, not to their own analysis.

"It compares models of help-seeking skill based on student practice in an intelligent tutoring system in three different contexts: Costa Rica, the Philippines, and a site in the country where the system was originally developed, the United States, and finds that the models are not transferrable across contexts."
