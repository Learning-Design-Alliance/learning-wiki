Matched-cohort secondary outcome analysis of clinical specialty grouped into general practice/emergency, surgery, and physician training. The quote names the surgical-training association and the null findings for the other specialties; Table 3 prints OR 1.11 (P = 0.792) for emergency/GP and OR 1.13 (P = 0.722) for physician.
