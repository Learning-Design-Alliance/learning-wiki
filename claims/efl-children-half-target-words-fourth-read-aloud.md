Descriptive result from the quasi-experimental read-aloud study with 45 EFL fourth graders: mean target-word score of 7.0 (maximum 16) at the fourth session, which the article compares to Elley's (1989) 40% gains under an explanation condition.

> "The overall mean number of the target words correctly identified by the children in the fourth session was 7.0, suggesting that the EFL children in this study learned almost half of the target words meanings by the fourth listening."
