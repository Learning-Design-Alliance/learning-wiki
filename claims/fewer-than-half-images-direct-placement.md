## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Jee 2022

Benjamin D. Jee, Bryan J. Matlen, Monica Greenlaw, Nina Simms, Dedre Gentner. (2022). Spatial supports for comparison in educational science images. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09599-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of spatial placement in the 103-image working data set (after excluding four images with extraordinarily high numbers of unit pairs and three broad array comparisons). The authors report "fewer than half had an optimal layout for comparison," and note 25 of 103 images had one or more impeded placements.

> "if we consider the number of images with direct placements only (n = 47) relative to the total number of images involving comparison (n = 103), the results seem less favorable: fewer than half had an optimal layout for comparison."
