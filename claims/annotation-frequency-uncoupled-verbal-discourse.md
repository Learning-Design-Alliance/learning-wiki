## Evidence

### Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang 2026

Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang. (2026). From individual annotations to shared attention: How re-annotation supports collaborative knowledge building in CSCL. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09486-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null result from the group-level Kendall's tau correlation analysis in RQ2: the rank of initiating/re-annotation frequency did not significantly correspond to the rank of frequency of codes such as confirming, modifying, or introducing new knowledge. Equivalence was not tested; the authors interpret annotation and discussion as "distinct yet complementary processes".

> "Interestingly, the correlation analysis further showed no significant associations between annotation behaviors and verbal knowledge construction codes when aggregated across groups (Fig. 5)."
