### Gamble Blakey Althea 2019

Gamble Blakey Althea, Smith-Han Kelby, Anderson Lynley, Collins Emma, Berryman Elizabeth, Wilkinson Tim. (2019). “They cared about us students:” learning from exemplar clinical teaching environments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1551-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2, emphasised universally by all staff from both ECA departments across the six focus groups. Shared tasks included organising rosters, supervising tasks, giving tutorials, and reassuring students they could seek help from any staff member; no effect size is printed.

> "Specifically, staff reported that they all had a role in organising/teaching medical students, to include administration staff. Responsibility for student teaching was therefore collective"
