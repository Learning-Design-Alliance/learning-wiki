## Evidence

### Hale Caleb 2021

Hale Caleb, Crocker Jonathan, Vanka Anita, Ricotta Daniel N, McSparron Jakob I, Huang Grace C. (2021). Cohort study of hospitalists' procedural skills: baseline competence and durability after simulation-based training. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8370503/

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' report of self-reported prior-year procedure counts in the Discussion, corroborated by their own 2012 billing audit (2.7 LP, 1.9 AP); they suggest hospitalists have scarce opportunity for the booster effect of procedural volume. Procedural volume was not tracked between training and retesting.

> "the number of procedures our participants self-reported in the year prior to our study were quite low (2.8 for LP, 2.2 for AP), and these numbers are similar in magnitude to an audit we conducted in 2012 (average 2.7 for LP, 1.9 for AP among hospitalists who billed for procedures)"
