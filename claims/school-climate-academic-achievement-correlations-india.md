## Evidence

### Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi 2025

Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi, Navaneetham Janardhana, Bhola Poornima. (2025). Influences of school climate on emotional health and academic achievement of school-going adolescents in India: a systematic review. BMC Public Health 25, 54. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-21268-0

`q2 · i3` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional survey study (Gaur 2010, N = 300, Meerut) using the Sharma learning climate scale and Raven's Progressive Matrices; the review reports grade means of 73.39, 67.76, and 65.17 across high, average, and low learning climates and a correlation of 0.55. A second included study (Bhat & Mir 2018, N = 240) reported r = 0.637 at the 0.01 level.

> "Students in schools with a high learning climate achieved significantly higher grades (average of 73.39) compared to those in schools with average (67.76) and low (65.17) learning climates. There's a strong positive correlation (0.55) between the learning climate of the school and student academic achievement."
