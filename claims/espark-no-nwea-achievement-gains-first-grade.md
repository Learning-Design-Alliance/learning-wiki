Analysis of NWEA reading percentiles from fall, winter, and spring assessments for the eSpark cohort at Avonworth. Fall mean percentile was 76.43 (SD 22.49), winter 71.42, spring 70.74; the report states "we do not see patterns of increasing student achievement scores in NWEA data."
