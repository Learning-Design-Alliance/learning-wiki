Evidence¶

Wang 2010¶

Yushan Wang. (2010). To Give Control to Learners or Not? A Comparative Study of Two Ways of Teaching Listening. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 2. http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive questionnaire results from the experimental group: Item 7 (I can have good learning autonomy without teachers) averaged 2.66 and Item 13 (ability to plan listening study outside classroom) averaged 2.58 on a 1-5 scale. The article reports learners "still want to have teachers’ help in their studies".

"From Item 7 and Item 13 we know that learners still want to have teachers’ help in their studies. Few students thought that they had had good learning autonomy and they thought that they needed teachers’ help to plan their study."
