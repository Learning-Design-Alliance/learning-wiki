## Evidence

### Jussila Juuso J 2023

Jussila Juuso J, Pulakka Anna, Halonen Jaana I, Salo Paula, Allaouat Sara, Mikkonen Santtu, Lanki Timo. (2023). Are active school transport and leisure-time physical activity associated with performance and wellbeing at secondary school? A population-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression in the cross-sectional cohort found a "positive linear association between AST and school enjoyment", with odds rising monotonically from OR 1.16 (95% CI 1.07–1.25) to OR 1.31 (95% CI 1.19–1.45) across active transport dose categories versus non-active transport.

> "We also observed a positive linear association between AST and school enjoyment (figure 2). The largest dose, more than 30 min a day, was associated with 31% higher odds (OR 1.31, 95% CI 1.19–1.45), followed by 10–30 min (OR 1.25, 95% CI 1.16–1.34) and less than 10 min (OR 1.16, 95% CI 1.07–1.25)."
