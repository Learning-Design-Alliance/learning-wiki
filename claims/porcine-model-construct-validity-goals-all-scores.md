## Evidence

### Higuchi M 2020

Higuchi M, Abe T, Hotta K, et al. (2020). Development and validation of a porcine organ model for training in essential laparoscopic surgical skills. International Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1111/iju.14315

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of 54 participants (13 experts, 8 intermediates, 33 novices) completing three laparoscopic tasks using porcine organs. Two blinded expert raters scored video recordings using GOALS and the newly developed ALL score. The article reports 'significant differences both in GOALS and ALL scores among the three groups in all three tasks.'

> "There were significant differences both in GOALS and ALL scores among the three groups in all three tasks, although the difference between experts and intermediates was marginal."
