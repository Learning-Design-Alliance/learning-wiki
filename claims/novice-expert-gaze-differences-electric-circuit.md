# Novices fixate the middle capacitor circuit while experts also attend the upper coil circuit; upper-circuit focus correlates weakly, non-significantly with electricity knowledge (r=0.41)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Gaze heatmaps showed novices focused mainly on the middle circuit part with capacitors and the middle lamp while experts also focused on the upper circuit (upper lamp and coil). [→ D. Sonntag 2021](#d-sonntag-2021)
`q2 i3` Quantitatively, focus on the upper circuit correlated weakly and not significantly with knowledge in electricity theory (r=0.41, p=0.08). [→ D. Sonntag 2021 (2)](#d-sonntag-2021-2)

## Evidence

### D. Sonntag 2021

D. Sonntag, O. Bodensiek. (2021). EYE-TRACKING-BASED DESIGN OF MIXED REALITY LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS IN STEM. Technische Universität Braunschweig. [doi:10.21125/inted.2021.1990](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2021.1990)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative eye-tracking analysis comparing gaze heatmaps of visual attention and learning paths of novices and experts during the circuit experiment, interpreted via the eye-mind hypothesis: "the novices were focused mainly on the middle circuit part".

> "As shown in the heatmaps in Figure 1 the novices were focused mainly on the middle circuit part with the capacitors and the middle lamp while experts also focus on the upper circuit (upper lamp and coil)."

### D. Sonntag 2021 (2)

D. Sonntag, O. Bodensiek. (2021). EYE-TRACKING-BASED DESIGN OF MIXED REALITY LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS IN STEM. Technische Universität Braunschweig. [doi:10.21125/inted.2021.1990](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2021.1990)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Quantitative analysis of fixation frequencies in defined areas of interest (upper, middle, lower circuit, single components) comparing novices with experts found "a weak, however not significant, positive correlation" between upper-circuit focus and electricity knowledge, r=0.41.

> "From the quantitative evaluation of the eye -tracking data we found a weak, however not significant, positive correlation between the focus on the upper circuit and their knowledge in electricity theory  (r=0.41, p=0.08) ."
