### Solaini L. 2023

Solaini L., Cavaliere D., Rocco G., Avanzolini A., Di Pietrantonio D., Ercolani G. (2023). Differences in the learning curve of robotic transabdominal preperitoneal inguinal hernia repair according to surgeon's robotic experience. Hernia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10029-023-02846-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-learning-phase comparison (Table 2; 26 expert vs 43 trainee procedures): skin-to-skin time was 52 min (45–65) versus 65 min (53–75), "significantly shorter than the one of the surgeon in training" (p = 0.0001).

> "the skin-to-skin operative time of the experienced robotic surgeon was significantly shorter than the one of the surgeon in training with the robotic platform (52 min, 45–65 versus 65, 53–75; p = 0.0001)"
