### Fairburn Christopher G. 2017

Fairburn Christopher G., Patel Vikram. (2017). The impact of digital technology on psychological treatments and their dissemination. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2016.08.012

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article's digital training section states that conventional therapist training is not scalable and is a barrier to dissemination, and cites testing of a training website for enhanced CBT for eating disorders in a proof-of-concept study, a North American RCT, and a global cohort study with over 750 clinicians.

> "Training research can now be conducted on a much larger scale than before as digital training allows large numbers of geographically dispersed clinicians to be trained simultaneously. For example, a training website for enhanced cognitive behaviour therapy for eating disorders has been tested in a proof-of-concept study across an entire country; a RCT across North America; and in a global cohort study involving over 750 clinicians."
