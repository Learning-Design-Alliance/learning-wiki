## Evidence

### Jenny van Dongen et al 2018

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Overlap analysis of the 58 education-associated sites against published EWAS meta-analyses of individual smoking (18,760 sites) and prenatal maternal smoking (6073 sites). Mediation analysis showed smoking mediated 7–47% (average 28%) of the education–methylation effect; 11 CpGs were genome-wide significant after adjusting for smoking.

> "all educational attainment associated sites are also associated with individual smoking and 29 (50%) are currently known to be associated with maternal prenatal smoking"
