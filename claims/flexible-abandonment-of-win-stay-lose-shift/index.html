Yajing Zhang 2023¶

Yajing Zhang, Thi Kim Truc Huynh and Benjamin James Dyson. (2023). Deliberately making miskates: Behavioural consistency under win maximization and loss maximization conditions. npj Science of Learning 8:55. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00206-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation (Discussion) of the four-experiment series, in which participants under loss maximization needed win-shift/lose-stay strategies against repetition opponents and deployed them, showing "a remarkable ease with which we can switch out of putatively fundamental operant conditional outcome-action associations".

Related Claims¶

  • In loss maximization, the pattern reverses: performance is more consistent following losses than wins — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Win-stay/lose-shift associations are not privileged: consistency sometimes favours win-shift, and reaction times show no outcome-by-action interaction — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In win maximization, performance is more consistent following wins than losses in both repetition- and alternation-exploitable opponents — related
  • Behavioural consistency shows a double dissociation: actions following wins are more consistent during win maximization, and actions following losses are more consistent during loss maximization — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Win-calmness appears only against exploitable opponents; exploiting opponents instead reproduce the reversed pattern — related
  • Against unexploitable opponents, neither win-calmness nor loss-restlessness appears; calmness is instead lower after wins and restlessness lower after losses — related
  • All groups, including individuals with Down, Fragile X, and Williams syndromes, adapted their choices based on deterministic feedback, staying with rewarded options and switching after losses — related
  • Calmness against unexploitable opponents rises when they are played in the context of exploitable, but not exploiting, opponents — related
