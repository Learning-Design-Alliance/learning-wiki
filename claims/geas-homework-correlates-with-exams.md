## Evidence

### Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise 2015

Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise. (2015). An Online Tutor for Astronomy: The GEAS Self-Review Library. [doi:10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990](https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A correlational analysis (Figure 3) relating average review quiz (homework) scores at exam time to midterm and final exam scores for a remote cohort and an in-class peer set at NMSU. The article prints correlation coefficients that "range between 60% and 80%"; the relationship is associational, not causal.

> "Linear fits are consistent with the data distribution, and correlation coefficients range between 60% and 80%. Note that the locus of points is shifted to higher exam and homework scores for the distance learners."
