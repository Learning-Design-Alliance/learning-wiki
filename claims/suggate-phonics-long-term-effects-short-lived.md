The Suggate (2016) meta-analysis of long-term impacts of teaching methods, as reported in this commentary. Weighted effects of systematic phonics fell from d = 0.29 immediately after training to d = 0.07 after a mean delay of around 1 year, the smallest among the methods compared.

> "Suggate ( 2016) reported a meta-analysis that focused on the long-term impact of various teaching methods, and it showed that effects of systematic phonics were short-lived (reducing from d = 0.29 immediately after training to d = 0.07 following a mean delay of around 1 year) and indeed had the smallest effect size following a delay."
