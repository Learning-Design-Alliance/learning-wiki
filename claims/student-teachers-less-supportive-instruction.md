## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Maikki Pouta 2020

Maikki Pouta, Erno Lehtinen & Tuire Palonen. (2020). Student Teachers' and Experienced Teachers' Professional Vision of Students' Understanding of the Rational Number Concept. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09536-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Video-based coding of instruction in fraction-related episodes as supportive or non-supportive of fraction understanding. The article reports the significant group difference with printed percentages; no effect size is printed.

> "Student teachers attended to episodes classified as supportive only in 21.2 % of episodes, whereas experienced teachers attended to episodes classified as supportive in 52.8 % of episodes. The difference was significant, χ2 (1, N =6 9 )=7 . 3 0 6 , p < 0.01."
