## Evidence

### Kühne Franziska 2020

Kühne Franziska, Heinze Peter Eric, Weck Florian. (2020). Standardized patients in psychotherapy training and clinical supervision: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial. Trials 21, 276. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-020-4172-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Hypothesis stated in the protocol's Objectives section for a single-center RCT comparing video modeling by an experienced psychotherapist plus written instructions against unspecific tutorials plus the same written instructions. No outcome data exist yet; this is a pre-registered directional hypothesis, not a finding.

> "We expect post-training, psychology students randomly assigned to the active strategy of modeling to display more therapeutic competencies than students who were only reading the written instructions on behavioral activation or cognitive techniques (hypothesis 1)."

### Kühne Franziska 2020 (2)

Kühne Franziska, Heinze Peter Eric, Weck Florian. (2020). Standardized patients in psychotherapy training and clinical supervision: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial. Trials 21, 276. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-020-4172-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The durability hypothesis for the training comparison, stated in the protocol's Objectives section. Role-plays at baseline, post, and 3-month follow-up are videotaped and rated by two independent raters on a standardized competence scale; no results are yet reported.

> "Furthermore, we expect the competence differences between the two groups to persist until the 3-month follow-up (hypothesis 2)."
