## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret the gains as incidental learning caused by incorporation of target words into the cartoons functioning as context, not by subtitling. [→ Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban 2011](#ali-karakas-and-arif-saricoban-2011)

## Evidence

### Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban 2011

Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban. (2011). The Impact of Watching Subtitled Animated Cartoons on Incidental Vocabulary Learning of ELT Students. Teaching English with Technology, 12(4), 3-15. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1144953

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of their own experimental findings, not a separately tested result: the cartoons "functioned as a context, obviously a fundamental notion within the process of incidental vocabulary learning". Participants were not told the study purpose and could not use dictionaries.

> "cartoons that functioned as a context, obviously a fundamental notion within the process of incidental vocabulary learning (DeRidder, 1999)"
