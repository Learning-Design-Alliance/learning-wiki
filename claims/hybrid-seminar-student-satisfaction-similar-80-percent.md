## Evidence

### Bellini Raffaella 2024

Bellini Raffaella, Rossettini Giacomo, Letafatkar Amir, Dell’Isola Andrea, Battista Simone. (2024). Physiotherapy students’ academic performance and satisfaction after following a chest physiotherapy course in hybrid mode: a case-control study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-76051-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

An anonymous 5-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire was sent 24 hours after the practical test; 40 of 41 students (98%) responded. The article reports "32 students were satisfied with this hybrid class (80%)" with no statistical test between groups.

> "Among the 19 face-to-face students who answered the question, 18 were satisfied, and only one was dissatisfied. Among the 21 remote students, 14 were satisfied, three were dissatisfied, and four were dissatisfied. In general, 32 students were satisfied with this hybrid class (80%)."
