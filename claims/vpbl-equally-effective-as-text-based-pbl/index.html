Evidence¶

Jun J 2014¶

Jun J, Bridges SM. (2014). Educational Technologies in Problem-Based Learning in Health Sciences Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3240

q2 · i? · review · r3

Quasi-experimental, posttest-only design comparing a virtual problem-based learning exercise delivered via the Web against a text-based version of the same PBL exercise, measuring achievement on physiology examination items and perceptions via the Teaching and Learning Environment Questionnaire. The review reports VPBL "is equally as effective as the text-based version."

"The VPBL is equally as effective as the text-based version for enhancing students’ learning and their learning environment in small-group PBL sessions."
