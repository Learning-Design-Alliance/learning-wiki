## Evidence

### Melissa Dancy & Apriel K. Hodari 2023

Melissa Dancy & Apriel K. Hodari. (2023). How well-intentioned white male physicists maintain ignorance of inequity and justify inaction. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1186/s40594-023-00433-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-023-00433-8)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Critical discourse analysis of interviews with 27 self-identified progressive white male physics faculty and graduate students found a common discourse move of "locate inequity at a physical distance", justified by appeals to the goodness of local people rather than evidence or input from women and people of color.

> "They commonly claim that it isn't an issue in their classrooms, their research group, their department, their geographic region or in their specific field, or subfield."
