Allensworth 2020¶

Allensworth, E. (2020). High school GPAs and ACT scores as predictors of college graduation. Educational Researcher, 49(3), 198-211. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-GPAs-and-ACT-scores-as-predictors-of-college-completion-er-article

q1 · i? · associational · r?

The authors' key-finding interpretation states "most of the signal from the ACT score seems to represent factors associated with the student’s school rather than the student." This is an author interpretation, not a printed effect size.

"there is little evidence that students will have more college success if they work to improve their ACT score because most of the signal from the ACT score seems to represent factors associated with the student’s school rather than the student."
