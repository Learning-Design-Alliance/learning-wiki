### Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele 2025

Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele. (2025). Without Integration, Everything Is Nothing: A Meta-Analysis of the Effectiveness of Instructional Support for Drawing-to-Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10067-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis of comprehension outcomes in the random-effects meta-analysis; Hypothesis 2 was rejected. No effect size is printed for this moderator contrast, only the homogeneity test statistic.

> "reduction of extraneous cognitive load (support including/not including the reduction of ECL as a potential impact on cognitive processing) turned out to be a non-significant moderator of the effect of drawing support on students' comprehension, Q(1) = 0.00, p = 0.976"
