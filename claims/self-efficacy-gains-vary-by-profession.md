## Evidence

### FitzGerald J 2025

FitzGerald J, Regan B, Wells Y, Bhar S, Browne W. (2025). Improving clinicians' self‐efficacy in delivering a diagnosis of dementia: A before‐after study. Australasian Journal on Ageing. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajag.70078

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup repeated-measures analyses within the same before-after workshop study, analysing each profession group separately. The article reports χ2 = 2.1, p = 0.36 for the "17 participants from the medical and nursing professions" and χ2 = 19.0, p < 0.001 for the allied health and psychology group; no effect size is printed.

> "This analysis showed no change overall for the 17 participants from the medical and nursing professions (χ2 = 2.1, p = 0.36) but a significant change overall for the 15 participants who were in the allied health and psychology group (χ2 = 19.0, p < 0.001)."
