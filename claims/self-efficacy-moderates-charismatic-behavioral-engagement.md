## Evidence

### Hazzam 2023

Hazzam, J., & Wilkins, S. (2023). The influences of lecturer charismatic leadership and technology use on student online engagement, learning performance, and satisfaction. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104809

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis using Hayes' Process macro model 1 with 5000 bootstrap samples; interaction effect significant (β = -0.08, t = -2.90, p < .01, ΔR² = 0.008), effect non-significant at +1 SD (p = .11).

> "The results suggest that for -1 standard deviation from the mean on self-efficacy the effect (β = 0.20, t = 4.81, p < .001) is higher than the effect on the mean level of self-efficacy (β = 0.13, t = 4.01, p < .001)."
