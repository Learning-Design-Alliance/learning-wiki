### Diener 2026

Diener, J. P. W., Frick, B. L., & Kriek, J. (2026). Determining the degree of randomness in multiple-choice question distractors. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.01431](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.01431)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ability-conditioned check splitting each cohort at the median total score (cohort I median 19%, cohort II 15%) and recomputing match rates and D̄ within halves (Tables V–VI). The article reports "the match rate is higher in the higher-scoring half in all but one" comparison; D̄ falls from lower to higher half in 13 of 14.
