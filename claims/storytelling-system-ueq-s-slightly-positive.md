### Sami Saeed Alghamdi 2025

Sami Saeed Alghamdi, Christopher Bull, Ahmed Kharrufa. (2025). Designing for Self-Regulation in Informal Programming Learning: Insights from a Storytelling-Centric Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.22671](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.22671)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the UEQ-S completed by 15 informal programming learners after a self-paced trial of the system. The mean of 0.54 (SD = 1.29) on the -3 to +3 scale indicated "slightly positive experiences"; no inferential test was reported.

> "The average score was 0.54 (SD = 1.29), indicating slightly positive experiences, especially given that the system is an unpolished MVP, with wide variability (range: -1.75 to 3)."
