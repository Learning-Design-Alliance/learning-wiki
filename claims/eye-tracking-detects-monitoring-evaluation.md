# Eye-tracking data enables detection of more frequent Monitoring and Evaluation processes that navigational and peripheral channels miss

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` From Log+eye-tracking, median frequency was 0.93% for Evaluation and 3.45% for Monitoring processes, which are hard to detect from Nav_only and Enhanced_log. [→ Yizhou Fan 2022](#yizhou-fan-2022)

## Evidence

### Yizhou Fan 2022

Yizhou Fan, Lyn Lim, Joep van der Graaf, Jonathan Kilgour, Mladen Raković, Johanna Moore, Inge Molenaar, Maria Bannert, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević. (2022). Improving the measurement of self-regulated learning using multi-channel data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09304-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Frequency-based descriptive analysis of metacognitive processes across the three channels for all 25 participants. The article notes these processes took only a tiny proportion of sessions but were detected more frequently with eye-tracking data added.

> "we detected the median values of 0.93% (frequency-based) for the Evaluation processes (e.g., IRRELEVANT_READING to NOTE_READING) and 3.45% (frequency-based) for the Monitoring processes (e.g., checking the TIMER during reading)"
