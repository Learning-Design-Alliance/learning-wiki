Jerrim et al. 2016¶

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Subgroup analysis of the clustered RCT for FSM-eligible pupils. The summary table reports an effect size of 0.01 (95% CI -0.18, 0.19) and 0 months' progress for pupils in CSC schools eligible for FSM.

"There is no evidence that the intervention had a positive impact on Key Stage 2 scores for children eligible for free school meals (FSM)."
