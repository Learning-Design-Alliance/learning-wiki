# After expert correction of benchmark materials, GPT-5.6-Sol's measured accuracy rises substantially on HLE-Physics, CMT-Benchmark, and CritPt

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPT-5.6-Sol's mean@4 rises from 47.3% to 78.7% on HLE-Physics and from 61.0% to 87.2% on CMT-Benchmark after correcting evaluation errors and repairing or excluding flawed questions. [→ Ali Ansari 2026](#ali-ansari-2026)
`q2 i?` On the 54 retained CritPt challenges, GPT-5.6-Sol's corrected pass@4 reaches 94.4% after expert repair and independent derivation of reference solutions. [→ Ali Ansari 2026 (2)](#ali-ansari-2026-2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Pre-audit versus validated/repaired evaluation of GPT-5.6-Sol on expert-authored benchmarks, with corrected scores computed on retained subsets after expert review. The article reports the rises verbatim: "mean@4 rises from 47.3% to 78.7%" on HLE-Physics and "from 61.0% to 87.2%" on CMT-Benchmark; no effect size is printed.

> "On HLE-Physics, GPT-5.6-Sol's mean@4 rises from 47.3% to 78.7%. On CMT-Benchmark (Pan et al., 2026), it rises from 61.0% to 87.2%. On CritPt, the mean@5 over the 70 evaluated challenges is 32.3% before the audit, and the mean@4 over the 54 retained challenges is 87.5% after it, with a pass@4 of 94.4%."
