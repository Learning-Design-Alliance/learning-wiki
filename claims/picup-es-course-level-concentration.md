## Evidence

### W. Brian Lane 2021

W. Brian Lane. (2021). Analysis of the PICUP Collection: Strengths and Areas for Development. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.02408](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.02408)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive census of Course Level tags across the PICUP Collection, shown in Figure 2. The author reports the "vast majority of ESs are designed for a First Year or Beyond the First Year context", with only 2 High School and 6 Advanced Exercise Sets.

> "The vast majority of ESs are designed for a First Year or Beyond the First Year context, while currently only a handful are designed for High School (2 ESs) or Advanced (6 ESs) contexts."

### W. Brian Lane 2021 (2)

> "An ES can list multiple Course Levels, and we see in Figure 3 that 24 out of the 62 total ESs list both the First Year and Beyond the First Year levels."
