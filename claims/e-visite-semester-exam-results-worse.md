## Evidence

### van Bonn Sara M. 2024

van Bonn Sara M., Grajek Jan S., Rettschlag Stefanie, Schraven Sebastian P., Mlynski Robert. (2024). Interactive electronic visualization formats in student teaching. Hno. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00106-024-01436-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of joint written exam grades across four winter semesters (n = 226, 231, 209, 242), analyzed with the Mann-Whitney test at p < 0,05. Mean grades were 2,21; 2,5; 2,17 and 2,81 respectively. Only p-values are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Die Klausurergebnisse des Wintersemesters 2020/2021 sind im Durchschnitt signifikant schlechter im Vergleich zu den Vorjahren mit Präsenslehre (2019/2020: p < 0,0001; 2018/2019: p = 0,0004; 2017/2018: p < 0,0001)."
