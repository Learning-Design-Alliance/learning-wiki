ACCESS scores were available for ELLs in both grades, administered midyear before and during the intervention. The test was not significant, but the printed effect size of 0.13 suggests an advantage for the control group; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "No significant difference was found for ELLs on this measure; however, the effect size yielded a disadvantage with a magnitude of 0.13 for ELLs in the treatment condition."
