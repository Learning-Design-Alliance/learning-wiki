# Low prior knowledge students learned more from PyKinetic's Parsons problems than high prior knowledge students, especially in the experimental group with SE prompts

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` LP students in the experimental group had a Cohen's d of 1.95, more than double the LP control group's d of .91, supporting that Parsons problems with incomplete LOCs are more beneficial for low prior knowledge learners. [→ Fabic 2019](#fabic-2019)

## Evidence

### Fabic 2019

Fabic, G. V. F., Mitrovic, A., & Neshatian, K. (2019). Evaluation of Parsons Problems with Menu-Based Self-Explanation Prompts in a Mobile Python Tutor. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 29, 507–535. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00184-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the top and bottom 25% of participants by pre-test score (9 LP and 9 HP in experimental; 7 LP and 7 HP in control). Table 8 prints LP experimental d = 1.95 vs. LP control d = .91; HP experimental d = −.31 vs. HP control d = .50. The authors state this shows "evidence of H3."

> "there is a substantial difference between the Cohen's d effect sizes of LP vs. HP participants in both groups. More importantly, the LP in the experimental group had a more than double effect size than LP in the control group."

### Fabic 2019 (2)

Fabic, G. V. F., Mitrovic, A., & Neshatian, K. (2019). Evaluation of Parsons Problems with Menu-Based Self-Explanation Prompts in a Mobile Python Tutor. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 29, 507–535. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00184-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the experimental group, LP students' normalized gains significantly exceeded HP students' on all questions (U = 66, p = .024), procedural (U = 68, p = .014), and easier-to-guess questions (U = 68.5, p = .011). LP post-test scores were not significantly different from HP students' post-test scores.

> "LP students had a significantly higher normalized gain than HP students for all questions, procedural, and easier-to-guess questions."
