## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the teacher Emotion-AI model, privacy had a significant negative impact on confidence (β = -0.6581, p = 0.0012), confidence significantly impacted ITU (β = 0.4744, p = 0.0447), and the indirect effect of privacy on ITU through confidence was statistically significant (Effect = -0.3122, BootLLCI = -0.6559, BootULCI = -0.0511). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Mediation analysis of teacher responses to the Emotion-AI vignette. The direct effect of privacy on ITU was not statistically significant (β = 0.1118, p = 0.6144); the indirect effect through confidence was statistically significant, indicating privacy indirectly affects acceptance through its impact on confidence.

> "privacy had a signiﬁcant negative impact on con ﬁdence (β = -0.6581, p =0.0012), indi-cating that higher levels of privacy were associated with lower conﬁdence levels in the context of an AI that supports emotional health"
