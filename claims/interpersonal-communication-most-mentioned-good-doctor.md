### Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017

Steiner-Hofbauer Verena, Schrank Beate, Holzinger Anita. (2017). What is a good doctor? Wiener Medizinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10354-017-0597-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of 20 included articles and open-access questionnaires, in which extracted attributes were grouped and counted per category. The review reports that general interpersonal qualities were mentioned "n = 66" times and communication and patient involvement "n = 59" times, with medical management (n = 32) and teaching/research/continuous education (n = 28) least often mentioned.

> "Most frequently, attributes of the categories “General interpersonal qualities” (n = 66) and “Communication and patient involvement” (n = 59) were mentioned, followed by “Medical competence” (n = 50), and “Ethics” (n = 48)."
