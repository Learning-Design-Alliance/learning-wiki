## Evidence

### Thalluri 2014

Thalluri, Jyothi; O'Flaherty, Jackie A.; and Shepherd, Paul L. (2014). Classmate peer-coaching: "A Study Buddy Support scheme". Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 92-104. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous end-of-semester evaluation with 21 closed-ended 5-point items and four open questions, completed by 28 of 141 Buddies and Buddy Leaders. Table 3 shows 100% agreement on items 14 and 19 and 96.5% agreement on several others.

> "As is clearly demonstrated in Table 3, positive statements in the survey (response rate just under 20%) were overwhelmingly agreed to, with a negative reaction to only three questions by a single student, and very few providing a neutral response."
