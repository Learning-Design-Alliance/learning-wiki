## Evidence

### Kuhn 2023

Kuhn, Jochen; Müller, Andreas; Vogt, Patrik. (2023). Newspaper Story Problems and Other Tasks for Context Based Physics Education: A Research Based Report on Classroom Practice. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary covariate analyses across the co-operation network studies (>1,600 pupils, 6 school types, >20 teachers). The article reports that "no influences (or only weak ones) were found" for the listed pupil characteristics on the treatment effects.

> "for all positive effects on motivation and achievement, no influences (or only weak ones) were found for gender, reading competence, physics, mathematics, or German language grades."
