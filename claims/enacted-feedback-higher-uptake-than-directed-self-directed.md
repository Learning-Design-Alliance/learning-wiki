# Enacted Feedback yields higher workflow-specific uptake of AI-generated feedback than Directed and Self-Directed Feedback workflows

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Uptake was significantly higher in Enacted Feedback (estimated 26.2%) than in Directed Feedback (14.1%) and Self-Directed Feedback (0.1%), with Enacted vs Directed OR = 2.16; no standardised effect size was printed. [→ Omar Alsaiari 2026](#omar-alsaiari-2026)

## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2026

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Naomi Winstone, Dragan Gašević, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). Making AI-Generated Feedback Matter: A Large-Scale Study of Feedback Workflows and Student Enactment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11625

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Binomial GLMM on log data from 51,296 resources across the three workflow conditions, with student ID as random intercept. The omnibus likelihood-ratio test was significant, and the Enacted/Directed contrast gave OR = 2.16, a model-estimated difference of 12.1 percentage points. Observed uptake was "26.2%" versus 14.1% and 0.1%.

> "Model-estimated probabilities followed the same pattern:Enacted Feedback, 26.2% (95% CI [25.3%, 27.2%]);Directed Feedback, 14.1% (95% CI [13.3%, 15.0%]); andSelf-Directed Feedback, 0.1% (95% CI [0.1%, 0.2%])."
