### Charlotte Zimmerman 2025

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2025). Assessing physics quantitative literacy development in algebra-based physics. [doi:10.1103/lnd6-pxyt](https://doi.org/10.1103/lnd6-pxyt)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Validation interviews with six faculty across institution types (high school, two-year college, HBCU, R1 research, math education); the article reports that all the physics instructor experts agreed the items represented desired learning outcomes for their courses, though emphasis varied, with some instructors more focused on proportional reasoning than covariation generally.

> "During individual validation interviews, all physics instructor experts expressed that the items on the GERQN collectively represented desired learning outcomes for their introductory physics courses."
