## Evidence

### Kalyuga 2004

Slava Kalyuga and John Sweller. (2004). Measuring Knowledge to Optimize Cognitive Load Factors During Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.96.3.558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1 with 24 Year 9 advanced and 21 Year 10 intermediate mathematics students compared a rapid first-step test with a traditional complete-solution test in one 15-min session. The article reports "a very high degree of the concurrent validity for the rapid test" and a test-time reduction by a factor of 4.9; Cronbach's alpha was .78 (traditional) and .63 (rapid).

> "A Pearson product–moment correlation, r(44) ⫽ .92, p ⬍ .01, between scores for the traditional and rapid tests was obtained, with a 95% confidence interval extending from .86 to .96, sug- gesting a very high degree of the concurrent validity for the rapid test."
