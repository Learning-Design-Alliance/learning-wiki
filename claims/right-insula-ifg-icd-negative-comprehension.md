### Li 2018

Li, M., Malins, J. G., DeMille, M. M. C., Lovett, M. W., Truong, D. T., Epstein, K., Lacadie, C., Mehta, C., Bosson-Heenan, J., Gruen, J. R., Frijters, J. C. and the GRaD Study Consortium. (2018). A molecular-genetic and imaging-genetic approach to specific comprehension difficulties in children. npj Science of Learning 3:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0034-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Resting-state fMRI ICD analysis in 58 children from the GRaD sample, controlling for sex, SES, maternal education, and genetic ancestry PCs. The focal relationship remained significant after range restriction (p = .0013) and removing influential points (p = .00015).

> "That is, ICD beta values were larger for children with negative comprehension residuals (analogous to UPC) than they were for children with positive comprehension residuals. This result indicates a greater density of strong correlations in the right hemisphere in subjects with UPC compared to UGC."
