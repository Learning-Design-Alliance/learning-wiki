### Sakles John C MD 2014

Sakles John C MD, Mosier Jarrod MD, Patanwala Asad E. PharmD, Dicken John BS. (2014). Learning Curves for Direct Laryngoscopy and GlideScope® Video Laryngoscopy in an Emergency Medicine Residency. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2014.9.23691

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

This is the authors' proposed explanation (type e) offered in the Discussion for the lack of DL improvement: previously, if "DL failed, there were no other options for rescue intubation, so DL was attempted again".

> "operators are more likely to abort the use of DL after a failed intubation and switch to video laryngoscope (VL). Thus the opportunity for residents to learn from their mistakes and advance their DL skills after failed first intubation attempts is lost."
