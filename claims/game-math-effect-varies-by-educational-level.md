## Evidence

### Sedat Turgut 2017

Sedat Turgut, Özlem Dogan Temur. (2017). The Effect of Game-Assisted Mathematics Education on Academic Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017236115

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Analog ANOVA subgroup analysis across preschool (ES = 1.162), elementary (0.868), middle school (0.661) and higher school (1.207) levels, with "QB= 7.830, p= 0.050" as printed. Higher-school and preschool effects were classified large; the others medium.

> "Accordingly, a statistically significant difference between groups in terms of educational level is found. Regarding findings, using games in mathematics teaching has maximum effect on higher school and minimum effect on middle school in terms of educational level."
