`q2 i?` On the ten-item Likert self-assessment questionnaire, the 3D-printed PBL group scored higher on every item, with all differences statistically significant (P < 0.05). [→ Wang Xiao-Le 2025](#wang-xiao-le-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang Xiao-Le 2025

Wang Xiao-Le, Xie Ying, Yue Ye-Xin, Yang Xue-Wen, Li Jian. (2025). A novel paradigm in cleft lip education: integration of 3D-printed simulator and problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07719-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment questionnaire (ten items, 5-point Likert scale each) compared by Mann-Whitney U test (n = 20 per group). All items reached P < 0.001; for example item 1 (interest in learning) 8(8, 10) vs 6(5, 7.75), Z = -3.689.

> "The self-assessment questionnaire results showed that the 3D-printed PBL group students scored higher than the traditional group PBL students in several areas. These areas included interest in learning, efficiency of learning, understanding of common surgical methods, improvement of clinical practice skills, understanding of the anatomical structure of the lips, understanding of how to design fixed points, understanding of muscle dissection and separation methods, understanding of how to rotate and align tissue flaps during suture, confidence in future cleft lip surgery, and satisfaction with teaching methods (Table 4)."
