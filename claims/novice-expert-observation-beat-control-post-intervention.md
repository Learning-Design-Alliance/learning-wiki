## Evidence

### Harris David J. 2017

Harris David J., Vine Samuel J., Wilson Mark R., McGrath John S., LeBel Marie-Eve, Buckingham Gavin. (2017). The effect of observing novice and expert performance on acquisition of surgical skills on a robotic platform. PLoS ONE 12(11): e0188233. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0188233

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A 3 (trial) x 4 (group) mixed ANOVA on errors/second in the randomized four-group experiment with 120 medical students. The article reports a significant effect of group at post-intervention with printed effect size ω² = .069 (quoted as "ꙍ2 = .069"), attributed to the observation intervention.

> "At post-intervention, there was a significant effect of group, F(3,112) = 3.88, p = .01, ꙍ2 = .069, BF10 = 3.66, indicating an intervention effect."

### Harris David J. 2017 (2)

Harris David J., Vine Samuel J., Wilson Mark R., McGrath John S., LeBel Marie-Eve, Buckingham Gavin. (2017). The effect of observing novice and expert performance on acquisition of surgical skills on a robotic platform. PLoS ONE 12(11): e0188233. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0188233

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-Holm corrected post-hoc tests following the post-intervention one-way ANOVA. "significant differences between Control and Novice (p = .02, BF10 = 4.58) and Control and Expert (p = .04, BF10 = 2.55)" were found; no pairwise differences among observation groups.

> "Post-hoc analysis showed significant differences between Control and Novice (p = .02, BF10 = 4.58) and Control and Expert (p = .04, BF10 = 2.55) groups, but there were no differences between Novice and Expert (p = .69, BF10 = 0.31), Novice and Mixed (p = .49, BF10 = 1.34), Expert and Mixed (p = .61, BF10 = 0.59) or Mixed and Control (p = .40, BF10 = 0.61)."
