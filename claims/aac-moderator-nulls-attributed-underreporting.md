Authors' interpretation of their moderator findings across the meta-analysis: "This is likely due, in part, to the participant heterogeneity and an under-reporting of implementation factors." They add that controlling for extractable participant and intervention characteristics did not explain substantial variation.

`q3 · i?`

Limitation discussion of the non-overlap metric: Tau loses sensitivity when baseline-intervention overlap is near zero, so "a large grouping of Tau estimates at or near 1.0" may have masked real moderator differences; LRR excluded studies with near-zero baselines.

> "This ceiling effect created a large grouping of Tau estimates at or near 1.0, which may have contributed to the lack of statistically significant differences we found between moderator categories."
