# Audio input enhancement produces a significant short-term advantage over conventional instruction in EFL learners' knowledge of English intensifiers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After two months of instruction, the audio input enhancement group significantly outperformed the control group on the immediate post-test of English intensifiers. [→ Negari 2018](#negari-2018)

## Evidence

### Negari 2018

Negari, G. M., Azizi, A., & Arani, D. K. (2018). Investigating the Effectiveness of Audio Input Enhancement on EFL Learners’ Retention of Intensifiers. International Journal of Instruction, 11(1), 123-138. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1119a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group quasi-experiment with 43 male sophomore students (experimental n=23, control n=20) taught intensifiers over two months. The independent samples t-test showed a significant post-test difference, "t (41) = 2.32, p <  .05"; the article reports the effect size as medium per Cohen (1988).

> "Based on the given information in Tables 3 and 4, there is a significant difference in the performance of the experimental group (M=9.57, SD= 2.59) and the control group (M=7.85, SD= 2.18) regarding their post –test on English intensifiers, t (41) = 2.32, p <  .05."
