## Evidence

### Goedegebuure E.P. 2026

Goedegebuure E.P., Boellaard T.N., van Dijk-de Haan M.C., et al. (2026). Improving membranous urethral length measurements on prostate MRI: a comparison of online training methods. European Radiology 36(8):6419-6427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12468-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression on pre/post MUL measurements from 81 radiologists across three training groups (written instructions, case-based self-study, expert-led). Diagnostic performance was defined as "the absolute difference (mm) between participants' measurements and this reference."

> "There was a statistically significant improvement in diagnostic performance for all groups (p < 0.001). Improvement was consistent across all groups, with no significant differences in diagnostic gain between the groups."
