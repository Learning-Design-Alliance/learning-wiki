### Nilsen 2020

Nilsen, S. A., Breivik, K., Wold, B., Askeland, K. G., Sivertsen, B., Hysing, M., & Bøe, T. (2020). Divorce and adolescent academic achievement: Heterogeneity in the associations by parental education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229183

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Population-based cross-sectional youth@hordaland survey linked to national registries, analyzed with OLS regression adjusted for gender and age. The baseline model found adolescents "with divorced parents on average had 0.30 points lower GPA score (Cohen’s d = 0.34)".
