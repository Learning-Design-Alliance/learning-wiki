## Evidence

### Pardej S. K. 2026

Pardej S. K., Russo O., Green T. (2026). Effects of Neighborhood Level Health and Environment Quality on Academic and Executive Abilities in Youth with Noonan Syndrome Spectrum Disorder. Journal of the International Neuropsychological Society. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1355617726101969

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of WRAT scores in 140 youth with NSSD and 85 unaffected youth; all four models were significant (e.g., Spelling: F(3, 160)=40.42, p<.001, R2=.420), and "youth with NSSD had poorer academic functioning than unaffected youth" in each.

> "Diagnosis was a significant predictor in each model with large effects (p<.001, partial η2=.36–.42); in each model, youth with NSSD had poorer academic functioning than unaffected youth."
