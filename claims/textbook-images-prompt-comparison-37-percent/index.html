Evidence¶

Benjamin D. Jee 2022¶

Benjamin D. Jee, Bryan J. Matlen, Monica Greenlaw, Nina Simms, Dedre Gentner. (2022). Spatial supports for comparison in educational science images. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09599-0

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Content analysis of 313 images sampled from two chapters each of the three most popular U.S. middle school science textbooks (176 pages, anatomy and evolution). The authors report "116 (37%) were identified as involving com- parison," with evolution images slightly more likely to prompt comparison than anatomy images.

"Of the 313 total images, 116 (37%) were identified as involving com- parison. Images in the evolution chapters were slightly more likely to involve comparison"
