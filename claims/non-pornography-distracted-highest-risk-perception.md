## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among those not feeling isolated, the highest sense-of-risk percentage (71.6%) occurs among those not using or only limitedly using pornography, and within that group distraction from studies further differentiates risk (72.9% highest). [→ Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023](#munoz-rodriguez-2023)

## Evidence

### Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CHAID segmentation of the non-isolated segment: pornography is the second most predictive variable (χ2 = 21.093; p = 0.000), and distraction from studies adds a significant differentiation (χ2 = 8.369; p = 0.008) with the highest risk percentage at 72.9%. Only χ2 statistics are printed.

> "the highest percentage in this group experiencing a sense of risk (71.6%) involves those individuals that either do not use or make only a limited use of the internet, and within this group the study control variable introduces a new significant differentiation (χ2 = 8.369; p = 0.008; df = 1), with a greater sense of risk among those that have at some time or often been distracted from their studies"
