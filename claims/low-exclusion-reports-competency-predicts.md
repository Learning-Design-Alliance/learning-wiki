## Evidence

### Eddy SL 2015

Eddy SL, Brownell SE, Thummaphan P, Lan M-C, Wenderoth MP. (2015). Caution, Student Experience May Vary: Social Identities Impact a Student's Experience in Peer Discussions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-05-0108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study II logistic regression with model averaging on coded open responses to "What is the worst part about working in a group?" (n = 278). The article reports the overall rate of reporting groupmate limits was low and that "students who were one SD below the mean biology competency were 1.5 times more likely to report" it.

> "All else being equal, students who were one SD below the mean biology competency were 1.5 times more likely to report that a groupmate limited participation than students at the mean biology competency."

### Eddy SL 2015 (2)

Eddy SL, Brownell SE, Thummaphan P, Lan M-C, Wenderoth MP. (2015). Caution, Student Experience May Vary: Social Identities Impact a Student's Experience in Peer Discussions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-05-0108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Study II Likert-scale dominator question answered by 323 students in terms A and B; this descriptive statistic is printed directly in the article's Results for the prompted dominator item.

> "When specifically prompted with a question about dominators, 29.4% of students agreed or strongly agreed that someone in their group dominated the discussion."
