Narrative subgroup synthesis of the four prespecified AI modalities (GenAI/LLM n=7, virtual patients/mannequins n=5, AI-VR/MR n=5, chatbots n=2) concluded "GenAI/LLM modalities provide the strongest evidence for cognitive and communication outcomes" and underscored aligning modality with learning objectives.
