### Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang 2021

Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang. (2021). Investigating student understanding of heat engine: a case study of Stirling engine. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison across three separately taught classes; only the Xiang class received the voluntary tutorial, which did not cover the Stirling cycle itself. The article also reports "more students got correct answer in Xiang class (above 40%) than the other two classes (∼ 20%)". No significance test or effect size is printed.

> "Although the engineering errors were high for the three classes, there was a big diﬀerence between Xiang class and the other two classes: the engineering ratio of Xiang class was smaller by approximately 20% than those of the other two."
