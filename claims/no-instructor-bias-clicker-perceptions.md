## Evidence

### Miller 2013

Miller, T. K. (2013). Pre-service Elementary Teachers' Perceptions of a Modified Peer Instruction Implementation of Clickers in their Mathematics Content Course. IUMPST: The Journal, Vol 3 (Technology). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043050

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact test compared responses across the two instructors' sections because chi-square was unreliable with low unfavorable counts. No significant differences at the 0.05 level were found for liking or perceived learning benefit.

> "The results revealed no significant differences at the 0.05 level for the proportions of students both liking the clicker implementation and viewing it as benefitting the learning process. Thus, instructor bias was not evident in terms of students liking the implementation or believing that clicker use was beneficial for learning."
