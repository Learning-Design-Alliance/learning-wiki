## Subclaims
`q1 i?` SP reflections distinguished prepared from unprepared students: prepared students were more fluent and self-confident, while unprepared students were hesitant and their simulations ended earlier than expected. [→ Delibalta B 2026](#delibalta-b-2026)

## Evidence

### Delibalta B 2026

Delibalta B, Güner Y, Üçüncüoğlu M, Türk Y, Dilbaz AD, Ateş E, Aykut DS. (2026). Role of self-regulated learning in a communication skills curriculum: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09417-y

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative arm: Gibbs-cycle reflection sessions with eight simulated participants after each simulation training, audio-recorded and thematically analysed. SPs reported feeling challenged or bored with nonprepared students and excited with prepared students.

> "The students who were prepared before the simulation were more successful in using communication skills according to the SPs."
