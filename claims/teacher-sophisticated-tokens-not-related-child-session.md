## Evidence

### McLeod et al. 2019

Ragan H. McLeod, Ann P. Kaiser, and Jessica K. Hardy. (2019). The Relation Between Teacher Vocabulary Use in Play and Child Vocabulary Outcomes. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121418812675

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed model analysis of the same 53 EMT sessions; sophisticated words were defined as words not on the Dale–Chall list. The article reports the within-session relation was "not significant (SE = .03, p = .07)"; no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "The relation between teacher use of sophisticated words and child use of sophisticated words during the session was not significant (SE = .03, p = .07)."
