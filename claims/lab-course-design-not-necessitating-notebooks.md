## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Lab notebooks as scientific communication: investigating development from undergraduate courses to graduate research. arXiv preprint arXiv:1604.00403. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of 13 graduate students' evaluations of undergraduate lab courses. Seven interviewees attributed the failure to activity design; interviewees explained that "there is not a real continuity from day to day" since experiments were completed in one session and never revisited.

> "One common criticism (made by seven of the interviewees) was that the design and implementation of the lab curriculum and lab activities themselves did not actually necessitate the use of a lab notebook."
