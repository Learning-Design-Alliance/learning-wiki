## Evidence

### Qasem & Viswanathappa 2016

Qasem, A. A. A., & Viswanathappa, G. (2016). Blended Learning Approach to Develop the Teachers' TPACK. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(3), 264-276. https://doi.org/10.30935/cedtech/6176

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent groups study of 60 in-service science teachers (30 per group) in Ibb, Yemen. T-test on gain means found the blended group's TK gain (M=26.167) significantly exceeded the control group's (M=22.1), with "significant difference (t=3.068, p< 0.05)"; the same held for TPK (M=29.00 vs 22.70, t=5.404), TCK (M=23.93 vs 20.57, t=3.141) and TPACK (M=15.77 vs 12.83, t=3.030), each p < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 reveals a significant difference (t=3.068, p< 0.05) on gain mean of TK, and the gain mean score in TK is higher in the experimental group (M=26.167) when compared to the control group (M=22.1)."
