# Blended learning professional development produces significantly higher gain means in ICT knowledge (TK, TPK, TCK, TPACK) than traditional training for in-service science teachers

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Arwa Ahmed Abdo Qasem 2016

Arwa Ahmed Abdo Qasem, Gandla Viswanathappa. (2016). Blended Learning Approach to Develop the Teachers' TPACK. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(3), 264-276. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2013.09.248

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent groups study of 60 in-service science teachers (30 per group) in Ibb, Yemen. T-test on gain means found the blended group's TK gain (M=26.167) significantly exceeded the control group's (M=22.1), with "significant difference (t=3.068, p< 0.05)".

> "Table 4 reveals a significant difference (t=3.068, p< 0.05) on gain mean of TK, and the gain mean score in TK is higher in the experimental group (M=26.167) when compared to the control group (M=22.1)."
