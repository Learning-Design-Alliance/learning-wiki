Study-characteristics analysis of the 288 included studies; 82% (235/288) were conducted with pre-registration learners. The review questions whether uni-professional TBL prepares learners for multi-disciplinary health-care teams and flags possible hidden-curriculum effects.

"In study characteristics of 288 studies, 259 (90%) were conducted purely with medical students, learners or teachers, while 29 (10%) referred to inter‐professional TBL (medical plus another health‐care profession)."
