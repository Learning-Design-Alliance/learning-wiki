## Evidence

### Bonacci 2020

Bonacci, E. (2020). On Teaching Quantum Physics at High School. Athens Journal of Education, 7(3), 313-330. https://doi.org/10.30958/aje.7-3-5

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-study report of the author's own pre-university class VA at the Scientific High School G.B. Grassi in Latina, taught with the proposed strategies; the author reports that "four out of the twenty exam papers were centered on Quantum Mechanics", a shift from prior years in which only Electromagnetism and Relativity had been chosen.

> "After years in which Electromagnetism and Relativity had been the only subjects chosen by the students for the leaving certificate, four out of the twenty exam papers were centered on Quantum Mechanics."

### Enzo Bonacci 2021

Enzo Bonacci. (2021). Proposals for High School Teaching of Quantum Physics. ATINER's Conference Paper Proceedings Series PHY2018-0082, Athens. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.01219](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.01219)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-class pilot in the VA class of a scientific high school in 2007-2008, addressing three of the six critical aspects. The author reports "four out of the twenty exam papers were centered on Quantum Mechanics" and describes the outcome as surprisingly good learner transport to QM topics.

> "We obtained surprisingly good results with a genuine transport of learners to the QM topics and their domination of the basic mathematical tools. After years in which Electromagnetism and Relativity had been the only subjects chosen by the students for the leaving certificate, four out of the twenty exam papers were centered on Quantum Mechanics."
