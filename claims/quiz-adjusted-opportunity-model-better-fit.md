## Evidence

### Gautam Yadav 2024

Gautam Yadav, Paulo F. Carvalho, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2024. Beyond Repetition: The Role of Varied Questioning and Feedback in Knowledge Generalization. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3664688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

KC model comparison on log data from 32 students across 78 KCs, selecting between labeling approaches using AIC. The quiz-adjusted model showed better fit, indicating "counting opportunities only after quizzes—and thereby incorporating the effect of feedback—results in a more accurate model of student learning".

> "The quiz-adjusted model in Table 2, which only counts opportunities after quiz feedback is provided, fitted the data better (AIC = 35126.2) than the model based on practice opportunity counts (AIC = 35165.4)."
