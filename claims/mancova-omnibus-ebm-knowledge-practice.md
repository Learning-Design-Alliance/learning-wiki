# Knowledge and practice scores differ significantly by designation, workshop participation, research experience, and facilitating-factor level in an omnibus MANCOVA

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` With Wilks' criterion, the combined knowledge and practice dependent variables differed significantly by designation (partial η2 = 0.02), EBM workshop participation (partial η2 = 0.05), research experience (partial η2 = 0.04), and level of facilitating factors (partial η2 = 0.1), after controlling for years of service. ## Evidence

### Albart Stephenie Ann 2025

Albart Stephenie Ann, Looi Irene. (2025). Knowledge, practice, and facilitating factors of evidence-based medicine among Malaysian doctors: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06865-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus MANCOVA on the 409-doctor survey with knowledge and practice scores as dependent variables and years of service as covariate; the largest effect was facilitating-factor level with "partial η2 = 0.1". A post-hoc power analysis reported achieved power of 0.97 for medium effect sizes.

> "With the use of Wilks’ criterion, the combined DVs were significantly different by designation [Wilk’s Λ = 0.98, F(2,397) = 3.16, P = 0.043, partial η2 = 0.02], EBM workshop participation [Wilk’s Λ = 0.95, F(2,397) = 10.41, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.05], research experience [Wilk’s Λ = 0.96, F(2,397) = 7.59, P = 0.001, partial η2 = 0.04], and level of facilitating factors [Wilk’s Λ = 0.82, F(4,794) = 20.96, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.1], after controlling for years of services."
