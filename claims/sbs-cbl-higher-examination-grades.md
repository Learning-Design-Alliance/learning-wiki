# SBS & CBL trainees earned significantly higher professional knowledge and surgical skills examination grades than LBL & CBL trainees after training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After training, grades for both professional knowledge and surgical skills for otoendoscopic operations were significantly higher in the SBS & CBL group than in the LBL & CBL group (P < 0.05). [→ Wei Fanqin 2021](#wei-fanqin-2021)

## Evidence

### Wei Fanqin 2021

Wei Fanqin, Sun Qiyang, Qin Zili, Zhuang Huiwen, Jiang Guangli, Wu Xuan. (2021). Application and practice of a step-by-step method combined with case-based learning in Chinese otoendoscopy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02513-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Objective skill assessment of the same randomised 59-physician cohort: both groups took 100-point examinations on professional knowledge and surgical skills before and after training. Grades rose significantly in both groups, and the SBS & CBL group finished "significantly higher than those in Group B". No effect size is printed.

> "the grades for both professional knowledge and surgical skills for otoendoscopic operations in Group A were significantly higher than those in Group B (P < 0.05; Fig. 2), indicating that the SBS & CBL method was more conducive to improving professional knowledge and surgical skills for otoendoscopic operations in teaching than the LBL & CBL method"
