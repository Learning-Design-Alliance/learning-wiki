## Evidence

### Mägi 2010

Mägi, K., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., Rasku-Puttonen, H., & Kikas, E. (2010). Relations Between Achievement Goal Orientations and Math Achievement in Primary Grades: A Follow-Up Study. Scandinavian Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.1080/00313831003764545

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null longitudinal finding in the 179-child sample: "We did not find support for our expectations that children's mastery goal orientation positively, and performance goal orientations negatively, predict children's subsequent effort investments." A weak but significant same-time correlation between mastery orientation and teacher-rated effort was found.

> "We did not find support for our expectations that children's mastery goal orientation positively, and performance goal orientations negatively, predict children's subsequent effort investments."
