## Evidence

### Pozdniakov 2026

Pozdniakov, S., Brazil, J., Banihashem, S. K., Noroozi, O., Gašević, D., Sadiq, S., & Khosravi, H. (2026). AI assistance in peer feedback provision: Pedagogically sound, but minimally adopted. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105591

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log analysis of 7670 peer feedback instances from 433 students in a semester-long undergraduate course found that "just 9% (690) of AI assistance suggestions leading to revisions". Most AI assistance was positive and well structured.

> "Our results indicate that AI-A was typically positive, well structured, and focused on pedagogical strengths (79% of all instances). However, uptake remained relatively low, with just 9% (690) of AI assistance suggestions leading to revisions."
