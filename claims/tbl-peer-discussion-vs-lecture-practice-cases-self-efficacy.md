## Evidence

### Kehrli Kelsey F. 2026

Kehrli Kelsey F., Conner Kelly R., Eyadiel Lauren, Sisson Caroline B., Smith Natalie. (2026). Team-based learning versus lecture-based instruction for chest radiograph interpretation in physician associate education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09452-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses coded inductively by two independent investigators with consensus resolution. Discussing with peers was cited by 62.8% of TBL vs 6.2% of lecture respondents; practice cases by 32.1% TBL vs 60.0% lecture.

> "Responses from the lecture-based cohort most frequently referenced practice cases/examples and a systematic approach to interpretation. In contrast, TBL participants emphasized peer discussion and active engagement with practice cases."
