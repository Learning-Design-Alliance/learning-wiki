## Evidence

### Lowe B 2022

Lowe B, Ng J, Jordan S, Waynforth D, Angstetra D, Brazil V. (2022). Test‐enhanced learning improves learner attendance during a laparoscopic box trainer simulation program. Australian and New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13528

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-cohort pre-post comparison of the thread transfer exercise in the prospective cohort study; Table 3 prints end-of-study mean thread transfer times of 42.8 s (TEL) versus 47 s (control), P = 0.44.

> "Both intervention and control groups had a significant improvement in mean times for thread transfer from the beginning to the end of the project, but there was no statistically significant difference between the groups’ mean time scores at the end of the project."
