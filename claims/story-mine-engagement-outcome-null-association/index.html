q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory correlational analysis of engagement and outcome change in the pilot RCT. Spearman rank correlations between narratives consumed and changes on four outcome measures were all close to zero, providing no clear indication of a dose-response relationship.

"No clear associations were observed between intervention engagement and outcome changes (MHCS: ρ=−0.11; HHI: ρ=0.01; ISMI: ρ=0.02; and MHRM: ρ=−0.19)."
