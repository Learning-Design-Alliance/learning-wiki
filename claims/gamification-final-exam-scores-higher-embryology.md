# Medical students taught embryology with a gamification platform added to routine instruction scored significantly higher on the final exam than students taught by routine instruction alone

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a quasi-experimental comparison, the gamification group (mean 17.32, SD 0.99, n=122) scored significantly higher than the traditional group (mean 15.68, SD 1.02, n=143) on the embryology final exam (t = 8.79, p < 0.001). [→ Rahimipour Marzieh 2025](#rahimipour-marzieh-2025)
