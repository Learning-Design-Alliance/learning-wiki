# LCSM on the same simulated PALMA-like data shows negative cross-lagged effects, contradicting the reciprocal effects model

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a latent change score model on the simulated PALMA-like data, prior within-individual achievement had a negative effect on subsequent latent change in self-concept and vice versa. [→ Kimmo Sorjonen 2025](#kimmo-sorjonen-2025)

## Evidence

### Kimmo Sorjonen 2025

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent change score model fitted on the same simulated data (acceptable fit: χ2 = 1366, DF = 45, CFI = 0.945, RMSEA = 0.091). The article reports "a negative effect on subsequent latent change in self-concept" and the mirrored negative effect, contrary to the reciprocal effects model.

> "prior within-individual achievement had a negative effect on subsequent latent change in self-concept (b = −0.393 [−0.415; −0.372], p < 0.001) and vice versa (b = −0.418 [−0.440; −0.395], p < 0.001)"
