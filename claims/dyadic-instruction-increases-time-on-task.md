## Evidence

### Kühl Susanne J. 2019

Kühl Susanne J., Schneider Achim, Kestler Hans A., Toberer Matthias, Kühl Michael, Fischer Martin R. (2019). Investigating the self-study phase of an inverted biochemistry classroom – collaborative dyadic learning makes the difference. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1497-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported preparation time in self-study phase 2 compared across groups with Kruskal-Wallis H tests and Mann-Whitney U post hoc tests; the quote prints the z(U) statistics and p-values but no effect size.

> "In contrast, more students in the collaborative group learned longer compared to the students in the basic (z (U) = 3.30, p = 0.001) and the individual groups (z (U) = 3.17, p = 0.002)."
