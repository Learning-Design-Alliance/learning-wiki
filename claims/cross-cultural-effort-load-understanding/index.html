Evidence¶

Tino Endres 2025¶

Tino Endres, Lisa Bender, Stoo Sepp, Shirong Zhang, Louise David, Melanie Trypke, et al. (2025). Developing the Mental Effort and Load–Translingual Scale (MEL‑TS) as a Foundation for Translingual Research in Self‑Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09978-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative semi-structured interviews with bilingual participants in five language combinations explored cultural understanding of effort and load. The article reports that "Participants in all our six languages could relate to the distinction between mental effort and load", though clarity of the distinction varied by culture. No effect sizes are reported for this qualitative finding.

"Participants in all our six languages could relate to the distinction between mental effort and load in their respective cultures, with Chinese participants perceiving only a subtle difference of the two constructs and Dutch participants not being sure about how common a conscious distinction would be in daily life."
