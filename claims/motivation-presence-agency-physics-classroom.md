## Evidence

### Paolo Boffi 2026

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Physics Playground ecological study, RIMMS motivation scores differed by condition with "IVR significantly higher than DVR"; IVR and control did not differ (p=.053), nor DVR and control. Presence was also higher for IVR than DVR (U=64.5, p<.001).

> "Motivation scores were analyzed through a Kruskal-Wallis test, revealing a significant effect of condition (χ 2 = 14.4,p < .001), with IVR significantly higher than DVR (W=−5.65,p < .001;MIV R= 49.3,M DV R= 45.3)"

### Paolo Boffi 2026 (2)

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the same deployment, presence measured only in VR groups correlated positively with Sense of Positive Agency (r=0.468, p<.001) and showed no correlation with Sense of Negative Agency; Sense of Agency scores themselves did not differ by medium in either subscale.

> "a Pearson's correlation between presence and agency subscales revealed a significant positive correlation with SoPA (r=0.468,p < .001), and no correlations with SoNA (r=−0.121,p=.404)"
