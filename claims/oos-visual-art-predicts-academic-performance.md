### Deer Genman 2024

Deer Genman, Wu Hao, Zhang Li, Tadesse Endale, Khalid Sabika, Duan Congyu, Tian Wang, Gao Chunhai. (2024). Effect of out-of-school visual art activities on academic performance. The mediating role of socioeconomic status. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0298901

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model (AMOS 21.0) on survey data from 1624 primary students in Guizhou, China. Path analysis found "children's participation in VAP1 and VAP2 determines their academic performance (β = 0.14, p < .001, respectively)"; Table 4 reports standardized effects of .11 and .14.
