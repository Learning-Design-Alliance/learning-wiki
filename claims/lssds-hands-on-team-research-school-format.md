La Serena School for Data Science and the Spanish Virtual Observatory Schools: Initiatives Based on Hands on Experience. [doi:10.1017/s1743921324003223](https://doi.org/10.1017/s1743921324003223)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive report in Sec. 2 of the school's evolution since its first 2013 edition, written by the organizers. The article states the style has "remained constant" across editions, responding to an identified lack of data-science training in university curricula. No evaluation data are printed.

> "The teaching style adopted to offer this training has remained constant: a very hands-on, intensive program that promotes team work to tackle realistic research problems."
