Singaravelu 2014¶

Singaravelu, G. (2014). Impact of Gadget Based Learning of Grammar in English at Standard II. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2, April-June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068432

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Finding 2 of the equivalent-group experiment: the 40-student control group in Shri Vani Vilas Middle School showed no significant pre-post difference under conventional teaching. The author concludes conventional teaching "is not effective in learning English Grammar at standard II".

"There is no significant difference in the achieved mean score between pre test of control group and post test of control group in learning English Grammar of the students of standard II in Shri Vani Vilas Middle School."
