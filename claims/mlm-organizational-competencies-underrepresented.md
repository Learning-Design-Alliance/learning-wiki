> "This synthesis shows that intrapersonal and interpersonal competencies such as emotional intelligence, self-awareness, communication, and teamwork are widely represented across curricula, whereas organizational-level competencies (e.g., systems thinking, quality improvement, change management) appear in relatively few studies, suggesting important gaps in systems-level leadership development."
