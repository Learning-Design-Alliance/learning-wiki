# Five-month implementation of the CIEA produced significant group-by-time interactions favouring the experimental group on five of eight basic social-behaviour scales

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Mixed between-subject ANOVA showed significant group × time interactions on the Joyful-Depressed, Tolerant-Angry, Calm-Aggressive, Cooperative-Oppositional and Autonomous-Dependent scales, with the experimental group scoring higher afterwards. [→ Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić 2015](#robi-kroflic-and-helena-smrtnik-vitulic-2015)
`q2 i2` The largest interaction effects were on Calm-Aggressive (partial η² = .13) and Autonomous-Dependent (partial η² = .12), which the authors characterise as medium effects. [→ Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić 2015](#robi-kroflic-and-helena-smrtnik-vitulic-2015)

## Evidence

### Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić 2015

Robi Kroflič and Helena Smrtnik Vitulić. (2015). The Effects of the Comprehensive Inductive Educational Approach on the Social Behaviour of Preschool Children in Kindergarten. c e p s Journal, Vol.5, No1. https://journals.cepsj.si/index.php/cepsj

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental equivalent control-group post-test study in a Ljubljana kindergarten; 52 EG and 48 CG children rated by teachers on the SV-O before and after five months of CIEA implementation. The ANOVA interactions were significant, e.g. Calm-Aggressive with "Wilks' Lambda = .92, F = (1,99) = 14.17, p < .01, par- tial h2 = .13", i.e. partial η² = .13, a medium effect size.

> "The results of the mixed between-subject analysis of variances indicated statistically significant interactions between the social behaviour scores of the EG and the CG and time on five of the eight basic subscales of social behaviour"
