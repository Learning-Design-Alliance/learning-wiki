## Evidence

### Sangers 2025

Sangers, N. L., van der Sande, L., Welie, C., Dobber, M., & van Steensel, R. (2025). Learning a Language Through Reading: A Meta-analysis of Studies on the Effects of Extensive Reading on Second and Foreign Language Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10068-6

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator subgroup analysis over all effect measures combined (Table 3). Interventions including accountability (k = 59) yielded d = 0.51 versus d = 0.01 without accountability (k = 23), a significant moderator (Q = 8.72**). Most interventions included accountability (k = 59, 72%), e.g., reading logs or short tests about the book.
