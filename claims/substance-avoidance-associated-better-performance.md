### Kassaw Chalachew 2023

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2023). Determinants of academic achievement among higher education student found in low resource setting, A systematic review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294585

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study at Hawassa University (n = 592) reviewed in the synthesis: never-users of tobacco, alcohol, or khat were "twice as likely to score a self-reported cumulative GPA above 3.0" (adjusted odds ratio 1.95, 95% CI 1.25–3.02) and less likely to be delayed or warned (adjusted odds ratio 0.47).
