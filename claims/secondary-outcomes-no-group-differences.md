## Evidence

### Coelho Laila Pinto et al 2024

Coelho Laila Pinto et al. (2024). Rapid cycle deliberate practice versus postsimulation debriefing in pediatric cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a randomized controlled study. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2024AO0825

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcomes analysis in the same randomized study, from the Results section. The quote reports the significant pre-to-post decrease in time to defibrillation in both arms; the article adds these times increased after washout with no interaction or group effects. No effect size printed.

> "The mean time to defibrillation, e.g., decreased from 160.1 seconds to 92.3 seconds in the RCDP Group (p<0.001), and it decreased from 152.2 seconds to 97.6 seconds in the PSD Group (p=0.008)."

### Coelho Laila Pinto et al 2024 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chest compression quality measures extracted from the SimPad SkillReporter in the randomized study. The quote reports no statistically significant between-group differences on these variables at either time point; no effect size printed.

> "Regarding the quality of chest compression variables (chest compression rate, chest compression depth, chest recoil) and the chest compression fraction, statistically significant differences were not observed between groups immediately after the intervention and after the washout period."
