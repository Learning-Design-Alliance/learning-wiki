## Evidence

### Timonen Tomi 2022

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Landmark identification task on five cadaver temporal bones; novices correctly identified 95.3% (VR) and 92.8% (PACS), experts 97.3% and 96.2%. The quote reports the significant PACS group contrast at "p = 0.016".

> "Experts made significantly fewer errors in cross-sectional viewing than novices (p = 0.016). No statistically significant difference was found in the VR interpretation between both groups (p = 0.070)."
