# Framing educational research evidence within AI reduces its perceived credibility compared with neuroscience and educational psychology frames (the in-credible AI effect)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Educational research evidence framed within AI is judged less credible than the same evidence framed within neuroscience or educational psychology, controlling for familiarity. [→ Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent 2020](#mutlu-cukurova-rosemary-luckin-carmel-kent-2020)

## Evidence

### Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent 2020

Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent. (2020). Impact of an Artificial Intelligence Research Frame on the Perceived Credibility of Educational Research Evidence. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00188-w

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

One-way MANCOVA in an online experiment where 605 participants (345 analysed after cleaning) rated the same evidence in AI, neuroscience, or educational psychology frames; the multivariate effect was significant with "partialη2 = 0.036".

> "The MANCOV A resulted in a statistically significant difference between the groups on the combined dependent variables, after controlling for the participants’ discipline familiarity, F(10, 674) = 2.488,p = 0.006; Wilks’ Λ = 0.930; partialη2 = 0.036."
