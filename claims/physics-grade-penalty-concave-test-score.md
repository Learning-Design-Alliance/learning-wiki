### W. K. Black 2023

W. K. Black, Rebecca L. Matz, Mark Mills, and A. E. Evrard. (2023). Practice Makes Better: Quantifying Grade Beneﬁts of Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

KLLR fits of mean GPE as a function of normalized ACT/SAT math score T across five PR-supported STEM courses (Fig. 3, Table V), fit with quadratics pivoted at T0 = 0.9; even students with above-average math scores of T = 0.75 tend to score lower grades in physics relative to the other courses.

> "Compared to the non-physics courses, the two physics courses have intercepts at T0 = 0.9 lower by ∼ 0.7 grade points, the local slopes are nearly twice as large, and they have positive curvatures (a2∼ +7) whereas the other courses display negative curvatures ( a2∼− 3)."
