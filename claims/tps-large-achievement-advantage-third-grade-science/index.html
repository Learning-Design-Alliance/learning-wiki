Evidence¶

Dr. Ribhi Khaleel Ahmad Hamdan 2017¶

Dr. Ribhi Khaleel Ahmad Hamdan. (2017). The Effect of (Think – Pair – Share) Strategy on the Achievement of Third Grade Student in Sciences in the Educational District of Irbid. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1139082

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Semi-experimental post-measure study of 120 third graders in two Irbid schools, experimental and control groups of 60 each, analyzed with ANCOVA on post achievement scores. The corrected mean was 26.970 for the experimental group versus 19.114 for the control, with eta squared 0.881, which the article calls "a high value".
