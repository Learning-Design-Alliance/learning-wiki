`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Program-type coefficients from the meta-regression (Table 2), e.g., school restructuring (B = 1.41, p = .00), community service (B = 1.74, p = .00), attendance monitoring mixed (B = 2.01, p = .00); mentoring/counseling was not significant (p = .28).

> "When controlling for other influences on outcome (e.g., implementation), all program types produced positive results, though the mentoring and “other” programs fell short of significance. Attendance monitoring & incentives, child care, community service, and school restructuring programs produced best results."
