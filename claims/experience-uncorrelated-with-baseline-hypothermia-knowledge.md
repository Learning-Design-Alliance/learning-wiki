### Podsiadło Paweł 2018

Podsiadło Paweł, Kosiński Sylweriusz, Darocha Tomasz, Sałapa Kinga, Sanak Tomasz, Brugger Hermann. (2018). The Use of E-Learning in Medical Education for Mountain Rescuers Concerning Hypothermia. High Altitude Medicine & Biology. https://doi.org/10.1089/ham.2018.0050

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman's rank correlation analysis of experience versus pretest score in the 187-rescuer cohort, reported before training. Neither correlation reached significance at α = 0.05.

> "We did not find any significant correlation between experience and the pretest results either in the professional mountain rescuer group (R = 0.10, p = 0.493) or in the volunteer group (R = 0.06, p = 0.521)."
