`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Delphi survey results section reporting revisions across two rounds of expert consultation with 23–24 experts in Sichuan and Chongqing; the article states the final scale "comprised five primary indicators, twelve secondary indicators, and sixty-six tertiary indicators".

> "After the second round of expert consultation, "nutritional risk screening and status assessment" was revised to "nutritional screening, assessment and diagnosis" in the secondary indicators, and two tertiary indicators were added, three were deleted, and twenty were revised. The final scale comprised five primary indicators, twelve secondary indicators, and sixty-six tertiary indicators."
