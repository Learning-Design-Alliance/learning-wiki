# In one uncontrolled pre-post study of GTAs, student-focused ATI mean scores rose from pre-test to post-test after one semester of GTA preparation and teaching, while teacher-focused ATI mean scores did not change

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The distribution of student-focused (CCSF) ATI mean scores differed statistically from pre to post (D = 0.28, p = 0.032), and the student-focused grand mean rose from 3.52 to 3.72 (Z = −2.278, p = 0.023). [→ Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018](#emily-alicea-munoz-2018)
`q2 i?` The distribution of teacher-focused (ITTF) mean scores showed no statistical difference from pre to post. [→ Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018 (2)](#emily-alicea-munoz-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ATI pre/post administration at first and last class meeting; a KS test found the CCSF distribution "statistically different from pre to post ( D = 0 .28, p = 0 .032)", and a Wilcoxon signed-ranks test found the student-focused grand mean changed from 3.52 to 3.72 (Z = −2.278, p = 0.023). No effect size printed.

> "the distribution of student-focused mean scores is statistically different from pre to post ( D = 0 .28, p = 0 .032)"

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018 (2)

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same ATI pre/post dataset; the KS test found "no statistical difference in the distribution of mean scores for teacher-focused approaches", and the teacher-focused grand mean did not change significantly. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "Using a non-parametric two-sample KS test we found no statistical difference in the distribution of mean scores for teacher-focused approaches"
