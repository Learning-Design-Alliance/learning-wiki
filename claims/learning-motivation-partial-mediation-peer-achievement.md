## Evidence

### Shao Yanhong 2024

Shao Yanhong, Kang Shumin, Lu Quan, Zhang Chao, Li Ruoxi. (2024). How peer relationships affect academic achievement among junior high school students: The chain mediating roles of learning motivation and learning engagement. BMC Psychology 12, 278. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01780-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5000 resamples, 95% bias-corrected CI) within the SEM. The learning motivation indirect pathway was significant, with the printed CI excluding zero; the article reports the motivation pathway accounts for 59.5% of the total indirect effect.

> "The pathway of peer relationships→learning motivation→learning engagement had an indirect effect of 0.713 (t = 2.493,95% bias-corrected CI [0.193, 1.326], P < 0.01)"

### Shao Yanhong 2024 (2)

Shao Yanhong, Kang Shumin, Lu Quan, Zhang Chao, Li Ruoxi. (2024). How peer relationships affect academic achievement among junior high school students: The chain mediating roles of learning motivation and learning engagement. BMC Psychology 12, 278. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01780-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model path coefficient from the same SEM of 717 students. The article reports "peer relationships and learning motivation (β = 0.534, P < 0.001)" as the strongest path in the model.

> "A significant and positive correlation was observed between peer relationships and learning motivation (β = 0.534, P < 0.001 ), conforming H2"
