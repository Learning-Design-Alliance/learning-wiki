## Evidence

### Mohamad A. Yusuf 2014

Mohamad A. Yusuf, Tam S. Sim & Su’ad A. (2014). Students’ Proficiency and Textual Computer Gloss Use in Facilitating Vocabulary Knowledge. English Language Teaching, 7(11). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n11p99

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on total clicks among 99 ESL diploma students stratified by SPM English grade into low (n=30), mid (n=39) and high (n=30) proficiency. The article reports "F (2, 96) = 1.056 P-value = 0.352", indicating "the gloss use of the students in the different proficiency levels was almost the same". No effect size printed.

> "ANOVA and post-hoc comparisons both indicated that in low proficiency level there was no statistically significant difference between different proficiency levels in the number of clicks. F (2, 96) = 1.056 P-value = 0.352."
