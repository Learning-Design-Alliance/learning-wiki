## Evidence

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post self-report survey of 2016 first-time GTAs before (N = 22) and after (N = 24) the Orientation, comparing preparedness distributions; the KS test showed GTAs "feel sig- niﬁcantly better prepared for teaching after participating in the Orientation" (D = 0.65, p ≪ 0.001). No effect size printed.
