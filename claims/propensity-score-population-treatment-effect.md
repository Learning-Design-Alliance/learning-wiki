# Propensity score analysis estimated a Texas generalizability index of 0.91, and post-stratification yielded a population average treatment effect of 1.47 versus a conventional estimate of 1.44

## Subclaims

> "Using the data in this study plus the pilot, we estimate the generalizability index to be β = 0.91."
