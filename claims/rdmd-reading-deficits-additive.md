# The reading deficit profile of children with comorbid RDMD is additive relative to isolated RD and MD

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic group comparisons of reading outcomes across the 74 included studies, with pooled Hedges' g values of 0.51 (C-RD), 0.15 (C-MD), and 0.69 (C-RDMD) that "pointed to an additive effect for the cognitive profile of children with RDMD".

> "the overall effect sizes of group comparisons in reading outcomes (cf. Table 5) for C-RD (0.51), C-MD (0.15), and C-RDMD (0.69) pointed to an additive effect for the cognitive profile of children with RDMD"
