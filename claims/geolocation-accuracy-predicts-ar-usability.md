## Evidence

### J. Mercier 2023

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-test comparison of SUS scores between the GNSS control group and RTK experimental group after the 15-minute AR exploration. The GNSS group's higher score supports the hypothesis that inaccurate data negatively predicts usability.

> "the mean SUS score for the GNSS group is M = 81.7 (SD = 9.74). The mean SUS score for the RTK group is M = 74.4 (SD = 12). The results show a significant difference between the two groups (t(51) = 2.45, p = 0.018)."

### J. Mercier 2023 (2)

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

UEQ scale comparisons in the results section: attractiveness, perspicuity (t(46.7) = 2.61, p = 0.012) and novelty (t(51) = 2.45, p = 0.018) favoured the GNSS group, while efficiency, dependability, and stimulation showed no significant difference.

> "On the attractiveness scale, the mean score for the GNSS group is M = 1.72 (SD = 0.7) and that of the RTK group is M = 1.1 (SD = 0.98). The results show a significant difference (t(51) = 2.65, p = 0.011)."
