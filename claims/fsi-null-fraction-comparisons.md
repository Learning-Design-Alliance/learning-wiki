## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in FSI classes did not outperform control children on fraction comparisons; neither group showed significant pretest-to-posttest growth on that task. [→ Nancy C. Jordan 2024](#nancy-c-jordan-2024)

## Evidence

### Nancy C. Jordan 2024

Nancy C. Jordan, Nancy Dyson, Taylor-Paige Guba, Megan Botello, Heather Suchanec-Cooper, Henry May. (2024). Exploring the Impact of a Fraction Sense Intervention in Authentic School Environments: An Initial Investigation. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2024.105954

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same classroom RCT's multivariate model. The article attributes the lack of difference possibly to "the relatively low task reliability" (α = .692) and to the cross-multiplication shortcut strategy; equivalence was not tested.

> "Students in the FSI classes did not outperform the control children on fraction comparisons as neither the FSI group nor the control group showed significant growth from pretest to posttest on the fraction comparison task."
