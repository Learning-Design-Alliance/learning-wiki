## Evidence

### Yin David 2019

Yin David, Cabana François, Tousignant-Laflamme Yannick, Bédard Sonia, Tousignant Michel. (2019). Can a physiotherapy student assume the role of an advanced practice physiotherapist in Orthopaedic surgery triage? A prospective observational study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-019-2864-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective inter-rater reliability study of 86 consecutive patients in a university hospital orthopaedic outpatient clinic; a trained physiotherapy student evaluated patients independently before a blinded surgeon review. The study reports "94.2% of cases (κ = 0.86; 95% CI: 0.74–0.98)" for triage agreement.

> "The PS and OSs agreed on surgical triage outcomes for 94.2% of cases (κ = 0.86; 95% CI: 0.74–0.98). The OSs considered 31.4% of participants required surgery"
