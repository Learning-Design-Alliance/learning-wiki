## Evidence

### Tanielian 2014

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation tests within the immersion group (n = 37) comparing FLCAS scores against three maths exams (English word problems, Thai word problems, numerical computational). No statistically significant correlation was found for any.

> "No statistically significant correlation was found between FLCAS and any maths exam score, which supports theories that maths is a language that transcends other linguistic boundaries."
