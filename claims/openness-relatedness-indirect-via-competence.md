### Feng 2026 (2)

Feng, S., Koch, M. J., & Carolus, A. (2026). AI Mindset – An empirically tested theoretical framework on the psychological factors shaping AI competence and AI use. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100402

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Discussion of the SEM results. The authors report the negative relatedness-to-competence association (table: β = -0.16, p = .012) was unexpected and offer explanations: the single-item operationalisation captures general interpersonal connectedness, and reliance on interpersonal support within teaching contexts may reduce perceived need to develop AI competence.

> "The negative association between need for relatedness and peda - gogical AI competence was unexpected. Relatedness was operational - ised using a single item (“I feel close and connected with other people who are important to me”) which captures general interpersonal connected - ness."
