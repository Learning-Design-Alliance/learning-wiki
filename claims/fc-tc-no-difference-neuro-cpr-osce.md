# No significant difference between flipped and traditional classroom interns in neurological physical examination or CPR OSCE scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference between groups in neurological physical examination (P = 0.157) or CPR (P = 0.920). [→ Zhang Weihao 2021](#zhang-weihao-2021)

## Evidence

### Zhang Weihao 2021

Zhang Weihao, Gu Jiaoyang, Li Fan, Feng Feifei, Chen Huiqiang, Xing Xiaowei, Liu Lan. (2021). The effect of flipped classroom in multiple clinical skills training for clinical interns on Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE). Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.2013405

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized OSCE comparison, "There was no difference in average skills scores between groups A and B" for neurological examination and CPR; equivalence was not tested. The neurological station had the lowest average score of all stations.

> "There was no difference in average skills scores between groups A and B in neurological physical examination (91.620 ± 4.818 vs. 93.050 ± 3.993, P = 0.157) and CPR (97.840 ± 1.424 vs. 97.880 ± 2.233, P = 0.920)."
