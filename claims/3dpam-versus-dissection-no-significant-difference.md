`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing O'Reilly et al., a comparison of one hour of 3DPAM learning with one hour of dissection on the lower limb; "There was no significant difference between the two teaching methods." Equivalence was not tested.

> "A single study compared one hour of learning using a 3DPAM (lower limb) with one hour of dissection on the same anatomical area [78]. There was no significant difference between the two teaching methods."
