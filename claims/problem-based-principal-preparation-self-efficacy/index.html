Evidence¶

Sutcher 2017¶

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's review of principal preparation research describes problem-based learning methods, including group discussions, role-playing, video case studies, and simulations, as grounding candidates' learning in authentic experiences while broadening problem-framing skills.

"Effective programs use context-specific problems to connect coursework and practice to enrich candidates' skill development."

Sutcher 2017 (2)¶

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report describes San Diego State University's redesigned 18-month comprehensive fieldwork project focused on improving achievement for a candidate-selected target group of students, with coursework supporting each project stage.

"Researchers have noted that the restructuring was effective in providing candidates opportunities to connect program experiences with genuine school leadership responsibilities and in improving candidates' sense of self-efficacy."
