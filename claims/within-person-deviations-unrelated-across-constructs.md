# Within-person deviations from math and self-efficacy growth trajectories at a given time point are not related across constructs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within-person deviations from growth trajectories at a given point in time do not appear related across the math and self-efficacy constructs. [→ James Soland 2019](#james-soland-2019)

## Evidence

### James Soland 2019

James Soland. (2019). Modeling academic achievement and self-efficacy as joint developmental processes: Evidence for education, counseling, and policy. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology 65, 101076. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2019.101076

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Joint latent curve models fitted to repeated measures of math achievement and self-efficacy examined how the constructs relate within persons. The article reports that within-person deviations "do not appear related across constructs"; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Results indicate that, while there is a significant association between growth in self-efficacy and growth in math, within-person deviations from those growth trajectories at a given point in time do not appear related across constructs."
