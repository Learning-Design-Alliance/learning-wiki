### Alizadeh Maryam 2024 (2)

Alizadeh Maryam, Masoomi Rasoul, Mafinejad Mahboobeh Khabaz, Parmelee Dean, Khalaf Rusul Jasim, Norouzi Ali. (2024). Team-based learning in health professions education: an umbrella review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06147-x

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Umbrella review synthesis: three reviews (13%) reported on student preferences for TBL versus lecture, with mixed results. Three reviews also consistently found positive attitudes toward TBL versus lecture.

> "Two of the reviews indicated that learners preferred TBL over traditional forms of instruction, particularly lecture [19, 32]. However, one review reported that students did not prefer TBL over lectures [28]."
