Yasin 2018¶

Yasin, B., Mustafa, F., & Permatasari, R. (2018). How Much Videos Win Over Audios in Listening Instruction for EFL Learners. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165770

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Post-test score distributions from the same pre-test/post-test control-group experiment (Table 2). Applying the effectiveness criterion the article attributes to Mopili (2012), the authors conclude video material "was proven to be an effective medium" while audio was less effective.

"More than three-quarters of the students in the video group (88%) scored 60 or more in the post-test. Meanwhile, only 39% of the students in the audio group achieved a score of 60 or more."
