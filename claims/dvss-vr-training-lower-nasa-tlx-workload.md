`q3 i?` The VR-training group reported significantly lower NASA TLX overall score (40.04 ± 10.55 vs. 48.2 ± 9.88; p < 0.01), lower physical demand (3.82 ± 2.46 vs. 8.42 ± 6.04; p < 0.01) and higher performance satisfaction (18.72 ± 8.09 vs. 12.55 ± 5.62; p < 0.01) than the control group. NASA TLX self-assessments collected between tasks in the randomized controlled study. The study reports the training group's "significantly lower Physical demand" and overall score and "significantly higher Performance" than controls, all at p < 0.01.

> "Compared with the control group, the VR-training group had significantly lower Physical demand (3.82 ± 2.46 vs. 8.42 ± 6.04; p < 0.01) and NASA TLX overall score (40.04 ± 10.55 vs. 48.2 ± 9.88; p < 0.01) and significantly higher Performance (18.72 ± 8.09 vs. 12.55 ± 5.62; p < 0.01)"
