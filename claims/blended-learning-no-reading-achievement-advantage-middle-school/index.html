Pace and Mellard 2016¶

Jesse R. Pace and Daryl F. Mellard. (2016). Reading Achievement and Reading Efficacy Changes for Middle School Students With Disabilities Through Blended Learning Instruction. Journal of Special Education Technology. https://doi.org/10.1177/0162643416660837

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental repeated-measures ANCOVA comparing growth in MAP reading percentile ranks across two blended schools (BELA1, BELA2) and one face-to-face school (TELA), with September scores as covariate. The study reports "no s ignificant changes in reading achievement were found" attributable to treatment, gender, or disability status.

"The results o f the analysis indicated that no s ignificant changes in reading achievement were found that could be attributed solely to treatment versus comparison, to gender, or to disability status."
