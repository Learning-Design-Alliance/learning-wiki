# Participation in the Pathways to Quantum immersion program increased high school students' awareness of and interest in QIS and QIS-adjacent careers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the program, students could identify QIS careers beyond quantum physicist or computer scientist, and their interest in pursuing those careers increased. [→ Simons 2025](#simons-2025)

## Evidence

### Simons 2025

Simons, J. R., Holincheck, N., Rosenberg, J. L., & Akesson, L. M. (2025). Pathways to Quantum: Fostering High School Student Interest in Quantum Information Science. [doi:10.1109/qce65121.2025.20509](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce65121.2025.20509)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of focus-group interviews with 45 Pathways participants conducted at the end of the in-person immersion week; students reported expanded awareness of quantum careers, with one noting "there's a lot of fields within quantum that you can go into for research."

> "At the end of the Pathways program, students overwhelmingly noted an increase in awareness of potential QIS careers and interest in pursuing QIS or QIS-adjacent careers. Prior knowledge of quantum career paths was often confined to quantum computing or physics."
