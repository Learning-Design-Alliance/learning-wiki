# For some students, finding an expression for the electric field triggered use of the mathematical form of Gauss’s law while plotting the field triggered qualitative reasoning

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Asking students to find an expression for the electric field triggered them to use the mathematical form of Gauss’s law or try to recall a mathematical expression, while asking the same students to plot the electric field led them to think qualitatively about Gauss’s law. [→ Maries 2016](#maries-2016)
`q1 i?` When plotting the electric field, the same students reasoned qualitatively about Gauss’s law to determine the field’s behavior, such as whether it is zero or proportional to 1/r2. [→ Maries 2016 (2)](#maries-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Maries 2016

Maries, A., Lin, S.-Y., and Singh, C. (2016). The impact of students’ epistemological framing on a task requiring representational consistency. 2016 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.048

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of think-aloud interviews with three introductory physics students who had completed electrostatics. The authors report that ‘asking them to find an expression for the electric field’ triggered use of the mathematical form of Gauss’s law.

> "asking them to find an expression for the electric field in the given situation triggers them to use the mathematical form of Gauss’s law or try to recall a mathematical expression for the electric field"

### Maries 2016 (2)

Maries, A., Lin, S.-Y., and Singh, C. (2016). The impact of students’ epistemological framing on a task requiring representational consistency. 2016 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.048

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the same interview study, ‘when asked to plot the electric field, the same students may think qualitatively about Gauss’s law’ to determine the field’s behavior in different regions.

> "when asked to plot the electric field, the same students may think qualitatively about Gauss’s law in order to determine the behavior of the electric field"
