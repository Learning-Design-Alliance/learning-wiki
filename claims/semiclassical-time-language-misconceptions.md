### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The article's analysis of difficulties with time-development argues students who "do not understand the limits of these models" come to believe, literally from semi-classical language, in time-dependence within stationary states.

> "Students who do not understand the limits of these models take the word “time" literally and believe that there is a non-trivial time-dependence even in a stationary state."
