## Evidence

### Koedinger 2010

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc association analysis in the quasi-experiment; all 125 special education students using ASSISTment were immersed and only 15 immersed treatment students were non-users. The authors note the technology may make immersion easier to implement.

> "In the treatment schools, 70% of special education students (140 of 199) are immersed, but only 43% of special education students (24 of 56) are immersed in the control school, a significant association ( /c992 = 14.39, p = .000)."
