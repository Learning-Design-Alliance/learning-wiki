## Evidence

### Aharonian K 2024

Aharonian K, Winter V, Schlesinger T, Simanton E. (2024). Medical students as teachers: impact of peer-led teaching sessions on NBME exam performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06069-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of de-identified program evaluation records from 67 first-year medical students, comparing 26 PLTS attendees (at least five sessions) with 31 non-attendees before the first exam. The article reports "an average higher score of 4.28%" for attendees, statistically significant at p = 0.036. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The PLTS attendees demonstrated an average higher score of 4.28% compared to the control group, a result that was statistically significant with a p-value of 0.036."
