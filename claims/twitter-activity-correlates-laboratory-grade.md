## Evidence

### Smaragdi Loutou 2018

Smaragdi Loutou, Nikolaos Tselios, Panagiota Altanopoulou. (2018). The effect of twitter-mediated activities on learning outcome and student engagement: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the same 17-student case study; the laboratory grade was the average of four compulsory mini-projects. The article reports it was "highly correlated with the total number of comments made by the students", with Pearson's r = 0.63, p = 0.00.

> "For the final grade of the laboratory appear to be highly correlated with the total number of comments made by the students (Pearson's r = 0,63, p = 0,00 <0,05, s)."

### Smaragdi Loutou 2018 (2)

Smaragdi Loutou, Nikolaos Tselios, Panagiota Altanopoulou. (2018). The effect of twitter-mediated activities on learning outcome and student engagement: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Secondary research question analysis in the same case study, relating laboratory grade to how understandable students found the weekly Twitter questions. The article reports "a high correlation between the two variables", with Pearson's r = 0.62, p = 0.00.

> "It found a high correlation between the two variables (Pearson's r = 0,62, p = 0,00 <0,05, s)."
