Borman 2013¶

Borman, G. D., & Grigg, J. (2013). An Effort to Close Achievement Gaps at Scale through Self-Affirmation. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563077

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel ITT analysis of reading scores; the Female x Self-Affirmation interaction was -8.641 (SE 3.532, p < 0.05), offsetting the female main effect of 8.694 (p < 0.01). The article reports the "8.7 point (0.15 SD) advantage that girls had over boys in the comparison group was not apparent in the self-affirmation group."
