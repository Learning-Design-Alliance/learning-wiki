## Evidence

### Randall Armel 2025

Randall Armel, S. (2025). Small programs, big challenges: Reimagining the evaluation of clinical teaching in genetic counseling. Journal of Genetic Counseling, 34, e1924. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.1924

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Study reported in this review (attributed to Boerboom et al., 2011): teachers received a feedback report combining MCTQ student ratings with self-ratings, then completed a pre-structured written reflection, with a subset joining a peer group first; "those who participated in the peer group had deeper critical reflection".

> "Results demonstrated that those who participated in the peer group had deeper critical reflection and more specific plans for modifying their teaching."
