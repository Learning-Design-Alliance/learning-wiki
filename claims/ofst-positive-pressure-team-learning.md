## Evidence

### Wang Ting-Huai 2025

Wang Ting-Huai, Xu Xiao-Dan, Long Zhi-Yuan, Cao Ying-Nan, Lai Ning-Yi, Ma Yong-Hong, Tan Xiao-Qiu, Xin Min, Jiang Jin-Yan, Yang Da-Ya. (2025). Exploring the integration of OFST and IPE in cross-regional physiology teaching-a novel approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07412-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary psychological fluctuation survey (N = 68) administered during Step 3 team learning, adapted from frameworks on emotional dynamics in team-based learning. The article reports only three students (4.4%) held the opposite opinion and one student (1.5%) reported feeling inferior.

> "When faced with a temporary gap in knowledge or skills with their team members, 39 students (57.4%) felt some psychological pressure. Still, they believed it could stimulate their learning enthusiasm"

### Wang Ting-Huai 2025 (2)

Wang Ting-Huai, Xu Xiao-Dan, Long Zhi-Yuan, Cao Ying-Nan, Lai Ning-Yi, Ma Yong-Hong, Tan Xiao-Qiu, Xin Min, Jiang Jin-Yan, Yang Da-Ya. (2025). Exploring the integration of OFST and IPE in cross-regional physiology teaching-a novel approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07412-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same N = 68 survey, Table 2–1, on responses to disagreement in interprofessional group discussions. The article reports no students chose options of being easily influenced or refusing dialogue.

> "During group discussions, when encountering disagreement with the opinions of other members, 64 students (94.1%) maintained their views while listening to valuable opinions from group members"
