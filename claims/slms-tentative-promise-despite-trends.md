## Evidence

### Borman 2013

Borman, K., Murphy, S. P., LoSasso, J., & Schlanger, L. (2013). Preliminary Findings from an Efficacy Study of a Distributed Leadership Model of School Organization. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564091

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretive conclusion in the Conclusions section, resting on three stated reasons: implementation with low levels of fidelity, a seeming minority of championing participants, and signs that the structural dimension fosters "vast and dense collegial ties not immediately evident in control schools." No effect size or test is reported.

> "Tentatively, we conclude the SLMS intervention shows promise for boosting student achievement outcomes, despite the descriptive quantitative trends implying otherwise."
