### Fahl Jan Torge 2023

Fahl Jan Torge, Duvivier Robbert, Reinke Laurens, Pierie Jean-Pierre E. N., Schönrock-Adema Johanna. (2023). Towards best practice in developing motor skills: a systematic review on spacing in VR simulator-based psychomotor training for surgical novices. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04046-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Randomized control trial (n=20 students) using the SIMENDO VR simulator, three daily sessions versus three same-day sessions with a 15-minute break. The review reports the spaced group "performed significantly faster (18.7%) than the massed group (p < .05)"; collision and path-length differences were not significant.
