Evidence¶

Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019¶

Diego Pablo de la Hera, Mariano Sigman and Cecilia Ines Calero. (2019). Social interaction and conceptual change pave the way away from children 's misconceptions about the Earth. npj Science of Learning 4:12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0051-3

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Paired-samples t tests within the 15 peer-interaction dyads across interviews. Less-knowledgeable members showed a "significant positive knowledge level shift" with d = 0.83, t(14) = 3.21, p = 0.006.

"These analyses showed a signiﬁcant positive knowledge level shift for the less- knowledgeable dyad members ( ΔL 12), t(14) = 3.21, p = 0.006, d = 0.83, 95% CI (0.23, 1.41)."
