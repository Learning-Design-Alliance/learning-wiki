# Explicitly discussing that feeling stupid is a legitimate part of science reduced educator disengagement in NITARP

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After NITARP began explicitly and often discussing that it is legitimate to feel stupid and legitimate to not like it, fewer teachers disengaged from the program. [→ Rebull 2018](#rebull-2018)

## Evidence

### Rebull 2018

Rebull, L. M., Fitzgerald, M., Roberts, T., French, D. A., Laurence, W., Gorjian, V., & Squires, G. K. (2018). The NASA/IPAC Teacher Archive Research Program (NITARP). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1804.08743](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1804.08743)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Program-experience report from the Bootcamp section: some educators previously shut down because the unfamiliar feeling of not having command of information was overwhelming. The program shares Schwarz (2008), "The Importance of Stupidity in Scientific Research". No effect size is printed.

> "Fewer teachers disengage now since this discussion has been implemented."
