## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 2, unconscious thinkers significantly outperformed conscious thinkers and immediate responders in choosing both their most creative and their least creative idea. [→ Ritter 2012](#ritter-2012)

## Evidence

### Ritter 2012

Ritter, S.M.; van Baaren, R.B.; Dijksterhuis, A.J. (2012). Creativity: The role of unconscious processes in idea generation and idea selection. Thinking Skills and Creativity, 7, 21-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2011.12.002

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 replicated Experiment 1 with 68 students on a different task (ideas for how students can earn some extra money) and added least-creative-idea selection; the condition effect was significant for both selection outcomes, /US2(2) = 8.00 and /US2(2) = 7.20, both p < .05.

> "unconscious thinkers signiﬁcantly outperformed conscious thinkers and participants who immediately listed their ideas in choosing their most creative idea, /US2(1) = 4.83, p < .05, /US2(1) = 6.42, p < .05, and their least creative idea, /US2(1) = 5.11, p < .05, /US2(1) = 5.87, p < .05, respectively."
