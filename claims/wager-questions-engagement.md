## Evidence

### Chou E 2026

Chou E, Cooper D, Goel P, Tang J, Henkle G, Zemeida S, Wolfson H, Shyu YL, Chang E, Abram A, Gerriets VA, Cusick JK. (2026). The use of voiceovers and review games in teaching immunology. ImmunoHorizons. https://doi.org/10.1093/immhor/vlag042

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-study survey on review game logistics, scored on a 7-point Likert scale. The study reports students "overwhelmingly thought this option helped keep them engaged, especially for students not listed on the leaderboard periodically shown throughout the review game (5.68; n = 56)."

> "Students overwhelmingly thought this option helped keep them engaged, especially for students not listed on the leaderboard periodically shown throughout the review game (5.68; n = 56), because wager questions provide a better opportunity for poorly performing teams to make it back onto the leaderboard prior to the conclusion of the game."
