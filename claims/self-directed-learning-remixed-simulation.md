## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students enjoyed controlling variables and evaluating hypotheses on their own, and most addressed their alternative misconception (that an object can only reach the same height as its initial position) before it was formally addressed. [→ Lye 2014](#lye-2014)

## Evidence

### Lye 2014

Lye, S. Y., Wee, L. K., Kwek, Y. C., Abas, S., & Tay, L. Y. (2014). Design, customization and implementation of energy simulation with 5E model in elementary classroom. Educational Technology & Society, 17(3), 121-137. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1408.7040](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1408.7040)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Triangulated qualitative and survey data from the implementation stage: field observations, focus group discussion, Google Form submissions and the perception survey (customization enjoyment M=4.24, SD=1.23). Students experimented with starting speed and height so the coaster could arrive higher than the initial position.

> "From field observations, the students figured out how to cus tomize the roller coaster  on their own. They enjoyed the customization process as evident from the perception survey (M=4.24, SD=1.23)."
