## Evidence

### Gider Neslihan Yağmur 2025

Gider Neslihan Yağmur, Tosun Betül, Dirgar Ezgi (2025). Evaluation of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Intensive Care Unit Nurses Regarding Eye Care: A Descriptive Study. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison within the 207-nurse Turkish survey: 145 nurses following current practices versus 62 who did not, compared on the ECCCQ total score. The article reports a significant difference (p = 0.036) with no printed effect size; the implementation sub-dimension difference (p = 0.076) was not significant.

> "Nurses who actively followed current eye care practices had significantly higher total scores (p = 0.036)."
