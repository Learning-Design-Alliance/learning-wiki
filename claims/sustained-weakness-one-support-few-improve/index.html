The brief reports, citing Bryk et al. (2010), that a sustained weakness in one domain ultimately undermined other change efforts. This prior finding motivates measuring all five supports together in early education settings.

"Fewer than 10 percent of schools with a sustained weakness in one essential support showed improvements in student outcomes"
