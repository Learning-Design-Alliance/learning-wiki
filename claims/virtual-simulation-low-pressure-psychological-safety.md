## Evidence

### Molabe Manoko 2026

Molabe Manoko, Rasesemola Richard, Matlala Sidwell, Matshaka Lerato. (2026). Virtual simulation as a catalyst for equitable and authentic interprofessional learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09773-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group participants described accessing high-fidelity simulation from home laptops, completing scenarios independently without a note-taker, and reduced anxiety from absent physical observation. One said they could "finally demonstrate what I knew". Self-reported perceptions only.

> "Virtual simulation enabled me to focus on the clinical decision-making without the unnecessary anxiety of everyone physically staring at me. I could finally demonstrate what I knew"
