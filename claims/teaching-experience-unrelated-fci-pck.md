## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 15 instructors who had taught introductory mechanics in the past seven years (average 6.33, std. dev. 0.77) with 15 who had not (average 6.17, std. dev. 1.03). The article concludes recent teaching experience is not related to this PCK ability.

> "The average of the instructors who had taught introductory mechanics courses in the past seven years was nearly identical to the average of instructors who had not taught those introductory physics courses recently"

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013 (2)

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

t-test comparison of the total PCK scores of the 30 instructors and the 25 graduate students. The article reports no significant difference between the two groups despite instructors' much greater classroom teaching experience.

> "t-tests revealed no significant difference between instructors and graduate students in terms of their FCI related PCK scores"
