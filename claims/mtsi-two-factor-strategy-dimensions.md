# A task-specific multiple-text strategy inventory yields two correlated strategy dimensions: cross-text elaboration and accumulation of information

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exploratory factor analysis of the 15-item MTSI in 216 undergraduates produced a two-factor solution with all 10 cross-text elaboration items on one factor and all five accumulation items on the other. [→ Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø 2011](#ivar-bråten-helge-i-strømsø-2011)
`q2 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis indicated an acceptable but not spectacular fit for the two-factor model. [→ Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø 2011 (2)](#ivar-bråten-helge-i-strømsø-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø 2011

Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø. (2011). Measuring strategic processing when students read multiple texts. Metacognition Learning 6:111–130. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9075-7

`q2 · i?`

Maximum likelihood exploratory factor analysis with oblique rotation on the 15 MTSI items in a sample of 216 Norwegian education undergraduates. The two factors explained "52.1% of the total sample variation"; factor reliabilities were .88 (cross-text elaboration) and .82 (accumulation).

> "This two-factor solution included all 15 items, and the two factors had eigenvalues of 5.08 and 2.74, respectively, explaining 52.1% of the total sample variation."

### Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø 2011 (2)

Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø. (2011). Measuring strategic processing when students read multiple texts. Metacognition Learning 6:111–130. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9075-7

`q2 · i?`

Confirmatory factor analysis in Mplus 6 on the same 216-student sample tested the two-factor model. The authors describe the fit as "acceptable but not spectacular", reporting CFI=.88 and RMSEA=.089.

> "the fit indices indicated an acceptable but not spectacular fit of the model, with χ²(89, n=216)=244.47, p<.001, CFI=.88, RMSEA=.089, and SRMR=.065"
