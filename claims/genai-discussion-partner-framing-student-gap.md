### Vidar Skogvoll and Tor Ole Odden 2026

Vidar Skogvoll and Tor Ole Odden. (2026). How Physics Professors Use and Frame Generative AI Tools. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study, discussion-partner section. The authors report “eight of the professors to frame GenAI as a discussion partner”, focusing on discussing and refining one’s own ideas rather than obtaining answers.

> "This fact led eight of the professors to frame GenAI as a discussion partner, often using metaphors that described GenAI as a“buddy”or a“sparring partner.”"

### Vidar Skogvoll and Tor Ole Odden 2026 (2)

Vidar Skogvoll and Tor Ole Odden. (2026). How Physics Professors Use and Frame Generative AI Tools. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same section, on student uptake. The authors report Robert “saw that students mostly used it for low-level technical tasks”, attributing this to a lack of good role models for interacting with the AI.

> "While Robert had hoped that students would engage in “Socratic dialogue” and sensemaking with the AI, in reality, he saw that students mostly used it for low-level technical tasks."
