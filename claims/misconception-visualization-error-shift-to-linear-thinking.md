## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the abstraction question on exponential growth of folded paper, 47.4% of students answered some cm thick, reflecting avoidance of exponential thinking. [→ M. Risch 2010](#m-risch-2010)

## Evidence

### M. Risch 2010

M. Risch. (2010). Investigations about Science Misconceptions. ArXiv.org. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paper-folding exponential growth question (Question 1, Table I) answered by 941 students: "will become some cm thick" was chosen by 446 students (47.4%), only 12.1% chose thousands of km. The pattern is described as the authors' interpretation of answer distributions.

> "These answers show a rather naive flat-minded way of thinking avoiding the complications of grasping the exponential. Here imagination is required rather than observation, the misconception is caused by shifting imagination from difficult exponential thinking to straightforward linear thinking."
