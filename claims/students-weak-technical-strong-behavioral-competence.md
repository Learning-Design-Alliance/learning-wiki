## Evidence

### Liu Fengqiong 2025

Liu Fengqiong, Huang Shuqian, Chen Fa, Yang Suhua, Wu Lixin, Lin Yulan, He Baochang, Hu Zhijian. (2025). Development of a core competence model for improving medical college students’ ability in respond to public health emergencies. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1467832

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 441 public health undergraduates at Fujian Medical University, sampled by specialty and admission year with a response rate above 90%. Scores are self-reported on a 5-point scale. These competence levels are reported as descriptive means; the article also fits regressions of competence on student characteristics (for example β = −0.141 for sex, β = −0.237 for school year), but prints no effect size for the levels themselves.

> "the average performance of intellectual and practical competences is 3 in a 5-point scale, which equals 60 converted to 100 points."

### Liu Fengqiong 2025 (2)

Liu Fengqiong, Huang Shuqian, Chen Fa, Yang Suhua, Wu Lixin, Lin Yulan, He Baochang, Hu Zhijian. (2025). Development of a core competence model for improving medical college students’ ability in respond to public health emergencies. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1467832

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 441-student survey; Table 4 shows medians of 4 for resilience, communications, execution, teamwork, big picture thinking, dedication, discipline, carefulness, actively learns, responsibilities and professional identity, with innovation at 3. Descriptive only.

> "participants exhibited much better performance in indicators of behavioral competences and personnel and motivation with an average score of 4 for most of the items, which equals 80 converted to 100 points."
