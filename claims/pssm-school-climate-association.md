## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among middle-school students, a more favorable school climate on the QSL significantly distinguished High from Low PSSM groups (Wilks lambda = .733, p < .001), with all subscales and Total significant and the Total-score correlation reaching r = .52. [→ Hagborg 1994](#hagborg-1994)

## Evidence

### Hagborg 1994

Winston J. Hagborg. (1994). An Exploration of School Membership among Middle- and High-School Students. Journal of Psychoeducational Assessment. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=An+Exploration+of+School+Membership+among+Middle-+and+High-School+Students

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Study 2 middle-school students completed the Quality of School Life scale; one-way ANOVAs (df 1, 118) on the three QSL subscales and Total were all statistically significant, with PSSM correlations ranging up to r = .52 for the Total score.

> "a MANOVA confirmed that a more favorable school climate, as indicated by higher scores on the QSL, significantly distinguished the Low and High groups, Wilks lambda = .733, Rao's R (3, 116) 14.08, p < .001."
