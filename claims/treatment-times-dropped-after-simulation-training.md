## Evidence

### Høllesli 2022

Høllesli, L. J., Ajmi, S. C., Kurz, M. W., Tysland, T. B., Hagir, M., Dalen, I., Qvindesland, S. A., Ersdal, H., & Kurz, K. D. (2022). Simulation‐based team‐training in acute stroke: Is it safe to speed up? Brain and Behavior, 12, e2814. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.2814

`q3 · i?`

Yearly comparison of median door-to-needle times in the prospective registry (Table 1 shows medians falling from 25 minutes in 2016 to 15 in 2017), tested with the Mann–Whitney test. The article reports the drop as significant at p < .001; no effect size is printed.

> "Treatment times dropped significantly between 2016 and 2017, after introduction of the simulation training (p < .001) (Table 1)."
