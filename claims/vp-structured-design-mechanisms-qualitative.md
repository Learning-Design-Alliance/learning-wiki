### Chen Yuanshuai 2026

Chen Yuanshuai, Jiang Leyi, Wu Lingyan, Gong Wenxin, Wu Jingni, Zhen Junhai, Tong Yuling, Guo Yi, Xu Zhijie. (2026). A virtual patient-based training program to enhance clinical decision-making competencies in general practice residents: mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09607-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of eight focus group interviews with 46 of the 50 residents (4–7 per group), following Braun and Clarke's six-phase framework. Residents described "immediate, criterion‑referenced feedback" consolidating a structured, metacognitive decision-making approach.

> "Participants perceived the immediate, criterion‑referenced feedback as a key mechanism for consolidating this cognitive change. Residents refined their clinical judgment through this iterative practice"

### Chen Yuanshuai 2026 (2)

A virtual patient-based training program to enhance clinical decision-making competencies in general practice residents: mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09607-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group theme on reflective learning. Residents reported that hearing peers' alternative diagnostic pathways in weekly workshops "transformed isolated VP exercises into collaborative learning experiences" and prevented entrenchment of errors.

> "Weekly facilitated debriefing sessions transformed isolated VP exercises into collaborative learning experiences. By verbalizing their clinical reasoning to peers and experts and hearing alternative diagnostic pathways"
