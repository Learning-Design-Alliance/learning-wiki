### Yaël Nazé 2012 (2)

Yaël Nazé, Sébastien Fontaine. (2012). An astronomical survey conducted in Belgium. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moon-phase cause question answered by students; the correct proposal (Moon orbiting Earth) was chosen by 28% of nightly-timescale and 38% of monthly-timescale students, and lunar eclipses appear totally mixed with lunar phases.

> "The propo sal that phases are linked to the Moon passing into Earth’s shadow is chosen by about 40% of students assigning a nightly or monthly timescale for the phases."
