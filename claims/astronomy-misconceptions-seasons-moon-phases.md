## Evidence

### Yaël Nazé 2013

Yaël Nazé, Sébastien Fontaine. (2013). An astronomical survey conducted in Belgium. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reasoning questions on the cause of seasons in the 2012 survey (students and public). The article notes this error coexists with correct answers that seasons are inverted between hemispheres, an apparent contradiction respondents do not see.

> "However, a sh orter Earth-Sun distance is to blame for summer’s heat for half of the respondents, with onl y 30% rejecting this possibility."

### Yaël Nazé 2013 (2)

Yaël Nazé, Sébastien Fontaine. (2013). An astronomical survey conducted in Belgium. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.3963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moon-phase cause question answered by students; the correct proposal (Moon orbiting Earth) was chosen by 28% of nightly-timescale and 38% of monthly-timescale students, and lunar eclipses appear totally mixed with lunar phases.

> "The propo sal that phases are linked to the Moon passing into Earth’s shadow is chosen by about 40% of students assigning a nightly or monthly timescale for the phases."
