## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For Focusing on Essential Information, D-Illustrate was rated significantly higher than D-Slowdown; for Fostering Connection between Text and Image, D-Illustrate was rated significantly higher than D-Declutter. [→ Si Chen 2026](#si-chen-2026)

## Evidence

### Si Chen 2026

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Post-hoc analysis of linear mixed models on LCD ratings in the 16-participant study; the same section reports the Fostering Connection contrast, D-Illustrate (M = 5.46) significantly higher than D-Declutter (M = 4.69, t(252) = 3.33, p < .01). For Reducing Irrelevant Information there were no significant differences between the four design ideas.

> "for Focusing on Essential Information , D-Illustrate (M = 5.50, SD = 1.15) was rated significantly higher than D-Slowdown (M = 4.83, SD = 1.28) (t(252) = 3.34, p < .01)."
