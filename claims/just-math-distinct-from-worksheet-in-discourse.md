# Just Math differs from the Worksheet e-frame in discourse content and expectations, not behaviors

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The distinction between Just Math and the Worksheet e-frame lies in discourse content and expectations: in Just Math students expect to do math, whereas in the Worksheet e-frame they expect to write down an answer. [→ Steven F. Wolf 2014](#steven-f-wolf-2014)

## Evidence

### Steven F. Wolf 2014

Steven F. Wolf, Leanne Doughty, Paul W. Irving, Eleanor C. Sayre and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Just Math: A New Epistemic Frame. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' comparative interpretation from their video analysis: behaviors are typical of the Worksheet e-frame, but distinctions are evident in group discourse, including possible off-topic conversation and the emphasis on math-oriented discourse to infer shared framing.

> "This is in contrast to the Worksheet e-frame where students’ expectation is that they now need to write down an answer."
