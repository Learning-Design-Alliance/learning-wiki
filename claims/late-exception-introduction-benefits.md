The review reports Heffernan et al.'s (2021) experiment in which adults learned to categorize flower images with exceptions presented either from the start or later; later introduction produced significantly better category learning and exception recognition. No effect size is printed.

> "Later introduction of exceptions resulted in significantly better category learning as well as better recognition of the exceptions."
