# College students who overestimate their pretest retrospective judgments engage less in preparatory metacognitive strategies before quizzes in computer-based learning environments

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher pretest calibration discrepancy (overconfidence) is associated with less engagement in preparatory metacognitive actions before quizzes. [→ HaeJin Lee · Nigel Bosch 2025](#haejin-lee-nigel-bosch-2025)

## Evidence

### HaeJin Lee · Nigel Bosch 2025

HaeJin Lee · Nigel Bosch. (2025). Calibration Discrepancy Predicts Students’ Subsequent Metacognitive Strategy Use in Computer-based Learning Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00514-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational regression analysis of trace data from 210 college students in a self-paced web-based statistics environment found "Students who overestimated their pretest retrospective judgments engaged less in metacognitive strategies", with overconfidence measured as estimated minus actual pretest grade.

> "Students who overestimated their pretest retrospective judgments engaged less in metacognitive strategies, particularly in preparatory actions before quizzes ( b = -9.100, p < .001)."
