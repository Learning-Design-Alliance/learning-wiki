## Evidence

### Marquez 2014

Marquez, B., Marquez, J., Vincent, C. G., Pennefather, J., Sprague, J. R., Smolkowski, K., & Yeaton, P. (2014). The Iterative Development and Initial Evaluation of We Have Skills!, an Innovative Approach to Teaching Social Skills to Elementary Students. Education and Treatment of Children, 37(1), 137-161. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070194

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A multi-level regression on posttest ESBA scores nested under teacher, controlling for pretest, with 1,616 students across 70 randomly assigned classrooms. "students in the WHS condition improved by an average of 3.5 points on the ESBA" versus 1.7 for controls.

> "students in the WHS condition improved by an average of 3.5 points on the ESBA while students in the control condition improved by 1.7 points (Hedges' g = .27)"
