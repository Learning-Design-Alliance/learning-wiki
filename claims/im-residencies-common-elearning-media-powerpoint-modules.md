Wittich CM, Agrawal A, Cook DA, Halvorsen AJ, Mandrekar J, Chaudhry S, Dupras DM, Oxentenko AS, Beckman TJ. (2017). E-learning in graduate medical education: survey of residency program directors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0953-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the national APDIM e-learning survey of 214 responding internal medicine residency programs. The survey collected information on types of electronic media used; the two most reported approaches were narrated slide shows and organizational online modules. No inferential test accompanied these frequencies.

> "The most commonly reported e-learning approaches were locally developed PowerPoint slide shows with narration (147; 68.7%) and online modules from professional organizations (144; 67.3%)."
