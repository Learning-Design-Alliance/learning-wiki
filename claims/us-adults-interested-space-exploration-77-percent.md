The article reports, from the NSF Science and Engineering Indicators survey series of US public attitudes, that "a huge number of Americans -- 77% in 1997" expressed interest in space exploration. The same section notes a 30% male-female difference and about 42% interest in the 1998 ESA survey of 14 countries.
