Komachali and Khodareza 2012¶

Maryam Eslahcar Komachali, Mohammadreza Khodareza. (2012). The Effect of Using Vocabulary Flash Card on Iranian Pre-University Students' Vocabulary Knowledge. International Education Studies, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n3p134

The article's own limitations section states the sample was "restricted to pre-university students of a public school in Iran" (100 female students screened to 50), that only textbook vocabulary was used, and that psychological factors such as intelligence, anxiety and motivation were not considered.
