## Evidence

### H. Chad Lane 2023

H. Chad Lane. (2023). Commentary for the International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education Special Issue on K-12 AI Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00359-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Evaluation of the ML4STEM professional development program, which the commentary reports included 18 teachers spread across grade levels in two 75-minute sessions framed as Teachers as Learners and Teachers as Designers. The commentary describes the TPACK-dimension evaluation as "very positive" with no effect sizes printed.

> "A robust evaluation along the dimensions of the TPACK-based approach was very positive and stands as initial evidence that we can bring teachers up to a sufficient level of expertise in a relatively short amount of time to teach ML and improve their interest in doing so."
