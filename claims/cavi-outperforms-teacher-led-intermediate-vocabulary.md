# CAVI using Vocaboly produced significantly higher post-test vocabulary scores than teacher-led instruction for intermediate Iranian EFL learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intermediate learners taught with CAVI scored significantly higher on the vocabulary post-test than intermediate learners taught by the teacher-led method. [→ Nejati 2018](#nejati-2018)
`q3 i?` Before treatment, the intermediate CAVI and teacher-led groups did not differ significantly in vocabulary knowledge. [→ Nejati 2018 (2)](#nejati-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Nejati 2018

Nejati, E., Jahangiri, A. & Salehi, M. R. (2018). The effect of using computer-assisted language learning (CALL) on Iranian EFL learners’ vocabulary learning: An experimental study. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 13(2), 351–362. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1183258

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test on post-test scores of intermediate groups (n = 10 per group, Table 9) after eight sessions. CAVI mean "M = 29.4000" exceeded teacher-led "M = 23.7333"; Table 10 reports t = 3.599, df = 28, Sig. 0.001.

> "According to Table 10, the mean score of the CAVI group ( M = 29.4000) is considerably higher than that of the teacher-led group (M = 23.7333)."

### Nejati 2018 (2)

Nejati, E., Jahangiri, A. & Salehi, M. R. (2018). The effect of using computer-assisted language learning (CALL) on Iranian EFL learners’ vocabulary learning: An experimental study. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 13(2), 351–362. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1183258

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-test on pre-test scores of intermediate groups (n = 10 per group, Table 7): CAVI M = 2.6000 vs teacher-led M = 2.3333, Sig. 0.606, indicating no statistically significant pre-treatment difference; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "the t-test result (0.606) shows that there is no statistically significant difference between the means of the two groups in the pre -test before the administration of the treatment."
