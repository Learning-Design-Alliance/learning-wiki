### Sun and Wu 2015

Sun and Wu. (2015). Analysis of Learning Achievement and Teacher–Student Interactions in Flipped and Conventional Classrooms. http://www.oeconsortium.org/about-oec/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test on post-course teacher–student interaction survey scores (6-point Likert scale): flipped classroom M = 4.46 (SD = .60, n = 68) versus conventional classroom M = 4.41 (SD = .75, n = 88). No effect size was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "As seen in Table 3, there was no significant difference between the teaching methods in terms of teacher–student interactions (t(154) = .44, p = .66)."
