Evidence¶

Sabrina Ludwig 2026¶

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Multiple regression analyses relating log-derived behavioural indicators to problem-solving performance and knowledge gains per group. For DI-PS problem-solving performance, "note-taking' (p = .049)" and both spreadsheet calculations were significant predictors.

"Notably, the factors 'note-taking' (p = .049) and calculations in both spreadsheets 'Price calculation' and 'Decision Table' (p = .026 and p = .001) emerged as statistically significant predictors, indicating their noteworthy influence on problem-solving for group DI-PS"

Sabrina Ludwig 2026 (2)¶

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Regression models for knowledge acquisition showed the predictors "hardly explained the variance in the learning outcomes"; for procedural knowledge, spreadsheet price calculation was the only predictor, and only for group PS-DI.

"Regarding conceptual knowledge, no predictor had a significant effect on problem solving or the learning outcomes in both groups."
