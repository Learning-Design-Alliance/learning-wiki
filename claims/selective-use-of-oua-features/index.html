Evidence¶

Herodotou 2019¶

Herodotou, C., Rienties, B., Boroowa, A., Zdrahal, Z., & Hlosta, M. (2019). A large-scale implementation of predictive learning analytics in higher education: the teachers' role and perspective. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 1273–1306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09685-0

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of six semi-structured interviews (inter-rater agreement 90%) found teachers attended mainly to red/amber flags and VLE activity. The article reports "Other system features, such as the nearest student functionality... were not used by teachers."

"The two main functions teachers found more interesting and helpful when they made use of the OUA dashboard were the OUA colour-coded system indicating students "at risk" (i.e., Fig. 3) and students' activity in the VLE (i.e., Fig. 2)."
