### Zhang Duo 2024

Zhang Duo, Zheng Haoyang, Zhao Ya, Wang Xiaoxiao, Xiao Qi, Zhao Ruiyi, Wang Lan, Hu Kaili, Chen Zelin, Zhou Yanrong. (2024). Unlocking academic achievement of nursing master’s students: ethical leadership, teacher-student relationship and growth mindset – a cross-sectional survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06436-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 262 nursing master’s students in Wuhan, China, analyzed with a saturated structural equation model in Mplus 8.3. The model showed ethical leadership "positively predicting it (β = 0.223, t = 5.542, P < 0.001)"; the bootstrap direct effect was 0.224 (95% CI [0.144, 0.302]), 58.49% of the total effect.

> "Our results indicated that ethical leadership had a significant direct effect on academic achievement, positively predicting it (β = 0.223, t = 5.542, P < 0.001)."
