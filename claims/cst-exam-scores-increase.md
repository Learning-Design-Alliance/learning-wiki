Hui Zheng, Binjing Hua, Qiang Sun, Jun Cao and Fangmin Liu. (2020). Applying a Chemical Structure Teaching Method in the Pharmaceutical Analysis Curriculum to Improve Student Engagement and Learning. J. Chem. Educ. 97, 421−426. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00551

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison of final exam averages from 2013 to 2018 (cohorts of 36, 34, 35, 34, 34, 30 students respectively), shown in Fig. 5. The article reports "the average scores have an increase after the implementation of the CST method"; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

From Fig. 5, the average scores have an increase after the implementation of the CST method, which is positive evidence to support the CST teaching effect."
