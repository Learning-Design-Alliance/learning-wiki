## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Review of Student Difficulties in Upper-Level Quantum Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Multi-university study reviewed (Ref. [24]) using written surveys and interviews after traditional instruction. The review reports the overemphasis on the TISE in courses as a possible cause, and interviews showed students arguing the TISE must hold for a superposition because it holds for each stationary state.

> "More than one-third of the students incorrectly stated that the expression “ ˆHΨ = EΨ” is unconditionally correct, with statements such as the following being typical: “Agree. This is what 80 years of experiment has proven."
