# Teacher candidates' advice for improving technology-infused courses clustered into three themes: better instruction and instructors, more tools and practice, and focus on school-available technology

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Open-ended advice responses reflected three themes: instruction and instructors could be improved, candidates wanted more tools and resources with more practice, and candidates wanted a stronger focus on technology available in schools. [→ Buss 2017](#buss-2017)

## Evidence

### Buss 2017

Buss, R. R., Lindsey, L., Foulger, T. S., Wetzel, K., & Pasquel, S. (2017). Assessing a technology infusion approach in a teacher preparation program. International Journal of Technology in Teaching and Learning, 13(1), 33-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Assessing+a+technology+infusion+approach+in+a+teacher+preparation+program

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative thematic analysis of responses to the advice question. Candidates suggested dedicated technology classes, instructors fluent in technology, more hands-on practice with tools, and learning to capitalize on the technologies actually available in their placement schools.

> "The first theme indicated they thought instruction and instructors could be improved. A second theme represented the desire for more tools and resources and more practice with the tools. A third theme showed respondents wanted a stronger focus on technology that was available in the schools."
