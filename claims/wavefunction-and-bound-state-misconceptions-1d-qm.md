## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

QMS item analysis of written-representation Question 30 among the 226 surveyed students. For the paired pictorial Question 1, "The most common difficulty, experienced by 50% of the students, was that the system did not allow for the asymmetric wavefunction."

> "Question 30 was very challenging and only 29% of the students chose the correct response; 35% claimed that the possible wavefunctions for a particle in an even potential energy well must either be even or odd"

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

QMS item analysis of bound/scattering-state items. On Question 24, "About 2/3 of the students failed to notice the difference between pictures (3) and (4)", believing any well-shaped potential with classical turning points allows bound states.

> "In Question 18, 39% did not know that the scattering states have a continuous energy spectrum and claimed that energy is always discrete in quantum mechanics."
