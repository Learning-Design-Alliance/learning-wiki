## Evidence

### Matthew Tennant 2025

Matthew Tennant, Maggie Meeks, Ben Beaglehole, Chris Frampton, Jane Foley, Emily Ide, Dylan Hill, Carol Dean. (2025). Preparing House Officers for Emergencies in a Psychiatric Setting with Simulation-Based Learning. Academic Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-025-02217-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post evaluation of simulation training for house officers beginning a 3-month psychiatry rotation; confidence was self-rated 1-5 before and directly after training and analysed with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The article reports that "doctors reported feeling more confident in managing a medical emergency in a psychiatric hospital (p < 0.001)". No effect size is printed.
