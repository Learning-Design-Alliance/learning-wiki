Joshua W. Tabor 2020¶

Joshua W. Tabor. (2020). Chaos: exploring an engaging online model for rapid application during the pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09878-y

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary's Limitations and constraints section, citing Comas-Quinn (2011), asserts many teachers lack training in creating effective online learning, a constraint magnified during the pandemic pivot. The review attributes this to the cited work; no new data are reported.

"Unfortunately, many teachers have never been trained, either through education programs or professional development, on how to create effective online learning (Comas-Quinn 2011). This constraint was magnified as schools scrambled to assist teachers in moving their content online."
