Evidence¶

Teodóra Vékony 2022¶

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Mixed-design ANOVA on median RTs across four epochs of the Different Instruction Phase; the Speed Group's RT advantage was significant, with a printed partial eta-squared of 0.45, plus a significant main effect of Epoch and a non-significant Epoch × Group interaction.

"Response times were significantly faster in the Speed Group than in the Accuracy Group [M Accuracy Group = 464 ms ± 13.22 SE, M Speed Group = 348 ms ± 13.22 SE, main effect of Group: F(1,47) = 38.13, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.45, BFexclusion < 0.01]."

Teodóra Vékony 2022 (2)¶

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Mixed-design ANOVA on accuracy in the Different Instruction Phase showed the Accuracy Group near ceiling at "98.6%", a significant group effect with a printed partial eta-squared of 0.35, and decreasing accuracy over epochs overall.

"The Accuracy Group were more accurate than the Speed Group [M Accuracy Group = 98.6% ± 2.2 SE, M Speed Group = 83.3% ± 2.2 SE, main effect of Group: F(1,53) = 24.18, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.35, BFexclusion < 0.001]."

Teodóra Vékony 2022 (3)¶

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

The Epoch × Group interaction on accuracy in the Different Instruction Phase was significant, with a printed partial eta-squared of 0.12: accuracy declined gradually only in the Speed Group, while the Accuracy Group stayed near ceiling.

"The change in accuracy over the course of learning was different between the two groups as a gradual decrease in accuracy was only detectable in the Speed Group [Epoch × Group interaction: F(1.23, 56.57) = 6.16, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.12, BFexclusion = 0.01]."
