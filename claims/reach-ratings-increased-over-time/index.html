Sartain 2020¶

Sartain, L., Zou, A., Gutiérrez, V., Shyjka, A., Hinton, E., Brown, E.R., & Easton, J.Q. (2020). Teacher Evaluation in CPS: Perceptions of REACH Implementation, Five Years In. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

Administrative ratings data across REACH implementation years. The release reports that "REACH evaluation ratings in CPS increased over time," with the highest-rating share rising from 26 percent to 40 percent, and nearly 9 in 10 teachers in the top two ratings in 2016-17.
