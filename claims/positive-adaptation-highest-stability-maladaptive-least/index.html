Wu Y 2026¶

Wu Y (2026) Proﬁles and transitions of psychological adaptation in AI-assisted Japanese language learning. Front. Psychol. 17:1837484. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1837484

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile transition analysis of the three-wave panel; transition probabilities (Table 7) show positive adaptation stability of 84.6% and 83.6%, moderate adaptation 72.9% and 74.0%, and maladaptive adaptation 51.8% and 59.2% across the two intervals.

Related Claims¶

  • Three latent profiles of psychological adaptation to AI-assisted Japanese learning (maladaptive, moderate, positive) are identified at each of three waves — related
  • During a year-long AI curriculum, most students maintained or moved upward in motivational profile, with substantial upward transitions out of the Disengaged profile — related
  • At Time 3, profile membership was significantly associated with Japanese learning self-efficacy and learning burnout, with all three profiles differing pairwise — related
