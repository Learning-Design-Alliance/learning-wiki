### The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning (Henderson 2010

The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning (Henderson, J., Gaston, M., Kingsley, J., Lezin, N., Siri, D.). (2010). Grappling with the Gaps: Toward a Research Agenda to Meet the Educational Needs of Children and Youth in Foster Care. The Stuart Foundation. www.cftl.org. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED509733

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Expert-interview report section on instructional practices. The 12 experts reported a complete absence of research identifying effective instructional practices for students in foster care, stating "we know virtually nothing about what happens in classrooms."

> "As a group, the experts agreed that "we know virtually nothing about what happens in classrooms" for children and youth in foster care. They knew of no research that specifically identified effective instructional practices for this student population."

### The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning (Henderson 2010 (2)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Same expert-interview section: most experts reframed instruction as a general at-risk issue rather than foster-care-specific, noting heterogeneity of causes (developmental delays, interrupted schooling, trauma, mobility).

> "most reframed the issue more broadly and suggested that "any good quality instructional method for any child academically at risk would be good for a child in foster care." They were "uncertain that foster children should be considered a homogeneous group for instruction,""
