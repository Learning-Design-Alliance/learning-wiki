`q2 i?` Self-reported academic performance is positively correlated with information literacy in the moderation model (estimate 0.317, p 0.004). [→ Zhang Xiangle 2025](#zhang-xiangle-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhang Xiangle 2025

Zhang Xiangle, Tian Hongxia. (2025). Modeling information literacy in Chinese physical education: the roles of technology integration, teaching practices, self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26990-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression moderation model (Table 8) among 650 Chinese PE undergraduates; SRAP and IL showed "an estimate of 0.317" at p 0.004. Academic performance was self-reported on the Student Academic Performance Scale.

> "In Model 2, which evaluates the moderating impact, self-reported academic performance (SRAP) and information literacy (IL) are positively correlated with an estimate of 0.317 and a p-value of 0.004."
