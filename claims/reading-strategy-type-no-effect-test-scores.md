# Learners' metacognitive reading strategy type (global, problem-solving, or support) showed no statistically significant effect on reading test scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A one-way ANOVA found no significant effect of reading strategy type on reading test scores. [→ Altay 2017](#altay-2017)

## Evidence

### Altay 2017

Altay, İ. F., & Altay, A. (2017). The impact of online reading tasks and reading strategies on EFL learners' reading test scores. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 136-152. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the experiment group's reading test scores with MARSI-derived strategy type as the independent variable. The article reports no significant effect, "[F(3, 47) = .65, p=0.58]", so no post-hoc test was run.

> "When the scores of learners on reading test were dependent variable, there was not a significant effect of strategies on reading test scores at the p<.05 level for four groups of learners with different strategy use in reading test [F(3, 47) = .65, p=0.58]."
