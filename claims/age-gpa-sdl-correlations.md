### Maghaminejad Farzaneh F 2026

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional study of 300 nursing students. Age correlated moderately with self-directed learning (r = 0.37) while GPA showed a weak but statistically significant association (r = 0.124).

> "the relationship between age and self-directed learning score was moderate (r = 0.37 p < 0.05), while the relationship between overall grade point average and self-directed learning score was weak but significant (r = 0.124)."
