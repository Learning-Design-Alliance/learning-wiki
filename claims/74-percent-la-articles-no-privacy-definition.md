## Evidence

### Olga Viberg 2022

Olga Viberg, Chantal Mutimukwe, Åke Grönlund. (2022). Privacy in LA Research: Understanding the Field to Improve the Practice. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 169–182. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7751

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review of 59 peer-reviewed LA articles (2011–July 2021) coded independently by two authors with Cohen's kappa of .89. The analysis found "a majority (74%; Figure 2) of the papers in our sample addressed privacy in the setting of LA without defining or conceptualizing it." No effect size is printed.

> "Our analysis shows that a majority (74%; Figure 2) of the papers in our sample addressed privacy in the setting of LA without defining or conceptualizing it; 6% defined privacy as a right, 11% as a state, 15% as control, and 16% used other approaches to explain privacy."
