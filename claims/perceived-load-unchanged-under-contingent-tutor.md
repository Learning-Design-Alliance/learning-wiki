# Perceived task load and germane load did not differ between the contingent tutor and either baseline

## Evidence

### Hou 2026

Hou, X., Weng, Y., Yeh, C. H., Lee, D. L., Zheng, L., Li, F., Wang, W., & Liu, Y. (2026). Adaptive Scaffolding Needs Contingency: An AI Tutor That Escalates and Fades on What the Learner Does. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22993

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

After-task self-report from the within-subjects study (N=131), with two one-sided tests against a 0.25-point bound. Perceived task load means were 3.36, 3.30 and 3.29 and germane load 3.85, 3.74 and 3.78 across the three tutors; "neither perceived-load item separates any pair of tutors".

> "neither perceived-load item separates any pair of tutors, and both are equivalent between Tutor C and Tutor B within the same bound: perceived task load sits at 3.36, 3.30 and 3.29 (𝑝TOST =. 020) and germane load at 3.85, 3.74 and 3.78 (𝑝TOST =. 007)"
