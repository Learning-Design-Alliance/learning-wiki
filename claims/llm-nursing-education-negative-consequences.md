## Evidence

### Harrington J 2025

Harrington J, Booth RG, Jackson KT. (2025). Large Language Models in Nursing Education: Concept Analysis. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/77948

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The concept analysis reports emerging rather than empirically established concerns: plagiarism and intellectual property questions, inaccurate or outdated output because LLMs "depend on historical data", embedded biases from training data, and automation bias where students place undue trust in LLM responses.

> "By providing immediate, automated responses, LLMs may weaken students' critical thinking, problem-solving skills, learner creativity, and ability to engage in independent analysis and decision-making"
