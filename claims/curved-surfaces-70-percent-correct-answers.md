### Matteo Luca Ruggiero 2021

Matteo Luca Ruggiero, Sara Mattiello, Matteo Leone. (2021). Physics for the masses: teaching Einsteinian gravity in primary school. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac1ca3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac1ca3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention questionnaire item asking what happens to parallel lines and triangle angles on curved surfaces, administered one month after activities. The article reports 70% correct and correctly motivated answers, and among correct answers "the great majority (70%) are correct but without interpretation" while 30% gave a suitable scientific interpretation.

> "we see that 70% of the ans wers are correct and correctly motivated: "They meet because the Universe is not ﬂat; the sum of inner angles i s not 180 degrees because the surface is modiﬁed""
