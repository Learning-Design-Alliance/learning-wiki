# Unconscious thinkers outperform conscious thinkers and immediate responders in selecting their most creative idea (Experiment 1)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, participants in the unconscious thought condition selected their most creative idea in accordance with trained raters more often than conscious thinkers and immediate responders. [→ Ritter 2012](#ritter-2012)

## Evidence

### Ritter 2012

Ritter, S.M.; van Baaren, R.B.; Dijksterhuis, A.J. (2012). Creativity: The role of unconscious processes in idea generation and idea selection. Thinking Skills and Creativity, 7, 21-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2011.12.002

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a randomized three-condition experiment with 112 Radboud University students, tested idea selection against judgments of three trained raters. The Chi-Square main effect of condition was /US2(2) = 11.15, p < .01, and "unconscious thinkers outperformed conscious thinkers" and immediate responders in selecting their most creative idea.

> "unconscious thinkers outperformed conscious thinkers, /US2(1) = 5.67, p < .05, and those who immediately listed their ideas, /US2(1) = 10.46, p < .01, in selecting their most creative idea."
