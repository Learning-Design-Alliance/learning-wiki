## Evidence

### Roces Matthew MD 2025

Roces Matthew MD, Alacala-Arcos Trinidad BS, Addo Newton BS, Boyle Michael MD, Hewlett Meghan MD, Nguyen Reginald MD, Wong Angela BSN, Peabody Christopher R. MD, Madhok Debbie Y. MD. (2025). Emergency Medicine Residents’ Performance with National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale and Its Impact on Key Stroke-care Metrics. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.39671

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective observational comparison of 369 ischemic stroke patients (247 pre-intervention, 122 post-intervention) at a Level I trauma center. Median door-to-CT was "25 minutes (interquartile range [IQR] 8–74)" pre and 28 minutes post (P=.2); door-to-needle and door-to-groin puncture medians also showed no significant difference (P=.7 and P=.1).

> "Median door-to-CT times were 25 minutes (interquartile range [IQR] 8–74) during the pre-intervention period and 28 minutes (IQR 20–65) for the post-intervention period (P=.2)."
