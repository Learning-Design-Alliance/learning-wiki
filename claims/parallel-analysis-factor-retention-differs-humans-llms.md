`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Factor-retention stage of the EFA pipeline (Table 1), applying Kaiser criterion and parallel analysis separately to human and LLM tetrachoric correlation matrices. Parallel analysis showed group differences; the Kaiser criterion yielded matching counts, so the article calls the between-group results mixed and method-dependent.

> "In Chemistry, humans consistently retained five fac- tors, whereas LLMs most often retained 4. In Quan- titative Reasoning, humans retained 7–8 factors, whereas LLMs consistently retained 5 (same as kaiser retention)."
