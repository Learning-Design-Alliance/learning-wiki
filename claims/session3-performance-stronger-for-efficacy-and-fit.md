## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, H., Thompson‐Lee, S., & Klassen, R. M. (2025). Performance on classroom simulations enhances preservice teachers' motivation in teaching: A latent change perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S239–S255. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent change SEM comparing Session 3 versus Session 2 paths in the same 1411‐participant model. Session 3 performance "had a stronger impact compared with Session 2" on self‐efficacy and fit; the career‐intentions path fell to marginal non‐significance (p = .055).

> "In Session 3, higher performance had a stronger impact compared with Session 2 on the change in participants' self‐efficacy (β = .141, p < .001) and perceived fit (β = .104, p = .001), but not on career intentions (β = .058, p = .055)."
