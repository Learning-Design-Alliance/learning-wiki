`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Coding of posted lecture slides from both courses into three categories (light, matter, contrasting perspectives), with point totals of 15 vs 9 (light) and 28 vs 22 (contrasting perspectives) for PHYS3A vs PHYS3B. The difference is attributed largely to topic category B.

> "PHYS3A had a greater number of slides that scored in the light and contrasting perspectives categories, though the graphs in Fig. 1 (which group the point totals for each course by topic area, as listed in Table I) show that this difference can be largely attributed to instructor choices at the outset of the quantum physics section of the courses, in topic category B (photoelectric effect and photons)."
