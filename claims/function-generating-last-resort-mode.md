## Evidence

### Charlotte Zimmerman 2023

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Michael Loverude, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2023). Expert covariational reasoning resources in physics graphing tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative timeline-chart and transcript analysis of the main-study interviews; the article reports Function Generating as "consistently observed to be a last-resort option" and notes nearly every expert solved Drone by engaging with it. No effect size is printed.

> "Function Generating was consistently observed to be a last-resort option: experts were likely to begin with Function Knowing or Choosing and finally engage in Function Generating only if Function Knowing or Choosing had failed."

### Charlotte Zimmerman 2023 (2)

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Michael Loverude, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2023). Expert covariational reasoning resources in physics graphing tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The article reports that in the observed Function Generating instances, "12 of the 18 instances" involved participants naming the function matching their drawn curve; the rest ended the task after completing the graph. No effect size is printed.

> "In 12 of the 18 instances we observed of function generating, participants named the function that looks like the curve they drew."
