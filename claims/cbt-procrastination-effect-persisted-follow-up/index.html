Evidence¶

Betül Toker 2015¶

Betül Toker, Raşit Avcı. (2015). Effect of Cognitive-Behavioral-Theory-based Skill Training on Academic Procrastination Behaviors of University Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.0077

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up measurement obtained four weeks after the posttest in the same quasi-experimental study (n = 26). The experimental group's follow-up mean (44.09) was lower than the control group's (58.92): "a significant difference between the follow-up test means for the experimental group vs. control group".

"there was a significant difference between the follow-up test means for the experimental group vs. control group [t(24) = 3.439, p < .01]."

Betül Toker 2015 (2)¶

Betül Toker, Raşit Avcı. (2015). Effect of Cognitive-Behavioral-Theory-based Skill Training on Academic Procrastination Behaviors of University Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.0077

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group repeated-measures t-test in the same study found no significant difference between the experimental group's posttest and follow-up means, which the authors read as persistence of the training effect; equivalence was not tested.

"There was no significant difference between the posttest and follow-up test means [t (12) = 1.26, p > .05]"
