`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The trial's within-group comparison of the simulator and human-subject DOPS tests at T2 shows the human-trained group transferred fully (equivalent results, p = 0.97), while the simulator-trained group performed "significantly worse results in the DOPSHuman" (p < 0.01).

> "When comparing results in DOPSSim with DOPSHuman, the control group achieved equivalent results in both DOPS (p = 0.97), whereas the study group showed significantly worse results in the DOPSHuman(p < 0.01)."
