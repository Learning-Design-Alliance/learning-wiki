## Evidence

### Yurdagül Gülistan 2025

Yurdagül Gülistan (2025). Investigation of Nurse's Pressure Ulcer Management Self‐Efficacy Status and Influencing Factors: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal 22, no. 7: e70714. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70714

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-group comparisons (ANOVA and t-tests) in the Results section of the 436-nurse cross-sectional study. The article reports these three characteristics each showed "significantly higher scores than the others" on the scale total; only test statistics are printed, no effect sizes.

> "It was observed that nurses who were 34 years old and above (F(2, 433): 4.970; p: 0.007), had 3 years or more experience in the same unit (T: −2.270; p = 0.024) and worked in intensive care units (F(3, 432): 4.042; p: 0.007) had significantly higher scores than the others."
