Evidence¶

Mayer 1995¶

Mayer, R.E., Steinhoff, K., Bower, G., & Mars, R. (1995). A generative theory of textbook design: Using annotated illustrations to foster meaningful learning of science text. ETR&D, 43, 31-41. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02300480

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the lightning-text experiments with college students, the authors report the integration benefit was "stronger for students who lacked experience in meteorology than for high-experience students." No moderator effect size is printed in the available text.

"The integrated group generated approximately 50% more creative solutions on transfer problems than the separated group, and this pattern was stronger for students who lacked experience in meteorology than for high-experience students."
