The review's narrative synthesis of role play studies with medical and nursing students reports successes including "an increase in empathy" and safe practice, while students noted "the artificiality of the scenario" and poor acting skills. No effect sizes are printed.

> "These studies report successes: an increase in empathy [23, 24]; an opportunity to practice communication skills in a safe and supportive environment [23]; an opportunity to experience more unusual clinical scenarios [24]; the value of peer feedback [26, 27]"
