Evidence¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Self-reported time-allocation shares from the post-learning survey in the randomized experiment; Qualtrics-recorded learning-phase duration changes by only 0.2 minutes and self-reported duration by 0.5 minutes, neither significant. The article reports corresponding TOT effects of a 7.7 pp writing-share decrease and a 6.4 pp research-share increase.

"Treated students spend about 5.3 pp less of their learning time on writing activities (drafting, editing, note-taking, and organizing), down from a control-group share of 53.4 percent (p= 0.028), and about 4.4 pp more on research activities (reading and searching), up from 44.5 percent (p= 0.050)."
