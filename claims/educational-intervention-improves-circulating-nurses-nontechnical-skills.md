# A face-to-face educational intervention significantly improves circulating nurses' overall nontechnical skill scores

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kalantari Reza 2021

Kalantari Reza, Zamanian Zahra, Hasanshahi Mehdi, Faghihi Seyed Aliakbar, Jamali Jamshid, Niakan Hadi. (2021). The Effect of Educational Intervention on the Improvement of Nontechnical Skills in Circulating Nurses. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/5856730

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semiexperimental study of 300 circulating nurses in four public hospitals, divided into intervention and no-intervention groups of 150 each, rated with the CPLINTS observational tool before and eight weeks after training. Postintervention medians were "2.26 and 2.73 (out of 4)" with overall improvement significant at p < 0.001.

> "The median scores of nontechnical skills were 2.26 and 2.73 (out of 4) in the no intervention and intervention groups, respectively, after implementing the educational program. The overall scores of nontechnical skills were significantly improved after the educational intervention (p < 0.001)."
