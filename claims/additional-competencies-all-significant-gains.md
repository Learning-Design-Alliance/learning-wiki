## Evidence

### Gundo Rodwell 2021

Gundo Rodwell, Gundo Beatrice, Chirwa Ellen, Dickinson Annette, Mearns Gael Janine. (2021). Effect of an educational programme on critical care nurses' competence at two tertiary hospitals in Malawi. Malawi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.4314/mmj.v33i4.3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison on a locally developed 4-point scale of 10 competencies (Q3) in the same study (N=41). Table 3 shows the largest descriptive gains for basic ECG interpretation (1.6 to 3.6) and arterial blood gas interpretation (1.8 to 3.5), each p <0.0001.

> "The overall score on the additional competencies from Time 1, pretraining (M = 25.4, SD = 6.2) to Time 2, post training (M = 37.3, SD = 2.8), was also statistically significant, t(40) = 13.3, p <.0001 (two-tailed)."
