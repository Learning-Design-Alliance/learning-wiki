`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis in the same 1,025-student cross-sectional survey. The standardized path from digital literacy to narrative competence was "β = 0.201, P < 0.001", with digital literacy as the exogenous latent variable.

> "digital literacy was directly positively associated with narrative competence (β = 0.201, P < 0.001), whereas self-directed learning aptitude was the strongest predictor (β = 0.428, P < 0.001)"
