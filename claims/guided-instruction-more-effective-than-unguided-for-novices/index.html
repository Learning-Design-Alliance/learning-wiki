Evidence¶

Fred Paas 2010¶

Fred Paas, Tamara van Gog, John Sweller. (2010). Cognitive Load Theory: New Conceptualizations, Specifications, and Integrated Research Perspectives. Educational Psychology Review, 22, 115-121. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9133-8

q3 · i? · review · r1

The review cites Kirschner, Sweller and Clark (2006), reporting "a clear body of research using controlled experiments" comparing guided and unguided procedures. The comparison is attributed to that cited work, not tested in this article; no effect size is printed.

"P . A. Kirschner et al.( 2006) have shown that there exists a clear body of research using controlled experiments indicating that the relatively guided instructional procedures supported by cognitive load theorists are more effective (at least for novice students) than relatively unguided instructional procedures"
