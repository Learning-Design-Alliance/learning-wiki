## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2025

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check in preregistered Experiment 1 (n = 48) using a head-diagram externalization scale based on Plenge (1972) and Best et al. (2020); the printed effect size was φ = .49. The authors note that in the normal audio condition externalization ratings were "distributed across all possible answers instead of being perceived mainly inside the head as expected".

> "The item on externalization showed significant differences between the conditions, χ2(2) = 11.34, p = .003, ϕ = .49. With the spatial audio settings, four participants answered that they perceived the audio explanations external in the room, 20 participants external near the head, and one participant internal in the head."
