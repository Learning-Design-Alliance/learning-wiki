## Evidence

### Simon Cullen 2018

Simon Cullen, Judith Fan, Eva van der Brugge and Adam Elga (2018). Improving analytical reasoning and argument understanding: a quasi-experimental field study of argument visualization. npj Science of Learning 3:21. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0038-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental field study comparing 105 seminar students across seven course iterations with 56 control students recruited from interested freshmen, tested on equivalated LSAT forms 85 days apart. The between-group improvement difference was d = 0.71; seminar students gained "an additional 2.6 questions correctly (SD = 2.8)".

> "the degree of improvement exhibited by seminar students was greater than that exhibited by control students, t(159) = 4.3, p < 0.001, d = 0.71, 95% CI: [0.37, 1.04]"
