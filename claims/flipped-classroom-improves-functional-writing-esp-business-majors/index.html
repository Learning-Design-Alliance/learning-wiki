Evidence¶

Salem 2018¶

Salem, A. A. M. S. (2018). Engaging ESP University Students in Flipped Classrooms for Developing Functional Writing Skills, HOTs, and Eliminating Writer's Block. English Language Teaching, 11(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n12p177

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 51 third-year business majors (26 experimental, 25 control) taught functional writing via flipped sessions or traditional lectures. Table 9 reports total-score means of 75.61 (experimental) vs 61.56 (control), t = 4.61, p = .00, with subskill t values from 3.56 to 5.08, all significant. The study reports "relatively high gains" for the flipped group.

"Close inspection of Table 9 reveals that non-English majors business students in the experimental group achieved relatively high gains in using functional writing skills compared with their counterparts in the control group"
