## Evidence

### W. S. Painter 1914

W. S. Painter. (1914). Efficiency in Mental Multiplication Under Extreme Fatigue. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Final-test observation: after the last four-place by four-place problem (solved in sixteen minutes and thirty seconds), the experimenter could not secure even the first partial product of a three-place by three-place problem. The conclusion reports the loss generalized to any multiplication.

> "when ability mentally to multiply a four-place by a four-place number had come to a stand-still as a result of extreme mental fatigue, ability mentally to multiply any number by any other number was also lost."
