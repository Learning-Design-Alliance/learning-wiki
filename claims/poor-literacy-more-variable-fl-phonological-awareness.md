`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of the natural logarithm of the ratio of coefficients of variation (CVR) for phonological awareness across four studies (95 poor readers/spellers, 104 controls); heterogeneity was non-significant (Q = 3.03), and the CVR of −0.37 [−0.56, −0.19] was significant (Z = −3.98). No standardized effect size is printed for this CVR finding.
