`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review attributes this to Begg and Green (1988): a word-pair cued-recall experiment with between-subject control conditions equating input serial position for single and repeated presentations. All ten comparisons revealed superadditivity.

> "In fact, because this study included another between-subjects variable not relevant to our present considerations, the design yielded ten possible comparisons, all of which revealed superadditivity."
