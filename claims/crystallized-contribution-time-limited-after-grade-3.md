## Evidence

### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' summary of the two path models: in QNTS the direct paths from crystallized abilities to grades 4 and 6 were non-significant (grade 4 β = .07, p = .24; grade 6 β = .05, p = .47), while in QLSCD modest paths remained (grade 4 β = .15, p < .03; grade 6 β = .15, p < .02).

> "Indeed, the present study revealed that crystallized abilities did not add (QNTS) or added modestly (QLSCD) to the prediction of school achievement beyond grade 3."
