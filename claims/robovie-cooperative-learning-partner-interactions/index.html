Evidence¶

Michael J Timms 2016¶

Michael J Timms. (2016). Letting Artificial Intelligence in Education Out of the Box: Educational Cobots and Smart Classrooms. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:701–712. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0095-y

q2 · i? · design · r?

The paper reports, citing Oshima et al. (2012), a study of Robovie-W supporting engagement in a cooperative learning group of pre-service teachers. The paper notes the robot was largely a Wizard-of-Oz system with pre-scripted explanations and no intelligent learning software.

"They found that the style of student interactions with Robovie-W was similar to that with the human participants in the learning group and that adaptive learners interacted best with the robot."
