Thematic analysis of definitions of engagement across the 18 defining studies in the scoping review. The review reports the ranked theme counts: "participation (n = 8, 44.4 %), the second most common theme was effort (n = 7, 38.9 %)" and interpersonal skills and behaviour third. Themes attention and motivation each appeared in five studies (27.8%).

> "Among the 18 studies that explicitly defined engagement, the most common theme identified was participation (n = 8, 44.4 %), the second most common theme was effort (n = 7, 38.9 %) and interpersonal skills and behaviour was the third largest theme (n = 6, 33.3 %)."
