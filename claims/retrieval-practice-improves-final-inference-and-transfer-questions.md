# Retrieval practice enhances performance on final inference and transfer questions relative to restudying, although a single-word fill-in-the-blank task did not

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Butler (2010, Experiment 1), that practicing retrieval enhanced performance on new final inference questions relative to restudying, and variable tests were no better than same tests. [→ Karpicke 2017](#karpicke-2017)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Hinze and Wiley (2011, Experiment 3), that paragraph recall outperformed fill-in-the-blank tests on a final multiple-choice transfer test 2 days later. [→ Karpicke 2017 (2)](#karpicke-2017-2)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Tran et al. (2015), that a fill-in-the-blank task recalling one word per sentence did not enhance final inference performance relative to restudy. [→ Karpicke 2017 (3)](#karpicke-2017-3)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Eglington and Kang (2016), positive effects on final inference performance with Tran et al.'s materials when sentences were studied simultaneously and free recall was practiced. [→ Karpicke 2017 (4)](#karpicke-2017-4)
