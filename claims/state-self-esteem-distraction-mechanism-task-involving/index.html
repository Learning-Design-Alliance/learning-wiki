Cheng-Hong Liu 2026¶

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Manipulation check t test in the preliminary analyses of the 146 completers showed person praise raised state self-esteem: "t (144) = 2.39, p =.018, d = 0.40" (M = 4.45 vs. M = 4.11).

"participants under the person praise condition scored higher on the state self-esteem items (M = 4.45, SD = 0.86) than did participants under the no praise condition ( M = 4.11, SD = 0.84), t (144) = 2.39, p =.018, d = 0.40."

Cheng-Hong Liu 2026 (2)¶

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

PROCESS Model 92 moderated serial mediation (PP→state self-esteem→trials→performance, FM moderating two paths) in the 146-participant experiment. The serial moderated mediation index was "Index = 0.88, 95% CI = 0.04 to 2.20"; the negative conditional indirect effect occurred only under task-involving feedback (−0.83, CI −2.12 to −0.06).

"Simple slope analysis revealed that for participants under the task-involving feedback condition, state self-esteem was negatively related to the number of trials, b = −0.68, t = −3.00, p =.003."
