## Evidence

### Lehmann Ronny 2024

Lehmann Ronny, Klinke Petrowsky Michaela, Seitz Anke, Meyburg Jochen, Eppich Walter, Hoffmann Georg F., Tönshoff Burkhard, Huwendiek Sören. (2024). A novel blended and interprofessional approach to pediatric emergency training: self-assessment, perception, and perceived long-term effects. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06381-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis of self-assessments from 55 medical and 48 nursing staff who completed T1–T3, using a three-factor ANOVA with group, score and measurement factors. The article reports a high partial Eta² of 0.755 for ‘measurement’ and that both groups showed statistically significant increases at T3 after training.

> "A high partial Eta² of 0.755 for ‘measurement’ (Suppl. 5) indicated distinct increasing values for knowledge and skills over the three measurement time points."
