## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The regression coefficient of EA on PGS was equal across SES groups, i.e., no genotype-by-SES interaction at the level of measured variants. [→ Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019](#eveline-l-de-zeeuw-2019)

## Evidence

### Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019

Eveline L. de Zeeuw, Kees-Jan Kan, Catharina E. M. van Beijsterveldt, Hamdi Mbarek, Jouke-Jan Hottenga, Gareth E. Davies, Michael C. Neale, Conor V. Dolan and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2019). The moderating role of SES on genetic differences in educational achievement in the Netherlands. npj Science of Learning 4:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0052-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-family twin-model regression of EA on PGS within each SES group; the PGS regression coefficient could be equated across groups (Δ−2LL = 2.3, p = 0.517), indicating no moderation of the PGS-EA association by SES.

> "the regression coef ﬁcient in the association of educational achievement on PGS did not differ between the SES groups ( Δ−2LL = 2.3, Δdf = 3, p = 0.517)"
