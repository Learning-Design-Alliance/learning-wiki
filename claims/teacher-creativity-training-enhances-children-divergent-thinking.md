### Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting 2024

Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that "providing teachers with access to research-led creativity-based programs and training enhanced children's divergent thinking compared to control groups" in Spanish and Italian studies.

> "a recent review of studies in Spain and Italy found that providing teachers with access to research-led creativity-based programs and training enhanced children's divergent thinking compared to control groups (Ruiz-del-Pino et al., 2022)."

### Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting 2024 (2)

Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that teacher training "has a marked impact on teachers' definitions and understanding of creativity", also shifting views from creativity being innate and possessed only by some children to being harnessable in all.

> "creative and professional development training has a marked impact on teachers' definitions and understanding of creativity, bringing it closer to researchers' conceptualisations (Mullet et al., 2016)."
