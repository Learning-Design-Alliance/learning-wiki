## Evidence

### Liu Jie 2025

Liu Jie, Hu Tingting, Xu Ke, Cheng Zihui, Bu Wenjie, Lin Aiqin. (2025). Does a virtual simulation teaching platform improve surgical training for medical undergraduate students? BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03029-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null finding on the hands-on laparoscopic simulator task scored on suture placement accuracy (40 points), knot-tying proficiency (40 points), and time efficiency (20 points). Experimental group scored "66.08 ± 3.97 points" versus 65.15 ± 3.82 (P = 0.179; t = 1.350). No effect size printed.

> "No significant differences were observed between students in the experimental group (66.08 ± 3.97 points) and those in the control group (65.15 ± 3.82 points) in terms of their scores on the simulator-based laparoscopic suturing and knot-tying test (P = 0.179)."
