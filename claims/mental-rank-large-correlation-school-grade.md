### Woolley 1915

Woolley, Helen Thompson. (1915). A new scale of mental and physical measurements for adolescents, and some of its uses. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(9), 521-550. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075644

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Graphical and tabular analysis (Graphs 1 to 8 and tables of averages of percentile ranks by type of school, sex, and school grade) of fourteen- and fifteen-year-old working children, relating composite mental and physical percentile averages to the school grade each child had completed.

> "The graphs for the averages in physical tests show a small positive correlation with school grade, and those for the mental tests a large positive correlation, so large that the graphs for fifth and eighth grade children cross for only a small portion of their extent."
