Evidence¶

Raffael Schmitt 2023¶

Raffael Schmitt, Martin Meyer and Nathalie Giroud. (2023). Improvements in naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in middle-aged and older adults after 3 weeks of computer-based speechreading training. npj Science of Learning 8:32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00179-6

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Multiple regression on pre-training visual enhancement (normalized difference between AV and A-only scores) with PTA, age, visual working memory, and processing speed as z-standardized predictors. Hearing aid use and training intensity were not entered in this model. "None of the predictors showed a signi ﬁcant association with VE"; the regression coefficients and 95% CIs are printed (below), and the wide intervals mean a non-significant result here is not evidence of no association.

"Using multiple regression analysis, none of the predictors showed a signi ﬁcant association with VE (PTA, β = 1.81, 95% CI[ −4.13, 7.76], p = 0.544; age, β = −5.07, 95% CI[−11.42, 1.28], p = 0.115; visual working memory, β = 0.82, 95% CI[ −4.66, 6.30], p = 0.767; processing speed, β = 0.09, 95% CI[ −5.56, 5.74], p = 0.975)"

Raffael Schmitt 2023 (2)¶

Raffael Schmitt, Martin Meyer and Nathalie Giroud. (2023). Improvements in naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in middle-aged and older adults after 3 weeks of computer-based speechreading training. npj Science of Learning 8:32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00179-6

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Multiple regression on mean learning rate in the speechreading training group, aggregating trial-level data. The article reports that "None of the variables showed a signi ﬁcant association with learning rate" and notes information is lost by averaging trial-level data into a mean learning rate.

"None of the variables showed a signi ﬁcant association with learning rate (PTA, β = −0.91, 95% CI[ −7.99, 6.18], p = 0.794; age, β = 0.29, 95% CI[−6.99, 7.56], p = 0.936; hearing aid use, β = 2.49, 95% CI[ −5.98, 10.96], p = 0.550; training intensity, β = 2.18, 95% CI[ −4.46, 8.82], p = 0.505; visual working memory, β = −1.60, 95% CI[ −6.77, 3.58], p = 0.531; processing speed, β = −1.42, 95% CI[ −7.83, 4.99], p = 0.653)"
