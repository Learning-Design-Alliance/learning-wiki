## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants made significantly smaller angular errors when pointing to invisible landmarks with VGL support than without it, with SBSOD as covariate. [→ Jia Liu 2022](#jia-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Jia Liu 2022

Jia Liu, Avinash Kumar Singh, Anna Wunderlich, Klaus Gramann and Chin-Teng Lin. (2022). Redesigning navigational aids using virtual global landmarks to improve spatial knowledge retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00132-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects repeated-measures ANCOVA of the pointing task in a VR navigation experiment with 27 participants, comparing VGL and non-VGL trials. The article reports "a statistically signiﬁcant difference in angular errors" with F1,25 = 13.384, p = 0.001, partial η² = 0.349, and a 31.8% reduction in errors with VGLs.

> "a statistically signiﬁcant difference in angular errors for the different trial conditions ( F1,25 = 13.384, p = 0.001, partial η2 = 0.349) with less angular errors in augmented landmark trials"
