### Rosemary Luckin 2008

Rosemary Luckin. (2008). The Learner Centric Ecology of Resources: A Framework for using Technology to Scaffold Learning. Computers & Education 50, 449-462. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.09.018

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Design analysis of the Kambu scenario (author's theoretical application of the model, no empirical test). The mapping revealed "a potential tension... between what the community believes to be good farming practice and what current agricultural expertise believes to be good practice", and strong links between knowledge, resources and environment shaped design choices such as bicycle transport.

> "if we consider the Curriculum filter that organizes the knowledge to be learnt it is clear that there is a potential tension at this point in our Ecology between what the community believes to be good farming practice and what current agricultural expertise believes to be good practice."
