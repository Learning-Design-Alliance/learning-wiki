Evidence¶

Wang 2020¶

Wang, S., & Li, S. (2020). Calibration of Spanish MAP Growth math tests. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/calibration-of-spanish-map-growth-math-tests/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Item selection analysis of the collected versus selected samples (Tables 2.2 and 2.3), filtering items whose old and new RITs differ by more than 20 RITs. The article reports 47 outlier items overall, 28 status 10 outliers (2.4%) and 8 status XX outliers (0.2%); all status 12 and 13 items were outliers. No effect size is printed.

"These rates of outliers by calibration status are extremely low, which proves that the quality of the calibration of Spanish math items using Spanish test responses is extremely high."
