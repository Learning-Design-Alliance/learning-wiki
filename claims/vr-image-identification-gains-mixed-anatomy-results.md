### Hu Kai-Chun 2020

Hu Kai-Chun, Salcedo Daniel, Kang Yi-No, Lin Che-Wei, Hsu Chin-Wang, Cheng Chung-Yi, Suk Fat-Moon, Huang Wen-Cheng. (2020). Impact of virtual reality anatomy training on ultrasound competency development: A randomized controlled trial. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0242731. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the RCT using 10-question MCQ ultrasound image identification tests pre and post. The intervention group showed greater change (median = 3 vs 2); the article states "These two significant findings appear to be underpowered."

> "Students in the intervention group (median = 3, IQR = 3) demonstrated greater improvement in test scores of ultrasonographic images than the control group (median = 2, IQR = 3; Mann-Whitney U = 960; P < 0.05) (Tables 2 and 3)."
