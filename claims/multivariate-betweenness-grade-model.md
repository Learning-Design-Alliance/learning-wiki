## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe 2015

Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe. (2015). The impact of social integration on student persistence in introductory Modeling Instruction courses. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likelihood ratio test comparing multivariate logistic regression models (centrality plus gender, ethnicity, academic plan and final grade) against grade-only models for persistence; Table II reports, e.g., betweenness in MI with χ2(1) = 15.28 (p < 10−4) and closeness with χ2(1) = 11.17 (p < 10−3), but the article reports "signiﬁcantly better ﬁt only for the full betweenness model."

> "However, when we compared the ﬁt of the multivariate models to the ﬁt of the models reduced to a grade as a sole predict- ing variable, we found signiﬁcantly better ﬁt only for the full betweenness model (χ2(1) = 7 .89, p = 0.005)."
