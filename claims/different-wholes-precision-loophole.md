## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Frank Quinn 2013

Frank Quinn. (2013). Fractions in Elementary Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.2235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.2235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical analysis of the Common Core grade 3 preamble's paint-bucket example. The author argues the bucket comparison is "a difficulty with physical applications, not fractions" and that the different-wholes restriction dodges the needed precision. He illustrates with the Al-and-Jose pizza problem, where the data concern vertex angle but the implicit question concerns area.

> "But 1/2 is greater than 1/3 no matter how big the buckets are. This is a diﬃculty with physical applications, not fractions, and “diﬀerent wholes” is used to avoid having to be clear about this distinction."
