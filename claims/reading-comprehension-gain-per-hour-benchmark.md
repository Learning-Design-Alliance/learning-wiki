## Evidence

### Wolff 2011

Ulrika Wolff. (2011). Effects of a Randomised Reading Intervention Study: An Application of Structural Equation Modelling. Dyslexia 17: 295–311. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.438

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Author-reported comparison with standard gains from a review of intervention studies (Torgesen, 2005), placing the present study among the more efficient for reading comprehension, comparable to Rashotte et al. (.32) and Hatcher et al. (.39) per hour. The author notes the test–retest effect is not known.

> "The mean gain on a reading comprehension test (Lundberg, 2001) was 0.38 standard scores per hour of intervention. However, this test was given both before and after intervention, and the test–retest effect is not known, but the multiple-choice format of this test does not make it likely that children perceive or recall the correct responses."
