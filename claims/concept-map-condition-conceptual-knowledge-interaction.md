# Concept map study yields higher immediate conceptual knowledge than completion, but the difference disappears after the self-study phase

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` There was a significant learning condition × test moment interaction on conceptual knowledge: concept map study scored higher on the immediate post-test but conditions did not differ on the delayed post-test. [→ Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017](#montpetit-tourangeau-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-tests after a significant learning condition × test moment interaction (ηp² = .090) on conceptual knowledge in the 2×2×2 mixed ANOVA; on the delayed post-test the conditions did not differ significantly (87.4% vs 86.0%, p = .575).

> "conceptual knowledge (60.7 ± 17.2%) was higher in the concept map study condition than in the concept map completion condition (50.6 ± 18.4%, t (59) = 2.194, p = .032, two-tailed, Cohen's d = .567, medium effect size)"
