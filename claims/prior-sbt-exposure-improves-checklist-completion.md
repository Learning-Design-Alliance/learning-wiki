## Evidence

### Liu Zhenhua 2022

Liu Zhenhua, Chen Qiong, Wu Jing, Li Xinhua, He Yuchen, Yu Qiao. (2022). Simulation-based training in asthma exacerbation for medical students: effect of prior exposure to simulation training on performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03300-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational study of 203 third-year students grouped by student number into pre-exposure and unexposed groups; performance scored by blinded assessors during simulation. The article reports "Checklist completion was improved in the pre-exposure group compared with the unexposed group" with the printed means and p value.

> "Checklist completion was improved in the pre-exposure group compared with the unexposed group (19.32 ± 1.50 vs. 17.6 ± 1.61, p < 0.001)."
