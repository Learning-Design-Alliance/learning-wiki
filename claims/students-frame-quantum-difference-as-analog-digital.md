## Evidence

### Josephine C. Meyer 2022

Josephine C. Meyer, Gina Passante, Steven J. Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). Investigating student interpretations of the differences between classical and quantum computers: Are quantum computers just analog classical computers?. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.meyer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.meyer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview analysis across the three courses found students citing the continuous, complex nature of the state vector; the authors note that even students mentioning entanglement treated "the continuous nature of the quantum state vector ... on equal footing" with exponential effects.

> "a frequent initial response was that storing the state of the quantum computer requires additional memory (or is entirely impossible) because the quantum state can only be deﬁned in terms of continuous coefﬁcients vs. the discrete 0s and 1s of digital classical computers"
