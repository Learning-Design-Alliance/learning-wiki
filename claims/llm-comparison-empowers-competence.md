### Nicola Brocca 2026

Nicola Brocca, Davide Garassino. (2026). LLMs in text linguistics teaching: an exploratory study with genAI novices in higher education. Computers and Education Open 11 (2026) 100414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100414

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation of the reflection data (Dataset 2): the activity's design, which followed prior manual analysis, led students to "perceived themselves to be more capable than the LLM". Some reflections also indicated reduced concern about LLMs replacing human expertise.

> "By comparing their own analyses with the LLM-generated analyses, the activity empowered their sense of competence, as they perceived themselves to be more capable than the LLM."
