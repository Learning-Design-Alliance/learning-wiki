## Evidence

### Matsumoto Shiro 2024

Matsumoto Shiro, Kawahira Hiroshi, Fukata Kyohei, Doi Yasunori, Kobayashi Nao, Hosoya Yoshinori, Sata Naohiro. (2024). Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy skill evaluation from video: a new artificial intelligence-based instrument identification system. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-63388-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of 18 videos from three experts (more than 100 laparoscopic gastrectomies each) and three novices (fewer than 20 each), three videos per surgeon, analyzed with the Mann–Whitney U-test. No effect size is printed for the time difference.

> "The average number of frames per surgery was 50,519 frames (28.1 min) for the Expert group vs. 88,815 frames (49.3 min) for the Novice group, with the Expert group taking significantly less time (Fig. 3a, P < 0.001, Mann–Whitney U-test)."
