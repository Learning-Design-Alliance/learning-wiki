## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No association between right IPS sulcal pattern and arithmetic ability was found, and the weak left IPS association was not supported by Bayesian evidence, in a pre-registered ANCOVA study of grade 4 children. [→ Roell M. 2022](#roell-m-2022)

## Evidence

### Roell M. 2022

Roell M., Bellon E., Polspoel B., Declercq M. & De Smedt B. (2022). Investigating the association between variability in sulcal pattern and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 12, 12323. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15335-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA analysis in 90 grade 4 children (after exclusions) controlling for sex, dataset and IQ. The article reports a weak left IPS association ("F(1,77) = 4.29, p = .04", BF10 = 2.64) and no right IPS association (p = 0.20, BF01 = 1.55). No effect size was printed for these findings.

> "An association between left IPS and arithmetic, F(1,77) = 4.29, p = .04, was found but the evidence for this association was weak BF10 = 2.64, see Fig. 5. Contrastingly, no association between right IPS and arithmetic was found, F(1,77) = 1.65, p = 0.20, and the evidence for the absence of an association was weak BF01 = 1.55"
