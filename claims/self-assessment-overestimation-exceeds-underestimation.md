Item-level comparison of self-survey versus scenario-based scores for the 14 shared questions answered by 6 Cameroonian doctors, with agreement defined as a difference within one point. The article reports "58.3% agreement rate, 17.9% under-estimation rate, and 23.8% over-estimation rate".
