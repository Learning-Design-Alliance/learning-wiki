## Evidence

### Marshman 2024

Marshman, E., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2024). Using multiple representations to improve student understanding of quantum states. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Written-response and interview data from 85 undergraduates on Q4-Q7. Only 13% correctly recalled that ⟨x|p′⟩ = e^{ip′x/ℏ} and ⟨p|x′⟩ = e^{−ipx′/ℏ}. Interviews confirmed students claimed eigenstates of position and momentum are orthogonal or incompatible.

> "In these cases, since momentum and position have continuous eigenvalues, one should write the inner products as Dirac delta functions, whereas t he Kronecker delta is for inner products of eigenstates that have discrete eigenvalues."
