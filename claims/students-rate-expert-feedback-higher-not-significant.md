## Evidence

### Alpay Suha 2025

Alpay Suha, Darafarin Yasemen, Dagdelen Burcu, Al-Shammari Sana Mahroos Mkhailef, Buyukbayram Isil Kaya. (2025). Comparison of ChatGPT-4o and expert evaluation in endodontic education: a cross-sectional pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08358-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire study in which the 32 participating students rated both feedback sources on 5-point Likert scales; paired-sample t-tests compared means. Expert feedback was rated higher in all categories, with "no statistically significant differences were observed (p > 0.05)" (p-values 0.072, 0.475, 0.614).

> "Expert feedback consistently received higher mean scores than AI feedback in all categories. Educational contribution scored 4.29 (expert) versus 3.90 (AI), clinical thinking support 4.19 versus 4.06, and perceived reliability 4.00 versus 3.91"
