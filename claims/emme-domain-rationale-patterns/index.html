Review synthesis based on all 72 studies (Table 3 contingency table of domain by rationale); the review reports that medical studies also mention attention guidance and that "studies that deal with tasks from STEM domains make use of a variety of rationales" and refer more often to social mechanisms.

"Studies that focus on sports/physical education mostly mention that EMME can reveal and teach a quiet eye strategy."

Related Claims¶

  • In Quiet Eye Training literature, the EMME video is typically one component of a larger training package rather than the main manipulation — related
  • EMME study designs vary widely: most experiments added information, used dynamic scan-path visualizations, and over half did not report how eye movement data were processed — related
  • Most EMME experiments that measured immediate learning scores reported at least one positive effect of gaze visualization on immediate learning — related
  • Different AR approaches might be used for learning and providing feedback in sports training — related
