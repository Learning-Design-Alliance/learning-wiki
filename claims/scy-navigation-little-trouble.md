### Ton de Jong 2012

Ton de Jong, Armin Weinberger, Isabelle Girault, Anders Kluge, Ard W. Lazonder, Margus Pedaste, Sten Ludvigsen, Muriel Ney, Barbara Wasson, Astrid Wichmann, Caspar Geraedts, Adam Giemza, Tasos Hovardas, Rachel Julien, Wouter R. van Joolingen, Anne Lejeune, Constantinos C. Manoli, Yuri Matteman, Tago Sarapuu, Alex Verkade, Vibeke Vold, Zacharias C. Zacharia. (2012). Using scenarios to design complex technology-enhanced learning environments. Education Tech Research Dev, 60, 883–901. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-012-9258-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of the four SCY missions used analyses of video recordings, screen captures, logfiles, and student ELOs across five countries. The article reports that overall, students "had (surprisingly) little trouble navigating through a SCY mission"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The four SCY missions have been tested with over 600 students from five different countries."

### Ton de Jong 2012 (2)

Ton de Jong, Armin Weinberger, Isabelle Girault, Anders Kluge, Ard W. Lazonder, Margus Pedaste, Sten Ludvigsen, Muriel Ney, Barbara Wasson, Astrid Wichmann, Caspar Geraedts, Adam Giemza, Tasos Hovardas, Rachel Julien, Wouter R. van Joolingen, Anne Lejeune, Constantinos C. Manoli, Yuri Matteman, Tago Sarapuu, Alex Verkade, Vibeke Vold, Zacharias C. Zacharia. (2012). Using scenarios to design complex technology-enhanced learning environments. Education Tech Research Dev, 60, 883–901. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-012-9258-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The article describes an iterative design approach in which evaluation data shaped the interface; navigation features became more structured and the mission map was added. Support included a mini-mission, help videos, and bubble help; no effect sizes are printed.

> "in the course of the design process the navigation features became more structured and explicit for students; in particular, the mission map was added to present students with an overview of the mission"
