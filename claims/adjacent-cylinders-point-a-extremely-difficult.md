## Evidence

### Jing Li 2023

Jing Li, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students apply superposition principle in problems involving spherical, cylindrical and planar charge distributions. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.02003](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.02003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test question (3) asked students to draw net field directions for two adjacent infinite charged cylinders. Tutorial sections reached "at least 90%" at points B and C versus 37%-53% in the comparison group; at point A "only 60% of students found the direction at point A correctly" even in the tutorial group. Percentages only; no effect size.

> "In the tutorial sections, at least 90% of the students found the directions of the electric field at points B and C correctly but in the comparison group, only 37%-53% of students found the directions of the field at points C and B correctly"
