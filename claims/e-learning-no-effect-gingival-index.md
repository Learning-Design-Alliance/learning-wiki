### Kaneyasu Y 2023

Kaneyasu Y, Shigeishi H, Sugiyama M, Ohta K. (2023). Effectiveness of e-learning to promote oral health education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000036550

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 7 randomized trials comparing e-learning with conventional education on gingival index. The pooled SMD was −0.04 (95% CI −0.88 to 0.80), with high heterogeneity (I2 = 97%, P < .001) and no significant difference (P = .92).

> "The pooled SMD of the GI in 7 studies[21,33,36,39,44,46,47] was −0.04 (95% CI −0.88 to 0.80) , thus not indicating a better result for the groups that received e-learning"
