Phase 2 construct-validity analysis: 7 of 11 novices completed the prescribed training and the recorded 10th run of the Manipulation Module; improvement from baseline was significant (P=0.0004 per the abstract) and matched expert baseline performance.

> "The mean number of targets successfully touched was 4 (Figure 4). The mean time spent on the Manipulation module was 23 seconds (Figure 5). The differences from baseline were significant and were similar to those of the "experts" at baseline."
