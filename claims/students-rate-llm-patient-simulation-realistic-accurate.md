Post-training survey of the 21 trial participants assessing perceived realism, authenticity, and accuracy of the simulation; ratings were "highly accurate (M = 8.67/10 ± 2.01)" on a 10-point scale, with feasibility and realism means as printed. Only descriptive means were reported.

> "Furthermore, the participants in both groups rated the simulations as being highly accurate (M = 8.67/10 ± 2.01), feasible (M = 8.38/10 ± 1.88), and realistic (M = 7.81/10 ± 1.40)."
