Trevor I. Smith, Warren M. Christensen, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Identifying Student Diﬃculties with Entropy, Heat Engines, and the Carnot Cycle. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Videotaped tutorial episode at School 1 in which two students reasoning about an engine with W = 0 used differential labels for cyclic quantities, correctly invoked the first law, but incorrectly concluded there would be no heat transfer and an undefined efficiency. No effect size is printed.

> "During this discussion, Jonah and Bill both refer to cyclic quantities by the diﬀerential labels, namely d U instead of ∆ U and d−Q instead of Qnet."
