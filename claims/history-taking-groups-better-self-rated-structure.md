## Evidence

### Graf Joachim 2020

Graf Joachim, Loda Teresa, Zipfel Stephan, Wosnik Annette, Mohr Daniela, Herrmann-Werner Anne. (2020). Communication skills of medical students: survey of self- and external perception in a longitudinally based trend study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02049-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unpaired t-test comparing self-perception of students with anamnesis group participation (n = 258) against those without (n = 726) found a statistically significant difference only in structure; no effect size is printed.

> "Here, the students with previous history-taking group participation rated their communication skills much better than the students in the control group (mean 4.51 vs. 4.33, p = 0.02)."
