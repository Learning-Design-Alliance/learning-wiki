## Evidence

### Kahalon 2023

Kahalon, R., Shnabel, N., Sharvit, K., Halabi, S., & Wright, S. C. (2023). High-Quality Contact With Fellow Majority Group Students Is Associated With Better Academic Performance of Minority Group Students. Personality & Social Psychology Bulletin, 49(12), 1723–1736. https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672221115943

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary t-test in Study 1's archival analysis comparing Sechem scores (pre-university achievements) of Arab participants (n = 125 total) who took versus did not take the course. The article reports "no difference in "Sechem scores"" with d = −.08, p = .649.

> "A t-test revealed no difference in "Sechem scores" between Arab students who participated (M = 682.35, SD = 27.89) and did not participate in the course (M = 680.09, SD = 26.47), t(123) = .46, p = .649, d = −.08, 95% (CI)[−.44, .27]."
