## Evidence

### Zhang Huiqun 2025

Zhang Huiqun, Li Shanshan, Zheng Guoquan, Chen Xiaoyun, Zhong Lujie, Li Jiaying, Li Yun. (2025). Network analysis of an OSCE-based graduation skills assessment for clinical medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07091-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across three majors (clinical medicine, anesthesiology, medical imaging) in the same 3,710-student cohort. Pairwise tests showed clinical medicine scored higher than anesthesiology on physical examination (p = 0.01), auxiliary examination (p = 0.02), and case analysis (p = 0.04).

> "specialties differed significantly in physical examination (F(2,3255) = 5.75, p = 0.003), basic operational skill (F(2,3707) = 7.43, p < 0.001), auxiliary examination analysis (F(2,3040) = 3.77, p = 0.02) and case analysis(F(2,3040) = 3.32, p = 0.04)."
