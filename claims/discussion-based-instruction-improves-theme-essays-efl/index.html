Evidence¶

Shen 2013¶

Shen, F.-Y. (2013). Using Group Discussion with Taiwan’s EFL College Students: A Comparison of Comprehension Instruction for Book Club, Literature Circles, and Instructional Conversations. English Language Teaching, 6(12), 58-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p58

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Chi-square test on theme explanation essay score distributions across four conditions. The article reports the experimental groups had "significantly greater" numbers of students receiving a full score of 4 (χ2 = 21.80; p = .010).

"It indicates that the number of the experimental-group students receiving a full score of 4 was significantly greater than that of the control group (χ2 = 21.80; p = .010)."

Shen 2013 (2)¶

Shen, F.-Y. (2013). Using Group Discussion with Taiwan’s EFL College Students: A Comparison of Comprehension Instruction for Book Club, Literature Circles, and Instructional Conversations. English Language Teaching, 6(12), 58-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p58

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Chi-square test on theme exemplification essay score distributions across four conditions. The article reports "significantly greater numbers of students receiving a full score of 4" in experimental groups (χ2 = 19.80; p = .019).

"post-hoc analyses indicate that experimental approaches produced significantly greater numbers of students receiving a full score of 4 than did the control approach (χ2 = 19.80; p = .019)."
