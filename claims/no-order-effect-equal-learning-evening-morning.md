## Evidence

### Backhaus et al. 2008

Jutta Backhaus, Ralf Hoeckesfeld, Jan Born, Fritz Hohagen, Klaus Junghanns. (2008). Immediate as well as delayed post learning sleep but not wakefulness enhances declarative memory consolidation in children. Neurobiology of Learning and Memory 89, 76-80. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlm.2007.08.010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the 27-child counterbalanced design: words recalled at criterion did not differ between first (27.4 ± 0.5) and second (27.9 ± 0.5) test sessions (t = 0.68, p = 0.49). The authors also report no difference in trials to criterion between evening and morning learning.

> "There were no order eﬀects in the number of learned word pairs at the last criterion trial between the counterbalanced test conditions (ﬁrst test: 27.4 ± 0.5, second test: 27.9 ± 0.5, t = 0.68, p = 0.49)"
