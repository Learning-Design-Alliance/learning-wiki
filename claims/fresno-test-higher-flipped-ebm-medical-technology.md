### Huang Hsien-Li 2020

Huang Hsien-Li, Chou Chia-Pei, Leu Steve, You Huey-Ling, Tiao Mao-Meng, Chen Chih-Hung. (2020). Effects of a quasi-experimental study of using flipped classroom approach to teach evidence-based medicine to medical technology students. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 62 medical technology interns (24 traditional, 38 flipped), both courses six sessions with identical teachers, assessors and study cases. Means were 64.28 ± 12.27 versus 31.46 ± 11.34 with "p < 0.001"; no effect size printed.

> "FC-EBM trained students had a higher Fresno test score than TC-EBM trained students (64.28 ± 12.27 vs 31.46 ± 11.34, p < 0.001) (Fig. 3)."
