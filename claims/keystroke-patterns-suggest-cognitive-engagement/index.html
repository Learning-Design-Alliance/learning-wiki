Ahmad D. Suleiman 2026¶

Ahmad D. Suleiman, Daqing Hou, and Maliha Noushin Raida. (2026). LLM-Generated Design Problems for Assessing Higher-Order Thinking in Project-Based Learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11032. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11032 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.11032

q2 · i? · design · r2

Keystroke-log analysis across the two web-based deployments (Breached! and Touchalytics) found mean planning latencies of 167.2s and 114.6s, a "revision ratio" of 12-16 deletions per 100 characters, and about 15 pauses per session over 10 seconds.

"Therevision ratioshowed that students deleted 12-16 characters for every 100 characters typed, indicating that they interleaved writing and revision."
