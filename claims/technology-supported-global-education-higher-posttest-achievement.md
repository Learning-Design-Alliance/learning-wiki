### Egüz 2019

Egüz, Ş., Öztürk, C. & Kesten, A. (2019). Technology-supported global education: A mixed method study. Pegem Eğitim ve Öğretim Dergisi, 9(4), 1209-1244. http://dx.doi.org/10.14527/pegegog.2019.040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`
