Evidence¶

Song 2023¶

Song, Yu. (2023). Apprenticeship As a Developmental Mechanism in Argumentation Skill Development. Ph.D. dissertation, Columbia University. https://academiccommons.columbia.edu/doi/Song_columbia_0054D_18218

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The dissertation's intervention included a comparison group that engaged in the same intervention and assessments with similar-ability partners. The abstract reports that "Both groups also advanced in individual dialogic argument skills" after engagement in argumentation. No effect sizes are printed.

"Both groups also advanced in individual dialogic argument skills following their engagement in argumentation, a result thereby demonstrating the passage of higher-order intellectual skills from a social to individual level."
