Evidence¶

Tanriseven 2014¶

Tanriseven, I. (2014). A Tool That Can Be Effective in the Self-regulated Learning of Pre-service Teachers: The Mind Map. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 39(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2014v39n1.1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 60 second-year pre-service teachers (n=30 per group) in a Teaching Principles and Methods course; independent sample t-tests on MSLQ post-test means showed "a meaningful difference in favour of the experimental group" on these strategy and belief scales.

"among elaboration, organisation, metacognitive self-regulation, time and study environment, effort management strategy, intrinsic goal orientation and self-efficacy perception, there is a meaningful difference in favour of the experimental group"

Tanriseven 2014 (2)¶

Tanriseven, I. (2014). A Tool That Can Be Effective in the Self-regulated Learning of Pre-service Teachers: The Mind Map. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 39(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2014v39n1.1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same quasi-experiment, independent sample t-tests on MSLQ post-test means (n=30 per group): the article reports no meaningful between-group difference for test anxiety and rehearsal; equivalence was not tested.

"It is also determined that there is no meaningful difference between the experimental and control groups’ test anxiety and rehearsal strategies average post-test means."
