## Evidence

### Kestel 2026

Kestel, Sıdıka, and Korkmaz, Fatoş. (2026). Nursing Students' Perceptions of Instructional Approaches in Nursing Diagnosis Education: A Qualitative Study. Nursing Open, 13(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70571

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same qualitative interview study, students reported variability in feedback quality. One participant stated that "some instructors provide immediate feedback, while others delay or omit it altogether," and another cited the student-to-instructor ratio limiting one-on-one discussion.

> "There are variations in feedback quality—some instructors provide immediate feedback, while others delay or omit it altogether."
