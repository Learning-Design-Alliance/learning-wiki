q2 · i? · review · r1

Policy scan of state actions: North Carolina posts program-level data on candidates entering and completing in-state teacher preparation programs, invests in the Teacher Assistants Reimbursement Pilot Program (TAs to Teachers), created the Future Teachers of North Carolina, and "provides a three-year induction and mentoring program for new teachers," earning green ratings on those criteria.

"North Carolina provides a three-year induction and mentoring program for new teachers."

EdTrust 2022 (2)¶

EdTrust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: NORTH CAROLINA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622852

q2 · i? · review · r2

Policy scan finds the state "does not invest in scholarships and/or loan-forgiveness programs to attract students of color," has no program approval standards that compel programs to recruit and graduate candidates of color, does not intentionally invest in programs preparing high numbers of candidates of color, and shows no evidence on residencies, leadership development, or cultural competence training for hiring managers and leaders.

"North Carolina does not invest in scholarships and/or loan-forgiveness programs to attract students of color into teacher preparation programs."
