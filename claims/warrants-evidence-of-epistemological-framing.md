### Bing 2007

Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2007). Analyzing Problem Solving Using Math in Physics: Epistemological Framing via Warrants. https://www.compadre.org/PER/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Methodological claim grounded in the authors' videotaped observations of group problem solving: because framing is usually unconscious, disagreements make warrants explicit, and analyzing them offers a window onto framing. No effect size is printed.

> "When students disagree on a procedure or result, they are often explicit about why something should be believed or not. These comments, referred to as warrants in argumentation theory, show us what epistemic assumptions they are making at the moment."
