## Evidence

### Kaur 2017

Kaur, T., Blair, D., Moschilla, J., Stannard, W. & Zadnik, M. (2017). Teaching Einsteinian Physics at Schools: Part 3, Review of Research Outcomes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post conceptual questionnaire study of Year 10 academically talented students following a four-lesson program in 2015 focused on Einsteinian concepts underlying the synthesis of gold in the universe. The article reports improvement was significant but prints no effect size or test statistic.

> "Figure 4 shows that students’ pre-test scores were in the range 15-50%. Again, the students in the class improved significantly after the program. As shown in Figure 5, the improvement by girls again exceeded that of the boys."
