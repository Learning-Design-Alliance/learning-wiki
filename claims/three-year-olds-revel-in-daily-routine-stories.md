The reviewer reports, from Mitchell and colleagues' close observation of children at the City and Country School, that "3-year-olds simply revelled in the mere recital in story form" of daily events. No systematic data are printed; this is the reviewer's descriptive report.

> "You would find, for example, that 3-year-olds simply revelled in the mere recital in story form of the, to them, exciting things of the day, but to us the humdrum routine of life."
