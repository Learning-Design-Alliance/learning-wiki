## Evidence

### Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı 2017

Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı. (2017). Effect of pop music on students’ attitudes to music lessons. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3321

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control group of 91 students taught songs traditionally from the educational music repertoire; significant t values included item 5 t(180) = 2.87, p<.01 and item 15 t(180) = 4.49, p<0.01. The article states "There was no significant difference observed between the pretest and posttest attitude points of the control group for other items."

> "After application, in the control group taught with traditional methods using examples from the educational music repertoire items 5, 6, 9, 11 and 15 had positive significant differences between pretest and posttest attitude points"
