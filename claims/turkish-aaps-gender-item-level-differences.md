## Evidence

### Nuri Balta 2016

Nuri Balta, Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying Turkish high school and university students' attitudes and approaches to physics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of item-level response frequencies among 528 Turkish students. Table III lists significant items including item 1 (χ2=21.3, p=.000, male more expert-like) and item 8 (χ2=22.0, p=.000, female more expert-like); only items 8 and 16 exceeded a 20% favorable-response gap, both favoring females.

> "although there is no statistically significant difference between the overall averages of male and female students, Table III shows that differences between the frequency distributions of male and female responses for 33% of the items were statistically significant."
