## Evidence

### Dupéré 2014

Dupéré, V., Leventhal, T., Dion, E., Crosnoe, R., Archambault, I., & Janosz, M. (2014). Stressors and Turning Points in High School and Dropout: A Stress Process, Life Course Framework. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Stressors+and+Turning+Points+in+High+School+and+Dropout

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review's analysis of five major dropout theories finds precipitating events acknowledged but not elaborated: Finn "does not explicitly integrate such precipitating factors in his model," and Rumberger's model "makes no reference to developmental period or chronological age."

> "these theorists usually discuss some of the contemporary circumstances that could precipitate dropout.  However, these circumstances are often mentioned more as a side note than as a central component of dropout models."
