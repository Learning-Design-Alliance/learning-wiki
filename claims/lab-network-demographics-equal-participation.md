## Evidence

### Kontro 2020

Kontro, I.; Génois, M. (2020). Combining surveys and sensors to explore student behaviour. [doi:10.3390/educsci10030068](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10030068)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of sensor-derived participation in seven laboratory sections (final data set of 151 individuals, 144 students). Table 2 reports, e.g., All: 5.02 ± 0.12 present, 75 ± 2% strongly connected; Men 75 ± 3%, Women 75 ± 3%, Phys 76 ± 3%, Other 73 ± 4%. The authors state "The numbers are strikingly similar for all student groups"; no effect size is printed for these comparisons.

> "The numbers are strikingly similar for all student groups: men and women, physics majors and other majors, and ﬁrst year students and non-ﬁrst year students."
