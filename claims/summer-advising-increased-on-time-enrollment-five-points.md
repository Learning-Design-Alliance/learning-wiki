`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The 2011 Massachusetts randomized controlled trial assigned 927 Boston graduates (87% students of color, 74% free/reduced lunch) to treatment or control. The article reports the advising treatment "increased on-time enrollment rates by five percentage points in comparison with the control group"; no effect size is printed.
