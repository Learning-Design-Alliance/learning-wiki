## Evidence

### Bleiweiss-Sande R. 2019

Bleiweiss-Sande R., Chui K., Wright C., Amin S., Anzman-Frasca S., Sacheck J.M. (2019). Associations between Food Group Intake, Cognition, and Academic Achievement in Elementary Schoolchildren. Nutrients 11(11):2722. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11112722

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Poisson regression (Digit Span) and linear regression (Stroop ratio) on the FLEX baseline sample of 868 children aged 8–10. Food groups were "non-significant predictors of digit span score" in adjusted models; individual food groups were likewise non-significant for both cognitive outcomes.

> "Combined categories of healthful foods (fruits, vegetables, and unsweetened beverages) and less healthful foods (salty and sweet snacks and SSBs) were non-significant predictors of digit span score in unadjusted (not shown) and age-adjusted models (Table 3)."
