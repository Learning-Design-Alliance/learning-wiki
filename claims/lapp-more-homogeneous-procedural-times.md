`q2 i?` Completion times were more homogeneous among Lapp-trained participants, while between-group differences in procedural times were not significant. [→ Ulloa G 2022](#ulloa-g-2022)

## Evidence

### Ulloa G 2022

Ulloa G, Neyem A, Escalona G, Ortiz C, Varas J. (2022). Remote asynchronous feedback for unsupervised laparoscopic training: the "Lapp" platform. ABCD Arq Bras Cir Dig. https://doi.org/10.1590/0102-672020220002e1712

`q2 · i?`

The authors' interpretation of the completion-time data from the two-group comparison: "there is more homogeneity in participants who trained using Lapp", with no significant between-group difference in procedural times. No variance statistic or effect size is printed for this observation.

> "Regarding time to complete the procedure, there is more homogeneity in participants who trained using Lapp, indicating that even though no significant differences were found in procedural times among both the groups, the participants who trained with Lapp achieved a more similar level of competency."
