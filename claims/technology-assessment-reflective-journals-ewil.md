## Evidence

### Schuster 2017

Schuster, Lisa & Glavas, Charmaine (2017). Exploring the dimensions of electronic work integrated learning (eWIL). Educational Research Review, 21, pp. 55-66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.04.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of the reviewed studies' assessment uses of technology: online reflective journals and e-portfolios were the common forms; the review notes this is expected given research showing technology can successfully support reflection (Kori et al., 2014).

> "Several other studies utilized technologies to assess students’ performance during WIL, commonly in the form of online reflective journals (e.g., Edgar, Francis-Coad, & Connaughton, 2013; Larkin & Beatson, 2014; Sheridan, Kotevski, & Dean, 2014) and e-portfolios (e.g., Kaider et al., 2009)."
