Evidence¶

Osama Swidan 2026¶

Osama Swidan. (2026). Responding to AI-generated emotional alerts: teachers' intervention and students' engagement in the mathematics classroom. ZDM-MathematicsEducation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01810-7

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of one whole-class episode; after the triple alert the teacher posed a deeper question about the location of point D, and student responses referencing geometric properties were read as activation of the new orientation. The author states the branching occurred across multiple engagement structures simultaneously, not at the level of a single student.

"The simultaneous appearance of three red-framed rectan- gles on Dash4Emotion coincided with a moment when sev- eral students’ engagement structures seemed to lose momen- tum around a narrow task framing (“produce the minimum value”)."
