## Evidence

### Drost-de Klerck Amanda M 2020

Drost-de Klerck Amanda M, Olgers Tycho J, van de Meeberg Evelien K, Schonrock-Adema Johanna, ter Maaten Jan C. (2020). Use of simulation training to teach the ABCDE primary assessment: an observational study in a Dutch University Hospital with a 3–4 months follow-up. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank analyses of 40 separate skills and competences across the three measurement moments. Table 2 lists the seven declining skills, including "Mentions abnormal findings in the C", "Examines the D completely", "Communicates clearly", "Shows confidence" and "Shows good leadership".

> "Most of the separate skills did not show significant differences between mean rank at T2 and T3 (30 out of 40). Some skills (7 out of 40) had a significantly lower mean rank at T3 than at T2, but significantly higher than at T1 (table 2)."
