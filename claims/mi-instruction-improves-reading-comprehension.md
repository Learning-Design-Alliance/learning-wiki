## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Because ANCOVA assumptions were not obtained for reading comprehension, an independent-samples t-test showed a significant difference between groups at the .01 level. [→ Habib Soleimani 2012](#habib-soleimani-2012)

## Evidence

### Habib Soleimani 2012

Habib Soleimani, Ahmad Moinnzadeh, Zohreh Kassaian & Saeed Ketabi. (2012). The Effect of Instruction Based on Multiple Intelligences Theory on the Attitude and Learning of General English. English Language Teaching, 5(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p45

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing experimental (n=32) and control (n=29) groups on the reading comprehension subtest, applied because the homogeneity assumptions for ANCOVA "was not obtained"; t = 2.95, df 55, exceeded the critical value 2.16, significant at p = .01.

> "The t-observed which is 2.95 with the df of 55 is larger than the critical amount which is 2.16. Therefore, with a 99% confidence it can be claimed that the research hypothesis claiming the existence of difference between MI-based instruction of reading comprehension compared with the traditional ways of teaching is accepted"
