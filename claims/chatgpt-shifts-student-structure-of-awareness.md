## Evidence

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode analysis of Group 4's end-of-lab ChatGPT dialogue shows the interaction "led to a shift of awareness" away from emerging standing-waves reasoning toward an incorrect air-particles-pushing explanation.

> "Thus, the interaction led to a shift of awareness regarding critical aspects of acoustic levitation with the equipment; from having to do with sound waves from the two speakers sometimes “strengthening” each other (I.e., on their way to discovering standing waves) and towards one students original notion that the styrofoam levitates mainly due to “air particles” physically pushing on it from below."

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025 (2)

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode analysis of Group 2's kelvin-to-celsius exchange shows the interaction "strengthened their focus on temperature" while they lacked any model linking speed of sound to wavelength.

> "Instead, here the group’s interaction with ChatGPT strengthened their focus on temperature, and they kept on haphazardly trying various calculations to finish the lab."
