## Evidence

### Khodadady and Elahi 2012

Ebrahim Khodadady, Majid Elahi. (2012). The Effect of Schema-Vs-Translation-Based Instruction on Persian Medical Students' Learning of General English. English Language Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n1p146

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA results on the 120-item schema-based cloze MCIT: pretest means 35.20 (control) and 33.50 (experimental) showed homogeneity; posttest means were 34.90 versus 77.70, a "superior performance" for SBI.

> "the control and experimental groups were at the same level of language proficiency when the experiment started. However, instructing the experimental group based on the microstructural approach of schema theory has resulted in their superior performance not only on the post test schema-based cloze MCIT (F = 217.451, df =59, p <.001)"
