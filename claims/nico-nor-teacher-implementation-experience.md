## Evidence

### teacher-exp-1

Dominguez, X., Vidiksis, R., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Presser, A., Bueno, M., & Orr, J. (2023, April). Integrating science, mathematics, and engineering: Linking home and school learning for young learners. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/180

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Teacher interviews (24 total, one per unit per teacher) and post-survey data from the eight intervention classrooms. Teachers described both hands-on and digital resources as engaging and reported children really enjoyed the hands-on activities in each unit.

> "Overall teachers reported appreciating the program's resources and finding it easy to implement in their classroom."

### modifications-1

Dominguez, X., Vidiksis, R., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Presser, A., Bueno, M., & Orr, J. (2023, April). Integrating science, mathematics, and engineering: Linking home and school learning for young learners. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/180

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview-reported modifications across units: shadow activities required dark areas, leading some teachers to convert small-group to whole-group formats; ramps activities were time- and space-intensive; plant growth that was slower than expected forced substitutions such as planting grass seeds.

> "A few teachers needed to make activity modifications related to challenges finding and/or making dark enough areas in their classrooms to support shadow creation and exploration."
