### Eka Woldeab Daniel 2025

Eka Woldeab Daniel, Tola Tiruwork Tamiru, Negasi Reda Darge. (2025). Effects of students’ mathematics learning strategy and perceived task difficulty on their achievement of mathematics. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337115

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of survey and teacher-made test data from 351 grade 10–11 students found the two latent predictors jointly accounted for 22% of achievement variance; "the joint effects of learning strategy and perceived task difficulty" were significant. The authors report the remaining 78% was not predicted.

> "The path analysis indicated that 22% of variance in mathematics achievement was significantly explained by the joint effects of learning strategy and perceived task difficulty."
