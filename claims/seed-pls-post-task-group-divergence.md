## Evidence

### Cortese 2026

Cortese, F., Hruska, P., McLaughlin, K. J., Coderre, S. P., Protzner, A. B., Krigolson, O. E., Hecker, K. G. (2026). Expertise Related Changes in Resting‐State Functional Connectivity Patterns Following a Clinical Reasoning and Decision‐Making Task. Brain and Behavior 16, no. 1: e71153. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71153

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Seed-PLS analysis (500 permutation tests, bootstrap resampling) of FPPFC connectivity: "The first LV (p < 0.05; 70.65% crossblock covariance explained)" separated groups post-task; novices coupled with PCC, mPFC, angular gyrus, experts with PaCG, ITG, SFG, SPL. The pre-task condition did not contribute.

> "The first LV (p < 0.05; 70.65% crossblock covariance explained) revealed both positive and negative correlations that differentiated between groups during the post‐task resting‐state condition."
