### Sarvestan Javad 2025

Sarvestan Javad, Thananithichot Stithorn, Miyosawa Yukihide, Hirabayashi Koichi, Yamada Kodai, Kobayashi Fumiya, Ogihara Nanami, Takeda Noa, Okamura Eri, Matsumura Shogo. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the same 160-respondent postgame survey assessing game balance via three Likert parameters. The authors report the means "3.05, 2.49, and 3.65" and interpret them as "a balanced design"; this is the authors' interpretation of descriptive data, not a tested comparison.

> "On the 5-point Likert scale, a score of 3 indicates an ideal rating. The mean scores for length, difficulty, and gameplay were 3.05, 2.49, and 3.65, respectively, indicating a balanced design."
