Reliability analysis of LLM coding across the four themes in the 14-transcript study. With optimal settings and polished prompts, MT was the only theme at "moderate agreement ( κ = 0.55)", a limit the article attributes to MT "not being domain specific".

> "Even after the LLMs were used with optimal settings and polished few-shot prompts, the only theme that showed moderate agreement ( κ = 0.55) with human raters was MT. This limitation of LLMs can be due to MT not being domain specific, which LLMs cannot always reliably rate [34]."
