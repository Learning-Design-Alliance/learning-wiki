### Patricia H. Manz 2013

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Record review of Family Partnership Agreements in one Head Start program found adult centered goals predominated: "all but two families identified adult centered goals" and "A small portion of families (n = 16) identified child development goals."

> "Among the families who established goals, all but two families identified adult centered goals, which largely centered on broad educational or financial achievements (e.g., obtain GED, buy a home) or on improving their relationships with partners. A small portion of families (n = 16) identified child development goals."
