# Three OER competencies depend on educational level, though the definition-of-elements association is weak and non-linear

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square tests found significant dependence between educational level and competence for defining OER elements (p = .006), communicating in another language (p = .011), and OER search (p = .001). [→ García-López 2017](#garcia-lopez-2017)
`q2 i2` For defining OER elements, the printed effect size Spearman's Rho = .258 indicates the association with level of studies is very low and not linear. [→ García-López 2017 (2)](#garcia-lopez-2017-2)

## Evidence

### García-López 2017

García-López, R.-I., Cuevas Salazar, O., Ramírez-Montoya, M.-S., & Tenorio-Sepúlveda, G.-C. (2017). Competencies for Production, Search, Diffusion and Mobilization of Open Educational Resources. International Education Studies, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n4p78

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square independence test from the 134-teacher survey, module one question six on defining OER elements (objective, strategy, purpose, audience). The article's summary states only three of 37 tests were significant, with the other two being module two questions five and seven.

> "The independence of the variables (level of education and competence on definition of elements) was also tested, obtaining a Chi-square of 10.24 with a value of p = .006, which is significant, thus there is dependence between the two variables."

### García-López 2017 (2)

García-López, R.-I., Cuevas Salazar, O., Ramírez-Montoya, M.-S., & Tenorio-Sepúlveda, G.-C. (2017). Competencies for Production, Search, Diffusion and Mobilization of Open Educational Resources. International Education Studies, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n4p78

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman's Rho computed on the same definition-of-elements variables; the printed effect size is Spearman's Rho = .258, described by the authors as "a very low dependency", so the significant chi-square does not reflect a strong linear trend.

> "To determine whether this dependence between variables is linear, the Spearman's Rho correlation coefficient for nonparametric data was completed, obtaining a value of .258, which is a very low dependency, thus can probe that the level of studies and the level of this competence is not linearly related."
