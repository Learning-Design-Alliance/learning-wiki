Kim Seung Jae, Kwon Oh Deog, Kim Kyae Hyung, Lee Ji Eun, Lee Seung-Hee, Shin Jwa-Seop, Park Sang Min. (2019). Investigating the effects of interprofessional communication education for medical students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.125

Kirkpatrick reaction-stage evaluation via two extra post-training questions on a 5-point Likert scale. The authors interpret the 3.94±1.00 method rating as students being "generally satisfied with the teaching methods." These are descriptive means with no significance test.
