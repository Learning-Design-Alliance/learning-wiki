## Evidence

### Almuzaini Ohood K. 2025

Almuzaini Ohood K., Wali Saad M., Harbi Maan H., Azhari Hala F. (2025). Evaluating the impact of a journal club activity on pharmacy students’ learning outcomes and professional skills development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07691-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's Exact Test comparisons in the learning outcomes domain across course/year groups (77 hospital pharmacy vs. 130 cardiovascular pharmacology students); both contrasts reached significance with the printed p-values. No effect sizes were reported.

> "A greater proportion of students in the hospital pharmacy course (58.4%) reported improvement in critical thinking compared to 42.3% in the cardiovascular pharmacology course (p = 0.031). Similarly, 72.7% of hospital pharmacy students reported improved presentation skills, compared to 50.8% of cardiovascular pharmacology students (p = 0.002)."
