## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh 2020

Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Not all disadvantages are equal: Racial/ethnic minority students have largest disadvantage of all demographic groups in both STEM and non-STEM GPA. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.04376](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.04376)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of mean STEM GPA by gender within demographic groups (Fig. 2). The article reports the difference "typically ranges from 0 to 0.1 grade points" with no significant differences for many groups; Cohen's d values printed in Fig. 2 are mostly near zero (e.g., d = 0.13, 0.04).

> "in STEM GPA that diﬀerence is much less consistent and typically ranges from 0 to 0.1 grade points. For many demographic groups we see no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between men and women’s mean STEM GPA."
