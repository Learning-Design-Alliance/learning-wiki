Evidence¶

Khanlarzadeh and Nemati 2016¶

Mobin Khanlarzadeh, Majid Nemati. (2016). The effect of written corrective feedback on grammatical accuracy of EFL students: An improvement over previous unfocused designs. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 4(2). https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired t-tests within the control group (df = 16) comparing draft and revision scores on the three writing tasks. The study reports each revision was "significantly different" from its draft; no effect size was printed.

"the mean scores of the control group on revision 1, revision 2, and revision 3, respectively, are significantly different from their mean scores on writing task 1, writing task 2, and writing task 3 (t = -3.22, p = .007; t = -2.68, p = .01; t = 3.12, p = .008)."
