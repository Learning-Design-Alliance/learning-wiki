### Shiying Wang 2026

Shiying Wang, Joan Bosson-Heenan, Andrew Grotzinger, Jan C. Frijters & Jeffrey R. Gruen. (2026). Shared genetic architecture of reading and attention disorders using genomic structural equation modeling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00433-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SuSiE Bayesian fine-mapping of the 12q13.12 locus using European 1000 Genomes LD, covering 194 SNPs within ±100 kb. The article reports "a single 95% credible set containing 22 variants" and that no single variant reached PIP > 0.5, reflecting dense LD.

> "The association signal was resolved into a single 95% credible set containing 22 variants (Table 2). All of them had suggestive p-values (𝑝 < 1 × 10−5) in the GWAS predicated on Cg. The credible set achieved 95% cumulative posterior probability, indicating a high likelihood that it contains the causal variants."
