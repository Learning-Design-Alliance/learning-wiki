## Evidence

### Pan Pan 2026

Pan Pan, Zhou YaXiong, Jiang YaoWen, Xia YiQin, Han TianYong, Pan E., Chen Yao. (2026). Knowledge-based Basic Life Support competency and associated factors among healthcare professionals across different hospital levels: a multicenter cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1881120

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 2,017 responses with technicians/other clinical staff as reference. The article reports 'Physicians (OR = 3.01, 95% CI: 1.58–5.74, p < 0.001) and nurses (OR = 2.46, 95% CI: 1.31–4.63, p = 0.005)'.

> "Physicians (OR = 3.01, 95% CI: 1.58–5.74, p < 0.001) and nurses (OR = 2.46, 95% CI: 1.31–4.63, p = 0.005) had significantly higher odds of achieving high knowledge-based BLS competency than the reference professional group."

### Pan Pan 2026 (2)

Pan Pan, Zhou YaXiong, Jiang YaoWen, Xia YiQin, Han TianYong, Pan E., Chen Yao. (2026). Knowledge-based Basic Life Support competency and associated factors among healthcare professionals across different hospital levels: a multicenter cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1881120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression for total BLS knowledge score. The article reports unstandardized B coefficients of '18.46 (95% CI: 14.76–22.17, p < 0.001)' for physicians and '16.89 (95% CI: 13.30–20.48, p < 0.001)' for nurses.

> "Physicians and nurses had significantly higher BLS scores than the reference group, with B coefficients of 18.46 (95% CI: 14.76–22.17, p < 0.001) and 16.89 (95% CI: 13.30–20.48, p < 0.001), respectively."
