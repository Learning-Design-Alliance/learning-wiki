## Evidence

### Bureau 2022

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of meta-analytic correlations comparing classroom education and physical education samples. The amotivation correlations with autonomy and relatedness were "substantially more negative in the classroom samples as compared to the physical education samples", while competence–amotivation was consistent across contexts.

> "Regarding education context (i.e., classroom education vs. physical education), the correlations between amotivation and both autonomy and relatedness is substantially more negative in the classroom samples as compared to the physical education samples."
