### Atharva Dange 2026

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Binary logistic regression predicting A/B versus C/D grades with engagement level and three prior-performance covariates. High-engagement students "exhibited a large odds ratio for earning a high grade relative to the low-engagement group," but with "wide confidence intervals" and no statistical significance.

> "Students in the high-engagement group exhibited a large odds ratio for earning a high grade relative to the low-engagement group"

### Atharva Dange 2026 (2)

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Same logistic regression model: "two prior performance measures, pre-aiPlato homework average (p = 0.013) and quiz average (p = 0.008), emerged as statistically significant predictors of final grade category," which the authors contrast with Exam 1's dominance for the final exam.

> "Interestingly, two prior performance measures, pre-aiPlato homework average (p = 0.013) and quiz average (p = 0.008), emerged as statistically significant predictors of final grade category in the logistic regression analysis."
