## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A Kruskal-Wallis test found a significant difference in commitment in attending AC among enrollees, volunteers, and audiences, though pairwise post-hoc tests showed no significant difference between any pair. [→ Santos 2021](#santos-2021)

## Evidence

### Santos 2021

Santos, D. J. D., Goto, T., Lu, T.-Y., Ho, S. C.-C., Wang, T.-W., On, A. Y. L., Hashimoto, T., & Young, S.-C. (2021). Investigative Study on Preprint Journal Club as an Effective Method of Teaching Latest Knowledge in Astronomy. arXiv:2106.01688. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-semester Likert-scale questionnaire of the NTHU AC elective class (10 enrollees, 8 volunteers, 2 audiences), analyzed with non-parametric tests. The omnibus test was significant: "H(2) = 6.36, p = 0.042". The pairwise Dunn test with Bonferroni adjustment showed no significant difference for any pair, with enrollees vs audiences at p = 0.089.

> "Our countercheck with Kruskal- Wallis test suggests that there is a signiﬁcant diﬀerence in commitment in attending AC among enrollees, vol- unteers, and audiences, H(2) = 6.36, p = 0.042."
