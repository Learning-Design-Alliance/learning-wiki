## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Panse et al., a diagnostic forced-option test administered to undergraduate students (N = 111) in which "students tend to treat frames of reference as being fixed to concrete objects". The review attributes the finding to that study; no effect size is printed.

> "First, Panse et al. showed that students tend to treat frames of reference as being fixed to concrete objects, in the sense that they are localized and physically extended to the dimensions of that object."
