Vats Shivangi 2026¶

Vats Shivangi, Thomas Manuel S., Jathanna Vinod. (2026). Efficacy of Hand Gestures and the Hand as Foot Teaching Method Compared to Traditional Methods in Medical and Dental Education: A Scoping Review. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70190

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's introduction summarizes, citing Yohannan et al., a randomized controlled trial of Air Anatomy gestures combined with lectures on extraocular-muscle anatomy; gesture learners "scored significantly higher in post‐tests." No effect size is printed.

"This randomized controlled trial revealed that students who learned with the inclusion of hand gestures scored significantly higher in post‐tests for both recall and application of knowledge compared to those who received traditional lectures only."
