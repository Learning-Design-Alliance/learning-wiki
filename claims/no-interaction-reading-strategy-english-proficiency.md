## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two-way ANOVA found no significant strategy-by-proficiency interaction (F = .180, p = .673), suggesting PRWR applies across proficiency levels. [→ Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo 2018](#azhar-aziz-lubis-gunadi-harry-sulistyo-2018)

## Evidence

### Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo 2018

Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo. (2018). PRWR: Evidence of Its Effectiveness in Teaching Academic Content-Area Reading across English Proficiency. Dinamika Ilmu, Vol. 18 No. 1. http://dx.doi.org/10.21093/di.v18i1.1070

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on posttest scores of the 58 students with strategy (PRWR vs TRA) and English proficiency (high vs low) as factors; the Groups*TOEFL interaction yielded F = .180, p = .673, so "there was no interaction between the reading strategy and English proficiency".

> "This turned out that the obtained p-value was .673, meaning that it was greater than the level of significance .05 (sig .673 > .05). This means that the null hypothesis was accepted and the alternative hypothesis was rejected, and thus there was no interaction between the reading strategy and English proficiency."
