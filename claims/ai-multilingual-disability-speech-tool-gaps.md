### anchor-speech

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A teacher's observation reported in the interviews about speech tool limitations for accented speakers, noting the AI guesses wrong on certain sounds, which can isolate kids.

> "However, she noted that current speech tools are very hit and miss, especially for different accents: "If you are a Puerto Rican Spanish speaker, the AI won't pick up on dropped sentence endings, so it looks like they have grammatical errors.""
