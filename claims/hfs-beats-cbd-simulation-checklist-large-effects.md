## Evidence

### Moliterno Nathalia Veiga 2024

Moliterno Nathalia Veiga, Paravidino Vitor Barreto, Robaina Jaqueline Rodrigues, Lima-Setta Fernanda, da Cunha Antônio José Ledo Alves, Prata-Barbosa Arnaldo, de Magalhães-Barbosa Maria Clara. (2024). High-fidelity simulation versus case-based discussion for training undergraduate medical students in pediatric emergencies: a quasi-experimental study. Jornal de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2024.03.007

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 33 medical interns (HFS n=18, CBD n=15) in a Brazilian private medical school; post-intervention OSCE-type simulation activity scored by two independent raters with standardized checklists, analyzed with linear and logistic regressions adjusting for student ranking. The article reports large effect sizes, "Hedges g varying from 1.15 to 2.20".

> "The HFS group performed significantly better than the CBD group in all dimensions with large effect sizes (Hedges g varying from 1.15 to 2.20), except in correct diagnosis in both scenarios and anamnesis in the second scenario."
