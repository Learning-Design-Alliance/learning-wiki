Evidence¶

Torabian and Tajadini 2017¶

Asefeh Torabian, Massoud Tajadini. (2017). Fostering EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension: Animation Film Technique. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.2p.55

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on the 60 homogenized learners (30 per group, selected via the Oxford Placement Test). Pretest means were 12.93 (control) and 13.27 (experimental); the article prints no effect size for this null comparison.

"Regarding the P – value that is more than 0.05 (P – value =0.5), it can be said with more than 95% confidence, that mean of reading comprehension in control and experimental group in pre-test was not significantly different (t=0.74, df=58, p>0.05)."
