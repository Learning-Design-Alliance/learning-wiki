## Evidence

### Rashid Basima 2026

Rashid Basima, Janke Natasha, Gohar Basem, Blokland Kirsten, Taylor Becky, Coe Jason B. (2026). A professional skills and knowledge training programme enhances the communication confidence of registered veterinary technologists/technicians. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1002/vetr.70493

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group repeated-measures survey of 29 RVTs who completed all three surveys; the seven-item mean score was assessed with Friedman's test (p = 0.002; 𝜒2 = 12.89; df = 2), and post hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni adjustment showed "a statistically significant increase between the pre‐programme and 2‐month follow‐up". Medians rose from 70.21 pre-programme to 78.14 at follow-up.

> "Post hoc analyses revealed a statistically significant increase between the pre‐programme and 2‐month follow‐up (p = 0.006), and between the post‐programme and 2‐month follow‐up (p = 0.003)."
