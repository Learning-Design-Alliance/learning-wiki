### Amogh Sirnoorkar 2025

Amogh Sirnoorkar, Winter Allen, Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Examining Student and AI Generated Personalized Analogies in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of student responses from an online extra-credit activity in a first-semester calculus-based large-enrollment physics course (Spring 2024 enrollment N = 1446). Coding showed "501 (60%) did not include any comparisons," with 323 analogies and 15 similarity statements among the rest.

> "Of the 839 student responses, 501 (60%) did not include any comparisons. These explanations primarily simplified technical vocabulary or provided descriptive accounts of the curve. Among the remaining responses, 338 included comparisons, 323 of which were analogies and 15 were similarity statements."
