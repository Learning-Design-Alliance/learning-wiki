## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2014

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, Julia Gouvea, and Edward F. Redish. (2014). A Vision of Interdisciplinary Education: Students' Reasoning about "High-Energy Bonds" and ATP. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010115](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010115)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interview with a pre-medical student (pseudonym Gregor) immediately after quizzes were returned, part of a set of 22 interviews with 11 students. Gregor, who answered B, C, and D, said "just like in a larger biological context, that reaction does release energy," attributing the difference to whether one looks at the bond alone or the whole reaction.

> "I don't [argue] that breaking a bond releases energy, but just like in a larger biological context, that reaction does release energy."
