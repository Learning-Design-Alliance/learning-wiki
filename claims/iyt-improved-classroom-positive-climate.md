### Murray 2014

Murray, D. W., Rabiner, D. L., & Carrig, M. M. (2014). Grade Level Effects of the Incredible Years Teacher Training Program on Emotion Regulation and Attention. SREE Spring Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562822

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the cluster-randomized trial, blinded observers rated classrooms with the CLASS at pre and post. The article reports "a significant main effect of the intervention on Positive Climate (b = .77, p = .007)"; no effect size was printed.

> "Results indicate a significant main effect of the intervention on Positive Climate (b = .77, p = .007), reflecting that teachers in the intervention group were rated as having significantly more positive change in classroom climate than control teachers over time."
