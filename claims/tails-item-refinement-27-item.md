# CFA refinement removes three weakly loading items and adds one theoretically justified residual covariance, yielding a 27-item scale

## Evidence

### Salim Nabhan 2026

Salim Nabhan, Anita Habók. (2026). Language teachers' AI literacy: A psychometric study based on the ED-AI framework. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence 10 (2026) 100583. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100583

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

CFA-based model refinement with sample 2 (N = 227). CON2 and EVA5 were deleted for weak loadings, then COL2 (loading below 0.60); a residual covariance between ETH1 and ETH2 was allowed based on modification indices and theoretical justification. Each affected dimension retained at least four items.

> "Examination of standardized factor loadings revealed that two items (CON2 and EVA5) loaded weakly (<0.60) on their intended constructs, suggesting limited contribution to construct representation."
