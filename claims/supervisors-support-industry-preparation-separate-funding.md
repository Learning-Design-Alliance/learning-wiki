### Sudhir Malik 2022 (2)

Sudhir Malik, Aneliya Karadzhinova-Ferrer, Julie Hogan, Rachel Bray, Rami Kamalieddin, Kevin Flood, Amr El-Zant, Guillermo Fidalgo, David Bruhwiler, Matt Bellis. (2022). Facilitating Non-HEP Career Transition. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.11665](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.11665)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Supervisor responses from the Snowmass survey (Figure 7 context). The authors report strong supervisor support for HEPA-industry partnerships and note that funding "must come from a source separate from their core HEPA research support".

> "Among supervisors, there is also strong support for their students and mentees to participate in HEPA-industry partnerships and mobility programs that would prepare them well for industry jobs"
