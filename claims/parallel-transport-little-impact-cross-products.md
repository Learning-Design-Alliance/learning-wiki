## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Features associated with parallel transport (whether vectors are head-to-tail or tail-to-tail, and whether they are together or separate) have the least impact on correctness and response time for cross product problems. [→ Mary Bridget Kustusch 2015](#mary-bridget-kustusch-2015)

## Evidence

### Mary Bridget Kustusch 2015

Mary Bridget Kustusch. (2015). Assessing the impact of representational and contextual problem features on student use of right-hand rules. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analyses of 2119 problems found Tails (p = 0.84 for correctness, p = 0.4246 for response time) and Separation (p = 0.72 for correctness, p = 0.0381 for response time) were the least significant predictors, suggesting "parallel transport may not be as much of an issue for cross products as it is for vector addition and subtraction".
