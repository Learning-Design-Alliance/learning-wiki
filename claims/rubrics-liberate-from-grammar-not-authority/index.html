Evidence¶

Turgut 2015¶

Turgut, F., & Kayaoğlu, M. N. (2015). Using rubrics as an instructional tool in EFL writing courses. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 11(1), 47-58. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105202

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive conclusion from the combined quantitative and interview results: students felt they could produce better writing when teaching emphasized process over product, yet the product-oriented EFL tradition still transmitted fixed cultural and linguistic behavior and teachers remained the primary audience.

"It can be said, however, that students were virtually liberated from the narrow perception of writing based on the notion of correct grammar."
