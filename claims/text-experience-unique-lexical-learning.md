### Nation 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Montag, Jones and Smith (ref 45), that "Even text written for young children is more lexically diverse than speech", with syntax differences also noted (ref 46). > "Even text written for young children is more lexically diverse than speech"
