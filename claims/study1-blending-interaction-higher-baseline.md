### Lonigan 2016

Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2016). Response to Instruction in Preschool: Results of Two Randomized Studies with Children At Significant Risk of Reading Difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000054

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 follow-up of the marginally significant midyear-score-by-treatment interaction for Pre-CTOPPP Blending. At 1 SD above the midyear mean, intervention children (Adj. M = 14.59) scored significantly lower than controls (Adj. M = 17.33), F(1, 76.00) = 5.16, p < .03; no differences at or below the mean.

> "for children 1 SD above the mean on the Blending subtest at midyear, children who received the code-focused instruction (Adj. M = 14.59) scored significantly lower (p < .03) than did children in the BAU control group (Adj. M = 17.33), F(1, 76.00) = 5.16, p < .03"
