### Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI) program evidence summary 2024

Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI) program evidence summary. (2024). Evidence for ESSA. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/strategic-adolescent-reading-intervention-stari/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

One student randomized study: 483 6th-8th graders below proficient on the MCAS in 8 Title 1 schools were randomly assigned to STARI or business-as-usual control. After one year, "STARI students scored significantly higher" on the three RISE/ReadBasix measures, with small effect sizes (d = +0.20, +0.21, +0.18).

> "STARI students scored significantly higher on measures of word recognition (Cohen’s d = +0.20), efficiency of basic reading comprehension (Cohen’s d = +0.21), and morphological awareness (Cohen’s d = +0.18), qualifying STARI for a Strong rating."
