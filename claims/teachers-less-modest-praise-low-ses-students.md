## Evidence

### Schoneveld 2023

Schoneveld, E., & Brummelman, E. (2023). "You did incredibly well!": teachers' inflated praise can make children from low-SES backgrounds seem less smart (but more hardworking). npj Science of Learning, 8:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00183-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis in the same within-subjects online experiment with 106 primary school teachers. "teachers gave low-SES students less modest praise than they gave high-SES students" (V = 71, p = 0.003); the preregistered test of this score against zero was not significant (V = 71, p = 0.997).

> "An exploratory, not-preregistered one-tailed Wilcoxon signed rank test showed that the location of teachers' modest praise difference scores was significantly smaller than 0, V = 71, p = 0.003, indicating that teachers gave low-SES students less modest praise than they gave high-SES students."
