# Directly prompted Claude 3.7 micro-level deep feedback was judged both necessary and effective in 94.69% of 630 comments

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 630 micro feedback comments from Claude 3.7, 600 were finalized as necessary (95.24%) and 596 of those as effective, yielding 94.69% both necessary and effective. [→ Adnan Labib 2026](#adnan-labib-2026)

## Evidence

### Adnan Labib 2026

Adnan Labib, Yixuan Huang, Jiahui Wu, John Maurice Gayed, Zheng Yuan, Qiao Wang. (2026). WrAFT: a Modularized Automated Writing Evaluation System for Argumentative Essays. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14524 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.14524](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2607.14524)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Human evaluation of Claude 3.7 micro feedback on the same 40 essays, rated on necessity and effectiveness by two raters with third-rater resolution; inter-rater agreement was AC1 = 0.91 for necessity and AC1 = 1.00 for effectiveness (both p <.001).

> "Among the necessary comments, 596 were deemed effective while 4 were ineffective, and thus the proportion of both necessary and effective comments is 94.69%."
