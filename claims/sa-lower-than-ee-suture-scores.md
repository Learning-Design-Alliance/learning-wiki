## Evidence

### Riquin Élise 2026

Riquin Élise, Le Nerzé Thomas, Goulin Jeanne, Rony Louis, Boucher Sophie, Martin Ludovic, Schmitt Françoise. (2026). Student self-assessment: feasibility, advantages and limitations; example of a workshop for trainee surgeons using a suture score. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08223-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 70 trainee doctors' suture scores comparing self-assessment and expert evaluation on a multimodal grid found "the mean of the total difference in scores between the SA and the EE was − 2.2 (± 4.9)".

> "The mean of the total difference in scores between the SA and the EE was − 2.2 (± 4.9). For the checklist and OSATS sub-scores, the mean of the difference in points was respectively − 1.1 (± 2.6) and − 1.1 (± 3.1)."
