## Evidence

### Hazaea and AlZubi 2017

Abduljalil Nasr Hazaea & Ali Abbas AlZubi. (2017). Effects of CDA Instruction on EFL Analytical Reading Practices. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 11(2), 88-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1171140

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the 14-item interpretation subscale in the same 43-student quasi-experiment; the questionnaire mean rose from 2.89 to 4.01, significant at .000. The article notes interpretation "scored the lowest mean (1.12) in improvement" on the questionnaire and 1.19 on the test.

> "Discourse analysis (interpretation practice) in the post - questionnaire scored a mean of 4.01 compared to 2.89 in the pre- questionnaire with standard deviations of .445 and .410; respectively."
