## Evidence

### Becker 2025

Becker, E. S., Hascher, T., Goetz, T., & Staub, F. C. (2025). Exploring antecedents of student teachers' emotions during instructional experiences: A situation‐specific analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S133–S152. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12738

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of diary reports from 178 student teachers after three lessons of a six-lesson unit (N = 511 lessons). The article reports "enjoyment was the most intensely experienced emotion among student teachers (M = 4.07)" and, in a frequency analysis, intense enjoyment in 79.9%, anxiety in 14.1% and anger in 7.8% of lessons.

> "The results confirm Hypothesis 1a, that is enjoyment was the most intensely experienced emotion among student teachers (M = 4.07), followed by anxiety (M = 2.25) and anger (M = 1.79)."
