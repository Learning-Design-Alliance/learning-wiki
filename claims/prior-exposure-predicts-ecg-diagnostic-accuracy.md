## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2021

Viljoen Charle André, Millar Rob Scott, Manning Kathryn, Hoevelmann Julian, Burch Vanessa Celeste. (2021). Clinically contextualised ECG interpretation: the impact of prior clinical exposure and case vignettes on ECG diagnostic accuracy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02854-x

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of associations between reported prior exposure and correct diagnoses across the 25 tested ECGs. The article reports OR 1.46 (95% CI 1.24–1.71) with vignettes and OR 1.58 (95% CI 1.35–1.84) without, i.e. participants were "more likely to make a correct ECG diagnosis" after prior exposure.

> "All participants were more likely to make a correct ECG diagnosis if they reported having seen the condition during prior clinical training, whether they were provided with a case vignette (odds ratio [OR] 1.46, 95% confidence interval [CI] 1.24–1.71) or not (OR 1.58, 95% CI 1.35–1.84)."
