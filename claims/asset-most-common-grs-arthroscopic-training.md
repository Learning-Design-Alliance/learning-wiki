Frequency analysis of subjective tools across the 28 included studies. Six types of GRS were reported; ASSET led with 10 studies, BAKSSS followed with three, and other GRS appeared in one study each.

> "The most commonly used GRS for assessment was the Arthroscopic Surgical Skill Evaluation Tool (10 studies) [32,33,36–40,43,45,46], followed by Basic Arthroscopy Knee Skill Scoring System (three studies)"
