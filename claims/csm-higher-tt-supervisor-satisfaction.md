## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gürsoy 2016

Gürsoy, E., Kesner, J. E., & Salihoglu, U. M. (2016). Clinical Supervision Model in Teaching Practice: Does it Make a Difference in Supervisors' Performance?. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 41(11). https://doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2016v41n11.5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nonparametric comparison of supervisor satisfaction surveys (12 Likert-type items, Cronbach's alpha .97 for TTs) between experimental (n=191 TTs) and control (n=100 TTs) groups over two years. The article reports "statistically significant differences in the overall rating of the US" favoring the experimental group; no effect size is printed.

> "The Mann-Whitney U test for the TTs evaluations of the US revealed statistically significant differences in the overall rating of the US, U = 6958.00, p < .001. TTs who were in the experimental group had higher scores on their evaluation of their US."
