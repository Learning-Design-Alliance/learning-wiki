q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Pearson correlation analysis in the 980-student sample; the printed coefficients include r = 0.507 for academic integrity and transparency and r = 0.370 for data protection, privacy, and risk management, all p < 0.001. The strongest correlation r = 0.507 corresponds to a large effect (|r| ≥ .37), so impact is 3.

"All six predictor variables exhibited positive and statistically significant correlations with the ethical use of generative artificial intelligence (all p < 0.001): ethical and technical AI literacy (r = 0.449), academic integrity and transparency (r = 0.507), critical thinking and content verification (r = 0.477), self-regulation and academic responsibility (r = 0.437), data protection, privacy, and risk management (r = 0.370), and institutional guidance and ethics education (r = 0.396)."
