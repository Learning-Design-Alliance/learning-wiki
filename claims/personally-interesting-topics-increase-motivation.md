This is the author's report of their topic-and-data task design, from the paper's seven tasks section, contrasting with earlier assessments that provided a list of topics. One student analysed Pokémon play frequency, another birds, another missing people in their country.

> "Instead, we encouraged students to select topics that interested them personally, based on their hobbies, political views, or backgrounds. The goal was to increase motivation by allowing them to work on ideas they were already passionate about."
