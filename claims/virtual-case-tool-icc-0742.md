### Klemenc-Ketis Z 2013

Klemenc-Ketis Z, Kersnik J. (2013). New virtual case-based assessment method for decision making in undergraduate students: a scale development and validation. BMC Medical Education 13:160. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-160

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional observational study at Maribor Medical School: scoring sheets of 147 (92.5%) fourth-year students' virtual case reports were independently assessed by two teachers. The study reports "The ICC for matching of the final total scores on assessment tool of both assessors was 0.742"; per-item ICCs ranged from 0.604 to 0.782 in Table 1.
