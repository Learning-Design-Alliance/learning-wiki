## Evidence

### Lin Chen 2019

Lin Chen, Gao Junyi, Zheng Hua, Zhao Jun, Yang Hua, Zheng Yue, Cao Yihan, Chen Yufei, Wu Guoliang, Lin Guole, Yu Jianchun, Li Hanzhong, Pan Hui, Liao Quan, Zhao Yupei. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled trial randomized 71 surgical residents at PUMCH into 3D and CT groups by stratified randomization on PGY. The article reports the "significantly different" PGY1 result with "mean difference (MD) = 3.2, p = 0.03"; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Overall, the PGY1 and PGY2 sum scores (Fig. 2a) were significantly different between the 3D and CT groups (PGY1, 3D vs. CT: mean difference (MD) = 3.2, p = 0.03."
