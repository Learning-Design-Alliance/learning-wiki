## Evidence

### Expósito 2020

Expósito, A., Sánchez-Rivas, J., Gómez-Calero, M. P., & Pablo-Romero, M. P. (2020). Examining the use of instructional video clips for teaching macroeconomics. Computers & Education, 143, 103705. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103709

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The experimental group received online access to videos via the university virtual platform for one week, while the control group attended two chalk-and-talk lectures covering similar content and received written static material. The design ensured "the only significant difference between the two treatment groups was the use of the instructional videos."

> "the experiment was designed so that the only significant difference between the two treatment groups was the use of the instructional videos; one group of students with online access to the media materials (experimental group) and others in a pure face-to-face setting (control group)."
