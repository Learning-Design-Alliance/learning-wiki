## Evidence

### Mok 2022

Mok, S. Y., Rupp, D., & Holzberger, D. (2022). What kind of individual support activities in interventions foster pre-service and beginning teachers' self-efficacy? A meta-analysis. Preprint submitted for peer-review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100552

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression moderator analyses across the 28-study sample found no significant effects for feedback on lessons, modeling of teaching or planning practices, or guided reflection. The article reports no effect sizes for these null moderators, so impact is unquantified.

> "No further moderator effects were significant in our meta-analysis."
