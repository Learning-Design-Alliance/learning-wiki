Interrupted time series study observing teamwork behaviours during 39 real trauma activations across four periods (pre-test baseline, didactic-based phase, simulation phase, decay phase) in an emergency department with various staff. "CTS measurements for teamwork improved in 12 out of 14 domains during ISTS phase"; all measures returned to baseline during decay.
