Evidence¶

D. Hallen 2015¶

D. Hallen, N. Sangeetha. (2015). Effectiveness of Mind Mapping in English Teaching Among VIII Standard Students. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 1, January-March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068370

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two equivalent-group pretest-posttest experiment with 30 students per group; Table 1 reports calculated 't' values of 1.990 (knowledge), 3.048 (understanding) and 2.646 (application), all "Significant" at the 5% level against a table value of 1.96 for df = 58.

"Here, the calculated 't' values are greater than the table value. Therefore, we reject the null hypothesis. Thus, there is a significant difference between control and experimental group students in their gain scores"
