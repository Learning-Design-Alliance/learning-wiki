## Evidence

### Okur A 2026

Okur A, Keskin A, Aydın Berktaş Ö. (2026). The relationship between sleep quality and academic achievement among students in health-related disciplines: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08861-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of lifestyle variables (Table 4) in the cross-sectional survey found the tablet association; "86.1% of students with a tablet had poor sleep quality, compared to 71.7% of students without a tablet".

> "Additionally, the presence of a tablet in the bedroom was significantly associated with poor sleep quality; 86.1% of students with a tablet had poor sleep quality, compared to 71.7% of students without a tablet (p = 0.002)."

### Okur A 2026 (2)

Okur A, Keskin A, Aydın Berktaş Ö. (2026). The relationship between sleep quality and academic achievement among students in health-related disciplines: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08861-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' discussion of the multivariable logistic regression (Table 5) reports the association "did not persist after adjustment for potential confounders in the multivariable model".

> "While the presence of a tablet in the bedroom was associated with poor sleep quality in univariate analysis, this association did not persist after adjustment for potential confounders in the multivariable model."
