## Evidence

### Monteyne S. 2024

Monteyne S., Struyve C., Gesquière N., Neutens T., Wyffels F., van Braak J. & Aesaert K. (2024). Teachers’ computational thinking content knowledge: Development of a measurement instrument. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105181

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CTT item analysis in which “Items 6 and 17 have point-biserial correlations below the critical threshold of .20”, and item 10 also fell below .20; all three were excluded. Items 6, 10 and 17 “do not provide meaningful information” about respondents’ knowledge.

> "Since these items neither have 451 acceptable difficulty levels, nor discriminate well, they were excluded from further analysis."
