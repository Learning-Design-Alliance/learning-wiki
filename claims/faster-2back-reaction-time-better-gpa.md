## Evidence

### Kassaw Chalachew 2025

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2025). Psychosocial and cognitive predictors of academic achievement among higher education students in Southern Ethiopia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335968

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same 30-student sample. Faster 2-back reaction time and higher 2-back correct ratio, plus lower Go/No-Go RT deviation, each correlated significantly with GPA; RT average was retained in the regression where it showed only a trend (p = 0.060).

> "as well as between cognitive performance measures (Correct ratio (ρ = 0.52, p < 0.01) and RT average (ρ = –0.56, p < 0.01) in the 2-Back task; RT deviation in Go/No-Go Task (ρ = –0.48, p < 0.01))"
