Starch 1910¶

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Equivalence study in which all grades were tested with list 1 on one day and another list the next, permitting direct comparison of lists 2–6 with list 1. Printed differences: list 2 is .9 point easier, list 3 .9 easier, list 4 2.6 easier, list 5 .9 harder, list 6 3.4 harder than list 1.

"These results showed that the lists are practically identical in difficulty, list 6 being slightly more difficult and list 5 slightly easier."
