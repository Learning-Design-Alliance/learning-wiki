## Evidence

### Schildkraut 2019

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief's cost synthesis reports purchase costs of "$1,000 to $30,000 or more per unit," with school-appropriate devices at $4,000-$5,000, excluding maintenance, repairs and replacement.

> "the cost may range from $1,000 to $30,000 or more per unit for the initial purchase, though devices best suited for schools fall in the range of $4,000 to $5,000"

### Schildkraut 2019 (2)

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief describes staffing needs: multiple people per station including screeners, backup screeners, bag checkers and an overseeing guard, plus ongoing training. Slow screening can force staggered first periods, as in NYC where over 91,000 students daily pass through detectors in 68 buildings.

> "Each individual scanning station typically requires multiple people: One person acts as the screener on the initial check; another is responsible for performing backup screens"
