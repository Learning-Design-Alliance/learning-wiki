Descriptive statistics from the quasi-experiment (Table 3): control group M 51.66 (SD 3.05) to 50.80 (SD 2.61); treatment group M 51.77 (SD 2.65) to 52.36 (SD 1.94). These are descriptive means; no test is attached to this contrast in the article.

> "Table 3 shows a decrease in the mean value (51.66 to 50.80) for the control group. The mean score for the treatment group increased in the post-test to 52.36 from 51.77 in the pre-test."
