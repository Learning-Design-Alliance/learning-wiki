Clinical simulation in the teaching of cardiovascular physical examination with nursing students: a systematic review. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2023-0429

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of six included experimental and quasi-experimental studies (four quasi-experimental, two randomized) comparing clinical simulation with traditional strategies. The review reports "statistically significant results in favor of clinical simulation" in the majority of studies, with heterogeneity in methods and sample sizes.

> "The majority of the studies(12,26,27,29) presented statistically significant results in favor of clinical simulation for cardiovascular assessment, compared to traditional teaching methods."
