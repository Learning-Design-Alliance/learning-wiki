# Both Math Flash (NC) tutoring and Pirate Math (WP) tutoring outperform no tutoring on number-combination fluency, and the two tutoring groups do not differ

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Third graders with mathematics difficulty assigned to NC tutoring outperformed control students on the NC factor score (d = 0.55). [→ Fuchs 2009](#fuchs-2009)
`q3 i2` Third graders with mathematics difficulty assigned to WP tutoring also outperformed control students on the NC factor score (d = 0.62), with no difference between the two tutoring groups. [→ Fuchs 2009](#fuchs-2009)

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2009

Fuchs, L. S., Powell, S. R., Seethaler, P. M., Cirino, P. T., Fletcher, J. M., Fuchs, D., Hamlett, C. L., & Zumeta, R. O. (2009). Remediating Number Combination and Word Problem Deficits Among Students With Mathematics Difficulties: A Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 101(3), 561-576. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014701

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Posttest analysis of covariance from the randomized control trial of 133 third graders stratified by site and difficulty status, with pretest as covariate. On the NC factor score, "Students in NC tutoring (p < .0002, d = 0.55) and WP tutoring (p < .003, d = 0.62) outperformed control students."

> "Students in NC tutoring (p < .0002, d = 0.55) and WP tutoring (p < .003, d = 0.62) outperformed control students, whereas the two tutoring groups did not differ."
