q2 · i? · review · r2

The article cites reviewed studies of integrated quality projects using service-learning, reporting student self-reported improvements in self-efﬁcacy, self-esteem, attitudes, and community-participation behaviors. No effect sizes or sample details are printed in the article.

"For instance, evidence of the impact can be found in integrated quality projects that used service-learning as a methodology. Students reported improvements in self-efﬁcacy, self-esteem, positive attitudes towards school, and an increase in behaviors related to community participation"
