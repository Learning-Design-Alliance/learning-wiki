Clark 2010¶

Clark, J. W., Sayre, E. C., and Franklin, S. V. (2010). Fluctuations in Student Understanding of Newton's 3rd Law. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0260

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Reported in the abstract and conclusions for the E&M response curve: after the week-4 dip during scalar instruction, response recovers when vector-based magnetic-field topics resume.

"Student response rebounds to its initial values, however, once instruction returns to the vector-based topics involving magnetic fields."
