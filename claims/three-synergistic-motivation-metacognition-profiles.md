## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Latent profile analysis of 1,046 Greek secondary students yielded three well-defined profiles (minimal 28.5%, adequate 48.2%, exceptional 23.3%) in which motivational beliefs and metacognitive self-regulation covaried positively. [→ Katsantonis I 2023](#katsantonis-i-2023)
`q3 i?` No profile combined high motivation with low metacognitive self-regulation or the reverse, contradicting prior person-centred findings of negative higher-order interactions. [→ Katsantonis I 2023](#katsantonis-i-2023)

## Evidence

### Katsantonis I 2023

Katsantonis I, McLellan R. (2023). Profiling students' motivation and metacognitive self-regulation: Links with school language achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0289367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent profile analysis of 1,046 Greek secondary students (Mage = 13.97) on six z-scored MSLQ indicators; the three-profile solution was retained after fit-index and elbow-plot evaluation. The study reports "A significant proportion (28.5%) of the students belonged to profile 1" with minimal scores, 48.2% adequate, and 23.3% exceptional.

> "The analysis showed that the students within each profile had similar levels of motivation and metacognitive self-regulation, whilst profiles where students scored high on motivation but low on metacognitive self-regulation, or the reverse, did not emerge."
