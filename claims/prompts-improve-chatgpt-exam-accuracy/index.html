Subgroup 2 meta-analysis comparing studies with and without prompts, random-effects models. The review reports "Statistically significant differences were observed between the results (P=.03)" for GPT-3.5 and P<.01 for GPT-4; meta-regression identified prompt use as a significant heterogeneity source.
