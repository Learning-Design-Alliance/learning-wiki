# ChatGPT-4o achieves higher final-attempt accuracy than Gemini 1.5 Pro on ophthalmology MCQs, significantly in English but not in Turkish

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the final attempt ChatGPT-4o outperformed Gemini 1.5 Pro in English (97.7% vs 93.2%, p=0.039), while the Turkish difference was not statistically significant (97.3% vs 94.1%, p=0.159). [→ Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025](#sabaner-mehmet-cem-2025)
`q3 i?` Across all three attempts overall (n=220), ChatGPT-4o gave more correct answers than Gemini 1.5 Pro, reaching statistical significance only in the English final attempt (215 vs. 205). [→ Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025 (2)](#sabaner-mehmet-cem-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025

Sabaner Mehmet Cem, Yozgat Zübeyir. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini 1.5 Pro on Ophthalmology-Related Questions in the Turkish Medical Specialty Exam. Turkish Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4274/tjo.galenos.2025.27895

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional evaluation of 220 ophthalmology MCQs from Turkish MSEs, asked to both premium chatbots in Turkish and English. The final-attempt comparison named "ChatGPT-4o demonstrated higher accuracy rates" in both languages, tested with chi-square/Fisher/Yates methods.

> "In the final attempt, ChatGPT-4o demonstrated higher accuracy rates in both Turkish (97.3%) and English (97.7%) compared to Gemini 1.5 Pro (94.1% and 93.2%, respectively). This difference reached statistical significance in the English-language comparison (p=0.039), while it did not reach significance in Turkish (p=0.159)."

### Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025 (2)

Sabaner Mehmet Cem, Yozgat Zübeyir. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini 1.5 Pro on Ophthalmology-Related Questions in the Turkish Medical Specialty Exam. Turkish Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4274/tjo.galenos.2025.27895

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Overall analysis of the same 220-item corpus across the three attempts. ChatGPT-4o led in every attempt, with the only significant contrast being the English final attempt (215 vs. 205; p=0.039).

> "In Turkish, ChatGPT-4o achieved 209, 210, and 214 correct answers, while Gemini 1.5 Pro produced 202, 204, and 207, respectively (p>0.05 for all comparisons). In English, this difference reached statistical significance in the final attempt (215 vs. 205; p=0.039), although earlier attempts did not achieve significant differences (p=0.312)."
