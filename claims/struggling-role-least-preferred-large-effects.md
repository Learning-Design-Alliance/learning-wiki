# The Struggling Student role was rated significantly less favorably than each of the four constructive roles, with large effect sizes

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Post-hoc Wilcoxon tests showed the Struggling Student role was rated significantly less favorably than the Tutor, Excellent Student, Peer, and TA roles, all with large effect sizes (r > .50). [→ Wangda Zhu 2025](#wangda-zhu-2025)

## Evidence

### Wangda Zhu 2025

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon signed-rank post-hoc comparisons (N = 26) of role rankings; all four contrasts against the Struggling Student role were significant with large effects, e.g. versus Tutor "r= 0.85".

> "the Struggling StudentrolewasratedsignificantlylessfavorablythantheTutorrole(Z= 21.00, p < .001,r= 0.85), the Excellent Student role (Z= 28.00,p < .001,r= 0.80), the Peer role (Z= 47.50,p < .001,r= 0.67), and the TA role (Z= 64.00, p=.004,r= 0.57). All effect sizes were large (r > .50)"
