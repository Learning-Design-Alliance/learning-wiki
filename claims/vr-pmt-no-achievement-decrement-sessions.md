Session-level task achievement rates for all ten participants across VRT sessions 5–8 were examined; elderly achievement was more variable than young participants, but rates stayed relatively stable for most participants as complexity increased.

> "Table 4 indicates that there were no significant decrements in task achievement rates across sessions and that the difficulty progression in the training design was generally appropriate."
