## Evidence

### Berkeley J. Dietvorst 2014

Berkeley J. Dietvorst, Joseph P. Simmons, and Cade Massey. (2014). Algorithm Aversion: People Erroneously Avoid Algorithms After Seeing Them Err. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000033

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confidence ratings (5-point scales) collected after the choice across Studies 1, 3a, 3b, and 4 showed the asymmetric pattern; e.g., in Study 1, seeing the model perform lowered confidence in the model significantly while seeing the human perform did not (p = .262).

> "Whereas seeing the human perform did not consistently decrease confidence in the human’s forecasts—it did so significantly only in Study 4, seeing the model perform significantly decreased participants’ confidence in the model’s forecasts in all four studies."
