`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Haponik et al., a study of 20 experienced primary care practitioners, 23 uninstructed medical interns and 22 interns with sleep-medicine instruction, using simulated 30-minute consultations. Instructed interns asked about sleep more often, while uninstructed groups did not record sleep history.

> "Interns who had received instruction in sleep medicine more often asked about sleep (81.8%), but uninstructed interns (13%) and physicians (0%) did not record sleep history during consultation."
