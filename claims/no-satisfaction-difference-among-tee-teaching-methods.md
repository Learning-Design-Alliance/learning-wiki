# Resident satisfaction with TEE training did not differ significantly among the three teaching methods

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Average satisfaction scores on a 6-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire (maximum 30) showed no significant difference between the didactic (27.47 ± 4.37), simulation (29.41 ± 1.23), and operating-room (29.18 ± 2.04) groups. [→ Weber U 2019](#weber-u-2019)

## Evidence

### Weber U 2019

Weber U, Zapletal B, Base E, Hambrusch M, Ristl R, Mora B, Schaller B. (2019). Resident performance in basic perioperative transesophageal echocardiography: Comparing 3 teaching methods in a randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(36):e17072. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000017072

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction questionnaire with six statements covering overall satisfaction, perceived benefit, session duration, relevance, expectations, and recommendation, rated 1–5. All three groups rated their training highly; the null difference was not accompanied by a printed effect size, and equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the average score of satisfaction with the training on the Likert scale in the didactic (27.47 ± 4.37; range 13–30), the simulation (29.41 ± 1.23; range 26–30), and the operating room group (29.18 ± 2.04; range 23–30)."
