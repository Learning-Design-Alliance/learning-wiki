### Tanja Bross 2024

Tanja Bross, Ulrike Elisabeth Nett, & Martin Daumiller. (2024). Interrelations Among Achievement Goals and Achievement Emotions: A Meta-Analytic Examination. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09931-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

In moderator analyses across the pooled effect sizes, the differentiation of mastery goals into task versus learning facets emerged as a relevant moderator, whereas population (students, teachers, athletes, etc.) did not moderate the interrelations.

> "Facets of mastery goals were identified as relevant moderators of the interrelations among achievement goals and emotions, in contrast to population."
