`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire (9 Likert items, Cronbach α = 0.84, KMO = 0.91) completed by both groups after the game. Independent samples T-tests found the face-to-face group rated these items higher; Table IV reports item 1: 4.63 vs 4.37, p < 0.01, d = 0.36, and item 9 d = 0.39.

> "a statistically significant difference with small to medium effect size (Cohen’s d = 0.36) was found for overall opinion (item 1) and statistically significant differences with small effect size (Cohen’s d ≥ 0.2) were found for self-reported learning effectiveness (item 2), fun (item 6) future use (item 8)"
