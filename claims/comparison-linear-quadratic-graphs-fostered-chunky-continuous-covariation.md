Teaching experiment in which two ninth-grade students compared a curved height-time graph with a linear whiteboard graph; the authors infer that 'earlier they demonstrated reasoning at the stage of gross coordination of values' and that the students then exhibited chunky continuous covariation.

> "Whereas earlier they demonstrated reasoning at the stage of gross coordination of values, they now exhibited reasoning characteristic of the chunky continuous covariation stage."
