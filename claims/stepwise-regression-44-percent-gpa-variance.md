## Evidence

### Keles Muserrefe Nur 2025

Keles Muserrefe Nur, Yesiloglu Anil, Inal Ozun Ozgu. (2025). The role of demographic and psychosocial factors in academic achievement: insights from a cross-sectional study on physiotherapy and rehabilitation students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06958-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression on 256 students, with entry threshold p < 0.05 and collinearity checks (tolerance above 0.1, VIF below 2). The article reports "44.0% of the variance in GPA was explained" across the three retained predictors; R² is variance explained, not a standardized effect size.

> "The analysis revealed that 44.0% of the variance in GPA was explained by STAI score (R² = 0.31, p < 0.001), ULS-8 score (R² = 0.11, p < 0.001), and gender (R² = 0.02, p = 0.002)."
