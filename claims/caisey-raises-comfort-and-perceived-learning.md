## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, D. J., Jain, N. M., Burbano, V., Guzman, J., Keum, D., Kim, S., Kogut, B., & Wright, N. (2026). How assigned AI use before class shapes active student engagement in class. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7585318](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7585318)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Two-wave survey panel of 627 students who completed both midterm and end-of-term surveys, with student fixed effects and wave-specific dose counts instrumented by assignment. Each qualifying use corresponds to roughly one tenth of a midterm standard deviation in either rating; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Use between the midterm and the end of the term was associated with greater comfort, whether measured by qualifying use (Supplementary Table S11, Model 2: 𝜆! = 0.131, SE = 0.063, p < 0.05) or by assignment (Model 1: 𝜆! = 0.076, SE = 0.035, p < 0.05). Perceived learning showed the same pattern (Model 8: 𝜆! = 0.124, SE = 0.055, p < 0.05; Model 7: 𝜆! = 0.073, SE = 0.031, p < 0.05; Figure 6)."

### Wang 2026 (2)

Wang, D. J., Jain, N. M., Burbano, V., Guzman, J., Keum, D., Kim, S., Kogut, B., & Wright, N. (2026). How assigned AI use before class shapes active student engagement in class. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7585318](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7585318)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Same two-wave survey panel; four-item outcomes rated on a seven-point agreement scale. Focus and motivation showed no detectable association with later use (p > 0.1), and the four-item index also showed no detectable change; coefficient estimates are printed (focus λ = −0.035, SE = 0.056; motivation λ = 0.040, SE = 0.066; both p > 0.1), so the null is a non-detection.

> "Qualifying use between the midterm and the end of the term was not associated with ratings of mental focus (Supplementary Table S11, Model 4: 𝜆! = −0.035, SE = 0.056, p > 0.1) or of motivation to prepare and participate (Model 6: 𝜆! = 0.040, SE = 0.066, p > 0.1)."
