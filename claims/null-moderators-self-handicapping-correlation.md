## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Gender composition of samples did not significantly moderate the correlation, and the article likewise reports non-significant results for the other hypothesized moderators. [→ Schwinger 2014](#schwinger-2014)

## Evidence

### Schwinger 2014

Schwinger, M., Wirthwein, L., Lemmer, G., & Steinmayr, R. (2014, February 10). Academic Self-Handicapping and Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0035832

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Mixed-effects moderator analyses across the 49 effect sizes. For gender the authors report a descriptive trend toward "slightly higher effect sizes in samples with larger proportions of males" that was not significant; publication status, origin, ethnicity, time, indicator, specificity, domain, and domain matching were also non-significant.

> "With respect to gender, there were slightly higher effect sizes in samples with larger proportions of males. However, the differences were not significant."
