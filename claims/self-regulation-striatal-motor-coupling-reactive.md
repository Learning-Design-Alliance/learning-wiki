# Self-regulation scores correlate with functional coupling between the right striatum and left motor cortex during reactive inhibition, but not frontostriatal coupling during proactive inhibition

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` During reactive inhibition, self-regulation scores showed a moderate relationship with coupling between the left motor cortex and right striatum (p = 0.04); during proactive inhibition there was no effect (p = 0.37). [→ Pas P. 2021](#pas-p-2021)

## Evidence

### Pas P. 2021

Pas P., Hulshoff Pol H.E., Raemaekers M., Vink M. (2021). Self-regulation in the pre-adolescent brain. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychophysiological-interaction analysis in the cohort found "a moderate relationship of self-regulation questionnaire scores and coupling between the left motor cortex and the right striatum" during reactive inhibition. No effect size was printed; the proactive-inhibition coupling effect was not significant (F(1, 536) = 0.81, p = 0.37).

> "we found a moderate relationship of self-regulation questionnaire scores and coupling between the left motor cortex and the right striatum, F(1, 536) = 4.18, p = 0.04; but no interaction with sex, F(1, 536) = 3.08, p = 0.08 (Fig. 3)."
