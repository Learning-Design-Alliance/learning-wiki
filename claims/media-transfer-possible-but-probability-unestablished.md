# Transfer from instructional media is empirically possible but its probability under heavy real-world exposure remains unestablished

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` It remains unclear whether heavy exposure to TV, analogous to much engagement in computer programming, has lasting effects that transfer to new contents, situations, or activities. [→ Salomon 1983](#salomon-1983)

## Evidence

### Salomon 1983

Salomon, Gavriel; Gardner, Howard. (1983). The Computer as Educator: Lessons from Television Research. Harvard Project Zero. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED253201

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors argue that experimental demonstrations establish possibility, not probability, of far transfer from heavy exposure, while far transfer is precisely the major educational justification claimed for computers, as Perkins (1983) comments regarding video games.

> "They do not show, however, that transfer is probable; thus one is still unclear whether heavy exposure to TV (analoguous to, say, much engagement in computer programming) has any lasting effects that transfer to new contentssituations, or activities."
