### FONTES 2024

FONTES, Daniel Trugillo Martins. (2024). Natureza da ciência em sala de aula: aspectos empíricos para uma investigação em física. A Física na Escola, 22, 230136. https://doi.org/10.59727/fne.v22i1.136

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative classroom study of one 90-minute session with 25 third-year high school students in São Paulo state; the author reports from interactions and final discussion that students "assimilated some of the consensual aspects of the nature of science". No effect sizes are printed.

> "interactions with the students revealed that they assimilated some of the consensual aspects of the nature of science, namely: there is no universal scientific method, scientists use imagination and belief in scientific practice, and theory is not a consequence of experimental observation and vice versa."
