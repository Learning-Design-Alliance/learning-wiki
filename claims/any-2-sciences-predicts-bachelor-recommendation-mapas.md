## Evidence

### Curtis Elana 2015

Curtis Elana, Wikaire Erena, Jiang Yannan, McMillan Louise, Loto Rob, Airini, Reid Papaarangi. (2015). A tertiary approach to improving equity in health: quantitative analysis of the Māori and Pacific Admission Scheme (MAPAS) process, 2008–2012. International Journal for Equity in Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12939-015-0133-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple logistic regression on 918 MAPAS interviewees (2008–2012), adjusted for interview year, gender and ancestry, predicting final January recommendations. Science exposure was the strongest Bachelor predictor, with "exposure to any 2 Sciences (OR:7.897, CI:3.855-16.175; p < 0.0001)"; no standardised effect size printed.

> "The significant predictors associated with a final recommendation of Bachelor included: exposure to any 2 Sciences (OR:7.897, CI:3.855-16.175; p < 0.0001); higher scores on the MAPAS mathematics test (OR:1.043, CI:1.028-1.059; p < 0.0001)"
