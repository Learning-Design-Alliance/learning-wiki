Semi-structured interviews with 18 stratified-purposive experimental-group participants, coded by two coders (inter-coder kappa = 0.82). Themes included reduced evaluative threat (12/18), volitional shift (11/18), monitoring activation (13/18), evaluative reflection (9/18), and residual planning difficulty (10/18).

> "The first theme, self-efficacy repair through adaptive difficulty (endorsed by 14 of 18 participants), captured narratives in which a sequence of small successes on ability-matched tasks gradually restored confidence"
