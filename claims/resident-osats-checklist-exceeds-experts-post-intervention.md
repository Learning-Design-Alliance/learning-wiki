## Evidence

### Moktar Joel MD 2016

Moktar Joel MD, Bradley Catharine S. BScPT MSc, Maxwell Alexandra BSc, Wedge John H. MD FRCS(C), Kelley Simon P. MBChB FRCS(Tr&Orth), Murnaghan M. Lucas MD MEd FRCSC. (2016). Skill Acquisition and Retention Following Simulation-Based Training in Pavlik Harness Application. The Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery. American volume. https://doi.org/10.2106/JBJS.15.00905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA with post-hoc testing at post-intervention across the three measures. The discussion attributes the difference to the checklist: residents "achieved more points on the task-specific checklist on which they were trained", while GRS scores were indistinguishable.

> "there were no significant differences found at time point 2 among the expert, resident, and allied health groups for any of the three measures, with the exception of a slightly higher OSATS score in the resident group compared with the expert group (p = 0.006)."
