## Evidence

### Sulisworo 2017

Sulisworo, D., Sulistiyo, E. N., & Akhsan, R. N. (2017). The motivation impact of open educational resources utilization on physics learning using Quipper School app. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 18(4), Article 9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1161803

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA in the same quasi-experiment (27 control, 34 treatment grade XI physics students, one semester). Table 3 prints relevance F = 8.268, Sig. .006 and confidence F = 11.481, Sig. .001. The article states the groups "were significantly different" on these factors; no effect size is printed.

> "From Table 3 above it can be seen that the attention factor, confidence, and relevance between the control group and treatment group were significantly different at the level of error of 5% or 95% level of confidence."
