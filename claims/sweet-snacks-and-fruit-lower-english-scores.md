## Evidence

### Bleiweiss-Sande R. 2019

Bleiweiss-Sande R., Chui K., Wright C., Amin S., Anzman-Frasca S., Sacheck J.M. (2019). Associations between Food Group Intake, Cognition, and Academic Achievement in Elementary Schoolchildren. Nutrients 11(11):2722. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11112722

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Ordered logistic regression of 430 fourth graders from the FLEX baseline. The article reports "Higher reported intakes of fruits (OR = 0.75, 95% CI = 0.62, 0.91)" and for sweet snacks OR = 0.72, each associated with lower English scores. No significant associations existed between individual food groups and math score.

> "Higher reported intakes of fruits (OR = 0.75, 95% CI = 0.62, 0.91) and sweet snacks (OR = 0.72, 95% CI = 0.59, 0.88) were associated with lower English score after adjustment for sex, English language learner status, enrollment in IEP, mother's education level, and BMI Z-score."
