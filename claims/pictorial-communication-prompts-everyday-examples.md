### N. Poljak 2018

N. Poljak, A. Bosilj, S. Brzaj, J. Dragovic, T. Dubcek, F. Erhardt, M. Jercic. (2018). Physionary - a scientific version of Pictionary. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aadde4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aadde4)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' observation of student behaviour during Physionary play at the summer school. They report students conveying concepts through everyday examples once pictorial communication begins; no systematic data are reported.

> "Once the students start to communicate concepts pictorially, they move from their definitions and try to use everyday examples to convey them to their group."
