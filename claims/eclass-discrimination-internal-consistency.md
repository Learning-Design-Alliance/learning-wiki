## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). Students' epistemologies about experimental physics: Validating the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. arXiv:1512.06869. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-test discrimination via Ferguson's delta on the national pre and post datasets; delta ranges from 0 to 1 and anything above 0.9 indicates good discriminatory power.

> "For both the pre- and post-instruction E-CLASS, we found δ > 0.98, well above the standard threshold."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2015 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). Students' epistemologies about experimental physics: Validating the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. arXiv:1512.06869. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level discrimination using item-test correlations against the r > 0.2 standard threshold adopted for dichotomously scored items (Fig. 4).

> "The majority of the E-CLASS items and the average post-instruction item-test correlation ( r = 0.33) fall above this threshold."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2015 (3)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). Students' epistemologies about experimental physics: Validating the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. arXiv:1512.06869. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010123)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cronbach's alpha for the matched dataset; the article argues alpha is a conservative estimate because multidimensionality drives it downward, and the E-CLASS targets multiple, potentially overlapping aspects of students' ideas rather than a single construct.

> "For the matched data set, we found α = 0 .76 for the pre-survey and α = 0 .83 for the post-survey."
