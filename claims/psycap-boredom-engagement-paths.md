## Subclaims
`q2 i3` EFL PsyCap had a large negative association with academic boredom (β = −0.58) and a large positive association with behavioural engagement (β = 0.56). [→ Wu Yajun 2025](#wu-yajun-2025)

## Evidence

### Wu Yajun 2025

Wu Yajun, Kang Xia, Li Yiyan. (2025). Linking EFL psychological capital and EFL achievement: the chain mediating effect of academic boredom and behavioural engagement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03685-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis in the sample of 1460 secondary EFL learners. PsyCap→boredom "β = − 0.58" and PsyCap→engagement "β = 0.56" were both significant at p <.001; boredom→engagement β = −0.31 (p <.001).

> "First, EFL PsyCap exhibited a large but negative association with academic boredom (β = − 0.58, SE = 0.03, p <.001). Second, the path from EFL PsyCap to behavioural engagement (β = 0.56, SE = 0.04, p <.001) was positive and large in magnitude."
