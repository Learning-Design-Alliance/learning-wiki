### Matilda Knierim 2024

Matilda Knierim, Sahil Jain, Murat Han Aydoğan, Kenneth Mitra, Kush Desai, Akanksha Saran, and Kim Baraka. (2024). Prosody as a Teaching Signal for Agent Learning: Exploratory Studies and Algorithmic Implications. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Offline evaluation of a prosody-augmented TAMER agent on the four interactive RL sessions needing no interpolation (N = 4), using count of optimal actions under the learned feedback function H. "The prosody condition statistically significantly outperformed the TAMER baseline" for three of four participants.

> "However, when looking at individual participants, for three out of the four participants, the prosody condition statistically significantly outperformed the TAMER baseline."
