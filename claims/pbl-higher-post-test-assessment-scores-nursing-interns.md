## Evidence

### Xue Huarong 2025

Xue, H., Lu, Y., & Liu, L. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning in enhancing health education skills, self-directed learning, and critical thinking among nursing interns: a prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07936-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective cohort study with 142 nursing interns randomly assigned (71 per group) to traditional or PBL teaching for two months. Post-test scores: traditional 77.36 ± 6.78 vs. PBL "80.43 ± 6.15, t = 2.820, P = 0.006"; pre-test scores showed no significant difference.

> "the post-test scores revealed a statistically significant difference (77.36 ± 6.78 vs. 80.43 ± 6.15, t = 2.820, P = 0.006)"
