# Standardized patients joining a DRE training program report mainly intrinsic motivation and interest, with no predominant motive pattern

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SPs participating in DRE training were mainly motivated by intrinsic motivation and interest, and the study found no obviously predominant motives for program participation. [→ Nikendei Christoph 2015](#nikendei-christoph-2015)

## Evidence

### Nikendei Christoph 2015

Nikendei Christoph, Diefenbacher Katja, Köhl-Hackert Nadja, Lauber Heike, Huber Julia, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Herzog Wolfgang, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Jünger Jana, Krautter Markus. (2015). Digital rectal examination skills: first training experiences, the motives and attitudes of standardized patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0292-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with 4 standardized patients after their first DRE training session at Heidelberg, analysed by qualitative content analysis with inductive coding. The interviews showed SPs were "mainly there due to intrinsic motivation and interest"; one SP could not name a concrete motive and another agreed spontaneously.

> "When asked about their motives for participating in the DRE training, the SPs indicated that they were mainly there due to intrinsic motivation and interest."
