`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis across elementary, middle, higher and undergraduate levels reported in Table 5 (elementary ES=0,729; middle school ES=1,046; higher school ES=0,295; undergraduate ES=0,991). The between-groups test did not exceed the critical value, so no significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "It is seen that Q value is smaller than the critical value that corresponds to 3 degree of freedom in chi -square table (Q B=24,996, p=0,000). Regarding this, there is not a statistically significant difference between groups in terms of educational level."
