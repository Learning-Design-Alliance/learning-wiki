## Evidence

### Turgut 2018

Turgut, S., & Gülşen Turgut, İ. (2018). The Effects of Cooperative Learning on Mathematics Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 663-680. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11345a

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis across elementary, middle, higher and undergraduate levels reported in Table 5 (elementary ES=0,729; middle school ES=1,046; higher school ES=0,295; undergraduate ES=0,991). The between-groups test did not exceed the critical value, so no significant difference was detected (Table 5: QB=6,521, p=0,089); equivalence was not tested. The article's prose repeats the figures "QB=24,996, p=0,000" here, which are the technique moderator's values from Table 4, not the educational-level result.

> "The inter-groups homogeneity value (QB) in terms of educational level was calculated as 6,521. The critical value of 3 degree of freedom is 7,815 in chi -square table at 95% significance level. [...] Regarding this, there is not a statistically significant difference between groups in terms of educational level."
