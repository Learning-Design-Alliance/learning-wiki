## Evidence

### Huang Hsiang-Ping 2025

Huang Hsiang-Ping, Tien Yun, Lin Yung-Chang, Yu I-Chen, Chien Nai-Hui. (2025). Effects of empathy mapping and mini-simulation on second-year nursing students’ empathy and communication self-confidence: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06686-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective quasi-experimental study of 98 second-year nursing students (52 experimental, 46 control) measured with the self-rated Confidence in Communication Scale at pre- and post-test; the post-test comparison favored the experimental group, with means of "40.20 ± 6.54 vs. 43.88 ± 6.35" and p = 0.006.

> "After the course, the self-confidence in communication was significantly lower in the control group than in the experimental group (40.20 ± 6.54 vs. 43.88 ± 6.35, respectively; p = 0.006)."
