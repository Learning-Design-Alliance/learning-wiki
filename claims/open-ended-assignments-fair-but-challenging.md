## Evidence

### Jerzak 2015

Jerzak, S. and Lew, R. R. (2015). Introducing interdisciplinary science to second year undergraduates in a Current Topics in Biophysics course. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1504.06557](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1504.06557)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert-scale course evaluation item administered after an assignment was returned, aggregated over six years. "82% (n=87) of the students selected 4 or 5", indicating perceived fairness of the generous, effort-weighted grading scheme. No effect size reported.

> "When asked soon after an assignment was returned whether "The grading system was fair", 82% (n=87) of the students selected 4 or 5 on a Likert scale of 1 (least positive) to 5 (most positive)."
