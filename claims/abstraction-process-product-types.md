## Evidence

### F R Fiantika 2018

F R Fiantika, I K Budayasa and A Lukito. (2018). Internal process: what is abstraction and distortion process? Journal of Physics: Conference Series 983. https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/983/1/012086

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative explorative study with deep interviews of visual-learning-style 5th graders solving a cube-net task. The conclusion reports that "Abstraction as process is mental process" and notes a second type: abstraction as product, an object that is the referent of an abstract idea.

> "Abstraction as process is mental process, it is happen in human mind. There have collected the similar characteristics and ignore irrelevant characteristics."
