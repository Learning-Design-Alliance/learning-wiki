## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Silver 2022

Silver, A. M., Elliott, L., & Libertus, M. E. (2022). Parental math input is not uniformly beneficial for young children: The moderating role of inhibitory control. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000679

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slopes analyses of the regression model showed that "at high levels of children's inhibitory control (1 SD above the mean)", the association was marginally significant (B = 0.10, p = .076), whereas at 1 SD below it was not significant (B = −0.06, p = .163).

> "Simple slopes analyses suggested that, at high levels of children's inhibitory control (1 SD above the mean), there was a marginally significant association between children's math performance and parents' number talk, B = 0.10, 95% CI [−0.01, 0.20], p = .076"
