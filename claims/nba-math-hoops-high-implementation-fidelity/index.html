Evidence¶

McKinney 2026¶

McKinney, D., Walters, K., Powell, K., Sabalburo, E., & Morton, C. (2026). The impact of NBA Math Hoops on students' math achievement: A randomized controlled trial. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681343

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Daily instructional logs from 11 NBA Math Hoops teachers showed mean of 8.31 lessons taught (SD 1.4) and 14.5 games played (SD 8.82). "On average, teachers reported implementing NBA Math Hoops components" on 12 of 14-15 potential days.

"Teachers reported teaching some or all of NBA Math Hoops lessons on an average of 8.44 days, completing an average of 8.31 of the 10 NBA Math Hoops lessons (Table 1). All but three teachers completed at least 8 lessons."

McKinney 2026 (2)¶

McKinney, D., Walters, K., Powell, K., Sabalburo, E., & Morton, C. (2026). The impact of NBA Math Hoops on students' math achievement: A randomized controlled trial. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681343

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Classroom observations and teacher/student interviews found NBA Math Hoops classrooms emphasized "interactive, game-based learning, often using real-world connections to basketball" with higher engagement than control STEM classrooms, which relied more on worksheets and direct instruction.

"Overall, NBA Math Hoops fostered higher levels of participation, deeper cognitive engagement, and greater retention of mathematical concepts."
