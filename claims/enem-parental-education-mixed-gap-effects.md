## Evidence

### Luis E C Rocha 2018

Luis E C Rocha, Luana de F Nascimento. (2018). Assessing student's achievement gap between ethnic groups in Brazil. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of ENEM 2014 scores grouped by six parental education levels per state. In Sao Paulo the White-Black and White-Pardo gaps were "statistically signiﬁcant (p-value< .01)" for all groups but G1; in Amazonas no significant differences except White-Black in G4 mathematics.

> "Looking at pairs of ethnic groups, the gap is statistically signiﬁcant (p-value< .01) between White (higher scores) and Black (lower) students, and White (higher) and Pardo (lower) students for all groups but G1."
