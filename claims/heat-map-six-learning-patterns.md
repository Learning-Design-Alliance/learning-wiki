## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Individual learning trajectories on data-evaluation items fell into six patterns (early, mid, late, unrewarded, flicker on, flicker off), and mid plus late learners averaged 24% of the class, approximately equal to the 22% final exam gain attributed to the format. ## Evidence

### Bailey E. G. 2017

Bailey E. G., Jensen J., Nelson J., Wiberg H. K., Bell J. D. (2017). Weekly Formative Exams and Creative Grading Enhance Student Learning in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-02-0104

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Heat-map analysis of student-by-exam performance on evaluate-experimental-data items across three semesters (Figure 3A, B); proportions in the six categories were consistent across semesters. The article reports mid and late learners "comprised 24% of the class on average".

> "the mid and late learners (those who did not show stable mastery until at least halfway through the semester) comprised 24% of the class on average, a level approximately equal to the gain we observed on this type of item on the final exam with the formative midterm scheme compared with the original course format (22%, Figure 2B)."
