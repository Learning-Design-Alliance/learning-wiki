`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' account of longitudinal missing data: active collection only reached students still attending their entry school, and active parental consent in several schools further reduced eligible participants.

> "Applying these attrition, non-consent, and non-response weights, along with the IPTW weights, in our analysis improved both internal and external validity to the extent that weights were able to appropriately adjust for the factors related to students' likelihood of missing outcome data."
