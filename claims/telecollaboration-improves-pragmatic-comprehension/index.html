Evidence¶

Rafieyan et al. 2014¶

Vahid Rafieyan, Maryam Sharafi-Nejad, Zahra Khavari, Lin Siew Eng & Abdul Rashid Mohamed. (2014). Pragmatic Comprehension Development through Telecollaboration. English Language Teaching, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n2p11

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

True experimental study: 30 Iranian EFL undergraduates randomly assigned to two groups of 15; experimental group received pragmatic instruction plus twice-weekly Facebook contact with American peers for 18 weeks. Independent-samples t-test showed "t (-9.03) = -8.67, p < 0.05, r2 = 0.74"; the printed effect size r2 = 0.74 is described by the article as a very large effect, with experimental mean 18.00 vs control 9.33.

"The magnitude of difference in the mean scores obtained by the control group and the experimental group was extremely significant, t (-9.03) = -8.67, p < 0.05, r2 = 0.74."
