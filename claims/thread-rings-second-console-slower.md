Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

Crossover trial time-to-proficiency analysis for the console B first group on Thread the rings 2; the printed means are B = 368.05 s vs A = 732.80 s with p = 0.040. The console A first group approached significance (p = 0.052).

> "There was a statistically significant mean difference in time to proficiency between consoles only for the Thread the rings 2 exercise for the console B first group (B = 368.05 s vs A = 732.80 s, p = 0.040)."
