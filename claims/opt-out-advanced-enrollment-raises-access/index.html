Kristen Hengtgen 2023¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641932

q1 · i? · associational · r1

The brief reports, citing researchers without naming them, that opt-out placement increases enrollment and success among students of color and low-income students. Washington adopted automatic enrollment statewide for advanced math, English, and science by 2021-22, the first state to do so.

"When advanced courses become opt-out instead of opt-in, larger numbers of students of color and students from low-income backgrounds enroll and succeed in advanced courses, researchers found."
