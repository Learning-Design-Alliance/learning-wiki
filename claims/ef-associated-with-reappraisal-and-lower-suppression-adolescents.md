## Evidence

### Shi Guochun 2026

Shi Guochun, Shi Zimo, Wang Qianqian. (2026). Cross-sectional bidirectional associations between executive function and emotion regulation in adolescents: the moderating role of digital engagement intensity. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05038-8

`q3 · i2`

Structural equation modeling in a cross-sectional study of 368 adolescents aged 12–17, with EF and ER as latent variables indicated by behavioral tasks and self-reports. The article reports "the cross-sectional association between EF and cognitive reappraisal was robust (β = 0.42, p < .001)". Hierarchical regressions additionally showed EF explained 23% of reappraisal variance versus 11% for suppression.

> "the cross-sectional association between EF and cognitive reappraisal was robust (β = 0.42, p < .001), and—consistent with H2—the reverse association from reappraisal to EF was also significant (β = 0.19, p = .002)"
