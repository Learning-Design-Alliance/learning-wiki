# Learning from a single static picture is significantly more successful than learning from an animation or four pictures when identifying spatial arrangements of joined links

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The Picture group significantly outperformed the Four Pictures group (d = 1.11) and the Animation group (d = 1.30) on identification of arrangements. [→ Rolf Ploetzner 2021](#rolf-ploetzner-2021)

## Evidence

### Rolf Ploetzner 2021

Rolf Ploetzner, Sandra Berney, Mireille Bétrancourt. (2021). When learning from animations is more successful than learning from static pictures: learning the specifics of change. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09541-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same three-group experiment (N = 88). On arrangement identification the single-picture group scored highest (M = 3.93 of 4); post-hoc tests showed "d = 1.11" versus Four Pictures and "d = 1.30" versus Animation, both p < 0.01, while Four Pictures and Animation did not differ (LSD = 0.00, p = 0.985).

> "With regard to the identification of arrangements, post-hoc analyses reveal that the group ‘Picture’ significantly differs from the group ‘Four Pictures’ (LSD = 1.00, p < 0.01, d = 1.11) and the group ‘Animation’ (LSD = 1.00, p < 0.01, d = 1.30)."
