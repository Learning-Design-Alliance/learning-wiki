## Evidence

### Shuang Li 2026

Shuang Li, Haiyang Xin, Yimeng Sun, Qiannan Niu, Lingyun Huang, Gaowei Chen, Yibing Zhang, Ching Sing Chai. (2026). MIRACLE: Multi-Agent Intelligent Regulation to Advance Collaborative Learning Environment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.22318/cscl2026.255772](https://doi.org/10.22318/cscl2026.255772)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental repeated-measures ANOVA on 90 fifth-grade students (MIRACLE n=42, general GPT assistant n=48) using the SSRL Questionnaire pre and post. The printed Planning interaction was F = 7.44, p = .008, η²p = .078 and Monitoring F = 7.15, p = .009, η²p = .075; experimental within-group Planning change t = -2.07, p = .045 and Monitoring t = -3.09, p = .004. "Repeated measures ANOVA revealed significant Time × Group interactions for Planning and Monitoring".

> "Repeated measures ANOVA revealed significant Time × Group interactions for Planning and Monitoring (see Table 1). The experimental group improved significantly on Planning, Monitoring, and Reflection, while the control group showed no changes."
