### Imagine Math by Imagine Learning: Evidence Rating Promising 2024

Imagine Math by Imagine Learning: Evidence Rating Promising. (2024). https://www.imaginelearning.com/imagine-math

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A retrospective quasi-experimental study of K–8 students in 16 schools in a charter network in Florida and North Carolina during the 2020–2021 school year. The "treatment group consisted of 9,015 students" matched with an equal number of non-users; the effect size was +0.08.

> "The treatment group consisted of 9,015 students who logged any usage of Imagine Math and were matched with an equal number of non-users from the same network. Students using Imagine Math scored significantly higher than comparison students on the NWEA MAP Growth Math assessment (effect size = +0.08)."
