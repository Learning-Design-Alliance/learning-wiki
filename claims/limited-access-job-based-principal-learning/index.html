Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2022¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Wechsler, M. E., Levin, S., Leung-Gagné, M., & Tozer, S. (2022). Developing effective principals: What kind of learning matters? [Report]. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/641.201

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of survey data from representative national samples of principals affiliated with NAESP and NASSP, plus California and North Carolina statewide samples, found that "only 46% of principals reported having had an internship" with real leadership responsibilities; coaching and mentoring access was rarer.

"Only 46% of principals reported having had an internship during their preparation that allowed them to take on real leadership responsibilities characteristic of a high-quality internship experience, and very few in-service principals reported having access to coaching or mentoring."
