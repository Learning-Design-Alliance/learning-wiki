## Evidence

### Parchment Amelia 2025

Parchment Amelia, Lawrence Wendy, Rahman Em, Townsend Nick, Wainwright Elaine, Wainwright David. (2025). Increasing confidence and competence in supporting behaviour change in physiotherapy practice using Making Every Contact Count Healthy Conversation Skills: a before and after evaluation. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of typed responses to four patient statements (n participants = 23; n responses = 92), scored on an eight-category coding matrix by two researchers. The article reports responses differed significantly (p < 0.001); 100% of participants used at least one open discovery question after training and 91% used 3–4.

> "The number of open discovery questions physiotherapists responded with increased after training from 20 (22% of total (92) responses) to 78 (85% of total (92) responses), whilst responding with giving information/ making suggestions and using closed questions decreased from 35 to 1 and 29 to 5, respectively."
