### Quon S 2022

Quon S, Riddell J, Bench KF, Roepke C, Burner E. (2022). Training Leaders in Trauma Resuscitation: Teacher and Learner Perspectives on Ideal Methods. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2021.5.51428

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative modified grounded theory analysis of 25 semi-structured interviews with trauma team providers at an urban academic Level 1 trauma center, coded with a pooled inter-rater kappa of 0.96, identified the seven methods. "Based on interviewees’ perceptions, we identified seven training methods used to train leadership in trauma resuscitation."

> "Based on interviewees’ perceptions, we identified seven training methods used to train leadership in trauma resuscitation: reflection; feedback; hands-on learning; role modeling; simulation; group reflection; and didactic."
