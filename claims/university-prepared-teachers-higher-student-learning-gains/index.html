Bland 2023¶

Bland, J. A., Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Darling-Hammond, L., & Wei, W. (2023). Strengthening pathways into the teaching profession in Texas: Challenges and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/957.902

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report cites a University of Texas at Austin study comparing student learning gains by teacher preparation route. It reports the gaps "show up in every tested grade level and subject," with the quoted 9th-grade ELA and math figures.

"students with university-prepared teachers gained 2.2 additional months of learning in 9th-grade English language arts (ELA) and 1.8 additional months of learning in 9th-grade math as compared to those whose teachers entered through alternative routes."
