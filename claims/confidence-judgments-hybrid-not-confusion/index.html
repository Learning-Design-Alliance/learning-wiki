Evidence¶

Bauer 2020¶

Bauer, P. J., Esposito, A. G., & Daly, J. J. (2020). Self-derivation through memory integration: A model for accumulation of semantic knowledge. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101271

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Confidence-rating analysis in Experiment 2 (one-way repeated measures ANOVA, 4-point Likert scale). "the ANOVA revealed a main effect of statement type, F(2, 98) = 48.30, p < .001, η2 = .33." Children were also significantly more confident accepting hybrid than new statements.

"the ANOVA revealed a main effect of statement type, F(2, 98) = 48.30, p < .001, η2 = .33. Follow-up comparisons with Bonferroni corrections revealed that children were significantly more confident in their judgments for old statements than new or hybrid statements."
