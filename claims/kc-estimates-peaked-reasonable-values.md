Numerical answers to the fireflies test game collected via a NetLogo extension: the Kc estimate distribution from "106 students" peaked at 0.6≤Kc≤0.75, which the authors read as evidence most students understood the transition; descriptive only.

> "As shown in Fig. 26, the distribution of theK c estimate done by 106 students (with additional 6 “not remembering” null answers) is peaked around reasonable values (0.6≤K c ≤0.75), indicating that most of students understood the game."
