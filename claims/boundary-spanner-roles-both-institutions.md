## Evidence

### Burns 2015

Burns, R. W., & Baker, W. (2015). The Boundary Spanner in Professional Development Schools: In Search of Common Nomenclature. School-University Partnerships, 9(2), 28-40. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130688

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-analysis of role identities across thirteen studies. The authors found "both university-based and school-based individuals assumed boundary-spanning roles" but note the foci of the empirical literature appears more on roles assumed by university-based faculty.

> "From what we could ascertain given the diversity in deﬁnitions, it appeared that both university-based and school-based individuals assumed boundary-spanning roles."
