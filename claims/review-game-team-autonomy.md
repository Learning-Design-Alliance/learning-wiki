## Evidence

### Chou E 2026

Chou E, Cooper D, Goel P, Tang J, Henkle G, Zemeida S, Wolfson H, Shyu YL, Chang E, Abram A, Gerriets VA, Cusick JK. (2026). The use of voiceovers and review games in teaching immunology. ImmunoHorizons. https://doi.org/10.1093/immhor/vlag042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-study survey on review game logistics, scored on a 7-point Likert scale. The study reports students "strongly objected to the idea of preassigned teams in review games (1.89; n = 86)." The authors interpret this as evidence that student choice of teams increased perceived autonomy and intrinsic motivation.

> "Students did not have a strong preference for the ability to use personal phones versus clickers in the review game (3.44; n = 55), yet they strongly objected to the idea of preassigned teams in review games (1.89; n = 86)."
