`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's analysis of the deliberative version identifies its pedagogical repertoire: "Those drawing on deliberative democracy, as constructed by Habermas and Rawls, recommend deliberative pedagogies," alongside communities of inquiry, communicative education such as rhetoric and media education, and social studies, geography, and history as key subjects.

> "Those drawing on deliberative democracy, as constructed by Habermas and Rawls, recommend deliberative pedagogies, including generic deliberative pedagogical strategies (Kessel, 2015; Parker, 2010; Waghid, 2009), working with controversial issues (Camicia & Dobson, 2010; Hess, 2008; Tannebaum, Peterson, & Tierney, 2015), and with structured academic controversies (Bickmore & Parker, 2014; Lo, 2017; Parker, 2010)."

### Edda Sant 2019 (2)
