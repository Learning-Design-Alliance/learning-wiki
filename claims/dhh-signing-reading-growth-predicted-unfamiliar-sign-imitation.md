## Evidence

### Holmer Emil 2017

Holmer Emil, Heimann Mikael, Rudner Mary. (2017). Computerized Sign Language-Based Literacy Training for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Children. The Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education. https://doi.org/10.1093/deafed/enx023

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal cross-over study of 16 DHH signing children in Swedish state special schools, assessed at five occasions with hierarchical linear modeling. The article reports reading skills "improved over time" and word reading development was "predicted by the ability to imitate unfamiliar lexical signs". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "Results indicated that reading skills improved over time and that development of word reading was predicted by the ability to imitate unfamiliar lexical signs, but there was only weak evidence that it was supported by the intervention."
