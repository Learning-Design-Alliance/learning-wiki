## Evidence

### Kalyuga 2004

Slava Kalyuga and John Sweller. (2004). Measuring Knowledge to Optimize Cognitive Load Factors During Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.96.3.558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effect test in Experiment 3 following the significant interaction, comparing the two instructional formats within the low-knowledge group (n = 21 across the two subgroups). The article reports the significant advantage of worked examples for less knowledgeable learners with the statistics quoted.

> "For less knowledgeable learners, the worked-examples group (M ⫽ 4.80, SD ⫽ 1.99; 24% correct, actual range of test perfor- mance scores from 2 to 8) performed significantly better than the problem-solving group (M ⫽ 1.18, SD ⫽ 1.08; 6% correct, actual range of test performance scores from 0 to 3), F(1, 19) ⫽ 27.58, MSE ⫽ 2.49, p ⬍ .01, Cohen's f effect-size index ⫽ 1.20."
