## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wouters 2007

Wouters, P., Tabbers, H. K., & Paas, F. (2007). Interactivity in Video-based Models. Educational Psychology Review, 19, 327–342. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-007-9045-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites Kalyuga et al. (2003) on the expertise reversal effect, and multimedia studies (Lowe 2004; Schnotz and Rasch 2005) showing interactivity can impose extraneous rather than germane load for some expertise levels.

> "Recent research on cognitive load theory, for example, has proven that design guidelines that are beneficial for novice learners can be ineffective or even detrimental when applied to experts (Kalyuga et al. 2003)."
