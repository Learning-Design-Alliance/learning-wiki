## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a two-and-a-half-month treatment, the experimental group using Khate Sefid scored significantly higher than the control group on a reading comprehension post-test (p = 0.04). [→ Abbasi 2015](#abbasi-2015)

## Evidence

### Abbasi 2015

Abbasi, Z., Azizifar, A., Gowhary, H., & Heidari, M. (2015). The Effect of Supplementary Materials on Reading Comprehension Improvement of Iranian Female High School EFL Learners Based on Gaj and Khate Sefid Text books. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4), 179-184. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.179

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 30 Iranian female high school EFL learners per group; the Khate Sefid experimental group (M = 15.57, SD = 3.28) outperformed the control group (M = 13.93, SD = 3.56) on the post-test, t(58) = -2.05, p = 0.04, per Table 4.
