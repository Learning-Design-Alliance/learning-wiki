### Julia Lademann 2024

Julia Lademann, Jannik Henze, and Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2024). Building Bridges – AI Custom Chatbots as Mediators between Mathematics and Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of N = 214 sixth-grade students; emotions measured retrospectively after a 15-minute learning phase with AI-generated versus textbook explanations, analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test. The EG showed higher positive-activating emotions with a printed effect size of d = 0.48 ("small effect size").

> "For the former, a statistically significant difference ( 𝑝𝑝 =  0. 00093, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 48, small effect size) was observed in favor of the EG."
