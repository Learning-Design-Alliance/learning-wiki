# Repeated Reasoning (SMP 8) benchmark levels improved linearly across all three time points (d ≈ 0.77) with no significant quadratic trend

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The linear effect of time on Repeated Reasoning benchmark level was significant (b = 1.41, p = .006; d ≈ 0.77), while the quadratic term was non-significant. [→ Gage 2025](#gage-2025)

## Evidence

### Gage 2025

Gage, N. (2025). The impact of the Mindsets mathematics program on student engagement, ability to reason, and repeated reasoning. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677579

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Three-time-point CLMM on the Repeated Reasoning dataset with polynomial contrasts and a challenge random intercept (SD = 0.579). The quadratic term was non-significant (b = –0.47, SE = 0.56, z = –0.84, p = .399), and the Chinn approximation gave "an estimated d ≈ 0.77."

> "The CLMM revealed a significant linear effect of time on achievement level, b = 1.41, SE = 0.51, z = 2.77, p = .006, indicating that students’ Repeated Reasoning benchmark levels improved across the time points"
