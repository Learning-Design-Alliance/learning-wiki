## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In cross-sectional fMRI, adults and 14-year-olds showed selective anterior hippocampal activation for episodic memory, whereas 8-year-olds showed activation consistent with item recognition. [→ Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A 2012](#ghetti-simona-bunge-silvia-a-2012)

## Evidence

### Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A 2012

Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A. (2012). Neural Changes Underlying the Development of Episodic Memory During Middle Childhood. Developmental cognitive neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2012.05.002

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cross-sectional fMRI study of incidental encoding of object drawings in green or red ink across 8-year-olds, 10–11-year-olds, 14-year-olds, and young adults; adults' left anterior hippocampus responded selectively to item-plus-context memory. The article reports this as "initial support for increased functional selectivity in the anterior hippocampus."

> "These results suggest that the anterior hippocampus becomes functionally specialized for episodic memory around the transition to adolescence."
