## Evidence

### J. Fitzgerald et al 2014

J. Fitzgerald et al. (2014). Important Text Characteristics for Early-Grades Text Complexity. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037289

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Measurement analysis linking the two Rasch substudies: the student maze-based and teacher-judgment logit scales correlated at .79 (p < .01) across 89 texts, which the authors call "evidence of concurrent validity"; a linking equating procedure then brought them onto one 0–100 scale.

> "Next, the correlation between the two logit scales (N = 89 texts) was .79 (p < .01), suggesting that the texts ordered on text complexity similarly whether teachers or students were involved."
