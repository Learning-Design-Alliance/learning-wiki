## Evidence

### Smit 2025

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered one-way ANOVA on posttest outcome scores in a randomized experiment with 285 three- to four-year-olds across four conditions; the article reports F(3, 281) = 0.97, p = .407, "BF = 17.9" favoring no mean differences. All planned contrasts were nonsignificant, including contingent versus other conditions, t(281) = −0.26, p = .795.

> "the mean number of unaided correct operations (or outcome scores) did not signiﬁcantly differ by condition: F(3, 281) = 0.97, p = .407, with the Bayes factor showing strong evidence in favor of the null hypothesis (BF = 17.9)"
