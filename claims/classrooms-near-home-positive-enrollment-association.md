# The number of pre-k classrooms near a student's home was positively related to enrollment, more strongly for full-day classrooms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Each additional full-day pre-k classroom within 0.5 miles of home was associated with a 0.7 percentage point increase in full-day enrollment likelihood, a stronger association than for any pre-k. [→ Ehrlich 2020](#ehrlich-2020)

## Evidence

### Ehrlich 2020

Ehrlich, S.B., Connors, M.C., Stein, A.G., Francis, J., Easton, J.Q., Kabourek, S.E., & Farrar, I.C. (2020). Closer to home: More equitable pre-k access and enrollment in Chicago. Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research, NORC at the University of Chicago, and Start Early. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/closer-to-home

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Pre-policy multilevel logistic regression on CPS cohorts relating the count of (full-day) pre-k classrooms within a 0.5-mile radius of home to enrollment. The classroom-count association was smaller than the distance association for any pre-k (2.5 classrooms near home meant only 1 percentage point higher enrollment), but stronger for full-day pre-k at 0.7 percentage points per classroom.

> "the relationship between the number of full-day pre-k classrooms within 0.5 miles of a student's home and enrollment in full-day is stronger, such that each additional full-day classroom near a student's home was associated with a 0.7 percentage point increased likelihood of enrollment"
