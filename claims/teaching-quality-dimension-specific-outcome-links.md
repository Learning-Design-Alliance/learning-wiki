# Individual student support is positively associated with all three school adjustment outcomes, while cognitive activation is unexpectedly negatively related to reading comprehension

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the student level, individual student support was positively associated with reading comprehension, reading motivation, and school belonging. [→ Heppt 2025](#heppt-2025)
`q2 i?` Contrary to expectations, cognitive activation was negatively related to reading comprehension. [→ Heppt 2025 (2)](#heppt-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Heppt 2025

Heppt, Birgit; Schwarzenthal, Miriam; Scharf, Jan. (2025). Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02147-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel mediation analyses (Model 2a–2c) of the PISA 2018 German sample found all three student-level teaching quality dimensions were significant predictors: "individual student support was positively associated with all three outcome variables".

> "While individual student support was positively associated with all three outcome variables, classroom management was positively related to students' reading comprehension and sense of school belonging"

### Heppt 2025 (2)

Heppt, Birgit; Schwarzenthal, Miriam; Scharf, Jan. (2025). Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02147-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the same multilevel models predicting reading comprehension, the cognitive activation dimension (four items, α = 0.73, referring to German instruction) showed an unexpected negative relationship, contrary to the authors' Hypothesis 3 expectation.

> "cognitive activation was negatively related to students' reading comprehension, indicating that students whose teachers formulated clear learning goals and closely monitored their students' understanding and learning progress performed more poorly on the reading comprehension test than did students in German classrooms with less cognitive activation"
