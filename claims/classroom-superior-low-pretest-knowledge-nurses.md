## Evidence

### Simonsen Bjoerg O 2014

Simonsen Bjoerg O, Daehlin Gro K, Johansson Inger, Farup Per G. (2014). Improvement of drug dose calculations by classroom teaching or e-learning: a randomised controlled trial in nurses. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc auxiliary subgroup analysis (table 2) of participants with pretest score <9 (n=7 e-learning, n=15 classroom): post-course test score 8.4 vs 10.7 (p=0.01) and improvement 0.7 vs 3.6 (p=0.001), favouring classroom. Risk of error reduction also favoured classroom (p=0.04).
