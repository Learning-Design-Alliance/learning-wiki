The toolkit asserts, citing research it does not further specify, that "low-income children lose one to three months of reading skills" each summer while higher-income peers gain, and that cumulative losses can exceed two years by fifth grade.

"Every summer low-income children lose one to three months of reading skills and two months of math skills while higher income peers make slight gains."
