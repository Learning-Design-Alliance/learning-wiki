### Emslander 2022

Emslander, Levy, Scherer, Fischbach. (2022). Value-added scores show limited stability over time in primary school. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279255

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analysis of school VA scores computed from multilevel models on Luxembourg school monitoring data (N = 7,016 students, 151 schools), comparing mathematics VA ranks in 2017 and 2019. The study found VA mathematics scores "correlated at r = .34" across the two years. No effect size beyond the correlation is printed.
