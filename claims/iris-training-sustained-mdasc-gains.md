## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was some evidence of an interaction between previous IRIS training and the RESPONDS training (F (2, 58) = 3.81, P = 0.05), and at 3-month follow up IRIS-trained respondents scored 12.1 units higher than non-IRIS-trained colleagues whose scores dropped. ## Evidence

### Lewis Natalia V. 2017

Lewis Natalia V., Larkins Cath, Stanley Nicky, Szilassy Eszter, Turner William, Drinkwater Jessica, Feder Gene S. (2017). Training on domestic violence and child safeguarding in general practice: a mixed method evaluation of a pilot intervention. BMC Family Practice 18:33. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-017-0603-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary repeated-measures analysis of the interaction between previous IRIS training and RESPONDS training on total M-DASC score; the interaction F (2, 58) = 3.81, P = 0.05 and the T3 mean difference of 12.1 units are printed. No effect size is given.

> "However, at 3-month follow up, mean M-DASC score of IRIS trained respondents stayed 12.1 units higher compared to their non-IRIS trained colleagues whose mean score dropped (95% CI 4.7 to 19.6; P = 0.02)."
