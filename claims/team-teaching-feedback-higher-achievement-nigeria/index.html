Evidence¶

Anani 2016¶

Anani, O. A., Badaki, J. V., & Kamai, R. (2016). Effects of Feedback Intervention on Team-teaching in English Language Classrooms in Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(25), 27-38. https://journals.iiste.org/index.php/JEP/article/view/34512

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact SS2 classes (30 experimental, 32 control) at Concordia College, Yola, Nigeria. The experimental class received rotational team-teaching with feedback mechanisms; the control class received traditional one-teacher instruction. The article reports that "the av erage final exam scores of students receiving team teaching were higher than those of students receiving tradit ional teaching."
