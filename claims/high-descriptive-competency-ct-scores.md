Görgülü Deniz, Cömert Melike, Demir Mustafa, Şahin Çiğdem. (2026). The role of the 2023 earthquakes in the competencies and critical thinking skills of teacher candidates in Türkiye. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05006-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from Tables 1 and 2 for 304 teacher candidates. PPCPS sub-dimension means ranged from X̄ = 3.87 to X̄ = 4.22; CTSS sub-dimensions ranged from X̄ = 3.80 (Precision and Accuracy) to X̄ = 4.22 (Depth, Breadth and Adequacy).

> "The overall mean score was X̄ = 4.12, indicating high perceived competency across all sub-dimensions. The lowest mean was observed for Communication and Social Competence (X̄ = 3.87), suggesting relatively lower perceived competency in interpersonal and social dimensions compared to the other sub-scales."
