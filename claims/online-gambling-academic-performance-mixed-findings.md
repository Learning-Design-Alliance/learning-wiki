## Evidence

### Allen Hannah K. 2025

Allen Hannah K., Knight Tonya, Hall Becky, Dennis Melissa. (2025). Links Between Gambling and Academic Performance Among Undergraduate College Students: A Scoping Review. Journal of Gambling Studies, 41(4), 1517-1531. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10899-025-10440-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis section on online gambling reports that online gamblers "had significantly lower GPAs than students who did not gamble online" in one study, while Harris et al. (2013) and Petry and Gonzalez-Ibanez (2015) found no significant differences. No effect sizes are printed.

> "While Petry & Weinstock (2007) found that students who gambled online had significantly lower GPAs than students who did not gamble online, two other studies found no significant associations."
