## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Knoop‐van Campen CAN 2018

Knoop‐van Campen CAN, Segers E, Verhoeven L. (2018). The modality and redundancy effects in multimedia learning in children with dyslexia. Dyslexia. 2018;24:140–155. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1585

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA on retention scores in the modality analysis of the experiment (N = 64 children); children could recall less information after a week compared with directly after the lessons, with printed effect size η2 p = .208. The transfer redundancy analysis showed a significant decrease over time, F(1, 60) = 4.76, p = .033, η2 p = .073.

> "Analysis of the retention knowledge with verbal and visual working memory as covariates showed a significant decrease in scores over time, F(1, 60) = 15.79, p < .001, η2 p = .208."
