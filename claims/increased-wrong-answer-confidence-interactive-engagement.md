`q2 i?` Taken together, all n-coding CGPS groups were statistically more confident of their wrong answers after instruction than the control group (p=0.016), and the high n-coding group alone also differed (p=0.042). [→ N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007](#n-lasry-mw-aulls-2007)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Confidence analyses across the quasi-experiment: combined n-coding groups vs control in wrong answer confidence after instruction (p=0.016); the high n-coding group alone vs control reached p=0.042.

> "Furthermore, taken together, all n-coding groups were statistically more confiden t of their wrong answers after instruction when compared to the control group (p=0.016)."

### N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test average confidence comparisons using two-tailed t-tests with Bonferroni correction: high and medium n-coding groups each differed from the control group (p=0.036 and p=0.043).

> "differences were found in post-test average confidence with the control group. Specifically, the high n-coding and medium n-coding groups differed in post-test average confidence from the control (p=0.036 and p=0.043 respectively)."
