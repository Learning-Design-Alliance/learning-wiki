Systematic review searching MEDLINE, SCOPUS, and ERIC in May 2016; 440 citations screened to 84 included articles. The review reports the outcome-type distribution verbatim: "Twenty-four articles described reaction outcomes, eleven described learning outcomes, and one described behavioral outcomes."

> "Eighty-four articles met inclusion criteria. A qualitative synthesis of the evidence was done using Kirkpatrick’s model for evaluating outcomes. Twenty-four articles described reaction outcomes, eleven described learning outcomes, and one described behavioral outcomes. None measured patient impact."
