### Natto Zuhair S 2026

Natto Zuhair S. (2026). Blended peer-led research curriculum with AI integration improves postgraduate students’ academic performance and satisfaction: a quasi-experimental mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08576-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test of final course grades collected with identical rubrics across the two sections. The intervention mean was higher but "the difference was not statistically significant"; no effect size was printed. Grade distribution showed 53.8% A grades in intervention vs 38.5% in control.

> "Total academic performance was higher in the intervention group (M = 92.21, SD = 18.50) than the control group (M = 85.50, SD = 22.30), but the difference was not statistically significant, t(48.35) = 1.18, p = 0.24, 95% CI [− 4.71, 18.13]."
