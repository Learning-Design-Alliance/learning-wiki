RMC Research Corporation 2015¶

RMC Research Corporation. (2015). Underuse of Title VII funding for Indian education in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah. Phoenix, AZ: West Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559751

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview findings from representatives of eligible-but-unfunded LEAs. The report states five respondents "thought (incorrectly) that they could not apply because they already received Impact Aid or Johnson-O'Malley funding," demonstrating some eligible LEAs were misinformed.
