## Evidence

### Ginzburg Denise M. 2012

Ginzburg Denise M., Bohn Christiane, Höfling Volkmar, Weck Florian, Clark David M., Stangier Ulrich. (2012). Treatment specific competence predicts outcome in cognitive therapy for social anxiety disorder. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2012.09.001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory item analysis correlating the 16 CTCS-SP items with CGI, with significance adjusted by Bonferroni correction. Table 1 shows significant items including pacing and efficient use of time (−.65), resource activation (−.61), and selection of appropriate strategies (−.59).

> "Of the 6 individual competence items that significantly predict outcome, four are specific to the CT for social anxiety disorder competence scale (e.g. "focus on social-phobia-related cognitions, self-focused attention and imagery"; "selection of appropriate strategies for change in social-phobia-related cognition and maintaining factors"; "appropriate implementation of techniques for change in social-phobia-related cognition and maintaining factors" and "resource activation"), and two ("interpersonal effectiveness" & "pacing and efficient use of time") are more general CT items whose phrasing was modified"
