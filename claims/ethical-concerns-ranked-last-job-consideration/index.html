Descriptive ranking analysis of survey responses from 4th-year undergrads and recent graduates in Canada and the US, reporting average ranks of six job considerations. The article reports "'Ethical Concerns' ranked last (with an average rank of 5.01)"; no inferential test accompanies this overall ranking.

"Across all applicants, we observed that 'Remuneration' ranked the highest (with an average rank of 2.31 out of 7) and 'Ethical Concerns' ranked last (with an average rank of 5.01)."
