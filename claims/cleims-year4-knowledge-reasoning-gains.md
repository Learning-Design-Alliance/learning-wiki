## Evidence

### Rogers Gary D 2014

Rogers Gary D, McConnell Harry W, Jones de Rooy Nicole, Ellem Fiona, Lombard Marise. (2014). A randomised controlled trial of extended immersion in multi-method continuing simulation to prepare senior medical students for practice as junior doctors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-90

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End of Year 4 week assessments; Table 3 reports MCQ4 15.0 (60%) vs 13.3 (53%), P < 0.01, and SCQ4 18.5 (62%) vs 17.3 (58%), P = 0.02, 43 vs 35 participants.

> "participants in the Intervention arm demonstrated improved acquisition of new knowledge and understanding, as evidenced by significantly higher mean scores on both multiple choice and script concordance questions related to material covered in the seminars and workshops that both groups had attended during the week."
