Ansarian et al. 2016¶

Loghman Ansarian, Ali Akbar Adlipour, Mehrnoush Akhavan Saber, Elmira Shafiei. (2016). The Impact of Problem-Based Learning on Iranian EFL Learners' Speaking Proficiency. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.3p.84

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-samples t-test confirming pretest homogeneity of the two 24-participant groups selected within 1 SD of the mean on a second IELTS speaking test; t(46)=-.24, p=.81. Equivalence was not framed as tested beyond this check.

"There was no significant difference in scores for experimental (M= 15.75, SD= 1.15) and control group [M=15.66, SD= 1.23; t (46) = -.24, p=.81> .05], that is, both groups were homogeneous."
