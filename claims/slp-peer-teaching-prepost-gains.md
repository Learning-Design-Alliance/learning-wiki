## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post short-answer content assessment of the 2012 cohort (MS undergrads n = 19, HS undergrads n = 38), administered about 4 wk apart. The article reports the posttest gains: "HS undergraduates scored significantly higher on all five questions, compared with the pretest" (p < 0.05).

> "On the posttest (Figure 1B), HS undergraduates scored significantly higher on all five questions, compared with the pretest, as determined by an exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p < 0.05)."

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014 (2)

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Redesigned 2013 pre/posttest (all undergraduates n = 52) with two-part questions testing lower and middle Bloom's levels. The article reports "a significant increase in the posttest score compared with the pretest score" for all questions except 1B (p < 0.05).

> "For all questions except 1B, there was a significant increase in the posttest score compared with the pretest score, as determined by an exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p < 0.05)."
