## Evidence

### Mash R 2017

Mash R, Pather M, Rhode H, Fairall L. (2017). Evaluating the effect of the Practical Approach to Care Kit on teaching medical students primary care: Quasi-experimental study. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v9i1.1602

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative finding from focus group analysis: supervisors' non-use reinforced the idea that competence means not needing a tool. One student praised a site coordinator who "would take it out of every single consultation just to check the contraindications".

> "It appeared that few nurses or doctors explicitly used the tool in their own consultations."
