Evidence¶

Brahm Fleisch 2011¶

Brahm Fleisch, Nick Taylor, Roelien Herholdt and Ingrid Sapire. (2011). Evaluation of Back to Basics mathematics workbooks: a randomised control trial of the Primary Mathematics Research Project. South African Journal of Education, Vol 31:488-504. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136612

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Coverage analysis of the RCT tracked through three classroom visits; Table 7 reports the >79% coverage gain difference between PM and CM groups as significant (p value of .00). No effect size printed.

"Also, those CM learners who covered 79% or more achieved higher gain scores (12 percentage points) than their PM counterparts (8.85)."

Brahm Fleisch 2011 (2)¶

Brahm Fleisch, Nick Taylor, Roelien Herholdt and Ingrid Sapire. (2011). Evaluation of Back to Basics mathematics workbooks: a randomised control trial of the Primary Mathematics Research Project. South African Journal of Education, Vol 31:488-504. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136612

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same coverage analysis, <79% category: Table 7 reports the gain difference between the PM and CM groups as "not significant (p value of 0.06)". No effect size printed.

"On the other hand, those PM learners who covered less that 79% of the material showed higher gains (7.72) than the equivalent fraction of CM learners (6.61)."
