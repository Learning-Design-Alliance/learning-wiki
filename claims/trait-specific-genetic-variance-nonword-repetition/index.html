Multivariate genome-wide covariance analyses of literacy, language and working memory skills reveal distinct etiologies. npj Science of Learning 6:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00101-y

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Same multi-domain GSEM (passage reading subset, N = 6453): the trait-specific genetic factor for non-word repetition explained "12% (SE = 0.05)" of phenotypic variance, i.e. 47% (SE = 0.14) of SNP-h2.

"These factors explained 17% (SE = 0.06) and 12% (SE = 0.05) of the phenotypic variance respectively (Supplementary Table 13 and Fig. 2b), corresponding to 56% (SE = 0.14) and 47% (SE = 0.14) of the SNP-h2"
