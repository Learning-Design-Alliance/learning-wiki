Chapman, J. Crosby and Dale, A. Barbara. (1921). A Further Criterion for the Selection of Mental Test Elements. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=A+Further+Criterion+for+the+Selection+of+Mental+Test+Elements

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

From the matched-groups finding that total-score-equal groups score differently across the five subtests, the authors conclude "according to the type of test used", intellectual development is not a simultaneous and uniform improvement in all mental functions.

> "The Old Dull subject of mental age x is the superior, equal, or inferior of the Young Bright of mental age x, mental age being measured by totals, according to the type of test used."
