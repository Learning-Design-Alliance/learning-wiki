Evidence¶

Rim Mekonnen Hadgu 2016¶

Rim Mekonnen Hadgu, Sophia Hoang-Vy Huynh, & Chaya Gopalan. (2016). The Use of Pre-recorded Lectures on Student Performance in Physiology. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v5n1p105

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Comparison of comprehension-question performance between students who attended the live lectures and students who watched the pre-recorded lectures, reported as significant at P<0.05; the results figures report the LL group at 78% correct versus 51% for the PRL+ group. No effect size is printed.

"However, students in the LL group performed significantly better on CQ compared to the PRL+ group (P<0.05)."
