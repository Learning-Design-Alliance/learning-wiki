## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The chapter conjectures that inquiry environments are challenging to scale because they are Ambitious Learning Activity Systems: ambitious relative to existing practice, dependent on active learner engagement that cannot be distributed as resources alone, and requiring coherent change across many system factors. `q1 · i?` · `review · r3`

Theoretical argument in the chapter's Section 3, offered as a conjecture building on prior work. The authors argue inquiry involves new teacher and student roles, active engagement that cannot be scaled by distributing resources, and system-level coherence needs. No empirical test is reported for this framing itself.
