Gentges Joshua, Pascoe Michael, Al-Redouan Azzat, Sumner Caroline BS, Case Sami L PhD, Franklin Samuel PhD, Platt Kristen PhD. (2026). Interactive, Image-Based Modules as a Complement to Prosection-Based Anatomy Laboratories: Multicohort Evaluation. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85028

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort-level comparability analysis of incoming academic measures for the 2023 (N=206) and 2024 (N=205) MD cohorts using Shapiro-Wilk and Mann-Whitney U tests; the measures were "not significantly different" between cohorts.

> "For all 3 measures we used to test comparability (MCAT scores, uGPA, and sGPA), the data were not normally distributed (Shapiro-Wilk test, P<.001) and not significantly different (Mann-Whitney U, P<.001) between the 2 MD cohorts."
