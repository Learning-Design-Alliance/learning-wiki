## Evidence

### Babette Bühler 2024

Babette Bühler, Efe Bozkir, Hannah Deininger, Peter Gerjets, Ulrich Trautwein, and Enkelejda Kasneci. 2024. On Task and in Sync: Examining the Relationship between Gaze Synchrony and Self-Reported Attention During Video Lecture Learning. Proceedings of ACM Symposium on Eye Tracking Research & Applications (ETRA '24). [doi:10.1145/3655604](https://doi.org/10.1145/3655604)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on post-test scores (0-14 points, M = 5.63, sd = 2.663) for 84 participants, controlling for pre-test scores. The "significant positive relationship" between on-task report share and post-test scores held after adjusting for prior knowledge (Attentive Share Estimate = 0.05, p < 0.001).

> "Our findings indicate a significant positive relationship between the overall proportion of time participants reported being on task and their post-test scores, even after adjusting for their prior knowledge of the session topic."
