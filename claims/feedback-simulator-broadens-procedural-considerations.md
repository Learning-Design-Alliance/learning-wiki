### Duan D  2025

Duan D , Gan MJS , Lim LZ , Foong KWC . (2025). Simulator practice of temporary anchorage device (TAD) insertion on inter‐radicular sites improves learning and confidence in dental residency. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized two-round simulator study with 9 intervention and 9 control residents. The article reports that "the intervention group showed changes across all three phases" while the control group adjusted only intraoperative themes.

> "Comparing the first and second rounds of placement, improvements were observed in procedural considerations (Figure 3). The control group showed adjustments to the intraoperative themes, but the intervention group showed changes across all three phases."
