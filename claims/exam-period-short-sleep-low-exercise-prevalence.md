Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey of 403 medical students at five Palestinian universities show "89.3% of students slept less than 7 h/day during midterm and finals periods, and only 19.1% reported exercising". These are descriptive percentages with no test statistics.

> "Sleeping and physical activity showed evidence of high academic pressure, as 89.3% of students slept less than 7 h/day during midterm and finals periods, and only 19.1% reported exercising"
