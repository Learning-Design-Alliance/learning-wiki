V. A. C. Henmon 1917¶

V. A. C. Henmon. (1917). The Measurement of Ability in Latin. Part I. Vocabulary. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22The+Measurement+of+Ability+in+Latin%22+Henmon

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Validity analysis correlating test scores with teachers' marked final grades for eight schools (Table II), with individual correlations ranging from .26 to .90. The article reports "a tolerably close agreement between scores and teachers estimates."

"The correlations, except in two instances, show a tolerably close agreement between scores and teachers estimates and indicate the usefulness of the tests in rating pupils."
