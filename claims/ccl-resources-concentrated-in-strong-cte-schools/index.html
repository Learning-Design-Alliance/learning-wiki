The Landscape of Career Readiness Opportunities in Chicago Public Schools: Strengthening Career and Technical Education and Establishing Career-Connected Learning for All. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Finding from the report's document reviews and interviews with district leaders about CCL strategy. The report describes the concentration rationale as stated by district leaders; no outcome evaluation is reported in this report.

"District leaders have concentrated CCL resources in a subset of schools that oﬀer numerous CTE programs of study, aiming to optimize investments in CTE program quality and student supports by directing them to schools with strong and established CTE commitments."
