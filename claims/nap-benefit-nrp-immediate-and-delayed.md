## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The learning condition × nap status interaction was significant (ηp2 = 0.18), with the nap group outperforming the wake group only under no-feedback retrieval practice in both immediate and delayed tests. [→ Jing Zhang 2025](#jing-zhang-2025)

## Evidence

### Jing Zhang 2025

Jing Zhang, Xiaoning Huo, Hongbo Lv, Jiahua Xu & Xiaofeng Ma. (2025). Offline consolidation mechanisms of the retrieval practice effect: an analysis based on EEG signal characteristics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00349-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA on recall accuracy with learning condition and test timing within-subjects and nap status between-subjects; ηp2 = 0.18 for the interaction, with nap exceeding wake (p < 0.05 immediate; p < 0.001 delayed) only for no-feedback retrieval practice.

> "The interaction between learning condition and nap status was signiﬁcant [F(2,92) = 10.00, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.18]. Further simple effect analyses revealed that in the immediate test condition, only under the retrieval practice without feedback condition did the nap group signiﬁcantly outperform the wake group (p < 0.05)"
