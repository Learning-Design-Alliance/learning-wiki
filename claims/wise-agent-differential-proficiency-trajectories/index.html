Longitudinal comparative analysis of three proficiency cohorts stratified by pre-test scores, combining quantitative tracking and thematic analysis. The article reports the printed percentage gains and describes asymmetric adaptation: structured writing scaffolds for low-level students versus counter-questioning heuristic strategies for high-level students.
