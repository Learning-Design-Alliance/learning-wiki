Evidence¶

Akkuzu 2016¶

Akkuzu, N. & Uyulgan, M.A. (2016). How to Improve Students' Comprehension concerning the Major Terms of Functional Groups?--In the Experiment of OrCheTaboo Game. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n2p196

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Semi-structured interviews with each experimental group student after the experiments and game, five open-ended questions, 20-25 minutes each, subjected to content analysis with a researcher agreement rate of .94. This is student self-report, not an achievement measure.
