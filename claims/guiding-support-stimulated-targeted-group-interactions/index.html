Evidence¶

Ishari Amarasinghe; Davinia Hernández-Leo; H. Ulrich Hoppe 2021¶

Ishari Amarasinghe; Davinia Hernández-Leo; H. Ulrich Hoppe (2021). Deconstructing orchestration load: comparing teacher support through mirroring and guiding. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09351-9

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Log data from guiding-condition sessions showed 14 predefined messages posted to groups versus 4 in mirroring; Table 4 shows 14 posting interventions in guiding vs 4 in mirroring and 10 increase-time actions (9 alert-based) vs 5. One teacher reported alerts "made me aware that some groups require more time for voting and discussion".

"we interpret the lack of criticism and surveying in the guiding condition as the result of teachers engaging in direct communication with problematic groups by posting messages."
