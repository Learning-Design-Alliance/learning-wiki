`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports that Guerrier et al.'s study of anaesthesia residents performing an anaesthetic bloc, recorded twice with a 7-day gap, is the only included study with Kirkpatrick level 4 outcomes; "All of these differences were significant."

> "The video review group engaged in self-reflection and feedback, and showed a greater increase in “akinesia score”, greater decrease in duration of procedure and lesser need for supplemental injection on day five compared to the non-video review group. All of these differences were significant."
