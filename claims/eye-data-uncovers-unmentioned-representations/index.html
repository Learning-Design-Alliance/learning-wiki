Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Students allocated roughly equal attention to the graph and the molecular model, both above chance, while attending little to the equations — related
  • Eye fixation measures of attention to representations were highly correlated with verbal mentions (correlations from .63 to .96), supporting both as measures of representation use — related
  • In verbal protocols, references concentrated on the model (44.4%) and graph (39.4%) with few to the equation (16.1%), and students referenced multiple representations on 42.6% of questions — related
  • Eye transition data showed students made saccades between representations, indicating attempts to relate and integrate information rather than relying only on the model — related
  • Frequent eye transitions between graph and problem statement were marginally associated with correct answers
