## Evidence

### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This conclusion synthesizes the study's quasi-experimental, retrospective, cross-sectional findings across 1,032 children in a large urban school district in the Southwest, comparing HIPPY and matched comparison children on attendance, retention, discipline referrals, and TAKS scores in 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th grades.

> "Results indicate that children who participated in the HIPPY program as a 3-, 4-, or 5-year-old appear to have benefited long-term from the experience."
