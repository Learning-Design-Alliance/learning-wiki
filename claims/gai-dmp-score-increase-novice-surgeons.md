## Evidence

### Hasegawa Takumi 2026

Hasegawa Takumi, Etoh Tsuyoshi, Nakano Tomoki, Aoyama Yoshimasa, Matsunobu Yusuke, Fujishima Reo, Fujita Shunsuke, Shitomi Yuki, Akagi Tomonori, Ninomiya Shigeo, Ueda Yoshitake, Tokuyasu Tatsushi, Inomata Masafumi. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-13043-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory Wilcoxon signed-rank analyses restricted to pairs with measured score changes (JT suprapancreatic 11/48 pairs; ST infrapyloric 20/96 pairs); effect sizes "r = 0.89" and "r = 0.54". The JT infrapyloric trend was not significant (p = 0.15, r = 0.32).

> "In the infrapyloric area, median scores significantly increased from 3 (IQR 1–3) to 4 (IQR 3–5) in the ST group (p = 0.03, r = 0.54) and showed a trend toward an increase from 2 (IQR 1–3) to 3 (IQR 2–4) in the JT group (p = 0.15, r = 0.32). In the suprapancreatic area, median scores increased significantly from 1 (IQR 1–2) to 3 (IQR 3–5) in the JT group (p < 0.01, r = 0.89)."
