Interview-based study of five students' willingness to communicate (WTC) in English, using the MacIntyre, Dörnyei, Clément & Noels (1998) construct, with 30 interviews total. The results section reports "increasing their willingness to communicate" as a gameplay benefit. No effect size is reported.
