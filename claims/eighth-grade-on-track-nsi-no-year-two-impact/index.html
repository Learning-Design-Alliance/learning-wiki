Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Matched comparison analysis in Research Question 3 comparing students in 8th-grade on-track NSI schools with students in similar within-district comparison schools on academic and behavioral outcomes; no statistically significant differences were found and no effect size is printed.

"Although there were small differences between NSI schools and comparison schools for the 8th-grade on-track NSI, these differences were not statistically significant."

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024 (2)¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The report's year-1 versus year-2 analysis finds 8th-grade on-track NSI had positive first-year impacts on GPA, math and ELA course pass rates, and attendance that faded to non-significance by year 2; the report suggests pandemic disruption as a possible factor.

"These impacts were comparable in size to the impacts of the 9th-grade on-track NSI but they did not persist over time; the impacts after two years of participation were close to zero and not statistically significant for any outcome (as noted earlier)."
