## Evidence

### Feller et al. 2014

Avi Feller, Todd Grindal, Luke Miratrix, Lindsay C. Page. (2014). Compared to what? Estimating Causal Effects for Latent Subgroups to Understand Variation in the Impacts of Head Start by Alternate Child Care Setting. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562722

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model-based re-analysis of HSIS data, with results shown in Figures 1 and 2. The article reports Head Start has "a larger effect on key cognitive outcomes relative to non -center care than to center care," with non-zero effects only for Non-Center Compliers.

> "for almost all models explored, the effect for Non -Center Compliers stochastically dominates effects for Center Compliers; the only non -zero effects we find are for the Non -Center Complier group."
