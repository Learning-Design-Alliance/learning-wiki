## Evidence

### O'Dwyer M 2022

O'Dwyer M, Fleming CA, Ahern S, Barrett S, Raftery NB, Ní Dhonnchú T, Doddakula K. (2022). Objective improvement with coronary anastomosis simulation training: meta-analysis. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrab147

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of seven studies (14 groups) comparing five-point-scale skill scores before versus after simulation. The pooled standardized improvement was "1.68 points" (95 per cent c.i. 1.23 to 2.13; P < 0.001), with I2 = 77 per cent and individual effect sizes from 0.51 to 3.14 points.

> "Trainees who used simulation had an associated improvement of 1.68 points (95 per cent c.i. 1.23 to 2.13; P < 0.001) (Fig. 4)."
