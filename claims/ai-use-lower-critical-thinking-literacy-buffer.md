The article reports, citing Kulal (2025), that AI use is associated with lower critical thinking, moderated by trust and buffered by AI literacy. No effect sizes are printed; the finding is reported second‐hand as background for educational recommendations.

> "AI use is associated with lower levels of critical thinking, an effect that becomes stronger when users place greater trust in AI; however, strong AI literacy substantially weakens this harmful influence of trust (Kulal 2025)."
