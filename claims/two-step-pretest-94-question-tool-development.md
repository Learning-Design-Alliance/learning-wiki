## Evidence

### Kim Sang Chul 2017

Kim Sang Chul, Ro Young Sun, Shin Sang Do, Wi Dae Han, Jeong Joongsik, Park Ju Ok, Sun Kyong Min, Bae Kwangsoo. (2017). Assessment of Competence in Emergency Medicine among Healthcare Professionals in Cameroon. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2017.32.12.1931

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-step pretest: an expert consensus survey with 7 Korean emergency physicians reduced 113 questions to 94, then junior (n = 7) and senior (n = 5) Korean emergency residents completed the survey. The article reports "All 94 questions in the 23 domains showed high discrimination power" using an AUC > 0.7 criterion.

> "All 94 questions in the 23 domains showed high discrimination power, and as a result, were selected for the competence assessment tool for emergency healthcare professionals in Yaoundé"
