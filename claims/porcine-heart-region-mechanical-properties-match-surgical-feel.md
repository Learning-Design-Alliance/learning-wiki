## Evidence

### Yamada Toshiyuki 2017

Yamada Toshiyuki, Osako Motohiko, Uchimuro Tomoya, Yoon Ryogen, Morikawa Toshiaki, Sugimoto Maki, Suda Hisao, Shimizu Hideyuki. (2017). Three-Dimensional Printing of Life-Like Models for Simulation and Training of Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery. Innovations (Philadelphia, Pa.). https://doi.org/10.1097/IMI.0000000000000423

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mechanical measurement of elastic modulus and breaking strength of left ventricle, papillary muscle, mitral annulus, fibrous trigone, mitral leaflet, and left atrium of three parts of three porcine hearts; the authors judged the regional differences "similar to those in the feeling experienced in an actual operation."

> "The breaking strength, with tissue location and texture, was the strongest in the fibrous trigone, and the papillary muscle was brittle and delicate. These differences in physical properties were similar to those in the feeling experienced in an actual operation."
