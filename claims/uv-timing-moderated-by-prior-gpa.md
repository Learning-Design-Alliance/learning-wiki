# Timing of a single UV essay is moderated by prior performance: early UV essays help low performers, late UV essays help high performers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a 3-cell subset analysis, the timing dummy by prior GPA interaction on course grade was significant (t(204) = 2.15, β = -.17, p = .033): low-GPA students earned higher grades with a first-unit UV essay, high-GPA students with a third-unit UV essay. [→ Canning 2018](#canning-2018)

## Evidence

### Canning 2018

Canning, E. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Hecht, C. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2018). Improving Performance and Retention in Introductory Biology with a Utility-Value Intervention. Journal of educational psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000244

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory supplementary analysis of a 3-cell subset (UV first, second, or third with two control essays), using dummy-coded regressions with prior GPA interactions and covariates. The article reports "t(204) = 2.15, β = -.17, p = .033" for the timing × prior GPA interaction on course grade — a small effect.

> "This shows that students with low prior GPAs earned higher grades in the course when they wrote about UV in the first unit (Ŷ = 2.68), compared to writing a UV essay in the third unit (Ŷ = 2.49). The opposite was true for students with high prior GPAs, in that writing a UV essay in the third unit was more beneficial (Ŷ = 3.51) than writing a UV essay in the first unit (Ŷ = 3.33)."
