### Murat Oztok 2012

Murat Oztok, Daniel Zingaro, Clare Brett, Jim Hewitt. (2012). Exploring Asynchronous and Synchronous Tool use in Online Courses. Preprint submitted to Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.08.007

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational log analysis across all 222 students in the nine dual-mode courses, relating messages sent to total time online. The quote reports "r = 0.61,p = 0", supporting the interpretation that active synchronous students were more active overall.
