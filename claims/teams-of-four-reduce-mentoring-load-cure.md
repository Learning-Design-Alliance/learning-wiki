Baxter AL, Lang RF, Zywicki C, Gardner SM, Kopec A, Jung A. (2023). A scalable approach to undergraduate research in physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.05384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.05384)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructors' experience-based report from the course design section describing team assignment. The authors state teams are assigned "to create accountability for students and reduce the mentoring load", noting the size was chosen from experience and supported by literature; odd-sized groups of three and five were tried and suffered from imbalanced work. No effect size is reported.
