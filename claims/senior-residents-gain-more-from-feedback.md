## Evidence

### Tee Yu-San 2024

Tee Yu-San, Cheng Chi-Tung, Li Pei-Hua, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Liao Chien-Hung, Fu Chih-Yuan. (2024). Optimizing modern surgical simulation through instructor feedback – insights from a retrospective observational study in a tertiary hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06090-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prefeedback–postfeedback paired comparison of median LapSim scores from the retrospective training program; juniors (n = 17) performed pattern cutting, seniors (n = 15) performed laparoscopic appendectomy. The article prints only descriptive medians and IQRs; the Wilcoxon signed rank test result is not printed for this contrast, so no effect size is reported.

> "The junior group exhibited a modest improvement of less than 10% in terms of the median LapSim score, which increased from 90.0 to 97.0. In contrast, the senior group exhibited an improvement of nearly 50% in their median LapSim score, which increased from 52.0 (IQR 42.5, 70.5) to 77.0 (IQR 57.0, 83.5)."
