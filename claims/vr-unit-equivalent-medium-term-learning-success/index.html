Evidence¶

Thomas Keller 2019¶

Thomas Keller, Fabian Hagen and Elke Brucker-Kley. (2019). A FIELD STUDY ABOUT THE IMPACT OF A VR LEARNING UNIT. 16th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2019). https://doi.org/10.33965/celda2019_201911L038

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Field experiment, Test 1 to Test 3 comparison one month after the unit. Experimental group averaged 0.64 points (8.88%) and control group 0.45 points (6.40%) medium-term learning success; the difference was not statistically significant.

"Similar observations can be made for the average learning outcomes between Test 1 an d Test 3. There it is a difference of 0.19 points with a statistical significance of 64.71%."
