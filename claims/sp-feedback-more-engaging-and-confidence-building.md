### Steenstra Ivan 2026

Steenstra Ivan, Flanagan Octavia, Benni Jyoti M, Schreckenbach Teresa, Gayan Mondeep MS, Shrivastava Sandeep MS, Sonowal Kiran MS, Gogoi Amilee MD, Goswami Hrishikesh MS, Pathak Prasenjit MS, Kumar Bhawesh MS. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student feedback collected on a peer-validated 5-point Likert Google form regarding appeal, effectiveness, and outcome of the two methods; the article states no descriptive statistical analysis of the feedback was performed, so ratings are reported descriptively only.

> "Overall, students in the SP group rated their learning experience marginally more favorably, finding simulation with an SP to be a more engaging and effective method. Students in the SP group also reported marginally greater satisfaction and confidence in their ability to apply the learned skills in a primary care setting when required (Figure 7)."
