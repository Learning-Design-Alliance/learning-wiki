Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Perceived irrelevance and mastery vs. performance achievement goals: Two mindset variables within attitudinal experiences of life science majors in introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Item-cluster analysis of the CLASS survey comparing relevance groups; the author interprets the strong Personal Interest effect as suggesting perceived irrelevance "may be strongly linked to apathy towards physics" and reduced real-world connections.

> "Only a few item clusters sho wed statistical significance for post-tests and normalized gains, namely Personal Interest, Real-World Connections, and Conceptual Understanding; the difference in normalized gains is moderate in all three cases (0.3 < d < 0.4), but the Personal Interest c luster shows a large effect size on post-test scores (d = 0.53)."
