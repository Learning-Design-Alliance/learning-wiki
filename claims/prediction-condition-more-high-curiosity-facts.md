## Evidence

### Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser 2019

Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser. (2019). Lighting the wick in the candle of learning: generating a prediction stimulates curiosity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0056-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered within-subjects experiment with 29 university students learning numerical trivia facts; paired one-tailed t test comparing percentage of facts categorized as high curiosity across conditions. The study reports "d = 0.330" and absolute curiosity ratings were also significantly higher in the prediction condition (d = 0.411).

> "more facts received high-curiosity ratings in the prediction condition ( M = 55.55, SD = 14.36) than in the example condition ( M = 46.43 and SD = 15.96), t(28) = 1.78, p = 0.043, and d = 0.330"
