# ITSS significantly improves Grade 7 reading comprehension in rural and suburban schools (effect size +0.18)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a randomized study of 2,489 seventh-grade students in 108 classrooms across 25 rural and suburban schools, students using ITSS scored significantly higher on the Gray Silent Reading Test with an effect size of +0.18. [→ Evidence for ESSA 2021](#evidence-for-essa-2021)

## Evidence

### Evidence for ESSA 2021

Evidence for ESSA. (2021). Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS): Elementary Reading and Secondary Reading evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/intelligent-tutoring-for-the-structure-strategy-itss-elementary/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized study in which "Teachers and their classrooms were randomly assigned within schools to use ITSS or continue with regular language arts instruction." After 6 to 7 months, "students using ITSS scored significantly higher on the Gray Silent Reading Test, with an effect size = +0.18."

> "A third randomized study included 2,489 seventh-grade students in 108 classrooms across 25 rural and suburban schools."
