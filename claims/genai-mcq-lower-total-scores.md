# Participants scored significantly lower on human-edited GenAI-assisted MCQs than on educator-crafted MCQs, indicating GenAI-assisted items were not easier

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Postgraduate FM trainees scored lower on GenAI-assisted than educator-crafted items (mean 19.12 vs 21.10 out of 30; Cohen d=0.62). [→ Sze Kai Ping 2026](#sze-kai-ping-2026)

## Evidence

### Sze Kai Ping 2026

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Blinded within-participant psychometric evaluation in Singapore: 73 postgraduate FM doctors each answered both item sets, 30 items per origin. This participant-level paired comparison found a significant mean difference of −1.97 (95% CI −2.72 to −1.23; P<.001) with "a moderate effect size (Cohen d=0.62)".

> "Participants scored significantly lower on GenAI-assisted items (mean 19.12, SD 2.83) than on educator-crafted items (mean 21.10, SD 3.42), with a mean paired difference of −1.97 (95% CI −2.72 to −1.23; P<.001) and a moderate effect size (Cohen d=0.62)"
