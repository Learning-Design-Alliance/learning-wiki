### Josephine Meyer 2022

Josephine Meyer, Noah Finkelstein, and Bethany Wilcox. (2022). Ethics education in the quantum information science classroom: Exploring attitudes, barriers, and opportunities. Submitted to the 2022 ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.01849](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.01849)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of 6 faculty who taught introductory QIS courses at US institutions, summer 2021, analyzed with a hybrid coding scheme classifying barriers as personal or structural. The authors report that faculty "perceive signiﬁcant barriers to the incorporation of ethic s and social issues in the QIS classroom."

> "Consistent with prior work in the engineering ethics litera ture (e.g. [54]), we ﬁnd that faculty perceive signiﬁcant barriers to the incorporation of ethic s and social issues in the QIS classroom."
