## Evidence

### Nishiyama Masahiro 2023

Nishiyama Masahiro, Kyono Yuki, Yamaguchi Hiroshi, Kawamura Aoi, Oikawa Shizuka, Tokumoto Shoichi, Tomioka Kazumi, Nozu Kandai, Nagase Hiroaki. (2023). Association of early bedtime at 3 years of age with higher academic performance and better non-cognitive skills in elementary school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective population-based cohort analysis of 4395 children linking municipal health-check records at age 3 to first-grade tests. Adjusted regression (n = 3268) found bedtime at 22:00 (p = 0.034) and ≥23:00 (p < 0.001) associated with lower scores, "2.9 points lower for bedtime at 22:00" and 8.65 points lower at ≥23:00 relative to 18:00–20:00.

> "The scores were 2.9 points lower for bedtime at 22:00 and 8.65 points lower for bedtime at 23:00 than for bedtime at 18:00–20:00. Multiple regression analysis adjusted for the above covariates revealed that bedtime was associated with academic performance in the national language test."
