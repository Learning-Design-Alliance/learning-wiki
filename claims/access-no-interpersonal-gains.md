## Evidence

### Eddy et al. 2021

Laura D. Eddy, Arthur D. Anastopoulos, Melissa R. Dvorsky, Paul J. Silvia, Jeffrey D. Labban & Joshua M. Langberg (2021). An RCT of a CBT Intervention for Emerging Adults with ADHD Attending College: Functional Outcomes. Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2020.1867989

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Latent growth curve analysis of AIM-A Relationships and Communication in the RCT found no significant between-condition difference ("Wald (1) = .71, p = .398"); equivalence was not tested.

> "The immediate ACCESS (b = 5.43, SE = 2.57, p = .035) condition significantly improved in interpersonal relationships over time, but the DTC (b = 2.51, SE = 1.83, p = .169) condition did not. Nevertheless, the rate of change did not differ significantly between the groups, Wald (1) = .71, p = .398, d = .12 [-.13, .37]."
