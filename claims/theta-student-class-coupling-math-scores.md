## Evidence

### Jingjing Chen 2023

Jingjing Chen, Penghao Qian, Xinqiao Gao, Baosong Li, Yu Zhang and Dan Zhang. (2023). Inter-brain coupling reflects disciplinary differences in real-world classroom learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00162-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of one semester of classroom EEG recordings from 34 grade-10 students during Math sessions. Theta-band student-class coupling correlated with Math final exam scores: "r = 0.339, Pearson's p = 0.0498" (uncorrected); permutation p = 0.024 against 5000 shuffled versions.

> "The students with better learning outcomes in Math were found to have stronger inter-brain couplings to other classmates (r = 0.339, Pearson's p = 0.0498, uncorrected for multiple compar- isons, same below, n = 34)."
