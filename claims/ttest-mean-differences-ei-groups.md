Independent t-tests (Table 3) comparing mean socio-demographic variables between EI groups in the 560-respondent census; e.g. mean Post Jamb Score 62.9 (below-average EI) vs 53.6 (above-average EI). Age, father's children and parents' ages were not significant.

> "Respondents with above-average EI had lower average monthly allowances, fewer children from their mothers, and lower Post UTME scores compared to those with below-average EI. These associations were statistically significant (T = 2.220, p = 0.027; T = 2.764, p = 0.006; T = 4.817, p = 0.001, respectively)."
