### Loretan 2024

Loretan, C., Delaval, M., Müller, A., Rocha, S., & Weiss, L. (2024). Understanding of Size and Scale and Order-of-Magnitude Reasoning in Secondary Science: A Teaching Experiment with Worked Examples as Educational Scaffold. Université de Genève. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.16480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.16480)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The article's review of worked-example research reports meta-analytic values of "Cohen d = 0.57" for learning in general and "d = 0.7" for physics; the underlying meta-analyses are attributed to the cited sources, not conducted in this article.

> "Meta-analysis yields a value of Cohen d = 0.57 for learning in general (across fields), and d = 0.7 for physics (Crissman, 2006)"

### Loretan 2024 (2)

Loretan, C., Delaval, M., Müller, A., Rocha, S., & Weiss, L. (2024). Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.16480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.16480)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports, citing Wittwer & Renkl (2010), that meta-analytic effects of worked examples on transfer were "not significantly different from zero on either near or far transfer"; the meta-analysis itself is not reported in this article.

> "In particular, a meta-analytic review found that these effects were not significantly different from zero on either near or far transfer (Wittwer & Renkl, 2010)."
