## Evidence

### Chungsoo Na 2024

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis of OSLQ survey responses from 750 undergraduates in a 15-week online introductory mathematics course at a South Korean public university. The five-profile solution had entropy 0.88 and was selected over four- and six-profile models.

> "Latent profile analyses identified five distinct SRL profiles: minimal regulators, low regulators with limited social skills, low regulators, moderate regulators, and competent regulators."

### Chungsoo Na 2024 (2)

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA across the five retained profiles on the seven SRL sub-constructs, reported with Pillai's Trace: 1.43 and p < 0.001 in the Results. The authors state this "implies that the emerging five SRL profiles are qualitatively and quantitatively differentiated."

> "we conducted MANOVA with seven SRL sub-scales as a depend- ent variable and five profiles as an independent variable, Pillai's Trace: 1.43"
