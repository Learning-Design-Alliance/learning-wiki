`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' stated limitations of the multi-centered cross-sectional pre-post study: no non-VR comparison arm, sawbone-only accuracy assessment, small sample size, and participant heterogeneity.

> "The absence of a non-VR control arm prevents direct quantification of the simulator's incremental benefit over conventional training repetition alone. Secondly, although our VR cases are derived from imaging data of actual clinical cases, both evaluations of screw accuracy were conducted using sawbones, which may not fully reflect real-world clinical situations."
