### Sanjoy Mahajan 2005

Sanjoy Mahajan. (2005). Observations on teaching first-year physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Tutorial evidence from Problem 26: students' graphs of center-of-mass velocity v and scaled angular velocity rω looked like Figure 3, missing that the curves coincide after t0. A Stirling's-formula problem showed similar difficulty judging integral under- or overestimation from a graph.

> "Students did not realize that the rolling condition, v = rω, requires that the two curves coincide after t0."
