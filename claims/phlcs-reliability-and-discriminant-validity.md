### Chang C-C 2025

Chang C-C, Tseng P-L, Liu C-C, Ming J-L, Fan S-H, Tung C-Y. (2025). Development and validation of the Physician’s Health Literacy Competence Scale: A step towards effective doctor–patient communication. Medicine 104(9): e41643. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041643

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item analysis and internal consistency testing of the pilot scale. The article reports a Cronbach alpha of 0.944 and states all items passed high–low group discrimination. Subscale intercorrelations ranged from 0.42 to 0.71, with AVE square roots exceeding subscale correlations, supporting discriminant validity.

> "The Cronbach alpha reliability coefficient for the scale was 0.944, indicating excellent internal consistency and the ability to distinguish health literacy among medical students."
