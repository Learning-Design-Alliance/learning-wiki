# Central executive and focus of attention factors were highly correlated but statistically distinct, warranting separate factors in young children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The correlation between central executive and visuospatial/focus of attention factors was lower in this study (.75 Cowan, .76 Baddeley-Hitch) than the .91 and .81 reported in prior child studies, supporting separate factors. [→ Gray Shelley 2016](#gray-shelley-2016)

## Evidence

### Gray Shelley 2016

Gray Shelley, Green Samuel, Alt Mary, Hogan Tiffany P., Kuo Trudy, Brinkley Shara, Cowan Nelson. (2016). The Structure of Working Memory in Young Children and Its Relation to Intelligence. Journal of Memory and Language. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5157932/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of factor correlations with prior studies by Campos et al. and Michalczyk et al. In this study of 168 children "the correlation was lower in both the Cowan (.75) and Baddeley and Hitch (.76) models," supporting distinct factors.

> "In these studies the correlations between the central executive and visuospatial factors were .91 and .81 respectively, but in our study the correlation was lower in both the Cowan (.75) and Baddeley and Hitch (.76) models."
