### Seol 2013

Seol, K. O., & Jung, S. W. (2013). School Belonging as a Mediator of the Relationship Between Individual and Parental Factors and Academic Achievement of Elementary School Students. The Korean Journal of School Psychology, 10(1), 41-58. https://koreapsych.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Correlational analysis of survey and March 2012 diagnostic-assessment data from 250 fifth graders in one Seoul elementary school; Table 1 reports the belonging–grades correlation. The article states that "적과 정적상관을 보였다." between school belonging and elementary students' school grades.

> "예상대로 학교소속감은 초등학생의 학교성
적과 정적상관을 보였다. 이는 학교소속감과 높은 학업성적에 대한 많은 선행연구와 일치
하는 결과이다."
