Evidence¶

Yermahanova 2016¶

Yermahanova, A., Nurmakhambetova, D., Bozhig, Z., & Imanbetov, A. (2016). Evaluation of Features of Development of Sports Way of Swimming of Students of Various Sports Specialization. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(18), 10895-10904. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pedagogical experiment with control group taught by traditional swimming training and experimental group taught by the authors' differentiated practical-training program. The article reports "the experimental - 91.7%" improvement versus 49.8% in control, significant at P <0,05; no effect size is printed.

"In sports with the complex coordination of movement was improved in the control group - 49.8% in the experimental - 91.7%. All indicators were significant differences P <0,05."
