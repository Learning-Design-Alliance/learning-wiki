### Randall Armel 2025

Randall Armel, S. (2025). Small programs, big challenges: Reimagining the evaluation of clinical teaching in genetic counseling. Journal of Genetic Counseling, 34, e1924. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.1924

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports two meta-analyses (attributed to Cohen, 1980 and Menges & Brinko, 1986) in which "teaching effectiveness, as measured by student ratings, was improved by augmenting review of student evaluations with a teaching consultation"; a later meta-analysis (Penny & Coe, 2004) found a moderate positive effect.

> "In two studies using a meta‐analytic approach, teaching effectiveness, as measured by student ratings, was improved by augmenting review of student evaluations with a teaching consultation"
