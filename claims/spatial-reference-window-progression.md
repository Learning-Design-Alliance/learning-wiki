The Role of Central Conceptual Structures in the Development of Children's Thought. This is the Monograph's hypothesized spatial progression: a merged object-shape and object-location structure at 6 (referencing objects to a ground line), two sub-scenes at 8, and conjoint referencing of two orthogonal axes at 10, which the authors link to Cartesian coordinates and perspective. No effect size is printed.

> "Finally, by the age of 10, they appear capable of examining any set of objects in a spatial array and noting the position of those objects with regard to two orthogonal reference axes simultaneously."
