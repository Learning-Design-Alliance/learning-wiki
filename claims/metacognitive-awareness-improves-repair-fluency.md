## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experimental participants produced significantly more fluent task-based speech (lower repair-fluency scores) than control participants after a fifteen-session metacognitive awareness program. [→ Seifoori 2016](#seifoori-2016)

## Evidence

### Seifoori 2016

Seifoori, Z. (2016). Metacognitive awareness and the fluency of task-based oral output across planning conditions: The case of Iranian TEFL students. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 4(1), 11-26. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on post-test oral narrative data from 58 experimental and 56 control Iranian TEFL students. Lower scores indicate more fluent speech: "the experimental participants, with an averages of .36, outperformed the control participants" (control mean .60).

> "the experimental participants, with an averages of .36, outperformed the control participants, with an average of .60 and that the difference between the groups reached significance level t (112) = -2.62, p<.05"
