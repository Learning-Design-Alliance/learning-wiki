### Höbaus Clemens 2025

Höbaus Clemens, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2025). Individual and group reflection in lecture-based large groups lead to comparable learning success. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-025-02610-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental cross-over lecture study; mean ARS MCQ scores compared with Student's unpaired t-test were "similar for L1 (0.47 ± 0.27 vs. 0.49 ± 0.31, p = 0.867)" and also for L2 (p = 0.971).

> "The ARS results between IR and GR measured at the end of the lecture were similar for L1 (0.47 ± 0.27 vs. 0.49 ± 0.31, p = 0.867) as well as L2 (0.49 ± 0.17 vs. 0.49 ± 0.17, p = 0.971)"
