## Evidence

### Tripp Brie 2025

Tripp Brie, Ravi Akshaya, Pang Ethan, Furrow Robert E. (2025). Undergraduate STEM Students’ Perceptions of Grading Practices Reveal that Quiz Retakes Positively Impact Drivers of Self-determination. CBE—Life Sciences Education 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-06-0167

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semistructured interviews with 18 undergraduates (13 statistics, 5 neurobiology) who experienced in-class quiz retakes, analyzed via inductive and deductive content analysis aligned to SDT. "78%, n = 14" of participants linked retakes to perceived competence; students described relearning material and retaining more information.

> "Over three-quarters of student participants (78%, n = 14) reliably made connections between alternative grading practices and perceived competence (Table 3)."
