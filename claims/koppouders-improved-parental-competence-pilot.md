## Evidence

### van der Zanden Rianne AP 2010

van der Zanden Rianne AP, Speetjens Paula AM, Arntz Karlijn SE, Onrust Simone A. (2010). Online Group Course for Parents With Mental Illness: Development and Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1394

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Completers-only paired t tests on the OOO questionnaire in the 48-parent pilot. The article reports "a large effect (d = 0.61)" for incompetence and "a moderate effect size (d = 0.46)" for competence; intention-to-treat analyses were consistent.

> "Feelings of parenting incompetence also diminished significantly from pretest to posttest, reflecting a large effect (d = 0.61). Feelings of competence grew and showed a moderate effect size (d = 0.46)."
