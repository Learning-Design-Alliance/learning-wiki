### O'Hare et al. 2017

Dr Liam O'Hare, Dr Patrick Stark, Professor Carol McGuinness, Dr Andy Biggart, Professor Allen Thurston. (2017). Spaced Learning: The Design, Feasibility and Optimisation of SMART Spaces — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581430

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Literature review synthesis reported in the report's background section: Cepeda et al. (2006) reviewed 317 spacing effect experiments in 184 articles, all verbal memory tasks with recall tests, and found spacing interval should scale with retention interval. The report attributes this finding to Cepeda et al.

> "Cepeda et al. determined that the spacing interval during studying should increase as retention interval increases to optimise recall."
