## Evidence

### Najjuma Josephine N. 2024

Najjuma Josephine N., Muhumuza Albert, Santorino Data, Sekyere Samuel Owusu, Ocheke Amaka N., Yiltok Simon J., Bajunirwe Francis. (2024). Barriers and facilitators to interprofessional simulation-based learning in a Ugandan medical school: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06521-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A faculty key informant described medical-only scenarios: "you find a case scenario about management of stroke and all of it is medical and nobody has said anything about the nursing care," recommending cases prepared by both professions so both sides' content is executed in one case.

> "Like I said, you find a case scenario about management of stroke and all of it is medical and nobody has said anything about the nursing care. If we can have these cases prepared by both so that you have the nursing section and you have the medical section"
