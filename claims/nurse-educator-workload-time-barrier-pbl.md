## Evidence

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding from eighteen nurse educators at ten Ethiopian public universities, analyzed with Colaizzi's seven-step content analysis. Participants stated PBL "requires a long time for discussion and feedback" and that preparing multidisciplinary case material takes time.

> "More than half of the participants emphasized that high workloads and a lack of time were known challenges in the implementation of problem-based learning and explained that they needed to be prepared in multidisciplinary areas to deliver the approach."
