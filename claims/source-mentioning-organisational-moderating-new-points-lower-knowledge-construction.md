# Messages merely mentioning sources, containing organisational moderating, or introducing new points reflect significantly lower levels of knowledge construction, while discussing sources shows no significant difference.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Messages in which students mention but do not discuss new sources reflect a significantly lower mean level of knowledge construction, whereas discussing sources shows no significant difference. [→ De Wever 2007](#de-wever-2007)
`q3 i?` Messages containing organisational moderation reflect a significantly lower level of knowledge construction. [→ De Wever 2007 (2)](#de-wever-2007-2)

## Evidence

### De Wever 2007

De Wever, B., Van Keer, H., Schellens, T., & Valcke, M. (2007). Applying multilevel modelling to content analysis data: Methodological issues in the study of role assignment in asynchronous discussion groups. Learning and Instruction, 17, 436-447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2007.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Four-level model C: the source dimension contrasts mentioning versus actively discussing external sources against messages without sources; the article notes this is in line with earlier research of Schellens et al. (2005).

> "messages in w hich students mention but do not discuss new sources reflect a si gnificantly lower mean level of knowledge construction, whereas messages including this discussion of the external sources do not reflect a significant different level of knowledge construc tion (mean = 1.321 - 0.273 = 1.048, χ² = 16.510, df = 1, p < .001 and mean = 1.321 + 0.061 = 1.382, χ² = 0.779, df = 1, p = .377 respectively)."

### De Wever 2007 (2)

De Wever, B., Van Keer, H., Schellens, T., & Valcke, M. (2007). Applying multilevel modelling to content analysis data: Methodological issues in the study of role assignment in asynchronous discussion groups. Learning and Instruction, 17, 436-447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2007.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same model C: the organisational-moderating contrast was negative and significant; messages containing both organisational and content moderating were not significantly deviant (p = .099).

> "the mean level of knowledge construction is signi ficantly lower for messages containing organisational moderation ( mean = 1.321 - 0.399 = 0.922, χ² = 16.378, df = 1, p < .001)"

### De Wever 2007 (3)

De Wever, B., Van Keer, H., Schellens, T., & Valcke, M. (2007). Applying multilevel modelling to content analysis data: Methodological issues in the study of role assignment in asynchronous discussion groups. Learning and Instruction, 17, 436-447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2007.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same model C: the new-points contrast was small, negative, and significant, contrary to the hypothesis that introducing new discussion points would reflect higher levels.

> "Additionally, messages introducing new points reflect a significantly lower mean level of knowledge construction (mean = 1.321 - 0.074 = 1.274, χ² = 4.038, df = 1, p = .044)."
