## Evidence

### von Reibnitz D 2024

von Reibnitz D, Weinzierl A, Grünherz L, Giovanoli P, Lindenblatt N. (2024). Learning curve of robotic assisted microsurgery in surgeons with different skill levels: a prospective preclinical study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02114-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 13-participant, 117-anastomosis preclinical trial, "the mean SARMS score across the three sessions" differed significantly (p = 0.032); printed means were 2.6, 2.9, and 3.1. Blinded videos were scored by a single experienced microsurgeon. No effect size printed.

> "Repeated measures ANOVA showed a significant difference between the mean time per anastomosis and the mean SARMS score across the three sessions (p = 0.001, p = 0.032, respectively)"

### von Reibnitz D 2024 (2)

von Reibnitz D, Weinzierl A, Grünherz L, Giovanoli P, Lindenblatt N. (2024). Learning curve of robotic assisted microsurgery in surgeons with different skill levels: a prospective preclinical study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02114-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-tests compared session 1 and 3 means for all ten SARMS categories; "The improvement in the categories motion and speed was statistically significant" while the article reports mean scores in all categories improved with every session. No effect size printed.

> "The improvement in the categories motion and speed was statistically significant with p = 0.003 and p = 0.007, respectively."
