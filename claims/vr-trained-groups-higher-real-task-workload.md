## Evidence

### Cooper Natalia 2021

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

NASA TLX workload ratings collected during the real task for all 42 participants. The article reports significantly higher ratings for both VR-trained groups versus the RO control (p = 0.001 each) and no significant difference between the two VR groups (p = 0.592).

> "Independent samples t-tests showed that the average workload ratings were significantly higher for the two groups that were trained in VR, compared to the group trained only on the real task (RO, M = 5.3, SD = 0.93); NC group (M = 6.72, SD = 0.98; t(26) = -3.956, p = 0.001), ATV group (M = 6.53, SD = 0.86; t(26) = -3.643, p = 0.001)."

### Cooper Natalia 2021 (2)

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

NASA TLX ratings during the virtual training phase for the two VR groups (14 each). The article reports no significant difference between NC and ATV (p = 0.343), indicating the additional augmented multisensory cueing did not impact workload levels in virtual environments.

> "During virtual training, cognitive workload for both VR training groups was comparable. No significant differences were recorded between two VR training groups (NC, M = 5.22, SD = 1.36; ATV, M = 5.34, SD = 1.22; p = 0.343)."
