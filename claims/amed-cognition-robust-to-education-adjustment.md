### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i?`

Sensitivity analyses (Table S4 in supporting information) repeating the cross-sectional models with additional adjustment for education level, plus mediation analyses. The article reports the associations "remained significant and were only slightly attenuated"; no adjusted effect sizes are printed.

> "However, in both cohorts, after adjustment for education, the odds ratios for cognitive impairment and the estimates for cognitive function scores remained significant and were only slightly attenuated."
