### Kim 2015

Comparative finding from Experiment 2 (71 pre-service teachers): of the two expertise agents, the Mentor was more engaging and led to improved self-efficacy, giving it the best combined impact. No effect size printed.

> "Of the two agents with expertise, the Mentor was perceived to be more engaging and also led to improved self-efficacy, thus having the overall best impact on learning and motivation."
