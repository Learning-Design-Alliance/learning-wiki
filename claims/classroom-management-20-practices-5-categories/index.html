q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The article reports, citing Simonsen and colleagues (2008), a systematic best evidence review whose evaluation of 81 studies identified 20 general practices in 5 broad categories meeting evidence-based criteria. This is second-hand attribution from the article's literature review, not a finding of the present meta-analysis.

"Results of their evaluation of 81 studies identified 20 general practices that fell into 5 broad categories that met the criteria for evidence-based: (1) maximize structure and predictability; (2) post, teach, review, and provide feedback on expectations; (3) actively engage students in observable ways; (4) use a continuum of strategies to acknowledge appropriate behavior; and (5) use a continuum of strategies to respond to inappropriate behavior."
