Maiia Marienko and Kateryna Bezverbna. (2020). The Key Features of the CoCalc Cloud Service Use in the Process of Mathematics and Science Teachers Training. [doi:10.55056/ceur-ws.org/vol-2732/20201141.pdf](https://doi.org/10.55056/ceur-ws.org/vol-2732/20201141.pdf)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pedagogical experiment in 2017 at the Pedagogical Institute of the Kryvyi Rig National University comparing an experimental group using the author's CoCalc method with a control group using the traditional method. The article reports "high – from 16% to 20 %" and low falling "from 31% to 16 %" after the formation stage.

> "Comparison of the distribution of the experimental and the control groups of students after the formation stage of the experiment ( scale levels): high – from 16% to 20 %, sufficient – from 26% to 36%, average – from 27% to 28%, low – from 31% to 16 %."
