# JOBSTART's completion gain came entirely from GED receipt, with a small significant negative effect on earning a high school diploma

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` JOBSTART had a statistically significant positive effect on earning a GED certificate (37.6% vs. 21.1%; effect size 0.49; improvement index +19). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2008](#what-works-clearinghouse-2008)
`q3 i1` JOBSTART had a small, statistically significant negative effect on the likelihood of earning a high school diploma (4.4% vs. 7.5%; effect size -0.34; improvement index -13). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)](#what-works-clearinghouse-2008-2)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: JOBSTART. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

48-month follow-up of the Cave et al. (1993) randomized controlled trial; Appendix A4.2 reports GED receipt of 37.6% for JOBSTART versus 21.1% for control, mean difference 16.5, effect size 0.49, statistically significant.

> "This positive effect on comple-tion came entirely from JOBSTART's positive and statistically significant effect on receiving a GED certificate."

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: JOBSTART. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Same 48-month follow-up of the randomized controlled trial; footnote 6 reports diploma percentages of "4.4% and 7.5% respectively" for JOBSTART and control, and Appendix A4.2 gives effect size -0.34, statistically significant, improvement index -13.

> "JOBSTART was found to have a small, but statistically significant, negative effect on the likelihood of earning a high school diploma."
