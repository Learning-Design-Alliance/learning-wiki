### Galuschka Katharina 2014

Galuschka Katharina, Ise Elena, Krick Kathrin, Schulte-Körne Gerd. (2014). Effectiveness of Treatment Approaches for Children and Adolescents with Reading Disabilities: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089900

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication bias analysis (Duval and Tweedie's trim and fill) applied to the phonics instruction studies with reading performance as the dependent variable, whose funnel plot "displayed asymmetry with a gap on the left of the graph". The adjusted effect size was g = 0.198 (95% CI [0.039, 0.357]) after 10 studies were trimmed.

> "This procedure trimmed 10 studies into the plot and led to an estimated unbiased effect size of g' = 0.198 (95% CI [0.039, 0.357]) (see Figures 4 and 5, Table 4)."
