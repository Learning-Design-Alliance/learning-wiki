Evidence¶

Joep van der Graaf 2022¶

Joep van der Graaf, Lyn Lim, Yizhou Fan, Jonathan Kilgour, Johanna Moore, Dragan Gašević, Maria Bannert, Inge Molenaar. (2022). The Dynamics Between Self-Regulated Learning and Learning Outcomes: an Exploratory Approach and Implications. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09308-9

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Wilcoxon signed-rank pre-/post-test comparisons in the Results section of the exploratory study with 44 university students. The article reports "p < .001, r = .51" for the domain test and "p = .003, r = .31" for concept maps, described as large and medium effects respectively.

"Results showed that learning occurred, domain test scores were higher on posttest than pretest, p < .001, r = .51, (a large effect), and concept map scores as well, p = .003, r = .31, a medium effect"
