## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral results from the 20-minute Academic Diligence Task in 40 girls; participants also solved 180.75 problems on average (SE = 8.64), rated math moderately boring (M = 2.71/5) and games moderately tempting (M = 2.84/5). The article reports diligence "did not correlate significantly with IQ (r(38) = −0.03, p = .841) or SES (r(31) = −0.06, p = .741)".

> "Diligence scores, reflecting percentage of time spent doing math, were high overall (M = 84.14%, SE = 2.70%) but individual scores ranged from 34.44% to 96.67%."
