The review's own thematic synthesis of 38 peer-reviewed studies published between 2010 and 2024, retrieved through database searching and thematic analysis. The author concludes the games are spaces of "multidomain learning" spanning mathematics, literacies, culture, and AI exploration.

> "This review shows that classic African board games are spaces that multidomain learning and further situates these games in the current discourse about the relationship between gameplay and learning."
