### Bian Xiao Jie 2026

Bian Xiao Jie, Gao Su Yu, Zhang Shi Qing, Xue Fang, Liu Feng Lian and Chen Shu Fang. (2026). Perceived organizational support and self-efficacy as mediating factors between job burnout and emergency response competence among new nurses: a study based on emergency departments and intensive care units. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1775808

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 samples) with 321 new nurses, controlling for length of employment, resuscitation experience, and drill participation. The path "X → M1 → Y" had β = −0.137, 95% CI −0.216 to −0.044, accounting for 30.24% of the total effect.

> "The results suggested that perceived organizational support mediated the relationship between job burnout and emergency response competence, with a mediation effect value of −0.137."
