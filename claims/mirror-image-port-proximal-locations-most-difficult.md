## Evidence

### Inagaki Yuriko MD 2024

Inagaki Yuriko MD, Kawai Kazushige MD, PhD, Sonoda Hirofumi MD, PhD, Anzai Hiroyuki MD, PhD, Nagai Yuzo MD, PhD, Abe Shinya MD, PhD, Yokoyama Yuichiro MD, PhD, Ozawa Tsuyoshi MD, PhD, Kishikawa Junko MD, PhD, Emoto Shigenobu MD, PhD, Murono Koji MD, PhD, Sasaki Kazuhito MD, PhD, Nozawa Hiroaki MD, PhD, Ishihara Soichiro MD, PhD. (2024). Differences in the Difficulty of Accessing Various 3-Dimensional Locations Under Mirror-Image Conditions During Laparoscopic Surgery. Surgical Laparoscopy, Endoscopy & Percutaneous Techniques. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLE.0000000000001087

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Right-hand trials in the 11-surgeon box-trainer simulation: proximal and right-side spots spanned "0.31 to 0.58" versus "0.27 to 0.45" for distal and left-side spots, significantly harder (P=0.010).

> "Moreover, the range of the time-point to touch the proximal and right sides in the deep and superficial ends were 0.31 to 0.58, which contrasted with 0.27 to 0.45 for the distal and left sides in the deep and superficial ends. The proximal and right sides were difficult to access using the right hand (P=0.010)."

### Inagaki Yuriko MD 2024 (2)

Inagaki Yuriko MD, Kawai Kazushige MD, PhD, Sonoda Hirofumi MD, PhD, Anzai Hiroyuki MD, PhD, Nagai Yuzo MD, PhD, Abe Shinya MD, PhD, Yokoyama Yuichiro MD, PhD, Ozawa Tsuyoshi MD, PhD, Kishikawa Junko MD, PhD, Emoto Shigenobu MD, PhD, Murono Koji MD, PhD, Sasaki Kazuhito MD, PhD, Nozawa Hiroaki MD, PhD, Ishihara Soichiro MD, PhD. (2024). Differences in the Difficulty of Accessing Various 3-Dimensional Locations Under Mirror-Image Conditions During Laparoscopic Surgery. Surgical Laparoscopy, Endoscopy & Percutaneous Techniques. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLE.0000000000001087

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Left-hand deep-area trials in the same simulation: proximal spots spanned "0.36 to 0.42" versus "0.28 to 0.39" for other sides, with proximal spots significantly slowest (P=0.005); superficial left-sided spots were also slowest overall (P=0.002).

> "When the deep areas were accessed using the left hand, the time-point range for proximal spots was 0.36 to 0.42, while that for the distal and right and left sides was 0.28 to 0.39, and the proximal spots required the longest time (P=0.005)."
