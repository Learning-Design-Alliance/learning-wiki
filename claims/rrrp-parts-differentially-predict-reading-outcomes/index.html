Evidence¶

Day 2017¶

Day, Stephanie L.; Connor, Carol M. (2017). Examining the relations between self-regulation and achievement in third grade students. Assessment for Effective Intervention, 42(2), 97-109. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508416670367

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Separate two-level HLM models tested fall RRRP against three spring WJ-III reading measures in the 282-student sample. The authors report "students who performed better on Part 1 of fall RRRP also exhibited higher scores" on Letter-Word ID and Passage Comprehension, while Part 2 predicted Picture Vocabulary; no effect sizes are printed.

"In the Letter-Word and Passage Comprehension Models, Part 1 of the RRRP significantly predicted spring outcomes (Table 5), such that students who performed better on Part 1 of fall RRRP also exhibited higher scores on both the Letter-Word ID and Passage Comprehension subtests in the spring. However, for vocabulary, Part 2 of the fall RRRP predicted spring outcomes on the Picture Vocabulary subtest (Table 5)."

Day 2017 (2)¶

Day, Stephanie L.; Connor, Carol M. (2017). Examining the relations between self-regulation and achievement in third grade students. Assessment for Effective Intervention, 42(2), 97-109. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508416670367

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Three additional two-level HLM models controlled for fall WJ scores to test gains. The authors report fall RRRP "did not predict gains on the Letter-Word ID tests from fall to spring," while Part 2 predicted gains in Passage Comprehension and Picture Vocabulary; no effect sizes are printed.

"When controlling for fall Letter-Word ID scores, fall RRRP no longer predicted spring outcomes, which indicates that while fall RRRP predicted spring absolute outcomes on Letter-Word ID, it did not predict gains on the Letter-Word ID tests from fall to spring."
