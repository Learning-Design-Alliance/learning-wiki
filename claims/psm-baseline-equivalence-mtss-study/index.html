Evidence¶

Gage 2024¶

Gage, N. A., Salomonson, K., Ballew, T., Clavenna-Deane, B., & Grasley-Boy, N. (2024). The Impact of Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) on Student Attendance and Behavior. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655758

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Propensity scores were estimated by logistic regression on gender, race/ethnicity, low-income, disability, EL status, fall 2020 NWEA MAP ELA scores, and 2021 discipline incidents, followed by one-to-one optimal matching. Standardized mean differences for all baseline characteristics were below the WWC 0.25 threshold, with the largest at g = -0.24 (English Learner status).

"Table 1 provides the descriptive statistics for the 653 students in the three treatment schools and the 653 PSM students. We also calculated standard mean difference effect sizes (g) for each baseline measure to confirm equivalence. The students were equivalent (g < 0.25) on all variables."
