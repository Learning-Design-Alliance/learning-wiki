## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both treatment groups (PBI and TBI) obtained better scores than the control group, which received conventional instruction on the same target items (matching, multiple-choice and sentence-making exercises), on post-tests of formulaic sequences, with advantages most prevalent on the delayed test. ## Evidence

### Ha 2017

Do Thi Ha. (2017). The Impact of Different Instructions on Vietnamese EFL Students' Acquisition of Formulaic Sequences. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 48 Vietnamese EFL students in three intact classes (CT N=16, PBI N=17, TBI N=15), each class randomly assigned to one condition; the control class received conventional instruction on the same target expressions (a matching exercise with feedback, multiple-choice questions and sentence production), not no instruction; Mann-Whitney tests on immediate and delayed post-tests. The article reports "PBI and TBI groups have more learning gains than the controlled".

> "More often than not, PBI and TBI groups have more learning gains than the controlled, especially when it comes to a fortnight retention."
