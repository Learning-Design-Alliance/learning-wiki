Evidence¶

Samuel P. León 2021¶

Samuel P. León, Inmaculada García-Martínez. (2021). Impact of the provision of PowerPoint slides on learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104283

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA in a within-subject quasi-experiment with 173 university students, comparing exam scores on topics with (Y-ppt) versus without (N-ppt) provided slides. Mean scores were 3.44 (SD 2.06) in Y-ppt and 4.13 (SD 2.21) in N-ppt; the printed effect size was η2 p = .059, and "scores within the N-ppt group were significantly higher".

"Outcomes revealed that scores within the N-ppt group were significantly higher than those in the Y-ppt group (F(1, 161) = 10.0, p = .002, η2 p = .059, CI95% [0.008–0.139])."
