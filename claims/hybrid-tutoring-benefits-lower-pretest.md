`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Site 1 mixed-effects linear regression on normalized IXL time spent; the pretest:Tutoring interaction was β = -0.212, 95% CI:[-0.325, -0.096], p < .001, indicating tutoring benefits were higher for lower pretest students.

> "the interaction of the pretest and hybrid human-AI tutoring on time spent, pretest:Tutoring, demonstrated a negative and statistically significant effect, (𝛽 = -0.212, 95% CI:[-0.325, -0.096], t(4235) = 2.734, p < .001)"

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Site 1 analysis referencing RQ2 found the lower-pretest advantage held across time spent, questions answered, and skills proficient; the article reports this pattern without printing effect sizes for each measure.
