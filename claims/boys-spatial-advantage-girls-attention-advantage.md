Geary David C., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara, Ünal Zehra E. (2023). Sex Differences in Developmental Pathways to Mathematical Competence. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000763

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Results-section report of mean sex differences in the same 342-adolescent longitudinal sample, covering spatial measures (JLAP, MRT, Corsi) and teacher SWAN in-class attention ratings. The article prints the effect sizes ds = .29 to .58 favoring boys on spatial measures and ds = −.28 to −.37 favoring girls on attention.

> "In keeping with prior studies, boys had advantages on all spatial measures (ds = .29 to .58) and girls were more attentive in classroom settings (ds = −.28 to −.37)."
