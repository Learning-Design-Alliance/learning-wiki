## Evidence

### Thanukos Anastasia 2026

Thanukos Anastasia, MacDonald Teresa, Quimby Claire, White Lisa D. (2026). VENOMventure, an Immersive Escape-Style Game, Teaches Families the Foundations of Phylogenetics. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0172

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests compared post/follow-up change scores of 66 Phase 1 respondents classified as high versus low post-game engagement, for all activities and for "science-concept-related activities specifically"; neither contrast was significant.

> "which found no difference in the size of post/follow-up raw change scores across the high and low engagement groups for all activities (N = 66, Z = −0.197, p = 0.844), and for science-concept-related activities specifically (N = 66, Z = −0.734, p = 0.463)"
