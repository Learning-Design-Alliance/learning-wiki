## Subclaims
`q2 i?` General hospital nurses scored higher on self-efficacy (42.58 ± 8.44 vs 34.43 ± 11.2, p = 0.001) and attitudes (40.29 ± 8 vs 36.05 ± 9.52, p = 0.001), while psychiatric nurses scored higher on feasibility (17.09 ± 1.88 vs 16.24 ± 3, p = 0.02). ### Farokhzadian Jamileh 2025

Farokhzadian Jamileh, Sabzi Amirreza, Farmitani Zakieh. (2025). A comparative study of nurses’ competencies in integrating religion/spirituality into patient care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12613-z

Independent t-tests comparing the four RSIPAS subscales between the 100 general-hospital and 100 psychiatric-hospital nurses. Self-efficacy and attitudes favored general-hospital nurses (p = 0.001); feasibility favored psychiatric nurses (p = 0.02); no effect sizes printed.
