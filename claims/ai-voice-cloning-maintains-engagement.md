Kanzow Philipp, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Wilkinson Jenny, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Nourredine Mikaïl. (2026). Voice Cloning Using AI vs Traditional Audio Recording for Prerecorded Courses in Medical Pedagogy: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/86569

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Engagement was measured as the number of prerecorded course videos watched entirely (>90%). 76.6% watched all 4 videos, with "no significant difference between the AI voice cloning and audio recording groups".

> "Overall, 76.6% (49/64) of the participants watched the 4 prerecorded course videos entirely, with no significant difference between the AI voice cloning and audio recording groups (P=.98)."
