Observational analysis of the same 43-trainee cohort, comparing 3-mm instrument performance at the end of sessions 1 and 2. The article reports the OSATS score "significantly improved between the end of the two sessions" with p < 0.0001; knots with 3-mm instruments were not timed in session 1.

> "The median [IQR] OSATS score significantly improved between the end of the two sessions (22 [21–24] vs. 31 [28–33], p < 0.0001)"
