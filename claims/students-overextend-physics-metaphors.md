### David T. Brookes and Eugenia Etkina 2007

David T. Brookes and Eugenia Etkina. (2007). Using conceptual metaphor and functional grammar to explore how language used in physics affects student learning. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1319 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of a four-student homework group videotaped while solving a French and Taylor problem about classical wave analogs for reflection at potential steps. The transcript shows students talking at cross purposes, with one student proposing a physical-form analog (a water wave over a river-bed step) without linking it to how wave resistance varies with water depth.

> "A group of four junior students in their ﬁrst QM course, were video taped while working on their QM homework problems."
