## Evidence

### Berger B 2010

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Feasibility study reporting 14 courses conducted between September 2002 and April 2005 at the University of Hamburg, recruiting self-help group representatives, professional counsellors and patient advocates. The article reports "all but five finished the course" and 71% held a higher education degree.

> "Fourteen EBM courses were conducted including 161 participants without previous EBM training (n = 54 self-help group representatives, n = 64 professional counsellors, n = 36 patient advocates, n = 7 others); 71% had a higher education degree; all but five finished the course."
