Evidence¶

Naeimeh Bakshiri 2011¶

Naeimeh Bakshiri, Arshya Keyvanfar. (2011). Time Matters: Proactive vs. Reactive FOF. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 2, April - June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071093 doi:10.26634/jelt.1.2.1457

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The study's discussion synthesizes its two level-specific t-test comparisons (beginner and upper-intermediate), reporting that "learners who received proactive FOF improved significantly more" than reactive learners at both levels, with proficiency level acting as moderator variable in the design.

"To be more specific, learners who received proactive FOF improved significantly more than those learners who received reactive FOF at both levels."
