# An instructional guide and examples led peer reviewers to write longer feedback comments, with a small effect size

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students with access to the guide wrote longer comments on average than untrained students (d = 0.24). [→ George Gyamfi 2024](#george-gyamfi-2024)

## Evidence

### George Gyamfi 2024

George Gyamfi, Barbara E. Hanna, Hassan Khosravi. (2024). Impact of an instructional guide and examples on the quality of feedback: insights from a randomised controlled study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10346-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled experiment with 195 postgraduate students in a 13-week applied linguistics course; student-level analysis of average comment word length across moderations. The trained group's mean of 26.23 words exceeded the untrained group's 21.47, and "this difference was statistically significant, U = 42,621 p < 0.001, however the effect size was small, d = 0.24."

> "on average students from the trained group provided longer comments (µ = 26.23, Mdn = 23, σ = 20.05) than the untrained group (µ = 21.47, Mdn = 20, σ = 18.14)"
