# A Best Project competition across simulation methods proved impractical because fair competition across unequal methods is difficult to design

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors report that a competition element, intended to incentivize excellent projects, turned out to be impractical because a fair competition giving all simulation methods an equal chance of winning is difficult to design. [→ Florian Krötz 2025](#florian-krotz-2025)

## Evidence

### Florian Krötz 2025

Teaching quantum computing to computer science students: Review of a hands-on quantum circuit simulation practical. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/qce65121.2025.20531](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce65121.2025.20531)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' lessons-learned reflection from running the practical twice: the Best Project award discouraged choosing methods like density matrix, which is "known to be the slowest one but provides interesting insights into the simulation of noise". This is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.

> "However, the challenge turned out to be impractical, since it is difficult to design a fair competition that gives all simulation methods an equal chance of winning."
