Evidence¶

Markus Huff 2025¶

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

q2 · i1 · causal · r3

Exploratory participant-level Spearman correlation analysis in the main study (N = 1487) found "youth text reading experience positively and significantly correlated with narrative comprehension (ρ = .10, p < .001)"; the authors note these results are correlational and do not imply causation.

"Spearman correlation analyses showed that youth text reading experience positively and significantly correlated with narrative comprehension (ρ = .10, p < .001)."

Markus Huff 2025 (2)¶

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

q2 · i? · causal · r3

The same exploratory analysis found no significant correlations for current text reading experience (ρ = .05, p = .068) or youth comic reading experience (ρ = -0.02, p = .512). Current comic reading experience was excluded due to a highly skewed distribution.

"Both correlations of current text reading experience and narrative comprehension (ρ = .05, p = .068) and of youth comic reading experience and narrative comprehension (ρ = -0.02, p = .512) were not significant."
