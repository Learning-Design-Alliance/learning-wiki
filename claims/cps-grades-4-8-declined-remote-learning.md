## Evidence

### Gwynne 2022

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of fourth-quarter course grades for grades 4-8 students in district-run CPS schools, comparing spring 2020 and spring 2021 to the three pre-pandemic spring terms. The report states "a 10-point decline in the percentage of grades that were either As or Bs, from 77 percent of all grades pre-pandemic to 67 percent in spring 2021."

> "There was also a decline in the percentage of Bs, relative to pre-pandemic years, from 27 percent to 22 percent of all grades. This represented a 10-point decline in the percentage of grades that were either As or Bs, from 77 percent of all grades pre-pandemic to 67 percent in spring 2021."
