# The meta-analysis finds no major publication bias in the teacher personality–outcome associations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Symmetrical funnel plots, non-significant Egger's regression tests, and fail-safe N analyses indicated no major publication bias, except a weak fail-safe N for agreeableness (n = 18). [→ Lisa E. Kim 2019](#lisa-e-kim-2019)

## Evidence

### Lisa E. Kim 2019

Lisa E. Kim, Verena Jörg & Robert M. Klassen. (2019). A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Teacher Personality on Teacher Effectiveness and Burnout. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9458-2

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Publication-bias assessment using three indicators per Big Five domain with combined outcomes: funnel-plot symmetry, Egger's regression tests (all non-significant, e.g., openness intercept 0.54, 95% CI −0.62 to 1.70), and Rosenthal's fail-safe N (113–589 studies for four domains, but only n = 18 for agreeableness).

> "Thus, there did not appear to be major publication bias in the findings."
