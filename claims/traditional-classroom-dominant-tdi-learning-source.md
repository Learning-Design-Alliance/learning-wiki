### Yu Jingjing 2025

Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang, Yu Jian. (2025). Chinese dental students’ knowledge, attitudes, and practice regarding traumatic dental injuries in immature permanent teeth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07584-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of learning sources and attitudes (Tables 2 and 4) among 502 students. Traditional classroom was the dominant learning approach (90.0%); dissatisfaction rose with training level, with residents significantly more dissatisfied than undergraduates.

> "About 50.6% of participants were dissatisfied with traditional classroom for TDI knowledge learning, with 2nd - (68.9%) and 3rd -year (60.6%) residents reporting significantly higher percentages than 3rd - (42.0%) and 5th -year (35.1%) undergraduates (p < 0.05)."
