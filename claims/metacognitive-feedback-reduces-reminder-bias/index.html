Evidence¶

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert 2026¶

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert (2026). Metacognitive training facilitates optimal cognitive offloading. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-026-00714-0

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Pre-registered between-group online experiment (N = 164, Prolific) comparing a feedback group with a no-feedback group in an updated optimal offloading task with financial incentive. The one-tailed independent-samples test showed "significantly reduced in the feedback compared with the no-feedback group" (d = .347).

"However, the magnitude of this reminder bias was significantly reduced in the feedback compared with the no-feedback group (t(162) = 2.22, p = .01, d = .347; note that this p value is for a one-tailed test, based on the pre-registered hypothesis that feedback should reduce the reminder bias)."
