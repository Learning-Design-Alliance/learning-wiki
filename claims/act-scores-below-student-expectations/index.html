Evidence¶

Elaine Allensworth 2008¶

Elaine Allensworth, Macarena Correa, Steve Ponisciak. (2008). From High School to the Future: ACT Preparation–Too Much, Too Late. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-act-preparation-too-much-too-late

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative longitudinal interviews with 105 CPS juniors conducted before and after the ACT found widespread expectation shortfalls; after receiving scores, students began changing their college plans downward.

"80 percent of our interview sample performed lower on the ACT than they expected, and almost 80 percent of the interviewed students expressed an interest in retaking the ACT with hopes of getting a better score."
