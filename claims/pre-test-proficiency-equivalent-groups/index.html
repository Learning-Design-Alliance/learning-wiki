Hassan et al. 2014¶

Aminuddin Hassan, Norhasni Zainal Abiddin & Sim Kuan Yew. (2014). The Philosophy of Learning and Listening in Traditional Classroom and Online Learning Approaches. Higher Education Studies, 4(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n2p19

Pre-test comparison of the two groups of 22 students each before the month-long intervention; the article reports the t-test showed "no significant difference in students' proficiency level" and that "both groups were equivalent in this regard."

"The scores in t-test was t (42) = -.431, p < .05. Since the sig-t is .669, it indicates that both groups were equivalent in this regard."
