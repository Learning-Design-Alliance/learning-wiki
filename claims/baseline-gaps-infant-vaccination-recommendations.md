Baseline (pre-lecture) questionnaire results reported in the Discussion, showing unpreparedness on the innovations of the National Vaccination Plan 2017-2019 for the first year of age.

> "only 2% and 10% of students were aware about the recommendation for the vaccination respectively for Rotavirus and S. Pneumoniae before our intervention, and only 28% of students knew, before the lecture, that the protection against Haemophilus influenzae type B is contained in the hexavalent vaccine."
