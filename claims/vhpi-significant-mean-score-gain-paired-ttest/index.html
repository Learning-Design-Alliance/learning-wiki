Evidence¶

Armah 2018¶

Armah, R.B., Cofie, P.O., & Okpoti, C.A. (2018). Investigating the effect of van Hiele Phase-based instruction on pre-service teachers' geometric thinking. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 4(1), 314-330. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.383201

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test on VHGT scores of the 75 experimental-group pre-service teachers across the three VHPI lessons; the mean difference of 12.467 is printed in Table 6. No effect size is reported.

"However, in the experimen tal group, there was a significant difference in their mean scores for the pre-test (M = 18.32, SD = 3.739) and post-test (M = 30.79, SD = 4.294) conditions; t (74) = -30.776, p = 0.000 < 0.05."

Armah 2018 (2)¶

Armah, R.B., Cofie, P.O., & Okpoti, C.A. (2018). Investigating the effect of van Hiele Phase-based instruction on pre-service teachers' geometric thinking. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 4(1), 314-330. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.383201

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test for the 75 control-group pre-service teachers taught by conventional lecture-format instruction; the small mean increase of 1.410 was not statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested.

"The results of the paired samples t -test (Table 6) of PTs in the control group indicated that there was no significant difference in their mean scores for the pre-test (M = 18.84, SD = 4.182) and post-test (M = 20.25, SD = 4.305) conditions; t (74) = -0.445, p = 0.658 > 0.05 ."
