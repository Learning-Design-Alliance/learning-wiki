# Providing free vision services on the school campus was perceived to remove financial and logistical barriers to obtaining corrective lenses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adult participants reported that free, school-based delivery removed barriers including cost, navigating the healthcare system, taking time off work, and transportation. [→ Dudovitz 2016](#dudovitz-2016)

## Evidence

### Dudovitz 2016

Dudovitz, R. N., Izadpanah, N., Chung, P. J., & Slusser, W. (2016). Parent, Teacher, and Student Perspectives on how Corrective Lenses Improve Child Wellbeing and School Function. Maternal and Child Health Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10995-015-1882-z

`q2 · i?`

Focus-group findings from parent and teacher groups; barriers were "perceived for children with and without health insurance" and felt to contribute to lack of access in low-income communities.

> "All adult groups cited how providing free services in school removed financial and logistical barriers to students receiving corrective lenses. In addition to paying for the eye exams and glasses, participants described challenges identifying that a child has a visual deficit, navigating the healthcare system to find an optometrist, parents taking time off of work, finding transportation to access health services"
