## Evidence

### Bataineh 2017

Bataineh, R. F., & Salah, N. A. (2017). The Effectiveness of Drama-Based Instruction in Jordanian EFL Students' Writing Performance. TESOL International Journal, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1247797

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Component-level post-test comparison (Table 4): wording and cohesion means were 17.58 (experimental) versus 14.88 (control), t = 2.80, df = 78, Sig. 0.006 — the researchers link this to "the intensive practice involved in drama-based instruction".

> "Even though marked improvement was observed in the students' scores on the overall test and on the various components, wording and cohesion improved most."
