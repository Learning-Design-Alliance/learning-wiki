Meta-synthesis subtheme on emotional relief: examples included distraction during revision, mood enhancement through humour, and immersive gaming to escape everyday stresses. The review cautions that "context is critical when interpreting these quotes", noting scrolling or gaming can be adaptive when maintaining normalcy during difficult circumstances.

> "While adolescent use of online technologies is often viewed negatively, this subtheme shifts the narrative from passive consumption to highlight the proactive ways adolescents use online technologies to proactively support their wellbeing."
