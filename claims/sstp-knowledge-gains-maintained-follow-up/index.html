Evidence¶

Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013¶

Elif SAZAK PINAR, Bülbin SUCUOGLU. (2013). The Outcomes of a Social Skills Teaching Program for Inclusive Classroom Teachers. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2247-2261. https://www.edam.com.tr/kuyeb

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Mann-Whitney U comparison of posttest and follow-up SSTKT scores of the 12 experimental and 17 control teachers: the follow-up difference remained significant (U=.5, p=.00), with the experimental group's mean rank higher at follow-up.

"(experiment and control group posttest U=.00, p=.00, experiment and control group follow-up U=.5, p=.00). The mean rank of the experiment group was higher than the mean rank of the control group in the follow-up stage."

Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013 (2)¶

Elif SAZAK PINAR, Bülbin SUCUOGLU. (2013). The Outcomes of a Social Skills Teaching Program for Inclusive Classroom Teachers. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2247-2261. https://www.edam.com.tr/kuyeb

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of the follow-up data: a small decrease in knowledge occurred, attributed to the absence of guidance and performance feedback during instruction; no effect size is printed for this decrease.

"In the follow-up data, it was found that there was a small decrease in the knowledge levels of the teachers. The researchers believed that this was a result of not guiding teachers in using the skills they had learned and not providing feedback to them about their performances during instruction."
