## Evidence

### Grainger R 2018

Grainger R, Dai W, Osborne E, Kenwright D. (2018). Medical students create multiple-choice questions for learning in pathology education: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1312-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of student-generated MCQs in a fourth-year anatomic pathology course (N = 106 enrolled) using a three-level Bloom's-based rubric. The authors report "74% (313/421) of the questions were classified as cognitively challenging (Level 2 or 3)"; only 26% (108/421) were Level 1.

> "Expert rating was undertaken on 421 MCQs: 74% (313/421) of the questions were classified as cognitively challenging (Level 2 or 3) involving knowledge application and evaluation"
