## Evidence

### Zheng Xie 2020

Zheng Xie. (2020). Modelling the Dropout Patterns of MOOC Learners. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.26599/tst.2019.9010011](https://doi.org/10.26599/tst.2019.9010011)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Goodness-of-fit analysis across eight iCourse courses (Table 2). At significance level 5% the KS test cannot reject lognormality for these learners, with printed p-values above 0.05 for all courses (e.g. 0.059 for Calculus, 0.355 for Psychology); no effect size is printed.

> "When a learner has viewed a certain number of videos, his or her viewing duration follows a lognormal distribution (the results of the KS test are shown in Table 2)"
