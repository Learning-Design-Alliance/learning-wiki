`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 6) in the 458-adolescent survey; Table 5 lists the PA→SC→PMH→AA pathway as "Supported" with a Bootstrap 95% CI of 0.016 to 0.053.

> "Specifically, the indirect effect of PA on AA through SC and PMH was β = 0.031, accounting for 8.86% of the total effect. This chain mediation model offers new insights into the relationship between PA and AA."
