## Evidence

### Özkan Çiğdem Gamze; Demırbağ Birsel Canan 2025

Özkan Çiğdem Gamze; Demırbağ Birsel Canan. (2025). First year nursing students’ knowledge, skill levels and learning persistence in nursing practices: a comparison between classical lecture and flipped classroom. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07574-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled quasi-experiment with 71 first-year nursing students (35 experimental FCM / 36 control traditional). Table 2 prints post-test means of 85.147 (SD 8.527) vs 67.132 (SD 12.434) and persistence means of 88.929 vs 74.097; the between-group t-tests were significant ("p < 0.001").

> "After the lesson, knowledge post-test and persistence test scores of the students in the experimental group were statistically significantly higher than the students in the control group (p < 0.001)."
