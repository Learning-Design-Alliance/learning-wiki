### John Stewart 2018

John Stewart, Cabot Zabriskie, Seth DeVore, and Gay Stewart. (2018). Multi-Dimensional Item Response Theory and the Force Concept Inventory. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Partial correlation matrix for dichotomous FCI item scores correcting for total FCI score (only |r| > 0.1 shown), constructed by regressing total score on each item with a general linear model. The theoretical model explains the extra groups: items 4, 15, and 28 require only Newton's 3rd law; 17 and 25 share L1 and LM10; 5 and 18 share L2, L4, and C2; 6 and 7 share C1 and L1.

> "Examination of Fig. 2 shows that the problem blocks {8,9}, {21,22,23,24}, and {25,26} still stand out as highly correlated. Four other groups of questions emerge as correlated {5,18}, {6,7}, {17,25}, and {4,15,28}."
