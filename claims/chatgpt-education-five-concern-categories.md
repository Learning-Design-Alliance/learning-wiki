## Evidence

### Li 2023

Li, L., Ma, Z., Fan, L., Lee, S., Yu, H., & Hemphill, L. (2023). ChatGPT in education: A discourse analysis of worries and concerns on social media. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-12256-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-12256-9)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of 16,011 original negative-sentiment tweets, clustered by BERTopic into 200 topics and manually assigned to categories by two authors. The article reports that it "identified five frequently discussed concerns among Twitter users" spanning integrity, learning impact, capability limits, policy, and workforce.

> "Our analysis identified five frequently discussed concerns among Twitter users, including “(A) Academic integrity,” “(I) Impact on learning outcomes and skill development,” “(L) Limitation of capabilities,” “(P) Policy and social concerns,” and “(W) Workforce challenges.”"
