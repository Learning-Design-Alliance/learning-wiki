q3 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC analysis of O'Connor et al. (1996) in the alphabetics domain. Although the study authors reported significant effects across seven measures, "The WWC confirmed statistically significant effects only for the segmenting measure"; the printed effect size for segmenting was 1.94 with improvement index +47, and the average across the seven outcomes was not statistically significant but exceeded 0.25.
