Henry May. (2014). Distortions in Distributions of Impact Estimates in Multi-Site Trials: The Central Limit Theorem is Not Your Friend. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562702

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of 1,148 treatment and 1,148 control students from 380 schools in the Reading Recovery i3 multi-site randomized trial, using a three-level HLM with students nested in matched pairs nested in schools. The article reports "large and positive" overall effects with "substantial variation in impact estimates across sites".
