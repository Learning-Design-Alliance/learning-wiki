### Gaudet Andrew D. 2010

Gaudet Andrew D., Ramer Leanne M., Nakonechny Joanne, Cragg Jacquelyn J., Ramer Matt S. (2010). Small-Group Learning in an Upper-Level University Biology Class Enhances Academic Performance and Student Attitudes Toward Group Work. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive percentile comparison of final exam grade distributions across the same five-term quasi-experiment (Figure 3b), reported as part of the exam-grade analysis of 219 students across the three environment groups; values are descriptive with no effect size printed.

> "The 10th percentile on the final exam was at 47.5% (nearest rank) in the Individualistic setting. In contrast, the 10th percentile was increased to 60% in the Initial SGL environment, and to 65% in the Refined SGL environment."
