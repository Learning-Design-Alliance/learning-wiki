Huang 2026¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cognitive load measured with Klepsch et al.'s 8-item instrument after the collaborative phase in the RCT. There was "no statistically significant differ - ence in total cognitive load between the two groups" (d = 0.168); a separate test showed intrinsic load marginally greater in the experimental group (p = 0.079, d = 0.365), with no significant differences in germane or extraneous load.

"For the cognitive dimension, there was no statistically significant differ - ence in total cognitive load between the two groups [ t (93) = 0.821, p = 0.414, d = 0.168]"
