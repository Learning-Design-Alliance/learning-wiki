## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Question quality scores (openness, syntactic construction, use of questioning words) did not differ significantly between any pair of the three agent conditions. [→ Abdelghani 2023](#abdelghani-2023)

## Evidence

### Abdelghani 2023

Abdelghani, R., Wang, Y.-H., Yuan, X., Wang, T., Lucas, P., Sauzéon, H., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2023). GPT-3-driven pedagogical agents for training children's curious question-asking skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://hal.science/hal-03890447v1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Syntactic quality was scored on a 2-8 point grid (inter-rater reliability 76.8%) for questions generated during training in the 75-student experiment. All pairwise comparisons were non-significant with low power; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results showed no significant difference between the two incentive agents (t=0.57, p-value=0.57 and power=0.08). The same observation was also found between two automated agents, i.e. incentive vs open (t=-0.81, p-value=0.41 and power=0.12) and between the control agent (the hand-crafted incentive agent) and the the automated open one: t=-0.29, p-value=0.77 and power=0.06"
