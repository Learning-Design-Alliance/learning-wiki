## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Non-linear PBL grades (67.6 ± 10.4%, n = 140) were comparable to essays (65.3 ± 12.7%, n = 83) and oral presentations (66.0 ± 6.5%, n = 56), though lower than online quizzes (71.4 ± 11.3%, n = 141, P = 0.002). [→ Francis WR 2026](#francis-wr-2026)

## Evidence

### Francis WR 2026

Francis WR, Davies NA, Seeley A. (2026). Using non-linear slide decks to administer individualized problem-based learning assessments within pharmacology education. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70248

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-test comparing grades across four assessment techniques over 5 years in a second-year pharmacology module. Non-linear PBL grades were comparable to essays and oral presentations but significantly lower than online quizzes.

> "online quiz assessment grades were significantly higher (n = 141, mean 71.4 ± 11.3%, range 37-91%) than non-linear PBL assessments (n = 140, mean 67.6 ± 10.4%, range 19-92%, P = 0.002"
