### Pathmavarthini G 2026

Prediction of academic outcomes by serum cortisol, perceived academic stress, perceived memory performance, and physical activity among preclinical undergraduate medical students: a short-term observational study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis among 106 preclinical medical students found no significant continuous-scale correlations between serum cortisol and stress, memory-problem, or physical-activity measures; the authors report this as a null finding with no effect size printed.

> "No significant correlation was identified between serum cortisol and overall MSSQ score (Fig. 1a), serum cortisol and overall PRMQ score (Fig. 1b), serum cortisol and total MET-minutes (Fig. 1c)"
