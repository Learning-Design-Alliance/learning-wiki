## Evidence

### Anahita Golrang 2026

Anahita Golrang, Kshitij Sharma, Simon Dehaen, Olga Viberg. (2026). ProPACT: A Proactive AI-Driven Adaptive Collaborative Tutor for Pair Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02703

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Order-effect check conducted first because the experiment was within-subjects with balanced order of control and experimental tasks; all three tests were non-significant (p>.05). Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes printed.

> "We observed no order effect on any of the dependent variables i.e., debugging success (t[24] = 0.45, p>.05), debugging time on task (t[24] = 0.56, p>.05), and the feedback uptake (t[24] = 1.01, p>.05)."
