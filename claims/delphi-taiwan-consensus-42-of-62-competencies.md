## Evidence

### Tsai Han-Yi 2024

Tsai Han-Yi, Lee Shoou-Yih D., Coleman Cliff, Sørensen Kristine, Tsai Tzu-I. (2024). Health literacy competency requirements for health professionals: a Delphi consensus study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05198-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Modified three-round online Delphi with 20 purposively sampled Taiwanese health literacy experts (65% female, 65% professors/researchers). The abstract reports the final consensus counts: "a consensus agreement was reached on 42 of 62 health literacy competencies." Consensus was defined as 70% or more panel agreement.

> "After three rounds of ratings and modifications, a consensus agreement was reached on 42 of 62 health literacy competencies, including 12 of 24 knowledge items, 9 of 11 attitude items, and 21 of 27 skill items."
