q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of schools serving the most versus fewest Black students: 10% versus 4% of students attend schools with high (≥20%) percentages of novice teachers, and 50% versus 44% attend schools with high (≥10%) percentages of uncertified teachers. Percent novice teachers in most-Black schools is 10% versus 4% in fewest-Black schools.

Descriptive comparison for Latino students: 6% versus 5% attend schools with high (≥20%) novice-teacher percentages, and 51% versus 44% attend schools with high (≥10%) uncertified-teacher percentages. Schools serving the most Latino students have 8% novice teachers versus 6% in schools serving the fewest.
