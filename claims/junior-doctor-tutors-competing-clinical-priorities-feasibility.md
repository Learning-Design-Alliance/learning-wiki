Descriptive cross-sectional online survey of current and past NPMT tutors (26 complete responses) conducted October 2022 to April 2023, using visual analogue scales. Respondents reported they could only "‘sometimes’ teach without interruptions from work" and teaching times were only sometimes convenient; leaving the pager with a colleague scored mean 30.59.
