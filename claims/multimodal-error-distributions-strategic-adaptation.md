### Wei Ding 2026

Wei Ding, Anjuli Niyogi, Jordan A. Taylor & Jonathan S. Tsay. (2026). Hypothesis Testing Governs Strategic Motor Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00428-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Von Mises mixture modeling (EM algorithm, BIC model selection) of movement angles in Experiment 1. Early motor variability (Inner: 46.8°±2.1°; Outer: 64.2°±2.3°) far exceeded baseline noise (Phase main effect: F = 856.6, p < .001, η² = 0.76); distributions were multimodal at probe targets too.

> "This analysis revealed that movements to the training targets were strikingly multimodal (Figure 2e, top; Best BIC, inner = −5369.6 with 5 modes ; outer = −8563.3 with 6 modes )."
