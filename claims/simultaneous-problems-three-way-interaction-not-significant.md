# Co-occurring motivational and comprehension-related problems did not produce the hypothesized highest combined strategy frequencies

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Contrary to Hypothesis H2, the three-way interaction of motivational problems, comprehension-related problems, and strategy type was not significant (η2 = 0.003), so simultaneous problems did not yield the highest combined strategy frequencies. [→ Nadine Melzner · Markus Dresel · Ingo Kollar 2022](#nadine-melzner-markus-dresel-ingo-kollar-2022)

## Evidence

### Nadine Melzner · Markus Dresel · Ingo Kollar 2022

Nadine Melzner · Markus Dresel · Ingo Kollar. (2022). Examining the regulation of motivational and comprehension-related problems during collaborative learning. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:813–836. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09316-9

`q2 · i?`

Null three-way interaction from the same vignette-based repeated-measures ANOVA (N = 278); Table 4 reports η2 = 0.003 and p = .335 for the motivational × comprehension × strategy type effect. The article states the result was "contrary to our assumption"; the test of H2 was non-significant.

> "Finally, Hypothesis 2 assumed that students would react with most problem-sensitive (i.e., cognitive plus motivational) strategies when motivational and comprehension-related problems are present at the same time. As a consequence, we examined the three-way-interaction of the factors motivational problems, comprehension-related problems and strategy type. Contrary to our assumption, this interaction effect was not significant."
