## Evidence

### Abiola Lateef Olori and Adekunle Olusegun Igbosanu 2016

Abiola Lateef Olori and Adekunle Olusegun Igbosanu. (2016). Effect of Computer- Based Multimedia Presentation on Senior Secondary Students’ Achievement in Agricultural Science. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122547

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison of two intact classes (N=40 each) in a quasi-experimental design, analyzed by t-test (df 78). CBMP post-test mean 16.45 (SD 2.06) versus conventional 13.18 (SD 2.05); t-calculated 6.31 exceeded the tabulated value, so "the null hypothesis is rejected".

> "Table 3 shows that there is significant difference (P<0.05) in students’ achievement scores in agricultural science when taught using Computer-Bas ed Multimedia Presentation."
