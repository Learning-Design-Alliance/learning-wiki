Pulido Francisco Rodríguez, García Ángeles Rodríguez, Estebaranz Nayra Caballero. (2026). Simulation-based education in undergraduate psychiatry: a focused review and interpretive analysis of clinical competence representation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09885-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Interpretive synthesis across the four included studies reports that relational competence was represented through constructs such as empathy, rapport, therapeutic engagement and active listening, with studies reporting positive effects on students' confidence in managing emotionally complex interactions.

> "However, relational competence was less consistently operationalised than behavioural competence and was frequently assessed through global judgements rather than clearly defined performance indicators."
