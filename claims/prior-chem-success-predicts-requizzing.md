## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2019

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Cahill, M. J., McDaniel, M. A., & Frey, R. F. (2019). Participation in Voluntary Re-quizzing Is Predictive of Increased Performance on Cumulative Assessments in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0163

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise correlation in the full analytic sample (n = 310): "a weak but positive correlation" with printed effect size r(310) = 0.170 between re-quizzing participation and prior chemistry success, meaning struggling students — who benefited most on the final — were significantly less likely to re-quiz.

> "we looked at the number of quizzes that students retook in relation to their chemistry final exam grade and found a weak but positive correlation (pairwise r(310) = 0.170, p = 0.002)."
