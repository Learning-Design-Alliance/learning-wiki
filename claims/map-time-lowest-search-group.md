## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The three time-on-map groups differed significantly on orientation ratio (H(2)= 6.8, P<0.05) and access ratio (H(2)= 9.5, P<0.05), but not significantly on search ratio (H(2)= 5.2, P=0.06). [→ Stanton 1997](#stanton-1997)
`q2 i?` The group spending the longest time on the map had the lowest mean rank on search; students who spent less time in the map found more relevant information. [→ Stanton 1997 (2)](#stanton-1997-2)
