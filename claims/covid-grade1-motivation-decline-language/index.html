Golle 2026¶

Golle, J., Lösche, P., Beißert, H., Wieland, A.-K., Rieber, R., Schult, J., Hasselhorn, M., Nagengast, B., & Trautwein, U. (2026). COVID-19-related school closures and students' achievement and motivation: Did teacher support make a difference. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00439-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multiple regression with cohort as treatment variable, all pretest measures as controls, cluster-robust SEs, FIML. Regression coefficients interpreted as effect sizes in SDY units: b = −0.12 (reading intrinsic value), b = −0.15 (writing intrinsic value), b = −0.14 (reading self-concept), b = −0.17 (writing self-concept). Math intrinsic value and self-concept effects were not significantly different from zero (ps ≥ 0.456).
