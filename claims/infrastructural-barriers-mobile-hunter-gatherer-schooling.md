### Ninkova 2025

Ninkova, V., Hays, J., Lavi, N., Ali, A., Lopes da Silva Macedo, S., Davis, H. E., & Lew-Levy, S. (2025). Hunter-Gatherer Children at School: A View From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241255614

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's infrastructural-barriers synthesis reports this "incongruence between stationary schools and mobile hunter-gatherer lifestyles" as central, with children travelling long and sometimes dangerous distances or families moving to school villages. It is a narrative synthesis with no printed effect sizes.

> "One central barrier to children's school access is the incongruence between stationary schools and mobile hunter-gatherer lifestyles"

### Ninkova 2025 (2)

Ninkova, V., Hays, J., Lavi, N., Ali, A., Lopes da Silva Macedo, S., Davis, H. E., & Lew-Levy, S. (2025). Hunter-Gatherer Children at School: A View From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241255614

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this figure to Mokibelo (2014), reporting that "40% of San dropouts inter - viewed stated that they left school due to uninhabitable hostel conditions." Hostels are also reported as overcrowded and poorly managed, and several reports outline rampant emotional, physical, and sexual abuse of San boarders.

> "Mokibelo (2014) reports that 40% of San dropouts inter - viewed stated that they left school due to uninhabitable hostel conditions."
