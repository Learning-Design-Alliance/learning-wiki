`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Type II negative binomial regression of undergraduate-only QIS course counts (Table III) found the public-private gap not separable from other predictors: the authors "no longer see a specific disparity associated with public vs. pri- vate institutions", and the funding-by-urbanization interaction is no longer significant.

> "The primary differences are twofold: (1) we no longer see a specific disparity associated with public vs. pri- vate institutions, and (2) there is no longer a statistically sig- nificant interaction term between funding and urbanization"
