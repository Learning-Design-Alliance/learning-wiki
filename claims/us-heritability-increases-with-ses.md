## Evidence

### Tucker-Drob 2016

Tucker-Drob, E. M., & Bates, T. C. (2016). Large Cross-National Differences in Gene × Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Intelligence. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797615612727

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Figure 1 presents variance components implied by the US meta-analytic parameter estimates as a function of SES; the article reports the shared and non-shared environmental components decreased with SES but "these interactions were not statistically significant".

> "Genetic variance in intelligence increases from .24 at 2 SD below the mean SES to .61 at 2 SD above the mean SES. As instantaneous proportions of variance, these correspond to heritability estimates of 26% at 2 SD’s below the mean SES, and 61% at 2 SD’s above the mean SES."
