`q3 i1` Among students identified as eligible for ROOTS, intervention students showed statistically significant greater gains than control students on the TEMA standard score (t = 2.19, df = 27, p = .0371; Hedges's g = .38). [→ Clarke 2016](#clarke-2016)

## Evidence

### Clarke 2016

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Smolkowski, K., Baker, S. K., Fien, H., & Strand Cary, M. (2016). Examining the Efficacy of a Tier 2 Kindergarten Mathematics Intervention. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 49(2), 152–165. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219414538514

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial with 29 classrooms (14 treatment, 15 control) using a nested time-by-condition mixed model on teacher-nominated at-risk kindergartners. The article reports "statistically significant gains" on the TEMA with g = .38 for the Time × Condition effect.

> "Among those students identified as eligible for ROOTS, we found statistically significant gains among students provided with ROOTS over those in control classrooms on the TEMA standard scores (t = 2.19, df = 27, p = .0371)"
