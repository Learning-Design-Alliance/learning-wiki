`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis via path analysis in the same sample. The article reports mindfulness reduced its influence on school results "(r = .14) when EI was added to the model (r = .09)" and that the model explained 64.5% of the total variance of well-being with significant indirect effects.

> "Mindfulness reduced its influence on school results (r = .14) when EI was added to the model (r = .09). Additionally, the model explained 64.5% of the total variance of well-being and the total indirect effects were .375, thus statistically significant"
