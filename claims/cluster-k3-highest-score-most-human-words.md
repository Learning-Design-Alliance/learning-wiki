### Woo 2025

Woo, D. J., Susanto, H., Yeung, C. H., & Guo, K. (2025). Approaching the Limits to EFL Writing Enhancement with AI-generated Text and Diverse Learners. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Expectation-Maximization cluster analysis of the 59 submissions using Principal and Supplementary 1 feature sets. The article reports Cluster 0 averaged 8.1 normalized total score with 64.1% human words, Cluster 1 averaged 7.3528 with 90.99% AI words, and Cluster 2 averaged 5.4.

> "Cluster 0 shows the highest average total score and the largest percentage of human words, suggesting these students are more capable of drafting and revising a composition independent of AI-generated text."
