# In a three-group eye-tracking experiment, the pointing group significantly outperformed both the tracing group and the no-touch group on comprehension performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The pointing group scored significantly higher on the comprehension test than the tracing group (ΔM = −3.185, p < 0.001) and than the no-touch group (ΔM = −1.911, p = 0.025). ## Evidence

### Babette Park · Andreas Korbach · Paul Ginns · Roland Brünken 2023

Babette Park · Andreas Korbach · Paul Ginns · Roland Brünken. (2023). How Learners Use Their Hands for Learning: an Eye-Tracking Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09833-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Contrast tests in the three-group experiment (pointing vs. tracing vs. no-touch; analyzed N = 83 university students) showed for comprehension "ΔM = − 3.185, p < 0.001" favoring pointing over tracing, contradicting hypotheses H1a and H2a. A separate contrast showed pointing also beat no-touch on comprehension, ΔM = − 1.911, p = 0.025.

> "a significant difference between the pointing and the tracing groups for terminology performance, ΔM = − 2.593, p = 0.010, LLCI = − 4.356, and ULCI = − 0.650, and comprehension, ΔM = − 3.185, p < 0.001, LLCI = − 4.873, and ULCI = − 1.497, with significantly higher performance for the pointing group"

### Babette Park · Andreas Korbach · Paul Ginns · Roland Brünken 2023 (2)

Babette Park · Andreas Korbach · Paul Ginns · Roland Brünken. (2023). How Learners Use Their Hands for Learning: an Eye-Tracking Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09833-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Univariate testing within the learning-performance MANOVA in the same experiment found a significant group difference on the comprehension test, "F(2, 82) = 7.15, p = 0.001, η2 = 0.152", a large effect by the η² convention used here.

> "the comprehension test, F(2, 82) = 7.15, p = 0.001, η2 = 0.152"
