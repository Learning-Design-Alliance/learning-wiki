### Lehmann Ronny 2019

Lehmann Ronny, Lutz Thomas, Helling-Bakki Astrid, Kummer Sebastian, Huwendiek Sören, Bosse Hans Martin. (2019). Animation and interactivity facilitate acquisition of pediatric life support skills: a randomized controlled trial using virtual patients versus video instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1442-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus multivariate analysis of variance with between-group factor Group (Vid vs. VPanim vs. VPstat) and within-subject factor Domain (algorithm, temporal demands, procedural quality). "their interaction was strongly significant with F(6,198) = 8.081, p < 0.001"; the omnibus test was followed by individual ANOVAs and post hoc tests. No effect size was reported for the omnibus test.

> "In the MANOVA examining the association between study group and domain, their interaction was strongly significant with F(6,198) = 8.081, p < 0.001."
