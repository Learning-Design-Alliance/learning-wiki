## Evidence

### Carl Wieman 2013

Carl Wieman, Louis Deslauriers, Brett Gilley. (2013). Use of research-based instructional strategies: how to avoid faculty quitting. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.023102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.023102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program-level census by the science education specialists of adopters in six CWSEI departments, excluding faculty not yet on their own for at least a year or unable to teach. The article reports "only one subsequently stopped using these strategies" versus a 33% rate in the comparison survey.

> "Of the 70 that adopted such strategies with the support of the CWSEI program, only one subsequently stopped using these strategies. This is a tiny fraction of the 33% stopping rate for physics faculty in general"
