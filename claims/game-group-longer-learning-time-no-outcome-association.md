## Evidence

### Dankbaar Mary E. W. 2017

Dankbaar Mary E. W., Richters Olivier, Kalkman Cor J., Prins Gerrie, ten Cate Olle T. J., van Merrienboer Jeroen J. G., Schuit Stephanie C. E. (2017). Comparative effectiveness of a serious game and an e-module to support patient safety knowledge and awareness. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0836-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported time-on-task comparison between the game and e-module groups; the significant time difference carried no association with knowledge, self-efficacy, or rotation outcomes. Logged game-time for 18 students averaged 3.3 h and correlated with self-reported time (r = 0.77, p = 0.001).

> "Self-reported average learning time for the game group was 2.9 h (SD = 1.1 h) and for the e-module group 0.9 h (SD = 1.0 h, p < 0.001). There was no association between learning time and outcome measures."
