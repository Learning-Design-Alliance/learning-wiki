### Yunjo An 2025

Yunjo An, Ji Hyun Yu and Shadarra James. (2025). Investigating the higher education institutions' guidelines and policies regarding the use of generative AI in teaching, learning, research, and administration. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00507-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sentiment analysis using the VADER Sentiment Intensity Analyzer on 214 guideline documents; a normality test indicated non-normal distribution, so a Mann–Whitney U test compared private (mean = 0.945355) and public (mean = 0.963464) institutions. The article reports "no statistically significant differences in sentiment scores"; equivalence was not tested.

> "the Mann–Whitney U test was conducted, resulting in a statistic of 5664.5000 (p = 0.9664). These results indicate no statistically significant differences in sentiment scores between private and public institutions."
