# Nurse anesthesia students taught with the BOPPPS model outperform traditionally taught peers on post-test knowledge and clinical skills in pre-anesthesia care

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The BOPPPS group scored significantly higher than the control group on post-test pre-anesthesia care knowledge (d = 0.99, p < 0.001). [→ Khalafi Ali 2026](#khalafi-ali-2026)
`q2 i3` The BOPPPS group scored significantly higher than the control group on post-test clinical skill performance (d = 5.04, p < 0.001). [→ Khalafi Ali 2026](#khalafi-ali-2026)

## Evidence

### Khalafi Ali 2026

Khalafi Ali, Parhizkar Mahdieh, Albooghobeish Masoumeh, Sarvi-sarmeydani Nooshin. (2026). Effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care education for nurse anesthesia students in Iran: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08696-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group independent t-test comparison in a two-group pre-test–post-test quasi-experiment (60 students, 30 per group) with blinded bedside skill evaluators. After identical content delivered as BOPPPS-based or traditional training, the intervention group "performed significantly better than the control group", with d = 0.99 for knowledge and d = 5.04 for skills.

> "However, comparison of post-test scores showed that the intervention group performed significantly better than the control group (p < 0.001, d = 0.99 for knowledge; d = 5.04 for skills)."
