## Evidence

### Schneid Stephen D. 2022

Schneid Stephen D., Kelly Carolyn J., Brandl Katharina. (2022). Relationships between preadmission variables and academic outcomes for postbaccalaureate students in medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10129-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression for the PBPM cohort (n = 59) predicting preclerkship performance, with variables entered by temporal proximity to medical school start. The study reports the PBPM GPA step's ΔR² of 10% and total of 37%; a power analysis indicated an R² as small as 0.16 was detectable with power 0.8.

> "Beyond BCPM GPA and MCAT scores, the PBPM program GPA explained an additional 10% of the variance in preclerkship performance. All variables entered in the regression analysis accounted for 37% of the variance."
