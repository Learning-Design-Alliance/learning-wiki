Washington, DC: National Center for Special Education Research, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED544212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The synthesis reports, citing Burchinal et al., a study designed to test for a quality threshold below which little learning occurs. Findings were "not consistent with a 'good-enough' threshold of quality." No effect sizes are printed.

> "there was a substantially stronger association between instructional support and children's language, reading, and math outcomes in classrooms in which teachers provided a higher quality of instructional support."
