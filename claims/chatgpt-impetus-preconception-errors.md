## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Several of ChatGPT's wrong FCI answers reflected the impetus preconception — assuming an object immediately moves in the direction of an applied force regardless of initial movement, and that acting objects exert greater force than passive ones — preconceptions the article describes as common among beginning physics students. ### Gerd Kortemeyer 2023

Error analysis of ChatGPT's FCI answer pattern (specifically answers 8A, 9B, 21B, and later 23A, 25D, 28D) identifies impetus-style reasoning; the article states this is "a common preconception, shared by beginning physics students".

> "Several errors are related to “impetus” [21]: more than once did ChatGTP assume that an object im-mediately moves in the direction of an applied force, in-dependent of initial movement (answering 8A, 9B, and 21B)"
