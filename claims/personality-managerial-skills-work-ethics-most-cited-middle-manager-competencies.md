### Al Ansari Ahmed Mohamed 2026

Al Ansari Ahmed Mohamed. (2026). Managerial perceptions of core competencies for healthcare middle managers in Bahrain and Saudi Arabia: a qualitative study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6957.fb46

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 27 purposively sampled managers (senior n=6, middle n=10, frontline n=11) in Bahrain and Saudi Arabia. The results report that "Personality, integrity, and organizational ability were the most frequently cited traits (93%)" with time management at 90% and teamwork at 83%. No effect sizes are reported; percentages are descriptive.

> "Personality, integrity, and organizational ability were the most frequently cited traits (93%). Managerial and social competencies such as time management (90%), teamwork (83%), communication (70%), and decision-making (60%) were emphasized across all managerial levels."
