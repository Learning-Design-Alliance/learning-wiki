### Kahalon 2023

Kahalon, R., Shnabel, N., Sharvit, K., Halabi, S., & Wright, S. C. (2023). High-Quality Contact With Fellow Majority Group Students Is Associated With Better Academic Performance of Minority Group Students. Personality & Social Psychology Bulletin, 49(12), 1723–1736. https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672221115943

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null correlational finding from Study 2's longitudinal data (N = 90 at Time 1; 78 at Time 2), reported in the Results section. The article states belonging at Time 2 "was not associated with their GPA at the end of the year"; the footnote reports the indirect effect through belonging was non-significant, 95% CI[−1.001, 1.644].

> "Unexpectedly, however, Arab students' sense of academic belonging at Time 2 was not associated with their GPA at the end of the year."
