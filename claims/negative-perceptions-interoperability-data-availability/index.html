Laakkonen Noora, Jarva Erika, Hammarén Mira, Kanste Outi, Kääriäinen Maria, Oikarinen Anne, Mikkonen Kristina. (2024). Digital Competence among Healthcare Leaders: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/8435248

q2 · i? · review · r3

Content analysis of qualitative studies in the review, drawing on Wong et al.; leaders reported challenges using data for staffing management, including lack of software harmonisation and data system configurations delaying responses to events.

"In their study, leaders perceived the lack of intraorganizational harmonisation in software usage as a challenge in using data to guide inpatient staffing management decisions. They also saw that data system configurations challenged the timely addressing of events."
