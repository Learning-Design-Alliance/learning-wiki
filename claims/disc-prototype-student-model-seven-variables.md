### Mislevy et al. 2003

Robert J. Mislevy, Russell G. Almond, and Janice F. Lukas. (2003). A Brief Introduction to Evidence-centered Design. ETS Research Report RR-03-16, Educational Testing Service, Princeton, NJ. https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2333-8504.2003.tb01908.x

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Descriptive account (type d) of the DISC prototype developed by ETS and the Chauncey Group for the Dental Interactive Simulations Corporation; evidence is gathered as students work through examination and treatment scenarios with simulated patients. No empirical performance results are reported in this primer.

> "Figure 4 shows a more complex student model for the DISC prototype assessment, one that has variables for six areas of knowledge in the domain of dental hygiene, along with an overall proficiency. Testing scenarios based on interactions with simulated patients would provide information about two to four of these variables."
