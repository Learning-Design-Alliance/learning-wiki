## Evidence

### Fu Zhihui 2025

Fu Zhihui, Wu Yuhong, Xu Lingling, Cai Fen, Liu Ren, Jiang Zhehan. (2025). Optimizing cost-effectiveness in remote objective structured clinical examinations through targeted double scoring methodologies. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2467477

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the 550-examinee remote OSCE pass/fail decisions at the cut-off of 60. Single-rater decisions produced a "pass rate of 71.45%" versus 68.72% when the second rater's scores were incorporated; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Using the aforementioned cut-off score (i.e., 60), employing Rater 1s' values alone results in a pass rate of 71.45%, while incorporating Rater 2s' data reduces the success rate to 68.72%."
