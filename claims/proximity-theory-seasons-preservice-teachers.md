Results from Q3 of the questionnaire on simple astronomical concepts: the study reports "nearly a 41% of the total answers employed the so ca lled proximity theory". In linked Q5, 36% used the same explanation and no complete adequate answer was given; 18.5% answered in the right direction via incidence of solar rays.
