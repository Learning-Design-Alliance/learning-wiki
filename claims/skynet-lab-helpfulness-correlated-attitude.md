# Perceived helpfulness of Skynet-based telescope labs is positively correlated with science attitudes, while other lab components show no significant impact

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the 508 students who participated in at least one Skynet-based lab, Skynet Helpfulness Index is weakly but significantly correlated with Attitude Index (coefficient 0.044, p = 1.5E-02). [→ Adam S. Trotter 2019](#adam-s-trotter-2019)
`q2 i?` The same analysis for indoor labs and non-Skynet-based telescope labs found no significant impact. [→ Adam S. Trotter 2019 (2)](#adam-s-trotter-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Adam S. Trotter 2019

Adam S. Trotter, Daniel E. Reichart, Aaron P. LaCluyzé & Rachel Freed. (2019). Factors Contributing to Attitudinal Gains in Introductory Astronomy Courses. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.32374/rtsre.2019.014](https://doi.org/10.32374/rtsre.2019.014)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subset regression on 508 of the 749 respondents who participated in at least one Skynet-based lab during the semester. Table 6 prints Skynet Helpfulness Index coefficient 0.044 with p-value 1.5E-02 (0.047, p = 8.8E-03 without Instructor Index). Students who found the labs helpful left with more positive attitudes.

> "As with Lecture Helpfulness, we ﬁnd a weak but statistically signiﬁcant correlation between Science Attitude Index and Skynet Help- fulness Index for the subset of students who were exposed to Skynet-based labs."

### Adam S. Trotter 2019 (2)

Adam S. Trotter, Daniel E. Reichart, Aaron P. LaCluyzé & Rachel Freed. (2019). Factors Contributing to Attitudinal Gains in Introductory Astronomy Courses. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.32374/rtsre.2019.014](https://doi.org/10.32374/rtsre.2019.014)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same subset regression approach applied to other lab components among the 749 respondents. The analysis of indoor labs and non-Skynet-based telescope labs found no significant impact on Attitude Index; equivalence was not tested.

> "we performed this same analysis for the helpfulness of other lab components, including indoor labs and non-Skynet-based telescope labs, and found no sig- nificant impact."
