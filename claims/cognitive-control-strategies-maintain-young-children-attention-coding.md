## Evidence

### Chanjuan Fu 2025

Chanjuan Fu, Xiaoxin Hao, Xinyue Shi, Hanxin Qian, Shanyun He & Fengji Geng. (2025). Effect of cognitive control strategies on young children’s attention and emotion during coding learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00360-z

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 46 children aged 5–6 in two kindergarten classes; video of three lessons coded every three seconds. The strategy group stayed in the attentional state statistically longer than controls, with "d = 0.671". Means: control 0.632, experimental 0.780.

> "the experimental group, which received integrated cognitive control strategies, stayed in the attentional state statistically longer than the control group (t(82.221) = 3.174,p = 0.002,d = 0.671"

### Chanjuan Fu 2025 (2)

Chanjuan Fu, Xiaoxin Hao, Xinyue Shi, Hanxin Qian, Shanyun He & Fengji Geng. (2025). Effect of cognitive control strategies on young children’s attention and emotion during coding learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00360-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Per-lesson comparison of the proportion of time in the attentional state found no significant group difference in Lessons 2 and 6 (ps > 0.05) but a significant difference in Lesson 12, "d = 1.086", favoring the strategy group.

> "such difference was statistically signiﬁcant in Lesson 12 ( t(30) = −2.975,p = 0.006, d = 1.086), suggesting that the experimental group was more focused than the control group"
