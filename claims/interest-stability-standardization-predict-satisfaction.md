## Evidence

### Sun Jiyu 2025

Sun Jiyu, Zhang Xidan, Qin Ziqi, Tang Zihua, Ruan Yifeng, Shen Yifei, Zhu Zhuoli, Yu Haiyang, Shen Jiefei, Gan Xueqi. (2025). Enhancing preclinical dental education through a VR-based digital platform for inlay tooth preparation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08138-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple linear regression on questionnaire data (n = 166) with satisfaction (Item 8) as the dependent variable and seven predictors; the model reported R² = 0.735 and F(7,158) = 62.705, p = 0.000. Coefficients are unstandardized; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The findings revealed that stability in embedded digital platform usage (regression coefficient = 0.207, p < 0.01), interest (regression coefficient = 0.577, p < 0.01), and academic standardization of learning resources (regression coefficient = 0.201, p < 0.05) significantly and positively influence student satisfaction."

### Sun Jiyu 2025 (2)

Sun Jiyu, Zhang Xidan, Qin Ziqi, Tang Zihua, Ruan Yifeng, Shen Yifei, Zhu Zhuoli, Yu Haiyang, Shen Jiefei, Gan Xueqi. (2025). Enhancing preclinical dental education through a VR-based digital platform for inlay tooth preparation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08138-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null findings from the same multiple linear regression (n = 166): four of the seven predictors were not statistically significant; no effect sizes are printed, so the study did not detect an effect rather than measuring a small one.

> "However, convenience, operational flexibility, section organization, and richness of course resources did not exert a significant impact on student satisfaction."
