## Evidence

### Cheverko CM 2026

Cheverko CM, Mavrych V, Bolgova O, Mohamed FRR, Westrick J, Juarez L, Rush E, Solka KA, Doubleday AF, Byram JN, Becker R, Gomez V, Ganeng BKA, Hoffman LA, Roach VA, Brown KM, DeVaul N, Garnett CN, Herriott HL, Lufler RS, Mussell JC, Balta JY, Pascoe MA, Middleton JW, Duffy S, Stephens GC, Wilson AB. (2026). The performance of ChatGPT and other large language models on multiple‐choice questions in biomedical disciplines: A meta‐analysis. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70262

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random‐effects proportional meta‐analysis of 41 articles comprising 207 proportions of LLM accuracy on text‐based biomedical MCQs. The analysis reports "a 90.1% pooled accuracy (CI: 88.0%–91.9%)" for GPT‐4o_o1 versus GPT‐4 (81.9%) and GPT‐3/3.5 (59.3%), p < 0.001. No standardised effect size printed for this contrast.

> "Meta‐analytic findings revealed that GPT‐4o_o1 versions achieved a 90.1% pooled accuracy (CI: 88.0%–91.9%), representing a statistically significant advancement (p < 0.001) from previous versions [GPT‐4 = 81.9% (CI: 78.4%–85.0%); GPT‐3/3.5 = 59.3% (CI: 54.2%–64.3%)]."
