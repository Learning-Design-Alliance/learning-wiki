# Both receptive and expressive vocabulary correlate with number-word knowledge independent of age, replicating Experiment 1.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Negen 2012

Negen, J., & Sarnecka, B. W. (2012). Number-Concept Acquisition and General Vocabulary Development. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8624.2012.01815.x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 2 tested 33 children (mean age 3;9) with Give-N, the Woodcock-Johnson expressive test, and the added Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test III for receptive vocabulary. Printed effect sizes r = .582 (expressive) and r = .546 (receptive) were both significant, addressing whether only expressive vocabulary relates to number-word knowledge.

> "Replicating Experiment 1, results showed that number-word knowledge was related to expressive vocabulary, independent of age, r(30) = .582, p < .01. The same relation held for receptive vocabulary, r(30) = .546, p < .01."
