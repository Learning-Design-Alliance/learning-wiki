## Evidence

### Marsh 1983

Marsh, Herbert W. (1983). Relationships Among Dimensions of Self-attribution, Dimensions of Self-concept and Academic Achievements. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED243914

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational results (Table 3) from the 559-student sample, reported in the Results section on achievement scores. The authors note this asymmetry also occurred, less pronounced, in the earlier Marsh et al. (1984) study, and state they have no explanation for the anomalies with the two external scales.

> "Failurelester:nal attributions are virtually uncorrelated with achievement indicators. while success/external attributions are. significantly (negatively) correlated with achievement indicators."
