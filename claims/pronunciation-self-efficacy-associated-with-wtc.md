## Evidence

### Lu Y 2026

Lu Y, Yang Y, Cui T, Yang Z, Cai Y and Jing B (2026) Associations between generative AI–based pronunciation feedback and willingness to communicate in English: the mediating role of English pronunciation self-efficacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1918980. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918980

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Direct path estimated in the same CB-SEM path analysis with bias-corrected bootstrapping (5,000 resamples) on the 1,701-respondent sample. The EPSE→WTC path was significant, "β = 0.661", with the 95% CI excluding zero.

> "the path from EPSE to WTC was positive and statisti -cally significant, B = 0.665, β = 0.661, 95% CI [0.610, 0.724], p < 0.001, indicating a strong association between EPSE and WTC."
