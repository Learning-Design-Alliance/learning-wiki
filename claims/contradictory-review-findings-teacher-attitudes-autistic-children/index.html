q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative summary of the Han and Cumming (2024) review of 33 studies on teachers' beliefs about education provision for students with autism, which identified five content facets and concluded most teachers are positive yet concerned about their abilities and support.

"They concluded that although most teachers have a positive attitude toward the inclusion of autistic children, they also have major concerns, particularly with respect to their own abilities and the inadequate support provided by schools and the state."
