## Evidence

### McBain Lynn 2016

McBain Lynn, Pullon Susan, Garrett Sue, Hoare Kath. (2016). Genital examination training: assessing the effectiveness of an integrated female and male teaching programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0822-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post questionnaire study of 84 fifth-year medical students; 62 matched pre-post questionnaires analysed with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. Self-assessments "showed significant improvement after the teaching programme"; Table 2 reports post medians of 3–4 with p <.0001 for skill, confidence and comfort on both exam types.

> "When comparing the pre-teaching programme questionnaire results with the post-teaching programme, students’ self-assessments showed significant improvement after the teaching programme (Fig. 2)."
