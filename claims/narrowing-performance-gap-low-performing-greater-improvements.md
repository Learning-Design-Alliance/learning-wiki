## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression regressing average relative error rate in 2019 against 2020 for the within-group cohort, controlling for assignment difference and repetition difference. The coefficient was "below 1", contrary to the Matthew effect hypothesis.

> "the regression coefficient for time window is below 1 (b = .56; t = 34.02; p < .001), suggesting that low-performing students in 2019 showed greater decrements in relative error rate than high-performing students."

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021 (2)

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple regression without covariates, with regression dilution bias correction relying on the simplifying assumption that measurement noise of regressor and regressand are uncorrelated, potentially violated here.

> "the 95% confidence interval for the corrected regression coefficient is estimated to lie between (0.30, 0.99). Thus, the corrected regression coefficient (below 1.0) indicates a narrowing performance gap."
