Impact and perceptions of Active Learning Classrooms on reducing sedentary behaviour and improving physical and mental health and academic indicators in children and adolescents: A scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317973

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of two studies using the BASC-3 questionnaire; the review reports improvements in girls only, and one study found worse internalising problems, inattention/hyperactivity and emotional symptoms in boys.

"One showed that girls attending classes with flexible furniture improved their school well-being and mental health (<internalising problems, inattention/hyperactivity, and emotional symptoms) compared to girls who attended traditional classrooms, but these differences were not observed in boys [20]."
