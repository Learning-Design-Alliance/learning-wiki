`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' observation from the fitted error and time learning curves of Task 2 (intracorporeal suturing and knotting) in the randomised box-trainer study. The article states "knot quality improved much more slowly than time efficiency" and draws a teaching implication.

> "Interestingly, knot quality improved much more slowly than time efficiency. This suggests that teaching should be improved and encourages the conservative surgeon to put quality first, even if it takes longer."
