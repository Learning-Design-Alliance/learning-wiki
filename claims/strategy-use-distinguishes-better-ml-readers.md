`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of Klingner et al. (2006) findings reported within this review of reviews: strategy use distinguished better readers more than fluency, MLs' perceptions of cognitive strategies predicted English reading comprehension, and negative strategies were negatively related to comprehension gains. No effect size is printed.

> "Notably, the use of strategies distinguished better readers more accurately than their English fluency. In addition, the authors highlighted findings that showed MLs’ perceptions of cognitive reading strategies were predictive of their English reading comprehension"
