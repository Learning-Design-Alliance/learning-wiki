## Evidence

### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of final-score versus HWC-completion scatterplots for Instructor A's QM2 and Instructor B's CM1 courses, with corresponding plots for all twelve courses. The article reports a "fairly even distribution" in QM2 and a triangular shape in CM1 where low-scoring students rarely did HWCs.

> "Hence, in our dataset, HWC correction engagement is not limited to only higher-scoring or only lower-scoring students."
