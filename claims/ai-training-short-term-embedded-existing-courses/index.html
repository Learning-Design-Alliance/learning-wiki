Evidence¶

Pinto F 2026¶

Pinto F, Rodrigues RN, Brito-Costa S, Costa C, Gonçalves S, Pires NA and Martins F (2026) Artificial intelligence in initial teacher training for pre-service primary school teachers in mathematics: a systematic review. Front. Educ. 11:1934366. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934366

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review synthesis of 11 studies answering research question 1 on training types. The review reports the two-category typology with study counts, noting the first typology "was clearly predominant" and that AI is incorporated into existing courses rather than standalone training.

"the training implemented with Pre-Service Primary School Teachers in the field of AI can be classified into two distinct categories: (a) single-session or short interventions consisting of a small number of sessions embedded within existing courses (9 studies); (b) five- to eight-week modules integrated into existing courses (2 studies)."
