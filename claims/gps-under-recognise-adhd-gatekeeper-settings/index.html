Evidence¶

Tatlow-Golden M 2016¶

Tatlow-Golden M, Prihodova L, Gavin B, Cullen W, McNicholas F. (2016). What do general practitioners know about ADHD? Attitudes and knowledge among first-contact gatekeepers: systematic narrative review. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-016-0516-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic narrative synthesis of the review's included surveys, reporting GP self-report data from Australia (2002) and Canada (2005). The review reports that in Canada, 20% of GPs said they saw no children in whom they would consider ADHD, compared to only 6% for mood disorders. No effect size is printed.

"In Australia (2002), GPs saw over 550 children aged 4–16 annually yet reported identifying just 1–5 ADHD cases in any given year, suggesting a recognition rate of under 1 %."
