### Mislevy et al. 2003

Robert J. Mislevy, Russell G. Almond, and Janice F. Lukas. (2003). A Brief Introduction to Evidence-centered Design. ETS Research Report RR-03-16, Educational Testing Service, Princeton, NJ. https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2333-8504.2003.tb01908.x

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the report's own conceptual argument (type e/d) from the abstract and overview, not an empirical test. It positions graphical models as the machinery that 'effects probability-based accumulation and synthesis of evidence over task performances,' with IRT as the special case used in GRE-CAT and Bayes nets in DISC.

> "To unify the ideas and to provide a foundation for extending probability-based reasoning in assessment applications more broadly, however, a more general expression in terms of graphical models is indicated."
