Evidence¶

Vasilopoulos Fotini 2023¶

Vasilopoulos Fotini, Jeffrey Holly, Wu Yanwen, Dumontheil Iroise. (2023). Multi-level meta-analysis of whether fostering creativity during physical activity interventions increases their impact on cognitive and academic outcomes during childhood. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-35082-y

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup meta-analysis of the five studies measuring creativity as an outcome, four of which used highly creative interventions (creative movement PE and dance); summary effect g = 0.73, p = 0.04, with nine of 14 effect sizes showing moderate to large beneficial effects on creativity.

"Sub-group analysis for the highly creative sub-group revealed a large summary effect of this group of interventions on creativity measures (g = 0.73, p = 0.04 (95% CI 0.06–1.40), I3 = 86.4%, I2 = 0.0%)."
