### Mahoney B 2021

Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A recent review of simulation team training with human factors components identified five single prospective site studies in high- and low-resource countries. The review reports "a 34% reduction in maternal mortality" and other reductions; no effect sizes or confidence intervals are printed.

> "Two showed a 34% reduction in maternal mortality and 3 a 41% to 50% reduction in blood transfusion, whereas cluster analysis revealed a 17% reduction in PPH incidence and a 37% reduction on weighted obstetrics adverse outcomes."
