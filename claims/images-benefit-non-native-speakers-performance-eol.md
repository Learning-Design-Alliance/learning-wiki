## Evidence

### Weil-Lotan D 2026

Weil-Lotan D, Solnica A, Benbenishty J, Ilsar T. (2026). Does the Addition of Images to the Medication Dosage Calculation Exam Improve Metacognitive Components and Performance? A Randomized Prospective Study. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000002215

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup ANCOVAs within non-native language speakers, with image as fixed factor and age and gender as covariates, showed significant main effects on both performance and EOL; the EOL effect was "partial η2 = 0.323."

> "The results for test performance (number of correct responses out of 8 items) revealed a significant main effect of image group, F(1, 42) = 7.34, P = .010, partial η2 = 0.149, with higher scores for participants in the image group (M = 6.58, SD = 1.43) compared with those in the control group (M = 5.41, SD = 1.93)."
