## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh (2023). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with basics and change of basis for two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/acf5b3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/acf5b3)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post-test comparison in a mandatory junior-/senior-level QM course over three years (N = 29 online, 25 and 27 in person), with pre-test after traditional lecture. The article reports "equal to or higher than that on the pre -test for every concept tested" with no pooled effect size.

> "Overall, students who engaged with the CQS demonstrated performance on the post-test equal to or higher than that on the pre -test for every concept tested, and this held true across both instructors and both modes of instruction."
