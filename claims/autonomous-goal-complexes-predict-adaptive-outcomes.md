## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Andrew J. Elliot · Nicolas Sommet 2023

Andrew J. Elliot · Nicolas Sommet. (2023). Integration in the Achievement Motivation Literature and the Hierarchical Model of Achievement Motivation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09785-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports a series of studies by Sommet and Elliot (2017) linking achievement goals to autonomous versus controlled reasons grounded in self-determination theory. 'Controlled mastery-approach goal complexes did not show the same benefits,' and comparable results emerged for performance-approach complexes.

> "an autonomous mastery-approach goal complex positively predicted adaptive achievement outcomes (e.g., interest, persistence, help-seeking) over and above the influence of mastery-approach goals alone and autonomous reasons alone"
