# Teachers reported that students needed dedicated time to learn and explore technologies before being expected to gain mathematics understanding from them

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers reflected that the curriculum needed to expand to include teaching about the technology before expecting students to automatically gain mathematics understanding from working with it. [→ Niess 2018](#niess-2018)

## Evidence

### Niess 2018

Niess, M. L., & Roschelle, J. (2018). Transforming Teachers' Knowledge for Teaching Mathematics with Technologies through Online Knowledge-Building Communities. Proceedings of the 40th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education, Greenville, SC. https://www.pmena.org/proceedings/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Teacher reflection from Janis's fraction unit case in the curriculum-and-materials component analysis; she saw the need to allow students time to become familiar with the virtual manipulatives. Descriptive self-report evidence, no effect size.

> "However, I simply had not factored in enough time to let them explore with the manipulatives so that they could effectively utilize them during the lessons."
