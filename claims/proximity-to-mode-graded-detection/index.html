Evidence¶

Luthra 2025¶

Luthra, S., Luor, A., Tierney, A. T., Dick, F., & Holt, L. L. (2025). Statistical learning dynamically shapes auditory perception. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00328-z

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of Exp 1b-d (unimodal 75% distributions over five frequencies, 320 trials) showed a significant graded proximity effect (p = .014, η² = 0.022). When the high-probability frequency was centered, the near > far detection difference was symmetric (p = .004, η² = 0.259); near an edge, an asymmetric 'ski slope' profile emerged (p = .015, η² = 0.034).

"The low-probability frequencies of Exp 1b-d share the same probability, yet those closer to a high-probability frequency are better detected than those further away (ANOVA, p =0 . 0 1 4 ,η2 = 0.022)"
