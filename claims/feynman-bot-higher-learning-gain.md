## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a three-day controlled experiment with 14 working adults, the Feynman Bot group had a higher mean learning gain (3.83) than the Passive Learners control group (2.17). [→ Akshaya Rajesh 2024](#akshaya-rajesh-2024)

## Evidence

### Akshaya Rajesh 2024

Akshaya Rajesh, Sumbul Khan. (2024). Learn Like Feynman: Developing and Testing an AI-Driven Feynman Bot. Singapore University of Technology and Design. [doi:10.1109/tale62452.2024.10834370](https://doi.org/10.1109/tale62452.2024.10834370)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled experiment, 14 working adults randomly split into Passive Learners (CG) and Feynman Bot Learners (FB), studying two edX justice lectures; learning gain was summative minus formative MCQ score. The article reports mean gains of "( CG= 2.17, FB =3.83)"; no effect size is printed.

> "We found that the Feynman Bot group experienced a higher mean learning gain as compared to the Passive Learners while studying the same material for the same length of time ( CG= 2.17, FB =3.83)."
