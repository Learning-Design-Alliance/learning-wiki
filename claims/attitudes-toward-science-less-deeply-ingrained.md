This is the author's interpretation in the conclusions section, offered as a possible explanation for the observed gains; the article presents it as a hypothesis, not a tested result, citing prior studies of attitudes toward learning.

> "Several previous studies have shown that attitudes tend to decline or remain unchanged after introdu ctory physics and astronomy courses, 4,9 with just a few small-scale counterexamples. 10,11 However, these studies have focused more on attitudes toward learning. Attitudes toward science may be less deeply ingrained."
