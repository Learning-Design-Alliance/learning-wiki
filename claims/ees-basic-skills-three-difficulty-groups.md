## Evidence

### Favier V 2025

Favier V, Tewfik MA, Nguyen LHP, Rumeau C, Gallet P. (2025). Quantifying Difficulty in Endoscopic Endonasal Surgery—A Modified Delphi Method Approach. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1177/19160216251390285

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of a three-round modified Delphi e-survey: 15 international EES experts ranked basic skills from least to most difficult to learn; the principal investigator consolidated the 23 skills into "3 groups of increasing difficulty". Group 1 items included navigation with a 0° telescope and suction; group 3 included drilling and suturing.

> "Group 1 is comprised of 5 items, Group 2 of 12 items, and Group 3 of 6 items."
