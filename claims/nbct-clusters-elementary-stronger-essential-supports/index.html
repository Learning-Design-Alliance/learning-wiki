q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Analysis of CCSR 2007 teacher survey data comparing 44 elementary cluster schools to non-cluster schools on essential supports measures, controlling for teacher and school characteristics. Effect sizes in Table 5 range from .13 to .36 standard deviation units.

"Elementary schools with clusters of three or more NBCTs demonstrated greater strength in 7 out of 12 essential supports measures, when compared to other schools, even taking into account underlying demographic and other differences."

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Analysis of CCSR 2007 teacher survey data for 16 high schools with NBCT clusters; teacher-principal trust effect size .38 in Table 5. The chapter also reports teachers in cluster high schools reported less teacher-parent interaction (effect size -.13).

"Among high schools, only teacher-principal trust was stronger for the cluster schools."
