Wang Hai, Liang Jun-jie, Zhang Zheng-liang, Pei Hong-hong, Pan Long-fei. (2025). Theory–practice discrepancy in cardiopulmonary resuscitation performance: a multi-centre evaluation of emergency healthcare providers in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08433-8
