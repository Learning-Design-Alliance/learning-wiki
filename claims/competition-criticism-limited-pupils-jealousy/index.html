An editorial statement in the Journal of Educational Psychology summarizing objections to competition as an incentive for school work, reporting that critics say it arouses "jealousy and bitterness" and may lead pupils of poor vitality to "bankrupt their health". No data or test is reported; the objections are attributed as the article attributes them.

"lain hopes of success in the trial of wits and skill, that it arouses jealousy and bitterness, and that it may lead pupils of poor vitality to bankrupt their health. It is argued, moreover, that the spirit of competition is foreign"
