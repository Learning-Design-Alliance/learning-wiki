# STAC intervention students showed a trend toward decreased anxiety relative to wait-list control students (p = .06)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The Time x Group interaction for anxiety approached significance, with intervention students decreasing and control students increasing in anxiety from baseline to 30-day follow-up. [→ Midgett 2016](#midgett-2016)

## Evidence

### Midgett 2016

Midgett, A., Doumas, D., Trull, R., & Johnston, A. (2016). A Randomized Controlled Study Evaluating a Brief, Bystander Bullying Intervention with Junior High School Students. Boise State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146195

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLM repeated measures ANOVA using the BASC-3 SRP-A Anxiety scale (sample α = .91) in the randomized controlled study. As seen in Table 1, "students in the intervention group reported a decrease in anxiety, whereas students in the control group reported an increase in anxiety."

> "results of the GLM repeated measures ANOVA indicated a trend toward significance for the interaction effect Time x Group for anxiety, Wilks' Lambda = .39, F(1, 51) = 3.62, p = .06, η 2 p = .07. Examination of the η 2 p indicates the effect size approached the medium range."
