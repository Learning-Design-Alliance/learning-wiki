Evidence¶

E. Geboers 2013¶

E. Geboers, F. Geijsel, W. Admiraal, G. ten Dam. (2013). Review of the effects of citizenship education. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2012.02.001

q3 · i? · review · r?

The review's synthesis of empirical citizenship education studies from 2003-2009 concludes that "an open and democratic classroom climate" with discussion and dialogue effectively promotes citizenship development among secondary school students. No effect size is printed in the reviewed summary.

"An open and democratic classroom climate in which discussion and dialogue takes place appears to effectively promote the development of citizenship among secondary school students."
