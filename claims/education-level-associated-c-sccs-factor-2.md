## Evidence

### Hu Yanli 2019

Hu Yanli, Leeuwen René Van, Li Fan. (2019). Psychometric properties of the Chinese version of the spiritual care competency scale in nursing practice: a methodological study. BMJ Open, 9(10), e030497. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparisons (Student's t-test or F test) of C-SCCS scores against demographic variables in the psychometric sample. The article reports the F=3.394, p=0.018 association for factor 2, with post hoc mean differences (I−J) of 3.94 (p=0.008) and 2.90 (p=0.009) favouring junior college and undergraduate nurses over graduate-educated nurses.
