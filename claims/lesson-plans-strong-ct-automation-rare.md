Rubric-based descriptive analysis of 17 teacher-submitted integrated lesson plans (7 science, 5 math, 4 English language arts, 1 social studies), scored 0–3 on CT concepts. The article reports "an average score of 2.27 out of 3" for algorithms and "0.6" for automation; no inferential test was possible.
