Evidence¶

Foster 2016¶

Foster, M. E., Anthony, J. L., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., & Williams, J. M. (2016). A Randomized Control Trial Evaluating the Effectiveness of Computer Assisted Instruction in Numeracy on Math Outcomes for Monolingual English Speaking Kindergartners from Title 1 Schools. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566981

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized control trial of 243 kindergartners randomized within classroom to Building Blocks or Earobics CAI (90 minutes weekly for 21 weeks). Multilevel ANCOVA showed "The Building Blocks Software group outperformed the comparison group" with a least squared means difference of 1.85 and effect size 0.43.

"For numeracy scores at posttest, there was a main effect for group, F(1, 178) = 8.08, p < .01, after accounting for numeracy scores from pretest and classroom nesting. The Building Blocks Software group outperformed the comparison group, with a difference in least squared means of 1.85 raw score units."
