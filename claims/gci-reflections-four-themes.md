### Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan 2026

Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan. (2026). From Individual Prompts to Collective Intelligence: Mainstreaming Generative AI in the Classroom. [doi:10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210](https://doi.org/10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of 7 open-ended questionnaire questions from the two-course implementation, coded with Microsoft Co-Pilot assistance and refined by the authors. The four themes spanned human-AI synergy, over-reliance risks, bias/accuracy, and strategic use.

> "Thematic analysis of open-ended responses revealed four recurring themes illuminating students’ nuanced understanding of AI integration in learning, with notable differences between courses reflecting their distinct experiences with AI tools."
