# The DLS developer's reduced coaching-support model for K-1 teachers in year two may not meet all teachers' needs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After year one, the developer's model gives K-1 teachers less coach support while adding support for 2nd-grade teachers, but K-1 teachers varied significantly in implementation and may continue to need consistent external coaching support. [→ Mai Chou Vang 2020](#mai-chou-vang-2020)

## Evidence

### Mai Chou Vang 2020

Mai Chou Vang, Zareen Kasad, & Viki M. Young. (2020). Implementing and scaling Differentiated Literacy System: A case of evaluators' voices channeling outside-in and bottom-up perspectives for equity and continuous improvement. American Evaluation Association, Eval2020 Reimagined Conference. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/244ec2ff-be10-4063-aad1-f798a4a45369

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from the multi-year qualitative evaluation across 21 schools. The article reports the developer's model provides DLS coaches with less time supporting K-1 teachers and more time supporting 2nd-grade teachers new to DLS in the second year.

> "our findings demonstrate that K–1 teachers varied significantly in implementation levels after the first year and may continue to need consistent external coaching support due to a wide range in student readiness each year."
