## Evidence

### Burch 2016

Burch, P., Good, A., & Heinrich, C. (2016). Improving Access to, Quality, and the Effectiveness of Digital Tutoring in K–12 Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373715592706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational ratings from a standardized instrument across 185 full tutoring sessions (46 digital) over 4 years in five urban districts. Digital sessions averaged lower on rigor indicators such as "Ask students why, how, or what if questions" (.24 vs .52) and "Challenge students to push themselves intellectually" (.30 vs .50).

> "Comparing the average rating of digital and nondigital tutoring sessions across 4 years (2009–2013), the digital tutoring sessions were rated low overall as well as in comparison with nondigital settings."
