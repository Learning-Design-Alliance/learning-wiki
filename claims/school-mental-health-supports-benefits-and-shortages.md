## Evidence

### dePaoli 2023

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

`q3 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports multiple studies on counselor benefits, plus a meta-analysis of school-based mental health services in elementary schools finding positive effects on students' mental health.

> "Studies have found that counselors reduce disciplinary incidents and disciplinary recidivism, improve teachers’ perceptions of school climate and student behavior, and increase boys’ academic achievement."

### dePaoli 2023 (2)

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports national staffing data: the counselor ratio of 451:1 versus the recommended 250:1, a psychologist ratio of 1,162:1 versus a recommended 500:1, and that only 42% of schools offered mental health treatment services in 2019–20.

> "The American School Counselor Association recommends a student-to-counselor ratio of 250:1, but nationally, schools average a ratio of 451:1."
