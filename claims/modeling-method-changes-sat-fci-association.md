# On a sample multilevel dataset, the estimated association between SAT verbal scores and FCI post-test scores changes sign and size depending on whether data are disaggregated, aggregated, or modeled with HLM

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Disaggregated single-level regression found a small negative association (−0.067) between student SAT verbal scores and FCI post-test scores. [→ Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2019](#ben-van-dusen-and-jayson-nissen-2019)
`q1 i?` Aggregated course-level regression found a negative association (−0.2), three times larger than the disaggregated coefficient. [→ Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2019 (2)](#ben-van-dusen-and-jayson-nissen-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2019

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen. (2019). Modernizing use of regression models in physics education research: A review of hierarchical linear modeling. Physical Review Physics Education Research 15, 020108. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.15.020108

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

A worked example analysis of a sample multilevel dataset (Table II) shows the disaggregation model: "A single-level linear regression of the disaggregated data from our example data found that student SA T verbal scores had a small negative association ( −0.067) with FCI post-test scores," plotted in Fig. 1(b).

> "A single-level linear regression of the disaggregated data from our example data found that student SA T verbal scores had a small negative association ( −0.067) with FCI post-test scores"

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2019 (2)

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen. (2019). Modernizing use of regression models in physics education research: A review of hierarchical linear modeling. Physical Review Physics Education Research 15, 020108. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.15.020108

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The same example dataset aggregated to course means was reanalyzed; the article states "A single-level linear regression of the aggregated course-level data finds that course mean SA T verbal scores have a negative association ( −0.2) with course mean FCI post-test scores," a coefficient 3 times larger than the disaggregation model's.

> "A single-level linear regression of the aggregated course-level data finds that course mean SA T verbal scores have a negative association ( −0.2) with course mean FCI post-test scores"

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2019 (3)

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen. (2019). Modernizing use of regression models in physics education research: A review of hierarchical linear modeling. Physical Review Physics Education Research 15, 020108. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.15.020108

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The HLM analysis of the same sample dataset found, per the article, a "positive association (0.2) between student SA T verbal and FCI post-test scores" alongside a negative class-mean association, plotted in Fig. 1(d); the article predicts the highest post-test scores for high-SAT students in low-average-SAT classes.

> "Analyzing the data from Table II with HLM shows a positive association (0.2) between student SA T verbal and FCI post-test scores and a negative association ( −0.2) between class mean SA T V erbal and FCI post-test scores"
