## Evidence

### Ait Baha 2024

Ait Baha, T., El Hajji, M., Es-Saady, Y., & Fadili, H. (2024). The impact of educational chatbot on student learning experience. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-12166-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in Moroccan public schools with 109 secondary students in three classes: a control class using a traditional approach and two experimental classes using digital content and the chatbot-based method. The article reports preliminary findings that chatbot use enhanced the learning experience, "allowing them to study at their own speed with less stress". No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "Preliminary findings indicate that employing chatbots can greatly enhance student learning experiences by allowing them to study at their own speed with less stress, saving them time, and keeping them motivated."
