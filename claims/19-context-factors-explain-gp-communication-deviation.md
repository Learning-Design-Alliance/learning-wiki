## Evidence

### Essers G 2011

Essers G, van Dulmen S, van Weel C, van der Vleuten C, Kramer A. (2011). Identifying context factors explaining physician's low performance in communication assessment: an explorative study in general practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-138

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Explorative qualitative observational analysis of a purposive sample of 17 videotaped real-life Dutch GP consultations from a NIVEL 2007-2008 database, rated on the MAAS-Global and analyzed by three researchers with inductive reasoning. The authors report, "We found 19 context factors in GP consultations that could be related to low scores on the MAAS-Global."
