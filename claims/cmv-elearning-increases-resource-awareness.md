## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic regression model with random intercept, controlling for prior CMV education and length of practice, examined awareness of resources from the Department of Health, Cerebral Palsy Alliance Research Institute and CMV Australia. Awareness rose from 10% to 97%.

> "Prior to completing the course (T1) very few participants (37/361, 10%) were aware of available CMV education resources. At T2, 232/238 (97%) (P ≤ 0.001) of respondents were aware of how to access free CMV education resources"
