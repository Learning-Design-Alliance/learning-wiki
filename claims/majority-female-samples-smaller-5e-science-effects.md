Exploratory meta-regression Model 1 on the science domain (R² = 36.6%): the only significant predictor was sample sex composition, with majority-female samples showing smaller effects than evenly distributed samples.

> "effects differed significantly for samples that were comprised of a majority of female students (56–99%) compared to samples that had an even distribution of male and female students (55–45% female students; b = −0.59, SE = 0.26, p = .05)"
