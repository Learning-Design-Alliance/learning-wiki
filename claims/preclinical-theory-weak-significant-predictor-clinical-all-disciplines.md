### Andreani Y 2025

Andreani Y, Gunaratne BC, Sadr A, Martin FE, Divnic‐Resnik T, Sukumar S. (2025). Do Pre‐Clinical Summative Assessments Predict a Student's Clinical Performance? A Retrospective Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective longitudinal cohort analysis of summative marks from six DMD cohorts (2013–2019), n = 413 for theory/clinic analysis. Pearson correlation gave theory–clinic r 2 of 0.13 (p = 0.01) for Periodontics, 0.15 (p = 0.00) for Restorative and 0.17 (p = 0.00) for Endodontics; the article calls these "weak but significant predictor of clinical marks".

> "correlation analysis identified only two significant predictors of clinical performance: (i) pre‐clinical theory marks in DMD 2 were a weak but significant predictor of clinical marks in all three disciplines"
