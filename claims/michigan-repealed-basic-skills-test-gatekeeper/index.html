The Education Trust 2022¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: Michigan. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The policy scan's licensure criterion reports that Michigan "reviewed the licensure process and determined that the basic skills test/SAT required for Michigan teaching certification served as a gatekeeper", and the MDE worked with the legislature in 2018 to repeal the pre-student-teaching requirement. Descriptive policy account; no effect size.

"Michigan reviewed the licensure process and determined that the basic skills test/SAT required for Michigan teaching certification served as a gatekeeper for candidates entering preparation programs and disproportionately excluded people of color"
