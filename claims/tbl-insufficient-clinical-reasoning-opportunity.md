## Evidence

### Burgess A. 2018

Burgess A., Roberts C., Ayton T., Mellis C. (2018). Implementation of modified team-based learning within a problem based learning medical curriculum: a focus group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1172-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group data from the same 14-student qualitative study; students compared TBL and PBL problem-solving phases. One student noted PBL gave "a lot more focus" to areas like "working the management side of things" not covered in TBL.

> "For other students, the problem solving step within the TBL did not give the same opportunity for discussion on clinical reasoning compared to PBL."
