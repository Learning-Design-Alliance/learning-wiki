Çakiroğlu, Ü., Güven, O., Yaman, H., & Mazlum Güven, E. (2025). Scientific habits of arguing minds: enhancing online argumentation to foster SHOM. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10474-1

Pearson product-moment correlation between SHOM Survey post-test scores and final argumentation rubric scores for 33 teacher candidates, after checking normality, linearity and homoscedasticity assumptions; r = 0.462, a medium positive correlation.

> "There was a medium, positive correlation between the two variables (r = 0.462, n = 33, p < 0.005) with high levels of SHOM associated with higher levels of argumentation skills."
