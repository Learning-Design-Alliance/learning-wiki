## Evidence

### Surcouf JW 2018

Surcouf JW, Mumphrey CG, Barkemeyer BM, Buis M, Gupta RW, Olister S, Patrick-Esteve J, Rivera D, Zeringue A, Chauvin SW. (2018). Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Boot Camp: A Preparatory Curriculum for Pediatric Residents. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10720

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-rotation 12-item questionnaire administered to residents who completed the boot camp between November 2014 and February 2017; 46 of 57 residents (80.70%) responded. Descriptive percentages only, as the article reports "Quantitative results are reported as descriptive statistics."

> "Session 1 (Introduction to the NICU and TPN/Calculations): 82.61%, Session 2 (Respiratory and On-Call Problems): 78.26%, and Session 3 (Simulation): 82.60%, with 86.95% combined very useful and extremely useful responses for the overall boot camp"
