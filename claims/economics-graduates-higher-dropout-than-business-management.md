### Cristina Mollica and Lea Petrella 2015

Cristina Mollica and Lea Petrella. (2015). Bayesian binary quantile regression for the analysis of Bachelor-Master transition. [doi:10.1080/02664763.2016.1263835](https://doi.org/10.1080/02664763.2016.1263835)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BQR results on the Sapienza cohort show significant Economics degree-class effects over all quantile levels; descriptively, non-continuation was 36.2% for Economics versus 26.5% for Business Management.

> "Bachelor graduates in Economics have a higher probability to conclude their study experience after the ﬁrst cycle than undergraduates in Business Management."
