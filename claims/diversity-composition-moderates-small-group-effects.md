# Small-group learning effects vary by classroom gender and ethnic diversity, with effects smaller in female-dominated and predominately White classrooms

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Female-dominated classrooms (more than 60%) showed a much lower effect size (d = 0.23) than classrooms that did not report gender diversity (d = 0.53) and male-dominated classrooms (d = 0.42). [→ Kalaian 2018](#kalaian-2018)
`q3 i1` Predominately White classrooms (more than 60%) showed a smaller, nonsignificant effect size (d = 0.26) than studies not reporting ethnic diversity (d = 0.49). [→ Kalaian 2018 (2)](#kalaian-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Kalaian 2018

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

`q3 · i1`

Subgroup analysis of classroom gender diversity (Table 2): predominately female classrooms (3 effect sizes, d = 0.23, p = 0.15, nonsignificant), predominately male (10, d = 0.42, p = 0.01), not reported (13, d = 0.53, p = 0.00).

> "The remaining three effect sizes were extracted from the primary studies with female-dominated classrooms (more than 60%) and had much lower d-value of 0.23 than the studies that did not report the gender composition of the classrooms"

### Kalaian 2018 (2)

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

`q3 · i1`

Subgroup analysis of classroom ethnic diversity (Table 2): predominately White classrooms (5 effect sizes, d = 0.26, p = 0.08, nonsignificant) versus studies not reporting ethnic diversity (21, d = 0.49, p = 0.00). No studies had predominately minority students.

> "Five effect sizes were extracted from the primary studies that were predominately White (more than 60%) with a d-value of 0.26, and the majority of the primary studies did not report the ethnic diversity of the classrooms"
