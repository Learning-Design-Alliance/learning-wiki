## Evidence

### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Validation experiment: independent cohorts of mice (n = 6 per group) conditioned at 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 or 0.5 mA; CS-only retention tests showed 0.1-0.2 mA groups with persistently low avoidance and 0.4-0.5 mA groups failing, with "0.3 mA providing conditions that optimally supported active avoidance expression".

> "behavioural output in the PBA paradigm is strongly shaped by threat intensity, with 0.3 mA providing conditions that optimally supported active avoidance expression underthe present task conditions."

### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026 (2)

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control analysis of the same validation experiment separated stay trials (conditioning stay rate: 46%; retention test stay rate: 15%) from cue-evoked entries; restricted to cue-evoked platform entries, 0.3 mA mice "were the only group to con- sistently enter the safe zone within the 5 s avoidance success window".

> "mice conditioned at 0.3 mA continued to show the strongest cue-evoked avoid- ance performance and, by retention test 3, were the only group to con- sistently enter the safe zone within the 5 s avoidance success window even when analyses were restricted to cue-evoked platform entries"
