Evidence¶

Kim 2012¶

Kim, N. H., & Park, J. (2012). The effects of the family-involved SDLMI on academic engagement and goal attainment of middle school students with disabilities who exhibit problem behavior. International Journal of Special Education, 27(1), 117-127. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979719

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Using the Goal Attainment Scale measured five times (pretest, three times during intervention, and posttest), the study found "a significant difference in goal attainment between the two groups," with experimental students showing "significantly higher attainment in Korean language."

"there was a significant difference in goal attainment between the two groups (t=3.12, p<.01). The students in the experimental group showed significantly higher attainment in Korean language compared to those in the control group."

Kim 2012 (2)¶

Kim, N. H., & Park, J. (2012). The effects of the family-involved SDLMI on academic engagement and goal attainment of middle school students with disabilities who exhibit problem behavior. International Journal of Special Education, 27(1), 117-127. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979719

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Progress data taken five times throughout the experiment showed experimental students' goal attainment "showed a gradual increase as the intervention proceeded," with "ten students among 11" reaching the acceptable score of 50 points.

"The students in the experimental group showed a gradual increase as the intervention proceeded. Ten students among 11 in the experimental group reached an acceptable score (50 points)."
