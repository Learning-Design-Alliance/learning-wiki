Descriptive analysis of state education spending data reported in the brief's Key Conclusion #1 for program design. It finds the spending difference "has grown since the adoption of the Local Control Funding Formula in 2014" but remained around $1,000 more per pupil, consistent with PPIC analysis.

"in alignment with recent analysis by the Public Policy Institute of California, the difference was still relatively low in recent years—only around $1,000 more per pupil was spent on English Learners."
