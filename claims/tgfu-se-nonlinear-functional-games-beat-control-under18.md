## Evidence

### Tian Qi 2026

Tian Qi, Liu Yaowen, Wang Ziyu, Qin Gang. (2026). Comparative effects of physical education teaching models on motor skill development in school-aged children and adolescents: a systematic review and network meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1905122

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Network meta-analysis of 13 under-18 school-PE studies (random-effects, netmeta), Table 4. Hybrid TGfU-SE led: "Hybrid TGfU-SE had the highest P-score in the under-18 primary network (P-score = 0.871; SMD = 1.35, 95% CI 0.60 to 2.11)."

> "Relative to Control, Hybrid TGfU-SE, Nonlinear, and Functional games had positive estimates with 95% confidence intervals that excluded zero."
