## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2020

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2020). Student behavior and test security in online conceptual assessment. arXiv:1912.05698v1 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of their post-administration Google searches of QMCA and CUE item prompts. No student-posted prompts or solutions were found, but searches pulled up publications whose supplemental material contained grading rubrics, buried at the end of long documents and not clearly marked.

> "this suggests that the greate st threat to the security of these RBAs in an online format may actually be our own publications combined with the fact that the premier PER publication venue is open access."
