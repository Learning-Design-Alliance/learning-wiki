## Evidence

### Yang Hao 2026

Yang Hao, Jing Xiaosha, Guan Yujia, Yang Min, Bai Ting, Xing Lingling, Mu Dan, Wang Xihong, Liu Hongqian. (2026). A BOPPPS‐PBL Nursing Innovation Course for Newly Recruited Nurses: A Single‐Group Quantitative Pilot Evaluation of Creativity, Self‐Efficacy and 1‐Year Project Outputs. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70734

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the same 60-nurse single-group pilot, using a locally modified five-response-category GSES. The article reports "mean difference 3.70, 95% CI 0.84–6.56" with dz = 0.33, a small effect. The modified GSES format was not formally validated.

> "The mean total self‐efficacy score increased from 38.78 ± 8.64 before the course to 42.48 ± 6.10 after the course (mean difference 3.70, 95% CI 0.84–6.56; t = 2.588, p = 0.012; Cohen's dz = 0.33)."
