Evidence¶

Rosyida 2018¶

Rosyida, F., & Ghufron, M. A. (2018). Herringbone and Tri Focus Steve Snyder Technique: The Techniques for Teaching Reading Comprehension Viewed from Students' Reading Habit. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 603-616. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11341a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA on post-test scores of 51 ninth-graders whose reading habit was measured by a 34-item questionnaire and split at the median into high and low groups. The between-rows (reading habit) effect was significant as printed.

"The significant difference can be seen from Fobs=43.3862 which was higher than Fα=4.0471. It means that the students having high reading habit have better reading comprehension than those having low reading habit."
