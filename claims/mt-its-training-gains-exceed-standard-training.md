## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a mixed-factor ANOVA of pre- and post-training sessions, the performance gain in the MT-ITS condition was significantly stronger than in the Standard condition for both shooting accuracy and aiming lead (Training x Condition: accuracy F(1, 26) = 4.99, p < .05). ## Evidence

### Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski 2023

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski. (2023). Moving-Target Intelligent Tutoring System for Marksmanship Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00308-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Controlled experiment in the DRDC Virtual Immersive Soldier Simulator with 28 Reserve Canadian Army novices split equally between MT-ITS and Standard conditions. The mixed-factor ANOVA showed the "performance gain in MT-ITS training condition" was significantly stronger for accuracy and lead; Table 4 reports partial η2 = 0.16 for Training x Condition on both measures.

> "There was also a significant interaction of training with condition, F(1, 26) = 4.99, p < .05, indicating that the performance gain in MT-ITS training condition—for both shooting accuracy boost and a reduction in aiming lead—was significantly stronger than in the Standard condition."
