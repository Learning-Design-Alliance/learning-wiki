Evidence¶

Fong 2018¶

Fong, A., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2018). Examination of data usability options for assessing eligibility for higher education in California. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592524

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Availability analysis of over 18 million CALPADS course records for 428,410 2015 graduates across school years 2011-12 to 2014-15. The report states "20% of the students had no course records in CALPADS for school year 2011–12 but had course records for all other years," consistent with the 62% voluntary LEA submission rate that year.

"Specifically, while 62% of the 2015 graduates had taken at least one course during each of the school years of the analysis, 20% of the students had no course records in CALPADS for school year 2011–12 but had course records for all other years."
