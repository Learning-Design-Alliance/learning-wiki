## Evidence

### Arachchilage 2013

Arachchilage, N. A. G., Love, S., & Maple, C. (2013). Can a Mobile Game Teach Computer Users to Thwart Phishing Attacks. International Journal for Infonomics (IJI), Volume 6, Issues 3/4. [doi:10.20533/iji.1742.4712.2013.0083](https://doi.org/10.20533/iji.1742.4712.2013.0083)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group contrast in the think-aloud user study (20 game participants vs 20 website readers), reported in the Results section: the game condition shows the greatest improvement (by 29%) per Figure 3, versus 3 percent for the website group. The article hedges with somewhat effective.

> "Therefore, the current study concluded that the mobile game prototype is somewhat effective in teaching computer users to thwart phishing attacks than traditional online learning."
