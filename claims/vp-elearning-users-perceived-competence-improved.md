`q2 i3` Among students who used the e-learning tool, perceived competence improved significantly in almost every item from baseline to follow-up, with standardized effect sizes ranging from 1.6 to 0.4. [→ Schulz Marco S. 2025](#schulz-marco-s-2025)

## Evidence

### Schulz Marco S. 2025

Schulz Marco S., Pantelmann Ragna, Hillebrecht Anna-Lena, Reissmann Daniel R. (2025). Bridging the gap to clinical practice: a concept for virtual patients in preclinical education in prosthetic dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08097-4

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Prospective controlled trial with self-administered competence questionnaires at baseline and follow-up; 24 users analyzed. The article reports the overall agreement increase and that effect sizes ranged from 1.6 to 0.4 in this group, i.e. large standardized effects.

> "From baseline to follow-up, the overall agreement in users increased from 8.3% to 75.0% and a statistically significant improvement in almost every item was observed after applying the e-learning tool (Table 2)."
