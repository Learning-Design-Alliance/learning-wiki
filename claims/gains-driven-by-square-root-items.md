## Evidence

### Liu and Koedinger 2017

Ran Liu, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2017). Closing the loop: Automated data-driven cognitive model discoveries lead to improved instruction and learning gains. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 9, No 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1155896

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analyses of post-test subscores among the 91 analyzed students. On the three square-root problems condition was significant (β = 0.13, p = 0.034); on problems not requiring a square root it was not (β = 0.06, p = 0.085), suggesting gains were "predominantly driven by performance on the problems that required applying a square root."

> "The effect of condition on this subscore (β = 0.13, p = 0.034) was significant in a regression that again controlled for pre-test scores."
