Content validity analysis of the TURP questionnaire completed by the same fourteen experts, computing I-CVI per item and S-CVI/Ave. The article reports "The S-CVI/Ave for all 16 items was 0.71" versus 0.92 for the 12 core items, and separately that domain-specific analysis was justified because non-modeled items "may underestimate the educational value of the model".

> "The S-CVI/Ave for all 16 items was 0.71, and the Adjusted S-CVI/Ave for the 12 core skill items was 0.92 (Table 2 )."
