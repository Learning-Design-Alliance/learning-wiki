q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the article's section on conceptualizing education's role, illustrating that the same educational practice is framed oppositely across disciplines. The article cites psychology and sociology strands of research for the two framings; no empirical data are reported in this comment.

"An example of this potential paradox is the topic of differentiated instruction within classrooms and differentiated curricula between tracks, which may be studied as a potential remedy for unequal achievement in one strand of research (e.g. psychology30), while they are studied as a catalyst for unequal achievement in a different discipline (e.g. sociology 31)."
