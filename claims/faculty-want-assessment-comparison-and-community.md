### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The phenomenographic analysis identified the Want Help family, discussed by 12 of 24 participants, with the Comparison subcategory (7 faculty) on benchmarking scores against other schools and the Community subcategory (6 faculty) on interacting with other faculty and accessing forums or case studies.

> "They want to norm their assessment results by comparing to others and interacting with faculty from other schools to learn about how they do assessment."
