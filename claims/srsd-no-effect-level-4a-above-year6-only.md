## Evidence

### Torgerson 2014

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Ainsworth, H., Buckley, H., Heaps, C., Hewitt, C., & Mitchell, N. (2014). Improving Writing Quality: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581140

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis repeating the primary model using only pupils at Level 4a and above (94 intervention, 87 control) who received the intervention only in Year 6. The report found "no evidence of a difference" with an effect size of approximately 0.00.

> "There was no evidence of a difference in extended writing score between the allocated groups, with a non-significant increase of less than 0.01 marks for those in the intervention group when compared with those in the control group (p =1.00, 95% CI: -1.38 to 1. 38)."
