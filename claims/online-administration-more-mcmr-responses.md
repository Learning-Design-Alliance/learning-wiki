## Evidence

### Alexis Olsho 2021

Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Charlotte Zimmerman, Andrew Boudreaux, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2021). Online test administration results in students selecting more responses to multiple-choice-multiple-response items. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.02137](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.02137)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of Institution 1 paper (N = 1697) and online (N = 1404) PIQL administrations across eight academic terms. The article reports a Mann-Whitney U test with "p < . 001 for all items" and "rank-biserial correlation coefﬁcients between −0.0799 and −0.258".

> "in the average number of answer choices is statistically signiﬁcant (Mann-Whitney U test, p < . 001 for all items), with effect size ranging from small to medium (rank-biserial correlation coefﬁcients between −0.0799 and −0.258)"
