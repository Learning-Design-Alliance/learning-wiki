### Youjie Chen 2026

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression comparisons of engagement-type proportions across institution selectivity, with standard errors clustered at student and class levels. Highly selective institutions showed higher deep engagement (19.36% vs. 12.42%, t(199)=5.75, p<0.01) and lower exam-driven engagement (10.65% vs. 36.98%).

> "In highly selective universities, the proportion of deep engagement and routine-learning engagement was significantly higher (deep engagement: 19.36% vs. 12.42%,𝑡( 199)= 5.75, 𝑝< 0.01; routine-learning engagement: 58.88% vs. 40.41%, 𝑡( 199)= 4.42, 𝑝< 0.01), while the proportion of exam-driven engagement was lower (10.65% vs. 36.98%, 𝑡( 199)=− 5.91, 𝑝< 0.01)."
