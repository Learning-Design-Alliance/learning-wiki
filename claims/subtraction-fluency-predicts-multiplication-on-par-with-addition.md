## Evidence

### Rautiainen 2026

Rautiainen, J., Aro, T., Aro, M., Tolvanen, A., & Koponen, T. (2026). Hierarchical Nature of Arithmetic Skills: Similarities and Differences Between Arithmetic Operations. Journal of Educational Psychology, Early online. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001066

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subtraction fluency latent SEM model in the longitudinal study (n = 207). The article reports "The subtraction construct strongly predicted multiplication skill" and that numerical abilities remained, similar to the addition model; the model explained R2 = .42 (R2 = .50 prior residualization).

> "The subtraction construct strongly predicted multiplication skill. After partialling out the covariance with subtraction fluency, the strong association that was previously present in the baseline from numerical abilities to multiplication fluency remained, similar to the addition model."
