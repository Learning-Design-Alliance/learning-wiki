`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Descriptive synthesis of 56 articles (96 entries) summarized in Table 1, with relative loss scores calculated by the reviewers from summary measures; the reviewers report the mean relative loss scores and conclude "much of what is taught in school is remembered."

> "The overall mean loss scores for these tasksare -16.17 (n = 52) for recognition, - 28.25 (n = 22) for recall, and -13.32 (n = 26) for cognitive skills. These means support our observation that much of what is taught in school is remembered."
