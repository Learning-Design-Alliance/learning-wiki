## Evidence

### Esteves 2021

Esteves, A., McConnell, M., Ferretti, E., Garber, A., & Fung-Kee-Fung, K. (2021). "When in Doubt, Ask the Patient": A Quantitative, Patient-Oriented Approach to Formative Assessment of CanMEDS Roles. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11169

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A G-theory variance decomposition across 69 patient questionnaires and 70 fellow questionnaires found that "the interaction between trainee and competency (facet t∗c) accounted for a large portion of variance across both self (89%) and patient (93%) questionnaires". This indicates both trainees and patients discriminated performance across the three competencies.

> "While we found 0% variance attributable to differences among our trainees, the interaction between trainee and competency (facet t∗c) accounted for a large portion of variance across both self (89%) and patient (93%) questionnaires."
