## Evidence

### Ku Grace Marie V. 2014

Ku Grace Marie V., Kegels Guy. (2014). Integrating chronic care with primary care activities: enriching healthcare staff knowledge and skills and improving glycemic control of a cohort of people with diabetes through the First Line Diabetes Care Project in the Philippines. Global Health Action. https://doi.org/10.3402/gha.v7.25286

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-assessment on a 5-point Likert scale gathered before and after the workshop from the same 110 healthcare workers, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test; all three skill domains improved significantly "in both sites". Median biopsychosocial domain scores rose from 1.32 to 3.41.

> "significant increase in self-assessment of biopsychosocial and communication skills (p<0.001), anthropometric measurement and interpretation skills (p<0.001), and skills on basic clinical and non-pharmacological approaches to diabetes (p<0.001) in both sites"
