q4 · i2 · review · r2

A series of statistical meta-analyses of parental involvement research; a 2017 meta-analysis using only studies with sophisticated controls found overall parental involvement associated with better school outcomes by 0.52 standard deviation units for Latino students. A review of 51 studies found a positive relationship across backgrounds and ages.

"A series of statistical meta-analyses also found significant relationships between parental involvement and better outcomes for students across racial backgrounds, with effect sizes ranging from 0.2 to 0.75 standard deviations."
