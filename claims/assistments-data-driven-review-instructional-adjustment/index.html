Evidence¶

Feng 2025¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

q2 · i? · design · r2

Triangulation of system use data, instructional logs, and post-intervention survey for Step 3 of the four-step routine. The article reports teachers used reports to reteach topics (65.8% of changes), select individual students (32.4%), or groups (20.0%).

"92 percent of teachers reviewed at least half of the ASSISTments assignment reports for their classes (system data)"

Feng 2025 (2)¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-intervention survey and log data for Step 4. The article states projecting anonymized reports can "help make the class discussion more inclusive, build a culture of collective learning," and may specifically benefit lower-performing students.

"Many teachers (74.1%, post-intervention survey) indicated that they used ASSISTments reports to determine which problems to review. Teachers also frequently projected these reports for the class— 89.7 percent reported doing so during the school year"
