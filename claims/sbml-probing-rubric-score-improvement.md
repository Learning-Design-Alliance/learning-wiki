## Evidence

### Theerakulpisut Saralee 2026

Theerakulpisut Saralee, Tengtrisorn Supaporn, Singha Penny, Rattanalert Narisa. (2026). Simulation-based mastery learning for nasolacrimal duct probing in ophthalmology residency: a prospective pre–post educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09213-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post comparison in the same 20 residents using a 40-point performance rubric adapted from the Ophthalmology Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric; Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed significant improvement (p < 0.001).

> "The median rubric score before training was 16 (interquartile range [IQR]: 13.5–19), increasing to 26.5 (IQR: 24–29.5) after training (p < 0.001)."
