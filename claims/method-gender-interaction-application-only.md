## Evidence

### Obafemi 2015

Obafemi, D. T. A. (2015). Bridging Gender Gap in the Physics Classroom: The Instructional Method Perspective. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The 4x2 ANCOVA of application of Light waves (Table 4) shows a significant Method * Gender interaction, F = 3.488 against a critical value of 2.76; no effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 shows that the interaction of Method and Gender is  significant since its calculated F 3,46 value is 3.488 at degree of freedom of 3,46 and probability level of 0.05 against the F 3,46 critical value of 2.76."

### Obafemi 2015 (2)

Obafemi, D. T. A. (2015). Bridging Gender Gap in the Physics Classroom: The Instructional Method Perspective. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079043

> "Table 8  shows that the interaction of Method and Gender is  not significant since its calculated F 3,46 value is 0.793 at degree of freedom of 3,46 and probability level of 0.05 against the F 3,46 critical value of 2.76."
