Thematic analysis of two one-hour exploratory workshops with German school principals (N= 30, 15 per session) after a FACET tool demonstration. The finding that the teacher-in-the-loop design "was viewed positively, as it preserves professional judgment" was one of three recurring themes on practical integration.
