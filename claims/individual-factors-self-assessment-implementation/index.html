Evidence¶

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan 2023¶

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan. (2023). A Systematic Review on Students' Perceptions of Self-Assessment: Usefulness and Factors Influencing Implementation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09799-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of four studies: motivation and engagement in self-assessment were strongly influenced by perceived positive impact such as identifying strengths and weaknesses; the finding spans mathematics and writing.

"Students who found self-assessment beneficial for their learning were willing to use it frequently and voluntarily (42)."

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan 2023 (2)¶

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan. (2023). A Systematic Review on Students' Perceptions of Self-Assessment: Usefulness and Factors Influencing Implementation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09799-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Yan, Brown et al. (2020), that higher self-efficacy predicted four self-assessment behaviours; it also notes high self-efficacy could lead students to overestimate their own performance.

"Yan, Brown et al.'s (2020) study explicitly demonstrated that students with higher self-efficacy in self-assessment were more likely to conduct four self-assessment behaviours, including seeking external feedback through monitoring, seeking external feedback through inquiry, seeking internal feedback, and self-reflection (43)."
