## Evidence

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous 8-item post-module survey (Cronbach's alpha 0.82–0.89) completed by all 147 students after the nine-week module. Exact probability tests showed no significant between-group differences on clarity or preparedness items.

> "82.4–94.3% reported clear explanations (p = 0.140–0.595). Tutor preparedness and engagement received high positive ratings (99.3% agreed or strongly agreed; p = 0.297)"
