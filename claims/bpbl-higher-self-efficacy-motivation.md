## Evidence

### Shimizu Ikuo 2019

Shimizu Ikuo, Nakazawa Hideyuki, Sato Yoshihiko, Wolfhagen Ineke H. A. P., Könings Karen D. (2019). Does blended problem-based learning make Asian medical students active learners?: a prospective comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1575-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of conditions on a 13-item questionnaire on tutorial group functioning, analysed with ANOVA (Table 2): motivation for learning (p = 0.032) and self-efficacy (p = 0.007) "significantly higher in the bPBL condition than in original PBL condition". Means were 3.479 vs 3.521 and 3.319 vs 3.597. No effect size is attributable from the table layout, so impact is uncoded.

> "Scores on the scales motivation for learning and self-efficacy were significantly higher in the bPBL condition than in original PBL condition (p = 0.032 and 0.007, respectively)."
