## Evidence

### Henriikka Vartiainen 2025

Henriikka Vartiainen, Nicolas Pope, Juho Kahila, Sonsoles López-Pernas, and Matti Tedre. 2024. Classroom Activities and New Classroom Apps for Enhancing Children's Understanding of Social Media Mechanisms. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. 1, 1, Article 1 (January 2024). Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.16494](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.16494)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative content analysis (inductive, Cohen's kappa consensus 83.2%, p < 0.001) of open-ended pre/post answers to the Jarmo/Instagram question. Category-2 responses describing "the traces and data collection mechanisms" tripled from 9.8% to 30.9%.

> "While the pre-test showed scant signs of recognizing the depth and variety of data traces (9.8%), the number of responses that explained the traces and data collection mechanisms more than tripled in the post-test (30.9%)"
