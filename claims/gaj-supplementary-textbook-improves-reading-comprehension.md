# Supplementary textbook use (Gaj) improves reading comprehension of Iranian female high school EFL learners compared with the national textbook alone

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a two-and-a-half-month treatment, the experimental group using Gaj scored significantly higher than the control group on a reading comprehension post-test (p = 0.04). [→ Abbasi 2015](#abbasi-2015)

## Evidence

### Abbasi 2015

Abbasi, Z., Azizifar, A., Gowhary, H., & Heidari, M. (2015). The Effect of Supplementary Materials on Reading Comprehension Improvement of Iranian Female High School EFL Learners Based on Gaj and Khate Sefid Text books. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4), 179-184. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.179

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 30 Iranian female high school EFL learners per group; the Gaj experimental group (M = 15.81, SD = 3.32) outperformed the control group (M = 13.93, SD = 3.56) on the post-test, t(58) = -2.09, p = 0.04, per Table 3.

> "The T-test analyses (Table 3) indicated that the difference in the post-test means between the Control group and the Experimental group was statistically significant, because the P value (0.04) was lower than 0.05."
