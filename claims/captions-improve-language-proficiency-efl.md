## Evidence

### BavaHarji 2014

BavaHarji, M., Alavi, Z. K., & Letchumanan, K. (2014). Captioned Instructional Video: Effects on Content Comprehension, Vocabulary Acquisition and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n5p1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same quasi-experimental study, the captioned group's overall post-MET performance was significantly better, "t = -6.92, p = .000", with the experimental group improving from a pre-MET mean of 67.42 to a post-MET mean of 82.88, compared to 63.10 to 68.12 for the control group.

> "The t-test results confirm that the experimental group performed significantly better than the control group in the overall performance of the post-MET ( t = -6.92, p = .000)"
