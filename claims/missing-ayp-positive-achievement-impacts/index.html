q3 · i? · causal · r?

Exploratory quasi-experimental analyses using a regression discontinuity design examined NCLB accountability effects in Title I schools in two states and three districts; the report states it "found some positive achievement impacts for schools that missed AYP."

"The study found some positive achievement impacts for schools that missed AYP, but not for schools that were identified for the first year of school improvement."
