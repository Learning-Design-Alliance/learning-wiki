# Peer evaluation scores in all three domains were significantly higher in PBL sessions than in first-year book review sessions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Peer evaluation scores for degree of prior learning, contribution to group discussion, and cooperative attitude were each significantly higher in PBL sessions than in book review sessions (e.g., cooperative attitude Z = -10.363, p < 0.001). [→ Linn Zayar 2022](#linn-zayar-2022)

## Evidence

### Linn Zayar 2022

Linn Zayar, Tashiro Yasura, Morio Kunimasa, Hori Hiroki. (2022). Peer evaluations of group work in different years of medical school and academic achievement: how are they related?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03165-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of paired peer evaluation scores within the same 340 students. Prior learning (Z = -8.881, p < 0.001) and contribution to discussion (Z = -5.331, p < 0.001) were also significantly higher in PBL; cooperative attitude showed "Z = − 10.363, p < 0.001".

> "The scores of cooperative attitudes during groupworks in PBL sessions were found to be significantly higher than those in book review sessions (Z = − 10.363, p < 0.001)"
