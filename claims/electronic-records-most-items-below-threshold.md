## Evidence

### González-Chordá VM 2015

González-Chordá VM, Maciá-Soler ML. (2015). Evaluation of the quality of the teaching-learning process in undergraduate courses in Nursing. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0104-1169.0393.2606

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the electronic record checklist items completed by students on tablets during clinical practice. Five items averaged below 0.7 and "These last three are statistically significant (p-value<0.05)", the largest count of any assessment tool.

> "the items that obtained a score less than 0.7 were: "Complete correctly the medical record" (x̄=0.68; s=0.23), "Record of evolution per shift" (x̄=0.68; s=0.19), "Performing the physical examination" (x̄=0.54; s=0.16), "Record of referred activities" (x̄=0.62; s=0.12) and "Identify consultations diagnostics" (x̄=0.48; s=0.32). These last three are statistically significant (p-value<0.05)."
