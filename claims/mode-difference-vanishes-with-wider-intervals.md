### Frasier 1915

Numerical regrouping example: the article groups the same data in class intervals of eight (Table 1, Part 4), stated as merely illustrating how unstable the modal value is; the modes for the two sexes then lie at the same interval.

> "Here the modes for the two sexes are at the same interval, the boys having a frequency of 10 and the girls a frequency of 8."
