## Evidence

### Dilli 2016

Dilli, R. (2016). Conducting museum education activities within the context of developing a nature culture in primary school students: MTA natural history museum example. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(2), 75-84. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijese.2016.292a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 128 fourth-grade students (54 boys, 74 girls) who visited the MTA Natural History Museum; repeated measures t-test after Shapiro-Wilk normality check. Pre-test mean was 4.523 and post-test mean 5.281; the table reports t = -7.511, p = .000, df = 127. No effect size is printed.

> "From the analysis, the difference between the pre-test mean scores (𝑥̅ = 4.523) and post-test mean scores (𝑥̅ = 5.281) was significant."
