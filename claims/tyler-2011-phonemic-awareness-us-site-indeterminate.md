# Tyler et al. (2011) found no statistically significant differences between a phonemic awareness intervention and a morphosyntactic intervention at the US site (indeterminate effect)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a matched-pairs randomized design with 16 US children ages 3 to 5 with co-occurring speech and language impairments, a phonemic awareness intervention with integrated direct speech focus showed no statistically significant differences versus a morphosyntactic intervention. [→ Phonological Awareness Training 2012](#phonological-awareness-training-2012)

## Evidence

### Phonological Awareness Training 2012

Phonological Awareness Training. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Matched-pairs randomized trial at two sites (US and New Zealand); the WWC review includes the 16 children at the US site. Each group received its assigned intervention in small groups, with instruction totaling 24 hours over 12 weeks in two blocks. Effect is indeterminate per WWC criteria.

> "Tyler et al. (2011) found, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant differences between the intervention and comparison groups."
