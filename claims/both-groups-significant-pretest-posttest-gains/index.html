Evidence¶

Carolina Salamanca & Sara I Montoya 2018¶

Carolina Salamanca & Sara I Montoya. (2018). Using CLIL Approach to Improve English Language in a Colombian Higher Educational Institution. English Language Teaching, Vol. 11, No. 11. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n11p19

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test for related samples (Table 3): experimental pretest-postest mean difference -22.091, t -10.139, sig .000; control difference -20.467, t -12.283, sig .000. The article states this evidences "a learning process during the four months of difference between the dates of the pretest and the posttest".

"For both the experimental group and the control group, the value of P sig ˂ 0.05 therefore, there is evidence to reject the null hypothesis, so the means are significantly different, in this case the mean of the posttest is statistically higher than the mean of the pretest for the two groups"
