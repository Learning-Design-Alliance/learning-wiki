## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with task (grasp versus dissect) as a within-subjects factor in the randomized crossover study of 33 participants. The article reports dissection was significantly faster than grasping, with ηp2 = 0.579.

> "The dissection tasks took statistically significant less time to be performed compared to the grasping tasks (54.30 s (41.89) versus 79.21 s (63.49), F1, 27 = 37.120, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.579)."

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019 (2)

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Second dependent measure from the same repeated-measures ANOVA: damages per task. The article reports dissection tasks caused significantly more accidental ossicular chain damage than grasping tasks, with ηp2 = 0.416.

> "Moreover, the dissection tasks were associated with significantly more accidental damage to the ossicular chain than the grasping tasks (1.05 (1.26) versus 0.30 (0.86), F1, 27 = 19.249, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.416)."
