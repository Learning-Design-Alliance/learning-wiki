## Evidence

### Dreyfus 2014

Dreyfus, B. W., Gupta, A., and Redish, E. F. (2014). Applying Conceptual Blending to Model Coordinated Use of Multiple Ontological Metaphors. Department of Physics, University of Maryland. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2015.1025306](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2015.1025306)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interpretive case analysis of a Spring 2013 interview with an undergraduate pre-medical student in the IPLS course, in which she explained ATP hydrolysis using Lennard-Jones graphs. Her substance predicates ("hold energy") interleaved with location gestures support "blending the two mental spaces."

> "We take this close integration of the two metaphors as evidence of blending the two mental spaces to produce the idea (in blended space) that “releasing energy makes things go down.”"
