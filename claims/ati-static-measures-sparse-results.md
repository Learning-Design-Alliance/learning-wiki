## Evidence

### Leonard Tetzlaff 2021

Leonard Tetzlaff, Florian Schmiedek & Garvin Brod. (2021). Developing Personalized Education: A Dynamic Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09570-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of the ATI literature: the article reports that the vast majority of ATI studies found no or only ordinal interactions, and quotes Cronbach and Snow's conclusion that 'no aptitude by treatment interactions is so well confirmed that they can be used directly as guides to instruction.'

> "Over the following 40 years, a lot of research was carried out using this paradigm —with remarkably sparse robust results (see Tobias 1989)."
