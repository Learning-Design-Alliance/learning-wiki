## Evidence

### Emma Harvey 2025

Emma Harvey, Allison Koenecke, and René F. Kizilcec. (2025). "Don't Forget the Teachers": Towards an Educator-Centered Understanding of Harms from Large Language Models in Education. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3713210](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713210)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 29 semi-structured interviews with six edtech providers and 23 educators, conducted between November 2023 and February 2024. The discussion states the study highlights "gaps between conceptions of harm" with providers focused on technical harms and educators on broader impacts. No effect size is reported.

> "we highlight gaps between conceptions of harm by edtech providers (who focus primarily on technical harms) and thos e by educators (who are most concerned about harms resulting from the broader impacts caused by interactions b etween LLM-based edtech and students, educators, and/or school systems)"
