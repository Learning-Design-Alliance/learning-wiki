## Evidence

### Fan 2025

Fan, G., Liu, D., Zhang, R., & Pan, L. (2025). The impact of AI-assisted pair programming on student motivation, programming anxiety, collaborative learning, and programming performance: a comparative study with traditional pair programming and individual approaches. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00537-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on PAS scores across three time points in the 234-student quasi-experiment; the anxiety Group main effect was significant, F(3, 230) = 4.216, p = 0.006, η2p = 0.052, with "significantly greater reductions in anxiety" for both AI groups in post hoc tests.

> "Post hoc comparisons revealed that both AI-assisted groups experienced significantly greater reductions in anxiety compared to the Individual group (pGPT-3.5 = 0.005, pClaude3 = 0.002)."
