## Evidence

### Guoyang Liu 2025

Guoyang Liu, Yueyuan Zheng, Michelle Hei Lam Tsang, Yazhou Zhao & Janet H. Hsiao. (2025). Understanding the role of eye movement pattern and consistency during face recognition through EEG decoding. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00316-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level correlation analysis (Table 1, ERP band): ERP decoding TOI-AUC correlated −0.274 with overall entropy (p < 0.05) and −0.52 with face recognition RT (p < 0.001), so higher ERP decoding accuracy accompanied "more consistent eye movements during face recognition".

> "the TOI-AUC was negatively correlated with face recognition response time (RT) and eye movement overall entropy (i.e., positively correlated with eye movement consistency)."

### Guoyang Liu 2025 (2)

Guoyang Liu, Yueyuan Zheng, Michelle Hei Lam Tsang, Yazhou Zhao & Janet H. Hsiao. (2025). Understanding the role of eye movement pattern and consistency during face recognition through EEG decoding. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00316-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational finding from the same 84-participant study: "a more consistent eye movement pattern was associated with reduced face recognition time", with printed effect size r = 0.59 (p < 0.001). The correlation is with entropy, so lower entropy (higher consistency) goes with shorter RT.

> "we found a strong positive correlation between eye movement consistency and reaction time of face recognition (r(84) = 0.59, p < 0.001), indicating that a more consistent eye movement pattern was associated with reduced face recognition time."
