q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CPS graduating classes of 2003-09 (n=112,096): students with GPAs above 3.3 and ACT 26+ were the only group with greater than 50 percent four-year graduation prospects; average four-year graduation was below 10 percent for graduates with GPAs below 2.5 and ACT below 18.

"This analysis finds only a small portion of CPS college-goers—those with an average combination of above a 3.3 unweighted GPA and an ACT score of 26 and above—have four-year graduation prospects that exceed 50 percent."
