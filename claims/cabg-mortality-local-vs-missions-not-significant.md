Chi-square comparison of mortality across the same retrospective cohort of 290 CABG patients at JKCI. The local team had 18 deaths (13.7%) and missions 14 (8.8%); the article reports "no significant statistical difference (χ2 =1.782 [DF=1, N 290], P=0.126)". No effect size is printed.
