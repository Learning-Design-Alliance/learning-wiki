q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis of human neuroimaging studies. The review identifies reduced hippocampal volume as "one of the most reliable findings in children exposed to early life stress," linked across abuse, neglect, and poverty to increased psychopathology symptoms and learning deficits. No effect sizes are printed.

"one of the most reliable findings in children exposed to early life stress is reduced hippocampal volume [ 29, 53, 56]. Reduced hippocampal volume in children exposed to a range of different types of early life stress, including abuse, neglect, and living in poverty, has been linked to increased symptoms of psychopathology"

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review synthesis of human functional neuroimaging studies. The review reports cumulative stress, severe neglect, and abuse are all associated with "heightened amygdala reactivity to emotional images," attributed at least partially to altered PFC-amygdala connectivity. No effect sizes are printed.

"Cumulative stress, severe neglect from early institutionalization, and abuse have all been associated with heightened amygdala reactivity to emotional images [ 28, 108, 109]. This heightened reactiv- ity appears to be at least partially a result of altered PFC – amygdala connectivity"
