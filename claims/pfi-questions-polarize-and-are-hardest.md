## Evidence

### Allan L. Alinea 2020

Allan L. Alinea. (2020). Cognitive Reflection Test and the Polarizing Force-Identification Questions in the FCI. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aba8e9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aba8e9)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Response-distribution analysis with 163 students (triple the ~50-student earlier study) confirming the polarization pattern first identified in the authors' earlier work. The article states "majority of the students, for the six force identiﬁcation questions, eﬀectively answer only two (polarizing choices) out of the ﬁve choices", except possibly PFI question 3 where choices B and D were similar.
