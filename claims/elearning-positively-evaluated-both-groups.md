## Evidence

### Ruppert Johannes 2025

Ruppert Johannes, Krüger Rebecca, Göbel Sebastian, Wolfhard Susanna, Lorenz Liv-Annebritt, Weimer Andreas Michael, Kloeckner Roman, Waezsada Elias, Buggenhagen Holger, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Weimer Johannes Matthias. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Evaluation of the e-learning via 7-point Likert questionnaires at T2 (n=104; Cronbach's alpha 0.90). Ratings were "markedly positively" (4.1–6.8 scale points) in both groups; no effect size printed.

> "Altogether, the reference group gave a significantly higher rating than the study group (study 5.8 ± 0.9 vs. reference 6.2 ± 0.7; p = 0.04). This higher score was true for the subitems “structure of the menu” (p < 0.01)"
