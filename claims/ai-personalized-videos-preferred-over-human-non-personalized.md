## Evidence

### Tomlinson 2026

Tomlinson, B., Black, R.W., Patterson, D.J., van der Hoek, A., Ferguson, J. & Bietz, M.J. (2026). Students prefer personalized, AI-generated educational videos over non-personalized, human-recorded videos. Scientific Reports 16:21804. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-52798-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

End-of-term ranking survey in two offerings of a large online undergraduate course (493 respondents of 534 enrolled, 92.3% response rate). Post-hoc pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction showed AI-generated personalized videos were "significantly lower (more preferred)" than human-recorded non-personalized videos.

> "Across both cohorts, AI-generated personalized videos received a significantly lower (more preferred) average rank than human-recorded non-personalized videos (mean rank 2.26 vs. 2.69; Wilcoxon signed-rank test, p< 0.001)."
