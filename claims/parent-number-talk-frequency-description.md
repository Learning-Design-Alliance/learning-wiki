### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2011

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Levine Susan C. (2011). Some types of parent number talk count more than others: Relations between parents' input and children's cardinal-number knowledge. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2011.01050.x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the 7.5 hours of transcribed parent-child interaction at ages 14–30 months (N=44). Counting and cardinal-value labeling together accounted for 73 percent of total number talk; 13 parents (30 percent) never produced large-number present-object talk.
