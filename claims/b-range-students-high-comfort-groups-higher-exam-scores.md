# B-range students in high-comfort or high-activity software-matched groups had significantly higher final exam scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` B-range students (68-89% on the first midterm) in high-comfort groups scored significantly higher on the final exam than B-range students not in high-comfort groups (72.2% vs. 63.9%, p = 0.004, Student's t-test). [→ Sumer Kohli 2023](#sumer-kohli-2023)
`q2 i?` Similar gains were observed for B-range students in high-activity groups versus those not in high-activity groups (71.7% vs. 65.8%, p = 0.008). [→ Sumer Kohli 2023 (2)](#sumer-kohli-2023-2)
`q2 i?` Students in high-comfort groups overall had higher final exam scores than others (72.2% vs. 66.1%, p = 0.011). [→ Sumer Kohli 2023 (3)](#sumer-kohli-2023-3)

Correlational analysis linking evaluation-survey group-quality indicators to final exam grades in the same cohort. The article reports B-range students in high-comfort groups "saw significantly higher final exam grades than B-range students not in high-comfort groups (72.2 % vs. 63.9%, p = 0.004". No effect size printed.
