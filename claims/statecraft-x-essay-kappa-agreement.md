Reliability analysis of the essay rubric scoring: 20 of 84 scripts (24%) were randomly selected and rated by two qualified independent assessors before the second author rated the remaining essays.

> "The measures of inter-rater agreement based on Cohen’s kappa were 0.78, 0.70, 0.81, and 0.83 on criteria (1) to (4) respectively, indicating a substantial level of agreement."
