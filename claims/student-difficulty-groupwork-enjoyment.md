Quintas-Hijós Alejandro, Peñarrubia-Lozano Carlos, Bustamante Juan Carlos. (2020). Analysis of the applicability and utility of a gamified didactics with exergames at primary schools: Qualitative findings from a natural experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231269

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Open-question questionnaire administered at the end of each intervention to both control and experimental students (n = 356), quantitatively processed into descriptive contingency tables. Working in groups was the greatest difficulty (C = 38.4%, E = 44.3%), and enjoyment was the most frequently shared feeling (C = 32.3%, E = 55.2%).

> "The OQQ allowed us to find that the greatest difficulty expressed by students (control = C, Experimental = E) lay in working in groups (C = 38.4%, E = 44.3%), complexity of dances (C = 44.5%, E = 19.3%) and body expression activities (C = 5.5%, E = 17.2%)."
