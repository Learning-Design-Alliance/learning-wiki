### Brulet A 2015

Brulet A, Llorca G, Letrilliart L. (2015). Medical Wikis Dedicated to Clinical Practice: A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4392552/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of medical wikis identified through Google queries in ten languages, PubMed, Embase, Lilacs, and Web of Science. The review reports the distribution of main purposes across the 25 included wikis: "11 aimed at building an encyclopedia, five a textbook, three lessons, two oncology protocols, one a single article, and three at reporting clinical cases."
