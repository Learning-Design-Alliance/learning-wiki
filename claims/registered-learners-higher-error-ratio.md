## Evidence

### Gallego-Romero 2020

Gallego-Romero, J.M., Alario-Hoyos, C., Estévez-Ayres, I. et al. (2020). Analyzing learners' engagement and behavior in MOOCs on programming with the Codeboard IDE. Education Tech Research Dev 68, 2505–2528 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09773-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Log analysis of 613 compilation errors among 5,110 actions in three Java MOOCs. The article interprets errors as evidence of code modification since base codes were nearly error-free; per-session analysis (Table 3) shows 0.177 vs 0.091, t=-4.40, p<0.0001.

> "Table 2 shows that the ratio of compilations with errors and total number of compilations is 0.296 for registered learners and 0.185 for anonymous learners."
