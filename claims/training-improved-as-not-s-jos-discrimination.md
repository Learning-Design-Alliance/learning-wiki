# Training with longer-duration anagrams improved AS JOS discrimination in blocks 1–3 but not S JOS discrimination

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The three-way interaction of discrimination × block × group was significant for AS JOSs but not for S JOSs in blocks 1–3. [→ Burton 2023](#burton-2023)

## Evidence

### Burton 2023

Burton, O. R., Bodner, G. E., Williamson, P., & Arnold, M. M. (2023). How accurate and predictive are judgments of solvability? Explorations in a two-phase anagram solving paradigm. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 1–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09313-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (182 participants, 91 per group) mixed-factor ANOVA comparing training (16/8/4 s) versus no-training (2 s) groups in blocks 1–3. AS JOS three-way interaction η²p = .04; S JOS three-way interaction not significant (η²p = .01). "longer-duration anagrams in the training group improved AS JOS discrimination".

> "For AS JOSs, this interaction was significant, indicating that longer-duration anagrams in the training group improved AS JOS discrimination. However, the three-way interaction was not significant for S JOSs"
