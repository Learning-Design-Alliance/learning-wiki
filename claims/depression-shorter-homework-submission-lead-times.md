# In Group 1, higher depression was associated with shorter submission lead times on practical homework assignments

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Depressed students in Group 1 submitted the first practical homework closer to the deadline than non-depressed students (d=-0.818). [→ Arya VarastehNezhad 2026](#arya-varastehnezhad-2026)
`q3 i2` The same pattern appeared for the second practical homework (d=-0.565) and a log-transformed version of the first lead-time measure (d=-0.546). [→ Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (2)](#arya-varastehnezhad-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Deadline-related behavior analysis in Group 1: "the depressed group had a mean submission lead time of -3903 seconds" versus 3272 seconds for non-depressed students (d=-0.818), with a negative Spearman correlation between depression score and lead time (ρ=-0.284).

> "For the first practical homework, the depressed group had a mean submission lead time of -3903 seconds, compared with 3272 seconds in the non -depressed group, corresponding to a mean difference of -3663 minutes (p=0.0031, d=-0.818), and depression score was also negatively correlated with this submission lead time (ρ=-0.284, p=0.0280)."

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (2)

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same Group 1 section, second practical homework: depressed students "showed a shorter submission lead time" (790 vs 4603 minutes, d=-0.565); the weighted log-transformed first-homework measure showed the same direction (1.55 vs 2.87, d=-0.546).

> "depressed students again showed a shorter submission lead time than non-depressed students, with mean values of 790 minutes versus 4603 minutes, a mean difference of -3812 minutes (p=0.0378, d=-0.565), and depression status was negatively correlated with this lead-time measure (ρ=-0.254, p=0.0498)."
