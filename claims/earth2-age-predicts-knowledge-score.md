## Evidence

### Straatemeier 2008

Straatemeier, M., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Jansen, B. R. J. (2008). Children's knowledge of the earth: A new methodological and statistical approach. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2008.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on the main experiment's EARTH-2 mean scores, with age as covariate and school, gender, and condition as factors; results confirmed with nonparametric tests. Age was a "significant predictor" (F(1, 369) = 178.78, p < .001, g2 = .33); no significant school or condition differences were found, so those data were pooled.

> "Age was a signiﬁcant predictor of the score on the EARTH-2, F(1, 369) = 178.78, p < .001, g2 = .33. The solid line in Fig. 2D shows that the older children were, the more scientiﬁcally correct knowledge they had of the earth."
