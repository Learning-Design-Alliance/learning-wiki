### Dockrell et al. 2015

Julie Dockrell, Chloë Marshall and Dominic Wyse. (2015). Talk for Writing: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581467

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative literature review synthesising studies of oral language and writing; the review reports that links between oral language and writing vary by developmental phase and that oral language influence on writing is partly mediated through reading decoding and comprehension.

> "In sum, there is indicative evidence that oral language skills support writing in younger pupils and in those struggling with writing."
