## Evidence

### Veronica Pimenova 2026

Veronica Pimenova, Chris Lee, Baramee Bhakdibhumi, Simon Chu, and Andrew Begel. (2026). Leveling the Playing Field: Temporal Video Segmentation for Individuals with ADHD in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24612

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary analysis of the within-subjects study data revealed a significant floor effect on Easy tasks across all participants. After removal, the final dataset for H2, H3, and H4 consisted of N=104 observations across Medium and Hard difficulty levels.

> "performance on these tasks remained consistently high, with low error means below 0.20 (𝑀Control = 0.20,𝑀ADHD = 0.18) and low hesitations showing a restricted range of [0–1]."
