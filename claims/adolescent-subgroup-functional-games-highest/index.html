Exploratory age-stratified subgroup network (nine adolescent studies, broad outcomes, one connected component with one independent cycle). In the child/preadolescent subgroup the leading estimate depended entirely on the single Akil visual-motor integration study.

"In the adolescent subgroup, Functional games had the highest P-score (0.830; SMD = 2.568, 95% CI 1.277 to 3.859), followed by Game-based and Sport Education; between-design inconsistency remained significant (p = 0.0046)."
