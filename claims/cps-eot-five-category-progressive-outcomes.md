## Evidence

### Seeskin 2022

Seeskin, A., Massion, T., and Usher, A. (2022). Elementary on-track: Elementary school students' grades, attendance, and future outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/middle-grades-network-improvement-community

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis (Figures B.1 and B.2) of students in grades 3-8 from school year 2010-11 (N=93,617), connecting the five-level CPS EOT categories to high school graduation and immediate college enrollment as a comparison to the Condensed EOT analysis. The printed college enrollment percentages appear reversed in the report text relative to the figure labels.

> "Students in the CPS EOT On-Track category graduated high school at almost three times the rate of students who were off-track (93 percent vs. 34 percent) and enrolled in college at five times the rate of students who were off-track (14 percent vs. 70 percent)."
