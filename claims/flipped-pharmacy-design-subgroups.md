## Evidence

### Cui He 2023

Cui He, Xie Xinyu, Wang Boyang, Zhao Yuan. (2023). Effectiveness of flipped classroom in pharmacy education – a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04865-2

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by research design (Table 3). Printed effect sizes: historical control SMD = 1.58 (1.49,1.68), quasi-experiment SMD = 0.31 (0.04,0.58, p = 0.023), RCT SMD = 1.41 (1.03,1.79, single study). The article states both designs "revealed an advantage for flipped classroom over the traditional classroom."

> "For research design, historical control studies and quasi-experiments both revealed an advantage for flipped classroom over the traditional classroom."
