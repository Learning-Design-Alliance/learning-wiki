## Evidence

### Andria C. Schwortz 2025

Andria C. Schwortz, Michael Frey and Andrea C. Burrows Borowczak. (2025). How to Engage Active Pedagogy with Physics Faculty: Watch Out for Powerlessness. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 8. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16010008

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative findings from interviews with Bennett and Timothy, whose excerpts were merged into a combined theory; Timothy attributed class-size assignments to Dean and Provost levels and called standard lecture "the most cost effective (i.e., cheapest) means available."

> "The school needs more money, so administrators choose large class sizes to maximize profit (students) and minimize expense (faculty), large classes inevitably result in large lecture halls, and large lecture halls inevitably result in primarily using lecture."
