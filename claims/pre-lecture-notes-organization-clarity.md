Thematic analysis of five interviews found pre-lecture notes ensured "the lecture was not the first time the students were processing the material." Michael contrasted well-organized stat mech and condensed matter courses, where he "did know what I was supposed to be learning," with disorganized ones.

> "the most common response was centered around a few professors who would post well-organized notes before the lecture on their subject"
