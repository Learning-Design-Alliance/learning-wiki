`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive feasibility analysis of the global evaluation part of the questionnaire completed by 24 physicians after the e-learning course, using a 5-point Likert scale. The description quotes "96% of the participating physicians were satisfied with the tool"; the highest approval (99%) was for usefulness for work per the discussion.

> "In total, 96% of the participating physicians were satisfied with the tool and all participants felt that it was useful for their work. With regard to the specific content, 92% (statement 11) and 96% (statement 3) found the e-learning tool to be comprehensive."
