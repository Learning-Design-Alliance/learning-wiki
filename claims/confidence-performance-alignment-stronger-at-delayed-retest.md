`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A post-hoc analysis of the retention study's OSCE data found a stronger confidence-performance correlation at six-month follow-up (r = .69) than at baseline (r = .50, p = .024). The authors note these correlations are not adjusted for control variables and thus overestimate effects relative to the mixed-effects model.

> "In a post-hoc analysis, we found a stronger correlation between the average confidence rating and average performance at T2 (Pearson correlation coefficient = .69) compared to T1 (.5, p = .024)"
