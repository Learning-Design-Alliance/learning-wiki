## Evidence

### Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and Evaluation of a Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial on Larmor Precession Of Spin. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retention sub-study: one class of 18 students (a subset of the 72) answered four matched final-exam questions more than two months after the QuILT. Scores show "increased from 32% on the pre-test to 82% on the post-test, and was 85% on the final exam."

> "Under the strict rubric, the average cumulative scores for this group on matched questions increased from 32% on the pre-test to 82% on the post-test, and was 85% on the ﬁnal exam."
