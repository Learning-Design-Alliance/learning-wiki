## Evidence

### Keller 2023

Keller, L., Lüdtke, O., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2023). Educational Inequalities at the Intersection of Multiple Social Categories: An Introduction and Systematic Review of the Multilevel Analysis of Individual Heterogeneity and Discriminatory Accuracy (MAIHDA) Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09733-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Analytical comparison in the methods exposition of parameter counts for the two approaches with four social categories. The article states the traditional approach needs "42 parameters" while MAIHDA needs only ten to account for all main effects and all possible interactions.

> "In our example with 11 potential memberships across four social categories, 42 parameters (i.e., 40 dummy-coded variables for the social categories, an intercept term, and the variance of the residuals) would have to be modelled in the traditional regression approach, whereas only ten parameters"
