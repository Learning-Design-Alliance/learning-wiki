# Unguided practice at a formative OSCE was associated with statistically higher summative OSCE station pass rates than non-attendance, but the cohort's median matched non-attendees and the gain did not reach educational significance.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The unguided practice cohort's mean ranks were significantly higher than the other year 3 students at the summative OSCE, yet its median pass rate equalled that of non-attendees, so the gain lacked educational significance. [→ Robertson C. 2024](#robertson-c-2024)

## Evidence

### Robertson C. 2024

Robertson C., Noonan Z., Boyle J.G. (2024). A case-controlled trial evaluating the summative performance of the 3-D skills Model. BMC Medical Education 24:954. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05943-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summative results analysis of a case-controlled trial comparing formative OSCE attendees against the year cohort. Post-hoc Dunn-Bonferroni testing found the mean ranks comparing the "unguided practice cohort to the other year 3 students" significant (P < 0.01).

> "The Post-Hoc Dunn’s test using the Bonferroni corrected alpha of 0.017 indicated that the mean ranks comparing the 3-D skills cohort to the other year 3 students and the unguided practice cohort to the other year 3 students were significant(P < 0.01)."

### Robertson C. 2024 (2)

Robertson C., Noonan Z., Boyle J.G. (2024). A case-controlled trial evaluating the summative performance of the 3-D skills Model. BMC Medical Education 24:954. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05943-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade-correlation analysis in the results section: the unguided practice cohort and non-attending year 3 students "achieved the same median pass rate (11/12 stations passed)", so the statistically significant difference did not reach educational significance.

> "However, as the unguided practice cohort and the year 3 students who did not attend achieved the same median pass rate (11/12 stations passed), although statistically significant this result did not reach educational significance."
