### Jiajing Li 2025

Jiajing Li, Jianhua Zhang, Ching Sing Chai, Vivian W. Y. Lee, Xuesong Zhai, Xingwei Wang & Ronnel B. King. (2025). Analyzing the network structure of students' motivation to learn AI: a self-determination theory perspective. npj Science of Learning 10:48. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00339-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network analysis (Model 1) of survey responses from 1465 university students from 47 universities, estimating a regularized network of five motivation types with 20 edges, of which 10 had non-zero weight. The centrality estimation found "introjected regulation had the highest expected influence" (CS coefficient = 0.75). No effect size printed; impact null.

> "We found that introjected regulation had the highest expected influence. Hence, introjected regulation plays the most pivotal role in connecting and inﬂuencing other motivational factors"
