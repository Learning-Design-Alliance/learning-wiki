`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

One-way ANOVA on GenAI shortcut reliance across clusters, F(2, 820) = 22.00, p < .001; no effect size is printed for this contrast. The article itself labels the direction counterintuitive: low-use Skeptical Minimal users reported the highest shortcut reliance when they did engage.
