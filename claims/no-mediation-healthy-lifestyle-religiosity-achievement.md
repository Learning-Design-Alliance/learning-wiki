## Evidence

### Berhanu Kelemu Zelalem 2023

Berhanu Kelemu Zelalem, Shiferaw Abraham Abeje. (2023). The mediating role of healthy lifestyle behavior in the relationship between religious practice and academic achievement in university students. BMC Psychology, 11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-023-01455-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis with LISREL 8.7 on the 440-student survey tested mediation; the indirect effect of religious practice was 0 (0.63*0.0), so healthy lifestyle behavior did not intervene between religious practice and academic achievement.

> "the path results showed students' healthy lifestyle behavior has no significant effect on their academic achievement (B = 0.0), Healthy lifestyle behavior doesn't play a mediating variable on the association between religious practice and academic achievement because the total value is not greater than the direct effect."
