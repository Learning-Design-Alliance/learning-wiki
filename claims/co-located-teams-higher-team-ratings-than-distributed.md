## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the cross-over simulation study, co-located scenarios scored significantly higher than distributed scenarios on TEAM Total (items 1–11), Teamwork domain (items 3–9), and Overall Performance (item 12). [→ Morian H 2024](#morian-h-2024)

## Evidence

### Morian H 2024

Morian H, Hultin M, Lindkvist M, Creutzfeldt J, Dubois H, Jonsson K, Amorøe TN, Härgestam M. (2024). Teamwork in Rural Emergency Health Care: A Simulation-Based Cross-over Study of Co-located and Distributed Teams. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-over simulation study of 17 three-person teams at seven Swedish cottage hospitals, analyzed with generalized estimating equations adjusted for order and rater. Co-located scenarios were rated higher, e.g. "0.22 units higher, mean of items 1–11", with printed β and P values; no standardized effect size was reported.

> "Table 4 shows that the co-located scenario received significantly higher ratings for Total (0.22 units higher, mean of items 1–11), as well as in the Teamwork domain (0.28 units higher, mean of items 3–9), and for Overall Performance (0.50 units higher, item 12)."
