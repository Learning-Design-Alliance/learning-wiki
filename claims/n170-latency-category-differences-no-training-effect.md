# N170 peak latency differs by category (faces fastest, tools slowest) but is unaffected by training or training group

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` N170 peak latency showed only a Category main effect — faces shorter than characters, characters shorter than tools — with no significant group, time, or interaction effects. [→ Zhao Pei 2015](#zhao-pei-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhao Pei 2015

Zhao Pei, Li Su, Zhao Jing, Gaspar Carl M., Weng Xuchu. (2015). Training by visual identification and writing leads to different visual word expertise N170 effects in preliterate Chinese children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.09.002

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on N170 peak latencies at T5/T6 in 29 children: only a Category main effect (F = 102.70, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.80), with "the N170 latency of faces was shorter than that of characters and tools" and characters shorter than tools; group, time and interaction effects were non-significant.

> "The ANOVA for the N170 peak latencies revealed that only the main effect of Category was significant (F = 102.70, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.80). The N170 latency of faces was shorter than that of characters and tools (p < 0.01) and the N170 latency of characters was shorter than that of tools (p < 0.01)"
