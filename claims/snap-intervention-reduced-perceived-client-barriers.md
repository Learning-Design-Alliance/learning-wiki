## Evidence

### Chan Bibiana C 2013

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of barrier scores in the SNAP trial; Table 4 shows a group-by-time interaction p = 0.010 for client- and support-related barriers, while clinician-related and service-related barriers did not change.

> "There were no overall group differences in nurse's perception of the importance of barriers over time except for a significant linear trend for a decrease in 'client and support related barriers' (e.g. lack of client interest in lifestyle changes; lack of appropriate education materials for clients) in the intervention group."
