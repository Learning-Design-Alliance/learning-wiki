Beckmann Johanna, Nqweniso Siphesihle, Ludyga Sebastian, du Randt Rosa, Gresse Annelie, Long Kurt Z., Nienaber Madeleine, Seelig Harald, Pühse Uwe, Steinmann Peter, Utzinger Jürg, Walter Cheryl, Gerber Markus, Lang Christin. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group change scores in the cluster-RCT were significant for all four arms (e.g., congruent accuracy change 0.03–0.04, p < 0.05), indicating a similar learning effect across groups. The article reports "children from all intervention groups demonstrated significant improvements".

> "Over the course of the intervention, children from all intervention groups demonstrated significant improvements in reaction time and accuracy, both in inhibitory control (incongruent) and information processing (congruent) (Figure 2)."
