## Evidence

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison of Vocabulary Knowledge Scale gain scores in the same quasi-experimental study. Cohen's d yielded d = 0.69 for vocabulary depth, which the article qualifies as medium-to-large; deep word knowledge is described as accumulating more gradually than breadth.

> "Depth gains followed the same pattern, with the experimental group advancing from M = 2.31 to M = 3.18 while controls moved from M = 2.28 to M = 2.72 (between-group gain comparison t = 3.89, p < .001)"
