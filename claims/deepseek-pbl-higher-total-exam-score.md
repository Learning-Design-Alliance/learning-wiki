## Evidence

### Hou Jinxiao 2025

Hou Jinxiao, An Furun, Qin Hui, Zhang Lulu, Wang Juan, Zhang Cui, Fan Dachuan. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning in hematology residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07852-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled trial of 30 residents per group at one hospital; independent t-test on a five-part 100-point examination administered after two hematology PBL courses. The DeepSeek-assisted group's "significantly higher total score" was 86.03 ± 2.55 vs. 81.73 ± 3.38 (p = 0.000); no standardized effect size was reported.

> "The DeepSeek-assisted PBL group achieved significantly a significantly higher total score in the comprehensive examination compared to the traditional PBL group (86.03 ± 2.55 vs. 81.73 ± 3.38), with the difference being statistically significant (p = 0.000)"
