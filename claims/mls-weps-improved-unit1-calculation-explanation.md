# MLS-WEPs intervention predicted higher Unit 1 calculation and explanation scores among English learners when controlling for prior knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` English learners who received the MLS-WEPs intervention scored 0.481 standard deviations higher on calculation and 0.702 standard deviations higher on explanation in Unit 1 than controls, holding prior knowledge constant. [→ Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024](#xiaojuan-ke-kristie-j-newton-2024)

## Evidence

### Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression on Unit 1 pre/post scores from 78 English learners in sheltered algebra classes, controlling for prior knowledge and proficiency. The intervention condition predicted higher standardized scores: "0.481 (t(77) = 2.82, p = 0.01) standard deviations higher on the calculation component" and 0.702 on explanation.

> "In addition, ELs who received the intervention scored 0.481 (t(77) = 2.82, p = 0.01) standard deviations higher on the calculation component and 0.702 (t(77) = 4.77, p < 0.001) standard deviations higher on the explanation"
