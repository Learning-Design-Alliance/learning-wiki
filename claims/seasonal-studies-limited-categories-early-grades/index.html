Evidence¶

Dennis Condron 2021¶

Dennis Condron, Douglas Downery, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Schools as refractors: Change in variance in children's cognitive skills change while in school versus out. Sociology of Education, 94(4), 316-340. https://journals.sagepub.com/home/soe doi:10.1177/00380407211041542

q1 · i? · causal · r?

This is the article's own framing of the prior seasonal-comparison literature in its abstract; it notes such gaps "constitute only a small fraction of overall skill inequality". It is an author interpretation, not a tested result.

"the most persuasive seasonal studies (1) focus primarily on skill gaps between social categories (e.g., social class, race/ethnicity), which constitute only a small fraction of overall skill inequality, and (2) are restricted to early grades"
