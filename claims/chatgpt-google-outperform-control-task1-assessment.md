## Subclaims
`q3 i3` On the Task 1 assessment, the ChatGPT group scored significantly higher than the E-textbook and control groups, and the Google group scored significantly higher than the control group. [→ Mahir Akgun 2025](#mahir-akgun-2025)

## Evidence

### Mahir Akgun 2025

Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker. (2025). Short-Term Gains, Long-Term Gaps: The Impact of GenAI and Search Technologies on Retention. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on Task 1 assessment scores in a randomized four-group lab experiment (control, e-textbook, Google, ChatGPT; 123 students). "partial η² = .212" with significant pairwise differences favoring ChatGPT and Google over control.

> "The univariate ANOV A results for Task 1 showed significant differences between groups, F(3, 119) = 10.650, p < .001, partial η² = .212, explaining 21.2% of the variance. The ChatGPT group outperformed the E-textbook and control groups significantly, with mean differences of 16.60 and 22.39, respectively (p < .01)."
