# Memory-based strategy use continues to increase from childhood through adolescence into adulthood

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-sectional comparisons show adolescents and adults use memory-based retrieval more than children, indicating continued development of retrieval fluency beyond childhood. [→ Qin Shaozheng 2014](#qin-shaozheng-2014)

## Evidence

### Qin Shaozheng 2014

Qin Shaozheng, Cho Soohyun, Chen Tianwen, Rosenberg-Lee Miriam, Geary David C., Menon Vinod. (2014). Hippocampal-neocortical functional reorganization underlies children's cognitive development. Nature Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3788

`q3 · i?`

Cross-sectional ANOVA comparing strategy mixes of children at Time 2, 20 adolescents, and 20 adults. Post-hoc tests showed "greater memory-based strategy use in adolescents and adults than in children (Scheffe's Ps<0.03)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Cross-sectional comparisons between children at Time 2, adolescents, and adults revealed that the transition to memory-based problem solving continued into adolescence and adulthood (F(2, 65)>3.78, Ps<0.028)"
