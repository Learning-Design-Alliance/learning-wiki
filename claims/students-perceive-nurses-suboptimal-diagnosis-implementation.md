### Kestel 2026

Kestel, Sıdıka, and Korkmaz, Fatoş. (2026). Nursing Students' Perceptions of Instructional Approaches in Nursing Diagnosis Education: A Qualitative Study. Nursing Open, 13(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70571

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview study, students described observing nurses' care plans in clinical practice. One participant reported that "identical care plans and diagnoses assigned to each patient, disregarding individual nuances," and that patients were not involved in care planning decisions.

> "Diagnoses are transcribed into electronic records, with identical care plans and diagnoses assigned to each patient, disregarding individual nuances."
