`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational study at a single university hospital; after propensity-score matching on height and sex, 818 matched patients per year were compared, and LPV use "increased" from 0.0% to 34.2% (p < 0.001).

> "There was a significant increase in the number of patients receiving LPV (VT < 10 mL/kgIBW and PEEP ≥5 cm H2O) between 2004 and 2014 (0/818 [0.0%] vs. 280/818 [34.2%]; p < 0.001)."
