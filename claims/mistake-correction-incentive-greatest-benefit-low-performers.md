## Evidence

### Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2015

Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh, Andrew Mason. (2015). The effect of giving explicit incentives to correct mistakes on subsequent problem solving in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.012](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.012)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of pretest-to-posttest gains broken into low, medium and high pretest performance categories (Table II). Low-category incentivized students gained 45.0% versus 16.2% for comparison students; Table II reports the low-high gap shrinking from 42.2% to 19.9%. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The students in the Low category on the pretest in the incentivized and comparison groups had an average gain of 45.0% and 16.2%, respectively (see Table II)."
