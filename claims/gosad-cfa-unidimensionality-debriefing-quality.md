## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis based on average ratings of the G-OSAD categories (excluding 'establishes learning environment') for the 57 debriefings, using robust maximum likelihood estimation with bootstrap and Monte-Carlo checks due to the small sample. The article reports a single underlying factor with acceptable fit indices. No effect size printed.

> "Factor analyses supported the unidimensionality of the G-OSAD, which indicates that these G-OSAD ratings measure debriefing quality as intended."
