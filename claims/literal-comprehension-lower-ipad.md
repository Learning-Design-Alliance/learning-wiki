## Evidence

### Sackstein S 2015

Sackstein S, Spark L & Jenkins A. (2015). Are e-books effective tools for learning? Reading speed and comprehension: iPad vs. paper. South African Journal of Education, 35(4), Art. #1202. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n4a1202

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of comprehension by level showed lower literal-level performance on iPad for both Grade 10 and university students. University students answered 47% of literal questions correctly on paper and 36% on iPad.

> "Grade 10 students from both Schools answered correctly 60% of all literal questions on paper, and 51% o f the same type of questions on the iPad."
