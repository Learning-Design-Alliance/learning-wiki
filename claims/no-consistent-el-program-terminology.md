### Palacios 2022

Palacios, M., Kibler, A., Farren Burho, J., Garrett Sandstead, M., Wiger, S., & Woodruff, K. (2022). Who is in charge of English Learner services? Lessons learned from a national co-teaching and collaboration study. WestEd: National Research & Development Center to Improve Education for Secondary English Learners. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625797

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Findings from the protocol-driven contact search across the national sample of districts. The review found "no consistent way of referring to English Learner programs," listing the varied office names encountered.

> "Across the districts, there was no consistent way of referring to English Learner programs. Offices in charge of English Learner programs and services were known by different names, such as Student/ Pupil Services, English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL), English as a Second Language (ESL), English as a New Language (ENL), Bilingual or Multilingual Programs, World Languages, and Title III."
