# Over 70% of operational SSSCs are situated within larger state agencies and are funded mainly through state appropriations and federal or state grants

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Over 70 percent of currently operational SSSCs were reported to be situated within larger state agencies, including education, public safety, justice, and emergency management agencies. [→ McKenna 2022](#mckenna-2022)
`q2 i?` A large majority reported state and/or federal funding: 75 percent state appropriations, over 46 percent federal grants, and nearly 18 percent state agency grants, with many reporting multiple funding streams. [→ McKenna 2022 (2)](#mckenna-2022-2)

## Evidence

### McKenna 2022

McKenna, J., Russo, S., Sutherland, H., Boal, A., & Petrosino, A. (2022). States’ school safety centers: A brief look into history, characteristics, and activities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621272

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey finding on agency placement (Exhibit 4). The article reports "Over 70 percent of currently operational SSSCs were reported to be situated within larger state agencies," with education agencies the most common (50%), followed by public safety/law enforcement (30%), justice (15%), and emergency management (10%).

> "Over 70 percent of currently operational SSSCs were reported to be situated within larger state agencies. These agencies included those related to education, public safety (i.e., law enforcement), justice, and emergency management"

### McKenna 2022 (2)

McKenna, J., Russo, S., Sutherland, H., Boal, A., & Petrosino, A. (2022). States’ school safety centers: A brief look into history, characteristics, and activities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621272

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey finding on SSSC funding sources. The article reports state appropriations within the state's budget (75%), federal grants (over 46%), and state agency grants (nearly 18%), noting many respondents reported multiple funding streams.

> "In some cases, these funds were state appropriations within the state’s budget (75%), but in other cases these were grants given by federal (over 46%) and/or state agencies (nearly 18%). Many respondents reported multiple funding streams for their SSSC."
