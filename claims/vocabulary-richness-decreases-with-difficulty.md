### Antoun Yaacoub 2025

Antoun Yaacoub, Zainab Assaghir, Lionel Prevost, Jérôme Da-Rugna. (2025). Analyzing Feedback Mechanisms in AI-Generated MCQs: Insights into Readability, Lexical Properties, and Levels of Challenge. ESIEA Lab / Lebanese University. [doi:10.1109/iccsm66818.2025.00026](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccsm66818.2025.00026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis of Table 4 vocabulary richness means across tone and difficulty. Supportive feedback maintained relatively high richness for harder questions; neutral showed a more pronounced drop and challenging the steepest decline. The authors suggest a shift toward precision for complex topics.

> "The interaction for vocabulary richness shows a consistent pattern: vocabulary richness decreases as difficulty increases, and this trend is present for all tones. However, the rate of decrease is slightly different."
