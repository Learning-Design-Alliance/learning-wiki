### F. Bouquet 2021

F. Bouquet, G. Creutzer, D. Dorsel, J. Vince and J. Bobroff. (2021). Enhance your smartphone with a Bluetooth Arduino nano board. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac35af](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac35af)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive report from the authors' physics-projects teaching experience: third-year university-level physics students performed the Beer-Lambert experiment outside the classroom as part of an experimental project class. The figure 3 data show an exponential Beer-Lambert behaviour appearing linear in the plotted representation, with the red channel indicating that only 15% of the light intensity is lost per sheet and the red-channel linear fit intersecting the zero-layer axis at 72%.

> "The nano board allowed students owning an iPhone to participate (the iPhone light sensor access is blocked to users), and moreover the nano board light sensor can measure the light intensity on three different wavelengths (red, green, blue), allowing for some crude spectrometry experiments"
