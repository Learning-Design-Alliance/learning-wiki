## Evidence

### Stoddard 2026

Stoddard, S. A., Moore, C. R., Polick, C. S., & Moss, J. M. (2026). Cumulative Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) and School Outcomes During Adolescence: A Scoping Review. The Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.70209

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing two longitudinal studies, that externalizing problems (e.g., aggression, substance use) were associated with increased odds of dropout while internalizing problems were associated with decreased odds, and that depression and substance use but not anxiety mediated dropout. No effect sizes are printed.

> "When looking specifically at depression, anxiety, and substance use as individual mediators, researchers found depression and substance use were associated with increased odds of dropout, but anxiety was not associated with dropout [22]."
