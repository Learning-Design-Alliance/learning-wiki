Transcribed communication from the team leader to the team at three measuring points was analysed with the LIWC program, which calculates the percentage of words matching categories such as big words, negation, approvals, emotions and cognitive processes. The pattern is an authors' interpretation, not a tested result.

> "Our linguistic analysis of the communication from the team leader to the team at measuring point t0 to t2 showed an increasing number of words in the categories "big words" and "articles" (Fig 2)."
