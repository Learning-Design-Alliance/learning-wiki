Black 2009¶

Black, P., & Wiliam, D. (2009). Developing the theory of formative assessment. Educ Asse Eval Acc, 21, 5–31. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11092-008-9068-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument stated in the article's abstract: the authors assert, as the motivating problem for the paper, that "there is no clear rationale to define and delimit" formative assessment "within broader theories of pedagogy" despite many offered definitions. No empirical evidence is reported for this assertion.
