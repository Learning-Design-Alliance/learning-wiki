Evidence¶

Michael J Timms 2016¶

Michael J Timms. (2016). Letting Artificial Intelligence in Education Out of the Box: Educational Cobots and Smart Classrooms. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:701–712. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0095-y

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper reports, citing Kennedy et al. (2015), a study using the Nao robot in a tutoring role with a touch screen feed; the robot could not use the screen itself. The social-versus-asocial comparison found higher gains for the asocial robot; no effect size is printed.

"The study investigated whether a more social robot would produce higher learning gains than one that was asocial. An unexpected finding was that the asocial robot produced higher learning gains, which is contrary to similar research."
