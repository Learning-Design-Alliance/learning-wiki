Evidence¶

McDougall 2015¶

McDougall, D., Ornelles, C., Mersberg, K., & Amona, K. (2015). A Meta-Analytic Review of Tactile-Cued Self-Monitoring Interventions Used by Students in Educational Settings. JAASEP. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134294. doi:10.64546/jaasep.289

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's synthesis of instituting-TCSM phase comparisons (A-B and A1-B1) across the eight studies with adjacent baseline phases, reporting Phi distributions and magnitude counts as printed.

"For the aforementioned 27 instituting TCSM comparisons, Phi ranged from 0.18 to 0.89 (Md = 0.74), with magnitude of effect sizes being large (n = 22), medium (n = 2), and small (n = 3)."

McDougall 2015 (2)¶

McDougall, D., Ornelles, C., Mersberg, K., & Amona, K. (2015). A Meta-Analytic Review of Tactile-Cued Self-Monitoring Interventions Used by Students in Educational Settings. JAASEP. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1134294. doi:10.64546/jaasep.289

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The review's synthesis of removing-TCSM comparisons (B-A1) in the ABAB studies, reporting smaller Phi values; the reviewers note performance may not revert to initial baseline levels if the behavior is not prone to being unlearned (Kazdin, 1982).

"For the aforementioned 7 removing TCSM comparisons, Phi ranged from 0.00 to 0.53 (Md = 0.32), with magnitude of effect sizes being large (n = 1), medium (n = 3), and small (n = 1), and zero (n = 2)."
