### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Kimme S. Johnson, Soojin E. Park, Jacob T. Stanley, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Student perceptions of laboratory classroom activities and experimental physics practice. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of interview rationale excerpts from the post-project Likert survey in the optics and lasers lab study. The article reports interpersonal activities "accounted for almost all instances" of status-dependent codes, with asking a supervisor for help most frequent. The discussion notes such activities were viewed as conditional on novice or expert status; no effect size is printed.

> "Interpersonal activities were identiﬁed as deﬁnitely a feature of experimental physics research by a majority of participants, and they accounted for almost all instances in which participants discussed the status-dependent nature of an activity. Compared to other activities, asking a supervisor for help was most frequently described as status-dependent."
