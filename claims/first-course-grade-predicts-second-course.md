## Evidence

### Creech 2012

Creech, L. R., & Sweeder, R. D. (2012). Analysis of Student Performance in Large-Enrollment Life Science Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 11(4), 386–391. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-02-0019

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

HLM results (Table 1) show First series of course β values of 0.528 (PHY 432), 0.491 (BC 462), 0.389 (Orgo 252), and 0.392 (Orgo 352); for BC 462 the first-course β (0.491) exceeded entering GPA's (0.336).

> "BC 462 and Orgo 352 may have showed no gender differences because BC 461 grade and Orgo 351 grade, respectively, were used as predictors; these grades already include the gender differences from the first course in the series."
