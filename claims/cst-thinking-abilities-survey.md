## Evidence

### Hui Zheng 2020

Hui Zheng, Binjing Hua, Qiang Sun, Jun Cao and Fangmin Liu. (2020). Applying a Chemical Structure Teaching Method in the Pharmaceutical Analysis Curriculum to Improve Student Engagement and Learning. J. Chem. Educ. 97, 421−426. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00551

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaires on synthesizing information, thinking logically, and analyzing problems independently were employed to evaluate teaching effects; all 98 participant questionnaires were returned. Percentages are self-reported perceptions with no effect size printed.

> "For the synthesizing information ability, 98% students thought the CST method was helpful to promote this ability as well as the ability of cultivating independent analytical problem -solving. There were 96% students who thought the CST method could promote their logical thinking ability."
