## Evidence

### Palladino 2025

Palladino, P., Trotta, E., Bonvino, A., Carlucci, L., & Cottini, M. (2025). How do you feel during English class? Emotions and metacognition in primary school children learning English as a second language. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09414-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged Pearson correlations between metacognition at T0 and emotions at T1. Children better at discriminating correct from incorrect responses in the English vocabulary test at T0 were less bored and less anxious about learning English at T1. At T1, metacognition and emotions were unrelated.

> "In contrast, a better ability to monitor and control one's English vocabulary performance at T0 was negatively related to later feelings of boredom and anxiety at T1."
