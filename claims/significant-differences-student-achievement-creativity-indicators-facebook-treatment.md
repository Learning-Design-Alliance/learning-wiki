## Evidence

### Alias et al. 2013

Norlidah Alias, Saedah Siraj, Mohd Khairul Azman Md Daud & Zaharah Hussin. (2013). Effectiveness of Facebook Based Learning to Enhance Creativity Among Islamic Studies Students by Employing Isman Instructional Design Model. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008868

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A one-way ANOVA analysis of the treatment group's achievement test marks based on creativity indicator. The study reports the mean between group for post test is 78.32 and the mean within group is 23.31 with test F(3,156) = 3.36, p < .05, rejecting null hypothesis H4.

> "Table 5 indicates that there is significant difference in students’ achievement based on creativity indicator after treatment through Facebook based learning."
