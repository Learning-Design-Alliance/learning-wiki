## Evidence

### Hsu 2026

Hsu, T.-C., Zhang, Z., Chen, Z., Liu, Y., & Liu, Y. (2026). Designing More Engaging Serious Games to Support Students' Mental Health: A Pilot Study Based on A CBT-Informed Design Framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pilot RCT IMI comparison (N=28). Independent-samples t-tests found "no significant difference between the two groups in terms of effort/importance" (d=-0.13) or pressure/tension (d=0.41); equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no significant difference between the two groups in terms of effort/importance (E_Group M=4.07, SD=1.13; C_Group M=4.19, SD=0.51; p=.732, d=-0.13), nor was there a significant difference in the pressure/tension dimension (E_Group M=1.89, SD=0.45; C_Group M=1.70, SD=0.45; p=.290, d=0.41)"
