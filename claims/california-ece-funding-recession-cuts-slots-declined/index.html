Melnick 2017¶

Melnick, H., Tinubu Ali, T., Gardner, M., Maier, A., & Wechsler, M. (2017). Understanding California's early care and education system. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/ca-ece-report

Descriptive fiscal analysis in the Overall Funding Levels section. The report states programs "experienced over $1 billion in budget cuts", that "approximately 25% of child care slots were cut between 2008 and 2013", and that reimbursement rates flatlined while state spending increased from 2013–2017 without full recovery.
