Evidence¶

Zhao Yan 2025¶

Zhao Yan, Yue Yuhe, Sun Zhonghua, Jiang Qiang, Li Gangsheng. (2025). Does Generative Artificial Intelligence Improve Students’ Higher-Order Thinking? A Meta-Analysis Based on 29 Experiments and Quasi-Experiments. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence13120160

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis (Table 5) of intervention duration across subgroups: 0–8 weeks N = 15, 8–16 weeks N = 31, >16 weeks N = 13, with significant between-groups difference (Q = 9.106, p = 0.011). The quote reports all three subgroup effect sizes.

"interventions lasting 0–8 weeks yielded a moderate effect (ES = 0.494, p < 0.001), interventions of 8–16 weeks produced the most significant effect (ES = 0.759, p < 0.001), and interventions exceeding 16 weeks showed a comparatively more minor effect (ES = 0.372, p < 0.001)"
