## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2021

Thomaschewski M, Heldmann M, Uter J C, Varbelow D, Münte T F, Keck T. (2021). Changes in attentional resources during the acquisition of laparoscopic surgical skills. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zraa012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioural measures from the same pre/post dual-task sessions (12 analysed subjects). Neither "accuracy (percentage correct responses: W = 48, P = 0.518)" nor reaction times showed a significant before–after difference; no effect sizes were printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "This decrease in ERP amplitude was not accompanied by significant before–after differences in accuracy (percentage correct responses: W = 48, P = 0.518) and reaction times (W = 42, P = 0.850) in the auditory change detection task."
