## Evidence

### Baczyńska Anna 2026

Baczyńska Anna, Cai Zhenyao, Urbański Konrad, Szajda Łukasz. (2026). Comparison of virtual reality development centers and 270-degree evaluations in the context of mid-level managers' competencies. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0339872

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate correlation analysis (Fig 1A) relating others' 270-degree ratings to VRDC scores found "Significant positive correlations between others' assessments and VRDC", with r = 0.40, r = 0.29, and r = 0.37 across the three competencies.

> "Significant positive correlations between others' assessments and VRDC were observed for Managing People and Tasks (r = 0.40, p < .01), Goal Orientation (r = 0.29, p < .05), and Change Management (r = 0.37, p < .01)."
