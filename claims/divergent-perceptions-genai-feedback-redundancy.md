Jin, F. J.-Y., Maheshi, B., Lai, W., Li, Y., Gasevic, D., Chen, G., Charwat, N., Chan, P. W. K., Martinez-Maldonado, R., Gašević, D., & Tsai, Y.-S. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of student perceptions, triangulated with usage trace data, from the study's 18 participants across information technology, education, business and economics, and engineering. Students diverged: some "appreciated the GenAI functionalities", others "found them redundant".

> "While some students appreciated the GenAI functionalities, others found them redundant when they perceived the feedback as clear and easy to understand."
