# Ability and speed are negatively correlated, and the reading test's ability estimates are more affected by speed than the math test's

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The correlation between person ability and speed was negative under all models, meaning higher-ability students tended to respond faster. [→ Wang 2011](#wang-2011)
`q2 i?` The absolute magnitude of the ability-speed correlation was larger for LI52 (range -0.34984 to -0.29045) than for NS34 (range -0.18389 to -0.16582). [→ Wang 2011 (2)](#wang-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Wang 2011

Wang, S., & Jiao, H. (2011). Incorporating Person Covariates and Response Times as Collateral Information to Improve Person and Item Parameter Estimations. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, New Orleans, LA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/incorporating-person-covariates-and-response-times-as-collateral-information-to-improve-person-and-item-parameter-estimations/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of jointly estimated person parameters across nested models (Table 2 and Figures 5-6). The dependence parameter ρ between theta and zeta was directly estimated in the GLMM G matrix; all values were negative.

> "The negative sign of ρ means that higher ability students tend to response the item fast."

### Wang 2011 (2)

Wang, S., & Jiao, H. (2011). Incorporating Person Covariates and Response Times as Collateral Information to Improve Person and Item Parameter Estimations. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, New Orleans, LA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/incorporating-person-covariates-and-response-times-as-collateral-information-to-improve-person-and-item-parameter-estimations/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same person-parameter analysis across the five nested models per test. The authors conclude "the student ability measured by LI52 test tend to be affected more by the speed than the ability measured by NS34 test."

> "The range of ρ for NS34 test under different models are from -0.18389 to -0.16582; The range of ρ for LI52 test under different models are from -0.34984 to -0.29045."
