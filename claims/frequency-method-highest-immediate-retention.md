### Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar 2025

Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar, Dwyer Eric, Barzegar Mohammad, Hamidi Hadi. (2025). Teaching medical terminology: comparing the effectiveness of lecture-based and corpus-assisted methods. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07584-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the quasi-experimental study (40 students, four groups): at T1 the frequency group's mean VLT score was 7.3 (0.5), the highest of the four methods, per Table 2; the text states "the frequency method demonstrated the highest retention, closely followed by concordancing". The abstract reports the same 7.3 ± 0.5 figure.
