## Evidence

### Bureau 2022

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Weighted-covariance GLS meta-analytic structural equation modeling, run as three models because parental autonomy support had too few samples to impute: one with all variables and pairwise deletion (k = 2–71 per correlation), one with multiple imputation and no parental autonomy support (k = 7–71), and one with pairwise deletion and no parental autonomy support (k = 7–71), from a database of 144 samples. Across the three models, "Competence was the strongest predictor of amotivation (β = −.23 to −.30), identified regulation (β = .31 to .34), and intrinsic motivation (β = .33)".

> "Competence was the strongest predictor of amotivation (β = −.23 to −.30), identified regulation (β = .31 to .34), and intrinsic motivation (β = .33)."

### Bureau 2022 (2)

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Relative weight analysis on meta-analytic correlations between needs and motivation types, estimating unique variance per need with multicollinearity suppressed. Identified regulation (R2 = .30) "was most strongly predicted by competence (44%), followed by autonomy (30%) and relatedness (26%)".

> "Identified regulation (R2 = .30) was most strongly predicted by competence (44%), followed by autonomy (30%) and relatedness (26%)."

### Bureau 2022 (3)

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Relative weight analysis of need satisfaction predicting intrinsic motivation from the pooled meta-analytic correlations. The analysis found "intrinsic motivation (R2 = .38) was predicted in a similar way by autonomy (39%) and competence (42%), but relatedness was less important (18%)".

> "Finally, intrinsic motivation (R2 = .38) was predicted in a similar way by autonomy (39%) and competence (42%), but relatedness was less important (18%)."
