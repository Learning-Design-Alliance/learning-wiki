`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 201 O&G physicians in French-speaking Switzerland rating 66 R-track items on a 5-point Likert scale; the article reports the full area ranking, with Social sensibility at "4.17 ± 0.58" and Resistance capacity lowest at 3.73 ± 0.60. No effect size is printed for the area ordering.

> "Overall, the order of importance for the competence areas was: “Social sensibility” (4.17 ± 0.58), “Psychomotor & multitasking abilities” (4.08 ± 0.78), “Verbal competencies” (3.97 ± 0.56), “Personality traits” (3.91 ± 0.58)"
