# ChatGPT-assisted skill learning produces lower cognitive load than video-only learning, measured by change in pupil diameter

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` During learning, the ChatGPT group showed a significantly smaller increase in pupil diameter from baseline than the control group (mean 0.137, SD 0.036 vs mean 0.312, SD 0.032; P<.001), interpreted as lower cognitive load. [→ Qiao J 2025](#qiao-j-2025)

## Evidence

### Qiao J 2025

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Eye-tracking analysis of pupil diameter change from baseline during the learning period, using an aSee eye-tracker at 60 Hz with median-based smoothing in MATLAB. The authors interpret smaller pupil dilation as lower cognitive load: "the cognitive load borne by participants in the ChatGPT group was significantly lower than that in the control group."

> "The results of the t test (P<.001), performed at a significance level of α=.05, showed significant differences between the ChatGPT group (mean 0.137, SD 0.036) and the control group (mean 0.312, SD 0.032)."
