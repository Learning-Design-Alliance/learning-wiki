Reported for the attention-matched standardized group in the 16-week trial; the group received fixed evidence-based programs with equivalent notification frequency. The article describes "partial maintenance of effects" at week 28 with d = 0.62 from baseline; the immediate post-intervention gain was d = 0.34.

> "The standardized intervention group showed modest improvements (3.76% GPA increase, d = 0.34, p = 0.024), consistent with benefits of structured digital interventions. Follow-up at week 28 showed partial maintenance of effects (7.2% GPA improvement from baseline, d = 0.62)."
