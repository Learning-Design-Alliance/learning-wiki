## Evidence

### Felix G. Hamza-Lup 2019

Felix G. Hamza-Lup, Benjamin Page. (2019). Haptics-Augmented Physics Simulation: Coriolis Effect. The 7th International Conference on Virtual Learning ICVL 2012. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.11567](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.11567)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 24 undergraduates in Principles of Physics I divided into four GPA-matched groups of six; objective assessment compared quiz results. The visuo-haptic groups showed a "15% increase in quiz scores" versus the reading-and-video group.

> "Both the G1-G4 and the G1-G3 pairing show a 15% increase in quiz scores for the groups that took part in a visuo-haptic simulation of the Coriolis effect, versus the group that only had reading material and a video."
