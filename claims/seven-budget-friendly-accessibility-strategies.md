# Seven budget-friendly strategies increase expected accessibility by 105%–112% while limiting budget increase to less than 7% and keeping unmet-need increases under $500

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` From 235 optimized strategies, seven met the criteria of budget within ±7% of actual and unmet need within $500 of actual, and each raised expected accessibility by more than 100%. [→ Vinhthuy Phan 2022](#vinhthuy-phan-2022)

## Evidence

### Vinhthuy Phan 2022

Vinhthuy Phan, Laura Wright, Bridgette Decent. (2022). Optimizing Financial Aid Allocation to Improve Access and Affordability to Higher Education. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 14, No 3. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/609

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation/optimization results from runs of simulated quenching on the Fall 2020 applicant data, reported in Table 2. The seven strategies show accessibility gains of 105%–112%, enrollment gains of 1.3%–3.6%, revenue gains of 0.1%–4.2%, unmet-need increases of $3–$481, and budget changes from -0.4% to 7.0%.

> "Expected accessibility is between 105% and 110% higher than the actual accessibility. These numbers are shown in the first column of Table 2."
