Evidence¶

Alshehri OAO 2026¶

Alshehri OAO, Lamouchi A, Abdellatif MS, Al-Dosari MNA, Mekheimer M and Nemt-allah MA (2026). From tool to scaffold: structured human–AI collaboration and its effects on academic writing and digital critical thinking among Saudi EFL learners. Front. Psychol. 17:1830103. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1830103

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Between-group posttest comparison in a quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study of 53 Saudi EFL undergraduates in intact classes (experimental n = 31, control n = 22). The experimental group mean was 15.23 (SD 1.65) versus 11.91 (SD 1.44) for controls, "a large standardized effect (Hedges' g = 2.09, 95% CI [1.41, 2.76])".

"Between-group comparisons at posttest indicated a large and highly significant advantage for the experimental group, t(51) = 7.60, p < 0.001, with a mean difference of 3.32 points (95% CI [2.44, 4.20]) and a large standardized effect (Hedges' g = 2.09, 95% CI [1.41, 2.76])."

Alshehri OAO 2026 (2)¶

Alshehri OAO, Lamouchi A, Abdellatif MS, Al-Dosari MNA, Mekheimer M and Nemt-allah MA (2026). From tool to scaffold: structured human–AI collaboration and its effects on academic writing and digital critical thinking among Saudi EFL learners. Front. Psychol. 17:1830103. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1830103

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline equivalence analysis using independent-samples t-tests on pretest scores in the same quasi-experiment. No statistically significant pretest group differences emerged for writing proficiency or DCTS total, indicating "observed post-intervention differences are unlikely to reflect substantial pre-existing group disparities".

"Baseline equivalence analyses showed no statistically significant pre-test differences between the experimental and control groups for writing proficiency, t(51) = 0.03, p = 0.977, or DCTS total, t(51) = 0.33, p = 0.741."
