## Evidence

### Harb Gerlinde C. 2020

Harb Gerlinde C., Schultz Jon-Håkon. (2020). The nature of posttraumatic nightmares and school functioning in war-affected youth. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0242414. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242414

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of baseline self- and teacher-report data from 64 treatment-seeking Gazan students in the Better Learning Program, using Pearson correlations with Bonferroni corrections. The study reports "teacher-rated r = -.28, p = .01" and "student-rated r = -.30, p = .007" for frequency, and "r = -.34, p = .003" for intensity with student-rated functioning.

> "Nightmare frequency was significantly associated with reduced functioning (teacher-rated r = -.28, p = .01 and student-rated r = -.30, p = .007), and nightmare intensity with reduced student-rated functioning (r = -.34, p = .003)."
