## Evidence

### Gerdesmann 2026

Gerdesmann, D., Schwichow, M., & Koerber, S. (2026). Factors that explain simulation-based inquiry performance in physics learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09806-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Block 2 of the hierarchical multiple regression added interest, self-concept, personality dimensions, and current motivation. Only "A more positive phys-ics self-concept was associated with better performance"; dispositional measures as a set added no explanatory power.

> "A more positive phys-ics self-concept was associated with better performance, although including dispositional measures did not increase the model's overall explanatory power."

### Gerdesmann 2026 (2)

Gerdesmann, D., Schwichow, M., & Koerber, S. (2026). Factors that explain simulation-based inquiry performance in physics learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09806-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Block 3 of the hierarchical regression added cognitive load measures, with a "significant negative effect of extraneous load on inquiry performance" and 5% additional explained variance. No effect size is printed for the extraneous load coefficient.

> "The final model (block 3, orange) added intrinsic and extraneous cognitive load, revealing a significant negative effect of extraneous load on inquiry performance and producing an additional 5% increase in explained variance."
