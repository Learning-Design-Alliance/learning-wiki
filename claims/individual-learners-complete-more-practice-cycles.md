# Individual learners complete more flashcard practice cycles than paired learners, though inconsistently across experiments

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2`–`r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 1, Individual learners completed about one more learning cycle than Paired learners (d = 0.68). [→ Imundo 2024](#imundo-2024)
`q3 i2` In Experiment 2 the cycle difference was nonsignificant, but it reappeared in Experiment 3 (d = 0.45). [→ Imundo 2024 (2)](#imundo-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Imundo 2024

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 1 self-reported cycle counts showed Individual learners completed significantly more cycles through the 40-card deck in the same total learning time, d = 0.68, p < .001, likely because paired logistics slow the pace.

> "Individual learners typically completed one more learning cycle (M = 5.36, SD = 1.64) across the entire flashcard phase than did Paired learners (M = 4.32, SD = 1.44). This difference was significant, t (150) = 4.16, p < .001, d = 0.68, 95% CI [0.55, 1.54]."

### Imundo 2024 (2)

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 replicated the cycle-count difference (d = 0.45, p < .001). In Experiment 2, however, the difference was nonsignificant (d = .21, p = .22), so the effect did not replicate across all experiments.

> "Individual learners (M = 4.55, SD = 1.82) completed about one more practice cycle through the flashcard set than Paired learners (M = 3.82, SD = 1.38), t (401) = 4.53, p < .001, d = 0.45, 95% CI [0.41, 1.04]."
