## Evidence

### Hyll Mary 2019

Hyll Mary, Schvarcz Robert, Manninen Katri. (2019). Exploring how medical students learn with the help of a digital presentation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1569-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with 14 third-year medical students (8 interviews, including a pilot) at Karolinska Institutet, analysed by qualitative content analysis. Students reported that "it could be watched where, when, and in the pace they wished", enabling reflection, rewinding, note-taking and repetition; some described feeling relaxed rather than pressured.

> "They appreciated that it could be watched where, when, and in the pace they wished. This flexibility gave them time to reflect, rewind, take notes, repeat, and not lose focus."
