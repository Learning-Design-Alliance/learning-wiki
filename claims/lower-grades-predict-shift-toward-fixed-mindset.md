## Evidence

### Fargol Seifollahi 2026

Fargol Seifollahi, Christian D. Schunn, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Questioning Your Brilliance in Physics: Differential Shifts in Fixed Mindsets by Grade and Gender. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression models (Table 2) predicting ending mindset category from initial mindset, grade, gender, and grade-by-gender interaction in the N = 508 sample. Table 2 prints OR = 0.16 (B) and 0.10 (C) for Confident, and OR = 2.47 (B, p = 0.036) and 3.73 (C, p = 0.003) for Hesitant, relative to A grades; pseudo-R-squared values were 0.23 and 0.19.

> "In particular, students who received a B or C were much less likely to be in the Confident category and much more likely to be in the Hesitant category."
