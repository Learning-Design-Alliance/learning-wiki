## Evidence

### Dromey BP 2021

Dromey BP, Ahmed S, Vasconcelos F, Mazomenos E, Kunpalin Y, Ourselin S, Deprest J, David AL, Stoyanov D, Peebles DM. (2021). Dimensionless squared jerk: An objective differential to assess experienced and novice probe movement in obstetric ultrasound. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.5855

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational study at a UK teaching hospital: 10 experienced (>200 examinations) and 10 novice (<25 examinations) sonographers scanned a second-trimester phantom with electromagnetic probe tracking. The study found "19.26 SD 3.02 and 22.08 SD 1.05" (p = 0.01).

> "The mean DSJ for experienced operators was 19.26 SD 3.02 and 22.08 SD 1.05 for the novice group respectively (p = 0.01)."
