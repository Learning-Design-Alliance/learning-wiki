# Roughly half of students diagonalize the entire H′ matrix instead of only its degenerate subspace when finding a good basis

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When the original basis was not good and H0 and H′ do not commute, roughly half of students diagonalized the entire H′ matrix rather than only in the degenerate subspace of H0. [→ Christof Keebaugh 2017](#christof-keebaugh-2017)

## Evidence

### Christof Keebaugh 2017

Christof Keebaugh, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Developing and evaluating an interactive tutorial on degenerate perturbation theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

From the difficulty investigation on finding a good basis. One interviewed student incorrectly stated "We must ﬁnd the simultaneous eigenstates of ˆH 0 and ˆH ′", not realizing non-commuting operators cannot be simultaneously diagonalized.

> "The most common mistake was diagonalizing the en- tire ˆH ′ matrix instead of diagonalizing the ˆH ′ matrix only in the degenerate subspace of ˆH 0. For example, roughly half of students diagonalized the entire ˆH ′ matrix."
