## Evidence

### Burk-Rafel J 2026

Burk-Rafel J, Park H, Cornwell M, Holmboe ES, Yamazaki K, Santen SA, Park YS, Andrews JS, Hogan SO, Richardson J. (2026). Residency Competency Ratings and Early-Career Physician Antibiotic Prescribing. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.34365

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective national cohort linking mid-final-year Milestone ratings to Medicare Part D claims for 31,800 PCPs (129,225 physician-years), modeled with negative-binomial mixed-effects models. The adjusted "IRR, 0.98; 95% CI, 0.97-0.99" corresponds to 0.7 fewer prescriptions per 100 beneficiaries per year.

> "Each 1-SD increase in program-standardized overall competency rating was associated with a 2% lower annual antibiotic prescription rate (IRR, 0.98; 95% CI, 0.97-0.99; P < .001), while adjusting for panel-level patient comorbidities, prescribing volume, and repeated measures"
