The article reports, citing Weber and Lennon (2007), a meta-analysis of ten comparative studies of traditional versus web-based course delivery. Outcomes were "better than traditional versions in four cases, worse in two, and indistinguishable in four"; no effect size is printed. The authors also note convenience as the significant pro and the impersonal nature of the online environment as the notable con.
