## Evidence

### Bazen 2022

Bazen, Loes L.; de Bree, Elise H.; van den Boer, Madelon; de Jong, Peter F. (2022). Perceived negative consequences of dyslexia: the influence of person and environmental factors. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-022-00274-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of 123 Dutch university students with dyslexia. Self-perceived literacy disability had standardized betas of .275 (academic, p=.001), .347 (anxiety, p=.000), and .228 (depression, p=.005), showing "self-perceived literacy disability significantly contributed to more perceived consequences in all domains."

> "In contrast, self-perceived literacy disability significantly contributed to more perceived consequences in all domains. Higher outcomes on the socio-emotional person factor coping contributed to less depression consequences only."
