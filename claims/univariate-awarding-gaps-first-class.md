Exploratory univariate analysis using z-tests of proportions and odds ratios (Table III). It also found disability OR 0.82 (95% CI: 0.72–0.93), lower SES OR 0.80 (95% CI: 0.71-0.91), and 4-year programmes OR 3.69 (95% CI: 3.34-4.07) versus 3-year programmes.

> "Black students have 0.43 (95% CI: 0.29-0.63) times the odds of achieving a first-class degree compared to White students, and Asian students have 0.67 (95% CI: 0.55-0.81) times the odds."
