Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/bridging-the-gap-in-workforce-and-education-services-career-coaching-in-the-virginia-rethinks-health

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

A comparison of coaching participants with other nonparticipating students in the same state community college system, using administrative records, found participants "were more likely than other students to obtain career studies certificates and associate degrees" despite more risk characteristics. No effect size is printed.

> "Students who participated in coaching activities were more likely than other students to obtain career studies certificates and associate degrees even though the coaching participants were more likely than nonparticipants to have characteristics typically associated with lower educational attainment."
