`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Focus-group study in a paediatric clerkship in which at least ten VPs were integrated with other educational activities; 39 of 116 exposed students participated. The analysis "revealed six themes" including "optimal sequencing (e.g.: lecture – 1 to 2 VP(s) – tutor-led small group discussion – real patient)".

> "The analysis of eight focus group interviews revealed six themes which were deemed by students to be important for the optimal implementation of VPs: continuous and stable online access, increasing complexity, adapted to students' knowledge"
