`q2 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, change-score correlations showed that Nine Hole Peg Test performance in the non-dominant hand rose as sensitivity fell in the middle (r² = 0.416), ring (r² = 0.328) and little (r² = 0.356) fingers of that hand; no standardised effect size is printed. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of change scores (Table 3) in the 37-student pre-post cohort, printing r² values of 0.416, 0.328 and 0.356. This is a secondary, exploratory association between co-evolving variables, not a manipulated contrast, and the article offers no causal interpretation for it.

> "In addition, an increase in dexterity is associated with a decrease in sensitivity in the medial annulus (r2 = 0.416*), ulnar annulus (r2 = 0.328*), and cardia (r2 = 0.356*) of the non-dominant hand."
