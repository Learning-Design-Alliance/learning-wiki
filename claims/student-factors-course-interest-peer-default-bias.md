`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data on group influence: participant PH003 describes classmates inducing low scores through peer pressure; the article also reports conformity to majority scoring tendencies.

> "There's also the persuasion or instigation from classmates around them. Maybe their friends or classmates have a bad impression of the teacher or course, so out of so-called brotherhood or friendship, they give a low score."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data: participant DE021 describes a default path of high scores under vague impressions of teachers; the article also cites concern for teachers' professional title evaluation as a reason for defaulting high.

> "If we don't know the teacher, we'll generally give a relatively high score because we don't really remember. Generally, everyone defaults to giving teachers a high score (default path)"
