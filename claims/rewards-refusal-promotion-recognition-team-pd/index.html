Gast 2017¶

Gast, Inken; Schildkamp, Kim; van der Veen, Jan T. (2017). Team-Based Professional Development Interventions in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704306

q2 · i? · review · r3

Rewards theme in the review's organizational-level synthesis, drawing on Bakah et al. (2012); the review adds that team accomplishments should be taken into account when deciding about teacher promotion, and that teachers felt team participation could diminish tenure chances.

"Monetary rewards for team members were refused, as teachers believed that financial rewards for team members could hinder upscaling and sustainability within the institution and that some teachers might only participate in teams for the financial benefits, undermining team activities"
