## Evidence

### Yoo H 2025

Yoo H, Zhou X, Phillips B. (2025). Predictive validity of preadmission assessment on early nursing academic readiness: impact of COVID-19. Nurse Educ. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001891

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis by pandemic period (Table 2): pandemic-period correlations were Science 0.344, Reading 0.327, English 0.294, Mathematics 0.271, each lower than the pre-pandemic values (0.362, 0.356, 0.316, 0.292). The study reports "All correlations were statistically significant (P < .001)."

> "Furthermore, the correlation differences were significant for all 4 content areas between pre-pandemic and pandemic periods."
