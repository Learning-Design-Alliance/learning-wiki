## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experts noticed opportunities for students to generate their own ideas and work 1.8 times more often than novices (p < 0.05, adjusted). [→ Auerbach A. J. 2018](#auerbach-a-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Auerbach A. J. 2018

Auerbach A. J., Higgins M., Brickman P., Andrews T. C. (2018). Teacher Knowledge for Active-Learning Instruction: Expert–Novice Comparison Reveals Differences. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-07-0149

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Generalized linear model comparison of lesson-analysis counts (14 experts, 29 novices); estimate 1.8 (95% CI 1.3–2.4), significant at p < 0.05 after adjustment. Participants attended to tasks requiring explanation, data analysis, and instructor moves that keep the onus of learning on students.

> "Creating opportunities for generative work	the level of cognitive engagement of students, including when the instructor gave students responsibility for constructing their own ideas and engaging in scientific practices, either alone or with their peers."
