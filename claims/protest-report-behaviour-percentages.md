## Evidence

### Minkang Kim 2021

Minkang Kim, Jean Decety, Ling Wu, Soohyun Baek and Derek Sankey. (2021). Neural computations in children's third-party interventions are modulated by their parents' moral values. npj Science of Learning 6:38. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00116-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioural coding of video recordings from the live transgression experiment in which an unfamiliar adult confederate tore a page from a library book in the child's presence. Protest reactions were coded into five categories (inter-rater κ = 0.95) and reports into three (κ = 1.00).
