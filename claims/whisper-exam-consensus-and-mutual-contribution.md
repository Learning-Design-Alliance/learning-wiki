Descriptive survey results on perceived benefit and contribution (n = 191 respondents), reported with printed counts and percentages. The discussion adds that consensus-based decision-making was reported by 95.8% of participants.

> "Most students reported a substantial (n = 79/191, 41.4%) or very substantial (n = 91/191, 47.6%) benefit from their partner’s knowledge. Nearly all respondents also perceived their own contribution as essential (n = 114/191, 59.7%) or very essential (n = 69/191, 36.1%) to the examination outcome."
