## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In an OSCE video study, the DPCC-MS and the Côté et al. instrument had similar internal consistency (both above .90) and convergent validity with a global rating, but the DPCC-MS had higher mean inter-rater reliability (mean ICC = .61 vs .45). [→ Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc 2017](#renaud-jean-sebastien-cote-luc-2017)

## Evidence

### Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc 2017

Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc. (2017). Validation of the 5-item doctor-patient communication competency instrument for medical students (DPCC-MS) using two years of assessment data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1026-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Phase 2 comparison in which eleven clinical teachers assessed 35 OSCE videos with the DPCC-MS, the 13-item Côté et al. instrument, and a 2-item global assessment. "the DPCC-MS resulted in higher levels of inter-rater reliability than the Côté et al. scale", with mean ICC .61 versus .45.

> "Overall, the DPCC-MS resulted in higher levels of inter-rater reliability than the Côté et al. scale, which were good and fair respectively based on the mean and median ICC for the four rater pairs."
