### Mueller Sophia 2011

Mueller Sophia, Weichert Nina, Stoecklein Veit, Hammitzsch Ariane, Pascuito Giulia, Krug Christian, Holzer Matthias, Pfeiffer Mona, Siebeck Matthias, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2011). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

LOESS-smoothed plot of 3,260 pooled TSS/QI data points from both semesters (Figure 3). The article reports the inverse relation as an observed pattern, with TSS/QI tending to be higher with two assessments at the same QI; no effect size is printed.

> "TSS/QI and QI appear as inversely related, and TSS/QI tends to be higher in participants with 2 assessments than in those with 1 assessment, given the same QI."
