National Charter School Resource Center 2021¶

National Charter School Resource Center, Evan, A., & Sullivan, H. (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools, Part 2: The Roles and Perspectives of Charter School Leaders and Board Members. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616211

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview study of 12 school leaders from 11 recently distressed charter schools across 7 states. The report states "schools often showed signs of distress when leaders cannot execute systems to address school needs," with leaders described as overtasked and defensive or suspicious of support.

"Our research found schools often showed signs of distress when leaders cannot execute systems to address school needs."
