The article reports, citing prior research [1–3] (Trowbridge & McDermott 1980, 1981; Beichner 1994), that for almost thirty years studies worldwide have found students have serious difficulties studying MUA, with the main mistakes being "ineficiencia en la operatividad matemática" and graph-reading problems. The article narrates these cited findings without describing their designs.

> "las principales equivocaciones que cometen los estudiantes est´an relacionadas con: ineﬁciencia en la operatividad matem´atica; la inadecuada narraci´on de situaciones representadas gr´aﬁcamente; la lectura incorrecta de gr´aﬁcos simples y la no identiﬁcaci´on e interpretaci´on de ´areas y pendientes"
