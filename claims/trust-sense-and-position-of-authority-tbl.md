## Evidence

### N. Mitchell 2025

N. Mitchell, M. Rashid, J. Foulds, and K. Forbes. (2025). Thinking About the Why: A Qualitative Study on Students' Perspectives of Paediatric Team‐Based Learning Discussions. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70161

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme analysis of six focus groups found students noted medicine's hierarchy, trusting clinical knowledge more when heard from a staff or resident than a peer, and were more influenced by peers who "were confident and knew what they were speaking about".

> "The importance of trust in others was described by students, where both a sense of authority and someone's position of authority would increase students' trust in the knowledge, experiences and opinions of others."
