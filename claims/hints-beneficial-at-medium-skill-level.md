## Evidence

### Aleven 2016

Aleven, V., Roll, I., McLaren, B. M., & Koedinger, K. R. (2016). Help Helps, But Only So Much: Research on Help Seeking with Intelligent Tutoring Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0089-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

A data mining study analyzing Geometry Cognitive Tutor log data examined whether hint use on a step affected the next opportunity to apply the same knowledge component, finding hints beneficial only at medium skill; the effect was seen in tutor data but apparently not measurable at post test.

> "They found that hints are beneficial for local within-tutor learning when students have a medium level of skill. When students have a low or high level of skill, attempts at solving (without help) are more effective."
