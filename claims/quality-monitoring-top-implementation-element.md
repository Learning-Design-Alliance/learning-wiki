# Quality monitoring was the most common implementation element and regular support to the intervention receiver the most common process element in effective OSTA interventions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among implementation elements, quality monitoring had the highest frequency (peak FV 13), followed by distributing educational materials (12) and ongoing consultation (10). [→ Engell 2020](#engell-2020)
`q3 i?` Process elements with peak FVs included received by caregiver (14), delivered by professional (13), delivered by caregiver (13), 1-on-1 delivery (12), repeated training (12), and feedback on performance (12). [→ Engell 2020](#engell-2020)

`q3 · i?`

Systematic review frequency analysis of 49 process and 36 implementation elements coded across included interventions. The review reports these peak FVs, noting they are practice element–specific and vary by combination; no effect sizes were computed.

> "Overall, process elements with peak FVs were received by caregiver (14), delivered by professional (13), delivered by caregiver (13), 1on1 delivery (12), repeated training (12), and feedback on performance (12). Implementation elements with peak FVs were quality monitoring (13), distributing educational materials (12), and ongoing consultation (10)."
