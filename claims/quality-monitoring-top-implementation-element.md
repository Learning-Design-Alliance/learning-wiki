Common Elements of Practice, Process and Implementation in Out-of-School-Time Academic Interventions for At-risk Children: a Systematic Review. `q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review frequency analysis of 49 process and 36 implementation elements coded across included interventions. The review reports these peak FVs, noting they are practice element–specific and vary by combination; no effect sizes were computed.

> "Overall, process elements with peak FVs were received by caregiver (14), delivered by professional (13), delivered by caregiver (13), 1on1 delivery (12), repeated training (12), and feedback on performance (12). Implementation elements with peak FVs were quality monitoring (13), distributing educational materials (12), and ongoing consultation (10)."
