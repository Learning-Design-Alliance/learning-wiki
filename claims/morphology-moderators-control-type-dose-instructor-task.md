# Instructional moderators: effects significant against passive controls and for researcher-delivered, high-dose, and experimenter-designed trained-item tasks, but moderator contrasts not significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Effects were distinguishable from zero against passive controls (0.23) and other non-linguistic controls (0.57) but not against active linguistic controls (0.07); Wald tests comparing moderator subgroups were not significant. [→ Danielle Colenbrander 2024](#danielle-colenbrander-2024)

## Evidence

### Danielle Colenbrander 2024

Danielle Colenbrander, Alexa von Hagen, Saskia Kohnen, Signy Wegener, Katherine Ko, Elisabeth Beyersmann, Ali Behzadnia, Rauno Parrila, Anne Castles. (2024). The Effects of Morphological Instruction on Literacy Outcomes for Children in English-Speaking Countries: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09953-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analyses across 172 effect sizes. Against passive controls the effect was 0.23 (p < .05) and against other controls 0.57 (p < .01), but "Effects were not statistically distinguishable from zero when morphology interventions were compared to active linguistic control groups (SMC/SMD = 0.07; SE = 0.10; nstudies = 16; neffect.sizses = 82)". Researcher-delivered (0.35), >20-hour dose (0.54), and experimenter-designed trained-item tasks (0.51) were also significant, while standardised tasks were not.
