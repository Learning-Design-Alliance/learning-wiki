Comparison of simulation debriefs with traditional needs assessment methods: a qualitative exploratory study in a critical care community setting. BMJ Open. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretive discussion of how simulation debriefs could prioritise needs. The article states debriefs "may also help identify the highest priority needs" because readily uncovered needs may be highest priority. This is an author interpretation, not a tested result.

> "Performing simulation debriefs may also help identify the highest priority needs and determine the initial set of needs to be targeted, in that the needs that are most readily uncovered may be the highest priority contrasted to those that require more probing and questioning."
