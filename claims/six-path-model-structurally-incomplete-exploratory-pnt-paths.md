# The six-path specification was structurally incomplete: exploratory direct paths from PNT to ACS, SRL, and CT improved fit beyond the hypothesised model

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Adding a direct PNT → ACS path substantially improved fit (Δχ2(1) = 52.87, p < 0.001), and PNT showed an independent association with ACS (β = 0.304) while the LOU → ACS coefficient fell from 0.504 to 0.311. [→ Yang Z 2026](#yang-z-2026)
`q2 i3` Direct PNT → SRL and PNT → CT paths were small (β = 0.092 and β = 0.103) but produced a further significant fit improvement. [→ Yang Z 2026 (2)](#yang-z-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Yang Z 2026

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc exploratory structural extension on the student sample. In this model 'PNT remained positively associated with LOU, β = 0.544', and both LOU and PNT were positively associated with ACS (β = 0.311 and β = 0.304).

> "Adding a direct path from PNT to ACS substantially improved model fit, Δχ2(1) = 52.87, p < 0.001, and reduced SRMR from 0.055 to 0.045."

### Yang Z 2026 (2)

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Second exploratory extension adding direct paths from PNT to SRL and CT (Δχ2(2) = 9.05, p = 0.011; SRMR decreased to 0.044). PNT remained positively associated with LOU (β = 0.535) and with ACS (β = 0.297).

> "In the fully expanded model, the PNT → SRL and PNT → CT associations were small, β = 0.092, p = 0.030, and β = 0.103, p = 0.018, respectively."
