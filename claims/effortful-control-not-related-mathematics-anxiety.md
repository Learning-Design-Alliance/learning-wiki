## Evidence

### Sharma 2016

Sharma, Y. (2016). Alleviating mathematics anxiety of elementary school students: A situated perspective. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(2), 509-517. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1110268

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 2×2 ANCOVA of the quasi-experiment (n=99, covariate pre-mathematics anxiety), the effortful control main effect was non-significant: "F (1, 94) = .052, p>0.05". No effect size was printed for this null finding; equivalence was not tested.

> "From Table 2, it can be seen that F (1, 94) = .052, p>0.05 is not significant. It indicates that mathematics anxiety was not correlated with effortful control of grade VII students."
