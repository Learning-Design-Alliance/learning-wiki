q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of coach logs filled out for the 22 teachers who began the intervention shows participation in group learning, coach modeling, collaborative planning, and observations was 80%, 80%, 77%, and 76% for local-coach teachers versus 47% overall completion for no-local-coach teachers, per the printed figure.

"Teacher participation in the required professional learning activities was particularly low for those without a local coach. Participation was higher for those with a local coach, but even those teachers with local coaches were not able to attend all the activities."
