Controlled & AI-Free Assessment room discussion of where AI helps learning rather than short-circuiting it. The report notes generative AI produces personalized tutorials but weaker students need training before benefiting, so benefit accrues fastest to those who need it least.

"AI is most useful to students who already understand the material, who have solid computational thinking, or who understand the characteristics of the tool well enough to direct it."
