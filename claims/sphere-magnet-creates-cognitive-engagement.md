## Evidence

### Okulu 2018

Okulu, H. Z., & Oğuz Ünver, A. (2018). The Process of Facilitating Knowledge Acquisition and Retention: An Inquiry into Magnetic Poles with Challenging Questions. International Education Studies, 11(5), 25-37. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n5p25

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Conclusion drawn from the study's research data on the frequency values of correct answers related to different magnets in pretest and retention test, in which the sphere magnet had no correct-answer variable available. The study reports that 58 participants correctly identified two poles on the sphere magnet in the retention test.

> "Finally, with a sphere magnet, both upper-lower or internal-external surfaces have lost their functions and the inquiry begins with the question “How to find the poles of a magnet?” In that process, students get to engage and feel that they do not know something that they should know."
