Evidence¶

Tari 2008¶

Somayeh Kazemi Tari. (2008). The Effect of Two Types of Feedback on Iranian EFL Learners' Tense-marker Production. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.2n.1p.108

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest comparison between the two intact classes of 15 learners each (n=15 per group). Table 5 prints posttest M=12.3 (SD=1.4) for the explicit correction group and M=10.1 (SD=1.8) for the or-choice group; the between-group t-test gave t=2.12, p<0.05, with no effect size printed.

"The means of the two groups on posttest, shown in Table 5 and also evident in Figure 1 show that the explicit correction group outperformed the or-choice group on the posttest: t=2.12, p<0.05."
