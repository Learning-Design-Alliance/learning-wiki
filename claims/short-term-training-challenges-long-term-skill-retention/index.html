This is the protocol's discussion-section synthesis (authors' interpretation) of prior studies of short-term courses, which are often delivered as extended learning of a few hours to days due to their flexible nature and reduced resource mandate. No effect sizes are printed; the underlying studies were not read for this protocol.

"However, studies have also reported significant drawbacks associated with such training programmes, along with non-longitudinal training models, resulting in challenges in estimating long-term skill retention, either due to the accelerated nature of the programmes or lack of follow-up assessments within the same population."
