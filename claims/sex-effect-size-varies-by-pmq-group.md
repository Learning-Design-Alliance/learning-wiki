## Evidence

### Unwin Emily 2018

Unwin Emily, Potts Henry W. W., Dacre Jane, Elder Andrew, Woolf Katherine. (2018). Passing MRCP (UK) PACES: a cross-sectional study examining the performance of doctors by sex and country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1178-2

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup logistic regression analysis of 2179 non-UK medical graduates without GMC registration, adjusted for ethnicity. The article prints "OR = 1.99; 95% CI = 1.65-2.39" for female versus male candidates in this group.

> "Female non-UK graduates who were not registered with the GMC had nearly twice the odds of passing PACES compared to their male counterparts (OR = 1.99; 95% CI = 1.65-2.39; P < 0.0001); see Table 2."
