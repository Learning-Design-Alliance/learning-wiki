Evidence¶

Shazia Rafiullah Miller 1999¶

Shazia Rafiullah Miller, Stuart Luppescu, Robert M. Gladden, and John Q. Easton. (1999). How Do Kenwood Academy Students Perform? Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/how-do-kenwood-graduates-perform

q2 · i? · associational · r2

From the report's Definitions section, an observational assertion about the on-track measure: "Whether or not students are on track is correlated with whether they will graduate, so it is an early indicator of students' academic success." The report also states being off track correlates with dropping out. No coefficient is printed.
