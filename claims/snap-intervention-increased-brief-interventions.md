## Evidence

### Chan Bibiana C 2013

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the SNAP trial Results section; post hoc analyses indicated "differences in linear trends over time", with intervention-group scores increasing significantly while control scores did not change. Printed interaction p-values: smoking <0.001, physical activity 0.007, weight 0.025.

> "The repeated measure analyses of variance comparing the intervention and control groups on all five management scores at baseline, six and twelve months ( Table 2) showed significant group-by-time interaction effects for nurses' management of smoking, physical activity and weight."
