# A high proportion of high-performing classmates lowers sixth-graders' situational academic self-concept, an experimental BFLPE at the extreme contrast

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The 80% hand-raising condition produced a significant negative effect on situational self-concept versus 20% (β = −0.12, p = 0.015, d = 0.30), while 35% and 65% showed no significant effect. [→ Lisa Hasenbein 2024](#lisa-hasenbein-2024)

## Evidence

### Lisa Hasenbein 2024

Lisa Hasenbein, Ulrich Trautwein, Jens-Uwe Hahn, Stephan Soller, Richard Göllner. (2024). An experimental test of the Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect using an immersive virtual reality classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09646-4

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized IVR experiment, regression of situational self-concept on dummy-coded conditions found only the extreme contrast significant, with the printed effect "d = 0.30"; contrasts of 35% (p = 0.390) and 65% (p = 0.252) vs. 20% were nonsignificant.

> "the condition with 80% hand-raising classmates had a statistically significant negative effect on the mean level of situational self-concept compared with the 20% condition (β = −0.12, SE = 0.05, p = 0.015, d = 0.30)"
