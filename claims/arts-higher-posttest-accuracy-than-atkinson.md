## Evidence

### Mettler et al. 2011

Everett Mettler, Christine M. Massey, Philip J. Kellman. (2011). Improving Adaptive Learning Technology through the Use of Response Times. Proceedings of the 33rd Annual Meeting of the Cognitive Science Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED549436

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized geography experiment with 50 undergraduates; the article reports immediate post-test accuracy means of 0.827 (ARTS) vs 0.732 (Atkinson), t(48)=2.39, p=0.021; no effect size printed.

> "Post-test accuracy considered apart from learning trials invested was also reliably higher in the ARTS condition (M=0.827 vs. 0.732), (t(48)=2.39, p=0.021)."
