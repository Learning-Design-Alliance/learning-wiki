### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of 504 children with ADHD with hyperactivity/impulsivity as predictor. The direct pathway to mathematics was significant in model 1 but not model 2; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "This indicates hyperactivity/impulsivity symptom severity significantly predicts mathematics performance, but this relation is not mediated through PS or WM"
