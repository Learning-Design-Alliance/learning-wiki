# State accountability systems that rank schools on FAFSA completion are perceived to penalize schools whose students pursue non-college pathways

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` School staff argued that FAFSA completion ranking systems, such as Illinois' tiered bronze-to-platinum designations, lower school ratings when students legitimately opt out for military or other non-college pathways. [→ Victoria Jackson 2026](#victoria-jackson-2026)

### Victoria Jackson 2026

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the qualitative interviews, an Illinois school counselor argued that students entering the military have "no reason to apply for college financial aid," yet "our school rating is [lowered] because those students opt out." Illinois awards tiered designations at roughly 65%, 75%, 85%, and 95% completion.

> "[Y]et our school rating is [lowered] because those students opt out."
