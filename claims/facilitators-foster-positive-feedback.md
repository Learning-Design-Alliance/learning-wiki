`q2 i?` Facilitators emphasized positive feedback first, believing students' learning increased when they received it, and encouraged self-critical students to highlight positive aspects of their performances. [→ Lervik W 2025](#lervik-w-2025)

## Evidence

### Lervik W 2025

Lervik W, Solberg M, Wiig AC, Berg H. (2025). Turning nursing students' mistakes into resources for learning in simulation-based training: facilitators' assumptions about providing feedback in debriefing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06628-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data from nine facilitators: an experienced facilitator (Mary) stated "Everyone must get something positive back" and that positive aspects must be addressed first, by facilitators and observers alike. Facilitators linked mastery to self-esteem and future professional roles.

> "I am very concerned that they receive positive feedback. Everyone must get something positive back. One must address those things first and focus on what they did well, and it applies to me as a facilitator, and it applies to the observers."
