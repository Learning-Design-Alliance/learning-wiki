Evidence¶

Pozdniakov 2026¶

Pozdniakov, S., Brazil, J., Banihashem, S. K., Noroozi, O., Gašević, D., Sadiq, S., & Khosravi, H. (2026). AI assistance in peer feedback provision: Pedagogically sound, but minimally adopted. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105591

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Comparing acted-on versus ignored AI assistance in a stratified sample, the study found that "less accurate, less complete in describing the original peer feedback, or less positively worded" assistance was more likely to prompt revision, with small-to-medium effect sizes.
