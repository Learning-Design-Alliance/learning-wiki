Evidence¶

Ngo Hazel 2025¶

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Univariate mixed-effects meta-regression within the random-effects pairwise meta-analysis (Table 2: didactic Yes k = 88, SMD .59; No k = 21, SMD .24; Q = 6.33, p = .01). The article reports didactic interventions "were more effective (d = 0.61, 95% CI: 0.45, 0.78)" than those without. The authors note this diverges from a previous meta-analysis of 18 medical-student RCTs that found no single teaching method superior.

"Interventions using didactic teaching methods were more effective (d = 0.61, 95% CI: 0.45, 0.78), on average, than those that did not use this method (d = 0.24, 95% CI:,12, 0.36)."
