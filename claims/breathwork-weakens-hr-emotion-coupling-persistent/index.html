Evidence¶

Wang 2026¶

Wang, P., Ganushchak, L., Welie, C., & van Steensel, R. (2026). Learning proximal cyclic sighing buffers anxiety across learning moments and boredom across days. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00446-2

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory, non-preregistered Gaussian graphical models of anxiety, enjoyment, boredom and heart rate within the breathwork group (n = 47), compared across phases with the Network Comparison Test. Global strength did not differ (p = .26); edge tests localized change to HR–anxiety and HR–enjoyment links (both p < .02), with no reversion by Post (M = 0.03, p = .95).

"The NCT indicated a significant structural change from Baseline to Intervention (M = 0.11, p = .030), suggesting that the pattern of covariation among affective states and HR shifted when participants were practicing breathwork."
