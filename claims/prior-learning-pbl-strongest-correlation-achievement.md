## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paper test scores correlated with peer evaluation scores for degree of prior learning in PBL sessions (rs = 0.551, p < 0.001), the strongest of the domain correlations with achievement. [→ Linn Zayar 2022](#linn-zayar-2022)

## Evidence

### Linn Zayar 2022

Linn Zayar, Tashiro Yasura, Morio Kunimasa, Hori Hiroki. (2022). Peer evaluations of group work in different years of medical school and academic achievement: how are they related?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03165-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same 340-student cohort between peer evaluation domain scores and preclinical paper test scores. The degree of prior learning in PBL showed "rs = 0.551, p < 0.001" with paper test scores, stronger than the book review session correlation (rs = 0.338).

> "Paper test scores were found to be correlated with peer evaluation scores for the degree of prior learning in book review sessions (rs = 0.338, p < 0.001) and to those in PBL sessions (rs = 0.551, p < 0.001)"
