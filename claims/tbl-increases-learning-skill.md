# TBL significantly increases students' learning skill compared with LBL (pooled SMD = 2.70)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In five studies reporting learning skill, the pooled TBL effect versus LBL was significant in a random-effects model (SMD = 2.70, 95% CI: 1.33–4.07). [→ Chen Minjian 2018](#chen-minjian-2018)

## Evidence

### Chen Minjian 2018

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. (2018). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of team-based learning on medical education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1179-1

`q3 · i3`

Random-effects pooling of self-directed learning skill outcomes from questionnaire surveys in 5 of the 13 included studies (607 subjects). The pooled effect was "SMD = 2.70, 95% CI: 1.33–4.07" with very high heterogeneity (I2 = 97.4%).
