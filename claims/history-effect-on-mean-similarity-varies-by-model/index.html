Linear mixed-effects model (Table 1; N = 488, 61 focal messages, random intercept for focal message) with LLM type, history condition, their interaction, and LLM-human similarity as fixed effects. "the interaction terms for GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini were positive and statistically significant."

"For the Similarity Mean Model, the with-history condition was associated with lower mean pairwise similarity for the reference model, GPT-4o mini. However, the interaction terms for GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini were positive and statistically significant."

Related Claims¶

  • Replies generated with chat history were only moderately similar to replies generated without history (median cross-history similarity around 0.40 to 0.45) — related
  • Without chat history, semantically similar focal messages elicited similar LLM replies; this positive relationship was weaker when history was included — related
  • GPT-5.4 showed the highest within-model reply similarity, and models within the GPT-5.4 family were more similar to one another than to GPT-4o mini — related
  • GPT-5.4 showed higher variation in pairwise reply similarity than GPT-4o mini, GPT-5.4 nano significantly lower, and with-history increased variation for the reference model — related
  • LLM-generated replies were consistently not similar to human replies, though including chat history slightly improved alignment — related
