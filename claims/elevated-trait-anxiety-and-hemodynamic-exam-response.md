### Usichenko Taras 2020

Usichenko Taras, Wenzel Anna, Klausenitz Catharina, Petersmann Astrid, Hesse Thomas, Neumann Nicola, Hahnenkamp Klaus. (2020). Auricular stimulation vs. expressive writing for exam anxiety in medical students – A randomized crossover investigation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238307

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Baseline characteristic of the enrolled sample (25 females; mean age 23 ± 3): trait anxiety "was 46 ± 10, significantly exceeds the mean found in the norm sample for the female population aged between 15 and 29 years" with d = 0.9; a one-sample comparison, not a between-condition test.

> "The trait anxiety, assessed with STAI was 46 ± 10, significantly exceeds the mean found in the norm sample for the female population aged between 15 and 29 years: 36 ± 10; t37 = 4.3; P < 0.001; d = 0.9"

### Usichenko Taras 2020 (2)

Usichenko Taras, Wenzel Anna, Klausenitz Catharina, Petersmann Astrid, Hesse Thomas, Neumann Nicola, Hahnenkamp Klaus. (2020). Auricular stimulation vs. expressive writing for exam anxiety in medical students – A randomized crossover investigation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238307

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

General linear model for repeated measures of hemodynamic parameters across the three crossover conditions: systolic blood pressure and heart rate "increased on the day of exam in comparison with baseline" under all conditions; only diastolic pressure rose further after the exam after AS and EW.

> "Systolic blood pressure and heart rate increased on the day of exam in comparison with baseline (P<0.01; Fig 6 and Supplementary Table) for all conditions."
