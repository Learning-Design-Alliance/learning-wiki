### Seashore 1922

Seashore, C. E. (1922). Elementary tests in psychology. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The author's personal observation of four infants during their first ten days, offered as evidence that elemental sensory discriminations manifest themselves very early in infancy and that the process is automatic. No measurements or statistics are reported.

> "I have observed four infants during the first ten days with reference to their localization of sound, and in all four cases a surprising ability to observe the direction of a source of sound was present as soon as the infant began to notice sound at all."
