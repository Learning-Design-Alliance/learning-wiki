# Covert retrieval is the most time-efficient practice strategy, yielding the largest gains per minute of practice for both terms and definitions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For participants who practiced and were tested on definitions, gains per minute were larger for covert retrieval than overt retrieval (gav = 1.30) and restudy (gav = 1.45). [→ Michelle L. Rivers 2025](#michelle-l-rivers-2025)
`q3 i3` The same efficiency ordering held for terms: covert > overt > restudy in gains per minute (ηp2 = 0.40). [→ Michelle L. Rivers 2025 (2)](#michelle-l-rivers-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Michelle L. Rivers 2025

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Gains-per-minute analysis (Fig. 4; 153 participants who practiced and were tested on definitions) showed covert retrieval (M = 0.12) beat overt retrieval (M = 0.05), "g av = 1.30", and restudy (M = 0.04), gav = 1.45; the ANOVA gave ηp2 = 0.53.

> "Follow-up analyses revealed that gains per minute were larger for covert retrieval (M = 0.12, SE = 0.01) than for overt retrieval (M = 0.05, SE = 0.002), t(152) = 12.96, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.01, 0.06], g av = 1.30;"

### Michelle L. Rivers 2025 (2)

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

For the 95 participants who practiced and were tested on terms, covert retrieval (M = 0.24) beat overt retrieval (M = 0.16), "g av = 0.74", and restudy (M = 0.13), gav = 0.99; the ANOVA gave ηp2 = 0.40.

> "Follow-up analyses revealed that gains per minute were larger for covert retrieval (M = 0.24, SE = 0.01) than for overt retrieval (M = 0.16, SE = 0.01), t(94) = 8.28, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.06, 0.10], g av = 0.74;"
