## Evidence

### B. P. Johnson 2023

B. P. Johnson, I. Iturrate, R. Y. Fakhreddine, M. Bönstrup, E. R. Buch, E. M. Robertson and L. G. Cohen. (2023). Generalization of procedural motor sequence learning after a single practice trial. npj Science of Learning 8:45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00194-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 (n = 551, MTurk) randomly assigned participants to 1, 2, 5, or 12 trials of skill A before testing skill B; tapping speed measured generalization. Only the one-trial group showed "the one trial practice group was the only group that started skill B at a higher performance level than they ended skill A ( p = 0.001".

> "Relating performance at the end of skill A and the onset of skill B ( GenB0 /C0 Af ), we found that the one trial practice group was the only group that started skill B at a higher performance level than they ended skill A ( p = 0.001, Fig. 1d)."
