## Evidence

### Bahmani Dena Sadeghi 2018

Bahmani Dena Sadeghi, Faraji Parviz, Faraji Robab, Lang Undine E., Holsboer-Trachsler Edith, Brand Serge. (2018). Is emotional functioning related to academic achievement among university students? Results from a cross-sectional Iranian sample. Brazilian Journal of Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1590/1516-4446-2017-2434

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations among the 275 students (Table 1): loneliness correlated r = 0.44 with depression, r = -0.29 with satisfaction with family, r = -0.26 with satisfaction with other people, and r = -0.25 with overall social satisfaction; depression correlated r = -0.49 with family satisfaction.

> "Higher loneliness scores were associated with higher depression scores and with lower scores on all dimensions of social satisfaction."
