# Intervention and control groups did not differ significantly in attitudes towards statistics at pre-test

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-test Rasch scores of the two groups were very close (.51 vs .55) and an independent t-test showed no significant difference. [→ Koparan 2014](#koparan-2014)

## Evidence

### Koparan 2014

Koparan, T., & Güven, B. (2014). The Effect on the 8th Grade Students' Attitude towards Statistics of Project Based Learning. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(2), 73-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent two-sample t-test on pre-test Rasch scores of 35 intervention and 35 control students. The article reports "t(68) = -.336, p > .05" with pre-test means of .51 and .55 logits, establishing baseline equivalence.

> "The result of the intervention and control groups compared to pretest prior to the application by the t test results, there is no difference between ATS of groups t(68) = -.336, p > .05."
