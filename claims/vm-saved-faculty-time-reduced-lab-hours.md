## Evidence

### Triola Marc M 2011

Triola Marc M, Holloway William J. (2011). Enhanced virtual microscopy for collaborative education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Administrative/workflow report from the implementing course: 15 lab sessions each taught by 14 faculty preceptors; session duration fell from three hours to two hours, "saving 210 hours per year in faculty time" and freeing 15 hours of laboratory space.

> "Due to the efficiencies and workflow of the VM system, laboratory sessions were reduced from three hours to two hours without reducing the number of slides taught in each session. The course's 15 laboratory sessions are each taught by 14 faculty preceptors overseeing groups of students. The laboratory duration reduced the overall precepting time from 630 to 420 hours, saving 210 hours per year in faculty time and freeing up an additional 15 hours of laboratory space for other courses."
