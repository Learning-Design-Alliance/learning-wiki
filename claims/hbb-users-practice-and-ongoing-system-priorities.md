## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a 59-question online survey, 102 frontline HBB users ranked sufficient time for practice during the workshop (91%), enough mannequins to reach a ratio of 1 mannequin per 2 participants (54%), and a system for ongoing practice after the workshop (87%) as the most important ways to ensure providers could perform their skills. ### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional semistructured online survey of 102 frontline HBB users (65% physicians, 77% North America-based, 93% global facilitators). Respondents prioritized "sufficient time for practice during the workshop" and a post-workshop ongoing practice system over training greater numbers of providers (66% vs 33%).

> "respondents identified sufficient time for practice during the workshop (91%), enough mannequins to reach the goal ratio of 1 mannequin per 2 participants (54%), and a system for ongoing practice after the workshop (87%)"
