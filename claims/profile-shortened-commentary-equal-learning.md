### Fletcher 1955

Fletcher, Richard M. (1955). Profile Analysis and Its Effect on Learning When Used To Shorten Recorded Film Commentaries. Technical Report SDC Z69-7-55, Instructional Film Research Program, Pennsylvania State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Fletcher+profile+analysis+film+commentaries

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled experiment with companies of men hearing shortened Human Reproduction commentaries; the abstract reports "equal learning" for the profile version versus the longer versions. No effect size is printed.

> "The major finding of this study was that, with the film commentary of Human Reproduction there was equal learning for those hearing the shortest version--the profile vel~sion, as compared to the longer script writers' and full versions."
