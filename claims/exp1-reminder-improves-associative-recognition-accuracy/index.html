Evidence¶

Michael Batashvili 2022¶

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

A 2×2 repeated-measures ANOVA on associative recognition accuracy in a three-day within-subjects experiment with 40 young adults. The reminder main effect was significant, with reminder pairs at Mean = 92% versus 85% for no reminder. The article prints "η² = 0.22" for this effect.

"For accuracy, there was a significant main effect of reminder, F(1,39) = 29.43, p < 0.001, η² = 0.22, BF 10 = 0.75, showing that participants had greater accuracy for pairs where a reminder was employed"
