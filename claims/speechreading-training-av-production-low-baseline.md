## Evidence

### Pimperton 2019

Pimperton, H., Kyle, F., Hulme, C., Harris, M., Beedie, I., Ralph-Lewis, A., Worster, E., Rees, R., Donlan, C., & MacSweeney, M. (2019). Computerized Speechreading Training for Deaf Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research. https://doi.org/10.1044/2019_JSLHR-H-19-0073

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ANCOVA for AV speech production at T2 failed the equal-slopes assumption; the interaction indicated "a shallower slope in the intervention group than the maths control group." Follow-up tests showed a significant advantage only 1 SD below the covariate mean; the authors urge caution. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "for children scoring at 1 SD below the mean of the covariate, there was a significant advantage for the intervention group, F(1, 53) = 8.55, p = .005. This pattern needs to be interpreted with caution but suggests that speechreading training improved T2 AV speech production in children who started with particularly low scores on this measure."
