## Evidence

### Zhang Xiangle 2025

Zhang Xiangle, Tian Hongxia. (2025). Modeling information literacy in Chinese physical education: the roles of technology integration, teaching practices, self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26990-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 650 undergraduate PE students in Henan, Hebei, Guangdong, Jiangsu, and Sichuan; the TI → IL path was significant (estimate 0.674, p 0.001, Table 7). The article states "incorporating technological tools and resources into education" supports information literacy.

> "The positive correlation between technological Integration and Information Literacy (TI → IL) highlights the significance of incorporating technological tools and resources into education to improve students' information literacy abilities."
