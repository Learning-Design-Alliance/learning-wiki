## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Z.; Singh, C. (2025). Opportunities and Challenges in Harnessing Digital Technology for Effective Teaching and Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3390/higheredu4010006](https://doi.org/10.3390/higheredu4010006)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

An observational analysis of students' interactions with online learning modules in a university-level introductory physics course. The analysis found that 'students spontaneously use diﬀerent strategies to engage with online learning modules, based on their incoming knowledge and assessment outcomes,' represented in Figure 2B bar graphs. Cluster 1–1 mostly passed modules without studying instruction, cluster 1–4 needed instructional materials from module M-1, and cluster 1–2 changed strategies between the first and last module, which the authors read as active self-regulation.

> "the data show that students spontaneously use diﬀerent strategies to engage with online learning modules, based on their incoming knowledge and assessment outcomes [80]."
