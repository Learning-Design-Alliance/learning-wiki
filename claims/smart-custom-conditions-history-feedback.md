## Evidence

### Gilbert 2017

Gilbert, S. B., Slavina, A., Dorneich, M. C., Sinatra, A. M., Bonner, D., Johnston, J., Holub, J., MacAllister, A., & Winer, E. (2017). Creating a Team Tutor Using GIFT. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0151-2

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Description of the 'smart conditions' implemented for the Surveillance Tutor's Transfer, Acknowledge, and Identify tasks, holding default performance state as 'Unknown' to enable state-change triggering. The authors call this method critical for managing feedback frequency; no user outcome data are reported for it.

> "This approach allowed the custom condition classes to 1) maintain a history of the user's performance (e.g., the last five events) and 2) tell GIFT to provide feedback for ongoing good or bad performance even though a member's or team's official GIFT state performance state (outside the custom conditions) had not changed."
