Evidence¶

Kluger 1996¶

Kluger, A. N., & DeNisi, A. (1996). The effects of feedback interventions on performance: A historical review, a meta-analysis, and a preliminary feedback intervention theory. Psychological Bulletin, 119(2), 254-284. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Sensitivity analysis within the same meta-analysis: 91 effects came from Mikulincer's laboratory experiments, whose weighted average d was -0.39 versus 0.47 for the remaining data. After exclusion, "the weighted variance of d was .92" and 33% of remaining effects were still negative. No effect size is printed for the variability itself.

"Yet, after the exclusion of Mikulincer's studies, the weighted variance of d was .92, such that 33% of the remaining effects were still negative."
