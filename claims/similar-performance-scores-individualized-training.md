### Jiang Luyang 2023

Jiang Luyang, Yang Qingyue, Li Qingyue, Jiang Bailin, Laba Ciren, Feng Yi. (2023). Optimal Duration of High-Fidelity Simulator Training for Bronchoscope-Guided Intubation: A Noninferiority Randomized Trial. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000739

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome comparison (Table 2): simulator performance scores averaged 80.4 versus 82.3 (P = 0.46) and blinded patient performance scores 81.1 versus 83.3 (P = 0.46), both standardized to a 100-scale; no significant difference was detected.

> "There was no significant difference between the groups in the intubation time on simulators and performance scores on simulators during the last 3 simulation procedures. The performance scores in patients were similar across the groups."
