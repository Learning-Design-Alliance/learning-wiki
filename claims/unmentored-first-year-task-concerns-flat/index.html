Olsen 1990¶

Olsen, Dwayne G.; Heyse, Kathy L. (1990). Development and Concerns of First-year and Reentry Teachers with and without Mentors. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED323188

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Panel data from the same longitudinal study, for the 8 first-year teachers without assigned mentors across the three interviews. The authors conclude the Fuller theory "does explain the development of first-year teachers without mentors but not as well as the development of first-year teachers with mentors."
