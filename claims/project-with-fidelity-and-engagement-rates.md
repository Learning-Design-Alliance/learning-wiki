## Evidence

### Nakamoto 2025

Nakamoto, J., Carter, C., & Russo, S. (2025). Implementation and impact of the Project With curriculum in juvenile justice facilities and group homes. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673580

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of facilitator-completed fidelity logs covering 89 percent of lessons across the 50 cohorts; an average of 7.5 youth (range = 2 to 17) participated per lesson. The logs showed "89 percent of the Project With activities were completed," with Lesson 9 lowest at 81 percent.

> "The fidelity log data showed that 89 percent of the Project With activities were completed."

### Nakamoto 2025 (2)

Nakamoto, J., Carter, C., & Russo, S. (2025). Implementation and impact of the Project With curriculum in juvenile justice facilities and group homes. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673580

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Lesson-level fidelity log analysis found site staff support was the weakest implementation component: staff "were supportive of the Project With activities during only 72 percent of the lessons." Facilitators reported easier implementation when staff encouraged participation and minimized distractions.

> "One area of relative weakness from the fidelity log data was that the probation and group home staff were supportive of the Project With activities during only 72 percent of the lessons."
