## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity analysis comparing operative metrics of 4 experts vs 7 novices after three simulated procedures each, using Mann-Whitney U tests with significance at P < .05. Experts were faster at every stage; path length differences were also significant (e.g., 50 vs 232 cm, P = .01).

> "time from exiting stomach to application of first clip (74 vs 242 seconds, P = .01), time from application of first clip to application of last clip (83 vs 281 seconds, P = .02), time from application of last clip to completed dissection of gallbladder from liver bed (333 vs 780 seconds, P = .02)"
