Evidence¶

Tozadore 2025¶

Tozadore, D., Ertug, N., Chaker, Y., & Abderrahim, M. (2025). RoboBuddy in the Classroom: Exploring LLM-Powered Social Robots for Storytelling in Learning and Integration Activities. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16706 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the 1-week classroom study, one class (Year 2-1) received Lecture-to-Storytelling mode and the other (Year 2-2) Robot Lecture Explanation mode; students rated enjoyment on a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports "A significant difference was found" on both days, with the printed W and p-values; no effect size is given.

"A significant difference was found between the enjoyment of activities with storytelling, performed with student from Year 2-1, and with explanation group, Year 2-2, both on Monday (W=25, p-value=0.033) and on Tuesday (W=86, p-value=0.024)."

Tozadore 2025 (2)¶

Tozadore, D., Ertug, N., Chaker, Y., & Abderrahim, M. (2025). RoboBuddy in the Classroom: Exploring LLM-Powered Social Robots for Storytelling in Learning and Integration Activities. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16706. doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706

q2 · i? · design · r2

In the Thursday activity both classes did the same thing: each child shared a word from their own or another language and the robot made up a short English paragraph using it. The article reports "a significant difference was found" between the classes on it (W=72, p-value=0.04); on Wednesday, the one day both classes had storytelling, no difference was found (W=73, p-value=0.177). Enjoyment was rated for every activity on the Friday. No effect size is printed.

"Again, a significant difference was found (W=72, p-value=0.04) in the language activity on Thursday."
