Levitan 2024¶

Levitan, S., & Munyan-Penney, N. (2024). Meaningful Assessment Reporting: Making Assessment Reports More Meaningful for Students & Families. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658190

q2 · i? · review · r1

Nationally representative polling conducted by EdTrust, reported in the brief's introduction, found that "About 1 in 2 parents (45%) were unaware" grade-level information existed; the brief also reports 1 in 3 unaware of parent-teacher conferences and fewer than 10% aware of hotlines, data nights, and training.
