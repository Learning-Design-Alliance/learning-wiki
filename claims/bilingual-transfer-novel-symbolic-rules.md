This is the authors' interpretation (type e) in the general discussion, linking their hard-item results to Stocco and Prat's finding that bilinguals responded faster on novel trials. It is an explanatory claim, not a directly tested transfer measure.

> "Since participants did not receive any training on the hard items in the Symbol Math task, these can be consid- ered transfer items. Better performance on these items suggests that bilingualsmayhaveaspecificadvantageintransferringtheirunder- standing of novel symbolic rules to a new context."
