Evidence¶

Woods 2007¶

Woods, N.N., Brooks, L.R. & Norman, G.R. (2007). The role of biomedical knowledge in diagnosis of difficult clinical cases. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 12, 417–426. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-006-9054-y

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experiment 1: novices learned artificial diseases via causal mechanisms or feature lists and were tested on case summaries with irrelevant findings. The article reports that "Participants with an understanding of underlying mechanisms out performed their counterparts on both types of cases." No effect size is printed.
