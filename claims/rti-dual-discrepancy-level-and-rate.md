## Evidence

### Powell 2007

Powell, S. R., & Seethaler, P. M. (2007). Grade One: Math Computation (Math Case Study #1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED502453

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The case study's overview of RTI defines the identification criterion as a "dual discrepancy in terms of level and rate," contrasting RTI with the traditional IQ/achievement discrepancy method of LD identification. No empirical data are offered for this definitional claim.

> "With RTI, special education is considered only if a student's performance reveals a dual discrepancy in terms of level and rate: The student a) performs below the level demonstrated by classroom peers, and b) demonstrates a learning rate substantially below that of classmates."
