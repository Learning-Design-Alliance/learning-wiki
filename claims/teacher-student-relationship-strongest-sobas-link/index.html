Evidence¶

M.M. Chiu 2016¶

M.M. Chiu, B.W.Y. Chow, C. McBride, S.T. Mol. (2016). Students’ sense of belonging at school in 41 countries: cross-cultural variability. Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022022115617031

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analyses of survey and test data from 193,073 15-year-old students in 41 countries showed the teacher-student relationship "had the strongest link with SOBAS" and mediated the egalitarianism link. No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Second, the teacher-student relationship had the strongest link with SOBAS and mediated the link between egalitarianism and SOBAS."
