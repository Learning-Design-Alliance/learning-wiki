### Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies 2012

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/364

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Randomized field trial by McMaster et al. (2005) with 41 first-grade nonresponders in eight Nashville schools, comparing PALS to one-on-one adult tutoring; the WWC found "no statistically significant difference between the PALS group and the comparison group." The WWC domain average effect size was –0.15 (improvement index –6), not significant.

> "no statistically significant difference between the PALS group and the comparison group of first graders on the seven alphabetics outcomes: Blending, Rapid Letter Naming, Rapid Letter Sound, Word Attack, Word Identification, Spelling, and Segmentation."
