`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal pre-post cohort assessment: 835 active birth attendants who passed OSCE B after initial training were retested with the same comprehensive skills scenario before refresher training, a mean of 6.7 (SD 2.49) months later. The article reports the pass proportion "fell to 81% (p < 0.0001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Table 3 also shows the proportion of BAs passing the OSCE B skills test post-initial training (99%). Prior to refresher training, the proportion passing fell to 81% (p < 0.0001)"
