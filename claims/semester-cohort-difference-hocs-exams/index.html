Deborah K. Anderson 2023¶

Deborah K. Anderson, Michelle Schoenleber, and Stuart Korshavn. (2023). Higher-Order Clicker Questions Engage Students and Prepare Them for Higher-Order Thinking Activities. Journal of Microbiology and Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1128/jmbe.00151-22

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Follow-up independent-samples t tests interpreting the significant exam question type × semester interaction in the mixed ANOVA. The article reports "semester 2 students outperformed semester 1 students on HOCS exam questions" under both clicker conditions, with medium-to-large effect sizes.

"semester 2 students outperformed semester 1 students on HOCS exam questions, both when reviewed using LOCS clicker questions (t [51] = 3.10, P = 0.003, Cohen’s d = 0.79) and when reviewed using HOCS clicker questions (t [51] = 2.52, P = 0.015, Cohen’s d = 0.67)"
