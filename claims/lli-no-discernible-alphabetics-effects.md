### Leveled Literacy Intervention 2017

Leveled Literacy Intervention. (2017). What Works Clearinghouse Beginning Reading intervention report. U.S. Department of Education. http://www.fountasandpinnell.com/intervention/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Analysis of four DIBELS alphabetics subtests from the 2010 RCT covering 422 students in nine schools. For Nonsense Word Fluency, "For the combined (grades K–2) sample, the WWC found no statistically significant or substantively important difference between the intervention and comparison groups," with domain average effect size 0.13 not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.
