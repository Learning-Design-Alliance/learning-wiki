`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Discussion of the 40-participant SQL-Tutor experiment results: HPK students improved significantly from pre- to post-test only in the Low-Agency condition; the article reports the post-test comparison "with a large effect size (d = .95)".

> "On the contrary, HPK students in the Low-Agency condition had higher post-test scores than the counterparts in the High- Agency condition with a large effect size (d = .95). Therefore Hypothesis 2a was rejected."
