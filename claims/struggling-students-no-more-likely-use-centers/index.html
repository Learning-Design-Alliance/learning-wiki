Descriptive finding from the rural district's systematic study of flex-time use, reported in the Research Findings section of the use case. The article reports that "struggling students were no more likely than their peers to use the centers"; no effect size or test statistic is printed, so this is a detected non-difference, not a measured null effect.

"Notably, struggling students were no more likely than their peers to use the centers, signaling a disconnect between available support and student behavior."
