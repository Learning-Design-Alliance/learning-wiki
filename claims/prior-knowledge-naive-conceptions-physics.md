# Students are not blank slates: naive conceptions from everyday experience can interfere with physics learning, so instructors should assess initial knowledge and build on it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' naive conceptions based on everyday experiences (e.g., motion implies net force; a truck exerts a larger force on a car in a collision) can interfere with learning, so instruction should be targeted where students struggle appropriately and stay engaged.
