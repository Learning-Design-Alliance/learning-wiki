# Partner confidence does not significantly affect whether learners change their assumption in the face of disagreement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Within-subject correlations between partner confidence and change under disagreement did not deviate significantly from zero (d = 0.09), and mean partner confidence did not differ between changed and persisted cases (d = 0.12). [→ Schnaubert 2021](#schnaubert-2021)

## Evidence

### Schnaubert 2021

Schnaubert, L., Krukowski, S., & Bodemer, D. (2021). Assumptions and confidence of others: the impact of socio-cognitive information on metacognitive self-regulation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09269-5

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject experiment testing hypothesis 1c with point-biserial rank correlations between partner confidence and change under disagreement; the mean coefficient was slightly positive (M = 0.04, SD = 0.43) and "no significant deviation from zero (t(50) = 0.70, p = 0.490, d = 0.09)" was found. Mean partner confidence also did not differ between changed and persisted cases (t(50) = 0.83, p = 0.408, d = 0.12).
