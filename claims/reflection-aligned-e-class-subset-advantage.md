## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2022

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Students' attitudes toward experimental physics in a conceptual inquiry-based introductory physics lab. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2021-0121](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2021-0121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary linear model using only the five E-CLASS items aligned with the reflection questions (theoretical range -5 to +5); the curriculum coefficients totaled "a total advantage of 1 .47 ± 0.85 points" over the Traditional curriculum.

> "the RealTime Physics + Reﬂections curriculum predicts a total advantage of 1 .47 ± 0.85 points compared with the Traditional curriculum (i.e., 0.33 + 1.04) for students' score s on a 5-item subset of E-CLASS items related to the reﬂection questions."

### Danny Doucette 2022 (2)

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Students' attitudes toward experimental physics in a conceptual inquiry-based introductory physics lab. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2021-0121](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2021-0121)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mean comparison on the aligned subset: scores "decreased from a pre of 1 .20 ± 0.21 to a post of 0 .12 ± 0.25 with the Traditional curriculum, but were unchanged, remaining at 1 .68 ± 0.24" with RealTime Physics + Reflections.

> "Mean scores on the 5-item subset decreased from a pre of 1 .20 ± 0.21 to a post of 0 .12 ± 0.25 with the Traditional curriculum, but were unchanged, remaining at 1 .68 ± 0.24, with the RealTime Physics + Reﬂections curriculum."
