Descriptive overview of the weighted NEPS SC6 indicators (mathematics bias M = 0.18; reading bias M = 0.06) showed positive bias for both genders in both domains, i.e., postdictions exceeded actual percentage-correct scores.

"On average, men's and women's bias indicators were positive. That is, men and women stated that they solved more items correctly than they really did."
