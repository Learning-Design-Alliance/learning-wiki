## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, D. J., Jain, N. M., Burbano, V., Guzman, J., Keum, D., Kim, S., Kogut, B., & Wright, N. (2026). How assigned AI use before class shapes active student engagement in class. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7585318](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7585318)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive uptake analysis of platform logs from the randomized field experiment (504 students with observed assigned sessions). Reported conversation duration averaged 11.27 minutes (SD = 7.21; n = 851) in the participation estimation sample.

> "Among the 504 students whose two assigned sessions were observed, between 92.2% and 98.9% of assigned students in each session submitted at least one conversation with CAiSEY, and between 38.1% and 67.0% held a conversation of at least ten minutes, which we define as qualifying use (Figure 1; Supplementary Table S1)."
