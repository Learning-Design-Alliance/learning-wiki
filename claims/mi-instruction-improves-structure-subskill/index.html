Habib Soleimani 2012¶

Habib Soleimani, Ahmad Moinnzadeh, Zohreh Kassaian & Saeed Ketabi. (2012). The Effect of Instruction Based on Multiple Intelligences Theory on the Attitude and Learning of General English. English Language Teaching, 5(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p45

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA comparing the 32-student experimental and 29-student control groups on the 10-item structure subtest; the calculated F of 71.68 (df 1 and 58) exceeded the critical value 8.34, indicating a significant difference favoring MI-based teaching of structure.

"the amount of calculated F which is 71.68 with the df of 1 and 58 is larger than the critical amount which is 8.34. Therefore, at the .01 le vel of significance it can be said that teaching structure of general English to students based on Multiple intelligence theory and procedures has had a meaningful effect"
