## Evidence

### Tobin 2015

Tobin, T., Boulmier, P., Zhu, W., Hancock, P., & Muennig, P. (2015). Improving outcomes for refugee children: A case study on the impact of Montessori education along the Thai-Burma border. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 14(3). http://openjournals.library.usyd.edu.au/index.php/IEJ/index

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental difference-in-difference analysis of 66 displaced children aged 3 to 6 in two Mae Sot classrooms, one converted to Montessori and one traditional. The Montessori group went from scoring over 7 points lower at baseline to 1 point higher at follow-up, "for a gain of 8.8 points (p < 0.05)".

> "During the base-line test, the Montessori group scored over 7 points lower than their counterparts. In the follow-up assessment, however, they scored 1 point higher than those in the control group, for a gain of 8.8 points (p < 0.05)."
