## Evidence

### Çobanoğlu Necati 2026

Çobanoğlu Necati, Gökalp Serkan. (2026). Psychological skills and their educational outcomes in adolescence: self-regulation, time management, and academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04368-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapping procedure with 5,000 resamples testing mediation in the SEM. The article reports the standardized indirect effect as β = 0.44 (unstandardized b = 0.28, SE = 0.06), with a "bias-corrected 95% confidence interval" of 0.17 to 0.41 excluding zero.

> "The bias-corrected 95% confidence interval ranged from 0.17 to 0.41, and because the interval did not include zero, the mediation effect was confirmed as statistically significant."
