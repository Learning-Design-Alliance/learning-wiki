### Emery 2021

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Averaged linear model of mean RTOP scores for 64 faculty (35 FIRST IV, 29 comparison). The FIRST IV factor had relative importance 1, estimate -0.425 for non-participation, z = 2.16, P = 0.031. The study was a paired comparison design within the same departments.
