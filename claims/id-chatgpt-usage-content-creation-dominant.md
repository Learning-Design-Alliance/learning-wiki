### Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak 2025

Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak. (2025). An exploration of instructional designers' prioritizations for integrating ChatGPT in design practice. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10509-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of open-ended responses (N = 40 response patterns) identified five usage themes; the article reports that "instructional designers use ChatGPT mainly to generate content, help improve writing and problem-solving, communicate, and engage in information searching." Content creation represented 57.5% and writing assistance 30% of responses.
