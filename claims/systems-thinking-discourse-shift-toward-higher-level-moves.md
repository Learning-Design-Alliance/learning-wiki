## Evidence

### Dina Soliman 2026

Dina Soliman, Xueqi Feng, Marlene Scardamalia, Carol Chan. (2026). Knowledge building–modeling: Grade 5 students creating models, using discourse analytics, and advancing systems thinking. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09493-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive analysis of 469 Knowledge Forum notes coded with the four-level systems thinking framework (kappa = 0.83). The article reports that discourse 'shifted toward higher -level practices' with ST-3 rising from 13.28% to 47.47%.

> "Descriptive analysis shows that the distribution of discourse shifted toward higher -level practices: ST-1 decreased from 28.04% to 8.08% and ST-2 from 50.92% to 29.29%, while ST-3 rose from 13.28% to 47.47% and ST-4 from 7.75% to 15.15% (Table 5)."
