q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of cross-sectional studies (Section 3.5.1) reporting that dependence-oriented scores co-occurred with "fatigue, lower critical thinking, procrastination, cognitive failures, and poorer well-being". No effect sizes are printed; the review stresses these are concurrent correlates, not temporal outcomes.

"Cross-sectional studies associated dependence-oriented scores with fatigue, lower critical thinking, procrastination, cognitive failures, and poorer well-being (Chen, Y . et al., 2025; Tian and Zhang, 2025; Goh et al., 2025 )."
