## Evidence

### Karlsen 2026

Karlsen, N., & Aalbergsjø, S. (2026). Developing pre-service teachers' TPACK for programming simulations. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10674-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Fixed effects analysis of Prototype 2 (n = 13). The authors report that "also PCK contributes significantly to explaining the variation in TPACK for Prototype 2", with TK 0.91 (p < .001) and PCK 0.53 (p < .05).

> "The regression coefficients (TK and PCK) in Table 8 show that the main difference between the two prototypes is that also PCK contributes significantly to explaining the variation in TPACK for Prototype 2."
