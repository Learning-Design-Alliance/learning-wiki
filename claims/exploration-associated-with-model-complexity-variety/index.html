Evidence¶

Sungeun An 2025¶

Sungeun An, Jennifer Hammock, Ashok Goel. (2025). How Online Learners Engage in Self-Directed Modeling: A Behavioral Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00506-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Association analysis of behavior patterns and model quality across the 822 VERA models. The article reports "fcomplexity = 75. 36" and "fvariety = 26. 80", both p < 0.001, with Type 3 means of 12.5 (complexity) and 3.50 (variety) versus Type 2 means of 6.22 and 2.30.

"there is a statistically significant difference between the types as determined by one-way ANOVA test (p complexity < 0. 001, fcomplexity = 75. 36; pvariety < 0. 001, fvariety = 26. 80)"

Sungeun An 2025 (2)¶

Sungeun An, Jennifer Hammock, Ashok Goel. (2025). How Online Learners Engage in Self-Directed Modeling: A Behavioral Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00506-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Pairwise t-tests between the three behavior types on model complexity and variety. The article reports significant differences "between all pairwise comparisons" for complexity (e.g., t = −11.2651 for Type 2 vs Type 3) and for variety (e.g., t = −6.5342 for Type 2 vs Type 3).

"For behavior types and model complexity, significant differences were found between all pairwise comparisons (e.g., pt1t2 < 0.005, tt1t2 = 2. 9835, pt1t3 < 0. 001, tt1t3 = − 7.6527, pt2t3 < 0. 001, tt2t3 = − 11.2651)"
