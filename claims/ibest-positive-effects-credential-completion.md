# I-BEST has positive effects on industry-recognized credential, certificate, or license completion (improvement index +18 across three studies)

## Subclaims

### cred-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, September). Integrated Basic Education Skills and Training (I-BEST). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/706

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

WWC synthesis of three studies (two RCTs meeting standards without reservations, one QED with reservations) covering 44,367 students. The outcome average effect size was 0.48 with improvement index +18; each study's credential effect was statistically significant (p <.01).

> "Based on findings from the three studies that meet WWC standards, the effectiveness rating for the industry-recognized credential, certificate, or license completion domain is positive effects, indicating strong evidence of a positive effect with no overriding contrary evidence. These findings are based on 44,367 students."
