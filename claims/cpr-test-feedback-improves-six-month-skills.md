## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised trial of 587 seventh grade students; 6-month modified Cardiff retention test compared the CPR+test-and-feedback group with the CPR-only group, showing "32 (3.9) points (56% of maximum score) versus 30 (4.0) points (50% of maximum score), p<0.001".

> "At 6 months, group T (n=224) performed better than group O (n=171) in terms of total score: 32 (3.9) points (56% of maximum score) versus 30 (4.0) points (50% of maximum score), p<0.001."

### Nord Anette 2017 (2)

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level analysis of the 6-month Cardiff test in the same cluster randomised trial found the test-and-feedback group "performed significantly better in 8 of 12 variables", including calling 112 (80% vs 53%, p<0.001, table 2).

> "For the individual variables, group T performed significantly better in 8 of 12 variables."
