## Evidence

### George Gyamfi 2024

George Gyamfi, Barbara E. Hanna, Hassan Khosravi. (2024). Impact of an instructional guide and examples on the quality of feedback: insights from a randomised controlled study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10346-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of sampled comments (285 trained, 305 untrained, every tenth comment by time of creation) coded in NVIVO against the adapted S.P.A.R.K. criteria. The comparison of percentages showed the trained group produced "a higher percentage of comments containing traits of high-quality feedback."

> "A higher percentage of comments from the trained group, 71.20%, displayed at least one S.P.A.R.K. trait compared to those from the untrained group (59.10%)."
