## Evidence

### Sibieta 2016

Sibieta, L., Kotecha, M., & Skipp, A. (2016). Nuffield Early Language Intervention: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects/language-for-learning/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Same child-level randomised trial of 350 pupils; the summary impact table reports an effect size of "0.16 (-0.02; 0.34)" for the 20-week group versus untreated control, equivalent to "2 months" of progress. The report describes this result as slightly more likely to have occurred by chance than the 30-week result.

> "20-week vs. untreated control 0.16 (-0.02; 0.34) 2 months"
