### Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Developing a Magnetism Conceptual Survey and Assessing Gender Differences in Student Understanding of Magnetism. PERC Proceedings. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.04871](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.04871)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA of MCS post-test scores in eight regular calculus-based classes (403 males, 168 females) after traditional instruction. The article reports "males outperformed females" with means 15.3 and 13.0 and p = 0.019; no effect size is printed.

> "However, the results shown in Table 4 suggest that there is a significant difference on the post -test and males outperformed females. The mean for the males is 15.3 compared to the mean for the females which is 13.0 (p-value, 0.019)."
