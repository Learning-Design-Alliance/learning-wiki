## Evidence

### M. Madden 2007

M. Madden, P.W.H. Chung, C.W. Dawson. (2007). The Effect of a Computer-Based Cartooning Tool On Children's Cartoons and Written Stories. Loughborough University Institutional Repository. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/2.5/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cartoon comparison in the Ramayana sub-study of all cartoon producers (control n=17, experimental n=13), coded for shot distance and completion. The left-hand side of Table 4 shows experimental participants produced on average about twice as many long shots; no significant differences were found for medium shots or close-ups.

> "All but one of the thirteen experimental group participants ﬁnished their cartoons compared to fewer than half of the control group participants, and on average experimental group participants produced almost twice as many shots as control group participants over the two afternoons of work."
