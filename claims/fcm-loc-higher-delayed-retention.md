## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Three weeks after a 14-week intervention, the flipped-classroom experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on the delayed learning achievement post-test, with a medium effect size (d = 0.56). [→ Aslan Seda Aktı 2025](#aslan-seda-akt-2025)

## Evidence

### Aslan Seda Aktı 2025

Aslan Seda Aktı, Turgut Yigit Emrah, Ünlü Gökhan, Allison Jordan. (2025). Effectiveness of flipped classroom model in distance education: An experimental study in Turkish language course. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335721

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with intact undergraduate classes in a Turkish Language course taught via live online classes; independent groups t-test on delayed post-test scores showed the experimental group (M = 55.21) significantly higher than the control group (M = 44.30), with a printed effect size of Cohen d, was 0.56.

> "When looking at the mean scores of the groups in the learning achievement delayed post-test, it is observed that the mean scores of the experimental group (M = 55.21, SD = 17.18) are higher than those of the control group (M = 44.30, SD = 21.24). The effect size for the difference between the groups, calculated using Cohen d, was 0.56, indicating a medium effect size."
