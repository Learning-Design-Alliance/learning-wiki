Analysis of Washington State Report Card data comparing AP enrollment by race and income across SY 2018-19 and 2021-22. The brief reports enrollment "declined slightly — by 1-2 percentage points — among all student groups" with gaps unchanged; the figure shows 11% low-income AP enrollment in 2021-22.

"From 2019 to 2022, AP enrollment declined slightly — by 1-2 percentage points — among all student groups. Existing gaps for Black and Latino students and students from low-income backgrounds did not change"
