Hierarchical regressions (Table 9) comparing order of entry of MQ and OPQ scales. The article reports the combined MQ scales are significant at the .1% level for Potential and ROA, and that "MQ scales significantly augmented the initial variance accounted for by the OPQ scales alone on two of the three DVs".
