## Evidence

### Bacon 2006

Donald R. Bacon and Beth Bean. (2006). GPA in Research Studies: An Invaluable but Neglected Opportunity. Journal of Marketing Education, Vol. 28 No. 1, April 2006 35-42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0273475305284638

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability analysis of the 157-student undergraduate sample (Table 1) shows intercourse grade correlation declines from .34 to .26 across years while reliability rises from .84 to .94 as k grows; the article states the decrease "is more than offset by the increase in the number of grades".

> "Thus, 4-year over- all GPA has a higher reliability (.94) than any of the limited time frame GPAs, including most recent year (.84) or most recent 2 years (.91)."
