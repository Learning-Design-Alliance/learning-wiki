"The average sentence-to-sentence cosine ( http://lsa. colorado.edu/ ) of the scrambled text in Experiment 2 was significantly lower ( M=0. 23, SD =0.19) than the cosine of the text used in Experiment 1 (M=0.39, SD =0.22), t(116)=4.16, p<.001, d=0.77, indicating that the disruption of the text coherence had been successful."
