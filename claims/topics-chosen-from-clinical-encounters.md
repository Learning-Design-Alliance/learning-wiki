### Mallin Mike MD 2014

Mallin Mike MD, Schlein Sarah MD, Doctor Shaneen MD, Stroud Susan MD, Dawson Matthew MD, Fix Megan MD. (2014). A Survey of the Current Utilization of Asynchronous Education Among Emergency Medicine Residents in the United States. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000170

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Topic-selection item in the 226-resident survey (220 answering). "80.0% [176/220]" chose topics from recent clinical encounters, versus podcast/blog schedule and conference schedule.

> "most residents (80.0% [176/220]) reported choosing the topics they accessed on the basis of recent clinical encounters. Other residents (31.4% [69/220]) reported selecting topics on the basis of the podcast/blog schedule, and some (29.1% [64/220]) choose extracurricular educational topics on the basis of the assigned didactic residency conference schedule (P < .001)."
