## Evidence

### Arora Samridhi 2025

Arora Samridhi, Shingne Ranjana, Ravi Sathyanarayan Kelegere, Jha Indira. (2025). Comparison of Self-directed Learning and Didactic Lecture in Teaching Clinical Anatomy to 1st Year MBBS Students. Annals of African Medicine. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_104_25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional observational comparative study of 124 first-year MBBS students in a crossover design (Batch A lecture, Batch B SDL on day 1 for the median nerve topic). "MBBS Batch B SDL (n = 62) 7.43 ± 0.75" versus 7.13 ± 1.23 after lecture, reported as significant by unpaired t-test; no effect size printed.

> "Mean scores for the topic “median nerve injury” MBBS Batch A (n = 62), didactic lecture 7.13 ± 1.23. MBBS Batch B SDL (n = 62) 7.43 ± 0.75 and P = 0.0009 by student unpaired t-test"

### Arora Samridhi 2025 (2)

Arora Samridhi, Shingne Ranjana, Ravi Sathyanarayan Kelegere, Jha Indira. (2025). Comparison of Self-directed Learning and Didactic Lecture in Teaching Clinical Anatomy to 1st Year MBBS Students. Annals of African Medicine. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_104_25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

On day 2 the batches crossed over: "MBBS Batch A (n = 62), SDL was 7.66 ± 0.95" while Batch B under didactic lecture scored 6.84 ± 1.01, reported significant at P = 0.0001. No effect size printed.

> "Mean scores for the topic “radial nerve injury” MBBS Batch A (n = 62), SDL was 7.66 ± 0.95. MBBS Batch B didactic lecture (n = 62) was 6.84 ± 1.01 and Student’s paired t-test, P = 0.0001"
