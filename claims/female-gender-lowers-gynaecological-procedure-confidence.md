## Evidence

### Meister Tara 2023

Meister Tara, Foessleitner Philipp, Breuer Georg, Winder Franziska M., Favero Martine, Friemann Margareta, Krischer Benedict, Weiss Martin, Windsperger Karin (2023). The impact of gender on the self-confidence of practical and surgical skills among OBGYN residents: a trinational survey. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-023-07202-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 422 OBGYN residents in Austria, Germany and Switzerland; multivariate linear regression (F[5,415] = 4.97; p < 0.001; n = 421) found gender (p = 0.03) significantly affected gynaecological-procedure confidence. The article reports "odds ratio [OR] − 1.53" for being female.

> "Being a woman significantly decreases the feeling of confidence (odds ratio [OR] − 1.53; 95% confidence interval [CI] − 2.84 to − 0.22; p = 0.03)."
