Evidence¶

Michos 2025¶

Michos, K., Schmitz, M.-L., & Petko, D. (2025). Learning Analytics in Schools: Is Digital Data Use Influenced by Teacher-Level or School-Level Factors? Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(3), 87–101. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8567

q3 · i? · associational · r2

From the same multilevel regression model (Model 1, N = 2,225 after exclusions, 112 school clusters). The authors suggest less experienced teachers may be more comfortable adopting new digital practices, while more experienced teachers may be hesitant due to established practices.

"Other teacher characteristics, such as age and gender, did not significantly affect the use of digital data, but years of teaching experience had a minor negative impact (B = –0.09, p < 0.001)"
