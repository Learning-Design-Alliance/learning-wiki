## Evidence

### Spiller J 2023

Spiller J, Clayton S, Cragg L, Johnson S, Simms V, Gilmore C. (2023). Higher level domain specific skills in mathematics; The relationship between algebra, geometry, executive function skills and mathematics achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291796

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression model 1 (n = 95) showed number fact knowledge (β = .27) and calculation skills composite (β = .56) were significant unique predictors; conceptual understanding was not (β = -.01), possibly due to ceiling effects (mean accuracy 92%).

> "It is important to note that, even in secondary school, basic arithmetic skills (calculation skills, number fact knowledge and understanding of arithmetic concepts) were significantly associated with mathematics achievement, explaining 56% of the variance."
