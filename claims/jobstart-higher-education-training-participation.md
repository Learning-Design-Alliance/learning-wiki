### What Works Clearinghouse 2008

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison-condition description in Appendix A1 of the Cave et al. (1993) randomized controlled trial: control youth could use community services but their participation rate was substantially lower than JOBSTART youth's, whose 800 average hours included their JOBSTART participation plus other activities, versus 432 hours for controls.

> "During the four years after random assignment, 56% of control group youth reported receiving remedial or occupational instruction, compared with 94% of JOBSTART youth."
