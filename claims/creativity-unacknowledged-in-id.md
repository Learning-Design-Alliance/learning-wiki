### Carolina Cuesta-Hincapie 2025

Carolina Cuesta-Hincapie. (2025). Exploring expert perceptions of creativity in instructional design: a phenomenographic study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10560-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Category 2 finding from Elizabeth Boling's interview and questionnaire responses, part of the phenomenographic analysis of six experts. Boling states creativity is "happening but not acknowledged" in ID education for lack of specific vocabulary.

> "In a lot of ID education, creativity is happening but not acknowledged through lack of specific vocabulary to do so and through the assumption that creativity is mainly required in the form component of design (what do things look and sound and act like) but not really a key element of instructional strategy."
