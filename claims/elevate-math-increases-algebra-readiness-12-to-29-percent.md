## Evidence

### Snipes 2016

Snipes, J., Huang, C.-W., Jaquet, K., & Finkelstein, N. (2016). The effects of the Elevate Math summer program on math achievement and algebra readiness. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567052

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same summer 2014 randomized controlled trial, algebra readiness was defined as passing three or more of the seven MDTP topic areas, the threshold the article says gives a greater than 50 percent chance of success in grade 8 algebra I. The reported percentages are "12 percent in the control group" and "29 percent in the treatment group."

> "The program increased the percentage of students who passed at least three MDTP Algebra Readiness topic areas from 12 percent in the control group to 29 percent in the treatment group, an improvement of 142 percent compared to the control group."
