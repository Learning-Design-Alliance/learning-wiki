# Stabilizing test-based indicators produced substantial changes in simulated CSI designations, with larger schools replacing smaller schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 88 schools designated for CSI using either unstabilized or stabilized test-based indicators, 55 (63 percent) were designated under both; 17 of 72 schools designated using unstabilized indicators (24 percent) would not be designated after stabilization, and 16 of 1,908 non-designated schools (0.84 percent) would be newly designated. [→ Rosendahl 2024](#rosendahl-2024)
`q2 i?` Median school size was 551 for schools newly designated under stabilization and 479 for schools losing their designation, versus 475 overall, indicating larger schools replaced smaller schools after stabilization. [→ Rosendahl 2024 (2)](#rosendahl-2024-2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulated 2018/19 CSI designations applying NJDOE's bottom-5th-percentile-of-Title-I-schools rule to stabilized versus unstabilized composite scores (graduation-rate and ATSI-tenure criteria not simulated). Results in table 2.

> "Of the 72 schools designated for CSI using unstabilized indicators, 17 (24 percent) would not be designated if indicators were stabilized. Of the 1,908 schools not designated for CSI using unstabi -lized indicators, 16 (0.84 percent) would be designated if test-based indicators were stabilized."

### Rosendahl 2024 (2)

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/stabilizing-school-performance-indicators-new-jersey-reduce-effect-random-error

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Median school size analysis of CSI designation changes (figure 8); overall median school size was 475 students. The article explains composite scores for smaller schools shift away from the extremes while larger schools' scores are more stationary.

> "The median size of schools that received a CSI designation using stabilized test-based indicators and that did not receive one using unstabilized test-based indicators was 551 students. The median size of schools that received a CSI designation using unstabilized test-based indicators and that did not receive one using stabilized test-based indicators was 479 students."
