## Evidence

### Daumiller 2025

Daumiller, M., Gaspard, H., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2025). Bridging teacher motivation and instruction: Relevance of student‐oriented goals for teaching alongside personal achievement goals and self‐efficacy. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S98–S114. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12776

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level path modelling of between-teacher differences in lesson-diary teaching practices. The article reports self-efficacy was "associated with increased interestingness, cognitive stimulation, individualization, autonomy support and structuring" with no significant performance-practice linkages.

> "Self‐efficacy, however, was a relatively influential predictor that was associated with increased interestingness, cognitive stimulation, individualization, autonomy support and structuring, but was not significantly linked to any of the performance‐based instructional practices."
