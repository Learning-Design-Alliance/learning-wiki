# A one-day existential communication course significantly increased Danish GPs' self-efficacy for most measured communication skills, with the largest gains in religious/spiritual communication, working with own barriers, and reflecting on own values

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean self-efficacy scores increased significantly from before to immediately after the course on most of the eight measured skills. [→ Hvidt EA 2018](#hvidt-ea-2018)
`q2 i?` The skills with the largest self-efficacy increases were communicating about religious/spiritual concerns, working with own barriers, and reflecting on own existential/spiritual values. [→ Hvidt EA 2018](#hvidt-ea-2018)
`q2 i?` Self-efficacy for communicating about R/S concerns rose from a mean of 2.9 before to 3.7 after the course (paired t-test p = 0.002). [→ Hvidt EA 2018 (2)](#hvidt-ea-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Hvidt EA 2018

Hvidt EA, Ammentorp J, Søndergaard J, Timmermann C, Hansen DG, Hvidt NC. (2018). Developing and evaluating a course programme to enhance existential communication with cancer patients in general practice. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1459235

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative pre/post evaluation in which nineteen GPs answered a self-efficacy questionnaire before (T1) and just after (T2) the one-day course, analysed with paired samples t-tests. The authors report which "mean scores of self-efficacy that increased the most"; Table 2(a) shows significant paired t-test p values for most items.

> "The mean scores of self-efficacy that increased the most were related to: The ability to communicate with patients about religious/spiritual (R/S) concerns, The ability to work with own barriers and The ability to reflect upon own existential/spiritual values brought into the consultation."

### Hvidt EA 2018 (2)

Hvidt EA, Ammentorp J, Søndergaard J, Timmermann C, Hansen DG, Hvidt NC. (2018). Developing and evaluating a course programme to enhance existential communication with cancer patients in general practice. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1459235

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Row of Table 2(a) reporting the paired t-test for the R/S communication item: mean self-efficacy 2.9 (SD 1.1) before and 3.7 (SD 0.7) after the course, p = 0.002, for the nineteen GPs answering at both time points.

> "communicate about R/S concerns and needs in connection with a disease? 2.9 (1.1) 3.7 (0.7) 0.002"
