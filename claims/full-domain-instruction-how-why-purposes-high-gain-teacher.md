# High-gain teacher's high-change students received full explicit domain instruction on how and why readers set purposes, unlike the typical teacher's students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the case studies, only the high-gain teacher's high change score students received domain instruction fully conveying how and why readers set purposes, while none of the typical teacher's students received instruction fully conveying how readers set purposes. [→ Fatima Zehra Allahverdi 2025](#fatima-zehra-allahverdi-2025)

## Evidence

### Fatima Zehra Allahverdi 2025

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi, Lynn Gelzheiser. (2025). Comparison of more effective and typical teachers' instructional quality and implementation of engaged reader processes. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09725-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Multiple case study coding of five transcribed sessions per teacher for eight students, using the Instructional Quality and Implementation Coding system. The high-gain teacher followed ISA-X explicit instruction templates; the typical teacher's students received only partial or inappropriate instruction on how readers set purposes.

> "the H-teacher's students who made large gains (high change score students) were provided with domain instruction during acquisition/early consolidation phases that fully conveyed how and why readers set purposes."
