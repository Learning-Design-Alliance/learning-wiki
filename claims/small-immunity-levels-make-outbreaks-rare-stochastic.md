## Evidence

### Bellan Steve E. 2012

Bellan Steve E., Pulliam Juliet R. C., Scott James C., Dushoff Jonathan, the MMED Organizing Committee. (2012). How to Make Epidemiological Training Infectious. PLoS Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001295

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Numerical stochastic simulation result shown in Figure 3B: the median outbreak size among 1,000 stochastic simulations per immune proportion, with 10% quantiles, using transmission parameters fit from the 2011 MMF outbreak data. The printed result is that 'with a fifth of the population immune 50% of simulated outbreaks never exceed more than two secondary cases'.

> "Only small levels of immunity are necessary to make outbreaks rare (with a fifth of the population immune 50% of simulated outbreaks never exceed more than two secondary cases), but just by chance larger outbreaks still occur."
