Misra Satya Ranjan, Das Rupsa, Das Subhra Jyoti. (2026). Preparedness of dental interns to identify oral potentially malignant disorders and oral cancer: a multi-state cross-sectional survey in India. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08681-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four Likert-scale attitude items (Cronbach’s α = 0.88) in the same survey. Mean attitude score was "19.67 ± 1.09 out of 20"; the authors note a ceiling effect limiting discrimination.

> "For the statement “Early detection of OPMDs significantly reduces oral cancer-related mortality,” 94.3% (n = 1150) strongly agreed and 3.3% (n = 40) agreed; only 0.8% (n = 10) disagreed."
