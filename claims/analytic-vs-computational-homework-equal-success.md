## Evidence

### Marcos D. Caballero 2011

Marcos D. Caballero, Matthew A. Kohlmyer and Michael F. Schatz. (2011). Fostering Computational Thinking In Introductory Mechanics. [doi:10.1063/1.3679982](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3679982)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Weekly online homework completion rates compared for analytic versus computational problems in the same mechanics course. The article prints "Analytic HW 84.6% vs Computational HW 85.8%"; no statistical test or effect size is reported.

> "Students solved both analytic and computational modeling homework problems with equal success (Analytic HW 84.6% vs Computational HW 85.8%)"

### Caballero 2012

Caballero, M. D., Schatz, M. F., and Kohlmyer, M. A. (2012). Implementing and assessing computational modeling in introductory mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.020106](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.020106)

> "We found no statistical diﬀerence betw een students’ perfor- mance on their computational homework and their performance on analytic homework using a rank-sum test (Analytic 84.6% vs. Computational 85.8%)."
