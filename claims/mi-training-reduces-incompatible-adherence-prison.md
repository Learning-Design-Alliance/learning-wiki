## Evidence

### Martín-Coca A 2025

Martín-Coca A, Martín Herrero JA. (2025). Training program on motivational interviewing for prison professionals. Revista Española de Sanidad Penitenciaria. https://doi.org/10.18176/resp.00108

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA and Friedman test over three moments in a quasi-experimental study of 25 prison professionals; incompatible adherence dropped from 4.65 to 3.53, with the printed effect size η² = 0.360 (rendered "h2 = 0.360" in the English text) and observed power = 0.990. The authors call it one of the most solid achievements of the intervention.

> "Adherence incompatible with MI showed a significant and sustained reduction, which was visible in the ANOVA of repeated measures (F = 13.501, p <0.001, h2 = 0.360, observed power = 0.990) and in the Friedman test (c2 = 20.796, p <0.001)."
