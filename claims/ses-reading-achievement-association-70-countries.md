## Subclaims
`q3 i2` SES shows a positive meta-analytic correlation with reading achievement across countries (r = 0.339), with significant between-country heterogeneity. [→ Hofer 2024](#hofer-2024)

## Evidence

### Hofer 2024

Hofer, S. I., Heine, J.-H., Besharati, S., Yip, J. C., Reinhold, F., & Brummelman, E. (2024). Self-perceptions as mechanisms of achievement inequality: evidence across 70 countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00211-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Fixed-effects meta-analysis of country-specific bivariate correlations from PISA 2018 (n = 520,729 students, 70 countries). The meta-analytic correlation was "r = 0.339", significant at Bonferroni-adjusted alpha, and varied significantly between countries (Q-Statistic).

> "More speci ﬁcally, we found a positive association between SES and reading achievement across countries ( r = 0.339), demon- strating socioeconomic disparities in achievement."
