### Yu Tana 2025

Yu Tana, Yang Yanwei, Zeng Qing, Xu Zihang, Li Caiyue, Zhang Hongchen. (2025). Relationship between academic self-concept and psychological truancy among medical college students in China: the chain mediating role of academic motivation and school climate. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07650-5

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of survey data from 628 medical undergraduates at 8 Chinese medical colleges. The direct path from academic self-concept to psychological truancy was significant: "β = -0.110, 95% CI = -0.150 to -0.092, p<0.05". The SEM fit indices were RMSEA = 0.078, CFI = 0.92, TLI = 0.91, SRMR = 0.045.
