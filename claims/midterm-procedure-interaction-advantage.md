## Evidence

### Huang Tzu-Hao 2021

Huang Tzu-Hao, Liu Fen, Chen Li-Chen, Tsai Ching-Ching. (2021). The acceptance and impact of Google Classroom integrating into a clinical pathology course for nursing students: A technology acceptance model approach. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0247819. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247819

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing experimental (n = 39) and control (n = 35) groups at mid-semester on the procedure and interaction subscale; the article reports "ES = 0.51, p = 0.03". This was the only significant inter-group difference.

> "only significantly higher for the mid-semester learning satisfaction subscale of procedure and interactions (33.8 ± 1.9 vs. 32.6 ± 2.7, ES = 0.51, p = 0.03)"
