Evidence¶

Patience E. Idoga 2022¶

Patience E. Idoga, Dokun Iwalewa Oluwajana, Ibrahim Adeshola. (2022). Educational quality and technological complexity on recognition of enhanced learning platform in developing countries using PLS-SEM in a post COVID era. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10150-8

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Structural model results (Table 5) from the PLS-SEM analysis: H5 (TC→PEOU, t = 1.956, p = 0.051) and H6 (TC→PU, t = -0.662, p = 0.508) were not supported. The article reports "an insignificant effect"; equivalence was not tested.

"instructors’ technology complexity and perceived ease of use (β = 0.158), and technology complexity and perceived usefulness (β = -0.054). Thus, H2, H5, and H6 show an insignificant effect in this regard"
