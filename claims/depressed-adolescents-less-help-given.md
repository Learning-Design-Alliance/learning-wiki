## Evidence

### van Rijsewijk 2016

van Rijsewijk, L., Dijkstra, J. K., Pattiselanno, K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2016). Who Helps Whom? Investigating the Development of Adolescent Prosocial Relationships. Developmental Psychology, 52(6), 894-908. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000106

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RSiena meta-analysis: depressive mood did not predict receiving help (b = 0.01), negatively predicted giving help (b = -0.02, odds = 0.98, described as small), with a significant similarity effect (b = 0.26, odds = 1.07).

> "Symptoms of depressive mood did not predict receiving help (b ! .01, SE ! .04, odds ! 1.01), and negatively predicted giving help, but the effect size was small (b ! #.02, SE ! .00, odds ! .98). The similarity effect was significant (b ! .26, SE ! .10, odds ! 1.07)."
