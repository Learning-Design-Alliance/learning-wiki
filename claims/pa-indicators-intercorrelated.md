Correlation analysis among active play, time with family and peers, and sedentary behavior in 1447 participants aged 6–17 (Table 3) showed "All indicators were associated with each other"; sedentary behavior correlations were r = 0.081 (p = 0.002) and r = 0.088 (p = 0.001).
