### Jafar Tavakoli 2025

Jafar Tavakoli, Marisa Exter, Ankita Kotangale. (2025). Integrating dispositions in instructional design: an exploratory study in higher education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09762-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of RQ2 challenges: course modality, time, energy, and stakeholder buy-in; "buy-in was the most frequently cited challenge in this study", consistent with Tagare et al. (2023b) on faculty buy-in.

> "The challenges of fostering and teaching dispositions mentioned by the participants included the modality of the course, whether online or face-to-face, along with time, energy, and buy-in from SMEs and faculty. Among these, buy-in was the most frequently cited challenge in this study."

### Jafar Tavakoli 2025 (2)

Jafar Tavakoli, Marisa Exter, Ankita Kotangale. (2025). Integrating dispositions in instructional design: an exploratory study in higher education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09762-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of RQ3 challenges: participants such as Mia and Noah described dispositions as hard to measure because they are internal mental states lacking measurable, observable definitions.

> "many participants highlighted that, in addition to issues such as grading and instructor’s time, the lack of a clear and measurable definition of dispositions presents a significant barrier, making assessment particularly difficult"
