Theoretical argument within the TSMS model (Assumption 6), not an empirical test. The article proposes that task-related motivational evaluations 'are integrated in the motivationally informed judgment of solvability (MJOS)', which constitutes the task-specific motivational stimulus. No effect size is reported; the claim is a model assumption awaiting validation.

> "MJOS predicts the probability that students engage in physics-specific reasoning processes for building a task representation by questioning their FIMM and, if necessary, revising it – with engagement expected to be most likely at intermediate MJOS values."
