# Senior cardiothoracic surgery residents significantly improved most modified OSATS motor skills and anastomosis time across all three anastomosis types

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In senior residents, significant improvements were found in S-T-S graft orientation (P=0.019), appropriate spacing (P=0.032), use of forceps (P=0.045), needle angles (P=0.009), needle transfer (P=0.043), and anastomosis time (19.21 to 8.11 min, P=0.021). `q2 i?` Senior residents also improved significantly in E-T-S and E-T-E anastomoses, including graft orientation, spacing, bites, needle angles, needle transfer, suture management, and anastomosis time (e.g., E-T-E time 15.10 to 6.56 min, P=0.038). [→ Cubas W Samir MD 2024](#cubas-w-samir-md-2024)

## Evidence

### Cubas W Samir MD 2024

Cubas W Samir MD, MSc, Paredes-Temoche Anna MD, Dongo Wildor R., Inga Katherine E., Luna-Victoria Wilfredo MD, Velarde-Revilla Enrique MD. (2024). Construction and Surgical Training of Coronary Anastomosis on a Low-Cost Portable Simulator: Experience in a Peruvian Multicenter Study. Brazilian Journal of Cardiovascular Surgery. https://doi.org/10.21470/1678-9741-2023-0479

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational multicenter study of 140 residents, 44.29% senior, assessed in two 45-day periods with paired tests on modified OSATS scores. The article reports "a significant improvement in graft orientation (P=0.019)" and other skills for seniors.

> "Of the residents, 44.29% were classified as senior, and it was identified that in S-T-S anastomoses (FP vs. SP), there was a significant improvement in graft orientation (P=0.019), appropriate spacing (P=0.032), use of forceps (P=0.045), needle angles (P=0.009), needle transfer (P=0.043), and anastomosis time (19.21 min. vs. 8.11 min., P=0.021)."
