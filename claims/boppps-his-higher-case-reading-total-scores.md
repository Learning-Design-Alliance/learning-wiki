# The HIS-based BOPPPS teaching model group scored significantly higher than the traditional teaching group on case reading and total end-of-term exam scores in both semesters

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the first semester, the BOPPPS group scored higher on case reading (39.27 ± 3.39 vs 35.31 ± 2.77, Cohen's d = 1.28) and total scores (77.47 ± 6.61 vs 74.33 ± 4.17). [→ Yang Ziqing 2025](#yang-ziqing-2025)
`q3 i3` In the second semester, the BOPPPS group again scored higher on case reading (39.79 ± 3.45 vs 35.47 ± 3.15, Cohen's d = -1.29) and total scores (78.36 ± 5.11 vs 74.53 ± 5.68). [→ Yang Ziqing 2025 (2)](#yang-ziqing-2025-2)
`q3 i3` Within the same group across semesters, case reading and total scores were significantly higher in the semester using BOPPPS (Cohen's d = 1.15 and -1.42 for image reading). [→ Yang Ziqing 2025 (3)](#yang-ziqing-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Yang Ziqing 2025

Yang Ziqing, Zhen Siyu, Pan Ben, Wei Hanyu, Li Qiang, Yue Junyan, Hu Ying, Liang Changhua. (2025). Investigating the use of the HIS-based BOPPPS teaching model in medical imaging experimental course instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07607-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental crossover study of 117 medical imaging interns; first-semester between-group comparison from Table 4. Image reading Cohen's d was 1.28 [0.86–1.70], total score d = 0.57 [0.18–0.96].

> "in the first semester, the first group of students received much higher scores overall in the case reading questions (case reading score 39.27 ± 3.39 vs. 35.31 ± 2.77, t = 6.925, p < 0.001; total score 77.47 ± 6.61 vs. 74.33 ± 4.17, t = 3.087, p = 0.003)"

### Yang Ziqing 2025 (2)

Yang Ziqing, Zhen Siyu, Pan Ben, Wei Hanyu, Li Qiang, Yue Junyan, Hu Ying, Liang Changhua. (2025). Investigating the use of the HIS-based BOPPPS teaching model in medical imaging experimental course instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07607-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Second-semester between-group comparison after the crossover swap, from Table 4; the second group then used BOPPPS. Image reading Cohen's d = -1.29 [-1.71-0.87], total d = -0.70 [-1.09-0.31].

> "in the second semester, the second group of students performed better in the same aspects as the first group(case reading score 35.47 ± 3.15 vs. 39.79 ± 3.45, t = -7.066, p < 0.001; total score 74.53 ± 5.68 vs. 78.36 ± 5.11, t = -3.844, p < 0.001)"

### Yang Ziqing 2025 (3)

Yang Ziqing, Zhen Siyu, Pan Ben, Wei Hanyu, Li Qiang, Yue Junyan, Hu Ying, Liang Changhua. (2025). Investigating the use of the HIS-based BOPPPS teaching model in medical imaging experimental course instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07607-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-group cross-semester comparison from Table 5: image reading Cohen's d = 1.15 [0.74–1.56] for Group 1 and -1.42 [-1.86-0.98] for Group 2, favouring the BOPPPS semester.

> "there were statistically significant differences in the average scores for case reading questions and total scores for the same group of students in different semesters (p < 0.05)"
