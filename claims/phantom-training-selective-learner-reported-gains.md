## Evidence

### Wei Tianhong 2026

Wei Tianhong, Ke Mujing, Zhang Bo, He Zhiyou, Xie Xiuyun. (2026). Structured phantom-assisted simulation for early abdominal ultrasound skill training in ultrasound medicine residents: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09395-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Item-level comparison of a 6-item learner-reported questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.856, 100% response rate) using the Mann–Whitney U test with effect size r. The largest between-group effect was for "Understanding of ultrasound anatomy" (r = 0.461, P < 0.001).

> "the intervention cohort scored significantly higher than the historical comparison cohort in understanding of ultrasound anatomy, mastery of abdominal ultrasound skills, and clinical readiness (all P < 0.05). No statistically significant between-group differences were found for ability to recognize abdominal lesions, engagement and participation, or learning interest (all P > 0.05)."
