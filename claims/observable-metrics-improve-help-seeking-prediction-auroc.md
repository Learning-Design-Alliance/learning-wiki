# Observable metrics computed from IDE telemetry improved held-out prediction of query imminence (AUROC .726) and guided versus dependent help-seeking (AUROC .717) over raw telemetry

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For query-imminence prediction, observable metrics alone performed best at AUROC 0.726, an absolute improvement of 0.037 over raw telemetry (0.689). [→ pred-1](#pred-1)
`q2 i?` For help-seeking-type prediction, observable metrics alone performed best at AUROC 0.717, an absolute improvement of 0.027 over raw telemetry (0.690). [→ pred-2](#pred-2)

## Evidence

### pred-1

David Barron, Xiaohang Tang, Rezky Dwisantika, Minsun Kim, David H. Smith IV, Jiaming Cui, and Yan Chen. 2026. TutorTrace: A Dataset and Taxonomy for Classifying Learner Behavioral States during AI-Assisted Programming Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26184

`q2 · i?`

Cross-cohort Random Forest evaluation trained on Deployment 1 (n=190) and evaluated on held-out Deployment 2 (n=113), with semantic inputs excluded. "Observable metrics alone performed best at 0.726"; behavioral-sequence features (0.719) did not improve on observable metrics.

> "For query-imminence prediction, raw telemetry achieved an AUROC of 0.689, while the full representation including behavioral-sequence features achieved 0.719. Observable metrics alone performed best at 0.726, an absolute improvement of 0.037 over raw telemetry."

### pred-2

David Barron, Xiaohang Tang, Rezky Dwisantika, Minsun Kim, David H. Smith IV, Jiaming Cui, and Yan Chen. 2026. TutorTrace: A Dataset and Taxonomy for Classifying Learner Behavioral States during AI-Assisted Programming Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26184

`q2 · i?`

Same held-out Random Forest protocol for help-seeking-type prediction (guided vs dependent within 15 seconds), labels generated by GPT-4o with human-rater agreement κ=.897 and κ=.709/.690 between GPT-4o and the raters. Observable metrics reached 0.717.

> "Raw telemetry achieved an AUROC of 0.690, while the full representation including behavioral-sequence features achieved 0.705. Observable metrics alone performed best at 0.717, an absolute improvement of 0.027 over raw telemetry."
