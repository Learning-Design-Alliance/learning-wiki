# Simulations with two dominant base rates also show L* at chance centered at zero

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When two base rates (A and B) dominate equally, L* values remain small in absolute value and centered at zero, with maximum 0.006 and minimum -0.004. [→ Jeffrey Matayoshi and Shamya Karumbaiah 2020](#jeffrey-matayoshi-and-shamya-karumbaiah-2020)

## Evidence

### Jeffrey Matayoshi and Shamya Karumbaiah 2020

Jeffrey Matayoshi and Shamya Karumbaiah. (2020). Adjusting the L Statistic when Self-Transitions are Excluded in Affect Dynamics. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 4. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/478

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second simulation study: 23 sets of 50,000 sequences with base rates of A and B equal, ranging from 0.25 to 0.47, and C and D ranging from 0.25 to 0.03. The two base-rate computation methods again gave essentially equivalent results.

> "As before, the L∗ values are small in absolute value and centered at zero, with the maximum and minimum values given by 0.006 and -0.004, respectively."
