Evidence¶

Mukundan et al. 2012¶

Jayakaran Mukundan, Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Vahid Nimehchisalem. (2012). The Effect of an Intensive English Program on Malaysian Secondary School Students’ Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 5(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n11p1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test on pre- and post-test scores of 30 low-scoring Form 4 students following a single group quasi-experimental design. The article reports the difference was "statistically significant [t(29)= 31.74, p= .000 <.05]".

"According to the table, the difference between the pre-test and post-test scores was statistically significant [t(29)= 31.74, p= .000 <.05]."

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive statistics reported in Table 2 for the same 30 students. The article states "the students scored considerably higher at post-test ( M = 79.17, SD = 3.37)" compared to pre-test (M = 49.5, SD = 4.04), with a mean difference of 29.67.
