## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported attendance over a 10-week quarter was significantly reduced in remote versus in-person discussion sections (Cramer's V = 0.27). [→ Mueller 2026](#mueller-2026)

## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared test of self-reported sessions attended (0–10) by modality in the student survey. The article reports "Cramer's V = 0.27"; only 4% of in-person students reported attending zero sections versus 13% of remote students.

> "We found that student attendance was significantly and moderately reduced for remote discussion sections compared with in-person sections, χ² (10, N = 4860) = 355.07, p = <0.001; Cramer's V = 0.27."
