## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Older adults' speech reception thresholds in noise significantly decreased after the immersive training session (about 0.6 dB) but not after the auditory training session (about 0.12 dB). [→ Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025](#vanessa-frei-nathalie-giroud-2025)
`q3 i?` Across both trainings, speech reception thresholds were significantly lower after training, with SNR decreasing on average by 0.35 dB. [→ Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025 (2)](#vanessa-frei-nathalie-giroud-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud. (2025). Immersive auditory-cognitive training improves speech-in-noise perception in older adults with varying hearing and working memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00306-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model on OLSA speech reception thresholds in a cross-over study of 39 older adults exposed to both training modalities. The quote shows the significant pre-to-post SNR improvement was specific to the immersive session; the reported Δβ = −0.592 was significant at p < 0.001.

> "Post-hoc comparisons revealed that SNR signi ﬁcantly improved, but only after the immersive training session (IMpost – IMpre: Δβ = −0.592, SE = 0.135,t = −4.395, p < 0.001, see Fig. 2B)"
