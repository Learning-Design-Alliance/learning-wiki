Benjamini-Hochberg post-hoc comparisons following the significant Kruskall-Wallis test of Jo's likeability across the four script conditions in the randomized experiment. The article also reports the Humor (No Snark) script produced higher Jo likeability than the Original and Snark (No Humor) scripts (both p < .0001).
