## Evidence

### Rudd 2017

Rudd, P., Villaneuva Aguilera, A. B., Elliott, L., & Chambers, B. (2017). MathsFlip: Flipped Learning — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/search?q=&filters=intervention%3AMathsFlip%2CfunderName%3AEducation+Endowment+Foundation&searchType=advanced-search

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Secondary ITT analyses of KS2 fine grade reading, writing and average point score using the same three-level model controlling for corresponding KS1 scores (Table 4): reading g = 0.22 (-0.13 to 0.57), p = 0.21; writing g = 0.24 (-0.11 to 0.59), p = 0.17; APS g = 0.21, p = 0.30. The executive summary states "three additional months' progress in reading and writing" but says the result "should be treated with caution".

> "First, there is not an obvious route by which this maths intervention could improve literacy results so much more than maths results. Second, the relatively small number of schools involved increases the likelihood that we would see a result like this just by chance rather than due to the intervention itself."
