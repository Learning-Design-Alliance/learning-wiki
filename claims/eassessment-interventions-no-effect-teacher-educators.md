## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Admiraal 2014

Admiraal, W., Janssen, T., Huizenga, J., Kranenburg, F., Taconis, R., & Corda, A. (2014). E-assessment of student-teachers’ competence as new teachers. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043184

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire study of the Non satis scire project: of 115 teacher educators, 60 completed both pre-test and post-test; repeated measures ANOVA compared experimental and control programs. The article reports no significant increase in use, and for evaluation "no differences were found between the experimental and control conditions".

> "The analyses did not show a significant increase in teacher educators’ use of the particular assessment procedure, compared to the control condition (consisting of programs that did not use the particular e-assessment form)."
