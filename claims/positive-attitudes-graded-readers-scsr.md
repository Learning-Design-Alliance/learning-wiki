## Evidence

### Martinez 2017

Liza E. Martinez. (2017). Adult Hispanic ESL Students and Graded Readers. The CATESOL Journal, 29(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164286

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative findings from journals, reading logs, and interviews with four treatment participants over the 14-week study. The article reports all but one participant gave positive self-reports of growth; one self-identified nonreader said his view of reading stayed the same.

> "All of the participants said they like the graded readers because of the variety of topics and levels. They did not find the graded readers difficult or boring."
