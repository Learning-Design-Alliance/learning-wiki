`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Main finding reported in the discussion of this qualitative think-aloud study of 30 GPs from Belgium, England, the Netherlands, Poland and Spain, who worked through the INTRO intervention and discussed its content and format. The article reports that GPs "would be a useful tool to help improve clinical practice" was their shared view.

> "GPs in all five countries reported the view that the intervention addressed an important topic, was broadly acceptable and feasible to use, and would be a useful tool to help improve clinical practice."
