### Lior M. Burko 2008

Lior M. Burko. (2008). Transversality of Electromagnetic Waves in the Calculus-Based Introductory Physics Course. https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2993 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0808.2993](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0808.2993)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation (Section III.7) adapting an argument originally made by Feynman: a current sheet abruptly turned on produces a propagating wavefront, and applying Faraday's law yields E = cB. The article states this result is general, though the general proof is beyond the introductory course.

> "The result that E = cB appears in many introductory texts (e.g., in [3]). However, in such books an extra assumption is typically made, namely that the ﬁelds are harmonic and are in phase."
