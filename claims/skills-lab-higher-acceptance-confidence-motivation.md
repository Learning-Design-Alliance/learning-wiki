## Evidence

### Lund Frederike 2012

Lund Frederike, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Krautter Markus, Möltner Andreas, Werner Anne, Weyrich Peter, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2012). Effectiveness of IV Cannulation Skills Laboratory Training and Its Transfer into Clinical Practice: A Randomized, Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0032831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Post-interventional acceptance questionnaires rated on six-point Likert scales compared the two randomized groups. The intervention group was "significantly more confident of being able to memorize the learning contents" (p=0.032) and more motivated (p=0.019); feedback benefit also favoured IG (1.39 vs 2.40, p=0.016).

> "After the session, however, students of the IG felt significantly more confident of being able to memorize the learning contents in particular (IG: 1.50 SD(0.68); CG: 2.00 SD(0.99), p = 0.032) as well as significantly more motivated regarding the insertion of an IV catheter on their own (IG: 1.49 SD(0.64); CG: 2.13 SD(1.20), p = 0.019)."
