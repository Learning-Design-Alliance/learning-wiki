## Evidence

### Lou 2013

Lou, S.-J., Kao, M.-C., Yen, H.-L., & Shih, R.-C. (2013). Effects of applying blogs to assist life education instruction for elementary school students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018031

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-sample t-test analysis of the researchers' 21-item learning attitude scale (12 collaborative learning items, 9 attitude-toward-blog-use items). The article reports "p values are .000, reaching a significance level of .05" against the test value; no effect size is printed.
