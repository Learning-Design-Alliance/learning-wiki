## Evidence

### Mohan Jenanan 2025

Mohan Jenanan, Pishia Michaela, Alim Duaa, McGown Patrick J., Amin Anjali, Pinder Richard J., Halse Omid, Brown Celia, Sam Amir H. (2025). The impact of ethnicity information in single best answer questions on student performance, item response time and psychometric properties. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10489-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item discrimination analysis of 195 paired SBA items in the randomised parallel-forms exam; paired t-test on point-biserial correlations. The article reports "0.20 for questions with ethnicity vs. 0.21 for those without; t = 0.48, p = 0.63".

> "Adding ethnicity to the questions did not result in a statistically significant difference in item discrimination (mean item–total score point-biserial correlation = 0.20 for questions with ethnicity vs. 0.21 for those without; t = 0.48, p = 0.63)."
