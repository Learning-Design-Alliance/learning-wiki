## Evidence

### Raja Saud Mohammed 2025

Raja Saud Mohammed, Elfaki Mohammed Ahmed, Ibrahim Alaa, Maroof Hibaallah, Abdelrahim Abrar. (2025). The relationship between physical activity and academic achievement in medical students: a single-institution cross-sectional study in Sudan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07985-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 295 medical students at Omdurman Islamic University; independent samples t-test compared mean GPA between physically active and inactive students. The article reports "p-value = 0.7", with no effect size printed.

> "The mean GPA of the physically active group was 3.09 ± 0.47 while the physically inactive medical students had an average GPA of 3.16 ± 0.44 out of 4 (p-value = 0.7)."
