### Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year 2007

Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year. (2007). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

An observational analysis of Chicago Public Schools longitudinal data on students entering high school in September 2001, followed until September 2005, found that "More than 95 percent of students with a B average or better in their freshman year graduate." The brief also reports an 80-percent chance of finishing with at least a 3.0 GPA.
