## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The final GP population shows a trade-off between fit accuracy and model complexity, with Pareto-front solutions non-dominated in both respects. [→ J.O. Orove 2015](#jo-orove-2015)

## Evidence

### J.O. Orove 2015

J.O. Orove, N.E. Osegi, B.O. Eke. (2015). A Multi-Gene Genetic Programming Application for Predicting Students Failure at School. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1503.03211](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1503.03211)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical result shown in Figure 6 (fitness vs. complexity of the evolved multi-gene GP solutions), with blue points as dominated solutions and green points as the Pareto front. The article notes that a lower fitness value implies higher model complexity and vice-versa.

> "Solutions which are on the Pareto front are non -dominated in the sense that there are no solutions which have both a lower fitness and a lower fitness and a lower model complexity simultaneously than these ones."
