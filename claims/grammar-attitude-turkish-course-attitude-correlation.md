# Attitudes toward grammar correlate positively and moderately with attitudes toward the Turkish course

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Grammar attitude and Turkish course attitude scores were positively, moderately, and significantly correlated in both groups at pretest and posttest (e.g., r=0.681 in the experimental group at posttest). [→ Adil Kayan & İbrahim Seçkin Aydın 2020](#adil-kayan-ibrahim-seckin-aydn-2020)

## Evidence

### Adil Kayan & İbrahim Seçkin Aydın 2020

Adil Kayan & İbrahim Seçkin Aydın. (2020). The Effect of Computer-Assisted Educational Games on Teaching Grammar. World Journal of Education, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v10n1p117

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of pretest and posttest attitude scores (N=35 per group). Experimental posttest r=0.681, p<0.05; control posttest r=0.689; experimental pretest r=0.615; control pretest r=0.379, all p<0.05.

> "it has been brought to conclusion that there is a positive, moderate and statistically significant relationship between the  scores of grammar attitude posttest and Turkish course attitude posttest of the students in the experimental group (r=0.681, p<0.05)."
