### Emma Franchino 2026

Emma Franchino, Francesco Gariboldi, Alessandro Grecucci, Gianluca Lattanzi, and Massimo Stella. (2026). Complex networks map test anxiety and wellbeing levels in students and ChatGPT. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.13302](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.13302)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network analysis quantifying semantic overlap via the Jaccard Index shows that "Jaccard similarity between exam and anxiety frames exceeds 0.13" in high-anxiety human groups, versus GPT-oss networks that "rarely surpass 0.04".

> "In high-anxiety human groups, Jaccard similarity between exam and anxiety frames exceeds 0.13, approximately three to four times higher than values observed in GPT-oss networks, which rarely surpass 0.04."
