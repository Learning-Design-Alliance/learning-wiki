## Evidence

### Heijne-Penninga M. 2012

Heijne-Penninga M., Kuks J. B. M., Hofman W. H. A., Muijtjens A. M. M., Cohen-Schotanus J. (2012). Influence of PBL with open-book tests on knowledge retention measured with progress tests. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-012-9386-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of backup-knowledge subtests of four progress tests for all three curricula (n = 1,648); of eight test moments only one PBLob–TC and two PBLcb–TC differences reached significance (ES = 0.28, 0.34, 0.32). "PBLob students’ scores on backup knowledge did not significantly differ" across curricula; equivalence was not tested.

> "PBLob students’ scores on backup knowledge did not significantly differ from those of PBLcb and TC students (Table 3). Fifth and sixth-year TC students scored lowest on backup knowledge."
