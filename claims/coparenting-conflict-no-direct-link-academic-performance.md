# Co-parenting conflict is not directly associated with adolescent academic performance in Chinese adolescents aged 10–15

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a latent-variable SEM of 1989 Chinese adolescent-caregiver dyads, the direct path from co-parenting conflict to academic performance was not statistically significant. [→ Yang Hexin 2022](#yang-hexin-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Hexin 2022

Yang Hexin, Wu Chaoyue, Chen Ji-Kang. (2022). Interparental and Intergenerational Co-parenting Conflict and Adolescent Academic Performance: The Mediating Roles of Adolescent Academic Engagement and Depression. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315952

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent-variable SEM (WLSMV estimator, bootstrapping with 5000 samples) on the 2020 CFPS sample of 1989 dyads of adolescents aged 10–15 and primary caregivers. The direct path was "not directly associated with academic performance (β = −0.035, p > 0.05)". Model fit: RMSEA = 0.025, CFI = 0.993, TLI = 0.992, SRMR = 0.025.

> "Although the co-parenting conflict was not directly associated with academic performance (β = −0.035, p > 0.05), the indirect effects of academic performance and co-parenting conflict via academic engagement and depression were significant."
