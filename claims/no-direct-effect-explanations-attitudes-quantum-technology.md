## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the online experiment, explanation type showed no statistically significant difference on either affect-based or cognition-based attitudes towards quantum technology; equivalence was not tested. [→ Meinsma 2026](#meinsma-2026)

## Evidence

### Meinsma 2026

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus ANOVAs in the same pre-registered randomized online experiment on the two attitude indices; the article reports "no statistically significant differences between the conditions" and prints no effect sizes for these null results.

> "We found no statistically significant differences between the conditions for affect-based attitudes (F(2,1164) = 1.48, p = 0.23) or cognition-based attitudes (F(2,1164) = 1.81, p = 0.16)."
