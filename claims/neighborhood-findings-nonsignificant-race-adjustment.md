## Evidence

### Rakesh 2025

Rakesh, D., Sadikova, E., & McLaughlin, K. A. (2025). Associations among socioeconomic disadvantage, longitudinal changes in within-network connectivity, and academic outcomes in the ABCD study. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2025.101587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary models (Table S4) adjusted for race/ethnicity in the ABCD sample; the authors report the neighborhood-disadvantage findings did not survive this adjustment and offer an interpretation they explicitly make "with caution" given non-uniform ADI distribution across racial and ethnic groups.

> "Importantly however, findings for neighborhood disadvantage were no longer significant when models were adjusted for race and ethnicity. We suspect that this is likely due to the shared variance between neighborhood disadvantage and race in the United States"
