### Chao Xiaopei 2025

Chao Xiaopei, Li Xiaoyan. (2025). The application of the conceive-design-implement-operate combined with the problem-based-learning teaching method in an 8-year rotation system for obstetrics students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07646-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Level-distribution analysis of DOPS ratings in the 55-student skills evaluation, where level 1 equals dissatisfied (scores 1–3), level 2 satisfied (4–6), and level 3 excellent (7–9). The article reports the printed percentage shifts with P < 0.001; no effect size is given.

> "the CDIO teaching method significantly decreased the proportion of students assessed at level 1 from 42% to 0 and level 2 from 51.3 to 20.0% and increased the proportion of students assessed at level 3 from 1.7 to 78.1% (P < 0.001) (Fig. 4)."
