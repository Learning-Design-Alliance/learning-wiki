## Evidence

### Fieux Maxime 2021

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interobserver agreement analysis of anonymized video evaluations by two expert otologists from a different centre, blinded to participants' experience level; intraclass correlation coefficients were 0.98 for total PS and 0.99 for GRS. No systematic rater bias was found (−0.01, p = 0.266).

> "The scores from the two raters agreed closely, with an intraclass correlation coefficient of 0.98 [95 % CI, 0.97–0.99] for the total PS and 0.99 [95 % CI, 0.99–1.00] for the GRS score."
