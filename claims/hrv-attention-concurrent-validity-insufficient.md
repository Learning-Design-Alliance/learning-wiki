Hyun Jin Kim, Yuyi Park, Jihyun Lee. (2024). The Validity of Heart Rate Variability (HRV) in Educational Research and a Synthesis of Recommendations. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09878-x

In the same systematic review's validity synthesis, attention studies yielded only one high-correlation pair and two uncorrelated pairs; the authors state "the sample size is notably small" and evidence is insufficient. No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

> "For attention measurement, there was one L1/L2 pair and two L4 pair, the sample size is notably small, suggesting that there is insufficient evidence to support the concurrent validity of HRV data for measuring attention."
