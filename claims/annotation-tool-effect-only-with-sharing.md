# The Web-based annotation tool alone did not affect achievement, but with a sharing mechanism it produced significantly higher achievement than paper-and-pen annotation

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2011 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In phase 1, without sharing, there was no main effect for the annotation tool on achievement (F(1,120)=2.520, p>.05). [→ Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011](#wu-yuin-hwang-guo-liang-hsu-2011)
`q3 i?` In phase 2, with the sharing mechanism, the VPEN group significantly outperformed the paper-based group (F(1,120)=40.467, p<.01). [→ Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011 (2)](#wu-yuin-hwang-guo-liang-hsu-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase 1 two-way ANCOVA among the four classes; the VPEN group mean (78.61, N=62) was descriptively higher than the paper-based group (74.87, N=63), but the article reports "no main effect for the annotation tool"; equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no main effect for the annotation tool ( F (1,120) =2.520, p>.05) while having controlled pre-test scores"

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011 (2)

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase 2 two-way ANCOVA with the sharing mechanism active for VPEN classes; the VPEN group mean was 83.15 (N=62) versus 73.19 (N=63) for the paper-based group, a statistically significant difference per the article.

> "by employing a sharing annotation mechanism, the result indicates that a main effect of the annotation tool is F (1,120) =40.467, p<.01"
