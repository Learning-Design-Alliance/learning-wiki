## Evidence

### Pan Wenwen 2025

Pan Wenwen, Zhang Hongbao, Xu Long. (2025). Association between physical activity and academic achievement in adolescents mediated by self-concept and physical and mental health. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-18559-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 6, bootstrap CIs) in the same 458-adolescent survey; Table 5 lists the PA→SC→AA pathway as "Not supported" with a Bootstrap 95% CI of −0.000 to 0.060.

> "Specifically, the indirect effect of PA on AA through SC was β = 0.026, accounting for 7.43% of the total effect. Although the effect approached significance, the modest size of this pathway suggests that the role of SC in bridging PA and AA is likely context-dependent."
