The authors' chronological analysis associating workshop topics with the five generations of intelligent textbooks (Fig. 6) finds that adaptive interaction support remains important, semantic integration gave way to knowledge extraction, and extraction has now given way to LLM-driven work. The authors expect this trend to continue.

> "In turn, very recently, this line of research itself gave way to a new wave of papers driven by the recent success of Large Language Models (Generation 5)"
