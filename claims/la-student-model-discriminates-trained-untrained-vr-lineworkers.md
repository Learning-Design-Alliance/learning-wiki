### Santamaría Bonfil 2020

Santamaría Bonfil, G., Ibáñez Espiga, M. B., Pérez Ramírez, M., Arroyo Figueroa, G., & Martínez Álvarez, F. (2020). Learning analytics for student modeling in virtual reality training systems: Lineworkers case. Computers & Education, 151. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103871

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The abstract reports that a student model built from trace data of 1399 students in 329 VRTS courses can "discriminate between trained and untrained students", with Random Forest standing out among several classifiers tested.

> "The results show that the model can discriminate between trained and untrained students, the Random Forest algorithm standing out."
