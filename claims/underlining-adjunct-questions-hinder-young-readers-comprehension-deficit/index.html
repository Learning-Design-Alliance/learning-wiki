Evidence¶

Rickards 1979¶

Rickards, J.P., Denner, P.R. (1979). Depressive effects of underlining and adjunct questions on children's recall of text. Instructional Science, 8, 81–90. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00054983

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The authors' interpretation of the N=69 experiment's results: the findings "suggested a comprehension rather than a metacomprehension deficit", with the aids possibly hindering recall. This is the authors' stated conclusion from the reported pattern.

"These results suggested a comprehension rather than a metacomprehension deficit, whereby underlining and adjunct questions may hinder rather than help recall performance in young readers"
