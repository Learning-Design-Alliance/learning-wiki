Related Claims¶

  • Word reading efficiency and sentence comprehension show distinct behavioral subcomponent profiles: word reading correlates with phonological subcomponents, comprehension with morphological awareness and orthography-semantic mapping — related
  • Reading-defined regions uniquely predict distinct cognitive subcomponents: LSTG for phonetic representation, LSMG for phonological awareness, and LITG for orthography-phonology mapping — related
  • In Chinese children aged 10-12, oral word reading efficiency is positively correlated with cortical thickness in the LSTG, LITG, LSMG and RSTG — related
  • The word-reading and comprehension cortical regions are specific to their respective reading level: cross-level correlations were nonsignificant after FDR correction — related
  • In Chinese children aged 10-12, silent sentence reading comprehension is positively correlated with cortical thickness in the left parahippocampus and right calcarine fissure — related
