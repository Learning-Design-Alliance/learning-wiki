## Evidence

### Simon Veenman 1996

Simon Veenman. (1996). Effects of multigrade and multi-age classes reconsidered. Review of Educational Research, 66(3), 323-340. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170526

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic reanalysis of non-basic-subject outcomes from the best-evidence synthesis data. The tests for homogeneity/heterogeneity revealed the studies were significantly heterogeneous (p < .01); the largest effect size (g = -.91) came from an atypical study comparing a two-room rural schoolhouse with single-grade urban students.

> "I found the following weighted average ^-indexes: (a) for science -.19 (6 studies, N  =  5,989). (b) for social studies -.25 (4 studies, N = 3,970), and (c) for English as a foreign language .04 (3 studies, N = 9,429)."
