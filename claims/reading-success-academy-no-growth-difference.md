### Burlison 2014

Burlison, K., & Chave, J. (2014). The Impact of Reading Success Academy on High School Reading Achievement. Journal for Leadership and Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081400

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t test on developmental scale score (DSS) growth from the winter FCAT diagnostic to the 2012 reading FCAT, with significance set at p < .05. Descriptive means in Table 2 show treatment mean 3.20 (N 20, SD 11.414) versus control mean .70 (N 20, SD 11.457); the difference was not statistically significant.

> "The result of the statistical test (t = .494, p < .05) reveals that there was no significant difference in the amount of reading growth achieved by the treatment group subjects on the 2012 reading FCAT compared to the amount of reading growth achieved by the control group subjects on the 2012 reading FCAT."
