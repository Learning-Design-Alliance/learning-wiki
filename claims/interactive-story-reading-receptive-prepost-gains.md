## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experiment group children's receptive language standard scores rose significantly from pretest to posttest (z=-4.458, p=0.001), but the control group's also rose significantly (z=-4.284, p<0.05) and the two groups' receptive posttest scores did not differ significantly (z=-1.589, p=0.112): a within-group gain, not a between-group effect. [→ Okyay 2017](#okyay-2017)

## Evidence

### Okyay 2017

Okyay, O. & Kandir, A. (2017). Impact of the Interactive Story Reading Method on Receptive and Expressive Language Vocabulary of Children. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 395-406. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1149725

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed Rank analysis of TIFALDI receptive subtest pretest/posttest standard scores for the 26-child experiment group; the article reports "a statistically significant difference" with z= -4.458; p=0.001. No effect size is printed. The 26-child control group, on its existing programme, also improved significantly on the same subtest (118 to 130.1, z = -4.284), and the experiment-versus-control receptive posttest comparison was not significant (z = -1.589, p = 0.112), so this gain does not show the method outperformed the control condition on receptive vocabulary.

> "reveal a statistically significant difference between the pre -test and post -test standard scores achieved by the children in the experiment group (z= -4.458; p=0.001) (p<0.05)"
