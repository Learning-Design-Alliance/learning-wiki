## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, C. R., Vaughn, S., & McClelland, A. M. (2017). Intensive Reading Interventions for Inadequate Responders in Grades K–3: A Synthesis. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 191–210. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714446

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Study in Table 6: grade 1 inadequate responders were randomly assigned to one of two one-on-one Tier 3 conditions targeting fluency via re-reading, phonological skills, sight words, and writing; the "Text-Emphasis condition spent more time reading and re-reading text". Effects versus typical school remediation were positive.

> "Treatment (Text Emphasis) > Control: WRMT-R Basic Skills: d = 0.58; WRMT-R Word ID: d = 0.59; WRMT-R Word Attack: d = 0.40; WIAT Reading Comprehension: d = 0.42"
