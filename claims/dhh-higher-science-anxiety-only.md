### Pizzie 2025 (2)

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group comparisons (Table 1) found no significant differences between DHH and hearing participants on MR commission error rate, spatial habits (SHOMI-VIS, VVQ-VIS), or self-reported STEM interest, all p > 0.05. The article reports "no differences in MR commission error rate based on hearing status".

> "our results showed that there were no differences in MR commission error rate based on hearing status. The DHH group did not perform better on this task compared to the hearing group (Table 1), p > 0.05."
