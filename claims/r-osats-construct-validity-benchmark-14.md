`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Siddiqui et al.'s development and validation of robotic objective structured assessments of technical skills, with each of five generic drills scored across four categories from 1 to 5, giving a maximum of 20 per drill. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Using this, they were able to demonstrate construct validity for R-OSATS. They were also able to set up benchmark scores for R-OSATS [31 ••] using the modified Angoff and the contrasting groups methods. Using these methods, the minimum score for competence was determined to be 14 per drill."
