## Evidence

### Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026

Michael W. Asher & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Conditions for Effective Learning Without Upfront Instruction: How Practice with Feedback Supports Memory, Generalization, Motivation, and Metacognition. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10103-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Study 2 measured calibration by the difference between actual and predicted posttest scores before the test. Lecture participants overestimated performance "by 26 percentage points"; "average prediction errors were 13 points lower in the explanatory feedback condition" and 13.6 points lower with correct-response feedback; the practice conditions did not differ (p =.912).

> "Compared to lecture, average prediction errors were 13 points lower in the explanatory feedback condition, t(294) = 2.69, p =.008, and 13.6 points lower in the correct-response feedback condition, t(294) = 2.77, p =.006."
