## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Martins 2023

Martins, T., Santos, F., Lumini, M. J., Sousa, M. R., Peixoto, M. J., Freire, R. M., Salazar, B., Fernandes, C., & de Fátima Araújo, M. (2023). Realistic simulation in nursing education: Testing two scenario‐based models. Nursing Open, 10, 3326–3335. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1585

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test results from the within-subjects experiment (103 questionnaires completed; 87 in dimension analysis) comparing the traditional and digital scenarios on the 9-item Understand the clinical data dimension (Cronbach alpha 0.77): means of 79.81 (SD 6.10) versus 85.78 (SD 5.52), "8.101 (87)***" with p < 0.0001.
