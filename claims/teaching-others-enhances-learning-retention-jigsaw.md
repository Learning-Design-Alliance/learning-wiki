### Tran 2012

Tran, V. D., & Lewis, R. (2012). The Effects of Jigsaw Learning on Students' Attitudes in a Vietnamese Higher Education Classroom. International Journal of Higher Education, 1(2), 9. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v1n2p9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A student quote from the open-ended attitude survey (Subject 21) illustrating the third most frequent response category (22.5% of students) about the effectiveness of teaching others. The article notes this factor was 'the main technique of cooperative learning' and links it to greater long-term retention as suggested by Johnson & Johnson (2006).

> "I became "a teacher" because I had to teach the part which I had learned to the other group members. This helped me broaden my knowledge and retain this knowledge for a long time."
