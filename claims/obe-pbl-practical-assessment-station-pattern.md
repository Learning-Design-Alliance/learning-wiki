## Evidence

### Shi Wenjie 2025

Shi Wenjie, Shi Xiangsong, Zhuang Lirong, Zhu Tingting, Li Gongyin, Huang Chengbing. (2025). Integrating Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in psychiatric clinical practice teaching: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08212-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-station SP/OSCE practical assessment of the same 43 interns across four weighted stations. Table 1 reports total practical score t = 4.161, p < 0.001, with the Study Group at "84.68 ± 3.27" versus 80.57 ± 3.20 for controls.

> "The scores of the study group in the three stations (clinical skills, clinical thinking, interpretation of auxiliary examinations) and the total score of practical assessment were significantly higher (p < 0.05) than those of the control group, In contrast, the 4th station “Neurological Examination” showed no statistically significant difference between the two groups (p > 0.05)"

### Shi Wenjie 2025 (2)

Shi Wenjie, Shi Xiangsong, Zhuang Lirong, Zhu Tingting, Li Gongyin, Huang Chengbing. (2025). Integrating Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in psychiatric clinical practice teaching: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08212-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Station-level analysis in Table 1: clinical skills (p = 0.034), clinical thinking (p = 0.001) and auxiliary examination interpretation (p = 0.043) favored the Study Group, while the neurological examination station did not (t = 1.061, p = 0.295).

> "Only the “Neurological Examination” station, which is a technical operation-oriented station, showed no significant difference between the groups in the practical assessments."
