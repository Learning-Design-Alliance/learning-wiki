Frequency-based co-occurrence analysis of the ten AILEs across the 39 reviewed studies (Fig. 6 diagonal). The review reports the printed study counts and concludes that "practical, creative, and managerial dimensions of AI literacy remain relatively underrepresented in current educational practices".

"Fundamental AI Concepts (n = 36 studies), Computational Thinking (n = 29), and Data Literacy (n = 30) emerged as the most frequently developed AI literacy elements across the studies, confirming their central role in AI education"
