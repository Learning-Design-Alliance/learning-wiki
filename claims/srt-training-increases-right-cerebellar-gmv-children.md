## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Gray matter volume in right cerebellar lobule 8 (extending to 7b and adjacent crus) increased from day 1 to day 2 after SRT training, with no group or group × day interaction, in a sample of 17 DD and 18 TD children. [→ Olivo 2024](#olivo-2024)

## Evidence

### Olivo 2024

Olivo, G., Persson, J. & Hedenius, M. (2024). Exploring brain plasticity in developmental dyslexia through implicit sequence learning. npj Science of Learning, 9:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00250-w

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal VBM analysis (CAT12 pipeline) of structural MRI acquired 24 h apart around two in-scanner SRT training sessions in 17 DD and 18 TD children aged 9–13. The article reports increased GMV with "p FWE-corr < 0.001", "t=4 . 6 8", and "C o h e n’s d = 0.605"; no group × day interaction was detected.

> "A statistically signiﬁcant effect of time on GMV was found in the right cerebellar lobule 8, extending to the lobule 7b and adjacent crus, exhibiting increased GMV on day 2 compared with day 1 (p FWE-corr < 0.001; t=4 . 6 8 ;C o h e n’s d = 0.605;"
