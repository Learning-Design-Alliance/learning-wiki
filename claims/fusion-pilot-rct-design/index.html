Strand Cary 2013¶

Strand Cary, M., Doabler, C., Clarke, B., Fien, H., Baker, S. K., & Jungjohann, K. (2013). Evaluating the Promise of the FUSION Tier 2 Math Intervention. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563132

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Design description of the pilot randomized control trial in nine schools in two suburban districts with 151 students (68 female) taught by nine interventionists; no outcome results are printed because data were still being analyzed.

"Within school, students determined at-risk for math difficulties were randomly assigned to one of two conditions, FUSION Intervention or At-risk Control. Additional students were randomly selected to participate in an Average Control condition."
