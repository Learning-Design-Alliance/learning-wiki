### Breindahl Niklas 2025

Breindahl Niklas, Bay Emma Therese, Heiring Christian, MacLean-Nyegaard Ingrid Rose, Gudiksen Amalie, Frithioff Andreas, Henriksen Tine Brink, Tolsgaard Martin Grønnebæk, Aunsholt Lise. (2025). Assessing competency in less invasive surfactant administration: simulation-based validity evidence for the LISA-AT scores. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-03868-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Learning-curve analysis of 23 novices practising in a second round with one minute of feedback after each attempt until passing twice consecutively. The article reports a median of "6 [5–7]" attempts, range 4–9, all within the 90-minute limit.

> "The median [IQR] number of attempts required for the novices to reach the minimum passing score in the simulation setting was 6 [5–7], ranging from 4 to 9, which was possible within one training session of 90 min."
