### Leavy JE 2022

Leavy JE, Della Bona M, Nelson B, Leaversuch F. (2022). A comparison of face‐to‐face and fully online problem‐based learning: Student results and staff experiences, 2014–2020. Health Promot J Austral. https://doi.org/10.1002/hpja.636

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of administrative student results (n = 472; fully online n = 114, face-to-face n = 358) using independent sample t-tests. The article reports the Final Unit Mark was "significantly higher for fully online students" in 2018 (P = .007) and 2019 (P = .001); no effect size was printed.
