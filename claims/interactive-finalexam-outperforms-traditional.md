### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized two-group experiment (n = 50 per group), the final examination covering the course's twelve topics was compared via ANCOVA with pre-test covariate. The study reports "F(1,47) = 36.87, p < .001", with the interactive group averaging 80.80% and the traditional group 61.44%. No effect size was printed for this comparison.

> "The ANCOVA revealed a highly significant group difference, F(1,47) = 36.87, p < .001 , with the interactive group scoring 80.80% on average, far surpassing the traditional group’s 61.44%  — a 31.51% improvement"
