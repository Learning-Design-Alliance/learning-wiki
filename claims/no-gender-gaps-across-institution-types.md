### Salehi S. 2021

Salehi S., Berk S. A., Brunelli R., Cotner S., Creech C., Drake A. G., Fagbodun S., Hall C., Hebert S., Hewlett J., James A. C., Shuster M., St. Juliana J. R., Stovall D. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects regression on exam scores across 33 courses at seven institutions found no gender effect; the article reports "no significant effect of gender on average exam scores" with β and p values printed for each institution type. Equivalence was not tested.

> "We found no significant effect of gender on average exam scores (mixed-effects model: βCC = 0.09 ± 0.11, p = 0.96; βinclusive = 0.07 ± 0.08, p = 0.93; βselective = 0.08 ± 0.03, p = 0.13)."
