Qualitative finding from the focus groups and matrix analysis: given the lack of objective diagnostic criteria, all participant groups reported symptom recognition as important for identifying Long COVID, and IWLC wanted PCPs who would not dismiss their symptoms. The article states "recognizing both common and atypical symptom presentations emerged as a core competency."

> "Across stakeholder groups, recognizing both common and atypical symptom presentations emerged as a core competency. IWLC particularly emphasized the importance of being believed when describing less visible or poorly understood symptoms"
