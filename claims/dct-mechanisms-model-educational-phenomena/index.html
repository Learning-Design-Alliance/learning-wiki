Clark 1991¶

Clark, J. M., & Paivio, A. (1991). Dual coding theory and education. Educational Psychology Review, 3(3), 149–210. doi:10.1007/bf01320076

q2 · i? · review · r?

The article describes its methodological approach in a narrative review: after presenting the theory's premises, the authors show how "the basic DCT mechanisms can be used to model diverse educational phenomena." This is the article's own characterization of its modeling enterprise, not a measured comparison.
