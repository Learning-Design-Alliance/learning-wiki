`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

fsQCA sufficiency analysis. C2 paired core autonomy need satisfaction with core GAI use, suggesting GAI use may accompany high engagement when co-occurring with autonomous choice, but its low unique coverage led the authors to interpret it as supplementary.

> "C2 had a consistency of 0.868, a PRI of 0.785, a raw coverage of 0.599, and a unique coverage of only 0.020, indicating substantial overlap with the other configurations."
