# Potential early adopters of micro-credentials are driven by intrinsic motivations and heavy informal professional learning, while late adopters are more experienced and driven by extrinsic motivations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Early adopters are most engaged in professional development, especially informal activities, and are intrinsically motivated (70 percent say making me a better teacher), whereas late adopters are clustered among the most experienced teachers (49 percent report 16 or more years) and driven by extrinsic motivations such as pay and meeting requirements. [→ Grunwald Associates LLC and Digital Promise 2015](#grunwald-associates-llc-and-digital-promise-2015)
`q3 i?` Early adopters use new informal modalities at multiples of late adopters' rates: twice as likely to use expert practice videos, more than three times as likely to read professional learning blogs, and four times as likely to try a MOOC or follow professional learning resources on Twitter. [→ Grunwald Associates LLC and Digital Promise 2015](#grunwald-associates-llc-and-digital-promise-2015)
