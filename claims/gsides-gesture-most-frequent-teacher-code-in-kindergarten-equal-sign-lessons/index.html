Sung 2022¶

Sung, Y., Stephens, A., Veltri Torres, R., Strachota, S., Blanton, M., Gardiner, A., Stroud, R., & Knuth, E. (2022). Teacher Language and Gesture in an Intervention Focused on Developing Kindergarteners' Understandings of the Equal Sign. Proceedings of the forty-fourth annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED630361

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative coding of classroom video from one kindergarten classroom's seven equivalence lessons. The article reports "38 instances of the GSIDES code", alongside 28 ISA, 26 BAL, 17 DEF, 11 GBAL and 6 FORM instances. Counts are descriptive; no test is reported.

"Across the seven lessons that addressed mathematical equivalence, we observed 26 instances of the BAL code, 17 instances of the DEF code, 6 instances of the FORM code, 28 instances of the ISA code, 11 instances of the GBAL code and 38 instances of the GSIDES code (see Table 2)."
