## Evidence

### Zähringer 2024

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Welch Two Sample t-tests on AP ratings in the crossover study; the female-male difference was significant, with "IaePP: d = 0.45, [0.18 - 0.72]" and "IafPP: d = 0.38, [0.11 - 0.65]".

> "This difference turned out to be significant (IaePP: t(173) = 3.32, p < 0.005, IafPP: t(199) = 2.80, p < 0.01), with effect sizes around d = 0.4 (IaePP: d = 0.45, [0.18 - 0.72], IafPP: d = 0.38, [0.11 - 0.65])."

### Zähringer 2024 (2)

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same t-test analysis found no significant gender differences "in the ratings of CEPs" for either instrument; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant differences in the ratings of CEPs between female (IaePP: M = 2.98, SD = 1.13, IafPP: M = 3.01, SD = 1.01) and male (IaePP: M = 2.83, SD = 1.30, IafPP: M = 2.83, SD = 1.13) participants."

### Zähringer 2024 (3)

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of learner characteristics and picture ratings; only IME ("ρ = 0.35") and interest in physics ("ρ = 0.32") correlated significantly with aesthetic ratings of the APs.

> "For the aesthetic perception, the only two weak but statistically significant correlations were between IME and I_Phy and the rating of the APs: (IME: ρ = 0.35, 95% CI: [0.18, 0.41], p < 0.001; I_Phy: ρ = 0.32, 95% CI: [0.17, 0.43], p < 0.001)."

### Zähringer 2024 (4)

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same correlational analysis "revealed no significant correlations of the affective perception for any combination of picture categories with topics"; no effect sizes printed.

> "Regarding a possible influence of intrinsic motivation/engagement (IME), interest in physics (I_Phy) and interest in photography (I_Pho), the analysis revealed no significant correlations of the affective perception for any combination of picture categories with topics."
