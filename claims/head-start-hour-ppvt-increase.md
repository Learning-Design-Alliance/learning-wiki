## Evidence

### Li 2013

Li, W., Farkas, G., Duncan, G. J., Vandell, D. L., & Burchinal, M. (2013). Effects of Head Start hours on children's cognitive, pre-academic, and behavioral outcomes: An instrumental variable analysis. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563041

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instrumental variable (2SLS) analysis of the Head Start Impact Study random assignment, using hours offered by centers as instruments for hours attended. The article reports a statistically significant increase in PPVT scores of .062 SD per additional daily hour.

> "The IV results indicate that for the full sample of both cohorts, an additional hour per day spent in Head Start centers increased PPVT scores by .062 SD (se = .011; p < .001)."
