Quasi-experimental study of 108 pupils with learning disabilities. Quality of reading was assessed via a questionnaire adapted from Biderman (1983), evaluating character descriptions (credibility, precision, depth, range) and story summarisation, then validated with a five-index overall grade.

> "Figure 5 shows that the rating of the research group’s quality of reading is significantly higher than that of the control group in all parameters ( p<.001)."
