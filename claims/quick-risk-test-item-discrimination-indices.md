## Evidence

### Jenny Mirjam Annina 2018

Jenny Mirjam Annina, Keller Niklas, Gigerenzer Gerd. (2018). Assessing minimal medical statistical literacy using the Quick Risk Test: a prospective observational study in Germany. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020847

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Psychometric item analysis of the student pretest data (n=169); the index was calculated as the point-biserial correlation between a question's score and the total score, and all other questions had values between 0.20 and 0.40.

> "In the student data before the training, the Quick Risk Test's median item discrimination index was 0.23 (IQR=0.14–0.28). Three questions had values below 0.2, which are considered low indices (question 5=0.10; question 8=0.11; question 10=0.10)."
