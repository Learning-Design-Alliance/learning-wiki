Sociodemographic data collected from 242 nurse educators across Spain show that only 21.9% reported pedagogical training was required to become a professor, while 78.1% reported it was not. The article contextualizes this within the European university context where 'specific training in teaching is not always an essential requirement for accessing a teaching position in nursing.'
