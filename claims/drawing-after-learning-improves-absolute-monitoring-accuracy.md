# Drawing after learning, both from scratch and with spatial scaffolds, improves absolute monitoring accuracy (calibration) compared with a time-matched mental reactivation control condition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students who drew after each text paragraph showed better absolute monitoring accuracy than students who mentally reactivated the contents, d = 0.36. [→ Julia Kollmer 2020](#julia-kollmer-2020)

## Evidence

### Julia Kollmer 2020

Julia Kollmer, Katrin Schleinschok, Katharina Scheiter, Alexander Eitel. (2020). Is drawing after learning effective for metacognitive monitoring only when supported by spatial scaffolds? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09521-6

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 88 university students (29 control, 29 drawing-from-scratch, 30 scaffolded drawing) learning a five-paragraph text on auroras. A linear mixed effects model on JOL minus performance per paragraph showed the control condition had higher overconfidence than both drawing conditions, "b = 2.84, SE = 0.81, t = 3.50, p < 0.001, d = 0.36".

> "Results revealed significant differences in line with this contrast, b = 2.84, SE = 0.81, t = 3.50, p < 0.001, d = 0.36"
