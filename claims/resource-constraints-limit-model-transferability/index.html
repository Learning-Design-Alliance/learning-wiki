Authors' interpretive synthesis in the discussion, attributing transferability concerns to contextual conditions. The review states these environments are "marked by high patient load, staff shortages, limited infrastructure, and competing service demands" which may hinder effective application; it adds that without empirical evidence of adaptation, "the transferability of such models may be ineffective."

"The clinical environments in low- and middle-income countries are often marked by high patient load, staff shortages, limited infrastructure, and competing service demands which may hinder effective application of the structured, time-intensive, and feedback demanding clinical teaching models"
