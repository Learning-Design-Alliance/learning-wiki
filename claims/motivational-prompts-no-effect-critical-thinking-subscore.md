## Evidence

### Hawthorne 2015

Hawthorne, K. A., Bol, L., Pribesh, S., & Suh, Y. (2015). Effects of Motivational Prompts on Motivation, Effort, and Performance on a Low-Stakes Standardized Test. Journal of General Education, 64(2).

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experimental study with 87 undergraduates in four conditions. Although students in the personal condition descriptively outperformed the other three conditions on the critical thinking subscore, the ANOVA showed no significant condition effect.

> "The results failed to support the main effect of condition on students’ critical thinking subscores, F(3, 83) = 1.131, p > .05, η2 = .039."
