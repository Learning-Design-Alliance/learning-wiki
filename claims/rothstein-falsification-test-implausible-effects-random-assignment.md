# The Rothstein falsification test generates large and implausible future teacher effects even when students are randomly assigned to classrooms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Rothstein falsification test yields large and implausible future teacher effects even under random assignment of students to classrooms, so such effects are not by themselves evidence of bias in value-added models. [→ Dan Goldhaber 2015](#dan-goldhaber-2015)
