## Evidence

### Bosch 2017

Bosch, N., & D'Mello, S. (2017). The Affective Experience of Novice Computer Programmers. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0069-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six Wilcoxon signed rank tests comparing affect across the two study phases in the same 99-student lab study found significant (p<.001) differences for engagement, neutral, and curiosity; the article reports "more engagement in the fadeout phase" and more curiosity and neutral in the scaffolded phase.

> "However, there was more engagement in the fadeout phase ( M=.329, SD=.266) compared to the scaffolded phase ( M=.210 , SD=.1 74), Z=−4.45, p<.0 01."
