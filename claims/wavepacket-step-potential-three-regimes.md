## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the wavepacket simulation, if ⟨E⟩ ≪ Uo the wavepacket is completely reflected; if ⟨E⟩ ≈ Uo a portion is reflected and a portion transmitted; if ⟨E⟩ ≫ Uo almost all of the wave is transmitted. [→ R. Garcia 2007](#r-garcia-2007)

## Evidence

### R. Garcia 2007

R. Garcia, A. Zozulya, and J. Stickney. (2007). MATLAB codes for teaching quantum physics: Part 1. https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1622 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0704.1622](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0704.1622)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Results of Program 6 shown in Fig. 3, where a wavepacket built from a Gaussian momentum distribution propagates into a step potential of height ~⟨E⟩ located at x/L = 0.5, propagated via exp(−iHt/ℏ). The regimes are stated as qualitative simulation outcomes.

> "If ⟨E⟩ ≪ Uo, the wavepacket is completely reﬂected from the barrier. If ⟨E⟩ ≈ Uo, a portion of the wave is is reﬂected and a portion is transmitted through. If ⟨E⟩ ≫ Uo, almost all of the wave is transmitted"
