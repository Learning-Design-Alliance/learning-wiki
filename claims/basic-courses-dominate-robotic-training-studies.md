Rahimi A. Masie, Uluç Ezgi, Hardon Sem F., Bonjer H. Jaap, van der Peet Donald L., Daams Freek. (2024). Training in robotic-assisted surgery: a systematic review of training modalities and objective and subjective assessment methods. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10915-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review divided studies into basic courses (fundamental skills) and advanced courses (specific technical and procedural skills). Basic-course studies most often involved medical students or mixed experience groups; advanced courses used mostly da Vinci dry and wet lab.

> "Out of the 209 studies, 165 (79%) consisted of basic courses, and 44 (21%) consisted of advanced courses. The majority of the basic courses consisted of dry lab on the da Vinci Surgical System (73/195; 37%) and a virtual simulator (83/195; 43%)"
