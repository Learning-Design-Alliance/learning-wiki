# MDT-CDIO integrated teaching yields higher theoretical assessment scores than traditional teaching in orthopedic clinical education

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Orthopedic interns taught with the MDT-CDIO model scored significantly higher on the theoretical examination (47.23 ± 0.72) than those taught with traditional disease-centered lectures (45.40 ± 1.16), P < 0.001. [→ Nian Zhixuan 2026](#nian-zhixuan-2026)

## Evidence

### Nian Zhixuan 2026

Nian Zhixuan, Li Mingchun, Liang Junwen, Li Yijia, Wei Ziting, Pan Liqiang, Fang Sen, Yang Xudong, Du Wenjia, Yun Xiangdong. (2026). Effectiveness of an MDT-CDIO integrated teaching model in orthopedic clinical education: a controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09758-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled study of 60 orthopedic interns randomized 1:1 to MDT-CDIO or traditional teaching; theoretical assessment compared by independent-samples t-test. The experimental group scored "47.23 ± 0.72 vs. 45.40 ± 1.16" with P < 0.001; Mann–Whitney U sensitivity analysis confirmed significance (U = 804.5).

> "Students in the MDT–CDIO group achieved significantly higher scores than those in the control group in the theoretical assessment, practical assessment, and total score (47.23 ± 0.72 vs. 45.40 ± 1.16, 46.03 ± 0.76 vs. 44.63 ± 0.89, and 93.18 ± 1.00 vs. 90.03 ± 1.37, respectively; all P < 0.001; Table 4)."
