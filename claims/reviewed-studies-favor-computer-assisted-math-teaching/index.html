Evidence¶

Salah 2016¶

Dr. Raed Omar Salah. (2016). Effectiveness of Using Computerized Educational Packages in Teaching Math Curriculum on the Learning of Student in Eighth Grade at Ma'an City. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099555

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Narrative review attributing this finding to Afaneh (2003), a study of 86 fifth-grade students in Ramala governorates taught an area unit via a PowerPoint program versus two traditional methods. The review reports the difference in favor of the computer group and, on gender, in favor of females.

"The results shows presence of functional differences in favor of the experimental group which studied by using the computer and presence of difference in learning by using computer refers to gender in favor of females."
