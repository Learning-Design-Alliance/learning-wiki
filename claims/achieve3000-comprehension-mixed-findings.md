### What Works Clearinghouse 2018

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2018). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: Achieve3000®. https://whatworks.ed.gov

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

WWC synthesis of two studies (12,698 students in 36 schools) reporting: Tracey and Young's SRI effect size of 0.29 was substantively important though not statistically significant after clustering correction, and Hill and Lenard's EOG composite effects were indeterminate.

> "Thus, for the comprehension domain, one study reported a substantively important positive effect and one study reported an indeterminate effect."
