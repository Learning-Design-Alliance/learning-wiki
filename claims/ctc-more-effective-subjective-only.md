# 'Challenge the challenger' was more effective than 'group quiz' for the subjective section, but not for the objective section

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The understanding-by-intervention interaction was significant for the subjective section, F(1, 141) = 24.352, p = .000, favouring challenge the challenger. [→ Chang Peng Kee 2013](#chang-peng-kee-2013)
`q2 i?` For the objective section the interaction was not significant, F(1, 141) = 2.204, p = .157, so challenge the challenger could not be said to be more effective. [→ Chang Peng Kee 2013 (2)](#chang-peng-kee-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Chang Peng Kee 2013

Chang Peng Kee, Kamisah Osman & Fauziah Ahmad. (2013). Challenge in Enhancing the Teaching and Learning of Variable Measurements in Quantitative Research. International Education Studies, 6(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n6p15

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interaction test from the General Linear Model difference test (Table 2) on subjective-section scores of the 143 completers. The article reports the interaction F (1, 141) = 24.352, p = .000 and concludes challenge the challenger is "more effective than" group quiz for the subjective section; no effect size is printed.

> "difference test value F (1, 141) = 24.352, p = .000 is presented to see the effect of interaction in understanding increment. The results of the second research question revealed that "challenge the challenger method" is proven to be more effective than "group quiz" for the subjective section."

### Chang Peng Kee 2013 (2)

Chang Peng Kee, Kamisah Osman & Fauziah Ahmad. (2013). Challenge in Enhancing the Teaching and Learning of Variable Measurements in Quantitative Research. International Education Studies, 6(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n6p15

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interaction test on objective-section scores in the same analysis (Table 2). The article reports F (1, 141) = 2.204, p = .157 and states the null that both interventions' effects are the same was not rejected; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "However, F (1, 141) = 2.204, p = .157 had failed to reject the null hypothesis that said the effect of both interventions is the same. "Challenge the challenger" technique can't be said to be more effective than "group quiz" for objective section."
