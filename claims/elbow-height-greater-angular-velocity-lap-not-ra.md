## Evidence

### Amirthanayagam Anumithra 2026

Amirthanayagam Anumithra, Zecca Massimiliano, Chitrakar Anisha, Chavda Vijay, Moss Esther L. (2026). Looks like a surgeon? Impact of anthropometry on the kinematics of surgical novices: counterbalanced cross-over design study of minimally invasive surgery. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-04077-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model analysis of upper-arm sensor data from 54 surgical novices performing three simulated tasks on LAP and da Vinci Xi platforms, controlling for sex, task, order, starting modality and arm. The model showed "a significant inverse relationship was observed in LAP tasks, where decreasing elbow height was associated with greater angular velocity" with the printed slope, CI and p value; no effect size was printed.

> "Specifically, a significant inverse relationship was observed in LAP tasks, where decreasing elbow height was associated with greater angular velocity (− 0.82 °/s per 10 cm; 95% CI − 1.34 to − 0.31; p = 0.002) (Fig. 4)."
