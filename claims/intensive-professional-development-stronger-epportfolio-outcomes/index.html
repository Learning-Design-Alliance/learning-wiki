q2 · i? · design · r2

The paper reports this as a cross-institutional pattern, citing Getman-Eraso & Culkin (2018) as an example, congruent with the IUPUI ePDP pilot's non-sustained results. The comparison names three conditions (extended intensive PD, brief training, no PD); no effect sizes are printed.

"This development was congruent with findings at other universities that also demonstrated more powerful outcomes from ePortfolio when faculty and staff participated in extended intensive professional development than when they engaged in brief training programs or no professional development."
