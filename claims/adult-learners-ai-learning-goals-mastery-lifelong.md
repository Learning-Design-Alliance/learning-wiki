## Evidence

### Jinhee Kim 2025

Jinhee Kim, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick, Xi Lin, Na Li. (2025). Designing AI-powered learning: adult learners' expectations for curriculum and human-AI interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10549-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 48 e-portfolios and interviews with 20 adult learners in a virtual exchange program; the theme appeared in 85% of e-portfolios and 70% of interviews (Table 1). Learners in STEM professions sought "mastery in AI-powered data analysis and programming tools."

> "ALs emphasized the importance of achieving content mastery as a foundational goal of AI-powered learning, aligning with the principle that adult education should focus on directly applicable knowledge and skills"

### Jinhee Kim 2025 (2)

Jinhee Kim, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick, Xi Lin, Na Li. (2025). Designing AI-powered learning: adult learners' expectations for curriculum and human-AI interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10549-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on lifelong learning habits; participants connected the habit to self-regulation such as "cultivating a learning mindset and monitoring progress" (P10) and mapping routines to evaluate outcomes (P15).

> "Participants highlighted that lifelong learning requires intentionality and alignment with personal objectives: "It's about determining what you really want to know and setting goals that align with your personal and professional objectives." (P16)."
