# No included systematic review reported outcomes on nurses' practice change (Kirkpatrick level 3), so the transfer of e-learning to practice remains unknown

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No outcomes related to nurses' changes in practice were reported in any of the 22 included SRs, and the review concludes effectiveness regarding transfer to practice and patient outcomes remains unknown. [→ Rouleau G 2019](#rouleau-g-2019)

## Evidence

### Rouleau G 2019

Rouleau G, Gagnon MP, Côté J, Payne-Gagnon J, Hudson E, Dubois CA, Bouix-Picasso J. (2019). Effects of E-Learning in a Continuing Education Context on Nursing Care: Systematic Review of Systematic Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed-Studies Reviews. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/15118

`q3 · i?`

Discussion of the Kirkpatrick level 3 synthesis across the 22 included SRs. The review states "We found no SRs that reported outcomes regarding the translation of the content of e-learning interventions into nurses' practice and behavior", while cautioning this is not evidence of no effect.

> "We found no SRs that reported outcomes regarding the translation of the content of e-learning interventions into nurses' practice and behavior (ie, evaluation level 3). This finding does not mean that e-learning had no outcomes on practice."
