# The learning patterns model differs from SRL models by being multi-dimensional, non-normative, and including external regulation and lack of regulation

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Compared to SRL models, the learning patterns model is less normative, multi-dimensional, postulates three forms of regulation, and includes dimensions such as application-directed and undirected learning absent from most SRL models. ## Evidence

### Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

Theoretical argument in the review's framework section: the learning patterns model postulates self-regulation, external regulation, and lack of regulation, and its four-component distinction includes concepts such as concrete processing and ambivalent orientation not present in most SRL models.

> "Basically, the SRL model is unidimensional and posits one ideal way of learning, namely self-regulated learning, while the learning patterns model is multi-dimensional and grounded in students'experiences of learning in normal study settings"
