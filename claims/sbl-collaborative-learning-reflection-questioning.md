## Evidence

### Fukamizu Junichi 2021

Fukamizu Junichi, Verstegen Daniëlle, Ho Sin Chi. (2021). International trainer perceptions of simulation-based learning: a qualitative study. International Journal of Medical Education, 12:267-273. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.61b3.214c

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview themes 'Facilitating learning as a group' and 'Stimulating reflection': trainers reported they "promote dialog by asking questions" and advocate thinking aloud; one noted one-to-one interaction makes learners stop thinking.

> "Trainers feel responsible for guiding the trainees to reflect. They describe how they promote dialog by asking questions and advocate thinking aloud to stimulate prior knowledge and reflection."
