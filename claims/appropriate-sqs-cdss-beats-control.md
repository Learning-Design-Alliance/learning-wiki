## Evidence

### Yanagita Yasutaka 2025

Yanagita Yasutaka, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yokokawa Daiki, Ishizuka Kosuke, Tsukamoto Tomoko, Li Yu, Uehara Takanori, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2025). Appropriate semantic qualifiers increase diagnostic accuracy when using a clinical decision support system: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07294-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the randomized controlled trial comparing the 22-student CDSS group with the 20-student control group on the 248 appropriate-SQ diagnoses: 93 correct (69.9%) in the CDSS group versus 56 correct (48.7%) in the control group, reported as "significantly higher".

> "With appropriate SQs, diagnostic accuracy was significantly higher in the CDSS group compared to the control group (χ2(1) = 1.16 × 10, p < 0.001)"
