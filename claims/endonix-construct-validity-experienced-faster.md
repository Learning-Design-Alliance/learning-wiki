### Hohlstein Philipp 2026

Hohlstein Philipp, Hollenbach Marcus, Cahyadi Oscar, Beck Maximilian, Bittel Miriam, Dimitriadis Stavros, Garbe Jakob, Geissler Mark E., Heilani Myriam W., Huber Yvonne, Koch Alexander, Kruse Leah, Mondorf Antonia, Schlosser-Hupf Sophie, Staudacher Jonas J., Welsch Lukas, von Hahn Thomas, Michael Florian A., Hamesch Karim. (2026). Feasibility, usability, and validity assessment of a novel plug-and-play virtual endoscopy simulator. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12396-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional observational study of 310 congress delegates (114 novices, 49 beginners, 147 experienced) each completing two simulator modules. Novices took "85.4 s vs. 47.4 s" longer than experienced endoscopists; the beginners-versus-experienced contrast was not significant, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Time to completion of the "Trace the Line" and "ESD marking" modules was longer for novices compared to beginners (85.4 s vs. 55.0 s, P < 0.001) and compared to experienced endoscopists (85.4 s vs. 47.4 s, P < 0.001). No significant difference was found between beginners and experienced endoscopists"
