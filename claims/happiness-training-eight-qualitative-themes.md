Qualitative phase: semi-structured interviews with five purposefully selected teachers (3–20 years' experience), analyzed via a six-phase thematic analysis process; no effect sizes are reported for these themes.

> "The main themes identified in the narratives of teachers participating in the happiness training include emotional exhaustion and recovery, reconnection with purpose, improved classroom engagement, stress management techniques, positive relationships with students and colleagues, increased job satisfaction, work-life balance, and sustained professional growth."
