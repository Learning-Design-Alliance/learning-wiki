`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cost accounting from the Botswana phone-assessment pilot, compared in the article with PIRLS country participation (about US$250 000, about 4000 students, "about US$62.5 per child", called a likely lower bound) and a Liberia school-based and home-based tracking study at US$150 per child.
