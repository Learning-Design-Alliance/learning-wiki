Evidence¶

Li 2020¶

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline equivalence analysis reported in the participant characteristics section, using a two-level regression model accounting for random-assignment blocks with Huber-White standard errors, on both the matched sample (n=318) and baseline sample (n=754). "No statistical differences" were found; equivalence beyond the test was not established and no effect size is printed.

"Regardless of the sample used, there were no statistical differences between the treatment group and the control group at baseline."
