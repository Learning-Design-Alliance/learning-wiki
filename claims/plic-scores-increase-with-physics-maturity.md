## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Pre-instruction PLIC means differ statistically between physics-maturity groups, with effect sizes from small (d = 0.31, BFY vs graduate) to very large (d = 1.6, FY vs experts). [→ Cole Walsh 2019](#cole-walsh-2019)

## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, and N. G. Holmes. (2019). Assessment of critical thinking in physics labs. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of matched pre-instruction PLIC scores across 584 FY, 108 BFY, and 34 graduate students plus 48 experts, collected 2017-2018 across 12 institutions. Unpaired t-tests gave p ≪ 0.01 between all groups except BFY vs graduate (p = 0.12), with "very large (d = 1.6) between students in FY labs and experts".

> "The effect sizes ranged from small (Cohen's d = 0.31) between the students in BFY and graduate labs to very large (d = 1.6) between students in FY labs and experts."
