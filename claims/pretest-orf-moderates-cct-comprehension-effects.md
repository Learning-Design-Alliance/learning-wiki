## Evidence

### Nathan H. Clemens 2019

Nathan H. Clemens, Eric Oslund, Oi-man Kwok, Melissa Fogarty, Deborah Simmons, and John L. Davis. (2019). Skill Moderators of the Effects of a Reading Comprehension Intervention. Exceptional Children, 85(2), 197–211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918787339

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of a randomized controlled trial with 226 Grade 6–8 struggling readers, using latent variable models with pretest comprehension and demographic covariates. The interaction of condition with pretest ORF was significant: "β = −0.116, p = .03".

> "However, pretest ORF demonstrated a statistically significant interaction with condition (β = −0.116, p = .03), indicating pretest reading fluency moderated the effects of intervention condition. The negative effect indicates that as students' pretest ORF scores decreased, a greater effect of the CCT intervention was observed"
