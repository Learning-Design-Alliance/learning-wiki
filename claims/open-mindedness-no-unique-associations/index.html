Evidence¶

Burić 2023¶

Burić, Irena, Butković, Ana and Kim, Lisa (2023) Teacher personality domains and facets: Their unique associations with student-ratings of teaching quality and teacher enthusiasm. Learning and Instruction. 101827. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101827

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Open-mindedness bifactor-(S-1) MSEM in the Croatian sample; the article reports that although the domain and its facets correlated positively with student support and teacher enthusiasm bivariately, the model "failed to reveal any significant unique associations". Equivalence was not tested.
