  • Immersion principle: people do not necessarily learn better in 3D immersive virtual reality than with a corresponding 2D desktop presentation (d = -0.10 across 9 tests) — related
  • An LX design integrating an immersive VR simulation in an inquiry-based Physics learning environment was enacted with 109 high-school students and evaluated for conceptual learning gains — related
  • Substituting learner–content interaction for learner–learner interaction produced no significant difference in measured performance — related
  • Affective outcomes of virtual-laboratory modalities are divided and interact with learners' initial attitude profiles — related
  • Several included studies found haptic VR training equivalent rather than superior to conventional dental training — related
  • Immersive reality training produced higher knowledge and skill scores than conventional training in dental studies — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Three comparative studies reported superior outcomes for distance simulation over traditional training, involving augmented reality telementoring, remote robotic teaching, and a virtual classroom — related
