### Tsukamoto Atsushi 2026

Tsukamoto Atsushi, Zan Thum Su, Nitta Makie, Yoshida Hiromitsu, Katahira Hirotaka, Fujita Yoshiharu, Takagi Satoshi, Nakamura Shinichiro. (2026). Educational efficacy of training videos and simulators for teaching basic mouse experimental skills to novice veterinary students. Experimental Animals. https://doi.org/10.1538/expanim.25-0093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-practice questionnaire of 149 second-year veterinary students rating use frequency and necessity of three alternative tools on five-point Likert scales. The survey found "a large proportion of students used training videos during most or all practice sessions, followed by the Mimicky® Mouse and fabric mouse toy", with necessity ratings showing a similar distribution.
