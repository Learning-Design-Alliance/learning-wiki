Jeanne Wanzek 2019¶

Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same latent moderated SEM analysis of the class-randomized trial, the moderating effect of pretest ASK content knowledge on treatment effects for ASK content reading comprehension (controlling for pretest comprehension) was tested. The interaction "does not differ statis- tically from 0 (p = .54)"; no effect size was printed for this model.

"The interaction term, γ04z.jxj, does not differ statis- tically from 0 (p = .54). Because PACT does not have a main effect on content reading comprehension (Vaughn et al., 2015), the absence of a moderating effect represents a less promising finding"
