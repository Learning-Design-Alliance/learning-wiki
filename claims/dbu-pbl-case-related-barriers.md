### Tefera Aklile Semu 2024

Tefera Aklile Semu, Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Urgie Besufekad Mulugeta, Hassen Erzik Muhammed, Tamene Tilahun Deresse, Gebeyaw Enguday Demeke. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme analysis of tutor and student interviews on PBL cases found that "some of the cases lack clarity and do not provide a clear objective for discussion". Students also reported that equal time for all cases is not fair and that cases were discussed before the relevant class lectures.

> "some of the cases lack clarity and do not provide a clear objective for discussion. In certain cases, PBL cases are exceptionally difficult to understand, lacking a clearly stated objective"
