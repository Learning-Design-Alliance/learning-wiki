Evidence¶

Cheng-Wen He 2025¶

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quade ANCOVA of coded problem-solving performance in Experiment 1, controlling for prior knowledge. As expected, "significantly outperformed the PS-I students" during problem-solving; the I-PS group also generated significantly more ideas (F(1,365) = 11.53, p = .001).

"the I-PS students ( M=2 .72,SD = 3.37 ) significantly outperformed the PS-I students ( M=0 .93,SD = 1.58 ) during the problem-solving phase, F(1,365) = 44.54 , p < .001"

Cheng-Wen He 2025 (2)¶

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quade ANCOVA of embedded video-quiz scores in Experiment 1, controlling for prior knowledge: F(1,365) = 6.69, p = .010. The authors interpret that "experiencing the problem-solving phase first enhanced learning from the instructional video".

"the PS-I group ( M=5 .38,SD = 0.91 ) scored significantly higher on the quizzes than the I-PS group ( M=5 .10,SD = 1.04 )"
