## Evidence

### Martinho S 2026

Martinho S, Nóbrega S, Barroso C, Correia-Pinto J. (2026). Trainee involvement in laparoscopic pyloromyotomy does not compromise outcomes: evidence from a longitudinal institutional experience. Pediatric Surgery International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06435-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort analysis of 77 infants. The composite endpoint combined surgery-related complications and incomplete pyloromyotomy; the study reports "11.1% for the specialist group vs. 3.1% for the resident group (p = 0.39)". Time to full oral tolerance, length of stay, reintervention and readmission were statistically comparable (Table 2).

> "When both events were analyzed together, the composite adverse outcome rate was 11.1% for the specialist group vs. 3.1% for the resident group (p = 0.39)."
