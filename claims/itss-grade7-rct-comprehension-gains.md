# In the grade 7 cluster RCT, ITSS produced statistically significant positive effects on all eight comprehension outcomes (average effect size 0.23)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### gsrt-7-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, April). Web-based Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/703

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial in 25 rural and suburban middle schools; 59 intervention and 49 comparison classrooms. On the GSRT with 1,868 seventh-grade students, effect size was 0.17 and improvement index +7, p < .01; posttest means were not reported for the analytic sample.
