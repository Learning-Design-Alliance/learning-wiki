### Najafi and Talebinezhad 2018

Maryam Najafi, Mohammad Reza Talebinezhad. (2018). The Impact of Teaching EFL Medical Vocabulary Through Collocations on Vocabulary Retention of EFL Medical Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(5). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.5p.24

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired and independent t-tests on the 40-item medical collocation test: the collocation group's mean rose from "8.2±7.1" to 17.7±11.3 (P<0.001); the control group's change was not significant (P=0.07).

> "The mean score of pretest in the collocation group was 8.2±7.1, which was increased to 17.7±11.3 after the treatment (P<0.001). Also, in the control group, the mean pretest score was 8.7±6.5, which was increased to 11.1±8.5 after the treatment (P=0.07)."
