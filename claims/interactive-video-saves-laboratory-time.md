### Natarajan Jansirani 2022

Comparative design: experimental group attended six-hour laboratory days after watching the video at home; control group attended eight-hour days. The article reports four hours of face-to-face time was gained.

> "As the nursing students in the experimental group watched the interactive educational videos at home, a total of four hours was gained and reduced from the face-to-face laboratory session."
