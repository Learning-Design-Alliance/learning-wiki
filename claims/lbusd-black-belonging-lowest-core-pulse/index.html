Powell 2024¶

Powell, K. (2024). The center of black student excellence: A place without barriers and the 10 elements that lay its foundation. Long Beach Unified School District. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED663856

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

District-administered 2023-2024 CORE Survey of students and families assessing school climate indicators. The survey "revealed that 65 percent of elementary and 47 percent of secondary Black students and families reported a positive sense of belonging." Percentages are descriptive; no effect sizes printed.

"The survey revealed that 65 percent of elementary and 47 percent of secondary Black students and families reported a positive sense of belonging—which are lower reports than all other groups in grades 4 through 12."

Powell 2024 (2)¶

Powell, K. (2024). The center of black student excellence: A place without barriers and the 10 elements that lay its foundation. Long Beach Unified School District. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED663856

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The 2024 PULSE Survey replicated the belonging pattern and extended it: Black students reported lower scores in "having a sense of belonging, as well as identity, agency, and sense of safety, than all other groups in grades 6 through 12." Descriptive percentages only.

"The 2024 PULSE Survey confirmed these findings, maintaining that Black students reported lower scores in having a sense of belonging, as well as identity, agency, and sense of safety, than all other groups in grades 6 through 12."
