`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis of the 38 SET-CPR residents across the three assessment time points, reported in Results (Table 2). The article reports a statistically significant decline with no printed effect size.

> "From mid-test to post-test, the scores for medical task performance, team behavior performance, patient safety attitude and time to deliver defibrillation showed statistically significant decline in SET-CPR group."
