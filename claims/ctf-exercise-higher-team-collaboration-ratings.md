## Evidence

### Sang-Yoon Chang 2022

Sang-Yoon Chang, Kay Yoon, Simeon Wuthier, and Kelei Zhang. (2022). Capture the Flag for Team Construction in Cybersecurity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quantitative comparison of end-of-course questionnaire means between the 2021 course with CTF (ten students) and the 2020 course without CTF (seven students), with all other course elements identical. The printed means favor the CTF group, e.g., "mean with CTF=5.0, mean without CTF=4.2" for understanding of team processes.

> "Students who experienced the CTF exercise showed higher ratings in their understanding of team processes (mean with CTF=5.0, mean without CTF=4.2), their confidence in team collaboration skills (mean with CTF=4.2, mean without CTF=4.0), the appreciation for team efforts (mean with CTF=4.6, mean without CTF=4.2), and the applicability of course materials (mean with CTF=4.2, mean without CTF=4.0)"
