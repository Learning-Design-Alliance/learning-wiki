## Evidence

### I. Andrews 2018

I. Andrews, K. Nikolopoulos. (2018). Introducing particle physics concepts through visual art. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aad276](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aad276)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-workshop student feedback collected during the pilot implementation phase with approximately 110 KS4 students in the West Midlands. The article reports that "Discussing physics through visual arts, led to an increased interest in physics for four out of five students" and gives the percentages for university study intentions. No statistical tests or effect sizes are reported.

> "Discussing physics through visual arts, led to an increased interest in physics for four out of five students. Approximately 35% of the students found it more likely to study physics at university after this event, while this fraction was 20% among students that were not originally inclined to study physics."
