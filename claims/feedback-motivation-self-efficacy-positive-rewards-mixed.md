### Grønli 2024

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of the four studies measuring feedback effects on motivational aspects during oral reading. It reports self-efficacy-focused feedback "had a positive effect" while error-count feedback and rewards such as stickers showed mixed long-term effects; the review states no clear conclusions can be drawn from the four studies.

> "Feedback focusing on reading self-efficacy had a positive effect (Aro et al., 2018), while negative feedback (e.g., number of errors) and rewards (e.g., stickers) had mixed effects on long-term interest and engagement (Aymett, 2018; Carroll, 2008; Conte & Hintze, 2000; Henze & Williams, 2013)."
