Evidence¶

Pamela Davis-Kean 2021¶

Pamela Davis-Kean, Thurston Domina, Megan Kuhfeld, Alexa Ellis, Elizabeth Gershoff. (2021). It matters how you start: Early numeracy mastery predicts high school math course-taking and college attendance. Infant and Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.2281

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The abstract describes the study's measurement design: mastery of "three numeric competencies" was measured "at 54 months of age" using the Woodcock-Johnson Applied Problems subtest with 1,364 SECCYD children. This establishes the feasibility of assessing these distinct early math skills at preschool age in a longitudinal cohort.

"this study measures children’s mastery of three numeric competencies (counting, concrete representational arithmetic and abstract arithmetic operations) at 54 months of age"
