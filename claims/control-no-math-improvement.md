## Evidence

### Cheng 2014

Cheng, Y.-L., & Mix, K. S. (2014). Spatial Training Improves Children's Mathematics Ability. Journal of Cognition and Development, 15(1), 2-11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-tailed paired t-tests comparing control children's (n = 27) pre- and posttest scores found no significant improvement on any math subskill; only test statistics, no effect sizes, are printed.

> "In contrast, children in the control group failed to show significant improvement on any of the math subskills (missing term: t (26) = 1.19, p =. 13; multi-digit: t (26) = .49, p = .32; number fact: t (26) =. 34, p = .37)."
