# Quantity of evidence generation is higher for standardized patients than for virtual patients but is unrelated to diagnostic accuracy

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C 2021

Fink Maximilian C, Reitmeier Victoria, Stadler Matthias, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Assessment of Diagnostic Competences With Standardized Patients Versus Virtual Patients: Experimental Study in the Context of History Taking. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/21196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired analysis in the 86-student experiment; descriptive means were 29.01 questions for SPs versus 17.34 for VPs, consistent with faster oral questioning than menu selection.

> "H3.2 that students display an increased quantity of evidence generation with SPs than with VPs was supported (1-tailed t69=12.26; P<.001; Cohen d=1.47)."

### Fink Maximilian C 2021 (2)

Fink Maximilian C, Reitmeier Victoria, Stadler Matthias, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Assessment of Diagnostic Competences With Standardized Patients Versus Virtual Patients: Experimental Study in the Context of History Taking. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/21196

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlation analysis found no association (r=0.11; P=.15), and this held for SPs (r=−0.09; P=.76) and VPs (r=−0.10; P=.82) separately; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, in an explorative follow-up analysis, we found no evidence that the quantity of evidence generation was related to diagnostic accuracy (1-tailed r=0.11; P=.15)."
