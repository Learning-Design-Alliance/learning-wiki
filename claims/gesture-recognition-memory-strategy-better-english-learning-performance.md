## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The gesture recognition group achieved significantly better learning performance scores than the control group (ANCOVA F = 6.69, p = 0.012; η² = 0.095, a medium effect). [→ Hsien-Sheng Hsiao 2025](#hsien-sheng-hsiao-2025)

## Evidence

### Hsien-Sheng Hsiao 2025

Hsien-Sheng Hsiao, I-Hsiung Chang, Yi-Hsuan Chen, & Jyun-Chen Chen. (2025). Using gesture recognition with the memory strategy to improve preschoolers’ learning performance, motor skills, and executive function. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 1791–1809. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10471-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest control group study of 67 kindergarten Level 3 preschoolers; ANCOVA with pretest as covariate showed "the EG achieved significantly better scores than the CG" with η² = 0.095, a medium effect.

> "The ANCOVA results showed that the EG achieved significantly better scores than the CG (F = 6.69, p = 0.012 < 0.05). The pretest score as the covariate was 16.07, and the adjusted mean scores were 18.35 for the EG and 17.69 for the CG. Moreover, the η 2 value was provided as a substitute for effect size (η 2 = 0.095)"
