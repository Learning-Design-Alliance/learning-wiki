## Evidence

### Coenen Johan 2021

Coenen Johan, Golsteyn Bart H. H., Stolp Tom, Tempelaar Dirk. (2021). Personality traits and academic performance: Correcting self-assessed traits with vignettes. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0248629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions on grades (Table 6: uncorrected reversed Emotional Stability 0.168*, vignette-corrected baseline 0.065 and extended 0.044, both not significant). The article reports no significant relationship for Emotional Stability after correction.

> "the point estimates of the relationships between these personality traits and grades are much larger if the vignette is not used than if it is used."
