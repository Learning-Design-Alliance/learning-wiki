## Evidence

### Xin Liu 2023

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of pooled PISA 2012 student questionnaire data (29 items on teacher support, learning environment, and learning process). The article reports "Five dimensions were identified in the pooled student responses" with a well-fitting five-factor model, replicated in each country.

> "Five dimensions were identified in the pooled student responses when focusing on mathematics instructional quality: teacher support (TS-p), cognitive activation (CA-p), classroom disciplinary climate (DC-p), classroom management (CM-p), and student-orientated instruction (SO-p)."
