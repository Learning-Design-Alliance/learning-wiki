q2 · i? · design · r2

Efficiency observation from the pipeline applied to 535 quotations from 51 students across three time points. The authors offer this as a rough estimate, noting human labeling sessions "were generally limited to one to two hours due to cognitive fatigue; however, the LLM processed batches of quotes continuously".

"As a rough estimate, the LLM processed approximately two to three quotes in the time it typically took the human researcher to label a single quote."
