### Feigerlova Eva 2024

Feigerlova Eva, Ioan Iulia, Pape Elise, Boursier Caroline, Berguer Marion, Hani Hind, Braun Marc. (2024). Team-based learning (TBL) curriculum combined with video vignettes improves performance of undergraduate medical students on OSCE compared with TBL alone. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05861-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group comparison of the generic skills subscore (physician–patient relationship, care planning, summary of paraclinical test results) in the quasi-experimental posttest-only study of 296 fifth-year medical students. Only printed means and the P value are reported.

> "Students who received TBL with the video vignette curriculum attained a significantly higher mean percentage score in generic skills (42.99%) than students in the comparator group (40.33%) (P = 0.027)"
