# True-score-model correlations among fall, winter, and spring scores exceed 0.90 for almost all course-specific tests, indicating strongly correlated administrations across terms.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Pearson correlations computed from true score model estimates are above 0.90 for almost all tests and are higher than observed-score correlations because they are corrected for attenuation. [→ Thum 2019](#thum-2019)

## Evidence

### Thum 2019

Thum, Y. M., & He, W. (2019). Achievement and growth norms for course-specific MAP® Growth™ Algebra 1, Geometry, and Algebra 2 tests. NWEA Research Report. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/achievement-and-growth-norms-for-course-specific-map-growth-algebra-1-geometry-and-algebra-2-tests/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of observed RIT scores and true score model estimates (Table 3.2) for Algebra 1, Geometry, and Algebra 2. Printed correlation coefficients (r) include r = 0.91 (Algebra 1 fall–winter, true score model) versus r = 0.86 observed, with model-based coefficients "above 0.90 for almost all tests" and corrected for attenuation.

> "the Pearson correlation coefficients computed based on the estimates from the true score models are above 0.90 for almost all tests, suggesting that scores from each administration were strongly correlated"
