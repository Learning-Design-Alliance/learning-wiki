# Suh et al. (2019) found a positive but non-significant effect on course passing rate (effect size 0.80) and a negative non-significant effect on final exam score

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` In a cluster RCT, the intervention group's course passing rate exceeded the laughter/stress comparison group's (64.8 vs 32.8, effect size 0.80, improvement index +29), but was not statistically significant (p = .17). [→ Growth Mindset Interventions 2022](#growth-mindset-interventions-2022)
`q4 i?` The same study's final exam score slightly favored the comparison group (effect size -0.04, improvement index -2, p = .94), a non-significant negative finding. [→ Growth Mindset Interventions 2022 (2)](#growth-mindset-interventions-2022-2)

### Growth Mindset Interventions 2022

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Cluster randomized controlled trial in seven sections of a developmental mathematics course at a public four-year university in Indiana; the printed effect size is 0.80 (improvement index +29, p = .17), and the WWC applied clustering and multiple-comparison corrections, characterizing the effect as potentially positive because the mean effect size is ≥ 0.25 but not statistically significant.
