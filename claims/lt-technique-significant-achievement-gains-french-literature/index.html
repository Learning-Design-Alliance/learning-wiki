Evidence¶

Orprayoon 2014¶

Orprayoon, S. (2014). Effects of Cooperative Learning on Learning Achievement and Group Working Behavior of Junior Students in Modern French Literature Course. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.1, 80-98. https://uncw.edu/jet

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of twelve junior French majors taught 12 topics with the Learning Together technique over 11 weeks; pre-tests and post-tests were administered before and after each topic and analyzed by mean, standard deviation and t-test. The article reports achievement "significantly higher after cooperative learning at the level of 0.01"; no effect size is printed.

"the results revealed that learning achievement of the students was significantly higher after cooperative learning at the level of 0.01, especially the students whose scores of the pre -test were quite low gained the most benefit from Learning Together technique."
