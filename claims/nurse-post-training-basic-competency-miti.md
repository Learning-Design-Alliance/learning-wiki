## Evidence

### Magill Nicholas 2018

Magill Nicholas, Graves Helen, de Zoysa Nicole, Winkley Kirsty, Amiel Stephanie, Shuttlewood Emma, Landau Sabine, Ismail Khalida. (2018). Assessing treatment fidelity and contamination in a cluster randomised controlled trial of motivational interviewing and cognitive behavioural therapy skills in type 2 diabetes. BMC Family Practice, 19, 60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0742-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-training competency assessment of the 11 intervention-arm nurses by the MITI-trained nurse trainer, one tape recording per nurse, rated on MITI and BECCI scales. Scores were "median MITI MI-Adherence was 86.2%" and mean Empathy 4.09; BECCI Practitioner Score was 2.78 (SD 0.50).

> "After training, median MITI MI-Adherence was 86.2% (IQR 76.9–100%) and mean MITI Empathy was 4.09 (SD 1.04)."
