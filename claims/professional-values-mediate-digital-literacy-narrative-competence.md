### Meng Ya 2026

Meng Ya, Jia Xin, Hou Tingting, Deng Yufang, Song Jian. (2026). The mediating effects of self-directed learning and nursing professional values on the relationship between digital literacy and narrative competence among nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08757-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM parameter estimates from the 1,025-student survey (Table 4). Digital literacy predicted nursing professional values at "β = 0.292, P < 0.001"; professional values predicted narrative competence at β = 0.266, supporting the mediated pathway.

> "Notably, digital literacy also significantly predicted nursing professional values (β = 0.292, P < 0.001)."

### Meng Ya 2026 (2)

The mediating effects of self-directed learning and nursing professional values on the relationship between digital literacy and narrative competence among nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08757-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Methodological detail of the mediation analysis: a "bootstrap method with 5,000 resamples" with 95% CIs judged indirect-effect significance. Table 5 reports a standardized indirect effect of digital literacy on narrative competence of 0.670 (0.562–0.802).

> "To test for mediation effect testing, the bootstrap method with 5,000 resamples was adopted, and the 95% confidence interval (CI) was used to judge the significance of indirect effects."
