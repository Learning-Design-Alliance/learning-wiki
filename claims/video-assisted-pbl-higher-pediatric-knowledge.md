## Evidence

### Goda Fatma Alm Eldeen 2026

Goda Fatma Alm Eldeen, Sabaq Amal Gharib, Abdullah Reda Mohamed. (2026). Effect of traditional versus video-assisted problem-based learning on pediatric nursing students' academic self-efficacy and self-directed learning: implications for pediatric patients care. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10037-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled trial of 150 pediatric nursing students reported post-intervention ANOVA results showing the video-assisted PBL group achieved the highest post-intervention mean scores in total knowledge (77.30 ± 4.42), followed by traditional PBL and control groups.

> "The video-assisted PBL group achieved the highest post-intervention mean scores in total knowledge (77.30 ± 4.42), academic self-efficacy (72.16 ± 6.18), and self-directed learning readiness (118.38 ± 3.76), followed by the traditional PBL group"
