## Evidence

### Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin 1996

Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin. (1996). One Hundred Years of Forgetting: A Quantitative Description of Retention. Psychological Review. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.103.4.734

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative review of early experimental work, citing Luh (1922), who had participants learn word lists to compare anticipation, relearning, written reproduction, recognition, and reconstruction. The article concludes "different measures of relearning produce the same retention function, but this function is different from that produced by recall."

> "Relearning, measured in percent savings, fell much less rapidly at longer time intervals than the other measures, resulting in a differently shaped plot."
