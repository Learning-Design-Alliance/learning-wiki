# Synchronous communication is the most common collaboration support integrated in educational VRLs, and few labs offer none

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the reviewed corpus, synchronous communication tools are the most frequent form of communication support in educational VRLs, and only 5 publications provide no communication tools at all. [→ Kurtz 2025](#kurtz-2025)

## Evidence

### Kurtz 2025

Kurtz, M., Benabbou, A., Pons, C., & Broisin, J. (2025). Collaboration in virtual and remote laboratories for education: A systematic literature review. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09454-7

`q2 · i?`

Systematic review coding of communication tools across the 62 analysed publications. Synchronous communication occurred in 33 publications, both modes in 15, and "synchronous communication is the most frequent (n = 33)". Five publications provide no communication tools and two are asynchronous-only.

> "Communication modalities in VRL environments, as presented in Table 5, reveal that synchronous communication is the most frequent (n = 33), followed by VRLs that support both synchronous and asynchronous modes (n = 15)."
