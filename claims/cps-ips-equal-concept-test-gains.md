## Evidence

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the two-factor ANCOVA on posttest ECT scores with pretest covariate, "the type of problem-solving did not have a statistically significant effect on the students’ posttest performance in the ECT"; equivalence was not tested. Both treatment groups showed significant pretest-to-posttest ECT gains.

> "the type of problem-solving did not have a statistically significant effect on the students’ posttest performance in the ECT (F = 0.10, p = 0.753)"

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023 (2)

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same ANCOVA found achievement level significant: "partial η2 = 0.157, effect size = large". High-achievers significantly outperformed low-achievers in both the IPS and CPS groups, so the achievement gap on concepts persisted under both conditions.

> "the level of achievement did lead to a statistically significant effect on the students’ posttest performance in the ECT (F = 19.38, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.157, effect size = large)"
