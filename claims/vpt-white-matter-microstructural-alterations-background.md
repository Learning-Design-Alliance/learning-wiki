Background statement attributed to prior DWI studies: VPT infants show "reduced fibre density and fractional anisotropy (FA) and increased radial diffusivity (RD) and mean diffusivity (MD)" compared with FT infants, likely reflecting delayed brain development. The article offers this as cited background, not a new analysis.
