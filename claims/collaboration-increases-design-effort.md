`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney tests showed significant differences in design time and actions between single teachers and collaborative groups. "All of these differences indicate that collabora- tion increased design efforts significantly, especially collaboration between teachers and project members." Design time averaged 433 minutes for teacher-project member teams versus 251 minutes for single teachers.
