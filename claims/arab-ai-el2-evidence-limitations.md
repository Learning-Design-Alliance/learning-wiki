`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's methodological critique of its own evidence base: perception-heavy designs dominate, sample sizes ranged from 20 (Iraqi Grammarly study) to 350 (Syrian ChatGPT study), and limited reporting on AI versions constrains replicability and precise evaluation.

> "First, the majority of studies relied heavily on self -reported perceptions and questionnaire -based data, with relatively few employing experimental or quasi -experimental designs capable of measuring actual learning gains."
