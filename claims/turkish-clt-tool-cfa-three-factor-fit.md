## Evidence

### Yıldız Çelik Hilal 2025

Yıldız Çelik Hilal, Uğur Esra. (2025). Turkish validity and reliability study of the simulation self-report cognitive load measurement tool 2.0. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07504-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of responses from 101 second-year nursing students supported the adapted tool's three-factor internal structure; the article reports χ2/df = 1.423, RMSEA = 0.0643, CFI = 0.950, and that 'the standardized root mean square residual (SRMR) was 0.084'.

> "The fit criterion of the model was χ2/df = 1.423, the root mean square error of approximation (RMSEA) was 0.0643, the comparative fit index (CFI) was 0.950, and the standardized root mean square residual (SRMR) was 0.084 (Table 3)."
