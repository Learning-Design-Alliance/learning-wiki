`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative data on student race/ethnicity and school SRO status (Figure 4). Although SRO presence declined for all groups, "large racialized differences emerged during implementation of the policy".

> "In 2022–23, 63% of Black high school students had at least one SRO in their school, compared with 29% each for Latinx and White students, 22% for Asian/Pacific Islander students and 27% of students in additional race/ethnicity groups."
