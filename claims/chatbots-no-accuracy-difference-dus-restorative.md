## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 188 DUS restorative dentistry MCQs, the eight chatbots did not differ significantly in overall accuracy (p = 0.18), though numerical accuracy ranged from 86.70% (DeepSeek) to 96.28% (Gemini Advanced). [→ Haberal Merve 2026](#haberal-merve-2026)

## Evidence

### Haberal Merve 2026

Haberal Merve, Hançerlioğulları Dilek. (2026). Can artificial intelligence chatbots think like dentists? A comparative analysis based on dental specialty examination questions in restorative dentistry. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07612-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In silico cross-sectional comparison of eight chatbots each answering 188 restorative dentistry MCQs from 19 DUS sessions, scored against official ÖSYM answer keys by two blinded reviewers. The study reports "did not differ significantly in terms of response accuracy (p = 0.18)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The analysis of this study indicated that the eight chatbots did not differ significantly in terms of response accuracy (p = 0.18)."
