Evidence¶

Erlidawati & Syarfuni 2018¶

Erlidawati & Syarfuni. (2018). The Effect of Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition on Reading Comprehension of IAIN Lhokseumawe, Indonesia. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.4p.153

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of two third-semester units at IAIN Lhokseumawe (experimental n = 31, control n = 32), analyzed with a Student t-test after homogeneity and normality checks. The t-test yielded t = 2.210 with significance 0.031, so "the null hypothesis was rejected and alternative hypothesis was accepted". No effect size is printed.

"Based on the table above it can be seen that tscore assumed that score variance is not the same or not homogenous was 2.210 and score significant was 0.031. The score significant ≤ 0.05, so the null hypothesis was rejected and alternative hypothesis was accepted."
