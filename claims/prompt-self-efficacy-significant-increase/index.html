Evidence¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian 2026¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian. (2026). Scaffolding Critical Engagement with GenAI: Transforming Ethnic Minority Preparatory Students’ Collaborative Discourse in Prompt Engineering Tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28630

q2 · i? · design · r2

Paired-samples t-test on an eight-item 5-point prompt self-efficacy scale administered before and after the three-week course; complete data were available for 71 of 78 students. The article reports the significant increase but prints no effect size, so no magnitude is asserted.

"The paired-samplest-test (t= 3.729,p<.001) indicates that students’ perceived prompt self-efficacy was significantly higher after the intervention than before it, suggesting a meaningful improvement in their confidence in crafting prompts."
