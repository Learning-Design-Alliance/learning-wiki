## Evidence

### Guo 2025

Guo, J.; Bai, H.; Long, X.; Su, X.; Pang, W. (2025). The process of tool innovation in young children: An attempt to bridge the Gestalt and the perception-action theories. Learning and Instruction, 99, article 102182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102182

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the nine tasks administered to 139 children aged 3–6 from three kindergartens. The authors report that "Task success rates ranged from 33 % to 94 %" and ideal manipulation rates from 24% to 91%, confirming the intended difficulty variation.

> "As intended, these tasks showed nicely varied difficulty levels: Task success rates ranged from 33 % to 94 %, and the ideal manipulation rates ranged from 24 % to 91 %."
