## Evidence

### Xinyue Wang 2024

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on gesture counts across BOTH, BOTH/2 and ONE conditions in the 27-dyad experiment (Fig. 3b). The article interprets: "when only one person could gesture, they tended to reduce the use of interactive gestures and compensate by using isolated, ﬂuid gestures". No effect sizes printed for this contrast.

> "Results showed that compared to the‘BOTH/2’condition, signiﬁcantly moreﬂuid gestures were used under the‘ONE’condition, while signiﬁcantly fewer interactive gestures were found (Fig.3b, Supplementary Table 1 and 2)."
