## Evidence

### Langkilde Fredrik 2024

Langkilde Fredrik, Masaba Patrick, Edenbrandt Lars, Gren Magnus, Halil Airin, Hellström Mikael, Larsson Måns, Naeem Ameer Ali, Wallström Jonas, Maier Stephan E., Jäderling Fredrik. (2024). Manual prostate MRI segmentation by readers with different experience: a study of the learning progress. European Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-023-10515-4

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same 100-scan segmentation study, four medical-student novices (three completing only rounds 1–3) segmented against the expert reference with feedback after each round. The article reports the rising round-by-round percentages as "indicating learning progress"; novices totaled 194/250 (77.6%) accurate segmentations.

> "In round 1, the novices had 19/40 (47.5%) accurate segmentations, in round 2 41/60 (68.3%), and in round 3 84/100 (84.0%) indicating learning progress which also can be appreciated in the figures."
