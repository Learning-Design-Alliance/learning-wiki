`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of interaction logs aligned to exam intervals. The article reports r = −.31 for chatbot and r = −.22 for proof-review usage among students with lower Midterm 2 scores.

> "Similarly, students with lower Midterm 2 scores used both tools more frequently during the subsequent interval (chatbot: r = −.31, p < .001; proof-review: r = −.22, p = .008)."
