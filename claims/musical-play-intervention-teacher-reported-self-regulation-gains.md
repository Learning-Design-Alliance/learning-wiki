# Musical play intervention produced a significantly greater increase in teacher-reported self-regulation than usual music lessons in 6-year-olds

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The intervention group showed a significantly greater increase in teacher-reported self-regulation between pre- and post-tests than the control group (η²p = .58). [→ Zachariou 2023](#zachariou-2023)

## Evidence

### Zachariou 2023

Zachariou, A., Bonneville-Roussy, A., Hargreaves, D., & Neokleous, R. (2023). Exploring the effects of a musical play intervention on young children's self-regulation and metacognition. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09342-1

`q3 · i3`

Quasi-experimental pre/post control-group study of 98 children aged 6 in Cypriot primary schools; repeated-measures ANCOVA on CHILD 3–5 teacher checklist controlling for age, gender and SES found a significant group × time interaction, "F (1, 93) = 127.09, p < .001, η2p =.58".

> "we found a significant interaction effect between group and time, with the intervention group showing a significantly greater increase in teacher-reported self-regulation between pre- and post-tests, F (1, 93) = 127.09, p < .001, η2p =.58"
