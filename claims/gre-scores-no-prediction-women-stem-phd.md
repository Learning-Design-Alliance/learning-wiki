## Evidence

### Petersen Sandra L. 2018

Petersen Sandra L., Erenrich Evelyn S., Levine Dovev L., Vigoreaux Jim, Gile Krista. (2018). Multi-institutional study of GRE scores as predictors of STEM PhD degree completion: GRE gets a low mark. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206570

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA and logistic regression on 767 women in the four-institution cohort (Table 2). Women who completed had mean GRE V 534.2 and GRE Q 671.6 versus 532.5 and 666.1 for women who did not complete; the article states "GRE scores were not associated with PhD completion for women". No effect size printed.

> "Our two-way ANOVA verified that GRE scores were not associated with PhD completion for women"
