### Alhadithi Ahmed 2025

Alhadithi Ahmed, Almansour Mohammed. (2025). Leadership and management competency frameworks in undergraduate medical education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08489-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic search of PubMed, Embase, and ERIC up to February 2025, screened by two independent reviewers per PRISMA-ScR. The review reports that "16 met the inclusion criteria and were included in the final analysis" from 1,547 records after duplicate removal.

> "From an initial pool of 2,422 articles retrieved through database searches and an additional 24 identified through manual searching, 1,547 records remained after the removal of duplicates (n = 899). Title and abstract screening led to the full-text assessment of 31 publications, of which 16 met the inclusion criteria and were included in the final analysis."
