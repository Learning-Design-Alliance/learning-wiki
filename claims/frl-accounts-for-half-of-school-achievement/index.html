Andy Hegedus 2018¶

Andy Hegedus, Ed.D. (2018). Evaluating the Relationships between Poverty and School Performance. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research-data-galleries/poverty-and-school-performance

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of about 1,500 randomly sampled NWEA MAP Growth schools linked to NCES demographics, plotting median achievement percentile against school %FRL (Figure 2.1). The analysis found "about 50% of a school’s achievement is accounted for by the percentage of students eligible for FRL"; best-fit lines put typical median achievement at the 29th percentile in all-FRL schools and the 73rd percentile in no-FRL schools.

"The analysis determined that about 50% of a school’s achievement is accounted for by the percentage of students eligible for FRL in a school, as shown in Figure 2.1."
