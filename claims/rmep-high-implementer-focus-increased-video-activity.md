## Evidence

### Nagle 2016

Nagle, K., Pratt-Williams, J., Schmidt, R., Swantek, C., Lyulchenko, M., & McGhee, R. (2016). Evaluation of the Rural Math Excel Partnership Project: Final Report. SRI International. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/projects/other/i3_ruralmath.asp

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

This finding comes from the implementation study's observation of teacher video assignment behavior through MARi platform usage data. In fall 2014, 61 percent of participating math teachers had assigned no videos, whereas by Year 3 the majority of high-implementing teachers assigned at least 10 videos.

> "Once the RMEP staff decided to focus resources in fall 2015 on high-implementing teachers, SRI evaluators began to see more videos being assigned by teachers and completed by students."
