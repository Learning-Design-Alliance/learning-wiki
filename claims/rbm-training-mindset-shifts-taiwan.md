## Evidence

### McWilliam R. A. 2020

McWilliam R. A., Boavida Tânia, Bull Kerry, Cañadas Margarita, Hwang Ai-Wen, Józefacka Natalia, Lim Hong Huay, Pedernera Marisú, Sergnese Tamara, Woodward Julia. (2020). The Routines-Based Model Internationally Implemented. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17228308

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Training-outcome study of 218 professionals attending RBM training in Taiwan, based on McWilliam's 12 mental shifts and 10 mistakes. Job-embedded professional development correlated with mindset shifts ("r = 0.56–0.82"); high interrater agreement on the mental-shifts scale was reported.

> "Professionals with less experience shifted their mindsets more than did those with more experience (p < 0.05) [48]. Those who had ever attended any presentations on the RBM or who had read anything about the model were more aware of the practices at pretest (p < 0.05) compared to those who had not. Those who received job-embedded professional development were more likely to have shifted mindsets compared to those who received other forms of professional development (r = 0.56–0.82)."
