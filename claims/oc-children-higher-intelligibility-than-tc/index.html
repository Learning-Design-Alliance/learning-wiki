Evidence¶

Lorin Lachs 2001¶

Lorin Lachs, David B. Pisoni, and Karen Iler Kirk. (2001). Use of Audiovisual Information in Speech Perception by Prelingually Deaf Children with Cochlear Implants. Ear Hear. 2001 June ; 22(3): 236–251. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Independent-samples t-test comparing speech intelligibility of 11 children in Oral Communication settings versus 12 in Total Communication settings. The article reports OC children scored "significantly higher than the scores for TC children, t(21) = 2.99, p = 0.04." No effect size was printed.

"A t-test for independent samples confirmed the finding that speech intelligibility scores for OC children were significantly higher than the scores for TC children, t(21) = 2.99, p = 0.04."
