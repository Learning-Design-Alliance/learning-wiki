### Kozak Agnessa 2017

Kozak Agnessa, Freitag Sonja, Nienhaus Albert. (2017). Evaluation of a Training Program to Reduce Stressful Trunk Postures in the Nursing Professions: A Pilot Study. Annals of Work Exposures and Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/annweh/wxw002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standardized telephone interviews with 18 closed or open questions 2 weeks after the seminar, with a 96% participation rate (N = 22). The article reports "all participants reported that they were more conscious of adopting awkward postures during work" and 96% attended more to bed height; 50% admitted not adjusting the bed properly in some stressful situations.
