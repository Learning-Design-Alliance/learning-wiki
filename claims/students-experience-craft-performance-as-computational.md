## Evidence

### Anna Keune 2022

Anna Keune (2022). Material syntonicity: Examining computational performance and its materiality through weaving and sewing crafts. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2022.2100704

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured crafting interviews with each student at least once per unit. One participant stated "I did loops," and a fabric manipulation student said it was "almost like coding your hands to do something rather than an actual computer," linking bodily performance to computational concepts.

> "When asked, students articulated intersections of weaving and computation, providing pointers toward whether and how weaving can become a context for computational learning. For example, one of the participants stated “I did loops,” directly connecting their crafting with the computational concept of loops."
