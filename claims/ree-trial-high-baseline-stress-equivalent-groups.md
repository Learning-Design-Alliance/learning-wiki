## Evidence

### Ilechukwu LC 2021

Ilechukwu LC, Egenti NT, Aloh HE, Uwakwe RC, Obande-Ogbuinya N, Eke CL, Kalu IA, Ejionueme LK, Iremeka FU. (2021). Rational emotive education for reducing stress of undergraduate students of religious education program: An experimental study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000026177

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline (Time 1) between-group comparison in Table 2 from the randomized controlled trial, measured with the Perceived Stress Scale-10 (study Cronbach α of 0.87). Both group means fell in the instrument's high range (27–40); the difference was non-significant and equivalence was not formally tested.

> "the Religious Education students perceived stress level is very high with no significant difference recorded between the treatment (mean = 36.14 ± 3.72) and control groups (mean = 36.85 ± 3.92); t(1,148) = –1.133, P = .259."
