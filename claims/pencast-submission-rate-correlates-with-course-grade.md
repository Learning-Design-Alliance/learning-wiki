## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nandana Weliweriya 2017

Nandana Weliweriya, Eleanor C Sayre, Dean A Zollman. (2017). The Effectiveness of “Pencasts” in Physics Courses. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.09349](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.09349)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of one twenty-student fall 2015 Electromagnetism I section: final grades (with pencast contribution excluded) compared across low and high pencast-submission clumps using a Wilcoxon rank sum test, "𝑝<.0001". The relationship is correlational; the authors themselves "conclude that the students’ submission of more pencasts correlates well".

> "These clumps are different (𝑝<.0001 by Wilcoxon rank sum test), and the high-submitters earned better grades than the low-submitters.  We conclude that the students’ submission of more pencasts correlates well with their overall performance in the course."
