## Evidence

### Yilmaz Merve 2026

Yilmaz Merve, Korkmaz Fatos. (2026). Newly Graduated Nurses' Professional Competency and Caring Perception: A Cross‐Sectional Correlational Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70614

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis among 249 newly graduated nurses. The strongest correlation was between CINS Caring and CBI-30 Respectful Deference to Others (r = 0.572); the weakest was between CINS Clinical Biomedical Science and CBI-30 Respectful Deference to Others (r = 0.364).

> "The Spearman correlation test revealed a direct and significant relationship between the total scores of the Competency Inventory for Nursing Students (CINS) and the Caring Behaviors Inventory‐30 (CBI‐30) (r = 0.653, p < 0.01), as well as across all sub‐dimensions (r = 0.364–0.572, p < 0.01)."
