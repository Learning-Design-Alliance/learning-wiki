## Evidence

### Lei Bao and Edward F. Redish 2002

Lei Bao and Edward F. Redish. (2002). Model Analysis: Assessing the Dynamics of Student Learning. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207069](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207069)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Steinberg and Sabella asked engineering physics students at the University of Maryland two equivalent questions on Newton's first law, one in physics terms and one in everyday speech. "On the physics-like problem, 90%" answered correctly versus only 54% on the everyday version, with 36% choosing an answer reflecting a common incorrect model.

> "On the physics-like problem, 90% of the students gave the correct answer that the normal force on the sphere is equal to the downward force due to gravity. On the everyday problem, only 54% chose the correct answer"
