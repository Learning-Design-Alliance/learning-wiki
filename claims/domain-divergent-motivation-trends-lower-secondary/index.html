Evidence¶

Anna Widlund 2023¶

Anna Widlund, Markku Niemivirta, Heta Tuominen, Johan Korhonen. (2023). Growth Trajectories of Self-Concept and Interest in Mathematics and Language – Individual Differences and Cross-Domain Relations. Preprint version 1. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5563-5044

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Combined growth model of the 612-student sample (quadratic factors for math, linear for L1) fit well (CFI = .986, RMSEA = .037); "significant declines (linear slopes) occurred in both math and L1 self-concept" with a positive math quadratic trend.

"The growth trajectories revealed that significant declines (linear slopes) occurred in both math and L1 self-concept from seventh to ninth grade, although the quadratic trend of math self-concept was positive, indicating that the negative trend subsided towards the end of lower secondary school."
