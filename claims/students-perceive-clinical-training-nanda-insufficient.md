`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 29 fourth-year nursing students from eight Turkish public universities, analysed with Braun and Clarke's six-phase thematic analysis. A student participant stated that "theoretical instruction covers NANDA‐I diagnoses comprehensively, we lack opportunities to apply them in clinical settings."
