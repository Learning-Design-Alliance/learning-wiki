`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Internal mini meta-analysis combining the five experiments' estimates, conducted following Goh et al. recommendations to synthesize the article's own studies. The pooled estimate was ES = 0.00 with 95% CI [-0.10, 0.09], showing the intervention "did not produce the expected positive effects on learning".

> "Internal meta-analytic estimates (ES = 0.00, 95% CI [-0.10, 0.09]) showed that, overall, the jigsaw intervention did not produce the expected positive effects on learning."
