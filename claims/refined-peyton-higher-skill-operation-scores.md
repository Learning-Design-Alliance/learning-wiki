## Evidence

### Zhang Dandan 2024

Zhang Dandan, Wang Xiaoxue, Chen Xueting, Lou Yingze, Zhou Xin, Zhang Kai. (2024). Optimizing resident training in obstetrics and gynecology: a new perspective on the refined Peyton four-step teaching method. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05851-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of 57 RP residents (2022 intake) with 59 TTM residents (2021 intake) at a single hospital, using an eight-station OSCE with two examiners per station. The article reports "84.60 ± 5.65 for RP group vs. 77.05 ± 5.39 for TTM group, P < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

> "However, the RP group demonstrated superior performance in skill operation assessments (84.60 ± 5.65 for RP group vs. 77.05 ± 5.39 for TTM group, P < 0.001), indicating enhanced practical skill acquisition through the refined Peyton method."
