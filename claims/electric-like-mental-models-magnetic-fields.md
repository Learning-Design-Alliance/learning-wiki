The author reports these student difficulties citing the physics-education research literature (references 5–8); the studies themselves are not described in this article. The author argues a clear geometrical distinction between electric and magnetic field descriptions could make such confusions less likely.

> "describing the magnetic field in the same vector language as the electric field has its own costs. Students very often analyze magnetic field problems us- ing electric-like mental models (e.g. treating north/south poles like positive/negative charges)."
