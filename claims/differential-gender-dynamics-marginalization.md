## Evidence

### Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). The impact of perceived recognition by physics instructors on women's self-efficacy and interest. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of the 38 interviews (14 of 38 students). Students described TAs brushing off their questions while answering men's, instructors giving more detailed answers to male students, overexplaining to women, and office hours dominated by male students' questions.

> "In our analysis, 37% of the interviewed female students reported experiences that were coded under the subtheme feeling marginalized due to differential gender dynamics, which includes two codes: differential treatment of female and male students and letting men dominate the class."
