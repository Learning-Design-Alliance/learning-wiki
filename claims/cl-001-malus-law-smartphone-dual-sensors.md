## Evidence

### Martin Monteiro 2019

Martin Monteiro, Cecilia Stari, Cecilia Cabeza, and Arturo C. Martí (2019). Los sensores basados en dispositivos micromecánicos: laboratorios móviles al servicio de la enseñanza de las ciencias experimentales. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1901.05079](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1901.05079)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Optics demonstration reported in §4.1 and Fig. 3: polarizer film (obtained from an old calculator) is placed over the device while an LCD monitor provides polarized light, and light and inclination data are recorded with a suitable app as the phone is rotated. The article concludes that 'es posible veriﬁcar la ley de Malus' this way, reporting excellent agreement between measured intensity and the expected curve.
