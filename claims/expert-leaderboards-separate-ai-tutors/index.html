q2 · i? · causal · r2

Second study: 51 expert human tutors completed 2,028 pairwise reviews of AI lesson plans and practice problems based on 381 student pre-tests; correlations with student flag rates were "Spearmanρ=0.71 and0.77". Correlational, exploratory; flags record disputes, not verified errors.

"Interestingly, AI tutors scoring highly on practice problem design (Figure3B), i.e., AI tutors preferred by experts for practice-problem creation, also tended to receive fewer student answer disputes in Quantitative and Combined (Spearmanρ=0.71 and0.77;p=.035andp=.033; Appendix F.2)."
