### Rocic Petra 2024

Rocic Petra, Garrison Ryan, Stitle Kyle, Reynolds Austin, Andrews-Dickert Rebecca. (2024). Third-year medical students’ perceptions of confidence and readiness to perform EFAST after training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06513-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous Qualtrics survey of curriculum participants one year after training; 21 of 69 (30.4%) responded. Descriptive Likert results show "mean values from 4.24–4.59" for perceived knowledge, confidence, and likelihood outcomes (Fig. 2).
