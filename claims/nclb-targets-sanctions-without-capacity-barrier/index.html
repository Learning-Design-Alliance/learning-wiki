The author's retrospective assessment, stated in the Executive Summary of this policy report, drawing on his career observing the NCLB era. It identifies the accountability-without-capacity imbalance as the barrier, with no effect size reported.

"A key barrier to the implementation of the new standards during the No Child Left Behind era was a focus on targets and sanctions without attention to educator capacity-building or alignment among all the necessary components of reform."
