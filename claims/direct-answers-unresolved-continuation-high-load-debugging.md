## Evidence

### Mohammad Fahim Abrar 2026

Mohammad Fahim Abrar, Shyala Sharmin, and Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2026). When LLM Tutoring Responses Work: Evidence from Student Programming Conversations. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.09919](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.09919)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two Table 1 Fisher's exact comparisons (both passing FDR correction) show direct answers followed by more unresolved continuation in demanding debugging contexts; the high-load row reports n present 520, n absent 1200, pFDR<.001.

> "In high-confusion debugging contexts, direct-answer responses had an unresolved-continuation rate of 15.0%, compared with 8.3% when direct-answer responses were absent. In high-cognitive-load debugging contexts, direct-answer responses had an unresolved-continuation rate of 15.4%, compared with 8.3% when direct-answer responses were absent."
