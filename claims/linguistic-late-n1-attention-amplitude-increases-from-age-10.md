## Evidence

### Karns Christina M. 2015

Karns Christina M., Isbell Elif, Giuliano Ryan J., Neville Helen J. (2015). Auditory attention in childhood and adolescence: An event-related potential study of spatial selective attention to one of two simultaneous stories. Developmental cognitive neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.03.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the dichotic story ERP study, linguistic-probe late-N1 amplitude showed significant attention modulation at every age from 10 years onward: "p < .001" at 10 years, an Attention x Hemisphere interaction at 13 (F(1,17) = 14.8, p = .001), and p < .05 at 16 and in adults. No late-N1 was present in 3–5-year-olds.

> "By age 10 years, attention robustly increased the late-N1 amplitude (p < .001). At age 13 years, attention interacted with hemisphere (Attention x Hemisphere: F(1,17) = 14.8, p = .001). By age 16 years, attention increased the late-N1 amplitude (p < .05), as it did with adults (p < 0.05)."
