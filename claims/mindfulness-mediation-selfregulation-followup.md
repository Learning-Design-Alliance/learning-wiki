## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-step mediation analysis (Bühner & Ziegler procedure) regressing follow-up outcomes on condition and post-test mindfulness change. For ability to stop ruminating, the indirect effect via mindful acceptance accounted for 38% of variance (adjusted r2, F(2,25)=9.55) and via mindful presence 31% (F(2,25)=7.00).

> "Of the eight qualifying outcome variables, six were fully mediated by changes in one or both mindfulness scales at post -test"
