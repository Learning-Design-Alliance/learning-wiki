Kruskal–Wallis testing of estimated blood loss across the three groups (means 207/185/183 mL) was non-significant; transfusion occurred in 3 of 90 patients and no case converted to open surgery in the first 90-case series.

> "Although a decrease in blood loss was observed over time, it did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.102). The need for transfusion remained minimal (3.3%)."
