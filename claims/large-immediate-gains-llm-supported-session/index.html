Evidence¶

Vangelis Tsiligkiris 2026¶

Vangelis Tsiligkiris. (2026). What students ask matters: LLM interaction depth, task quality, and immediate recall in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00617-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Single-group pre-post study of 22 postgraduate business students in a 90-minute supervised session with a 10-minute pre-test, 60-minute LLM-assisted task, and 10-minute post-test. The paired t-test showed "a large improvement from pre- to post-test" with dz = 2.12; a Wilcoxon signed-rank test corroborated the pattern.

"A paired t-test showed a large improvement from pre- to post-test, t(21) = 9.93, p < .001, Cohen’s dz = 2.12 (95% CI [1.54, 2.70])."
