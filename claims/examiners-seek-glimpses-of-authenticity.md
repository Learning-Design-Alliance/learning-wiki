## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2022

Hyde Sarah, Fessey Christine, Boursicot Katharine, MacKenzie Rhoda, McGrath Deirdre. (2022). OSCE rater cognition – an international multi-centre qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03077-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Second theme ('looking for glimpses of truth') from thematic analysis of the 31 think-aloud interviews. Examiners described prioritising rapport, technical fluency or perceived safety, sometimes overriding the marking system for candidates they judged genuine.

> "The firmly held, but previously not fully examined or expressed, belief of raters that most aspects of an OSCE are 'fake' leads examiners to seek out glimpses of a student's true ability."
