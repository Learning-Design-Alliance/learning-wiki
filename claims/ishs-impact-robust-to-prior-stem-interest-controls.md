## Evidence

### Means 2018

Means, Wang, Wei, Iwatani, & Peters. (2018). Broadening Participation in STEM College Majors: Effects of Attending a STEM-Focused High School. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418806305

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analysis using the Class of 2017 cohort surveyed as freshmen and seniors, adding middle school STEM interest and activity scales to the impact model (Table 7, model B). The printed odds ratio of 1.34 is a small effect; the same six Grade 12 outcomes remained significant.

> "For interest in a STEm career at the end of high school, for example, comparing model B with model A reveals that the log odds for the impact of ISHS attendance changed only slightly from .31 (with an odds ratio of 1.38) to .28 (odds ratio of 1.34), both statistically significant at the p < .01 level."
