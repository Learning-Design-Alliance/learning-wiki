Descriptive analysis of the 207-teacher sample found "mastery, relational, and ability-approach goals were endorsed at similarly high levels." The discussion notes this differs from Western contexts where mastery goals are typically most strongly endorsed, and suggests possible cultural response tendencies such as avoidance of extreme responses.

> "The mean scores for the goals indicated that mastery, relational, and ability-approach goals were endorsed at similarly high levels, with only minor differences among them."
