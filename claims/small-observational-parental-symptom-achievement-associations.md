Linear regression in the total MoBa sample (observational cohort analysis). The article reports standardised βs from −0.016 to −0.057, i.e. "an increase of one standard deviation in the parental symptom score is associated with a decrease of 0.02 SD of the child’s standardised test score, and on maximum 0.06 SD."

> "Parental anxiety and depression, ADHD and eating disorders symptoms are significantly negatively associated with scores in mathematics and reading tests, with standardised ßs from − 0.016 for anxiety and depression symptoms at age 8 with reading (SE = 0.00) to −0.057 for eating disorders at age 8 with maths (SE = 0.00)."
