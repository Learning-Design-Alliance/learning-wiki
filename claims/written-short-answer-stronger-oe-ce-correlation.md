## Evidence

### Breuer Sonja 2023

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analyses B found "larger correlations between scores from OE and CE assessments" for written short answer than other OE formats.

> "yielded a significant overall moderating effect of the type of OE format, with larger correlations between scores from OE and CE assessments found when written short answer items were used rather than other OE formats"

### Breuer Sonja 2023 (2)

Breuer Sonja, Scherndl Thomas, Ortner Tuulia M. (2023). Effects of response format on achievement and aptitude assessment results: multi-level random effects meta-analyses. Royal Society Open Science. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220456

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analyses A "revealed no significant moderating effect of the type of OE format" on mean differences; equivalence not tested.

> "Further analyses with the aggregated OE format categories ( written short answer versus other OE types ) revealed no significant moderating effect of the type of OE format overall or for any of the individual cognitive ability categories"
