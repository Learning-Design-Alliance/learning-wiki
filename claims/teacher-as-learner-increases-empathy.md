### Thorndike 1922

Thorndike, E. L., & Upton, C. B. (1922). An Experiment in Learning an Abstract Subject. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 13(6). https://doi.org/10.1037/h0065229

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' opinion, based on answers to end-of-week questions, notes on learning, written work, and the test: the experiment increases "appreciation of pupils' difficulties and sympathy with their efforts." No measure of empathy change was taken.

> "The experiment seems a useful one as a means of increasing the subject's appreciation of pupils' difficulties and sympathy with their efforts, and as a means of increasing the experimenter's knowledge of learning."
