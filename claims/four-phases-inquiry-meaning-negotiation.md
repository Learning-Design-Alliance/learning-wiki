Qualitative longitudinal study of the first cohort of four teachers using lesson-sharing, periodic reflections, a scenario-based scientific inquiry survey, and videos, coded a-priori against NSES inquiry elements plus open coding. The analysis "yielded four phases of meaning negotiation." No effect size is printed.
