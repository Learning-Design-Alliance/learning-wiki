Evidence¶

Pace and Mellard 2016¶

Jesse R. Pace and Daryl F. Mellard. (2016). Reading Achievement and Reading Efficacy Changes for Middle School Students With Disabilities Through Blended Learning Instruction. Journal of Special Education Technology. https://doi.org/10.1177/0162643416660837

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA using percent-complete as covariate to test whether dosage of BELA activities predicted change in MAP percentile ranks. The authors note "dosage did not contribute to a significant improvement" and suggest low variability in completion may explain the null result.
