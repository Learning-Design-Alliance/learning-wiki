### Wilton Mike 2021

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate regression of CHEM 1A final grades (GPA points) on academic habit complexity ability estimates (Table 4): coefficient 0.176, SE 0.057, t = 3.107, p = 0.002. The article reports the relationship as "positively related" with no standardized effect size.

> "Additionally, a multivariate regression provided evidence that academic habit complexity and grades in CHEM 1A were positively related: students earned an average of half a letter grade higher in chemistry for every four academic habits they reported, even after controlling for SAT math scores, BIOME enrollment, and high school GPA"
