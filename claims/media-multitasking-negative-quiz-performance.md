# Media multitasking, but not mind wandering, is negatively associated with quiz performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Study 1, media multitasking was negatively correlated with quiz performance (r = -0.22). [→ Wammes J.D. 2019](#wammes-jd-2019)
`q3 i2` In Study 2, media multitasking was negatively correlated with quiz performance (r = -0.27), while mind wandering was not. [→ Wammes J.D. 2019 (2)](#wammes-jd-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Wammes J.D. 2019

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

In Study 1, Spearman correlations showed media multitasking probe rates were negatively associated with "in-class quiz performance, r(161) = -0.22" and later test performance (r = -0.21).

> "The proportion of thought probes to which participants responded that they were media multitasking was negatively associated with both in-class quiz performance, r(161) = -0.22, p = .005, and later test performance, r(161) = -0.21, p = .007."

### Wammes J.D. 2019 (2)

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

In Study 2, media multitasking was again negatively associated with quiz performance ("r(68) = -0.27"), whereas mind wandering (r = 0.07) and elaborative responses (r = 0.09) showed no relation with quiz performance.

> "The proportion of thought probes to which participants responded that they were media multitasking was again negatively associated in-class quiz performance, r(68) = -0.27, p = .022. There was no relation between quiz performance and the proportion of either mind wandering, r(68) = 0.07, p = .554, or elaborative responses, r(68) = 0.09, p = .483."
