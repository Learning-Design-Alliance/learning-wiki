# Younger students solve letter equations less accurately than number equations, but this difference disappears by age 16–17

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Abstraction level (numbers vs. letters) affected accuracy, effect size η2 p = 0.126, with an Age × Abstraction interaction, η2 p = 0.043. [→ Susac A 2014](#susac-a-2014)
`q3 i?` In the 2nd grade of secondary school (age 16–17) there was no statistically significant accuracy difference between number and letter equations. [→ Susac A 2014 (2)](#susac-a-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Susac A 2014

Susac A, Bubic A, Vrbanc A and Planinic M (2014) Development of abstract mathematical reasoning: the case of algebra. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 8:679. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00679

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Two-way mixed-design ANOVA with between-subjects factor Age and within-subjects factor Abstraction level (numbers vs. letters) on accuracy; printed effect sizes η2 p = 0.126 (Abstraction level) and η2 p = 0.043 (interaction). "Participants were more accurate on equations with numbers, but only in primary school and in the 1st grade of secondary school."

> "the statistically signiﬁcant main effects of Age [F(3,327) = 8.37, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.071] and Abstraction level [F(1,327) = 47.17, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.126], as well as the interac­tion effect [ F(3,327) = 4.89, p < 0.01, η2 p = 0.043], were found"

### Susac A 2014 (2)

Susac A, Bubic A, Vrbanc A and Planinic M (2014) Development of abstract mathematical reasoning: the case of algebra. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 8:679. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00679

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc pattern of the Age × Abstraction interaction: "no sta­tistically signiﬁcant difference" in accuracy between number and letter equations for 2nd-grade secondary students; no effect size printed, and equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "In the 2nd grade of secondary school there was no sta­tistically signiﬁcant difference in the accuracy of solving equations with numbers and letters."
