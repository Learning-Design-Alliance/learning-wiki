# Only SOPT, IT and SART-CO showed significant impairment at the lowest blood alcohol concentration of 0.048%, with large effect sizes (d = 0.76, 0.65 and 1.06)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` At BAC 0.048% only SOPT, IT and SART-CO showed significant effects, with large effect sizes d = 0.76, 0.65 and 1.06 respectively. [→ Dry MJ 2012](#dry-mj-2012)

## Evidence

### Dry MJ 2012

Dry MJ, Burns NR, Nettelbeck T, Farquharson AL, White JM (2012) Dose-Related Effects of Alcohol on Cognitive Functioning. PLoS ONE 7(11): e50977. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0050977

`q3 · i2`

Post-hoc comparisons at the +60 minute test-point (target BAC 0.048%) in the 56-participant mixed-design experiment found significant alcohol-control differences only for SOPT, IT and SART-CO, with printed effect sizes d = 0.76, d = 0.65 and d = 1.06 respectively.

> "For only three tasks (SOPT, IT and SART-CO) was there a significant effect at the lowest blood alcohol concentration of 0.048%."
