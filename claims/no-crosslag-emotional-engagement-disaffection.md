Vasalampi, K.; Muotka, J.; Malmberg, L.; Aunola, K.; Lerkkanen, M. (2021). Intra‐individual dynamics of lesson‐specific engagement: lagged and cross‐lagged effects from one lesson to the next. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(3), 997-1014. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12404

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 3 (DIC = 4508.60) tested cross-lagged paths between emotional engagement and disaffection on the same 975-rating dataset. The article reports that disaffection "did not predict emotional engagement in the subsequent lesson or vice versa"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results of the model indicated that none of the cross-lagged paths were significant across the whole sample. Disaffection in a lesson did not predict emotional engagement in the subsequent lesson or vice versa (Fig. 2)."
