### Charlotte Zimmerman 2023

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Michael Loverude, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2023). Expert covariational reasoning resources in physics graphing tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.00921)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The article reports its preliminary replication of Hobson and Moore's experiment with physics graduate students on motion-based animation tasks, finding these two behaviors distinct from the mathematics graduate students' results. No effect size is printed.

> "In particular, physics graduate students were found to: (1) Use other quantities than those given in the task (i.e. time in place of distance), and (2) Create a graph by applying a model they already knew, or considering the rate of change only near a small number of physically meaningful points."
