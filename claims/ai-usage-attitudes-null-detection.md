## Evidence

### Milička J 2025

Milička J, Marklová A, Drobil O, Pospíšilová E. (2025). Humans can learn to detect AI-generated texts, or at least learn when they can't. Preprint. Manuscript submitted to PLOS One. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed model analysis of the 254-participant experiment treating an ordinal self-report of LLM usage frequency as a predictor; participants who reported frequent interactions with AI did not perform better than those with less experience.

> "We did not find a significant effect of AI usage frequency in either group."

### Milička J 2025 (2)

Milička J, Marklová A, Drobil O, Pospíšilová E. (2025). Humans can learn to detect AI-generated texts, or at least learn when they can't. Preprint. Manuscript submitted to PLOS One. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Attitude-battery analysis in the same experiment: only isolated statements reached significance in each group (avoidance p=0.022 in feedback; grammar p < 0.001 in no-feedback), and the article advises interpreting these with caution as directions for future research.

> "However, we found no systematic correlation between the attitudes and the ability to correctly asign AI texts."
