# Practice effects are larger when retesting is accompanied by test coaching, but coaching shows diminishing returns with contact time

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Studies including test coaching showed an overall effect size (δ = .70) larger than studies without coaching (δ = .24), with non-overlapping 95% confidence intervals. [→ Hausknecht 2005](#hausknecht-2005)
`q3 i2` Coaching contact time was positively and significantly related to practice effects in WLS regression (β = .26, p < .05), with logarithmically diminishing returns. [→ Hausknecht 2005 (2)](#hausknecht-2005-2)

## Evidence

### Hausknecht 2005

Hausknecht, J. P., Halpert, J. A., Di Paolo, N. T., & Moriarty Gerrard, M. O. (2005). Retesting in Selection: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Effects for Tests of Cognitive Ability. Journal of Applied Psychology (in press). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/apl

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Subgroup moderator analysis across the meta-analytic database; 23 of 107 samples received some test coaching, with a median of 2.5 hours candidate contact time. The article reports a corrected overall effect size of δ = .70 with coaching versus δ = .24 without.

> "studies that included some form of test coaching had an overall effect size (δ = .70) that was larger than the average practice effect in studies that did not include any type of test coaching (δ = .24)."

### Hausknecht 2005 (2)

Hausknecht, J. P., Halpert, J. A., Di Paolo, N. T., & Moriarty Gerrard, M. O. (2005). Retesting in Selection: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Effects for Tests of Cognitive Ability. Journal of Applied Psychology (in press). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/apl

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Weighted least squares regression entering four moderators simultaneously; coaching contact time emerged as statistically significant with β = .26. The article confirms "practice effects are logarithmically related to candidate contact time in coaching."

> "the coefficient for coaching contact time was statistically significant (β = .26, p \<.05)."
