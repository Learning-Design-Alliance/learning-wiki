## Evidence

### Morris Marie C. 2012

Morris Marie C., Gallagher Tom K., Ridgway Paul F. (2012). Tools used to assess medical students competence in procedural skills at the end of a primary medical degree: a systematic review. Medical Education Online. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3427596/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review synthesis across the 12 tools. The review states that "Construct validity was not established in any of the checklists or global assessment tools", with validity limited to face and content established through expert review.

> "Construct validity was not established in any of the checklists or global assessment tools utilised in the pre-practice setting, yet Reznick (19) reports the importance of ensuring construct validity of a tool in surgical skills assessment where there is an expectation of increasing levels of competence with increasing years of training to identify and instigate remediation of students at the earliest opportunity."
