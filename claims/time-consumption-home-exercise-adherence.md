## Evidence

### Escolar-Reina Pilar 2010

Escolar-Reina Pilar, Medina-Mirapeix Francesc, Gascón-Cánovas Juan J, Montilla-Herrador Joaquina, Jimeno-Serrano Francisco J, de Oliveira Sousa Silvana L, del Baño-Aledo M Elena, Lomas-Vega Rafael. (2010). How do care-provider and home exercise program characteristics affect patient adherence in chronic neck and back pain: a qualitative study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-60

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus-group study of 34 patients with chronic neck or low back pain; in the time-consumption sub-theme participants reported that "adherence to the programme was difficult when they had to spend too much time doing the exercises at home." Ten participants said they would prefer drugs if the programme required a lot of time.

> "Consequently, participants reported that adherence to the programme was difficult when they had to spend too much time doing the exercises at home."
