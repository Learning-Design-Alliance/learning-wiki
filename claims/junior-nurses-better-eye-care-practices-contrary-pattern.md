## Evidence

### Y. Zhang 2025

Y. Zhang, C. Li, Y. Zhou, and X. Yu. (2025). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Critical Care Nurses Regarding Eye Care Across 17 Hospitals: A Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Survey. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the survey's univariate analyses, the practice dimension showed significant differences by ICU experience (H = 20.483, p < 0.001), education (H = 27.344, p < 0.001) and professional title (H = 22.658, p < 0.001), with junior nurses scoring higher; the article states this is "contrary to the results observed in the knowledge dimension". Regression showed education level significantly related to practice (β = −0.068, t = −2.783).

> "Besides, nursing staff with lower job titles, fewer years of experience, lower educational levels, lower positions and those working in lower level hospitals exhibited better eye care practices than others. This finding is contrary to the results observed in the knowledge dimension."
