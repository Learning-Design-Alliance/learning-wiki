Evidence¶

Şimşek 2021¶

Şimşek, E., Xenidou-Dervou, I., Hunter, J., Gillon Dowens, M., Pang, J., Lee, Y., McNeil, N., Kirkland, P., & Jones, I. (2021). Factors Associated with Children's Understanding of Mathematical Equivalence: An Investigation across Six Countries. https://figshare.com/s/78625a6b0061200db6b7

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel SEM with country dummy variables (England as reference) across 2760 students and 108 classes. The article reports significant country differences with no standardised effect sizes printed.

"Specifically, classrooms in China, South Korea and the US had higher overall equivalence scores compared to classrooms in England. There was a significant difference between the participating schools in England and New Zealand. Specifically, classrooms in New Zealand had lower overall equivalence scores compared to classrooms in England."
