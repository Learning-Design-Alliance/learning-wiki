Evidence¶

Delavari S 2024¶

Delavari S, Barzkar F, Rikers MJP, Pourahmadi M, Soltani Arabshahi SK, Keshtkar A, Dargahi H, Yaghmaei M, Monajemi A. (2024). Teaching and learning clinical reasoning skill in undergraduate medical students: A scoping review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309606

q3 · i3 · review · r3

The review reports, from an included study attributed to reference [75], that SNAPPS versus traditional clinical teaching yielded SMD = 1.65 and SNAPPS versus OMP yielded SMD = 1.013, both statistically significant. The review attributes this to reference [75].

"SNAPSS, compared with traditional clinical teaching, had a statistically significant large effect (SMD, 1.65 [0.932–0.367); P = 0.001) on clinical reasoning skill improvement; SNAPPS, compared with OMP, had a statistically meaningful effect on clinical reasoning skill (1.013 [0.355–1.672; P = 0.005)"
