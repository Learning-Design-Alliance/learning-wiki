Evidence¶

Jing Teng 2025¶

Jing Teng, Xinuo Qiao, Kelong Lu, Tuo Liu, Xinyue Wang, Zhenni Gao, Tingting Yu & Ning Hao (2025). Neural mechanisms underpinning the association between visual arts education and creativity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00388-1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Sliding-window plus k-means clustering identified three dynamic neural coupling states per task; weighted independent-samples t-tests compared dwell time, state ratios, and transition frequency in the matched sample. The quote reports the cover-task flexibility contrast; the design group also spent a higher proportion of time in the efficient State 2 during the product task (M = 0.22 vs 0.16, pcorr = 0.040). State 2 showed lower path length and higher clustering and efficiency.

"the design group (Mweighted = 13.59) compared to the non-design group (Mweighted = 9.28) showed significantly higher transition frequency between brain states during the early cover thinking phase ( t (75.63) = 2.81, pcorr = 0.044), indicating greater neural flexibility."
