# The three instruction items intercorrelate strongly enough to form an internally consistent scale, yet achievement is positively associated with two items and negatively with the third, so their common variance does not represent instructional quality

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The three class-level instruction variables were strongly positively correlated across Swedish waves, with internal consistency comparable to the Instruction to Engage Students in Learning Scale, but achievement was positively associated with two items and negatively with the third. ## Evidence

### Kimmo Eriksson 2019

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the Swedish class-level correlations (Table 3, all significant at p < .001) showing the three items covary. The article's plausible interpretation is that what the items have in common does not represent instructional quality, so composite scales of such items are unwarranted.
