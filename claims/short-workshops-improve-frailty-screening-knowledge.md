The review reports this finding, citing Kotsani et al., a quantitative educational-program study (n = 31) whose participants after three months used screening methods more and had better knowledge and self-confidence. The review narrates the study; its design is not independently verified here.

> "Short, targeted workshops significantly improve the use of screening strategies, knowledge, self-confidence in dealing with frailty, and change attitudes — frailty is no longer seen as an inevitable part of aging [66]."
