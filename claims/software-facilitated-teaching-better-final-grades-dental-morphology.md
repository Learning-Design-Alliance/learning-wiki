## Evidence

### Al-Thobity Ahmad M. 2017

Al-Thobity Ahmad M., Farooq Imran, Khan Soban Q. (2017). Effect of software facilitated teaching on final grades of dental students in a dental morphology course. Saudi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.15537/smj.2017.2.15627

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective analysis of final examination grades of 294 second-year dental students across three academic years (group 1 = 94 control; groups 2 and 3 = 100 each with software CDs). The article reports that in the software years the percentage scoring good grades was higher than the control year, where "only 52% achieved good grades".

> "For both years with the software intervention, the percentage of students' scoring good grades was higher than those of students who scored good grades during 2013-2014 (group 1, the control group), where only 52% achieved good grades."
