Evidence¶

Jemstedt 2017¶

Jemstedt, A., Kubik, V., & Jönsson, F. U. (2017). What moderates the accuracy of ease of learning judgments? Metacognition and Learning, 12, 337–355. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-017-9172-3

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Experiment 1 within-group analysis of EOL accuracy by criterion measure found the binary criterion outperformed the TTL criterion, F(1, 30) = 9.60, p = .004; the article states the effect size was "very small, d =. 1 4", a negligible magnitude, and the direction was unexpected.

"gamma was reliably higher when EOL judgments were correlated with the binary criterion (M =. 4 8 4 ,SD= .13) than to the TTL criterion ( M =. 4 6 5 ,SD =. 1 4 ) ,F(1, 30) = 9.60, p = .004, mean difference = .019, 95% CI = [.01, .03]. The latter finding was unexpected as correlations in most cases benefit from scales that can take a larger range of values (TTL criterion) than if it is binary. However, the main effect of criterion measure on EOL accuracy was in terms of the effect size very small, d =. 1 4."
