The review reports "Items addressing RI topics, as we defined them, were included in all 15 measures" and "Ten measuresincluded items on RE topics".

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The majority of published RCR education evaluations use measures without reported validation evidence
  • Validation evidence across the 15 RCR measures is partial and heterogeneous, with no test–retest, criterion validity or factor-analytic evidence reported
  • Most validated RCR measures assess a single learning outcome, with attitudes most common and only four measures testing knowledge
  • Validated RCR measures omit emerging and social-safety topics including EDI, whistleblower protections, AI use in research, predatory journals and open-science practices
  • Mono-disciplinary RCR courses were not more effective than multi-domain courses, contradicting prior reviews — related
