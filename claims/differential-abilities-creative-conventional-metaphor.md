Summary contrast from the abstract of the SEM study of 191 undergraduates: "Creative metaphors rely more on executive processes, whereas conventional metaphors primarily draw from acquired vocabulary knowledge." The specific betas are reported in the sibling claims' evidence.

> "Creative and conventional metaphors thus appear to be anchored in dif- ferent patterns of abilities: Creative metaphors rely more on executive processes, whereas conventional metaphors primarily draw from acquired vocabulary knowledge."
