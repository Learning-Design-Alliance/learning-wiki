McGee RG, Wark S, Mwangi F, Drovandi A, Alele F, Malau-Aduli BS, The ACHIEVE Collaboration. (2024). Digital learning of clinical skills and its impact on medical students’ academic performance: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06471-2

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

The review attributes this cost analysis to Nathan et al. (2022), a randomised trial of suturing training formats; the review reports the printed savings of 58.9% and 44.2% versus face-to-face training. The review notes other included studies lacked detailed cost breakdowns.

> "In 2022, Nathan et al. showed that computer based learning and virtual classroom training for teaching suturing skills resulted in a cost saving of 58.9% and 44.2% respectively compared to face-to-face training [17]."
