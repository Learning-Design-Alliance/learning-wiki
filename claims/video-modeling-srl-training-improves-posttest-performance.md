# Video modeling training of self-assessment and task-selection skills improves secondary students' problem-solving performance after a self-regulated learning phase

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both heuristic and algorithmic training led to significantly better posttest problem-solving performance than no training. [→ Raaijmakers 2018](#raaijmakers-2018)

## Evidence

### Raaijmakers 2018

Raaijmakers, S. F., Baars, M., Schaap, L., Paas, F., van Merriënboer, J., & van Gog, T. (2018). Training self-regulated learning skills with video modeling examples: Do task-selection skills transfer? Instructional Science, 46, 273–290. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9434-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 122 second-year Dutch secondary students assigned to control (n = 43), heuristic (n = 37), or algorithmic (n = 42) training. Median tests showed both training conditions outperformed control on the five-problem posttest (heuristic r = .24; algorithmic r = .25).

> "Post-hoc Median Tests showed that in line with Hypothesis 1, both heuristic training, v2(1) = 4.732, p = .030, r = .24, and algorithmic training, v2(1) = 5.195, p = .023, r = .25, led to signiﬁcantly better problem-solving performance on the posttest than the control condition."
