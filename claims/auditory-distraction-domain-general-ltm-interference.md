# Auditory distraction disrupts episodic retrieval equivalently to visual distraction, indicating a domain-general interference effect in long-term memory

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Retrieval of relevant visual details declined in the auditory distractor condition relative to no-distractor and control-distractor conditions, mirroring the visual distraction pattern. [→ Wais 2014](#wais-2014)
`q3 i?` A mixed-design ANOVA across the two experiments found no main effect of distractor modality and no condition × modality interaction. [→ Wais 2014 (2)](#wais-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Wais 2014

Wais, P. E., and Gazzaley, A. (2014). Distractibility during retrieval of long-term memory: domain-general interference, neural networks and increased susceptibility in normal aging. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00280

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Behavioral study using written cues to probe recall of visual details during silence, white noise, or ambient café sounds (AD), analyzed in a mixed-design ANOVA with the visual distraction experiment. "retrieval of relevant visual details during the distractor conditions declined"; no effect size printed.

> "The results showed a main effect of condition such that retrieval of relevant visual details during the distractor conditions declined relative to both the no distractor and control distractor conditions."

### Wais 2014 (2)

Wais, P. E., and Gazzaley, A. (2014). Distractibility during retrieval of long-term memory: domain-general interference, neural networks and increased susceptibility in normal aging. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00280

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Direct mixed-design comparison (2 distractor modality × 3 condition ANOVA on conditional correct scores) between the auditory and visual distractor experiments. "no main effect of distractor modality and no interaction"; no effect size printed.

> "Critically, there was no main effect of distractor modality and no interaction between condition and distractor modality."
