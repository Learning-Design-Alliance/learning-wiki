## Evidence

### N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007

N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls. (2007). The Effect of Multiple Internal Representations on Context Rich Instruction. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1119/1.2785190](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2785190)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned MANOVA comparison in the classroom quasi-experiment combining the two n-coding CGPS sections (combined n=28) against the conventional CGPS section (n=18): they "differed statistically from the conventional CGPS approach (p=0.003)".

> "These data shows that the two n-coding CGPS groups (nCodMed and  nCodHi) differed statistically from the conventional CGPS approach (p=0.003)."

### N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007 (2)

N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls. (2007). The Effect of Multiple Internal Representations on Context Rich Instruction. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1119/1.2785190](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2785190)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate ANOVAs for the nCodHi&Med vs nCodLo comparison: Exam p<0.001, FCI gain p=0.115, wFCI gain p=0.090, RConf gain p=0.383; only the final exam was singly significant, with FCI and wFCI "close to the conventional (p<0.05) level of confidence".

> "Given the small sample sizes, significant differences between n-coding groups and traditional CGPS were only found in final exam grades. However, contributing effects in the required direction were given by the FCI and wFCI at confidence levels close to the conventional (p<0.05) level of confidence."
