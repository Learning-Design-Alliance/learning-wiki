## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Horwitz and Barowy, an evaluation of RelLab embedded in a cyclic teaching sequence, using a diagnostic open-ended questionnaire with first year (N = 112) and second year (N = 22) undergraduates plus clinical interviews. The comparison is against an earlier study by Villani and Pacca, not a concurrent control group; no effect size is printed.

> "It was concluded that the possibility to switch between frames of reference provides a way of supporting student reasoning that is unavailable through static event diagrams."
