## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Xu Bu, Xing Xiaoli, Zhang Guangyun, Zhang Wenting, Zhang Min, Gan Yan, Wei Tao, Xia Xiaodan. (2025). Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-made teaching satisfaction questionnaire (eight dimensions, Cronbach's α = 0.84; 405 valid of 408 distributed) compared between groups by chi-squared test. Table 5 prints overall 192 (94.12%) vs. 163 (81.09%), χ² = 15.868, p = 0.000, and the two null dimensions at p = 0.067 and p = 0.293.

> "the overall teaching satisfaction of the observation group was higher than that of the control group (p < 0.05). Specifically, the observation group was superior to the control group in five dimensions: whether it was innovative and diversified, whether it helped improve communication skills, whether it helped improve clinical thinking ability, whether learning interest was improved and whether self-learning ability was improved (p < 0.05); however, no statistically significant difference was found between groups in terms of whether the arrangement of the teaching plan was reasonable or whether the knowledge imparted could be closely integrated with clinical practice (p > 0.05)."
