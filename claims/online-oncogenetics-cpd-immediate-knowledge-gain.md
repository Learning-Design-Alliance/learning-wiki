### Houwink Elisa JF 2014

Houwink Elisa JF, van Teeffelen Sarah R, Muijtjens Arno MM, Henneman Lidewij, Jacobi Florijn, van Luijk Scheltus J, Jan Dinant Geert, van der Vleuten Cees, Cornel Martina C. (2014). `q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of the RCT with 80 randomized Dutch GPs (44 completers), controlling for pre-test score; the article reports the standardized regression coefficient of 0.27, an almost moderate effect size of 0.27, "indicating an almost moderate effect size".

> "The effect of the intervention was found to be statistically significant, amounting to 0.055 on the proportion correct scale; the corresponding value for the standardized regression coefficient was 0.27, indicating an almost moderate effect size."
