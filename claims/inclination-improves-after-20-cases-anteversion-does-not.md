## Evidence

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney comparison of the first 20 versus last 92 trainee cases in the retrospective cohort; inclination error fell from "4.3°±3.2° vs. 2.0°±2.0°, p < 0.01". No effect size is printed.

> "The absolute difference in inclination between navigation records and postoperative measurement was significantly less after 20 cases (first 20 vs. last 92 cases [4.3°±3.2° vs. 2.0°±2.0°, p < 0.01])."

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025 (2)

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same Mann–Whitney comparison for anteversion: the difference "was not significantly changed after 20 cases" (p = 0.08); equivalence was not tested. Vertical and horizontal distance differences also did not change significantly (p = 0.14 and p = 0.80).

> "The absolute difference of anteversion between the navigation records and postoperative measurements was not significantly changed after 20 cases (first 20 vs. last 92 cases [3.1°±2.1° vs. 2.3°±2.0°, p = 0.08])"
