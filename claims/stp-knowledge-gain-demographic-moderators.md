`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary demographic analysis (Mann-Whitney and Kruskal-Wallis tests) of knowledge gain in the 26-participant sample. "Female participants' knowledge gain level was significantly higher than that of male participants (p = 0.045)"; the 6-10 years' experience group also gained significantly more (p = 0.008).

> "Female participants' knowledge gain level was significantly higher than that of male participants (p = 0.045). Participants who were in the younger age group demonstrated lower knowledge gain: the mean knowledge level of 30–39-year-olds was significantly higher than the mean for 18–29-year-olds (p = 0.028)."
