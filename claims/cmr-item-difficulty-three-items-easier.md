### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2014

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2014). Coupled Multiple-response vs. Free-response Conceptual Assessment: An Example from Upper-division Physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Item-level analysis of the split-class comparison, plotted per-item in Fig. 4. The authors attribute Q15's easier CMR version to an eliminated explanation component, Q9's to recognition of justifications among reasoning elements, and Q3's to a tempting reasoning element that helps identify the correct method.

> "Diﬀerences between the scores are signiﬁcant for 3 of 16 items (Mann-Whitney U-test, p < 0.05; see Fig. 4). In all three cases, the diﬃculty went down for the new version."
