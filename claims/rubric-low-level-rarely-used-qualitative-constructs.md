## Evidence

### Young 2022

Young, N. T., Tollefson, K., Zegers, R. G. T., and Caballero, M. D. (2022). Rubric-based holistic review: a promising route to equitable graduate admissions in physics. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the fraction of all 321 rated domestic applicants in each rubric level. For research, noncognitive, and fit constructs, "faculty rarely used the "low" level of the rubric"; academic preparation and test-score constructs used all three levels, and diversity contributions was the fit construct where low was most common.

> "Second, for the research, noncognitive, and ﬁt constructs, faculty rarely used the "low" level of the rubric, with only three of the twelve constructs in those categories having more than 10% of applicants earning a "low.""
