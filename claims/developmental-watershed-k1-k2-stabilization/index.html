Evidence¶

Duan 2025¶

Duan, R., Sun, Q., & Tong, X. (2025). Non-linear development in statistical learning of visual orthographic regularities. npj Science of Learning 10, 6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00298-2

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Permutation tests (10,000 permutations, Bonferroni corrected) comparing fitted exponents across consistency conditions. Differences were significant between all pairs, indicating development of high-consistency learning was more rapid than moderate or low, and moderate more rapid than low.

q2 · i? · causal · r2

First-derivative curves of the three power-function models (Fig. 2b) show increasing rates across K1 to adults. The article reports a significant enhancement between ages 4–5 and 5–6, followed by stabilization of performance after 8–9 years.

"It clearly shows that distributionalstatistical learning improves signiﬁcantly between K1 and K2 groups, and then continues to show improvement at a steady rate from K2 children to adults"
