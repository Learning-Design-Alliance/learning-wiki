# Latent class analysis of children's EARTH responses finds no classes corresponding to Vosniadou and Brewer's initial or synthetic mental models of the earth

## Evidence

### Straatemeier 2008

Straatemeier, M., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Jansen, B. R. J. (2008). Children's knowledge of the earth: A new methodological and statistical approach. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2008.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent class analysis of responses to the EARTH-2 in the main experiment. The authors report that no latent class contained response patterns matching the initial or synthetic models, "formulated by Vosniadou and Brewer (1992)", even when Items 6 and 9 were excluded and alternative item weightings were used.

> "None of the response patterns of the latent classes corresponded to the initial or synthetic models that were formulated by Vosniadou and Brewer (1992). Latent class models with more classes also did not contain classes associated with these mental models."

### Straatemeier 2008 (2)

Straatemeier, M., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Jansen, B. R. J. (2008). Children's knowledge of the earth: A new methodological and statistical approach. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2008.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-classification analysis of the EARTH-2 responses in the main experiment (N = 381 recruited; N = 379 in Table 1). Only a "small percentage" of response patterns fit any mental model, contradicting the 80-85% classification rates reported in earlier interview studies.

> "The response patterns of only 7.2% of the children were completely consistent with one of the mental models, and the response patterns of only three children (0.8%) were completely consistent with a nonscientiﬁc model."
