Evidence¶

Samson 2022¶

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis across studies of memory and recall outcomes, including two studies using the Hopkins Verbal Learning Test that both found no significant effect of iron supplementation on memory and recall scores.

"We found no evidence to suggest that iron status, anemia, food-based interventions, nor iron supplementation influenced memory and recall."
