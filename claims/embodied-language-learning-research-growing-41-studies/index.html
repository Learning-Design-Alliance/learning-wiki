Evidence¶

Sofia Jusslin 2022¶

Sofia Jusslin, Kaisa Korpinen, Niina Lilja, Rose Martin, Johanna Lehtinen-Schnabel, Eeva Anttila. (2022). Embodied learning and teaching approaches in language education: A mixed studies review. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2022.100480

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic mixed studies review searching seven databases plus hand-searches, screening 6529 references. The synthesis ultimately included 41 studies: "23 qualitative, 12 quantitative, and six mixed methods". The review reports the publication-timing pattern as evidence of rapid growth.

"It encompasses 41 empirical studies with a majority published between 2019 and 2020, suggesting that the research area is growing rapidly."
