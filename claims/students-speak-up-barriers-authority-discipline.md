## Evidence

### Doyle Patricia 2015

Doyle Patricia, VanDenKerkhof Elizabeth G, Edge Dana S, Ginsburg Liane, Goldstein David H. (2015). Self-reported patient safety competence among Canadian medical students and postgraduate trainees: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2014-003142

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey items on comfort speaking up administered to 255 medical students. Third-year students were significantly less comfortable approaching someone engaging in unsafe care than first-year students (M 2.8 vs 3.3, F=3.1, p=0.03).

> "Only 38% of medical students felt they could approach someone engaging in unsafe practice. Similarly, 85% of medical students agree it is difficult to question the decisions or actions of those with more authority and the majority (67%) worry they will face disciplinary action if they make a serious error."
