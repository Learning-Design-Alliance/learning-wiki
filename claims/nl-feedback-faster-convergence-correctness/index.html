Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan 2026¶

Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan. (2026). A Classroom Study of LLM-Generated Feedback Intervention in Introductory Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08807 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.08807

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cox proportional hazards models with right censoring, measured in submissions since first feedback exposure, in the same classroom study. The authors report "HR = 1.09" and note the significant hazard ratio "indicates that this effect reflects faster convergence, not merely a ceiling-driven success rate difference".

"natural language feedback is associated with a modest but significant increase in the hazard of reaching correctness (HR = 1.09,p= 0.03 ): at any given attempt, students receiving natural language feedback are approximately 9% more likely to succeed"
