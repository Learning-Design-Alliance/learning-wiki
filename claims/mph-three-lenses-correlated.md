## Evidence

### Zweigenthal V 2026

Zweigenthal V, Christofides N, Dlungwane T, Matlala SF, Mokgatle MM, Opare A, Patrick SM, Schaay N, Shung-King M, Tshitangano T, Rispel L. (2026). Self-perceived competencies of Master of Public Health graduates and recommendations for programs in South Africa. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0360789

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rho correlations across the three lenses for 11 domains in the graduate survey; most rho values were moderate to very strong (e.g. M&E use vs confidence ρ = 1.00), with weak correlations only for academic and leadership confidence vs acquisition.

> "the scores for being applicable to respondents’ work, perceived to be obtained from the MPH and confidence in use, were mostly correlated and not independent (p = 0.00). The exceptions are in academic and leadership domains where confidence in using the skills and obtaining them from the MPH were weakly correlated (0.40 > ρ ≥ 0.1)."
