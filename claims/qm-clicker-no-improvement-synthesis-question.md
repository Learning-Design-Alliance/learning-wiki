### Ryan Sayer 2016

Ryan Sayer, Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). The impact of peer interaction on the responses to clicker questions in an upper-level quantum mechanics course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.071](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.071)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-question comparison of average unadjusted and adjusted GCT vs ICT performance across all students (Fig. 2). The article states students "on average showed improvement for most questions" but not on Question III, which "involves synthesis of mathematical knowledge and skills with knowledge of quantum physics".

> "However, Fig. 2 shows that, on average, students did not improve  from the ICT to the GCT on Question III, which asked them to determine the probability of finding an electron in a hydrogen atom at a position between 𝑟 and 𝑟 + 𝑑𝑟 from the nucleus ."
