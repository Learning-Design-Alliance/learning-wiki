## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 13 assessment years, main effects of year on achievement-g reliability and explained variance were negative but did not reach significance, indicating no substantial weakening of g over time. [→ Pietschnig 2023](#pietschnig-2023)

## Evidence

### Pietschnig 2023

Pietschnig, J., Oberleiter, S., Toffalini, E., & Giofrè, D. (2023). Reliability of the g factor over time in Italian INVALSI data (2010-2022): What can achievement-g tell us about the Flynn effect? Personality and Individual Differences, 214, 112345. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2023.112345

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian generalized linear models (beta-distributed outcomes fitted via brms/STAN) on R2 and omega indices extracted from 39 bifactor models of INVALSI math and reading data (1,951,334 assessments). The article reports "decreases in achievement g over time" that "did not reach significance"; no effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Interestingly, the sign of the main effect for year reversed when grade by year interactions were included, thus indicating decreases in achievement g over time, although the main effect did not reach significance."
