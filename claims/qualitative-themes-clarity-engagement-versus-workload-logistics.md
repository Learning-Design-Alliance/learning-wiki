## Evidence

### Natto Zuhair S 2026

Natto Zuhair S. (2026). Blended peer-led research curriculum with AI integration improves postgraduate students’ academic performance and satisfaction: a quasi-experimental mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08576-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke six-phase) of one intervention focus group of 6–8 participants plus reflective responses, conducted after grading. The theme course organization and clarity was consistent with the higher quantitative satisfaction ratings.

> "Intervention students consistently described the course as clearly structured, with transparent expectations and logical sequencing."

### Natto Zuhair S 2026 (2)

Natto Zuhair S. (2026). Blended peer-led research curriculum with AI integration improves postgraduate students’ academic performance and satisfaction: a quasi-experimental mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08576-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of the control-group focus group identified perceived excessive workload, exam timing concerns, LMS confusion, and mismatch with learner needs, contextualizing the control group's lower satisfaction ratings.

> "Control students commonly reported feeling overwhelmed by the number and pacing of critiques and assignments."
