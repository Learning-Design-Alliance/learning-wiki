## Evidence

### Someko Hidehiro 2026

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of GM-ITE scores by clinical research experience, adjusted for confounders including emergency department cases, weekly working hours, and number of patients in charge. The article reports "0.67 points [95% CI: -0.21 to 1.55] and -1.05 points [95% CI: -3.00 to 0.90]". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "GM-ITE scores were similar between residents who engaged in scholarly activities (with or without clinical research) and the reference group (adjusted differences: 0.67 points [95% CI: -0.21 to 1.55] and -1.05 points [95% CI: -3.00 to 0.90], respectively)"

### Someko Hidehiro 2026 (2)

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of GM-ITE scores by program requirement, adjusted for confounders. The article reports "0.59 points [95% CI: -0.56 to 1.74] for those without requirements and 0.43 points [95% CI: -0.88 to 1.74] for those with requirements". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "GM-ITE scores were similar across all groups (adjusted differences: 0.59 points [95% CI: -0.56 to 1.74] for those without requirements and 0.43 points [95% CI: -0.88 to 1.74] for those with requirements)"
