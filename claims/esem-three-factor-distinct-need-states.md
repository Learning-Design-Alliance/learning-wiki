# Students empirically distinguish autonomy dissatisfaction from autonomy satisfaction and autonomy frustration (3-factor ESEM solution)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exploratory structural equation modeling of 14 items showed the 3-factor solution (satisfaction, dissatisfaction, frustration) fit well and fit significantly better than a 2-factor solution combining dissatisfaction and frustration. [→ Sung Hyeon Cheon 2019](#sung-hyeon-cheon-2019)

## Evidence

### Sung Hyeon Cheon 2019

Sung Hyeon Cheon, Johnmarshall Reeve, Youngsun Lee, Nikos Ntoumanis, Nicolas Gillet, Bo Ram Kim, Yong-Gwan Song. (2019). Expanding Autonomy Psychological Need States from Two (Satisfaction, Frustration) to Three (Dissatisfaction): A Classroom-Based Intervention Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(4), 685-702. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000306

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ESEM analysis (Mplus, MLR estimator, oblique rotation) of the 14 autonomy need-state items from the study's student sample. The 3-factor solution fit reasonably well (RMSEA = .051, CFI = .976) with well-defined factors and "minimal cross-loadings" across satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and frustration.

> "The 3-factor solution provided a significantly better fit than did the alternative 2-factor solution (that combined the dissatisfaction and frustration items into a second factor; MD Δ X2 = 1,279.34, df = 12, p < .001; Δ CFI = .033)."
