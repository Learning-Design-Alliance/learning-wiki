Evidence¶

Karaazmak 2018¶

Fulda Karaazmak. (2018). The Effects of a Grammar Error Correction Session on Language Learners' Success. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 10(1), 61–70. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207240

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test within the control group (n=35), which received no treatment, comparing pretest and posttest grammar scores; the article reports a significant difference with t=-2.840, df=34, p=.008. No effect size is printed.

"Table 3 shows that there is a significant difference ( p.008<.05) between the mean test scores of control group for pretest ( M=44.05, SD=20.47) and the posttest ( M=49.14, SD=17.70)."
