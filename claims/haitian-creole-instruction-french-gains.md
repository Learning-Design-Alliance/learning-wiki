### Piper 2015

Piper, B., Bulat, J., & Johnston, A. (2015). Reading Skill Transfer Across Languages: Outcomes from Longitudinal Bilingual Randomized Control Trials in Kenya and Haiti. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562423

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of ToTAL trial outcomes in Haitian Creole and French. The article describes "patterns that suggest" cross-language gains, presenting this as a suggestive pattern rather than a tested transfer estimate, and the French curriculum focused on oral language development.
