Post hoc pairwise comparisons for the third group of 56 children (mean age 3.8) against Synchronizers and Non-synchronizers; the article reports "meaningful differences only for Phonological awareness (trending, p = 0.064) and Rapid automatized naming ( p = 0.004)". For FFR measures, only envelope-encoding precision differed significantly (p = 0.036).
