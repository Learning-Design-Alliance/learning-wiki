## Evidence

### Shafaei 2015

Shafaei, A., & Abdul Rahim, H. (2015). Does project-based learning enhance Iranian EFL learners' vocabulary recall and retention? Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 3(2), 83-99. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delayed post-tests (weeks 7-11) measured retention five weeks after each post-test; delayed post-test means were 26.05 (experimental) vs 21.30 (control), and the article reports significance in all modules except module 3, with no effect size printed.

> "Results of the t-test reveal that the experimental group had a significantly higher delayed post-test score in all modules at a confidence level of 99  %, except for module 3 where the difference was not statistically significant."
