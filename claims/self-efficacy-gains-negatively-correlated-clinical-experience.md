`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation in the same pilot sample relating background variables to pre-post change. The article reports "r = -.54, p = .01", suggesting greater gains for clinicians with fewer years of experience.

> "the correlation analysis revealed a significant negative relationship between years of clinical experience and the improvement in self-efficacy (r = -.54, p = .01)"
