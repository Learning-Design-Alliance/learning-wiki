Evidence¶

Arnold 2012¶

Arnold, D. H., Kupersmidt, J. B., Voegler-Lee, M. E., & Marshall, N. (2012). The Association between Preschool Children's Social Functioning and Their Emergent Academic Skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.12.009

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Exploratory HLM moderation analyses including only African American and Caucasian children. The article reports three significant interactions, e.g. "interaction B = −.17, SE = .08, p = .04" for attention problems and mathematics, each accounting for less than 4 percent of unexplained variance.

"ethnicity moderated the relationship between attention problems and mathematics development (interaction B = −.17, SE = .08, p = .04), between aggression and preliteracy development (interaction B = −.22, SE = .10, p = .03), and between aggression and mathematics development (interaction B = −.16, SE = .08, p = .05)"
