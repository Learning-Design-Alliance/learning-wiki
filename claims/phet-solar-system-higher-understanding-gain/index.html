Prima 2018¶

Prima, E. C., Putri, A. R., & Rustaman, N. (2018). Learning Solar System using PhET Simulation to Improve Students' Understanding and Motivation. Journal of Science Learning, 1(2), 60-70. https://jurnal.uns.ac.id/science-learning

Quasi-experiment with matching pretest-posttest design; two classes of 8th graders (n=42 total, 21 per group analyzed) at a private junior high school in Padalarang. The article reports "Asymp.Sig. (2-tailed ) is .038", so the difference in N-gain between groups is significant; no effect size is printed for the test.
