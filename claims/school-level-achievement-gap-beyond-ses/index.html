Voight 2013¶

Voight, A. (2013). The Racial School-Climate Gap. A report from the Region IX Equity Assistance Center at WestEd. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580366

q2 · i? · associational · r2

School-level analysis of 2004–06 Cal-SCHLS and testing data. After socioeconomic adjustment, "49 percent of ninth graders in predominantly White schools were predicted to score at proficient levels in English language arts" versus 37 percent in schools serving large numbers of African American and Hispanic students.

"after taking socioeconomic factors into account, 49 percent of ninth graders in predominantly White schools were predicted to score at proficient levels in English language arts compared to only 37 percent of ninth graders in schools serving large numbers of African American and Hispanic students."
