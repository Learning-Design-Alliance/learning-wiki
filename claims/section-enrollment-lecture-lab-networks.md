# Section enrollment relates differently to lecture and lab interaction networks: lecture interactions occur with both lab and discussion section peers, lab interactions only with lab section peers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In all four remote courses, students discuss lecture material with peers in their lab section significantly more than with peers not in their lab section, and mostly also with discussion section peers. [→ Meagan Sundstrom 2022](#meagan-sundstrom-2022)
`q2 i?` Students interact about lab material significantly with lab section peers but proportionately with peers in and not in their discussion section. [→ Meagan Sundstrom 2022 (2)](#meagan-sundstrom-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Andy Schang, Ashley B. Heim, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Understanding interaction network formation across instructional contexts in remote physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM homophily coefficients for section enrollment (Fig. 4, left panel) in four remote physics courses show that students "discuss lecture material with peers enrolled in their lab section" significantly more than with peers not in their lab section; with the exception of EM-Phys, students also tended to discuss lecture material with discussion section peers.

> "In all four courses, students discuss lecture material with peers enrolled in their lab section signiﬁcantly more than with peers not in their lab section"

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022 (2)

Meagan Sundstrom, Andy Schang, Ashley B. Heim, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Understanding interaction network formation across instructional contexts in remote physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM results for lab networks (Fig. 4, right panel) show students have "a signiﬁcant tendency to interact with peers in their lab section about lab material", while students proportionately interacted with peers in and not in their discussion section about lab material.

> "In all four courses, students have a signiﬁcant tendency to interact with peers in their lab section about lab material"
