## Evidence

### Venezia 2010

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The report's findings on system practices state that "cut scores to place into transfer-level mathematics courses ranged from 43 to 63 on the ACCUPLACER College Level Mathematics exam." Some interviewed students had received different placements at two different colleges based on the same test scores. The authors call for more research on the rationale for and implications of cut score variation.

> "in mathematics, cut scores to place into transfer-level mathematics courses ranged from 43 to 63 on the ACCUPLACER College Level Mathematics exam. Such variation in cut scores can send mixed signals to high school students across the state about what qualifies as college readiness."
