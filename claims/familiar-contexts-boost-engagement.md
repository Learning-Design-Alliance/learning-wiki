# Anchoring activities in familiar contexts, such as photos of the children's own school, immediately engaged every child

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Replacing an imaginary-school activity with photos of the children's own school immediately engaged every child and prompted them to guide the robot collaboratively. [→ Yanghee Kim 2021](#yanghee-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Yanghee Kim 2021

Yanghee Kim, Sherry Marx, Hung Viet Pham, Tung Nguyen. (2021). Designing for robot-mediated interaction among culturally and linguistically diverse children. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10051-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic findings report that an initial imaginary-school activity left children distracted, while a revised activity using photos of classrooms, hallways, cafeteria, gym, and playground from the children's own school engaged them; children worked together to give Skusie directions.

> "Every child was immediately engaged when they saw pictures of their own school."
