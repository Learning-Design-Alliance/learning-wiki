## Evidence

### Stauffer Tobias 2026

Stauffer Tobias, Gutiez Blanca, Babst Reto, Lohmeyer Quentin, Meboldt Mirko. (2026). Augmented reality enhanced physical simulation for femoral nailing training. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-026-03646-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on 23 analyzed participants showed insertion time fell from 134.7 s to 71.2 s, a "decreased significantly" result with p < 0.001. Retention time remained stable at 68.0 s, still significantly lower than run 1 (p < 0.001).

> "For the nail insertion step, the average completion time decreased significantly from 134.7 s (median 101.0 s) in the first run to 71.2 s (median 62.9 s) in the third run (p < 0.001)."
