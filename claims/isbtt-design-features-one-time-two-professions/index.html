The review's charting of design features (Table 2) across 58 articles; it also reports "The majority of articles described 1-time training events" (42 articles, 72.4%).

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's summary of session format; separately it reports debriefing described in 53 (91.4%) articles and psychological safety addressed in only 7 (12.1%).

"Prebriefings were described in the majority of articles (36, 62%) and focused on 1 or several of the following topics: goals for the session, collaborative practice, clinical aspects of the scenario, and orientation to the simulation setting and equipment."
