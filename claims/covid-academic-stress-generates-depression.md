## Evidence

### Jiang Zuopeng 2022

Jiang Zuopeng, Jia Xuhong, Tao Ran, Dördüncü Hazar. (2022). COVID-19: A Source of Stress and Depression Among University Students and Poor Academic Performance. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.898556

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structural equation modeling of survey responses from 186 usable questionnaires collected February–May 2021 from Pakistani higher-education students. The SEM path from academic stress to depression was significant: "a beta value of 0.298 and a p-value of 0.003"; no standardized effect size beyond the beta is printed.

> "According to the findings, students' academic stress promotes depression with a beta value of 0.298 and a p-value of 0.003."
