### M.-L. Tremblay and C. Fournier 2025

M.-L. Tremblay and C. Fournier. (2025). Involving Patients Throughout Sensitive Simulation. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70163

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme from the thematic analysis of 14 student interviews on factors shaping PLE integration effectiveness; students described feeling able to ask questions without fear of mistakes.

> "Participants emphasised that PLE's communication skills and ability to create a safe environment were key to maximising learning."

### M.-L. Tremblay and C. Fournier 2025 (2)

M.-L. Tremblay and C. Fournier. (2025). Involving Patients Throughout Sensitive Simulation. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70163

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two further themes from the same interview analysis: stronger PLE impact on stigmatised topics, and desire for more PLE involvement because, due to logistical constraints, only a few teams received direct observation and personalised feedback.

> "Participants noted that the impact of a PLE could be particularly strong when simulations addressed sensitive or stigmatised topics"
