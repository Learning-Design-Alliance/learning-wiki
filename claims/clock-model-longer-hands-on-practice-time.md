## Evidence

### Zhou Z 2024

Zhou Z, Zhang K, Zhao X, Hu Y, He Y, Wan L, Yao W. (2024). Evaluation of a 12-hole clock model for improving bronchoscopic skills in simulated normal and difficult airways among anesthesia residents: A randomized controlled study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000038510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

During the 60-minute training session, participants self-recorded attempt times with a timer; means were 1568 ± 478 s (intervention) versus 497 ± 172 s (control), "longer in the intervention group than in the control group" (P < .0001).

> "the total hands-on time of bronchoscopic practice was longer in the intervention group than in the control group (1568 ± 478 seconds vs 497 ± 172 s, P < .0001)."
