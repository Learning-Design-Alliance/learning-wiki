## Evidence

### Li Xiaoling 2024

Li Xiaoling, Li Fanju, Liu Wei, Xie Qinfang, Yuan Boyao, Wang Lijuan, Wang Manxia. (2024). Effectiveness of the application of small private online course combined with PBL model based on massive open online course in the teaching of neurology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06460-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction questionnaire administered to all 48 students before scores were released (100% valid return), rated on a five-level Likert scale across nine aspects. The article reports the between-group differences as "statistically significant (P < 0.05, effect size = 0.785, power = 0.997)" (Table 4, Fig. 5).
