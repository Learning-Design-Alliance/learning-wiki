## Evidence

### Li 2024

Li, Z., Wang, J., Chen, Y., Li, Q., Yin, S. & Chen, A. (2024). Attenuated conflict self-referential information facilitating conflict resolution. npj Science of Learning 9, 47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00256-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral results of the modified Stroop experiment with 33 participants in a blocked 2 (congruency) × 2 (self-referential information) design. The repeated-measures ANOVA on RT yielded a significant interaction (F(1,32) = 12.19, p = 0.001, ηp² = 0.28), with "self-reference condition (34.88 ms) reduced the CE compared to the non-self-reference condition (55.77 ms)". Incongruent RTs were faster under self-reference (668.84 ms) than non-self-reference (697.36 ms).
