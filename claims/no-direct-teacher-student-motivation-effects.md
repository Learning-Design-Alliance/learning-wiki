### Johannes Jud 2024

Johannes Jud, Yves Karlen, Carmen Nadja Hirt. (2024). Linking teachers' and students' motivation for self-regulated learning: is there a signal and how is it transmitted? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09393-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the full-perspective MSEM (167 teachers, 2,785 students), the Results section reports that "no significant direct effects between teachers' and students' motivation could be found"; Table 2 direct-effect confidence intervals all include zero. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Regarding RQ3, no significant direct effects between teachers' and students' motivation could be found (see Fig. 1; Table 2)."
