`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional nationwide survey of 2038 Indian grade 9 students using chi-square tests. On the size-ordering question, the paper reports 65% correct overall and the socioeconomic contrast: "85% of students from resource-rich schools answered it correctly in contrast to only 50% of resource-poor students."

> "85% of students from resource-rich schools answered it correctly in contrast to only 50% of resource-poor students. The disparities in response accuracy are highly statistically significant across both gender lines (p-value < 10–7) and resource distribution (p-value < 10–15) categories."
