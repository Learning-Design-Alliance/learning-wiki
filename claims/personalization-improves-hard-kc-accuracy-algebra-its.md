## Evidence

### Walkington 2013

Walkington, C. A. (2013). Using adaptive learning technologies to personalize instruction to student interests: The impact of relevant contexts on performance and learning outcomes. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 105*(4), 932–945. [doi:10.1037/a0031882](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031882)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 145 ninth-grade Algebra I students in Cognitive Tutor Algebra. Hierarchical logistic regression of problem-part accuracy showed personalization improved hard KC accuracy with "odds = 1.53, p < .001". Hard KCs were writing symbolic expressions with slope and intercept.

> "Table 4 shows that students in the personalization condition performed significantly better than the control group on hard KCs within Unit 6 (odds = 1.53, p < .001)."
