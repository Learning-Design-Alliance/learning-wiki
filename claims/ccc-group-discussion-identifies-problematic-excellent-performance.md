## Evidence

### Duitsman ME 2019

Duitsman ME, Fluit CRMG, van Alfen-van der Velden JAEM, de Visser M, ten Kate-Booij M, Dolmans DHJM, Jaarsma DADC, de Graaf J. (2019). Design and evaluation of a clinical competency committee. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0490-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative interviews with all members of a paediatric CCC after two meetings at Amalia Children's Hospital. Members reported "a richer picture of the residents by discussing their performance" and detection of performance extremes not recognized individually.

> "All CCC members perceived that they had gained a richer picture of the residents by discussing their performance. They had also identified problematic and excellent performance that they would not have recognized individually."
