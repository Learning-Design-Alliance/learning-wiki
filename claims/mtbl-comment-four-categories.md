## Evidence

### Murata 2023

Murata, H., Asakawa, S., Kawamura, T., Yamauchi, H., Takahashi, O., & Henker, R. (2023). Efficacy of modified team‐based learning in a flipped classroom for an acute‐care nursing course: A mixed‐methods study. Nursing Open, 10, 4786–4796. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1730

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Content analysis of free comments collected at post-test 1 (eight subcategories) and post-test 3 (seven subcategories); coding was done independently by each author with discrepancies resolved to consensus. The article states the analysis "yielded four categories". At post-test 3 a new subcategory, sense of unfairness at contribution, emerged under issues related to course approach.

> "The qualitative analysis yielded four categories: <<achievement of teamwork>>, <<sense of learning efficacy>>, <<satisfaction with course approach>> and < <issues related to course approach>>."
