## Evidence

### Carolyn P. Rosé 2019

Carolyn P. Rosé, Nancy Law, Ulrike Cress, Sten Ludvigsen. (2019). Highlighting tools and technologies for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. (2019) 14:1–6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09297-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The editorial reports the Steier, Kersting, and Silseth qualitative study of a web-based map task with two- and three-dimensional spatial representations, where students creatively fashioned improvised multi-modal representations during collaboration. The editorial, drawing on Conceptual Metaphor Theory, states that these representations "serve as metaphors that create an intersubjective space to house joint reasoning."

> "Just like verbal metaphors, multi-modal representations serve as metaphors that create an intersubjective space to house joint reasoning. They create affordances within interaction that guide and shape the direction the interaction takes."
