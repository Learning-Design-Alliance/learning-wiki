## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Planning, monitoring and evaluation scores decreased significantly between semester 2 and semester 6, with small effect sizes, while reflection, effort and self-efficacy did not change. [→ Lucieer Susanna M. 2016](#lucieer-susanna-m-2016)

## Evidence

### Lucieer Susanna M. 2016

Lucieer Susanna M., van der Geest Jos N., Elói-Santos Silvana M., de Faria Rosa M. Delbone, Jonker Laura, Visscher Chris, Rikers Remy M. J. P., Themmen Axel P. N. (2016). The development of self-regulated learning during the pre-clinical stage of medical school: a comparison between a lecture-based and a problem-based curriculum. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21, 93-104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9613-1

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional ANOVA comparing 384 medical students in semester 2 (n=177) and semester 6 (n=207) at two Brazilian medical schools on the SRL-SRS. The article reports significant decreases in planning, monitoring and evaluation with "η2 = .012", .021 and .024, all small; reflection, effort and self-efficacy showed no significant change.

> "This decrease is seen for the skills planning; F(1383) = 4.743, p = .030, η2 = .012; monitoring; F(1383) = 7.987, p = .005, η2 = .021; and evaluation; F(1383) = 9.250, p = .003, η2 = .024, although the effect sizes are small."
