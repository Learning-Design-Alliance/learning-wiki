## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Most IHCPs could not identify correct initial resuscitation steps: 17% chose bag-and-mask ventilation, 41% chose holding the baby by the legs and patting the back, 14% knew to clean the mouth and nose, and 13% would wipe the baby with dry pre-warmed clothes. ## Evidence

### Gikandi MI 2020

Gikandi MI, Singh BS, Shuchi S, Shailja V, Megha S, Vishal D, Tamhankar AJ, Stålsby Lundborg C, Pathak A. (2020). Identifying the know-do gap in evidence-based neonatal care practices among informal health care providers—a cross-sectional study from Ujjain, India. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-020-05805-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Knowledge survey responses among 830 IHCPs given a vignette of a baby with poor respiration after birth; the article reports "41% (n = 369) IHCPs incorrectly opted to hold the baby by the legs and pat the back."

> "17% (n = 156) correctly identified that they would perform resuscitation using a bag and mask. However, 41% (n = 369) IHCPs incorrectly opted to hold the baby by the legs and pat the back."
