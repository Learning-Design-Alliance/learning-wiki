## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across all three experiments, post-rest RT gain was not significantly associated with cued-recall or vowel-counting task accuracy. [→ Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard 2025](#mohan-w-gupta-and-timothy-c-rickard-2025)

## Evidence

### Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard 2025

Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard. (2025). The Effects of Declarative Learning on Early and Late Motor Skill Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00386-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 regression analyses of post-rest gain on task accuracy: cued-recall (p = .09, r2 = .055) and vowel counting (p = .06, r2 = .068) both "no significant associations". Experiments 1 and 2 similarly reported no significant associations (e.g., p = .07 and p = .13 in Experiment 1).

> "We found no significant associations between post -rest RT gains and performance on the cued -recall task, F(1, 51) = 2.96, p = .09, r2 = .055, nor the vowel counting task, F(1, 50) = 3.67, p = .06, r2 = .068"
