## Evidence

### Sidorkin 2026

Sidorkin, A. M. (2026). From One-Size Texts to Tailored Readings: Student Experiences with AI-Generated Course Materials. Open Praxis, 18(3), 506–522. https://doi.org/10.55982/openpraxis.18.3.1141

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Artifact analysis using dictionary-based automated counts on a systematic 1/5-page sample of logs B–K (samples 13,728–22,034 words). The article reports "a mean of 52.24 markers per 10,000 words (SD = 13.73)", showing customization language was frequent but not uniform.

> "the overall rate of tailoring markers was substantial, with a mean of 52.24 markers per 10,000 words (SD = 13.73), and a wide observed range from 38.74 (Log K) to 74.29 (Log B)"

### Sidorkin 2026 (2)

Sidorkin, A. M. (2026). From One-Size Texts to Tailored Readings: Student Experiences with AI-Generated Course Materials. Open Praxis, 18(3), 506–522. https://doi.org/10.55982/openpraxis.18.3.1141

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

TF-IDF cosine similarity computed across sampled log text in Table 3, with robustness checks (stopword removal, bigram vectors) shifting averages by no more than 0.03. The article interprets this as "a common instructional backbone across logs, with meaningful contextual overlays".

> "Average cosine similarity across logs ranges from 0.50 to 0.61"
