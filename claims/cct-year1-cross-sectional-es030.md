## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Treatment 1.1 students significantly outperformed Control 2.1 students by about 1.9 points after adjusting for pretest scores and teacher experience (ES = 0.30). [→ Irving 2016](#irving-2016)

## Evidence

### Irving 2016

Irving, K. E., Pape, S. J., Owens, D. T., Abrahamson, L., Silver, D., & Sanalan, V. A. (2016). Classroom Connectivity and Algebra 1 Achievement: A Three-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Computers in Mathematics and Science Teaching, 35(2), 131-151. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095322

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional HLM (model 1p) comparing Treatment 1.1 with Control 2.1 classrooms, controlling for pretest score and years of teaching experience. The article reports the printed effect size ES = 0.30, p = .034, and γ01 = 1.86; years of teaching approached significance (γ02 = 0.11, p = .076).

> "the treatment group significantly outperformed the control group (p = .034; ES = 0.30) by approximately 1.9 points (γ01 = 1.86) even after adjusting for pretest scores and teacher experience."
