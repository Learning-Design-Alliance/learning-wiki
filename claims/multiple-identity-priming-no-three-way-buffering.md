## Evidence

### Mocevic 2025

Mocevic, H., and B. Gniewosz. 2025. "Stereotype Threat and a Multiple Identity Intervention Among First‐ and Second‐Generation Students of Migrant Descent." Journal of Community Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1002/jcop.70065

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confirmatory moderated moderation analysis (PROCESS Model 3) in the classroom quasi-experiment testing Hypothesis 2; the null finding means no buffering was detected, not that equivalence was established. No effect size was printed.

> "The three‐way interaction between second generation status, stereotype activation, and identity condition was not significant, b = 1.51, SE = 2.38, p = 0.527, 90% CI [–2.41, 5.43], nor was the corresponding three‐way interaction for first‐generation students, b = –3.17, SE = 3.85, p = 0.410, 90% CI [–9.52, 3.17]."
