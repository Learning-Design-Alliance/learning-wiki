BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-020-03034-y

Observational log analysis of all 49 students' Moodle activity during the 6-day review; descriptive hit counts only, no inferential test reported.

### Li Lei 2020 (2)

Li Lei, Tam Chi Wing, Wang Ning, Cheung Fan, Zhou Qing, Zhang Cheng, Cheng Chien-shan, Xiong Lei, Feng Yibin. (2020). Effectiveness of blending E-learning with field trip on Chinese herbal medicine education: quasi-experimental study. BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-020-03034-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Log analysis shown in Fig. 2c and d compares activity hits per activity between cohorts; the 5-year cohort had about twice as many total herbal plant atlas hits as the 6-year cohort, while photo sharing and exercise totals were nearly the same.

> "The most frequently used content area was the herbal plant atlas (12,623 hits). The herbal photo sharing (2667 hits) and self-test exercises (1169 hits) were also frequently visited by the students."
