Esse Ciego 2026¶

Esse Ciego, Shubbhi Taneja, Wilson Wong, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Student Perceptions and Preferences Regarding AI-Generated Instructional Videos in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28203 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28203

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive post-test survey of 170 computing students at two U.S. institutions after watching three 3-minute AI-generated Markdown videos. Likert ratings showed "92% agreement" on professional production, comprehension, and accuracy, with means of 4.52, 4.39, and 4.43 respectively.

"A large majority of the 170 students agreed or strongly agreed that the videos were professionally produced (𝜇= 4.52, 𝜎= 0.77; 92% agreement), helped them understand Markdown (𝜇= 4.39, 𝜎= 0.76; 92% agreement), and explained Markdown accurately (𝜇= 4.43, 𝜎= 0.78; 88% agreement)."
