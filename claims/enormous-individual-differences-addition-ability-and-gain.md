Descriptive analysis of individual initial and final scores in Table 1 and distributions in Tables 5 and 6. In the seventh grade the range is from 8 right and 8 wrong to 38 right and 6 wrong; the article states the tables show "the enormous overlapping of the different grades in respect to ability in adding at the start of the experiment."
