Evidence¶

Yizhou Fan 2022¶

Yizhou Fan, Lyn Lim, Joep van der Graaf, Jonathan Kilgour, Mladen Raković, Johanna Moore, Inge Molenaar, Maria Bannert, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević. (2022). Improving the measurement of self-regulated learning using multi-channel data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09304-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of SRL process duration medians across data channels for 25 participants in a 45-minute lab learning session. The article attributes the shift to more NOTE_EDITING and WRITE_ESSAY actions labelled "mostly based on keyboard strokes".

"We detected many more Organisation processes (43.03% based on Enhanced_log compared to 0% based on Nav_only), and far fewer First-reading processes (19.14% based on Enhanced_log compared to 65.09% based on Nav_only)."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Adding eye-tracking data corrects inflated Organisation durations and re-distributes First-reading time relative to both other channels
  • Adding data channels refines roughly 29–52% of detected SRL process time, measured by a new percent-of-processes-refined metric
  • Adding peripheral data raises specific transition probabilities in the SRL process map, e.g., Elaboration-to-Monitoring from 24% to 44%
  • Most refinements from adding peripheral data convert First-reading or Re-reading into Organisation, and eye-tracking then corrects many back to First-reading — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The largest source of mismatch between trace-based and think aloud SRL processes was confusion between elaboration/organisation and first-reading processes — related
