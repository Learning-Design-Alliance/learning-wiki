## Evidence

### Federer Meghan Rector 2016

Federer Meghan Rector, Nehm Ross H., Pearl Dennis K. (2016). Examining Gender Differences in Written Assessment Tasks in Biology: A Case Study of Evolutionary Explanations. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Rasch-based DTF analysis of ACORNS item sequences (Table 4) compared male and female KC and NI difficulty measures. The article reports the familiarity of the item scenario "may be an important factor for explaining performance differences"; Table 4 prints Cohen's d values of 0.17–0.53 for significant contrasts.

> "Analyses of overall test performance, as measured by the sum total of KCs and NIs for individual items, revealed significant differences in test functioning (DTF) between genders with regard to item familiarity, suggesting that the familiarity of the item scenario may be an important factor for explaining performance differences"
