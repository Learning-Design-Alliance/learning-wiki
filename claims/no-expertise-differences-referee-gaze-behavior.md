# Higher- and lower-level referees show no significant differences in fixation and saccade measures

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Two-way ANOVAs (referee level × game situation) found no significant differences between higher- and lower-level referees in fixation counts, fixation duration, saccade count, or saccade duration. [→ Świdwa 2025](#swidwa-2025)

## Evidence

### Świdwa 2025

Świdwa, Jacek; Klatt, Stefanie; Kantanista, Adam. (2025). Gaze behavior and decision-making among handball referees: exploring gender and expertise differences. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19401

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVAs on eye-tracking data from 31 higher-level and 20 lower-level referees; all four gaze measures were non-significant, e.g. fixation counts "F(4,1561) = 0.701, p = 0.590". No effect sizes were printed.

> "No significant differences were found between higher- and lower-level referees in fixation counts, F(4,1561) = 0.701, p = 0.590, average fixation duration, F(4,1561) = 0.227, p = 0.923, saccade count, F(4,1561) = 0.210, p = 0.932, or average saccade duration, F(4,1561) = 0.298, p = 0.879."
