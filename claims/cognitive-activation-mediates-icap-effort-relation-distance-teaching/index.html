Evidence¶

Fütterer 2023¶

Fütterer, T., Hoch, E., Lachner, A., Scheiter, K., & Stürmer, K. (2023). High-quality digital distance teaching during COVID-19 school closures: Does familiarity with technology matter? Computers & Education, 199, 104788. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104788

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent path analysis (mediation models) in Mplus for mathematics (N = 721) and German (N = 722). The study reports "a partial mediation was evident" such that "the prediction of students' effort by student-observed learning activities (direct path) remained statistically significant even when considering the statistically significant indirect paths." Models explained approximately 30% (math) and 28% (German) of variance in academic effort.

"a partial mediation was evident (Baron & Kenny, 1986; Rucker, Preacher, Tormala, & Petty, 2011), which is in line with expectations. This means that the prediction of students' effort by student-observed learning activities (direct path) remained statistically significant even when considering the statistically significant indirect paths"
