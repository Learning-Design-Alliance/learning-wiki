Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the ANAKIN randomized pretest-posttest control group study (N = 60), confidence increased significantly after booster exposure at 4- and 8-month assessments (P < 0.001); at 8 months a nonsignificant correlation was found between knowledge/performance scores and self-reported confidence.
