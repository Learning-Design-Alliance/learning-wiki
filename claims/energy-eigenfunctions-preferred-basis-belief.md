## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Many students believed that probabilities for measuring any observable should be computed by expanding the wavefunction in energy eigenfunctions, rather than in the eigenfunctions of the operator corresponding to that observable. [→ Singh 2016](#singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Singh 2016

Singh, C. (2016). Student Difficulties with Quantum Mechanics Formalism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Written surveys and interviews on measurement of observables other than energy. The article reports "Many students believed" the wavefunction should be expanded in energy eigenfunctions, when it should be expanded in the eigenfunctions of the operator corresponding to the observable measured.

> "Many students believed that even when answering questions related to the probability of different possible outcomes for the measurement of an observable other than energy, the wavefunction should be expanded in terms of the energy eigenfunctions"
