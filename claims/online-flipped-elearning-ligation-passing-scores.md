## Evidence

### Okada Takuhisa 2022

Okada Takuhisa, Asao Takayuki, Inoue Hitoshi, Harimoto Norifumi, Tanaka Kazumi, Shiraishi Takuya, Sano Akihiko, Ogawa Hiroomi, Sohda Makoto, Shirabe Ken, Saeki Hiroshi. (2022). Retrospective study of online ligation student practice incorporating flipped learning into e-learning. Surgery Today. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00595-022-02490-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective study of 134 fourth- and fifth-year medical students trained online from March 2020 to June 2021; two instructors independently scored three ligation tasks on a 5-point Global Rating Scale. The article reports the "total average score of the three tasks was 12.4" for Instructor 1 and 12.0 for Instructor 2, with all students passing.

> "The total average score of the three tasks was 12.4 (range, 9–15) for Instructor 1, 12.0 (range, 9–15) for Instructor 2, and 12.2 (range, 9–15) for both instructors. All students obtained a passing score of ≥ 9."
