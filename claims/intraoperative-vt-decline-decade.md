## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective comparison of 818 propensity-matched cases per year: mean VT/kgIBW "reduced" from 9.9 to 8.4 mL/kg (p < 0.001); VT > 10 mL/kgIBW fell from 356/818 (43.5%) to 48/818 (5.9%).

> "the mean VT/kgIBW had reduced between 2004 and 2014 (9.9 [1.4] vs. 8.4 [1.0] mL/kg; p < 0.001; Table 3)."

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018 (2)

BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Obese subgroup (BMI > 30 kg/m2) of the same retrospective cohort: mean VT/kgIBW showed "a similar reduction" from 10.7 to 8.9 mL/kg (p < 0.001).

> "the mean VT / kgIBW also exhibited a similar reduction (10.7 ± 1.5 vs. 8.9 ± 0.9 mL/kg; p < 0.001)."
