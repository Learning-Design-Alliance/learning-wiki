# Non-traditional letter writing improved 9th graders' conceptual understanding of a chemistry mixture unit more than traditional writing, with a small effect

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` After controlling for pretest scores, the non-traditional writing group scored significantly higher on the mixture posttest than the traditional writing group, with a small effect size (d = 0.25). [→ Kingir 2013](#kingir-2013)

## Evidence

### Kingir 2013

Kingir, S. (2013). Using Non-traditional Writing as a Tool in Learning Chemistry. Eurasia Journal of Mathematics, Science & Technology Education, 9(2), 101-114. https://doi.org/10.12973/eurasia.2013.922a

`q3 · i1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 524 9th graders in 2 public high schools; ANCOVA on post-MAT controlling pre-MAT showed the non-traditional writing group "outperformed those in the traditional writing group" with "small, d = 0.25".

> "Results indicated a significant difference between the groups, F(1, 521) = 7.90, p < 0.05. Students in the non-traditional writing group (M = 6.69, SD = 2.01) outperformed those in the traditional writing group (M = 6.02, SD = 2.23) with respect to measures of the posttest. The size of the mean difference between the groups was small, d = 0.25."
