## Evidence

### Ning 2025

Ning, Zhuang, Wen, Xu, Wang, Peng, Huang and Tian. (2025). Evaluating ECAMM-based training efficacy for malaria microscopists in Hunan Province, China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1598917

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-training baseline assessment of the 24 participants on validated blood smear panels. The article reports "particularly low accuracy" for species identification with a mean of 14.58%; negative and positive smear qualitative accuracies were 43.75% and 63.02% respectively. Descriptive statistics only.

> "Parasite species identification in positive smears demonstrated particularly low accuracy (range: 0 to 50%; mean: 14.58%; 95% CI: 9.00 to 21.15)."

### Ning 2025 (2)

Ning, Zhuang, Wen, Xu, Wang, Peng, Huang and Tian. (2025). Evaluating ECAMM-based training efficacy for malaria microscopists in Hunan Province, China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1598917

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Participant background survey in the Results section documenting practical experience gaps: 45.83% never examined malaria smears in the period and 62.50% lacked hands-on Plasmodium morphological identification experience. The 75.00% figure is printed for the 24-person cohort.

> "Regarding training history, 75.00% (18/24) had received no formal theoretical or practical malaria microscopy training in the preceding 5 years."
