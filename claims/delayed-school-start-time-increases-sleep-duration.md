## Evidence

### Li Shenghui 2013

Li Shenghui, Arguelles Lester, Jiang Fan, Chen Wenjuan, Jin Xingming, Yan Chonghuai, Tian Ying, Hong Xiumei, Qian Ceng, Zhang Jun, Wang Xiaobin, Shen Xiaoming. (2013). Sleep, School Performance, and a School-Based Intervention among School-Aged Children: A Sleep Series Study in China. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0067928

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

School-based intervention trial in six Shanghai primary schools using a 3 groups (control, intervention1, intervention2) × 2 times (baseline, 2-year follow-up) design with two schools randomly assigned per group; comparative cross-sectional analysis of pre- (n = 525) and post-intervention (n = 553) surveys of fourth and fifth graders. The article reports "sleep duration correspondingly increased by 15.6 minutes and 22.8 minutes" and a significant improvement in sleep duration and daytime sleepiness; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "After delaying school start time 30 minutes and 60 minutes, respectively, sleep duration correspondingly increased by 15.6 minutes and 22.8 minutes, respectively. Moreover, intervention significantly improved the sleep duration and daytime sleepiness."

### Li Shenghui 2013 (2)

Li Shenghui, Arguelles Lester, Jiang Fan, Chen Wenjuan, Jin Xingming, Yan Chonghuai, Tian Ying, Hong Xiumei, Qian Ceng, Zhang Jun, Wang Xiaobin, Shen Xiaoming. (2013). Sleep, School Performance, and a School-Based Intervention among School-Aged Children: A Sleep Series Study in China. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0067928

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same intervention trial, control schools keeping the 7:30am start time; Table 6 shows control sleep duration fell from 9.17±0.73 to 9.01±0.67 (t = −31.48, p<0.001) and frequent daytime sleepiness rose from 23.86% to 36.61%. The article states "an opposite change was observed: a decrease in sleep duration and an up-regulation of daytime sleepiness"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "For the control group, an opposite change was observed: a decrease in sleep duration and an up-regulation of daytime sleepiness."
