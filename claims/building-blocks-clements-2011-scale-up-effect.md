# In a large-scale 106-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.48 (improvement index +18)

## Subclaims

`q4 i2` Clements et al. (2011), a cluster randomized controlled trial with 1,305 children in 106 preschool classrooms, found a statistically significant effect of Building Blocks on the REMA (effect size 0.48, improvement index +18, p < 0.01). [→ study-2011](#study-2011)

`q3 i2` Hofer et al. (2013), a cluster randomized controlled trial with 1,714 children in 139 preschool classrooms, found a statistically significant effect of Building Blocks on the REMA (effect size 0.55, improvement index +21, p < 0.01). [→ study-2013](#study-2013)

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Large-scale cluster randomized trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations; preschool sites randomly assigned to Building Blocks or business as usual in two urban public school districts. Effect size 0.48 (improvement index +18); intervention mean 52.40 (17.25) versus comparison 44.01 (17.89) on the REMA.

### study-2013

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, December). Building Blocks. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/733

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards With Reservations because it satisfies baseline equivalence but has high individual-level nonresponse; preschool sites randomly assigned in three urban districts in Tennessee, New York, and Massachusetts. Effect size 0.55 (improvement index +21); intervention mean 48.00 (4.71) versus comparison 44.91 (5.42).

> "End of 30 weeks of implementation 1,714 children 139 preschool classrooms in the United States 48.00 (4.71) 44.91 (5.42) 0.55 +21 Yes (p < 0.01)"
