# In a large-scale 106-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.48 (improvement index +18)

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Clements et al. (2011), a cluster randomized controlled trial with 1,305 children in 106 preschool classrooms, found a statistically significant effect of Building Blocks on the REMA (effect size 0.48, improvement index +18, p < 0.01). [→ study-2011](#study-2011)

### study-2011

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, December). Building Blocks. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/733

Large-scale cluster randomized trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations; preschool sites randomly assigned to Building Blocks or business as usual in two urban public school districts.
