## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis in the randomized intervention compared task 1/2 times for novices with more versus less than one hour of weekly videogaming (printed means 172.52 ± 52.3 s vs. 201.31 ± 56.8 s); times were "not significantly different". No test statistic or effect size was printed.

> "The performance times for tasks 1 and 2 in laparoscopy novices with > 1 h of experience in videogaming per week were not significantly different (refer to Table 3)."
