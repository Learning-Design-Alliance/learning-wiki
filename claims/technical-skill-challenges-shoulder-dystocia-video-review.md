`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Blinded checklist scoring of technical performance in 17 video-recorded cases by two obstetricians. The article reports maximum-score percentages of 12% for recognizing impaction, 13% for suprapubic pressure, and 41% for discouraging pushing, while most teams scored maximally on McRoberts and internal rotational maneuvers.

> "less than 50% achieved maximum scores for recognizing and making a verbal statement of shoulder impaction (12%), applying suprapubic pressure (13%), and discouraging women from pushing (41%)"
