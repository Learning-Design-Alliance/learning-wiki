## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Defective children are generally slower in responding to association-test stimulus words than normal children, and their response times are highly irregular. [→ Otis 1915](#otis-1915)

## Evidence

### Otis 1915

Otis, M. (1915). A Study of Association in Defectives. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VI, No. 5. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Study+of+Association+in+Defectives%22+Otis

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of timed individual association tests of 130 defectives at Vineland, recorded with a stop-watch in tenths of seconds. The author reports that "the defective children are slower in responding than the normal children" with great irregularity; no effect size is printed.

> "In general, the defective children are slower in responding than the normal children. There is great irregularity, also, in the time of response."
