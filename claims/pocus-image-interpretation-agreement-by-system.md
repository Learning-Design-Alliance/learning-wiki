Discussion of the retrospective study: of 3014 clips/images, 999 were excluded for poor quality and 2109 (69.97%) were assessed by blinded expert reviewers for agreement between the performing clinician's described sonographic findings and the experts' judgment.

> "In terms of the competence of image interpretation, our results showed 60.9% rated as “Agree”, which was considered to be of satisfactory level of this competency."
