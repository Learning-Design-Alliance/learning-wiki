# New teachers introduced to Knowledge Forum first tended to use it for teacher-directed instruction rather than collaborative knowledge building

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` New teachers who were first introduced to Knowledge Forum used it as a venue for teacher-directed instruction, resembling a lethal mutation of the innovation. [→ Chan 2011](#chan-2011)

## Evidence

### Chan 2011

Chan, C. K. K. (2011). Bridging research and practice: Implementing and sustaining knowledge building in Hong Kong classrooms. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9121-0

`q2 · i?`

Observation from phase one of the Knowledge-Building Teacher Network. New teachers "tended to focus on the tool itself and used the forum as a technology-based venue merely for teacher-directed instruction (e.g., posting questions as assignments)." The authors liken this to Brown and Campione's lethal mutation.

> "The new teachers who were first introduced to Knowledge Forum tended to focus on the tool itself and used the forum as a technology-based venue merely for teacher-directed instruction"
