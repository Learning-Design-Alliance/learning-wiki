Perceived effectiveness measured with the DASH-SV questionnaire (1–7 scale) in the pilot trial; mean difference 1.4 favouring facilitator-guided debriefing, p = 0.04. The article reports this as a statistically significant perception difference.

> "We found that the facilitator-guided group perceived the debriefing to be significantly more effective (DASH) compared to the self-guided group (mean difference 1.4, CI 0.1 to 2.7, p = 0.04)."
