### Breitwieser 2026

Breitwieser, J., Brod, G., & Malmberg, L.-E. (2026). Testing a three-phase model of the relationship between motivational appraisals, observable engagement, and subjective engagement: Insights from combining mobile learning data and daily diaries. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001009

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Sample-size-weighted between-person correlations from 197–204 participants reported in the descriptive results and Table 1. Only the value–engagement correlation was significant, "r = .14, p < .05"; engagement's correlations with satisfaction, effort, and task delay were not significant.

> "However, at the between-person level, only value was weakly correlated with vocabulary engagement (r = .14, p < .05), suggesting that students who valued vocabulary learning more tended to engage more with the vocabulary app than students who valued learning less."
