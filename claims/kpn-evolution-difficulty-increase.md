### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Evolution analysis of four stage-level IKPNs built from the 35 papers grouped by curriculum-reform policy dates (10, 8, 8, 9 papers per stage). The authors also report the overall Fd trend "indicates a noticeable increase in the difficulty of high school exams"; the second stage had the highest Fd.

> "the average degree progressively increases across the stages, and the average shortest path length decreases."
