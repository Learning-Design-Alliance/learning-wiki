Evidence¶

Adurangba Victor Oje 2026¶

Adurangba Victor Oje, Nathaniel J. Hunsu. (2026). Desktop virtual reality with scaffolded interaction and reflective explanation: effects on cognitive, motivational, and learning outcomes in engineering education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10722-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bayesian estimation using a t-distribution model with unequal variances compared triggered situational interest between conditions. The credible interval included zero, with only anecdotal evidence ("Bayes factor of 2.8") for a difference.

"For triggered situational interest, the posterior mean difference between the groups was β = 0.62, with a 95% credible interval ranging from −0.01 to 1.25. Although the 95% credible interval included zero, the Bayes factor of 2.8 provides anecdotal evidence for a group difference"

Adurangba Victor Oje 2026 (2)¶

Adurangba Victor Oje, Nathaniel J. Hunsu. (2026). Desktop virtual reality with scaffolded interaction and reflective explanation: effects on cognitive, motivational, and learning outcomes in engineering education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10722-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bayesian estimation compared maintained situational interest between conditions. The credible interval spanned zero ("β = 0.33 (95% CI [− 0.39, 1.05])"), indicating no clear evidence of a difference.

"In contrast, the comparison of maintained situational interest yielded a posterior mean difference of β = 0.33 (95% CI [− 0.39, 1.05]), indicating no clear evidence of a difference between groups"
