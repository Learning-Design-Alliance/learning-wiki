# Hospitals with nurse simulation-training participation above the median showed significant increases in vaginal deliveries, while hospitals at or below the median showed a significant increase only between Periods 2 and 3

> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In hospitals with more than 2.12 trained nurses per 100 deliveries, vaginal delivery percentages rose from 27.8% to 36.1% to 39.8% across periods (p<0.001 for both Period 2 and Period 3 versus Period 1; p=0.046 for Period 3 versus Period 2). [→ Molina Mariana Santos Alecrim 2024](#molina-mariana-santos-alecrim-2024)
`q2 i?` In hospitals with 2.12 or fewer trained nurses per 100 deliveries, the Period 2 versus Period 1 and Period 3 versus Period 1 differences were not significant (p=0.855; p=0.522), with significance only for Period 3 versus Period 2 (p=0.014). [→ Molina Mariana Santos Alecrim 2024 (2)](#molina-mariana-santos-alecrim-2024-2)
