Evidence¶

Dağ 2026¶

Dağ, E., Nal, M., Topuz, İ., Kılınç, H., Demir, Y. & Bektaş, G. (2026). Artificial intelligence perceptions and career anxiety among health sciences students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-42030-z

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multivariate regression (N = 821): the authors report that "age, gender, class level and programme choice did not demonstrate statistically significant predictive power" after covariate adjustment; equivalence was not tested.

"By contrast, variables such as age, gender, class level and programme choice did not demonstrate statistically significant predictive power once the effects of the other variables had been taken into account."
