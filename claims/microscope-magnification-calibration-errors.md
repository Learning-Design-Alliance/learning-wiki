# Students measuring magnification on their home-built microscope obtain incorrect magnifications when the CMOS camera is misplaced or the wrong tube lens is used, and the trial-and-error correction process promotes tinkering.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Incorrect placement of the CMOS camera relative to a 160 mm objective yields an incorrect pixel size and magnification (e.g., 2.2 µm and 2.3x instead of the correct 1.3 µm and 4x for a 4x objective). [→ Rachel Kemp 2016](#rachel-kemp-2016)
`q1 i?` With an infinity-corrected objective, using an incorrect tube lens focal length (100 mm or 175 mm instead of 180 mm) yields incorrect magnification (11.5x or 18.2x instead of 20x). [→ Rachel Kemp 2016](#rachel-kemp-2016)
