The article reports a mixed-methods study in which a genAI agent was placed into the small group processes of student teams during a session of a virtual interprofessional education methodology, explicitly to address groupthink; the article states it "addresses groupthink by introducing a generative artificial intelligence-based agent (genAI agent)". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
