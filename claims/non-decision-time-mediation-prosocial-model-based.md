## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Non-decision time was longer for self than for others (d = 0.47), and this difference partially mediated the self-other difference in model-based weights. [→ Yangchu Huang 2026](#yangchu-huang-2026)

## Evidence

### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

RLDDM parameter comparison showing "longer non-decision time" for self with printed effect size d = 0.47. A separate exploratory mediation analysis (Fig. 4c) "confirmed that self-other differences in non-decision time partially mediated the heightened self-related model-based weights", and remained significant when SVO scores were included as a control variable.

> "deciding for self was associated with longer non-decision time, t(91) = 4.71, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.02, 0.05], d = 0.47"
