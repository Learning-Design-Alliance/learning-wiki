## Evidence

### Al Rushood Maysoun 2020

Al Rushood Maysoun, Al-Eisa Amal. (2020). Factors predicting students’ performance in the final pediatrics OSCE. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236484

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman's rho correlational analysis of the 286-student record set. The final written exam and final OSCE showed "a positive correlation (r = 0.448)" (r = 0.448); per-year coefficients ranged from 0.356 to 0.570, all p < 0.001 (Table 2).

> "The results showed a positive correlation (r = 0.448) between the overall scores on the written final and the final OSCE examinations"

### Al Rushood Maysoun 2020 (2)

Al Rushood Maysoun, Al-Eisa Amal. (2020). Factors predicting students’ performance in the final pediatrics OSCE. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236484

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the full 286-student sample (Table 2). The end-of-block written correlation with the final OSCE was r = 0.473, per the printed "correlation coefficients of 0.508 and 0.473", and held "in each year and over the whole sample", per-year coefficients 0.428–0.574, all p < 0.001.

> "There were strong positive correlations between students’ performance on the in-training examinations (OSCE and written) and the final OSCE (correlation coefficients of 0.508 and 0.473, respectively)"
