Evidence¶

Nagy 1984¶

Nagy, William E.; Herman, Patricia A.; Anderson, Richard C.; Pearson, P. David. (1984). Learning Words from Context. Center for the Study of Reading, Technical Report No. 319, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED248470

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary interaction analysis in the same within-subject regression study: the prior-knowledge interaction reached significance only for the multiple-choice measure (F = 7.58, p < 0.01) and not for the interview measure, so the effect is measure-dependent.

"The interaction of learning from context with Prior Target Word Knowledge was significant for the multiple choice data, F = 7.58, p < 0.01; subjects learned more about words not previously known. There was no such trend in the interview analysis."
