### Yang Yuehang 2026

Yang Yuehang, Sheng Li, Huang Liang, Guo Qiannan, Hu Yuting, Shi Jiawei. (2026). Application of an AI-enabled personalized medical education model for improving clinical thinking among cardiovascular surgery trainees. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09993-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized single-center educational intervention with 79 trainees per group; after one semester the experimental group's mean theoretical examination score was 84.56 ± 5.07 versus 77.95 ± 9.04, a "6.62-point difference on a 100-point scale" reported as statistically significant (P < 0.001).
