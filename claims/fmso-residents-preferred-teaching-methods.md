### Rouis Sana 2025

Rouis Sana, Chouchene Imed, Gaddas Meriem, Naija Walid, Ben Saad Helmi, Lassoued Latifa. (2025). Residents' contribution to teaching at a Tunisian faculty of medicine. The Libyan Journal of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/19932820.2025.2597563

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Residents' opinions, not a tested comparison: this is the article's own summary of perceived efficiency from the survey (n = 105), with discussion-section percentages such as "discussion of clinical cases" favoured by 40.9%. The article offers no evidence beyond opinion for these ratings.

> "Respondants think that the following teaching methods are more efficient: examine and discuss videos, role-playing exercises with other residents, brainstorming with fellow residents, discussion of clinical cases, half-day or full-day workshops, Clinical-reasoning based learning (CRBL), and Case-based learning (CBL)."
