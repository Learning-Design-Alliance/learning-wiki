## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Prior to treatment, the convergent and divergent groups showed no significant difference in writing scores (p = 0.931) or AMTB motivation scores (p = 0.962). [→ Marashi 2015](#marashi-2015)

## Evidence

### Marashi 2015

Marashi, H., & Tahan-Shizari, P. (2015). Using convergent and divergent tasks to improve writing and language learning motivation. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 3(1), 99-117. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests on pretest scores confirmed group equivalence before treatment. Writing means were 12.25 (divergent) and 12.28 (convergent); AMTB means were 303.03 and 303.33 respectively. The article reports "no significant difference between the writing mean scores of the two groups at the outset," establishing that posttest differences could be attributed to the instructional treatments.

> "The results (t = -0.087, p = 0.931 > 0.05) indicate that there was no significant difference between the writing mean scores of the two groups at the outset."
