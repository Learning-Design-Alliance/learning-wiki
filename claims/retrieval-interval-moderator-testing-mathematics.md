## Evidence

### Murray 2023

Murray, E., Horner, A. J., & Göbel, S. M. (2023). A Meta-analytic Review of the Effectiveness of Spacing and Retrieval Practice for Mathematics Learning. https://osf.io/

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Exploratory moderator analysis within the retrieval subset, reported as nearing but not reaching significance. The authors report the restricted weighted mean as "g = 0.378 (se 0.108) (95% confidence interval [-0.013, 0.768])".

> "one factor that neared significance despite our small sample size was retrieval interval, which was coded as a univariate variable: less than one day or more than one day ((β=0.626, SE=0.162, p=.058))."
