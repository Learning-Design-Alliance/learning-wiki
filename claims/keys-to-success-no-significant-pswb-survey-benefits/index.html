Evidence¶

Crooke 2014¶

Crooke, A. H. D., & McFerran, K. S. (2014). Recommendations for the investigation and delivery of music programs aimed at achieving psychosocial wellbeing benefits in mainstream schools. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2014(1), 15-37. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061963

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-post survey study of the Keys to Success classroom music program with 20 intervention and 22 control Year 7 students, using 18 scales across eight PSWB domains. The article reports mean changes were "minimal and not statistically significant".

"Survey data showed no reported benefits. Improvement was reported on five of the 18 scales by the intervention class, and four by the control, yet these mean changes were minimal and not statistically significant."
