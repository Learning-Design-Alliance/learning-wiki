### Reading Corps program description and evaluation summary 2024

Reading Corps program description and evaluation summary. (2024). https://www.readingcorps.org

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Student-randomized evaluation in 10 Milwaukee, Wisconsin schools with 176 K-1 students (85% Black, 81% FRL). After one semester, students "significantly outperforming control students" on Letter Sound and Nonsense Word Fluency, with a near-null Oral Reading Fluency effect.

> "Literacy skills were assessed after one semester, with Reading Corps students significantly outperforming control students on Letter Sound Fluency (effect size = +0.47) and Nonsense Word Fluency (effect size = +0.43), and similarly on Oral Reading Fluency (effect size = +0.06)."
