## Evidence

### Mou Qiaoxing 2024

Mou Qiaoxing, Zhuang Jie, Wu Qunhong, Zhong Yaqin, Dai Qianqian, Cao Xin, Gao Yuexia, Lu Qingyun, Zhao Miaomiao. (2024). Social media addiction and academic engagement as serial mediators between social anxiety and academic performance among college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01635-7

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap analysis of the 2,661-student survey showed "the sequential pathway of social anxiety → social media addiction → academic engagement → academic performance was also significant (effect= -0.013, 95% CI: -0.018 to -0.008)". Social media addiction and academic engagement were negatively related (β = -0.160, p < 0.001).
