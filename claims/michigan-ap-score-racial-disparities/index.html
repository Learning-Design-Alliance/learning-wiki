Analysis of College Board AP Performance Report (2024-2025) score distributions for Michigan students. The report also prints that "only 6% of AP tests taken by white students received a 1, while 27.2% of AP exams taken by Black students received a 1," and 77% versus 46% scored 3 or higher.

"An EdTrust-Midwest analysis found that 16.6% of AP exams taken by white students earned a 5, but only 5.6% of AP exams taken by Black students earned a 5."
