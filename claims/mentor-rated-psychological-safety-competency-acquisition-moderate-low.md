`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Phase 1 web-based consensus conference: 173 mentors from 11 pan-European countries rated 20 competency items on a 5-point Likert scale. Descriptive results showed a "medium to low level of assimilation" with skills weakest (mean 3.3, SD 1.0). No inferential tests were reported.

> "the mean scores assigned by the mentors ranged from 2.9 to 3.8 points, suggesting a medium to low level of assimilation (global mean 3.4, SD 0.8; knowledge mean 3.4, SD 0.8; attitude mean 3.5, SD 0.8; skills mean 3.3, SD 1.0)"
