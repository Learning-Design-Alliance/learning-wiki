Evidence¶

Diamond Adele 2011¶

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports PATHS trains teachers to build children's self-control, emotion recognition, and interpersonal problem-solving, and teaches teachers techniques intended to generalize skills learned in PATHS lessons to other contexts during the school day.

"After a year of PATHS, 7–9 year-olds showed better inhibitory control and cognitive flexibility than control children (43). Children who showed greater inhibitory control at post-test showed fewer internalizing or externalizing behavior problems one year later."

Diamond Adele 2011 (2)¶

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a randomized-control trial with 18 of 35 Head Start classrooms assigned to CSRP, which provided teacher behavior-management training and stress-reduction workshops but no academic curricula.

"EFs (attention, inhibition, and experimenter-rated impulsivity) of 4-year-olds in CSRP classes improved over the year and significantly more so than did EFs of controls. CSRP didn’t affect delay of gratification, however. CSRP children improved in vocabulary, letter-naming, and math, and significantly more than controls. CSRP’s improvement of academic skills was mediated largely via its improvement of EFs."
