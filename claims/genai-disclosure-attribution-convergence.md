Document analysis of official university GenAI guidance pages across the 50-institution corpus. The convergence section reports that "Institutions commonly instruct students to acknowledge, cite, or otherwise document AI assistance" when AI use is permitted, framing transparency as the integrity norm and an instructional design practice.

> "Institutions commonly instruct students to acknowledge, cite, or otherwise document AI assistance rather than present AI-generated text, ideas, code, or outputs as entirely their own"
