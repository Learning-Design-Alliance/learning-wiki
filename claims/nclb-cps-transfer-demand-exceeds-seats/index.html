Sebring 2003¶

Sebring, Penny Bender. (2003). Research Agenda 2004-2008. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The agenda's NCLB section reports Chicago's implementation: 270,000 students were eligible to transfer and "About 19,000 requested transfers, but space was available for only about 1,000 students." These are descriptive counts, not effect sizes.

"In 2003-04, 270,000 students, or over 50 percent, were eligible to transfer. About 19,000 requested transfers, but space was available for only about 1,000 students."

Sebring 2003 (2)¶

Sebring, Penny Bender. (2003). Research Agenda 2004-2008. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The same section reports instability in school classification under the law: "Forty schools that could receive transfers in the first year did not meet second-year standards," illustrating classification churn in the first two years of implementation.

"Forty schools that could receive transfers in the first year did not meet second-year standards and will have to give their students the opportunity to transfer out."
