### Alrehaily Ali 2026

Alrehaily Ali, Alomair Abdulmohsen M., Kattan Thuraya, Alrumaihi Nouf, Carrick Frederick Robert. (2026). Educational activities as a pathway to postgraduate standardization: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08922-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 124 curricula using Excel-based open coding by two coders with third-reviewer adjudication; coding produced "four themes and 10 subthemes" spanning didactic, interactive, clinical, authentic, scholar, and self-learning categories.

> "Data from the reviewed documents were coded and analysed, revealing four themes and 10 subthemes (Table 2). In addition, a review of available data and literature was used to generate recommendations to improve the organization by standardizing the delivery of EAs."

### Alrehaily Ali 2026 (2)

Alrehaily Ali, Alomair Abdulmohsen M., Kattan Thuraya, Alrumaihi Nouf, Carrick Frederick Robert. (2026). Educational activities as a pathway to postgraduate standardization: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08922-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-curriculum comparison found identical activities labelled inconsistently; the article contrasts a Family Medicine curriculum describing "journal reviews" with a Pain Medicine Fellowship curriculum planning "a monthly journal club activity."

> "EAs were widely shared between programs, although the extent of sharing varied. Additionally, the same EAs received different types of labels in the analysed curricula. More specifically, journal review, journal club, and systematic review were inconsistently named across distinct curricula, even though they shared the same intended outcome."
