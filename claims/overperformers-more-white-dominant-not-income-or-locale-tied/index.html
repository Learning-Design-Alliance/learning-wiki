Yamada 2024¶

Yamada, H. (2024). New Normal in Early Elementary Mathematics Learning: Part II - School Level Variation in Growth in Grades 1 and 2. E4 Center, Northwestern University. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/06/25/new-normal-in-early-elementary-mathematics-learning-part-ii-school-level-variation-in-growth-in-grades-1-and-2/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Categorical analysis of 6,348 Grade 1 and 6,350 Grade 2 schools segmented by growth status, using Census and NCES demographic variables. The printed contrast shows "37% of the schools were from White-dominant schools" among overperformers versus 24% (average) and 16% (underperforming).

"In Grade 1, for example, 37% of the schools were from White-dominant schools with 75% or higher of White students, in contrast with 24% with the average growth status and 16% with the underperforming status."
