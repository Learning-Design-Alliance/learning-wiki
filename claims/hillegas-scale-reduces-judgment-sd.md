`q2 i?` Judgments made with the Hillegas Scale show reduced standard deviations in all except two compositions, averaging 11.2 without and 8.9 with the scale. [→ Kate Gordon 1916](#kate-gordon-1916)

## Evidence

### Kate Gordon 1916

Kate Gordon. (1916). A Class Experiment with the Hillegas Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of Table I from the class experiment with forty-one judges grading twenty-five compositions found "The standard deviations are reduced in the cases of all except two of the compositions," with average S.D. falling from 11.2 to 8.9. Only descriptive statistics are reported.

> "The standard deviations are reduced in the cases of all except two of the compositions. The average S. D. without the scale being 11.2 and the average S. D. with the scale 8.9."
