### Pedersen Tom Andre MD 2026

Pedersen Tom Andre MD, Mori Yuichi Prof., Botteri Edoardo Dr., Engjom Trond Dr., Seip Birgitte Dr., Dimcevski Georg Gjorgji Dr., Havre Roald Flesland Prof. (2026). Learning and deskilling effects of artificial intelligence in colonoscopy among endoscopists with different levels of experience: a pragmatic, prospective trial. Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2858-7084

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In a prospective, multicenter, registry-based pragmatic clinical trial of 5013 colonoscopies, generalized linear mixed models showed CADe use was "significantly associated with an increased PDR-5 among inexperienced endoscopists" (OR 1.43, 95%CI 1.11–1.84).

> "CADe temporarily increased PDR-5 among inexperienced endoscopists from phase 1 to 2 (31.9% [216/678] to 39.5% [257/651]; odds ratio [OR] 1.43, 95%CI 1.11–1.84)."
