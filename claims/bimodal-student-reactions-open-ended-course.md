### Marco Cosentino Lagomarsino 2022 (2)

Marco Cosentino Lagomarsino, Guglielmo Pacifico, Valerio Firmano, Edoardo Bella, Pietro Benzoni, Jacopo Grilli, Federico Bassetti, Fabrizio Capuani, Pietro Cicuta, and Marco Gherardi. (2022). Remote teaching data-driven physical modeling through a COVID-19 open-ended data challenge. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac79e1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac79e1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same questionnaire analysis of the course cohort: “19/21 students declared that they were interested in the topics (13/21 very interested)”, while only “3/21 declared that they felt strongly about this”.

> "Conversely, 19/21 students declared that they were interested in the topics (13/21 very interested), 14/21 students felt that the main instructor motivates their interest toward the topic, but only 3/21 declared that they felt strongly about this."
