## Evidence

### Duan D  2025

Duan D , Gan MJS , Lim LZ , Foong KWC . (2025). Simulator practice of temporary anchorage device (TAD) insertion on inter‐radicular sites improves learning and confidence in dental residency. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of audio-recorded think-aloud placement rounds for the 9-person control and 9-person intervention groups. The article reports control residents "focused more on establishing their insertion pathway and placement technique in round 2".

> "Due to this, they focused more on establishing their insertion pathway and placement technique in round 2 than they do on other factors. Contrary to this, residents in the intervention group were more certain about the insertion path they selected when the sensor was not sounded."
