In the same RCT, TAPSE non-inferiority analysis showed a "mean difference 0.49 mm; 95% CI -1.4 to 2.6 mm", within the predefined 4 mm margin. Two standard-group participants could not obtain the A4C view and were excluded.

> "For TAPSE, the measurement was 4.0 mm (SD 3.0) off the expert value in the XR group and 3.5 mm (SD 4.3) in the standard training group (mean difference 0.49 mm; 95% CI -1.4 to 2.6 mm)."
