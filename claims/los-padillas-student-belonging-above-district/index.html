Survey results reported in the Progress section from the 2023–24 APS Skills, Habits, and Mindsets Student Survey. "66% of students reported a sense of belonging, well above students in the rest of the district (44%)."

"On the 2023–24 APS Skills, Habits, and Mindsets Student Survey, Los Padillas students’ results for perceived perseverance, self-efficacy, and social awareness were above the district average. Importantly, 66% of students reported a sense of belonging, well above students in the rest of the district (44%)."
