## Evidence

### Gwynne 2022

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of high school fourth-quarter grades in district-run and Options CPS schools across pre-pandemic and pandemic terms. The report finds As "much higher than in pre-pandemic years (33 percent)" and a rise in Fs from 6 to 8 percent, with the F increase appearing only in the spring term.

> "In spring 2021, 41 percent of high school grades recorded as As in spring 2021, which was much higher than in pre-pandemic years (33 percent) and similar to spring 2020 (43 percent). But there was also a slight rise in the percentage of grades that were Fs, relative to pre-pandemic years, from 6 to 8 percent of grades being Fs."
