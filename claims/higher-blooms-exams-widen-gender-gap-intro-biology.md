## Evidence

### Wright 2016

Wright, C. D., Eddy, S. L., Wenderoth, M. P., Abshire, E., Blankenbiller, M., & Brownell, S. E. (2016). Cognitive Difficulty and Format of Exams Predicts Gender and Socioeconomic Gaps in Exam Performance of Students in Introductory Biology Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-12-0246

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel model analysis of 4810 students' scores on 87 exams across 3 years of an introductory biology series, controlling for incoming GPA, difficulty, and instructor. The model-averaged gender × Bloom's interaction was significant (β = −0.0418 ± 0.0205 [SE], p value = 0.0416), while the "main effect of gender averaged across the model set was not significant."

> "the main effect of gender averaged across the model set was not significant (β = −0.00295 ± 0.0115 [SE], p value = 0.798; Table 2 and Figure 1a), but the interaction between gender and weighted Bloom's index was significant (β = −0.0418 ± 0.0205 [SE], p value = 0.0416"
