Evidence¶

Goetz 2006¶

Goetz, T., Frenzel, A. C., Pekrun, R., Hall, N. C., & Lüdtke, O. (2006). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analysis of between-domain homogeneity per emotion construct in the two grade-level samples. "Enjoyment and anxiety showed the weakest" relations and pride the strongest; selected pairwise differences were statistically significant but the authors characterize them as relatively weak.

"Enjoyment and anxiety showed the weakest, and pride the strongest, between-domains relations in both samples. However, all of the differences were weak, even though some were statistically significant."
