## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After propensity-score matching on pre-test scores, post-test scores did not differ significantly across the three hint-writing conditions, though the AI-assistance condition had the lowest average post-test score. [→ Anjali Singh 2026](#anjali-singh-2026)

## Evidence

### Anjali Singh 2026

Anjali Singh, Christopher Brooks, Warren Li, Juho Kim, Xu Wang. (2026). Hint-Writing with Deferred AI Assistance: Fostering Critical Engagement in Data Science Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.19931](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.19931)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on post-test scores of 55 students (20 Baseline, 20 AI-assistance, 15 Deferred) selected via propensity score matching because pre-test differences were marginally significant (p = 0.09). The article reports "not statistically significant (p = 0.18)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "students in theAI-assistance condition had the lowest average post-test score of 4.75, while students in the Baseline and Deferred AI-assistance conditions had higher average post-test scores of 5.70 and 5.67 respectively. However, an ANOVA revealed that the difference in post-test scores of students between conditions was not statistically significant (p = 0.18)."
