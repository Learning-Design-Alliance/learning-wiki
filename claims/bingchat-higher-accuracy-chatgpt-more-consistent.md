### Xuan-Quy Dao 2023

Xuan-Quy Dao, Ngoc-Bich Le, Xuan-Dung Phan, Bac-Bien Ngo, The-Duy Vo. (2023). Evaluation of ChatGPT and Microsoft Bing AI Chat Performances on Physics Exams of Vietnamese National High School Graduation Examination. arXiv preprint. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance evaluation per year and averages (Table II, Fig. 3) on the 200-question VNHSGE physics evaluation set, ChatGPT Feb 13 version and BingChat April 15 version. The article reports BingChat obtained 66% versus ChatGPT's 61%, and that "ChatGPT is more consistent than BingChat."

> "ChatGPT’s performance is 61% (from 57.5% to 65% ), while BingChat obtained 66% (from 72.5% to 55% ). Max performed consistently well, 78.5%, while Min’s performance is 48.5%. Interestingly, ChatGPT outperformed BingChat only in 2019."
