## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a case-study interview with no written or verbal guidance, a 6th-grade student began interacting with the sim in less than 10 seconds and verbalized an attempt to explain the skater's motion. [→ Podolefsky 2013](#podolefsky-2013)

## Evidence

### Podolefsky 2013

Podolefsky, N. S., Moore, E. B., & Perkins, K. K. (2013). Implicit scaffolding in interactive simulations: Design strategies to support multiple educational goals. http://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/energy-skate-park-basics

`q2 · i1`

Qualitative microanalysis of one think-aloud interview from a set of nine middle-school student interviews conducted on the final ESPB sim, with no external guidance. The student's first interactions were changing tracks and dropping the skater, and she verbalized that motion "transfers" uphill, signaling sense making.

> "Notice how quickly the student began interacting – in less than 10 seconds – and trying to make sense of what was happening in the sim."
