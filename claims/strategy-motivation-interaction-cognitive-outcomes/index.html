Evidence¶

Bambang Hariadi 2016¶

Bambang Hariadi, Tutut Wurijanto. (2016). Influence of Web Based Cooperative Learning Strategy and Achiever Motivation on Student Study Outcome. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3, pp. 189~199. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Omnibus two-path ANOVA hypothesis test at 0.05 significance (Table 5: Learning Strategy*Archievement Motivation F = 3.841, Sig. .027). The discussion concludes outcome "is not only caused by application of learning strategy, but also their achiever motivation"; no effect size printed.

"There is influence of interaction between learnin g strategy (web -based STAD type of cooperative and textual STAD type of cooperative) and students’ achiever motivation on learning result of Organizational Behaviors subject in cognitive aspect."
