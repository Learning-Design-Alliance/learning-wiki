## Evidence

### Sarah Bowen 2012

Sarah Bowen, Kevin Haworth, Joel Grow, Mavis Tsai, and Robert Kohlenberg. (2012). Interpersonal Mindfulness Informed by Functional Analytic Psychotherapy: Findings from a Pilot Randomized Trial. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, Vol. 7, No. 2-3. [doi:10.1037/h0100931](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0100931)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the pilot randomized trial, ANCOVA on the single-item postcourse connectedness measure showed "differences between control and both the intrapersonal ( p = .018) and interpersonal group (p = .003)", with baseline outcome levels covaried.

> "Significant differences were found on the single-item postcourse measure of connectedness, F(2.97) = 5.34, p = .006. Specifically, post hoc Tukey HSD test revealed differences between control and both the intrapersonal ( p = .018) and interpersonal group (p = .003)."
