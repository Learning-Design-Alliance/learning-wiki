# In the elective course, posttest scores improved significantly overall and for the Magic Mirror and Theory groups, but no significant pre-post difference was recorded for the Anatomage group.

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Across all 72 elective-course participants, scores rose from 48.87% at pretest to 56.77% at posttest, a significant difference with η 2 = 0.08. [→ Bork F 2019](#bork-f-2019)
`q2 i2` The Magic Mirror group (η 2 = 0.08) and the Theory group (η 2 = 0.10) each achieved significantly higher posttest scores. [→ Bork F 2019 (2)](#bork-f-2019-2)
`q2 i?` The Anatomage group showed no statistically significant difference between pretest and posttest scores. [→ Bork F 2019 (3)](#bork-f-2019-3)

## Evidence

### Bork F 2019

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest analysis in the elective anatomy and radiology course, with three groups sorted for comparable pretest and MRT scores. All participants combined improved significantly: "These differences were significant at the P < 0.001 level (F(1,148) = 13.56, η 2 = 0.08)".

> "For all participants combined, the scores were 48.87 ± 13.17% during the pretest, whereas during the posttest results increased to 56.77 ± 17.17%. These differences were significant at the P < 0.001 level (F(1,148) = 13.56, η 2 = 0.08)."

### Bork F 2019 (2)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the elective course groups of 24 participants each. "participants achieved significantly higher scores during the posttest in both the Magic Mirror group" and the Theory group, with the printed η 2 values of 0.08 and 0.10.

> "participants achieved significantly higher scores during the posttest in both the Magic Mirror group (F(1,48) = 4.34, P < 0.05, η 2 = 0.08) as well as in the Theory group (F(1,48) = 5.08, P < 0.05, η 2 = 0.10)"

### Bork F 2019 (3)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result for the Anatomage group (N = 24) in the same elective-course repeated-measures ANOVA: "no significant differences between pre‐ and posttest scores for participants in the Anatomage group". No effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there were no significant differences between pre‐ and posttest scores for participants in the Anatomage group (F(1,48) = 3.97, P = 0.52, ns)."
