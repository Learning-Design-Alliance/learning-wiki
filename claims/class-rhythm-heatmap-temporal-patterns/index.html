Germán Arroyo 2026¶

Germán Arroyo, Luis López, and Juan Carlos Torres. (2026). Securing the Sandbox: A Rootless Containerized Framework for Process-Oriented Monitoring in Computer Graphics Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05929 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.05929

q2 · i? · design · r2

Class rhythm heatmap analysis (Figure 3) of the pilot cohort's temporal interaction density across hours of the day and days of the week. The temporal signatures suggest "a non-traditional student body that primarily engages with the platform outside of standard daytime hours—likely balancing academic coursework with professional commitments."

"Peak activity consistently occurs during the evening hours (15:00 to 20:00), with Wednesdays exhibiting the highest concentration of interactions. Furthermore, the cohort demonstrates notable late-night to early-morning activity (00:00 to 05:00 and 20:00 to 00:00) specifically on Saturdays."
