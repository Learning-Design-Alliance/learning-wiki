## Evidence

### Wolf 2013

Wolf, S., Aber, J. L., & Morris, P. A. (2013). Understanding Impact Variation in Family Rewards: The Role of Schools and Neighborhoods on a Conditional Cash Transfer Program. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564103

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The abstract reports subgroup findings from the Family Rewards RCT, citing Riccio et al. (2010), on high school academic outcomes including "school attendance, course credits, grade advancement, and standardized test results".

> "While few impacts were observed for the full sample of high school students' academic outcomes (i.e., school attendance, course credits, grade advancement, and standardized test results), there were no impacts for those who were not academically prepared at baseline and strong impacts among those who were better academically prepared at baseline (Riccio et al., 2010)."
