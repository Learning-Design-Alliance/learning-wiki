## Evidence

### Guo 2025

Guo, Z., Fryer, L. K., & Shum, A. (2025). Utilizing generative AI to promote high school students' personal relevance to math and interest in the math class: An intervention. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Fully-forward latent-variable SEM (good fit: CFI .941, RMSEA .061) on the 218-student longitudinal data across five weeks; the model showed the E2 effect "positively predicted the participants' relevance as identification with math (time point 2)" and carried a one-week indirect effect of 0.108.

> "E2 intervention (collective generative AI group) positively predicted the participants' relevance as identification with math (time point 2) (β = 0.18, p<.01).  Mediated by the participants' relevance as identification with math after the intervention, the E2 intervention (collective generative AI group) further predicted the participants' relevance as identification to a specific math lesson one week after the intervention (time point 3) (indirect effect = 0.108, p <0.05)."
