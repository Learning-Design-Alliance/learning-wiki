Evidence¶

Leonard Tetzlaff 2021¶

Leonard Tetzlaff, Florian Schmiedek & Garvin Brod. (2021). Developing Personalized Education: A Dynamic Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09570-w

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports meta-analytic evidence that ITS, which model learners' psychological states to provide personalized instruction, increase learning gains compared with regular instruction. No pooled effect size is printed in the quoted passage.

"Several meta-analyses have shown the effectiveness of these systems across different domains and contexts (Corbett 2001;Ma et al. 2014; Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper 2014), leading to the conclusion that dynamic student modeling and subsequent adaptations are an effective mechanism for promoting learning gains."
