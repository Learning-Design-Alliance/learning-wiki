Hsin-Yun Wang & Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun. (2026). Understanding online knowledge communities via social networks and self-regulation in synchronous VR co-creation for distance learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev, 74, 1037–1061. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10588-0

Successive cross-sectional one-mode SNA over four weeks (Table 1) of the two groups' Google Classroom conversation networks. The quote prints the diverging week-3-to-4 trajectories; no effect size was reported.

"However, the opposite trend appeared over the last two weeks; as the average linkage degree (4.5 → 5), the density (0.214 → 0.238), and the reciprocity (0.97–1) in 3D VR co-creation continued to climb, those in 2D digital co-creation began falling (linkage degree: 2.955 → 2.591; density: 0.141 → 0.123; reciprocity: 0.892–0.807)."
