q2 · i? · associational · r3

Chi-square test of independence across 56 school-based sites comparing timing of tutoring (during school only, both, outside school hours) with weekly frequency. More than three-quarters of during-school-only sites (78 percent) exceeded the two-day minimum versus less than one-quarter (23 percent) of out-of-school-hours sites.

"Chi-square tests of independence conducted to examine associations between pairs of tutoring implementation features found a significant association between the timing and the frequency of tutoring ( χ2(2) = 12.83, p < .01)."
