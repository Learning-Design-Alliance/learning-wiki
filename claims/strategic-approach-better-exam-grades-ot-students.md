# Higher strategic approach scores are associated with better exam grades among first-year occupational therapy students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After adjusting for all variables, better exam grades were associated with higher scores on the strategic approach scale (β = 0.31, p < 0.001). [→ Bonsaksen T. 2021](#bonsaksen-t-2021)

## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). Associations between occupational therapy students’ academic performance and their study approaches and perceptions of the learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple linear regression of GPA on demographics, CEQ learning environment scales, and ASSIST approach scales in 174 first-year occupational therapy students. The strategic approach scale showed "higher scores on strategic approach (β = 0.31, p < 0.001)"; no standardized effect size beyond β was printed.

> "Among the study approach scales, better exam grades were associated with higher scores on strategic approach (β = 0.31, p < 0.001), and lower scores on surface approach (β = -0.20, p < 0.01)."
