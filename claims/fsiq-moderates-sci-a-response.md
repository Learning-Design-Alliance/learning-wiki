# FSIQ moderated SCI-A response: lower-FSIQ students benefited on awareness, communication, and motivation, while higher-FSIQ students fared worse in SCI-A

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The FSIQ × condition interaction was significant for the Communication subscale; at lower FSIQ (85), SCI-A students showed more positive social behavior on awareness (d = −0.40), communication (d = −0.46), and motivation (d = −0.27). [→ Janine P. Stichter 2018](#janine-p-stichter-2018)

## Evidence

### Janine P. Stichter 2018

Janine P. Stichter, Melissa J. Herzog, Stephen P. Kilgus, and Alexander M. Schoemann. (2018). Exploring the Moderating Effects of Cognitive Abilities on Social Competence Intervention Outcomes. Behavior Modification. https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445517698654

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis of the CRT probing simple slopes at FSIQ 85 and 115. The interaction was significant for Communication (p = .037); simple-slope effect sizes at low FSIQ were d = −0.40 to −0.46, and at high FSIQ SCI-A students had higher scores relative to BAU students.

> "For groups characterized by lower FSIQ, students in SCI-A had lower T2 SRS-2 scores relative to BAU students, indicating more positive social behavior within the SCI-A group, on social awareness (d = −0.40), communication (d = −0.46), and motivation (d = −0.27)."
