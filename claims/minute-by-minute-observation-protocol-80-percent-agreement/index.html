Kurki, A., Wang, W., Li, Y., & Poduska, J. (2013). Measurement of Child Behavior via Classroom Observations in the Good Behavior Game Professional Development Models Randomized Control Trial. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563300

Reliability analysis of the observation protocol used in the GBG PD Models trial, first cohort. The article reports percentage agreement: "the observers reached 80% or higher agreement levels for student behavior". No effect size is printed.
