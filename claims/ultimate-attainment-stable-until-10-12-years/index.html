Evidence¶

Hartshorne Joshua K. 2018¶

Hartshorne Joshua K., Tenenbaum Joshua B., Pinker Steven. (2018). A critical period for second language acquisition: Evidence from 2/3 million English speakers. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6559801/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multivariate adaptive regression splines fitted to 11,371 immersion learners with at least 30 years of experience and at most 70 years old. Slopes "B = −0.009" before age 12 versus "B = −0.06" after, per the printed coefficients.

"Immersion learners showed only a minimal decline in ultimate attainment until an age of first exposure of 12 years (B = −0.009; 0.01 SDs/year), after which the decline became significantly steeper (B = −0.06; 0.07 SDs/year)."

Hartshorne Joshua K. 2018 (2)¶

Hartshorne Joshua K., Tenenbaum Joshua B., Pinker Steven. (2018). A critical period for second language acquisition: Evidence from 2/3 million English speakers. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6559801/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same MARS analysis applied to 29,708 non-immersion learners with at least 30 years of experience. Proficiency "showed no decline" from exposure ages 4 to 9 (B = 0.01), then a steep decline (B = −0.06).

"Non-immersion learners showed similar results: From 4 years to 9 years, proficiency showed no decline (in fact it increased slightly; B = 0.01; 0.01 SDs/year), followed by a steep decline (B = −0.06; 0.07 SDs/year)."
