## Evidence

### Lehmann Stine 2024

Lehmann Stine, Hysing Mari, Sivertsen Børge. (2024). Academic Performance, Health and Support Needs: Comparing Foster Care Alumni and Peers in Higher Education in Norway. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph21111470

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison within the SHoT2022 national survey (n = 508 foster care alumni vs 59,036 general students), reported in Results §3.2 with chi-square tests. The study found "25.0% of the foster care alumni students expressed an unmet need for special academic assistance" versus 10.6% of peers; no effect size was printed.

> "Furthermore, a higher percentage of students from foster or RYC reported receiving special assistance or accommodations for their studies (11.4%) compared to those not from such backgrounds (6.7%). Notably, 25.0% of the foster care alumni students expressed an unmet need for special academic assistance, which was significantly higher than the 10.6% reported by their non-foster peers."
