Zhang Zhen; Fan Siyu; Qi Chunhui. (2026). Teacher or Artificial Intelligence? The Effect of Decision-Making Agent on Junior High School Students’ Decision Acceptance: A Moderated Mediation Model. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071227

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the 2×2 between-subjects vignette experiment (N = 250 seventh-graders), a 2×2 factorial ANOVA with decision acceptance as the dependent variable showed a significant main effect of decision-making agent, F(1,246) = 40.61, partial η2 = 0.14, with "significantly higher decision acceptance in the teacher condition" than the AI condition.

> "Participants exhibited significantly higher decision acceptance in the teacher condition (M = 4.48, SD = 1.50) compared with the AI condition (M = 3.47, SD = 1.16), p < 0.001."
