## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Full HPSF schools showed a small but significant reading comprehension advantage over control schools in year 1 (ES = 0.28, p < 0.001), which was no longer significant by year 4 (ES = 0.12, p = 0.092). [→ van Engelen Bo H. W. 2026](#van-engelen-bo-h-w-2026)

## Evidence

### van Engelen Bo H. W. 2026

van Engelen Bo H. W., Hahnraths Marla T. H., Winkens Bjorn, Schils Trudie, van Schayck Onno C. P. (2026). Food for thought? The effects of the Healthy Primary School of the Future on children's educational outcomes. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334638

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

MMRM analysis of national standardised reading comprehension tests; the year-1 effect "corresponded to an ES of 0.28" and the article reports the effect did not sustain over time, no longer being significant by year 4. Reading scores were not corrected for baseline due to missing values.

> "the full HPSF group showed only a significant intervention effect compared to the control group in year 1 (corrected mean difference = 2.7, 95%CI 1.2 to 4.2, p < 0.001), which corresponded to an ES of 0.28"
