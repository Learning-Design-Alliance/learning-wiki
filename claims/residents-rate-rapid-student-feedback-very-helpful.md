### Katz-Sidlow Rachel J. 2016

Katz-Sidlow Rachel J., Baer Tamar G., Gershel Jeffrey C. (2016). Providing rapid feedback to residents on their teaching skills: an educational strategy for contemporary trainees. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.56dc.908a

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Resident survey within a Resident-as-Teacher program study (September 2013 to September 2015); 20 PGY-2 residents who led 44 teaching sessions completed a 4-point survey, with a 73% (32/44) response rate. The article reports "Ninety-four percent (30/32)" rated the process very helpful; no effect size is printed.
