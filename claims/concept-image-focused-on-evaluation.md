## Evidence

### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Pretest concept-image question (N=30, Table I) asking students to write what they think of for ∇A, ∇·A and ∇×A. Correct concepts: 10%, 0%, 10%. Two students thought the divergence of a vector field is again a vector field; a student wrote the gradient of a scalar is again a scalar.

> "Only a few students gave a description of the operators we deemed conceptual."
