## Evidence

### Yamamoto R 2026

Yamamoto R, Nojiri T, Yoshida D, Oyamada Y, Fukutoku A, Kon K. (2026). Accuracy of dynamic computer-assisted implant surgery among novice operators using three different patient registration methods: an in-vitro study. International Journal of Implant Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40729-026-00666-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In vitro experiment with five novice operators placing 25 implants per registration group in maxillary models; mean angular deviation was 3.16° (ND) versus 1.18° (XC) and 0.97° (XM), with the ND group exhibiting "significantly greater angular deviation than both the XC group" and the XM group.

> "Post hoc analysis using Dunn's test with Bonferroni correction showed that the ND group exhibited significantly greater angular deviation than both the XC group (adjusted p = 0.000028) and the XM group (adjusted p = 0.00000019)."
