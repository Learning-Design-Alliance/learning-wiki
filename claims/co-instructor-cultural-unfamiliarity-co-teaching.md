### Aliakbari 2012

Aliakbari, M. & Bazyar, A. (2012). Exploring the Impact of Parallel Teaching on General Language Proficiency of EFL Learners. Journal of Pan-Pacific Association of Applied Linguistics, 16(1), 55-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979922

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured individual interview with the co-instructor conducted after the parallel-teaching experience. He stated that "both teachers and students are unfamiliar with co-teaching models" and that co-presence of instructors seemed unnatural and culturally threatened teacher authority.

> "In Iran both teachers and students are unfamiliar with co-teaching models; it takes time to be set as a bone in the educational system."
