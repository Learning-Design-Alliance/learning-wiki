## Evidence

### Vaughn 2017

Vaughn, S., Martinez, L. R., Wanzek, J., Roberts, G., Swanson, E., & Fall, A.-M. (2017). Improving Content Knowledge and Comprehension for English Language Learners: Findings From a Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(1), 22–34. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000069

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Class-randomized within-teacher RCT with 1,629 eighth-grade students in 94 sections; three-level regression models on the ASK. The intervention coefficient was 3.58, p < .01, with a printed effect size of ES = .40. Both ELs and non-ELs in treatment outperformed comparison students.

> "For the ASK, the estimated coefficient for Intervention (/H9253010 /H11005 3.58, p /H11021.01, Effect Size (ES) /H11005.40) represents the amount by which the intercept ( /H9253000 /H1100521.74) increases for non-ELs in treatment classes with 10% ELs."
