Qualitative reception report from approximately 400 participants in LLMs Unplugged sessions at the Australian National University, in groups of five to fifty, ranging from school-age students to senior executives. The author reports participants “consistently report that the hands-on activity helps them to build a new mental model of how LLMs work.”

> "Participants consistently report that the hands-on activity helps them to build a new mental model of how LLMs work. The most common insight people articulate is that LLMs are “just” doing probability and randomness at scale"
