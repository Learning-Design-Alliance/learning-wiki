### Yeh M.-L. 2025

Yeh M.-L., Chang B.-Y., Dai H.-D., & Chung M.-H. (2025). Effect of training program on evidence-based practice competencies in hospital nurses in Taiwan: A quasi-experimental study. The Journal of Nursing Research, 33(5), e412. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000701

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A linear random-effects model analyzed within-group change for exposure A (n=20) in the quasi-experimental study. The article prints mean differences with 95% confidence intervals for Science and Assess but no standardized effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The exposure A group showed improved performance in all five domains, with the most improvement shown in Science (mean difference [MD]=29.93; 95% CI [20.54, 39.32]), followed by Assess (MD=25.04; 95% CI [16.63, 33.45])."

### Yeh M.-L. 2025 (2)

Yeh M.-L., Chang B.-Y., Dai H.-D., & Chung M.-H. (2025). Effect of training program on evidence-based practice competencies in hospital nurses in Taiwan: A quasi-experimental study. The Journal of Nursing Research, 33(5), e412. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000701

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA with Scheffe test among the three groups (exposure A n=20, exposure B n=20, control n=40) in the quasi-experimental study. The comparison names exposure A as the better performer against both exposure B and control, with p<.05 and no effect size printed.

> "A significant difference between pretest and posttest was observed in terms of the change in scores in all five domains of the HS-EBP questionnaire among the three groups, with the exposure A group performing better than the exposure B and control groups (p<.05)."
