## Evidence

### Montalbano V 2014

Montalbano V. (2014). An inquiry-based laboratory on friction. ICPE-EPEC 2013 Proceedings. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.2307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.2307)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative exploratory laboratory with two high school classes (44 students, age 16–17). Students guessed and drew a qualitative graph of friction force versus roughness; the article reports "All students draw essentially a direct proportional behaviour" and notes the sketches resembled those found in an analogous higher-education study.

> "All students draw essentially a direct proportional behaviour for friction force intensity vs. surface roughness"

### Montalbano V 2014 (2)

Montalbano V. (2014). An inquiry-based laboratory on friction. ICPE-EPEC 2013 Proceedings. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.2307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.2307)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

After the full qualitative path, students could redraw their friction-versus-roughness graph. The article reports 74% revised their graph while "only few of them (4%)" produced the graph consistent with all previous observations and phenomenological evidence.

> "The most part of students (74%) revised their graph, but only few of them (4%) were able to sketch the one consistent with all previous observations"
