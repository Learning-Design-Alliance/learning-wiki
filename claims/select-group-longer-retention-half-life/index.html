Evidence¶

Utkarsh Upadhyay 2021¶

Utkarsh Upadhyay, Graham Lancashire, Christoph Moser and Manuel Gomez-Rodriguez. (2021). Large-scale randomized experiments reveals that machine learning-based instruction helps people memorize more effectively. npj Science of Learning 6:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00105-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive medians from the randomized controlled trial comparing the 'select' group against 'random' and 'difficulty' groups. The article reports "the corresponding median increase in the median half-lives was ~92% and ~40%"; no standardized effect size or significance test is printed for these medians.

"the median decrease in the median empirical forgetting rate for learners in the ‘select’ group was ~48% and ~40% when compared to learners in the ‘random’ and ‘difﬁculty’ groups, respectively, and the corresponding median increase in the median half-lives was ~92% and ~40%."
