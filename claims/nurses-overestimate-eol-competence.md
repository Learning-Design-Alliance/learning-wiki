Authors' interpretation comparing baseline self-report percentages with the baseline quiz pass rate in the same cohort; the article cites the Dunning-Kruger effect as a partial explanation. This is an observational contrast within the QI project, not a manipulated comparison.

> "While nearly 80% of nurses rated themselves competent or confident in certain EOL care domains, only 15% successfully passed the modified Palliative Care Quiz, underscoring the significant gap between perceived and actual competence."
