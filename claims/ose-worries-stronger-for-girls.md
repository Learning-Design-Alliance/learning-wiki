Multiple-group SEM comparison of 932 student-parent pairs tested sex differences in path coefficients. The article reports the OSE→WOR path "signi ﬁcantly stronger" for girls with a difference coefficient of 0.35, p < 0.05; no standardized effect size is printed for the difference itself.
