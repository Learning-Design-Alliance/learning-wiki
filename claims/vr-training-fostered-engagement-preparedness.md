## Evidence

### Chartash David 2026

Chartash David, Jones Geraint, Mergen Marvin, Sanverdi Mustafa, Sharma Vanshika, Park Sohee, Voinescu Alexandra, Jacobs Chris. (2026). Medical Students’ Experiences With Virtual Reality Simulation Training: Qualitative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/74301

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis under the ITEM motivation domain of interviews with 11 medical students. Participants described self-directed decision-making and confidence gains; "practicing clinical protocols in the virtual environment enhanced their preparedness for real-life scenarios."

> "Another key outcome of the training was an increased sense of preparedness as future medical professionals. Several participants noted that practicing clinical protocols in the virtual environment enhanced their preparedness for real-life scenarios."
