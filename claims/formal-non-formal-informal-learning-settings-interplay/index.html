Duraiappah 2021¶

F., Mizala, A., Mochizuki, Y., Okano, K., Piech, C., Pugh, K., Ramaswamy, R., Singh, N. C. & Vickers, E. (2021). q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

A descriptive statement of the ISEE Assessment's third key question (where we learn), defining the learning settings whose interplay the assessment will evaluate. The commentary gives the Covid-19 shift to online learning as an urgent example.

"We evaluate the interplay between the formal (e.g., at school), non-formal (e.g., at work) and informal (i.e., unintentional such as via peer interactions) learning settings, face-to-face and online learning and the impact it has on learning."
