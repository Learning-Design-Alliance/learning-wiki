Would you let a humanoid play storytelling with your child? A usability study on LLM-powered narrative Human-Robot Interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02505 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.02505

q3 · i? · design · r2

During-interaction ad-hoc questionnaire (two items, 1-10 scale) completed at the end of each of three storytelling trials per participant. The article reports descriptive means and standard deviations for likability and coherence; no inferential test is printed.

"Results indicate that the participants liked the stories invented by iCub (mean likability score 8.61±1.38) and perceived the stories as coherent (mean coherence score 8.28 ± 1.27)."
