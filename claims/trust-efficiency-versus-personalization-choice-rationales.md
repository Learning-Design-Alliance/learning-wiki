## Evidence

### Helen Weixu Chen 2026

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic synthesis of open-ended survey responses from three waves (up to 53, 50, and 44 responses) using deductive and inductive coding; the article frames this as strategic reasoning rather than passivity.

> "Students who preferred instructor-provided cheat sheets often valued trust in instructor expertise, perceived alignment with the exam, and the efficiency of delegating part of the preparation process."

### Helen Weixu Chen 2026 (2)

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative themes from the same coding process; participants described writing the sheet as "a deliberate method of studying" and cited spatial familiarity and tailoring to knowledge gaps.

> "students who preferred self-created cheat sheets often valued personalization, control over content and layout, and the act of creating the sheet as a form of studying."
