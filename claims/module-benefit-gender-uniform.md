## Evidence

### Goff Eric E. 2017

Goff Eric E., Reindl Katie M., Johnson Christina, McClean Phillip, Offerdahl Erika G., Schroeder Noah L., White Alan R. (2017). Efficacy of a Meiosis Learning Module Developed for the Virtual Cell Animation Collection. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1):ar9. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0141

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Gender-stratified analysis of the quasi-experiment found "significantly higher normalized gain scores by students in the module treatment group than those in the traditional group for both males (t(96.68) = 3.05, p = 0.003, d = 0.51) and females (t(215.03) = 3.39, p < 0.001, d = 0.37)."
