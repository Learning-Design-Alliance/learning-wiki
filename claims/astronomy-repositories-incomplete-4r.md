# Existing astronomy education repositories do not fully satisfy the OER 4R model

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A comparison of astronomy education repositories (Compadre, Galileo Teachers Training Program, Astronomical Society of the Pacific, NASA Wavelength, astroEDU) found that none fully satisfies the OER 4R framework, particularly regarding revision and remixing rights. [→ Russo 2015](#russo-2015)

## Evidence

### Russo 2015

Russo, P., Heenatigala, T., Gomez, E., & Strubbe, L. (2015). Peer-review Platform for Astronomy Education Activities. eLearning Papers, n.º 40. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.07116](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.07116)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive comparison of five astronomy education repositories collated in Table 1 of the article, covering review type, openness to submissions, licensing and resource types. The authors conclude that "sometimes the material cannot be revised or remixed" and that most repositories provide only English materials without source texts.

> "The information collated on those repositories is mostly well organized and of high quality, however none of those repositories satisfies completely the OER 4R model previously mentioned; sometimes the material cannot be revised or remixed"
