## Evidence

### Chang Hui-Ping 2025

Chang Hui-Ping, Lin Tzu-Wen, Chiu Yi-Hsiang, Chou Chien-Chih, Chen Jui-Fu, Pan Cheng-Chen. (2025). Associations of physical fitness with sustained overt attention and academic performance in children with learning disabilities. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19549

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in a cross-sectional study of 71 children with LD (mean age 11.03 years) found "significant positive correlations between physical fitness and both Chinese language (r = .22, p < .05) and mathematics (r = .24, p < .05)"; the printed correlations are small (r around .22–.24).

> "The results revealed significant positive correlations between physical fitness and both Chinese language (r = .22, p < .05) and mathematics (r = .24, p < .05)."
