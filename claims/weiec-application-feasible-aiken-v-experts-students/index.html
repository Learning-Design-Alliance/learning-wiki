Evidence¶

Mitra Yadiannur and Supahar 2017¶

Mitra Yadiannur and Supahar. (2017). Mobile Learning based Worked Example in Electric Circuit (WEIEC) Application to Improve the High School Students' Electric Circuits Interpretation Ability. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 539-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141565

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Expert judgement validation of the WEIEC application product quality using Aiken's V on a 1-5 Likert-based instrument. The article reports the score "is 0.80" for both materials and media validity, interpreted as a very good coefficient.

"Table 5 shows that Aiken's V score on the validity of the materials and validity of the media quality of WEIEC application is 0.80."

Mitra Yadiannur and Supahar 2017 (2)¶

Mitra Yadiannur and Supahar. (2017). Mobile Learning based Worked Example in Electric Circuit (WEIEC) Application to Improve the High School Students' Electric Circuits Interpretation Ability. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 539-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141565

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Preliminary field testing with 35 students of class XII SMA 5 Samarinda assessing the revised WEIEC application. The mean Aiken's V "was 0.81", which the authors interpret as very good and as justifying progression to main field testing.

"Table 6 shows that the mean score of Aiken's V on the WEIEC application quality by the students in the preliminary field testing was 0.81."
