# Students in every condition sustained voluntary pedagogical prompting use above once per week, and confidence grew in step with skill

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported voluntary use exceeded once per week in every condition (M=3.50–3.83 on a 5-point scale); ANOVA was significant (F(3,427)=2.88, p=.036, η2=.020), with Condition 1 higher than Condition 3 (Tukey HSD, p=.032). [→ Xiao 2026](#xiao-2026)
`q3 i2` Confidence gains were larger in Conditions 2–4 (M=0.60–0.69) than Condition 1 (M=0.17; F(3,427)=6.64, p<.001, η2=.045), and confidence gains correlated with prompting skill gains (Spearman ρ=0.31, p<.001, n=431). [→ Xiao 2026 (2)](#xiao-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Delayed post-survey single-item measure of voluntary pedagogical prompting use over six weeks, asked about any context. Six students described applying learning-oriented prompting outside CSC108, four in a mathematics course.

> "Self-reportedvoluntary useof pedagogical prompting exceeded “once per week” in every condition ( M=3.50 –3.83 on a 5-point scale). A between-condition analysis of variance reached significance ( F(3,427) =2.88 , p=.036 , η2 =.020 )"

### Xiao 2026 (2)

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Secondary outcome analysis in the RCT. Confidence gains were largest in instructed conditions; the authors interpret this as calibrated rather than borrowed confidence, consistent with avoiding the illusion of competence.

> "confidence gains were positively correlated with prompting skill gains (Spearman ρ=0.31 , p< .001, n=431 )"
