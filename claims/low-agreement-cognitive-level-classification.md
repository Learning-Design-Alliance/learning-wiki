## Evidence

### Kibble JD 2011

Kibble JD, Johnson T. (2011). Are faculty predictions or item taxonomies useful for estimating the outcome of multiple-choice examinations? Adv Physiol Educ 35: 396–401. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00062.2011

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Agreement analysis comparing the truncated Bloom's taxonomy classifications made by item-writing faculty with the consensus scores assigned independently by the study author and the Course Director for all analyzed items in the course.

> "The classifications by item writers and the consensus raters matched on only 155 of 272 items (57%): 88 items matched at the knowledge level, 32 items matched at the comprehension level, and 35 items matched at the application level (κ = 0.33, P < 0.01)"
