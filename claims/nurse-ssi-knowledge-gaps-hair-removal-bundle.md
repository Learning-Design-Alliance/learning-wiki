### Zucco R 2019

Zucco R, Lavano F, Nobile CGA, Papadopoli R, Bianco A. (2019). Adherence to evidence-based recommendations for surgical site infection prevention: Results among Italian surgical ward nurses. PLoS ONE 14(9): e0222825. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0222825

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National cross-sectional survey of Italian surgical ward nurses (1305 of 1313 questionnaires returned, 99.4% response rate) testing knowledge via five Likert-scale statements. The survey found "only 53.8% knew that preoperative hair removal, if necessary, should take place shortly before surgery".

> "Regarding knowledge, only 53.8% knew that preoperative hair removal, if necessary, should take place shortly before surgery, and 28.9% of the sample did not know the right definition of “bundle”."
