## Evidence

### Bork F 2019

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of all 72 elective-course students using the same 22-statement VAS instrument: "the Magic Mirror outperformed the Anatomage in terms of approval rates in almost all statements during the second survey". No single effect size is attached to this overall statement.

> "While the Anatomage seemed to be the students’ preferred choice for self‐directed anatomy and radiology learning during the first survey, the Magic Mirror outperformed the Anatomage in terms of approval rates in almost all statements during the second survey."

### Bork F 2019 (2)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

> "The VAS scores were significantly higher for the Magic Mirror at the P = 0.001 level (F(1,142) = 50.53, P = 0.25, η 2 = 0.26)."
