## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the emergency module, the TBL group had significantly higher CRQ difficulty indices in quiz1 (p = 0.017) and quiz2 (p < 0.001). [→ Imran M 2022](#imran-m-2022)
`q3 i?` In the endocrinology module, TBL was superior in quiz1 (p = 0.04) but quiz2 showed no statistical difference (p = 0.32), though the TBL group still performed better. [→ Imran M 2022](#imran-m-2022)

## Evidence

### Imran M 2022

Imran M, Halawa TF, Baig M, Almanjoumi AM, Badri MM, Alghamdi WA. (2022). Team-based learning versus interactive lecture in achieving learning outcomes and improving clinical reasoning skills: a randomized crossover study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03411-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of mean difficulty indices of clinical reasoning questions between the two arms of 6th-year students under a randomized crossover design; the same section reports the endocrinology quiz1 result p = 0.04 and quiz2 p = 0.32. Only test statistics are printed; no effect size.

> "In quiz1, group1 with TBL as an instructional strategy performed better in the CRQ (p = 0.017), while in quiz2, group2 with TBL as the instructional strategy performed better (p < 0.001)."
