`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this fNIRS finding to Modi et al. (2018), examining temporal pressure on laparoscopic suturing performance and neurocognitive correlates of task load; the review reports the descriptive pattern with no effect size printed.

> "Performance scores decreased for all trainees under time-pressure conditions when compared to self-pacing. All trainees reported greater task load under time pressure and showed decreased PFC activity, especially junior trainees."

### Toy 2022 (2)

Toy, S., Huh, D. D., Materi, J., Nanavati, J., & Schwengel, D. A. (2022). Use of neuroimaging to measure neurocognitive engagement in health professions education: a scoping review. Medical Education Online, 27(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.2016357

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this subsequent fNIRS study to the same group [65], comparing trainees who maintained stable performance under time pressure with those who did not; the review adds that stress sensitivity and performance degradation were unrelated to experience level and not captured by heart-rate variability or Surgical-TLX.

> "Resilient trainees showed greater bilateral ventral PFC activation, suggesting better attentional control and vigilance."
