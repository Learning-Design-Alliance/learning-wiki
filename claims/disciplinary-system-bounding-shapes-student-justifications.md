## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2012

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Julia Svoboda, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2012). Students' Interdisciplinary Reasoning about "High-Energy Bonds" and ATP. [doi:10.1063/1.4789667](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789667)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Results section of the Gregor interview: he is not talking about physical scale but about whether one looks at the bond breaking on its own, which requires energy input, or the ATP hydrolysis reaction as a whole, which releases energy. He calls this "a salient difference between the disciplines."

> "He sees the boundary of the phenomenon under consideration as a salient difference between the disciplines."

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2012 (2)

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Julia Svoboda, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2012). Students' Interdisciplinary Reasoning about "High-Energy Bonds" and ATP. [doi:10.1063/1.4789667](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789667)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the second interview, with Wylie, three weeks after the first and prompted directly about the ATP quiz question. Wylie explains that in a biology course he would get points for choice C because "the focus of the question would be on the significant quantity of energy, not necessarily breaking the bond."

> "Because I think in the biology course, the focus of the question would be on the significant quantity of energy, not necessarily breaking the bond."
