# Seven nonacademic variables are selected as predictors of GMKT performance from 98 candidate nonacademic factors in Chinese medical students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cross-validated DSA variable selection retains seven of 98 candidate nonacademic variables (BMI, working memory RT, state anxiety, conscientiousness, openness, father's education, mother's occupation) as predictors of GMKT performance. [→ Sun Jie 2025](#sun-jie-2025)

### Sun Jie 2025

Sun Jie, Xie Yingying, Zhang Ningnannan, Cheng Jingliang, Wang Meiyun, Zhang Bing, Zhu Wenzhen, Zhang Hui, Qiu Shijun, Xu Xiaojun, Yu Yongqiang, Han Tong, Geng Zuojun, Liao Weihua, Gao Bo, Qin Wen, Liu Feng, Liang Meng, Xu Qiang, Fu Jilian, Xu Jiayuan, Liu Mengge, Zhang Peng, Li Wei, Shi Dapeng, Wang Caihong, Zuo Xi-Nian, Zhang Quan, Chen Feng, Li Jiance, Yan Zhihan, Shen Wen, Miao Yanwei, Xian Junfang, Zhang Longjiang, Xu Kai, Ye Zhaoxiang, Zhang Jing, Cui Guangbin, Yu Chunshui, for the CHIMGEN Consortium. (2025). Nonacademic predictors of China medical licensing examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06652-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational analysis of 1202 CHIMGEN medical students; the DSA iterative regression variable-selection with five-fold cross-validation retained seven predictors, and the selection held after adjusting for age and gender, with all seven showing "significant correlations with GMKT at P < 0.05" in the primary linear regression model.

> "Seven predictive variables were selected from the 98 candidate nonacademic variables by the DSA algorithm with a threshold of being selected at least in five runs (Table S5). These variables were also selected after adjusting for age and gender (Table S5)."
