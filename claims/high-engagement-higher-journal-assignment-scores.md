### Kates Meghan 2026

Kates Meghan, Fitzpatrick Alexa, Zhang Yupeng, Gibbs Alison, Liu Qin, da Silveira Fleck Alan, Freedman Leora, Andreopoulos Stavroula. (2026). Effectiveness of Scientific Literacy and Communication E‐Modules in a Molecular Biology Course: A Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70043

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pilot single-cohort study of Winter 2022 BCH311 activity logs (n = 1 cohort); K-medoids clustering of timeliness and regularity metrics matched to Canvas grade reports. The high engagement group had a "small, but consistent, increase in average course assignment scores" across all assignments.

> "The high engagement group had a small, but consistent, increase in average course assignment scores compared to the Medium and Low engagement groups across all assignments (Table 4 and Figure 4)."

### Kates Meghan 2026 (2)

Kates Meghan, Fitzpatrick Alexa, Zhang Yupeng, Gibbs Alison, Liu Qin, da Silveira Fleck Alan, Freedman Leora, Andreopoulos Stavroula. (2026). Effectiveness of Scientific Literacy and Communication E‐Modules in a Molecular Biology Course: A Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70043

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same pilot study; Kruskal–Wallis tests with Dunn's post hoc comparisons across engagement clusters (High N = 102, mean 64.0; Medium N = 132, mean 59.7). The difference "only reached statistical significance for the Journal Article assignment" with "p = 0.01".

> "This difference only reached statistical significance for the Journal Article assignment where students in the High engagement group had significantly higher assignment scores (p = 0.01) compared to students in the Medium engagement group."
