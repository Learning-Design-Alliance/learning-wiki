## Evidence

### Secchi Agostina 2025

Secchi Agostina, Whiskey Eromona, Nair Sajitha, Shergill Sukhi, Thomas Trudy. (2025). Competence and confidence of health care professionals in using clozapine: a qualitative systematic review and thematic synthesis. Acta Neuropsychiatrica. https://doi.org/10.1017/neu.2025.10024

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Survey evidence reported within the review: registered psychiatric mental health nurses questioned about clozapine side-effect risks largely answered incorrectly, with "95.3% (n = 123) answered incorrectly on myocarditis risk". Similar gaps were reported for psychiatrists.

> "70.5% (n = 91) of the nurses surveyed by Sanjeevi and Cocoman gave incorrect answers when questioned about the risk of seizures; 65.1% (n = 84) gave incorrect answers when questioned about the risk of metabolic effects and 95.3% (n = 123) answered incorrectly on myocarditis risk."
