Post-training assessment of the 2023 QPic intervention with 54 high-school students, reported in the Results section. The article reports that "82% of participants successfully passed the exam" and "48% of participants achieved a distinction" once canary and sentinel questions were included in scoring. No effect size is printed.

> "Based on the inclusion of canary and sentinel questions in the assessment, the pass/fail rates among participants were: 82% of participants successfully passed the exam, reflecting a substantial majority who met the minimum criteria set by the canary questions. Additionally, 48% of participants achieved a distinction"
