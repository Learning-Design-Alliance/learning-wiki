## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-efficacy is significantly and positively related to both math and reading scores, with a stronger relationship for math, across OLS models with covariates and school fixed effects. [→ Akhmedjanova Diana 2026](#akhmedjanova-diana-2026)

## Evidence

### Akhmedjanova Diana 2026

Akhmedjanova Diana, Kanonire Tatjana, Zakharov Andrey. (2026). Moderation analysis of subjective well-being, self-efficacy, and academic performance of 4th grade children in Russia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0341318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression analysis of 1,962 fourth graders' survey and Progress test data. The article reports the relationship "remains" significant with school fixed effects, with the coefficient declining "from 0.39 to 0.33 SD" for math and "from 0.24 to 0.22 SD" for reading.

> "As for self-efficacy, it shows a robust and statistically significant relationship (p < 0.01) with students’ outcomes in both math and reading, indicating a stronger relationship with math."
