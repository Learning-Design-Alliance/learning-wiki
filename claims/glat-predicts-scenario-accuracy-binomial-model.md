# Each additional correct GLAT response is associated with a 13.9% increase in modeled odds of correctly classifying a scenario

## Evidence

### Reed 2026

Reed, J. M., Ferdig, R. E., Dodson, T. M., Gunstad, J., & Hughes, J. W. (2026). AI Literacy and Scenario-based Ethical Judgment About Generative AI Among Undergraduate Students. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09757-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binomial generalized linear model with logit link in the RQ3 results, holding sex and academic status constant. The fitted model improved on the intercept-only model, likelihood-ratio χ²(5) = 76.04, p < .001; each additional correct GLAT response was associated with a 13.9% increase in modeled odds.

> "The 15-item Non-Ethics GLAT score was significantly and positively associated with classification accuracy, B = 0.130, SE = 0.033, Wald χ²(1) = 15.37, p < .001, OR = 1.14, 95% CI [1.07, 1.22]."
