Hagen M. S., Dunahoe J., Parker K. M., Kweon C. Y., Kennedy S. A. (2026). Longitudinal Pilot Study on the Use of Low-Cost Modules to Improve Junior Orthopaedic Resident Surgical Skills. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t tests on timed tasks (Table 2): drill plunge P = 0.015, depth gauge P = 0.012, angled drilling P = 0.034; accuracy comparisons were non-significant in all modules. No effect sizes printed.

> "PGY1 residents at the end of the year demonstrated significantly higher task efficiency in drill plunge, depth gauge, and angled drilling compared with the control residents (Figure 4). No statistically significant difference in task accuracy was observed between the groups in any of the modules."
