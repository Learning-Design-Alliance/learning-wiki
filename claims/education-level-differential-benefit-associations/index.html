q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression (Model 9) on the 814-student survey. The article reports this difference and notes it "may be associated with variations in academic experience and research expectations across educational levels, rather than indicating a direct causal inﬂuence".

"master’s students perceived signiﬁcantly higher psychological and emotional support beneﬁts than doctoral students (β = −0.438, SE = 0.218, p < 0.05)"

Du W 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2
