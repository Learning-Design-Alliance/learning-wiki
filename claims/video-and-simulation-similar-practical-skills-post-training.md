Pilot RCT practical skills evaluation using a standardised SimBaby paediatric cardiac arrest scenario scored with a 10-item checklist, each item rated on a 0–2 scale, assessed by two trained assessors. The article reports "median 90.0%" and "p=0.18" for the between-group comparison.
