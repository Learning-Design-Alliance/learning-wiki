`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed-effects regression from the KaziAfya trial (n = 559): both MMNS-containing arms significantly improved Kiswahili scores versus placebo, with baseline Kiswahili a strong predictor (p < 0.001). No effect size was printed.

> "For performance in Kiswahili, the MMNS intervention led to significant improvements (p < 0.001), while the PA + MMNS group also showed a significant positive effect (p = 0.01), whereas PA + placebo showed no significant association."
