## Evidence

### Subramaniam Anusuiya 2015

Subramaniam Anusuiya, Silong Abu Daud, Uli Jegak, Ismail Ismi Arif. (2015). Effects of coaching supervision, mentoring supervision and abusive supervision on talent development among trainee doctors in public hospitals: moderating role of clinical learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0407-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Interaction-term regression within the SEM of the 355-respondent survey. The coaching × environment interaction is significant and positive, "β = 0.296, p = 0.002", supporting H4.

> "This indicates that clinical learning environment moderates the relationship between (1) coaching supervision and talent development (β = 0.296, p = 0.002), (2) mentoring supervision and talent development (β = −0.259, p = 0.006) and (3) abusive supervision and talent development (β = 0.170, p = 0.001)."
