## Evidence

### Spénard Sarah 2022

Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial (N = 44 family medicine residents) comparing hands-on, video, and control boosters given 3-5 months after NRP, all groups made lifesaving skills errors at 6-8 month follow-up, but the hands-on group made significantly fewer life-supporting errors (P = 0.021).

> "the participants in the hands-on group made significantly fewer errors at follow-up compared to the combined control and video groups averaged in five scenarios (72% and 63%, respectively, P = 0.021)."
