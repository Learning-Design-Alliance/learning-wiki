`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 2, Figure 2 heatmap) among the four core constructs in the 2016-respondent sample; printed coefficients include usability–load r = −0.409, usability–engagement r = 0.284, and flow–engagement r = 0.468, all p < 0.001.

> "Correlation analysis (Table 2 and Figure 2) revealed that a more usable AI system was associated with lower extraneous cognitive load and higher learning engagement. Meanwhile, higher cognitive load was linked to lower flow experience and lower engagement."
