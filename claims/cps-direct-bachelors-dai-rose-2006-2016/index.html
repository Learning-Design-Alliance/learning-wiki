Nagaoka 2017¶

Nagaoka, J., Seeskin, A., & Coca, V.M. (2017). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2016. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/data

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of CPS milestone rates composing the Direct Bachelor's DAI. The report estimates the 2016 index at 16 percent versus 9 percent in 2006, “an immediate transition after high school to enroll in a four-year college and graduate within six years.”

"We estimate that 16 percent of 2016 ninth-graders, compared to 9 percent of 2006 ninth-graders, will take a direct path to a bachelor’s degree by making an immediate transition after high school to enroll in a four-year college and graduate within six years"
