## Evidence

### Amasha 2014

Amasha, M., & Alkhalaf, S. (2014). The Effect of Using Facebook Markup Language (FBML) for Designing an E-Learning Model in Higher Education. International Journal of Research in Computer Science, 4(5), 1-9. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 20-phrase questionnaire measured student attitudes toward the Facebook learning method. All 19 reported items exceeded the critical χ² value, with agreement percentages ranging from 75.9% to 100%, indicating "the distinction between the students' approval or disapproval of the phrases of the questionnaire was in favor of the use of using Facebook site."

> "Table 4 shows that the χ 2 value of all phrases concerned with the students' viewpoints on using a class Facebook site is bigger than the χ 2 (1, N = 29) = 3.84; p =.05."
