### Foster 1911

Foster, W. S. (1911). School Instruction in Matters of Sex. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22School+Instruction+in+Matters+of+Sex%22+Foster

`q1 · i?` · `review · r1`

Argumentative conclusion of the section on biological science courses, which reports favorable accounts of such courses but lists reasons they are inadequate: they come too late, their content is insufficient, and teachers must remain objective. The author quotes Henderson and Cabot's objections.

> "It seems necessary in general, therefore, that the courses in biology should be at least supplemented by more personal and direct means."
