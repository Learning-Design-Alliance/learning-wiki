Evidence¶

Schildkraut 2019¶

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports NYC data on racial disparities: "48 percent of Black high school students and 38 percent of Hispanic high school students" pass through detectors daily versus 14 percent of White students.

"48 percent of Black high school students and 38 percent of Hispanic high school students, compared to just 14 percent of White students, pass through metal detectors each day"

Schildkraut 2019 (2)¶

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports a study using the School Crime Supplement of the National Crime Victimization Survey (cited to Toldson 2011) found Black and Latino/a students were "4.8 and 2.7 times, respectively, more likely" to pass through detectors.

"Compared to White students, Black and Latino/a students were 4.8 and 2.7 times, respectively, more likely to pass through a metal detector at their schools"
