## Evidence

### Vij O 2024

Vij O, Calver H, Myall N, Dey M, Kouranloo K. (2024). Evaluating the competency of ChatGPT in MRCP Part 1 and a systematic literature review of its capabilities in postgraduate medical assessments. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0307372

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Combined analysis of the 12 included SLR studies plus this study's MRCP result, summarising ChatGPT-3.5's performance across nine postgraduate UK exams with an average score of 56% and an average difference from the pass mark of -5.0%.

> "ChatGPT-3.5 failed eight out of nine postgraduate UK medical exams it performed, and only achieved a mark within or above passing range in MRCP Part 1. The average score for ChatGPT-3.5 was 56% (SD 0.084, range 43%-69.7%), with an average difference between GPT-3.5 score and pass mark of -5.0%."

### Vij O 2024 (2)

Vij O, Calver H, Myall N, Dey M, Kouranloo K. (2024). Evaluating the competency of ChatGPT in MRCP Part 1 and a systematic literature review of its capabilities in postgraduate medical assessments. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0307372

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Combined analysis across eleven examinations (Table 1) showing ChatGPT-4 passed nine, averaging 76.2% with a +13.56% difference from the pass mark; the failures were FRCS (Orth) Part 1 and FRCR Part 1, which require 6 and 2 years of postgraduate experience respectively.

> "ChatGPT-4 passed nine out of eleven examinations it performed with an average score of 76.2% (SD 1.23, range 50%-100%), with an average difference between GPT-4 score and pass mark of +13.56%. The specialties that ChatGPT-4 failed were orthopaedics and radiology."
