## Evidence

### Crome Marius 2021

Crome Marius, Adam Knut, Flohr Marco, Rahman Alexander, Staufenbiel Ingmar. (2021). Application of the inverted classroom model in the teaching module “new classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases and conditions” during the COVID-19 pandemic. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001485.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a 17-item questionnaire administered to the 55 participating students; the authors state the tool "could not be checked for reliability, so the data recorded were only analyzed descriptively". Item 8 showed "88.9% (n=48) said at the end of the periodontology module that they now felt more confident".

> "Basically, the periodontology module was rated “very good” by the students, with an average score of 13.66±1.04 out of 15 points."
