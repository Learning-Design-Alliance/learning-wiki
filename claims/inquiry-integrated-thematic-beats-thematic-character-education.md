### Chumdari 2018

Chumdari, Sri Anitah, Budiyono, & Nunuk Suryani. (2018). Inquiry-based Integrated Thematic Instruction On Character Education Of Primary School Students. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.2p.69

Quasi-experiment using a Randomized Static Group Comparison Design with 129 experimental and 139 control students in Surakarta elementary schools. The Mann-Whitney U Test showed the experimental group "Mdn = 3.513" versus control "Mdn = 3.299", significant at .000<.05; no standardized effect size is printed.
