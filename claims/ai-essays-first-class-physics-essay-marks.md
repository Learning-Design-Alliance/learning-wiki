# AI-generated short-form physics essays marked by five independent markers averaged 71 ± 2%, in strong agreement with the module average of 71 ± 5%

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Ten AI-generated submissions, each of five 300-word essay answers, achieved an average mark of 71 ± 2% when independently marked, comparable to the 71 ± 5% module average. [→ Will Yeadon 2023](#will-yeadon-2023)

## Evidence

### Will Yeadon 2023

Will Yeadon, Oto-Obong Inyang, Arin Mizouri, Alex Peach, Craig Testrow. (2023). The Death of the Short-Form Physics Essay in the Coming AI Revolution. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/acc5cf](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/acc5cf)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmarking study of ten davinci-003 submissions on the Physics in Society exam, marked independently by five markers; the AI average of "71 ± 2%" matched the module average of "71 ± 5%" and the all-second-year average of "72±3%".

> "The ten AI generated submissions were marked independently by five diﬀerent markers and achieved an average mark of 71 ± 2%. This compares to the average of 71 ± 5% in Physics in Society and an average score of 72±3% for all second year Physics modules at Durham University"
