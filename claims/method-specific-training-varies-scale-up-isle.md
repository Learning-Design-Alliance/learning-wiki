## Evidence

### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2026

Ibukunoluwa Bukola, Eric Brewe, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Meagan Sundstrom. (2026). Characterizing adopters of named active learning methods in physics: An interview study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of method-specific training (Fig. 3b, Table III). The article reports none of the SCALE-UP instructors received formal professional development training; one instructor noted "there was no formal training on SCALE-UP".

> "Instead, the SCALE-UP in- structors identified informal professional development oppor- tunities and direct engagement with the method (e.g., observ- ing the method in practice and/or implementing the method themselves under the supervision of a faculty member) as their primary training experience"

### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2026 (2)

Ibukunoluwa Bukola, Eric Brewe, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Meagan Sundstrom. (2026). Characterizing adopters of named active learning methods in physics: An interview study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of method-specific training for the five ISLE users (Fig. 3b). The article reports all ISLE instructors trained via direct engagement with multiple training forms, while most Tutorials and Peer Instruction instructors trained via formal professional development.

> "All ISLE instructors reported receiving method-specific training through direct engagement with the method and they all reported receiving more than one form of method-specific training"
