### Kang Angela J. 2019

Kang Angela J., Gielissen Katherine, Windish Donna. (2019). Using an Entrustable Professional Activity to Assess Consultation Requests Called on an Internal Medicine Teaching Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10854

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational assessment on two internal medicine inpatient teaching services at Yale New Haven Hospital, April–June 2018, with 26 paired observations collected over three 1-month rotations; correlations compared resident and intern instrument responses, with a "moderate positive correlation was found (r = .43)".

> "A moderate positive correlation was found (r = .43) comparing question 1 on the Resident Supervisor Instrument (“How do you feel about your intern's ability to call a consult?”) to question 1 on the Intern Self-Reflection Instrument (“How do you feel about your ability to call a consult?”)."
