Evidence¶

Del Rey 2018¶

Del Rey, R., Mora-Merchán, J.A., Casas, J.A., Ortega-Ruiz, R., & Elipe, P. (2018). 'Asegúrate' Program: Effects on cyber-aggression and its risk factors. Comunicar, 56, XXVI. https://doi.org/10.3916/C56-2018-04

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA on students identified as cyber-aggressors at T1. The article reports a significant time × condition interaction, "F (1, 40)=6.959, p=.012, η2p=.148", indicating the decline in cyber-aggression intensity is associated with the intervention.

"However, these effects are qua- lified by the interac- tion between time and condition, F (1, 40)=6.959, p=.012, η2p=.148."

Del Rey 2018 (2)¶

Del Rey, R., Mora-Merchán, J.A., Casas, J.A., Ortega-Ruiz, R., & Elipe, P. (2018). 'Asegúrate' Program: Effects on cyber-aggression and its risk factors. Comunicar, 56, XXVI. https://doi.org/10.3916/C56-2018-04

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the same cyber-aggressor subsample for traditional aggression. The article reports "a significant interaction, F (1, 40)=9.990, p=.003, η2p=.200", showing the program reduces aggression intensity in this group.

"Regarding aggression, the results followed a similar pat- tern, a significant effect of time, F (1, 40)=9.034, p=.005, η2p=.184; a lack of significance for condition, F (1, 40)=1.138, p=.292, η2p=.028; and a significant interaction, F (1, 40)=9.990, p=.003, η2p=.200."
