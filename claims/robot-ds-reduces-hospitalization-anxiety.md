## Evidence

### Ching-Yi Chang 2023

Ching-Yi Chang, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Ya-Lien Chou, Zi-Yin Xu, & Hsiu-Ju Jen. (2023). Effects of robot-assisted digital storytelling on hospitalized children's communication during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10209-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t test in the same randomized controlled experiment: the experimental group (mean = 2.92) "obtained lower anxiety scores than the control group" (mean = 3.43), measured with the emotional expressivity category of the mYPAS.

> "A significant difference in anxiety could be found between the two groups (t = 2.43, p < .05), with a medium effect size (d = 0.73)."
