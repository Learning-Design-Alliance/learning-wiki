Evidence¶

Shih 2013¶

Shih, R.-C., Cho, C.-L., Tsai, C.-C., & Lou, S.-J. (2013). Employing Microsoft Live@Edu Cloud Platform to Assist in Teaching Chinese Reading for Junior High School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018038

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 63 eighth-grade students (experimental n=32, control n=31) in Taiwan; ANCOVA with pre-test PISA reading scores as covariate showed the experimental group's post-test reading effectiveness was significantly better than the control group's (p=.027<.05).

"The results of ANCOVA show that the learning effectiveness of the experimental group is better than the control group (p=.027<.05), indicating Live@edu cloud platform can effectively provide the experimental group students a communication platform to share and discuss ideas, questions, thoughts, and reflections and thus to enhance their abilities in obtaining useful information to improve their reading effectiveness."
