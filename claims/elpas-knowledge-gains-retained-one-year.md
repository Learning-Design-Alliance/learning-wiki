# A case-based e-learning course on patient safety significantly increased medical students' technical knowledge, and the gains persisted one year later

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Technical knowledge increased significantly in the intervention group after the e-learning intervention and remained significantly higher than the control group after 12 months (η² = .243). [→ Gaupp R 2019](#gaupp-r-2019)

## Evidence

### Gaupp R 2019

Gaupp R, Dinius J, Drazic I, Körner M. (2019). Long-term effects of an e-learning course on patient safety: A controlled longitudinal study with medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210947

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Controlled quasi-experimental repeated-measures study with 54 intervention and 36 control participants completing all three measurements. ANCOVA showed a significant knowledge effect; means rose from 33.09 (IG) at T0 to 72.78 at T1 and 71.65 at T2, versus 26.32 to 49.37 in the control group. "η2 = .243" is a large effect.

> "the intervention had a statistically significant effect on technical knowledge levels (F(2, 84) = 13.506, p < .001, η2 = .243). Technical knowledge increased significantly in the intervention group after the intervention, whereas changes in the technical knowledge of the control group remained statistically non-significant."
