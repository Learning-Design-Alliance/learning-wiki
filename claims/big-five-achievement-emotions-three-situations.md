## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2020

de la Fuente Jesús, Paoloni Paola, Kauffman Douglas, Yilmaz Soylu Meryem, Sander Paul, Zapata Lucía. (2020). Big Five, Self-Regulation, and Coping Strategies as Predictors of Achievement Emotions in Undergraduate Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17103602

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 3, n = 642) of Big Five factors against AEQ-measured achievement emotions in three academic situations. The article reports neuroticism held the strongest positive association with anxiety, hopelessness and anger, and the strongest negative association with hope and enjoyment; these relations "held constant in the three situations".

> "These relationships held constant in the three situations evaluated (class, study time and testing), with a consistent, stable tendency. See Table 3."
