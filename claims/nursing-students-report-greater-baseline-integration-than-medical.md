## Evidence

### Mestre André 2022

Mestre André, Muster Marek, El Adib Ahmed Rhassane, Ösp Egilsdottir Hugrun, Byermoen Kirsten Røland, Padilha Miguel, Aguilar Thania, Tabagari Nino, Betts Lorraine, Sales Leila, Garcia Pedro, Ling Luo, Café Hugo, Binnie Alexandra, Marreiros Ana. (2022). The impact of small-group virtual patient simulator training on perceptions of individual learning process and curricular integration: a multicentre cohort study of nursing and medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03426-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional t-test comparison of baseline (pre-session) responses between 324 nursing and 293 medical students. Nursing students scored higher on practical balance, communication, confidence and group/conflict management items; medical students reported studies more focused on theory.

> "Nursing students reported greater balance between theoretical studies and the practical application of knowledge (P < 0.001), more development of communication skills (P < 0.001), greater confidence in their knowledge and in the decision-making process (P < 0.001)"
