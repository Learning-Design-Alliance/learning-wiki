## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 13-item DOVCS checklist had internal consistency of 0.92 and inter-rater reliability (ICC) of 0.68 with wide confidence intervals. [→ Liew Siaw Cheok 2023](#liew-siaw-cheok-2023)

## Evidence

### Liew Siaw Cheok 2023

Liew Siaw Cheok, Tan Maw Pin, Breen Emer, Krishnan Kuhan, Sivarajah Inthirani, Raviendran Nivashinie, Aung Thidar, Nimir Amal, Pallath Vinod. (2023). Microlearning and online simulation-based virtual consultation training module for the undergraduate medical curriculum – a preliminary evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04777-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the DOVCS checklist scored by two independent faculty raters per video using a mean-rating, absolute-agreement, 2-way mixed-effects model. The wide ICC interval (0.201–0.901) is reported in the article itself.

> "The DOVCS checklist had an internal consistency of 0.92 with inter-rater reliability determined by an intraclass coefficient of 0.68, and 95% confidence intervals between 0.201 and 0.901, p < 0.001, based on a mean-rating (k = 2), absolute-agreement, 2-way mixed-effects model."
