Evidence¶

Alexander J Savage 2022¶

Alexander J Savage, Patrick W McNamara, Thomas W Moncrieff, Gerard M O'Reilly. (2022). E‐learning in emergency medicine: A systematic review. Emergency Medicine Australasia. https://doi.org/10.1111/1742-6723.13936

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review synthesis of 15 included studies (15 of 19 used MCQ examinations for knowledge gain), screened from 1586 non-duplicate records with two independent reviewers. The review reports "11 of the 15 demonstrated no statistically significant difference between EL and TL".

"Of the studies analysing MCQ performance, 11 of the 15 demonstrated no statistically significant difference between EL and TL. A further two studies reported EL statistically significantly improved MCQ performance, 31 , 34 while two studies yielded significant findings that TL outperformed EL on MCQ examinations."
