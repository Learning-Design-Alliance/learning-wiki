Evidence¶

Chen 2023¶

Chen, Y.-C., Chang, G.-C., Huang, W.-M. & Hwang, I.-S. (2023). Quick balance skill improvement after short-term training with error amplification feedback for older adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00151-w

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized between-groups parallel experiment with 36 older adults (n = 18 per group); one 60 s-round training day between pre- and post-tests. The independent t test on standardized error differences favored EA, "better training beneﬁts in the EA group". No standardized effect size printed; impact null.

"The results of independent t statistics showed better training beneﬁts in the EA group, as indicated by the greater reduction in the standardized differences in postural errors between the pre-test and post-test ( t34 = 2.440, p = 0.02)"
