Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox 2026¶

Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox. (2026). Online professional development to support K‑12 teachers' computer science integration. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10640-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Enrollment and completion counts across three years of data collection (2020–2023) for the MICRO courses. The article reports "an 82% retention rate"; separately, 78% and 74% of participants ranked "online instruction" and "asynchronous instruction" as appealing or very appealing.

"at least 556 in-service and preservice teachers engaged with the micro-courses and at least 458 teachers completed all three courses. These data show an 82% retention rate, which was much higher than expected for free, self-paced, online courses."
