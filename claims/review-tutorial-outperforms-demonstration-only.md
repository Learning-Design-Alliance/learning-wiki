# The demonstration-with-review tutorial outperformed the demonstration-only control on practice, immediate post-test and delayed post-test

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A significant main effect of condition favored the review tutorial (d = 0.92), with significant pairwise advantages on practice (d = 0.83), immediate post-test (d = 0.53) and delayed post-test (d = 0.52). [→ Hans van der Meij 2016](#hans-van-der-meij-2016)

## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2016

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing a demonstration-only control (n = 27) with a demonstration-with-review condition (n = 28) under experimenter-controlled video play. "All of these comparisons favored the review tutorial"; ANCOVAs with pre-test as covariate confirmed the findings.

> "Condition had a signiﬁcant effect on the practice test score, F(1, 54) = 9.55, p = 0.002, d = 0.83, on the immediate post-test score, F(1, 54) = 3.90, p = 0.027, d = 0.53, on the delayed post-test score, F(1, 54) = 3.63, p = 0.031, d = 0.52. All of these comparisons favored the review tutorial (see Table 1)."
