## Evidence

### Songlin Xu 2023

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Gaze-behavior analysis in the N=311 between-subjects experiment: crowd AoI gaze consistency, the percentage of students whose gaze falls into similar slide areas, was significantly higher for Feedback (F1,636 = 31.402, P < 0.001); no effect size printed.

> "Critically, the Feedback group showed significantly higher gaze consistency on the same text/image elements (F1,636 = 31.402,P < 0.001) (Fig. 2(left))."

### Songlin Xu 2023 (2)

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same gaze analysis: although peer cues raised consistency, the specific regions where gazes converged differed between groups, which the authors read as students adapting viewing strategies instead of strictly mirroring peers; descriptive, no effect size.

> "However, consistent gaze regions differed between groups, suggesting students retained their own strategies to decide whether following peer attention visual region in the course."
