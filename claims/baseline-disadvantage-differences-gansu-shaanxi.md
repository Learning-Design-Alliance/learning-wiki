# Students in Gansu were disadvantaged at baseline: lower math scores, poorer households, more parental out-migration, less eyeglass wear, and far more blackboard teaching

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Five baseline characteristics differed significantly between provinces: eyeglass wearing at baseline, baseline math score (0.09 SD lower in Gansu), parental out-migration (52.4% vs 42.8%), household assets (14,800 vs 40,000 RMB), and blackboard use (83.1% vs 56.5% of teachers). ## Evidence

### Du Kang 2022

Du Kang, Wang Huan, Ma Yue, Guan Hongyu, Rozelle Scott. (2022). Effect of Eyeglasses on Student Academic Performance: What Matters? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710923

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Cross-province comparison of baseline characteristics (Table 6) in the RCT sample. The article reports five significantly different variables, all related to economic status, including household assets of 14,800 RMB in Gansu versus 40,000 RMB in Shaanxi and blackboard use by 83.1% versus 56.5% of teachers.

> "students in Gansu scored lower than students in Shaanxi by 0.09 standard deviations at the baseline standardized math score (p-value < 0.05). Third, parents in Gansu out-migrated for work more frequently than those in Shaanxi. About 52.4% of students in Gansu had at least one migrant parent compared to 42.8% in Shaanxi (p-value < 0.05). Fourth, student families in Gansu were much poorer."
