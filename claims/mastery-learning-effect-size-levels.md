`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Re-analysis of Hattie's (2009) synthesis on mastery learning, which Willett, Yamashita, & Anderson (1983) identified as having the highest effects among teaching strategies. Nine meta-analyses yielded "ES = .58 and a CLE = 41%".

> "Specifically, stating that in elementary school it had an effect of ES = .94, in high school ES = .72 and in college ES = .65, all having a high to very high effect (Guskey & Gates, 1986). Overall there have been nine meta-analyses with ES = .58 and a CLE = 41%"
