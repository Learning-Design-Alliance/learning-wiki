## Evidence

### Charlène Truong 2023

Charlène Truong, Célia Ruffino, Jérémie Gaveau, Olivier White, Pauline M. Hilt and Charalambos Papaxanthis. (2023). Time of day and sleep effects on motor acquisition and consolidation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00176-9

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Control experiment 1: G8sleep trained at 8 p.m. and was retested 2 h after waking (14 h after training); its T2_T3 gain was statistically equivalent to G8pm's (d = 0.07), supporting that consolidation was independent of time awake the day after training.

> "the G8 sleep was not different (t = −0.18, p = 0.86, d = 0.07; Bayesian equivalence test: BF OH 01 = 2.64 and BFNOH 01 = 2.86) to that obtained for the G8pm."

### Charlène Truong 2023 (2)

Charlène Truong, Célia Ruffino, Jérémie Gaveau, Olivier White, Pauline M. Hilt and Charalambos Papaxanthis. (2023). Time of day and sleep effects on motor acquisition and consolidation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00176-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`
