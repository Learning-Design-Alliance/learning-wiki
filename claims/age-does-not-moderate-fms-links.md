# Age and age variance do not moderate FMS–academic-cognitive links, inconsistent with a maturation account

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither age (QM(1) = 2.297, p = .130) nor age variance (QM(1) = .016, p = .899) moderated the overall FMS–academic-cognitive effect, and no age × domain interaction was significant (all ps > .216). [→ Sebastian P. Suggate 2025](#sebastian-p-suggate-2025)
