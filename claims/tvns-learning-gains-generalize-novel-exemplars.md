## Evidence

### Llanos 2020

Llanos, F., McHaney, J. R., Schuerman, W. L., Yi, H. G., Leonard, M. K. and Chandrasekaran, B. (2020). Non-invasive peripheral nerve stimulation selectively enhances speech category learning in adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0070-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logit mixed-effects model of trial-by-block outcomes in the Generalization block versus Block 1, where participants categorized new exemplars from novel speakers without stimulation or feedback. Only the tVNS-easy interaction was significant (p = 0.002; tVNS-hard p = 0.84).

> "We found a positive and signiﬁcant group-by-block interaction only for the tVNS-easy group (tVNS-easy: β = 0.1, z = 3.07, p = 0.002; tVNS-hard: β = 0.006, z = 0.19, p = 0.84; Fig. 2a)."
