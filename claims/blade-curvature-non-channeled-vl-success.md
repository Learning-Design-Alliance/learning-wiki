## Evidence

### Lim Jonathan ZM 2020

Lim Jonathan ZM, Chew Shi Hao, Chin Benjamin ZB, Siew Raymond CH. (2020). Proficiencies of military medical officers in intubating difficult airways. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-020-00375-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Conclusions, the authors state this was not the study's primary objective but that the manikin results "suggest" blade curvature mattered. The Discussion adds that the MG shares blade curvature with the familiar Macintosh DL, while the KVNC's acute angle made tube passage difficult.

> "our results suggest that the blade curvature for non-channeled VLs contributed significantly to the success or failure of intubation by novice operators."
