### Edward Jay Wang 2024

Edward Jay Wang. (2024). Using Peer-Customers to Scalably Pair Student Teams with Customers for Hands-on Curriculum Final Projects. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.08299](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.08299)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study from the course in which a customer wanted a fidget cube whose lights activate only when picked up, with no button control. The team worried the simpler project would earn a bad grade; the instructor reframed the goal as satisfying the customer, and the team removed unneeded wiring. The authors interpret this as a situation that would not occur in a self-ideated project.

> "The instructor told the students, "The only thing that will end up with a bad grade is if you don't give your customer what they want. If they don't want it to control the lights, well great! Less work for you."
