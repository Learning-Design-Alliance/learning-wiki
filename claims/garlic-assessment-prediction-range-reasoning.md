## Evidence

### Nathan Moore and John Deming 2008

Nathan Moore and John Deming. (2008). Using Garlic As A Far-Transfer Problem of Proportional And Probabilistic Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2133 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Assessment question posed near the end of the activity, asking students to guess the number and weight of cloves in an unopened bulb. The article describes the shift in student reasoning qualitatively, with no measured outcome.

> "Many students used the class aver- age of cloves/bulb to predict the number of cloves in the next bulb. However, we asked the students to describe a reasonable number or range of cloves to expect, which forced the class to be more speciﬁc in their rationale for a given prediction."
