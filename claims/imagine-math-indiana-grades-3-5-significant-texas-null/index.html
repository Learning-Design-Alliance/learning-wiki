Imagine Math by Imagine Learning: Evidence Rating Promising 2024¶

Imagine Math by Imagine Learning: Evidence Rating Promising. (2024). https://www.imaginelearning.com/imagine-math

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental study during the 2023–2024 school year in two public school districts in Indiana and Texas, with an analytic sample of 8,379 students in grades 1–8 with fall and spring NWEA MAP Growth Math scores; users were matched with non-users in the same districts.

"In Indiana, a statistically significant positive effect was found for students in grades 3–5 (effect size = +0.13). Effects for other grade bands and for students in Texas were not statistically significant."
