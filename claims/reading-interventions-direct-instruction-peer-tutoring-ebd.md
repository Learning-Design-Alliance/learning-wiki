## Evidence

### Campbell 2018

Campbell, A. R., Bowman-Perrott, L., Burke, M. D., & Sallese, M. R. (2018). Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 11 studies (Rivera et al., 2006), 7 single-case and 4 group designs, published 1977-2003, focusing on reading interventions targeting fluency for students at-risk and with EBD.

> "The study results indicated that direct instruction and peer-tutoring are effective reading interventions for students with EBD."

### Campbell 2018 (2)

Campbell, A. R., Bowman-Perrott, L., Burke, M. D., & Sallese, M. R. (2018). Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 9 single-case design studies (Garwood et al., 2014) published 2004-2012 on reading comprehension and fluency for adolescents with EBD; the review reported a range of moderate to large effects for fluency and small to large effects for comprehension.

> "Five of the nine studies included in their review focused on words per minute (WCPM) as the measure of fluency and reported an increase in WCPM. Four studies indicated higher levels of reading comprehension."
