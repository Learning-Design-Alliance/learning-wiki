### Alexander Skulmowski 2024

The review summarizes several studies applying the biologically primary/secondary distinction to instructional design, citing Agostinho et al. (2015) and Korbach et al. (2020) on finger tracing. Such simple motor actions yield benefits without generating load; no effect sizes are printed.

> "easily performed biologically primary motor actions such as finger tracing can provide cognitive benefits (such as linking visually displaced information) without causing cognitive load (e.g., Agostinho et al., 2015; Korbach et al., 2020)."
