Howard 2017¶

Howard, S. J., Burianová, H., Calleia, A., Fynes-Clinton, S., Kervin, L. and Bokosmaty, S. (2017). The method of educational assessment affects children's neural processing and performance: behavioural and fMRI Evidence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0010-9

Theoretical argument in the Introduction, reasoned from the WM engagement literature and Cognitive Load Theory; the article offers this account as an explanation for discrepant performance across otherwise equivalent tests, not a directly measured mediation.

"This introduces a test impurity issue that could inﬂuence test-taker performance in a non-uniform manner along a gradient of students ' WM capacities."
