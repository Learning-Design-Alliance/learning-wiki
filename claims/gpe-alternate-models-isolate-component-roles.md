### The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting 2022

The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting, Performance, and Emotions in Self-Regulated Learning: A Computational Modeling Approach — Supplementary Materials. (2022). https://gpemodel.shinyapps.io/gpe-model/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Definitional/descriptive specification of the alternate model family, based on work the article attributes to Wang & Mukhopadhyay (2012). Alternate Models 2-4 respectively fix the GPD effect on goal setting, the GPD effect on performance, or both emotion effects to zero; model fit was compared via WAIC using computed log likelihoods.

> "The first alternate model accounts for the differing effects that goal success (reaching or surpassing the goal) and goal failure (not reaching the goal) may have on goal setting."
