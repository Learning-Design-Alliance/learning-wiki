## Evidence

### Casey W. Miller 2009

Casey W. Miller, Michelle D. Chabot, and Troy C. Messina. (2009). A student's guide to searching the literature using online databases. Am. J. Phys., 77(12), 1112-1117. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0931](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0931)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of twenty undergraduate Advanced Laboratory students, reported in the Student Perspectives section. Nine students with little or no prior database use (Group A) rated likelihood of using online journal databases before and after the methodology; the article reports a statistically significant change with no printed effect size.

> "Group A showed a statistically signiﬁcant change in the use of online journal databases, which jumped from 1 .4 ± 0.2 to 4.4 ± 0.2."
