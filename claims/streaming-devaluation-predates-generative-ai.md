The article's economic analysis of the streaming era reports per-stream payouts of "$0.003 to $0.005 on Spotify" and argues the structural devaluation of recorded music predates generative AI by well over a decade. This is an analytical argument, not a tested empirical result.

> "Per-stream payouts remain a small fraction of a cent on most major platforms – on the order of $0.003 to $0.005 on Spotify, somewhat higher on Apple Music and Tidal, and lower still on YouTube Music's ad-supported tier."
