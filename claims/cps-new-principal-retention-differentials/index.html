New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals 2023¶

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals, Explore Their Previous Experiences. (2023). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://www.norc.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The eight-year observational analysis of CPS personnel data reports five-year retention rates for newly hired principals differing "by elementary (52%) versus high school (32%)" and across student-body racial composition categories. Descriptive percentages; no effect size printed.

"A newly hired principal’s likelihood of remaining in their position five years later differed by elementary (52%) versus high school (32%); by student body racial composition categories of predominantly Black (49%), Latinx (58%), racially mixed (57%), and Black and Latinx (23%); and by free/reduced-price lunch category."
