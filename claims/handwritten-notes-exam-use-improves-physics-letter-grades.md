## Evidence

### Adhikari 2025

Adhikari, C. M. (2025). Effective Note-taking and its Impact on Learning Undergraduate Introductory Physics Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21326](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21326)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study of 69 no-notetaking and 66 notetaking students in algebra-based introductory physics at FSU, using third midterm letter grades. The article reports "The percentage of students getting the letter grade "A" is more than triple with the implementation of notetaking" and that over 40% earned A grades; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "The percentage of students getting the letter grade "A" is more than triple with the implementation of notetaking"
