## Evidence

### Dhlamini 2016

Dhlamini, J. J. (2016). Enhancing learners’ problem solving performance in mathematics: A cognitive load perspective. European Journal of STEM Education, 1(1), 27-36. https://doi.org/10.20897/lectito.201604

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews and in-between activity questions in the pilot study. Responses such as "This problem reminds me of an earlier problem that we solved" were coded as reflecting schema construction.

> "It was observed that when faced with novel context-based problems, study participants reported thinking about how an earlier problem (example) had been solved."
