Discussion of the pre/post design with experimental and control groups: the article reports decreases in all measures and attributes them to habituation or the normal secondary-school interest decline, calling the control group essential for interpretation.

> "Results show a decrease in all measures during the total intervention period. These results coincide with observations from other contexts that use the same questionnaire."
