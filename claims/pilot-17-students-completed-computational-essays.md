`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot implementation in a fall 2018 introductory electricity and magnetism course at the University of Oslo, with 17 students working individually or in pairs on an open investigation challenge. The article reports "all students in the pilot group successfully completed computational essays," with 6-14 reported hours and varied topics.

> "In the end, all students in the pilot group successfully completed computational essays. Students reported spending around 6-14 hours on the project, and chose a roughly even spread of topics, with no two groups choosing exactly the same investigation question."
