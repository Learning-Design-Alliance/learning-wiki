## Evidence

### Sikström 2024

Sikström, P., Valentini, C., Sivunen, A., & Kärkkäinen, T. (2024). Pedagogical Agents Communicating and Scaffolding Students' Learning : High School Teachers' and Students' Perspectives. Computers and Education, 222, Article 105140. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105140

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from the PAs' activities theme in the interview study: teachers described scaffolding of self-regulated learning via goal setting, feedback, and "gradually offer hints and pose questions" instead of giving answers. A student similarly said a PA "could give little hints."

> "The teachers emphasized that when a PA supports a student, it should avoid directly providing the solution. Instead, it should gradually offer hints and pose questions that encourage students to think critically and solve problems on their own, which is more effective for learning and retention."
