## Evidence

### Ramsburg 2015

Ramsburg, J. T., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Category Change in the Absence of Cognitive Conflict. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Category+Change+in+the+Absence+of+Cognitive+Conflict%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of misconception-consistent and target-consistent response proportions per block (Figure 4) in Experiment 1; the authors report the curves for the two conditions "differ minimally" and that unlearning and re-learning "proceeded in parallel", contradicting the dissatisfaction-first prediction.

> "In addition, we observed that the increase in the participants' reliance on the target category was not delayed with respect to the decrease in the participants' reliance on the initial category; instead, both the unlearning and the re-learning proceeded in parallel."
