`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post crossover study of 326 physicians who took a precourse test, attended a 1.5-hour AI course, then took a postcourse test with GPT-4.0 permitted. The article reports "a mean difference of 20.68% (P<.001)"; pass rates rose from 21/326 to 191/326 (McNemar χ21=153.55). No effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
