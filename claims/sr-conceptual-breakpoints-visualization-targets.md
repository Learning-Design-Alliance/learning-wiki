## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article enumerates conceptual breakpoints in learning SR — abandoning everyday common sense, the 4-D nature of space-time, generalizing between relativistic and non-relativistic schemes, invariance of c, and connecting invariance and simultaneity to SR postulates — and states that the first, second, and fifth can be directly attacked with properly contextualized visualizations. `q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical/didactic analysis in section 2.3.1, drawing on Janssen's "Arches and Scaffolds" idea, lists breakpoints where "students are likely to misunderstand or reject new information". The author states the first, second, and fifth points "can be directly attacked with visualizations of SR properly contextualized within the didactic sequence"; the third and fourth are declared out of scope. No empirical data are offered.
