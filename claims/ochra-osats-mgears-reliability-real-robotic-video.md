## Evidence

### Sirajudeen N. 2024

Sirajudeen N., Boal M., Anastasiou D., Xu J., Stoyanov D., Kelly J., Collins J. W., Sridhar A., Mazomenos E., Francis N. K. (2024). Deep learning prediction of error and skill in robotic prostatectomy suturing. Surgical Endoscopy 38(12). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11341-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability analysis of two blinded raters scoring 20 videos from the SAR-RARP50 dataset. The article reports "strong IRR for OSATS scores (r = 0.7835" and strong M-GEARS reliability (r = 0.7026); OCHRA showed very strong IRR (r = 0.8726, p < 0.001).

> "There was strong IRR for OSATS scores (r = 0.7835, 95% CI [0.5219, 0.9104], p < 0.001) and ranks (r = 0.7609, 95% CI [0.48, 0.9003], p < 0.001). Strong IRR was demonstrated for M-GEARS scores (r = 0.7026"

### Sirajudeen N. 2024 (2)

Sirajudeen N., Boal M., Anastasiou D., Xu J., Stoyanov D., Kelly J., Collins J. W., Sridhar A., Mazomenos E., Francis N. K. (2024). Deep learning prediction of error and skill in robotic prostatectomy suturing. Surgical Endoscopy 38(12). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11341-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rater training analysis on 10 JIGSAWS videos before the main annotation. The article reports "OSATS (rho = 0.954" and "M-GEARS (rho = 0.841" between the two trained raters.

> "Strong to very strong IRR was demonstrated on the JIGSAWS dataset between the two raters in both the OSATS (rho = 0.954, 95% CI [0.806, 0.990], p < 0.001) and M-GEARS (rho = 0.841, 95% CI [0.433, 0.963], p = 0.002) scoring systems."
