Moderator analysis by cooperative learning technique (STAD, EKM, LT, TAI, unstructured, undefined) reported in Table 3 of the meta-analysis. The article reports no significant differences among techniques in terms of the general effect size.

> "it was observed that the most effective cooperative learning method for increasing mathematics achievement was unstructured ( d+ = 0.91) and STAD techniques ( d+ = 0.72). For the effect among the grades, no significant differences were seen among the effect sizes for seven grades."
