### Kreiter Clarence D 2013

Kreiter Clarence D. (2013). A measurement perspective on affirmative action in U.S. medical education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.20531

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of measurement research on changing tests and supplementing measures concludes that "replacing existing cognitive tests with alternate validated testing formats will not significantly reduce racial subgroup differences". Meta-analytic summaries show alternate variables in composites will not eliminate underrepresentation at medical school selection ratios.

> "The research shows that all predictive and validated cognitive testing formats display similar mean differences across racial categories, and replacing existing cognitive tests with alternate validated testing formats will not significantly reduce racial subgroup differences"
