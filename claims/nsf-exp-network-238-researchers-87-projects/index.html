Social network analysis visualizing investigator relationships, with projects (blue nodes) connected to investigators (red nodes) and topic areas via has-expertise edges. The analysis aimed to show diversity of expertise and intensity of inter-expertise collaboration.

"This network depicts 238 researchers connected to 87 projects and represents 116 expertise areas that form the “building blocks” for interdisciplinary research in this community."
