## Evidence

### Rooholamin et al. 2016

Amir Rooholamin, Reza Biria & Hamid Reza Haghverdi. (2016). Effectiveness of Proactive Intensive FFI in Cultivating Grammatical Knowledge of Iranian EFL Learners at Different Proficiency Levels. English Language Teaching, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test comparing pretest and posttest grammar scores of the intermediate experimental group (n = 29). Posttest mean was 15.82 versus pretest 12.10; the article reports "statistically significant" improvement with t = -33.91, p = .000.

> "Since the Sig. (2-tailed) value represents a p value lower than the significance level (.000 < .05) for the IEG, it could be construed that the difference between the pretest and posttest grammar scores of the IEG learners had been statistically significant."

### Rooholamin et al. 2016 (2)

Amir Rooholamin, Reza Biria & Hamid Reza Haghverdi. (2016). Effectiveness of Proactive Intensive FFI in Cultivating Grammatical Knowledge of Iranian EFL Learners at Different Proficiency Levels. English Language Teaching, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t test for the advanced experimental group (n = 28): posttest mean 16.46 versus pretest 14.35, t = -19.66, p = .000. The article concludes both proficiency levels improved significantly after proactive intensive FFI.

> "The same was also the case with the pretest and posttest grammar scores of the AEG. This means that for both intermediate and advanced group learners, the grammatical knowledge of the learners improved significantly as a result of exposure to proa ctive intensive FFI."
