### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise linear regression in an exploratory prospective cohort study of 65 Croatian medical students followed from first to sixth year, with first-year GPA excluded due to its high correlation with sixth-year GPA. The regression found "Only the SGE score was a significant predictor of final academic performance" with β=0.35 (95%CI=0.06-0.64) and 12.3% of variance explained.
