Zhandire, T. T., Bakare-Fatungase, O. D., & Mkhize, N. (2026). Mental health, resilience, and academic outcomes among university students: a global scoping review with particular attention to African and Global South contexts. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05242-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The scoping review's thematic synthesis of empirical studies (RQ3) reports this negative relationship across contexts, citing a large South African study where "increasing depression severity associated with greater risk of academic progression delay"; no pooled effect size is printed.

> "A consistent negative relationship between psychological distress and academic outcomes was identified across contexts. A large South African study (N = 5,912) identified a dose-response relationship between depression and academic outcomes, with increasing depression severity associated with greater risk of academic progression delay"
