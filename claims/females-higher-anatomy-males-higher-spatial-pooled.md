## Evidence

### Erli Sarilita 2022

Erli Sarilita, Lita Yurika Ambar, Firman Dani Rizali, Tracey Wilkinson, Sri Susilawati, Risti Saptarini, Dudi Aripin, Endang Sjamsudin. (2022). Spatial ability and anatomy learning performance among dental students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2022.239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison using independent t-tests on pooled 1st- and 2nd-year dental students; Table 2 reports the pooled anatomy contrast at p=0.005 favoring females, with PSVT-R p<0.001 and MRT p=0.003 favoring males. The article prints no effect size.

> "When the 1st- and 2nd-year cohorts were pooled, males showed significantly higher spatial ability test scores than females in both tests, but in contrast, females presented significantly higher anatomy scores than males (Table 2)."
