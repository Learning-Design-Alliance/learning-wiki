DreamBox Math evidence summary 2024¶

DreamBox Math evidence summary. (2024). https://support.dreambox.com/hc/en-us/articles/204906318-System-Requirements-for-Schools

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Randomized study "in 24 schools across North Carolina with over 10,000 students in grades K-2 and 4-5". Significant positive effect for K-2 on the Number Knowledge Test; a negligible +0.03 for grades 4-5 on the state assessment.

"On the Number Knowlege Test, K-2 students in schools using DreamBox significantly outperformed control students (effect size = +0.12), whereas results on the North Carolina End-of-Grade assessment were not meaningfully higher for 4th and 5th-grade students exposed to DreamBox (effect size = +0.03)."
