Tung Nhu Nguyen 2015¶

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA interaction test reported in the results section for post-game competence among the 53 students; the article reports the product term as significant with the printed statistic F(5.620) = 0.022 < 0.05 and no effect size.

"However, with pre-game competence as a covariate, the product of pre-game competence and learning condition is also significant (F(5.620) = 0.022 < 0.05), meaning that an interaction effect exists between these two variables."
