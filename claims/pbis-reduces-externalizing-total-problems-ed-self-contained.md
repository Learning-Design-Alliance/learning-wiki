# PBIS implementation is associated with significant pre-to-post reductions in externalizing and total problem behaviors of students with ED in self-contained settings

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Benner 2008

Benner, G. J., Beaudoin, K. M., Chen, P.-Y., Davis, C., & Ralston, N. (2008). The Impact of Intensive Positive Behavioral Supports on the Behavioral Functioning of Students with Emotional Disturbance: How Much Does Fidelity Matter? JBAIC, Volume 1, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Impact+of+Intensive+Positive+Behavioral+Supports

`q2 · i1`

Pre-post naturalistic study of 37 students with ED in self-contained classrooms; Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Tests on TRF scores showed "statistically significant reductions" on Externalizing (p = .028) and Total Problems (p = .004). No effect size printed.
