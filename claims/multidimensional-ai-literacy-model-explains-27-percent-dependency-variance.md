## Evidence

### Hilit Maizel 2026

Hilit Maizel, Maya Kalman Halevi, Miri Sarid, and Rony Tutian. (2026). Understanding Student Dependency on AI: The Role of AI Literacy, Academic Self-Efficacy, and Resource Management Strategies. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 1123. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16071123

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 478-student analytic sample with AI dependency as criterion. Multicollinearity diagnostics showed no concern (all VIF ≤ 1.87). The article reports "a meaningful increase over the 22.1%" for the multidimensional treatment.

> "The overall model was statistically significant, F(8, 469) = 22.03, p < 0.001, and accounted for 27.3% of the variance in AI dependency (R2 = 0.273; adjusted R2 = 0.261)—a meaningful increase over the 22.1% obtained when AI literacy was modeled as a single aggregate score"
