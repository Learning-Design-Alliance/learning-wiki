Bugler 2021¶

Bugler, D. (2021). School District Collaboration: Lessons From Districts Participating in California Education Partners' California Language & Learning Initiative (CALLI). WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616075

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview participants described adopting PDSA cycles after years of planning without launching: "Now we launch lots of little trials and see what works and what doesn't and learn from that." Self-reported perceptions, no outcome data or effect size.

"One participant noted the importance of not trying to do everything at once: "Let's try one thing. Start small, then scale. That was the biggest learning for me.""
