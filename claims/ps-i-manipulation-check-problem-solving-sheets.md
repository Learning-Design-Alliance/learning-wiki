## Evidence

### Esther Ziegler 2021

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA over four process measures (mathematics grade F(4,74) = 6.36, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.26; condition F(4,74) = 81.12, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.81), followed by univariate ANOVAs; the solution rate on the six problem-solving sheets served as a manipulation check.

> "Separate univariate ANOVAs on the process measures revealed an expected significant effect in favor of the I-PS condition on problem solving, F(1,77) = 243.42, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.76, indicating that the implementation of the two conditions worked as anticipated"
