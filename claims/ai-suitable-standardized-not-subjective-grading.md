`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the second phase of the six-instructor interview study, participants shared impressions of integrating AI tools like ChatGPT into grading. The article reports that instructors were "open-minded and positive" yet "most emphasized the necessity of maintaining a certain level of human control". No effect size is printed.

> "All instructors expressed an open-minded and positive attitude toward using AI to support the evaluation of project reports. At the same time, most emphasized the necessity of maintaining a certain level of human control over key parts of the grading workflow."
