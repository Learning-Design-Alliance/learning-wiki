# Tutoring efficacy does not differ for students with mathematics difficulty alone versus mathematics plus reading difficulty

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2009 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The interaction between difficulty status (MD vs. MDRD) and treatment was not significant for the NC outcome, so the subtyping scheme did not predict differential response to tutoring. [→ Fuchs 2009](#fuchs-2009)

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2009

Fuchs, L. S., Powell, S. R., Seethaler, P. M., Cirino, P. T., Fletcher, J. M., Fuchs, D., Hamlett, C. L., & Zumeta, R. O. (2009). Remediating Number Combination and Word Problem Deficits Among Students With Mathematics Difficulties: A Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 101(3), 561-576. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014701

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest analysis of covariance in the randomized trial tested treatment, difficulty status, and site interactions; the Difficulty Status × Treatment interaction was trimmed as not significant. The article states "there were no interactions among the three factors or with pretest."

> "For NCs, there were no interactions among the three factors or with pretest, including the interaction between difficulty status and treatment."
