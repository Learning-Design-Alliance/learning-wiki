## Evidence

### Vajsbaher Tina 2018

Vajsbaher Tina, Schultheis Holger, Francis Nader K. (2018). Spatial cognition in minimally invasive surgery: a systematic review. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-018-0416-1

`q2 · i?`

The review's methodological-limitations analysis of its own 26 included studies. It states the expert sample is too small to infer what novice performance ought to be like, that 'novice' and 'experienced' labels were used heterogeneously, and that "the transferability of the cognitive skills from the simulator in the OR remains unknown".

> "out of 1214 participants from all 26 reviewed studies, only 13 of them were experienced surgeons (attending or consultant surgeons), which either acted as a comparison group or were used as a method of judging performance."
