### Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim 2020

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of the Cognitive, Affective, and Interpersonal Outcomes of Flipped Classrooms in Higher Education. Educ. Sci. 2020, 10, 115. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10040115

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random effects subgroup analysis by subject area across the 43 studies (Table 6). STEM subjects including "Engineering, ES = 0.17, Physics, ES = 0.15, Math, ES = 0.13" and Chemistry (ES = 0.10) showed small effects; Computer Science (ES = 0.96) and Education (ES = 0.80) the largest. Business (ES = −0.93, k = 1) requires careful interpretation.

> "The eﬀect sizes by subject were Computer Science, ES = 0.96, Education, ES = 0.80, Psychology, ES = 0.45, Nursing, ES = 0.44, Mixed subject, ES = 0.44, Science, ES = 0.43, Medicine, ES = 0.37, Pharmaceutical Medicine, ES = 0.29, Physiology, ES= 0.22, Management, ES = 0.19, Engineering, ES = 0.17, Physics, ES = 0.15, Math, ES = 0.13, English, ES = 0.12, Chemistry, ES= 0.10, and Business, ES = −0.93."
