### Pedersen Tom Andre MD 2026

Pedersen Tom Andre MD, Mori Yuichi Prof., Botteri Edoardo Dr., Engjom Trond Dr., Seip Birgitte Dr., Dimcevski Georg Gjorgji Dr., Havre Roald Flesland Prof. (2026). Learning and deskilling effects of artificial intelligence in colonoscopy among endoscopists with different levels of experience: a pragmatic, prospective trial. Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2858-7084

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable generalized linear mixed models showed male sex (OR 1.33, 95%CI 1.12–1.56), a 5-year age increase (OR 1.17, 95%CI 1.14–1.20), and surveillance indication (OR 2.49, 95%CI 1.97–3.14) were associated with higher PDR-5, while IBD was associated with lower PDR-5 (OR 0.52, 95%CI 0.39–0.70).

> "Sex, age, and indication also significantly affected PDR-5 across all endoscopist groups."
