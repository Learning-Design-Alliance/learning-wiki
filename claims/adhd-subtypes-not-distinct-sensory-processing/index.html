Ghanizadeh A 2011¶

Ghanizadeh A. (2011). Sensory Processing Problems in Children with ADHD, a Systematic Review. Psychiatry Investig 2011;8:89-94. https://doi.org/10.4306/pi.2011.8.2.89

The review's abstract-level synthesis states that "Findings do not support that ADHD subtypes are distinct disorders with regard to sensory processing problems"; the discussion repeats this for tactile and auditory processing. No effect size is printed.
