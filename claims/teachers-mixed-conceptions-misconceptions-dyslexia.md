## Evidence

### Erin K. Washburn 2022

Erin K. Washburn, Benjamin C. Heddy, Emily Binks-Cantrell, Tiffany Peltier. (2022). What do teachers know about dyslexia? It’s complicated! The State Education Standard. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/what-do-teachers-know-about-dyslexia-its-complicated/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 524 teachers in a Midwestern state with recent dyslexia legislation, administered the Dyslexia Knowledge Questionnaire-2 with 37 Likert scale items. The article reports "a complicated conceptualization of dyslexia" across concepts, with no effect sizes printed.

> "Results indicated a complicated conceptualization of dyslexia. For some concepts, teachers either mostly held scientific conceptions or held misconceptions. For other concepts, teachers were either distributed between scientific conceptions, misconceptions, and/or unsure"
