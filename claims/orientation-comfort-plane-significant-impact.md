## Evidence

### Mary Bridget Kustusch 2015

Mary Bridget Kustusch. (2015). Assessing the impact of representational and contextual problem features on student use of right-hand rules. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic and log-linear regression analyses of 2119 problems found both features significant at p < 0.01 for both measures, indicating "these features separately contribute to the diﬃculty of using a RHR". The study used the most physically easy and most physically awkward orientations.

> "The impact of both physical awkwardness ('Comfort') and the plane of the given vectors ('Plane') was signiﬁcant at the p < 0.01 level for both correctness and response time"
