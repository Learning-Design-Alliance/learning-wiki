## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2019

Zhao Xin, Yu Xiaofeng, Zhang Xiaomei. (2019). The Role of Peer Support Education Model in Management of Glucose and Lipid Levels in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Chinese Adults. Journal of Diabetes Research. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/5634030

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the randomized study of 120 T2DM patients: the article reports "no significant difference in decreasing FBG and HbA1c levels between the two method education" (F = 0.54, p = 0.46; F = 0.42, p = 0.52). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "At the same time, there was no significant difference in decreasing FBG and HbA1c levels between the two method education (F = 0.54, p = 0.46; F = 0.42, p = 0.52), as shown in Table 2 and Figure 1."
