Evidence¶

Doyle C 2018¶

Doyle C, Smeaton AF, Roche RAP and Boran L (2018) Inhibition and Updating, but Not Switching, Predict Developmental Dyslexia and Individual Variation in Reading Ability. Front. Psychol. 9:795. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00795

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Case-control ANOVA on the computerized coding task (Wechsler, 2003), scored as trials correctly completed after 30 s, in the dyslexia vs control sample. The article reports "a processing speed impairment" with Cohen's d = 0.60.

"Dyslexia is also associated with a processing speed impairment [F(1, 54) = 4.84, p < 0.05, Cohen's d = 0.60; Figure 2]."
