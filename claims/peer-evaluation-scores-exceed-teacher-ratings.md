## Evidence

### Jin Peipei 2025

Jin Peipei, Chen Jinyu, Wang Ping, Xie Yadian, Zhang Cen, Shen Liqiong, He Li, Ye Yanqing. (2025). Application of peer-assisted learning under the LSPPDM mode in resident clinical skill training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06813-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort (Grade 2023) comparison of peer versus teacher scoring of the same randomly assigned skill videos, reported in Results Table 3; all differences statistically significant (P < 0.001). No effect size is printed.

> "the three total scores, internal medicine mean scores, surgical mean scores and emergency mean scores of the peer mutual evaluation were 98.15 ± 2.63, 98.10 ± 2.47, 97.62 ± 4.57 and 98.74 ± 1.96, respectively, which were all higher than the 90.84.±3.24, 90.05 ± 3.95, 87.64 ± 8.30 and 94.84 ± 3.72, of teacher ratings"
