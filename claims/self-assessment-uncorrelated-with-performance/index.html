q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's discussion states that "self-assessed abilities are uncorrelated with actual performance measures", underpinning its recommendation against relying on Kirkpatrick level 1 outcomes.

"Kirkpatrick level 1, being self-assessment, is generally not recommended in medical education studies, as studies of self-assessment have shown that self-assessed abilities are uncorrelated with actual performance measures."
