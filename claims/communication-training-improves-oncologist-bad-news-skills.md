The commentary cites Fallowfield et al.'s randomized controlled trial of a Cancer Research UK communication skills training model for oncologists, concluding that "experience and self-learning are not sufficient and must be augmented by specialized training".

> "A randomized study by Fallowfield et al. demonstrated that even experienced oncologists have difficulties in these areas that are not solved by their clinical experience, but that proper training of needed skills improves communication"
