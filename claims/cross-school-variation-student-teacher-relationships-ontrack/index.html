Moeller 2018¶

Moeller, E., Seeskin, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2018). Practice-Driven Data: Lessons from Chicago's Approach to Research, Data, and Practice in Education. Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/practice-driven-data-lessons-chicagos-approach-research-data-and-practice-education

q2 · i? · design · r1

The paper reports a Consortium research finding on cross-school variation: after controlling for demographics and prior achievement, ninth-grade course failure rates remained substantially different across high schools. The paper presents this as evidence that schools have agency to move student outcomes. No effect size or test statistic is printed in this passage.

"For example, in the same research report, Consortium researchers found that even when controlling for a number of considerations like the demographic and prior achievement data of CPS high schools, rates of ninth-grade course failure were substantially different across high schools."

Moeller 2018 (2)¶

Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/practice-driven-data-lessons-chicagos-approach-research-data-and-practice-education

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The same Consortium report is described as identifying school culture measures — especially student-teacher relationships — associated with better-than-expected Freshman OnTrack rates. The paper frames this as an evidence-based theory of action: building better student-teacher relationships might yield higher OnTrack rates. The association is reported without printed coefficients.

"Furthermore, researchers identiﬁed several measures of school culture—especially those related to student-teacher relationships—that were associated with better-than-expected Freshman OnT rack rates."
