## Subclaims
`q2 i2` On days when children report higher mastery goals than usual in the morning, they report more academic success that evening (β = .255). [→ Neubauer 2022](#neubauer-2022)

## Evidence

### Neubauer 2022

Neubauer, A. B., Schmidt, A., Schmiedek, F., & Dirk, J. (2022). Dynamic Reciprocal Relations of Achievement Goals with Daily Experiences of Academic Success and Failure: An Ambulatory Assessment Study. https://osf.io/gfvja/?view_only=b87fe179cf134471948726cb0e482a80

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Dynamic structural equation model of four weeks of ambulatory assessment data from 108 Grade 5 students, with morning goals predicting evening reports. Mastery goals predicted success ("β = .255, 95% credible interval [.186, .319]"); the other two goals did not.

> "Results further showed that daily academic success was predicted by mastery goals, β = .255, 95% credible interval [.186, .319], but the associations with performance-approach goals, β = -.028, [-.083, .030], and performance-avoidance goals, β = .050, [-.011, .112], were not statistically meaningful."
