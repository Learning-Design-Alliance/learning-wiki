Sustainability challenge from interviews: "if a school leader who believes in RJ leaves his or her leadership position, the implementation of RJ practices often ends." Funding ending also ends implementation.

"They noted that if a school leader who believes in RJ leaves his or her leadership position, the implementation of RJ practices often ends."
