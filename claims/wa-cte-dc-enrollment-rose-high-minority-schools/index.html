Kristen Hengtgen 2023¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641932

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of Washington State Report Card data on CTE DC enrollment by race for SY 2018-19 and 2021-22. The brief reports enrollment "increased by roughly 5 percentage points for all students" and that between-group differences are relatively small in both years.

"Enrollment increased by roughly 5 percentage points for all students between the 2019 and 2022 school years. In addition, differences in enrollment between student groups — in both years — are relatively small."

Kristen Hengtgen 2023 (2)¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641932

q2 · i? · associational · r2

School-level analysis comparing CTE DC enrollment across low, medium, and high Black and Latino population schools. High-concentration schools showed a "spike of 8 percentage points between 2018-19 and 2021-22 — from 38% to 46%"; the brief notes CTE's controversial history of funneling marginalized students away from college prep.

"CTE DC enrollment rose during the pandemic, especially in schools with high concentrations of Black and Latino students (which experienced a spike of 8 percentage points between 2018-19 and 2021-22 — from 38% to 46%)."
