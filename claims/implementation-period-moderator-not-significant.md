### Turgut 2018

Turgut, S., & Gülşen Turgut, İ. (2018). The Effects of Cooperative Learning on Mathematics Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 663-680. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11345a

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis reported in Table 7 across implementation periods from 11-15 hours to 31 or more hours plus an unidentified category (e.g., 16-20 hours ES=0,838 with 12 effect sizes; unidentified ES=0,998 with 25). The test did not exceed the critical value, so no significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "It is seen that Q value is smaller than the critical value that corresponds to 5 degree of freedom in chi -square table (Q B=10,594, p=0,060). Regarding this, there is not a statistically significant difference between groups in terms of implementation period."
