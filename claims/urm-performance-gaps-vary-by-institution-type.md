> "We found that URM students underperformed relative to non-URM students on exams at both selective and inclusive 4-year institutions, but not at community colleges (mixed-effects model: βCC = −0.23 ± 0.12, p = 0.30; βinclusive = −0.27 ± 0.07, p = 0.003; and βSelective = −0.44 ± 0.05, p < 0.001)."
