`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Experiment 2 compared direction bias indices between males and females with an independent-samples t-test; the test found "no differences" between the groups (dcohen = -0.04) and the mean difference was centered around 0 (Δmean = 0.01, 95% CI [−0.17 0.2]).

> "For the direction bias (Fig. 5A), no differences (t36 = −0.12; p = 0.9; dcohen = −0.04; BF10 = 0.32) were found between males (M = 0.12; SD = 0.3) and females (M = 0.11; SD = 0.29)."
