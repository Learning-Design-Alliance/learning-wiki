## Evidence

### Raffael Schmitt 2023

Raffael Schmitt, Martin Meyer and Nathalie Giroud. (2023). Improvements in naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in middle-aged and older adults after 3 weeks of computer-based speechreading training. npj Science of Learning 8:32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00179-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of average audiovisual pre-training scores by learner status, reported in Fig. 4 with the model statistic OR = 0.54, 95% CI[0.33, 0.90], p = .018, suggesting learners perform worse at pre-training than non-learners. The article notes baseline and change scores are inherently correlated in clinical trials.

> "Learners and non-learners differed signi ﬁcantly in the pre-training score in both active control and speechreading training group."
