## Evidence

### Leijte Erik 2019

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study (five-point Likert scale, mean > 3.5 considered valid) completed after each task by participants across the three expertise groups. The reported means for realism, didactic value and usability on all three tasks exceeded the validity threshold, and ANOVA found no significant group differences on the overall scores.

> "Face and content validity results showed a mean positive opinion on realism (3.9 Task 1, 3.6 Task 2 and 3.7 Task 3), didactic value (4.0, 3.4 and 3.7, respectively) and usability (4.2. 3.7 and 4.0, respectively). There were no significant differences in these outcomes between the specified expertise groups."
