Chi-square tests across 56 sites also found significant timing-by-grade (χ2(2) = 52.10, p < .01) and timing-by-baseline-DIBELS (χ2(2) = 27.26, p < .01) associations: over 80 percent of grade 1–2 students and 84 percent of at-risk students received during-school tutoring versus 68 percent of grade 3 students.
