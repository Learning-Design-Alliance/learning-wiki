## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Condelli 2010

Condelli, L., Cronen, S., Bos, J., Tseng, F., and Altuna, J. (2010). The Impact of a Reading Intervention for Low-Literate Adult ESL Learners—Executive Summary (NCEE 2011-4004). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom observation analysis of instructional intervals in the randomized study (N = 980 intervals for Sam and Pat group and 1,034 for control), reporting that "the difference was statistically significant" based on a 2-tailed t-test at the 0.05 level.

> "The Sam and Pat classrooms spent more time on reading development instruction than control classrooms (66 percent vs. 19 percent of observed time intervals, respectively), and the difference was statistically significant (Figure ES.1)."
