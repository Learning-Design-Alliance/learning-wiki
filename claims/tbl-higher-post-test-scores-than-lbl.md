### Xie Zhi-Bo 2025

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 28 studies comparing TBL and LBL final examination scores in medical education, using a random-effects model. The pooled analysis found "significantly higher post-test scores than students with LBL (SMD = 0.96, 95%CI 0.70 to 1.22".

> "We found that students with TBL method had significantly higher post-test scores than students with LBL (SMD = 0.96, 95%CI 0.70 to 1.22, I2 = 93.4%, random-effects model)"
