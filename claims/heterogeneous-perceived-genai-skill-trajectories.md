`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional retrospective questionnaire of 313 analyzed university responses; trajectory was a single nominal item (B13). Descriptive percentages show the three largest perceived trajectory groups, with fluctuating development reported by 12.8% and "little or no improvement" by 2.9%.

> "The most frequent was rapid early improvement followed by a plateau (34.2%), followed by steady improvement (28.4%) and delayed acceleration (21.1%)."
