## Evidence

### Fugelli Caroline Guldberg; Hansen Britt Sætre; Ersdal Hege; Kurz Martin 2025

Fugelli Caroline Guldberg; Hansen Britt Sætre; Ersdal Hege; Kurz Martin. (2025). Unveiling team needs: a qualitative study of simulation training for endovascular cerebral thrombectomy. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-002981

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interviews with 33 EVT team members at a single Norwegian centre analysed by inductive qualitative content analysis. Participants valued simulation for procedural knowledge, becoming "acquainted with equipment placement and movement within the angio suite", faces of others, and interprofessional communication practice.

> "EVT team members viewed the simulation training as a necessary form of preparation for EVT; however, the current simulation training programme did not adequately reflect the clinical delivery of EVT."
