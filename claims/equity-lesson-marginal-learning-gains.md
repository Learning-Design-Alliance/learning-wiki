## Evidence

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Do Tutors Learn from Equity Training and Can Generative AI Assess It?. In LAK25: The 15th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2025), March 03–07, 2025, Dublin, Ireland. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706531

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-effects ANOVA on 81 undergraduate remote tutors who completed all training and test items, with scenario as between-subjects factor and time as within-subjects factor. The article reports "a marginally significant main effect of time on tutor performance" with F(1, 79) = 3.20, p = .078; no effect size is printed.

> "The analysis revealed a marginally significant main effect of time on tutor performance, 𝐹(1, 79) = 3.20,𝑝 = .078, indicating an overall improvement in tutors’ performance from pretest to posttest."
