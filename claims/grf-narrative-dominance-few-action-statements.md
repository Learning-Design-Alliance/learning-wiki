# Guided reflection submissions are dominated by narrative statements (~60%) and broad growth statements (~30%), with few specific action or achievement statements

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In both the Metacognition and Vibrations & Waves courses, coded GRF statements are mostly narrative, followed by growth statements, with under 20% being specific action or achievement statements. [→ Amanda Matheson 2018](#amanda-matheson-2018)

## Evidence

### Amanda Matheson 2018

Amanda Matheson, Laura Wood, and Scott Franklin. (2018). Guided and Unguided Student Reflections. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding analysis of 399 Metacognition and 271 V&W GRF submissions using the rubric from Dounas-Frazer and Reinholz, with inter-rater reliability ⟨κ⟩ = 0.6. The authors report narrative statements "make up almost 60% of all coded statements" and growth statements almost 30%, with fewer than 20% action or achievement statements.

> "Both groups of student reﬂections are dominated by narrative statements, which make up almost 60% of all coded statements. This is followed by broad growth statements, which comprise almost 30% of all statements."
