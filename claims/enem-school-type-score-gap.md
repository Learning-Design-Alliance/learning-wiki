### Marta F. Barroso 2017 (2)

Marta F. Barroso, M. S. O. Massunaga and G. Rubini. (2017). Brazilian National High School Examination: assessing student's learning in Physics. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of official Censo Escolar enrollment data with Enem participation; the authors infer "an educational exclusion mechanism that begins even before the Enem signing up process".

> "circa 85%  of high school students  are enrolled in stat e schools (versus ~75% in Enem) and 13% in private schools (versus almost  one fourth in Enem), so it is evident that a significant percentage of state school students do not even try to enter university"
