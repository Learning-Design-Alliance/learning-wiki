## Subclaims
`q4 i?` The odds of earning a final grade of A or B did not differ for U-Pace versus concept mastery students (p = .102) or U-Pace versus amplified assistance students (p = .083), and concept mastery and amplified assistance did not differ (p = .959). [→ Fleming 2018](#fleming-2018)

## Evidence

### Fleming 2018

Fleming, R., Pedrick, L.E., Soiber, L., Kienzler, S., Fleming, R.R., & Reddy, D.M. (2018). Increasing undergraduate success: A randomized controlled trial of U-Pace instruction. Online Learning, 22(3), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i3.1317

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the same binary logistic regression of A or B grades, the pairwise contrasts among the three online conditions were not statistically significant; the quote prints "p = .102" and "p = .083" for U-Pace versus each component, and concept mastery versus amplified assistance was p = .959. Equivalence was not tested.

> "did not differ for U-Pace students and concept mastery students (Wald χ2  = 2.669, p = .102, Exp(B) = 1.51), and did not differ for U-Pace students and amplified assistance students (Wald χ2 = 2.99, p = .083, Exp(B) = 1.53)."
