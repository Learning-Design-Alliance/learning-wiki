q2 · i? · associational · r?

Survey-based evaluation comparing career paths of program participants and nonparticipants; the report states that "participation in loan repayment programs is associated with longer stays at the NIH and in research careers more generally." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"The findings suggest that eligibility for loan repayment is associated with higher recruitment success and that participation in loan repayment programs is associated with longer stays at the NIH and in research careers more generally."
