### Sporte 2016

Sporte, S.E., & Jiang, J.Y. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year 3 Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Year 3 (2014-15) MVMS teacher survey of CPS teachers, reported in the Findings section. The survey found "Almost 60 percent of teachers surveyed disagreed or strongly disagreed" about performance tasks and "72 percent disagreed or strongly disagreed" about value-added measures, while 76 percent agreed observations were fair.

> "Almost 60 percent of teachers surveyed disagreed or strongly disagreed with the statement that performance tasks were a fair way to evaluate their performance and 72 percent disagreed or strongly disagreed that value-added measures were a fair way to evaluate their performance"
