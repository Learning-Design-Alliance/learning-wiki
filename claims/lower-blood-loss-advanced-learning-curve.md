`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of mean blood loss (mL) across included studies; the printed mean difference MD 69.13 (95% CI 32.53 to 105.72; p < 0.001; I² = 46%) is unstandardized, so no standardized effect size is printed. Subgroup analyses confirmed the finding for RALP (MD 66.77) and laparoscopic (MD 71.41) approaches.

> "As for intraoperative blood loss, a significant reduction was observed in the advanced learning curve group (MD 69.13; 95% CI 32.53 to 105.72; p < 0.001; I² = 46%)."
