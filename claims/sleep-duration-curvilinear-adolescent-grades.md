`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional spline regression analysis of the 2019 YRBSS adolescent sample (N = 13,677) found a significant sleep effect, "F(3,34) = 20.81, p < 0.001". The discussion reports an inverse-U relationship with optimal grades at 7–9 h/night, below the 8–10 h recommendation.

> "Results from our sleep ANOVA model revealed a significant effect of sleep on academic achievement (F(3,34) = 20.81, p < 0.001)."
