## Evidence

### Herrmann 2025

Herrmann, S., & Bach, K. M. (2025). Ability grouping in German secondary schools: The effect of non‐academic track schools on the development of Math competencies. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 578–602. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12741

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statistical test comparing the ATTs of the low‐SES and high‐SES subgroup propensity score weighting analyses (Table 5). The test "indicates that the difference between these effects is not significant (p > .05)", leading the authors to conclude the influence "is similarly large for both groups".

> "Additional statistical testing between both ATTs indicates that the difference between these effects is not significant (p > .05), suggesting that the influence of attending a non‐academic track on Math competencies is similarly large for both groups (Table 5)."
