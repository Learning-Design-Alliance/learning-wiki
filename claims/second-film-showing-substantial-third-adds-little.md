Analysis of learning gains by number of showings in McTavish's four-group experiment (319 college freshmen), where each group saw one film once, twice, three times, or four times within one class period. The abstract states the first repetition "resulted in substantial increments in learning" but a third showing did not add materially or significantly. No effect size is printed.
