### Dange 2026

Dange, A. and Lopez, R.E. (2026). Chatbot Conversations in Physics Education: Using Artificial Intelligence to Analyze Student Reasoning through Computational Grounded Theory. arXiv:2603.04616. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational analysis of API request logs from the Fall 2024 course (47 consenting students). The September case study found the highest spike occurred on September 26, the day of Exam 1 (Module 4), with 92 recorded interactions; semester-wide logs showed further spikes on Exam 2, Module 11, Exam 3, and the Final Exam.
