## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Response time per item was moderately correlated with item word count for both formats of the ITE-CCM (r = 0.64 and 0.65) and the ITE-PA (r = 0.50 and 0.53). [→ Chen Dandan 2023](#chen-dandan-2023)

## Evidence

### Chen Dandan 2023

Chen Dandan, Harman Ann E., Sun Huaping, Ye Tianpeng, Gaiser Robert R. (2023). A comparison of 3- and 4-option multiple-choice items for medical subspecialty in-training examinations. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04277-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of response time per item against item word count (stem plus options) across the 150 matched items of each exam and format. The ITE-CCM correlations were "r = 0.64" (4-option) and r = 0.65 (3-option); ITE-PA correlations were r = 0.50 and r = 0.53.

> "Although response time per item was moderately correlated with item word count for both formats of the ITE-CCM [r = 0.64 (95% CI, 0.54 to 0.73) for 4-option items; r = 0.65 (95% CI, 0.55 to 0.74) for 3-option items] and ITE-PA [r = 0.50 (95% CI, 0.37 to 0.61) for 4-option items; r = 0.53 (95% CI, 0.40 to 0.63) for 3-option items]"
