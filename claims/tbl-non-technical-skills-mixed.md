### Alizadeh Maryam 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Umbrella review synthesis: three reviews reported improved communication skills; one review reported improved clinical decision-making; a meta-analysis reported significant improvement in learning skills in Chinese students; problem-solving results were mixed.

> "The results of this review confirmed that TBL has a positive effect on various sub-concepts of emotional intelligence, including the awareness of emotions of self and others, as well as the management of emotions [36]."
