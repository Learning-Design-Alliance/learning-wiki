## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Using Liu and Wang's t-test for floor effects, the tracing and pointing group reported lower extraneous load than controls (d = −0.52), supporting H3. [→ Yining Wang 2025](#yining-wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Yining Wang 2025

Yining Wang, Kexin Han, Paul Ginns. (2025). Tracing and Pointing Support Multimedia Learning: A Cross-Cultural Replication. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10005-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multi-item extraneous load self-reports were substantially skewed (70.5% of responses at the lowest three options), so the authors applied Liu and Wang's (2021) floor-effect t-test, finding "d = −0.52 [95% CI -∞, −0.03]".

> "extraneous load ratings reported by the tracing and pointing group (M = 1.67, SD = 1.19) were lower than those for the non-tracing and pointing group (M = 2.33, SD = 1.31), t(82) = −2.13, p = 0.021, d = −0.52 [95% CI -∞, −0.03]; thus, H3 was supported under this analysis."

### Yining Wang 2025 (2)

Yining Wang, Kexin Han, Paul Ginns. (2025). Tracing and Pointing Support Multimedia Learning: A Cross-Cultural Replication. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10005-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The conventional independent-groups t-test on the same extraneous load ratings did not reach significance ("t(82) = −1.35, p = 0.090"); no effect-size threshold was met for a supported finding under this analysis.

> "The difference in self-reported extraneous cognitive load ratings reported by the tracing and pointing condition (M = 1.66, SD = 1.71) and the non-tracing and pointing condition (M = 2.21, SD = 1.99) was also not statistically significant, t(82) = −1.35, p = 0.090, d = −0.30 [95% CI ∞, −0.14]."
