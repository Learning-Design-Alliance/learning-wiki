Same KLLR observational analysis of PR study logs. Fitting around the median NQ,tot = 40 gives ⟨∆GPE⟩ = (0.00±0.01) + (0.22±0.02)ν + (0.08±0.02)ν², so gains are quadratic in log study volume; variance reduction is "∆Var1→2 = (9± 3)%" when sampling evenly in log NQ,tot.
