## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A significant gender difference in self-efficacy favoring boys was present at all four measurement points, largest at baseline (d = 1.29) and reduced but still significant at the second during-training point (d = 0.75). [→ Hans van der Meij 2015](#hans-van-der-meij-2015)

## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2015

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Ruth Harmsen. (2015). Animated pedagogical agents effects on enhancing student motivation and learning in a science inquiry learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9378-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the 61-student experiment; gender favored boys at every point, with d = 1.29 at baseline, d = 1.26 at SE during-1, d = 0.75 at SE during-2, and d = 0.78 after training. The article notes girls increased their self-appraisals more than boys but remained significantly lower.

> "A signiﬁcant difference in self-efﬁcacy for gender favoring boys was found at each of the four measurement points. This gender difference was evident at the outset of the study (before), F(1,55) = 26.3, p = 0.00, d = 1.29"
