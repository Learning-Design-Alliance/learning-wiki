## Evidence

### Morimoto Y 2025

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Taxonomy classification of all 2880 items across six NMLE sessions using the study's four-type system, with Cochran-Armitage trend tests. The article reports the significant decline in Type I recall items with printed percentages and P<.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Over 23 years, the proportion of Type I items declined significantly, from 50.7% (279/550) in 2001 to 29.5% (118/400) in 2024 (P<.001)."

### Morimoto Y 2025 (2)

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same taxonomy analysis; the article reports significant increases in Type II and Type IIIb items with printed percentages and P values, noting the Type IIIb increase primarily accounts for the overall Type III increase while no significant trend was found for Type IIIa.

> "Conversely, the proportions of Types II and IIIb increased significantly, from 15.1% (83/550) to 25.8% (103/400; P=.007) and from 3.8% (21/550) to 18.8% (75/400; P<.001), respectively (Figures 3C and D)."
