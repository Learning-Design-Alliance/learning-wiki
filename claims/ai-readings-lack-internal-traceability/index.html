Sidorkin 2026¶

Sidorkin, A. M. (2026). From One-Size Texts to Tailored Readings: Student Experiences with AI-Generated Course Materials. Open Praxis, 18(3), 506–522. https://doi.org/10.55982/openpraxis.18.3.1141

q2 · i? · design · r2

Automated page-level screen over the full 4,487-page corpus for citation signals. The article reports "only about 0.80% of pages contain APA-style in-text citations" and that most claims remain difficult to audit from within the artifact itself.

"Across the 4,487 pages, only about 0.80% of pages contain APA-style in-text citations in the form “(Author, year)” or similar. URLs appear on about 0.53% of pages, and DOI strings are essentially absent."

The same automated screen identified institution-specific assertive statements. The article calls this "the most consequential failure mode in an instructional setting, because specificity creates a higher cost of being wrong than generic content."

"About 1.03% of pages contain patterns pairing “Sacramento State” or “Sac State” with assertive policy verbs."
