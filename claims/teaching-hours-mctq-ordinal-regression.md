`q2 i3` Time teaching anyone was significantly associated with MCTQ teaching rating (likelihood ratio p = 0.02); those teaching 15–20 hours weekly had OR = 79.32 (95% CI: 5.32–1182.6; p = 0.002), and those teaching fellows 10–15 hours weekly had 14.4 times higher odds of a higher MCTQ average rating (95% CI: 1.09–189.43, p = 0.04).
