## Evidence

### Junod Perron Noelle 2016

Junod Perron Noelle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). The quality of feedback during formative OSCEs depends on the tutors' profile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0815-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Objective content analysis of the same 140 audiotaped direct-observation sessions: mean items per feedback were 4.76 for history taking, 5.17 for physical examination, 2.30 for communication, and 0.64 for global performance (Table 4).

> "We found that feedback content focused on history taking and physical exam skills and to a lesser extent on communication skills and rarely included global comments on performance"
