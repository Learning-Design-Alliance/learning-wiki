### L. E. Frailey and C. M. Crane 1912

L. E. Frailey and C. M. Crane. (1912). Correlation of Excellence in Different School Subjects Based on a Study of School Grades. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of average grades of 18 girls and 14 boys across the six tabulated subjects. The printed table shows girl advantages of 1 point in civics, 2 in geometry, 5 in Latin and 2 in English, with algebra and history equal; the authors conclude "in none of the six subjects does the average boy excel the average girl."

> "(2) the average girl student excels the average boy student in Latin to a greater degree than in any of the six subjects selected; (3) the average girl seems to be slightly more intelligent than the average boy; or perhaps she goes less into outside activities and therefore gives more time to the preparation of her studies; and (4) in none of the six subjects does the average boy excel the average girl."
