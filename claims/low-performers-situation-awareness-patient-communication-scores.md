## Evidence

### Hjorth‐Hansen KR 2024

Hjorth‐Hansen KR, Rosvig L, Hvidman L, Kierkegaard O, Uldbjerg N, Manser T, Brogaard L. (2024). Video analysis of real‐life shoulder dystocia to assess technical and non‐technical performance. Acta Obstetricia et Gynecologica Scandinavica. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of Global Assessment of Team Performance item means between the two low-scoring and four high-scoring teams in the 17-case video review. The article reports means of 1.0 vs 3.5 for situation awareness and 1.5 vs 3.8 for patient communication; these are descriptive means with no test statistic printed.

> "Teams with low technical performance had a mean score of 1.0 in “situation awareness,” as compared with a score of 3.5 in teams with high technical performance. Similar results were obtained for “communication with the patient,” with a mean score of 1.5 vs 3.8"
