## Evidence

### Limeri 2025

Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results from the 1537-undergraduate survey: the "original mindset measure failed to detect a relationship between mindset and intent to persist" while the ULTrA fixed factor significantly explained variance in it; the combined model showed 5% more variance explained with ULTrA included.

> "The original measure model indicated that the original mindset measure failed to detect a relationship between mindset and intent to persist (β = −0.02, p = 0.62; Figure 1A). However, in the ULTrA model, the fixed factor of the ULTrA significantly explained variance in intent to persist (β = −0.20, p < 0.001; Figure 1B)."

### Limeri 2025 (2)

Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent factor correlations with course grades collected from institutional data after the semester: the ULTrA fixed factor correlated with grade (r = −0.10, p = 0.008) while "the original mindset measure did not correlate with course grade"; the ULTrA added 3% variance in the combined model.

> "Fixed mindset as measured by the ULTrA correlated with course grade in the expected direction with small effect size (r = −0.10, p = 0.008; Table 4). However, the original mindset measure did not correlate with course grade (r = 0.04, p = 0.13; Table 4)."
