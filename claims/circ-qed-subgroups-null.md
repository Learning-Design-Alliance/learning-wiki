# In the Bramlett quasi-experiment, no comprehension subtest or ability-level subgroup showed a statistically significant CIRC effect after WWC adjustments

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Subgroup analyses for medium-ability and high-ability third graders showed no statistically significant differences on any CAT outcome (all p > 0.05), with effect sizes ranging from –0.09 to 0.26. [→ bramlett-subgroups](#bramlett-subgroups)

## Evidence

### bramlett-subgroups

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Beginning Reading intervention report: Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/109

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplemental subgroup findings from Bramlett (1994), where students were divided into ability levels based on CAT total reading percentile rankings; the largest subgroup effect size was 0.26 for high-ability students on CAT Reading Comprehension, p > 0.05. The lowest 33% subgroup was excluded because baseline equivalence was not established.
