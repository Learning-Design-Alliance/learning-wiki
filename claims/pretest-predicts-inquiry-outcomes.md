## Evidence

### Eman Ganaiem 2025

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA in the experimental design topic (n = 113) found pre-test a significant positive predictor of both application and transfer scores, partial η² = 0.257, a large multivariate effect.

> "Pre-test scores showed a significant positive relationship with post-test scores, indicating a main effect on application and transfer scores: Wilk's Λ = 0.743, F (2, 106) = 18.371, p < 0.01, partial η2 = 0.257 (Fig. 3)."
