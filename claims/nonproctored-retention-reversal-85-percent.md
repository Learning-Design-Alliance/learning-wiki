> "AI-susceptible items becomemorelikely to be answered correctly after ChatGPT's release than AI-resistant items, by 5.61% per quarter on the log-odds scale in the random-assignment subsample (β= + 0.0561, p= 0.021). The cumulative effect is an 85% increase in the odds of correct response."
