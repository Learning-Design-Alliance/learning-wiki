## Evidence

### Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika 2015

Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika, Yoshikawa Kayoko, Yasuoka Junko, Poudel Krishna C, Fernando Nilani, Jayatilleke Achala Upendra, Jimba Masamine. (2015). Training Sri Lankan public health midwives on intimate partner violence: a pre- and post-intervention study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1674-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on 408 PHMs' scale scores. Tables 4–6 show the total barrier score fell from 2.43 to 1.14, responsibility rose from 3.20 to 4.60, and self-confidence from 1.81 to 2.75, each p < 0.001, with all individual items also significant.

> "PHMs' perceived barriers decreased from 2.43 to 1.14 (p < 0.001)."
