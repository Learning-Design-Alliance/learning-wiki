Evidence¶

Kenneth Fortson 2015¶

Kenneth Fortson, Philip Gleason, Emma Kopa, Natalya Verbitsky-Savitz. (2015). Horseshoes, Hand Grenades, and Treatment Effects? Reassessing Whether Nonexperimental Estimators are Biased. Economics of Education Review, vol. 44. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/horseshoes-hand-grenades-and-treatment-effects-reassessing-whether-nonexperimental-estimators

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The study benchmarks nonexperimental regression and propensity score estimators against a large-scale RCT of charter schools, finding that "baseline data that are strongly predictive of the key outcome measures considerably reduces" bias. No effect size is printed.

"The use of baseline data that are strongly predictive of the key outcome measures considerably reduces but might not completely eliminate bias."
