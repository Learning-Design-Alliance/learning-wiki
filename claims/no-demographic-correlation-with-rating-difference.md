## Evidence

### Castanelli Damian J 2024

Castanelli Damian J, Woods Jennifer B, Chander Anusha R, Weller Jennifer M. (2024). Trainee anaesthetist self-assessment using an entrustment scale in workplace-based assessment. Anaesthesia and Intensive Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/0310057X241234676

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null secondary analysis in the results section of 113 pairs across eight demographic variables including gender, experience and case difficulty; no statistically significant correlation was found. Equivalence was not tested, and the discussion notes the sample could only detect correlations greater than 0.25.

> "We found no significant correlation between the difference between trainee and supervisor ratings and any of the demographic variables collected (trainee gender, supervisor gender, trainee years of training, trainee training period, supervisor years of experience, times worked together, American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status score, case difficulty)."
