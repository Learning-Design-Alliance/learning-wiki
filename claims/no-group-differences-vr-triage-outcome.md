## Evidence

### Heldring Sara 2025

Heldring Sara, Jirwe Maria, Wihlborg Jonas, Lindström Veronica. (2025). Acceptability and applicability of using virtual reality for training mass casualty incidents- a mixed method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07319-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory non-parametric analyses (Kruskal–Wallis, Mann–Whitney U) of computer-recorded triage data from 95 participants. The article reports "no significant difference was found between different ages, genders, educational backgrounds, and previous experience of using VR that affected the outcome of triaging timely and correctly."

> "Different types of statistical tests were done depending on the type of variable, but no significant difference was found between different ages, genders, educational backgrounds, and previous experience of using VR that affected the outcome of triaging timely and correctly."
