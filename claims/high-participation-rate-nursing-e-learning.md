## Evidence

### van de Steeg L 2015

van de Steeg L, IJkema R, Wagner C, Langelaan M. (2015). The effect of an e-learning course on nursing staff's knowledge of delirium: a before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0289-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Response-rate analysis across 17 hospitals (Figure 1 flow chart). The article separately reports that 907 of 978 participants, 95.4% (95% CI 92.3 to 97.3), continued to complete the final test. The authors attribute high participation partly to email reminders and team-leader encouragement.

> "978 invitees started the baseline test, resulting in a participation rate of 86.4% (95% CI 81.1 to 90.5), adjusted for clustering on the ward and hospital level."
