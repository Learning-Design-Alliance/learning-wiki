# ASAP likely increases credit accumulation and persistence rates

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In two RCTs meeting WWC standards, ASAP produced a positive, statistically significant effect on credit accumulation and persistence, with a domain-average effect size of 0.25 and improvement index of +10. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
`q4 i2` In the Scrivener et al. (2015) RCT, ASAP students earned 42.9 college-level credits over three years versus 35.2 for comparison students (effect size 0.28, p < .0001). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
`q4 i2` In the Sommo et al. (2018) Ohio RCT, 60.2 percent of ASAP students enrolled in the fourth semester versus 50.5 percent of comparison students (effect size 0.24, p < .01). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
