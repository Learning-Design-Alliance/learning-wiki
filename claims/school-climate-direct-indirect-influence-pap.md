## Evidence

### Izaguirre 2023

Izaguirre, L. A., Rodríguez‐Fernández, A., & Fernández‐Zabala, A. (2023). Perceived academic performance explained by school climate, positive psychological variables and life satisfaction. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 93, 318–332. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12557

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling of cross-sectional self-report data from 1397 adolescents aged 12–16 in the Basque Country, estimated with robust maximum likelihood. Table 3 reports the school climate total effect on PAP of βt = .380 (βd = .235**, βi = .145**), described as "similar to the direct effect of life satisfaction". All β values are standardized path coefficients with p < .01.

> "The results presented in Table 3 reveal the moderate direct effect of life satisfaction on PAP (β d = .39, p < .01). The overall effect of school climate (β t = .38, p < .01) was similar to the direct effect of life satisfaction."
