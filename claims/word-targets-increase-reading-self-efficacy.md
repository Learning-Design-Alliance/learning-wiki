## Evidence

### McLean 2018

McLean, S., & Poulshock, J. (2018). Increasing reading self-efficacy and reading amount in EFL learners with word-targets. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1), 76–91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RSE was measured with Burrows' 14-item L2 Reading Self-Efficacy Survey, Rasch-scored, administered pre- and post-treatment in this quasi-experiment. Word-target gains (g 1.07) were significant; other groups' gains were not.

> "Table 7 demonstrates that only the word-target group participants demonstrated a statistically significant increase in RSE (p = .001), in contrast to the comparison (p = .250) and SSR groups (p = .637). The comparison (g .28) and SSR (g .09) group treatments yielded negligible effect sizes. In contrast, the word-target group treatment yielded a medium effect size (g 1.07)."
