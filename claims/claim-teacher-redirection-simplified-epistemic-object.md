# Teacher-introduced redirection that simplified a team's epistemic object was associated with a decline in that team's knowledge-creation activity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r?` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` When teachers directed the Sunny team from a water-turbine charger toward a solar panel charger, the team's application of product design practices declined and its epistemic object was simplified. [→ Sini Davies 2024](#sini-davies-2024)

## Evidence

### Sini Davies 2024

Sini Davies, Pirita Seitamaa-Hakkarainen, Kai Hakkarainen. (2024). Knowledge creation through maker practices and the role of teacher and peer support in collaborative invention projects. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09427-2

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

On-site observational finding about the Sunny team reported in the Findings section: the "teacher-introduced idea to design the solar panel and a case for it caused the team to simplify their epistemic object", and the new direction did not trigger sufficiently new or complex design challenges for deeper knowledge creation.

> "Based on our on-site observations, the teacher-introduced idea to design the solar panel and a case for it caused the team to simplify their epistemic object, discarding the complex knowledge and visions involved."
