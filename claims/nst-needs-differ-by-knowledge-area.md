## Evidence

### Ayllón Sara 2019

Ayllón Sara, Alsina Ángel, Colomer Jordi. (2019). Teachers’ involvement and students’ self-efficacy: Keys to achievement in higher education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216865

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Separate regressions with subject, year and lecturer fixed effects for six knowledge areas show INVOL_1 positive at p<0.05 or better in Humanities, Social Sciences and Architecture/Engineering, and non-significant in Sciences, Life Sciences and Medical Sciences.

> "Results relative to INVOL_1 are driven by students in Humanities, Social Sciences, and Architecture/Engineering."

### Ayllón Sara 2019 (2)

Ayllón Sara, Alsina Ángel, Colomer Jordi. (2019). Teachers’ involvement and students’ self-efficacy: Keys to achievement in higher education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216865

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the subgroup regressions, Humanities is the only area where AUTON_1 is positive at p<0.01, and the article notes that "only among students in Architecture/Engineering does one find a positive relationship between achievement and STRUC_2" (p<0.05).

> "students in Humanities show a strong appreciation for autonomy support, as they are the only group for whom AUTON_1 is positive and statistically significant at the 99% confidence level."
