## Evidence

### Acun Aysun 2026

Acun Aysun, Aksoy Bulgurcu Rahime. (2026). The effect of collaborative learning approach on nursing students' knowledge and skill learning for enteral nutrition: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education 26:328. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08687-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subscale analysis of the Self-Directed Learning Skills Scale in the experimental group (n = 45) of the randomized trial; the table also prints dependent t values (2.859 to 29.536, all p < 0.001). The article reports "significant increases from pre-test to post-test" with "all effect sizes classified as large".

> "Subscale analyses indicated significant increases from pre-test to post-test in self-monitoring (p < 0.001; d = 4.502), motivation (p < 0.001; d = 5.398), self-control (p < 0.001; d = 3.700), and self-confidence (p < 0.001; d = 6.034), with all effect sizes classified as large."
