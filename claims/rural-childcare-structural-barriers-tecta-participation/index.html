Thematic analysis of focus groups with rural providers identified travel burden: 'Rural participants often face lengthy commutes of one hour or more each way to attend in-person TECTA classes', with stated impacts including exhaustion and reduced participation.

"Rural participants often face lengthy commutes of one hour or more each way to attend in-person TECTA classes. These commutes are particularly challenging after a full day of work, which is when some in-person classes are offered."
