`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of the 16 included observational studies. The pooled estimate favored advanced learning curve surgeons on positive surgical margins with printed effect size OR 1.61 (95% CI 1.19–2.18; p = 0.002; I² = 88%); subgroups were RALP OR 1.34 (p < 0.05), LAP OR 1.83 (p = 0.16), Open OR 2.28 (p < 0.001).

> "Regarding the secondary outcomes, we observed a lower PSM rate in those surgeons in the advanced learning curve (OR 1.61; 95% CI 1.19–2.18; p = 0.002; I² = 88%)"
