## Evidence

### Bangura Marie 2025

Bangura Marie, Ganyaglo Gabriel, Kupualor David, Boateng Alex, Sepenu Perez, Koranteng Isaac, Lawrence Emma R., Rooney Deborah, Schmidt Payton. (2025). Evaluating Fundamentals of Vaginal Surgery for Use Among Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Accra, Ghana. International Urogynecology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00192-025-06343-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Task-level analysis of the same 34-resident FVS hands-on assessment in Ghana. Median task scores were compared between novice and experienced residents per task using the Mann–Whitney U test. The article reports "only statistically significant for task 1... and task 3, plication suturing".

> "Experienced participants consistently scored higher than novice participants on all five tasks; however, the difference was only statistically significant for task 1, one-handed knot tying (median score 0 vs 38 for novice and experienced participants respectively, P < 0.001) and task 3, plication suturing (median score 21 vs 74 for novice and experienced participants respectively, P = 0.004)"
