### Cheung H. C. Kristy 2026

Cheung H. C. Kristy, Zhong Lily Shengjia, Wall Shelley L., Lisk Kristina. (2026). Exploring the impact of virtual reality anatomy training on preparing biomedical illustrators for drawing anatomical structures. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70150

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis between MRT-A scores (mean 12.35 ± 3.69) and illustration scores in the crossover study, contradicting the hypothesis of a positive Vz–performance correlation. All three correlations were negligible and non-significant.

> "Results showed negligible correlations between visuospatial ability and participants' performance across all three reference modalities (MRT-2D: r(15) = -0.07, p = 0.78; MRT-VR: r(15) = 0.20, p = 0.45; MRT-Prosection: r(15) = 0.03, p = 0.91)."
