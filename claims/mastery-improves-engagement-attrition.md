## Evidence

### Guskey 1985

Guskey, Thomas R.; Gates, Sally L. (1985). A Synthesis of Research on Group-Based Mastery Learning Programs. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED262088

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Synthesis of time-related variables: five studies gathering time-on-task data through classroom observations of students at regular intervals, recording overt behaviors as on- or off-task; the printed effect size of .68 is the positive average effect size across the five studies, a printed effect size with its value.

> "Comparisons between mastery and nonmastery classes yielded a positive average effect size across the five studies of .68."

### Guskey 1985 (2)

Guskey, Thomas R.; Gates, Sally L. (1985). A Synthesis of Research on Group-Based Mastery Learning Programs. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED262088

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Evaluation by Guskey and Monsaas (1979) of a mastery learning program in an eight-campus community college system involving over 7000 students in seventy-seven class sections; the printed effect size of .85 is the average effect size for lower attrition in mastery-taught classes, a printed effect size with its value.

> "In seven of eight academiclisciplines, attrition rates were lower in classes taught by mastery learning, with an average effect site of_:-.85."
