Kevin Coldren, Nkodia Ngondala, Audrey Claar, Mike Verostek, and Diana Sachmpazidi. (2026). Student Perspectives on Traditional Pedagogy Used in Graduate Physics Coursework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of five graduate student interviews. Riley summarized that "the most useful classes [...] are ones where I can see some applicability to my own work," mostly referring to subfield-specific electives. Ashley valued particle physics electives she returned to while writing her thesis.

> "The interviewed students consistently placed high value on courses and learning outcomes that were directly relevant to the research they were involved with toward their eventual dissertations"
