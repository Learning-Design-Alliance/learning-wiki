### Ginsburg S 2014

Ginsburg S. (2014). Duty hours as viewed through a professionalism lens. BMC Medical Education 14(Suppl 1):S15. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S15

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a study in which 30 faculty attendings responded to a standardized vignette of a clerk worried about a patient before a Friday social invitation. The review reports that "much disagreement was found", with 13 endorsing escalation to another resident and 2 explicitly forbidding raising the matter with the attending.

> "In one study, 30 faculty attendings were asked what they thought a student should and should not do in this situation, and much disagreement was found [11]."
