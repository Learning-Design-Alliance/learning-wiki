Evidence¶

Setiyo Prajoko 2017¶

Setiyo Prajoko, Mohamad Amin, Fatchur Rohman, Muhana Gipayana. (2017). The Usage of Recycle Materials for Science Practicum: Is There Any Effect on Science Process Skills? International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.1. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: they offer three reasons — facility problems challenging students to think higher, increased motivation, and developed creativity — citing prior literature. No data test these mechanisms.

"First, there was no laboratory facility and infrastructures that have challenged the students to find solutions to overcome those problems. Given the problems make students think higher"

Setiyo Prajoko 2017 (2)¶

Setiyo Prajoko, Mohamad Amin, Fatchur Rohman, Muhana Gipayana. (2017). The Usage of Recycle Materials for Science Practicum: Is There Any Effect on Science Process Skills? International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.1. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' observational statement in the Discussion about worksheet use during learning; students were guided and assisted via worksheets in the experimental condition. No statistical test of the worksheet difference is reported.

"In the control class that did not use students’ work sheet achieved lower science process skills score."
