## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In one single-group pre-post study with no comparison group, all eight clinical domains (trauma, shock, dizziness, headache, dyspnea, chest pain, abdominal pain, pediatric fever) had significantly higher post-training than pre-training clinical reasoning scores (P < 0.001), with domain-level Cohen's d ranging from 0.62 (dyspnea) to 1.18 (headache).
