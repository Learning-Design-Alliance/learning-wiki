Vocabulary Improvement Program for English Language Learners and Their Classmates 2006¶

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

In the WWC analysis of the English language learner subsample of the Carlo et al. (2004) trial, the Word Mastery measure (36 curriculum-taught target words with four-definition selection) showed effect size 1.03, mean difference 0.40, statistically significant at α = 0.05, improvement index +35. Multiple word meanings (0.59) and word association (0.44) were "substantively positive".

"The Word Mastery impact was statistically significant; the multiple word meanings and word association impacts were substantively positive."
