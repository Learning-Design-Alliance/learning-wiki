## Evidence

### Critten 2006

Critten, S., Pine, K., & Steffler, D. (2006). Spelling Development in Young Children: A Case of Representational-Redescription? Manuscript submitted for publication (Date of Submission: Thursday 3rd August 2006). https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.1.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-sample t-test comparing the same 51 children's production (M = 6.53, SD = 4.03) and recognition (M = 9.96, SD = 2.23) scores on the same 15 words. The article interprets this as support for a multiple-representational system underlying spelling development.

> "A paired sample t-test on number of correct responses indicated children performed better on the spelling recognition task than the spelling production task, t (50) = 8.11, p < .05 (M = 6.53, SD = 4.03 for spelling production and M = 9.96, SD =2.23 for spelling recognition)"
