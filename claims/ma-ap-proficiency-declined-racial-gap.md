# AP scores of 3 or higher declined in Massachusetts from 2019 to 2022 for all groups, with a 39-percentage-point gap between White and Black students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The share of AP test scores of 3, 4, or 5 fell between SY 2018-19 and 2021-22 for Black (34% to 31%), Latino (47% to 42%), and all students (67% to 65%), leaving a 39-percentage-point White-Black difference. EdTrust analysis of DESE data on the percentage of AP test scores (in any subject) scoring 3, 4, or 5 by race/ethnicity between SY 2018-19 and 2021-22; the figure annotates a 39 percentage point difference and shows declines for Black, Latino, and total test takers.

> "All students, but especially Black and Latino students, were less likely to receive the instruction and supports needed to earn a 3, 4, or 5 on their AP exams in 2022 compared to 2019."
