## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After excluding items with factor loadings below 0.30, the shortened eight-question form showed better CFA fit (χ2/df = 1.33, CFI = 1.00, TLI = 0.99, RMSEA = 0.04, SRMR = 0.04) than the ten-item form. [→ Lertsakulbunlue 2025](#lertsakulbunlue-2025)

## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue 2025

Lertsakulbunlue, S., Thammasoon, K., & Kantiwong, A. (2025). Reliability and validity of simulation-based Electrocardiogram assessment rubrics for cardiac life support skills among medical students using generalizability theory. Medical Education Online, 30(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479962

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis (maximum likelihood, Stata 17) of rubric ratings from the ACLS course. After questions with a loading factor below 0.3 were excluded, the eight-item form showed "a better fit for the data in comparison with the full 10-question form".

> "a shortened eight-question assessment form was created with a goodness of fit index of normed chi-square value (χ2/df) of 1.33, CFI = 1.00, TLI = 0.99, RMSEA = 0.04, and SRMR = 0.04, indicating a better fit for the data in comparison with the full 10-question form"
