Analytical demonstration (Figure 3) comparing School A (50% Latinx, 50% White) and School B (68% Latinx, 21% White, 7% Asian, 3% Other, 1% Black): "These two contexts have nearly equivalent Simpson’s D index values despite their vastly different racial/ethnic compositions."

> "These two contexts have nearly equivalent Simpson’s D index values despite their vastly different racial/ethnic compositions because the index is insensitive to the presence of small groups owing to its probabilistic origin."
