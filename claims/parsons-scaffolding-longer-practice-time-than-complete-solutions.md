# Novice programmers given personalized Parsons puzzle scaffolding on help requests spend significantly longer practicing write-code problems than those given complete LLM-generated solutions

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xinying Hou 2025

Xinying Hou, Zihan Wu, Xu Wang, and Barbara J. Ericson. (2025). Personalized Parsons Puzzles as Scaffolding Enhance Practice Engagement Over Just Showing LLM-Powered Solutions. Proceedings of the 56th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 2 (SIGCSE TS 2025). https://doi.org/10.1145/3641555.3705227

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized between-subjects classroom experiment during an 80-minute lecture; 118 students (51 PC, 67 CC) who completed all four nested-dictionary practice problems. The Mann–Whitney U test showed PC students "spent significantly more time with the practice" (U = 2368.0, p < .001), about 7 more minutes on average.

> "students in the PC condition (M = 22.7, SD = 10.1, Median = 22.8) spent significantly more time with the practice than those in the CC condition (M = 15.8, SD = 12.7, Median = 11.7), U = 2368.0, p < .001, CLES = .69."
