Evidence¶

Tutal 2023¶

Tutal, Ö (2023). Does Context-Based Learning Increase Academic Achievement and Learning Retention?: A Review based on Meta-Analysis. Journal of Practical Studies in Education, 4(5), 1-16. https://doi.org/10.46809/jpse.v4i5.71

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Publication bias analyses for academic achievement: classic fail-safe N of 9,501 missing null studies needed, Orwin's fail-safe N of 4,467,254, and trim-and-fill imputing zero studies (observed and adjusted values both 0.970). Retention analyses likewise showed no asymmetry, with a fail-safe N of 269 and zero studies trimmed.

"Taken together, the results of these statistical procedures collectively indicate that publication bias did not significantly influence the overall findings of the meta -analysis in a reliable manner."
