## Evidence

### Gormally C. 2012

Gormally C., Brickman P., Lutz M. (2012). Developing a Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS): Measuring Undergraduates' Evaluation of Scientific Information and Arguments. CBE—Life Sciences Education 11(4):364–377. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-03-0026

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

ANCOVA of pre- and posttest TOSLS scores from the three public research university classes (project-based nonmajors, traditional nonmajors, biology majors), using pretest scores as covariate; private research university and state college data were excluded because homogeneity of variance was violated. "Post hoc pairwise comparisons with Bonferroni corrections indicated that the students in the project-based nonmajors class made significantly higher learning gains than the students in both the traditional nonmajors class and the biology majors class."

> "Results from the ANCOVA yielded a significant main effect for class (F = 11.380, p < 0.001)."
