## Evidence

### Springvloet Linda 2014

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative pretest of the basic version in which 28 participants worked through the intervention at home and filled out a questionnaire on appreciation, user-friendliness and comprehensibility; the printed result is that the intervention "was appreciated quite well (average rating = 7.98 (SD = 1.06))". Points for improvement were layout attractiveness and text length, so texts were shortened.

> "The basic version of the intervention was appreciated quite well (average rating = 7.98 (SD = 1.06)) and the information was rated as useful, interesting, easy to understand, personally relevant and user-friendly."

### Springvloet Linda 2014 (2)

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative and quantitative pretest of the plus version with six participants plus consumer-panel feedback on price and availability information; the article reports it "was scored with a 7.7 (SD = 0.98)" and notes participants saw the environmental information as too independent and somewhat commercial. The authors responded by embedding environmental information in attitude and self-efficacy sections.

> "The plus version was scored with a 7.7 (SD = 0.98)."
