Yim 2026¶

Yim, K.-H.; Lui, M. Y. (2026). Supporting Undergraduate Students' Learning in Practical Chemistry Courses through AI-Supported Experimental Design. Journal of Chemical Education. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.5c01311

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pre- and postsurvey Likert data from the same cohort. Median for troubleshooting rose from 3 to 4; agreement on evaluating results rose from "50% presurvey to 80% postsurvey" despite median remaining unchanged on that item.

"The median of their responses to the question regarding their confidence in troubleshooting and optimization rose from 3 (neutral) to 4 (agree) (Figure 2, Q2). Agreement (agree or strongly agree) on the ability to evaluate experimental result rose from 50% presurvey to 80% postsurvey"
