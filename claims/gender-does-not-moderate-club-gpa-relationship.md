# Gender does not moderate the club participation–GPA relationship in math or science

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The relationship between math club participation and math GPA, and between science club participation and science GPA, is approximately the same for males and females across OLS and matching models. [→ Gottfried 2013](#gottfried-2013)

## Evidence

### Gottfried 2013

Gottfried, M. A. & Williams, D. (2013). STEM Club Participation and STEM Schooling Outcomes. Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (79). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1361

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fully interacted models in Tables 5 and 6 show similar male and female coefficients (e.g., math: male 0.222**, female 0.239** full OLS; science: male 0.224**, female 0.285***). The article reports the gender pattern as consistent across both subjects.

> "Gender, once again, did not seem to moderate the association of science club participation on science GPA."
