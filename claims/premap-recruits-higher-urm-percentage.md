Observational comparison of self-identified ethnicity data from the UW student database for Pre-MAP participants versus all entering freshmen, 2005-2011. Table 1 shows, for example, 25% all ethnic minorities in Pre-MAP versus 10% in UW, and women 56% versus 53%. The authors state "our targeted recruiting techniques, as a whole, have been successful."
