## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Assessment of non-technical skills of trauma teams using the T-NOTECHS tool via video analysis of real resuscitations is reliable, with excellent ICC for the overall score when rated by three assessors (0.94) and by a single assessor (0.84). [→ van Maarseveen O. E. C. 2020](#van-maarseveen-o-e-c-2020)

## Evidence

### van Maarseveen O. E. C. 2020

van Maarseveen O. E. C., Ham W. H. W., Huijsmans R. L. N., Dolmans R. G. F., Leenen L. P. H. (2020). Reliability of the assessment of non-technical skills by using video-recorded trauma resuscitations. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-020-01401-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective video analysis of 18 consecutive real trauma resuscitations at a level one trauma center, each rated independently by three trained research assistants using a two-way mixed-model ICC. The study reports "an ICC of 0.94 and 0.84" for the mean of three assessors and a single assessor respectively.

> "We found an ICC of 0.94 and 0.84, respectively, when measured as the mean of three assessors or a single assessor using video analysis, while in their study an ICC of 0.48 was found for assessment of actual resuscitations by live observers."
