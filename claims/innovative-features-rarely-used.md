`q2 i?` The most innovative item types and features of Siette, such as generative questions, external items, and adaptive selection, are rarely used relative to basic features. [→ Conejo 2016](#conejo-2016)

## Evidence

### Conejo 2016

Conejo, R., Guzmán, E., & Trella, M. (2016). The SIETTE Automatic Assessment Environment. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 270–292. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0078-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of Siette usage data (Fig. 17) showing counts of questions with different features out of 30958 total questions. Basic features such as MCQ single answer dominate while features like external items, branching items, and calibrated items have much smaller counts.

> "Some of the most innovative items and particular features of Siette are rarely used (see Fig. 17). Several different hypotheses could explain this fact."
