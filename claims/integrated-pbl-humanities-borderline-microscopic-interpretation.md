In the cluster-randomized trial, the microscopic image interpretation test (total score 100) was analyzed using linear mixed-effects models accounting for class-level clustering. The "borderline between-group difference" had an adjusted mean difference of 5.72 (95% CI: -0.63 to 12.07) and P = 0.061, favoring the experimental group.
