## Evidence

### Diaconu-Gherasim 2024

Diaconu-Gherasim, L. R., Elliot, A. J., Zancu, A. S., Brumariu, L. E., Măirean, C., Opariuc-Dan, C., & Crumpei-Tanasă, I. (2024). A Meta-Analysis of the Relations Between Achievement Goals and Internalizing Problems. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09943-5

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup moderator analyses (Table 2) found education level, terminology, and setting moderated the mastery-approach–anxiety relation; e.g., college samples showed a stronger negative relation (QB = 6.89, p = 0.014).

> "Education level was significant; the negative relation was stronger in studies with college samples than in studies with middle school samples (QB = 6.89, p = 0.014)"

### Diaconu-Gherasim 2024 (2)

Diaconu-Gherasim, L. R., Elliot, A. J., Zancu, A. S., Brumariu, L. E., Măirean, C., Opariuc-Dan, C., & Crumpei-Tanasă, I. (2024). A Meta-Analysis of the Relations Between Achievement Goals and Internalizing Problems. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09943-5

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

For performance-approach goals and depression (Table 3), the goal model and cultural context moderated the relation; the pooled relation was positive only in Western-context studies and dichotomous-model studies.

> "Cultural context was significant; the relation was positive and significant only in studies conducted in Western countries"
