Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the final ad hoc satisfaction questionnaire of the pre-post evaluation, the overall course rating "mean obtained was 7" out of 10, with individual scores from 2 to the maximum of 10.

> "At the end of the questionnaire, participants were asked to rate the course overall; the mean obtained was 7, with scores ranging from 2 to the maximum of 10."

### Reviriego E 2014 (2)

Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Content ratings showed the four modules were generally considered adequate or very adequate, but "a quarter of participants considered that the diagnostic tests module was not adequate", despite that module being among the most popular and most difficult.

> "Notably, however, a quarter of participants considered that the diagnostic tests module was not adequate."
