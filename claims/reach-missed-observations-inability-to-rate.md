Descriptive analysis of first-year implementation: "Twenty four percent of non-tenured teachers did not receive a REACH rating in 2012-13." Over 400 schools had at least one affected teacher, and 47 schools had 100 percent of non-tenured teachers unable to be rated.

> "Twenty four percent of non-tenured teachers did not receive a REACH rating in 2012-13. Teachers missing even one of the required four observations were unable to receive ratings and instead were assigned an "Inability to Rate.""
