### Starch and & Elliott 1911

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1911). Reliability of the Grading of High-School Work in English. The School Review. http://www.journals.uchicago.edu/t-and-c

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors re-tabulate Jacoby's earlier small-scale study, in which six Columbia astronomy professors graded eleven papers on a 10-point scale. The article argues that even excluding Professor D's marks, ranges such as 4.3 on paper nine show "such wide variations certainly impeach the reliability of the marks".

> "Even when we omit Professor D's marks, the range of the marks given to paper nine is 4.3, to paper one 1.7, to paper seven i. 6, to papers ten and eleven I.5, and so on. Such wide variations certainly impeach the reliability of the marks."
