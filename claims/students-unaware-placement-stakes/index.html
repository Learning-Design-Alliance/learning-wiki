Evidence¶

Venezia 2010¶

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the five-college student focus groups, most students were unaware of the stakes of placement testing; one student reported being told at the test center that "It doesn't matter how you place. It's just to see where you," which the student later recognized as untrue. Students described the tests as discrete tasks, not part of an education plan.

"Before taking the assessment, most students did not understand that their performance on it would determine which classes they would be able to take."
