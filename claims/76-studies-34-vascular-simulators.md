`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines: 525 articles found after duplicate removal, 178 assessed by full-text screening, and 76 eligible articles identified for 34 simulators, categorized into open, endovascular, nontechnical skills, and courses. The review reports "76 eligible articles were found for 34 different simulators".

> "Overall 76 eligible articles were found for 34 different simulators (See Supplementary Table I). Simulators were allocated to open vascular surgery, endovascular surgery, nontechnical skills, and simulation courses."
