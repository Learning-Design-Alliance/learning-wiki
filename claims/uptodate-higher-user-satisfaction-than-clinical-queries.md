## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sayyah Ensan Ladan 2011

Sayyah Ensan Ladan, Faghankhani Masoomeh, Javanbakht Anna, Ahmadi Seyed-Foad, Baradaran Hamid Reza. (2011). To Compare PubMed Clinical Queries and UpToDate in Teaching Information Mastery to Clinical Residents: A Crossover Randomized Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023487

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon comparison of five-point Likert satisfaction ratings (Table 3) in the crossover trial. Medians: interaction 4 vs 2, accuracy 3 vs 2, overall 3 vs 2 (all P<0.001); amount of retrieved information 3 vs 3 (P = 0.114). No effect sizes are printed.

> "Users were satisfied with accuracy of retrieved answers from UpToDate significantly more than PubMed Clinical Queries .They also reported significantly easier interaction with UpToDate compared to the PubMed Clinical Queries."
