## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The review reports, citing Lin (2006) and Akbaş and Gençtürk (2011), that primary students commonly believe higher temperature or greater height means higher atmospheric pressure, and hold listed misconceptions about when gases exert pressure. [→ Lai 2016](#lai-2016)

## Evidence

### Lai 2016

Lai, C. (2016). Third graders' understanding of air concepts facilitated by the iPod inquiry teaching method. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(1), 1-9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105183

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Narrative literature review attributing these misconceptions to Lin (2006) for Taiwanese primary students and to Akbaş and Gençtürk (2011) for atmospheric-pressure misconceptions; the reviewed studies' designs are not described in this article. The article also reports Chang (2000) found the hardest air concept is that air expands when heated and contracts when cooled.

> "they do not know that temperature is related to atmospheric pressure and often believed that the higher the temperature, t he higher the pressure; they do not know that height is related to atmospheric pressure and often believed that the greater the height, the higher the pressure"
