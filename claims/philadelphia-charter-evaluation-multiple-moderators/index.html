Ron Zimmer 2008¶

Ron Zimmer, Suzanne Blanc, Brian Gill, Jolley Christman. (2008). Evaluating the Performance of Philadelphia's Charter Schools. Santa Monica, CA: RAND. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/evaluating-the-performance-of-philadelphias-charter-schools

The report describes the scope of its evaluation: it examines "effects of years of operation, grades served, mission, and demographics on student achievement" along with student composition, turnover, and impacts on traditional public schools. Results for these moderators are not printed in the available text.

"They also examine effects of years of operation, grades served, mission, and demographics on student achievement; types of students charter schools attract; turnover rates; and charter schools’ impact on student achievement in traditional public schools."
