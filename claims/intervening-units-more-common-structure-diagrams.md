# Intervening visual units were moderately common and appeared more often in structure diagrams than process diagrams

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Structure diagrams were more likely to contain intervening units (39%) than process diagrams (10%) and photos (30%). [→ Benjamin D. Jee 2022](#benjamin-d-jee-2022)
`q3 i?` The presence of intervening units did not vary significantly with spatial placement type, so a tradeoff between direct placement and intervening units was not supported. [→ Benjamin D. Jee 2022 (2)](#benjamin-d-jee-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Jee 2022

Benjamin D. Jee, Bryan J. Matlen, Monica Greenlaw, Nina Simms, Dedre Gentner. (2022). Spatial supports for comparison in educational science images. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09599-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of intervening units (units occupying the space between a matched pair) across the 72-image placement data set by figure type. The article reports structure diagrams at "12 of 31; 39%" versus process diagrams at "3 of 31; 10%", χ2(2, N = 72) = 7.12, p = 0.03.

> "There was a slight but significant relationship between the frequency of images with intervening units and the figure type, χ2(2, N = 72) = 7.12, p = 0.03. Structure diagrams were more likely to contain intervening units (12 of 31; 39%) than process diagrams (3 of 31; 10%) and photos (3 out of 10; 30%)."

### Benjamin D. Jee 2022 (2)

Benjamin D. Jee, Bryan J. Matlen, Monica Greenlaw, Nina Simms, Dedre Gentner. (2022). Spatial supports for comparison in educational science images. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09599-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the same data set: 14 of 47 direct-only images (30%), 4 of 18 impeded-only (22%), and 0 of 7 mixed images contained intervening units, but the association with placement type was not significant, χ2(2, N = 72) = 2.98, p = .23; equivalence was not tested.

> "Although this pattern is consistent with the possibility of a tradeoff, the presence of intervening units did not vary significantly with spatial placement type, χ2(2, N = 72) = 2.98, p = .23."
