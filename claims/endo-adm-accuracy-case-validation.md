`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manual validation of the automated system on an independent case test set (218 colonoscopies, 96 gastroscopies) isolated from training sets. Stomach-site identification averaged "an average accuracy of 91%"; timing was 99% start/100% end; withdrawal time 91.3%, cecal intubation 96.3%, pathology linking 95.7%.

> "Among the 96 gastroscopy examinations, Endo.Adm identified stomach sites with an average accuracy of 91% and a separate accuracy for each site ranging from 75% to 100% in the 96 gastroscopic cases"
