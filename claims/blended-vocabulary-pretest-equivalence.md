## Evidence

### Ebadi and Ghuchi 2018

Saman Ebadi, Khadijeh Davood Ghuchi. (2018). Investigating the Effects of Blended Learning Approach on Vocabulary Enhancement from EFL Learners' Perspectives. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 8 No. 2, April - June 2018. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1185411

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on pre-test scores of the same 40-learner randomized experiment: control M = 24.50 (SD = 3.98), experimental M = 24.15 (SD = 3.87). The article reports "no significant difference between the research sample groups" at pre-test; equivalence beyond the non-significant test was not tested.

> "The value of (F) would be 0.006, and the level of significance would be 0.938 (p = 0.780 > 0.05), indicating no significant difference between the research sample groups with regard to their vocabulary knowledge in pre-test."
