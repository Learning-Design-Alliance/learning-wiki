### Smith Christopher J. MD 2024

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post survey analysis of 30 matched responses from three workshop cohorts (2022–2024), analyzed by paired t test with p < .05 considered significant. Scores "improved significantly for all five attitudinal questions", with the instructional-design item at pre: 2.2 vs. post: 4.3, p < .01.
