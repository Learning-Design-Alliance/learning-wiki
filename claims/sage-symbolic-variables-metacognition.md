## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Requiring students to declare unknown physical quantities as symbolic variables promotes metacognitive skill development because students must be aware of the physical concepts the known and unknown quantities represent. [→ Dominik Borovský 2023](#dominik-borovsky-2023)

Dominik Borovský, Jozef Hanč, and Martina Hančová. (2023). Scientific Computing with Open SageMath not only for Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.07199](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.07199)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretive statement (type e) about the PhO thermodynamics example, where unknown quantities are declared as symbolic variables in Sage cells; the article offers this claim with no empirical test or effect size.

> "It promotes the development of metacognitive skills, as students need to be properly aware of relevant physical concepts which are represented by known and unknown quantities in the problem solving"
