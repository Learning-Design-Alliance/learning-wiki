Mediation analysis (Mediation Toolbox, bootstrapping with 10,000 samples for 95% CIs) with impulsivity as mediator between region GMV and GPA, using residualized values plus covariates. The study reports "the relationship between GMVs in the left cerebellum (right CN) and school performance was found to be reduced, although this effect was still significant", i.e., partial mediation (Fig. 4a, b).
