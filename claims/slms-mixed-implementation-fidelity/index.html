Evidence¶

Borman 2013¶

Borman, K., Murphy, S. P., LoSasso, J., & Schlanger, L. (2013). Preliminary Findings from an Efficacy Study of a Distributed Leadership Model of School Organization. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564091

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Implementation-fidelity analysis using MRSH Attendance Logs for SLMS professional development sessions plus field researchers' observations. Each Action Team was charged with at least 14 PD sessions, but "This was not the case for each Action Team at each treatment school site," and self-run team meetings occurred regularly at only one treatment school.

"Field researchers discovered that only Action Teams at one of the three treatment schools held regular meetings, made agendas, and kept minutes."
