## Evidence

### David Roundy 2014

David Roundy, Allison Dorko, Tevian Dray, Corinne A. Manogue, and Eric Weber. (2014). Experts' understanding of partial derivatives using the Partial Derivative Machine. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of expert interview transcripts shows physicists and engineers computed a single numerical ratio and acknowledged but did not explore functional dependence, while mathematicians saw the derivative as a function. The authors note "the physicists and engineers were satisﬁed with a derivative that omits the function layer of Zandieh."

> "Their comfort with a single nu- merical answer for the derivative suggests to us that the physicists and engineers were satisﬁed with a derivative that omits the function layer of Zandieh. 12 Both groups did acknowledge and discuss that there is a functional dependence of the derivative on the force."
