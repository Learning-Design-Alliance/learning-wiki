In the propensity-weighted latent state treatment-effects models, the family-obligations finding ran opposite to the pre-registered direction: intervention students reported slightly higher state family obligations at T2 versus Control Group 2, dissipating by 12 months. No effect size was printed.

> "Contrary to our expectations, relative to students in the intervention, those in Control Group 2 expressed slightly lower state Family Obligations at T2, but state Family Obligations did not differ by the 12-month follow-up (H4 not supported)."
