### Middleton et al. 2011

James A. Middleton, Mona Toncheff, Susan Haag. (2011). Growth in Secondary Teachers' Content Knowledge and Practice in Discrete Mathematics. Proceedings of the 33rd Annual Meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://www.pmena.org/pmena-proceedings/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trained observers completed RTOP evaluations of participant and comparison teachers teaching discrete mathematics topics once in Fall 2009 and once in Spring 2010, with no major differences in timing or method. The "nine -point gain" in reformed, inquiry-based practice was statistically significant.

> "The Post RTOP score ( M = 68.56, SD = 17.29) revealed a nine -point gain from the Pre RTOP score ( M = 59.78, SD = 17.59), and the gain was significant ( p = .028)."
