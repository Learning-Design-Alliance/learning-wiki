### McKinlay JB 2025

McKinlay JB, Kearns K. (2025). Improving the learning experience in an undergraduate course on microbial metabolism by using an illustrated story. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21869

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Impressions-survey self-ratings (0 totally lost to 4 highly confident) were compared between weeks 4 and 16 using unpaired two-tailed t-tests; the article reports "the improvement was significant in all years". Only one student across all years reported declining understanding. No effect size printed.

> "In all years, students thought that their understanding of metabolism improved between weeks 4–16 (Figure 7). When these impressions of performance were ranked using a scoring scheme, the improvement was significant in all years"
