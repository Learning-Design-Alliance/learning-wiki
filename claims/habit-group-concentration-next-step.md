Survey-based assessment in the article's review of experimental pedagogy works (Meumann, Lay, Huey, Thompson, Book, Cornman, Browne). The author reports these have given encouraging results but that "nearly all the work so far has been done in the habit group of studies," identifying Gilbert's zoology experiment as the sole exception.

> "It is true that nearly all the work so far has been done in the habit group of studies. Gilbert's An Experiment on Methods of Teaching Zoology seems to be the only piece of work outside of this group."
