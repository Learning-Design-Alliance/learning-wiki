# After the Creative AI workshop, more middle school students correctly answered how a GAN discriminator's feedback drives the generator (70.6% post vs 41.2% pre)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The proportion of students choosing the correct answer about the generator responding to discriminator feedback increased from 41.2% pre-workshop to 70.6% post-workshop. [→ Randi Williams 2023](#randi-williams-2023)

Pre/post assessment question in the Creative AI workshop asking what happens when a discriminator does not classify a GAN-generated image as a cloud.
