## Evidence

### Berg H 2023

Berg H, Prasolova-Førland E, Steinsbekk A. (2023). Developing a virtual reality (VR) application for practicing the ABCDE approach for systematic clinical observation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04625-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational development study with opportunistic recruitment of students, healthcare professionals, and persons without health or VR knowledge; data collected via observation notes and oral questions from February 2018 to May 2019. "Approximately 10 versions of the application were tested."

> "For the iterative testing and development of the application, a total of 120 persons participated in addition to the authors. Approximately 10 versions of the application were tested."

### Berg H 2023 (2)

Berg H, Prasolova-Førland E, Steinsbekk A. (2023). Developing a virtual reality (VR) application for practicing the ABCDE approach for systematic clinical observation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04625-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

During iterative testing, players were often unaware of their virtual hands. The final onboarding added a video with a laser-pointing hand and a tutorial progressing from touching a red ball to gripping, moving, and documenting a blood pressure measurement.

> "A major challenge was the onboarding process where some players did not use their hands once they had entered the application. They seem not to be aware of their hands as they could only see them if they lifted their hand."
