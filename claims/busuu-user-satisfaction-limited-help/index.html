Evidence¶

Kétyi 2015¶

Kétyi, A. (2015). Practical evaluation of a mobile language learning tool in higher education. Critical CALL – Proceedings of the 2015 EUROCALL Conference, Padova, Italy. http://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2015.000350

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-survey of the experimental group on user satisfaction (Figure 1). The article reports 73.3% thought busuu "can help, but is not the ultimate tool for language learning", that apps like busuu were largely unknown beforehand, and that the app was easy and worked smoothly.

"The overwhelming majority of the students (73.3%) think that busuu can help, but is not the ultimate tool for language learning."

Related Claims¶
