## Evidence

### Liu Xiao-Yu 2022

Liu Xiao-Yu, Lu Chunmei, Zhu Hui, Wang Xiaoran, Jia Shuwei, Zhang Ying, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng. (2022). Assessment of the effectiveness of BOPPPS-based hybrid teaching model in physiology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03269-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of the distribution of students across score sections in the three cohorts, reported alongside Fig. 2B. The "rate of excellence" of Class 2019 was significantly higher and its failure rate significantly lower; no effect size was printed.

> "the rate of excellence (scores between 90 and 100) of Class 2019 (197, 38.0%; P < 0.01 by chi-square test) was significantly higher than that of Class 2017 (159, 29.9%) and Class 2018 (142, 27.0%)"
