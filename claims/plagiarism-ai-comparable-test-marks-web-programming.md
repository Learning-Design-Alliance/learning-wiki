## Evidence

### Oscar Karnalim 2023

Oscar Karnalim, Hapnes Toba, Meliana Christianti Johan, Erico Darmawan Handoyo, Yehezkiel David Setiawan, Josephine Alvina Luwia. (2023). Plagiarism and AI Assistance Misuse in Web Programming: Unfair Benefits and Characteristics. [doi:10.1109/tale56641.2023.10398397](https://doi.org/10.1109/tale56641.2023.10398397)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Marks analysis of the controlled experiment, in which sixteen tutors and high-performing students completed web programming tasks across three sessions. Plagiarizing participants "had comparable test marks to those who completed the tests independently", and "This also applied to AI-assisted participants".

> "Our experiment shows that for both tests (FT and FT-ALT), plagiarizing participants had comparable test marks to those who completed the tests independently. This also applied to AI-assisted participants."
