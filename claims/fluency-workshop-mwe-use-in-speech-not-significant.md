## Evidence

### Thomson 2017

Haidee Thomson. (2017). Building Speaking Fluency with Multiword Expressions. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test on gain scores of target multiword expression use in 23 dyadic role-play recordings, scored by sequential-word matching with partial credit. The test showed "no significant difference between the experimental group and the control group".

> "Gain scores in pre- to posttest target multiword expression use were calculated and an independent t-test showed no significant difference between the experimental group and the control group, t(21) = -.877, p = .391; d = 0.41"
