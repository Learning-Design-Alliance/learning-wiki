`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final-segment performance in the 200-case prospective study. Model-based final Cohen's κ values were 0.64 (urology) and 0.61 (radiology), which the article states match the inter-expert κ = 0.63 and the pooled κ of 0.57 from a recent meta-analysis. No significant difference between specialties remained by training end.

> "Final agreement with the reference standard for binary PI-RADSv2.1 (≥ 3 vs < 3) was 80.9% (72.4, 91.8) for urology trainees and 77.4% (68.5, 86.7) for radiology trainees (OR 1.24 [0.55, 3.10])."
