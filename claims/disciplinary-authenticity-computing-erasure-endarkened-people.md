The article's own contrapuntal analysis: authenticity in computing education positions professional practice as politically neutral and limits vision to professionals' work, obscuring "the extraction of labor that necessarily undergirds and defines the reality of these practices."

> "When authentic practice, as it is represented in our field, is read alongside the work of Banerjee and her colleague (Banerjee, 2019; Banerjee & Rincon, 2019), it becomes clear the notion of disciplinary authenticity is predicated on the erasure of endarkened people in computing."
