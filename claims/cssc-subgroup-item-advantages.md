## Evidence

### Shen Yi 2026

Shen Yi, Wang Ye, Wu Shiyu, Yang Rumei, He Ming, Ma Qi. (2026). A cross-sectional comparison of nursing knowledge attitudes and practices in adverse event management based on simulation training experience. Scientific Reports 16, 8401. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39781-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Unadjusted subgroup item analysis in the cross-sectional survey (Table 6). Attitude subgroups: perceived necessity r = 0.89, benefits/harms r = 0.87, understanding/control r = 0.92; practice subgroups: prevention r = 0.88, handling r = 0.93, post-event summary r = 0.96, psychological r = 0.64, all P < 0.001.

> "Further subgroup analysis of individual items revealed that CSSC-trained nurses demonstrated significantly better understanding of ANE definitions and classification (K1-K5, P < 0.001) and ANE management principles (K6-K9, P < 0.001)."
