## Evidence

### Andy J. Frey 2023

Andy J. Frey, Jason W. Small, Hill M. Walker, Brandon Mitchell, John R. Seeley, Edward G. Feil, Jon Lee, and Steven R. Forness. (2023). First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis comparison of efficacy trials using research-staff coaches against the effectiveness trial using school-employed coaches. The review reports that with school staff "the ESs were all in the small range, except for the teacher-reported ES for prosocial behavior, which was medium."

> "When FSN was delivered by school personnel (as in the effectiveness study) rather than by research staff (i.e., efficacy studies), its impact was less robust, as expected."
