q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of a questionnaire completed by 34 of 67 active participants in an iLed e-course (response rate 51%, mostly HE teachers) found support needs were "mainly seen as training" in LD (56%), AI use (50%), data use (44%) and pedagogy (38%), with 41% needing additional resources.

"This support was mainly seen as training, either in LD (56% of the respondents), use of AI (50%), data collection and use to inform decision-making (44%) or pedagogy (38%)."
