## Evidence

### Chou E 2026

Chou E, Cooper D, Goel P, Tang J, Henkle G, Zemeida S, Wolfson H, Shyu YL, Chang E, Abram A, Gerriets VA, Cusick JK. (2026). The use of voiceovers and review games in teaching immunology. ImmunoHorizons. https://doi.org/10.1093/immhor/vlag042

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-study survey on voiceover and review game logistics, scored on a 7-point Likert scale and compiled across all years. The study reports a "strong preference for formative quizzes contained within the voiceovers (6.07; n = 55)" and preference for "short voiceover lectures that only focus on a single high-yield topic."

> "Students expressed a strong preference for formative quizzes contained within the voiceovers (6.07; n = 55) and preferred short voiceover lectures that only focus on a single high-yield topic (4.71; n = 63). Students preferred being provided a "script" of the lectures (5.13; n = 40) and few indicated having a script of the voiceover would dissuade them from watching the voiceover (1.72; n = 50)."
