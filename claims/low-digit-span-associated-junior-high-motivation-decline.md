# Low digit span forward test score is associated with decreased intrinsic academic motivation in junior high school students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multivariate logistic regression adjusted for grade and gender, lower digit span forward test scores were positively correlated with prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation (p for trend = .003). [→ Mizuno K 2011](#mizuno-k-2011)

## Evidence

### Mizuno K 2011

Mizuno K, Tanaka M, Fukuda S, Imai-Matsumura K, Watanabe Y. (2011). Relationship between cognitive function and prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation in adolescents. Behavioral and Brain Functions 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-7-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in 133 junior high students (50 not motivated), adjusted for grade and gender. Tertile 3 (≤5) versus Tertile 1 (≥7) showed "OR: 5.10, 95% CI: 1.88 to 13.86"; p for trend = .003.

> "Digit span forward test score ([Tertile 2 (6) vs. Tertile 1 (≥7); OR: 3.41, 95% CI: 1.39 to 8.33; Tertile 3 ( ≤5) vs. Tertile 1 ( ≥7); OR: 5.10, 95% CI: 1.88 to 13.86; p = .003])"
