Related Claims¶

  • Email-detection confidence judgments show good calibration but poor resolution, with aggregate overconfidence of 18% — related
  • Individual and task factors fail to predict resolution, while perceived consequences predict calibration in opposite directions for phishing and legitimate emails — related
  • Participants are consistently overconfident for phishing emails but relatively well calibrated for legitimate emails except at 100% confidence — related
  • Future anti-phishing interventions should measure both performance and metacognition, aiming to shift the phishing calibration curve toward the legitimate-email pattern — related
  • Phishing email difficulty varies widely (15–85% correct) while mean confidence varies little (80–90%), leaving some victims unaware they were fooled — related
  • Better resolution on the laboratory phishing task predicts lower odds of malicious files on participants' home computers — related
