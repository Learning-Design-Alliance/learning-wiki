The review developed questions corresponding to the six barriers to adoption from the State of XR Report and mapped extracted framework data against them. The authors expected more response from practical frameworks, explaining the gap because adoption barriers correspond to technological and practical aspects rather than theories.

> "By rigorously analyzing the data extracted from the frame- works, we found that no work directly addresses the above questions."
