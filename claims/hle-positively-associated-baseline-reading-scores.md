## Evidence

### Dowd 2016

Dowd, A. J., Friedlander, E., Jonason, C., Leer, J., Sorensen, L. Z., D'Sa, N., Guajardo, J., Pava, C., & Pisani, L. (2016). Life-wide Learning and Early Reading Development in Twelve African and Asian Sites. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567231

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-country multilevel regression analysis (Model 1) fit separately for each of 12 sites, relating baseline literacy scores to home reading materials and habits indices. The article reports "statistically significant positive effect sizes ranging from 0.01 to 0.30".

> "Analyses reveal 1) a modest but consistent relationship between students' home literacy environments and their reading scores with statistically significant positive effect sizes ranging from 0.01 to 0.30"
