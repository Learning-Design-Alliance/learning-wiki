# Elaborative interrogation increased perceived difficulty and time spent with the lesson

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i1`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants in the elaborative-interrogation condition spent more time with the lesson than participants in the read-twice or embedded-questioning conditions. [→ Clinton 2016](#clinton-2016)
`q3 i1` Participants in the elaborative-interrogation condition rated the lesson as more difficult than participants in the read-twice condition. [→ Clinton 2016 (2)](#clinton-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Clinton 2016

Clinton, V., Alibali, M. W., & Nathan, M. J. (2016). Learning About Posterior Probability: Do Diagrams and Elaborative Interrogation Help? The Journal of Experimental Education, 84(3), 579-599. https://doi.org/10.1080/00220973.2015.1048847

`q3 · i3`

ANOVA on minutes spent with the lesson (square-root transformed prior to analysis) in the randomized factorial experiment. The elaborative-interrogation condition exceeded both comparison conditions, with Cohen's d = 1.48 versus read-twice and d = .40 versus embedded questioning.

> "Participants in the elaborative-interrogation condition spent more time with the lesson than did participants in either the read-twice condition, p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.48, or the embedded-questioning condition, p = .001, Cohen's d = .40"

### Clinton 2016 (2)

Clinton, V., Alibali, M. W., & Nathan, M. J. (2016). Learning About Posterior Probability: Do Diagrams and Elaborative Interrogation Help? The Journal of Experimental Education, 84(3), 579-599. https://doi.org/10.1080/00220973.2015.1048847

`q3 · i1`

ANOVA on 1-7 Likert difficulty self-ratings after the lesson. The elaborative-interrogation condition rated the lesson as more difficult than the read-twice condition, Cohen's d = .59; the embedded-questioning condition also rated it more difficult (d = .63).

> "Participants in the elaborative-interrogation condition rated the lesson as more difficult than did participants in the read-twice condition, p = .001, Cohen's d = .59"
