## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants found the example UoL with GSI quite comprehensible (Q2.b, 3.98 out of 5) and all successfully completed the activities (RQ3). [→ Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín 2011](#luis-de-la-fuente-valentin-2011)
`q2 i?` The difficulty/benefit rating dropped significantly from Q2.c to Q3.a after the authoring activity (t-test p-value 0.03408). [→ Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín 2011](#luis-de-la-fuente-valentin-2011)

## Evidence

### Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín 2011

Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín, Abelardo Pardo, Carlos Delgado Kloos. (2011). Generic service integration in adaptive learning experiences using IMS learning design. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.12.007

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire data from the workshop case study, based on 18 valid collected answers analysed with means, standard deviations and a paired t-test. The article reports the example was "quite comprehensible (3.98 out of 5)" and that participants "realized the difficulty in the creation of adaptive material" after the final activity.

> "The t-test for the comparison of Q2.b and Q3.a samples (H0 : mQ 2:c ¼ mQ3:a ; H1 : mQ 2:c > mQ3:a ) reveals that there is statistical significance of the result ( p-value ¼ 0.03408)."
