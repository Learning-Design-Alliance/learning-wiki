# Experimenter-generated underlining is as effective as student-generated underlining for test performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Students who generate their own underlining do not perform significantly better than those given experimenter-generated underlining. [→ Nist 1985](#nist-1985)

## Evidence

### Nist 1985

Nist, S. L., & Hogrebe, M. C. (1985). The Effects of High and Low Relevant Text Underlining on Test Performance. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED265520

`q3 · i0`

In a controlled experiment, the authors found that "subjects who generated their own underlining did not perform significantly better" than those in the experimenter-generated conditions.

> "Subjects who generated their own underlining did not perform significantly better than those who were given experimenter-generated underlining."
