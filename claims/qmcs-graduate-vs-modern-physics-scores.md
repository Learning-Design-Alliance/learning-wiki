## Evidence

### L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan 2009

L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan. (2009). Graduate quantum mechanics reform. *American Journal of Physics, 77*(4), 308–319. Originally released as a preprint 2008. [doi:10.1119/1.3079689](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.3079689)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Comparison of QMCS post-test scores across student backgrounds: 86% ± 5% for M.S. students, 80% ± 5% for Ph.D. students, 88% ± 4% for University of Colorado graduate students, versus 55% ± 1% after a traditionally taught modern physics course.

> "The average score for all three groups of students enrolled in graduate quan- tum mechanics is statistically diﬀerent from the average score for the modern physics students (according to a two-tailedt-test,p< 0.001)."
