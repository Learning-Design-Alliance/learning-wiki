## Evidence

### Beyene Lise Saestad 2025

Beyene Lise Saestad, Vikman Miriam Dubland, Fugleberg Bruaset Geir Tarje, Hem Marit Helene, Andersen Elisabeth, Hammervold Unn Elisabeth. (2025). What is known about the development of relational competence in the mental health education of healthcare professionals – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08353-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing SmithBattle et al., that therapeutic letters support receivers and build trust, but that when treated as "an instrumental activity" students offered little reflection "and relational competence was not cultivated".

> "However, if students approached writing therapeutic letters as an instrumental activity, they offered little reflection on the process without engaging with the patient, and relational competence was not cultivated [62]."
