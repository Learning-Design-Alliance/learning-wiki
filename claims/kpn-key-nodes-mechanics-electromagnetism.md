### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Node-importance analysis of the IKPN using eigenvector centrality E, which showed the highest monotonicity (0.9966). The top 30 nodes are "pre- dominantly within the domains of mechanics and electromagnetism, with mechanics nodes comprising 60% of the total"; electromagnetism contributed 12 nodes, six electricity and six magnetism.
