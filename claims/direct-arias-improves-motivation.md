## Evidence

### Mustami 2018

Mustami, M. K., & Safitri, D. (2018). The Effects of Numbered Heads Together -Assurance Relevance Interest Assessment Satisfaction on Students’ Motivation. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 123-134. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1139a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of motivation questionnaire scores for the direct ARIAS group (n = 39) before and after one semester of biology instruction. Table 2 reports pretest average 67.49 and posttest average 80.41; Table 4 reports normalized gain, with the majority in the medium category. No inferential test or effect size is printed for this within-group change.
