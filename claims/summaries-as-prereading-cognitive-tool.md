## Evidence

### Ryugo Morita 2025

Ryugo Morita, Ko Watanabe, Jinjia Zhou, Andreas Dengel, and Shoya Ishimaru. (2025). GenAIReading: Augmenting Human Cognition with Interactive Digital Textbooks Using Large Language Models and Image Generation Models. [doi:10.1145/3745900.3746066](https://doi.org/10.1145/3745900.3746066)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of gaze scan-path and score data (PID=11 comparison, Fig. 10), not a direct statistical test: "summaries function as an effective pre-reading tool". Text summaries required extra reading time; image summaries freed time for the main text.

> "These results suggest that summaries function as an effective pre-reading tool for cognitive augmentation. In time-sensitive scenarios like this study, visual summaries outperformed text summaries due to their rapid processing advantage."
