### Negar Kamali 2026

Negar Kamali, Candice Rockell Gerstner, Jessica Hullman, Matthew Groh. (2026). Generative AI Literacy Training Improves Intelligence Analysts' Discrimination of Real and AI-Generated Images. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-registered OLS regression of trial-level accuracy with the BA×Post interaction as the critical test, standard errors clustered at the participant level, from the counterbalanced within-subject experiment. The article reports "overall accuracy increased by 9 percentage points (95% CI: [2.7, 15.4])".

> "The training intervention produced a meaningful improvement in detection performance: overall accuracy increased by 9 percentage points (95% CI: [2.7, 15.4]), corresponding to a predicted post-training accuracy of 82%."

### Negar Kamali 2026 (2)

Negar Kamali, Candice Rockell Gerstner, Jessica Hullman, Matthew Groh. (2026). Generative AI Literacy Training Improves Intelligence Analysts' Discrimination of Real and AI-Generated Images. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same pre-registered OLS analysis applied to pair accuracy, a stricter post hoc measure requiring both members of a matched real–fake pair to be correctly classified, reducing inflation from chance-level guessing. The article reports "pairwise accuracy increased by 11 percentage points".

> "Results were consistent across outcome measures: pairwise accuracy increased by 11 percentage points (95% CI: [1.8, 21.0])."
