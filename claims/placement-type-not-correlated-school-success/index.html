Expert-interview report section on out-of-home placement types and school success. Experts characterized the existing evidence as preliminary and flagged cautions: there are good and bad placements of all types, and children with learning disabilities are hardest to place, potentially skewing results.

"The preliminary evidence the experts cited indicates that placement type is not correlated with educational outcomes or, "more controversially, suggest better outcomes for kids outside family care.""
