Ugwuanyi 2025¶

Ugwuanyi, K., Mair, C., Dovchin, S., Erdocia, I., Kuteeva, M., & Airemionkhale, E. (2025). Generative AI and linguistic diversity in academic writing and publishing: Perspectives from World Englishes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28505

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

An editor's observational account in the dialogue: fewer manuscripts sent back for proofreading, more submissions, and much undeclared AI use. The editor cautions that "sheer quantity of manuscripts" in correct English does not improve research quality. Anecdotal, no systematic data.

"The sheer quantity of manuscripts written in what appears to be correct English does not lead to better quality of research."

Ugwuanyi 2025 (2)¶

Ugwuanyi, K., Mair, C., Dovchin, S., Erdocia, I., Kuteeva, M., & Airemionkhale, E. (2025). Generative AI and linguistic diversity in academic writing and publishing: Perspectives from World Englishes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28505

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

A second editor/reviewer's observational account in the dialogue, judging quality improved through AI-assisted proofreading and optimisation, while objecting to AI-generated padding of trivial generalities. Anecdotal and contradicted in part by another contributor.

"Yes, the quality has improved, as many authors seem to have their drafts proofread and optimised in other ways."
