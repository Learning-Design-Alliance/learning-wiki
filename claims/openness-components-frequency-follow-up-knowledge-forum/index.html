Evidence¶

U. Cress 2019¶

U. Cress, C. P. Rosé, N. Law, S. Ludvigsen. (2019). Investigating the complexity of computer-supported collaborative learning in action. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09305-2

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Coding study of Knowledge Forum contributions from two mixed cohorts of students and teachers, after validating the two-factor structure; frequency and follow-up likelihood of the eight components were analyzed. Three components were "present in the large majority of Knowledge Forum notes"; following analysis showed transactivity and authority "associated with decreased rather than increased likelihood of follow-up."

"Results indicated that three of the eight components were present in the large majority of Knowledge Forum notes."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Component frequencies: elaboration, other transactivity and expert authority are frequent; multiple justification, personal positioning and questioning are rare
  • At the discourse-act level, notes expressing uncertainty are associated with more follow-up and notes open on community orientation with less, and notes lacking building-knowledge openness receive less follow-up — related
  • Social openness components transactivity and authority are associated with less follow-up, and dimension-level openness shows no significant association with follow-up — related
  • Group-level feedback visualizations increased shared vocabulary connections, but student metadiscourse remained teacher-dependent — related
