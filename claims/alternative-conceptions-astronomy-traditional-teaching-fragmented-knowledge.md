Theoretical argument in a position paper: the authors review science-education literature (citing Camino 1995; Gangui et al. 2010, among others) reporting alternative conceptions about seasons, the zenithal passage of the Sun and lunar phases, and state that transmission from an external observer "conduce a construir conocimientos fragmentados".
