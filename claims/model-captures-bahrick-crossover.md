### Philip I. Pavlik Jr. 2005

Philip I. Pavlik Jr., John R. Anderson. (2005). Practice and Forgetting Effects on Vocabulary Memory: An Activation-Based Model of the Spacing Effect. Cognitive Science 29. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0000_14

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fit of the activation-based model to Bahrick (1979) and Bahrick and Phelps (1987) Spanish–English vocabulary data, with an encoding strength multiplier of 3.79 and estimated decay parameters; "The r2 was .926 with an adjusted RMSD of 0.060". The 8-year retention fit supported the long-term forgetting mechanism.

> "Table 2 shows that the model's results were similar to the participants' data. More important, the crossover interaction across the final 30-day retention period was captured by the model. The r2 was .926 with an adjusted RMSD of 0.060."
