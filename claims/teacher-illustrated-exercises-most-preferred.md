## Evidence

### Katy Klauenberg 2025

Katy Klauenberg, Peter Harris, Philipp Möhrke and Francesca Pennecchi. (2025). The three most common needs for training on measurement uncertainty. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.2478/msr-2025-0029](https://doi.org/10.2478/msr-2025-0029)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire respondents chose among 'Theoretical', 'Exercises illustrated by the teacher', 'Exercises carried out by the attendee' and 'Use of dedicated software'; assessors most preferred teacher-illustrated exercises, which the authors read as "the general demand for more exercises in uncertainty courses".

> "For all topics, ‘Exercises illustrated by the teacher’, is the (or amon g the) most preferred teaching method for technical assessors. One could interpret th is as the general demand for more exercises in uncertainty courses"

### Katy Klauenberg 2025 (2)

Katy Klauenberg, Peter Harris, Philipp Möhrke and Francesca Pennecchi. (2025). The three most common needs for training on measurement uncertainty. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.2478/msr-2025-0029](https://doi.org/10.2478/msr-2025-0029)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same questionnaire put to calibration laboratories shows the same preference for all topics, which the authors link to the need to transfer knowledge into practice and for tailored courses.

> "The preferred teaching method is, again, ‘Exercises illustrated by the teacher’ for all topics. One could interpret this preference as the general demand for more exercises in uncertainty courses, which reiterates the need to better tran sfer knowledge into practice and for tailored courses"
