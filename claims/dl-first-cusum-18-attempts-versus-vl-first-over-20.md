## Evidence

### Gu Minglu MM 2019

Gu Minglu MM, Lian Ming MM, Gong Chao MM, Chen Lianhua MD, PhD, LI Shitong MD, PhD, Lu. Min. (2019). The teaching order of using direct laryngoscopy first may improve the learning outcome of endotracheal incubation: A preliminary, randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(21): e15624. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000015624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-curve analysis using risk-adjusted CUSUM charts plotted for each trainee in the randomized trial (Table 3, Figs. 1 and 2). The quote reports the printed attempt counts: 18 attempts for DL-first trainees versus more than 20 for VL-first trainees.

> "The mean number of intubation attempts per trainee was 18 in the DL-first group, and all 10 (100%) trainees crossed the 20% acceptable failure rate line in the CUSUM analysis. More than 20 attempts were required to achieve an 80% ETI success rate for the 10 trainees in the VL-first group"
