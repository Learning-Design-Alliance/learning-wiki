### R. Schoonen 2003

R. Schoonen, A. van Gelderen, K. de Glopper, J. Hulstijn, A. Simis, P. Snellings, M. Stevenson. (2003). First language and second language writing: the role of linguistic knowledge, speed of processing, and metacognitive knowledge. Language Learning, 53(1), 165-202. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9922.00213

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The study's stated design modeled three components — linguistic knowledge, metacognitive knowledge, and fluency — in both L1 and L2 writing using structural equation modeling with 281 grade 8 students. The abstract states the "relative importance of the three components was studied and compared across L1 and L2 writing."

> "In this study the relative importance of linguistic knowledge, metacognitive knowledge, and fluency or accessibility of this linguistic knowledge in both first language (L1; Dutch) and second language (L2; English) writing was explored."
