## Evidence

### Lederer AM 2025

Lederer AM, Oswalt SB, Eddens KS. (2025). Individual and collective positive health behaviors and academic achievement among U.S. undergraduate students. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323610

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log-binomial regression (Table 3) on the Spring 2023 ACHA-NCHA III national dataset; the article states this was "the only tobacco use behavior with statistically significant differences in prevalence among GPA groups," with cigarettes, smokeless tobacco, cigars, and hookah showing no significant GPA differences. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "there was a 21% higher prevalence of no reported e-cigarette or vape use in the past three months among students reporting an A GPA (p < .0001) and a 13% higher prevalence among students reporting a B GPA (p = .0008)"
