## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-group quasi-experimental trial: independent samples t test of total SALG scores comparing the CoI-based blended learning group with the face-to-face control group. The article reports a statistically significant difference with "mean = 2.78, SD = 0.49" versus "mean = 2.45, SD = 0.46"; the Results text prints no effect size for this total score.

> "the mean total score of SALG was significantly higher in the CoI‐based blended learning group (mean = 2.78, SD = 0.49) compared with the control group (mean = 2.45, SD = 0.46)"
