RQ2 thematic analysis of focus groups reports this tension in participants' ethical reasoning about AI-assisted ideation, distinguishing "being supported in thinking and outsourcing thinking", a distinction the article ties to authorship and originality in research.

"GenAI could be valued as a dialogic aid that stimulates reflection, but it became ethically problematic when it displaced the researcher's own conceptual labour."
