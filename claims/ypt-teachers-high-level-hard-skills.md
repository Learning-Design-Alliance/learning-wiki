## Evidence

### Sergej Faletič 2025

Sergej Faletič, Assen Kyuldjiev, Thomas Lindner, Dorottya Schnider, Éva Izsa, Mihály Hömöstrei, Péter Jenei, Boyka Aneva, Daniela D. Ivanova, Hynek Němec, František Kundracik, and Martin Plesch. (2025). Development of soft and hard skills of high-school students via Young Physicists' Tournament. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.15324](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.15324)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Interview survey of 32 teachers with first-hand YPT acquaintance in Bulgaria, Czechia, Hungary, Slovakia and Slovenia, using paired differences between activity ratings. Teachers rated YPT above regular classes and other competitions, with differences "exceeding one third of the full scale almost in all cases".

> "Teachers assess that all high-level hard skills of students (Designing experiments , Interpreting experimental data and data analysis, Developing own theoretical model, Numerical simulations, Independent research in scientific literature , Critical assessment of others' results ) are developed by YPT considerably more than by their regular physics classes or by other physics competitions."
