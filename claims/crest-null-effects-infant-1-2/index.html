Evidence¶

Krystal M. Hinerman 2014¶

Krystal M. Hinerman, Darrell M. Hull, Darrell M. Hayes, Marvin G. Evans, Maria L. Reyes. (2014). Childhood Resiliency Effects from Schoolwide Treatment: A Cluster Randomized Trial. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562710

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Intent-to-treat HLM analyses for the Infant 1 and 2 survey version of the cluster-randomized trial, with treatment as a Level 2 predictor and composite pretest at Level 1, on total CREST, Attachment, and Engagement outcomes. No significant effects; equivalence was not tested.

"No statistically significant main treatment effects or cross-level interaction effects were noted and adding the treatment indicator to the Level 2 models explained less than 1% additional variance in slope and intercept over the model with only the pretest as a predictor."
