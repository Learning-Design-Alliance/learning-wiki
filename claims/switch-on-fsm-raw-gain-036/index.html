Evidence¶

Gorard et al. 2014¶

Stephen Gorard, Beng Huat See, Nadia Siddiqui. (2014). Switch-on Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Sensitivity analysis for the 98 FSM-eligible pupils using raw gain scores; the printed effect size was +0.36, and the report concludes that "the 69 threshold led to an underestimate of the gains for FSM-eligible children".

"the analysis was also re-run using the raw scores (age before standardisation and the lower limit of 69 was imposed). This produced an effect size of +0.36 , which is actually larger than the overall result using the standardised scores."
