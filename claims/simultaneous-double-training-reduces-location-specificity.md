## Subclaims
`q3 i?` When the II category task is interleaved with an irrelevant contrast discrimination task at the transfer location, post-test accuracy no longer differs significantly between trained and transfer locations. [→ Zhu 2026](#zhu-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2026

Zhu, J.-P., Xie, X.-Y., Yu, C. & Zhang, J.-Y. (2026). Double training promotes retinotopic transfer of category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00396-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 2: 20 participants performed the II category task at one location while completing eight blocks of an irrelevant contrast discrimination task at the mirror-symmetric location, interleaved in four-block segments. The difference was not significant (p = 0.16); mean TI = 0.95 ± 0.03 did not differ from 1.

> "post-test analyses indicated no significant difference in II task accuracy between trained and transfer locations (trained: 0.81 ± 0.02; transfer: 0.78 ± 0.02; difference = 0.029 ± 0.02, t = 1.42, p = 0.16, 95% CI [-0.01, 0.07], Cohen's d = 0.38"
