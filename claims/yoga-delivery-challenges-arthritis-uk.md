Biswas I, Adebusoye B, Lewis S, Chattopadhyay K (2025). Clinical knowledge, experiences, and perceptions of yoga providers in arthritis treatment: a UK-based qualitative study. Rheumatology International 45(4):88. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00296-025-05843-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis found "most yoga providers highlighted the lack of regulation of yoga delivery in the UK as a common concern", fearing untrained delivery could injure attendees with arthritis; providers also reported limited accessible scientific evidence and low awareness of yoga among attendees.
