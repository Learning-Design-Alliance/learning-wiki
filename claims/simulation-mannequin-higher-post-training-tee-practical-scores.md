## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Residents scored significantly higher on the post-training practical examination (maximum 110) after simulation-mannequin training (108.41 ± 2.09) than after e-learning (106.88 ± 4.53) or hands-on training (106.82 ± 2.01). [→ Weber U 2019](#weber-u-2019)

## Evidence

### Weber U 2019

Weber U, Zapletal B, Base E, Hambrusch M, Ristl R, Mora B, Schaller B. (2019). Resident performance in basic perioperative transesophageal echocardiography: Comparing 3 teaching methods in a randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(36):e17072. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000017072

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reported group means for the practical exam (0–110) and theoretical exam (0–50), 17 residents per group, with the practical score tested by Kruskal-Wallis due to skewed distribution. Means printed are descriptive group results; no standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Residents received significantly higher test scores in both practical and theoretical examinations after training with the simulation mannequin (108.41 ± 2.09, 40.6 ± 5.23, n = 17) compared with e-learning (106.88 ± 4.53, 36 ± 4.76, n = 17) or hands-on training (106.82 ± 2.01, 34.94 ± 4.72, n = 17)."
