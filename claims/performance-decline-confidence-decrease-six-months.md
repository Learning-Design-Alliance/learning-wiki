## Evidence

### Hautz Wolf E. 2026

Hautz Wolf E., Marcin Thimo, Schauber Stefan K., Walter Robin, Hautz Stefanie C., Birrenbach Tanja, Lehmann Beat, Sauter Thomas C., Hari Roman, Kämmer Juliane E. (2026). Self-Monitoring Accuracy over Time for a Complex Diagnostic Skill. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1767

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A Wilcoxon signed-rank test on 127 participants' averaged OSCE ratings found performance significantly higher at T1 (median 36.0) than T2 (median 34.3 out of 50). A parallel test found average confidence significantly decreased from a median of 3.0 at T1 to 2.8 at T2.

> "the performance score averaged across all six OSCE stations for each of the remaining 127 participants was significantly higher at T1 with a median (IQR) of 36.0 (31.7–39.2) points compared to T2 with 34.3 (31.0–37.8) out of 50 points"
