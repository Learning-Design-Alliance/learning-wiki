## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2021

de la Fuente Jesús, Malpica-Chavarria Erika Andrea, Garzón-Umerenkova Angélica, Pachón-Basallo Mónica. (2021). Effect of Personal and Contextual Factors of Regulation on Academic Achievement during Adolescence: The Role of Gender and Age. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178944

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis (n = 430) in the Linear Association section found self-regulation "negatively and significantly correlated with procrastination (r = −0.484, p < 0.001)". Table 4 reports a direct-only effect of SR on procrastination of −0.48 [−0.47, −0.50].

> "However, self-regulation (SR) was negatively and significantly correlated with procrastination (r = −0.484, p < 0.001), and positively with self-efficacy (r = 0.559, p < 0.001) and with academic achievement r = 0.494, p < 0.001)."
