Discussion of the final SEM (Model 5, Figure 4) in the 342-adolescent longitudinal sample; the article prints "83%" variance explained with a .17 residual for the Mathematics latent variable.

"The study provides a robust assessment of mathematical competencies from 6th to 9th grade, inclusive, and revealed that a combination of general ability, spatial abilities, and in-class attention explained 83% (1 - .17 residual for Mathematics, Figure 4) of the individual differences in these competencies."
