## Evidence

### ElHassan NO 2018

ElHassan NO, Bai S, Gibson N, Holland G, Robbins JM, Kaiser JR. (2018). The impact of prematurity and maternal socioeconomic status and education level on achievement-test scores up to 8th grade. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198083

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive proficiency rates by GA group and grade, reported in Results and plotted in Fig 1. The article reports PT, LPT, and term literacy proficiency rose from 45-50% to 70-74% while mathematics proficiency fell from 65-67% to 53-58%.

> "From 3rd–8th grade, the unadjusted ELGAN literacy proficiency rate increased from 46 to 62% and decreased for mathematics from 46 to 30% (Fig 1)."
