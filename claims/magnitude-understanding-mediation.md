# Improvement in fractions magnitude understanding, but not part-whole understanding, mediates the intervention's effects on fraction outcomes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the first three RCTs, fractions magnitude understanding partially mediated intervention effects on the NAEP total score, while part-whole understanding did not mediate effects on magnitude outcomes. [→ Fuchs 2015](#fuchs-2015)

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2015

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A. S., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., & Wang, A. (2015). Fraction Intervention for Students with Mathematics Difficulties: Lessons Learned from Five Randomized Control Trials. Vanderbilt University. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/Fraction-Intervention-for-Students-with-Mathematics-Difficulties

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analyses in the first three RCTs testing the NMAP hypothesis; no standardized effect size is printed for the mediation itself. In Year 1 the indirect effect via magnitude improvement was significant despite controlling for calculation improvement of "a substantial 2.50 SDs."

> "Results showed that improvement in fractions magnitude understanding completely mediated the effects of intervention on the part-whole understanding outcome, but improvement in part-whole understanding did not mediate the effects of intervention on the outcome of fractions magnitude understanding."
