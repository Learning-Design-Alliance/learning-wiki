## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On a five-point Likert scale, participants gave the highest ratings to teaching materials (mean = 4.6), instructional methods (4.5), content relevance (4.5), clarity (4.4) and applicability to EBP project development (4.4), while course duration received the lowest rating (3.7). ## Evidence

### Marqueze 2026

Marqueze, E. C., Portela, H. O., Mauricio, E. C. B., Mizoi, C. S., de Sousa, M. R. G., Oliveira, S. B., & Franco, M. E. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Course evaluation survey using a researcher-designed five-point Likert scale administered to intervention group participants (n = 18); teaching materials and other aspects "received the highest ratings", while "the course duration received the lowest average rating (mean = 3.7, SD = 1.08)".

> "Teaching materials (mean = 4.6, SD = 0.47), instructional methods (mean = 4.5, SD = 0.70), relevance and usefulness of the content for understanding and improving EBP competencies (mean = 4.5, SD = 0.70), clarity and comprehensibility of the classes (mean = 4.4, SD = 0.72), and applicability of the learning to EBP project development (mean = 4.4, SD = 0.80) received the highest ratings."
