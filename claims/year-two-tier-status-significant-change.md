## Evidence

### Elizabeth R. Kazakoff 2018

Elizabeth R. Kazakoff, Paul Macaruso, Pam Hook. (2018). Efficacy of a blended learning approach to elementary school reading instruction for students who are English Learners. Education Tech Research Dev 66:429–449. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9565-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of aimsweb tier status for the 130 Year Two students (71 ELs, 59 non-ELs). Tier 3 percentages fell from 15% ELs / 19% non-ELs at end of Year One to "11% ELs, 7% non-ELs" at end of Year Two; no significant group difference at end of Year Two.

> "There were, however, significant changes in tier status by the end of the Year Two study ( v2 = 26.57, df = 4, p \ 0.001). Nearly half of ELs and over half of non-ELs ended the Year Two study in Tier 1."
