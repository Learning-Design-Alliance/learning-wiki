`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Friedman test with Holm-corrected post-hoc comparisons on Likert ratings from the final pilot evaluation of 20 cases by six panellists; only p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "Ratings differed significantly across the four domains (Friedman test p < 0.01). Post-hoc pairwise comparisons with Holm correction for multiplicity showed that educational value was rated significantly higher than accuracy (p < 0.01), adaptability (p < 0.01) and realism (p < 0.01)."
