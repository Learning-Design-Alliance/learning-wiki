## Evidence

### Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben 2020

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben. (2020). Critical analysis of evidence about the impacts on surgical teams of ‘mental practice’ in systematic reviews: a systematic rapid evidence assessment (SREA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02131-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The SREA's results section states the headline mapping finding: across the six reviews, "reported impacts on a modified Kirkpatrick’s framework did not exceed level 3". Only three reviews referred to evidence for behaviour change (level 3), and in all three reviewers expressed concerns for data quality.

> "However, reported impacts on a modified Kirkpatrick’s framework did not exceed level 3."

### Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben 2020 (2)

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben. (2020). Critical analysis of evidence about the impacts on surgical teams of ‘mental practice’ in systematic reviews: a systematic rapid evidence assessment (SREA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02131-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the reported-impact section the authors state that "None of the studies reported results at Level 4", i.e. no review reported organisational or system-level changes attributable to mental practice. Table 5 shows empty cells at levels 4a–4c for all six reviews.

> "None of the studies reported results at Level 4 (Changes in practice at system level)."
