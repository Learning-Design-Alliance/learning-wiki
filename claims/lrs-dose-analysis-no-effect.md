## Evidence

### Wiggins et al. 2017

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity/dose analysis of the trial categorised schools as high, medium, or low implementation using monitoring data on resources submitted and teacher survey data. The report states "even for high dose schools there is insufficient evidence" of a positive effect, so the null result is not attributable only to low usage.

> "Schools that delivered medium doses of the LRS intervention did not perform better on the primary outcome measures than the control group, and even for high dose schools there is insufficient evidence to conclude that the intervention had a positive effect."
