### H. H. Hahn and E. L. Thorndike 1914

H. H. Hahn and E. L. Thorndike. (1914). Some Results of Practice in Addition Under School Conditions. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Volume V, Number 2. https://www.google.com/search?q=Hahn+Thorndike+1914+Some+Results+of+Practice+in+Addition+Under+School+Conditions

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of practice-curve records for all groups, shown in Figs. 1-10 and combined for grades 6 and 7 in Fig. 9 and grades 4 and 5 in Fig. 10, with scores expressed as percentages of initial score in Figs. 11 and 12. The curves show "The first half of the practice is the more productive."

> "There are notable changes in the rate of improvement, this being defined as the gross gain in score per unit of time. The first half of the practice is the more productive."
