# A brief structured VAP prevention workshop significantly improved ICU nurses' knowledge scores in a resource-limited setting (group-level improvement)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Mean knowledge scores increased from 3.63 (SD 1.57) to 5.29 (SD 1.57) out of 8 after a 3-hour structured workshop, a large effect (d = 1.06). [→ Yakubu H. Yakubu; Saani Muniru Mohammed 2026](#yakubu-h-yakubu-saani-muniru-mohammed-2026)

## Evidence

### Yakubu H. Yakubu; Saani Muniru Mohammed 2026

Yakubu H. Yakubu; Saani Muniru Mohammed. (2026). Effect of Structured Training on ICU Nurses' Knowledge‐Based Competence in Ventilator‐Associated Pneumonia Prevention in a Resource‐Limited Setting: An Explanatory Sequential Mixed‐Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70662

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group quasi-experimental pre-test–post-test study of ICU nurses (pre-test n = 57, post-test n = 56) using an adapted eight-item VAP knowledge questionnaire; the independent-samples t-test showed "a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.06)". Responses were not linked across assessment points, so the finding is group-level.

> "Independent‐samples t‐test analysis demonstrated a statistically significant group‐level improvement following the educational intervention (t(111) = −5.61, p < 0.001), with a mean difference of −1.65 (95% CI: −2.24 to −1.07) and a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.06)."
