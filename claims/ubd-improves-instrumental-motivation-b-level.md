## Evidence

### Yurtseven 2015

Yurtseven, N., & Altun, S. (2015). The Investigation of the Impact of Understanding by Design (UbD) on EFL Teaching: Students' Foreign Language Learning Motivation and Views. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i3.1204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on instrumental motivation post-test scores of 115 treatment versus 113 control B-level students, with pre-test scores as covariate; the printed table shows Contrast F = 5.40, p = .02. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "the results of the analysis of covariance indicate that there is a significant difference between post-test scores in favor of the treatment group when instrumental motivation pre-test scores of treatment and control groups are taken under control."
