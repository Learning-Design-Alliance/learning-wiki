# Students sharing a prompting theme showed common activity-system qualities: unawareness-theme students came mostly from low-achievement schools and scored low on writing quality, while overcoming-theme students used intermediate/advanced tools and scored normal or high

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The majority of unawareness-theme students came from low academic achievement schools and none from high-achievement schools; the majority scored low on overall writing quality and none scored high. [→ David James Woo 2024](#david-james-woo-2024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Matrix-display analysis of activity-system variables (community element, Figure 5) for theme-coded students, excluding 20 students with incomplete data. For writing quality (Figure 9), "the majority of unawareness theme students scored low and none scored high".

> "we observed the majority of unawareness theme students come from low academic achievement schools and none come from high academic achievement schools."

### David James Woo 2024 (2)

David James Woo, Kai Guo, Hengky Susanto. (2024). Exploring EFL Students’ Prompt Engineering in Human-AI Story Writing: An Activity Theory Perspective. [doi:10.1080/10494820.2024.2361381](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2024.2361381)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Tool-element analysis (Figure 6) of Python programming sophistication of student-built NLG tools. For writing quality, "overcoming theme students scored normal or high, none low" (Figure 9).

> "students with the overcoming theme used tools only at the intermediate and advanced levels."

### David James Woo 2024 (3)

David James Woo, Kai Guo, Hengky Susanto. (2024). Exploring EFL Students’ Prompt Engineering in Human-AI Story Writing: An Activity Theory Perspective. [doi:10.1080/10494820.2024.2361381](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2024.2361381)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Object-element analysis (Figures 6-9): developing-theme students used tools at all sophistication levels and AI words at all levels; the authors read this as a diversity of activity systems in constant flux.

> "developing theme students were found at all levels of overall writing quality."
