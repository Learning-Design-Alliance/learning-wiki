## Evidence

### Durmazpinar Parla Meva 2025

Durmazpinar Parla Meva, Ekmekci Ece. (2025). Comparing diagnostic skills in endodontic cases: dental students versus ChatGPT-4o. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05857-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 70 third-year and 80 fifth-year dental students and ChatGPT-4o (75 presentations) on 15 AAE-based endodontic MCQs via Google Forms; "ChatGPT-4o provided 91.4% correct answers" versus 60.8% and 79.5% for students. No effect size printed.

> "ChatGPT-4o provided 91.4% correct answers, 8.2% incorrect answers, and 0.4% unanswered. Third-year students demonstrated 60.8% correct, 32.9% incorrect, and 6.3% unanswered responses, while fifth-year students showed 79.5% correct, 18.5% incorrect, and 2% unanswered responses"
