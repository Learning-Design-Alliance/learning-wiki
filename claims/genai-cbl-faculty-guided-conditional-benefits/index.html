Evidence¶

Zhao J 2026¶

Zhao J, Yang K, Gao H and Jiang G (2026). Generative artificial intelligence and the transformation of medical education: a scoping review. Front. Educ. 11:1907589. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1907589

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing four comparative studies, benefits in this 21-report category; a tutor-managed ChatGPT design was associated with higher self-reported critical thinking and changed tutor–peer roles.

"Comparative studies reported benefits in some biochemistry, surgery, radiology, and ophthalmology contexts, particularly when GenAI was embedded within faculty-guided CBL or PBL rather than used as an unrestricted answer source (Gu et al., 2026; Hui et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2026)."

Zhao J 2026 (2)¶

Zhao J, Yang K, Gao H and Jiang G (2026). Generative artificial intelligence and the transformation of medical education: a scoping review. Front. Educ. 11:1907589. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1907589

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports this null/unfavorable pilot; it also reports that ChatGPT and expert feedback produced similar key-feature performance, with expert feedback superior for more complex cases.

"In a trauma-education pilot, LLM access did not improve accuracy or retention, prolonged responses, and disrupted teamwork; confidence increased more without AI (Gustafsson et al., 2026)."
