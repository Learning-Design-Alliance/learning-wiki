## Evidence

### Slaviša Radović 2025

Slaviša Radović, Niels Seidel. (2025). Uncovering variations in learning behaviors and cognitive engagement among students with diverse learning goals and outcomes. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10528-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA and post hoc tests of self-assessment task traces across the three outcome groups. Submissions, repetitions, and scores were not significant; only views differed, with "the Mastering group (p = 0.007)" and Passing (p = 0.039) exceeding Non-passing.

> "concerning engagement with the self-assessment tasks, no differences were found among the three groups regarding the number of self-assessment task submissions, repetitions, or scores. The only statistically significant difference emerged in the number of self-assessment tasks viewed, favoring both the Mastering group (p = 0.007) and the Passing group (p = 0.039) over the Non-passing group."
