Shiels 2025¶

Shiels, L., Carew, P., Tomlin, D. & Rance, G. (2025). The impact of classroom soundfield systems on reading fluency in normal-hearing students. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00350-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effect analysis of the longitudinal crossover study with participants as a random variable and fixed factors of condition, condition order and school. The omnibus classroom-condition effect was significant "F = 20.87, p < 0.001"; attention was a significant covariate (F = 16.26, p < 0.001) while working memory was not (F = 3.47, p = 0.067).

"Classroom conditions were significantly associated with reading fluency development (F = 20.87, p < 0.001), whereas school (F = 2.12, p = 0.072) and condition order (F = 0.00, p = 0.968) were not."
