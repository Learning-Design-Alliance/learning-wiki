## Evidence

### Kunal Handa 2023

Kunal Handa, Margaret Clapper, Jessica Boyle, Rose E Wang, Diyi Yang, David S Yeager, Dorottya Demszky. (2023). "Mistakes Help Us Grow": Facilitating and Evaluating Growth Mindset Supportive Language in Classrooms. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey comparison of the two model conditions in the teacher and student evaluation. "self-critique facilitates GMSL" with a 1-6% rating increase for one critique over zero critiques.

> "We also find that self-critique facilitates GMSL, increas- ing ratings for MODEL (1- CRITIQUE ) compared to MODEL (0- CRITIQUES ) by 1-6%."

### Kunal Handa 2023 (2)

Kunal Handa, Margaret Clapper, Jessica Boyle, Rose E Wang, Diyi Yang, David S Yeager, Dorottya Demszky. (2023). "Mistakes Help Us Grow": Facilitating and Evaluating Growth Mindset Supportive Language in Classrooms. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student survey challenge-seeking item (binary choice of easy vs. hard extra-credit assignment) comparing MODEL (1-CRITIQUE) to ORIGINAL; the model condition "almost doubles students' likelihood of choosing hard assignments".

> "Notably, MODEL (1- CRITIQUE ) almost doubles students' likelihood of choosing hard assignments (hypothet- ical challenge-seeking behavior), which is the most important GMSL student outcome, in contrast to ORIGINAL ."
