## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Higher post-simulation happiness and hopefulness predicts more positive perceptions of team mood (b = .18, η² = .06). [→ Grewal 2026](#grewal-2026)

## Evidence

### Grewal 2026

Grewal, K., Azher, S., Moreno, M., Pekrun, R., Wiseman, J., Flake, J., Lajoie, S., Sun, N.‐Z., Fried, G. M., Khalil, E., & Harley, J. M. (2026). Medical trainees' emotions and their effects on perceptions of performance and team mood in team‐based simulations. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 306–336. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70017

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multi-level model predicting perceived team mood from post-simulation emotions with covariates found "more positive perceptions of team mood" as happiness and hopefulness scores rose (b = .18, p = .001, η² = .06).

> "Higher scores for the happiness and hopefulness variable were associated with more positive perceptions of team mood, b = .18, p = .001, η 2 = .06, power > .99."

### Grewal 2026 (2)

Grewal, K., Azher, S., Moreno, M., Pekrun, R., Wiseman, J., Flake, J., Lajoie, S., Sun, N.‐Z., Fried, G. M., Khalil, E., & Harley, J. M. (2026). Medical trainees' emotions and their effects on perceptions of performance and team mood in team‐based simulations. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 306–336. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70017

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

The same team mood multi-level model found that when "ratings of post‐simulation frustration were higher, perceptions of team mood were lower" (b = −.19, p = .003, η² = .04).

> "Conversely, when ratings of post‐simulation frustration were higher, perceptions of team mood were lower, b = −.19, p = .003, η 2 = .04, power = .96."
