q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Theme 3 of the focus-group thematic analysis, in which educators critiqued ANVIL-generated artifacts; the Stack example illustrates how a visualization omitting a defining constraint can make some operations less meaningful without instructor framing.

"in theStack as a Pile of Pancakesanimation, showing the entire pile at once weakened the sense of restricted access that defines aStack. As a result, this property became less salient, and some operations (e.g.,peek) may appear less meaningful without additional instructor framing."
