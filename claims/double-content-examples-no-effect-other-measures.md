### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVAs on residualized change scores in the quasi-experimental pre-post study showed no significant group differences for alternatives of action, F(2,406) = 1.03, p = .358, η² = .005, and similarly null results for inferences and multiple perspectives (both η² = .002).
