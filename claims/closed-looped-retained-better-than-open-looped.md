## Evidence

### Arthur 1998

Arthur, W., Jr., Bennett, W., Jr., Stanush, P. L., & McNelly, T. L. (1998). Factors That Influence Skill Decay and Retention: A Quantitative Review and Analysis. Human Performance, 11(1), 57-101. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327043hup1101_3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis (Table 4) comparing closed-looped and open-looped task subsets collapsed across retention intervals. The finding ran counter to the hypothesis that open-looped tasks would decay less.

> "The ds for these moderators are different from the overall (-0.95) with a higher overall level of retention being reported for closed-looped (-0.71) instead of open-looped tasks (-1.04)-a finding that is in contrast to the study hypothesis."
