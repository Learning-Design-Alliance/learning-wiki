## Evidence

### P. Klein 2021

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey study with a MANOVA (Wilks λ = 0.87) followed by ANOVAs on duration-of-studies groups among 578 respondents. The article prints "F (5, 567) = 13.1, p <10−4" for perceived learning achievement and notes duration of studies affected no other variable.

> "Duration of studies had a signiﬁcant impact on perceived learning achievement [F (5, 567) = 13.1, p <10−4], see Fig. 2 (right), but on no other variable."

### P. Klein 2021 (2)

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected post hoc pairwise comparisons on the duration-of-studies groups. The article reports "students who study physics for less than 1 year obtain signiﬁcant lower scores than all other groups of students"; no effect size is printed.

> "Pairwise comparisons revealed that students who study physics for less than 1 year obtain signiﬁcant lower scores than all other groups of students ( p = 0.01 to p = 0.001)."

### P. Klein 2021 (3)

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA among the 220 students enrolled in physics laboratories on perceived acquisition of experimental skills (α = 0.75). The article reports "F (3, 204) = 4.41, p < 0.01" and that "Students in their ﬁrst academic year perceived the labs as less eﬀective concerning experimental skills compared to students in their second year".

> "First, the duration of the study had a signiﬁcant impact on students’ perceived acquisition of experimental skills, F (3, 204) = 4.41, p < 0.01. Students in their ﬁrst academic year perceived the labs as less eﬀective concerning experimental skills compared to students in their second year."
