## Evidence

### D.R. Robert Joan and S.P. Denisia 2012

D.R. Robert Joan and S.P. Denisia. (2012). Multimedia Approach and its Effect in Teaching Mathematics for the Prospective Teachers. li-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-post comparison for the 30-student multimedia group in the randomized pre-test post-test experiment; the mean rose from 55.07 to 76.80 and the "calculated 't' value (4.95)" exceeded the table value, so the null hypothesis was rejected.

> "Table 8 shows that the Mean and Standard Deviation for the pre-test of experimental group are 55.07 and 17.75 respectively. The Mean and Standard Deviation for the post- test of experimental group is 76.80 and 16.25 respectively. Here the calculated 't' value (4.95) was found to be greater than the table value (1.96) at 0.05 level of significance."
