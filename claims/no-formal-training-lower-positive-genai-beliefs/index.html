Xiu RL 2026¶

Xiu RL, Aguilar SJ, Macías AJ, Shikanova A, Al-Sulaiti R, Junjunia M and Jebril ST (2026) K-12 in-service teachers’ beliefs about generative AI in classrooms: insights from the United States, India, Qatar, Colombia, and the Philippines. q3 · i? · associational · r2

Model 1 regression, training-pathway contrasts with university preparation as reference (N = 1,204). "teachers without formal training reported substantially lower PAIB" and other non-standard pathways also showed lower beliefs. Authors link this to formal pedagogical preparation shaping technology evaluation.

"teachers without formal training reported substantially lower PAIB [B = −.39, 95% CI (−.60, −.17), p < .001], as did those in other non-standard pathways [B = −.25, 95% CI (−.44, −.06), p < .05], relative to university-trained teachers."
