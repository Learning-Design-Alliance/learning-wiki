The report's data page, citing NCES, A Profile of the American High School Senior in 1992, states that "Students From Low-income Families Were Less Likely To Be Enrolled In The College-preparatory Track," with 28% of low-income, 49% of middle-income, and 65% of high-income students enrolled.
