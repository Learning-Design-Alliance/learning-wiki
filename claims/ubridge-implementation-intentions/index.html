Post-institute survey of the ten participants (Table 13) reporting implementation intentions. The article prints these endorsement percentages descriptively; no effect size or inferential test is reported.

"All participants expressed that they will continue to use AI during instruction after the institute and intend to recommend the institute to colleagues. Impressively, 70% of participants had already begun to include AI/prompted tools or assignments during the instructional design of their courses by the end of the six-week period."
