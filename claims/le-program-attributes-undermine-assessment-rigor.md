## Evidence

### Hastings 2025

Hastings, L. J., and D. Rosch. 2025. "Framing Metrics, Outcomes, and Assessment Overview in Leadership Programs." New Directions for Student Leadership 2025, no. 187: 85–91. https://doi.org/10.1002/yd.70007

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' interpretive analysis of educational-sector LE programs argues that embedded diverse participation and programs' dual growth-and-spotlight role produce weak assessment: "most LE programs possess weakly defined outcomes, self‐reported and ambiguous metrics, and a structure of assessment designed to almost undoubtedly report positive information about their impact."
