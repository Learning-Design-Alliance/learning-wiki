Hecht, C. A., Murphy, M. C., Dweck, C. S., Bryan, C. J., Trzesniewski, K. H., Medrano, F. N., Giani, M., Mhatre, P., & Yeager, D. S. (2023). Shifting the mindset culture to address global educational disparities. npj Science of Learning 8:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00181-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A small-scale pilot randomized 57 Algebra I teachers (3,234 students) during the COVID-19 pandemic to the fellowship or a cognitive-science professional development control. Fewer fellowship teachers than controls (12.5% vs 23.1%) reported "seriously considering leaving their job."

> "fewer teachers in the fellowship condition reported seriously considering leaving their job as an Algebra I teacher (12.5%) than those in the control condition (23.1%)."
