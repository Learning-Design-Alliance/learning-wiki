# A 2-h situation awareness education programme improved TEAM task management scores in interprofessional ICU teams from case 1 to case 2 compared with control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intervention group's TEAM task management subscale increased from case 1 to case 2 (p = 0.018) with a lower corresponding increase in the control group. [→ Jonsson K 2021](#jonsson-k-2021)

## Evidence

### Jonsson K 2021

Jonsson K, Brulin C, Härgestam M, Lindkvist M, Hultin M. (2021). Do team and task performance improve after training situation awareness? A randomized controlled study of interprofessional intensive care teams. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00878-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of video-rated TEAM task management in the randomized in situ simulation study of 20 ICU teams; intervention mean rose from 2.50 to 3.12 while the control increase "was lower" (2.60 to 2.85); mean change difference p = .018.

> "The mean value for the subscale task management increased between case 1 and case 2 for the intervention group (2.50, SE .11 vs. 3.12, SE .07) and the corresponding increase for the control group was lower (2.60, SE .08 vs. 2.85, SE.14)"
