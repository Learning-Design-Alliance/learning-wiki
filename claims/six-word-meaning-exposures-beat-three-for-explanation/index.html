Evidence¶

Korat et al. 2017¶

Korat, O., Kozlov-Peretz, O., & Segal-Drori, O. (2017). Repeated E-book Reading and Its Contribution to Learning New Words among Kindergartners. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 7. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i7.2498

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-experimental-group comparison across posttest 1 (after three meaning exposures) and posttest 2 (after six) in the randomized study. The article calls this its unique finding: three repetitions suffice for progress in explaining words, and three more bring further progress.

"The findings show that there was significant progress in the ability to explain new words from the story after three repetit ions of the dictionary words, and after three additional repetitions (six in total) the children achieved further progre ss."
