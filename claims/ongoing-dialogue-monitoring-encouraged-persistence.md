## Evidence

### Latif Zahira P. 2018

Latif Zahira P., Nakafero Georgina, Jenkins Wendy, Doherty Michael, Abhishek Abhishek. (2018). Implication of nurse intervention on engagement with urate-lowering drugs: A qualitative study of participants in a RCT of nurse led care. Joint, Bone, Spine. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbspin.2018.10.008

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme 3: participants attributed persistence to periodic nurse contact, individualised dose adjustment, and "ongoing dialogue with a healthcare professional"; information on current and target SU levels provided psychological motivation. No effect size reported.

> "Participants valued the long-term monitoring and review of their condition and treatment – frequently suggesting that ongoing dialogue with a healthcare professional, especially while establishing a new regime, encouraged them to persevere with ULT."
