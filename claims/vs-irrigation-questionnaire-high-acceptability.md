## Evidence

### Yue Xin 2025

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a 26-item Likert-scale questionnaire completed by all 34 participants after training; dimension means ranged from 4.28 to 4.45. The clinical relevance item Q21 drew relatively more neutral responses (14.7%).

> "Mean ratings exceeded 4.2 on a five-point Likert scale, and more than 90% of responses fell within the 4–5 range, reflecting strong agreement regarding the system's ease of use, perceived learning effectiveness, instructional value, and overall satisfaction (Table 4)."

### Yue Xin 2025 (2)

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of the four questionnaire domains (perceived learning effectiveness, usability, instructional value, overall satisfaction) reported in the Results section; α values ranged from 0.87 to 0.91 overall.

> "All questionnaire dimensions demonstrated excellent internal consistency, with Cronbach's α coefficients exceeding 0.80 across all domains."
