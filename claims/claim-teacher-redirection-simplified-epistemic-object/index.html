q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

On-site observational finding about the Sunny team reported in the Findings section: the "teacher-introduced idea to design the solar panel and a case for it caused the team to simplify their epistemic object", and the new direction did not trigger sufficiently new or complex design challenges for deeper knowledge creation.

"Based on our on-site observations, the teacher-introduced idea to design the solar panel and a case for it caused the team to simplify their epistemic object, discarding the complex knowledge and visions involved."
