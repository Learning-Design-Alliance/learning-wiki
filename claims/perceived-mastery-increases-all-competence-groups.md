## Evidence

### Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun 2024

Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun, Oeser Reinhard, Seemann-Hlawati Barbara, Varga Katja, Wagner-Menghin Michaela. (2024). Self-directed learning in post-graduate medical education: Self-judgement and supervisor judgement of competence development in Austrian nine-month basic training. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4): Doc42. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001697.shtml

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal self-judgement data at months 3, 6 and 9 on 81 ÖÄK learning objectives grouped by target competence level; the total-score correlation with time gave "r=0.39 (df=439, p<0.001, n=441, r2=0.15, weak effect)", which the article itself labels a weak effect.

> "The average achievement of learning objectives per competence group (K, E and P) increased throughout the training. After three months, the average mastery for all three competence areas was 86%, rising to 96% after nine months."

### Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun 2024 (2)

Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun, Oeser Reinhard, Seemann-Hlawati Barbara, Varga Katja, Wagner-Menghin Michaela. (2024). Self-directed learning in post-graduate medical education: Self-judgement and supervisor judgement of competence development in Austrian nine-month basic training. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4): Doc42. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001697.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive box plots per competence group (figures 4–6) showed rising medians and decreasing dispersion; the article reports the same effect for the E (87%-98%) and P (79%-93%) groups.

> "After three months, JPs were perceived to have mastered 94% of the learning objectives in the K group. This value increased to 99% after nine months."
