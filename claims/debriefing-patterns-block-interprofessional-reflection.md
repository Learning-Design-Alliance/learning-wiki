## Evidence

### Nyström S 2016

Nyström S, Dahlberg J, Edelbring S, Hult H, Abrandt Dahlgren M. (2016). Debriefing practices in interprofessional simulation with students: a sociomaterial perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0666-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Collaborative qualitative video analysis of 18 debriefing sessions from two Swedish simulation centres, involving 106 students and seven instructors. The authors report that "interprofessional learning was rarely brought up for reflection" under both debriefing patterns; no effect sizes are reported in this qualitative study.

> "However, both patterns of debriefing practice showed that interprofessional learning was rarely brought up for reflection in the discussion, even though the overall aim of the simulation activity was to provide opportunities for students to engage in teamwork and interprofessional collaboration."
