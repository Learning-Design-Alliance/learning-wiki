### Kim 2020

Kim, K.-J. (2020). Project-based learning approach to increase medical student empathy. Medical Education Online, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2020.1742965

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests comparing pre/post K-IRI scores for groups split at the median pre-test score (lower group n = 23, higher group n = 22); the lower group "improved significantly in their empathy scores over the semester (t = 3.44, p < .01)". No effect size printed.
