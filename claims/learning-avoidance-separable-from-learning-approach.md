## Evidence

### Daumiller 2019

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CFA comparisons in the 1,066-instructor sample showed a single-factor learning goal model fit poorly, while "an adequate fit was only realized when disti nguishing between learn- ing approach and learning avoidance goals", which were highly correlated (ρ = .85).

> "For learning goals (Hypothesis 1b), an adequate fit was only realized when disti nguishing between learn- ing approach and learning avoidance goals. The fit of a single factor model was poor."
