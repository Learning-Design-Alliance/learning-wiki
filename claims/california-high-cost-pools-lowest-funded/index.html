Jason Willis 2020¶

Jason Willis, Sara Menlove Doutre, Kelsey Krausen, Tyson Barrett, Tye Ripma, and Ruthie Caparas. (2020). Study of California's Special Education Funding System: Phase I Report — A Descriptive Analysis of Special Education Funding in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616102

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-state comparison in the executive summary reports California's high-cost pools are "among the lowest-funded pools in the nation." SELPA directors reported the benefit of the pools "is outweighed by the paperwork requirements for requesting reimbursement."

"California funds its total high-cost pool (available through its two statewide pools) at $9 per student with a disability. New York and Pennsylvania invest $2,278 and $37 per student with a disability, respectively."
