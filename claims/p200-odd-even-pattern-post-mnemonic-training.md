## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2023

Yafeng Pan, Ning Hao, Ning Liu, Yijie Zhao, Xiaojun Cheng, Yixuan Ku and Yi Hu. (2023). Mnemonic-trained brain tuning to a regular odd-even pattern subserves digit memory in children. npj Science of Learning 8:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00177-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Planned repeated-measures ANOVAs (Position, Session, Area) on P200 amplitude in the MT group (n = 16) showed a significant Position × Session interaction; post-hoc tests confirmed "greater P200 amplitude in response to even-position digits (3.08 ± 0.96 μV) compared to odd-position digits (1.34 ± 0.80 μV)". The NC group showed no such effect (all ps > 0.41).

> "we found the interaction effect of Position × Session, F (1, 15) = 3.79, p < 0.05, η2 partial = 0.17: the post-training session showed greater P200 amplitude in response to even-position digits"
