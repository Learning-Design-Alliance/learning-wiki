## Evidence

### McKinlay JB 2025

McKinlay JB, Kearns K. (2025). Improving the learning experience in an undergraduate course on microbial metabolism by using an illustrated story. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21869

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pooled resource-reflection responses across four years ranked by week-16 mentions; "lectures (both online and in person) with storytelling tied for #1 among items". The authors call the ranking likely conservative because undescribed 'lectures' mentions were assumed to exclude storytelling.

> "When ranked by the number of week 16 responses, lectures (both online and in person) with storytelling tied for #1 among items (Figure 8). This ranking is likely conservative as we assumed that “lectures,” without additional descriptors, meant that students did not find the story to be helpful enough to mention."

### McKinlay JB 2025 (2)

McKinlay JB, Kearns K. (2025). Improving the learning experience in an undergraduate course on microbial metabolism by using an illustrated story. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21869

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

University-mandated course evaluations were scanned for story mentions across the four study semesters; positive mentions "ranged from 13% of respondents in 2019 to 35% in 2022" and "There were no negative mentions."

> "Positive mentions of the use of the story ranged from 13% of respondents in 2019 to 35% in 2022 (Figure 6b). There were no negative mentions."
