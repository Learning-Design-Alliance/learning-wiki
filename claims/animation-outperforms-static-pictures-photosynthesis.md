## Evidence

### Marta M. Koć-Januchta 2020

Marta M. Koć-Januchta, Tim N. Höffler, Helmut Prechtl, Detlev Leutner. (2020). Is too much help an obstacle? Effects of interactivity and cognitive style on learning with dynamic versus non-dynamic visualizations with narrative explanations. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09822-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized 2×2 experiment (N = 235), the GLM analysis with visual cognitive style as covariate showed the visualization main effect with printed effect size ηp² = .069 (a medium effect by the η² code); the article reports "Manimation = 32.56" versus "Mstatic picture = 27.94".

> "we found a significant main effect of type of visualization in favour of animation (learning with animation outperforms learning with static pictures), F(1,227) = 16.81, p < .001, η p 2 = .069; Manimation  = 32.56; SDanimation  = 8.33; Mstatic picture  = 27.94; SDstatic picture  = 9.41."
