### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Pre-test content analysis of six undergraduate physics students' pencil-and-paper answers mapped criterial attributes for the four target concepts. The authors report the "level of generality of the concepts was essentially very high", with low variation among students, indicating few differentiated subsumers available for anchoring new knowledge.

> "The level of generality of the concepts was essentially very high and this indicates general, abstract and, in this case, even poor concepts of Physical Systems, Dynamical Variables, State, and Time Evolution."
