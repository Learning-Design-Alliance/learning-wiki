## Evidence

### Hibbert Emily J 2013

Hibbert Emily J, Lambert Tim, Carter John N, Learoyd Diana L, Twigg Stephen, Clarke Stephen. (2013). A randomized controlled pilot trial comparing the impact of access to clinical endocrinology video demonstrations with access to usual revision resources on medical student performance of clinical endocrinology skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analyses of individual OSCE criteria in the randomized pilot trial (Table 1) show, for example, dorsalis pedis palpation correct in 91.7% of video versus 30.0% of control students (p = 0.009) and light touch technique 100.0% versus 30.0% (p = 0.002).

> "In the LLE assessment, students who had viewed the video performed significantly better on 4 out of the 18 individual criteria (Table 1). These criteria were: palpating the dorsalis pedis pulse at the correct site bilaterally, checking for light touch sensation using correct technique, performing knee reflexes correctly and indicating a plan to check lower limb motor function."

### Hibbert Emily J 2013 (2)

Hibbert Emily J, Lambert Tim, Carter John N, Learoyd Diana L, Twigg Stephen, Clarke Stephen. (2013). A randomized controlled pilot trial comparing the impact of access to clinical endocrinology video demonstrations with access to usual revision resources on medical student performance of clinical endocrinology skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 2 reports criterion-level differences in the same trial: exercise 75.0% versus 20.0% (p = 0.036) and cerebrovascular disease 75.0% versus 20.0% (p = 0.03); the retinopathy criterion reached 100.0% versus 60.0% with p = 0.053, marginal at the printed threshold.

> "Students randomized to view the DMH video performed significantly better in 3 of the 22 criteria (Table 2) than those who had not been randomized to view the video. These criteria were: asking about exercise, history of ischaemic heart disease and history of cerebrovascular disease."
