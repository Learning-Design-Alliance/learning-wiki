Evidence¶

Zou J 2026¶

Zou J, Li N, Wang X and Du J (2026) From AI literacy to AI-integrated inquiry-based science teaching: the serial mediating roles of AI-TPACK and science teaching self-efficacy among Chinese pre-service science teachers. Front. Psychol. 17:1911909. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1911909

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bootstrapped specific indirect effects (5,000 samples) in the PLS-SEM model of 548 teachers; the serial pathway was significant with "β = 0.093" and 95% CI [0.070, 0.121]. Direct paths remained significant, so the authors label it complementary partial mediation.

"The serial indirect association between AI literacy and AI integration intention through AI-TPACK and science teaching self- efficacy was also significant ( β = 0.093), supporting H6."
