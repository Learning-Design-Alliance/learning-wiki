### Chen Yuanshuai 2026

A virtual patient-based training program to enhance clinical decision-making competencies in general practice residents: mixed-methods study. `q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on the 100-point computer-based theoretical examination (A1–A4 and B1 formats) for all 50 residents. The article reports "statistical significance [95%CI (4.2, 9.1), p< .001]" and later states Cohen's d = 0.76 for this knowledge gain.

> "The mean score increased 6.7 points from baseline (68.1 ± 6.4) to post-intervention (74.8 ± 8.0). This improvement reached statistical significance [95%CI (4.2, 9.1), p< .001]"
