## Evidence

### Zhu An 2026

Zhu An, Ye Jian-Hong. (2026). The Impact of Scaffolding on Academic Achievement in Undergraduate Emergency Language Courses. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-026-10254-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Structural model validation in the quasi-experiment showed "a positive correlation with preparation (β = 0.486, p < 0.001)" and similar positive paths for performance and self-evaluation, validating H1–H3.

> "perceived teacher support showed a positive correlation with preparation (β = 0.486, p < 0.001), with an explanatory power of 23.6%; perceived teacher support showed a positive correlation with performance (β = 0.580, p < 0.001), with an explanatory power of 33.6%; perceived teacher support showed a positive correlation with self-evaluation (β = 0.519, p < 0.001), with an explanatory power of 26.9%."
