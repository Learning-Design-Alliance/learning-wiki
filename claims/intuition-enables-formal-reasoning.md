Theoretical argument from two parables: Fauconnier and Turner's 'inner Achilles' metaphor, in which formal reasoning is armor powerless without the invisible warrior, and Lewis Carroll's tortoise, showing that accepting premises does not force acceptance of a conclusion without an unstated inferential step. The article offers no empirical data; this is a conceptual argument.
