### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the stepwise linear regression in the prospective cohort study of 65 students who completed six years of medical school; the article reports no effect size for these non-significant predictors, so no magnitude is stated. The authors write these constructs "showed no ability to predict GPA in medical school".

> "EI, motivation, and self-esteem, attitudes towards science, and perceived personal incompetence showed no ability to predict GPA in medical school, in the sample of participants who completed six years of medical school."
