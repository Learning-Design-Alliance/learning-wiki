`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

POS tagging of the same six-student interview corpus, visualized as keyword networks in Figs. 6 and 7, zooming in on the term strategy. The authors interpret the strategy-learning connection as a preliminary finding that students identified the project as supporting their SRL learning.

> "the interviewees predominantly mention it in combination with other words that suggest a promotion of SRL through a digital tool. Specifically, the link between “strategy” and terms such as “personal”, “different” “new”, as well as “learning”, clearly indicate that the expected effect of the tool can be assumed"
