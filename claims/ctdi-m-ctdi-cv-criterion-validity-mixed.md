## Evidence

### Xiaoxia Wang 2018

Xiaoxia Wang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Tianhao Huang, Renqiang He, Weina Hao, Li Zhang. (2018). Development and validation of Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory for Chinese medical college students (CTDI-M). Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Criterion validity analysis correlating CTDI-CM subscales with CTDI-CV subscales. Open-mindedness of CTDI-CM correlated with maturity and self-confidence (r=0.170, P<0.01); systematicity/analyticity correlated with self-confidence (r=0.215) and negatively with inquisitiveness (r=-0.219); truth seeking correlated negatively with inquisitiveness (r=-0.318) and analyticity (r=-0.129).

> "However, there was no significant correlation between the total scores of CTDI-CV and CTDI-CM (r=0.028, P =0.643)"
