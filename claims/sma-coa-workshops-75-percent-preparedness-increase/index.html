Self-reported preparedness and familiarity rose by an average of 75% from before to after peer-led SMA clinical outcome assessment workshops among 79 Canadian clinical evaluators, in an uncontrolled pre-post survey

Pre- and post-workshop surveys of 79 volunteer Canadian clinical evaluators showed an average 75% increase in self-reported preparedness and familiarity with SMA outcome assessments; with no comparison group and self-report only, the study cannot show the workshops caused the change or that skill improved
