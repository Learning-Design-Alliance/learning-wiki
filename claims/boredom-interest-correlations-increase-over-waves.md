## Evidence

### Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025

Katharina Luisa Boehme, Thomas Goetz, Markus Feuchter, Franzis Preckel. (2025). Is Boredom the Opposite of Interest? A Longitudinal Reciprocal Effect Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09991-5

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Factor correlations from the CFA models at each wave, with printed correlations r = −0.67 to −0.87 (mathematics) and r = −0.70 to −0.90 (German), large effects whose magnitude grows over time. The authors interpret this via the differential distinctiveness hypothesis, stating the constructs "increasingly diverge in their extent of oppositeness during secondary school" while their statistical dependency increases.

> "Within the two-factor models, negative correlations between boredom and interest increased over the four waves of measurement (mathematics: rt1 = − 0.67 to rt4 = − 0.87; German: rt1 = − 0.70 to rt4 = − 0.90)"
