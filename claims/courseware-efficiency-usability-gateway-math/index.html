Higgin 2025¶

Higgin, T., Holl-Cross, C., Moorjani, A., & Edwards, A., with Cornfield, O., Eisen, S., Hosie, L., McNamara, H., Ray, D., & Tsinnajinnie, B. (2025). Faculty and student perspectives on gateway math courses and courseware. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677594

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Primary qualitative finding from the study of faculty and student experiences in gateway math courses. Participants reported that courseware must be "user-friendly, intuitive for students, and easy for faculty to adapt without advanced technical skills."

"Thoughtfully designed courseware enhances efficiency, supports independent student navigation, and allows faculty to focus on teaching, rather than on technical challenges."
