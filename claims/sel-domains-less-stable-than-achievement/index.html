James Soland 2019¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld, Emily Wolk, Sharon Bi. (2019). Examining the state-trait composition of social-emotional learning constructs: Implications for practice, policy, and evaluation. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness 12(3), 550-557. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/examining-the-state-trait-composition-of-social-emotional-learning-constructs-implications-for-practice-policy-and-evaluation/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The study's abstract reports a comparison of stability of academic achievement relative to four SEL domains (growth mindset, self-efficacy, social awareness, self-management), stating the achievement rank ordering is "highly stable over time" while SEL domains are more contextually influenced.

"While the rank ordering of math and reading skills are highly stable over time, the four SEL domains seem to be more strongly influenced by contextual factors."
