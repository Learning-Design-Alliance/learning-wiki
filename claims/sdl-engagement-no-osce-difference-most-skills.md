## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median OSCE performance did not differ significantly by SDL engagement (none vs one or more sessions) for each clinical skill in first year except BLS (p = 0.024), and for no skill in second year. [→ McGrath Deirdre 2015](#mcgrath-deirdre-2015)

## Evidence

### McGrath Deirdre 2015

McGrath Deirdre, Crowley Louise, Rao Sanath, Toomey Margaret, Hannigan Ailish, Murphy Lisa, Dunne Colum P. (2015). Outcomes of Irish graduate entry medical student engagement with self-directed learning of clinical skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0301-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney tests compared median OSCE percentages (end of 2010/11) between students with no SDL sessions and one or more sessions for the skill tested; first-year n = 99, second-year n = 89. No effect size was printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "There was no statistically significant difference between the median examination performance by group for each clinical skill for first year students, with the exception of Basic Life Support (BLS) (p = 0.024)"
