## Evidence

### Pechstein 1917

Pechstein, L. A. (1917). Alleged Elements of Waste in Learning a Motor Problem by the "Part" Method. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071944

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control learners were taught a single section and allowed to rest for the same intervals the part learners established; disintegration of the motor habit was measured by relearning expenditure. The article reports "the disintegration is shown to be practically nothing," with only slightly slower first relearning speed.

> "the disintegration is shown to be practically nothing. Most of the subjects retained a complete control over the activity and only showed a slightly slower speed for the first relearning trials."
