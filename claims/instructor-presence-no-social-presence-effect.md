## Evidence

### Tim Kühl 2025

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 2 (219 participants, three-item social presence scale adapted from Sondermann & Merkt, α = 0.83, plus a perceived-learning item adapted from Kizilcec et al.) found "neither differences for the social presence scale nor for perceived learning", η2p < 0.007.

> "Also, there were neither differences for the social presence scale nor for perceived learning observable, both Fs < 1, both ps > 0.478, both η2p < 0.007."
