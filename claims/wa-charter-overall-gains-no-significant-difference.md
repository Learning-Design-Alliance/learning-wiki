# Washington charter students' overall annual reading and math gains do not differ statistically from matched TPS peers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across all communities and years studied, charter school students posted larger average academic gains than their VCR peers in reading and math, but the differences were not statistically significant. [→ CREDO 2020](#credo-2020)

## Evidence

### CREDO 2020

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2020). Charter School Performance in the State of Washington. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-the-state-of-washington-2/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental VCR matched-comparison analysis of Washington charter students over three growth periods (2014-15 to 2017-18), with virtual matches created for 87 percent of tested charter observations in reading and 88 percent in math. The report concludes "there is no appreciable difference in academic gains for students in charter schools compared to their TPS counterparts."

> "The bars in Figure 2 shows that across all communities and years of study, charter school students posted larger academic gains in a year's time than their VCR peers. However, in both tested subjects, these differences were not statistically significant"
