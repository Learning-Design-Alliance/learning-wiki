## Evidence

### Boet Sylvain 2014

Boet Sylvain, Bould M. Dylan, Fung Lillia, Qosa Haytham, Perrier Laure, Tavares Walter, Reeves Scott, Tricco Andrea C. (2014). Transfer of learning and patient outcome in simulated crisis resource management: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Anaesthesia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4028539/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cluster-randomized trial of three hospitals (134 participants: obstetricians, family practitioners, pediatricians, registered nurses, certified RN anesthetists, one physician assistant) randomized to simulation-based, didactic-based, or no intervention. The simulation hospital showed "a statistically significant and persistent improvement of 37% (P < 0.05) in perinatal morbidity"; didactic-only and control hospitals showed no significant change.

> "A statistically significant and persistent improvement of 37% (P < 0.05) in perinatal morbidity was observed between the pre- and post-intervention for the hospital exposed to the simulation program. There were no statistically significant differences in the didactic-only or the control hospitals (P > 0.05)."
