## Evidence

### Xu Kailun 2026

Xu Kailun, Jiang Lingting, Zhang Yun, Du Yushen, Shen Yong, Chen Yiding, Shao Yingkuan. (2026). A laddered training model for breast surgery specialists: development and validation of a CBME-based precision education framework. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09688-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial comparing 10 residents receiving stepwise CBME training to 10 receiving conventional training over 6 months. The L1-stage biopsy concordance rate was 98.0% in the intervention group versus 86.0% in controls, a statistically significant difference (χ²=6.742, P = 0.009).

> "The experimental group demonstrated significantly higher histopathological concordance rate under ultrasound-guided biopsy (98.0% [49/50 cases]) compared to the control group (86.0% [43/50 cases]; χ²=6.742, P = 0.009)."
