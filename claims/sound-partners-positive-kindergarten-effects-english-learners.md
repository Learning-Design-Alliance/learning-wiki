### Sound Partners 2024

Sound Partners. (2024). Sound Partners. https://www.evidenceforesight.org/programs/sound-partners

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

One of two studies evaluated Sound Partners with English learners, involving kindergartners. Effect sizes were "significantly positive compared to controls," with a printed average effect size of +0.60 across Woodcock and CTOPP measures.

> "The effect size across Woodcock and CTOPP measures was +0.60 for kindergartners, +0.15 for first graders."
