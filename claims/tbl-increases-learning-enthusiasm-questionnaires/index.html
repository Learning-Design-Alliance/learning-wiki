Evidence¶

Lang Bingchen 2019¶

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of four studies involving 567 participants (288 receiving TBL) using questionnaire data on learning enthusiasm; a random-effects model was used due to substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 83%). The article prints no standardized effect size for this finding, only "RR = 1.38 with 95% CI [1.13,1.69]".

"the results from questionnaires indicated that the introduction of TBL pedagogy developed more students' learning enthusiasm (the ratio of students thinking the pedagogy method had great influence on developing learning enthusiasm: 95.49% in TBL group vs 64.87% in LBL group, RR = 1.38 with 95% CI [1.13,1.69], p < 0.0001, I2 = 83%)"
