### Kanzow Philipp 2026

Kanzow Philipp, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Wilkinson Jenny, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Nourredine Mikaïl. (2026). Voice Cloning Using AI vs Traditional Audio Recording for Prerecorded Courses in Medical Pedagogy: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/86569

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Production time was measured as total active time from slides without audio to final video with recorded sound. AI voice cloning showed "a 36% reduction" compared to traditional recording.

> "The AI voice cloning method was faster for generating the audio, with a mean of 22.5 (SD 6.45) minutes per video for AI voice cloning and 35 (SD 7.07) minutes per video for audio recording, representing a 36% reduction."
