Katrina E. Black and Michael C. Wittmann. (2008). Visualizing Changes in Student Responses Using Consistency Plots. https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.3136 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0812.3136](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0812.3136)

Analysis of the 2007 consistency plot (figure 11), where the final exam question reversed the positive direction to up. Severe setup errors with wrong signs were coded as other, so the hierarchical vertical axis hid the prevalence of the -v0 boundary condition error that table II showed.

> "An initial perusal of ﬁgure 11 suggests that only three students ended in the “boundary condition error” row of the diagram. However, the hierarchical nature of our vertical axis obscures the fact presented in table II that nine students actually made the−v0 error."
