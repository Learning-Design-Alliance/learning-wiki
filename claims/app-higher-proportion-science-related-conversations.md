# Intervention families had a larger proportion of science-related conversations than comparison families, most strongly after episode viewing (64% vs 32%)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Intervention families' after-viewing prompts addressed science at roughly twice the rate of comparison families (64% vs 32%), with smaller differences before viewing (25% vs 17%). [→ Dominguez 2021](#dominguez-2021)

## Evidence

### Dominguez 2021

Dominguez, X., Rood, E., Kamdar, D., Leones, T., & Huynh, K. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Field Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Coding of family conversations as addressing science, peripherally, or not, across Cohort 1 in-person sessions. The report states "Approximately, 64% of the intervention families’ after episode viewing prompts were found to address science, while only 32% of prompts from comparison families were science related"; before viewing the figures were 25% vs approximately 17%.
