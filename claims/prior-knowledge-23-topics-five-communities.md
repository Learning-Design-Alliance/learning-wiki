`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Bibliometric analysis of studies (1980–2021) from PsycInfo, PSYNDEX, ERIC, and Web of Science using structural topic modeling; after artifact-topic screening the corpus retained "N = 13,507 publications and k = 23 topics", and community detection grouped the topics into five communities labeled Education, Learning Environments, Cognitive Processes, Nonacademic Settings, and Language.

> "By doing so, we removed n = 160 irrelevant publications. Totals of N = 13,507 publications and k = 23 topics remained and were analyzed further."
