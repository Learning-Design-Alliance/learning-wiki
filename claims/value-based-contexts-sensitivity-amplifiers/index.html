q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Analytical result 4 of the article's value-critical analysis. The article treats these contexts not as distinct empirical cases but as analytically revealing environments, arguing they help explain why universal one-size-fits-all sustainability claims are analytically weak: the same AI system may be experienced as enabling in one context and as corrosive in another.

"Because education in these contexts is understood not merely as a service, but as a value-transmitting and morally charged practice, AI adoption can have disproportionate effects on the perceived authority of educators and the normative status of knowledge itself."
