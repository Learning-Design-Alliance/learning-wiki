The fact sheet states, without citing data or studies, that children entering kindergarten and first grade "may need support" and that "difficulty adjusting to these new environments can put them at academic risk." No evidence or effect size is offered.

"Children may need support when they enter kindergarten and first grade, because difficulty adjusting to these new environments can put them at academic risk."
