`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In a superiority randomized parallel controlled trial with 35 nurses per group, the PainScan training group's pNPAK knowledge scores rose from 38.40 ± 13.69 pre-intervention to 64.46 ± 19.08 post-intervention, a mean difference of 26.06 (95% CI 20.89 to 31.22), reported as "significantly improved PICU nurses' knowledge".

> "Both PainScan training and on‐site training significantly improved PICU nurses' knowledge (PainScan: t = 10.249, on‐site: t = 10.592, both p < 0.001) and skill levels in pain assessment"
