### Kalyuga 2004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 4 with 26 Year 10 and Year 11 students in a computer-based faded-worked-examples training environment; the adapted group's sequence was based on rapid diagnostic test performance, the yoked control's was random. The article reports the significant knowledge-gain difference above and no significant training-time difference, t(24) = .54, f = 0.11.
