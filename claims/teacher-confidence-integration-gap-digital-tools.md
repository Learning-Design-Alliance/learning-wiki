Quantitative survey of 200 teachers stratified across primary, secondary, and higher education using five-point Likert-scale questionnaires. Descriptive results showed 68% confidence versus 45% regular integration, and "only 45% of these educators integrated digital tools regularly into their lessons". No effect size or test statistic is printed for this contrast.

> "68% of teachers in the study reported feeling confident in using digital tools, indicating a strong sense of self -assurance in their ability to navigate technology. However, only 45% of these educators integrated digital tools regularly into their lessons"
