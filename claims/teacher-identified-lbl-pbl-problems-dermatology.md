## Evidence

### Li Yidan 2025

Li Yidan, Lei Li, Jiang Ling, Mao Ping, Fu Chuhan, Chen Jing, Zeng Qinghai. (2025). The dynamic case-based learning model: conducive to enhancing the practical learning effectiveness of dermatology for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07474-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Teacher questionnaire item assessing problems with the traditional bedside/outpatient (LBL) model, answered by 53 dermatologists with teaching experience. Percentages are proportions endorsing each listed problem.

> "Teachers further reported that due to limited clinical time, it was difficult for students to observe the dynamic changes in skin lesions (92.45%) and encounter typical cases (90.57%) and all the disease types required by the curriculum (86.79%)."

### Li Yidan 2025 (2)

Li Yidan, Lei Li, Jiang Ling, Mao Ping, Fu Chuhan, Chen Jing, Zeng Qinghai. (2025). The dynamic case-based learning model: conducive to enhancing the practical learning effectiveness of dermatology for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07474-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Teacher questionnaire item on PBL problems; the article adds that 70% believed PBL "lacked content regarding systematic curriculum" and 62% agreed it lacked strong instructional direction.

> "For the issues associated with the PBL model (Fig. 3c), most participants reported that the PBL model demands a significant expenditure of time and energy (75.47%)."
