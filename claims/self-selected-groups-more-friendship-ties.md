## Evidence

### Diego Ramírez 2023

Diego Ramírez, Eugenio J. Guzmán-Lavín, Javier Pulgar, Cristian Candia. (2023). Affinity-Based Groups in Secondary Education: Increased Stability at the Expense of Collaboration. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of four physics courses (two control, two experimental) at a private school in Chile. Regression models predicting within-group friendship in-degree at time t1 found the experimental (affinity-based) condition significant, with the authors reporting the treatment as "the main predictor (p < 0.001 and p < 0.01)" in Models 3 and 4.

> "we found, unsurprisingly that self-selected groups displayed a higher number of incoming friendship ties at the end of the semester compared to randomly assigned groups."
