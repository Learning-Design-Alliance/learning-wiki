## Evidence

### Ono Yuko 2018

Ono Yuko, Tanigawa Koichi, Kakamu Takeyasu, Shinohara Kazuaki, Iseki Ken. (2018). Out-of-hospital endotracheal intubation experience, confidence and confidence-associated factors among Northern Japanese emergency life-saving technicians: a population-based cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021858

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multivariable logistic regression on 2567 complete responses: "availability of regular simulation training using a mannequin (AOR 1.31; 95% CI 1.02 to 1.68)" and availability of regular operating room training (AOR 1.44; 95% CI 1.14 to 1.83) were independently associated with confidence in performing ETI.

> "availability of regular simulation training using a mannequin (AOR 1.31; 95% CI 1.02 to 1.68) and availability of regular operating room training (AOR 1.44; 95% CI 1.14 to 1.83) were independently associated with confidence in performing ETI."

### Ono Yuko 2018 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 1 comparison between confident and non-confident ELSTs in the same survey: availability of ETI skill retention programmes "was significantly greater (p=0.013 and p=0.002, respectively) for confident ELSTs".

> "The availability of ETI skill retention programmes, including regular simulation training using a mannequin and re-education in the operating room, was significantly greater (p=0.013 and p=0.002, respectively) for confident ELSTs."
