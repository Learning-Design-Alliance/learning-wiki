# The training proved effective irrespective of reading ability and was more effective for the Test Dyslexic group than for the Test Nondyslexic group, which made greater gains on rapid naming, L3 receptive vocabulary and L3 picture naming

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Test Dyslexic group made even greater gains than the Test Nondyslexic group on the L2 and L3 rapid automatized digit/picture naming tasks, the L3 receptive vocabulary test and the L3 picture naming task, catching up to the performance of the Control Nondyslexic group and/or the Test Nondyslexic group. [→ Pfenninger 2015](#pfenninger-2015)
`q2 i?` On the L3 picture naming task, the Test Dyslexic group was the only group to have significantly higher scores on the posttest than on the pretest (z = -2.52, p = .012), though differences with the two non-dyslexic groups remained significant on the posttest (U = 86, p = .007 and U = 81, p = .021). ## Evidence

### Pfenninger 2015

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion of pre-post results across tasks in the intervention study; the article links this pattern to Lovett et al.'s (2008) finding that lower language-ability learners improve at a faster rate than their higher language-ability peers in intervention programs.

> "The Test Dyslexic group made even greater gains than the Test Nondyslexic group on the L2 and L3 rapid automatized digit/picture naming as well as the L3 receptive vocabulary test and the L3 picture naming task, to the extent that they were able to catch up to the performance of the Control Nondyslexic group and/or the Test Nondyslexic group"

### Pfenninger 2015 (2)

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post Wilcoxon comparison on the L3 picture naming task (maximum = 25) in the intervention study; the Test Dyslexic group's mean rose from 13.8 (55.2%) to 18 (72%), while the Control Dyslexic group's mean fell from 12.4 to 11.2.

> "the Test Dyslexic group was the only group to have significantly higher scores on the post-PICNAM-L3 than on the pre-PICNAM-L3 ( z = -2.52, p = .012)"
