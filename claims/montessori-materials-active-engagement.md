# All Montessori materials encourage active engagement, illustrated by a fetching-and-matching activity that exercises working memory

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Montessori materials require hands-on engagement rather than passive reception, e.g., fetching a Number Rod from across the room to match a numeral held in mind. [→ Marshall 2026](#marshall-2026)

## Evidence

### Marshall 2026

Marshall, C., Livingston, L., & Van Herwegen, J. (2026). Mapping the Montessori Mathematics Curriculum to Dehaene's Four Pillars of Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00436-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' mapping argument: the fetch-and-match activity "requires holding the visual image of that quantity and its symbolic representation ‘in their head’", which they link to strengthening working memory and enabling consolidation.

> "All Montessori materials encourage Active Engagement. For example, after children have manipulated the number rods, their experience is extended by an activity in which they are being asked to fetch a particular Number Rod and match it to a numeral from across the room."
