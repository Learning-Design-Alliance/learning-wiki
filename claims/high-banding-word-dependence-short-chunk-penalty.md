### Woo 2025

Woo, D. J., Susanto, H., Yeung, C. H., & Guo, K. (2025). Approaching the Limits to EFL Writing Enhancement with AI-generated Text and Diverse Learners. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MLR analysis of the 37 compositions from high-banding schools. The article reports higher word-count partial correlations than the full sample, plus "a slight statistical significance represented by a p-value between 0.05 and 0.1" for negative short-AI-chunk correlations and positive human-chunk correlations with C and L scores.

> "the values of the partial correlations between the number of AI and human words in Table 6 are higher than those in Table 5, implying that the scores are even more strongly dependent on the number of words in the submissions from the high-banding school students."
