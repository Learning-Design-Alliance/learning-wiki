### Graham Wild 2022

Graham Wild. (2022). Improving Capstone Research Projects: Using Computational Thinking to Provide Choice and Structured Active Learning. School of Engineering and Information Technology, UNSW Canberra. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.15947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.15947)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of student feedback from the 2020-2021 implementations at a new university; quantitative scores showed 100% agreement overall for course and lecturer in both years, but the small number of students limited analysis to qualitative data. A student quote highlights "[t]he broad scope of what our topics could be".

> "The key themes identified in the responses addressing the courses "best elements" were: 1. Choice 2. Learning (prepared, trained), 3. Structured (weekly), and 4. Examples"
