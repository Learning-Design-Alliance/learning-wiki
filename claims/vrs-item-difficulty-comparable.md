Analysis of item characteristics from the randomized OSCE comparison (Table 1): the VRS scenarios' difficulty values of 0.67 and 0.58 were "comparable to the same scenarios in PHS (0.71 and 0.64, respectively)", with the overall average difficulty across stations at 0.68.
