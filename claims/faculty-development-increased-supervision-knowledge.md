## Evidence

### Minouei Marzieh Sadat 2025

Minouei Marzieh Sadat, Omid Athar, Yamani Nikoo Nikooyamani, Nasri Peiman, Ahmady Soleiman. (2025). Enhancing clinical faculties’ knowledge, attitudes, and performance in clinical supervision: a workplace-based faculty development program using proctor’s model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06769-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post single-group study of 20 pediatric faculty members; paired t-test on self-report questionnaire scores (before: 5.55 ± 2.31, after: 8.25 ± 1.53). The article reports "Cohen’s d effect size indicated a large effect of the development program on knowledge (Cohen’s d = 1.046)". No control group, so causal strength is limited.

> "A paired t-test revealed a significant difference in the mean self-reported knowledge of clinical supervision among faculty members post-intervention compared with pre-intervention. Furthermore, Cohen’s d effect size indicated a large effect of the development program on knowledge (Cohen’s d = 1.046)"
