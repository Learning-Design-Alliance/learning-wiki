Evidence¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r3

First-stage estimates from the randomized experiment using activity in the provided ChatGPT account and self-reports. The article reports adoption was almost entirely ChatGPT (a 60.2 pp increase versus 2.9 pp for other models, the latter not significant) and that 88 percent of AI users reported AI somewhat or very helpful.

"Assignment to the AI-allowed group increased AI usage byˆβ= 65.9pp as measured by activity in the provided ChatGPT account (p <0.001)."

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026 (2)¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r2

LLM-classified ChatGPT conversation logs from treated students, complemented by self-reports that show a similar picture except for writing: self-reported explaining (46 percent), drafting (17 percent), and editing (14 percent) are lower than the log-based rates.

"In the logs, explaining concepts is the most common use (56 percent of treated students), followed among the substantive categories by drafting responses (29 percent), editing responses (23 percent), and summarizing readings (16 percent)."
