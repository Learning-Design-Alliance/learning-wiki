Hahnel 2026¶

Hahnel, C. & Willis, J. (2026). Declining enrollment in America's public schools: How states can respond strategically. Bellwether; WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681594

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Bellwether analysis applying federal enrollment projections to 2022–23 per-pupil spending, finding the system "could lose $11.5 billion in state revenues annually by 2030–31". The brief notes the estimate holds 2020–21 per-student spending constant and may understate losses due to inflation.

"Based on each state's enrollment projections and 2022–23 per-pupil spending, the nation's public education system could lose $11.5 billion in state revenues annually by 2030–31, as compared with the revenue they received in 2025–26."
