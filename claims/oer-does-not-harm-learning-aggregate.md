## Evidence

### Hilton 2020

Hilton, J., III. (2020). Open educational resources, student efficacy, and user perceptions: a synthesis of research published between 2015 and 2018. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 853-876. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09700-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The synthesis's aggregate finding across all 25 efficacy studies published through 2018, combining its own 16 studies with the 9 identified in the prior 2016 synthesis.

> "A consistent trend across this OER efficacy research (spanning from 2008 to 2018) is that OER does not harm student learning. Although anecdotal reports that OER are not comparable to CT exist, the research does not bear this out with respect to student learning. While the impact of OER on student learning appears to be small, it is positive."
