Evidence¶

Sun 2026¶

Sun, C., Major, L., Daltry, R., Obradovic, L., & Friedberg, A. (2026). Enhancing literacy and numeracy through teacher dashboards and digital personalised learning: an A/B/C test in Kenyan early years education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10703-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Chi-square analysis of the independence of rating types and the three learner groupings within the Grouped Dashboard partition. Learners in the 'practised enough' category were perceived as less accurately placed than those in the other two categories.

"The “practised enough” group had significantly fewer “yes” ratings than the “need more practice” group (χ 2 (1, N = 5,325) = 8.96, p =.02). Also, the “practised enough” group had significantly more “no” ratings than the “keep going” group (χ 2 (1, N = 2,525) = 17.48, p <.001) and “need more practice” group (χ 2 (1, N = 2,694) = 20.73, p <.001)."
