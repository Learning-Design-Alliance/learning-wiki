# CLIL instruction through L2 was significantly better than teaching through L1 in promoting L2 vocabulary development across two experiments

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across two quasi-experiments, CLIL instruction produced significantly better L2 vocabulary development than content teaching through L1 (p = .001). [→ Xanthou 2011](#xanthou-2011)

## Evidence

### Xanthou 2011

Maria Xanthou. (2011). The impact of CLIL on L2 vocabulary development and content knowledge. English Teaching: Practice and Critique, 10(4). http://education.waikato.ac.nz/research/files/etpc/files/2011v10n4art7.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined analysis of the two quasi-experiments (77 Grade 6 learners in intact classes, ANCOVA-balanced) comparing CLIL and L1-medium science teaching on a 100-item L2 vocabulary test. The article reports CLIL was "significantly better (p= .001) than teaching through L1"; no effect size is printed.

> "Results showed that CLIL instruction was significantly better (p= .001) than teaching through L1, in promoting L2 vocabulary development."
