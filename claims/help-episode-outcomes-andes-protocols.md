VanLehn, K. (2015). Reflections on Andes' Goal-free User Interface. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0067-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Verbal protocol analysis by Ranganathan et al. (2014) of students using Andes, with episodes defined as beginning when Andes turned an entry red and ending when it was corrected or deleted. Episodes without help "usually ended poorly", with guessing or deletion.

> "then it turned out that in 38 % of the episodes, the student never asked for help; in 52 % of the episodes, the students asked for help from Andes alone; and in the remaining 12 % of the episodes, the students asked for help from the experimenter and Andes."
