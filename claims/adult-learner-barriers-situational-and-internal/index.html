DeSchryver 2015¶

DeSchryver, David A., with Deirdre Dlugoleski. (2015). Accelerating Change: How education technology developers can jump-start a new adult education market. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/29f39d7d-87f2-4e1e-920e-0c1ce86035cc

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The first design principle, citing Darkenwald and Valentine (1985), reports that "the most cited barriers to learning are situational" and that adults also face internal struggles like low confidence. The report summarizes this research secondhand without new data.

"Research has shown that the most cited barriers to learning are situational (e.g., job responsibilities, child care, lack of money and more),33 but adults also face internal struggles, such as a lack of confidence in their ability to learn."
