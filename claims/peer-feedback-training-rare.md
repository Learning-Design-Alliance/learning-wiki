## Evidence

### Dickinson Karen J. 2022

Dickinson Karen J., Kimbrough Mary Katherine, Young Amanda, Goddard Clayton, Urban Kelly, Kalkwarf Kyle J., Bhavaraju Avi, Margolick Joseph. (2022). An Educational Module to Teach Interprofessional Learner Feedback Skills for Trauma Simulation Events. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00268-022-06551-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-assessment survey of the 25 learners' prior experience. A further 16% had received didactics, 12% a workshop, and two had prior simulation experience; "Most learners (64%) had delivered peer feedback less than ten times".

> "The majority of learners had either not received any previous training in how to effectively deliver peer feedback (40%) or had engaged in self-directed learning only (24%)."
