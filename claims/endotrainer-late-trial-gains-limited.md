## Evidence

### Hernansanz A 2023

Hernansanz A, Rovira R, Basomba J, Comas R, Casals A. (2023). EndoTrainer: a novel hybrid training platform for endoscopic surgery. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-023-02837-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subset comparison (trials 14-16 versus 28-30) in the same 30-trial study with 80 gynaecologists: Table 3 shows non-significant differences for Accuracy (p = 0.941), Orientation Error (p = 0.211) and Tool Rotation (p = 0.054), with significant differences only in Exec Time and Tool Translation. The article also reports the expected subjects' fatigue was not detected.

> "Gains are significantly reduced compared to Ini-Mid. There are statistically significant differences only in Exec Time and Tool Translation."
