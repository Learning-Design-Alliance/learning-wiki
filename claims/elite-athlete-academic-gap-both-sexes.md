## Evidence

### Pinto-Escalona T. 2022

Pinto-Escalona T., Valenzuela P.L., Esteban-Cornejo I., Martínez-de-Quel Ó. (2022). Sport Participation and Academic Performance in Young Elite Athletes. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315651

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified sub-analysis (Table 2) of the same school cohort: girls (n = 570; 336 controls, 234 elite) and boys (n = 556; 307 controls, 249 elite). Controls scored higher than elite athletes on the standardized UEE grade in both sexes, p < 0.001; the Baccalaureate GPA difference for boys was p = 0.020.

> "Standardized UEE grade	7.52 ± 2.5	6.46 ± 2.1	<0.001	7.14 ± 2.4	6.61 ± 2.3	<0.001"
