q1 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review describing a small-n study comparing exercise intensities to a self-chosen intensity condition. The review reports the self-chosen condition had the highest enjoyment scores, but notes the condition included only n = 10. Quotes the fragment "choose their own workout experience".

"the greatest mood benefits in the form of high enjoyment scores after exercise came from those who were able to choose their own workout experience (based on the Physical Activity Enjoyment Scale) [57]"
