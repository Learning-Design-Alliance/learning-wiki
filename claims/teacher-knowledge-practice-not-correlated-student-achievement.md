Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on teachers' mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive professional development (NCEE 2016-4010). Washington, D.C.: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20164010

`q3 · i?`

Correlational analysis within the randomized study contrasting the study's assumption that content knowledge relates to practice and achievement; the printed "estimates of association between 0.00 and −0.05" are generally not statistically significant.

> "both knowledge and instructional practice in the study were generally not statistically significantly associated with student achievement (estimates of association between 0.00 and −0.05)."
