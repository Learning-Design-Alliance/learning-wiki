## Evidence

### Manher Jariwala 2016

Manher Jariwala, Jada-Simone S. White, Ben Van Dusen, and Eleanor W. Close. (2016). In-class vs. online administration of concept inventories and attitudinal assessments. 2016 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of per-instructor paper pre- and post-test participation rates across all sections (Fig. 2), reported as percentages: pre-tests 87-97%, post-tests 55-95%. The paper describes the cross-instructor variation qualitatively and attributes it to administration details; no inferential statistic is reported for this contrast.

> "Participation was uniformly high during pre -tests administered in class ( 87-97%). However, despite in- class administration, post- test participation varied widely across instructors (55 -95%), suggesting that differences in the details of administering assessments among instructors significantly impacted student participation ."
