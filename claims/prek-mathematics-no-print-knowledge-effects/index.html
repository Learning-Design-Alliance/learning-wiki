Evidence¶

U.S. Department of Education 2013¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013). Pre-K Mathematics with DLM Early Childhood Express Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/381

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

PCER Consortium (2008) randomized trial, 297 children in 36 schools, assessed print knowledge with the TERA-III (effect size 0.00), WJ-III Letter-Word Identification (0.06), and WJ-III Spelling (0.17), all p > 0.05. Domain average effect size 0.08, improvement index +3, characterized as an indeterminate effect.

"The authors reported that differences between the Pre-K Mathematics with DLM Early Childhood Express Math group and the comparison group were not statisti- cally significant on any of these measures, and the WWC confirmed those calculations."
