Greg Roberts 2018¶

Greg Roberts, Nancy Scammacca, and Garrett J. Roberts. (2018). Causal Mediation in Educational Intervention Studies. Behavioral Disorders, 43(4), 457–465. https://doi.org/10.1177/0198742917749560

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

A worked hypothetical illustration (not real or simulated data) of a randomized trial of a self-regulation intervention for first graders, with a total effect of 0.65. The article states "we have partial mediation through effortful control, which would be a reportable finding."

"In our example, Path a is 0.50, Path b is 0.40, and the indirect effect of treatment via effortful control (ab) is 0.20 (see Figure 2). This means that Path c′ is 0.45 (c′ = c – ab)"
