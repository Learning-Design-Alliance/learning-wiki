## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Redundant-condition students reported higher mental effort than non-redundant students, and high-complexity sentences elicited higher effort ratings than low-complexity sentences. [→ Tianlong Zu 2017](#tianlong-zu-2017)

## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2017

Tianlong Zu, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2017). Use of Eye-Tracking Technology to Investigate Cognitive Load Theory. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.113

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

2x2 mixed ANOVA on subjective mental effort ratings in the same multimedia-lesson study. Redundancy showed a significant main effect (F(1, 44) = 6.652, p = .013, partial η² = .102), and sentence complexity also showed a significant main effect, "F(1, 44) = 24.9,  p < .001, hp2 = .361" (partial η² = .361, a large effect).

> "We found that there is a significant main effect of redundancy on mental effort rating , F(1, 44) = 6.652 , p = .013, hp2 = .102. Students from  redundant condition reported higher mental  effort than those from non -redundant condition ."
