The review reports, citing Rolón-Dow et al. (2022), focus groups with Latinx undergraduates at a PWI that had launched a diversity campaign; students saw increased diversity messaging but an absence of structural representation of Latinx culture, and 'Shallow attempts at diversity reinforced a form of intersectional invisibility.'

> "Shallow attempts at diversity reinforced a form of intersectional invisibility, even promoting essentialist views of Latinx identity, in ways that undermined Latinx students' belonging."
