### Holmer Emil 2017

Holmer Emil, Heimann Mikael, Rudner Mary. (2017). Computerized Sign Language-Based Literacy Training for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Children. The Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education. https://doi.org/10.1093/deafed/enx023

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' summary claim for the longitudinal cross-over study of 16 DHH signing children in Swedish special schools, with reading indices assessed at five occasions. Development in word reading was predicted by precise imitation of unfamiliar signs; phonological awareness and working memory predicted initial level on their own, but those associations lapsed once sign imitation was included. The article presents this as the first such longitudinal demonstration in this population.

> "These results demonstrate for the first time a longitudinal link between sign-based abilities and word reading in DHH signing children who are learning to read."
