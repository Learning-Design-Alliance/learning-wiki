Effect of nursing simulation teaching information system based on HIS in comprehensive training course for senior nursing undergraduates: a randomized controlled trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons of questionnaire items in the RCT (n = 53 experimental, 58 control). Printed item statistics include Z = -2.447, p = 0.014 for admission focus assessment and Z = -2.287, p = 0.022 for health education; other objective items such as blood gas analysis did not differ significantly.

> "Significant differences were observed in the total scores of the achievement of teaching objectives and three specific items: "Familiarity with the nursing measures for the patient," "Mastering COPD-related admission focus assessment," and "Mastering COPD-related admission health education," with the experimental group demonstrating higher achievement levels (p < 0.05)."
