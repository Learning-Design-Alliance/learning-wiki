## Evidence

### MacKenzie Kenneth R. 2016

MacKenzie Kenneth R., Aning Jonathan. (2016). Defining competency in flexible cystoscopy: a novel approach using cumulative Sum analysis. BMC Urology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-016-0143-9

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Stated limitation of the prospective single-trainee case series: "only one trainee’s performance was assessed". The authors cite Maguire et al.'s CUSUM study of sling procedures, which found considerably variable procedure counts per trainee, as reason to expect similar inter-trainee variability.

> "CUSUM was demonstrated to be an objective technique however the authors acknowledge that only one trainee’s performance was assessed."
