Evidence¶

Altamura 2023¶

Altamura, L., Vargas, C., & Salmerón, L. (2023). Do New Forms of Reading Pay Off? A Meta-Analysis on the Relationship Between Leisure Digital Reading Habits and Text Comprehension. Review of Educational Research, 95(1), 53–88. doi:10.3102/00346543231216463

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Sensitivity and bias analyses on the 39 effect sizes: Egger's regression test and a published-versus-unpublished moderator comparison both indicated no publication bias; a separate comparison of five extra-large-sample studies (N > 10,000) against the rest was also non-significant (p = .153).

"The Egger test of the intercept did not reach a statistically significant result (b = -.039; 95% CI: -1.39, .61; p =. 427). In addition, multilevel meta-regression analyses with robust variance estimation revealed that the mean effect sizes from published versus unpublished studies were not statistically different (p = .525)."
