Instructing nontraditional physics labs: Toward responsiveness to student epistemic framing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140)

Interaction analysis of Group B (three students testing whether string tension explains the angle dependence). The TA cued the Discovery frame; the group stated they were trying to see if tension makes angle matter, but the framing was only brief before a later TA interaction.
