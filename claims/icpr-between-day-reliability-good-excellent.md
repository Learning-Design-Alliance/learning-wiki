## Evidence

### Almeida Debora 2020

Almeida Debora, Clark Carol, Jones Michael, McConnell Phillip, Williams Jonathan. (2020). Consistency and variability in human performance during simulate infant CPR: a reliability study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-day reliability analysis comparing time point 1 (immediately after training) and time point 2 (1 week later) for the same 27 students; Table 2 reports ICCs of .86 (rate), .92 (depth), .78 (leaning) and .75 (duty cycle), described as "good consistency between days".

> "The ICC values ranged from good to excellent, with duty cycle demonstrating the lowest ICC and compression depth the greatest. The SEMs were low suggesting good consistency between days."
