`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-training study: 3 intermediates and 3 residents performed 8 training sessions on the 3D-printed models at fixed intervals, with scores and operation times analyzed (Figures 3C-D). The article reports that "Intermediates performed at expert level, and residents approached intermediate level, with two residents surpassing intermediate scores by the 8th operation."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CUSUM analysis of operation time across the 8 sessions for the same 6 trainees, used to identify the plateau training period. The authors report operation time stabilized after 5 trainings for residents and 3–4 trainings for intermediates, attributing intermediates' earlier plateau to prior laparoscopic and bariatric experience.

> "Figure 3E indicated that the learning curve plateaued by the 5th training for 3 residents and by the 4th, 3rd, and 4th training for 3 intermediates."
