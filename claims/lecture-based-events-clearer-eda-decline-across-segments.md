## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hartikainen 2026

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects models on segment-level mean EDA (double-normalized EDA index, 1–100; 1161 segment observations from 546 events, M = 60.57, SD = 16.08) tested the segment main effect and segment × teaching implementation interaction via likelihood-ratio tests. The article reports the lecture-based decline as the implementation-moderated pattern; no standardised effect size is printed.
