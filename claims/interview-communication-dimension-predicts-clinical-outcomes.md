# The interview communication skills dimension is a small but statistically significant predictor of clinical skills, final WAM, and later-year knowledge outcomes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The communication skills interview dimension significantly predicted clinical skills phase 1 and 3, WAM program final, and knowledge exam phase 3 scores, with relatively small unique variance (about 3–4%). [→ Simpson Paul L 2014](#simpson-paul-l-2014)

## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on 318-student cohorts (clinical skills models N = 251 and 245). The communication dimension accounted for 3.4% unique variance in clinical skills phase 1 and 3.3% in phase 3, and significantly predicted WAM program final (p < 0.01) and knowledge exam phase 3 (p < 0.01).

> "Significant predictors for both clinical skills phase 1 and 3 scores were UAI (p < 0.01) and the communication skills dimension (p < 0.01)"
