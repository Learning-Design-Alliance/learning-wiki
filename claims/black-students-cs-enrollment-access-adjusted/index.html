Evidence¶

Barrow 2020¶

Barrow, L., Freire, S., & de la Torre, M. (2020). Trends in computer science education: Access, enrollment, and performance in CPS high schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606832

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Analysis of CS enrollment by race/ethnicity with and without adjustment for school-level CS access across CPS high schools. The report also finds Asian students more likely than average to enroll even after adjusting for access.

"Black students were the least likely overall to enroll in a CS course, in part because they were the least likely to attend a school that offered CS. Once we account for differences in access to CS, Black students were the most likely to enroll in a CS course."
