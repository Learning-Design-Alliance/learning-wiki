Build-cost accounting for the 2017 Merrimack College Mobile Lightboard, reported by the builders. Materials cost "was about $2500", with a stated 2025 estimate of "$3500 and $4000" and a CWL estimate that a comparable 2025 DIY build could be built for $2500 using mail-order glass and economical framing.

> "The cost of all materials for our 2017 build – T-slotted aluminum framing and hardware from 80/20, custom-drilled low iron “starfire” glass (whatever that was), LED lights and associated electrical components – was about $2500."
