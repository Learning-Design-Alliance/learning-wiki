## Evidence

### Lohbeck 2019

Annette Lohbeck. (2019). Social and dimensional comparison effects on academic self-concepts and self-perceptions of effort in elementary school children. Educational Psychology, 39:1, 133-150. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2018.1527018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 2) of the 200-child sample; e.g. MSCc-MSE r = 0.85 vs MSCa-MSE r = 0.80. The article states this is "not consistent with previous research" such as Arens and Hasselhorn (2015).

> "Contrary to expectations, in both domains, competence self-concepts were more highly related to self-perceptions of effort than affective self-concepts of corresponding domains. At least, the correlations with self-reported grades were as expected: In both domains, competence self-concepts were more highly related to self-reported grades than affective self-concepts of corresponding domains."
