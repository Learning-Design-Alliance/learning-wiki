### Bortolotti Alessandro 2025

Bortolotti Alessandro, Ricci Fabrizio, Cieri Carmelita, Cocco Federica, Martini Chiara, Panunzi Marcello, Rossi Davide, Sorella Anna, Saraullo Silvio, Scordo Davide, Renda Giulia, Gallina Sabina, Palumbo Riccardo. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Eye-tracking analysis (Tobii Pro Glasses 2) of the eight-participant pilot showed experts made fewer fixations than residents and held fixations longer, trends the authors report as "consistent across all levels of ECG complexity". Residents showed greater fixation-pattern variability on challenging cases.

> "Experts exhibited fewer fixations per ECG compared to residents (mean 67.68 ± 25.74 vs. 143.66 ± 29.89; p < 0.001), indicating a more streamlined visual approach. Fixation duration was also longer in experts"
