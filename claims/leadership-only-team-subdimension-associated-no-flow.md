## Evidence

### Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan 2026

Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan. (2026). Nontechnical Skills and Leadership Are Associated With More Efficient Mechanical Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Deployment in Real‐World Cardiac Arrest. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.125.049012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory analysis of TEAM subdimensions in the 85-case video-based cohort, using separate adjusted linear mixed-effects models with team leader as random intercept. The adjusted leadership effect was "−4 seconds [95% CI, −7 to −1]" (P=0.010).

> "leadership was the only TEAM subdimension that remained significantly associated with cumulative no‐flow time in adjusted models (adjusted effect, −4 seconds [95% CI, −7 to −1]; P=0.010)"

### Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan 2026 (2)

Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.125.049012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null results from the same exploratory subdimension models in the 85-case cohort; no effect sizes were printed for these null associations.

> "Teamwork scores (P=0.81) and task management scores (P=0.54) were not significantly associated with cumulative no‐flow time in the adjusted models"
