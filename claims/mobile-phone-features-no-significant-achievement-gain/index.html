Evidence¶

Khrisat and Mahmoud 2013¶

Abdulhafeth A. Khrisat & Salameh Saleem Mahmoud. (2013). Integrating Mobile Phones into the EFL Foundation Year Classroom in King Abdulaziz University/KSA: Effects on Achievement in General English and Students' Attitudes. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p162

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of forty male foundation-year students at King Abdulaziz University, one control and one experimental group taught over seven weeks. The article reports the post-test difference "in favor of the experimental group" was not significant, t-test significance 0.125 exceeding 0.05; equivalence was not tested.

"Table 4 shows that although there are differences in the mean scores on the post test in favor of the experimental group (14,10 ), the significance (2-tailed) on the t- test for equality of means ( 0.125 ) is bigger than (0.05)."
