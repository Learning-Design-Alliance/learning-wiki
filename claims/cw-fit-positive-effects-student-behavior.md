### student-behavior-summary

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, May). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams (CW-FIT). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/729

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

WWC systematic review synthesizing eight studies (two group design, six single-case design) using a weighted average of effect sizes. The student behavior summary effect size is 1.38 with improvement index +42, statistically significant (p<0.01), rated "Positive effects" at TIER STRONG based on 669 students.

> "Based on the eight studies, there is strong evidence that CW-FIT positively impacted student behavior and promising evidence that CW-FIT positively impacted teacher practice."
