Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-professional-development-chicago-supporting-effective-practice

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Finding from the report's analyses of school-level supports, using three-level hierarchical linear models on the 1997 and 1999 Consortium teacher surveys with school demographic controls. No effect sizes are printed.

> "we found that the quality of professional development can be promoted by principal leadership, school orientation toward innovation, and the strength of teacher professional community. At the same time, high-quality teacher professional development appears to strengthen these school- level supports in a mutually influential manner."
