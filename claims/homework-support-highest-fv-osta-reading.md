# Training parents to provide homework support was the most frequent common practice element in effective OSTA interventions for reading, and all 11 interventions using it were effective

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Homework support had the highest frequency count value (FV=12) for reading outcomes among common practice elements in effective OSTA interventions. [→ Engell 2020](#engell-2020)

`q3 · i?`

Systematic review synthesis of 30 effective and six ineffective OSTA interventions using vote-counting frequency values. The review reports "Homework support had the highest FV with 12 for reading"; no standardized effect sizes were calculated.

> "Homework support had the highest FV with 12 for reading, followed by training in parental school involvement at home and positive reinforcement with FVs of 10."
