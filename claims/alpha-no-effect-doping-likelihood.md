## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Abstract-reported ANOVA results from the quasi-experiment (intervention vs. no-treatment control, pre/post design); no significant intervention effect on the doping likelihood scale, though the article notes "a noticeable but insignificant intervention effect in the benefit situation."

> "Significant intervention effects were observed for knowledge (p < 0.01, η2 = 0.21) and explicit attitude (p < 0.05, η2 = 0.12) but not for doping likelihood (p > 0.05; benefit situation: η2 = 0.04; cost situation: η2 = 0.02)."
