## Evidence

### Meng Ya 2023

Meng Ya, Song Jian, Yu Xiaojing, Xu Xiaoxia, Zhang Hao. (2023). Design and evaluation of blended teaching in the smart classroom combined with virtual simulation training in basic nursing courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04721-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison in a quasi-experimental study of two intact classes taught over a semester. The blended smart-classroom group's scores were "significantly higher than those in the control group (P < 0.05)" on all three measures.

> "The scores for the theoretical assessment, experimental assessment, and subjective questions in the experimental group were significantly higher than those in the control group (P < 0.05)."

### Meng Ya 2023 (2)

Meng Ya, Song Jian, Yu Xiaojing, Xu Xiaoxia, Zhang Hao. (2023). Design and evaluation of blended teaching in the smart classroom combined with virtual simulation training in basic nursing courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04721-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 1 prints theoretical-assessment means of "82.54 ± 8.08" versus "77.58 ± 8.65" (3.429, 0.001*) and subjective-question means of "34.54 ± 3.87" versus "32.02 ± 4.44" (3.524, 0.001*) for the experimental and control groups.
