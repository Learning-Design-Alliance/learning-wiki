### Ji Ying 2026

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that all reviewed studies are static, cross-sectional designs relying heavily on self-reported data with social desirability risk, and that the studies collectively call for "longitudinal studies that track student perceptions and behaviours over time" and intervention-based designs testing educational approaches.

> "there is an urgent need for longitudinal studies that track student perceptions and behaviours over time and for intervention-based experimental or quasi-experimental designs that test the efficacy of educational approaches or interventions"
