## Evidence

### N.G. Holmes and Z. Yasemin Kalender 2020

N.G. Holmes and Z. Yasemin Kalender. (2020). Preliminary evidence for available roles in mixed-gender and all-women lab groups. arXiv:2007.14833v1 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.14833](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.14833)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structured observational coding of one voluntary lab session at a Danish technical university; graphical analysis of coded intervals found that, in mixed-gender groups, "men spent more time on the equipment than women and women spent more time on paper than men".

> "For example, on average, men spent more time on the equipment than women and women spent more time on paper than men."
