Evidence¶

Chen 2026¶

Chen, X., Zhou, Z., & Padilla, A. M. (2026). Do Females Outperform Males on Second/Foreign Language (L2) Achievement? A Meta-Analytical Review across Three Decades. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10092-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Mixed-effects moderator analysis across the pooled studies. Secondary settings showed g = 0.41, elementary g = 0.33, language institute g = 0.26, and university settings the smallest effect (g = 0.03).

"the three-level mixed-effects model identified population type ( F (3, 133) = 2.68, p < 0.05) as a significant moderator."
