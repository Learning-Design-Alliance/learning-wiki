Review synthesis of content reported in 84 of 134 studies, spanning topics from sexual behaviour and contraception to sexual pleasure and human rights. The review states "uncertainty and fragmentation appear to dominate over comprehensive and coordinated initiatives". No effect size printed.

"The increased focus on RSE has been accompanied by a divergence rather than a convergence of content. This has resulted in ever increasing new and hybridised content combinations."
