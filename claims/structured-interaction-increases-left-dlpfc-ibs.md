# Structured teacher-student interaction yields higher interbrain synchrony in left DLPFC than spontaneous interaction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Class-related IBS at left DLPFC channel CH11 was significantly higher in FT than in LT (d = 1.09) and CT (d = 0.76). [→ Qi Li 2024](#qi-li-2024)

## Evidence

### Qi Li 2024

Qi Li, Die Wang, Weilong Xiao, Yingying Tang, Qi Sun, Binghai Sun & Zhishan Hu. (2024). Structured interaction between teacher and student in the flipped classroom enhances learning and interbrain synchrony. npj Science of Learning 9:73. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00286-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA across lecture types on fNIRS-hyperscanning class-related IBS at CH11 (5 dyads excluded for bad channels); the main effect was significant (F(2,56) = 5.30, p = 0.031, ηp² = 0.16, FDR corrected), with FT higher than both spontaneous-interaction conditions.

> "Class-related IBS in the FT condition (M = 0.052, SE = 0.014) was higher than that in the LT condition (M = -0.012, SE = 0.012, p = 0.012, Cohen's d = 1.09, Bonferroni corrected) and the CT condition ( M = −0.0029, SE = 0.019, p = 0.040, Cohen's d = 0.76, Bonferroni corrected)"
