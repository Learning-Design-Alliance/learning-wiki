### Yim 2026

Yim, K.-H.; Lui, M. Y. (2026). Supporting Undergraduate Students' Learning in Practical Chemistry Courses through AI-Supported Experimental Design. Journal of Chemical Education. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.5c01311

Postsurvey on AI perspectives (Figure 3). Students found AI "particularly effective at generating a variety of ideas and directions" but noted it "often lacks detailed information or advanced chemistry knowledge" and provides impractical materials lists.
