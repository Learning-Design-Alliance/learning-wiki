### Ashtiani and Zafarghandi 2015

Farshid Tayari Ashtiani & Amir Mahdavi Zafarghandi. (2015). The Effect of English Verbal Songs on Connected Speech Aspects of Adult English Learners' Speech Production. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.212

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A paired-samples t-test compared the experimental group's (n=20) posttest scores on reading-aloud (M = 84.78, SD = 7.15) and speaking (M = 84.75, SD = 4.38). The difference was not statistically significant, t(19) = .031, p > .05, with a weak effect size of R = .009.

> "The results of the paired-samples t-test (t (19) = .031, P > .05, R = .009 representing a weak effect size) (Table 11) indicate that there was not any significant difference between the experimental group's mean scores on the posttests of reading-aloud and speaking."
