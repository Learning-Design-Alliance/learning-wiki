# Residency-trained principals raised math and reading test scores more than non-residency new principals in their first two years

Quasi-experimental event-study analysis of CPS principals and residency participants entering the principalship 2013–2019, addressing staggered treatment timing. The printed effect sizes are 0.07 standard deviations per year in math and roughly 0.05 standard deviations in reading, favoring residency completers' schools over non-residency principals' schools.
