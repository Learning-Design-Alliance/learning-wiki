## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Fluoroscopy time (FTP) and cumulative air kerma (CAK) were significantly lower for ST-group residents than CT-group residents. [→ Yang Lin 2020](#yang-lin-2020)
`q2 i?` Dose area product (DAP) did not differ significantly between the two groups. [→ Yang Lin 2020 (2)](#yang-lin-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Yang Lin 2020

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Radiation-related parameters recorded at the final angiography assessment in the retrospective cohort. CT-group residents had higher FTP and CAK values than ST-group residents; no effect sizes were printed.

> "the residents in the CT group showed higher mean values of FTP (631.47 vs 489.33 s, p<0.001) and CAK than the residents in the ST group (463.16 vs 401.30 mGy, p=0.043)"

### Yang Lin 2020 (2)

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

DAP at the final angiography test did not differ significantly between groups (p>0.052); equivalence was not tested. CAK and FTP reductions therefore did not extend to DAP in this cohort.

> "the mean DAP value in both groups indicated no difference (128 624.30 vs 128 012.10 mGy.cm2, p>0.052)"
