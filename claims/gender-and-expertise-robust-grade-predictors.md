## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In regressions of 13 grades, gender and expertise were the most robust predictors, with females outperforming males in 9 of 13 subjects (η² of 0.01–0.02 for 8 subjects; 0.09 for Astronomy) and expertise-group differences of η² 0.02–0.13. [→ Likhanov M. 2026](#likhanov-m-2026)

## Evidence

### Likhanov M. 2026

Likhanov M., Alenina E., Bloniewski T., Zhou X., Kovas Y. (2026). Anxiety and Performance in High‐Achieving Adolescents: Associations Among 8 General and Specific Anxiety Measures and 13 School Grades. PsyCh Journal. https://doi.org/10.1002/pchj.70088

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA/regression analyses of gender and expertise effects on grades and anxiety; the article also reports expertise-group grade differences (eta squared ranging from 0.02 to 0.13), highest for Science and Literature groups, lowest grades for the Sports track.

> "In our study, females outperformed males in 9 out of 13 school subjects, with negligible effect for 8 subjects (eta squared of 0.01–0.02) and a slightly stronger effect for Astronomy, for which the N was reduced (eta squared of 0.09)."
