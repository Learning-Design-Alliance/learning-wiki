## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group (mean 9.54 of 13 possible points) significantly outperformed the control group (mean 6.89) on the five visualization tasks (P = 0.000). [→ Herga et al. 2014](#herga-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Herga et al. 2014

Nataša Rizman Herga, Milena Ivanuš Grmek, Dejan Dinevski. (2014). Virtual Laboratory as an Element of Visualization when Teaching Chemical Contents in Science Class. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043246

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the five test tasks (tasks 2, 3, 8, 10, 11) with visualization elements, worth 13 points. The t-test gave EG mean 9.54 (SD 2.19) versus CG mean 6.89 (SD 2.03), statistically significant (t = 6.455, P = 0.000), confirming hypothesis H2.

> "Those pupils from the experimental group who learned about the chemical content through a virtual laboratory, had an advantage over  the pupils in the control group,regarding these tasks."
