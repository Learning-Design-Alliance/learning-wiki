# Content analysis of project reports identified five factors contributing to PLC formation (common interests, communication systems, mutual learning, collaborative work, UNAL values) and three hindering factors (administrative management, project scheduling, personal non-compliance)

### Layton-Jaramillo 2025

Layton-Jaramillo, S.E., Duarte-Velasco, O.G., Aguaded-Ramírez, E.M., & Carrillo-Rosúa, F.J. (2025). Innovation in university science teaching through professional learning communities. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 15(1), 142-161. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.3115

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive content analysis of teams' interim and final reports (analyzed in Nvivo) yielded the contributing categories; "common interests were recognized as the primary factor for joining and/or developing an innovation initiative within I3P".

> "Regarding the factors that helped the teams consolidate as PLC, the following categories were identified: common interests, communication systems, mutual learning, collaborative work, and UNAL values."

### Layton-Jaramillo 2025 (2)

Layton-Jaramillo, S.E., Duarte-Velasco, O.G., Aguaded-Ramírez, E.M., & Carrillo-Rosúa, F.J. (2025). Innovation in university science teaching through professional learning communities. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 15(1), 142-161. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.3115

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content analysis of project reports identified three hindering categories, illustrated by team quotes on "the administrative timelines required to submit a request to the university", fund disbursement delays, and a member's unfulfilled commitments.

> "On the other hand, regarding the factors that hindered the consolidation of PLCs during the execution of the project, three categories were identified: administrative management, project scheduling, and personal non-compliance."
