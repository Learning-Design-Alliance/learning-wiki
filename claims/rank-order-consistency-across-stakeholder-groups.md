## Evidence

### Conlon Peter 2012

Conlon Peter, Hecker Kent, Sabatini Susan. (2012). What should we be selecting for? A systematic approach for determining which personal characteristics to assess for during admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-105

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of group-level weighted z-scores from the paired-comparison survey (n = 1,312 respondents). The article reports "r = .97, p< .001" for sex and correlations between .86 and .99 for the other groupings, indicating highly consistent rank orders.

> "The z scores were significant correlated for sex (r = .97, p< .001), for self-identified practitioner type (r’s between .92 and .99, p<.001), for employment status (r’s between .86 and .96, p <.001)"
