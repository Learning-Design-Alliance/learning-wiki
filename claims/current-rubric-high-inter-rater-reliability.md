## Evidence

### Ryan 2014

Ryan, Q. X., Astolfi, C., Baily, C., & Pollock, S. J. (2014). Validation of a Conceptual Assessment Tool in E&M II. 2014 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability check with 90 CURrENT exams (CU N=47, 4-yr college N=43) scored independently by two raters after discussing 11 exams as initial training. Raters agreed on overall scores to within ±5% for 86% of students.

> "The inter-rater reliability for the two raters working independently was very high: the total CURrENT score given by these two raters only differed by 0.2% (0.01% after discussion)."

### Ryan 2014 (2)

Ryan, Q. X., Astolfi, C., Baily, C., & Pollock, S. J. (2014). Validation of a Conceptual Assessment Tool in E&M II. 2014 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cohen's kappa computed for all sub-questions from a contingency table of all possible scoring combinations (N=90). Individual question kappa values in Table 1 range from 0.70 to 1.

> "We generated a contingency table based on all possible scoring combinations and computed κ (N=90) for all sub-questions. All of our κ values (raters 1&2) are "substantial" or better [13], suggesting a satisfactory inter-rater agreement (See Table 1)."
