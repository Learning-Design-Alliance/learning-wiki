## Evidence

### Gattas 2022

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

View-invariant object recognition experiment (sample 10 min, 5-min delay) 3 months post-enrichment in the four-group design. The ANOVA group effect was not significant, F(3, 36) = 1.468, p = 0.240, partial η2 = 0.109; only the EH-vs-novelty contrast was tested significantly.

> "The OC group, to the most extent (numerically), treated both rotated and non-rotated objects as familiar, exploring them equally (Fig. 5d). Conversely, a paired-samples t-test showed that the EH group displayed a preference for the rotated object, suggesting that this group tended to perceive the rotated object as novel t(9) = −3.046, p = 0.014."
