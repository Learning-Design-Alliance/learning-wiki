### John Chick 2026

John Chick, Laura Morello and Stephanie Staffey. (2026). From fear to innovation: a case study of transformative faculty development for ethical AI integration in higher education. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00225-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Pre/post survey comparison of ten institute participants. The article reports that high-confidence responses increased by 60 percentage points, "from 0% to 60%", with 90% at moderate/high confidence post-institute; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Confidence in helping students use AI was flipped from pre to post: while participants responded overwhelmingly with low confidence or unsure before the institute (80%), most participants responded with moderate/high confidence after the institute (90%)"
