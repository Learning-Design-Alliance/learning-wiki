## Evidence

### O’Brien BC 2024

O’Brien BC, Collins S, Haddock LM, Sani S, Rivera JA. (2024). More Than Maintaining Competence: A Qualitative Study of How Physicians Conceptualize and Engage in Lifelong Learning. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1327

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 34 academic physicians; practices included accountability and access to research (contextual), observing others and seeking feedback (social), and looking things up and reflection (independent), summarized as "contextual; social; and independent processes".

> "Participants described many specific lifelong learning practices, which we categorized as contextual; social; and independent processes."
