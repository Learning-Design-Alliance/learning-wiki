## Evidence

### de Jong 1996

Ton de Jong & Monica G.M. Ferguson-Hessler. (1996). Types and Qualities of Knowledge. Educational Psychologist, 37(2), 105-113. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep3102_2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Berry and Broadbent (1984), a series of experiments in which subjects controlled a simulated sugar factory and were questioned afterward. Written-posttest ability and successful control were uncorrelated, indicating tacit knowledge; no effect size is printed.

> "Results from this study showed that the ability to answer questions on a written posttest was not correlated with the ability to control the factory success- fully."
