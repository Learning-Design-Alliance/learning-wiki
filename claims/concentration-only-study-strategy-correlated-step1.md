## Evidence

### West Courtney 2014

West Courtney, Kurz Terri, Smith Sherry, Graham Lori. (2014). Are study strategies related to medical licensing exam performance? International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5439.6491

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of 79 pre-clinical medical students at one institution, with Step 1 score as dependent variable and MCAT, UGPA, year averages, NBME exam data and ten LASSI subscales as predictors. The article reports "Concentration (Model 1 - β = .264; Model 2 - β = .254)" as the sole significant study strategy predictor.

> "Concentration (Model 1 - β = .264; Model 2 - β = .254) is the only study strategy correlated with Step 1 performance."
