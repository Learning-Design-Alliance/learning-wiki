### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Neha Rani, Weedguet Mildort, and Eva-Marie Cook. (2026). Students' Reliance on AI in Higher Education: Identifying Contributing Factors. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-12773-0_9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-12773-0_9)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the fifty-student controlled experiment. The article prints r = −0.439 (p = .001) for programming literacy, r = −0.312 (p = .027) for need for cognition, and r = −0.308 (p = .030) for programming self-efficacy against underreliance.

> "The strongest negative correlation with underreliance was observed for programming literacy (r = −0.439, p = .001), indicating that students with stronger programming knowledge were significantly less likely to dismiss helpful AI recommendations."
