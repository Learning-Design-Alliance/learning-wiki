q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in Phase 3 (experimentation and evidence-based inference). The article cites examples such as supervised ML automatic coding and unsupervised clustering, where "it is the clustering algorithm—not the human researcher—that performs the initial exploratory analysis." The authors argue researchers increasingly need data science skills alongside traditional expertise.

"the growing automation of research analyses moves the researcher from being the primary analyst to becoming the interpreter and validator of AI‑generated outputs"
