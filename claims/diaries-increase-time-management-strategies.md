## Evidence

### Arsal 2010

Arsal, Z. (2010). The effects of diaries on self-regulation strategies of preservice science teachers. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 5(1), 85-103. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ884413

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same pre-/post-questionnaire control group study, the experimental group's time management scores increased while the control group's decreased, showing "significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of... time management".

> "there was significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of metacognition (t=2.17, p=.034) and time management (t=2.36, p=.022) strategies"
