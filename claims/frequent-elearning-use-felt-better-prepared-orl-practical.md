## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within the e-learning group, students who used the preparation content frequently more often strongly agreed that e-learning prepared them sufficiently for the practical course, a statistically significant difference by Fisher's exact test (p = 0.001). ## Evidence

### Dombrowski T 2018

Dombrowski T, Wrobel C, Dazert S, Volkenstein S. (2018). Flipped classroom frameworks improve efficacy in undergraduate practical courses – a quasi-randomized pilot study in otorhinolaryngology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1398-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of the intervention group (n = 62 complete responses per item) after a mandatory ORL practical course; responses split by frequency of e-learning use (frequently, occasionally, rarely) on a four-point Likert scale. The article reports the item difference as statistically significant, "p = 0.001, Fisher’s exact test", with no effect size printed.

> "The difference was statistically significant for the item “E-learning prepared sufficiently”, p = 0.001, Fisher’s exact test."
