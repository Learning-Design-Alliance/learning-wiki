## Evidence

### The EVALUATE Group 2019

The EVALUATE Group. (2019). Evaluating the impact of virtual exchange on initial teacher education: a European policy experiment. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2019.29.9782490057337

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

At the end of the exchanges, 564 participants completed a four-item satisfaction survey. The report states "64% of participants were positive overall with their virtual exchanges" and that 75.2% would recommend telecollaboration to other student-teachers (mean 3.91).

> "Taking a cut-off of 3.4 in line with Rienties, Brouwer, and Lygo-Baker (2013b), 64% of participants were positive overall with their virtual exchanges based upon the combined satisfaction scores of the four items."
