### Södersved Källestedt M-L 2020

Södersved Källestedt M-L, Asp M, Letterstål A, Widarsson M. (2020). Perceptions of managers regarding prerequisites for the development of professional competence of newly graduated nurses: A qualitative study. Journal of Clinical Nursing, 29, 4784–4794. https://doi.org/10.1111/jocn.15522

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of nine managers in a Swedish healthcare region, analysed with data-driven inductive thematic analysis. The theme 'Building relationships' with subthemes on team and patient relationships emerged from the interviews; managers stated that "Building relationships with colleagues and team members as well as with patients is regarded as a prerequisite".

> "Building relationships with colleagues and team members as well as with patients is regarded as a prerequisite for the development of professional competence of nurses."
