## Evidence

### Bielik Viktor 2025

Bielik Viktor, Nosáľ Vladimír, Nechalová Libuša, Špánik Milan, Žilková Katarína, Grendar Marian. (2025). The prediction model of academic achievement based on cardiorespiratory fitness and BMI status for ninth-grade students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05353-2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression on the 6,324-child analytic sample, with unfit-obesity as reference. Fit normal-weight boys showed significantly higher odds at Slovak levels 3 and 4, including "level 4 (OR = 5.84; 95% CI, 2.92–11.67; p < 0.001)".

> "Fit + normal weight boys compared to unFit + BwO had significantly higher odds for having a greater level of academic achievement in Slovak language: level 3 (OR = 3.47; 95% CI, 1.93–6.24; p < 0.001), and level 4 (OR = 5.84; 95% CI, 2.92–11.67; p < 0.001)."

### Bielik Viktor 2025 (2)

Bielik Viktor, Nosáľ Vladimír, Nechalová Libuša, Špánik Milan, Žilková Katarína, Grendar Marian. (2025). The prediction model of academic achievement based on cardiorespiratory fitness and BMI status for ninth-grade students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05353-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same multinomial regression, mathematics outcome. The comparison of fit normal-weight boys with unfit boys with obesity was significant for the greatest mathematics level, "OR = 2.65; CI, 1.41- 4.99; p < 0.001".

> "In mathematics Fit + normal weight boys compared to unFit + BwO had significantly higher odds for having the greatest level of academic achievement (OR = 2.65; CI, 1.41- 4.99; p < 0.001)."
