## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Emery 2021

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model averaging of the best linear models (ΔAICc < 2) for mean RTOP scores of 64 faculty (35 FIRST IV, 29 comparison) at 35 institutions. The Self-efficacy: Teaching Methods factor had relative importance 1, estimate 0.442, z = 4.72, P < 0.001, described as "related to higher RTOP scores".

> "With a positive coefficient, higher “Self-efficacy: Teaching Methods” was related to higher RTOP scores, while greater “Intentions: Knowledge Transmission” and “Percent Teaching Time Allocation” were related to lower RTOP scores."
