## Evidence

### Escribano Nuria 2023

Escribano Nuria, Belliard Virginia, Baracco Bruno, Da Silva Dayana, Ceballos Laura, Fuentes M. Victoria. (2023). Rubric vs. numeric rating scale: agreement among evaluators on endodontic treatments performed by dental students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04187-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of overall scores awarded to the same 42 portfolios under both methods, tested with Student's t test. The study reports mean overall scores "were lower for E1, E3 and E4 (p < 0.05)" with the rubric; no effect size is printed.

> "Student’s t test showed that with the use of a rubric mean overall scores were lower for E1, E3 and E4 (p < 0.05), while for E2 differences were not found (p > 0.05)."
