## Evidence

### Al Musawi 2015

Al Musawi, A., Ambusaidi, A., Al-Balushi, S., & Al-Balushi, K. (2015). Effectiveness of e-lab use in science teaching at the Omani schools. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057335

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports, citing Al-Shannaq et al. (2004), an experimental study of the dry laboratory strategy with 142 University of Jordan students divided into experimental (84) and control (59) groups, finding "statistically significant difference at the level of (0.05) in academic achievement in favor of the experimental group". No effect size is printed.
