## Evidence

### Peters Philipp 2023

Peters Philipp, Lemos Martin, Bönsch Andrea, Ooms Mark, Ulbrich Max, Rashad Ashkan, Krause Felix, Lipprandt Myriam, Kuhlen Torsten W., Röhrig Rainer, Hölzle Frank, Puladi Behrus. (2023). Effect of head-mounted displays on students' acquisition of surgical suturing techniques compared to an e-learning and tutor-led course: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000464

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multivariable linear regression in the 150-novice RCT; ΔGRS was also correlated with pretest suture number (β=−1.80±0.29, P<0.001). The article concludes only the number of sutures performed is a good determinant of competence improvement.

> "However, a multivariable analysis (adjusted for group, age, and sex) showed that ΔGRS was significantly correlated with the number of sutures practiced during the training time [β=0.15±0.49 (estimate±SD), P=0.002]"

### Peters Philipp 2023 (2)

Peters Philipp, Lemos Martin, Bönsch Andrea, Ooms Mark, Ulbrich Max, Rashad Ashkan, Krause Felix, Lipprandt Myriam, Kuhlen Torsten W., Röhrig Rainer, Hölzle Frank, Puladi Behrus. (2023). Effect of head-mounted displays on students' acquisition of surgical suturing techniques compared to an e-learning and tutor-led course: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000464

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Discussion of the RCT's primary endpoint: all three courses advanced skills, and the tutor-led course with feedback did not outperform the self-directed courses on ΔGRS. The article notes this is consistent with prior laparoscopic suturing work.

> "Contrary to our expectations and the desires of the students, feedback from a tutor in our RCT did not show a significant impact on the quality of surgical suturing techniques among novices."
