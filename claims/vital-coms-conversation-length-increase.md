## Evidence

### Fiona Quigley 2025

Fiona Quigley, Leona Ryan, Raymond Bond, Toni McAloon, Huiru Zheng, Anne Moorhead. (2025). A Virtual Simulator to Improve Weight-Related Communication Skills for Health Care Professionals: Mixed Methods Pre-Post Pilot Feasibility Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65949

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Paired-samples t test in the feasibility study (n=31) on the female virtual patient scenario: the increase was statistically significant (t30=7.49, P=.001) with "a large effect size (Cohen d=1.34)".

> "For the female scenario, the mean conversation length increased from 3.73 (SD 1.36) minutes pretraining to 6.08 (SD 2.26) minutes post training, representing a mean difference of 2.35 (SD 1.75, 95% CI 1.73‐2.97) minutes"

### Fiona Quigley 2025 (2)

Fiona Quigley, Leona Ryan, Raymond Bond, Toni McAloon, Huiru Zheng, Anne Moorhead. (2025). A Virtual Simulator to Improve Weight-Related Communication Skills for Health Care Professionals: Mixed Methods Pre-Post Pilot Feasibility Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65949

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Same paired-samples analysis (n=31) on the male virtual patient scenario: statistically significant increase (t30=8.03, P=.001) with "a large effect size (Cohen d=1.44)".

> "for the male scenario, the mean conversation length increased from 3.61 (SD 1.12) minutes pretraining to 5.65 (SD 1.76) minutes post training, with a mean difference of 2.03 (SD 1.41, 95% CI 1.53‐2.53) minutes"
