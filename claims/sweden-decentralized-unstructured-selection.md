## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Sweden selects surgical trainees locally at each hospital without national guidelines, using an informal 6–12 month locum system, and interviewed surgeons reported unsuitable candidates slipping through. [→ Hagelsteen K 2021](#hagelsteen-k-2021)

## Evidence

### Hagelsteen K 2021

Hagelsteen K, Pedersen H, Bergenfelz A, Mathieu C. (2021). Different approaches to selection of surgical trainees in the European Union. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02779-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study based on 13 semi-structured interviews with experienced Swedish surgeons plus literature and research-group knowledge. Interviewees saw the unstructured locum assessment as problematic but supported evaluating candidate fit in situ, and "had experienced unsuitable candidates slipping through the system."

> "Sweden conducts strictly local selection processes at each hospital without standardized guidelines or consensus on selection criteria."
