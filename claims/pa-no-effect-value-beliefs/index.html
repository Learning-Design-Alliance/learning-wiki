Evidence¶

Gladstone 2025¶

Gladstone, J. R., Schroeder, N. L., Heidig, S., Zhang, S., Palaguachi, C., & Pitera, M. (2025). Do Pedagogical Agents Enhance Student Motivation? Unraveling the Evidence Through Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10050-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Three-level meta-analysis with CHERVE of PA-versus-non-PA comparisons measuring value beliefs/task relevance. The pooled effect was non-significant with significant heterogeneity (Q(24) = 58.91, p < 0.001).

"The random effects 3LMA with CHERVE exploring the impact of PAs on learners' value beliefs showed that PAs have a small, non-significant effect (g = 0.09, p = 0.40, kcomparisons = 25, kstudies = 9)."
