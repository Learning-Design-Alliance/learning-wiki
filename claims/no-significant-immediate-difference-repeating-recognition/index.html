Evidence¶

Ha 2017¶

Do Thi Ha. (2017). The Impact of Different Instructions on Vietnamese EFL Students' Acquisition of Formulaic Sequences. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p18

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Mann-Whitney paired comparisons (CT vs PBI, TBI vs CT, PBI vs TBI) on Form Recognition of Repeating chunks immediately after the 45-minute treatment; no significant difference was detected. Descriptive means were 9.125 (CT), 9.647 (PBI), 9.667 (TBI).
