## Evidence

### Grissa Mohamed Habib et al 2023

Grissa Mohamed Habib et al. (2023). Comparison of simulation and video-based training for acute asthma. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04836-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-year follow-up: students retook the same MCQ online. Relative improvement over pre-test remained "43.7% [11.4- 75] vs 15.5% [1.3- 39.2]" (p = 0.017) favoring HFS; both groups' scores declined from post-test 1.

> "These MCQ post-test 2 scores were both statistically improved compared with pre-test scores: Δ-score 2 was significantly higher in HFS group than the IVC group (43.7% [11.4- 75] vs 15.5% [1.3- 39.2]) (p = 0.017)."
