Evidence¶

Rachel Lam 2019¶

Rachel Lam. (2019). What students do when encountering failure in collaborative tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0045-1

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental analysis of dyadic posttest scores from two intact fourth-grade classes (n = 40 students, 20 dyads), with English and Science exam scores as covariates. "Cohen's d = 0.77" favors Select; Levene's test showed equal variance, P = 0.21.

"With English and Science scores as covariates, an ANCOVA showed a significant effect of condition, F(3,16) = 8.89, P < 0.01, and we observed a moderate to large effect size, Cohen's d = 0.77."
