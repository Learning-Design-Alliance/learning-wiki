# Individual and collaborative study modes of learning objects produced no significant pre-post gain differences in three of four learning units

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mann-Whitney U tests found no significant difference between individual and collaborative groups' pre-post gains for the Pressure/Buoyant force, Work and energy, and Map scales learning objects. [→ Akpinar 2014](#akpinar-2014)

## Evidence

### Akpinar 2014

Akpinar, Y. (2014). Different modes of digital learning object use in school settings: Do we design for individual or collaborative learning? International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 10(3), 87-95. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1747

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study with 102 seventh graders and 25 ninth graders in four state schools; non-parametric Mann-Whitney U tests on pre-post gains. The article reports "did not significantly differ in three of the studies" with the printed Z, df and p values for each unit.

> "the groups’ pre and post test differences did not significantly differ in three of the studies, with the seventh grade Science LO for Pressure/Buoyant force (Z= -0.28; df=51; p>0.05), Work and energy (Z= -0.79; df=19; p>0.05), and a ninth grade Geography LO, Map scales (Z=-0.14; df=23; p>0.05)"
