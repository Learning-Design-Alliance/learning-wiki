## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the cluster randomised trial, the reflection+test group was compared with the test-and-feedback group on the modified Cardiff test; "there were no significant differences" in total score, calling 112 or hand position at either time point. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Directly after training and at the 6-month follow-up, there were no significant differences between group RT (n=192) and group T (n=224) with regard to the total score of the modified Cardiff test, calling 112 or hand position during compressions (table 3)."
