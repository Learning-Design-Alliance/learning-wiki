Rahimi A. Masie, Uluç Ezgi, Hardon Sem F., Bonjer H. Jaap, van der Peet Donald L., Daams Freek. (2024). Training in robotic-assisted surgery: a systematic review of training modalities and objective and subjective assessment methods. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10915-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the 45 transferability studies. Operating room performance was assessed in real time or with videos using the GEARS form, and control groups received no simulation training; the review reports "In 37 (82%) of the studies, simulation performance was correlated with better performance in the operating room."

> "In total, 45 studies primarily focused on the transferability of simulation skills to the operating room. In 42 (93%) of the studies, the initial training and assessment consisted of virtual training on the dVSS with dVSS metrics as assessment, and dry lab sessions with GEARS assessment."
