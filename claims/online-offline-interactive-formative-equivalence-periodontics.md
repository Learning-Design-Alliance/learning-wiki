### Veeraiyan Deepak Nallaswamy 2022

Prospective comparison of 24 online (Zoom) and 25 offline students taught identical Periodontics modules; cumulative written-test formative scores compared by unpaired t-test. The study reports "a statistically significant difference was not observed in terms of formative assessment" (p = 0.98). No effect size was printed.
