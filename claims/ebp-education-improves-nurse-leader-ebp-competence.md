Meta-analysis of three studies measuring EBP competence with scales such as the EBP Nurse Leadership Scale and Nursing Manager EBP Competency Scale, comparing baseline and post-intervention values. The review reports "a SMD of 1.76 (95% CI: 1.39, 2.13; p < 0.00001)".
