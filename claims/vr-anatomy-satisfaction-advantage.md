## Evidence

### Zhao Jingjie 2020

Zhao Jingjie, Xu Xinliang, Jiang Hualin, Ding Yi. (2020). The effectiveness of virtual reality-based technology on anatomy teaching: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1994-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Secondary-outcome synthesis of five of the 15 included trials that assessed satisfaction levels, pooled with a fixed effects model. The review reports "SMD = 0.77; 95% CI 0.47–1.07, p < 0.05" with low heterogeneity (I2 = 20.5%).

> "The pooled results based on the fixed effects model showed that most students have a greater interest in learning via VR methods, rather than conventional or 2D teaching methods (SMD = 0.77; 95% CI 0.47–1.07, p < 0.05; I2 = 20.5%)"
