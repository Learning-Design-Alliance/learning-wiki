## Evidence

### Aliakbari 2012

Aliakbari, M. & Bazyar, A. (2012). Exploring the Impact of Parallel Teaching on General Language Proficiency of EFL Learners. Journal of Pan-Pacific Association of Applied Linguistics, 16(1), 55-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979922

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative open-ended questionnaire administered to the experimental-group students after the null post-test result. Respondents listed reasons including that "it was distracting to the learners", that the noisy atmosphere caused loss of attention, and that they felt shy with co-teachers present.

> "Since the co-instructors did their job simultaneously in the same setting, it was distracting to the learners."
