Interaction analyses in the randomized experiment testing whether conventional academic markers moderated the treatment effect; pre-intervention exam scores showed no significant interaction with treatment status (p= 0.2). The article calls this null result notable, challenging the assumption that such interventions magnify prior-knowledge gaps.
