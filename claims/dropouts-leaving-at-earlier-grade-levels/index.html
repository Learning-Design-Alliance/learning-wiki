Analysis of highest grade attained among dropouts, shown in Figure 2 for dropouts by age 16 and by age 19 across cohorts. The printed percentages are descriptive; no test statistic is reported.

"Fifty-four percent of the students that dropped out by age 16 in the 1991 cohort did not go beyond ninth grade. This increased with subsequent cohorts of students so that 81 percent of the 16-year-old dropouts in the 1997 cohort had not gone beyond ninth grade."
