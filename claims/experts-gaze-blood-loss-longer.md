## Evidence

### Desvergez A 2019

Desvergez A, Winer A, Gouyon J-B, Descoins M. (2019). An observational study using eye tracking to assess resident and senior anesthetists’ situation awareness and visual perception in postpartum hemorrhage high fidelity simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0221515

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-hoc analysis of the Group × ROI interaction on mean fixation duration (F (16.2601) = 2.18, p <0.05) in the eye-tracking video phase; the blood-loss ROI was the only region experts gazed significantly longer. The authors call bleeding evaluation the center of experts' cognitive strategy for monitoring PPH dynamics.

> "Experts gazed significantly longer blood loss ROI than Novices (865 ms ± 439 vs. 717 ms ± 362, p <0.001)."
