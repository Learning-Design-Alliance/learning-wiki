q2 · i? · associational · r?

Descriptive survey of six exploratory ethics questions answered by the 600 evaluators (Figure 4B); e.g., 52.7% disagreed that GenAI use is unethical, 26.5% versus 58.7% accepted AI for an initial idea, and majorities supported compensating source creators (32.2% versus 51.7%) and disclosing AI content alongside the story.

"they overwhelmingly tended to disagree with the idea that AI could be used for a story without acknowledgement of its use (70.2% versus 20.2%)."
