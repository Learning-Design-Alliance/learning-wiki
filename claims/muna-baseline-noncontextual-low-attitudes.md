Preliminary field study at the two target schools: observation and interviews found material "tends to be non -contextual", abstract textbook content limited to the cognitive domain, students less interested and sleepy, and very low social attitudes (mocking, insulting, fighting, disrespect).

> "The results of the field study showed that the social studies learning process in  elementary schools' number 6 Katobu and elementary school number 3 Bataliworu Muna Discrit tends to be non -contextual."
