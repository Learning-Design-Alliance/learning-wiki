## Evidence

### Segura-Fornieles 2026

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest study (N = 156); procedural performance assessed with a checklist based on manufacturer recommendations. Table 1 reports all items improved significantly (p < 0.001), e.g., XABCDE before placement rose from 34% to 100%.

> "When comparing the pre- and post-intervention measurements, statistically significant differences were found across all parameters (Table 1)."
