### Lee 2017

Internal validation through model usability tests with the instructional design team; the team identified that course-level and lesson-level design tasks differ and should be separated in the model.

> "The first related to course level design and lesson level design; these were seen as needing to be divided since the instructional designers usually do different tasks for each level of design."
