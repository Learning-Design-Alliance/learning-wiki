## Evidence

### Rémeau Margot 2026

Rémeau Margot, Raffy Gaël, Borst Grégoire. (2026). Academic Resilience in Contexts of Inequality: Motivational and Self‐Efficacy Profiles of Disadvantaged High Achievers. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.70190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bayesian ANCOVA across nine learner profiles in the French panel found decisive evidence for self‐efficacy differences (model‐averaged R² = 0.064). The article reports that "low‐SES high achievers exhibited markedly higher self‐efficacy than their low‐SES peers with low academic achievement" and that "no meaningful differences emerged between low‐SES high achievers and high‐achieving students from middle‐ or high‐SES backgrounds."

> "low‐SES high achievers exhibited markedly higher self‐efficacy than their low‐SES peers with low academic achievement (BF10 ≈ 9.8 × 1013), providing decisive evidence for differences within the same level of socioeconomic disadvantage. In contrast, no meaningful differences emerged between low‐SES high achievers and high‐achieving students from middle‐ or high‐SES backgrounds (BF10s < 1), indicating comparable levels of self‐efficacy across high‐achieving profiles regardless of SES."
