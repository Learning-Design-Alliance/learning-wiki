`q3 i2` Mean Vte increased from 3.54±1.96 to 4.38±1.72 mL/kg when feedback was visible in the hidden-first group with the term manikin (mean difference 1.86 mL, BCa 95% CI 0.35 to 3.29; p=0.014; Cohen's d=0.54). [→ alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane 2026](#alrashed-latifah-bodanese-eliane-2026)

## Evidence

### alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane 2026

alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane. (2026). Impact of real-time respiratory function monitoring on neonatal mask ventilation training: a multicentre simulation-based crossover study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004894

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test with BCa bootstrap estimates comparing hidden versus visible conditions in the hidden-first group during 30-s ventilation periods with the term manikin. The article reports "a moderate effect size (Cohen's d=0.54)" with mean difference 1.86 mL, p=0.014.

> "In Group 1, mean Vte increased when feedback was visible (3.54±1.96 mL/kg vs 4.38±1.72 mL/kg). This difference was significant (mean difference 1.86 mL, BCa 95% CI 0.35 to 3.29; p=0.014), with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d=0.54)."
