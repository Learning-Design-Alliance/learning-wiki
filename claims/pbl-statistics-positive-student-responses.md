## Evidence

### Tatag Yuli Eko SISWONO 2018

Tatag Yuli Eko SISWONO, Sugi HARTONO, Ahmad Wachidul KOHAR. (2018). Effectiveness of Project Based Learning in Statistics for Lower Secondary Schools. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 75, 197-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181463

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire data collected after learning in both classes. The article reports 78.02% positive responses in PBL versus 55% in conventional learning; the discussion separately reports 85.83% of students agreed with all questionnaire statements. No inferential test is reported.

> "The result of a questionnaire for student responses to the learning shows that the number of students who selected response items in the positive category is 78.02%. Thus, the student responses are positive. Meanwhile, in conventional learning  the student responses are positive but much lower, with only 55%  selecting positive responses."
