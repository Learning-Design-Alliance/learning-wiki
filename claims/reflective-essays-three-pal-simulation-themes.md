## Evidence

### Chiu Po-Wei 2025

Chiu Po-Wei, Chu Shao-Chung, Yang Chia-Han, Lee Huan-Fang, Hung Hsuan-Man, Hsu Hsiang-Chin. (2025). Peer-assisted learning in critical care: a simulation-based approach for postgraduate medical training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2497333

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of reflective essays from all 29 participants using NVivo 14, with a triangulation method across three authors to enhance credibility. Qualitative findings; no effect sizes.

> "The findings were divided into major themes: Enhancement of Clinical Performance, Value of Simulation Training, and Value of Flipped Simulation (Table 2). Overall, participants reported increased clinical confidence, a deepened understanding of emergency care teamwork, and an appreciation for the active learning process involved in scenario creation."
