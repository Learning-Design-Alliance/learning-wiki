# Team-Based Learning in an IPLS course produces a small positive overall self-efficacy shift for she-identifying students

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` She-identifying students in a TBL-taught IPLS course showed a small but statistically significant overall self-efficacy gain (Cohen's d = 0.322, p = 1.2 × 10^-3) from beginning to end of semester. [→ Brokk Toggerson 2020](#brokk-toggerson-2020)

## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre/post SOSESC-P survey data from 131 students (91 she-identifying) in three TBL sections of first-semester IPLS at UMass Amherst. The article reports "a small positive shift for both the class as a whole and for 'she'-identifying individuals" with Table III printing Cohen's d = 0.322 and p = 1.2 × 10^-3 for she-identifying students.

> "The Cohen's d of approximately 0.2 indicates a small positive shift for both the class as a whole and for 'she'-identifying individuals."
