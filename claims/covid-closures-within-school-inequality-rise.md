# Inequality within schools increased after the 2020 school closures, with the Gini coefficient rising by 17% for mathematics and 20% for Dutch

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within-school inequality rose significantly on all three measures (Gini, 90/10 ratio, generalised entropy) in both mathematics and Dutch. [→ Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020](#joana-elisa-maldonado-and-kristof-de-witte-2020)

## Evidence

### Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inequality estimation on individual-level test data aggregated to compare cohorts, using school-level regressions with full controls; the 90/10 ratio increased by 0.23 (mathematics) and 0.22 (Dutch) and entropy by 0.01 in both subjects, all significant at the 1% level. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "the Gini coefﬁcient increases due to the COVID-19 crisis by 0.02 in both mathematics and Dutch, signiﬁcant at the 1%-level. This corresponds to an increase by 17% and 20% for mathematics and Dutch, respectively."
