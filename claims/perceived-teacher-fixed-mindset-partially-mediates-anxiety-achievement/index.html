Evidence¶

Ramirez Gerardo 2018¶

Ramirez Gerardo, Hooper Sophia Yang, Kersting Nicole B., Ferguson Ronald, Yeager David. (2018). Teacher Math Anxiety Relates to Adolescent Students' Math Achievement. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418756052

q3 · i? · associational · r2

SEM mediation analysis with covariates in the same National Mindset Study sample; model fit was acceptable (CFI = .99, RMSEA = .02). The article reports the indirect effect "was significant, b = –.02, p = .03" and characterizes the mediation as partial. No standardized effect size is printed.

"More interestingly, the indirect effect of teacher math anxiety on ninth-grade math achievement through perceived teacher fixed mindset was significant, b = –.02, p = .03, 95% CI [–.03, –.002]. This suggests that the relationship between teacher math anxiety and student achievement is partially mediated via students' perceptions of their teacher's fixed mindset."

Related Claims¶

  • Higher teacher math anxiety relates to fewer process-oriented teaching practices as reported by students, which partially explains students' perception of the teacher's fixed mindset — related
  • Higher teacher math anxiety is associated with lower ninth-grade math GPA, controlling for prior achievement and student mindset — related
  • Teachers' usable mathematical teaching knowledge does not explain the relationship between teacher math anxiety and students' perceptions of teacher fixed mindset — related
