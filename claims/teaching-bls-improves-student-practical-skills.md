## Evidence

### Veloso Sérgio Geraldo 2019

Veloso Sérgio Geraldo, Pereira Gabriel Santos, Vasconcelos Nathália Nascimento, Senger Maria Helena, de Faria Rosa Malena Delbone. (2019). Learning by teaching basic life support: a non-randomized controlled trial with medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1500-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired pre/post analysis (Table 2) in a non-randomized controlled trial: case group (n = 50) reassessed 60–75 days after first practical assessment after teaching; control group (n = 36) reassessed 50–70 days after without teaching. The case group's "practical students’ performance" improved significantly while the control's did not (14.4 ± 1.6 to 14.4 ± 1.4, p = 0.877); the difference between the groups' gains was p < 0.001.

> "The practical students’ performance of the case group went from 13.3 ± 2.1 to 15.3 ± 1.2 (maximum = 17, p < 0.001) and theoretical from 10.1 ± 3.0 to 16.4 ± 1.7"
