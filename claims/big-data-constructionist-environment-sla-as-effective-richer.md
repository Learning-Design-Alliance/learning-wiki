## Evidence

### Geovani Lopes Dias; Renato P. dos Santos 2017

Geovani Lopes Dias; Renato P. dos Santos. (2017). Big Data as a technology-to-think-with for Scientific Literacy. Acta Scientiae, v.19, n.2, p.350-372. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.04534](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.04534)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' overall conclusion from a post-test-only experiment with two paired first-grade high-school physics classes in Novo Hamburgo, Brazil, combining t-tests on SLA scores with qualitative content analysis of student reports. The article states the Big Data environment was "statistically as effective as the control environment under the SLA framework" while being richer.

> "a constructionist environment based on Big Data analysis to develop scientific literacy showed a richer strategy for that development, compared to a free schoolyard exploration, and was statistically as effective as the control environment under the SLA framework."
