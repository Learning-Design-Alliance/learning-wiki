Evidence¶

Peters 1916¶

Peters, C. C. (1916). The Influence of Speed Drills Upon the Rate and the Effectiveness of Silent Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073150

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The author's interpretation (observation 3) of the departure from trend in the third test, in which eight cases of relative loss in reading speed appeared against four of gain, and seven cases of simultaneous loss of speed and quality.

"It is true that this exhortation was given to both groups but, in as much as it was new to the no-drill group and an everyday occurrence to the drill group, it may have acted as a more effective spur to the former than to the latter, and thus unbalanced the conditions."
