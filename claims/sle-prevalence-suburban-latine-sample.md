`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Frequency analyses of the Stressful Urban Life Events Scale responses in the sample of 293 adolescents. By domain, "school‐related challenges were reported by 62.1% of adolescents, family structure‐altering transitions affected 66.9%, familial loss was experienced by 74.3%, and violence was witnessed by 61.4%."

> "a substantial majority of the participants (95.2%) experienced at least one significant life stressor. Further analyses indicated that 89.1% encountered at least two events, while 75.4% experienced three or more SLEs."
