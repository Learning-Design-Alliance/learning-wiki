`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression in the cross-sectional sample found medical specialty enrolment significantly predicted higher GPA (B = 0.179, p = 0.001); the printed effect size for the bivariate specialty association with binary GPA is Cramér's V = 0.211, described as a moderate effect size.

> "enrolment in a medical specialty (B = 0.179, p = 0.001) and adherence to the recommended level of physical activity (B = 0.137, p = 0.013) were found to significantly predict higher GPAs"
