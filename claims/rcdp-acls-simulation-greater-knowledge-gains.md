## Evidence

### Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi 2025

Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi. (2025). Rapid cycle deliberate practice simulation with standardized prebriefing and video based formative feedback in advanced cardiac life support. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97222-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing RCDP ACLS simulation to traditional simulation in nursing students. Experimental knowledge rose from 9.63 ± 2.61 to 14.77 ± 1.36; the experimental group's "scores increased significantly more than those of the control group". No effect size printed.

> "The program effectiveness test indicated that the experimental group's scores increased significantly more than those of the control group (t = 6.83, p < 0.001), thus supporting Hypothesis 2"
