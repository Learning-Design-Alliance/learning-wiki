# Number of cell-cycle starts and cell-cycle destroys in Progenitor X were negatively correlated with pre-post learning gains, while total cycle starts across the game were not

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Cell-cycle starts (r = -.2965) and cell-cycle destroys (r = -.3390) correlated negatively with pre-post learning gains; total cycle starts of all types showed no significant correlation. [→ Richard Halverson 2012](#richard-halverson-2012)

## Evidence

### Richard Halverson 2012

Richard Halverson, Elizabeth Owen, Nathan Wills, R. Benjamin Shapiro. (2012). Game-Based Assessment: An integrated model for capturing evidence of learning in play. ERIA Working Paper. http://www.eriainteractive.com/index.php

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cycle-specific analysis of play data from 39 students (Table 2, α = .10): # of cell cycle starts showed a significant negative correlation with pre-post gains (r = -.2965) and # of cell cycle destroys a significant negative correlation (r = -.3390); total starts showed no significant correlation.

> "The first indicator was the number of times a player started a cell cycle; the second was the number of time the cell cycle was destroyed (i.e. the player failed to collect the appropriate number and kind of cells)."
