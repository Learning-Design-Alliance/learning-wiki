## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the end of the synchronous practical phase, all responding participants reported confidence in their ability to perform virtual consultation sessions with patients. [→ Liew Siaw Cheok 2023](#liew-siaw-cheok-2023)

## Evidence

### Liew Siaw Cheok 2023

Liew Siaw Cheok, Tan Maw Pin, Breen Emer, Krishnan Kuhan, Sivarajah Inthirani, Raviendran Nivashinie, Aung Thidar, Nimir Amal, Pallath Vinod. (2023). Microlearning and online simulation-based virtual consultation training module for the undergraduate medical curriculum – a preliminary evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04777-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kirkpatrick Level 1 feedback after week 5, from 48 of 52 students (92.3% response rate). The authors attribute the confidence boost to experiential simulation, "immediate feedback from peers, facilitators, and SPs", and deliberate one-on-one practice.

> "The feedback sought from participants at the end of week 5, after the completion of the practical synchronous phase, revealed that all respondents, 48 (100%) (response rate 48/52, 92.3%), were confident in their ability to perform VC sessions with patients."
