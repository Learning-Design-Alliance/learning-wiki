### Hand et al. 2013

Brian Hand, William Therrien, Mack Shelley. (2013). Examining the impact of using the Science Writing Heuristic approach in learning science: A cluster randomized study. SREE Fall 2013 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563211

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretive conclusion (type e) from the Conclusions section, not an independently tested result: they argue the disappearance of the Mathematics and Reading disadvantage reflects "transfer of benefits gained by students from their SWH-based exposure to critical thinking."

> "This finding provides useful confirmation of the transfer of benefits gained by students from their SWH-based exposure to critical thinking, as the Mathematics and Reading components of Iowa Tests of Basic Skills/Iowa Assessments more closely track with the expectations of critical thinking than is true of the Science component"
