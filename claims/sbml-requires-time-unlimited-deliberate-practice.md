`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observation from the SBML sessions: two subjects, pre-clinical students early in second year with very limited clinical exposure, failed mastery; authors state sessions "were limited to two hours only given institutional constraints."

> "while 151/153 students completed the entire SBML, two subjects required greater than four post-testing attempts and were still unable to reach mastery due to unanticipated time constraints for additional deliberate practice."
