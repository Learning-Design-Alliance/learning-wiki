# Middle episodes of wiki text generation show a cumulative orientation, later with co-construction, in which students expand and re-elaborate each other's ideas

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In episode three, students shared new ideas with reasons, discussed arguments in the text without deleting others' ideas, and gave explicit reasons for changes, indicating cumulative orientation with co-construction. [→ Pifarré 2011](#pifarre-2011)

## Evidence

### Pifarré 2011

Pifarré, M. & Kleine Staarman, J. (2011). Wiki-supported collaborative learning in primary education: How a dialogic space is created for thinking together. International Journal of Computer Supported Collaborative Learning, 6(2), 187-205. https://www.springer.com/journal/11412

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of contributions in negotiation and group spaces during the third episode. The authors report students expressed disagreement respectfully in the negotiation space and waited for the authoring pair to change, correct or remove ideas.

> "Although students did not delete ideas from the collaborative text, they did contribute critically, identified inconsistencies in others' arguments and proposed alternatives."
