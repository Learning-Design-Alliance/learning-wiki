Minju Park 2026¶

Minju Park, Ivan Orozco Vasquez, and Cristina Conati. (2026). Characterizing Students' LLM Usage Behaviors and Their Association with Learning in Critical Thinking Tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04534 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.04534

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational analysis of mandatory LLM-use reports from two offerings of a research-oriented course totaling 68 students, who were free to use LLMs without penalty if they disclosed use. The study reports that "23 out of 68 students (34%)" used LLMs at least once, averaging 12% and 11% of the two task types.

"23 out of 68 students (34%) reported using LLMs in at least one submission. On average, students used LLMs in 12% of discussion point submissions and 11% of critical summary submissions."
