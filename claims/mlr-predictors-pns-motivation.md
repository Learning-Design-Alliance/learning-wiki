## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multiple linear regression, gender, prior experience with online learning, and GPA jointly predicted autonomy, competence, relatedness, and amotivation, while only experience and GPA predicted intrinsic motivation. [→ Frikha Mohamed et al 2024](#frikha-mohamed-et-al-2024)

## Evidence

### Frikha Mohamed et al 2024

Frikha Mohamed et al. (2024). Towards improving online learning in physical education: Gender differences and determinants of motivation, psychological needs satisfaction, and academic achievement in Saudi students. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297822

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regressions on the 308-student sample with gender, experience, and GPA as categorical predictors. Ranged R2 values: autonomy 0.442, competence 0.470, relatedness 0.384, intrinsic motivation 0.397, amotivation 0.447; extrinsic motivation was "weakly affected" (R2 = 0.088) by gender and experience only.

> "The MLR showed that gender, prior experience with online learning and GPA are the common predictors affecting positively autonomy (R2 = 0.442; β = 0.887, p < 0.001; β = 2.392, p < 0.001, and β = 1.425, p < 0.001, respectively)"
