Descriptive analysis of SES covariate means across profiles in eight samples (Fig. 4). The overall range of z-scores of SES across profiles was -0.25 to 0.45, indicating 'a rather substantial discriminating value of the profiles regarding SES.'

> "Overall, our results point to a positive relation between the level of sophistication of epistemic belief profiles and SES."
