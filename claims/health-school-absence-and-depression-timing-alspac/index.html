Evidence¶

Hughes 2021¶

Hughes, A., Wade, K. H., Dickson, M., Rice, F., Davies, A., Davies, N. M. & Howe, L. D. (2021). Common health conditions in childhood and adolescence, school absence, and educational attainment: Mendelian randomization study. npj Science of Learning 6:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00080-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Phenotypic regression analysis in ALSPAC comparing depressive-symptom timing. The article states depressive symptoms at 10 showed a "considerably stronger association with GCSEs than depressive symptoms at 13", with the printed coefficients above; all health conditions were also associated with school absence (Table 2).

"Depressive symptoms at 10 showed a considerably stronger association with GCSEs than depressive symptoms at 13 (GCSE points scores: −16.4 (95% CI: −19.7, −13.1) compared to −6.8 (95% CI: −9.9, −3.8) per SD MFQ score)."
