### de la Fuente Jesús 2020

de la Fuente Jesús, Paoloni Paola, Kauffman Douglas, Yilmaz Soylu Meryem, Sander Paul, Zapata Lucía. (2020). Big Five, Self-Regulation, and Coping Strategies as Predictors of Achievement Emotions in Undergraduate Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17103602

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 1, n = 642) of Big Five factors against the Short Self-Regulation Questionnaire. The article reports neuroticism's "negative relation to decision making (r = −0.365; p < 0.001) and learning from mistakes (r = −0.302; p < 0.001)".

> "the personality factor neuroticism (N) had a significant negative associative relationship with self-regulation (SR), both globally and in its factors; especially important was its negative relation to decision making (r = −0.365; p < 0.001)"
