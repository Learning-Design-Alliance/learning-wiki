Group comparison in the survey of 109 academics found non-users reported significantly higher guilt for research paper writing than any-usage respondents; the anticipated-remorse item also differentiated non-users (M = 3.00) from users (M = 1.94), t(7.79) = 2.44, p = .041.

> "For research paper writing, those who never use AI reported significantly higher guilt (M = 2.77, SD = 0.92) than those reporting any usage (M = 2.19, SD = 0.99), t(65.9) = 2.92, p =.005."
