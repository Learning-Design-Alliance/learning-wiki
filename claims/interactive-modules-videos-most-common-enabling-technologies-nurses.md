`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review of 60 articles on asynchronous e-learning in clinical nursing continuing education, classifying technologies per the Ngenzi et al. TEET framework. The review reports "interactive online modules (25 articles)" and videos (25 articles) as the most common enabling technologies, followed by slides or PowerPoint (11) and online discussions (7).

> "The most commonly used technologies among enabling technologies were interactive online modules (25 articles) [11, 21–24, 28, 29, 31, 33, 35, 37, 42, 43, 45, 48–50, 53, 54, 56, 57, 62, 65, 66, 72] and videos (25 articles)"
