Martin Erik Horn, Antje Leisner, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2004). Difficulties of Model Construction in Optics. German Physical Society Spring Conference Leipzig 2002. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative result from the six-student case study's answer to research question 4; the authors state that "what is model and what reality is not confused as often, since the model and its characteristics are applied purposely".

> "As a consequence of meta-conceptual phases in teaching, in which the formation, application and relationship to other models of light were covered, light in its complex physical nature was becoming more comprehensible to the tested persons."
