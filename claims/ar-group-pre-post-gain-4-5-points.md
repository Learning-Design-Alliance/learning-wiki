## Evidence

### Purificacion Toledo-Morales & Jose Manuel Sanchez-Garcia 2018

Purificacion Toledo-Morales & Jose Manuel Sanchez-Garcia. (2018). Use of Augmented Reality in Social Sciences as Educational Resource. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 19(3), Article 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1183349

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparison of the experimental group (5th A, 25 students) using t-test analysis in SPSS 23.0. The article reports the gain descriptively and notes grade distribution: "36% of students obtained qualification of outstanding and remarkable notable, 8% of good and 20% of approved. There was no suspense." No effect size is printed.

> "students score higher after the use of the technology of AR in the teaching and learning process, because the average obtained with the pre-test is 3.4 and the post-test 7.9, with a difference of 4.5 points more"
