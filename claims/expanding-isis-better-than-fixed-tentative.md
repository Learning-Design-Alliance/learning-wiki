## Evidence

### Cepeda 2006

Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006). Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A Review and Quantitative Synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354-380. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic comparison of expanding versus fixed ISI schedules, 22 accuracy comparisons and 8 effect sizes, analyzed with independent samples t tests. The article notes large standard errors indicative of large between-study variability.

> "Overall, expanding ISIs led to better performance than fixed intervals (see Table 8). Fifteen out of 18 studies used a paired associate learning task, and we did not detect any systematic differences related to type of task"
