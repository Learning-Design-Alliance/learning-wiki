## Evidence

### Ajmi Soffien 2024

Ajmi Soffien, Kurz Martin, Lindner Thomas Werner, Dalen Ingvild, Ersdal Hege Langli. (2024). Does clinical experience influence the effects of team simulation training in stroke thrombolysis? A prospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-086413

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second mixed effects linear regression on all 532 patients (36 registrars), allowing interaction between experience and simulation attendance. Adjusted effects at the first and third experience quartiles were ΔDNT −26.3% (p<0.001) and −16.0% (p=0.025).

> "simulation attendance was on average associated with a statistically significant 21.8% decrease in DNT (adjusted, ΔDNT −21.8%; 95% CI, −30.9% to −11.6%; p<0.001)."
