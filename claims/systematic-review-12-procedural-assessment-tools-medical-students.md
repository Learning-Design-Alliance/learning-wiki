`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Systematic review conducted within the PRISMA framework, searching eight bibliographic databases with two independent reviewers. The review reports that of 659 abstracted articles, "Only 11 specifically assessed medical students", yielding 12 heterogeneous tools for analysis.

> "Of 659 abstracted articles 56 identified procedural assessment tools. Only 11 specifically assessed medical students. The final 11 studies consisted of 1 randomised controlled trial, 4 comparative and 6 descriptive studies yielding 12 heterogeneous procedural assessment tools for analysis."
