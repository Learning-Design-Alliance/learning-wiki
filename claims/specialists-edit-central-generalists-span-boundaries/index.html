Evidence¶

Peter Holtz & Joachim Kimmerle & Ulrike Cress 2018¶

Peter Holtz & Joachim Kimmerle & Ulrike Cress. (2018). Using big data techniques for measuring productive friction in mass collaboration online environments. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9285-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this from further SNA analysis (Halatchliyski et al. 2014) of contributors' previous edits in the psychology and education domains; both article types were authored by more experienced users. No effect size is printed.

"In comparison, articles with high betweenness scores, which spanned the two domains, were primarily authored by generalists who regularly contributed to both domains."
