## Evidence

### Bowman K 2023

Bowman K, Cadman T, Goncalves Soares A, Robinson O, Hughes A, Heron J, Segal AB, Huerta MC, Howe LD. (2023). Mediators of the association between childhood body mass index and educational attainment: Analysis of a UK prospective cohort study. Pediatric Obesity. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpo.13014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sequential causal mediation (models 5–8) in the ALSPAC imputed sample, ordering bullying, externalizing behaviours, depressive symptoms and school enjoyment by a proposed causal ordering. The article reports "the overall proportion mediated was 41.9% in females and 23.3% in males".

> "the overall proportion mediated was 41.9% in females and 23.3% in males. Most of this effect was driven by externalizing behaviours. In males, the confidence interval for the NIE was wide and included the null value."
