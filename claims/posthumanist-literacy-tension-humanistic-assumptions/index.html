Zhaozhe Wang 2025¶

Zhaozhe Wang, Chaoran Wang. (2025). A posthumanist approach to AI literacy. Computers and Composition 76 (2025) 102933. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compcom.2025.102933

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case study of two multilingual undergraduates (Zhimo and Asuka) in writing-intensive courses, drawing on drafts, AI logs, written reflections, and 1.5–2 hour interviews, analyzed thematically through a critical AI literacy model and posthuman theory. The authors state the study "reveals a productive tension between these students' experiments with posthumanist literacy and their entrenched humanistic assumptions."
