Evidence¶

Lee 2013¶

Lee, J., Shin, H. & Amo, L. C. (2013). Evaluating the impact of NCLB school interventions in New York state: Does one size fit all? Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (67). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1122

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Comparison of unmatched group analysis (Figure 1) with IPTW-matched analysis (Tables 3-4, Figure 2) of New York State school PI gains. The unmatched analysis showed, for example, Grade 4 ELA mean PI gain of 5.56 for treatment schools versus 1.49 for schools in good standing, but "the larger PI gains observed in the treatment group mostly get smaller or disappear after matching."

"Generally, results from the IPTW-matched analysis demonstrated a null or mixed effect of treatment on achievement outcomes. In other words, the larger PI gains observed in the treatment group mostly get smaller or disappear after matching such that true effects of school interventions on ELA and math achievement outcomes are questionable."
