Evidence¶

Pablo-César Muñoz-Carril 2021¶

Pablo-César Muñoz-Carril, Nuria Hernández-Sellés, Eduardo-José Fuentes-Abeledo, Mercedes González-Sanmamed. (2021). Factors influencing students' perceived impact of learning and satisfaction in Computer Supported Collaborative Learning. Computers & Education, 174. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104310

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

PLS structural model result in the 701-student study; the article states this confirms students found CSCL valuable, with printed β = 0.203, 0.212, and 0.423, consistent with Cheung and Vogel (2013).

"perceived usefulness had a significant positive effect on satisfaction (H1a; β = 0.203; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 0.103), perceived impact on learning (H1b; β = 0.212; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 0.088), and attitude (H1c; β = 0.423; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 0.163)"
