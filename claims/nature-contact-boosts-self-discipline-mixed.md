## Evidence

### Kuo M 2019

Kuo M, Barnes M and Jordan C (2019) Do Experiences With Nature Promote Learning? Converging Evidence of a Cause-and-Effect Relationship. Front. Psychol. 10:305. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00305

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of studies tying nature contact to self-discipline notes that "impulse control eﬀects are not always statistically signiﬁcant" in some studies, while concluding that in general impulse control is better during or after children's contact with nature. Attributed studies include Amoly et al. (2014) and Schutte et al. (2015).

> "Note that impulse control eﬀects are not always statistically signiﬁcant (e.g., Amoly et al., 2014; Schutte et al., 2015). Nonetheless, in general, impulse control is better during or after children's contact with nature."
