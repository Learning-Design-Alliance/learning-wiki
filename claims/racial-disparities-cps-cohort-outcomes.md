### Sattler 2025

Sattler, Kierra; Font, Sarah; Panlilio, Carlomagno. (2025). Foster care entry and later academic achievement among infants involved with child protective services. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted logistic regression (Table 3) in the full sample of 8,795 children. Descriptively, 68% of White versus 34% of Black children demonstrated basic-or-above reading, and 4% versus 18% experienced punitive discipline. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Black children were less likely than White children to test at basic or above in reading (OR = .40, p < .001) and math (OR = .40, p < .001) and more likely to be experience punitive discipline (OR = 2.50, p < .001)."
