## Evidence

### Mohammad Hadra 2026

Mohammad Hadra, Karleen Cambridge and Mostefa Mesbah. (2026). Evaluating the accuracy and reliability of AI content detectors in academic contexts. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00213-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of two commercial detectors on a balanced 192-text dataset (48 texts each of EFL student, professional, AI, and hybrid authorship), with outputs mapped to Human/Hybrid/AI via established thresholds. The results section reports "Originality achieved a higher macro-average accuracy (0.69) than Turnitin (0.61)" with macro-averaging used due to moderate class imbalance.

> "Originality achieved a higher macro-average accuracy (0.69) than Turnitin (0.61), as well as a higher macro-average recall (0.60 vs. 0.51). Despite this, both detectors produced macro-average F1-scores below 0.55, indicating limited consistency across all classes."
