### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative analysis of 60 ChatGPT-completed TUG-K surveys compared to the printed student sample of Zavala et al. The chatbot's mean was "10.85 points (41.7%)" versus the students' 12.25 (47%); no effect size or significance test is reported for the comparison.

> "The average score of the sample of 60 ChatGPT-solved surveys is 10.85 points (41.7%), with the median score being 11 points. This is similar to the average (12.25, or 47%) and median (12) scores reported by Zavala et al."
