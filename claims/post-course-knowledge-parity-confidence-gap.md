# At post-course evaluation, lecture-based and PBL knowledge scores did not differ significantly, but lecture-based confidence scores were significantly higher than problem-based confidence scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Post-course knowledge scores showed no statistically significant difference between problem-based and lecture-based items (p = 0.14). [→ Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018](#al-madi-ebtissam-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired 't' test comparing post-course knowledge scores of the two item types in the same 33-student cohort; equivalence was not tested beyond the non-significant test, and no effect size is printed.

> "No statistically significant difference was noted in the post-course evaluation knowledge scores of both the problem based and lecture based items (p = 0.14)"

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018 (2)

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired 't' test of post-course confidence scores for the two item types in the same cohort, with "a higher mean score for lecture based items"; no effect size is printed.

> "a statistically significant improvement was noted in the confidence scores of the post-course evaluation with a higher mean score for lecture based items in comparison to problem based items. (p = 0.002)"
