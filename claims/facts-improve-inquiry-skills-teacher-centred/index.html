Evidence¶

Mária Ganajová 2021¶

Mária Ganajová, Ivana Sotáková, Stanislav Lukáč, Zuzana Ješková, Veronika Jurková, Renáta Orosová. (2021). Formative Assessment as a Tool to Enhance the Development of Inquiry Skills in Science Education. Journal of Baltic Science Education, Vol. 20, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.33225/jbse/21.20.204

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test two-group study in three Slovak upper-secondary schools; experimental group n=73 taught with FACTs, control group n=48 taught without them, same teachers, content and duration. Post-test comparison was significant.

"However, the comparison of the post-test results showed a statistically significant difference between the experimental and the control group (p=.001, therefore p<α)."

Mária Ganajová 2021 (2)¶

Mária Ganajová, Ivana Sotáková, Stanislav Lukáč, Zuzana Ješková, Veronika Jurková, Renáta Orosová. (2021). Formative Assessment as a Tool to Enhance the Development of Inquiry Skills in Science Education. Journal of Baltic Science Education, Vol. 20, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.33225/jbse/21.20.204

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-sample t-test comparing pre-test and post-test within each group; experimental group mean difference .054 (p=.004), control group .011 (p=.613), confirming hypothesis H2.

"The paired t-test value in the experimental group (Pair E) was p=.004, therefore p<α. In the experimental group, a statistically significant difference in the inquiry skill development, was confirmed."
