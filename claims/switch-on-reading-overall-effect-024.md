## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The intervention raised post-test reading scores by +0.24 standard deviations of the gain score compared with the waiting-list control. [→ Gorard et al. 2014](#gorard-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Gorard et al. 2014

Stephen Gorard, Beng Huat See, Nadia Siddiqui. (2014). Switch-on Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Individually randomised waiting-list trial in 19 schools; 155 treatment and 153 control pupils analysed. Treatment gain was 4.40 versus 2.59 control on NGRT scores; the printed effect size was +0.24, described as "a small noticeable positive impact".

> "The overall effect size of the intervention was +0.24 standard deviations of the overall gain score, meaning that it has shown a small noticeable positive impact"
