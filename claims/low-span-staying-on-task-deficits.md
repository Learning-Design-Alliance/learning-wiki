# Low working memory span individuals are more affected by Stroop task lapses and report more task-related mind-wandering

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Individuals with low operation span performance are more affected by Stroop conditions when most trials match, and low-span adults report more mind-wandering on tasks they want to attend to. [→ Cowan Nelson 2014](#cowan-nelson-2014)

## Evidence

### Cowan Nelson 2014

Cowan Nelson. (2014). Working Memory Underpins Cognitive Development, Learning, and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4207727/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Kane and Engle's Stroop experiment, in which mostly matching trials induced lapses from the goal of naming ink color, and "the individuals who are more affected by the Stroop conditions are those with relatively low performance on the operation span test of working memory." Kane et al. (2007) experience-sampling found low-span adults reported more mind-wandering on tasks they wanted to attend to.
