## Evidence

### Tang Diana Hai Yen 2024

Tang Diana Hai Yen, Østdal Theresa Bruun, Vamadevan Anishan, Konge Lars, Houlind Kim, Stadeager Morten, Bjerrum Flemming. (2024). No difference between using short and long intervals for distributed proficiency-based laparoscopy simulator training: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10522-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory Mann–Whitney analysis of registered instructor-feedback time during intervention and retention test. The authors report "288 (IQR 76–467)" versus 236 seconds during training with no significant difference; at retention most participants requested none.

> "They used, respectively, 288 (IQR 76–467) and 236 (IQR 12–450) seconds in the intervention and control group (p = 0.52). For the retention test, the median time was 0 (IQR 0–0) in both groups (p = 0.37)."
