### Sissi L. Li 2015

Sissi L. Li, Gabriela Serna, Jocelyn S. Read, Joshua R. Smith, Heather Chilton, Michael E. Loverude. (2015). A collaboration to support novice instructors in research-based astronomy teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Peer-instructor end-of-class summary counts from Fall 2012 and Spring 2013 field notes documenting student contention about not being able to check Lecture-Tutorial answers: "18/ 53 wanted LT answers and 20/ 53 disliked the Lecture-Tutorials."

> "In the Spring 2013 semester, 18/ 53 wanted LT answers and 20/ 53 disliked the Lecture-Tutorials."

### Sissi L. Li 2015 (2)

Sissi L. Li, Gabriela Serna, Jocelyn S. Read, Joshua R. Smith, Heather Chilton, Michael E. Loverude. (2015). A collaboration to support novice instructors in research-based astronomy teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor-reported outcome after modifying responses: beginning the post-tutorial clicker sequence with explicit debriefing language and having the class answer a key tutorial question aloud in unison. The article reports "the instructors have reported that there is less resistance to the tutorials."

> "These changes add almost no time to the lecture, but the instructors have reported that there is less resistance to the tutorials."
