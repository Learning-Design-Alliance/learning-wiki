### Wen MC 2020

Wen MC, Kau K, Huang SS, Huang WH, Tsai LY, Tsai TY, Tsay SL. (2020). Smartphone education improves embarrassment, bowel preparation, and satisfaction with care in patients receiving colonoscopy: a randomized controlled trail. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023102

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression in the RCT using a 10-item author-developed satisfaction scale (Cronbach α = 0.98); group means were 48.7 vs 43.42 (P < .001). The printed β is unstandardized, so no standardized effect size is available.

> "People who received the smartphone education program showed better satisfaction with colonoscopy care (β = 4.60, P < .001). The final model explained 25% of the variation in satisfaction of colonoscopy care (Adj-R2 = 0.25, P < .05)"
