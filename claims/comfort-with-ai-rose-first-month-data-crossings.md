Analyses emphasize directional trends rather than inferential generalization; no effect size or significance test is reported.

> "Participants who began with low or moderate com- fort showed the largest upward movement, while those entering with higher com- fort levels largely remained stable. Minor downward shifts among the most confi- dent respondents appear consistent with calibration rather than disengagement."
