## Evidence

### Ghasempour Saeed 2023

Ghasempour Saeed, Esmaeeli Maede, Abbasi Ali, Hosseinzadeh Ali, Ebrahimi Hossein. (2023). Relationship between academic success, distance education learning environments, and its related factors among medical sciences students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04856-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Enter method) in the 208-student cross-sectional survey; satisfied students scored 9.797 units higher than dissatisfied students (SE 2.971, t 3.3, P = 0.001). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In addition, students who were more satisfied with their field of study had 9.797 units of higher academic success than students with less satisfaction (P = 0.001)."

### Ghasempour Saeed 2023 (2)

Ghasempour Saeed, Esmaeeli Maede, Abbasi Ali, Hosseinzadeh Ali, Ebrahimi Hossein. (2023). Relationship between academic success, distance education learning environments, and its related factors among medical sciences students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04856-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the three field-satisfaction groups in the same survey; satisfied students (mean 110.98) scored higher than unsure (104.29) and dissatisfied (102.20) students per Table 1 and Tukey post hoc comparisons. No effect size is printed.

> "In addition, the mean score of academic success of students according to their satisfaction with their field of study also had a statistically significant difference (P < 0.001), so that students with high satisfaction compared to students with moderate (P < 0.001) and low satisfaction (P = 0.005) reached a higher level of mean scores in academic success."
