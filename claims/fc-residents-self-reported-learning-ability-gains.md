## Evidence

### Hou Weishu 2025

Hou Weishu, Xue Yangyang, Pan Hongli, Zhao Xuemei, Shu Hongmin, Li Xiaohu, Yu Yongqiang. (2025). Comparison of flipped classroom and lecture-based models in a radiology residency training program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07702-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous questionnaire completed by all 127 residents (100% response rate) after the 12-week training; FC percentages of "79.1%,80.6%, 80.6%, and 81.0%" exceeded LB percentages of "58.4%, 58.4%, 46.7%, and 35.0%" (all P < 0.05). Self-reported, not objectively tested.

> "79.1%,80.6%, 80.6%, and 81.0% agreed that the “ability to communicate”, “ability to analyze and solve problems”, “ability to retrieve information”, and “ability to work in a team” have significant or little improvement respectively. In contrast, only 58.4%, 58.4%, 46.7%, and 35.0% of the LB group residents had positive responses (all P < 0.05)"
