## Evidence

### Chamtouri I 2025

Chamtouri I, Kechida M, Jomaa W, Ben Hamda K. (2025). Medical students’ evaluation by serious game in the era of Covid-19 infection. La Tunisie Médicale. https://doi.org/10.62438/tunismed.v103i2.5012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Case-control study of 64 volunteer medical students randomly divided into an SG group and a clinical case-MCQ group, both on the same STEMI case with 15 minutes allotted. The Results section reports "a total score ≥ 50% versus 68.1%" with p = 0.02; no effect size is printed.

> "Thirtyone learners (96.9%) in-SG group obtained a total score ≥ 50% versus 68.1% in clinical case-MCQ group (p = 0.02)."
