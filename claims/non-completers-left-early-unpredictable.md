## Evidence

### Yates Janet 2011

Yates Janet. (2011). Development of a 'toolkit' to identify medical students at risk of failure to thrive on the course: an exploratory retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-95

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of the 75 non-completers in the retrospective database. A further 21% left at the end of Year 3, some academically able students choosing to move elsewhere, choices the author says are "unlikely to be predictable in any way".

> "Over half (46/75, 61%) left within the first two years, many within the early weeks or months, and sometimes withdrawing before completing their examinations. These students had no academic flags and their files held little or no information on their personal circumstances."
