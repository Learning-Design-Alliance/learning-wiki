`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Allensworth et al. (2014), a Chicago study of middle grade predictors in which "the combination of indicators of grades and attendance provided the best prediction of high school on-track rates." Test scores and grit measures did not further improve prediction. No effect sizes or accuracy percentages are printed for this finding.

> "In a study of middle grade predictors of high school success in Chicago, the combination of indicators of grades and attendance provided the best prediction of high school on-track rates, and additional information—including test scores and measures of student "grit"—did not further improve the prediction and thus are not necessary components of an indicator system."
