## Evidence

### Christian T. Jacobs 2016

Christian T. Jacobs, Gerard J. Gorman, Huw E. Rees, Lorraine Craig. (2016). Experiences with efficient methodologies for teaching computer programming to geoscientists. arXiv. [doi:10.5408/15-101.1](https://doi.org/10.5408/15-101.1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

SOLE survey and exam-mark analysis of the 2012 cohort taught via recorded videos; students "felt unsupported by this approach" and comments showed they did not perceive traditional teaching. Learning outcomes were still better than 2010.

> "a much higher proportion (28%) of ﬁrst year students feeling that the “The structure and delivery of the lectures” was less than satisfactory, a relatively low score of 0.38 for “Structure/delivery” (see Table 7), and a fairly broad distribution of marks with the mean being 60.3%."
