## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The relative fraction of homework questions covering FCI force and motion concepts differed by more than a factor of 2 in favor of the traditional curriculum, consistent with the performance differences. [→ Caballero 2011](#caballero-2011)

## Evidence

### Caballero 2011

Caballero, M. D., Greco, E. F., Murray, E. R., Schatz, M. F., Bujak, K. R., Marr, M. J., Catrambone, R., & Kohlmyer, M. A. (2011). Comparing large lecture mechanics curricula using the Force Concept Inventory: A five thousand student study. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.1055](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.1055)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of 575 traditional and 756 M&I homework questions categorized by FCI force and motion concepts. The traditional curriculum devoted roughly twice the fraction of homework to FCI-related concepts compared to M&I.

> "The relative fraction of homework questions covering FCI force and motion concepts diﬀered by more than a factor of 2 in favor of the traditional curriculum."

### Caballero 2011 (2)

Caballero, M. D., Greco, E. F., Murray, E. R., Schatz, M. F., Bujak, K. R., Marr, M. J., Catrambone, R., & Kohlmyer, M. A. (2011). Comparing large lecture mechanics curricula using the Force Concept Inventory: A five thousand student study. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.1055](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.1055)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of lecture and reading topics using the same categories as the homework analysis. The traditional curriculum's fraction of lecture topics covering FCI force and motion concepts was nearly twice that of M&I.

> "The relative fraction of lectures/readings which cover FCI force and motion concepts was greater by nearly a factor of 2 for the tradit ional curriculum."
