### De Leng Wendy E. 2018

De Leng Wendy E., Stegers-Jager Karen M., Born Marise Ph., Themmen Axel P. N. (2018). Influence of response instructions and response format on applicant perceptions of a situational judgement test for medical school selection. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1390-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVAs on the survey of 372 applicants, controlling for pu-GPA ≥ 7.5 status, tested subgroup main effects on all perception items; none reached significance. Equivalence was not tested. Table 3 reports subgroup means, e.g. process favourability 4.47 (first-generation) vs 4.36 (non-first-generation).

> "No significant main effects were found for gender, ethnic background or first-generation university status on the judgements of process favourability, the procedural justice factors and ease of cheating."
