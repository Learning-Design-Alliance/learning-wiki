Bong 2026¶

Bong, M., Jeong, D., Park, S., Suh, J., Kim, S. G., Park, J. C., & Kim, J. (2026). Cultural Insights into the Motivation Paradox of East Asian Learners. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10140-9

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports cross-cultural comparisons of Canadian and Japanese college students, attributing the pattern to studies such as Ross et al. (2005) and the meta-analysis by Heine and Hamamura (2007). The review attributes this to modesty being 'a highly valued trait that significantly reduces self-enhancement motivation' in collectivistic cultures.

"Cross-cultural studies comparing Canadian and Japanese college students have indeed documented that Euro-Canadians exhibit greater self-enhancement than their Japanese counterparts (e.g., Ross et al., 2005), a pattern supported by Heine and Hamamura's (2007) meta-analysis."
