# Before implementation, the experimental and control groups showed no statistically significant difference in academic achievement pre-test scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One-way ANOVA on PCAAT pre-test scores found no statistically significant difference among the three groups, supporting group equivalence. [→ Akbaş 2019](#akbas-2019)

## Evidence

### Akbaş 2019

Akbaş, Y.; Şahin, İ. F.; Meral, E. (2019). Implementing Argumentation-Based Science Learning Approach in Social Studies: Academic Achievement and Students' Views. Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 9(1), 209-245. https://doi.org/10.33403/rigeo.529139

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test equivalence check on the same 94-student quasi-experiment. Pre-test means were EG = 66.24, CG1 = 63.67, CG2 = 65.29; ANOVA gave "[F(2, 91) = 0.267, p = 0.76]", so the authors assumed the groups had similar conditions. No effect size was printed.

> "Results of one -way ANOVA analysis revealed that there was no statistically significant difference between the groups in terms of academic achievement according to the data obtained from the PCAAT applied to students before starting implementation [F(2, 91) = 0.267, p = 0.76]."
