Kaliisa 2025¶

Kaliisa, R., Baker, R. S., Wasson, B., & Prinsloo, P. (2025). The Coming but Uneven Storm: How AI Regulation Will Impact AI and Learning Analytics Research in Different Countries. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8495

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Comparative analysis of three African countries' AI strategies (Egypt, Mauritius, Rwanda) found they are "high level, more focus ed on economic goals than on establishing specific requirements." The authors attribute this partly to unique circumstances and demographics calling for customized approaches and to different AI industry development within Africa.
