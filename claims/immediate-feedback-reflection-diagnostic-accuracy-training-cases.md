`q3 i3` Immediate feedback + reflection, compared to attending an outpatient clinic or outpatient clinic plus lecture, had a statistically significant and very large effect on diagnostic accuracy in training cases (SMD 1.462 and 1.237 respectively). [→ Delavari S 2024](#delavari-s-2024)

## Evidence

### Delavari S 2024

Delavari S, Barzkar F, Rikers MJP, Pourahmadi M, Soltani Arabshahi SK, Keshtkar A, Dargahi H, Yaghmaei M, Monajemi A. (2024). Teaching and learning clinical reasoning skill in undergraduate medical students: A scoping review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309606

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review reports this from Choi et al.'s study (reference [77]): feedback plus reflection beat both comparison conditions on diagnostic accuracy for training cases, with printed effect sizes SMD = 1.462 and SMD = 1.237. The discussion notes effects in the control set were nonmeaningful and minimal.

> "immediate feedback + reflection in comparison to attended outpatient clinic or combination of outpatient clinic + lecture had a statistically significant and very large effect on diagnostic accuracy in training cases (SMD,1.462 [0.894–2.029] and SMD, 1.237 [0.649–1.825], respectively)"
