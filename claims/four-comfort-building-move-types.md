## Evidence

### Mahmood 2024

Mahmood, M. S., Talafian, H., Shafer, D., Kuo, E., Lundsgaard, M., & Stelzer, T. (2024). Navigating socio-emotional risk through comfort-building in physics teacher professional development: A case study. Journal of Research in Science Teaching. https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21949

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interaction analysis of a 1-h video episode selected from approximately 80 h of PD workshop video, with thematic coding by three coders reaching "Intercoder agreement before discussion was 92% (Cohen's κ = 0.69)". The four move types are the study's headline analytic result.

> "Our analysis suggests four types of comfort-building moves that can mitigate socio-emotional risks and contribute to a safe space allowing teachers to discuss physics content."
