## Evidence

### Anne van der Linden 2022

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2022). Learning Newtonian mechanics with an intrinsically integrated educational game. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing posttest scores of players who used the correct setting on their last completed level (M = 1.88, SD = 1.026) with players who did not (M = 1.86, SD = .948); no significant difference was found and no effect size was printed.

> "No significant difference was found; t(217) = .108, p = .914."
