## Evidence

### Querido Sophie 2018

Querido Sophie, van den Broek Sjoukje, de Rond Marlies, Wigersma Lode, ten Cate Olle. (2018). Factors affecting senior medical students’ career choice. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c14.de75

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study at UMC Utrecht: thematic analysis of 24 interviews found students citing patient population features, e.g. a student drawn to elderly care after a year working in elderly care. The authors state it "should be classified as a separate factor".

> "Patient population characteristics appeared also important. This includes frequency of patient contacts, duration of patient contacts, type of relationship with patients and type of patients (e.g., children or elderly)."
