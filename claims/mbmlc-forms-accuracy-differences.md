# Accuracy differs significantly across moment-by-moment learning curve forms, with immediate drop highest and a downward trend in attempt accuracy as curve complexity increases.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Immediate drop curves showed higher accuracy on unique problems than immediate peak, close multiple spikes and separated multiple spikes curves. [→ Inge Molenaar 2021](#inge-molenaar-2021)
`q2 i?` All curve forms differed significantly from each other in accuracy on problem solving attempts, with accuracy declining as curve complexity increased. [→ Inge Molenaar 2021 (2)](#inge-molenaar-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Inge Molenaar 2021

Inge Molenaar, Anne Horvers, Ryan S. Baker. (2021). What can moment-by-moment learning curves tell about students' self-regulated learning? Learning and Instruction, 72, 101206. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on accuracy of unique problems (Table 8, N = 265 graphs, 92 students) with post-hoc pairwise comparisons. The intercept for immediate drop was 0.92, and the article reports "Immediate drop showed higher accuracy than the other curves" with the printed t statistics above.

> "Immediate drop showed higher accuracy than the other curves (immediate peak t(209.15) = 5.03, p < 0.001, close multiple spikes t(193.66) = 5.73, p < 0.001, separate multiple spikes t(205.05) = 4.94, p < 0.001)."

### Inge Molenaar 2021 (2)

Inge Molenaar, Anne Horvers, Ryan S. Baker. (2021). What can moment-by-moment learning curves tell about students' self-regulated learning? Learning and Instruction, 72, 101206. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second linear mixed model on accuracy of problem solving attempts (Table 9) with all pairwise contrasts significant (Table 10, e.g. immediate drop versus separated multiple spike t(239.75) = 7.06). The article describes "a downward trend in accuracy as curve complexity increased".

> "All curves differed significantly from each other with respect to accuracy on problem solving attempts, with a downward trend in accuracy as curve complexity increased, see Table 10."
