## Evidence

### Bi S 2021

Bi S, Liu R, Li J, Gu J. (2021). The effectiveness of problem-based learning in gynecology and obstetrics education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000024660

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of 5 randomized controlled trials reporting clinical operation scores on a continuous scale (239 PBL vs 256 traditional participants); no studies were excluded because of the small number. Pooled effect size was SMD = 1.15 (95% CI: 0.93, 1.37) with significant heterogeneity (I2 = 98%).

> "The meta-analysis results showed that the PBL teaching model increased clinical operation scores significantly compared with traditional teaching (SMD: 1.15, 95% CI: 0.93, 1.37, Fig. 7). Significant heterogeneity was found in the pooled effect (I2 = 98%, P < .01)"
