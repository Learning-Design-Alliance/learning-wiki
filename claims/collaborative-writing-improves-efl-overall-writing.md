## Evidence

### Khatib and Meihami 2015

Mohammad Khatib, Hussein Meihami. (2015). Languaging and Writing Skill: The Effect of Collaborative Writing on EFL Students' Writing Performance. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.203

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 35 low-intermediate Iranian EFL students (experimental N=17, control N=18) over a 12-session writing program with pretest/posttest design. The article reports a statistically significant difference in total mean scores between the collaborative and individual groups, with means of 68.26 (SD 5.06) versus 60.53 (SD 3.97); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "An independent-sample t test comparing the total mean scores of the experimental and control group found a significant difference between the means of two groups ( t (28) =.168, p < .05)."
