## Evidence

### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulated standardized counseling encounters with mock index clients, scored by blinded assessors on 15-item checklists and analyzed with generalized estimating equations. Mean fidelity was 65.9% in the enhanced arm versus 35.4% in the standard arm; "Fidelity was 30.5 percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (mean difference 30.5%, 95% CI 26.0%-35.0%; P<.001)".

> "The mean fidelity score for index client-HCW encounters was higher in the enhanced arm (65.9%, 95% CI 62.1%-69.8%), compared to the standard arm (35.4%, 95% CI 33.1%-37.7%). Fidelity was 30.5 percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (mean difference 30.5%, 95% CI 26.0%-35.0%; P<.001)."

### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026 (2)

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same simulated fidelity assessment with mock contact clients. Mean fidelity was 72.0% in the enhanced arm and 48.0% in the standard arm; "Fidelity was 24.0 percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (mean difference 24.0%, 95% CI 20.6%-27.3%; P<.001)".

> "The mean fidelity score for contact client-HCW encounters was 72.0% (95% CI 69.4%-74.5%) in the enhanced arm and 48.0% (95% CI 45.7%-50.1%) in the standard arm. Fidelity was 24.0 percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (mean difference 24.0%, 95% CI 20.6%-27.3%; P<.001; Figure 3)."
