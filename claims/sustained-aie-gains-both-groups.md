### Pau Benazet i Montobbio 2026

Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Janne Rotter, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Experiential Versus Instructional Approaches for Eliciting Metacognitive Awareness in AI-Assisted Learning: A Short-Term Longitudinal Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20047 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-group Wilcoxon analysis of the instructional follow-up sample (n1 = 57) from T0 to T5. AIE was the only instructional construct surviving corrections; the experiential group showed a comparable sustained gain (∆M = +0.30, r = .73, both corrections p < .001).

> "AI Engagement (AIE) showed a substantial sustained gain (M pre = 3.66,M postW5 = 4.01; ∆M= +0.35; Wilcoxonp<.001,r= .72; Bonferronip<.001, BHp<.001)."
