## Evidence

### Camille Dickson-Deane 2021

Camille Dickson-Deane. (2021). Influencing the design outcome. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09943-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial's framing of the pandemic context: teachers modified pedagogical designs to reduce risk of harm, and in the author's words "the appropriate designs had to be sacrificed to meet deadlines" that were difficult to achieve. No empirical data are reported here.

> "For many, switching from one mode of delivery (i.e., face to face) to a digital format meant that the appropriate designs had to be sacrificed to meet deadlines. Deadlines which were, in most cases difficult to achieve."
