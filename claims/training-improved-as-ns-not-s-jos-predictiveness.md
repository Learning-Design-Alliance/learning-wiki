## Evidence

### Burton 2023

Burton, O. R., Bodner, G. E., Williamson, P., & Arnold, M. M. (2023). How accurate and predictive are judgments of solvability? Explorations in a two-phase anagram solving paradigm. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 1–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09313-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 solved-versus-not-solved ANOVAs with group as between-subjects factor: AS JOS outcome × group interaction significant (F(1, 157) = 7.80, p = .01, η²p = .05); NS JOS outcome × group interaction significant (F(1, 157) = 20.86, p < .001, η²p = .12); S JOS outcome difference not significant in either group. "training with longer-duration anagrams in blocks 1–3 enhanced the predictiveness of AS and NS JOSs but not S JOSs".
