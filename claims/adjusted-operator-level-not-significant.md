### Taşdemir Murat 2026

Taşdemir Murat, Keskin Büşra Ekin, Aydoğan Ayaz Elif, Çelik Davut. (2026). Adverse outcomes of post-core restorations performed by undergraduate and postgraduate dental students: a retrospective comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09270-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable binary logistic regression on 160 restorations including age, gender, tooth region, tooth location, and operator education level. The article reports the adjusted association "did not reach statistical significance after adjustment" (aOR = 2.18, 95% CI: 0.96–4.94, p = 0.062); no variable remained significant.
