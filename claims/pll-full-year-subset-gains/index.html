q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Full-year analysis of the Ohio study, limited to the subset of students who scored lower on MAP at pretest and had Acadience Reading and NWEA MAP Reading posttest scores. The article reports effect sizes of +0.19 (Acadience), +0.30 (MAP), and +0.54 (Ohio State Test, fifth grade).

"Acadience and MAP Growth assessments showed significant improvement in reading scores for 95 PLL students relative to the comparison group (effect sizes = +0.19, +0.30 respectively), especially among the fifth grade students. These strong results for 5th grade students also held true on the Ohio State Test (effect size =+0.54)."
