Sutcher 2017¶

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's review of coaching and mentoring in education distinguishes coaching as more formal and skill-focused from broader interpersonal mentoring, and states these practices improve leader effectiveness and productivity.

"Principals often report that this is the most valuable learning opportunity for them, but also the one they are least likely to experience in many states."
