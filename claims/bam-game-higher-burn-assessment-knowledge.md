### Nasirzade Amirreza 2024

Nasirzade Amirreza, Deldar Kolsoum, Froutan Razieh, Shakeri Mohammad Taghi. (2024). Comparison of the effects of burn assessment mission game with feedback lecture on nursing students’ knowledge and skills in the burn patients’ assessment: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-024-02558-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT, the 20-item knowledge test showed the BAM game group mean of 17.4 versus 14.7 for feedback lectures; the difference was "statistically significant (P < .001)" and remained significant after covariate adjustment (P < .05).

> "the knowledge scores were 17.4 (SD 2.2) for the intervention group and 14.7 (SD 2.6) for the control group. Both differences were statistically significant (P < .001)."
