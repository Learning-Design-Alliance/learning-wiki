Haberal Merve, Hançerlioğulları Dilek. (2026). Can artificial intelligence chatbots think like dentists? A comparative analysis based on dental specialty examination questions in restorative dentistry. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07612-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of accuracy by content domain across the 188-question DUS benchmark (Fig. 2). Most chatbots reached up to 100% in adhesive systems, dental anomalies, dentin hypersensitivity, dental lasers, dental plaque, and saliva, but "greater variability in accuracy was observed" in anterior/posterior restorations and cavity preparation.

> "in “cavity preparation,” Gemini 1.5 achieved an accuracy rate of 42.86%, Copilot 57.14%, and ChatGPT-4o Plus 57.14%, whereas ChatGPT-4o Free and Gemini Advanced attained 100% accuracy."
