### Cummins 1917

Cummins, R. A. (1917). 'Bright' and 'Slow' Pupils in Elementary and High School. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of the Ethical Culture School's high school and elementary record cards for 116 graduates and 184 non-graduates (1913-14 to 1916-17). The author reports "no cases presenting, any marked change" in standing between early grades and high school; no effect size is printed.

> "we find that there were no cases presenting, any marked change from "Bright" to "Slow," or vice versa. as they passed from the "Early" period in the elementary school to the high school, in either the graduate or the non-graduate group."
