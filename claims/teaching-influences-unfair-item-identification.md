## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across three lecturer groups (B, D, L) taking the same exam, item unfairness partly differs by group — e.g., Item 3 is unfair in D's group instead of Item 11 in B's group — while items 5, 25, and 28 are unfair in all groups. [→ Bakman 2012](#bakman-2012)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative analysis of two additional student groups (lecturers D and L) who took the same course and exam as lecturer B's group. Table 2 prints distances di for five items per group and pooled; e.g., Item 25 ranges from -0.29 (B) to -0.56 (L).
