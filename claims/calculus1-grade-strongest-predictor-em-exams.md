## Evidence

### James K. Hirons 2025

James K. Hirons, Jonathan D. Perry, Dawson T. Nodurft, Scott Crawford, William Bassichis, and Tatiana L. Erukhimova. (2025). Click, Watch, Learn: The Impact of Student Self-Study Materials on Physics E&M Course Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 1368 students with complete data from three fall semesters (2021-2023) of a large-enrollment E&M course, with average of three common midterm exams as response. Calculus I grade had the largest beta coefficient, β = 0.327, p < 0.001; the hybrid and log models gave β = 0.314 and 0.325.

> "The model has an R-square of 0.302, indicating that it took into account 30.2% of the variability in student performance. The strongest predictor of student perfor- mance was their Calculus 1 grade ( β = 0.327)."
