Overconfidence defined as an incorrect response with a self-reported confidence score of 90% or higher; compared across the three models using Cochran's Q with pairwise McNemar tests.

> "The frequency of high-confidence incorrect responses also differed significantly among models (Cochran's Q, χ2(2) = 14.53, P = 0.001). Gemini had the highest overconfidence rate (9.8%), followed by ChatGPT (5.7%), whereas Claude had no overconfident responses"
