# Baseline General Anatomy scores were comparable across stages, but post-R-ISAM Neuroanatomy scores showed no significant differences, paralleled by the non-anatomy control course

## Evidence

### Almasi-Turk Sahar 2026

Almasi-Turk Sahar, Roozbehi Amrollah. (2026). The Roozbehi Integrated Spiral Anatomy Model (R-ISAM): a five-phase framework for fully integrated anatomical sciences education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09833-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA of objective course scores across three stages; baseline comparability confirmed with F = 1.77, p = 0.172.

> "General Anatomy (baseline) scores did not differ significantly across the three groups (F = 1.77, p = 0.172)"

### Almasi-Turk Sahar 2026 (2)

Almasi-Turk Sahar, Roozbehi Amrollah. (2026). The Roozbehi Integrated Spiral Anatomy Model (R-ISAM): a five-phase framework for fully integrated anatomical sciences education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09833-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ANOVA of post-R-ISAM Neuroanatomy scores across stages; means rose from 14.87 to 15.14 but the trend was not statistically significant (p = 0.312).

> "Neuroanatomy scores did not differ significantly across educational stages (p = 0.312). Although the mean increased from 14.87 (Basic Sciences) to 15.14 (Clerkship), this trend was not statistically significant"

### Almasi-Turk Sahar 2026 (3)

Almasi-Turk Sahar, Roozbehi Amrollah. (2026). The Roozbehi Integrated Spiral Anatomy Model (R-ISAM): a five-phase framework for fully integrated anatomical sciences education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09833-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ANOVA of the non-anatomical control course Neurophysiology across stages (p = 0.185, means 14.92–15.24), which the authors say suggests modest increases reflect general academic maturation rather than a specific delayed effect of R-ISAM.

> "the non-anatomy control course (Neurophysiology) showed a nearly identical non-significant pattern (p = 0.185; means from 14.92 to 15.24)"
