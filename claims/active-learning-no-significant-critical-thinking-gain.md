## Evidence

### Nelson 2014

Nelson, L. P., & Crow, M. L. (2014). Do Active-Learning Strategies Improve Students’ Critical Thinking? Higher Education Studies, 4(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n2p77

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 190 pre-service teachers: a lecture-style control (N=90) and an active-learning intervention (N=100) taking the same teaching methods course with service-learning. The study found "no case did the active-learning sequence show any significant improvement on Evaluation Vignette scores between conditions."

> "Findings from the study indicated that in no case did the active-learning sequence show any significant improvement on Evaluation Vignette scores between conditions (Table 1)."
