`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 195 healthcare professionals (42.6% nurses, 35.9% residents, 21.5% attendings) at Mogadishu Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Training and Research Hospital using a 23-item structured survey; "knowledge levels were predominantly poor across all professional groups regarding specific technical parameters".
