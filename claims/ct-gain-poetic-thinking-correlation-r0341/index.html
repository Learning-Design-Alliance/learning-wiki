Evidence¶

Jenkins 2015¶

Craig Jenkins. (2015). Poem Generator: A comparative quantitative evaluation of a microworlds-based learning approach for teaching English. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 11(2), 153-167. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1972

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Subsidiary Pearson's r analysis pooling both the microworld intervention group and the comparison group (N = 27). The correlation was r = 0.341 with p = 0.082, described as "a modest -to-moderate positive correlation".

"The analysis revealed that there was a modest -to-moderate positive correlation (r = 0.341) between improvement in computational thinking and improvement in poetic thinking."
