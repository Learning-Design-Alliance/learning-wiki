`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-test analysis of Q6 (smallest and largest things students can think of) across the three trials. The article reports narrow-range answers "had reduced to 10%" and 90% gave atomic-to-cosmological answers; 8% gave number counts, revealing a need for clearer question wording.

> "After the trials the number of students giving a narrow range had reduced to 10%: 90% gave answers encompassing atomic to cosmological scales, with 50% mentioning subatomic particles."
