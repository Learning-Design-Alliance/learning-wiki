### Wu Qiong 2026

Wu Qiong. (2026). The impact of teacher-student relationship quality on achievement emotions in English as a foreign language within a Confucian heritage cultural context: an examination of the mediating roles of cognitive appraisals. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04561-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent structural equation model among 1103 Chinese college EFL learners surveyed March–May 2025, controlling for gender, age, and home resources. The abstract reports that "TSRQ was positively associated with enjoyment and negatively associated with boredom and anxiety." No standardized effect size for these direct paths is printed in the quoted passage.

> "TSRQ was positively associated with enjoyment and negatively associated with boredom and anxiety. Control and value appraisals fully mediated the TSRQ-anxiety association and partially mediated the TSRQ-enjoyment and TSRQ-boredom associations."
