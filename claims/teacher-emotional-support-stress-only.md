## Evidence

### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2022

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Rahmadi Imam Fitri, Chotimah Siti, Jailani Jailani, Wutsqa Dhoriva Urwatul. (2022). A Case Study of Factors That Affect Secondary School Mathematics Achievement: Teacher-Parent Support, Stress Levels, and Students’ Well-Being. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316247

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hypothesis testing in the PLS-SEM survey analysis found teachers' emotional support paths to well-being, achievement, and interest unsupported; the stress path (H2) was supported (β = −0.182, t = 3.445, p = 0.001). Equivalence not tested for the nulls.

> "Surprisingly, teachers’ emotional support was found to have no significant relationship with students’ well-being (β = 0.076, t = 1.478, p = 0.140), mathematics achievement (β = −0.025, t = 0.676, p = 0.499), and interest (β = 0.060, t = 1.087, p = 0.277)."
