`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative validation finding from expert and practitioner sessions on the initial draft framework. The experts also advised distinguishing digital literacy for life, learning and general work from profession-specific digital literacy, and added ethical competences, noting that because of the GOT ethical questions became even more relevant, such as the consequences of using data.

> "In the validation sessions, the experts suggested to replace 'fostering students' digital literacy' with 'empowering students' digital literacy'."
