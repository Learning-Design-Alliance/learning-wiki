## Evidence

### Serçe Sibel 2025

Serçe Sibel, Ovayolu Özlem, Ovayolu Nimet. (2025). The effect of simulator-assisted application on anxiety, satisfaction, and self-confidence level of students taking internal medicine nursing course: a randomized controlled and experimental trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08202-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomised trial, intervention-group state anxiety moved from 39.5 ± 5.8 to 40.6 ± 5.3 and trait anxiety from 46.6 ± 6.0 to 46.1 ± 5.5 with p > 0.05; the discussion states anxiety "did not change statistically significantly compared to the control group". No effect size is printed.

> "However, the anxiety level of the students who practiced with the simulation-assisted programme did not change statistically significantly compared to the control group."
