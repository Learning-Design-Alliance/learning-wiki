## Evidence

### Okuyama Ayako 2011

Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's conclusion states that "a reliable and valid toolbox for summative testing that covers all patient safety domains at Miller's four competency levels cannot yet be constructed". It also states many tools are useful for formative feedback, and that 17 tools (50%) were used for summative evaluation while three were used for formative evaluation.

> "However, a reliable and valid toolbox for summative testing that covers all patient safety domains at Miller's four competency levels cannot yet be constructed. Many tools, however, are useful for formative feedback."
