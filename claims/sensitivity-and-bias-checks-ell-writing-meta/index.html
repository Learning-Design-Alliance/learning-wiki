Evidence¶

Aitken 2026¶

Aitken, A. A., Graham, S., Cao, Y., & Nusrat, A. (2026). The Impact of Writing Instruction for English Language Learners in the United States: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10197-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Sensitivity analysis varying the assumed within-cluster correlation ρ across 0.20, 0.40, 0.60, and 0.80 in the RVE framework. The article reports "The pooled ES estimates remained at g = 0.33", supporting robustness to dependence assumptions.

"The pooled ES estimates remained at g = 0.33, indi- cating that the results were not sensitive to the choice of ρ."

Aitken 2026 (2)¶

Aitken, A. A., Graham, S., Cao, Y., & Nusrat, A. (2026). The Impact of Writing Instruction for English Language Learners in the United States: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10197-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Publication bias assessment using funnel plots, trim-and-fill, and Egger's regression test on study-level aggregated effect sizes. The article reports the funnel plot "appeared asymmetrical", while Egger's test (intercept = 0.49, p =.363) found no statistical evidence of publication bias.

"The funnel plot appeared asymmetrical, suggesting the potential absence of larger positive effect sizes"
