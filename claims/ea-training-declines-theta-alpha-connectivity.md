## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the EA group, mean phase-lag index decreased in the theta (4–7 Hz) and alpha (8–12 Hz) bands after training, while control-group connectivity did not vary with training. [→ Chen 2023](#chen-2023)

## Evidence

### Chen 2023

Chen, Y.-C., Chang, G.-C., Huang, W.-M. & Hwang, I.-S. (2023). Quick balance skill improvement after short-term training with error amplification feedback for older adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00151-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hotelling's T-squared and post-hoc t tests of scalp EEG phase-lag index within the EA group (n = 18) across pre- and post-test stance trials; connectivity declined in theta and alpha bands. Effect sizes (ηp²) not quoted here; impact null.

> "The results of Hotelling’s T-squared statistics revealed a signiﬁcant difference in m-PLI in the EA group (Wilks ’ Λ = 0.594, p = 0.045) but not in the control group (Wilks ’ Λ = 0.873, p = 0.551). In the EA group, post hoc analysis further revealed that the m-PLIs of the theta (t17 = −2.861, p = 0.011) and beta ( t17 = −2.424, p = 0.027) bands were smaller in the post-test than in the pre-test."
