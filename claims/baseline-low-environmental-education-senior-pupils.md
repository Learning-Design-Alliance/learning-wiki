Aida G. Sadykova, Marianna E. Yashina & Luiza R. Zakirova. (2014). Implementing Environmental Culture in the Language Learning Laboratory. English Language Teaching, 7(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n12p85

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Ascertaining-stage testing of 215 senior pupils in gymnasium №7 and school №165 of Kazan, reported in Table 1 as 10% high, 42% middle, 48% low. The authors state "Only one-tenth of senior pupils have a high level of environmental education".

> "Thus, the level of the ecological education of senior pupils, according to the results of our initial study, leaves much to be desired. Only one-tenth of senior pupils have a high level of environmental education, almost half of students are with low levels of environmental education."
