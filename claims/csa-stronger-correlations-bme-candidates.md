## Evidence

### Wakeford Richard 2015

Wakeford Richard, Denney MeiLing, Ludka-Stempien Katarzyna, Dacre Jane, McManus I C. (2015). Cross-comparison of MRCGP & MRCP(UK) in a database linkage study of 2,284 candidates taking both examinations: assessment of validity and differential performance by ethnicity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0281-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlational comparison by ethnicity merging old and new CSA formats (Table 4). The authors interpret the higher CSA-PACES correlation in BME candidates as suggesting less extraneous variance, and note regression slopes of CSA on PACES are steeper for BME candidates.

> "AKT showed similar correlations with Part1/Part2/PACES in White and BME candidates (p = .502, .849 and .161). However, CSA showed stronger correlations with Part1/Part2/PACES in BME candidates than in White candidates (p = .037, .054 and .005)."
