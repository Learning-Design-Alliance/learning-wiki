### Jessi Randolph 2022

Jessi Randolph, Jonathan Perry, Jonan Phillip Donaldson, Callie Rethman, and Tatiana Erukhimova. (2022). Do female physics students benefit from informal physics programs they facilitate?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview frequency analysis (codes counted once per interview, Fig. 1) of 11 female interviewees. The article separately reports that "the majority of female students reported an increase in their performance and competency beliefs" and positive confidence/self-efficacy and recognition impacts.

> "All of the female students reported increases in in- terest and motivation (Fig. 1), as participation in in- formal physics programs allowed the students to gain a deeper appreciation of physics and develop their interests."
