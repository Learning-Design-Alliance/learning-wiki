## Evidence

### Elaine M. Allensworth and Stuart Luppescu 2016

Elaine M. Allensworth and Stuart Luppescu. (2016). Why do students get good grades, or bad ones? The influence of the teacher, class, school, and student. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research working paper. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-classified model 2a on the full CPS grade dataset shows teacher random main effects reduce within-student variance from 0.87 to 0.70, so "teacher effects account for about one-fifth of the within-student variation in grades"; the student variance component was almost unchanged (0.95 versus 0.96).

> "Accounting for teacher effects decreases the within-student variance from 0.87 to 0.70. Thus, teacher effects account for about one-fifth of the within-student variation in grades"
