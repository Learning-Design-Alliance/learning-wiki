Evidence¶

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025¶

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Comparative experiment in which 13 teachers each produced a traditional and a ChatGPT-assisted plan (25 plans total) and 6 professors rated all plans blind on a six-dimension rubric adapted from the 5E ILP rubric. The study reports "an average score of 21.0 compared to 17.0 for traditional plans"; no effect size is printed.

"Results demonstrate that ChatGPT significantly improved the quality of lesson planning, with ChatGPT-assisted plans achieving an average score of 21.0 compared to 17.0 for traditional plans."
