## Evidence

### Stieglitz Verena 2023

Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney-U-test comparison at α = 0.05 between medical students (n = 160) and midwifery trainees (n = 14) on the simulation score. The article reports "no significant difference between medical students and trainees: p = 0.204"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Medical students scored a mean of 38.3 points (standard deviation (SD) 6.5) on simulation training, whereas trainees scored 40.6 points (SD 3.3). There was no significant difference between medical students and trainees: p = 0.204 (α = 0.05)."
