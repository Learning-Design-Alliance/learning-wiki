# The damage advantage of the endoscopic technique over the microscopic technique is significant for students and residents but not for consultants

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A significant interaction between technique and training status was found for ossicular chain damage (ηp2 = 0.254), with students (ηp2 = 0.447) and residents (ηp2 = 0.319) producing significantly more damage microscopically, while the consultant comparison was not significant. [→ Anschuetz Lukas 2019](#anschuetz-lukas-2019)

## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons following the significant technique × training status interaction (F1, 27 = 4.602, p = 0.019, ηp2 = 0.254) in the randomized crossover study. Students and residents each showed significantly more microscopic-technique damage; the article reports the consultant comparison was not significant (p = 0.141).

> "Students (t7 = 2.527, p = 0.039, ηp2 = 0.447) and residents (t12 = 2.369, p = 0.035, ηp2 = 0.319) produced significantly more damage to the ossicular chain with the microscopic technique compared to the endoscopic technique."
