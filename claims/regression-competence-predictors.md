## Evidence

### Alyahya D 2025

Alyahya D, Alharbi A, Kashoo F, Alsaheli S, AlMubarak FM, Aljuhni R. (2025). Knowledge and competence in vestibular rehabilitation: a cross-sectional study of physical therapy interns. PeerJ 13. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20213

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on survey data from 233 interns: the model was significant, F(12, 219) = 5.96, p < .001, "explaining 24.6% of the variance (adjusted R2 = .21, RMSE = 1.78)"; the quoted coefficients are the only unique contributors.

> "Internship vestibular-test knowledge was the strongest unique predictor, B = 0.25, SE = 0.05, β = .32, t = 4.91, p < .001, while workshop attendance also showed a positive effect, B = 0.82, SE = 0.30, t = 2.78, p = .006."
