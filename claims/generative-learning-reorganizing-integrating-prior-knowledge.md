The article's opening definitional statement of generative learning, offered as the framing thesis of the review rather than as a tested result. It defines generative learning as "mentally reorganizing and integrating it with one's prior knowledge" and ties it to transfer to new situations. No empirical data are reported for this definition in the available text.
