Building a National Early Childhood Education System That Works 2021¶

Building a National Early Childhood Education System That Works. (2021). Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED614493

q2 · i? · review · r1

The whitepaper's Background section reports participation rates for U.S. early childhood programs, citing Child Trends indicator data. It states that "just 54% of 3- and 4-year-olds" attend any preschool and "just 35% of eligible children participate in Head Start." No effect sizes are reported; these are descriptive participation statistics.

"Yet just 54% of 3- and 4-year-olds in the United States participate in any preschool; just 35% of eligible children participate in Head Start; and only a small fraction of eligible infants and toddlers receive subsidized, licensed child care."
