## Evidence

### St-Onge C 2026

St-Onge C, Boulais I, Bergeron L, Dion V, Le Sieur I, Marceau M, Poitras M-E, Dubé T, Schumacher DJ. (2026). Patients’ Voices and the Assessment of Medical Trainees: A Discourse Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2588

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of 54 published articles; the survey-filler discourse positions patients as respondents completing surveys to satisfy accreditation, with discussion focused on "the number of patients that needed to complete the surveys to achieve acceptable reliability" and survey format.

> "The integration of patients in the assessment of medical trainees seems to be mainly driven by accreditation standards. Fulfilling accreditation bodies’ requirements was documented as a significant and dominant reason for involving patients in the assessment of medical trainees"
