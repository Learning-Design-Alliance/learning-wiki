## Evidence

### Sparn Moritz B. 2025

Sparn Moritz B., Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Warschkow Rene, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2025). Does mental imagery make a difference in virtual reality simulation training? Results of a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy 39(10). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12012-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the modified OSATS score used in the Davos Course RCT's porcine cholecystectomy exercise. Table 2 reports an overall Cronbach's Alpha of "0.89 (0.87–0.90)", with item alphas ranging from 0.84 (Time and motion) to 0.88 (Respect for tissue; Instrument knowledge).

> "When assessing participants’ performance in the porcine cholecystectomy, internal consistency of the OSATS scores was high. In all items and in the overall score, Cronbach’s Alpha was > 0.8 (Table 2)."
