### Watson AL 2026

Watson AL, Peterson NE, Thatcher B, Thomas M, Hunsaker S, Hooley C, Erekson D, Simpson A, Snow G, Detrick R. (2026). Experiential Course Learning, Wellness, and Higher Education: Qualitative Descriptive Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/88642

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive reflexive thematic analysis of open-ended postcourse survey narratives from 110 of 125 consenting students across 6 wellness courses; Theme 1 responses described adopting new behaviors and routinizing them, with students reporting they saw "a large improvement in my life." No effect sizes; qualitative descriptive findings only.

> "Participants across courses described their experiences in wellness courses as a catalyst for tangible, day-to-day transformation. Rather than learning abstract principles, they reported adopting new behaviors, routinizing them, and noticing measurable gains in well-being."
