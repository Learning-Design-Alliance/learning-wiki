Wei W 2026¶

Wei W, Nielsen MS, Nielsen AB, Han Y, Russell L, Konge L, Cold KM. (2026). Hands-on Distance Simulation of Technical Skills: A Systematic Review. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000881

q2 · i? · review · r3

Randomized study of 19 novice medical and premedical students comparing augmented reality telementoring with traditional laparoscopic mentoring; the review reports faster completion times (167 vs. 242 seconds, P = 0.014) favoring ART.

"Participants in the ART group demonstrated a steeper learning curve, faster task completion times (167 vs. 242 seconds, P = 0.014), and fewer failed attempts (8 vs. 13) than those receiving traditional guidance."

Wei W 2026 (2)¶

Wei W, Nielsen MS, Nielsen AB, Han Y, Russell L, Konge L, Cold KM. (2026). Hands-on Distance Simulation of Technical Skills: A Systematic Review. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000881

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Study of 40 medical students comparing the ReTeRoS multichannel video-based remote teaching system with live one-on-one in-person instruction for robotic surgery; the review reports higher simulator scores on specific tasks for the remote group.

"The remote training group achieved higher simulator scores on specific tasks and reported lower trainer fatigue, suggesting the feasibility and potential scalability of this approach."

Wei W 2026 (3)¶

Wei W, Nielsen MS, Nielsen AB, Han Y, Russell L, Konge L, Cold KM. (2026). Hands-on Distance Simulation of Technical Skills: A Systematic Review. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000881

q2 · i? · review · r3

Study of 11 novice surgical trainees evaluating a virtual classroom training platform with the Fundamentals of Robotic Surgery curriculum; the review reports significantly higher R-OSAT scores (44.8 vs. 35.3, P = 0.006) with VCT added.

"Compared to self-directed FRS learning alone, the addition of VCT led to significantly higher R-OSAT scores (44.8 vs. 35.3, P = 0.006), with further improvement observed when the control group later received VCT."
