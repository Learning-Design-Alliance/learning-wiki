Evidence¶

Michele D. Dickey 2007¶

Michele D. Dickey. (2007). Barriers and enablers in integrating cognitive apprenticeship methods in a Web-based educational technology course for K–12 (primary and secondary) teacher education. ALT-J, Research in Learning Technology. https://doi.org/10.1080/09687760701482218

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Author's interpretive summary of the case study experience: OMS videos and animations took a great deal of time to create, and unlike the time-locked traditional classroom, Web-based coaching had no imposed end point, making it "an ongoing teaching experience" and a potential barrier for the teacher.

"Without time-lock limits, coaching students can rapidly become an ongoing teaching experience, which, although it is a great enabler for students, can rapidly become a barrier for the teacher."
