Evidence¶

Piper 2015¶

Piper, B., Bulat, J., & Johnston, A. (2015). Reading Skill Transfer Across Languages: Outcomes from Longitudinal Bilingual Randomized Control Trials in Kenya and Haiti. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562423

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Findings from the two-year ToTAL randomized control trial in Haiti, in which over 4,000 Grade 1 and 2 students in treatment and control schools were tested at four points with EGRA-based tasks in Haitian Creole and French. The article reports a positive impact on the two named foundational skills.

"Analysis similarly shows that ToTAL has a positive impact o n key foundational skills of initial sound identification and letter sound identification, although not on all combinations of language and grade."
