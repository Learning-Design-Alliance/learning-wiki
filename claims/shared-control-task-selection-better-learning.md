## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2023

Garvin Brod, Natalia Kucirkova, Joshua Shepherd, Dietsje Jolles, Inge Molenaar. (2023). Agency in Educational Technology: Interdisciplinary Perspectives and Implications for Learning Design. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09749-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports initial data from the Corbalan et al. (2006) study of a personalized task-selection model with shared instructional control, in which an algorithm preselects a task subset and learners make the final selection. It reports "higher learning scores with shared instructional control than with full system control."

> "Initial data from the research group (Corbalan et al., 2006) indicate higher learning scores with shared instructional control than with full system control."

### Garvin Brod 2023 (2)

Garvin Brod, Natalia Kucirkova, Joshua Shepherd, Dietsje Jolles, Inge Molenaar. (2023). Agency in Educational Technology: Interdisciplinary Perspectives and Implications for Learning Design. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09749-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a classroom experiment by Long and Aleven (2016) in intelligent tutoring systems, where mastery-level information guided learners' selection of practice activities. Shared control "led to better learning outcomes than fully system-controlled problem selection."

> "In a classroom experiment, Long and Eleven (2016) showed that shared control over problem selection led to better learning outcomes than fully system-controlled problem selection."
