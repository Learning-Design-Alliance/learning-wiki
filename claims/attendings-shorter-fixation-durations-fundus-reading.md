## Evidence

### Gupta P 2025

Gupta P, Sheth N, AlAhmadi R, Yao X, Heiferman MJ. (2025). The Effect of Experience on Visual Search Patterns in Retinal Imaging Analysis. Ophthalmic Surg Lasers Imaging Retina. https://doi.org/10.3928/23258160-20250228-03

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational eye-tracking study with five participants per experience group (PGY2–PGY4, fellows, attendings) reading 13 ultra-widefield fundus images. The five-group ANOVA found attendings' fixation duration "significantly different than PGY2s F(2,20) = 10.21, P = 0.0016"; no effect size is printed.

> "The highest average fixation duration was found among the PGY2s, at 0.31 ± 0.034 seconds, while the attendings recorded the shortest average fixation duration, 0.15 ± 0.045 seconds (Figure 2C), significantly different than PGY2s F(2,20) = 10.21, P = 0.0016."
