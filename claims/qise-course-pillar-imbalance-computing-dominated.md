The roadmap reports, citing the Piña et al. catalog analysis of 1,456 US institutions, that "418 of 514 categorized QISE courses focus on quantum computing and information (81 percent)". The review attributes the counts to that study; its design is not described here as if independently read.

> "Of the dedicated QISE courses identified across all institutions, 418 of 514 categorized QISE courses focus on quantum computing and information (81 percent); 70 courses cover quantum technology implementation (which subsumes sensing hardware) and all other QISE categories have fewer than 15 courses each."
