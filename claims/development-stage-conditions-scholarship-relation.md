# When body size at fourteen and period of rapid growth are considered, heavy or tall early developers rank better in scholarship than late developers, and light late developers rank better than light early or medium developers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Heavy boys developing early, average boys developing at medium periods, and light boys developing late rank well in scholarship in eight cases and poor in three; heavy-late and light-early-or-medium boys rank well in no case and poor in four. [→ Stewart 1917](#stewart-1917)
`q2 i?` For height, tall boys increasing rapidly at early or medium periods and average boys at medium periods do good work in six cases and poor in three; short boys of medium or late period do well in one case and poorly in six. [→ Stewart 1917 (2)](#stewart-1917-2)

## Evidence

### Stewart 1917

Stewart, S. F. (1917). A study of physical growth and school standing of boys. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070804

`q2 · i?`

Chart XII classifies the twenty-nine boys by weight at fourteen years and period of most rapid weight increase against scholarship standing. The complementary group is described: heavy boys developing late and light boys developing early or medium "rank well in no case and poor in four cases."

> "Heavy boys who develop early, average boys who develop at medium periods, and light boys who develop late, rank well in scholarship in eight cases and poor in three cases."

### Stewart 1917 (2)

Stewart, S. F. (1917). A study of physical growth and school standing of boys. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070804

`q2 · i?`

Chart XIII classifies the same boys by height at fourteen and period of rapid height increase. Short boys of medium or late period do well in one case and poorly in six; boys with indefinite rapid-growth periods do well in four cases and average in one.

> "Tall boys who increase rapidly at early or medium periods and average boys who increase rapidly at medium periods are doing good work in six cases and poor work in three cases."
