## Evidence

### Keith B. Lyle 2022

Keith B. Lyle, Campbell R. Bego, Patricia A. S. Ralston, Jason C. Immekus. (2022). Spaced Retrieval Practice Imposes Desirable Difficulty in Calculus Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09677-2

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Within-participants classroom experiment in a Fall 2020 calculus course for engineering undergraduates (N = 180 analyzed); paired t test on end-of-semester criterial test scores showed "higher on spaced objectives" with Hedges' gav = 0.32 and a common-language effect size of 63.5%.

> "Mean performance was higher on spaced objectives (M = 0.77, SD = 0.17) than massed objectives (M = 0.71, SD = 0.18). This difference was statistically significant, t(179) = 4.64, p < 0.001, Hedges' gav = 0.32."
