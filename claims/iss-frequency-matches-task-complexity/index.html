Evans Kerry, Woodruff Jenny, Cowley Alison, Bramley Louise, Miles Giulia, Ross Alastair, Cooper Joanne, Baxendale Bryn. (2022). GENESISS 2—Generating Standards for In-Situ Simulation project: a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03401-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review category 3: seventeen studies aimed at improving clinical skills, performance and clinical management; ISS frequency varied from single sessions to repeat training lasting 18 months, with session length 30 min to 3 h. The review reports that studies with "more complex practices and clinical outcomes implemented regular ISS interventions over longer time periods".
