### Hartman 2021

Hartman, J. R.; Nelson, E. A. (2021). A Paradigm Shift: The Implications of Working Memory Limits for Physics and Chemistry Instruction. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.00454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.00454)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review distinguishes primary learning (instinctive, automatic, effortless, e.g. learning to speak the local dialect) from secondary learning, noting physical science is primarily symbol rather than sound-based and its definitions are precise, so storing them verbatim requires repeated effort at recall. No effect size is printed.

> "Learning to solve problems in math and science is secondary: it requires effort and it benefits from structured instruction."
