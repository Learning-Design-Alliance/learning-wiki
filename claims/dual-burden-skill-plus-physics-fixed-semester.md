## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ashleigh Leary 2019

Ashleigh Leary, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). The difficulties associated with integrating computation into undergraduate physics. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview thematic analysis found the time-structure force replicates because semesters remain a fixed length, while computation requires teaching tool syntax in addition to physics, which "can be quite challenging when there is a lot to teach and not a lot of time."

> "Adding computation to a curriculum creates a new challenge of teaching the skill and the lesson that is encompassed in that skill at the same time. Basically, syntax or how to use a Jupyter Notebook needs to be taught in addition to the physics of what the program is teaching."
