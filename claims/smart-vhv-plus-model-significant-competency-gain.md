# Competency scores rose significantly from 86.14% to 98.16% after SMART VHV Plus Model training, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (Cohen's d = 1.96)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, overall competency scores of 33 VHVs rose significantly from a pre-intervention mean of 86.14% to a post-intervention mean of 98.16% after SMART VHV Plus Model training (Cohen's d = 1.96). [→ Chaleephrom Pariyakorn 2026](#chaleephrom-pariyakorn-2026)

## Evidence

### Chaleephrom Pariyakorn 2026

Chaleephrom Pariyakorn, Leethongdissakul Songkhamchai and Promasatayaprot Vorapoj. (2026). A model for developing the competencies of Village Health Volunteers in the prevention and control of communicable diseases at the community level: a case study of Chaiyaphum Province, Thailand. Frontiers in Public Health 14:1781727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1781727

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest–posttest evaluation with 33 VHVs in Nong Bua Ban Village using a paired-samples t-test on a 15-item integrated competency performance test. Mean competency increased from 86.14% (SD = 7.65) to 98.16% (SD = 1.95), "a mean difference of 12.02 percentage points", "Cohen's d = 1.96".

> "representing a mean difference of 12.02 percentage points (95% CI: 9.84–14.20, p < 0.05). Effect size was calculated as Cohen's d = 1.96, with Hedges' g = 1.91 applied as a bias-corrected alternative appropriate for small samples (n = 33)."
