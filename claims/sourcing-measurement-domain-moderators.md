Anmarkrud, Ø., Bråten, I., Florit, E., & Mason, L. (2022). The Role of Individual Differences in Sourcing: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09640-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

This is the review's own synthesis conclusion, stated in its abstract, drawing on its coding of sourcing measures (from noting and remembering source information to credibility evaluation and use in task products) and of varied domains and topics across the 72 included studies.

> "the review suggested that relationships between individual differences and sourcing may vary not only with the way sourcing is measured but also with the domain or topic addressed in the reading materials."
