## Evidence

### Melissa Dancy 2022

Melissa Dancy, Charles Henderson, Nanah Apkarian, Estrella Johnson, Marilyne Stains, Jeffrey R. Raker, Alexandra Lau. (2022). Physics instructors' knowledge and use of active learning has increased over the last decade but most still lecture too much. Proceedings of the Physics Education Research Conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2211.13082](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.13082)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey comparison of 722 instructors in 2008 and 1176 in 2019, restricted to 8 RBISs asked about on both surveys, with respondents classified into knowledge and use categories for a specific introductory course. The article reports "knowledge levels are reaching saturation" and majority use.

> "For every strategy, both knowledge and use levels have greatly increased over the 10 years betweensurveys. Notably, knowledge levels are reaching saturation (i.e nearly all instructors are aware of RBIS's)and use levels are reported to be high (i.e. a majority of instructors report use)."
