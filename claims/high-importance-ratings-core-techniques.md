`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive competence distributions from the survey (Table 6). The article adds that only one-third felt competent in postural drainage (n = 32; 29.1%) and just over half in manual chest techniques (n = 58; 52.7%); no effect size is printed.

> "Good or very good competence was reported by the majority of respondents for strength and endurance training (n = 75; 68.1%) and breathing exercises (n = 75; 68.2%)."
