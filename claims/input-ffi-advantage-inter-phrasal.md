# Input-based FFI is more effective than output-based FFI for inter-phrasal grammatical features, with a medium-sized difference

## Evidence

### Schenck 2024

Schenck, A. (2024). Learning How to Use Input-Based and Output-Based Form-Focused Instruction: A Meta-Analytic Comparison of Persian EFL Learners. Journal of Second Language Acquisition and Teaching, 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1457419

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Collation of effect sizes across 18 Persian-L1 studies; Table 2 reports inter-phrasal input mean 3.5031 (n=12) vs output 2.7787 (n=9). The article calls the .72 difference "a medium difference in effect".

> "At the inter-phrasal level, input-based instruction had a much larger impact than its output-based counterpart, yielding a difference in effect size of .72."
