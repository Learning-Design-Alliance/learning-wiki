## Evidence

### YANGÜEZ MARC 2021

YANGÜEZ MARC, BEDIOU BENOIT, HILLMAN CHARLES H., BAVELIER DAPHNE, CHANAL JULIEN. (2021). The Indirect Role of Executive Functions on the Relationship between Cardiorespiratory Fitness and School Grades. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002630

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple mediation SEM results for mathematics. The article reports the specific indirect path "through cognitive flexibility (path ei: β = 0.11, SE = 0.04, P = 0.007)" with fitness predicting flexibility (β = 0.26) and flexibility predicting mathematics (β = 0.44).

> "This global indirect effect was mostly driven by a specific indirect path through cognitive flexibility (path ei: β = 0.11, SE = 0.04, P = 0.007). More specifically, cardiorespiratory fitness predicted cognitive flexibility (path e: β = 0.26, SE = 0.07, P < 0.001), and cognitive flexibility predicted mathematics (path i: β = 0.44, SE = 0.12, P < 0.001)."
