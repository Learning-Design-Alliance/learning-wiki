Evidence¶

Jacqueline Leybaert and Marie-Noëlle Van Cutsem 2002¶

Jacqueline Leybaert and Marie-Noëlle Van Cutsem. (2002). Counting in Sign Language. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1006/jecp.2002.2660

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive error analysis (Table 1) of object-counting trials for all 28 hearing and 16 deaf children who passed the small and medium sets; percentages of attention, principle, and number-string errors are reported without statistical tests.

"On average across the three age groups, there were no differences in attention or principle errors between deaf and hearing children, but deaf children made more sequence number counting errors (see the two "All" columns)."
