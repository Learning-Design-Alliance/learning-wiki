## Evidence

### Taghi Ali 2026

Taghi Ali, Atia Mohammad, Mohaisen Mais Alreem, Al-Noufi Bushra, Sibai Mohamad, Nouh Sara, Arfeh Yaman, Alhamid Ahmad Abdul Hakim, Taghi Olya, Kheder Karam, Mahfouz Fawaz Ali. (2026). Assessment of knowledge and awareness of cardiopulmonary resuscitation and heart attack symptoms amongst medical students in Syria - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08800-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression on the survey's CPR score (0–11, n = 500) with robust HC3 standard errors; model R² = 0.174. Clinical versus pre-clinical stage carried "adjusted B 1.66 (95% CI 1.10 to 2.22)". Unadjusted means were 5.59 ± 2.41 (clinical) versus 3.83 ± 2.24 (pre-clinical).

> "In multivariable regression, CPR knowledge remained independently higher amongst clinical-year students (adjusted B 1.66 (95% CI 1.10 to 2.22)) and public university students (adjusted B 1.07 (95% CI 0.62 to 1.51))."
