`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of assessment points for knowledge outcomes found no significant differences for either online or hybrid training subgroups; equivalence was not tested.

> "For online training (one study, four comparisons), no significant difference in nurses' knowledge was found between the post‐intervention and follow‐up subgroups (Chi2 = 0.02, p = 0.880; Figure 5). Similarly, for hybrid training, the difference in knowledge outcomes between the two assessment points was also not significant (Chi2 = 0.43, p = 0.510; Figure 6)."
