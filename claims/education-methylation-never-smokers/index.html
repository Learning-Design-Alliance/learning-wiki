Evidence¶

Jenny van Dongen et al 2018¶

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Restricted analysis excluding all current and former smokers (52% of the sample; new N = 2001). Effect sizes at cg05575921 (AHRR) were 86% smaller in never smokers; CpGs in PCDH9, SPATA5 and SNORD93 showed the strongest evidence of association beyond own smoking, with small smoking-mediated proportions.

"effect sizes in never-smokers were strongly correlated with effect sizes in the entire population (r = 0.83), were in the same direction at 57 sites, and were at least nominally significant in never-smokers (p < 0.05) at 27 sites"
