`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The perspective reports, citing Wilschut et al.'s adaptive retrieval practice study with typing-based and speech-based response modes, that "learners with dyslexia recalled more words when us-307 ing speech than typing." Mathematical modeling of response times attributed the typing disadvantage to processing delays rather than memory per se.

> "The results showed that learners with dyslexia recalled more words when us-307 ing speech than typing, suggesting that removing a bottleneck caused by phonological decoding308 and orthographic mapping can make retrieval practice more effective for this group."
