Ninkova, V., Hays, J., Lavi, N., Ali, A., Lopes da Silva Macedo, S., Davis, H. E., & Lew-Levy, S. (2025). Hunter-Gatherer Children at School: A View From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241255614

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's positive-impacts synthesis reports that parents link schooling to rights claiming and socioeconomic empowerment, citing Tshireletso (1997) that "96% of them saw education as an important opportunity." It also reports schools facilitating new relationships with outside groups and, in Latin America, intercultural bilingual curricula valorizing Indigenous languages. Narrative synthesis; no effect sizes.
