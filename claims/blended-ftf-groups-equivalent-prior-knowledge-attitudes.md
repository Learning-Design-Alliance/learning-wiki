## Evidence

### Adileh 2012

Adileh, M. (2012). Teaching music as a university elective course through e-learning. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2012(1), 71-79. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000246

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test comparison using the same achievement test (reliability 0.87) with FTF M = 26.173 (SD = 13.587) and blended M = 24.071 (SD = 14.331), t(177) = .999, p = .319. A parallel attitude pre-test comparison yielded p = .813 (Table 3).

> "According to the test results, there was no significant difference in prior knowledge about the course between the FTF and blended groups (p=.319)."
