## Evidence

### Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah 2017

Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah. (2017). Effectiveness of using the iPad in Learning to Acquire the Mental and Performance Skills in Teaching Social Studies Curriculum. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v6n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

T-test comparison of post-test performance-skill scores in the same 48-student quasi-experiment: experimental mean 5.75 versus control 5.04, T-test value 2.967, significance level .005. Skills named include drawing, reading and determining maps, and drawing and analyzing charts and graphs. No effect size is printed.

> "there are statistically significant differences at the significance level (0.05) between the two experimental and the control groups in the performance skills in the post-test for the experimental group."
