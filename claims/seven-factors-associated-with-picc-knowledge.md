### Xu Binbin 2026

Xu Binbin, Wang Yan, Mo Yun, Zhang Rong, Chen Hongyan, Zhou Xuezhi, Hu Li. (2026). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge on peripherally inserted central catheter care in Guizhou, China: A web-based cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55786-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the full sample of 3528 nurses with variables selected from prior literature. The quote reports unstandardised regression coefficients B with 95% CIs for professional title, education, and PICC care experience; the article separately reports significant B for guidelines, training, training need, and hospital level.

> "Multiple regression analysis revealed that higher knowledge scores were significantly associated with being a supervision nurse/co-chief nurse/chief nurse (B = 4.910, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 3.744 to 6.077, p < 0.001), holding an undergraduate degree or higher (B = 1.845, 95% CI: 0.883 to 2.808, p < 0.001), and having experience in conducting PICC care (B = 5.700, 95% CI: 4.742 to 6.658, p < 0.001)"
