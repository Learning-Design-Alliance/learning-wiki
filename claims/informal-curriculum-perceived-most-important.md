Quon S, Riddell J, Bench KF, Roepke C, Burner E. (2022). Training Leaders in Trauma Resuscitation: Teacher and Learner Perspectives on Ideal Methods. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2021.5.51428

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the qualitative interview study of 25 providers, participants described the informal curriculum — role modeling, hands-on learning, reflection, and feedback — as crucial, and "Hands-on learning was viewed as the most effective method of training."

> "Interviewees identified informal curriculum as a crucial part of their training. Modeling by seniors and attending physicians allowed learners to develop their own style."
