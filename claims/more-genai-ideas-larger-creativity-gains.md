## Evidence

### Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser. (2023). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. https://aspredicted.org/136723

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Condition-comparison analysis in the results section of the randomized writer study: the five-idea condition beat both the no-access baseline and the one-idea condition on usefulness; novelty gains were 8.1% versus 5.4% respectively. The article concludes "having access to more AI ideas lead to more creative storytelling."

> "Having access to up to five AI ideas increased usefulness by 9.0% (b=0.453, p<0.001) over those with no GenAI access and 5.1% (p=0.0012, compared to the Human with 1 GenAI idea mean of 5.21) over those with access to one GenAI idea."
