# Hypermedia learners show the lowest total engagement in learning processes, devoting most processing to superficial activities

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learners in the hypermedia learning environment engaged in significantly fewer instances of superficial, elaborative, and regulative processes than learners in the self-explanation-based and inquiry environments. [→ Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong 2012](#tessa-h-s-eysink-ton-de-jong-2012)

## Evidence

### Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong 2012

Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong (2012). Does Instructional Approach Matter? How Elaboration Plays a Crucial Role in Multimedia Learning. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2011.611776

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Bonferroni-corrected Mann-Whitney pairwise comparisons from the think-aloud experiment, showing hypermedia learners engaged in fewer processes of all three main categories than self-explanation-based learners, with the same pattern against inquiry learners.

> "learners in the hypermedia learning environment engaged in signiﬁcantly fewer instances of superﬁcial, elaborative, and regulative processes than those in the self-explanation-based learning environment ( Z = –3.78, p < .001; Z = –3.78, p < .001; and Z = –2.80, p < .008, respectively)"
