Metcalfe 2023¶

Metcalfe, J., Vuorre, M., Towner, E., & Eich, T. S. (2023). Curiosity: The effects of feedback and confidence on the desire to know. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, vol. 152, no. 2, pp. 464-482. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0001284

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 2 multilevel regression of curiosity on confidence, feedback condition and accuracy. At confidence 10 the feedback-versus-no-feedback difference was near zero (difference at 10: −0.32), but "curiosity increased selectively and signi ﬁcantly" at confidence 90 (difference 17.28, pd = 100.0%). Experiment 1 (44 participants, errors only) similarly found a positive linear confidence-curiosity relation (bLinear = .24).

"However, curiosity increased selectively and signi ﬁcantly for errors that had been made with high con ﬁ- dence (difference at 90: 17.28, SE = 3.16, 95% CI [11.14, 23.5],"
