## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After ten weeks of SQ4R-based Turkish language instruction, the experimental group's reading comprehension post-test mean (4.08) was significantly higher than the control group's (2.94). [→ Başar 2017](#basar-2017)

## Evidence

### Başar 2017

Başar, M. & Gürbüz, M. (2017). Effect of the SQ4R Technique on the Reading Comprehension of Elementary School 4th Grade Elementary School Students. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 131-144. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_9.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test control-group quasi-experiment with 57 fourth-graders (26 experimental, 31 control); the experimental group received SQ4R instruction one hour per week for ten weeks. The article reports the post-test difference as "significant [ t(55)=2.531, p<.001]"; no effect size is printed.

> "the reading comprehension skills of experimental group students (Avg=4.08) is higher than that of the control group students (Avg=2.94). This difference between the two groups is found to be significant [ t(55)=2.531, p<.001]"
