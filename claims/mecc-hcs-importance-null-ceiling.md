## Evidence

### Parchment Amelia 2025

Parchment Amelia, Lawrence Wendy, Rahman Em, Townsend Nick, Wainwright Elaine, Wainwright David. (2025). Increasing confidence and competence in supporting behaviour change in physiotherapy practice using Making Every Contact Count Healthy Conversation Skills: a before and after evaluation. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon test on importance ratings (n = 23). The article reports medians (IQR) of 9 (8–10) pre-training and 9 (8–10) post-training, indicating a ceiling effect; no equivalence test was performed. The 6-month subset (n = 11) similarly showed no significant change (p = 0.336).

> "MECC HCS training did not, however, elicit a statistically significant change in the perceived importance of supporting behaviour change pre- post (Z = −0.643, p = 0.520) training (n = 23)."
