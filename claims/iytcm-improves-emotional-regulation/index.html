Reinke 2014¶

Hierarchical linear model analysis of the TOCA-C Poor Emotional Regulation subscale in the cluster randomized trial; the negative coefficient reflects reduced poor emotional regulation, reported as "emotional regulation (b = -0.16, p < .01, E.S. = -.14)". The article prints E.S. = -.14 but no standardized metric, so impact is coded null.
