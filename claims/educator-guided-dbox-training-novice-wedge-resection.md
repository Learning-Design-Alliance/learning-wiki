## Evidence

### Grossi Sarah 2023

Grossi Sarah, Cattoni Maria, Rotolo Nicola, Imperatori Andrea. (2023). Video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery simulation and training: a comprehensive literature review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04482-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review narrates Bjurstrom and collaborators' study of the D-Box Basic Simulator with three scenarios of increasing fidelity, with and without a dedicated educator, assessed by two blinded thoracoscopic experts using a modified validated tool; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Intensive simulator training with a dedicated educator has been shown to enable novices to perform an acceptable wedge resection in a simulation model. Moreover, although the difference observed in the final score was not significant, it should be underlined that the presence of the educator during the training led to a positive effect"
