## Evidence

### Koedel 2015

Koedel, C., Mihaly, K., & Rockoff, J. E. (2015). Value-Added Modeling: A Review. Preprint, forthcoming at Economics of Education Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Value-Added+Modeling+A+Review+Koedel

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Random-assignment experiment in which students were assigned to teachers within pairs or blocks; pre-period VAM estimates predicted randomly assigned students' scores. The review reports coefficients of "0.85 and 0,99"-equivalent values as printed (0.85 and 0.99), "Neither coefficient can be statistically distinguished from one"; Kane et al. (2013) obtain analogous estimates of 1.04 and 0.70.

> "Kane and Staiger (2008) estimate coefficients on the non-experimental value-added estimates in math and reading of 0.85 and 0.99, respectively, for their models that are closest to what we show in equations (4) and (5)."
