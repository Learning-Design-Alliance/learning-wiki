## Evidence

### Watanabe K 2013

Watanabe K, Nagata N, Shimbo T, Nakashima R, Furuhata E, Sakurai T, Akazawa N, Yokoi C, Kobayakawa M, Akiyama J, Mizokami M, Uemura N. (2013). Accuracy of endoscopic diagnosis of Helicobacter pylori infection according to level of endoscopic experience and the effect of training. BMC Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-230X-13-128

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post McNemar-test comparison of the two beginners' diagnostic yield before and after two years of training. The study reports "a significant increase" for beginner 1 on uninfected and eradicated statuses; increases for beginner 2 and for infected status were not significant.

> "A significant increase was noted in diagnostic yield of H. pylori-uninfected (82.6% and 88.6%, p < 0.05) and H. pylori-eradicated (37.5% and 46.2%, p < 0.05) in beginner 1."
