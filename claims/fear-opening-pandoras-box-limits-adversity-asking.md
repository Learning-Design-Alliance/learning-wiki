### Loveday Sarah 2024

Loveday Sarah, Chen Lingling, Constable Leanne N., Kabir Ashraful, White Natalie, Goldfeld Sharon, Sanci Lena, Hiscock Harriet. (2024). Opening Pandora's box - key facilitators of practice change in detecting and responding to childhood adversity - a practitioner perspective. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-04918-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme from reflexive thematic analysis of interviews with 21 practitioners; one participant said "I limit what I ask sometimes because as soon as you open the questions, you get Pandoras' box". The article identifies this as a barrier theme.

> "This fear led to practitioners limiting both what adversities they asked about and the number of adversities they asked about to try to decrease the chance of having more unmet social needs that they can help with."
