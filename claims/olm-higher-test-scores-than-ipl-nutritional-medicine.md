## Evidence

### Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael 2026

Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael, Eilers Jantje, Herrmann Hans Joachim, Neurath Markus Friedrich, Kohl Matthias, Zopf Yurdagül, Burgard Leonie Cordelia. (2026). In-Person vs. Virtual: A Comparative Study of Teaching Methods in Nutritional Medicine. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18050821

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled single post-test trial with 78 students completing the test (OLM n = 40, IPL n = 38). The permutation Welch t-test showed "a medium effect size (Cohen's d = 0.65)" favoring the OLM group; the Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney test confirmed the difference (r = 0.34).

> "The mean difference was 2.4 points (95% CI [0.7, 4.0]; t = −2.89, p = 0.004), corresponding to a medium effect size (Cohen's d = 0.65)."
