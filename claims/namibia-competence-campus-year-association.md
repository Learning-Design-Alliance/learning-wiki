`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square cross-tabulations in the results section of the cross-sectional survey of 272 students. Campus A students were more likely to report competence than campus B, and fourth-year students more likely than earlier years; age (p = 0.364) and gender (p = 0.764) were not significant.
