## Evidence

### Connolly 2018

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal observational study surveying 1445 early-career STEM scholars (67% response in 2011) with OLS regression. Covariate-adjusted comparisons show "TD participants were more confident than nonparticipants in course planning and teaching methods"; no effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "Even after controlling for relevant covariates, we found that TD participants were more confident than nonparticipants in course planning and teaching methods."
