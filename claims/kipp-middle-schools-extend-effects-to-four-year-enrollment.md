## Evidence

### Ira Nichols-Barrer 2022

Ira Nichols-Barrer, Maria Bartlett, Thomas Coen, Philip Gleason. (2022). The Impacts on College Enrollment of a Charter Network Serving Disadvantaged Students: Evidence from KIPP Middle School Lotteries. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness (online ahead of print). https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impacts-on-college-enrollment-of-a-charter-network-serving-disadvantaged-students-evidence

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The study's headline finding is that the positive effects of KIPP middle schools extend beyond middle school achievement to rates of enrollment in 4-year college programs, estimated with lottery-based random assignment across 13 schools.

> "Building on prior studies of KIPP that show KIPP middle schools have strong positive effects on middle school achievement, we find that KIPP middle schools also positively affect rates of enrollment in 4-year college programs."
