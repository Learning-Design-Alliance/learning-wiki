Evidence¶

Marlene Bönstrup 2020¶

Marlene Bönstrup, Iñaki Iturrate, Martin N. Hebart, Nitzan Censor and Leonardo G. Cohen. (2020). Mechanisms of offline motor learning at a microscale of seconds in large-scale crowdsourced data. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0066-9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 3: 181 participants trained 48 trials of alternating 5 s practice and 10 s rest, compared with the 10 s practice group. Micro-offline gains remained substantial in both groups (5 s: 1.89 ± 0.23; 10 s: 1.48 ± 0.17 keypresses/s, P < 0.001), inconsistent with a substantial contribution of motor slowing.

"No signi ﬁcant differences in total early learning or microscale learning were evident ( P > 0.05) and microscale learning between groups was statistically equivalent (TOST for equivalence)."
