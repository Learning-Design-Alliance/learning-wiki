## Evidence

### Moreno-Murcia 2018

Moreno-Murcia, J.A., Huéscar Hernández, E., & Ruiz, L. (2018). Perceptions of Controlling Teaching Behaviors and the Effects on the Motivation and Behavior of High School Physical Education Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15102288

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (maximum likelihood, AMOS 21.0) on data from 416 students; each proposed relationship attained significance. Table 1 correlations: motivation–importance r = 0.43, importance–intention r = 0.53, both p < 0.001.

> "Global intrinsic motivation was positively associated with the perceived importance of physical education which, in turn, explained the intention to be physically active."
