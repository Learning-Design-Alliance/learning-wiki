### Walters 2025

Walters, K., & Kopcha, T. J. (2025). Negotiating social justice issues in research and practice in the field of learning, design, and technology: a multiple case study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10542-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative multiple case study (Stake's method) of four LDT scholars, using interviews and published documents coded structurally and in-vivo. The findings section reports that participants described "an ongoing effort to create balance between three primary entities" in their social justice work.

> "Each described their work as an ongoing effort to create balance between three primary entities—themselves and their own perspective, the needs of the project or project stakeholders, and the perspectives of their team members."
