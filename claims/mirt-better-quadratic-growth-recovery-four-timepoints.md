### Kuhfeld 2020

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simulation Study 2 generated data for an eight-item measure with N=2,000 simulated respondents across four timepoints under a quadratic growth model, with 100 replications in flexMIRT. Table 5 reports means, variances, and covariances for intercept, slope, and quadratic terms.

> "In general, the MIRT model marginally outperforms the sum score and cross-sectional models for the fixed effect estimates of the slope and quadratic means. Whereas the MIRT model overstates the linear slope mean slightly, the other three approaches tend to understate the slope."
