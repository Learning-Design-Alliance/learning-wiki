### Zeng Yanglin 2025

Zeng Yanglin, Wang Ya, Dong Bo, Liu Liu, Cao Yubin, Huang Dingming, Wang Shida, Zheng Qinghua. (2025). Effectiveness of “colored teeth and digital evaluation” on preclinical tooth preparation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07963-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two academic-year cohorts (traditional n = 231, digital n = 233) in preclinical training. The digital group's mean exam score was "85.85 ± 7.791" versus "82.73 ± 8.465" (p < 0.05, Mann–Whitney U test).

> "Quantitative analysis of final assessments on standard tooth revealed significantly higher scores in the digital group (85.85 ± 7.791) compared to the traditional group (82.73 ± 8.465) (p < 0.05)"
