`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility outcomes (RQ1a) of the pilot trial: IMI motivation 5.6 vs 5.2 (p = 0.47), SUS usability 77.7 vs 73.2 (p = 0.60), and cybersickness in 9 of 22 participants (41%) plus one more severe case. The article reports these descriptively and via mixed models.

> "Nine of 22 participants (41%) experienced mild, transient cyber sickness, and one (5%) discontinued immersion due to more severe symptoms but continued the training by observing the scenarios on a screen"
