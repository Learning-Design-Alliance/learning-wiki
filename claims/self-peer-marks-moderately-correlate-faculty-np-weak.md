Fitzgerald K, Vaughan B. (2018). Learning through multiple lenses: analysis of self, peer, nearpeer, and faculty assessments of a clinical history-taking task in Australia. J Educ Eval Health Prof. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2018.15.22

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study in which year 2 osteopathy students (n= 9), NPs (n= 3), and faculty (n= 5) marked the same video assessment. Spearman rho analysis found "Correlations between self and peer (r= 0.38) and self and faculty (r= 0.43) marks were moderate," with the self–NP correlation weak.

> "Correlations between self and peer (r= 0.38) and self and faculty (r= 0.43) marks were moderate. A weak correlation was observed between self and NP marks (r= 0.25)."
