What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Program™. July 16, 2007. http://www.pearsondigital.com/pdfs/werp/researchsummary-werp.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC analysis of the comprehension domain in Hecht & Close (2002): one outcome, the Stanford-Binet Fourth Edition Vocabulary subtest, with 76 students across six schools. The effect size was "neither statistically significant nor substantively important" (improvement index +4 percentile points).

"The study authors examined one vocabulary development outcome (the Vocabulary subtest of Stanford-Binet, Fourth Edition) and reported no statistically significant effect. The effect size was neither statistically significant nor substantively important."
