## Evidence

### Bortes C 2020

Bortes C, Strandh M, Nilsson K. (2020). Sibling Ill Health and Children's Educational Outcomes. Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.12887

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 4 of the linear regression on the register cohort tested three interaction terms (one per parental-education level above compulsory); none was significant. The logistic models showed the same null interaction. No effect sizes are printed for the interactions.

> "In model 4 we introduced the interaction terms "sibling ill health × parent's level of education." No significant interaction effects between having had a sibling with ill health and parental level of education were found."
