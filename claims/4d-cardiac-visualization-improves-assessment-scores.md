### Owolabi Joshua O. 2025

Owolabi Joshua O., Gardner Katresa, Agboola Rasheed, Yesudas Rekha R., Shaw Jennifer H. (2025). Use of simulation for teaching biomedical sciences to undergraduate medical students- a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07819-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Study of 97 (88%) of 110 Swedish medical students using a 4D model combining 3D anatomy with blood flow simulation and heart-beating actions; "significantly improved mean score (18.1 ± 4.5 vs 20.3 ± 4.9, p = 0.002)". No effect size printed.

> "Summative assessments indicated a significantly improved mean score (18.1 ± 4.5 vs 20.3 ± 4.9, p = 0.002); partly attributed to enhanced knowledge of myocardial hypertrophy and pressure–velocity differences over a stenotic valve."
