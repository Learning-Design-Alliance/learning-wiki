### Fornander L 2024

Fornander L, Garrido Granhagen M, Molin I, Laukkanen K, Björnström Karlsson K, Berggren P, Nilsson L. (2024). The use of specific coordination behaviours to manage information processing and task distribution in real and simulated trauma teamwork: an observational study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-024-01287-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational coding of 2476 verbatim transcribed utterances from four video-recorded real trauma resuscitations using the modified CoMet-E category system. The article reports that "64% of utterances was related to information management and 26% to task management", with relational communication at 6%.

> "In the IRL domain, 64% of utterances was related to information management and 26% to task management. Relational communication constituted a minor proportion, 6%."
