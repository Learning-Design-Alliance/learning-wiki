### E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald 2017

E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald. (2017). Robotic Telescopes in Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264](https://doi.org/10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review assessment of the evidence base for colour imaging, described as a gateway activity using authentic tools such as FITS Liberator and GIMP or Photoshop. The review reports no efficacy study, so this is a documented gap rather than a measured effect.

> "there has not been a great deal of research on the eﬃcacy, in terms of knowledge gain or engagement, on the student. It is a commonly held belief that colour imaging has merit amongst most projects and groups and, while unlikely to be shown dramatically false, this assertion still needs to be validated."
