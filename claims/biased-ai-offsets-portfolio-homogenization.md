## Evidence

### Lai 2025

Lai, S., Choi, J., Kim, J., Kunievsky, N., Potter, Y., & Evans, J. (2025). Biased AI improves human performance but reduces perceived helpfulness. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.09297](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.09297)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Investment-experiment diversity analysis using mean pairwise L1 distance between participants' normalized asset weights. Default assistance homogenized portfolios; risk-biased assistance offset most of that homogenization, leaving diversity statistically indistinguishable from the pre-interaction baseline (−0.015, adj. p = 0.117). No standardized effect size printed.

> "default assistance sharply reduced cross-participant portfolio diversity (Δ = −0.175, [−0.207, −0.142], adj. p < 0.001), whereas risk-averse assistance offset 36% of that homogenization (difference = 0.062, adj. p = 0.006) and risk-seeking assistance 91% (difference = 0.160, adj. p < 0.001)"
