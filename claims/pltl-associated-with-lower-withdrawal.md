Snyder JJ, Sloane JD, Dunk RDP, Wiles JR. (2016). Peer-Led Team Learning Helps Minority Students Succeed. PLoS Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002398

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of course withdrawal by PLTL attendance across all students whose URM status could be determined (n = 479), reported in the Our Findings section. The article reports significantly higher withdrawal among non-attendees; no effect size is printed.
