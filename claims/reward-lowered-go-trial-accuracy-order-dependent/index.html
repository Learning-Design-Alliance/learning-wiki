Evidence¶

Sinopoli 2011¶

Sinopoli, K.J., Schachar, R., & Dennis, M. (2011). Reward Improves Cancellation and Restraint Inhibition Across Childhood and Adolescence. Developmental Psychology, 47(5), 1479-89. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0024440

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated measures ANOVA of go-trial accuracy across reward conditions and tasks. The article reports a significant reward effect with partial η2=0.079; only a test statistic is printed, so no effect size magnitude is coded.

"Accuracy on the go task was significantly affected by reward (linear effect, F(2, 78)= 3.352, p=0.040, partial η2=0.079), with greater percent correct in the NO condition relative to the LOW and HIGH conditions (see Table 3)."
