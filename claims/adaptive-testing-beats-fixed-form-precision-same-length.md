### MAP Growth Technical Report for 2024–2025 2026

MAP Growth Technical Report for 2024–2025. (2026). HMH Education Company. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-technical-report/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The technical report's design overview states this precision advantage for the computer adaptive format, noting "a fixed-form test could achieve similar precision only with a longer and more time-consuming test." No effect size or study statistic is printed for this assertion.

> "The adaptive nature of the assessment produces better measurement precision throughout the range of the scale than a traditional fixed-form assessment of the same length; a fixed-form test could achieve similar precision only with a longer and more time-consuming test."

### Meyer 2022

No data or effect size is reported.
