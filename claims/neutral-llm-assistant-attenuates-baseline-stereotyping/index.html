q2 · i? · causal · r2

SCR comparison across the three conditions of the 123-participant experiment; control and neutral were statistically indistinguishable (p=.656, h=0.12), so the attenuation suggestion is descriptive rather than a confirmed difference.

"The neutral condition produced the lowest SCR (0.390), even below the control (0.450), suggesting a well-configured LLM may attenuate baseline stereotyping."
