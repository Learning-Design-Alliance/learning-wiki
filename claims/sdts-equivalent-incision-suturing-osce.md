## Evidence

### Liu Boqiang 2026

Liu Boqiang, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Lu Chen, Zheng Bin, Yang Jin. (2026). Self-directed teaching system driven by video analysis feedback improves surgical skill training for clinical interns: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09104-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 56 surgical interns comparing SDTS (n = 31) with traditional teaching (n = 25) on an OSCE incision/suturing module. The article reports "There was no significant difference between the post-training test scores of the two groups (Fig. 4A)" with P > 0.05.

> "The SDTS group achieved a post-training test score of 91.65 ± 4.98, with an average improvement of 15.26 ± 8.34."
