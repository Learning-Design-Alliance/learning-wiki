Definitional/descriptive statement in the repayment section about federal loan program rules: "Parent borrowers, however, can only access an IDR plan by consolidating their loan into a Direct Loan" and only ICR, the least generous plan. Without consolidating, parents are limited to standard, graduated, or extended plans based on total owed and years in repayment.

> "Parent borrowers, however, can only access an IDR plan by consolidating their loan into a Direct Loan and they may only access the income-contingent repayment (ICR) plan, which is the least generous of all the plans."
