### Loose T 2025

Loose T, Collet O, Nuyt AM, Goulet-Pelletier JC, Worrell FC, Côté S, Luu TM. (2025). Long-Term Educational Outcomes of Individuals Born Preterm. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.34918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression on the Quebec birth-cohort case-control sample with term births as reference; after full adjustment the extremely preterm OR was 1.68 (99% CI, 1.39-2.02) for not obtaining a university degree, in the article's words "at highest odds of not obtaining a university degree." No standardized effect size is printed.

> "individuals born extremely preterm were at highest odds of not obtaining a university degree (OR, 1.64 [99% CI, 1.40-1.92]), followed by very preterm (OR, 1.31 [99% CI, 1.23-1.39]) and moderate-to-late preterm (OR, 1.15 [99% CI, 1.12-1.18])"
