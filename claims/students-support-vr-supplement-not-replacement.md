## Evidence

### Hammouda Sana Ben 2025

Hammouda Sana Ben, Maoua Maher, Bouchahma Majed. (2025). The effectiveness of VR-based human anatomy simulation training for undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07402-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item from the 179-student study contrasting two attitudes: support for VR integration (93%) versus belief in full replacement of physical labs (51%). The article reports these as descriptive percentages with no test statistic.

> "When asked about the overall usefulness of VR as a learning tool, 93% of students (59% strongly agree + 34% agree, as shown in Fig. 10) supported integrating VR scenarios into their curriculum, recognizing its value in enhancing engagement and improving their understanding of complex topics. However, only 51% (29% strongly agree + 22% agree, as shown in Fig. 10 believed that VR simulations could fully replace physical lab sessions."
