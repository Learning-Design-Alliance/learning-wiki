Evidence¶

Gandolfi 2021¶

Gandolfi, E., Traverso, L., Zanobini, M., Usai, M. C., & Viterbori, P. (2021). The longitudinal relationship between early inhibitory control skills and emergent literacy in preschool children. Reading and Writing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-021-10131-y

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Zero-order and age-partialled Pearson correlations among Time 1 measures in the final sample of 50 toddlers (28–36 months). The two inhibitory tasks correlated with each other (r = 0.382, p < 0.012; age-adjusted r = 0.335, p < 0.032), but only the Fish task retained a significant association with expressive vocabulary after age adjustment (r = 0.454).

"Only the interference suppression task (the Fish task) correlated with the expressive vocabulary measure (r = 0.478, p < 0.001).) when age was partialled out (r = 0.454, p < 0.003). Differently, the response inhibition task (the Circle Drawing task) correlated moderately with expressive vocabulary (r = 0.307, p < 0.048) and this association did not remain significant after controlling for age (r = 0.246, p < 0.122)."
