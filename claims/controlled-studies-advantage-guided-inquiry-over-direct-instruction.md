The authors' revisit of the literature cites the Minner et al. (2010) research synthesis of controlled comparisons: "55% showed a significant advantage of inquiry-based learning" while only one study (2%) favored the less inquiry-focused condition. No effect size is printed for this synthesis.

> "For example, 42 of the studies in the review by Minner et al. (2010) involved comparisons between conditions with various degrees of inquiry saturation. Of these, 55% showed a significant advantage of inquiry-based learning, and only 1 (2%) showed an advantage for a less inquiry-focused condition."
