## Evidence

### Bock Anna 2021

Bock Anna, Kniha Kristian, Goloborodko Evgeny, Lemos Martin, Rittich Anne Barbara, Möhlhenrich Stephan Christian, Rafai Nicole, Hölzle Frank, Modabber Ali. (2021). Effectiveness of face-to-face, blended and e-learning in teaching the application of local anaesthesia: a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02569-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous end-of-course evaluation on a ten-point Likert scale (1 = fully agree/satisfactory) completed by all 37 participants. "They indicated their high satisfaction with the teaching approach" in the blended group, with all three groups rating the course highly.

> "They indicated their high satisfaction with the teaching approach (mean score = 1.62, SD = 0.49). The e-learning group (mean score = 1.67, SD = 0.47) and the lecture group (mean score = 1.58, SD = 0.64) expressed their high satisfaction with the course as well."
