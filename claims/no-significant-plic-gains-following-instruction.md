## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, and N. G. Holmes. (2019). Assessment of critical thinking in physics labs. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Paired t-tests on matched pre/post PLIC scores from 726 students (FY d = 0.10, p = 0.064; BFY and Grad non-significant; Table I). The article reports "the effect sizes are small or very small (Cohen's d ≤ 0.1)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "For all three groups of students, the differences between the pre- and post-instruction means are not statistically significant and the effect sizes are small or very small (Cohen's d ≤ 0.1)."
