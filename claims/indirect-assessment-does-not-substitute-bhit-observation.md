Lelli Danny A., Rourke Ryan, Tse Darren. (2024). Competence of Senior Otolaryngology Residents with the Bedside Head Impulse Test—Has There Been Improvement After 5 Years of Competency By Design? Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1177/19160216241288817

Pearson correlations among the three assessment forms in the 30-resident cohort. The quote reports r = .52 for MCQ versus bHIT rating and r = .11 for video interpretation versus bHIT rating.

> "The scores on the MCQ quiz had fair correlation with mean bHIT rating scores (Pearson r = .52), but there was no correlation between video interpretation and mean bHIT rating scores (Pearson r = .11)."
