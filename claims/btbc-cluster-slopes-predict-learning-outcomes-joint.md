## Evidence

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation between NMF-clustered BtBC slopes and behavioural learning slopes, significant only for the joint context: Cluster 1 BtBC slope correlated negatively with RT slope, r = −0.39, P = 0.043.

> "the slope of BtBC within NMF-derived Cluster 1 (see Fig. 3e) was negatively correlated with the slope of RT ( r = −0.39, P = 0.043; see Fig. 3f), indi- cating that the increasing trend in BtBC (i.e., a more positive slope) within Cluster 1 predicted the decreasing trend in RT"

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024 (2)

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation in the joint context between Cluster 2 (parietal–parietal) BtBC slopes and intra-brain functional-connectivity slopes: r = 0.44, P = 0.021, with correlations observed only for the joint context.

> "the slope of BtBC within NMF-derived Cluster 2 (see Fig. 3g) was positively correlated with the slope of intra-brain functional connectivity ( r = 0.44, P = 0.021; see Fig. 3h), indicating that the increasing trend in BtBC (i.e., the more positive slope) within Cluster 2 predicted the increasing trend in intra-brain functional connectivity"
