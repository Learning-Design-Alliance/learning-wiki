Evidence¶

Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl 2021¶

Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl. (2021). Young children's embodied interactions with a social robot. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09978-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Observational finding from Study One's song activity in which the robot used its wheels to move on the floor while singing about colors and shapes; children mimicked its behaviors and repositioned to stay in its line of sight. The authors summarize that "the children's learning space was fluid."

"In summary, in the learning activities afforded by the robot, the children's learning space was fluid, and their engagement was mobile."
