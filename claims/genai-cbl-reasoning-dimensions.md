## Evidence

### Waseem J 2026

Waseem J, Ritter Nicola L, McMordie J, Su P, Hu M, Chen C, Xu S. (2026). Generative AI–Assisted Progressive-Disclosure Case-Based Learning for Clinical Reasoning in Occupational Medicine: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/103584

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subdimensional analysis of the case-analysis score across 4 reasoning dimensions. Three dimensions showed large effect sizes (d≈1.10, 0.89, 0.80); treatment planning did not differ (P=.19).

> "the intervention group outperformed the control group in information gathering (mean 22.66, SD 1.87 vs mean 20.46, SD 2.13), hypothesis generation (mean 22.28, SD 1.99 vs mean 20.47, SD 2.06), and differential diagnosis (mean 22.34, SD 1.96 vs mean 20.72, SD 2.09), with all differences reaching statistical significance (all P<.001)"
