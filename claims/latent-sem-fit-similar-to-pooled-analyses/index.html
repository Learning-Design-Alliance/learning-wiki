Fit statistics from the latent cross-lagged model on pooled parallel data from Studies 1, 2, 5, and 6: the model "yielded a good fit to the data" with CFI = .94, RMSEA = .08, SRMR = .04, and its "coefficients are highly similar" to the main manuscript's pooled analyses. Fit thresholds (RMSEA/SRMR ≤ .08, CFI ≥ .90) are cited from Kline (2015).

"This model yielded a good fit to the data, χ2 (84) = 3010.04, p < .001, CFI = .94, RMSEA = .08, SRMR = .04. The findings are shown in Figure S1. The coefficients are highly similar to those derived in the pooled analyses in the main manuscript."
