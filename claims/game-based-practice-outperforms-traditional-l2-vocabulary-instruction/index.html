Claims

Newly arrived migrant children taught Italian through games showed larger vocabulary gains than traditionally taught peers on a vocabulary checklist inventory, not a direct test.

Ukrainian children aged 6–7 taught Italian by games reached 171.91 words against 139.29 for traditional instruction over four weeks, as counted on the MacArthur–Bates CDI checklist in non-randomised groups — a population that transfers to almost nothing.
