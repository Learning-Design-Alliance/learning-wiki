# Competence and relatedness show distinct content pathways: competence with capability indicators (self-efficacy, progress, resilience) and relatedness with interpersonal-climate indicators (collegial support, trust, social capital)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the C3 contrast, competence was more strongly associated with capability-focused indicators while relatedness was more strongly related to interpersonal-climate indicators. [→ Marsh 2025](#marsh-2025)

Orthogonal-contrast GLM (C3: competence vs relatedness) over the 64-variable nomological network from the Australian Principal OHS&W Survey. The article reports "competence is more strongly associated with capability-focused indicators" and relatedness with interpersonal-climate indicators.

> "C3 exhibits interpretable content distinctions: competence is more strongly associated with capability-focused indicators (e.g., self-efficacy, progress/goal attainment, resilience), whereas relatedness is more strongly related with interpersonal-climate indicators (e.g., support from colleagues, collegial trust, social capital)."
