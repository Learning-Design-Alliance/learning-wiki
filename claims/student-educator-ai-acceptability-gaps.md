## Evidence

### Skulmowski 2024

Skulmowski, A. (2024). Placebo or Assistant? Generative AI Between Externalization and Anthropomorphization. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 58. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09894-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The essay reports, citing Barrett and Pack (2023), a direct comparison of university students' and educators' assessments across six subtasks of academic writing, in which "educators found it far more unacceptable to use generative AI in the brainstorming phase without acknowledgment". No effect size is printed.

> "The educators found it far more unacceptable to use generative AI in the brainstorming phase without acknowledgment than the students did."

### Skulmowski 2024 (2)

Skulmowski, A. (2024). Placebo or Assistant? Generative AI Between Externalization and Anthropomorphization. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 58. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09894-x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The essay reports, citing Barrett and Pack (2023), that for outlining "students found it more acceptable to use an AI to come up with initial ideas" than educators, while for essay writing and revision assessments were similar but educators disagreed more strongly. No effect size is printed.

> "For outlining a paper, students found it more acceptable to use an AI to come up with initial ideas and to use an AI tool with or without acknowledgment compared with the educators."
