## Evidence

### Loturco 2022

Loturco, I.; Montoya, N.P.; Ferraz, M.B.; Berbat, V.; Pereira, L.A. (2022). A Systematic Review of the Effects of Physical Activity on Specific Academic Skills of School Students. Educ. Sci. 2022, 12, 134. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci12020134

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effect meta-analysis of 31 included studies, split into subgroups of 4 language-test, 10 reading-assessment, and 9 composite-score studies. The review reports the interventions "were not capable of signiﬁcantly improving academic achievement in language, reading, and composite scores." No effect size values are printed for these null results.

> "The sub-group analyses revealed that the interventions were not capable of signiﬁcantly improving academic achievement in language, reading, and composite scores."
