## Evidence

### Okuyama K 2025

Okuyama K, Lönn SL, Khoshnood AM, Assari S, Sundquist J, Sundquist K. (2025). School performance gap between non-immigrant and second-generation immigrant children in Sweden—time trends and contributing factors. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1521387

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed models on Swedish register data: model 2 added parental education and neighborhood socioeconomic status, model 3 added parental mental disorders measured during compulsory school years. The female gap coefficient shrank stepwise, showing partial attenuation at each step; a residual gap remained.

> "the gap in school performance between non-immigrant and second-generation immigrant children was partially explained by differences in parental education and neighborhood socioeconomic status, i.e., the coefficient was attenuated from −11.14 to −7.20. This gap was slightly further explained by adding parental mental disorders in the third model; here, the coefficient was attenuated from −7.20 to −6.37."
