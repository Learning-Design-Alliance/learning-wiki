## Evidence

### Verhoeven 2019

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of 11 exclusively empirical studies (nine qualitative, one quantitative, one mixed-methods) on peer norms, based on ethnographic methods. In one programming-class study, peers refused to regard a student as the attentive expert his teacher had made available to him.

> "were concerned with and found that peers may deny each other access to certain identity positions through peer norms."

### Verhoeven 2019 (2)

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that in one middle-school study students feared the social consequences of engaging in class as someone becoming a good reader, because classmates reinforced the norm that it is embarrassing to have reading difficulties, so they hid their reading difficulties.

> "when adolescents actually do identify themselves with identity positions that are stigmatized by their peers, they may hide that they do in order to safeguard their reputation."
