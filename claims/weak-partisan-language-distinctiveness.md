## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A LASSO language classifier predicted ideology only weakly, with the relationship stronger in American Government than Saving Schools. [→ Yeomans 2018](#yeomans-2018)

## Evidence

### Yeomans 2018

Yeomans, M., Stewart, B. M., Mavon, K., Kindel, A., Tingley, D., & Reich, J. (2018). The Civic Mission of MOOCs: Engagement across Political Differences in Online Forums. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28, 553–589. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0161-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supervised learning analysis using penalized linear regression (LASSO, glmnet) with nested ten-fold cross-validation on 4,516 and 5,452 posts. Predicted-actual partisanship correlation "is not especially strong": rτ = .179 in American Government and rτ = .077 in Saving Schools, both p < .001.

> "However the relation- ship between predicted and actual partisanship is not especially strong. Additionally, it seems to be somewhat stronger in American Government (r τ = .179, z(5452) = 19, p < .001), while it is weaker in Saving Schools (r τ = .077, z(4516) = 7.7, p < .001)."
