## Evidence

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

BORIS-coded analysis of all TA-group interactions across the seven groups, using full-class video and TA-worn microphone audio, with 100% interrater agreement on a 10-minute reliability sample. No pattern distinguished the groups.

> "Overall, there was no clear pattern in either the num- ber of interactions or the time or content of those discus- sions among groups that did and did not problematize."

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021 (2)

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phase 2 coding of TA interaction time (Fig. 7) showed interpretation-of-results discussion ranged from "a low of 15 seconds (Group D) to a high of 650 seconds (Group F)"; Group D problematized while Group F did not.

> "the time spent discussing interpretation of results varying from a low of 15 seconds (Group D) to a high of 650 seconds (Group F)."
