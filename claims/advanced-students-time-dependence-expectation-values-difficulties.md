### Singh 2010

Singh, C. and Zhu, G. (2010). Surveying Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of responses on QMS Question (2), in which a 1D infinite square well is in the first excited state; the item asked which expectation values depend on time. "The most common mistake in Question (2)" was believing <x> and <p> depend on time in a stationary state. No effect size is printed.

> "The most common mistake in Question (2) was that the students believed that the expectation values of position and momentum depend on time in a stationary state."

### Singh 2010 (2)

Singh, C. and Zhu, G. (2010). Surveying Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response analysis of QMS Question (23), where a 1D simple harmonic oscillator is in a superposition of stationary states; the correct answer is D. "18% of the students mistakenly thought that all the expectation values" depend on time and 14% chose C. Descriptive percentages only.

> "In Question (23), 18% of the students mistakenly thought that all the expectation values (position, momentum and energy) depend on time and 14% chose option C (only <H> depends on time) which is the opposite to the correct answer D"
