Findings from the RPIP feedback sessions with 10 educators, reported as educator perceptions of FH2T's curricular alignment. One educator noted FH2T "effectively incorporates the Standards for Mathematical Practice" in contrast to rote practice problems.

> "FH2T's mathematics content effectively incorporates the Standards for Mathematical Practice, aligning with recommended classroom learning approaches by encouraging students to reason abstractly, attend to precision, make sense of problems and persevere in solving them, and make use of structure"
