Definitional statement from the theoretical framework section, grounded in Nelson and Narens' monitoring (Processes→Structures) and control (Structures→Processes) flows. Bidirectionality means both nodes take turns influencing each other over time, with consequences for causal modeling.

"three possible internal arrangements: P→S (bottom-up monitoring dominates), S→P (top-down control dominates), or P⇄S (fully integrated bidirectional processing)."
