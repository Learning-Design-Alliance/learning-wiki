## Evidence

### Fathima Ameen 2026

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Follow-up comparison across the median-split groups (Table 3) showed low-prior high-%KBR students' post-test scores were "statistically equivalent to those of their high-prior peers", which the authors interpret as compensation for lower prior knowledge.

> "Notably, their post-test scores were statistically equivalent to those of their high-prior peers, indicating that produc- ing a high percentage of knowledge-building responses may have compensated for their initial disadvantage in prior knowledge."
