## Evidence

### Vignal 2022

Vignal, M., & Wilcox, B. R. (2022). Investigating Unprompted and Prompted Diagrams Generated by Physics Majors During Problem Solving. arXiv. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Fisher Exact tests with Monte Carlo-simulated p-values, Bonferroni-adjusted to a 0.005 threshold, compared correctness across four cohorts (19 students, 331 problems); the graduate cohort's higher correctness reached significance at "FE p = 0.017".

> "we found that graduate students were the most likely group to select a correct answer (78.7% of the time), compared to seniors (55.1%), juniors (55.8%), and lower-division students (61.2%). That graduate students answered more questions correctly than did any other cohort is statistically significant (FE p = 0.017), but the diﬀerences between the other groups were not."
