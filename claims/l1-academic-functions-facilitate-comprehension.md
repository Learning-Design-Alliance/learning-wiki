## Evidence

### Yıldız 2021

Yıldız, M. (2021). Language choices of language teachers and learners: Meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 17(Special Issue 1), 472-492. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1285162

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis finding across studies including Sali (2014), Al Masaeed (2016), Bilgin (2016), Cai & Cook (2015), and others. The article reports L1 is used for "explaining some aspects of the target language" to avoid confusion and mislearning, particularly for low-proficient students. Qualitative evidence; no effect size.

> "Besides helping the students comprehend the intended meaning, L1 use may also serve as scaffolding for language learners especially when their language proficiency is not advanced"
