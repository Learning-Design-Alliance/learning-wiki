## Evidence

### Knoop‐van Campen CAN 2018

Knoop‐van Campen CAN, Segers E, Verhoeven L. (2018). The modality and redundancy effects in multimedia learning in children with dyslexia. Dyslexia. 2018;24:140–155. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1585

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summary of the repeated-measures ANCovas on retention and transfer scores (short and long term) in the experiment with 26 children with dyslexia and 38 typically developing peers; e.g., modality retention condition effect F(1, 60) = 0.09, p = .765, η2 p = .002, with no two- or three-way interactions (ps > .10).
