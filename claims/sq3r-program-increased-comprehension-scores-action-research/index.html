Bulut 2017¶

Aydın Bulut. (2017). Improving 4th Grade Primary School Students' Reading Comprehension Skills. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(1): 23-30. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050103

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Pre–during–post test administration of 20-question Reading Comprehension Tests (texts equated at .96 correlation) in a qualitative action research study with 7 fourth graders. All students' scores rose across the three administrations; the authors note Enes, Güler and Şevval "increased greatly but a similar improvement was not observed in Umut's scores."
