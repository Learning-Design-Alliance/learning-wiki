## Evidence

### Yasuda Yukinori 2026

Yasuda Yukinori, Takehara Shoichiro, Inoue Soichiro. (2026). Quantitative analysis of gaze and body movement differences by proficiency in direct and video laryngoscope intubation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44432-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-novice t-test comparing minimum head-to-mouth distance between device types in eight novices; the video laryngoscope value (0.457 ± 0.069 m) exceeded the direct laryngoscope value (0.331 ± 0.055 m), "a statistically significant difference".

> "The result revealed a statistically significant difference [t (14) = 4.355, p = 0.001]."
