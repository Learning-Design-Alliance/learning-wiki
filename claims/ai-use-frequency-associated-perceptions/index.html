Evidence¶

Ciğercci FM and Uygun N 2026¶

Ciğercci FM and Uygun N (2026). Evaluation of primary school teachers' use and perceptions of artificial intelligence in primary school mathematics instruction: a mixed-methods study. Front. Psychol. 17:1741116. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1741116

q2 · i? · associational · r2

One-way ANOVA by frequency of AI use in the quantitative phase (N = 302), described by the authors as the strongest pattern in the quantitative findings. The article explicitly states the relationship should not be interpreted causally.

"Teachers who reported using AI more frequently also tended to report more positive perception scores across all dimensions."
