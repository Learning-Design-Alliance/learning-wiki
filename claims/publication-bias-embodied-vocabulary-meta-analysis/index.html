q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Multilevel Egger's regression tests on the meta-analytic corpus showed a significant association between standard error and effect size in both designs, indicating small-study effects. Leave-one-study-out analyses showed pooled effects were not driven by any single study.

"The results indicated a significant association between standard error and effect size for both between-subject studies ( B = 14.34, p < .001) and within-subject studies (B = 7.33, p < .001), suggesting the presence of small-study effects."
