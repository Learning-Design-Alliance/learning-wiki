Authors' summary of the four pre-unit case study interviews. Tammy used the ΔG = ΔH − TΔS relation to check her conclusion about the sign of the entropy change in free expansion, ultimately deciding she must have reasoned incorrectly.

> "across all of our interviews with the case study students, the notion that spontaneity requires a negative change in the Gibbs free energy of a system was one of the most consistently leveraged ideas, and was well coordinated with other elements of our students' thermodynamic knowledge."
