## Evidence

### Yu Catherine H. Y. 2015

Yu Catherine H. Y., Straus Sharon, Brydges Ryan. (2015). The ABCs of DKA: Development and Validation of a Computer-Based Simulator and Scoring System. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-015-3273-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal structure analysis of the simulator scoring system in the validation study with 75 participants. The article reports "Cronbach’s α was 0.795, indicating adequate internal consistency", but the exploratory factor analysis was underpowered (KMO = 0.25).

> "For the seven subscales, Cronbach’s α was 0.795, indicating adequate internal consistency. The exploratory factor analysis revealed that the Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin value was 0.25, which suggests our sample size was inadequate for conducting such an analysis (the value should be >0.50)."
