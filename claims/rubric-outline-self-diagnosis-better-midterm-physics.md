## Evidence

### E. Yerushalmi 2008

E. Yerushalmi, A. Mason, E. Cohen, C. Singh. (2008). Effect of Self Diagnosis on Subsequent Problem Solving Performance. AIP Conf. Proc. 1064. [doi:10.1063/1.3021272](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3021272)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Classroom quasi-experiment comparing treatment groups against control within one TA's sections; ANCOVA p-value comparison of midterm physics scores. The article reports group B "did significantly better than the control group A"; Table 4 lists p = 0.112 for that comparison.

> "Table 4 shows ANCOVA p-value comparisons between group B and the control group and between group C and group D, respectively. They show that indeed group B, that was provided with a rubric and solution outline, did significantly better than the control group A."
