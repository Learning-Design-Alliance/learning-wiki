## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xu J 2025

Xu J, Sha J, Jia S, Li J, Xu L, Shao Z. (2025). Effectiveness of a 5G Local Area Network–Based Digital Microscopy Interactive System: Quasi-Experimental Design. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/70256

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-experience questionnaire (Cronbach α 0.98) completed by 36 of 39 students; course content was the domain with a printed significant difference (Table 5: 19.6 SD 1.00 vs 18.25 SD 1.88, P=.02).

> "Compared to the control group, the DMIS group more strongly supported the appropriateness of the course difficulty, the reasonableness of the schedule, and found it both enjoyable and challenging (P<.05)."

### Xu J 2025 (2)

Xu J, Sha J, Jia S, Li J, Xu L, Shao Z. (2025). Effectiveness of a 5G Local Area Network–Based Digital Microscopy Interactive System: Quasi-Experimental Design. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/70256

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire's self-efficacy domain showed a significant group difference (Table 5: 19.8 SD 0.89 vs 18.56 SD 1.93, P=.03); teaching quality and teaching effectiveness did not reach significance (both P=.07).

> "Similarly, students in the DMIS group reported higher self-efficacy and greater confidence in facing difficulties and challenges after finishing the lectures (P<.05)."
