## Evidence

### Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students' Difficulties with Equations involving Circuit Elements. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multiple-choice administration to 237 introductory students. The article reports the printed capacitance percentages and states that for inductance "the answers were almost equally distributed across various choices suggesting students may be guessing the answer."

> "Only 35% of the students correctly answered the question about capacitance; 29% incorrectly claimed that capacitance depends on the charge on the plates, the voltage and the distance between the plates ; and 27% claimed that all of the given factors determine the capacitance."
