`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of secondary school suspension rates across all 50 states and Washington, DC using CRDC data (Appendix B, Table B1). The report states use "decreased in 48 states and in Washington, DC" and that only Hawaii and Kansas did not see a decrease.

### Leung-Gagné 2022 (2)

Leung-Gagné, M., McCombs, J., Scott, C., & Losen, D. J. (2022). Pushed out: Trends and disparities in out-of-school suspension. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/235.277

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

State-level CRDC analysis of whether out-of-school declines reflected genuine reductions in exclusion. The report finds "an even larger increase in in-school suspensions" in five states, signaling continued exclusion from learning opportunities.

> "in five states the decline in out-of-school suspensions came with an even larger increase in in-school suspensions, suggesting that in these states students continued to be excluded from learning opportunities even though they remained in school."
