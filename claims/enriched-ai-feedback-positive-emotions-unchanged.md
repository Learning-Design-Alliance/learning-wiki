## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2025

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

AEQ-S survey in the RCT: enjoyment medians 3.31 (control) and 3.12 (experimental), "no statistically significant difference (p = 0.984, effect size |r| = 0.002)"; hope (p = 0.773) and pride (p = 0.854) were likewise non-significant.

> "In terms of enjoyment, both the control and experimental groups reported moderate levels, with median responses of 3.31 and 3.12 respectively, indicating no statistically significant difference (p = 0.984, effect size |r| = 0.002)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2025 (2)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Boredom medians were 2.81 (control) vs 2.47 (experimental) in the same survey; the article reports the difference as "not statistically significant (p = 0.212)" with |r| = 0.124, described as a small but notable lower level.

> "Although this difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.212), the effect size (|r| = 0.124) suggests a small but notable lower level of boredom in the experimental group."
