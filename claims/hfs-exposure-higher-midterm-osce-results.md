### Guerrero 2023

Guerrero, J. G., Rosales, N. S., & Castro, G. M. T. (2023). Impact of high‐fidelity simulation exposure of nursing students with their objective structured clinical examination: A quasi‐experimental study. Nursing Open, 10, 765–772. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Midterm component of the same quasi‐experimental cohort comparison reported in Table 3: combined OSCE mean was 90.86 (13.13) with HFS exposure versus 80.75 (11.29) without, and the 'mean difference between the groups was 10.11%' with a p‐value of less than .01.

> "the combined OSCE midterm results of the group of nursing students with HFS exposure and those without HFS exposure was 90.86 (13.13) and 80.75 (11.29), respectively, and the mean difference between the groups was 10.11% supported with the p‐value of less than .01."
