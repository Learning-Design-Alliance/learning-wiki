### Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur 2025

Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur, Ozbey Furkan, Yilmaz Birkan Eyup. (2025). Effect of artificial intelligence-assisted personalized feedback on radiographic diagnostic performance of dental students: a controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07875-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled pre-test–post-test study of 110 fifth-year dental students randomly assigned to ChatGPT-4o personalized MeSH-based guides or correct/incorrect feedback. The "experimental group showed a significantly greater increase (3.6 ± 1.0)" than controls (1.3 ± 1.2), p < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "When the improvement in performance (pre-test – post-test) was compared between the experimental and control groups, the experimental group showed a significantly greater increase (3.6 ± 1.0) than the control group (1.3 ± 1.2) (p < 0.001)"
