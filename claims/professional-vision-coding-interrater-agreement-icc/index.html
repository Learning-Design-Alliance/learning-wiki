Evidence¶

Meg Farrell 2024¶

Meg Farrell, Monika Martin, Ricardo Böheim, Alexander Renkl, Werner Rieß, Karen D. Könings, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer, Tina Seidel. (2024). Signaling cues and focused prompts for professional vision support: The interplay of instructional design and situational interest in preservice teachers' video analysis. Instructional Science (2024) 52:879–917. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09662-y

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Scaled qualitative content analysis (Mayring, 2014) of pretest and posttest open-response video-analysis comments, coded by two raters on four-point scales for description and interpretation quality. The authors report "very good agreement" (ICC = 0.90 descriptions; ICC = 0.89 interpretations). No effect size, only reliability coefficients.

"Consistency scores indicated very good agreement for descriptions (ICC = 0.90) and interpretations (ICC = 0.89) (Koo & Li, 2016)."
