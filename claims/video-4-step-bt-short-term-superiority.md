## Evidence

### Seifert Lukas B. 2020

Seifert Lukas B., Schnurr Benedikt, Stefanescu Maria-Christina, Sader Robert, Ruesseler Miriam, Sterz Jasmina. (2020). Comparing video-based versions of Halsted's 'see one, do one' and Peyton's '4-step approach' for teaching surgical skills: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02105-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-randomized controlled curricular trial; Table 3 reports post-training means of 81.3% vs 75.5% (rater 1) and 83.7% vs 77.6% (rater 2), with "p < .001" and η² = 0.1703 / 0.1545 favoring the video-based 4-step group.

> "At T1, students that were trained with the video-based ‘4-step Approach’ showed highly significantly better results in the performance of a modified Bellocq’s tamponade (p < 0.001) than students that were trained with the ‘See One, Do One’ approach."
