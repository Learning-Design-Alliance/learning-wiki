# Springboard Summer scholars show positive, statistically significant reading growth from end-of-year to beginning-of-year assessments, averaging about 1.8 months

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all grades, scholars who participated in Springboard Summer improved on district reading assessments from EOY before the program to BOY after it, with an average gain of about 1.8 months of reading growth. [→ Piccinino 2020](#piccinino-2020)

## Evidence

### Piccinino 2020

Piccinino, K., Pepper, S. K., Salomon, H., Greenfield, S., McClanahan, W., & Robertson-Kraft, C. (2020). Springboard Summer Reading Program Evaluation Report. https://www.cde.state.co.us/cdelib/summerslide

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests of EOY-to-BOY grade level equivalent change for 673 Springboard Summer scholars in five districts. The report states "the average change is positive and statistically significant" within each grade and overall, with gains from about 1 month (third grade) to 3 months (fourth grade).

> "Figure 3 shows, on average, scholars showed about 1.8 months of reading growth (ranging between approximately 1 month for third graders and 3 months for fourth graders)."
