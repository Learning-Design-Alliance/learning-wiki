The review reports, citing Clark et al. (2016), that transfer evidence is mixed: near transfer is generally supported while far transfer is less robust, and some studies found students "struggle to generalize that learning without explicit connections made to other contexts."

> "Meta-analyses suggest that games are generally effective for near-transfer of knowledge (applying learned content to similar problems), but evidence of long-term or far transfer is less robust (Clark et al., 2016)."
