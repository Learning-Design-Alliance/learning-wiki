Evidence¶

Al-Rsa'i 2013¶

Al-Rsa'i, M. S. (2013). Promoting Scientific Literacy by Using ICT in Science Teaching. International Education Studies, 6(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n9p175

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment at Al-Hussein Bin Talal University: control group (123 students) taught traditionally, experimental group (121 students) taught with ICT tools including e-mail, social media, online conferencing, and astronomy learning sites. The article reports a t-test finding that "the impact of teaching using ICT was positive at the level of scientific literacy."
