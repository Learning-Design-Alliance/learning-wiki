# Average first-year baccalaureate units earned decreased with each lower QR placement category, from about 27 units in Category I to about 20 in Category IV

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in Category I earned significantly more units than students in other placement categories, with Category II averaging about 25 units, Category III about 23, and Category IV about 20. [→ Bracco 2020](#bracco-2020)
`q2 i?` The gap between units attempted and units earned increased with QR placement status, from fewer than 2 units in Category I to more than 5 units in Category IV. [→ Bracco 2020 (2)](#bracco-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Bracco 2020

Bracco, K., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2020). Student progress under California State University's Executive Order 1110: A systemwide analysis of outcomes in 2018/19. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613408

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of full-year credit accumulation for all 59,790 students, with pairwise Least Squares Means tests adjusted with a Bonferroni correction. Category I students earned "significantly more than the average earned by students in other placement categories"; Category IV averaged about 20 units, a 7-unit gap.

> "those placed in Category I earned an average of about 27 baccalaureate units per student during their first year of college, significantly more than the average earned by students in other placement categories"

### Bracco 2020 (2)

Bracco, K., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2020). Student progress under California State University's Executive Order 1110: A systemwide analysis of outcomes in 2018/19. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613408

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same cohort-wide credit analysis (Figure 3). The report reports the attempted-earned gap "increased with QR placement status, from fewer than 2 units for students placed in Category I to more than 5 units for students placed in Category IV."

> "the gap between the average numbers of baccalaureate units attempted and earned during the first year of college increased with QR placement status, from fewer than 2 units for students placed in Category I to more than 5 units for students placed in Category IV"
