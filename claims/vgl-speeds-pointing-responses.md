`q3 i3` Participants responded significantly faster in the pointing task with VGLs than without, suggesting faster retrieval of spatial information. [→ Jia Liu 2022](#jia-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Jia Liu 2022

Jia Liu, Avinash Kumar Singh, Anna Wunderlich, Klaus Gramann and Chin-Teng Lin. (2022). Redesigning navigational aids using virtual global landmarks to improve spatial knowledge retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00132-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of pointing-task response times comparing VGL and non-VGL trials in the same 27-participant VR experiment. The article reports F1,26 = 6.311, p = 0.019, partial η² = 0.195, with a mean difference of 3.774 s (95% CI, .686 to 6.86) favouring VGL trials.
