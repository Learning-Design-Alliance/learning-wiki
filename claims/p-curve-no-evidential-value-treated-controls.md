## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Melby-Lervåg 2016

Melby-Lervåg, M., Redick, T. S., & Hulme, C. (2016). Working Memory Training Does Not Improve Performance on Measures of Intelligence or Other Measures of “Far Transfer”: Evidence From a Meta-Analytic Review. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616635612

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

P-curve analysis of published studies reporting significant (p < .05, two-tailed) far-transfer effects, run separately for untreated- and treated-control studies. Six studies claiming significance with one-tailed tests exceeded p = .05 two-tailed and were excluded.

> "this confirms our hypothesis, as there is no evidential value from the studies of working memory training using active controls, z(11) = −2.19, p = .01."
