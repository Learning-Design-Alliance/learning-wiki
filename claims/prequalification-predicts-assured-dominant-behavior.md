## Evidence

### Mielke I 2025

Mielke I, Hampe W, Ehrhardt M, Knorr M. (2025). Spoilt for Choice in Undergraduate Medical Admission: Selection of Confident and Considerate Students Using Professional Prequalification and Situational Judgement Test. Perspectives on Medical Education 14(1). https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1571

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal prospective study of N = 210 medical students rated by general practitioners and up to two staff after a mandatory one-week practical training, using linear mixed models with cohort as random effect. The regression showed "β = 0.21; 95% CI [0.04; 0.39], p = 0.019" for prequalification predicting assured-dominant ratings.

> "The positive relation between professional prequalification and ratings of assured-dominant behavior remained when controlling for all covariates (β = 0.21; 95% CI [0.04; 0.39], p = 0.019, see Table 3)."
