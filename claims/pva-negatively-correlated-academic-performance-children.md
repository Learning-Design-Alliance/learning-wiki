## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a multivariate GLM analysis, presenting visual acuity (logMAR) was significantly negatively correlated with average academic T-scores (β = −7.03, 95% CI: −10.63 to −3.43; P < 0.001). [→ Liu Guoyong 2026](#liu-guoyong-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu Guoyong 2026

Liu Guoyong, Guo Bin, Yu Junli, Xu Qian, Wang Yirong, Si Peiran, Gong Yizhuo, Huo Huanhuan, Ma Xiaohui, Hu Yuanyuan, Bi Hongsheng. (2026). Correlation analysis between visual factors and academic performance in Chinese children. Scientific Reports 16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-46397-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate generalized linear model on N = 1,766 children from a school-based cross-sectional study in Shandong, with average academic T-score as dependent variable, adjusting for gender and IQ. The article reports "PVA (β = −7.03, 95% CI: −10.63 to −3.43; P < 0.001)" as a significant negative correlate; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In contrast, PVA (β = −7.03, 95% CI: −10.63 to −3.43; P < 0.001) and astigmatism (β = −1.44, 95% CI: −2.33 to −0.55; P = 0.001) were significantly and negatively correlated with academic performance."
