# Math anxiety relates more strongly to Mathematical Reasoning than to Numerical Operations in second and third graders

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Math anxiety significantly and negatively correlated with Mathematical Reasoning (β = −0.34, p < 0.001) but not Numerical Operations (β = −0.18, p = 0.17) after controlling for trait anxiety and FSIQ. [→ Wu SS 2012](#wu-ss-2012)
`q3 i?` When the two highest SEMA rating categories were collapsed, math anxiety correlated significantly with both subtests, with a significantly stronger effect on Mathematical Reasoning (z = −3.65, p < 0.0001). [→ Wu SS 2012 (2)](#wu-ss-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Wu SS 2012

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analyses of the 162-child sample on the two WIAT-II math subtests, controlling for trait anxiety and FSIQ. The article reports the printed β, t, p and R2 values for each subtest; the initial slope comparison was not significant (z = −1.10, p = 0.13).

> "math anxiety was significantly and negatively correlated with Mathematical Reasoning [ β =− 0.34, t (127) =− 3.66, p < 0.001, R2 = 0.57] but not with Numerical Operations [β =− 0.18, t (127) =− 1.37, p = 0.17, R2 = 0.23]."

### Wu SS 2012 (2)

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis collapsing the "Very Nervous" and "Very Very Nervous" rating categories and recalculating SEMA scores. A difference of slopes test suggested "math anxiety has a significantly stronger effect on Mathematical Reasoning (z = −3.65, p < 0.0001)".

> "This analyses indi- cated that SEMA was signiﬁcantly correlated with both Mathe- matical Reasoning ( ρ =− 0.48, p < 0.001) and Numerical Oper- ations ρ =− 0.26, p < 0.01), even after controlling for FSIQ and trait anxiety."
