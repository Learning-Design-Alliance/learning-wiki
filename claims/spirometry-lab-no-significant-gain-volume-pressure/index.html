Evidence¶

Wolf et al. 2015¶

Alex M. Wolf, Carlos Liachovitzky & Abass S. Abdullahi. (2015). Active Learning Improves Student Performance in a Respiratory Physiology Lab. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 4(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n1p19

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Midterm exam comparison between test and control sections on volume-pressure-airflow concept questions. Test sections scored "74.3±12.5%" versus "66.3±9.6%"; the difference was not significant (p=0.09).

"On questions relating to the relationship between pressure, volume and airflow, the test sections (74.3±12.5%) performed better than the control sections (66.3±9.6%), though the difference was not significant (p=0.09)."
