## Evidence

### Yu Fanyi 2025

Yu Fanyi, Nyanamba Juliet M., Anderson Brittany, DeerInWater Kathy, Estrada Mica, Hosoda K. Kanoho, Maldonado Natalia, Smith Tiffany, Flores Lilibeth, & Hernandez Paul R. (2025). Navigating Science Pathways: Belonging Mediates the Impact of Goal Congruity on Persistence (but Not Life Satisfaction) Among Indigenous Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000967

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis in the same longitudinal model (N = 127). The article reports that "13.5% of the effect of communal incongruity" was mediated through belonging; the printed proportions are descriptive mediation percentages, not standardized effect sizes.

> "Further, 13.5% of the effect of communal incongruity and 13.8% of the effect of agentic incongruity on persistence intentions was mediated through belonging."
