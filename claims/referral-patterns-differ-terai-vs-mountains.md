## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Referral-question responses from the 107-PHCW quantitative survey: corneal ulcer (78.9%) and cataract (78.9%) were most likely to be referred, conjunctivitis least (27.6%). The study found "In the terai on average across all four conditions 94.3% of PHCWs would refer to SCEH" versus 20.7% in the mountains.

> "In the terai on average across all four conditions 94.3% of PHCWs would refer to SCEH. In contrast, only 20.7% of PHCWs working in the mountains would refer patients to SCEH, most would refer only to the local ECC"
