## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline production data from the typically developing Cantonese comparison group (23 children), analyzed with linear mixed effects models; focus condition was significant for duration, f0 and intensity (e.g., duration χ2 = 301.18; df = 6; p < 0.001). No effect size printed.

> "Post-hoc comparisons showed that the TD group produced post-focus words with significantly shorter duration, lower mean f0 and intensity than the broad focus words under both narrow and contrastive conditions, whereas they only produced contrastive on-focus words with significantly longer duration and higher f0 than broad focus words."
