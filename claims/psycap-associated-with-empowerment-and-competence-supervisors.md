## Evidence

### Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed; Alghamdi Abdulaziz Rashed 2026

Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed; Alghamdi Abdulaziz Rashed. (2026). Exploring educational supervisors’ professional commitment: The roles of psychological capital, psychological empowerment, and professional competence. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0357360

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling with robust maximum likelihood (Satorra–Bentler corrections) on survey data from 358 Saudi educational supervisors; the structural model fit well (CFI = .998, RMSEA = .017). The path from psychological capital to empowerment was "β = .68, p < .001".

> "Psychological capital showed a strong positive association with psychological empowerment (β = .68, p < .001), supporting H1."
