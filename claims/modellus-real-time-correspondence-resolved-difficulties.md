### Rui Gomes Neves 2010

Rui Gomes Neves, Jorge Carvalho Silva & Vítor Duarte Teodoro. (2010). Computational Modelling with Modellus: An Enhancement Vector for the General University Physics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1006.4662](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1006.4662)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' qualitative evaluation of the course component, reported in the conclusions. They attribute the result to the "real time visible correspondence between the animations with interactive objects and the object's mathematical properties deﬁned in the model". No quantitative learning measures are reported in this paper.

> "We have concluded that during class the computational modelling activities with Modellus were successful in identifying and resolving several student diﬃculties in key physical and mathematical concepts of the course."
