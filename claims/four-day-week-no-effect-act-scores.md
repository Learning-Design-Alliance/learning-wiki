`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the Oklahoma district-level difference-in-differences analysis, the study finds "no detectable effect" on ACT scores. No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text, so the null is reported without an equivalence claim.

> "Results indicate that 4-day school weeks decrease per-pupil bullying incidents by approximately 39% and per-pupil fighting incidents by approximately 31%, but have no detectable effect on other incident types, ACT scores, or attendance."
