## Evidence

### Rodriguez Buritica 2025

Rodriguez Buritica, J., Berboth, S., Hoferichter, F., & Raufelder, D. (2025). Neural and behavioural correlates of adolescents changing academic self‐concept. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 234–248. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12667

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal mixed-effects generalized linear model on behavioural data from 39 adolescents (T1 mean age 13.19; T2 mean age 14.74), controlling for puberty scores. The analysis "showed that ASC decreased over time (β = −0.31, t = −2.86, p = .005)". ASC was measured with the five-item absolute school self-concept subscale (α = .80/.82).

> "The results of this analysis (see Table S1) showed that ASC decreased over time (β = −0.31, t = −2.86, p = .005) and increased with higher grades (β = 0.18, t = 2.28, p = .024)."
