# Medical students' ECG interpretation scores rose from pre-test to post-test across all three outcome scores within the participating group after completing the online ECG training session

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Basic, relative, and conservative ECG interpretation scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test within the participating group after the ECG e-learning session, with large effect sizes. [→ Scherff Aline D. 2024](#scherff-aline-d-2024)

## Evidence

### Scherff Aline D. 2024

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2024). EYE-ECG: An RCT of the influence of student characteristics and expert eye-tracking videos with cued retrospective reporting on students’ ECG interpretation skills. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4):Doc40. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001695.shtml

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RCT of an online ECG training session with N=91 medical students; paired t-tests on pre-post scores showed gains such as "pre-post gain BS=4.80±9.10" with Cohen’s d=1.27 (BS), d=1.19 (RS), and d=1.60 (CS).

> "partaking in the study significantly improved participant ECG interpretation skills to moderate effects (pre-post gain BS=4.80±9.10, t(90)=5.03, p<.03*10-3, Cohen’s d=1.27; RS=10.45±10.29, t(90)=9.68, p<.01*10-13, d=1.19; CS=15.97±9.02, t(90)=16.88, p<.03*10-14, d=1.60)"
