`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Major-level comparison (surgical vs. internal medicine) in the 153-participant cross-sectional survey, reported in supplementary tables. The article reports "there were no significant differences between the surgical students and internal students in either mental health or academic performances"; equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed.

> "Among the medical postgraduates, there were no significant differences between the surgical students and internal students in either mental health (Supplementary Table 2) or academic performances (Supplementary Table 3)."
