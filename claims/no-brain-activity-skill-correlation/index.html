Evidence¶

Girard 2026¶

Girard, C., Longo, L., Chesnokova, H., Epinat-Duclos, J., & Prado, J. (2026). Home numeracy experiences are associated with number-related brain activity and connectivity in early childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00419-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Null pre-registered whole-brain analysis in the fMRI subsample (n = 37); hypothesis #3 (mediation of the home-environment/skills relation) could not be formally tested. Equivalence was not tested; the authors attribute the null partly to the passive task and immature processing.

"our whole-brain analyses failed to reveal any significant relation between number-speciﬁc activity and any of the children's numeracy skills anywhere in the brain (at least at the statistical threshold chosen)"
