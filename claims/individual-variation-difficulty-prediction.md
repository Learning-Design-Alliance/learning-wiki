## Evidence

### Kibble JD 2011

Kibble JD, Johnson T. (2011). Are faculty predictions or item taxonomies useful for estimating the outcome of multiple-choice examinations? Adv Physiol Educ 35: 396–401. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00062.2011

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of prediction success rates across eight consenting faculty members in the physiology course, using grade-based performance categories of item P values. Individual correlations between predicted and actual difficulty ranged from 0.07 to −0.56, and the authors could not detect any relationship with teaching experience, age, or sex.

> "Individually, faculty members differed significantly in overall rates of successful prediction (χ2 = 16.84, P = 0.02). Overall rates of successful prediction ranged from 27% to 75%"
