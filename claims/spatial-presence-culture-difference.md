## Evidence

### Figueroa 2025

Figueroa, R. B., Jr., & Jung, I. (2025). Integrating VR tours in online language learning: A design-based research study. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 41(1). [doi:10.14742/ajet.9498](https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.9498)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Friedman tests of spatial presence ratings across age groups (20s, 30s, 40+), cultures (Japanese, French, cosmopolitan) and learning environments in the DBR study. Only the culture contrast was significant; post-hoc group differences were not, and no effect size was printed.

> "The Friedman sign-rank test showed no significant differences in spatial presence by age group (F(2) = 4.34, p = 0.11) or learning environment (F(2) = 1.33, p = 0.51), but by culture (F(2) = 6.61, p = 0.04). Cosmopolitan learners had the highest median spatial presence. Nemenyi post-hoc tests showed no significant group differences."
