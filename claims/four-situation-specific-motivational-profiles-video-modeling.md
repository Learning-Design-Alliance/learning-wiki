# Latent profile analysis of situation-specific motivation for studying video modeling examples yields four profiles: good-quality, moderately positive, moderately negative, and poor-quality

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` LPA on motivation subscales identified a four-profile solution, supporting the hypothesis of three to six profiles. [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021](#lisette-wijnia-2021)
`q2 i?` The good-quality profile showed high intrinsic/identified motivation with moderate introjected and external motivation; the poor-quality profile showed low autonomous motivation with the highest external motivation. [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)](#lisette-wijnia-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Profile description from the LPA results with class assignment probabilities above .80 for all profiles.
