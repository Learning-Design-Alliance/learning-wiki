## Evidence

### Kalabamu FS 2026

Kalabamu FS, Daudi V, Moshiro R, Kamala B, Mdoe P, Bishanga D, Kvaløy JT, Ersdal H, Mpembeni R. (2026). Neonatal resuscitation skills retention among healthcare providers one year after implementation of in situ low-dose high-frequency simulations using innovative tools across two regions in Tanzania. Global Health Action. https://doi.org/10.1080/16549716.2026.2639774

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal follow-up of 226 healthcare providers in 12 facilities in Geita and Shinyanga, Tanzania, re-evaluated with the HBB second-version OSCE one year after baseline training; "174 (77%) retained skills one year after the implementation of LDHF-SBT".

> "Of all participants who completed follow-up, 174 (77%) retained skills one year after the implementation of LDHF-SBT, while 52 (23%) did not retain their skills."
