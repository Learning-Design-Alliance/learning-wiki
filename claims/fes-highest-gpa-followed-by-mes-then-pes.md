# FES students have the highest GPA, followed by MES, then PES, for both FTIC and transfer students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The FES group has the highest average GPA (2.97), the PES group the lowest (2.55), and the MES group lies between them (2.78), with all pairwise differences statistically significant. [→ Shahab Boumi 2020](#shahab-boumi-2020)
`q2 i?` For FTIC students, FES groups have the highest average GPAs (3.03), followed by MES (2.75) and PES (2.42); for transfer students, FES (2.85), MES (2.76), and PES (2.44). [→ Shahab Boumi 2020 (2)](#shahab-boumi-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Shahab Boumi 2020

Shahab Boumi, Adan Vela. (2020). Quantifying the relationship between student enrollment patterns and student performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of average cumulative GPA across enrollment strategy clusters from UCF records (2008–2017). Games-Howell post hoc tests show "the p-values for all the hypothesis tests are 0, indicating that each group's average GPA is statistically diﬀerent from others."

> "Results show that the FES group has the highest average GPA (2.97). The lowest GPA corresponds to the PES group (2.55), while the MES group's GPA (2.78) lies between PES and FES."

### Shahab Boumi 2020 (2)

Shahab Boumi, Adan Vela. (2020). Quantifying the relationship between student enrollment patterns and student performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive GPA analysis separated by FTIC and transfer student populations. KS2 tests show GPA distributions for FES, MES, and PES groups statistically differ from each other for both populations. Effect sizes are larger for FTIC students than transfer students.

> "For both FTIC and transfer students, FES groups have the highest average GPAs (3.03 for FTIC and 2.85 for transfer), followed by MES (2.75 for FTIC and 2.76 for transfer), and PES (2.42 for FTIC and 2.44 for transfer) respectively."
