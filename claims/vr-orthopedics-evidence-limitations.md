## Evidence

### Li Ting 2025

Li Ting, Yan Jingxin, Gao Xin, Liu Hangyu, Li Jin, Shang Yuanting, Tang Xiaoyu. (2025). Using Virtual Reality to Enhance Surgical Skills and Engagement in Orthopedic Education: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/70266

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Risk-of-bias assessment of the 23 included RCTs using Cochrane RoB 2.0 by 2 independent researchers; most studies raised concerns, and GRADE certainty across outcomes was moderate to very low.

> "Two studies did not report the RCT design, 9 studies are ambiguous about random sequence generation, and 12 studies claimed the RCT design. It was found that 3 studies were of low-risk bias and therefore had some concerns about the risk of bias for many of the criteria."

### Li Ting 2025 (2)

Li Ting, Yan Jingxin, Gao Xin, Liu Hangyu, Li Jin, Shang Yuanting, Tang Xiaoyu. (2025). Using Virtual Reality to Enhance Surgical Skills and Engagement in Orthopedic Education: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/70266

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Begg plot assessment of publication bias across included studies; asymmetry for knowledge scores suggests possible publication bias, while clinical operation scores showed symmetry and clinical understanding ability showed no bias (P=.29).

> "For studies in knowledge scores, the funnel plot had no symmetry (P=.01; see Figure 11). But, for studies in clinical operation scores, the funnel plot had symmetry (P=.09; see Figure 12)"
