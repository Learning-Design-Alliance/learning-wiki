Evidence¶

Bird 1921¶

Bird, Grace E. (1921). The Devious Path of Slow Work. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ123456

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Bird reports O'Brien's experiment in which "photographic records were made of the eye movements of ten pupils in grades III to VIII before and after training in silent reading," showing fewer fixation pauses, fewer regressive movements, and regular rhythmic eye-movement habits.

"A study of the records showed that the improvement on the physiological side was effected chiefly by a lessening of the number of the fixation pauses rather than a decrease in the duration of these pauses."
