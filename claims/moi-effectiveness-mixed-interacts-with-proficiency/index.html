Murphy 2020¶

Foreign language learning and its impact on wider academic outcomes: A rapid evidence assessment. University of Oxford Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED612981

q2 · i? · review · r3

Synthesis of four seed reviews (Fitzpatrick et al 2018; Graham et al 2018; Goris et al 2019; Lo & Lo 2014) with updates finding 2, 8, 4 and 0 new studies respectively. The review notes "Students' skills and proficiency is a key variable interacting with outcomes".

"While there is evidence that supports the implementation of MoI programmes for developing both language and content, the effectiveness of this approach interacts with many variables, and will depend on the settings, implementation strategies, skills of the practitioner, and individual learner characteristics."
