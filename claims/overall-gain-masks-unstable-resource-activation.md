Tianlong Zu, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Transition Matrix Analysis: Analyzing Students' Use of Cognitive Resources in Physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Proof-of-concept classroom study using DIRECT (29 multiple-choice questions) as pretest and posttest for N=135 students across a 7-week instructional unit. The authors argue this information "would be ignored if we only report the overall gain in performance".

> "the TMA results indicate that even though students significantly improved their overall performance on DIRECT (total class average), students do not typically have solid understanding of the concept of power of direct current circuits."
