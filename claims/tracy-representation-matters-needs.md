Shams El-Adawy, Alexandra C. Lau, Eleanor C. Sayre, and Claudia Fracchiolla. (2024). Motivation and needs of informal physics practitioners. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis identified the Tracy persona, frustrated by pushback from a physics community that does not always see informal physics as integral to a physicist's job. Needs include "More buy-in from institutions," funding, and infrastructure investment.

> "More buy-in from institutions on the value of their informal physics work, which would foster their sense of agency in what they can do in this space;"
