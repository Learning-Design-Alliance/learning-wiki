# Number of students and %Hispanic are statistically significant predictors of physics offerings (p<0.001), with a non-significant interaction term

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an ordinal logistic regression with number of students and %Hispanic, both predictors were significant with p<0.001 after the non-significant interaction term was removed. [→ David Marasco and Bree Barnett Dreyfuss 2020](#david-marasco-and-bree-barnett-dreyfuss-2020)

## Evidence

### David Marasco and Bree Barnett Dreyfuss 2020

David Marasco and Bree Barnett Dreyfuss. (2020). SCHOOLS ON DIFFERENT CORNERS: An Investigation into the Effects of Ethnicity and Socioeconomic Status on Physics Offerings in Northern California Public High Schools. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2010.08476](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2010.08476)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression on the 410-school dataset with no physics, just physics, AP physics, and multiple-levels of AP physics as result categories; the interaction term "was found to be not statistically significant, and was removed from the model". No effect size beyond coefficients is printed.

> "In the revised model, both number of students and %Hispanic were significant with p<0.001."

### David Marasco and Bree Barnett Dreyfuss 2020 (2)

David Marasco and Bree Barnett Dreyfuss. (2020). SCHOOLS ON DIFFERENT CORNERS: An Investigation into the Effects of Ethnicity and Socioeconomic Status on Physics Offerings in Northern California Public High Schools. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2010.08476](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2010.08476)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Printed coefficients and odds ratios from the same revised regression model: "0.0015 with an odds ratio of 0.86 per 100 students" for enrollment and 0.0273 (OR 1.03) for %Hispanic. The article notes the enrollment result is consistent with earlier work.

> "The coefficient for number of students was 0.0015 with an odds ratio of 0.86 per 100 students. The coefficient for %Hispanic was 0.0273, with and odds ratio of 1.03."
