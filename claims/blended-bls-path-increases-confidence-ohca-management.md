### Taramarcaz V 2022

Taramarcaz V, Herren T, Golay E, Regard S, Martin-Achard S, Mach F, Schnetzler N, Ricci G, Zamberg I, Larribau R, Niquille M, Suppan M, Schiffer E, Suppan L. (2022). Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/38508

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired comparison of precourse and postcourse confidence (1-5 Likert) among the 97 practice-session attendants who answered the postcourse questionnaire, 92.8% response. The article reports a significant increase with means "4.2, SD 0.6 vs 2.1, SD 0.9; P<.001"; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "There was a significant increase in confidence regarding OHCA management skills after following the learning path (4.2, SD 0.6 vs 2.1, SD 0.9; P<.001)."
