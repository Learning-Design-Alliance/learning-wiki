`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Contextual-effect model for multiple ethnic groups fitted to the 2011 SABER 11 census data. The article prints no standardised effect size for these within-school gaps, so no magnitude code is assigned.

> "Table 3 also shows that within schools, Afrocolombian, Indigenous and other minority groups score 0.09 SD (ˆβWA ), 0.11 SD (ˆβWI ) and 0.03 SD (ˆβWO ) lower than their White peers."

### Gallo Cordoba 2022 (2)

Gallo Cordoba, B., Leckie, G., and Browne, W.J. (2022). Decomposing Ethnic Achievement Gaps across Multiple Levels of Analysis and for Multiple Ethnic Groups. Sociological Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1177/00811750221099503

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multiple-group contextual-effect model: the school contextual effects of Afrocolombian, Indigenous and other minority composition are 0.71, 0.67 and 0.17 SD per ten percentage points. The article prints no standardised effect size for these coefficients, so no magnitude code is assigned.

> "Additionally, a ten percentage-point increase in the proportion of Afrocolombian, Indigenous and other minority students is linked to an average decrease of 0.71 SD ( ˆβCA 10 ), 0.67 SD ( ˆβCI 10 ) and 0.17 SD ( ˆβCO 10 ) in the students' maths test scores, repectively, independently of their own ethnicity."
