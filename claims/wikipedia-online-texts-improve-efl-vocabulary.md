## Evidence

### Khany & Khosravian 2014

Khany, R., & Khosravian, F. (2014). Iranian EFL learners' vocabulary development through Wikipedia. *English Language Teaching, 7*(7). [doi:10.5539/elt.v7n7p57](https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n7p57)

`q3 · randomised two-group experiment` · `i?` · `n=36` · `causal · r1`

Randomised experiment: 36 intermediate female EFL learners aged 16 to 22 at a language institute in Ilam, Iran, selected from 56 on an Oxford Placement Test, "randomly were assigned to two equal groups" of 18 (the procedure also says the placement test, used as the pretest, divided them into two homogeneous groups; pretest means 13.72 and 14.22, p = .612). Over twelve sessions in one semester the experimental group worked with online Wikipedia pages chosen for their hyperlinks, podcasts and videos and for learners' interests, and the control group with printed Wikipedia handouts. On a 30-item researcher-made multiple-choice vocabulary posttest, means were 17.00 (SD 1.78, Wikipedia) vs 13.38 (SD 1.91, Traditional); the independent samples t-test gave t = -5.85, df = 33.82, Sig. .000, which "confirms the significant difference between Traditional and Wikipedia groups' performance". No effect size is printed.

> "The significance of .000 confirms the significant difference between Traditional and Wikipedia groups' performance. In other words, it denotes that students in the Wikipedia class had a performance that was superior to those in the Traditional class."
