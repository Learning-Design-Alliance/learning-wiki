## Evidence

### Reza Khany & Fereshteh Khosravian 2014

Reza Khany & Fereshteh Khosravian. (2014). Iranian EFL Learners' Vocabulary Development through Wikipedia. English Language Teaching, 7(7). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n7p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 36 intermediate female EFL learners (18 per group) at a language institute in Ilam, Iran, assigned via Oxford Placement Test. Posttest means were 17.00 (Wikipedia) vs 13.38 (Traditional); the Independent Samples Test gave Sig. .000, which "confirms the significant difference between Traditional and Wikipedia groups' performance". No effect size is printed.

> "The significance of .000 confirms the significant difference between Traditional and Wikipedia groups' performance. In other words, it denotes that students in the Wikipedia class had a performance that was superior to those in the Traditional class."
