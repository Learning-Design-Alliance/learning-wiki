## Evidence

### Jung Sua 2025

Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding across career stages: experienced nurses observed that junior nurses now ask peers in chatrooms rather than seniors or doctors, and a novice described confusion after a doctor's verbal instruction to use only half an ampoule.

> "Furthermore, some medication errors were due to junior nurses’ reluctance to seek advice from senior nurses regarding administrative procedures. Miscommunication between physicians and nurses regarding medication prescriptions can also lead to errors, with verbal prescriptions being the most common cause."
