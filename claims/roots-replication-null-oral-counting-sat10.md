## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Doabler 2016

Doabler, C. T., Clarke, B., Kosty, D. B., Kurtz-Nelson, E., Fien, H., Smolkowski, K., & Baker, S. K. (2016). Testing the efficacy of a tier 2 mathematics intervention: A conceptual replication study. Exceptional Children, 83, 92–110. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402916660084

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null findings from the same partially nested RCT: the Time × Condition model for oral counting (Time × Condition g = 0.159, p = .2276) and the analysis-of-covariance model for the SAT-10 administered in first grade showed no statistically significant condition differences. No effect size beyond the printed g values was reported for a detected effect.

> "We did not detect statistically significant differences between conditions in gains on oral counting or differences between conditions on covariate adjusted SAT-10 scores with ASPENS and TEMA-3 as pretest covariates."
