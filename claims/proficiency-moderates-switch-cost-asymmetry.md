## Evidence

### Mathieu Declerck & Andrea M. Philipp 2015

Mathieu Declerck & Andrea M. Philipp. (2015). A review of control processes and their locus in language switching. Psychon Bull Rev. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-015-0836-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Meuter and Allport (1999), that "the highly proficient group revealed symmetrical switch costs and the second language learners revealed asymmetrical switch costs," with converging evidence from Filippi et al. and Schwieter and Sunderman on proficiency correlations and thresholds. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Their results indicated that asymmetrical switch costs are indeed dependent on language proficiency, since the highly proficient group revealed symmetrical switch costs and the second language learners revealed asymmetrical switch costs."

### Mathieu Declerck & Andrea M. Philipp 2015 (2)

Mathieu Declerck & Andrea M. Philipp. (2015). A review of control processes and their locus in language switching. Psychon Bull Rev. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-015-0836-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Declerck, Thoma, et al. (2015c), that highly proficient Turkish-German bilinguals showed symmetrical switch costs and n-2 language repetition costs, with larger n-2 costs for Turkish and German than English. The review states these results are "completely in line with the notion of persisting, reactive inhibition."

> "These results are completely in line with the notion of persisting, reactive inhibition (e.g., Green, 1998). They are not, however, in line with models that assume that highly proficient bilinguals implement no inhibition."
