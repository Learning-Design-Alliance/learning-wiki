Funding timeline survey findings (Figure 22): fewer than half of coaches overall are funded at consistent rates; district-based coaches are funded over multiple years slightly more than school-based coaches.

"Forty-three percent of district-based administrators reported that coaches are funded multi-year, compared to 37 percent of school-based administrators"

"Coaches are funded primarily by Title II federal funds, which are designated to support preparing, training, and recruiting high-quality teachers and principals"
