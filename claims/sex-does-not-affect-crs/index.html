Leila Etemadi 2023¶

Leila Etemadi, Dan-Anders Jirenhed and Anders Rasmussen. (2023). Effects of working memory load and CS-US intervals on delay eyeblink conditioning. npj Science of Learning 8:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00167-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Fixed effect of sex (male or female) in the linear mixed effects model of CR percentage across 42 participants. "sex did not affect CRs" with no statistically significant effect; no effect size was printed.

"However, contrary to our previous ﬁndings20, sex did not affect CRs ( t = 1.621, P = 0.11, CI = −1.67 to 17.4%)."
