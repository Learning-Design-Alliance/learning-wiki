## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Aligned Rank Transform (ART) ANOVA, a robust non-parametric analysis, examined main effects of group and time and their interaction on knowledge. All three effects reached significance; the article prints no effect size for these F-tests.

> "Based on the results extracted by ART ANOVA, the mean knowledge level in the two study groups differed (F-value = 107.58, P-value < 0.001). The mean of knowledge before and after the intervention was different (F-value = 293.34, P-value < 0.001). The mean of knowledge before and after the intervention in the two study groups (time × group) was different (F-value = 133.20, P-value < 0.001)."
