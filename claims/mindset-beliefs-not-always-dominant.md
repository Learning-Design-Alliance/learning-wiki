## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Little 2018

Little, A. J., Humphrey, B., Green, A., Nair, A., & Sawtelle, V. (2018). Exploring Mindset's Applicability to Students' Experiences with Challenge in Transformed College Physics Courses. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.11062](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.11062)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' theoretical interpretation within the discussion of mindset threat conditions, concerning challenges where students perceive racism and cannot connect to a classroom support network. The article states that "a student might respond to a challenge negatively in such a case should be more related to racism than beliefs". This is an argued position, not a tested result; no effect size is reported.

> "The underlying theory for why a student might respond to a challenge negatively in such a case should be more related to racism than beliefs (see additional arguments made by McGee and Bentley, 2017)."
