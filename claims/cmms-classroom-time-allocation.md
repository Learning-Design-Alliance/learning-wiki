## Evidence

### Javid 2014

Javid, C. Z. (2014). EMP Needs of Medical Undergraduates in a Saudi Context. Kashmir Journal of Language Research, 14(1), 89-110. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Structured observation protocol applied to 11 classes (6 theoretical lectures, 3 small-group discussion sessions, 2 practical sessions), each 30 minutes; Table 1 prints totals: SRT 110, SLT 132, SWT 13, TST 88, SST 44, AST 98 minutes. Student writing was done "only by a few students".

> "Table 1 shows the total time for different factors and the data has offered deep insights into the fact that reading skills is identified an extremely important language skill for their studies at CMMS."
