### Kaup 2023

Kaup, C. F., Pedersen, P. L., & Tvedebrink, T. (2023). Integrating computational thinking to enhance students’ mathematical understanding. Journal of Pedagogical Research, 7(2), 127-142. https://doi.org/10.33902/JPR.202318531

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative observation from the second intervention year: pairs built a calculator via block programming in MakeCode, downloaded it to a micro:bit, and compared machine versus hand calculation of multiplication.

> "We argue that this task also illustrated students’ CT thought process since students could recognize when it is more efficient to let the computer do the work or calculate it themselves."
