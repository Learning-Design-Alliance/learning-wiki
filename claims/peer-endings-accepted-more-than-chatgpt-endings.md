`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire item Q12 in the post-activity survey compared acceptance of the alternative ending between students who analyzed peer-written endings (control) and students who analyzed ChatGPT-generated endings (experimental). Percentages reported descriptively in Table 2 with no statistical test.

> "A substantial majority of the control group (72.73%) accepted the new version as a valid ending, contrasting sharply with only 33.33% in the experimental group."
