## Evidence

### Koç Cemre 2025

Koç Cemre, Akyıldız Bahar Melis, Taş Ayşe. (2025). Evaluation of 3D‐Printed Models in Dentoalveolar Trauma Simulation: A Randomized Trial in Undergraduate Dental Training. Dental Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1111/edt.13065

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial comparing experimental and control groups on STAI-S and a 10-cm stress VAS at T2. The results report no significant between-group differences, p > 0.05; no effect size printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "However, no significant differences were detected between the groups in terms of STAI‐S and VAS scores (p > 0.05)."

### Koç Cemre 2025 (2)

Koç Cemre, Akyıldız Bahar Melis, Taş Ayşe. (2025). Evaluation of 3D‐Printed Models in Dentoalveolar Trauma Simulation: A Randomized Trial in Undergraduate Dental Training. Dental Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1111/edt.13065

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired comparisons in the experimental group from T1 to T2 on STAI-S and VAS; the results report STAI-S and VAS scores "significantly lower compared to T1" for both dentition types. No effect size printed.

> "In the experimental group, for both permanent and mixed dentition, self‐confidence and knowledge scores at T2 were significantly higher, while STAI‐S and VAS scores were significantly lower compared to T1 (Table 1)."
