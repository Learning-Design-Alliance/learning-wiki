## Evidence

### van Wijk EV 2024

van Wijk EV, de Jonge M, van Blankenstein FM, Janse RJ, Langers AMJ. (2024). The battle of question formats: a comparative study of retrieval practice using very short answer questions and multiple choice questions. BMC Medical Education, 24:1547. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06538-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test across the 45 students comparing combined practice test performance with final retention test performance over three- to six-week intervals. The article reports "practice tests: 66.2% (12.1) vs. final test: 52.7% (10.1)" with t(44) = 6.99, p < 0.01.

> "The mean total score on the final test was 14% lower than the mean overall score of all four practice tests combined (mean percentage score (SD) practice tests: 66.2% (12.1) vs. final test: 52.7% (10.1); paired t-test: t(44) = 6.99; p < 0.01; 95%CI [0.10,0.17])."
