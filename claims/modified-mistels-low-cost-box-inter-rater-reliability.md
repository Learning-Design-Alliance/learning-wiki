## Evidence

### Hasnaoui Anis 2019

Hasnaoui Anis, Zaafouri Haithem, Haddad Dhafer, Bouhafa Ahmed, Ben Maamer Anis. (2019). Reliability testing of a modified MISTELS score using a low-cost trainer box. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1572-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First series of tasks scored by two raters, one on the spot and one blinded from videotapes; the ICC, calculated with a two-way mixed model with absolute agreement, was 1. The article reports no effect size, only the ICC statistic.

> "As for inter-rater and test-retest reliabilities that were assessed by ICCs, they yielded excellent scores that were at 1 and 0.95 (95% CI, 0.891–0.978) respectively."
