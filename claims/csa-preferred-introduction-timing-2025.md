Survey item on appropriate introduction year among 2058 non-opposing respondents; the article reports the printed percentages and P < .0001, with no effect size.

> "The 2058 respondents who did not oppose the introduction of CSA believed that the appropriate time for the introduction of the CSA is 2025 (64.5%), 2027 (11.9%), 2026 (10.7%), 2029 (8.0%), and 2028 (4.8%). Perceived need to introduce CSA was related to the appropriate time for its introduction (P < .0001)."
