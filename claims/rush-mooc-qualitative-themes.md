Cevik Arif Alper; Abu-Zidan Fikri M. (2025). Open-access RUSH protocol course improves knowledge and perceived confidence among healthcare learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07984-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke's six-phase framework) of written feedback from 105 of 512 post-course survey respondents, using ChatGPT-4o, a Custom-GPT, and NoteBookLM triangulated with manual coding, yielding nine codes and five themes.

> "Five themes emerged from the qualitative data: Learner Satisfaction and Future Expansion, Engaging and Clear Content Delivery, Hands-on Learning Experience, Effective Assessment Strategies, and Access to Learning Resources."
