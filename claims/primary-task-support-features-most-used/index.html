Lehto Tuomas MSc 2011¶

Lehto Tuomas MSc, Oinas-Kukkonen Harri PhD. (2011). Persuasive Features in Web-Based Alcohol and Smoking Interventions: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3222186/

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of 23 RCTs of Web-based alcohol and smoking interventions published 2004-2009, coded using interpretive categorization against the PSD model. The review reports that "Reduction, self-monitoring, simulation, and personalization seem to be the most used features to support accomplishing user’s primary task."
