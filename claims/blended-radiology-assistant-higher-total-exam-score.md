## Evidence

### Zhang Tonghua 2026

Zhang Tonghua, Fu Dingwei, Liu Huan, Ruan Yuanzhi, Liu Houjun, Mei Guangbao. (2026). Effectiveness of a Radiology Assistant–based blended learning model in medical imaging internship education: a prospective randomized comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09014-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized comparative study of 60 medical imaging interns assigned 1:1 to blended (n = 30) or traditional (n = 30) groups over an 18-week internship. The blended group's "total examination scores (maximum = 100 points)" were significantly higher (87.7 ± 5.1 vs. 77.3 ± 6.3, t = 4.032, P < 0.001); no effect size was printed.
