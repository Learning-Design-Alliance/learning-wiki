## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Teacher-student interaction positively and significantly influences student interaction in work groups (β = 0.252, p < 0.01, f² = 0.072). [→ Hernández-Sellés 2019](#hernandez-selles-2019)

## Evidence

### Hernández-Sellés 2019

Hernández-Sellés, N., Muñoz-Carril, P.-C., & González-Sanmamed, M. (2019). Computer-supported collaborative learning: An analysis of the relationship between interaction, emotional support and online collaborative tools. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.04.012

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r?`

A cross-sectional survey of 106 students in five online university subjects analyzed via PLS-SEM. The path from teacher-student interaction to student interaction in work groups was "positive and significant" with β=0.252 and f²=0.072, a small effect.

> "The results obtained show that teacher-student interaction has a positive and significant effect in relation to the interaction processes that students develop in their respective teams (H1; β=0.252; p<0.01; ƒ2=0.072)."
