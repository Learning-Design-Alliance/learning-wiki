## Evidence

### Van Wijk EV 2024

Van Wijk EV, Donkers J, De Laat PCJ, Meiboom AA, Jacobs B, Ravesloot JH, Tio RA, Van Der Vleuten CPM, Langers AMJ, Bremers AJA. (2024). Computer Adaptive vs. Non-adaptive Medical Progress Testing: Feasibility, Test Performance, and Student Experiences. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1345

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Motivation and engagement items (adapted Short Motivation and Engagement Scale) in the 526-responder questionnaire: "Eighty-four percent" persisted, 71% used time well, and majorities reported no anxiety (63%) or giving up (61%). Descriptive percentages only; no effect size is printed.

> "Eighty-four percent of the students agreed that they persisted even when the CA-PT was challenging or difficult. Most students did not want to receive a bad grade for this exam (77%), made good use of their time during the CA-PT (71%), and were focused on understanding the questions (71%)."
