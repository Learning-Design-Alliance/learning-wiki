### Paudel 2026

Paudel, K. R., Sotto, M., Jack-Edwards, F., & Shetty, P. (2026). Correlation of NBME pharmacology subject exam performance with medical school internal pharmacology scores and academic metrics. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57653-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the same retrospective dataset of 290 students; MCAT correlation with NBME Pharmacology scores was "moderate correlation" with 95% CI 0.35–0.54.

> "MCAT scores showed a moderate correlation (r = 0.45, p < 0.001), while GPA and BCPM were weak but statistically significant predictors (r = 0.18 each, p < 0.01)."
