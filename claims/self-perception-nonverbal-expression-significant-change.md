## Evidence

### Graf Joachim 2020

Graf Joachim, Loda Teresa, Zipfel Stephan, Wosnik Annette, Mohr Daniela, Herrmann-Werner Anne. (2020). Communication skills of medical students: survey of self- and external perception in a longitudinally based trend study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02049-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA across all seven semester cohorts of students' self-ratings (overall means: verbal expression 4.41, structure 4.38, empathy 4.37, non-verbal expression 4.11) found significance only for non-verbal expression; no effect size is printed.

> "All four dimensions improved slightly over time, although there was no significant change between the first and the last semester cohort when using the paired t-test. Only in the dimension of non-verbal expression could a statistically significant change be found (ANOVA, p = 0.006)."
