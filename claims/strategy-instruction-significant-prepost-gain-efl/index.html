Evidence¶

Bogale 2018¶

Bogale, Y. N. (2018). Conceptualizing Reading to Learn: Strategy Instruction and EFL Students' Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 10(2), 93–117. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207221

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test on the experimental group (n=55) in a pretest posttest quasi-experimental design; Table 2 reports pre-test mean 58.27 (SD 7.279) and post-test mean 62.73 (SD 6.993). The article reports the difference as "significantly different from their pre- test mean score" with no effect size printed.

"the post-test means scores of the participants instructed with the reading strategy instruction wa s significantly different from their pre- test mean score at a significant level of 0.05, indicating that the reading strategy instruction helped in enhancing students' reading comprehension."
