## Subclaims
`q3 i1` On a 15-point online retention test seven months post-course, there was no significant difference between the FC-TBL group (11.89 ± 1.82) and the LBL group (11.38 ± 1.86), with a small printed effect size (Cohen's d = 0.275) favouring FC-TBL. [→ Qu Meng 2024](#qu-meng-2024)

## Evidence
