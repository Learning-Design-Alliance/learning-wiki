Caskurlu 2025¶

Caskurlu, S., Ocak, C., & Dai, C.-P. (2025). The Scope of Multimodal Learning Analytics in K–8: A Systematic Review. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2), 224–236. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8505

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's systematic search of Web of Science, ERIC, ProQuest, APA PsycInfo and manual searches yielded 14 studies: 10 journal articles and 4 conference papers, spanning elementary (n=8), middle school (n=3), and both (n=3). The authors note "a small portion of MMLA research has focused on K–8 students."
