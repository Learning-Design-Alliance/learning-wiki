McElhaney 2024¶

McElhaney, K. W., & Kamdar, D. (2024). Updated summary and synthesis of OpenSciEd research [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/231

The review reports, citing Watkins and Penuel (2023), self-report surveys from more than 300 high school teachers who field tested biology, chemistry, or physics units and received up to six intensive PL workshops. No effect sizes are printed.

"more than 70% of teachers said the field test PL workshops prepared them to implement distinctive aspects of OpenSciEd pedagogy"
