Rowan 2021¶

Rowan, L., Bourke, T., L'Estrange, L., Lunn, J., Ryan, M., Walker, S., & Churchward, P. (2021). How does Initial Teacher Education Research frame the challenge of preparing future teachers for student diversity in schools?: A systematic review of literature. Review of Educational Research, 91(1), 112-158. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320979171

The review's interpretive synthesis of 209 included peer-reviewed articles reports three qualitatively different clusters of claims about preparing teachers for diversity, alongside the diversity groups referenced and constraining factors. The article states it analyzed "209 peer-reviewed journal articles (2009-2019)".

"Analyzing 209 peer-reviewed journal articles (2009-2019), the paper identifies: groups most frequently described as diverse; three qualitatively different clusters of claims regarding how teachers can be prepared for diversity; and factors identified as constraining preparation."
