q2 · i? · associational · r2

HLM analysis of the 2003 survey; the printed table reports professional development in technology at 0.36 (SE 0.05, p < 0.000) and teacher use of technology at 0.44 (SE 0.06, p < 0.000). Bar graphs of Grow scores by quartile of each factor show, in the brief's words, "large differences" — descriptive, untested visual contrasts. No standardized effect size is printed.

"We find that teachers' Grow use is strongly influenced by three important factors: Professional Development in Technology , Teachers' Own Use of Technology, and Principal Support for Grow."
