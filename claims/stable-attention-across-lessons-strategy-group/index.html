Evidence¶

Chanjuan Fu 2025¶

Chanjuan Fu, Xiaoxin Hao, Xinyue Shi, Hanxin Qian, Shanyun He & Fengji Geng. (2025). Effect of cognitive control strategies on young children’s attention and emotion during coding learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00360-z

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up analyses after a nonsignificant Group × Lesson interaction showed lesson differences in attentional state were significant in the control group, where children were more focused in Lesson 2 than Lesson 12 (t(10) = 2.945, p = 0.015, d = 0.560), but not in the experimental group.

"The difference observed across different lessons was signiﬁcant in the control group (ß = 0.200, SE = 0.070, t(26.501) = 2.841,p = 0.009), while such dif- ference was not signiﬁcant in the experimental group (p = 0.353)"
