q3 · i? · causal · r2

Signal detection analysis aggregating each participant's hit and false-alarm rates from raw pre- and post-training trials, averaged across the AB and BA counterbalance orderings so set-difficulty differences cancel. The article reports "a substantial improvement in discrimination sensitivity" while the criterion barely moved.

"Training produced a substantial improvement in discrimi- nation sensitivity (∆d′ = +0.36,p= 0.003) alongside a small, non-significant criterion shift toward classifying im- ages as AI-generated (∆c=−0.12,p= 0.230)."

Related Claims¶

  • A 30-minute generative AI literacy training increased intelligence analysts' overall accuracy at distinguishing real from AI-generated images by 9 percentage points — related
  • The training gain was driven by a 14.2-percentage-point improvement in accuracy on real images, while the gain on AI-generated images was not statistically significant — related
  • After training, analysts' free-text detection reasoning shifted away from physics-violation and anatomical cues toward functional and stylistic artifacts and became more specific — related
  • Training effects concentrated in portrait images (+39.8 percentage points, significant), with directional non-significant negative estimates for full-body and posed-group images — related
