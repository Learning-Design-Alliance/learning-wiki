## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2019

Zhao Xin, Yu Xiaofeng, Zhang Xiaomei. (2019). The Role of Peer Support Education Model in Management of Glucose and Lipid Levels in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Chinese Adults. Journal of Diabetes Research. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/5634030

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scale evaluation in the randomized study of 120 T2DM patients: the article reports a "significant change in levels of WHO-5 Happiness Scale, SDS scale, DSQOLS scale, and SDSCA scale" with t = −38.66, 9.75, −47.92, and 44.73, all p < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "Compared with the baseline, there was a significant change in levels of WHO-5 Happiness Scale, SDS scale, DSQOLS scale, and SDSCA scale in the two groups after 3 months of intervention (t = −38.66, p < 0.05; t = 9.75, p < 0.05; t = −47.92, p < 0.05; t = 44.73, p < 0.05)."
