# Exploratory factor analysis indicates a four-factor solution best fits the survey response data

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` EFA using scree test, variance explained, and interpretability criteria indicated a four-factor solution provided the best fit, with factors combining self-efficacy/social support/belonging, interest/intention, interest/motivation, and practical support. [→ Kao 2023](#kao-2023)

## Evidence

### Kao 2023

Kao, Y., Murphy, D., Hubbard Cheuoua, A., Kannan, P., Tsan, J., Jennings, K. E., Smith, H., Emanuel, S., & Miller, E. R. (2023). Survey development for assessing student computing career intentions: Technical report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638631

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis (maximum likelihood extraction, varimax and oblimin rotations) on the field-test responses compared solutions retaining two to seven factors. The article reports the four-factor solution had each factor accounting for at least 5 percent of variance, at least three items per factor, and simple structure under oblimin rotation.

> "Taken as a whole, the preponderance of the evidence from the results of the EFA indicated that a four-factor solution provided the best fit to the survey data."
