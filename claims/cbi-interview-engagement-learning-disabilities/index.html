Evidence¶

Gunarhadi 2016¶

Gunarhadi, Sunardi, Mohammad Anwar, Tri Rejeki Andayani, Abdull Sukor Shaari. (2016). +THE EFFECT OF CLUSTER-BASED INSTRUCTION ON MATHEMATIC ACHIEVEMENT IN INCLUSIVE SCHOOLS. International Journal of Special Education, 31(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099973

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative interview data with six students with learning disabilities, used as secondary data to support the quantitative findings. Students reported enjoying learning "through working with others"; no effect size applies.

"The result indicated that students with learning disability became increasingly enthusiastic in learning Mathematics. Four out of six students with learning disability enrolled in inclusive schools were seriously engaged in learning through CBI indeed."
