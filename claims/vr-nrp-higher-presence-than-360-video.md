### Mustafa Yalin Aydin 2024

Mustafa Yalin Aydin, Vernon Curran, Susan White, Lourdes Peña-Castillo, Oscar Meruvia-Pastor. (2024). VR-NRP: A Virtual Reality Simulation for Training in the Neonatal Resuscitation Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.15598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.15598)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the crossover questionnaire study, presence was measured with a 17-question instrument; the VR simulation showed "an increased feeling of presence" with FDR-corrected WMW p<0.01 on 10 of 17 questions. No effect size was printed.

> "They also reported an increased feeling of presence when experiencing the VR simulation (FDR-corrected WMW test p<0.01 for 10 out of 17 questions of the presence questionnaire)."
