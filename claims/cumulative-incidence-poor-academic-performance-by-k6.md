Descriptive cumulative-incidence analysis (Figure 2) of the 1823-participant cohort; the printed percentages are 24.4%, 25.7%, and 41.4%, with the figure noting p < 0.0001 versus the 0–4-point group.

> "A continuous increase in the crude cumulative incidences of poor academic performance starts at 24.4% in the 0–4‐point K6 group, 25.7% in the 5–12‐point K6 group, and 41.4% in the 13–24‐point K6 group"
