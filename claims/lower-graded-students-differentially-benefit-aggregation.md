## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young 2026

Nicholas T. Young, Rebecca L. Matz, Eric F. Bell, and Caitlin Hayward. (2026). Reducing the weight of low exam scores may raise average grades but does not appear to impact equity gaps. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/ym5f-hs2g](https://doi.org/10.1103/ym5f-hs2g)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Differential analysis comparing per-student grade increases from each alternative method between students who earned an A versus B, C, D, or E in Physics I, using t-tests and Cohen's d (Table V). The non-significant pairs were all under the replace-lowest-with-final method. A earners still outperformed other students under every method.

> "We find a statistically significant difference of at least 0.45 percentage points and as much as 2.13 percentage points in 10 of the 11 aggregation-method-and-assessment-style pairs."
