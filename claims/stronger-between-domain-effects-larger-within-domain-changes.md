# Stronger between-domain effects in the I/E model versions are associated with stronger changes in within-domain effects after controlling for the between-domain effects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across country subsamples in the PISA 2000 reanalysis, the magnitude of change in within-domain effects after controlling for between-domain effects scaled with the strength of those between-domain effects. [→ Fabian Wolff 2026](#fabian-wolff-2026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Secondary analysis of PISA 2000 country subsamples comparing classic and difference I/E models. The authors report that "stronger between-domain effects in the two I/E model versions were related to stronger changes in the within-domain effects after controlling for the between-domain effects," citing Dutch and Latvian subsamples as examples.
