Evidence¶

Louise Starkey 2023¶

Louise Starkey, Anne Yates, Mairead de Roiste, Karsten Lundqvist, Adreanne Ormond, John Randal, Allan Sylvester. (2023). Each discipline is different: teacher capabilities for future-focussed digitally infused undergraduate programmes. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10196-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Cross-case thematic analysis of five written case studies by disciplinary experts in one New Zealand university, developed through appreciative inquiry group discussion and abductive reasoning. The authors report that "the context and disciplinary culture" shape pedagogy and technology use; no effect size is reported.

"This study has found that in Higher Education, it is the context and disciplinary culture; the traditions, skills and knowledge within a subject that influences pedagogical practices, how digital technology is used, and the capabilities needed to use digital technology within each context."
