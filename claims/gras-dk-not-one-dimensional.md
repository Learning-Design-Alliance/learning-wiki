## Evidence

### Andersen Nina Bjerre 2014

Andersen Nina Bjerre, O'Neill Lotte, Gormsen Lise Kirstine, Hvidberg Line, Morcke Anne Mette. (2014). A validation study of the psychometric properties of the Groningen Reflection Ability Scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-214

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis (principal component analysis with varimax rotation) conducted after the CFA was rejected. The authors concluded that "no factor model could explain enough of the variance to be a satisfactory fit" and that reversed items seemed to function poorly.

> "The EFA, which included trying a set three factor model as well as leaving items with consistent low loadings out of the analysis, produced a diffuse distribution of loadings that did not conform to a one-dimensional model."
