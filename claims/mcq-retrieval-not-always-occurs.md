## Evidence

### van den Broek 2023

van den Broek, G., Gerritsen, S. L., Oomen, I. T. J., Velthoven, E., van Boxtel, F. H. J., Kester, L., & van Gog, T. (2023). Optimizing multiple-choice questions for retrieval practice: Delayed display of answer alternatives enhances vocabulary learning. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000810

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's motivating rationale, stated as a premise rather than a tested result in the available text: MCQ benefits depend on retrieval from memory, which easy-to-guess options can bypass. The article offers this as the theoretical motivation for the stepwise manipulation.

> "Answering MCQs is beneficial for learning, especially when learners retrieve knowledge from memory to evaluate plausible answer alternatives. However, such retrieval may not always occur (e.g., with easy-to-guess answers)."
