Related Claims¶

  • Gene-based tests identify associations including KCNC1 with pure-tone phonological composite, GTF3C1 with Chinese vocabulary measures, and CATSPERD with English word reading — related
  • Hong Kong sample phenotypes show genetic dependence with GenLang reading traits, with 42 of 170 trait pairs reaching nominal significance and 22 reaching FDR < 0.1, mostly with word reading — related
  • Five independent loci reach genome-wide significance for language/literacy phenotypes including Chinese vocabulary, character and word reading, rapid digit naming, and English lexical decision in Hong Kong Chinese bilingual children — related
  • The most significant single-variant association is rs6905617 with English Lexical Decision (p = 3.29E-09), located near MANEA and MANEA-AS1 — related
