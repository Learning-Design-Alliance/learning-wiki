Tamara van Gog & Fred Paas & John Sweller. (2010). Cognitive Load Theory: Advances in Research on Worked Examples, Animations, and Cognitive Load Measurement. Educ Psychol Rev (2010) 22:375–378. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9145-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Salden et al. (2010), studies on integrating worked examples in cognitive tutoring systems; benefits held against tutored problem solving, indicating the worked example effect is "not an artefact of lousy control conditions" (Schwonke et al. 2009).

> "The studies reviewed by Salden et al. show, however, that worked examples have beneficial effects on learning (either in terms of performance, time, or both) even when compared to tutored problem solving in which instructional guidance is available on demand"
