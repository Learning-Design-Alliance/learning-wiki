## Evidence

### Domen 2020

Domen, J., Hornstra, L., Weijers, D., van der Veen, I., & Peetsma, T. (2020). Differentiated need support by teachers: Student‐specific provision of autonomy and structure and relations with student motivation. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(2), 403–423. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12302

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between‐level path from the multilevel model for autonomous motivation using aggregated class means. The printed coefficient was b = −.28, p = .008; the model explained 64.0% of between‐level variance in autonomous motivation.

> "The results indicated a negative relation between teacher perceptions of autonomy support and students' autonomous motivation (b = −.28, p = .008), indicating that in classes where teachers – on average – report higher levels of autonomy support, students' autonomous motivation is actually lower."
