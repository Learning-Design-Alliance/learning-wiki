## Evidence

### Badeleh 2011

Alireza Badeleh. (2011). The Effect of Laboratory Training Model of Teaching and Traditional Method on Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Skills-Components of Achievement, Total Achievement and Retention Level in Chemistry. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 7, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102743

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Delayed post-test administered after a one-month gap to both groups; independent sample t-test (Table 8) prints means of 56.2958 (Laboratory) vs 27.2583 (Traditional), n = 60 per group. No effect size printed.

> "the t-value of 18.019 was found to be statistically significant as the p value of .000 was smaller than the cut-off .05. This showed that there was significant difference between students taught by LTM, and traditional methods with respect to post scores in retention of achievement in chemistry"
