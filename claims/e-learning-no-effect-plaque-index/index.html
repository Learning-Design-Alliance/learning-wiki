Evidence¶

Kaneyasu Y 2023¶

Kaneyasu Y, Shigeishi H, Sugiyama M, Ohta K. (2023). Effectiveness of e-learning to promote oral health education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000036550

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials comparing e-learning with conventional education on plaque index. The pooled SMD was 0.12 (95% CI −0.33 to 0.57), "thus not indicating a better result for the groups that received e-learning"; heterogeneity was high (I2 = 91%, P < .001) and the difference was not significant (P = .60).

"The pooled standardized mean difference (SMD) of the PI in 8 studies[21,33,36,39,42,44,46,47] was 0.12 (95% CI −0.33 to 0.57) , thus not indicating a better result for the groups that received e-learning"
