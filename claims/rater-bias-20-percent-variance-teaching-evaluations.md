### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance-component analysis of 145 students' ratings. Table 1 prints rater proportions of .182 (trailer) and .184 (class) for teaching evaluations; rater effects for positive and negative affect exceeded 35% of variance.

> "There were large rater effects for teaching evaluations for both trailers and live lectures, accounting for nearly 20% of the variance (Table 1)."
