### Ji Zhe 2024

Ji Zhe, Jiang Yuliang, Sun Haitao, Qiu Bin, Chen Yi, Li Mao, Fan Jinghong, Wang Junjie. (2024). Enhancing puncture skills training with generative AI and digital technologies: a parallel cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06217-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cumulative learning-curve analysis plotting aggregate checklist scores against practice sessions, reported in the Results learning-curve section and Fig. 3, calculating slope, time to plateau, and goodness of fit. The printed slopes name Group 3 highest on both measures, with R2 of 0.81–0.91 across groups.

> "For theoretical knowledge, Group 3's learning curve slope was 13.02, higher than Group 1 (5.17) and Group 2 (11.28). For skills, Group 3's learning curve slope was 0.62, higher than Group 1 (0.53) and Group 2 (0.38). All groups had good curve fitting (R2: 0.81–0.91)."
