Rana Abou Khamis 2026¶

Rana Abou Khamis, Hala Assal, and Ashraf Matrawy. (2026). Unanticipated Effects of Generative AI on Expertise Pathways and Performance Perception in System Administration. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28650

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 14 sysadmins and IT professionals using GenAI in daily work, analyzed with Braun and Clarke's reflexive thematic analysis. Participants described GenAI as "shortcuts expertise development" by removing hands-on practice and mentorship. No effect size is reported; findings are qualitative themes.

"While GenAI accelerates task execution and allows less-experienced sysadmins to produce work quickly, it simultaneously shortcuts expertise development by eliminating the traditional sequence of hands-on practice, mentorship, and iterative problem solving."
