Exploring paths of change: Analyzing teachers’ emotion regulation repertoire over time using multi-level latent profile analysis. This is the article's background hypothesis motivating the study: "As teachers gain job experience, they may select different, more adaptive strategies." The abstract offers this as a possibility rather than a tested effect size, and the profile-membership finding on less experienced teachers is reported separately without an effect size.
