## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The control (slideshow) group showed higher post-test scores and higher pre-to-post gain (delta_test) than the VR group, and the condition x test performance interaction was significant. [→ Battipede 2025](#battipede-2025)

## Evidence

### Battipede 2025

Battipede, E., Giangualano, A., Boffi, P., Clerici, M., Calvi, A., Cassenti, L., Cialini, R., Van Den Weghe, T. L. A., Addimando, L., Lanzi, P. L., Gallace, A. (2025). Physics Playground: Insights from a Qualitative-Quantitative Study about VR-Based Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-88881-6_8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-88881-6_8)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 44-participant randomized experiment, a Mann-Whitney U test on delta_test (post minus pre score) showed the "control condition having a higher mean" than the VR condition; post-test scores also differed significantly (U = 152, p = .029).

> "A Mann-Whitney U test showed that the means of this variable were significantly different between conditions ( U = 130, p = .008), with the control condition having a higher mean ( M = 4.27, SD = 2.35) than the VR condition (M = 2.32, SD = 2.03)."
