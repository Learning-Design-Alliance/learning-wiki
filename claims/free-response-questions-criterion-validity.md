Criterion validation comparing coded video vignettes with coded written responses for each LA at TTU and FIU; the authors report "reasonable agreement between the video and written free-response responses", with student AF mostly Qa and Qb in both.

> "We found reasonable agreement between the video and written free-response responses. For example, for student AF, the written responses were mostly Qa and Qb and the video was also mostly Qa and Qb."
