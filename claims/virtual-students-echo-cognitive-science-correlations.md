## Evidence

### Songlin Xu 2024

Songlin Xu, Xinyu Zhang, Lianhui Qin. (2024). EduAgent: Generative Student Agents in Learning. Preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2404.07963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.07963)

`q1 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Experiment 2 correlation analysis of 705 simulated virtual students with no real demonstration data, using Pearson coefficients between personas and behaviors. The article reports "agents with high learning curiosity personas are also highly curious in cognitive states(r = 0.92)", r = -0.44 for smart persona vs workload, and r = 0.44 for attentive persona vs focus.

> "We find that agents with high learning curiosity personas are also highly curious in cognitive states(r = 0.92). Smart personas correspond to low course workload (r = -0.44) and attentive personas lead to better focus ( r = 0.44)."
