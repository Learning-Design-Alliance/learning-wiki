The review's procedural integrity analysis distinguishing initial training adherence from ongoing treatment fidelity; where fidelity indices were reported (four studies), each equaled 100% based on checklist observations in 21% to 72% of sessions.

> "Seven of nine studies did not include a numerical index to quantify adherence to initial training procedures. Both of two remaining studies included a numerical index of 100% for adherence to initial training procedures."
