`q2 i3` SDLRS correlated significantly with all ten LASSI subscales, with first-measure correlations ranging from r(152) = .255 to r(152) = .592. [→ Zhou Yuanyuan 2016](#zhou-yuanyuan-2016)

## Evidence

### Zhou Yuanyuan 2016

Zhou Yuanyuan, Graham Lori, West Courtney. (2016). The relationship between study strategies and academic performance. Int J Med Educ 7:324-332. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.57dc.fe0f

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis with scatterplots in the 168-student cohort confirmed "the linear bivariate positive correlation between the SDLRS and each LASSI subscale"; Selecting Main Idea and Information Processing had the highest correlations with SDLRS in both measures, with the largest printed correlation r = .592 (a large effect by the r code table).

> "The SDLRS was significantly correlated with all LASSI subscales in both the first and the second measure: the correlations ranged from r(152) = .255, p = .001 to r(152) = .592, p = .000 for the first measure"
