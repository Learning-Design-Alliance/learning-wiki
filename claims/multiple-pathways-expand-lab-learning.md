The report's argument connecting accessibility, creativity, and innovation. It cites Wanda Díaz Merced's sonification of astronomical data and Steven Sahyun's auditory and tactile graph representations as examples where accessibility adaptations provide new ways for all learners to engage with data. This is the report's interpretation, not a tested result.

> "The development of multiple pathways for laboratory investigation creates opportunities to expand the learning of all participants in a physics lab program."
