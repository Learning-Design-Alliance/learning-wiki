## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2021

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Twelve two-step hierarchical models per exam (exam 1 n = 424; exam 2 n = 471), each adding one strategy to the base model. The article reports that passive strategies were "either nonsignificant or negatively related to performance on at least one exam."

> "students who reported having completed problem sets, explained concepts, self-quizzed, or attended review sessions earned 4.0–7.7% higher on average on both exams 1 and 2 than students who did not report using the strategy"

### Walck-Shannon 2021 (2)

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc analysis adding all six active strategies into the model in the same step, indicating non-overlapping variance explained by these three strategies. The article notes this was not part of the planned analyses.

> "we found that the following active strategies were distinctly predictive for both exams 1 and 2: explaining concepts, self-quizzing, and completing problem sets (Supplemental Table 6)."
