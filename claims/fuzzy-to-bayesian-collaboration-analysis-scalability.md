B. Barros & M. F. Verdejo (2015). Computer-Based Interaction Analysis with DEGREE Revisited. Int J Artif Intell Educ (2016) 26:113–123. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0063-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports its fourth core contribution: a fuzzy model that infers qualitative indicators from quantitative indicators obtained statistically from the log, dialogue model and outcome schema, plus teachers' expert knowledge. The authors state the modelling of teachers' criteria "was very tedious and not easily scalable", motivating the Bayesian redesign cited to Barros et al. (2007).

> "This process was very tedious and not easily scalable, and so we redesigned the method to use Bayesian reasoning to analyse larger groups, such as small learning communities (Barros et al. 2007)."
