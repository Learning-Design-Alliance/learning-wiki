Sufficiency analysis in the fsQCA identified three configurations for high learning engagement. C1 (core competence; peripheral autonomy and relatedness; GAI unconstrained) had the highest coverage and was retained under both raised thresholds. Overall solution coverage was 0.858.

> "C1 had a consistency of 0.832, a PRI of 0.757, a raw coverage of 0.829, and a unique coverage of 0.250. Both its raw coverage and unique coverage were the highest among the three configurations, indicating that it was the principal configuration with the broadest empirical coverage."
