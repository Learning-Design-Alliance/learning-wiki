## Evidence

### Mendoza 2025

Mendoza, N. B., Zadorozhnyy, A., & Dizon, J. I. W. T. (2025). A rising tide lifts all boats: The social contagion of achievement in L2 classrooms and the role of intrinsic motivation and engagement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1155–1175. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12752

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects Model 6 (766 students, 30 classrooms) tested the relative-achievement × IM-to-Experience-Stimulation interaction. The article reports the "interaction term was also significant [β = .10, SE = .03, t(731.98) = 3.45, p < .001]". Only the coefficient is printed; no standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Importantly, the interaction term was also significant [β = .10, SE = .03, t(731.98) = 3.45, p < .001], suggesting that ACE was stronger for students with higher levels of IM to Experience Stimulation."

### Mendoza 2025 (2)

Mendoza, N. B., Zadorozhnyy, A., & Dizon, J. I. W. T. (2025). A rising tide lifts all boats: The social contagion of achievement in L2 classrooms and the role of intrinsic motivation and engagement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1155–1175. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12752

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slopes probing of Model 6's interaction: the effect "increased from β = .60 at low levels (−1 SD) to β = .80 at high levels (+1 SD) of IM to Experience Stimulation". JN analysis indicated ACE was significant across all observed levels [1.33, 7.00]. Slope values are unstandardized coefficients; no effect size printed.

> "Simple slopes analysis showed that the effect of relative achievement on Time 2 achievement increased from β = .60 at low levels (−1 SD) to β = .80 at high levels (+1 SD) of IM to Experience Stimulation."
