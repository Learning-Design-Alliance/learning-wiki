## Evidence

### Rosemary Luckin 2008

Rosemary Luckin. (2008). The Learner Centric Ecology of Resources: A Framework for using Technology to Scaffold Learning. Computers & Education 50, 449-462. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.09.018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interviews and parental diaries during the HOMEWORK evaluations showed homework activity was often formalised, e.g. at a table in a family space. Parents "who reported having spent increased time working with their child at home also reported increased enjoyment" and felt better informed and more able to support learning.

> "those who reported having spent increased time working with their child at home also reported increased enjoyment."

### Rosemary Luckin 2008 (2)

Rosemary Luckin. (2008). The Learner Centric Ecology of Resources: A Framework for using Technology to Scaffold Learning. Computers & Education 50, 449-462. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.09.018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Teacher interview data from the HOMEWORK project showed "teachers were keen to have lesson planning tools and were willing to spend time on customisation provided the technology was integrated into their normal classroom practice", and that they appreciated monitoring lesson activity and offering alternative homework forms.

> "teachers were keen to have lesson planning tools and were willing to spend time on customisation provided the technology was integrated into their normal classroom practice"
