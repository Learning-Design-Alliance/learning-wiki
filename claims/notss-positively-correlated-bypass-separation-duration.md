## Evidence

### Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD 2024

Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD, Dias Roger D. MD PhD, Kennedy-Metz Lauren R. PhD, Varni Giovanna PhD, Gombolay Matthew PhD, Yule Steven PhD, Salas Eduardo PhD, Zenati Marco A. MD. (2024). Deep Learning Analysis of Surgical Video Recordings to Assess Nontechnical Skills. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.22520

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of NOTSS scores against phase duration in the 30-case cross-sectional dataset; the article reports "ρ = 0.47, P = .008" and interprets longer separation time as accompanying better-rated nontechnical skills.

> "The overall NOTSS score was positively correlated with the duration of the separation from the bypass phase (ρ = 0.47, P = .008), meaning that teams that took longer to terminate the patient from bypass were rated as presenting better nontechnical skills."
