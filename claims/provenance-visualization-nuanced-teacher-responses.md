`q2 i?` In a qualitative evaluation focus group, five teachers reported that seeing detailed student problem-solving provenance alongside overall accuracy helped them gain a more nuanced interpretation of student mistakes and tailor responses to different students. Grace Guo, Aishwarya Mudgal Sunil Kumar, Adit Gupta, Adam Coscia, Christopher J. MacLellan, and Alex Endert. (2024). Visualizing Intelligent Tutor Interactions for Responsive Pedagogy. International Conference on Advanced Visual Interfaces 2024 (AVI 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3656650.3656667

Qualitative evaluation focus group with the same five teachers who participated in the design study, categorizing feedback via affinity diagramming. Teachers valued stepwise provenance combined with summary statistics, finding it “useful to see what part of the process that they get tripped on”.
