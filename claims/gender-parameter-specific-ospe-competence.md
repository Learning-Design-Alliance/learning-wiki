`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Binary logistic regression in the RCT examined gender effects on OSPE parameters. The infection control parameter showed "a significant difference between males and females" with p value 0.000 and odds ratio 40.3; operator position was significant at p = 0.001 (OR 40.389); tray organization showed no significant gender difference.

> "The effect of the infection control parameter on the students' competence level demonstrated a significant difference between males and females (p value, 0.000; odds ratio, 40.3) in which females were considered constant."
