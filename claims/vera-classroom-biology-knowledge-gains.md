## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in a general ecology lecture course (N=52 paired pre/post) scored significantly higher on biology-related questions in the post-test compared to the pre-test after using VERA (p=0.0322). [→ An 2022](#an-2022)

## Evidence

### An 2022

An, S., Bates, R., Rugaber, S., Hammock, J., Weigel, E., & Goel, A. K. (2022). Cognitive Assistance for Inquiry-Based Modeling. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Classroom quasi-experiment in a general ecology lecture course at a large southeastern R1 university (N=91 enrolled, 52 completed paired pre/post-test). Students used VERA across four increasingly complex predator-prey modeling phases. The article reports a statistically significant pre-to-post gain on biology questions with "p value=0.0322 at 0.05 level"; no effect size is printed.

> "the calculated t-value between the pre-test and the post-test on questions pertaining to biology is found to be signiﬁcant (p value=0.0322 at 0.05 level)"
