### B. Barros & M. F. Verdejo 2015

B. Barros & M. F. Verdejo (2015). Computer-Based Interaction Analysis with DEGREE Revisited. Int J Artif Intell Educ (2016) 26:113–123. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0063-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Authors' discussion of the state of educational data mining and learning analytics: they observe that most studies collect data from a variety of sources, process it mainly by counting, and visualize it, while identifying "a pending issue is how to exploit the potential of the mined data in the learning cycle" for awareness, formative assessment, feedback, evaluation and intervention.

> "there is still a gap in interpreting and modelling this information in terms of understanding the learning process and how the actors interact. Thus, a pending issue is how to exploit the potential of the mined data in the learning cycle"
