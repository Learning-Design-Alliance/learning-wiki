## Evidence

### Phehla 2024

Phehla, L.B., Makhene, A. & Matshaka, L., 2024, 'Student nurses' utilisation of self-directed simulation learning at a University in Gauteng,' Curationis 47(1), a2490. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v47i1.2490

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from the subtheme on limited access to the clinical simulation laboratory. Participants "cited that access to the clinical simulation laboratory was restricted", with one participant noting a planned self-practice session was abandoned because the laboratory closes at 4pm.

> "Participants cited that access to the clinical simulation laboratory was restricted as the clinical simulation laboratory operates from Monday to Friday, between 07:30 and 16:00 at the host HEI."
