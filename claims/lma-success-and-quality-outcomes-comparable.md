## Evidence

### Liu Zi-Jia 2017

Liu Zi-Jia, Yi Jie, Chen Wei-Yun, Zhang Xiu-Hua, Huang Yu-Guang, Martin Samantha. (2017). Comparison of learning performance of 2 intubating laryngeal mask airways in novice: A randomized crossover manikin study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000006905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Initial evaluation after training in the randomized crossover manikin study: the article reports "no significant differences were shown in success rates and first attempt success rate between 2 LMAs"; at follow-up, total insertion success remained similarly high for both devices.

> "For the immediate evaluation after training, no significant differences were shown in success rates and first attempt success rate between 2 LMAs."

### Liu Zi-Jia 2017 (2)

Liu Zi-Jia, Yi Jie, Chen Wei-Yun, Zhang Xiu-Hua, Huang Yu-Guang, Martin Samantha. (2017). Comparison of learning performance of 2 intubating laryngeal mask airways in novice: A randomized crossover manikin study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000006905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fiberoptic bronchoscopy assessment of LMA position in the same crossover manikin study; glottic view grades were statistically comparable between devices at initial evaluation (P = .50) and at the 90-day reevaluation (P = .71), with satisfactory views for both devices.

> "No significant differences in the view of the glottis confirmed by bronchoscopy were found between 2 groups (i-gel: 66% grade 1, 32% grade 2, 2% grade 3; Aura-i: 73% grade 1, 25% grade 2, 2% grade 3, P = .50)."
