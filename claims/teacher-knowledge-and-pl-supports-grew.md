# Many teachers' knowledge of students' GC and CoV increased in sophistication over the school year, and teachers reported the professional learning and just-in-time supports were helpful

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In post-interviews teachers were more attentive to how students described relationships between variables and to connections among data, graphs, and claims, rather than variable identification or generic graph descriptions. [→ McElhaney 2025](#mcelhaney-2025)
`q2 i?` Teachers reported that just-in-time experiences (mid-year PL workshop, pre-enactment check-in meetings) were helpful to their teaching. [→ McElhaney 2025 (2)](#mcelhaney-2025-2)

## Evidence

### McElhaney 2025

McElhaney, K. W., Hodkowski, N. M., McMahon, L. H., Jacobs, J., Penuel, W. R., Anton, G., Henson, K., Urban, R., & Parker, D. A. (2025). Promoting Mathematical Reasoning in OpenSciEd Middle School Curriculum Units. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4ea4b3c5-c663-4484-8727-258bff346d2d

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of pre- and post-year teacher interviews about hypothetical student work found shifts in teachers' perceptions of students' GC and CoV: "teachers were more attentive in the post-interview to the ways that students described relationships between variables."

> "teachers were more attentive in the post-interview to the ways that students described relationships between variables and less attentive to whether students merely identified variables as dependent or independent."

### McElhaney 2025 (2)

McElhaney, K. W., Hodkowski, N. M., McMahon, L. H., Jacobs, J., Penuel, W. R., Anton, G., Henson, K., Urban, R., & Parker, D. A. (2025). Promoting Mathematical Reasoning in OpenSciEd Middle School Curriculum Units. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4ea4b3c5-c663-4484-8727-258bff346d2d

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-PL questionnaires, interviews, and written reflections indicated teachers valued learning connections across GC, CoV, and science content, discussing pedagogy, and the Data Routine, and "reported in interviews and written reflections that the just-in-time experiences (mid-year PL workshop, pre-enactment check-in meetings) were helpful."

> "Teachers also reported in interviews and written reflections that the just-in-time experiences (mid-year PL workshop, pre-enactment check-in meetings) were helpful."
