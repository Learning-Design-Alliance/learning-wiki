## Evidence

### Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos, Patelarou Athina, Laliotis Aggelos, Wan Andrew C, Matalliotakis Michail, Tsiou Chrysoula, Patelarou Evridiki. (2016). Educational strategies for teaching evidence-based practice to undergraduate health students: systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5066070/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Controlled trial of 14 two-hour weekly EBM sessions for medical students (n=289) in Mexico, comparing exposed and non-exposed year groups. Confidence in critical appraisal skills was 17.1 (3.6) for exposed 5th-years versus 8.4 (5.7) for non-exposed 5th-years, P<0.001.

> "Confidence in critical appraisal skills: mean (SD) M4=11.7 (6.3) vs. M5 non-EBM=8.4 (5.7) vs. M5 EBM=17.1 (3.6) vs. M6=16.8 (4), P<0.001"
