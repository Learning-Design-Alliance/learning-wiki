## Evidence

### Jessica Watkins 2010

Jessica Watkins, Janet E. Coffey, Edward F. Redish, & Todd J. Cooke. (2010). Disciplinary authenticity: Enriching the reforms of introductory physics courses for life-science students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1109.4983](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1109.4983)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical analysis of an exam problem from a reformed IPLS course. The authors note the problem could be situated in a discussion of co-evolution in predator-prey relations to increase its biological authenticity, but it was one of a set of kinematics problems in a course not focused on bridging the disciplines.

> "Although there is a backdrop of biology in that living organisms are being considered, this problem does not demand that students engage in using mathematical and physical tools for biological ends."
