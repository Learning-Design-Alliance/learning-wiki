# Nurses' age, hospital level, and perceived EBN knowledge are significant related factors of EBP competency in multiple linear regression

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the multiple linear regression, age (β = −0.132), hospital level (β = 0.069), and perceived EBN knowledge (β = 0.162) significantly related to EBP competency. [→ Fu Liang 2020](#fu-liang-2020)

## Evidence

### Fu Liang 2020

Fu Liang, Wei Su, Xianghong Ye, Ming Li, Jun Shen, Chen Chen, Qi Guo, Liping Ye and Yuhong He. (2020). Evidence-Based Practice Competency and Related Factors Among Nurses Working in Public Hospitals. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/0046958020927876

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 1036-nurse survey sample, entering variables significant at P < .20 in univariate analyses. The model was "statistically significant (F = 9.858, P < .001)" with standardized coefficients β = −0.132, 0.069, and 0.162, explaining 6.3% of variance.

> "The significant related factors were age (β = −0.132, P < .001), hospital level (β = 0.069, P < .05), and perceived EBN knowledge (β = 0.162, P < .01). The regression model was statistically significant (F = 9.858, P < .001) and had an explanatory power of 6.3%."
