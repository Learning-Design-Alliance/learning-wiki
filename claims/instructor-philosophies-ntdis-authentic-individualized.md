## Evidence

### Dietrich EL 2025

Dietrich EL, McWatt SC. (2025). Exploring perceptions of alternative assessment and grading in graduate anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11797536/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured instructor interviews (n = 4, all course instructors) identified authentic learning experiences as a theme; instructors linked oral, public, frequent evaluation to thinking on their feet and workplace-like feedback reception.

> "I opted for things like a public evaluation, and it's meant to be emphasizing and engaging those real‐world skill sets, like being able to public speak [and] being able to communicate in front of your peers."

### Dietrich EL 2025 (2)

Dietrich EL, McWatt SC. (2025). Exploring perceptions of alternative assessment and grading in graduate anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11797536/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Under the individualized assessment theme, instructors described tailoring conversational assessments to student responses, redirecting conversations going too broad or deep, and opening with easy questions to calm assessment-related nerves.

> "I can tell, [students] walk in and they're almost shaking, I try to disarm that, I try to start with kind of an easy question just to get the conversation going, just to get the nerves calmed down."
