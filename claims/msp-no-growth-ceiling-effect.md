### Sean Savage 2026

Sean Savage, Anand Shanker, Grace Michlitsch, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Using LLMs to Detect Growth in Computational Thinking in Introductory Physics. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06200

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on the human-graded sample (N=50) found no growth in MSP, with a printed effect size of d = 0.00 and p = 1.000. The pre-instruction mean for MSP on Q27 was 1.62 against a rubric maximum of 2, which the authors call "an assessment ceiling effect".

> "However, Modeling and Simulation Practices showed no statistical growth (p= 1.000,d= 0.00). This lack of change reflects an assessment ceiling effect, in which students entered the course already able to list simulation pa- rameters, leaving no room for measurable growth on this spe- cific prompt."
