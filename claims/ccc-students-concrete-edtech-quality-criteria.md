`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Phase 1 focus groups and survey with CCC students surfaced concrete edtech quality expectations. Students "want edtech that supports learners with varying digital skills, backgrounds, and access to devices", alongside demands that vendors prove claims with evidence, that products be user-friendly and accessible, be designed with community college learner input, and connect students with peers and faculty.
