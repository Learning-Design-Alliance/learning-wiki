## Evidence

### Fryer 2021

Fryer, L. K., Shum, A., Lee, A., & Lau, P. (2021). Mapping students' interest in a new domain: Connecting prior knowledge, interest and self-efficacy with interesting tasks and a lasting desire to reengage. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101493

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal-SEM fully-forward model of the 310-student course study. The model shows prior Domain interest predicted "just one Task interest (H1a: Task6, β=.25)" and post Domain interest, with no direct path to Course interest.

> "prior Domain interest presented significant predictive direct relationships with just one Task interest (H1a: Task6, β=.25) and post Domain interest (H1a: β=.24), but not future Course interest (H1a: β=-.02, p=.53)."
