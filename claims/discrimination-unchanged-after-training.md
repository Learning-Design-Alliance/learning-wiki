## Evidence

### Kristin Kolloff 2025

Kristin Kolloff, Ebru Ger, Claudia M. Roebers. (2025). The role of metacognitive experience: Can feedback training affect kindergarteners' monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09422-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model on relative monitoring accuracy found measurement point and both feedback conditions were non-significant predictors. The article states children's ability to discriminate between correct and incorrect answers did not change over training, contrary to expectations.

> "measurement point was not a significant predictor (γ = 0.02, SE = 0.10, t (214) = 0.19, p = 0.85). Further, we found no main effects for MC Monitoring Feedback yielded (γ = 0.03, SE = 0.15, t(214) = 2.13, p = 0.83)"
