## Evidence

### Eisenmann D 2018

Eisenmann D, Stroben F, Gerken JD, Exadaktylos AK, Machner M, Hautz WE. (2018). Interprofessional Emergency Training Leads to Changes in the Workplace. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.11.35275

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-month follow-up survey (t2) of the same C2C cohort; 32 participants (50%) provided written comments on their original commitments. All realization figures are self-reported by participants.

> "At follow-up, 57 of the 62 (91.9%) commitments were reported to be at least partly realized, and only five (8.1%) commitments (still) lacked realization. The best rate of commitment realization was (self-) reported by trainees of emergency nursing, with 13 fully implemented commitments out of 31 (41.9%)."
