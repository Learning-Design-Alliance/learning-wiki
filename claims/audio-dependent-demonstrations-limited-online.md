## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In one online physics course, a resonance demonstration's auditory effect was limited by audio equipment, and some students could not hear subtle sounds, limiting the demonstration's expected learning outcome. [→ Da Yang Tan & Jer-Ming Chen 2021](#da-yang-tan-jer-ming-chen-2021)

## Evidence

### Da Yang Tan & Jer-Ming Chen 2021

Da Yang Tan & Jer-Ming Chen. (2021). Bringing Physical Physics Classroom On line – Challenges of Online Teaching in the New Normal. The Physics Teacher (submitted). Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0028641](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0028641)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Practitioner report from the online course's demonstration sessions. The perceived effect of the resonant sound from the second passive sound box was limited by the instructor's microphone receiver and students' speakers or headphones, and student reports indicated the intended outcome was not fully achieved.

> "some students flagged that they were unable to hear clearly when the resonances were mismatched (a subtler sound), and this limited the expected learning outcome of this demonstration."
