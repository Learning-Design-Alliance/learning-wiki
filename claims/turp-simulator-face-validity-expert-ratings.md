`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face validity questionnaire study at EUREP 2025 in which fourteen expert consultant urologists rated the TURP simulator on 4-point Likert items. Highest mean scores were "3.86 ± 0.36" for resectoscope handling/ergonomics and procedural steps; the study reports the lowest ratings were for intraoperative bleeding and obturator reflex (mean 1.07 ± 0.27), with visibility and access at 3.29 ± 0.73.

> "The mean rating for the model’s usefulness in teaching handling and ergonomics of the resectoscope was 3.86 ± 0.36, and for practicing procedural steps such as resection of the median and lateral lobes was 3.86 ± 0.36."
