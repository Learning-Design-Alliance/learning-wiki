### Phenyo Phemelo Moletsane 2026

Phenyo Phemelo Moletsane, Michael W. Asher, Christine Kwon, Paulo F. Carvalho, and Amy Ogan. (2026). Inclusive Mobile Learning: How Technology-Enabled Language Choice Supports Multilingual Students. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791139

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prize-code accuracy analysis of 1,326 learners' live-broadcast listening; fewer than 14% submitted a correct code in any given week. "learners enrolled in the English version of the course were significantly less likely to tune in for live broadcasts than those enrolled in the Leb Lango version"; Leb Lango and Hybrid engaged at similar rates (OR=.90, p=.484).

> "learners enrolled in the English version of the course were significantly less likely to tune in for live broadcasts than those enrolled in the Leb Lango version ( 𝑂𝑅=. 47, 𝑧=− 4.40, 𝑝<. 001)"
