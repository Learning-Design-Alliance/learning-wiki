# ACT scores relate positively to transfer-question performance but show a different, inconsistent pattern for retention questions

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` ACT interacted with question type, F(1, 62) = 4.17, p = 0.046, ηp2 = 0.06, with a more positive ACT-performance slope for transfer items (0.011) than retention items (0.002). [→ McDaniel Mark A. 2022](#mcdaniel-mark-a-2022)
`q3 i2` With RAPM added, RAPM interacted with question type, F(1, 53) = 7.29, p = 0.009, ηp2 = 0.12, with a nearly flat slope for transfer (0.001) and a negative slope for retention (−0.011). [→ McDaniel Mark A. 2022 (2)](#mcdaniel-mark-a-2022-2)

## Evidence

### McDaniel Mark A. 2022

McDaniel Mark A., Cahill Michael J., Frey Regina F., Limeri Lisa B., Lemons Paula P. (2022). Learning Introductory Biology: Students' Concept-Building Approaches Predict Transfer on Biology Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-12-0335

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Covariate results from the first mixed-effects exam model. The article reports the ACT-by-question-type interaction "represented a smaller effect" than the concept-building interaction, and notes the two ability indices showed inconsistent interaction patterns.

> "ACT performance was not significantly related to exam performance in general, F(1, 62) = 3.03, p = 0.087; but did interact with question type, F(1, 62) = 4.17, MSE = 0.005, p = 0.046, ηp2 = 0.06."

### McDaniel Mark A. 2022 (2)

McDaniel Mark A., Cahill Michael J., Frey Regina F., Limeri Lisa B., Lemons Paula P. (2022). Learning Introductory Biology: Students' Concept-Building Approaches Predict Transfer on Biology Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-12-0335

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Covariate results from the second mixed-effects model adding RAPM. The article interprets the inconsistent interaction patterns as suggesting a general-intelligence relation to transfer may be restricted to crystallized intelligence.

> "RAPM did not relate to exam performance in general, F(1, 53) = 1.24, p = 0.27; but did significantly interact with question type, F(1, 53) = 7.29, MSE = 0.004, p = 0.009, ηp2 = 0.12."
