## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PHQ-9 scores independently predicted follow-up GPA (p = 0.006) while GAD-7 scores did not; the depression effect was no longer significant after adjusting for baseline GPA (p = 0.09). [→ Awadalla Suheir 2020](#awadalla-suheir-2020)

## Evidence

### Awadalla Suheir 2020

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Entry-method linear regression among the 134 follow-up responders with follow-up GPA as dependent variable, reporting "adjusted R2 = 0.048" and "B = −.043" for PHQ-9. GAD-7 scores did not predict follow-up GPA.

> "The overall model was significant (F = 2.66, df = 4127, p = 0.035) but only accounted for a small proportion of variance (adjusted R2 = 0.048). Only PHQ-9 scores (B = −.043, p = 0.014) at baseline predicted GPA at 6 months follow-up (Table 6)."

### Awadalla Suheir 2020 (2)

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

When baseline GPA was added to the regression, the depression effect "failed to reach significance" (p = 0.09), with baseline GPA accounting for most variance (adjusted r2 = 0.633).

> "Including baseline GPA into the regression meant that the relationship between depressive symptoms at baseline and follow-up GPA just failed to reach significance (B = -.019, p = 0.09) and the total adjusted r2 increased to 0.633"
