## Evidence

### Subramaniam Anusuiya 2015

Subramaniam Anusuiya, Silong Abu Daud, Uli Jegak, Ismail Ismi Arif. (2015). Effects of coaching supervision, mentoring supervision and abusive supervision on talent development among trainee doctors in public hospitals: moderating role of clinical learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0407-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 355 trainee doctors at six Malaysian public hospitals, analysed with SEM in AMOS. The structural model shows a significant positive coaching supervision path, "β = 0.200, p = 0.001", supporting H1.

> "Table 3 reveals that the path between coaching supervision and talent development (β = 0.200, p = 0.001), as well as mentoring supervision and talent development (β = 0.121, p = 0.019). These values are statistically significant, thus hypotheses H1 and H2 is supported."
