## Evidence

### Dudley 2015

Dudley, D. A., Cotton, W. G., & Peralta, L. R. (2015). Teaching approaches and strategies that promote healthy eating in primary school children: a systematic review and meta-analysis. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity, 12, 28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-015-0182-8

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three experiential learning studies (school/community gardens, cooking lessons, food preparation) reporting food consumption and energy intake outcomes; two reported p < .05 or better for at least one variable. The review reports a mean Cohen's d of Md = 1.31.

> "Whilst there were only a small number of effect sizes that were able to be calculated based on the reporting method in these studies, the mean effect size was Md = 1.31 and within the Zone of Desired Effects."
