## Evidence

### intervention-year

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2015, June). Teacher Training, Evaluation, and Compensation intervention report: MyTeachingPartner–Secondary. http://whatworks.ed.gov

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in 12 Virginia middle and high schools; intervention-year analytic sample of 76 teachers and 1,267 students. Appendix C reports SOL means of 468.9 (MTP-S) vs 465.6 (comparison), effect size 0.04, improvement index +2, p-value > .05 — "not statistically significant".

> "For the intervention year, the authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, that the difference between the MTP-S group and the comparison group at the end of the school year was not statistically significant."
