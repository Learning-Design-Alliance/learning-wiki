### Moura 2023

Moura, N., Vidal, M., Aguilera, A. M., Vilas-Boas, J. P., Serra, S. & Leman, M. (2023). Knee flexion of saxophone players anticipates tonal context of music. npj Science of Learning 8:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00172-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of smoothed knee curves (functional PCA smoothing) against rhythmical density profiles in the expressive condition; the article reports "r = − 0.7" and "r = 0.63", both p < 0.0001.

> "Pearson correlation coefficient is r = − 0.7, p < 0.0001 for the L2 norm scores and r = 0.63, p < 0.0001 for the phase changes (Fig. 1c) with regard to the rhythmical density scores"
