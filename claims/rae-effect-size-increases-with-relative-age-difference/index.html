Evidence¶

Cobley 2009¶

Cobley, S., Baker, J., Wattie, N., McKenna, J. (2009). Annual Age-Grouping and Athlete Development. Sports Med 39, 235–256. https://doi.org/10.2165/00007256-200939030-00005

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Follow-up analysis of the pooled odds-ratio estimates from the 253 independent samples reported that "Effect size increased linearly with relative age differences." No coefficient or effect-size value for the trend is printed in the available text.

"Overall results identified consistent prevalence of RAEs, but with small effect sizes. Effect size increased linearly with relative age differences."
