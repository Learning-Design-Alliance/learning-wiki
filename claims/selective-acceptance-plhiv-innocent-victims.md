### Mutiara Tirta Prabandari Lintang Kusuma 2020

Mutiara Tirta Prabandari Lintang Kusuma, Kidd Tandalayo, Muturi Nancy, Procter Sandra Butin, Yarrow Linda, Hsu Wei-Wen. (2020). The Symbolic Meaning of HIV: Understanding Lecturers’ Ambivalence over Teaching HIV to Dietetic Students. Journal of the International Association of Providers of AIDS Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/2325958220939755

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 13 lecturers, interpreted through attribution theory's personal-responsibility and controllability dimensions. The article reports participants "tend to show more empathy to these people and more willing to give assistance," illustrated by contrasting participant accounts.

> "Children and women who were infected from their spouse, a health professional who was infected during their work and those who contracted HIV from blood transfusion were often seen as the innocent victims of HIV."
