Evidence¶

Silander 2016¶

Silander, M., Moorthy, S., Dominguez, X., Pasnik, S., & Llorente, C. (2016). Using Digital Media at Home to Promote Young Children's Mathematics Learning: Results of a Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567485

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre/post parent surveys in the RCT compared intervention and BAU caregivers; reported proportions favor the intervention on both confidence and attitudes toward media as math-learning tools, each p<.05.

"These caregivers also reported significant increases in their confidence to support math learning for their children, as compared to BAU caregivers (PBS KIDS: 96%; BAU: 81%; p<.05), as well as in their agreement that technology and media were tools for math learning (PBS KIDS: 51%; BAU: 28%; p<.05)."
