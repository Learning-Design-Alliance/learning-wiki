## Evidence

### Pixner 2014

Pixner, S., Leyrer, M., and Moeller, K. (2014). Number processing and arithmetic skills in children with cochlear implants. Frontiers in Psychology 5:1479. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01479

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Standardized calculation test (HRT 1–4) administered to the 45 children with CI; seven scored below percentile 10, the ICD-10/DSM-IV dyscalculia cut-off, so "seven children of our experimental group (i.e., 15.6%)" showed dyscalculia indications. Mean T-scores (M = 48.40) did not differ from the standardization sample, t(44) = 0.89, n.s.

> "Hence, seven children of our experimental group (i.e., 15.6%) would fall below the diagnostic threshold of developmental dyscalculia."
