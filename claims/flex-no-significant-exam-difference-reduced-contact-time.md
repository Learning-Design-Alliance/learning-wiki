## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An independent-samples t-test found no significant difference in 2014 final exam results between the FLEX group (M = 35.9, SD = 8.32) and the CONV group (M = 37.20, SD = 8.72); the effect size was low (Cohen's d = 0.15). [→ Claude Müller 2018](#claude-muller-2018)

## Evidence

### Claude Müller 2018

Claude Müller, Michael Stahl, Mark Alder, Maximilian Müller. (2018). Learning Effectiveness and Students' Perceptions in a Flexible Learning Course. European Journal of Open, Distance and e-Learning, 21(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1208426

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test-only comparison of 2014 final exam scores in the IBA course: 140 administratively assigned FLEX students versus 849 CONV students, with identical content, exam questions and grading. The t-test was not significant (t(987) = -1.56, p = 0.10) and Cohen's d = 0.15 was low.

> "The t -test reveals no significant difference in the final exam results 2014 for the FLEX ( M = 35.9, SD = 8.32) and the CONV ( M = 37.20, SD = 8.72) groups, t (987) = -1.56, p = 0.10 (ns). Accordingly, the effect size ( Cohen's d = 0.15) is low."
