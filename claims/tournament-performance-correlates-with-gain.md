## Evidence

### Daniel Dziob 2015

Daniel Dziob, Łukasz Kwiatkowski, Dagmara Sokołowska. (2015). Studying the impact of the class tournament as an assessment method on student achievements in physics courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlation analysis (Fig. 8(e)) on the n = 28 sample relating the modified gain factor to tournament scores. The authors suggest "the learner gain is generally higher in the case of those who scored higher on TNT", attributing this to active involvement in cooperative work.

> "it appears that student improvement (as measured by MGF) is significantly and positively influenced by the tournament performance, though the correlation coefficient is only ca. 0.38"
