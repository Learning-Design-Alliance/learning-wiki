### Kozato An 2020

Kozato An, Patel Nimesh, Shikino Kiyoshi. (2020). A randomised controlled pilot trial of the influence of non-native English accents on examiners’ scores in OSCEs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02198-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fleiss Kappa computed on the examiners' markings showed "fair inter rater reliability" for both video types, k = 0.313 (95% CI 0.301–0.326) accented and k = 0.310 (95% CI 0.298–0.322) non-accented, indicating similar agreement across accent conditions.

> "Fleiss Kappa analysis showed there was a fair inter rater reliability for both accented videos, k = 0.313 (95% CI, 0.301 to 0.326) and non-accented videos, k = 0.310 (95% CI, 0.298 to 0.322)."
