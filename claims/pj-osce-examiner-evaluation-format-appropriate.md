`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Anonymous voluntary online questionnaire completed by examiners after the PJ-OSCE. The mean overall rating was "12.59/15 points (SD 1.67)" and "86.3% of examiners" rated the format as appropriate.

> "Among examiners (n = 51), the mean overall rating was 12.59/15 points (SD 1.67). The examination format was rated as appropriate by 86.3% of examiners (n = 44), while 1.9% (n = 1) rated it as inappropriate."
