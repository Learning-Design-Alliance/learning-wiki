## Evidence

### Kraus 2016

Kraus, S. E. C., & Cleveland, R. E. (2016). The Effects of a Cross-age Peer Mentoring Program on School Connectedness with Rural Populations. Georgia School Counselor Association Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1140852

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 47 seventh-grade mentees (treatment homeroom n = 28, waitlist control n = 19) in a rural 100% FRL Georgia school; t-tests compared mentoring and non-mentoring groups' Hemingway MAC global scores. The article reports "scores for the experimental group were not statistically significant."

> "While both groups saw increases in mean differences, scores for the experimental group were not statistically significant."
