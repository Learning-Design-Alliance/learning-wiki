`q3 i3` After a four-week treatment, the financial accounting achievement of students taught with cooperative learning was significantly better than that of students taught with the conventional approach, with a large effect size (partial eta squared = .929). [→ Inuwa 2017](#inuwa-2017)

## Evidence

### Inuwa 2017

Inuwa, U., Abdullah, Z. & Hassan, H. (2017). Assessing the Effect of Cooperative Learning on Financial Accounting Achievement among Secondary School Students. International Journal of Instruction, 10(3), 31-46. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2017.1033a

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test as covariate in a randomized pre-test-post-test-control group design (N = 120, 60 per group). The article reports F (1,117) = 1526.442, p = .000, with the printed effect size partial eta squared = .929, described as a large effect.

> "The Results shows in Table 3 reveals that there was a statistically significant difference between the financial accounting achievement of students who were exposed to the cooperative learning approach and that of those who were exposed to the conventional approach, F (1,117) = 1526.442, p = .000, partial eta squared = .929."
