## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). The Physics GRE does not help applicants “stand out”. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Score-distribution and admission-fraction analysis by institution size and selectivity (Table II and Figs. 4–5). The article reports that at scores of "900 and above", applicants from the largest or most selective universities seem to be admitted at a higher rate, and a higher fraction of them achieve such scores.

> "For the highest physics GRE scores, 900 and above, applicants from the largest or most selective universities seem to be admitted at a higher rate and a higher fraction of applicants from large or selective universities achieve these high scores compared to applicants from smaller universities."
