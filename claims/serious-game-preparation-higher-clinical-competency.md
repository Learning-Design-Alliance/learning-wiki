## Evidence

### Dankbaar Mary E.W. 2017

Dankbaar Mary E.W., Roozeboom Maartje Bakhuys, Oprins Esther A.P.B., Rutten Frans, van Merrienboer Jeroen J.G., van Saase Jan L.C.M., Schuit Stephanie C.E. (2017). Preparing Residents Effectively in Emergency Skills Training With a Serious Game. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000194

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study with a historical control: an extra pretraining scenario assessment with standardized patients was completed by 18 reading and 24 reading-and-game residents. The game group performed better "with a medium-large effect size (Glass's d = 0.62)"; improvements were largest on initial assessment, treatment, and requests for additional diagnostics.

> "Before training (after the game), the reading and game group performed better on clinical competency skills (P = 0.03, Table 3) with a medium-large effect size (Glass's d = 0.62) than the reading group."
