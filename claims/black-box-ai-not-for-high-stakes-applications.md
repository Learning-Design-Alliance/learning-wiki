## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The report asserts that AI systems relying on black-box models, whose hidden-layer computations are intractable to human interpretation, should not be used for high-stakes applications. [→ Mills 2024](#mills-2024)

## Evidence

### Mills 2024

Mills, K., Ruiz, P., Lee, K., Coenraad, M., Fusco, J., Roschelle, J., & Weisgrau, J. (2024). AI Literacy: A Framework to Understand, Evaluate, and Use Emerging Technology. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/218

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The report's mechanistic explanation of artificial neural networks—arranged in input, hidden, and output layers with weighted associations—states that hidden layers perform statistical functions that are intractable, making the model a 'black box.'

> "Given AI systems and tools rely on black boxes that do not allow humans to understand how outputs are decided on, these AI systems and tools should not be used for high-stakes applications."
