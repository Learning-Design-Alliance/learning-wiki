# Indirect range restriction on a selection predictor is small under realistic conditions: restricted SDx stays at .90 or larger when the correlation with the selection variable is .50 or smaller and the selection ratio exceeds .1

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2022 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Under indirect range restriction via selection on a third variable z, restricted SDx is .90 or larger whenever the z-x correlation is .50 or smaller and the selection ratio is larger than .1, so the correction factor is generally under 10%. [→ Sackett 2022](#sackett-2022)
`q2 i?` By contrast, direct range restriction is substantial: a selection ratio of .50 yields a restricted SDx of .60 and a U ratio correction of 1.67. [→ Sackett 2022 (2)](#sackett-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Sackett 2022

Sackett, P. R.; Zhang, C.; Berry, C. M.; and Lievens, F. (2022). Revisiting Meta-Analytic Estimates of Validity in Personnel Selection: Addressing Systematic Overcorrection for Restriction of Range. Journal of Applied Psychology, 107(11), 2040-2068. https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/6894

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical results in Table 1 (verified by a one-million-case simulation reproducing the table) show the effect on SDx of indirect restriction via selection on z. The authors state the indirect correction factor "will generally be less than 10%, commonly much less than 10%."

> "With a correlation between z and x of .50 or smaller and anything less than a very extreme selection ratio (i.e., any ratio larger than .1), restricted SDx is .90 or larger."

### Sackett 2022 (2)

Sackett, P. R.; Zhang, C.; Berry, C. M.; and Lievens, F. (2022). Revisiting Meta-Analytic Estimates of Validity in Personnel Selection: Addressing Systematic Overcorrection for Restriction of Range. Journal of Applied Psychology, 107(11), 2040-2068. https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/6894

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Table 1 Panel 1 numerical results for direct range restriction on x: a .50 selection ratio yields restricted SDx of .60 and a U ratio of 1.67, illustrating that direct restriction has a much larger effect than indirect restriction.

> "a selection ratio of .50 produces a restricted SDx of .60 (relative to 1.0 as the unrestricted value). Dividing unrestricted SDx by restricted SDx gives the correction factor (U ratio) of 1.67"
