## Evidence

### Budakoğlu Işıl İrem 2023

Budakoğlu Işıl İrem, Coşkun Özlem, Özeke Vildan. (2023). e-PBL with multimedia animations: a design-based research. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04298-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

CORE exam taken by 57 e-PBL and 60 f2f participants at the end of scenario-specific sessions (Endocrine for 2nd year, Gastrointestinal for 3rd year). The t-test found no significant platform difference; no effect size was printed.

> "There was no significant difference in the CORE scores between the online (57.54 ± 20.12) and f2f (57.98 ± 17.44) groups (t 115 = 0.127; p > .05)"

### Budakoğlu Işıl İrem 2023 (2)

Budakoğlu Işıl İrem, Coşkun Özlem, Özeke Vildan. (2023). e-PBL with multimedia animations: a design-based research. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04298-x

`q3 · i1` · `design · r3`

Pearson correlations (N = 117; e-PBL: 57, f2f: 60): CORE-GPA r = .188 (p < .05), a weak positive correlation; CORE-PBL r = .059 and PBL-GPA r = .045 were not significant.

> "There was a weak and positive correlation between the CORE scores of the participants and their academic achievement (r = .188; p < .05). There was no significant relationship between PBL scores and CORE and academic achievement."
