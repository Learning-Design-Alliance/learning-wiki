### Z. Yasemin Kalender 2020

Z. Yasemin Kalender, Martin Stein, and N. G. Holmes. (2020). Sense of agency, gender, and students' perception in open-ended physics labs. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.kalender](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.kalender)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of the open-ended post-survey question about students' favorite lab unit in the same course. The article reports Project Lab was chosen by 45 students and Lab 3 by 30, while the first two lab units were selected by 6 and 10 students respectively; coding showed "the most common explanation given by students who selected the Project Lab as their favorite unit was the freedom that the lab afforded them."

> "the majority of the students (n = 45) chose the Project Lab unit as their favorite, followed by Lab 3 (n = 30)"
