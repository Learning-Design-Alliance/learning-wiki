# Canadian students were more likely than South Korean students to judge GenAI-assisted coding scenarios as unethical

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across eight scenarios, Canadian students rated the actor as having acted unethically more than Korean students (mean 2.6 vs 1.78 on a five-point Likert scale), with statistically significant differences on all but one scenario. [→ Brian Harrington 2025](#brian-harrington-2025)

## Evidence

### Brian Harrington 2025

Brian Harrington, Irina Zlotnikova, Gayathri Nadarajan, and Samuel Ekundayo. (2025). Did Alice Do Wrong? Cross-Cultural Differences in Student Perceptions of Generative AI Use in University Computing Education. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. 26, 1, Article 15. https://doi.org/10.1145/3776558

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scenario-based survey at two universities (262 CAN, 48 KOR respondents) comparing Likert judgments of eight GenAI coding scenarios. Canadian students were "much more likely to say that Alice acted unethically" (mean = 2.6) than Korean students (mean = 1.78); Mann–Whitney U tests showed significant differences on all prompts except Scenario 1 ethicality.
