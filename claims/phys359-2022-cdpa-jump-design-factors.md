### Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau 2024

Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau, Benjamin J. Dringoli, Jack C. Sankey, Thomas Brunner, and Kenneth Ragan, April Colosimo, Armin Yazdani, Janette Barrington, and Marcy Slapcoff. (2024). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of CDPA scores for the small 2022 PHYS 359 honors cohort (N=19 tested per Appendix). The authors attribute the increase, through discussion with the instructor, to smaller enrollment allowing one-on-one interaction, more weekly lab time, experiments requiring higher precision, a self-paced Python curve-fitting module, and 20% of the lab report grade allocated to uncertainty skills.

> "the 2022 mean CDPA score was significantly higher, from a class average of 2 .5 ± 2.0 by the end of PHYS 258 to 4 .5 ± 1.0 by the end of PHYS 359."
