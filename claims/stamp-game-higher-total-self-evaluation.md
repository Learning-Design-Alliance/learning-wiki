## Evidence

### Han Mingyu 2025

Han Mingyu, Shi Wei, Yu Ling, Yuan Tianming, Zhang Yuanyuan. (2025). Stamp collecting game improves internship performance and experience in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07764-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot RCT of medical students on neonatology rotation; 81 self-evaluation questionnaires collected (44 control, 37 experimental). The experimental group's total self-evaluation was significantly higher, "which were 11.81 ± 1.47 and 10.59 ± 1.91", p = 0.002. No effect size printed.

> "We observed a statistically significant difference in the total score of self-evaluation between the experimental and control group, which were 11.81 ± 1.47 and 10.59 ± 1.91, respectively."
