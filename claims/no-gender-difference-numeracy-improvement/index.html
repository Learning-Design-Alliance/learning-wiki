Evidence¶

Meloni et al. 2017¶

Carla Meloni, Rachele Fanari, Andrea Bertucci and Sara Berretti. (2017). IMPACT OF EARLY NUMERACY TRAINING ON KINDERGARTENERS FROM MIDDLE-INCOME FAMILIES. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two Wilcoxon tests on the BIN improvement score in the 25-child low-ability subsample compared males and females in the two experimental groups. The article reports that results "did not show significant differences between males and females" (p > .05); no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

"Two Wilcoxon Tests were also performed to test for a gender difference in children’s BIN-IS. Results did not show significant differences between males and females in the two experimental groups (p > .05)."
