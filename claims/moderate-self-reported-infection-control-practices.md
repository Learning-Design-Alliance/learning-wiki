`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported practice results from the cross-sectional questionnaire (N = 420), scored 6–30 (mean 23.0, SD 4.3). The study reports "the majority 50.2% showed moderate practice" and that fewer than half always performed hand hygiene (39.8%), PPE use (40.7%) and instrument handling (42.6%).

> "Overall, the mean Practice score (SD) was 23.0 (4.3). Only 3.6% showed negative practice, 46.2% showed good practice, and the majority 50.2% showed moderate practice"
