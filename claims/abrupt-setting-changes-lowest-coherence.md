## Evidence

### F. Rogosch 2026

F. Rogosch, A. Schrader. (2026). AI-Generated Interactive Fiction for Educational Use: A Pilot Study of Perceived Comprehensibility, Coherence, and Engagement. Proceedings of EDULEARN26 Conference. https://doi.org/10.21125/edulearn.2026.1075

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative finding from the pilot's thematic analysis: two of ten free-text responses reported abrupt setting changes, exemplified by a participant quote about being teleported from a museum to a radio station, "co-occurring with the lowest coherence scores in the sample (≤ 1.75)".

> "A second, more severe cluster (Theme 3, two responses) involves abrupt location or character changes mid-scenario ("Suddenly we were teleported from a museum to a radio station, with new characters praising me who had not been there before."), co-occurring with the lowest coherence scores in the sample (≤ 1.75)."
