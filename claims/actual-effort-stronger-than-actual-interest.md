## Evidence

### Kehle 2025

Kehle, L., & Urhahne, D. (2025). Interest and effort in learning and performance. *British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96*(2), 593–611. [doi:10.1111/bjep.70040](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70040)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 2 (N = 120) likelihood ratio test comparing standardized coefficients in the SEM controlling for prior knowledge; the comparison was significant, Δχ2 = 4.523, Δdf = 1, p = .033, with actual effort the stronger predictor.

> "The LR test revealed that the relative effect of actual effort on test achievement was significantly stronger than that of actual interest on test achievement"
