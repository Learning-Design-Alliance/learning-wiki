# Lung-model-trained residents outperformed FLS-trained residents on overall OSATS technical skill in live animal thoracoscopic wedge resection, with advantages in four of five domains but comparable stapling scores

## Evidence

### Liu Ganwei 2025

Liu Ganwei, Yang Feng, Zhou Zuli, Jiang Guanchao. (2025). A Novel Competency-Based Simulation Model for Thoracoscopic Lung Resection. The Thoracic and Cardiovascular Surgeon. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2702-2239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Live animal thoracoscopic wedge resection assessment one week after training, scored with a modified OSATS across five domains (maximum 25). The lung model group's mean total was "19.18 ± 2.43 vs. 15.41 ± 2.41"; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean OSATS score for the lung model group was significantly higher than that for the FLS group (19.18 ± 2.43 vs. 15.41 ± 2.41; p < 0.001; Table 2 )"

### Liu Ganwei 2025 (2)

Liu Ganwei, Yang Feng, Zhou Zuli, Jiang Guanchao. (2025). A Novel Competency-Based Simulation Model for Thoracoscopic Lung Resection. The Thoracic and Cardiovascular Surgeon. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2702-2239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Domain-level OSATS comparison in the same live animal assessment (Fig. 4). Four domains favored the lung model group; stapling showed "comparable between groups" performance (p = 0.390). No effect sizes are printed.

> "higher scores were observed in four of the five OSATS domains, including thoracoscope handling (4.11 ± 0.65 vs. 3.19 ± 0.85; p < 0.001), tissue handling (3.65 ± 0.74 vs. 2.73 ± 0.91; p < 0.001), orientation (4.00 ± 0.56 vs. 3.04 ± 0.82; p < 0.001), and specimen extraction (3.50 ± 0.86 vs. 2.69 ± 0.88; p = 0.002), whereas stapling scores were comparable between groups (3.92 ± 0.62 vs. 3.76 ± 0.65; p = 0.390)"
