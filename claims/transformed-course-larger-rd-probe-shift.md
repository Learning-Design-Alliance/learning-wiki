## Evidence

### Benjamin Pollard 2020

Benjamin Pollard, Alexandra Werth, Robert Hobbs, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Impact of a course transformation on students' reasoning about measurement uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020160](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020160)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Probe-level paradigm analysis of matched pre/post PMQ responses in the two semesters; the article reports "larger shifts towards S than the original course" on the RD probe with Cohen's h effect sizes of h=0.45 (transformed) and h=0.21 (original).

> "In the RD probe, the transformed course showed larger shifts towards S than the original course (with Cohen’s h eﬀect sizes of h=0.45 and h=0.21, respectively), while in the SMDS probe, the transformed course showed no signiﬁcant shifts at all."
