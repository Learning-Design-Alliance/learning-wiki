### Dannenberg Katja Anne 2016

Dannenberg Katja Anne, Stroben Fabian, Schröder Therese, Thomas Anke, Hautz Wolf E. (2016). The future of practical skills in undergraduate medical education – an explorative Delphi-Study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001061](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001061)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Preparatory phase: 9 partially structured interviews with experts in public health, medical technology, pedagogy, clinical care and students, analysed by Mayring qualitative text analysis to inductively derive theses. The article states "A total of 11 theses on the future of health care were derived from the partially structured interviews."

### Dannenberg Katja Anne 2016 (2)

Dannenberg Katja Anne, Stroben Fabian, Schröder Therese, Thomas Anke, Hautz Wolf E. (2016). The future of practical skills in undergraduate medical education – an explorative Delphi-Study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001061](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Expert survey of physicians at German university hospitals contacted by email plus conference recruitment; 738 registered for round 1 with 594 usable complete data sets, 188 usable in round 2. Most worked inpatient in maximum-care hospitals across 26 disciplines.

> "738 experts registered online for round 1 of the study, and 594 complete data sets (19.5% dropout rate) were usable. 314 experts were registered for round 2, and 188 complete data sets (40.1% dropout rate) were usable."
