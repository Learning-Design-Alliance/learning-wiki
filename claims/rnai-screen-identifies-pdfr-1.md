### Evan L. Ardiel 2017

Evan L. Ardiel, Alex J. Yu, Andrew C. Giles and Catharine H. Rankin. (2017). Habituation as an adaptive shift in response strategy mediated by neuropeptides. npj Science of Learning 2:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0011-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RNAi-by-feeding screen in a neuronal-RNAi-sensitized glr-1; lin-15b; sid-1 background, testing knockdown of egl-3 and one of 57 GPCRs, with critical values at |z-score| > 4.46 (P < 0.0008). The screen used a more responsive sra-6p::ChR2 strain.
