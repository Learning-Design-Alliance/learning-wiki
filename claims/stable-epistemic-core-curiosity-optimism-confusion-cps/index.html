Sifatul Anindho 2026¶

Sifatul Anindho, Videep Venkatesha, Jaclyn Ocumpaugh, and Nathaniel Blanchard. (2026). Ordered Network Analysis of Epistemic Emotions during Collaborative Problem Solving. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23317

q2 · i? · associational · r1

ONA of retrospective cued-recall affect reports from 9 groups completing a situated CPS task shows a "stable epistemic core which links curiosity, optimism and confusion" with the strongest ordered connections and self-transitions, indicating short-range persistence rather than frequent switching.

"Across ONA models, we observe a stable epistemic core which links curiosity, optimism and confusion. These states were consistently involved in the strongest ordered connections, including self-transitions, indicating that aﬀective experience during collaboration is often characterized by short-range persistence rather than frequent switching."

Related Claims¶

  • Probe-caught affect reports show stronger self-loops among core epistemic states, while self-caught reports show more distributed transitions involving higher-arousal states — related
