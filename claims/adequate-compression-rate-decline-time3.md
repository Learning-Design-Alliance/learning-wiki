## Evidence

### Zucca A 2024

Zucca A, Bryant J, Purse J, Szwec S, Sanson-Fisher R, Leigh L, Richer M, Morrison A. (2024). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Advanced Technology Clinical Simulation Manikins in Improving the Capability of Australian Paramedics to Deliver High-Quality Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation: Pre- and Postintervention Study. JMIR Cardio. https://doi.org/10.2196/49895

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-effects ordinal regression in the pre-post study found "significantly lower odds (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.12-0.78)" of adequate rate at time 3 versus time 2 (P=.01), with compressions 2.1 per minute slower. The article notes this did not significantly detract from overall ECC performance.

> "However, paramedics had significantly lower odds (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.12-0.78) of achieving compressions with adequate rate at time 3 compared to time 2 (P=.01). Compressions were of a slower rate at time 3 (mean 85.7, SD 26.2) compared to time 2 (mean 87.8, SD 23.2) with an average difference of 2.1 fewer compressions every minute."
