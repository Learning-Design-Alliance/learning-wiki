### Naghshi Maryam 2024

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of 33 semi-structured interviews yielded the assessment process challenges sub-theme, naming time limitation within a long implementation process, increased numbers of ACs in a short period, subjective assessment tendencies, and lack of specialized skill assessment. A participant summarized: assessment is one day but the process is long.

> "The limitation of assessment time despite the long implementation process from the time of introducing people to the announcement of results, Increasing the number of ACs implemented in a short period, Tendency to subjective assessments, Lack of clarification of the center's process before the assessment, And Lack of assessment of specialized skills are among the most important challenges."
