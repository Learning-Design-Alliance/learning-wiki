Same 2025 pilot pre/post assessment (N = 60 pre-test, 44 valid post-test). Table II prints Passive Video Instruction Modules declining from 50.0% pre-test to 28.3% post-test accuracy. The authors' interpretation, not a tested comparison.

> "In contrast, modules that used only video showed lower post-test scores, suggesting that passive formats were less effective for learning new concepts."
