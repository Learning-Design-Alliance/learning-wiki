## Evidence

### Braaten EB 2020

Braaten EB, Ward AK, Forchelli G, Vuijk PJ, Cook NE, McGuinness P, Lee BA, Samkavitz A, Lind H, O'Keefe SM, Doyle AE. (2020). Characteristics of child psychiatric outpatients with slow processing speed and potential mechanisms of academic impact. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-019-01455-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixed effects modeling in the LOGIC clinical cohort of 751 referred youth compared PSI, WMI, and GAI as a within-subjects factor, controlling for age, sex, and psychotropic medication. The main effect for cognitive domain was significant (Wald χ2 = 406.84, p < .001), with "PS Wald χ2was significantly more impaired than WM" and general ability.

> "PS Wald χ2was significantly more impaired than WM (Mdifference = −1.5, p = .003) and general cognitive ability (Mdifference = −9.8, p < .001) among referred youth."
