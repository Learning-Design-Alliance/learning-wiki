`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

One-way ANOVA on the realistic task composite score in the RCT; the quote prints the group means with 95% CIs and the non-significant test result. Equivalence between groups was not tested.

> "The mean [95% CI] composite scores on the realistic task were − .343 [− .450 − .236] for post hoc feedback group, − .233 [− .330 − .136] for real-time AI group, and − .263 [− .371 − .156] for expert instruction group (Fig. 4a). No statistically significant between groups differences were observed, F(2, 94) = 1.241, p = .294."
