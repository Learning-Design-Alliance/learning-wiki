# Time spent on specific mentoring activities varied widely, with some teachers never observed by mentors and others observed an hour or more a month

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers differed substantially in monthly time on mentoring activities: 21 percent reported never being observed by their mentor while 25 percent reported being observed an hour or more a month, and 44 percent spent an hour or more in one-on-one meetings versus 53 percent spending less than an hour.
