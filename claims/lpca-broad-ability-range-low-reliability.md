## Evidence

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Heri Retnawati. (2024). Validating Light Phenomena Conceptual Assessment Through The Lens of CTT and IRT Framework. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT test information function analysis (Figure 3) of the LPCA. The authors state the LPCA "performed well to accommodate a broad range of the students' understanding of light phenomena from low, medium, to high abilities", with maximum information of 1.24.

> "LPCA could measure students’ abilities within the range between -1.58 to 2.49."

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024 (2)

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Heri Retnawati. (2024). Validating Light Phenomena Conceptual Assessment Through The Lens of CTT and IRT Framework. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CTT internal-consistency analysis of the pretest data found alpha = 0.373, lower than the test-retest alpha = 0.55 reported by the LPCA creators; the authors cite Doran (1980) that the LPCA is more appropriate only for group averages or surveys. Peak information I(theta) = 1.24 was below 3.3, so IRT reliability was also low.

> "Yet, in this study, we found a low level of reliability  (𝛼 = 0.373)."
