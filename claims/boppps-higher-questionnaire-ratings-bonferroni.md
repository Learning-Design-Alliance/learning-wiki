## Evidence

### Cheng Wei-Qin 2025

Cheng Wei-Qin, He Ling, Cai Jin-Hua, Fan Xiao. (2025). Hybrid BOPPPS model in radiology education: a case study of pediatric undergraduates. BMC Medical Education, 25. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07279-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire administered to all 418 students with a 100% response rate, five-point Likert scale, analyzed with unpaired t tests and Bonferroni-adjusted alpha of 0.00625. Means for BOPPPS vs LBL included teacher-student interaction 4.40 ± 0.65 vs 3.76 ± 0.71 (t = 9.570, p < 0.001).

> "Students preferred the BOPPPS model more than the LBL model in terms of course enthusiasm, language proficiency, diagnostic reasoning and imaging interpretation ability, and teacher-student interaction (all p < 0.00625 after Bonferroni correction)."
