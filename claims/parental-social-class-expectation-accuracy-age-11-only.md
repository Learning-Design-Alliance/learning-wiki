## Evidence

### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of teacher expectation accuracy on parental social class at Key Stage 2 in the ALSPAC imputed sample, showing graded negative coefficients for classes II through IV relative to class I. A second result line reports the Key Stage 3 associations were "not for parental social class".

> "Children of parents from lower socioeconomic backgrounds underperformed their teacher’s expectations relative to pupils whose parents were from the highest socioeconomic background (II: − 0.11, 95% CI − 0.20, − 0.015; III non-manual: − 0.17, 95% CI − 0.28, − 0.07; III manual: − 0.22, 95% CI − 0.34, − 0.10; IV: − 0.34; 95% CI − 0.50, − 0.18)."
