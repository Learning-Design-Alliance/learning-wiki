### Watson AL 2026

Watson AL, Peterson NE, Thatcher B, Thomas M, Hunsaker S, Hooley C, Erekson D, Simpson A, Snow G, Detrick R. (2026). Experiential Course Learning, Wellness, and Higher Education: Qualitative Descriptive Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/88642

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 of the thematic analysis of 110 usable postcourse narratives; students described a progression from conceptual clarity about stress, to rehearsing concrete strategies in low-stakes settings, to generalizing skills to real-world contexts. Qualitative; no effect sizes reported.

> "Several students highlighted that understanding "how the brain works" or "how the autonomic nervous system works and why" fundamentally shifted their appraisal of anxiety and pressure. This psychoeducation component laid the groundwork for skill adoption."
