Systematic quality appraisal with RoB 2 for RCTs and ROBINS-I-V2 for nonrandomized studies; interrater agreement was reported as Cohen's kappa 0.91 (95% CI: 0.86–0.96). Seven of 13 nonrandomized studies had serious, six critical risk of bias.

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discussion identifies the scarcity of rigorous Level 4 evidence as its principal finding: from 2481 screened articles, 16 studies met inclusion criteria, nearly all at high or critical risk of bias.

> "Despite the global adoption of simulation over the past 15 years, our analysis identified only 16 studies meeting our criteria, nearly all of which were limited by a high or critical risk of bias"
