# Teachers find ChatGPT a creative, fast, iterative brainstorming partner that adds specific numbers and details when posing math questions from photos

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the photo-based question-posing task, teachers valued ChatGPT's support for creativity, multiple examples, iterative prompting, time efficiency, generated details, and versatility. [→ Candace Walkington and Katie Bainbridge 2025](#candace-walkington-and-katie-bainbridge-2025)

## Evidence

### Candace Walkington and Katie Bainbridge 2025

Candace Walkington and Katie Bainbridge. (2025). Mathematics Teachers' Use of Generative AI to Create Active Learning Experiences. https://socialinnovationsjournal.com/index.php/sij/article/view/9994

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of video-recorded interviews with 5 Algebra 1 teachers who used ChatGPT to pose math questions from photos of their communities (Table 3 themes); one teacher noted ChatGPT "brought in actual numbers that transformed my problems from conceptual ones to calculable ones".

> "teachers recognized that brainstorming with GenAI could enhance their creativity and give them many varied examples to choose from, with one teacher describing how “When you are not inspired, we could use this tool to help us be creative.”"
