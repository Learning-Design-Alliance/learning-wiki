### Mazzotta Alessandro Dario 2026

Mazzotta Alessandro Dario, Gamberini Giulia, Giuliani Giuseppe, Tognarelli Selene, Petrucciani Niccolò, Pichetto Andrea, D’Ambrosio Giancarlo, Mennini Gianluca, Coratti Andrea, Menciassi Arianna. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same porcine-model feasibility study, GEARS subdomain scores were secondary exploratory outcomes analyzed with the Wilcoxon rank-sum test. The Results section reports the significant depth perception difference, with Table 2 printing a mean difference of 1.5 (95% CI 0.5 to 2.5).

> "Notably, depth perception was significantly better in trained participants (mean score 4.33 ± 0.81 vs. 2.83 ± 0.75, p = 0.028)."

### Mazzotta Alessandro Dario 2026 (2)

Mazzotta Alessandro Dario, Gamberini Giulia, Giuliani Giuseppe, Tognarelli Selene, Petrucciani Niccolò, Pichetto Andrea, D’Ambrosio Giancarlo, Mennini Gianluca, Coratti Andrea, Menciassi Arianna. (2026). Preliminary translational assessment of robotic surgery skills for vascular dissection: from simulator to in vivo porcine model. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03662-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same exploratory subdomain analysis reports non-significant positive trends for bimanual dexterity and efficiency in the trained group; the article notes no adjustment for multiple comparisons was applied, so domain findings are exploratory.

> "Bimanual dexterity and efficiency also showed higher mean values in the trained group (4.33 ± 0.81 vs. 3.33 ± 0.51 and 4.33 ± 0.81 vs. 3.33 ± 0.81, respectively), although these differences did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.080 and p = 0.119)."
