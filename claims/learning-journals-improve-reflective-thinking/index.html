Jado 2015¶

Dr. Saleh Mohammad Abu Jado. (2015). The Effect of using Learning Journals on Developing Self-Regulated Learning and Reflective Thinking among Pre-Service Teachers in Jordan. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083603

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of 61 pre-service teachers (30 experimental, 31 control) in an educational psychology course in Jordan; MANCOVA on the reflective thinking scale (Shdaifat, 2007) showed significant group differences, e.g. the scale-level Group effect was significant at .000 with "in favor of the experimental group".

"Table (5) shows that there are significant differences between the means of the subjects' responses on the domains of the reflective thinking scale in the pre and post-tests in favor of the experimental group."
