Post-hoc comparison by turret position in the same study (N = 156); Mann–Whitney U tests. Turret 9 showed higher confidence and skill; turret 12 showed higher comfort. No effect sizes printed.

> "According to the turret position, statistically significant differences were found in confidence (U = 2144.500; Z = −3.347; p < 0.001), skill (U = 1591.000; Z = −5.367; p < 0.001), and comfort (U = 2398.000; Z = −2.264; p = 0.024) at the post-intervention stage."
