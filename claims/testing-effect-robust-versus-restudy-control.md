## Subclaims

## Evidence

### meta-1

Rowland, C. A. (2014, August 25). The Effect of Testing Versus Restudy on Retention: A Meta-Analytic Review of the Testing Effect. Psychological Bulletin. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0037559

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of testing versus equal-duration restudy comparisons; the author states that "positive effects under these conservative conditions firmly establishes the robust character of the testing effect" and that practical effects may be larger.

> "The observation of positive effects under these conservative conditions firmly establishes the robust character of the testing effect, and one can reasonably expect that the practical application of the procedures investigated may yield substantially larger effects."
