## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students whose parents were assigned to receive additional teacher-to-parent communication were 6.5 percentage points more likely to earn course credit than control students. [→ Kraft and Rogers 2013](#kraft-and-rogers-2013)

## Evidence

### Kraft and Rogers 2013

Matthew A. Kraft and Todd Rogers. (2013). Teacher-to-Parent Communication: Experimental Evidence from a Low-Cost Communication Policy. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563011

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blocked randomized field trial with 435 recruited students in a summer credit recovery program; pooled ITT analysis of the binary credit outcome. The article reports a "6.5 percentage points (p=.048)" increase relative to control, where 87.2% of control students earned credit.

> "students whose parents were assigned to receive either form of additional information were 6.5 percentage points (p=.048) more likely to earn a course credit for classes they enrolled in compared to the control group"
