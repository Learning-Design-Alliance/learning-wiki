## Evidence

### Newmann 2001

Newmann, F. M., Smith, B., Allensworth, E., & Bryk, A. S. (2001). School Instructional Program Coherence: Benefits and Challenges. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Field study of 11 Chicago Annenberg Challenge elementary schools rated on 13 coherence indicators using a 4-point scale per indicator. The authors conclude "strong instructional coherence is not common among Chicago elementary schools."

> "The field reports indicated substantial variation in instructional program coherence, as shown in Figure 1. While three schools rated fairly close to the highest possible score of 52, eight of the 11 schools were rated 33 or below."
