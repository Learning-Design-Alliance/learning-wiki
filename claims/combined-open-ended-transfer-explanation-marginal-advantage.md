ANCOVA on combined open-ended explanation quality (NEI plus non-NEI transfer items) in the N=92 experiment showed a "marginally significant effect (β=+7.3%, p=.057, d=0.38)" favoring the open-ended condition over control.

> "Effects for the open-ended condition were also positive across open-ended explanation measures, with combined post-test open-ended explanations yielding a marginally significant effect (β=+7.3%, p=.057, d=0.38)."
