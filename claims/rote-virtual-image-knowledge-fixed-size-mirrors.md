### J. Christopher Moore 2011

J. Christopher Moore, Richard D. Floyd and Cody V. Thompson. (2011). Virtual images and billiards. Coastal Carolina University. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1103.3254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1103.3254)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' own post-instruction observation in introductory physics courses: students succeed at locating virtual images "when the mirror is sufficiently large in comparison to the object", but the authors report this knowledge appears rote and descriptive-level.

> "we have found post-­‐instruction that students can correctly identify the location of a virtual image with little difficulty when the mirror is sufficiently large in comparison to the object."
