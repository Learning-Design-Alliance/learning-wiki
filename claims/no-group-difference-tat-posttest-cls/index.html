Kala 2023¶

Kala, N., & Ayas, A. (2023). Effect of instructional design based on cognitive load theory on students' performances and the indicators of element interactivity. Journal of Turkish Science Education, 20(3), 468-489. https://doi.org/10.36681/tused.2023.027

Univariate follow-ups in the same MANOVA on the 23-item TAT post-test and the 9-point CLS: the article reports "no significant difference between the TAT post-test" and CLS scores, both with η p2 = .09 and p > .05. Equivalence was not otherwise tested.
