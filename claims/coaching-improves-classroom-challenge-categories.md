Mahsa Bakhshaei, Angela Hardy, Jason Ravitz, John Seylar. (2020). Instructional Coaching Holds Promise as a Method to Improve Instruction with Technology. SITE 2020 - Online, April 7-10, 2020. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5465e54a-b290-4210-97fb-514a3aa13e21

Self-report from coached DLP teachers on improvement in their self-selected classroom challenges; "More than 90%" reported at least some improvement in six named categories, with no inferential test or effect size printed.

> "More than 90% of participating teachers reported at least some improvement in the following teaching challenges as a result of working with their coach: assessment, differentiation, instructional strategies to support a specific content area, classroom management, planning and preparation, and professional growth. Remarkably, in all six of these categories, more than half of participating teachers reported “much” or “very much” improvement."
