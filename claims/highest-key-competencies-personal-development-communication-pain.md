### Pieters Jolien 2020

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Delphi key-competency ratings (N = 54): the three top-scoring competencies were "Personal development and well-being (mean score = 4.54; SD = 1.02)", communication (4.50), and pain and symptom management (4.48).

> "Personal development and well-being (mean score = 4.54; SD = 1.02), communication (mean score = 4.50; SD = 0.75), and pain and symptom management (mean score = 4.48; SD = 0.74) scored highest, showing that at least a part of the panel expected junior doctors to be able to perform these tasks in the workplace under close supervision."
