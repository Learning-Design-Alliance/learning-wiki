## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pretest t-tests and ANOVAs found no significant accuracy differences among focused, unfocused, and control groups for rule-bound or non-rule-bound structures. [→ Alavinia 2018](#alavinia-2018)

## Evidence

### Alavinia 2018

Alavinia, P., Shafaei, A., & Salimi, A. (2018). The Effects of Focused/Unfocused Audio-Appended Reading Tasks on Intermediate Female EFL Learners' Written Accuracy. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 203-220. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11214a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest comparisons: rule-bound t = 0.28, p = .78 (M = 0.42 vs 0.40); non-rule-bound t = -0.11, p = .91 (M = 0.52 vs 0.53); ANOVAs F = 0.07, p = .92 and F = 0.53, p = .59. No effect sizes printed; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "there was no significant difference between the performances of learners of focused an d unfocused rule -bound groups in terms of accuracy on rendering task pretest of the study"
