`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-case interpretation from the three video case studies in the 33-teacher study. The Indian-accent group's open-ended icebreaker integrated Phoenix as a peer, the Black-accent group used it to test limits, and the British-accent group's minimal engagement reinforced a background-tool framing.

> "Its initial framings as partner, object of interrogation, or background tool persisted, suggesting that role expectations are anchored in early agent encounters rather than renegotiated throughout collaboration [12]."
