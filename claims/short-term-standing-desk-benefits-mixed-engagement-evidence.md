`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The protocol's background and discussion narrate prior short-term studies: Dornhecker et al. observed greater academic engagement among students with stand-promoting desks, Blake et al. reported positive teacher-rated attention effects in grade one, while Koepp et al. "did not find a significant difference in teacher reported concentration" between desk types in sixth graders.

> "However, Koepp et al. did not find a significant difference in teacher reported concentration between sixth graders that used desks that promote standing and those that used regular desks [31]."
