`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RQ2 analysis of the same controlled experiment's log traces (21 students) measured completeness as distinct cells executed; the Mann-Whitney U test with a 0.01 cutoff found no significant difference between groups, so equivalence was not established.

> "For completeness, the Mann-Whitney U test yields a p-value p = 0.53 and an effect size rbc = −0.145455. Thus, we cannot reject the null hypothesis that the completeness is the same between the groups."
