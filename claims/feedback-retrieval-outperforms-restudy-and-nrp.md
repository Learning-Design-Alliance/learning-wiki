## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Recall accuracy showed a significant main effect of learning condition (ηp2 = 0.73), with feedback retrieval significantly outperforming both restudy and no-feedback retrieval, and restudy outperforming no-feedback retrieval. [→ Jing Zhang 2025](#jing-zhang-2025)

## Evidence

### Jing Zhang 2025

Jing Zhang, Xiaoning Huo, Hongbo Lv, Jiahua Xu & Xiaofeng Ma. (2025). Offline consolidation mechanisms of the retrieval practice effect: an analysis based on EEG signal characteristics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00349-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of word-pair recall rates across the three learning conditions; ηp2 = 0.73 with the printed pairwise comparisons, indicating different memory strengths under the three conditions.

> "The results showed a signiﬁcant main effect of learning condition [F(2,92) = 125.27, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.73]. Further simple effect analysisrevealed that the retrieval practice with feedback group signiﬁcantly outperformed both the restudy and retrieval practice without feedback groups (p < 0.001), and the restudy group signiﬁcantly outperformed the no-feedback group (p = 0.001)"
