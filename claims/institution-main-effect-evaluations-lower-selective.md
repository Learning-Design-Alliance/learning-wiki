## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Graves 2017

Graves, A.L., Hoshino-Browne, E., & Lui, K.P.H. (2017). Swimming against the Tide: Gender Bias in the Physics Classroom. Journal of Women and Minorities in Science and Engineering 23(1), 15-36. [doi:10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584](https://doi.org/10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-way 2x2x2 ANOVA (lecturer sex, student sex, institution) on overall evaluation across the two universities (n = 66 more selective, n = 55 less selective). The article reports the mean was "significantly lower at the more selective university", and the gender interaction persisted at each university independently.

> "There was a significant main effect of institution, F(1, 113) = 8.42, p = .004, but this variable did not interact with lecturer sex or student sex."
