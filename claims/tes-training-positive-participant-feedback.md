## Evidence

### Popa Călin 2023

Popa Călin, Schlanger Diana, Prunoiu Virgiliu Mihail, Puia Ion Cosmin, Zaharie Florin. (2023). A novel step-by-step training program for transanal endoscopic surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04296-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous post-workshop feedback form assessing overall impression, current practical activity in transanal surgery, and perceived advantages of structured training. Self-reported percentages as printed; no effect size and no comparison group.

> "Thirty-one responses were received (77.5%). Regarding the overall impression of the training program, the responses were either ‘Good’ (25.8%) or ‘Very good’ (74.2%). Regarding their current practical activity in transanal surgery, 93.5% reported a higher involvement in cases compared to before undergoing the training program."
