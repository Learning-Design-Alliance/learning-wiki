# Campbell intervention reviews published after the 2014 introduction of MECCIR standards have higher methodological quality than those published before

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Reviews published after MECCIR's release were of higher AMSTAR 2 methodological quality than pre-MECCIR reviews (OR = 6.61). [→ Wang X 2021](#wang-x-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang X 2021

Wang X, Welch V, Li M, Yao L, Littell J, Li H, Yang N, Wang J, Shamseer L, Chen Y, Yang K, Grimshaw JM. (2021). The methodological and reporting characteristics of Campbell reviews: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1134

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Ordered logistic regression across 96 Campbell intervention reviews (50 post-MECCIR, 46 pre-MECCIR), adjusting for co-registration, found post-MECCIR reviews of "higher quality than those published before" with OR = 6.61 (95% CI [2.86, 15.27], p < .001).

> "reviews published after the release of MECCIR were of higher quality than those published before (odds ratio [OR] = 6.61, 95% confidence interval [CI] [2.86, 15.27], p < .001)"
