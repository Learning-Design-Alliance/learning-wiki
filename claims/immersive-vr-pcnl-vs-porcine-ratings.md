## Evidence

### Chahal Baldev 2023

Chahal Baldev, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran. (2023). Virtual reality vs. physical models in surgical skills training. An update of the evidence. Current Opinion in Urology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MOU.0000000000001145

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A study comparing a porcine tissue simulator, the PercMentor VR simulator and the immersive Marion K181 simulator using participant ratings and performance data found the immersive VR beat only conventional VR on ratings; "it did not differ from the porcine model in any domain".

> "While the immersive VR simulator achieved higher ratings across all metrics than the conventional VR simulator, it did not differ from the porcine model in any domain with participants scoring both similar ratings for simulation fidelity and tactile feedback."
