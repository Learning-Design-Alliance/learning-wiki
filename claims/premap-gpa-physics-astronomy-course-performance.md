## Evidence

### Sarah Garner 2012

Sarah Garner, Michael J. Tremmel, Sarah J. Schmidt, John P. Wisniewski, Eric Agol. (2012). Evaluation of a College Freshman Diversity Research Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of course-grade records for students taking six or more upper-level (>223) physics courses (Figure 2). Pre-MAP students perform similarly to Astronomy students in all areas. The authors speculate the astronomy advantage stems from cohort building and early exposure to astronomy in the first two years.

> "Pre-MAP students are receiving lower grades in upper level physics classes, as indicated by a mean GPA of 2.94 for Pre-MAP students compared to 3.28 for all students."
