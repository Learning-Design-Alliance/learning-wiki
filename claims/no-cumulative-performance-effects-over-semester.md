Unit exam scores were analyzed as progressive timepoints to test two competing predictions: gaps increasing via accumulating identity threat, or decreasing as teams develop positive norms. Neither pattern was observed; model outcomes were similar across exams.

> "There was no evidence of any cumulative effects or effects over time that affected student performance during the period of the course."
