## Evidence

### Zheng Que 2022

Zheng Que, Cheng Yu Yan, Sonuga-Barke Edmund, Shum Kathy Kar-man. (2022). Do Executive Dysfunction, Delay Aversion, and Time Perception Deficit Predict ADHD Symptoms and Early Academic Performance in Preschoolers. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-022-00937-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regressions predicting KeyMath-3 numeration and RAST-K Chinese word reading from Time 1 neuropsychological measures, controlling for age, general ability, treatment condition, and household income, in 131 preschoolers (96 followed up).

> "Specifically, time discrimination significantly predicted word reading at Time 1 (ß= − 0.20, p = .02) and numeration was significantly predicted by time reproduction (ß = − 0.17, p = .04) and working memory (ß = − 0.30, p = .02) respectively at Time 1 and Time 2."
