## Evidence

### Fang Fang 2026

Fang Fang, Bu Bing, Jiang Wenmin. (2026). Combining Problem-Based Learning Methods With the WeChat Platform in Teaching Ophthalmology: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65279

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire (adapted from the Course Experience Questionnaire and Study Process Questionnaire) completed by all 108 students, analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test. The scale ran 1=strongly agree to 5=strongly disagree, so the WeChat group's lower mean 1.8333 (SD 0.5746) versus 3.5471 (SD 0.7915) indicates more favorable perception (P=.004). Table 4 also prints effect sizes (e.g., 2.5169 for motivation), but these are not standardized effect sizes in the d/r family, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "More students believed (rather than did not believe) that the WeChat-based PBL class enhanced their learning motivation (traditional group: mean 3.5471, SD 0.7915; WeChat group: mean 1.8333, SD 0.5746; P=.004)"
