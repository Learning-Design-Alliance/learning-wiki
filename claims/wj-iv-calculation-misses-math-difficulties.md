# Standard achievement assessments often fail to detect mathematical difficulties in children with dyslexia

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Around 30% of dyslexic children with no apparent math difficulties scored below the 16th percentile on the WJ-IV Calculation test, while ~25% of dyslexic children with math difficulties scored at or above the 25th percentile on it. [→ B. Pedemonte 2024](#b-pedemonte-2024)
`q2 i?` The authors interpret that commonly administered assessments may not capture mathematical difficulties, especially non-severe or non-number deficits. [→ B. Pedemonte 2024 (2)](#b-pedemonte-2024-2)

## Evidence

### B. Pedemonte 2024

B. Pedemonte, C. W. Pereira, V. Borghesani, M. Ebbert, I. E. Allen, P. Pinheiro-Chagas, J. De Leon, Z. Miller, B. L. Tee & M. L. Gorno-Tempini. (2024). Profiles of mathematical deficits in children with dyslexia. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00217-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-check of the WJ-IV Calculation test against clinician and MCB classification. "around 30% of children with no apparent difﬁculties in mathematics (8 out of 25 children) obtained a result below the 16 percentiles outside the average range", yet none had low prior math scores.

> "In the WJ-IV Calculation test, around 30% of children with no apparent difﬁculties in mathematics (8 out of 25 children) obtained a result below the 16 percentiles outside the average range"

### B. Pedemonte 2024 (2)

B. Pedemonte, C. W. Pereira, V. Borghesani, M. Ebbert, I. E. Allen, P. Pinheiro-Chagas, J. De Leon, Z. Miller, B. L. Tee & M. L. Gorno-Tempini. (2024). Profiles of mathematical deficits in children with dyslexia. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00217-x

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion, based on their cohort results, that "commonly administered assessments such as WJ... WIAT... KTEA... and FAM may not capture difﬁculties in mathematics", particularly non-severe or non-number deficits. This is an interpretive claim, not a tested comparison.

> "The commonly administered assessments such as WJ (Calculation Test, Broad Math, Applied Problems, Fluency), WIAT (Math, Numerical, Problem Solving, Fluency), KTEA (Math Concepts and Applications, Computation, Fluency, Problem Sol-ving), and FAM may not capture difﬁculties in mathematics, particularly if those difﬁculties are not severe or related to number deﬁcits."
