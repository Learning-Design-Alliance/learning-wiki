## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2015

Trevor I. Smith, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Student Understanding of the Boltzmann Factor. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Teaching-experiment interview with four students at School 1 after classroom instruction; one participant (Joel) quoted the textbook derivation practically verbatim yet failed to connect Eqs. 11 and 14. "Joel was at a loss" when asked about multiplicity.

> "However, when asked how the multi- plicity of the reservoir relates to the Boltzmann factor, Joel was at a loss."
