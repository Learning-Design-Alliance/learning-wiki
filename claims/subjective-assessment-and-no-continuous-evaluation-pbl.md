## Evidence

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding under the assessment-methods theme. Participants attributed subjectivity to the open-ended nature of problem situations and the importance of critical thinking, proposing clear assessment criteria, checklists, standards, and guidelines.

> "We, as well as the students, could grow dissatisfied with tests' subjective nature, which also raises questions about their fairness and reliability."

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025 (2)

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: without continuous assessment, teachers cannot identify students' strengths and flaws, and end-of-term evaluations may not depict students' advancement during PBL; frequent formative assessments are proposed.

> "About two-thirds of the participants raised their perspectives on the challenges they encounter due to the lack of continuous evaluation in PBL implementation and explained the limitations of traditional end-of-term or spot assessments in capturing ongoing student progress."
