### Shih-Yin Lin 2016

Exploring University Students’ Engagement with Online Video Lectures in a Blended Introductory Mechanics Course. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Daily clickstream analysis of total video-accessing sessions (Figure 6). The article reports "a spike in lab video accessing sessions on lab due dates" but none for lecture videos on homework due dates, and lab videos were accessed most the week before lab due dates.

> "there was a spike in lab video accessing sessions on lab due dates, but there was no spike in access ing sessions  for lecture-oriented videos on homework due dates"

### Shih-Yin Lin 2016 (2)

Shih-Yin Lin, John M. Aiken, Daniel T. Seaton, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Michael F. Schatz. (2016). Exploring University Students’ Engagement with Online Video Lectures in a Blended Introductory Mechanics Course. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Per-video daily access analysis (Figure 7) of 161 students. "only about one-fifth of the students (21.7% for midterm exam and 23.6% for final exam) revisited at least one old video during the exam period," showing students focused on current-week videos.

> "only about one-fifth of the students (21.7% for midterm exam and 23.6% for final exam) revisited at least one old video during the exam period"
