Evidence¶

Liang 2023¶

Liang, M., Lim, C. P., Park, J., & Mendoza, N. B. (2023). A review of ICT-enabled learning for schoolgirls in Asia and its impacts on education equity. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10178-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 30 empirical studies identified through three-round screening of four databases, with open, axial and selective coding of each study's key ICT usage. The review reports the four approaches and their study counts, noting "CAL is the most common approach among the included studies".

"These inter-related approaches are: (1) Emerging technologies-enabled learning (3 studies); (2) Digital game-based learning (6 studies); (3) Mobile-enabled learning (7 studies); and (4) Computer-assisted learning (14 studies)."
