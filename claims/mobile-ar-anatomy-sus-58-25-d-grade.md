## Evidence

### Williams A 2026

Williams A, Sun Z, Vaccarezza M. (2026). Augmented reality for teaching undergraduate human anatomy: An educators' perspective. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70214

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Survey of 10 anatomy educators at Curtin University using the validated System Usability Scale after interacting with Complete Anatomy's mobile AR feature on an iPad. The study reports a "mean score of 58.25, SD ± 15.41" with a median of 56.25, below the SUS average of 68. No effect size is printed.

> "The SUS survey returned a mean score of 58.25, SD ± 15.41 and a similar median score of 56.25 for the mobile AR feature using the CA application and iPad. A mean score of 58.25 is considered below the SUS average of ‘68’ and translates to a ‘D’ on the curved grading scale"
