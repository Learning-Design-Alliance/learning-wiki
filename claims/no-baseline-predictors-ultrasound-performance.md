## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mulder Tessa A. 2026

Mulder Tessa A., Jacobs Robin C., Zuurmond Manon S., Qaddoumi Adel, Regeer Madelien V., Dekker Friedo W., Bauer Martijn P., Srámek Alexandr, Langers Alexandra M.J., Hierck Beerend P. (2026). Augmedicine: Ultrasound: Training in ultrasound using XR – a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08897-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT, baseline MRT and anatomical knowledge tests showed no significant correlations with ultrasound outcomes; e.g., "EPSS: R = 0.13, p = 0.33" for MRT. Multivariable regression and group interaction analyses were also non-significant.

> "No significant correlations were found between MRT scores and any of the ultrasound performance outcomes (EPSS: R = 0.13, p = 0.33; TAPSE: R = 0.07, p = 0.62; total time: R = −0.22, p = 0.10; optimal image score: R = 0.08, p = 0.56)."
