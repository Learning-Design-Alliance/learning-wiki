### Mahembe GR 2025

Mahembe GR, Mash R. (2025). The competency of nurses in basic life support at district hospitals in Cape Town, South Africa. S Afr Fam Pract. https://doi.org/10.4102/safp.v67i1.6056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Four-point Likert-scale attitude items in the cross-sectional survey of 243 nurses; the highest-rated items were regular review courses (mean 3.5) and needing more training (mean 3.3), while confidence in infant BLS scored 2.5. Descriptive means only; no effect size.

> "In general, participants had positive attitudes towards BLS but felt that they needed more training, particularly for BLS in infants and in the use of the AED."
