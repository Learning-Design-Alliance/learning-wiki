`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A cross-domain analysis evaluated both apprentice learner models across seven additional tutoring domains beyond experimental design, comparing them to Example-Tracing and human learners. Both models learned across a wide range of tutors and domains without additional specialized domain knowledge.

> "show that they are capable of learning an expert model for seven additional tutoring systems that teach a wide range of knowledge types (associations, categories, and skills) across multiple domains (language, math, engineering, and science)"
