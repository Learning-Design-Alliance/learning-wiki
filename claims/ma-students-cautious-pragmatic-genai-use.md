Pilot survey with open-ended questions conducted in March 2026 among first-year MA students (n=22) in the Applied Linguistics programme at the University of Warsaw; the author reports that students "generally adopt a cautious and pragmatic approach to GenAI". Prompts were mostly introductory zero-shot or few-shot requests.

> "The findings indicate that students generally adopt a cautious and pragmatic approach to GenAI, which is broadly consistent with recent surveys of professional translators"
