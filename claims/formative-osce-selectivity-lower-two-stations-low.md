## Evidence

### Möltner Andreas 2020

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results-section comparison of corrected selectivities across the formative OSCE and the summative comparison OSCEs. The article cautions the point total rests on only three stations, so "this sum is subject to more error variance in the OSCE General Medicine".

> "The corrected selectivities were on average lower than in the comparative OSCEs, only the OSCE Internal Medicine SS 2018 showed lower values (rit=0.358 compared to 0.386, see table 4 (Tab. 4) and figure 3 (Fig. 3))."

### Möltner Andreas 2020 (2)

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Station-level analysis of the formative OSCE's 15 stations: eleven exceed 0.300 part-whole corrected selectivity, two are just below, and the two named stations are markedly lower. The Discussion recommends "a review is recommended due to low selectivity" for two physical examination stations.

> "Significantly lower are the stations "Physical Examination Liver" with rit=0.112 and "Pulse status" with rit=0.099."
