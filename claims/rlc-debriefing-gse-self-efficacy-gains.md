## Evidence

### Almomani Emad 2025

Almomani Emad, Tobin John, Fernandes Sonia, Sullivan Jacqueline, Saadeh Omar, Mustafa Emad, Pattison Natalie, Alinier Guillaume. (2025). A reflective learning conversation debriefing model for interprofessional simulation based education. BMC Medical Education, 25:1434. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07765-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported General Self-Efficacy questionnaire completed pre- and post-course by all 130 participants, compared between groups with the Mann-Whitney U test (Table 3). The article reports "statistically significant improvements" with no effect size printed.

> "Post-course comparisons revealed statistically significant improvements in the experimental group across all GSE subscales compared to the control group (p ≤.002)."

### Almomani Emad 2025 (2)

Almomani Emad, Tobin John, Fernandes Sonia, Sullivan Jacqueline, Saadeh Omar, Mustafa Emad, Pattison Natalie, Alinier Guillaume. (2025). A reflective learning conversation debriefing model for interprofessional simulation based education. BMC Medical Education, 25:1434. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07765-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline (pre-course) GSE comparisons in the same quasi-experiment, using the Mann-Whitney U test. No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "the pre-course comparisons revealed no statistically significant differences between the experimental and control groups across any of the General Self-Efficacy (GSE) subscales (p >.05)."
