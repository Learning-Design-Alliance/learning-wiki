`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE interaction analysis (Time 2 × Age; Time 2 × Gender) in the RCT; the article notes the male finding was inconsistent with a previous study and attributes it to mostly female clinical professionals in Taiwan. Coefficients are unstandardized, so no effect size is printed.

### Wen MC 2020 (2)
