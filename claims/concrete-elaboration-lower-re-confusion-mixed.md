## Evidence

### Shayla Sharmin 2026

Shayla Sharmin, Mohammad Fahim Abrar, Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2026). Micro-level AI Feedback Features and Student Responses in Consecutive LLM Tutoring Interactions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests on 1,718 confusion windows from naturalistic LLM tutoring logs, with Cramér's V as effect size. The article reports "lower re -confusion (15.6% vs. 26.4%; χ²(1) = 4.69, p = .030, V = .052)".

> "Concrete elaboration was associated with higher understanding (21.1% vs. 11.2%; χ²(1) = 7.21, p = .007, V = .065) and lower re -confusion (15.6% vs. 26.4%; χ²(1) = 4.69, p = .030, V = .052)"
