## Evidence

### Usán Pablo 2022

Usán Pablo, Salavera Carlos, Quílez-Robres Alberto, Lozano-Blasco Raquel. (2022). Behaviour Patterns between Academic Motivation, Burnout and Academic Performance in Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191912663

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 3) among motivation, burnout, and performance scales in 398 primary students. The study reports "exhaustion (r = 0.535) and cynicism (r = 0.395)" as positive correlations with amotivation, and negative correlations with intrinsic motivations.

> "Amotivation was shown to be positively correlated with the two less self-determined dimensions of burnout: exhaustion (r = 0.535) and cynicism (r = 0.395), as well as with some extrinsic motivations, and negatively correlated with predominantly intrinsic motivations."
