`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The review reports this prior meta-analysis as background motivation, citing it as aggregating findings from only 10 studies with restricted search terms. The current authors argue averaging across domains and measures can mask domain-specific effects.

> "Klassen and Tze ( 2014) also conducted a meta-analysis between teacher personality and teacher effectiveness and found a small but significant relationship ( r = .08, p < .05)."
