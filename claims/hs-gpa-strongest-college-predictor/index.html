Evidence¶

Stevens 2014¶

Stevens, W. D., Allensworth, E. M., Pareja, A. S., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J. A., Rosenkranz, T., & Sebastian, J. (2014). Free to Fail or On-Track to College: An Introduction to the UChicago CCSR Research Series. UChicago CCSR Research Brief. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/free-fail-or-track-college

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The brief summarizes research in Chicago and nationally on predictors of college outcomes, reporting that "a student’s high school GPA is by far the strongest predictor" of admission, persistence, and graduation compared with ACT and SAT scores. The brief also notes students need at least a B average to have even a chance of obtaining a four-year degree.

"Research in Chicago and across the country has shown that a student’s high school GPA is by far the strongest predictor of college admis -sion, persistence, and graduation—much more so than test scores such as the ACT and SAT."
