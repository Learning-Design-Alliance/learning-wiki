## Evidence

### Usán Pablo 2022

Usán Pablo, Salavera Carlos, Quílez-Robres Alberto, Lozano-Blasco Raquel. (2022). Behaviour Patterns between Academic Motivation, Burnout and Academic Performance in Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191912663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis ('introduction' method) with performance as criterion and motivation and burnout factorial scores as predictors (Table 5). Significant predictors include self-efficacy (t = 2.270, Sig. 0.027), intrinsic achievement (t = 2.421, Sig. 0.018), and amotivation (t = 2.373, Sig. 0.021); "Nagelkerke's R2 statistics yielded an adjustment value of 0.289".

> "The most valuable variables in terms of prediction were the most self-determined variable of burnout (self-efficacy), intrinsic achievement motivation, and amotivation. Nagelkerke's R2 statistics yielded an adjustment value of 0.289 to explain the model."
