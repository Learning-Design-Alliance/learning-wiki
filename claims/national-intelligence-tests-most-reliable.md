### Bell 1921

Bell, J. C. (1921). Group tests of intelligence: An annotated list. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 12*(2), 103–108. [doi:10.1037/h0073682](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073682)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r1`

Annotated-list entry for the National Intelligence Tests, outgrowths of the Army Intelligence Tests prepared under National Research Council auspices by a committee of Haggerty, Terman, Thorndike, Whipple and Yerkes (chairman), for grades 3 to 8. The reliability judgment is the author's evaluative comment, not a printed coefficient.

> "The General Education Board appropriated $25,000 for the standardization of these tests, and they are probably the most reliable of any group tests of intelligence. Each scale is furnished in five forms of equivalent difficulty."
