Evidence¶

Fitamen 2019¶

Fitamen, C., Blaye, A. & Camos, V. (2019). Five-year-old children's working memory can be improved when children act on a transparent goal cue. Scientific Reports 9:15342. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-51869-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

ANCOVA in the 5-year-old group of the same between-subjects experiment found no cue effect under visual tracking (F < 1) and a significant Cue × Tracking interaction, "F(1,73) = 4.82, p = 0.03, ηp2 = 0.06".

"This beneficial cue effect vanished in the visual-tracking condition, F < 1, resulting in a significant interaction between Cue and Tracking in 5-year-olds, F(1,73) = 4.82, p = 0.03, ηp2 = 0.06."
