`q3 i3` A repeated-measures ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of Condition on RPP originality (ηP² = 0.49), sqrt(IOC) (ηP² = 0.23) and fluency (ηP² = 0.12), with the BOTH condition higher than ONE and NO on IOC and originality. [→ Xinyue Wang 2024](#xinyue-wang-2024)

## Evidence

### Xinyue Wang 2024

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA across 27 dyads solving RPPs under BOTH, ONE and NO gesture conditions. The article reports significant main effects with ηP² of 0.23 for sqrt(IOC) and 0.49 for originality, and post-hoc tests showing "higher sqrt (IOC) and originality in the‘BOTH’condition".

> "Results demonstrated a signiﬁcant main effect of Condition on sqrt (IOC),F (2,52) = 7.96, P = 0.001, ηP 2 = 0.23; RPP originality, F (2,52) = 25.29, P < 0.001, ηP 2 =0 . 4 9"
