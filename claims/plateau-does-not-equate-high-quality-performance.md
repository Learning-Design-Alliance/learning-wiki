`q1 · i?` · `review · r3`

Conceptual argument in the article's introduction, offered without data: performance thresholds such as time to plateau are often simplistic and may not capture sufficient evidence about the learning process to support claims of mastery or proficiency.

> "a plateau in performance does not necessarily equate to high‐quality performance; it only implies that a surgeon is no longer improving1."
