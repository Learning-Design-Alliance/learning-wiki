# In Physics I Lab control sections women were responsible for less equipment usage than men, but no gendered difference was observed in Physics II Lab

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Matthew Dew 2024

Matthew Dew, Emma Hunt, Viranga Perera, Jonathan Perry, Gregorio Ponti, and Andrew Loveridge. (2024). Group Dynamics in Inquiry-based Labs: Gender Inequities and the Efficacy of Partner Agreements. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling of video-coded equipment group fractions in one recorded Physics I control section (127 women, 61 observations of men and women as printed). The article reports β = −0.226±0.061, p <0.001 for women versus men.

> "In Physics I Lab without partner agreements, we find that women are responsible for less equipment usage than men (β = −0.226±0.061, p <0.001) accounting for group size, lecture track, equipment preference, and random effects."

### Matthew Dew 2024 (2)

Matthew Dew, Emma Hunt, Viranga Perera, Jonathan Perry, Gregorio Ponti, and Andrew Loveridge. (2024). Group Dynamics in Inquiry-based Labs: Gender Inequities and the Efficacy of Partner Agreements. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same hierarchical linear modeling applied to the recorded Physics II control section. The article reports no gendered difference in equipment usage, β = 0.066 ± 0.088, p = 0.452; no effect size is printed.

> "However, in Physics II Lab without partner agreements, we do not observe a gendered difference in equipment usage (β = 0.066 ± 0.088, p= 0.452)."
