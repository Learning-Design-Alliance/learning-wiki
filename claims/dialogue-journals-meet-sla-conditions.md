# Dialogue journals inherently meet conditions deemed necessary for second language acquisition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Dialogue journals provide meaningful communication, comprehensible input, output opportunities, and interaction, conditions researchers identify as necessary for second language acquisition. [→ Kimberly Miller Linnell 2010](#kimberly-miller-linnell-2010)

## Evidence

### Kimberly Miller Linnell 2010

Kimberly Miller Linnell. (2010). Using Dialogue Journals to Focus on Form. Journal of Adult Education Information Series, No. 1, Vol. 39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ891083

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument by the author, citing second language acquisition research (Ellis, Krashen, Long), that dialogue journals satisfy requisite acquisition conditions: "meaningful communication", comprehensible input slightly above current proficiency, output, and interaction. No empirical data are reported.

> "they are ideally suited for language learning, meeting many of the conditions deemed necessary for the acquisition of a second language"
