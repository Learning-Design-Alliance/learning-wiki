## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors propose that more SP simulation opportunities and sufficient post-simulation debriefing time to share feelings of anxiety and tension can provide more realism and reduce anxiety when communicating with standardized patients. [→ Kim Eunyoung 2020](#kim-eunyoung-2020)

## Evidence

### Kim Eunyoung 2020

Kim Eunyoung, Kim Sang Suk, Kim Sunghee. (2020). Effects of Infection Control Education for Nursing Students Using Standardized Patients vs. Peer Role-Play. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18010107

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

This is the authors' interpretive recommendation in the Discussion, offered without direct testing in the present study; it is grounded in their reading of prior literature on SP-related nervousness and debriefing.

> "Therefore, to provide more realism and reduce anxiety when communicating with SP, the opportunities for simulation using SPs should be increased, and sufficient debriefing time between colleagues after simulation to share the feelings of anxiety and tension should be provided [62]."
