Executive Summary and Chapter 3 graduation-rate comparisons for the 2001 cohort by disability category among on-track students. The report concludes being on-track does not ensure students with mild cognitive disabilities or emotional disturbances are no longer at risk of dropping out.

> "87 percent of on-track students without disabilities graduated in five years, while only 78 percent of on-track students who entered high school two or more years below grade graduated in five years. Seventy-seven percent of on-track students with learning disabilities and 67 percent of on-track students with mild cognitive disabilities graduated in five years."
