# Students in the flipped classroom group scored higher on the final theory exam than lecture-group students (8.2 ± 0.7 vs 7.0 ± 0.8)

## Evidence

### Wei Yaru 2025

Wei Yaru, Peng Zhengjun. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom model based on Bloom’s Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in endodontics education for undergraduate dental students. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact groups of sixteen third-year stomatology students taught the same chapter by the same professor. Exam scores were converted to a 10-point scale; the flipped group scored "8.2 ± 0.7" versus 7.0 ± 0.8, p < 0.001. No effect size was printed.

> "In the final theory exam, students’ exam scores in the flipped classroom group increased from 7.0 ± 0.8 to 8.2 ± 0.7, with an average improvement of 17.1% (p < 0.001)."
