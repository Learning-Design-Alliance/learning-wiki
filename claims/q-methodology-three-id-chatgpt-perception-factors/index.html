Evidence¶

Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak 2025¶

Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak. (2025). An exploration of instructional designers' prioritizations for integrating ChatGPT in design practice. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10509-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Q-methodology study in which 19 practicing instructional designers sorted 25 statements about integrating ChatGPT into design practice; principal component analysis with varimax rotation produced a three-factor solution. The article reports that the "three-factor solution, which explained 61% of the variance in the model, was generated from the Q-factor analysis."
