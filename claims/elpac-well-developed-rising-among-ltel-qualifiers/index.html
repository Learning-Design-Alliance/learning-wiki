Trend analysis shown in Figure 3 of CDE summative ELPAC results. The report states proportions meeting the well-developed benchmark are "rising from about 17% in 2020–21 to nearly 23% in 2022–23." Of those evaluated in 2022–23, 62% reached moderately or well-developed benchmarks.
