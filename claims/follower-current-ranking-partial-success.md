## Evidence

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer 2018

Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2018). Investigating student understanding of bipolar junction transistor circuits. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Follower current ranking task administered to one U1 physics class (N = 12) and three U2 physics classes (N = 155); students were "considerably more successful" than on the amplifier comparison task, and no incorrect ranking exceeded 10% of responses.

> "only between 50% and 75% of students in a given class indicated the correct ranking of currents, and at least two-thirds of such students also provided correct reasoning in support of their ranking."
