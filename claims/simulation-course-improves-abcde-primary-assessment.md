## Evidence

### Drost-de Klerck Amanda M 2020

Drost-de Klerck Amanda M, Olgers Tycho J, van de Meeberg Evelien K, Schonrock-Adema Johanna, ter Maaten Jan C. (2020). Use of simulation training to teach the ABCDE primary assessment: an observational study in a Dutch University Hospital with a 3–4 months follow-up. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational pre/post/follow-up study of 30 voluntary participants of a 2-day simulation-based ABCDE course, scored on video-recorded simulations by two blinded observers. Median total score was "2,79 at T1, 5.22 at T2 and 4.70 at T3"; Wilcoxon tests showed T2>T1 and T3>T1, both p<0.001.

> "The mean ranks on the total primary assessment directly after the course (T2 vs T1 p<0.001) and 3–4 months after the course (T3 vs T1 p<0.001) were significantly better than before the course."
