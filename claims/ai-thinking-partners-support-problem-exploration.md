# Adult learners perceive conversational AI as thinking partners that support problem exploration without direct instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adult learners perceived chatbots and conversational AI as supporting problem exploration by surfacing assumptions and anticipating failure points before guidance is given. [→ Jinhee Kim 2026](#jinhee-kim-2026)

## Evidence

### Jinhee Kim 2026

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Designing AI systems to support a productive-failure-based learning: insights from adult learners on AI applications and AI system design principles. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10655-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Focus group interviews with 35 graduate students in seven cross-institutional teams, anchored in storyboards and paper prototypes, thematically analyzed. Learners described chatbots posing failure-based questions; one participant noted the chatbot encouraged learners to "surface assumptions and anticipate failure points before receiving any guidance."

> "one team’s prototype included a chatbot that posed questions such as, “Under what real-world conditions would this solution break down, and why?”, encouraging learners to surface assumptions and anticipate failure points before receiving any guidance."
