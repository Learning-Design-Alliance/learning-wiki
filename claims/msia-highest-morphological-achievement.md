## Evidence

### Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of morphology subtest scores in the 120-student quasi-experiment, pretest as covariate. The article reports "the calculated f value which is 11.554 was significant"; no effect size is named in the reporting text.

> "Table 7 shows  that the calculated f value which is 11.554 was significant at p>0.05.The hypothesis is therefore rejected; this result indicates that the instructional ap proaches had significant eff ect on students morphological achievement."

### Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012 (2)

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Duncan test on morphology means (Table 8): MSIA 10.67 (A); MCIA 7.80, MSIA&MCIA 8.37, CIA 7.80 (B). The article states MSIA is "still the most effective, having the highest mean score".

> "The table above indicates that all the three instructional approaches, which are grouped under letter ‘B’, have the same effect on the morphological ability of the students. Ho wever, MSIA which is grouped under letter ‘A’ is still the most effective, having the highest mean score."
