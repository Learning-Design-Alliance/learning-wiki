Key finding from the evaluation of the 2021-2022 school-year tutoring pilot at Breakthrough Central Texas and Breakthrough Greater Boston, measured with i-Ready Math and Renaissance Star Math assessments; the report states gains were "similar to gains from a national sample in a pre-pandemic school year." No effect size is printed.
