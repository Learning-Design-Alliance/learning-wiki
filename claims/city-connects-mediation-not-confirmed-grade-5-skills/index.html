Evidence¶

O'Dwyer 2016¶

O'Dwyer, L. M., Lee-St. John, T., Raczek, A. E., Luna Bazaldua, D. A., & Walsh, M. (2016). Examining the role of early academic and non-cognitive skills as mediators of the effects of City Connects on middle school academic outcomes. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567236

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel mediation analysis (Models 1-3) with Aroian Sobel tests of indirect effects in the Boston Public Schools sample. The article reports the indirect effects "are not significantly different from zero" for all hypothesized mediators.

"Against our original hypothesis, these results indicate that the indirect effect of City Connects on mathematics achievement through the grade 5 academic measures and through effort are not significantly different from zero."
