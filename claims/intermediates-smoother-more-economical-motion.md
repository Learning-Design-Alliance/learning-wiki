## Evidence

### Gholami Soheil 2024

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Jerk-based motion smoothness (MMS) computed from motion capture in the anastomosis task; independent t-tests across all four segments and both instruments were significant (e.g., S.3 tweezers t=-6.97, p<.001), favoring intermediates.

> "These disparities in motion smoothness were determined to be statistically significant throughout the entire analysis, indicating that intermediates displayed more fluid and stable movements with the instruments compared to novices"

### Gholami Soheil 2024 (2)

Gholami Soheil, Manon Anaëlle, Yao Kunpeng, Billard Aude, Meling Torstein R. (2024). An objective skill assessment framework for microsurgical anastomosis based on ALI scores. Acta Neurochirurgica 166(1):104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-05934-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Economy of volume (MEoV), the ratio of maximum covered volume to path length, was analyzed per segment; all eight instrument-by-segment comparisons favored intermediates (e.g., S.3 tweezers t=-5.03, p<.001).

> "intermediate participants demonstrated more economical performance in completing all segments compared to the novices, using both instruments. These disparities in the economy of volume were statistically significant in all instances"
