## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Questionnaire mean scores differed significantly across education levels (F = 9.754, p = 0.000), with postgraduate nurses (14.48 ± 2.70) scoring higher than licensed (12.77 ± 2.85) and vocational school nurses (10.86 ± 3.24). ## Evidence

### Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi 2025

Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi. (2025). Investigation of Nurses' Level of Knowledge on Medical Device‐Related Pressure Injuries: A Descriptive and Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70776

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One‐way ANOVA across education groups in the 217‐nurse survey; Table 1 prints F = 9.754, p = 0.000 with group means 10.86 ± 3.24 (vocational), 12.77 ± 2.85 (license), and 14.48 ± 2.70 (postgraduate). No effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "The mean questionnaire scores of graduate nurses were found to be significantly higher than those of high school and undergraduate graduates."
