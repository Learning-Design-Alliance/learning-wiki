Exploratory isolated-learner analysis in the same course offering comparing isolated learners against comparison students. The article reports "AUC = 0.96" with 95% CI [0.89,1.00] and permutation p = .011, versus AUC = 0.91 for gap prevalence alone.

"exposure to higher-priority topics during weeks they reported difficulty ranked them above comparison students (AUC = 0.96,𝑛iso = 2,𝑛comp = 31, 95% CI [0.89,1.00] , permutation𝑝=.011 ), compared with AUC = 0.91 for gap prevalence alone."

Cohort-level synthesis counts (n = 279): three students were surfaced through synthesis alone among 134 baseline-unidentified students, and 22 more were identified jointly. A representative Case A showed no single-source concern signal yet produced σ = 0.509.

"Across the full cohort ( 𝑛= 279 ), three students ( 2.2% of 134 baseline-unidentified students) were surfaced through synthesis alone, while 22 additional students were identified jointly by synthesis and at least one individual signal."
