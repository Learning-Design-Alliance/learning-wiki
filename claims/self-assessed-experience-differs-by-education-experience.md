## Evidence

### Kopciuch D 2026

Kopciuch D, Ciosek K, Adamska K, Chojara N, Paczkowska A, Zaprutko T, Ratajczak P, Nowakowska E, Kus K. (2026). Analysis of the knowledge and skills of physiotherapists and physiotherapy students regarding pulmonary rehabilitation in post-COVID-19 patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08552-2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 110 respondents (60 physiotherapists, 50 students) in Poland, analysed with Kruskal–Wallis tests. The article reports the between-group difference as "H = 37.49; p = 0.003" with a printed effect size of η²_H = 0.26, described as a large effect size.

> "Self-assessed experience differed significantly across education and experience groups (H = 37.49; p = 0.003; η²_H = 0.26, indicating a large effect size)."
