# Medical students taught with CBL combined with a Metronomic Online Classroom achieve significantly higher exam scores than students taught with traditional CBL after extreme scores are removed

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After removing extreme values, the CBL+MOC group outperformed the traditional CBL group on the end-of-course exam (102.50 ± 19.66 vs. 86.58 ± 24.95, p = 0.04). [→ Zhi Fei-Hang 2025](#zhi-fei-hang-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhi Fei-Hang 2025

Zhi Fei-Hang, Liu Wei, Zhang Xinyao, Zhang Tao, Su Yuhan, Huang Ruoxuan, Feng Yan-Fen, Lei Yi-Yan. (2025). Exploration of CBL combined with “Metronomic” online classroom in thoracic trauma and closed chest drainage teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized two-group study of 43 fifth-year medical students; 39 completed the online test (21 CBL, 18 CBL+MOC). After excluding extreme scores the CBL+MOC group “outperforming the traditional CBL group” reached significance at p = 0.04; no effect size is printed.

> "However, when extreme values were removed, the difference reached statistical significance, with the CBL + MOC group (102.50 ± 19.66) outperforming the traditional CBL group (86.58 ± 24.95; p = 0.04)."
