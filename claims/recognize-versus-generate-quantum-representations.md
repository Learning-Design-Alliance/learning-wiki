# Student performance depends on whether students must generate or only recognize a representation: 89% recognized the correct position-space wavefunction but only 43% could generate it

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On a multiple-choice retention question, 89% of undergraduates recognized that Ψ(x) = ⟨x|Ψ⟩, but on the free-response Q1 only 43% generated the expression, indicating students are still developing expertise. [→ Marshman 2024](#marshman-2024)

## Evidence

### Marshman 2024

Marshman, E., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2024). Using multiple representations to improve student understanding of quantum states. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Comparison of the same content posed in multiple-choice (retention question from the QMFPS survey) versus free-response (Q1) format to 85 undergraduates. The authors note the retention question "provides some cues as well as the multiple-choice format" while Q1 provides no scaffolding.

> "The difference in student performance in Q1 vs. the retention question indicates that student performance is dependent on whether they must generate or only recognize an answer"
