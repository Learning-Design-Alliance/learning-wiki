## Evidence

### Kevin A. Pimbblet 2003

Kevin A. Pimbblet, John C. Newman. (2003). Ex-nihilo II: Examination Syllabi and the Sequencing of Cosmology Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/38/3/307](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/38/3/307)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' recommendation for GCSE courses, grounded in their analysis of examination syllabus content. They note the CMB provides "unrivalled evidence for an evolving Universe" and that steady state fell with the CMB's discovery, which only the Big Bang model explains naturally.

> "Therefore, within any G.C.S.E. course, we advise teachers to commence cosmology with a review of some of the observational evidence in favour of the evolutionary Big Bang model: the Hubble relation and the cosmic microwave background radiation."
