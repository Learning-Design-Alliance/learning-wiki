## Evidence

### Bagheri Sara 2025

Bagheri Sara, Sedigh Amin, Zarei Zahra, Kazemzadeh Mozhgan, Sharifi Nader. (2025). Comparison of A Mini-Clinical evaluation exercise with the routine logbook in assessing clinical competence of operating room trainees: A parallel randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07560-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the two groups over three assessments one week apart (Greenhouse-Geisser corrected). Table 3 prints the Time effect with effect size 0.816 (P < 0.001) and the Time*group interaction with effect size 0.108 (P = 0.004).

> "Based on the results of this analysis, the effect of time on the group was also significant (P-value = 0.001)."
