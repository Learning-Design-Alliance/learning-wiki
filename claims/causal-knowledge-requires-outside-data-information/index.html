Weidlich 2022¶

Weidlich, J., Gašević, D., Drachsler, H. (2022). Causal Inference and Bias in Learning Analytics: A Primer on Pitfalls Using Directed Acyclic Graphs. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 183–199. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7577

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the introduction, citing Hernán et al. and Bareinboim et al.: the article argues that "the leap from patterns in data to causal claims is a qualitative one, needing information from 'outside' the data." No empirical data are offered; this is the paper's foundational premise.

"Causal knowledge cannot be derived from mere analysis of data, as the mechanistic reality we are interested in, and the data generated from it are fundamentally distinct"
