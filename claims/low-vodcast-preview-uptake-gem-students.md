## Evidence

### Rae 2017

Rae, M. G., & McCarthy, M. (2017). The impact of vodcast utilisation upon student learning of Physiology by first year graduate to entry medicine students. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 17(2), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v17i2.21125

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported survey data from 62 of 73 students on how many of the nine vodcasts they previewed. Descriptive statistics only; no effect size or inferential test applies.

> "The results indicate that 77% of respondents viewed at least one vodcast prior to attending the respective lecture, but that only 15% did so for all nine of the GM1001 vodcasts. It is also significant that over 20% of the class did not view/listen to any of the vodcasts at all prior to attending classes (overall class average = 2.6 ± 0.3, n=62)."
