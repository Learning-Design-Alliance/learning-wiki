# Positive motivational assessments of introductory activities correlate weakly with learning outcomes on near-in-time tests but not with distant exam results

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students' ratings of how interesting, instructive and fun the introductory activities were correlated positively and mainly significantly, but weakly, with test results close in time, with no significant relationship to the more distant exams. [→ Lauc 2020](#lauc-2020)

## Evidence

### Lauc 2020

Lauc, T., Jagodić, G. K., & Bistrović, J. (2020). Effects of Multimedia Instructional Message on Motivation and Academic Performance of Elementary School Students in Croatia. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 491-508. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13431a

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations between pooled motivational assessments and test/exam results (N=172) were positive and mainly significant but low (e.g. Test 2.3 fun r = .36**, Test 1.2 instructive r = .30**), with no significant correlations for Exam 1 or Exam 2.

> "The correlations between students’ assessments of the emotional and motivational aspects of introductory activities irrespective of their mode of delivery were positive and mainly statistically significant but rather low."
