## Evidence

### Sridhar Ashwin N. 2017

Sridhar Ashwin N., Briggs Tim P., Kelly John D., Nathan Senthil. (2017). Training in Robotic Surgery—an Overview. Current Urology Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11934-017-0710-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a two-group resident study comparing dV-Trainer training with routine dVSS dry lab training; the dV-Trainer group showed improved Pattern Cutting and Peg Board times with the printed P values. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Lerner et al. [24] showed that training with the dV-Trainer™ substantially improved performance in Pattern Cutting and Peg Board times (P = 0.04 and P = 0.006, respectively) on the dVSS (dry lab) when compared with training on the dVSS alone."
