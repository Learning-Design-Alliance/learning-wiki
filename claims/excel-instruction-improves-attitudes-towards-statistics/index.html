Serhat Aydın 2016¶

Serhat Aydın. (2016). Using Excel in Teacher Education for Sustainability. Journal of Teacher Education for Sustainability, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1515/jtes-2016-0017

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest t-tests for independent samples on Rasch mean SATS-36 scores of the control (n=52) and experiment (n=50) groups after instruction. The article reports "significant differences between control and experiment groups" on all six factors; Table 5 prints p = .00 for each factor. No effect size is printed.

"The pretest scores show that there are significant differences between control and experiment groups in terms of the six factors of attitudes towards statistics."
