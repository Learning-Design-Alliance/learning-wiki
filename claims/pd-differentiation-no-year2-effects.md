## Evidence

### Prast 2018

Prast, E. J., Van de Weijer-Bergsma, E., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Van Luit, J. E. H. (2018). Differentiated instruction in primary mathematics: Effects of teacher professional development on student achievement. Learning and Instruction, 54, 22-34. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year 2 latent growth curve models (T3-T5) with the same covariates; adding the intervention did not explain additional slope variance. The multiple-group Year 2 model likewise showed no significant short- or long-term PD effect in any achievement group.

> "In contrast to the Year 1 ﬁndings, participation in the PD programme in Year 2 did not have a signiﬁcant short-term eﬀect on the slope ( β = 0.03, p = .640). Regarding the long-term eﬀect of the intervention, PD in Year 1 had no signiﬁcant eﬀect on the slope of students in Cohort 1 in Year 2 (β = −0.06, p = .665)."
