### Yu 2016

Yu, K. C., Sahami, K., Denn, G., Sahami, V., & Sessions, L. C. (2016). Immersive Planetarium Visualizations for Teaching Solar System Moon Concepts to Undergraduates. Journal of Astronomy & Earth Sciences Education, 3(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1123383

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Aggregated longitudinal gain analysis (Table 2, plotted in Fig. 1) across the three groups shows immediate post-instruction gains for all students, with all groups losing ground between the contemporaneous and delayed post-instruction assessments; no significance tests are reported for these contrasts.

> "All students regardless of which treatment group they were from had positive Cont-Pre gains, which show gains immediately after instruction. However, the Post-Cont differences were all losses"
