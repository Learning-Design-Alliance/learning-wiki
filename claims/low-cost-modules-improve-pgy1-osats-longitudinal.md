# Orthopaedic interns' OSATS Global Rating Scores rose significantly across all five modules from the beginning to the end of the academic year in a within-group pre-test/post-test comparison after year-long at-home access to low-cost skills modules

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among PGY1 residents with home module access, GRS rose statistically significantly across all modules from the beginning to the end of one year in a within-group pre-test/post-test comparison, and all residents had higher GRS at the end. [→ Hagen M. S. 2026](#hagen-m-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Hagen M. S. 2026

Hagen M. S., Dunahoe J., Parker K. M., Kweon C. Y., Kennedy S. A. (2026). Longitudinal Pilot Study on the Use of Low-Cost Modules to Improve Junior Orthopaedic Resident Surgical Skills. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study of eight PGY1 residents tested before and after a year of at-home module access; Wilcoxon signed-rank testing showed "statistically significant improvement in the OSATS GRS across all modules". No effect size printed.

> "Comparing the PGY1 residents at the beginning of the year with end of the year, there was statistically significant improvement in the OSATS GRS across all modules (Figure 3). All residents improved, including lowest and highest performing residents."
