Evidence¶

Khansir 2014¶

Khansir, A. A., & Dashti, J. G. (2014). The Effect of Question-Generation Strategy on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension Development. English Language Teaching, 7(4), 38-44. http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n4p38

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Between-gender comparison of 60 experimental-group students (30 male, 30 female). The t-test was significant (t = -3.796, p = .000), with female post-test mean of 30.4 exceeding the male mean of 24.83.

"In the third hypothesis, t-test revealed that there is significant difference between the males and females' scores in their pre-test and post-test regarding to experimental and control groups in this research."
