Evidence¶

Fosco 2019¶

Fosco, W. D., Hawk, L. W. Jr., Colder, C. R., Meisel, S. N., & Lengua, L. J. (2019). The development of inhibitory control in adolescence and prospective relations with delinquency. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2019.08.008

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null prospective finding from the same growth-model prediction analysis in the 387-family sample; the slope also did not predict middle-adolescent delinquency (b= 0.0001, SE= 0.003, p=.99). No statistically significant association was detected; equivalence was not tested.

"Individual differences in the rate of change (slope) in inhibition across early to middle adolescence was unrelated to delinquent behavior in emerging adulthood (b= 0.006, SE= 0.05, p=.90)"
