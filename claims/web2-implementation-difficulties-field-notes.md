### Eren 2015

Omer Eren. (2015). Vocabulary Learning on Learner-Created Content by Using Web 2.0 Tools. Contemporary Educational Technology, 6(4), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105759

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Researcher field notes taken throughout the twelve-week treatment recorded three difficulties: initial low membership (16 of 23), unwanted mother-tongue commenting that created arguments, and continuous complaints about group members. The membership problem "was solved in a couple of weeks".

> "As it was not compulsory, 16 students out 23 became members of the group and this was not enough to do an activity on Facebook and conduct the backbone of the treatment"
