## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA with Tukey's range test on TOSLS scores at three time points (start of Winter 2023, end of Winter 2023, start of Fall 2023; n = 46 at reassessment); reassessed scores did not differ from baseline (P > 0.05, no effect size printed).

> "Upon reassessment, students' overall TOSLS scores decreased compared to the end of the Winter 2023 semester (i.e., the end of the flipped classroom semester) and did not differ from the baseline TOSLS scores from the start of the Winter 2023 semester (P > 0.05)."
