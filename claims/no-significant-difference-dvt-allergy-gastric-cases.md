## Evidence

### Karakuş Akan 2014

Karakuş Akan, Duran Latif, Yavuz Yücel, Altintop Levent, Çalişkan Fatih. (2014). Computer-based simulation training in emergency medicine designed in the light of malpractice cases. BMC Medical Education, 14, 155. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-155

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-case analyses in the same controlled study found no significant differences on cases 2, 4 and 5 (p = 0.109, 0.247, 0.461). For the DVT case the study reports "54.2% vs. 37.5%, p = 0.247"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Simulation-trained students were more successful compared with traditionally trained students (54.2% vs. 37.5%, p = 0.247) in the performance of an accurate diagnosis and treatment of deep vein thrombosis and its complications."
