## Evidence

### Tseng Liang-Wei 2025

Tseng Liang-Wei, Lu Yi-Chin, Tseng Liang-Chi, Chen Yu-Chun, Chen Hsing-Yu. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 on Taiwanese Traditional Chinese Medicine Licensing Examinations: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/58897

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Human-curated review of AI-generated explanations categorized errors into misinterpretation of the question, misunderstanding of concepts, and incorrect application of principles. The Results section reports "most of the errors (263/476, 55.3%)" were concept misunderstanding; concept errors were more common in LOTS, recall, and SAMRMC questions.
