## Evidence

### Vaz DV 2021

Vaz DV, Ferreira EMR, Palma GB, Atun-Einy O, Kafri M, Ferreira FR. (2021). Testing a new active learning approach to advance motor learning knowledge and self-efficacy in physical therapy undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education 21:62. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02486-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing pre- and post-course PTP-ML scores of the 28 third-semester intervention students in this quasi-experimental study. Part A rose from Mdn 1.46 to 3.96 with printed effect size r = − 0.39; Part B rose from Mdn 1.69 to 4.81 (z = − 2.27, p = 0.02, r = − 0.31).

> "After the ML course, third-semester student scores significantly increased for Part A (Mdn = 1.46 [1.00, 3.75] to Mdn = 3.96 [3.25, 4.58]; z = − 2.81, p = 0.005, r = − 0.39)"
