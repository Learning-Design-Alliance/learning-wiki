### Ochandorena-Acha Mirari 2026

Ochandorena-Acha Mirari, Jolis-Prat Ramon, Escribà-Salvans Anna, Oliveira Vinicius Rosa, Rierola-Fochs Sandra, Parés-Martínez Carles, Terradas-Monllor Marc. (2026). The changes in perceived self-efficacy, academic engagement, and empathy following simulation-based learning among undergraduate physiotherapy students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09389-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified analysis of fourth-year students (n = 120 pre, n = 113 post) using Mann–Whitney U tests. The article reports significant improvements across all measured dimensions with effect sizes described as small but approaching medium magnitude.

> "Finally, among fourth-year students, significant differences were found in the EAPESA Total Score (p < 0.001), UWES-17 Vigor (p < 0.001), UWES-17 Dedication (p = 0.007), UWES-17 Absorption (p < 0.001), UWES-17 Total Score (p < 0.001), JSE Compassionate Care (p < 0.001), and JSE Total Score (p < 0.001), with effect sizes that were small but approaching medium magnitude."
