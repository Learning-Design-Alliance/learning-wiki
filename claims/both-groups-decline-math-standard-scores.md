Descriptive analysis of average WIAT math standard scores at each developmental age cluster, shown in Figure 1. Both groups declined; the ADHD sample moved from the 37th percentile in childhood to the 18th percentile by adulthood.

> "Participants both with and without ADHD declined in average math scores over time, and the ADHD sample mean score was at the 18th percentile of national norms by adulthood (standard score = 86)."
