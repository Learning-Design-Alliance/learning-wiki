The study evaluated whether rapid guessing influenced teacher value-added estimates and reports that "this type of behavior did not appear to affect estimates of teacher performance" under the specifications used. No effect size is printed in the available text.

"However, based on the value-added specifications used in this research, this type of behavior did not appear to affect estimates of teacher performance."
