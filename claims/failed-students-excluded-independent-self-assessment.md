## Evidence

### Knof Harald 2024

Knof Harald, Berndt Markus, Shiozawa Thomas. (2024). Prevalence of Dunning-Kruger effect in first semester medical students: a correlational study of self-assessment and actual academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11515314/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Methodological limitation stated in the Discussion regarding the scatterplot (Fig. 1) of self-assessment deviation versus actual score. The study reports "there are no students who did not pass the exam listed" because failing students "were therefore excluded from the study". This restricts the sample to passing students only. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Looking at Fig. 1, it is noticeable that there are no students who did not pass the exam listed. The respective students immediately received information about their non-passing status and were therefore excluded from the study, as they were unable to make an independent self-assessment."
