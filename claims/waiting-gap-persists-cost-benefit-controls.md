## Evidence

### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear probability models sequentially adding controls for admission probability (track, Baccalaureate grade), value of waiting (pending programs, waitlist rank, offer rank and prestige, prestige gain), and housing-cost-related distance; the study found the gap persisted at −0.05, −0.06, and −0.05 (all P < 0.001).

> "We found that financial aid beneficiaries were 5 percentage points less likely to always wait (SE = 0.002, P < 0.001) even after accounting for differences in academic performance, see Table 3."
