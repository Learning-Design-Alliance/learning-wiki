### Aadithreya Varman 2024

Aadithreya Varman, Varman N V Arulmozhi, Balakumar Dinesh. (2024). Assessing the surgical competency of novice surgeons by using a three-dimensional heads-up display microscope. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/IJO.IJO_2677_23

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Complication analysis across all 400 surgeries found 60 (15%) complications, led by posterior capsular rupture in 28 (7%) and Descemet membrane detachment in 26 (6.5%); "No significant differences were observed in the rate of occurrence of complications between TM and 3D surgeries."

> "Complications were observed in 60 (15%) of 400 patients. Posterior capsular rupture (PCR) was the most common complication found in 28 (7%), followed by Descemet membrane detachment (DMD) in 26 (6.5%). No significant differences were observed in the rate of occurrence of complications between TM and 3D surgeries."
