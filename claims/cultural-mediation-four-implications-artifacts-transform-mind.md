# Cultural mediation has four implications, including that artifacts fundamentally transform mental functioning and the mind is distributed in artifacts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The article enumerates four implications of treating cultural mediation as primary: artifacts transform mental functioning fundamentally; psychological functions are culturally and historically situated with no universal form of mediation; action and context are mutually specifiable; and the mind works via artifacts and cannot be bounded by the head or body. [→ Liu 2010](#liu-2010)
