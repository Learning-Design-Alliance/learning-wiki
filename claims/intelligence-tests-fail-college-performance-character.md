## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Colvin 1921

Colvin, S. S., & Pintner, R. (1921). Intelligence and Its Measurement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/edu

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Colvin's informal observational report from his own student advising work, presented as expert experience rather than a systematic study. He attributes the lack of relation in many instances to character and temperament qualities playing a deciding role.

> "In my work with students at Brown University, I have found scores of instances in which intelligence tests have not only failed to indicate in a positive way college performance, but have also shown results at variance with this performance."
