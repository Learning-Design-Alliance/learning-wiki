### Naghshi Maryam 2024

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the phenomenological interview study, the assessment tools challenges sub-theme emerged from content analysis of 33 interviews; participants stated these design and scoring gaps "have caused the lack of reliability and validity of some assessment tools in different job groups." No effect size is reported.

> "The lack of an expert team for designing assessment tools, the lack of a precise definition of the criteria for determining each competency, and the inconsistency of scoring competencies in different tools have caused the lack of reliability and validity of some assessment tools in different job groups."
