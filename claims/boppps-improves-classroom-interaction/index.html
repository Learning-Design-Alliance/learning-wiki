Evidence¶

Zhu Jing 2025¶

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of two studies (197 participants) with continuous classroom interaction measures, covering both peer-to-peer and student-teacher interactions; pooled effect size SMD = 0.83 (95% CI 0.46–1.21), I² = 0.38 in Table 5.

"Two studies employed continuous variables, indicating a substantial improvement within the BOPPPS group concerning classroom interaction when compared to the control group (SMD 0.83, 95% CI 0.46–1.21; P < 0.001; Table 5)."

Zhu Jing 2025 (2)¶

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of three studies (505 participants) with dichotomous interaction variables; the pooled OR = 4.11 was not statistically significant (P = 0.09), with I² = 0.78. Equivalence was not tested.

"In three studies utilizing dichotomous variables, no statistically significant difference was observed between the two groups, although relevant trends were evident (OR 4.11, 95% CI 0.79–21.35; P = 0.09; Table 5)."
