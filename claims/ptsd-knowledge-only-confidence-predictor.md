`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable ordered logistic regression from the national GP survey. The article reports that "only GPs' PTSD knowledge level was found to predict confidence in diagnosing and managing PTSD", with the demographic variables not predictive.

> "For diagnosing and managing WAD and PTSD, only GPs' PTSD knowledge level was found to predict confidence in diagnosing and managing PTSD, but gender, years of experience, location of practice, and number of GPs in practice were not found to be predictors (Table 3)."
