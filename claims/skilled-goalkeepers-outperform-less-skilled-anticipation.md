## Evidence

### Causer Joe 2017

Causer Joe, Smeeton Nicholas J., Williams A. Mark. (2017). Expertise differences in anticipatory judgements during a temporally and spatially occluded task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171330

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on correct-response accuracy for 24 male goalkeepers (12 skilled professional/semi-professional, 12 less-skilled recreational). The skilled group's mean accuracy was "M = 70, SD = 14%" versus "M = 45, SD = 12%", partial eta squared = 0.98.

> "There was a significant main effect for group, F1, 22 = 290.59, p < 0.001, partial eta squared = 0.98. The skilled group recorded significantly higher accuracy scores (M = 70, SD = 14%) when compared to the less-skilled group (M = 45, SD = 12%)."
