## Evidence

### Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh 2010

Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh. (2010). Surveying Instructors’ Attitudes and Approaches to Teaching Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1063/1.3515227](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515227)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey ratings on a 1-5 scale of preparation in linear algebra, special functions, ordinary and partial differential equations; most faculty rated each category "either average (rating of 3) or below average (rating of 2 or 1)".

> "The data suggest that the mathematical preparation according to most faculty members in each of these categories is either average (rating of 3) or below average (rating of 2 or 1)."

### Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh 2010 (2)

Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh. (2010). Surveying Instructors’ Attitudes and Approaches to Teaching Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1063/1.3515227](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515227)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

On special functions, "2/3rd of the faculty gave a rating of 2", and this held regardless of the size of the school.

> "a majority of faculty members noted that students’ understanding of special functions is below average (2/3rd of the faculty gave a rating of 2)"

### Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh 2010 (3)

Shabnam Siddiqui and Chandralekha Singh. (2010). Surveying Instructors’ Attitudes and Approaches to Teaching Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1063/1.3515227](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515227)
