## Evidence

### Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan 2015

Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan. (2015). Dynamic Cultural Contextualisation of Educational Content in Intelligent Learning Environments using ICON. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 25:249–270. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0033-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A study in which 37 U.W.I. Computer Science and IT students rated CRITS content on a five-point Likert scale at each cultural density level. A single factor ANOVA found "significant differences in student preferences since F>F critical and p <0.00001"; the 'Liked' category held 45.3% of ratings.
