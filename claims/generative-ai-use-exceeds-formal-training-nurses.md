Descriptive characteristics of the 233-nurse online convenience sample (Table 1). The authors interpret this marked difference as suggesting use "may not be adequately supported by formal and institutional training."

> "The majority of participants (n = 215, 92.27%) reported that they had not received training in the use of generative AI tools. However, 65.67% indicated that they used generative AI tools. Regarding the type of AI tools used, the most common was the use of only ChatGPT (n = 111, 50.00%)."
