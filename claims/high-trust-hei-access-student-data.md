# Teaching staff showed high trust in HEIs' provision of access to student progress data, with no significant ideal–predicted expectation gap

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Teaching staff reported high predicted expectations and the smallest ideal–predicted gap for accessing data about students' progress, and the paired t-test was not significant despite a small printed effect size. [→ Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023](#asma-shannan-alzahrani-2023)

## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis with paired t-tests of ideal vs predicted expectations among 65 Saudi teaching staff. Item 4 (access data about students' progress) had the lowest gap and highest ideal rating; the t-test "did not show significant differences" (t = 1.814, p = 0.074, Cohen's d = 0.266).

> "A paired t-test did not show significant differences between the two expectations, with a small effect size for both items (t = 1.814, p = 0.074, Cohen's d = 0.266)."
