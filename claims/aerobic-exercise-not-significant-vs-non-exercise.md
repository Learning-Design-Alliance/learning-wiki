## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Harveson Andrew T. 2019

Harveson Andrew T., Hannon James C., Brusseau Timothy A., Podlog Leslie, Papadopoulos Charilaos, Hall Morgan S., Celeste EvaRose. (2019). Acute Exercise and Academic Achievement in Middle School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16193527

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized crossover study, repeated measures ANOVA found the aerobic-versus-non-exercise math contrast statistically insignificant (p = 0.124). The results section also states "There were no significant differences between aerobic exercise and non-exercise across any of the Stroop Test elements." No equivalence testing was reported.

> "Differences in mean math test performance were statistically insignificant between aerobic exercise and non-exercise (F1,62 = 2.43, p = 0.124, η2 = 0.04), and aerobic exercise and resistance exercise (F1,62 = 0.214, p = 0.645, η2 = 0.003)."
