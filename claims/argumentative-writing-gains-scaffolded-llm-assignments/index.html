Evidence¶

Daniel M. Oppenheimer 2025¶

Daniel M. Oppenheimer, Trent N. Cash, Allison E. Connell Pensky. (2025). You've Got AI Friend in Me: LLMs as Collaborative Learning Partners. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00521-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

Paired-sample t-tests comparing each student's Assignment 1 and Assignment 5 scores graded on 3-point scales by a teaching assistant. Means rose from 2.58 to 2.90 (initial argument) and 2.34 to 2.89 (prompt engineering); the article states "Student improvement was equivalent to approximately a full standard deviation on each of the dimensions." No standardized effect size is printed.

"Students showed significant improvement on all three dimensions (see Fig. 1): Initial Argument (x̄ assignment1 = 2.58, x̄ assignment5 = 2.90, t(109) = 6.22, p <. 001), Prompt Engineering (x̄ assignment1 = 2.34, x̄ assignment5 = 2.89, t(109) = 8.80, p <. 001)"
