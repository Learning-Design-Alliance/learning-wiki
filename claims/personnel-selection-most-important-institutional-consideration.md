This is the authors' expert assertion in the outline's opening points, not a tested result. They argue that good understanding requires education in the needs of young people, and that "the prime requisite is capacity for social service."

> "The selection of the working personnel is the most important single consideration. The influence of a man or woman of good understanding upon youthful delinquents, whenever in contact with them, is not to be overvalued."
