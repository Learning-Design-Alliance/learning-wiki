Lithoxoidou 2017¶

Lithoxoidou, L. S., Georgopoulos, A. D., Dimitriou, A. Th., & Xenitidou, S. Ch. (2017). “Trees have a soul too!” Developing Empathy and Environmental Values in Early Childhood. International Journal of Early Childhood Environmental Education, 5(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158501

Authors' interpretation of their interview findings against Piaget's egocentrism thesis. They argue perceptual role-taking is not a prerequisite for understanding another's internal situation, citing later research showing young children decenter in interpersonally motivated, play-like contexts.
