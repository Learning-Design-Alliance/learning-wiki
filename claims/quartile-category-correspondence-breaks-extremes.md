# One-to-one correspondence between ITBS national quartiles and ISAT performance categories breaks down at the first and fourth quartiles

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Although low ITBS scorers mostly land in Warning or Below Standards and high scorers in Meets or Exceeds, many first-quartile ITBS scorers fall in Below Standards and many fourth-quartile scorers in Meets rather than Exceeds. [→ Easton 2003](#easton-2003)

## Evidence

### Easton 2003

Easton, J. Q., Correa, M., Luppescu, S., Park, H.-S., Ponisciak, S., Rosenkranz, T., & Sporte, S. (2003). How Do They Compare? ITBS and ISAT Reading and Mathematics in the Chicago Public Schools, 1999 to 2002. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications/briefs/onlineresources.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 2002 ISAT performance categories by ITBS national quartile (Figure 5) for grades three, five, and eight in reading and mathematics. The article states "Many first quartile scorers are in the Below Standards rather than Warning category," attributing this to the standards-setting process placing Warning very low and Exceeds very high.

> "Many first quartile scorers are in the Below Standards rather than Warning category; more fourth quartile ITBS scorers are in the Meets Standards category rather than Exceeds Standards."
