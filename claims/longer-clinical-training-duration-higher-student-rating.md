## Evidence

### Seitz T. 2017

Seitz T., Turk B. R., Löffler-Stastka H. (2017). Can we still stop the migration of physicians from Austria? An evaluation of clinical internships by students of the Medical University of Vienna. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-016-1095-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlations in the same 304-student survey showed general assessment "strongly correlated with the duration of training in weeks (r = −0.94, p = 0.001)"; the negative coefficient with a 1–5 grade scale means longer training received better grades. Effect size magnitude as printed is very large.

> "Students’ general assessment of clinical training strongly correlated with the duration of training in weeks (r = −0.94, p = 0.001), significantly improving the longer the training lasted."
