## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Omnibus interaction analysis within the ALSPAC conditional regression models testing whether school-entry year modifies the gestational-age association for each outcome. The article reports only p values for these interaction terms and no effect size.

> "The year of school entry appeared to modify the association between gestational age and the risk of a low KS1 score (pinteraction = 0.036), KS2 score (pinteraction = 0.002), SEN (pinteraction = 0.043), but not KS3 (pinteraction = 0.304) or KS4 (pinteraction = 0.158)."
