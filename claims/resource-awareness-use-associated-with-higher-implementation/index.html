Boal 2025¶

Boal, A., McCullough, S., & Chin, A. (2025). Exploring implementation of trauma-engaged practices in Alaska schools (REL 2025–011). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Northwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/rel/Products/RWR/Publication/108318

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Mixed-effects multilevel regressions on 2022 survey data from 271 schools, nested in districts, found "both awareness and use of the Trauma-Engaged Schools resources were significantly associated with increased implementation." Descriptively, average practices scores rose from 3.29 (strongly disagree awareness) to 4.58 (strongly disagree use) across awareness/use levels.

"In regression models that controlled for school characteristics, both awareness and use of the Trauma-Engaged Schools resources were significantly associated with increased implementation of trauma-engaged practices"
