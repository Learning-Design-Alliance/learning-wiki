# No single measure exists that assesses children's emotional wellbeing in the context of primary-secondary school transitions; existing measure designs are inadequate

## Evidence

### Bagnall 2025

Bagnall, C. L., Jindal-Snape, D., Banwell, E., Panayiotou, M., Mason, C., & Qualter, P. (2025). Emotional Wellbeing in the Context of Primary-Secondary School Transitions: A Concept Analysis Paper. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09990-6

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

International systematic literature review (Bagnall & Jindal-Snape, 2023) of child self-report measures used in transitions research. The review found "no single measure which assesses children's emotional wellbeing in the context of primary-secondary school transitions".

> "It was found that, to date, there is no single measure which assesses children's emotional wellbeing in the context of primary-secondary school transitions."

### Bagnall 2025 (2)

Bagnall, C. L., Jindal-Snape, D., Banwell, E., Panayiotou, M., Mason, C., & Qualter, P. (2025). Emotional Wellbeing in the Context of Primary-Secondary School Transitions: A Concept Analysis Paper. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09990-6

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review classified measure use into three designs: transitions experiences only, emotional wellbeing only, or unrelated pairs of measures, and judged all three unable to assess the contextually specific construct, inhibiting progress in the field.

> "All three of these measure designs are inadequate, as they are unable to holistically assess emotional wellbeing in the context of primary-secondary school transitions."
