Evidence¶

Jarodzka 2020¶

Jarodzka, H., Skuballa, I., & Gruber, H. (2020). Eye-Tracking in Educational Practice: Investigating Visual Perception Underlying Teaching and Learning in the Classroom. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09565-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Haataja, Salonen, Laine, Toivanen, and Hannula's case study, described as the first publication on joint eye-tracking of pupils and teachers within authentic lessons, using multiple mobile eye-tracking glasses, specially developed analysis software, and classroom video coding. The review attributes the finding to the authors.

"The authors found that the joint attention between teachers and their pupils depends on situational differences, as well as on teachers'agency and communion (cf. Gurtman 2009)."
