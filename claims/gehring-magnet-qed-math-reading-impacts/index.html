Evidence¶

Nakamoto 2022¶

Nakamoto, J., Grant, A., Snipes, J., Allender, S., & Figueroa, T. (2022). Clark County School District Magnet Schools Assistance Program (MSAP): Spring 2022 interim evaluation summary report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621271

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental design comparing Gehring magnet students in grades 1-5 with propensity-score-matched comparison students on winter 2020 MAP assessments. The report states "effect sizes ranging from 0.24 to 0.34" for mathematics and "0.24 to 0.39" for reading, all significant after Benjamini-Hochberg correction; science estimates were not significant.

"There were three statistically significant positive impact estimates for mathematics, with effect sizes ranging from 0.24 to 0.34. There were also four statistically significant positive impact estimates for reading, with effect sizes ranging from 0.24 to 0.39."
