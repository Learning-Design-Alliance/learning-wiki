Challenge identified in the I-PREP statewide scan. The combination of multiple requirements such as internship placements and mentor qualifications creates additional burden for some programs, and finding placements with some student populations was cited as a challenge particularly in rural and less diverse areas.

"Although viewed as a benefit for statewide program consistency, some respondents believed increased requirements limited their ability to be flexible to meet local needs and viewed the new policy as a "one-size-fits-all" approach."
