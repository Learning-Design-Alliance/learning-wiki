Nagaoka 2023¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Arneson, A. (2023). The four years fallacy: Four-year vs. six-year bachelor's degree completion rates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-four-years-fallacy

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Disaggregated analysis of 2012–14 CPS immediate enrollees by GPA, ACT score, race/ethnicity, and gender (Figure 5; 25,527 students). The report states Black young men's four-year rate was 13% with six-year rates "around 20 percentage points higher" (31% per the Implications section). No effect size is printed.

"The four-year completion rates for Black young men (13%), Black young women (20%), and Latino young men (23%) were lower than the rates for their peers. The six-year completion rates for these groups were around 20 percentage points higher."
