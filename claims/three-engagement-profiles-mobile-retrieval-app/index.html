Evidence¶

Wong 2026¶

Wong, J., Khalil, M., Suschevskiy, V., Baars, M., de Koning, B., & Paas, F. (2026). Student engagement profiles in a mobile app: Links to self-regulated learning and performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10586-2

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile analysis (tidyLPA in R) of ten standardized log-data engagement indicators from 310 undergraduates' optional mobile app use in a Law course; Model 2 with three profiles "had the lowest value for AIC (−2549)" and other fit criteria. Profiles were labeled disengaged, utilitarian, and active.

"Results of the latent profile analysis on the ten student engagement indicators revealed that Model 2 (i.e., varying variances and covariances fixed to zero) with three profiles had the lowest value for AIC (−2549), AWE (−1906), BIC (−2367), and KIC (−2490)"
