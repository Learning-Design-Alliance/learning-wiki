### Noah D. Finkelstein 2005

Noah D. Finkelstein. (2005). Teaching and learning physics: A model for coordinating physics instruction, outreach, and research. Originally published 2004. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0505091](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0505091)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study from ethnographic field-notes and audiotapes of one student's model change; the article links it to prior findings that a confident but less expert peer may convince a more advanced but less confident student.

> "On the pre-test, the student consistently demonstrates a more expert view.  On the post-test, the student consistently uses a more naive model of current consumption. Analysis suggests that this student learned of the more naive model from class discussion, and in particular from another student."
