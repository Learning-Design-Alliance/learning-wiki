### Sophia E. Economou 2020

Sophia E. Economou, Terry Rudolph, and Edwin Barnes. (2020). Teaching quantum information science to high-school and early undergraduate students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.07874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.07874)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors describe their outreach program as presented in settings including Virginia high schools and the CTech2 two-week camp for approximately 60 rising junior and senior female high-school students, stating it "allows students to perform meaningful hands-on calculations with quantum circuits and algorithms, without requiring knowledge of advanced mathematics."

> "We present a simple, accessible, yet rigorous outreach/educational program focused on quantum information science and technology for high-school and early undergraduate students. This program allows students to perform meaningful hands-on calculations with quantum circuits and algorithms, without requiring knowledge of advanced mathematics."
