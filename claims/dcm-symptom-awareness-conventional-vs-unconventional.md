### Dawson 2025

Dawson, Esther, and Fi Macintosh. 2025. “Evaluating Musculoskeletal Physiotherapist's Self‐Perceived Knowledge and Understanding of the Assessment, Diagnosis and Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy: A Cross‐Sectional Survey.” Musculoskeletal Care: e70157. https://doi.org/10.1002/msc.70157

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey symptom‐identification item (Chart 1a) in which respondents selected potential DCM symptoms from a list; the most recognised were “reduced grip strength, impaired hand dexterity, clumsiness, and poor balance”, with unconventional symptoms least identified.

> "The most recognised symptoms included reduced grip strength, impaired hand dexterity, clumsiness, and poor balance, followed by paraesthesia and numbness in the upper limbs, falls, paraesthesia and numbness in the lower limbs, weakness and stiffness in the upper limbs, and neck pain."
