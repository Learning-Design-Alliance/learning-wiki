Wormley Michelle Elizabeth, Romney Wendy, Veneri Diana, Oberlander Andrea. (2022). Doctoral physical therapy students' increased confidence following exploration of active video gaming systems in a problem-based learning curriculum in the United States: a pre- and post-intervention study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2022.19.7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive pre-post comparison of median (IQR) confidence scores for two consecutive cohorts (cohort 1 n=60; cohort 2 n=55). "Both cohorts showed increased confidence" in the reported medians. The article states cohort 2 data could not be matched, so the Wilcoxon signed-rank test was not completed for cohort 2.

> "Both cohorts showed increased confidence, with median (IQR) scores as follows: cohort 1 total score, pre-intervention: 57.1 (44.3–63.5), post-intervention: 79.1 (73.1–85.4), and cohort 2: pre-intervention: 61.4 (48.0–70.7), post-intervention: 89.3 (80.0–93.2)"
