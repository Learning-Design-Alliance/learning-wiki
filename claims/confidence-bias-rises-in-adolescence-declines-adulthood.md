## Evidence

### Weil L.G. 2013

Weil L.G., Fleming S.M., Dumontheil I., Kilford E.J., Weil R.S., Rees G., Dolan R.J., Blakemore S.-J. (2013). The development of metacognitive ability in adolescence. Consciousness and Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2013.01.004

`q3 · i?`

Univariate ANOVA of Broc in the same sample found a significant main effect of age group (F(1,52) = 10.50, p = .002) with higher Broc in adolescents; "the ratings tended to be biased towards greater confidence over the course of adolescence and declined in adulthood".

> "the interaction effect was driven by a positive correlation between age and Broc in the adolescents (r = .41, p = .028), and a negative correlation in the adults (r = −.49, p = .008)."
