Maike Trautner 2025¶

Maike Trautner, Carola Grunschel, Malte Schwinger. (2025). Motivating Motivation Regulation Research—An Evidence and Gap Map Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10019-1

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic scoping review with a preregistered search across multiple databases and grey-literature sources, synthesizing the motivation regulation evidence base. The review reports that "Approximately three quarters of the studies examined motivation regulation of university students, used cross-sectional study designs, and self-report questionnaires" out of N = 289 included studies.

"Based on a systematic literature search, N = 289 studies were included. Approximately three quarters of the studies examined motivation regulation of university students, used cross-sectional study designs, and self-report questionnaires."

Related Claims¶

  • Most studies provide an explicit definition of motivation regulation, and most definitions describe complex action programs such as strategies — related
  • The evidence map reveals gaps: little is known about motivation regulation in pre- and primary school children, its development across school years, and its contextual characteristics — related
  • School climate measurement relies heavily on student self-report questionnaires, with families and observational methods rarely represented — related
  • The interleaving literature is dominated by laboratory studies of university undergraduates, with no included study using high-school participants — related
  • The only significant cross-method correlation within one SRL component is motivation assessed by questionnaire and closed-ended microanalytic questions — related
  • SE measurement in undergraduate mathematics relies predominantly on log files for online behavioral engagement and self-reports for course participation, with behavioral engagement the most examined dimension — related
