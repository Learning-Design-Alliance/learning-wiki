# A formative assessment script increases the quantity and quality of peer feedback vocational students generate on a peer's draft

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` FAS-supported students generated more comments on the peer draft, were more sensitive in detecting errors and missing information, and generated more suggestions for improvement. [→ Peters 2018](#peters-2018)

## Evidence

### Peters 2018

Peters, O., Körndle, H. & Narciss, S. (2018). Effects of a formative assessment script on how vocational students generate formative feedback to a peer's or their own performance. Eur J Psychol Educ, 33, 117–143. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-017-0344-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-factorial quasi-experimental study in which 75 vocational students gave peer feedback on a fictitious erroneous peer draft with vs. without FAS support. The article reports beneficial FAS effects: students "generated more suggestions for improvement" and detected more errors and missing information.

> "Results yield beneficial effects of the FAS on generating peer feedback. Students, who were supported by the FAS-generated more comments on the peer draft, were more sensitive in detecting errors and missing information in the peer draft and generated more suggestions for improvement."
