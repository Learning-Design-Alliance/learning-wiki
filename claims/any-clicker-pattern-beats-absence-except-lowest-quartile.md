### Dana L. Kirkwood-Watts 2022 (2)

Dana L. Kirkwood-Watts, Emily K. Bremers, Emily A. Robinson, Kathleen R. Brazeal, and Brian A. Couch. (2022). Revisiting Clickers: In-Class Questions Followed by At-Home Reflections Are Associated with Higher Student Performance on Related Exam Questions. Journal of Microbiology and Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1128/jmbe.00038-22

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory quartile analysis of the same comparison; the lowest-quartile students showed no significant exam benefit from any clicker pattern relative to absence, while the top three quartiles showed apparent correct-correct benefits reaching significance for Q2 and Q4.

> "Compared to being absent, the lowest quartile saw no signiﬁcant beneﬁts of either answering correctly on both votes or having any other clicker pattern."
