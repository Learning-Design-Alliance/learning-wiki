## Evidence

### Lang Bingchen 2019

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of four studies with 567 students (288 receiving TBL) pooling questionnaire responses on self-study ability; statistical heterogeneity was absent (I2 = 32%), so a fixed-effects model was employed. The article prints no standardized effect size, only "RR = 1.32 with 95% CI [1.21,1.43]".

> "The results from questionnaires indicated that the introduction of TBL pedagogy also developed more students' self-study ability compared to LBL (the ratio of students thinking the pedagogy method had great influence on developing self-study ability: 93.40% in TBL group vs 70.97% in LBL group, RR = 1.32 with 95% CI [1.21,1.43], p < 0.0001, I2 = 32%)"
