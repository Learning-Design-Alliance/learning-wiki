## Evidence

### Arponen Heidi 2026

Arponen Heidi, Juuri Emma. (2026). Student Preference on Teaching Mode and the Impact of Remote Teaching on Academic Performance in Undergraduate Orthodontics Course, a Follow‐Up Study. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13995

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of the summative examination of all 51 course completers, comparing topics taught remotely versus in-class in an alternating allocation. Scores showed no association with delivery mode: "r s = 0.044, p = 0.795, n = 38". No effect size beyond the near-zero correlation was printed.

> "the examination scores of different topics were not associated with either the lecture delivery mode or the attendance requirement of the lecture (r s = 0.044, p = 0.795, n = 38 and r s = 0.061, p = 0.718, n = 38 respectively)"
