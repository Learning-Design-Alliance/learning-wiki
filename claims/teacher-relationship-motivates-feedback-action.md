`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended question Q2 on motivations (n = 471, 73.48% answered). The article reports that "social-affective aspects (n = 11, 11.96%) emerged as prominent motivators", with students citing trusting, supportive teacher-student relationships, e.g., detailed video feedback.

> "Additionally, in this class, social-affective aspects (n = 11, 11.96%) emerged as prominent motivators for taking action on feedback compared to other classes."
