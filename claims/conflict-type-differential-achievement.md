## Evidence

### Premo 2022

Premo, J., Wyatt, B. N., Horn, M., & Wilson-Ashworth, H. (2022). Which Group Dynamics Matter: Social Predictors of Student Achievement in Team-Based Undergraduate Science Classrooms. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-06-0164

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In biology, task conflict (B = 0.39) positively predicted achievement while relationship conflict (B = -0.39) negatively predicted it. In chemistry, "group process conflict predicted positive increases in one's grade, which was a form of conflict that was not predictive in biology."

> "greater presence of task conflict in their group predicted increased achievement, while more relationship conflict predicted decreased achievement"
