Evidence¶

Gehlbach 2018¶

Gehlbach, H., & Vriesema, C. C. (2018). Meta‐bias: A practical theory of motivated thinking. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9454-6

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Gilovich et al. (2000), that college student participants wearing embarrassing or flattering t-shirts, and students in group discussions, overestimated how much others noticed them relative to others' actual noticing. The article narrates this prior work; its design details beyond the quote are not described here.

"Through five studies, Gilovich et al. (2000) established that the bias generalizes from potentially embarrassing to potentially flattering clothing, as well as extending from appearance (e.g., t-shirts) to behaviors (e.g., verbal contributions to a discussion)."
