### Paterna 2024

Paterna, Adrian; Alcaraz-Ibáñez, Manuel; Aguilar-Parra, Jose M.; Salavera, Carlos; Demetrovics, Zsolt; Griffiths, Mark D. (2024). Problematic smartphone use and academic achievement: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Behavioral Addictions. https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2024.00014

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Univariate meta-regressions (Table 2) tested sex, region, PSU assessment, achievement indicator, publication status, age, percentage of females, year of publication and quality; none reached p < 0.05 (e.g., sex F(2, 30) = 0.569, p = 0.572). No effect sizes are printed for these null results.

> "the relationship PSU and academic achievement was not found to differ across sex, nor to depend on variables such as age, year of publication, study quality, academic achievement indicator, PSU assessment or geographical region."
