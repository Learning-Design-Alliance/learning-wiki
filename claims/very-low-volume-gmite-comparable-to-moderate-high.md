## Evidence

### Ishizuka K 2026

Ishizuka K, Nishizaki Y, Kataoka K, Shimizu T, Nojima M, Yamamoto Y, Shikino K, Fukui S, Nagasaki K, Kobayashi H, Ohta M, Tokuda Y. (2026). Impact of inpatient volume on residents' In-training examination scores and burnout in Japanese community hospitals: a nationwide cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08664-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same multilevel multivariable linear regression of 4,867 residents at 564 hospitals; raw means were 45.8 ± 7.3 (Very Low) vs 44.9 ± 6.7 (Moderate) and 46.2 ± 6.9 (High). No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "the GM-ITE® scores of the Very Low-Volume group did not differ significantly from those of the Moderate-Volume (coefficient: 1.637; 95% CI: -0.070–3.344, p = 0.060) and High-Volume (coefficient: 1.638; 95% CI: -0.206–3.482, p = 0.082) groups"
