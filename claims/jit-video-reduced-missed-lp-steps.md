## Evidence

### McGuire SS 2024

McGuire SS, Finch AS, Thomas JM, Lazaro O, Hevesi SA, Mullan AF, Homme JL. (2024). Improving emergency medicine resident pediatric lumbar puncture procedural performance through a brief just-in-time video intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05654-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experimental pre/post assessment of 9 interns scored on the 26 major steps of the published LP Performance Scoring Checklist. The article reports "median 1 (1–2) major step missed at time of post-intervention assessment (p = 0.015)".

> "A median of 5 (4–7) major steps were missed by each intern at baseline, compared to median 1 (1–2) major step missed at time of post-intervention assessment (p = 0.015)."
