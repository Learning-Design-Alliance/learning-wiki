## Subclaims
`q2 i?` None of the two-way or three-way interactions among depressive symptoms, grit, and social desirability predicted GPA, including the depressive symptoms x grit interaction. [→ Kilgore Jenna 2023](#kilgore-jenna-2023)

## Evidence

### Kilgore Jenna 2023

Kilgore Jenna, Collins Amanda C., Miller Julie Anne M., Winer E. Samuel. (2023). Grit, depression, social desirability, and GPA. PLoS One 18(7): e0288270. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288270

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional moderated-moderation analysis in 520 undergraduates. The article reports "none of the interactions were significant", including depressive symptoms x grit ("b = .03 ... p = .562"). No standardized effect sizes were printed.

> "Moreover, none of the interactions were significant (depressive symptoms x grit: b = .03, 95% CI [-.062, .113], t = .58, p = .562; depressive symptoms x social desirability: b = .01, 95% CI [-.090, .109], t = .18, p = .855;"
