## Evidence

### Su 2013

Su, C.-H., & Cheng, C.-H. (2013). 3D game-based learning system for improving learning achievement in software engineering curriculum. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1015378

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statistical analysis of ARCS questionnaire and test data from the quasi-experiment (analyzed with SPSS 17). Table 2 reports H3 with F=2.153 and Sig. p<0.003, rejecting the null that motivation does not affect achievement.

> "The results show that learning motivations of students have significant impact on learning achievement, and learning achievements of students with game-based learning are better than those who use traditional face-to-face teaching."
