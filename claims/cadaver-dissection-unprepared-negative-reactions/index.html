Evidence¶

Anning Naomi 2024¶

Anning Naomi, Tehan Peta. (2024). Simulation in podiatry teaching and learning: A scoping review. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.70020

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cross-sectional survey (Weir and Thomas, as charted in Table 1) of first-year podiatry students at the University of Edinburgh reacting to their initial experiences in the human cadaver dissection laboratory. The review reports unpreparedness and significant negative reactions; no effect size is printed.

"Students felt unprepared for cadaver dissection, experiencing significant negative reactions, and suggested the need for better preparatory and postexperience support"
