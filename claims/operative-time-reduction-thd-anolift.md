## Evidence

### Galli Andrea Cesare 2026

Galli Andrea Cesare, Dionigi Gianlorenzo, Lauricella Sara, Agradi Sergio, Sassun Richard, Leone Angelo Emanuele, Cirocchi Roberto, Brucchi Francesco. (2026). Defining proficiency in THD-Anolift: a CUSUM analysis of the learning curve in 51 consecutive cases. International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-026-05151-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort analysis of operative time as a continuous measure of technical efficiency in 51 cases. The article reports "a reduction of 6.7 min (24.7%; Mann–Whitney p < 0.001)"; segmented regression showed a Phase 1 slope of −0.58 min/case (p = 0.009) flattening to −0.23 min/case in Phase 2 (p = 0.098).

> "At the primary cutoff of case 23, mean operative time was 27.1 ± 7.4 min in Phase 1 versus 20.4 ± 6.0 min in Phase 2, a reduction of 6.7 min (24.7%; Mann–Whitney p < 0.001)."
