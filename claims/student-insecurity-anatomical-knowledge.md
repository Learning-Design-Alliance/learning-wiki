## Evidence

### Bergman EM 2013

Bergman EM, de Bruin ABH, Herrler A, Verheijen IWH, Scherpbier AJJA, van der Vleuten CPM. (2013). Students' perceptions of anatomy across the undergraduate problem-based learning medical curriculum: a phenomenographical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-152

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group finding: students reported mastering broad outlines but not important details. Juniors feared losing face with physicians, fellow students and patients; seniors, with clinical experience, could contextualise the uncertainty. Whether students should master everything remained an unresolved debate.

> "The vast majority of junior and senior students alike believed that their anatomical knowledge was deficient. They had mastered the broad outlines, but not all the important details. As a result, junior students anticipated problems in the clerkships"
