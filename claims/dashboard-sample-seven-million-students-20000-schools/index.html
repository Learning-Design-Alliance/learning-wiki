Descriptive documentation of the dashboard's data source in the guide's overview section. The guide states the sample covers "over seven million students in 20,000 schools" and calls it large and representative, while noting it does not include every student. No effect sizes apply; this is a descriptive scale statement.

"It is based on data from over seven million students in 20,000 schools—about one in four US public schools. So while the dashboard does not include every student, this large and representative sample offers a comprehensive snapshot of national patterns."

  • The dashboard reports median achievement percentiles normed to a nationally representative pre-COVID sample and projected proficiency against state cut scores — related
