Qualitative interview finding from the sense-of-community theme. Participants described near-peers as knowing "what it's like to struggle with the content", supporting relatability and psychological comfort in student-led settings.

> "I feel like the environment was a bit more open in order to ask questions, not feel like pressured by having someone really like (…) senior, you know, kind of like maybe judging you about the questions"
