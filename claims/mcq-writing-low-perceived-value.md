## Evidence

### Grainger R 2018

Grainger R, Dai W, Osborne E, Kenwright D. (2018). Medical students create multiple-choice questions for learning in pathology education: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1312-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course paper-based survey of 62 consenting students (67% response rate) using the Survey of Student-Generated MCQs (Cronbach's α = 0.971) on a seven-point Likert scale. The authors report "Only 24% (15/62) of students agreed" on perceived educational value; 81% (50/62) were not satisfied with the process.

> "Only 24% (15/62) of students agreed (combined Likert scale 5–7) they perceived high educational value of the MCQ writing process, and 22% (14/62) of students agreed that MCQ writing improved their learning experience."
