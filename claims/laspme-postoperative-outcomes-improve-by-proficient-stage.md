`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-phase comparison of perioperative outcomes (Table 3) in the retrospective 182-patient cohort, using Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise tests reported in the Results section.

> "the median postoperative hospital stays decreased from the preliminary to the proficiency stage [15 (13 −31) d vs 13 (11 −17) d, P = 0.006], and the incidence of anastomotic fistula also decreased significantly (19.8% vs 5.2%, P = 0.017)"
