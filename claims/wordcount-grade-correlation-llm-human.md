# Word count correlates with suggested final grade, with the ordering Gemini Pro > humans > ChatGPT > Gemini among statistically significant correlations (human Spearman's rho = 0.603)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Brevik A 2026

Brevik A, Jerpseth H and Lafontan SR (2026). Large language models as grading assistants in public health education: a method-comparison study of essay-style exam assessment. Front. Educ. 11:1904452. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1904452

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis between submission word count and final grade (Table 4). The discussion reports the printed effect size "Spearman's rho = 0.603" for humans (p < 0.001), with Gemini Pro at 0.642, ChatGPT at 0.501, and Gemini at 0.495; Kimi's 0.311 was not significant (p = 0.083).

> "Whether the slightly higher than expected human grade and word count correlation (Spearman's rho = 0.603) indicates that this metric can serve as a benchmark metric in attempts at LLM-human grading alignment remains to be confirmed in larger studies."
