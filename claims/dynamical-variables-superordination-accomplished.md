## Evidence

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Predicative knowledge analysis across the six tasks. The authors report students did not emphasize the time-varying aspect in the first task but half did in the third, inferring the feature was used implicitly as a Concept-in-action.

> "The conceptual superordination has been accomplished, because the students may have assimilated the main feature of the concept of Dynamical Variables, strictly speaking, the one of being time-varying physical quantities (a.1.V.)."

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017 (2)

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the second task, where only one shared attribute was found; the authors give x and px, or sx and sy, measurements as the students' examples of sequential measurement destroying prior information.

> "Three students related the Incompatibility among Dynamical Variables to destruction of prior information obtained of one of them in a process of sequential measurement (b.1.VI.)"
