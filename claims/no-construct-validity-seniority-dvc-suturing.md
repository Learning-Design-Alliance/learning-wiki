Construct validity test comparing three seniority groups on the RARP suturing task. The article reports "no significant difference" on all three tools; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "There was no significant difference in M-GEARS (p = 0.483), OSATS (p = 0.207) and OCHRA (p = 0.691) scores comparing seniority groups using the Kruskall Wallis ANOVA test. Pairwise comparison showed no significant differences between groups (p = 0.723–0.999)."
