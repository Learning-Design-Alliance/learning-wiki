`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Figure 3 line graph averages helpfulness ratings for each student and exam through each of the six semesters; for the most part "a study method that starts oﬀ as helpful remains that way", with review questions the only exception.

### Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters 2020 (2)

Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters. (2020). Determination of the helpfulness of physics exam study methods. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors note many more questions were added to the review questions each year with improvements made, which they offer as the reason this method alone changed in perceived helpfulness across semesters.

> "Because review questions shifted throughout the semesters, as seen in Figure 3, it cannot be designated as always helpful or unhelpful."
