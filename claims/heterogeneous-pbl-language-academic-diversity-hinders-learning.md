## Evidence

### Singaram Veena S. 2010

Singaram Veena S., van der Vleuten Cees P. M., Stevens Fred, Dolmans Diana H. J. M. (2010). “For most of us Africans, we don’t just speak”: a qualitative investigation into collaborative heterogeneous PBL group learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9262-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Student focus group quote: when the group is not "on the same level," some students "do get left behind" due to language, understanding, or differing Matric school backgrounds.

> "if you are all not on the same level, you find that some people do get left behind. Whether its language or they don’t understand or maybe you are not perhaps from the same Matric school and some people live in the rural areas, there are going to be those people who are going to be left behind"

### Singaram Veena S. 2010 (2)

Singaram Veena S., van der Vleuten Cees P. M., Stevens Fred, Dolmans Diana H. J. M. (2010). “For most of us Africans, we don’t just speak”: a qualitative investigation into collaborative heterogeneous PBL group learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9262-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Facilitator focus group (11 facilitators, second round cancelled): stronger students' learning is "hindered due to the lack of academic stimulation and motivation"; one facilitator described a student "streets ahead" of his group who was frustrated.

> "Facilitators also found that the learning of stronger students is hindered due to the lack of academic stimulation and motivation."
