# Robustness checks with fixed-effect and first-difference models reproduce the three-way interaction; continuous-time growth curve modelling does not

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fixed-effect panel regression reproduced the gender × time × condition interactions more clearly than the main analysis (self-reported: χ²(2, N = 23,329) = 2.78, p = .025; utility: χ²(2, N = 23,329) = 3.65, p = .006). [→ Rudmann 2023](#rudmann-2023)
`q3 i?` Continuous-time growth curve modelling reproduced Hypotheses 1 and 2 but not the three-way interactions, which the authors attribute to non-linear change over time. [→ Rudmann 2023 (2)](#rudmann-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Rudmann 2023

Rudmann, O., Batruch, A., Visintin, E. P., Sommet, N., Bressoux, P., Darnon, C., ... Butera, F. (2023). Cooperative Learning Reduces the Gender Gap in Perceived Social Competences. A Large-Scale Nationwide Longitudinal Experiment. Journal of Educational Psychology (accepted for publication). https://osf.io/qarxk/?view_only=e772332576a5421cbf61b3092e567ac9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robustness check using fixed-effect panel regression modelling, described by the article as often regarded as the gold standard for longitudinal data; it reproduced Hypotheses 2 and 3 for both outcomes.

> "the gender × time × condition interactions were even clearer than in the main analysis for both the self-reported social competences, χ² (2, N = 23,329) = 2.78, p = .025, and the utility value of social competences, χ² (2, N = 23,329) = 3.65, p = .006."

### Rudmann 2023 (2)

Rudmann, O., Batruch, A., Visintin, E. P., Sommet, N., Bressoux, P., Darnon, C., ... Butera, F. (2023). Cooperative Learning Reduces the Gender Gap in Perceived Social Competences. A Large-Scale Nationwide Longitudinal Experiment. Journal of Educational Psychology (accepted for publication). https://osf.io/qarxk/?view_only=e772332576a5421cbf61b3092e567ac9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robustness check treating time as continuous in a standard growth curve approach; it failed to reproduce the three-way interactions, which the article attributes to the non-linear effects of time illustrated in Figures 2 and 3.

> "we reproduced the effects for Hypothesis 1 (i.e., the main effects of gender) and Hypothesis 2 (i.e., the gender × time interactions), but not the effects pertaining to Hypothesis 3 (i.e., the gender × time × condition interactions)."
