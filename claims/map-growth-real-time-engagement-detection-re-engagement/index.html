q2 · i? · design · r2

The fairness and administration chapters describe test engagement functionality, citing evidence that engaged students achieve better scores (Wise & DeMars, 2005). The stated purpose is to help students give their best effort so scores more accurately reflect achievement. No effect size is printed.

"the system can detect a lack of test-taking engagement in real time. It will attempt to re-engage unmotivated students through messages and proctor intervention."
