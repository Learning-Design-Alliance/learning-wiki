## Evidence

### McGlonn-Nelson 2005

McGlonn-Nelson, K. (2005). Looking Outward: Exploring the Intersections of Sociocultural Theory and Gifted Education. The Journal of Secondary Gifted Education, 17(1), 48-55. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ746045 [doi:10.4219/jsge-2005-391](https://doi.org/10.4219/jsge-2005-391)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Literature review section reporting, citing Johnsen (1997) and Kanevsky (1995), that widely used IQ-style identification tests "none of them can fully determine a students learning potential." The review also notes these tests typically lack teacher assistance and peer feedback.

> "Most of these tests are considered IQ tests and none of them can fully determine a students learning potential (Johnsen, 1997; Kanevsky, 1995)"
