### Li Lan 2026

Li Lan, Yeonhee Seo, Fan Qianqian. (2026). Enhancing psychological well-being through embodied bilingual teaching: the mediating role of basic psychological needs and learning motivation. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04185-2

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative phase: thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews and lesson plans from 16 bilingual Tai Chi instructors, coded via open and axial coding. The article reports the 45% figure and the comprehension contrast without a printed statistic.

> "45% of the movements are expressed using culturally metaphorical English, and their level of comprehension is significantly higher than that of direct translations of movement names."
