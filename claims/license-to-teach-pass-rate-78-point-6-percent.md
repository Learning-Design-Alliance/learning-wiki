Prospective evaluation of a single-hospital certification intervention launched in 2021 with data collected in 2025. The Results section reports the overall pass rate of "78.6%" and component pass rates for classroom teaching, skill training, and teaching ward rounds as descriptive counts and percentages; no inferential test is reported.

> "A total of 193 candidates participated in the certification assessment, with 152 successfully obtaining certification, resulting in an overall pass rate of 78.6%. The pass rates for individual components were: Classroom Teaching 93.3%, Skill Training 86.5%, and Teaching Ward Rounds 83.9%."
