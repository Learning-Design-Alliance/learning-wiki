## Evidence

### Pierre Dillenbourg 2016

Pierre Dillenbourg. (2016). The Evolution of Research on Digital Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0106-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Dickson and Vereen (1983), that when researchers had to put several children per computer, learning gains should have been lower, but the opposite was observed. The review provides no further detail about the original study's design.

> "The opposite has been observed (Dickson and Vereen 1983): the lower finesse of adaptation was compensated by the higher richness of interactions among learners."
