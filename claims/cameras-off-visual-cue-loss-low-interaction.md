## Evidence

### Carlos González 2023

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results-section interview findings: teachers reported that "most students attended online classes with their cameras off," making it hard to judge attention and understanding. Teachers attributed this variously to motivation and shame and to lower socioeconomic students' connectivity and space constraints.

> "Teachers noted that they perceived that most students attended online classes with their cameras off. This situation deprived teachers of the visual cues that they usually employed to familiarize themselves with the classroom climate and make adjustments when necessary."
