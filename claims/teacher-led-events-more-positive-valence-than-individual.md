## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Teacher-led events were associated with more positive post-event emotional valence than individual events (within-student B = 0.49, 95% CI [0.03, 0.95]). [→ Hartikainen 2026](#hartikainen-2026)
`q3 i?` The teacher-led advantage was moderated by teaching implementation: the within-student teacher-led × implementation interaction was negative (−1.31, 95% CI [−2.23, −0.38]), indicating the advantage was particularly pronounced in the lecture-based group. [→ Hartikainen 2026 (2)](#hartikainen-2026-2)
