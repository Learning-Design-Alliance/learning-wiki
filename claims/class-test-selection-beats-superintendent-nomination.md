Pintner, R. (1917). The Mentality of the Dependent Child, Together with a Plan for a Mental Survey of an Institution. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075825

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of the superintendent's pre-testing nomination list of 23 suspected backward children with the lowest 23 as ranked by class-test I. Q.s, both distributed by the combined method of diagnosis. In the F.M.—B. group 46% versus 64% were detected.

> "Comparing the two methods of selection we note that by the superintendent's selection only 60% of the feeble-minded would have been detected, whereas by the class tests every one would have been examined."
