## Evidence

### Will Yeadon 2024

Will Yeadon, Elise Agra, Oto-obong Inyang, Paul Mackay, Arin Mizouri. (2024). Evaluating AI and Human Authorship Quality in Academic Writing through Physics Essays. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Appendix B reports an ANOVA and Levene's test on marks from the five markers of the 60 submissions, plus ICC1/ICC2 reliability analysis. Table B1 gives ICC1 = -0.053 and ICC2 = 0.035; the article concludes "the markers were not consistent in their evaluations".

> "The ANOV A revealed a highly significant p-value of 5 .106 × 10−29, indicating strong evidence against the null hypothesis that all group means are equal, suggesting that at least one marker’s scores significantly differ from the others."
