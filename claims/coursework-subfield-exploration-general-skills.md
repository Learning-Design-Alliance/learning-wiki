## Evidence

### Kevin Coldren 2026

Kevin Coldren, Nkodia Ngondala, Audrey Claar, Mike Verostek, and Diana Sachmpazidi. (2026). Student Perspectives on Traditional Pedagogy Used in Graduate Physics Coursework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview thematic analysis: Riley described coursework as the easiest way of exploring subfields, echoed by Andrew and James. Andrew noted general outcomes like learning to code. Michael reported "a couple areas where I'm missing some foundational information" that was "way harder" to fill in without guidance.

> "Exposing people to different kinds of physics, the easiest way of exploring [subfields] is taking a bunch of courses and seeing what things exist and what they're applied to."
