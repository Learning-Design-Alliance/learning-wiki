## Evidence

### Betül Akyol & Songül Pektaş 2018

Betül Akyol & Songül Pektaş. (2018). The Effects of Gymnastics Training Combined With Music in Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder and Down Syndrome. International Education Studies, 11(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n11p46

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group independent t test comparison of post-test scores for the 30 children (Tables 2 and Results text). The ASD group showed "a statistically significant difference" on all four measures, with higher functional reach, coordination and flexibility scores.

> "At the end of gymnastics training; in group comparison there was a statistically significant difference in the values flamingo (ASD: 6.03±2.05, DS: 8.14±1.03), functional reach test (ASD: 23.03±1.03 DS: 16.11±2.36), Wall Catch coordination test (ASD: 13.03±1.18, DS: 8.06±2.12), Sit and reach test (ASD: 19.11±1.14, DS: 17.05±1.16) (p<0.05)"
