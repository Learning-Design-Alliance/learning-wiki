## Evidence

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 287 female students at the College of Health and Rehabilitation Sciences, PNU, using the PRCA-24. The article reports "28.2% were categorized as having a high level of communication apprehension"; no effect size is printed.

> "In assessing the overall PRCA in the sampled students, 28.2% were categorized as having a high level of communication apprehension, and only 9.8% had a low level of communication apprehension (Table 5)."

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022 (2)

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of PRCA-24 subscales in the same survey; subscale means were group discussion 18.6, meetings 18.3, interpersonal speaking 19.3, public speaking 16.7. The article reports "the score for interpersonal speaking indicates a high level of apprehension".

> "the results of the two dimensions, including group discussions and meetings, showed some degree of apprehension. However, the score for interpersonal speaking indicates a high level of apprehension, whereas public speaking shows a low level of apprehension"
