### Granić 2009

Granić, A., Mifsud, C., & Ćukušić, M. (2009). Design, implementation and validation of a Europe-wide pedagogical framework for e-Learning. Computers & Education, 53, 1052–1081. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.05.018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation-phase observation across the network of 14 European schools comparing teachers across the two validation phases. The article reports that second-phase teachers "became more confident in their application of the framework principles and evaluated more positively the outcomes". No effect size is printed.

> "With increased experience of the system the teachers' implementation of the pedagogical framework developed into increased mastery in the school context. Teachers from the second validation phase became more confident in their application of the framework principles and evaluated more positively the outcomes."
