## Evidence

### Benjamin 2010

Benjamin, Aaron S., & Tullis, Jonathan. (2010). What makes distributed practice effective? http://www.psych.uiuc.edu/~asbenjam/SuperadditivityReferences.htm. [doi:10.1016/j.cogpsych.2010.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2010.05.004)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this to Cepeda et al. (2008): a very large Internet-based study in which retention interval was controlled independently of lag, with cued recall testing. It showed clear nonmonotonic lag functions and replicated the retention-interval-by-lag interaction.

> "They also replicated the important findings of prior researchers (Glenberg, 1976; Peterson, Hillner, & Saltzman, 1962) that the optimal interval between study events for retention increased with the interval prior to the test."
