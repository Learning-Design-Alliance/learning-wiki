Evidence¶

Ira Nichols-Barrer 2022¶

Ira Nichols-Barrer, Maria Bartlett, Thomas Coen, Philip Gleason. (2022). The Impacts on College Enrollment of a Charter Network Serving Disadvantaged Students: Evidence from KIPP Middle School Lotteries. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness (online ahead of print). https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impacts-on-college-enrollment-of-a-charter-network-serving-disadvantaged-students-evidence doi:10.1080/19345747.2022.2049406

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The study's lottery-based instrumental analysis yields two estimates: 6.9% for an admission offer and 12.9% for actual enrollment at a KIPP school. The text provided prints only these percentages without test statistics.

"The magnitude of the estimated impact of a middle school admission offer (6.9%) and enrollment at a KIPP school (12.9%) is substantial relative to nationwide disparities in college enrollment across racial groups."
