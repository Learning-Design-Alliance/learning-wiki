## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Compagnoni 2019

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANCOVA with 147 kindergarteners controlling gender, age, and CBSR; children with a mastery orientation scored higher on the HTKS (M = 43.68) than children with a performance orientation (M = 38.74), a "medium effect size" with partial η² = .059.

> "The analysis of variance for EF showed a significant main effect of goal orientation on EF with a medium effect size (F(1, 132) = 8.21 , p = .005, partial η 2 = .059, f = .25)"

### Compagnoni 2019 (2)

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same ANCOVA found the trait beliefs × goal orientation interaction on EF not significant, so the goal orientation–EF relation "appeared to be independent of trait beliefs".

> "The interaction term of trait beliefs and goal orientations on EF was not significant (F(1,132) = 0.48, p = .827, partial η2 = .000)."
