## Evidence

### Belhan Çelik 2025

Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA with pre-test scores as covariates in the randomized controlled trial (study n=17, control n=17). The study group's adjusted post-test means were higher for LOTCA Total (102.48 vs 86.98) and Visual Perception (13.85 vs 11.40), showing "statistically significant differences in the post-intervention study group adjusted mean".

> "LOTCA total and LOTCA VP were found to be statistically significant differences in the post-intervention study group adjusted mean compared to the control group (respectively; (F(1.31) = 28.211, p <.00; F(1.31) = 96.670, p <.001)."
