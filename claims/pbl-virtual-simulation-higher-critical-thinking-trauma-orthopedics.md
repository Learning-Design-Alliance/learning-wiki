## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2026

Xue Gang, Zhang Xin, Ji Shengyi, Qi Jin. (2026). Integrating problem-based learning with virtual simulation in trauma orthopedic education: effects on learning outcomes and student satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09558-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same assessor-blinded RCT of 50 clinical medical students. Critical thinking was measured with an anonymous questionnaire of seven dimensions rated on a 6-point Likert scale (total range 70 to 420). The experimental group's total critical thinking score was significantly higher, with a "mean difference = 43.84" and "p < 0.05". The article reports Cohen's d = 2.76 for this outcome in the Results section.

> "The experimental group also showed higher total critical thinking scores (314.12 ± 10.68 vs. 270.28 ± 19.76,mean difference = 43.84,95% CI: 34.70 − 52.98, p < 0.05)"
