### Allen Nie 2024

Allen Nie, Yash Chandak, Miroslav Suzara, Ali Malik, Juliette Woodrow, Matt Peng, Mehran Sahami, Emma Brunskill, and Chris Piech. (2024). The GPT Surprise: Offering Large Language Model Chat in a Massive Coding Class Reduced Engagement but Increased Adopters' Exam Performances. [doi:10.31219/osf.io/qy8zd](https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/qy8zd)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analysis of 180 low-HDI students active after week 1 (111 experiment, 69 control) in the RCT. Exam participation was 42.3% versus 27.5%, "Cohen’s d=0.31", but the raw t-test (P=.045) was not significant after adjustment (∆=14.8pp, P=.680).

> "the exam participation in the experiment group is notably higher at 42.3% compared to participation in the control group, at 27.5% (see Figure 4(a)). The effect size is small to medium (Cohen’s d=0.31), which is notable. However, our sample size is small"
