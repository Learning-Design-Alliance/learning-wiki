# After treatment, demonstration-method students significantly outperform conventional-approach students in financial accounting achievement, with a large effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ANCOVA with pre-test as covariate found a significant difference in post-test financial accounting achievement favouring the demonstration method group over the conventional approach group, with partial eta squared = .840. [→ Inuwa 2018](#inuwa-2018)

## Evidence

### Inuwa 2018

Inuwa, U., Abdullah, Z., & Hassan, H. (2018). A Mixed-Method Study of the Effect of the Demonstration Method on Students' Achievement in Financial Accounting. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 577-592. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191665

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test FAAT scores in the 4-week pre-test-post-test-control group experiment (60 demonstration, 60 control), pre-test as covariate. The article reports F (1,117) = 612.369, p =.000, and a large effect size, partial eta squared = .840 (Cohen, 1988).

> "the result of ANCOVA documented in Table 3 shows the significant difference between the financial accounting achievement of students who were exposed to the demonstration method and that of those who were exposed to the conventional approach (F (1,117) = 612.369, p =.000, partial eta squared =.840)"
