## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Thirteen students (33% of the intervention group) recorded a favorable opinion of taking part in the citizen science project, and the majority of respondents (n=20) cited being in nature as what they enjoyed most. `q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-ended questionnaire given to the intervention group (n=39) after the intervention; only 25% (n=10) answered all six questions and 28% (n=11) answered none. A member check was conducted on the qualitative interpretation.

> "Thirteen students (33% of the intervention group participants) recorded a favorable opinion with regards to taking part in the citizen science project."
