## Evidence

### Kutbay 2020

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test in the quasi-experiment comparing spoken text with signaled written text under abstract animation. No significant difference: "t (178) = .47, p = .967"; the Sp-C versus Sg-C test was likewise not significant (p = .385).

> "there was not a significant difference between the mean achievement scores of the Sp-A group and of the Sg-A group; t (178) = .47, p = .967"

### Kutbay 2020 (2)

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing signaled written plus spoken text versus written plus spoken text with abstract animation. No significant difference: "t (158) = .76, p = .441"; the concrete-representation counterpart was also not significant (p = .911).

> "there was not a significant difference between the mean achievement scores of Sg+Sp-A group and of the W+Sp-A group; t (158) = .76, p = .441"
