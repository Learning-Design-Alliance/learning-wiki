### Newmann 1998

Newmann, F. M., Lopez, G., & Bryk, A. S. (1998). The Quality of Intellectual Work in Chicago Schools: A Baseline Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/quality-intellectual-work-chicago-schools-baseline-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sample-representativeness check in the Research Design and Methods sidebar comparing the 12 selected Annenberg schools with Chicago schools as a whole on ITBS performance. The sample also excluded integrated and predominantly Hispanic schools.

> "While 32 percent of Chicago Public Schools students are at or above national norms on the ITBS in math, only 22 percent of the students in the Annenberg sample reach this level. Reading is similar, with 30 percent of the students in the system scoring at or above the ITBS national norms compared with 21 percent of the students in the Annenberg sample schools."
