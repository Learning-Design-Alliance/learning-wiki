### Gorard et al. 2014

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2014). Response to Intervention: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581264

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Process evaluation of the trial, based on field visits, observations and interviews in five schools. The report found the end-of-Year 6 timing made the intervention necessarily brief and in competition with other end-of-school activities, with some schools delivering very few sessions compared to prior studies involving 20 or more hours of tuition.

> "Some schools were only able to implement a small number of sessions, which made it impossible to implement, monitor and adjust the intervention as intended."
