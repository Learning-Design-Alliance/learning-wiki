## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In primary school, hyperactivity–impulsivity does not predict overall GPA but does predict mathematics, language, and social sciences; in secondary school it appears in all models except visual arts and physical education. [→ Flores Jerome 2022](#flores-jerome-2022)

## Evidence

### Flores Jerome 2022

Flores Jerome, Caqueo-Urízar Alejandra, López Verónica, Acevedo Daniel. (2022). Symptomatology of attention deficit, hyperactivity and defiant behavior as predictors of academic achievement. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-022-03714-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression analysis of the primary school sample (n = 1754). The article reports hyperactivity–impulsivity was significant in three of seven primary models (e.g., β = .079 in mathematics, p = .016) and in five of seven secondary models, but was absent from the overall GPA model in primary school. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Although hyperactivity–impulsivity does not contribute to explaining overall academic achievement, it is present in the prediction models of mathematics, languages, and social sciences."
