Evidence¶

Feng 2023¶

Feng, M., Huang, C.-W., & Collins, K. (2023). Technology-based support shows promising long-term impact on math learning: Initial results from a randomized controlled trial in middle schools. WestEd. https://osf.io/exqpn

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

School-level moderator analysis splitting the 63 randomized schools into two groups by average percentage of economically disadvantaged students and estimating differential intervention effects (Hedges' g = 0.22).

"students in intervention schools with a higher percentage of economically disadvantaged students benefited more than those in intervention schools that had a lower percentage of students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds (p = 0.045, Hedges' g = 0.22)."

Feng 2023 (2)¶

Feng, M., Huang, C.-W., & Collins, K. (2023). Technology-based support shows promising long-term impact on math learning: Initial results from a randomized controlled trial in middle schools. WestEd. https://osf.io/exqpn

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Companion school-level moderator analysis using average percentage of White students; the p value of 0.056 is marginally above conventional thresholds, and the authors hedge with "seemed to benefit more."

"Furthermore, students in the intervention schools with a lower percentage of White students seemed to benefit more than those in the intervention schools with a higher percentage of White students (p = 0.056, Hedges' g = 0.19)."
