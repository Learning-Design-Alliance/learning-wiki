`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cognitive-engagement survey item from the RCT's learning-experience survey. The article reports "p=.012, η2 =.06" for the omnibus test and interprets that "the integration of visuals, unique toMuDoC, helps learnersintegrate information from two modalities, therefore making them think more."

> "However, for the question 'makes me think' (p=.012, η2 =.06),MuDoCwas the only system to maintain a statistically significant advantageover theDocSearchbaseline (p=.010in Dunn's test).TexDoCshowed apositive trend but did not reach statistical significance (p=.163in Dunn's test)."
