q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' analysis of California Longitudinal Pupil Achievement Data System (CALPADS) data, presented in Figure 1 as predicted probabilities of dual enrollment failure by 8th-grade math percentile and SED status. The quoted sentence reports the printed percentages; no effect size is printed.

"At the 90th percentile of math achievement—that is, among the most academically prepared students in California—students who are not from low-income or first-generation backgrounds had a failure rate of about 6.5 percent. However, students from low-income and first-generation backgrounds at the same achievement level had a failure rate of 10.2 percent, a rate 58 percent higher."
