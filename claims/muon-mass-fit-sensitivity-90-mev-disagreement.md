`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of the same experimental data (Ntotexp = 43) with simulation output under a 90 MeV assumption, shown in Fig. 6. The article reports that the distributions "signiﬁcantly disagree" under this assumption.

> "To demonstrate the sensitivity of our approach to the as- sumed value of the muon mass, Fig. 6 displays the result of simply changing the value of mµc2 used in the simula- tion to 90 MeV. We see that Nexp(nS) and Nsim(nS) now signiﬁcantly disagree."
