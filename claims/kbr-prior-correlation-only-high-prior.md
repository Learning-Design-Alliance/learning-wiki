# %KBR correlated with prior knowledge only among high-prior students, not among low-prior students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among high-prior students, %KBR correlated with CKTpre (r(9) = 0.76, p < 0.05) and PSTpre (r(9) = 0.64, p < 0.05). [→ Fathima Ameen 2026](#fathima-ameen-2026)

## Evidence

### Fathima Ameen 2026

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Pearson correlations on the median-split groups from the classroom study; among high-prior students %KBR and pre-test scores "were highly correlated", consistent with their knowledge-building capacity being already developed.

> "correlation analyses showed that among high-prior students, CKT pre and %KBR were highly correlated, r (9) = 0 .76,p < 0.05, as were PST pre and %KBR, r (9) = 0 .64,p < 0.05."

### Fathima Ameen 2026 (2)

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations among low-prior students in the same ad hoc analysis; neither correlation was significant, suggesting their knowledge-building responses "may not be determined by prior understanding but rather through interaction with the adaptive questioning framework."

> "the correlation between %KBR and CKTpre was not significant for low- prior students, r (9) = 0.21, p =0 .54, nor was the correlation between %KBR and PST pre, r (9) = 0.15, p =0 .67."
