`q3 i?` GE-CLASS cumulative score distributions are shifted to lower scores than E-CLASS pre- and post-instruction, particularly for YOU-questions. [→ Teichmann 2022](#teichmann-2022)

## Evidence

### Teichmann 2022

Teichmann, E., Lewandowski, H. J., and Alemani, M. (2022). Investigating students' views of experimental physics in German laboratory classes. arXiv:2201.12145. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of cumulative likelihood distributions of total three-point scores (range -30 to +30) in Fig. 4: the GE-CLASS curve rises at a lower score and tops out lower, with the maximum YOU-question total score "+28 as compared to the E-CLASS results where there are students who reach up to +30".

> "fewer students reach the highest survey scores before instruction (the maximum total reached score is in fact for the YOU-questions +28) as compared to the E-CLASS results where there are students who reach up to +30."
