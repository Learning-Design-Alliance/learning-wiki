### McInerney Niall 2022

McInerney Niall, Nally D., Khan M.F., Heneghan H., Cahill R.A. (2022). Performance effects of simulation training for medical students – a systematic review. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2022-39/zma001572.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Nassif et al.'s study comparing hybrid simulation (standardized patient wearing a silicone breast simulator jacket) against a standardized tabletop breast model, with both groups assessed by OSCE. The review reports the hybrid group was "significantly better at lesion reporting."

> "Students who participated in hybrid simulation training were significantly better at lesion reporting, identification of malignant features and accurate location identification compared to the group who received traditional teaching."
