### Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto 2015

Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An icampus survey let students vote on whether they liked using a one-page A4 cheatsheet in the exam, introduced to shift studying from plain memorizing toward understanding concepts. The report notes only "a little less than half of the students voted in the survey".

> "It may not be a total surprise that students rejected it; 61% and 55% of the students in the respective courses said not to like the cheatsheet."
