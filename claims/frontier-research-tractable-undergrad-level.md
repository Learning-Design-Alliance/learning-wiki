This is the authors' first-person account, in the project report's introduction, of how they selected and scoped Macher's three-month ENS-Lyon student project on compact stars. It reports no measured learning outcome; the support is the authors' own experience and assertion that basic coursework "is suﬃcient to tackle the scientiﬁc problem".

> "The chosen scientiﬁc research area is at the forefront of present active research on neutron stars, while being tractable at the undergraduate level. In particular, basic knowledge about thermodynamics, quantum statistics and relativity theory is suﬃcient to tackle the scientiﬁc problem."
