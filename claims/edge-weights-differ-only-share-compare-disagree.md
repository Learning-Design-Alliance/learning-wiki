How does collaborative task design shape collaborative knowledge construction and group-level regulation of learning? A study of secondary school students' interactions in two varied tasks. Intern. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

T/ONA edge-weight comparison between task networks at a 5% significance level. The article reports it "only found significant differences between the two CKC phases 'share/compare' and 'disagree'," meaning group transitions between these phases differ significantly by task.

> "We compared the edge weights (connections between the codes) between the two task types using a Mann–Whitney U test with a significance level of 5% (e.g., whether emotion–motivation regulation more often led to negotiation in Simua than in SimSketch), but only found significant differences between the two CKC phases "share/compare" and "disagree.""
