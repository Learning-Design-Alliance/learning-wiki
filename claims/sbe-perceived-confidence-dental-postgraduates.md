## Evidence

### Mascarenhas Sharon 2021

Mascarenhas Sharon, Al-Halabi Manal, Otaki Farah, Nasaif Mohamed, Davis Dave. (2021). Simulation-based education for selected communication skills: exploring the perception of post-graduate dental students. Korean J Med Educ 33(1):11-25. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.183

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Post-intervention self-assessment of all 43 participating students on a 5-point Likert-type scale, reported descriptively in the Results; 19% strongly agreed and 67% agreed they felt competent, and "None of the students selected "disagree" or "strongly disagree"". No inferential test or effect size was reported.

> "The post-intervention self-assessment showed that 19% of the students "strongly agree" that they are competent in handling similar situations in their clinical practice, 67% selected "agree", and only 14% were "neutral". None of the students selected "disagree" or "strongly disagree"."
