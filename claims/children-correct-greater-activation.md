## Evidence

### Denervaud 2020

Denervaud, S., Fornari, E., Yang, X.-F., Hagmann, P., Immordino-Yang, M. H., & Sander, D. (2020). An fMRI study of error monitoring in Montessori and traditionally-schooled children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0069-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second-level mixed-design ANOVA of fMRI BOLD activation (main effect of response, FDR cluster-corrected) found correct responses elicited "higher brain activity" in bilateral PCC, left precuneus, middle frontal gyrus, inferior temporal gyrus, and cerebellum.

> "relative to incorrect responses, correct responses elicited higher brain activity (Fig. 3 top panel, Table 2A) in the bilateral posterior cingulate cortex and left precuneus, as well as within the middle frontal gyrus, the left inferior temporal gyrus, and both left 1st and 2nd crus of the cerebellum."
