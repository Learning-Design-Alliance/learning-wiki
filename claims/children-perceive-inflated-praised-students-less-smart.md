## Evidence

### Schoneveld 2023

Schoneveld, E., & Brummelman, E. (2023). "You did incredibly well!": teachers' inflated praise can make children from low-SES backgrounds seem less smart (but more hardworking). npj Science of Learning, 8:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00183-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered within-subjects paper-and-pencil experiment with 63 primary school children (ages 10-13) reading vignettes where two students scored identically but received different praise. "deemed the student who received inflated praise less smart than the student who received modest praise"; also inflated vs. no praise (p̂ = 0.76, z = 4.84) and modest vs. no praise (p̂ = 0.76, z = 4.84), both p < 0.001.

> "Significantly more than half of the children, p̂= 0.79, deemed the student who received inflated praise less smart than the student who received modest praise, z = 5.71, p < 0.001."
