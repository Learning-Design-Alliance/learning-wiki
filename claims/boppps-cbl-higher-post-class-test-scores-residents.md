### Fan J 2025

Fan J, Wang M, Lv Q, Li D, Jiang H, Fu G, Zhang W. (2025). Application of the BOPPPS-CBL model in emergency chest pain management teaching for resident physicians: a randomized comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06939-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 118 resident physicians on cardiology rotation, randomized to BOPPPS-CBL (n=59) or LBL (n=59). The article reports "significantly higher" after-class test totals for BOPPPS-CBL, with no printed effect size.

> "After teaching, the BOPPPS-CBL group’s after-class test scores were significantly higher than those of the LBL group (26.95 ± 2.90 vs. 22.58 ± 3.56, P < 0.001)."
