`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

> "Near-peer mentorship emerged as the most common (44%), followed by experiences in social service (30.8%), during clinical practice (13.2%), giving class presentations (11.7%), experiences not related to medicine (8.8%) and finally, in patient-care teaching experience (4.4%)."
