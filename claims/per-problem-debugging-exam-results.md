`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results of the four-problem debugging exam (Table I) in the quasi-experimental pilot. The misoriented IC problem was most challenging, with success rates of 54% (7/13) and 60% (3/5) and times of 27:00 and 25:20; the article reports these "emerging trends" without statistically significant p-values.

> "For the misoriented IC and floating body circuits, students in the experimental group successfully debugged the circuit faster on average. In addition, the success rates for the misoriented IC and 10x probe bugs are higher."
