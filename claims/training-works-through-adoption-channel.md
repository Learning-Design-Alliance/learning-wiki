Partial-identification analysis using principal stratification with monotone treatment response, an exclusion restriction for never users, a global support restriction, and mean dominance; bounds constructed with 2000-replication percentile bootstrap. "Confidence intervals are wide and overlapping, so we cannot statistically reject effectiveness as the dominant mechanism".

> "Point estimates suggest the adoption effect for induced users exceeds the effectiveness effect for always users: at γ = 0, the adoption lower bound exceeds the effectiveness upper bound by 0.64 grade points."
