### Christopher T. Stripling and T. Grady Roberts 2013

Christopher T. Stripling and T. Grady Roberts. (2013). Effects of Mathematics Integration in a Teaching Methods Course on Self-Efficacy of Preservice Agricultural Education Teachers. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(2), 114-129. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.02114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group study in a University of Florida teaching methods course with an experimental group of 13 and a control group of 6 preservice teachers; MANOVA on the personal mathematics efficacy construct was non-significant, so the researchers "failed to reject the null hypothesis."

> "The analysis of hypothes is one did not reveal a significant difference in the preservice teachers' personal mathematics efficacy based upon the MTIS treatment, T = .02, F(2, 16) = 0.15, p > .05"
