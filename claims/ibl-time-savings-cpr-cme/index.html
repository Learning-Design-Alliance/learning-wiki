Evidence¶

Hemmati 2013¶

Hemmati, N., Omrani, S., & Hemmati, N. (2013). A comparison of internet-based learning and traditional classroom lecture to learn CPR for continuing medical education. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006264

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Self-reported study time for the IBL group of 40 physicians, covering pretest, content, and post-test at self-pace, compared in the discussion with the 6-hour traditional lecture; the discussion reports "40.2% less time". No effect size was printed.

"The mean time for Internet based CME group was 145 minutes ranging from 125 minutes to 182 minutes."
