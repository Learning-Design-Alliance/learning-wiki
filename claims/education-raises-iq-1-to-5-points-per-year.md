Ritchie, S. J., & Tucker-Drob, E. M. (2018). How much does education improve intelligence? A meta-analysis. Psychological Science, 29(8), 1358-1369. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797618774253

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis pooling three categories of quasi-experimental designs. The authors report "consistent evidence for beneficial effects of education on cognitive abilities" of roughly 1 to 5 IQ points per additional year; no single standardized effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Across 142 effect sizes from 42 datasets involving over 600,000 participants, we found consistent evidence for beneficial effects of education on cognitive abilities, of approximately 1 to 5 IQ points for an additional year of education."
