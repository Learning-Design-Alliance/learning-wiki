### Marjorie Chinen and Mohammed Elmeski 2016

Marjorie Chinen and Mohammed Elmeski. (2016). Evaluation of the Transformative Potential of Positive Gender Socialization in Education for Peace Building. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567431

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline survey data from Table 3 (attitudes toward gender roles, Question 12): control teachers averaged 6.62 on the summed equal-capability items versus 7.03 (training only) and 6.94 (training plus text), with clustered p-values of 0.03 and 0.08. Individual items such as engineering (p = 0.02) and working as a nurse (p = 0.00) also favored the training-only group.
