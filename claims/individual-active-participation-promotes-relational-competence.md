What is known about the development of relational competence in the mental health education of healthcare professionals – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08353-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Thematic synthesis of forty included studies (qualitative n = 18, quantitative n = 16, mixed methods n = 6) in a scoping review; the synthesis "organised into two themes", the first being "individual active participation" with five promoting sub-themes.

> "The first, individual active participation, describes promoting interventions and includes the sub-themes mapping training needs, learning through reflection, learning through active engagement in the classroom, learning in real-life situations, and learning through knowledge of lived experience."
