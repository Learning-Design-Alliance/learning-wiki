## Evidence

### Basoz 2016

Basoz, T. & Can, T., D. (2016). The effectiveness of computers on vocabulary learning among preschool children: A semiotic approach. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science, 11(1), 02-08. http://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/cjes/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test scores of the CAVI group (n=16) and TVI group (n=19) after the four-week experiment. The article reports no significant difference, t(33) = -1.012, p = .319, d = .02, a small effect size.

> "The result signified that there was no significant difference between the CAVI group’s (M = 13.31, SD = 4.28) and t he T VI group’ s (M = 14.78, SD = 4.31), t (33) = -1.012, p = .319, d = .02 post-test scores with a small effect size."
