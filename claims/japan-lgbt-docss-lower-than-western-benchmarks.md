> "Nowaskie et al. [21] conducted a large multicenter study of 1,701 U.S. health professional students and reported an overall mean LGBT-DOCSS score of 5.82 (Attitudinal Awareness: 6.43; Basic Knowledge: 5.54; Clinical Preparedness: 5.12). In contrast, our PGY-1 cohort demonstrated markedly lower scores (Overall: 4.0; Attitudinal Awareness: 5.3; Basic Knowledge: 3.9; Clinical Preparedness: 2.5; Clinical Training: 2.3)."
