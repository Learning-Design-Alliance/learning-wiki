Bao and Redish model analysis applied to 2014 and 2015 ACI data for the principle of stationary action concept (Q7, Q9, Q16), contrasting the correct "stationary action" model with the incorrect "least action" model based on the misconception that only minima are stationary cases. The plot (Fig. 2) places students in the mixed region pre and post.

> "The model plot shows that both before and after instruction, individual students were in the mixed model region. However after instruction the correct model was more likely to be used."
