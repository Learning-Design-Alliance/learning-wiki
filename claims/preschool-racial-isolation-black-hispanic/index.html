Evidence¶

Plasencia 2024¶

Plasencia, S., & Melnick, H. (2024). Strategies to foster integration in early childhood education. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/639.507

q2 · i? · review · r2

A study of publicly funded preschool programs reviewed by the report found that "almost half of all Black and hispanic preschoolers (48% and 49%, respectively)" attended highly segregated schools. The same study found 56% of dual language learners in schools that were 90% or more students of color.

"The study found that almost half of all Black and hispanic preschoolers (48% and 49%, respectively) were enrolled in schools in which over 90% of students are students of color, while just under 20% of White students were enrolled in highly segregated, mostly White schools."
