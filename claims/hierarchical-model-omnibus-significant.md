Omnibus ANOVA F-test of the full hierarchical regression model in the 448-student sample. The article reports the five-block model was "statistically significant (P<0.05, F (df 7 = 5, 442 = 128.848))"; only the test statistic is printed, no effect size.

> "The Analysis of variance (ANOVA) results in (Table 8) show that the models as a whole (which includes all the five models or blocks of variables) is statistically significant (P<0.05, F (df 7 = 5, 442 = 128.848)) which illustrate that the independent variables significantly predict the dependent variable (Academic performance of children)."
