Evidence¶

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim 2020¶

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of the Cognitive, Affective, and Interpersonal Outcomes of Flipped Classrooms in Higher Education. Educ. Sci. 2020, 10, 115. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10040115

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random effects meta-regression of effect sizes on publication year (Table 8) across the 43 studies. The printed parameters were intercept 1021.4 (SE 141.8, z = 7.20, p = 0.001) and "publication year −0.51" (SE 0.070, z = −7.20, p = 0.001).

"The slope of the meta-regression by publication year is negative overall, but it is statistically signiﬁcant (Table 8) and has a signiﬁcant moderating eﬀect on the relationship between ﬂipped classrooms and a study’s year of publication."
