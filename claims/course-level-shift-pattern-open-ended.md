## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Separate analysis of FY (N = 592 open-ended, 3487 guided) and BFY (N = 553 open-ended, 279 guided) populations; in FY courses open-ended students showed no significant shift (p = 0.4) while guided-only students shifted negatively (d = −0.2).

> "in BFY courses, courses using open-ended activities showed a small ( d = 0.2) but statistically signiﬁcant positive shift from pre- to postinstruc- tion (p ≪ 0.01), while courses using only guided activities showed a small ( d = −0.1) negative shift ( p = 0.03)."
