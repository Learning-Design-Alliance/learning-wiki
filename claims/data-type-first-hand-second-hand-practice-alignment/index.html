Data-type coding of the 42 reviewed studies. The review reports the distribution of first-hand, second-hand, and both data types and relates them to grade levels and domains (Fig. 7).

"Specifically, 9 studies employed first-hand data collected by students, 23 studies used second-hand data (either given to students or retrieved by students from repositories), and 10 studies used both."
