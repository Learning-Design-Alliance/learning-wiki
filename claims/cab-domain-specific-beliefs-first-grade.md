Jacquelynne S. Eccles · Allan Wigfield. (2024). The Development, Testing, and Refinement of Eccles, Wigfield, and Colleagues' Situated Expectancy-Value Model of Achievement Performance and Choice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09888-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The Childhood and Beyond cohort-sequential study measured children yearly from first, second, and fourth grades into adulthood (sample 95% white). Children had no general cross-domain belief; anxieties did not differentiate by domain, and math gender differences disappeared over time.

> "The key findings of this study were (1) children's SCAs and STVs were differentiated from each other in the first grade and become more strongly differentiated with age (Eccles et al., 1993); (2) children's SCAs and STVs formed distinct factors in each domain."
