Site 3 comparison of MATHia workspace lessons completed per hour between the Pilot Treatment phase (1:8 ratio) and Maturing Treatment phase (1:4 ratio) in an all-treatment charter school with no control group; the article prints the 0.36 workspaces-per-hour difference and t-test but no standardized effect size.
