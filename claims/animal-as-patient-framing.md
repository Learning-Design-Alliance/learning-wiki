### Swain Cara 2025

Swain Cara, Cohen Hugo, Routh Jennifer, Helgesson Gert, Rickard Rory, Karlgren Klas. (2025). Optimising the educational utility of live tissue training in trauma surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07846-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Framework Method analysis of fifteen interviews (Delivering theme). The study found that "By considering the pig as a patient throughout, the curriculum is delivered in a manner that is less akin to simulation training and more like real-life surgical practice"; educators tended to call animals models, few participants called them patients.

> "By considering the pig as a patient throughout, the curriculum is delivered in a manner that is less akin to simulation training and more like real-life surgical practice, and impacts on the learner’s experience of LTT"
