# Test-taking experience ratings differed between the midterm and final computer-based exams, with poorer concentration at the midterm

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A multivariate ANOVA showed test-taking-experience questions were evaluated differently for the midterm versus final computer-based exam (partial-η² = .14). [→ Boevé A.J. 2015](#boeve-aj-2015)
`q3 i2` Students were less able to concentrate in the midterm computer-based exam than in the final computer-based exam (partial-η² = 0.054). [→ Boevé A.J. 2015 (2)](#boeve-aj-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Boevé A.J. 2015

Boevé A.J., Meijer R.R., Albers C.J., Beetsma Y., Bosker R.J. (2015). Introducing Computer-Based Testing in High-Stakes Exams in Higher Education: Results of a Field Experiment. PLoS ONE 10(12): e0143616. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0143616

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate ANOVA on post-exam questionnaire ratings of the computer-based exam experience, comparing midterm and final administrations. The overall omnibus test was significant, F(6,258) = 7.021, p < .001, partial-η² = .14.

> "Results of the overall model test (α = .05) showed that there was a difference in how the questions were evaluated between the midterm and final exam (F(6,258) = 7.021, p < .001, partial-η² = .14)."

### Boevé A.J. 2015 (2)

Boevé A.J., Meijer R.R., Albers C.J., Beetsma Y., Bosker R.J. (2015). Introducing Computer-Based Testing in High-Stakes Exams in Higher Education: Results of a Field Experiment. PLoS ONE 10(12): e0143616. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0143616

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected univariate follow-up of the same MANOVA on questionnaire ratings. Concentration was rated lower for the midterm computer-based exam, F(1,265) = 22.39, p = .00014, partial-η² = 0.054.

> "Additional (Bonferroni corrected) univariate analyses showed that students were less able to concentrate in the midterm computer-based exam compared to the final exam. (F(1,265) = 22.39, p = .00014, partial-η² = 0.054)."
