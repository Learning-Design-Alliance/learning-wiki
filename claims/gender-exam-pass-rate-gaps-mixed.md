`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of academic-achievement studies: two UK studies found lower MRCS pass rates for female trainees, but Robinson et al. found no significant gender differences in MRCS, and no significant disparities were reported for ABSCE, FRCS or OITE/ABOS scores. The review reports these as second-hand findings.

> "In the UK, two studies reported significantly lower Membership of the Royal College of Surgeons (MRCS) pass rates for female trainees compared to males [4, 14]. However, Robinson et al. [15] presented no significant gender differences in MRCS success rates."
