# Han students' metacognitive knowledge scores were significantly higher than Yao students' at each of four annual measurements from grade 3 to grade 6

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The main effect of ethnic group on MCK was significant, with Han students scoring significantly higher than Yao students across all four measurements. [→ Mark Feng Teng 2024](#mark-feng-teng-2024)

## Evidence

### Mark Feng Teng 2024

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on four annual MCK measurements of 115 Yao and 108 Han students (223 total with complete longitudinal data). The group main effect was significant: "F = 165.565, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.428". Descriptive means show Han higher in every year (e.g., grade 6: Han 51.82 vs Yao 31.96).

> "Based on the ANOVA results, the main effect of ethnic group on MCK was significant, F = 165.565, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.428, indicating that the MCK scores of the Han students were significantly higher than those of the Yao students."
