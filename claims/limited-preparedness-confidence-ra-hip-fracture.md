`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Attitude-section items on preparedness and confidence in the survey of 80 ED doctors. The article reports "25/79 (32%) were comfortable performing RA for hip fractures"; 81% of those performing RA reported the procedure took less than 15 min. Descriptive percentages only.

> "Perceived preparedness to perform the techniques was limited. Only 11/80 (14%) felt their training had been sufficient, and 25/79 (32%) were comfortable performing RA for hip fractures, although 37/80 (46%) agreed that they felt comfortable using POCUS to perform RA for hip fractures."
