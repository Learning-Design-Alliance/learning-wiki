Questionnaire survey of 284 third-year medical students evaluating their PBL tutors, analyzed descriptively in SPSS. The article reports the "mean performance ranking for tutors was above halfway" at 6.32 ± 2.0 on the 1–10 scale.

> "The results reveal that the mean performance ranking for tutors was above halfway (mean ± SD = 6.32 ± 2.0, N = 284, min = 1, max = 10)."
