## Evidence

### Kim 2020

Kim, Lisa E. and Burić, Irena. (2020). Teacher self-efficacy and burnout: Determining the directions of prediction through an autoregressive cross-lagged panel model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000424

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged paths from TSE to both burnout dimensions in the final covariate-adjusted models. The article reports "the only statistically significant path was TSE predicting disengagement from Time 1 to Time 2" with β = -.075; no other TSE-to-burnout path reached significance.

> "we found that the only statistically significant path was TSE predicting disengagement from Time 1 to Time 2 (β = -.075, p < .05)"
