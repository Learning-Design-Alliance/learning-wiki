Herbert W. Marsh 2026¶

Herbert W. Marsh. (2026). Four Decades After the Big-Fish–Little-Pond Effect: Critical Boundary Tests and Integrative Models of Mean-Context, Ordinal-Standing, and Distributional-Scaling in 46 Countries. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10200-0

Multilevel Model 3 in the same TIMSS 2019 sample added a quadratic achievement term, confirming "modest nonlinearity in the L1–MSC relation (L1² = 0.104, SE = 0.002)" while the contextual effect stayed essentially unchanged (L2 = − 0.660, SE = 0.006).

"Model 3 (Table 1) added a quadratic term for individual achievement and confirmed modest nonlinearity in the L1–MSC relation (L1² = 0.104, SE = 0.002; L1 = 0.577, SE = 0.003). Crucially, however, inclusion of the quadratic term left the contextual effect essentially unchanged (L2 = − 0.660, SE = 0.006, compared with − 0.633 in Model 2)."
