## Evidence

### Mielke I 2025

Mielke I, Hampe W, Ehrhardt M, Knorr M. (2025). Spoilt for Choice in Undergraduate Medical Admission: Selection of Confident and Considerate Students Using Professional Prequalification and Situational Judgement Test. Perspectives on Medical Education 14(1). https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1571

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed models on supervisor ratings from the same longitudinal sample of 210 students. The article reports "β = 0.17, 95% CI [0.02; 0.31], p = 0.024" for HAM-SJT predicting warm-agreeable ratings, controlling for GPA, HAM-Nat, gender, and age.

> "Regressions models with the HAM-SJT and all covariates confirmed the positive relationship of HAM-SJT performance (β = 0.17, 95% CI [0.02; 0.31], p = 0.024) and the simultaneous negative relationship of HAM-Nat performance (β = –0.18, 95% CI [–0.33; –0.03], p = 0.022) to ratings of warm-agreeable behavior."
