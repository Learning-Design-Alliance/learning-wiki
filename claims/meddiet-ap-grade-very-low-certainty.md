GRADE assessment of the primary meta-analysis using GRADEpro GDT. Observational evidence began at low certainty and was downgraded across four domains; the article concludes the pooled estimates reflect a possible cross-sectional association rather than evidence that MedDiet adherence improves AP.

> "the certainty of evidence for the association between MedDiet adherence and objective AP was rated as very low. The evidence was downgraded for serious risk of bias, very serious inconsistency, very serious indirectness, and serious imprecision."
