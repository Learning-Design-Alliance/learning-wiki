## Evidence

### Pedde M 2024

Pedde M, Szpiro A, Hirth RA, Adar SD. (2024). School Bus Rebate Program and Student Educational Performance Test Scores. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.3121

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of 1941 school district applicants to the 2012-2016 EPA lotteries, using SEDA district-average test scores for grades 3-8, adjusted for prior-year score, region, lottery year, and multi-entrant status. The overall outcome of replacement was 0.005 (95% CI, −0.007 to 0.018) for RLA and −0.001 (95% CI, −0.011 to 0.010) for math, with CIs crossing zero; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Districts randomly selected for School Bus Rebate Program funding had comparable changes in educational test scores for RLA and math in the year after the lottery to districts not selected for funding (mean SD change in scores, 0.005 [95% CI, −0.007 to 0.018] higher for RLA and −0.001 [95% CI, −0.011 to 0.010] lower for math)"
