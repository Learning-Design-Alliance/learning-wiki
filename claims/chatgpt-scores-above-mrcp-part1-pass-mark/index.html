Evidence¶

Vij O 2024¶

Vij O, Calver H, Myall N, Dey M, Kouranloo K. (2024). Evaluating the competency of ChatGPT in MRCP Part 1 and a systematic literature review of its capabilities in postgraduate medical assessments. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0307372

q2 · i? · design · r2

Experimental evaluation in which the 197-question MRCP UK Part 1 sample paper was entered into ChatGPT-3.5 four times and marked against the official mark scheme. The average of 66.4% was "significantly higher than the historical pass mark (P = 0.0042, 95% CI [1.74, 7.05])".

"ChatGPT-3.5 took the MRCP UK Part 1 sample paper four times and scored 126, 136, 128 and 133 out of 197 giving an average percentage of 66.4% (SD 2.32, range 63.4–69.0%) which is significantly higher than the historical pass mark (P = 0.0042, 95% CI [1.74, 7.05])."

Vij O 2024 (2)¶

Vij O, Calver H, Myall N, Dey M, Kouranloo K. (2024). Evaluating the competency of ChatGPT in MRCP Part 1 and a systematic literature review of its capabilities in postgraduate medical assessments. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0307372

q2 · i? · review · r2

Same four-repeat evaluation with ChatGPT-4, averaging 84.8% out of 197, "significantly above the historical pass mark (P = 0.0001, 95% CI [20.62, 24.97])"; the article also reports ChatGPT-4 significantly better than ChatGPT-3.5 (P = 0.0001, 95% CI [15.25, 21.54]).

"ChatGPT-4 took the MRCP UK Part 1 sample paper four times and scored 164, 168, 169 and 167 out of 197, giving an average percentage of 84.8% (SD 1.10, range 83.2–85.8%) which is significantly above the historical pass mark (P = 0.0001, 95% CI [20.62, 24.97])."
