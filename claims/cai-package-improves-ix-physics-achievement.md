## Evidence

### Maheswari and Ramakrishnan 2015

I. Uma Maheswari, N. Ramakrishnan. (2015). EFFECTIVENESS OF CAI PACKAGE ON ACHIEVEMENT IN PHYSICS OF IX STANDARD STUDENTS. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, Vol. 10 No. 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097442

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest equivalent groups experiment with two Madurai schools; 40 experimental and 40 control students, equated on entry-level test. The t-test on posttest CTWPE scores gave a t value of 4.695 with "significant at 0.01 and 0.05 level for the DF 78"; the experimental mean of 69 exceeded the control mean of 58.

> "the 't' value between the experimental and control groups' students in their mean achievement on Criterion Test Work, Power and Energy is 4.695. It is significant at 0.01 and 0.05 level for the DF 78. The mean of Experimental group (69) is higher than the mean of control group (58)."
