Evidence¶

Adesope 2017¶

Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis of practice test format (Table 2): free-recall g = 0.62, cued-recall g = 0.58, multiple-choice g = 0.70, short-answer g = 0.48, mixed-format g = 0.80; between-levels QΒ(7) = 27.02, p < .001. Post hoc tests showed mixed formats significantly higher than free-recall, cued-recall and short-answer.

"Post hoc analysis also revealed that multiple-choice practice tests were associated with higher weighted mean effect size than short-answer tests, and differed significantly from them."
