Descriptive statistics of HWC participation across twelve courses taught by two instructors in four upper-division subjects. The article reports mean weekly participation "ranges from 18% to 41%" and that medians fall below means, indicating positive skew.

> "The mean HWC participation for each course ranges from 18% to 41%, with the lowest value for a single week being 11% and the highest being 55%. The median participation rate is lower than the mean for all the courses"
