# Standardized patients were rated significantly more believable than virtual patients on behavior, emotion, and overall subscales

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The conventional unit scored significantly higher than the VR unit on all three Believability Scale subscales, with the largest difference on overall believability (η2p = 0.72). [→ Graf Linda 2026](#graf-linda-2026)

## Evidence

### Graf Linda 2026

Graf Linda, Golz Vera, Prehn Sophia, Masuch Maic, Gradl-Dietsch Gertraud. (2026). Virtual vs. standardized patients - a preliminary comparison of advantages and disadvantages of a VR and a conventional learning unit for medical students in child and adolescent psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08760-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on the Believability Scale in the within-subjects questionnaire study. "the conventional unit was rated higher in all three subscales compared to the VR unit"; the VR unit was rated over average on behavior and emotion but under average on overall believability.

> "the conventional and VR units significantly differ between all three subscales: behavior (F (1, 43) = 39.6, p < .001, η2p = 0.48), emotion (F (1, 43) = 25.8, p < .001, η2p = 0.38), and overall (F (1, 43) = 111, p < .001, η2p = 0.72)"
