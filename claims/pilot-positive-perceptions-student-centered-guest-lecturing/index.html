Evidence¶

Lei Li; Rong Guo 2015¶

Lei Li; Rong Guo. (2015). A student-centered guest lecturing: A constructivism approach to promote student engagement. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies, 15. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pilot implementation of study period 1 in an online graduate-level course: a web-based survey administered to 27 invited subjects, 19 completed. Positive perceptions on a 5-point scale included "I enjoyed watching the recorded interviews 84.21%" and engagement items at 73.68%–78.94%. No effect size or inferential test is reported; findings are descriptive percentages only.

"Over 80% of them enjoyed the recorded interview sessions with guest speakers and learned a lot from them. More than 70% of the participants think student-centered approach is very useful: it made them interested in the course; helped them understand course material, made them engaged in the course."
