# Cornelius (2013) finds a positive, statistically significant effect of Odyssey® Math versus teacher-directed supplemental instruction on STAR Math scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among low-performing seventh- and eighth-grade students, those receiving 30 minutes of supplemental Odyssey® Math instruction scored significantly higher on the STAR Math Assessment than peers receiving teacher-directed supplemental instruction. [→ cornelius-1](#cornelius-1)

## Evidence

### cornelius-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2017, January). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Odyssey® Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/669

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental evaluation of 105 low-performing students in five rural Tennessee schools; 60 Odyssey® Math students versus 45 teacher-directed instruction students, analyzed via ANCOVA with STAR Math pretest as covariate. The WWC characterizes this as a statistically significant positive effect.

> "Cornelius (2013) reported, and the WWC confirmed, a positive and statistically significant difference between the Odyssey® Math and comparison groups on the mathematics achievement domain."
