Evidence¶

Ahtisham 2026¶

Ahtisham, B., Vanacore, K., Napoli, A., Arens, J., Ionova, K., Cohn, C., Salehi, S., & Kizilcec, R. (2026). Examining Variation in How Guided AI Tutors Resolve Student Impasses. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38346

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Discrete-time event model (Model 3) on N=6,163 impasse turns with session-clustered standard errors, testing depth×move interactions. Predicted recovery showed scripted questions falling from +20 to +3 percentage points between depths 1 and 4, and addressing the error rising from +5 to +12 pp.

"The benefit of scripted questions (depth× scripted question AOR =0.78 , [0.66, 0.91], p=.002 ) and of follow-up questions (AOR =0.83 , [0.73, 0.95], p=.008 ) declined with each additional impasse turn, whereas the benefit of addressing the student's incorrect answer increased (AOR =1.14 , [1.02, 1.28], p=.026 )."
