Case study of Horace Mann Elementary (PK–6, St. Louis), a SIG school in SY2010–13. Chart 2 illustrates steady growth in English language arts MAP index scores from 2009 to 2014, based on Missouri DESE Comprehensive Data System data; accreditation status is determined by MAP growth, subgroup achievement, and attendance.

"In 2013, Horace Mann achieved provisional accreditation status from the state. They missed full accreditation status by less than 1 point, meaning that the school has moved from one of the lowest performing schools in the district to within the top quarter of schools in the district."
