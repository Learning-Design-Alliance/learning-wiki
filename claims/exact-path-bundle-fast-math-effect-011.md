`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of middle school students in a large Florida district comparing users of the bundled program to matched non-users. The study reports a "positive effect size of +0.11 on Spring 2025 FAST Mathematics scores" after matching and clustering adjustments.

> "After matching students on prior achievement and demographics and accounting for school-level clustering, the bundled intervention was associated with a positive effect size of +0.11 on Spring 2025 FAST Mathematics scores."
