Evidence¶

Shadreck and Enunuwe 2017¶

Mandina Shadreck, Ochonogor Chukunoye Enunuwe. (2017). Problem Solving Instruction for Overcoming Students' Difficulties in Stoichiometric Problems. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 10, Number 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164987

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Comparative pre-test/post-test analysis of difficulty percentages (Table 2 and Figure 5) after a two-week intervention: experimental group taught with problem-solving instruction, control group with the conventional lecture method. All six difficulties showed lower post-test percentages in the experimental group.

"problem-solving instruction reduced difficulty 1 from 61% at the pre -stage to 10% at the post -stage giving an effective rate of 83% in comparison to 27% in the conventional (control) conditions."
