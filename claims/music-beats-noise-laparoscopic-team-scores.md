## Evidence

### Han Ying 2022

Han Ying, Zheng Bin, Zhao Linyong, Hu Jiankun, Zhang Chao, Xiao Ran, Wang Chunyan, Pu Dan. (2022). Impact of background music on the performance of laparoscopy teams. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03503-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject experiment: 57 two-person resident teams each performed both tasks under music, neutral, and randomized acoustic conditions. The music condition outscored the noisy condition on both tasks, with "performance score: 66.3 ± 8.6 vs. 57.6 ± 11.2" for object transportation and 78.6 ± 5.4 vs. 67.2 ± 11.1 for suturing, both p < 0.001.

> "A music environment elicited higher performance scores than a noisy environment for both the object transportation (performance score: 66.3 ± 8.6 vs. 57.6 ± 11.2; p < 0.001) and collaborative suturing tasks (78.6 ± 5.4 vs. 67.2 ± 11.1; p < 0.001)."
