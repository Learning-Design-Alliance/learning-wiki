## Evidence

### Margina Ruiter & Sofie Loyens & Fred Paas 2015

Margina Ruiter & Sofie Loyens & Fred Paas. (2015). Watch Your Step Children! Learning Two-Digit Numbers Through Mirror-Based Observation of Self-Initiated Body Movements. Educ Psychol Rev (2015) 27:457–474. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9324-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Gabriel comparison of the two non-movement control conditions on practice-phase instruction score in the randomized experiment. The article describes this as an unexpected finding: children in the sit condition "performed significantly better during the training phase than children in the write condition".

> "The test indicated that the control groups differed significantly at p=0.04, η2=0.13"
