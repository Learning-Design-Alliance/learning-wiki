Evidence¶

Jenna Van Sickle 2016¶

Jenna Van Sickle. (2016). Discrepancies between Student Perception and Achievement of Learning Outcomes in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 16, No. 2, April 2016, pp. 29-38. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v16i2.19216

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Course-evaluation item comparison between flipped (N=42) and traditional (N=34) sections on the item "Overall, the instructor effectively facilitated my learning"; flipped mean 4.05 versus 4.41, significant at "p = .04". The author states that based on final exam scores the instructor more effectively facilitated learning in the flipped classes, but student perceptions indicated the opposite.

"In a one-tailed t-test, this difference was statistically significant, with p = .04."
