## Evidence

### Ausburn 2010

Ausburn, F. B., Ausburn, L. J., & Kroutter, P. (2010). An Exploration of Desktop Virtual Reality and Visual Processing Skills in a Technical Training Environment. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 6(4), January - March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098351

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the exploratory quasi-experimental study with 18 LPN students, two-way ANOVAs on the six dependent variables found no significant effects at the .10 level for these three measures. The article reports "No significant main or interaction effects were obtained at p =.10 or less" for these outcomes.

> "No significant main or interaction effects were obtained at p =.10 or less for time spent on operating room (OR) learning presentation, timed number of recalled scenic details, or total score on the multiple choice test of scenic orientation."
