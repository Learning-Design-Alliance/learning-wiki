# Stratified analyses show a significant total effect for girls (OR 2.30) but not boys (OR 1.64), with indirect effects for both sexes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For girls the total effect was OR 2.30 (95% CI: 1.40; 3.78) and for boys OR 1.64 (95% CI: 0.93; 2.89); natural indirect effects were significant for both sexes (girls 1.47, boys 1.30). [→ Andersen Mikkel Porsborg 2017](#andersen-mikkel-porsborg-2017)

## Evidence

### Andersen Mikkel Porsborg 2017

Andersen Mikkel Porsborg, Starkof Liis, Sessa Maurizio, Mortensen Rikke Nørmark, Vardinghus-Nielsen Henrik, Bøggild Henrik, Lange Theis, Torp-Pedersen Christian. (2017). The indirect and direct pathways between physical fitness and academic achievement on commencement in post-compulsory education in a historical cohort of Danish school youth. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-017-4712-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified mediation analysis in the same cohort. The article reports natural indirect effects of OR 1.47 (95% CI: 1.12; 1.93) for girls and 1.30 (95% CI: 1.10; 1.53) for boys, "providing evidence of an indirect pathway for both sex"; direct effects were non-significant for both sexes.

> "Stratifying the overall analysis by sex and adjusting for age, ethnicity and socioeconomic status, the total effect shows an OR of 2.30 (95% CI: 1.40; 3.78) for girls and 1.64 (95% CI: 0.93; 2.89) for boys."
