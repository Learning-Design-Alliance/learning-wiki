## Evidence

### Karyn Lewis 2022

Karyn Lewis, Megan Kuhfeld, Meredith Langi, Scott J. Peters, Erin Fahle. (2022). The widening achievement divide during COVID-19. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-widening-achievement-divide-during-covid-19/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of pre-pandemic and spring 2022 MAP Growth samples found the widened dispersion was concentrated at the low end: "test scores for lower-achieving students dropping more than the declines observed for higher-achieving students." No effect size is printed.

> "this increased variability in test scores is primarily driven by a spreading out at the bottom end of the distribution, with test scores for lower-achieving students dropping more than the declines observed for higher-achieving students"
