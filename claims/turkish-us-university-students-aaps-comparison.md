`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 43 Turkish science-education majors with 397 US algebra-based introductory physics students. The article reports average normalized scores of +0.21 (Turkish) and +0.38 (US), a t-test with p<0.05 favoring US students, and item-level chi-square differences in favor of Turkish students only on items 9, 20 and 22.

> "Table V shows that on more than half of the items (18 items), the responses of the US and Turkish university students in algebra-based introductory physics courses were statistically significantly different."
