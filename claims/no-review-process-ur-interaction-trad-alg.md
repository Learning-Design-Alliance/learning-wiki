## Evidence

### Orellana Minerva A. 2025

Orellana Minerva A., Beetler Danielle J., Silvano Carmen J., Wuertz Ryan, Weisbrod Jennifer L., Roberts Lewis R., Windebank Anthony J., Enders Felicity T., Walther-Antonio Marina R. (2025). Integrative review in PhD admissions: A case study of efficiently minimizing bias while maximizing the student narrative. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323426

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Effect modification models pooling traditional (n = 166) and algorithmic (n = 54) review cohorts tested the process-by-UR-status interaction. The study reports the preference was "not driven by an interaction between review process and UR status"; printed interaction p-values were 0.36, 0.62, and 0.63 across phases.

> "However, these models also showed that this was not review process-specific, as this was not driven by an interaction between review process and UR status."
