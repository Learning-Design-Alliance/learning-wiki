## Evidence

### Xiaoyun Chen 2024

Xiaoyun Chen, Katherine E. Twomey, Miranda Hayes, Gert Westermann. (2024). The limits of curiosity? New evidence for the roles of metacognitive abilities and curiosity in learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09407-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Binomial GLMM on 4010 incorrect-guess trials from 100 adults; the article reports "curiosity did not have a significant effect on recall accuracy ( β(logit) = 0.07, SE = 0.05, z = 1.38, p =.17)". No effect size was printed, so impact is null; equivalence was not tested.
