### Sonja Cwik 2022

Sonja Cwik, Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Role of inclusiveness of learning environment in predicting students' outcomes in courses in which women are not underrepresented. [doi:10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666](https://doi.org/10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM path analysis (RQ4) in the same 501-student course; the article prints the four direct-effect coefficients but no standardized effect size. The authors state that since the learning environment predicts self-efficacy, instructors should try to improve belonging, peer interaction, and recognition.

> "Post self-efficacy has direct effects from pre self-efficacy (b = 0.45), belonging (b = 0.49), peer interaction (b = 0.21), and a smaller direct effect from perceived recognition (b = 0.13)"
