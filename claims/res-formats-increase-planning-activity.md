# The RES planning formats increase planning activity compared with the 1993 plan's four-question format, and some teachers view the added documentation as bureaucratic work

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparing the fields of the 1993 plan's Annual Work Plan format with the RES 2006 planning format shows an increase in planning activity, now including inicio, desarrollo and cierre activity phases per subtopic. [→ Alfonso Cuervo 2008](#alfonso-cuervo-2008)

## Evidence

### Alfonso Cuervo 2008

Alfonso Cuervo, César Mora y R. García-Salcedo. (2008). Analysis of the Educative Reform in the Secondary School Education in Mexico and its implications in Science II in the new curriculum. http://www.journal.lapen.org.mx

`q2 · i?`

Documentary comparison of the two SEP planning formats printed as Tables I and II: the 1993 format asked four base questions (purposes, strategies, resources, evaluation), while the RES format adds "las actividades de inicio, de desarrollo y de cierre para cada subtema". The article reports an increase in planning activity.

> "Al hacer la comparación de los campos a llenar en el formato de la tabla I correspondientes al plan 1993, con los de la tabla II (RES 2006), se puede observar un incremento de la actividad de planeación."
