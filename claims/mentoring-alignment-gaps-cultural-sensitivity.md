## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mentors and mentees perceived comparable situations as critical (e.g. mentor presence, guidance of reflection), but gap analysis (question 2 minus question 1) revealed misalignments, and the cultural-sensitivity item appeared in both groups' Top 10 gaps. ## Evidence

### Heeneman Sylvia 2019

Heeneman Sylvia, de Grave Willem. (2019). Development and initial validation of a dual-purpose questionnaire capturing mentors’ and mentees’ perceptions and expectations of the mentoring process. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1574-2

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive gap analysis comparing experienced situations (question 1) with deemed-important situations (question 2) for mentees (n = 103) and mentors (n = 20); the cultural item scored 2.06 ± 0.34 (n = 17) for mentees and 2.31 ± 0.25 (n = 16) for mentors.

> "Item 37 (Mentee list: The mentor is sensitive to my (cultural) background (deleted from Scale 5)) was present in the mentees’ and mentors’ Top 10, indicating that this was a potential situation or critical incident to consider for a needs analysis."
