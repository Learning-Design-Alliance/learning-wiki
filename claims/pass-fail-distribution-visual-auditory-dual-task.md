# More bilingual than monolingual children pass the 60% accuracy criterion on the visual dual-task, but not on the auditory dual-task

## Evidence

### Bialystok Ellen 2011

Bialystok Ellen. (2011). Coordination of Executive Functions in Monolingual and Bilingual Children. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3139691/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pass/fail analysis using a 60% correct criterion (scores below were not different from chance) on dual-modality data. "For the visual responses (Table 3a), more bilinguals passed than monolinguals, χ2 (1) = 10.68, p < .001"; the auditory distributions did not differ.
