## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants held that group supervision should not be used for achieving basic competency and that skill development would be enhanced if reflective group sessions were complemented by direct supervision and practical pool-based activities. [→ Burke 2025](#burke-2025)

## Evidence

### Burke 2025

Burke, K. L. A., Snowdon, D. A., Milne, S. C., & Farlie, M. K. (2025). Exploring Physiotherapists' Perspectives of Group Clinical Supervision Effectiveness for Aquatic Physiotherapy Skill Development: A Qualitative Study. Physiotherapy Research International. https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70044

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group findings across the skill development and interactivity themes: participants wanted practical learning activities in the hydrotherapy pool to complement reflective sessions, and the discussion concludes the interactive environment "is not a substitute for direct supervision but rather an adjunct".

> "While participants believed that group supervision improved their skill development in aquatic physiotherapy, they stressed that it should not be used for achieving competency (i.e. basic skills required to safely practice aquatic physiotherapy)"
