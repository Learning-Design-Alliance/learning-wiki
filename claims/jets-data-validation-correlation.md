### Ward Stephen Thomas 2014

Ward Stephen Thomas, Mohammed Mohammed A, Walt Robert, Valori Roland, Ismail Tariq, Dunckley Paul. (2014). An analysis of the learning curve to achieve competency at colonoscopy using the JETS database. Gut. https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2013-305973

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Validation study comparing JETS submissions with the local endoscopy reporting database at Queen Elizabeth Hospital over two years for 10 of 28 trainees. The study reports "r2=0.99; p<0.01" and a mean difference of three procedures.

> "There was a high correlation between the number of procedures recorded in JETS and the number of procedures recorded in the local reporting software databases for these 10 trainees over this time period (r2=0.99; p<0.01), but a Bland–Altman plot showed that the local number of procedures was on average three more than the JETS number"
