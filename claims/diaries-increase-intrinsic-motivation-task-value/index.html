Evidence¶

Arsal 2010¶

Arsal, Z. (2010). The effects of diaries on self-regulation strategies of preservice science teachers. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 5(1), 85-103. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ884413

q3 · i? · causal · r1

The pre-/post-questionnaire control group study found that although both groups declined in intrinsic motivation and task value, the experimental group declined less, showing "significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of intrinsic motivation" and task value.

"there was significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of intrinsic motivation (t=2.16, p=.034), task value (t=2.04, p=.046)"
