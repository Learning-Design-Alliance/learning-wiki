## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2022

Yafeng Pan, Suzanne Dikker, Yi Zhu, Cuirong Yang, Yi Hu and Pavel Goldstein. (2022). Instructor-learner body coupling reflects instruction and learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00131-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects models in the same dyadic experiment found no significant main effect or interaction of Instructional Personalization on learning outcome. Exploratory models on instructor motion and BtBC vs learners' pre-test performance also showed no significant effects (βs < 0.54, ps > 0.14), suggesting instructors may have failed to adaptively adjust teaching.

> "The main effect of Instructional Personalization (β = 1.20, SE = 2.20, t = 0.54, p = 0.59) and the interaction effect (β = −3.13, SE = 3.12, t = −1.01, p = 0.32) were not significant."
