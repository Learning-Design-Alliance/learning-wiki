### Montero-Marin et al 2021

Montero-Marin et al. (2021). Teachers “Finding Peace in a Frantic World”: An Experimental Study of Self-Taught and Instructor-Led Mindfulness Program Formats on Acceptability, Effectiveness, and Mechanisms. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000542

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial of 206 teachers from 43 schools randomized by school to instructor-led or self-taught mindfulness training, analyzed with linear mixed-effects models. The article reports that changes over time significantly differed between the groups in all these outcomes, "favoring the instructor-led arm."
