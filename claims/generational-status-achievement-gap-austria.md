### Mocevic 2025

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA in the preliminary analyses of the classroom study, controlling for parental education and gender, tested generational status differences on the 40-item performance score. The printed effect size η² = 0.127 falls in the medium-to-large range, so impact is coded 2.

> "The overall effect of generational status was statistically significant, F(2, 431) = 31.47, p < 0.001, η² = 0.127, indicating a substantial achievement gap. On average, students of nonmigrant descent (M = 26.22, SD = 5.09) outperformed second‐generation students of migrant descent (M = 22.19, SD = 5.59) by 3.84 points and first‐generation (M = 20.84, SD = 4.76) by 5.38 points."
