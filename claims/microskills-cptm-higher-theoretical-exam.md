## Evidence

### Peng Yizhong 2026

Peng Yizhong, Hu Juanjuan, Wang Minjie, Zhou Xing, Bing Kaijian, Li Song, Yu Haiyan. (2026). Evaluation of an integrated micro-skills training and clinical pathway model in surgical internship education. PeerJ 14:e21501. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21501

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled trial of 136 surgical interns (68 per group) comparing the synergistic micro-skills training-CPTM model with traditional teaching. The experimental group scored "79.13 ± 5.13 vs. 75.96 ± 5.28" with "Cohen’s d = 0.61".

> "the average theoretical examination score of students in the experimental group was significantly higher than that of the control group (79.13 ± 5.13 vs. 75.96 ± 5.28; mean difference 3.17, 95% CI [1.34–5.00]; Cohen’s d = 0.61, 95% CI [0.26–0.96]; P < 0.001)"
