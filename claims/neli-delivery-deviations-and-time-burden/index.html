Evidence¶

Sibieta 2016¶

Sibieta, L., Kotecha, M., & Skipp, A. (2016). Nuffield Early Language Intervention: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects/language-for-learning/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Process evaluation finding from staff interviews and project-team delivery data. The team reported that "5 of the 31 schools" completing the intervention showed significant deviations, including delayed starts, failing to deliver three group sessions per week, and merging the 20- and 30-week groups; deviations were otherwise generally in line with the prescribed model.

"Teaching assistants delivering the programme reported that they found it difficult to devote enough time to it, and that support from senior staff was required to protect the programme time."
