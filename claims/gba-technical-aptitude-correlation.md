## Evidence

### Gazit N 2025

Gazit N, Ben-Gal G, Eliashar R. (2025). Game-Based Assessment of Cognitive Abilities and Personality Characteristics for Surgical Resident Selection: A Preliminary Validation Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/72264

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of intern performance on the gamified test and a Lap-X VR laparoscopic technical aptitude test. The printed effect size is r = 0.46 (P<.001), with the controlled semipartial r = 0.38, attributed to shared underlying competencies rather than gaming experience alone.

> "We found a significant correlation between the 2 sets of scores (r150=0.46, P<.001). When controlling for video game experience, the correlation remained significant, though slightly reduced (semipartial r152=0.38, P<.001), suggesting that while gaming experience contributes to the association, the majority of the shared variance likely reflects underlying competencies relevant to both assessments."

### Gazit N 2025 (2)

Gazit N, Ben-Gal G, Eliashar R. (2025). Game-Based Assessment of Cognitive Abilities and Personality Characteristics for Surgical Resident Selection: A Preliminary Validation Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/72264

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Secondary correlational analysis linking six specific GBA competencies to technical aptitude test scores. The largest printed effect size is r = 0.50 for flexibility, with the others from r = 0.27 to r = 0.30; the residual total-score correlation was "no significant correlation with the technical aptitude test (r150=0.11, P=.17)".

> "Supporting this interpretation, we found significant correlations between technical aptitude test scores and several nontechnical competencies measured by the GBA: planning, r150=0.28; problem-solving, r150=0.28; analytical thinking, r150=0.27; learning ability, r150=0.30; flexibility, r150=0.50; and precision, r150=0.30; all P<.001. In the absence of these 6 competencies, the total GBA scores showed no significant correlation with the technical aptitude test (r150=0.11, P=.17)."
