Evidence¶

Dawit T. Tiruneh 2014¶

Dawit T. Tiruneh, An Verburgh & Jan Elen. (2014). Effectiveness of Critical Thinking Instruction in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intervention Studies. Higher Education Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n1p1

q2 · i? · review · r3

This is the review's synthesis of the direct instruction category of its 33 included studies, in which instruction begins with teacher explanation of thinking procedures followed by discussion and practice. The review states such direct instruction "appear to consistently result in" greater CT improvement than implicit strategies; no effect sizes are printed.
