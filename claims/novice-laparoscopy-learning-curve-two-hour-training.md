# Repeated attempts within a two-hour basic laparoscopy training significantly decrease novices' completion times on both tasks, and failure rates decrease significantly on task 1 but not task 2

## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA across five attempts per task in the randomized teaching intervention showed mean times "decreased significantly in tasks 1 and 2 with ongoing training", with partial η2 = 0.51 for task 1 and η2 = 0.37 for task 2, both p < 0.001.

> "Repeated measures ANOVA with a Greenhouse‒Geisser correction revealed that the mean times of all participants decreased significantly in tasks 1 and 2 with ongoing training, with Ft_task1(2,76, 99.50) = 36.98, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.51 and Ft_task2(2,13, 76.49) = 21.46, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.37."

### Kölbel Benny 2024 (2)

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same repeated-measures ANOVA on failure rates showed a significant decrease "in task 1" (partial η2 = 0.14, p < 0.001) but no significant decrease in task 2 (partial η2 = 0.04, p = 0.27), a mixed pattern across the two tasks.

> "The failure rates of all participants decreased significantly in task 1, with Ff_task1(3.49, 125.62) = 5.87, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.14, but not significantly in task 2, with Ff_task2(3.78, 136.10) = 1.31, p = 0.27, partial η2 = 0.04"
