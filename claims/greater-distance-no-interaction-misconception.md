## Evidence

### Ajredini 2013

Ajredini, F., Izairi, N., Zajkov, O. (2013). Real Experiments versus PhET Simulations for Understanding Electrostatic Charging. European J of Physics Education, 5(1). https://ejpe.phywe.de

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Analysis of incorrect posttest drawings for situation b) across all three groups. The authors attribute the pattern to everyday experience and note students forget that "the electrostatic force decreases with squared distance, 1/R2". The difference between the three groups' percentages was described as negligible.

> "Students’ answers show that they think if the distance is greater, then the interaction stops."
