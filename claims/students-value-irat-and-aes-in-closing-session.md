Conceptual content analysis of open-ended survey responses from Year 1 medical students (n = 253). Findings show the IRAT and AES were considered particularly beneficial by students (66; 26% of respondents) by allowing them to gauge their own breadth and depth of knowledge and to apply knowledge to scenarios perceived as pertinent to clinical practice.
