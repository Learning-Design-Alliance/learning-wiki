# Learning disability scores correlate negatively with ABR wave III/IV/V latencies, I-III and I-V intervals, and epileptiform EEG activity

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Learning disability scores showed significant negative correlations with absolute latencies of waves III (r = -0.412), IV (r = -0.343), V (r = -0.400), interpeak intervals I-III (r = -0.474) and I-V (r = -0.464), and with epileptiform EEG activity (r = -0.313).
