Interview study of 260 male white collar and professional New York City residents under 36, surveyed six to nine weeks after the televised Hearings. The printed counts show a steep drop from "expressed concern" (100 percent) to action (13 percent).

> "Overall, 260 persons (I00 percent), all citizens of voting age, expressed concern with a local social problem. Sixty-five persons (25 per- cent) 'felt like' doing things classified as ~problem-solving.' Thirty-three per- sons (13 percent) actually did things which, liberally interpreted, might be classified as behavior directed toward the solution of the problem."
