`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reports, citing Liang et al. (2023), that detectors use perplexity as a primary signal and that non-native English writers 'tend, in the data studied by those authors, to produce text with lower perplexity'. The paper's extension of the mechanism to other populations is presented as a plausible inference, not an established finding.

> "Non-native English writers tend, in the data studied by those authors, to produce text with lower perplexity (more predictable word choices, simpler sentence constructions), which is the same statistical feature that detection tools use to identify AI output."
