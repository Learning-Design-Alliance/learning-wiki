## Evidence

### Ulupınar Fadime 2025

Ulupınar Fadime, Karasu Şeyda. (2025). Perceived vs. experienced care: a comparison between nursing students’ self-assessed competence and patients’ reported care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12880-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 167 student–patient dyads at a Turkish public city hospital using the NSCS (1–7) and PPNSCS (1–5). Descriptive means show "a total competence score of 3.8 ± 0.3" and "a total care perception score of 2.3 ± 0.7"; no inferential test of the gap is reported.

> "Students rated their overall competence slightly below the theoretical midpoint, while patients perceived the care they received well below the theoretical midpoint, suggesting a potential perceptual gap."
