### Liu 2016

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Planned ROI contrast comparing the subsequent memory effect for Phase 2 STT-T trials (short delay) with Phase 3 SST-T trials (long delay), each partitioned on later recall. "In left PFC, the subsequent memory effect was larger for the long‐delay condition compared to the short‐delay condition (t 18 = 2.28, P = 0.035, d = 0.53)".
