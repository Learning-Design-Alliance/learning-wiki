## Evidence

### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA (N=55) shows significant modality main effects on NASA-TLX in all three modules, e.g. "F = 21.095, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.478". Post hoc results indicate text materials led to higher NASA-TLX scores.

> "Material modalities had a significant effect on subjective cognitive load (NASA-TLX) (F = 5.039, P = 0.011, ηp2 = 0.180;F = 18.209, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.442; F = 21.095, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.478)"
