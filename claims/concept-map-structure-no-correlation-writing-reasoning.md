# Structural complexity of concept maps shows no significant correlation with scientific reasoning in thesis writing after multiple-comparison correction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After Holm-Bonferroni correction, none of the structural features of concept maps (concepts, propositions, branches; drafts, revisions, finals, or changes) correlated significantly with any BioTAP writing and reasoning measure. [→ Dowd J.E. 2015](#dowd-je-2015)

## Evidence

### Dowd J.E. 2015

Dowd J.E., Duncan T., Reynolds J.A. (2015). Concept Maps for Improved Science Reasoning and Writing: Complexity Isn't Everything. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-06-0138

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 49 undergraduates' three concept-map versions against BioTAP thesis scores. "p values range from 0.03 to 0.99 for the 108 relationships tested"; the one r = 0.31, p = 0.03 correlation did not survive Holm-Bonferroni correction.

> "when we account for multiple comparisons, we do not find any statistically significant relationships between any of the structural features of concept maps assessed and the aspects of science reasoning in writing assessed using BioTAP."
