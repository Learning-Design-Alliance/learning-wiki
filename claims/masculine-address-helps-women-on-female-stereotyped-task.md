# On a task stereotypically associated with women's strengths (empathy reading comprehension), women perform better when addressed in the masculine

## Evidence

### Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev 2021

Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev. (2021). The effect of language on performance: do gendered languages fail women in maths? npj Science of Learning 6:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00087-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A supplementary online experiment (690 participants, 335 women and 345 men, randomly assigned) used a reading comprehension test about empathy associated with positive stereotypes about women. Women scored higher under masculine address (p = 0.042); men's differences were statistically nonsignificant.

> "When addressed in the feminine, women’s average score was only 51, whereas when addressed in the masculine the average score of women was 56.6 ( t-test, p = 0.042)."
