Descriptive adoption statistics from the two-year deployment where tutors were offered as optional supplemental resources integrated into Blackboard and Canvas via LTI standards.

> "2,990 (85.18% of 3,510) did not use the tutors, and 520 (14.81% of 3,510) students voluntarily used the tutors at least once. Of those 520 students, 214 (41.15% of 520) unique students used the platform at least once during Unit 1 of the College Algebra course."
