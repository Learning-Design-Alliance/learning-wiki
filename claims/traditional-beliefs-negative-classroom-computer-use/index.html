Hermans 2008¶

Hermans, R., Tondeur, J., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). The impact of primary school teachers’ educational beliefs on the classroom use of computers. Computers & Education, 51(4), 1499–1509. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.02.001

Same multilevel Model 3 analysis of 525 Flemish primary teachers: traditional beliefs showed a significant negative association with classroom computer use (χ² = 7.36, p < 0.05). The article prints no standardised effect size for this contrast.
