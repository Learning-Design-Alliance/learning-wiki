## Evidence

### Cortés-Rodríguez 2024

Cortés-Rodríguez, López-Rodríguez, Roman, Granero-Molina, Fernández-Sola and Hernández-Padilla. (2024). Effect of two teaching methods on nursing students' acquisition of patient-centered communication competence in older people care: a cluster randomized trial. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1510620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

McNemar tests compared pre-test and post-test benchmark attainment within each group; e.g., overall competence rose from 1 (2%) to 35 (55%) in the standardized patient simulation group and from 2 (3%) to 33 (53%) in the role-play group, with "statistically significant improvement" (p < 0.05).

> "The results showed a statistically significant improvement in the three components of the competence as well as on overall patient-centered communication in older people care competence in both intervention groups (p < 0.05)."
