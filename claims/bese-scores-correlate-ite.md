## Evidence

### Wallihan Rebecca 2018

Wallihan Rebecca, Smith Keely G., Hormann Mark D., Donthi Rajesh R., Boland Kimberly, Mahan John D. (2018). Utility of intermittent online quizzes as an early warning for residents at risk of failing the pediatric board certification examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1366-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis reported in Results and shown in Fig. 2, linking composite and PGY-1 BESE scores to final-year ITE and first-attempt ABP CE scores. The composite score showed "weak to moderate correlation" with both outcomes, all p < 0.0001.

> "there was an association between the composite BESE score and both the final ITE score (n = 318, r = 0.53, p < 0.0001) and ABP CE score (n = 287, r = 0.39, p < 0.0001). The PGY-1 BESE score was also associated with the final ITE score (n = 190, r = 0.47, p < 0.0001) and the ABP CE score (n = 181, r = 0.44, p < 0.0001)."
