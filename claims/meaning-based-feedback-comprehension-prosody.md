### Grønli 2024

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis for the comprehension domain, reporting two studies (Crowe, 2003, 2005) in which "meaning-based feedback combined with instruction positively impacted both fluency and comprehension" whereas decoding-focused feedback enhanced fluency only. The review notes the limited number of studies prevents broad generalizations.

> "two studies found that meaning-based feedback combined with instruction positively impacted both fluency and comprehension, while decoding-focused feedback only enhanced fluency (Crowe, 2003, 2005)."

### Grønli 2024 (2)

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of elaborative corrective feedback studies, reporting that "prosody-focused feedback enhanced fluency, comprehension, and motivation" in Ardoin et al. (2013), alongside the meaning-based comprehension advantage over word-reading feedback.

> "Meaning-based feedback improved reading comprehension more than word-reading feedback (Crowe, 2003, 2005), and prosody-focused feedback enhanced fluency, comprehension, and motivation (Ardoin et al., 2013)."
