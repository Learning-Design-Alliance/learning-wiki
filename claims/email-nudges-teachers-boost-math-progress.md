## Evidence

### Duckworth 2025

Duckworth, A. L., Ko, A., Milkman, K. L., et al. (2025). A national megastudy shows that email nudges to elementary school teachers boost student math achievement, particularly when personalized. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2418616122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Weighted OLS regression on the megastudy's primary outcome (average Zearn Math lessons completed per classroom) among 140,461 randomized teachers, with school fixed effects and preregistered controls. The article reports the pooled effect as a "1.89% increase (Cohen’s d = 0.01, P = 0.02)"; only d = 0.01 is printed, so no standardized magnitude beyond that is claimed.

> "megastudy intervention messages led students to complete an average of 0.03 more lessons than students in the megastudy control condition, a 1.89% increase (Cohen’s d = 0.01, P = 0.02)"
