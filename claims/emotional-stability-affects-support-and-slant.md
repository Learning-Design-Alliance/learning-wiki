# In the emotional stability experiment, emotional support use and slant depend on score, trait level, and their interaction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Score had a significant effect on the use of Advice, Reassurance, Praise and Emotional Reflection in the emotional stability experiment. [→ Matt Dennis 2016](#matt-dennis-2016)
`q3 i?` Trait level (emotional stability) had significant effects on slant and on Emotional Reflection, and trait-level x score interactions were significant for Advice, Emotional Reflection and total emotional support statements. [→ Matt Dennis 2016 (2)](#matt-dennis-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Matt Dennis 2016

Matt Dennis, Judith Masthoff, Chris Mellish. (2016). Adapting Progress Feedback and Emotional Support to Learner Personality. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0059-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Crowdsourced 6x2 between-subjects experiment on learner emotional stability; the 2-way MANOVA showed "a significant effect of score on the use of Advice, Reassurance, Praise and Emotional Reflection". No effect sizes are printed.

> "H1 is confirmed, as there is a significant effect of score on the use of Advice, Reassurance, Praise and Emotional Reflection."

### Matt Dennis 2016 (2)

Matt Dennis, Judith Masthoff, Chris Mellish. (2016). Adapting Progress Feedback and Emotional Support to Learner Personality. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0059-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

From the 2-way MANOVA table for the emotional stability experiment: "ER F( 5, 228) = 3.57, p<. 01 F( 1, 228) = 6.21, p<. 02 F( 5, 228) = 2.27, p<. 05", i.e. significant score, trait-level, and trait-level x score effects on Emotional Reflection use.

> "ER F( 5, 228) = 3.57, p<. 01 F( 1, 228) = 6.21, p<. 02 F( 5, 228) = 2.27, p<. 05"
