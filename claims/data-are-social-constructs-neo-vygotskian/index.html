Evidence¶

Peter Smagorinsky 1995¶

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543065003191

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the article's framing section: the author states that "data are social constructs" jointly produced by researcher, participants, context, and collection means, challenging the petri-dish purity metaphor. No empirical data are offered; the support is the argument itself.

"From a neo-Vygotskian perspective, data are social constructs developed through the relationship of researcher, research participants, research context (including its historical antecedents), and the means of data collection."
