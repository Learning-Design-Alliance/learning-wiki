# Specific emotion patterns: relationship quality predicts later enjoyment, pride, anger, and shame but not anxiety or boredom; earlier enjoyment, anger, boredom, shame, and anxiety predict later relationship quality

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Better initial relationships predicted more enjoyment (math, German, French), more pride (German, French), and less anger and shame (French) one year later; no such associations for anxiety and boredom. [→ Goetz 2021](#goetz-2021)
`q3 i?` More initial enjoyment, lower anger, and lower boredom predicted better relationship quality across domains; lower shame (math, French) and lower anxiety (French) also did. [→ Goetz 2021 (2)](#goetz-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Goetz 2021

Goetz, T., Bieleke, M., Gogol, K., van Tartwijk, J., Mainhard, T., Lipnevich, A. A., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Getting along and feeling good: Reciprocal associations between student-teacher relationship quality and students' emotions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101349. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101349

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 9 estimated all 24 domain-by-emotion combinations in the sample of 535 students, plotted with 95% confidence intervals. The article reports significant relationship-to-emotion paths "mainly in the domain of positive emotions" and states "No such associations were observed for anxiety and boredom."

> "better initial relationships were associated with more enjoyment in math, German, and French classes and with more pride in German and French classes one year later."

### Goetz 2021 (2)

Goetz, T., Bieleke, M., Gogol, K., van Tartwijk, J., Mainhard, T., Lipnevich, A. A., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Getting along and feeling good: Reciprocal associations between student-teacher relationship quality and students' emotions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101349. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101349

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same Model 9 results, lower panel (emotions→relationship). The article reports "more initial enjoyment was associated with better relationship quality across academic domains one year later", plus shame (math, French) and anxiety (French) paths; no pride associations were found.

> "lower initial anger and boredom were associated with better relationship quality across school domains one year later."
