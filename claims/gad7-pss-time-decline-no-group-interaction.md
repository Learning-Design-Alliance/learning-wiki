# GAD-7 and PSS declined significantly over time in both groups, but treatment-by-time interactions were not significant

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Anxiety (GAD-7) and perceived stress (PSS) decreased significantly over time, but the condition-by-time interaction was not significant for either measure. [→ Ta 2025](#ta-2025)
`q3 i?` For PSS, the treatment group's decline was steeper than the control group's, though the interaction was not significant (p = .475). [→ Ta 2025 (2)](#ta-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Ta 2025

Ta, A.; Salgin, N.; Demir, M.; Reindel, K. P.; Mehta, R. K.; McDonald, A.; McCord, C.; Sasangohar, F. (2025). Real-Time Stress Monitoring, Detection, and Management in College Students: A Wearable Technology and Machine-Learning Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.15974](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.15974)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis of weekly self-reported surveys in the 12-week trial. Linear time effects were significant for GAD-7 (p = 0.009) and PSS (p = 0.004); "the interaction effects were insignificant for the three measures".

> "only the main effects of the  linear time were statistically significant for GAD 7 and PSS  (p = 0.009 and p = 0.004, respectively). That is, as time progressed, anxiety and stress decreased significantly"

### Ta 2025 (2)

Ta, A.; Salgin, N.; Demir, M.; Reindel, K. P.; Mehta, R. K.; McDonald, A.; McCord, C.; Sasangohar, F. (2025). Real-Time Stress Monitoring, Detection, and Management in College Students: A Wearable Technology and Machine-Learning Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.15974](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.15974)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple slope analysis of PSS in the same GLMM framework; both groups' declines were statistically significant, but the condition-by-time interaction was not (p = .475).

> "the treatment group [βStd = −0.016 (0.007), t(885) = -2.41, p < .016] experienced a steeper decline in self-reported stress compared to the control group [βStd = −0.012 (0.004), t(885) = -2.90, p = .004]"
