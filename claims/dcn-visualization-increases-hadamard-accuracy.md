# Adding the DCN visualization to Dirac Notation increases accuracy on Hadamard-gate problems in multi-qubit systems for participants with little quantum physics experience

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Participants with two or fewer years of quantum physics experience answered Hadamard-gate questions significantly more accurately with DCN visualization than without, with a small effect size (d = 0.32). [→ Bley 2026](#bley-2026)

## Evidence

### Bley 2026

Bley, J., Rexigel, E., Arias, A., Krupp, L., Longen, N., Lukowicz, P., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, M., and Widera, A. (2026). Visualization Enhances Problem Solving in multi-Qubit Systems. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/hqzq-fbrb](https://doi.org/10.1103/hqzq-fbrb)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test (Holm-adjusted) of accuracy in the within-subject main phase of the eye-tracking study (N=31). Mean accuracy rose from "0 .43 ± 0.32" without visualization to "0 .53 ± 0.33" with visualization, t(30) = 2.21, p(adj.) = .035, Cohen's d = 0.32.
