Annamalai N, Nasor M, Din Eak A and Ayoub Alkubaisy AA (2026) Students’ experiences of using ChatGPT for English language learning: a qualitative study in a Malaysian higher education institution. Front. Psychol. 17:1875727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1875727

In the same interview study of 25 students, participants imbued interactions with social meaning, reporting a perception of being listened to; the article states relatedness "was experienced not through genuine human connection". Qualitative finding; no effect size printed.
