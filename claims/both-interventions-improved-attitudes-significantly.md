Repeated measures ANOVA on attitude scores in the three-group trial was significant for both intervention groups; generalized Eta-Squared was 0.79 for e-learning and 0.88 for gamification (Table 4).

> "the e-learning group (Group 1) showed an increase in mean score from 45.73 to 56.86, while the gamification group (Group 2) improved from 47.33 to 61.46. Both groups exhibited statistically significant improvements in attitude scores post-intervention (P < 0.001)."
