Esteban U. Vega Barajas 2026¶

Esteban U. Vega Barajas. (2026). A Durability and Cross-Language Transfer Benchmark for a Validated Teaching-Feedback Classification Protocol. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11873 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.11873

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive paired comparison of the two frozen-embedding arms under the identical protocol on the 233-comment Spanish held-out split. The article reports near-even McNemar counts (thematic 𝑏=29, 𝑐=28; sentiment 𝑏=12, 𝑐=17) and states "no significance test is run".

"Frozen BETO-2019 and the modern e5-base encoder are numerically close, with no descriptive separation: thematic McNemar 𝑏=29, 𝑐=28 and sentiment 𝑏=12, 𝑐=17, and no significance test is run."

Related Claims¶

  • Sentiment classification transfers to English under the unchanged protocol, with a frozen multilingual encoder the strongest arm at wF1 0.73 and LLMs matching it only with few-shot prompting — related
  • On Spanish sentiment, the 2026 frontier model shows no advantage over the cheap model, with Haiku zero-shot at 0.923 versus Opus zero-shot at 0.884 and the head-to-head McNemar favoring Haiku — related
  • Under the fixed protocol, hosted LLMs produce the largest gain on Spanish thematic classification, with thematic macro F1 rising from 0.46 (TF-IDF) to roughly 0.82-0.86 for the two hosted LLMs — related
