## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Women who dropped the first STEM major cluster earned significantly lower STEM GPAs than men who persisted to a degree in those majors (p < 0.001, d = −0.65). [→ Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh 2020](#kyle-m-whitcomb-and-chandralekha-singh-2020)
