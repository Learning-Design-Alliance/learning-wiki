## Evidence

### Hattabi Soukaina 2022

Hattabi Soukaina, Forte Pedro, Kukic Filip, Bouden Asma, Have Mona, Chtourou Hamdi, Sortwell Andrew. (2022). A Randomized Trial of a Swimming-Based Alternative Treatment for Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316238

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Academic performance was assessed via school grades in reading comprehension, math, and overall average pre and post in the randomized trial. Post hoc comparisons showed no pre-intervention differences (e.g., reading p = 0.830, math p = 0.890) but significantly higher experimental-group scores post-intervention (p < 0.001). The results text reports no numeric effect sizes.

> "The experimental group exhibited enhanced performance after exercise on marks of reading comprehension with a strong effect, math with a strong effect, and finally, better pass marks with a strong effect."
