### Ashutosh Kumar Pathak 2026

Ashutosh Kumar Pathak, Mark A. J. Parker, Ross K. Galloway, Andrew J. A. James, Sally E. Jordan, Jonathan Nylk (2026). What do student responses to the car-truck problems tell us? An investigation into two Force Concept Inventory questions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.01477](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.01477)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined 2019/20 to 2023/24 conventional FCI administrations at University A; Option C selection fell and Option A rose from pre-test to post-test, with "the percentage of responses selecting Option A increased from 40% (pre-test) to 71% (post-test)".

> "the percentage of responses selecting Option C decreased from 56% (pre-test) to 27% (post-test) while the percentage of responses selecting Option A increased from 40% (pre-test) to 71% (post-test)."

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "The pre-test responses in the earlier dataset were noticeably less likely to be correct, with the percentage selecting Option A ranging from 6% to 14%."
