Salazar 2015¶

Salazar, D. A. (2015). Razalas' Grouping Method and Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082724

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of math journals and follow-up group interviews from both groups; students also reported disadvantages including some students not sharing in solving questions and off-topic conversation causing chaos, which the professor controlled.

"They have developed the value of sharing and helping others. It promoted effective communication and exchange of ideas towards a common goal. The incentives made the seatwork enjoyable."
