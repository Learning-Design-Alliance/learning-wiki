### Francois St-Hilaire 2021

Francois St-Hilaire, Nathan Burns, Robert Belfer, Muhammad Shayan, Ariella Smofsky, Dung Do Vu, Antoine Frau, Joseph Potochny, Farid Faraji, Vincent Pavero, Neroli Ko, Ansona Onyi Ching, Sabina Elkins, Anush Stepanyan, Adela Matajova, Laurent Charlin, Yoshua Bengio, Iulian Vlad Serban, and Ekaterina Kochmar. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparative study: 25 participants completed the course on Platform A and 22 on Platform B after random assignment, with pre- and post-quizzes of 20 multiple-choice questions. The study reports the Platform B average was "70.43% higher than the Platform A average" (p=0.038). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "When considering raw learning gains g, the Platform B average is 70.43% higher than the Platform A average with 95% conﬁdence ( p=0.038)."
