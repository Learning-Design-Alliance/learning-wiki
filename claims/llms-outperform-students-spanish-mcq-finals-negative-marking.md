Descriptive aggregation across four course finals at Miguel Hernández University comparing 442 student scores with five LLMs (two runs each, averaged). The article reports the "weighted average was 5.76" for students and 8.75 across LLMs; no inferential student–LLM test was performed.

> "The student cohort’s weighted average was 5.76 (max 8.66), and the mean across all LLMs was 8.75."
