### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Student t-test comparing preclinical (N = 21) and clinical (N = 97) students on the Perceived Stress Scale; Table 7 reports means of 31.714 vs 30.164 and "higher in preclinical students (p < 0.028)". No effect size is printed.

> "The only significant difference between clinical and preclinical students consists in the amount of perceived stress, higher in preclinical students (p < 0.028)."
