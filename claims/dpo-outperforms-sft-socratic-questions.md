## Evidence

### Nischal Ashok Kumar and Andrew Lan 2024

Nischal Ashok Kumar and Andrew Lan. (2024). Improving Socratic Question Generation using Data Augmentation and Preference Optimization. arXiv preprint arXiv:2403.00199. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.00199](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.00199)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results-section comparison of SFT Greedy, SFT Sample-5, DPO Greedy, and DPO Sample-5 variants of the fine-tuned Llama 2 model on Rouge-L and BERTScore. The article states that "DPO consistently outperforms SFT" across these experiments.

> "Among the preference optimization experiments, we see that DPO consistently outperforms SFT."

### Nischal Ashok Kumar and Andrew Lan 2024 (2)

Nischal Ashok Kumar and Andrew Lan. (2024). Improving Socratic Question Generation using Data Augmentation and Preference Optimization. arXiv preprint arXiv:2403.00199. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.00199](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.00199)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative case study (Section 7) comparing questions generated by LLama, LLama (CoT), SFT Greedy, DPO Greedy, SFT Sample-5, and DPO Sample-5 for a factorial bug. The article concludes "DPO Sample-5 is highly capable of generating valid yet diverse questions".

> "Through these comparisons, we see that DPO improves Socratic question generation compared to SFT and that DPO Sample-5 is highly capable of generating valid yet diverse questions."
