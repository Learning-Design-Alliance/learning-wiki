## Evidence

### Bochatay N 2025

Bochatay N, Ju M, O'Brien BC, van Schaik SM. (2025). A Scoping Review of Interprofessional Simulation-Based Team Training Programs. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000792

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of framework use across the 58 included articles reports that despite most post-2011 publication, IPEC competency use in design or assessment was rare.

> "While 51 (87.9%) of all 58 articles in this review were published after the IPEC competencies were first released in 2011, only 5 (8.6%) articles mentioned using these competencies to design the ISBTT program and/or assess participants, 2 of which reported on the same program."
