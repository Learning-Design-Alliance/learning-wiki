Evidence¶

Christian Doabler 2014¶

Christian Doabler, Ben Clarke, Keith Smolkowski, Hank Fien, Scott Baker. (2014). Testing the Efficacy of a Tier 2 Kindergarten Mathematics Intervention: Findings of Year 1. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562831

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive statistics from the first round of Classroom Observations of Student-Teacher Interactions (COSTI) observations, with 16 observed groups per condition. For example, individual independent practice averaged 1.64 per minute in small groups versus 1.25 in large groups; the article presents these as preliminary and descriptive, with no significance test reported.

"While preliminary, these results suggest that students in the ROOTS-small group are receiving rates of practice opportunities that are greater than or equal to their at-risk peers in the ROOTS-large groups."
