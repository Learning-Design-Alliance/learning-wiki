Evidence¶

Frith Chris D 2012¶

Frith Chris D. (2012). The role of metacognition in human social interactions. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2012.0123

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports two experimental literatures: Vohs & Schooler's manipulation of free-will beliefs increasing cheating, and Job et al.'s experiments showing told beliefs about willpower (depletable versus strengthenable) changed persistence after exerting will.

"On a subsequent arithmetic test, these students were more likely to cheat than a group who had not been told anything about free will."
