# Collaborative inquiry learners obtain significantly higher post-test overall scores than individual learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in the collaborative learning setting obtained significantly higher post-test overall scores compared to individual learners. [→ Kolloffel 2011](#kolloffel-2011)

## Evidence

### Kolloffel 2011

Kolloffel, B., Eysink, T. H. S., & de Jong, T. (2011). Comparing the effects of representational tools in collaborative and individual inquiry learning. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 6, 223–251. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9110-3

`q3 · i?`

Between-subjects experiment with 61 dyads and 95 individuals in a simulation-based inquiry environment; ANCOVA with math grade and time-on-task as covariates showed "significantly higher post-test overall scores" for the collaborative setting, F(1,201)=17.33, p<.001.

> "It was found that students in the collaborative learning setting obtained significantly higher post-test overall scores ( F (1,201)=17.33, p<.001) compared to individual learners."
