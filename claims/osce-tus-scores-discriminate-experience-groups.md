## Evidence

### Pietersen Pia Iben 2023

Pietersen Pia Iben, Bhatnagar Rahul, Andreasen Freja, Konge Lars, Laursen Christian Borbjerg, Rahman Najib, Nielsen Anders Bo. (2023). Objective structured clinical examination in basic thoracic ultrasound: a European study of validity evidence. BMC Pulmonary Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-022-02285-4

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional validation study in which 25 candidates (4 novices, 8 intermediates, 13 experienced) completed the seven-station OSCE. Group means were "42 ± 10.1 points", "79.1 ± 8.1 points", and "89.0 ± 6.2 points (one-way ANOVA, p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The novice group (n = 4) had a mean test score of 42 ± 10.1 points, the intermediate group (n = 8) scored 79.1 ± 8.1 points, and the experienced group (n = 13) 89.0 ± 6.2 points (one-way ANOVA, p < 0.001)."
