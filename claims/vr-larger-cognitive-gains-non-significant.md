## Evidence

### Hing Lie 2023

Hing Lie, Kachina Studer, Zhen Zhao, Ben Thomson, Dishita G Turakhia, John Liu. (2023). Training for Open-Ended Drilling through a Virtual Reality Simulation. [doi:10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of individual normalized change (c) on a 21-question multiple-choice pre/post quiz between the VR and control groups, analyzed with t-tests and Mann-Whitney-U tests in SPSS. The discussion attributes the gains trend to VR's low-risk environment and visual and spatial representation (14%, 6%, 12% more gain in the three constructs, 8% overall).

> "While these findings are not statistically significant, the VR group scored larger gains both overall and in each category (component identification, safety, set up and operations), which indicates a positive trend."
