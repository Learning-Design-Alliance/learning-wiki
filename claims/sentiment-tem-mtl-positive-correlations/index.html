Samuel Falcon 2023¶

Samuel Falcon, Jaime Leon. (2023). How do teachers engaging messages affect students? A sentiment analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10230-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis (Table 3) among sentiment, TEM and students' MTL, with N = 963 below the diagonal and N = 61 above the diagonal. The article reports positive significant correlations at both levels, with level-2 correlations limited to sentiment–TEM and each with MTL.

"All variables were positively and significantly correlated at level 1 (below the diagonal). However, at level 2 (above the diagonal), only sentiment and TEM, and these with students’ MTL, were significantly correlated, showing a positive correlation."
