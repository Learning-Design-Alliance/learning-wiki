## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zoanetti N 2013

Zoanetti N, Beaves M, Griffin P, Wallace E M. (2013). Fixed or mixed: a comparison of three, four and mixed-option multiple-choice tests in a Fetal Surveillance Education Program. BMC Medical Education 13:35. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level analysis of the four-option form (763 practitioners) using the Tarrant, Ware and Mohammed definition of non-functioning distractors (under 5% selection or positive discrimination); "The average number of non-functioning options per item in the four-option test form was 1.18."

> "The average number of non-functioning options per item in the four-option test form was 1.18."

### Zoanetti N 2013 (2)

Zoanetti N, Beaves M, Griffin P, Wallace E M. (2013). Fixed or mixed: a comparison of three, four and mixed-option multiple-choice tests in a Fetal Surveillance Education Program. BMC Medical Education 13:35. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item characteristic curves for item 45 in both versions, produced with ConQuest software (Figures 1 and 2), showed option D did not behave as a distractor should; "Its removal resulted in a sharp increase in item discrimination" and improved model fit.

> "Its removal resulted in a sharp increase in item discrimination (refer to Table 1) and an improvement in the fit of the data to the item response model (refer to Figure 1 and Figure 2)."
