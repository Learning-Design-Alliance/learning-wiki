### Jonathan D. Perry 2025

Jonathan D. Perry, Toni Sauncy, Susan White, Rachel L Ivie, John Tyler, and Tatiana Erukhimova. (2025). Impact of Outreach on Physics Student Development: Quantitative Results from a National Survey. [doi:10.1103/mj85-87vk](https://doi.org/10.1103/mj85-87vk)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of 704 responses to a national SPS-distributed survey, with respondent and institutional characteristics and factor-analysis factors as predictors. The article reports the outreach-belonging and outreach-mindset relationships as "strongly positive" at the study's p<0.01 threshold; no effect size is printed.

> "Regression models showed student participation in outreach is statistically significantly connected directly to both growth mindset and a sense of belonging. These relationships were observed to be positive"
