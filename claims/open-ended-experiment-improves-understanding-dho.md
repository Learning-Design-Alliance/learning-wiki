## Evidence

### O. I. González-Peña 2021

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, R. Rodríguez-Masegosa, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2021). Effects of a thermal inversion experiment on STEM students learning and application of damped harmonic motion. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.3390/su13020919](https://doi.org/10.3390/su13020919)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative questionnaire study of 26 first-semester STEM students at Tecnologico de Monterrey who completed the 5-week experiment; open-coded responses showed "Almost all the students (24) reported that the thermal inversion experiment helped them to better understand the damped harmonic oscillation". Only two students found it confusing. No effect size is reported.
