## Evidence

### Portilla Ximena A. 2014

Portilla Ximena A., Ballard Parissa J., Adler Nancy E., Boyce W. Thomas, Obradović Jelena. (2014). An Integrative View of School Functioning: Transactions between Self-Regulation, School Engagement, and Teacher-Child Relationship Quality. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12259

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the best-fitting path model (Model 3), gender predicted child behaviors and relationship quality at kindergarten entry: "Boys experienced more conflict with teachers (B = −.29, β = −.17, p < .001)" and girls more closeness and engagement. Gender was not correlated with first-grade academic competence in bivariate relations.

> "Boys experienced more conflict with teachers (B = −.29, β = −.17, p < .001) and more inattention and impulsivity (B = −.24, β = −.37, p < .001). Girls experienced closer relationships with teachers (B = .29, β = .17, p < .001) and more school engagement (B = .13, β = .17, p < .01)."
