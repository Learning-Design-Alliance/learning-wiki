## Evidence

### Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner 2020

Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner. (2020). How Well do Remote Labs Work? A Case Study at Princeton University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

End-of-semester Zoom interviews asked in which lab students thought they learned the most; those choosing the project lab said it made them "understand all details of the lab, and be involved in every step".

> "The students that said they learned the most in the project lab indicated that this was because it made them understand all details of the lab, and be involved in every step."

### Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner 2020 (2)

Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner. (2020). How Well do Remote Labs Work? A Case Study at Princeton University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Asked, given the lab goals of critical thinking about experiments and data-analysis skills, what type of labs they would assign, "75% of the students said the project lab" and the rest a project-virtual mix.

> "For this 75% of the students said the project lab and the remaining 25% said a mix of the project and the virtual experiment lab."

### Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner 2020 (3)

Saumya Shivam and Kasey Wagoner. (2020). How Well do Remote Labs Work? A Case Study at Princeton University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.04499)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the same interviews, "a majority of the students interviewed (75%)" indicated they found the process of the project lab the most interesting, describing troubleshooting and figuring everything out on their own.

> "In addition to responses from these questions, a majority of the students interviewed (75%) indicated that that they found the process of the project lab the most interesting"
