Interaction engagement quantified by counting conversational turns in chat logs during the revision step. Means were 14.100 (SD 8.102) for the experimental group versus 10.386 (SD 7.874) for controls, "d = 0.465".

"The experimental group engaged in significantly more conversational turns with Doubao than did the control group. This difference was statistically significant [ t (93) = 2.261, p = 0.026, d = 0.465]"
