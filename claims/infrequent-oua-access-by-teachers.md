`q2 i?` Log data showed most teachers did not access the dashboard weekly; seven of 59 never accessed it, and interviewed teachers logged in mainly near assignment deadlines. [→ Herodotou 2019](#herodotou-2019)

## Evidence

### Herodotou 2019

Herodotou, C., Rienties, B., Boroowa, A., Zdrahal, Z., & Hlosta, M. (2019). A large-scale implementation of predictive learning analytics in higher education: the teachers' role and perspective. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 1273–1306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09685-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of login logs from 59 teachers (Fig. 5) and six interviewees (Fig. 6) showed access concentrated in early weeks and near submissions. The article states "This infrequent access to OUA data by teachers might have limited the potential effectiveness of OUA interventions."

> "The majority of the participating teachers did not access the dashboard regularly (i.e., every week when predictions were updated), yet they accessed it throughout a course presentation at selected weeks."
