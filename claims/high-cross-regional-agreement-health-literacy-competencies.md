Tsai Han-Yi, Lee Shoou-Yih D., Coleman Cliff, Sørensen Kristine, Tsai Tzu-I. (2024). Health literacy competency requirements for health professionals: a Delphi consensus study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05198-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Delphi Round 1 ratings on a 4-point scale with a 70% consensus cutoff; appropriateness means ranged 1.15–2.70 and importance means 1.00–2.25 (Appendix Table 1). The discussion compares the Taiwanese figure with a previous European study's 90% and concludes "similarities across Western and Eastern countries."

> "In this study, 91.9% consensus was achieved regarding the appropriateness and importance of 57 out of 62 health literacy competency items among a panel of Taiwanese experts. A previous European study had an expert group census of 90%."
