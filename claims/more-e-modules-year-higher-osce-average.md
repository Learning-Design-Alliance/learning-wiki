## Evidence

### Kwant Kelly J. 2015

Kwant Kelly J., Custers Eugene J. F. M., Jongen-Hermus Femke J., Kluijtmans Manon. (2015). Preparation by mandatory E-modules improves learning of practical skills: a quasi-experimental comparison of skill examination results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0376-4

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of total average OSCE scores across all stations between the 2011-2012 and 2012-2013 cohorts, based on 2010 individual student scores. The article reports the average was "significantly higher in the academic year in which more e-modules were available," with t = −3.68, p < 0.01, Cohen's d = 0.16.

> "The average score for all stations in 2011–2012 is 3.51 ± 0.65 (n = 954) and in 2012–2013 3.62 ± 0.69 (n = 1056) (t = −3.68, p < 0,01; Cohen’s d = 0.16)."
