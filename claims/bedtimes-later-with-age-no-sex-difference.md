## Evidence

### Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L 2010

Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L. (2010). Normative data on the sleep habits of Australian children and adolescents. SLEEP. https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.10.1381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-factor ANOVA of bedtimes in the 4032-participant cohort found age (P < 0.0001) and day type (P < 0.0001) significant with a weak day type × age interaction (P = 0.03); the article reports rates of "17 (16-18) min per year of age on school days" and states there were no differences between boys and girls.

> "Bedtimes got later with age (P < 0.0001), at the rate of 17 (16-18) min per year of age on school days, and 16 (14-18) min on non-school days."
