## Evidence

### Radulović and Stojanovic 2015

Branka Radulović, Maja Stojanović. (2015). Determination Instructions Efficiency of Teaching Methods in Teaching Physics in the Case of Teaching Unit „Viscosity. Newtonian and Stokes law“. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 8, Number 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073276

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pedagogical experiment with two parallel groups (control taught traditionally, experimental taught via experiment), equalized by initial test, 125 students from two Novi Sad secondary schools. The article reports a statistically significant difference at p < 0.05 confirmed by "factor analysis of variance (p <  0.05) and Tukey's test"; no effect size is printed.

> "After completion of pedagogical experiment is obtained that the students of the experimental group achieved higher average achievement in the final test than students of the control group. This difference was confirmed by factor analysis of variance (p <  0.05) and Tukey's test ."
