Surjono 2015¶

Herman Dwi Surjono. (2015). The Effects of Multimedia and Learning Style on Student Achievement in Online Electronics Course. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057334

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Abstract-level statement of the study's overall finding, consistent with the separate within-group multilevel analyses reported for the AES and NON-AES groups in the results section. No effect size is printed.

"The findings showed that students in which their multimedia preferences and learning style matched with the way the material presented in online electronics course have higher scores significantly compared to those in which their learning mode were mismatched. The difference happened both in adaptive and non-adaptive online courses."
