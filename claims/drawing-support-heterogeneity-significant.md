## Evidence

### Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele 2025

Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele. (2025). Without Integration, Everything Is Nothing: A Meta-Analysis of the Effectiveness of Instructional Support for Drawing-to-Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10067-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Heterogeneity tests reported with the overall-effect analysis in the random-effects meta-analysis. The article states the heterogeneity "indicated differences between the effect sizes, suggesting the need for moderator analyses"; forest plots are shown in Figs. 4 and 5.

> "with significant heterogeneity for both the comprehension test, Q (15) = 37.99, p < 0.001, and the transfer test results, Q(6) = 28.33, p < 0.001"
