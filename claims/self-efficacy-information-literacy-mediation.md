## Evidence

### Zhang Xiangle 2025

Zhang Xiangle, Tian Hongxia. (2025). Modeling information literacy in Chinese physical education: the roles of technology integration, teaching practices, self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26990-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression mediation model (Table 8) in the same 650-student survey; the SE → IL path reached "a significant estimate of 0.478" with p below 0.001. The article attributes the mechanism to Bandura's Social Cognitive Theory.

> "Model 1, focused on the mediation effect, shows a strong positive association between self-efficacy (SE) and information literacy (IL), with a significant estimate of 0.478 and a p-value below 0.001."
