The commentary summarizes Zhang et al. (2026), a "cluster-randomized trial in authentic classroom settings" administering brief pre-class exercise before instruction. The dissociation between a domain-aligned and a domain-general measure is the empirical basis of the argument. No effect sizes are printed here.

"Their central finding was a selective enhancement of mathematics-specific inhibitory control following instruction, alongside no reliable change in performance on a canonical measure of domain-general interference control (Flanker task)."
