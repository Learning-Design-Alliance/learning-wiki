## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, A., Lian, J., Kuang, X., & Jia, J. (2026). Can theory-driven learning analytics dashboard enhance human-AI collaboration in writing learning? Insights from an empirical experiment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.iheduc.2025.101054](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iheduc.2025.101054)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Epistemic Network Analysis of coded learner-ChatAI dialogue logs (coding framework adapted from Cheng et al., Cohen's Kappa = 0.77); the EG pattern was "Reflective and Evaluative" and the CG "Instrumental and Information -Seeking".

> "A Mann-Whitney U test confirms this visual separation, showing a statistically significant difference between the groups' positions on the X-axis (U = 38.50, p < 0.001, r = 0.83), but not on the Y-axis (U = 228.50, p = 0.94)."
