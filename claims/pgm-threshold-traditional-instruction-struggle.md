### Marshman 2015

Marshman, E. and Singh, C. (2015). A Framework for Understanding the Patterns of Student Difficulties in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article's framework section (Figure 2 schematic) proposes that prior preparation, goals, and motivation can be weighted into a composite PGM score, and that students below the instruction threshold 'will struggle to develop expertise in a traditionally taught physics course that does not take into account individual student's prior knowledge and builds on it.'

> "If a student's PGM is below a certain threshold, it can result in learning difficulties and impact the student's path toward expertise, especially in a traditionally taught course"
