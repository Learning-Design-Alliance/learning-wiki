## Evidence

### Baric 2019

Baric, V., Andreassen, M., Öhman, A., & Hemmingsson, H. (2019). Using an interactive digital calendar with mobile phone reminders by senior people - a focus group study. BMC Geriatrics, 19, 116. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-019-1128-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of the focus group findings on the provided support: the introduction course (approximately 30–90 min) let participants try features at their own pace, and the weekly research-assistant calls provided individual support and emotional reassurance participants greatly valued.

> "A promising finding in this study is that participants stressed that the short introduction course, the written manual, and the weekly phone support (based on the user's needs and questions) were sufficient for delivering instructions and tips on using the technology."
