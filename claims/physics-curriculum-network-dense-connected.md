Manual network analysis of the official Physics curriculum (54 contents in 6 blocks), with relationships coded by the authors. The article reports "a density of 0.29", a single connected component, diameter 2, average path length 1.71, and clustering coefficient 0.65, denoting non-linear, tightly interconnected content.

> "For this subject we found 409 connections between contents out of the 1431 possible. This means a density of 0.29 and that, on average, each piece of content is connected to 15.15 other pieces of content."
