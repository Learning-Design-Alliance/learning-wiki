`q3 i?` ToM skills measured in 5th grade partially mediated all four tested relations, with indirect effects from 0.069 to 0.093. [→ Le Diagon Sarah 2025](#le-diagon-sarah-2025)

## Evidence

### Le Diagon Sarah 2025

Le Diagon Sarah, Jacquel Marie, Van der Henst Jean-Baptiste, Prado Jérôme. (2025). Individual differences in executive functions and theory of mind mediate the relation between academic skills from kindergarten to 5th grade. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analyses using the 5th-grade ToM composite (Story-based Empathy Task plus Reading the Mind in the Eyes), controlling for age and gender. All four confidence intervals excluded zero; the authors report this as a novel finding.

> "We found that ToM skills partially mediated all relations (between early and later math skills: indirect effect = 0.087, 95% CI = [0.01, 0.18], indirect/total effect = 0.18; between early reading skills and later reading skills: indirect effect = 0.069, 95% CI = [0.007, 0.14], indirect/total effect = 0.23; between early math skills and later reading skills: indirect effect = 0.093, 95% CI = [0.02, 0.20], indirect/total effect = 0.27; between early reading skills and later math skills: indirect effect = 0.073, 95% CI = [0.007, 0.17], indirect/total effect = 0.20)."
