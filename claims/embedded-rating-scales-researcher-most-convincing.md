## Evidence

### Colin Lescarret 2022

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square and binomial logistic regression on students' convincingness choice in the experiment, association size "Cramer’s V=.291" (χ²(1)=22.1, p<.001). ERS was a significant predictor (Estimate=1.202, Z=4.641, p<.001, OR=3.326); the instructional video was not (OR=1.149, p=.592); R²CS=.083. The article calls this mixed support for Hypothesis 3a.

> "a significant association between the proportion of students who considered the researcher to be the most convincing interviewee and the factor Embedded Rating Scales was found, χ²(1)=22.1, p<.001, Cramer’s V=.291"
