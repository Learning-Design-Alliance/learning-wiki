# Experts rate the Circle Notation and Qake higher for visualising relative phase and amplitude, with high effect sizes against the Quantum Bead

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Expert ratings differ significantly for relative phase visualisation (Quantum Bead vs Bloch sphere d = 0.64; vs Circle Notation d = 0.60) and for amplitude visualisation (Quantum Bead vs Qake d = 0.76), with Circle Notation and Qake rated higher on average. [→ Qerimi 2025](#qerimi-2025)
`q2 i2` For amplitude, Qake (mean 4.60 ± 0.52) and the Circle Notation (mean 4.50 ± 0.51) were rated higher on average, with high effect sizes. [→ Qerimi 2025 (2)](#qerimi-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Qerimi 2025

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of relative phase visualisation; Friedman test significant (χ2(3) = 15.528, p < .001). Global phase was excluded due to rater disagreement.

> "Post hoc tests showed that these differences were mainly between the Quantum Bead and Bloch sphere representations (p < .01, d = 0.64), and also between the Quantum Bead and Circle Notation (p < .01, d = 0 .60). A high effect size can be assigned to this."

### Qerimi 2025 (2)

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of amplitude visualisation; Friedman test significant (χ2(3) = 29.008, p < .001), with pairwise differences between Quantum Bead and Circle Notation (d = 0.67), Quantum Bead and Qake (d = 0.76), and Qake and Bloch sphere (d = 0.66).

> "Qake (mean: 4.60 ± 0.52) and the Circle Notation (mean: 4.50 ± 0.51) were rated higher on average. A high effect size can be assigned to this."
