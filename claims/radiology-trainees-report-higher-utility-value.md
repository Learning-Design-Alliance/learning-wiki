## Evidence

### Witti MJ 2026

Witti MJ, Coleman J-C, Richters C, Schönberger J, de Ruijter M-A, Stortz A, Bauer M, Kiriakidou R, Stadler M. (2026). Simulation-Based Radiology Training for Medical Technical Assistant Trainees: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/97489

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample 2-tailed t tests comparing specialization groups on motivational variables. Radiology-specialized trainees rated the simulation as considerably more useful (mean 4.29, SD 0.74) than laboratory-specialized trainees (mean 2.79, SD 1.43), "whereas no group differences emerged in intrinsic value or self-efficacy."

> "Radiology-specialized trainees reported higher utility value for the training than laboratory-focused trainees (t46=4.43; P<.001; Cohen d=1.28), whereas no group differences emerged in intrinsic value or self-efficacy."
