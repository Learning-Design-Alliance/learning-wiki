Emergent thematic coding of interviews, reflections, and photovoice data from the five-student cohort. The theme that "it's not just quantum computing" was raised by four of five students; Owen said the project "opened my eyes to the fact that there's also a lot of other preliminary stuff" and "that's where all the jobs are."

> "This idea was brought up by four out of the five students at some point throughout the course, with the first mention of this theme occurring at the end of the first semester of the course."
