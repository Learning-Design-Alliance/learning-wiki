## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 25 UK A100 programmes, total written assessment volume ranged from 1000 to 3200 minutes (median 1900 min), with greater variation in preclinical years (SD = 500) than clinical years (SD = 300). [→ Devine OP 2015](#devine-op-2015)

## Evidence

### Devine OP 2015

Devine OP, Harborne AC, McManus IC. (2015). Assessment at UK medical schools varies substantially in volume, type and intensity and correlates with postgraduate attainment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0428-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational survey of 25 of 27 UK A100 programmes, using guided-questionnaire interviews with student representatives validated with school assessment departments. The article reports "range = 1000–3200" minutes and greater preclinical variation (SD = 500 vs SD = 300).

> "The median volume of written assessment across an entire medical course was 1900 min (Fig. 1a; mean = 2000, SD = 600, LQ = 1500, UQ = 2400, range = 1000–3200)"
