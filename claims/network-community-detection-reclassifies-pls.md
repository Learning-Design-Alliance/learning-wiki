Descriptive comparison from the detected course network of 8905 students; the study reports "the percentage of female students enrolled in all physics courses (including PLS) is 40%" versus around 25% in Physics-Maths, with no inferential test printed.

> "For example, the percentage of female students enrolled in all physics courses (including PLS) is 40%. Our community detection shows that female students only make up around 25% of the main Physics-Maths community. The difference between these percentages is substantial, and raises important implications for the way in which universities report the number of students studying in different disciplines."
