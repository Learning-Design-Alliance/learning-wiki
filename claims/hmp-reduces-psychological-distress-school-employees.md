## Evidence

### Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered intention-to-treat piecewise linear mixed effects models on N=662 randomized Wisconsin school system employees. The HMP group showed "significantly larger reductions in psychological distress" versus wait-list control at post-intervention (d=−0.53).

> "the HMP group demonstrated significantly larger reductions in psychological distress over the intervention period compared to the WLC (d=−0.53 CI [−0.69 to −0.38], p<.001; Figure 3)"

### Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022 (2)

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Same intention-to-treat mixed effects models at the three-month follow-up (T6) in the N=662 sample. Assignment to the HMP "continued to predict significantly larger reductions in distress at T6 compared to the WLC" (d=−0.34).

> "Consistent with our hypothesis, assignment to the HMP continued to predict significantly larger reductions in distress at T6 compared to the WLC (d=−0.34 [−0.49 to −0.19], p<.001)"
