### Hyoungwook Jin 2026

Hyoungwook Jin, Weirui Peng, Jieun Han, Q. Vera Liao, and Xu Wang. (2026). HelpCoach: Scaffolding Targeted AI Help-Seeking During Problem-Solving. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28918 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects study with 40 college students completing three Vue.js programming tasks with a GenAI chatbot, HelpCoach (n=20) versus Baseline (n=20). The article reports a significantly higher proportion of specific questions with a printed effect size of 1.262 (p = .001) for initial drafts.

> "HelpCoachparticipants formulated a significantly higher proportion of specific questions (𝑝=. 001, effect size = 1.262), counting only their initial question drafts before system interventions for that question."
