## Evidence

### Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports Verga and Kotz's neuroimaging comparison of simulated partner-based versus individual-based learning of word meanings. Right SMG was more activated in partner-based learning, and right lingual gyrus and caudate nucleus activity correlated with temporal coordination between learner and partner during simulated interactive learning.

> "participants with higher learning outcomes showed higher activity in the right IFG during an interactive learning condition but not during an individualized non-interactive learning condition"
