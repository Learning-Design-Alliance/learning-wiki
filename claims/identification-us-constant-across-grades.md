### Mario Carretero & Floor van Alphen 2014

Mario Carretero & Floor van Alphen. (2014). Do Master Narratives Change Among High School Students? A Characterization of How National History Is Represented. Cognition and Instruction, 32(3), 290-312. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2014.919298

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of identification in the same 38-student interview sample, coded by use of us/we/our versus them/they/their in the spontaneous first part of the interview. The article reports 56% 'us' in 8th grade and 65% in 11th grade.

> "The first person plural was used in the past tense in 61% of the narratives, with no significant difference between 8th graders and 11th graders, χ 2 (1, N = 38) = 0.354, p = .55."
