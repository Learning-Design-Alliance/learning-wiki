q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Paired samples t-tests within the experimental group (n = 33) comparing pre-test and post-test means on NOSS and SKS; the article reports "the effect sizes (r NOSS = .83; r SKS = .61) of this increase are in high level."

"It has been found that experimental group has a significant increase in post -test results (t NOSS = 12.036; p < 0.05; t SKS = 6.396; p < 0.05) and the effect sizes (r NOSS = .83; r SKS = .61) of this increase are in high level."
