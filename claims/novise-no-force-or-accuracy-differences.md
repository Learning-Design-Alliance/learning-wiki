## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary metric analysis from the same construct validity comparisons. The discussion reports "no significant difference in terms of overall clipping and cutting accuracy" and no significant diathermy accuracy difference; the authors interpret this as novices operating safely but inefficiently.

> "Experts and novices did not differ in the forces that they applied to the tissues with the tip of the endoscope, endoscope shaft velocity or acceleration. This suggests that novices knew how to manipulate the endoscope safely, albeit not as efficiently as experts."
