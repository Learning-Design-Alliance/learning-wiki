# HIPPY children scored significantly higher than comparison children on Reading and Math TAKS scale scores in all four grades

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests compared Reading TAKS scale scores for 516 HIPPY and 516 matched comparison children. Statistically significant differences favoring the HIPPY group were found at every grade level examined, with t-values ranging from 2.69 to 7.26.

> "HIPPY children outperformed comparison children in all four grades (see Table 3): 3rd grade, t = 3.27, p = .001; 5th grade, t = 7.26, p = .000; 7th grade, t = 2.69, p = .003; and 9th grade, t = 3.99, p = .000."

### Brown 2014 (2)

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests compared Math TAKS scale scores for 516 HIPPY and 516 matched comparison children. Statistically significant differences favoring the HIPPY group were found in all four grades: 5th grade t = 7.90, p = .000; 7th grade t = 3.08, p = .008; and 9th grade t = 3.86, p = .000.

> "According to independent samples t-tests, HIPPY children outperformed comparison children in math."
