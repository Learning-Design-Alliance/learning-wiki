# Being a teacher at a school with higher collective efficacy in classroom management is associated with higher self-reported classroom management over and above individual self-efficacy

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A unique context effect of collective efficacy in classroom management on self-reported classroom management (β = .57, p < .001) was found, meaning the same teacher reports better classroom management at a more efficacious school. [→ Holzberger 2021](#holzberger-2021)

## Evidence

### Holzberger 2021

Holzberger, D., & Prestele, E. (2021). Teacher Self-Efficacy and Self-Reported Instructional Quality: A Multilevel Perspective on the Role of School Characteristics. Learning and Instruction, 76, 101513. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101513

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Context-effect multilevel regression on Austrian TALIS 2018 data (246 schools). Only collective efficacy in classroom management showed a significant unique context effect (β = .57, p < .001); external school characteristics (collaboration, participation, leadership) were non-significant.

> "With regard to classroom management, there was a specific positive association with unique collective efficacy in classroom management (collective efficacy in instruction: b(SE) = 0.01(.09), β =.02, p = .87; efficacy in classroom management: b(SE) = 0.37(.09), β = .57, p < .001)"
