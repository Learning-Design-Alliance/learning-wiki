# Students rated the helpfulness of smartphones in experimental tasks comparably to Python in programming tasks, with no significant difference

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Ratings of use of technologies did not differ significantly between smartphones in Exp-tasks (M = 3.71) and Python in Pro-tasks (M = 3.54). [→ Lahme 2025](#lahme-2025)

## Evidence

### Lahme 2025

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Weekly-survey use-of-technologies scale (N = 87): smartphones in Exp-tasks rated M = 3.71 (SD = 0.63) and Python in Pro-tasks M = 3.54 (SD = 0.79) on a 5-point scale; Wilcoxon test showed "no significant difference" (p = 0.24, r = 0.13).

> "N = 87 students rated the use of technologies, i.e. the helpfulness and usefulness of smartphones in the Exp-tasks, as M = 3.71 (SD = 0.63) on a scale of 1 (strongly disagree)
to 5 (strongly agree). Regarding Python in the Pro-tasks, the rating is M = 3.54 (SD = 0.79). Wilcoxon test shows no significant difference (Z = 1.18, p = 0.24, r = 0.13)."
