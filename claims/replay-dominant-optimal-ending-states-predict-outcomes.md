## Evidence

### Shan Zhang 2026

Shan Zhang, Siddhartha Pradhan, Ji-Eun Lee, Ashish Gurung, and Anthony F. Botelho. 2026. Let Me Try Again: Examining Replay Behavior by Tracing Students' Latent Problem-Solving Pathways. LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions (N = 777, BH-FDR adjusted) on FH2T state proportions with pretest covariates found Replay-dominant engagement predicted conceptual (b = 1.20, p < .05), flexibility (b = 1.10, p < .05), and state test (b = 0.63, p < .01) scores; post-math (b = 1.87) was not significant (p = .066).

> "Students who spent more time in State 4 (Replay-dominant) showed positive associations with multiple outcomes, predicting higher conceptual (𝑏= 1.20,𝑝<. 05) and flexibility scores (𝑏= 1.10,𝑝<. 05), as well as stronger performance on the state test (𝑏= 0.63,𝑝<. 01), compared to the Incomplete group."

### Shan Zhang 2026 (2)

Shan Zhang, Siddhartha Pradhan, Ji-Eun Lee, Ashish Gurung, and Anthony F. Botelho. 2026. Let Me Try Again: Examining Replay Behavior by Tracing Students' Latent Problem-Solving Pathways. LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same regression models showed Optimal-ending engagement predicted higher conceptual (b = 0.61, p < .05) and state test (b = 0.34, p < .01) scores; a flexibility effect (p = 0.041) was no longer significant after p-value correction (p = 0.057).

> "In addition, students who spent more time in State 3 (Optimal-ending) demonstrated higher conceptual scores (𝑏= 0.61,𝑝<. 05) and performed better on the state test (𝑏= 0.34,𝑝<. 01) in relation to the Incomplete group."
