Evidence¶

Esther Ziegler 2021¶

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Univariate ANOVA on the ratio of correctly solved practice problems (process measure 3) across the six worksheets, from the same MANCOVA; the article reports F(1,77) = 8.22, p = 0.005, η2p = 0.10 in favor of PS-I.

"On subsequent practice problems, the effect reversed to a significant effect in favor of the PS-I condition, F(1,77) = 8.22, p = 0.005, η2p = 0.10, but no significant effect on immediate learning tests"

Esther Ziegler 2021 (2)¶

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Sub-analysis comparing the first two worksheets with the later four; the article reports t(79) = 2.35, p = 0.021, d = 0.52 on the first two worksheets and suggests there might have been an influence of the pretest on initial practice problems only.

"we found a significant difference between the PS-I and the I-PS conditions on the first two worksheets, t(79) = 2.35, p = 0.021, d = 0.52, distinct from later worksheets and the above reported practice problem outcomes."
