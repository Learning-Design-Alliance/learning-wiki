Descriptive analysis of the four achievement means and variances in the BURN11 dataset. The article attributes the drop in the last measure to the increasing difficulty of learning unit 4, where the full Xenograde System was presented.

> "Figure 1 shows the means and variances, indicating that the achievement growth process fluctuated across the four observation points as a sinusoid-like pattern, with a rather flat growth in the first three achievement measures, and with a pronounced drop in the last achievement measure."
