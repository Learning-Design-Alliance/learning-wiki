`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Email follow-up survey of 59 institutions that downloaded the show (35 responded, 15-28 February 2023), asking how they had used or planned to use it. The article reports 26 had used the show and nine had not yet presented it.

> "Despite the positive feedback and high uptake of the show for specially planned BVI and other SEND events, we are only aware of two planetariums that have incorporated this show into their regular programs of public events for all audiences (e.g., a daily, weekly or monthly regular planetarium program)."
