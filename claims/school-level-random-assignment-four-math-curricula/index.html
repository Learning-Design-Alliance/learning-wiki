The report describes its own evaluation design: a large-scale national study comparing four early elementary math curricula in disadvantaged schools, in which "a school-level random assignment design" is used to estimate relative effects. No effect sizes are printed because the study was in progress.

"Experimental methods are being used to evaluate the relative effects of the curricula, using a school-level random assignment design."
