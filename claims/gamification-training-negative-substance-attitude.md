## Evidence

### Taghipour Esmaeel 2023

Taghipour Esmaeel, Vizeshfar Fatemeh, Zarifsanaiey Nahid. (2023). The effect of gamification-based training on the knowledge, attitudes, and academic achievement of male adolescents in preventing substance and internet addiction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04858-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing intervention and control groups on the Attitude to Substances Questionnaire at three time points. The intervention group's total substance-attitude mean fell from 81.07 ± 15.97 pre-test to 33.21 ± 7.59 post-test, described as a "more negative attitude toward substances".

> "Specifically, the intervention group's attitude toward substances significantly decreased, indicating the development of a more negative attitude toward substances (P < 0.001)."
