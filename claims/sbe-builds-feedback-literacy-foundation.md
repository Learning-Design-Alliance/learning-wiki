### Mulhall Claire M 2025 (2)

(2025). “It’s Still Exposure Just in a Slightly Different Way”—Understanding the Contribution of Simulation to Developing Physical Therapist Skills in Ireland: An Interpretive Description Study. Physical Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1093/ptj/pzaf102

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant quote illustrating the feedback paradox: a high-achieving, anxious student perceived constructive feedback as catastrophizing, and educators reported problems when students were overwhelmed or defensive. Authors suggest simulation may help reframe feedback as a learning tool.

> "The very high-achieving person who was quite anxious and they’re so super anxious to do well that any feedback that’s constructive is seen as nearly catastrophizing."
