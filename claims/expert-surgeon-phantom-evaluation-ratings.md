## Evidence

### Hong Dayeong 2021

Hong Dayeong, Kim HaeKang, Kim Taehun, Kim Yong-Hee, Kim Namkug. (2021). Development of patient specific, realistic, and reusable video assisted thoracoscopic surgery simulator using 3D printing and pediatric computed tomography images. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-85738-w

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation evaluation in which one expert thoracic surgeon with over 15 years of experience performed VATS for EATEF on the 3D-printed phantom and on a conventional vendor-provided simulator, then rated three questionnaire items. The surgeon also commented that pre-operation planning was easier and surgery time reduced.

> "Based on the questionnaire, the surgeon responded as (a) prediction of the effectiveness before using our phantom, 3; (b) effectiveness after using our phantom comparing to the conventional one, 5; and (c) the reality of the EATEF simulator, 5."
