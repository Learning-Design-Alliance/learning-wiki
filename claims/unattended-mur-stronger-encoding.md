## Evidence

### Jacqueline Phelps 2022

Jacqueline Phelps, Adam Attaheri & Mirjana Bozic. (2022). How bilingualism modulates selective attention in children. Scientific Reports 12:6381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-09989-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of unattended-stream reconstruction accuracy (three conditions) in 48 children. The unattended MuR condition exceeded English and Latin distractors in both groups; no group main effect or interaction emerged.

> "the between group comparisons showed a significantly higher encoding of unattended envelopes in the English–MuR condition for monolinguals compared to bilinguals [t = 4.05, p < 0.001, d = 0.11]."
