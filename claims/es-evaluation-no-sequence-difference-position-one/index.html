Franco Londra 2025¶

Franco Londra, Magalí Martínez, Gaston Saux. (2025). Sources within sources: The dynamic influence of multiple documents trustworthiness when evaluating and using an embedded source. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09427-z

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-model analysis of first-position ES ratings among 72 participants. The article reports "the trustworthiness evaluations of the ES did not vary between sequences, all p >.294", with estimated mean 4.36 (SE = 0.36); no effect size is printed.
