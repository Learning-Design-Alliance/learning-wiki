## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoran 2024

Wang Xiaoran, Liu Jiangheng, Jia Shuwei, Hou Chunmei, Jiao Runsheng, Yan Yan, Ma Tengchuang, Zhang Ying, Liu Yanyan, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng, Zhu Hui, Liu Xiao-Yu. (2024). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire crossover analysis in the 2022 cohort relating perceived learning burden to teaching-method preference (Fig. 8 reports ES = 0.261, P < 0.05 by chi-squared test for R×C table). Approximately half of students reported hybrid teaching heightened their burden.

> "students who felt increased learning burden from hybrid teaching method preferred to choose offline teaching (Strongly increased, 75.0%; Increased 58.8%), while students who thought online teaching didn’t increase or reduce their learning burden preferred to choose hybrid teaching (Reduced, 50.0%; Fair, 52.3%)"
