## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gross Megan 2014

Gross Megan, Buac Milijana, Kaushanskaya Margarita. (2014). Conceptual scoring of receptive and expressive vocabulary measures in simultaneous and sequential bilingual children. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1044/2014_AJSLP-13-0026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of English receptive conceptual scores in the 98-child sample with SES covariate; sequential bilinguals (n = 19) remained significantly below monolinguals and simultaneous bilinguals, as quoted; no effect size printed for this contrast.

> "Sequential bilinguals’ conceptual scores were significantly lower than monolinguals’ scores, p < .001, and simultaneous bilinguals’ conceptual scores, p = .008."
