### Heindrich 2025

Heindrich, L., Consul, S., & Lieder, F. (2025). An Intelligent Tutor for Planning in Large Partially Observable Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 35, 3095–3127. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00493-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered online experiment (AsPredicted RL3_YDD) with 330 Proliﬁc participants randomly assigned to Choice Tutor, Dummy Tutor, or No Tutor conditions. After 12 training trials with graduated environments, all completed 10 unassisted test trials in a 60-node environment. The article states the tutor "is highly effective at improving people's ability to make good decisions."

> "A prereg-istered experiment with 330 participants demonstrated that the new intelligent tutor is highly effective at improving people's ability to make good decisions in partially observable environments."
