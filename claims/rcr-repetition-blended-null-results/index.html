Evidence¶

Johannes Katsarov 2021¶

Johannes Katsarov, Roberto Andorno, André Krom, Mariëtte van den Hoven. (2021). Effective Strategies for Research Integrity Training—a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:935–955. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09630-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Hypothesis H7 tested in the meta-regression of the 30-study sample; the article also reports that the number of treated cases had no significant impact on either learning outcome. No effect size is printed for this null contrast.

"In contrast to our hypothesis, courses that challenged learners to practice their abilities repeatedly did not appear to be more effective than courses with little or no repetition."

Johannes Katsarov, Roberto Andorno, André Krom, Mariëtte van den Hoven. (2021). Effective Strategies for Research Integrity Training—a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:935–955. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09630-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Hypothesis H8 tested against pooled outcomes in the meta-regression; the authors note a possible explanation is the small number of studies that employed blended learning. No effect size is printed for this null contrast.

"Against our predictions, courses that employed blended learning did not tend to be more effective than pure online or pure face-to-fac e courses."
