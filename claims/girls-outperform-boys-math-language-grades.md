`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive comparison of self-reported grades among 1,658 boys and 1,730 girls in the I.Family cohort using the five-level ECTS-harmonized scale; printed percentages and chi-square p-values are copied from the Results text and Table 1.

> "girls outperformed boys by approximately 5 percentage points in mathematics and 13 percentage points in language. Consistently, the chi-square tests based on the five-level scale confirmed statistically significant differences between boys and girls in both mathematics (p = 0.015) and language (p < 0.001)"
