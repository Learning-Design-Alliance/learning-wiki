### Cody T. Havard & Skylar S. Workman 2018

Cody T. Havard & Skylar S. Workman. (2018). Adventures With Sport Rivalry Man: Initial Testing of a Classroom Method Using Comics and Cartoons to Teach about Rivalry and Fan Behavior. The University of Memphis. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1200586

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate results from the MANCOVA showed differences on Out-Group Sportsmanship (F(1, 28) = 5.053, p = .033). The exposed group's OS mean was 4.31 (SD = 1.20) versus 3.42 (SD = 0.95) for controls; the article reports "rival fans behaved more poorly". No effect size was printed.

> "Participants exposed to the comic strips or cartoons reported that their rival fans behaved more poorly (M = 4.31, SD = 1.20) but experienced less satisfaction when their favorite team beat the rival (M = 6.12, SD = 0.93) than those not exposed to the comic strips or cartoons"
