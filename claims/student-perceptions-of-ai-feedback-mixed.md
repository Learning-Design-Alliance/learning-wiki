## Evidence

### Ning Yu 2025

Ning Yu, Jie Zhang, Sandeep Mitra, Rebecca Smith, Adam Rich. (2025). AI-Educational Development Loop (AI-EDL): A Conceptual Framework to Bridge AI Capabilities with Classical Educational Theories. State University of New York (SUNY) Brockport. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open coding of student self-reflections responding to how students described their experience with the AI feedback system; themes included balance and constructiveness, clarity and specificity, encouragement to elaborate, immediacy, helpfulness, and positive learning experience and growth.

> "Many students reported real-time feedback as a significant benefit, allowing for immediate reflection and revision."

### Ning Yu 2025 (2)

Ning Yu, Jie Zhang, Sandeep Mitra, Rebecca Smith, Adam Rich. (2025). AI-Educational Development Loop (AI-EDL): A Conceptual Framework to Bridge AI Capabilities with Classical Educational Theories. State University of New York (SUNY) Brockport. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative themes also included concerns about clarity and specificity (e.g., AI not recognizing equivalent answers and prompting to elaborate further), technical challenges such as unsaved answers, and requests for an accessible feedback archive.

> "Students expressed a range of emotions—from appreciation and curiosity to frustration and anxiety—highlighting the affective dimension of AI integration in education and showing the emotional complexity when interacting with generative AI."
