Evidence¶

Potvin et al. 2017¶

Patrice Potvin, Abdelkrim Hasni, and Ousmane Sy. (2017). Using inquiry-based interventions to improve secondary students' interest in science and technology. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 5, No. 3, 262-270. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Using+inquiry-based+interventions+to+improve+secondary+students+interest+in+science+and+technology

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre/post comparison of gains on the six-item interest subconstruct (Cronbach's α = 0.89) between experimental (N = 78) and control (N = 60) groups; the article reports a "positive and statistically significant effect" with a "medium" effect size (Table 2 prints θ = 0.506, p ≤ 0.005).

"The results presented in Table 2 suggest that the intervention had a positive and statistically significant effect compared with equivalent contexts where the interventions were not implemented. The effect size between the two treatments can be considered as medium, according to Cohen's standards (Cohen, 1988)."
