## Evidence

### Lahme 2025

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Weekly-survey analysis in a first-year mechanics course comparing mean overall task ratings (1–10 scale) of nine Exp-tasks and three Pro-tasks via Mann-Whitney-U test; the article reports "no significant difference" with p= 0.46 and r= 0.24. Ratings ranged from M = 5.6 for Pro 1 to M = 7.1 for Pro 2.

> "Mann-Whitney-U test shows no significant difference between the average ratings of the nine Exp-tasks and three Pro-tasks (U = 9.00, Z= 0.83, p= 0.46, r= 0.24)."
