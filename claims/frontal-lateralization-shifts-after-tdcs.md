## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xie 2024

Xie, C., Zhang, S., Qiao, X., & Hao, N. (2024). Transcranial direct current stimulation targeting the bilateral IFG alters cognitive processes during creative ideation. npj Science of Learning, 9, 75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00285-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on weighted frontal degrees across the 80 alpha-band models per group; the quote reports the significant group×hemisphere interaction with ηp 2 = 0.274. Post hoc, Sham showed right > left frontal activity while "L+R− exhibited a reversed pattern" (t237 = 9.17, pbonferroni < 0.001), and L−R+ showed no hemispheric difference.

> "as Fig.4a depicts, for the alpha band, there was a signi ﬁcant interaction between group and hemi- sphere ( F2,237 = 44.78, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.274)"

### Xie 2024 (2)

Xie, C., Zhang, S., Qiao, X., & Hao, N. (2024). Transcranial direct current stimulation targeting the bilateral IFG alters cognitive processes during creative ideation. npj Science of Learning, 9, 75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00285-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on weighted frontal degrees across beta-band models; the quote reports the significant group×hemisphere interaction with ηp 2 = 0.109. Post hoc, Sham showed left > right activity, the stimulation groups showed no hemisphere effect, and "the degree of left frontal activity in the L−R+ group was signiﬁcantly lower than that in the Sham group" (t237 = 5.84, pbonferroni < 0.001).

> "For the beta band (Fig. 4b), an interaction between group and hemi- sphere was also conﬁrmed (F2,237 = 14.40, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.109)"
