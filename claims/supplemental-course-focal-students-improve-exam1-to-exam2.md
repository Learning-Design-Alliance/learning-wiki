## Evidence

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of lecture exam scores for 54 focal and 457 control students in Fall 2014 (BIO 115). Planned contrasts showed "focal students improved relative to control students between exam 1 and exam 2," with no further relative change on later exams.

> "planned contrasts revealed that focal students improved relative to control students between exam 1 and exam 2 (F(1, 504) = 30.51, p < 0.0001; Figure 1) but that relative performance did not change between exams 2 and 3 (F(1, 504) = 0.09, p = 0.7604"

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017 (2)

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of exam scores for 44 focal and 506 control students in Spring 2015 (BIO 116). The contrast between exams 1 and 2 was significant; trajectories between exams 2 and 3 were "indistinguishable."

> "planned contrasts revealed that focal students improved relative to their peers who did not enroll between exams 1 and 2 (F(1, 535) = 6.78, p = 0.0095; Figure 1) but that the trajectories for focal and control students between exams 2 and 3 were indistinguishable"
