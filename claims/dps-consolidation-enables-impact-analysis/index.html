Case-study interview finding about an emerging vendor partnership under the consolidated tool list. The report quotes a core team member explaining that "with a narrowed amount we can have those conversations" about effectiveness; no results of the comparison are yet reported.

"For example, a partnership with one vendor is creating the opportunity for the district to systematically compare app usage with student data to see if the tool is truly effective."
