## Evidence

### Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja 2025

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja. (2025). Different Topics, Similar Patterns: The Consistency of Learners and Learning Across Interactive STEM Simulations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00512-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of pre-test to conceptualized-model transitions (Sankey diagram, Fig. 7) in the study of 135 undergraduates found systematic-cluster learners reached quantitative understanding of simpler variables more often, and "students in the systematic clusters learned more than their free counterparts regardless of their incoming knowledge levels".

> "students in the systematic clusters learned more than their free counterparts regardless of their incoming knowledge levels, be it none or qualitative."
