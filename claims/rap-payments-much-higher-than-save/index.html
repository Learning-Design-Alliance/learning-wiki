The brief reports a TICAS analysis of the RAP plan: for the named family scenario, "a $440 payment under RAP but only a $36 payment the SAVE plan." No effect size is printed; the figures are illustrative payment amounts.

"An analysis of the RAP plan by The Institute for College Access and Success (TICAS) found a family of four with two dependent children and an adjusted gross income (AGI) of $81,000 would have a $440 payment under RAP but only a $36 payment the SAVE plan."
