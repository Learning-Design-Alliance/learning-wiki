f1-partnership¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview and observation study of the first five-district edtech OBC cohort. Districts described joint contract creation; one leader said, "We sat with the edtech vendor from day one."

"Overall, districts reported meaningful partnerships with their providers that supported joint contract development"

f1-population¶

Noakes, S., Shell, A., & Vollavanh, A. (2026, March). Instructional coherence and shared responsibilities: OBC's impact on EdTech procurement. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/288

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview-based finding on population decisions across the cohort; Table 3 lists each district's student population, grades, and eligibility criteria such as emergent bilingual status or below-percentile literacy scores.

"All of the districts in this cohort focused their contracts on students who needed additional support, either because they performed below average or they were emergent language learners"

f1-outcomes-challenge¶

Noakes, S., Shell, A., & Vollavanh, A. (2026, March). Instructional coherence and shared responsibilities: OBC's impact on EdTech procurement. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/288

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding on outcomes selection; the report notes assessment selection "required a new internal process" and that most districts ultimately selected state assessments (Table 4).

"Many districts struggled with the first step of outcomes decision-making: selecting the assessment they would use to evaluate payment outcomes"
