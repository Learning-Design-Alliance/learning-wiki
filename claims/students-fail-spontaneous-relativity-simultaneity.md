## Evidence

### Rachel E. Scherr 2001

Rachel E. Scherr, Peter S. Shaffer, and Stamatis Vokos. (2001). Student understanding of time in special relativity: simultaneity and reference frames. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1371254

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview task with 7 physics graduate students who had undergraduate instruction in special relativity. Only one "recognized spontaneously the relativity of simultaneity"; the rest drew identical time coordinates in both frames.

> "only one recognized spontaneously the relativity of simultaneity in this context. All the others indicated on their spacetime diagrams and in their verbal explanations that the two eruptions had identical vertical (time) coordinates in the ground frame"
