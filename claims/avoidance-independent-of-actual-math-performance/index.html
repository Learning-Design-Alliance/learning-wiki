Evidence¶

Jie Liu 2025¶

Jie Liu, Zhifeng Li, Jiawang Yang, Hao He & Fang Cui. (2025). The neurocognitive mechanism underlying math avoidance among math anxious people. npj Science of Learning 10:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00343-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of null arithmetic-ability differences: the printed t-tests show no group difference in calculation ability (t(59) = 0.15, p = 0.88 in-scanner; t(64) = 0.06, p = 0.95 pre-experimental), supporting "the motivation to engage in math related task was independent of the actual math perfor- mance". Note the sentence's opening clause contradicts its own t-tests; the printed statistics show no difference.

"Notably, we observed signiﬁcant differences in calculating abilities between the two groups, neither in pre-experi mental computational tasks nor in computational tasks during the experiment, suggesting the motivation to engage in math related task was independent of the actual math perfor- mance, its primary inﬂuencing factor maybe the subjective estimation of task difﬁculty."
