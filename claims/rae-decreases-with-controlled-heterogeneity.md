## Subclaims
`q3 i0` When the effects of SES and type of institution are controlled, RAE decreases; the high-performing subsample with lower score dispersion showed a lower RAE value (-.030) than the low-performing subsample (Cv = .081, RAE = -.044). [→ Navarro Juan-José 2015](#navarro-juan-jose-2015)

## Evidence

### Navarro Juan-José 2015

Navarro Juan-José, García-Rubio Javier, Olivares Pedro R. (2015). The Relative Age Effect and Its Influence on Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141895

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup SEM analyses on the full Chilean SIMCE sample and performance-based subsamples; the discussion reports the Pearson coefficient of variation was .059 in the high-scoring subsample versus .081 in the low-scoring subsample, paralleling RAE values of -.030 and -.044.

> "the data show that when the effects of SES and type of institution are controlled, namely with decreasing heterogeneity, they also decrease RAE."
