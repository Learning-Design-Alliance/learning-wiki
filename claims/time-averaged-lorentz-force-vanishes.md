# Exact solutions of the electron's equation of motion show the velocity and B-field are functionally orthogonal, so the time-averaged magnetic force vanishes and no net force is exerted

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The time average of the product of velocity and magnetic field vanishes, and the time averages of both velocity derivatives vanish to all orders, so a net force on the particle is impossible in this treatment. [→ Tony Rothman and Stephen Boughn 2008](#tony-rothman-and-stephen-boughn-2008)

## Evidence

### Tony Rothman and Stephen Boughn 2008

Tony Rothman and Stephen Boughn. (2008). The Lorentz Force and the Radiation Pressure of Light. https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1310 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0807.1310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0807.1310)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation: solving the coupled first-order equations for the electron velocity gives exact solutions whose averages contradict the textbook claim; the authors add that the time averages of both velocity derivatives vanish to all orders, so "it is in fact impossible to exert a net force on the particle".

> "the velocity and magne tic ﬁeld are functionally orthogonal and consequently the time average of the ir product vanishes"
