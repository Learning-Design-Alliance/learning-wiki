Cohort counts of retained eighth graders across the seven CPS cohorts, shown in Figure 2. The report notes retention declined to 9.3 percent with the fourth cohort as achievement increased and more students were promoted despite not meeting the cutoff.

"After the policy was initiated, retention rates surged in the first year to 8.3 percent. Be- cause the cutoff scores for promo- tion were set higher each year, retention rates continued to rise with the next two cohorts, peak- ing at 11.1 percent."
