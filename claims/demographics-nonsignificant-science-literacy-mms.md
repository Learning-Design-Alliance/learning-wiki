### Bayu Setiaji 2023 (2)

Using multilevel modeling to evaluate science literacy and technology course of the Indonesian non-science students. Identifier: none printed in text. [doi:10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457](https://doi.org/10.15294/jpii.v12i1.41457)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA screening (Table 3) found significant mean differences only for high school major (F = 5.182, p = 0.024) and mother education (F = 2.245, p = 0.042) on the affective measure; the article reports gender, admission pathway, funding, scholarship, residence, father education and parents' income were non-significant.
