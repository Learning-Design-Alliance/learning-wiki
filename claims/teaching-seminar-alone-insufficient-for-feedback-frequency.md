## Evidence

### Andrews 2011

Andrews, A., Bobo, L., & Spurlock, A. (2011). Use of video feedback in the training of pre-service teachers. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. (no URL printed in the article text). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056349

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized eight-week experiment, the seminar-only group (Group Two) and control group (Group Three) showed no significant pairwise difference in total feedback in Table 3; the article states "provided similar amounts of feedback to their students". Equivalence was not formally tested.
