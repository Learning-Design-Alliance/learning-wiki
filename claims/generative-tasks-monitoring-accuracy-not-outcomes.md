# Generative and visualizing tasks improve metacomprehension and monitoring accuracy but show small or null effects on learning outcomes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A meta-analysis of generative tasks found a medium positive effect on metacomprehension accuracy and a small positive effect on learning. [→ Tina Seufert 2020](#tina-seufert-2020)
`q2 i?` Visualizing activities improved monitoring accuracy only when learners were instructed to focus on relational and structural information, and did not improve learning outcomes. [→ Tina Seufert 2020 (2)](#tina-seufert-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Tina Seufert 2020

Tina Seufert. (2020). Building Bridges Between Self-Regulation and Cognitive Load—an Invitation for a Broad and Differentiated Attempt. Educational Psychology Review, 32, 1151–1162. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09574-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Prinz, Golke, and Witwer, a meta-analysis on generative tasks: "a medium positive effect on metacomprehension accuracy" while "the effect on learning itself was also positive but rather small." No effect sizes are printed in the review.

> "The study of Prinz, Golke, and Witwer (this issue) presents a meta-analysis on the effects of such generative tasks, and they revealed a medium positive effect on metacomprehension accuracy. The effect on learning itself was also positive but rather small."

### Tina Seufert 2020 (2)

Tina Seufert. (2020). Building Bridges Between Self-Regulation and Cognitive Load—an Invitation for a Broad and Differentiated Attempt. Educational Psychology Review, 32, 1151–1162. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09574-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing van de Pol et al., that visualization activities improved monitoring accuracy only under direct instruction to focus on "relational and structural information in the text to be visualized," with no learning-outcome improvement and unreliable restudy decisions.

> "These generative activities also turned out to improve monitoring accuracy, if learners were directly instructed to focus on relational and structural information in the text to be visualized. However, they did not improve learning outcomes, and the cues could also not always be used to make the right decisions on which text to restudy."
