## Evidence

### Yuan Liu 2024

Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Seed-based whole-brain resting-state fMRI analysis with S1M1_L, SMA_L, S1M1_R, SMA_R seeds, compared across groups pre and post training. "A signiﬁcant TIME × GROUP interaction effect was found in the FC between S1M1_L and Frontal_Mid_L", with significant between-group change at post (p = 0.0014).

> "A signiﬁcant TIME × GROUP interaction effect was found in the FC between S1M1_L and Frontal_Mid_L (Fig.3a and Supplementary Table 1) (F (1, 17) = 10.45,p = 0.0049). Post-hoc tests showed a signiﬁcant increase after training only for the BCI-SRF group (Post vs Pre: p = 0.0041)"
