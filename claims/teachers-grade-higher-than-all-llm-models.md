Descriptive comparison of human teachers' grades with all 18 models' grades on the same submissions. The study reports "Teachers obtained the highest mean score (0.726, SD = 0.391)", indicating models tended to be stricter, assigning lower scores more frequently while teachers rewarded higher grades more often.
