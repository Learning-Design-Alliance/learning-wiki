Evidence¶

Bird T. Baldwin 1922¶

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Repeated Stanford-Binet (Stanford Revision) testing of 143 children begun in 1917, 42 with four and 36 with five consecutive examinations. The author reports "the coefficient between the first IQ and the fifth IQ is +0.82 for the boys and girls."

"the coefficient between the first IQ and the fifth IQ is +0.82 for the boys and girls, which indicates that they keep their relative positons after an interval of approximately 4 years."
