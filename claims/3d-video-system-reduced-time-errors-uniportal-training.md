## Evidence

### Grossi Sarah 2023

Grossi Sarah, Cattoni Maria, Rotolo Nicola, Imperatori Andrea. (2023). Video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery simulation and training: a comprehensive literature review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04482-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review narrates Han and collaborators' study in which 113 trainees (85 surgeons in training, 28 medical students) completed peg transfer, needle transfer, and suturing tasks under 2D and 3D video systems; no effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that the 3D video system reduced the time performance and the number of errors compared to the 2D system. Participants indicated that the 3D display was advantageous due to the better depth perception and the consequent better endoscopic device handling."
