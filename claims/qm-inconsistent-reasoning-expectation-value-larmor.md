## Evidence

### Marshman 2015

Marshman, E. and Singh, C. (2015). A Framework for Understanding the Patterns of Student Difficulties in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Multi-university study in which 89 students answered a question about the time dependence of the expectation value of an electron spin component in Larmor precession. Interviewed students argued 'since the expectation value of 𝑆̂𝑦 is zero in the initial state ... so is its time dependence.'

> "In the context of an electron which is initially in an eigenstate of 𝑆̂𝑥 in a uniform magnetic field, the student was unable to differentiate between the expectation value and rate of change of the expectation value."
