## Evidence

### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Pfurtscheller Theresa, Hapfelmeier Alexander, Grünewald Marc, Häusler Janina, Seidel Tina, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Teacher questions and student responses in case-based learning: outcomes of a video study in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1895-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Evolutionary tree analysis (node 2 branch, RQ3) comparing open and closed reproduction questions. Open reproduction questions showed lower reproductive responding, higher elaboration and higher non-response than closed ones. The text adds closed reproduction questions were posed more than 14 times more often.

> "Open vs. closed reproductive questions are associated with a lower degree of reproductive student responses (31% vs. 53%), more elaborative student responses (11% vs. 5%), and a higher student nonresponse (57% vs. 42%)"
