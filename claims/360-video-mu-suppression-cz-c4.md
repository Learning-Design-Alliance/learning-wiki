Independent-samples t tests on μ-suppression scores in the quasi-experiment showed "greater in the experimental group at Cz (P=.03) and C4 (P=.03) but not at C3 (P=.13)". μSC reflects eyes-open resting μ-band modulation rather than task-evoked suppression.

> "μ-Suppression, which is an indirect EEG correlate that is often reported during action observation, was greater in the experimental group at Cz (P=.03) and C4 (P=.03) but not at C3 (P=.13)."
