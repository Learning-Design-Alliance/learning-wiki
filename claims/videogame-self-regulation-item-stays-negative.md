### Marín Díaz 2014

Marín Díaz, V.; Martín-Párraga, J. (2014). Can videogames be used to develop the infant stage educational curriculum? New Approaches in Educational Research, 3(1), 20-25. https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.3.1.20-25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey item on self-regulating the learning process. The article notes this item "show[s] continuity" with the negative pre-test, contrasting with the positive shifts on other items.

> "The results of the post-test are especially meaningful, since they show continuity: 87% of students claimed to fully disagree, 4% disagreed; 3% chose the indifferent option, and 2% and 4% respectively said that they agreed and fully agreed."
