Evidence¶

Haycock 1999¶

Haycock, Kati, Ed.; Barth, Patte, Ed.; Mitchell, Ruth, Ed.; Wilkins, Amy, Ed. (1999). Ticket to Nowhere: The Gap between Leaving High School and Entering College and High-Performance Jobs. Thinking K-16; v3 n2 Fall 1999. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED457262

q2 · i? · review · r1

The report describes the post-A Nation at Risk trend toward the "New Basics" curriculum (four years of English, three each of mathematics, science and social studies, one-half year of computer science) and concludes that "even among those who do complete the recommended number of core units, many are not taking the right courses."

"The result is that even among those who do complete the recommended number of core units, many are not taking the right coursesthose that prepare students for college."
