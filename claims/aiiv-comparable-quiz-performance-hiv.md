### Dengsheng Zhang 2025

Dengsheng Zhang. (2025). Transforming Higher Education with AI-Powered Video Lectures. [doi:10.36227/techrxiv.176231680.07716157/v1](https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.176231680.07716157/v1)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison across two semesters of two courses (Course A postgraduate, 37 students; Course B undergraduate, 134 students), same lecturer and materials, HIV in semester 1 and AIIV in semester 2. Four two-sample Welch t-tests at α = 0.05 yielded p-values of 0.43, 0.07, 0.80 and 0.19; "none of them has significant difference".

> "It can be observed that in all the 4 tests, none of them has significant difference, indicating that the AIIV has the comparable or equal performance to the HIV ."
