## Evidence

### Taylor A. W. R. 2026

Taylor A. W. R., Patel F. Y., Smyth E. K., Gay S., and Bird T. (2026). AI in the Classroom: Observing Preclinical Students' Use of ChatGPT During Case‐Based Learning at a UK Medical School. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 2: e70360. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70360

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focused ethnography of 45 students across seven CBL sessions, using field notes, 114 screenshots and focus groups analysed by reflexive thematic analysis; screenshots showed "frequent submission of superficial queries or direct copy‐pasting of workbook questions".

> "Screenshots showed frequent submission of superficial queries or direct copy‐pasting of workbook questions (Figure 1). Outputs could then be pasted back into the workbook. This bypassed retrieval, elaboration, application of theory to cases and group problem‐solving."
