## Evidence

### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Ratekin Carly Schade, Auko Erick, Wafula Meshack Malenya, Omwanda Wycliffe Okoth, Wamae Jane, Mudhune Sandra A, Rogers Ash, Mbeya Julius, Ouma Frederick Ochieng, Njoki Lillian, Young William, Starnes Joseph R. (2025). Evaluation of the Helping Babies Breathe programme in Migori County, Kenya. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003887

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unadjusted χ² and adjusted logistic regression across 557 deliveries at 26 Migori facilities; table 3 prints OR 3.79 (95% CI 2.62 to 5.47, p=0.00) for trained-provider presence, plus significant increases in warming (OR 3.24) and suctioning (OR 3.47); stimulation (OR 1.66, p=0.11) was not significant. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The percentage of deliveries that had an HBB trained provider at birth increased significantly from 24.34% (n=65) in the pretraining period to 54.48% (n=158) in the post-training period"
