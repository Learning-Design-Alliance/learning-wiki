`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study in Boston Public Schools: six volunteer schools implemented Word Generation while other schools only did pre- and post-testing. The article reports "significant improvement on knowledge of the academic words taught"; a subgroup analysis showed greater gains for language minority students, but students still classified as limited in English proficiency did not benefit.
