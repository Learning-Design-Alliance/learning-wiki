## Evidence

### Pang Xuefei 2024

Pang Xuefei, Li Ling, Liu Xu, Wang Yan, Yang Bo. (2024). Application of Emerging Teaching Models in Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.05.016

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by teaching method (Figure 3B) in the meta-analysis; PBL appeared as the experimental method in 6 studies. The pooled PBL effect was significant at P = .045 with a confidence interval (0.01-0.65) barely excluding zero.

> "PBL (SMD = 0.33, 95% CI = 0.01-0.65, P = .045) and FC (SMD = 0.50, 95% CI = 0.31-0.69, P < .001) had significantly higher teaching effects than traditional teaching method LBL"
