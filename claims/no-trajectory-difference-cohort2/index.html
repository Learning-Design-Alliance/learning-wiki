Hanselman 2017¶

Hanselman, P., Rozek, C. S., Grigg, J., & Borman, G. D. (2017). New evidence on self-affirmation effects and theorized sources of heterogeneity from large-scale replications. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(3), 405-424. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000141

This is a secondary analysis of cohort 2 term-specific GPA data testing whether the intervention improved the trajectory of performance throughout the year. The article reports "there was no difference by experimental condition (p=0.77)."

"Considering students' grades in each of the four terms of the school year, we tested for an interaction between treatment and term. GPA declined by 0.05 GPA points per term on average among Black and Hispanic students, but there was no difference by experimental condition (p=0.77)."
