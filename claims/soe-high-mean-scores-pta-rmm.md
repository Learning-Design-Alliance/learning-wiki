## Evidence

### Günther S.I. 2025

Günther S.I., Lüdke T., Polk M.-L., Zahnert T., Neudert M. (2025). Competence-oriented assessment: the structured oral examination. HNO. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00106-025-01687-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Curricular assessment of 10th-semester medical students: 407 SOEs were analyzed (PTA n = 217; RMM n = 190), rated in five categories with point deductions for examiner assistance. "The mean total score was 25.20 ± 1.94 for PTA and 24.84 ± 1.94 for RMM." Category-level means showed identification and performance highest, description (PTA) and interpretation (RMM) lowest.

> "The mean total score was 25.20 ± 1.94 for PTA and 24.84 ± 1.94 for RMM. The highest scores were obtained for identification and performance, the lowest for description (PTA) and interpretation (RMM)."
