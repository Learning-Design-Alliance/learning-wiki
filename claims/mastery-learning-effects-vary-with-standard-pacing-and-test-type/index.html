Claims

Mastery learning effects are larger with a higher mastery standard, group-based pacing, local tests, and less feedback for the comparison group

In a multiple regression over 103 studies, larger effects went with group-based rather than self-paced programs, higher unit mastery standards, locally developed rather than standardized criterion tests, and comparison groups that received less quiz feedback; social-science courses showed larger effects than mathematics and natural science.
