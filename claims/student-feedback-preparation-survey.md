### Hobbs F. Collin 2013

Hobbs F. Collin, Johnson Daniel J., Kearns Katherine D. (2013). A Deliberate Practice Approach to Teaching Phylogenetic Analysis. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe-13-03-0046

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous in-class feedback survey of the intervention group (107 of 134 students responded; 127 responses categorized for question 1, 103 for question 2), with categories created a posteriori. "79% of responses" cited the new exercises as most effective preparation; 13% wanted more between-tree comparison practice and 6% felt insufficiently prepared to construct trees.

> "Students in the intervention group overwhelmingly cited our new exercises as the most effective preparation for the lab 10 activities on phylogenetic tree construction and analysis (79% of responses; Figure 4)."
