## Evidence

### Gove 2017

Gove, A., Brunette, T., Bulat, J., Carrol, B., Henny, C., Macon, W., Nderu, E., & Sitabkhan, Y. (2017). Assessing the impact of early learning programs in Africa. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 158, 25–41. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20224

`q3 · i1` · `review · r2`

A longitudinal tracer study following 3,000 children assessed children in January and October 2016 within the Tayari cluster randomized controlled trial. Tayari children outgained the control group on a School Readiness Index, with "an average effect size of 0.34 SD".

> "Tayari children had much larger gains in learning than the control group: 18.4 points compared with 13.5 points on a School Readiness Index with an average effect size of 0.34 SD (Kwayumba, Piper, Oyanga, & Oyagi, 2017)."
