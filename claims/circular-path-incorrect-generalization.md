### Cristian Raduta 2005

Cristian Raduta. (2005). General Students' Misconceptions Related to Electricity and Magnetism. The Ohio State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132)

Survey statement administered by Bagno et al. (Ref. 4) on the velocity of a charged particle in a magnetic field. The authors attribute the difficulty to most problems dealing with particles whose initial direction is perpendicular to the field. No effect size is printed.

> "Giving the students the statement, " the velocity of a charged particle moving in a magnetic field is always perpendicular to the direction of the field, " 37% of the students agreed, out of which group (those students who answered incorrectly) 81% gave answers similar to: "v, B, and F are always perpendicular to each other according to the left hand or right screw law. ""
