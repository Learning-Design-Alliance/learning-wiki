### Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak 2019

Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak. (2019). Practitioner’s guide to social network analysis: Examining physics anxiety in an active-learning setting. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple linear permutation regression models testing indegree at four α tuning values against normalized anxiety shift in two active-learning physics sections. The article reports "no signiﬁcant relationship between normalized anxiety shifts and indegree"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Our tests on the network data collected at the end of the semester reveals no signiﬁcant relationship between normalized anxiety shifts and indegree, regardless of the tuning parameter value."
