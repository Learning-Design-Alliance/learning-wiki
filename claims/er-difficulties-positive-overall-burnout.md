## Evidence

### Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David 2024

Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David. (2024). Emotion Regulation and Academic Burnout Among Youth: a Quantitative Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09930-w

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 20 studies of children, adolescents, and youth; pooled correlation for ER difficulties with overall burnout: "r = 0.25 (95% CI = 0.182; 0.311), p < 0.001". Post hoc power for this pooled analysis was 0.78, just below the 0.80 threshold.

> "significant positive associations between ER difficulties and overall burnout (k = 13), r = 0.25 (95% CI = 0.182; 0.311), p < 0.001"
