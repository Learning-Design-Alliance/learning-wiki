## Evidence

### Sonja Cwik 2020

Sonja Cwik, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How the learning environment predicts male and female students' motivational beliefs in algebra-based introductory physics courses. Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.cwik](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.cwik)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey study of 544 matched students (345 women, 199 men) in algebra-based physics 1 and 2 at a large US university; t-tests with Cohen's d. The authors report means "statistically significantly different in favor of men"; Table 1 prints Cohen's d = 0.76 for interest in physics 2 and d = 0.41 for self-efficacy in physics 2.

> "Table 1 shows that the mean values of all constructs for men and women are statistically significantly different in favor of men."

### Sonja Cwik 2020 (2)
