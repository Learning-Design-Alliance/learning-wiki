### Marcos D. Caballero and Laura Merner 2018

Marcos D. Caballero and Laura Merner. (2018). On the Prevalence and Nature of Computational Instruction in Undergraduate Physics Programs across the United States. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The survey analysis reports a contingency table test of association between computational teaching experience and institution type: "χ2 = 27.38; p ≪ 0.05; ν = 3".

> "A contingency table analysis ( χ2 = 27.38; p ≪ 0.05; ν = 3) shows an association with the preva- lence of computational teaching experience and an insti- tution’s highest available degree."

### Marcos D. Caballero and Laura Merner 2018 (2)

Marcos D. Caballero and Laura Merner. (2018). On the Prevalence and Nature of Computational Instruction in Undergraduate Physics Programs across the United States. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up pairwise χ2 comparisons with Bonferroni correction "( p <0.0125)" attribute the association entirely to "a lower frac- tion of two-year college faculty reporting they have com- putational teaching experience".

> "However, pairwise χ2 comparisons using a Bonferroni correction ( p <0.0125) demonstrate that this eﬀect is only due to a lower frac- tion of two-year college faculty reporting they have com- putational teaching experience."
