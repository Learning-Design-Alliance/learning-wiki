### Schubach F 2017

Schubach F, Goos M, Fabry G, Vach W, Boeker M. (2017). Virtual patients in the acquisition of clinical reasoning skills: does presentation mode matter? A quasi-randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1004-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric results for the 24-case, 59-item SCT (Cronbach's alpha 0.78) comparing 16 reference experts in visceral surgery with the combined student group; the article reports the gap in expert standard deviations with p < 0.001 and no standardized effect size.

> "Experts (80 points, 95% CI = [77.1;82.9]) significantly outperformed the combined student group (59.5 points, 95% CI = [50.7;68.2]) by four expert standard deviations (p < 0.001)."
