## Evidence

### Király 2016

Sándor Király. (2016). How to Implement an E-learning Curriculum to Streamline Teaching Digital Image Processing. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 9, Number 2. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/2065-1430

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correspondence-course experiment (Table 2): after an F-test found variances not substantially different, two-sample t-tests were reported for theory and practical parts, with experimental averages 86.10% and 57.37% versus control 68.78% and 37.33%. The article states the improvement was higher than in the regular course.

> "According to the two sample t -test, it has 99.87% chance of that the developed material caused the difference of achievements in the case of theory test, and that it has 98.70% chance of that the developed material caused the difference of achievements in the case of the practical test."
