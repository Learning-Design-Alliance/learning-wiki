# Within-lesson sequencing in the PATHS Feelings and Relationships Unit proceeds from cognitive to behavioral to affective and from external to internal

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Analysis of the seven steps of a PATHS lesson shows the within-lesson sequence is structured to proceed from cognitive to behavioral to affective, and from external to internal. [→ Wu 2000](#wu-2000)

## Evidence

### Wu 2000

Wu, Yann-Shya. (2000). Guidelines for Instructional Sequencing in Emotional Literacy Learning Using PATHS Curriculum as an Example. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED455810

`q2 · i?`

The author's document analysis of the seven-step lesson structure in the PATHS Feelings and Relationships Unit maps each step to a domain of learning, concluding the sequence moves "from cognitive to behavioral to affective" and external to internal.

> "the within-lesson sequence is mainly structured so as to proceed from cognitive to behavioral to affective, and from external to internal through recognition, reflection and ultimately to the empathetic--(emotional)--state which is feeling."
