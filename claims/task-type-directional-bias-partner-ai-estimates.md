Preprint, Technical University of Munich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23237 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.23237](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.23237)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Benjamini–Hochberg correction on differences between partner self-reports and estimates across ten task types, measured at t2 and t3 in the 103-pair survey. The quoted values are unstandardised five-point-scale mean differences; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "AI use for automatically implementing full features was systematically underestimated (Mdiff = +0.21, p_adj = .049), while AI use for asking about programming principles (Mdiff = −0.29, p_adj = .029) and for confirming the validity of an approach (Mdiff = −0.19, p_adj = .049) were overestimated."
