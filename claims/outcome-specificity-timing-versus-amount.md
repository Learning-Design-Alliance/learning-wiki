`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stacked-dataset regression with outcome × exposure interactions and participant-clustered standard errors, testing specificity directly rather than by comparing significance; a joint Wald test of the two exposures was significant (χ2(2) = 8.30, p = 0.016).

> "The association of pre-sleep social media use with sleep quality was stronger than its association with examination performance (difference in standardized β = −0.271, 95% CI − 0.463 to −0.079, p = 0.006), and the association of total daily use with examination performance was stronger than its association with sleep quality (difference = 0.235, 95% CI 0.032 to 0.437, p = 0.023)"
