# Collaborative annotation is equivalent to individual annotation for junior medical science students' quiz performance (no significant difference)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Junior Year 2 Medical Science students' mean quiz scores did not differ significantly between individual and collaborative annotation conditions. [→ Sahota Michael 2016](#sahota-michael-2016)

## Evidence

### Sahota Michael 2016

Sahota Michael, Leung Betty, Dowdell Stephanie, Velan Gary M. (2016). Learning pathology using collaborative vs. individual annotation of whole slide images: a mixed methods trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0831-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-over trial with junior Year 2 Medical Science students (n = 119); paired t-test on quiz items linked to each condition. Individual mean was 61.51% versus collaborative 57.98%; "did not differ significantly". Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Junior students’ mean quiz scores following individual annotation did not differ significantly from mean quiz scores following collaborative annotation—(individual mean = 61.51%, 95% CI = 57.54–65.49%; collaborative mean = 57.98%, 95% CI = 53.62–62.34%; t = 1.549, df = 118; P = 0.1241; n = 119)"
