## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Correlations with Stanford Revision mental age were .64 for 64 six-year-olds, .69 for 33 seven-year-olds, and .79 for 29 eight-year-olds. [→ Pressey 1919](#pressey-1919)

## Evidence

### Pressey 1919

Pressey, L. W. (1919). A Group Scale of Intelligence for Use in the First Three Grades. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072897

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Validity correlations with the Stanford Revision, computed separately by age using the product-moments method on unselected groups: r = .64 (six-year-olds), r = .69 (seven-year-olds), r = .79 (eight-year-olds).

> "For 64 unselected six year old children, the correlation is .64; for a group of 33 unselected seven year olds, it was .69; and for a similarly unselected group of 29 eight year olds, it was .79."
