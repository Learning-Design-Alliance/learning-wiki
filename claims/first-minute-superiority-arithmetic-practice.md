### Phillips 1916

Phillips, F. M. (1916). Comparison of the work done in the successive minutes of a ten minute practice period in the fundamentals of arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of medians from ten-minute timed tests in the four fundamentals, about one hundred pupils per grade from fourth to eighth, with 36,820 minute scores. The article reports first-minute medians "from 6 per cent, to 21 per cent, higher" than later-minute averages.

> "The medians for the first minute are higher than those of the later minutes in practically every case. They range from .21 points to 7.45 points higher than the average of the work of the other minutes, or from 6 per cent, to 21 per cent, higher"
