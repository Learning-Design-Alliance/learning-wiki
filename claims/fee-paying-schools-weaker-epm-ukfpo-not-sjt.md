## Evidence

### MacKenzie R K 2016

MacKenzie R K, Cleland J A, Ayansina D, Nicholson S. (2016). Does the UKCAT predict performance on exit from medical school? A national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-011313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel modelling of the national UKCAT-UKFPO linked cohort, adjusted for confounders including gender, age, IMD quintile and UKCAT year. The article reports the fee-paying disadvantage was "particularly apparent on the total EPM and hence the UKFPO total score"; no effect size is printed.

> "Those attending fee-paying schools performed significantly more weakly on the EPM decile, the EPM total and the total UKFPO score, but not the SJT, than those attending other types of school."
