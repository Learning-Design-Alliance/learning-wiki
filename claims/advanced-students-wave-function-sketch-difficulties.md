Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu. (2016). Cognitive Issues in Learning Advanced Physics: An Example from Quantum Mechanics. arXiv:1602.05619. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05619](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05619)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Survey and interview investigation of students enrolled in quantum mechanics courses. Students were given a potential energy diagram for a one-dimensional finite square well and asked to "draw a qualitative sketch" of the ground state and a scattering state wave function, with think-aloud interviews of a subset.

> "administering written surveys to more than two hun- dred physics graduate students and advanced undergrad- uate students enrolled in quantum mechanics courses and by conducting individual interviews with a subset of stu- dents"
