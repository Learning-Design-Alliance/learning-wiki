## Evidence

### van Rijsewijk 2018

van Rijsewijk, L.G.M., Oldenburg, B., Snijders, T.A.B., Dijkstra, J.K., & Veenstra, R. (2018). A description of classroom help networks, individual network position, and their associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208173

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 54 classroom networks using a scatterplot and K-means clustering showed that "classrooms theoretically most beneficial for achievement ... were highly uncommon in our sample". Every classroom was segmented and unequal to some extent.

> "the scatterplot demonstrates that classrooms theoretically most beneficial for achievement (with an equal distribution of nominations, little segmentation, a few isolates, and / or high density), of which some can be found in the lower left section of the plot, were highly uncommon in our sample."
