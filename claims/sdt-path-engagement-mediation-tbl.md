# Perceived competence, intrinsic motivation, external regulation, engagement, and perceived learning rose from pre-test to post-test in one group with no comparison group (path analysis)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the fitted SDT path model, perceived competence, intrinsic motivation, external regulation, engagement, and perceived learning rose from pre-test to post-test in one group with no comparison group; the model accounts for 70% of variance in engagement and 17% in perceived learning. [→ Jeno Lucas M. 2017](#jeno-lucas-m-2017)
`q2 i?` Sobel tests reported indirect statistical associations among pre-test-to-post-test increases in perceived competence, external regulation, intrinsic motivation, engagement, and perceived learning, in one group with no comparison group. [→ Jeno Lucas M. 2017](#jeno-lucas-m-2017)

## Evidence

### Jeno Lucas M. 2017

Jeno Lucas M., Raaheim Arild, Kristensen Sara Madeleine, Kristensen Kjell Daniel, Hole Torstein Nielsen, Haugland Mildrid J., Mæland Silje. (2017). The Relative Effect of Team-Based Learning on Motivation and Learning: A Self-Determination Theory Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0055

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Path analysis of standardized residual change scores from the 64-student study, fitting the SDT engagement model in AMOS. The article reports "70% of the variance in engagement and 17% of the variance in perceived learning", and Sobel tests of "β = 0.209, z = 2.73, p < 0.01" (competence), "β = 0.14, z = 2.48, p < 0.05" (external regulation), and "β = 0.16, z = 2.36, p < 0.05" (intrinsic motivation).

> "Model fit was excellent (χ2(7) = 4.198, p = 0.757, CFI = 1.0, χ2/df ratio = 0.60, RMSEA = 0.000 [confidence interval, CI: 0.00 – 0.108]; see Figure 4). Specifically, increases in perceived competence predict increases in engagement."
