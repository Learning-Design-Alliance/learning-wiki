  • Open textbooks appear to offer financial benefits to students without decreasing learning outcomes in higher education — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Synthesized studies of OER in college courses found academic outcomes comparable to traditional textbooks and positive perceptions among students and instructors — related

  • Hilton's reviews find OER equally efficacious to and less expensive than commercial textbooks — a broader claim this one bears on
