Sartain 2011¶

Sartain, L., Stoelinga, S. R., & Brown, E. R., with Luppescu, S., Kapadia Matsko, K., Miller, F. K., Durwood, C. E., Jiang, J. Y., & Glazer, D. (2011). q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statistical comparison of 501 matched principal-observer ratings of the same lesson found principals "significantly more likely to call practice Distinguished when observers rated the same instruction as Proficient." Figure 3 shows an odds ratio of 6.18, significant at the .01 level; the article prints no standardized effect size for this contrast.

"Across all components, principals were also significantly more likely to call practice Distinguished when observers rated the same instruction as Proficient"

Sartain 2011 (2)¶

Rethinking Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Lessons Learned from Classroom Observations, Principal-Teacher Conferences, and District Implementation. q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive ratings data from the matched-observation reliability sample of 257 teachers: "52 percent received at least one Distinguished rating from principals, but only 24 percent received a Distinguished rating from observers." Overall, principals gave 17 percent Distinguished ratings versus 3 percent for observers.

"Of the 257 teachers observed simultaneously by the principal and observer, 52 percent received at least one Distinguished rating from principals, but only 24 percent received a Distinguished rating from observers."
