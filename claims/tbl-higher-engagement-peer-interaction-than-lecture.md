## Evidence

### Kehrli Kelsey F. 2026

Kehrli Kelsey F., Conner Kelly R., Eyadiel Lauren, Sisson Caroline B., Smith Natalie. (2026). Team-based learning versus lecture-based instruction for chest radiograph interpretation in physician associate education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09452-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-session survey using adapted SEEQ and SSES items on a 5-point Likert scale (TBL n=78, lecture n=65). TBL mean engagement rating was 4.72 vs 4.35 for lecture; peer/instructor interaction 4.86 vs 4.18; both p < 0.001. Cronbach's alpha for satisfaction was 0.88.

> "The largest between-group differences were observed in engagement (p < 0.001) and peer/instructor interaction (p < 0.001), favoring TBL."
