How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities 2014¶

How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities, Creates Economic Losses for the State of Arizona. (2014). A Project of WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559628

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's summary presents model-based calculations comparing lifetime profiles of dropouts and graduates, expressed as present values at age 18 in 2013 dollars. It states "the lifetime social loss for the state of Arizona is $421,280" per dropout, aggregating to "$7 .6 billion" across the annual cohort of 18,100.

"For each high school dropout, the lifetime social loss for the state of Arizona is $421,280. Across the 18,100 students in Arizona who dropout of high school annually, this social loss amounts to $7 .6 billion."
