# Course completion effects differed by subject: reduced DFW in Government and reduced dropping in Microeconomics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Overall, treated students were not significantly less likely to DFW or drop; in Government treatment reduced DFW by three percentage points relative to 18% of control students, while in Microeconomics it reduced dropping by three percentage points relative to 8% of control students. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ITT analysis of course completion outcomes (Table 2, panels B and C). Government DFW reduction was driven by a non-significant two-point reduction in D or F grades; Microeconomics showed a three-point drop in course withdrawal relative to 8% of control students.

> "In Government (panel B), students assigned to treatment are three percentage points less likely to DFW compared to 18% of control students. This effect on the DFW outcome is primarily driven by a reduction in earning a D or F (where we observe a two percentage point reduction, not statistically significant)."
