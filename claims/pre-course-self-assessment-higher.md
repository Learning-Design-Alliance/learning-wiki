### Wei Yingying 2025

Wei Yingying, Deng Na, Zheng Hongmei, Guo Haijun, Zhang Huiling, Li Bing, Wu Daiqin, Li Wei, Si Xiaoyun. (2025). Comparison of teacher-standardized patients and peer role-play in assessing medical students' history-taking skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07758-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests on 620 valid self-assessment forms (12 items, 12–72 points) showed pre-course scores higher than post-course in every group (TSP t = 4.101, spRP t = 3.691, non-spRP t = 6.026, all p = 0.000); between-group pre and post differences were not significant (p = 0.054; p = 0.900).

> "the self-assessment scores of students in each group before the courses were higher than those after the courses, and the differences were statistically significant (all p < 0.05)"
