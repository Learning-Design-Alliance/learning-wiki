# In a small upper-secondary class, computer simulations held up at least as well as ordinary student experiments on circuit quizzes once deteriorations were taken into account, though the effect may be much weaker than the per-item counting suggests

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2010 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Counting both improvements and deteriorations on before/after quizzes, simulations compared favourably with ordinary student experiments, but improvements clustered in a few students so the observed effect may be substantially weaker. [→ Björn S. Schmekel 2010](#bjorn-s-schmekel-2010)

## Evidence

### Björn S. Schmekel 2010

Björn S. Schmekel. (2010). Sind Computersimulationen geeigneter als Schülerexperimente, um das Verständnis elektrischer Grundschaltungen zu verbessern? Eine Untersuchung im Physikunterricht der Oberstufe am Gymnasium. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1001.4395](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1001.4395)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Test evaluation (§4.1) of before/after quizzes in a 13-student S1/S2 course, scored per task as improved or deteriorated after each experiment or simulation unit. The author notes the counting "durch die Zählwe ise getrennt nach Aufgaben verstärkt" the effect, which is "möglicherweise wesentlich schwächer", since improvements clustered in single students.

> "Berücksichtigt man die Möglichkeit einer Verschlechterung, so können sich die Computersimulatione n gegenüber den gewöhnlichen Schülerexperimen- 13 ten behaupten."
