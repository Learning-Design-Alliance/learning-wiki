# Year Up may result in little to no change in industry-recognized credential, certificate, or license completion

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Based on one RCT meeting WWC standards, Year Up showed no discernible effects on earning a vocational certificate three years after program completion. [→ credential-completion](#credential-completion)

## Evidence

### credential-completion

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, November). Year Up. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/722.

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The WWC review of Roder & Elliott (2014), based on 143 students, rated credential completion as no discernible effects; 27% of the intervention group versus 25% of the comparison group earned a vocational certificate three years after program completion, an effect size of 0.06, improvement index +2, not statistically significant (p = 0.81).

> "The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations (Roder & Elliott, 2014) showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Year Up on industry-recognized credential, certificate, or license completion."
