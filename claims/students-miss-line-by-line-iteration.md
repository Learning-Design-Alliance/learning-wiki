The authors' assertion in the inelastic-collision assessment discussion, supported by their citation of Aiken et al.'s earlier study. The article offers this as an observation about student understanding of "the iterative nature of computer programs" rather than new empirical data of its own.

Perhaps the first study of CT in introductory physics was by Aiken et al. [27] who found that high school students struggle to understand the iterative nature of computer programs."
