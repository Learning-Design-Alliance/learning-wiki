Evidence¶

Chassell 1921¶

Chassell, C. F., & Chassell, L. M. (1921). A survey of the three first grades of the Horace Mann School by means of psychological tests and teachers' estimates, and a statistical evaluation of the methods employed. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(5), 243-252. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0068285

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of group test scores against Stanford Revision mental ages in the same first-grade survey, based on 37 and 45 cases respectively; the printed correlations r = .45 and r = .53 are large effects by the r code table, as is r = .37 between the two group tests themselves.

"The correlations with the two group tests" are much lower, namely, .45 for the Pressey Primer Scale, and .53 for the Meyer Tests" (based on 37 and 45 cases, respectively). A correlation of only .37 was found between the two group tests themselves."
