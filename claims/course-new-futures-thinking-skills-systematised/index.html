Tapio Rasa 2022¶

Tapio Rasa, Elina Palmgren, Antti Laherto. (2022). Futurising science education: students' experiences from a course on futures thinking and quantum computing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09572-3

Interview excerpt (Marcus) coded under the theme 'New futures thinking skills (e.g. scenario building, backcasting) systematise one's thinking', the most prevalent theme (76%, 16 of 21 interviews). The authors note backcasting seemed to leave a lasting impression.

"I have a more concrete idea that there are these tools, even concrete tools for thinking of the future."
