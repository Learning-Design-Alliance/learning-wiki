### Charnetski MD 2025

Charnetski MD, Asoodar M, Wawersik D, van Mook W. (2025). Navigating cultural landscapes: a thematic analysis of home faculty experiences in transborder simulation curriculum development and adaptation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07515-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the thematic analysis of five interviews, the Psychological Safety theme concerned faculty and staff involved in adaptation; the article reports that viewing the home-context member as the "expert" creates hierarchical challenges to bilateral adaptation, illustrated by a participant's account of teacher-student authority norms.

> "Particularly if the expectation is that the member from the home context is seen as the"expert,"this can lead to hierarchical challenges to the bilateral adaptation relationship."
