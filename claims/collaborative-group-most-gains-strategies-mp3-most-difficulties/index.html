Evidence¶

Tsai Pi-hua 2015¶

Tsai Pi-hua. (2015). Computer-Assisted Pronunciation Learning in a Collaborative Context: A Case Study in Taiwan. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077659

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Constant comparative content analysis of weekly learning logs from all 90 participants, categorized into difficulties, gains, strategies, and monitoring; Table 4 frequency counts are reported descriptively with no statistical test.

"The MP3 Group reported the highest frequency of difficulties (N=406). In contrast, the Collaborative Group had the lowest frequency in reporting their difficulties (N=192) and the highest frequency in describing their gains (N= 263), and strategy brainstorming (N=172)."
