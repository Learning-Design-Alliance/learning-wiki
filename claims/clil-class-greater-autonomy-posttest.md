# Postgraduate students in a CLIL Spanish class demonstrated greater autonomy at posttest than students in a traditional textbook class, with 40% reaching the 'largely aware' level versus 12% in the control group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taking the CLIL Spanish class demonstrated greater autonomy at the time of the posttest, with 40% of experimental-group answers categorized as level 3 'largely aware' versus 12% in the control group. [→ Oliva Parera 2015](#oliva-parera-2015)

## Evidence

### Oliva Parera 2015

Oliva Parera, P. M., & Núñez Delgado, M. P. (2015). An Approach to Integration: The Integration of Language and Content to promote L2 Learner Autonomy at the College Level. Gist Education and Learning Research Journal, 10, 92-112. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1692-5777

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative-quantitative action research with intact groups of graduate students (13 experimental, 14 control) taking low/mid intermediate Spanish. In the conclusions the authors report that "40% of the students were able to justify their answers in category 3" while the control group reached only 12%; no inferential test statistic is printed.

> "We noticed that 40% of the students were able to justify their answers in category 3, “largely aware,” but the control group’s answers demonstrated a lower value (12%) in that category."
