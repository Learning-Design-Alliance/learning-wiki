### Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling 2026

Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling. (2026). Active Learning with Bayesian Reasoning: A POGIL-Based Pedagogy in Introductory Statistics. arXiv:2606.08660. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ordinal cumulative-logit component of the Bayesian bivariate model on satisfaction recoded to Low/Medium/High for 94 students. Model-based Pr(High satisfaction) was 51.4% for POGIL and 62.5% for Lecture; "the 95 percent credible interval that spanned 0."

> "The posterior difference in the probability of High satisfaction was likewise small with a 95 percent credible interval that spanned 0."

### Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling 2026 (2)

Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling. (2026). Active Learning with Bayesian Reasoning: A POGIL-Based Pedagogy in Introductory Statistics. arXiv:2606.08660. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplementary sensitivity analyses using the original 1–10 ordered responses and alternative regroupings; broader upper-tail definitions such as 7–10 or 6–10 produced more negative contrasts than the pre-specified High = 8–10 comparison.

> "Across these codings, the estimated teaching-style contrast in satisfaction remains directionally consistent, with POGIL generally lower than Lecture on upper-tail satisfaction probabilities, but its magnitude depends on how the upper end of the scale is defined."
