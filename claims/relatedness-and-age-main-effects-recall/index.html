Murphy 2024¶

Murphy, D. H., Rhodes, M. G., & Castel, A. D. (2024). Age-related differences in metacognitive reactivity in younger and older adults. Metacognition and Learning, 19, 863–877. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09391-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r3

Main effect of relatedness in the pre-registered experiment: "related word pairs were better recalled (M = 0.86, SD = 0.16) than unrelated word pairs (M = 0.40, SD = 0.27)", F(1, 477) = 2,182.68, ηp2 = 0.82; t-test d = 2.03.

"Results revealed that related word pairs were better recalled (M = 0.86, SD = 0.16) than unrelated word pairs (M = 0.40, SD = 0.27), [F(1, 477) = 2,182.68, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.82, BF10 > 100]; a separate t-test corroborated this effect [ t(480) = 44.73, p < .001, d = 2.03"

Related Claims¶

  • JOL reactivity depended on pair relatedness: negative reactivity for unrelated word pairs but no reactivity for related word pairs

  • JOL reactivity differed by age: making JOLs impaired older adults' recall but did not affect younger adults' recall
