Evidence¶

Bagheri 2013¶

Bagheri, M., Wan Ali, W. Z., Abdullah, M. C. B., & Daud, S. M. (2013). Effects of Project-based Learning Strategy on Self-directed Learning Skills of Educational Technology Students. Contemporary Educational Technology, 4(1), 15-29. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105523

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA on SDLRS scores collected three times (pre-test, post-test one, post-test two) in the randomized two-group study. The within-subjects effect across "the three sequencing measurements" was significant, F(2,152)=39.97, p<.001, η² = .345.

"The within -subject effects data also indicated that there is a significant difference in the overall self -directed learning mean scores across the three sequencing measurements, F(2,152)= 39.97, p<.001, η 2 =.345."
