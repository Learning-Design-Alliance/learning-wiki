The preface summarizes Grandy's chapter 'Constructivism and Objectivity: Disentangling Metaphysics from Pedagogy'. This is the editor's attribution of Grandy's philosophical argument, not an empirical test reported here.

"He says that the claims of cognitive constructivism need to be distinguished from those of metaphysical constructivism, and that cognitive constructivism has strong empirical support and suggests important new directions for the conduct of science education"
