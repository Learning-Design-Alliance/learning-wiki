### N. Mitchell 2025

N. Mitchell, M. Rashid, J. Foulds, and K. Forbes. (2025). Thinking About the Why: A Qualitative Study on Students' Perspectives of Paediatric Team‐Based Learning Discussions. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70161

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion-level interpretation of the focus-group data: students who had seen a real patient with a similar presentation were seen by peers as having more expertise and being more trustworthy, particularly if they could describe what a staff or resident taught them.

> "Students with more perceived knowledge, experience or confidence were, however, noted to instill more sway in group decision‐making."
