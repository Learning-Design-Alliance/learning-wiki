## Evidence

### Abdorreza Tahriri 2014

Abdorreza Tahriri, Masoud Khalili Sabet, Afrouz Aeineh. (2014). The Effect of Portfolio Assessment on Learning Idioms in Writing. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 2(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.2n.2p.53

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 30 upper-intermediate EFL learners (15 per group) taught 50 idioms over 10 sessions; independent samples t-test on posttest scores. The article reports "Significance of t-test is .0" with a mean difference of 13.40000, and concludes using portfolio "had a significant effect on the students' knowledge of idioms".

> "Significance of t-test is .0 which is less than .05. As a result , there is a significant difference between the means of the two groups. Hence, the null hypothesis of this study was rejected."
