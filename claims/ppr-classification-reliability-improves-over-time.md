## Evidence

### Shioiri Toshiki 2024

Shioiri Toshiki, Nakashima Michiyo, Tsunekawa Koji. (2024). When can we identify the students at risk of failure in the national medical licensure examination in Japan using the predictive pass rate? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05948-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary-classification analysis of PPR against actual NMLE results at the five time points (Table 4). The article prints sensitivity rising from 82.9% to 92.7%, specificity from 72.3% to 89.3%, likelihood ratio from 3.0 to 8.6, FNR from 1.6% to 0.6%, and NPV from 98.4% to 99.2%.

> "the numbers in the “high-risk group” gradually decreased, and the sensitivity, specificity, and likelihood ratio edged upward together during the 6 years in school. By contrast, the FNR and the NPV were maintained at very low and high levels, respectively."
