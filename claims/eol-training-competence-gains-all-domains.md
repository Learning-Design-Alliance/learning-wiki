# After the training programme, nurses' self-reported competence in end-of-life care improved significantly across all three domains, with the largest gain in non-pharmacological management (49% to 86%)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The percentage of RNs reporting competence rose in all three domains (assessing EOL symptoms 79% to 83%; pharmacological management 80% to 90%; non-pharmacological management 49% to 86%), each reaching statistical significance.
