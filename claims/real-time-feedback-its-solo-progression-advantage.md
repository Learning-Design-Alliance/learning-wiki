## Evidence

### Ogunsakin IB 2026

Ogunsakin IB, Ayanwale MA, Falebita OS and Felemu OJ (2026) Reimagining mathematics education through intelligent tutoring: evidence, opportunities, and implementation challenges. Front. Educ. 11:1943906. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1943906

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports Marwiang et al. (2025)'s comparison of learners using the same adaptive diagnostic ITS with and without automated real-time feedback. The feedback group's means rose from 1.90 (SD = 1.43) to 3.54 (SD = 0.85) versus 1.90 (SD = 1.31) to 2.85 (SD = 1.27) on the four-point SOLO scale, a "meaningful advantage associated with real-time feedback".

> "The significant time-by- group interaction, F (1, 76) = 9.24, p = .003, partial η2 = .108, together with the reported Cohen’s d of 0.73, indicates a meaningful advantage associated with real-time feedback."
