# On true–false and SBA-I interpretative questions, ChatGPT-o1pro, ChatGPT-4, and AtlasGPT outperform Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5, with all models weaker on interpretative items

## Evidence

### Çamlar Mahmut 2025

Çamlar Mahmut, Sevgi Umut Tan, Erol Gökberk, Karakaş Furkan, Doğruel Yücel, Güngör Abuzer. (2025). Comparative performance of neurosurgery-specific, peer-reviewed versus general AI chatbots in bilingual board examinations: evaluating accuracy, consistency, and error minimization strategies. Acta Neurochirurgica 167(1):241. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-025-06628-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 87 true–false items in the six-model benchmark (Table 1). The article reports the top three models outperformed the other three, with no significant differences among the top three.

> "No significant differences were observed among the accuracy reported by ChatGPT-o1pro, AtlasGPT, and ChatGPT-4; however, these three models outperformed Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 (p < 0.001) (Table 1)."

### Çamlar Mahmut 2025 (2)

Çamlar Mahmut, Sevgi Umut Tan, Erol Gökberk, Karakaş Furkan, Doğruel Yücel, Güngör Abuzer. (2025). Comparative performance of neurosurgery-specific, peer-reviewed versus general AI chatbots in bilingual board examinations: evaluating accuracy, consistency, and error minimization strategies. Acta Neurochirurgica 167(1):241. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-025-06628-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 23 clinically interpretative SBA-I questions (Table 1). ChatGPT-o1pro achieved 69.6% accuracy, ChatGPT-4 and AtlasGPT 65.2%, while Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 remained in the range of 30–35%; the article states all models' success was lower for SBA-I questions.

> "Statistical analysis showed higher correct response rates for ChatGPT-o1pro, ChatGPT-4, and AtlasGPT than for Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 (p < 0.05) (Table 1)."
