### Arthur I. Gates 1920

Arthur I. Gates, Teachers College, Columbia University. (1920). The True-False Test as a Measure of Achievement in College Courses. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=true-false+test+as+a+measure+of+achievement+in+college+courses+gates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational classification of the Table IX single-test criterion correlations by presentation method: oral (n = 18, average .65), oral and visual written on board (n = 8, average .62), and visual mimeographed (n = 13, average .66). These are descriptive averages of small subsets; the article prints no effect size for the differences, so the null finding carries no measured magnitude.

> "While the data are too few to make generalization secure, no method of presentation shows superiority."
