### Zhang Yuejiao 2025

Zhang Yuejiao, Li Xiaolan, Wei Xi, Xiao Jiexuan, Zhang Qi, Mai Sui. (2025). A pilot study on the application of a digital dental teaching evaluation system in Class II cavity preparation teaching. BMC Oral Health 25, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05695-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

T-test comparison of Fair Grader 2000 final scores between 11 completed-residency and 7 ongoing-residency clinicians; final means were 67.8 ± 6.469 versus 57.73 ± 6.36, with t = −3.18, P < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "When comparing the scores in the two groups separately, the results showed that the final scores of those who had completed their residency were higher than those who had not completed it. The difference was statistically significant (t = −3.18, P < 0.05, Table 3)."
