Evidence¶

Sebring 1996¶

Sebring, P. B., Bryk, A. S., Roderick, M., Camburn, E., Luppescu, S., Thum, Y. M., Smith, B., & Kahne, J. (1996). Charting Reform in Chicago: The Students Speak. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-chicago-students-speak

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Student survey safety questions covering classes, hallways and bathrooms, travel between home and school, and areas outside school. The authors conclude "about two-thirds of the students in all grades feel uneasy when they are outside around their school."

"Only 38 percent of the sixth gaders, 43 percent of the eighth graders and 34 percent of the tenth graders feel this is a very safe or mostly safe place to be"
