Tutorial problem evidence: on Problem 12 (bouncing steel ball), freebody diagrams were "wildly incorrec t for case 3"; students justified R = mg by zero velocity, illustrating the belief F ∝ v rather than F = ma.

> "The most common justiﬁcation for R = mg was that the velocity is zero, so the body must be in equilibrium; another justiﬁca tion was that most reaction forces met in A-level problems were equal to mg"
