# Whether PA itself causes the positive mathematics effects remains unclear, with engagement, attention from adults and innovative teaching proposed as alternative explanations

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The review states it is unclear whether PA per se caused the positive effects; attention from adults, change in routine or pedagogical practices, and increased engagement and enjoyment may have produced the results. [→ Sneck S. 2019](#sneck-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Sneck S. 2019

Sneck S., Viholainen H., Syväoja H., Kankaapää A., Hakonen H., Poikkeus A.-M., Tammelin T. (2019). Effects of school-based physical activity on mathematics performance in children: a systematic review. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-019-0866-6

`q1 · i?`

Authors' interpretive argument in the Discussion, noting that despite including only intervention-versus-control designs, alternative explanations for positive effects cannot be ruled out. No new data are reported for this point.

> "it is possible that instead of or in addition to direct effects of increased PA, it was the attention from adults, a change in routine or pedagogical practices and increased engagement and enjoyment that produced the positive results."
