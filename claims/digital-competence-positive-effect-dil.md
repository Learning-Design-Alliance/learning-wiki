Computers & Education 167 (2021) 104184. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model analysis (AMOS SEM) of 319 Shiraz University students testing H2, the digital-competence-to-DIL path. The printed path coefficient "β = 0.59, p = 0.0001" is an unstandardised regression coefficient, so no standardised effect size is reported.

> "The findings also showed that increasing students' digital competence had a positive and significant effect on increasing DIL (β = 0.59, p = 0.0001), which confirms the second hypothesis (i.e., H2)."
