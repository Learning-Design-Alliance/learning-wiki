## Evidence

### Kilimci 2017

Kilimci, A. (2017). Integrating cognitive linguistics insights into data-driven learning: teaching vertical prepositions. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 681-719. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159243

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 52 second-year English-major students in Turkey, with a control group (N=26) receiving traditional dictionary-based instruction and an experimental group (N=26) receiving DDL-based CL instruction, each tested by pre-, post- and delayed post-tests of 30 fill-in-the-blank items. The article reports both groups "significantly improve their knowledge of the vertical prepositions"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The statistical ana lyses revealed that the two teaching methods, the traditional and the DDL -based CL-inspired instruction, helped the students significantly improve their knowledge of the vertical prepositions"
