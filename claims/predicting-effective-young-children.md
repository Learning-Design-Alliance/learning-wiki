## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2021

Garvin Brod. (2021). Generative Learning: Which Strategies for What Age? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09571-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports multiple classroom and laboratory studies with children: fourth-graders learning the conservation law, third- and seventh-graders in text comprehension, third- to fifth-graders on belief revision, and second-graders showing memory benefits of prediction before feedback.

> "a facts-learning study with second-graders revealed memory benefits of generating a prediction before seeing the correct answer relative to control conditions without the prior prediction"

### Garvin Brod 2021 (2)

Garvin Brod. (2021). Generative Learning: Which Strategies for What Age? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09571-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A study comparing fourth- and fifth-graders with university students learning trivia facts under prediction versus example generation found the strategies similarly effective for adults but predictions clearly superior for children, suggesting benefits beyond prior-knowledge activation.

> "Although these two GLSs were similarly effective for the university students, the children clearly gained more from generating predictions than from generating examples."
