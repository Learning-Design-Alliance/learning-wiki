Grading of GenAI answers on ten exam questions per topic set from the Moodle question bank, compared with the historical student distribution (student average 26.9, SD 3.62, median 27.33). ChatGPT scored highest at 31.01.

"Whereas last year ChatGPT's average score fell below the bottom quartile and Copilot's was below the median [4], this year only DeepSeek and Copilot scored within the upper quartile, with the remaining chatbots exceeding it. However, none of the AI tools reached the top 10th percentile."
