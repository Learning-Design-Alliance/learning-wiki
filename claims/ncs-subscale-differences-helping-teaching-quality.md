## Evidence

### Önal 2024

Önal, A. & Seren İntepeler, Ş. (2024). Comparison of self-competency and executive (charge) nurse competency assessments of clinical nurses. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.12955

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Subscale-level comparison from the same cross-sectional study (nurses n = 162, managers n = 21 assessing all nurses). Table 2 prints r = −0.186 for helping role, a small correlation, matching "a low negative correlation" between the two rater groups.

> "Comparing the VAS scores by subscales, statistically significant differences were found between the helping role (p = 0.001), teaching‐coaching (p = 0.028), and ensuring quality (p = 0.002) subscales. There was a low negative correlation between the evaluations of nurses and manager nurses."
