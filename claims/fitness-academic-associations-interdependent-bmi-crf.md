## Evidence

### Cadenas-Sanchez 2020

Cadenas-Sanchez, C., Migueles, J. H., Esteban-Cornejo, I., Mora-Gonzalez, J., Henriksson, P., Rodriguez-Ayllon, M., Molina-García, P., Löf, M., Labayen, I., Hillman, C. H., Catena, A., & Ortega, F. B. (2020). Fitness, physical activity, and academic achievement in overweight/obese children. Journal of Sports Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2020.1729516

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression (Model 2) in 106 children with overweight/obesity. Results "were attenuated, and significant associations disappeared except for those in the lower-limbs muscular strength (1-RM leg press)". No multicollinearity was observed (variance inflation factors < 3).

> "After additional adjustment of BMI and cardiorespiratory fitness or speed-agility (Model 2), the results observed for the field and laboratory tests were attenuated, and significant associations disappeared except for those in the lower-limbs muscular strength (1-RM leg press)"
