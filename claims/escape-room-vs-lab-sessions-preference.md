## Evidence

### Enrique Barra 2023

Enrique Barra, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Aldo Gordillo, Alejandro Pozo, Andres Muñoz-Arcentales, Javier Conde. (2023). Empowering Database Learning through Remote Educational Escape Rooms. IEEE Internet Computing. https://doi.org/10.1109/MIC.2023.3333199

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey comparison item from the N=247 perception questionnaire. The table prints M 4.3 (SD 1.0) for liking the escape room "to a larger extent than a laboratory session" and M 3.6 (SD 1.2) for learning more; no inferential test or effect size is reported for this contrast.

> "When comparing the escape room activity with computer lab sessions, students strongly agreed that they liked the former better, but only slightly agreed that they learned more."
