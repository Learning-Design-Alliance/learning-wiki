### Huang Yinxue 2025

Huang Yinxue, Yin Xintong, She Rui, Tian Linqing, Cai Ming, Han QunChao, Huo Lijun. (2025). A PBL-, CBL-, and seminar-based blended teaching model for chairside instruction of undergraduate dental students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08214-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 58 fourth-year dental students randomized to blended PBL-CBL-seminar (n=29) or LBL (n=29) chairside teaching; post-teaching Mini-CEX comparison showed "higher scores in interviewing skills (6.90 ± 0.94 vs. 6.21 ± 1.15, p < 0.05)" and significant differences on five further dimensions; clinical judgement was not significant (p > 0.05).

> "In the Mini-CEX scores, compared with the control group, students in the experimental group obtained higher scores in interviewing skills (6.90 ± 0.94 vs. 6.21 ± 1.15, p < 0.05), clinical examination skills(7.21 ± 0.90 vs. 6.17 ± 0.97, p < 0.05)"
