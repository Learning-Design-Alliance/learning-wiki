## Evidence

### Carolina Daza-Beltrán 2026

Carolina Daza-Beltrán, Angélica Paola Fajardo Escolar, Martha Caro, Daniel R. Suárez (2026). Situation Awareness Assessment for Anesthesia Residents (SAAAR): Development and Preliminary Evaluation of a Multimodal System. Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.1177/00187208251413484

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

One-group pre- and posttraining evaluation in which residents completed a theoretical session, a pretraining simulation scenario, a posttraining scenario of similar difficulty, and video-based debriefing. One-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed significant improvement in all four domains: perception r = .52, comprehension r = .93, projection r = .80, communication r = .76.

> "Specifically, perception (W = 37.00, p = .031, r = .52), comprehension (W = 5.00, p < .001, r = .93), projection (W = 17.00, p = .001, r = .80), and communication (W = 19.00, p = .003, r = .76) showed consistent increases posttraining."
