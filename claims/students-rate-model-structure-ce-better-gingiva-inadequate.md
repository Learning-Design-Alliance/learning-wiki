## Evidence

### Schaffrath K 2025

Schaffrath K, Ooms M, Bock A, Katz MS, Hölzle F, Modabber A. (2025). Improvement of surgical skills in students using a newly developed 3D printed osteotomy model of a partially retained wisdom tooth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08394-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-exercise student evaluation sheets (10-point Likert) compared between the 26 PE and 29 CE students with the Mann-Whitney test. The CE group's higher model-structure total is reported with the printed medians and p-value; no effect size is given.

> "In general, the model was evaluated with good results but was evaluated better by the CE group in terms of model structure (PE: 7.5 (3.3), CE: 10 (2); p = 0.001)."

### Schaffrath K 2025 (2)

Schaffrath K, Ooms M, Bock A, Katz MS, Hölzle F, Modabber A. (2025). Improvement of surgical skills in students using a newly developed 3D printed osteotomy model of a partially retained wisdom tooth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08394-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student evaluation of model components (Table 4). The article reports unanimous student criticism of the gingiva mask due to tearing, matching the oral surgeons' worst rating for the gingiva; no effect size is printed.

> "All students indicated that the gingiva mask was not adequate (Table 4) because it often tears."
