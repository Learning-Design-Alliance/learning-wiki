## Evidence

### Middeke Angélina 2018

Middeke Angélina, Anders Sven, Schuelper Madita, Raupach Tobias, Schuelper Nikolai. (2018). Training of clinical reasoning with a Serious Game versus small-group problem-based learning: A prospective study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203851

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Log-file analysis of the final 45-minute EMERGE session, in which all students treated four cases not previously discussed in either format; scores were compared with Mann-Whitney-U tests. The EMERGE group "achieved significantly higher aggregate scores than students in the PBL group in all four cases".

> "Students in the EMERGE group achieved significantly higher aggregate scores than students in the PBL group in all four cases (NSTEMI: 68.4 (IQR: 15.8)% vs. 47.4 (IQR: 15.8)%; p<0.001; pancreatitis: 66.7 (IQR: 13.3)% vs. 46.7 (IQR: 13.3)%; p<0.001; gastrointestinal haemorrhage: 58.3 (IQR: 16.7)% vs. 41.7 (IQR: 16.7)%; p<0.001; asthma exacerbation: 66.7 (IQR: 16.7)% vs. 58.3 (IQR: 16.7)%; p = 0.004)."
