## Evidence

### Majuri M 2024

Majuri M, Malin M, Antila A, Luukkonen R, Palosaari M, Reijula K. (2024). Perceived mental health skills and competence among occupational health physicians. Occupational Medicine (Oxford, England). https://doi.org/10.1093/occmed/kqae080

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey analysis of 59 trainers using Spearman correlation between years as a trainer and self-rated competence; the negative correlation was statistically significant. No standardised effect size was printed. The authors link this to prior findings that physicians in practice longer had less knowledge.

> "Trainer physicians’ perception of mental health competence is better during the first years of being an active trainer (mean 4.60, SD 0.55) than among all trainers (mean 3.97, SD 0.71), and that slowly decreases over time (Spearman −0.3394, P = 0.0085)"
