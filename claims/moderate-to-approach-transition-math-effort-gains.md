## Evidence

### Hornstra 2017

Hornstra, Lisette; Majoor, Marieke; Peetsma, Thea. (2017). Achievement goal profiles and developments in effort and achievement in upper elementary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 606-629. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12167

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Transition analysis of self-reported mathematics effort; the transition effect was not statistically significant in the multilevel model (b = 0.08) but the descriptive effect size was large. The article explicitly flags the N = 11 group size as requiring caution.

> "the group who transitioned from a moderate to an approach‐oriented profile showed strong gains in self‐reported effort compared to the other groups. The effect size for the difference in effort gains was d = .79, which can be considered a large effect. Nonetheless, given the small number of students who made this particular transition (N = 11), this number has to be interpreted with caution"
