`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis and Mann–Whitney tests on the number of patients hospitalised per group over the previous 5–6 months, from clinical records in the three-arm RCT. The article prints "Kruskal–Wallis test = 6.81, p = 0.005" for the overall comparison. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "the PBSP had significantly fewer patients re-hospitalised at Time 3 (n = 8, 22%) than both FPGP (n = 13, 37%) and UPC (n = 20, 57%), i.e., Mann–Whitney U test = 8.4 and 17.9, p = 0.05 and 0.001, respectively."
