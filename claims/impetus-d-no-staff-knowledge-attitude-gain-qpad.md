## Evidence

### Tropea Joanne 2022

Tropea Joanne, Nestel Debra, Johnson Christina, Hayes Barbara J., Hutchinson Anastasia F., Brand Caroline, Le Brian H., Blackberry Irene, Caplan Gideon A., Bicknell Ross, Hepworth Graham, Lim Wen K. (2022). Evaluation of IMproving Palliative care Education and Training Using Simulation in Dementia (IMPETUS-D) a staff simulation training intervention to improve palliative care of people with advanced dementia living in nursing homes: a cluster randomised controlled trial. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-022-02809-x

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Staff survey at 6 months using the qPAD knowledge test and attitude scale, completed by 330 staff (122 intervention, 208 control); 218 completed both baseline and follow-up. Mixed models adjusting for cluster showed "no significant between-group difference"; 93% of variability was between individuals.

> "There was no significant between-group difference in total qPAD scores; intervention group mean score=60.1 (95% CI 57.4-62.8) compared to the control group mean score=59.5 (95% CI 57.2-61.8), p = 0.77."
