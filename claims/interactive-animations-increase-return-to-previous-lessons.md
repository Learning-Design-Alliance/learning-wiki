## Evidence

### Magdin 2016

Magdin, M., & Turčáni, M. (2016). Modeling the Behavior of Students in E-Learning Courses on the Basis of Use Interactive Animations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086224

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental log analysis of transition matrices across academic years 2009/2010 to 2013/2014, comparing an experimental group using interactive animations with a control group. The authors report that the experimental group "kept returning to the previous study lessons at any time."

> "Based on the results of interactive matrices in individual academic years it is clear that th e experimental group, which kept the e-learning course with implemented interactive animations at their disposal for the whole period of study, kept returning to the previous study lessons at any time."
