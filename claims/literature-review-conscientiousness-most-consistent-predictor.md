### Corazzini Luca 2021

Corazzini Luca, D'Arrigo Silvia, Millemaci Emanuele, Navarra Pietro. (2021). The influence of personality traits on university performance: Evidence from Italian freshmen students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258586

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A narrative review tabulating roughly twenty prior studies of Big Five traits and post-secondary examination grades (Table 1). The article reports that "conscientiousness is the most meaningful dimension of personality for academic performance" and that relationships for other traits are "more heterogeneous and smaller in magnitude, with openness to experience the most sizeable factor."

> "Studies reported in Table 1 show that conscientiousness is the most meaningful dimension of personality for academic performance."
