### Flueckiger Lavinia 2014

Flueckiger Lavinia, Lieb Roselind, Meyer Andrea H., Mata Jutta. (2014). How Health Behaviors Relate to Academic Performance via Affect: An Intensive Longitudinal Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111080

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic prediction of examination success (dichotomized pass/fail of six obligatory exams; 47% passed) from average learning goal achievement in 72 students: "OR = 3.54, 95% CI: 1.42–8.81, p = .006".

> "For every 1-point increase in average learning goal achievement over the examination period, the odds of passing all six examinations increased by three and a half (OR = 3.54, 95% CI: 1.42–8.81, p = .006)"
