## Evidence

### Grasby KL 2026

Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Spearman correlations on the full twin sample (p < .007 threshold) showed weekend sleep duration positively associated with reading and maths in Grades 3, 5, and 7, and less snoring associated with higher scores in Grades 7 and 9; correlations were "small in size (varying in absolute value from .07 to .12)."

> "The significant correlations between the sleep variables and reading and maths outcomes were all small in size (varying in absolute value from .07 to .12)."
