### Fritúz Gábor 2026

Fritúz Gábor, Kovács Gergely, Kállai András, Nagy Tamás, Maár Csaba, Szvath Petra, Berczi Máté, Fábry Szabolcs, Skultéti Dalma, Iványi Zsolt, Gál János, Kovács Enikő. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Across three skill retention assessments (n=20, 16, 23), correct performance remained high for core steps, e.g. shouting for help 90–100%, checking signs of life 95–100%, and chest release, rhythm and bag–mask ventilation mostly above 85%.

> "we found that our BLS training was effective, as both short- and long-term skill retention were acceptable in the most important BLS steps: activating the chain of survival and performing high-quality chest compressions."
