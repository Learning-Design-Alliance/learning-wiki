## Evidence

### Bellini Raffaella 2024

Bellini Raffaella, Rossettini Giacomo, Letafatkar Amir, Dell’Isola Andrea, Battista Simone. (2024). Physiotherapy students’ academic performance and satisfaction after following a chest physiotherapy course in hybrid mode: a case-control study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-76051-3

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Retrospective case-control study of 41 physiotherapy students randomly split by Excel RAND into face-to-face (n=20) and remote (n=21) groups for a hybrid seminar. Quartile regression with bootstrapped CIs showed a median total-score difference of 6 with "an effect size of 1.48 [95%CI 0.72;2.23]" in favour of face-to-face.

> "The total score presented respectively a median of 19 [Q1-Q3; 15-21.5] in the remote group and 24.5 [22.25–26.75] in the face-to-face one, with a median difference of 6 [95%CI 2.89;9.10] in favour of face-to-face lecture and an effect size of 1.48 [95%CI 0.72;2.23]."
