Adams 2017¶

Adams, C. M., Ford, T. G., Forsyth, P. B., Ware, J. K., Olsen, J. J., Lepine, J. A., Sr., Barnes, L. B., Khojasteh, J., & Mwavita, M. (2017). Next generation accountability: A vision for improvement under ESSA. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/next-generation-accountability

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report's conceptual analysis of Oklahoma's A–F letter grades argues that composite indicators derived from test scores cannot capture competencies such as "critical thinking and problem solving" and collaboration. It cites a 2012 RAND study finding fewer than 2% of math items assessed deeper cognitive competencies.

"At best, a summative indicator derived from student test scores may yield information about basic content knowledge and skills, but it does not capture other cognitive and noncognitive competencies required for productive functioning in the emerging economy."
