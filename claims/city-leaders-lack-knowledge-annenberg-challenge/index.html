Evidence¶

Dorothy Shipps 1999¶

Dorothy Shipps, Karin Sconzert, with Holly Swyers. (1999). The Chicago Annenberg Challenge: The First Three Years. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/chicago-annenberg-challenge-first-three-years

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Semi-structured interviews with 70 organizational leaders from eight sectors of the Challenge's institutional environment, conducted summer–fall 1997, with qualitative content analysis and tabulation. The authors report "about half of the business and government leaders would not even speculate on its goals or strategy."

"About half of the business and government leaders would not even speculate on its goals or strategy; however, most interviewees had advice for the Challenge Board. About half of these leaders suggested broadening the focus to include influencing policy systemwide, and specifically to focus on policies to enhance teacher skills and competencies."
