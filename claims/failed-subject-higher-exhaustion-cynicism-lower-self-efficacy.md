## Evidence

### Molero Jurado María del Mar 2021

Molero Jurado María del Mar, Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen, Martos Martínez África, Barragán Martín Ana Belén, Simón Márquez María del Mar, Gázquez Linares José Jesús. (2021). Emotional intelligence as a mediator in the relationship between academic performance and burnout in high school students. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253552

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian independent-samples t-test comparing 274 students who had never failed with 1008 who had failed, on the MBI-SS burnout subscales. The study found "significantly higher scores in exhaustion" and cynicism among those who had failed, and higher self-efficacy among those who had not; BF10 values supported H1 for all three dimensions.

> "those who had failed had significantly higher scores in exhaustion (t(1284) = -3.20, p < 0.01, d = -0.21) and cynicism (t(1284) = -6.03, p < 0.001, d = -0.41), while those who had never failed a subject had a higher mean score in self-efficacy (t(1284) = 3.67, p < 0.001, d = 0.74)"
