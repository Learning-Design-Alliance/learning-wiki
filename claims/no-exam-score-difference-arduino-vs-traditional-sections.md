## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini 2023

Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini. (2023). Evaluation of the effectiveness of an introductory mechanics Lab with Arduino and smartphone. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of overall final exam scores between the reformed and traditional lab sections across two course editions. The authors read this as showing the reformed format, despite the time cost of learning Arduino and making decisions, "did not put students at a disadvant age in the course".

> "Analysis of overall student scores on the final exam of the 2020/2021 and 2021/2022 editions [6] reveals no measurable difference between students in the Arduino/smartphone lab section and students enrolled in the "traditional" lab sections."
