## Evidence

### Kamali D 2018

Kamali D, Illing J. (2018). How can positive and negative trainer feedback in the operating theatre impact a surgical trainee's confidence and well-being: a qualitative study in the north of England. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017935

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of the narrative interview findings; the authors suggest negative emotions require more thinking and processing, so trainees reflect on negative events more, making them easier to recall. Respondents had also found very positive feedback a rare occurrence with examples often many months old.

> "Interestingly, trainees found negative events much easier to recall, which is also in concordance with previously published literature."
