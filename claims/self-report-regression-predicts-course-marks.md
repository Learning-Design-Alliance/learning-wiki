## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First set of hierarchical regressions with course marks as the dependent variable among 271 undergraduates: "In model 2, perceptions of integration (β = 0.17, p < 0.001), self-reported surface approach (β = -0.14, p = 0.02)" and self-reported learning time were entered; together they contributed approximately 10% of the variance in course marks.

> "In model 2, perceptions of integration (β = 0.17, p < 0.001), self-reported surface approach (β = -0.14, p = 0.02), and self-reported learning time (β = 0.19, p < 0.001) all significantly predicted course marks, with surface approach being a negative predictor, whereas perceptions of integration and self-reported learning time were positive predictors."
