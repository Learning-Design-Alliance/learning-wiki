## Evidence

### Iannella G 2025

Iannella G, Pace A, Greco A, De Virgilio A, Bellizzi MG, Croce E, Lechien JR, Maniaci A, Cocuzza S, Gioacchini FM, Re M, Collettini A, Gatti L, Perrone T, Simon F, Gargula S, Magliulo G. (2025). The SAPIENS 3D-printed temporal bone model: a real tool for advanced otologic surgery education. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology 282(6):3051–3063. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-024-09199-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative questionnaire (10 items, 5-point Likert scale) completed by residents after dissecting both 3D models and a fresh-frozen human temporal bone. In the transparent-model sub-analysis, facial nerve dissection reached 4.8 while mastoid dissection and ossicular chain scored lower at 3.2 each; in the opaque-model sub-analysis, epitympanic and posterior tympanotomy dissections scored 4.67.

> "The 3D models were rated as very similar to the human TB across all surgical steps. The total scores were 4/5 for the transparent TB model and 4.2/5 for the opaque TB model."
