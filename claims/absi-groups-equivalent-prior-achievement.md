# Treatment and comparison groups showed no prior differences in physics and chemistry achievement before the intervention

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One-way ANOVA found no statistical performance difference between groups on modern physics midterm and final scores or general chemistry midterm and final scores. [→ Demirbag 2014](#demirbag-2014)

## Evidence

### Demirbag 2014

Demirbag, M., & Gunel, M. (2014). Integrating Argument-Based Science Inquiry with Modal Representations: Impact on Science Achievement, Argumentation, and Writing Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(1), 386-391. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.1.1632

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing groups' previous year physics and chemistry grades to establish baseline equivalence in the quasi-experimental study; all four comparisons showed p > 0.05, so equivalence was not tested beyond non-significance.

> "ANOV A results yielded no statistical performance difference between the groups on modern physics midterm scores (F(1,104)=3.36, p>0.05), final scores (F(1,104)=0.35, p> 0.05) as well as general chemistry midterm scores (F(1,104) = 0.26, p> 0.05) and final scores (F(1,104)=0.27, p> 0.05)."
