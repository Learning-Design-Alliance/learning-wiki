## Evidence

### Santaló María Isabel 2019

Santaló María Isabel, Gibbons Sandra, Naylor Patti-Jean. (2019). Using Food Models to Enhance Sugar Literacy among Older Adolescents: Evaluation of a Brief Experiential Nutrition Education Intervention. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081763

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial, repeated measures ANOVA for daily portions knowledge: F(1, 201) = 59.84, p = 0.00, printed effect size η2 = 0.23 in Table 2; intervention rose from 23.0% to 59.3% correct, controls from 18.3% to 21.0%.

> "The intervention group correctly answered 60% of the daily food group portion questions compared to 20% of correct answers for the control group at follow-up."

### Santaló María Isabel 2019 (2)

Santaló María Isabel, Gibbons Sandra, Naylor Patti-Jean. (2019). Using Food Models to Enhance Sugar Literacy among Older Adolescents: Evaluation of a Brief Experiential Nutrition Education Intervention. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081763

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial, repeated measures ANOVA (Table 2): F(1, 201) = 64.68, p = 0.00, printed effect size η2 = 0.24 for added sugar content knowledge; intervention rose from 18.5% to 47.7% correct.

> "Knowledge about sugar content in foods and beverages increased from 18% to 48% correct answers for the intervention group and was significantly different than the correct responses in the follow-up measure for control group."
