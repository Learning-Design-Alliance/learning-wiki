## Evidence

### Zhao H and Ji J 2026

Zhao H and Ji J (2026) Human-written and AI-generated revisions: an appraisal analysis of dialogic positioning in argumentative writing. Front. Educ. 11:1904852. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1904852

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on the 168-essay corpus reported a significant Draft Type × Appraisal Subsystem interaction, F(4, 492) = 28.209, p < .001. Attitude means rose from 11.574 (first) to 15.677 (peer) and 18.429 (AI); Graduation from 4.737 to 5.074 and 6.006; Engagement "remained stable across all three draft types".
