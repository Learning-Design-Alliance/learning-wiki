## Evidence

### Harris Cooper 2010

Harris Cooper, Ashley Batts Allen, Erika A. Patall, and Amy L. Dent. (2010). Effects of Full-Day Kindergarten on Academic Achievement and Social Development. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309359185

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 40 reports (254 effect sizes, 55 samples, 1979–2009) comparing FDK to HDK on immediate achievement. The weighted mean d-index was d = 0.24 (fixed) and d = 0.31 (random), each "significantly different from 0"; unadjusted effects ranged from d = –0.98 to +1.83.

> "Using fixed-effect assumptions, the weighted mean d-index was 0.24 and was significantly different from 0 (95% CI = 0.23/0.25). Using a random-effects model the weighted average d-index was 0.31 (95% CI = 0.25/0.38)."
