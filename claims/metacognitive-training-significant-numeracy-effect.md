### Taiwo Oladunni Gbenga-Akanmu & Philip Olu Jegede 2019

Taiwo Oladunni Gbenga-Akanmu & Philip Olu Jegede. (2019). Influence of Meta-cognitive Training and Cognitive Styles on Numeracy Achievement of Nigerian Pupils’ in Computer-mediated Classrooms. World Journal of Education, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v9n1p221

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of pretest–posttest control-group quasi-experiment with 39 primary three pupils in two intact classes; the article reports a significant main effect (F = 11.16; p<0.05) with pre-test score as covariate. No effect size is printed for this main effect row.

> "From Table 1, the main effect indicated that there exists a significant (F = 11.16; p<0.05) difference in the pupils' performance."
