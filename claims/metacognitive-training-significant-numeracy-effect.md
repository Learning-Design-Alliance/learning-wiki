`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of pretest–posttest control-group quasi-experiment with 39 primary three pupils in two intact classes; the text reports a significant main effect (F = 11.16; p<0.05) with pre-test score as covariate. In Table 1, however, F = 11.16 is the row labelled "Field-dependent and independent" (cognitive style), which carries a partial eta squared of 0.12; the Group (training) row is F = 13.15, p = 0.0023, with no effect size printed. The table's degrees of freedom (pre-test score df = 14) are irregular, so the attribution of each statistic is uncertain.

> "From Table 1, the main effect indicated that there exists a significant (F = 11.16; p<0.05) difference in the pupils' performance."
