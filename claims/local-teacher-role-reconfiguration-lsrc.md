### Yuling Sun 2024

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the study of local teachers' efforts. Teacher P7 described more thorough lesson preparation, anticipating where students might struggle, and laying groundwork before class to prevent difficulties during streamed lessons.

> "You might think our lesson preparation pressure is reduced since we are not lecturing. It is quite the opposite. We prepare even more thoroughly than before."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the findings section on local teachers' efforts, based on informal student conversations during the three-week observation at the rural school. Students viewed local teacher support as essential to understanding streamed content.

> "all students we informally communicated with also agreed that the role of local teachers was significant for their adoption and understanding of lecture content."
