q2 · i? · review · r3

Epistemic network analysis (ENA Web Tool version 1.7.0) of coded study characteristics across the four approaches. The review summarises that unimodal and horizontal studies feature large samples (many over 100 participants) in authentic higher education, integrated studies typically have fewer than 50 participants, and all approaches were more often conducted in authentic than laboratory settings.

"the unimodal and horizontal approaches predominantly rely on large sample sizes and focus on individual learning in higher education, whereas the vertical approach often uses smaller samples and diverse data streams for collaborative learning tasks in secondary education."
