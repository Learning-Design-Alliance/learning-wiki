# Elementary schools in Math in Common districts exceeded predicted CAASPP mathematics performance at significantly higher rates than other California schools between 2016 and 2018

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The percentage of MiC elementary schools performing beyond regression-based prediction rose from 48 percent in 2016 to 55 percent in 2018, significantly higher than non-MiC-selected and remaining California districts (p < .05). [→ Perry 2019](#perry-2019)

## Evidence

### Perry 2019

Perry, R., & Huang, K. (2019). Spotlight on student achievement: Analyses of statewide assessment data in Math in Common districts. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-level profile regression analysis using statewide data predicted school achievement from prior performance and school characteristics; Table 3 shows MiC elementary schools beyond prediction rose from 48 percent (2016) to 55 percent (2018), versus 47 percent in non-MiC-selected districts, a "significantly higher (p < .05)" rate.

> "schools in MiC districts made significantly more progress, compared to schools in the two other comparison groups, in exceeding their predicted performance"
