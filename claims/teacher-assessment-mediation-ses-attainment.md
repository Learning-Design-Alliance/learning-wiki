# Primary school teacher assessment at age 11 alone mediates 59.7% of the association between parental SES and adolescent educational attainment

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Weinberg D. 2019

Weinberg D., Stevens G. W. J. M., Finkenauer C., Brunekreef B., Smit H. A., Wijga A. H. (2019). The pathways from parental and neighbourhood socioeconomic status to adolescent educational attainment: An examination of the role of cognitive ability, teacher assessment, and educational expectations. PLoS ONE 14(5): e0216803. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216803

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM Model 2b (SES → teacher assessment → attainment) on the PIAMA cohort; Table 6 prints standardised paths SES → teacher assessment 0.41 and teacher assessment → attainment 0.70, with the separate mediation test explaining "59.7% of the total association".

> "Models 2b and 2c showed similar results for teacher assessment (at age 11) and educational expectations (at age 14); both were predicted by SES and in turn predicted adolescent educational attainment at age 17. Separate mediation tests found that each significantly mediated the path from SES to educational attainment, with teacher assessment explaining 59.7% of the total association and educational expectations 2.2% of the total association."
