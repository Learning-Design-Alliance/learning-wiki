Denniston C 2017¶

Denniston C, Molloy E, Nestel D, Woodward-Kron R, Keating JL. (2017). Learning outcomes for communication skills across the health professions: a systematic literature review and qualitative synthesis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014570

Systematic review and qualitative synthesis searching MEDLINE, PsycINFO, ERIC, CINAHL plus and Scopus to August 2016. The review reports that "168 papers met the eligibility criteria" and 1669 outcomes were extracted, refined to a final set of 205 presented in 4 domains.
