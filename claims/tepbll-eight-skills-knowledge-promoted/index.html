The review authors' synthesis of the 31 included studies, stated in the Conclusion. This is a narrative synthesis rather than a pooled quantitative estimate; no meta-analysis was conducted due to heterogeneity. The review reports that "eight language skills and knowledge would be improved after the application of TEPBLL."

"Thus, eight language skills and knowledge would be improved after the application of TEPBLL: speaking skills, listening skills, reading skills, writing skills, vocabulary knowledge, creative and critical thinking skills, intercultural awareness, and content knowledge."
