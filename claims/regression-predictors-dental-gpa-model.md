## Evidence

### Almalki Sultan A. 2024

Almalki Sultan A., AlJameel AlBandary H., Alghomlas Ziyad, Alothman Tameem, Alhajri Fawaz. (2024). Assessing the predictive validity of pre-admission criteria on dental students’ academic performance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03839-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression (backward method based on Akaike's information criteria) on the 900-student dataset. The study reports "an F-statistic of 11.22 on 35 and 851 degrees of freedom, with a p-value of less than 0.001" and an adjusted R-squared of 0.2876. No standardized effect size is printed for the omnibus model.

> "The model's Multiple R-squared value is 0.3158, indicating that it explains approximately 31.58% of the variance in GPA scores."

### Almalki Sultan A. 2024 (2)

Almalki Sultan A., AlJameel AlBandary H., Alghomlas Ziyad, Alothman Tameem, Alhajri Fawaz. (2024). Assessing the predictive validity of pre-admission criteria on dental students’ academic performance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03839-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Initial linear regression with bootstrapped p-values (1000 samples). Table 4 prints standardized coefficients including High School Grade Beta = 0.226 (p < 0.001) and Age Beta = -0.372 (p < 0.001); the model's R-squared was 0.241.

> "The regression model, as shown in Table 4, revealed that High School Grade and Achievement Test Score had significant positive impacts on GPA, while Age and Suspended status showed negative effects."
