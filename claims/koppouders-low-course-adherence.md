### van der Zanden Rianne AP 2010

van der Zanden Rianne AP, Speetjens Paula AM, Arntz Karlijn SE, Onrust Simone A. (2010). Online Group Course for Parents With Mental Illness: Development and Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1394

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Adherence monitoring in the pilot cohort of 59 course starters. The article reports "20% (12/59) all eight sessions"; dropout reasons often involved unstable home situations such as relational problems, divorce, or relocation.

> "Of the 59 parents who began the course, 42% (25/59) took part in fewer than four sessions, and 57% (34/59) in four sessions or more; 37% (22/59) attended seven or eight sessions, and 20% (12/59) all eight sessions."
