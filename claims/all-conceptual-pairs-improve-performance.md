### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Student Expertise in Introductory Physics with Isomorphic Problems, Part II: Effect of Some Potential Factors on Problem Solving and Transfer. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07678](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07678)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 2 used IPPs in which neither question was quantitative (e.g., ice-skater/white-dwarf angular momentum pair). Table 1 Chi-square comparisons support improvement; interviews show students explicitly transferring reasoning between paired contexts.

> "Table 1 shows that, in support of hypothesis H4, students’ performance often improved when both questions of an IPP were given compared to when only one of the two questions was given."
