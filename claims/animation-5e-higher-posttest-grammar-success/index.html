Evidence¶

Özcan and Kiliç 2017¶

Mehmet Fatih Özcan, Latife Kirbaşoğlu Kiliç. (2017). The Effects of Animation Supported 5E Model on Teaching 'Indicative and Subjunctive Moods' in 7th Grade Turkish Lesson. Universal Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051407

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental t-test comparison of post-test scores between the experimental group (animation-supported 5E) and control group (current programme), each with 25 analysed students per the printed table. The article reports "a significant difference between the levels of the value of winning" with t(48)=2.223, p=.007; no effect size is printed.

"T48=.2.223 and P=,007 p<0.05 for significance, it is observed that significant level. After the application of this finding experimental and control groups indicate that there is a significant difference between the levels of the value of winning."
