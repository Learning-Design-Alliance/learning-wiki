## Evidence

### Fathima Ameen 2026

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis of data from 23 middle school tutors teaching SimStudent in APLUS, with %KBR computed from responses to adaptive follow-up questions. The article reports "%KBR also significantly predicted conceptual post-test performance" with β = .138, p < 0.05, smaller than the pre-test path.

> "Although %KBR also significantly predicted conceptual post-test performance ( β = .138,p < 0.05), the effect size was roughly half that of conceptual pre-test on conceptual post-test ( β = .301,p =0 .001)."

### Fathima Ameen 2026 (2)

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The authors' summary of the path analysis model (CFI 1.0, TLI 1.0, RMSEA 0.0, SRMR 0.00) fitted to the classroom study data, stating that "the percentage of knowledge-building responses to follow-up questions significantly predicts conceptual and procedural post-test scores."

> "Path analysis revealed that the percentage of knowledge-building responses to follow-up questions significantly predicts conceptual and procedural post-test scores."
