## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to expectation, MMI score did not correlate significantly with any of the three OSCE stations directly or indirectly assessing communication skills (ρ = .14, .02, and .13, all p > .05). [→ Knorr M 2018](#knorr-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Knorr M 2018

Knorr M, Schwibbe A, Ehrhardt M, Lackamp J, Zimmermann S, Hampe W. (2018). Validity evidence for the Hamburg multiple mini-interview. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1208-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 3 (same OSCE data, n = 89 with MMI data): station-level Spearman correlations between MMI and the one communication station and two history-taking stations were all non-significant; no effect size printed. The expected stronger station-level relationship was not supported.

> "There were no significant correlations between MMI score and specific scores on the three OSCE stations, which directly or indirectly focus on communication skills (communication: ρ = .14, p > .05, 95% CI: −.07/.34; history taking 1: ρ = .02, p > .05, 95% CI: −.20/.22; history taking 2: ρ = .13, p > .05, 95% CI: −.08/.34)."
