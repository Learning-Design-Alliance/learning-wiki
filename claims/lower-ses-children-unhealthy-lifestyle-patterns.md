## Evidence

### Yamada Masaaki 2019

Yamada Masaaki, Sekine Michikazu, Tatsuse Takashi, Asaka Yukiko. (2019). Association between lifestyle, parental smoke, socioeconomic status, and academic performance in Japanese elementary school children: the Super Diet Education Project. Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12199-019-0776-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc chi-squared and trend analyses by SES (n = 1663) showed significant trends for screen time ≥2 h (trend p = 0.02), late bedtime (trend p = 0.04), daytime sleepiness (trend p < 0.01), and cram school attendance (trend p = 0.02).

> "Children of lower SES tended to watch screen longer, stay up late, and feel sleepy and were less likely to go to cram school."
