## Evidence

### Wijnia 2024

Wijnia, L., Noordzij, G., Arends, L. R. A., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Loyens, S. M. M. (2024). The Effects of Problem-Based, Project-Based, and Case-Based Learning on Students' Motivation: a Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09864-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis of instructional method type in the three-level and CHE models across 139 subsamples. The analysis found PBL, PjBL, and CBL "had similar effects on students' motivation, F(2, 285) = 0.61, p = 0.546". Table 4 shows combined d values of 0.453 (PBL), 0.615 (PjBL), and 0.473 (CBL).

> "Our analyses indicated that PBL, PjBL, and CBL had similar effects on students' motivation, F(2, 285) = 0.61, p = 0.546."
