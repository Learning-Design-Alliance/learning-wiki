# Foreign language classroom anxiety correlates negatively with English exam performance among Thai secondary students

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` A weak, statistically significant negative Pearson correlation exists between FLCAS scores and English midterm exam performance across all groups (r = -.163, n = 423, p = .001). [→ Tanielian 2014](#tanielian-2014)
`q3 i3` Within the immersion group, a strong, statistically significant negative Pearson correlation exists between FLCAS scores and English midterm scores (r = -.524, n = 37, p = .001). [→ Tanielian 2014 (2)](#tanielian-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Tanielian 2014

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson product-moment correlation across all 424 sampled students comparing FLCAS scores with English midterm exam performance. The article reports a "very weak, negative correlation" that is statistically significant at the 0.01 level.

> "There was a very weak, negative correlation between FLCAS and English exam performance (r = - .163, n = 423, p = .001)."

### Tanielian 2014 (2)

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson product-moment correlation within the immersion group (n = 37) comparing FLCAS scores with English midterm exam scores. The article reports a "strong, negative correlation" (r = - .524) that is statistically significant.

> "A strong, negative correlation was found between FLCAS levels and English midterm scores, which was significant (r = - .524, n = 37, p = .001)."
