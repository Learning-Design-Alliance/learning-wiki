Hochberg N and Waitzman R 2026¶

Hochberg N and Waitzman R (2026). From information providers to learning designers: heutagogical teaching practices in AI-enhanced education. Front. Educ. 11:1905833. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1905833

q2 · i2 · qualitative · r2

Exploratory quantitative strand comparing 61 consenting pre-program with 26 consenting post-program anonymous submissions using Welch independent-samples t tests. Reflective teaching showed a mean difference of 0.40 with Hedges' g = 0.41, 95% CI [−0.05, 0.87]; unadjusted p = 0.077, Holm-adjusted p = 0.307.

"Reflective teaching was descriptively higher in the post-program group, M = 3.27, SD = 0.92, than in the pre-program group, M = 2.87, SD = 0.98, with a mean difference of 0.40, 95% CI [−0.04, 0.84]"

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Exploratory Welch independent-samples comparisons of unlinked pre- and post-program questionnaire submissions on four author-developed indicators. The article reports that "None of the four comparisons was statistically significant after Holm adjustment, and every confidence interval included zero," providing limited, imprecise corroboration rather than evidence of program effects.
