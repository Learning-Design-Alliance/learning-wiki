## Evidence

### Hongfei Yan 2026

Hongfei Yan, Jiangkai Xiong, Yiqing Li, and Chong Chen. (2026). From Evaluated Models to Evaluation Aids: A Multi-Evidence Study of LLM-Based Difficulty Calibration for Programming Examinations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07523 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.07523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.07523)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional review-based calibration of 79 problems from 11 parallel-class final machine exams, using a single auditable API reviewer. Printed effect size: Spearman rho = -0.871; "Table 7 shows a strong negative correlation between AI review diﬃculty and actual student problem pass rates."

> "The Spearman correlation between raw AI overall diﬃculty and student pass rate is -0.871 (p<0.001, Fisher-approximate 95% CI [-0.916, -0.805]), and -0.857 after exposure adjustment."
