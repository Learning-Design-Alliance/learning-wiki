Jacobson 2026¶

Jacobson, M. J.; Rodriguez-Rivera, G.; Drineas, P.; and Xue, Y. (2026). Teaching AI, Robotics, & Community: A Hubs-Based K–12 Education Framework for Reaching Rural Schools. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18072

q1 · i? · design · r2

Numerical Monte Carlo simulation (1,000 runs per level, 40 years) of the spatially-explicit Markov model instantiated for Indiana's 1,925 public K–12 schools and 32 potential college hubs, reported in the Spatially-Explicit Markov Model of Growth section. This is a simulation result, not an empirical measurement.

"After 40 years,ARC offpro- duces a mean of 161 programs, while even optimism level 1 ofFull ARCproduces 338, more than twice as many. Atlevel6,themodelproduces992programsandreaches1,415 schools."
