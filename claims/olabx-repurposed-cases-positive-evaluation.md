## Evidence

### Dafli E 2015

Dafli E, Antoniou P, Ioannidis L, Dombros N, Topps D, Bamidis PD. (2015). Virtual Patients on the Semantic Web: A Proof-of-Application Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3933

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Output quality evaluation in which 48 undergraduates chose approximately 5 cases from a pool of about 30 repurposed virtual patients and completed the validated eViP questionnaire (7 subsets, 14 items). The article reports "Most evaluators provided positive statements regarding the virtual patient content"; Likert ratings are printed in Table 5 with no effect size.

> "Most evaluators provided positive statements regarding the virtual patient content when the educational experiences with both the content and the system were considered."
