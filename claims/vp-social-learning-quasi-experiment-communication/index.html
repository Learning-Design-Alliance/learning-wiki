Evidence¶

Deng Jing 2025¶

Deng Jing, Tan Yifei, Chen Jing, Guo Xiujing, Wang Ling, Xie Chuan. (2025). Virtual reality simulation to teach communication for undergraduate nursing students: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08450-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

A quasi-experimental study with 40 senior nursing students (20 VP-based social learning, 20 conventional) reported significantly greater learning performance, self-efficacy and communication skills in the experimental group. Non-randomized allocation is the stated limitation.

"Students’ learning performance, self-efficacy, and communication skills were significantly greater in the experimental group than in the control group."

Hobenu Kafui A. 2025¶

Hobenu Kafui A., Adefuye Anthonio O., Naab Florence, Nyoni Champion N. (2025). Strategies to enhance clinical teaching and learning in undergraduate nursing education: A scoping review. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305789

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Hwang et al.'s comparison of a virtual patient-based social learning system against conventional didactic education, finding "significantly higher post-test scores" for communication skills in the intervention group. No effect size is printed.

"Hwang et al.'s findings indicated that the virtual patient-based social learning approach improved students' learning achievement and self-efficacy in the intervention group more than in the control group when compared to the conventional didactic education approach. In terms of communication skills, the intervention group had significantly higher post-test scores than the control group"
