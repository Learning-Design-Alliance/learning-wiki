Evidence¶

Mireles 2014¶

Mireles, S. V., Acee, T. W., & Gerber, L. N. (2014). FOCUS: Sustainable Mathematics Successes. Journal of Developmental Education, 38(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071436

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Within-subjects pretest-posttest study of FOCUS Intervention students using the THEA mathematics test at semester start and end; 115 of 127 students completed both tests. The paired t-test showed a "statistically significant increase from pretest to posttest" with d = .61. Passing-standard attainment rose from 23.5% at pretest to 52.2% at posttest.

"We found that students made a statistically significant increase from pretest to posttest ( Mpre = 210.43, SD pre = 28.85, Mpost = 227.61, SDpost = 28.49, Mdiff = 17.17, t = -6.51 (114), p = .01, d = .61)."
