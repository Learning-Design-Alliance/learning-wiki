Descriptive survey statistics from the degree-information section; the report states the differences were not significant. Quote: "neither difference was significant".

> "The number of courses required for “Astrophysics” majors is, on average, slightly higher (21±6) than the number of courses required for “Astronomy” majors (18±5). Similarly, the number of courses required for a BS (22 ±5) is slightly higher than for a BA (15 ± 3). However, neither difference was significant."
