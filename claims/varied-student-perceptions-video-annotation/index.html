Evidence¶

Hengtao Tang 2025¶

Hengtao Tang, Jessica Perry Ellison. (2025). Self-regulated learning and video annotation in a high school acting classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09699-z

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative theme with categories Benefits to SRL, Benefits to acting, and Issues with video annotation tools. One student "felt embarrassed looking at a video of myself"; another issue "included the additional workload."

"This theme described participants developed varied perceptions about video annotation. Video annotation enables users to create notes using timestamps, text, and other tools in order to facilitate self-regulated behaviors, such as self-monitoring, self-reflection, self-evaluation, and self-judgment"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The authors hypothesize that self-regulated learning acts as a mediating factor linking video annotation advantages to acting performance improvement — related
  • Interviewed students reported that the SRL intervention elicited self-regulated learning behaviors across goal setting, strategic planning, self-observation, and self-reflection — related
  • A six-week SRL video annotation intervention was followed by a statistically significant increase in high school students' ITS-AR monologue scores — related
  • Participants unanimously reported increased confidence in acting performance over the course of the intervention — related
  • Qualitative findings indicate perceived benefits of recognition, meaning-making, and practical tips, alongside mixed emotional responses including distress — related
