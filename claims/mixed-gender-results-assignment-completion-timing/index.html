Evidence¶

Mingyu Feng 2016¶

Mingyu Feng, Jeremy Roschelle, Craig Mason, Ruchi Bhanot. (2016). Investigating Gender Differences on Homework in Middle School Mathematics. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining, June 29-July 2, 2016, Raleigh, NC. https://educationaldatamining.org/edm2016/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

HLM overall results (Table 2) on 1033 students; late_perc showed no difference (difference -0.000, p = 0.907).

"Girls tend to complete more assignments on time than boys, but the difference is only marginally significant ( p = .086)."

Mingyu Feng 2016 (2)¶

Mingyu Feng, Jeremy Roschelle, Craig Mason, Ruchi Bhanot. (2016). Investigating Gender Differences on Homework in Middle School Mathematics. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining, June 29-July 2, 2016, Raleigh, NC. https://educationaldatamining.org/edm2016/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Group-wise HLM results (Table 3): late_perc difference -0.021 (p = 0.032) in Progressing or Below, +0.018 (p = 0.034) in Near Proficient, -0.005 (p = 0.521) in Proficient or Above; in the Proficient or Above group girls were more likely to complete on time.

"Results regarding assignment completion status are mixed. Low -performing girls completed fewer assignments after they were due than low -performing boys did; yet in the Near Proficient group, girls completed more as signments late than boys did."
