## Evidence

### Xie Yu Jin 2019

Xie Yu Jin, Cao De Pin, Sun Tao, Yang Li Bin. (2019). The effects of academic adaptability on academic burnout, immersion in learning, and academic performance among Chinese medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1640-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression (M2) in a cross-sectional survey of 1977 medical students from four Chinese medical schools, with gender, age, and grade as control variables. The article reports "a significant negative predictive effect on academic burnout (Beta = − 0.705, P<0.01)". No effect-size magnitude beyond the standardized coefficient is printed.
