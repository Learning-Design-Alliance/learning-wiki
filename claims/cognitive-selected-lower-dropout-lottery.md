## Evidence

### Lucieer SM 2015

Lucieer SM, Stegers-Jager KM, Rikers RMJP, Themmen APN. (2015). Non-cognitive selected students do not outperform lottery-admitted students in the pre-clinical stage of medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. [doi:10.1007/s10459-015-9610-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9610-4)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression with Wald statistics on dropout (quitting voluntarily within 2 years or not earning all first-year ECTS within 2 years); dropout OR for cognitive selected students was .32 (.14–.77) versus lottery, significant at p = .01, and no effect size was printed.

> "In addition, cognitive-selected students had a significantly lower probability to drop out than lottery-admitted students (11 % vs. 21 %)"
