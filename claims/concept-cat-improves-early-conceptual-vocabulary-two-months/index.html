Evidence¶

Miguel Subosa 2025¶

Miguel Subosa, Fin Oades, Erin Dysart, Louise Tracey, Anna Brian, and Elena Rosa Speciani. (2025). Concept Cat: A two-armed cluster randomised controlled trial — Evaluation report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED680933

q4 · i? · causal · r2

Two-armed waitlisted cluster RCT of 89 EY settings; primary outcome was the 'Basic Concepts' subtest of CELF Preschool-2 UK. The results table reports effect size "0.18 (0.05 – 0.30)", +2 months' progress, p=0.01, for 902 children.
