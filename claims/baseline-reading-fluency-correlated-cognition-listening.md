## Evidence

### Shiels 2025

Shiels, L., Carew, P., Tomlin, D. & Rance, G. (2025). The impact of classroom soundfield systems on reading fluency in normal-hearing students. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00350-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of baseline measures in the cohort (Table 1 reports N of 128–139 per pair). Table 1 prints WARP correlations of r = 0.228 (LiSN-S), r = 0.171 (TONI-4), r = 0.242 (Digit span), r = 0.290 (attention quotient), and age r = 0.090 (p = 0.292).

> "At baseline, reading ﬂuency was signiﬁcantly associated with partici- pant IQ, attention, auditory working memory, and speech discrimination in noise. There was no association between reading ﬂuency and age."
