Tugba Inciman Celik 2021¶

Tugba Inciman Celik, Tolga Cay & Sedat Kanadli. (2021). The Effect of Total Physical Response Method on Vocabulary Learning/Teaching: A Mixed Research Synthesis. English Language Teaching, 14(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v14n12p154

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Categorical moderator analysis in the meta-analysis classified the 13 studies by education level (young vs adult). The moderator was significant (p=0.030); adult studies (k=3) averaged "1.717 1.149 2.286" versus 0.942 for young learners (k=10).
