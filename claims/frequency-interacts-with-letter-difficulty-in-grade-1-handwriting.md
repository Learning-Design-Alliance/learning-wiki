# The lexical frequency effect on writing a difficult first letter is present only in 1st graders, indicating spelling-motor interference before handwriting automation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In 1st graders, the frequency effect on handwriting was present only when writing the difficult letter f, not the easy letter t, and not in Grades 3 or 5. [→ Danna 2021](#danna-2021)

## Evidence

### Danna 2021

Danna, J., Longcamp, M., Nalborczyk, L., Velay, J.-L., Commengé, C., & Jover, M. (2021). Interaction between orthographic and graphomotor constraints in learning to write. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3596-880X

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main experiment in which 65 French students in Grades 1, 3 and 5 wrote eight five-letter words from memory on a digitizing tablet. ANOVAs on number of stops showed the frequency-by-letter interaction; the post-hoc tests showed that "the Frequency effect was only present in 1st graders writing the letter f (p < .001)".

> "As can be seen in Figure 2B, the Bonferroni's post-hoc tests showed that the Frequency effect was only present in 1st graders writing the letter f (p < .001)."
