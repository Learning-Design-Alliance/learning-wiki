# Absolute monitoring accuracy showed a mixed, inconsistent group-by-time interaction rather than a stable competitiveness difference

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Absolute monitoring accuracy showed a significant group-by-time interaction, but with opposite directions at different time points (HC < LC at Kahoot 2; LC < HC at Kahoot 5), which the authors interpret as no meaningful difference. [→ Daniell DiFrancesca 2025](#daniell-difrancesca-2025)

## Evidence

### Daniell DiFrancesca 2025

Daniell DiFrancesca, Dan Spencer. (2025). The effects of competitive classroom activities on undergraduates with varied levels of competitive preference. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09741-8

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

A 2x8 mixed ANOVA on absolute accuracy (η2 = 0.043 interaction) decomposed into opposite-sign differences at two Kahoots; there were "no significant differences across time in either" group and no between-group difference.

> "Results showed a significant interaction between group and time, F(7, 350) = 2.26, p =.029, η2 = 0.043. The interaction was decomposed and showed a significant difference between the HC and LC groups at the second (HC < LC; p =.008) and fifth (LC < HC; p =.028) Kahoots."
