## Evidence

### Shewach 2019

Shewach, O. R., Sackett, P. R., & Quint, S. (2019). Stereotype Threat Effects in Settings With Features Likely Versus Unlikely in Operational Test Settings: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Applied Psychology, 104(12), 1514–1534. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/apl0000420

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis (Hunter-Schmidt procedure) of the focal subset of lab studies sharing four operational test-like features. The printed effect size is d = -.14 (k = 45, N = 3,532, SDδ = .31), described as "small in magnitude", with large true heterogeneity.

> "Critically, in this focal sample a mean threat effect of d ⫽ ⫺.14 (k ⫽ 45, N ⫽ 3,532, SD␦ ⫽ .31) was found. Thus, our estimate of stereotype threat in studies with features most similar to operational testing conditions is small in magnitude."
