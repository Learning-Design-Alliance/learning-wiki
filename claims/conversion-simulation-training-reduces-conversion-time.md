`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Non-randomised experimental simulation study by Zattoni et al. reported within the review; four strategies were implemented after the first simulation: "improving leadership, clearly defining roles, improving the knowledge base, and reorganising the operating theatre".

> "Zattoni et al. reported that 20 simulations of emergency open conversion and reorganisation of the operating theatre during robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP) reduced time to conversion by 55.2%, increased leadership and improved role delineation."
