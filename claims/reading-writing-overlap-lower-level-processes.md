## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Correlations between reading and writing measures typically never exceed .50, and studies find strong overlap in lower-level processes such as phonemic awareness and vocabulary knowledge but weaker overlap in higher levels such as discourse knowledge, strategy knowledge, and inferencing abilities.
