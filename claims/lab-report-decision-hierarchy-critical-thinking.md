## Evidence

### Laszlo Frazer 2019

Laszlo Frazer, Heather F. Higginbotham, Toby D. M. Bell, and Alison M. Funston. (2019). 'It's fundamental': Quantum dot blinking experiment to teach critical thinking. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Retrospective qualitative analysis of 45 students' reports, written with no instructions to describe decisions. The authors infer a cognitive-process hierarchy (Fig. 5) from action reporting through decision criteria, effects, hypotheses, and tests, e.g. a student noting threshold changes could shift µ by ± 0.05.
