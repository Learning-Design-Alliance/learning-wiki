### Alyahya D 2025

Alyahya D, Alharbi A, Kashoo F, Alsaheli S, AlMubarak FM, Aljuhni R. (2025). Knowledge and competence in vestibular rehabilitation: a cross-sectional study of physical therapy interns. PeerJ 13. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20213

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the 233-intern cross-sectional survey. Case-report knowledge was "0.58 out of 2 (29%)" while self-reported competence averaged 5.35 out of 8 (67%), a discrepancy between perceived and applied knowledge.

> "The mean score for case report knowledge was 0.58 out of 2 (29%), indicating a low level of understanding. The competence score, which reflects overall self-reported confidence, had a mean of 5.35 out of 8 (67%), reflecting a high level of perceived competence."
