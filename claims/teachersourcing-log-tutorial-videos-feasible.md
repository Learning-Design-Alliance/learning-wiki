## Evidence

### Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer 2017

Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer. (2017). A Crowdsourcing Approach To Collecting Tutorial Videos – Toward Personalized Learning-at-Scale. [doi:10.1145/3051457.3053973](https://doi.org/10.1145/3051457.3053973)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive throughput result of Experiment I, the crowdsourcing deployment on Amazon Mechanical Turk. Workers were paid $5 each and created videos explaining one of 18 logarithm problems; the article reports "approximately 100 videos per week of active data collection" and about 6 videos per participant.

> "Over 2 data collection periods consisting of approximately 2 weeks each, we collected 399 videos from 66 unique teachers (17% female; minimum reported age of 18, maximum reported age of 55) – approximately 6 vidoes per participant. This corresponds to approximately 100 videos per week of active data collection."
