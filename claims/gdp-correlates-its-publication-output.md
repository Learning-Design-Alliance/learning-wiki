### Benjamin D. Nye 2015

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

Correlational analysis across 186 countries with recent World Bank GDP data (2008 or later), plotted in Figure 4 with GDP on a logarithmic scale. The review reports the relationship was "highly linear (20 papers per trillion dollars GDP, after the first 66 billion dollars of GDP)", implying research funding may be a limiting factor.

> "A high correlation (r=0.88, p<0.001) was found when considering the relationship between 2012 country GDP and the number of relevant papers over the period, as shown in Figure 4."
