## Evidence

### Patterson 1909

Patterson, T. L. (1909). Pedagogical Suggestions from Memory Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Applying a seventy-five per cent correct-judgment standard to the same digit-span data, the article reports the six-digit limit for pupils below 6th grade and over thirteen, and seven for all others. The criterion is the article's own choice, with no test statistic printed.

> "If we then choose seventy-five—though it seems a less figure might be chosen—the results show that all pupils below the 6th grade and over thirteen years of age reach the limit of their memory span at six, and all others at seven."
