Question-level analysis of the human-graded sample (Table II) revealed what the authors call "a clear compartmentalization of skills": on Q25 DP and correctness grew in tandem, while on Q26 CPP growth outpaced physics correctness growth.

"Alternatively, on Q26, students showed highly significant growth in CPP (specifically procedural code trac- ing), while physics correctness only improved slightly."
