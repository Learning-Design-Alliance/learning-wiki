## Evidence

### He Qian 2026

He Qian, Chang Guanglei, Su Ning, Yang Yingying, Ma Jun. (2026). Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Learning Engagement Among Medical Undergraduates: Statistical Indirect and Configurational Associations Involving Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091694

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap re-estimation with 5000 resamples in the SEM of 498 medical undergraduates. The direct GAI use–engagement path was non-significant; equivalence was not tested. The 95% bootstrap CI included zero.

> "The direct association did not reach statistical significance (B = 0.004, β = 0.035, p = 0.416, 95% bootstrap CI [−0.006, 0.016])."

### He Qian 2026 (2)

He Qian, Chang Guanglei, Su Ning, Yang Yingying, Ma Jun. (2026). Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Learning Engagement Among Medical Undergraduates: Statistical Indirect and Configurational Associations Involving Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091694

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same 5000-resample bootstrap analysis. The indirect path through basic psychological need satisfaction was "statistically significant but small in magnitude" (β = 0.081, 95% CI [0.004, 0.017]).

> "The statistical indirect association through basic psychological need satisfaction was statistically significant but small in magnitude (B = 0.010, β = 0.081, p = 0.003, 95% bootstrap CI [0.004, 0.017])."
