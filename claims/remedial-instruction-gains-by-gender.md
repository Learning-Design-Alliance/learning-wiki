## Evidence

### Kumar and Raja 2012

S. Praveen Kumar & B. William Dharma Raja. (2012). Remedial Instruction to Enhance Mathematical Ability of Dyscalculics. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 6 No. 1 May - July 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102291

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Gender subgroup analysis in the same study: 31 male students (t = 13.21) and 15 female students (t = 9.570), each p = 0.000, with higher post-instruction means in both groups.

> "Since p value is lesser than 0.01, the null hypothesis is rejected at 1% level of significance. Hence it is concluded that there is significant difference between the scores of the treatment group before and after remedial instruction with regard to gender."
