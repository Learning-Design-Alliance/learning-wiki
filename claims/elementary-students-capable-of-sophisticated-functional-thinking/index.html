Related Claims¶

  • After an early algebra intervention, the number of kindergarteners giving a relational definition of the equal sign went from one to nine, and acceptance of non-standard equation forms rose, as reported from the authors' earlier study — related
  • After the intervention, posttest differences in identifying patterns in a function table were only marginally significant at grade 3, but experimental fifth graders more often described covariational relationships than recursive patterns — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • On items requiring algebraic generalization, experimental students tended to express rules algebraically and reason with variable quantities, while comparison students gave single numeric answers, left items blank, or treated the number line as static — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
