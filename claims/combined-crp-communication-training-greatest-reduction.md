`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Secondary individual-group analysis in the same cluster-randomised trial, comparing each intervention group with usual care. Antibiotic prescribing "was decreased the most in the combination intervention group compared with usual care" (combined risk ratio 0·38, 95% CI 0·25–0·55, p<0·0001).

> "In the individual group results for clinical characteristics and outcomes (appendix pp 5–6) antibiotic prescribing was decreased the most in the combination intervention group compared with usual care (CRP group risk ratios 0·53, 95% CI 0·36–0·74, p<0·0001; communication group 0·68, 0·50–0·89, p=0·003; combined group 0·38, 0·25–0·55, p<0·0001)"
