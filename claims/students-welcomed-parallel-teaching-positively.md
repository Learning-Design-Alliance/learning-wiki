## Evidence

### Aliakbari 2012

Aliakbari, M. & Bazyar, A. (2012). Exploring the Impact of Parallel Teaching on General Language Proficiency of EFL Learners. Journal of Pan-Pacific Association of Applied Linguistics, 16(1), 55-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979922

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student questionnaire responses after the treatment. Participants "all appreciated and received the model with open arms", citing positive competition, smaller groups allowing more use of educational time, fun, and a warm friendly atmosphere.

> "It was considered motivating for the members of the EG groups to compete positively with one another, though it did not result in better outcome."
