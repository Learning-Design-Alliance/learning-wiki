`q2 i?` On the affective aspect, text-based STAD (mean 78.34) outperformed web-based STAD (mean 69.24), a significant difference by ANOVA at 0.05. [→ Bambang Hariadi 2016](#bambang-hariadi-2016)

## Evidence

### Bambang Hariadi 2016

Bambang Hariadi, Tutut Wurijanto. (2016). Influence of Web Based Cooperative Learning Strategy and Achiever Motivation on Student Study Outcome. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3, pp. 189~199. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Affective outcomes were collected by observation during role-play activity after treatment and tested with two-path ANOVA (Table 6). The discussion states textual STAD "is proved to have better influence on learning result ... in affective aspect"; no effect size printed.

> "theoretical and empirical foundation suggest that textual form STAD type of cooperative learning strategy (average value of 78.34) excels, better than the web -based form of the same type (average value of 69.24)."
