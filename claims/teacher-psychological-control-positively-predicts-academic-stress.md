## Evidence

### Trigueros R. 2020

Trigueros R., Padilla A., Aguilar-Parra J.M., Lirola M.J., García-Luengo A.V., Rocamora-Pérez P., López-Liria R. (2020). The Influence of Teachers on Motivation and Academic Stress and Their Effect on the Learning Strategies of University Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17239089

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model (AMOS, maximum likelihood, bootstrapping of 6000 interactions) of 2456 university students at the Universities of Almeria and Malaga. The model fit was adequate (CFI = 0.97, RMSEA = 0.059), and standardised regression showed "Teacher control has been positively related to academic stress" while autonomy support was negatively related.

> "Teacher control has been positively related to academic stress (β = 0.43, p < 0.01), while autonomy support has been negatively related to academic stress (β = −0.13, p < 0.001)."
