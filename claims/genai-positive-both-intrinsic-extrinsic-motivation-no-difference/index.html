Evidence¶

Fang L 2026¶

Fang L, Li Z, Xiong B and Lu Z (2026). Effects of generative artificial intelligence on students' learning motivation: evidence from a meta-analysis. Front. Psychol. 17:1832451. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1832451

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of motivation type within the meta-analysis (intrinsic k = 36, g = 0.549; extrinsic k = 8, g = 0.382; Table 3). The article reports "QBET = 1.415" with p = 0.234, so the intrinsic-versus-extrinsic contrast was not significant.

"GenAI showed positive eﬀects on both intrinsic and extrinsic motivation. Therefore, H-1a and H-1b were supported. However, the between-group comparison between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation was not statistically signiﬁcant. Therefore, H-1c was not supported."
