## Evidence

### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Pfurtscheller Theresa, Hapfelmeier Alexander, Grünewald Marc, Häusler Janina, Seidel Tina, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Teacher questions and student responses in case-based learning: outcomes of a video study in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1895-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive video-analysis of 19 coded CBL seminars (RQ1 results). Relative frequencies of question qualities per seminar were reported as medians; the study reports that teachers "more often posed initial (67%) as compared to follow-up questions (12%)".

> "the clinical teachers more often posed initial (67%) as compared to follow-up questions (12%)"

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same descriptive analysis (Fig. 2, Openness section). Median relative frequencies showed teachers "asked much more closed (70%) than open questions (28%)", a pattern the authors note they did not expect.
