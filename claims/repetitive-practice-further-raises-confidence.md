## Evidence

### Wang Wei 2020

Wang Wei, Ma Hucheng, Ren Haozhen, Wang Zhongxia, Mao Liang, He Ningning. (2020). The Impact of Surgical Boot Camp and Subsequent Repetitive Practice on the Surgical Skills and Confidence of Residents. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00268-020-05669-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort paired comparison of the experimental group's confidence survey at post-boot camp versus the annual survey after a year of repetitive practice (at least 5 h of skills training per week). All items were "significantly higher" (p < 0.05 or < 0.01); no effect sizes are printed.

> "After one year of repetitive practice, the scores on all the items were significantly higher compared to those after the boot camp."
