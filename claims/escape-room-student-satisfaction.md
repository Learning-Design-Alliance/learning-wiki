`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive statistics (means, SDs) from the 15-item evaluation questionnaire completed by the 45 participants after the escape room sessions; the ratings are ordinal-scale means with no effect size reported for the satisfaction findings.

> "The highest evaluation ratings were given for "recommending the escape room to other students" (M = 1.20, SD = 0.46), "having fun" (M = 1.22, SD = 0.52), "relevant for future physicians" (M = 1.24, SD = 0.48), and "positive learning atmosphere" (M = 1.44, SD = 0.62)."
