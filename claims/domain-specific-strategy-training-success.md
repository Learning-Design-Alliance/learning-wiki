`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Larkin (1989), that studies claiming success in training problem-solving skills all worked with domain-specific strategies. Examples named include Schoenfeld's mathematics heuristics teaching and modeling training in a university physics course; no effect sizes are printed.

> "These programs all work with domain specific strategies, whereas programs concentrating on general problem-solving strategies have had limited success (Larkin, 1989)."
