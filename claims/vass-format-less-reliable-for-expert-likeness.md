## Evidence

### Adrian Madsen 2017

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Eleanor C. Sayre, and Cassandra A. Paul. (2017). Resource Letter RBAI-2: Research-based assessment instruments: Beyond physics topics. American Association of Physics Teachers. [doi:10.1119/1.4977416](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4977416)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review assessment of the Views About Science Survey, which uses a contrasting alternative design in which students compare agreement with two viewpoints (e.g., a special talent versus serious effort). This is the review's own evaluative comparison of the format against the CLASS and MPEX, not a measured comparison.

> "Because this format can be confusing to students if they don't agree that the answer choices given are opposites, and the expert-like response isn't always clear, the VASS is useful for discussing the ideas around students' beliefs about learning physics but is less useful for reliably measuring how expert-like your students' beliefs are."
