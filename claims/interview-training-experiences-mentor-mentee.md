## Evidence

### Walsh 2024

Walsh, H. K., Fluke, C. J., Webb, S. and Wise, L. (2024). How Do Observational Astronomers Learn to Inspect Imaging Data? Astronomy Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.32374/2024.4.1.115aer

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Three structured pilot interviews (Subjects A, B, C) conducted via Zoom with notes but no audio recording. The article reports all three identified much training as working with data to understand what to look for.

> "All three interview participants indicated that their training in visual inspection began with or included a significant component of informal mentor-mentee training."

### Walsh 2024 (2)

Walsh, H. K., Fluke, C. J., Webb, S. and Wise, L. (2024). How Do Observational Astronomers Learn to Inspect Imaging Data? Astronomy Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.32374/2024.4.1.115aer

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Interview Question 2 responses. Subjects B and C preferred work-integrated learning over being told how to perform a task; Subject C cited jargon and prior-knowledge differences as onboarding challenges.

> "All three interviewees indicated some dissatisfaction with the training they had experienced. Subjects A and B described a lack of support, reporting that they had been "Thrown in the deep end"."
