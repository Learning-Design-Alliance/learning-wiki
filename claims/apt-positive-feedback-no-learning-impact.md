### Adamson 2014

Adamson, D., Dyke, G., Jang, H., & Rosé, C. P. (2014). Towards an Agile Approach to Adapting Dynamic Collaboration Support to Student Needs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0012-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same 2×2 factorial study, the APT Feedback behavior (positive reinforcement when students engaged in APT behavior with one another) showed no significant main effect on learning. The article reports "no significant main effect of Feedback" and "a trend for it to have a negative effect."
