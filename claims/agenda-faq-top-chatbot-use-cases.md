## Evidence

### Juan J. Merelo 2023

Juan J. Merelo, Pedro A. Castillo, Antonio M. Mora, Francisco Barranco, Noorhan Abbas, Alberto Guillén and Olia Tsivitanidou. (2023). Chatbots and messaging platforms in the classroom: an analysis from the teacher's perspective. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple-choice question in the first survey (N=282) listing potential chatbot functionalities; Table 5 shows agenda facilitation (171, 60.6%), FAQs (148, 52.5%) and sharing class materials (136, 48.2%) as the top responses, with assigning grades at 40.1%.

> "The most favourable use case for chatbots in Higher Education and vocational training is their use for the facilitation of an agenda formation (171 positive response, 60.6%), followed by the FAQs use case (148 positive responses, 52.5%)"

### Juan J. Merelo 2023 (2)

Juan J. Merelo, Pedro A. Castillo, Antonio M. Mora, Francisco Barranco, Noorhan Abbas, Alberto Guillén and Olia Tsivitanidou. (2023). Chatbots and messaging platforms in the classroom: an analysis from the teacher's perspective. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square test of independence on the first survey cross-tabulating agenda and FAQs use-case preferences (Table 6): of 171 teachers favouring agenda chatbots, 103 also favoured FAQ chatbots; the association was significant at p = 0.001.

> "The relation between agenda and the FAQs use case was signiﬁcant, X 2(1,N =282) = 10.467, p = 0.001."
