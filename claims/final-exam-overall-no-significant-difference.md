## Evidence

### McKinlay JB 2025

McKinlay JB, Kearns K. (2025). Improving the learning experience in an undergraduate course on microbial metabolism by using an illustrated story. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21869

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of unscaled cumulative final exam scores between pre-story years (2015–2018) and story years (2019, 2021–2023) with the same instructor and similar course content; averages were "77 ± 3% SD vs. 75 ± 5% SD; t-test p = 0.63". Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in overall final exam scores before and after implementing the story (Figure 10a average: 77 ± 3% SD vs. 75 ± 5% SD; t-test p = 0.63)."
