## Evidence

### Wu Junxia 2024

Wu Junxia, Ke You, Chen Zhida, Alhendi Mhd Alaa, Zhu Lina, Ma Kunling. (2024). Online-to-offline combined with problem-based learning is an effective teaching modality in the standardized residency training of nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05675-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

All 60 trainees completed an anonymous online questionnaire on a five-point scale, compared by chi-square test. Both groups had 17 very satisfied, 11 relatively satisfied, and 2 generally satisfied trainees, a satisfaction rate of 93.33% in each group.

> "Teaching satisfaction survey showed no statistical difference in overall teaching satisfaction, learning difficulty, learning interest, classroom atmosphere, and knowledge points consolidation, as shown in Fig. 2 (P > 0.05)."
