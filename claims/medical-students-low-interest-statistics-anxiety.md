`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results of a self-administered questionnaire survey of 274 medical students from five Chinese institutions, reported in the Results section. The survey found "10.22% of medical students expressed a strong interest in statistics" while 61.31% felt anxious and 64.23% studied under 3 h weekly; no effect sizes were reported for these descriptive percentages.

> "10.22% of medical students expressed a strong interest in statistics. In terms of effort in learning statistics, 8.76% are very hard-working. 64.23% of medical students spend less than 3 h per week studying statistics. 61.31% of medical students feel anxious about learning statistics, 75.18% believe that learning statistics is difficult."
