## Evidence

### Brown 2025

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

RCT in community-based cancer clinics, academic medical centers, and community hospitals testing a combined oncologist, patient, and caregiver intervention. The review reports a "significant intervention effect (estimated adjusted intervention effect, 0.34; 95% CI, 0.06 − 0.62; P = .02)", with no significant effects on several individual scales or survival estimates.

> "the composite communication score showed a significant intervention effect (estimated adjusted intervention effect, 0.34; 95% CI, 0.06 − 0.62; P = .02)"

### Brown 2025 (2)

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

RCT of VitalTalk-based training (one hour didactic, two hours roleplay) for solid tumor oncologists, with blinded coding of pre/post audio-recorded encounters. The review reports eliciting patient values at 55% vs. 0% (p = 0.01) and no significant difference in any other skills.

> "In the post-INT audiotaped visits, INT oncologists were significantly more likely to elicit patient values (55% vs. 0%; p = 0.01)."

### Brown 2025 (3)

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of a 1-day lecture and simulated-patient roleplay program for 342 inpatient oncology nurses, with pre/post roleplay assessment. The review reports a significant overall skill-use increase, largest for empathic skills, and significantly improved self-efficacy.

> "Significant increase in overall skill use from pre- to post-training. The biggest gain was observed in empathic skills (other skill categories failed to demonstrate significance)"
