# Effect sizes of Arduino-based education do not differ statistically by school level, though high school estimates rest on minimal data

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANOVA found no statistically significant difference in effect size across elementary (0.574), middle (0.807), and high school (0.150) levels (p > .05). [→ Lee 2020](#lee-2020)

## Evidence

### Lee 2020

Lee, E. (2020). A meta-analysis of the effects of Arduino-based education in Korean primary and secondary schools in engineering education. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(4), 1503-1512. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.4.1503

`q3 · i?`

Meta-ANOVA across school levels: elementary 0.574 (18 studies), middle 0.807 (12), high school 0.150 (1). The article reports "no statistical difference in the effect size based on school level" and cautions that high school data came from a single study.

> "From the analysis of variance (ANOVA), the significance level was p > .05, and there was no statistical difference in the effect size based on school level. However, the high school data requires careful interpretation because the number of studies was minimal."
