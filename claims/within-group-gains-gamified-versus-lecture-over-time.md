# Knowledge and performance scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in both gamified and lecture groups; the gamified group's knowledge gain was more than 2.5 times the lecture group's, and its performance gain was three times the lecture group's

## Evidence

### Behmanesh Fereshteh 2025

Behmanesh Fereshteh, Gholamnia-Shirvani Zeinab, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Nikbakht Hossein-Ali, Nazmi Sana. (2025). Designing, implementing, and evaluating a gamification approach in childbirth training for midwifery students using computer and mobile application. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07370-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests compared scores before and four weeks after intervention in each group. The gamified knowledge difference was "3.30" (standardized 1.56), lecture knowledge 1.30 (standardized 0.7), gamified performance 3.62 (standardized 2.38), and lecture performance 0.77 (standardized 0.49, P = 0.007).

> "Additionally, the average performance score difference in the game group before and four weeks after the study was 3.62 (95% CI = 3.09–4.15), which was statistically significant (P < 0.001)."
