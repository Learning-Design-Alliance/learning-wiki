## Evidence

### Bursztyn Leonardo 2015

Bursztyn Leonardo, Jensen Robert. (2015). How Does Peer Pressure Affect Educational Investments? The Quarterly Journal of Economics 130(3). https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv021

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A field experiment in four low-income Los Angeles high schools randomly assigned eleventh graders within classrooms to sign-up forms differing only by one word (except versus including). The article reports the nonhonors "private sign-up rate is 72 percent, while the public rate is 61 percent", significant at the 1 percent level, with regressions robust to wild bootstrap and permutation inference.

> "In nonhonors classes, the private sign-up rate is 72 percent, while the public rate is 61 percent. The difference is significant at the 1 percent level."
