## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After two validation rounds with eight experts, both the ForPEC form and its Operational Manual reached a total content validity index of 0.98, and every item reached or exceeded the 0.80 adequacy threshold. [→ Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama 2022](#kaneko-regina-mayumi-utiyama-2022)

## Evidence

### Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama 2022

Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama, Monteiro Inês, Lopes Maria Helena Baena de Moraes. (2022). Form for planning and elaborating high fidelity simulation scenarios: A validation study. PLoS ONE 17(9): e0274239. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274239

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validation by eight Brazilian simulation experts using the Content Validity Index on four-point relevance, clarity, scope and general-appearance ratings. The article reports that "the total CVI of the ForPEC–version 1.2 was 0.98" and the manual's total CVI was likewise 0.98.

> "After the second round, the total CVI of the ForPEC–version 1.2 was 0.98 and that of the Operational Manual–version 1.1 was 0.98, as shown in Table 3."
