`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validation study of the newly developed SSI with stratified random sampling of medical students at King Saud University. Confirmatory factor analysis retained 23 items with loadings >=0.4; the "five-factor solution explained a cumulative variance of 52%" with total alpha 0.84 and subscale alphas 0.65-0.76.

> "Based on 372 student responses (response rate of 93%), the five-factor solution explained a cumulative variance of 52% and Cronach alpha was 0.84."
