## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, P., Yu, G., Xu, R., Cao, S., Lin, M., Liu, Y., & Wu, Y. (2026). Factors associated with medication safety competence among clinical nurses in China: A latent profile analysis. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000749

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis (Mplus 8.3) of Medication Safety Competence Scale responses from 1,228 nurses in a multicenter cross-sectional online survey. The four-profile model was retained as best fitting; the article notes "the four-profile model also achieved relatively higher entropy and greater parsimony."

> "Four profiles were derived from the latent profile analysis: Profile 1 (low medication safety competence; 9%), Profile 2 (medium low medication safety competence; 8%), Profile 3 (medium high medication safety competence; 40%), and Profile 4 (high medication safety competence; 44%)."
