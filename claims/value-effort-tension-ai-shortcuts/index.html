In the navigating-the-tension findings of this interview study, twelve of thirteen participants described simultaneous endorsement of effort and temptation toward AI shortcuts, the study's central emergent pattern.

"Nearly all students (12 of 13) described valuing learning through effort while also feeling drawn toward using AI to reduce that effort."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

The same section reports the split in how students acted on their values: seven limited AI use while five used it extensively against their stated values; one student fell outside this framework.

"The majority (n=7) described limiting their AI use in ways consistent with their stated values. Five students described using AI more extensively, in ways they themselves viewed as undermining their learning."
