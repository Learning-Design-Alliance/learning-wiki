# Both growth mindset messages and structural opportunities matter: the mindset–choice link is strongest when both are growth-aligned, weakest when both are fixed, and intermediate when they are misaligned

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a 2 (messages) × 2 (opportunities) crossed experiment, the mindset–choice association was stronger in the growth messages/growth opportunities condition than the fixed/fixed condition (probability of difference in βs = 0.98). [→ Hecht 2022](#hecht-2022)
`q3 i?` Mixed conditions fell in between: each single-growth condition exceeded the fixed/fixed condition (probability of difference > 0.95) but was weaker than the fully growth condition (probability of difference > 0.77). [→ Hecht 2022 (2)](#hecht-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Hecht 2022

Hecht, C. A., Dweck, C. S., Murphy, M. C., Kroeper, K. M., & Yeager, D. S. (2022). Efficiently exploring the causal role of contextual moderators in behavioral science. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 120(1). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2216315120

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered crossed factorial experiment (study 3) with 1,082 national adolescents from Dynata, randomly assigned to four conditions combining fixed/growth messages and fixed/growth opportunities (272, 271, 273, and 266 participants). BCF analysis gave a probability of difference in βs of "0.98" for the fully growth versus fully fixed contrast.

> "Consistent with studies 1 and 2, the association was stronger and more positive in the growth messages/growth opportunities condition than in the fixed messages/fixed opportunities condition (probability of difference in βs between conditions = 0.98)."

### Hecht 2022 (2)

Hecht, C. A., Dweck, C. S., Murphy, M. C., Kroeper, K. M., & Yeager, D. S. (2022). Efficiently exploring the causal role of contextual moderators in behavioral science. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 120(1). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2216315120

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Pairwise contrasts from the same crossed experiment showed mixed conditions intermediate between the fully fixed and fully growth conditions, with reported probabilities of difference in βs "for each pairwise comparison" as quoted.

> "The association in the fixed messages/growth opportunities and growth messages/fixed opportunities conditions was also more positive than in the fixed messages/fixed opportunities condition (probability of difference in βs between conditions > 0.95 for each pairwise comparison) but weaker and less positive than the association in the growth messages/growth opportunities condition"
