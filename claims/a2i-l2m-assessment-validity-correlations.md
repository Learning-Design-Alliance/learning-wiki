## Evidence

### Connor 2022

Connor, Carol McDonald; May, Henry; Sparapani, Nicole; Hwang, Jin Kyoung; Adams, Ashley; Wood, Taffeta S.; Siegal, Sarah; Wolfe, Cassidy; Day, Stephanie. (2022). Bringing Assessment-to-Instruction (A2i) Technology to Scale: Exploring the Process from Development to Implementation. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000758

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Psychometric analyses of the three integrated A2i assessments using Rasch scaling, exploratory factor analysis, and test information functions. Convergent validity was supported by the printed correlation values r = .65 to .76; the description quotes the article's phrase "correlations (r) ranging from .65 to .76". The article also reports R2C is not appropriate for students with less-developed reading skills.

> "The L2M correlated highly with both the LW subtest (given only to kindergarten and first grade students) and the GM (given only to second and third grade students) with correlations (r) ranging from .65 to .76."
