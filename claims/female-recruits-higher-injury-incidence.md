### Orr Robin M. 2020

Orr Robin M., Cohen Bruce S., Allison Stephen C., Bulathsinhala Lakmini, Zambraski Edward J., Jaffrey Mark. (2020). Models to predict injury, physical fitness failure and attrition in recruit training: a retrospective cohort study. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-020-00260-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified analysis of injury incidence in the retrospective cohort of 19,769 recruits. The study reports women's incidence of "43.3%" versus men's "26.0%" (P < 0.001), and "1.97 injuries per person-year for women and 1.19 injuries per person-year for men". Stratified by course and sex, 55.7% of women in the 80-day course were injured versus 32.4% of men.

> "Overall, women had a higher any-type injury incidence (43.3%) than men (26.0%) in both courses combined (P < 0.001); this held true even when exposure time was accounted for: 1.97 injuries per person-year for women and 1.19 injuries per person-year for men in both courses combined."
