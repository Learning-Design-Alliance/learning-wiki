## Evidence

### Gurbuz Emine 2018

Gurbuz Emine, Hanley Mary, Riby Deborah M. (2018). University Students with Autism: The Social and Academic Experiences of University in the UK. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3741-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis (ASD-related issues theme, sensory subtheme mentioned by 13 of 26 students) of open-ended responses; students described noisy lectures and difficulty finding quiet places to work. Descriptive qualitative finding; no effect size.

> "More than half of the autistic students reported issues with the sensory overload (hyper-sensitivity to sensory input) and noted that this affected both their academic performance and their motivation to participate in social events."
