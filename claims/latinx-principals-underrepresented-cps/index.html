New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals 2023¶

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals, Explore Their Previous Experiences. (2023). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://www.norc.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The same eight-year observational analysis of CPS principal demographics found that "Latinx principals are underrepresented relative to the CPS student population." The recommendation to support Latinx leaders is the authors' interpretation; no effect size is printed.

"Yet Latinx principals are underrepresented relative to the CPS student population, suggesting that it is important to identify and support high-quality Latinx leaders moving into the principal pipeline."
