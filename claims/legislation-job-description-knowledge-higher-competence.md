# Knowledge of nursing legislation and job descriptions is associated with higher managerial competence scores among new nurse managers

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aksu Miray 2026

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison in the survey of 185 new nurse managers; Table 3 reports total score Z = −3.675, p = 0.000, r = 0.27 for legal legislation knowledge, a medium effect.

> "managers who had sufficient knowledge in legal legislation tended to show higher competence both in total score (p = 0.000) and in sub-dimensions (general unit management p = 0.002, personnel management p = 0.000, management of care p = 0.000)"

### Aksu Miray 2026 (2)

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison in the same survey; Table 3 reports total score Z = −2.704, p = 0.007, r = 0.20 for job description knowledge, a medium effect.

> "Higher scores were observed among managers who had knowledge about job descriptions related to nursing showed higher competence in total score (p = 0.007) and in all sub-dimensions (general unit management p = 0.032, personnel management p = 0.012, management of care p = 0.003)"
