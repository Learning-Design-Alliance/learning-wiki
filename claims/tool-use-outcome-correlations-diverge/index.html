Evidence¶

Sarah Manlove 2007¶

Sarah Manlove, Ard W. Lazonder & Ton de Jong. (2007). Software scaffolds to promote regulation during scientific inquiry learning. Metacognition Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-007-9012-y

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis across both conditions (Table 3): view goals r=−0.54** and take notes r=−0.40* with model quality; helpfile viewing positive with p=0.07.

"Correlational analyses further revealed that higher model quality scores were associated with lower instances of goal viewing and note taking (see Table 3). The correlation between model quality and helpfile viewing approached significance ( p=0.07), suggesting that dyads who consulted the helpfiles more often also built more accurate models."

Sarah Manlove 2007 (2)¶

Sarah Manlove, Ard W. Lazonder & Ton de Jong. (2007). Software scaffolds to promote regulation during scientific inquiry learning. Metacognition Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-007-9012-y

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Table 3 correlations: view goals with report structure r=0.62** and view helpfiles with report structure r=−0.59**, both p<0.01 two-tailed.

"A reverse pattern was obtained for both lab report scores, with significant positive correlations for frequencies of regulative tool use, and negative correlations for instances of helpfile viewing."
