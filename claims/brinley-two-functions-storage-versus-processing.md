## Evidence

### Jaroslawska 2019

Jaroslawska, A. J., & Rhodes, S. (2019). Adult Age Diﬀerences in the Eﬀects of Processing on Storage in Working Memory: A Meta-Analysis. Psychology & Aging. https://doi.org/10.1037/pag0000358

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Bayesian hierarchical Brinley analysis (brms/Stan) of logit-transformed accuracy from 34 references, with study-level effects and error in both axes. WAIC comparison favored Model 2 over Models 1 and 3; the task coefficient differed from zero in Models 2 and 3.

> "Using the estimate from Model 2, the diﬀerence between the two intercepts is 0.125 [0.063, 0.184], indicating that there are two distinct Brinley functions diﬀering in intercept for measures of storage only"
