### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA interaction terms were non-significant across modules ("P > 0.05 in all interaction terms" for cognitive load); no effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). Authors note aptitude-treatment interactions may need greater power.

> "we found minimal evidence for interaction effects; the influence of pre-academic performance and material modality on cognitive load (P > 0.05 in all interaction terms among three knowledge modules) and learning outcomes (in two out of the three knowledge modules, the P > 0.05 of the interaction term) was statistically independent"
