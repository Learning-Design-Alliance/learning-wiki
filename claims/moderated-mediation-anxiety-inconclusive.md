Moderated mediation analyses testing whether anxiety accounted for the income inequality × social class interaction in PISA 2012 and 2015 data. The discussion adds the moderation "appeared to be explained only by mathematics anxiety (Study 1 and Study 2a), but not by test anxiety (Study 2b)".
