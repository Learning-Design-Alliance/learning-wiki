`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports this faculty-impact study of the ACGME duty-hour limits as self-reported attending perceptions compared with pre-regulation days; these are reported perceptions, not tested effects, and no effect size is printed.

> "Arora and colleagues found that, in comparison with the days before regulation, Internal Medicine attendings reported that they had fewer hours for teaching, were less satisfied with their own professional development, hours worked and level of stimulation, and felt that residents were becoming more dependent on them [20]."
