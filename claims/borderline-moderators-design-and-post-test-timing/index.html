q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Between-subject meta-regression of post-test timing. Delayed post-tests (g = 0.84) exceeded immediate post-tests (g = 0.55) at the borderline threshold (F = 3.96, p = .05); not significant in within-subject analyses.

"Post-test timing also showed a borderline moderating effect (F = 3.96, p = .05), with delayed post-tests (g = 0.84, 95% CI [0.56, 1.13]) associated with larger effects than immediate post-tests ( g = 0.55, 95% CI [0.33, 0.78])."
