Ahmad D. Suleiman 2026¶

Ahmad D. Suleiman, Daqing Hou, and Maliha Noushin Raida. (2026). LLM-Generated Design Problems for Assessing Higher-Order Thinking in Project-Based Learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11032. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11032

q2 · i? · design · r2

Correlational analysis comparing Task A DP grades with project grades across three courses found correlations "ranging from−0.05to0 .03"; the authors interpret this as DPs capturing dimensions not reflected in artifact-based assessment.

"Spearman's 𝜌 between project and DP grades was negligible across all three courses (ranging from−0.05to0 .03). This lack of association suggests that DPs may capture dimensions of HOT in transfer contexts not reflected in traditional artifact-based assessment."

Ahmad D. Suleiman 2026 (2)¶

Ahmad D. Suleiman, Daqing Hou, and Maliha Noushin Raida. (2026). LLM-Generated Design Problems for Assessing Higher-Order Thinking in Project-Based Learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11032. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11032

q2 · i? · design · r2

Learner profiling using a performance matrix (high project ≥95%, high design >50%) classified 55.1% as Masters and 28.2% as Implementers, suggesting strong deliverables do not always reflect individual transfer-oriented design mastery.

"While most students (55.1%) wereMasterswho performed well in both assessments, a substantial group (28.2%) wereImplementers: students with high project grades who scored below the passing threshold on the DP."
