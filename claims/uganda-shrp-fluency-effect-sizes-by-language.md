## Evidence

### Gove 2017

Gove, A., Brunette, T., Bulat, J., Carrol, B., Henny, C., Macon, W., Nderu, E., & Sitabkhan, Y. (2017). Assessing the impact of early learning programs in Africa. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 158, 25–41. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20224

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial results from Uganda's School Health and Reading Program, reported by language of instruction. Students were 1.5 to 6 times more likely to be reading 40 or more words per minute in program compared to control schools; with 3 years of intervention, effect sizes ranged from 0.17 to 1.12 SD.

> "For the SHRP students who had 4 years of intervention, the effect sizes in oral reading fluency (correct words per minute) were Leblango, 0.28 standard deviations (SD); Runyunkore Rukiga, 0.39 SD; Ateso, 0.71 SD; and Luganda, 0.85 SD (RTI International, 2017b)."
