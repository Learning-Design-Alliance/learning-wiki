# CA-PT test performance correlates strongly with conventional progress test performance (parallel-forms reliability r = 0.834)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The correlation between the total score on the conventional PT and the theta on the CA-PT was 0.834, indicating the adaptive test reliably estimates the same ability. [→ Van Wijk EV 2024](#van-wijk-ev-2024)

## Evidence

### Van Wijk EV 2024

Van Wijk EV, Donkers J, De Laat PCJ, Meiboom AA, Jacobs B, Ravesloot JH, Tio RA, Van Der Vleuten CPM, Langers AMJ, Bremers AJA. (2024). Computer Adaptive vs. Non-adaptive Medical Progress Testing: Feasibility, Test Performance, and Student Experiences. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1345

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cross-over study of 1432 medical students in three Dutch medical schools who took both tests in May 2022; the printed parallel-forms correlation (Pearson r = 0.834) between conventional PT total score and CA-PT theta was "0.834", dropping only to 0.832 after adjustment for order-group differences.

> "The parallel-forms reliability, i.e. the correlation between the total score of the conventional PT, and the theta of the CA-PT was 0.834. After adjustment for the differences in PT score between PTfirst and CA-PTfirst the correlation becomes slightly less: 0.832."
