`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive analysis of the NKLM's own structure: the Scholar role holds "a total of 47 learning objectives" distributed across the four named sub-roles, and the article notes these contents "only slightly correspond" to the five international components.

> "The NKLM itself structures the Scholar (containing a total of 47 learning objectives) into the “lifelong learner” (8 objectives), the “critical applier” (11), the “teacher/tutor” (13) and the “innovator” (11)."
