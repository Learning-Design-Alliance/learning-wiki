## Evidence

### Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2025

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Eason Chen, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Jionghao Lin. (2025). SlideItRight: Using AI to Find Relevant Slides and Provide Feedback for Open-Ended Questions. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_27)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online crowdsourcing experiment (N=91 after attention-check filtering) with a 2x2 design on the OLI Torus platform; paired t-tests per condition (e.g., slide feedback t = -5.00, p = 0.00005) showed "significant learning gains" in every condition.

> "Students who received human-written feedback demonstrated significant learning gains, as did those who received relevant content from the slide page, AI-generated feedback, and combined slide and AI feedback."
