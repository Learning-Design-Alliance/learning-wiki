`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discussion of the three-stage training outcomes on the Surgical Ability and Proficiency Scale by Videosurgery, a Likert scale from Insufficient to Excellent applied to simulated Nissen fundoplication endosutures within 18 minutes. The article reports no student achieved the maximum level, and states continued training is necessary to reach it.

> "Although no student was able to achieve the maximum level of proficiency by running the six points with five stitches in less than 12 min, all of them progressed in the ability to execute endosutures."
