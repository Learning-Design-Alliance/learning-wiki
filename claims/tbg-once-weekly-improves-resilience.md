## Evidence

### Prihadi 2018

Prihadi, K., Cheow, D. Z. Y., Yong, J. H. E., & Sundrasagran, M. (2018). Improving Resilience and Self-Esteem among University Students with Entrepreneurship Simulation Board Game. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 7(1), 48-56. http://iaescore.com/journals/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subject MANOVA on 12 participants randomly assigned to three groups of four. Univariate follow-up with Bonferroni correction found resilience improved significantly, "there was a statistically significant difference in RE" while self-esteem showed no significant difference.

> "there was a statistically significant difference in RE [F(2, 11) = 17.287, p < .001] but not in SE [F(2, 11) = .240, p =.79]"
