## Evidence

### Conijn 2022

Conijn, R., Kleingeld, A., Matzat, U., & Snijders, C. (2022). The fear of big brother: The potential negative side-effects of proctored exams. Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, 38(6), 1521-1534. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcal.12651

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-level regression with a random intercept for course (18% of variance at course level, p < 0.001) on survey data from 1760 students across 105 courses. The study found "no effect of online (p = 0.99) or proctored exams ( p = 0.56)"; hypothesis 1.2 was not supported.

> "This model showed that there was no effect of online (p = 0.99) or proctored exams ( p = 0.56) on the reported levels of exam difficulty."
