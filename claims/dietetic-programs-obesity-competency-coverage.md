## Evidence

### Watowicz Rosanna P. 2024

Watowicz Rosanna P., Krishnan Varsha, Butsch W. Scott. (2024). A cross-sectional survey benchmarking the coverage and importance of obesity-related competencies in U.S. dietetic supervised practice programs. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11622600/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 36 U.S. dietetic supervised practice directors (10.4% of directors), asking the extent to which 31 competencies from the 2017 Provider Competencies were covered. Directors reported "'evaluate BMI' and 'perform effectively in an interprofessional team'" most covered, at 82% and 71%; 'not at all incorporated' was rare. Details for all competencies appear in Fig. 1.

> "The competencies most frequently reported to be covered ‘to a great extent’ were ‘evaluate BMI’ and ‘perform effectively in an interprofessional team’; these were reported as being covered ‘to a great extent’ by 82% and 71% of respondents, respectively. Very few programs reported ‘not at all incorporated’"
