## Evidence

### Cristina Conati 2019

Cristina Conati, Oswald Barral, Vanessa Putnam, Lea Rieger. (2019). Toward Personalized XAI: A Case Study in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. Preprint submitted to the JAI – Special Issue on Explainable Artificial Intelligence. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Interaction-log and eye-tracking analysis of the 24 explanation users, who received on average 2.6 hints (SD 2.2). Table 6 reports 3.11 page accesses per initiation and 38.5s attention per hint (SD 39.18s); Figure 15 shows WhyHint and WhyLow together making up "about two thirds of total accesses", with HowRank access reaching zero.

> "The ratio of explanation initiations per number of hints received (second row) is 0.75 on average, i.e., participants initiated the explanation for 3/4 of the hints received, indicating that some participants were eager to view explanations and went back to the explanations for subsequent hints."
