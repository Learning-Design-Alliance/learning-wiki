Double-selection lasso regression contrasting Cluster 2 vs Cluster 1 (N = 47 countries) with covariate controls. The article reports "a statistically significant difference was observed only in science (approximately 20 PISA points, p = .002)"; no significant differences in the other two domains.

> "Compared with countries characterised by more Family-Supported Asynchronous Virtual Instruction (Cluster 1), countries in Cluster 2 demonstrated higher student performance in 2022 across all domains.
