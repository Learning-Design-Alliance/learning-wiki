### Reisslein 2004

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA of the 51-participant experiment's attitude survey found "a significant overall main effect... across the items relating to continuing motivation for the format of prompts", favoring text-based prompts with Cohen's f = .37.

> "a significant overall main effect was discovered across the items relating to continuing motivation for the format of prompts ( M = 3.83 for text -based prompts and 3.36 for pictorial -based prompts), F(1,47) = 6.36, MSE = .45, p = .02.
