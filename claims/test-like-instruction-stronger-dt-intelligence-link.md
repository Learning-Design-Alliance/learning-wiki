## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator meta-regression across the 849 coefficients comparing game-like, test-like, and strategy instruction settings. Specific contrasts showed the correlation was significantly higher for test-like (z = −4.94, p < .001) and strategy instructions (z = −3.96, p < .001) than game-like instructions; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Game-like DT instructions yielded a non-significant correlation between DT and intelligence, whereas effect sizes were small to moderate for test-like or strategy DT instructions. The moderator test was significant, QM(2) = 25.94, p < .001."
