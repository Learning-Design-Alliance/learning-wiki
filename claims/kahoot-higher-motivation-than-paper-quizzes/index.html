Evidence¶

Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet 2017¶

Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet. (2017). Enhancement of Performance and Motivation through Application of Digital Games in an English Language Class. Teaching English with Technology, 18(1), 77-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170635

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Ten-item motivation questionnaire (modified from Keller's Course Interest Survey, reliability coefficient .86) administered to both groups at week 14. The experimental group scored higher on all ten items; an independent t-test gave p = .003, significant at the .05 level.

"the average score of motivation of students in the experimental group (Mean = 3.42, SD = .44) was much higher than that of the control group (Mean = 3.02, SD = .66)"

Related Claims¶

  • In a Thai university EFL course, students revising with Kahoot achieved higher adjusted post-test grammar and vocabulary scores than students revising with paper quizzes — related

  • The authors observed higher engagement, enthusiasm, and peer study-group use in the Kahoot group than in the control group — related
