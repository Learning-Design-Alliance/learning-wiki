`q3 i?` In Year 1, students reported that their coursework felt like work the vast majority (76%) of the time; in Year 3, game-based coursework was reported as like work and play more frequently than like work. [→ Coller et al. 2011](#coller-et-al-2011)

## Evidence

### Coller et al. 2011

B. D. Coller, D. J. Shernoff, and A. D. Strati. (2011). Measuring Engagement as Students Learn Dynamic Systems and Control with a Video Game. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076061

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Aggregating responses to the item that asked if the activity felt like work, play, both, or neither while doing homework or labwork yielded an average percentage of time that each student marked a given category. In both years, students responded like work and like work and play a combined total of 91% of the time.

> "in Y ear 1, the students reported that their coursework felt “like work” the vast majority (76%) of the time. In Y ear 3, the students reported that their game-based coursework was “like work and play” more frequently than “like work” (50% of the time compared to 41%)."
