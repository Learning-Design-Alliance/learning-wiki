Evidence¶

Qin Yongjie 2016¶

Qin Yongjie, Wang Yungui, Floden Robert E. (2016). The Effect of Problem-Based Learning on Improvement of the Medical Educational Environment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Medical Principles and Practice. https://doi.org/10.1159/000449036

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Cochrane risk-of-bias assessment of the six included RCTs by two independent reviewers. Three studies were at high risk for allocation sequence generation; three used adequate randomization (random number table or stratified randomization). The authors state "The blinding of participants and personnel or outcome assessment was not described in any of the studies."

"The allocation sequence of the remaining 3 studies [27,29,30] was generated by the preference of the researchers, who assigned the students to the experimental group or control group; these studies were therefore judged to be high risk in this domain."
