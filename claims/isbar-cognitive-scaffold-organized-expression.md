### Sun Jui-Chiung 2026

Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of focus group interviews (26 participants), 57 reflective journals and teaching reflection records identified mastering the handover framework to enhance organized expression as a theme. One student described the handover as having structure so the receiver can understand more clearly.

> "Students generally reported that ISBAR provided a clear handover procedure, enabling them to organize and convey information more systematically, reducing the confusion and omissions they had previously experienced."
