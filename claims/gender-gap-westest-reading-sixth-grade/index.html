Evidence¶

Securro 2010¶

Securro, S., Jr., Jones, J. D., & Cantrell, D. R. (2010). Effects of Extensive Engagement with Computer-Based Reading and Language Arts Instructional Software on Reading Achievement for Sixth Graders. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102789

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Independent Samples t-test on Westest scaled scores across gender, with equivalent gender balance across groupings (43 and 42) and equal variances assumed (Levene's F .877, p .352). The article reports the printed effect size of .47 favoring girls, described as "a moderate effect size of .47".

"Significance occurred for Girls, (M= 661.4, Sd., 29) over Boys (M = 644.9 sd., 37), t (83) = 2.294, p .024, with a moderate effect size of .47."
