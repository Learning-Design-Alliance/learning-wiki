Descriptive analysis of California Department of Education cohort data for 2017–18 compares four-year graduation and college-going rates. The report finds "Only 69% of students experiencing homelessness graduated in 4 years", versus 83% statewide, and 50% versus 64% college enrollment. No effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Only 69% of students experiencing homelessness graduated in 4 years in 2017–18, as compared to the statewide average of 83%. And just 50% of graduates experiencing homelessness enrolled in college the year after high school, as compared to 64% of all California graduates."
