## Evidence

### Liang K 2021

Liang K, Xie Q, Nie J, Deng J. (2021). Study on the effect of education for insulin injection in diabetic patients with new simulation tools. Medicine. 2021;100:14(e25424). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025424

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results of the 120-patient study using a 20-item self-designed insulin pen questionnaire scored 1 point per factor; medians "18.0 points,16/19 vs 12.0 points,11/14, P < .001".

> "the score of the first subcutaneous injection in the test group was significantly higher than that in the control group (median, 25/75-percentile 18.0 points,16/19 vs 12.0 points,11/14, P < .001)"

### Liang K 2021 (2)

Liang K, Xie Q, Nie J, Deng J. (2021). Study on the effect of education for insulin injection in diabetic patients with new simulation tools. Medicine. 2021;100:14(e25424). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025424

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-discharge assessment in the same two-group study; the test group scored "18.0 points, 16/19 vs 13.0 points, 11/15, P < .001". Time per injection was also shorter in the test group.

> "The pre-discharge score in the test group was higher than that in the control group (median, 25/75-percentile 18.0 points, 16/19 vs 13.0 points, 11/15, P < .001)"

### Liang K 2021 (3)

Liang K, Xie Q, Nie J, Deng J. (2021). Study on the effect of education for insulin injection in diabetic patients with new simulation tools. Medicine. 2021;100:14(e25424). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025424

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-month follow-up (face-to-face, phone, or WeChat) in the same study; both groups improved over pre-discharge (P < .01) and the trial group remained higher, "18.0 points, 17/19 vs 13.0points, 12/15, P < .001".

> "One month later, the score for operation skill in the 2 groups was higher than that before discharge (P < .01), and the score in the trial group was higher than that in the control group (median, 25/75-percentile 18.0 points, 17/19 vs 13.0points, 12/15, P < .001)"
