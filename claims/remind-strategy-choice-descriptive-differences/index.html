Evidence¶

Elaheh Sanoubari 2026¶

Elaheh Sanoubari, Neil Fernandes, Keith Rebello, Alicia Pan, Andrew Houston, and Kerstin Dautenhahn. (2026). Play-Testing REMind: Evaluating an Educational Robot-Mediated Role-Play Game. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00300 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.00300

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of game-log strategy choices in the play-testing study; only 4 participants chose 'Comfort the Victim'. The article states these "descriptive evidence should be interpreted with caution due to the severe group size imbalance"; no test statistics are printed.

"On average, participants who chose to comfort the victim reported higher perceived self-efficacy for their chosen action (M = 64.5, SD = 25.2) compared to those who chose to confront the bully (M = 42.6, SD = 21.1)."
