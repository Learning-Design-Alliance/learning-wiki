## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Emotions are moderators rather than mediators: they can amplify or block empathy, as in Bella's case where differing emotional responses despite similar experience weakened the path to affective empathy. [→ Alia Hamdan 2024](#alia-hamdan-2024)

## Evidence

### Alia Hamdan 2024

Alia Hamdan, Ash Bista, Dina Newman, Scott Franklin. (2024). A Conceptual Framework for Understanding Empathy in Physics faculty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based analysis in Section IV.B using Bella's narrative of a female student in her lab; Bella, despite sharing the student's gender, experienced different emotions, which 'weakened the path towards affective empathy'. PM's case, by contrast, shows emotions are not a necessary intermediary step.

> "These emotions may not necessarily mirror those of the other person and can even block the pathway to empathy, as in a case where both faculty and student go through the same experience but have different emotional responses."
