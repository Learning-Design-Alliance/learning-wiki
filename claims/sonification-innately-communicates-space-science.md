## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Natt Day, and Sarah Barnes. (2021). Demonstrating change from a drop-in space soundscape exhibit by using graffiti walls both before and after. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-57-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-57-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation (conclusions) of the thematic and quantitative-linguistics results from the soundscape exhibit: "simply by listening to the sounds these simple aspects of the underlying space plasma physics were successfully and innately communicated" before researcher dialogue. This is an interpretation, not a separately tested result.

> "It is astounding that simply by listening to the sounds these simple aspects of the underlying space plasma physics were successfully and innately communicated to participants before they even spoke to the researchers."

### Martin O. Archer 2021 (2)

Martin O. Archer, Natt Day, and Sarah Barnes. (2021). Demonstrating change from a drop-in space soundscape exhibit by using graffiti walls both before and after. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-57-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-57-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Log-linear reliability check on a subset coded by three coders showed the expected IJ(K) significance and mostly non-significant coder interactions, except some inconsistency for the 'full' code of emptiness. The authors conclude "the main results of the paper are robust".

> "Therefore, the main results of the paper are robust and hence we have demonstrated a change in conceptions of space, well-aligned with the underpinning research, that resulted from this drop-in engagement activity."
