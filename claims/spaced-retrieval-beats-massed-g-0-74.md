# Spaced retrieval practice produces better final retention than massed retrieval practice (g = 0.74)

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Across 39 effect sizes aggregated with meta-regression and robust variance estimation, spaced retrieval practice induced better memory retention than massed retrieval practice, g = 0.74. [→ Latimier 2021](#latimier-2021)

## Evidence

### Latimier 2021

Latimier, A., Peyre, H., & Ramus, F. (2021). A Meta-Analytic Review of the Benefit of Spacing out Retrieval Practice Episodes on Retention. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis (subset 1) aggregating 39 effect sizes from studies comparing spaced with massed retrieval practice, using meta-regression with robust variance estimation. The review reports "a strong benefit of spaced retrieval practice in comparison with massed retrieval practice ( g = 0.74)".

> "Results from subset 1 indicated a strong benefit of spaced retrieval practice in comparison with massed retrieval practice ( g = 0.74)."
