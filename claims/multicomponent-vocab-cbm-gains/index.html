Evidence¶

Graham 2015¶

Graham, L., Graham, A., & West, C. (2015). From Research to Practice: The Effect of Multi-Component Vocabulary Instruction on Increasing Vocabulary and Comprehension Performance in Social Studies. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(1), 147-160. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078850

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study with teachers randomly assigned to treatment or comparison across 3 districts and 5 schools; 375 fourth graders in 15 treatment and 14 comparison sections. Difference score analysis on the five-minute vocabulary-matching CBM showed "a significant d ifference between post -test 1 and pre-test for the intervention group" (p < 0.001 in Table 4; mean differences 9.03 vs 3.86).
