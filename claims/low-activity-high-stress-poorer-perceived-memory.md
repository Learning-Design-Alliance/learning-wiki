### Pathmavarthini G 2026

Pathmavarthini G, Ughanthinii K, Shalini SB, Rubini SS, Trisha P, Devaashiny SV, Nursiati MT, Fatiah RMRZ, Dewi RR, Anwar ZK. (2026). Prediction of academic outcomes by serum cortisol, perceived academic stress, perceived memory performance, and physical activity among preclinical undergraduate medical students: a short-term observational study. `q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H test of PRMQ scores across combined physical-activity and stress-level groups (n = 106). The significant trend (p = 0.027) carries a printed effect size of η2 = 0.10, a medium effect by the article's own scale; no post-hoc pairwise comparisons were performed.

> "Poorer perceived memory performance was also significantly associated with lower physical activity levels combined with higher perceived stress (moderate to high) (p for trend = 0.027, η2 = 0.10)"
