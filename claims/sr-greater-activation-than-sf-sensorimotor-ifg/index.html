Evidence¶

Xiaodong Xu 2025¶

Xiaodong Xu, Cheng Jia, Kang Chen & Lijuan Chen. (2025). Unveiling the neural mechanisms of supernatural fiction comprehension using fNIRS. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00327-0

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Group-level fNIRS activation analysis of 61 adult readers in a 3×2 mixed design (Scenario Type within-subject; HP familiarity between-subject). The activation results (Table 1) show FDR-corrected p values of 0.010, 0.006 and 0.009 for the "higher activation in the left preCG (CH2), poCG(CH3) and IFG (CH13)" SR-vs-SF contrasts.
