## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2023

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Progression in self-efficacy, interest, identity, sense of belonging, perceived recognition and effectiveness of peer interaction of physics majors and comparison with non-majors and Ph.D. students. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression models on post-responses (Table 4) predicting self-efficacy from recognition, peer interaction, and belonging. Every model explains more than 50% of variance (R2 0.54-0.73); peer interaction is significant only in year 2 (beta 0.23, p < 0.05).

> "among year 3 and 4, the best predictor of self -efficacy is sense of belonging, with the standardized regression coefficient 𝛽 of perceived recognition slowly decreasing over time, though always remaining statistically significant."
