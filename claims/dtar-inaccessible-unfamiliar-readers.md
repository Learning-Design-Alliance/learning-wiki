## Evidence

### Zhelev Zhivko 2013

Zhelev Zhivko, Garside Ruth, Hyde Christopher. (2013). A qualitative study into the difficulties experienced by healthcare decision makers when reading a Cochrane diagnostic test accuracy review. Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1186/2046-4053-2-32

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Think-aloud interviews with a purposive sample of 21 clinicians, researchers and policy makers reading the first three Cochrane DTARs, analysed thematically. The study reports that "Participants unfamiliar with the design and methodology of DTARs found the reviews largely inaccessible".

> "Participants unfamiliar with the design and methodology of DTARs found the reviews largely inaccessible and experienced a range of difficulties stemming mainly from the mismatch between background knowledge and level of explanation provided in the text."
