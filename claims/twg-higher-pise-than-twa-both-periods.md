Same survey study comparing PISE between students who TWA and TWG across both time periods; the difference was 'statistically significant both before and during the COVID-19 pandemic', with small effect sizes that 'increased from 0.21 to 0.29 during the pandemic'.

> "The difference in PISE scores between the students who TWA and TWG was statistically significant both before and during the COVID-19 pandemic. Although the magnitude of this difference falls within the range of small effect sizes, it increased from 0.21 to 0.29 during the pandemic."
