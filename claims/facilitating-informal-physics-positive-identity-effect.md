### Prefontaine 2020

Prefontaine, B., Mullen, C., Güven, J. J., Rispler, C., Rethman, C., Bergin, S. D., Hinko, K., & Fracchiolla, C. (2020). Informal physics programs as communities of practice: How can program structures support university students' identities?. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.03022](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.03022)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Normalized frequency analysis of coded interviews from a subset of 18 facilitators (29 total interviews) drawn from 58 collected across the three programs; coding used the operationalized framework with inter-rater agreement over 80%. The authors report "high percentages of inbound codes" in all programs.

> "Overall, we observe that participation in informal physics programs has a positive effect on facilitators' identities, as shown by the high percentages of inbound codes across all three programs (see first row of table in Figure 3 for details)."
