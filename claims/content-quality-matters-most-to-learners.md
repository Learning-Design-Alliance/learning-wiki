## Evidence

### Sisson SD 2012

Sisson SD, Rastegar DA, Hughes MT, Bertram AK, Yeh HC. (2012). Learner feedback and educational outcomes with an internet-based ambulatory curriculum: a qualitative and quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-55

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Authors' interpretation of their own thematic analysis of 3000 sampled learner comments, in the Discussion; the article states this confirms what others have shown about content quality mattering most to learners. No effect size is printed.

> "The majority of learner comments on the curriculum were related to the overall quality of the modules and their educational content, suggesting that the educational quality of the content was most important to learners."
