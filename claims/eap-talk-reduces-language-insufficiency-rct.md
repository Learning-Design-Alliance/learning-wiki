## Evidence

### Zou 2026

Zou, B., Yuan, Y., Wang, C., & Du, Y. (2026). Can AI Reduce Acculturative Stress? Exploring the Role of AI-Mediated Speaking Practice in Chinese International Students' Perceived Language Insufficiency, Social Isolation, and Academic Pressure. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial with 126 Chinese international students in UK universities, analysed with linear mixed-effects models. The group × time interaction for language insufficiency was significant: "β = -0.91, SE = 0.14, t = -6.50, p < .001". Experimental means fell from 4.92 to 3.78; control from 4.85 to 4.62.

> "The strongest effect was found for language insufficiency, β = -0.91, SE = 0.14, t = -6.50, p < .001, indicating a substantial intervention-related reduction in perceived English-language difficulties."
