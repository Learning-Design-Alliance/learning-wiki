# Cognitive inhibition is not significantly associated with arithmetic fact retrieval in typically developing third graders

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither stroop-based cognitive inhibition nor teacher-rated inhibition (BRIEF) correlated significantly with arithmetic fact retrieval in 86 third graders. [→ Bellon E 2016](#bellon-e-2016)

## Evidence

### Bellon E 2016

Bellon E, Fias W and De Smedt B (2016) Are Individual Differences in Arithmetic Fact Retrieval in Children Related to Inhibition? Front. Psychol. 7:825. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00825

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in 86 typically developing third graders; computerized task correlations controlled for motor reaction time. The study "found no signiﬁcant correlations between the stroop tasks and arithmetic veriﬁcation," and the BRIEF correlated with no other measure.
