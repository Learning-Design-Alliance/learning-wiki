## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Y., Li, J., Chui, H., & King, R. B. (2025). Peer cooperation and competition are both positively linked with mastery‐approach goals: An achievement goal perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 941–961. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12784

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Doubly latent multilevel SEM (Step 2, basic model with focal variables) on PISA 2018 data from 565,732 students nested in 20,227 schools across 75 countries. The article reports "mastery‐approach goals were positively associated with" individual-level peer cooperation, β = .21, p < .001, a medium effect.

> "The results showed that mastery‐approach goals were positively associated with individual‐level (β = .21, p < .001, medium effect) and school‐level peer cooperation (β = .44, p < .001, large effect), supporting our H1 and H2."

### Chen 2025 (2)

Chen, Y., Li, J., Chui, H., & King, R. B. (2025). Peer cooperation and competition are both positively linked with mastery‐approach goals: An achievement goal perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 941–961. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12784

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Same Step 2 doubly latent ML-SEM on the PISA 2018 cross-cultural sample. School-level peer cooperation was "positively associated" with mastery-approach goals at β = .44, p < .001, a large effect, supporting H2.

> "The results showed that mastery‐approach goals were positively associated with individual‐level (β = .21, p < .001, medium effect) and school‐level peer cooperation (β = .44, p < .001, large effect), supporting our H1 and H2."
