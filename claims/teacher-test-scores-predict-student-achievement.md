# Teachers' verbal and math skills, measured by teacher test scores, are significantly related to student achievement

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Haycock 1998

Haycock, Kati. (1998). Good Teaching Matters: How Well-Qualified Teachers Can Close the Gap. Thinking K-16, v3 n2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED457260

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ferguson's analysis relating teacher performance on the Texas Examination of Current Administrators and Teachers to student scores on the Iowa Test of Basic Skills, with other differences controlled. The printed coefficient is "a change of one standard deviation in a district's teacher scores" corresponding to .17 standard deviation in student scores; no standardized effect size is printed.
