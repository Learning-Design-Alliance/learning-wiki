### Rachel Sortino 2026

Rachel Sortino, Christina Kim, Thalia Guettler, Katie McClyman, Lorna Quandt, Alicia Wooten & Rachel Pizzie. (2026). Producing concept-motivated signs supports learning of STEM content in American Sign Language. npj Science of Learning 11:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00418-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM predicting matching-task accuracy (with response time as a fixed factor and topic and participant as random effects) in the within-participant study; only post-test scores predicted accuracy, providing "further evidence that differences in the pre- and post-test scores were linked to learning".

> "We found that pre-test scores did not signiﬁcantly predict matching task accuracy ( X2(1) = 1.45, p = 0.228). However, post-test scores were signiﬁcantly, positively related to increased scores on the matching task (X2(1) = 4.99, p = 0.026)."
