`q2 i?` LSD post-hoc tests show significant achievement differences between every pair of grouping compositions, including heterogeneous versus each homogeneous group. [→ Sumadi 2017](#sumadi-2017)

## Evidence

### Sumadi 2017

Sumadi, I. N. S. Degeng, Sulthon, & Waras. (2017). Effect of Ability Grouping in Reciprocal Teaching Technique of Collaborative Learning on Individual Achievements and Social Skills. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education, 6(3), 216-220. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1157151

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

LSD post-hoc tests in the quasi-experiment report significance values below 0.05 for all six pairwise comparisons among the four grouping compositions, e.g. "the high group of homogeneous with the heterogeneous group significance value of 0.002".

> "While LSD test results show that among the high group of homogeneous with the middle group of homogeneous significance value of 0.00, the high group of homogeneous with the low group of homogeneous significance value of 0.00, the high group of homogeneous with the heterogeneous group significance value of 0.002, the middle group of homogeneous with the low group of homogeneous significance val ue of 0.00, the middle group of homogeneous with the heterogeneous group significance value of 0,016, and the low group of homogeneous with the heterogeneous group significance value of 0,016."
