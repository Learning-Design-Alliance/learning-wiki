### J.O. Orove 2015

J.O. Orove, N.E. Osegi, B.O. Eke. (2015). A Multi-Gene Genetic Programming Application for Predicting Students Failure at School. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1503.03211](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1503.03211)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical result of the GPSFARPS simulation reported in the Discussion. The evolved expression was Y = x6 + x5 + x3 + x4, which the authors say "clearly shows the strength of multi-gene GP in minimizing or simplifying complex predictor equations".

> "Also four candidate response variables, x6, x5, x4, and x3, were generated by the system and then used in the prediction process."
