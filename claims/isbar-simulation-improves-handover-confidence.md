## Evidence

### Sun Jui-Chiung 2026

Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A single-group pretest-posttest study of 57 nursing students. Paired-samples t-test showed a significant increase in Confidence Scale total score, with a large within-subject effect size (Cohen's dz = 1.41).

> "Following the ISBAR-structured handover simulation intervention, students' overall post-test scores on the Confidence Scale (mean = 19.15, SD = 3.53) significantly exceeded pre-test scores (mean = 13.64, SD = 3.69) (t = 10.63, p < 0.001; mean difference 5.51, 95% CI [4.47, 6.55], Cohen's dz = 1.41)."
