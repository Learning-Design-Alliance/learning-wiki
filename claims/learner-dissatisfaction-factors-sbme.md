## Evidence

### Yao Yuanyuan 2025

Yao Yuanyuan, Rui Min, Zhou Xiangyong, Han Zixin, Luo Ge, Liu Yi, Tao Jiachun, Guo Jin, Tang Biyun, Zheng Bin, Wang Yi, Yan Min. (2025). Perioperative simulation-based medical education in China: a nationwide cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08445-4

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Single-factor logistic regression (P-values unadjusted) on learner satisfaction (median score 4 (3, 5); 202 of 681 learners not satisfied), reported in the Results section with the printed effect size "OR: 3.63 (2.38–5.64)" for simple simulators versus standardized patients, a medium-to-large odds ratio.

> "Using simple simulators made respondents 3.63 times more likely to be not satisfied than using standardized patients [OR: 3.63 (2.38–5.64)], whereas infrequent or irregular simulation sessions increased being not satisfied by 2.2 and 3.3 times, respectively [OR: 2.19 (1.39–3.52), OR: 3.25 (2.10–5.13)]."
