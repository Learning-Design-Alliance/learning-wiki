## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 1 and accompanying text in the ALSPAC cohort results describe the proportion of children failing Key Stages 1–4 and requiring SEN by month of birth, with school entry based on age on 1 September. Risk increases per month born after September are printed as percentages with confidence intervals, "all p<0.001".

> "The increased risks for a poor score, for each month born after September were: KS1 1.6% (1.4%-1.9%), KS2 1.2% (0.9%-1.4%), KS3 0.8% (0.6%-1.1%), KS4 1.0% (0.8%-1.2%), SEN 0.6% (0.4%-0.8%); all p<0.001."
