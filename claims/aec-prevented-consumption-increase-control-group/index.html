Evidence¶

Pamela Portelli 2018¶

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon Signed Ranks within-group analysis of the intervention group's TLFB consumption. A further significant reduction occurred at 2 months (T = 23, p = 0.02, r = 0.20), but no significant difference at 4 months (T = 13, p = 0.18, r = 0.09).

"A significant reduction in alcohol consumption was observed at 1 month post-intervention ( Mdn = 2 ) period compared to the pre-intervention period ( Mdn = 7 ), T = 29, p < 0.001, r = 0.40."

Pamela Portelli 2018 (2)¶

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon Signed Ranks within-group analysis of the information-only control group, which showed no significant change at 1 or 2 months but a significant increase at 4 months. The authors interpret this as the intervention possibly preventing an increase in consumption.

"On the other hand, the control group significantly consumed more alcohol at a 4 month post-intervention (Mdn = 6) period compared to the pre-intervention period ( Mdn = 4), T = 16, p = 0.02, r = 0.20."
