Evidence¶

Means 2020¶

Means, B., & Neisler, J. (2020). Unmasking Inequality: STEM Course Experience During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/c978880f-6afe-4778-9df2-dac931a9c471 doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/102

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-tabulation of an eight-point challenge scale against satisfaction (Table 1) from the national survey; the quote contrasts the zero-challenge group (25% very satisfied, 4% very dissatisfied) with the four-or-more-challenge group (7%, 52%).

"Among students who did not report any major problems or technology issues, 25% described themselves as very satisfied and 4% as very dissatisfied with how well they were learning in their course after it went online. In contrast, among students reporting four or more challenges, only 7% described themselves as very satisfied and 52% described themselves as very dissatisfied with how well they were learning."
