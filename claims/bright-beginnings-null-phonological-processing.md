## Evidence

### phon-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013, March). Early Childhood Education intervention report: Bright Beginnings. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/57

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

The PCER Consortium (2008) randomized trial (14 classrooms, 198 children) assessed phonological processing with the Pre-CTOPPP Elision subtest. Appendix C.3 reports an effect size of -0.08, improvement index -3, p > 0.05; the WWC characterizes the finding as an indeterminate effect, rating the domain no discernible effects.

> "The authors reported that differences between the Bright Beginnings group and the comparison group were not statistically significant on this measure."
