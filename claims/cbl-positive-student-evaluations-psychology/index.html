Evidence¶

Wu Fanghui 2023¶

Wu Fanghui, Wang Tao, Yin Danxu, Xu Xiaoxiao, Jin Cancan, Mu Nan, Tan Qingrong. (2023). Application of case-based learning in psychology teaching: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04525-5

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Qualitative synthesis of questionnaire and open-ended survey data from eleven of the included RCTs; the article reports uniformly positive student attitudes toward CBL with no effect sizes for these self-report outcomes.

"All eleven studies reported that CBL was beneficial for students, and its beneficial effects included: (1) aroused learning interests [16, 24, 28, 30, 33]; (2) enhanced self-study abilities [16, 18]; (3) improved communication and cooperation [16, 28, 34]; and (4) improved problem-solving capabilities [30, 33]."
