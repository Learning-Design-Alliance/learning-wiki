Surya 2017¶

Surya, E., Putri, F.A., & Mukhtar. (2017). Improving Mathematical Problem-Solving Ability and Self-Confidence of High School Students through Contextual Learning Model. Journal on Mathematics Education, 8(1), 85-94. https://jme.ejournal.ums.ac.id/index.php/jme

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study of two grade VIII classes (60 students) at SMP Muhammadiyah 11 Pangkalan Brandan; normalized gain (N-gain) on problem-solving was compared by two-way ANOVA. The article reports "calculated F value of 23.588 and F table 4.020", so the learning factor was significant.
