`q2 i?` Recall was measured in four trials, with the fourth trial administered 10 days after the third, and the musical group outscored the non-musical group across the trials. [→ Bahrami 2019](#bahrami-2019)

## Evidence

### Bahrami 2019

Bahrami, Z. N., Izadpanah, S. & Bijani, H. (2019). The Impact of Musical Mnemonic on Vocabulary Recalling of Iranian Young Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 977-994. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201149

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Procedural description of the post-test in the same experiment: recall was tested in three successive fill-in trials plus "fourth trial 10 days after the third trial". The article treats this as evidence of longer-term memorization but prints no separate delayed-trial statistic, so the delayed effect is descriptive only.

> "Data were collected scoring post -test in which participants answered to one, two, and three trials of filling in word list in succession, and fourth trial 10 days after the third trial"
