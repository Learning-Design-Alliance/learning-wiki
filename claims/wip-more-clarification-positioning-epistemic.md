## Evidence

### Esther Tan 2021

Esther Tan, Jacob Gerolf de Weerd, Slavi Stoyanov. (2021). Supporting interdisciplinary collaborative concept mapping with individual preparation phase. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09963-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field study of 42 third-year university students randomly assigned to WIP or WOIP; audio recordings of collaborative discourse coded for epistemic processes (Cohen's Kappa κ = 0.79) and compared with chi-square: χ2 (5) = 22.85, p < 0.05, Cramer's V = 0.14. "significantly more occurrences of clarification statements, positioning statements" in WIP.
