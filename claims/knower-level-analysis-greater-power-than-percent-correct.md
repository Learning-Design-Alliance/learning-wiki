## Evidence

### Negen 2012

Negen, J., & Sarnecka, B. W. (2012). Number-Concept Acquisition and General Vocabulary Development. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8624.2012.01815.x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Computer simulation (Experiment 3) using the Lee & Sarnecka generative model to create Give-N data for 28 simulated children, repeated 10,000 times; both observed correlations (this study's and Ansari et al.'s) fell within the resulting 95% sampling-distribution interval.

> "Analyzing Give-N data by inferred knower-level was more powerful than analyzing the same data by percent correct, detecting a significant correlation about 12% more often. It also tended to produce larger sample correlations."
