Evidence¶

Fluency Formula™ WWC Intervention Report 2007¶

Fluency Formula™ WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://teacher.scholastic.com/products/fluencyformula/pdfs/FF_EffectivessReport.pdf

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial (Sivin-Kachala & Bialo, 2005) with 128 second-grade students below the 75th OFA pretest percentile in two suburban districts; WWC-computed fluency effect size was 0.26, "ns", improvement index +10 (Appendix A3.1).

"The WWC found no statistically significant difference between the groups, however, the effect was positive and substantively important according to WWC criteria (that is, an effect size of at least .25)."
