## Evidence

### Soheila Shafiee Varzaneh & Roya Baharlooie 2015

Soheila Shafiee Varzaneh & Roya Baharlooie. (2015). The Effect of Virtual vs. Traditional Classroom Instruction on Creative Thinking of Iranian High School EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n5p177

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 60 VLI and 60 TCI female learners; paired t-tests on Gilford creative thinking pretest-posttest scores. The VLI group's "mean difference in the two scores was 32.90" versus 7.79 for TCI, with the article reporting significant pre-post gains.

> "As Table 5 shows for VLI groups, T value for creativity was 22.03 and df was 59. The mean difference in the two scores was 32.90"
