`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational analysis of 342 lumbar puncture and 945 paracentesis evaluations on a medicine procedure service; McNemar's χ2 test compared checklist completion with achieving GRS ≥4, finding "significantly more likely to perform all items on the checklist."

> "Residents were significantly more likely to perform all items on the checklist than obtain a GRS score ≥4 (P < 0.001) for both procedures."
