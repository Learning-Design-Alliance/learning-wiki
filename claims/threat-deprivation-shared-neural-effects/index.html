Evidence¶

Karen E. Smith and Seth D. Pollak 2020¶

Karen E. Smith and Seth D. Pollak. (2020). Early life stress and development: potential mechanisms for adverse outcomes. Journal of Neurodevelopmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s11689-020-09337-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis reporting convergent findings across the deprivation/threat literature. The review states "both threat and deprivation have been linked to negative PFC– amygdala connectivity in late childhood and adolescence" and that both abuse and neglect affect hippocampal volume. No effect sizes are printed.

"both threat and deprivation have been linked to negative PFC– amygdala connectivity in late childhood and adolescence [ 51, 55]. Additionally, both threat experiences such as abuse and deprivation experiences such as neg- lect have been demonstrated to have specific effects on hippocampal volume [ 53, 56, 57]."
