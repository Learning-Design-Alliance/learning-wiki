### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preliminary model for student ownership of projects. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of post-project interview transcripts from 4 instructors and 15 students at five institutions; two rounds of consensus coding assigned excerpts to the model's nine phase-by-interaction groups. The authors report "a good mapping between the model and the data."

> "Each interaction category was identified in a majority of transcripts, and likewise for each project phase. This indicates a good mapping between the model and the data."
