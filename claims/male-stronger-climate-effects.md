Multigroup SEM comparison (Hypotheses 2 and 3) of 140 male and 418 female trainee PE teachers; the results section reports that "the male sex presents a greater causal relationship of task climate" and ego climate on anxiety and mean score. Female submodel coefficients were smaller and several were non-significant.
