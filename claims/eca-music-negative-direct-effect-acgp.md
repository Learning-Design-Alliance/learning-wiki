## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In SEM on 488 children, the direct effect of ECA music on academic GP was negative (b* = −0.058, p = 0.022) and the total effect was not positive (b* = −0.072, p = 0.071). [→ Chiaki Ishiguro 2023](#chiaki-ishiguro-2023)

## Evidence

### Chiaki Ishiguro 2023

Chiaki Ishiguro, Toru Ishihara and Noriteru Morita. (2023). Extracurricular music and visual arts activities are related to academic performance improvement in school-aged children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00155-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results in the full longitudinal sample (N = 488) with SES controls. The "direct effect of ECA music was negative (direct effect: b* = −0.058, p = 0.022)"; the indirect 1 effect via first-year music GP was positive (b* = 0.055, p = 0.004). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "With respect to music, although the indirect effect via MUGP in the ﬁrst year on the ACGP in the ﬁrst year was positive (indirect 1: b* = 0.055, p = 0.004), the direct effect of ECA music was negative (direct effect: b* = −0.058, p = 0.022), and the total effect was not positive ( b* = −0.072, p = 0.071)."
