### U Kraus 2007

U Kraus. (2007). First-person visualizations of the special and general theory of relativity. The European Journal of Physics (accepted for publication). https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3454 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire-based evaluation of the Einsteinmobil travelling exhibition (section 4), which has brought interactive simulations and movies into secondary schools since the beginning of 2006, with exhibits staying a typical duration of 2 weeks. The authors report "most or all students show much interest".

> "To evaluate the project, teachers of the participating schools are asked to complete questionnaires. A general ﬁnding is that most or all students show much interest, independent of age, of gender and of their general interest in physics."
