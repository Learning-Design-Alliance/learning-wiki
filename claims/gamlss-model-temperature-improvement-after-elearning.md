## Evidence

### Thielmann Anika 2024

Thielmann Anika, Schmitz Marie-Therese, Welchowski Thomas, Weltermann Birgitta. (2024). Effectiveness of the online-eLearning program KeepCoool at improving the vaccine cold chain in general practices. PLoS ONE 19(4): e0301847. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301847

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Numerical prediction-model analysis (GAMLSS, skewed power exponential type four distribution) of N = 2,110,282 single temperature measurements from participating practices' refrigerators; the average Euclidean distance of the 95% prediction intervals from (5, 5) was compared before and after eLearning completion.

> "Euclidean distances were significantly reduced after the first participant finished, as indicated by a paired t-test (p-value = 0.0046)."
