# Earlier boredom predicts positive changes in later interest from the second measurement point onward

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Individuals with higher boredom relative to others on average increased in interest at later waves, an unexpected positive cross-lagged effect of negligible printed magnitude at T1→T2. [→ Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025](#katharina-luisa-boehme-2025)

## Evidence

### Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025

Katharina Luisa Boehme, Thomas Goetz, Markus Feuchter, Franzis Preckel. (2025). Is Boredom the Opposite of Interest? A Longitudinal Reciprocal Effect Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09991-5

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Latent cross-lagged models in mathematics and German. The T1→T2 boredom-to-interest path was negligible and not significant (β = 0.04, p = 0.407 in mathematics; German β = −0.03, p = 0.614); later paths were positive (German β = 0.17 and 0.33), with the German T2→T3 path not significant (p = 0.054).

> "Interestingly, earlier boredom was associated with positive changes in later interest (β BO-M[T1] →INT-M[T2] = 0.04; βBO-M[T2] →INT-M[T3] = 0.17; βBO-M[T3] →INT-M[T4] = 0.17)"
