## Evidence

### Forsberg Lisa 2010

Forsberg Lisa, Forsberg Lars G, Lindqvist Helena, Helgason Asgeir R. (2010). Clinician acquisition and retention of Motivational Interviewing skills: a two-and-a-half-year exploratory study. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/1747-597X-5-8

MITI percentage of Open Questions index, coded for three counsellors across 11 assessment periods, shows the mean "remains below the recommended threshold for Beginning Proficiency until 20 months after initial MI training", with the skill not retained after October 2006.
