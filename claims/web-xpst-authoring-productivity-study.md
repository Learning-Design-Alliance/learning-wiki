### Stephen B. Gilbert 2015

Stephen B. Gilbert, Stephen B. Blessing, Enruo Guo. (2015). Authoring Effective Embedded Tutors: An Overview of the Extensible Problem Specific Tutor (xPST) System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:428–454. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0045-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of five authors (two instructors, three undergraduates) building a college statistics tutor using JotForm and Web xPST. The quote separates total time from "7.37 h editing the xPST Instruction File".

> "Authors averaged 28.57 h logged on to the system across the month for the total time (with a range of 18.45 to 36.85 h), and a mean of 7.37 h editing the xPST Instruction File (a range of 4.87 to 9.48 h)."
