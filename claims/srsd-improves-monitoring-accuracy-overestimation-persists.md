## Evidence

### Jonathan Fernandez 2024

Jonathan Fernandez, Jessica Guilbert. (2024). Self-regulated strategy development's effectiveness: underlying cognitive and metacognitive mechanisms. Metacognition and Learning (2024) 19:1091–1135. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09398-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Monitoring accuracy was measured by asking students to self-rate their stories' overall quality on an 8-point scale after writing, compared with hypothesis-blind teachers' ratings. The article reports SRSD students "evaluated their texts' quality more accurately, despite persistent overestimation". No effect size is printed in the supplied text.

> "The results indicated that compared with students who received regular writing instruction, students who underwent the SRSD intervention produced higher-quality texts and evaluated their texts' quality more accurately, despite persistent overestimation."
