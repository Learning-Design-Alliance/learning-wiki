### Senol Sen & Ozge Ozyalcin Oskay 2016

Senol Sen & Ozge Ozyalcin Oskay. (2016). The Effects of 5E Inquiry Learning Activities on Achievement and Attitude toward Chemistry. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on ASCI affective post-test scores in the same five-week quasi-experiment found no significant difference (t (32) = .260, p = .797); equivalence was not tested. The article reports "no significant differences" between groups on affective attitude.

> "it was found that there were no significant differences between the students’ cognitive gain scores (MExp. = 1.11, SD = 2.81; MCont. = 2.19, SD = 4.28; t (32) = -.877, p = .387, two-tailed) and their affective attitude ( MExp. = 17.56, SD = 4.19; MCont. = 17.19, SD = 4.05; t (32) = .260, p = .797, two-tailed) scores"
