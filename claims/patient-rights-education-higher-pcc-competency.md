### Ozturk Melek Aksut 2026

Ozturk Melek Aksut, Oner Beratiye. (2026). The Relationship Between Nurses' Ethical Behaviours for Protecting Patients' Rights and Patient‐Centred Care Competencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70744

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of PCC competency scores by patient rights education status in the cross-sectional survey (n = 397; 346 with, 51 without education). Only the test statistic and medians are printed; no effect size.

> "Participants who had received patient rights education demonstrated significantly higher PCC competency scores (Median = 74.0, IQR: 68–83) compared to those who had not (Median = 68.0, IQR: 63–78; U = 11,436, p = 0.001)."
