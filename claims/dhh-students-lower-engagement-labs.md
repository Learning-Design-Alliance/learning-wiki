Comparative engagement level analysis of nine mixed hearing-ability units controlling lab activity, group, and gender; DHH students tended to participate less in on-task conversations, social interactions, and instructor interactions.

> "the total raw c.e.l. score for DHH students is -11, and the maximum is 23. Thus the c.e.l. index can be calculated by dividing the total raw c.e.l. score by the maximum, which gives -0.48."
