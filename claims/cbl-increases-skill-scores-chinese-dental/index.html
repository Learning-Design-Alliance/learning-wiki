Evidence¶

Dong Haitao 2022¶

Dong Haitao, Guo Chunlan, Zhou Lian, Zhao Jizhi, Wu Xiaomin, Zhang Xinyuan, Zhang Xin. (2022). Effectiveness of case-based learning in Chinese dental education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-048497

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis pooling skill-score data from 8 included RCTs (252 CBL and 249 LBL students), random-effects model. The pooled effect size SMD=1.22 (95% CI 0.34 to 2.11) with high heterogeneity (I2=95%).

"The meta-analysis of the skill scores found that the CBL pedagogy significantly increased skill scores by a mean of 1.22 compared with that of LBL teaching model (95% CI: 0.34 to 2.11; p<0.0001)."
