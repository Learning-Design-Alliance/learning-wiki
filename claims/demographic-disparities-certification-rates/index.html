Regression analysis of district-level TEA and federal data (2023–24 and 2024–25) controlling for district enrollment, student demographics, educator labor market characteristics, and policy context (Table A3). The report describes the finding as raising equity concerns given lower achievement growth for students of uncertified teachers.

"districts serving higher shares of Black, Hispanic/Latino, and economically disadvantaged students had significantly lower teacher certification rates, and these relationships hold for all districts and only among traditional public school districts"
