`q3 i1` Across 21 reviewed studies, PBL shows a small positive average effect on the deep approach to learning (ES = .11), with an increase in eleven studies, a decrease in four, and no effect in six. [→ Dolmans Diana H. J. M. 2015](#dolmans-diana-h-j-m-2015)

## Evidence

### Dolmans Diana H. J. M. 2015

Dolmans Diana H. J. M., Loyens Sofie M. M., Marcq Hélène, Gijbels David. (2015). Deep and surface learning in problem-based learning: a review of the literature. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9645-6

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review with vote counting, sign test, and effect sizes (Lipsey and Wilson) of 21 PBL studies. The review reports "a small positive effect of PBL on the deep approach" with ES = .11; the two-sided sign test was not significant at the 5% level.

> "The average effect size of .11 points towards a small positive effect of PBL on the deep approach."
