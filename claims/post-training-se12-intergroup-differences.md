## Evidence

### Sapkaroski Daniel 2021

Sapkaroski Daniel, Mundy Matthew, Dimmock Matthew Richard. (2021). Immersive virtual reality simulated learning environment versus role‐play for empathic clinical communication training. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t-test analysis of post-training SE-12 scores after the study found "no significant difference in mean scores" at pre-training across the CVR versus CRP and TVR versus TRP comparisons. Both post-training contrasts favoured the VR groups with the printed percentages and P values quoted.

> "Analysis of post‐training data found a significant difference for the CVR versus CRP groups (12%, P < 0.05). The analogous test for the TVR versus TRP groups also demonstrated a significant difference (7%, P < 0.05)."
