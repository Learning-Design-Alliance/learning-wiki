## Evidence

### Karlsen 2026

Karlsen, N., & Aalbergsjø, S. (2026). Developing pre-service teachers' TPACK for programming simulations. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10674-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Fixed effects regression on Prototype 1 pre-post data (n = 19). The authors report "a change in TPACK of about 0.6 points" at the intercept, significant at p < .01 (0.60, SE 0.17).

> "The results indicate that the PSTs on average experienced a change in TPACK of about 0.6 points, and the average individual change in TPACK is significantly different from zero."
