Interpretive qualitative study using reflexive thematic analysis of two focus groups with 20 sixth-year medical students and one instructor interview. Participants described videos as allowing them to "approach the workshop with prior familiarity with the procedures and reducing the need for real-time procedural recall."

> "Students described asynchronous videos as a preparatory resource that supported their engagement with hands-on practice, allowing them to approach the workshop with prior familiarity with the procedures and reducing the need for real-time procedural recall."
