## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In the exploratory complete-case follow-up, the GEM parent rating showed a group effect with larger empathy increases for the training group from T1 to T3; none of the other outcomes showed significant changes over time. [→ Naumann 2023](#naumann-2023)

## Evidence

### Naumann 2023

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory complete-case follow-up ANCOVA including families completing T1, T2 and T3 (Zirkus Empathico N = 17; Controls N = 24) after 44.6% data loss. The GEM "detected a group effect" (d = 0.77, p = 0.002); other outcomes showed no significant changes over time.

> "For the GEM parent rating, we detected a group effect ( d = 0.77 [0.21, 1.31], p = 0.002) with larger increases in empathy for the Zirkus Empathico group compared to controls from T1 to T3."
