## Evidence

### Glassman T 2010

Glassman T, Dodd V, Kenzik K, Miller EM, Sheu JJ. (2010). Social norms vs. risk reduction approaches to 21st birthday celebrations. Am J Health Educ. 2010;41(1):38-45. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ871144

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 4 reports dichotomous yes/no consequence items across all 702 respondents; hangover (29.2%), vomiting (20.3%), and blackout (19.7%) were most frequent, with no statistically significant cohort differences. No effect sizes printed.

> "participants most often reported having a hangover (29.2%), followed by vomiting (20.3%) and experiencing a blackout or memory lapse (19.7%). There were no statistically significant differences among the four cohorts."

### Glassman T 2010 (2)

Glassman T, Dodd V, Kenzik K, Miller EM, Sheu JJ. (2010). Social norms vs. risk reduction approaches to 21st birthday celebrations. Am J Health Educ. 2010;41(1):38-45. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ871144

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 5 reports protective behavior use; 91.8% used safe transportation, 88.9% ate before drinking, 70.0% drank water. The article states differences among cohorts were not statistically significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "Most students (91.8%) reported using safe transportation and another 88.9% reported eating before drinking."
