# Chicago White students score as well as or better than rest-of-state White peers in reading, and about the same in math

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In reading, Chicago White students on average consistently score as well as or better than White peers statewide, with the gap favoring CPS at every grade. [→ John Q. Easton 2007](#john-q-easton-2007)

### John Q. Easton 2007 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 2006 ISAT math scores for White students; White students at the 25th percentile in CPS are a little behind at all grade levels but less behind in eighth grade, while 75th-percentile CPS White students are consistently ahead.

> "Average White students in CPS score about the same as average White students in the rest of the state at all grade levels."
