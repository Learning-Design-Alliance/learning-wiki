Evidence¶

Hmelo-Silver 2004¶

Hmelo-Silver, C.E. (2004). Problem-Based Learning: What and How Do Students Learn?. Educational Psychology Review, 16, 235–266. https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034022.16470.f3

q2 · i? · review · r?

The article's review of the empirical evidence base for PBL states that "considerable research on the first 3 goals" exists but "little on the last 2," and that research outside medical and gifted education is minimal. This is the article's own assessment of the literature.

"There is considerable research on the first 3 goals of PBL but little on the last 2. Moreover, minimal research has been conducted outside medical and gifted education."
