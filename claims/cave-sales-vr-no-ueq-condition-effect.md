# In a CAVE-based VR sales simulation, experimental condition (customer personality × environmental atmosphere) had no significant effect on user experience (UEQ-S pragmatic, hedonic, or total)

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Repeated-measures ANOVAs revealed no significant main effect of condition on pragmatic quality, hedonic quality, or UEQ-S total value, with negligible effect sizes. [→ Francesco Vona 2025](#francesco-vona-2025)

## Evidence

### Francesco Vona 2025

Francesco Vona, Michael Stern, Navid Ashrafi, Julia Schorlemmer, Jessica Stemann, and Jan-Niklas Voigt-Antons. (2025). Sales Skills Training in Virtual Reality: An evaluation utilizing CAVE and Virtual Avatars. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects RM-ANOVA with 20 university students across four conditions on the UEQ-S pragmatic quality subscale: "no significant main effect of condition" with F(3, 57) = 0.24, p = 0.865, η2G = 0.004. Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons found no significant differences between any pair of conditions (p > 0.05).
