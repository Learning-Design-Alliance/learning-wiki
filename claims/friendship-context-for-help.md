## Evidence

### van Rijsewijk 2019

van Rijsewijk, L. G. M., Snijders, T. A. B., Dijkstra, J. K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2019). The Interplay Between Adolescents' Friendships and the Exchange of Help: A Longitudinal Multiplex Social Network Study. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RSiena evaluation-function analysis of help-network dynamics in the same 41 classrooms. The article reports a "positive effect of one‐sided versus no friendship on receipt of help" (η = 1.24, SD = .09, p > .99).

> "there was a positive effect of one‐sided versus no friendship on receipt of help (parameter 5; η = 1.24, SD = .09, p > .99)"

### van Rijsewijk 2019 (2)

van Rijsewijk, L. G. M., Snijders, T. A. B., Dijkstra, J. K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2019). The Interplay Between Adolescents' Friendships and the Exchange of Help: A Longitudinal Multiplex Social Network Study. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same RSiena analysis. The article reports a "positive effect of mutual versus one‐sided friendship on receipt of help" (η = 0.92, SD = .07, p > .99), and mutual versus no friendship at η = 2.15 (p > .99).

> "In addition, there was a positive effect of mutual versus one‐sided friendship on receipt of help (parameter 6; η = 0.92, SD = .07, p > .99)."
