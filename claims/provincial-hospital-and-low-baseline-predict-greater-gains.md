## Evidence

### Horii Satoko 2021

Horii Satoko, Nguyen Chinh Thi Minh, Pham Huong Thi Thu, Amaike Naomi, Ho Hien Thi, Aiga Hirotsugu. (2021). Effectiveness of a standard clinical training program in new graduate nurses’ competencies in Vietnam: A quasi-experimental longitudinal study with a difference-in-differences design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254238

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalized linear model (adjusted R2 = 0.58, F = 65.44) on the 280-nurse DID sample. The article reports the provincial-hospital coefficient as "significantly positive association (β = 0.11, P = 0.007)" and the baseline-score coefficient as "significantly negative association"; these are printed β values, not standardized effect sizes.

> "However, type of health facility produced a significantly positive association (β = 0.11, P = 0.007), and baseline competency score had a significantly negative association with a mean increase in competency scores (β = -0.75, P < 0.001)."
