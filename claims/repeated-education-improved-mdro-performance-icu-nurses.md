## Evidence

### Jang Kyeongmin 2025

Jang Kyeongmin. (2025). Effect of Repeated Education on ICU Nurses' Knowledge and Performance in MDRO Infection Control: A Pretest–Posttest Study. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70123

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on 43 ICU nurses' self-reported performance (22-item scale, Cronbach's α = 0.92). The article reports "a medium-to-large effect size (η 2 = 0.202)" with significant Bonferroni-adjusted improvements from T0 to T1 (MD = 6.84, p = 0.002) and T0 to T2 (MD = 7.98, p = 0.002).

> "The repeated measures ANOVA demonstrated a statistically significant effect of time on performance scores, F(2, 84) = 10.65, p < 0.001, with a medium-to-large effect size (η 2 = 0.202)."

### Jang Kyeongmin 2025 (2)

Jang Kyeongmin. (2025). Effect of Repeated Education on ICU Nurses' Knowledge and Performance in MDRO Infection Control: A Pretest–Posttest Study. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70123

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparison showed no significant difference between the two post-intervention performance measurements; the article suggests "behavioural gains were maintained over 3 months". Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The difference between T1 and T2 was not statistically significant (MD = 1.14, SE = 1.57, p = 0.566), suggesting that the behavioural gains were maintained over 3 months."
