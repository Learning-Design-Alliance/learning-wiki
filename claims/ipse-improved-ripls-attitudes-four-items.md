## Evidence

### Wang Rongmei 2015

Wang Rongmei, Shi Nianke, Bai Jinbing, Zheng Yaguang, Zhao Yue. (2015). Implementation and evaluation of an interprofessional simulation-based education program for undergraduate nursing students in operating room nursing education: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 15:115. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0400-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial: 55 nursing students assigned to IPSE (n = 28) or traditional course (n = 27); RIPLS completed before and after. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests found significant post-intervention differences on Questions 3 (p = 0.046), 7 (p = 0.040), 13 (p = 0.023) and 14 (p = 0.013), "which reflects more positive responses". No effect size printed.

> "in nursing students from the IPSE group, there was a significant difference in the post-intervention questionnaire for Questions 3 (p = 0.046), 7 (p = 0.040), 13 (p = 0.023) and 14 (p = 0.013), which reflects more positive responses"
