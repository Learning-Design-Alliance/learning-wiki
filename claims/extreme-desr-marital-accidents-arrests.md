## Evidence

### Surman Craig B. H. 2013

Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4009378/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-ADHD comparisons of adverse outcomes between extreme-DESR and non-DESR groups. The study reports "higher risk for traffic accidents (z = 2.15, p<0.05) and being arrested (z = 2.31, p<0.05)"; marital findings were never-married/divorced rates of 22.5/20.7% versus 32.6/9.0% at p<0.05.

> "ADHD subjects with extreme DESR had higher risk for traffic accidents (z = 2.15, p<0.05) and being arrested (z = 2.31, p<0.05) than ADHD subjects without DESR."
