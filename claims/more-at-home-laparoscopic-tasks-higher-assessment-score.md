### Brian Riley MD 2024

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Regression analysis of end-of-curriculum assessment data from 25 participating residents, relating number of at-home tasks completed to assessment score. The article reports residents "scored higher on the end-of-curriculum assessment" with p = .009 and adjusted R2 of .28 (Figure 1). No standardized effect size is printed.
