# School-based physical activity interventions have a small positive weighted population effect on children's academic performance (rw = 0.181)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 44 included interventions, groups with increased physical activity showed a weak positive effect on academic performance compared to control groups, with a weighted mean population effect of rw = 0.181. [→ Sember Vedrana 2020](#sember-vedrana-2020)

## Evidence

### Sember Vedrana 2020

Sember Vedrana, Jurak Gregor, Kovač Marjeta, Morrison Shawnda A., Starc Gregor. (2020). Children's Physical Activity, Academic Performance, and Cognitive Functioning: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2020.00307

`q3 · i?`

Random-effects meta-analysis (Hunter-Schmidt validity generalization) of 44 school-based PA interventions including 13,681 children, all longer than 6 weeks with control groups. The article reports "weighted mean effect or population effect size is rw = 0.181" with a 95% confidence interval of 0.083 to 0.279.

> "Unweighted mean effect of population is ru = 0.351, whereas weighted mean effect or population effect size is rw = 0.181. This means that groups with increased PA experienced a positive weak effect on AP compared to control groups."
