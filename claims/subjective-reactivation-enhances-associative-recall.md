## Evidence

### Marlieke Tina Renée van Kesteren 2018

Marlieke Tina Renée van Kesteren, Lydia Krabbendam and Martijn Meeter (2018). Integrating educational knowledge: reactivation of prior knowledge during educational learning enhances memory integration. npj Science of Learning 3:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0027-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on experiment 1 associative recall with congruency and reactivation factors; the reactivation main effect "reactivation: F(2,24) = 8.16, P = .002, η2 = .41" — a large effect (η² = .41). Participants rated reactivation strength as strong, a bit, or none.

> "congruency: F(1,12) = 9.87, P = .009, η2 = .45 reactivation: F(2,24) = 8.16, P = .002, η2 = .41; congruency x reactivation: F < 1"

### Marlieke Tina Renée van Kesteren 2018 (2)

Marlieke Tina Renée van Kesteren, Lydia Krabbendam and Martijn Meeter (2018). Integrating educational knowledge: reactivation of prior knowledge during educational learning enhances memory integration. npj Science of Learning 3:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0027-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 repeated-measures ANOVA on associative recall; the reactivation main effect "Greenhouse-Geisser F(1.36,21.69) = 22.24, P < .001, η2 = .58" (large, η² = .58) replicates experiment 1's reactivation benefit for inferred BC-memory.

> "reactivation: Greenhouse-Geisser F(1.36,21.69) = 22.24, P < .001, η2 = .58; congruency x reactivation: F < 1"
