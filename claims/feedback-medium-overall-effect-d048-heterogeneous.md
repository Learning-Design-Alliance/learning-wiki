# Feedback has a medium overall effect on student learning (d = 0.48 after outlier removal) with substantial heterogeneity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The random-effects integration of all study effects yields a weighted average effect size of d = 0.55 (95% CI 0.48–0.62), reduced to d = 0.48 after excluding 3.5% extreme values. [→ Wisniewski B 2020](#wisniewski-b-2020)
`q3 i?` Effect sizes are highly heterogeneous (I2 = 86.47% before outlier removal; 83.40% after), indicating feedback is not a single consistent treatment. [→ Wisniewski B 2020 (2)](#wisniewski-b-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Wisniewski B 2020

Wisniewski B, Zierer K and Hattie J (2020) The Power of Feedback Revisited: A Meta-Analysis of Educational Feedback Research. Front. Psychol. 10:3087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis integrating 994 effect sizes from 435 studies (about 61,000 subjects) identified from 32 existing meta-analyses. The pooled d = 0.55 (CI 0.48–0.62) fell to "a reduction of the average weighted eﬀect size to 0.48" after excluding 35 extreme effect sizes.

> "The integration of all study eﬀects with the random-eﬀects model leads to a weighted average eﬀect size of d = 0.55. 17% of the eﬀects were negative. The conﬁdence interval ranges from 0.48 to 0.62."

### Wisniewski B 2020 (2)

Wisniewski B, Zierer K and Hattie J (2020) The Power of Feedback Revisited: A Meta-Analysis of Educational Feedback Research. Front. Psychol. 10:3087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Q-test of homogeneity across all 994 effect sizes shows "The probability of homogenous eﬀects is <0.001" with I2 = 86.47%, i.e., considerable heterogeneity; after outlier removal I2 remained 83.40%.

> "The probability of homogenous eﬀects is <0.001 with Q = 7,339 (df = 993) and I2 = 86.47%."
