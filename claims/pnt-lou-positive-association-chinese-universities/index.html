Evidence¶

Yang Z 2026¶

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

q2 · i3 · associational · r3

Covariance-based SEM of cross-sectional survey data from 1,004 students at three public Chinese universities. The covariate-adjusted model reports the focal path, noting it 'was β = 0.570, p < 0.001, compared with β = 0.568 in the unadjusted model', confirming the unadjusted standardised coefficient of 0.568.

"PNT → LOU was β = 0.570, p < 0.001, compared with β = 0.568 in the unadjusted model."

Yang Z 2026 (2)¶

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Primary structural results for the six-path model (χ2(269) = 400.03, CFI = 0.988, RMSEA = 0.022, SRMR = 0.055). The article reports that 'All six specified coefficients were positive and statistically significant', covering the focal PNT–LOU path and the five positioning paths.

"All six specified coefficients were positive and statistically significant and were consistent with H1–H6 within the theory-constrained model."

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Explained-variance results for the endogenous constructs in the hypothesised structural model, reported alongside Figure 2 and Table 6. The model 'explained 32.2% of the variance in LOU', with the remaining variance in ACS, SRL, and CT also reported.

"The six-path model explained 32.2% of the variance in LOU, 25.4% in ACS, 32.2% in SRL, and 23.9% in CT."

Related Claims¶

  • The five-factor measurement representation (PNT, LOU, ACS, SRL, CT) showed unusually close fit, replicated in a held-out subsample, and the constructs did not collapse into one general tendency — related
  • The PNT–LOU association remains positive after adjustment for guidance clarity, demographic covariates, institution exclusion, and ordinal estimation — related
