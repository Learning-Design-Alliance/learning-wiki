## Evidence

### Kashikar 2025

Kashikar, L., Lüke, T., & Grosche, M. (2025). Effects of Diagnostic Labels for Students With Learning Problems on Teachers' Stereotypes and Performance Expectations. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194251315187

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on sympathy ratings in the 413-teacher experiment found a teaching-profession main effect, ηp2 = .019; the pity ANOVA found GETs felt more pity than SETs, ηp2 = .033, with the profession difference significant in LG0 (p < .001) and LG2 (p = .011). Graduation-level and vocational-training ANOVAs showed SETs lower than GETs (ηp2 = .070 and .053), and the track-recommendation regression showed SETs more often advised a special school, p = .009.
