Acosta-Gonzaga, E., & Walet, N. R. (2016). The role of attitudinal factors in mathematical on-line assessments: a study of undergraduate STEM students. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02313](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02313)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
