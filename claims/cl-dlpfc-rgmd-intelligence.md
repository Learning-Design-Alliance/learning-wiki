## Evidence

### Wang Song 2017

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses showed "the rGMD of the left DLPFC was significantly related to general intelligence (r = 0.34, p < 0.001)", and left DLPFC density explained ΔR 2 = 3.2% additional variance in intelligence beyond the covariates.

> "the rGMD of the left DLPFC was significantly related to general intelligence (r = 0.34, p < 0.001). Furthermore, the left DLPFC density explained additional variance in general intelligence (ΔR 2 = 3.2%, p < 0.001) beyond that explained by gender, age and the total GMV."
