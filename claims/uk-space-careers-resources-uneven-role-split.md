## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2022

Martin O. Archer, Cara L. Waters, Shafiat Dewan, Simon Foster, and Antonio Portas. (2022). GC Insights: Space sector careers resources in the UK need a greater diversity of roles. [doi:10.5194/gc-5-119-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-5-119-2022)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Desk research identified five UK resources with at least 9 roles; jobs were classified into the Space Census scheme by two independent coders (91% agreement, Cohen's κ = 0.9) and tested with chi-squared tests. The article reports "none of the current space careers resources have a near-even split of 11% per category".

> "Our results show that, to high confidence, none of the current space careers resources have a near-even split of 11% per category (corresponding to 1–3 roles). Indeed, between 2–4 of the categories are missing in each resource."
