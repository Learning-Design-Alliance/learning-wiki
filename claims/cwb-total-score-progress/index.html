Liu et al. 2012¶

Yuan-Chen Liu, Wan-Chun Lee, Tzu-Hua Huang, Hsiao-Mei Hsieh. (2012). IMPROVING STUDENTS’ CHINESE WRITING ABILITIES IN TAIWAN WITH THE “CONDITIONED WRITING SYSTEM”. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 11(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ989211

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-sample t-test of total scores (argumentation plus organization plus language use) in the quasi-experiment. Post-test means were 67.56 (SD 11.427) for the experimental group versus 54.97 (SD 15.234) for the control group; pre-test means showed no significant difference (t = 1.476, p = .145).

"Statistical analysis of the scores in the post-test of the experimental group and control group shows that the t-value is 3.798 and the p-value is .000<.05, indicating a significant difference."
