## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hsiao 2022

Hsiao, J. H., An, J., Hui, V. K. S., Zheng, Y. & Chan, A. B. (2022). Understanding the role of eye movement consistency in face recognition and autism through integrating deep neural networks and hidden Markov models. npj Science of Learning 7:28. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00139-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same study: ANCOVA with eye movement pattern (eyes- vs nose-focused, n = 37 and n = 50) and age as covariate found no performance difference; the EN scale–performance correlation was not significant.

> "Participants adopting the two patterns did not differ in performance, F(1, 83) = 0.31, p = 0.58. There was no correlation between EN scale and performance with age controlled, r(83) = 0.12, p = 0.26"
