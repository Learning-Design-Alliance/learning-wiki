## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents' average performance scores increased significantly across three filmed suture tests (29%, 43%, 88%), Kruskal-Wallis H=97.59, GL=2, p<0.0001. [→ Campos MEC 2022](#campos-mec-2022)

## Evidence

### Campos MEC 2022

Campos MEC, Monteiro MVC, Kakehasi FM. (2022). Residency training for minimally invasive surgery. Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-6991e-20213040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective observational study of 43 residents completing face-to-face Basic and Endosuture modules; performances scored by an expert via GOALS plus a specific suture checklist at three times. Scores rose from "29% (SD 3.4) in Test 1 (initial)" to 88% (SD 2.9), with H=97.59; GL=2; p<0.0001.

> "The percentage averages and standard deviations (DP) of the residents’ performance scores were, respectively, 29% (SD 3.4) in Test 1 (initial), 43% (SD 4.3) in Test 2 (intermediate), and 88% (SD 2.9) in Test 3 (final). The performance improvement was evident during training"
