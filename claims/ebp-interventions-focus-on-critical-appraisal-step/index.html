Evidence¶

Albarqouni Loai 2018¶

Albarqouni Loai, Hoffmann Tammy, Glasziou Paul. (2018). Evidence-based practice educational intervention studies: a systematic review of what is taught and how it is measured. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1284-1

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 85 controlled studies of EBP educational interventions, with intervention content categorised into the five EBP steps. The review reports "EBP step 3 (critical appraisal of evidence) was the step taught most frequently" and that only 10 (12%) studies covered all five steps.

"EBP step 3 (critical appraisal of evidence) was the step taught most frequently in EBP educational interventions (n = 63; 74%), followed by step 2 (acquiring the evidence; n = 52; 63%) and step 1 (asking a clinical question; n = 51; 61%)"
