Post-hoc pairwise comparisons within the I-PS group in the PharmaSim experiment showed the far-transfer client C2 post-test mean (32.9) fell below clients B (49.7) and C1 (53.4); only means and p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "There was a significant difference in the I-PS group post-test of client C2 (µI−P SC2 = 32.9, σI−P SC2 = 29.5) with both clients B (µI−P SB = 49.7, σI−P SB = 18.9), p = .0190 and C1 (µI−P SC1 = 53.4,σI−P SC1 = 29.0), p = .0023)."
