Evidence¶

Duchesneau 2023¶

Duchesneau, N., & Zapata, M. (2023). Creating Safer Schools: A Case Study. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641760

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interviews with 7 school board members and 8 district administrators in TSD and 3 district administrators in RSD, plus board meeting transcripts in RSD. The report attributes retained security to leaders' "filtering of student and community voices through their own perspectives." Qualitative interview finding; no effect size.

"Findings suggest this choice was, in part, due to district leaders’ filtering of student and community voices through their own perspectives — including the belief that physical security measures are necessary for school safety."
