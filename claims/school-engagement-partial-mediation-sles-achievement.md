## Evidence

### Cruz 2025

Cruz, I. N., Moreno, K. A., Stovall, B., de la Vega, L. P., & Chavira, G. (2025). Connecting the dots: Examining stressful life events, campus climate, and school engagement on academic achievement and psychological distress in a predominantly Latine sample. Journal of Research on Adolescence, 35, e70096. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.70096

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same cross-sectional path analysis (N = 293). Both the direct path from stressful life events to GPA and the indirect path through school engagement were significant, "indicating that school engagement partially mediated the relationship between SLEs and academic achievement."

> "there was a significant negative direct effect between SLEs and academic achievement (B = −1.42, β = −.27, SE = 0.28, p < .001). Moreover, SLEs had a significant negative indirect effect on academic achievement through school engagement (B = −0.18, β = −.04, SE = 0.08, p = .026)"
