Irma Eloff and Michelle Finestone; Brian Forsyth. (2016). HIV/AIDS infected mothers’ experience of a group intervention to enhance their children’s behavior. South African Journal of Education, Volume 36, Number 2. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v36n2a1285

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative and descriptive analysis of mother quality assurance questionnaires administered at six-month follow-up interviews. Mothers described improved communication ("now I know I have to communicate with my son"), patience, support and discipline; percentages are descriptive, no effect size.

> "The majority of mothers who completed the evaluation feedback form indicated that they experienced their parenting to be easier after the group support sessions (95%). Only 5% of the mothers indic ated that the support sessions made no difference to their parenting."
