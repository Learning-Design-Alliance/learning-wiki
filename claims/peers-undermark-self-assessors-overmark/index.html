Evidence¶

Bouzidi 2009¶

Bouzidi, L., & Jaillet, A. (2009). Can Online Peer Assessment be Trusted?. Educational Technology & Society, 12(4), 257-268. https://www.ifets.info/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive mean differences from the CA-2006 item analysis (Table 3): peers under-marked relative to the teacher by a printed average difference of 0.12; the value is descriptive, with no test statistic reported for it.

"The students, with a positive average difference between their marks and those of the teacher (0.12), very slightly under-mark the papers of their peers."

Bouzidi 2009 (2)¶

Bouzidi, L., & Jaillet, A. (2009). Can Online Peer Assessment be Trusted?. Educational Technology & Society, 12(4), 257-268. https://www.ifets.info/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive mean difference from the same CA-2006 item analysis: self-assessors over-marked their own papers by a printed average difference of -0.83 relative to the teacher; no significance test is reported for this difference.

"On the other hand, the authors of the papers, with a negative average difference between their marks and those of the teacher (-0.83), over-mark their papers."
