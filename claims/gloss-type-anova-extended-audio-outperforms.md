## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi 2012

Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi. (2012). The Effect of Gloss Type and Mode on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n12p100

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way between-groups ANOVA on post-test reading comprehension across marginal gloss, audio gloss, extended audio gloss, and no-gloss groups; Table 11 prints F = 13.798 with Sig. .000 (df 3 and 56). No effect size is printed.

> "The result of ANOV A, as illustrated in Table 11, shows that there was a significant difference between the scores of the participants in four groups."

### Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi 2012 (2)

Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi. (2012). The Effect of Gloss Type and Mode on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n12p100

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tukey HSD post-hoc tests on the four groups' post-test scores; the extended audio gloss group (M = 7.87) differed significantly from marginal gloss (mean difference 1.400, p = .006), audio gloss (1.133, p = .035), and no gloss (2.600, p = .000). No effect size is printed.

> "Post-hoc comparisons using the Tukey HSD test (see Table 12) indicated that the mean score for marginal gloss group (M = 6.47, SD = 0.99) was significantly different from extended audio gloss group (M = 7.87, SD = 1.06) and control group (M = 5.27, SD = 1.43)."
