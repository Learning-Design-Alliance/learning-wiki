## Evidence

### Yang Xiuli 2026

Yang Xiuli, Jiang Hui, Wang Huan, Wang Lixia, Chen Lijian. (2026). Effect of scenario-based simulation on students’ learning outcomes and satisfaction in perioperative anaphylaxis: a pilot randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Within the same pilot RCT, delta score was computed as (post-test−pretest)/pretest×100. Both groups improved, with the SBS gain of "39.2% (30.7%–44.6%)" exceeding the VBT gain of "16.6% (8.3%–20.0%)" (p<0.001).

> "Both groups improved dramatically from the delta-score in the SBS group of 39.2% (30.7%–44.6%) and 16.6% (8.3%–20.0%) in the VBT group (p<0.001)."
