Youjie Chen 2026¶

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive regression analysis of adoption across 200 classes with cluster-robust standard errors. STEM students were "20 percentage points less likely to adopt" (51% vs. 71%, t(199)=-4.04, p<0.01), and adopters in STEM courses averaged 10.76 fewer conversation sessions (t(199)=-3.48, p<0.01).

"Compared to non-STEM students, those in STEM disciplines are 20 percentage points less likely to adopt the GenAI Tutor (51 % vs. 71%; 𝑡( 199)=− 4.04,𝑝< 0.01)."

Youjie Chen 2026 (2)¶

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Process mining on STEM and non-STEM subgroups showed STEM students more likely to initiate shallow engagement (TP = 0.18 vs. 0.05) but to end the session (TP = 0.14 vs. 0.04), while non-STEM students transitioned from shallow to exam-driven engagement (TP = 0.17 vs. 0.07).

"for STEM students, they were more likely to initiate shallow engagement (TP = 0.18 for STEM courses vs. 0.05 for non-STEM courses), and among those who did, they tend to end the session (TP = 0.14 vs. 0.04)."
