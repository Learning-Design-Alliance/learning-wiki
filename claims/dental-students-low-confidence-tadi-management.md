`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported confidence measured via Likert-scale items in Section E of the questionnaire administered to 118 fourth-year BDSc students in two cohorts (2022 and 2023) at one Australian university (The University of Queensland); the finding describes this sample, not dental students generally. The study found that "A high percentage of participants (83.9%) reported low confidence in managing trauma cases."
