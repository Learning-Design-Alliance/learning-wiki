## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The review identifies four course-level factors reported to increase MOOC effectiveness: promoting discussion and collaboration, using multiple resources and delivery systems, facilitating interactivity, and ensuring constructive communication, feedback, and support by teachers and staff. Longhini Jessica, De Colle Beatrice, Rossettini Giaocmo, Palese Alvisa. (2021). What knowledge is available on massive open online courses in nursing and academic healthcare sciences education? A rapid review. Nurse Education Today. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nedt.2021.104812

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of facilitators and barriers extracted from the 36 primary studies and categorised in Table 2. The review reports these factors "have been reported to increase effectiveness", attributing each to the underlying primary studies rather than testing them.

> "the following have been reported to increase effectiveness: (a) promoting discussion and collaboration through forums, collective tasks, and social media groups—e.g., Facebook—(e.g., Aboshady et al., 2015); (b) using multiple resources and delivery systems"
