## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparing v.2 post-test means with v.1 sample means showed significant differences at p<.01 on the whole scale and all subfactors except decision making, with a whole-scale difference of 5.5 points (CI 95%: 3.98-6.12). [→ Saiz et al. 2015](#saiz-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Saiz et al. 2015

Carlos Saiz, Silvia F. Rivas and Sonia Olivares. (2015). Collaborative learning supported by rubrics improves critical thinking. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v15i1.12905

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student's t tests contrasting v.2 post-test means (N=144) against the v.1 sample's mean values. Total scale: 31.70 vs 26.65, difference 5.505, t=9.336, p=.000. Problem solving rose from 3.53 to 5.56; the article notes deduction's comparison is confounded by an anomaly in the v.1 pre-measurement.

> "The difference between the means of the improved version and v.1 proved to be statistically significant at p<.01 on the whole scale and on all the subfactors, except decision making"
