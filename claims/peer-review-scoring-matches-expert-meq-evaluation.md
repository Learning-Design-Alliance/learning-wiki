## Evidence

### Büssing Oliver 2021

Büssing Oliver, Ehlers Jan P., Zupanic Michaela. (2021). The prognostic validity of the formative for the summative MEQ (Modified Essay Questions). GMS Journal for Medical Education 38(6), Doc99. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001495.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of peer-review scores against post-correction by a qualified author for the formative MEQ* in both cohorts. The scores were "not significantly higher" under peer review and correlated at Spearman r=.837, which the authors take as evidence of the MEQ*'s reliability.

> "The points awarded by medical students in the peer-reviewed formative MEQ* are not significantly higher than in the objectified post-evaluation by a qualified author (OB) (see table 2 (Tab. 2)). Both total scores are highly correlated with each other (Spearman: r=.837, p=.000)."
