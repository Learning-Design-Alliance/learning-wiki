## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The interview study found students cannot be assumed to readily transfer math and computer science knowledge to AI problems, but with explicit support they adeptly leverage prior knowledge to solve AI problems. [→ Leitner 2023](#leitner-2023)

## Evidence

### Leitner 2023

Leitner, M., Greenwald, E., Wang, N., Montgomery, R., & Merchant, C. (2023). Designing Game-Based Learning for High School Artificial Intelligence Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 33, 384–398. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00327-w

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation of their cognitive interview study with high school STEM students. They add that "when provided explicit support to incorporate prior knowledge and skills into an AI learning experience, students are adept at leveraging this knowledge to solve AI problems."

> "there is little evidence from this study that AI can be successfully approached as a near-transfer task in which students can be expected to readily apply knowledge from one context to another"
