### Kaushal Kanica 2026

BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-14169-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional pre-post intervention study of 207 medical officers from all 33 districts of Chhattisgarh, assessed with a self-designed pretested questionnaire before and immediately after a three-day training; analysis used Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The "mean difference of 1.03" was statistically significant. No effect size was printed.

> "The mean pre-training score was 2.86 ± 1.42, which improved significantly to 3.89 ± 1.39 post-training (p < 0.001), representing a mean difference of 1.03 (95% CI: 0.77–1.29)."
