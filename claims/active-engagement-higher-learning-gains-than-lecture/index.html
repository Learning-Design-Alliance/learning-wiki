Finkelstein 2026¶

Finkelstein, N. (2026). A principled way to think about AI in education: guidance for educators and policy makers on action based on goals, models of human learning, and use of technologies. Draft manuscript, University of Colorado, Boulder. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01467

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article reports this as a summary of a recent National Academies consensus study on principles of educational activity design, not as its own empirical analysis. It states that "environments that proactively engage students show demonstratively higher learning gains" than lecture. No effect size or sample is printed in the article.

"Decades of evidence and foundational theories of learning note that learning is an active process (not the absorption of information), and environments that proactively engage students show demonstratively higher learning gains than our traditional, one-way, lecture environments."
