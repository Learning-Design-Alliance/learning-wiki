## Evidence

### Mubeen Raffa 2026

Mubeen Raffa, Khan Asghar, Kiyani Humaira Shamim, Butt Ayesha Kamal, Bogaardt Hans, Noveen Sadaf. (2026). Development and validation of dysphagia knowledge and self-reported competence questionnaire for nurses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0358250

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validation involved five speech language pathologists using a Table of Specification and seven experts rating item relevance on a 4-point scale. The initial S-CVI of 0.91 increased to 0.95 after removing one item with a zero CVI score.

> "Expert review demonstrated strong content validity (S-CVI = 0.95). Minor revisions were made after cognitive interviews to improve clarity."
