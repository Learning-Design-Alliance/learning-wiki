## Evidence

### Burton 2023

Burton, O. R., Bodner, G. E., Williamson, P., & Arnold, M. M. (2023). How accurate and predictive are judgments of solvability? Explorations in a two-phase anagram solving paradigm. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 1–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09313-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 linear contrast ANOVAs on AS + S JOSs: block × discrimination interaction F = 51.01, p < .001, η²p = .34. "Hits decreased about .04 per block as anagram duration was halved, whereas false alarms increased about .04 per block".
