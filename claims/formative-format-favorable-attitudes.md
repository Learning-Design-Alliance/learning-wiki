`q3 i?` Anonymous course evaluation ratings on four assessment-related questions were generally more favorable with the formative format (p = 0.03), with three of four questions significant at the 0.05 level and no format-by-question interaction. [→ Bailey E. G. 2017](#bailey-e-g-2017)

## Evidence

### Bailey E. G. 2017

Bailey E. G., Jensen J., Nelson J., Wiberg H. K., Bell J. D. (2017). Weekly Formative Exams and Creative Grading Enhance Student Learning in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-02-0104

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA of normalized anonymous course evaluation scores on four questions (fair exams, fair grading, prompt feedback, useful feedback) before and after the intervention (Figure 4); Fisher LSD p-values were 0.002, 0.05, 0.009, and 0.1 respectively.

> "Two-way ANOVA demonstrated that students felt essentially the same about each of these items (no significant differences among questions or interaction) and that the attitudes were generally more favorable with the formative format of the course (p = 0.03)."
