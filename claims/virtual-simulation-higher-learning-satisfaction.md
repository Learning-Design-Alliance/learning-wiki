## Evidence

### Liu Jie 2025

Liu Jie, Hu Tingting, Xu Ke, Cheng Zihui, Bu Wenjie, Lin Aiqin. (2025). Does a virtual simulation teaching platform improve surgical training for medical undergraduate students? BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03029-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Learning satisfaction measured by a 10-question questionnaire (100 points total) covering platform usefulness, instruction quality, and overall satisfaction. Experimental group scored "83.08 ± 9.74 points" versus 76.03 ± 5.36 (P < 0.001; t = 5.108). No effect size printed.

> "The learning satisfaction score obtained by students in the experimental group (83.08 ± 9.74 points) was significantly greater than that obtained by students in the control group (76.03 ± 5.36 points) (P < 0.001)."
