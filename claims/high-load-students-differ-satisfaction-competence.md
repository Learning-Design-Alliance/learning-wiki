## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants with increased BOSS I loads (n=78) differ significantly (p<.001) in satisfaction and competence characteristics from those suffering less stress; the same holds for those with increased BOSS II psychosomatic loads (n=67, p≤.002). [→ Dilly Marc 2016](#dilly-marc-2016)

## Evidence

### Dilly Marc 2016

Dilly Marc, Tipold Andrea, Geuenich Katja. (2016). Stressed out or subjective acquisition of competence – how do veterinary students see their curative work placement? GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(1), Doc9. [doi:10.3205/zma001008](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001008)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison (T tests) within the 142-student survey between high-load and lower-load participants; the article reports the quoted p<.001 for BOSS I groups and p≤.002 for the BOSS II psychosomatic-load groups. Only p values are printed, no effect size.

> "Participants (n=78) with increased loads during the work placement (BOSS I) differ significantly (p<.001) in their characteristics of satisfaction and acquisition of competences during the work placement from those participants who suffer less stress."
