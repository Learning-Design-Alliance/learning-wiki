q2 · i? · review · r?

Systematic literature review of studies on AI ethics in education and academic misconduct. The review states there is "some evidence that over-dependence on AI can be a factor in Cognitive Offloading" and related outcomes, while guided use can help. No effect sizes are printed.

"There is also some evidence that over-dependence on AI can be a factor in Cognitive Offloading, decreased independent reasoning, and altered academic behavior, and that it can be helpful to use AI guided."
