## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Relative to a mastery goal, a performance goal produced less utilization of the low-quality information received (ηp² = 0.03), and also less than the no-goal condition (ηp² = 0.05). [→ Poortvliet 2007](#poortvliet-2007)

## Evidence

### Poortvliet 2007

Poortvliet, P. M., Janssen, O., Van Yperen, N. W., & Van de Vliert, E. (2007). Achievement Goals and Interpersonal Behavior: How Mastery and Performance Goals Shape Information Exchange. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167207305536

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In Study 2 (N = 125) participants received the experts' ranking in reverse order with poor arguments; utilization of this low-quality information was measured with Spearman correlations against the inverse experts' ranking. The quote reports the contrast statistics, including the marginal p = .06.

> "relative to the mastery goal manipulation, the performance goal manipulation produced less utilization of the low-quality information, F(1, 122) = 3.66, p = .06, ηp² = 0.03. The performance goal manipulation also resulted in lower utilization of the received information than the no-goal condition, F(1, 122) = 5.86, p = .02, ηp² = 0.05"

### Poortvliet 2007 (2)

Poortvliet, P. M., Janssen, O., Van Yperen, N. W., & Van de Vliert, E. (2007). Achievement Goals and Interpersonal Behavior: How Mastery and Performance Goals Shape Information Exchange. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167207305536

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test of perceived quality of the received information in Study 2 (N = 125); the printed 95% CI from .0041 to .0536 excluded zero, supporting mediation of the mastery-versus-performance effect on utilization.

> "Results indicated a 95% CI ranging from .0041 to .0536, and from this we conclude that the mediated effect is significantly different from zero (p < .05, two-tailed; 5,000 bootstrap resamples). This result supports our expectation."
