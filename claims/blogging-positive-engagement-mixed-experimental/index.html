Evidence¶

Carlos R. del Blanco 2018¶

Carlos R. del Blanco, Ivan García-Magariño. (2018). The use of blogs in the education field: A qualitative systematic review. https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05421

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of the engagement category: 11 of 16 studies relied exclusively on self-reported data and only four of 16 used experimental designs. The review reports these studies "concluded that students had positive feelings towards the use of blogs and a higher interest in learning activities".

"The studies that were based on self-reported data and lacked control group concluded that students had positive feelings towards the use of blogs and a higher interest in learning activities."

Carlos R. del Blanco 2018 (2)¶

Carlos R. del Blanco, Ivan García-Magariño. (2018). The use of blogs in the education field: A qualitative systematic review. https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05421

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of the four experimental engagement studies. Three (Yang & Chang, Papastergiou et al., Chhabra & Sharma) found positive perceptions, but the review states Hsu & Wang "showed that the use of blogs did not improve the students’ learning motivation".

"However, the study conducted by Hsu & Wang (2011) showed that the use of blogs did not improve the students’ learning motivation."
