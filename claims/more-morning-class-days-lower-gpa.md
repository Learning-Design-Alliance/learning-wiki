## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of grades from 33,818 students taking an equivalent course credit workload, with grade point average compared by the number of days per week with morning classes, adjusting for covariates. The article reports "Cohen's d = −0.20, 95% CI = −0.25 to −0.14" for one morning-class day and d = −0.32 for two days.

> "Relative to students with no morning classes, having morning classes on one or two days of the week was associated with a small effect size for grade point average (Cohen's d = −0.20, 95% CI = −0.25 to −0.14 and −0.32, 95% CI = −0.37 to −0.27, respectively)"
