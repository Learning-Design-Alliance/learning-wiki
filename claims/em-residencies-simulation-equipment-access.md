### Miller BD 2025

Miller BD, Khoury C, Raper J, Walter LA, Bloom A. (2025). The State of Simulation in Emergency Medicine Residency Programs in the United States. West J Emerg Med. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.42048

Survey of EM residency program directors reporting equipment and resources. Table 1 lists manikin simulators at 97.9% and partial task simulators at 94.7%; the results text reports "Most (95.8%) programs reported access to a simulation center," with 34.7% of centers SSH-accredited.
