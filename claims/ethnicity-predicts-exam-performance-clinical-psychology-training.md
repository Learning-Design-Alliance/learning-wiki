## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the pre-course variable regression models, ethnicity (being non-white) predicted exam 2 marks (B = −0.65, p = .003) and exam 4 marks (B = −5.0, p = .039). White and BME trainees did not differ in A-level scores (t(251) = 1.30, p = .20) or degree class (χ2(2) = 1.05, p = .59).

> "Demographic factors were also predictive: non-white students performed worse in the year 1 research methods exam and the year 2 statistics exam."
