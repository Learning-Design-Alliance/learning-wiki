Analysis of the Spring 2019 state summative assessment found that score aggregations at the school level were minimally affected by disengagement. The abstract reports the qualitative finding that aggregations were "only minimally affected" with no printed effect sizes.

> "However, school mean performance, proficiency rates, and relative ranking were only minimally affected by disengagement."
