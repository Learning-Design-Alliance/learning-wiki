## Evidence

### Gage 2025

Gage, N. (2025). The impact of the Mindsets mathematics program on student engagement, ability to reason, and repeated reasoning. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677579

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post CLMM on the SMP dataset (889 observations at beginning, 763 at end) with a random intercept for challenge (SD = 0.695); the classroom indicator was not available for SMP models. The log-odds effect was converted via Chinn's (2000) method, "yielding an estimated d of 1.20, representing a large effect."

> "The CLMM revealed a significant effect of time, b = 2.16, SE = 0.86, z = 2.51, p = .012, indicating that students were more likely to score in a higher Ability to Reason benchmark category at post compared to pre"
