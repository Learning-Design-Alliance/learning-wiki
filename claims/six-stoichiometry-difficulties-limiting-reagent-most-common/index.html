Evidence¶

Shadreck and Enunuwe 2017¶

Mandina Shadreck, Ochonogor Chukunoye Enunuwe. (2017). Problem Solving Instruction for Overcoming Students' Difficulties in Stoichiometric Problems. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 10, Number 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164987

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of open-ended pre-test responses from 485 Advanced level chemistry learners in 8 high schools in Gweru district, Zimbabwe, using a difficulty identification index. Table 1 reports percentages per difficulty, e.g. inconsistent stoichiometric relationships at 78% in both groups.

"An analysis of Table 1 shows that only six difficulties were encountered by students during stoichiometric problem-solving."

Shadreck and Enunuwe 2017 (2)¶

Mandina Shadreck, Ochonogor Chukunoye Enunuwe. (2017). Problem Solving Instruction for Overcoming Students' Difficulties in Stoichiometric Problems. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 10, Number 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164987

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-test solution analysis: students randomly selected one of the given masses as the limiting reagent without using the stoichiometry of the reaction, identifying the one with the smallest mass. Table 1 shows 88% in both experimental and control groups.

"Problem 27 reveals that the majority of the students (88%) had difficulties in identifying the limiting reagent as well as justifying their solutions."
