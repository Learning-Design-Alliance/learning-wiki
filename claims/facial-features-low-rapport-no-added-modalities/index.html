Rachelle Esterhazy 2025¶

Rachelle Esterhazy, Rogers Kaliisa, Daniel Sanchez, Malcolm Langford and Crina Damsa. (2025). Advancing Multimodal Collaboration Analytics: A Scoping Review. Journal of Learning Analytics 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8625

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Müller et al. (2018), a multimodal low-rapport detection approach based on audio and video data including facial expressions, hand motion, speech activities, and prosodic features. "certain facial features related to negative emotions (e.g., nose wrinkling, brow-raising) are also associated with low rapport". No effect sizes are printed.

"They find that certain facial features related to negative emotions (e.g., nose wrinkling, brow-raising) are also associated with low rapport but that additional information from the other modalities did not further improve predictive power in this regard."
