## Evidence

### Haggerty 1918

Haggerty, M. E. (1918). Tests of applicants for admission to University of Minnesota Medical School. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 9*(5), 278–286. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071405

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 93 tested applicants, of whom 69 entered; 50 full-work students (24 with fifteen credits, 26 with fourteen) supplied medical marks. The article reports the combined-measure correlations as printed: ".65 for the group of 50, .81 for the fifteen-credit cases and .69 for the 14 credit cases."

> "the correlation of these combined measures with the marks subsequently made by these students is .65 for the group of 50, .81 for the fifteen-credit cases and .69 for the 14 credit cases. Either of these tests alone gives a slightly lower but still substantial correlation. In no case is it less than' .56."
