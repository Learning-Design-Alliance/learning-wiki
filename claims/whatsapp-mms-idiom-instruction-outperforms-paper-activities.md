## Evidence

### Ahmet Basal 2016

Ahmet Basal, Selahattin Yilmaz, Asli Tanriverdi, & Lutfiye Sari. (2016). Effectiveness of Mobile Applications in Vocabulary Teaching. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(1), 47-59. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/cet

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-groups comparison in a posttest-only two-group quasi-experiment with 50 first-year ELT students in Turkey (control n=25, experimental n=29 analyzed per Table 3). The independent-samples t-test on posttest scores gave t = -5.210, df = 52, p = .001, with means of 23.00 versus 31.55, showing the "experimental group achieved significantly bett er results o n the posttest".
