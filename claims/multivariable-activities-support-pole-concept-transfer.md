### Okulu 2018

Okulu, H. Z., & Oğuz Ünver, A. (2018). The Process of Facilitating Knowledge Acquisition and Retention: An Inquiry into Magnetic Poles with Challenging Questions. International Education Studies, 11(5), 25-37. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n5p25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest and retention test analysis of 65 pre-service teachers' responses about the cube magnet, a shape not included in the pretest. The study reports that 55 participants correctly identified two poles on the posttest and 54 on the retention test, with 53 and 52 respectively locating them at two opposite side areas of the cube.

> "An examination of Table 3 shows that, after implementation, the number of poles of the cube magnet and the locations of the poles are determined in accordance with the nature of the magnetic field without focusing on the end variable."
