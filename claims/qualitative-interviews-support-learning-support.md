## Evidence

### Sebastian Hobert 2023

Sebastian Hobert, Florian Berens. (2023). Developing a digital tutor as an intermediary between students, teaching assistants, and lecturers. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10293-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative interview study with 23 students, conducted face-to-face, recorded, transcribed, coded, and translated. Students reported the tutor "supports my learning, as the digital tutor offers me good guidance" and highlighted practicing exercises and looking up specific terms.

> "Overall, the qualitative interviews with the students support the quantitative results. Students stated, for instance, that the digital tutor "supports my learning, as the digital tutor offers me good guidance"  (Studentfinal23)"

### Sebastian Hobert 2023 (2)

Sebastian Hobert, Florian Berens. (2023). Developing a digital tutor as an intermediary between students, teaching assistants, and lecturers. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10293-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 23-interview study. The article reports students found the audience response functionality useful for instant in-class feedback, illustrated by Studentfinal23's statement that live votings force participation and let the lecturer go into more detail about what students want.

> "the students also evaluated the audience response functionality as useful because it allowed them to provide the lecturer and the teaching assistants instant feedback during in-class lectures and tutorial sessions."
