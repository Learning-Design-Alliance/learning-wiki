## Evidence

### Alsamadani 2011

Hashem Ahmed Alsamadani. (2011). The Effects of the 3-2-1 Reading Strategy on EFL Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p184

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Item-level ANCOVA on the same 85-student quasi-experiment, with alpha re-adjusted to .01 to avoid type I error. The author notes students "provided more correct answers to the main idea questions in the post-test than in the pre-test", with η² = .28 (partial eta squared = .28), a large printed effect size.

> "The results indicate that there was a statistically significant gain in scores for the main idea questions, F (1, 82) = 32.02, p = .000, partial eta squared = .28."
