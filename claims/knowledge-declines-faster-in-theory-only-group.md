## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MCQ correct-answer percentages regressed at 3 and 6 months in both groups, with a significantly more pronounced regression in the control group (p = 0.000); the control group's 6-month mean of 45.4% was almost the same as its pre-training level. ### Buhendwa Cikwanine Jean Paul 2025

Buhendwa Cikwanine Jean Paul, Mukwege Denis, Mongwa Justin, shalamba Emile Mapatano, Maroyi Raha, Yoyu Jonathan Tunangoya, Martin Ludovic. (2025). An assessment of the effects of simulation training on management of postpartum hemorrhage: short- and long-term impact on knowledge retention, skills and teamwork in the Democratic Republic of Congo. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section comparing mean MCQ percentages at 3 and 6 months post training in the two randomized groups; the control group declined more, "with a p-value of 0.000", reverting to 45.4% at 6 months. Table 2 gives 67.9% vs 53.3% at 3 months and 61.3% vs 45.4% at 6 months.

> "The percentage of correct answers at 3- and 6-months post training had declined in both groups, but more so in the purely theory-based training group with a p-value of 0.000. In control group, the mean percentage of correct answers at 6 months had reverted to almost the same rate as prior to training, namely 45.4% (Table 2)."
