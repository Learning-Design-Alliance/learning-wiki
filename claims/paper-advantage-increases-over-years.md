## Evidence

### Delgado 2018

Delgado, P., Vargas, C., Ackerman, R., & Salmerón, L. (2018). Don't throw away your printed books: A meta-analysis on the effects of reading media on reading comprehension. Educational Research Review, 25, 23-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.09.003

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Simple meta-regression of publication year on between-participants effect sizes (k = 56). The printed unstandardized regression coefficient was b = -0.01 (QR = 4.95, p = .03), meaning the effect size favouring paper increased by 0.01 points a year, explaining 64% of between-comparison variance (R2 = .64). No standardized effect size is printed for this trend.
