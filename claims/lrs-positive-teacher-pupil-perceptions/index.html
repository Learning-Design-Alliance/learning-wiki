Wiggins et al. 2017¶

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Process evaluation using classroom observations, teacher interviews, pupil focus groups, and teacher surveys in case study schools over two years. The report states "Classroom teachers and children were generally positive about the LRS" and that it "helped to engage pupils and allowed different pupils to work at their own pace".

"Classroom teachers and children were generally positive about the LRS. Teachers welcomed the ability to quickly assess pupil responses for certain types of tasks and give instant feedback."
