`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports this second-hand from Swanson et al. (1996), a study whose retention interval covered parts of the clinical years; the review attributes better abnormal-process retention to rehearsal during clinical training. No effect size printed.

> "Swanson et al. (1996) found that knowledge organized around specific organ systems (e.g., gastrointestinal, cardiovascular) is better retained than general knowledge not assignable to a specific organ system, and that per- formance on basic science items which concerned abnormal processes (i.e., knowledge related to principles of treatment and mechanisms of disease) actually improved, whereas performance on normal-process items declined substantially."
