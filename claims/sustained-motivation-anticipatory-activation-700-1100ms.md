# Exploratory analysis reveals sustained greater anticipatory activation for high versus low motivation conditions from 700–1100 ms post-cue over parieto-occipital scalp

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The high motivation condition showed greater amplitude than the low motivation condition in all four 100-ms time periods between 700 and 1100 ms post-cue. [→ Banerjee Snigdha 2015](#banerjee-snigdha-2015)

## Evidence

### Banerjee Snigdha 2015

Banerjee Snigdha, Frey Hans-Peter, Molholm Sophie, Foxe John J. (2015). Interests shape how adolescents pay attention: The interaction of motivation and top-down attentional processes in biasing sensory activations to anticipated events. The European Journal of Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.12810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory statistical cluster-plot analysis (point-wise paired t-tests, alpha criterion 0.05 sustained ≥20 consecutive points) followed by repeated-measures ANOVAs over right and left parieto-occipital ROIs in 24 adolescents.

> "The high motivation condition was of greater amplitude for all 4 time-periods (700-800 ms, F(1,23) = 9.54, p = .005; 800-900 ms, F(1,23) = 9.48, p = .005; 900-1000 ms, F(1,23) = 8.67, p = .007; 1000-1100 ms, F(1,23) = 9.28, p = .006)."
