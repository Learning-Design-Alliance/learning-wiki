`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 24 EBHC programme coordinators purposively sampled worldwide. Interviewees described implementation as slow, with one noting "There's no quick fix" as building blocks were placed "over several years" (P1).

> "Successful implementation of clinically integrated teaching and learning of EBHC takes much time. Student learning needs to start in pre-clinical years with consolidation, application and assessment following in clinical years."
