# The multiple-choice placement test component and high school GPA similarly predicted passing credit-bearing ELA courses, while the writing component did not predict any outcomes

## Subclaims

### Shannon 2026

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, Regional Educational Laboratory Pacific. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/examining-english-language-arts-course-placement-system-college-marshall-islands

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analyses on subsamples enrolled in English 105 (n = 381) and English 111 (n = 383), accounting for cohort and high school type. The report states the multiple-choice component and GPA "were both statistically significant and positive predictors of passing credit- bearing ELA courses," while the writing component was not significant.

> "Students' performance on the multiple- choice component of the ELA course placement test and their cumulative high school GPA were both statistically significant and positive predictors of passing credit- bearing ELA courses, after other factors were accounted for."
