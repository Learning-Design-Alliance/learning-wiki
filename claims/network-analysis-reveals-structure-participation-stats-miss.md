## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Adrienne Traxler 2018

Adrienne Traxler, Andrew Gavrin, and Rebecca Lindell. (2018). Talking by the numbers: Networks identify productive forum discussions. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the semester comparison from Table I and Fig. 4 network visualizations. The observed density ranking (1, 3, 2) reverses the natural ranking expected from network size, indicating structural differences beyond participation volume.

> "In particular, Semesters 1 and 3 show a higher level of connectivity that is not easily explained by ﬂuctuations in class size or numbers of discussion threads and comments."

### Adrienne Traxler 2018 (2)

Adrienne Traxler, Andrew Gavrin, and Rebecca Lindell. (2018). Talking by the numbers: Networks identify productive forum discussions. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise Wilcoxon tests comparing thread length and posts per student across the three semesters, reported in Results. Only Semester 3's replies-per-thread differed significantly; posts per student showed no significant differences between semesters.

> "Only Semester 3 had a signiﬁcantly diﬀerent (p < 10−5) average number of replies per thread."
