Lovaas Model of Applied Behavior Analysis 2010¶

Lovaas Model of Applied Behavior Analysis. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/295

q3 · i? · review · r3

WWC review of Sallows and Graupner (2005), which examined three outcomes in this domain. The average improvement index is +7 percentile points, with a range of +2 to +14 percentile points across findings; the domain mean fell below the substantively-important threshold.

"Although none of the impacts in this domain were statistically significant, one finding was large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria. The mean effect size across all three outcomes was not large enough to be considered substantively important"
