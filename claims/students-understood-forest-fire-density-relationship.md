## Evidence

### Alessio Focardi 2026

Alessio Focardi, Franco Bagnoli, Andrea Guazzini, Giorgio Gronchi. (2026). Introducing the Physics of Complex Systems through Videogames. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of students' anonymous written and Google-form answers after the forest fire games; the authors report that "almost all of them understood the relationship between the percentage of burned trees and the density". No effect size is printed.

> "we can say that as far as the games related toforest firewere concerned, almost all of them understood the relationship between the percentage of burned trees and the density (and also size) of the trees."

### Alessio Focardi 2026 (2)

Alessio Focardi, Franco Bagnoli, Andrea Guazzini, Giorgio Gronchi. (2026). Introducing the Physics of Complex Systems through Videogames. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same qualitative analysis: some students first said burned percentage increased with density, then denied any relationship; the authors attribute this to students knowing mainly direct or inverse proportional relationships.

> "A small portion of the responses revealed difficulties in understanding what a relationship between quantities is."
