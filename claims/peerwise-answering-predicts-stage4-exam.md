## Evidence

### Guilding C 2021

Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. Pharmacol Res Perspect. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.833

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on the 2015 cohort (n = 444) with Stage 4 score predicted and Stage 3 entered first; Model 2 predicted 55.3% of variance with a significant R2 change, and "the only significant variable was mean answer". No effect size printed.

> "the only significant variable was mean answer (t = 3.751, p < .001), with mean reputation not a significant predictor (t = 0.407, p = .684)"
