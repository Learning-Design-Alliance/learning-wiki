## Evidence

### Tiffany D. Do 2025

Tiffany D. Do, Usama Bin Shafqat, Elsie Ling, and Nikhil Sarda. (2025). PAIGE: Examining Learning Outcomes and Experiences with Personalized AI-Generated Educational Podcasts. PREPRINT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3713460](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713460)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the study's UEQ results, the article reports that "personalization did not impact Attractiveness ratings", suggesting enjoyment was driven by lesson format rather than content. No effect size or test statistic is printed for this contrast.

> "Interestingly, we found that personalization did not impact Attractiveness ratings, contrary to our hypothesis."

### Tiffany D. Do 2025 (2)

Tiffany D. Do, Usama Bin Shafqat, Elsie Ling, and Nikhil Sarda. (2025). PAIGE: Examining Learning Outcomes and Experiences with Personalized AI-Generated Educational Podcasts. PREPRINT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3713460](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713460)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For the Stimulation subscale of the UEQ, the article reports no significant main effects or interaction effects of content style or subject. The authors suggest initial discomfort with the novel podcast style may have influenced this null result, and note Stimulation ratings showed trends similar to Attractiveness.

> "We did not find any significant main effects or interaction effects of content style or subject."
