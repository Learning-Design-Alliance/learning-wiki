## Evidence

### Munzer Brendan W. MD 2020

Munzer Brendan W. MD, Bassin Benjamin S. MD, Peterson William J. MD, Tucker Ryan V. MD, Doan Jessica MD, Harvey Carrie MD, Sefa Nana MD, Hsu Cindy H. MD, PhD. (2020). In-situ Simulation Use for Rapid Implementation and Process Improvement of COVID-19 Airway Management. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.7.48159

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retrospective pre/post survey of physician participants in in-situ simulation sessions at Michigan Medicine Adult Emergency Department; 81 of 93 participants responded (87% response rate). Comfort was rated on a five-point Likert scale, showing "a difference of 1.42 (1.20–1.63, p < 0.001)".

> "The average level of comfort with PPE procedures increased significantly from 2.94 (95% CI, 2.71–3.17) to 4.36 (4.24–4.48), a difference of 1.42 (1.20–1.63, p < 0.001)."
