Benchmarking evaluation of nine open-weight LLMs answering every item in the middle school and high school NGSS benchmarks three times under a standardized zero-shot protocol. The results table shows OSS 120b ranked first (one-shot 0.98 on both benchmarks), and "most of the models tested here scored above 90% on both benchmarks."

> "We observe that OSS 120b performed best across both benchmarks, and most of the models tested here scored above 90% on both benchmarks."
