## Evidence

### Esteves 2021

Esteves, A., McConnell, M., Ferretti, E., Garber, A., & Fung-Kee-Fung, K. (2021). "When in Doubt, Ask the Patient": A Quantitative, Patient-Oriented Approach to Formative Assessment of CanMEDS Roles. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11169

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Post-hoc t tests following a within-subjects ANOVA across 69 patient questionnaires and 70 fellow questionnaires revealed that "mean health advocate scores (M = 4.1 [95% CI, 3.8–4.3]) were significantly lower than mean professional scores (M = 4.4 [95% CI, 4.1–4.7], pbonferroni = .002, Cohen d = 1.17)". Mean communicator scores did not differ from either.

> "Subsequent t tests revealed that mean health advocate scores (M = 4.1 [95% CI, 3.8–4.3]) were significantly lower than mean professional scores (M = 4.4 [95% CI, 4.1–4.7], pbonferroni = .002, Cohen d = 1.17)."
