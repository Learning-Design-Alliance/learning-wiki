## Evidence

### Damianus D. Samo 2017

Damianus D. Samo, Darhim & Bana Kartasasmita. (2017). Developing Contextual Mathematical Thinking Learning Model to Enhance Higher-Order Thinking Ability for Middle School Students. International Education Studies, 10(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n12p17

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative investigation-phase analysis of lesson plans, classroom implementation and learning results in one Junior High School in Kupang City, East Nusa Tenggara. The article also reports students' overall higher-order thinking ability was poor and teachers had not yet understood or taught higher-order thinking.

> "Learning activities designed without specific details showed the orientation of the higher-order thinking ability. For the implementation in the classroom, the teachers emphasizes on the conceptual mastery of the material and lack of attention on how the understanding was enhanced in a higher level."
