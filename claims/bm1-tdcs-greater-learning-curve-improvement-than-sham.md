## Evidence

### Cox Morgan L. 2020

Cox Morgan L., Deng Zhi-De, Palmer Hannah, Watts Amanda, Beynel Lysianne, Young Jonathan R., Lisanby Sarah H., Migaly John, Appelbaum Lawrence G. (2020). Utilizing transcranial direct current stimulation to enhance laparoscopic technical skills training: A randomized controlled trial. Brain Stimulation. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2020.03.009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed effects modeling of normalized overall scores from the randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled trial (20 subjects per group) showed the interaction, with the bM1 group showing "significantly greater improvement in overall scores" than sham. No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "A significant group by block interaction was observed: compared to the sham group, the bM1 group showed significantly greater improvement in overall scores over the six training blocks (t = 2.07, p = 0.039)"
