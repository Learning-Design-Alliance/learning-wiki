## Evidence

### Kaśka Porayska-Pomsta 2016

Kaśka Porayska-Pomsta. (2016). AI as a Methodology for Supporting Educational Praxis and Teacher Metacognition. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:679–700. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0101-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports a controlled study by Lin et al. using the CEBLE multimedia learning shell, involving 30 participants, in which the study condition proceeded through five reflection steps (observe, generate, listen to perspectives, act, reflect) while the control did not. The study group "produced substantially more new and more specific solutions than the control group". No effect size is printed.

> "produced substantially more new and more specific solutions than the control group in which teachers did not zoom in on the situations and did not compare their solutions with others"
