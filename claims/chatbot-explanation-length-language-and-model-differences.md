# Chatbot explanations are longer in English than Turkish, and ChatGPT-4o produces longer explanations than Gemini 1.5 Pro in both languages

## Evidence

### Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025

Sabaner Mehmet Cem, Yozgat Zübeyir. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini 1.5 Pro on Ophthalmology-Related Questions in the Turkish Medical Specialty Exam. Turkish Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4274/tjo.galenos.2025.27895

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of explanation word counts from the MCQ evaluation. The article prints medians of "ChatGPT-4o: median 178 vs. 88 words; Gemini 1.5 Pro: median 124 vs. 81.5 words"; no effect size was reported.

> "Explanations generated in English were notably longer than their Turkish counterparts (ChatGPT-4o: median 178 vs. 88 words; Gemini 1.5 Pro: median 124 vs. 81.5 words; all comparisons, p<0.001)."

### Sabaner Mehmet Cem 2025 (2)

Sabaner Mehmet Cem, Yozgat Zübeyir. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini 1.5 Pro on Ophthalmology-Related Questions in the Turkish Medical Specialty Exam. Turkish Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4274/tjo.galenos.2025.27895

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U inter-model comparison of explanation length for the same corpus, significant at p<0.001 in each language; only the test statistic and medians are printed.

> "Furthermore, across both languages, ChatGPT-4o produced longer responses than Gemini 1.5 Pro (p<0.001 for both Turkish and English comparisons)."
