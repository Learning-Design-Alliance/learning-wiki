### Imbroane M 2026

Imbroane M, Fleckenstein K, Vest H, Higgins E. (2026). Breaking Bad News: A Simulation-Based Training Program for OB/GYN Residents. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11606

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post survey of 13 OB/GYN residents completing the curriculum, analyzed with descriptive statistics only. Agreement with feeling prepared rose from "46% vs 85%" pre to post.

> "Comparing pre- and postsession survey results, residents felt more prepared when breaking bad news (46% vs 85% saying that they partially agreed or totally agreed)."
