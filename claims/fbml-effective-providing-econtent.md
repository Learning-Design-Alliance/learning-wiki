### Amasha 2014

Amasha, M., & Alkhalaf, S. (2014). The Effect of Using Facebook Markup Language (FBML) for Designing an E-Learning Model in Higher Education. International Journal of Research in Computer Science, 4(5), 1-9. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The study tested whether using FBML was effective in providing e-content by comparing pre-group (M = 15.52, SD = 4.306) and post-group (M = 45.41, SD = 3.96) degrees using a t-test, finding "a statistically significant distinction between the mean of the students' degrees."

> "Results indicate that there is a statistically significant distinction between the mean of the students' degrees of the first group (the pre-group) in favor of the second group (the post-group). The distinction is essential t( 28, N = 29) = 30.451; p = .05."
