### Fugelli Caroline Guldberg; Hansen Britt Sætre; Ersdal Hege; Kurz Martin 2025

Fugelli Caroline Guldberg; Hansen Britt Sætre; Ersdal Hege; Kurz Martin. (2025). Unveiling team needs: a qualitative study of simulation training for endovascular cerebral thrombectomy. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-002981

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of six focus group interviews; participants described parallel task performance under time pressure as absent from simulation, since basic technical tasks were "simulated much more quickly than the time they usually required in reality".

> "However, they reported that the simulation did not prepare them for challenges inherent to NORA procedures encountered during clinical EVT. These included managing multidisciplinary task interactions, maintaining haemodynamic and respiratory stability, and working within spatial and access constraints."
