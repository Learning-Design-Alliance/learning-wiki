## Evidence

### Bordbar Shima 2025

Bordbar Shima, Mirzaei Saeid, Bahmaei Jamshid, Atashbahar Omolbanin, Yusefi Ali Reza. (2025). Predicting students' academic performance based on academic identity, academic excitement, and academic enthusiasm: evidence from a cross-sectional study in a developing country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07374-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 290-student sample; the article reports the three Fredericks enthusiasm dimensions as significant predictors with P < 0.001 in Table 5.

> "the most important dimensions of academic enthusiasm predicting academic performance were cognitive enthusiasm (β = 0.662), behavioral enthusiasm (β = 0.659), and emotional enthusiasm (β = 0.652), respectively"
