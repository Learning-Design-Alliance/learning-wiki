Kelley, T. L. (1915). Report of the Twenty-fourth Annual Meeting of the American Psychological Association, Chicago, December 28-30, 1915. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r?`

Meeting-report account of Scott's paper on vocational selection, describing two validation approaches for fitness tests. The report prints a large correlation effect size, r = .90 ('Correlations as high as .90 were obtained'), from the known-employee method.

> "Scott proposed, in his paper upon "Experiments on Vocational Selection," two means of checking up test results: (1) by testing employees whose ability is known to the employers, and, (2) as" mentioned above, by examining applicants and correlating their standing in tests with success after employment. Correlations as high as .90 were obtained by use of the former method."
