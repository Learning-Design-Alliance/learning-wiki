## Evidence

### Federer Meghan Rector 2016

Federer Meghan Rector, Nehm Ross H., Pearl Dennis K. (2016). Examining Gender Differences in Written Assessment Tasks in Biology: A Case Study of Evolutionary Explanations. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response verbosity, measured as total word count, was compared across gender subgroups. Males averaged "25.08 ± 0.95" words versus females' "29.18 ± 1.10" on the Penguin item, with "F(1638) = 7.44, p = 0.007"; results were not consistent across items or strands.

> "Evaluation of response verbosity across gender subgroups revealed that, on average, explanation length was equivalent between males and females. However, corroborating the results from the preceding section, explanations for particular items (e.g., trait loss in Penguins) were significantly shorter for males compared with females (25.08 ± 0.95 vs. 29.18 ± 1.10 words, respectively; F(1638) = 7.44, p = 0.007)"
