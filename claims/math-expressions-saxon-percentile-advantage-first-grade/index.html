Roberto Agodini 2010¶

Roberto Agodini, Barbara Harris. (2010). An Experimental Evaluation of Four Elementary School Math Curricula. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, vol. 3, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/an-experimental-evaluation-of-four-elementary-school-math-curricula

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experimental evaluation of four elementary math curricula reporting a curricula comparison for first-grade math achievement. The article reports a 9 to 12 percentile-point advantage for "Math Expressions or Saxon™, instead of Investigations® or SFAW" for an average-performing first-grader; no standardized effect size is printed.

"An average-performing first-grader's percentile rank in math would be 9 to 12 points higher if the school used Math Expressions or Saxon™, instead of Investigations® or SFAW."
