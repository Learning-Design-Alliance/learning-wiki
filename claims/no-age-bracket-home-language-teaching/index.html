Gobodwana 2024¶

Gobodwana, A., 2024, 'The strategies in teaching isiXhosa semantics in Foundation Phase: A systematic review', Reading & Writing 15(1), a493. https://doi.org/10.4102/rw.v15i1.493

q2 · i? · review · r2

Under research question 3, the review states the best way to start reading and teaching language to children should be as early as when they are born, citing Magona's view that the child learns in the womb, and Gobodwana (2023) on primary-school home-language use.

"Recent studies show no specific age bracket for teaching home language to children. However, the child can learn through the mother’s conversation while pregnant and at birth."
