Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Survey paired t-test for LA-tool item 13 among 65 teaching staff. The article reports the largest gap and calls it "an aspect of the highest distrust among respondents of LA tools in terms of providing professional skill development" (t = 4.490, p = 0.000, Cohen's d = 0.7).

> "item 13 (Promoting student academic and professional skill development) had the largest rating gap between predicted and ideal expectations with a large effect size (t = 4.490, p = 0.000, Cohen's d = 0.7)."
