## Evidence

### Ilić IM 2023

Ilić IM, Ilić MD. (2023). The relationship between the burnout syndrome and academic success of medical students: a cross-sectional study. Archives of Industrial Hygiene and Toxicology. https://doi.org/10.2478/aiht-2023-74-3719

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression within the GPA ≤8 subgroup of the 760-student cross-sectional survey, adjusted for age, study year, housing, study financing, smoking and other covariates. The article reports "adjusted OR=2.44; 95 % CI=1.14–5.23; P=0.022" for male versus female gender.

> "within the GPA group the multivariate logistic regression analysis has singled out male gender as a significant independent predictor of high burnout risk in medical students with GPA ≤8 (adjusted OR=2.44; 95 % CI=1.14–5.23; P=0.022)"
