Authors' interpretive comparison in the Discussion mapping their survey's umbrella topics onto the five core ideas Seegerer et al. identified via expert focus groups; superposition, entanglement, quantum algorithms, and quantum communication "correspond quite strongly", while qubits, gates, and measurement together represent the quantum computer.

> "In fact, we find that Seegereret al.'s core ideas correspond quite strongly to the umbrella topics we find are covered in ≥ 80% of the intro QISE courses we surveyed: Superposition, entanglement, quantum algorithms, and quantum communication."
