## Evidence

### Haataja 2021

Haataja, E., Salonen, V., Laine, A., Toivanen, M., & Hannula, M. S. (2021). The Relation Between Teacher-Student Eye Contact and Teachers' Interpersonal Behavior During Group Work: a Multiple-Person Gaze-Tracking Case Study in Secondary Mathematics Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09538-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

In the gaze-duration correlation analysis of the case study, student-started dyadic eye contacts were the category with the lowest teacher agency, showing a negative correlation with printed effect size r = −.147 (p = .012), i.e. "longer gaze durations corresponding with lower teacher agency".

> "In general, teacher agency and the durations of student-started eye contacts had a negative correlation, with longer gaze durations corresponding with lower teacher agency ( r(292) = − .147, p =. 0 1 2 )."
