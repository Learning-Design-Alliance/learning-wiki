# Learning gain, educational experience and adjective ratings did not differ by year of study; only SUS usability was significantly higher for current-year students

## Evidence

### Michalis Xenos 2019

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparisons (RQ5) between 63 current-year and 81 higher-year (course-repeating) students in the same study; the article reports no statistically significant differences on these three measures. Equivalence was not tested.

> "no statistically significant differences were found for these two groups for the normalized learning gain (U=2470, Z=-0.329, p=0.742) as well as for the self-reported educational experience (U=2153.5, Z=-1.619, p=0.106) and for the usability adjective rating (U=2159.5, Z=-1.736, p=0.083)"

### Michalis Xenos 2019 (2)

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same group comparison on the SUS measure; current-year students (M=85.16, SD=6.67) rated usability higher than higher-year students (M=80.87, SD=8.49), the single significant year-of-study difference.

> "the usability evaluation of the Ethics Game, based on the SUS, was statistically significantly higher for the students of the current year than this of students from higher years (U=1932, Z=-2.509, p=0.012)"
