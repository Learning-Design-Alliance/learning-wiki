## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Active learning, flipped classroom and team-based learning interventions show no consistent improvement in shelf exam scores, though student satisfaction increases. [→ Astroth C 2026](#astroth-c-2026)

## Evidence

### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports, citing Crisafio & Cho (n=518), that increasing active learning time did not improve psychiatry NBME scores (p=0.402). Similar null findings for team-based learning and flipped classroom in surgery clerkships.

> "Students who spent more time in active learning environments, defined as team-based learning, small group activities or clinical simulations, did not fare significantly better regarding their shelf scores [35]."
