## Evidence

### Burke 2025

Burke, K. L. A., Snowdon, D. A., Milne, S. C., & Farlie, M. K. (2025). Exploring Physiotherapists' Perspectives of Group Clinical Supervision Effectiveness for Aquatic Physiotherapy Skill Development: A Qualitative Study. Physiotherapy Research International. https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70044

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding interpreted through social positioning theory: balancing the needs of supervisees with varied experience levels is a requirement unique to group supervision, and facilitator awareness of individual skill levels was perceived as key to effective sessions.

> "Senior physiotherapists with experience in aquatic physiotherapy were seen as making particularly valuable contributions to group discussions. Consequently, inexperienced participants were hesitant to contribute to group discussions"
