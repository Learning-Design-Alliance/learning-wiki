Evidence¶

National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER) 2014¶

National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER), evaluation team led by Simon Rutt. (2014). Catch Up® Numeracy: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The ITT analysis was replicated on secondary outcomes (reading ability and general ability). While "significant increases were found in the post-test scores", no difference separated control from either intervention group; no effect sizes are printed.

"Secondary outcomes analysed included pre and post-test data from the Salford Sentence Reading Test and the Non-Reading Intelligence Tests 1-3. Data was analysed in the same way as that used for the main ITT analysis and while significant increases were found in the post-test scores there was no significant difference between pupils in the control group and either of the two intervention groups."
