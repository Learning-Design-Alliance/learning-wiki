Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch 2025¶

Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch. (2025). Learning against the machine: the double edged sword of (Gen)AI in STEM education. International Journal of STEM Education 12:66. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00588-6

The commentary attributes these three documented challenge categories to Fan et al. (2025), Kosmyna et al. (2025), Krupp et al. (2023), and Bastani et al. (2025); the underlying studies' designs are not described in this article. The authors note these effects can interact in manifold ways across systemic levels.

"such as (A) outsourcing cognition and metacognition leading to "cognitive debt" and "metacognitive laziness" (Fan et al., 2025; Kosmyna et al., 2025), (B) passive copy-paste strategies, i.e., "unre -flected acceptance") when using GenAI (Krupp et al., 2023), or (C) overreliance on GenAI-access (Bastani et al., 2025)."
