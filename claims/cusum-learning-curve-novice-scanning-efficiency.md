## Evidence

### Nguyen Anh TV 2013

Nguyen Anh TV, Hill Geraldine B, Versteeg Matthew PT, Thomson Ian A, van Rij Andre M. (2013). Novices may be trained to screen for abdominal aortic aneurysms using ultrasound. Cardiovascular Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-7120-11-42

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective CUSUM analysis of scan times using the Foresee cumulative summation calculator, charted in Figure 2. The article reports "a plateau on the learning curve of all trainees was observed" after scan 110; two trainees later paralleled assessors at the 10-minute criterion, the third had not by scan 210.

> "Using the 10-minute criterion with an expected 90% success rate as the benchmark, an improvement in the novice's performance was seen over the course of the scanning period. Initially all novice trainees failed to achieve an acceptable success rate, but after scan 110 a plateau on the learning curve of all trainees was observed."
