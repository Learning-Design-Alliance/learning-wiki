Howard, S. J., Burianová, H., Calleia, A., Fynes-Clinton, S., Kervin, L. and Bokosmaty, S. (2017). The method of educational assessment affects children's neural processing and performance: behavioural and fMRI Evidence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0010-9

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlational analysis in the behavioural Results of the repeated-measures fMRI study. The article reports the conditions "captured a common core of spelling" while performance still differed systematically by test condition (r = .87 to .91).

> "As expected, correlations between the conditions were high, but non-perfect (ranging from r = .87 to .91), suggesting that although the conditions captured a common core of spelling, there remained systematic differences in performance as a function of test condition."
