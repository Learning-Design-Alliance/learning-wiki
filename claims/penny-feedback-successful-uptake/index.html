Evidence¶

Steve Woollaston 2026¶

Steve Woollaston, Brendan Flanagan, Yuko Toyokawa and Hiroaki Ogata. (2026). Penny: Transition Network Analysis of Learner-Chatbot Interactions in Scaffolded EFL Writing. Preprint, accepted at the 1st Workshop on Transition Network Analysis (TNA) at LAK '26. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14575

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Coded behavioural analysis of 63,328 events from the same 119-learner log dataset. Learner revisions following feedback were classified as successful, unsuccessful, or no uptake, with LLM classification validated by researchers (Fleiss' κ of 0.71); "successful_uptake (7.25%) was observed significantly more often".

"successful_uptake (7.25%) was observed significantly more often than unsuccessful_uptake (1.66%) or no_uptake (1.55%), suggesting that learners frequently and effectively incorporated the feedback provided."
