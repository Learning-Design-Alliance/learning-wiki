# Corpus reuse in the deployed support system is concentrated on a single process step: three certificate/offer-letter entries account for 56.8% of 871 resolutions, with a Gini of 0.586 across entries

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reuse of the 114-entry FAQ corpus is highly concentrated: entries 4, 3 and 1, all concerning certificate submission and offer letter issuance, account for 56.8% of the 871 corpus-mediated resolutions, and the Gini coefficient of reuse across the 113 well-formed entries is 0.586. Descriptive analysis of knowledge-reuse volume across corpus entries in the nine-week operational record (N=871 corpus-linked queries). The three top entries cover "certificate submission and offer letter issuance"; median reuse per entry is 3 against a mean of 7.7, and 30 entries were used exactly once.
