# A pop-up quiz tablet refocused idle multiplayer players during long observation steps

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multiplayer testing, players observing the active player lost focus during inactive stretches; an introduced pop-up quiz tablet was observed to catch their attention and engage them more. [→ Berg H 2023](#berg-h-2023)

## Evidence

### Berg H 2023

Berg H, Prasolova-Førland E, Steinsbekk A. (2023). Developing a virtual reality (VR) application for practicing the ABCDE approach for systematic clinical observation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04625-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation during multiplayer version testing: the quiz tablet appeared each time the active player started a new observation, asking which observation to do next with instant correctness feedback. The authors observed that "this helped to catch the attention of the others".

> "it was obvious that the two players observing and helping the player who carried out the ABCDE examination lost focus when e.g., pulse was taken as this involved a long stretch of inactivity and silence. A pop-up quiz tablet was introduced to help all players refocus"
