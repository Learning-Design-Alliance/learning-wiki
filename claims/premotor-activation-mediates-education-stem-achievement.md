## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Activation in the left premotor cortex significantly mediated the relation between parental education and STEM achievement (bootstrapped standardized indirect effect 0.33, 95% CI 0.13–0.55). [→ Pilato Jazelle 2025](#pilato-jazelle-2025)
`q2 i?` When both premotor activation and MRT performance were included, the relation was significantly partially mediated by premotor activity but not MRT performance. [→ Pilato Jazelle 2025](#pilato-jazelle-2025)

## Evidence

### Pilato Jazelle 2025

Pilato Jazelle, Cortes Robert, Peterson Emily Grossnickle, Uttal David, Kolvoord Robert, Green Adam. (2025). Neurocognitive correlates of spatial thinking mediate the relation between socioeconomic status and STEM achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29345-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis in the neural subset (N=44) with gender and age covariates, using the significant premotor cluster mask. The article reports "Β = 0.59, t(42) = 3.83, p < .001" and "adjusted R2 = 0.29"; bootstrapped indirect effect 0.33, CI 0.12–0.55 in the combined model.

> "Analysis revealed that the neural activity in the cluster significantly predicted STEM academic achievement, Β = 0.59, t(42) = 3.83, p < .001. The neural activity also explained a significant proportion of variance in STEM GPA, adjusted R2 = 0.29, F(4, 40) = 5.60, p = .001"
