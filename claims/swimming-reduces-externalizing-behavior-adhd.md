## Evidence

### Hattabi Soukaina 2022

Hattabi Soukaina, Forte Pedro, Kukic Filip, Bouden Asma, Have Mona, Chtourou Hamdi, Sortwell Andrew. (2022). A Randomized Trial of a Swimming-Based Alternative Treatment for Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316238

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Parents completed the CBCL/4–18 (Arabic form) pre and post in the cluster-randomized trial. The externalizing interaction "was in favor of the experimental group, with a large effect" with a printed effect size of ἠ2 = 0.908; children showed significantly lower social problems (ἠ2 = 0.801), attention problems (ἠ2 = 0.863), delinquent (ἠ2 = 0.827) and aggressive behavior (ἠ2 = 0.880).

> "Starting with the externalizing behavior items, the interaction between the group variable and the time variable (pre- and post-program) was in favor of the experimental group, with a large effect (F (1, 38) = 375.317, p < 0.0001, ἠ2 = 0.908)."
