## Evidence

### Baherimoghadam Tahereh 2025

Baherimoghadam Tahereh, Naseri Navid, Tabesh Amir, Hamedani Shahram, Mousavi Seyedeh Fatemeh. (2025). The influence of learning styles on the efficacy of flipped classroom in dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07916-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative study of 67 fourth-year dental students assigned by stratified random sampling to flipped (n = 34) or face-to-face (n = 33) wire-bending training; satisfaction measured with an 18-item Likert questionnaire. Table 5 reports flipped mean 81.1 (SE 2.3) versus face-to-face 61.6 (SE 1.7), "P-value 0.005*".

> "The results of the chi-square test showed that the students in the flipped class group had significantly higher satisfaction with training than those in the face-to-face classroom (Table 5)."
