Moderator analysis of publication type across 40 journal and 18 grey-literature comparisons. Publication bias tests were significant (Begg p = 0.035; Egger p = 0.007), and trim-and-fill estimated 16 missing studies, attenuating the estimate to g = 0.403.

"journal publications had larger effect sizes than dissertations and conference papers, and this difference was statistically significant (g = 1.039 vs. 0.254, see Table 2)"
