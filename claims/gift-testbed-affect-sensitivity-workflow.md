Special Issue on the Generalized Intelligent Framework for Tutoring (GIFT): Creating a Stable and Flexible Platform for Innovations in AIED Research. `q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The preface describes the special-issue article by DeFalco et al. (2017), which presents the entire process of developing an affect-sensitive system: affect detection research, embedding models in a running system, driving affective interventions, and running an experimental study of impacts on student outcomes. The preface reports that "GIFT can be used to obtain data for detector development." No effect sizes are printed for that study here.

> "This paper shows that GIFT can be used to obtain data for detector development, and that the resultant models – both models based on student interaction and models based on physical sensors – can be embedded back into GIFT for reuse."
