Evidence¶

Sanchez 2018¶

Sanchez, A. C. & Magro, L. J. C. (2018). Humour in the teaching of English and German equivocal words. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(4), 645-653. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1202228

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pilot quasi-experiment with four groups of Spanish B1 learners (36 English, 36 German); experimental groups received joke-based lessons, control groups neutral presentation. Results section reports the delayed post-test contrast: "85% of correct answers" versus "only 58% of correct answers". No effect size or significance test is printed.

"more students (85% of correct answers) from the EG, who had atte nded the lesson in which jokes were used to teach some words, passed Test III with a higher number of correct answers than CG (only 58% of correct answers)"
