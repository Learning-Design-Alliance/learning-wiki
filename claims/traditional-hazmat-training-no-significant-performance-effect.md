`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study (Chiang et al. 2020, Taiwan) of 161 registered nurses in a military hospital, assessed through a standardized 10-minute tabletop exercise simulating a chemical tanker overturn with mass casualties, using a validated 12-item Task-Based Checklist. The review reports "F = 0.09" and "F = 0.59"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Traditional Hazmat training had no significant effect on site control (F = 0.09, p > 0.05) or patient care (F = 0.59, p > 0.05) after controlling for education, age, and gender."
