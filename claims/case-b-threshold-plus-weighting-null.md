## Evidence

### Fielding Shona 2018

Fielding Shona, Tiffin Paul Alexander, Greatrix Rachel, Lee Amanda J, Patterson Fiona, Nicholson Sandra, Cleland Jennifer. (2018). Do changing medical admissions practices in the UK impact on who is admitted? An interrupted time series analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023274

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Segmented regression with 2013 as interruption for case study B, which added a large factor weighting for academic tariff and UKCAT to an existing threshold; the non-selective school interruption coefficient was 10.4 (p=0.055) and "None of the estimates of relative change were significant".

> "The only evidence of the interruption having an effect was for the proportion from non-selective school, where there was a jump up, although the trend before and after was not statistically significant"
