## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pupil outcomes were similar across the TAC tutor tablet, teacher tablet, and pupil e-reader groups, so the study could not determine which ICT intervention was better. [→ Piper 2015](#piper-2015)

## Evidence

### Piper 2015

Piper, B., Jepkemei, E., Kwayumba, D., & Kibukho, K. (2015). Kenya's ICT Policy in Practice: The Effectiveness of Tablets and E-readers in Improving Student Outcomes. FIRE: Forum for International Research in Education, 2(1). http://preserve.lehigh.edu/fire/vol2/iss1/2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-treatment comparison from the Kisumu County randomized controlled trial reported in the Results section. The article states the "similarity in pupil outcomes across the three treatment groups" prevented determining a best intervention; no effect size or test statistic for the between-treatment contrasts is printed in this paper.

> "definitive answers about which ICT intervention was better for Kenya was impossible to determine from this experimental study given the similarity in pupil outcomes across the three treatment groups"
