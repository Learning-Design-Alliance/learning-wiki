Analytical derivation in Section 7: substituting the metric coefficients E(r) and G(r) into the Gaussian-curvature formula for the Riemannian metric (46) yields K = −ε1, and by duality the dual geometry has constant curvature −ε2.

> "This clariﬁes the geometric meaning of the parameters ǫ1 and (by duality) ǫ2: the corresponding Cayley-Klein geometry has the constant curvature −ǫ1 and its dual geometry, the constant curvature −ǫ2."
