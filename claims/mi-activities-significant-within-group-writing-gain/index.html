Evidence¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz 2016¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz, İsmail Doğan Ünal. (2016). Effects of Multiple Intelligences Activities on Writing Skill Development in an EFL Context. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(7): 1687-1697. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040722

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test on the experimental group's essay scores (N=25) in a quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study; Table 4 reports means of 31.48 (pre) and 51.80 (post), t = -5.97, p = .000. The article reports "significant (p<.05)" with no effect size.

"Paired samples t test analysis put forward that the difference between pre-test and post-test scores of experimental group was significant (p<.05)."
