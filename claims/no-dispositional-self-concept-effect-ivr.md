## Evidence

### Lisa Hasenbein 2024

Lisa Hasenbein, Ulrich Trautwein, Jens-Uwe Hahn, Stephan Soller, Richard Göllner. (2024). An experimental test of the Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect using an immersive virtual reality classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09646-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the randomized IVR experiment, regression of dispositional computational-thinking self-concept on the hand-raising conditions found no significant differences for any contrast; the article prints only test statistics (80% vs. 20%: β = −0.01, p = 0.894; 35%: p = 0.078; 65%: p = 0.739), so equivalence was not tested.

> "the hand-raising conditions were not associated with significant differences in dispositional self-concept (β = −0.01, SE = 0.07, p = 0.894 for 80% vs. 20% hand-raising classmates)"
