Evidence¶

Gast 2017¶

Gast, Inken; Schildkamp, Kim; van der Veen, Jan T. (2017). Team-Based Professional Development Interventions in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704306

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative study by Deni and Malakolunthu (2013) using data triangulation of interviews, observations, audio recordings, and a qualitative questionnaire, as reported in the review's teaching-approach theme.

"Deni and Malakolunthu (2013) concluded that teachers were readjusting their teaching style to more student-centered teaching or a democratic teaching style with explicit empathy for students, as a result of participating in a teacher inquiry community."

Gast 2017 (2)¶

Gast, Inken; Schildkamp, Kim; van der Veen, Jan T. (2017). Team-Based Professional Development Interventions in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704306

q2 · i? · review · r3

Quantitative survey study (N = 33) of an 8–12-week online training with a team component; the review attributes the null to the study's technology focus and online approach, noting teachers still showed decreased belief in simple knowledge transmission.

"However, in the quantitative study by Rienties, Brouwer, and Lygo-Baker (2013), teachers did not develop a more student-centered teaching approach as a result of a team-based professional development intervention"
