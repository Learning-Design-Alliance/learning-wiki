`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported worksheet-use comparison between the randomized groups in the same study. The article states that "all learners in the "Google Sites (+)" group reported having used the nine-step ECG interpretation worksheet" while "37.5% of learners in the "Google Sites (–)" group reported not using the worksheet" during the rotation.

> "all learners in the "Google Sites (+)" group reported having used the nine-step ECG interpretation worksheet provided by the FMT from the first ECG tutorial session, which was applied systematically, both in the context of the study and during routine clinical activities. In contrast, 37.5% of learners in the "Google Sites (–)" group reported not using the worksheet for ECG interpretation during their clinical cardiology rotation"
