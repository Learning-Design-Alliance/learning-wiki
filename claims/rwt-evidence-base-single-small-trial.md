### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Read, Write & Type!™. http://www.fcrr.org/TechnicalReports/RWTfullrept.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The WWC's extent-of-evidence categorization (Appendix A5) records one study, five schools, and 146 students for alphabetics and comprehension, and zero studies for fluency and general reading achievement; a moderate-to-large rating would require at least two studies and a total sample of at least 350 students.

> "The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Read, Write & Type!™ to be small for alphabetics and comprehension. No studies that met WWC standards with or without reservations addressed fluency or general reading achievement."
