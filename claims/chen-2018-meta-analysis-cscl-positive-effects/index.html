Evidence¶

Jeroen Janssen 2020¶

Jeroen Janssen, Paul A. Kirschner. (2020). Applying collaborative cognitive load theory to computer-supported collaborative learning: towards a research agenda. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09729-5

q4 · i2 · theoretical · r3

A meta-analysis of three types of (quasi-)experimental CSCL studies, reported by the article as demonstrating positive effects of collaboration versus individual learning, with printed effect sizes "g = + 0.42" for knowledge achievement, g = + 0.64 for skill acquisition, and g = + 0.38 for perceptions.

"Chen et. al.'s (2018) recent meta-analysis demonstrated—amongst other things—a significant positive effect of collaborative learning versus individual learning on knowledge achievement (g = + 0.42), skill acquisition (g = + 0.64), and student perceptions (g = + 0.38) in computer-based settings."
