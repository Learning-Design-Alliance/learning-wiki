Jan Visser 2006¶

Jan Visser. (2006). Learning in a Global Society. Learning Development Institute. https://www.learndev.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument, not an empirical test: drawing on Bandura (1969) and Gagné (1985), who argue human modeling and practicing of the model are essential for changing attitudes, the author contends science taught purposefully can bring such models to life, with collaborative environmental-science projects across geopolitical boundaries facilitated by current technologies.
