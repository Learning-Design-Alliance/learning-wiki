### Gan Xuejing 2024

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous 5-point Likert questionnaire of 173 dental undergraduates (90 Group A, 83 Group B) assessing course pressure. Academic burden scores showed no significant difference (P = 0.248); the article also reports psychological stress (P = 0.644) and examination stress (P = 0.543) as non-significant. No effect sizes are printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "the average academic burden score for Group A was 2.967 ± 0.905, whereas it was 2.807 ± 0.911 for Group B (P = 0.248)."
