# Combined breakfast and exercise speeds Stroop reaction times mid-morning versus fasting-sedentary, without accuracy gains, and the effect fades by morning end

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Reaction-time improvement from start to mid-morning on congruent, incongruent and control Stroop trials was greater in B-E than F-S (all p < 0.05), but response accuracy did not improve and the reaction-time advantage was obviated by morning end. [→ Kawabata Masato 2021](#kawabata-masato-2021)

## Evidence

### Kawabata Masato 2021

Kawabata Masato, Lee Kerry, Choo Hui-Cheng, Burns Stephen F. (2021). Breakfast and Exercise Improve Academic and Cognitive Performance in Adolescents. Nutrients 13(4). https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13041278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stroop Color-Word Test results from the randomized trial: B-E reaction times were faster than F-S at test-series2 for all trial types, with no accuracy differences, and from mid- to end-of-morning F-S and B-S improved more on the congruent trial only.

> "the improvement in reaction time for congruent, incongruent and control trials in the B-E group from the start to middle of the morning was greater than that of the F-S group (congruent: β11 = 115.67, p = 0.024; incongruent: β11 = 158.75, p = 0.046; control: β11 = 104.20, p = 0.036)"
