# STARTS model shows a positive effect of prior achievement on subsequent self-concept but no effect of prior self-concept on subsequent achievement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the STARTS model, prior within-individual achievement positively predicted subsequent within-individual self-concept, while prior self-concept had no effect on subsequent achievement. [→ Kimmo Sorjonen 2025](#kimmo-sorjonen-2025)

## Evidence

### Kimmo Sorjonen 2025

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

STARTS model fitted on the simulated PALMA-like data (good fit: χ2 = 317, DF = 44, CFI = 0.989, RMSEA = 0.042). Achievement effect b = 0.090 [0.031; 0.149], p = 0.003; self-concept effect b = −0.004 [−0.053; 0.046], p = 0.880 — no statistically significant effect; equivalence was not tested.

> "prior within-individual achievement had a positive effect on subsequent within-individual self-concept when adjusting for prior within-individual self-concept (b = 0.090 [0.031; 0.149], p = 0.003). However, contrary to the self enhancement and reciprocal effects models, prior within-individual self-concept had no effect on subsequent within-individual achievement"
