## Evidence

### Xiaomeng Xu 2026

Xiaomeng Xu, Dan Zhang & Yu Zhang. (2026). Student teacher inter brain coupling forecast cross semester academic fluctuation in real world classrooms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00421-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dynamic Bayesian LME analysis of longitudinal wearable-EEG hyperscanning data from 107 students across 393 classes, relating wave-to-wave coupling change (ΔTI) to score change after controlling for baseline TI. The high-beta effects were "positively associated with improvements in student scores" in both subjects, with 95% HPDIs excluding zero and nonparametric p < 0.05.

> "For both Chinese and math, increases in ΔTI within the high -beta frequency band were positively associated with improvements in student scores across the full sample (Chinese: posterior mean = 0.256, 95% HPDI [0.003, 0.516]; math: posterior mean = 0.266, 95% HPDI [0.042, 0.483])."
