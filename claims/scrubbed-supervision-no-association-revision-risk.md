Fully adjusted FPM (Model 4) of NJR data shown in Fig 4, comparing trainee cases supervised by a scrubbed consultant (cases = 61,048; revisions = 1,457) with unsupervised trainee cases (cases = 35,496; revisions = 943). The article reports "no evidence of an association"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no evidence of an association between the level of scrubbed consultant supervision of trainee-performed TKRs and the risk of all-cause revision (Fig 4 and S5 Appendix)."
