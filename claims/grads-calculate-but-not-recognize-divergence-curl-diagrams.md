# Graduate students who can calculate divergence and curl mathematically without difficulty still struggle to recognize them in diagrammatic representations of vector fields

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Graduate students had difficulty recognizing whether diagrams of vector fields show non-zero divergence and curl, while having no difficulty calculating divergence and curl algorithmically. [→ Chandralekha Singh and Alexandru Maries 2012](#chandralekha-singh-and-alexandru-maries-2012)

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Alexandru Maries 2012

Chandralekha Singh and Alexandru Maries. (2012). Core Graduate Courses: A Missed Learning Opportunity?. [doi:10.1063/1.4789732](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789732)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Individual discussions in which the researchers asked some of the graduate students to calculate divergence and curl of vector fields. All of them calculated correctly without difficulty, showing they had 'no difficulty in calculating the divergence and curl' even though the diagrammatic recognition problems revealed conceptual difficulty.

> "they had difficulty in recognizing if the diagrams of the vector field have non-zero divergence and curl but they had no difficulty in calculating the divergence and curl"
