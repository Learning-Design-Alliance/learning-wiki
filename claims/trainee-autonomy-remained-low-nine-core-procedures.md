Analysis 2 summarized trainees' self-assessment of autonomy on the 5-point Dreyfus scale, divided by PGY group (Figure 2). These nine procedures also "did not reach a mean score of 4 in Q4 (feasibility of training in a corresponding procedure) for instructors."

> "However, in terms of PCNL (No. 8), open RPLND (No. 21), open neobladder reconstruction (NB, No. 26), open pyeloplasty (No. 27), open Boari‐flap (No. 28), robot‐assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP, No. 29), robot‐assisted partial nephrectomy (RAPN, No. 30), robot‐assisted radical cystectomy (RARC, No. 31), and varicocele repair (No. 40), self‐assessed surgical autonomy remained low‐level."
