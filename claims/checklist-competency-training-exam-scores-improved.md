## Evidence

### Wang Liuliu 2026

Wang Liuliu, Liu Zeyu, Tong Minghui, Guo Junyan, Zhu Hongmei. (2026). Effect of a checklist-based core competency training and evaluation system on work-related alienation among critical care nurses. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1708734

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject comparison of 150 nurses assessed via the hospital's standardized hierarchical evaluation system (100-point scale). The article reports "t = 15.966, p < 0.001" for theoretical and "t = 28.781, p < 0.001" for practical scores, with post-intervention means of 91.59 ± 2.57 and 92.14 ± 2.18.

> "Following the training period, significantly higher theoretical and practical examination scores were recorded in post-intervention compared with pre-intervention (t = 15.966, p < 0.001; t = 28.781, p < 0.001, respectively)."
