le Roux 2021¶

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

Related Claims¶

  • General media use behaviours predict 8% of variance in academic performance among Southern African university students, with media multitasking the strongest negative predictor — related
  • Adding online vigilance to the media use model increases explained variance in academic performance only from 8% to 9% for the full sample — related
  • Media use and online vigilance predict substantially more variance in academic performance among Namibian (27%) than South African (4%) students
  • Online vigilance correlates negatively with academic performance in the full sample and strongly in Namibia, but not in the South African or Botswana samples
