## Evidence

### S. J. Pollock 2009

S. J. Pollock. (2009). A Longitudinal Study of Student Conceptual Understanding in Electricity and Magnetism. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.5.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.5.020110)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Course-grade comparison across seven semesters of traditionally curved upper-division E&M (average grade ~3.0, B-centered). The Tutorial cohort averaged about 1/5 of a letter grade higher than non-Tutorial peers in the same classes, a difference the authors' faculty colleagues considered pedagogically meaningful despite non-significance.

> "For the "Tutorial" subgroup, the average upper-division course grade was 3.04±0.08 (s.d=0.8, N=97), marginally but not statistically significantly higher than students without Tutorials in the same upper-division classes. (p=.20, 2-tailed t-test)"
