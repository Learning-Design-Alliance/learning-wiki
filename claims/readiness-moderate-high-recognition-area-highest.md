Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey (Table 1, n = 553) on the Scale of Readiness for Clinical Practice. The discussion characterizes overall readiness as moderate to high.

> "The total item mean score was 3.21 ± 1.14. The item mean scores for four subscales were determined from high to low as Recognition of the practice area 3.24 ± 1.23, Ability to provide safety and effective communication 3.23 ± 1.20, Clinical decision-making and practice skills 3.20 ± 1.15 and Basic knowledge and holistic care 3.18 ± 1.15"
