## Evidence

### Abdelghani 2023

Abdelghani, R., Wang, Y.-H., Yuan, X., Wang, T., Lucas, P., Sauzéon, H., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2023). GPT-3-driven pedagogical agents for training children's curious question-asking skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://hal.science/hal-03890447v1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of cue-usage frequency during training in the 75-student experiment; the two incentive agents did not differ (M=77.28% vs M=76.12%, p=0.86). The authors suggest the keyword-based prompting strategy may have been more accessible for children.

> "children with the open automated condition used the agent's cues more often (M=91.78%, SD=8.83) and were significantly different from the hand-crafted incentive agent (p-value= 0.03, power=0.85) and the GPT-3-driven one (p-value=0.005 and power=0.81)"
