## Evidence

### Dawson 2025

Dawson, Esther, and Fi Macintosh. 2025. “Evaluating Musculoskeletal Physiotherapist's Self‐Perceived Knowledge and Understanding of the Assessment, Diagnosis and Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy: A Cross‐Sectional Survey.” Musculoskeletal Care: e70157. https://doi.org/10.1002/msc.70157

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross‐sectional online survey of 108 UK HCPC‐registered MSK physiotherapists recruited via purposive, snowball and convenience sampling; data reported as descriptive frequencies. “86.12% reported feeling ‘fairly’ or ‘very confident’” in subjective screening, with similar majorities for symptom recognition and test selection.

> "Most respondents (86.12%) reported feeling ‘fairly’ or ‘very confident’ in their ability to conduct a comprehensive screening for DCM through subjective questioning. When asked about their understanding of the clinical rationale behind the screening questions, 87.04% expressed being ‘fairly’ or ‘very confident’."
