Evidence¶

Dunn 2013¶

Michael Dunn. (2013). Comparing two story-writing mnemonic strategies: A randomized control trial study. International Journal of Special Education, 28(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1024423

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Effect sizes (Cohen's d) computed against the control group in the randomized control trial: ART d = 1.46 for story content, .85 for story quality, .95 for NWW; T3 d = .30 for story content, 1.13 for story quality, .87 for NWW. The article states ART students achieved larger effect sizes for story content and NWW.

Related Claims¶

  • In a randomized control trial with 12 fourth-grade struggling writers, the ART and T3 groups differed significantly on story content, and T3 differed significantly on story quality — related
  • T3's comparatively small story-content effect is attributed to its lack of specific story-content cue questions, while verbalizing aloud during pre-writing helped story quality — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
