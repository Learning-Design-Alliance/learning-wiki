`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The brief's comparative framing reports DC math impacts of "one-third of a standard deviation", comparable to New Orleans reforms and larger than Success for All and Tennessee class size reductions. This magnitude is stated descriptively; the brief prints no standardized effect size statistic (e.g. d or g), so no coded impact is warranted. Comparisons are narrative, not a formal synthesis.

> "At one-third of a standard deviation for math, the impacts we find in DC are similar in magnitude to those observed for math in New Orleans, where major school reforms were implemented starting in 2006–2007, immediately after hurricane Katrina, and larger than for some well-known education interventions like Success for All and the class size reductions in Tennessee."
