## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

EEG alpha-band analysis in the main experiment found a significant Background × Group interaction at 1600-2000 ms, with "larger event-related synchronization for the out-of-tune-fast group during background+". The printed effect size is η² = 0.13, only marginally significant after Bonferroni-Holm correction.

> "The interaction at time segment 5 (1600 - 2000 ms after word presentation) was qualified by larger event-related synchronization for the out-of-tune-fast group during background+ (F(1,14) = 6.3, p = 0.025, eta2 = 0.13, marginally significant after Bonferroni-Holm correction at the level of p = 0.05)"
