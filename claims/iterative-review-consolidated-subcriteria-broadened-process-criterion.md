Construct validation opportunity identified during guide development; early drafts had limited Process to portfolio compilation, which "limited the interpretation and application of this criterion."

> "This was improved by including parts of process-based assessment into the criterion, specifically reflective practices, opportunities for students to share their thought processes, and metacognitive activities.
