## Evidence

### Hmidani 2017

Hmidani, T. (2017). Didactic model: Teaching the English temporal system to Arabic freshman learners of English. Profile: Issues in Teachers' Professional Development, 19(Suppl. 1), 81-97. https://doi.org/10.15446/profile.v19n_sup1.68617

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on vocabulary test scores. Post-test means were "4.94 (sd = .95)" for the study group versus 3.42 (sd = .81) for the control group, p = .001; Table 3 reports F = 14.60. The author attributes the gain to instruction in using contextual clues to infer meaning.

> "both groups showed no statistically significant differences in the mid-test with p-value = .37. The differences were obvious in the post-test with the study group scoring 4.94 (sd = .95) and the control group, 3.42 (sd = .81) with p-value = .001."
