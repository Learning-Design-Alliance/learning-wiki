Evidence¶

Stephanie M. Jones 2017¶

Stephanie M. Jones, Sophie P. Barnes, Rebecca Bailey, and Emily J. Doolittle. (2017). Promoting Social and Emotional Competencies in Elementary School. The Future of Children, Vol. 27, No. 1, Spring 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1144815

q3 · i? · review · r2

The article's synthesis of two comprehensive meta-analytic reviews of school-based SEL and/or behavioral learning programs, compiling 213 studies in one case and 75 in the other. The reviews found that "universal, school-based SEL programs produced statistically significant positive effects." No exact effect size values are printed; the article describes magnitudes only as ranging from small to moderate-to-large.

"Both reviews found that universal, school-based SEL programs produced statistically significant positive effects on a host of social-emotional and related outcomes."
