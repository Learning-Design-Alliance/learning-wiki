## Evidence

### Orland 2010

Orland, M., Hoffman, A., & Vaughn, III, E. S. (2010). Evaluation of the Comprehensive School Reform Program Implementation and Outcomes: Fifth-Year Report. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#title

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-year follow-up analysis of school-level achievement in CSR schools receiving 2002 awards. The report finds that "After five years, any gains that CSR schools made had evaporated so that mathematics and reading achievement were similar to baseline levels." This contrasts with three-year findings showing some gains.
