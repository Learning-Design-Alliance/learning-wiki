## Evidence

### Archibald D 2014

Archibald D, Macdonald Colla J, Plante J, Hogue RJ, Fiallos J. (2014). Residents' and preceptors' perceptions of the use of the iPad for clinical teaching in a family medicine residency program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-174

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mid-evaluation survey in the eight-month pilot (17 survey respondents from 25 participants) reported descriptive percentages of iPad activities. The article states "About 41% of participants reported to use the iPad to access the curriculum", lower than email, medical apps and databases at 88% each.

> "About 41% of participants reported to use the iPad to access the curriculum. This was a lower percentage compared to the proportion of participants using the iPad for activities such as email (88%), medical apps (88%) and access to medical databases (88%)."
