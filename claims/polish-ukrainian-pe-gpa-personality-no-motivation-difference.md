## Evidence

### Kuśnierz Cezary 2020

Kuśnierz Cezary, Rogowska Aleksandra M., Pavlova Iuliia. (2020). Examining Gender Differences, Personality Traits, Academic Performance, and Motivation in Ukrainian and Polish Students of Physical Education: A Cross-Cultural Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17165729

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Student's t-tests comparing Polish (n = 224) and Ukrainian (n = 200) PE students (Table 3): GPA d = 0.39, emotional stability d = 0.31, extroversion d = 0.24; intrinsic motivation d = 0.03, p = 0.744, i.e., "Polish and Ukrainian samples did not differ in academic motivation".

> "Polish PE students scored higher in emotional stability and extroversion as a personality trait and also had a higher GPA than Ukrainian PE undergraduates. Polish and Ukrainian samples did not differ in academic motivation."
