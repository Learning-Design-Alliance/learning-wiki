## Evidence

### Yanghee Kim 2021

Yanghee Kim, Sherry Marx, Hung Viet Pham, Tung Nguyen. (2021). Designing for robot-mediated interaction among culturally and linguistically diverse children. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10051-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of recorded interaction sessions found that waiting after sending a prompt reduced interruptions; the authors add that as the team became more familiar with children's language and Skusie's lexicon grew, this aspect of the design improved.

> "One important step toward a solution was for the human wizard to send a prompt and wait a sufficient time until the prompt was delivered to children and the children started to respond."
