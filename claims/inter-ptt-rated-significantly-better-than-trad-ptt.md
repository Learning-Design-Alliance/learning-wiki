## Evidence

### Vogt Konstanze 2017

Vogt Konstanze, Pelz Jörg, Stroux Andrea. (2017). Refinement of a training concept for tutors in problem-based learning. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001115](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001115)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental cohort comparison of post-training evaluation questionnaires on a 6-stage Likert scale: 235 tutors completed Trad-PTT (43% response) and 195 completed Inter-PTT (85.6% response). The article reports "significantly better for Inter-PTT than for Trad-PTT" with all differences highly significant (p<0.001).

> "The overall assessment of the training was significantly better for Inter-PTT than for Trad-PTT. All differences between Trad-PTT and Inter-PTT were highly significant (p<0.001)."
