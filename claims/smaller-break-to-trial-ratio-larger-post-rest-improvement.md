Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard. (2022). Dissipation of reactive inhibition is sufficient to explain post-rest improvements in motor sequence learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00140-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the article's summary of the 2×2 experiment with four groups (trial length 10 s or 30 s; break 10 s or 30 s) and a 300 s rest; the abstract states "the smaller the ratio of break time to on-task trial time", the larger the post-rest improvement. Group-level statistics appear in the Results claims.

> "The results show that the smaller the ratio of break time to on-task trial time during training, the larger the improvement in reaction time after the rest period."
