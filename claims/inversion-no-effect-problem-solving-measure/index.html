Evidence¶

Joanne Eaves 2018¶

Joanne Eaves, Nina Attridge, Camilla Gilmore. (2018). Increasing the use of conceptually-derived strategies in arithmetic: using inversion problems to promote the use of associativity shortcuts. Learning and Instruction. https://figshare.com/s/230ee9491e0b67d9d3f3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 2 problem-solving measure: participants solved as many conducive then non-conducive 'a + b – c' problems as possible in 35 seconds each. Between-subjects t-tests found "no significant difference between the conditions" on problems correctly solved for either type. Equivalence was not tested.

"There was no significant difference between the conditions for either the conducive, t(50) = 1.38, p = 0.173, d = 0.38, or the non-conducive problems, t(50) = 0.93, p = 0.358, d = 0.26."
