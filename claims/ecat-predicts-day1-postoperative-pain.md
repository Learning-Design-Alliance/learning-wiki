## Evidence

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable OLS regression with HC3 robust standard errors in a prospective observational cohort of 160 patients treated by fifth-year dental students. The E-CAT score remained "an independent predictor of day-1 PP" after adjustment, with β = 2.10 per unit (95% CI: 0.36–3.84; p = 0.018; R² = 0.065).

> "In the multivariable linear regression model adjusting for sex, age, tooth type, diagnosis, and number of sessions, the E-CAT score remained an independent predictor of day-1 PP (β per 1-unit increase = 2.10; 95% CI: 0.36–3.84; p = 0.018; R² = 0.065)"

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026 (2)

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis analysis of VAS pain scores at day 1 across E-CAT complexity groups (low n=53, moderate n=88, high n=19) in the same cohort. "patients in the moderate and high E-CAT groups reporting significantly higher PP than those in the low group (p < 0.001)"; baseline pain was similar across groups (p = 0.139).

> "On the first day post-treatment, PP correlated positively with E-CAT scores (r = 0.25), with patients in the moderate and high E-CAT groups reporting significantly higher PP than those in the low group (p < 0.001)"
