Related Claims¶

  • SimCalc replacement units produced statistically significant pretest-to-posttest gains in conceptual understanding with student-level effect sizes of .63, .50, and .56 in three Texas experiments — related
  • An adapted SimCalc intervention in Florida produced strong learning gains (ES = 1.09), significantly higher posttests than predicted from the Texas control group — related
  • SimCalc scale-up study showed significant positive impacts on curriculum-aligned conceptual items but no significant difference on state test items — related
  • An adapted SimCalc linear-function unit in England produced significant posttest gains versus Texas-control predictions (ES = 0.57), with no significant gain differences by poverty or prior achievement — related
