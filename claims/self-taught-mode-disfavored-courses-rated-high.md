## Evidence

### O. Bitter 2022

O. Bitter, E.V. Hansen, S. Kravitz, V. Velan, and Y. You (2022). Transforming U.S. Particle Physics Education: A Snowmass 2021 Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests of independence between training mode and training satisfaction (Table III, Figure 16) showed "the “Self-Taught” mode was broadly disfavored", with significant low ratings for every skill reaching p<0.05, including seven of nine professional skills.

> "the “Self-Taught” mode was broadly disfavored. It was given a signiﬁcant low rating compared to the other modes, for every skill that returned p <0.05 on the χ2 test."

### O. Bitter 2022 (2)

O. Bitter, E.V. Hansen, S. Kravitz, V. Velan, and Y. You (2022). Transforming U.S. Particle Physics Education: A Snowmass 2021 Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.08983)

> "when respondents reported an “External course” or “Uni- versity course” mode, they were far more likely to rate this mode highly."
