## Evidence

### Vélez 2023

Vélez, N., Chen, A. M., Burke, T., Cushman, F. A., & Gershman, S. J. (2023). Teachers recruit mentalizing regions to represent learners’ beliefs. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2215015120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROI-based univariate GLM (GLM 1) in 28 scanned teachers, using model-derived trial-by-trial estimates of learners' posterior belief and belief update during the "Show example to learners" event. One-sample t tests against 0 with Bonferroni correction (P < 0.05/8); no effect size printed.

> "After Bonferroni correction, all regions of interest except PC tracked learners’ posterior belief in the correct answer (Fig. 5C; all ps< 0.05/8)."

### Vélez 2023 (2)

Vélez, N., Chen, A. M., Burke, T., Cushman, F. A., & Gershman, S. J. (2023). Teachers recruit mentalizing regions to represent learners’ beliefs. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2215015120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian model selection comparing a control GLM tracking number of examples (GLM 5) against a GLM additionally tracking learners' belief and belief update (GLM 6) within each ROI, using protected exceedance probabilities. This rules out simpler confounding variables for TPJ and MPFC.

> "Three ROIs—ACCg, RSTS, and VMPFC—were best explained by the control GLM, while activations in bilateral TPJ, MMPFC, and DMPFC were best explained by the GLM that additionally represents learners’ beliefs."
