Evidence¶

Loyens 2008¶

Loyens, S. M. M., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Schmidt, H. G. (2008). Relationships between students' conceptions of constructivist learning and their regulation and processing strategies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9065-6

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Structural equation modelling with 98 third- and fourth-year psychology students in a problem-based curriculum; two hypothesized models (ILS and MSLQ variables) both fit, implying that "structural relations exist between instructional conceptions of a constructivist learning environment and regulation and processing strategies."

"Altogether, these ﬁndings imply that structural relations exist between instructional conceptions of a constructivist learning environment and regulation and processing strategies."
