Posner Mareike G., Dempsey Nina C., Unsworth Amanda J. (2023). The Benefits of Using Case Study Focussed, Problem Based Learning Approaches to Unit Design for Biomedical Science Students. British Journal of Biomedical Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10340524/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attendance for randomly selected tutorial sessions in 21/22 and 22/23 was collected via the University PRESTO attendance recording software and in-class head counts. The article reports a 20% attendance increase from 21/22 to 22/23; only descriptive percentages are printed, with no test statistic or effect size.

> "We observed an increase in student engagement with the case study tutorial sessions, with attendance increasing by 20% for the academic year 22/23 compared with 21/22."
