# In a CAVE-based VR sales simulation, condition had no significant effect on presence (IPQ) or social presence (SPQ)

## Evidence

### Francesco Vona 2025

Francesco Vona, Michael Stern, Navid Ashrafi, Julia Schorlemmer, Jessica Stemann, and Jan-Niklas Voigt-Antons. (2025). Sales Skills Training in Virtual Reality: An evaluation utilizing CAVE and Virtual Avatars. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on IPQ total value across four conditions in the 20-participant within-subjects study: "no significant main effect of condition" with F(3, 57) = 0.22, p = 0.879, η2G = 0.004; Greenhouse-Geisser and Huynh-Feldt corrections did not alter the non-significant result. The SPQ analysis was likewise null (F(3, 57) = 0.21, p = 0.887, η2G = 0.002).

> "The repeated-measures ANOVA assessing the impact of condition on the total value of the IPQ revealed no significant main effect of condition,F(3, 57) = 0.22,p= 0.879, η2G = 0.004."
