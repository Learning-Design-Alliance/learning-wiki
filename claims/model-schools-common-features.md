Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/tapestry-american-public-education

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report's review of school networks based on distinct educational designs (Montessori, community schools, NYC International High School), including EL Education (150+ schools in 33 states) and New Tech Network (72,000 students in 115 districts). Effects vary by model and implementation quality.

> "In general, the schools that had positive effects on student achievement have some common features, such as small size and personalized structures; a commitment to forging a positive relationship between students, faculty, and school leadership; and a culture of educator collaboration and professional development."
