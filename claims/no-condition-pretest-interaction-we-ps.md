## Evidence

### Eman Ganaiem 2025

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the experimental design MANCOVA (n = 113 students analysed), the condition-by-pre-test interaction was non-significant (p = 0.870, partial η2 = 0.006): the study found no evidence that the benefit of worked examples first differed with prior knowledge, which does not support the hypothesis that low prior knowledge students benefit most from WE first; equivalence was not tested.

> "Also, there is no significant interaction between condition and pre-test: Wilk's Λ = 0988, F (4, 212) = 0.315, p = 0.870, partial η2 = 0.006."

### Eman Ganaiem 2025 (2)

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the graphing MANCOVA (n = 120 students analysed, of 199 who took part, after high attrition; groups of 38, 40 and 42), the condition-by-pre-test interaction was also non-significant (p = 0.138); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was also no significant interaction between condition and pre-test: Wilk's Λ = 0.940, F (4, 226) = 1.760, p = 0.138, partial η 2 = 0.03."
