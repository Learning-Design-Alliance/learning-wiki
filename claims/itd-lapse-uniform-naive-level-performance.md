## Evidence

### Beverly A. Wright & Ruijing Ning 2024

Beverly A. Wright & Ruijing Ning. (2024). A transient memory lapse in humans 1–3h after training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00272-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

LMER analysis of ITD-tested groups during testing; the lapse "manifested as consistent performance at naïve levels" with slopes near zero for all groups, and the article prints the effect size "t(~113)≥ 2.51, p ≤ 0.02, d ≥ 0.65" for the poorer overall 1-h-group performance.

> "overall performance for the 1-h group was poorer than for the 30-min and 10-h groups, even when taking within-session perfor-mance into account (LMER direct comparison of the simple effect of group: t(~113)≥ 2.51, p ≤ 0.02, d ≥ 0.65), and did not differ from that of the naïve control group"
