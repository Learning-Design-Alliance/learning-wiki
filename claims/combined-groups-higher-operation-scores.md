## Evidence

### Zhang Baoping 2020

Zhang Baoping, Li Sihong, Gao Shuting, Hou Mingfang, Chen Hong, He Lulu, Li Yiting, Guo Yumeng, Wang Errui, Cao Rui, Cheng Jingyang, Li Ruiping, Zhang Kailiang. (2020). Virtual versus jaw simulation in Oral implant education: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02152-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Double-blinded subjective operation assessment on pig mandible scored by professional dentists, analyzed by one-way ANOVA across the four training groups. Combined groups scored higher; "No difference was found between the V-J and the J-V groups (P = 0.128)".

> "The V-J and J-V groups (73.98 ± 4.58; 71.85 ± 4.67) show higher scores than the V and M groups (67.275 ± 4.24; 66.15 ± 3.40), with a statistically significant difference. No difference was found between the V-J and the J-V groups (P = 0.128)."
