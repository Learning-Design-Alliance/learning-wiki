Stenseng, F., Tingstad, E. B., Wichstrøm, L., & Skalicka, V. (2022). Social withdrawal and academic achievement, intertwined over years? Bidirectional effects from primary to upper secondary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1354–1365. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12504

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlations from the weighted descriptive analyses in Table 1 of the TESS teacher-rating data. The discussion reports that withdrawal and achievement "were negatively related at all five measurement points", consistent with prior cross-sectional studies the authors cite.

> "First, and cross‐sectionally, we found that social withdrawal and academic achievement were negatively related at all five measurement points."
