## Evidence

### Li Y 2013

Li Y, Geary DC (2013) Developmental Gains in Visuospatial Memory Predict Gains in Mathematics Achievement. PLoS ONE 8(7): e70160. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0070160

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel models of 177 children's word reading growth (WIAT-II Word Reading subtest). The negative visuospatial effect became non-significant (p = .2457) when central executive and phonological memory were dropped, suggesting it was spurious.

> "with the exception of intelligence, none of the predictors of Numerical Operations scores predicted Word Reading scores (Table 3)."
