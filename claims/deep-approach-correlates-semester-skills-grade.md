## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlational analysis in the flipped classroom cohort between end-of-semester learning approach scores and TOSLS skill categories; Table 4 prints r = 0.225 (P = 0.043) for evaluating the validity of sources and r = 0.235 (P = 0.034) for creating graphical representations, both "positively correlated" with deep approach total scores.

> "Conversely, students' deep learning approach total scores were positively correlated with two specific SL skills, namely the ability to evaluate the validity of sources, and the ability to create graphical representations of data (P < 0.05; Table 4)."

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025 (2)

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same correlational analysis of learning approach scores with final grade; Table 4 prints r = 0.268 (P = 0.015) for the deep learning approach total score and r = 0.099 (P = 0.376) for the surface learning approach total score, the latter reported as "no significant relationship".

> "Students' final grade in the course was positively correlated with deep learning approach total scores (P < 0.05; Table 4), whereas there was no significant relationship between final grades and surface learning approach total scores."

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025 (3)

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null Pearson correlations for the surface learning approach total score with overall TOSLS (r = 0.157, P = 0.160) and all individual skill categories in the same cohort; the article reports "no significant relationships" and no effect sizes are interpreted.

> "There were no significant relationships between students' surface learning approach total score and either their overall TOSLS score or their score within each individual TOSLS skill category (P > 0.05; Table 4)."
