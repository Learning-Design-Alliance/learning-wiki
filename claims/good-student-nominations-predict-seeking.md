## Evidence

### Javier Pulgar 2019

Javier Pulgar, Carlos Rios and Cristian Candia. (2019). Physics problems and instructional strategies for developing social networks in university classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

OLS regression models (Table 1) on information-seeking networks from three class sections, with good-student nomination as a predictor. Model 3 coefficient 0.046 (p<0.01); out-degree 0.261 (p<0.05) and in-degree 0.098 (p<0.05) were also significant, though the article reports no standardised effect sizes.

> "even though good student nominations coeﬃcient is small (0.046, p<0.01) on model 3, its signiﬁcance shows that students will direct their attention towards students perceived as knowledgeable in physics."
