## Evidence

### Bartman I 2025

Bartman I, St-Onge C, Roy M, Gingerich A, Katsoulas E, Chahine S, Gagnon N. (2025). Multi-source feedback in undergraduate medical education: a pilot study. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79283

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility findings from the pilot's post-evaluation survey (Table 3), where students rated burden and stress of the MCC 360 UGME process. Five of seven rated effort required to find raters as moderately burdensome and two as very burdensome; asking supervisors to be raters was the only category rated very stressful by a student. Six of seven said the feedback's usefulness offset the time commitment. No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, they reported the self-assessment was not burdensome but requesting feedback from others was onerous, especially asking supervisors for feedback."
