`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire of 30 experimental-group students on a five-point Likert scale showed favorable attitudes; item 3 ("The teacher should use this program to supplement in-class teaching") had the highest mean (M=4.57, SD=0.50).

> "the students in the experimental group had favourable attitudes towards enhancing their language skills through e-learning. Most students thought that this program should be a supplement to in-class teaching."
