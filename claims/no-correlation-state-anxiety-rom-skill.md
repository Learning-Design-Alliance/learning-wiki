## Evidence

### Melikoğlu Seçil Erden 2025

Melikoğlu Seçil Erden, Demircan Burcu, Yorulmaz Yasemin Kıyak. (2025). The effect of video-assisted teaching on nursing students'practice skills and anxiety levels in Range of Motion (ROM) exercises: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary Spearman Rho correlation analysis within each trial group (n = 36 each). The article reports "r = −0.193, p = 0.258" in the video group and "r = 0.310, p = 0.066" in the control group; neither reached significance. The two correlations point in opposite directions and no single effect size is printed for the overall relationship.

> "No significant correlation was found between the State Anxiety Inventory and ROM application skills of the students in both video and control groups. (r = −0.193, p = 0.258; r = 0.310, p = 0.066)"
