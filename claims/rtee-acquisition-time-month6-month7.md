### Ablordeppey E 2025

Low Frequency, High Complexity: Assessing Skill Decay in Transesophageal Echocardiography Post-Simulation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.35857

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Timing outcome measured with the simulator's built-in timer during the month-6 and month-7 assessments of the seven participants; the article reports the month 6 vs month 7 first-attempt difference was not statistically significant (P = 0.12).

> "At month 6, the mean time for the first attempt to obtain all eight views was 7.3 minutes, decreasing to 4.6 minutes on the second attempt. At month 7, the first attempt time decreased to 5.7 minutes, and the second attempt time decreased to 4.0 minutes."
