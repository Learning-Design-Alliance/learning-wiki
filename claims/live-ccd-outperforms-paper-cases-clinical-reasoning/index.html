Delavari S 2024¶

Delavari S, Barzkar F, Rikers MJP, Pourahmadi M, Soltani Arabshahi SK, Keshtkar A, Dargahi H, Yaghmaei M, Monajemi A. (2024). Teaching and learning clinical reasoning skill in undergraduate medical students: A scoping review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309606

q3 · i3 · review · r3

The review reports, from an included study attributed to Weidenbusch et al. (reference [38]), printed effect sizes SMD = 1.936 at posttest and SMD = 1.933 at follow-up, both P < 0.001; video-CCD effects (SMD = 1.108 and 1.49) were smaller but significant.

"Live-CCD in comparison paper cases had a significant and very large influence on clinical reasoning performance in posttest (SMD, 1.936 [1.337–2.535]; P < 0.001) and follow-up (1.933 [1.334–2.532]; P < 0.001)"
