### Aaron Reinhard 2022

Aaron Reinhard, Alex Felleson, Paula C. Turner, and Maxwell Green. (2022). Assessing the Impact of Metacognitive Post-Reﬂection Exercises on Problem-Solving Skillfulness. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative observation from fall 2019, when groups sorted about 10 new problems of various types three times per semester, identifying problem type, listing identifying features, and setting up each problem on a whiteboard to the point where only math remained.

> "We observed much more enthusiastic conversations during this exercise, as compared with the discus-sions during the composition of problem-solving strategy maps. These interactions often featured rich debate and occurred at higher levels on Bloom’s taxonomy [48]."
