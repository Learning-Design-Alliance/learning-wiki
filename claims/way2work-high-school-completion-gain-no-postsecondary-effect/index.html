Evidence¶

David Mann 2021¶

David Mann, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Marlena Luhr. (2021). Way2Work Maryland Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/way2work-maryland-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

q3 · i? · causal · r2

RCT follow-up analysis 24 months after enrollment comparing treatment and control groups on education outcomes using survey and administrative data. The report finds no effect on postsecondary education and training, while "more treatment group members than control group members" completed high school within two years.

"Way2Work Maryland did not affect postsecondary education and training outcomes, though it did result in more treatment group members than control group members completing high school within two years of program enrollment."

David Mann 2021 (2)¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same RCT comparison on high school completion: the treatment group showed higher completion of high school within two years of program enrollment than the control group. No effect size is printed for this difference.

"Way2Work Maryland did not affect postsecondary education and training outcomes, though it did result in more treatment group members than control group members completing high school within two years of program enrollment."
