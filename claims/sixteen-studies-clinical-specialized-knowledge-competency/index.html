Evidence¶

Li Wansheng 2025¶

Li Wansheng, Li Li, Li Linbo, Xiaodong Cardenas, Chen Mudiao, Liu Hongye, Li Peirao. (2025). Competencies of nurses to participate in safe medication management practices for biologics: A scoping review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317750

q2 · i? · review · r2

This finding comes from the scoping review's synthesis of included studies in the Clinical specialized knowledge section. The authors report that "Sixteen studies indicated that the biologics-related knowledge required by nurses included, but was not limited to, the following: knowledge related to the disease, biologics administration and use, pharmacology, nursing, and therapeutic procedures, management of adverse reactions or events, and medication precautions for special conditions or populations."
