### Budakoğlu Işıl İrem 2023

Budakoğlu Işıl İrem, Coşkun Özlem, Özeke Vildan. (2023). e-PBL with multimedia animations: a design-based research. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04298-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Experimental phase of a design-based research study: 57 e-PBL and 32 f2f participants completed the 16-item Evaluation of PBL Effectiveness Scale posttest. The independent samples t-test found "no statistically significant difference" between platforms; general means were 3.83 (e-PBL) and 3.90 (f2f).

> "The independent samples t-test showed no statistically significant difference between the posttest results of e-PBL and f2f PBL groups regarding process efficacy (t 87 = 0.538; p > .05)."
