Simulator Sickness Questionnaire completed by the MetaRoundWorm group after the VR session; percentages are descriptive with no effect size printed, showing "Eye strain (41.7%), fullness of head (37.5%)" as the most frequent symptoms.

> "Eye strain (41.7%), fullness of head (37.5%), general discomfort (33.3%), and fatigue (33.3%) were the most frequently reported symptoms.
