## Subclaims
`q2 i2` An ANCOVA comparing post-scores across lab treatment with pre-scores as covariate shows lab treatment has a statistically significant impact with a medium effect (F = 18.61, effect size 0.18, p ≪ 0.01). [→ Cole Walsh 2019](#cole-walsh-2019)

## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, and N. G. Holmes. (2019). Assessment of critical thinking in physics labs. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.walsh)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on post-instruction PLIC scores for 383 traditional-FY and 201 SQI-FY students with pre-scores as covariate (Table II: Lab Treatment F = 18.61, effect size 0.18, p ≪ 0.01). The article reports "a statistically significant impact on post-instruction scores with a medium effect".

> "We see that, controlling for pre-instruction scores, lab treatment does have a statistically significant impact on post-instruction scores with a medium effect."
