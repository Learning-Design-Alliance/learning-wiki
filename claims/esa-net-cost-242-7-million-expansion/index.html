Griffith 2024¶

Net-cost estimation (Table 6) using a weighted average Universal ESA value of $6,915 per student: $222.5 million from 32,173 non-public school students, $11 million from NSA district students, $4.9 million from Basic State Aid district students, and a $275,000 savings from charter leavers.

"Thus, our estimated net cost to the state of the Universal ESA program is $242.7 million in 2022–23, increasing to $295.2 million so far in 2023–24."

Related Claims¶

  • Arizona's ESA voucher program grew 409%, from 12,127 to 61,689 students, between the 2021–22 and 2022–23 school years, with nearly all growth from the new Universal ESA — related
  • English learners are strongly underrepresented in the ESA program: 0.6% of ESA students versus 9.2% of public school students statewide — related
  • The state's lack of publicly available ESA data hinders cost estimation; the report recommends publishing enrollment, expenditure, and transfer data — related
  • Total ESA program cost reached about $587 million in 2022–23, a 211% increase over the prior year, equal to 8.8% of Basic Student Aid funding — related
  • The large majority (71.2%) of Universal ESA users had not previously attended a public school, adding new costs to the state education budget — related
  • The Universal ESA amount exceeds per-pupil district funding, so elementary students leaving district schools each add $425 in state cost, while students leaving charter schools save the state $752 each — related
