Evidence¶

Shao 2012¶

Jing Shao. (2012). A Study of Multimedia Application-based Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p202

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Questionnaire data from the experimental study of 80 freshmen using the multimedia software, analyzed with SPSS; the author reports that more than 80% "reacted positively" and that usefulness, ease and fun typified the most positive reactions. No effect size is printed.

"Table 1 shows that students’ general attitudes towards using the multimedia application were significantly favorable. According to the responses to the statements, more than 80% reacted positively and the application’s “usefulness” “ease” and “fun” characteristics typifies the most positive reactions."
