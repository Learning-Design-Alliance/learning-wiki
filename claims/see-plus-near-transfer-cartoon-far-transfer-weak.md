# SEE+ shows near transfer to cartoon character faces in both year groups, but far transfer to real faces is weak and limited to Year 3

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For cartoon faces, SEE+ outperformed both controls in both Year 3 and Year 5. [→ Mayer Sveta 2025](#mayer-sveta-2025)

## Evidence

### Mayer Sveta 2025

Mayer Sveta, Dumontheil Iroise, Wilkinson Hannah R., Porayska-Pomsta Kaska, Farran Emily K., Tolmie Andrew K., Mareschal Denis, the UnLocke Team. (2025). SEE+ computerized classroom-based training enhances 7- to 10-year-olds' socio-emotional cognition through observation and inference. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0330934

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up of the mixed ANOVA's Face type x Condition interaction for cartoon faces (SEE+ character stimuli used in training, i.e., near transfer) showed SEE+ "better performance in the SEE+ condition" than both controls, with no Year x Condition interaction for cartoon faces.

> "a significant main effect of training condition showing better performance in the SEE+ condition (M = 4.111 (SE = 0.024)) than the Stop & Think (M = 3.948 (SE = 0.018), p < .001) and TAU conditions (M = 3.923 (SE = 0.026), p < .001), which did not differ from each other (p = .672"

### Mayer Sveta 2025 (2)

Mayer Sveta, Dumontheil Iroise, Wilkinson Hannah R., Porayska-Pomsta Kaska, Farran Emily K., Tolmie Andrew K., Mareschal Denis, the UnLocke Team. (2025). SEE+ computerized classroom-based training enhances 7- to 10-year-olds' socio-emotional cognition through observation and inference. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0330934

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For real (photograph) faces, follow-ups in each year group separately showed the condition effect "significant in Year 3" but not Year 5; in Year 3, Bonferroni comparisons showed SEE+ more accurate than S&T (p = .007) but no other comparison reached significance. The multilevel model showed no condition effect for real faces, and the authors suggest the Year 3 real-face difference "may have been driven by class or school contexts".

> "the main effect of intervention condition was significant in Year 3 (F(2, 2735) = 5.53, p = .004, ηp2 = .004) but not in Year 5 (p = .200, ηp2 = .001)"
