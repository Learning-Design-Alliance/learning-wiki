## Evidence

### Vig 2014

Vig, R., Taylor, M. W., Star, J. R., & Chao, T. (2014). Assessing implementation fidelity: Challenges as seen through the lens of two experimental studies. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2014/annualmeeting/abstracts/view.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the TESLA program, a small subset of teachers (N=9) were videotaped and scored for fidelity using a binary yes/no instrument with weighted items. The researchers report that the instrument was successful in capturing variation in teachers' implementation.

> "fidelity of implementation scores varied from a low of 29 to a high of 50 (out of a possible 73). The 9 teachers varied in which components of the lesson they taught with high fidelity, the order they presented the lesson activities, the materials scaffolding they provided, and the length, focus, and depth of class discussions"
