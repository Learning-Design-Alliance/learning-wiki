The review reports, citing Preszler 2009, that peer-led workshops let students who previously learned material (peer-leaders) guide groups of peers working together on difficult concepts. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Studies have shown that peer-led teaching, through mechanisms such as workshops, increases competency mastery and success in biology, particularly in PEERs as well as women, although all groups benefit from this type of learning structure (Preszler 2009)."
