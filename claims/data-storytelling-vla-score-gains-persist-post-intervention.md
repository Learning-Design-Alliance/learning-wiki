## Evidence

### Lixiang Yan 2025

Lixiang Yan, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Yueqiao Jin, Vanessa Echeverria, Mikaela Milesi, Jie Fan, Linxuan Zhao, Riordan Alfredo, Xinyu Li and Dragan Gašević. (2025). The Effects of Generative AI Agents and Scaffolding on Enhancing Students' Comprehension of Visual Learning Analytics. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105322](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105322)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in the RQ1 analysis for the data storytelling condition (N=36). Correct-score medians rose from 3.0 pre-intervention to 5.0 in both later phases; the article reports "𝑟 = 0.91(large effect" and r = 0.88 (large effect), with the intervention-to-post change not significant.

> "Post-hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests indicated that the correct scores significantly improved from the pre-intervention (Median = 3.0, IQR = 2.0) to the intervention phase (Median = 5.0, IQR = 1.0),𝑊 = 46,𝑝<. 001,𝑟 = 0.91(large effect (Kerby, 2014)), and from the pre-intervention to the post-intervention phase (Median = 5.0, IQR = 2.0),𝑊 = 52,𝑝<. 001,𝑟 = 0.88 (large effect)."

### Lixiang Yan 2025 (2)

Lixiang Yan, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Yueqiao Jin, Vanessa Echeverria, Mikaela Milesi, Jie Fan, Linxuan Zhao, Riordan Alfredo, Xinyu Li and Dragan Gašević. (2025). The Effects of Generative AI Agents and Scaffolding on Enhancing Students' Comprehension of Visual Learning Analytics. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105322](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105322)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Friedman test across the three phases for the data storytelling condition (N=36) in the RQ1 within-subject analysis, an omnibus test of correct-score differences: "𝜒 2(2) = 30 .05,𝑝 < .001" with Kendall's W = 0.35, described as a medium effect; no standardised effect size is printed for this omnibus result.

> "For the DS condition (N=36), the Friedman test revealed a significant difference in correct scores across the three phases, 𝜒 2(2) = 30 .05,𝑝 < .001,𝑊 = 0.35 (medium effect (Pereira et al., 2015))."
