## Evidence

### Diebold 2020

Diebold, A., Ciolino, J. D., Johnson, J. K., Yeh, C., Gollan, J. K., & Tandon, S. D. (2020). Comparing Fidelity Outcomes of Paraprofessional and Professional Delivery of a Perinatal Depression Preventive Intervention. Administration and Policy in Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-020-01022-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary linear mixed model analysis of the 160-session fidelity dataset examining facilitator characteristics one at a time. The model-estimated adherence difference favoring facilitators with less than a master's degree was 7.5 (95% CI 0.5–14.5).

> "Education level (two-level factor) was significantly associated with adherence (p = 0.038, see Fig. 3). Those with less than a master's degree tended to show better adherence than those with a master's degree or higher (model-estimated difference = 7.5 with 95% CI 0.5–14.5; p = 0.038)."
