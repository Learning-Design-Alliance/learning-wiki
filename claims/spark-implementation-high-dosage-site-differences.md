## Evidence

### Curtis Jones 2018

Curtis Jones. (2018). SPARK Early Literacy: Testing the Impact of a Family–School–Community Partnership Literacy Intervention. School Community Journal, 28(2). http://www.schoolcommunitynetwork.org/SCJ.aspx

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation analysis of in-school participation data for 130 students across the six SPARK sites. The article reports students averaged "120.7 tutoring sessions out of 155 possible sessions"; 58% received a high level, 34% moderate, and 7% low.

> "These 130 students averaged 120.7 tutoring sessions out of 155 possible sessions, with 64.5 in the first year and 56.3 in the second"

### Curtis Jones 2018 (2)

Curtis Jones. (2018). SPARK Early Literacy: Testing the Impact of a Family–School–Community Partnership Literacy Intervention. School Community Journal, 28(2). http://www.schoolcommunitynetwork.org/SCJ.aspx

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Site-level implementation analysis (Table 4 and Figure 3) showing school means from 114.3 to 129.3 sessions; the article reports these differences were statistically significant (F = 2.29, p = .049), suggesting differing tutoring levels across schools.

> "Sites ranged from an average of 114 sessions per student to 129 sessions"
