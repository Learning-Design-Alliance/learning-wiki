## Evidence

### Mathis 2014

Mathis, E. T., & Bierman, K. L. (2014). The Transactional Influence of Parents and Children in a Parent-Administered School Readiness Program. 2014 SREE Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multi-group cross-lag structural equation modeling comparing treatment and control groups in a randomized trial of 210 Head Start families. The chi-square difference test showed "significant differences in the transactional processes" between groups; no effect size was printed.

> "The chi-square difference test comparing these two models showed that the fully constrained model was significantly different from the unconstrained model, χ2 (df = 10, N= 210) = 19.89, p = 0.03, demonstrating significant differences in the transactional processes characterizing parents and children in the intervention vs. control groups."
