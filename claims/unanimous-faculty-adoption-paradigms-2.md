David Roundy, Elizabeth Gire, Ethan Minot, Emily van Zee, and Corinne A. Manogue. (2017). Paradigms in Physics 2.0. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1706.09975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.09975)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case description of the Paradigms 2.0 change process in a physics department. The authors report that the week between two faculty meetings helped ensure faculty did not feel rushed, and that "the faculty unanimously voted to adopt the proposed changes." No outcome measures are reported.

> "After considerable discussion, largely on changes that were not part of the proposal, the faculty unanimously voted to adopt the proposed changes."
