`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Content analysis of open-ended survey answers from FLEX students about the learning design. The three most frequent negative themes were self-regulated learning problems (n = 26), missing direct interaction (n = 20) and time-estimation difficulty (n = 19); some comments applied to the course design generally, as CONV students made similar remarks.
