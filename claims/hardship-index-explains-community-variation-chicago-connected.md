### de la Torre 2023

de la Torre, M. (2023). Exploring Chicago Connected: Participation patterns. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/exploring-chicago-connected

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Variance decomposition from cross-nested hierarchical linear models (Table A.2) on 242,471 students in 649 schools and 77 community areas. Most variation was at the student level, followed by schools, with very little across community areas; student characteristics explained 12% of school-level variation.

> "The addition of community area hardship index explains 65% of the variation between community areas."
