## Evidence

### Mason Nena Lundgreen 2025

Mason Nena Lundgreen, Cohen Edan, Ganga Kiran, Zapata Isain, Hawke Aaron, Peck Meredith. (2025). Building ultrasound-guided procedure skills in regional anesthesiology: simulation training with a formalin-embalmed cadaver model. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03577-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey of 19 first-year medical students after a 15-minute video and 20-minute one-on-one cadaver workshop; confidence assessed on numerically converted Likert scales via GLMs. Scores rose "from 0.4 to 1" pre-training to "2.8 to 3.2" post-training, p < 0.001; no effect size printed.

> "Following training, participant self-confidence increased significantly across all aspects of the procedure (p = < 0.001) (Fig. 2). Pre-training self-confidence scores ranged from 0.4 to 1 and post-training they increased from a range of 2.8 to 3.2."
