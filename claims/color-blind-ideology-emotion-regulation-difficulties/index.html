The review reports the DeCuir-Gunby et al. (2020) study, which foregrounded pre-service teachers' color-blind or color-evasive racial ideology while investigating emotion regulation related to beliefs about race. The review attributes the finding to that study.

"pre-service teachers who scored higher on color-blind racial ideologies scales tended to have difficulties in both regulating their emotions in racial situations and changing their negative thoughts regarding other racial groups"
