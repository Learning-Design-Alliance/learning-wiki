Spain, A.K., Ehrlich, S.B., Cowhy, J.R., Dasgupta, D.K., & Lockaby, T. (2018). A community effort to support the transition from pre-K to kindergarten. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and Chapin Hall at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/community-effort-support-transition-pre-k-kindergarten

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Implementation-fidelity analysis in the developmental evaluation of PKTP's first full year, examining data related to the three components. Teachers reported students enjoyed and benefited from the three selected practices: self-regulation practices, trauma-sensitive practices, and dramatic play, with the grade-level implementation differences quoted above.

> "But pre-K teachers reported more consistent implementation of the common classroom practices compared to kindergarten teachers. Both pre-K and kindergarten teachers implemented the self-regulation practices consis tently, but dramatic play and trauma-sensitive practices were implemented with less frequency, particularly among kindergarten teachers."
