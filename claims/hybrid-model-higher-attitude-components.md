## Evidence

### Torres Anna 2020

Torres Anna, Horodeńska Magdalena, Witkowski Grzegorz, Torres Kamil. (2020). Hybrid simulation of pediatric gynecologic examination: a mix-methods study of learners' attitudes and factors affecting learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02076-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of 16 residents in a 2-day PAG simulation workshop, using an original web-based attitude questionnaire based on the Tripartie Model; combined answers showed residents "valued the hybrid model higher over task -trainer-SP-voice model" across all three components. The article reports no effect size for this comparison.

> "residents valued the hybrid model higher over task -trainer-SP-voice model in regards to all three attitude components: cognitive (95%), affective (87.5%) and behavioral (83.7%)."
