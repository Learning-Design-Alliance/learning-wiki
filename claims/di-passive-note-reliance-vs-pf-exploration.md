# DI students relied on notes rather than exploring the agent-based models, while PF classes showed lively collaborative exploration and questioning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Qualitative classroom observations indicated DI students spent most of their time checking notes and rarely used the models to reason, whereas PF students discussed ideas and explored the models collaboratively. [→ Jacobson 2017](#jacobson-2017)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2017

Jacobson, Michael, Markauskaite, Lina, Portolese, Alisha, Kapur, Manu, Lai, Polly, & Roberts, Gareth (2017). Designs for learning about climate change as a complex system. Learning and Instruction, 52, pp. 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.007

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Daily qualitative observational notes by the research team in the four classes. DI students rarely asked questions during instruction and showed little dyad discussion, while PF students discussed ideas with partners, asked questions during Consolidation, and tried parameter settings like the teacher's.

> "Rather than using the computer models to reason about the problems, the DI students seemed to assume the answers to the problems were in the notes, and so they spent much of their time looking at the notes instead of running computer experiments and trying to reason with the computer models."
