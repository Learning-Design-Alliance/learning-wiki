## Evidence

### Chen D 2026

Chen D, Zeng L, Ou W and Zhong S (2026) ASR technology in college English speaking instruction: the role of feedback internalization and metacognitive strategies. Front. Psychol. 17:1847238. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1847238

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

SEM path analysis of the 325-response survey tested accuracy as a predictor of reflective behavior. The path was non-significant (p = 0.885); the article prints no effect size, so the null is reported as a non-detected effect, not equivalence.

> "the path coefficient for recognition accuracy was −0.011 (SE = 0.077, CR = –0.144, p = 0.885). This indicates that recognition accuracy has no significant effect on triggering Reflective Behavior."
