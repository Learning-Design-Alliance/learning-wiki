Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Implementation analysis of CI artifacts, school leader surveys, and interviews in Research Question 2. A companion passage reports fewer than two-thirds of schools (58 percent) chose change ideas explicitly related to their theory of practice improvement.

"Nearly three-quarters of schools (72 percent) conducted a “root cause analysis” to develop an understanding of the problem, either on their own or with guidance from the intermediary."

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024 (2)¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Artifact and interview analysis in Research Question 2 found most schools showed equity attention in aim statements or root cause analyses, chiefly as a general focus on improved outcomes, with limited evidence of explicitly targeting Black, Latino, or students experiencing poverty in change ideas.

"Teams gave less attention to other dimensions of equity: increasing student access to resources, centering student identity, or supporting student agency (voice)."
