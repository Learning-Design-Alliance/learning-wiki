## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Hypothetical 1 SD boosts to kindergarten math achievement yield estimated grade 3 effects of roughly .5 (math) and .27-.37 (reading) under CLPM, but .06 or below under RI-CLPM and ST-CLPM models. [→ Bailey et al. 2020](#bailey-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### Bailey et al. 2020

Drew H. Bailey, Yoonkyung Oh, George Farkas, Paul Morgan, Marianne Hillemeier. (2020). Reciprocal Effects of Reading and Mathematics? Beyond the Cross-Lagged Panel Model. Developmental Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/dev0000902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Table 2 total-effect estimates of a hypothetical 1 SD kindergarten boost on grade 3 outcomes, compared across the article's models and Duncan et al. (2007). CLPM-family estimates are approximately .5 (math) and .27 to .37 (reading); RI-CLPM and ST-CLPM estimates are .06 or below.

> "Most notably, Duncan and colleagues' estimates and our CLPMs predicting large persistent effects of boosts to kindergarten math achievement on grade 3 math achievement (all approximately .5) and reading achievement (.27 to .37). In contrast, all of the corresponding estimated effects in the RI-CLPM and ST-CLPM models are .06 or below."
