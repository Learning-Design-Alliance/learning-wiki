# Young people who feel isolated when not online are the ones that do not experience a sense of online risk

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Young people that feel isolated when they cannot go online are precisely the ones that do not experience a sense of online risk; in the isolated segment most do not experience a sense of risk (54%). [→ Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023](#munoz-rodriguez-2023)

## Evidence

### Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CHAID segmentation analysis of the 1991-respondent questionnaire. The abstract states that "the young people that feel isolated when they cannot go online are precisely the ones that do not experience a sense of online risk". No effect size is printed for this headline contrast.

> "our results indicate that the young people that feel isolated when they cannot go online are precisely the ones that do not experience a sense of online risk"

### Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023 (2)

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within the CHAID tree, the segment agreeing with feeling isolated when not online mostly does not experience a sense of risk (54%), confirming the inverse association between isolation feeling and risk perception in this sample.

> "An analysis of the segment comprising those young people that fully or fairly agree with feeling isolated, most of whom do not experience a sense of risk (54%)"
