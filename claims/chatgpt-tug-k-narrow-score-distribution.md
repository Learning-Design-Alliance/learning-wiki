## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Score-distribution analysis of the 60 ChatGPT surveys shown in Fig. 1: ChatGPT's interquartile range was "only 2" against 10 for the student sample, which the authors read as stability in chatbot performance.

> "The first and third quartiles for ChatGPT data are 10 and 12 points, respectively, giving an interquartile range of only 2, as shown in Fig. 1. For students in Zavala et al.'s study [46], the first and third quartiles were 7 and 17, respectively, giving an interquartile range of 10"
