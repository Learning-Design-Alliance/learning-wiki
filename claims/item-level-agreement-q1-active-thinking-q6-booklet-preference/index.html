q2 · i? · design · r2

Item-level descriptive results from the user survey, shown in Figure 1. The authors report Q1 at "95% agreement" and Q6 at "42% agreement", noting students were genuinely divided and that a well-established resource with worked solutions remained preferred; no test is reported.

"The single strongest item (Q1) was that students were actively thinking about the solution rather than guessing when using the platform (95% agreement), which speaks directly to the active-learning intent of the design. The weakest item (Q6) was a direct preference for the GenAI system over the existing problem booklet (42% agreement)"
