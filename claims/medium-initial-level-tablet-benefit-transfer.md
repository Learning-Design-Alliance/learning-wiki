## Evidence

### Nathalie Bonneton-Botté 2020

Nathalie Bonneton-Botté, Sylvain Fleury, Nathalie Girard, Maëlys Le Magadou, Anthony Cherbonnier, Mickaël Renault, Eric Anquetil, Eric Jamet. (2020). Can Tablet Apps Support the Learning of Handwriting? An Investigation of Learning Outcomes in Kindergarten Classroom. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103831

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Tukey HSD tests (set at the .05 level) following the 2x3x2 ANOVA on pre/post paper-and-pencil writing scores of 233 children; the tablet-app condition outperformed traditional instruction "only for children with a medium initial level". Only p-level significance printed, no effect size.

> "These revealed a significant effect of experimental condition, but only for children with a medium initial level. No learning effect was observed for students with a high initial level in either experimental group."
