### Shihao Yang 2026

Shihao Yang, Marshall Van Alstyne, and Chrysanthos Dellarocas. (2026). When AI Tutors Speak: Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23958

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' summary of the preregistered SOLO depth co-primary outcomes (mean SOLO level and share of answers at SOLO≥3) in the randomized field experiment; the abstract reports the relational share rising from 8% to 49% in the tutored arm against 8% to 27% in the holdout.

> "The gain was concentrated in the conceptual depth of written answers, where the share reaching relational quality grew sixfold, rather than in ceilinged multiple-choice scores."
