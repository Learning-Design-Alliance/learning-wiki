### Işıkgöz Mustafa Enes 2025

Işıkgöz Mustafa Enes. (2025). Mindfulness and sustainability competencies in physical education teachers: Demographic factors as moderators. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02885-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the Mindfulness in Teaching scale in this study's sample of 852 PE teachers, using WLSMV estimation. The article reports the two-dimensional structure was "confirmed" with the printed fit indices; the article also notes the X2/df value was above 3 but acceptable for large samples.

> "The WLSMV method was used, and the results confirmed the two-dimensional structure with an appropriate model fit (X2/df = 3.04, RMSEA = 0.05, SRMR = 0.06, CFI = 0.97, TLI = 0.96)."
