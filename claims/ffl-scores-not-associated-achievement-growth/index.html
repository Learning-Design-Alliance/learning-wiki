Concurrent-validity analysis using a regression model relating school leaders' estimated contributions to student achievement growth to their FFL scores, based on the 2012/13 pilot ratings. No association was found; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

"School leaders with larger estimated contributions to student achievement growth did not score higher than school leaders with smaller estimated contributions."
