# Captioning second-language videos produces large benefits for comprehension and vocabulary acquisition

## Evidence

### Noetel 2022

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Harris, N. R., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2022). Multimedia design for learning: An overview of reviews with meta-meta-analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211052329

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Synthesis of the Montero Perez et al. (2013) meta-analysis, which the authors rated more favourably on four of seven quality criteria than the alternative review. Captioning showed large effects on second-language comprehension (g = 0.99, k = 15) and vocabulary acquisition (g = 0.87, k = 10).

> "This review found captioning led to large benefits for both comprehension (g = 0.99, 95% CI [0.60, 1.38], k = 15; Montero Perez et al., 2013) and vocabulary acquisition (g = 0.87, 95% CI [0.58, 1.15], k = 10; Montero Perez et al., 2013)"
