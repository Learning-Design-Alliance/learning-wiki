### Wang Junyu 2025

Wang Junyu, Yang Yong, Wu Jun, Xie Ting, Guo Xuejun, Yuan Xun, Li Ting, Zhang Yu, Zhao Huiying, Zhuang Jie. (2025). Association between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity trajectories and academic achievement in Chinese primary school children: a 3-year longitudinal study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21487-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalized estimating equation models on the three-year cohort, adjusted for age, sex, BMI, sleep duration, parental education and sports attitudes. The article reports the printed β coefficients and 95% CIs for High vs Low MVPA; these are unstandardised score-point coefficients, so no standardised effect size is printed and, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The High MVPA group exhibited a significant association with score increases in reading (β = 11.3, 95% CI: 7.93–14.67), mathematics (β = 12.32, 95% CI: 9.61–15.02), and language (β = 11.53, 95% CI: 8.99–14.06)."
