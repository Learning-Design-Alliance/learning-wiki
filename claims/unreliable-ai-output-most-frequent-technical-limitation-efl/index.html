Qualitative evidence synthesis of 54 Scopus-indexed empirical studies (2021–2026) on AI-supported undergraduate EFL instruction; study-level frequencies from the cross-study coding show "unreliable AI-generated output was reported in 22 studies (41%)". Percentages are descriptive prevalence, not effect sizes.

"Within this category, unreliable AI-generated output was reported in 22 studies (41%), followed by limited contextual understanding in 16 studies (30%), while prompt sensitivity, speech-recognition limitations, hallucinations, and feedback limitations were reported less frequently across the reviewed studies."
