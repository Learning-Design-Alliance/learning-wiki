The review reports Galinsky and Moskowitz's (2000) experiment assigning participants to write essays from an out-group member's perspective, about in-group/out-group similarity, or about their own similar behavior; the perspective-writing manipulation was "the most effective for reducing the favoritism of the in-group over the out-group".
