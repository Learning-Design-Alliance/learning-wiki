`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Conclusion of a case report of technology support delivery within a pragmatic cluster-randomized controlled trial at 10 hemodialysis facilities, based on program delivery data, staff interviews, reflection papers, and postintervention surveys. The article reports support "received by all mentees and 46.2% of mentors".

> "In conclusion, technology support provided by human intermediaries, received by all mentees and 46.2% of mentors, helped close potential hemodialysis patient-related digital divides in this trial of a patient-facing digital intervention."
