## Evidence

### Rebecca 2017

Rebecca, Ufonabasi Etiubon & Nsimeneabasi Michael Udoh. (2017). Effects of Practical Activities and Manual on Science Students’ Academic Performance on Solubility in Uruan Local Education Authority of Akwa Ibom State. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131863

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of the practical-activities group (53 students) classified by gender with pretest as covariate. The gender effect was not significant (p=.809); "null hypothesis two is retained". Descriptive mean gains were 40.10 for males and 37.89 for females, though the test showed no significant difference.

> "As shown in Table 5, the calculated P-value .809 of gender is greater than alpha level .05. Therefore, null hypothesis two is retained."
