## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Edelsbrunner et al. 2018

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Lennart Schalk, Ralph Schumacher, Elsbeth Stern. (2018). Variable control and conceptual change: A large-scale quantitative study in elementary school. Learning and Individual Differences 66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2018.02.003

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent transition analysis with CVS as covariate on N = 1809 students; transition paths whose probabilities varied at least 10% between students at +1.5SD and −1.5SD CVS were interpreted. The article reports "a positive predictive value of CVS" for this path.

> "For students starting in the high misconceptions proﬁle (Fig. 6A), with increasing CVS the probability decreased that they would stay in this proﬁle. Rather, they were more likely to transition into the intermediate proﬁle."

### Edelsbrunner et al. 2018 (2)

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Lennart Schalk, Ralph Schumacher, Elsbeth Stern. (2018). Variable control and conceptual change: A large-scale quantitative study in elementary school. Learning and Individual Differences 66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2018.02.003

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same LTA covariate analysis. The article states "Whether this indicates a positive predictive value will be discussed", marking this path as mixed rather than uniformly positive.

> "For students starting in the low misconceptions-proﬁle (Fig. 6C), the probability decreased that they would transition into the scientiﬁc proﬁle, while increasing in the probability to transition into the intermediate proﬁle."
