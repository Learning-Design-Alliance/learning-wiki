## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2026

Yilmaz, M., Temur, H. B., Emmungil, L., Çelik, E., Gauthier, A., & Cukurova, M. (2026). Supporting self-regulated learning through generative AI feedback in online higher education: the importance of student perceptions of the source of feedback. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 23(16). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00592-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of post-intervention perception questionnaire scores (adapted from Nazaretsky et al.) between the GenAI (n = 20) and tutor (n = 21) groups; 41 of 46 students completed it. The study reports "a statistically significant difference was observed only for the Genuineness dimension" with r = .36.

> "While GenAI-generated feedback received higher ratings across all perception dimensions, a statistically significant difference was observed only for the Genuineness dimension, favouring the GenAI group and yielding a moderate effect size (p = .036, r = .36)."
