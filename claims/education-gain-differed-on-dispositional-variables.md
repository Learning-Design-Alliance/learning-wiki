The editorial reports this retrospective study of adult basic and secondary education students, which examined goals, goal-directed thinking via hope theory, attendance behaviors, and affective-domain attributions; the printed finding is that gainers "signiﬁcantly differed from those who did not on dispositional or demographic variables".
