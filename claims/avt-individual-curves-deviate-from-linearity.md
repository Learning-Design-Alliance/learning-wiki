## Evidence

### Haider and Frensch 2002

Hilde Haider & Peter A. Frensch. (2002). Why Aggregated Learning Follows the Power Law of Practice When Individual Learning Does Not: Comment on Rickard (1997, 1999), Delaney et al. (1998), and Palmeri (1999). Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.28.2.392

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six single-case studies in which participants evaluated 100 alphabet verification strings across 30 practice blocks; statistical tests of individual log-log learning curves for deviation from linearity (Myers & Well, 1991) found that "all 6 participants showed significant deviations from linearity". Figure 1 panels display the discontinuities visually.

> "all 6 participants showed significant deviations from linearity, suggesting that their latencies improved nonlinearly and probably even discontinuously with practice."
