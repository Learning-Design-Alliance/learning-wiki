# The Experienced/Novice factor combination has a significant simple main effect on GUI-programming exam success

## Evidence

### Mihci 2017

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same 2x2 factorial ANOVA as the interaction test: the experience factor's simple main effect on Practical Exam scores was significant, with experienced subgroups scoring higher (e.g., Control experienced M=67.00) than novice subgroups (e.g., Control novice M=20.00).

> "The ―Experienced/Novice‖ factor combination also has a significant simple main effect upon academic success in GUI-programming, as measured by the Practical Exam (F(1,44) = 29,48 p = .00)."
