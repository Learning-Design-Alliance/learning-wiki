## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2018

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on log-based total coverage (mean 70.2%) across the three conditions of 65 elementary students. Post hoc Tukey LSD contrasts showed the 2-demo tutorial had lower total coverage than both the control and review tutorials; control and review did not differ.

> "An ANOVA showed a main effect of condition, F(2,62) = 6.49, p = 0.003. Post hoc analyses revealed that total coverage for the 2-demo tutorial was lower than for the control ( p = 0.001), and the review tutorial (p = 0.041)."
