## Evidence

### Jonsson K 2021

Jonsson K, Brulin C, Härgestam M, Lindkvist M, Hultin M. (2021). Do team and task performance improve after training situation awareness? A randomized controlled study of interprofessional intensive care teams. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00878-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 20 interprofessional ICU teams (11 intervention, 9 control) trained in situ; GEE analysis of video-rated TEAM scores. The intervention group's leadership mean rose from 2.67 to 3.17, with "no corresponding increase in the control group"; mean change difference p = .003.

> "in the intervention group, the mean value for the subscale leadership increased from case 1 to case 2 (2.67, SE .11 vs. 3.17, SE .10), while there was no corresponding increase in the control group (2.50, SE .12 vs. 2.59, SE .11)"
