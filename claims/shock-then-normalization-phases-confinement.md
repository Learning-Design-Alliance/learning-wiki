## Evidence

### Ramos-Morcillo A.J. 2020

Ramos-Morcillo A.J., Leal-Costa C., Moral-García J.E., Ruzafa-Martínez M. (2020). Experiences of Nursing Students during the Abrupt Change from Face-to-Face to e-Learning Education during the First Month of Confinement Due to COVID-19 in Spain. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17155519

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the 'time' theme of the interview analysis: "On the first days, the shock phase appears (1st phase), within which we find "disorientation". This first phase lasts between 7–10 days." Students reported less concentration and chaotic timetables.

> "On the first days, the shock phase appears (1st phase), within which we find "disorientation". This first phase lasts between 7–10 days. During this first week, it is observed that mental performance decreases, along with the ability to concentrate."
