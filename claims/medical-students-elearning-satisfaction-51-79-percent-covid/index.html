Evidence¶

Tabatabaeichehr Mahbubeh 2022¶

Tabatabaeichehr Mahbubeh, Babaei Samane, Dartomi Mahdieh, Alesheikh Peiman, Tabatabaee Amir, Mortazavi Hamed, Khoshgoftar Zohreh. (2022). Medical students’ satisfaction level with e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic and its related factors: a systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2022.19.37

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of 24 cross-sectional studies identified through searches of Scopus, PubMed, Web of Science, and Persian databases to July 10, 2022. The review reports "The level of satisfaction with e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic among medical science students was 51.8%" (51.79% in the synthesis section). No meta-analysis was performed.

"A total of 15,473 medical science students were enrolled in 24 studies. The level of satisfaction with e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic among medical science students was 51.8%."
