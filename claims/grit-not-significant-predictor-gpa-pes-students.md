## Evidence

### Tannoubi Amayra 2023

Tannoubi Amayra, Quansah Frank, Magouri Iteb, Chalghaf Nasr, Bonsaksen Tore, Srem-Sai Medina, Hagan John Elvis, Handrianto Ciptro, Azaiez Fairouz, Bragazzi Nicola Luigi. (2023). BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-023-01454-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis in the same 459-student sample; Table 4 reports GRIT→GPA Estimate = 0.037, p = 0.511. The authors conclude "the Grit did not have any significant impact on students GPA"; no effect size is printed.

> "In summary, the findings of the current study partially support the suggested hypotheses regarding (i) the significant impact of engagement elements and (ii) study processes on success. However, (iii) the Grit did not have any significant impact on students GPA."
