Rivkin 2023¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Regression discontinuity-style comparison of schools just above versus just below the merit award threshold. Award winners left at "16.9%" versus "5.9%" for near-miss principals; footnote reports 14.1/16.9 = 83% of departures were district exits, suggesting winners leveraged the award's labor-market signal.

"Principals who received the award were nearly three times as likely to leave their school the year after they won the award (16.9%), compared to principals with similar effectiveness who just missed the merit award cut-off (5.9%)."
