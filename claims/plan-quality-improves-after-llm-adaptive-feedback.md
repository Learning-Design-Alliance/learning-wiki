## Evidence

### Sukjaitham 2026

Sukjaitham, S., Schaaf, M., Brod, G., & Breitwieser, J. (2026). Adaptivity Makes Feedback Effective: Evidence From AI-Generated Feedback on Children's Plans. Preprint. https://osf.io/4w6rz/overview?view_only=dd3ec8d2878243579a463ccd0d77d723 [doi:10.31234/osf.io/t2eac_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/t2eac_v1)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered within-subjects experiment with 155 children (Mage = 12.08 years) completing six planning trials. A Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing pre- and post-support plans in the LLM condition found a significant increase, "a large effect size (r = .86)".

> "Results from the nonparametric Wilcoxon signed -rank test showed that plan quality scores were significantly higher after receiving LLM-generated adaptive feedback than before feedback (V = 10,440, p < .001). This improvement was associated with a large effect size (r = .86)"
