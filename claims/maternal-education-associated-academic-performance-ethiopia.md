`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the same cross-sectional study of 514 students. Children of literate mothers showed "4 times better academic performance" with adjusted odds ratio AOR = 4.083529 (CI: 1.603–10.398, p=0.003); primary-completed mothers AOR = 3.000349, secondary-completed AOR = 4.720083.
