## Evidence

### Tamimi Ahmad 2023

Tamimi Ahmad, Hassuneh Mariam, Tamimi Iskandar, Juweid Malik, Shibli Dana, AlMasri Batool, Tamimi Faleh. (2023). Admission criteria and academic performance in medical school. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04251-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective longitudinal cohort of 808 students admitted in 2012–2013; 626 (77.5%) graduated on time. The logistic regression (adjusted for high school score and high school type in Table 1) found "students admitted through the parallel pathway and International students were significantly less likely to graduate on time".

> "Logistic regression analysis revealed that students admitted through the parallel pathway and International students were significantly less likely to graduate on time, while students admitted through the national unified admission and through the quota for university employees were more likely to graduate on time."
