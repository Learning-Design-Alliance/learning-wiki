## Evidence

### Başaranoğlu Mert 2025

Başaranoğlu Mert, Akbay Erdem, Erdem Erim. (2025). AI-generated questions for urological competency assessment: a prospective educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07202-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective single-center study of 42 fourth-year medical interns (93.3% completion of 45 invited) assessed at three points during a 72-hour urology rotation using repeated measures ANOVA. The article reports "F = 156.4, p < 0.001" for the improvement across assessment points; no effect size is printed.

> "Performance improved significantly across the three assessment points (F = 156.4, p < 0.001), with mean scores increasing from baseline (mean: 45.2%, 95% CI: 42.6–47.8%) through mid-rotation (mean: 62.8%, 95% CI: 60.4–65.2%) to final assessment (mean: 78.4%, 95% CI: 76.5–80.3%)."
