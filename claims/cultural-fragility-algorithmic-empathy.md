`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A theoretical argument in this conceptual analysis, contrasting correctable content errors with emotional errors that touch "recognition, dignity, belonging, and psychological safety." The article argues this is especially risky for teachers, whose emotion is intertwined with professional identity and pedagogical authority.
