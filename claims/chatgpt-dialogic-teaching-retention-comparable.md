# Undergraduates learning via dialogic teaching with ChatGPT show retention test scores comparable to those learning with a human teacher

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who dialogued with ChatGPT scored similarly to students who dialogued with a human teacher on a knowledge retention test, with no statistically significant difference (t = 0.133, p = 0.895). [→ Jiayue Zhang 2024](#jiayue-zhang-2024)

## Evidence

### Jiayue Zhang 2024

Jiayue Zhang, Yiheng Liu, Wenqi Cai, Lanlan Wu, Yali Peng, Jingjing Yu, Senqing Qi, Taotao Long, Bao Ge. (2024). Investigation of the effectiveness of applying ChatGPT in Dialogic Teaching Using Electroencephalography. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.16687](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.16687)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A randomized experiment compared 17 undergraduates taught dialogically by ChatGPT (EG) with 17 taught by a human teacher (CG); both then took a 40-point retention test. Means were 32.53 (SD 4.87) for CG and 32.29 (SD 5.42) for EG, and "the difference is not significant."

> "Considering the t-test results, the average scores of the two groups of students are very close, and the difference is not significant."
