## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of four students who misdiagnosed at Step 1, three changed their diagnosis at Step 2 (one to the correct diagnosis) and one changed at Step 3 (still incorrect); no effect size is printed. [→ Kojima J 2025](#kojima-j-2025)

### Kojima J 2025

Exploratory analysis of diagnosis changes in the cross-sectional vignette study (107 valid respondents). Four of 107 students revised a Step 1 misdiagnosis; "one changed the diagnosis in Step 2 to a correct diagnosis". No effect size printed.

> "Four participants had misdiagnosed in Step 1, and changed their first impression in Steps 2 or 3: one changed the diagnosis in Step 2 to a correct diagnosis; two changed their diagnoses in Step 2, but still misdiagnosed; and the remaining one participant changed the diagnosis in Step 3, but still misdiagnosed"
