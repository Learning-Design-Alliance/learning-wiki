## Evidence

### Robins Jason A. 2014

Robins Jason A., McInnes Matthew D. F., Esmail Kaisra. (2014). What information is provided in transcripts and Medical Student Performance Records from Canadian Medical Schools? A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25181

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Blinded double extraction of one anonymised transcript and MSPR from each of 17 Canadian medical schools (2012 or 2013 CaRMS applications), assessing presence of pre-selected criteria. The study reports "ranging from 22.2% (Dalhousie) to 70.4% (McGill)" with mean 47.4% and median 48.1% (Fig. 1).

> "Overall inclusion of all of the criteria varied by school, ranging from 22.2% (Dalhousie) to 70.4% (McGill); the mean was 47.4% and the median 48.1%"
