### Evans Kerry 2022

Evans Kerry, Woodruff Jenny, Cowley Alison, Bramley Louise, Miles Giulia, Ross Alastair, Cooper Joanne, Baxendale Bryn. (2022). GENESISS 2—Generating Standards for In-Situ Simulation project: a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03401-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic mapping review searching seven databases from inception to October 2020; 69 papers included and synthesised descriptively and tabularly into four purpose categories. The review reports that "Findings were organised into categories to reflect the aims and objectives of the included studies using ISS".

> "Findings were organised into categories to reflect the aims and objectives of the included studies using ISS: 1) as an assessment tool; 2) to assess and promote system readiness and safety cultures; 3) to improve clinical skills and patient outcomes; 4) to improve NTS, knowledge and comfort and confidence."
