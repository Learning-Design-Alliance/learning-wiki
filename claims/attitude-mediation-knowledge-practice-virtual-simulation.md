### Xia Qing 2026

Xia Qing, Bu Juanjuan, Zuo Hang, Wang Jie, Hou Yuesong, Wang Jinian, Wu Xiaoli. (2026). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of medical students at a Chinese university toward virtual simulation experimental training: a cross-sectional survey of self-perceived experience. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis using the PROCESS macro with Bootstrap (5000 samples) on the same cross-sectional survey data. The article reports the indirect effect of 0.437 and that "The indirect effect accounts for 69.59% of the total effect"; knowledge and attitude together explained 73.51% of variance in practice (R²=0.735, F = 406.47).

> "The total effect of knowledge on practice was significant (β = 0.628, 95% CI [0.523, 0.733], P < 0.001). After introducing attitude as a mediating variable, the direct effect of knowledge on practice remained significant (β = 0.191, 95% CI [0.114, 0.268], P < 0.001)."
