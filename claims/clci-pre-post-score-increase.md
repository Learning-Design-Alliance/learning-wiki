## Evidence

### Wörner 2022

Wörner, S., Becker, S., Küchemann, S., Scheiter, K., & Kuhn, J. (2022). Development and validation of the Converging Lenses Concept Inventory for middle school physics education. Receipt Date: 19.05.2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020131)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of total test scores (excluding item Q2) from N = 318 posttest students in a pre–post administration around school lessons on converging lenses. The article reports the shift as "statistically highly significant, indicated by a t-test (p < .0001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The test scores’ mean and standard deviation shifts from Mpre = 11.07 points out of 30 (36.89%), SDpre = 4.37 (14.55%) to Mpost = 17.84 points out of 30 (59.47%), SDpost = 5.80 (19.34%). This shift is statistically highly significant, indicated by a t-test (p < .0001)."
