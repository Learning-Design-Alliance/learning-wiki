## Evidence

### Postiglione A and De Angelis I 2021

Postiglione A and De Angelis I. (2021). Students' understanding of gravity using the rubber sheet analogy: an Italian experience. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abd1c4)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of 6 high school classes (153 pre-, 125 post-, 42 delayed responses) attending the Roma Tre activity. The authors report that students "have signiﬁcantly improved their knowledge about the topics addressed", confirmed at four months; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, the students who participated in our activity with the rubber sheet have signiﬁcantly improved their knowledge about the topics addressed. This trend is also conﬁrmed by the responses obtained four months after the activity."
