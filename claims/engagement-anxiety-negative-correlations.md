## Subclaims
`q2 i3` All three engagement dimensions show significant negative correlations with learning anxiety (e.g., dedication r = -0.404) and significant positive correlations with self-efficacy dimensions. [→ Zhou Tian 2025](#zhou-tian-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhou Tian 2025

Zhou Tian, Zhou Xuejun, Wang Baozhu. (2025). Latent profiles of learning engagement and anxiety in high school students: the mediating role of academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03114-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of the 936-sample survey (Table 1): dedication correlated r = -0.404 with learning anxiety, and vigor–dedication r = 0.552, both printed with ** significance markers in the article's correlation matrix.

> "According to the correlation analysis results, the dimensions of learning engagement and academic self-efficacy show significant negative correlations with learning anxiety. Additionally, the dimensions of learning engagement are significantly positively correlated with the dimensions of academic self-efficacy"
