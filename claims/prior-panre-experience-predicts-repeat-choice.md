## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression in the full cohort; the model printed "2.62 times higher odds of choosing it again". Subgroup analyses (Table 2) showed 9.4% traditional PANRE selection among PAs without prior experience and 30.7% among those with prior experience. No standardized effect size is printed.
