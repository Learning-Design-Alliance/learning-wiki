Evidence¶

Cho 2019¶

Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G., Roberts, G. J., & Vaughn, S. (2019). Examining Sources and Mechanisms of Reading Comprehension Difficulties: Comparing English Learners and Non-English Learners within the Simple View of Reading. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000332

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Results of the multigroup SEM comparing 229 ELs and 211 non-ELs (as reported in the abstract summarizing the model results) show the two groups' standardized path patterns diverged, with linguistic comprehension paths dominating for ELs and word reading dominating for non-ELs.

"For non-ELs, the effect of word reading was larger than the effects of linguistic comprehension (vocabulary and listening comprehension combined); however, for ELs, the effects of linguistic comprehension were greater than the effect of word reading."
