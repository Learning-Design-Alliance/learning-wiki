From the qualitative questionnaire and interview analysis of the main study (45 students), 77,7% expressed that "time is absolute and unaffected by the inertial observer", illustrated by the student quote that time "can't change".

> "A common view among students (77,7%) was that time is absolute and unaffected by the inertial observer relative to whom it is measured ."
