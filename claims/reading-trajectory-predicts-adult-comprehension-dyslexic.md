## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The growth curve slope (reading change from grade 1 to 5) was strongly related to adult comprehension for dyslexic readers (βs = 0.567) but much less so for typical readers (βs = 0.280). [→ Emilio Ferrer 2023](#emilio-ferrer-2023)

## Evidence

### Emilio Ferrer 2023

Emilio Ferrer, Bennett A. Shaywitz, John M. Holahan and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Early reading at first grade predicts adult reading at age 42 in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00205-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal regression predicting adult Total Comprehension from the grade 1-5 growth slope, comparing typical (n = 246) and dyslexic (n = 66) readers; dyslexic readers show "βs = 0.567" vs 0.280 for typical readers (standardized coefficients, p = 0.000).

> "whereas the relation from the slope decreased for typical readers ( βs = 0.280), it increased for dyslexic readers ( βs = 0.567)"
