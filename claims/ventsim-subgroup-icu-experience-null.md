## Evidence

### Ippolito M. 2022

Ippolito M., Simone B., Safadi S., Spinuzza E., Catania T., Ingoglia G., Milazzo M., Raineri S.M., Giarratano A., Gregoretti C., Cortegiani A. (2022). Effectiveness of a remote simulation training in mechanical ventilation among trainees. Pulmonology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pulmoe.2022.05.007

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis basing on previous clinical rotation in ICU, reported in Table 1: among trainees with ICU experience the groups scored "32.7 ± 7.8 (65%) 31.3 ± 7.1 (63%)" with P = 0.52; among those without experience, 26.8 ± 8.4 vs. 25.7 ± 9.4 with P = 0.54. Both subgroup contrasts were non-significant.
