### Saeed Sana 2024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Evaluator-rated CTS mean rater scores pre- versus post-workshop for the three interprofessional teams; the article reports these significant paired t-test improvements with no effect size printed.

> "Overall situational awareness rating significantly improved from 2.66 ± 2.05 to 7.27 ± 1.48, decision-making rating rose from 3.50 ± 1.54 to 7.16 ± 1.42, while overall role responsibility rating improved from 2.50 ± 1.54 to 8.66 ± 1.02 (p < 0.001) (Table 3)."
