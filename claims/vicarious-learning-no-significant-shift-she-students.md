## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level paired t-tests on the vicarious-learning questions of the SOSESC-P for she-identifying students (N = 91) in the TBL IPLS course. The article calls this "particularly interesting" given Zeldin and Pajares' work suggesting vicarious learning may be an important self-efficacy source for she-identifying students. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Figure 9 shows that none of the questions have a signiﬁcant shift for ‘she’-identifying individuals at the p < 0.05 level using a paired t-test."
