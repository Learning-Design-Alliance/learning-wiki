Evidence¶

David W Chadwick 1999¶

David W Chadwick, Rana Tassabehji and Andrew Young. (1999). Experiences of Using a Public Key Infrastructure for the Preparation of Examination Papers. IT Institute, University of Salford. http://www.salford.ac.uk/its024/X500.htm

q1 · i? · design · r2

Observational account of infrastructure failures: a router failure left the intranet not fully operational for a week and several days with no spare in stock; unannounced DNS table changes made the security software fail to register users; and the CMS Notes email system was down for a week during QA exchange.

"No organisation would tolerate its telephone system or postal system to be out of action for a week or longer, but during the course of this pilot various network and e-mail components were unavailable for nearly 3 weeks."
