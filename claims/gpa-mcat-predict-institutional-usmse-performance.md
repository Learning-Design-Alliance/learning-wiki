# Institutional mean GPA and MCAT strongly predict institutional mean USMLE Step 1 (adjusted R² 72%) and Step 2 CK (adjusted R² 41%) scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multiple regression across 89 institutions, mean GPA and MCAT achieved an adjusted R² of 72% for Step 1 (standardized βMCAT 0.7, βGPA 0.2) and 41% for Step 2 CK (βMCAT 0.5, βGPA 0.3). [→ Burk-Rafel J 2019](#burk-rafel-j-2019)

## Evidence

### Burk-Rafel J 2019

Burk-Rafel J, Pulido RW, Elfanagely Y, Kolars JC. (2019). Institutional differences in USMLE Step 1 and 2 CK performance: Cross-sectional study of 89 US allopathic medical schools. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0224675

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational institution-level regression across 89 US allopathic medical schools using self-reported 3-year averages. The model "achieved an adjusted R2 of 72% for Step 1" and 41% for Step 2 CK, with standardized βMCAT 0.7/0.5 and βGPA 0.2/0.3; both model P < .001. No effect size beyond R² is printed.

> "The final regression model utilizing GPA and MCAT achieved an adjusted R2 of 72% for Step 1 (standardized βMCAT 0.7, βGPA 0.2, model P < .001) and 41% for Step 2 CK (standardized βMCAT 0.5, βGPA 0.3, model P < .001)."
