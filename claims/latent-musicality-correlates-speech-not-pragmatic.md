# Latent musicality correlates moderately with speech-related communication (r = 0.50) and modestly with cognition (r = 0.33), but not with pragmatic or social-communication factors

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In a data-driven five-factor phenotypic SEM, the musicality factor correlates with the speech factor (r = 0.50, SE = 0.02) and the cognition factor (r = 0.33, SE = 0.02), but not with the pragmatic or SCDC social-communication factors. [→ Lucía de Hoyos 2025](#lucia-de-hoyos-2025)

## Evidence

### Lucía de Hoyos 2025

Lucía de Hoyos, Ellen Verhoef, Aysu Okbay, Janne R. Vermeulen, Celeste Figaroa, Miriam Lense, Simon E. Fisher, Reyna L. Gordon & Beate St Pourcain. (2025). Preschool musicality is associated with school-age communication abilities through genes related to rhythmicity. npj Science of Learning 10:39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00329-y

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Data-driven multivariate modelling (EFA in one random half, CFA in the other) of 25 ALSPAC measures yielded a correlated 5-factor model with good fit (CFI = 0.90, TLI = 0.88, RMSEA = 0.049, SRMR = 0.034); the article reports "associations of F musicality with F cognition (r = 0.33(SE = 0.02)) and F speech (r = 0.50(SE = 0.02))".

> "Focusing on meaningful factor correlations ( | r | >0.3), we observed associations of F musicality with F cognition (r = 0.33(SE = 0.02)) and F speech (r = 0.50(SE = 0.02)) but not with factors underlying more social aspects of communication (Fpragmaticand FSCDC)."
