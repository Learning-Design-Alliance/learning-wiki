q1 · i? · review · r3

The review attributes this to a 1997 research report evaluating increases in resource room instructional group size in New York City public schools; teachers reported diminished opportunities to help students and observations showed little individual instruction. No effect size is printed.

"The report indicated that increases in instructional group sizes from 5 to at most 8 students per teacher led to decreases in the reading achievement scores of resource room students."
