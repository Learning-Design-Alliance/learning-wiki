Xu Hongxuan, Möllerberg Marie-Louise, Dalhammar Karin, Bengtsson Mariette, Sjögren Forss Katarina. (2026). Nurse Managers' Awareness of and Competence in Palliative Care in Municipal Care Settings: A Systematic Mixed Studies Review. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70352

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantitative cross-sectional study (Mitchell et al. 2016, Ireland) of 56 care home managers using the Palliative Care Quiz for Nursing; the review appraises it as moderate quality. Managers scored "an average score of 12.89 out of 20 (64.45%)"; items on electrolyte imbalances and respiratory depression were answered correctly by less than 50% of participants.

> "Care home managers demonstrated their level of palliative care knowledge on the PCQN, with an average score of 12.89 out of 20 (64.45%). They had a good understanding of palliative care principles, therapeutic pain‐relieving strategies, and the emotional and psychological aspects of care."
