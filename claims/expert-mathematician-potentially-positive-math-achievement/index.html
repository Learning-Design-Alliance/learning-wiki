Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: The Expert Mathematician, Middle School Mathematics, Revised October 14, 2006. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/513

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial of 90 eighth-grade students (70 completing pre- and posttests) in four classrooms in a suburban St. Louis middle school, comparing The Expert Mathematician (n = 45) with Transition Math (n = 45). Appendix A3 reports means of 45.10 (SD 12.03) versus 40.80 (SD 12.41), "Mean difference" 4.30, "Effect size" 0.35, "ns", improvement index +14.

"Baker (1997) found The Expert Mathematician group did not score statistically significantly higher than the comparison group on the posttest measure of achievement after adjusting for group pretest differences."
