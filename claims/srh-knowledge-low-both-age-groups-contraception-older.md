## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Older girls were more likely to spontaneously name a method of modern contraception (adjusted OR 1.56), while pregnancy, STI and HIV knowledge scores showed no significant differences between age groups. [→ Kangwana Beth 2020](#kangwana-beth-2020)

## Evidence

### Kangwana Beth 2020

Kangwana Beth, Muthengi Eunice, Austrian Karen. (2020). Intra-grade variability in educational and psychosocial competencies of school going adolescent girls, in the coastal region of Kenya: implications for school based interventions. BMC Public Health 20:1097. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-020-09097-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baseline survey analysis of SRH knowledge items among 3489 grade 7 girls; the results table reports adjusted OR 1.56 (95%CI 1.36, 1.80) for naming modern contraception and non-significant coefficients for pregnancy, STI and HIV knowledge.

> "Girls in the older age groups were only more knowledgeable than their younger counterparts on being able to mention a modern contraception; not only was there no difference in knowledge on pregnancy and STIs between the two groups but also knowledge on these topics across the two groups was generally low"
