### Jenkins Anna 2021

Jenkins Anna, Weeks Molly S., Hard Bridgette Martin. (2021). General and specific stress mindsets: Links with college student health and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256351

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the 498-participant cross-sectional study (Table 2 and Table 3). The article reports the rs as "very modest" and interprets this as mindsets varying with stressor features.

> "Correlations among the general and source-specific stress mindsets measures were very modest (rs ranging from .01 to .33, see Table 2), and in some cases non-significant, indicating that participants' mindsets did vary as a function of the features of sources of stress"
