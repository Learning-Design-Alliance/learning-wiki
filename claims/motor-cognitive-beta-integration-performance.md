## Evidence

### Shafiei Somayeh B. 2018

Shafiei Somayeh B., Hussein Ahmed Aly, Guru Khurshid A. (2018). Dynamic changes of brain functional states during surgical skill acquisition. PLoS ONE 13(10): e0204836. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204836

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of integration and recruitment quantities from 524 recordings across six sessions (Table 1). The printed statistic is motor-cognitive integration versus performance level at beta: -0.84 (0.03); integration of motor-visual and cognitive-visual modules did not display significant changes by performance improvement.

> "Significant correlation between performance level and motor-cognitive integration during the β frequency band may indicate at higher performance levels (expertise improvement) tendency of channels in motor and cognitive modules to be integrated together decreases."
