## Evidence

### Berhenke 2011

Berhenke, Amanda; Miller, Alison L.; Brown, Eleanor; Seifer, Ronald; Dickstein, Susan. (2011). Observed Emotional and Behavioral Indicators of Motivation Predict School Readiness in Head Start Graduates. Early childhood research quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical OLS regression with 131 Head Start graduates in kindergarten; observed motivation variables predicted teacher-rated outcomes. The article reports "High persistence in the task predicted higher math and reading skills" (R2 = .17 reading; R2 = .20 math).

> "High persistence in the task predicted higher math and reading skills; competence, pride, and help-seeking were not significantly related to academic skills."

### Berhenke 2011 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "Increased task persistence predicted greater social competence, interpersonal and work-related skills, self-regulation, and less hyperactivity."
