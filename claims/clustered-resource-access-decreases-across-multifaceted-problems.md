### Pavlo D. Antonenko 2007

Pavlo D. Antonenko, John Jackman, Piyamart Kumsaikaew, Rahul R. Marathe, Dale S. Niederhauser, C. A. Ogilvie, and Sarah M. Ryan. (2007). Understanding Student Pathways in Context-rich Problems. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Log analysis of PSLP tracking data from teams solving five multifaceted physics problems across a semester. The authors report the clustered-access percentage fell from "39% in pr oblem 1" to 3% by problems 4 and 5, confirming Stevens et al.'s IMMEX finding that students move from prolific gathering to interspersed resource use.

> "The percentage of t eams who exhibited a “clustered” pattern of accessing resources (Figure 1) was 39% in pr oblem 1. This decreased significantly in problem 2 (24% of teams), and disappeared almost completely in problem 3 (5% of teams), problem 4 (3% of teams), and problem 5 (3% of teams)."
