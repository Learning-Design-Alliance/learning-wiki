# ANCOVA with pre-test as covariate confirms significant post-intervention increases in total attitude score and subscales 2 and 3

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` With pre-test as covariate, ANCOVA showed significant increases after intervention in total attitude (P = 0.010) and subscales 2 and 3 (P = 0.004 and P = 0.003). [→ Mohseni Fatemeh 2024](#mohseni-fatemeh-2024)
`q3 i?` Subscale 1 (Perceived Interactions) showed no significant adjusted difference (P = 0.652). [→ Mohseni Fatemeh 2024](#mohseni-fatemeh-2024)

## Evidence

### Mohseni Fatemeh 2024

Mohseni Fatemeh, Mohammadi Aeen, Khabaz Mafinejad Mahboobeh, Khajavirad Nasim, Basiri Kamal, Gruppen Larry D. (2024). Teaching conflict management to medical students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06514-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test scores adjusted for pre-test in the RCT; Table 4 reports F = 6.906 (total), F = 8.934 (F2), F = 9.483 (F3), and F1 "(F1)" was non-significant at P = 0.652. No effect size was printed.

> "The ANCOVA results, considering the type of education as the independent variable, the post-test as the dependent variable, and the pre-test as the covariate, showed that after the educational intervention, the the mean total attitude score, along with the mean scores for subscales 2 and 3, increased significantly (P = 0.010, P = 0.004, and P = 0.003, respectively)."
