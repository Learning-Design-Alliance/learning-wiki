# Think-pair-share instruction produced higher conceptual learning in classical mechanics than conventional teacher-centered teaching in a quasi-experimental physics course

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The main treatment effect and the test x group interaction on MBT post-test scores were statistically significant in favor of the experimental (TPS) group. [→ Gok 2018](#gok-2018)
`q2 i?` The fractional learning gain of the TPS group (g=0.67, medium) exceeded that of the control group (g=0.25, low). [→ Gok 2018 (2)](#gok-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Gok 2018

Gok, T. (2018). The evaluation of conceptual learning and epistemological beliefs on physics learning by think-pair-share. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 4(1), 69-80. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170940

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A two-group pre-test/post-test quasi-experiment with 35 TPS students and 36 control students taught by the same instructor; a 2x2 repeated measures ANOVA on MBT scores gave Group F=200.16, Test F=734.81, Test x Group F=156.86 (df 1,69, p=0.000), and the article reports the interaction "statistically significant in favor of EG".

> "The main treatment effect and interaction (test x group) between EG and CG were calculated to be statistically significant in favor of EG."

### Gok 2018 (2)

Gok, T. (2018). The evaluation of conceptual learning and epistemological beliefs on physics learning by think-pair-share. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 4(1), 69-80. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170940

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hake fractional gains computed for the same quasi-experiment: the TPS group's gain was "gEG=0.67 “medium”" while the control group's was "gCG=0.25 “low”"; no standardized effect size was printed, so impact is uncoded.

> "The learning gain of EG was found to be gEG=0.67 “medium” and the learning gain of CG was calculated to be gCG=0.25 “low”."
