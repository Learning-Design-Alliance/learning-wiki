## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within the BEST in CLASS group, all targeted teacher practices, engagement, disruption, and interactions changed significantly from baseline to post-test, and all except OTR were maintained from baseline to maintenance. [→ Conroy 2014](#conroy-2014)

## Evidence

### Conroy 2014

Conroy, M. A., Sutherland, K. S., Algina, J. J., Wilson, R. E., Martinez, J. R., & Whalon, K. J. (2014). Measuring Teacher Implementation of the BEST in CLASS Intervention Program and Corollary Child Outcomes. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426614532949

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group planned comparisons for the BEST in CLASS group (N = 60 at post-test, N = 59 at maintenance); e.g., engagement rose from 0.87 to 0.98 at post-test and 0.96 at maintenance, disruption fell from 0.10 to 0.01 and 0.02.

> "the results indicated that the teachers who received the BEST in CLASS intervention significantly increased the overall percentage of intervals from baseline to post-test and from baseline to maintenance of their use of rules, precorrection, behavior specific praise, instructive feedback, and corrective feedback."
