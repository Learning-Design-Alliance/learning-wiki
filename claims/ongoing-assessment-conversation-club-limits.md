### Castillo Losada 2017

Castillo Losada, C. A., Insuasty, E. A., & Jaime Osorio, M. F. (2017). The impact of authentic materials and tasks on students’ communicative competence at a Colombian language school. PROFILE Issues in Teachers’ Professional Development, 19(1), 89-104. http://dx.doi.org/10.15446/profile.v19n1.56763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tape-recorded conversation club sessions assessed with the Assessing Speaking Performance: Level A2 rubric. Qualitative observation: the native speaker "did most of the talking"; problems were pronunciation and low vocabulary range.

> "Results from the observation in the conversation club showed that students seemed to understand the native speaker but could not maintain a simple, basic conversation. The native English speaker did most of the talking as students’ answers were limited."

### Castillo Losada 2017 (2)

Castillo Losada, C. A., Insuasty, E. A., & Jaime Osorio, M. F. (2017). The impact of authentic materials and tasks on students’ communicative competence at a Colombian language school. PROFILE Issues in Teachers’ Professional Development, 19(1), 89-104. http://dx.doi.org/10.15446/profile.v19n1.56763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pen-pals project written-competence assessment: of 42 expected letters, "only thirteen were submitted by seven participants". Submitted letters showed basic structures, connectors, logical sequencing, and non-impeding errors.

> "A total of forty-two letters were expected; however, only thirteen were submitted by seven participants."
