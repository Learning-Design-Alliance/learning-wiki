### van den Boer 2015

van den Boer, M., & de Jong, P. F. (2015). Parallel and serial reading processes in children's word and nonword reading. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(1), 141-151. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037101

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of 2nd, 3rd, and 5th graders (N = 314) using latent classes of naming-task performance. The authors state the classes "seemed to reflect a developmental shift"; no effect size is printed.

> "Interestingly, the different classes seemed to reflect a developmental shift from reading all letter strings serially toward parallel processing of words, and later of nonwords."
