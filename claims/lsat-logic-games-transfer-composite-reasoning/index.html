Evidence¶

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge 2018¶

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge. (2018). Eye gaze patterns reveal how reasoning skills improve with experience. npj Science of Learning 3:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0035-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bayesian analysis of pre/post composite reasoning scores (Odd One Out, Object Reasoning, Analogical Reasoning, Analysis Synthesis) in a pseudo-randomized two-group study; the article reports "approximately a 22% improvement" for the Reasoning group and strong support for the Group×Time interaction model.

"The Reasoning group showed approximately a 22% improvement on this metric; by contrast, there was no evidence that performance of the Comprehension group changed between time points"
