Evidence¶

Kim McDonough 2005¶

Kim McDonough. (2005). IDENTIFYING THE IMPACT OF NEGATIVE FEEDBACK AND LEARNERS' RESPONSES ON ESL QUESTION DEVELOPMENT. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 27, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263105050047

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Analysis of treatment and test data from a study of 60 Thai EFL learners performing communicative tasks with native English speakers in four conditions, with four oral production tests over 8 weeks. The article reports that "the only significant predictor of ESL question development was the production of modified output involving developmentally advanced question forms in response to negative feedback." No effect size is printed.

"Logistic regression indicated that the only significant predictor of ESL question development was the production of modified output involving developmentally advanced question forms in response to negative feedback."
