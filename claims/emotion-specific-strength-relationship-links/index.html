Evidence¶

Goetz 2021¶

Goetz, T., Bieleke, M., Gogol, K., van Tartwijk, J., Mainhard, T., Lipnevich, A. A., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Getting along and feeling good: Reciprocal associations between student-teacher relationship quality and students' emotions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101349. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101349

q3 · i? · associational · r2

χ² difference tests in the cross-lagged panel analysis of 535 students. The article reports Model 6 "fitted our data significantly better than Model 1" and Model 8 likewise (∆χ²(5) = 11.83, p = .037), indicating the associations varied across the six discrete emotions.

"Model 6 allowed interactions of T1 positive emotional experience and dummy variables representing discrete emotions and fitted our data significantly better than Model 1, ∆χ²(5) = 15.39, p = .009."
