## Evidence

### Kang 2018

Kang, Y., & Liang, L. (2018). The Effect of EFL Teachers’ Training in Rural West China: Evidence From Phonics-oriented Program. English Language Teaching, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n3p40

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student test using Cambridge Young Learners English examination questions compared three experimental classes (trained teachers) with three ordinary classes (untrained teachers) in one Ningxia primary school. The overall comparison table reports an average score of 30.62 versus 18.74, with t = 8.200 and p = 0.000, a "Mean Deviation" of 11.88.
