Cross-sectional survey (Kaur 2011, N = 1011, Punjab, government schools) using the Psychological Hardiness Scale and School Organizational Climate Description Questionnaire; the review reports F values of 4.21, 3.74, 2.87, and 5.70 at p < .01. No effect size is printed.

"When there are open, autonomous, familiar, and controlled school climates, significantly impact students' commitment to studies, and challenge-accepting tendencies (F = 4.21, 3.74, 2.87, 5.70; p < .01), with a significant role of gender indicating that male students have shown greater results on school climate domains"
