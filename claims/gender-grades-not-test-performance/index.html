Lehikoinen 2025¶

Instructional Science, 53, 239–261. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09678-4

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate correlations in the descriptive analysis of the full sample showed gender (0 = girls, 1 = boys) correlated with teacher-rated grades but not test performance. The later correlation was marginal (p = .064).

"Gender was somewhat associated with earlier (r = –.21, p = .012) and later (r = –.15, p = .064) mathematics achievement, showing girls to have slightly higher teacher-rated grades at the end of fifth and sixth grade, but not with test performance"
