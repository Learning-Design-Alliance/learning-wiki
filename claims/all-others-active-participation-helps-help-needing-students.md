## Evidence

### Webb 1993

Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Separate multiple regression for the 27 students coded as needing help (Table 3), where whether all other students actively participated added change in R2 of .07 beyond ability (.15) and behavior (.46). Table 4 shows, for example, help-receiving students who tried to understand scoring .83 versus .00 in groups with a nonparticipating member.

> "Students needing help scored better when all other group members actively participated than when another group member did not actively participate, even after their own ability and behavior were taken into account."
