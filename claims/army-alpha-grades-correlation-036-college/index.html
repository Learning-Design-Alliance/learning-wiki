Gates 1921¶

Gates, A. I. (1921). Educational Psychology at the Chicago Meetings of Scientific Societies. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2). https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Conference report of a correlational study presented at the Chicago meetings: Army Alpha point scores were correlated with academic grades in a college sample of nearly 6000 students, yielding a printed correlation averaging "+ 0.36" (r = .36). Sub-test variations suggested college-specific tests.

"J. W. Bridges, Ohio State University, found correlations averaging -+ 0.36 between academic grades and point scores on the Army Alpha with a group of nearly 6000 college students."
