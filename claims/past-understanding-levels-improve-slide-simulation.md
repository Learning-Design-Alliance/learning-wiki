## Evidence

### Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang 2023

Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Leveraging generative artificial intelligence to simulate student learning behavior. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.19206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.19206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Task 1 of the N = 27 experiment simulating per-slide understanding levels (0 to 1) for two courses segmented into 10 and 6 slides, using gaze-based ISC for engagement and pupil size for understanding level. The authors report correlation "increases significantly from the first slide to the last slide".

> "the correlation between virtual (Type c) and real students increases significantly from the first slide to the last slide in both course birth (r = 0.30 to r = 0.89) and course star (r = -0.17 to r = 0.86)."
