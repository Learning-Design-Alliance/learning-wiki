## Evidence

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation structural model with 2000-case bootstrap in the 227-student sample; direct paths from VI, C, O, and strategic EI to GPA were all significant and the model fit excellently (CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = 0.000).

> "The outcome of the bootstrap estimation procedure confirmed that the effects of VI and O on GPA were also partially mediated by strategic EI (Table 2). As for A, both its direct and indirect pathways to GPA were nonsignificant."

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021 (2)

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct effects structural model in the same sample; all predictors except A had significant independent effects on GPA, and overlapping 95% CIs indicated "none of them showed supremacy over the others". Fit was excellent (CFI = 0.994).

> "A comparison of the 95% confidence intervals for standardized coefficients of significant predictors C (95% CI [0.206, 0.441]), VI (95% CI [0.123, 0.330]), O (95% CI [0.059, 0.328]), and strategic EI (95% CI [0.031, 0.268]) revealed significant overlaps"
