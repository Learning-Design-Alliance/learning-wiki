## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zapata-Martínez I 2025

Zapata-Martínez I, Rius-Diaz F, Lorenzo-Álvarez R, De la Peña-Fernández L. (2025). Radiation Oncology Active Learning in Undergraduate Medical Education: The Usefulness of Kahoot and TikTok. Journal of Cancer Education 40(6):847–853. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02583-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA across three participation groups among the 151 exam takers in the experimental group (106 in both activities, 44 in one, 1 in none), shown in Fig. 1, showing "a linear increase of the score with the number of activities" (p=0.019). No effect size printed.

> "there was a linear increase of the score with the number of activities (6.19, 6.88 ± 1.48 and 7.51 ± 1.20 points, when the student did not participate in any activity, in at least one or in both, respectively) (p = 0.019)"
