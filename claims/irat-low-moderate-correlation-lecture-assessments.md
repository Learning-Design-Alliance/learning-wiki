## Evidence

### Bell 2022

Bell, E. C. (2022). Individual Vs. team based readiness assurance testing in pharmacy calculations. International Journal of Instruction, 15(1), 733-746. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2022.15142a

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of score data from 69 students in a pharmacy calculations lecture course, using Spearman's rank correlations at α = .05. The study found "low-to-moderate (.2 < r < .6) correlations" between IRAT scores and lecture assessments including four exams, a quiz, and a final exam; no single effect size is printed for the overall pattern.

> "Individual readiness assurance tests exhibited low-to-moderate (.2 < r < .6) correlations with applicable lecture assessment scores (Table 1), all in statistically significant fashion."
