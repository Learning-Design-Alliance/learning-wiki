# Hot executive function (CGT risk adjustment) shows a bell-shaped developmental curve, peaking at age 14 and declining thereafter

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Risk adjustment on the Cambridge Gambling Task rises to a peak at age 14 and declines significantly by age 17. [→ Poon K 2018](#poon-k-2018)

## Evidence

### Poon K 2018

Poon K (2018) Hot and Cool Executive Functions in Adolescence: Development and Contributions to Important Developmental Outcomes. Front. Psychol. 8:2311. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.02311

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA across six age groups with IQ as covariate on CGT risk adjustment. The article reports a "bell shape" across age groups, F(5, 135) = 3.87, p < 0.01, η p2 = 0.13, with the 14-year-old group showing the highest risk-taking tendency, which declined after age 14.

> "analysis of CGT risk adjustment showed a bell shape (see Table 11, Figure 6) across the age groups, [ F(5, 135) = 3.87, p < 0.01, η p2 = 0.13]."
