Evidence¶

Qiao Weiguang 2014¶

Qiao Weiguang, Bai Yang, Lv Ruxi, Zhang Wendi, Chen Yuqing, Lei Shan, Zhi Fachao. (2014). The Effect of Virtual Endoscopy Simulator Training on Novices: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089224

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of colonoscopy RCTs; required-assistance outcome from "1 study, 16 participants". The review reports "RR 2.51, 95% CI 1.38–4.55, P = 0.002", with more assistance needed in the simulator group.

"The number of required assistance in simulator group is more than that in control group. Seventy per cent of participants in the simulator group required assistance compared to 28% of participants in the no simulator group (1 study, 16 participants, RR 2.51, 95% CI 1.38–4.55, P = 0.002; see Figure 9)."
