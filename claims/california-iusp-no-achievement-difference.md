Two studies of California's Immediate Intervention/Underperforming Schools Program, each examining more than 2,000 schools, compared participating schools to similar nonparticipating schools. "Both Bitter et al. (2005) and Goe (2006) reported that student achievement was similar in participating schools and comparison schools before the intervention and remained similar after the intervention."
