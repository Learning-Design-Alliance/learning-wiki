`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation network analysis of questionnaire contribution scores from the same 149-student survey, visualized as a network graph. The analysis found "the strongest correlation was observed between auditory and dynamic visual information (r=0.74)", suggesting lecture–video combination may play an important role in skill acquisition.

> "The strongest correlation was observed between auditory and dynamic visual information (r=0.74). Other correlations among auditory, dynamic visual, kinesthetic, and still images were moderate (r=0.52–0.56)"
