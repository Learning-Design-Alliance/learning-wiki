## Evidence

### Kumar Manish 2025

Kumar Manish, Mittal Mahima, Chavan Seema Shankarsingh, Krishnan S Vimal, Gilbert John H.V., Sanatombi Devi Elsa. (2025). Interprofessional simulation training in acute pediatrics using rapid cycle deliberate practice (ip-star). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08302-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of two independent raters scoring team performance in all 12 sessions. The article reports Kappa 0.80 and 0.76 for OSCE and ICC 0.94 (95% CI: 0.60–0.98) and 0.93 (95% CI: 0.77–0.98) for TPOT, indicating good agreement throughout the study.

> "For the pre-intervention OSCE scores, the overall Kappa was 0.80, indicating substantial agreement. Post-intervention OSCE scores showed a similar level of agreement with a Kappa of 0.76."
