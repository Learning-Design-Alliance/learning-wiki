### Claussen AH 2018

Claussen AH, Bitsko RH, Holbrook JR, Bloomfield J, Giordano K. (2018). Impact of Tourette Syndrome on School Measures in a Nationally Representative Sample. Journal of Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1097/DBP.0000000000000550

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted prevalence-ratio analysis of the pooled nationally representative survey sample comparing children currently with TS to children who never had TS. Fully adjusted prevalence ratios were 0.8 for grade repeated and 1.0 for missing more than 2 school days, both not significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "Repeating a grade (p = 0.06) and missing more than 2 days of school (p = 0.1) were more common among children with TS, but the difference did not reach statistical significance (Table 2)."
