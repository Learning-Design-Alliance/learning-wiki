`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

First-year implementation data from the qualitative evaluation of 21 schools in 9 districts. The article reports these teachers' needs, "in contrast to those more familiar with small-group instruction, thus were different in kind."

> "Data from the first year of implementation showed that in 10 schools, teachers had been facilitating whole-class instruction for several years and had a steeper curve to learn how to differentiate their instruction within small groups."
