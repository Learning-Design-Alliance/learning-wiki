## Evidence

### Kevin Zhongyang Shao 2026

Kevin Zhongyang Shao, Denise Wilson, Yale Quan, Sep Makhsous. (2026). Designing Needs- and Attention-Aware AI Learning Tools for Engineering Education: Insights from Psychological Outcomes. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey SEM of 206 frequent chatbot users among 335 engineering undergraduates. The structural model "explained 52.3% of the variance in perceived competence, 37.5% of the variance in perceived autonomy, and 9.7% of the variance in perceived relatedness."

> "Overall, the model explained 52.3% of the variance in perceived competence, 37.5% of the variance in perceived autonomy, and 9.7% of the variance in perceived relatedness."

### Kevin Zhongyang Shao 2026 (2)

Kevin Zhongyang Shao, Denise Wilson, Yale Quan, Sep Makhsous. (2026). Designing Needs- and Attention-Aware AI Learning Tools for Engineering Education: Insights from Psychological Outcomes. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the same cross-sectional survey; descriptive means show perceived relief from competence frustration (M = 3.02) and autonomy (M = 2.96) above perceived relatedness (M = 2.52). Students reported "the greatest perceived relief from competence frustration following AI chatbot use."

> "students reported the greatest perceived relief from competence frustration following AI chatbot use, more moderate improvements in perceived satisfaction of autonomy needs, and the lowest perceived improvements in satisfaction of relatedness needs."
