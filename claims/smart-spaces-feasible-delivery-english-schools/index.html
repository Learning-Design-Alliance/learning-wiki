O'Hare et al. 2017¶

Dr Liam O'Hare, Dr Patrick Stark, Professor Carol McGuinness, Dr Andy Biggart, Professor Allen Thurston. (2017). Spaced Learning: The Design, Feasibility and Optimisation of SMART Spaces — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581430

q2 · i? · design · r3

Feasibility evidence from the qualitative feasibility study in four schools and the optimisation study in 12 schools, including post-test implementation survey data. The report concludes the programme "was delivered successfully and was acceptable to both teachers and pupils".

"Teachers generally reported that they delivered SMART Spaces lessons as prescribed and did not make major alterations."
