## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A repeated measures ANOVA showed significant within-subject differences across the five tests (F = 174.1, P = 0.001) and no between-subjects differences among Groups 3, 4, and 5 (F = 0.006, P = 0.994). [→ Kooloos Jan G.M. 2020](#kooloos-jan-gm-2020)

## Evidence

### Kooloos Jan G.M. 2020

Kooloos Jan G.M., Bergman Esther M., Scheffers Marieke A.G.P., Schepens-Franke Annelieke N., Vorstenbosch Marc A.T.M. (2020). The Effect of Passive and Active Education Methods Applied in Repetition Activities on the Retention of Anatomical Knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1924

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the three repetition-activity groups across the five assessments in the curriculum study. The between-subjects test "showed no differences among the scores of Group 3, 4, and 5 (df = 2; F = 0.006; P = 0.994)," indicating the groups did not differ on any of the five tests.

> "Repeated measures ANOVA for within‐subject effects on Groups 3, 4, and 5 showed a significant difference (df = 1; F = 174.1; P = 0.001), which means that the students' scores differed on all five tests."
