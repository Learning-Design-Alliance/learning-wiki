### Kim Hyeon Ji MD 2024

Kim Hyeon Ji MD, Lee Hee Kyeong BS, Jang Ji Yeon MD, Lee Kyong-No MD, Suh Dong Hoon PhD, Kong Hyoun-Joong PhD, Lee Seung-Hee PhD, Park Jee Yoon PhD. (2024). Immersive virtual reality simulation training for cesarean section: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000843

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-intervention questionnaire in the same RCT (VR n=53, control n=52) rated confidence 0–10. Table 3 medians: managing PROM 7 vs 6 (P=0.005), performing CS 7 vs 5 (P=0.007), indications 7 vs 6 (P=0.026), complications 7 vs 6 (P=0.025). No effect size printed.

> "The VR group demonstrated significantly higher median scores on the confidence questionnaire compared to the control group in all four aspects, including confidence about managing patient with PROM, performing CS as an operator, and knowledge on indications and complications of CS."
