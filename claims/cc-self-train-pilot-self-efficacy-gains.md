## Evidence

### Zain Naboulsi 2026

Zain Naboulsi. (2026). Agentic Education: Using Claude Code to Teach Claude Code. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17460 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.17460](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.17460)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Within-subjects pilot (n=27 professional software engineers from a single client organization) with pre- and post-training self-assessments on a 10-item 5-point Likert questionnaire, analyzed with paired t-tests. The article reports "All 10 items showed statistically significant gains (p <0.001)" with Cohen's d range 0.89–2.79.

> "All 10 items showed statistically significant gains (p <0.001), with large effect sizes across the board (Cohen'sdrange: 0.89–2.79)."

### Zain Naboulsi 2026 (2)

Zain Naboulsi. (2026). Agentic Education: Using Claude Code to Teach Claude Code. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17460

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Non-parametric confirmation of the overall pre/post gain in the same pilot. The article reports "a Wilcoxon signed-rank test (W= 0.0,p <0.00001)" with 26 of 27 participants improving; no effect size is printed for this test.

> "The overall gain was confirmed by a Wilcoxon signed-rank test (W= 0.0,p <0.00001), indicating that 26 of 27 participants improved (one advanced user scored at ceiling on both administrations)."
