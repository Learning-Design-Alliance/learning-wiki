Nick Gerrit Hasche 2026¶

Nick Gerrit Hasche, Charlotte Dignath, Sumeja Mustafi, Mareike Kunter. (2026). An International Review of Assessment Tools for Teachers' Professional Competence in Teaching Autistic Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10176-x

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's coding of aspect labels across the 52 studies found, for example, that 'awareness' was usually used for knowledge but by one study for motivational aspects; the authors conclude "different terms were often used for the same aspects and vice versa."

"As a result, different terms were often used for the same aspects and vice versa."

Nick Gerrit Hasche 2026 (2)¶

Nick Gerrit Hasche, Charlotte Dignath, Sumeja Mustafi, Mareike Kunter. (2026). An International Review of Assessment Tools for Teachers' Professional Competence in Teaching Autistic Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10176-x

q3 · i? · review · r?

As part of RQ1, the review coded disciplinary origin (first author's affiliation) and definition source (e.g., DSM-IV, DSM-5, CDC) for each instrument and reports it "found no significant correlations" between discipline and definition or terminology choice.

"We examined whether the choice of definition and terminology used depended on the discipline but found no significant correlations."
