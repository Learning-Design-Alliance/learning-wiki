## Evidence

### Netzer R. 2025

Netzer R., Levi N., Ganchrow K., Naan Y., Elboim-Gabyzon M. (2025). Clinical Competency in Managing Malnutrition–Sarcopenia Syndrome Among Physical Therapists: A Cross-Sectional Survey. Nutrients 17(2), 281. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17020281

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results from the 16-item knowledge test administered in the nationwide survey of 337 PTs; scores were calculated by summing correct responses per domain. The article reports the total as "69.6 ± 13.3 out of 100, reflecting moderate knowledge". No effect size printed.

> "The overall MSS knowledge test score averaged 69.6 ± 13.3 out of 100, reflecting moderate knowledge. Participants scored 66.8 ± 19.7 on sarcopenia-related questions and 71.4 ± 14.7 on malnutrition-related."

### Netzer R. 2025 (2)

Netzer R., Levi N., Ganchrow K., Naan Y., Elboim-Gabyzon M. (2025). Clinical Competency in Managing Malnutrition–Sarcopenia Syndrome Among Physical Therapists: A Cross-Sectional Survey. Nutrients 17(2), 281. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17020281

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Five multiple-choice skill questions scored out of 100 in the same survey. The article reports "30.7 ± 19.9 out of 100, indicating an overall generally low proficiency", with 6.2% familiar with sarcopenia diagnostic methods and 22.9% identifying malnutrition risk indicators. No effect size printed.

> "The average skills score was 30.7 ± 19.9 out of 100, indicating an overall generally low proficiency in sarcopenia and malnutrition screening and treatment skills. Screening skills were notably deficient: only 6.2% were familiar with clinical diagnostic methods for sarcopenia, and 22.9% could identify key indicators of malnutrition risk."
