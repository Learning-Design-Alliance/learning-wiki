`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data from the phenomenological study showed teachers valued ready-to-use materials. One teacher noted completing summer with "a lot of practical stuff that I could actually use in my classroom pretty much right away." The six teachers discussing practicality were a mix of career stages.

> "Another core feature of the program pointed out by teachers was the practicality of the instructional materials. This means that teachers could implement the activities in their classes with few or no modifications."
