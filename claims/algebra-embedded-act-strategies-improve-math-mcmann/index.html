Evidence¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Ten-week randomized trial in second-year algebra sections at a Michigan high school; the WWC row reports intervention mean 26.89 (SD 8.24) versus comparison 24.01 (SD 8.69), mean difference 2.88, effect size 0.34, improvement index +13, p .02 — a statistically significant positive effect.

"Practice ACT mathematics exam High school students 196 26.89 (8.24) 24.01 (8.69) 2.88 0.34 +13 .02"

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016 (2)¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Supplemental (non-rating) subgroup findings from the same trial: male students (n=96) show effect size 0.35, +14, p .09, and the table reports female students (n=100) at effect size 0.38, +15, p .06 — neither subgroup contrast statistically significant at the printed p-values.
