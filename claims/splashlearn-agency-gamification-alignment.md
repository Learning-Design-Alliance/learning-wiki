Case study 2: the authors' rapid evidence review and logic-model construction for SplashLearn Math, an EdTech platform of math and reading games for elementary-aged learners. The review identified alignment with agency (placement-based game recommendations students choose from) and gamification, plus ZPD-based scaffolding that adjusts content by performance. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Our review and construction of a logic model for SplashLearn Math revealed that the product aligns with two key learning principles: supporting student agency and using gamification."
