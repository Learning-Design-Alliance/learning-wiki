### Ðurić Saša 2021

Ðurić Saša, Bogataj Špela, Zovko Vinko, Sember Vedrana. (2021). Associations Between Physical Fitness, Objectively Measured Physical Activity and Academic Performance. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.778837

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

In the same Spearman analysis of 20 girls, correlations between academic measures and PA dropped relative to fitness correlations: "r = 0.45" for combined daily PA and r = 0.28 for weekend PA. The authors speculate some girls may have removed the accelerometer during training sessions.

> "Nevertheless, correlations surprisingly decrease for the combined daily PA (r = 0.45), especially the weekend daily PA (r = 0.28)."
