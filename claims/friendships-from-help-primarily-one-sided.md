## Evidence

### van Rijsewijk 2019

van Rijsewijk, L. G. M., Snijders, T. A. B., Dijkstra, J. K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2019). The Interplay Between Adolescents' Friendships and the Exchange of Help: A Longitudinal Multiplex Social Network Study. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive configuration analysis (Tables 3 and 4) tracking how wave-1 and wave-2 friendship-help configurations resulted in friendship or help at the next wave. The article reports "Rarely did mutual friendships arise from help only (6–10%; exception at wave 3: 20%)" and that one-sided friendships emerged from one-sided help about 30% and mutual help about 40% of cases.
