## Evidence

### XinYue Ma 2025

XinYue Ma, JingShi Huang. (2025). Alignment Between Classroom Education and Clinical Practice of Root Canal Treatment Among Dental Practitioners in China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65534

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 83 dental practitioners in Eastern Coastal China, analyzed with ANOVA and nonparametric tests at P<.05; in the x-ray imaging stage, bachelor's and master's holders' congruence was "notably superior to that of junior college graduates".

> "in the x-ray imaging and observation stage, the congruence between bachelor’s and master’s degree holders was notably superior to that of junior college graduates (P<.05)."

### XinYue Ma 2025 (2)

XinYue Ma, JingShi Huang. (2025). Alignment Between Classroom Education and Clinical Practice of Root Canal Treatment Among Dental Practitioners in China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65534

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results from the same survey of 83 practitioners: junior college degree holders accounted for "50% (11/22) of the total who expressed negative evaluations of school teaching", selecting the three negative Likert options.

> "Participants with junior college degrees account for 50% (11/22) of the total who expressed negative evaluations of school teaching (selecting “completely unsatisfied,” “most cannot satisfy,” and “slightly unsatisfied” options)."
