### Rachele Toniolo 2026

Rachele Toniolo, Anita Zanella, Andrea Cottinelli, Giovanni Liuzzi, Sara Ricciardi, Massimo Grassi, Stefano Delle Monache. (2026). AMACA: Astronomy education with a Multi-sensory, Accessible, and Circular Approach. International Journal of Science Education, Part B: Communication and Public Engagement. https://doi.org/10.1080/21548455.2025.2594048

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory word-cloud analysis of answers from 200 HS students aged 16-18 collected 2021-2024, classified into five semantic groups via thematic analysis. At the start the main expectation was "knowledge" (49.5%); the ranking was "completely flipped" at the end as quoted.

> "That ranking is completely flipped at the end of the project, with "relationships" (34.1%) as the main output, together with "emotions/feelings" (29.4%) and "knowledge" (21.8%)."
