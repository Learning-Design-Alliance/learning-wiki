Median-split visualization (Fig. 4b) showed in-the-moment anxiety most elevated in high math anxiety/low HF-HRV group, while underperformance in high math anxiety did not depend on HF-HRV. The total effect remained highly significant for high HF-HRV individuals.

> "we also found that math-anxious people with high HF-HRV nevertheless underperformed in math to the same degree as those with low HF-HRV (Fig. 4c), despite feeling significantly less anxious (Figs. 3c, 4b)."
