Definitional/design-rationale statement (§4.1, type (d)) about the platform choice. The author argues source code offers flexibility that "even the most ﬂexible GUI interface, cannot come close to," while SciPy allows complex problems "with a minimum of programming overhead."

> "The software in this course is at neither of these two extremes. It is recognized that the majority of physics and engineering majors have not had a rigorous numerical methods course, but at the same time do typically have a fair degree of programming proﬁciency."
