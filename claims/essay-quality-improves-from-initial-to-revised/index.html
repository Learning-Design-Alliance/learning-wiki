Evidence¶

Farrokhnia 2026¶

Farrokhnia, M., Latifi, S., Papadopoulos, P. M., Hogenkamp, L., Gijlers, H., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2026). Generative AI offers more, but students revise less: comparing the effects of teacher and AI feedback on student essay revisions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00579-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA across the three conditions (total N = 70) showed a significant main effect of time from initial to revised drafts with partial ηp2 = .79, "indicating a large effect size"; descriptive means rose in every condition.

"This was further supported by a repeated measures ANOVA, which revealed a significant main effect of time, F(1, 67) = 255.82, p < .001, partial ηp2 = .79, indicating a large effect size."
