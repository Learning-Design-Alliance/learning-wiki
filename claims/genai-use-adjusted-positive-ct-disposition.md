### Shojaei SA 2026

Shojaei SA, Armosh F, Lihe R, Hamacha R and Labib J (2026) Helpful or harmful? generative AI dependence, self-reported critical thinking disposition, and self-perceived employability among business students. Front. Educ. 11:1885990. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885990

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM analysis of cross-sectional survey data from 412 undergraduate business students in Oman, with bootstrap resampling of 5,000. The adjusted path was significant, but the article notes the "zero-order correlation between GenAI use and self-reported critical thinking disposition was very small (r = 0.050)".

> "GenAI use was positively associated with self- reported critical thinking disposition in the adjusted structural model (β = 0.185, p < 0.001; H1)"
