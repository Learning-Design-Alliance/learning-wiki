### Guill Karin 2019

Guill Karin, Lüdtke Oliver, Köller Olaf. (2019). Assessing the instructional quality of private tutoring and its effects on student outcomes: Analyses from the German National Educational Panel Study. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12281

Interaction term in the same NEPS regression models (N = 11,358); the printed standardized interaction coefficient was β = −0.062 for mathematics and β = −0.034 for German, both significant, indicating "tutored students with lower midterm grades had higher grades at the end of the school year".
