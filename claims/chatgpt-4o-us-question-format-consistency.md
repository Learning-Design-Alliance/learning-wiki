Analysis of ChatGPT-4o's performance across the three U.S. Dental Decks question formats found the chi-squared test showed no significant differences among question types (χ² = 0.686, p = 0.567), indicating consistent performance regardless of format.

> "In contrast, ChatGPT-4o's performance across three types of questions in the English set was relatively stable (Table 3): 90% accuracy on both plain-text and case-box questions, and 100% accuracy on image-based questions."
