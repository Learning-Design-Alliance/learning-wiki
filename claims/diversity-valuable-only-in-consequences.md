## Evidence

### Mathieu Bouville 2006

Mathieu Bouville. (2006). Should there be more women in science and engineering?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611089](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611089)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual argument (Section II.D) criticizing articles that call for increasing diversity without explaining why. The author argues that without a stated reason, no criterion of relevance for diversity dimensions can exist, and that "Diversity is valuable only in its consequences".

> "Clearly, the idea that diversity is good in and for it self is not tenable. Diversity is valuable only in its consequences and the actuality of these consequences must be explicitly shown."
