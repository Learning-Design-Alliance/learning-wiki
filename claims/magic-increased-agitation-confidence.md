# Students' confidence in managing and communicating with agitated patients rose from pre-test to post-test after MAGIC in two single-group pre-post measures

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-rated confidence in managing agitated patients rose from pre-test to post-test after MAGIC (mean 3.82 vs 2.56). [→ Tay GWN 2025](#tay-gwn-2025)
`q2 i?` Post-MAGIC confidence in communicating with an agitated person was significantly higher (mean 3.79 vs 2.55). [→ Tay GWN 2025 (2)](#tay-gwn-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Tay GWN 2025

Tay GWN, Tong MM, Yap J, Mak HK, Goh SYS, Ho CSH. (2025). Virtual reality for experiential learning: enhancing agitation management skills, confidence, and empathy in healthcare students. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2542809

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre/post confidence ratings on a 7-point Likert scale in the 152-participant quasi-experiment; the article reports "t = −11.12, p < 0.001". No effect size printed.

> "Participants generally reported feeling more confident in managing patients who are agitated after experiencing MAGIC (mean = 3.82, SD = 1.24), with significantly higher confidence levels compared to their pre-MAGIC ratings (mean = 2.56, SD = 1.24, t = −11.12, p < 0.001)."

### Tay GWN 2025 (2)

Tay GWN, Tong MM, Yap J, Mak HK, Goh SYS, Ho CSH. (2025). Virtual reality for experiential learning: enhancing agitation management skills, confidence, and empathy in healthcare students. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2542809

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second confidence measure (communication with an agitated person) in the same quasi-experiment, reported separately with "t = −11.37, p < 0.001". No effect size printed.

> "Similarly, they also reported increased confidence in communicating with an agitated person post-MAGIC (mean = 3.79, SD = 1.03), showing significantly higher confidence levels than their pre-MAGIC ratings (mean = 2.55, SD = 1.23, t = −11.37, p < 0.001)."
