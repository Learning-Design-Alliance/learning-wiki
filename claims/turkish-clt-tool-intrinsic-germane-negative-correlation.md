## Evidence

### Yıldız Çelik Hilal 2025

Yıldız Çelik Hilal, Uğur Esra. (2025). Turkish validity and reliability study of the simulation self-report cognitive load measurement tool 2.0. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07504-0

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the 101 students' subdimension scores found a statistically significant negative intrinsic–germane correlation (r = − 0.192, p = 0.001), which the Discussion calls an unexpected finding.

> "A statistically significant negative correlation (r = − 0.192, p = 0.001) was found between the intrinsic and germane subdimensions, suggesting that these constructs may represent distinct aspects of cognitive load."

### Yıldız Çelik Hilal 2025 (2)

Yıldız Çelik Hilal, Uğur Esra. (2025). Turkish validity and reliability study of the simulation self-report cognitive load measurement tool 2.0. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07504-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same Pearson correlation analysis found no statistically significant extraneous–germane relationship (p > 0.05); the article suggests these subdimensions 'measure distinct aspects of cognitive load', and equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant relationship was detected between the scores for the extraneous and germane subdimensions (p > 0.05), which may indicate that these subdimensions measure distinct aspects of cognitive load."

### Yıldız Çelik Hilal 2025 (3)

Yıldız Çelik Hilal, Uğur Esra. (2025). Turkish validity and reliability study of the simulation self-report cognitive load measurement tool 2.0. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07504-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations with the total score were positive and significant for the extraneous (r = 0.562) and germane (r = 0.520) subdimensions, which the article says 'further support the internal structure' of the tool.

> "the positive relationships between the scores for the extraneous subdimension and the total score (r = 0.562; p < 0.01) and between the scores for the germane subdimension and the total score (r = 0.520; p < 0.01) further support the internal structure"
