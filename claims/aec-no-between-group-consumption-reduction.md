# The brief Alcohol Expectancy Challenge produced no significant between-group reductions in alcohol consumption at 1, 2 or 4 months post-intervention

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Mann Whitney tests found no significant differences in alcohol consumption between intervention and control groups at any follow-up point, including 4 months (r = 0.06). [→ Pamela Portelli 2018](#pamela-portelli-2018)

## Evidence

### Pamela Portelli 2018

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Mann Whitney comparisons of TLFB-measured drinks in the past 30 days in the cluster RCT. No significant differences at 1 month (p = 0.18, r = 0.09), 2 months (p = 0.41, r = 0.02) or 4 months; the hypothesis of significant consumption reductions "was not supported".

> "Finally, no significant differences in alcohol consumption were observed at 4 month post-intervention period between the intervention ( Mdn = 5.5 ) and the control group ( Mdn = 6.0), U= 1313, p = 0.26, r = 0.06."
