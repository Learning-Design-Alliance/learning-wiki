Pearson correlations in the 21-student pilot sample showed specific IC associated with prior 3D experience (r=0.57) and self-rated technical competence (r=0.46); the article separately reports that "no relevant correlations were found between specific IC and technology enthusiasm or spatial ability."

> "Moreover, specific IC correlated moderately to strongly with prior 3D experience (r=0.57; P=.007) and self-rated technical competence (r=0.46; P=.04)."
