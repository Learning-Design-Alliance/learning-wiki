## Evidence

### Mostafavi Marzieh 2025

Mostafavi Marzieh, Shokrpour Nasrin, Afraz Shahram. (2025). Beliefs, practices, and awareness of written corrective feedback among EFL teachers: a mixed-methods study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03861-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of 1500 corrected student assignments collected from 100 Iranian EFL university teachers, coded with a modified version of Lee's framework. The corpus analysis found "1065 direct (71%) and 435 indirect (29%)" instances and 16% comprehensive versus 84% selective types; the article reports the numbers descriptively with no effect size.

> "there were 1065 direct (71%) and 435 indirect (29%) types; on the other hand, as to subcategories of the strategy-2 type, there were 240 comprehensive (16%) and 1260 (84%) selective types"
