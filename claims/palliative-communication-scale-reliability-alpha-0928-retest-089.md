### Qiu Lanying 2025

Qiu Lanying, Yang Xi, Ye Yingjun, Liang Guanmian, Hou Jianwen, Han Shuai, Wen Xiaoxue, Shen Xiaoying, Guo Yitong, Li Haoyang, Fan Dan, Xie Shuping, Fu Xiaowei. (2025). World Journal of Surgical Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12957-025-04002-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis in the validation sample of 444 healthcare staff. Internal consistency was assessed with Cronbach's α and Spearman-Brown coefficients; "the reliability coefficients ranged from 0.817 to 0.862" across dimensions. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The overall Cronbach's α coefficient was 0.928, indicating significant homogeneity among the indicators. For the individual dimensions, the reliability coefficients ranged from 0.817 to 0.862"

### Qiu Lanying 2025 (2)

Qiu Lanying, Yang Xi, Ye Yingjun, Liang Guanmian, Hou Jianwen, Han Shuai, Wen Xiaoxue, Shen Xiaoying, Guo Yitong, Li Haoyang, Fan Dan, Xie Shuping, Fu Xiaowei. (2025). Construction and verification of reliability and validity of the communication skill assessment scale in cancer palliative care for healthcare staff. World Journal of Surgical Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12957-025-04002-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-retest reliability was assessed with a 2-week interval by randomly selecting 20 participants from the 444 healthcare staff for a second scale administration. Dimension test-retest coefficients ranged from 0.69 to 0.84.

> "the reliability coefficient for the total scale at two different time points was 0.89, highlighting the stability of the measurement results"
