## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors argue that extensive MC-question use helps students feel more confident of their knowledge, a paradox, because the mental check-off ends once an option is chosen. [→ Cristian Raduta 2005](#cristian-raduta-2005)

Cristian Raduta, Gordon Aubrecht. (2005). A comparison between the styles Romanian and American students use to approach an E&M physics problem. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503123](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503123)

Theoretical argument in the discussion of learning-system differences; the article offers no data of its own for this assertion, only reasoning about how MC exams work. The authors add that if wisely combined and designed, both objective and essay tests can be very useful.
