Evidence¶

Keller 2022¶

Keller, L., Cortina, K. S., Müller, K., & Miller, K. F. (2022). Noticing and weighing alternatives in the reflection of regular classroom teaching: Evidence of expertise using mobile eye-tracking. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09570-5

q3 · i? · associational · r1

T-test comparison of 37 teachers (19 novices, 18 experts) reflecting on their own MET videos found no significant difference in noticed events: "M Diff = −0.56, 95% CI [–14.18, 13.06], p = .933". This contradicted Hypothesis 1.

"expert and novice teachers did not differ significantly in the number of noticed classroom events (M Diff = −0.56, 95% CI [–14.18, 13.06], p = .933; Hypothesis 1)"
