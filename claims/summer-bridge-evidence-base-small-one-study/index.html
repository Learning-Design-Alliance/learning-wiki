Summer Bridge Programs 2016¶

Summer Bridge Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Supporting Postsecondary Success. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/661

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review conducted using the WWC Procedures and Standards Handbook, version 3.0. The review identified 31 eligible studies and an additional 106 studies that did not meet eligibility criteria; only the Murphy et al. quasi-experiment met group design standards, "with reservations".

"The WWC reviewed the 31 eligible studies against group design standards. One study (Murphy, Gaughan, Hume, & Moore, 2010) used a quasi-experimental design that meets WWC group design standards with reservations."
