Survey analysis using the validated Mayo High Performance Teamwork Scale (16 questions) as a sum score, from the same single-center pre/post survey study. The article reports the descriptive averages of 71% and 95% with no statistical test.

> "The 16 focused teamwork questions demonstrated a positive shift in teamwork ability; that is, in the presurvey participants had a 71% average response on performing each question consistently, postsurvey showed an increase of the average to 95% consistently"
