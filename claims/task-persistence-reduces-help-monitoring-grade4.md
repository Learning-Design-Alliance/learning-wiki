# Children's Grade 3 task persistence negatively predicts mothers' Grade 4 help and monitoring, but not earlier

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Task persistence did not predict mothers' help or monitoring from Grade 2 to 3, but from Grade 3 to 4 more persistence predicted less maternal help and monitoring. [→ Viljaranta 2017](#viljaranta-2017)

## Evidence

### Viljaranta 2017

Viljaranta, J., Silinskas, G., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Hirvonen, R., Pakarinen, E., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2017). Maternal Homework Assistance and Children’s Task-Persistent Behavior in Elementary School. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Maternal+Homework+Assistance+and+Children%E2%80%99s+Task-Persistent+Behavior+in+Elementary+School

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the cross-lagged model of 365 triads, "from grade 3 to grade 4 task avoidance was a negative predictor of both help and monitoring"; no such paths were significant from Grade 2 to 3. No standardized coefficients are printed.

> "from grade 3 to grade 4 task avoidance was a negative predictor of both help and monitoring: The more task persistence teachers reported children to exhibit in grade 3, the less mothers reported helping or monitoring them in grade 4."
