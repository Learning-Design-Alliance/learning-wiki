Rattanavich 2015¶

q2 · i? · design · r1

Second pilot program (1987) conducted in two poor schools in Surin province, northeastern Thailand, where students had difficulty communicating in Thai because they spoke Laotien and Suay. The article reports "Significant results in Thai language development in the two pilot schools" citing Rattanavich & Walker (1987); no effect size or sample size is printed.

"Significant results in Thai language development in the two pilot schools were found as well as other personal developments, such as creativity, self-confidence, and responsibility (Rattanavich & Walker, 1987)."
