# Music training RCTs show improved verbal working memory (backward digit span), but not visuospatial or forward-span tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A 6-week harmonica training RCT showed a significant group × time interaction (p = 0.015) in digit span backward performance. [→ Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C 2022](#rodriguez-gomez-da-and-talero-gutierrez-c-2022)
`q2 i?` Neither RCT observed improved performance in visuospatial WM or forward digit/word tasks. [→ Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C 2022 (2)](#rodriguez-gomez-da-and-talero-gutierrez-c-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C 2022

Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C (2022) Eﬀects of music training in executive function performance in children: A systematic review. Front. Psychol. 13:968144. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.968144

`q2 · i?`

RCT allocating 40 Japanese school-age children to 6-week harmonica lessons (n = 20) or control (n = 20). The review reports "a signiﬁcant group × time interaction ( p = 0.015) in the digit span backward (DSB) performance." Nie et al. (2022) similarly found DSB improvement (p < 0.001) after a year-long Kodály intervention.

> "the results showed a signiﬁcant group × time interaction ( p = 0.015) in the digit span backward (DSB) performance, suggesting an improved verbal working memory with harmonica training."

### Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C 2022 (2)

Rodriguez-Gomez DA and Talero-Gutiérrez C (2022) Eﬀects of music training in executive function performance in children: A systematic review. Front. Psychol. 13:968144. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.968144

`q2 · i?`

The review states that the Guo et al. (2018) and Nie et al. (2022) RCTs showed backward-span gains only: "Neither of these RCTs observed improved performance in visuospatial WM or forward digit/word tasks."

> "Neither of these RCTs observed improved performance in visuospatial WM or forward digit/word tasks."
