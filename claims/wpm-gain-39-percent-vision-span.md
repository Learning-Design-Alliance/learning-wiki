## Evidence

### Kana'an et al. 2014

Basim Hamdan Ibrahim Kana'an, Salahud Din Abdul Rab & Ahlullah Siddiqui. (2014). The Effect of Expansion of Vision Span on Reading Speed: A Case Study of EFL Major Students at King Khalid University. English Language Teaching, 7(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive reading-speed scores in words per minute reported in Table 9 and Figure 3 for both groups before and after training. The discussion states the test group's gain "means a total increase of 39 percent with no loss in comprehension"; these are descriptive means, not test statistics.

> "Table 9 shows that the reading speed of the control group is 76.6 wpm in the pre-training and 74.9 wpm in the post-training tests. For the test group, on the other hand, the average reading speed of students is 73.6 wpm and 102.3 wpm in the pre-test and post-test, respectively."
