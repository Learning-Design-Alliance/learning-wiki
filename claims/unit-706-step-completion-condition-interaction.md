## Evidence

### Qianru Lyu 2026

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory mixed-effects model predicting the number of completed problem steps in the crossover study; the main effect of condition was not significant (β = −2.73, p = .699), but Unit 7.06 showed a significant advantage for the Redesigned Tutor. Logged-in time differences were most pronounced in this unit (28% increase vs. 16% in Unit 7.05, a 4% decrease in Unit 1 Equations, and 18% in Unit 2 Equations).

> "exploratory analyses revealed a significant condition-by-unit interaction such that in Unit 7.06 (Graphs and Equations), where students in the Redesigned Tutor completed more steps than those in the Original Tutor (β= 37.76,p=.001) (but not in other units)."
