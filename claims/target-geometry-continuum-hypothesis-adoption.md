### Wei Ding 2026

Wei Ding, Anjuli Niyogi, Jordan A. Taylor & Jonathan S. Tsay. (2026). Hypothesis Testing Governs Strategic Motor Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00428-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined cross-experiment analysis (N = 560) coding target geometry along a continuum (2 outer → 8 outer → 8 inner → 2 inner) in mixed-effects regressions; exploratory von Mises weights for non-solution modes showed the same graded pattern, reversed at the probe target.

> "at the training targets,  early adaptation increased continuously from 2 -outer to 8 -outer to 8 -inner to 2 -inner—precisely mirroring the proportion of outer (constrained) versus inner (unconstrained) targets (Figure 4a; mixed-effects regression: β = 6.0, p = .005)."
