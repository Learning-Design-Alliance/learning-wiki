`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous Google Forms questionnaire sent to 600 dentists during the COVID-19 lockdown (March–May 2020); 304 responded. The abstract reports that "the mean fraction of correct responses was 0.524" and that therapeutic management issues were not well understood.

> "The response rate was 50.6% and the mean fraction of correct responses was 0.524. Issues related to the therapeutic management of avulsed teeth were shown to be not well understood by the respondents."
