## Evidence

### Schelfhout Stijn 2019

Schelfhout Stijn, Wille Bart, Fonteyne Lot, Roels Elisabeth, De Fruyt Filip, Duyck Wouter. (2019). The effects of vocational interest on study results: Student person – environment fit and program interest diversity. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214618

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program-level regression of interest diversity (AD COR) on average autonomous and controlled motivation across the 39 open-access programs, reported in the Results section for research question 1. The omnibus test was "significant, F(2, 36) = 6.86, p = .003" with "an explained variance of 28%"; graphical inspection suggested effects were strongest at extreme motivation levels.

> "The omnibus test proved to be significant, F(2, 36) = 6.86, p = .003, with an explained variance of 28%. Standardized regression coefficients (β1 = -0.33, β2 = 0.28, for autonomous and controlled motivation respectively)"
