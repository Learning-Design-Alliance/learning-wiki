## Evidence

### Zhang Dandan 2024

Zhang Dandan, Chen Yong-Jun, Cui Tianxin, Zhang Jianzhong, Chen Si-Ying, Zhang Yin-Ping. (2024). Competence and Training Needs in Infectious Disease Emergency Response Among Chinese Nurses: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Public Health and Surveillance. https://doi.org/10.2196/62887

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 2055-nurse survey (adjusted R2=0.087, F12,2042=17.322, P<.001). Drill frequency dummies showed "7.344 points higher" for 1-4 drills and 14.432 points higher for ≥10 drills versus zero drills.

> "Compared with nurses who did not participate in drills, those who participated in 1‐4 drills in the past 5 years scored 7.344 points higher (B=7.344, P<.001). Similarly, those who participated in 5‐9 drills scored 11.639 points higher (B=11.639, P<.001)"
