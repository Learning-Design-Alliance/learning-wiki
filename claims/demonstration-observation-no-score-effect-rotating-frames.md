## Evidence

### S. Küchemann 2020

S. Küchemann, P. Klein, H. Fouckhardt, S. Gröber, and J. Kuhn. (2020). Improving students' understanding of rotating frames of reference using videos from different perspectives. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010112)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

POE test-score analysis of 21 freshman physics majors in a Predict-Observe-Explain study with a rotating-disc demonstration. The article reports the demonstration-only intervention showed "no signiﬁcant eﬀect on the score ( p = 0.27)" and that 80% could not report the trajectory after observing twice.

> "The demonstration of the ex- periment which is the only intervention between P redict and Observe has no signiﬁcant eﬀect on the score ( p = 0.27), which means that 80 % of the students are unable to report the trajectory of a sphere in two coordinate systems after observing the experiment twice."
