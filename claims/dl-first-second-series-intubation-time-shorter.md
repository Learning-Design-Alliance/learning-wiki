## Evidence

### Gu Minglu MM 2019

Gu Minglu MM, Lian Ming MM, Gong Chao MM, Chen Lianhua MD, PhD, LI Shitong MD, PhD, Lu. Min. (2019). The teaching order of using direct laryngoscopy first may improve the learning outcome of endotracheal incubation: A preliminary, randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(21): e15624. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000015624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 20 trainees (10 per group), each performing 10 intubations with DL then 10 with VL, or the reverse. The intragroup comparison of the two series shows the DL-first group improved significantly in its second series, quoting "the mean intubation time of the second series was significantly shorter (P = .006)"; the VL-first group did not (P = .173).

> "In the DL-first group, the mean intubation time of the second series was significantly shorter (P = .006), while in the VL-first group there was no significant difference between the first and second series (P = .173)."
