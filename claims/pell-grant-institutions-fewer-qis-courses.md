## Evidence

### Josephine C. Meyer 2024

Josephine C. Meyer, Gina Passante, and Bethany Wilcox. (2024). Disparities in access to US quantum information education. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial regression across 456 US institutions (Table II) found Pell grant share negatively associated with active QIS course counts; high-Pell institutions "offer significantly fewer QIS courses", which the authors read as reduced access for low-income students.

> "Institutions with a high percentage of undergraduates receiving Pell grants offer significantly fewer QIS courses, indicating that low-income students may have fewer opportunities to access QIS coursework."
