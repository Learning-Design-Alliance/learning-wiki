Romain Puech 2026¶

Romain Puech, Dewang Kumar Agarwal, Antonio Santamaría Escobar, Dimitris Bertsimas. (2026). Bespoke: Generating MOOC-Quality Industry-Personalized Lecture Videos at Scale. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26540

q2 · i? · design · r2

Between-group comparisons within the 92-video expert rating corpus, reported with Mann–Whitney U tests and confidence intervals. The article reports the medium-versus-long difference of 0.17 with p = 0.60, and separately the held-out-versus-development difference of −0.15 (95% CI[−0.48, 0.18]).

"Medium and long videos are similarly close (3.36 vs. 3.53; Mann–Whitney𝑈 𝑝= 0.60; difference0.17, 95% CI[−0.17, 0.50]; Table 2)."

Romain Puech 2026 (2)¶

Romain Puech, Dewang Kumar Agarwal, Antonio Santamaría Escobar, Dimitris Bertsimas. (2026). Bespoke: Generating MOOC-Quality Industry-Personalized Lecture Videos at Scale. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26540

q2 · i? · design · r2

Generalizability check comparing the 21 lectures unused while developing the pipeline against the 10 development lectures, using the same expert ratings. The article reports G = 3.36 versus 3.51, a difference of −0.15, and concludes overall quality does not drop sharply on new lectures.

"Videos from the 21 held-out lectures score 𝐺= 3.36, versus 3.51on the 10 development lectures (difference −0.15, 95% CI [−0.48, 0.18])."
