## Evidence

### Graafland Maurits 2017

Graafland Maurits, Bemelman Willem A., Schijven Marlies P. (2017). Game-based training improves the surgeon's situational awareness in the operation room: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5456-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 24 surgical residents (12 per group) completing a basic laparoscopy course and assessed on a live anesthetized pig model with three standardized equipment malfunction scenarios. The game group "solved a median of 59%" versus 33%, p = 0.03 (Mann–Whitney U).

> "The participants in the game-enhanced curriculum group solved a median of 59% (interquartile range (IQR) 33–67%) of the problems presented to them, compared to 33% (8–33%) in the regular curriculum group (Fig. 4, p = 0.03)."
