`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective multicenter study of eight trainees across eight Italian tertiary IBD centers; 99 independent examinations each. Results section reports basic parameter group agreement rose from "κ = 0.792 (95% CI: 0.743-0.842)" to "κ = 0.842 (95% CI: 0.795-0.890)", a 6.3% relative improvement.

> "For basic parameters, overall agreement increased progressively from κ = 0.792 (95% CI: 0.743-0.842) in Period 1 to κ = 0.842 (95% CI: 0.795-0.890) in Period 3, representing a 6.3% relative improvement"
