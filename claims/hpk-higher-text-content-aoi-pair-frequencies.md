## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square analyses of AOI-pair fixation frequencies showed HPK students transitioned significantly more from text content to six of the eight other AOIs than LPK students; text content-timer and text content-agent pairs did not differ. [→ Taub 2019](#taub-2019)

## Evidence

### Taub 2019

Taub, M., & Azevedo, R. (2019). How Does Prior Knowledge Influence Eye Fixations and Sequences of Cognitive and Metacognitive SRL Processes during Learning with an Intelligent Tutoring System? Int J Artif Intell Educ (2019) 29:1-28. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0165-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Eight chi-square analyses (α = .00625) on the eye-tracking subset (n = 30) tested text-content-to-AOI pair frequencies by prior knowledge group. Six pairs were significant (e.g., text content-notes χ2(1) = 187.3, p < .001), while the text content-timer and text content-agent pairs were not (HPK = LPK).

> "participants with high prior knowledge had significantly higher frequencies of the text content-diagram AOI pair, the text content-notes AOI pair, the text content-table of contents AOI pair, the text content-SRL palette AOI pair"
