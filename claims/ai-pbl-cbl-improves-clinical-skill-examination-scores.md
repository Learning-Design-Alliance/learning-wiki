## Evidence

### Fang 2026

Fang, Y., Jingmei, W., & Liping, H. (2026). Application of AI-assisted PBL-CBL combined teaching in the diagnosis and treatment of post-herpetic neuralgia: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education.

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same RCT (n = 120, 60 per group). Clinical skills were assessed via simulation scored by three dermatology specialists using a standard rubric (maximum 100). The observation group scored "significantly higher than the control group" on the clinical skill examination (d = 0.51).

> "the observation group scored significantly higher than the control group in both the theoretical examination (t = 2.98, P < 0.01, 95% CI: 0.87–4.49, Cohen's d = 0.54) and the clinical skill examination (t = 2.79, P < 0.01, 95% CI: 0.55–3.23, Cohen's d = 0.51)"
