In the NSLM machine learning analysis, race/ethnicity was among the most important variables (M = 0.012, SD = 0.005). Predictions were "highest for White students (M = 0.011 relative to the mean, n = 2390), and lowest (M = −0.022, n = 730) for Black/African American students".
