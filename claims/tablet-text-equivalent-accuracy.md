## Evidence

### Kryklywy James H. 2021

Kryklywy James H., Roach Victoria A., Todd Rebecca M. (2021). Assessing the efficacy of tablet-based simulations for learning pseudo-surgical instrumentation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0245330

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

TOST equivalence analyses within the same 3x2 rm-ANOVA on 36 subjects; equivalency between tablet- and text-based training held at both immediate and delayed testing (all pTOSTs < 0.015). The null establishes no detected difference; equivalence was supported by TOST.

> "No differences in accuracy were noted between tablet and text-based training (pMM = 1.00), a null finding supported by follow-up equivalency testing (pTOST = 0.018)."
