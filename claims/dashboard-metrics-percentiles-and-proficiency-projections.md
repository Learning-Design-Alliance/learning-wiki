The guide's key-metrics section describes median achievement percentiles by grade, subject, and term, based on NWEA 2020 norms that provide insight into typical achievement and growth "prior to COVID-related disruptions."

> "This set of results contextualizes student achievement in a state relative to a nationally representative norming sample of other same-grade students from across the country."

### User guide for interpreting the NWEA state dashboard: Exploring the educational impacts of COVID-19 2021 (2)

User guide for interpreting the NWEA state dashboard: Exploring the educational impacts of COVID-19. (2021). NWEA Center for School and Student Progress. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/user-guide-for-interpreting-the-nwea-state-dashboard-exploring-the-educational-impacts-of-covid-19/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The guide explains proficiency projections contextualize achievement relative to each state's own proficiency thresholds, which "may vary in difficulty from state to state," with details in state-specific linking studies.

> "Briefly, at each term, a student’s MAP Growth RIT score can be compared to cut scores found in a state’s linking study; this allows educators to identify students who are or are not projected to be proficient on state summative tests."
