## Evidence

### Maya Usher 2025

Maya Usher, Ido Roll, Orly Fuhrman, Ofra Amir. (2025). Supporting Coordination and Peer Editing in Students’ Online Collaborative Writing Processes. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00450-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Screen-capture outcome measure from the 62-student lab experiment: time elapsed until the first major peer action (major addition/deletion or coordination). The article reports the time was "reduced by more than 50%" with r = .54.

> "using SITU has significantly reduced the time it took students to make their first major peer action in the document, which was reduced by more than 50% following the use of SITU, from an average time of 6 min and 12 s to 2 min and 47 s, with statistical significance (U (32,30) = 180, Z = -4.22, p <.001, r =.54)."
