## Evidence

### Eric Matthew Hickok 2016

Eric Matthew Hickok. (2016). Teaching practices of graduate teaching assistants. San José State University Master's Project. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of seven Physics 50W workshop observations (Fig. 4.3). The author reports greater variation than in labs: one TA spent the majority of class time with the Whole Class while another spent none, not even greeting the class. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The Not Interacting portion is 29.8% of the total time, followed by Small Groups , Whole Class , and Individual at 26.0%, 23.3%, and 20.9%, respectively."
