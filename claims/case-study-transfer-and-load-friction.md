### Haotian Zhang 2026

Haotian Zhang, Shucun Wang, Jinze Wu, Liang Ding, Shuochen Liu, Zhenya Huang, Jing Sha, Shijin Wang, and Qi Liu. (2026). Incorporating Cognitive Load and Knowledge Transfer for Multi-Domain Knowledge Tracing. arXiv:2608.24005. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24005

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative case study (Figure 5) of a SeniorH student's cross-domain knowledge state evolution; the model assigned an increased state to c3 based on mastery of correlated c2, and the student failed q11 after frequent domain switching, showing that "cognitive load can weaken immediate learning gains".

> "This indicates that cognitive load can weaken immediate learning gains even for pre- viously well-trained concepts. Overall, this case study provides intuitive evidence that LT-MKT can jointly model beneficial knowl- edge transfer and load-induced learning friction."
