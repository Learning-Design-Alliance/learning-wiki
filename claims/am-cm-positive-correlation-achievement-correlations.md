### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive correlational analysis of the TIMSS 2019 sample. The article reports a "large, positive association between the two kinds of motivation" with r = .544, country-level correlations ranging from .427 to .639, contradicting the negative correlation assumed by the unidimensional simplex approach.

> "A large, positive association between the two kinds of motivation was apparent across all countries (𝑟= .544, p < .001, 𝑟𝑎𝑛𝑔𝑒(𝑟)=[.427, .639])"

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation in the TIMSS 2019 descriptive statistics: "achievement levels were higher among students experiencing autonomous motivation" with r = .248, a moderate printed effect size. The article notes joint regression analyses are needed to untangle these effects.
