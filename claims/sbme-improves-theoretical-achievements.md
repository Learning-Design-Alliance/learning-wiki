## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SBME students scored significantly higher on final theoretical achievements than control students in both cohorts. [→ Wang S 2021](#wang-s-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang S 2021

Wang S, Ren X, Ye J, Wang W, Huang H, Qin C. (2021). Exploration of simulation-based medical education for undergraduate students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025982

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade comparison in the Results section of the same 1178-student controlled study. Final theoretical examination scores (50 points) were significantly higher in the SBME group in both the 2018 and 2019 junior cohorts, as the printed means and P values show.

> "SBME also had a positive effect on the final theoretical achievements (for the 2018 junior students, control group: 40.87 ± 0.47 vs SBME group: 42.63 ± 0.51, P = .001; for the 2019 junior students, control group: 41.32 ± 0.43 vs SBME group: 42.02 ± 0.52, P = .001)"
