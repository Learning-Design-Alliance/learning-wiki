Li Y 2026¶

Li Y (2026) Beyond output metrics: reframing AI-assisted vocal pedagogy through human learning and educational value. Front. Psychol. 17:1844841. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1844841

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual argument drawing on educational psychology: in singing, feedback is tied to self-expression, vulnerability, and vocal identity, so human learning remains interpretive, situated, and belief-laden. The article cites recent work suggesting performance and effort expectancy shape intentions to use AI in music education. No new empirical data are reported.
