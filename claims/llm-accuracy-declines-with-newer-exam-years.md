`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Yearly accuracy analysis (Fig. 3) across the 2022, 2023, and 2024 examinations. GPT-4o's accuracy declined from 86.7 ± 1.7% (2022) to 79.4 ± 2.1% (2024); GPT-4o and GPT-4o-RAG differed significantly from GPT-4o-mini in all years (p < 0.001), while GPT-4o versus GPT-4o-RAG was non-significant in every year.

> "GPT-4o achieved 86.7 ± 1.7%, 82.0 ± 1.4%, and 79.4 ± 2.1%, respectively, with significant differences (p < 0.001) compared to GPT-4o-mini in all years."
