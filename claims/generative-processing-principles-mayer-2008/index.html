Richard E. Mayer 2008¶

Richard E. Mayer. (2008). Applying the Science of Learning: Evidence-Based Principles for the Design of Multimedia Instruction. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066x.63.8.760

q3 · i3 · theoretical · r3

Controlled experiments on paper-based and computer-based lessons on brakes, pumps, generators, lightning, and arithmetic comparing words-and-pictures with words-only presentations; the article reports the quoted 11-of-11 result and that "The median effect size was 1.39, which is a large effect."
