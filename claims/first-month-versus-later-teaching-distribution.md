## Evidence

### Mapiour Deng 2014

Mapiour Deng, Prytula Michelle, Moser Michael. (2014). A classification of the verbal methods currently used to teach endoscopy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-163

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparison of 18 cases done during the first month versus 13 done after the first month, using the Mann–Whitney U test on standardized category proportions. The article reports the printed percentages, means and p-values; the tips/tricks trend in the first month (p = 0.07) is also reported.

> "Overall, there was less non-procedural teaching (6.7% vs 23.7%, p = 0.01) and a trend towards more teaching moments per case (13.2 vs 7.9, p = 0.07) in the first month of the rotation compared to the later months."
