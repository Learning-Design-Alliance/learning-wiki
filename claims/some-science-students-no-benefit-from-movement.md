## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some science students felt movement was not beneficial for reinforcing scientific concepts and that communication through dance was too abstract. [→ Baljon 2021](#baljon-2021)

## Evidence

### Baljon 2021

Baljon, A.R.C., Alter, J., & Bresciani Ludvik, M. (2021). Embodied engagement with scientific concepts: An exploration into emergent learning. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the same qualitative focus-group data, dissenting science students stated there was "any sort of net gain" absent, that movement was not beneficial for reinforcing concepts, and that everything communicated was abstract rather than concrete. No effect size is reported.

> "I do not think there was any sort of net gain: beneficial or not beneficial for us from teaching the concepts."
