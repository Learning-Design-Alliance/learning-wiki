## Evidence

### Taşdemir Murat 2026

Taşdemir Murat, Keskin Büşra Ekin, Aydoğan Ayaz Elif, Çelik Davut. (2026). Adverse outcomes of post-core restorations performed by undergraduate and postgraduate dental students: a retrospective comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09270-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariable binary logistic regressions on 160 restorations, each variable analyzed separately. The article reports patient age (OR = 1.03, 95% CI: 1.00–1.05, p = 0.027) and undergraduate versus postgraduate operator status (OR = 2.40, 95% CI: 1.10–5.24, p = 0.028) as associated with adverse outcomes.

> "In the univariable logistic regression analysis, patient age (OR = 1.03, 95% CI: 1.00–1.05, p = 0.027) and operator education level (OR = 2.40, 95% CI: 1.10–5.24, p = 0.028) was associated with adverse outcomes"
