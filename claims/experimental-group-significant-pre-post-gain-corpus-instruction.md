## Evidence

### Uçar 2015

Uçar, S., & Yükselir, C. (2015). The Effect of Corpus-based Activities on Verb-Noun Collocations in EFL Classes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – April 2015, volume 14 issue 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057374

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test within the 15-student experimental group receiving corpus-based instruction using COCA concordance lines. Scores rose from pretest M=31.87 (SD=13.352) to posttest M=65.93 (SD=20.126), a statistically significant gain (t(14)=-4.481, p<.005).
