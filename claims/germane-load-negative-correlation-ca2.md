## Evidence

### David James Woo 2026

David James Woo, Deliang Wang, Kai Guo. (2026). AI Slop or AI-enhancement? Student Perceptions of AI-generated Media for an English for Academic Purposes Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16275)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis of survey germane-load items against course scores. The authors attribute the pattern to the College's practice that "students' academic performance is normalized and moderated for the course", suggesting criterion-referenced scoring might yield different results.

> "Surprisingly, GL (promoting deep thinking) showed a certain degree of negative correlation with the total score (-0.206) and CA2 (-0.223) (see Figure 7). This may imply that under the current assessment mechanism, students who engage excessively in deep reflection and knowledge linking (high GL) might not necessarily have an advantage"
