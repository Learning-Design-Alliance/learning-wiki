Evidence¶

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska 2022¶

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska. (2022). Effects of Nature (Greenspace) on Cognitive Functioning in School Children and Adolescents: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1217–1254. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09658-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports this mediation analysis from Li and Sullivan's window-view experiment, which measured self-reported attention and stress via Visual Analogue Scale plus physiological stress (blood volume pulse, electrocardiography, skin conductance, body temperature) entered into regression models.

"Since the analysis revealed no correlation between attention and stress changes, Li and Sullivan (2016) proposed that nature exposure was likely to have a direct impact on working memory performance rather than through the effect of a stress pathway."
