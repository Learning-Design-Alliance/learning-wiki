## Evidence

### Ni Cheng-Hua 2020

Ni Cheng-Hua, Guo Shu-Liu, Chao Chia-Yu, Wang Chia-Hui, Susanty Sri, & Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2020). Nurses’ Late-Life Depression Knowledge and Attitudes Toward Depression: A Cross-Sectional Study. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/0046958020945179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional self-report survey of 307 nurses from 3 hospitals in the Taipei area. The mean LLDQ score was 7.17 (SD = 2.10) out of 13, with "55.15% correct answers among all questions"; the authors interpret this as insufficient knowledge.

> "The mean score on the late-life depression scale was 7.17 (SD = 2.10; max = 0, min = 12; full score is 13) with 55.15% correct answers among all questions."
