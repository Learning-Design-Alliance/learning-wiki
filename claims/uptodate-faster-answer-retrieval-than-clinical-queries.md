To Compare PubMed Clinical Queries and UpToDate in Teaching Information Mastery to Clinical Residents: A Crossover Randomized Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023487

Log Rank survival analysis of time-to-answer recorded by purpose-built software in the same crossover trial of 44 residents, with a 20-minute cap per scenario. The article reports the medians and confidence intervals quoted; no effect size is printed.
