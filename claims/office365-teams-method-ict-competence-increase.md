### Maiia Marienko 2020

Maiia Marienko, Yulia Nosenko, Alisa Sukhikh, Viktor Tataurov, and Mariya Shyshkina. (2020). Personalization of learning through adaptive technologies in the context of sustainable development of teachers’ education. E3S Web of Conferences 166, 10015. https://doi.org/10.1051/e3sconf/202016610015

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pedagogical experiment at Kamyanets-Podilskyi National University with masters of physical, mathematical and pedagogical specialties, sample size 160 students, introducing Microsoft Teams services to support collaboration and project work. The article reports "a statistically significant increase in the level of ICT -competence and student performance"; no effect size is printed.
