### Stamer Tjorven 2023

Stamer Tjorven, Essers Geurt, Steinhäuser Jost, Flägel Kristina. (2023). From summative MAAS Global to formative MAAS 2.0 – a workshop report. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2023-40/zma001591.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive report of the instrument revision (section 3.3). The authors state the new scale "does not refer to quantitative evaluation", citing the higher focus on frame of reference and context; assessment must weigh both extent and quality of behavior.

> "MAAS 2.0 now uses now a formative evaluation range without numerical classification ranging from "good" through to "satisfactory", "adequate" and "poor". This represents a 4-point scale."
