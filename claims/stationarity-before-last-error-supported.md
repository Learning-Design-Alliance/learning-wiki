# Stationarity tests show no evidence of learning in responses prior to the last error across the experimental groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Binary Number experiment, the stationarity test over single-trial blocks for the first 10 trials supports the null hypothesis of no change in the proportion of correct responses before the last error. [→ Suppes 1963](#suppes-1963)
`q2 i?` In the Goodnow two-armed bandit data, the slight increase in correct responses prior to the last error over the first seven trials is not statistically significant. [→ Suppes 1963 (2)](#suppes-1963-2)
`q2 i?` In the Bower paired-associate data, the stationarity test on responses prior to the last error is not significant. [→ Suppes 1963 (3)](#suppes-1963-3)

## Evidence

### Suppes 1963

Suppes, P., & Ginsberg, R. (1963). A fundamental property of all-or-none models, binomial distribution of responses prior to conditioning, with application to concept formation in children. Psychological Review, 70(2), 139-161. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/rev/70/2/139

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Reanalysis of the Binary Number experiment (24 subjects learning binary concepts of 4 and 5): the stationarity test on responses before the last error supports the null hypothesis, with "x 2 = 8.00, df = 9, P > .30, N = 844".

> "The test of stationarity over blocks of single trials for the:first 10 trials again supports the null hypothesis (x 2 = 8.00, df = 9, P > .30, N = 844)."

### Suppes 1963 (2)

Suppes, P., & Ginsberg, R. (1963). A fundamental property of all-or-none models, binomial distribution of responses prior to conditioning, with application to concept formation in children. Psychological Review, 70(2), 139-161. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/rev/70/2/139

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Reanalysis of Goodnow's two-armed bandit probability-learning data with undergraduates: the pre-last-error curve shows only a slight, non-significant tendency to increase over the first seven trials, while the mean learning curve itself was highly significant.

> "Although there is a slight tendency for the proportion of correct responses prior to the last error to increase over these seven trials, the result is not statistically significant (N = 382,)(2 = 9.97, df = 6, P > .10)."

### Suppes 1963 (3)

Suppes, P., & Ginsberg, R. (1963). A fundamental property of all-or-none models, binomial distribution of responses prior to conditioning, with application to concept formation in children. Psychological Review, 70(2), 139-161. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/rev/70/2/139

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Reanalysis of Bower's paired-associate experiment (29 undergraduates, ten-item list): responses prior to the last error remain stationary while overall correct responses reached .96 by trial seven, with the quoted nonsignificant stationarity test.

> "the x 2 test of stationarity is not significant (N = 549, x2 = .97, df = 6, P > .95)."
