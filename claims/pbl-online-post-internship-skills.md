### Chen Xiuwen 2024

Chen Xiuwen, Xiao Yao, You Yunhui, Zhu Jianxi, Liu Shiqing, Chen Huiqiong, Zhu Hong. (2024). The effectiveness of the combined PBL and Tencent Conference online teaching mode in the clinical internship teaching of cardiac surgery. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315455

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-internship questionnaire comparison of 68 students reported in Table 4: Q13 history taking p = 0.009 and Q15 auxiliary examinations p = 0.044, with the experimental group scoring significantly higher. The article reports "no significant differences in the scores for the entries of satisfaction with overall teaching and impressions of surgical learning."

> "The results of the mastery and utilisation degree scores of the two groups for cardiac surgery history taking and the mastery and utilisation degree scores of the ancillary tests (application and interpretation of each test) after the internship were significantly different (p < 0.05) (Table 4)."
