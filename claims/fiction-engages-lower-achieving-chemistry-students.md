## Evidence

### Randy K Yerrick 2017

Randy K Yerrick, Tiffany Simons. (2017). The Affordances of Fiction for Teaching Chemistry. Science Education International, Volume 28, Issue 3. https://www.stemedu.org/science-education-international/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Teacher observation and reflective journal data from the intervention classroom: the first author observed that lower-quartile students offered the best contributions while higher-achieving students were less enthusiastic and asked more concrete questions. The authors attribute this partly to the cross-disciplinary nature of the excerpts or renegotiated discourse norms.

> "the insertion of fiction excerpts increased the interest of students who have typically underachieved in science and not regularly participated in open conversations about content."
