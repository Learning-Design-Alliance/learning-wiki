## Evidence

### Zhang Meng-lu 2025

Zhang Meng-lu, Bai Wen, Yan Zhi-peng, Ge Hui-min, Zhang Qiu-yang, Ding Xi-yan, Liu Yan-qing, Li Xiang, Jiang Qin, Li Ke-ran, Shang Wei-hong. (2025). Virtual simulation system in cataract education for medical undergraduates: a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08497-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Item-level Wilcoxon rank-sum analysis of the 10 surgical technical skills items in the VR assessment (Table 4). Phacoemulsification and aspiration: 2.31 ± 0.53 vs 1.64 ± 0.87, r = 0.384, Adj. P = 0.0040; viscoelastic reinjection: 1.77 ± 0.65 vs 1.06 ± 1.01, r = 0.308, Adj. P = 0.0099. Items such as IOL implantation and wound closure showed no significant difference.

> "after adjusting the P-values for multiple comparisons using the Holm method, the experimental group’s significant advantage in the core skills of phacoemulsification and anterior chamber reinjection of viscoelastic agent was rigorously confirmed"
