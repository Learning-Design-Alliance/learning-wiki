## Evidence

### Ramis Mary-Anne 2019

Ramis Mary-Anne, Chang Anne, Conway Aaron, Lim David, Munday Judy, Nissen Lisa. (2019). Theory-based strategies for teaching evidence-based practice to undergraduate health students: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1698-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review reports the single study (Kim et al., quasi-experimental with control, Johnston KAB questionnaire, self-report) measuring EBP use and future use; equivalence for future use was not tested, only non-significance (p = 0.255).

> "A small but significant difference between intervention and control groups was reported for EBP use (mean difference = 0.26, p = 0.015), however no significant difference between groups was reported for EBP future use (mean diff =0.13, p = 0.255)."
