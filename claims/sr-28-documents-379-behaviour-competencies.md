### Stoll J. T. 2024

Stoll J. T., Böhmdorfer-McNair B., Jeske M., Weidmann A. E. (2024). Development and validation of a national clinical pharmacy competency framework for hospital pharmacists in Austria: a multi-method study. International Journal of Clinical Pharmacy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11096-024-01781-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Systematic review reported per PRISMA 2020, screening 102 identified documents against pre-determined criteria with two independent reviewers. The review "resulted in 28 frameworks, guidelines and related documents" and 379 behaviour competencies were extracted; no effect size applies.

> "The systematic review (SR) resulted in 28 frameworks, guidelines and related documents and the identification of 379 behaviour competencies, with nineteen mapped to the “Patient Care and Clinical Pharmacy Skills” domain of the CTF (after removal of duplicates)."
