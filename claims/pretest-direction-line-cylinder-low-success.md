Analysis of more than 100 pre-tests in calculus-based introductory physics on a question with an infinite charged line parallel to an infinite charged hollow cylinder. The article reports "about 35% of all students found the direction of the electric field at point A correctly while only 14%" at point B; percentages only, no effect size.
