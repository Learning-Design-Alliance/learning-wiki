Evidence¶

Allensworth 2017¶

Allensworth, E. M., Moore, P. T., Sartain, L., & de la Torre, M. (2017). The Educational Benefits of Attending Higher Performing Schools: Evidence From Chicago High Schools. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373716672039

q2 · i? · associational · r3

IPW analysis of CPS graduation outcomes (top-tier overall n = 48,510; mid-tier n = 27,450). The article reports a boost to graduation for students who otherwise would attend bottom-tier schools, with the printed percentage-point effects.

"The effect is not only largest for those who attend top-tier schools (10 percentage points) but also significant for attending a mid-tier high school versus a bottom-tier (by 3 percentage points) school."
