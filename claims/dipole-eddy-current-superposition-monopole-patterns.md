## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The moving dipole's eddy current pattern of four counter-rotating swirls is a natural superposition of two moving-monopole patterns for oppositely charged monopoles displaced in the appropriate direction. [→ Liu 2006](#liu-2006)

## Evidence

### Liu 2006

Liu, Y., & Belcher, J. W. (2006). Magnetic Flux Diffusion and Expulsion with Thin Conducting Sheets. http://web.mit.edu/viz/soft/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.0182](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.0182)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical and visualization results (Fig. 6 shows the four-swirl pattern at v0/v = 8.1) support the superposition interpretation; the authors state the dipole pattern "can be seen as a natural superposition" of the monopole patterns.

> "The eddy current patterns for the moving dipole cases can be understood as a superposition of two eddy current patterns for the moving monopole case, for oppositely charged monopoles displaced in the appropriate directions."
