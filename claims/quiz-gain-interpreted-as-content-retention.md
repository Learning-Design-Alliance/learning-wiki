## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret the post-session improvement as indicating students retained concepts covered during the workshops, because no feedback on quiz performance was given. [→ Ricci A 2025](#ricci-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Ricci A 2025

Ricci A, Minearo I, Hielscher A. (2025). Focused anatomy workshops for clerkships and the USMLE Step 1 examination. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2536

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion: because the identical quizzes were not followed by feedback, "students did not receive any information regarding their performance on the quizzes", so the gain is attributed to retention of workshop content rather than feedback effects.

> "Although the pre‐ and post‐session quizzes consisted of identical questions, students did not receive any information regarding their performance on the quizzes and prior publications have used similar approaches."
