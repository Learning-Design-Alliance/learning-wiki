## Evidence

### Karaci 2018

Karaci, A., Akyuz, H. I., Bilgici, G., & Arici, N. (2018). Effects of Web-based Intelligent Tutoring Systems on Academic Achievement and Retention. International Journal of Computer Applications, 181(16). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED588768

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test of pre-/post-test scores in the 80-student controlled experiment. Control means rose from 3.62 to 16.26 and experimental means from 3.42 to 20.66, with "a significant difference between the mean pre - and post-test scores of both groups".

> "Table 3 shows a significant difference between the mean pre - and post-test scores of both groups after intervention (p < 0.05). In other words, the achievement level of both groups was higher after instruction."
