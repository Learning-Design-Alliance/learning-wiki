## Evidence

### Yousef Nadya 2022

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing a systematic review (Lindhard et al.), that team performance on simulated re-evaluation improved at 3 and 6 months after simulation training; only significance is stated, with no effect size.

> "There are few studies evaluating team performance after simulation training in neonatal resuscitation [53, 54]. A recent review found significantly improved team performance in simulated re-evaluation 3 and 6 months after simulation training"

### Yousef Nadya 2022 (2)

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review states that reviews of simulation's impact on neonatal mortality and morbidity found insufficient or poor quality data, so no effect on neonatal outcomes could be revealed; this is a null synthesis, not evidence of equivalence.

> "Several reviews on the impact of simulation on neonatal mortality and morbidity have not been able to reveal effects of team training on neonatal outcomes because of insufficient or poor quality data"
