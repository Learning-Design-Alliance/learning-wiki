## Evidence

### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Impact of evidence-based flipped or active-engagement non-flipped courses on student performance in introductory physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0171](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0171)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intra-group pooled FCI pre/post analysis (Table I) of algebra-based flipped (N=299 pretest) and LB courses and calculus-based LB courses. All groups improved significantly (p < 0.001); the EBAE Norm g of 30% exceeded the LB 23% and calculus LB 25%.

> "We find statistically significant improvements from the pretest to the posttest for each group but the normalized gain (Norm g) is largest (30%) for the EBAE courses."

### Nafis I. Karim 2018 (2)

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Impact of evidence-based flipped or active-engagement non-flipped courses on student performance in introductory physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0171](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0171)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intra-group pooled CSEM pre/post analysis (Table II) of calculus-based LB, calculus-based EBAE (flipped and interactive non-flipped combined), and algebra-based LB courses. All groups improved significantly (p < 0.001); EBAE Norm g of 36% exceeded LB gains of 21% and 25%.

> "We find that there are statistically significant differences between the pre-/posttest scores for each group but the normalized gain (Norm g) is largest (36%) for the EBAE courses."
