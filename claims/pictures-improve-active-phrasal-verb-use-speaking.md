# Teaching phrasal verbs through pictures significantly improves pre-intermediate EFL learners' active application of phrasal verbs in speaking

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2011 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Pre-intermediate EFL learners taught 24 phrasal verbs with pictures used significantly more of them in a speaking posttest than learners taught the same items without pictures (Cohen's d = 0.76). [→ Marashi and Maherinia 2011](#marashi-and-maherinia-2011)

## Evidence

### Marashi and Maherinia 2011

Hamid Marashi, Elaheh Maherinia. (2011). The Effect of Teaching Phrasal Verbs through Pictures on EFL Learners' Active Application of Phrasal Verbs in Speaking. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 2, April-June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071191

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 60 pre-intermediate learners (30 per group) homogenized by PET and taught the same content over 18 sessions; pictures only in the experimental group. Posttest means were 6.37 (experimental) versus 3.40 (control); the independent-samples t-test gave "the p value was 0.00" with t = -9.017, and "the observed power (Cohen's d) stood at 0.76".

> "Since the p value was 0.00 which is smaller than 0.05, there was a significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups at the posttest."
