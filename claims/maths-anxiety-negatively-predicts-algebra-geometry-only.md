Regression models (Table 7) predicting each of 13 teacher-assigned grades from 8 anxiety measures, expertise, age and gender in N = 741; Table 7 prints AMAS coefficients of −0.19*** for both Algebra and Geometry.

> "AMAS was the strongest predictor of performance out of all anxiety measures and was linked only with math‐related grades (Algebra and Geometry)."
