## Evidence

### Duchesneau 2020

Duchesneau, N. (2020). Social, Emotional, and Academic Development Through an Equity Lens (#ReEnvisionSEL). The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/social-emotional-and-academic-development-through-an-equity-lens/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group finding: one Black student said "being successful for me would mean feeling challenged every day" and a Black mother said "They need to be challenged mentally." Participants argued challenging coursework supports sense of self and emotional fulfillment.

> "Contrary to the belief that students want it easy in school, many students we spoke with, regardless of whether they participated in advanced courses, expressed a desire to be challenged by their coursework."
