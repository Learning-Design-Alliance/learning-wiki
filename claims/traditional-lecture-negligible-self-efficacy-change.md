In the quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study, the traditional teaching group (n = 30) received lecture and PowerPoint delivery of the same supplementary pediatric nursing sessions. The article reports a negligible 0.8% increase with "(p = 0.78)" and no printed effect size.

> "In contrast, traditional teaching led to only a negligible 0.8% increase (p = 0.78), which was not statistically significant."
