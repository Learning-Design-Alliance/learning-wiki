## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2012

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2012). Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. WWC Intervention Report, Students with Learning Disabilities. http://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

WWC analysis of one RCT (Fuchs et al., 1995) with 40 students with learning disabilities in grades 2–4, measured with the Tennessee Mathematics Operations Test–Revised and the Tennessee Mathematics Concepts and Applications Test. Average improvement index was +9 with no statistically significant effect.

> "Thus, for the mathematics domain, the WWC study did not find statistically significant effects, and the WWC-calculated average effect size was less than 0.25. This results in a rating of no discernible effects, with a small extent of evidence."
