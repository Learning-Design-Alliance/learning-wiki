Delayed-posttest administered eight weeks after the application to measure retention; t-test on the same 58 students showed no significant difference, with the experimental group mean (16.67) higher than the control group mean (14.35).

"It is understood from Table 5 that students' delayed-posttest scores did not significantly differ by the digital storytelling [t(56)=1.838, p>.05]. The mean of the delayed -posttest in the control and experimental groups are 14.35 and 16.67, respectively."
