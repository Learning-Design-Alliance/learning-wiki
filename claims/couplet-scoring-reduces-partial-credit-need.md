### Michael Vignal 2023

Michael Vignal, Gayle Geschwind, Marcos D. Caballero, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Overview of couplet scoring in content-focused physics assessments. arXiv:2307.03099. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Developers' experience report from scoring SPRUCE with couplet scoring: responses that would have earned partial credit under item scoring instead received full credit on one AO and zero on another, as the authors report that couplet scoring "eliminated the need for partial credit on all items except coupled multiple-response items". This is the authors' development experience, not a controlled comparison; no effect size is reported.
