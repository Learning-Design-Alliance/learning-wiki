This scoping review of 26 publications on faculty development in interprofessional health education found that "16 of the included studies highlighted the domains of teamwork and communication" as a relevant part of the interventions performed, though the studies "did not delve into the details of expected attributes and performances."

> "Among the collaborative interprofessional competencies, 16 of the included studies highlighted the domains of teamwork and communication(14,15,26,28,30,33–36,38–,41,44–46) as a relevant part of the interventions performed, but did not delve into the details of expected attributes and performances."
