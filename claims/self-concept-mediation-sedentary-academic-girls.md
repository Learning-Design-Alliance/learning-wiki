## Evidence

### Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira 2022

Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira, Werneck André de Oliveira, da Silva Danilo Rodrigues Pereira, Oyeyemi Adewale Luqman, Zambrin Lidyane Ferreira, Fernandes Romulo Araújo, Serassuelo Helio Junior, Romanzini Marcelo, Ronque Enio Ricardo Vaz. (2022). Association between patterns of sedentary time and academic performance in adolescents: the mediating role of self-concept. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2022/40/2021106IN

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis using the Karlson Holm Breen method decomposed total effects into direct and indirect effects in linear regression models; the authors report that "self-concept partially mediated the association between all types of ST patterns (shorter and longer bouts and breaks) and academic performance among girls".

> "Also, self-concept partially mediated the association between all types of ST patterns (shorter and longer bouts and breaks) and academic performance among girls and shorter bouts and academic performance among boys."
