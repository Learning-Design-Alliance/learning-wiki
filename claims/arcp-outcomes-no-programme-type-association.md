Impact of accelerated, graduate-entry medicine courses: a comparison of profile, success, and specialty destination between graduate entrants to accelerated or standard medicine courses in UK. BMC Medical Education 18:250. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1355-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Crosstabs (Chi2) analysis of first recorded F1 and F2 ARCP outcomes by programme type in the UKMED cohorts (Table 11). "no significant association with type of medicine programme (all ps > 0.1)"; unsatisfactory ARCPs were very few.

> "As can be seen above, there were very few unsatisfactory ARCPs overall, but a larger number of foundation doctors who provided incomplete evidence (all these obtained an outcome 1 later). Simple crosstabs (Chi2) analysis of this original data showed no significant association with type of medicine programme (all ps > 0.1)."
