## Evidence

### Clinton 2018

Virginia Clinton. (2018). Reflections versus Extended Quizzes: Which is Better for Student Learning and Self-Regulation? Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v18i1.22508

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on the 12-item Metacognitive Self-Regulation Scale of the MSLQ administered during the second week and second-to-last week of the semester. The article reports "There was no difference between condition, F(216) = .04, p = .84".

> "A one-way ANCOVA was conducted with condition as the independent variable, scale score at the beginning of the semester as a covariate, and scale score at the end of the semester as the dependent variable. There was no difference between condition, F(216) = .04, p = .84"
