### Gibson Louisa 2021

Gibson Louisa, Porter Melanie. (2021). Maternal drinking and smoking. Can it explain the exceptional academic performance of LBOTE children? A preliminary analysis. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-021-05544-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-way ANOVAs comparing NAPLAN completers and non-completers across LBOTE and ELB groups, run to check that performance was not an artefact of lower-SES LBOTE children staying home on test day. The article also prints Grade 5 maternal education interaction F = 6.07–7.12, p = 0.01.

> "Results indicated that Grade 3 and 5 NAPLAN non-completers were from families with lower levels of maternal education and lower family incomes (F = 15.77–24.59, p ˂ 0.001)."
