Jason Zagami. (2026). Generative AI as a cognitive partner: personalised GPTs and differentiated learning in undergraduate teacher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10746-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of weekly reflective journals (300–400 words) across the semester; Student 8 described shifting from copying suggestions to predicting and challenging the GPT, and the article aligns the progression with Zimmerman's self-regulated learning cycle.

> "Students demonstrated increasing sophistication in metacognitive behaviours across the semester. Early reflections were descriptive (“I used the GPT to explain…”). In contrast, later entries displayed strategic awareness (“I realised that adjusting the temperature setting gave me more creative responses when brainstorming assessments.”)."
