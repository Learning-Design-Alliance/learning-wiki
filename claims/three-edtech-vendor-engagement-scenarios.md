# Districts engage with edtech products primarily through three scenarios: RFPs, vendor pitches, and peer recommendations

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Districts encounter edtech mainly via requests for proposals, vendor sales pitches to decision-making roles, and peer recommendations, with a newer scenario of deciding which tools to keep as funding lessens. [→ scenarios-1](#scenarios-1)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive statement in the report's vendor-engagement section enumerating the three primary engagement scenarios in the districts' own practice, plus an emergent fourth scenario around sunsetting tools as funding sources lessen.

> "Districts primarily engage with edtech products in three scenarios: (1) Districts put out requests for proposals (RFPs) to find a product that serves a specific need; (2) vendors' sales teams pitch to those in roles that may have edtech decision making authority; and (3) peers offer recommendations about tools."
