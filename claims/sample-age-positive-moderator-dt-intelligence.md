## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Average sample age had a positive linear relationship with the intelligence–DT correlation (β = 0.01), such that a 10-year age difference yields a .10 difference in Fisher-z-transformed correlations. [→ Gerwig Anne 2021](#gerwig-anne-2021)

## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression of mean sample age on Fisher-z-transformed coefficients across the 112 studies, with printed unstandardized "β = 0.01" (negligible per-coefficient magnitude). A quadratic term did not improve fit (Δχ2(1) = 0.09, p = .762), and the linear effect was robust across checks; visualized in Figure 3.

> "The average sample age had a positive relationship with the intelligence–DT correlation, β = 0.01, z = 6.93, p < .001. This effect implies that a 10-year difference in average sample age yields a difference of .10 in the Fisher-z-transformed intelligence–DT correlation."
