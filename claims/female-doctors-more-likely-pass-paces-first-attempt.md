## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Female candidates had significantly higher odds of passing PACES at first attempt than male candidates (OR = 1.43) in the full study population. [→ Unwin Emily 2018](#unwin-emily-2018)

## Evidence

### Unwin Emily 2018

Unwin Emily, Potts Henry W. W., Dacre Jane, Elder Andrew, Woolf Katherine. (2018). Passing MRCP (UK) PACES: a cross-sectional study examining the performance of doctors by sex and country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1178-2

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate binary logistic regression of the full study population of doctors attempting PACES for the first time (October 2010 to May 2013), controlling for ethnicity, PMQ world region, GMC registration and examination centre location. The adjusted "OR = 1.43; 95% CI = 1.29-1.59" favours female candidates.

> "Adjusting for the other variables, female candidates were significantly more likely to pass PACES at first attempt compared with the male candidates (OR = 1.43; 95% CI = 1.29-1.59; P < 0.0001)."
