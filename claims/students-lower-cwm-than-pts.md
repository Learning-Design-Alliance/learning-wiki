## Evidence

### Yanase Yukiko 2026

Yanase Yukiko, Hiragi Saki, Kawahara Yasuhiro. (2026). Effects of generic medical communication training on physiotherapy students’ ability to communicate in movement-based clinical scenarios: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08560-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Steel post-hoc comparison of the learned student group (n = 15) against practicing physiotherapists (n = 15, at least five years post-licensure) on the eight-item CWM performance task. The PT group's median was 8 versus the students' 6, with "effect size: d = 1.339".

> "However, the learned group scored significantly lower than the PT group (median: 8) (p = 0.003, effect size: d = 1.339, power: 1 − β = 0.943)."
