Evidence¶

Carrie Gillispie 2021¶

Carrie Gillispie, Caitlin Codella, Aaron Merchen, Joseph Davis, Ashley Cappo. (2021). Equity in Child Care Is Everyone's Business. The Education Trust & U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617545

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report asserts, citing research without detail, that "Dual language learners especially benefit from high-quality early care and education and from dual language immersion programs." It defines dual language learners as children under 8 with at least one parent speaking a language other than English at home, about one-third of U.S. children. No specific study designs or effect sizes are printed.

"Dual language learners especially benefit from high-quality early care and education and from dual language immersion programs, where adults present educational content in multiple languages, and enrollment is balanced between native speakers of each of the languages."
