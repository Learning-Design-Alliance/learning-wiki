Chi-square comparisons in the post-series survey Results section: frontline providers reported lower confidence gains than public health practitioners and others (P = 0.04), and higher attendance (4+ sessions) was associated with higher reported confidence gains (P = 0.004). Observational comparisons; no effect size printed.

> "However, frontline providers were less likely to report an increase in confidence than public health practitioners or other occupations (72.5% vs. 86.4% vs. 85.7%, P = 0.04). Additionally, participants who attended four or more sessions were more likely to report their confidence increased “a lot” compared to those who attended fewer sessions (83.8% vs. 65.4%, P = 0.004)."
