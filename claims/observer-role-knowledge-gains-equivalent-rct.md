In the study's parallel randomized-controlled trial of an interprofessional cohort, the results section reports that "There was no significant difference in the increase in scores between the two groups", with both groups improving from pre-test to post-test.

> "There was no significant difference in the increase in scores between the two groups, indicating that the observer role plays an equal part in learning as the active participant role."
