## Evidence

### Durmazpinar Parla Meva 2025

Durmazpinar Parla Meva, Ekmekci Ece. (2025). Comparing diagnostic skills in endodontic cases: dental students versus ChatGPT-4o. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05857-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons following a significant Kruskal-Wallis test on correct answer rates of 70 third-year and 80 fifth-year students; "fifth-year students performed better than third-year students" (p < 0.001). No effect size printed.

> "Bonferroni post-hoc tests indicated significant differences between third-year and fifth-year students, third-year students and ChatGPT-4o, and fifth-year students and ChatGPT-4o (p < 0.001 for all comparisons). ChatGPT-4o outperformed both third- and fifth-year students in accuracy, and fifth-year students performed better than third-year students"
