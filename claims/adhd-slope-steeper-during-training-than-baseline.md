## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The ADHD symptom slope during the 12-week training phase was significantly steeper (more negative) than during the 6-week baseline waiting period in the intervention group. [→ Froelich 2012](#froelich-2012)

## Evidence

### Froelich 2012

Froelich, J., Breuer, D., Doepfner, M., & Amonn, F. (2012). Effects of a teacher training programme on symptoms of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. International Journal of Special Education, 27(3), 76-87. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001061

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group multilevel comparison of intervention-group slopes. Baseline slope was near zero (F = 0.122, p = 0.728); during training the ADHD score decreased by "-0.054 score values" per assessment (p < .001).

> "during the treatment period (t7 to t19) a significantly stronger symptom decrease was found (df = 1,501; F = 20.12; p < .001). The negative slope shows a decrease of -0.054 score values"
