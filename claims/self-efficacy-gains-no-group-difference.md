## Evidence

### Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024

Ghafouri Raziyeh, Zamanzadeh Vahid, Nasiri Malihe. (2024). Comparison of education using the flipped class, gamification and gamification in the flipped learning environment on the performance of nursing students in a client health assessment: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05966-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test results table for self-efficacy before and after the intervention, analyzed on 156 participants of the 166 enrolled; means and SDs are printed but no standardized effect size.

> "Self-efficacy before	19.27	156	2.42	0.001	
Self-efficacy after	27.32	156	1.63"

### Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024 (2)

Ghafouri Raziyeh, Zamanzadeh Vahid, Nasiri Malihe. (2024). Comparison of education using the flipped class, gamification and gamification in the flipped learning environment on the performance of nursing students in a client health assessment: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05966-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group ANOVA on post-intervention self-efficacy across the four randomized teaching groups; the article prints only the P value. The authors attribute the null possibly to participants being second- and third-semester students and to interactive skills not being assessed.

> "The result of the ANOVA was not significant difference between the mean of the self-efficacy after intervention in the four groups (P = 0.101)."
