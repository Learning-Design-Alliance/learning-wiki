### Nepal Hari Prasad 2026

Nepal Hari Prasad, Bramblett Debra E., Benson Marc. (2026). Peer instruction in preclinical osteopathic microbiology education: a single-group pre–post quasi-experimental study of short-term performance outcomes and student perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09515-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A paired-samples t-test in a single-group pre–post quasi-experimental study of 218 first-year osteopathic medical students found a statistically significant improvement from pre-PI to post-PI scores, "yielding a mean gain of 2.11 points" with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.23).

> "Post-PI scores (6.57 ± 1.72) were significantly higher than pre-PI scores (4.46 ± 1.75; yielding a mean gain of 2.11 points (95% CI: 1.88–2.34; t(217) = 18.18, p < 0.001) with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.23)."
