## Evidence

### Eifler Eike Friederike 2019

Eifler Eike Friederike, Starr Alexandra, Riemann Rainer. (2019). The genetic and environmental effects on school grades in late childhood and adolescence. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225946. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225946

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Twin-sibling SEM on the first wave of the German TwinLife study, using actual school-report grades residualized for sex, age, and school type. The article reports "substantial genetic effects (up to 62%) in both cohorts on all three variables"; no standardized effect size beyond variance components is printed.

> "Structural equation modelling on the data of 432 monozygotic and 529 dizygotic twin pairs as well as 317 siblings of the twins showed substantial genetic effects (up to 62%) in both cohorts on all three variables."
