# A DAA activity is rated significantly more motivating than a lecture on perceived sense of learning, enjoyment, and helpfulness

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2013 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students rated the DAA task significantly higher than the lecture on all three motivation measures (perceived sense of learning, enjoyment, helpfulness). [→ Dalrymple 2013](#dalrymple-2013)

## Evidence

### Dalrymple 2013

Dalrymple, O., Sears, D. A., & Evangelou, D. (2013). The Relative Pedagogical Value of Disassemble/Analyze/Assemble (DAA) Activities. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076105

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment in a first-year engineering lab (325 students across two task sequences). Paired-samples t-tests on seven-point ratings: learning 5.30 vs 4.84, t(324) = 7.580, p < .001; enjoyment 5.87 vs 5.23, t(324) = 10.641, p < .001; helpfulness 5.15 vs 4.81, t(324) = 5.263, p < .001. No effect sizes printed.

> "Using the paired samples t-test on each element of the motivation measure, the mean ratings for the DAA task were found to be significantly higher than the mean ratings for the lecture"
