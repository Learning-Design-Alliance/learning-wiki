Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training plus Letter Knowledge Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/376

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC domain synthesis across the three studies; Appendix A3.3 reports the "Domain average9 for phonological processing across all studies 0.78 na +30", i.e. a domain average effect size of 0.78. Gettinger (1986) showed statistically significant positive effects, Roberts and Neal (2004) was indeterminate, and Pietrangelo (1999) was substantively important but unconfirmed.

What Works Clearinghouse 2006 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training plus Letter Knowledge Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/376

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of 129 four-year-old low-income children in 10 Head Start classrooms in upstate New York; nine classrooms were randomly assigned and one placed by convenience. Appendix A3.3 reports effect sizes of 0.38 (alliteration), 0.38 (rhyming), and 0.47 (phoneme blending), all ns by WWC recalculation but rated substantively important.

"Pietrangelo (1999) examined the effect of the intervention on three phonological processing outcomes and found statistically significant differences favoring the intervention group on each measure."
