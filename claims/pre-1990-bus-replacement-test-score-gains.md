# Districts replacing pre-1990 buses showed significantly greater test score improvements than districts without replacements

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In secondary analyses, districts awarded funding to replace the oldest, highest-polluting (pre-1990) buses experienced significantly greater improvements in RLA (0.062 SD) and math (0.025 SD) scores than districts without replacements. [→ Pedde M 2024](#pedde-m-2024)

## Evidence

### Pedde M 2024

Pedde M, Szpiro A, Hirth RA, Adar SD. (2024). School Bus Rebate Program and Student Educational Performance Test Scores. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.3121

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis by model year of replaced buses within the same randomized cohort of 1941 districts, using indicators for pre-1990, 1990-1999, and 2000-or-newer model years. The printed effect sizes were 0.062 SD (95% CI, 0.050-0.074) for RLA and 0.025 SD (95% CI, 0.011-0.039) for math, both favoring pre-1990 replacement; these are small-to-small-standardized improvements, coded i = 1.

> "For districts replacing the oldest buses (pre-1990 models), however, we observed significantly larger SD improvements in mean RLA test scores of 0.062 (95% CI, 0.050-0.074) and math test scores of 0.025 (95% CI, 0.011-0.039) compared with districts without replacements."
