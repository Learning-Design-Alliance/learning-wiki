## Evidence

### Cerezo 2019

Cerezo, R., Bogarín, A., & Romero, C. (2019). Process Mining for self-regulated learning assessment in eLearning. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/s12528-019-09225-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-019-09225-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Process discovery comparison of Inductive Miner models for Pass (73 students) and Fail (28 students) groups. The article reports that "the Pass cluster performed actions related to forum-supported collaborative learning which do not appear in the model from the Fail cluster", a finding the authors align with prior forum-achievement research.

> "Additionally, the model let us detect that the Pass cluster performed actions related to forum-supported collaborative learning which do not appear in the model from the Fail cluster."
