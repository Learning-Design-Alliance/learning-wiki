Real-setting Mini-CEX evaluation two weeks post-course in the same RCT (Table 8): total score 64.81 ± 10.57 versus 52.66 ± 12.89 (t = 5.627, P < 0.001). Items 4-C (emergency endoscopic/surgical decision) and 6-A (vital-sign monitoring) showed no significant difference; no effect sizes are printed.
