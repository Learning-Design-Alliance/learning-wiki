## Evidence

### van Sassen C 2025

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on feedback focus in the 62-supervisor vignette experiment: nearly all feedback was process-focused ("grand mean proportion of process-related idea units, 0.97"), with small significant effects of outcome (η² = 0.12) and valence (η² = 0.08); the interaction was nonsignificant.

> "Nearly all feedback addressed process-related aspects (grand mean proportion of process-related idea units, 0.97; 95% CI, 0.95–0.98). Both vignette clinical outcome and feedback valence significantly affected the proportion of process-focused ideas (F1,61 = 8.48, P = .005, η2 = 0.12, and F1,61 = 5.51, P = .02, η2 = 0.08, respectively)."
