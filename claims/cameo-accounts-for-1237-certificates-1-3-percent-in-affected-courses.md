## Evidence

### Northcutt 2015

Northcutt, C. G., Ho, A. D., & Chuang, I. L. (2015). Detecting and Preventing “Multiple-Account” Cheating in Massive Open Online Courses.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of clickstream data from 1,893,092 enrollments in 115 edX courses (fall 2012–spring 2015). The authors report that "a total of 1,237 certificates were earned using the CAMEO strategy" by 657 unique users with 674 harvester accounts.

> "Across these courses, we estimate that a total of 1,237 certificates were earned using the CAMEO strategy, 1% across all 115 courses, by 657 unique users employing 674 harvester accounts."
