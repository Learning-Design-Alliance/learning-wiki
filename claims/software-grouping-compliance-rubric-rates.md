## Evidence

### Securro 2010

Securro, S., Jr., Jones, J. D., & Cantrell, D. R. (2010). Effects of Extensive Engagement with Computer-Based Reading and Language Arts Instructional Software on Reading Achievement for Sixth Graders. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102789

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Chi Square Test of Independence with Yates Correction comparing proportions of the 85 sixth graders in compliance versus noncompliance Westest rubric categories by grouping. The article reports the printed effect size phi = .39 and the 19% versus 60% noncompliance contrast.

> "These results indicated a significant association between compliance status and method of instruction, c²(1, n = 85) = 11.4, p =.000, phi= .39.  Approximately 19 % of those in the computer-based groupings did not meet compliance compared to nearly 60 % of those in the control groups."
