### Taehyun Kim 2025

Taehyun Kim, Mike Tissenbaum. (2025). Understanding when anger becomes productive or destructive in collaborative educational games. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09459-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative episode analysis of Red City's first gameplay episode in the City Settlers simulation. Verbal discourse coding showed an angry exchange preceding real-world reasoning: the student "began to draw connections between the virtual game mechanism and phenomena occurring in the real world."

> "Sofia started to get angry at Noah for mocking her strategy and began to draw connections between the virtual game mechanism and phenomena occurring in the real world to prove the validity of her strategy."
