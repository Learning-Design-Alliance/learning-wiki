Summary of the item exposure analysis of the empirical comparability study, as stated in the report's executive summary of findings. The article attributes the lower CBE Reading item-use rate to the passage item selection algorithm difference and the much smaller CBE sample, leaving more items than students at the lower end of the scale.

> "Item exposure rates are comparable, with most items having an item exposure rate below 10%. The CBE Reading item-use rate is lower than COLO’s. This difference was due to (1) the differences in the passage item selection algorithm and (2) the CBE sample size being much smaller than COLO’s."
