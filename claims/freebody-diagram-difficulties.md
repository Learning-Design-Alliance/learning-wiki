### Sanjoy Mahajan 2005

Sanjoy Mahajan. (2005). Observations on teaching first-year physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Tutorial evidence with Figure 1: students solved the ride problem correctly using F = mv2/r but their diagrams showed confusion. Only when asked what object causes the centrifugal force did they realize it labels an actual force.

> "Students insert the centrifugal force, because some force is ‘throwing the person outwards (which is why you feel pressed against the wa ll).’"
