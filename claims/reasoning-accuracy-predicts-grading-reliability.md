### Jonah L. Donaldson 2026

Jonah L. Donaldson, Aliya Nawaz, Konstantinos Doran, Alysta Lim, and Mario Campanelli. (2026). Using Large Language Models in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.23660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.23660)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis across the small set of evaluated models, relating each model's solution-generation accuracy to its grading error on imperfect handwritten scripts. The article reports r = −0.677 (R2 = 0.459) for text-based accuracy, and a weaker r = −0.498 (R² = 0.248) for diagram-based accuracy.

> "When the solution accuracy for text-based questions is considered, the relationship strengthens considerably (R2 = 0.459,r=−0.677). Here, approximately 46% of the variance in grading error can be associated with differences in reasoning performance."
