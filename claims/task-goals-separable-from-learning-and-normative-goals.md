## Evidence

### Daumiller 2019

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the CFA model comparisons, all other (less differentiated) models did not sufficiently fit the data; the separated task approach and task avoidance goals correlated moderately with learning and normative classes (ρ = .30–.61).

> "Also for task goals (Hypothesis 1c), an adequate model fit was only realized when modelling them separately from learning and normative goals, and when additionally differing between approach and avoidance components."
