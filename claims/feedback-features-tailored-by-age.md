## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Scaffolded feedback features (prompts/explanations, audio symbols, physical demonstrations) were concentrated in younger children ages three to seven, and written or paper-based feedback was provided only for children ages eight and older. [→ Fyfe Emily R. 2023](#fyfe-emily-r-2023)

## Evidence

### Fyfe Emily R. 2023

Fyfe Emily R., Borriello Giulia, Merrick Megan. (2023). A developmental perspective on feedback: How corrective feedback influences children's literacy, mathematics, and problem solving. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2022.2108426

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Feature coding of 60 unique feedback conditions across the 44 papers: audio signals and physical demonstrations appeared "only reported in papers with younger samples (ages three to seven)," suggesting feedback for younger children is made more perceptually salient.

> "Two modalities - audio feedback signals (e.g., ascending tones) and physical feedback demonstrations - were only reported in papers with younger samples (ages three to seven)."
