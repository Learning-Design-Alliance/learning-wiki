## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret their equivalent cross-condition outcomes as suggesting the learning activity may matter more for student learning than the instructional modality. [→ J. H. Madden 2019](#j-h-madden-2019)

## Evidence

### J. H. Madden 2019

J. H. Madden, A. S. Won, J. P. Schuldt, B. Kim, S. Pandita, Y. Sun, T. J. Stone, and N. G. Holmes. (2019). Virtual Reality as a Teaching Tool for Moon Phases and Beyond. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.madden](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.madden)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: because VR did not help on struggling topic areas any better than traditional methods, they suggest the learning activity may be the stronger factor than modality. No effect size is printed for this interpretation.

> "Learning in these areas may improve with alternate teaching methods since the results also suggest that the learning activity may be the stronger factor for student learning than the modality of instruction."
