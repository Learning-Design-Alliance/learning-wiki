Authors' observation from the reviewed corpus: "We did not detect any standard for providing access to games"; few games were freely available and none were promoted by prestigious publishing houses.

"We did not detect any standard for providing access to games. Some developers publish the URL to their game and try to sell it; few developers upload games for free public use, and most of them do not explain how to get access to their games."
