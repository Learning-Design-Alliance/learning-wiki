## Evidence

### Singh Veena Kumari 2025

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the same pre-post questionnaire (n = 52) using Z-score on McNemar's test. The article reports "The student's understanding of 12 out of 15 questions showed enhancement" and highlights items improving at p < 0.01, such as the most important CT step (13.4% to 84.6% correct).

> "The student's understanding of 12 out of 15 questions showed enhancement. The students' median score increased from 17 to 23 out of 27, indicating an improvement (p < 0.05) in their overall median score post-course."

### Singh Veena Kumari 2025 (2)

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same item-level pre-post analysis. The article reports that items on "the relation between confirmation and anchoring bias" and communicator competence showed no significant change (p > 0.05), and uncertainty persisted on two items post-course.

> "However, understanding of certain questions like the relation between confirmation and anchoring bias, and how to judge the competence of an effective communicator was already adequate (p > 0.05). For a few questions, such as judging the competence of an effective communicator and the optimal time to highlight critical terms and concepts, uncertainties persisted among the students"
