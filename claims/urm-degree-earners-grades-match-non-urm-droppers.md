Observational analysis of institutional records, GPA trends by race/ethnicity in Figs. 9c and 9d. The article reports URM degree-earners "earn on average the same grades as the Asian and White students who drop the major", a trend the authors call troubling.

> "Moreover, among students in the remaining STEM majors (biological sciences, economics, geology and environmental science, neuroscience, and statistics; Figs. 9c and 9d), the URM students who persist and earn a degree earn on average the same grades as the Asian and White students who drop the major."
