## Evidence

### Jacobs 2025

Jacobs, M., Haelermans, C., & Meeter, M. (2025). Effective remediation programs for vulnerable students to overcome learning loss. PLoS One, 20(5). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323352

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DiD analyses interacting participation with program group-size characteristics (Fig 3; S11 Table), on the same 66,439-student sample. The article reports timing and organization showed no relationship with learning growth, while group size, goals and support type did.

> "The results show that students in individual remediation programs have significant positive increases in their test scores for reading comprehension compared to non-participants. For mathematics, on the other hand, programs in small groups, approximately between 2 and 5 students, have positive effects on test scores."
