Systematic review synthesis of studies using semi-structured interviews. The review reports that "non-interviewed candidates had a higher likelihood of failing a communication-based OSCE" in one study, while other studies found similar communication skill ratings; no effect sizes were printed.

> "In one study, non-interviewed candidates had a higher likelihood of failing a communication-based OSCE;23 however, other studies showed interviewed and non-interviewed students had similar clinical communication skill ratings in medical school using either OSCEs23 or a novel and reliable assessment of clinical communication skills.25"
