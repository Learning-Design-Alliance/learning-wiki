### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview data on the QMCS tunneling question: the article reports that this question elicited signiﬁcant cognitive dissonance, as students struggled to reconcile two contradictory ideas about energy loss and energy conservation.

> "In many cases, this question elicited signiﬁcant cognitive dissonance, as students struggled to reconcile two contradictory ideas: that energy is lost, and that kinetic plus potential equals total."

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview data on the tunneling question: students who thought energy is lost typically lacked a clear idea of which energy is lost; the article reports that often a single student would use two or even all three types of energy interchangeably within the same explanation.
