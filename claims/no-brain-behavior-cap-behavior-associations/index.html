Exploratory brain-behavior correlation analyses. For example, the pre-to-post increase in CAP-6 temporal fraction was not significantly associated with increased FCI accuracy, and CAP-5 changes were not associated with PK accuracy or reaction time changes.

"Despite these behavioral improvements across time, no signiﬁcant associations were found between the p re-to-post-instruction behavioral changes and the corresponding changes in relevant temporal dynamic metrics"
