## Evidence

### Mayer Sveta 2025

Mayer Sveta, Dumontheil Iroise, Wilkinson Hannah R., Porayska-Pomsta Kaska, Farran Emily K., Tolmie Andrew K., Mareschal Denis, the UnLocke Team. (2025). SEE+ computerized classroom-based training enhances 7- to 10-year-olds' socio-emotional cognition through observation and inference. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0330934

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed model repeated measures ANOVA in a large-scale RCT (analysed n = 5585; Year 3 SEE+ n = 808, S&T n = 1299, TAU n = 631; Year 5 SEE+ n = 721, S&T n = 1485, TAU n = 641) showed a main effect of training condition; SEE+ "were more accurate than children in the Stop & Think" and TAU conditions, with controls not differing. The score difference (0.117) versus the Year 3–Year 5 difference (0.471) suggested "the equivalent of 6 months improvement"; the multilevel model gave a slightly lower estimate of 4 months.

> "children in the SEE+ condition (M = 4.282, SE = 0.022) were more accurate than children in the Stop & Think (M = 4.166, SE = 0.016, p = .007) and TAU conditions (M = 4.164, SE = 0.024, p = .004), while the two control groups did not differ (p = 1.0)"
