### Hongwen Guo 2020

Hongwen Guo, Mo Zhang, Paul Deane, Randy E. Bennett. (2020). Effects of Scenario-Based Assessment on Students’ Writing Processes. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/443

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample Wilcoxon rank tests comparing Group 1 and Group 24 on box-plot summaries (typing speed, essay score, essay time, word count); the article reports essay scores were comparable while “the mean total essay writing time and the mean total number of words” were significantly smaller for Group 1. Only significance, no effect size, is printed.
