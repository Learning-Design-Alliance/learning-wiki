## Evidence

### Natto Zuhair S 2026

Natto Zuhair S. (2026). Blended peer-led research curriculum with AI integration improves postgraduate students’ academic performance and satisfaction: a quasi-experimental mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08576-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two course sections (intervention n=26, control n=26) using an independent t-test on a 5-point Likert satisfaction survey. The t-test showed significantly higher satisfaction, "t(48.27) = 2.08, p = 0.043"; no effect size was printed.

> "Overall satisfaction was significantly higher in the intervention group (M = 4.35, SD = 1.56) than the control group (M = 3.35, SD = 1.89), t(48.27) = 2.08, p = 0.043, 95% CI [0.06 to 1.94]."
