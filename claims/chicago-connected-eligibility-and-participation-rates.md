### Schachner 2025

Schachner, J.N., Marwell, N.P., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J.A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2025). How did Chicago Connected broadband access eligibility and participation affect remote learning engagement and achievement during the COVID-19 pandemic? Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1153

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive participation analysis of the 77,056-student analytic sample. Eligibility was "based on their level of socioeconomic disadvantage," and approximately 30% of eligible students opted in, receiving a sponsored wired connection and/or hotspot device.

> "About 70% of 5th-8th grade students continuously enrolled in non-charter CPS schools since fall 2017 were deemed eligible in summer or fall 2020 for Chicago Connected, based on their level of socioeconomic disadvantage."
