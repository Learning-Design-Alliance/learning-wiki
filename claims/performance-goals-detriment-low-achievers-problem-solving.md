# Performance goals reduce the number of problems low achievers solve, but do not affect medium or high achievers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Low achievers solved fewer problems under performance goals than learning goals, with no goal effect for medium or high achievers. [→ Newman 1995](#newman-1995)

## Evidence

### Newman 1995

Richard S. Newman and Mahna T. Schwager. (1995). Students' Help Seeking During Problem Solving: Effects of Grade, Goal, and Prior Achievement. American Educational Research Journal, 32(2), 352-376. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1163435

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Day 3 performance MANOVA (grade x goal x achievement x problem) showed a goal x achievement interaction, F(2,106) = 4.78, p < .01; within the performance-goal condition only, low achievers did significantly worse than high achievers, Scheffé test, p < .05.

> "The goal X achievement interaction was explained by the fact that there was a detrimental effect for performance goals, in comparison with learning goals, among low achievers (Ms = 5.3 and 8.0) but not among medium (Ms = 6.9 and 6.8) or high achievers (Ms = 7.8 and 7.7)."
