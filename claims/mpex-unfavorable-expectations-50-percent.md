## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated MPEX 2 survey administration across several semesters of the Descriptive Physics course population. The article adds that the high number of neutral responses is consistent with observations of other non-science-major courses at the University of Maine and high school courses, and that informal results show students expect the mathematical nature of the course.

> "Based on several semesters of data using the Maryland Physics Expectations Survey 2 (MPEX 2),7, 8 we find that students typically enter the course having roughly 50% unfavorable and 25% favorable expectations about conceptual learning. These scores are substantially lower than a typical introductory class, as measured by the original MPEX."
