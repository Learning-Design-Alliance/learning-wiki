Evidence¶

Eitel 2025¶

Eitel, A., Krebs, M.-C., Schöne, C. (2025). Introducing the FoRe-Squares Model: Focus Support and Reward as Key Predictors of Effective Technology-Augmented Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10045-z

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Tentative support for Hypothesis 2: studies found signaling informed students about the irrelevance of seductive details and "fostered learning outcomes to a similar degree" as weeding them, so applying one of the two SOI-fostering techniques sufficed. The review argues piling on more techniques fostering the same process adds nothing.

"Studies found that signaling by means of informing students about the irrelevance of seductive details fostered learning outcomes to a similar degree than weeding the instruction by eliminating seductive details"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Weeding seductive details fostered learning only in a mandatory (high-reward) learning situation, not a voluntary one — reports the opposite
  • Techniques raising both focus support and reward simultaneously are predicted to foster learning best; content-irrelevant humor is predicted not to — related
  • Techniques yield learning gains only when they compensate for otherwise too-low focus support or reward (baseline-levels condition) — related
  • Stronger reward manipulations are predicted to yield learning gains with higher likelihood (magnitude-of-change condition) — related
  • An irrelevance prompt eliminates the recall cost of seductive details, supporting the diversion explanation — related
