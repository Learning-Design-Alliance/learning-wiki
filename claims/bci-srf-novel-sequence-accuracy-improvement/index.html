Yuan Liu 2024¶

Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-design ANOVA of control sequence accuracy at Pre, Post, and FU in 20 healthy right-handed participants randomized to BCI-SRF or Finger training. "Signiﬁcant difference in CS score (Post, p = 0.0016; FU, p = 0.0139)" was observed between groups, with the BCI-SRF group improving from 0.815 ± 0.08 to 0.919 ± 0.06 and the reported improvement ratio being 350%.
