Reliability analysis of the rater-blinded controlled trial: two trained, blinded raters independently scored video recordings; ICCs were classified per the article's thresholds, with ICCs of >0.9 regarded as excellent, supporting reproducible assessment.

> "An excellent interrater reliability was observed for the H-OSATS score, with an ICC of 0.91 (P<.001), and a good interrater reliability was observed for the OSATS score, with an ICC of 0.81 (P<.001)."
