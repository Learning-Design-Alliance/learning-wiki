## Evidence

### Vegada Bhavisha 2016

Vegada Bhavisha, Shukla Apexa, Khilnani Ajeetkumar, Charan Jaykaran, Desai Chetna. (2016). Comparison between three option, four option and five option multiple choice question tests for quality parameters: A randomized study. Indian Journal of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.4103/0253-7613.190757

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized study of 132 second professional M.B.B.S. students assigned to three-, four-, or five-option versions of the same 30-item ANS pharmacology test. The results report "Students score more (P = 0.000)" and less time (P = 0.009) for the three-option group; mean times were 32.80, 35.98, and 35.65 min.

> "Students score more (P = 0.000) and took less time (P = 0.009) for the completion of three options as compared to four options and five options groups."
