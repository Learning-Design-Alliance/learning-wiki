## Evidence

### Giese Julian 2026

Giese Julian, Ehlers Jan P., Weichert Olaf, Nitsche Julia. (2026). Effect of repeated exposure to an identical multi-user virtual reality anaphylaxis scenario on paramedic trainee performance: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-69133-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 125 German paramedic trainees in dyadic VR simulations; between-group comparison on the common VR2 assessment via unpaired t-test. The article reports "mean difference 11.5 points, 95% CI, 9.8 to 13.3, t(123) = 13.4, p < 0.001, d = 2.39".

> "The mean total score was 42.6 points in VR-I compared with 31.1 points in VR-C (maximum 62 points; mean difference 11.5 points, 95% CI, 9.8 to 13.3, t(123) = 13.4, p < 0.001, d = 2.39)."
