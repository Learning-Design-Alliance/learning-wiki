## Evidence

### Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor 2026

Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor, Komanek Thomas, Grundmann Carla Davina, Dietrich-Kiep Franziska, Wischermann Jan, Frey Ulrich Hermann. (2026). Blended e-learning with handheld ultrasound devices improves practical competence in eFAST: a randomised controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09054-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariable linear regression with the modified OSAUS total score as dependent variable, adjusted for age, semester, prior ultrasound experience, prior medical training and tutor assignment. The printed coefficient is an unstandardised B, so no standardised effect size is reported.

> "Linear regression revealed a mean increase of 3.29 OSAUS points for the intervention (95% CI 1.95–4.64, p < 0.001)."

### Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor 2026 (2)

Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor, Komanek Thomas, Grundmann Carla Davina, Dietrich-Kiep Franziska, Wischermann Jan, Frey Ulrich Hermann. (2026). Blended e-learning with handheld ultrasound devices improves practical competence in eFAST: a randomised controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09054-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on the binary outcome "complete eFAST achieved" using the same covariate set; the adjusted odds ratio was 2.52 (95% CI 1.27–4.98).

> "Logistic regression indicated increased odds of completing a full eFAST (aOR 2.52, 95% CI 1.27–4.98, p = 0.008)."
