# The negative expressivity trajectory predicted first-grade school engagement for children with lower or average kindergarten engagement, but not for children with higher kindergarten engagement

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hernández 2018

Hernández, M. M., Eisenberg, N., Valiente, C., Thompson, M. S., Spinrad, T. L., Grimm, K. J., VanSchyndel, S. K., Berger, R. H., Silva, K. M., Pina, A. A., Southworth, J., & Gal, D. E. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple-slopes analysis of the slope × K engagement interaction (b23 = 12.62, p = .06) in latent moderated structural modeling. The interaction model had better AIC (ΔAIC = −3.89) and BIC (ΔBIC = −.19).

> "the negative expressivity slope was negatively associated with school engagement in G1 for children with low (i.e., 1 SD below the mean; b2 = −6.9, p < .001) and average school engagement in kindergarten (b2 = −4.68, p = .01). The negative expressivity slope was not associated with school engagement in G1 for children with high initial school engagement (i.e., 1 SD above the mean; b2 = −2.46, p = .42)."
