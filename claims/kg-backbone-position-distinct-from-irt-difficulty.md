`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Network analysis of the knowledge graph built from partial correlations among 100 KPs. The article reports the main component "comprised 58 nodes, with 29 KPs incorporated into the final IRM".

> "The primary subnetwork within the backbone structure, identified as the main component, comprised 58 nodes, with 29 KPs incorporated into the final IRM. This main component captured 66 out of the 68 connections in the backbone structure."
