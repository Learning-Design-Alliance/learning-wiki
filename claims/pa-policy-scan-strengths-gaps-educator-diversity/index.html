The policy scan's Strategy 1 criteria table reports Pennsylvania meets one or no requirements on sharing school-level workforce demographics and lacks publicly available race/ethnicity-disaggregated persistence data, though PDE's Educator Workforce Strategy includes provisions to make disaggregated data publicly available.

The Education Trust 2022 (2)¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: Pennsylvania. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

Under Strategy 5, the scan rates Pennsylvania green on induction and mentoring, and green on residency investment: it uses Title II, Part A funds to expand residency programs through the Innovative Teacher and Principal Residency Programs Grant.

"Pennsylvania provides a two-year induction and mentoring program for new teachers, effective April 2022."
