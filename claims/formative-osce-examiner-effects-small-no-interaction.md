## Evidence

### Möltner Andreas 2020

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Generalizability-theory variance decomposition of the formative OSCE General Medicine 2018 scores with facets student, station, examiner and station x examiner (Table 5). The article reports the printed percentage shares of variance components.

> "Nearly 53% of the variance can be explained by the effects of the model, with 22% attributable to differences between students in terms of performance. The variability of the stations accounts for 21%, while the combined examiner influence was around 10%."

### Möltner Andreas 2020 (2)

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results of the generalizability-theory model for the formative OSCE: the station x examiner interaction variance component was not distinguishable from zero. The Discussion notes examiner effects "tend to be even lower than in the comparison OSCEs".

> "The interaction effect station x examiner was not detectable or significantly different from 0."
