# No significant difference in distinction grades by teaching methodology

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There is no significant relation between teaching methodology and obtaining a distinction mark (Fisher exact test, p = .999). [→ Torres-Martín 2022](#torres-martin-2022)

## Evidence

### Torres-Martín 2022

Torres-Martín, C., Acal, C., El-Homrani, M., & Mingorance-Estrada, Á. C. (2022). Implementation of the flipped classroom and its longitudinal impact on improving academic performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10095-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher exact test on the Distinction (A) grade band for 522 traditional and 635 flipped classroom students (Table 6). The article reports "no relation between getting a distinction mark or not depending on the teaching method applied", with p = .999. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There is no relation between getting a distinction mark or not depending on the teaching method applied. That is because the p value is higher than the usual level of significance."
