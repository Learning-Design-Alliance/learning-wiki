## Evidence

### Katherine N. Quinn 2018

Katherine N. Quinn, Carl Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2018). Interview validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative think-aloud interview study of 12 students completing the closed-response PLIC individually with an interviewer, audio-recorded, taking between 18 and 45 minutes. Detailed notes were compiled and examined to determine underlying themes and common behaviors, yielding the three patterns described as "selecting all options, cuing on keywords, and critically analyzing".

> "We describe a trend that has emerged from these interviews, with students' reasoning falling into three main patterns of behavior: selecting all options, cuing on keywords, and critically analyzing."
