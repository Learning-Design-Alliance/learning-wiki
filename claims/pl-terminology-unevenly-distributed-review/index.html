q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review results section reporting term usage across the final corpus of 55 studies screened from 861 papers in nine educational technology journals. The review reports that "Personalized learning accounted for just over half of the studies (28; 50.9%)" with the other five terms far less frequent.

"Personalized learning accounted for just over half of the studies (28; 50.9%), followed by adaptive learning (13; 23.6%) and intelligent tutoring (9; 16.4%). In contrast, individualized instruction (1; 1.8%), customized learning (2; 3.6%), and personalized adaptive learning (2; 3.6%) appeared infrequently."
