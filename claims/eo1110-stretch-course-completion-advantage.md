# Second-semester stretch courses had higher completion rates than other course types for B4 across all placement categories and for A2 in Categories III and IV, but not Category II; corequisite and traditional course completion rates did not differ significantly

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` B4 courses classified as second semester of stretch courses had higher completion rates than other course types for all three QR placement categories. [→ Bracco 2020](#bracco-2020)
`q2 i?` For A2, second-semester stretch completion rates were significantly higher than other course types only for WC Categories III and IV, not Category II, and corequisite versus traditional differences were not significant in any category. [→ Bracco 2020 (2)](#bracco-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Bracco 2020

Bracco, K., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2020). Student progress under California State University's Executive Order 1110: A systemwide analysis of outcomes in 2018/19. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613408

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Course-record analysis of B4 completion rates by course type and QR placement category (Figure 12), using Bonferroni-adjusted Least Squares Means. Second-semester stretch courses had higher completion rates across all three categories, though relatively few students took them.

> "B4 courses classified as second semester of stretch courses had higher completion rates than other types of courses for all three placement categories"

### Bracco 2020 (2)

Bracco, K., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2020). Student progress under California State University's Executive Order 1110: A systemwide analysis of outcomes in 2018/19. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613408

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Course-record analysis of A2 completion rates by course type and WC placement category (Figure 11). Stretch-semester rates were "significantly higher than for the other course types for students placed in WC Categories III and IV, but the difference was not significant for students placed in Category II."

> "The completion rates of courses classified as second semester of stretch courses were significantly higher than for the other course types for students placed in WC Categories III and IV, but the difference was not significant for students placed in Category II"
