## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, M., Chen, Z., Han, N., & Yao, H. (2025). Sleep duration and subject-specific academic performance among adolescents in China. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00361-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression on the observational dataset of Shanghai eighth-graders who provided both sleep and performance data (valid sample 54,102), across Chinese, math, English, science and art. The article reports "the squared term of sleep duration is signiﬁcantly negative (p < 0.001)" for all subjects.

> "sleep duration is signiﬁcantly and positively associated with academic achievement in all sub- jects (p < 0.001), while the squared term of sleep duration is signiﬁcantly negative (p < 0.001), indicating an inverted U-shaped relationship"
