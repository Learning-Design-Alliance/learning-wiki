### Emslander 2022

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analysis of VA scores across domains and time in the same 151-school Luxembourg dataset. Within-year mathematics–language correlations were "r = .59 in 2017 and r = .47 in 2019", larger than the over-time within-domain correlations reported in the same Results section.

> "the school VA scores in mathematics and language within the same years were moderately to highly correlated with correlation coefficients of r = .59 in 2017 and r = .47 in 2019."
