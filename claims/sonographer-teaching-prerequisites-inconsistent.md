# Prerequisites for sonographers teaching fellows were inconsistent across respondents and did not correlate with attitudes or teaching confidence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reported prerequisites varied (skills assessment 42%, certification 39%, time 36%, none 36%, observation 22%, didactics 11%) and did not correlate with total IPAS (r = −0.09, p = 0.60), MCTQ average rating (r = −0.01, p = 0.97), or teaching rating (r = 0.02, p = 0.90). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results from 45 sonographer respondents at four centers; the article separately reports that sonographer-reported prerequisites did not correlate with IPAS or MCTQ scores. Percentages are as printed.

> "Prerequisites for sonographers teaching fellows were competency-based skills assessment (42%), certification (39%), time in position (36%), none (36%), teaching observation (22%), and education-related didactics (11%)."
