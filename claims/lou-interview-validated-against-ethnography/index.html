Second validity comparison in the same ethnographic study, between consensus ratings by two readers of the written ethnographic protocols and the consensus interview rating, showing weaker agreement than the ethnographer comparison.

"The coefficient for the second comparison was .65, which lent support to the validity of the interview, although at the same time revealing the difficulty involved in conveying sufficient information second hand"

Discussion¶

The recorded entry is the second of the study's validity comparisons, against two readers' consensus ratings of the ethnographers' written protocols (r = .65). The first comparison, against the ethnographers' own ratings from full-day observation, is described in the entry only as stronger; its coefficient is not recorded on this page.
