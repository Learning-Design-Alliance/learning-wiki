`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey-based evaluation (Section 5.1): 7 learners from diverse national backgrounds completed a 12-question pre-survey, at least one full learning session, and a 14-question post-survey. Responses are descriptive; no statistical tests or effect sizes are reported. Qualitative comments included "I have noticed an improvement in the reduction of grammatical errors."
