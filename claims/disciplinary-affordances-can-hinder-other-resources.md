## Evidence

### Nandana Weliweriya 2019

Nandana Weliweriya, Eleanor C. Sayre, Dean Zollman. (2019). Case Study: Coordinating Among Multiple Semiotic Resources to Solve Complex Physics Problems. arXiv. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Episode 1 case analysis: after moving his argument onto the board diagram, Larry repeatedly changed the orientation of his right-hand gesture until he added the current direction using arrow as vector. The analysis is observational, drawn from transcript and video coding of the oral exam.

> "Here the semiotic resource coordinate system hinders the use of gestural semiotic resource right-hand grip rule without a specific current direction."

### Nandana Weliweriya 2019 (2)

Nandana Weliweriya, Eleanor C. Sayre, Dean Zollman. (2019). Case Study: Coordinating Among Multiple Semiotic Resources to Solve Complex Physics Problems. arXiv. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Episode 3 analysis: as Larry moved to mathematical formulas to find the field magnitude, the authors observe him switching from gesturing the loop to drawing square as loop on the board, since "just the hand gesture is not helpful in determining the current piercing the loop".

> "The inclusion of mathematical formulas constrains the use of semiotic resource hand for loop."
