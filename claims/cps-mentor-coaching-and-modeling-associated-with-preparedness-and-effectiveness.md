# Student teachers who perceived their mentors modeled effective instruction and provided coaching, feedback, and job assistance felt better prepared, and domain-specific coaching was related to higher first-year REACH ratings

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student teachers who perceived their mentors modeled effective teaching practices felt better prepared. [→ Gordon 2018](#gordon-2018)
`q2 i?` Mentors' coaching with constructive feedback, domain-specific support, a safe space for risk, and job search assistance was associated with greater preparedness. [→ Gordon 2018 (2)](#gordon-2018-2)
`q2 i?` When mentors coached student teachers in domain-specific instructional areas, the student teachers were more effective in their first year. [→ Gordon 2018 (3)](#gordon-2018-3)

## Evidence

### Gordon 2018

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the 2014-15 student teacher survey data, perceptions mattered: when student teachers "perceived their mentor teachers modeled effective teaching practices, student teachers felt better prepared" at the end of student teaching.

> "However, when student teachers perceived their mentor teachers modeled effective teaching practices, student teachers felt better prepared."

### Gordon 2018 (2)

Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey responses from the 2014-15 cohort showed student teachers were "much more likely to feel prepared when they reported their mentor teachers coached them by providing constructive feedback and domain-specific targeted support," along with a safe space for risk and job search assistance.

> "student teachers were much more likely to feel prepared when they reported their mentor teachers coached them by providing constructive feedback and domain-specific targeted support"

### Gordon 2018 (3)

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linking mentor coaching reports to first-year REACH ratings for hired student teachers, "when mentors said they coached their student teachers in domain specific areas of instruction, student teachers were more effective in their first year."

> "Specifically, when mentors said they coached their student teachers in domain specific areas of instruction, student teachers were more effective in their first year."
