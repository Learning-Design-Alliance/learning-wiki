Kuo, E., Hull, M. M., Elby, A., & Gupta, A. (2019). Mathematical Sensemaking as Seeking Coherence between Calculations and Concepts: Instruction and Assessments for Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.05596](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.05596)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theoretical argument from the literature review: quantitative step-wise problem-solving assessments miss conceptual shortcuts that bypass the execute-the-solution step, and qualitative questions do not specify whether students use calculation skills. The article argues assessment paradigms "are not designed to capture this reasoning." This is an argued position, not a measured result.

> "a key difficulty for instructional evaluation is that current assessment paradigms in physics education research are not designed to capture this reasoning, leaving these mathematical sensemaking-related learning outcomes usually undetected and undocumented."
