### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The phenomenographic analysis identified the Broader Context family, discussed by 17 of 24 participants: program-level assessment (10 faculty) on assessing progress through and at the end of programs, assessment pressures (16 faculty) primarily from accreditation requirements including ABET, and challenges with faculty buy-in (5 faculty).

> "Faculty consider their courses in a broader context of their departments and accountability and face issues and want help with assessment in this context."
