q2 · i3 · design · r2

Cohort-level correlational analysis (n = 279) of mid-course questionnaire measures against topic understanding (Q5). The article reports r = 0.427 for self-efficacy (i = 3 by the correlation code table), r = 0.369 for RTQ Understanding, and r = 0.168 for help-seeking, with p < .001, < .001, and .005 respectively.

"Self-efficacy showed the strongest association with topic understanding (𝑟= 0.427 ), followed by RTQ Understanding (𝑟= 0.369 ) and help-seeking behavior (𝑟= 0.168 )."
