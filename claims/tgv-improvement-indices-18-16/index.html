q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The WWC computed improvement indices based on effect sizes, representing the difference in percentile rank between the average intervention-condition student and the average comparison-condition student. The report states "The average improvement index is +18 percentile points for behavior and +16 percentile points for knowledge, attitudes, and values."
