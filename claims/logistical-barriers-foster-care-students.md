# Teachers report logistical barriers — transportation, weekly supply shopping, limited computer access, and missing basic supplies — that complicate school participation for students in foster care

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers reported that transportation constraints, weekly group-home supply shopping, limited access to common-area computers, and students arriving without basic supplies complicate school participation and after-school activity involvement. The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning. (2010). Ready to Succeed in the Classroom: Findings from Teacher Discussion Groups on their Experiences and Aspirations Teaching Students in the Foster Care System. Prepared for the Stuart Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED511840

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative finding from the Logistical Barriers section. Teachers described students showing up without pencils or in dirty clothes; hygiene issues sometimes drew harsh peer comments, exacerbating fears of not fitting in. One teacher bought backpacks at garage sales for students who needed them.

> "Transportation is often an issue, complicating participation in after-school activities (e.g., if a group home van makes just one trip from school to home each day)."
