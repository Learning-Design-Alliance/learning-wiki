## Evidence

### Adriana Postiglione 2022

Adriana Postiglione, Ilaria De Angelis. (2022). Introducing General Relativity in high school: a guide for teachers. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-06193-6_15](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-06193-6_15)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/ post/ delayed questionnaires administered to about 200 students across 9 classes (154 answers collected) exploring reactions and comprehension of topics addressed with the RSA activity. The article reports the phenomena were "visualized better, understood and remembered longer compared t o a traditional treatment"; no effect size is printed.
