## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants travelled significantly shorter distances in wayfinding with VGLs, while response-time differences between conditions were not significant. [→ Jia Liu 2022](#jia-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Jia Liu 2022

Jia Liu, Avinash Kumar Singh, Anna Wunderlich, Klaus Gramann and Chin-Teng Lin. (2022). Redesigning navigational aids using virtual global landmarks to improve spatial knowledge retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00132-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Wayfinding-task ANOVA comparing VGL and non-VGL trials in the same experiment. The article reports F1,26 = 26.939, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.509, a 10.104-metre (95% CI, 6.103 to 14.106) shorter distance and 43.0% shorter travelled distance with VGLs.

> "ANOVA showed a signiﬁcant difference in the distances travelled (F1,26 = 26.939, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.509) with a shorter distance participants travelled in VGL trials"

### Jia Liu 2022 (2)

Jia Liu, Avinash Kumar Singh, Anna Wunderlich, Klaus Gramann and Chin-Teng Lin. (2022). Redesigning navigational aids using virtual global landmarks to improve spatial knowledge retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00132-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same wayfinding task, when SBSOD scores were entered as a covariate due to a significant negative correlation (rs(27) = –.471, p = 0.013), the ANCOVA "did not reveal any statistical difference" in response times (F1,25 = 1.269, p = 0.271).

> "the ANOVA did not reveal any statistical difference between the response times for the two trial conditions (F1,25 = 1.269, p = 0.271, partial η2 = 0.048)"
