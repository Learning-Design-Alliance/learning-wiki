## Evidence

### Delibalta B 2026

Delibalta B, Güner Y, Üçüncüoğlu M, Türk Y, Dilbaz AD, Ateş E, Aykut DS. (2026). Role of self-regulated learning in a communication skills curriculum: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09417-y

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with eight students (five female, three male), each lasting approximately 35–40 minutes, analysed via six-step thematic analysis. Students reported initial confusion, then enjoyment; positive emotions dominated; many felt like a doctor for the first time.

> "Three themes emerged after the thematic analysis of individual interviews with students: (1) emotions, (2) feelings of being a doctor, and (3) interactions with simulated participants."
