Evidence¶

Foster 1910¶

Foster, W. S. (1910). The Effect of Practice upon Visualizing and upon the Reproduction of Visual Impressions. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075685

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Small-n laboratory study of three trained observers across about thirty series each; Table I reports period averages of percent correct and repetitions. The results section states gains "from 6% to 44%; average 22%"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"The percentages for all O's and for all kinds of material are considerably higher in the final than in the initial period. Gain in efficiency ranges from 6% to 44%; average 22%"
