## Evidence

### Hao Xia-ying 2025

Hao Xia-ying, He Yun, Zhang Dong-xu, Li Xian-yin. (2025). Teacher-focused approach to foster student engagement — a hierarchical linear model based on Chinese college students’ psychological capital. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03683-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intercept-as-outcomes HLM adding teacher-level ITTF and CCSF predictors for 67 teachers/classes; the article reports the printed γ01 and γ02 coefficients and concludes "student-focused" approaches "do not necessarily promote active student engagement".

> "the intercept for CCSF (γ01) is −0.07 (SE = 0.03, t=−2.56, p < 0.05), demonstrating a significant negative impact on student engagement. Conversely, the intercept for ITTF (γ02) is 0.09 (SE = 0.02, t = 4.23, p < 0.001), showing a positive predictive effect on student engagement."
