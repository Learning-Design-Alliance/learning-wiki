# Integrating POG assessments into traditional standardized-test-centered accountability systems is complex, and lack of a standardized framework can produce inconsistent measurement across regions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` POG competencies often require nuanced assessment methods not captured by state-mandated standardized tests, making integration into traditional accountability systems complex and difficult. [→ Terrell 2025](#terrell-2025)
`q2 i?` When assessment identification is left to individual districts or schools, the lack of a standardized statewide framework can lead to inconsistencies in how competencies are measured and difficulty tracking progress across regions. [→ Terrell 2025](#terrell-2025)
