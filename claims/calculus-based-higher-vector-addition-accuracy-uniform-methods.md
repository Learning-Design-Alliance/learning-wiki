## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, Michael C. Wittmann, Eleanor C. Sayre, Brian W. Frank. (2009). Students’ Responses To Different Representations Of A Vector Addition Question. 2009 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266745

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison administration reported in the conclusion: an identical study in the UMaine calculus-based course and a similar study at RIT with all four questions randomly distributed simultaneously. The article reports these studies "did not produce insightful data" beyond uniform correct responding.

> "In each of the calculus-based classes at both RIT and UMaine at least 85% of the responses received were correct, compared to 50% in the algebra-based class. The students who answered the questions correctly mostly did so using the same method and no significant differences in response patterns were seen."
