Jake Renzella 2026¶

Jake Renzella, Christian Bergh, Natasha Banks, and Alexandra Vassar. (2026). Gender Differences in AI Literacy Workshop Outcomes and Deepfake Engagement. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14718

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Within-gender pre–post OLS comparisons (outcome ~ phase + year level + school) on npost = 136 students. All four reported effects are medium by the article's thresholds, e.g. "d=.63" for male AI knowledge, all p<.001.

"Both genders showed statistically significant post-intervention gains in AI knowl- edge (Male:d=.63, medium; Female:d=.57, medium), and AI confidence (Male:d=.58, medium), (Female:d=.64, medium) (Table 3)."
