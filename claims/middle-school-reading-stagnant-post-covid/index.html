The brief's summary describes an analysis of MAP Growth National Dashboard data on post-COVID academic recovery, stating that "reading achievement among grades 6-8 remain stagnant" while math and elementary reading improved. No effect sizes or test statistics are printed in the available text.

"While students have shown encouraging progress in math and elementary reading since the pandemic, reading achievement among grades 6-8 remain stagnant, with the lowest-performing students falling even further behind."
