# The percentage of CPS elementary students whose ITBS scores count in accountability reporting fell from 82.3 percent in 1992 to 73.9 percent in 1999, with the lowest inclusion in third grade

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The share of tested students included in public ITBS reporting declined from 82.3 percent in 1992 to 73.9 percent in 1999, and almost one-third of third graders are not counted in the accountability system. [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)

## Evidence

### John Q. Easton 2000

John Q. Easton, Todd Rosenkranz, Anthony S. Bryk, Brian A. Jacob, Stuart Luppescu, Melissa Roderick. (2000). Annual CPS Test Trend Review, 1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Annual+CPS+Test+Trend+Review+1999

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of CPS administrative testing records for grades 3-8, 1992-1999 (Table A). The brief reports that "the percent of students included was down to 73.9" in 1999, and that in third grade "almost one-third of students are not counted in the accountability system." No effect size is printed.

> "In 1992, 82.3 percent of students were included in the public reporting of Iowa Tests of Basic Skills scores. In 1999, the most recent citywide test administration, the percent of students included was down to 73.9."
