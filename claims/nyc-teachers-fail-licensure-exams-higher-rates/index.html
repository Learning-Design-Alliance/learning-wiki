Barth 2000¶

Barth, Patte, Ed. (2000). Honor in the Boxcar: Equalizing Teacher Quality. Thinking K-16; v4 n1 Spr 2000. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED458350

q2 · i? · review · r1

The article reports New York licensure failure data (derived from a November 16, 1999 New York Times report), also shown in Table 7: 31% NYC versus 5% state on the liberal arts/science test, 47% versus 21% in mathematics, 27% versus 3% in elementary teaching skills. No standardized effect size is printed.

"Approximately one in three teachers employed in the last decade in New York City failed the main licensure examinationthe liberal arts and sciences testat least once. By contrast, fewer than one in 20 of the state's teachers outside of the city did so."
