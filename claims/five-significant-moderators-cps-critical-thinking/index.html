Xu 2023¶

Xu, E., Wang, W., & Wang, Q. (2023). The effectiveness of collaborative problem solving in promoting students' critical thinking: A meta-analysis based on empirical literature. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-023-01508-1

Subgroup moderator analysis of the 79 effect quantities across 36 studies (Table 4). Five of seven moderators showed "significant moderators" effects; only chi-square tests and p-values are printed, no effect size for the moderation itself.

"the subject area (chi 2 = 13.36, P < 0.05), group size (chi2 = 8.77, P < 0.05), intervention duration (chi 2 = 12.18, P < 0.01), learning scaffold (chi 2 = 9.03, P < 0.01), and teaching type (chi2 = 7.20, P < 0.05) are all significant moderators"

Related Claims¶

  • Teacher-supported learning scaffolds show the largest effect (ES = 0.92), and longer intervention durations yield larger effects — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Science-related subject areas (mathematics, science, medical science) show larger effects than programming technology and other fields — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Intervention duration significantly moderates Gen-AI's effect on HOT, with the strongest effects at 8–16 weeks (ES = 0.759) and weaker effects for shorter (0.494) and longer (0.372) interventions — related
  • Collaborative problem-solving improves attitudinal tendency (ES = 1.17) more than cognitive skills (ES = 0.70) — related
  • Subject discipline significantly moderates CSCL script effects on collaboration skills, while study duration and measurement level do not significantly moderate effects — related
  • Intervention duration does not significantly predict GenAI writing effect sizes, which the authors attribute to limited temporal diversity in the evidence base — related
  • Scaffolding's effect was highest in project-based learning contexts; design-based learning was the only context not significantly greater than zero — related
  • Collaborative problem-solving significantly improves students' critical thinking overall (ES = 0.82) — related
  • Dynamic (adaptive) scaffolding is linked to stronger effects of ICT-supported writing interventions — related
  • Cooperative and collaborative learning show larger achievement effects than problem-based and peer-led team learning in engineering and technology classrooms — related
