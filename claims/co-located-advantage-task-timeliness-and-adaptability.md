## Evidence

### Morian H 2024

Morian H, Hultin M, Lindkvist M, Creutzfeldt J, Dubois H, Jonsson K, Amorøe TN, Härgestam M. (2024). Teamwork in Rural Emergency Health Care: A Simulation-Based Cross-over Study of Co-located and Distributed Teams. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Generalized estimating equations with ordinal logistic outcome on individual TEAM items from the 17-team cross-over study. The article reports the two significant items without a standardized effect size; Figure 3 shows "estimated cumulative percentages depict ratings on different levels for items 4 and 7".

> "significant associations were found between the scenarios and item 4 (the team worked together to complete the tasks in a timely manner) and item 7 (the team adapted to changing situations), even after adjusting for the order of randomization and rater. The co-located teams had higher scores."
