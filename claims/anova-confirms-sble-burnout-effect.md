## Evidence

### Martín‐Parrilla 2025

Martín‐Parrilla, M. Á., Durán‐Gómez, N., Montanero‐Fernández, J., Casas‐Méndez, C., López‐Jurado, C. F., & Cáceres, M. C. (2025). Assessing Emotional Intelligence and Reducing Burnout in Nursing Students Through Simulation‐Based Learning Experiences: An Experimental Design. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing 34(5), e70151. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70151

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with Greenhouse–Geisser correction and Tukey's HSD post hoc tests on the full sample (n = 240). The article reports "significant intrasubject and intersubject factors (p < 0.001)" and prints the control group's post hoc p-values.

> "Results confirmed significant intrasubject and intersubject factors (p < 0.001). Specifically, the post hoc tests revealed significant improvements (p < 0.001) in the intervention group, while the control group showed no significant changes in exhaustion (p = 0.389), cynicism (p = 0.999), or reduced efficacy (p = 1.000)."
