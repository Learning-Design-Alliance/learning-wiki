## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2026

Tianlong Zu, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Transition Matrix Analysis: Analyzing Students' Use of Cognitive Resources in Physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Overall performance analysis on all 29 DIRECT questions for N=135 students. Individual pretest mean = 7.03 (S.D. = 2.27) vs group pretest mean = 8.15 (S.D. = 2.48), t(134) = -4.809, p < .001; a parallel posttest comparison was also significant (individual mean = 9.62 vs group mean = 11.87, t(134) = -9.071, p < .001).

> "A paired samples t-test revealed a significant difference between students’ performance of individual pretest (mean = 7.03, S.D. = 2.27) and that of group pretest (mean = 8.15, S.D. = 2.48), 𝑡(134) = -4.809, p < .001."
