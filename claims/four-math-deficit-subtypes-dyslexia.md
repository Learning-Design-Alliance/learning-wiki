### B. Pedemonte 2024

B. Pedemonte, C. W. Pereira, V. Borghesani, M. Ebbert, I. E. Allen, P. Pinheiro-Chagas, J. De Leon, Z. Miller, B. L. Tee & M. L. Gorno-Tempini. (2024). Profiles of mathematical deficits in children with dyslexia. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00217-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral-neurology classification of 75 children with dyslexia by a professional clinician team, followed by MCB evaluation of concordance. The article states "there is heterogeneity in the nature of these de ﬁcits" across the four domains assessed by the battery.

> "Our study reveals that within a cohort of children with dyslexia, 66% exhibit mathematical deﬁcits, and among those with mathematical deﬁcits, there is heterogeneity in the nature of these de ﬁcits."
