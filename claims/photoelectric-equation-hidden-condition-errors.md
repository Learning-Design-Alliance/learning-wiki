Anecdotal classroom report from a calculus-based engineering physics class on a modified photoelectric effect problem. The instructor reports "nearly a quarter of the class responded that af- ter the modification, there would be electrons knocked out", reasoning algebraically from eV0 = hf - phi, which hides a Heaviside condition experts apply tacitly.
