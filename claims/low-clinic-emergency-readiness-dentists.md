## Evidence

### Khattak Osama 2026

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported clinical readiness section of the cross-sectional survey (eight Yes/No and confidence items). The article reports "44.5%" with a clinic protocol and "only 7.5% reported availability of an AED"; readiness was self-reported rather than objectively assessed, and no effect size is printed.

> "Less than half (44.5%) reported having a BLS/CPR protocol in their clinic, 58.0% had an emergency kit, and only 7.5% reported availability of an AED."

### Khattak Osama 2026 (2)

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported confidence items on a two-point scale (Very confident / Not confident) in the same survey. The article reports 41.0% confident in chest compressions, 28.5% in AED use, 41.5% in recognizing cardiac arrest, and 66.0% confident managing choking; no effect size is printed.

> "confidence in skills was low: only 41.0% felt confident in chest compressions, 28.5% in AED use, and 41.5% in recognizing cardiac arrest. Confidence was highest for managing choking (66.0%)"
