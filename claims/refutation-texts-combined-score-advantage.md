## Evidence

### Jheng 2025

Jheng, Y.-L., Catrysse, L., Van de Cruys, S., Kendeou, P., Poels, K. & Gijbels, D. (2025). Investigating epistemic emotions experienced while reading refutation texts through a fine-grained measure of emotion. npj Science of Learning 10, 30. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00324-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same preregistered online experiment (56 adults, mixed-effects models with Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc comparisons): all three refutation conditions "attained significantly higher overall scores" than non-refutation texts on the combined metric.

> "In terms of thepostCombine, participants who read standard refutation texts (b = 1.15,p < 0.001), positive refutation ( b = 0.82, p = 0.02), and negative refutation texts (b = 0.81, p = 0.02) attained signiﬁcantly higher overall scores compared to those who read non-refutation texts."
