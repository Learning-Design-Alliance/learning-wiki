## Evidence

### Joekes K 2011

Joekes K, Noble LM, Kubacki AM, Potts HWW, Lloyd M. (2011). Does the inclusion of 'professional development' teaching improve medical students' communication skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-41

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis in the same longitudinal cohort comparing the 16-item (12-item scored) Interview Rating Scale with a 17-item shortened Doctor-Patient Scale covering holistic care, complexity of care and patient decision-making. No statistically significant relationships were detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "Furthermore, no relationships were found between total Interview Rating Scale score and patient-centred attitudes in year 1 or in year 2."
