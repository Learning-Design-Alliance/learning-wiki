## Evidence

### Nibal Abdelkarim Mousa Malkawi & Mona Smadi 2018

Nibal Abdelkarim Mousa Malkawi & Mona Smadi. (2018). The Effectiveness of Using Brainstorming Strategy in the Development of Academic Achievement of Sixth Grade Students in English Grammar at Public Schools in Jordan. International Education Studies, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n3p92

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-experimental two-group design with pre- and post-tests in four public schools (two male, two female) in the Wadi Al-Seer directorate, Jordan. Post-test means were 27.000 (experimental, n = 120) versus 23.567 (control, n = 114); T-test gave a significance of "(0.003)", favoring the brainstorming group.

> "there are statistically significant differences between the two groups: experimental (following the method of brainstorming) and control; (following the usual teaching method) in the post-test, because the statistical significance level is (0.003)"
