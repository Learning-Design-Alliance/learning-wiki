q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Interpretive argument in the innovation-management contribution, reasoning that in complex creative activities a good prompt is not enough for a generative AI system to independently generate a distinctively original product, especially where emotional intelligence is involved.

"there is a long way before AI platforms such as ChatGPT could be capable to lead independently to meaningful product, process, or business model innovation. At best they can help enhance human intelligence for innovation by augmenting human intelligence."
