## Evidence

### Zhao Jianping; Zhao Jianyi 2026

Zhao Jianping; Zhao Jianyi. (2026). The association between adolescents' recreational screen exposure and academic performance and its mediating mechanisms. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1840524

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional multiple linear regression on 175 CEPS middle school students, adjusting for gender, age, household registration, grade, family SES, and learning engagement. The article reports "significantly negatively associated" across all four academic outcomes with the listed β values.

> "Screen time was significantly negatively associated with Chinese (β = −0.029, P < 0.01), mathematics (β = −0.052, P < 0.01), English (β = −0.049, P < 0.01), and total academic scores (β = −0.129, P < 0.01)."
