Related Claims¶

  • Most reviewed studies found active learning improved satisfaction and knowledge acquisition and was superior to traditional lectures — a broader claim this one bears on
  • In Chinese neurology training, CBL, PBL, TBL, FCM and EBM each significantly improve theoretical exam scores compared with lecture-based learning — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • PBL and flipped classrooms frequently show better post-test performance and knowledge retention than traditional methods — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • TBL alone and TBL combined with other methods both outperform LBL, with no significant difference between the two TBL formats — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Student preference for CBL, PBL or TBL over traditional teaching was reported in 15 of 22 studies (68.1%), but only two studies used a controlled comparison with lecture — related
  • TBL in medical ethics education improves knowledge retention compared to lectures — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • TBL outperforms lecture-based learning on short-term cognitive outcomes such as theoretical exam performance — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
