## Evidence

### Corell A 2018

Corell A, Regueras LM, Verdú E, Verdú MJ, de Castro JP. (2018). Effects of competitive learning tools on medical students: A case study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0194096

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest analysis using Mann-Whitney U-tests comparing improvement (immunology grade minus related first-year course grades) between experimental (n = 172) and control (n = 113) groups. The article reports the improvement was "significantly different between the two groups (p < 0.001)"; Table 3 prints means of 3.40 vs 2.31.

> "the improvement obtained in Human Immunology (when compared to highly related courses grades obtained in the first year) was significantly different between the two groups (p < 0.001), what could indicate that the hypothesis H2 was also supported"
