## Evidence

### Corwin Lisa A. 2015

Corwin Lisa A., Runyon Christopher, Robinson Aspen, Dolan Erin L. (2015). The Laboratory Course Assessment Survey: A Tool to Measure Three Dimensions of Research-Course Design. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0073

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis of survey responses from 187 students (surveys at least 80% complete) using weighted least-squares means and variances adjusted estimation in Mplus 7.2. The authors concluded science practices do not constitute a single independent construct measurable by their approach.

> "Our initial EFA yielded a four-factor solution and revealed that eight items written to assess participation in science practices were not useful. Two items did not clearly load on any factors, and six items loaded on multiple factors (had loadings >0.35 for more than one factor)."
