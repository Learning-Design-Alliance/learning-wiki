Jacqueline Żammit. (2024). Could virtual reality be the next approach for international students learning Maltese? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10375-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Minor quantitative phase: survey of the 25 focus-group participants rating potential effectiveness on a 10-point scale, analyzed with ANOVA in MS Excel. The article reports the mean of 6.80 and an ANOVA (F = 31.70812, P value 9.89047E-06, Table 3).

> "The mean value of 6.80 suggests that most teachers who participated in this research were confident that the application of VR tools could significantly influence their students' learning outcomes."
