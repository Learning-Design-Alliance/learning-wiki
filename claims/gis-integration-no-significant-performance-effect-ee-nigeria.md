# GIS-integrated computer-mediated instruction showed no significant effect on pupils' performance in environmental education concepts compared to control groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The EGIS-integrated treatment groups did not perform significantly differently from the comparison method groups on the Environmental Information Achievement Test (F = 0.92, p > 0.05). [→ Adeleke 2017](#adeleke-2017)

## Evidence

### Adeleke 2017

Adeleke, A. G. (2017). Effects of Geographic Information System on the Learning of Environmental Education Concepts in Basic Computer-Mediated Classrooms in Nigeria. IAFOR Journal of Education, 5(3), 125-136. https://iafor.org/archives/2017/ijedu/volume-5-issue-3/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A non-randomized pretest-posttest study of 126 pupils in four intact computer-mediated classrooms in south-west Nigeria analyzed performance with one-way ANOVA. The result showed "F -ration of 0.92 which is not significant at the 0.05 level", indicating no significant effect of EGIS integration on performance.

> "It yielded F -ration of 0.92 which is not significant at the 0.05 level. The implication of this result is that the EGIS integration in co mputer-mediated basic social studies teaching of EE concept has no significance."
