## Evidence

### Mukherjee 2023

Mukherjee, S., Rohles, B., Distler, V., Lenzini, G., & Koenig, V. (2023). The effects of privacy-non-invasive interventions on cheating prevention and user experience in unproctored online assessments: An empirical study. Computers and Education, 207, 104925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104925

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the between-subjects experiment (N = 997), the monitoring message condition yielded an odds ratio of 0.683 (p = 0.103) versus control. The article notes a "32% decrease in the odds of cheating" that did not reach statistical significance.

> "However, the monitoring condition did not have a statistically significant (p = 0.103) effect on cheating, although there was a 32% decrease in the odds of cheating from control."
