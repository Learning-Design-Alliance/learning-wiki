## Evidence

### McBain Lynn 2016

McBain Lynn, Pullon Susan, Garrett Sue, Hoare Kath. (2016). Genital examination training: assessing the effectiveness of an integrated female and male teaching programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0822-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic review of free-text comments by three investigators; of 62 linked questionnaires, 22 students commented pre and 33 post. The discussion adds that "the quality of the individual tutor had a big impact on the experience" and notes a perceived need for more instruction/demonstration.

> "The pre teaching themes were largely based around lack of opportunity to participate in genital examinations on real patients, particularly male. The post teaching comments were largely positive, with a few helpful suggestions for improvement."
