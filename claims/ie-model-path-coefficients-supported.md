## Evidence

### Möller 2009

Möller, J., Pohlmann, B., Köller, O., & Marsh, H. W. (2009). A Meta-Analytic Path Analysis of the Internal/External Frame of Reference Model of Academic Achievement and Academic Self-Concept. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309337522

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Path analysis of the meta-analytically weighted correlation matrix, using maximum likelihood estimation; an alternative approach analyzing each study's matrix gave "almost identical results". Paths were .61 (math) and .49 (verbal) within domains, and -.21 and -.27 across domains. These coefficients are reported in the abstract; the detailed results section was not available in the provided text.

> "A path analysis revealed support for the I/E model, with positive paths from achievement to the corresponding self concepts (.61 for math, .49 for verbal) and negative paths from achievement in one subject to self-concept in the other subject (-.21 from math achievement on verbal self-concept, -.27 from verbal achievement to math self-concept)."
