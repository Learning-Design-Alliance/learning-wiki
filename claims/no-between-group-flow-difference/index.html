Mann–Whitney U comparison of all flow dimensions between the game-based group (n = 12) and traditional guide group (n = 9); no comparison reached significance (e.g., total Z = − .213, p = .831). Equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"The results showed that there was no significant difference between the two groups in the overall flow status, flow antecedents and flow experience, indicating that there was no difference in the flow between the two groups."
