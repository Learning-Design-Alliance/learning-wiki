### Du Xiangyu 2025

Du Xiangyu, Du Jiayi, Shang Lin, Yin Zhao, Jiang Li. (2025). Exploring influencing factors and facilitating strategies for self-directed learning from the perspective of medical trainees: a multicentre qualitative study in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-088688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group theme on potential improvement strategies. Participants suggested study groups with mutual quizzing, mobile APPs "very beneficial for self-directed learning", regular teacher–student discussions, and a "relevant course guide to improve SDL competence" from the regulating unit.

> "Participants emphasised the significance of implementing effective teaching techniques, such as collaborative learning methods, interactive teaching methods and using online applications, to promote and enhance active engagement in SDL."
