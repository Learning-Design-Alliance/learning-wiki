## Evidence

### Altay 2017

Altay, İ. F., & Altay, A. (2017). The impact of online reading tasks and reading strategies on EFL learners' reading test scores. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 136-152. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test on the 26-student control group's pre- and post-test scores. The article reports no significant difference, stating learners continuing in the traditional environment "did not show any progress in their reading scores".

> "The results showed that there was not a statistically significant difference between pre -test (M=20.05, SD=4.09) and post -test (M=19.73, SD=4.99) scores of controls group; t(25)=0.354, p=0.726."
