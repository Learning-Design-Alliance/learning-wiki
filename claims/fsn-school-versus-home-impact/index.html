Andy J. Frey 2023¶

First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

q3 · i? · review · r2

Cross-study comparison in the synthesis discussion of teacher versus parent SSiS reports across all five RCTs. The review reports "teacher reports of prosocial behavior were in the medium to large range across studies" while parent-reported prosocial measures were small except in one study.

"In each of the five RCTs, the impact of the FSN intervention was more robust in the school than in the home setting, as indicated by teacher versus parent reports on the SSiS measures."

Related Claims¶

  • In the FSN national effectiveness trial (Sumi et al., 2013), effect sizes were smaller, ranging from 0.21 to 0.67 — related
  • First Step Next replication RCTs produced small to large effect sizes and statistically significant behavioral gains compared with control conditions — related
