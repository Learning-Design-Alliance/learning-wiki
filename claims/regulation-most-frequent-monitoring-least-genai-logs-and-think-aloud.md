# In both interaction-log and think-aloud records from a GenAI-assisted instructional-design task, regulation was the most frequent operational label and monitoring the least frequent

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among retained semantic units, regulation was most frequent and monitoring least frequent in both the interaction-log record (41.6% regulation, 35.4% planning, 23.0% monitoring) and the think-aloud record (39.4%, 35.7%, 24.9%). [→ Li Shijin & Liu Junjian 2026](#li-shijin-liu-junjian-2026)

## Evidence

### Li Shijin & Liu Junjian 2026

Li Shijin & Liu Junjian. (2026). Mapping the Distribution and Depth of Metacognitive Processes in Generative AI-Assisted Learning: Evidence from Interaction Logs and Concurrent Think-Aloud Protocols. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081365

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive analysis of coded records from 79 undergraduates using DeepSeek during a 90-minute instructional-design task: 274 interaction-log and 193 think-aloud retained instances. The article reports "regulation accounted for 41.6% of retained instances" in logs and the listed think-aloud percentages; no inferential test was used because instances are nested within participants.

> "In the interaction-log record, regulation accounted for 41.6% of retained instances, planning for 35.4%, and monitoring for 23.0%. In the think-aloud record, the corresponding percentages were 39.4%, 35.7%, and 24.9%."
