# Regular PR use is selection-biased toward higher-ability students: the regular-user fraction declines from 62% of A students to 39% of C-or-lower students as beginning-of-term GPA drops from 3.67 to 2.88.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 518 graded Phys 240 students, the regular user fraction was 62% among A students and 39% among C-or-lower students, with mean beginning-of-term GPA falling from 3.67 to 2.88 across those grade categories. [→ Evrard 2013](#evrard-2013)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of the Fall 2012 Phys 240 grade distribution (156 A, 168 B, 194 C-or-lower) plotted against mean beginning-of-term GPA and regular-user fraction (Fig. 7). Descriptive percentages only; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The regular user fraction of A students is 62%, declining to 39% of C or lower students. At the same time, the mean GPA drops from 3.67 to 2.88 across those categories."
