## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Second-year dental students taught with procedure-specific videos plus live demonstration showed a significant post-intervention competence difference (p = 0.000), while the control group's pre-post change was not significant (p = 0.352). [→ Iqbal Azhar 2022](#iqbal-azhar-2022)

## Evidence

### Iqbal Azhar 2022

Iqbal Azhar, Ganji Kiran Kumar, Khattak Osama, Shrivastava Deepti, Srivastava Kumar Chandan, Arjumand Bilal, AlSharari Thani, Alqahtani Ali Mosfer A, Hamza May Othman, AbdelrahmanDafaalla Ahmed Abu El Gasim. (2022). Enhancement of Skill Competencies in Operative Dentistry Using Procedure-Specific Educational Videos (E-Learning Tools) Post-COVID-19 Era—A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19074135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 50 second-year dental students randomly divided into Group A (live demonstration only) and Group B (video via Blackboard plus live demonstration), assessed by a six-station OSPE one week after intervention. McNemar analysis found "a significant difference was found in the experimental groups" with p = 0.000.

> "The results (Table 1) show that there was no significant difference before the intervention between the control group and the experimental group in the competence level of the participants, but a significant difference was found in the experimental groups (Table 2) after the intervention. The p value was 0.000."
