Alshareef Ali M., Alhasani Mona, Qamar Nabeel, Alhazmi Aiman. (2026). Tailoring hybrid flipped learning course design: exploring motivation, personality, and experiences of students and faculty. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09570-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic analysis of open-ended responses from all 149 students, coded independently by two researchers. Seven themes emerged, including flexibility, self-assessment and skill development, desire for more in-person interaction, assessment and feedback, lack of engagement, scheduling issues, and recording improvements.

> "Students highly valued the flexibility and autonomy offered by the hybrid learning format. The ability to manage their own schedules, review materials at their own pace, and focus on self-learning was particularly appreciated"
