### Nuri Balta 2016

Nuri Balta, Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying Turkish high school and university students' attitudes and approaches to physics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of the translated 33-item Likert survey administered to 528 students. The validation process included a professional translation reviewed by science and English faculty, five bilingual physicists, and think-aloud interviews with four high school students; one item (item 9) was adapted to replace conservation of linear momentum with Newton's second law.

> "the Cronbach alpha statistic is robust (α > 0.80) and ranges from 0.82 to 0.90 across different student groups [30]. These values are comparable to the values reported by Mason and Singh for the English version of the survey"
