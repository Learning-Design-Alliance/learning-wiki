## Evidence

### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic interview study with 10 physics faculty and 14 department heads; the analysis produced four families of categories, and the last author independently categorized about 30% of the interview data with 89% agreement. The four families are practical needs, limited measure, want help, and broader context.

> "Using phenomenographic interviews of ordinary physics faculty and department chairs, we identified four families of issues that faculty have around research-based assessments (RBA) and suggest ways that the PER community can address these issues."
