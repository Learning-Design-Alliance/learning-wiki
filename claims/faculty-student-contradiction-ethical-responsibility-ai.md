### Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation of the interview data: both faculty and students acknowledged risks yet pragmatically embraced AI. The article links this attitude to differing user profiles in prior literature and its "potential to normalize unethical behavior".

> "In addition to this contradiction, the fact that both groups embrace AI with a pragmatic "conscious acceptance" while being aware of the risks further complicates the situation."
