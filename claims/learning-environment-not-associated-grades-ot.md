### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the hierarchical regression (n = 174), the learning environment block added "R2 change 4.7 %" with p = 0.06, and no individual CEQ scale reached significance (e.g., good teaching β = 0.12, p = 0.16). No effect sizes were printed for these null associations.
