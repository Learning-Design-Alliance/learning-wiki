## Evidence

### Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Rubric-based sub-measure analysis of the same preregistered classroom experiment, where the memory category indicated whether a necessary equation was recalled and written down correctly. The article reports "d = 0.41" and "d = 0.96" for Stages 1 and 2.

> "Sub-measure analyses revealed that interleaving improved long-term memory in Stage 1, d = 0.41, 95% CI [0.17, 0.66], t(288) = 3.49, p = 0.006, and in Stage 2, d = 0.96, 95% CI [0.70, 1.24], t(284) = 8.05, p < 0.0001."

### Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021 (2)

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Correctness sub-measure required the final numerical answer and unit to be exactly correct; the article notes the mean rate of correct answers across both conditions was no more than 0.34 proportion correct. Reported effects are "d = 0.25" and "d = 0.40".

> "Further, interleaving improved the correctness of answers in Stage 1, d = 0.25, 95% CI [0.02, 0.48], t(288) = 2.17, p = 0.0311, and in Stage 2, d = 0.40, 95% CI [0.16, 0.64], t(284) = 3.32, p = 0.0010."
