sLORETA source estimation for the second significant TANCOVA period (573–638 ms) in the EEG associative learning study; sources "include the superior frontal gyrus (BA 11)" and, to a lesser extent, the medial frontal gyrus (BA 6).

"The sources associated with this periodcomputed with sLORETA are shown in Fig.1c (lower panel) and include the superior frontal gyrus (BA 11), with the highest activation at MNI coordinates X = −5, Y = 60, Z = −20, and to a lesser extent, the medial frontal gyrus (BA 6)."
