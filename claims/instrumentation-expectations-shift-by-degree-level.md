### Shams El-Adawy 2025

Shams El-Adawy, Heather J. Lewandowski, A. R. Piña, & Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2025). Experimental Skills for Non-PhD Roles in the Quantum Industry (Quantum Workforce Report Series, Report 1). arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.12936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.12936)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview findings on the instrumentation category across the 22 positions: interviewees describe bachelor's roles needing basic tools (e.g., a fabrication engineer understanding how a multimeter works), while master's roles shift toward subsystem integration and CAD-based optomechanical design.

> "positions that require only a bachelor’s degree tend to emphasize familiarity with common laboratory tools and systems. For instance, when discussing the knowledge and skills needed for afabrication engineer, our interviewee explains the importance of being able to operate basic test and measurement devices"
