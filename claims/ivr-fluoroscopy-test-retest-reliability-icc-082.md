## Evidence

### Sønderup M 2024

Sønderup M, Gustafsson A, Konge L, Jacobsen M E. (2024). Intraoperative fluoroscopy skills in distal radius fracture surgery: valid and reliable assessment on a novel immersive virtual reality simulator. Acta Orthopaedica 95: 477–484. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.41345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-way mixed intraclass correlation with absolute agreement definition computed on composite scores from the two sessions of 11 novices and 9 experienced surgeons. The article reports ICC = 0.82 (CI 0.65–0.92), P < 0.001.

> "The estimated composite z-score showed good test–retest reliability with ICC for average measures being 0.82 (CI 0.65–0.92), P < 0.001."

### Sønderup M 2024 (2)

Sønderup M, Gustafsson A, Konge L, Jacobsen M E. (2024). Intraoperative fluoroscopy skills in distal radius fracture surgery: valid and reliable assessment on a novel immersive virtual reality simulator. Acta Orthopaedica 95: 477–484. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.41345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of estimated marginal mean composite z-scores across the two sessions for both groups. The article attributes the absence of improvement to the extended time-gap between sessions and, more importantly, the absence of feedback for participants.

> "There was no difference in estimated mean composite z-scores over the 2 sessions as the estimated mean composite z-score difference for all participants was 0.43 (CI –0.98 to 1.85), indicating no difference in performance for either of the 2 groups across the 2 sessions"
