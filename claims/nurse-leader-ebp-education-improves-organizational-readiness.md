## Evidence

### Varpula Jaakko 2026

Varpula Jaakko, Askola Riitta, Kahsay Desale Tewelde, Axelin Anna, Virtanen Laura, Maijala Riikka, Peltonen Laura‐Maria. (2026). Enhancing Nurse Leaders Evidence‐Based Practice Implementation Leadership Competence. A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis of Educational Interventions. Worldviews on Evidence‐Based Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.70164

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of two studies measuring organizational readiness with ORIC and OCRSIEP scales, pooled between baseline and post-intervention. The review reports "a SMD of 0.65 (95% CI: 0.12, 1.17, p = 0.02)" favoring the intervention.

> "Two studies assessed organizational readiness, and the meta‐analysis indicated a significant improvement, with a SMD of 0.65 (95% CI: 0.12, 1.17, p = 0.02), favoring the educational intervention."
