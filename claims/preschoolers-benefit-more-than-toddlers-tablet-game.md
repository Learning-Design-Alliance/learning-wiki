## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across experiments, preschoolers showed greater total gain than toddlers (r = -0.51), and Study groups showed greater total gain than Controls (r = -0.22). [→ Peña 2024](#pena-2024)

## Evidence

### Peña 2024

Peña, M., Vásquez-Venegas, C., Cortés, P., Pittaluga, E., Herrera, M., Pino, E. J., Escobar, R. G., Dehaene-Lambertz, G., & Guevara, P. (2024). A brief tablet-based intervention benefits linguistic and communicative abilities in toddlers and preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00249-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-experiment analysis pooling standardized total gains (ASQ-3 Communication + CDI Adverbs/Verbs in Exp 1; TECAL Vocabulary + TECAL total in Exp 2) via Wilcoxon Mann-Whitney test. "toddlers showed less total gain than preschoolers (Z = −8.00, P < 0.001, r = −0.51)" and Group main effect r = -0.22.

> "We found a main effect of Group (Z = −3.56, P < 0.001, r = −0.22), as the Control groups showed less total gain than the Study groups. A main effect of Experiment was also found, as toddlers showed less total gain than preschoolers (Z = −8.00, P < 0.001, r = −0.51)."
