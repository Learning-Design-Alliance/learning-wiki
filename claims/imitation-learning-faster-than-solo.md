## Evidence

### Okazaki Shuntaro 2019

Okazaki Shuntaro, Muraoka Yoshihiro, Osu Rieko. (2019). Teacher-learner interaction quantifies scaffolding behaviour in imitation learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44049-x

`q3 · i?`

Trial-by-trial Wilcoxon comparisons of puzzle-solving durations in 14 pairs; the article reports learners "significantly improved from the second trial, while that of teachers in the TP improved from the third", suggesting imitation learning is more efficient than solo learning.

> "The performance of learners in the IP significantly improved from the second trial, while that of teachers in the TP improved from the third."

### Okazaki Shuntaro 2019 (2)

Okazaki Shuntaro, Muraoka Yoshihiro, Osu Rieko. (2019). Teacher-learner interaction quantifies scaffolding behaviour in imitation learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44049-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon comparison of learners' first solo-trial duration against their first imitation-trial duration; "significantly shorter than the first trial in the IP (p = 0.023, R = 0.76)", which the authors take to show imitation functioned as learning.

> "The puzzle duration of the learners at the first trial of the LP was significantly shorter than the first trial in the IP (p = 0.023, R = 0.76)."
