M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna 2014¶

M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna. (2014). Reform-Based-Instructional Method and Learning Styles on Students' Achievement and Retention in Mathematics: Administrative Implications. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 2(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.2n.2p.48

Same quasi-experimental study's ANCOVA on retention scores: "the calculated f-ratio for retention is 65,249" with p = .000, so the null hypothesis was rejected. The article reports the reform-based group retained more knowledge than the traditionally taught group.

"the calculated f-ratio for retention is 65,249 with probability significant value of 0.000 which is less than 0.05. The null hypothesis of no significant mean difference in retention was therefore not retained."
