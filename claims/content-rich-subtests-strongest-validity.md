## Evidence

### Jaehn M 2025

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analyses of TMS subtests with PCGPA and M1 in the incumbent sample, corrected for range restriction. The article prints the corrected correlation ranges for reasoning/text subtests versus memory and search subtests; correlations are the printed effect sizes.

> "For the TMS, subtests consisting of reasoning tasks and text comprehension showed a stronger association (−0.17 ≤ r ≤ −0.35) than memory tasks, the visual search task, and the pattern recognition task (−0.04 ≤ r ≤ −0.15)."

### Jaehn M 2025 (2)

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analyses of HAM-Nat subtests with PCGPA and M1, corrected for range restriction. The article prints the knowledge test's corrected correlations of −0.35 to −0.47 versus −0.11 to −0.25 for the reasoning tests.

> "For the HAM-Nat, the knowledge test showed the highest association (−0.35 ≤ r ≤ −0.47), whereas associations of the HAM-Nat´s reasoning tests were considerably lower (−0.11 ≤ r ≤ −0.25)."
