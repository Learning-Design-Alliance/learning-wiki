Chun-Yi Lee 2015¶

Chun-Yi Lee. (2015). The Effects of Online Peer Assessment and Family Entrepreneurial Experience on Students' Business Planning Performance. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057346

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Correlation analysis of three-round review scores provided by the teacher and students in the quasi-experiment; the printed correlation coefficients rose from r = 0.528 to r = 0.715 to r = 0.939, and the article reports peer scores became increasingly consistent with teacher evaluations.

"The significant correlation coefficients of three-round review were 0.528, 0.715, and 0.939 respectively, showing a strong correlation between the scores marked by peers and the expert scores."
