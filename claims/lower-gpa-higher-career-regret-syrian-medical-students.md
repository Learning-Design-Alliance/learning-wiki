## Evidence

### Kheder Maria 2026

Kheder Maria, Nasra Ahmad Bishr, Alghoutani Sarah, Al-Hammash Hiba, Saleh Abdullah, Assaad Alaa, Almansour Shahd, Kreimesh Nussaiba, Kanaa Leen, Latifeh Youssef, Alsaid Bayan. (2026). Career regret, academic-related factors and health-related quality of life among Syrian medical students: a multicentre cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2026-118578

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted HC3-robust regression (n=1702) with percentile GPA entered in three categories, high (≥85%) as reference. The article reports "those with GPA≤74.99% had higher R5M" and the mid category also higher; only unstandardized coefficients were printed.

> "Compared with students with GPA≥85%, those with GPA≤74.99% had higher R5M (B=0.300, 95% CI 0.164 to 0.435, p<0.001), as did those with GPA of 75–84.99% (B=0.111, 95% CI 0.023 to 0.198, p=0.013)"
