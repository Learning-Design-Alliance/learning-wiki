The guide's own account of its literature search and screening: a search for algebra instruction literature published between 1993 and 2013 yielded more than 2,800 citations, of which 30 used experimental or quasi-experimental designs and "15 studies met the WWC's rigorous evidence group design standards."

"From this subset, 15 studies met the WWC's rigorous evidence group design standards and were related to the panel's recommendations."
