## Subclaims

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multicenter simple-randomized parallel-group trial in 8 European HIV-care settings with 112 HIV-positive MSM. Results of mixed effects modeling comparing CISS with controls: "yielding an OR of 3.83 between CISS and control participants", significant at P = 0.03; condom use rose from 35% to 68% in CISS vs 30% to 45% in controls at 3 months.

> "CISS-participants were more likely than controls to report condom use at last intercourse [ie, yielding an OR of 3.83 between CISS and control participants; 95% confidence interval (CI): 1.15 to 12.76]. This difference was statistically significant (P = 0.03)."
