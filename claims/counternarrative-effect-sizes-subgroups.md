`q2 · i1` · `theoretical · r?`

The comment restates the original study's Table V subgroup effect sizes: Cohen d's of 0.23, 0.13, 0.17, and 0.20 for female, non-female, minority and non-minority groups, with 95% confidence intervals (0.07,0.39), (-0.03,0.30), (0.05, 0.30) and (-0.05, 0.44). The comment describes these as "the modest survey effect reported" and argues their validity is eroded by the statistical and bias issues it raises.

> "The differences between the treatment and control groups are reported in Table V as Cohen d's  of 0.23, 0.13,0.17, and 0.20,  with 95% confidence intervals  (0.07,0.39), (-0.03,0.30), (0.05, 0.30) and (-0.05, 0.44), respectively."
