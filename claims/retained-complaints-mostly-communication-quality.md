`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of 1116 complaints filed in 22,585 practice-years, of which 696 were retained after investigation; complaint classifications were grouped into 6 categories and verified by regulatory investigators. These are descriptive percentages, not standardized effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Of the 3424 physicians, 21.5% had at least 1 complaint filed, and 17.1% had complaint(s) retained in their file after investigation. The majority (81.9%) of retained complaints were for attitude/communication and quality-of-care problems."
