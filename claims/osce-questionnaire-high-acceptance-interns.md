### Zhang Weihao 2021

Zhang Weihao, Gu Jiaoyang, Li Fan, Feng Feifei, Chen Huiqiang, Xing Xiaowei, Liu Lan. (2021). The effect of flipped classroom in multiple clinical skills training for clinical interns on Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE). Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.2013405

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous OSCE questionnaire (alpha 0.907 in group A, 0.925 in group B) given to all 79 interns after the examination. "Most interns said they knew more about the OSCE model (81%)"; 86% found it more novel, fair, and objective than traditional assessment. Descriptive percentages only.

> "Most interns said they knew more about the OSCE model (81%) and thought that it truly reflected the doctor's clinical ability (82.3%). Moreover, it helped to improve the doctor's comprehensive ability (95%)."
