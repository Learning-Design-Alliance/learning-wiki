### Søren Marker 2019

Søren Marker, Marlene Mohr, Doris Østergaard. (2019). Simulation-based training of junior doctors in handling critically ill patients facilitates the transition to clinical practice: an interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1447-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study (n=20, 6 months post-course) using systematic text condensation; algorithms were a main theme with subthemes for ABCDE assessment, ACCEPT transfer and SBAR communication. The article reports algorithms "were in general found to be useful and implemented in the clinical settings". No effect size is printed.
