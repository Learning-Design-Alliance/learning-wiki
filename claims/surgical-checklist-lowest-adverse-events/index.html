Evidence¶

Costar 2020¶

Costar, D. M., & Hall, K. K. (2020). Improving Team Performance and Patient Safety on the Job Through Team Training and Performance Support Tools: A Systematic Review. Journal of Patient Safety. https://doi.org/10.1097/PTS.0000000000000746

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a three-group comparison of adverse event rates: checklist group "8.9%", historical control "23.6%", and team training without checklist "15.9%". The comparison is against a historical control group; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

"Bliss et al9 where they reported that the use of a surgical safety checklist was associated with significantly lower adverse event rates (8.9%) as compared with the performance of a historical control group (23.6%) and a group that had received team training with no checklist (15.9%)."
