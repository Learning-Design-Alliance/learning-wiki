# In a six-week within-subject study, the Empathetic PA coaching chatbot improves intention to follow and self-efficacy faster over time than the Non-Empathetic condition

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Li Siyan 2026

Li Siyan, Kai-Hui Liang, Shopnil Shahriar, Yilin Ye, Shiyoh Goetsu, Wei-Wei Du, Masahiro Yoshida, Tsunayuki Ohwa, Xuhai Xu, and Zhou Yu. (2026). Invisible Impact of Empathy on Behavioral Change: Isolating the Effect of Empathy in Long-term Physical Activity Coaching Chatbot Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26641

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects model on check-in form responses from the six-week within-subject study of 13 participants, with empathetic condition, days in study, their interaction, and a weekday/weekend control. The article reports a "significant positive interaction between the Empathetic condition and the number of days"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "We observe a significant positive interaction between the Empathetic condition and the number of days on both intention to follow ( 𝛽= 0.011, 𝑝=. 031) and self-efficacy ( 𝛽= 0.011, 𝑝=. 047), compared to the Non-Empathetic condition."
