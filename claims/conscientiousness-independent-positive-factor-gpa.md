Association between the big five personalities and academic performance among grade 12 students at international high school in Thailand. Scientific Reports. Forward stepwise multivariable analysis in the same questionnaire study identified "the Conscientiousness personality domain (1.89 [1.12–3.20; p-value = .02])" as an independent positive factor for being in the AA-group. Only the odds ratio is printed; no standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Multivariate analysis (adjusted Odds Ratio [95% confidence interval]; p-value) identified the Conscientiousness personality domain (1.89 [1.12–3.20; p-value = .02]) and the female gender (2.66 [1.39–5.09; p-value = .003]) as the positively associated factors for academic achievement."
