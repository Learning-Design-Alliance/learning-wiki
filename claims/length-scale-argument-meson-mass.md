# A correct length-scale argument — a range of a bit over 10^-15 m corresponds to about 1/(200 MeV) — can replace the common misuse of the energy-time uncertainty relation when teaching why force-carrying particles have mass

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A proposed corrective teaching argument in Section 1, offered as the author's theoretical alternative to the incorrect use of the energy-time uncertainty relation in textbook presentations of Yukawa's mesons. It is an analytical proposal, not an empirically tested intervention.

> "a simple but correct argument would be to note that we need a length scale of a bit over 10 −15m and since 10 −15m ≈ 1/(200 MeV ), we need a ﬁeld whose quanta will have mass of that scale"
