### Nakhanu and Musasia 2015

Shikuku Beatrice Nakhanu, Amadalo Maurice Musasia. (2015). Problem Based Learning Technique and its effect on Acquisition of Linear Programming Skills by Secondary School Students in Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079054

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test post-test non-equivalent groups experiment with 745 experimental and 757 control form four students across 30 Kenyan schools. The post-test (MAT 2) means were 58.0733 (experimental) versus 34.9733 (control); the "significance of 0.000 which is les s than 0.05" led the authors to reject HO1. No effect size is printed.
