Systematic literature review of 25 articles (database search plus backward snowballing, PRISMA flow) in which value definitions from 16 articles providing definitions were thematically grouped by common terms; the review reports that "the thematic analysis resulted in six main EVs for AIED". No effect size applies.

> "The thematic analysis resulted in six main EVs for AIED. Hence, in answer to RQ1, the six main EVs of AIED are: non-discrimination, data stewardship, human oversight, goodwill, explicability, and educational aptness."
