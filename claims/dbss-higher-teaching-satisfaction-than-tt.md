## Evidence

### Ma Yanli 2026

Ma Yanli, Li Han, Zhang Huan, Gao Fengli, Ma Xiaobei. (2026). Defect-based scenario simulation teaching in the specialized skills training of nurse anesthetists: a before–after within-subject design. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09098-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-designed 8-item satisfaction questionnaire administered 1 week after each phase; the article states its content validity was not evaluated and internal consistency was 0.98, so results are "exploratory and descriptive in nature", reporting "36.23 ± 4.37" vs. "33.63 ± 4.98".

> "The NAs’ scores for satisfaction for DBSS (36.23 ± 4.37) were significantly higher than those for TT (33.63 ± 4.98), with a statistically significant difference (paired t-test, P < 0.05). In a dedicated survey on this teaching model, participants gave the item “I find the defect-based situational teaching highly meaningful” a score of 4.56 ± 0.55 and “I believe the design of defect-based situational teaching is reasonable” a score of 4.53 ± 0.55"
