Jacquemyn Xander, Guerrier Karine, Harvey Evan, Tackett Sean, Kutty Shelby, Wetzel Glenn T. (2024). pECGreview: Assessment of a Novel Tool to Evaluate the Accuracy of Pediatric ECG Interpretation Skills. Pediatric Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00246-024-03556-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cross-sectional review of responses to 19 ECGs in the pECGreview program, scored by four blinded evaluators on a 3-point Accuracy scale. The study reports "Participants were correct in 66.1 ± 5.1%" and a mean accuracy score of 76.6 ± 13.7% for the 107 participants with five or more responses.

> "Participants were correct in 66.1 ± 5.1%, had minor over- or underdiagnosis in 21.5 ± 4.6% and major over- or underdiagnosis in 12.3 ± 3.9% of interpretations."
