## Evidence

### Li Xinyan 2025

Li Xinyan, Pei Xiaoge, Zhao Jing. (2025). Intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy as pathways to innovative teaching: a mixed-methods study of faculty in Chinese higher education. BMC Psychology, 13, 859. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03177-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Bootstrapping mediation analysis (5,000 samples) within the SEM of 357 Chinese faculty. The article reports the indirect effect "was significant (β = 0.24, 95% CI [0.17, 0.32], p < 0.001), supporting the mediation hypothesis"; the direct effect (β = 0.33, p < 0.001) also remained significant, indicating partial mediation.

> "the indirect effect of autonomous motivations on innovative teaching practices via self-efficacy was significant (β = 0.24, 95% CI [0.17, 0.32], p < 0.001), supporting the mediation hypothesis."
