## Evidence

### Farhadi Roya 2023

Farhadi Roya, Azandehi Bita Khalili, Amuei Fattane, Ahmadi Mozhgan, Zazoly Atefeh Zabihi, Ghorbani Ali Asghar. (2023). Enhancing residents' neonatal resuscitation competency through team-based simulation training: an intervention educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04704-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Five-station OSCE (25 min, scored 0–10, pass at 5) administered four months post-training to assess Kirkpatrick level 3 behavior. Residents achieved 86% of maximum points overall, with station percentages of 89% briefing, 97% initial steps, 87% positive-pressure ventilation, 78% intubation/chest compression, and 79% medication.

> "In the evaluation of the third level (behavior) four months after the end of the training course, the mean score of residents' skills using OSCE, was 8.6 ± 1.1 with minimum and maximum scores of 7 and 10, respectively. All residents obtained a passing grade and the changes were retained."
