## Evidence

### J. Christopher Moore 2011

J. Christopher Moore, Richard D. Floyd and Cody V. Thompson. (2011). Virtual images and billiards. Coastal Carolina University. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1103.3254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1103.3254)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of the second activity phase with two plane mirrors: students "bank the cue ball off of both sidewalls" to knock the 8-ball into the corner pocket, then construct two image fields, one per mirror, and demonstrate that the same principles apply for images of virtual images.

> "in this case, they must bank the cue ball off of both sidewalls in order to successfully knock the 8-­‐ball into the corner pocket."
