Klassen, Robert Mark, Wang, Hui, Cutting, Joe, Thompson-Lee, Sophie, Snell, Rebecca J.S., See, Beng Huat, Saiger, Michael. (2025). Digital innovations in teacher recruitment: An experimental study. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase 2 latent change analysis with a delayed post-test six weeks later (N = 572) found the linear change model fit poorly, so a quadratic model was used to capture non-linear change in interest, self-efficacy, and fit.

> "The linear change model demonstrated a poor fit to the data, indicating that the constructs did not increase at a consistent rate over time."
