`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the introduction the authors argue, without reporting empirical data, that quantum cryptography lets learners "grasp the very fundamental principles of quantum physics", that it is rather easy to understand, and that it is mysterious and exciting. The support is a reasoned argument, not a test.

> "We consider quantum cryptography to be a very suit- able subject for an introductory role to quantum me- chanics."
