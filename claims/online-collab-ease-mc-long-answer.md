### Callaghan 2025

Callaghan, K., Kestin, G., Klales, A., McCarty, L., & Deslauriers, L. (2025). Active Learning Through Flexible Collaborative Exams: Improving STEM Assessments. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of students who completed the asynchronous online collaborative exam, reported in Results (Fig. 4). When asked directly whether multiple-choice sharing was easier, students responded neutrally (3.60 + 0.18); no effect sizes were printed.

> "The results indicate that students found it quite feasible to share and collaborate on multiple choice (4.47 ± 0.15) and long-answer questions (4.13 ± 0.17), suggesting the type of question had minimal impact."
