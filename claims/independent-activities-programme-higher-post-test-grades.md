## Evidence

### Monica-Iuliana Anca & Muşata Bocoş 2017

Monica-Iuliana Anca & Muşata Bocoş. (2017). The Role of Independent Activities in Development of Strategic Learning Competences and Increase of School Performance Level, within the Study of High School Pedagogy. Educatia 21 Journal, (15). https://doi.org/10.24193/ed21.2017.15.07

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pedagogical experiment comparing an experimental focus group (n=84) that received the independent-activities programme with a control focus group (n=81); post-test averages were 7.33 versus 5.92, a statistically significant difference per the printed t test.

> "The result at t test for independent focus groups revealed that the grades average at the post-test for the experimental focus group is significantly different, from statistical point of view, from the grades average at the post-test for the control focus group (t=7.122; df=138.305; p<0 .001)."
