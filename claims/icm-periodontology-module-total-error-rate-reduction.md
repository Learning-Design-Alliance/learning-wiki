# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, dental students' total error rates in classifying periodontal patients were significantly lower after participation in an ICM-based, case-based periodontology module, with a medium effect size

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Total error rates in patient case classification were significantly lower after module participation (T0 28.3% vs. T1 18.7%, Δ=9.6%), with a medium effect (r=.52). [→ Crome Marius 2021](#crome-marius-2021)

## Evidence

### Crome Marius 2021

Crome Marius, Adam Knut, Flohr Marco, Rahman Alexander, Staufenbiel Ingmar. (2021). Application of the inverted classroom model in the teaching module “new classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases and conditions” during the COVID-19 pandemic. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001485.shtml

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Before-after comparison of 55 10th-semester dental students' classifications of 33 patient cases before (T0) and after (T1) the module; students omitting parameters were excluded, leaving n=34. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed the error rate "significantly lower after participation" with "effect size may be considered medium (r=.52)".

> "The error rate proved to be significantly lower after participation in the periodontology module (asymptotic Wilcoxon test: z=-3.066; p=.002; n=34). Based on Cohen’s guidelines, the effect size may be considered medium (r=.52) [6]."
