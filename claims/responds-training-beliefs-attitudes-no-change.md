## Evidence

### Lewis Natalia V. 2017

Lewis Natalia V., Larkins Cath, Stanley Nicky, Szilassy Eszter, Turner William, Drinkwater Jessica, Feder Gene S. (2017). Training on domestic violence and child safeguarding in general practice: a mixed method evaluation of a pilot intervention. BMC Family Practice 18:33. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-017-0603-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on 37 completers for the beliefs/attitudes subscale; increases of 0.3 and 0.2 units were not statistically significant. The subscale also had internal consistency below minimally acceptable level (α = 0.51 at T1).

> "The mean measure of beliefs and attitudes did not change either post-training (increase by 0.3 units) or at follow up (increase by 0.2 units) (F (2, 108) = 0.23, P = 0.61)."

### Lewis Natalia V. 2017 (2)

Lewis Natalia V., Larkins Cath, Stanley Nicky, Szilassy Eszter, Turner William, Drinkwater Jessica, Feder Gene S. (2017). Training on domestic violence and child safeguarding in general practice: a mixed method evaluation of a pilot intervention. BMC Family Practice 18:33. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-017-0603-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative observation and trainer interviews found attitude change in some sessions; Trainer 4 remained concerned that participants in other sessions 'were still speaking of them as two separate issues', making the qualitative picture mixed against a null quantitative result.

> "Thus, participants in two sessions were observed (Obs 2 & 5) to make the link from DVA to CS when they had not done so before or ended up 'agreeing with the whole idea that you need to …treat it as a safeguarding issue' (Tr05)."
