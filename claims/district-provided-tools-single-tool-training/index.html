Evidence¶

Digital Promise 2018¶

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding across all four countries; the tendency was strongest in Scotland, where every teacher and student has access to the Glow platform including Office 365 and Forms.

"most teachers explained that they tended to use the tools provided by their district rather than seek out new tools on their own."

Digital Promise 2018 (2)¶

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview evidence from a technology coach whose district enabled Office 365 without training; she trained nearly the entire teaching staff on single tools over several months.

"When we were showing them all the tools we weren’t finding any uptake,” she explained, “because that’s what we did at the beginning. Now when teachers attend a training we start with one tool and actually walk them step-by-step on how to use it."
