## Evidence

### Mocellin D 2026

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' summary of the linear mixed-effects model results across 6 hands-on trials for health professionals randomized to the high-fidelity active configuration, from the Discussion. The Results section reports the group "failed to reach the target only in trial 2" for mean pressure and showed improved pressure consistency in trials 4 and 6.

> "Among health professionals, the best performance during practical training was observed in the high-fidelity active group, which achieved the target pressure in most trials, improved pressure consistency, and more closely approached the target ventilation rate compared with the other groups."

### Mocellin D 2026 (2)

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed-effects model of mean pressure across 6 trials among health professionals randomized to the high-fidelity passive configuration, reported in the Results section. The article reports the printed trial means and 95% CIs and persistent overventilation.

> "the global pattern for the high-fidelity passive group, showed a progressive upward trend, with significantly higher pressures in trials 4 through 6 compared with trial 1 (mean pressure per trial: trial 1, 27.0 cm H2O [95% CI, 25.4-28.6 cm H2O]; trial 4, 28.8 cm H2O [95% CI, 27.2-30.3 cm H2O]; trial 5, 29.7 cm H2O [95% CI, 28.1-31.3 cm H2O]; trial 6, 30.4 cm H2O [95% CI, 28.9-32.0 cm H2O]) and did not reach the target in any trial"
