## Evidence

### Wegner Lauren E. MS 2023

Wegner Lauren E. MS, Shiffermiller Jason F. MD, MPH, Vokoun Chad W. MD, O'Rourke Michael J. MD, Rohlfsen Cory J. MD. (2023). The Effects of an E-learning Module on Medical Trainees Rotating on a Perioperative Medicine Clinical Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11325

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the postmodule satisfaction survey (Kirkpatrick level 1) covering conciseness, clarity, level of detail, and perceived relevance; specific responses reported in Table 2. No effect size applies.

> "On our postmodule satisfaction survey, more than 75% of trainees either agreed or strongly agreed that the time to complete the module was acceptable and the material was concise, easy to understand, presented in sufficient detail, and relevant to future practice."

### Wegner Lauren E. MS 2023 (2)

Wegner Lauren E. MS, Shiffermiller Jason F. MD, MPH, Vokoun Chad W. MD, O'Rourke Michael J. MD, Rohlfsen Cory J. MD. (2023). The Effects of an E-learning Module on Medical Trainees Rotating on a Perioperative Medicine Clinical Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11325

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported intent to change practice on the postmodule survey; 60 (87%) said yes. The authors note they were not able to assess the actual impact on clinical practice (Kirkpatrick level 3).

> "When asked whether participants planned to alter previous practice patterns based on the material presented in the module, 60 (87%) said yes, five (7%) said no, and four (6%) did not respond."
