## Evidence

### Linda Bobo 2009

Linda Bobo, Amanda Andrews. (2009). Using video feedback to measure self-efficacy. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. https://www.aabri.com/journal-of-instructional-pedagogies/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive repeated-measures results from the eight-student cohort, reported in Results and Figure 1/Table 3: the group "self-efficacy values increased across the five trials", with trial means rising from 6.139 to 9.514 on the 10-point scale. No effect size is printed for this descriptive trend.

> "Before instruction, the baseline self-efficacy (Trial 1) mean level for the group was 6.14 (±2.04) of 10 points on the Likert scale for the upper body neurological screening. Respectively, the group self-efficacy values increased across the five trials, or for each time the Self-efficacy form was completed (see Figure 1)."
