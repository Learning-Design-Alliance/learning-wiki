### Haider Ambreen Khurshid 2025

Haider Ambreen Khurshid, Rehman Javeria, Ali Syeda Kauser. (2025). Comparative study of the effect of two small group discussion teaching methods (Tutorial vs Jigsaw) on academic motivation and achievement of undergraduate dental students – a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07244-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive results of the randomized pre-test/post-test study at Shifa College of Dentistry: post-test MCQ achievement means were 17.32 ± 3.859 and 19.50 ± 3.162, reported "for the control and experimental group respectively".

> "The mean post-test scores of the two groups were 17.32 ± 3.859 and 19.50 ± 3.162 for the control and experimental group respectively."

### Haider Ambreen Khurshid 2025 (2)

Haider Ambreen Khurshid, Rehman Javeria, Ali Syeda Kauser. (2025). Comparative study of the effect of two small group discussion teaching methods (Tutorial vs Jigsaw) on academic motivation and achievement of undergraduate dental students – a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07244-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test achievement scores of the randomly assigned tutorial and jigsaw groups; the results section reports "a significant statistical difference between the post-test scores of the two groups (after the intervention)".

> "Results showed a significant statistical difference between the post-test scores of the two groups (after the intervention) (Table 3)."
