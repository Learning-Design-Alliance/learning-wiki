## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Expectations that ICT use would improve performance and satisfaction correlated significantly with all four ICT-use factors, with the largest coefficients for Educational Platform Use (expectations of performance r = .154; expectations of satisfaction r = .150). ## Evidence

### Valentín 2013

Valentín, A., Mateos, P. M., González-Tablas, M. M., Pérez, L., López, E., & García, I. (2013). Motivation and learning strategies in the use of ICTs among university students. Computers & Education, 61, 52–58. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.09.008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same 543-student survey. Table 5 shows EPU correlated with expectations of performance (r = .154) and satisfaction (r = .150), both significant at the .01 level; other uses showed lower, mostly significant coefficients.

> "The coef ﬁcients relating to the expectations that use of ICTs would improve performance and satisfaction in studies employing these educational platforms are also signi ﬁcant, but of low magnitude."
