# Semi-immersion EMI students show a statistically significant gain in grammar over one academic year, while their cloze and listening gains are not significant

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` First-year semi-immersion EMI students improved significantly on a grammar sentence-manipulation task from T1 to T2 (p=.01), but showed no significant improvement on cloze or listening tasks. [→ Ament 2015](#ament-2015)

## Evidence

### Ament 2015

Ament, J. R., & Pérez Vidal, C. (2015). Linguistic outcomes of English medium instruction programmes in higher education: A study on economics undergraduates at a Catalan university. Higher Learning Research Communications, 5(1), 47-68. http://dx.doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v5i1.239

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-ranks analysis of pre/post scores for the semi-immersion group (N=9) over one academic year; grammar means rose from 32.0 to 43.6 while "A significant gain was detected in the grammar task (p=.01)"; cloze and listening were not significant.

> "The SIM group results (Table 7) show no significant improvements for the cloze or listening task, between T1 and T2.  A significant gain was detected in the grammar task (p=.01)."
