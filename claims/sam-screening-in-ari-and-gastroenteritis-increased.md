### Choi Sunhea 2020

Choi Sunhea, Yuen Ho Ming, Annan Reginald, Monroy-Valle Michele, Pickup Trevor, Aduku Nana Esi Linda, Pulman Andy, Portillo Sermeño Carmen Elisa, Jackson Alan A, Ashworth Ann. (2020). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Medical record review of children diagnosed with ARI or gastroenteritis, testing whether malnutrition status was checked on admission; the study reports "a significant increase in the proportion of children with ARI in whom SAM was identified (10.9% vs 7.1%, p=0.015)".

> "Postintervention there was a significant increase in the proportion of children with ARI in whom SAM was identified (10.9% vs 7.1%, p=0.015) and the same was found for gastroenteritis cases (13.3% vs 5.8%, p<0.001)."
