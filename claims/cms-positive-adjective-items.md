The EFA retained five positive adjectives for both CMS scales. The authors interpret this as replicating a previous finding that parental-adolescent metacognitive belief associations were significant for positive but not negative beliefs, and note that child responses for negative traits may be biased in adolescence.

> "The items loading on factor 1 represented for both scales were ﬁve positive adjectives, "polite, " "serious, " "kind, " "aﬀable, " and "graceful;" see Supplementary Appendix A–F."
