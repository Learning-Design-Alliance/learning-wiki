## Evidence

### Freeman Scott 2011

Freeman Scott, Haak David, Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2011). Increased Course Structure Improves Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-08-0105

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regressions of total exam points on final grade in each quarter, tested via analysis of deviance in a GLM with binomial error. The exam-points-only model was not improved by adding quarter (χ2 p = 0.6274) or an interaction (p = 0.9708), supporting the conclusion that grades were "not inflate[d]" by practice points.

> "R2 values indicate that, even in the most highly structured versions of the course, when exam points represented as little as 55% of the total course points, total exam points still explained 89% of the total variation in final grade (Table 4a)."
