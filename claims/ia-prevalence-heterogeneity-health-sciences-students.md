`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative synthesis of the 15 included cross-sectional studies reports heterogeneous prevalence, citing 84% of Bangladeshi medical students, "approximately 6% among Indian dental students [15] and 24.4% among Thai medical students [26]." It attributes heterogeneity to measurement tools, cut-offs, and cultural contexts.

> "The prevalence of IA among health sciences students varied considerably across studies, ranging from concerning to very high levels. In one sample, 84% of Bangladeshi medical students reported some degree of IA"
