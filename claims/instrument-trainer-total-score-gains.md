## Evidence

### Clarke David B. 2016

Clarke David B., Kureshi Nelofar, Hong Murray, Sadeghi Maryam, D'Arcy Ryan C. N. (2016). Simulation-based training for burr hole surgery instrument recognition. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0669-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of Group A residents' three PeriopSim Instrument Trainer sessions in the Rookie Camp randomized trial showed a significant main effect of total score (p < 0.0005), with Bonferroni-corrected post hoc tests reporting '10937 ± 1289 vs. 17029 ± 1427' between sessions 1 and 2 (p = 0.008).

> "Post hoc tests (Bonferroni corrected) revealed that the total score on the PeriopSim™ Instrument Trainer increased significantly from session 1 to session 2 (10937 ± 1289 vs. 17029 ± 1427, respectively, p = 0.008)."
