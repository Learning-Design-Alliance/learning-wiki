Evidence¶

Benedict du Boulay 2015¶

Benedict du Boulay. (2015). Recent Meta-reviews and Meta-analyses of AIED Systems. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0060-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The letter reports the Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper (2013) K-12 meta-analysis: "Smaller effect sizes were found" compared with other reviews, and "lower-achievers seemed to do worse with ITSs" than the broad spectrum of school pupils. No numeric effect sizes are printed in the letter.

"Smaller effect sizes were found by Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper ( 2013) in their meta-analysis of pupils using ITSs in a School setting. They also noted that lower-achievers seemed to do worse with ITSs than did the broad spectrum of school pupils"

Benedict du Boulay 2015 (2)¶

Benedict du Boulay. (2015). Recent Meta-reviews and Meta-analyses of AIED Systems. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0060-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The letter reports the companion meta-analysis of college students (Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper, 2014), which "found more positive effects for ITSs as compared to conventional instruction". No effect size is printed in the letter.

"In a parallel study of university students, Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper ( 2014) found more positive effects for ITSs as compared to conventional instruction"
