Bilgin and Dalkiran 2017¶

Nevruz Bilgin, Oğuzhan Dalkıran. (2017). Examining Attitudes of Students Regarding the Sports Education Model and Direct Teaching Model. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i12.2718

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of interview answers from the 18 experiment-group students after the 12-week season, evaluated by 3 domain experts from voice records. Students "stated that they enjoyed the non -player tasks"; coaching attracted the most interest, and students reported empathy and responsibility gains.

"all students stated that they enjoyed the non -player tasks, each student mentioned the tasks s/he likes according to his/her interest while some students said that these tasks became the occupation that they would like to do in the future."
