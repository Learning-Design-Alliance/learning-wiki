Evidence¶

Rodrigues Luiz 2023¶

Rodrigues Luiz, Palomino Paula T., Toda Armando M., Klock Ana C. T., Pessoa Marcela, Pereira Filipe D., Oliveira Elaine H. T., Oliveira David F., Cristea Alexandra I., Gasparini Isabela, Isotani Seiji. (2023). How Personalization Affects Motivation in Gamified Review Assessments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00326-x

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory moderator analyses (90% bootstrap CIs) across contextual moderators — four assessment subjects and usage intervals of 0, 4, or 6 weeks — plus user characteristics. The article concludes "did not moderate personalization's effect on any construct" for subject and interval, while listing the moderators that did; the abstract adds that motivation depended on fewer user characteristics under personalization.

"Overall, our findings indicate the assessment’s subject and usage interval did not moderate personalization’s effect on any construct, in contrast to gender, education, preferred game genre and playing setting, performance, and age (RQ1)."
