### Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema 2026

Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema. (2026). The Effect of Personalised Feedback in Simulation on Knowledge and Skills in the Management of Bleeding During Pregnancy: A Parallel Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70603

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison on the Evaluation of Simulation-Based Learning Scale in the RCT. Three of five subdimensions favoured the feedback group; "no statistically significant differences were found between the groups in the sub‐dimensions of Professional Knowledge (U = 323.0, p = 0.12) and Communication (U = 327.0, p = 0.57)".

> "the study group scored significantly higher than the control group in the sub‐dimensions of Nursing Process (33.9 ± 6.5 vs. 29.9 ± 7.0; U = 282.0, p = 0.03), Patient Security (27.1 ± 6.0 vs. 24.5 ± 3.5; U = 263.5, p = 0.01) and Reflected Behaviour (19.2 ± 5.1 vs. 16.1 ± 3.3; U = 278.5, p = 0.02)."
