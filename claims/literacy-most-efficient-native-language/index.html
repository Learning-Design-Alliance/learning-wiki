Evidence¶

Behrmann 2018¶

Behrmann, T. (2018). Evaluating the effects of mother tongue on math and science instruction. I. Sahin & T. Shelley (Eds.). Monument, CO, USA: ISTES Organization. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED623098

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Narrative literature review statement attributed to Spears (2010), quoted in the Statement of the Problem section. The author adds it could be assumed this includes achievement in math and science; no effect size is printed.

"Research has shown repeatedly that students acquire literacy most efficiently in their native language , that which they have spoken since infancy or early childhood. To expect them to acquire literacy in a language foreign to them is to unnecessarily hamper their academic achievement (Spears, 2010, p. 4)."
