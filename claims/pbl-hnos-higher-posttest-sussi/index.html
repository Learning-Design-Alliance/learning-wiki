Evidence¶

Ayşe Sert Çıbık 2016¶

Ayşe Sert Çıbık. (2016). The Effect of Project-Based History and Nature of Science Practices on the Change of Nature of Scientific Knowledge. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 453-472. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094587

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent samples t-test on SUSSI scores in a non-equivalent control group quasi-experiment (experimental N:41, control N:46). The article reports a significant post-test difference in favor of the experimental group, mean 103.10; no effect size is printed.

"The mean scores related to the overall questionnaire indicate that the mean score of the experimental group is higher than those of control group. That is to say, this significant difference is in favor of the experimental group [𝑋̅ (experimental)=103.10]."
