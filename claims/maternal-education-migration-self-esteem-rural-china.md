## Evidence

### Yu Wenjing 2022

Yu Wenjing, Qian Yiwei, Abbey Cody, Wang Huan, Rozelle Scott, Stoffel Lauren Ann, Dai Chenxu. (2022). The Role of Self-Esteem in the Academic Performance of Rural Students in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192013317

`q2 · i?`

OLS regression (Table 3, column 1) of RSES score on individual and household characteristics in the cross-sectional rural sample, with class-level clustered standard errors. The article reports the maternal education and maternal migration coefficients quoted above.

> "The RSES scores of students whose mothers had higher education levels were 0.62 points higher than those of their peers on average (p = 0.013), while the scores of students whose mothers migrated were 0.67 points lower (p < 0.001)."

### Yu Wenjing 2022 (2)

Yu Wenjing, Qian Yiwei, Abbey Cody, Wang Huan, Rozelle Scott, Stoffel Lauren Ann, Dai Chenxu. (2022). The Role of Self-Esteem in the Academic Performance of Rural Students in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192013317

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Probit model with marginal effects (Table 3, columns 2–3) on the binary low self-esteem outcome in the same sample. The article reports margins of 7% (p = 0.005) for maternal education and 6% (p = 0.003) for maternal migration.

> "Maternal education level was negatively associated with low self-esteem (margins = 7%, p = 0.005). Similarly, students were more likely to have low self-esteem if their mother migrated compared to those whose mother stayed at home (margins = 6%, p = 0.003)."
