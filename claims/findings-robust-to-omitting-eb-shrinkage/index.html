Evidence¶

Steven Glazerman and Jeffrey Max 2011¶

Steven Glazerman and Jeffrey Max. (2011). Do Low-Income Students Have Equal Access to the Highest-Performing Teachers? NCEE Technical Appendix. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20114016/pdf/20114016.pdf

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Alternative-specification check of the main distributional findings, re-estimating teacher performance measures without the empirical Bayes shrinkage step. The article states the "results were robust to this alternative specification".

"We also reproduced the findings when the value added estimates were not adjusted using Empirical Bayes shrinkage and the results were robust to this alternative specification."
