## Evidence

### Marta R. Stoeckel 2020

Marta R. Stoeckel. (2020). Constructivism in Online Science Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.07745](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.07745)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports this second-hand from Corter et al. (2011), comparing hands-on, remote, and simulated labs; hands-on groups "formed a group identity early in the process" while remote and simulation groups formed identity much later with significantly less cohesion. The review attributes this to hands-on labs requiring collaboration during data collection.

> "Corter, et al. (2011) found that when students conducted a hands-on lab, they formed a group identity early in the process which carried over into the data analysis and interpretation phases of the lab."
