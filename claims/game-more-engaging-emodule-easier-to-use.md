## Evidence

### Dankbaar Mary E. W. 2017

Dankbaar Mary E. W., Richters Olivier, Kalkman Cor J., Prins Gerrie, ten Cate Olle T. J., van Merrienboer Jeroen J. G., Schuit Stephanie C. E. (2017). Comparative effectiveness of a serious game and an e-module to support patient safety knowledge and awareness. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0836-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Evaluation questionnaire (23 items; engaging, educational, easy-to-use subscales) completed after the intervention. The article reports effect size magnitudes verbally without printing numeric values, so no standardized effect size is reported here. Interviews indicated the patient missions were the most motivating part.

> "The serious game was evaluated as more engaging than the e-module, with a very large effect size; the e-module was evaluated as more easy to use, with a medium effect size (Fig. 3)."
