Evidence¶

Fong 2025¶

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The 24-hour dosage analysis restricted the treatment group to students receiving at least 24 hours (80 percent of the 30-hour full dosage). It found "no statistically significant impact" on statewide summative assessments; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed for this outcome.

"This 24-hour dosage analysis found no statistically significant impact of the Air Tutors program on the statewide summative assessments but did find a positive and statistically significant impact on the district benchmark assessments in both districts."

Fong 2025 (2)¶

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

In the same 24-hour dosage analysis of benchmark growth, the program's estimated effect sizes were 0.13 in District A and 0.33 in District B, both positive and statistically significant per the report; both are small-to-moderate standardized effects, with 0.13 coded here as small.

"the program had an estimated effect size of 0.13 in District A and 0.33 in District B for the district benchmark assessments when using the 24-hour dosage threshold"
