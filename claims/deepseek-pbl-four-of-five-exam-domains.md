## Evidence

### Hou Jinxiao 2025

Hou Jinxiao, An Furun, Qin Hui, Zhang Lulu, Wang Juan, Zhang Cui, Fan Dachuan. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning in hematology residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07852-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same two-group trial (n = 30 per group), independent t-tests on the five 20-point exam components showed the DeepSeek-assisted group ahead in Exams I, III, IV and V, each reported with printed means and p-values; the Exam IV gain is described as "this modest improvement (0.97 points)" by the authors.

> "This group also obtained significantly higher scores in Exam Ⅰ (17.93 ± 0.74 vs. 16.80 ± 1.63, p = 0.001), Exam Ⅲ (17.87 ± 1.07 vs. 17.13 ± 1.11, p = 0.012), Exam Ⅳ (15.97 ± 0.89 vs. 15.00 ± 2.20, p = 0.031), and Exam Ⅴ (17.53 ± 0.90 vs. 16.60 ± 1.38, p = 0.003)"
