### Hengeveld B 2021

Hengeveld B, Maaskant JM, Lindeboom R, Marshall AP, Vermeulen H, Eskes AM. (2021). Nursing competencies for family‐centred care in the hospital setting: A multinational Q‐methodology study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 77(4), 1783–1799. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.14719

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative post hoc thematic analysis of participant commentaries on the five highest and lowest ranked competencies, coded independently by two researchers until consensus. Supporting quotes include that these competencies are "crucial to good support of patients and families" and "facilitates trust between nurse, family and patient".

> "The four main themes from the highest ranked competencies are believed to: (a) be preconditions for FCC; (b) promote a partnership between nurses, patients and families; (c) be a basic element of nursing; and (d) represent a necessary positive attitude and strong beliefs of the added value of FCC."
