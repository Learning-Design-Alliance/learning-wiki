### Zwolak 2015

Zwolak, J. P. and Manogue, C. A. (2015). Assessing student reasoning in upper-division electricity and magnetism at Oregon State University. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

New categorization of responses to CUE Q5 applied to N_OSU = 37 and N_CU = 37 relevant answers. The authors report that among correct answers, 23% of OSU versus 82% of CU students used the term, and attribute the difference to CU course materials emphasizing "superposition".

> "Of all relevant answers (combining A, B, C, D), 81% of CU students explicitly used the term “superposition,” compared to 22% students at OSU."
