## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-country path analysis comparing Czech (n = 1075) and Chinese (n = 710) samples. In the Czech model internet addiction negatively predicted coping with study demands (B = -0.09) and social adaptation (B = -0.07); in the Chinese model internet addiction positively predicted social adaptation (B = 0.13) and mobile phone addiction positively predicted academic performance (B = 0.11).

> "Surprisingly, the expected negative relationship of these addictions with academic achievement was found only for Czech students, while the relation was positive for Chinese students."
