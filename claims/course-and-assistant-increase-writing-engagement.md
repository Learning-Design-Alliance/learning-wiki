### Sven Scheu 2026

Sven Scheu, Simone N. Loeffler and Alexander Maedche. (2026). Designing a mobile chatbot-based learning journaling system for intrinsic motivation and engagement. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00589-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression modeling characters written per prompt (5181 unique messages after outlier removal; R²adj = 0.06, F(4, 1616) = 24.84, p < 0.001) in the field experiment. Table 2 prints unstandardized coefficients of Assistant B = 18.32 and Course B = 22.95 (both p < 0.001) and a negative Course * Assistant interaction of −15.21; mean message lengths were longer in all treatment groups (A: 71.25, C: 79.26, CA: 83.04) than baseline (51.21).
