### Glöckner 2013

Glöckner, A., Towfigh, E., & Traxler, C. (2013). Development of legal expertise. Instructional Science, 41, 989–1007. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-013-9266-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and regression analysis of 71,405 exams from 2,979 students. "Also both the general and the area-of-law specific learning effects of experience were higher for male students." No effect size is reported for this difference.

> "the data revealed a general gender effect, in that female (M = 5.83, SE = 0.039, N = 1,508) performed signiﬁcantly worse than male ( M = 6.10, SE = 0.041, N = 1,471) students, F(1, 2978) = 21.17, p\ 0.001."
