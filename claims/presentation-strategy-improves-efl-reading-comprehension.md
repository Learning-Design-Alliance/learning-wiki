Hooshang Khoshsima & Forouzan Rezaeiantiyar. (2014). The Effect of Presentation Strategy on Reading Comprehension of Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.101

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 61 intermediate EFL learners at Chabahar Maritime University randomly divided into experimental (n = 31) and control (n = 30) groups; the article reports a significant post-test difference with Sig. 0.000 < 0.05, so the "experimental group significantly achieved superior results than the control group". No effect size is printed.
