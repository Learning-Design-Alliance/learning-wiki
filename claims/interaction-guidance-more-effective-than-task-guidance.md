# Interaction-oriented pedagogical guidance was more effective than task-oriented guidance for 16-year-old students using video tools in history lessons

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Support for collaborative interactions was more effective than guidance directly related to students' assigned tasks in a group experiment with video tools for history lessons. [→ Gerry Stahl 2012](#gerry-stahl-2012)

Editorial summary of the experiment by Zahn, Krauskopf, Hesse & Pea reported in the issue: groups of 16-year-old students used video tools for history lessons under social-interaction versus cognitive-task guidance conditions.
