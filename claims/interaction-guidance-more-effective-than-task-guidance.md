### Gerry Stahl 2012

Gerry Stahl. (2012). Cognizing mediating: Unpacking the entanglement of artifacts with collective minds. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 7:187–191. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-012-9148-x

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Editorial summary of the experiment by Zahn, Krauskopf, Hesse & Pea reported in the issue: groups of 16-year-old students used video tools for history lessons under social-interaction versus cognitive-task guidance conditions. The editorial reports "support for their collaborative interactions was more effective than guidance directly related to their assigned tasks."

> "An experiment with groups of 16-year-old students using video tools for history lessons indicates that support for their collaborative interactions was more effective than guidance directly related to their assigned tasks."
