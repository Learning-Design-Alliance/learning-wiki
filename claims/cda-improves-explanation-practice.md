# CDA instruction significantly improved critical discourse analysis (explanation) practice, the largest test gain, and was the only practice where post questionnaire and test scores correlated

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Critical discourse analysis (explanation) questionnaire means rose from 2.46 to 3.70 and test means from 1.38 to 3.28, significant at .000; the post questionnaire and posttest correlated significantly only for explanation (.019). [→ Hazaea and AlZubi 2017](#hazaea-and-alzubi-2017)

## Evidence

### Hazaea and AlZubi 2017

Abduljalil Nasr Hazaea & Ali Abbas AlZubi. (2017). Effects of CDA Instruction on EFL Analytical Reading Practices. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 11(2), 88-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1171140

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the 11-item explanation subscale in the 43-student quasi-experiment; test means rose from 1.38 to 3.28, the highest mean improvement (1.91) of the three practices, significant at .000.

> "The critical discourse analysis (explanation practices) had a mean of 3.28 compared to 1.38."

### Hazaea and AlZubi 2017 (2)

Abduljalil Nasr Hazaea & Ali Abbas AlZubi. (2017). Effects of CDA Instruction on EFL Analytical Reading Practices. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 11(2), 88-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1171140

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational triangulation of post questionnaire and posttest scores in the same class: overall correlation was not significant (.428), and only the explanation category showed significance (.019, t = 2.438, df = 42).

> "The only significance can be seen in the critical discourse analysis (explanation category .019)."
