## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression results (Figure 5) in the full cohort; the article prints "aOR 1.78; P < .001" for male versus female and "aOR 1.58; P < .001" for African American/Black versus White PAs. The gender association held across bivariate, subgroup, and multivariate analyses.

> "Male PAs had 78% higher odds (aOR 1.78; P < .001) of choosing the traditional PANRE than female PAs, and African American/Black PAs, compared with their White colleagues, had 58% higher odds (aOR 1.58; P < .001) of selecting the traditional PANRE."
