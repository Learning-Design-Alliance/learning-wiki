`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment in an operations research course; the paper test was administered offline to all students just before the final exam after the treatment and control groups were merged. The article reports "a slight, yet statistically significant, advantage in favor of the AI group" and states Hypothesis 1 was not supported.

> "Not only did the offline group fail to outperform the AI-permitted group on the paper test, but there was in fact a slight, yet statistically significant, advantage in favor of the AI group (see Table 1)."
