## Evidence

### He Lidan 2026

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Standardized case-analysis assessment of clinical reasoning in 28 residents pre and post 12-month training. The two higher-order components had "the lowest pre-training scores" and largest improvement magnitudes; mean increases were 17.9 points (d = 2.40) and 16.8 points (d = 2.25).

> "risk assessment accuracy and comprehensive differential diagnosis, as higher-order clinical reasoning abilities, had the lowest pre-training scores (65.3 ± 8.4 and 67.8 ± 8.6 respectively) and the largest post-training improvement magnitudes (27.4% and 24.8% respectively)"

### He Lidan 2026 (2)

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Secondary hierarchical regression on the same cohort's reasoning data. The article reports β = 0.56, p < 0.001, R² = 0.31, and interprets that "these higher-order reasoning skills develop in an interdependent manner"; the authors present this as suggestive, not causal.

> "A hierarchical regression analysis revealed that improvements in risk assessment accuracy were significantly predicted by gains in comprehensive differential diagnosis ability (β = 0.56, p < 0.001, R² = 0.31), suggesting these higher-order reasoning skills develop in an interdependent manner."
