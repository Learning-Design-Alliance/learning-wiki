Annamalai N 2026¶

Annamalai N, Nasor M, Din Eak A and Ayoub Alkubaisy AA (2026) Students’ experiences of using ChatGPT for English language learning: a qualitative study in a Malaysian higher education institution. Front. Psychol. 17:1875727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1875727

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview thematic analysis: students reported learning independently from ChatGPT feedback, tailoring outputs to their level, and exercising judgement, e.g. one participant "had the freedom to modify the idea based on my understanding". Qualitative; no effect size printed.

"Overall, autonomy was experienced through learners’ perceptions of having control, choice, and responsibility in their Englishlanguagelearningratherthanthroughthemereavailability oftechnologicalassistance."
