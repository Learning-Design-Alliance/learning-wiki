Evidence¶

Deng Jing 2025¶

Deng Jing, Tan Yifei, Chen Jing, Guo Xiujing, Wang Ling, Xie Chuan. (2025). Virtual reality simulation to teach communication for undergraduate nursing students: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08450-7

q3 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review of seven studies of VR communication training for undergraduate nursing students. Among them, it charts a randomized controlled trial (Liaw et al., 2020) with 60 medical and 60 nursing students that compared 3 hours of team training in a 3D virtual environment versus a simulated ward. The review reports "no significant differences in the communication performance between virtual and simulation groups" and significant attitude increases in both groups, with no significant difference between them; neither modality was shown to outperform the other.

"There were no significant differences in the communication performance between virtual and simulation groups. Both groups showed significant increases in interprofessional attitudes, and no significant differences were reported between the groups over the 3 time points."
