## Evidence

### Richard Océane 2023

Richard Océane, Jollant Fabrice, Billon Grégoire, Attoe Chris, Vodovar Dominique, Piot Marie-Aude. (2023). Simulation training in suicide risk assessment and intervention: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2199469

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 7 RCTs (n = 1,646) assessing knowledge. Neither comparison was significant: "SMD =-0.10, 95% CI −0.39-0.19" versus active and "SMD = 1.14, 95% CI −0.48-2.75" versus inactive comparators; equivalence was not tested.

> "At immediate post-test to one-month follow-up, no significant difference was found when simulation was compared to an active comparator (two studies [42,43] SMD =-0.10, 95% CI −0.39-0.19) or to an inactive comparator (two studies [44,45]; SMD = 1.14, 95% CI −0.48-2.75)."
