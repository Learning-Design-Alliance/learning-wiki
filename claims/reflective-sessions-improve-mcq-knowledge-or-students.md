## Evidence

### Hassani Nia Nasser 2025

Hassani Nia Nasser, Karami Sahar, Shahraki Farimah, Kohan Mahmoud. (2025). Structured reflective sessions enhance knowledge and clinical skills in operating room technology students: a Solomon four-group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08431-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Solomon four-group quasi-experiment with 60 operating room technology students; intervention groups A and C showed higher post-test MCQ than controls, with "Cohen's d=3.12" and t=14.257, p<0.001.

> "Students who received reflective sessions (A and C) showed marked improvement (M≈17.46 ± 1.06, t=14.257, p<0.001, Cohen's d=3.12)."
