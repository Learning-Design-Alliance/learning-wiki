## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yangqiuting Li 2020

Yangqiuting Li, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How perception of being recognized or not recognized by instructors as a “physics person” impacts male and female students’ self-efficacy and performance. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1119/10.0002067](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002067)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Individual semi-structured think-aloud interviews with 30 student volunteers (20 women and 10 men). Women reported men were "generally praised more by the instructor/TA than women", that problems they asked about were labeled "easy", "obvious" or "trivial", and some had "contemplated switching out of their majors".

> "Some of them reported never going back to their instructor or TA after that for help."
