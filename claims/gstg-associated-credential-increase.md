Statewide credentialing data show "the number of preliminary teaching credentials issued to individuals prepared in California grew by 23% in 2023–24"; the report notes this increase is comparable to the number of additional GSTG awards granted in 2022–23, suggesting the grant may be strengthening the pipeline.

> "the number of preliminary teaching credentials issued to individuals prepared in California grew by 23% in 2023–24, rising from 11,325 credentials in 2022–23 to 13,914 in 2023–24."
