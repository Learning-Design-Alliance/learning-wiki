`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary outcome analysis in the same one-group pretest–posttest study of 44 students, using the Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test on the four error domains. Table 2 prints preparation 45.68% to 12.37%, administration 63.62% to 15.91%, follow-up 55.7% to 11.35%, recording 84.1% to 11.4%.

> "Similar statistically significant decreases were observed in preparation, administration, follow-up, and recording error rates (all P < .001; Table 2)."
