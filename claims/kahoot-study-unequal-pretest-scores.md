## Evidence

### Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet 2017

Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet. (2017). Enhancement of Performance and Motivation through Application of Digital Games in an English Language Class. Teaching English with Technology, 18(1), 77-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170635

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison of the two intact sections prior to intervention: the control group's pre-test mean was 14.15 (SD 2.23) and the experimental group's was 12.63 (SD 3.37). The article reports "a difference existed at a significance level of .05".

> "An independent samples t-test was employed to examine any significant difference. The finding showed that a difference existed at a significance level of .05 ( p< .05). This means that both groups were not equal."
