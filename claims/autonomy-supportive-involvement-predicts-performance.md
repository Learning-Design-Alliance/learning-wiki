## Evidence

### Yi Yang 2025

Yi Yang. (2025). Reciprocal Relationships Between Parental Involvement and Academic Performance in Early Adolescence: A Two-Year Longitudinal Study in China. Journal of Youth and Adolescence, 54(4), 876–889. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02102-7

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged SEM on the CEPS sample controlling for individual-, family-, school-, and county-level covariates. The article reports "education discussion (β = 0.13, p < 0.001)" as the strongest predictor; standardized βs of 0.04 and 0.02 are small by the study's benchmarks.

> "a higher frequency of educational trips (β = 0.04, p < 0.001), education discussion (β = 0.13, p < 0.001), and school contact and participation (β = 0.02, p < 0.05) in 7th grade predicted better academic performance in 8th grade."
