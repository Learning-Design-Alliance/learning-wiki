Documentation of the dashboard's data inclusion rules in the cautions section and FAQ. The guide states results appear only when "at least 20% of students were tested across most grades" and the sample has at least 10 students; State Groups requires both the 20% threshold and a minimum of 10 students in the group. No effect size applies.
