## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Experimental group affected expertise mentions (ε²=.093); ERS-only and combined groups mentioned expertise more than the control and video-only groups. [→ Colin Lescarret 2022](#colin-lescarret-2022)

## Evidence

### Colin Lescarret 2022

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis ANOVA on coded written justifications with effect size "ε²=.093" (χ²(3)=24.30, p<.001); 50.8% of students mentioned expertise, coded by two blind judges with 96% agreement on a 65-protocol subsample. DSCF pairwise comparisons showed ERS-only exceeded control (W=6.011, p<.001) and video-only (W=3.911, p=.029); combined exceeded control (W=5.425, p<.001).

> "A significant effect of the factor Experimental Groups was noted, χ²(3)=24.30, p<.001, ε²=.093"
