## Evidence

### Renza Metoděj 2023

Renza Metoděj, Peřan David, Sýkora Roman, Stern Michael, Zvoníček Václav, Waldauf Petr, Duška František. (2023). Influence of additional personal protective equipment on team performance in simulation-based emergency scenarios: a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2023.02.028

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis in the same 22-team randomised simulation trial. The article reports "average Laerdal index 11.5 [1.4] vs 11.2 [4.3] points, P=0.401" and no differences in milestone times; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "there were no differences in the overall quality of CPR (average Laerdal index 11.5 [1.4] vs 11.2 [4.3] points, P=0.401), or times to CPR milestones, such as time to start of chest compressions, time to first effective breath or time to securing the airway, and time to epinephrine administration."
