## Evidence

### Santos 2021

Santos, D. J. D., Goto, T., Lu, T.-Y., Ho, S. C.-C., Wang, T.-W., On, A. Y. L., Hashimoto, T., & Young, S.-C. (2021). Investigative Study on Preprint Journal Club as an Effective Method of Teaching Latest Knowledge in Astronomy. arXiv:2106.01688. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likert-scale survey of the NTHU AC class comparing enrollees (required to vote in VoxCharta, monitored each session) and volunteers. The test was significant: "U = 20, p = 0.035". Volunteers gave low comfort scores (6/8 with scores ≤ 3) versus enrollees (2/10 with scores ≤ 3).

> "Our countercheck with Mann- Whitney U test revealed that there is a signiﬁcant diﬀerence between enrollees and volunteers in their comfort of using VoxCharta (U = 20, p = 0.035)."
