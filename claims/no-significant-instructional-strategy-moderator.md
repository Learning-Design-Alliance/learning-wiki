`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression moderator analyses across the 19 included single-case studies testing three instructional-strategy moderators; none reached significance. The authors attribute this partly to the small number of studies and combined strategy packages.

> "We tested the impact of specific instructional strategies on the effect size of shared text reading, using a system of least to most intrusive prompts to help students answer comprehension questions (t(5.8) = 0.10, p = .924), explicit instruction of text comprehension strategies (t(6.5) = 1.32, p = .232), and teaching the meaning of wh-words (t(10.5) = 0.10, p = .921). No single instructional strategy is a significant moderator of the effect size."
