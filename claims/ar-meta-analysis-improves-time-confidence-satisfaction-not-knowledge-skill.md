`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Yeung et al., a meta-analysis of "13 studies with 654 participants" comparing AR to conventional teaching and non-AR techniques on skills, knowledge, confidence, performance time, and satisfaction. Only heterogeneity statistics (I²) and p-values are printed, no effect sizes.

> "Results showed that AR improved performance time (I² = 99.9%; P< 0.001), confidence (I² = 97.7%; P=0.02), and satisfaction (I² = 99.8%; P=0.006), but had no significant effect on knowledge or skill levels compared to control conditions"
