Assessing treatment fidelity and contamination in a cluster randomised controlled trial of motivational interviewing and cognitive behavioural therapy skills in type 2 diabetes. BMC Family Practice, 19, 60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0742-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

BECCI ratings of the fidelity-assessment recordings (266 usable recordings, 151 participants) assessed nurses' competence in eliciting patients' thoughts and cognitions, addressing the CBT element. Means were 1.07 (SD 0.48) control vs 1.42 (SD 0.51) intervention, and "The estimated standardised mean difference was 0.75".

> "A z-test from a mixed effects model showed a significant difference in the BECCI Practitioner Scores between the treatment arms (z = 3.22, p < .01, 95% CI 0.15–0.62). The estimated standardised mean difference was 0.75."
