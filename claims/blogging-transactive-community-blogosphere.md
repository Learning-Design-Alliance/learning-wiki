Editorial summary of Alterman and Larusson's extended theoretical and empirical analysis of knowledge creation in loosely coordinated learning activities, contrasted with meaning making in tightly coupled joint problem spaces more common in CSCL research.

> "While blogging is a predominantly individual writing and reading experience, it also provides for transactive building on the reasoning of other students and, over time, in the emergence of common knowledge from participation in the persistent and growing community blogosphere."
