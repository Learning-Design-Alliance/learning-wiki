### Mooney Kate E. 2024

Mooney Kate E., Cheung Rachael W., Blower Sarah L., Allen Richard J., Waterman Amanda. (2024). Do executive functions and processing speed mediate the relationship between socioeconomic status and educational achievement? Analysis of an observational birth cohort study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02243-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM Model 2 of the Born in Bradford estimation sample (n = 4201) measured inhibition with a Flanker task (RT congruent minus incongruent); the bootstrapped indirect effect was "not significant, at B = 0.001 [-0.003 to 0.006]" (p = 0.643). Higher SES was associated with better inhibition scores, but inhibition was not associated with educational achievement.

> "The path between socioeconomic status and educational achievement via inhibition was not significant, at B = 0.001 [-0.003 to 0.006]."
