## Evidence

### Cowan Nelson 2014

Cowan Nelson. (2014). Working Memory Underpins Cognitive Development, Learning, and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4207727/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that Jevons' 1871 self-experiment on estimating beans without counting, with 1,027 trials, found no errors for sets of 3 or 4 beans, and that "the finding that normal adults typically can keep in mind only about 3 or 4 items has been replicated many times in modern research" using many other methods.

> "the finding that normal adults typically can keep in mind only about 3 or 4 items has been replicated many times in modern research, using methods similar to Jevons"
