Rosenheck 2021¶

Rosenheck, Louisa; Cheng, Meng-Tzu; Lin, Chen-Yen; Klopfer, Eric. (2021). Approaches to illuminate content-specific gameplay decisions using open-ended game data. Springer US. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09989-0

The article asserts this framing statement about games as learning environments in its abstract; it offers no effect size or test statistic for the assertion itself, and the supporting study is the Radix Endeavor genetics analysis.
