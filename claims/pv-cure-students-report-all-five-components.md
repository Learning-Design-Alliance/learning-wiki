`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic coding of 108 deidentified end-of-semester Report to Future Researchers assignments from the Fall 2024 first implementation of the PHYS 2150 CURE, coded at the sentence level and reported at the student level. The article reports that "Students brought up using scientific practices in 87% of the reports."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Same qualitative coding study of student reports. Within the scientific-practices code, the article reports that "coding (programming) was the most commonly mentioned (72%)"; students framed coding as a professional tool, a relevance of modern research, and a confidence builder.
