## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All instructional groups improved from pretest to both posttests on production (Mdiff = 4.82, p < .001 at immediate; Mdiff = 4.92, p < .001 at delayed), sustaining gains three weeks after instruction. [→ Adem Soruç 2017](#adem-soruc-2017)

## Evidence

### Adem Soruç 2017

Adem Soruç, Jingjing Qin, YouJin Kim. (2017). Comparing the Effectiveness of Processing Instruction and Production-Based Instruction on L2 Grammar Learning: The Role of Explicit Information. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(2), 49–70. http://dx.doi.org/1018806/tesl.v34i2.1262

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted main-effect contrasts for time on the oral production task. The article reports immediate-posttest gains of "Mdiff = 4.82, p < .001" and delayed-posttest gains of "Mdiff = 4.92, p < .001" over pretest for all instructional groups. No effect size is printed.

> "All the instructional groups scored higher on the immediate posttest (Posttest 1) than they did on the pretest, Mdiff = 4.82, p < .001, and they similarly produced more accurately on the delayed posttest (Posttest 2) than they did on the pretest, Mdiff = 4.92, p < .001."
