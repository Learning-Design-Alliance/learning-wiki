## Evidence

### Chin Ronald Yoon-Kong 2019

Chin Ronald Yoon-Kong, Tjahjono Richard, Rutledge Michael John Raymond, Lambert Tim, Deboever Nathaniel. (2019). The evaluation of e-learning resources as an adjunct to otolaryngology teaching: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1618-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot randomized controlled trial: 19 final-year University of Sydney medical students randomized to e-learning access plus self-directed study (Group A, n=8) or study-as-usual (Group B, n=11), assessed by blinded examiners with standardized OSCE forms. The intervention group scored "78.50 ± 13.88 v. 55.82 ± 8.23; P = < 0.01" on the overall examination. No effect size was printed.

> "Students in Group A were able to perform significantly better than Group B in the overall (78.50 ± 13.88 v. 55.82 ± 8.23; P = < 0.01) and individual station scores."
