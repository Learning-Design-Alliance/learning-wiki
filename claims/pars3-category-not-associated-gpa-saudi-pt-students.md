## Evidence

### Alhusayni AI 2026

Alhusayni AI. (2026). Association of physical activity and mental health symptoms to the academic performance of physical therapy students in Saudi Arabia: A cross-sectional study. Medicine 2026;105:1(e46934). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000046934

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 600 undergraduate physical therapy students in Saudi Arabia; Pearson chi-square test of PARS-3 activity categories against GPA categories found "no statistically significant association between PARS-3 categories and GPA". The ANOVA of PARS-3 scores across GPA groups was likewise non-significant (P = .40).

> "The analysis indicated no statistically significant association between PARS-3 categories and GPA (χ² = 5.89, P = .21)."
