## Evidence

### Colin S. Wallace 2017

Colin S. Wallace, Timothy G. Chambers, Edward E. Prather. (2017). An item response theory evaluation of the Light and Spectroscopy Concept Inventory national data set. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of pre-instruction IRT-estimated abilities between high-IAS (average -1.2 logits) and low-IAS (average -0.95 logits) students in classes with at least 25 students. The article describes this result as "Interestingly – and unexpect- edly" significant, with "Cohen's d = 0.49".

> "a one-tailed t-test also revealed that the diﬀerence in the pre-instruction means was also statistically signiﬁcant (p < 0.0001) and of medium eﬀect size (Co- hen's d = 0.49)"
