## Evidence

### Wittich CM 2017

Wittich CM, Agrawal A, Cook DA, Halvorsen AJ, Mandrekar J, Chaudhry S, Dupras DM, Oxentenko AS, Beckman TJ. (2017). E-learning in graduate medical education: survey of residency program directors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0953-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National APDIM survey of US internal medicine residency program directors; 214 of 368 completed the e-learning section. Programs reported "153; 71.5%" using asynchronous e-learning versus "85 (39.7%)" using synchronous at least sometimes; frequencies are descriptive with no effect size reported.

> "Among the 214 programs with PDs who responded, 85 (39.7%) use synchronous e-learning at least sometimes, somewhat often, or very often (Fig. 1). In contrast to synchronous e-learning, more programs (153; 71.5%) use asynchronous e-learning at least sometimes, somewhat often, or very often."
