## Evidence

### Cheung H. C. Kristy 2026

Cheung H. C. Kristy, Zhong Lily Shengjia, Wall Shelley L., Lisk Kristina. (2026). Exploring the impact of virtual reality anatomy training on preparing biomedical illustrators for drawing anatomical structures. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70150

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group t-test at the second illustration in the crossover design (VR_1st n = 8, Pro_1st n = 9). Baseline drawings showed no group difference, confirming randomization; no group differences were found for the third illustration.

> "For the second illustration, participants who drew using a prosection (i.e., Pro_1st) performed significantly better than those in the VR_1st group (difference = 4.1, t(15) = 3.4, p < 0.01, ES = 1.46)."
