## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2014

Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square analysis of pass rates across the three EBM cohorts for each of the 15 items. The wide difficulty range is presented as allowing implementation "across a wide range of participants including novice to expert".

> "Relative difficulty (item difficulty) of each item was broad and ranged from a pass rate of 36% (item 15) to 84% (item 3)."

### Ilic Dragan 2014 (2)

Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Per-item chi-square tests compared pass rates among EBM-novice, intermediate and advanced groups. Items 3 and 4 (searching the literature) showed no significant between-group difference (item 3 p = 0.059; item 15 p = 0.49 also non-significant per Table 2).

> "Statistically significant pass rates were observed between groups for all items apart from items 3 and 4."
