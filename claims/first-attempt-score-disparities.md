Optional Exam Retakes Reduce Anxiety but may Exacerbate Score Disparities Between Students with Different Social Identities. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Model 1 regressions on first-attempt scores across the three courses found "Pell-eligible students received lower total scores on first exam attempts" in all three, and Black and Latinx students scored lower than White students with course-specific significance patterns.

> "Pell-eligible students received lower total scores on first exam attempts compared with students who were not eligible for Pell grants in all three courses. Black and Latinx students received lower total scores on first exam attempts compared with White students, although this was statistically significant for Black students only in Courses 1 and 2, and for Latinx students only in Courses 2 and 3."
