# Being good role models, taking interest in students' lives outside class, and allowing students choices were the behaviours most consistently associated with positive change in engagement

### Michael Noetel 2023

Michael Noetel, Philip Parker, Theresa Dicke, Mark R. Beauchamp, Nikos Ntoumanis, Ryan M. Hulteen, et al. (2023). Prediction Versus Explanation in Educational Psychology: a Cross-Theoretical Approach to Using Teacher Behaviour to Predict Student Engagement in Physical Education. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Elastic-net regression on student ratings of 71 teacher behaviours from 1324 Year 7 students in low-SES schools identified the behaviours most consistently associated with positive change in engagement, including "being good role models (β = 0.046)". Coefficients are reported as printed; no standardised effect size is given.

> "Some behaviours that were most consistently associated with a positive change in engagement were being good role models (β = 0.046), taking interest in students' lives outside of class (β = 0.033), and allowing students to make choices (β = 0.029)."
