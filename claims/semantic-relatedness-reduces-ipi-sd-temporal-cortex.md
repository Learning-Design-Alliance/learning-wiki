# Memorizing semantically related words increases the regularity of alpha-to-beta EEG oscillations (reduced IPI-SD) over posterior temporal cortex contralateral to the memory field

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` IPI-SD was significantly reduced in Sem versus NonSem trials in both visual fields, with small printed d values at temporal electrodes (e.g., d = −0.15 at T5). [→ Noguchi 2024](#noguchi-2024)

## Evidence

### Noguchi 2024

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 EEG analysis of 32-sensor data (n = 34) during delay 5: "signiﬁcant reductions in Sem compared to NonSem trials" in IPI-SD over posterior temporal cortex contralateral to the cued hemifield; e.g., T5 t(33) = −4.54, p = 0.00007, d = −0.15. Survived FDR correction and ANCOVA controlling amplitude.

> "the SD of IPIs, a measure for irregularity of EEG waveforms, showed signiﬁcant reductions in Sem compared to NonSem trials (Fig.4c) both when memory items were presented in left and right visualﬁelds"

### Noguchi 2024 (2)

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 memory task (n = 27 final) replicated Experiment 1: "more regular in Sem than NonSem trials" during D5, most distinctly at CP5, t(26) = 3.33, p = 0.003, d = 0.15. The article prints d values that are small despite significant t-tests.

> "Oscillatory signals during D5 were more regular in Sem than NonSem trials (left panel. The most distinct difference over the l e f tt e m p o r a lr e g i o nw a ss e e na te l e c t r o d eC P 5(t(26) = 3.33, p = 0.003, d = 0.15)."

### Noguchi 2024 (3)

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary correlational analysis in Experiment 1 linking the neural regularity measure to behavior: the "reduction in IPI-SD between Sem and NonSem was signiﬁcantly correlated with the FA rates to lure probes". No correlation coefficient is printed.

> "the reduction in IPI-SD between Sem and NonSem was signiﬁcantly correlated with the FA rates to lure probes (Supplementary Fig.1), suggesting a close relationship of IPI-SD with a false memory (semantic integration)."
