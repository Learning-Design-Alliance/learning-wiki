### Rusman 2020

Rusman, Sunarti, & Rahadian. (2020). Application of the "Among" Leadership Model to Improve Teacher Work Discipline. International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section of the action research, using a Likert-converted indicator of weekly hours fulfilled against a 37.5-hour benchmark. The fulfillment mean "tends to increase continuously" from 2.06 to 4.19; only descriptive means are printed, with no effect size.

> "the mean achievement of teacher work discipli ne in the aspect of working hours fulfillment before the action is 2.06 (low). When observed after the application of the “Among” leadership model, the mean aspect score of working hours fulfillment in the first cycle increased to 3.13 (medium), increased again in the second cycle to 3.50 (high), and increased again in the third cycle to 4.19 (high)."
