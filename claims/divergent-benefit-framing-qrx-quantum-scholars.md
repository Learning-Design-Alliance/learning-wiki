## Evidence

### Michael B. Bennett 2024

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis of six QRX students; five of six cited the end-of-program Quantum Recruiting Forum as a major benefit, and multiple students reported affective benefits such as increased sense of self-efficacy or community in QIS.

> "Across the board, QRX students couched their experience through the program in terms of career exploration, skill-building, and professional networking"

### Michael B. Bennett 2024 (2)

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis of six Quantum Scholars students; all six named a NIST laboratory tour as their favorite single experience, and benefits included guest lectures and community building such as finding roommates and building clubs.

> "the Quantum Scholars group spoke of their program experiences mostly in terms of the academic or intellectual benefit enrichment they provided in parallel to the students’ degree path"
