`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A theoretical argument in the comment: initial and final test differences can arise from any number of common causes, including unmeasured ones, so long as the trait affects both test sets similarly. The comment offers this as a logical critique, not an empirical test.

> "Thus the core result, the insignificance of direct demographic prediction terms for the college tests once pre-college tests are included in the model, does not provide information to distinguish what causes the stable individual differences."
