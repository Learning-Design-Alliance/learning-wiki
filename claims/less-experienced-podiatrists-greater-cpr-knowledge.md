### Brenton-Rule 2021

Brenton-Rule, A., Harvey, D., Moran, K., O'Brien, D., & Webber, J. (2021). Knowledge and perceptions of cardiopulmonary resuscitation amongst New Zealand podiatrists: a web-based survey. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research, 14, 40. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-021-00481-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational chi-square analysis within the survey (N=171) of knowledge by years of experience. The article reports "more correct responses among podiatrists with less experience (< 10 years, 60 %; >10 years, 41 %)" with χ2 (1) = 5.702, p = 0.017. No effect size printed.

> "One significant difference in CPR knowledge was evident in the correlational analysis of length of work experience, with more correct responses among podiatrists with less experience (< 10 years, 60 %; >10 years, 41 %) (χ2 (1) = 5.702, p = 0.017)."
