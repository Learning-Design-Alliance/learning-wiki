## Evidence

### Poorthuis 2015

Poorthuis, A. M. G., Juvonen, J., Thomaes, S., Denissen, J. J. A., de Castro, B. O., & van Aken, M. A. G. (2015). Do grades shape students' school engagement? The psychological consequences of report card grades at the beginning of secondary school. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(3), 842-854. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`
