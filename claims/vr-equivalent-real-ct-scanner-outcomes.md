The editorial reports, citing Taylor et al.'s narrative review, statistical evidence of no statistical difference between using VR and physically operating a real scanner for CT technical knowledge and decision-making. Equivalence was not formally established beyond the reported absence of difference.

> "Furthermore, statistical evidence is provided by Taylor et al., 7 suggesting there is a place for VR to aid technical knowledge and decision‐making in CT, with no statistical difference found between using VR and physically operating a real scanner."
