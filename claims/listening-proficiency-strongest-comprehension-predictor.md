## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, Y., & Hu, G. (2026). Investigating L2 listening comprehension and experience in immersive and interactive virtual reality: An experimental study. Computers & Education, 248, 105593. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105593

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression Model 1 on RCT data (N = 84) showed listening proficiency predicted comprehension. The article reports it "uniquely accounting for 46% of its variance" and remained the strongest predictor in subsequent models.

> "In Model 1 (see Table 3), listening proficiency significantly predicted listening comprehension (B = 0.62, β = .68, p < .001), uniquely accounting for 46% of its variance (r2 partial = .46)."
