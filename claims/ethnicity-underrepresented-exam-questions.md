The article reports, citing Marjadi (2024), an audit of 3,566 exam questions across three schools in Australia and Europe; the review attributes the finding that "only 7.2% of questions ... mentioned the patient's ethnicity". The reviewed audit's design is not described here beyond the audit itself.

> "In an audit of 3,566 medical school exam questions across three schools, one in Australia and two in Europe, only 7.2% of questions that presented a patient mentioned the patient’s ethnicity (Marjadi, 2024). By contrast, 89% of such questions specified the patient’s age and 94% specified the patient’s sex."
