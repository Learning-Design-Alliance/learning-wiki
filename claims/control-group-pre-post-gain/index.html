Oluwadare 2015¶

Oluwadare, F. A. (2015). ICT Use in Preschool Science Education: A Case Study of Some Private Nursery Schools in Ekiti State. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(31). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083397

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test within the control group (128 children) receiving traditional story-based teaching, experiments on dissolving materials, and bin-sorting recycling activities. Table 3 prints t = -31.511, df = 136, mean difference -9.130, significance 0.000; no effect size is printed.

"The mean grade for the pre-test in the study was 25.50 (SD=2.51) compared to 34.65 (SD=3.20) for the post-test. At α = .05 and df=136, the critical value of the t ratio was less than 0.0 01"
