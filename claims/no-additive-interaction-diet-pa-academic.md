## Evidence

### McIsaac J.-L.D. 2015

McIsaac J.-L.D., Kirk S.F.L., Kuhle S. (2015). The Association between Health Behaviours and Academic Performance in Canadian Elementary School Students: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph121114857

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Combined-model analysis using the 4-level diet quality/PA variable reported in the Results; the article states this is the first study to examine a potential additive interaction between diet quality and PA on academic performance and found none.

> "There was no additive interaction between diet quality and PA for either ELA or mathematics as the combined effect of having both low diet quality and low PA did not exceed the sum of the individual effects"
