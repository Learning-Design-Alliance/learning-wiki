## Evidence

### Renza Metoděj 2023

Renza Metoděj, Peřan David, Sýkora Roman, Stern Michael, Zvoníček Václav, Waldauf Petr, Duška František. (2023). Influence of additional personal protective equipment on team performance in simulation-based emergency scenarios: a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2023.02.028

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled simulation trial: 22 three-person ICU teams (one doctor, two nurses) randomised 1:1 after a baseline scenario. The SSS-PPE group scored "TEAM total score 30.6 [5.6] vs 36.2 [5.0], P=0.030". Study powered to 80% for detecting a 25% difference in TEAM scores.

> "We found a statistically significant difference in TEAM scores in the SSS-PPE group compared with the WHO-PPE group (TEAM total score 30.6 [5.6] vs 36.2 [5.0], P=0.030)."
