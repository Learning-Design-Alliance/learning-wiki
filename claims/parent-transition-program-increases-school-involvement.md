## Evidence

### Giallo 2010

Giallo, R., Treyvaud, K., Matthews, J., & Kienhuis, M. (2010). Making the Transition to Primary School: An Evaluation of a Transition Program for Parents. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 10, 1-17. https://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the same cluster-randomized trial, school-based parent involvement was measured at Time 2 using the Walker et al. involvement scale. Intervention parents reported significantly greater school involvement than controls, with a small effect size (d = 0.27).

> "Follow-up univariate results revealed that at post-test parents in the intervention condition reported greater involvement in their child's learning at school than the control condition, F(1,325)=5.96, p=.015, d=0.27, 95% CI (0.05, 0.49)."
