Evidence¶

Tanmay Sinha and Manu Kapur 2021¶

Tanmay Sinha and Manu Kapur. (2021). When Problem Solving Followed by Instruction Works: Evidence for Productive Failure. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211019105

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-regression within the meta-analysis modeling the interaction of intervention time span with overall PF fidelity score. The fidelity benefit "was in fact a decrease for interventions spanning a few hours (β = −0.0116, p = .0546, 95% CI [−0.0235, 0.0002])", marginally significant; longer interventions also had higher fidelity scores (mean 74.45 vs 42.01).

"Despite an overall increase in effect size with increase in overall PF fidelity (β = 0.0173, p = .0005, 95% CI [0.0077, 0.0269]), there was in fact a decrease for interventions spanning a few hours (β = −0.0116, p = .0546, 95% CI [−0.0235, 0.0002])."
