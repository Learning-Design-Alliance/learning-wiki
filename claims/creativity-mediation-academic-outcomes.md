## Evidence

### Denervaud Solange 2019

Denervaud Solange, Knebel Jean-François, Hagmann Patric, Gentaz Edouard. (2019). Beyond executive functions, creativity skills benefit academic outcomes: Insights from Montessori education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225319

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple mediation model on pooled z-scored data from all 201 children, with school system as predictor and executive functions, well-being, and creativity as simultaneous mediators; the standardized indirect effect via creativity was ß = 0.39 (p < 0.001), indicating "partial mediation of Montessori system by creativity on the academic outcomes".

> "There was a significant indirect effect of creativity skills only on academic outcomes, z > 2, p = 0.04."
