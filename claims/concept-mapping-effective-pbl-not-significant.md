## Evidence

### Lee JuHee 2016

Lee JuHee, Lee Yoonju, Gong SaeLom, Bae Juyeon, Choi Moonki. (2016). A meta-analysis of the effects of non-traditional teaching methods on the critical thinking abilities of nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5025580/

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis by educational method using random effects model. Concept mapping (three studies) showed significant effect with SMD = 0.68, 95% CI 0.26–1.11, p = 0.002, I² = 77%.

> "Analysis of the effects of teaching and learning methods revealed that concept mapping (SMD: 0.68, 95 % CI: 0.26–1.11, p = 0.002, I2 = 77 %) was effective in improving critical thinking (Fig. 6)."

### Lee JuHee 2016 (2)

Lee JuHee, Lee Yoonju, Gong SaeLom, Bae Juyeon, Choi Moonki. (2016). A meta-analysis of the effects of non-traditional teaching methods on the critical thinking abilities of nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5025580/

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Subgroup meta-analysis by educational method. PBL (three studies) was "not significantly effective in improving critical thinking" with SMD 0.34, 95% CI −0.03–0.70, p = 0.07, I² = 62%. No effect size interpretation beyond printed SMD.

> "However, PBL (SMD: 0.34, 95 % CI: −0.03–0.70, p = 0.07, I2 = 62 %) was not significantly effective in improving critical thinking."
