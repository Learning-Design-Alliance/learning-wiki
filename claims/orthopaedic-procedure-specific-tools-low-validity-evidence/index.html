Li Yibo 2024¶

Li Yibo, Chan Robert, Menon Matthew R.G., Ryan Joanna F., Mador Brett, Campbell Sandra M., Turner Simon R. (2024). Validity Evidence for Procedure-specific Competence Assessment Tools in Orthopaedic Surgery: A Scoping Review. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-23-00065

q3 · i? · review · r3

Scoping review in which two independent reviewers scored validity evidence for 24 procedure-specific orthopaedic assessment tools using the five-domain framework of Ghaderi et al with a maximum score of 15. The review reports that "Validity evidence was low across all studies, ranging from 1 to 9 of a maximum score of 15".

"Validity evidence was low across all studies, ranging from 1 to 9 of a maximum score of 15 (Table 2)."
