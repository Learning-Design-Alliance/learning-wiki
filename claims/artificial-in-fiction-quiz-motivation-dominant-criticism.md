### F. Rogosch 2026

F. Rogosch, A. Schrader. (2026). AI-Generated Interactive Fiction for Educational Use: A Pilot Study of Perceived Comprehensibility, Coherence, and Engagement. Proceedings of EDULEARN26 Conference. https://doi.org/10.21125/edulearn.2026.1075

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive single-rater thematic analysis of free-text responses from 10 of 22 participants (STEM higher-education students, staff and graduates) who played scenarios generated by one pipeline configuration from one STEM sub-domain (media technology); the authors present the themes as illustrating their quantitative findings for that configuration, not AI-generated interactive fiction in general. The dominant theme was "the artificial in -fiction motivation for the quiz prompts", named in six of ten responses and linked by several participants to lower coherence.

> "The dominant criticism is the artificial in -fiction motivation for the quiz prompts (Theme 2, six of ten responses): participants ask why the non-player characters would demand technical knowledge of the player at all"
