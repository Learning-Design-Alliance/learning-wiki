### Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025

Study 3 mixed ANOVA main effect on response frequency (sum scores): the main effect was significant with a medium printed effect size, partial eta squared ηp2 = 0.082 (ηp² = 0.082; M=54.5 high vs M=53.3 regular). Under high threat, participants favored flight over fight even when exposed to CS+A (fight), M=59.7 vs 49.7.
