`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design proposal (type d/e) describing the core game: the class splits into qubit-students and scientist-students, with scientists preparing and measuring qubits by hitting them with a ball, and "the students can experience ﬁrst-hand the puzzling features of quantum mechanics". No empirical evaluation is reported in the text provided.

> "The students play the role of both qubits and scientists and the measurement results that they obtain as scientists are identical to the ones they would obtain in a real laboratory."
