### Peters 2022

Peters, V., Pakhira, D., White, L., Fennelly-Atkinson, R., & Means, B. (2022). Designing gateway statistics and chemistry courses for today's students: Case studies of postsecondary course innovations. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/162

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of course completion data across fall 2019 (N = 102) and fall 2020 (N = 88) sections, adjusting for prior achievement, race, gender, age, enrollment status, and repeater status; the article reports improvement over terms with confidence intervals but no standardized effect sizes.

> "Student course outcomes improved as the instructor gained experience using adaptive courseware in her class. This was particularly the case for students who were Pell-eligible and students who were taking the co-requisite course."
