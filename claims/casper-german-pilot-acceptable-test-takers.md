### Knorr Mirjana 2024

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Acceptability survey of the 2020 pilot cohort of German applicants and students who took Casper; descriptive statistics only. Participants rated overall experience M=5.40 and fairness M=5.24 on 7-point scales, indicating they "perceived Casper as rather fair".

> "Overall, participants of the 2020 study evaluated Casper favorably with a mean rating of 7.55 (SD=1.64, n=368) on a 10-point scale."
