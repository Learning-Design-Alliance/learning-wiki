Evidence¶

Keller 2020¶

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Interrupted regression with the two-lines test applied to representative PISA mathematics data across 13 countries and three cycles, using the Robin Hood algorithm to locate the breakpoint. The printed coefficient for higher achievers was "mean β2 = 0.47"; the finding "held for the vast majority of countries".

"mathematics achievement was not significantly related to mathematics self-concept for lower achieving students, but the relation was significantly positive for higher achieving students (mean β1 = 0.03, 95% CI [-0.01, 0.07]; mean β2 = 0.47, 95% CI [0.41, 0.53]; Figure 1b)."
