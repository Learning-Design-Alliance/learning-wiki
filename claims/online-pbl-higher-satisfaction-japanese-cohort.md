## Evidence

### Basile 2026

Basile, C., Nishida, K., Kondo, T., Takahashi, N., Takami, H., Nishigori, H., Aleksic, B., Dolladille, C., Villalobos, I. B., Yagi, T., Skokauskas, N., & Kasuya, H. (2026). Comparing traditional and online problem-based learning in child and adolescent psychiatry in Nagoya: a novel statistical approach in Japanese educational settings. BMC Medical Education.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A naturalistic historical-control study of 109 in-person (2019) and 100 online (2021) fourth-year medical students used ordered logistic regression adjusted for age and sex; the article reports "significantly higher satisfaction across most domains (Odds Ratios 0.36–0.51, p < 0.05)". Odds ratios below 1 indicate greater online satisfaction due to reverse coding.
