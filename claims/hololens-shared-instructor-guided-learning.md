## Evidence

### Lin Joseph 2026

Lin Joseph, Ghazi Sarmad, Kuss Ethan, Shehata Sarah. (2026). Medical students’ experiences of mixed-reality-based anatomy instruction in a non-cadaveric curriculum: a qualitative case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09881-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 12 first-year osteopathic medical students; thematic analysis found shared viewing and instructor-guided pointing synchronized what students saw with faculty explanation. One participant said "That collaborative thing was a part of HoloLens that I didn’t find anywhere else."

> "The fact that the professors could kind of point things out to us. We see exactly what they’re referring to … That collaborative thing was a part of HoloLens that I didn’t find anywhere else."
