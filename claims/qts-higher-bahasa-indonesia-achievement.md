## Evidence

### Gunarhadi 2014

Gunarhadi, Mustapa Kassim & Abdull Sukor Shaari. (2014). THE IMPACT OF QUANTUM TEACHING STRATEGY ON STUDENT ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENTS AND SELF-ESTEEM IN INCLUSIVE SCHOOLS. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction: Vol. 11 (2014): 191-205. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137291

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttests-only quasi-experiment with two intact groups of 34 sixth graders each, sampled from 78 inclusive elementary schools in Boyolali, Central Java. Post-treatment t-test showed the experimental group "is higher and signiﬁ cant than that in the Control group" in Bahasa Indonesia, t(68) = 3.47, p = 0.001 (one-tailed).

> "Table 2 shows that the achievement of students in Bahasa Indonesia in the Experimental group (M = 8.00, SD = 1.17) is higher and signiﬁ cant than that in the Control group (M = 7.114, SD = 0.79), t (68) = 3.47, p = 0.001 (one-tailed)."
