## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pfund Christine 2015

Pfund Christine, Spencer Kimberly C., Asquith Pamela, House Stephanie C., Miller Sarah, Sorkness Christine A. (2015). Building National Capacity for Research Mentor Training: An Evidence-Based Approach to Training the Trainers. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-10-0184

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retrospective pre/post confidence survey of attendees at the 1.5-d facilitator training preparing facilitators for the 16-site RCT (Table 5 reports n = 31): attendees "reported significant gains in their confidence across five major learning objectives for the FT", with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.

> "At the end of the 1.5-d training, attendees reported significant gains in their confidence across five major learning objectives for the FT (Table 5)."

### Pfund Christine 2015 (2)

Pfund Christine, Spencer Kimberly C., Asquith Pamela, House Stephanie C., Miller Sarah, Sorkness Christine A. (2015). Building National Capacity for Research Mentor Training: An Evidence-Based Approach to Training the Trainers. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-10-0184

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retrospective confidence survey at four national Facilitating Entering Mentoring workshops (Table 7 reports n = 85): "all components resulted in significant gains (p < 0.01)", with objectives including utilizing the curricula, facilitating the process-based approach, and implementing RMT at home institutions.

> "As with previous FT, attendees rated each component of the workshop (Table 6) and their confidence, retrospectively, at the end of the FT (Table 7); all components resulted in significant gains (p < 0.01)."
