## Evidence

### Stone Alicia 2026

Stone Alicia, Vicente Maria Asuncion, Xia Oudong, He Yuchen, Chen Chen, Yin Rong, Zhang Wuyang, Chang Haoli, Yang Wei, Li Fei, Li Xinhua, Xia Zhuying, Xie Xiaoyun, Huang Jing, Zeng Qiuming, Yang Guang, Wu Jing. (2026). Effect of Large Language Model–Powered Virtual Standardized Patients on History-Taking Among Undergraduate Medical Students: Propensity-Matched Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/92486

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory multiple linear regression within group A (n=120) predicting final MHT scores from practice metrics and baseline. The article reports "β=.058; P=.56" for duration and "β=.096; P=.38" for AI score; adjusted R²=0.069.

> "after adjusting for baseline, neither the total duration of practice (β=.058; P=.56) nor the average AI-generated assessment score (β=.096; P=.38) could independently predict final test scores, whereas baseline scores showed a marginal predictive trend (β=.209; P=.06)"

### Stone Alicia 2026 (2)

Stone Alicia, Vicente Maria Asuncion, Xia Oudong, He Yuchen, Chen Chen, Yin Rong, Zhang Wuyang, Chang Haoli, Yang Wei, Li Fei, Li Xinhua, Xia Zhuying, Xie Xiaoyun, Huang Jing, Zeng Qiuming, Yang Guang, Wu Jing. (2026). Effect of Large Language Model–Powered Virtual Standardized Patients on History-Taking Among Undergraduate Medical Students: Propensity-Matched Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/92486

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory Pearson correlation analysis within group A before baseline adjustment. The article reports "r=0.183; P=.046" and "r=0.233; P=.01"; partial correlations controlling for baseline were nonsignificant (rpartial=0.075, P=.42; rpartial=0.097, P=.29).

> "only total duration of practice (r=0.183; P=.046) and the average AI-generated assessment score (r=0.233; P=.01) exhibited weak positive linear associations with final MHT test scores"
