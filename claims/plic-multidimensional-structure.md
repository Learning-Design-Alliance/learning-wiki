## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, C. Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Quantifying critical thinking: Development and validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Principal Component Analysis on matched pre- and post-survey question scores; the first three components explained 45% of variance and were nearly identical across surveys (inner products 0.98, 0.97, 0.94). The authors conclude Cronbach's α cannot be interpreted as internal reliability here.

> "We performed a PCA on matched pre-surveys and post-surveys and found that six principal components were needed to explain at least 70% of the variance in 10 PLIC question scores in both cases."
