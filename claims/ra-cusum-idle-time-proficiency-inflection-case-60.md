`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical modelling result: idle time was regressed on case order with PD-ROBOSCORE adjustment, residuals cumulated in a RA-CUSUM chart, and the breakpoint estimated by segmented regression with 1000 bootstrap resamples. The article reports "a single inflection on this RA-CUSUM trajectory in case 60".

> "Segmented regression identified a single inflection on this RA-CUSUM trajectory in case 60, with a relatively narrow bootstrap 95% confidence band centred in the middle of the series."
