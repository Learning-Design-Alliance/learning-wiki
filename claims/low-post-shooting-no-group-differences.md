`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Second basketball skill outcome analyzed with linear mixed-effects models across the three groups. The authors attribute the absence of group differences to a likely floor effect, as many students failed to score at both pre- and post-test.

> "For Low-post shooting no significant group × time interaction was found (F(2, 270.8) = 0.18, p = .84). A main effect of time was present (F(1, 271.0) = 16.91, p < .001), reflecting small and comparable improvements across all groups."
