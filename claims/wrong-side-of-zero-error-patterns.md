### Bofferding 2015

Bofferding, L., Hoffman, A., Suazo, E., & Lisy, N. (2015). Number Line Estimation with Negatives. Proceedings of the 37th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584204

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative and quantitative analysis of number line estimation data, illustrated in Table 2 and Figures 2 and 3: one error group spaced numbers "along the entire line, ignoring that 0 fell in the middle," and another formed distinct clusters by counting from 0 or 10.

> "While several kindergarteners' R2 values improved, they often had a tendency of placing the numbers on the wrong side of zero (see Table 2 for an example)."
