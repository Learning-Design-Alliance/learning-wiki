## Evidence

### Paschke 2025

Paschke, P., Steinmayr, R., Mertens, A., Neubauer, A. B., & Spinath, B. (2025). Re‐Examination of the Reciprocal Relation Between Intrinsic Motivation and Competence Beliefs in Math Using the Random‐Intercept Cross‐Lagged Panel Framework. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70028

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reanalysis of two longitudinal datasets (N=670 and N=542 German elementary students) using random-intercept cross-lagged panel models found "little to no evidence for reciprocal relations between the constructs". Cross-lagged paths were mostly small and nonsignificant.

> "The results revealed little to no evidence for reciprocal relations between the constructs."
