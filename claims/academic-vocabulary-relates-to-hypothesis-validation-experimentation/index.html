Evidence¶

Van de Sande 2019¶

Van de Sande, E., Kleemans, T., Verhoeven, L., & Segers, E. (2019). The linguistic nature of children's scientific reasoning. Learning and Instruction, 62, 20-26. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.02.002

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis (Table 3) in the same 269-child sample: academic vocabulary correlated r = .34 with hypothesis validation (p < .01) and r = .21 with experimentation (p < .05), and r = .04 with generating conclusions. Correlations are printed but no standardised effect size for the path model.

"Academic vocabulary did not relate to the ability to formulate conclusions, but exhibited moderate relations with hypothesis validation and experimentation skills."
