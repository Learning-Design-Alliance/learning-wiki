### Alexandru Maries 2023

Alexandru Maries, Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Using the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism to investigate progression in student understanding from introductory to advanced levels. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.17170](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.17170)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Systematic comparison of the most common incorrect answer choices between 401 introductory and 85 upper-level post-test responses across all 32 CSEM questions. The authors note that in certain cases upper-level percentages dropped below 10% or 5%, indicating those alternate conceptions were no longer common, but on many questions the same alternate conceptions persisted.

> "in most cases, the most common incorrect answer choices of upper-level students were the same as for the introductory students, except the percentages were lower in the upper-level student group"
