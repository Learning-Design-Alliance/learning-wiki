### Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim 2026

Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim, Tiong Nee, Kamarudin Mohammad Arif, Zamzuri Zamira Hasanah. (2026). Social network analysis as a technology-enhanced assessment tool for collaborative skills in medical PBL. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09397-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative SNA of 17 complete PBL groups (72 Year 1, 94 Year 2 students) reported group-level metrics in Table 3. The article reports Year 1 "longer average path length" and larger diameter than Year 2, with density .277 vs .313 and transitivity .443 vs .532.

> "The Year 1 network demonstrated a larger diameter (Mean = 3.00) and longer average path length (Mean = 2.06) compared to Year 2 (Mean = 2.80; Mean = 1.72, respectively), indicating that information or nominations in Year 1 required slightly more intermediaries to traverse the network."

### Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim 2026 (2)

Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim, Tiong Nee, Kamarudin Mohammad Arif, Zamzuri Zamira Hasanah. (2026). Social network analysis as a technology-enhanced assessment tool for collaborative skills in medical PBL. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09397-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-level network comparison by cohort using Mann-Whitney U tests. The article also notes reciprocity was greater in Year 1 (Mean = .50) than Year 2 (Mean = .40), suggesting mutual nomination exchanges were more common among Year 1 students.

> "In terms of clustering, transitivity was higher in Year 2 (Mean = .53) than in Year 1 (Mean = .44), indicating that Year 2 students were more likely to form tightly interconnected peer triads."
