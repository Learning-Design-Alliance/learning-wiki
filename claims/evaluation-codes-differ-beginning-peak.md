## Evidence

### Moreno 2024

Moreno, M., Patino Melo, L., Grewal, K., Matin, N., Azher, S., & Harley, J.M. (2024). Analyzing Multimodal Data to Understand Medical Trainees' Regulation Strategies and Physiological Responses in High-Fidelity Medical Simulation Scenarios. Metacognition and Learning, 19, 1161–1213. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09403-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same repeated measures ANOVA over the ten coded simulations; the omnibus test for Evaluation codes was F(2, 22) = 3.87, p = .03, and the Beginning-versus-Peak pairwise contrast quoted above was significant. No effect size is printed.

> "With ‘Evaluation’ codes, there were differences between ‘Beginning’ and ‘Peak’ (p= 0.03, 95% CI [-2.69, -0.13])"
