# Students' averaged number of correct physics answers was statistically significantly higher at posttest than pretest after completing the Eurogame escape room in physics, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sascha Albert Bräuninger 2024

Sascha Albert Bräuninger, Damian Alexander Motz, Matthias Lüpke, Hermann Seifert. (2024). Escape Room combined with European Board Game Concepts for self-adjusted Challenge Levels: An educational Eurogame Escape Room in Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.15454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.15454)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Raw-data pre/post analysis of 25 first-semester veterinary medicine students using four approaches (based on N1 = 25 students, N2 = 12 questions, group- and question-separated data) with t-tests, F-tests, David and Grubbs tests. The article reports "a statistically (highly) significant increase in the averaged numbers of correct answers in the posttest"; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, the evaluations revealed a statistically (highly) significant increase in the averaged numbers of correct answers in the posttest and, therefore, after the students had completed the ERP and proved the success in their physics knowledge transfer by the developed eEERP."
