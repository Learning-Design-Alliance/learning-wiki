M. J. Wangila 2015¶

M. J. Wangila, W. Martin, M. Ronald. (2015). Effect of Programmed Instruction on Students' Attitude Towards Structure of the Atom and the Periodic Table among Kenyan Secondary Schools. Science Education International, Vol. 25, Issue 4, 488-500. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086551

The study's stated null hypothesis was tested at the 0.05 alpha level using the one-way ANOVA on post-test attitude scores; the article reports the null hypothesis was rejected on the basis of both descriptive and inferential statistics. No effect size is printed.

"The study however found a statistically significant f -ratio on the four posttest attitude mean scores, which implies the contrary. The null hypothesis was therefore rejected"
