Utilizing transcranial direct current stimulation to enhance laparoscopic technical skills training: A randomized controlled trial. Brain Stimulation. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2020.03.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sub-analysis using independent samples t-tests on training-block metrics in the randomized trial; the article states the analysis "was limited by the small sample size with only four subjects endorsing previous laparoscopic surgical experience". No effect size printed.

> "Subjects with prior laparoscopic surgery experience had significantly better performance in the second (t = 4.1, p < 0.001) and third (t = 3.7, p = 0.001) 20-min training blocks compared to subjects without laparoscopic surgery experience"
