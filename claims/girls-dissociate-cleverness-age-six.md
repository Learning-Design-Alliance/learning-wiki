Ruxandra Bondarescu, Jayashree Balakrishna, Christine Corbett Moran, and Anuja DeSilva. (2018). Women in Science: Surpassing Subtle and Overt Biases through Intervention Programs. MNRAS. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/tqy3g](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/tqy3g)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Bian et al. (2017, 2018) and Archer et al. (2012), that girls dissociate gender from cleverness by age 6 and lose STEM interest by age 10-11. The article attributes this to societal cues about gender roles rather than innate ability differences.

> "Studies ﬁnd that by the age of 6 girls dissoci-ate their gender from cleverness (Bian et al. 2017, 2018). By the age of 10-11, STEM loses the interest of girls who already believe that a science career is not for them (Archer et al. 2012)."
