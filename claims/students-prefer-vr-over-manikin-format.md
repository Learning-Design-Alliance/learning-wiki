# Students preferred continuing the course in VR over the manikin format

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 65.1% of students preferred VR over manikin (20.9%), a significant difference. [→ Junga A 2025](#junga-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Junga A 2025

Junga A, Kockwelp P, Valkov D, Schulze H, Bozdere P, Hätscher O, Ahrens H, Marschall B, Risse B, Holling M. (2025). Teach the Unteachable with a Virtual Reality (VR) Brain Death Scenario – 800 Students and 3 Years of Experience. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1427

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pilot survey asking all students which course format they would prefer in the future, analysed with a χ2 test with Bonferroni correction (α = .0167). "65.1% of the vote" favoured VR versus 20.9% for manikin; χ2(1, N = 86) = 19.51, p < .001.

> "The students clearly favoured ‘VR’ with 65.1% of the vote, ahead of ‘manikin’ with 20.9%. 14.0% of students stated that they had no preference. Significant differences were found between ‘VR’ and ‘manikin’ (χ2(1, N = 86) = 19.51, p < .001)"
