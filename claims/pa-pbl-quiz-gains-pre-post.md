## Evidence

### Xie Jingtao 2026

Xie Jingtao, Chen Ziyan, Lin Duhuang, Zhuang Guansheng, Zheng Ziyuan, Su Yun, Xin Gang. (2026). Peer-assisted learning combined with problem-based learning enhances clinical reasoning and knowledge application among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08659-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-post comparison using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on identical 8-MCQ quizzes before and after each PA-PBL session, completed only by the experimental group. The article reports "post-intervention scores being higher than pre-intervention scores" for all four cases, with score-rate gains of 12.3%, 12.8%, 9.8% and 6.8% printed in Table 2. No standardized effect size is reported.

> "Following the intervention, statistically significant differences were observed in the learning outcomes of relevant knowledge (P < 0.05), with post-intervention scores being higher than pre-intervention scores (Table 2)"
