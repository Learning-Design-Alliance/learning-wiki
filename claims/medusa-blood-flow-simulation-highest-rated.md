Descriptive results from the 40-neurosurgeon external validation questionnaire: the blood flow simulation received a mean score of 4.25, preoperative planning 4.20, and standard surgical education 4.05. The discussion states the blood flow simulation was the highest-rated component.

> "Most participants agreed that the blood flow simulation is realistic (mean score of 4.25) and that the simulator should be integrated into preoperative planning (mean score of 4.20) and standard surgical education (mean score of 4.05)."
