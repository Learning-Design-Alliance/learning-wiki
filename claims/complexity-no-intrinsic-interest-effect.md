## Evidence

### Ines Zeitlhofer 2024

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach, Judith Schweppe. (2024). Complexity affects performance, cognitive load, and awareness. Learning and Instruction 94 (2024) 102001. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102001

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Intrinsic interest, measured with the Eccles and Wigfield subscale, did not differ between the consistently high and gradually increasing groups; the printed test was "F (1,84) = 0.06, p < .80, η2 = 0.00".

> "Moreover, there was no significant impact of task complexity on intrinsic interest (F (1,84) = 0.06, p < .80, η2 = 0.00)."
