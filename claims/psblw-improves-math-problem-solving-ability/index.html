Parwati et al. 2018¶

Ni Nyoman Parwati, I Gusti Putu Sudiarta, I Made Mariawan, I Wayan Widiana. (2018). LOCAL WISDOM-ORIENTED PROBLEM-SOLVING LEARNING MODEL TO IMPROVE MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM-SOLVING ABILITY. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 8(4), 310-320. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.401

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group with pretest-posttest; factorial 2x2 ANCOVA on 152 fifth-grade students in Singaraja, Bali. The learning model factor was significant (F = 1750.885, p < .001).

"the learning model and the type of problem are influence and contribute significantly to the students' mathematical problem-solving abilities."
