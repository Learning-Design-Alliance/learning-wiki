### Taş 2015

Taş, E., Gülen, S., Öner, Z., & Özyürek, C. (2015). The effects of classic and web-designed conceptual change texts on the subject of water chemistry. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(2), 263-280. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057892

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with 37 8th graders in Samsun, Turkey; experimental group (N=17) used web-assisted texts, control (N=20) used classic texts. The article reports "This improvement was 65% for the experimental group and 14% for the control group."
