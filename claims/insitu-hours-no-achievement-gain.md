## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The applied experience condition, which included 11 additional in-situ hours, recorded lower posttest and quiz achievement than the embedded design condition. [→ Lancaster 2019](#lancaster-2019)

## Evidence

### Lancaster 2019

Lancaster, J., & Bain, A. (2019). Designing University Courses to Improve Pre-Service Teachers' Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Evidence-Based Inclusive Practice. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 44(2). https://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol44/iss2/4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two conditions in the quasi-experiment: the applied experience cohort received the same 39 contact hours plus 11 hours of weekly one-to-one work with students identified as having learning difficulties, yet scored lower on the posttest and quizzes.

> "The additional eleven hours of in-situ applied experience did not result in increased achievement levels over those recorded in the embedded design condition."
