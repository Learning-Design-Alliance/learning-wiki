## Evidence

### Pearson 2013

Pearson, D., Young, R. B., & Richardson, G. B. (2013). Exploring the Technical Expression of Academic Knowledge: The Science-in-CTE Pilot Study. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(4), 162-179. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.04162

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM Level 2 model testing classroom-level predictors of the pretest quartile slope; the Group × pretest quartile interaction (γ52 = .97) was statistically significant at p < .05, indicating the treatment effect was incongruent across levels of pretest science achievement.

> "Holding all other predictors constant, treatment group membership was observed to have a statistically significant effect on the pretest quartile slope (p < .05). For participants in the treatment group classrooms, controlling for all other predictors, each unit increase above the first pretest quartile corresponded to a .97 unit increase in the effect of pretest quartile on mean posttest science achievement (p < .05)."
