### Ogunyemi D 2020

Ogunyemi D, Haltigin C, Vallie S, Ferrari TM, Hayashi S. (2020). Evolution of an obstetrics and gynecology interprofessional simulation-based education session for medical and nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000022562

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

2017 delivery station comparison of medical student self-assessment versus faculty assessment; self-assessment mean was 8.63 ± 0.82 versus faculty 8.93 ± 0.30, P < .001, so students were "more critical of their learning".

> "There was a significant difference between the medical students’ self-assessment score and the faculty-assessment score at the delivery simulation (8.63 ± 0.82 and 8.93 ± 0.30; P < .001)."
