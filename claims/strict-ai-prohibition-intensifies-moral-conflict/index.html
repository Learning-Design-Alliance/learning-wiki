Kim 2026¶

Kim, Jiyoon; Toyama, Kentaro; Kim, Sangmi; Carroll, John. (2026). "It's OK Because...": The Wild West of Student Rationalization of AI Use in Academic Writing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29090 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.29090

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview case from the belief-versus-behavior analysis: participant P16 expressed a strong desire to obey a course policy prohibiting AI entirely, yet continued using AI. The article reports the prohibition "intensified their internal moral conflict", and P16 summarized that "all it's doing is creating moral conflict".

""I don't think that is actually helping me stay away from it, and it's actually creating conflict for me, because I'm breaking the rules. "(P16)"
