### Chen Sitong 2021

Chen Sitong, Li Xiaoyun, Yan Jin, Ren Zhanbing. (2021). To Be a Sportsman? Sport Participation Is Associated With Optimal Academic Achievement in a Nationally Representative Sample of High School Students. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.730497

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate results from the fully adjusted weighted logistic regression of the YRBS 2019 sample (Table 2). The text reports that "higher values of BMI were negatively associated" and "appropriate sleep duration was positively correlated" with better academic performance; Table 2 shows sleep OR = 1.54 (95% CI: 1.30–1.82) and BMI OR = 0.97.

> "Specifically, higher values of BMI were negatively associated with better academic performance, but appropriate sleep duration was positively correlated with better academic performance."
