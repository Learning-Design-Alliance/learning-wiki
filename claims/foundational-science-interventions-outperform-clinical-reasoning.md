## Evidence

### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports that pathology-focused review sessions improved scores (McKean & Palmer p=0.002; Siddiqi et al. p=0.03; 0.0007) while a critical thinking curriculum lowered scores (67.5 vs 75.5, p<0.0001).

> "other studies have shown that review sessions focusing on disease pathology produced statistically significant improvements in shelf exam scores [27,28] while interventions aimed at critical thinking and clinical skills led to worse shelf performance [29]."
