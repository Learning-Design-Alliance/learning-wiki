Psychometric analysis of the study's 12-item, 5-point Likert reflection instrument (N = 131), reported in the validity and reliability section; the authors state the alpha "surpassed the .70 threshold". Face and content validity were reviewed by faculty.

"An item-total reliability analysis was performed on twelve items on a Likert scale to strengthen the internal consistency and structural integrity of the post -interaction reflection instrument. The overall Cronbach's alpha coefficient was α = .863."
