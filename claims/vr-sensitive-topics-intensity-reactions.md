## Evidence

### Sapci AH 2026

Sapci AH, Ugbor A, Ayub R, Lauritzen C, Reedtz C, Kristensen KB, Mathisen V, Næss ET, Furu R, Nermo H, Jentoft R. (2026). Development of Therapeutic Competencies in Health Care Students: Qualitative Focus Group Study Using 360-Degree Video and Virtual Reality Technology. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/75776

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group finding (n=12): one student described feeling caught off guard by the many stimuli and impressions during the VR modules, saying her brain was almost boiling from the amount of information, while also judging the resource great and the learning intensive.

> "This finding shows that it can also be overwhelming to work with such topics in VR, and that teachers therefore must consider that various reactions may arise in the student group."
