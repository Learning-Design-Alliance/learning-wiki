## Evidence

### Uysal Daniel 2020

Uysal Daniel, Gasch Claudia, Behnisch Rouven, Nickel Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Hohenfellner Markus, Teber Dogu. (2020). Evaluation of new motorized articulating laparoscopic instruments by laparoscopic novices using a standardized laparoscopic skills curriculum. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-08086-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exercise-failure analysis in the randomized cross-over study (n = 50 per instrument); the McNemar test compared failures per exercise, with needle guidance showing "12 attempts with ALI were not successful" versus zero for CLI (p = 0.0005). Laparoscopic suturing failures (7 vs. 13) did not reach significance (p = 0.06).

> "Interestingly, none of the participants failed the needle guidance task with CLI, whereas 12 attempts with ALI were not successful (Table 3)."
