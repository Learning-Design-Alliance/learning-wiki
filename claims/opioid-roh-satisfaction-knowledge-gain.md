### Sophia Hannou; Cristina Nicorici; Wanda Bosshard; Pierre Voirol; Farshid Sadeghipour; Nancy Perrottet; Chantal Csajka 2025

Sophia Hannou; Cristina Nicorici; Wanda Bosshard; Pierre Voirol; Farshid Sadeghipour; Nancy Perrottet; Chantal Csajka. (2025). Opioid Room of Horrors: a simulation approach to strengthen drug administration safety. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003728

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous voluntary post-training survey completed by 169 of 172 participants, including a 0-10 Likert satisfaction item and pre/post self-assessment of knowledge (none/low/medium/high). The authors acknowledge these are self-reported measures that "may not accurately reflect actual learning".

> "Satisfaction with the training was high, with an average rating of 9.2 out of 10 across all professionals and 9.2, 9.3 and 9.1 for nurses, healthcare assistants and physicians, respectively. Before the training, only 22% of participants rated their knowledge level as high, increasing to 79% afterward (figure 1)."
