Cookson 2022¶

Cookson, P. W., Jr., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2022). Building school communities for students living in deep poverty. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/121.698

q2 · i? · review · r1

The report cites recent analyses of school-equity-case data from more than 20 states, concluding that "schools serving large numbers of students of color and students from low-income families have significantly fewer resources" across every tangible measure. No effect size is printed.

"Recent analyses of data prepared for school equity cases in more than 20 states have found that on every tangible measure—from qualified teachers and reasonable class sizes to adequate textbooks, computers, facilities, and curriculum offerings—schools serving large numbers of students of color and students from low-income families have significantly fewer resources than schools serving more affluent White students."
