## Evidence

### M. Alamgir Hossain 2021

M. Alamgir Hossain, Petra M. Menz, and John M. Stockie. (2021). An Open-Access Clicker Question Bank for Numerical Analysis. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113](https://doi.org/10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mid-semester anonymous Canvas survey of the MACM 316 clicker implementation, with 137 responses from 199 registered students (69% response rate). The authors report that "more than 80% of students thought the clicker questions helped them to improve their understanding"; no effect size is printed.

> "Questions Q2 and Q3 suggest that more than 80% of students thought the clicker questions helped them to improve their understanding of the course material."
