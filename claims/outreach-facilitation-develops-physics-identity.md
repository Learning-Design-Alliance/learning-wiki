### Callie Rethman 2021

Callie Rethman, Jonathan Perry, Jonan Donaldson, Daniel Choi, and Tatiana Erukhimova. (2021). Creating a Physicist: The Impact of Informal Physics Programs on University Student Development. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020110)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed methods study combining a survey (117 responses) and interviews (35) with current and former undergraduate and graduate students who facilitated five programs at a large land-grant university. The abstract reports that "students who facilitated informal physics programs positively developed their physics identity." All data were self-reported.

> "Our findings, based on self-reported data, show that students who facilitated informal physics programs positively developed their physics identity, experienced increased sense of belonging to the physics community, and developed 21st century career skills."
