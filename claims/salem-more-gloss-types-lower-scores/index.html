Taylor 2014¶

Taylor, A. M. (2014). Glossing Frequency and L2 Reading Comprehension: The Influence of CALL Glossing. CALICO Journal, 31(3). https://doi.org/10.11139/cj.31.3.374-389

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The article's narrative account of Salem's (2006) experiment, the study with the largest effect size in the meta-analysis, which compared L1 textual glosses against combinations adding audio, pictorial, and written glossing. The article reports means declined as gloss types were added; no test statistic is printed for this pattern.

"the highest mean was simply for L1 textual glosses and the means got progressively lower as Salem added more and more glossing helps for each item."
