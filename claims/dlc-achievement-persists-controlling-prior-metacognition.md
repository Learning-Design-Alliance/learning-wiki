## Evidence

### Sijmkens 2024

Sijmkens, E., De Cock, M., & De Laet, T. (2023). Scaffolding Students' Use of Metacognitive Activities Using Discipline- and Topic-Specific Reflective Prompts. *Metacognition and Learning, 18*(3), 811–843. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09363-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis with N=272 students who completed the MSLQ pretest, predicting final exam score from completion of nine reflection modules and MSLQ subscale prescores. Modules 7, 8, and 2 and the time/effort regulation prescore emerged as significant predictors.

> "In the hierarchical regression model with students' final exam score as outcome variable the following indicators emerged as significant predictors: completion of reflection module 7, students' prescore on the 'time and study environment and effort regulation' scale of the MSLQ, completion of reflection module 8, and completion of reflection module 2"
