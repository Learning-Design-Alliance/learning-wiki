Evidence¶

Ruxandra I. Tivadar 2023¶

Ruxandra I. Tivadar, Benedetta Franceschiello, Astrid Minier and Micah M. Murray. (2023). Learning and navigating digitally rendered haptic spatial layouts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00208-4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Correlational analysis using Kendall's tau with FDR correction for multiple tests, linking the trajectory learning phase to behavioural performance. The nominal correlation "τ = −0.63, p = 0.04" was rendered non-significant after FDR correction (p = 0.14), and no effect size survived correction; correlations were non-significant for Group 1 for both trajectory types.

"we found a signi ﬁcant negative correlation between Jaccard and IE scores for Group 2 on untrained trajectories ( τ = −0.63, p = 0.04). However, FDR correction never- theless resulted in in ﬂation of the p value ( p = 0.14)."
