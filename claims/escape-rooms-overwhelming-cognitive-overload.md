`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interview and video data from the Round 1 pilot (n = 34) showed the Overwhelming theme: students reported puzzles were "too complex" and that they struggled to recall and integrate knowledge across topics, occasionally leading to frustration.

> "A few students gave up within 20–25 min and asked the supervisor to leave the room. Follow-up discussions revealed that these students felt overwhelmed and therefore wanted to quit the game."
