### Manuela Macedonia 2026

Manuela Macedonia, Lihui Hu, Brian Mathias. (2026). Why Some Gestures Work Better than Others: Toward a Model of Gesture-Supported Vocabulary Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10222-8

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Macedonia et al. (2011), a Vimmi artificial-corpus study in which words were paired with iconic gestures or meaningless self-adaptors; "Retention was significantly higher for words accompanied by the iconic, semantically congruent gestures." fMRI showed iconic gestures recruited broader motor networks.

> "Retention was significantly higher for words accompanied by the iconic, semantically congruent gestures."

### Manuela Macedonia 2026 (2)

Manuela Macedonia, Lihui Hu, Brian Mathias. (2026). Why Some Gestures Work Better than Others: Toward a Model of Gesture-Supported Vocabulary Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10222-8

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing García-Gámez and Macizo (2023), that "congruent gestures improved noun and verb learning relative to no gestures, whereas meaningless or incongruent gestures hindered performance." No effect sizes are printed in the review.

> "congruent gestures improved noun and verb learning relative to no gestures, whereas meaningless or incongruent gestures hindered performance"
