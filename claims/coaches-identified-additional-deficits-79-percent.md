### Redman Charles 2024

Redman Charles, Chung Catherine G., McFarlane Daniel, Meara Alexa, Ejaz Aslam. (2024). Graduate medical education success coaching for residents and fellows: a single-institution real-world experience. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2342102

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of 29 coaching referrals at one institution. The article reports that "coach-identified deficits differed (including identification of additional deficits) from those identified by the PD or learner in 79%". Clinical reasoning was the most common referral reason; interpersonal skills and communication was the most coach-identified need.

> "Interestingly, coach-identified deficits differed (including identification of additional deficits) from those identified by the PD or learner in 79%."
