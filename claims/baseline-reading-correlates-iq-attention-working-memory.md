## Evidence

### Gary Rance 2023

Gary Rance, Richard C. Dowell and Dani Tomlin. (2023). The effect of classroom environment on literacy development. npj Science of Learning 8:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00157-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise Pearson correlations between baseline WARP scores and demographic, audiometric and cognitive measures across the 196-student cohort (Table 1). Baseline reading was not significantly correlated with age or LiSN-S scores.

> "Reading scores were signiﬁcantly associated with IQ (r = 0.261, p < 0.001), attention scores ( r = 0.272, p < 0.001), and working memory scores ( r = 0.219, p < 0.01)."

### Gary Rance 2023 (2)

Gary Rance, Richard C. Dowell and Dani Tomlin. (2023). The effect of classroom environment on literacy development. npj Science of Learning 8:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00157-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

General linear model of baseline reading scores including the significant correlates and School. Tukey post hoc comparisons showed school HA had significantly lower mean reading score than FL, KI and EN, which the authors attribute to socio-economic factors.

> "This showed signiﬁcant results for School (F = 4.93, p < 0.001), IQ ( F = 6.72, p < 0.05) and Attention score (F = 7.47, p < 0.01)."
