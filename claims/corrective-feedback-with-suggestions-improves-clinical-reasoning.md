### de Beer Marianne 2015

de Beer Marianne, Mårtensson Lena. (2015). Feedback on students' clinical reasoning skills during fieldwork education. Australian Occupational Therapy Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/1440-1630.12208

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-methods study of 33 final-year occupational therapy students across six hospital placements. Pearson correlations between FPE grades and students' experiences of corrective feedback yielded r = 0.744, a strong positive association.

> "Students' clinical reasoning skills seem to be improved through corrective feedback if accompanied by suggestions on how to improve, irrespective of their average academic performance."
