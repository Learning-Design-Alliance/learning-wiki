## Evidence

### Hogg H. D. Jeffry 2020

Hogg H. D. Jeffry, Pereira Michael, Purdy Julian, Frearson Richard J. R., Lau Gordon B. (2020). A non-randomised trial of video and written educational adjuncts in undergraduate ophthalmology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1923-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of consecutive cohorts in a non-randomised trial; 95% of students (N = 93) volunteered whether they had done pre-placement work. The article reports "more likely (p = 0.05) to prepare for the placement (87%) than those who had not (70%)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Students who had been given specified written or video materials were more likely (p = 0.05) to prepare for the placement (87%) than those who had not (70%)."
