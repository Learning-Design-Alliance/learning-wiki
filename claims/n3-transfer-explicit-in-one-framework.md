Phase 2 document analysis found seven frameworks detailed competencies aligned with transferability across teaching modes or contexts. The authors report that "only one university framework explicitly referred to teachers' transferring and adapting digital practice as a competence," with the rest framed generally.

> "Yet, only one university framework explicitly referred to teachers' transferring and adapting digital practice as a competence. The other six frameworks made more general references to the transfer of pedagogical skill and knowledge across a range of contexts."
