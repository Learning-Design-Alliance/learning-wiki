# Non-symbolic training improved non-symbolic magnitude processing selectively by ratio and numerosity size

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The non-symbolic group improved collection comparison for ratios 1:2, 2:3 and 3:4, and improved positioning of large (but not small) numerosities on the non-symbolic number line. [→ Honoré Nastasya 2016](#honore-nastasya-2016)
`q3 i?` The symbolic group improved collection comparison only for ratio 1:2 and improved small numerosities on the non-symbolic number line. [→ Honoré Nastasya 2016 (2)](#honore-nastasya-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Honoré Nastasya 2016

Honoré Nastasya, Noël Marie-Pascale. (2016). Improving Preschoolers' Arithmetic through Number Magnitude Training: The Impact of Non-Symbolic and Symbolic Training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0166685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the collection comparison task with ratio as within-subjects factor; the ratio × time × group interaction was significant, F(6,150) = 2.91, p = .016, η2 = 0.104. The non-symbolic group improved for ratios 1:2, 2:3 and 3:4, the symbolic group for ratio 1:2 only, and the control group showed no change.

> "Corrected contrasts comparing performance at T1 and T2 for each ratio and each group showed that (1) in the non-symbolic group, performance increased from T1 to T2 for ratios 1:2, t(50) = 4.94; p <.001, η2 = 0.328"

### Honoré Nastasya 2016 (2)

Honoré Nastasya, Noël Marie-Pascale. (2016). Improving Preschoolers' Arithmetic through Number Magnitude Training: The Impact of Non-Symbolic and Symbolic Training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0166685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

On the non-symbolic number line task, the time × size × group interaction was significant, F(2,49) = 3.25, p = .047: the non-symbolic group improved on large numerosities (t(49) = 3.91, p <.001, η2 = 0.238) and the symbolic group on small numerosities (t(49) = 3.22, p = .012, η2 = 0.174).

> "the effect of interaction between time, size and group was significant, F(2,49) = 3.25, p = .047, η2 = 117, indicating a significant decrease of PAE for large numerosities in the non-symbolic group, t(49) 3.91, p <.001, η2 = 0.238"
