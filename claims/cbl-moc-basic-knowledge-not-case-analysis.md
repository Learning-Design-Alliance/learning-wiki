## Evidence

### Zhi Fei-Hang 2025

Zhi Fei-Hang, Liu Wei, Zhang Xinyao, Zhang Tao, Su Yuhan, Huang Ruoxuan, Feng Yan-Fen, Lei Yi-Yan. (2025). Exploration of CBL combined with “Metronomic” online classroom in thoracic trauma and closed chest drainage teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Section-level analysis of the 30-question end-of-course exam (10 basic knowledge, 20 case analysis) in the randomized study of 39 test completers. Basic knowledge favored CBL+MOC at p = 0.02; case analysis showed “no significant difference” at p = 0.14.

> "Further analysis showed that this improvement was particularly evident in basic knowledge mastery (p = 0.02), whereas no significant difference was observed for case analysis performance (p = 0.14) (Fig. 3)."
