## Evidence

### Maloney S 2015

Maloney S, Nicklen P, Rivers G, Foo J, Ooi YY, Reeves S, Walsh K, Ilic D. (2015). A Cost-Effectiveness Analysis of Blended Versus Face-to-Face Delivery of Evidence-Based Medicine to Medical Students. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.4346

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate sensitivity analysis (Table 4) constructing scenarios around increased BL staffing requirements and two effectiveness levels, with results reported as percentage differences in independent F2F and BL ICER values. The analysis shows BL "more cost-effective to operate up to staffing levels 20% higher than F2F"; at 50% higher the value is negative.
