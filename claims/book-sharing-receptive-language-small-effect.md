# Caregiver-directed shared book-reading interventions have a small positive effect on child receptive language (d=0.26)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 16 RCTs, interactive book-sharing interventions improved child receptive vocabulary relative to controls with a small pooled effect (d=0.26). [→ Dowdall 2020](#dowdall-2020)

## Evidence

### Dowdall 2020

Dowdall, N., Melendez-Torres, G. J., Murray, L., Gardiner, F., Harrtford, L. & Cooper, P.J. (2020). Shared picture book reading interventions for child language development: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Child Development, 91(2). https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13225

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Robust variance estimation meta-analysis of 16 RCTs providing 24 effect sizes from 1871 children. The pooled result showed "a small sized effect in improving receptive vocabulary when compared to controls" with d=0.26, CI 0.12-0.40, p=0.001, tau-squared=0.03.

> "The pooled result of the robust variance estimation meta-analysis suggested evidence (p=0.001) for interactive book-sharing interventions having a small sized effect in improving receptive vocabulary when compared to controls (d=0.26; CI: 0.12, 0.40; df: 11.28); tau-squared = 0.03."
