Тulegen A. Botagariyeva, Svetlana S. Kubiyeva, Venera E. Baizakova, Nurolla Mambetov, Yerkin K. Tulegenov, Alpysbay S. Aralbayev and Dulat U. Kairgozhin. (2016). q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of school test results and Presidential tests using percentile scale graphics among ninth-grade students in Aktobe. The author considers the "psychological factor" effect relevant, as Presidential standards demand students' full inner potential in competition with peers.

"For example, the students of school No. 19 who took the test "pulling up on the bar" with a score of "10 times", were in the third place, and after taking the Presidential test with a score of 8.3 they were in 23rd place."
