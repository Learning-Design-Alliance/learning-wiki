## Evidence

### Shaeema Zaman Ahmed 2022

Shaeema Zaman Ahmed, Carrie A. Weidner, Jesper H. M. Jensen, Jacob F. Sherson, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Student use of a quantum simulation and visualization tool. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cumulative counts of coded feature-use instances across the five interviews, presented as a bar chart (Fig. 4) and discussed in the results. The authors state students "work more ex- tensively with the relevant features". Counts are descriptive; no effect size.

> "The graph indicates that students engage regularly with all features with the exception of Set number of eigenstates, which did not need to be changed to com- plete the exercise. We see that students work more ex- tensively with the relevant features, including those re- lated to visualization of the system"
