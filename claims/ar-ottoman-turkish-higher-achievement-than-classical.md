## Evidence

### Özcan et al. 2017

Mehmet Fatih Özcan, Âdem Özkan, Nurullah Şahin. (2017). The Influence of the Augmented Reality Application on Students' Performances in Ottoman Turkish Readings. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(12B): 27-33. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051403

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 30 experimental and 30 control freshman students in the Department of Turkish Education, taught over four weeks (one pilot, three practice). The authors report that achievement of the AR-taught class "is higher than those in the class with classical method" on the 20-item achievement test; no effect size is printed.

> "According to the academic achievement test applied in this direction, the academic achievement of the students in the class taught with AR technology is higher than those in the class with classical method."
