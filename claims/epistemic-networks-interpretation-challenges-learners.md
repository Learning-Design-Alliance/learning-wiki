### Zhao 2026

Zhao, L., Echeverria, V., Gašević, D., Yan, L., Alfredo, R., Li, X., & Martinez-Maldonado, R. (2026). Automated multimodal visual analytics of social and epistemic dimensions in team communication for reflective practice in healthcare simulation. Computers & Education, 252, 105655. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105655

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Findings from post-hoc one-to-one semi-structured interviews with 15 learners, in which participants were asked to describe and interpret both network types, indicate that "epistemic networks presented interpretation challenges for some learners". The article draws no statistical comparison between network types.

> "While social networks were comprehensible to learners, epistemic networks presented interpretation challenges for some learners, highlighting a need for simplified representations to illustrate the epistemic dimension of communication."
