### Galli Andrea Cesare 2026

Galli Andrea Cesare, Dionigi Gianlorenzo, Lauricella Sara, Agradi Sergio, Sassun Richard, Leone Angelo Emanuele, Cirocchi Roberto, Brucchi Francesco. (2026). Defining proficiency in THD-Anolift: a CUSUM analysis of the learning curve in 51 consecutive cases. International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-026-05151-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective single-surgeon cohort of 51 consecutive THD-Anolift cases analyzed with CUSUM charts for a composite failure endpoint (recurrence and/or any complication). The phase comparison showed "the failure rate decreased from 52.2% in Phase 1 (cases 1–23) to 25.0% in Phase 2"; the difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.080).

> "The composite outcome CUSUM identified a proficiency point at case 23: the failure rate decreased from 52.2% in Phase 1 (cases 1–23) to 25.0% in Phase 2 (cases 24–51; p = 0.080, not statistically significant at the conventional threshold)."
