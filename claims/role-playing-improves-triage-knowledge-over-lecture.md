## Evidence

### Seo Yon Hee 2024

Seo Yon Hee, Lim Sun-Og. (2024). Korean Triage and Acuity Scale education using role-playing and its effects on triage competency: A quasi-experimental design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group pre–post-test study with 39 experimental and 39 control nursing students; ANCOVA used pre-knowledge as covariate due to baseline differences. The role-playing group's post-test knowledge mean was 15.41 versus 13.95 in the lecture group, a "significant improvement compared with that of the control group".

> "The experimental group’s pre-post triage knowledge score showed significant improvement compared with that of the control group (t = 2.944, p = .004)."
