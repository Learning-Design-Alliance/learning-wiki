## Evidence

### Taraman 2016

Taraman, S., Hassan, Y., Shawky, D., & Badawi, A. (2016). Employing Game theory and Multilevel Analysis to Predict the Factors that Affect Collaborative Learning Outcomes: An Empirical Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1610.05075](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1610.05075)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

CGT analysis using the TUGlab Matlab toolbox, with coalition gains assumed proportional to members' average contribution, followed by multilevel regression. The article reports significant Shapley coefficients and improved fit with content engagement; the discussion notes p-value for Shapley values < 0.05 and for content engagement < 0.01.

> "When the Shapley values were used alone as predictors, the resulting model has significant coefficients for them. In addition, when the “Content Engagement” was added as another predictor to the model, the goodness-of-fit has improved."
