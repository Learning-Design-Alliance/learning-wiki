## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The full predictor set explains 23.7% of corrected variance in primary school overall GPA and 17.1% in secondary school, with subsector models ranging from 5.7% to 17.8%. [→ Flores Jerome 2022](#flores-jerome-2022)

## Evidence

### Flores Jerome 2022

Flores Jerome, Caqueo-Urízar Alejandra, López Verónica, Acevedo Daniel. (2022). Symptomatology of attention deficit, hyperactivity and defiant behavior as predictors of academic achievement. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-022-03714-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression analysis of 4580 Chilean schoolchildren, with seven models per school group (overall GPA plus six subsectors). Secondary school GPA was explained by 17.1% of all predictors; subsector R² ranged from 11.2% (physical education) to 17.8% (language, social sciences) in primary and 5.7% to 15% in secondary.

> "In the primary school group, the results show that, with the exception of hyperactivity–impulsivity, the predictors explain 23.7% of the variance in overall academic achievement."
