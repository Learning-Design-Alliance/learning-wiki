## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). How understanding large language models can inform the use of ChatGPT in physics education. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad1420](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad1420)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Illustrative qualitative study in which the authors regenerated ChatGPT-4's answer to a conceptual mechanics question eight times through the end-user interface, starting a new chat each time. They report the correct answer in "four out of the eight responses"; no effect size is printed.

> "We regenerated the response in ChatGPT-4 eight times. We got the correct answer that the body with the larger mass has the larger momentum in four out of the eight responses."
