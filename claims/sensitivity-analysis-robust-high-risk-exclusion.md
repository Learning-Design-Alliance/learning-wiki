### Jin Hye Kyung 2024

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sensitivity analysis in the random-effects meta-analysis excluding the two studies judged at high risk of bias on the JBI checklist. Overall accuracy "remained at 71.3% (95% CI, 66.3-75.8%)" with I² of 98.1%.

> "The sensitivity analysis demonstrated that removing high-risk studies did not significantly alter the overall accuracy, which remained at 71.3% (95% CI, 66.3-75.8%), indicating that the results were robust (p < 0.001), with an I² of 98.1%"
