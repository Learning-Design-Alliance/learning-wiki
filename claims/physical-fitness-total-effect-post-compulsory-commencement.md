## Evidence

### Andersen Mikkel Porsborg 2017

Andersen Mikkel Porsborg, Starkof Liis, Sessa Maurizio, Mortensen Rikke Nørmark, Vardinghus-Nielsen Henrik, Bøggild Henrik, Lange Theis, Torp-Pedersen Christian. (2017). The indirect and direct pathways between physical fitness and academic achievement on commencement in post-compulsory education in a historical cohort of Danish school youth. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-017-4712-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Register-based historical cohort mediation analysis of 1084 Aalborg pupils (530 girls, 554 boys) aged 13–15 in 2010, followed to 2014. The overall analysis "adjusted for sex, age, ethnicity and socioeconomic status" yielded the total-effect OR of 1.87 (95% CI: 1.30; 2.73) per 10 units of VO2max.

> "From this analysis, an OR of 1.87 (95% CI: 1.30; 2.73) was obtained for the total effect."
