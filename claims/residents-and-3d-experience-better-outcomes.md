### Roni Lekar 2025

Roni Lekar, Tatiana Gerth, Sergey Prokudin, Matthias Seibold, Reto Bürgin, Benjamin Vella, Armando Hoch, Siyu Tang, Philipp Fürnstahl, and Helmut Grabner. (2025). Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the linear mixed models of the 56-participant RCT, resident status was an explanatory variable; the article reports "Residents performed better overall (β = 4.030,p = 0.012)", attributed to subject familiarity. No standardized effect size printed.

> "Residents performed better overall (β = 4.030,p = 0.012), likely due to their subject fa-miliarity, and those with 3D knowledge outperformed others (β = 5.490,p = 0.007)"

### Roni Lekar 2025 (2)

Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same linear mixed models, prior 3D experience was an explanatory variable; the article reports that "those with 3D knowledge outperformed others (β = 5.490,p = 0.007)", consistent with previous studies it cites. No standardized effect size printed.

> "Residents performed better overall (β = 4.030,p = 0.012), likely due to their subject fa-miliarity, and those with 3D knowledge outperformed others (β = 5.490,p = 0.007)"
