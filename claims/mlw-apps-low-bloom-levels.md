## Evidence

### Antonio Ruiz-Martínez 2022

Antonio Ruiz-Martínez, Linda Castañeda, and Jesualdo T. Fernández Breis. (2022). A systematic literature review on the development and use of mobile learning (web) apps by early adopters. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Activity classification of the 71 apps by Bloom's taxonomy shows low-level activities dominate; the review reports percentages of "66.2% apps cover knowledge activities, 66.91% comprehension" and only 7.04% evaluation.

> "As can be seen, in general, the apps covered more than one type of activity and most of the apps were used to work the low levels of Bloom's taxonomy (knowledge and comprehension). Only 5 apps reported the inclusion of evaluation activities."
