## Evidence

### Gross D 2015

Gross D, Pietri ES, Anderson G, Moyano-Camihort K, Graham MJ. (2015). Increased Preclass Preparation Underlies Student Outcome Improvement in the Flipped Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0040

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-model 2 (class type) × 5 (week) ANCOVA of online homework attempts binned by week relative to each exam, controlling for GPA. The printed effect size is partial η² = 0.08 (partial η2 = 0.08), a medium effect, for flipped students who "attempted online homework questions more often," F(1, 445) = 38.41, p < 0.001; steady across-week differences had p values < 0.001.
