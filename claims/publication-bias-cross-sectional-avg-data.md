# Publication bias is detected in the cross-sectional data set, and bias-corrected estimates are highly heterogeneous

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Modified Egger's tests and a 3-parameter selection model both detect significant small-study effects in the cross-sectional data, and correction yields highly heterogeneous adjusted estimates. [→ Benoit Bediou 2023](#benoit-bediou-2023)

## Evidence

### Benoit Bediou 2023

Benoit Bediou, Melissa A. Rodgers, Elizabeth Tipton, Richard E. Mayer, C. Shawn Green, and Daphne Bavelier. (2023). Effects of Action Video Game Play on Cognitive Skills: A Meta-Analysis. Technology, Mind, and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1037/tmb0000102

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication bias detection in the cross-sectional meta-analysis using modified Egger's regression with the CHE model and a 3-parameter selection model; the NULL model shows "β = 2.10, SE = 0.59, t(17.0) = 3.57, p = .002" and the FULL model is also significant (p = .008).

> "The modiﬁed Egger 's tests showed signiﬁcant small-study effects, indicating signs of publication bias, both in the model without moderators used to assess the average impact of action video game experience on cognition, NULL model, β = 2.10, SE = 0.59, t(17.0) = 3.57, p = .002"
