# Transgender students report substantially fewer school supports than non-transgender peers, especially caring adult relationships and high expectations

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hanson 2019

Hanson, T., Zhang, G., Cerna, R., Stern, A., & Austin, G. (2019). Understanding the experiences of LGBTQ students in California. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601909

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive tabulations of pooled 2017-19 CHKS data from approximately 800,000 secondary students, disaggregated by gender identity. The report states these results "were about 20 percentage points lower than those for non-transgender students."

> "Only about 4 in 10 transgender middle and high school students reported the presence of caring adult relationships in school (42% for middle school, 45% for high school) and about half reported that adults in school held them to high expectations"
