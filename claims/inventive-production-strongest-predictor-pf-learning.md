## Evidence

### Manu Kapur 2023

Manu Kapur, Janan Saba and Ido Roll. (2023). Prior math achievement and inventive production predict learning from productive failure. npj Science of Learning 8:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00165-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on ratios-posttest scores with school as factor and PSLE math grade, pretest, and number of RSMs as covariates. Number of RSMs was significant; the authors state "the greater the number of RSMs generated, the better the learning from PF outcomes".

> "The analysis of the ratios-posttest revealed that both the number of RSMs, F(1, 107) = 62.589, p < 0.001, and PSLE math grade, F(1, 107) = 4.436, p = 0.032, had significant effects on the posttest performance."

### Manu Kapur 2023 (2)

Manu Kapur, Janan Saba and Ido Roll. (2023). Prior math achievement and inventive production predict learning from productive failure. npj Science of Learning 8:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00165-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on variance-posttest scores: only the number of RSMs significantly predicted performance; PSLE math score, school, and topic-specific prerequisite knowledge were not associated with posttest scores.

> "The analysis of the variance-posttest revealed a significant effect only on the number of RSMs, F(1, 100) = 105.518, p < 0.001."
