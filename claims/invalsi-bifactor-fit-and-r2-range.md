### Pietschnig 2023

Pietschnig, J., Oberleiter, S., Toffalini, E., & Giofrè, D. (2023). Reliability of the g factor over time in Italian INVALSI data (2010-2022): What can achievement-g tell us about the Flynn effect? Personality and Individual Differences, 214, 112345. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2023.112345

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive result across the 39 bifactor models (12 grade 2, 12 grade 5, 8 grade 8, 7 grade 10); median fit indices were RMSEA = 0.041, SRMR = 0.010, CFI = 0.996, NNFI = 0.991, which the article calls satisfactory.

> "R2 values were largely consistent, ranging between 0.40 and 0.60 across years, but with a slight tendency to increase at least in the latter grades (8 and 10)."
