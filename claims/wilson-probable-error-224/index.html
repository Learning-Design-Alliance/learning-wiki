Wilson, G. M. (1922). Language error tests. Journal of Educational Psychology, 13(6), 341–349. doi:10.1037/h0064444

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reliability analysis of the tests using Otis's Difference Method, yielding "The resulting probable error was 2.24"; the author states this confirms the high correlation coefficient reported for the forms.

"Under the direction of Dr. Arthur S. Otis, the probable error of a score by the Difference Method1 was figured. The resulting probable error was 2.24. This shows a high degree of reliability for the test."
