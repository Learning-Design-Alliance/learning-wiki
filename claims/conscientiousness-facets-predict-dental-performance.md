## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Competence (r = 0.33), dutifulness (r = 0.35), achievement (r = 0.30) and self-discipline (r = 0.23) correlate with academic GPA, while clinical work correlates with dutifulness (r = 0.35), achievement (r = 0.18) and competence (r = 0.19). [→ Jaber Mohamed 2022](#jaber-mohamed-2022)

## Evidence

### Jaber Mohamed 2022

Jaber Mohamed, Al-Samarrai Basim, Salah Afraa, Varma Sudhir Rama, Karobari Mohmed Isaqali, Marya Anand. (2022). Does General and Specific Traits of Personality Predict Students' Academic Performance? BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9422299

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Facet-level correlational analysis (Table 6, N = 144) of NEO-PI-R conscientiousness scales against GPA and clinic marks. Printed effect sizes include dutifulness r = 0.35 with GPA and r = 0.31 with clinic; clinical work was "strongly associated with dutifulness (r = 0.35, p < 0.01)".

> "Table 6 shows that four conscientiousness facets were strongly associated with AGPA at the p = 0.01 level of significance: competence (r = 0.33), dutifulness (r = 0.35), achievement (r = 0.30), and self-discipline (r = 0.23)."
