## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Retention performance showed no significant group difference on any exam (all t values < 1, all p values > 0.35). [→ McDaniel Mark A. 2022](#mcdaniel-mark-a-2022)
`q3 i?` For transfer items, the group difference was not significant on exam 1 (t = 1.32, p = 0.19) but became significant by exam 2 and remained robust (t values 3.41 to 3.60, all p values < 0.001). [→ McDaniel Mark A. 2022](#mcdaniel-mark-a-2022)

## Evidence

### McDaniel Mark A. 2022

McDaniel Mark A., Cahill Michael J., Frey Regina F., Limeri Lisa B., Lemons Paula P. (2022). Learning Introductory Biology: Students' Concept-Building Approaches Predict Transfer on Biology Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-12-0335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up pairwise contrasts across the four exams, plotted in Figure 5 from the 21 exemplar and 44 abstraction learners with complete exam and ACT data. The transfer advantage was "generally stable across exams, though perhaps diminished on the first exam."

> "The difference became significant by exam 2 and remained robust for subsequent exams (t values varied from 3.41 to 3.60, all p values < 0.001). Thus, the differential group differences across retention and transfer items were largely stable over the duration of the course."
