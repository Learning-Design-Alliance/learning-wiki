## Evidence

### Lo Kristin 2017

Lo Kristin, Curtis Heather, Keating Jennifer L., Bearman Margaret. (2017). Physiotherapy clinical educators’ perceptions of student fitness to practise. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0847-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Sixty-four educators responded to a free-text question on client care impacts; thematic analysis identified five themes. The most common were length of stay (36 responses) and quality of care (30 responses), with safety and client experience also reported.

> "Educators also considered that FTP issues affect service delivery and impact on those seeking health care."
