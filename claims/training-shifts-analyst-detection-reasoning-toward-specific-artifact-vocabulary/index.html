Negar Kamali 2026¶

Negar Kamali, Candice Rockell Gerstner, Jessica Hullman, Matthew Groh. (2026). Generative AI Literacy Training Improves Intelligence Analysts' Discrimination of Real and AI-Generated Images. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28510

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory LLM-assisted coding of 365 free-text comments from 24 analysts along the five-category artifact taxonomy; 250 comments pre-training and 115 post-training. The article reports "the only between-phase difference for which the 95% Wilson confidence intervals do not overlap".

"References to physics violationsdecreasedfrom 20.0% (95% CI: [15.5, 25.4]) pre- training to 7.0% (95% CI: [3.6, 13.1]) post-training, the only between-phase difference for which the 95% Wilson confi- dence intervals do not overlap."

Negar Kamali 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Same exploratory coding of free-text detection reasoning. The article reports high-specificity comments "rose from 33.2% pre-training to 41.7% post- training (+8.5 percentage points)", with post-training comments citing concepts taught in the session.

"High-specificity comments, those naming a concrete cue instead of a gen- eral impression, rose from 33.2% pre-training to 41.7% post- training (+8.5 percentage points)."
