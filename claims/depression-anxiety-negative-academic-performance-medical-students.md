## Evidence

### Alshareef N 2024

Alshareef N, Fletcher I, Giga S. (2024). The role of emotions in academic performance of undergraduate medical students: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education 24:907. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05894-1

`q2 · i1` · `review · r3`

The review reports a cross-sectional study of 254 first- and second-year medical students using the Zung self-rating scales and GPA, finding "a significant negative correlation" with printed effect sizes rho=-0.14 (depression) and rho=-0.19 (anxiety).

> "Mihailescu (2016) found a significant negative correlation between levels of depression/anxiety (rho=-0.14, rho=-0.19), academic performance and GPA among medical students."

### Alshareef N 2024 (2)

Alshareef N, Fletcher I, Giga S. (2024). The role of emotions in academic performance of undergraduate medical students: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education 24:907. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05894-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of prevalence across retrieved articles reports "prevalence estimates ranging from 7.3 to 66.4% for anxiety and 3.7–69% for depression"; these are descriptive rates with no effect size.

> "Studies consistently show concerningly high rates, with prevalence estimates ranging from 7.3 to 66.4% for anxiety and 3.7–69% for depression."
