`q3 i?` Adding a quadratic term for arousal did not significantly improve model fit for any performance measure or for the mathematics grade, contradicting a Yerkes-Dodson-style prediction. [→ Schillinger 2021](#schillinger-2021)

## Evidence

### Schillinger 2021

Schillinger, F. L., Mosbacher, J. A., Brunner, C., Vogel, S. E., & Grabner, R. H. (2021). Revisiting the Role of Worries in Explaining the Link Between Test Anxiety and Test Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09601-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-comparison tests adding a quadratic arousal term to each regression model (e.g., intelligence χ2(1) = 0.04, p = .834; grade χ2(1) = 0.41, p = .523). The article states "the model fit did not improve significantly by including a quadratic term of the predictor arousal for any of the above-described models."
