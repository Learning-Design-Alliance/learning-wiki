## Evidence

### Yusuf S 2026

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across three chronological groups of 30 patients showed hospital stay declining with experience; Bonferroni post hoc testing localized the significant 1.4-day difference to Group 1 vs Group 3.

> "The mean length of hospital stay decreased from 5.5 ± 1.19 days in Group 1 to 4.1 ± 1.24 days in Group 3 (overall mean: 4.8 ± 1.38 days; p < 0.001). Post hoc analysis demonstrated a significant difference of 1.4 days between Group 1 and Group 3 (p < 0.001)."

### Yusuf S 2026 (2)

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Despite literature suggesting catheterization time may shorten with experience, the study found no significant difference across groups; the authors note removal timing appears protocol-dependent and "may represent a parameter independent of the learning curve".

> "Urethral catheterization times were comparable among the groups (p = 0.206)."
