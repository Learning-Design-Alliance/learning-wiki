## Evidence

### Wegner Lauren E. MS 2023

Wegner Lauren E. MS, Shiffermiller Jason F. MD, MPH, Vokoun Chad W. MD, O'Rourke Michael J. MD, Rohlfsen Cory J. MD. (2023). The Effects of an E-learning Module on Medical Trainees Rotating on a Perioperative Medicine Clinical Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11325

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post test design with 75 trainees (39 students, 18 interns, 18 residents) at three institutions; 20-question pre- and postmodule knowledge tests analyzed with Wilcoxon rank sum tests. Median scores rose from 10 to 16, "p < .001". No effect size reported.

> "Following the e-module, median postmodule knowledge test scores improved significantly across all participants (16 vs. 10, p < .001) as well as within student (16 vs. 9, p < .001), intern (15 vs. 9, p < .001), and resident (15.5 vs. 11.5, p < .001) subgroups."
