## Evidence

### McCormick 2016

McCormick, M. P., Cappella, E., O'Connor, E. E., Hill, J., & McClowry, S. G. (2016). Do Effects of Social-Emotional Learning Programs Vary by Level of Parent Participation? Evidence from a Randomized Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

IPTW-weighted impact models (Table 3) from the INSIGHTS school-randomized trial show main effects for low dosage participants on math (B = 1.17, p < .05) and reading (B = 3.26, p < .05); the high dosage group showed "no average treatment impacts" on achievement.

> "there were main treatment effects of INSIGHTS for low dosage participants for both math (B = 1.17, p < .05) and reading achievement (B = 3.26, p < .05), relative to the comparison group. At the final time point, low dosage participants in INSIGHTS had higher math and reading achievement relative to their comparison condition."
