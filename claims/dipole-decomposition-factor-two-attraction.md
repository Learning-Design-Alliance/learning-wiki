# Decomposing the dipoles into parallel and perpendicular components rewrites the interaction energy so that the factor-of-two difference between attractive configurations becomes explicit

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the rewritten interaction energy, attraction via the perpendicular term carries factor one while attraction via the parallel term carries factor two, so reorienting the dipoles can make the attraction exactly twice as large. [→ Ladislav Kocbach and Suhail Lubbad 2009](#ladislav-kocbach-and-suhail-lubbad-2009)

Ladislav Kocbach and Suhail Lubbad. (2009). Geometrical simplification of the dipole-dipole interaction formula. https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.1548 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0908.1548](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0908.1548)

Analytical derivation in the paper: decomposing both dipole moments into components along and perpendicular to the connecting vector yields the rewritten energy with the term "the factor −2m1‖· m2‖", making the two-fold difference between configurations apparent. No effect size applies.

> "We show that by a simple rewriting of the formula it becomes apparent that for example, by reorienting the two dipoles, their attraction can become exactly twice as large."
