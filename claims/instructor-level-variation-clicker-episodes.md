Analysis of clicker-episode percentages for 25 instructors from nine departments who were observed teaching at least two clicker episodes; 19 of the 25 incorporated at least one peer-discussion opportunity.

> "However, nearly all of the instructors used Individual Thinking Only at some point in their instruction, and this strategy accounted for more than 50% of the episodes for 16 of the 25 instructors."
