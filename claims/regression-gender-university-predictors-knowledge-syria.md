## Evidence

### Ataya J 2026

Ataya J, Ismail A, Kousa A, Shahoud Z, Alhalbi R, Ghanem R, Ranjous Y, Zaitouna M. (2026). Assessing theoretical knowledge related to surgical and clinical skills in Syrian medical students: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051241

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression with HC3 robust standard errors on 500 participants. The model was significant with "adjusted R2 = 0.152"; private universities scored lowest (β = −3.13) relative to Damascus.

> "The overall model was statistically significant (robust F = 7.23, P < .001) and explained 17.8% of the variance in knowledge score (adjusted R2 = 0.152). After adjustment, females scored on average 0.50 points lower than males (adjusted β = −0.50, 95% CI − 0.98 to − 0.02; P = .041)."
