### Tudge 1986

Tudge, Jon. (1986). Beyond Conflict: The Role of Reasoning in Collaborative Problem Solving. Paper presented at the Annual Symposium of the Jean Piaget Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED275395

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Result from the 19-child equal-rule condition. Table 1 shows equal-rule means of -0.316, -0.421 and -0.316 at treatment, first and second posttest, negative at every point (F values 1.24, 1.29, 1.32, all p > .25).
