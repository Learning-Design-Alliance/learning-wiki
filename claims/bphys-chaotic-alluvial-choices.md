### Anna Chrysostomou 2025

Anna Chrysostomou, Alan S. Cornell, and Wade Naylor. (2025). Dominant misconceptions and alluvial flows between Engineering and Physical Science students. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/adc4ad](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/adc4ad)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alluvial analysis of paired responses for six FCI questions (Figures 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8). The authors report Engineering students "answer in the post-test with almost exclusively either the persistent misconception or the correct answer", whereas BPhys. choices scatter across options.

> "However, Physical Science students’ responses are more “chaotic”, with the set of choices being made in the post-test moving towards almost all options. Note that this behaviour exists for all the questions studied."
