## Evidence

### Jacqueline Anton 2025

Jacqueline Anton, Dor Abrahamson. (2025). Walking the number line: towards an enactive understanding of integer arithmetic. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09707-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Episode 3 case analysis from the pilot: students who returned to the walking line when stuck on the desk-scale line used it to re-enact problems and confirm answers; the authors code this as perspectival mutuality, with synergy as a possible later achievement.

> "In this case, students had achieved perspectival mutuality, and were validating their thinking on the SNL by utilizing their perspective on the BNL. Eventually, students may negotiate between these two perspectives to achieve perspectival synergy."
