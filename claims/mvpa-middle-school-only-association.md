### Zhang Danqing 2022

Zhang Danqing, Hong Jintao, Chen Sitong, Liu Yang. (2022). Associations of physical activity with academic achievement and academic burden in Chinese children and adolescents: do gender and school grade matter? BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-13886-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Grade-stratified logistic regressions from the same cross-sectional survey found significant associations "in middle school"; Table 4 shows non-significant CIs for primary (AA OR = 1.53, 0.92–2.52) and high school (AA OR = 1.68, 0.70–4.04). No standardised effect size printed.

> "Compared with those who did not meet the PA guidelines, children and adolescents who met the PA guidelines may be significantly associated with above-average AA (OR = 1.68, 95% CI: 1.18–2.40) and reporting no AB (OR = 1.77, 95% CI: 1.08–2.91) in middle school."
