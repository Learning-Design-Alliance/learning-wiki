# BIOME's association with CHEM 1A grades and on-time CHEM 1B enrollment does not differ significantly by student ethnicity

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interaction terms between BIOME enrollment and student ethnicity were statistically insignificant in predicting CHEM 1A grades, and on-time CHEM 1B odds did not change as a function of subgroup and BIOME enrollment. [→ Wilton Mike 2021](#wilton-mike-2021)

## Evidence

### Wilton Mike 2021

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analyses regressing CHEM 1A grades and on-time CHEM 1B enrollment on the BIOME × ethnicity interaction (reference group white), in unmatched and matched samples; interaction terms were not significant (p > 0.05). No effect sizes printed for the null interactions.

> "Correspondingly, Table 6 shows that the odds of taking CHEM 1B on time do not change as a function of subgroup and enrollment in BIOME. In other words, as for CHEM 1A grades, it does not appear that there is a difference in outcomes of on-time course taking for students in BIOME of one ethnicity over another."
