Helen Weixu Chen 2026¶

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

q3 · i1 · associational · r1

Independent samples t-test comparing midterm grades by cheat sheet type found no significant difference; printed Cohen's d was −0.25. Equivalence was not tested.

"For the midterm, there was no significant difference in performance between instructor-provided cheat sheet users (mean = 91.7) and self-created cheat sheet users (mean = 92.7), where𝑡=− 0.76,𝑝= 0.46,𝐶𝑜ℎ𝑒𝑛′𝑠𝑑=− 0.25."

Helen Weixu Chen 2026 (2)¶

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

q3 · i2 · associational · r1

The final-exam t-test did not reach significance, but the article reports a printed Cohen's d of −0.63, a moderate effect, with self-created users averaging 69.5 versus 61.8.

"For the final exam, while not statistically significant, a trend emerged showing higher average scores for self-created cheat sheet users (mean = 69.5) compared to those who used instructor-provided cheat sheets (mean = 61.8), where𝑡=− 1.65,𝑝= 0.13,𝐶𝑜ℎ𝑒𝑛′𝑠𝑑=− 0.63."
