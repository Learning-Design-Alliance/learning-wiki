## Evidence

### Zou Liqin 2024

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression (Table 2) with general self-efficacy entered as mediator; the article reports "PI significantly predicts GSE (β = 0.183, p < 0.01), and GSE significantly predicts AA (β = 0.169, p < 0.01)".

> "After incorporating GSE into the regression equation, PI significantly predicts GSE (β = 0.183, p < 0.01), and GSE significantly predicts AA (β = 0.169, p < 0.01). Hypothesis 2 is supported."

### Zou Liqin 2024 (2)

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Deviation-corrected percentile bootstrap test (repeated sampling 5,000 times) of the indirect path; the confidence interval [0.066, 0.103] excludes 0, and "the mediating effect value is 0.084, indicating that GSE is the mediating variable between PI and the AA of nursing college students".

> "For PI→GSE→AAA, the confidence interval is [0.066, 0.103], excluding 0, and the mediating effect value is 0.084, indicating that GSE is the mediating variable between PI and the AA of nursing college students."
