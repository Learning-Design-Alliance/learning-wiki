### Kruger N 2025

Kruger N, Plinsinga M, Window P, Hayes S, Bunzli S. (2025). Comfortable knowing little about lymphoedema? A qualitative study of medical interns in Australia. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-089789

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 of the thematic analysis: among those who had encountered lymphoedema, it was discussed only regarding practical tasks such as taking blood pressure or cannulation, for which lymphoedema was considered a precaution.

> "most participants in this study did not consider lymphoedema to be a condition of high relevance or importance to their role, with limited exposure to lymphatic conditions described in their clinical practice so far"
