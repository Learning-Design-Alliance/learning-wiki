### Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip 2024

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip. (2024). Analyzing properties of success for assessment development in maker-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10286-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from the interview study: one staff member described success as a facilitator adapting to the people in the room, and an educator described tracking changes in parents' attitudes toward play at home. The article reports the patron "is not always the person they expect to demonstrate success."

> "However, staff and educators also suggested that the learner or patron interacting directly in a program is not always the person they expect to demonstrate success."
