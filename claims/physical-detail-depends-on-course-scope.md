### Liam Doyle 2025

Liam Doyle, Fargol Seifollahi and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Building Bridges in Quantum Information Science Education: Expert Insights to Guide Framework Development for Interdisciplinary Teaching and Evolution of Common Language. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00454-y](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00454-y)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the RQ2 results, the authors summarize educator views that the appropriate balance of abstraction and physical basis depends on course scope and goals, with students from different backgrounds holding different expectations about whether physical bases of concepts like gates should be discussed.

> "They emphasized that whether students are able to answer the question (mentioned as an example during the interviews about whether entanglement of qubits requires a direct or indirect interaction between them) correctly or not depends on the scope and goals of the course."
