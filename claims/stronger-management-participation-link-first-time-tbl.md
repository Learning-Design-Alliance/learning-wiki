## Evidence

### Jeong Somi 2025

Jeong Somi, Kim Seung Jung, Lee Kyoung Eun, Ko Ara. (2025). The moderating effect of team-based learning participation frequency on the relationship between medical students’ perception of systematic team-based learning class management and active class participation. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional-effects analysis within the moderation regression on 575 students' feedback data (Table 5). The first-time participation group showed β=0.409 (p<0.001) versus β=0.195 (p<0.001) for students with two or more participations; both are standardized regression coefficients, not effect sizes.

> "Further, the conditional effects analysis revealed that the influence of students’ perception of systematic TBL class management on active class participation was stronger in the first-time participation group ( =0.409, p<0.001) compared to the group that had participated 2 or more times ( =0.195, p<0.001)"
