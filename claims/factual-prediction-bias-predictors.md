# For prediction judgments on factual questions, males and learners with lower WMC and lower topic knowledge, but higher self-perceived topic knowledge and self-perceived metacomprehension ability, showed stronger overestimation

## Evidence

### Stefanie Golke 2022

Stefanie Golke, Tim Steininger, & Jörg Wittwer. (2022). What Makes Learners Overestimate Their Text Comprehension? The Impact of Learner Characteristics on Judgment Bias. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09687-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 1 plots of significant predictors showed the direction of effects on prediction bias for factual questions: "males, lower WMC, and lower topic knowledge" and, contrary to expectations, higher self-perceived topic knowledge and metacomprehension ability "were associated with stronger overestimations."

> "in line with expectations, males, lower WMC, and lower topic knowledge, and, contrary to expectations, higher self-perceived topic knowledge and self-perceived metacomprehension ability were associated with stronger overestimations while their counterparts showed hardly any overestimation"
