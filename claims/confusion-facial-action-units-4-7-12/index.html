Evidence¶

Scotty D. Craig 2008¶

Scotty D. Craig, Sidney D'Mello, Amy Witherspoon, and Art Graesser. (2008). Emote aloud during learning with AutoTutor: Applying the Facial Action Coding System to cognitive/affective states during learning. Cognition and Emotion, 22(5), 777-788. https://doi.org/10.1080/02699930701516759

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Association rule mining on the same 300-observation dataset (100 records each for boredom, confusion, frustration). Table 2 lists the AU4-7 rule with 52% coverage and the brow-lowerer kappa at 1.00; the article states "Confusion displayed associations with AUs 4, 7, and 12".

"Confusion displayed associations with AUs 4, 7, and 12. Action units 4 and 7 occur simultaneously and the presence of AU7 (tightened lids) tends to trigger AU4 (lowered brow)."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Boredom during learning shows a significant association with facial action unit 43 (eye closure), but no association rules between action units
  • Frustration during learning is associated with facial action units 1, 2, and 14, with a mutual trigger rule between the inner and outer brow raises
  • In over 60% of inquiry threads, curiosity led to confusion and joy or confusion triggered curiosity among low-achieving students
  • The emote-aloud procedure is limited by non-reporting and floor effects: 2 of 7 participants did not verbalise affective states and four states were removed from analysis
