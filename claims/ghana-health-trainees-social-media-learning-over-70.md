Descriptive survey (n = 233) using a structured questionnaire with Cronbach's alpha above 0.80. Findings include 88% daily WhatsApp use, about 80% using smartphones for "WhatsApp chatting" and "social networking" (creation of learning groups), though these usage patterns were described as largely informal.
