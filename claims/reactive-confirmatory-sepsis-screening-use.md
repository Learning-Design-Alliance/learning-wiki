### Lewis Michael Steven Paul 2026

Lewis Michael Steven Paul, Smith Paula J. W., Howard Ian Lucas, Farhat Hassan, Alinier Guillaume. (2026). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of pre-hospital emergency medical care practitioners regarding sepsis recognition: a cross-sectional study in Qatar. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01607-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 221 HMCAS practitioners; 56.6% reported using sepsis screening tools. The results state "79.2% applied the tools selectively (only when sepsis was already suspected)", with qSOFA the most frequently reported tool (30.9%).

> "Among these, 79.2% applied the tools selectively (only when sepsis was already suspected), indicating that screening was typically confirmatory rather than proactive."
