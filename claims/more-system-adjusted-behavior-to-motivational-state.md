The authors' brief formative evaluation of MORE gathered comments from a small number of participants about their experiences. It showed the system "did indeed adjust its behavior", but the help-refusal tactic raised issues about the social rules of felicitous engagement between learner and system.

> "This showed that the system did indeed adjust its behavior, but that some of the rules were not appreciated: such as when the system refused to give help when requested if it thought that the learner did not need help"
