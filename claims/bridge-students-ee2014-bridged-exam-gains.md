### Stephen W. Pierson 2001

Stephen W. Pierson, Suzanne T. Gurland, and Valerie Crawford. (2001). Improving the effectiveness of introductory physics service courses: Bridging to engineering courses. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0107001](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0107001)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched-comparison analysis of EE2014 exam scores over two years (Bridge N of 7 in 1998 and 14 in 1999), with Bridge students matched to Non-bridge students on SAT Math or GPA. The article reports Bridge students "scored between 7% and 25% better than the Non-bridge students did", with F-values from 0.00 to 1.75 and p-values from 0.20 to 0.95, none significant.

> "As can be seen in Table 1, the scores for the EE2014 exams or quizzes that covered bridged material were consistently higher for the Bridge group. The Bridge group scored between 7% and 25% better than the Non-bridge students did."
