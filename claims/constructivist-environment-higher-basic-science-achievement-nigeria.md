## Evidence

### Atomatofa 2016

Atomatofa, R., Okoye, N., & Igwebuike, T. (2016). Learning Environments as Basis for Cognitive Achievements of Students in Basic Science Classrooms in Nigeria. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(6): 1471-1478. [doi:10.13189/ujer.2016.040627](https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040627)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment in six intact classes (122 constructivist, 121 transmissive) taught 16 lessons over 8 weeks; BSACT pretest/posttest analyzed by t-test. Constructivist posttest mean 24.61 vs transmissive 22.19; the article reports "t-calculated value (3.58) is higher than the t-critical value (1.96)". No effect size printed.

> "The posttest scores were significantly different as seen in table 1, because t-calculated value (3.58) is higher than the t-critical value (1.96). Thus there is a significant difference in the academic achievement of basic science students taught in a constructivist learning environment and those taught in a transmissive learning environment in favour of the constructivist group."
