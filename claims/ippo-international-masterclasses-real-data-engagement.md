### IPPOG Collaboration 2025

IPPOG Collaboration. (2025). IPPOG: a global network for particle physics outreach and education. arXiv:2509.17643 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.17643](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.17643)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article reports the growth of the annual International Masterclasses campaign, described as "approximately 3 000 students in 18 countries in 2005" growing to "over 13 000 students across 60 countries at 225 host institutions in 2023". In 2024 alone, 14,700 participants attended 110 online discussion sessions moderated at CERN, Fermilab, GSI, KEK and the Pierre Auger site. These are descriptive participation figures; no effect sizes are reported.

> "The scale of the annual international campaign has grown remarkably from approximately 3 000 students in 18 countries in 2005 to over 13 000 students across 60 countries at 225 host institutions in 2023"
