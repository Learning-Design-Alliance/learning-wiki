## Subclaims

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical comparison adding purpose of use, study-time multitasking, pre-sleep use, and nocturnal checking to models containing demographic covariates and total daily use (F(5, 350) = 10.11, p < 0.001 for sleep; F(5, 350) = 2.64, p = 0.023 for examination percentage).

> "the four additional variables significantly contributed to sleep quality but had little impact on academic performance, where total daily use carried most of the signal"
