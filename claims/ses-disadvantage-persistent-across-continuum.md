`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Cross-outcome synthesis illustrated in Fig. 4 comparing pooled effects of the same predictors on selection and progression, with printed effect sizes OR = 0.62, OR = 0.57, OR = 0.58, and OR = 0.55. The authors describe "a persistent impact of socioeconomic inequality" with a potentially cumulative effect from selection to completion.

> "First-generation applicants and those with parents in the lowest income brackets had reduced odds of both admission (OR, 0.62; 95% CI, 0.56–0.69 and OR, 0.57; 95% CI, 0.43–0.75, respectively) and progression through medical school (OR, 0.58; 95% CI, 0.52–0.63 and OR, 0.55; 95% CI, 0.5–0.59, respectively)."
