## Evidence

### Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim 2026

Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim, Tiong Nee, Kamarudin Mohammad Arif, Zamzuri Zamira Hasanah. (2026). Social network analysis as a technology-enhanced assessment tool for collaborative skills in medical PBL. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09397-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Node-level Mann-Whitney U comparison across 166 students in 17 PBL networks. The article calls this a "medium effect size" (r = 0.28), indicating Year 1 networks relied more heavily on bridging individuals connecting separate subgroups.

> "By contrast, betweenness centrality was significantly higher in Year 1 (Mean = 9.01, SD = 8.43, Median = 8.00) than in Year 2 (Mean = 4.74, SD = 5.19, Median = 2.50; U = 4883, p < .01, r = 0.28)."

### Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim 2026 (2)

Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim, Tiong Nee, Kamarudin Mohammad Arif, Zamzuri Zamira Hasanah. (2026). Social network analysis as a technology-enhanced assessment tool for collaborative skills in medical PBL. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09397-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Node-level centrality comparison reported in Table 4. The article describes r = -0.31 as a "large effect size", suggesting Year 2 students were more efficiently positioned to reach others within the network.

> "Inversely, closeness centrality was significantly greater in Year 2 (Mean = 0.88, SD = 0.01, Median = 0.85) compared to Year 1 (Mean = 0.80, SD = 0.12, Median = 0.81; U = 2643.5, p < .001, r = -0.31)."

### Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim 2026 (3)

Ahmad Azman Ahmad Hathim, Tiong Nee, Kamarudin Mohammad Arif, Zamzuri Zamira Hasanah. (2026). Social network analysis as a technology-enhanced assessment tool for collaborative skills in medical PBL. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09397-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null node-level result from the same Mann-Whitney comparisons; the article suggests students in both cohorts engaged in a comparable number of peer nominations. Equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant test.

> "No significant difference was observed for in-degree centrality (Year 1: Mean = 2.73, SD = 1.90, Median = 2.00; Year 2: Mean = 2.75, SD = 2.22, Median = 2.00; U = 3933, p = .715, r = 0.03)"
