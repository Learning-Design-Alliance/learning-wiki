Evidence¶

Noguera 2017¶

Noguera, P. (2017). Taking deeper learning to scale. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/deeper-learning-to-scale

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Classroom observations conducted as part of the OUSD equity review, alongside student focus groups. The report states "most teachers throughout the district did not utilize strategies aimed at engaging students in deeper learning" and that students were expected to listen passively to lectures, feeling insufficiently challenged even in honors and AP courses.

"Classroom observations revealed that most teachers throughout the district did not utilize strategies aimed at engaging students in deeper learning."
