# Background characteristics independently predict 9th-grade off-track indicators: boys are 1.7 times as likely to be suspended and 3.5 times as likely to have a GPA below a C than girls

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Boys have 1.7 times the odds of suspension and 3.5 times the odds of a GPA below C relative to girls; higher 8th-grade math achievement is associated with moderately lower odds of chronic absence and suspension. [→ Soland 2019](#soland-2019)

## Evidence

### Soland 2019

Soland, J. & Kuhfeld, M. (2019). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th Grade: the role of social-emotional learning trajectories. (NWEA Collaborative for Student Growth Research Brief). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/identifying-students-who-are-off-track-academically-in-9th-grade-the-role-of-social-emotional-learning-trajectories/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regressions of each off-track indicator on background covariates without SEL constructs (Table 4, N per model noted as 2,352 in later tables). Printed odds ratios: male 1.713 (suspension), 3.545 (GPA below C), math achievement 0.974 (absence, suspension).

> "Boys are 1.7 times as likely to be suspended and 3.5 times as likely to have a GPA of below a C than girls. The odds of being chronically absent or ever suspended were moderately lower for students with higher math achievement in 8th grade."
