### Bursztyn Leonardo 2015

Bursztyn Leonardo, Jensen Robert. (2015). How Does Peer Pressure Affect Educational Investments? The Quarterly Journal of Economics 130(3). https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv021

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quartile-specific regressions of daily correct answers on a post-change indicator with student fixed effects in the leaderboard natural experiment. The article reports the top quartile's "1.93 fewer questions correctly per day (significant at the 1 percent level)", a 40 percent decline, and states effects become less negative down the distribution, turning positive for the bottom quartile.

> "For students in the top quartile in column (5), the change was associated with answering 1.93 fewer questions correctly per day (significant at the 1 percent level). This represents a 40 percent decline from the prechange baseline of 4.8."
