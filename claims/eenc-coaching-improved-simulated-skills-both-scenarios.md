## Evidence

### Tosif Shidan 2020

Tosif Shidan, Jatobatu Anna, Maepioh Anita, Gray Amy, Sobel Howard, Mannava Priya, Duke Trevor. (2020). Healthcare worker knowledge and skills following coaching in WHO early essential newborn care program in the Solomon Islands: a prospective multi-site cohort study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-020-2739-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standardised clinical skills assessment in simulated Breathing and Non-Breathing Baby scenarios under examination conditions. The article reports the Breathing Baby median rose "from 20% (IQR 11–32) pre-coaching, to 95% (IQR 91–95) post-coaching"; the Non-Breathing Baby scenario rose from 63% to 86% (p < 0.001).

> "Median scores in the Breathing Baby scenario went from 20% (IQR 11–32) pre-coaching, to 95% (IQR 91–95) post-coaching, and 82% (IQR 66–91) at time of evaluation (p < 0.001)"
