# First-grade students' modeling, strategy, and application measures rose from pre-test to post-test in one single-group pre-post study, with effect sizes of 1.66, 1.75, and 1.70 respectively

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` First-grade students' average modeling level improved from 2.50±0.59 on pretests to 3.41±0.51 on posttests with an effect size of 1.66, with comparable large gains in strategy (1.75) and application (1.70). [→ An et al. 2013](#an-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### An et al. 2013

Song An, Mary Margaret Capraro, Daniel A. Tillman. (2013). Elementary Teachers Integrate Music Activities into Regular Mathematics Lessons: Effects on Students' Mathematical Abilities. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/0js732gf

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-tests on five pre and five post MSA assessments for the first-grade class (n=21). The article reports the modeling gain "with an effect size of 1.66", and separately reports strategy effect size 1.75 and application effect size 1.70 in the same results passage.

> "the first grade students improved from 2.50±0.59 on the five pretests to 3.41 ±0.51 on the five posttests with an effect size of 1.66"
