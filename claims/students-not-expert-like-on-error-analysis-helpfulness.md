## Evidence

### Benjamin M. Zwickl 2012

Benjamin M. Zwickl, Noah Finkelstein and H. J. Lewandowski. (2012). Development and Validation of the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. [doi:10.1063/1.4789747](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789747)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

End-of-semester Spring 2012 survey administration in three CU physics lab courses; Fig. 3 histograms of responses to the error-analysis statement from Intro Lab (N = 241), Modern Lab (N = 43), and Advanced Lab (N = 16). The printed pattern shows "many students’ are personally neutral or disagree" while experts agree.

> "A clear pattern is that while students realize experimental physicists ﬁnd error analysis a valuable tool for understanding data (in fact, all 6 faculty agreed), many students’ are personally neutral or disagree that error analysis helps them understand their data."
