## Evidence

### Alyami Wadha 2026

Alyami Wadha, Alosaimi Manal H., Baeshen Arwa, Alghanimy Alaa, Salam Esra, Alhujaili Sultan, Alqahtani Mansour M., Saad Ibrahim E. (2026). Simulation-based and clinical training in undergraduate nuclear medicine education: evaluation of competency outcomes using a structured OSCE framework. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09443-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results of station 2 (dose preparation and Mo-99/Tc-99m generator handling) in a quasi-experimental comparison of two institutional cohorts (SBT n=30, CBT n=30). The SBT group "scored a mean of 7.533 out of 8, compared with 3.883 for the CBT group"; the difference was significant with Welch's t-test and survived Bonferroni correction.

> "The SBT group scored a mean of 7.533 out of 8, compared with 3.883 for the CBT group. An independent-samples t-test with Welch's correction showed a statistically significant difference between groups (t = 7.55, df = 38, p < 0.001), which remained statistically significant after Bonferroni correction."
