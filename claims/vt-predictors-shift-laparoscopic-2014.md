## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of factors affecting VT in the retrospective cohort (Table 4): laparoscopic surgery beta = −22.09 (p = 0.001) in 2004 versus beta = −6.15 (p = 0.319) in 2014; prone position "was not associated with VT".

> "Upon regression analysis, in both 2004 and 2014, height, weight, and male sex were the risk factors that increased the VT. The prone position was not associated with VT. In contrast, laparoscopic surgery was the factor associated with a decrease in VT, as compared to non-laparoscopic surgery, in 2004; however, this factor did not affect VT in 2014 (Table 4)."
