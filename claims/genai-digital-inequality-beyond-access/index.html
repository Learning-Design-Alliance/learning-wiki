Moganadas SR 2026¶

Moganadas SR, Marín-González F, Nun SH and Gan CL (2026) Generative AI as a didactic-pedagogical mediator: rethinking human roles and pedagogical design in higher education. Front. Educ. 11:1856839. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1856839

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article argues from its literature synthesis, citing established digital-inequality research, that students with advanced paid tools receive more sophisticated feedback, multimodal support, and stronger reasoning assistance while others rely on limited free versions. HEIs must therefore consider which tools students can access, under what conditions, and with what pedagogical support.

"These differences may create inequality in accessibility, learning opportunity, privacy exposure, and capacity for productive iterative engagement, as digital inequality extends beyond physical access to include differences in skills, usage, outcomes, and meaningful participation in technology-mediated practices"
