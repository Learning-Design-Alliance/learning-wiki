Case study process-view analysis across the three settings. Automated entry showed a median typing speed of 49 wpm and 2.7 revisions per 100 characters with no idle time; copy-typing showed 33 wpm and 5.4 revisions per 100 characters. Classroom writers "revised most (12.5 per 100 characters)" and were actively writing for 77% of the session. Descriptive medians only.
