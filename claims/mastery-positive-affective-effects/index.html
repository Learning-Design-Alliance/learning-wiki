Guskey 1985¶

Guskey, Thomas R.; Gates, Sally L. (1985). A Synthesis of Research on Group-Based Mastery Learning Programs. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED262088

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis of six studies measuring a wide range of affective variables (affect toward subject, academic self-concept, importance, attributions); the article prints that "Effect sizes for these affective outcomes ranged from .11 to .53", a range rather than a single effect size for the overall finding, so no single magnitude is coded.

"Students who learned under mastery conditions generally liked the subject they wer4 studying more, were more confident of their abilities in that subject, felt the subject was more important, and accepted greater personal responsibility for their learning than students who learned under nonmastery conditions.'Effect sizes for these affective outcomes ranged from .11 to .53."
