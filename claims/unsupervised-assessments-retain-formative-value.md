### Gerd Kortemeyer and Wolfgang Bauer 2024

Gerd Kortemeyer and Wolfgang Bauer. (2024). Cheat sites and artificial intelligence usage in online introductory physics courses: what is the extent and what effect does it have on assessments? arXiv preprint. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010145)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-cluster correlation analysis (Fig. 8, significant correlations at p < 0.05) across all four usage classes; the article reports persistent supervised–unsupervised correlations and concludes unsupervised assessment "still retains formative relevance". No effect size printed.

> "Notably, within all usage classes, significant correlations between the supervised final exam and the unsupervised assessment components of the course remain. This means that in spite of even the heaviest use of external resources, if not as a significant predictor, unsupervised assessment still retains formative relevance."
