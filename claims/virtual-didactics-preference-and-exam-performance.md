`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing a choice-based conjoint analysis by Li et al., that trainees preferred virtual learning (67.9% vs. 32.1%, p < 0.01). Preference percentages only; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Urologic trainees actually prefer virtual learning compared to in-person lectures (67.9% vs. 32.1%, p < 0.01) [8]"

### Khosla Lakshay 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

> "Residents who engage with virtual didactics tend to perform better on exams compared to those who don’t (post test score of 60% vs. 44%, p < 0.01) [9]"
