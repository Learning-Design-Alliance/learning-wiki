## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Thematic analysis of five instructor interviews found instructors had to make instructions clearer without tactile cues, and reported that skill acquisition depended on student motivation. [→ Gupta Chandni 2022](#gupta-chandni-2022)

## Evidence

### Gupta Chandni 2022

Gupta Chandni, Henein Christin, Ashton Christopher, Makuloluwa Achini, Mathew Rashmi G. (2022). Development of virtual ophthalmic surgical skills training. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-021-01896-1

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with all five instructors, transcribed and thematically analysed in NVivo 12.0. Emergent themes were teaching flexibility, visualisation, clarity of instruction, accessibility and personalised learning; this is instructor perception, not a tested result.

> ""[Acquisition of skills] depended on how motivated each candidate was. Some were very motivated and got on with the activity and acquired the skills but some were maybe not motivated enough or maybe feeling frustrated that they were not getting it, and it was difficult for me to motivate them virtually.""
