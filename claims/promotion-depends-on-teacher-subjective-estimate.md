`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of paper B's marks against schools' passing standards. The article reasons from this contrast that "the promotion or retardation of a pupil depends to a considerable extent upon the subjective estimate of his teacher".

> "The pupil who wrote paper B, the poorer of the two, received from his teacher a mark 5 points above the passing grade, whereas twenty-two out of the one hundred and forty-two teachers (Figs. 3 and 4) did not give a passing grade to this pupil."
