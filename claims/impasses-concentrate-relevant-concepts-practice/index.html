Evidence¶

Ahtisham 2026¶

Ahtisham, B., Vanacore, K., Napoli, A., Arens, J., Ionova, K., Cohn, C., Salehi, S., & Kizilcec, R. (2026). Examining Variation in How Guided AI Tutors Resolve Student Impasses. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38346 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.38346

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-tabulation of 6,630 impasse turns across eight problem-solving practices in 1,260 authentic tutoring sessions, with a Pearson χ2 test of independence. The article reports "Impasses were concentrated in the planning phases of problem solving" and impasse type depended strongly on practice (χ2(10) = 2313.9, p<.001, Cramér's V=.42).

"Impasses were concentrated in the planning phases of problem solving (Table 2): 52.3% occurred while identifying relevant concepts, 18.1% during strategy development, and 14.5% during execution."
