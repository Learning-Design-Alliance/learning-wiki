## Evidence

### Arendale 2014

David R. Arendale. (2014). Understanding the Integrated Learning Course Model: Academic Transition to Tertiary Education. International Journal of Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v3n4p1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis combining all IL and introductory biology sections across 2002 and 2003. Table 3 reports IL attendance + biology final grade r = 0.588, IL final grade + IL attendance r = 0.848, IL attendance + biology attendance r = 0.607, and IL final grade + biology final grade r = 0.820.

> "As demonstrated in Table 3, the correlations were consistently strong (r = 0.588 to 0.848). Similar patterns occurred in every class section in each academic term over the two years of the study."
